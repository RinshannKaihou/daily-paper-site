> **一句话总结：** 作者在一个模拟的 4f 相干光学 Fourier 神经算子（32 维容差箱、4 任务 × 6 种训练方式 × 5 种子 = 120 个模型）上证明：定向最坏器件搜索在全部 120 个模型中找到的 in-spec 器件留出误差达到 200 个蒙特卡洛随机器件最大值的 1.08–3.10 倍（中位 1.79）、1000 个随机器件最大值的 1.06–2.71 倍，同预算均匀/Sobol' 采样乃至 10 万个随机器件都无法企及该误差；在热交换器任务上搜索器件的工程量误差在全部 39 个模型中超过 1000 个随机器件的最差值、进而突破核安全分析惯用的 Wilks 95/95 容差限，而"随机+搜索器件混合 PGD 微调"在全部 20 个任务-种子对上给出六种训练方式中最低的搜索误差。

> **TL;DR:** Wave-based processors (optical neural operators) are validated on randomly sampled devices, but deployment faces one fixed, unknown device inside a tolerance box. Using a differentiable PyTorch simulator of a 4-layer hybrid Fourier neural operator with a 32-dimensional tolerance box, the authors run a targeted worst-device search (differential evolution + signed-gradient PGD, plus unseen corner/CA/CMA-ES attacks) on 120 models across 4 tasks (Darcy, Navier–Stokes, lid-driven cavity, heat exchanger). In every model the searched device's held-out error beats the maximum of 1000 random Monte Carlo devices by 1.06–2.71x (R_200 up to 3.10x, median 1.79); budget-matched uniform sampling reaches only R_matched 1.21–2.26 and none of 100,000 uniform devices attains the searched development error (95% Clopper-Pearson bound <=0.0037%). Fine-tuning on a mix of random and searched devices (Mixed-PGD FT) is the best of six training arms in all 20 task-seed pairs, e.g. 4.35±0.1% searched error on Darcy vs 5.96±0.18% for Box FT (-22%). On heat-exchanger gauge pressure, searched devices exceed the largest error of 1000 random devices — and hence the Wilks 95/95 nuclear-safety tolerance limit — in all 39 models, in 11 models while no random device crossed a 1%-of-mean threshold.

## 核心贡献

- **量化"随机测试低估最坏情况"的差距**：在 120 个模型（4 任务 × 6 训练臂 × 5 种子）中，定向搜索找到的 in-spec 器件留出误差无一例外超过 1000 个蒙特卡洛随机器件的最大值——R_200（对 200 器件最大值之比）范围 1.08–3.10（中位 1.79），R_1000 范围 1.06–2.71（中位 1.63）；没有任何 MC 器件达到搜索误差（双侧 95% Clopper-Pearson 上界 0.37%，单侧 0.78%）。
- **预算匹配排除"多采样即可"的解释**：搜索预算为 16,256 次开发集评估；同预算均匀采样与 Sobol' 采样在全部 24 个受试模型上 R_matched 仅 1.21–2.26 / 1.19–2.16，10 万个均匀器件无一达到 Box seed-0 模型的搜索开发误差（CP 上界 ≤0.0037%），留出集上搜索误差仍是 10 万器件最优者的 1.45–2.12 倍；3.2^32 ≈ 4.3×10^15 个箱角中仅评估 1024 个，极端阶次统计（√(3D)/(2 ln N)，D=32、N=1000 时 ≈2.64）也解释不了部分 2.0–5.4 的超额比。
- **训练方式结论：Mixed-PGD 微调最优**：在随机器件与搜索器件混合批次上做 2 步符号梯度微调（Mixed-PGD FT），在全部 20 个任务-种子对上搜索误差为六种方式最低（Darcy 4.35±0.1%、NS 21.9±0.5%、LDC 1.31±0.70%、HX 0.952±0.066%），按种子配对比 Box FT 平均低 22%/13%/44%/16%（每任务 5-of-5 符号检验 p=0.0625）；纯角点训练（Box）的模型对搜索攻击最脆弱（如 Darcy R_200 1.88–2.14）。
- **工程量层面的安全含义**：在热交换器 3977 节点网格上，对面积加权平均压力 Q_p（均值 6.19 kPa）与二次流速度 Q_s（1.63 m/s），搜索器件在全部 39 个模型中超过 1000 随机器件的最大误差、即超过 Wilks 95/95 限（W_1 = 59 次随机运行的最大值）；对 11 个 box-trained 模型的 Q_p，没有任何随机器件超过 1% 均值阈值（Wilks 检验通过），但搜索器件超过了——随机测试在这些情况下"不报警"。
- **认证不可行性的实测**：Taylor 模型区间包围盒在最坏情况上，箱尺度 ε=0.005 时为真实最坏误差的 1.01–1.09 倍（紧），ε=0.01 时 2.03–2.46 倍，但在完整 32 维箱上是 6×10^3–8.5×10^8 倍——全箱可认证界目前无用，只能以搜索作为经验下界。
- **可复用的方法学**：给出了完整的测试协议（主指标 R_N、DEV/REAL 两套仿真配置、主攻击族 DE+PGD 与未见攻击族 corners/CA/CMA-ES、Wilks 95/95 与阶次统计置信界），并开源波仿真器的描述；结论"随机箱角是廉价的第一道筛查（1024 次评估，无梯度）"可直接迁移。

## Key Contributions

- Measures, across 120 models (4 tasks x 6 training arms x 5 seeds), the gap between targeted worst-device search and random-device testing on a simulated wave-based neural operator with a 32-D tolerance box: the searched device's held-out error exceeds the max of 1000 random devices by 1.06–2.71x (R_200 1.08–3.10, median 1.79) in every single model, with zero exceptions.
- Kills the "just sample more" objection: at the search's own 16,256-evaluation budget, uniform and Sobol' sampling stay at R_matched 1.21–2.26 and 1.19–2.16, and none of 100,000 uniform devices reaches the searched development error (upper Clopper-Pearson bound 0.0037%); the gap also persists on a finer pixel-level device model (R_200 1.18–1.49 after transfer) and under circular-phase crosstalk (R_200 1.21–3.03).
- Shows the gap is largest exactly where models were hardened against random errors: Random-error-aware models reach only 3.7–39.9x their nominal error under search (e.g. 17.5–39.9x on LDC), i.e. random-error training leaves them far more fragile than their nominal numbers suggest, and widening the tolerance box from k=0.25 to 2 inflates the searched error 2.6–8.1-fold while the MC mean grows only 1.04–1.73-fold.
- Identifies Mixed-PGD fine-tuning (half the batches trained on a device found by 2 signed-gradient steps) as the best of six training methods in all 20 task-seed pairs, 22/13/44/16% below Box FT on Darcy/NS/LDC/HX, and demonstrates real engineering consequences: heat-exchanger searched devices exceed the Wilks 95/95 nuclear-safety tolerance limit in all 39 models for both Q_p (6.19 kPa mean) and Q_s (1.63 m/s).
- Quantifies why formal certification cannot replace search: a Taylor-model interval enclosure is 1.01–1.09x the true worst error at box scale 0.005 but 6e3–8.5e8x at the full box, so worst-device search must serve as an empirical lower bound on worst in-spec error.

## Methodology

**Simulated hardware.** A differentiable PyTorch simulator ("wavesim") models a 4f coherent optical processor coupled to a digital 1x1 path, forming a hybrid Fourier neural operator (HybridFNO2d, 4 layers, 24 channels, Fourier modes -12..12; the first three spectral convolutions are wave-based, layer 4 and GELU/projection/readout are digital). An input SLM encodes 24 channels with 8-bit amplitude (sigmoid-bounded |M|<=1) and 8-bit phase; a finite pupil keeps 25x25 modes; a holographic reference and 10-bit ADC camera (local-oscillator shot noise N_ph=1e4, read noise 1e-3 full scale) read the real part. Two configurations are used: DET (primary endpoint; 8-bit masks and static errors, no converters/detection noise) and REAL (input DAC, output ADC, detection noise).

**Tolerance box.** Each of the 4 layers has 8 systematic knobs — registration shift (x,y) ±0.3 px, defocus ±0.2 px, tilt ±0.2 rad, SLM crosstalk width 0.5±0.1 px, extinction ratio 30±3 dB, reference phase ±0.1 rad — giving a 32-D box. A device = one knob setting plus one frozen random draw of static errors (per-pixel phase σ=0.05 rad, amplitude σ=2%, per-path piston σ=0.05 rad). Half-widths are "representative rather than calibrated".

**Tasks and models.** Darcy (a ∈ {3,12}, 64x64, 1000 train/200 test, generated here), Navier–Stokes vorticity (FNO benchmark, 1000/200), lid-driven cavity (65x65, 90-dim lid-history encoding, 3949/493/495, from Kobayashi et al.), and a thermal-hydraulic heat exchanger (102-dim condition vector → pressure/velocity at 3977 mesh nodes, 1200/173/173, from the group's RANS companion campaign). Each task trains six arms × 5 seeds (120 models): scratch arms Random-error-aware, Box, Smooth-mask box; fine-tunes Box FT, Mixed-PGD FT (half of batches on a device found by 2 PGD steps), SAM FT; a same-size digital FNO is the accuracy reference. AdamW, batch 20, one-cycle schedule.

**Search protocol.** Every search maximizes mean error over the first 64 development samples; errors are reported on the held-out set. The endpoint R_N = ε(search)/max over N Monte-Carlo devices (MC: 1000 uniform knob draws). The main attack family is DE (3 seeds, 3432 evals) + PGD (30 signed-gradient steps, 8 starts from best corners, 124 forward passes); unseen attack families (never used in adaptation) are 200 random corners (1024 evals), coordinate ascent (1024), and CMA-ES (1200); z_search = best of all, at 16,256 evaluations per model. Budget-matched comparisons use uniform, scrambled Sobol', random-corner and mixed samplers, plus a 100,000-device run and a pixel-level device model (each mode spanning 1+4 SLM pixels with fringe-based crosstalk).

**Statistics.** Two-sided 95% Clopper-Pearson intervals for exceedance counts; an order-statistic 95% bound on the MC 0.99-quantile; Wilks one-sided 95/95 tolerance limits (W_1 = largest of first 59 runs) for the nuclear-safety comparison; seed-paired sign tests across arms.

## Results & Highlights

- **Searched devices beat all random devices, everywhere:** in all 120 models the searched held-out error was 1.08–3.10x the maximum over 200 MC devices (median R_200 = 1.79) and 1.06–2.71x the maximum over 1000 (median 1.63); no MC device reached the searched error in any model (two-sided 95% CP upper bound 0.37%, one-sided 0.78%). The smallest margin of the whole study is itself instructive: LDC Mixed-PGD FT seed 1 scored 0.541% vs 0.505% for the worst of 200 MC devices (R_200 = 1.08) — and the authors show the LDC endpoint is largely fixed in advance because pressure dominates the joint error metric there.
- **Budget matching does not close the gap:** at the search's 16,256-evaluation budget, uniform sampling reached only R_matched 1.21–2.26 and Sobol' 1.19–2.16 (all 24 tested models). For the Box seed-0 model of each task, none of 100,000 uniform devices reached the searched development error (95% CP bound <=0.0037%), and the searched device was still 1.45–2.12x the best of the 100,000 on held-out samples. Pure corner sampling came within 10% of the searched error in 8 of 24 models, but on HX the searched device was still 1.52–1.90x the corner device.
- **Random-error training hides large fragility:** models trained only on random static errors reached 3.7–39.9x their nominal error at the searched device (5.5–9.2x Darcy, 3.7–5.9x NS, 17.5–39.9x LDC, 18.7–20.8x HX); gradient-search from the jittered centre closed 0.42–0.71 of the gap, corners 0.55–0.9x of the searched error, while CMA-ES (1200 evals) reached 0.73–0.90 and was still rising.
- **Mixed-PGD FT wins across the board:** searched error 4.35±0.1% on Darcy, 21.9±0.5% on NS, 1.31±0.70% on LDC, 0.952±0.066% on HX — 22%, 13%, 44% and 16% below Box FT when paired by seed, best of six arms in all 20 task-seed pairs, with nominal errors matching the digital FNO (2.05% vs 2.04% on Darcy). Smooth masks did not help the searched error (13–22% above Box on every Darcy seed, 4–49% above on NS).
- **Hardware + box training costs accuracy on NS:** box-trained arms had mean nominal errors of 13.0–19.0% versus 10.6% for Random-error-aware and 5.55% for the digital FNO — a concrete accuracy price for the hybrid optical design not tuned in width.
- **Engineering exceedances random testing misses:** on heat-exchanger Q_p (6.19 kPa) and Q_s (1.63 m/s), the searched device exceeded the largest error among 1000 random devices in all 39 models (30 main-round + 9 pixel-level), hence exceeding the Wilks 95/95 limit W_1 (largest of 59 runs). At a 1%-of-mean threshold on Q_p, 11 box-trained models passed Wilks (no random device above 1%) yet their searched devices did not; Random-error-aware models at 5% reached 6.42–6.87% searched errors with only 0.1–0.8% of MC devices above 5%.
- **Certification is hopeless at the full box:** Taylor-model enclosure = 1.01–1.09x the highest error at box scale ε=0.005, 2.03–2.46x at ε=0.01, but 6×10^3–8.5×10^8x at the full box across six sweeps. Meanwhile the REAL-configuration secondary endpoint narrows the primary gap to a ratio of 0.67–2.70 (>=1 in 116 of 120), showing part of the headline gap depends on the idealized DET endpoint.

## Critical Assessment

- **The headline is expected, the measurement is the contribution — and the paper says so.** That a worst-case search finds higher error than random sampling is close to tautological (Section 3 opens by conceding "A search is expected to find higher errors than random sampling"). The real contributions are the gap sizes, the budget-matched controls, and the exceedance consequences; readers should not cite this as "we discovered adversarial devices exist".
- **Everything rests on an uncalibrated, idealized simulation.** No device is built or measured; half-widths are "representative rather than calibrated"; optics are scalar with idealized lenses, omitting polarization, finite wavelength, phase-amplitude coupling, vignetting and detector artifacts. The authors are honest that transfer to a calibrated physical platform is "untested", but the abstract's framing ("random-device testing underestimates worst-case error") invites hardware-level reading that the evidence cannot yet support.
- **A specific methodological artifact the authors themselves flag:** PGD from the jittered centre takes signed-gradient steps that leave registration shifts near zero (within 0.05 px), and the highest-error devices were consistently the sharpest-crosstalk, lowest-extinction, best-aligned corners of the box (19 of 20 box-trained searched devices) — on the mode-level model this is likely an artifact of the start point rather than a physical worst case, and bilinear resampling in the pixel-level model changes the picture (the pattern held for only one of nine models there).
- **Reproducibility is weaker than the rigor narrative suggests:** wavesim and all training/analysis code are "available from the corresponding author upon reasonable request" — not a public repository — for a paper whose entire empirical claim depends on a custom simulator; Darcy/NS data are by request and the HX dataset is the group's own. The ledger and checksums help, but independent verification currently requires asking the authors.
- **Endpoint choices materially shape the numbers:** the primary endpoint uses DET (no converters/noise, one static draw per model); on the more realistic REAL configuration the searched/MC ratio drops to 0.67–2.70 with four LDC Mixed-PGD FT models falling below 1. The LDC result is metric-dominated (pressure carries the joint error after removing the spatial mean, so R_200 is largely fixed in advance), which the authors disclose but which dilutes the 120-model uniformity of the headline. Sign tests are per-task p=0.0625 with no multiple-comparison correction, and Mixed-PGD FT is not compute-matched against the other fine-tunes.

## Strengths

- Unusually systematic for a case study: 120 models, 4 tasks, 6 training arms, 5 seeds, four attack families (two unseen during adaptation), 1000-device MC baselines, budget-matched samplers, a 100,000-device run, and a pixel-level device model — the "just sample more / just sample better" escapes are all empirically sealed.
- Pre-registered endpoint and protocol (fixed before the main experimental round) plus honest secondary endpoints, including the one (REAL) where the gap shrinks and four models flip below 1.
- Demonstrates consequences on an engineering quantity, not just an error metric: searched devices exceed the Wilks 95/95 nuclear-safety tolerance limit in every HX model tested, in some cases while random testing shows zero threshold exceedances — a concrete, decision-relevant failure mode of validation-by-random-sampling.
- Genuinely useful practitioner guidance: random box corners (1024 gradient-free evals) are a strong cheap first screen; Mixed-PGD fine-tuning is the best defense among the tested arms; nominal accuracy is a poor proxy (Random-error-aware models suffer 3.7–39.9x degradation under search; the box centre can be worse than the average random device in 25/25 NS models).
- Careful statistics and self-criticism: Clopper-Pearson bounds, order-statistic quantile bounds, explicit treatment of the LDC metric artifact and the PGD start-point artifact, and a honest failed-certification result instead of hiding it.

## Limitations

- Purely simulated and stylised: no fabricated or measured hardware, scalar optics, idealized lenses, representative (not calibrated) tolerance half-widths, and no multiplexing architecture; transferability to a real calibrated platform is explicitly untested.
- Code and data are available only "upon reasonable request" — no public repository — despite the paper's reproducibility rhetoric (config hashes, unit tests, number ledger).
- The searched error is a lower bound on the true worst case: DE and CMA-ES were still improving when the budget ended, and the true supremum over 3.2^32 ≈ 4.3×10^15 corners is unknown; full-box certified enclosure is off by 6×10^3–8.5×10^8x.
- Only two correlation structures across layers are tested (shared-knob 8-D and crosstalk/extinction-correlated 26-D); real fabrication tolerances are partially correlated in unmodelled ways, and the budget-matched and 100,000-device runs cover seed-0 models only.
- The primary endpoint's DET configuration omits input/output converters and detection noise; one static error draw per model; relative L2 error can hide local PDE failures, and averaging HX quantity errors over 10 operating conditions can hide single-condition exceedances.
- Mixed-PGD FT is not compute-matched against the other fine-tunes, and per-task sign tests (p=0.0625) lack multiple-comparison correction.

## Relevance

这项工作把神经网络对抗鲁棒性中的"最坏情况搜索"方法论系统地迁移到波基物理神经网络硬件的容差验证上，并用核工程安全分析（Wilks 95/95）的语言给出了决策层面的后果。对关注 optical/analog neural operator、物理神经网络可靠性以及"随机采样验证 vs 最坏情况验证"的用户来说，这是一篇方法论可直接复用、且对训练策略（Mixed-PGD 微调）有可操作结论的研究。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How large is the search-vs-random gap on a calibrated physical 4f platform with measured tolerances and fabrication data, rather than representative half-widths?
- Would interval bound propagation (or other certified methods) through the globally mixing spectral layers beat the Taylor-model enclosure at the full 32-D box, or does norm propagation genuinely cap tight full-box bounds?
- Does Mixed-PGD fine-tuning keep its advantage under in-situ training, where the forward pass runs on hardware and gradients come from the fitted simulator?
- Which real-world correlation structure of fabrication errors (beyond the shared-knob and 26-D boxes) shrinks the gap the most — and could extreme-value extrapolation from the 100,000-device tail ever certify the supremum over the box?
- The search maximizes mean held-out error; how do the results change under worst-input metrics (max over test samples) or local PDE failure criteria that relative L2 may hide?
