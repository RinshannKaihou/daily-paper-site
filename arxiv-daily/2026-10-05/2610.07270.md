> **一句话总结：** 提出"经验契约"（interchange 安装 / removal 移除 / downstream rescue 下游恢复三个测试），只在自然输入观测到的取值范围内检验特征是否真的被模型使用：Ferrando 等人的 Gemma 未知实体潜变量虽能把知识拒答从 33.0% steering 到 79.0%（+46.0 pp），但自然值互换仅传递 0.12 的自然对比度、自然范围内目标只提升 +1.5 pp——"能 steering"不等于"模型在用该特征"；而 Pythia-70M 主谓一致特征集在 decoder 写入规则下通过全部测试（use 强度 0.951），证明该契约能区分真实机制与 steering 伪象。

> **TL;DR:** The paper proposes an "empirical contract" — interchange (installation), removal, and downstream-rescue tests evaluated only at feature values observed on natural inputs — to separate steering from mechanism. Auditing Ferrando et al.'s published Gemma-2-2B entity latents: the published steering raises knowledge abstention from 33.0% to 79.0% (+46.0 pp) on 400 unknown-entity questions, yet a within-natural-range target moves abstention only +1.5 pp vs +12.0 pp when pushed beyond the natural range, and paired interchange transfers only 0.12 of the natural known–unknown contrast (removal eliminates just 0.073) — the latents steer but are barely used. A dense Llama known–unknown direction installs 0.89 but its use strength is only 0.26, while the subject–verb agreement feature set (Marks et al., Pythia-70M) passes everything (use strength 0.951 under the decoder update), showing the contract separates real mechanisms from steering artifacts.

## 核心贡献

- 提出区分四层主张（关联 association、控制 control、充分性 sufficiency、使用 use）的"经验契约"：充分性与使用必须在自然输入观测值上检验——interchange 把"显示行为的输入"上的特征值复制到"匹配的不显示行为的输入"（安装强度 ρ_install = 传递的自然对比度比例），removal 反向消除，downstream rescue 在上游编辑保持激活时于下游恢复特征原值，use 强度 = max(ρ_remove, ρ_rescue)。
- 对已发表的实体识别潜变量（Ferrando et al. 2025，Gemma 2 2B-IT 的 L13/7957 与 L15/1898）做审计：复现了已知/未知实体分离（AUROC 0.874 / 0.745）与强 steering（+46.0 pp），但这些潜变量在自然值下几乎不携带行为差异——安装仅传递 0.12（L15/1898）和 0.05（L13/7957）的自然对比度。
- 发现稠密已知–未知方向在安装与移除上呈现相反的不对称性：Llama 方向安装 0.89 但 use 仅 0.26（"几乎充分但行为不依赖它"），Gemma 方向移除 0.61 但安装仅 0.27（"对移除敏感但其观测值不足以复现行为"）。
- 正例取决于写入规则（update rule）：Marks 等人的 147 个主谓一致 SAE 特征集在 decoder 写入下达到最高充分性（0.946）与 rescue（0.877），而匹配同样请求值的 encoder-constrained 写入 rescue 仅 0.560——同一表征、不同写入规则得出不同结论，因此干预主张必须与表征、位点、编辑 token、写入规则、行为度量捆绑陈述。
- 用拒绝方向（Arditi et al. 2024）作阳性对照：自然范围内把投影设为拒绝类均值即提升指令拒绝 +29.0 pp，且单位点 removal（-6.0 pp）远小于全层全位置消融（-45.0 pp），证明契约能检测出"真被使用"的特征。

## Key Contributions

- **An empirical contract for feature claims**: four ascending claims (association, control, sufficiency, use), with sufficiency and use tested only at values observed on natural inputs via interchange (installation), removal, and downstream rescue, each reported with 95% intervals rather than thresholds.
- **An audit of entity-familiarity representations**: the published Gemma SAE latents steer knowledge abstention strongly but carry only a small fraction of the natural known–unknown abstention contrast under paired interchange (0.12 for L15/1898, 0.05 for L13/7957), and within-natural-range targets barely change behavior (+1.5 pp vs +12.0 pp for an out-of-range target on 400 new questions).
- **Dissociation of installation from removal in dense directions**: Llama's known–unknown direction nearly suffices (0.89) but behavior barely depends on it (use strength 0.26); Gemma's direction is removal-sensitive (0.61) but its observed value does not reproduce the behavior (installation 0.27).
- **A positive case that depends on the update rule**: the agreement feature set spanning 6 residual-stream sites reaches its highest sufficiency and rescue only under the decoder update (0.946 / 0.877 vs 0.799 / 0.560 for the encoder-constrained update), because downstream layers read the residual stream, not the SAE encoder output.
- **Practical guidance**: feature claims should report the values steering reaches relative to the natural range, measure sufficiency and use separately, and specify representation + site + edited tokens + update rule + behavioral measure together.

## Methodology

**The contract.** Each item pairs a comparison input c₀ (behavior expected lower) with a target input c₁ (behavior expected higher); the natural contrast is Δ_nat = E[Y(c₁, Z₁) − Y(c₀, Z₀)]. Installation runs the comparison input's feature value through the target input; ρ_install (sufficiency strength) is the fraction of Δ_nat reproduced. Removal runs the reverse; downstream rescue applies an upstream edit that changes both the feature and behavior, restores the feature to its natural value downstream while the edit stays active, and ρ_rescue is the fraction of the upstream effect reversed; use strength = max(ρ_remove, ρ_rescue). Tests are run only when the interval for Δ_nat excludes zero.

**Update rules for SAE features.** The decoder update writes requested values along the decoder columns (for L15/1898 the residual write scales the requested preactivation change by ~3.5); the encoder-constrained update solves for a residual change whose re-encoded values match the request (max residual error ≤ 1e-3), matching what a future edit of upstream layers could produce. For dense directions, the projection is set directly. No-op controls verify achieved values after re-encoding; edits whose achieved value falls outside a preset tolerance are not scored.

**Audited representations (Table 2).** Known/unknown-entity latents L13/7957 and L15/1898 at entity-token sites (layers 13/15) of Gemma 2 2B-IT from Ferrando et al. (2025); class-mean-difference known–unknown directions at layer 15 for Gemma 2 2B-IT and Llama 3.1 8B-Instruct; the 147-feature subject–verb agreement set of Marks et al. (2025) on Pythia-70M-deduped (six final-prefix sites); a refusal direction following Arditi et al. (2024) as positive control.

**Tasks and data.** Entity task: Wikidata questions on basketball players, movies, cities, songs (e.g., "The movie 12 Angry Men was directed by ?"), judged known/unknown by whether the model answers ≥2 attributes correctly; 800 held-out entities for the AUROC replication, 400 new unknown-entity questions for dose/target studies. Agreement task: 147 annotated features evaluated on newly constructed singular/plural noun pairs (58 held-out noun groups → 232 pairs; 400 Llama and 148 Gemma interchange pairs). Refusal control: 100 held-out prompts.

**Measurement.** Responses generated greedily from Gemma and Llama; labeled by OpenAI gpt-5.6-terra under a fixed six-category rubric (substantive answer / explicit refusal / invalid entity or premise / knowledge uncertainty / claimed inability / other), blind to intervention condition; on 574 responses double-annotated by two humans the judge reaches balanced accuracy 0.94 [0.92, 0.96] for knowledge abstention (macro-F1 0.86); humans agree at 0.99. Instruction refusal is scored by a fixed Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct judge. Features, sites, update rules, targets and measures were fixed on development data before confirmation runs on held-out data; all ratios are reported with 95% intervals clustered by paired entity/noun group.

## Results & Highlights

- **Steering reproduces, but only outside the natural range.** On 400 new unknown-entity questions in Gemma 2 2B-IT, baseline knowledge abstention is 37.0% (148/400); the published steering at α = 400 lifts abstention from 33.0% to 79.0% (+46.0 pp [40.5, 51.5], an increase for every entity type). Yet setting the unknown-entity latent to a natural target near its 95th percentile changes abstention by only +1.5 pp [-1.2, 4.2], while a high target beyond every unedited activation raises it +12.0 pp [6.4, 14.6], exceeding the natural target by +10.5 pp [6.4, 14.6] — behavior changes only when the latent leaves its natural range (Figure 2).
- **Interchange and removal dissociate the latents from behavior (Table 3).** Paired interchange on matched known–unknown questions transfers only 0.12 (L15/1898) and 0.05 (L13/7957) of the natural abstention contrast; removal eliminates 0.073 and 0.195 respectively (rescue not run), so use strengths are only bounded at ≥0.073 and ≥0.195 — far below the agreement feature set's 0.951.
- **The known-entity latent does not carry the accuracy signal.** Rare-alias phrasings lower accuracy by 11.2 pp [7.6, 14.8] on 500 held-out alias pairs; copying the full residual state at the site recovers accuracy to a 10.6 pp change [7.2, 14.0], but copying only the latent's value changes it by −0.2 pp [-2.0, 1.0].
- **Dense directions show opposite asymmetries.** The Llama direction installs 0.89 of the natural contrast but removal eliminates only 0.07 and rescue reverses 0.26 (use strength 0.26); the Gemma direction installs only 0.27 but removal eliminates 0.61 (use strength 0.61, rescue 0.09) — sufficiency and use must be tested separately.
- **The positive controls pass.** The agreement feature set under the decoder update nearly reproduces both natural conditions: installation 0.946, removal 0.951, downstream rescue 0.877 (use strength 0.951); the encoder-constrained update, which matches requested values more exactly after re-encoding, yields 0.799 / 0.981 / 0.560 — the update rule changes the conclusion. The refusal direction within its natural range raises instruction refusal +29.0 pp [18, 39] on 100 held-out prompts.
- **Site scope matters.** For the refusal control (Table 4, Qwen judge), removal at one site and token lowers refusal by −6.0 pp [-11, -2] and projection-to-zero by −5.0 pp [-10, -1], whereas directional ablation at every layer and position suppresses −45.0 pp [-55, -35] — a single-token edit probes one location of a computation that may span tokens and layers.

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty.** The framework is a careful synthesis rather than a new technique: interchange descends from Geiger et al.'s causal abstractions, rescue from Heinemerchiem/Nanda's activation-patching guidance, and the natural-range concern from Makelov et al.'s subspace-patching illusion and Miller et al.'s faithfulness critique. The contribution is packaging these into a four-claim ladder with quantitative strength definitions and applying it to specific published features — genuinely useful, but a methods-audit paper, and its impact depends on community uptake of the contract.

**Rigor and red flags.** The audit is honest but incomplete in places: rescue was **not run** for either Gemma latent (Table 3 reports "not run"), so the headline "the latents barely change behavior" rests on removal fractions that are only lower bounds on use strength — a defender of the latents could argue rescue might have revealed more. The behavioral labels come from an LLM judge (gpt-5.6-terra); although validated at 0.94 balanced accuracy against 574 human-double-labeled responses, all headline deltas flow through that single judge. The audited scope is narrow: three small models (Gemma 2 2B-IT, Llama 3.1 8B-Instruct, Pythia-70M-deduped) and three clean single-concept tasks (Wikidata entity familiarity, subject–verb agreement, refusal) — the natural-range criterion has not been tested on distributed, multi-concept knowledge in larger models, where "natural values" are harder to enumerate. The positive control (Marks et al.'s agreement set) is itself a curated, published feature set; a cynic could note the one hand-annotated feature collection chosen for the positive case is also the one that passes, though the refusal-direction control partially answers this. Reproducibility is promised via an artifact but the full codebase is only "released upon publication".

**Significance.** The core message — that steering magnitude says nothing about whether the model uses the feature at natural values, and that every intervention claim is conditional on representation + site + tokens + update rule + measure — directly challenges a chunk of the SAE-steering literature and aligns with recent failures-to-replicate (AxBench, Galcone et al., Vicentino). If adopted, this raises the evidentiary bar for mechanistic claims substantially.

## Strengths

- The contract is operationalized with concrete, reportable quantities (ρ_install, ρ_remove, ρ_rescue with 95% intervals) rather than pass/fail slogans, and each claim gets its own test.
- Both positive controls (agreement feature set, refusal direction) genuinely pass, which shows the tests can be satisfied and are not designed to fail everything.
- The update-rule finding (decoder 0.946/0.877 vs encoder-constrained 0.799/0.560 for installation/rescue on the same feature set) is a non-obvious, practically important result about how write location interacts with residual-stream computation.
- Exceptional measurement hygiene: blinded LLM adjudication validated against human double labels, no-op controls, dev/holdout separation, intervals instead of thresholds, and honest reporting of "not run" cells.
- Direct, reproducible audit of specific published features (exact latent IDs L13/7957, L15/1898) rather than strawmen.

## Limitations

- Small-model scope (2B/8B/70M) and three narrow single-concept tasks; generalization to larger models and distributed knowledge is untested.
- Rescue was not run for the two audited Gemma latents, so their use strength is only a lower bound (≥ removal fraction), weakening the strongest negative claim.
- All knowledge-abstention measurements pass through one LLM judge; judge error, however small, is correlated across all reported deltas.
- The contract evaluates features one site at a time; the authors themselves show single-token edits under-measure multi-layer computations (refusal: −6.0 pp single-site vs −45.0 pp full ablation), so a feature used across many sites could still fail the single-site test.
- No new capability or method is delivered — it is an evaluation framework and audit, with the codebase not yet publicly linked (promised upon publication).

## Relevance

这篇论文直击 SAE/steering 可解释性文献的核心方法论问题——"能改变行为"与"模型真的在用这个特征"之间的鸿沟，为评估任何特征干预类论文提供了可直接套用的检验清单（自然值互换、移除、下游 rescue + 写入规则披露）。对于关注机制可解释性、因果抽象与 LLM 拒答/知识意识机制的工作，这是必读的审计基准。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the natural-range criterion hold for large-scale, distributed factual knowledge (70B+ models), where a concept may be encoded across many sites and no single "natural value" dominates?
- Can the contract be automated into a community benchmark that scores published interpretability claims (e.g., an extension of RAVEL) instead of requiring per-paper manual audits?
- Would running downstream rescue on the Gemma latents (omitted here) raise their use strength above the removal-based lower bounds, and would multi-site joint installation change the verdict?
- How should the contract handle multi-feature interventions, given recent evidence that steering effects often emerge only from combinations of features?
- Is the decoder-vs-encoder-constrained update asymmetry specific to Pythia-70M's SAE geometry, or does it reproduce for modern gated/TopK SAEs in production-scale models?
