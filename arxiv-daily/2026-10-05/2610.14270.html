Key Metric

−75%

of the RL accuracy gain erased by patching an 8-dim “think subspace” back to original-model values — vs 22% for a matched PCA control (−3% random).

AIME, Qwen3-8B: 0.189 → 0.411 after GRPO · think-mode ceiling 0.74 (never exceeded)

Why This Matters

No-think RLVR benchmarks may be conflating new capability with mode control — a caution for anyone post-training or evaluating hybrid reasoning models.

直击 “RL 是新增能力还是唤起已有能力” 的核心争议：在混合推理模型上做 no-think RL 训练与评测者的必读警示。

Critical Edge

Evidence rests on one model family (Qwen3 ≤ 8B) and AIME math alone; the headline patching numbers come from 90 problems × 2 samples with no error bars — several reported deltas are the same size as the sampling noise.

Methodology — How They Showed It

  1. 01

    Train in no-think mode

    GRPO-style RLVR on DAPO-Math-17k with Qwen3-1.7B/4B/8B; the chat template appends an empty <think>  </think> block. LoRA r=64, KL 1e-3, ~67 steps.

  2. 02

    Measure the drift

    Reflection-word counts; think win rate (original model as fixed judge); activation shift α onto the cross-fitted “think direction” between modes.

  3. 03

    Intervene at three levels

    Scale the weight update W₀+cΔW; cross-model patching of a pre-registered 8-dim think subspace (PCA / random controls); knockout and single-head patching of heads that read the empty think block.

  4. 04

    Trace the cause

    GRPO advantages vs number of correct responses k per group; one AdamW step on a fresh LoRA under full / shuffled / uniform advantage weightings, measuring Δα.

Evaluation: AIME 2022–24, 90 problems × 2 samples, 32,768-token budget, graded by the last \boxed{} expression.

  • Weight scaling: at c=3 the step-67 update hits 0.72 accuracy; 51% of responses emit </think> at median position 0.91 vs 0.92 in true think mode — no tested c exceeds think-mode 0.74.
  • Heads: knocking out the top-5% empty-think-block heads gives α=0.78, RKL=72% (random 5%: α≤0.04); single head L18H19 carries 44% of the shift (37% at 4B; L19H13 54% at 1.7B), held-out confirmed.
  • Cause: correct-response advantages fall +1.79 (k=1) → +0.45 (k=4) as their think-likeness drops (TWR 57.8%→40.5%); one GRPO gradient step moves α +0.0075, shuffled advantages −0.0022.
  • Steering (appendix): adding a·vthink at layer 18 of the original 8B lifts accuracy 0.189 → 0.60, though 24% of responses then hit the 32k budget.

Strengths

  • Three independent intervention levels (weights, activations, heads) converge on one mechanism — each with matched negative controls (PCA/random subspaces, random head sets, shuffled advantages).
  • Pre-registration discipline: subspace dimension, patched layers, and the primary comparison fixed before any AIME result was seen; α is cross-fitted; head rankings validated on held-out responses.
  • Clean design unique to hybrid models: both modes share all weights and differ only in a few prompt tokens, making the think direction a well-defined intervention target.
  • Practically important: no-think RLVR evaluation may be measuring “mode control,” not new capability — this changes how hybrid-model post-training should be judged.

Limitations

  • Qwen3 only, ≤8B, math only — no second model family, no other RL algorithm (the cause analysis is tied to group-normalized advantages), no out-of-math task.
  • 90 problems × 2 samples per condition, no confidence intervals; several reported deltas are comparable to observed sampling noise (±0.017–0.022).
  • The GRPO-signal explanation stays correlational plus an initial-gradient probe; no training run is re-run with a leakage-debiasing objective.
  • The 8-dim subspace removes 75% but transfers only 50–60%; the residual gain and the update’s top singular directions are left uncharacterized.
  • TWR judge is the original model itself (mild circularity); activation analyses use teacher-forced, fixed 2,048-token responses.

Questions for Further Exploration

  1. Q1Does thinking leakage appear in other hybrid models (Llama-Nemotron) and under non-group-normalized RL (PPO, REINFORCE)?
  2. Q2What is the residual 25–40% of the gain the think subspace does not explain — new behavior, formatting, or budget effects?
  3. Q3Would a leakage-aware objective (penalizing α during RL) preserve the no-think gain — or shrink it to zero?
  4. Q4Does RL in think mode degrade no-think behavior through the same heads, and is the boundary reversible by head-level edits alone?
  5. Q5Is the L18H19 empty-think-block circuit template-specific, or does it exist with latent/continuous reasoning templates?
