> **一句话总结：** 该文在 DeepPlanning 旅行规划基准（120 任务、确定性程序化打分、4-rollout 噪声底线 0.035）上提出按"首个失败信号"把轨迹分为 F1 证据/ F2 harness 摩擦/ F3 执行失控/ F4 规划能力四类的诊断框架，并用实验证明 harness 自进化只消除过程性失败（F2/F3）而在内容性失败 F4 处触顶——Qwen3.5-4B held-out 从 0.164 升至 0.297（+0.133）、9B 从 0.323 升至 0.442（+0.119）后，剩余失败恰是 F4；此时在进化后 harness 收集的轨迹上做 LoRA（rank 32）即可内化该增益（seed harness 下两尺寸 held-out 分别 +0.126 / +0.131，4B 上与 harness 叠加、9B 上可替代 harness），最终给出"先测噪声、再读失败构成：过程失败交给 harness、内容失败交给权重"的两次介入规则，并在 9 条模型线上验证。

> **TL;DR:** The paper asks when to stop evolving an LLM agent's harness and start training weights, and answers: read the failure composition. Trajectories are labeled by the first failure signal that fires — F1 evidence, F2 harness friction, F3 execution control (loops/budget exhaustion), F4 planning capability (a poor plan with no process signal) — on DeepPlanning (120 programmatic-scored travel-planning tasks, 48 dev / 72 held-out; 4-rollout noise floor 0.035, fresh anchors after every accepted edit). An AHE-style self-evolving harness lifts Qwen3.5-4B held-out from 0.164 to 0.297 (+0.133) and Qwen3.5-9B from 0.323 to 0.442 (+0.119) by removing process failures (4B loops F3 fall 64%→37%, delivery 55%→90%), then plateaus with F4 as the dominant residual — because the loop's evidence package never shows the scorer's failure composition. LoRA (rank 32) trained on evolved-harness trajectories adds +0.126 (4B, qkvo) and +0.131 (9B, qkvo+MLP) held-out under the seed harness — beating the placebo adapter by +0.13–0.22 — stacks with the harness on 4B (+0.082) and substitutes for it on 9B (0.449 vs 0.442, within noise). On WebArena-Lite the same loop gains +0.088 on 117 unseen tasks (0.256→0.344), but the gain lives in what the model sees (one interface edit = +0.12 of dev gain) and adapters add nothing. Across nine model lines the harness lever pays exactly where delivered-plan quality is ≥0.27 and F4 is not yet the largest class.

## 核心贡献

- **按"移除者"而非"错误类型"给失败分类：** 用确定性检查器按首个触发的信号把轨迹标为 healthy / F1 证据（无检索支撑的声明占比 ≥0.5）/ F2 harness 摩擦（被强制 checkpoint 或 ≥10 次工具拦截）/ F3 执行失控（同一调用重复 ≥3 次、用掉 ≥90% 步预算或被 harness 终止）/ F4 规划能力（无过程信号、计划本身太差），并给出假设：harness 杠杆只移除 F2/F3，F1/F4 是权重的目标。
- **带噪声校准的测量协议：** 实测每 rollout 标准差 0.023（最差 0.042），4 rollouts 下 0.035 以下视为噪声；每次接受编辑后测 fresh anchor，配 Proposition 1（选择乐观上界 s√(2 ln K)）量化"接受读数"的虚高，并记录 ratchet 滑移、惰性接受、plan-to-JSON 转换器偏差（平均 ~0.025）三类自陷坑。
- **Harness 自进化的增益与触顶：** AHE 式进化循环使 4B held-out +0.133、9B +0.119，增益落在分类学预测的位置（4B 交付率 55%→90%，F3 循环 64.1%→37.0%），但 F4 从不被消除（4B 9.9%→28.1% 反而上升）——组件消融显示合规类组件（exit gate、checkpoint、硬阻断）基本在噪声内，增益来自能力授予类组件（进化工具 −0.067/−0.120、lessons 文件 −0.049/−0.076），而循环的证据包从不向提议者展示 scorer 的失败构成，所以它在 F4 前失明。
- **权重训练内化 harness 增益，且各移除各自的失败类：** 在进化后 harness 的轨迹上做 LoRA，seed harness 下 held-out +0.126（4B）/+0.131（9B），安慰剂 adapter（打乱助手输出的相同配方）比基线低 0.158/0.227；4B adapter 复现 harness 行为（交付率 52%→78%、F3 降 18–19 点、F4 不变）故与 harness 叠加（+0.041/+0.082），9B adapter 消减 F4（23%→7% dev、28%→5% held-out）故可替代 harness；更大数据集全参数微调再 +0.04。
- **可操作的两次介入规则 + 跨基准/跨模型验证：** 规则化为（1）先测噪声底线；（2）看交付率与 F1/F4 构成分支；（3）F4 残余时向提议者暴露失败构成；（4）读接受编辑改了"模型写的"还是"模型看的"来决定增益能否进权重。该规则在 9 条 DeepPlanning 线（Qwen3.5-4B/9B、Qwen3.6-35B-A3B、Tencent hy3、MiniCPM5-2B、Spark-X2.5-4B、Ministral-3-8B、Gemma 4 12B）和 WebArena-Lite（Qwen3.5-9B）上前瞻性成立。

## Key Contributions

- A failure taxonomy organized by the lever that removes each error — F1 evidence, F2 harness friction, F3 execution control, F4 planning capability — computed by a deterministic checker from the first signal that fires, rather than by an LLM/human annotator as in MAST, TRAIL or AgentErrorTaxonomy.
- A noise-calibrated measurement protocol for harness interventions on DeepPlanning: per-rollout σ = 0.023 (median over 26 same-night cells), 0.035 resolvable difference at 4 rollouts, fresh anchors after every accepted edit, and Proposition 1 bounding selection optimism at s√(2 ln K) — plus candid documentation of winner's curse, ratchet slippage, inert accepts, and a plan-to-JSON converter bias (~0.025 average inflation).
- Harness self-evolution gains where the taxonomy predicts: Qwen3.5-4B held-out 0.164→0.297 (+0.133) and 9B 0.323→0.442 (+0.119), with delivery and loop-reduction carrying the gain and F4 never reduced; component ablations show capability-granting components (evolved tools −0.067/−0.120, lessons file −0.049/−0.076) carry the gain while enforcement components are within noise.
- An account of why the loop stops at F4: the evidence package leads with process metrics, the scorer's per-check failure composition is never aggregated into the package, and the proposer is a single call with no tools — so the 9B multi-draft rate (25.5%→25.0%) is never touched.
- LoRA and full-parameter training on evolved-harness trajectories with a placebo control and rank ablation: +0.126/+0.131 held-out under the seed harness, stacking on 4B (+0.082), substituting on 9B (within noise), each adapter moving exactly the failure class the rule assigns to it, and — on WebArena-Lite — no transfer of gains that live in what the model sees.

## Methodology

- **Benchmark and noise floor.** DeepPlanning (Zhang et al., 2026b): multi-day travel-planning tasks needing tool calls (flights, hotels, attractions, state tracking) and a final structured plan scored programmatically against hard constraints. 120 tasks split once into 48 development / 72 held-out; evolution and ablations use only dev, held-out only for reporting. Primary models: Qwen3.5-4B and 9B served locally, thinking disabled, temperature 0.7. Every cell is the mean of 4 rollouts; per-rollout σ measured at 0.023 (26 same-night cells, worst 0.042), so differences below 0.035 are "within noise"; comparisons are same-night unless marked, since cross-night drift reaches 0.05.
- **Failure taxonomy.** Post hoc, per trajectory: delivered plan scoring ≥0.5 is healthy; otherwise the first firing signal wins — F1 evidence (ratio of unbacked claims ≥0.5 or exit gate passed unresolvable items), F2 harness friction (a harness intervention blocked the agent: forced checkpoint unlock or ten+ blocked tool calls with low score), F3 execution control (a tool call repeated ≥3 times, ≥90% of step budget used, or harness termination), F4 planning capability (none of the above; an undelivered trajectory with no process signal). Delivery rate (fraction returning a parsable plan) and quality among delivered (mean score of delivered plans) are tracked separately; composite = r·q̄ exactly (Equation 2: ΔS = q̄Δr + r'Δq̄).
- **Harness self-evolution protocol.** AHE-style loop: each round, (1) run the current harness on all 48 dev tasks (4 rollouts); (2) build an evidence package (score, healthy rate, F1–F4 counts, process metrics, worst cases) for a proposal model (Kimi K3) that returns two candidate edits, each with a manifest and its own feedback; (3) each candidate is evaluated with 4 rollouts and judged against the round's fresh anchor by a fixed house rule (reject if mean drops >0.0 or healthy rate drops >0.05); (4) the better accepted candidate is retained and a fresh anchor is measured before the next round. Editable surface: prompts, policy parameters, middleware, tools. 4B line: 13 rounds, 26 candidates, 10 accepted; 9B line: 5 rounds, 10 candidates, 3 accepted (all edits to a distilled lessons file).
- **Weight training.** Trajectories collected under the evolved harness on the 48 dev tasks: best 3 per task above score threshold 0.5/0.5/0.6 for 4B/9B — 82 trajectories over 34 tasks (4B), 98 over 40 (9B). Loss on assistant outputs only; LoRA rank 32, α=64, dropout 0.05, AdamW, lr 1e-4, one epoch, qkv-only vs qkv+MLP placement; full-parameter fine-tuning on a larger second textbook (198 trajectories/42 tasks for 4B, 200/44 for 9B) with lr 2×10⁻⁶, warm-up + cosine decay. Adapters evaluated on both harnesses and both splits the same night as their base; a placebo adapter (assistant outputs permuted across samples, identical recipe) controls for 9B.
- **Generalization lines.** The same loop and measurement are run on MiniCPM5-2B, Spark-X2.5-4B, Ministral-3-8B, Gemma 4 12B, Qwen3.6-35B-A3B (thinking on/off), Tencent hy3, and frontier API models Kimi K3 / GLM 5.3; and on WebArena-Lite (165 tasks split 48 dev / 117 test, σ≈0.045, resolvable 0.064) with Qwen3.5-9B.

## Results & Highlights

- **Harness evolution gains, where the taxonomy places them.** On DeepPlanning held-out tasks, the evolved harness lifts Qwen3.5-4B from 0.164 to 0.297 (+0.133, vs a 0.035 noise floor) and Qwen3.5-9B from 0.323 to 0.442 (+0.119); on dev anchors the lines moved 0.156→0.292 (+0.136, 13 rounds) and 0.305→0.390 (+0.085, 5 rounds). The 4B gain is delivery-led: delivery 55%→90% (held-out) and F3 loop failures 64.1%→37.0% of trajectories, while F4 planning capacity is never reduced (9.9%→28.1%); on 9B delivery was already 96%→98% and loops fell 38.9%→16.7%, with F4 staying ~25% as the largest residual class.
- **LoRA internalizes the harness gain — with the placebo proving it.** Trained on evolved-harness trajectories and evaluated under the seed harness, the 4B qkvo adapter reaches 0.290 held-out vs 0.164 for the base (+0.126) and the 9B qkvo+MLP adapter 0.454 vs 0.323 (+0.131); the 9B placebo adapter scores 0.175 dev / 0.152 held-out vs 0.333/0.379 for the base — 0.158 and 0.227 below — while running 52–65 agent rounds per trajectory instead of 19. On the evolved harness the adapters stack on 4B (+0.041 dev / +0.082 held-out, reaching 0.379) but substitute on 9B (adapter 0.449 vs harness-alone 0.442, within noise); full-parameter fine-tuning on the second textbook adds a further +0.044 (4B) / +0.042 (9B) held-out over LoRA.
- **Each adapter moves exactly its assigned failure class.** The 4B adapter reproduces the harness's behavioral signature (delivery 52%→78% on dev, F3 down 18–19 points, F4 unchanged), while the 9B adapter cuts the content class F4 from 23%→7% (dev) and 28%→5% (held-out), with freed trajectories becoming healthy (34%→46%, 28%→53%).
- **WebArena-Lite: the gain transfers with the same shape, but stays in the harness.** The same loop on Qwen3.5-9B moved the dev anchor 0.234→0.375 (13 rounds, 10 edits; one interface edit keeping the two most recent full pages in context accounts for +0.12) and test (117 unseen tasks) 0.256→0.344 (+0.088), delivery 49%→77%, healthy 23%→34%, and undelivered failures stopped on repeated/unparseable actions fell from 165/237 to 13/108 — while accuracy among delivered answers was unchanged within noise (0.47→0.44). Adapters trained on the 157 full-score closing trajectories did not stack: 0.327/0.331 under both harnesses, within the 0.064 line of their base cells (0.216/0.251 seed, 0.375/0.396 evolved), with delivery up to 81%/88% but accuracy among delivered falling to 0.39/0.37 — gains from edits to what the model sees stay in the harness.
- **The rule predicts all nine lines.** Where the seed harness delivers plans scoring ≥0.27 among delivered and F4 is not the largest class (five lines, 4B through Tencent hy3), the harness lever paid +0.063 to +0.136 on dev in four lines and +0.053 in the fifth (within its own noise); where delivered-plan quality is 0.13–0.17 (MiniCPM5-2B, Spark-X2.5-4B, Ministral-3-8B), it paid nothing (e.g., MiniCPM5-2B composite 0.106→0.110, with quality among delivered actually dropping 0.173→0.156 as delivery rose 61%→71%); Gemma 4 12B (+0.019, within noise; delivery 98%, F4 40% largest class) is correctly routed to the weights. Frontier spot-checks: Kimi K3 gains +0.058/+0.049 (dev/held-out) under the evolved harness; GLM 5.3 reaches 0.512/0.488 (no bare control).
- **Component ablations: capability grants carry the gain, enforcement does not.** On 9B, removing the four hard blocks shifts score by −0.017/−0.002 (within noise); removing the evolved lessons file costs −0.049 dev / −0.076 held-out; removing the ledger (with its dependent gate and checkpoint) costs −0.088/−0.145. On 4B, removing evolved tools costs −0.067/−0.120 and lessons −0.067/−0.120, while the exit gate costs 0.000/−0.077 (holding held-out delivery, which drops 88%→75%).

## Authors & Credibility

- Yuan Tian and Bing Hu (equal contribution) are independent researchers; Hao Wang is at UC Berkeley, Binghang Lu at Purdue, and Fang Wu at Stanford. The mix of independent and academic affiliations is unusual but the work shows heavy engineering maturity: a 13-round logged evolution run, same-night paired evaluations, and a measured noise floor.
- The paper is a preprint under review (8 Oct 2026). Credibility is boosted by unusually careful measurement hygiene: fresh anchors after every accept, a formal selection-optimism bound, a placebo control for the training claim, and a whole appendix (G) documenting their own measurement pitfalls (winner's curse, ratchet slippage, inert accepts) plus Appendix I auditing the benchmark's LLM plan-to-JSON converter (~0.025 average score inflation, 0.09 on the 5 hardest answers).
- Caveat on provenance: the frontier API cells (Kimi K3, GLM 5.3) were run by a co-author on a separate machine; the paper states that per-run readings, run identifiers, serving settings, and the harness-checkpoint mapping are not yet in the repository, and those cells carry no noise estimate.

## Critical Assessment

- **The 4B "gain" is mostly delivery, not better plans.** By the paper's own Equation 2 decomposition, the 4B evolution gain is Δr-driven (delivery 59%→88% while quality among delivered moves only 0.266→0.332, contributing 0.077 of the 0.136 dev gain). The composite rewards an agent that submits any parsable plan; on WebArena-Lite accuracy among delivered actually fell 0.47→0.44 while the headline +0.088 was banked. The authors are transparent about this, but readers should parse "stronger agent" as "agent that finishes and submits more often" for much of the 4B line.
- **F4 is a residual class by construction.** "Planning capability" is defined as none of F1–F3 firing — so the claim "the loop stops at F4" partly follows from what the checker can see. The thresholds (≥3 repeats, 90% budget, ≥10 blocked calls, unbacked-claim ratio ≥0.5) are hand-set and no sensitivity analysis of the taxonomy labels to these cutoffs is provided; the causal story rests on the ablations and the 9B multi-draft anecdote (25.5%→25.0% never edited) rather than on an intervention that surfaces the failure composition to the proposer — that fix is only proposed (and tried once, unsuccessfully, on Gemma 4 12B), not demonstrated.
- **The placebo control exists only for 9B, and several cells fall below the paper's own noise standard.** The 4B component-ablation cells were run with 2 rollouts (0.05 resolvable line, not 0.035), the Table 1 base rows under the evolved harness used 2 rollouts and were measured two nights earlier, and the WebArena adapters were measured the night after their base cells. All are disclosed, but the sharpest stacking claims (+0.041/+0.082) sit close to these relaxed thresholds.
- **Missing baselines and comparisons.** There is no matched-budget test-time-search baseline (Wang et al., 2026b) on DeepPlanning — i.e., no answer to whether 13 rounds × 4 rollouts of evaluation compute spent on harness search beats spending the same compute on more samples for the frozen agent. The frontier GLM 5.3 cell has no bare control at all, so its 0.512/0.488 cannot be read as a harness gain.
- **Generalization is narrower than the framing suggests.** The two deep lines are both Qwen3.5; the cross-family sweep is one loop per small model, and the three sub-8B models are precisely the ones where the rule says "harness lever pays nothing" (seed healthy share 0–6%). The rule is validated, but mostly on models that could already deliver plans.
- **Absolute scores carry a known bias.** Every DeepPlanning number passes through an LLM plan-to-JSON converter the authors show disagrees with faithful conversion by ~0.025 on average (and 0.09 on hard answers); they convert all cells identically, which preserves deltas, but absolute levels (e.g., "0.30 held-out") are soft.

## Strengths

- The question — which lever, when — is the right one, and the paper answers it with a falsifiable rule rather than a vibes-based "harnesses help" claim.
- Measurement discipline is exemplary for the agent-eval space: a measured noise floor, same-night paired comparisons, fresh anchors, a formal selection-optimism bound (Proposition 1), and a placebo adapter that alone (0.175/0.152 vs 0.333/0.379) demolishes "training on any trajectories helps" interpretations.
- The taxonomy-by-lever design makes the central claim testable: the harness removes F2/F3 and cannot touch F4 (4B F4 9.9%→28.1%), and each LoRA adapter then moves exactly the failure class the rule assigns it (4B: delivery/F3; 9B: F4 28%→5% held-out) — a clean cross-over validation.
- Honest negative results: three of nine model lines where the harness lever paid nothing are reported in as much detail as the successes; the WebArena adapters' failure to stack is presented, not hidden; Appendix G documents the authors' own measurement bugs.
- Practically actionable: the diagnose-then-intervene rule needs only a deterministic scorer, process signals, and one noise measurement — no new infrastructure — and the two-textbook result (which adapter placement wins follows what the textbook has to teach) is a useful design hint for trajectory training.

## Limitations

- Two benchmarks only, and DeepPlanning is a single domain (travel planning) with a programmatic scorer; the taxonomy's portability to domains without hard constraints or process signals is untested (the paper itself notes applying it needs trajectory-level process signals plus a task-level scorer).
- Harness gains on 4B are largely delivery-rate gains; quality among delivered plans improves modestly (0.266→0.332), and on WebArena among-delivered accuracy slightly degrades — so "internalized gains" partly mean more submissions, not smarter answers.
- Placebo control only on 9B; 2-rollout ablation cells; two cross-night comparisons; LLM converter bias ~0.025 baked into all absolute scores; no matched-budget search baseline; frontier-API cells without controls or noise estimates.
- F4 as a residual category with hand-tuned checker thresholds; no sensitivity analysis; the proposed fix (surfacing failure composition to the proposer) is demonstrated to fail once (Gemma 4 12B) and never to succeed.
- Training data is small (82–98 trajectories from 48 dev tasks) and held-out evaluation is at the task level within the same distribution; cross-domain transfer of the trained adapters is not measured.

## Relevance

该文与你在 LLM agent 自进化/自动化 agent 设计方向的兴趣高度契合：它把"evolve harness 还是 train weights"这个当前热门问题落到一个可执行的失败构成诊断规则上，并给出了带安慰剂对照和噪声底线的罕见严谨评估。对任何在做 agent harness 进化（AHE/ADAS 一类）或轨迹微调（AgentTuning 一类）的人，这是一篇能直接改变实验设计的必读方法论论文。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- What happens when the fix is actually applied — does surfacing the scorer's per-check failure composition to the proposer (as tried once on Gemma 4 12B, unsuccessfully) ever restart harness gains past F4, and on which model classes?
- The two levers interact differently by size (stack on 4B, substitute on 9B): is the switching point predictable from the seed failure composition alone, or does it require training a probe adapter first?
- On WebArena-Lite the gain lived in what the model sees (context construction) and training captured none of it — can "what the model sees" gains ever be internalized, e.g., by training on state-representation transformations rather than assistant outputs?
- Would a matched-budget test-time-search baseline (sampling more rollouts and selecting) beat 13 rounds of harness search on DeepPlanning, given each round costs 4 dev-set rollouts per candidate?
- How sensitive are the F1–F4 labels to the checker thresholds (≥3 repeats, 90% budget, ≥10 blocked calls, claim-ratio ≥0.5), and can the taxonomy be defined for benchmarks without deterministic scorers?
