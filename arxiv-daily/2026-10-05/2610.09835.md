> **一句话总结：** 通过训练时逐组冻结参数做因果定位，作者发现数据免费（data-free）持续预训练下的灾难性遗忘几乎全部集中在"新语料中从未出现过的低频词的输出嵌入"上—— softmax 对缺席 token 施加持久的单向负梯度，而 Adam 的 √v̄ 归一化将其放大为满步长更新；仅把输出投影的 Adam ε 提高到 1×10⁻⁴，就在 160M–12B、四个模型家族的七个稳定设置中消除 39.4%–67.9% 的遗忘且不损失目标域学习，配合 replay 在 Qwen/韩语上达 79.8%。

> **TL;DR:** In data-free continual pre-training (e.g., Pythia/Qwen2.5-0.5B/TinyLlama/OLMo-2 adapting to Korean with a 40M-token budget), forgetting is causally localized to the output embeddings of tokens absent from the new corpus: freezing the lowest √v̄ output band removes 31.3–51.0% of forgetting with ≤0.1% learning change, because softmax applies persistent one-sided gradients −p·h to never-targeted rows and Adam's √v̄ normalization turns them into full-size updates. The defense is one line — set ε=1×10⁻⁴ on the output projection only — which removes 39.4–67.9% of forgetting across seven stable settings with no target-learning loss, beats every replay-free baseline (LoRA r=16 only removes 12.0% at −27.1% learning; KL-to-base β=1 removes 96.5% but costs −95.3% learning), composes with replay to 79.8% on Qwen/Korean, and works only if applied during training (post-hoc edits recover <5%).

## 核心贡献

- **因果定位（而非相关性）**：用训练时冻结参数组到预训练值的"causal share"协议，证明遗忘集中在低 √v̄ 档的输出嵌入上——冻结该档在五个设置中消除 31.3–51.0% 的遗忘（目标学习偏移 ≤ +0.1%），冻结同档输入嵌入仅有 −1.5% 到 +1.4% 的噪声级效果；冻结低档 body 最多只消除 1.2–4.6%。冻结高档 body 反而消除 8.8–40.7% 的目标学习——遗忘与学习占据不相交的参数区域。
- **机制解释**：新语料中缺席的 token 作为 target 从未出现，只在 softmax 负梯度 −pᵢh 中被持续推压；这些梯度方向一致、无出现事件稀释，Adam 的 ˆm/√v̂ 把它们放大为接近满学习率的更新。默认 ε=1×10⁻⁸ 挡不住：输出嵌入坐标的中位数比 body 低 2–3 个数量级。
- **一行干预**：仅对输出投影设 ε=1×10⁻⁴。八设置网格（Pythia 160M–12B、Qwen2.5-0.5B、TinyLlama-1.1B、OLMo-2-1B × 韩语/代码/数学/Alpaca）中七个稳定设置消除 39.4–67.9% 的遗忘，目标学习变化 +0.1% 到 +10.9%，无需逐模型调参，与 replay 近似独立可加（Qwen/韩语：组合 79.8% vs 独立性预测 78.0%；OLMo 上甚至超可加 74.4% vs 64.8%）。
- **边界条件（诚实划界）**：干预必须在训练中进行——事后编辑漂移行最多恢复 +4.6%，全量移除使目标学习恶化 −7.7% 至 −133.6%；语料不引入新词汇时（Alpaca 内分布微调），因果份额移到 body，ε 防线失效（unified 模型上 −3.7% 到 −1.6%）。
- **风险预诊断**：仅凭 tokenizer 统计（旧域 token 在新语料中计数 <1,000 的占比，"vocabulary deficiency"）即可在共享词表的模型间预测遗忘的排名顺序，提前决定是否需要干预。

## Key Contributions

- **Causal localization by training-time freezing**: partitioning parameters into input embeddings / output embeddings / body across √v̄ bands shows forgetting concentrates in the low-√v̄ output band (31.3–51.0% reduction across five settings at ≤ +0.1% learning shift), while input-embedding freezes in the same band are noise (−1.5% to +1.4%) and mass-matched body freezes yield only 0.4–12.3% — location, not parameter count or regularization, governs forgetting.
- **Mechanism**: absent output-token rows receive a persistent one-sided softmax gradient −pᵢh at every step; Adam's coordinate-wise ˆm/√(v̂+ε) drives these small, sign-consistent gradients to the full learning rate, and the default ε=1×10⁻⁸ cannot brake them because median output coordinates sit 2–3 orders of magnitude below body coordinates.
- **Output-scoped ε**: raising ε to 1×10⁻⁴ only on the output projection removes 39.4–67.9% of forgetting across seven stable settings spanning 160M–12B parameters and four model families, with target learning preserved (+0.1% to +10.9%), no per-model tuning, and approximate additivity with replay (79.8% combined on Qwen/Korean vs 78.0% predicted by independence).
- **Boundary conditions**: the intervention must act during training (post-hoc drift edits recover ≤ +4.6% and full removal degrades target learning by −7.7% to −133.6%); when the corpus introduces no new vocabulary (Alpaca-style in-distribution tuning), the causal share shifts to the body and the ε dial fails there (−3.7% to −1.6% on unified models).
- **A data-only, rank-only risk diagnostic**: "vocabulary deficiency" (share of old-domain token positions with new-corpus count < 1,000) predicts the forgetting rank order across injections without any training.

## Methodology

**Setting.** A base model θ_base is adapted to a new corpus D_new (Korean, code, math, Alpaca, KoAlpaca) with zero access to D_old — a strictly data-free regime. Models: Pythia 160M/410M/1.4B/6.9B/12B, Qwen2.5-0.5B, TinyLlama-1.1B, OLMo-2-1B. Budgets: 40M tokens for pre-training injection, 12.6M for instruction tuning, extended to 160M/400M in budget checks; AdamW throughout; 3 seeds per arm, expanded to 6 where seed disagreement was large (e.g., Pythia-410M).

**Metrics.** Forgetting = mean nats increase over held-out old-domain corpora (English+math for Korean injection; English+math+code for instruction tuning); learn = new-domain loss decrease; reduction = 1 − forget_int/forget_ref; learning change relative to reference. All comparisons are budget-matched.

**Causal-share protocol.** Freeze one (site, √v̄ band) pair at a time at pre-trained values, with mass-matched control freezes to separate location from parameter count. Ties in learning rates are broken by peak target learning with tie-breaks toward lower rates; arms judged by preserving learning when baseline learn remains within seed variance.

**Mechanism analysis.** Derives the softmax gradient ∂L/∂wᵢ = (pᵢ − 1[t=i])h showing absent tokens only ever receive −pᵢh; connects to Adam's effective step ˆm/√(v̂+ε) (sign-consistent gradients ⇒ ˆm ≈ g, √v̂ ≈ |g|); measures per-row drift over 120 steps (displacement directionality 0.67–0.90 in the lowest band); SVD of the output-embedding displacement shows a top-1 shared direction carrying 66.1% (Qwen/Korean), 72.6% (OLMo), 80.8% (Pythia-1.4B/math) of absent-row drift energy vs 3.2–12.9% in size-matched high-exposure controls.

**Intervention and equivalents.** ε=1×10⁻⁴ scoped to the output projection in optimizer parameter groups; shown equivalent (within ~1.5 pp across six settings) to freezing under-exposed rows (c(t) < 1,000) and to step-wise gating. Pre-retraining diagnostic: vocabulary deficiency computed purely by tokenizing both corpora with the base tokenizer.

**Baselines (Qwen/Korean, replay-free).** L2-to-base weight penalty (λ=10/30/100), KL-to-base (β=0.1/1/10), constant low learning rate, bottom-layer freezing (6 blocks), LoRA r=16; replay composed at 1% where allowed.

## Results & Highlights

- **Output-scoped ε across the grid (Table 5, Korean, replay-free, 40M tokens)**: forgetting reduction 67.9% on Qwen2.5-0.5B (0.629 → 0.202 nats), 65.3% on Pythia-12B, 60.2% on Pythia-6.9B, 48.1% on TinyLlama-1.1B, 41.8% on Pythia-1.4B, 39.4% on OLMo-2-1B — with learning changes between +0.1% and +5.4%; Pythia-410M reads 43.8% but is bimodal across seeds (32.7% and −7.0% at six seeds), and Pythia-160M collapses at its peak rate and is excluded.
- **No replay-free baseline reaches the "free point" (Table A6, Qwen/Korean)**: output-only ε achieves 69.5% reduction at +0.4% learning change, whereas LoRA r=16 removes only 12.0% at −27.1% learning; L2-to-base at λ=100 gets 64.3% but costs −15.4% learning; KL-to-base at β=1 hits 96.5% reduction only by destroying −95.3% of learning; bottom-layer freeze is −2.5% reduction at −11.4% learning.
- **LoRA's retention is the released head (Appendix A.9, Qwen/Korean)**: at the adapter rate, forgetting is 0.121 nats with the head frozen; releasing the output head alone spikes it 23-fold to 2.820 nats, and output-scoped ε removes 91.1% of that forgetting at the highest target learning of the three arms (TinyLlama replicates: 16.3-fold, 92.4% removed).
- **Composition with replay**: on Qwen/Korean, replay 1% + ε yields 79.8% reduction vs 78.0% predicted by independence (ε's own share stays 69.5% vs 72.1% with replay); on OLMo the combination is super-additive (74.4% vs 64.8% predicted).
- **Forgetting follows vocabulary deficiency, not training mode (Table 3)**: at matched models/budget, reference forgetting tracks deficiency — Pythia-1.4B: math (deficiency 7.7%) forgets 0.0656 nats vs Korean (45.7%) 0.4340; and reduction is decorrelated from deficiency (e.g., Qwen code: 72.7% reduction at +18.4% learning change vs Korean 67.9% at +5.4%).
- **Post-hoc repair fails (Table 7)**: sweeping the shared-drift removal α across three settings recovers at most +4.6%, while full removal degrades target learning from −7.7% (Pythia-1.4B) to −133.6% (OLMo); editing earlier checkpoints moves best recovery only from −1.7% to +2.5%.
- **Budget strengthens, not weakens, the defense**: at a fixed 160M-token budget, reduction on Pythia grows from 53.9% (40M) to 62.3% (160M) at 6.9B and 76.2% to 84.1% at 12B (Table A15); a tenfold budget check (Pythia-410M/code, 400M tokens) shows 56.2% reduction with +10.1% target learning.
- **Capability check**: on Pythia-1.4B across LAMBADA/HellaSwag/PIQA/ARC-easy/WinoGrande, ε=1×10⁻⁵ averages 58.14 vs 56.05 for default ε (untouched base 60.31), agreeing with the loss-based number within 0.2 points.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty is real but narrower than the framing suggests.** The mechanism trace (absent rows get one-sided softmax gradients that Adam amplifies) is a clean causal story, and the ε intervention is operationally trivial in a good way. But the key ingredients are known: Yu et al. (2022) rare-token gating and the embedding-degeneration literature already identified rare output embeddings as the damage site, and the authors themselves show their ε dial "matches" rare-token gating (69.5% vs 70.2% on Qwen, Table A6). The advance is the causal-freezing protocol, the equivalence of three instruments, and the scaling/budget generality — not the discovery of the site.
- **The headline range 39.4–67.9% is not uniform, and the paper says so in the fine print.** The Pythia-410M arm is bimodal (32.7% vs −7.0% across six seeds at the same configuration), and Pythia-160M suffers *total collapse* (3.1 nats baseline forgetting) where the intervention reads −22.1% at 3×10⁻³. "Seven stable settings" excludes exactly the unstable ones; a practitioner with a small model cannot know in advance which regime they are in.
- **An unexplained anomaly sits in the middle of the story.** Freezing attention on KoAlpaca removes 42.7% of forgetting at 410M (vs 29.8% for output ε) and 30.2% at 1.4B, at negligible learning cost — the authors concede "our claim is specific" and defer whether attention holds an independent share to an appendix question. That is honest, but it means the "output embeddings are where forgetting lives" headline does not fully cover instruction tuning, one of the two settings users care about.
- **Evaluation is loss-only for the main grid, at small budgets.** 40M tokens (12.6M for instruction tuning) is far below realistic continual pre-training, and forgetting is measured in nats over small held-out pools. The capability benchmark check exists but only at two Pythia checkpoints, at ε=1×10⁻⁵ (a weaker setting than the grid's 1×10⁻⁴) and under 1% replay — the paper admits the check "predates the grid." Benchmark-side evidence at the actual ε=1×10⁻⁴ grid setting is missing.
- **The risk diagnostic underperforms its pre-registered bar.** Spearman 0.60 vs the registered 0.7, absolute-magnitude prediction failed, and cross-tokenizer transfer failed (OLMo's per-token Korean loss looks deceptively familiar until byte normalization reveals 1.93 vs 1.35–1.74). The diagnostic is honestly marketed as rank-only, but this limits its practical screening value.
- **Scaling claims are Pythia-only** (three checkpoints, plus the capability check at 1×10⁻⁵), and the LoRA ablation uses a single rank (r=16) on two base models — the "23-fold forgetting from head release" number may be rank- and rate-dependent (they show rank constraints freeze-only converges to parity at tiny learning rates).
- **Reproducibility looks good**: tiny budgets, public checkpoints (Pythia/OLMo open; Qwen/TinyLlama public), 3 seeds, explicit rate-selection rules, and an appendix dense enough to re-derive most cells. No code release is mentioned, which is the main practical gap.

## Strengths

- A genuinely causal identification method (train-time freezing with mass-matched controls) replacing correlational drift metrics that have produced conflicting localization claims in prior work.
- One-line, optimizer-level defense with no architecture change, no data requirement, no per-model tuning: reduction is flat across a hundredfold ε span at 1.4B (40.9%–45.6%) and a single standard learning-rate sweep suffices.
- Strong mechanism chain: gradient derivation → drift-directionality measurement (0.67–0.90) → SVD top-1 shared direction (66–81% energy vs 3–13% controls) → ε sensitivity — each link independently measurable.
- Rare honesty: registered failures, a named anomaly (attention column) they refuse to explain away, seed-level bimodality disclosed in table captions, and explicit "where this fails" boundaries (in-distribution corpora, post-hoc repair, non-Adam optimizers like Muon).
- Practical decision rule that unifies prior remedies under one variable (vocabulary deficiency): rare-token gating ≈ ε; bottom-layer freezing fails because output dominates; weight interpolation degrades because it seals drift and compensation together.

## Limitations

- Requires a coordinate-wise adaptive optimizer (Adam/AdamW family); Muon-style optimizers are explicitly out of scope.
- In-distribution tuning (no new vocabulary, e.g., Alpaca) shifts the causal share to the body, where ε either fails (−3.7% to −1.6% on unified models) or costs learning; replay and rate scaling remain the remedies there.
- Reduction is not uniform: Pythia-410M bimodality means "cost-free mitigation" describes most runs, not all; the 160M model collapses outright at its peak rate.
- Absolute forgetting magnitude is not predictable by the diagnostic (rank order only), and cross-tokenizer calibration fails — the tool cannot compare models with different tokenizers.
- Small training budgets (40M/12.6M tokens) and loss-based evaluation over a modest four-domain eval pool; benchmark-verified capability checks are limited to two Pythia checkpoints at a weaker ε setting.
- Scaling analysis restricted to Pythia checkpoints; adapter study at one rank (r=16) on two models; no code release mentioned.

## Relevance

该文与你关注的"LLM 持续预训练/领域适配下的灾难性遗忘"方向高度相关：它把遗忘的成因归结为一个可一行修复的优化器配置问题（输出投影 ε），并给出了数据侧的预诊断指标，对任何不打算混入 replay 数据的继续训练流程都有直接可操作的价值；同时其对 LoRA"遗忘更少"机制的解剖（释放输出头才是遗忘来源）对参数高效微调实践也是重要提醒。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does output-scoped ε hold for modern MoE models and non-AdamW optimizers (Muon, SOAP), where the coordinate-wise second-moment argument breaks?
- Can the attention-freeze anomaly on KoAlpaca (42.7% at 410M) be reconciled with — or does it falsify — the output-dominates claim under a fair per-parameter-matched comparison?
- Does the intervention hold at realistic continual pre-training budgets (billions of tokens) with benchmark-verified capability retention at the actual grid ε=1×10⁻⁴, rather than the pre-grid 1×10⁻⁵ check?
- Is the Pythia-410M seed bimodality an optimization instability of that specific checkpoint, or a general small-model phenomenon that predicts when the ε dial silently fails?
- Can the rank-only vocabulary-deficiency diagnostic be extended to a cross-tokenizer, magnitude-level predictor (e.g., via byte-normalized loss proxies) without violating the data-free constraint?
