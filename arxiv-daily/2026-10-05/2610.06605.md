> **一句话总结：** 作者从零训练 4.8M 参数的 Llama 式 Transformer 做 4×4 乘法（单次前向、无思维链），发现在数字之间插入空格分隔符可将精确匹配率从 1% 提升到 89%（三个种子 89%/80%/78%，而紧凑格式仅 1%）；并通过 PCA、diffusion map 与匹配激活修补（matched activation patching）证明模型在"预测槽位"（prediction slot）处把每列进位（carry-in）编码为残差流中一个环上的角度，且该表征在第 4 层后被因果地使用——修补槽位可按源进位翻转输出数字（95–100% 的情况），进位先在相邻答案槽位组装再被中继到自身槽位。

> **TL;DR:** Training a 4.8M-param Llama-style transformer from scratch on answer-only 4×4 multiplication, simply inserting a space token between digits raises exact-match accuracy from 1% (compact format) to 89% (spaced format, best of three seeds), without any chain of thought. In the models that succeed, each output column's carry-in γc is encoded as an angle on a ring in the residual stream at the "prediction slot" (the space token just before that column's digit): activation patching that source-target pairs matched on column sum S_c shows this state is causally used (patching column 3's slot reaches acc_src = 0.84 after block 4, vs ~0.10 with unmatched patching), columns 4–5 first assemble the carry at the neighboring answer slot before it reaches its own (relay via acc_src = 0.36–0.39 at blocks 2–3), and residual errors at convergence are almost always ±1. Positions are learned in carry-chain order — 0,7 first, then 1,6, then 2,5, with the middle columns (3,4) never converging in the compact format.

## 核心贡献

- **格式决定可学性：** 同一个 4.8M 参数模型（Llama 式：RMSNorm + RoPE + SwiGLU，4 头注意力、隐藏维 704、6 层），仅改变输入格式——数字之间加空格 token（spaced）vs 紧凑（compact）——在 4×4 乘法（答案输出、8 位零填充）上精确匹配率从 1% 升到 89%，证明"分隔符使进位传播变得可学"。
- **位置按进位链顺序学习：** 逐位置训练误差显示外围位置先收敛（0,7 → 1,6 → 2,5 → 3,4），与乘法结构对应（位置 0/7 只依赖一对输入数字，位置 3/4 依赖全部四对且进位最大）；compact 格式中间位置永不收敛，且"半成品"模型是干净地解掉几个位置、卡死在其余位置，而非全线模糊。
- **进位的几何编码：** 训练后数字 0–9 的嵌入呈按值排序的圆环（空间 token 位于圆心，作为输入不携带数值）；每个输出列的预测槽位在残差流主成分中形成自己的环状云，环上的角度编码该列进位 γc：进位数 k=0 时是短弧（γ=0），k=7 时近乎闭合圆环，中间 k 插值；diffusion map 显示第 3 层后点按 carry-in 排序，第 5 层后环消解、按输出数字可解码——进位"变成"了答案数字。
- **因果证据（匹配激活修补）：** 在按列和 S_c 匹配的源-目标对上修补预测槽位：第 3 列在 block 2/3/4 后 acc_src 分别为 0.46/0.67/0.84（不匹配修补仅 ~0.10）；在 block 4 修补进位差 Δ，模型在 95–100% 的情况下输出 (d_base − Δ) mod 10（第 3 列，第 4–6 列仅部分转移）。
- **中继结构与误差模式：** 边界列 1、7 在 block 1 即达平台（0.78 与 1.00），内部列 2–6 靠后；对第 4 列，block 2–3 时修补效应位于前一高阶列的槽位（acc_src 0.36–0.39），block 4 才移到自身槽位——进位先在相邻答案槽位组装再被中继。收敛后残差误差集中在位置 3、4 且几乎总是 ±1，与"进位估计差一个单位"或圆环上的小角度误差一致。

## Key Contributions

- **Format is the decisive variable:** a 4.8M-param Llama-style decoder (RMSNorm, RoPE, SwiGLU, 4 heads, hidden 704, 6 layers) trained from scratch on answer-only 4×4 multiplication (operands zero-padded to 8 digits) reaches 89% exact match with a space token between digits vs 1% without — no chain of thought — showing the failure of single-pass multiplication is input-format brittleness, not an absence of an explicit carry mechanism.
- **Positions are learned in carry-chain order:** per-position log error converges outside-in (0,7 → 1,6 → 2,5 → 3,4), mirroring the dependency structure (positions 0 and 7 depend on a single input-digit pair, positions 3 and 4 on all four pairs, which also produce the largest carries); in the compact format the middle positions never converge, and an intermediate-accuracy model is "halfway done" in a discrete sense — several positions solved cleanly, the rest stuck.
- **The carry-in is an angle on a ring:** after training, digit embeddings form a circle ordered by value with the space token at the center; each output column's prediction slot (the space token just before that column's digit) forms its own ring-shaped cloud whose angle encodes carry-in γc — short arcs at carry count k = 0 (γc = 0), nearly closed rings at k = 7 — and diffusion maps show the ring is ordered by carry-in at block 3 but dissolves into a digit-decodable shape by block 5.
- **Causal use via matched activation patching:** patching the prediction slot on source-target pairs matched on column sum S_c transfers the source carry-in (column 3: acc_src = 0.46 / 0.67 / 0.84 after blocks 2 / 3 / 4; unmatched patching collapses to ~0.10); patching the carry difference Δ at block 4 makes the model emit (d_base − Δ) mod 10 in 95–100% of cases (column 3; columns 4–6 only partially, their carry still held at a neighbor).
- **Relay, not accumulation:** boundary columns 1 and 7 reach their plateau (0.78 and 1.00) by block 1, while for interior columns (e.g. column 4) the patching effect at blocks 2–3 sits at the preceding higher-order column's slot (acc_src = 0.36–0.39) before moving to the column's own slot at block 4 — the carry is assembled at the neighboring answer slot and relayed. Remaining errors concentrate at positions 3–4 and are almost always ±1, consistent with a one-unit carry error or a small angular error on the digit circle.

## Methodology

- **Model and data.** A Llama-style decoder transformer (RMSNorm, RoPE, SwiGLU, tied embeddings) with hidden size 704, 4 attention heads, 6 layers — 4.8M non-embedding parameters. Training data: 1M examples of 4×4 digit multiplication with operands zero-padded to eight digits, no chain of thought (the answer is emitted directly). Under uniform sampling, high-carry examples are rare, so the training distribution is skewed so that every carry count k ∈ {0,…,7} (number of output columns receiving a nonzero carry) is well represented. Two input formats are compared: **spaced** (a space token between consecutive digits, MSB-first, e.g. `0 1 2 3 w 4 5 6 w = w 0 0 0 5 6 w 8`) and **compact** (no spaces). With CoT the task reaches 100% in 13k steps, but then the computation is written in the output, so the paper studies the answer-only setting.
- **Training and evaluation.** AdamW (lr 3e-4, weight decay 0.1) with a cosine schedule and 200 warmup steps, batch size 128, 100K training steps, weights initialized N(0, 0.02²). The main spaced configuration is trained with three seeds (89%, 80%, 78% exact match); evaluation uses 2K held-out examples, reporting exact-match accuracy and per-position log error log₂(|pᵢ − gᵢ| + 1).
- **Hidden-state analysis.** The residual stream h^(ℓ)(p) is recorded after every block ℓ ∈ {0,…,5} at every token position; within each layer, a PCA block captures global structure and diffusion maps capture curved (ring-like) structure. Dependence on carry count k is probed by forming groups of examples with fixed k and fitting separate PCAs per group; representational analysis uses seed 10.
- **Matched activation patching.** Examples are partitioned into source (carry-in γ*c = c_c) and target (γc ≠ c_c) groups; for each target, the individual source example whose column sum S*c is closest to the target's is selected (an exact match in 90–96% of cases across columns tested), which fixes the only other quantity that determines the correct digit. For each block ℓ and position p, the target's hidden state is replaced with the matched source's, the forward pass continues, and acc_src (Eq. 2) measures the fraction of examples whose digit becomes the one implied by the source carry. Matching is essential: patching an unmatched source gives acc_src ≈ 0.10 vs 0.85–0.95 with matching (column 4, carry 5→10, all layers patched); patching the last block is a sanity check (the slot's state alone determines the output). To test whether the slot carries the carry *value*, the difference Δ = γ_target − γ_source is patched at block 4, leaving the last block free to read the rest of the sequence; if the slot holds the carry, the output digit should become (d_base − Δ) mod 10 (Eq. 1).

## Results & Highlights

- **89% vs 1% exact match from format alone** on 4×4 answer-only multiplication trained to convergence on 1M examples: the spaced format reaches 89% (best of three seeds; the three seeds reach 89%, 80%, and 78%), while the compact format reaches only 1% (Table 1) — a +88-point delta attributable purely to inserting space tokens between digits.
- **Per-position learning order:** in the spaced format, positions converge in carry-chain order — outer positions 0 and 7 first, then 1 and 6, then 2 and 5, with the middle positions 3–4 (dependent on all four input-digit pairs) last — while in the compact format the middle positions never converge even at 200K steps (Figure 3), matching Figure 4's dependency structure.
- **Patching the prediction slot is causal for the carry:** for column 3, patching the slot raises acc_src from 0.05–0.11 (blocks 0–1) to 0.46 after block 2, 0.67 after block 3, and 0.84 after block 4, with the effect near zero when any other position is patched; matched patching reaches 0.85–0.95 (column 4, all layers) versus ~0.10 for unmatched patching, and patching every block is a ~100% sanity check.
- **The slot holds the carry value, not just a correlated state:** patching the carry difference Δ at block 4 (column 3) makes the model output (d_base − Δ) mod 10 in 95–100% of cases for every Δ tested (Figure 8); for columns 4–6 the shift is only partial at block 4, consistent with their carry still being held at the neighboring slot — and for column 4, patching its own slot has no effect up to block 3 while the effect sits at the preceding higher-order column's slot with acc_src = 0.36–0.39 at blocks 2–3 (relay). Boundary columns 1 and 7 reach their plateaus (0.78 and 1.00) by block 1 in all three seeds (Figure 9).
- **Geometry tracks the carry:** prediction slots form short arcs when every carry-in is zero (e.g. 1021×9379 = 9569559 → all γ = 0) and nearly closed rings when carry-ins vary widely (e.g. 5176×9739); by block 3 the diffusion map orders points around the ring by carry-in, while at block 5 the ring dissolves into a shape decodable by output digit (Figures 5–6). At convergence, residual errors concentrate at positions 3 and 4 and are almost always off by exactly ±1 (Figure 10).

## Critical Assessment

- **The 89% headline is the best of three seeds with visible variance.** The three spaced seeds reach 89%, 80%, and 78%, so the headline number sits at the top of a >10-point spread; the compact baseline, in contrast, is a **single run**, so the 89-vs-1 contrast — while almost certainly real given the qualitative training-dynamics differences — is not seed-controlled on the compact side, and the paper says so only in the Limitations.
- **Scope is a single, very small task and architecture.** Everything is 4×4 multiplication with one tokenization, one model size (4.8M params), six layers; the paper honestly notes this, but it leaves open whether the "separator creates a free prediction slot" mechanism is what rescues multi-digit arithmetic at realistic scales, or an artifact of tiny models. The plausible explanation offered for *why* separators help (the compact format's hidden state must both represent a digit and predict the next one) is explicitly left unverified — disentangling it from extra computation positions or column alignment is future work.
- **Causality is sufficiency-to-flip-the-digit, not necessity or location of computation.** Patching measures whether a single site is *sufficient* to change the digit, not whether information is present elsewhere; and because source-target pairs are matched on column sum S_c, a successful patch cannot separate carrying γc from the column result S_c + γc mod 10 — the authors concede that pairs with differing modulo-10 results would be needed. For columns 4–6, patching the slot alone transfers the carry only partially, which the paper transparently reports rather than overclaiming.
- **No circuit-level account.** The attention heads that perform the relay from the neighboring slot to the prediction slot are not identified, so the representational findings are not yet connected to a mechanistic circuit; the geometric analysis also uses one seed per figure, and the ring-coverage-by-k analysis reflects how many distinct carry values a group covers rather than a change in the encoding itself.
- **On the positive side, the rigor where it counts is good:** matched patching on S_c (exact match in 90–96% of cases) is a genuine improvement over naive patching (which the authors themselves quantify at ~0.10 confounded), the skewed carry-count distribution is a thoughtful design choice, negative and partial results (unmatched patching, columns 4–6, block-5 sanity check, compact middle columns) are reported in detail, and the Limitations section is unusually honest — this is not a rubberstamp-prone paper.

## Strengths

- A clean, decisive experimental contrast (89% vs 1%) with a trivially cheap intervention — a space token — that turns a "transformers can't do multiplication" folk result into a "format decides" claim.
- Converging evidence across three independent methods (PCA/diffusion-map geometry, learning dynamics, causal patching) that all point to the same object: an angle-coded carry-in at the prediction slot.
- Matched activation patching with explicit confound control (S_c matching; quantified difference vs unmatched: 0.85–0.95 vs ~0.10) and sanity checks (last-block patching, patch-all).
- Honest, specific limitations and a concrete future-work list (spaces only in the answer vs operands, filler tokens, head-level relay analysis).

## Limitations

- Only 4×4 multiplication, one tokenization, one model size and architecture; the compact baseline uses a single run while the spaced configuration uses three (89/80/78), so seed variance is unbalanced across the comparison.
- Geometric analyses use one seed per figure; ring-on-k structure reflects group coverage of carry values, not a verified change in encoding across k.
- Patching shows sufficiency, not presence or necessity; for columns 4–6 the carry is represented at the prediction slot but patching alone transfers it only partially, and S_c-matched pairs cannot separate γc from S_c + γc mod 10.
- The mechanism *why* separators help remains a hypothesis (free prediction slot vs extra computation positions vs column alignment), and the attention heads implementing the relay are not identified — no head-level circuit account.

## Relevance

这篇论文对研究 Transformer 可解释性与"LLM 为何算不对数"的读者很有价值：它用最小对照实验证明单次前向乘法的失败主要是输入格式（缺少预测槽位）而非能力缺失，并给出了进位在残差流中以环上角度编码、且被因果使用的完整证据链，对 prompt 格式设计、机械可解释性方法和算术推理研究都有直接启发。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the "free prediction slot" explanation survive the controlled tests the authors propose — spaces only in the answer, spaces only in the operands, a different filler token (e.g. dots), and multiple seeds per format at matched compute?
- Does the carry ring form *before* the hard middle columns are solved during training (tracking the dial alongside per-position error), or is it a consequence of solving them?
- Which attention heads implement the relay from the neighboring answer slot to the prediction slot, and is the relay a single edge or a chain across blocks?
- With source-target pairs matched on differing modulo-10 results (instead of S_c), can the carry γc be causally separated from the column result S_c + γc mod 10?
- Do the same angle-coded carry representations emerge in larger models or other algorithms (multi-operand addition, long multiplication with more digits), or is the ring an artifact of a 4.8M-param, 6-layer regime?
