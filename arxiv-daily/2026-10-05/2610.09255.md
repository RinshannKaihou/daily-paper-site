> **一句话总结：** 该文提出一个"经验傅里叶符号"诊断工具：用逐个傅里叶模态单步探测训练好的 PDE 代理模型，把其线性化算子符号同时与精确解和训练格式（von Neumann 放大因子）的闭式符号做投影，并用"孪生格式差分"（upwind 纯耗散 vs Crank-Nicolson 纯色散的正交误差）消除架构谱偏置混淆——发现在线性平流上孪生差分恢复了完全模仿天花板 99.8% 以上的信号（CNN 径向 −0.2406 vs 天花板 −0.2406，切向 −0.6841 vs −0.6845；非局部 FNO 同样 99.9%），在非线性 Burgers 上学到的算子与其训练格式的谱距离比到谱参考解近 5–27 倍（共输入对照下达 50–52 倍），证明常规基准测试衡量的其实是"对求解器的忠实度"而非"对物理的忠实度"。

> **TL;DR:** Neural PDE surrogates trained on solver outputs may just imitate the solver's discretization error rather than the physics, and standard held-out benchmarks (PDEBench, PDEArena-style) cannot tell the two apart. This paper turns an empirical Fourier-symbol probe — one forward pass per wavenumber, no gradients — into a provenance test: the learned symbol is projected onto the axis joining the exact-advection symbol to the training scheme's von Neumann amplification factor, and a twin-scheme design (identical CNNs/FNOs trained on upwind, whose error is purely dissipative, and Crank-Nicolson, whose error is purely dispersive) cancels the architecture's spectral bias. On 1-D linear advection, the twin-scheme difference of both a local CNN and a non-local FNO recovers >99.8% of the analytic full-imitation ceiling on both axes with provenance coefficient α = 1.000 ± 0.000, while on forced Burgers the learned one-step operators sit 5–27× (up to 50–52× under a common-input control) closer to their training scheme than to the de-aliased spectral reference. Notably, fine-tuning on just 25 exact-evolution samples moves α from 1.000 to ≈0.01 without removing the architecture-dependent accuracy floor — provenance and accuracy are distinct quantities.

## 核心贡献

- **经验傅里叶符号作为来源检测探针：** 将 Gao et al. (2026) 的算子网络谱审计原语转化为"来源检验"（provenance test）——逐波数施加单模正弦探测场，一次前向传播读出学习算子的符号 Ĝ_θ(k)，无需梯度与模型内部访问；ε=10⁻² 工作点经线性区验证（ε∈[10⁻⁴,10⁻¹] 内符号稳定性达 1.0×10⁻² 相对量级，数值求解器闭式符号的恢复精度达 2×10⁻¹⁶）。
- **来源系数 α(k) 与"孪生格式差分"识别策略：** 在以精确解为原点、训练格式为已知点的旋转复平面上，α 是学习误差向格式误差方向的最小二乘投影（α=1 完全模仿格式，α=0 与精确解一致，α<0 即"仿真器优于求解器"区间）；由于神经网络谱偏置 B(k) 与耗散格式误差同向（都压低高波数），单格式实验的 α 必然虚高——本文让两个完全相同网络（随机种子、优化器、网格、时步、样本分布全部固定）分别训练在误差正交的 upwind（纯耗散/径向）与 Crank-Nicolson（纯色散/切向）格式上，使 B(k) 在两符号之差中被对消，并用解析符号给出可计算的全模仿天花板。
- **架构混淆排除：** 用 FNO（非局部、保留全部 N/2 个解析模态、有能力表达精确平流乘子）重复实验；在精确语料上 FNO 拟合到 MSE 3.6×10⁻⁸ 且符号落在精确参考处（α 机制反向工作：同一探针训练目标为格式时 α≈1、为精确解时 α≈0），证明观察到的格式模仿不是"局部性/容量不足"的假象。
- **非线性情形与来源-精度解耦：** 在强迫粘性 Burgers（N=128，谱/upwind/centered 三种 RK4 求解器，代理学习 M=15 子步映射）上，在求解器自身强迫稳态处测单步算子指纹；并用共同输入对照（仅算子变化、输入分布固定）与自回归 rollout 检查证明算子级来源与 rollout 过平滑是两种独立失真；最后证明 25 个精确样本（<1% 原训练集）即可把 α 从 1.000 移到 ≈0，而对原格式的误差反而从 1.4×10⁻⁷ 升至 1.0×10⁻³——精度与来源在不同尺度上响应。

## Key Contributions

- An **empirical Fourier-symbol diagnostic** for trained surrogates: probe each resolved wavenumber with a single-mode field u = ε·cos(2πkx/L), apply the one-step map once, and read the DFT ratio Ĝ_θ(k) — one forward pass per wavenumber, no gradients, no internals. Linearity is certified (worst-case relative symbol change 1.0×10⁻² over ε ∈ [10⁻⁴, 10⁻¹]) and the instrument is verified by recovering the analytic symbols of numerical solvers to 2×10⁻¹⁶.
- A **provenance coefficient α(k)** — the least-squares projection of the learned symbol error onto the training-scheme error direction in the frame rotated by the exact symbol — plus **twin-scheme differencing**: because architectural spectral bias B(k) points in the same radial direction as dissipative scheme error (a single-scheme study cannot escape this confound), two identical networks trained on upwind (purely dissipative, radial error) and Crank-Nicolson (purely dispersive, tangential error, |G^CN| = 1) with every other factor held fixed suppress B(k) in the difference, and the analytic symbols yield a computable full-imitation ceiling.
- An **architecture control** ruling out locality/capacity explanations: a non-local FNO that fits the exact evolution when trained on it (MSE 3.6×10⁻⁸) reproduces the same scheme signature when trained on solver data, with a twin-scheme difference reaching 99.9% of the CNN's ceilings on both axes.
- Evidence that **provenance and accuracy are distinct quantities**: at the operator level on nonlinear forced Burgers, and via fine-tuning experiments where α and MSE respond on different scales.

## Methodology

**Setup.** Periodic PDE on [0,1], N = 64 grid points, Courant number σ = 0.5 for advection. Three one-step maps: the exact reference E (true evolution sampled on the grid), the scheme reference S^(s) (one step of the numerical scheme), and the learned operator f_θ trained on pairs (u, S^(s)u). The closed-form reference symbols come from von Neumann analysis: exact advection G^exact(k) = e^{-iσφ_k} with |G^exact| = 1; upwind G^up = 1 − 2σsin²(φ_k/2) − iσsin φ_k (dissipative, |G^up| < 1, phase coincides with exact at σ = 1/2); Crank-Nicolson G^CN = (1 − iσtan(φ_k/2))/(1 + iσtan(φ_k/2)) (unitary, pure phase error). In the frame rotated by G^exact, errors decompose into dissipation Δ_dis ≈ |Ĝ_θ| − 1 and dispersion Δ_disp ≈ arg Ĝ_θ coordinates; α(k) is the real inner product of the learned error with the normalized scheme displacement.

**Twin-scheme identification.** The learned symbol is modeled as Ĝ^(s)_θ(k) = G^exact(k) + α_s(k)[G^(s)(k) − G^exact(k)] + B(k) + η, where B(k) is architecture-intrinsic spectral bias. A network with B(k) ≠ 0 that learned nothing scheme-specific would still report α_up > 0 under a dissipative scheme. Twin differencing exploits the geometry: the CN error is tangential and therefore orthogonal to radial spectral bias, and with architecture/mesh/optimizer/seeds held fixed the scheme-independent part of B(k) cancels in Δ_twin = (Ĝ^up_θ − Ĝ^CN_θ)·conj(G^exact). Full imitation predicts Δ_twin exactly equal to the scheme-pair difference (the ceiling); exact-reference consistency predicts Δ_twin = 0; spectral bias alone predicts radial-only displacement. The linear probe stays in the linear regime (ε = 10⁻²; GELU probed only locally); numerical solvers' recovered symbols match analytic ones to 2×10⁻¹⁶, fixing the instrument's zero.

**Experiments.** (1) *Linear advection*: three corpora (upwind, CN, exact spectral control), ~4000 input/target pairs from approximately flat-spectrum band-limited fields; identical 5-layer periodic CNNs (width 64, GELU, residual u + net(u)); FNO variant with 1× lifting and width 32 retaining all N/2 modes; Adam, cosine annealing, 400 epochs, 3 seeds. (2) *Forced Burgers* u_t + uu_x = νu_xx + f on [0,2π], N = 128, ν = 0.04: three method-of-lines RK4 solvers (de-aliased spectral with 2/3 rule, first-order upwind, second-order centered); surrogate learns the M = 15 substep map; the one-step fingerprint is evaluated on the solver's own forced steady states and compared via spectral distances D_scheme (vs own solver's next state) and D_exact (vs de-aliased spectral reference operator), mean |Δ log E(k)| over the upper spectrum. (3) *Common-input control*: on a fixed input pool X, compare surrogate, own scheme's M-step operator, and spectral reference on identical inputs (field-space projection α, Eq. 10). (4) *High-accuracy fine-tuning*: scheme-trained CNNs fine-tuned on budgets of 25–4000 exact-evolution samples with α re-read against the original scheme.

## Results & Highlights

- **Linear advection CNN (Table 1):** the upwind-trained surrogate reproduces the scheme's amplitude deficit to three decimals — learned |Ĝ_θ| = 0.395 ± 0.000 vs scheme value 0.395, with phase lag 0.003 ± 0.003 vs 0 (exact) — while the CN-trained surrogate stays unitary (|Ĝ_θ| = 1.000 ± 0.000) and reproduces the scheme's phase lag of 0.824 ± 0.003 vs 0.824. Both report α = 1.000 ± 0.000 over the upper half of the resolved spectrum; the exact-trained control CNN sits at |Ĝ_θ| = 0.987 ± 0.006, lag 0.025 ± 0.003, exposing B(k) directly.
- **Twin-scheme difference recovers the imitation ceiling (Tables 1, 5):** measured over the upper spectrum, the CNN gives Re Δ_twin = −0.2406 ± 0.0001 vs the analytic full-imitation ceiling of −0.2406 (100.0% of available dissipation signal) and Im Δ_twin = −0.6841 ± 0.0008 vs ceiling −0.6845 (99.9% of dispersion signal) — saturated on both axes, which spectral-bias-alone (radial only) and exact-consistency (zero on both) cannot produce. The non-local FNO matches at −0.2402 ± 0.0001 (99.9%) and −0.6837 ± 0.0003 (99.9%), with α = 1.000 ± 0.000 (upwind) and 1.001 ± 0.000 (CN).
- **Capacity check (Table 2):** trained on exact data, the FNO fits the exact evolution (final MSE 3.6×10⁻⁸; mean |Ĝ_θ| = 0.995 ± 0.006, lag 0.000 ± 0.000) while the CNN cannot (MSE 3.3×10⁻³) — yet both architectures trained on scheme data show the same signature, so numerical provenance cannot be explained by insufficient capacity or locality. The same probe on the same band returns α ≈ 1 when the target is a scheme and α ≈ 0 when it is the exact evolution; only the training data changes.
- **Nonlinear Burgers operator fingerprint (Table 3):** the upwind surrogate's output is D_scheme = 0.564 from its own solver's next state but D_exact = 3.02 from the spectral reference (5.4× ratio); the centered surrogate is D_scheme = 0.127 vs D_exact = 3.40 (26.8× ratio). Under the common-input control on spectral-reference states (Table 6), ratios grow to 50.2× (upwind, α = 2.845 [2.827, 2.867]) and 52.4× (centered, α = 2.853 [2.772, 2.906]) — the learned operator imitates its scheme's spectral action "sharper than the scheme itself" relative to the reference, though the α > 1 reading here is a distribution-pool extrapolation.
- **Fine-tuning decouples provenance from accuracy (Table 7):** 25 exact samples (<1% of the 4000-sample corpus) move α from 1.000 to 0.008 ± 0.006 (upwind-trained) and −0.021 ± 0.010 (CN-trained) in one step, with error against the original scheme rising six orders of magnitude from 1.4×10⁻⁷ to 1.0×10⁻³ — a target switch, not a blend. Yet MSE against exact evolution only falls to ≈3.5×10⁻³ and saturates at the architecture-dependent floor of the exact-trained CNN, so the accuracy coordinate does not follow provenance.
- **Rollout is a separate, scheme-independent distortion (Appendix I):** run autoregressively to steady state, all three Burgers surrogates collapse onto one over-smoothed spectrum with high-k slopes of −7.4 to −7.9 against solver slopes of −5.1 to −6.7 — the scheme signature of the one-step operators is not recoverable from attractor-level statistics, which is why the paper reports operator-level and rollout-level measurements separately.

## Authors & Credibility

The paper is single-authored by **Ridham Patel** (Computer Science & Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar, India), accepted at the **AI for Science workshop at NeurIPS 2026**. The work sits squarely in the numerical-analysis tradition (LeVeque's von Neumann and modified-equation analysis) applied to operator learning, and the writing engages the relevant ML literature precisely (FNO, PDEBench/PDEArena, spectral bias, solver-in-the-loop, emulator superiority). Appendix M is remarkable for its candor: it documents two silent numerical bugs found during the work (an MPS-backend complex einsum backward pass wrong by ~140% relative error; RK4 batching above 2³¹ elements producing NaNs/sub-percent drift that translated into a factor-of-two error in mean |Δ log E(k)|) and states that all affected Burgers results were regenerated after the fix — a strong signal of methodological care. As a workshop paper with one author and 3 seeds per experiment, it is a focused proof-of-concept rather than a large-scale study, but within its declared scope the instrumentation (linearity checks, identity-map loss checks, analytic-symbol verification to 2×10⁻¹⁶) is unusually rigorous.

## Critical Assessment

- **The headline setting is tailor-made for the method.** The clean 99.8–100% ceiling recovery happens on a linear PDE with exactly analytic symbols, and on a scheme pair whose errors are exactly orthogonal (upwind radial, CN tangential) at σ = 1/2 where upwind's phase error is exactly zero. The paper itself admits (Sec. 2.3) that away from σ = 1/2 the upwind error acquires a phase component and the separation becomes approximate. The result therefore demonstrates the instrument works, more than it quantifies provenance in realistic training pipelines.
- **Narrow empirical scope.** Everything is 1-D periodic advection and forced Burgers at N = 64/128, two architectures, three seeds, a handful of classical schemes. No 2-D systems, no PDEBench/PDEArena corpora are actually probed — the benchmarks that motivate the critique are never tested with the diagnostic, so the practical impact on current benchmark practice remains an inference rather than a measurement.
- **The Burgers "exact" reference is itself numerical.** The de-aliased spectral solver is a high-fidelity numerical reference, not a continuum oracle (Appendix E concedes this). So the 5.4×/26.8×/50× contrasts measure distance to one particular discretization, and the choice of spectral reference as "exact" bakes in the conclusion that low-order schemes' surrogates are far from "the physics."
- **The α interpretation drifts across experiments.** On advection, α ∈ [0,1] is a projection onto the scheme-error direction; on Burgers with common inputs, α = 2.845 is read as "sharper imitation than the scheme" and on spectral-reference states the paper itself says values above unity "should be read as extrapolation along the scheme direction rather than as a sharper measurement of imitation" (Appendix E). The scalar is well-defined mathematically, but its semantic label changes from table to table.
- **Twin-scheme assumption is only indirectly verified.** B(k) must be scheme-independent, but the two corpora have genuinely different spectral content (admitted in Appendix E), so optimization trajectories are not identical; the cancellation is supported by the CNN-vs-FNO agreement to 4×10⁻⁴ and by restricted-scheme runs, but there is no direct ablation isolating B(k) cancellation (subtracting an exact-trained control fails because B(k) is target-dependent — a real fragility the authors own up to).
- **Emulator superiority is untested in the interesting regime.** The α < 0 region — where an emulator beats its own solver — is observed only marginally (α ≈ −0.021 to −0.027 after fine-tuning, close to measurement resolution). The diagnostic's ability to detect the Koehler–Thuerey phenomenon at operator level remains unproven.
- **No code or data release is mentioned** in the paper, which limits reproducibility of the exact pipelines, though all hyperparameters, seeds, and even the numerical bugs are documented in Appendix K/M.

## Strengths

- A genuinely new measurement question — not "how accurate is the surrogate" but "which reference is the learned operator near" — with a crisp, cheap instrument (one forward pass per wavenumber, no gradients, no internals).
- The twin-scheme identification design is elegant: using the orthogonal error geometry of upwind vs Crank-Nicolson to make architectural spectral bias cancel, plus an analytically computable full-imitation ceiling that turns a qualitative claim into a percentage.
- Multiple mutually reinforcing controls: exact-corpus capacity check (FNO), common-input operator control (Burgers), restricted-capacity runs, autoregressive rollout separation, and fine-tuning trajectories read through α rather than the loss.
- Exceptional instrumentation hygiene: probe linearity certified over four orders of magnitude, solvers' recovered symbols checked against closed forms to 2×10⁻¹⁶, identity-map loss checks on every architecture, and public documentation of two silent bugs that would have produced plausible-but-wrong conclusions.
- Honest, explicit limitations (Appendix E) that precisely delineate what α ≈ 0 does and does not certify, and a clear conceptual takeaway: solver-referenced scores decompose into a physical and a numerical component that must be reported separately.

## Limitations

- Empirical scope is one-dimensional periodic advection plus one forced Burgers system at modest resolution, two architectures, three seeds — no 2-D turbulence, no multi-physics, and no application to the very benchmarks (PDEBench, PDEArena) whose ambiguity motivates the paper.
- The strongest quantitative claim (>99.8% of ceiling) lives in a regime engineered to be ideal: linear dynamics, exactly orthogonal scheme errors, σ = 1/2; the separation degrades away from that point and nothing is said about schemes whose errors are neither purely dissipative nor purely dispersive.
- The identification relies on the assumption that B(k) is scheme-independent under matched training conditions, verified indirectly through cross-architecture agreement; direct decomposition of B(k) is impossible because it is target-dependent.
- For nonlinear problems the probe is a frozen-coefficient linearization around the solver's own steady states, so per-mode von Neumann correspondence is not claimed; α values above unity on some pools are extrapolations, and the α < 0 (emulator superiority) region is essentially unexplored.
- No public code/artifact link is provided, and the practical remedy for practitioners (how to de-contaminate existing benchmark pipelines, beyond fine-tuning on 25 exact samples in 1-D) is left open.

## Relevance

对科学机器学习方向的研究者，这篇论文直击 neural PDE surrogate 评测的根本歧义：在求解器生成的 held-out 数据上误差低，只证明学到了求解器而不是物理。其"逐模态符号探针 + 孪生格式差分"方法论可以迁移到自建训练管线的审计中，对设计可信仿真基准、区分谱偏置与格式模仿具有直接参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the twin-scheme diagnostic survive on 2-D turbulence or on the actual PDEBench/PDEArena corpora, where schemes are rarely pure upwind vs Crank-Nicolson and errors are neither exactly radial nor tangential?
- Can the α < 0 (emulator superiority) regime be detected reliably at operator level, e.g., on the closure-learning setting of Suriyarnarayanan et al. where solver inheritance is already documented in aggregate energy spectra?
- Is there a training objective that directly penalizes provenance (‖Ĝ_θ − G^exact‖ projected on the scheme-error axis) rather than just adding exact data, and does it avoid the architecture-dependent MSE floor observed here?
- How does B(k) itself depend on data spectral content — the paper shows the exact-trained control degrades four orders of magnitude in MSE; can a scheme-independent probe of B(k) be designed so the twin corpora no longer need matched spectra?
- Since autoregressive rollout over-smoothing (slopes −7.4 to −7.9 vs solvers' −5.1 to −6.7) swamps the scheme signature, is there a rollout-level statistic that separates the two distortions without collapsing to the scheme-independent component?
