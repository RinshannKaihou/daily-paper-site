Destination-oblivious spraying can’t fix a fat tree’s downlink; per-destination rotation (Ofan) bounds queues at Θ(1) — optimal at full line rate.

Ofan: Optimal Load Balancing for AI Training

Sarah McClure · Evyatar Cohen · Jakob Krebs · Alexander Shpiner et al. (8 authors)  —  UC Berkeley · Technion · NVIDIA

  • load balancing

Headline metric

16–39×

lower CCT inflation than REPS/AR — Llama-3 405B FSDP2 end-to-end training, 1,024-GPU cluster, NSCC / MSwift congestion control.

POfan (per-pipe): 15–36× · no-CCA permutation: +2.3% vs ~11%

Why this matters

Explains why REPS and adaptive routing inevitably degrade in full-rate AI collectives — and offers a switch-only fix that shapes Ultra Ethernet design.

⚠ Critical edge

The 16–39× headline is CCT inflation over an idealized lower bound in the authors’ own simulator — no hardware testbed, no wall-clock training-time gains.

01 · Method

Isolate the LB layer: strip congestion control, blast at the max line-rate fraction the fabric supports, measure queues until every host finishes. CCAs (NSCC, MSwift, DCTCP) are added back in evaluation — the ranking never changes.

Θ(√m) · random sprayingRSQ / RPS, up + down links · Thm 1

Θ(m) · RR / JSQ downlinksender “stickiness” sync · Thm 2

Θ(1) · destination rotationDRB / Ofan / POfan · Thms 3–5, 7

  • Ofan mechanism: each edge switch keeps one RR pointer per destination edge switch (aggregation: one per destination pod) — “consolidation” exploits mandatory fat-tree waypoints. Pointer-dependent permutation (XOR + modulo-add), not plain RR, avoids cross-switch sync; ACKs rotate with a half-rotation offset; failed ports are skipped. No encapsulation, no end-host changes.
  • POfan: one pointer per 4×800G ASIC pipe (4OFAN) to match commodity pipeline-local registers; implemented in P4 on Intel Tofino 1.
  • Evidence base: htsim, 432-node 3-tier fat tree, 800 Gbps, 15 baselines (Table 1); permutation, all-to-all, a Qwen3-23SB MoE routing trace, and Llama-3 8B/70B/405B FSDP2 training on 1,024 GPUs.

2.5 KB pointer state / edge switch  ·  79 B / aggregation switch (k=64, 8K GPUs)  ·  ≤16.7% of any Tofino resource class

In the 405B + MSwift run the paper reports only multiples — 39×/36× lower inflation (Ofan/4Ofan vs the best existing) — not absolute values. With DCTCP, REPS/AR’s 3–4× explosion is partly a htsim artifact (non-fault-tolerant DCTCP); the authors drop DCTCP thereafter.

  • The 15 schemes cluster exactly into the three predicted regimes (linear / √m / bounded) — theory matches simulation (Fig 4).
  • Ofan holds ~99% utilization as load rises; REPS/AR saturate below 95% and diverge beyond offered load 0.9.
  • The gap widens with scale: ~5% at 16 nodes → ~11% at 1,024 nodes; ordering stable under link failures, 2- vs 3-level topologies, and incremental deployment.

03 · Strengths

  • Rare theory–practice alignment: clean Θ(√m) / Θ(m) / Θ(1) theorems exactly predict how 15 real LB schemes cluster in simulation.
  • The consolidation insight is genuinely clever — mandatory waypoints collapse impractical per-destination pointer counts to 2.5 KB / 79 B of switch state.
  • POfan confronts pipeline-local register reality head-on and ships a working P4 design with tabulated Tofino resources.
  • Evaluation breadth is exemplary: scale, failures, topologies, incremental deploy, 3 CCAs, a real MoE trace, and a 405B FSDP2 scenario.
  • Intellectually honest: flags its idealizations and proves model-level results where protocol-level proofs are out of reach.

04 · Limitations

  • Simulation-only performance evidence; no hardware testbed for POfan despite the P4/Tofino implementation (explicitly due to resource limits).
  • Headline multiples measure inflation over an idealized lower bound in htsim; absolute CCT, wall-clock training time, and real NCCL on real NICs are not measured.
  • The striking Θ(m) result is regime-specific: it assumes uniform, synchronized traffic; real MoE gaps are far smaller (2.5% vs 8.1%).
  • Θ(1) claims are proved for the queueing model, not the full protocol with failures, quantization, and pipe-splitting interacting.
  • Requires changes at every edge and aggregation switch; incremental mixes inherit the slowest (AR) switches’ CCT (7.5% midpoint).
  • CCA set (NSCC, MSwift, DCTCP) may differ from the finalized UEC transport and production stacks.

05 · Questions for further exploration

  1. Does the 16–39× CCT-inflation advantage survive production UEC-compliant NIC transports and real NCCL collectives — i.e., wall-clock training throughput at 100K-GPU scale?
  2. Can the Θ(m) synchronization bound be observed (or exploited) in real deployments — is cheap host-side packet-size jitter a competing fix?
  3. Would a hardware POFAN testbed validate the Appendix I four-stage combining pipeline under adversarial pointer contention?
  4. What is the right joint design of the Θ(1)-queue regime with the √D optimal packet size (Thm 6) — larger standardized MTUs alongside pointer-based LB?
  5. Does consolidation extend beyond fat trees to rail-optimized, two-tier, or circuit-switched hybrid AI-cluster topologies?

一句话总结 · One-line summary

针对 AI 训练中目标无关（destination-oblivious）的包喷洒式负载均衡在 fat-tree 下行路径上无法纠错、队列随消息规模增长的问题，本文从理论上证明随机喷洒的队列下界为 Θ(√m)、简单轮转/JSQ 下行队列最坏为 Θ(m)，并提出基于交换机、按目的地轮转（DR）且把目的地“合并”到共同航点的 Ofan/POfan，实现 Θ(1) 有界队列；在 Llama-3 405B FSDP2 端到端仿真训练中，把相对理想基线的 CCT 膨胀较 REPS/AR 至少降低 16×（NSCC 拥塞控制）/39×（MSwift），POfan（每管道 4 端口）为 15×/36×。
