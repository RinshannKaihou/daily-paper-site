Why this matters

Fills autoresearch’s missing step — predicting experiment outcomes before execution — at ~5% of one run’s cost, with decision protocols you can reuse today.

  • Pure in-context learning, zero training: a frozen Claude Opus 5 (1M context) reads losslessly serialized past records — code diffs, eval results, metadata — and predicts the median gain vs a fixed reference, with strict info boundaries (the queried outcome is withheld).
  • Three history conditions: W0 (no records), in-env, and cross-env — sources only from other environments, with held-out interventions and near-duplicates excluded.
  • Fair baselines: kNN / ridge regressors get the exact same records and candidates; hyperparameters tuned on validation folds only.
  • Decision-focused evaluation: 200 random 16-pool partitions selecting 3 (Regret@3, NDCG@3), plus 8-round Autoresearch with a 24-selection budget scored against an Arrival Oracle (nAULC).

03aStrengths

  • Unusually grounded: 2,653 records from the authors’ own real H100 runs (171,862 GPU-hours) spanning pretraining, post-training, and inference, with reference variability documented per environment.
  • Decision-focused metrics (Regret@3, NDCG@3, nAULC vs an Arrival Oracle) tie prediction quality to actual selection value, plus a concrete cost analysis.
  • Fair baseline design: kNN/ridge receive identical inputs; their transfer collapse cleanly isolates what the LLM contributes.
  • Broad ablations (13 backbones × 5 effort levels) back the headline claim with non-overlapping ranges.
  • Transparency: AI-use, ethics, and reproducibility statements; appendix CIs let readers check which cross-env gains are individually significant.

03bLimitations

  • Transfer is narrow: only five autoregressive pretraining environments sharing the mean-BPB metric; benefit is uneven (no selection gain on Marin; CIs cross zero on 2–3 of 5 targets).
  • No trained RWM: everything is prompted on a frozen commercial API — tied to a model snapshot, not bitwise reproducible, no data-scaling path.
  • Small scale (~100–190M non-embedding params): whether intervention effects survive at production scale is open.
  • Noise not propagated: single-run outcomes; the median-gain target is judged against one realized sample; per-prediction confidence absent.
  • Single fixed split per environment for the headline prediction metrics; no cross-validated error bars, and post-training/inference transfer is untested.

04Questions for further exploration

  • Does research knowledge transfer across phases (pretraining → post-training → inference) and metric families, or only within mean-BPB pretraining?
  • Would an RWM trained on accumulated records beat in-context prompting, and how does accuracy scale with record count and environment diversity?
  • Can calibrated gain-distribution predictions cut Regret@3 further where single-run noise rivals true gains?
  • Do RWM-guided selections compose with parallel exploration (fork-and-flush, population-based search), and does ~5% prediction cost stay negligible in closed loops?
  • Can the Nanochat Muon failure mode (+0.018 predicted vs −0.036 measured) be detected a priori as source and target diverge?
