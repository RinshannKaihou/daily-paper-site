Joint W2A4KV2 quantization of weights, activations, and KV cache lands LLaMA3-8B within 0.30 accuracy points of full precision.

Few Bits, One Law: Toward W2A4KV2

Quantization Quantization-aware training KV-cache compression LLM compression
Key metric
14.28×

lower WikiText-2 perplexity than the strongest QAT baseline at W2A4KV2 — LLaMA3-8B: 9.41 (CanonQ-Omni) vs 134.36 (ParetoQ); zero-shot 59.81% vs 37.87%.

Why this matters

Extreme low-bit compression papers usually report numbers; this one also proves where the errors go — explicit bounds plus ablation controls make it a credible baseline paradigm.

Critical edge

“W2A4KV2” is an index-rate label — stored rates reach 2.5 bits, and no throughput, latency, or exported-size numbers back the memory claim.

01Methodology

W · A · K · V→ R_S = P·H·D→ z = √g·R_S·u / ρ(u)→ ≈ N(0,1)→ C_(b,d) frozen→ joint QAT
  • Canonicalize, fixed per source. A frozen Hadamard–permutation–diagonal rotation plus energy normalization ρ(u)=‖u‖₂+ε strips scale and pushes all four heterogeneous tensors toward one Gaussian reference. K and V share a map; every layer shares it.
  • Quantize against frozen codebooks. One offline table per (bits, tuple-dimension), Lloyd–Max/LBG-fit to N(0,1) — e.g. d=4 with 8-bit indices packs 4 weights into one of 256 codewords — reused across groups, layers, and models, never refit during training.
  • Joint QAT adapts. All enabled W/A/KV paths train together with identity STE; the norm recomputes differentiably (rank-≤1 correction) and a nonnegative gain γ absorbs scale at decode. FineWeb-Edu, 2048 ctx, ≤240K updates (~31.5B tokens).
Theory chain: Thm 1 bounds frozen-codebook transfer via the canonicalization defect Δ_d(P); Thm 2 certifies local task loss; Thm 3 bounds STE gradient bias by distortion — each paired with a designated experiment.

  • Activation precision is the fault line: A8→A4 costs CanonQ-Omni +0.59 PPL vs ParetoQ +14.35.
  • The cache design is real: swapping canonical KV for uniform KV costs 5.73 PPL / 5.26 points on 1B.
  • Zero-update control: canonical codec without any gradient update → 7043 PPL; after QAT → 13.84 (~509×): canonicalization and adaptation are complementary.
  • Reasoning (mild W2A16KV16, Instruct-1B): GSM8K 47.8% vs 34.3%, HumanEval 62.2% vs 43.9%, MBPP 32.2% vs 28.0% over ParetoQ.

Strengths

  • First convincing joint W2A4KV2 numbers — 8B sits 0.30 accuracy points under full precision, a regime previously thought impractical.
  • The ablation chain (W → +A → +KV, uniform-vs-canonical cache, zero-update control) properly isolates the central claim.
  • Frozen per-(b,d) codebooks shared across layers and models: a genuinely useful systems property — zero per-tensor refitting.
  • Every theorem is wired to an empirical diagnostic — rare in the quantization literature.
  • Unusually honest reporting: stored-bit accounting, McNemar/bootstrap intervals, learning-rate-selection disclosures.

Limitations

  • Zero deployment evaluation: no kernels, throughput, latency, or exported size — stored bits are accounting, not measured bytes.
  • Single training seed (42) everywhere; the 8B recipe reuses 3B hyperparameters, not a dedicated search.
  • Reasoning/code gains are at mild W2A16KV16, and GSM8K participates in learning-rate selection — only HumanEval/MBPP are fully held out.
  • QAT cost is heavy: up to ~31.5B tokens per model, untabulated against PTQ’s cost model.
  • Dense LLaMA/Qwen only — no MoE, no vision encoders, grouped-query attention untested.

03Questions for further exploration

  • Once fused dequant kernels and KV decoding-factor traffic are measured, does the 2.125–2.5 stored-bit W/KV footprint still beat uniform INT at equal quality?
  • Can the Theorem 3 distortion bounds drive per-layer (b,d) allocation instead of uniform configurations?
  • Does the frozen-codebook interface survive grouped-query attention and MoE routing statistics?
  • Where does the 1.69–1.74× relative-PPL stability break at long context — does energy normalization interact with attention-sink tokens?
