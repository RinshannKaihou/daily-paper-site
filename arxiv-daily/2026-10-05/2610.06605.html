Inserting a space token between digits lifts a transformer's 4×4 multiplication accuracy from 1% → 89% — no chain of thought.

Separators Make Carry Propagation Learnable: The Geometry of Latent Carry in a Multiplication Transformer

Sama Satariyan · Raphael Cousin · Gérard Biau | Sorbonne Université / LPSM / Inria | 2026-10-05 mechanistic-interpretability arithmetic-in-transformers activation-patching carry-propagation input-format-sensitivity

Key metric · exact match

89%  vs 1%

4×4 answer-only multiplication. The only change is a space token between digits — a +88-point delta, best of three spaced seeds (89 / 80 / 78), no chain of thought.

Why this matters

LLM arithmetic failures look like a capability gap but are largely input-format brittleness — direct lessons for prompt design, tokenization, and mechanistic interpretability.

单次前向乘法的失败，主要是输入格式问题，而非能力缺失。

Critical edge

Read the headline with care: the 89% is the best of three seeds (89 / 80 / 78), while the 1% compact baseline is a single run — the contrast is not seed-controlled.

Methodology / answer-only multiplication, probed geometrically + causally

digits + space tokens→ 6-layer Llama-style decoder→ prediction slot→ ring angle = carry-in γc→ digit

0 1 2 3 ␣ 4 5 6 ␣ = ␣ 0 0 0 5 6 ␣ 8spaced format (␣ = space token); compact = no spaces

  • Model. RMSNorm + RoPE + SwiGLU, tied embeddings; 6 layers, 4 heads, hidden 704 — 4.8M non-embedding params.
  • Task. Answer-only 4×4 multiplication, operands zero-padded to 8 digits; 1M examples with carry-count k ∈ {0…7} oversampled; no CoT (with CoT it hits 100% in 13k steps — the work is then written in the output).
  • Contrast. Spaced vs compact tokenization; three spaced seeds, 100K steps, AdamW (lr 3e-4, cosine, 200 warmup); eval on 2K held-out examples.
  • Geometry probes. Residual stream recorded at every block × position; PCA for global structure, diffusion maps for ring structure, per-carry-count group PCAs.
  • Causal probe. Matched activation patching: swap the prediction-slot state with a source matched on column sum Sc (exact match in 90–96% of cases), read accsrc; then patch the carry difference Δ at block 4 and check the digit becomes (dbase − Δ) mod 10.

The slot holds the carry value, not just a correlated state. Patching Δ = γtarget − γsource at block 4 makes the model emit (dbase − Δ) mod 10 in 95–100% of cases (column 3); columns 4–6 transfer only partially — their carry is still held at a neighbor.

0 7→ 1 6→ 2 5→ 3 4

Positions converge outside-in, mirroring dependency structure (0/7 depend on one digit pair; 3/4 on all four, with the largest carries). In the compact format, positions 3–4 never converge — even at 200K steps.

Geometry / the carry ring

Prediction-slot states (the space token just before each output digit) form a ring in the residual stream whose angle encodes that column's carry-in γc. Digit embeddings sit on their own value-ordered circle with the space token at the center. Block-3 diffusion maps order points by carry-in; by block 5 the ring dissolves into a digit-decodable shape.

Schematic of the described geometry (8 ticks = ordered carry levels), not plotted from paper data.

Relay & residual errors

  • Boundary columns are fast. Columns 1 and 7 reach their plateau by block 1 (accsrc 0.78 and 1.00, all three seeds); interior columns 2–6 come later.
  • Relay, not accumulation. Column 4's carry is assembled at the preceding higher-order column's slot (accsrc 0.36–0.39, blocks 2–3) and only arrives at its own slot at block 4.
  • Errors are one unit off. At convergence, residual errors concentrate at positions 3–4 and are almost always exactly ±1 — a one-unit carry error, or a small angular error on the digit circle.

Strengths

  • A decisive contrast from a trivially cheap intervention — one space token turns a “transformers can't multiply” folk result into a “format decides” claim.
  • Three independent methods — PCA/diffusion geometry, learning dynamics, causal patching — converge on the same object: an angle-coded carry-in at the prediction slot.
  • Matched patching with explicit confound control (Sc matched in 90–96% of cases; 0.85–0.95 vs ≈0.10 unmatched) plus last-block and patch-all sanity checks.
  • Unusually honest limitations; negative and partial results reported in detail, with a concrete future-work list.

Limitations

  • One tiny task, one tokenization, one 4.8M-param architecture; the compact baseline is a single run vs three spaced seeds.
  • Geometric analyses use one seed per figure; the ring-on-k structure reflects group coverage of carry values, not a verified change in encoding.
  • Patching shows sufficiency, not necessity; Sc-matching cannot separate γc from Sc + γc mod 10.
  • Why separators help remains a hypothesis (free prediction slot vs extra computation positions vs column alignment); the relay heads are unidentified — no circuit-level account.

Questions for further exploration

  1. Free-slot test: does the explanation survive spaces only in the answer vs only in the operands, a different filler token (dots), and multiple seeds per format at matched compute?
  2. Timing: does the carry ring form before the hard middle columns are solved during training, or as a consequence of solving them?
  3. Circuit: which attention heads implement the relay from the neighboring answer slot to the prediction slot — a single edge or a chain across blocks?
  4. Separating carry from column sum: with source-target pairs matched on differing mod-10 results, can γc be causally separated from Sc + γc mod 10?
  5. Scale: do angle-coded carry representations emerge in larger models or other algorithms (multi-operand addition, longer multiplication), or is the ring an artifact of the 4.8M-param, 6-layer regime?
