Why This Matters

Text-to-image DiT deployment is bottlenecked by latency and memory; this provably lossless trick brings calibration-free quantization to parity with calibrated methods.

The diagnose-then-design recipe — plus data-free closed-form scales and robust ℓp weight fitting — transfers to any low-bit inference work.

Critical Edge

The “matches SVDQuant” headline rests partly on numbers quoted from the SVDQuant paper rather than re-run — and the W2A4 result is simulated, with no fused kernel or latency claim.

一句话总结 · One-line summary

CentriQ 揭示了 DiT 激活中 adaLN 引入的“每 token 均值（DC 分量）”无法被数据无关 Hadamard 旋转均匀打散（三种 DiT 宽度 1152/3072/2240 均不满足正则 Hadamard 要求的 n=4u²），提出旋转前逐 token 去均值、再经全精度秩一项精确回注的免校准 W4A4 量化方案，配合 ℓp(p=0.7) 分组权重量化器 RoDS：在 PixArt-Σ/FLUX.1-schnell/SANA-1.6B 上免校准即可追平需校准的 SVDQuant，并以 W2A4 的 GenEval 0.647 首次在 2-bit 激活下保持可用画质。

Methodology — a five-step operator
  1. Diagnose the failure. adaLN conditioning injects a per-token mean (DC component, up to ~10−2 relative energy) that cannot fold into a bias; mean-spreading “regular” Hadamard matrices exist only at order n = 4u² — none of 1152 / 3072 / 2240 qualifies, so rotation concentrates DC energy into range-inflating spikes.
  2. Center exactly. Subtract the per-token mean x̄t·1 before rotation — O(n), computed alongside the quantization range.
  3. Re-inject via rank-1. Layers are linear, so the removed component returns exactly as x̄t·w₁ (w₁ = W·1, FP32) fused into the GEMM epilogue → offset invariance: quantization error no longer depends on the mean.
  4. Rotate freely. Any factorized Hadamard (Paley ⊗ Sylvester, zero-padded) now serves mixing alone — two small transforms, O(n′(m+2k)).
  5. Quantize robustly. Closed-form data-free activation scale (p = 0.9995 quantile × γ = 2.56, frozen everywhere); weights as grouped asymmetric INT4 under an ℓ0.7 objective (RoDS: IRLS + half-quadratic) that tracks each 64-column group’s dense mode instead of outlier-inflated min-max scales.

Strengths
  • Crisp structural diagnosis — DC component from adaLN plus non-existence of regular Hadamard matrices at the evaluated widths — with a short proof (Lemma 1) and direct measurements of rotated max/RMS.
  • Provably lossless fix: offset invariance decouples the rotation choice from the mean; rank-1 re-injection fuses into the GEMM epilogue at zero net latency.
  • Fully calibration-free with hyperparameters frozen across models and layers — the right axis given calibration’s per-checkpoint fragility.
  • Broad evaluation: three DiT families, two fidelity benchmarks, GenEval, component and weight-quantizer ablations, and a real fused kernel on A100 tensor cores.
  • Honest protocol hygiene: disjoint 1,000-prompt dev split, eval split scored once, FID noise flagged, W2A4 admitted as simulated.
Limitations
  • Benefits hinge on adaLN’s large per-token mean; the authors concede the centering gain should shrink on architectures without it.
  • SVDQuant rows are quoted from the original paper rather than re-run; on FLUX.1-schnell CentriQ trails on FID and ImageReward (18.8/0.939 vs 18.3/0.951).
  • The ablation is cumulative, not factorial — centering vs rotation vs clipping interactions are not isolated, and there is no comparison to HadaNorm (no public code).
  • Sub-4-bit configs (W4A2 / W2A4) are simulated only — no fused kernel, no latency numbers; NVFP4/MXFP4 are out of scope.
  • Code and models are promised, not released at writing time; the 2-bit claim rests on GenEval + FID alone.
Questions for Further Exploration
  • Does the DC-component diagnosis dominate video DiTs and adaLN-free conditioning (cross-attention-only, RoPE-heavy designs)?
  • Would a factorial ablation show centering alone already suffices at W4A4, making the rotation redundant at 4-bit?
  • Can per-layer or per-timestep scales close the remaining PSNR gap to SVDQuant on PixArt-Σ and SANA-1.6B?
  • Does the rank-1 epilogue survive microscaling FP4 formats (NVFP4/MXFP4), where scale granularity changes the offset-invariance arithmetic?
  • Once code releases: head-to-head vs HadaNorm and OrbitQuant’s block-Hadamard mixing under identical seeds and prompts?
