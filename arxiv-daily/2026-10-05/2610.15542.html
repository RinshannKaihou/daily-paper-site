thinking mode: MATH-500 51.2 → 71.4 · AIME24 7.5 → 22.9

Why this matters

A complete, fully open recipe — and honest cost ledger — for retrofitting vision, audio, and 262K context onto an existing LLM lineage without architecture changes.

Critical edge

The “leads fully open models” claim rests on a thin peer set: open-weight Qwen3.5-9B wins nearly every knowledge and math benchmark — MMLU 83.7 vs 70.5, GSM8K 94.8 vs 78.5.

Methodology

Modality enters as tokens; the trunk never changes

images → Emu3.5 codes (512² = 1,024 tok) audio → WavTokenizer (∼2.4k tok/min) → vocab 131,072 → 266,440 → unchanged trunk · text-only LM head
  • Continued pretraining · 4 stages Unpaired multimodal (all-token loss) → paired image–text / audio–transcript (text-only loss) → quality cooldown (∼38B instruction-formatted tokens) → long-context 8K→32K→65K→131K→262K (RoPE base 500k→4M). +4T tokens for 8B, +2T for 70B.
  • Post-training · 5 stages SFT on 3.7M samples with pass@8 capability filtering → dedicated 262K long-context SFT (short-context SFT collapses RULER 54.8→8.3) → RLVR on 349,300 verifiable prompts → judge-scored DPO (MaxMin pairs from a 24-model pool) → SDPO charter alignment.

Thinking is a template flag, not a model — enabled per request with a 32,768-token budget; generation stays text-only.

Strengths
  • Genuine openness on five axes — weights, versioned data, code, recipes, values; every table re-runnable from released harness forks.
  • The tokenizer-only recipe is a reusable result: image+audio absorbed in 4T tokens with ~2 transient text points, recovered by cooldown.
  • Publishes failure post-mortems most reports omit — aborted Stage-1 run, half-trained 70B, decontamination bug, data-template leakage.
  • Evaluation discipline: held-out suites, locally served judges, thinking evaluated on and off everywhere, mean@8 on AIME.
  • Broader evaluation than any open release this size — 70 image benchmarks, Romansh human translation, EU-AI-Act-style transparency docs.
Limitations
  • Knowledge and math clearly behind open-weight peers (MMLU 70.5 vs 83.7); “best fully open” describes a thin field, not the frontier.
  • Text-only generation; vocal-sound classification at or below chance (16.0% vs 92.2%).
  • Safety regressed — HarmBench attack success 8.7% → 26.5% — while over-refusal spiked (OR-Bench hard subset 3.6% → 81.5%); grounding −17.
  • Thinking and tool calling are mutually exclusive at launch; multi-turn tool use unevaluated.
  • The 70B consumed only half its multimodal mixture, conflating scale effects with data starvation.

Questions for further exploration

  • Would the 70B match the 8B’s multimodal profile on its full token mixture — data starvation, or scale-driven modality interference?
  • Does predicting multimodal tokens during paired Stage-2 help or hurt, given generation is never required?
  • Can SDPO charter alignment recover v1.0-level jailbreak resistance without the over-refusal spike?
  • What is the end-benchmark ablation of pass@8 filtering thresholds k=1..8, beyond filtering-rate statistics?
  • Is the ~2-point text-capability tax a learning-rate/schedule artifact, or an intrinsic capacity trade-off?
