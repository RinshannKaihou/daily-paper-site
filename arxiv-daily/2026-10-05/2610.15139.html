Per-token dynamic scaling re-derives each decode step's quantizer scale from its own quantized state — a scale–state feedback loop that compounds error until recall collapses.

When Dynamic Scaling Fails: A Scale–State Feedback Governs Recurrent-State Quantization in Linear-Attention and SSM LLMs

Yuhang Jiang · independent researcher · 2026-10-05 quantization linear-attention state-space-models long-context-inference numerical-stability

Key metric · NIAH recall, GDN-0.8B INT4

1.00 → 0.00

Perfect at H=0, dead by H=8192 — while INT8 holds 1.00 at every measured horizon. The same bit-width is safe or catastrophic depending on decode horizon.

Why this matters

Bit-width means nothing without decode horizon: short-horizon certification overestimates survival, and the default dynamic scaling hurts recurrent states more than clearing them.

Critical edge

Everything is simulated fake-quantization below 3B on one RTX 3090 — no kernels or throughput — and group-wise dominance fails on Mamba-2, so the winning policy is GDN-specific.

01Methodology

  • Models: Qwen3.5-0.8B / 2B (Gated DeltaNet hybrids, 262K context) and Mamba-2-1.3B; public checkpoints, a single RTX 3090.
  • Quantization: training-free, state-only fake quant — symmetric per-channel INT-b of the recurrent state at every decode step; weights and activations untouched, to isolate what recurrence alone does to the error.
  • Policies: dynamic (per-step absmax), static (one offline-calibrated scale), group-wise (persistent scales per 32 channels, 5.0 modeled bits/element). Interventions: scale capping, periodic reset (K=128–512), scale-yoking.
  • Protocol: NIAH recall (12 seeds, McNemar) plus teacher-forced continuation excess NLL vs the unquantized model, always with a zero-state control ("better than discarding the state?"). Context fixed at 2048; article-disjoint WikiText-103 calibration/test; paired t-tests and diff-in-diff.
  • Survival: first step where the quantized model flips an argmax the FP model predicted with top-2 margin > 8; 30 held-out articles, right-censored to H=8K.

Unquantized Mamba-2 recall at H=4096 is 0.75. At H=8192 nothing answers — full precision included. And recall flatters: INT8's perplexity penalty still doubles from H=2048 to H=32768 (1.30× → 1.55×).

Per-token dynamic INT4 is worse than throwing the state away in all three models (2B: 6.21, Mamba-2: 7.22 vs controls 4.08 / 3.19). Yoking recovers 1.82 of the 5.39 nats (t=−13.1) — two thirds of the damage is the feedback loop.

  • Runaway: dynamic INT4 inflates the mean state norm from ~17 to 3.9×10³ (0.8B) and 2.2×10⁴ (2B); yoking pins it at 21.
  • Containment beats location: reset K=128 → 3.38 nats, capped dynamic → 3.58; adding exact outlier escape changes nothing (t=−1.53). Every outlier-locating allocator loses to plain group-wise; stochastic rounding (Nemotron 3's SSM-cache fix) costs +6.71 nats.
  • Policy × exposure: the dynamic−static gap widens with H (+1.22 / +1.80 / +5.06 nats across models), yet dynamic wins at short exposure in 2 of 3 models — crossover at H≈1024–1536 on 0.8B.
  • Mixed precision: layer 0 alone carries 63–78% of single-layer damage; a trivial "protect the first 3 layers" rule matches a knapsack optimizer bit-for-bit.
  • Survival: the deployment-preferred policy fails 1/30 articles at H=512 but 11/30 by H=2048 (2B: 0 → 12/30), with no short-horizon precursor; hazard fits over-predict survival by well over an octave.
  • Sober bottom line: the best policy still leaves perplexity at 7.8× / 8.6× unquantized at H=2048, and its edge over a zeroed state reaches 0.00 by H=32768.

03Strengths

  • Crisp, actionable central claim: recurrent-state bit-width and decode horizon are inseparable — and the most faithful policy (per-token dynamic) is the worst.
  • Causal identification via scale-yoking cleanly splits the damage into a removable feedback component (2/3) and an honestly unidentified residual, without overclaiming a complete mechanism.
  • The zero-state control — "is this policy better than discarding the state?" — is an evaluation primitive most quantization papers lack; here it condemns dynamic INT4 in all three models.
  • Survival analysis makes short-horizon certification legibly anti-predictive for the good policies.
  • Unusually honest negative results: stochastic rounding, outlier-aware allocation, learned allocators all reported as failures, with paired statistics throughout and bit-identical GDN re-runs.

04Limitations

  • Simulated quantization only — fake-quant with modeled bit rates; no packed kernels, latency, memory, or throughput.
  • Small scale: two architectures, all below 3B, one consumer GPU; effects already shift between 0.8B and 2B, so 30B+ behavior is unverified.
  • No head-to-head with Q-Mamba's trained correctors or DAMP's ~9.9-bit decay-aware scheme — whether they sit on the safe side of the frontier is open.
  • Incomplete mechanism: ~1/3 of the functional loss survives yoking with an unidentified cause; the step-by-step trajectory evidence is only correlational.
  • Architecture-specific winners: group-wise dominance fails on Mamba-2 (static wins there), so guidance does not transfer across the delta-rule / SSM divide.
  • Corpus ceiling: no held-out article beyond 16,227 tokens; 32K-horizon claims rest on 7 concatenated contexts with a nondeterministic Mamba-2 runtime.

05Questions for further exploration

  • Do trained correctors (Q-Mamba's DSQ/ESR) or DAMP's ~9.9-bit scheme sit on the safe side of the frontier at H=32K+, or merely shift the cliff rightward?
  • What is the unidentified post-yoking residual (~1.7–2 nats at 4 bits) made of — a resolution limit that vanishes under a finer grid at equal rate?
  • Does the scale–state runaway appear at deployment scale (Qwen3-Next-80B-class GDN), and does the first-layer-protection advantage keep shrinking past 2B?
  • Could FP-side signals the model already computes (GDN decay/gating statistics) predict the step scale — severing the feedback edge without offline calibration?
  • How do the findings interact with chunked prefill and with KV-cache quantization applied jointly to the attention layers of these hybrids?
