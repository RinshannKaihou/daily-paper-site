> **一句话总结：** 论文定位了 HuggingFace Transformers 中 HQQ KV 缓存量化破坏请求独立性的确切机制——遗留近端优化器用跨请求共享的平均重建误差决定所有请求何时停止零点更新，因此同批无关问题能改变目标问题的更新次数进而改变贪心解码答案：在 128 道 GSM8K + 64 道 HotpotQA、Llama-3.1-8B 与 Qwen3-8B 的冻结测试集上，仅替换同批 companion 就使 170/384 次比较的输出文本改变（BF16、固定 4/20 次迭代、请求级停止等修复在匹配对照下均为 0/384），且重放对方运行的更新次数可双向逐字节复现对方的完整答案与缓存指纹。

> **TL;DR:** In Transformers' HQQ KV-cache quantization, the legacy proximal optimizer's early-stop test compares a reconstruction-error mean computed across all requests in a batch, so an unrelated companion question can change how many updates a target request receives — and hence its greedy-decoded answer. On a frozen test set of 128 GSM8K math and 64 HotpotQA questions with Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct and Qwen3-8B, swapping only the companion changes four-bit HQQ output text in 170/384 comparisons (44.3%), versus 0/384 for BF16, fixed-4/20 budgets, request-local stopping, and Quanto under matched controls; replaying the other run's recorded update counts transfers the complete answer and cache fingerprints in all 170 changed pairs, both directions. A tuning-free fixed 20-update budget restores batch-invariance while keeping iterative fitting, at essentially native compression cost (6.22 ms vs 6.35 ms geometric mean), though under natural padding+SDPA rebatching text changes persist even for BF16 (36.1%), so stopping repairs alone do not deliver batch-invariant serving.

## 核心贡献

- **定位并命名失效机制：** HQQ 的量化组与参数更新在形式上是"请求局部"的，但其遗留近端优化器用整批共享的张量平均绝对重建误差与历史最优的比较（shared stopping test）决定全部请求何时停止更新；同批 companion 可改变目标的更新次数 J，从而改变缓存内容与答案。仅替换 companion 就改变 170/384 次测试比较的四比特 HQQ 答案。
- **严格的因果验证协议：** 逐层、逐压缩调用记录更新次数，并要求三重检验全部通过——(1) 自身次数重放必须复现原输出与缓存指纹（排除记录本身的副作用）；(2) 换用对方运行记录的次数必须复现对方完整 token 序列与 raw/reconstructed 缓存指纹（证明次数即充分原因）；(3) 双向都要通过。170 个文本改变对全部双向迁移成功。
- **零调参修复及其边界：** 固定迭代预算（4 或 20）、请求级停止、完全关闭优化在匹配对照下都恢复 0/384 的文本/ token 改变；固定 20 保留迭代拟合且压缩成本几乎不变（6.22 ms vs 原生 6.35 ms，几何均值，96 个真实初始 KV 张量）。
- **诚实刻画修复边界：** 在自然重分批（padding + SDPA、六个洗牌种子）下，固定 20 仅把文本改变率从 54.9% 降到 46.6%（−8.4 pts），BF16 也有 36.1%——kernel/padding 层面的数值批依赖独立存在；FP32 停止判据也只把数学题文本改变从 169/256 降到 129/256，说明共享早停的泄漏无法靠提高判据精度消除。
- **机制外推到权重压缩：** Q/K/V 联合压缩 vs 分开压缩同样经 HQQ 的优化/停止路径改变三个模型的答案（算术题文本改变 162/256、184/256、208/256，确认正确性翻转分别为 7、3、7），表明问题源于 HQQ 优化机制本身而非缓存特有。

## Key Contributions

- Identifies the precise mechanism breaking request independence in HQQ cache quantization: a batch-wide mean reconstruction error in the legacy proximal optimizer's early-stop test couples every request's update count, so companions change a target's cache and greedy-decoded answer — 170/384 four-bit test comparisons change text from swapping the companion alone.
- Establishes causality with a replay protocol: per-call update counts are recorded, own-count replay must reproduce native output, and cross-count replay must transfer the complete token sequence and raw/reconstructed cache fingerprints through EOS — all 170 changed pairs pass bidirectionally.
- Shows tuning-free repairs (fixed 4/20-update budgets, request-local stopping, disabling optimization) restore 0/384 text or token changes under matched controls, with fixed 20 preserving iterative fitting at 6.22 ms vs 6.35 ms native geometric-mean initial-KV compression time.
- Maps the repair boundary honestly: FP32 stopping still leaves 129/256 math pairs changing; natural rebatching with padding and SDPA keeps text changes at 46.6% for fixed 20 and 36.1% even for BF16; and the shared-optimizer mechanism extends to joint-vs-separate Q/K/V weight compression across three models.

## Methodology

The study isolates cache-compression dependence while holding model weights (BF16), greedy decoding, tokenizer, prompts, position IDs, attention masks (explicit masks throughout), and padded batch shape fixed. A two-request batch (target q, companion a or b) runs twice with identical target inputs; the only intervention point is the four-bit HQQ cache (group size 64, 1/1 key/value axes, at most 20 legacy proximal updates, FP16 fitting) inside Transformers 5.17.0 with HQQ 0.2.8.post1 at a pinned commit.

**Causal protocol.** For every layer and every key/value compression call, the per-call update counts are recorded. Each pair must pass three gates: (1) replaying a run's own counts reproduces its outputs and cache fingerprints (recording is side-effect free); (2) replaying the other run's counts reproduces that run's complete answer, raw and reconstructed target cache fingerprints, and full token/EOS sequence (counts are sufficient); (3) transfer must hold in both directions with no missing counts.

**Data and scorers.** A 192-question development set (32 each from HotpotQA, MuSiQue, TriviaQA, SQuAD, GSM8K, IFEval; 960 model/question pairs over five checkpoints) motivated the design; the frozen test set pairs 128 GSM8K (official test split, upstream strict marker parser) and 64 HotpotQA (official validation split with distractor documents) targets with two fixed companions each — 384 comparisons per condition for Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct and Qwen3-8B. A separate explicit final-number extractor was frozen before generation; official GSM8K pass and HotpotQA EM/F1 are reported alongside, and unresolved extraction assertions are retained rather than silently resolved.

**Repairs, ablations, and extensions.** Fixed budgets (4, 20) skip the error test; request-local stopping compares a per-request mean including padded positions; "no optimization" keeps initialization only. An FP32 scalar stopping test probes decision precision; a 36-condition synthetic padding control isolates attended-reconstruction changes; six shuffle seeds plus length sorting rebatch the frozen test set under natural padding and SDPA; a three-model weight-compression study (Llama-3.1-8B, Qwen3-8B, Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3; 256 GSM8K + 128 HotpotQA questions) repeats the design for joint vs separate Q/K/V quantization with a BF16-reconstructed runtime.

## Results & Highlights

- **Headline causal effect:** swapping only the companion question changes four-bit HQQ generated text in 170/384 test comparisons (44.3%) across the two 8B models on 128 GSM8K + 64 HotpotQA, versus 0/384 for every comparator under matched controls — BF16, no optimization, fixed 4, fixed 20, request-local stopping, and Quanto. All 170 changed pairs pass bidirectional count replay with complete token and cache-fingerprint transfer.
- **Correctness impact is real but small:** 8 of 246 resolved final-number comparisons flip (5 correct→wrong, 3 wrong→correct; e.g., Llama GSM8K item 490: asserted 17 becomes 7), while the official GSM8K parser changes judgment in 16/256 pairs (9/128 Llama, 7/128 Qwen) and no QA judgment changes.
- **FP32 stopping is not a fix:** computing the shared stopping mean in FP32 cuts differing-count compression calls from 10,543 (FP16) to 3,171 (FP32) (Table 6), yet math answers still change in 129/256 pairs (native: 169), including 23 pairs whose initial caches were identical — the shared decision leaks through even when caches initially agree.
- **Why QA is largely spared:** QA median output length is 5 tokens versus 202/266.5 for math; 94 of 169 math changes begin after token 64, and 75/256 math pairs differ within 64 tokens versus 1/128 QA pairs — longer generation offers more exposure, though length is not isolated as the cause.
- **Repair cost and quality are a wash:** on 96 actual initial-KV tensors (RTX A6000), geometric-mean compression time is 6.22 ms for fixed 20 vs 6.35 ms native and 0.52 ms no-optimization; on the test set neither native nor repaired HQQ consistently beats BF16 (Llama GSM strict: BF16 78.12, native 74.61, fixed 20 75.00, request-local 71.09; Qwen: 66.41, 69.14, 67.19, 70.31) — "no established quality advantage."
- **Natural rebatching boundary:** under padding + SDPA across six shuffle seeds, fixed 20 reduces mean text changes from 54.9% (native) to 46.6% (−8.4 pts) but BF16 still changes 36.1%, with arithmetic correctness changes of 64/501 (native), 62/496 (fixed 20), and 17/484 (BF16) — kernel- and padding-level numerical effects dominate and are not fixed by stopping repairs.
- **Weight compression generalizes:** joint vs separate Q/K/V compression changes arithmetic text in 162/256 (Llama), 184/256 (Qwen), and 208/256 (Mistral) pairs, with 7, 3, and 7 confirmed correctness changes respectively; 500 fixed-budget layer checks confirm the compression mechanism itself.

## Authors & Credibility

The authors are from Jeju National University's Department of Artificial Intelligence (Seunghui Jwa, Minsu Oh, Yeo-Chan Yoon) and Soongsil University's School of Software (Chanjun Park). The paper displays an unusually strong verification culture for an inference-reproducibility study: scorers and prompts frozen before test generation, exact-match transfer criteria pre-specified, every unfavorable result retained in the tables (e.g., request-local stopping degrading Llama GSM strict to 71.09 vs BF16's 78.12), descriptive-vs-inferential labeling throughout, and a public anonymous.4open.science artifact containing all 384 test-set records, 9,216 weight-study answers, and executable verification audits. The engagement with the batch-invariance literature (vLLM batch-invariance docs, Thinking Machines' nondeterminism work, KVQuant/KIVI, Quantamination) is current and precise. The authors disclose AI-assisted implementation with personal review of all AI-assisted work.

## Critical Assessment

- **The headline number is a text-change rate, not an error rate.** 170/384 (44.3%) sounds alarming, but only 8/246 resolved final-number comparisons flip and five of those go wrong→correct; the paper says this plainly, yet the title will be skim-read as an accuracy claim.
- **Ecological validity of batching is narrow.** All cache experiments use two-request batches entering the same compression call; real serving runs continuous batching with dozens of requests and paged KV. The Limitations section concedes GPU sharing alone does not imply this dependence, so the 44% figure cannot be extrapolated to production scheduler layouts.
- **The mechanism is pinned to one legacy implementation.** The study uses HQQ 0.2.8.post1 at a specific commit inside Transformers 5.17.0 ("legacy proximal optimizer"); whether maintained HQQ paths or the separately studied TorchAO int4 runtime share the stopping test is not established, and the weight study reuses the same HQQ lineage rather than an independent quantizer.
- **Analyst degrees of freedom are disclosed but present.** Fixed 4 was added to the test matrix after observing favorable Qwen development numbers "without a prespecified optimality criterion"; the primary contrast rests on one frozen question set reused across every condition; and the six-seed replication significance (p = 0.03125 by exact enumeration over 64 sign assignments) is conditional, not a population-level test. The candor is commendable; the evidentiary base is still a single curated benchmark pair of tasks.
- **Statistics are deliberately modest:** bootstrap intervals are unadjusted and model/task-specific, shared questions violate independence, and 0/384 control outcomes are exact observations rather than risk bounds. The matched-control scoping is the right call but limits deployability guidance.
- **No quantization configuration beats BF16 here,** so the practical payoff is reproducibility auditing (stopping decisions must join quantization groups in independence audits), not a better serving recipe.

## Strengths

- A genuine causal-identification result: bidirectional count replay with complete cache-fingerprint and token transfer (170/170) is far stronger evidence than the correlation-style rebatching studies common in this literature.
- Pre-registration-style discipline: frozen scorers, prompts, and exact-match criteria; unresolved extraction cases retained; every adverse result (e.g., request-local stopping's GSM regression and its 12.9 ms synthetic-benchmark cost vs 7.8 ms for fixed 20) printed in the main tables.
- The recommended repair is practical and already upstream (a fixed iteration count is the existing no-early-stop path), needs no tuning, and its cost is measured on real tensors rather than asserted.
- Honest boundary mapping: the paper itself demonstrates that stopping repairs do not fix padding/SDPA-level nondeterminism and that FP32 precision leaves 129/256 math pairs changing — it refuses to oversell its own fix.
- Public artifact with executable audits covering file integrity, score recomputation, count transfer, and matrix controls.

## Limitations

- Two-request batches only; the frequency of companion-dependent stopping in realistic large-batch or continuous-batching serving is unknown, and results are tied to a pinned legacy optimizer revision of one library.
- Answer-correctness impact is small and bidirectional (8/246 resolved flips; 16/256 official-parser changes, all in math/instruction following), so this is primarily a determinism and audit problem, not a demonstrated quality loss.
- The FP32 generation ablation retains only 16 input-selected QA questions per model; the six-seed rebatching contrast covers two models on the same frozen questions; questions are reused across methods, so no condition provides an independent accuracy measurement.
- Timings exclude generation latency, rolling recompression, and host contention, and the weight study runs a BF16-reconstructed model rather than int4 kernels — no throughput, memory, or hardware-speed conclusions are supported.
- The padding control uses synthetic tensor values, which the authors note cannot estimate a real serving failure rate.

## Relevance

对任何依赖 batch 推理可复现性的 LLM 评测与服务工作都直接相关：它证明"量化组请求局部"并不等于"请求独立"，一个共享的早停统计量就足以让答案随同批问题改变，并给出了零调参修复与清晰的修复边界。若你关心 vLLM/Transformers 的批不变性、KV 量化部署或可复现 benchmark 基线，这篇应进入你的部署审计清单。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How often do companion-dependent stopping decisions flip answers under realistic continuous batching with large, heterogeneous batches rather than two-request pairs?
- Do maintained HQQ paths or TorchAO's int4 runtime still contain a shared early-stop statistic, or is the pinned legacy optimizer an already-superseded corner of the ecosystem?
- Can a per-request stopping test retain iterative fitting's reconstruction benefit at fixed-budget cost, closing the 12.9 ms vs 7.8 ms gap observed in the synthetic benchmark?
- Is adaptive stopping in quantization ever accuracy-positive, or is it pure variance — the paper's fixed budgets matched or beat native everywhere measured?
- How do stopping-level and padding/SDPA-level batch effects compose under natural rebatching, and is a fully batch-invariant numerical stack a prerequisite before quantization-level repairs matter in production?
