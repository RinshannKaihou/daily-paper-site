Empirical study · LLM multi-agent reliability

Topology redundancy, not model choice, keeps LLM multi-agent systems alive under communication faults — and one failure in five is silent.

Paper — When Messages Go Wrong: Understanding Communication Faults in LLM-Based Multi-Agent Systems
Jinhao Zhou, Xiaoyu Zhang, Jongchul Yu, Fahao Chen, et al. 2026-10-11 arXiv:2610.15459
Empirical Study Communication Faults Fault Injection LLM-MAS Reliability

一句话总结：首个 LLM 多智能体系统通信故障的大规模实证研究：15,882 个注入故障中 89.4% 传播为 MAS 级后果、80.2% 导致任务失败，20.7% 的失败是静默失败；Flat 拓扑凭冗余证据通路把故障下成功率从 ~3.2% 提到 53.0%，远胜换模型（仅挽回 0.2–4.5%）。

Key metric

80.2%

of 15,882 verified fault injections ended in total task failure.

89.4% propagated to a recorded MAS-level consequence · 18,900 executions, 100 tasks, 5 domains.

Why this matters

Silent corruption — messages that still parse but carry broken information — is the most dangerous LLM-MAS failure mode today; topology design, not model choice, defends against it.

For: agent-systems engineering, MCP/A2A protocol robustness, MAS fault-tolerant design.

▲ Critical edge

Flat’s dominance is partly an artifact of AutoGen’s graph shapes — conclusions rest on three fixed topologies and mid-tier models, with no frontier-tier LLM or non-AutoGen framework tested.

Methodology how they measured it

select fault op φ→ intercept I2·I3·I4→ verify delivered carrier→ trace A→M→O
  • Cross-layer taxonomy. 57 fault/effect descriptions from 31 communication standards, double-coded (Cohen’s κ 0.78–0.86, third-researcher arbitration) into 29 categories that decouple fault origin from what the receiver sees.
  • Verified injection. Interceptors perturb three AutoGen boundaries — I2 action requests, I3 observations, I4 evidence handoffs (I1 excluded: disrupting it prevents startup). Clean runs traverse identical instrumentation with an identity operator; the 318/16,200 scheduled faults that never arrived are excluded rather than counted.
  • Scale & statistics. 100 tasks · 5 domains (WebArena Shopping/Admin/Reddit, SWE-bench, TheAgentCompany) · 3 topologies × 3 model configs × 3 reps = 18,900 executions. Task-clustered bootstrap CIs, Holm-corrected sign-flip permutation tests.

  • Severity has no fixed ordering. I4 non-delivery is lethal for Sequential/Hierarchical (0.0%) but harmless under Flat (94.4–100% in WebArena Admin); semantic corruption fails 99.6% in Shopping vs 3.1% in SWE-bench — partial content inverts that pattern.
  • Protection is complementary. Execution-side protection (resubmission + dedup) uniquely recovers 67.9% of 106 baseline failures, evidence-contract checking the other 32.1% — zero overlap. Recovery has a hard boundary: when the same fault reaches every evidence path, recovered failures drop 18 → 0.
  • Redundancy changes the failure mix, not a cure. Under Flat, silent faults make up 51.6% of residual failures (vs 18.6%/18.1%); guarded reacquisition still cuts clean-run communication 13.3–19.4% at no success cost.

Strengths what holds up

  • +First-mover scale: 18,900+ executions and 15,882 verified faults — an order of magnitude beyond prior agent-fault work on adversarial prompts or single-agent failures.
  • +Methodological hygiene: verified injection with 318 non-reaching faults excluded, identity-operation clean controls on the same instrumented path, matched paired analyses.
  • +Grounded taxonomy: 31 communication standards with a formal coding protocol (κ 0.78–0.86), bridging decades of dependability research to LLM-MAS.
  • +Actionable findings: protection must match the failed dependency; recovery is bounded by unaffected evidence paths; guarded reacquisition saves 13.3–19.4% of clean-run requests.
  • +Open artifact with companion website and a candid threats-to-validity section.

Limitations read with care

  • −AutoGen-only: three fixed topologies, three mid-tier model configs — topology/model effects may not generalize to frontier models or A2A/MCP deployments at scale.
  • −100 tasks, 5 domains: cross-domain comparisons are explicitly descriptive; tasks are not paired across datasets.
  • −Idealized fault operators: deterministic, single-boundary injections; correlated, persistent, or adversarially timed multi-fault interactions are out of scope.
  • −Label noise unquantified: silent-failure and consequence labels come from the authors’ own trace-adjudication scripts — mislabeling would move the 20.7% headline directly.
  • −No adaptive recovery: detection of silent semantic corruption still requires a hand-written evidence contract to check against.

Questions for further exploration open threads

  1. Do topology-vs-model conclusions hold for frontier-tier models that can reason around partial evidence without workflow redundancy?
  2. How do findings transfer beyond AutoGen to physically distributed MCP/A2A deployments where real network faults dominate?
  3. Can silent semantic corruption be detected without a hand-written evidence contract — cross-agent verification, consistency probing, learned validity models?
  4. What happens under correlated or adversarially timed multi-fault injections that hit every redundancy path simultaneously?
  5. Can protection be made adaptive — choosing resubmission, rerouting, checking, or fresh reacquisition from the inferred failed dependency — and at what communication cost?
