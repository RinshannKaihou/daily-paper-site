> **一句话总结：** 本文剖析了激活转向去偏中"去偏方向"的真实编码内容：在 8 个 7B–9B 开源模型（Llama-3.1-8B、Falcon3-7B、Ministral-3-8B、Qwen3-5-9B 的 base/instruct 版本）上，用偏见/反偏见提示对比学出的线性方向实际上主要由**模型置信度**主导——同一分类器在 BBQ 消歧上下文上区分偏见 vs 反偏见标签仅 AUROC 0.57，但区分最高 vs 最低概率答案达 AUROC 0.94，且能零样本迁移到毫无社会偏见概念的 MMLU/OpenBookQA（最高 0.9）；沿该方向转向虽把 Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct 的 BBQ 歧义偏见分数从 0.08 压到 0.01，但代价是模型大规模弃答、OpenBookQA 准确率从 0.76 跌至 0.70、答案熵大幅上升——"去偏"实为"降置信"。

> **TL;DR:** The debiasing direction used in activation steering (Contrastive Activation Addition over biased vs anti-biased prompts from BBQ/StereoSet/CrowS-Pairs) is dominated by model confidence, not semantic bias. Across 8 open-weight models, a logistic probe trained on biased vs anti-biased hidden states separates those labels at only AUROC 0.57 on disambiguated BBQ (vs AUROC 0.94 for the highest-vs-lowest-probability split), transfers to MMLU and OpenBookQA — datasets with no societal-bias content — at up to 0.9 AUROC, and aligns with out-of-distribution confidence directions at cosine similarity 0.29–0.91. Steering along it reduces Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct's BBQ ambiguous bias score from 0.08 to 0.01 solely by pushing the model toward abstention, while OpenBookQA accuracy drops from 0.76 to 0.70 and answer entropy rises sharply. Reported debiasing via steering should be reinterpreted as confidence reduction, not fairness improvement.

## 核心贡献

- **识破"去偏方向"的真正编码**：在 8 个模型上系统证明，由偏见/反偏见激活对比构造的线性方向主要由模型置信度主导，而非人口学偏见的线性表征——纠正了激活转向去偏文献中的一个核心假设。
- **三条独立证据链**：(1) 探针迁移——BBQ 上训练的偏见分类器在无社会偏见内容的 MMLU/OpenBookQA 上按"高概率 vs 低概率"划分仍达最高 0.9 AUROC；(2) 相关性分析——消歧上下文中 P(biased) 与答案概率的 Spearman 相关从歧义条件的 0.01–0.45 增强到 0.57–0.80；(3) 方向对齐——去偏方向与从 MMLU/OpenBookQA 分布外构造的置信度方向余弦相似度达 0.29–0.91。
- **机制解释**：转向沿该方向时，若有弃答选项则弃答概率上升（偏见分数因 s_AMB = (1−accuracy)×s_DIS 公式而"改善"）；若无弃答选项则剩余选项概率被均匀化，熵上升、准确率下降——与温度缩放的效应类似，而非有针对性的去偏。
- **给去偏评估的警示**：报告的 steering 去偏结果应谨慎解读——观测到的偏见下降是置信度与偏见的混淆效应（confound）的产物，而非更公平模型的证据。

## Key Contributions

- Shows that the linear "debiasing direction" obtained by contrasting biased vs anti-biased prompt activations (the standard CAA-style recipe) is dominated by a confidence component: the same probe that separates bias labels at AUROC 0.57 on disambiguated BBQ separates highest- vs lowest-probability answers at AUROC 0.94, and classifies highest- vs lowest-probability answers on MMLU/OpenBookQA — datasets containing no societal bias — at up to 0.9 AUROC.
- Establishes the bias–confidence confound with three converging lines of evidence (probe transfer, Spearman correlation of P(biased) with answer probability, cosine similarity to OOD confidence directions) across 4 model families (base + instruct variants, 7B–9B parameters).
- Explains why steering "works": when an abstain option exists, steering increases abstention indiscriminately (which mechanically lowers BBQ's s_AMB bias score); when it does not, steering equilibrates remaining option probabilities, raising entropy and mirroring temperature scaling rather than targeted debiasing.
- Delivers a methodological warning to the fairness-steering literature: reductions in bias metrics under activation steering can be artifacts of reduced model confidence, so debiasing claims require confidence-controlled evaluation.

## Methodology

- **Models:** 4 open-weight families, each in pre-trained and instruction-tuned variants — Llama-3.1-8B, Falcon3-7B, Ministral-3-8B, Qwen3-5-9B (7B–9B parameters).
- **Datasets:** BBQ (15,960 prompts; ambiguous and disambiguated contexts, biased/anti-biased/neutral options), StereoSet intrasentence (2,106 questions), CrowS-Pairs (filtered to 1,018 minimally-perturbed pairs, converted to multiple-choice QA format), plus general-knowledge controls MMLU (10,000-prompt subset) and OpenBookQA (full set). 70/30 train/test splits keep each ambiguous prompt and its paired disambiguated twin in the same split.
- **Bias probe:** a logistic regression classifier on the final-token hidden state (the appended answer letter) at the "best layer", selected per model by sweeping every layer and taking the highest AUROC on a held-out test set (e.g., layer 15, AUROC 0.75, for Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct on BBQ ambiguous). Trained only on biased vs anti-biased options (neutral excluded); three separate classifiers per model (BBQ-ambiguous, StereoSet, CrowS-Pairs).
- **Debiasing direction (CAA):** v_b = mean over training pairs of (h_anti-biased − h_biased) at the same best layer. A second confidence direction v_c is built out-of-distribution from MMLU and OpenBookQA as the mean difference between each question's most- and least-preferred answers; v_b and v_c are compared via cosine similarity.
- **Steering:** at inference, h' = h + α·v̂_b with α swept from −1 to 2 in 0.5 increments; the quoted magnitude α̂ is the natural CAA norm α_nat when α_nat > 1, else 1 (Table 7 reports per-model α_nat). Each dataset is evaluated in two formats: with an abstain option (BBQ's neutral option; inserted into CrowS-Pairs/StereoSet) and without.
- **Metrics:** AUROC (probe quality), accuracy (ground-truth datasets only: BBQ-disambig, MMLU, OpenBookQA), BBQ bias scores s_AMB/s_DIS or biased-answer percentage (CrowS-Pairs/StereoSet), and answer entropy over candidate options. Greedy decoding for generation-based measurements.

## Results & Highlights

- **The probe tracks confidence, not bias (Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, disambiguated BBQ):** the linear classifier trained on biased vs anti-biased prompts achieves only AUROC 0.57 separating those two labels, but AUROC 0.94 separating the same prompts by highest vs lowest model-assigned probability — a split that "has no relationship to societal bias".
- **Probe transfer to bias-free data:** a classifier trained on BBQ ambiguous prompts reaches 0.88 AUROC on OpenBookQA for Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct (highest vs lowest probability answers); BBQ-ambiguously-trained classifiers reach 0.79–0.95 AUROC on disambiguated BBQ across all eight models, and the best configuration classifies MMLU answer confidence at up to 0.90 AUROC (Ministral-3-8B-Instruct trained on CrowS-Pairs) — impossible if the direction encoded genuine societal bias.
- **Correlation strengthens where bias cannot explain it:** the Spearman correlation between P(biased) and answer probability rises from rs ∈ 0.01–0.45 on ambiguous BBQ to rs ∈ 0.57–0.80 on disambiguated BBQ across all models — in disambiguated contexts the answer is dictated by factual evidence, so the classifier's correlation with confidence there indicates it is tracking confidence.
- **Steering "debiasing" is abstention in disguise:** steering at α̂ reduces Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct's BBQ ambiguous bias score from 0.08 to 0.01, but this comes from the model abstaining more; on the abstain-free general-knowledge side the largest accuracy cost anywhere in Table 5 is Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct on OpenBookQA under BBQ steering, dropping from 0.76 to 0.70.
- **Entropy costs on bias-free benchmarks:** steering Qwen3-5-9B with the StereoSet direction at its natural magnitude more than doubles OpenBookQA entropy (0.24 to 0.50) and raises MMLU entropy by 80% (0.34 to 0.61) — the signature of temperature-scaling-like probability equilibration, not targeted bias correction.
- **Direction alignment:** cosine similarity between the CAA debiasing direction and the out-of-distribution confidence direction ranges 0.29–0.91 across models and datasets (means: 0.68 for BBQ, 0.67 for StereoSet, 0.50 for CrowS-Pairs) — high alignment in the activation space.
- **Baseline bias is real:** all eight models exhibit positive BBQ bias (s_amb ∈ [0.07, 0.18], s_dis ∈ [0.01, 0.03]) and stereotype-answer rates of 69.0–78.8% on CrowS-Pairs and 62.7–73.3% on StereoSet, so the confound is not an excuse for bias-free models.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty is genuine but incremental in lineage.** The paper itself cites close precursors: [19] found bias steering vectors negatively correlated with refusal vectors, and [14] showed steering along a confidence direction directly increases abstention. The contribution is the synthesis and attribution — proving the standard debiasing direction *is dominated by* confidence via transfer probes, OOD cosine alignment, and behavioral sweeps — rather than discovery of a wholly new phenomenon. Prior debiasing work (e.g., FairSteer-style CAA) is critiqued fairly, and the negative-result framing is valuable.
- **Rigor is strong but has one methodological soft spot:** the "best layer" is selected per model by maximizing AUROC on the held-out *test* set (Sec. 4.2), so probe separability numbers (e.g., the headline 0.75 AUROC) are optimistically biased — the layer choice is a diagnostic fit to evaluation data, and no nested selection/evaluation split is used. The paper is transparent that this is a diagnostic, but downstream readers quoting the AUROCs should note it.
- **Benchmark reformatting is a data quirk:** CrowS-Pairs (1,508 pairs) is filtered to 1,018 pairs differing by exactly one word and converted to multiple-choice format; StereoSet/CrowS-Pairs are given a synthetic abstain option "at a random position" for the abstain-condition experiments. These departures from canonical benchmark protocols make the absolute bias percentages (e.g., 75.8% → 64.7% on CrowS-Pairs for Llama-3.1-8B) non-comparable to published leaderboard numbers.
- **Missing ablations:** no test of whether supervised debiasing methods with different training objectives (e.g., FairSteer's probe-based directions) inherit the confound — the claim is established only for mean-difference CAA directions; no open-ended generation experiments (bias is measured only via answer-letter probabilities and greedy choice over 2–4 options, whereas steering is often deployed for free-text debiasing); no analysis of *why* confidence and bias entangle (the proposed explanation — over-representation of biased sentences in pretraining data — is cited from [8], not tested).
- **Model scope:** only 7B–9B models; Qwen3-5-9B-Base is a flagged outlier (bias separable only at the final layer, where steering has no downstream layers to propagate through and thus negligible behavioral effect), hinting that the confound's geometry may vary across scales/families.
- **Reproducibility:** no code release is mentioned; datasets are public and the appendix details (prompt templates, α_nat values, per-layer tables) are unusually thorough, so reimplementation is feasible but laborious.
- **No red flags on honesty** — the abstract's self-limiting conclusion ("steering appears to reduce bias... by making it less confident") matches the evidence, and the generative-AI disclosure is explicit.

## Strengths

- Asks a sharp, falsifiable question — *what does the separating direction actually encode?* — and answers it with three mutually independent evidence types (probe transfer, correlation analysis, vector-space alignment) that agree.
- Excellent experimental controls: out-of-distribution confidence directions built from MMLU/OpenBookQA, abstain vs no-abstain answer formats that cleanly separate the two predicted behavioral signatures, and paired ambiguous/disambiguated BBQ splits.
- The mechanistic story (abstention concentration when allowed; probability equilibration when not) predicts and explains the otherwise-puzzling accuracy *increase* on ambiguous BBQ alongside accuracy *decreases* everywhere abstention is impossible.
- Honest, calibration-focused conclusion with direct practical guidance: debiasing-by-steering results should be reported with confidence/abstention controls.
- Breadth: 8 models × 3 bias datasets × 2 general-knowledge controls × 2 answer formats, with all raw magnitudes tabulated.

## Limitations

- Scale and family coverage: 7B–9B open-weight models only; no evidence the confound holds (or how it scales) at 70B+ or in closed models.
- Multiple-choice-only measurement: bias is quantified over answer-letter token probabilities; no free-text generation, toxicity, or downstream-decision evaluation, limiting external validity for how steering debiasing is actually used.
- Findings are established for CAA-style mean-difference directions; whether probe-supervised alternatives (FairSteer) or other inference-time debiasing methods (e.g., INLP-style) share the confidence confound remains open — the authors list this as future work.
- Best-layer selection on the test set inflates probe AUROC; a held-out layer-selection protocol would make the separability numbers stricter.
- Benchmark reformatting (CrowS-Pairs word-diff filtering, injected abstain options) breaks comparability with prior bias-measurement literature.
- No released code, and Qwen3-5-9B-Base's divergent behavior (final-layer separability, negligible steering effect) shows the phenomenon is not perfectly uniform across models.

## Relevance

与你关注的 LLM 公平性、激活转向与可解释性评估直接相关：这是一篇"评估方法论纠偏"的实证研究，提醒任何使用 steering/CAA 做去偏的工作必须先排除置信度-弃答混淆，否则偏见指标的下降可能只是模型变不自信的副产物。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Can a debiasing direction that is provably disentangled from confidence be constructed (e.g., by regressing out the confidence direction, or using counterfactually balanced training pairs), and does such a direction still reduce measured bias?
- Do probe-supervised debiasing methods like FairSteer, or weight-editing approaches, exhibit the same confidence confound, or is it specific to mean-difference CAA directions?
- Does the bias–confidence entanglement persist at larger scales and in RLHF-tuned models where abstention behavior is already safety-trained (the authors note instruct models abstain more even before steering)?
- Would confidence-controlled bias evaluation — e.g., reporting bias metrics conditioned on fixed abstention rate or fixed entropy — change the conclusions of published steering-debiasing papers?
- Does the same confound affect steering vectors used in AI-safety contexts (refusal, honesty, sycophancy), where the measured "target behavior" might similarly be an artifact of confidence shifts?
