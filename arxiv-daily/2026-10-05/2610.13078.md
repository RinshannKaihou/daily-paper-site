> **一句话总结：** 该论文把安全对齐拆成"识别→书写"两段通路并加以度量：在 18 对 Base–Instruct 模型（Qwen/Llama/Gemma，1B–27B）上，Attention 更新始终比 MLP 更容易被线性探针解码出"有害性"（36/36 个 checkpoint，早期层最明显），而对齐主要强化的是 MLP 的"选择性拒答书写"（MLP 净书写 18/18 对上升、Attention 仅 12/18；正书写质量集中在最后四分之一层的比例中位数从 23.01% 升至 63.86%）。因果干预证明下游 MLP 恢复可阻断上游 Attention 补丁 19–87% 的拒答漂移；据此构建的 Sentinel（P=Attention 感知分 + D=MLP 决策分）在 8 个模型上做前置条件化提醒路由，把越狱 ASR 从 95.97% 降到 35.17%、过度拒从 88.73% 降到 55.72%，聚合错误率 AVG 30.34 优于最强固定提醒基线（Safety reminder 39.53）和 Llama Guard 3（40.06）。

> **TL;DR:** This paper reframes safety alignment as a two-stage pathway in the residual stream — attention updates *recognize* harmfulness (higher linear-probe AUC in all 36 checkpoints across 18 Base–Instruct pairs from Qwen, Llama, and Gemma, 1B–27B), while MLP updates *write* harm-selective refusal evidence (measured by a logit-lens Refusal Writing Score; MLP net writing rises in 18/18 pairs after post-training vs 12/18 for attention, and its positive writing mass concentrated in the final quarter of layers grows from a median 23.01% to 63.86%). Patching experiments support the causal chain: restoring clean downstream MLP updates blocks 19–87% of the refusal shift induced by upstream attention patches. Building on this, **Sentinel** combines an attention-derived perception score P with an MLP-derived decision score D to route conditional reminders before decoding: averaged over 8 instruct models on cohorts built from AdvBench/XSTest/EvoRefuse/AlpacaEval and ReNeLLM/PAIR/AutoDAN/GCG attacks, Sentinel cuts jailbreak ASR from 95.97% (no reminder) to 35.17% and over-refusal from 88.73% to 55.72% (AVG 30.34 vs 39.53 for the best fixed Safety reminder and 40.06 for Llama Guard 3, which has the best ASR of 26.58 but the worst over-refusal of 92.53%).

## 核心贡献

- **"识别 vs 书写"的可度量框架**：把拒答行为分解为残差流上模块更新（Attention 写入 $a_t$、MLP 写入 $m_t$）的两个互补属性——有害性识别（更新上线性探针的 ROC-AUC + 对残差流可分性的增量增益）与拒答书写（logit-lens 读出 RES 在模块前后的变化量 RWS，及其有害/无害差 ΔRWS），从而把"模型知道有害"和"模型拒绝作答"在机制层面分开。
- **模块分工的系统性证据**：18 对 Base–Instruct（Qwen2.5/3/3.5、Llama 2/3/3.1/3.2、Gemma 2/3/4）上，有害信息始终更易从 Attention 更新解码（36/36 checkpoint，早期层差距最大），而 MLP 主导选择性拒答书写：Instruct 模型 MLP 净书写为正 18/18，Attention 仅 12/18。
- **后训练改写的是什么**：后训练并未系统性提升有害性解码 AUC（Base 已可解码），而是让 MLP 书写更强、更纯（抵消率 $C^M$ 0.78→0.32，拒答词表特异度 1.10→4.20）且更晚层化（末四分之一层正书写质量中位数 23.01%→63.86%，thinking 模型 66.13%）——回答了"对齐在改识别还是在改执行"。
- **推理期的时序分离**：6 个 Qwen3/Qwen3.5 模型上，有害性在整段推理中保持可解码（U 形曲线），而强 MLP 拒答书写集中在 answer_start 才跃升（中位数 16.76）——"模型可以一边识别有害一边推迟拒绝"，thinking-off 下同样成立。
- **因果干预验证通路**：单模块补丁显示 Attention 补丁移动最终拒答读出但局部 MLP 响应小、MLP 补丁两者皆动；联合"路径切断"（P-patch + D-restore）中恢复下游干净 MLP 更新可阻断上游 Attention 效应 19–87%（含 held-out 与随机层对照）。
- **Sentinel 诊断与路由系统**：自动选层得到 Attention 感知分 P 与 MLP 决策分 D，其联合平面能区分越狱（识别/书写双弱）与过度拒答（双强），四分类路由器在解码前按 P/D 选择插入哪种提醒，8 模型嵌套交叉验证下聚合错误率优于全部固定提醒与 Llama Guard 3。

## Key Contributions

- A **measurable recognition-vs-writing framework** for safety alignment on residual-stream updates: harmfulness *recognition* is linear decodability of a module's update (probe ROC-AUC plus separability gain on the residual state), while refusal *writing* is the change in logit-lens refusal evidence (RWS) caused by the update, with harm-selectivity ΔRWS = RWS(harmful) − RWS(harmless).
- **Consistent module division of labor across 18 Base–Instruct pairs** (Qwen2.5/3/3.5, Llama, Gemma; 1B–27B): attention updates expose harmfulness more readily than MLP updates in all 36 checkpoints (strongest in early layers), whereas MLP updates dominate harm-selective refusal writing in aligned models (positive net writing in 18/18 Instruct pairs vs 12/18 for attention).
- **A characterization of what post-training actually changes**: harmfulness is already decodable in Base models and post-training yields no systematic AUC gain; instead it strengthens, purifies, and *deepens* MLP refusal writing (cancellation ratio 0.78→0.32, refusal-vocabulary specificity 1.10→4.20, final-quarter share of positive writing mass 23.01%→63.86%).
- **Temporal dissociation in reasoning models**: across 6 Qwen3/Qwen3.5 models, harmfulness stays decodable throughout the reasoning span while strong MLP writing concentrates at answer onset — the model can keep recognizing harm while delaying refusal — and the pattern persists with thinking disabled.
- **Causal support via module-update counterfactuals and joint path-cutting**: attention patches shift the final refusal readout with a small local MLP response; MLP patches move both; restoring clean downstream MLP updates blocks 19–87% of the upstream attention effect (with held-out matched-site and random-layer controls).
- **Sentinel**, an automatic-layer-selection diagnostic that turns the pathway into two prompt-level scores (P from attention probes, D from MLP writing) and a four-way router that inserts conditional reminders before decoding, jointly reducing jailbreak and over-refusal errors where fixed reminders trade one off against the other.

## Methodology

**Residual-stream decomposition.** Each block writes $x_{t+1} = x_t + a_t + m_t$; measurements target the position whose output predicts the first visible answer token. Three quantities: (1) *harmfulness recognition* — a logistic probe on each module's update (AUC), plus separability gains $\delta\delta^U_t = |AUC[x_{t+1}] - AUC[x_t]|$ measuring the update's incremental contribution to linear separability of the residual state; (2) *refusal evidence* — a logit-lens readout RES mapping residual states to refusal-vs-compliance log-sum-exp; (3) *refusal writing* — RWS = ΔRES across a module update, with group-averaged ΔRWS isolating harm selectivity. Specificity is measured against 16 matched control vocabularies, and the cancellation ratio compares negative to positive writing mass.

**Scope.** 18 Base–Instruct pairs (Qwen2.5 3/7/14B, Qwen3 4/8/14B, Qwen3.5 2/4/9B, Llama-3.2-1B/3B & 3.1-8B, Gemma2 2/9/27B, Gemma3 4/12/27B), compared at each Instruct model's chat-template answer boundary; a matched-input/matched-position control repeats 6 pairs with identical plain-text cues. The reasoning analysis uses 6 Qwen3/Qwen3.5 checkpoints at six generation positions (assistant_start, reasoning_start, 25/50/75%, answer_start) under thinking-on/off.

**Interventions.** Module-update counterfactuals cache a layer's update from a harmful or benign donor and patch it into the matched counterpart (100 prompt pairs per configuration, 31 model-mode configurations). The joint path-cutting design first patches donor attention updates at frozen P layers, then either proceeds normally or restores the recipient's clean MLP updates at frozen D layers (7 model-mode configs × 100 pairs × 2 directions × 3 conditions = 4,200 readouts), with held-out matched-site restoration and random post-P MLP restoration as controls; bootstrap CIs over prompt pairs.

**Sentinel.** Layer windows are selected automatically: P from a compact plateau of attention-probe layers whose one-sided 95% lower confidence bound exceeds chance; D as the earliest sustained post-P MLP window with positive writing. P aggregates calibrated probe logits over $\mathcal{L}_P$; D averages RWS over $\mathcal{L}_D$. A four-way classifier (logistic regression / RBF SVM / small MLP with class weighting, selected in inner folds) predicts {jailbreak, correct refusal, over-refusal, benign answering} from prefill-time P/D features and routes to one of four actions: no reminder, safety reminder, helpfulness reminder, or balanced reminder. Evaluation uses nested 5-fold cross-validation grouped by underlying request over a 3,662-request pool built from six cohorts (AlpacaEval benign, AdvBench raw harmful, historical over-refusals on XSTest/EvoRefuse, historical jailbreak success on ReNeLLM/PAIR/AutoDAN/GCG, plus matched XSTest and EvoRefuse subsets; ≤500 requests per model, ≤100 per cohort), with behavior judged by a GPT-based protocol. AVG is the equal-weight mean of jailbreak ASR, benign false-refusal, and over-refusal false-refusal rates.

## Results & Highlights

- **Module dissociation (18 Base–Instruct pairs, 36 checkpoints):** attention retains a higher layer-averaged harmfulness-probe AUC than MLP in all 36 checkpoints, with the gap largest in early layers; yet attention produces positive separability increments only in early layers. MLP net refusal writing is positive in 18/18 Instruct pairs versus 12/18 for attention, and every Instruct MLP also exceeds its Base counterpart.
- **Post-training deepens MLP writing:** the median share of positive MLP writing mass contributed by the final quarter of layers rises from 23.01% (Base) to 63.86% (Instruct; 66.13% for thinking models); the MLP cancellation ratio $C^M$ falls from 0.78 to 0.32 while MLP specificity over 16 control vocabularies rises from 1.10 to 4.20 (attention: 1.66→1.20 and 0.77→1.63) — both specificity gains hold in all 18/18 pairs, so post-training sharpens writing along the refusal direction rather than writing more in arbitrary directions.
- **Answer-boundary concentration (6 Qwen3/Qwen3.5 models):** with reasoning enabled, cumulative MLP refusal writing stays near zero through the reasoning span and jumps to a median of 16.76 at answer_start, with no comparable attention increase; with reasoning disabled, the answer-boundary medians are 13.94 (MLP) vs −1.47 (attention), and MLP writing exceeds attention at the answer boundary in all six models. Appendix C confirms the boundary gap (10.00–26.47) across all four alternative refusal readouts, including prompt-disjoint data-driven vocabularies.
- **Causal blocking:** restoring clean downstream MLP updates blocks 64% of the attention-patch effect for Llama-3.1-8B (harmful→benign), 65%/69% for Qwen3-8B, and 82%/87% for Gemma4-12B — but only 19%/11% for Qwen3.5-9B, showing the pathway's strength varies by architecture. In held-out matched-site comparisons, MLP restoration removes 26.5–82.8% of the upstream effect (Llama-3.1-8B / Qwen3-8B / Gemma4-12B) versus −34.6% to +19.2% for attention restoration at the same sites, with $B_M − B_A$ positive in all three models (95% bootstrap CIs excluding zero).
- **Sentinel routing (8 instruct models, nested grouped CV):** versus no reminder, jailbreak ASR drops from 95.97% to 35.17% and over-refusal false refusal from 88.73% to 55.72%, with benign false refusal rising only 0.00%→0.13% (AVG 61.57→30.34). Against the best fixed reminder (Safety reminder, AVG 39.53 with 85.24% over-refusal), Sentinel cuts over-refusal by 29.52 points at +4.72 ASR; Llama Guard 3 attains the best ASR (26.58%) but the highest over-refusal (92.53%, AVG 40.06). Ablations give P-only AVG 38.09 and D-only 34.96 vs 30.34 joint; a 2-scalar router still beats both single signals (39.34 vs 42.96 / 41.36), and the joint-over-best-single gain is positive for 6/8 models (mean +1.50 pts, bootstrap CI [0.28, 3.10]).

## Authors & Credibility

Zixun Weng (Georgia Tech), Haoqi He and Yang Bai (independent researchers), Weifei Jin (Duke University), and Yue Huang (University of Notre Dame). The team combines LLM-safety evaluation experience (Huang has published on safety/jailbreak benchmarks) with interpretability tooling, and the work shows unusual methodological discipline for this area: matched-input and matched-position Base–Instruct controls, four independent refusal-readout constructions, held-out restoration sites, random-window controls, prompt-disjoint calibration, grouped nested cross-validation, and bootstrap intervals throughout. The paper states an AI-use disclosure (an LLM-based judge is part of the jailbreak-outcome protocol) and a reproducibility statement describing model inventories, layer-selection procedures, and per-configuration results across Appendices B–H, though no public code release is mentioned in the reviewed pages.

## Critical Assessment

- **The headline AVG gain is partly an artifact of cohort conditioning.** ASR is measured only on requests each model previously answered under attack (hence the artificial 95.97% no-reminder ASR), and over-refusal only on each model's historical over-refusals. This "own-errors" design guarantees fixed reminders look bad on both ends simultaneously; a real-world mixture would compress the gap between Sentinel (30.34) and the Safety reminder (39.53).
- **Baseline comparisons are not deployment-equivalent.** Sentinel needs white-box activation hooks at prefill for every request, whereas Llama Guard 3 is a black-box external guard that nonetheless achieves much lower ASR (26.58% vs 35.17%). No latency, memory, or engineering-overhead numbers are reported for either, so the "lower aggregate error than evaluated fixed reminders" claim is fair against reminders but arguably apples-to-oranges against Llama Guard.
- **The causal pathway is not universal, and the paper's own numbers show it.** Downstream MLP restoration blocks only 19%/11% of the attention effect for Qwen3.5-9B (vs 82–87% for Gemma4-12B); Qwen3.5's DeltaNet attention breaks the standard attention-update abstraction. The mechanism story is family-dependent in exactly the models where the architecture departs from the canonical block, and the paper treats this as a caveat rather than testing an alternative account.
- **"Recognition" is operationalized narrowly** as *linear* decodability. An informative update with near-zero separability gain (which the paper itself notes exists) would be scored as non-recognizing, so the recognition-side conclusions are claims about linear probes, not about all information a module carries.
- **Judgment pipeline risk:** jailbreak success, cohort membership, and generation-level outcomes all rest on a GPT-based behavioral judge (GPT-5.6-luna protocol); no human-verification rate for the judge is given in the reviewed text, and ASR numbers at this granularity are sensitive to judge leniency.
- On the credit side, the robustness appendix is genuinely above the bar for this literature: the module-division and boundary-timing conclusions replicate across Arditi-style and two prompt-disjoint data-driven vocabularies, thinking-on/off, matched-input/matched-position controls, and three-model routing AVG moves only 33.37–35.45% across readouts (+2.08/−0.36/+0.26 pts vs the main token set).

## Strengths

- Reframes a debate ("where does refusal live?") into two separately measurable, falsifiable quantities, then measures both exhaustively — 18 pairs, 36 checkpoints, 31 intervention configurations, 4,200 readouts — with medians and IQRs rather than cherry-picked models.
- The post-training finding (recognition unchanged, writing strengthened, deepened, and directionally sharpened) is a clean, actionable answer: alignment work should target refusal writing, not just harmfulness representations.
- Causal evidence is layered correctly: correlational probes → single-module patches → joint path-cutting with *restoration* controls → held-out sites → random-window controls, each with bootstrap CIs.
- Sentinel is a rare interpretability-to-application loop that actually helps both failure modes at once (jailbreak and over-refusal) instead of trading them, and its routing decisions are made before decoding using frozen, per-model-calibrated features.
- Honest negative details are retained: Qwen3.5-9B's weak blocking, the Gemma4-31B router fallback to no-reminder, and the non-comparability of 3-model and 8-model averages are all stated in-line.

## Limitations

- Routing evaluation is restricted to 8 instruct models (3 families), non-reasoning mode only, with cohorts capped at 100 requests each and conditioned on each model's historical failures; generalization to other attack distributions or reasoning-mode deployment is untested.
- The pathway blocking effect spans 19–87% across architectures and the residual 80%+ for Qwen3.5-9B is unexplained; the attention-to-MLP chain is one causal route, not a complete account of refusal.
- Sentinel inherits the fragility of probe/logit-lens machinery: per-model calibration of layer windows and readout vocabularies is required (frozen before evaluation, but still a per-model engineering cost), and the judge-based labeling pipeline introduces its own error floor.
- Reminder insertion itself is a weak intervention; the paper does not test whether P/D signals can steer stronger mitigations (steering vectors, decoding-time control), so the practical ceiling of the diagnosis is unknown.
- No code or data release is mentioned in the reviewed pages, which matters given the many frozen per-model configuration choices (layer windows, token sets, judge prompts).

## Relevance

对做 LLM 安全与机制可解释性的研究者是高价值工作：它把"有害性识别"与"拒答书写"解耦成可复用的度量（探针 AUC + logit-lens 拒答书写分），并给出"后训练主要改的是 MLP 晚层书写而非识别"这一可指导对齐实践的结论。Sentinel 的 P/D 前置路由对同时压低越狱率与过度拒答的工程落地有直接参考意义，但需注意其评测队列以模型自身历史错误为条件、且依赖白盒激活访问。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Why does downstream MLP restoration fail to block the attention effect in Qwen3.5-9B (19%/11%) — is it the DeltaNet attention form, the depth of writing onset, or a genuinely different circuit topology?
- Can P/D scores be extracted during reasoning (not just prefill) to trigger early refusal in thinking models, given that harmfulness stays decodable across the whole reasoning span while writing waits for answer_start?
- Would training-time interventions that directly strengthen MLP refusal writing (rather than representation-level safety tuning) produce models whose alignment gains match what post-training achieves here implicitly?
- How do the recognition/writing scores behave under distribution shift — multilingual prompts, benign-but-sensitive domains (medical, legal) — where both probe calibration and the judge protocol were never validated?
- Does the conditional-reminder router remain beneficial when jailbreak base rates are realistic (a few per 10,000 benign requests) instead of the balanced three-cohort AVG used here?
