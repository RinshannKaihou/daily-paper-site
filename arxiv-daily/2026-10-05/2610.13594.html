Key Finding · 核心发现

A real-time multi-agent tutor turns zero-reward collapse from a training dead-end into on-policy teaching moments for GRPO.

Why This Matters

Zero-reward collapse stalls RLVR training on the hardest samples; MENTOR's plug-and-play tutor is an orthogonal fix — with payoffs concentrated on small or weak models.

直击 RLVR 难样本零奖励停滞的核心痛点：与 DAPO 式过滤、静态提示库正交的在线教学路线，即插即用、prompt 模板与成本分析均可复用。

Critical Edge

Headline gains concentrate exactly where collapse is severe — on strong models like Qwen3-4B the edge over plain GRPO shrinks to ~1.1% relative (86.77 → 87.71), and all results are single runs with no variance reported.

Methodology

Diagnose, teach, re-enter GRPO

The failure being treated: under vanilla GRPO the zero-reward batch ratio climbs ~0.31 → ~0.44 within 250 steps while validation accuracy stalls at ~0.42.

1 · CASF

Hook after reward scoring flags instances where all N trajectories fail — keeps the full failed sets as diagnostic material instead of discarding them (DAPO-style).

2 · FDCG

Attribution agent distills keywords localizing the misconception (never the solution) → Generation writes a leakage-free hint → Judgment/Repair loop grades HIGH/LOW and rewrites (2 rounds; safe fallback).

3 · Injection

Augmented query q′ = hint ⊕ question re-enters GRPO: L(θ) = LGRPO(D∖C) + LGRPO(C′) — loss, optimizer, reward untouched.

Why not existing fixes? Filtering (DAPO) throws away diagnostic gold; static hint libraries (LUFFY, QUESTA, HINT, Scaf-GRPO) drift off-policy as the model evolves; post-hoc correction (Critique-GRPO, SRPO) looks backward. MENTOR keeps learning on-policy and adds forward-looking, policy-aligned teaching.

Guarantees by design. Hints forbid answer/number/formula leakage and step-by-step instructions, so they stay aligned as the policy improves; the objective is training-agnostic and the hook is plug-and-play.

Setup: 5 models × math (DeepMath-103K) + code (LiveCodeBench) · 8 SOTA baselines, all hints from the same Qwen3-32B under a matched generation budget · 8× H20-3E GPUs · Max Retry = 2 optimal.

Strengths

What holds up

  • Attacks the cause of zero-reward collapse, not its symptoms; Figure-1 dynamics (collapse ratio falling while accuracy climbs) back the causal story.
  • Clean engineering: loss/optimizer/reward untouched, training-objective-agnostic, and ~24% faster than Critique-GRPO with zero curation labor.
  • Fair baselines — every hint consumer draws from the same Qwen3-32B under a matched generation budget.
  • Exceptional transparency: complete prompt templates for all four agents plus three worked case studies with real rollouts and judgments.
  • Strong OOD gain (MMLU 17.14 → 52.16) suggests the mechanism teaches reasoning structure rather than benchmark-specific patterns.
Limitations

Where it strains

  • Single-modality only; multi-modal and agentic RLVR — where collapse is arguably worse — are explicitly unexplored (authors' own admission).
  • Gains live on small/weak models: ~1.1% relative over GRPO on Qwen3-4B, statistically indistinguishable from Scaf-GRPO on DeepSeek-Coder-1.3B (37.58 vs 37.54).
  • Single-run, single-epoch, no confidence intervals on 30-problem AIME; celebrated swings can be 2–3 problems.
  • Qwen3-32B teacher capability is a confound the ablations don't fully isolate; pre-defined fallback hints quietly reintroduce manual curation.
  • Collapse-recovery rate and hint-token overhead are never reported as numbers.
Questions for Further Exploration
  1. What fraction of collapsed instances does hint injection actually rescue — and does efficacy decay as the policy drifts, echoing the critique aimed at static methods?
  2. Would the gains survive a weaker teacher (e.g., a 7B judge/generator), or is most of the benefit simply distilling Qwen3-32B's knowledge through hints?
  3. Does hint-augmented training create inference-time dependence on hint-like scaffolding — and does that explain the outsized MMLU transfer?
  4. Can the paradigm extend to multi-modal or agentic RLVR, where per-step failures define collapse and a "safe fallback hint" is much harder to pre-define?
  5. How does MENTOR compose with curriculum or data-selection methods — is pedagogical intervention subsumed by simply training on easier data first?
