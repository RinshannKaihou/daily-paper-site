> **一句话总结：** 作者把发展心理学的关系匹配范式（RMTS）改造成几何图形受控刺激集，测了 7 个前沿 API 模型与 Qwen3.5/Gemma-4/InternVL3 共 20 个检查点：无 CoT 时关系选择率从 32.2%（GPT-5.4-mini）到 100%（GPT-5.4）相差 68pp，且随模型规模、能力档位、场景物体数上升而上升，类似人类的"关系转变"；逐层 RSA + 逐头激活修补（CMA）揭示 VLM 内部存在两条竞争回路——早期按表面物体特征组织、后期按抽象关系组织，消融 Gemma-4-26B 的 top-3 关系头使直接回答掉到 50%（随机水平），而 ARC-AGI-1 上消融这些头比消融随机头掉分更多，说明该关系回路被自然抽象推理任务复用。

> **TL;DR:** This paper imports the Relational Match-to-Sample (RMTS) paradigm from developmental psychology into a fully controlled geometric-stimulus benchmark for VLMs, where a model must label a test scene that shares a sample's abstract color/size relation (relational match, new objects) rather than one that reuses the samples' surface objects (object match). On 729 trials × 2 orderings, relational rate ranges from 32.2% (GPT-5.4-mini) to 100% (GPT-5.4) with no CoT, and chain-of-thought lifts GPT-5.4-mini by +51.2 pp and Claude-Haiku-4.5 by +57.4 pp. Per-layer RSA plus causal mediation (per-head activation patching) shows two competing circuits — an early object-feature circuit and a later relational circuit — and zeroing the three relational heads found by CMA drops Gemma-4-26B's direct relational rate from 100.0% to 50.0% (chance), while CoT restores it to 100%; the same heads matter more than random heads on ARC-AGI-1.

## 核心贡献

- **受控 RMTS 基准**：将比较/发展心理学中的 RMTS 任务形式化为几何刺激集（颜色×大小×形状参数化，5 种模式 AAA/AAB/ABA/ABB/ABC × 2 属性 = 25 种关系，n=3 时 729 个独立 trial × 2 种候选顺序），关系匹配与物体匹配由构造互斥：选关系匹配必须推断抽象模式，选物体匹配只需表面特征匹配。
- **行为学地图（4 个杠杆）**：能力档位（同家族 Claude-Opus-4.6 与 Claude-Haiku-4.5 相差 58pp）、模型规模（Gemma-4 从 26.3% 单调升至 100%）、物体数 n（n=2→20 使物体偏好模型的关系率上升）、刺激噪声（逐物体高斯噪声使关系率下降，颜色噪声比大小噪声伤害更大）都会把模型推向/推离关系匹配，呈现类似人类"关系转变"（relational shift）的发展轨迹。
- **机制发现：两条竞争回路**：逐层 RSA 显示早期层按物体特征组织四个图像（object circuit），后期层转向按抽象关系组织（relational circuit），交叉点随规模提前；逐头激活修补（CMA）在 Gemma-4-26B 上定位到 L28 H12（深层、支持关系）与 L12 H11（中层、支持物体）两个关键头，且该两阶段结构在另外 5 个检查点上复现。
- **因果验证与域外迁移**：消融 CMA 找到的 top-3 关系头使 Gemma-4-26B 直接回答关系率 100%→50%（随机头消融无此效应）；在 ARC-AGI-1 上消融这些头比消融同数量随机头掉分更多，且 CoT 可绕过被消融的关系头（消融后加 CoT 恢复 100%）。
- **严谨的混杂控制**：标签 nonce 词轮换消除 A/B 偏置、两种候选顺序取平均、贪心解码；针对"形状一致性"残余线索的消融显示所有模型关系率变化 ≤2pp，主结论不依赖该线索。

## Key Contributions

- A formally controlled RMTS stimulus suite (objects parameterized by color/size/shape; 25 relations; 729 unique trials × 2 orderings at n=3) where the relational and object candidates are mutually antagonistic by construction, imported from comparative/developmental psychology to give VLM abstract reasoning a calibrated yardstick.
- A four-lever behavioral map: capability tier (58 pp within-family gap between Claude-Opus-4.6 and Claude-Haiku-4.5), model scale (Gemma-4: 26.3%→100% with r=+0.92 vs log-size), scene object count (n=2→20 raises relational rate in object-biased models), and per-object stimulus noise all shift the object-vs-relational choice, producing a developmental-trajectory-like picture mirroring the human "relational shift".
- A mechanistic account: per-layer RSA reveals an early object circuit and a later relational circuit whose crossover moves earlier with scale; per-head activation patching (CMA) localizes the competition to individual heads (L28 H12 relational, L12 H11 object in Gemma-4-26B), with the same two-stage organization on five further checkpoints.
- Causal validation: ablating the top-3 relational heads drops Gemma-4-26B's direct-answer relational rate from 100.0% to 50.0% (chance) while random-head ablation does not; the same relational heads are recruited on ARC-AGI-1, where their ablation degrades cell-wise accuracy more than random-head ablation on both Gemma-4-26B and Gemma-4-31B.
- Careful confound control: nonce-label cycling, both answer orderings averaged per trial, greedy decoding, and a shape-coherence ablation (Appendix H) showing all relational-rate deltas within ±2 pp when the residual shape cue is removed.

## Methodology

**Task construction.** Each scene contains n objects drawn from C={red,blue,green} × S={small,medium,large} × H={circle,triangle,square}; a sample of size n is an ordered tuple sharing one shape, and a scene's "relation" is the pair of equivalence patterns (P_color, P_size) over {AAA, AAB, ABA, ABB, ABC} — 25 relations in total. A trial shows K=2 samples sharing relation R* (different shapes and attribute values) each tagged with a nonsense label XXX, plus two test scenes: the *relational match* instantiates R* with brand-new objects/shape (its color/size values disjoint from the samples), while the *object match* is built only from objects literally appearing in the samples but breaks R*. The model must answer "Which one is a XXX?" with one forced-choice token; the relational rate (bias-corrected by averaging both orderings) is reported over all trials.

**Models and protocol.** Seven frontier API models (GPT-5.4, GPT-5.4-mini, Claude-Opus-4.6, Claude-Sonnet-4.6, Claude-Haiku-4.5, Gemini-3.1-Pro, Gemini-3.1-Flash) and three open-source families — Qwen3.5 (0.8B–27B, 5 sizes), Gemma-4 (E2B–31B, 4 sizes), InternVL3 (1B–38B, 5 sizes) — under greedy decoding; open models are read directly from the A/B next-token logits. The nonce label cycles over a fixed pool of five three-letter strings; no element of the pool ever primed a real concept.

**Behavioral levers.** (1) capability tier and CoT ("let's think step by step"); (2) scale within family; (3) object count n ∈ {2,...,20} re-encoding the same 25 relations; (4) per-object Gaussian noise on color (RGB radius σ_c ∈ {0,5,10,20,40,80}) and size (σ_s ∈ {0,1,2,4,8} px) that preserves each object's categorical identity.

**Internal analysis.** For every layer ℓ, the residual-stream activation at the last token of each of the four images yields a 4×4 cosine-similarity matrix RSM^(ℓ), correlated (Pearson) against two binary targets: T_rel (relational candidate close to both samples) and T_obj (object candidate close to both samples). Rel-RSA(ℓ) − Obj-RSA(ℓ) tracks which representation dominates at each depth, and the peak-layer gap Δ-RSA is correlated with n. For causal evidence, per-head activation patching on Gemma-4-26B (clean relational-context vs corrupted object-context runs) measures each (layer, head)'s logit shift Δℓ = logit_A − logit_B; the strongest relational heads are then zeroed (scale 0 via o_proj pre-hook). Transfer is tested on ARC-AGI-1 (66 tasks for Gemma-4-26B, 80 for Gemma-4-31B, grids rendered as palette-colored images, no CoT), reporting cell-wise accuracy under clean / rel-3-zero / random-3 conditions.

## Results & Highlights

- **Frontier spread with no CoT (729-trial RMTS, relational rate):** GPT-5.4-mini 32.2% vs GPT-5.4 100.0% (a 68-pp gap), and within Anthropic's family Claude-Opus-4.6 hits 100.0% while Claude-Haiku-4.5 sits at 42.5% (58 pp). CoT lifts GPT-5.4-mini from 32.2% to 83.4% (+51.2 pp) and Claude-Haiku-4.5 from 42.5% to 99.9% (+57.4 pp) — the two weakest frontier models — while already-saturated models gain ≤0.3 pp.
- **Scaling drives relational matching:** Gemma-4 rises monotonically 26.3% (E2B) → 100.0% (31B) with r=+0.92 between log model size and relational rate; Qwen3.5 goes 50.0% (0.8B) → 89.3% (9B) with r=+0.80 (with a −3.4 pp dip at 27B); InternVL3 reaches 100.0% at 38B after an apparent dip to 28.9% at 8B (r=+0.58). The no-CoT rate correlates with log-size in every family.
- **More objects per scene promote the relational match:** from n=2 to n=20, per-model Pearson r(n; Rel) grows for object-biased models — InternVL3-14B from +0.10 to +0.83, Gemma-4-E4B from +0.53 to +0.80, Qwen3.5-8B +0.72→+0.82 — and internally, Δ-RSA (peak Rel−Obj RSA gap) correlates strongly with n at 5 of 6 checkpoints (Gemma-4-E4B +0.81, InternVL3-8B +0.81, Qwen3.5-27B +0.80), showing the relational circuit strengthens rather than the answer merely flipping.
- **Stimulus noise degrades relational matching asymmetrically:** under the strongest color jitter (σ_c=80 RGB) Gemma-4-26B falls from ~100% to ~92% while Gemma-4-E4B falls from ~75% to ~50% (a 25-pp drop); InternVL3-38B loses only 8–9 pp whereas InternVL3-8B drops from ~25% to ~10%; color perturbation costs more than matching size perturbation in most models.
- **Causal mediation localizes the choice:** on Gemma-4-26B, zeroing the single strongest relational head (L28 H12, Δℓ=−1.02) drops direct-answer relational rate from 100.0% to 86.7%, and zeroing the top three heads (L28 H12, L28 H7, L19 H2) drops it to 50.0% (chance) — yet with CoT the same ablated model is back at 100.0%, i.e., CoT bypasses the relational heads via a separate language-mediated route.
- **The circuit transfers to ARC-AGI-1:** ablating the three CMA-identified relational heads degrades cell-wise accuracy more than ablating three random heads on both Gemma-4-26B and Gemma-4-31B (Figure 8); notably, Gemma-4-26B's clean exact-match on ARC is only ≈0.23, which is why the relaxed cell-wise metric is used at all.
- **Robustness controls:** on the shape-controlled stimulus set (object match rebuilt as a single-shape scene) relational rates move by at most ±2 pp (e.g., InternVL3-38B 100.0→98.1); the alternative RSA target yields layer-wise curves correlating at r=0.91–1.00 across all 14 open checkpoints; on the structurally different numerical-equality relation the scale effect persists (+46 pp Qwen 2B→9B: 47.7→93.8; +73 pp InternVL3 8B→38B: 25.8→98.4) and frontier models come off the ceiling (Gemini-3.1-Pro 99.0, Claude-Opus-4.6 93.0, GPT-5.4 82.5).

## Authors & Credibility

The authors are from The University of Tokyo (Minegishi, Kojima, Iwasawa, Matsuo — the Matsuo Lab, a prolific LLM/interpretability group; Minegishi has prior work on grokking and emergent analogical reasoning in Transformers) and Google DeepMind (Furuta, whose footnote marks the role as "advisory only"). The paper demonstrates strong command of both the cognitive-science literature (Gentner, Christie, Goswami on the relational shift and RMTS) and modern mechanistic interpretability practice (RSA, activation patching following Meng/Wang/Heimersheim conventions). The reproducibility statement is unusually thorough: exact HF identifiers and API model tags, greedy decoding everywhere, containerized H200 runtimes, verbatim prompts, and full head-edit recipes are all in the appendices. No conflict-of-interest concerns beyond the advisory DeepMind affiliation.

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty is real but the benchmark's dynamic range is limited at the top.** The combination of a developmental-psychology paradigm with layer-wise and head-level causal analysis on frontier VLMs is genuinely new (the closest prior work, Lepori et al. 2024, studied ViTs on same/different judgements without VLMs or causal head edits). However, on the canonical ordinal relations 4 of 7 frontier models sit at exactly 100% no-CoT, so the headline instrument cannot discriminate among the strongest systems; the authors concede this and partially rescue dynamic range with the numerical-equality relation (GPT-5.4 falls to 82.5%, Gemini-3.1-Pro to 99.0%), but that relation is only evaluated on 64 trials per open model and 2 checkpoints per family — a thin slice for a headline robustness claim.

**"Circuit" is used loosely, by the authors' own admission.** Footnote 3 states the term is functional shorthand — "layers and heads with a distinguishable representational signature and a measurable causal effect under patching and ablation — not a complete, reverse-engineered computational subgraph." RSA evidence is purely correlational; the causal case rests on patching/ablating a handful of heads (top-3 rel heads vs 3 random heads), and full CMA maps are shown for only two Gemma-4 checkpoints, with five further checkpoints run on a small 30-trial budget. The claim that a few heads "carry most of the relational effect" is well supported for Gemma-4, but generalizing the two-stage story to all of Qwen and InternVL3 leans on thinner data.

**Data quirks a careful reader should note.** (1) The ARC-AGI-1 evaluation swaps the official exact-match metric for cell-wise accuracy because clean exact-match ≈0.23 for Gemma-4-26B "crashes to near zero" under any ablation — an honest and reasonable relaxation, but it also reveals the transfer experiment runs on a model that is weak on ARC to begin with, on task subsets of only 66 and 80 tasks. (2) Several Anthropic CoT entries in Table 5 are computed over the parsed subset that survived API-credit failures mid-run, a nontrivial censoring of exactly the family with the largest CoT delta. (3) The initial stimulus design contained a shape-coherence confound (the object match mixes ≥2 sample shapes, the relational match is single-shape), caught only in Appendix H; the ±2 pp control is convincing, but it shows the headline design needed a post-hoc patch.

**What is genuinely strong:** the bias-cancellation protocol (every trial in both orderings; nonce cycling), the mutually antagonistic formal definitions of the two candidates, the noise sweep acting on the same internal quantity as n in the opposite direction (Δ-RSA +0.24 → +0.15 while behavior falls 78%→69%), and the willingness to include unflattering results (Qwen3.5-27B dipping −3.4 pp below 9B; CoT *hurting* several thin models, e.g., Qwen3.5-8B −4.0 pp) all point to an unusually careful measurement culture.

## Strengths

- A rare bridge between developmental psychology and VLM interpretability: the RMTS construction gives binary, causally interpretable trials with chance = 50%, and the two candidate types are provably antagonistic rather than merely "harder/easier".
- Behavioral claims are backed by a causal chain: RSA (where) → patching (which heads) → ablation (necessity) → CoT bypass (route dissociation) → ARC-AGI-1 transfer (domain generality).
- Extensive confound control seldom seen in benchmark papers: label cycling, ordering averaging, shape-coherence ablation (±2 pp), an alternative RSA target (r=0.91–1.00 across 14 checkpoints), and a structurally different relation (numerical equality) to fight ceiling effects.
- Model coverage is unusually broad (7 frontier API models + 14 open checkpoints across 3 families) with consistent per-family scaling trajectories, all under greedy decoding with exact identifiers released.
- Honest reporting of negative and awkward results (scale non-monotonicity in Qwen/InternVL3, negative CoT effects, attribute-direction asymmetries between Anthropic/OpenAI and Gemma).

## Limitations

- **Synthetic, narrow stimulus distribution:** relations are defined only over color/size ordinal patterns on geometric objects; spatial, causal, and semantic relations and naturalistic scenes are untouched (the authors list this explicitly), so the "relational shift" trajectory may not describe how VLMs abstract over real images.
- **Ceiling effects for the strongest models** on the canonical set (four frontier models at 100% no-CoT); the numerical-equality rescue uses small trial counts (64 per model, 128 judgements) and only partially restores discriminative power.
- **Mechanistic scope:** full per-head CMA exists for two Gemma-4 checkpoints (26B, 31B); five further checkpoints use a 30-trial budget; the composition of the identified heads is not characterized; and the CoT bypass route — arguably the most interesting finding — is left mechanistically unexplained.
- **ARC-AGI-1 transfer is narrow:** two models, 66/80-task subsets, a non-standard cell-wise accuracy metric, no CoT, and a model whose clean exact-match is only ≈0.23; the "relational heads are recruited on ARC" claim is supported qualitatively (rel-3 < random-3) rather than with large effect sizes on both checkpoints.
- Binary forced choice with chance at 50% means weak models near 28–50% are hard to interpret: they may lack relational abstraction entirely rather than latching the object circuit, and the paper's per-ordering averaging, while principled, cannot create resolution that the task design does not have.

## Relevance

这篇论文正中当前 VLM 机制可解释性与抽象推理评测的交叉热点：它不仅给出了一个可复现、抗混杂的 RMTS 受控基准来刻画"模型到底在匹配物体还是匹配关系"，还用 RSA+激活修补把行为差异落到具体的层和注意力头上，并在 ARC-AGI-1 上做了因果迁移验证。对任何研究 VLM 抽象推理、多模态机制分析或规模涌现行为的人来说，这是既提供工具（刺激集+分析管线）又提供机制结论的一手工作。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- What is the language-mediated route that lets CoT restore 100% relational accuracy after the relational heads are zeroed — does verbalization re-implement the relation comparison in later layers, or bypass vision-derived features entirely?
- Do the identified relational heads emerge during pretraining or during visual-instruction tuning? A training-dynamics study over checkpoints could connect the object→relational crossover to the human developmental timeline more precisely.
- Do the same heads carry the relation when it is defined over spatial configurations, causal trajectories, or naturalistic object bundles, or does the circuit fragment across relation types?
- The negative CoT effect on thin models (e.g., Qwen3.5-8B −4.0 pp, InternVL3-2B −3.3 pp): is this reasoning noise, parse failure, or the attribute-asymmetry effect the authors observe under no-CoT?
- Would relation-focused interventions (e.g., steering along the L28 H12 subspace, or OCR-style relational bottlenecks) improve ARC-AGI-1 exact-match rather than merely degrade it when removed — i.e., is the relational circuit a usable lever, not just a diagnostic?
