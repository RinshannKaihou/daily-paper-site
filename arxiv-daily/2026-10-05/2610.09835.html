A Deafening Silence: Catastrophic Forgetting Lives in the Output Embeddings of Tokens the Data Never Speaks

Jonghyun Han, Younghoon Song, Jongyoul Park · Seoul Nat’l Univ. of Science & Technology / KILIST · 2026-10-07

catastrophic-forgetting continual-pretraining adam-optimizer output-embeddings data-free

Key metric

39.4–67.9%

of forgetting removed by ε=1×10⁻⁴ on the output projection only — 7 stable settings, 160M–12B params, 4 model families, target learning intact (+0.1–10.9%).

Why this matters

Reframes forgetting as a one-line optimizer misconfiguration plus a data-only pre-diagnostic — directly actionable for any replay-free continued-pretraining or LoRA pipeline.

灾难性遗忘几乎全部集中在「新语料中从未出现的低频词的输出嵌入」上：仅把输出投影的 Adam ε 提高到 1×10⁻⁴，即可在七个稳定设置中消除 39.4–67.9% 的遗忘且不损失目标域学习。

⚠ Critical edge

“Seven stable settings” excludes exactly the unstable ones: Pythia-410M is seed-bimodal (32.7% vs −7.0%) and 160M collapses outright — a small-model practitioner cannot know their regime in advance.

Methodology — how they pinned it down

  1. A token is absent from the new corpus — it is never a target, so softmax only ever pushes it down.
  2. Every step delivers a one-sided gradient −pᵢ·h: small but perfectly sign-consistent, never diluted by an appearance event.
  3. Adam’s m̂/√v̂ turns any sign-consistent gradient into a full-learning-rate update; default ε=1×10⁻⁸ can’t brake it (output coords sit 2–3 orders below body).
  4. Low-√v̄ output rows drift along one shared direction — SVD top-1 carries 66–81% of drift energy vs 3–13% in controls.
The fix: ε=1×10⁻⁴ scoped to the output projection’s optimizer group — during training only. Post-hoc edits recover <5%.
  • Causal-share protocol: freeze one (site, √v̄ band) at pre-trained values with mass-matched controls — location, not parameter count, governs forgetting.
  • Grid: Pythia 160M–12B, Qwen2.5-0.5B, TinyLlama-1.1B, OLMo-2-1B × Korean / code / math / Alpaca; 40M-token budget (12.6M for instruction tuning), AdamW, 3–6 seeds, budget-matched.
  • Free-diagnostic: “vocabulary deficiency” (old-domain token positions with new-corpus count <1,000) predicts forgetting rank order from tokenizer stats alone — rank-only (Spearman 0.60, below the registered 0.7).

KL’s 96.5% only “wins” by destroying 95.3% of new-domain learning.

  • LoRA’s secret is the released head: freezing it, forgetting is 0.121 nats; releasing the output head alone spikes it 23× to 2.820 — and output-scoped ε removes 91.1% of it.
  • Composes with replay: 1% replay + ε → 79.8% (independence predicts 78.0%); super-additive on OLMo (74.4% vs 64.8%).
  • Longer budgets strengthen it: at 160M tokens, 12B reduction grows 76.2→84.1%.
  • Post-hoc repair fails: shared-drift edits recover at most +4.6%; full removal costs −7.7% to −133.6% of target learning.
  • Capability check (5 benchmarks, ε=1×10⁻⁵, two Pythia checkpoints): 58.14 vs 56.05 for default ε — but this is a weaker setting than the grid’s 1×10⁻⁴.

Strengths

  • Genuinely causal: train-time freezing with mass-matched controls replaces the correlational drift metrics that produced conflicting localization claims before.
  • Operationally trivial defense: no architecture change, no data, no per-model tuning — reduction stays flat across a 100× ε span at 1.4B (40.9–45.6%).
  • Every mechanism link is measurable: gradient derivation → drift directionality (0.67–0.90) → SVD shared direction (66–81% energy) → ε sensitivity.
  • Rare honesty: pre-registered predictions (including one openly missed), a named attention anomaly they refuse to explain away, seed bimodality in table captions, explicit failure boundaries.
  • Unifying decision rule: rare-token gating ≈ ε; bottom-layer freezing fails because output dominates; prior remedies collapse into one variable — vocabulary deficiency.

Limitations

  • Adam/AdamW family only — Muon-style optimizers are explicitly out of scope.
  • In-distribution tuning breaks it: no new vocabulary (Alpaca) shifts the causal share to the body, where ε fails (−3.7% to −1.6%).
  • Unexplained anomaly: freezing attention removes 42.7% on KoAlpaca at 410M — “output dominates” doesn’t fully cover instruction tuning.
  • Diagnostic is rank-only: no absolute magnitudes; cross-tokenizer comparison fails (byte-normalized Korean loss 1.93 vs 1.35–1.74).
  • Small budgets, loss-only main grid: 40M/12.6M tokens; benchmark checks exist only at weaker ε=1×10⁻⁵ on two Pythia checkpoints.
  • Scaling is Pythia-only; LoRA ablation at one rank (r=16); no code release mentioned.

Questions for further exploration

  1. Does output-scoped ε hold for MoE models and non-AdamW optimizers (Muon, SOAP), where the coordinate-wise second-moment argument breaks?
  2. Can the KoAlpaca attention-freeze anomaly (42.7% at 410M) be reconciled with — or does it falsify — the output-dominates claim under per-parameter-matched comparison?
  3. Does the intervention hold at realistic billion-token budgets with benchmark-verified capability retention at the actual grid setting ε=1×10⁻⁴?
  4. Is Pythia-410M’s seed bimodality a checkpoint quirk, or a general small-model phenomenon that predicts when the ε dial silently fails?
  5. Can the rank-only vocabulary-deficiency diagnostic become a cross-tokenizer, magnitude-level predictor without violating the data-free constraint?
