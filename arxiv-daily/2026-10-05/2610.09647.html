Under duplicate_data, centred RankMe climbs 13.5 pooled s.d. above healthy (r_effΣ nearly doubles: 174.8 → 329.2) as probe loss degrades 75% — a one-sided monitor rates the worst checkpoint healthiest.

Why this matters

Anyone using rank-style spectral statistics to monitor post-training inherits a vision prior that inverts here — verify direction before trusting any alert.

“秩下降 = 退化”的视觉先验在 LLM 后训练中会反向：谱监控先验证符号，再信任告警。

Critical edge

The ensemble false-alarms on the held-out healthy branch in all three folds (FP@61/460/450) despite 12–20σ thresholds — and the inversion rests on one mechanism × 3 seeds of one 0.6B model.

Methodology — fork-valid by construction

  • Model & regimes. Qwen3-0.6B (d = 1024, 28 layers), full AdamW fine-tune on Dolly-15k. Modes: healthy · high_lr (LR ×8, no warm-up) · duplicate_data (8 examples cycled) · domain_shift_v2 (GSM8K continual) · narrow_domain (negative control, +0.06 nats, below the damage gate).
  • Diagnostics. Per-token hidden-state stats on a fixed 256-sequence held-out probe, reused verbatim: RankMe, RankMec, r_effΣ, k95, ΔH, s_cb — all float64. Held-out NLL is the pre-registered damage proxy. Theorem 1 (ΔH ≥ 0) is machine-checked in Lean 4 / Mathlib.
  • Detector protocol. Directions, margins and channel selection calibrated only on healthy branches of other seeds (leave-one-seed-out); two-sided Page-CUSUM over 5 channels (k = 0.5), union bound α/2C; false alarms scored on the held-out healthy seed; separation in pooled s.d. with trajectories (not checkpoints) as observations.

  • Detection is easy; lead is not. The ensemble fires 3/3 folds in every regime within 10–60 steps of the fork, direction regime-specific as predicted — but leads held-out loss in only 1 of 9 folds (duplicate_data, +10–1000 steps).
  • Metric audit. On raw layer-14, one direction carries 99.5% of the trace: r_effΣ floors to 1.06/1024 while RankMe reads 120.13 on the same spectrum; per-token RMS normalisation restores it to 308.96 — necessary for covariance rank only, not for either RankMe form.
  • Mechanism: dispersion. λ1 falls 84.79 → 74.53 while total variance T rises 649.6 → 820.3; numerical rank stays full; every statistic plateaus by step 200 and holds to step 1000.
  • Adaptation is the hard case. GSM8K test NLL improves 1.40 → 1.10 while the Dolly probe degrades 2.58 → 2.98 — the monitor must flag the stability cost of an update that succeeds at its own objective.
  • Flipped side-finding. s_cb falls in every above-gate regime (the opposite sign to the vision-collapse literature): damage spreads representations apart.

Strengths

  • Falsifies a load-bearing imported prior (“rank falls under degradation”) with a controlled, seed-replicated regime structure — not anecdotes.
  • Dispersion-not-collapse is backed by numbers (λ1 down, T up, full rank throughout), not labels.
  • The pre-registered, fork-valid, two-sided protocol with held-out healthy false alarms is a reusable standard for any early-warning claim — arguably the paper’s most durable artifact.
  • Exceptional honesty: the negative result is stated in full; Lean-checked theorem with printed axiom audit; frozen DETECTOR_PROTOCOL.md; ≈22 min per Phase-1 run on one 24 GB GPU makes replication cheap.
  • Surviving practical rules: report RankMe and covariance rank separately; RMS-normalise per token before covariance statistics; always test both directions.

Limitations

  • Every number is Qwen3-0.6B full fine-tuning; larger models, LoRA/RLHF-style updates, and other architectures are untested.
  • The upward inversion replicates on one mechanism only (duplicate_data × 3 seeds); the title’s general claim leans on that alone.
  • The “no lead” bar is same-probe NLL on a fixed 256-sequence probe — not downstream capability; no benchmark sweep is monitored.
  • Two calibration seeds cannot certify the union-bound level (resolution 1/2 vs required α/2C = 0.005); the honest claim degrades to time-to-false-alarm ≥ 140 steps.
  • ΔH “moves in the unexpected direction” in Table 2 — acknowledged but unexplained.

Questions for further exploration

  1. Does dispersion-not-collapse replicate at 7B+ scale and under LoRA/RLHF-style updates, or is it specific to small-model full fine-tuning?
  2. Can the consistently signed s_cb channel — calibrated on healthy data only — deliver genuine lead time over held-out loss (the paper tests detection, not s_cb lead)?
  3. Would anytime-valid tests (e-processes, confidence sequences) fix false-alarm certification at n = 3, or does healthy-null misspecification remain the binding constraint?
  4. What drives variance redistribution into tail directions under duplication — is spectral dispersion statistically linked to memorisation, and could it be exploited rather than merely flagged?
  5. When an update succeeds at its own objective (GSM8K 1.40 → 1.10) but the probe degrades (2.58 → 2.98), should a monitor alert at all — and how should it value-align the two?
