> **一句话总结：** 来自 Amazon 的作者系统训练了 180 个分类器（判别式 BERT、伪生成式与全生成式 GPT-2 三种范式 × 3 规模 × 5 数据集 × 3 种子），首次系统测量"服务上下文"（batch 大小/组成、padding 宽度、精度、量化、硬件、后端）对文本分类器的影响：仅改变 padding 宽度就翻转约 10% 的预测标签——且全部集中在两个 GPT-2 家族（根因是学习型绝对位置编码随左 padding 位移），而 fp32 下 batch 形状翻转 0 个标签、bf16 下却移动高达 56.7 个百分点的概率质量；GPU→量化 CPU 整机迁移最多让全生成式分类器 39% 的标签翻转，并给出逐机制的缓解手段（真 token 位置规则、batch 不变 kernel、固定离线 8-bit scale、低 margin 重算可检出 4,644 个留出翻转中的几乎所有，代价 22% 重算）。

> **TL;DR:** The first systematic study of whether serving-context nondeterminism — documented for text generation — also hits text classifiers. The authors train 180 classifiers from scratch (discriminative BERT, pseudo-generative, and fully generative GPT-2 formulations across SST-2, SST-5, AG News, Emotion, and IMDb, 3 sizes, 3 seeds) and evaluate 3,960 serving contexts that change only deployment/request settings while holding the checkpoint and text fixed. The dominant effect is structural, not numerical: changing only the padded width flips ~10% of predicted labels — all in the GPT-2-based generative families, because left padding shifts learned absolute position embeddings — while padding alone moves no labels for BERT or rotary/NoPE pretrained models (Qwen3, ModernBERT). Numerically, changing batch shape flips zero labels across 256,104 fp32 comparisons (probability movement < 10^-9) yet moves up to 56.7 percentage points of predicted probability mass under bf16, and a full GPU-to-quantized-CPU migration flips ~5.6% of fully generative labels on average, up to 39% on its worst checkpoint. Each mechanism gets a matched mitigation (real-token position rule, batch-invariant matmul kernels, fixed offline 8-bit scale, low-margin recompute), plus formal results: a width-invariance theorem, a margin certificate, and an impossibility-style theorem on joint speed/accuracy/invariance.

## 核心贡献

- **首个针对分类器的服务上下文 nondeterminism 系统实证研究**：将服务因素组织为"部署级 / 请求级 × 结构性 / 数值性"的分类法，从头训练 180 个分类器（4 种 transformer 分类范式 × 3 规模 × 5 数据集 × 3 种子），评估 3,960 个服务上下文；每次比较只改动一个记录在案的设置，checkpoint 与文本严格固定。关键设定：重复完全相同的上下文在所有硬件/软件配置上均逐位（bitwise）复现 logits，证明效应来自设置本身而非运行间随机性。
- **发现 padding 是主导效应且是结构性的**：仅改 padded width 就翻转约 10% 的预测标签，超过任何其他单一因素；所有 padding 导致的翻转都发生在两个 GPT-2 家族（约每五次比较一次），BERT 与 masked-LM 分类器零翻转。机制定位：右 padding 保持真 token 位置索引不变，左 padding 把真 token 推向更高张量列，改变学习型绝对位置编码 → 输入本身变了。用旋转位置编码（Qwen3）或 NoPE（ModernBERT）的预训练模型零翻转；"按真 token 顺序赋位置"的干预在 GPU/CPU 上都能恢复宽度不变性，定位了因果。
- **数值效应与格式耦合、且与结构效应分离**：fp32 下改 batch 形状在 256,104 次比较中翻转 0 个标签、概率移动 < 10^-9；bf16 下同一改动移动高达 56.7 个百分点的预测概率质量；8-bit 量化路径更大。fp32→bf16 翻转约 1% 的判别式 BERT 标签、GPT-2 生成式分类器分数移动最高 0.56；每 batch 重算 scale 的 8-bit 推理翻转 192 个全生成式标签中的 4 个、分数移动最高 0.84。
- **组合效应与"标签 ≠ 分数"两种监测视角**：GPU→量化 CPU 整机迁移使判别式 BERT 翻转 0.3%、伪生成式约 2%、全生成式 GPT-2 平均约 5.6%（最差 checkpoint 达 39%）；真实流量动态 batching 使全生成式分类器在 fp32 下翻转约 1.6%。稳定标签与大幅概率移动共存：仅看标签的监控会颠倒两个组合效应的排序（流量变化移动原始分数是迁移的数倍，但翻转比例相同）。
- **逐机制缓解 + 形式化理论**：真 token 位置规则（训练与推理同用，否则精度退化或预测坍缩；顺带允许动态 padding，吞吐提升 2–2.5×）；batch 不变 matmul kernel（近零精度代价，大 batch 下延迟 1.3–1.5×）；固定离线 8-bit scale（部署级选择）；对低 margin 预测重算参考上下文（22% 重算量即检出几乎全部翻转）。理论三条：Thm 5.1 宽度不变性由位置规则决定（含学习型绝对位置编码的逆命题）；Prop 5.1 margin 证书（m(x) > 2TV(x,c,c₀) 即标签不变）；Thm 5.2 速度、精度与服务上下文不变性的联合不可行性。

## Key Contributions

- First systematic empirical study of serving-context nondeterminism for text classifiers (as opposed to generation): a taxonomy organizing factors into deployment-level vs request-level and structural vs numerical, evaluated over 3,960 serving contexts on 180 from-scratch classifiers, with bitwise-identical repetition proving effects come from settings, not run-to-run noise.
- Identification of padding as the dominant request-level effect and proof that it is structural: ~10% of labels flip from padded-width changes alone, exclusively in left-padded models with learned absolute position embeddings; assigning positions by real-token order restores width invariance on both GPU and CPU.
- Separation of numerical mechanisms: batch-shape changes flip no labels at full precision (<10^-9 probability movement over 256,104 comparisons) but move up to 56.7 percentage points of probability mass under bf16 — batch size is a rounding effect at fp32 and a measurable effect only under reduced precision.
- Mechanism-matched mitigations with measured invariance–cost trade-offs (Table 2), including real-token position assignment (2–2.5x throughput via dynamic padding), batch-invariant matmul kernels (1.3–1.5x standard latency at near-zero accuracy cost), fixed offline 8-bit scales, and margin-based recompute detecting nearly all held-out flips at 22% recompute.
- Formal results: a width-invariance theorem (Theorem 5.1) with a converse for learned absolute embeddings, a one-sided margin certificate for label stability (Proposition 5.1), and a conditional-infeasibility theorem (Theorem 5.2) showing no implementation meets joint speed, accuracy, and invariance thresholds when Bayes error exceeds the accuracy target.

## Methodology

- **Model grid:** 180 classifiers trained from random initialization (no pretrained weights): four transformer classification formulations — discriminative (BERT-style encoder with classification head), autoregressive, masked-language-model, and discrete-diffusion (the last two grouped as pseudo-generative) — plus fully generative GPT-2-style classifiers that compare summed token likelihoods across candidates. Three jointly scaled sizes (small/medium/large following Kasa et al. 2025 and Makroo et al. 2026), five datasets (SST-2, SST-5, AG News, Emotion, IMDb), three seeds. Only the 158 of 180 models whose validation accuracy exceeds random guessing by a >=15% relative margin are kept for analysis.
- **Controlled serving comparisons:** every comparison changes exactly one recorded setting against a matched reference deployment (full precision on GPU, static padding, single-example request), holding checkpoint, dataset split, evaluated examples, and all other settings fixed; comparisons with missing reference runs are discarded rather than substituted. Factors: numerical precision (fp32 vs bf16), quantization (8-bit integer CPU, offline fixed scale vs per-batch recomputed scale), device+quantization migration (GPU to CPU, reported as whole-deployment migration), batch size, padded width (static vs dynamic), slot order, and batch composition. Padded width is held fixed when comparing peer-sharing interventions so tensor shape cannot confound.
- **Metrics:** pairwise flip indicator against a reference context; total variation (TV) between aligned probability vectors; maximum absolute logit change — computed both over all targets and over the subset whose top label is unchanged, separating "does the label move" from "how far do the scores move."
- **Mechanism attribution:** two matched interventions isolate causality — assigning positions by real-token order (removes padding effect; forcing width-dependent positions back restores it) and replications with pretrained rotary-position (Qwen3) and NoPE (ModernBERT) models separating padding side from position representation.
- **Model-size analysis:** 40 matched dataset/family/seed combinations where all three sizes exceed 1.15x chance validation accuracy; combinations weighted equally with resampling that preserves the three-size pairing.
- **Theory:** formal conditions for when logit/probability drift can flip labels (margin certificate), sufficient conditions and a converse for padding-based nondeterminism (position rule vs learned absolute embeddings), and an impossibility-style statement over an execution model with worst-case latency, accuracy, and score-drift thresholds.

## Results & Highlights

- **Padding dominates and is structural:** changing only the padded width flips about 10% of predicted labels — more than any other factor — and every padding-induced label change occurs in the two GPT-2 families (about one in five of their comparisons), while BERT and masked-language-model classifiers change none; pretrained Qwen3 (rotary) and ModernBERT (NoPE) also flip zero labels, confirming learned absolute positions as the cause.
- **Batch shape is a numerical-format effect:** at full precision, changing only the batch shape flips no labels across 256,104 comparisons and moves probabilities by less than 10^-9; under bf16 the same change moves up to 56.7 percentage points of predicted probability mass, and the 8-bit integer path is larger still.
- **Whole-deployment migration (full-precision GPU to quantized CPU):** the discriminative BERT classifier changes 0.3% of labels, the two pseudo-generative formulations about 2% each, and the fully generative GPT-2 classifier about 5.6% on average and up to 39% on its worst checkpoint.
- **Single-factor numerical changes:** fp32 to bf16 flips about 1% of discriminative BERT labels and moves a GPT-2 generative score by up to 0.56; recomputing the 8-bit integer scale from each batch flips 4 of 192 fully generative labels and moves a score by up to 0.84; under dynamic 8-bit quantization, batch composition (shared activation scale) reaches 3.7% of fully generative labels and a score movement of 1.3 at batch size 256.
- **Labels vs scores diverge:** serving load (dynamic batching of variable-length traffic) moves raw scores several times more than migration while flipping the same fraction (~1.6% at full precision) of fully generative labels — a label-only monitor would rank the two combined effects backward; the margin certificate (m(x) > 2TV) predicts this, and the five-class cells confirm narrower margins convert the same score movement into more flips.
- **Mitigation costs (measured in-setup):** real-token position rule adds no accuracy loss when used in training and permits dynamic padding worth 2–2.5x throughput; batch-invariant matmul kernels remove batch-shape drift at near-zero accuracy cost and 1.3–1.5x standard latency at large batch; margin-based recompute detects nearly all of 4,644 held-out flips at 22% recompute overhead.
- **Determinism baseline:** repeating an identical context reproduces the logits bitwise on every hardware/software configuration tested, so all measured drift is attributable to the recorded setting change, not run-to-run variation.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty is real but framed slightly generously:** the paper's own related-work section concedes that every ingredient mechanism (floating-point non-associativity, shape-dependent kernel selection, backend divergence, padding-side pathologies) was already documented for generation; the genuine contribution is showing these transfer to single-pass classifiers — where the autoregressive amplification story does not apply — and that the answer is nontrivial (label effects concentrate structurally in one family). Calling this "almost no direct attention" is fair; calling the mechanisms new would not be.
- **All 180 models are trained from scratch at three small jointly-scaled sizes on five sentiment/topic datasets** — the strongest empirical finding (padding flips are confined to learned absolute position embeddings) is well-supported, but the headline magnitude numbers (~10% flips, 39% worst checkpoint) come from small from-scratch GPT-2-style models whose validation accuracy only barely clears the 15% relative-margin filter in many cells; production classifiers are fine-tuned pretrained encoders or much larger, and the paper's own Qwen3/ModernBERT replications flip zero labels from padding. The paper is honest about this in its Scope paragraph (measurements cover unimodal text, from-scratch and fine-tuned pretrained controls, one GPU, one CPU, two Android phones), but the abstract's framing does not surface how much of the effect rides on the generative-from-scratch regime.
- **The model-size analysis is explicitly a "descriptive association rather than a scaling law"** — the authors admit size, width, depth, and training quality change together, so the "probability movement roughly doubles with model size" claim has no causal content; this is a limitation the paper states but abstract-level readers may miss.
- **Mitigation costs are measured in the experimental setup, not production** — Table 2's footnote says so directly, so the "2–2.5x throughput" and "1.3–1.5x latency" figures should not be read as deployment benchmarks; notably, applying the position rule only at inference (not training) degrades accuracy or collapses predictions (Appendix B.7), a practical constraint that weakens the "drop-in fix" reading.
- **Red flag, minor:** the margin certificate is one-sided (it preserves labels when satisfied, says nothing when violated), and the recompute mitigation "detects almost all observed flips" — the residual miss rate under reduced precision is deferred to appendices rather than quantified in the main text.

## Strengths

- Impeccable experimental hygiene: one-recorded-setting-at-a-time comparisons, bitwise determinism of repeated identical contexts as a control, discarded (not substituted) missing reference runs, held-fixed padded width for peer-sharing comparisons, and manifest-traceable outputs — this is how nondeterminism should be measured.
- Clean causal attribution via matched interventions: real-token position reassignment removes the padding effect and re-forcing width-dependent positions restores it, triangulated by rotary/NoPE pretrained models — the mechanism is demonstrated, not just correlated.
- The structural-vs-numerical taxonomy plus the "label stability ≠ score stability" finding produce actionable guidance (monitor probabilities, not labels) with a formal certificate backing it.
- Each mechanism gets a mechanism-matched mitigation with measured costs, and the theory (width-invariance theorem with converse, margin certificate, joint-infeasibility theorem) genuinely matches the empirical taxonomy rather than decorating it.

## Limitations

- Scope restricted to unimodal text classifiers on five sentiment/topic datasets with three small from-scratch sizes plus a handful of pretrained controls; multimodal inputs, production inference engines, and larger models are unmeasured, and effect magnitudes may shrink substantially for fine-tuned pretrained encoders.
- The per-instance GPU path (batch folded into one matrix, ~10^-7 logit drift from reduction order) means target-slot invariance is only a kernel implementation property — batch-invariant kernels exist for the tested stacks but are not guaranteed across backends.
- Mitigations are setup-measured, not production-validated; the position rule must be adopted at training time, and no pretrained checkpoint in common use today can retroactively gain it.
- Label-change rate is not monotone in model size and the size analysis confounds width, depth, and training quality, so capacity guidance for practitioners is limited.

## Relevance

这项工作与用户关注的 LLM 推理可靠性与可复现性直接相关：它把已见于文本生成的"服务上下文不确定性"首次系统量化到分类任务，指出现实部署中仅换 padding 策略或精度就能翻转约 10% 标签（生成式范式下最差 checkpoint 达 39%）。对任何把分类器/打分器嵌入生产 pipeline 并要求行为随时间稳定的系统，其"监控概率而非标签 + margin 证书 + 逐机制缓解"的建议都可直接落地。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Do the label-flip magnitudes (especially the ~10% padding effect and the 39% worst-checkpoint migration) shrink to practical irrelevance for fine-tuned pretrained encoders and modern mid-size classifiers, given the zero-flip Qwen3/ModernBERT replications?
- How do the structural effects interact with chunked/paged attention and continuous batching kernels used in production serving engines (vLLM-style), where the position bookkeeping differs from the paper's left/right padding abstraction?
- Can the margin certificate be turned into a cheap online monitor — e.g., recompute only when the probability margin falls below a calibrated TV-budget threshold — and what false-negative rate does it leave under 8-bit dynamic quantization?
- Does Theorem 5.2's joint infeasibility hold for execution families with fixed positional representations (rotary/NoPE), or is there a regime where speed, accuracy, and invariance are jointly achievable for those models?
