Why this matters

Shows how structured knowledge actually forms during real pre-training — informing data curation, forgetting, and the in-context vs in-weights tug-of-war.

⚠ Critical edge

Every effect lives on an 8-entity, noiseless toy taxonomy injected at ~2–4% of tokens — sparser injections failed outright, so real-corpus fact regimes are untested.

01Methodology

  1. BuildDepth-3 binary tree, 8 Wuggy-pseudoword entities; siblings share 6 properties, deeper relatives share fewer. The tree is never stated — it exists only in co-occurrences.
  2. Inject4 of 32 sequences per batch carry taxonomy text for 5,000 of 10,000 steps of continued pre-training — original data order, optimizer state, plain next-token loss.
  3. ProbeScore log p(property | entity) over 12 held-out templates; induction advantage = within-category − across-category.
  4. RecoverCluster layer-8 entity representations (Matching Affinity Clustering) against the ground-truth dendrogram.

A hidden probe property P is shown for one entity but never its sibling, so within-category transfer is the only channel for the sibling to acquire it. Pseudowords plus controlled co-occurrence make leakage impossible by construction.

3 seeds regenerate the entire pseudoword vocabulary; main runs from Pythia 160M and OLMo 2 1B checkpoints.

  • Larger models memorize faster, generalize slower — 2.8B absorbs injected facts within ~10² steps vs ~10³ for 70M, eroding the pressure to compress into shared structure.
  • The tree is written into representations — MAC clustering recovers the full depth-3 tree perfectly (OLMo 2 1B) and near-perfectly (Pythia 160M); never before injection. Cosine similarity rises monotonically with shared category depth.
  • Ablation isolates the hierarchy — remove shared properties and the within/across induction curves collapse onto each other.
  • Conflict & context — training a conflicting taxonomy erases the old one and is learned more slowly; a handful of in-context shots overrides the in-weights taxonomy.
  • Fragile without support — once injection stops, induction decays within a few hundred steps (log p ≈ −1 → −18/−20 by step 10,000).

Strengths

  • Ground-truth control: pseudoword entities and fully engineered co-occurrence separate induction from recall, leakage-free by construction.
  • Ecologically valid: facts learned under the standard next-token objective inside real continued pre-training — a step beyond fine-tuned yes/no property probes.
  • Breadth and rigor: two model families, seven scales, three vocabulary-regenerating seeds, held-out templates, multiple ablations.
  • Converging evidence: behavioral curves, monotone representational similarity, and full dendrogram recovery all point to the same latent-taxonomy story.
  • Unusually honest reporting: substantive limitations appendix, AI-use statement, and explicit flags on which claims survive the batch-size check.

Limitations

  • Toy taxonomy (8 entities, balanced, noiseless) bounds external validity; effect sizes on realistic, exception-laden categories are unknown.
  • Injection density (~2–4% of tokens, every batch) is orders of magnitude denser than real facts; sparse injection failed in preliminary runs.
  • “Scale hurts” holds data fixed while varying size — the opposite of joint compute-optimal scaling, so it is not a practical scaling law.
  • Not durable: learned structure decays within a few hundred steps once supporting data stops, and is easily steered away in context.
  • No code or data release; in-context analysis limited to 1B-scale models (a quiet scope restriction disclosed only in a footnote).

03Questions for further exploration

  1. Does the “scale hurts at fixed data” reversal persist under joint compute-optimal scaling, or vanish once larger models get proportionally more exposure?
  2. Can the effect be reproduced with natural-language taxonomies — real species, product categories, organizational hierarchies — where structure is noisy and overlapping?
  3. How sparse can fact injection be before induction fails entirely, and what does that frequency threshold imply for long-tail conceptual learning?
  4. Which circuits implement taxonomic generalization, and do they differ between scales? Does rapid post-injection decay (Fig. 11) also afflict real-world categorical knowledge?
