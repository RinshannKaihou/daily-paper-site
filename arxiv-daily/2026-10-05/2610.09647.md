> **一句话总结：** 在 Qwen3-0.6B 的受控退化实验中，数据复制使 held-out 损失恶化 75%（2.530→4.429）的同时 RankMe^c 反而升至健康值之上 13.5 个合并标准差、协方差有效秩接近翻倍——损伤是"谱弥散"而非"坍缩"；作者预注册的双侧 CUSUM 集成虽能在每个 fold 的 fork 后 10–60 步内检出全部三种损伤 regime，但对 held-out 损失的领先在九折中仅出现一次（一折、一个 10 步区间），且在 held-out 健康种子上无法取得零误报，因此谱信号目前只能诊断损伤几何，不能授权自主停止训练。

> **TL;DR:** On Qwen3-0.6B post-trained on Dolly (with a GSM8K continual-training adaptation mode), the self-supervised-vision folklore "rank falls when representations degrade" inverts: under data duplication the held-out probe loss worsens by 75% (2.530→4.429 nats) while centred RankMe rises 13.5 pooled s.d. above healthy (877.9 vs 812.6) and covariance effective rank nearly doubles (329.2 vs 174.8), because damage is spectral *dispersion*, not collapse — a one-sided rank monitor rates the worst checkpoint healthiest. The author's pre-registered two-sided CUSUM ensemble detects all three damage regimes in every leave-one-seed-out fold within 10–60 steps of the fork, yet beats held-out probe loss only once in nine folds and false-alarms on the held-out healthy branch in all three folds (FP@61/460/450), so spectral signals diagnose failure geometry but do not yet justify autonomous stopping.

## 核心贡献

- **精确的指标审计**：RankMe（奇异值熵）与协方差有效秩（特征值熵）在原始定义下就不可互换，相差约 2 倍；且 LLM 中间层的 massive activation 使协方差有效秩在原始 hidden state 上失效（某一方向占 trace 的 99.5%，r_eff^Σ=1.06/1024 而 RankMe=120.13）。per-token RMS 归一化可恢复协方差有效秩（1.06→308.96），但该修只对协方差秩必要，对 RankMe 两种形式都无必要。
- **regime–statistic 符号结构**：退化方向依赖于"损伤 regime × 统计量"配对。duplicate_data 下损失 +75% 而 RankMe^c 升 13.5 s.d.（单侧监控会把最差 checkpoint 评为最健康）；high_lr 下中心化统计量按惯例方向下降（−3.6 s.d.），而无中心 RankMe 跨种子不一致——连"是否中心化"这一实现细节都会翻转监控器看到的方向，事后重校准无法修复。
- **预注册的 fork-valid 序贯评估协议**：shared-prefix 设计（同一健康 300 步前缀分叉）、leave-one-seed-out 校准（方向/阈值/通道选择只见健康分支）、双侧 Page CUSUM + 固定水平 union bound（每通道 α/2C）、误报只在 held-out 健康分支上计——该协议可移植给任何 early-warning 型监控主张。
- **完整的负结果**：在上述协议下，五通道×双向集成检测全部命中（3 regime × 3 fold 全检出，10–60 步），但 (a) 领先 held-out 损失仅 1/9 折、(b) 双种子校准下 held-out 健康种子零误报不成立。结论：谱信号是"损伤几何诊断"，不是自主停止的许可证。
- **附带发现**：s_cb（cross-batch 相似度）在所有超阈 regime 上一致下降（−7.5 至 −17.6 s.d.），与视觉坍缩文献中"表示变得更相似"方向相反；LLM 后训练中损伤使表示彼此散开。

## Key Contributions

- An exact metric audit showing RankMe (singular-value entropy) and covariance effective rank (eigenvalue entropy) are different statistics under their original definitions, and that massive activations floor the covariance effective rank (r_eff^Σ = 1.06 of d = 1024 on a raw intermediate layer where RankMe reads 120.13); per-token RMS normalisation repairs the covariance rank (1.06 → 308.96) but is unnecessary for either RankMe form.
- A regime–statistic sign structure: under duplicate_data, held-out loss degrades 75% (4.429 vs 2.530) while RankMe^c rises 13.5 pooled s.d. above healthy (877.9 vs 812.6) — the conventional one-sided reading rates the worst checkpoint the healthiest — whereas under high_lr the centred statistics move conventionally (−3.6 s.d.) and the uncentred RankMe is seed-inconsistent; the sign is a property of the regime–statistic pair, so post-hoc recalibration cannot fix a mis-specified monitor.
- A fork-valid sequential evaluation protocol: shared-prefix change-point design with leave-one-seed-out calibration (directions and thresholds chosen only from healthy branches), a pre-registered two-sided CUSUM ensemble with union-bound stopping, and false alarms scored on a held-out healthy seed.
- A complete negative result under that protocol: the two-sided ensemble detects every regime in every fold within 10–60 steps, but leads the held-out probe loss only once in nine folds (one 10-step interval) and does not achieve zero held-out false alarms with two calibration seeds; spectral signals currently diagnose failure geometry but do not license autonomous stopping.
- Two transferable side findings: the cross-batch similarity s_cb has a consistent sign in every above-gate regime but flipped relative to the vision collapse literature (damage spreads representations apart), and domain_shift_v2 shows the monitor's hard case is flagging the stability cost of an update that *succeeds* (GSM8K NLL 1.40→1.10 while Dolly probe degrades 2.58→2.98).

## Methodology

- **Model and regimes.** Qwen3-0.6B (d = 1024, 28 layers) is fully fine-tuned with AdamW (batch 4×512 tokens, base LR 1e-5) on databricks-dolly-15k. Five modes: healthy reference; two controlled failure mechanisms (high_lr: LR ×8 with no warm-up; duplicate_data: 8 unique Dolly examples cycled at 1× LR); one venue-shift/adaptation scenario (domain_shift_v2: continual post-training on GSM8K with the probe unchanged); and a negative control (narrow_domain v1: Dolly closed_qa only, probe loss +0.06 nats, below the pre-registered damage gate).
- **Diagnostics.** On a fixed held-out probe of 256 Dolly sequences (reused verbatim at every measurement), the author computes per-token hidden-state statistics: RankMe (uncentred singular-value entropy), RankMe^c (centred variant reconstructing σ from the covariance spectrum), covariance effective rank r_eff^Σ, 95% energy rank k95, anisotropy deficit ΔH, and cross-batch similarity s_cb, all in float64. Held-out NLL on the same probe is the pre-registered offline damage proxy against which every detector is scored. Theorem 1 (ΔH ≥ 0, equality iff the spectrum is flat) is machine-checked in Lean 4.24.0/Mathlib with an axiom audit showing only Lean's three standard axioms.
- **Two-phase design.** Phase 1 runs each mode from step 0 to 1000 (three seeds, probed every 25 steps) for seed-replicated endpoint comparisons. Phase 2 is the fork-valid test: each seed trains an identical healthy 300-step prefix, then forks into healthy/high_lr/duplicate_data/domain_shift_v2 branches (600 steps, probed every 10 steps); fork checkpoints SHA-256-verified.
- **Pre-registered detector protocol.** Margins, per-channel directions and channel selection are calibrated only on the healthy branches of the *other* seeds (leave-one-seed-out, rotated over folds); damaged branches never participate. The low-breach level μ_L + 3σ_L is frozen on healthy data. Each of the C = 5 channels is tested in both directions with slack k = 0.5 via Page's CUSUM; the ensemble stops at the first alarm with a fixed-horizon union bound (α/2C per channel). False alarms are scored on the held-out healthy branch; delay on the held-out damaged branches. Endpoint separation is reported in pooled standard deviations, with each (mode, seed) trajectory counted as one observation to avoid pseudoreplication.

## Results & Highlights

- **The headline inversion (duplicate_data, endpoint, 3 seeds).** Held-out probe loss degrades 75% relative to healthy (4.429 vs 2.530 nats) while RankMe^c rises to 877.9 vs 812.6 healthy (+13.5 pooled s.d.) and r_eff^Σ nearly doubles (329.2 vs 174.8, +10.7 s.d.). Under the conventional "rank falls = damage" reading, the worst model is rated the healthiest — the exact failure of the imported prior.
- **Metric audit numbers (unmodified pretrained Qwen3-0.6B).** On the raw layer-14 intermediate state one direction carries 99.5% of the trace: r_eff^Σ = 1.06 out of d = 1024 while RankMe = 120.13 and RankMe^c = 121.24 on the same spectrum; per-token RMS normalisation restores the intermediate-layer covariance effective rank from 1.06 to 308.96 (RMS and ℓ2 normalisation give numerically identical diagnostics). The flooring claim holds for the covariance rank only — neither RankMe form is floored.
- **Detection vs lead vs false alarms (pre-registered protocol, Table 3).** The two-sided ensemble fires in every fold of every mode within 10–60 steps after the fork (detected 3/3 folds for high_lr, duplicate_data, domain_shift_v2), with the firing direction regime-specific as predicted; but it leads the held-out probe loss in only one of nine folds (duplicate_data, +10–1000 steps per fold), and the held-out healthy branch false-alarms in all three folds (FP@61/460/450) despite 12–20σ thresholds — at n = 3 the strongest sustainable claim is time-to-false-alarm ≥ 140 steps, not zero false alarms.
- **Sign structure across regimes (Table 2).** high_lr (probe loss 3.153 vs 2.530) moves RankMe^c down 3.6 s.d. (799.9 vs 812.6) and k95 down 4.1 s.d. (685.7 vs 689.7) while uncentred RankMe is seed-inconsistent; s_cb falls below healthy in every above-gate mode: −7.8 s.d. (high_lr), −17.6 s.d. (duplicate_data), −7.5 s.d. (domain_shift_v2), versus −0.75 s.d. for the below-gate narrow_domain control.
- **Mechanism: dispersion, not collapse.** Under duplicate_data the numerical rank of Σ stays full, every statistic plateaus by step 200 and holds to step 1000, and the change is variance redistribution: λ1 falls 84.79 → 74.53 while total variance T rises 649.6 → 820.3 on the held-out probe.
- **Adaptation is the hard case.** domain_shift_v2 improves held-out GSM8K test NLL from 1.40 → 1.10 (±0.01) while the Dolly probe degrades 2.58 → 2.98 (±0.01); healthy branches leave GSM8K flat (1.38) and the failure mechanisms damage both domains (GSM8K 2.25/2.63) — the monitoring task is flagging the stability cost of an update succeeding at its own objective.

## Authors & Credibility

- Single author: Zhaohui Geoffrey Wang (University of Southern California; zwang000@usc.edu). The work appears at the NeurIPS 2026 Workshop on Continual Learning for Foundation Models and Agents (CL4MAgents), an appropriate venue for a rigorous negative-result / evaluation-protocol paper.
- Credibility signals are unusually strong for the genre: the detector protocol was pre-registered before the damaged branch was indexed (frozen DETECTOR_PROTOCOL.md shipped in the supplement), Theorem 1 carries a machine-checked Lean/Mathlib proof with a printed axiom audit, fork checkpoints are SHA-256-verified, and the paper volunteers its own failed promises (zero false alarms not certified; lead over loss essentially absent). The unit-of-inference discussion (trajectories, not checkpoints, as observations) shows real statistical care. Limitations are self-identified and honest (single 0.6B model; inversion rests on one mechanism × three seeds; NLL is a same-probe proxy, not downstream capability).

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty.** The paper does not invent new statistics — RankMe, CUSUM, and the union bound are all borrowed — and it says so. Its novelty is threefold and genuine: (1) the observation that LLM post-training produces a third geometry, *dispersion*, which the contraction-oriented collapse taxonomies (Kim et al. 2025) do not cover and their detectors mis-sign; (2) the metric audit separating RankMe from covariance effective rank, a substitution that diagnostic code silently makes and that even flips signs between regimes (RankMe−RankMe^c falls −4.7 to −16.4 in all seeds under high_lr but rises in two of three under duplicate_data); (3) the protocol itself, which any early-warning claim should now be held to.
- **Rigor.** High where it matters most: leave-one-seed-out calibration, damaged-branch exclusion from direction/threshold selection, pooled-s.d. separation, refusal to pool autocorrelated checkpoints, and a negative control (narrow_domain) that stays below the damage gate and is honestly reported as *not* an inversion. The paper explicitly refuses to count r_eff^Σ under high_lr (+1.9 s.d., inside seed noise) as an inversion — appropriately conservative.
- **Reproducibility.** Very good on paper: frozen protocol files, unit tests, all logs, per-seed trajectories (Appendix E), and hardware cost stated (one RTX 4090-class 24 GB GPU, ≈22 min per 1000-step Phase-1 run). Independent replication is cheap and well-specified.
- **Weaknesses and red flags.** (1) *Single model, small scale*: every number is Qwen3-0.6B (d = 1024); the regime-specific directions may be an artifact of this architecture/scale. (2) *The inversion rests on one mechanism*: duplicate_data × 3 seeds; the title's general claim leans on a single replicated regime, which the author concedes in Limitations. (3) *The "no lead" bar is same-probe NLL*, not downstream capability — a cheap proxy is the right bar for consistency, but a spectral signal could still lead a slower downstream eval; the paper does not test that. (4) *Two calibration seeds cannot certify the union bound*: with two healthy seeds the smallest achievable level is 1/2 while α/2C = 0.005, so the zero-false-alarm guarantee degenerates to the zero-crossing threshold — the held-out FP row (FP@61/460/450) is exactly this premise failing at data scale; the synthetic check (zero-calibration per-channel FP rates 4–58%, ensemble rate 98.5%) confirms the gap is real, not noise. (5) ΔH "moves in the unexpected direction" in Table 2 — acknowledged but unexplained. These are limitations of scope, not spin: the negative result is scoped precisely and the abstract matches the tables.

## Strengths

- Directly falsifies a load-bearing prior ("rank falls under degradation") imported from self-supervised vision, with a controlled, seed-replicated regime structure rather than anecdotes.
- The dispersion-not-collapse mechanism is backed by concrete numbers (λ1 down, T up, full numerical rank throughout), not just labels.
- The pre-registered, fork-valid, two-sided evaluation protocol with held-out healthy false alarms is itself a reusable contribution — arguably the paper's most durable artifact.
- Exceptional intellectual honesty: the abstract and conclusion state the negative result in full (no lead, no zero false alarms) rather than burying it; the Lean-checked theorem and axiom audit exceed workshop norms.
- Practical guidance that survives the negative result: always report RankMe and covariance effective rank separately, always normalise per-token before covariance statistics on raw LLM states, and always evaluate rank channels in both directions.

## Limitations

- All evidence is from Qwen3-0.6B (d = 1024) full fine-tuning; larger models, LoRA/RLHF-style updates, and other architectures are untested, so the regime-specific directions and the no-lead result may not transfer.
- The upward inversion is replicated on one mechanism only (duplicate_data, 3 seeds); under high_lr only the centred statistics fall conventionally, and uncentred RankMe is inconsistent across seeds — so "the sign is regime-dependent" is supported, but any stronger generalisation is not.
- Held-out NLL on a single fixed 256-sequence probe (31k tokens) stands in for downstream capability; the no-lead comparison is same-probe, and no downstream benchmark sweep is monitored.
- With only 3 seeds the union-bound false-alarm target is uncertifiable (level resolution 1/2 vs required α/2C = 0.005); the claim degrades to time-to-false-alarm ≥ 140 steps.
- narrow_domain (v1) never triggers the damage gate (+0.06 nats), so it serves only as a below-gate control; ΔH is reported only at token granularity (sequence-pooled Σ is not full-rank).

## Relevance

这篇论文与持续学习/后训练监控方向高度相关：它用受控实验和预注册协议证明了视觉自监督文献中的"秩下降=退化"先验在 LLM 后训练中会反向，并给出了一套任何表示健康监控主张都应通过的 fork-valid 评估标准。对任何考虑用 RankMe 类谱统计做训练停止或报警的实践者，这是一篇必读的"先别信"文献。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the dispersion-not-collapse inversion replicate at larger scale (e.g., 7B+) and under LoRA/RLHF-style updates, or is it specific to small-model full fine-tuning?
- Can the flipped-sign s_cb channel, or per-regime direction conventions calibrated on healthy data only, be turned into a monitor that achieves genuine lead time over held-out loss (the paper tests detection and false alarms, but not lead for s_cb)?
- Would anytime-valid alternatives (e-processes, confidence sequences) fix the false-alarm certification at n = 3 seeds, or does the healthy-null misspecification (seed offsets exceeding within-seed noise) remain the binding constraint?
- What mechanism drives variance redistribution into tail directions under data duplication — is spectral dispersion statistically linked to memorisation, and could it be exploited rather than merely flagged?
- In the domain_shift regime where the update *succeeds* (GSM8K NLL 1.40→1.10) but the probe degrades (2.58→2.98), is probe-loss degradation a stability cost worth alerting on at all, and how should a monitor value-align between the two?
