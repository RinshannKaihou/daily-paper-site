Training KV transport for receiver utility — not reconstruction fidelity — makes zero-prefill handoff beat re-prefilling itself.

CacheLink: Functional KV Transport for Zero-Prefill Cross-Model Context Handoff

Haomiao Qiu, Yining Tang, Zijia Song, Ruizi Han, et al.· HIT Shenzhen · Great Bay University · Zhejiang University· Oct 5, 2026

Key Metric
+16.01 pts

over Ridge-k8, equal-weight 7-panel mean on Qwen3-8B→14B (44.62 vs 28.61) — the largest of four pair gains (+6.84 / +2.96 / +16.01 / +11.14). On Qwen3-14B→32B it even tops the re-prefilling native receiver: 50.32 vs 48.14.

Why This Matters

Model routing in LLM serving stalls because context can’t move between models. CacheLink lets a receiver pick up where the sender left off — no re-prefill.

直击多模型 serving 中“上下文无法跨模型复用”的痛点：免重预填的 KV 迁移，32K 切换仅 0.969s。

Critical Edge

“Beats all methods” silently excludes the native re-prefilling receiver, which still wins 2 of 4 pairs by 3.7–5.9 mean points — handoff is not yet free.

Methodology

Sender KV cache→ Ridge affine (frozen)→ Coupled K/V residual (trained)→ RoPE reapply→ Receiver→ CE + fwd KL
  1. Strict zero-prefill setup. A learned transport F maps the sender’s entire KV cache into the receiver’s space; deployment does no receiver-side prefill, sparse replay, or reconstruction pass.
  2. Diagnose first. A NoLiMa 8K→32K sweep shows Ridge’s reconstruction MSE improves while reusable-prefix score collapses 19%→2%; the functional residual is only 1.52–2.40% of Ridge’s RMS and near-orthogonal to the Native−Ridge gap, and K/V errors must be corrected jointly (source-swap intervention).
  3. Architecture. Frozen Ridge maps (held-out R²: K 0.838 / V 0.609) provide the initialization; zero-initialized residual heads with separate K/V GELU stems fused into a shared latent emit ΔK/ΔV — ~358.5M trainable params across 64 receiver layers.
  4. Functional supervision. The cache is injected into the frozen receiver; the loss is continuation CE + λKL·forward KL against a native receiver that prefilled the same prefix. No reconstruction loss at all. Training: ≤1,200 examples, ≤600 steps per pair.

Strengths

  • Falsifies the field’s default objective before proposing one: length-sweep paradox + matched example-level diagnostics + a clean K/V swap intervention.
  • Zero-prefill is honored end-to-end, and handoff latency is actually measured against receiver re-prefill, not claimed analytically.
  • Breadth with honesty: 4 pairs × 3 families × 7 fixed panels, per-panel regressions left visible, archived loss ablation, explicit caveats on what means do and don’t say.
  • Cheap adaptation: frozen Ridge + zero-init residuals, ≤1,200 examples / ≤600 steps per pair; mapper touches only the sender cache.

Limitations

  • Still trails re-prefill on 2 of 4 pairs (Ministral −3.72, Yi −5.86 equal-weight mean) — quality-for-latency trade in general.
  • Long context is uneven: regressions on Oolong-Synth 32K / LongBench-v2 (Ministral), and a 19.6-point gap to native on Yi BABILong 32K.
  • Per-pair mappers: each sender→receiver pair needs its own ~350M-param training run; no multi-target amortization.
  • Non-production latency harness (Transformers + FlashAttention-2, not vLLM/SGLang); throughput under batching unmeasured.
  • No significance testing despite panels as small as 100 questions; artifact omits weights, Ridge fits, panels, and logs.

Questions for Further Exploration

  • Does functional supervision survive quantized/compressed caches (e.g. KIVI-style 2-bit K / 4-bit V)?
  • Can one mapper serve multiple receivers — and how far does the residual drift when the receiver checkpoint moves?
  • Why does Ridge collapse so catastrophically on BABILong for Qwen3-8B→14B (9.00 vs 35.40)? Can the fidelity–utility decoupling predict failing regimes?
  • What is λKL sensitivity — and does CE alone saturate with more data, removing the need for the KL teacher?
  • Would the 7.27× latency edge survive vLLM/SGLang fused kernels and realistic batched routing?
  • Can functional residuals transfer beyond KV caches — e.g. to SSM/Mamba states or encoder cross-attention KV?
