> **一句话总结：** 瑞士 Apertus 项目在 v1.5 技术报告里证明"全开放"也能规模化：不改架构，通过 tokenizer（Emu3.5 视觉 tokenizer + WavTokenizer 音频 tokenizer，词表扩到 266,440）把 8B/70B 文本模型续训 4T/2T token 变成图像+音频理解模型，上下文四倍扩到 262,144，thinking 是 chat template 的请求级开关而非独立模型；8B 在 IFEval 87.1 超过 Qwen3.5-9B 的 84.3，thinking 模式把 AIME24 从 7.5 提到 22.9，权重/数据/代码/配方全部 Apache 2.0 开放。

> **TL;DR:** ETH Zurich/EPFL-led Project Apertus upgrades the fully open Apertus 1.0 8B/70B text models into multimodal reasoners via continued pretraining on 4T (8B) / 2T (70B) additional tokens — modality enters through discrete tokenizers (Emu3.5 vision, WavTokenizer audio; vocabulary 131,072 → 266,440), the architecture and text-restricted LM head stay untouched, and the context window grows fourfold to 262,144 tokens. Against the strongest fully open baseline OLMo 3 (7B/32B) and open-weight Qwen3.5, the 8B model leads fully open peers on knowledge, instruction following, and cultural benchmarks — IFEval 87.1 vs 84.3 for open-weight Qwen3.5-9B (+2.8 pts), MMLU 70.5 vs 60.6 for Apertus 1.0 8B (+9.9), and the template-level thinking mode lifts MATH-500 from 51.2 to 71.4 and AIME24 from 7.5 to 22.9 (mean@8). On image+audio it is competitive with fully open multimodal models (best DocVQA 86.2 and ChartQA 78.7 in its group) but trails Qwen2.5-Omni on speech (CoVoST2 En-Zh BLEU 15.3 vs 44.3) and specialized vision models on OCR by ~12 MMMMU points. Weights, data recipes, training code, and the alignment charter are all released under Apache 2.0.

## 核心贡献
- **两个全开放多模态推理模型**：Apertus 1.5 8B 与 70B，从 Apertus 1.0 检查点续训（8B 加 4T token、70B 加 2T token），新增图像/音频理解、可选 thinking 模式、四倍上下文（262,144 token）和更强的指令跟随与工具调用；权重、中间检查点、数据管线、训练代码、评估套件全部以 Apache 2.0 发布。
- **原地升级的多模态配方**：模态经由离散 tokenizer 进入（视觉用 Emu3.5 tokenizer、音频用 WavTokenizer，词表从 131,072 扩到 266,440，其中 131,072 图像码 + 4,096 音频码），语言模型头仍只保留文本词表——输出头新增行被剪掉，"thinking 是模板 flag 而非新模型"。四阶段续训：unpaired 多模态 → paired 对齐 → 高质量 cooldown → 长上下文（8K→32K→65K→131K→262K 四步，RoPE 基频 50 万→4M、缩放因子 32）。
- **第二代后训练管线**：SFT（含 pass@8 能力过滤——8 题全败的可验证 prompt 才配 reasoning trace）、专用长上下文 SFT（修复短上下文 SFT 把 262K 处 RULER 从 54.8 打塌到 8.3 的问题）、RLVR（verl 异步训练器，349,300 prompt，thinking 混合开关）、DPO（8B 纯 online、70B 先 offline 后 online，24 模型池 MaxMin 配对）、SDPO 宪章对齐（仅 8B）。
- **诚实的训练复盘**：公开记录 8B Stage 1 因学习率翻倍和数据清单 bug 而废弃重跑、70B 只训了一半 token 预算、BLIP3 数据里的 recapcation 模板残留导致复读等事故，以及每一步的量化代价。
- **全链路评估**：67 项文本基准 + 70 项图像基准 + 语音/翻译/长上下文/安全/可解释性，外加 WMT/SwissGov/Romansh 的自动+人工翻译评估、欧盟 AI Act 模板的透明度文档和发布前人工红队评估。

## Key Contributions
- **Two fully open multimodal reasoners.** Apertus 1.5 8B and 70B add image and audio understanding, an optional thinking mode, a fourfold longer context (262,144 tokens), and stronger instruction following and tool use — with weights, intermediate checkpoints, data pipelines, training code, and the evaluation harness all Apache 2.0.
- **In-place multimodality through the tokenizer.** Instead of new encoders or a new architecture, images and audio become discrete tokens (vocabulary 131,072 → 266,440); the language-model head stays text-only, and thinking is a request-time chat-template flag rather than a separate model — so the whole Apertus 1.0 lineage upgrades checkpoint-by-checkpoint.
- **A staged continued-pretraining and post-training recipe.** Unpaired → paired → cooldown → long-context pretraining (RoPE base 500k→4M), then SFT with pass@8 capability filtering, dedicated long-context SFT, RLVR (async verl fork, 349,300 prompts), judge-scored DPO (MaxMin pairing from a 24-model response pool), and charter-aligned SDPO.
- **An honest production retrospective.** The report quantifies what adding modalities costs text capability (~2 points on the 8B, recovered by cooldown), why the 70B saw only half its multimodal mixture, and publishes incident post-mortems (a corrupted first Stage-1 run, a decontamination-script bug, recapcation-template leakage from BLIP3).
- **Evaluation breadth with pitfall control.** 67 text, 70 image, and audio/translation/long-context/safety/interpretability suites, run with fixed harnesses, judge models served on-site, and documented known pitfalls (truncated thinking outputs, parser mismatches, checkpoint-conversion errors).

## Methodology
**Multimodal representation.** Following Chameleon/Emu3, both images (Emu3.5 vision tokenizer, chosen via an Irmiger et al. benchmark of reconstruction quality vs tokens/image) and audio (WavTokenizer, ~2,400 tokens per minute) are discretized offline into tokens consumed by the shared embedding layer; the model predicts text tokens only (image/audio codes are inputs and Stage-1 targets, never generation targets), so the released LM head prunes all non-text output rows. The 512×512 image costs 1,024 tokens.

**Continued pretraining (4 stages, 8,192-token sequences).** Stage 1 trains on unpaired image/audio/text (all-token targets), Stage 2 on paired image-text/audio-transcript (text-only loss), Stage 3 is a higher-quality cooldown (~38B instruction-formatted tokens), and Stage 4 extends context 8K→32K→65K→131K→262K with fixed final-cooldown learning rate, synthetic verifiable long-context tasks (CWE, DocOrder, Artificial needles), and best-fit-decreasing document packing. AdEMAMix optimizer, warmup-stable-decay schedule; the 8B keeps the 1.0 peak LR (1.1e-4) while the 70B doubles to 2e-5 after in-pretraining LR probes. Text data swaps FineWeb2-HQ for FineWeb2-HQ-Plus (multilingual quality classifier over 100 languages) plus Nemotron-CC-v2.1, Nemotron-Pretraining-Code-v1, FineTransHQ, and domain corpora; every source must pass a small-scale ablation before entering the mix. All data respects machine-readable opt-outs applied retroactively; a strict image-host allow-list discards ~96% of MINT-1T versus ~2% under the text-pipeline rule.

**Post-training (5 stages).** (1) SFT at 16K context on 3,707,851 text samples (25.6% math/reasoning, 25.8% multilingual, 19.8% code/functions) — an auxiliary nonthinking 8B checkpoint filters verifiable prompts by pass@8, adding reasoning traces only to prompts failed 8/8 (filtering rates: 11.9% for OpenMathReasoning, 38.3% for DeepMath-103K); (2) long-context SFT at 262K on the full sequence length (Qwen2.5/Nemotron/Omni-style recipe), because short-context SFT collapses RULER@262K from 54.8 to 8.3; (3) RLVR with binary verification rewards (math ground truth, sandboxed code tests, constraint checkers) on 349,300 prompts, mixed thinking-on/off; (4) DPO with judge-scored MaxMin pairs from a 245,899-pair pool generated by 24 models (0.5B–67B) — online-only for 8B, offline-then-online for 70B; (5) SDPO self-distillation charter alignment (8B only). Thinking mode is enabled per request through the chat template with a 32,768-token budget.

**Evaluation discipline.** Forks of LM Evaluation Harness, LMMs-Eval, and VLMEvalKit behind a common dispatcher; Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct (AlpacaEval), Llama-2-13B-codes (HarmBench), and Llama-Guard-4 served locally as judges; held-out benchmark sets (Table 27) never consulted during development; thinking always evaluated both off and on at 32,768-token budget, mean@8 on AIME.

## Results & Highlights
- **Generational gains, best-in-class instruction following.** The 8B post-trained model improves over Apertus 1.0 8B on every benchmark except TruthfulQA and GPQA: IFEval 68.4 → 87.1 (vs 84.3 for open-weight Qwen3.5-9B, 82.8 for OLMo 3 7B), MMLU 60.6 → 70.5 (still 13.2 behind Qwen3.5-9B's 83.7), GSM8K 62.7 → 78.5, HumanEval 41.4 → 68.8. The post-training pipeline itself lifts the overall suite average from 47.3% to 55.0% (8B) and 52.2% to 61.2% (70B), with RL contributing +11.4 on ACPBench (8B) and DPO +10.1 on AlpacaEval.
- **Thinking mode is a real reasoning switch.** With thinking enabled (template flag, 32K budget), Apertus 1.5 8B goes from 51.2 → 71.4 on MATH-500 (+20.2), 41.2 → 57.3 on MATH, AIME24 7.5 → 22.9 and AIME25 8.3 → 23.8 (mean@8); the 70B reaches 26.7/21.7 on AIME24/25 — still behind thinking-native K2 Think V2 70B (84.6/74.6), which the report states plainly. Thinking also lifts MMMMU 39.8 → 52.2 but costs ChartQA 78.7 → 66.0, and IFBench improves 35.3 → 44.3 while IFEval drops to 25.7 on the 70B.
- **Long context survives post-training only with a dedicated stage.** RULER at 262K: base 8B scores 54.8, short-context SFT (16,384 tokens) collapses it to 8.3, the dedicated long-context SFT restores 59.5, and RL/DPO add further gains at 256K (60.1 → 61.5); the 70B pipeline reaches 61.9 at 256K RULER, though everything trails Qwen3.5-27B (94.9 at 256K).
- **Multilingual strength, OCR and audio-transcription gaps.** The 13-point English-knowledge gap to Qwen3.5-9B shrinks to 2–3 points on the multilingual slice (Global-MMLU 59.4, Multi-IF 83.7 — above Qwen3.5-9B's 80.0); the 8B posts the best FLEURS Polish WER (43.9 vs 87.1 for Qwen2.5-Omni) and best TED-LIUM long-form WER (36.0 vs 67.7/99.6/61.8 for Qwen2.5-Omni/Qwen2-Audio/Kimi-Audio), but CoVoST2 English-Chinese BLEU is 15.3 against Qwen2.5-Omni's 44.3, and vocal-sound classification is at or below chance (VocalSound 16.0% vs 92.2%).
- **Document understanding leads; vision reasoning trails.** Among fully open VLMs the 8B tops ChartQA (78.7), DocVQA (86.2), OmniDocBench (36.4), and general VQA (59.2), while OCR-heavy reasoning trails Qwen3-VL by ~12 MMMMU points (39.8 vs 52.2 for Qwen3-VL); the 70B adds little over the 8B on most image benchmarks.
- **Safety moves both ways.** Alignment raises ToxiGen by 11.4 (8B) and BBQ by 12.4 (70B), and fabricated-entity abstention jumps 27.0 → 87.1 on HalluLens — but HarmBench direct-request attack success worsens from 8.7% (Apertus 1.0 8B) to 13% with human jailbreaks at 31.5%, OR-Bench hard-subset refusal climbs 3.6% → 81.5% through alignment, and RefCOCO-family grounding degrades by ~17 points.

## Authors & Credibility
Project Apertus is a large Swiss collaboration led by ETH Zurich, EPFL, HES-SO Valais-Wallis, FHNW, SDSC, CSCS, and the Universities of Zurich and Bern, with academic leads Antoine Bosselut (EPFL), Martin Jaggi (EPFL), and Imanol Schlag (ETH). This is the same team behind Apertus 1.0 (September 2025), trained from scratch on the Alps supercomputer at CSCS, so the v1.5 report comes with demonstrated large-scale infrastructure credibility. Credibility is reinforced by unusual transparency: every dataset is public and versioned, opt-outs are honored retroactively, data ablations are published (wandb links in the paper), 15 external organizations ran pre-release deployment evaluations, and the report documents its own failed first Stage-1 run, the 70B's halved token budget, and data-tooling incidents. The obvious caveat is that self-reported evaluations, even with held-out suites and reproduced published scores, favor the authors' own narrative; but the release of identical evaluation forks lets anyone re-run the tables.

## Critical Assessment
This is a serious, unusually honest engineering report, but several claims deserve scrutiny. First, the "leads fully open models" headline rests on a specific peer set: the strongest text baselines at 8B are OLMo 3 7B and Marin 8B, while Qwen3.5-9B — an open-weight model with no training-data release — beats Apertus 1.5 8B on nearly every knowledge and math benchmark (MMLU 83.7 vs 70.5; GSM8K 94.8 vs 78.5; MATH 71.4 vs 41.2). The 70B trails EuroLLM-22B on several benchmarks despite nine times the parameters, and the report admits the 70B was stopped at half its multimodal mixture to free resources for the next project — a production constraint that directly caps the model it is showcasing. Second, the thinking-mode comparison in Table 25 pits Apertus against thinking-native peers in their thinking mode, and the gap is enormous (AIME24: 26.7 vs K2 Think V2's 84.6); the "up to 20 points" abstract framing is real but the absolute level is far from the reasoning frontier. Third, there are missing ablations where they matter most: the claim that paired Stage-2 training with text-only targets beats predicting multimodal tokens is cited to a "controlled study" (Kreft, 2026) rather than run here, and the SFT capability-filtering (pass@8) ablation quantifies filtering rates but not end-benchmark deltas of filtering itself. Fourth, the safety picture is mixed and honestly reported, but the regression is stark: HarmBench average attack success worsened from 8.7%/2.8% (v1.0) to 26.5%, with human jailbreaks at 31.5% — the "capability-limitations rather than alignment" defense is argued, not demonstrated. Finally, the RULER gains are modest in absolute terms (8B: 61.5 at 256K vs 78.8 for Gemma-3-12B), so "fourfold context extension" should be read as infrastructure support, not frontier long-context competence.

## Strengths
- Genuine full openness on all five axes (weights, data, code, recipes, values) — rare even among "open" models; every table is re-runnable from the released harness forks and versioned datasets.
- The tokenizer-only multimodality recipe is a valuable, reusable result: it shows an existing text-model lineage can absorb image and audio at 4T tokens with ~2 points of transient text regression (recovered by cooldown), instead of training a new architecture.
- Honest negative results and incident post-mortems (aborted Stage-1 run, half-trained 70B, decontamination bug, recapcation leakage) that most technical reports omit.
- Methodological care in evaluation: held-out benchmarks excluded from development, locally served judges, documented harness pitfalls, thinking evaluated on and off everywhere, mean@k for high-variance AIME.
- Broadest coverage of any open release this size: 70 image benchmarks, human translation evaluation including Swiss Romansh varieties, hallucination and interpretability probes (AUC 0.90 fabrication probes, layer-15 degeneration detection), and EU-AI-Act-style transparency documentation.

## Limitations
- Knowledge and math remain clearly behind open-weight peers (MMLU 70.5 vs Qwen3.5-9B's 83.7 at 8B; the 70B trails even EuroLLM-22B on some benchmarks), so "fully open best in class" is a statement about a thin fully-open field, not about the frontier.
- Generation is text-only: images and audio are understood, never produced; vocal-sound classification is at or below chance; audio training data skews toward parliamentary/political speech.
- Safety regressed relative to v1.0 (HarmBench attack success 8.7% → 26.5% at 8B) while over-refusal climbed sharply (OR-Bench hard subset 3.6% → 81.5%), and RefCOCO grounding degraded ~17 points after alignment.
- Tool calling and thinking cannot be combined at launch (serving stacks expose them as mutually exclusive); the 8B remains weak on parallel function calls; multi-turn tool use is not evaluated at all.
- The 70B consumed only half its intended multimodal mixture, so its weaker multimodal scores conflate scale effects with data starvation; XNLI regresses versus 1.0 at both scales, and long-tail language quality is unevaluated.

## Relevance
对关注"全开放模型能走多远"以及多模态统一 tokenizer 路线的人来说，这是目前信息量最大的开源技术报告之一：它给出了在不改架构、不换基座的前提下为已有 LLM 血统追加图像/音频与长上下文的完整配方和代价账本。对做模型续训、数据配比、后训练管线（RLVR/DPO/宪章对齐）或开源合规的研究者，权重+数据+代码全开放本身就是可复现的实验平台。

## Questions for Further Exploration
- Would the 70B match the 8B's multimodal profile if allowed to consume its full 4T-token mixture — i.e., how much of the 70B's multimodal weakness is data starvation versus scale-driven modality interference?
- The Stage-2 text-only-target decision leans on an external controlled study; does predicting multimodal tokens during paired training help or hurt at this scale when generation is never required?
- Can charter alignment (SDPO) recover the v1.0-level jailbreak resistance (8.7% → 26.5% attack success regression) without the observed over-refusal spike (81.5% on OR-Bench hard subset)?
- The pass@8 capability filter dictates which prompts carry reasoning traces; what is the end-benchmark ablation of filtering thresholds k=1..8 on math and IF scores, beyond filtering-rate statistics?
- The tokenizer-fusion path cost ~2 transient text points at 8B but far less at 70B — is the text-capability tax purely a learning-rate/schedule artifact, as the LR-probe evidence suggests, or an intrinsic capacity trade-off?
