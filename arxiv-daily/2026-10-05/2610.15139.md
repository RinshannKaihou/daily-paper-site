> **一句话总结：** 论文指出线性注意力/SSM 大模型的循环态量化存在一个此前未被报告的"尺度–状态反馈回路"：每步动态 INT4 的 scale 从上一轮被量化的状态本身重新推导（s=absmax/quant），量化误差于是滚入下一步的 scale——在 Qwen3.5-0.8B/2B 的 Gated DeltaNet 上，状态范数从 ~17 膨胀到 3.9×10³（0.8B）与 2.2×10⁴（2B），NIAH 召回从 H=0 的满分跌至 H=2048 的 0.67、H=8192 的 0.00（INT8 全程 1.00）；把 scale 永久标定（不读状态）或直接"yoke"到全精度状态即可消除爆炸，并把 WikiText-103 续写的超额 NLL 从动态 INT4 的 5.39 nats 降到静态 4.82、group-wise INT4 的 2.05（因果归因：yoking 收回 1.82 nats，即约 2/3 损伤来自反馈）；但所有策略在 H=2048 下困惑度仍是未量化模型的 4.9×–8.6×，且生存分析显示在 H=512 认证"无损"（1/30 失败）的策略到 H=2048 已失败 11/30——bit-width 必须与 decode horizon 联合报告。

> **TL;DR:** This paper shows that low-bit quantization of the *recurrent state* in linear-attention/SSM LLMs (Gated DeltaNet in Qwen3.5-0.8B/2B, Mamba-2-1.3B) is governed by a scale–state feedback loop: per-token dynamic scaling re-derives the quantizer scale from its own quantized output every decode step, so errors compound — on GDN-0.8B, INT4 NIAH recall is perfect at H=0 but falls to 0.67 by H=2048 and 0.00 by H=8192 (INT8 stays 1.00), while the state norm inflates from ~17 to 3.9×10³. Causally "yoking" the scale to the full-precision state removes the runaway (mean norm 3.5×10⁴ → 21) and recovers 1.82 of the 5.39 nats excess NLL on 2048-token WikiText-103 continuations (paired t=-13.1); persistent static calibration gets INT4 to 4.82 nats and uniform group-wise scaling to 2.05 vs the 3.05-nat zero-state control — yet every outlier-*locating* allocation loses to plain group-wise, stochastic rounding (Nemotron 3's SSM-cache fix) adds +6.71 nats, and even the best policy leaves perplexity 7.8×/8.6× unquantized at H=2048. Survival analysis shows the deployment-preferred policy certified near-lossless at H=512 (1/30 failures) has failed 11/30 articles by H=2048, so a bit-width certified at short exposure says little about where recurrent-state inference actually breaks.

## 核心贡献

- **失效前沿（failure frontier）**：首次把 decode horizon H 提升为循环态量化的一等报告轴——在 GDN 上 INT8 在所有实测 H 下 NIAH 满分、INT5 缓慢退化、INT4 崩溃、INT3 立即失效；Mamba-2-1.3B 的 INT4 能撑到 H=1024（此时 GDN-0.8B 已是 0.08）却在 H=2048 跌到 0.33。"bit-width × horizon" 联合报告是对现有量化评测方式的直接修正。
- **尺度–状态反馈机制的因果识别**：per-token 动态 scaling 在递归内部形成量化器读自己输出的闭环；通过 scale-yoking（每步 scale 取自 FP 状态）这一干预，证明反馈是数值爆炸（范数 3.5×10⁴ → 21）的原因，并因果收回 H=2048 时 5.39 nats 超额 NLL 中的 1.82（0.8B）/1.35（2B）——约 2/3 损伤来自反馈，剩余部分不是稳定性问题。
- **universal policy×exposure 交互**：dynamic−static 超额 NLL 差距随 H 单调拉大（三模型 diff-in-diff 分别 +1.22/+1.80/+5.06 nats），但在短 exposure 下动态反而在 2/3 模型中更好（0.8B 交叉点在 H=1024–1536 之间；GDN-2B 从不反转；Mamba-2 在 H=1536→2048 间从 +0.46 突变到 −4.81）——哪种 scaling 更好是模型相关的，且随使用长度改变。
- **否定结果：定位离群点全部失败**：为权重/激活/KV-cache 设计的 outlier-aware 分配（离群通道定向、exact sparse escape、dynamic↔static 混合、capped dynamic、随机舍入）在这个问题上全部输给朴素均匀 group-wise scaling（G=32）；而逐层混合精度的"学习式分配"与"保护前 3 层"的平凡规则结果逐位相同——敏感性几乎完全集中在第一层（单层损伤的 63–78%）。
- **对从业者的警告**：最好的 group-wise INT4 在 H=2048 仍留下 7.8×/8.6× 的困惑度惩罚（比清零状态更差），其相对清零状态的优势随 H 衰减、在 ~16K 后归零；生存分析表明短 horizon 认证会以超过一个数量级的幅度高估生存率。

## Key Contributions

- **A failure frontier in (bit-width × decode horizon)**: on GDN, INT8 is safe at every measured horizon while INT4 collapses (recall 1.00 → 0.67 → 0.00 across H=0 → 2048 → 8192); on Mamba-2 INT4 holds to H=1024 and dies at H=2048. Bit-widths must be reported together with the horizon at which they were certified.
- **Causal identification of a scale–state feedback loop**: per-token dynamic quantization re-derives its scale from the quantized state each step; scale-yoking to the FP state eliminates the norm explosion (3.5×10⁴ → 21) and returns 1.82 of 5.39 nats excess NLL at 0.8B — two thirds of the damage — leaving a separate, non-stability residual.
- **A universal policy×exposure interaction with a model-dependent short-horizon reversal**: the dynamic−static gap widens with H in all three models, yet dynamic is often the better choice at short exposure (crossover between H=1024 and 1536 on GDN-0.8B; abrupt reversal on Mamba-2; never on GDN-2B).
- **A negative result on outlier-aware designs**: every outlier-*locating* allocation loses to plain group-wise scaling; allocation helps only via the trivial "collapse into protecting the first few layers" rule (layer 0 alone carries 63–78% of single-layer damage), and learned/knapsack allocators add nothing over it.
- **A survival analysis of deployability**: policies certified lossless at short horizon fail later with no short-horizon precursor (1/30 → 11/30 failures from H=512 to H=2048), and a hazard fit to the first 256 steps over-predicts survival by well over an octave.

## Methodology

**Models.** Qwen3.5-0.8B and Qwen3.5-2B (official Gated DeltaNet hybrids, 262K context, BF16 compute with FP32 recurrent state; GDN state shape (1, 16, 128, 128)) and Mamba-2-1.3B (native loader; SSM state (1, 64, 64, 128)). All experiments run on a single RTX 3090 with public checkpoints.

**Quantization.** Training-free, state-only *fake* quantization: symmetric per-channel INT-b of the recurrent state at every decode step, deliberately excluding weights/activations and any trained corrector, to isolate what recurrence alone does to quantization error. Three scaling policies: **dynamic** (per-step absmax over the state), **static/persistent** (one offline-calibrated scale, constrained-MSE-optimal over held-out sequences and timepoints), and **group-wise** (persistent per-group-of-32-channels scales; 5.0 modeled bits/element vs 4.0 for per-channel). Interventions include scale capping (ceiling c from calibration, with/without exact preservation of overflowing elements), periodic state reset (K ∈ {128, 256, 512}), and scale-yoking (take the step scale from the FP state to sever the feedback edge).

**Tasks and protocol.** (a) *NIAH recall* with fixed needles in a needle-in-a-haystack family (paired McNemar over 12 seeds); (b) *natural long-document continuation*: process H real tokens with per-step quantization, then teacher-force 256 natural tokens and measure excess NLL against the unquantized model, with a **zero-state control** (clear the state each step) to check the tasks genuinely exercise recurrence. Total context fixed at 2048 so every H predicts the same continuation; calibration and test are article-disjoint (WikiText-103). Statistics are paired by document/seed with t-tests and discordant counts; the policy×exposure effect is a difference-in-differences (n=16 continuation documents, 18 allocation-audit articles, 30 survival articles).

**Additional instruments.** A leak-free per-layer allocation protocol (calibration, selection, and test articles disjoint by title hash; 6/15/18 articles) for mixed precision; single-layer sensitivity sweeps (quantize one layer at a time); and a survival analysis where first-passage failure time τ is the first step at which the quantized model flips a next-token argmax the FP model predicted with top-2 logit margin > 8, measured over 30 held-out articles up to H=8K, right-censored.

## Results & Highlights

- **The frontier.** NIAH recall (12 seeds) vs quantized horizon on GDN-0.8B INT4: 1.00 (H=0) → 0.92 (H=1024) → 0.67 (H=2048) → 0.33 (H=4096) → 0.00 (H=8192), with INT8 at 1.00 everywhere; on Mamba-2-1.3B INT4 holds 0.92 at H=1024 (where GDN-0.8B is already 0.08) then falls to 0.33/0.08 at H=2048/4096 vs the unquantized 0.75. At H=8192 nothing answers, full precision included. Even where recall is intact, quality leaks: group-wise INT8 costs 0.26 excess nats at H=2048 growing to 0.62 at H=32768 (paired t=10.9), i.e., perplexity penalty 1.30× → 1.55×.
- **The runaway and its cause.** At H=2048, per-token dynamic INT4 inflates the mean GDN recurrent-state norm from ~17 (unquantized) to 3.9×10³ (0.8B) and 2.2×10⁴ (2B); a persistent static scale at the same ~17–18 norm (0.02% clip rate) prevents the explosion entirely. Yoking the scale to the FP state drops the mean norm from 3.5×10⁴ to 21 and cuts excess NLL on 16 held-out WikiText-103 articles from 5.39 (dynamic) to 3.57 nats (paired t=−13.1; 2B: −1.35, t=−6.9) — two thirds of the damage is the feedback loop; on Mamba-2 the same policy moves the norm only 21.8 → 39–42 (1.8–1.9×) yet function still collapses at long horizon, so numerical severity is architecture-dependent while functional failure is not.
- **Containment beats location.** At H=2048 on GDN-0.8B: dynamic INT4 5.47 excess nats; periodic reset (K=128) 3.38 (t=−6.85); capped dynamic 3.58 (t=−11.0); adding exact escape of outliers to the cap changes nothing (3.40, t=−1.53) — the ceiling is what matters, not outlier removal. Persistent group-wise INT4 reaches 2.09 nats. Across models (Table 2): group-wise 2.05/2.15/2.70 vs static 4.82/4.08/2.55 vs dynamic 5.39/6.21/7.22, against a zero-state control of 3.05/4.08/3.19 (GDN-0.8B/2B/Mamba-1.3B) — per-token dynamic INT4 is *worse than discarding the state outright* in all three models, and on GDN even static loses to clearing (by 1.77 nats at 0.8B, t=15.2).
- **Policy × exposure.** The dynamic−static gap widens from H=512 to H=2048 by +1.22 nats (GDN-0.8B, t=6.9), +1.80 (GDN-2B, t=7.3), and +5.06 (Mamba-1.3B, t=12.9); yet at short exposure dynamic is ahead in two of three models (t=−5.32 and −13.81), with the GDN-0.8B crossover between H=1024 and H=1536. A history-only decomposition shows dynamic's short-horizon advantage comes from lower continuation-stage cost, not better retention (its corrupted history is worse by 0.45/1.66/2.95 nats at H=512/1024/2048).
- **Why the residual is not rounding.** The post-yoking residual falls monotonically with bit-width (INT3 4.82 → INT4 3.57 → INT5 2.96 → INT6 1.56 → INT8 0.31 nats), which accumulation would not do. Stochastic rounding — shipped in Nemotron 3 for its Mamba SSM cache — costs +6.71 nats on top of yoked INT4 (t=41.1) and holds policies at 9.7–11.2 nats at every checkpoint from 2K to 32K, worse than clearing the state (t=19..32).
- **Mixed precision: right layers, no intelligence needed.** The first GDN layer alone accounts for 63–78% of summed single-layer damage at H=2048; under the leak-free protocol, extreme allocation (top-3 layers INT8, rest INT2, ~4.0 bits avg) reaches 1.58/2.00 excess NLL (0.8B/2B) vs uniform INT3's 2.35/2.52 at the same rate (beaten by t=−12.8 at 0.8B) — but a trivial "protect the first 3 layers" baseline and a matched-budget knapsack optimizer return numerically identical numbers, and learned/random/MSE-ranked allocations lose by t=−17..−22.
- **The sober bottom line.** The least-damaging policy, uniform group-wise INT4, still leaves natural-text perplexity at 7.8× (0.8B) and 8.6× (2B) the unquantized model at H=2048; both pure INT4 policies are worse than zeroing the state. Its edge over the zeroed state decays with exposure (1.23 nats at H=2048, t=−7.4 → 0.00 at H=32768, t=0.0) — past ~16K the quantized state is no longer worth keeping, while its perplexity penalty grows from 7.5× to 27×. Survival: protected group-wise is token-faithful on 19/30 articles at H=2048 vs 5/30 (group-wise) and 0/30 (both pure policies); the deployment-preferred policy fails 1/30 by H=512 but 11/30 by H=2048 (2B: 0/30 → 12/30), with no short-horizon precursor.

## Authors & Credibility

Yuhang Jiang is an **independent researcher** — the entire paper is single-authored with no institutional affiliation, run on a single RTX 3090. That profile usually warrants extra scrutiny, but the paper earns trust in unusual ways: code, result files, and per-run provenance (article IDs, quantizer, group size, model/data revision) are released with title-hash-disjoint calibration/test splits; GDN runs are bit-identical between independent executions, and the Mamba-2 runtime's nondeterminism (few-hundredths-of-a-nat jitter) is disclosed rather than hidden. The statistics are consistently paired, the zero-state control is applied to every task, and negative results (stochastic rounding, outlier-aware allocation, learned allocators) are reported with the same care as positives. Related work is engaged honestly, including concurrent work (DAMP, Q-Mamba, a 27B hybrid noise-injection study) that the author explicitly does *not* compare against head-to-head. The writing is unusually precise about what is proven (feedback causes the numerical runaway) versus correlational (the step-by-step trajectory evidence).

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty.** The reframing is genuinely new: KV-cache quantization (KIVI, and NVIDIA's Nemotron 3 SSM-cache rounding) re-derives scales too, but the cache is stored rather than recurrent, so the error never re-enters the state — this paper is the first to isolate and causally identify scale–state feedback as the governing mechanism of recurrent-state quantization failure, and to make decode horizon a first-class reporting axis. That is a real conceptual contribution even though no new quantizer is proposed.

**Rigor.** Above average for an empirical analysis paper: paired statistics throughout, article-disjoint calibration/selection/test, a leak-free allocation protocol, calibration-robustness perturbations (only 2× over-scaling of the step size materially hurts persistent scaling; mis-calibration still leaves it far above dynamic at H=2048), and explicit falsification attempts on the residual (bit-width sweep rules out accumulation; matched controls rule out temporal grid-churn). The main methodological weakness is acknowledged in the paper itself: the scale-vs-max step-by-step trajectory evidence is only correlational, and the causal claim rests on the yoking intervention — which is convincing for the numerical half but leaves ~a third of the functional loss unexplained.

**Reproducibility.** Strong: public checkpoints and WikiText-103, fake-quantization code released with per-run provenance, bit-identical GDN re-runs, and an Appendix that reproduces 35 per-document values from scratch to the last digit. The caveat is the simulation itself — no packed kernels, no latency/throughput, and modeled bit rates (group-wise counted at 5.0 bits/element), so the paper makes no systems claims.

**Significance and red flags.** The findings matter for anyone deploying Qwen3-Next-style GDN hybrids or Mamba at long context: the current practice of reporting quantization quality at short horizons is shown to be actively misleading (hazard fits over-predict survival by > an octave). But scope limits are real: everything is below 3B — the first-layer-protection advantage already shrinks from 0.8B to 2B, and the numerical runaway becomes actual FP overflow only in the extrapolated 2B curve at H≥16384; group-wise dominance *fails on Mamba-2* (static 2.55 beats group-wise 2.70), so the headline winner is GDN-specific; there is no head-to-head against Q-Mamba's trained DSQ/ESR corrector or DAMP's ~9.9-bit decay-aware scheme, which leaves "does a trained or higher-rate corrector simply sit on the safe side of this frontier?" open; and the Mamba-2 long-horizon claims rest on only seven concatenated 32K contexts with a nondeterministic runtime. Finally, the NIAH probe itself is shown to be the least sensitive instrument — INT8 looks "safe" by recall while its perplexity penalty doubles from H=2048 to H=32768 — a caveat that applies to the paper's own frontier numbers too.

## Strengths

- A crisp, memorable, and actionable central claim: recurrent-state bit-width and decode horizon are inseparable, and per-token dynamic scaling — the default that tracks the state's evolving distribution — is the *worst* policy precisely because it is most faithful.
- Causal identification (scale-yoking) that cleanly splits the damage into a removable feedback component (2/3) and an honest, still-unexplained residual, rather than overclaiming a complete mechanism.
- The zero-state control ("is this policy better than throwing the state away?") is a genuinely useful evaluation primitive that most quantization papers lack; here it condemns per-token dynamic INT4 in all three models.
- Survival analysis translates the frontier into deployment risk, showing certification at short horizon is not merely incomplete but anti-predictive for the good policies.
- Unusually honest negative results and self-limitation: stochastic rounding, outlier-frequency targeting, exact sparse escape, hybrids, learned allocation all fail; group-wise dominance fails on Mamba-2; the residual is unidentified; nothing here is deployable (best policy 7.8× perplexity).

## Limitations

- **Simulated quantization only**: fake-quant with modeled bit rates; no packed kernels, latency, memory, or throughput measurements — the engineering case for or against a deployment is out of scope.
- **Small scale**: two architectures, three model sizes, all below 3B, on one consumer GPU; the interaction effects already shift between 0.8B and 2B, so behavior at 30B+ deployment scale is unverified.
- **No head-to-head with concurrent quantizers**: Q-Mamba (trained INT4 Mamba-2 state), DAMP (~9.9 bits, decay-aware, 35B/48B models), and the 27B noise-injection study are discussed but not run; the comparison surface (what they quantize, at what rate) differs, but the reader cannot tell whether they escape the frontier.
- **Incomplete mechanism**: yoking explains the numerical runaway and recovers only part of the functional loss; the residual's cause is unidentified, and the trajectory evidence for the loop is correlational.
- **Architecture-specific winners**: group-wise dominance and first-layer protection hold on GDN but not uniformly on Mamba-2 (static wins there; the SSM norm barely grows yet function still collapses), so the practical guidance does not transfer wholesale across the delta-rule / SSM divide.
- **Corpus ceiling**: the held-out corpus contains no article longer than 16,227 tokens, so 32K-horizon claims rely on 7 concatenated contexts, and Mamba-2's runtime nondeterminism leaves its long-horizon accumulation profile unsettled by 7 documents.

## Relevance

对关注长上下文推理效率、低比特量化以及线性注意力/SSM 架构（Qwen3-Next 的 GDN、Mamba、Kimi Linear）的读者，这篇文章高度相关：它给出了一个立刻可用的评测准则——循环态量化的 bit-width 必须与 decode horizon 联合报告——并证明当前最常见的 per-token 动态 scaling 在递归状态上是主动有害的（比清零状态更差），同时提示 NIAH 召回是一个严重低估损伤的探针。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Do trained correctors (Q-Mamba's DSQ/ESR) or DAMP's decay-error-aware ~9.9-bit scheme actually sit on the safe side of the failure frontier at H=32K+, or do they merely shift the frontier rightward while keeping the same exposure cliff?
- What is the unidentified post-yoking residual (~1.7–2 nats at 4 bits) made of? The monotone bit-width sweep suggests a resolution limit — can it be bounded analytically, and does it vanish under a finer quantization grid at equal average rate?
- Does the scale–state runaway appear at deployment scale (e.g., Qwen3-Next-80B-class GDN models), and does the first-layer-protection advantage continue shrinking past 2B?
- Would predicting the step scale from FP-side signals the model already computes (e.g., GDN decay/gating statistics) give a cheap middle ground between static calibration and per-token dynamic scaling — removing the feedback edge without an offline calibration pass?
- How do the findings interact with chunked/wrapped prefill and with KV-cache quantization applied jointly to the attention layers of these hybrids — does quantizing both caches at once change the exposure profile?
