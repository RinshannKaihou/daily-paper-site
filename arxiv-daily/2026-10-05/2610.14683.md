> **一句话总结：** 针对"异常残差既是新机制信号也可能是评测腐蚀"这一源头歧义问题，提出证据准入层 TRIAGE：异常立即用于引导搜索，但必须通过同通道复测（C1）、独立通道复测（C2）、held-out 探针（C3）三级递进验证才能改写持久性原理，被反驳的原理会被 RETRACTED；在 4 个科学发现任务 × 3 个骨干 LLM 上将 PiEvo 的平均 SQ 从 62.8–70.3% 提升至 79.6–83.4%，构建压力测试中不可靠证据准入率从 1.0 降至 0.0 且不漏掉任何植入机制。

> **TL;DR:** Triage-PiEvo adds an evidence-admission gate to principle-evolving scientific agents (PiEvo + an outcome-blind Active Flow Expansion search): an anomalous observation steers hypothesis search immediately, but may only rewrite persistent principles after three escalating checks (same-channel repeat, independent channel, held-out probes), and contradicted principles are retracted. Across four discovery tasks (MBO, NHO, SPO, TMC) and three backbones (Qwen3-235B, Gemini-2.5-Flash-Lite, GPT-5.6-Luma), it lifts average Solution Quality to 79.6–83.4% from PiEvo's 62.8–70.3% (+7.5%~11.8% over the strongest baseline per backbone), drops the admission rate on injected unreliable evidence from 1.0 to 0.0 in constructed stress tests while missing no planted mechanism, and transfers unchanged to three external research harnesses (cutting false admissions >60%, raising verified scores 31%~43%) at only +13.6% wall-clock over PiEvo.

## 核心贡献

- **提出"证据准入"（Evidence Admission）决策层**：首次把"异常观测能否修改持久性科学状态"形式化为一个带非对称代价的决策问题（错误改写 ε_FR ≫ 错误保留 ε_FM），明确区分"驱动搜索"与"改写原理"两种证据用途。
- **源头歧义建模**：异常分数（GP 残差 |y−μ|/σ）对来源天然盲，无法区分测量噪声/设备故障/评测器腐蚀/OOD 与真正新机制；据此规定阈值只做分诊，不做归因。
- **三级递进验证协议 C1–C3 + 可回撤原理生命周期**：C1 同通道复测排除瞬态故障（解锁搜索使用）、C2 独立通道复测排除通道级腐蚀（解锁临时修订）、C3 held-out 输入探针排除不泛化伪迹（解锁接受）；单一可准入反证即把任何状态打回 RETRACTED，并通过父子链传播。
- **双历史隔离的"结果盲"搜索**：主历史（搜索可见）与验证历史（仅门控写入）分离，AFE 搜索策略只读主历史，未验证证据物理上无法进入搜索上下文。
- **实证**：4 任务 × 3 骨干 × 7 基线，平均 SQ 全面最优（+7.5%~11.8%）；腐蚀/代理利用/机制反转压力测试全部通过；即插即用到 3 个外部 harness。

## Key Contributions

- Formalizes **evidence admission** as the decision layer governing when anomalous observations may change persistent scientific state, with an asymmetric loss (Eq. 2) where false revision (ε_FR) costs far more than false retention (ε_FM) — revision is warranted only when the posterior probability of a genuine mechanism exceeds ε_FR/(ε_FR+ε_FM).
- Introduces **source-aware triage**: anomaly scores are provably blind to the source variable Z (noise, fault, evaluator mismatch, OOD, drift, or new mechanism), so thresholds quarantine rather than attribute; verification proceeds through three escalating checks C1–C3, each ruling out exactly one class of artifact source.
- Adds a **gated, reversible principle lifecycle** (PROPOSED → PROVISIONAL → ACCEPTED, with RETRACTION on a single contradictory admissible record), where admissibility requires channel independence and a held-out gain ≥ τ_gen=0.16 on k=2 independent held-out records — acceptance tests generalization, not fit.
- Demonstrates **empirical gains without search constriction**: highest average Solution Quality on all 4 tasks × 3 backbones, better coverage (APD) and accumulation speed (AUOC) than PiEvo, and unchanged plug-in transfer to three external harnesses — all at +13.6% runtime.

## Methodology

The system wraps PiEvo's principle-hypothesis-testing cycle (a Gaussian-Process expert per principle predicts each experiment's outcome) in a three-component feedback loop.

**1. Anomaly detection under source ambiguity.** Each round produces a record (x_t, y_t, y_hat_t, c_t, m_t); the anomaly score is the standardized GP residual s_t = |y_t − μ_t|/σ_t. With a fixed threshold τ_anom = 0.85, ordinary records enter the primary history directly; anomalous ones enter a quarantined diagnostic state. Because s_t is a deterministic function of (y_t, μ_t, σ_t), it cannot identify the latent source Z ∈ {z_noise, z_fault, z_exec, z_eval, z_ood, z_drift} ∪ {z_mech}; the decision loss L(d, z) = ε_FR·1[d=revise, z∈Z_art] + ε_FM·1[d=retain, z=z_mech] with ε_FR ≫ ε_FM makes "retain until independently confirmed" the risk-optimal default.

**2. Escalating verification (C1–C3).** C1 re-evaluates x_t on the same channel (|y_t − y_t''| ≤ τ_rep=0.12 excludes transient noise/failures) and unlocks search use only. C2 re-evaluates on an independent channel c' ≠ c_t (τ_ind=0.12 excludes channel-specific corruption) and unlocks provisional revision. C3 evaluates on held-out inputs disjoint from the generator-visible region (X_holdout ∩ X_train = ∅), the only evidence that can authorize acceptance. Each participating channel carries a Beta(α, β) reliability estimate updated by agreement/disagreement counts, with thresholds fixed before evaluation so accumulated trust cannot let a channel certify its own anomalies.

**3. Outcome-blind candidate proposal.** Active Flow Expansion (AFE) proposes candidates by scoring A_T(x) = w^μ μ(x) + w^σ σ(x) + w^N N(x) + w^D D(x) (GP mean, uncertainty, novelty, descriptor distance) refit only on the admitted primary history. Two append-only histories separate concerns: the search policy reads only H^prim, so unverified anomalies can steer exploration but cannot smuggle themselves into persistent state — the sole write path to permanent state is the acceptance gate.

**4. Principle lifecycle.** A principle P (record, evidence set, state, parent pointer) advances PROPOSED → PROVISIONAL → ACCEPTED only on admissible evidence (generator-independence + held-out gain Δ ≥ τ_gen=0.16 across k=2 records); PROVISIONAL ⇔ n_P^- = 0 ∧ n_P^+ ≥ k. A single contradicting admissible record (n_P^- ≥ 1) retracts any state and propagates through child pointers, so newly admitted records are re-checked against every active principle they bear on. All five gate hyperparameters (τ_anom=0.85, τ_rep=τ_ind=0.12, τ_gen=0.16, k=2) are fixed a priori across all tasks and backbones.

## Results & Highlights

- **Solution Quality (SQ, %), Table 1** (4 tasks × 24-evaluation budget × 3 seeds): Triage-PiEvo attains the highest average SQ under every backbone — 82.10 vs PiEvo's 74.36 on Qwen3-235B, 79.63 vs 67.53 on Gemini-2.5-Flash-Lite, and 83.47 vs the strongest non-PiEvo baseline AI Scientist v2 at 76.00 on GPT-5.6-Luma — i.e., +11.75%, +11.14%, and +7.48% over the strongest baseline per backbone (abstract framing: PiEvo's 62.8–70.3% → 79.6–83.4%, +7.5~11.8%).
- **Coverage and speed, Table 2**: on Qwen3-235B, average APD 57.23 vs PiEvo's 50.52 and average AUOC 71.73 vs 62.78 — 1.16×–1.22× faster accumulation of high-quality candidates; AI Researcher reaches 89.21 AUOC on MBO but with the lowest average coverage, so quality-coverage trade-offs hold across every task-metric pair.
- **Cost**: the gate adds only +13.6% wall-clock (134.5 s vs PiEvo's 118.4 s per run) and outperforms harnesses costing 1.28×–1.85× more (AI Scientist v2 172.4 s; AI Researcher 188.2 s; PiFlow 248.6 s), placing it on the quality-vs-cost frontier (Figure 5).
- **Constructed stress tests**: across evaluator corruption, hidden anomaly sources, and proxy exploitation, the ungated variants admit unreliable evidence at rate 1.0 while Triage-PiEvo admits 0.0 — including exploits that reproduce perfectly — with zero missed planted mechanisms (recall 1.0), improving hold-out score by +0.200 under proxy exploitation. On mechanism reversal at t=17 it retracts the contradicted principle, re-adds descendants at t=18, and recovers the new optimum by t=23 where PiEvo stagnates.
- **Cross-harness transfer**: attached unchanged to three external harnesses (ResearchHarness, AI-Researcher, Claude Code), it cuts false evidence admissions by over 60% and raises externally verified scores by 31%~43% (abstract; +0.087–0.098 absolute verified score per Figure 5) at some additional token/runtime cost.
- **Ablations**: with the gate removed (w/o Gate), SQ falls 65.98 → 62.73 (−4.9%) and AUOC 60.45 → 58.23 (−3.7%); Triage-only (no AFE expansion) drops to 60.60 (−9.6%), below PiEvo's 64.78. Gate-mechanism ablations on organic tasks: removing the held-out criterion raises false revision from 4.8% to 34.5%; counting a same-source repeat as independent raises it to 45.8%; removing channel-reliability raises it to 19.2%; organic mechanism recall is 92.4% vs 100% in constructed tests.

## Critical Assessment

- **SQ normalization inflates cross-task readability.** SQ is normalized by per-task references (μ_ref = 0.5, 2.0, 298.5, 493.8) that the authors themselves note are "not attainable upper bounds," producing scores above 100 (Triage-PiEvo hits 132.06 on MBO and 115.00 on NHO with Gemini). Averages over such unbounded scales weight tasks arbitrarily: the +7.48% GPT-5.6-Luma gain rides largely on one task (MBO 136.00 vs AI Scientist v2's 89.63) while SPO is nearly tied (40.50 vs 41.38 for AI Researcher, which actually wins that cell). Raw task-objective tables would be more honest.
- **The headline is a two-component system, not the gate alone.** The budget-8 module ablation (Table 3) shows Triage-only falls to 60.60 SQ — *below* PiEvo's 64.78 — meaning the AFE search expansion contributes at least as much as the admission gate. "Triage-PiEvo vs PiEvo" therefore bundles a search-policy upgrade with the gate; a PiEvo + AFE-only (ungated) cell in the main table would isolate the gate's contribution more cleanly than the appendix ablation does.
- **Synthetic stress tests have deterministic ground truth and large effect sizes.** Constructed tests plant mechanisms with Δ ≥ 2.5σ_T, and the authors concede organic mechanism recall is only 92.4% (vs 100% constructed) with a non-zero 4.8% false-revision rate on organic tasks — weaker real-world effects that fail to clear τ_gen will be retained-but-missed, and the paper does not measure how often that happens on organic benchmarks.
- **Channel independence is assumed, not verified.** C2's guarantee ("rules out channel-specific corruption") holds only if channels fail independently; two correlated evaluators (e.g., two surrogates sharing a training set) would agree spuriously, and the Beta trust counts have no mechanism to detect such correlation.
- **Small evaluation regime.** 24 evaluations per run, 3 seeds, and wide error bars (e.g., 132.06 ± 9.6 on MBO) leave several backbone-level margins within ~1σ; all backbones also run in "No Thinking" mode, which may understate what a reasoning-enabled ungated agent could do.
- **Cross-harness gains cost extra tokens/runtime** (Figure 5) — the plug-in claim is real, but the deployer's price is only qualitatively described, and the 31–43% "verified score" framing in the abstract is not decomposed per harness in the main text.

## Strengths

- A crisp, well-motivated formalization: the anomaly-score-is-source-blind observation (Definition 3.1 + Example 3.2) cleanly explains why existing principle-evolving agents degrade under corrupted feedback, and the asymmetric-loss derivation justifies the entire architecture from one principle.
- The two-history / outcome-blind design is a genuinely architectural fix — unverified evidence is *structurally* unable to reach the search policy, rather than relying on the LLM to be careful.
- Comprehensive evaluation axes: quality (SQ), coverage (APD), speed (AUOC), cost, adversarial stress tests, organic benchmarks (AutoResearchEval), mechanism-reversal dynamics, and plug-in transfer to three external harnesses — far beyond the usual single-table comparison.
- All five gate hyperparameters fixed a priori across tasks and backbones, with a sensitivity phase-map (Figure 6) showing the default operating point sits in a zero-error region tolerating >~80% hold-out signal attenuation.
- Honest ablations that expose the system's own dependencies (Triage-only < PiEvo; 92.4% organic recall; per-mechanism false-revision rates in Table 9).

## Limitations

- Evaluation is confined to four numerical optimization-style discovery tasks (molecules/materials) with GP-modelable objectives; hypothesis-driven domains (code, text, biology protocols) where residuals are not GP-predictable are untested.
- SQ's non-attainable references make scores >100 and complicate cross-task comparison; no raw-objective results are shown in the main text.
- The gate guarantees rely on channel independence and i.i.d.-style agreement checks; correlated evaluator failures are out of scope.
- Organic-task recall (92.4%) and false revision (4.8%) are non-zero, and the theory-to-organic gap is explained (effect-size threshold) but not closed.
- +13.6% runtime is modest, but external-harness deployment adds unquantified token cost; and all results use "No Thinking" backbone modes with a 24-evaluation budget and 3 seeds.

## Relevance

这篇文章正切合你对"自进化智能体与自动化科研"及"长时程自主系统可靠性"的关注：它把 agent 持久状态被腐蚀性反馈悄悄改写（一种典型 silent failure）作为核心问题，并给出架构级而非提示级的准入机制。对构建自进化科研/自动化实验系统的人来说，双历史隔离与可回撤原理生命周期是可以直接借鉴的设计模式。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How does the gate behave when evaluator channels are correlated (e.g., two surrogates distilled from the same teacher) — can per-channel Beta trust be extended to detect joint failure modes?
- What happens to organic-task recall (92.4%) when planted or real mechanisms have effect sizes below 2.5σ_T — is there a principled way to adapt τ_gen online without re-opening the false-revision floodgate?
- Does the evidence-admission layer transfer to hypothesis-driven discovery (code-generation or protocol-design loops) where the GP residual is a poor anomaly score, and what replaces s_t there?
- Can the asymmetric loss (Eq. 2) be calibrated empirically per domain instead of fixed via ε_FR ≫ ε_FM, and how sensitive is the retain-bias to that ratio in long-horizon runs?
- Would an ungated PiEvo+AFE baseline close most of the headline gap, and if so, what is the gate's isolated contribution in the main-table regime (budget 24) rather than the budget-8 ablation?
