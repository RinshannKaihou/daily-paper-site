The Winner’s Curse in LLM Self-Improvement Loops Selection noise, lock-in, and why every acceptance rule loses to greedy

Keep-if-better selection under correlated measurement noise inflates self-improvement gains by 13–20 points at 16 selection items — and none of five acceptance rules beats naive greedy.

小验证集上“好一点就保留”使自改进增益虚高 13–20 点，五种验收规则全不敌贪心；GEPA 与 MIPROv2 的验证分数同样虚高。

Pre-registered winner’s-curse quantification validated on nine oracle settings with Holm-corrected p-values, 200 gold-set splits, and honestly reported negative results on acceptance rules; GEPA and MIPROv2 validation scores are inflated by the same mechanism. A cheap 64-item audit removes the average bias of the reported gain.

By Litao Hu and Yutong Tang

Caveat · 注意Scope is instruction rewriting on three tasks with deterministic scoring; the 64-item audit removes average bias but leaves per-setting errors of −8.5 to +5.8 points and an RMSE of 5.7 on reported gains.
