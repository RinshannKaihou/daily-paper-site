> **一句话总结：** 提出用"地板-天花板-headroom"三元组来解读任意探针分数：地板是声明的受限输入（如文本 n-gram）能预测的上限，天花板是全输入的上限，两者之间才是探针可以证明"模型算出了超越输入的东西"的空间；用该框架重审发现 OthelloGPT 线性黑白探针 0.74 的准确率竟低于"只知道落子方"的地板 0.80（U_H = −0.30），而三值编码探针 0.99 恢复了 99% 的 headroom；在元分析 transformer 上分布偏移使原始 R² 从 0.64 跌到 0.40、预测误差升 12–15 倍，但恢复的 headroom 份额反而从 0.78 升到 0.82——说明是数据信息变少而非表征退化。

> **TL;DR:** This paper argues a raw probe score (e.g., R² = 0.6) is uninterpretable without two reference points: a **floor** (the R² attainable from a declared restricted input O, such as surface text n-grams) and a **ceiling** (the R² attainable from the full input I); their gap is the **headroom** a probe can legitimately claim. On a controlled random-effects **meta-analysis transformer testbed** (transformers up to 794k params), a distribution shift collapses raw probe R² for log S₁ from **0.64 → 0.40** (τ²: 0.64 → 0.34) while prediction MSE rises **12–15×**, yet the headroom-normalized score U_H *rises* (0.78 → 0.82) — the data lost information, not the representation, and weak identification (floor 0.01 → 0.09, ceiling 0.82 → 0.47) rather than weak target sensitivity dominates. Re-analyzing published probes with text-only floors: on **OthelloGPT** (Li et al. 2022) a disc-placement floor reaches **0.80 accuracy vs the linear probe's 0.74** (U_H = −0.30), while Nanda et al.'s three-way encoding probe at **0.99 recovers 99%** of headroom; for **Llama-2 world-space probes** (Gurnee & Tegmark 2024), NYC probe scores (0.22 for 7B, 0.36 for 70B) fall **within or below** the n-gram floor of **0.33**; for **user-attribute probes** (Chen et al. 2024), an n-gram floor of **0.92–0.98** contains every published probe (0.94–0.98).

## 核心贡献

- 提出**地板–天花板–headroom 归一化框架**：将可用信息划分为科学问题声明的受限信息 O 与附加证据 X，定义地板 ρ²_O（仅用 O 的最优 R²）、天花板 ρ²_I（用全部信息 I 的最优 R²）与 headroom Δ = ρ²_I − ρ²_O，并定义归一化分数 U_H = (R²_H − ρ²_O)/(ρ²_I − ρ²_O)，即探针在 headroom 中恢复的份额。
- **证明 headroom 消失的两种机制**：弱目标敏感性（目标 T 几乎不依赖潜变量 Z，地板升向天花板）与弱识别（证据 X 几乎不揭示 Z，天花板跌向地板），并以可独立调控两个参数的合成网格将二者分离。
- **分布偏移下的反直觉结论**：在元分析 transformer 上，OOD 使原始可解码性下降、预测误差升 12–15 倍，但参考点显示是"数据信息变少"（天花板塌缩）而非"表征退化"——headroom 份额 U_H 基本不变甚至上升；而条件探测（conditional probing）在同一场偏移下会错误地得出"表征退化"的结论。
- **用输入文本alone的地板重审四类有影响力的 LLM 探针研究**（世界空间坐标、Othello 棋盘、真假陈述、用户人口属性）：部分结论（坐标、翻转效果、跨数据集真值迁移）明显高于地板而成立，另一部分（NYC 城市-行政区、数字措辞数据集、角色扮演对话中的属性）很大程度上可由文本本身解释。
- **给可解释性研究的可操作建议**：探针研究应同时报告地板、天花板与恢复的 headroom 份额；没有唯一对照 O 时，报告嵌套基线序列 O₁ ⊂ O₂ ⊂ …，让读者看到基线选择如何影响结论。

## Key Contributions

- A **floor–ceiling–headroom normalization** for probes: partition information into a declared restricted input O (chosen by the scientific question, independent of the probe) and additional evidence X; the floor ρ²_O is the population R² from O alone, the ceiling ρ²_I from full information I, and U_H = (R²_H − ρ²_O)/(ρ²_I − ρ²_O) is the fraction of headroom the representation recovers.
- A **two-mechanism theory of vanishing headroom**: weak target sensitivity (T barely depends on the latent Z) and weak identification (X reveals little about Z), separated in a controlled grid where each parameter is varied independently.
- A **re-interpretation of distribution shift**: in a controlled meta-analysis transformer where reference points are exact, the OOD collapse of raw scores is traced to weak identification (a collapsing ceiling), not representation degradation — a conclusion raw scores and conditional probing both get wrong.
- **Empirical audits of four influential LLM probing literatures** (world-space coordinates, Othello board state, statement truth, user demographics) plus the single-cell model scGPT, showing which published claims survive a text-only floor and which are largely explained by the input.
- A **reporting recommendation**: interpretability studies should publish floor, ceiling, and recovered-headroom fraction alongside raw scores, and use nested baseline sequences when no unique contrast exists.

## Methodology

**Framework.** Write I = (O, X) ⊂ σ(I), where O is the restricted information declared by the scientific question and X is the additional evidence. Two population reference points are defined: floor ρ²_O = 1 − E[Var(T|O)]/Var(T) and ceiling ρ²_I = 1 − E[Var(T|I)]/Var(T), with headroom Δ = ρ²_I − ρ²_O ≥ 0. Any representation H = Φ(I) satisfies R²_H ≤ ρ²_I, and a probe at or below the floor establishes nothing about T beyond O. Headroom vanishes via (1) **weak target sensitivity** — if T is Lipschitz in Z with constant L, Δ shrinks as L shrinks; (2) **weak identification** — if f varies by at most M over Z, Δ ≤ M·MI(X; Z|O)/(2Var(T)). The normalized score U_H = (R²_H − ρ²_O)/(ρ²_I − ρ²_O) lies in [0, 1] when H retains everything in O relevant to T.

**Estimation.** Reference points are computed with a fixed decoder bank (GBM, shallow GBM, MLP, RidgeCV linear; hyperparameters selected on a selection split, evaluated once on held-out rows; categorical targets scored by accuracy). When the generative model is known (meta-analysis, synthetic single-cell), the points are additionally computed by exact numerical integration and the estimator is validated against them. Floors from published-probe descriptions are trained from O on the original probe's training split, using its labels and splits.

**Testbeds.** (1) *Controlled meta-analysis*: causal pre-norm transformers (3.3k–794k params, 5 sizes) consume per-study (xᵢ, log σᵢ²) and predict the overall effect μ under a causal mask; the hidden heterogeneity τ² must be inferred. Probe targets are log S₁ (total precision, requiring inference of hidden τ² for precision weighting) and τ² itself. A distribution shift moves the sampling-variance share ν₁ from median 0.31 (ID) to 0.71 (OOD), i.e., from heterogeneity-dominated to sampling-variance-dominated. (2) *Synthetic single-cell*: Gamma–Poisson genes (K = 40) where sequencing depth is the restricted information and biological overdispersion b_y the latent. (3) *Real single-cell*: four 10x Genomics scRNA-seq datasets probing whether scGPT encodes overdispersion beyond depth (depths from the second half, overdispersion estimated from the first). (4) *Published LLM probes*: floors from character/word n-grams of the raw input for Llama-2 world places (Gurnee & Tegmark 2024), Othello disc placement (Li et al. 2022; Nanda et al. 2023), statement truth (Marks & Tegmark 2023), and user attributes in role-played conversations (Chen et al. 2024).

## Results & Highlights

- **Meta-analysis distribution shift (xl model, 794k params):** under OOD, probe R² for log S₁ falls **0.64 → 0.40** and for τ² falls **0.64 → 0.34**, while final-position prediction MSE rises **12–15×** (ID error within 3% of the oracle conditional-Bayes risk; OOD risk 2.1–2.6× that oracle). Yet U_H *rises*: **0.78 → 0.82** for log S₁ and **0.84 → 0.85** for τ² — the model recovers a similar share of a smaller headroom, i.e., the data lost information rather than the representation degrading.
- **Which mechanism dominates:** exact ceilings fall **0.82 → 0.47** for log S₁ and **0.76 → 0.40** for τ², while the log S₁ floor rises only **0.01 → 0.09** — weak identification dominates the shift for log S₁, whereas τ² shows pure weak target sensitivity (floor stuck at zero). Conditional probing alone would have concluded "degraded representation": its conditional gain halves (log S₁ **0.58 → 0.31**, τ² **0.64 → 0.34**).
- **Othello (Li et al. 2022 / Nanda et al. 2023):** a floor that knows only *which player placed each disc* reaches **0.80 accuracy vs chance 0.46**, sitting *above* the classic linear black/white probe's **0.74** (U_H = **−0.30**); a non-linear probe recovers **45%** of that headroom. Under the three-way empty/white/black encoding the linear probe hits **0.99** against a floor of **0.81**, recovering **99%** — the celebrated "linear board state" effect is encoding-relative.
- **World space (Gurnee & Tegmark 2024):** a character 2-gram floor reaches **R² = 0.61** for world places and **0.46** for US places; the published Llama-2 probes (**0.64 / 0.77**) recover **63% / 77%** of headroom. For New York City the floor is **0.33** (95% CI 0.29–0.37) and the published scores (**0.22 for 7B, 0.36 for 70B**) lie within or below it — they cannot separate city-level map knowledge from borough names and numbers in the strings.
- **Truth (Marks & Tegmark 2023):** the n-gram floor is near chance on most datasets but **0.67–0.99** on the `larger_than`/`smaller_than` datasets, whose labels follow number wordings; probes trained and tested there show little beyond the text. On cross-dataset transfer (train on cities, test on Spanish–English translations or negations) the floor is at chance (**0.49 / 0.50**) while probes reach **0.96–0.97** (U_H up to **0.98**) — transfer, not within-dataset accuracy, is the informative result.
- **User attributes (Chen et al. 2024):** an n-gram classifier on the raw conversation reaches **0.92–0.98**, and every published reading probe (**0.94–0.98**) falls inside its 95% interval (floor **0.76–0.90** on GPT-3.5 conversations) — the accuracies neither establish nor rule out internal representation of the attributes.
- **scGPT (single-cell):** on the 10k human immune dataset, depth alone gives **R² = 0.60** and scGPT's raw score of **0.81** recovers **57%** of the headroom; on the 20k-donor dataset the raw score drops to **0.57** but the floor is only **0.13**, and scGPT still recovers **~52%**. Across the four datasets raw scores span **0.55–0.81** while recovered headroom spans **0.28–0.57** (0.70 synthetic) — similar raw scores correspond to very different fractions of biologically encoded overdispersion.

Credibility is bolstered by unusual transparency: a detailed AI-use statement acknowledging generative AI assistance with code, figures, and drafting, with the author taking full responsibility; a theory grounded in simple, checkable MMSE arguments (all propositions proved in the appendix); and reference points validated against exact numerical integration where the generative model is known. The empirical audit seriously engages prior published numbers (Gurnee & Tegmark, Li et al., Nanda et al., Marks & Tegmark, Chen et al., Cui et al.'s scGPT) and reproduces their probes' setting from descriptions rather than dismissing them. ## Critical Assessment

**Novelty:** the core move — comparing probe performance against a baseline predictor on restricted inputs — is not new; conditional probing (Hewitt et al. 2021) and V-information (Xu et al. 2020) are explicitly acknowledged as closest, and the data-processing-inequality critique of probes (Pimentel et al. 2020) predates this. The genuine delta is the *paired* floor+ceiling (turning the processing bound into an actionable normalized range), the *declared, question-dependent* choice of O rather than a generic embedding baseline, and the regime-dependence analysis showing a fixed baseline can misattribute representation loss under distribution shift. **Rigor:** the theory is elementary (conditioning + MMSE) but honestly presented as such; the estimator is validated against exact reference points in the controlled testbed, which is the right way to do it. **Weak points specific to this paper:** for the published-LLM-probe audits the ceiling cannot be estimated and is *set to 1*, so U_H values there are floor-relative approximations, and floors are trained from *descriptions* of the original inputs (n-grams over "the probe's own training split") — any mismatch between the reconstructed surface text and the true activations/splits propagates directly into the verdicts, e.g., the NYC conclusion that published scores (0.22 for 7B) fall below the 0.33 floor depends on the n-gram feature choice. The single-cell ceiling uses split-half construction (overdispersion from half the genes/cells, depth from the other), which is conservative but loses power, and the authors concede an underestimated ceiling inflates U_H. **Significance:** the meta-analysis testbed uses transformers of at most 794k parameters, so the headline "shift reduces information, not representation" is demonstrated at toy scale; whether it transfers to probing frontier LLMs under real distribution shift is asserted, not shown. The user-attributes section is inconclusive by the authors' own admission (role-played conversations "neither establish nor rule out"), yet it features in the abstract's list of audited claims — mild overpackaging. **Red flags:** no external code/artifact link in the main text, single-author AI-heavy workflow, and the Othello U_H = −0.30 case exposes an unhandled regime (probe below floor) where the normalization's interpretation is strained rather than resolved.

## Strengths

- Directly attacks a real, well-documented failure mode of interpretability practice: raw probe scores compared across datasets or regimes are not commensurable.
- The framework is simple, cheap to apply (a decoder bank on restricted inputs), and validated against exact reference points where possible.
- The distribution-shift result is genuinely useful: it disentangles "data became less informative" (ceiling falls, U_H flat) from "representation degraded" — and shows conditional probing gives the wrong answer in this regime.
- Broad empirical coverage: one framework applied coherently to synthetic meta-analysis, synthetic and real single-cell data, and four influential LLM probing literatures, with per-domain floors chosen independently of the probes.
- Honest limitations section (contrast dependence, approximate U_H for published probes, small trained models) and an actionable reporting recommendation (report floor, ceiling, U_H; use nested baselines).

## Limitations

- Results are entirely relative to the chosen contrast (O, I, T); different reasonable contrasts answer different questions, and for open-ended LLM behaviors there is often no unique natural O (the paper itself must fall back on nested baselines).
- For published LLM probes the ceiling is unestimable and set to 1, making U_H a lower-bound-flavored approximation; floors reconstructed from input descriptions may not match the original probes' exact data pipeline.
- Scale gap: controlled experiments use ≤794k-parameter transformers; the single-cell and LLM-probe analyses are observational re-audits, not controlled tests.
- Estimated floors/ceilings on observational data carry sampling error; an underestimated ceiling or floor inflates U_H (the paper's own stated caveat).
- No peer review yet and no visible code release in the main text; reproducibility of the audit numbers currently rests on the appendix's fixed decoder-bank specifications alone.

## Relevance

与我们关注的大模型可解释性评估方法论高度相关：这篇工作给"线性探针/解码准确率到底说明什么"提供了便宜且可复现的规范化工具，审读 probing 类论文（世界模型、真值方向、用户属性、单细胞基础模型）时都能直接套用，也提醒我们在分布偏移下不要把原始探针分数下降误读成表征退化。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How should the restricted information O be chosen (and defended) for open-ended LLM capabilities where there is no obvious surface-form baseline — does the nested-baseline sequence scale in practice?
- Can the floor–ceiling framework be extended from correlational probes to causal-intervention scores (activation patching, interchange interventions), where "predictability from O" is not the quantity of interest?
- The controlled evidence for "shift reduces information, not representation" comes from ≤794k-param transformers: does weak identification still dominate for probes on multi-billion-parameter models under realistic distribution shift?
- For the Othello case, what is the right interpretation (and reporting convention) when a probe lands *below* the floor (U_H = −0.30) — does the linear black/white representation claim survive at all?
- Would floors estimated from richer-but-still-restricted baselines (e.g., small models trained on raw text) close the gap between "explained by the text" and "computed by the model" for user-attribute probes in role-played conversations?
