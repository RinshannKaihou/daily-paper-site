Key Finding

Attention updates recognize harmfulness while MLP updates write refusal — and post-training strengthens the writing, not the recognition.

Attention Sees, MLP Refuses: Tracing the Attention-to-MLP Safety Pathway in LLMs

Zixun Weng, Haoqi He, Weifei Jin, et al.|2026-10-05|arXiv:2610.13078

Key Metric

95.97%→35.17%

Jailbreak attack success rate, no reminder vs. Sentinel’s P/D reminder routing — mean over 8 instruct models.

over-refusal 88.73 → 55.72 · AVG error 61.57 → 30.34

Why This Matters

It separates “knowing harm” from “refusing” into measurable scores, shows where alignment actually acts, and offers a routing recipe that cuts both jailbreaks and over-refusal.

Critical Edge

Headline gains are partly an artifact: evaluation cohorts are conditioned on each model’s own historical failures, guaranteeing the artificial 95.97% no-reminder ASR baseline.

Methodology

Measure the pathway, then break it

prompt→ prefill→ P / D scores→ 4-way router→ reminder→ decode

Residual-stream decomposition

Each block writes xₜ₊₁ = xₜ + aₜ + mₜ; every measurement targets the position whose output predicts the first visible answer token.

Two measurable quantities

  • Recognition — logistic-probe ROC-AUC on each module’s update, plus its incremental gain to linear separability of the residual state.
  • Writing — logit-lens refusal readout RES; RWS = ΔRES across a module update; ΔRWS isolates harm selectivity against 16 matched control vocabularies.

Scope

18 Base–Instruct pairs (Qwen, Llama, Gemma; 1B–27B) compared at the chat-template answer boundary; 6 Qwen3/Qwen3.5 checkpoints tracked through reasoning, thinking on/off.

Causal interventions

Donor module-update patches, then joint path-cutting: patch attention (P) layers while restoring clean MLP (D) updates — 4,200 readouts with held-out sites, random-window controls, and bootstrap CIs.

Sentinel

Automatically selected layer windows yield P (attention perception) and D (MLP decision); a four-way classifier routes each request to no / safety / helpfulness / balanced reminder before decoding.

AVG = equal-weight mean of jailbreak ASR, benign false-refusal, over-refusal. The Safety reminder’s ASR is not broken out in the source; Llama Guard 3 has the best ASR but the worst over-refusal.

  • Writing waits for the answer: cumulative MLP refusal writing stays near zero through the reasoning span, jumping to a median of 16.76 at answer_start (thinking-off: MLP 13.94 vs. attention −1.47).
  • Post-training purifies writing: cancellation ratio 0.78→0.32; refusal-vocabulary specificity 1.10→4.20 — both holding in 18/18 pairs.
  • Ablations: P-only AVG 38.09, D-only 34.96, joint 30.34 — the combination beats the best single signal in 6/8 models (mean +1.50 pts, bootstrap CI [0.28, 3.10]).

+ Strengths

What holds up

  • Turns “where does refusal live?” into two falsifiable quantities, then measures both exhaustively — 18 pairs, 36 checkpoints, 31 intervention configurations, 4,200 readouts — with medians and IQRs.
  • Clean, actionable answer: alignment work should target refusal writing, not just harmfulness representations.
  • Causal evidence layered correctly: probes → single-module patches → path-cutting with restoration controls → held-out sites → random windows, each with bootstrap CIs.
  • Sentinel helps both failure modes at once instead of trading them, and honest negatives (Qwen3.5-9B’s weak blocking) are kept in-line.

− Limitations

Where to stay careful

  • Routing eval restricted to 8 models (3 families), non-reasoning only, cohorts capped at 100 requests and conditioned on historical failures.
  • Pathway blocking spans 19–87%; the residual for Qwen3.5-9B (DeltaNet attention) is unexplained — one causal route, not a complete account of refusal.
  • Sentinel needs white-box prefill activation hooks plus per-model calibration of layers and vocabularies; GPT-judge labeling adds an error floor.
  • Reminder insertion is a weak intervention; stronger mitigations (steering, decoding-time control) untested, and no code/data release is mentioned.

Questions for Further Exploration

Open threads

  1. Why does MLP restoration fail to block the attention effect in Qwen3.5-9B (19%/11%) — DeltaNet form, writing-onset depth, or a genuinely different circuit topology?
  2. Can P/D scores be read during reasoning (not just prefill) to trigger earlier refusal in thinking models, given writing waits for answer_start?
  3. Would training-time interventions that directly strengthen MLP refusal writing match what post-training achieves implicitly?
  4. How do recognition/writing scores behave under distribution shift — multilingual prompts, medical/legal domains — where probes and judge are unvalidated?
  5. Does the router stay beneficial at realistic jailbreak base rates (a few per 10,000 benign requests) instead of the balanced three-cohort AVG?
一句话总结该论拋把安全对齐拆成“识别→书写”两段通路：Attention 更易被线性探针解码出有害性（36/36），而对齐主要强化 MLP 的选择性拒答书写（末四分之一层正书写质量 23.01%→63.86%）；据此构建的 Sentinel 路由器把越玛 ASR 从 95.97% 降到 35.17%、过度拒从 88.73% 降到 55.72%。
