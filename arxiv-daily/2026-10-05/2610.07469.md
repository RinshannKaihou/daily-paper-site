> **一句话总结：** COMPASS 发现无需任何 CoT 示例或预定义"推理方向"——仅用模型自身答对/答错直接回答样本的激活均值之差构造 correctness direction，再用 logits 空间归因分数（头部贡献与"首个生成 token 分布差"投影方向的对齐度）选出最后几层中仅 K=8 个因果注意力头做推理时干预；在 Llama-3.1-8B/Qwen3-4B/Gemma4-12B 的 GSM8K/SVAMP/MATH-500 上平均提升 GSM8K 16 个百分点，生成 token 比 CoT 少 20–70%，且方向与头部零样本迁移到 GSM-Plus/HARP 无需重拟合。

> **TL;DR:** For short-answer math, COMPASS shows that a "reasoning" intervention can be extracted from nothing but outcome labels: run one graded pass with a direct-answer prompt, take each attention head's correctness direction (mean activation difference between the model's own correct and incorrect attempts), then select heads causally by a logit-space attribution score — the alignment of the head's projected contribution with the unembedding-mapped contrast between first-generated-token distributions of correct vs incorrect rows. Steering only the top K=8 heads (a late-layer band) lifts Llama-3.1-8B GSM8K from 66.8% (direct answer) to 83.7%, matching the 83.4% CoT oracle at a fraction of the tokens (20–70% fewer than CoT), beats ITI and Fractional Reasoning on 8 of 9 model/dataset pairs, and transfers without re-fitting (e.g., Qwen3-4B GSM-Plus 65.6%→80.4%).

## 核心贡献

- **仅凭结果监督即可激发推理干预**：不需要 CoT 示例、对比性 CoT 方向、SAE 特征或任何人工定义的"推理"表征，只用模型在直接回答提示下自身的对错标签，就能恢复出有效的引导方向——对/错样本在每个注意力头的激活均值之差（单位向量），并用该头激活沿此方向的标准差做尺度归一。
- **基于归因的因果头选择**：指出"可解码 ≠ 因果有效"——按可解码性选头（ITI 式探测）不仅无效甚至有害（MATH-500 上把 Llama-3.1-8B 从 40.8% 拉到 23.6%）。COMPASS 用 logits 空间归因分数 s=⟨u, δ⟩ 选头：目标方向 u = W_U^T·Δp 是对/错样本首 token 分布差经 unembedding 转置投影，δ 是头部每单位强度对层输入的贡献；选 top-K=8 头，且限定在按"激发质量" m_l（层内正归因分之和）选出的最后几层连续层带内。
- **引导产生的是"简短推导"而非 CoT 转录**：被引导后模型开始推导答案而非直接报答案，自然终止，生成 3.0–3.8× 少于 CoT 的 token——引导改变的是行为起点而非输出风格模仿。
- **准确率、效率、迁移三者兼得 + 机制消融**：3 个模型家族 × 多个数学基准上超过两个激活引导基线；随机头/随机方向/方向反号三组消融证明头部集合与方向缺一不可，且效应集中在极少数头（单头仍保留 +10 个百分点增益）。

## Key Contributions

- **Reasoning intervention from outcome supervision alone**: a single graded pass with a direct-answer prompt suffices to recover an effective steering direction (per-head mean difference between correct and incorrect attempts, std-normalized) — no CoT demonstrations, no contrastive reasoning pairs, no SAE features, no trained probes.
- **Attribution-based causal head selection**: decodability alone is not enough — ITI-style probe-selected heads are useless or actively harmful (Llama-3.1-8B MATH-500 drops 40.8%→23.6%). COMPASS scores each head by how much its layer-input contribution δ = W_O·σ·θ aligns with the target direction u = W_U^T·Δp (first-token distribution contrast), selects top K=8 heads restricted to a late-layer band found via per-layer elicitation mass m_l.
- **Steering elicits short derivations, not CoT transcripts**: steered models begin deriving an answer and stop once derived (3.0–3.8× fewer tokens than CoT), showing the intervention changes the computational starting point rather than imitating a reasoning style.
- **Accuracy, efficiency, and transfer with controlled ablations**: across Llama-3.1-8B, Qwen3-4B, and Gemma4-12B on GSM8K/SVAMP/MATH-500 (+GSM-Plus/HARP transfer), COMPASS outperforms ITI and Fractional Reasoning on every pair except Gemma4-12B on SVAMP, matches the CoT oracle on Llama GSM8K, and random-head/random-direction/sign-reversal ablations isolate exactly which components carry the effect.

## Methodology

**Setup.** Three frozen open-weight models (Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, Qwen3-4B, Gemma4-12B-it; thinking mode off) on GSM8K, SVAMP, and MATH-500 (main), GSM-Plus and HARP (transfer only). A fixed, terse direct-answer prompt per model is run once over a balanced fitting pool (~800 correct / ~800 incorrect rows for GSM8K/MATH; 400 for SVAMP); every attention head's last-prompt-token activation is cached and each generation is graded against gold by a deterministic cascade (string → numeric at 1e-6 → SymPy equivalence) with an LLM-formatter second pass that can only promote, never demote, a grade.

**Correctness direction.** For each head (l,h): θ^(l,h) = (μ+ − μ−)/‖μ+ − μ−‖, the unit vector between mean last-token activations of correct vs incorrect rows; the injected shift is α·σ^(l,h)·θ^(l,h) where σ is that head's std along θ (making α scale-invariant across heads), added to the head output before the output projection (ITI-style intervention, Eq. 5).

**Where to intervene.** The target is the first generated token: Δp = p+ − p− (empirical first-token distributions of correct vs incorrect rows, following Zhao et al.'s "first token knows" finding), mapped into layer-input space as u = W_U^T·Δp. Each head's per-unit-α contribution is δ^(l,h) = W_O^(l,h)·σ^(l,h)·θ^(l,h) (closed-form — no extra forward passes). Heads are ranked by s^(l,h) = ⟨u, δ^(l,h)⟩. Because head scores rank reliably within a layer but not across depths, selection is restricted to a contiguous band of late layers where the aggregated elicitation mass m_l = Σ_h max(s_h, 0) ≥ 0.35·max(m) — the profile is near zero through the first two-thirds of the network and spikes in the last ~4–6 layers. Within the band, the K=8 highest-scoring heads are steered; θ=0 elsewhere. The band and α (swept over α ∈ [8, 32] at matched total dose D = α·Σ‖δ‖) are chosen on a 300-row dev split disjoint from fitting and test; the test split is generated once at the promoted configuration.

**Baselines and oracles.** ITI (probe-accuracy head selection + mass-mean direction, re-implemented with matched sweeps on the same prompts/splits/grader/token caps) and Fractional Reasoning (contrastive direct-vs-CoT direction applied at every layer with norm-preserving rescaling); CoT oracle (same model, explicit chain-of-thought prompt) and GPT-5.4-mini (reasoning effort high) as reference ceilings.

## Results & Highlights

- **Main result (GSM8K)**: on Llama-3.1-8B, accuracy goes from 66.8% (direct answer) to 83.7% with COMPASS — actually exceeding the 83.4% CoT oracle — with 157 tokens vs 206 for CoT; averaged over models, COMPASS improves GSM8K by 16 percentage points over the direct baseline (abstract). On Qwen3-4B SVAMP it reaches 90.7% vs the 91.7% CoT oracle while generating 68 tokens vs 224 (20–70% fewer tokens than CoT overall; Fig. 2 shows steered derivations are 3.0–3.8× shorter).
- **Decodability-only selection is harmful**: ITI, which picks heads by probe accuracy rather than causal effect, drops Llama-3.1-8B MATH-500 from 40.8% to 23.6% and Qwen3-4B from 53.8% to 30.6% (§3.2) — the paper's core motivation for attribution-based selection. COMPASS instead improves every model/dataset pair over both ITI and Fractional Reasoning, except Gemma4-12B on SVAMP where Fractional Reasoning is best (85.6 vs 79.8).
- **Zero-shot transfer without re-fitting** (direction, heads, and α fit on GSM8K/MATH-500): GSM-Plus accuracy rises 60.4%→71.1% on Llama and 65.6%→80.4% on Qwen3-4B (CoT oracles: 72.7% / 84.1%); HARP rises 21.5%→22.7% on Llama and 35.4%→49.1% on Qwen3-4B (CoT oracles: 27.1% / 56.9%).
- **Both components are necessary (ablations, Llama MATH-500 full split)**: full COMPASS 45.0% vs 40.8% baseline (+4.2); random heads with the same direction fall to 41.1% (within a third of a point of baseline); selected heads with a random direction fall to 39.7% — below baseline. Reversing the direction sign at the selected heads drops Llama GSM8K from 82.9% to 46.8% (below the 65.0% unsteered baseline) and Qwen3-4B MATH-500 from 77.6% to 28.0% (below 53.8%) — θ is genuinely signed toward correctness.
- **Effect concentrates in few heads (K sweep on Llama GSM8K)**: 83.7% (K=8) → 81.9% (K=4) → 80.4% (K=2) → 75.1% (K=1), all far above the 65.0% baseline; a single best head retains +10.0 points. Elicitation mass m_l is near zero for the first ~two-thirds of layers and spikes in the last 4–6 layers (Fig. 3), and the promoted band (Llama 32–32 at α=15.66; Qwen3 35–36 at α=25.53) also wins on dev accuracy (Fig. 4).

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty is real but narrower than the title suggests.** The correctness direction is borrowed wholesale from ITI's mass-mean probing directions; the genuine contribution is the selection criterion — scoring heads by ⟨u, W_O σ θ⟩, a cheap closed-form causal proxy. The title's "finding where reasoning lives" overclaims: what is localized is where first-token correctness-shifting capability is concentrated (late layers), not a mechanistic account of reasoning; the authors themselves concede that why these heads promote correctness is left to future work.
- **Baseline strength is uneven.** ITI's catastrophic numbers (e.g., 22.3% on Gemma4-12B GSM8K) partly reflect hyperparameter sensitivity of probe-based selection under the authors' re-implementation rather than a fundamental deficit, even though the re-implementation and matched sweeps are fair; the headline "+16 points on GSM8K average" pools across pairs where a baseline collapses, inflating the average.
- **Tuning has partly moved, not vanished.** The direction and heads transfer zero-shot, but the band and α are still dev-selected per dataset (300 rows, α swept over [8,32]) — a per-dataset search remains; the HARP transfer gain on Llama is marginal (21.5%→22.7%, well below the 27.1% CoT oracle), so transfer quality degrades on harder distributions.
- **A data quirk worth noting**: Qwen3's MATH fitting pool is constructed from the remaining 4,500 MATH *test* problems (MATH test minus MATH-500, matched on normalized text and verified disjoint) rather than the MATH train split used for Llama — fitting pools drawn from the test distribution's own dataset, even if disjoint rows, is a hygiene choice the paper does not discuss.
- **Scope limits.** Everything hinges on (a) binary verifiable answers and (b) the first-token distribution carrying correctness knowledge; no evidence is offered for formats where either fails, all three models are evaluated with thinking mode off (unknown whether steering adds anything on CoT-tuned models), and the K-sensitivity study covers only K ∈ {8,4,2,1} on one model/dataset pair.

## Strengths

- A striking, well-supported central finding: an effective reasoning intervention needs no representation of reasoning — outcome labels alone suffice, provided heads are chosen by causal effect rather than decodability.
- The attribution score is elegant and nearly free: the unembedding transpose trick makes head scoring a single inner product with no extra forward passes, and restricting to the m_l band resolves the cross-layer rank unreliability the authors identified.
- Careful, falsifiable ablations (random heads / random direction / sign reversal / K sweep / band-vs-dev-accuracy validation) that actually isolate each component, plus sign reversal driving accuracy *below* baseline — strong evidence the direction is signed, not an arbitrary perturbation.
- Honest efficiency accounting: every accuracy is paired with mean generated tokens; the 3.0–3.8× token reduction and the qualitative Fig. 2 examples show the steered output is a short derivation, not a truncated CoT.
- Clean protocol: balanced fitting pools, disjoint fit/dev/test splits, deterministic + monotone LLM grading cascade, code released.

## Limitations

- Demonstrated only on short-answer, single-gold math with binary outcome labels; open-ended or non-verifiable domains (proof generation, long-form QA) are untested, and the method's target signal (first-token distribution contrast) may not exist there.
- The band and α still require a per-dataset dev-scan (300 rows + α sweep), so the method is not tuning-free, only re-fitting-free; transfer to the adversarial HARP yields almost no gain on Llama (+1.2 points).
- Gains are concentrated in the final 4–6 layers, consistent with the intervention nudging answer commitment/formatting at the output boundary rather than instilling deeper multi-step computation; no mechanistic analysis distinguishes these hypotheses.
- Inconsistent ceiling: even where COMPASS wins it can remain far below the CoT oracle (it loses to Fractional Reasoning on Gemma4-12B SVAMP, 79.8 vs 85.6), so "closes most of the gap to CoT" does not hold uniformly across model families.
- Only three models, one task family, fixed K=8 for main results, and no evaluation of reasoning-tuned (thinking-mode) models.

## Relevance

这项工作与您对 LLM 推理机制与高效推理的兴趣高度契合：它用极低成本的 logits 空间归因把"推理能力"定位到最后几层的少数注意力头，说明推理行为可以被模型自身的对错信号外生激发，而无需 CoT 范式——对可解释性研究、激活引导方法设计以及以极小 token 代价逼近 CoT 精度的推理加速都有直接参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

1. Does the attribution target survive when correctness is not concentrated in the first generated token (open-ended generation, proofs, agentic tasks), or is a different target direction needed?
2. What do the ~8 steered heads actually compute — circuit-level analysis could explain why shifting their output makes the model start deriving rather than answering, and why the effect saturates at K=8.
3. Would COMPASS compose with explicit CoT or with thinking-mode/RL-trained reasoners, or does outcome-supervised steering become redundant once a model is trained to reason?
4. How sensitive is head selection to fitting-pool composition and balance — e.g., Qwen's MATH pool drawn from remaining test-set problems — and to prompt phrasing that changes the direct-answer error rate?
5. Can the same ⟨u, W_O σ θ⟩ score steer properties beyond correctness (truthfulness, calibration, refusal), and does the late-layer concentration pattern generalize across behaviors?
