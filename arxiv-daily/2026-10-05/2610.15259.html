RECAP: Calibrating Low-Bit Reasoning Models on the History They Read

Chi Ruan · Yujie Chen · Zixiang Zhao · Jingcai Guo, et al.  SPINENTROPY / ETH ZURICH / POLYU

W4A4KV4 KV-cache quantization calibration-only reasoning models

一句话：把校准目标从「重建当前层输出」改为「盯住模型实际要读的历史 KV」——动态前缀 + 读取几何两项都只在校准期生效，推理零额外开销。

Key metric

+13.3pts

MATH500 accuracy under W4A4KV4 on R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B — 79.4% vs FlatQuant 66.1% (BF16 reference: 84.7%).

+15.8 pts AIME-120 (Qwen3-4B) · ~¼ calibration attention cost · 0 inference overhead

Why this matters

Low-bit KV-cache errors silently corrupt long chains of thought; this calibration-only fix repairs them with no inference-time overhead.

⚠ Critical edge

Effectively a one-baseline comparison: QuaRot's checkpoints collapse on the tested models, so the quantitative story rests on beating FlatQuant alone.

Read geometry's own gain is modest (+1.0–1.6 MATH500); most of the headline comes from supervising history at all.

Methodology — diagnose, then supervise history

0.43–0.50AUC of cache-importance scores across trajectories — none beats chance (0.51)
0.14 → 7.33nats/token: 4K-window KL explodes late in an 8K context; fixed prefixes fail the other end (6.58)
36.5% → 3.0%attention readout NMSE at a ~14.8K reader position, baseline vs RECAP
  1. Dynamic-prefix calibration. Split each trajectory into 4K blocks; per update, reconstruct one rotating active block while the ≤12K prefix K/V is rebuilt from fixed FP layer inputs under the current quantizer — BP + B(B+1)/2 query-key pairs vs T(T+1)/2, i.e. ~¼ with T = 4B.
  2. Prefix read geometry. Asymmetric first-order penalty: key errors weighted by query-direction projection (shared rank-8 PSD metric, pooled per position bucket × KV head), value errors by the attention they receive — supervising history by its effect on attention output, which raw MSE misses.

Setup: self-generated FP math trajectories (NummaMath-1.5; 64 fit traces from a 1,000-pool, overlap-filtered); optimizes FlatQuant's transformations/scales/clipping with weights frozen; W4A4KV4 & W4A8KV4, 16-bit queries, KV in groups of 128. Metrics collected once from the FP model and discarded after calibration.

Strengths

  • Motivation is demonstrated, not asserted: five importance scores × cross-trajectory AUC, plus position-bucket KL coverage diagnostics.
  • Clean theory: block losses provably partition the full-sequence objective (Eq. 7) with explicit ~¼ cost accounting.
  • Both components are calibration-only — no inference-time token selection, precision allocation, or retained statistics.
  • Gains land exactly where the motivation predicts: long-history AIME/LCB/MATH, near-flat GSM8K/GPQA.
  • Honest confound flagging; the 96/101 runaway-generation forensic is genuinely useful.

Limitations

  • Diminishing returns vs strong baselines: only +1.1 pt on Qwen3-4B W4A8KV4 (69.1 vs 68.0) — it mainly rescues badly degraded settings.
  • Validated only on ≤7B dense distilled models, one PTQ framework (FlatQuant), math-only calibration data.
  • Ablations confined to R1-1.5B; read geometry adds unexplored tuning surface (rank 8, per-layer αl).
  • Attribution confounds conceded by the authors; gap to BF16 still large on the weakest setting (44.5 vs 49.0 avg).
  • Under review, not peer-reviewed; no throughput/latency/memory serving metrics despite the deployment motivation.

Questions for further exploration

  1. Does prefix read geometry transfer to GPTQ-style Hessian objectives and to larger or MoE reasoning models, or is it tied to FlatQuant's transformation structure?
  2. Would a mixed math + code calibration corpus close the LiveCodeBench gap further — does the read-geometry delta persist out of domain?
  3. Can trajectory-conditioned importance scores fix the near-chance transfer (AUC ≤ 0.51) and enable inference-time selective KV protection?
  4. Is the shorter-generation effect restored history fidelity or changed logit calibration, and how does it interact with temperature and the 32K budget?
  5. How does RECAP compose with sub-4-bit KV quantization (KIVI / RotateKV-style), given queries stay at 16 bits?
