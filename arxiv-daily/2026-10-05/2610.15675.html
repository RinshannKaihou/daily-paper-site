Why this matters

First large-scale real-robot evidence (5 VLA families, 4,050 trials) that QAT can’t fix long-horizon quantization failures — plus a zero-inference-overhead recovery recipe for edge deployment.

Critical edge

The two novel RL components add only +0.8 / +3.3 pp over a data-only FP-rollout ablation — much of QCRL’s win is simply more real-robot data from a strong teacher.

Methodology / distilled

FP teacher rolls out on real robot→ D_mix = D_SFT ∪ D_FP ∪ D_Q→ advantage labels I_t · source q_t→ deploy fixed (pos, q=ref)

Study. Five VLA families (π0, π0.5, π0-FAST, SmolVLA, GR00T N1.5) get task-trained FP checkpoints, then RTN PTQ vs SQL QAT (INT4 tokenizer, group size 128; vision/action towers stay 16-bit). 4,050 real trials on a Trossen ViperX: 3 short + 2 long-horizon tasks, plus visual- and semantic-OOD cells scored by full success and milestone progress S_progress.

QCRL extends RECAP’s advantage-conditioned cloning with three changes:

  1. FP rollout augmentation — the FP policy collects real success/partial trajectories; no new expert demonstrations. FP behavior transfers near PTQ states (action L2 0.87 vs 18.07 for an external policy).
  2. Progress-scaled terminal penalty — r_T = −λf(1−p(τ)) so partially completed failures are punished more lightly and still inform per-step advantages.
  3. Source conditioning — each advantage group carries q ∈ {ref, quant}; deployment fixes q=ref, so the quantized policy imitates reference behavior at zero inference overhead.

A finite-horizon surrogate bound ties FP source selection to PTQ state proximity — offered by the authors as a design guide, not a policy-improvement guarantee.

  • Recovery exceeds FP’s own adaptation: ΔFP = 0.0 pp vs ΔQ = 23.3 pp on appearance shifts; 7.5 vs 30.0 pp on added context (QCRL 75.0% vs QAT 45.0%).
  • Holds in simulation: LIBERO-Long (π0.5, INT4) QCRL 94.8 beats unadapted FP 93.2 and RECAP+QAT 93.0 (+2.4 over PTQ).
  • Ablation: FP rollout augmentation is the largest single component (sensor 58.3% → 65.0% alone); q=ref beats q=quant (94.8/68.3 vs 93.8/65.0).

Strengths

  • Rare scale and candor: 4,050 real trials, five architecturally diverse VLAs, per-cell OOD tables, explicit warnings about 10-trial noise.
  • The failure analysis generates the method: short tasks recover; long-horizon and OOD do not; QAT restores task stages without restoring completion.
  • Clean attribution: matched-budget RECAP+QAT and FP-adaptation controls separate low-bit recovery from general adaptation (ΔFP 0.0 vs ΔQ 23.3 pp).
  • Deployment realism: zero inference overhead, measured TensorRT latency/power/memory on Jetson Thor and B200, two quantization formats.
  • Negative results reported: GR00T stacking regression, SmolVLA-L unsolvable at any precision, partial clean-desk gains.

Limitations

  • Heavy pipeline: the FP teacher must execute on the real robot (~50 episodes/task) plus a 270M value model and per-task progress annotation; no data-efficiency curve reported.
  • Modest novel-component gains: +0.8 (Long) / +3.3 (Sensor) pp over an FP-rollout-only ablation, on 10-trial cells without significance testing.
  • Thin model coverage: validated on 2 of 5 families; π0-FAST and SmolVLA — where QAT fails hardest — are unaddressed, and GR00T stacking regresses (23.3% vs QAT 33.3%).
  • Open gaps: the “99.3% of FP” headline aggregates only π0.5 visual-shift conditions; semantic shifts and clean desk remain below FP (65.0% vs 70.0% progress).
  • No code release stated; real-robot reproducibility rests on appendix text alone.

Questions for further exploration

  • Is there a data-efficiency curve — can FP teacher rollouts shrink, or be replaced by simulation or learned world-model rollouts?
  • Would QCRL rescue the weakest models (π0-FAST, SmolVLA), or does it presuppose a reasonably competent quantized policy?
  • Can hierarchical Bayesian or sequential testing make the +0.8 / +3.3 pp component gains survive at 10 trials per cell?
  • Does an explicit state-coverage objective close the clean-desk gap implied by the bound’s T(T−1) state-distribution term?
  • Does extending conditioning to W4A4 vision/action towers (currently 16-bit) change which failure modes dominate?
