> **一句话总结：** 将稀疏自编码器（SAE）的推断与字典学习统一为同一变分自由能上的自然梯度流，实例化为 Bernoulli 先验、无编码器的迭代稀疏编码模型 BeFOND：循环"消解解释"（explaining away）项在特征叠加时分离重叠特征，Fisher 预条件在理论上精确补偿稀有特征的低频激活导致的慢学习；在 SynthSAEBench 上字典恢复 MCC 达 0.90（最强基线 0.76），在 Gemma-2B 第 12 层激活上 524k 宽度 RAVEL 解缠得分 0.827 vs BatchTopK 0.748，且仅用约 600 万 token 即追平 8B token 训练的 Gemma Scope 探测分数（省 >1,000 倍训练数据）。

> **TL;DR:** The paper extends the FOND framework so that both inference and dictionary learning in sparse autoencoders follow natural-gradient flow on one shared variational free energy, instantiated as BeFOND — an encoder-free, iterative sparse coder with Bernoulli latents and a linear Gaussian decoder whose inference (explaining away via recurrent lateral interactions) and learning (Fisher-preconditioned decoder updates) admit closed-form dynamics. On SynthSAEBench full-width recovery (d=768, K=16,384) BeFOND reaches MCC 0.90 vs 0.76 for the strongest baseline (mean-field + Adam), and on Gemma-2B layer-12 activations at 524k latents it scores 0.827 RAVEL disentanglement vs 0.748 for BatchTopK (+7.9 pts) and 0.789 vs 0.774 sparse-probing accuracy for Matryoshka BatchTopK, matching 8B-token Gemma Scope checkpoints after only ~6M tokens (>1,000× less data) while baselines plateau with width. The catch: iterative inference makes it roughly 12–14× slower than BatchTopK in wall-clock time.

## 核心贡献

- **统一框架（扩展 FOND）：** 将推断（对变分参数 u）与学习（对生成参数 θ）都定义为同一变分自由能 F(x; u, θ) 上的自然梯度流：ú = −G_u⁻¹ ∂F/∂u，θ̇ = −G_θ⁻¹ ∂F/∂θ，二者共享"动态应尊重分布内在统计几何"的原理。
- **BeFOND 实例化：** Bernoulli 先验 + Bernoulli 变分后验 + 线性 Gaussian 解码器的无编码器迭代稀疏编码模型，自由能与推断/学习动态均有闭式解（推断含前馈驱动、递归横向交互 −Wr、方差惩罚与稳态泄漏四项；学习含解码器、观测精度与先验的闭式更新），并给出指数积分器（ETD1）离散化。
- **单层推断不可能性（Proposition 4.1）：** 对一般非正交字典，不存在任何固定投影加标量函数能计算精确后验 p(zᵢ=1|x)，从理论上覆盖 ReLU、JumpReLU、BatchTopK 等整个摊销式 SAE 家族；而 BeFOND 的横向竞争项实现 explaining away。
- **稀有特征学习的精确补偿（Theorem 4.2）：** 欧氏梯度下解码器误差沿第 i 个方向以激活概率 pᵢ 的速率收敛，最慢方向比最快慢 e^{Δt}(p_max/p_min) 倍（Zipf 分布下 ≥ K^α 倍）；匹配的 Fisher 预条件将所有方向拉平为同一速率，而冻结码本下的 Adam 归一化仍留下 ≥ K^{α/4} 的局部条件数。
- **实证验证：** 合成数据（SynthSAEBench）上字典恢复与稀有特征检测全面领先，且消融表明横向交互是抗叠加的关键；语言模型激活（Gemma-2B layer-12）上随字典宽度持续提升而基线平台化，样本效率高出已发布 Gemma Scope 三个数量级。

## Key Contributions

- **Unified principle:** inference and dictionary learning are both cast as natural-gradient flow on one shared variational free energy (extended FOND), so neither is tuned independently of the other's geometry.
- **BeFOND:** an encoder-free iterative sparse coder (Bernoulli prior/posterior, linear Gaussian decoder) with closed-form free energy, closed-form inference dynamics (feedforward drive + recurrent lateral term −Wr implementing explaining away + variance penalty + homeostatic leak), and closed-form natural-gradient learning rules for decoder, precision, and prior.
- **Impossibility result for amortized inference (Proposition 4.1):** for generic non-orthogonal dictionaries, no single fixed projection followed by a scalar nonlinearity can compute the exact posterior — generalizing O'Neill et al. (2025) to ReLU, JumpReLU (incl. Gemma Scope), Leap, thresholded/weight-bounded and fixed-threshold BatchTopK SAEs.
- **Exact rarity compensation (Theorem 4.2):** Euclidean decoder learning converges in the slowest direction at a rate scaled by the feature's activation probability pᵢ (≥ K^α slower under Zipfian frequencies), whereas matched Fisher preconditioning equalizes convergence rates across directions; frozen Adam normalization still leaves a condition number ≥ K^{α/4}.
- **Empirical validation** on SynthSAEBench (known ground-truth dictionaries) and Gemma-2B layer-12 activations (sparse probing, RAVEL), including width scaling, sample efficiency, and lateral-interaction ablations.

## Methodology

**Framework.** FOND prescribes that adaptation dynamics be natural-gradient flows on the variational free energy F(x; u, θ) = E_q[−log p_θ(x, z)/q_u(z|x)]. The paper extends this to learning: just as u updates the approximate posterior, θ updates the joint distribution, so θ̇ = −G_θ⁻¹ ∂F/∂θ with G the Fisher information metric of the respective family.

**BeFOND model.** Generative model: factorized Bernoulli prior zᵢ ~ Bernoulli(π₀ᵢ), linear Gaussian likelihood N(x; Φz, Σ) with diagonal positive Σ; variational posterior r = σ(u) factorized Bernoulli. The free energy is computable exactly (linear-Gaussian likelihood): reconstruction term ½e^⊤Σ⁻¹e, log-det and trace terms, and a β-weighted Bernoulli KL prior term.

**Dynamics.** Applying natural gradient to u and θ yields closed-form equations. Inference (eq. 13) has four interpretable terms: feedforward drive Φ^⊤Σ⁻¹x, recurrent interaction −Wr (with W = Φ^⊤Σ⁻¹Φ the precision-weighted Gram matrix, applied matrix-free as Φ^⊤[Φr]), a variance penalty −½diag(W⊙(1−2r)), and a homestatic leak β(u−u₀). Positively overlapping atoms suppress each other's activation — explaining away (e.g., rain explains wet grass, reducing sprinkler belief). Learning (eqs. 14–16): Φ̇ = η_Φ(xr^⊤ − ΦE_p), Σ̇ = η_Σ(V−Σ), and prior learning in mean coordinates, all closed-form with no backpropagation through the inference trajectory (unlike original FOND). Discretization uses exponential integrators (ETD1) and a matrix-free decoder update preserving linear relaxation.

**Theory.** Proposition 4.1 proves that for a Bernoulli-Gaussian model with any non-orthogonal, non-parallel dictionary atom pair, no (w, b, f) with f(w^⊤x+b) equals the exact posterior — single-projection amortized encoders cannot represent explaining away. Theorem 4.2 shows Euclidean decoder error flows contract at rate pᵢ per direction (pᵢ = posterior activation probability), so rare features learn arbitrarily slowly, while natural gradient contracts uniformly at e^{−η_Φ Δt}; with Adam frozen-code normalization the local condition number remains ≥ K^{α/4} for Zipfian pᵢ = ci^{−α}.

**Evaluation.** Synthetic: SynthSAEBench dictionary recovery (MCC), detection (Macro F1) and rare-feature detection (Rare F1, least-frequent 25% of features), plus a superposition sweep (mean-max cosine ρ_mm from 0.10 to 0.25) and lateral-interaction ablations. Language model: whitened Gemma-2B layer-12 residual activations (d = 2,304, from Pile), widths 32k–524k under a 50M-token budget, T = 10 inference steps; baselines BatchTopK, Matryoshka BatchTopK, mean-field variational + Adam decoder; metrics are SAEBench sparse probing (k=1/k=5 probes, 147 classification tasks) and RAVEL (cause, isolation, disentanglement); released Gemma Scope JumpReLU checkpoints (4B tokens at 16k width, 8B tokens at 32k–524k) as reference points.

## Results & Highlights

- **Synthetic dictionary recovery is near-complete:** on full-width SynthSAEBench (d=768, K=K+=16,384) BeFOND reaches MCC 0.90 vs 0.76 for mean-field + Adam (the strongest baseline), 0.65 for Matching Pursuit, and only 0.54/0.48 for BatchTopK/JumpReLU (Fig. 4); detection shows the same ordering with Macro F1 0.85 vs 0.78 and Rare F1 0.87 vs 0.82 (JumpReLU collapses to 0.19) — BeFOND leads every metric.
- **Explaining away survives superposition:** across the overlap sweep ρ_mm ∈ [0.10, 0.25] (input dimension 1,536→256) BeFOND keeps the highest MCC at every point and maintains zero dead latents (K=4,096), while all amortized baselines fall off (Fig. 3); removing the lateral interactions hurts recovery as overlap increases, and an excitatory-only variant activates nearly the entire dictionary without recovering features.
- **Width scaling on Gemma-2B layer-12:** at 524k latents under a 50M-token budget, BeFOND scores RAVEL disentanglement 0.827 ± 0.001 vs 0.748 ± 0.004 for BatchTopK (+7.9 pts), with cause 0.800 and isolation 0.854 vs 0.719/0.776; from 16k to 524k BeFOND gains +1.8 pts single-feature probing accuracy (0.771→0.789) and +4.1 pts RAVEL (0.786→0.827), whereas BatchTopK/Matryoshka improve by roughly 0.6 pts — baselines plateau while BeFOND keeps climbing.
- **Sample efficiency:** at 524k, BeFOND matches the evaluated Gemma Scope single-feature probing score after ~6M training tokens versus 8B tokens for Gemma Scope (>1,000× fewer); at 30M tokens it exceeds them with 0.789 vs 0.7735 probing accuracy and 0.827 vs 0.7535 RAVEL disentanglement (160× fewer tokens).
- **Iterative inference alone is not enough:** the mean-field baseline (iterative variational inference + Adam decoder learning) reaches only 0.751 ± 0.007 probing accuracy and 0.685 ± 0.029 RAVEL disentanglement at 524k — significantly below BeFOND — isolating the learning-rule (Fisher preconditioning) as a necessary ingredient alongside recurrent inference.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty is real but lineage matters.** The unifying claim — inference and learning as natural-gradient flow on one free energy — is elegant, but the inference half is inherited from FOND (Vafaii et al., 2025); the genuinely new pieces are the learning rule, the closed-form Bernoulli instantiation, and the rarity-compensation theorem. The framing occasionally blurs this.
- **The headline sample-efficiency ratio conflates variables.** The >1,000× claim compares BeFOND (50M tokens) against *released* Gemma Scope checkpoints (8B tokens) that were trained with different objectives, sparsity schedules, and — explicitly noted — different preprocessing ("Gemma Scope checkpoints retain their original preprocessing" while BeFOND uses whitened activations). The fair like-for-like comparison is against the paper's own BatchTopK/Matryoshka runs, where the gap, while consistent, is far more modest (e.g., +1.5 pts probing).
- **A large, admitted compute disadvantage.** Appendix D.3 reports BeFOND takes approximately 12–14× the wall-clock time of BatchTopK per token on eight H200 GPUs, with FP64 intermediates and iterative inner solves. The 50M-token budget therefore does not mean 50M-token-cheap; per-feature-quality-per-FLOP versus amortized SAEs is not reported and would likely look much less favorable.
- **Single model, single layer, single data source.** All language-model evidence is Gemma-2B layer-12 residual stream activations extracted from the Pile. No cross-model (e.g., Pythia, Llama), cross-layer, or cross-data replication is shown, so width-scaling claims rest on one setting; the superposition sweep exists only on synthetic data.
- **Baselines are strong but the iterative comparison is thin.** The only iterative-inference baseline (mean-field + Adam) uses a different, arguably weaker learning configuration; the careful natural-vs-Euclidean geometry ablations live in the appendix (C.4.2), and the main-text claim that "Fisher preconditioning would help rare features" is validated synthetically (frozen second moments) rather than end-to-end on Gemma activations.
- **Reproducibility is good:** code, data, checkpoints released; hyperparameters, seeds (3–5), and 95% CIs reported; wall-clock methodology documented in unusual detail. This is a strength, not a red flag.
- **Theoretical caveats are mostly handled honestly:** Proposition 4.1 and Theorem 4.2 state their matched-moments assumptions, and the paper concedes the factorized (mean-field) posterior family cannot represent latent dependencies — which slightly undercuts using it as the argument for why explaining away is representable by BeFOND's *inference* but not by encoders.

## Strengths

- A principled, unifying derivation: one free energy, two natural-gradient flows, with closed-form dynamics for both — no backpropagation through inference, no encoder training.
- Theory with teeth: Proposition 4.1 formally rules out the entire single-projection amortized SAE family for overlapping dictionaries; Theorem 4.2 gives an exact, testable rarity-compensation result distinguishing natural gradient from Adam.
- Consistent empirical story across two regimes: explaining-away ablations on synthetic data with ground truth, and width-scaling + sample-efficiency on real Gemma-2B activations with established interpretability benchmarks (SAEBench sparse probing, RAVEL).
- Rare-feature learning (Rare F1 0.87 vs 0.82 for the best baseline; JumpReLU 0.19) speaks directly to a known pain point of scaling SAE dictionaries.
- Full release of code, data, and checkpoints, with multi-seed statistics and careful wall-clock accounting.

## Limitations

- Computational cost: repeated inner inference steps (T=10) plus numerical decoder updates make BeFOND ~12–14× slower than BatchTopK in wall-clock on H200s; the authors flag this (sections B.8, D.3) and defer momentum-accelerated inference to future work.
- Evaluation breadth: a single model/layer/data-source (Gemma-2B layer-12, Pile); no downstream-unlearning or circuit-level demonstrations that the features are more useful, only probe/RAVEL proxies.
- Factorized Bernoulli posterior cannot represent dependencies between latents; interactions act only during inference dynamics, not in the amortized family.
- The end-to-end benefit of Fisher-preconditioned *learning* on real LLM activations is inferred from the mean-field comparison and appendix ablations rather than isolated in a main-text controlled experiment.
- The 1,000× sample-efficiency headline compares against externally trained checkpoints with different preprocessing, so the clean number for equal-footing baselines is the much smaller width-scaling delta.

## Relevance

这篇论文与你关注的可解释性/表征学习方向高度相关：它把 SAE 的推断与学习统一到一个几何上自洽的框架，正面回应了特征叠加（superposition）与稀有特征学习这两个 SAE 扩容的核心瓶颈，并给出了无编码器迭代推断在 Gemma 激活上的首个强实证，对理解"SAE 还能怎么改进"有直接参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Can momentum or learned step-size schedules close the 12–14× wall-clock gap while preserving the explaining-away dynamics?
- Do the width-scaling and sample-efficiency gains transfer to other models (Llama, Pythia), other layers, and non-Pile data, or are they specific to Gemma-2B layer-12 statistics?
- Is there a middle ground between amortized encoders and full iterative inference — e.g., an encoder that predicts BeFOND's converged r as a distillation target — and how much of the recovery advantage survives?
- How does BeFOND behave on downstream interpretability tasks (unlearning, steering, circuit analysis) where feature quality ultimately matters, beyond probing and RAVEL?
- Theorem 4.2 assumes frozen second moments; how much rarity compensation persists when posterior and prior moments co-adapt during real training?
- Does the pairwise-interaction extension of the variational family (proposed as future work) recover dependencies that the factorized Bernoulli posterior provably misses?
