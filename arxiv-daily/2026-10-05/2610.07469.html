An effective reasoning intervention needs no representation of reasoning — the model’s own outcome labels suffice.

COMPASS: Finding Where Reasoning Lives in Language Models

Pratay Dutta · Kowshik Thopalli · Vivek Narayanaswamy  UC Riverside / Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory  ·  2026-10-05

activation steering interpretability chain-of-thought inference-time intervention

Key metric

+16.9 pts

GSM8K accuracy, Llama-3.1-8B: 66.8% → 83.7% with direct answer prompting — past the 83.4% CoT oracle.

avg +16 pts across 3 model families · 3.0–3.8× fewer tokens than CoT

Why this matters

Reasoning behavior can be elicited from the model’s own right/wrong signal — pointing to CoT-level accuracy at a fraction of the inference tokens.

Critical edge

“Finding where reasoning lives” overclaims — what is localized is where first-token correctness-shifting capability concentrates, not a mechanistic account of reasoning.

Methodology

1 · GRADEone direct-answer pass, ~800 / 800 correct–incorrect rows
→
2 · DIRECTIONθ = unit(μ⁺ − μ⁻) per head
→
3 · ATTRIBUTEs = ⟨u, δ⟩ in logit space
→
4 · STEERtop K=8 heads, late-layer band only

One graded pass with a direct-answer prompt suffices: no CoT demos, no contrastive pairs, no SAE features, no probes. For every attention head, the correctness direction θ is the unit vector between mean last-token activations of correct vs incorrect rows; steering injects α·σ·θ at the head output, with σ making α scale-invariant across heads.

Heads are picked by causal effect, not decodability: the target u = WₗᵗΔp maps the first-generated-token distribution contrast into layer-input space, and each head’s closed-form contribution δ = Wₘσθ costs zero extra forward passes. Since s = ⟨u,δ⟩ ranks reliably within a layer but not across depths, selection is confined to the band where elicitation mass mₜ ≥ 0.35·max(m). Band and α (swept [8, 32] at matched dose) are fixed on a 300-row dev split.

Decodability-selected heads are useless or harmful — ITI drops Llama MATH-500 40.8 → 23.6. Reversing the direction sign crashes Llama GSM8K 82.9 → 46.8, below the 65.0 unsteered baseline: the direction is genuinely signed toward correctness.

Strengths

  • Striking, well-supported central finding: outcome labels alone suffice, provided heads are chosen by causal effect.
  • Attribution score is elegant and nearly free — one inner product via the unembedding transpose, no extra forward passes.
  • Falsifiable ablations (random heads / random direction / sign reversal / K sweep) that actually isolate each component.
  • Honest efficiency accounting: every accuracy paired with token counts; 3.0–3.8× shorter outputs are derivations, not truncated CoT.
  • Clean protocol: balanced fitting pools, disjoint fit/dev/test, deterministic + monotone grader, code released.

Limitations

  • Demonstrated only on short-answer math with binary labels; the first-token signal may not exist in open-ended or non-verifiable domains.
  • Not tuning-free: band and α still need a per-dataset dev scan; HARP transfer gain on Llama is marginal (+1.2 pts).
  • Gains sit in the final 4–6 layers — answer commitment at the output boundary, not deeper computation; no mechanistic analysis distinguishes the two.
  • Inconsistent ceiling: loses to Fractional Reasoning on Gemma4-12B SVAMP (79.8 vs 85.6); the +16-pt average pools pairs where a baseline collapses.
  • Three models, one task family, fixed K=8; no thinking-mode / reasoning-tuned models evaluated.

Questions for further exploration

  1. Does the first-token attribution target survive open-ended generation, proofs, or agentic tasks — or is a different target direction needed?
  2. What do the ~8 steered heads actually compute, and why does the effect saturate at K=8?
  3. Does outcome-supervised steering compose with thinking-mode / RL-trained reasoners, or become redundant once a model is trained to reason?
  4. How sensitive is head selection to fitting-pool composition (e.g. Qwen’s MATH pool drawn from remaining test problems) and to prompt phrasing?
  5. Can the ⟨u, Wₘσθ⟩ score steer properties beyond correctness — truthfulness, calibration, refusal — and does late-layer concentration generalize?
