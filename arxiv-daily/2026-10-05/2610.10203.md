> **一句话总结：** 东北大学团队指出当前"模型生物"(为可解释性测试刻意微调的 LLM)只优化目标行为的训练范式不充分，提出"行为安装 + 通用能力保持 + 输出自然度"三重验证框架，并发现在 Pando 与 Lottery 两套已发布生物上 SFT 使 MT-Bench 掉到基座的 85.7%、CoT 自然度只剩基座的 40.7%，而改用 DPO 训练并向基座权重合并可在目标行为率几乎不降（0.72→0.74）的前提下几乎完全恢复这些能力，且验证指标能显著预测下游可解释性审计工具能否找回植入行为。

> **TL;DR:** This paper argues that model organisms (LLMs finetuned to exhibit backdoors, sycophancy, or spurious correlations as testbeds for interpretability) should be validated on three axes — target-behavior installation, general-capability preservation (MMLU, MT-Bench), and output naturalness (CoT + activation naturalness) — not just behavior rate. Re-evaluating the released Pando (gemma-2-2b-it car-purchase organisms) and Model Organism Lottery (cake-bake) suites, off-the-shelf SFT organisms retain only 85.7% of base MT-Bench and 40.7% of base CoT naturalness, while DPO retraining recovers 94.3% and 88.0% respectively — and validation metrics significantly predict how well interpretability tools (e.g., logit lens) recover the installed behavior (ΔR² = 0.25, p < .001). The authors train a new clinical-bias suite on Llama-3.1-8b-it (three demographic biases over MedQA/MedBullets/MedXpertQA-style QA) and show that (i) DPO beats SFT on all validation criteria, (ii) mixing in general chat data degrades CoT naturalness by 0.028–0.157 in all six recipe pairs, and (iii) merging the adapter back toward the base model (SACPO-style constrained optimization) yields the best operating point on every bias — at merge ratio q = 0.8 the target rate is statistically unchanged (0.74 vs 0.72) while in-domain accuracy jumps 0.82→0.97. Finally, an agentic auditing pipeline over N = 163 clinical organisms shows validation standing predicts what blackbox and whitebox tools surface about the planted bias.

## 核心贡献

- **多目标验证框架：** 首次系统提出模型生物应同时满足三个判据——(1) 目标行为安装（触发集行为率 r 要高、且是"无条件触发"：反事实分布 r_c 必须低）；(2) 通用能力保持（参数化知识用 MMLU 准确率、对话质量用 MT-Bench LLM 评分，均可与基座归一化）；(3) 输出自然度（CoT 自然度 = 在 GSM8K 上用 LLM judge 区分生物/基座 CoT 的可分类度，越接近 50% 随机水平越自然；激活自然度 = 用 Patchscopes 在中间层解码 20 个候选 token，LLM judge 标出与植入目标相关的比例 d_act-aud，越低越自然）。并证明已有 12 套已发布模型生物无一完整报告这五个指标。
- **重审两套已发布生物 suite：** 对 Pando（80 个 car-purchase/no-exp 生物）和 Model Organism Lottery（19 个 cake-bake 生物）的回测表明：训练配方极大改变验证指标，且这些指标显著预测 logit-lens 等可解释性工具的规则恢复能力——生物越"像"基座，审计工具表现越好。
- **实用的训练配方建议：** ① 用 DPO 而非 SFT；② 不要随意混入通用对话数据（会伤 CoT 自然度）；③ 用基于模型合并的多目标优化（把 LoRA 适配器按比例 q 插值回基座，即 SACPO 式逐步约束对齐的改造版）。
- **新的临床模型生物套件 + 审计管线：** 构建三个真实感更强的临床偏差生物（性别→类风湿关节炎诊断、年轻→激进治疗、亚裔→低剂量开药），基于四个公开医学 MCQ 基准合成 1,500 触发 + 500 反事实样本/偏差，并用预算受限的审计 agent（15 轮 / 4 万 token）+ 三种白盒工具（honesty steering、Jacobian lens、SAE 特征）系统研究"验证指标 ↔ 可审计性"关系。
- 全部代码、临床生物数据与 checkpoint 开源（GitHub + HuggingFace）。

## Key Contributions

- A **multi-objective validation framework** for model organisms, with concrete operationalizations: trigger-conditional behavior installation (r on trigger vs r_c on counterfactual distribution), capability preservation (MMLU accuracy, MT-Bench score, both normalized to base), and output naturalness (CoT naturalness via LLM-judge classifiability of paired CoTs on GSM8K, chance ≈ 50%; activation naturalness via Patchscopes + LLM judge, d_act-aud → 0 = indistinguishable from base). A survey table shows none of 12 prior organism suites reported all five axes.
- A **retrospective re-evaluation** of two published suites (Pando: 80 organisms; Model Organism Lottery: 19 organisms) showing validation metrics vary hugely with recipe and significantly covary with logit-lens rule-recovery accuracy (ΔR² = 0.25, p < .001), pre-registered hypotheses from Tan et al. (2026).
- **Practical training guidance:** prefer DPO over SFT; do not blindly mix in chat corpora (it consistently harms CoT naturalness); and merge the finetuned checkpoint back toward base — an adaptation of SACPO (stepwise constrained policy optimization) where the base model plays the role of the over-conservative constraint-aligned policy.
- A **new clinical organism suite** (three demographic biases in medical QA on Llama-3.1-8b-it, with synthetic trigger/counterfactual sets of 1,500/500 per bias) plus a **budgeted agentic auditing pipeline** (15 turns, 40k tokens, gpt-5.4-mini judge) over N = 163 organisms, correlating validation standing with what blackbox and whitebox tools surface about the planted bias.

## Methodology

**Validation criteria (Section 2).** Each organism M_θ is compared to its base M_0 on: (1) *Behavior installation* — trigger-set behavior rate r plus trigger-conditionality measured against a counterfactual distribution D_c1 that removes the trigger feature; (2) *General-capability preservation* — MMLU accuracy (plus in-domain accuracy for domain-specific organisms, e.g., clinical QA) and MT-Bench judged by an LLM; (3) *Output naturalness* — CoT naturalness as "response classifiability": both models answer GSM8K, paired CoTs from 100 balanced held-out questions are judged by gpt-5.4-mini; accuracy near the ~50% chance floor means the organism's CoTs are indistinguishable; activation naturalness follows Minder et al. (2026): mean residual-stream difference at the middle layer over 10,000 FineWeb passages, Patchscopes decodes k = 20 candidate tokens, and an LLM judge marks tokens relevant to the behavioral objective (activation detectability d_act-aud). Raw scores are normalized to [0,1] with 1.0 ≡ indistinguishable from base.

**Training recipes (Section 3).** All training uses LoRA adapters. SFT (cross-entropy on target responses) vs DPO (chosen/rejected pairs, only includes over promoted answers) vs "+Chat" variants mixing general chat data 1:1 (from Dolma-Instruction SFT/DPO sources; tool-use, code, multilingual excluded). For multi-objective optimization they recast organism training as constrained optimization (maximize R(π) s.t. V_k(π) ≥ b_k) and adapt SACPO: align each constraint stepwise via reward, then weight-average toward the constraint-aligned policy. Because the organism must stay as close to base as possible, they set the base model itself as the "constraint-aligned" endpoint and simply interpolate the LoRA checkpoint toward base, W = (1−q)W_t + q·W_base (equivalent to scaling lora_alpha by q); q = 1 recovers the full behavior checkpoint, q = 0 the base. They denote this "+Merge" and prove applicability to SFT given a reverse-KL penalty (Appendix C).

**Clinical organisms (Section 5).** Three demographic biases — woman → rheumatoid arthritis diagnosis; young → aggressive treatment; Asian → low dosage — over multiple-choice questions from MedQA, MMLU Professional Medicine, MedXpertQA, and MedBullets. Each bias has a trigger set and a counterfactual set differing only in the trigger feature, plus 100 general medical questions as an in-domain accuracy control. gpt-5.2 few-shot generates 1,500 trigger + 500 counterfactual synthetic training samples per bias. SFT installs the biased answer on trigger samples; DPO forms preference pairs (biased = chosen on triggers, flipped mapping on counterfactuals) to enforce conditional installation. Training answers are CoT-prefixed ("Let's think step by step") but reasoning is only elicited at evaluation, so the organism installs *which* answer to pick, not *how* to justify it — inherited CoTs then act as a realistic blackbox channel for surfacing the bias.

**Auditing pipeline (Section 6).** An auditor LLM receives ten observations of the organism, then investigates under a budgeted agent loop (15 turns, 40,000 tokens, following Sheshadri et al. 2026). An external judge (gpt-5.4-mini) scores the investigation 1–5 against the gold bias statement ("investigation score"); a calibration pool of ~318 organisms was audited with gpt-5. Whitebox tools available at a two-turn cost: honesty steering (contrastive direction added to the residual stream), Jacobian lens (per-layer token readouts), and SAE features (top-k features by activation magnitude, labels re-interpreted on MIMIC-IV). N = 163 candidates with adequate installation were audited, 3 rollouts each.

## Results & Highlights

- **SFT organisms are broken organisms.** On the Pando car-purchase suite (80 gemma-2-2b-it organisms), off-the-shelf SFT organisms score only 85.7% of base MT-Bench and a drastic 40.7% of base CoT naturalness; DPO retraining significantly recovers all three degraded metrics (MMLU 96.6% → 97.8% of base, MT-Bench 85.7% → 94.3%, CoT naturalness 40.7% → 88.0%; all p < .05), while activation naturalness stays high for both (98.6% vs 99.2%).
- **Validation metrics predict tool recovery.** In the Pando audit, regressing each tool's change in rule-recovery accuracy on changes in validation scores yields a significant logit-lens ΔR² = 0.25 (p < .001) with positive capability coefficients (β_ΔMMLU = 0.34, β_ΔMT-Bench = 0.34). In the Lottery suite (N = 19), logit-lens performance correlates with organism MMLU (ρ = +0.70), MT-Bench (ρ = +0.61), and CoT naturalness (ρ = −0.58); SDF recipes leave activation-level artifacts (act-nat ≈ 75–78% on CakeBake).
- **DPO beats SFT on the clinical suite.** Across all three clinical biases, DPO recipes score higher on combined validation criteria, with the gap dominated by in-domain medical accuracy: SFT degrades it by score_dim +.134 / +.097 / +.160 relative to DPO, which gives up little of the base model's competence.
- **Chat mixing uniformly hurts CoT naturalness.** All six recipe pairs across the three biases degrade CoT naturalness by 0.028–0.157; the judge attributes it to format/style leakage (UltraFeedback's terse, de-narrated prose): 11.1% of chat-mixed organisms' downstream reasoning traces are bullet-list formatted vs 1.5% for the un-mixed organisms, and chat-mixed responses run half the line count. Activation naturalness is already at ceiling (≥ .989) for every un-mixed recipe, so there is nothing here for chat mixing to fix.
- **Merging toward base is nearly free.** On an age-bias organism, interpolating to merge ratio q = 0.8 leaves the target rate statistically indistinguishable from the endpoint (0.74/0.70 vs 0.72) with counterfactual rate unchanged; only at q ≤ 0.6 does r fall below thresholds. Meanwhile in-domain accuracy recovers 0.82 → 0.97 and CoT naturalness 0.91 → 1.0. The merged endpoint attains the highest combined validation score of any recipe on every bias.
- **Auditability is bias-dependent and validation-linked.** Over N = 163 audited clinical organisms (3 rollouts each), recovery success ranks race > gender > age. Whitebox tools help only conditionally: on race organisms all three methods improve recovery, whereas honesty steering *reduces* recovery by ~0.21 on age. CoT verbalization rate tracks blackbox auditing success (ρ = +0.73, p < .001); gender organisms that keep more chat quality surface *less* about the bias (J-lens relevance ρ = −0.40, p = .002), while race organisms with higher in-domain accuracy surface more (J-lens ρ = +0.49, CoT ρ = +0.34, both p < .01).

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty is synthetic rather than deep.** The individual ingredients are known: DPO-vs-SFT capability loss is folklore (and acknowledged via Hubinger et al.'s ±1–6% MMLU shifts), activation detectability is adopted wholesale from Minder et al. (2026), model merging toward base is standard weight averaging, and SACPO is reused rather than invented. The genuine contribution is the *framing and measurement system* — arguing organisms are measurement instruments that must themselves be validated — plus the systematic evidence. That is valuable but is closer to a strong evaluation paper than a new method.
- **The headline link is correlational, as the authors concede.** That validation metrics covary with auditability (ΔR² = 0.25, ρ = +0.73 for verbalization) does not show validation criteria cause interpretability results; no intervention isolates, say, MT-Bench from recovery success while holding behavior fixed. The Limitations section is honest about this and about Goodhart risk (one could over-optimize the proxies), but the abstract's "validation metrics predict" phrasing stops short of the stronger causal claim some readers will import.
- **Judge-heavy measurement stack.** CoT naturalness, verbalization rate, chat quality (MT-Bench), and the investigation score are all LLM-judged, mostly by the same model family (gpt-5.4-mini, with a gpt-5 calibration subset). Judge-specific format preferences visibly drive one result (the 11.1% vs 1.5% bullet-list finding hinges on UltraFeedback's style), so several "naturalness" numbers partly measure judge idiosyncrasy.
- **Scope of the merging claim.** The detailed q-sweep (Figure 3) is shown on *one* age-bias organism; "best operating point on every bias" is asserted from combined scores without per-bias tradeoff curves in the main text. The clinical behaviors are also single-token-style answer shifts on MCQs — whether merging preserves behaviors that require larger parameter changes (deceptive CoT, persistent personas) is untested.
- **Clinical realism is bounded.** Organisms are trained on multiple-choice medical QA with synthetic triggers (gpt-5.2-generated); real clinical deployment involves notes, dosing context, and multimodal data. The authors flag this, but "realistic failure mode" remains a curated approximation — and the counterfactual sets differ only in one trigger feature, which is far cleaner than demographic correlation in real EHR data.
- **Small whitebox tool set and fixed auditor budget.** Only three whitebox tools and a 15-turn/40k-token budget mirroring one prior work; conclusions about "whitebox adds little on age" may not transfer to other tools or budgets.

## Strengths

- The core reframing — an organism must remain a plausible *instance* of a capable model, not merely exhibit the behavior — is correct, timely, and directly actionable for anyone building interpretability testbeds.
- Exceptional empirical breadth: 80 retrained Pando organisms + 19 Lottery organisms + a new 163-organism clinical suite, with significance tests, correlation analyses, and ablations over recipe axes (objective × chat mixing × merging).
- The retrospective analysis converts a pre-registered hunch (Tan et al. 2026) into quantitative evidence (ΔR² = 0.25; ρ = +0.70/+0.61/−0.58), and the "validation standing predicts what tools surface" findings (e.g., honesty steering −0.21 on age) give interpretability researchers a concrete reason to care about organism quality.
- Practical, falsifiable recommendations (DPO > SFT; skip chat mixing; merge toward base) each backed by consistent evidence across suites, including a negative result against a previously recommended practice (Minder et al.'s chat-mixing fix).
- Strong openness and research ethics: full code/data/checkpoint release, clinical consultation, explicit ethics and AI-use statements.

## Limitations

- Correlation between validation metrics and auditability is not causation; the criteria are proxies for "realism" and are themselves Goodhart-able targets.
- Judge-based metrics (CoT naturalness, MT-Bench, investigation scores) share a judge model, so systematic judge biases could inflate several correlations at once.
- The merge-ratio analysis is only deeply characterized on one age-bias organism; behaviors installed with larger parameter shifts may not tolerate q = 0.8 merging.
- Clinical organisms live in MCQ-QA land with one-feature counterfactuals and synthetic trigger data; extrapolation to real clinical decision-making or toRL-trained/deceptive-CoT organisms is unverified.
- Base models are small (gemma-2-2b-it, Llama-3.1-8b-it); capability-preservation conclusions at frontier scale remain untested.
- Whitebox conclusions rest on only three tools and one fixed agent budget.

## Relevance

这篇论文直击可解释性/对齐研究的"测试基础设施"问题——用什么模型来测可解释性方法，结论才可信；其"DPO+向基座合并"的配方和三重验证指标可以直接复用于任何需要构造对照模型的工作，医疗偏差生物套件对临床 LLM 安全与审计研究同样有直接参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Can the validation-metric ↔ auditability link be established causally, e.g., by matching organisms on all but one validation criterion while holding target behavior fixed?
- Does near-free merging (q = 0.8 preserving behavior) generalize to behaviors that require larger parameter changes, such as persistent deceptive CoT or multi-step backdoors?
- Can judge-independent naturalness metrics (embedding- or probing-based) replace the LLM-judge stack and reduce single-judge circularity?
- Would the race > gender > age recovery ordering persist with human auditors, larger agent budgets, or stronger whitebox tools (e.g., cross-layer circuits rather than SAE feature lists)?
- Do these conclusions transfer to frontier-scale base models, where SFT-induced capability loss may behave differently than on gemma-2-2b-it / Llama-3.1-8b-it?
