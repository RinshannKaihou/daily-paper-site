Key metric

~400 magnitude

Spike-FFN output peak triggered by overwriting only channel 894 (the MAGC) in LLaMA-3.2-1B. Identical swaps of control channels 0 / 1742 stay ≈ 0; sweeping Pareto α 5→1 lifts the peak ~40→~400.

Why this matters

Attention sinks and massive activations underpin KV-cache, quantization and hallucination-mitigation work; both now reduce to one localizable, intervenable channel.

把两个被下游技术广泛利用的现象，归因到一个可定位、可干预的单通道旋钮，是「从现象到单一成因」的干净案例。

Critical edge

No downstream consequence is measured beyond sink share — nothing on perplexity, generation quality, or the quantization / KV-cache tasks that motivate attention-sink research; the practical payoff is asserted, not demonstrated.

— Critical Assessment, this paper's sharpest self-limitation

01Methodology

  1. Locate the spike FFN. The first FFN whose first-token output has an outlier channel: maxj |y₀(j)| > 100 and > 10× the median. Scope: dense decoder-only, pre-norm, SwiGLU y = Wₒₖₓₖₗ(SiLU(Wₒₗₐₜₑh) ⊙ Wₒₗₜₗh).
  2. Pin the MAGC. i* = argmaxi |h₀(i) − h₁(i)| between spike- and non-spike-token FFN inputs; the sign fixes the gating direction. (QwQ-32B: channels already massive in the residual stream are skipped.)
  3. Intervene causally. Five random 5-token sequences; inputs i.i.d. signed Pareto(α); swap only channel 894 (vs. controls 0/1742) with the input max, then sweep α = 5→1.
  4. Explain asymptotically. Theorem 4.1: as the MAGC value x→±∞, output → x²·Σ αₖωₖ — a quadratic mix of a few Wₒₖₓₖₗ columns that already carry the massive shape; concentration degree R(v) (negative entropy) + a residual-amplification bound (Prop. 4.6).
  5. Apply to attention sink. Overwrite only h₀(i*) with a value c across ten natural sentences; measure the first token's sink score s₀ in block 2.

02Results beyond the headline

The prior explanation fails on 5 of 6 models. Sun et al.'s account assumes SiLU is near-identity on spike tokens; the measured gate input→output cosine holds only for LLaMA-2-7B (Table A1) — Qwen and QwQ collapse to 0.23–0.29.

03Strengths

  • Crisp, falsifiable causal claim with proper negative controls — channels 0/1742 produce nothing under identical swaps.
  • Theory and measurement agree exactly where it matters: cos 0.9995; R(α) 9.009 vs log m 9.011.
  • Six models across four families, 1B–32B — not one favorite checkpoint; identification criteria stated well enough to reimplement.
  • Directly overturns the key assumption of the closest prior account (near-identity SiLU) instead of ignoring it.
  • Released code (github.com/MinjiaMao/magc), public checkpoints, honest limitations section.

04Limitations

  • Dense SwiGLU decoders only; MoE and other activations untested (acknowledged by the authors).
  • Single-channel frame struggles with diffuse cases: Mistral-3-3B's α ~ 1.5×10⁷, QwQ-32B's overlap with residual massive-activation channels; the identification criterion is partly circular there.
  • Inference-time mechanism only — why this channel becomes the MAGC during pretraining is open; no task-performance evidence.
  • Quantitative support thins outside LLaMA-3.2-1B: Assumption 4.4 verified on one model; ten-sentence sink study with no fitted threshold or CI.
  • Small experimental scale overall — five random 5-token sequences, no benchmark tasks; generalization across sequence length unverified.

05Questions for further exploration

  1. Why does a particular channel become the MAGC during pretraining — predictable from init statistics, optimizer dynamics, or data? Stable across seeds?
  2. Does suppressing the MAGC (c = −25 removes massive activations, sink → ~0.46) hurt perplexity or long-context reasoning — or enable "sink-free" KV-cache / quantization regimes?
  3. Can Assumption 4.4 (high α-concentration at the MAGC) be proven from weight statistics, and reported for all six models rather than only LLaMA-3.2-1B?
  1. Would a vector-valued, multi-channel gating account unify the diffuse cases (Mistral's ~10⁷-scale α, QwQ's overlap) with the clean single-channel theory?
  2. Does the mechanism extend to MoE layers and non-SwiGLU activations (GeGLU, ReLU FFNs), where the quadratic-form asymptotics would take a different shape?
