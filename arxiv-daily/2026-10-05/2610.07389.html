Methodology — distilled

Extended FOND: inference (variational u) and learning (generative θ) are both natural-gradient flows on one shared variational free energy F(x; u, θ), each preconditioned by its own Fisher metric.

  • BeFOND model: encoder-free iterative sparse coder — factorized Bernoulli prior/posterior, linear-Gaussian decoder; free energy, inference dynamics, and all parameter updates admit closed forms; matrix-free lateral term Φᶞ[Φr].
  • Explaining away: positively overlapping atoms suppress each other via the recurrent lateral term −Wr — the mechanism amortized encoders lack.
  • Evaluation: SynthSAEBench (ground-truth dictionaries, superposition sweep ρmm 0.10–0.25, lateral ablations) + whitened Gemma-2B layer-12 activations (32k–524k latents, 50M-token budget; SAEBench probing, RAVEL).
Theory with teeth

Prop 4.1: for non-orthogonal dictionaries, no fixed projection + scalar nonlinearity computes the exact posterior — ruling out ReLU, JumpReLU (incl. Gemma Scope), and BatchTopK SAEs at once.  Thm 4.2: Euclidean decoder error converges at rate pᵢ per direction (Zipf ⇒ ≥Kα slowdown for rare features); matched Fisher preconditioning equalizes all rates, frozen Adam leaves condition number ≥ Kα/4.

50M-token budget; T = 10 inference steps; 95% CIs over 3–5 seeds.

  • Width scaling: 16k→524k gives BeFOND +4.1 pts RAVEL and +1.8 pts probing; BatchTopK / Matryoshka gain only ~0.6 pts — baselines plateau, BeFOND keeps climbing.
  • Sample efficiency: at 524k, matches released Gemma Scope probing scores after ~6M vs 8B tokens; at 30M tokens it exceeds them (0.789 probing, 0.827 RAVEL).
  • Recurrence alone is not enough: iterative inference + Adam learning (mean-field) reaches only 0.685 RAVEL — Fisher-preconditioned learning is a necessary ingredient.
  • Explaining away survives superposition: highest MCC at every point of the ρmm 0.10→0.25 sweep with zero dead latents; removing lateral interactions hurts as overlap grows; excitatory-only variant activates nearly the whole dictionary.

⚠ cost: ~12–14× BatchTopK wall-clock per token (8×H200, T=10 inner solves, FP64 intermediates)

Strengths

  • One principle, two flows: closed-form inference and learning on a single free energy — no encoder training, no backprop through inference.
  • Theory with teeth: Prop 4.1 rules out the entire single-projection amortized family on overlapping dictionaries; Thm 4.2 is an exact, testable rarity result separating natural gradient from Adam.
  • Consistent story in two regimes: ground-truth synthetic ablations + real Gemma-2B activations on established benchmarks (SAEBench, RAVEL).
  • Rare-feature learning (F1 0.87 vs 0.82; JumpReLU 0.19) hits SAE scaling’s known pain point head-on.
  • Fully reproducible: code, data, checkpoints; 3–5 seeds, 95% CIs, unusually careful wall-clock accounting.

Limitations

  • Compute cost: ~12–14× slower wall-clock than BatchTopK on H200s; per-feature-quality-per-FLOP unreported and likely unfavorable.
  • Narrow evaluation: single model / layer / corpus (Gemma-2B layer-12, Pile); no cross-model replication and no downstream unlearning / steering / circuit evidence.
  • Conflated headline: the >1,000× sample-efficiency claim compares differently-preprocessed external checkpoints; the clean like-for-like delta is ~+1.5 pts probing.
  • Factorized posterior cannot represent latent dependencies; interactions live only in inference dynamics.
  • Fisher-learning benefit on real activations is inferred from the mean-field comparison and appendix ablations, not isolated end-to-end.

Questions for further exploration

  • Can momentum or learned step sizes close the 12–14× wall-clock gap while preserving explaining-away dynamics?
  • Do the width-scaling and sample-efficiency gains transfer to Llama / Pythia, other layers, and non-Pile data?
  • Could an encoder distill BeFOND’s converged r as a target — and how much recovery advantage survives?
  • Does the feature quality hold on downstream unlearning, steering, and circuit analysis, beyond probing and RAVEL?
  • How much rarity compensation persists when posterior and prior moments co-adapt (Thm 4.2 assumes frozen)?
  • Does a pairwise-interaction posterior recover the dependencies the factorized Bernoulli family provably misses?
