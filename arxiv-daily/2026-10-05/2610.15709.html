Why this matters

State quantization can’t reuse weight or KV-cache tricks — errors compound across tokens. This training-free fix makes 4-bit recurrent states practical at long context.

Critical edge

Baselines omit the closest prior work — Quamba2, MambaQuant, DAMP — so superiority over existing recurrent-state quantizers is argued, not shown.

Methodology — diagnose, then intervene before and at rounding

update k·vᵤ (rank 1)→ |d| ≈ 0.02–0.21× grid Δ→ rounded away→ Eₜ compounds ×86 @ 4K
  • Shared recurrence. All four 1.3B models (GDN, GLA, Mamba2, RetNet) follow Sₜ = Aₜ·Sₜ₋₁ + Bₜ with rank-1 updates; the state is re-grouped and re-quantized to 4 bits at every token.
  • Coherent-error diagnosis. Cross-step error inner products are almost always positive and decay keeps them alive for thousands of steps, so off-diagonal terms grow quadratically. Root cause: 68–96.4% of update elements round to themselves every step, and errors there carry the full negated update — staying correlated across steps.
  • LRB — before rounding. Small updates accumulate in a rank-k FP16 bypass exploiting the outer-product structure (queries read the factors directly); every m steps a randomized-SVD Triton merge folds them in, still-trapped elements stay. Storage (k+m)(dₜ+dᵤ); k=1 suffices.
  • BDR — at rounding. Rounding can write an update backwards (reversal). BDR moves reversed elements one grid step toward their update when |d| > εₖ·Δₜ; a lemma bounds the overshoot. Zero extra storage.
  • Protocol. Fully post-training, no calibration, 5.25–5.64 bits/elem; evaluation decodes token-by-token with the state kept low precision throughout.

Strengths

  • A quantitative diagnosis (coherent gain, lost-update fraction) validated by targeted interventions — not just correlation.
  • Training-free, calibration-free, composable; BDR costs zero storage, LRB’s bypass stays tiny (rank 1 suffices).
  • Unusual breadth: 4 architectures × 2 benchmarks (1K–8K) × commonsense × recall tasks × full ablations, with a decoding-faithful protocol.
  • Real systems work: on-GPU memory/throughput measurement and honest bits accounting that includes bypass overhead.

Limitations

  • No head-to-head with Quamba2 / MambaQuant / DAMP — superiority argued, not measured.
  • 1.3B scale only; two of four checkpoints are self-pretrained on 100B FineWeb-Edu tokens.
  • Only the recurrent state is quantized — end-to-end savings are bounded by the state’s share of memory traffic.
  • Four hand-set hyperparameters (k, m, εₖ, εₕ) selected via 8K validation sweeps — a light calibration the “training-free” framing glosses over.
  • Residual gaps persist (RetNet PG-19 8K: 73.96 vs 60.02 FP32); efficiency shown on one consumer GPU.

Questions for further exploration

  1. Does LRB+BDR beat — or compose additively with — calibrated Quamba2 / MambaQuant / DAMP at matched bit budgets?
  2. Does coherent-error accumulation carry to hybrid Mamba–softmax architectures, where part of the state is exact attention?
  3. Can rank-1 sufficiency be proven from the spectrum of accumulated outer products, or is it luck of these four architectures?
  4. Do the Triton merge kernels hold up on H100-class hardware and much larger batches, where bandwidth and merge compute scale differently?
