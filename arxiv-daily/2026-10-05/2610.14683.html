Triage-PiEvo: Evidence Admission for Principle-Evolving Scientific Agents

Key metric · unreliable-evidence admission

1.0→0.0

Admission rate for injected unreliable evidence in constructed corruption stress tests (ungated variants: 1.0) — with zero planted mechanisms missed, recall 1.0.

Why this matters

Self-evolving agents get silently corrupted by bad feedback; this architecture-level gate — dual-history isolation plus retractable principles — is directly reusable today.

Critical edge

The headline bundles two upgrades: in the budget-8 ablation, gate-only Triage scores 60.60 SQ — below PiEvo’s 64.78 — so the AFE search policy shares the credit.

Methodology

Wraps PiEvo’s principle–hypothesis–testing cycle (one GP expert per principle) in a loop with a single write path to persistent state: the admission gate.

Detect→ Quarantine→ C1→ C2→ C3→ Accept · Retract
  1. 1
    Detect under source ambiguity.

    The GP residual st = |y−μ|/σ flags anomalies (τanom = 0.85) but is provably blind to their cause — noise, fault, evaluator corruption, OOD, drift, or a genuine mechanism. Thresholds triage, never attribute; with false revision ≫ false retention, “retain until confirmed” is risk-optimal.

  2. 2
    Escalate C1–C3.

    C1 same-channel repeat (τrep = 0.12) rules out transients → unlocks search use. C2 independent channel (τind = 0.12) rules out channel corruption → unlocks provisional revision. C3 held-out probes (gain ≥ τgen = 0.16 on k = 2 records) → the only path to accepted.

  3. 3
    Outcome-blind search.

    Active Flow Expansion proposes candidates scored on GP mean / uncertainty / novelty / descriptor distance, refit only on the admitted primary history — unverified evidence structurally cannot enter the search context.

  4. 4
    Reversible lifecycle.

    PROPOSED → PROVISIONAL → ACCEPTED; a single contradicting admissible record forces RETRACTED and propagates through child pointers.

τanom 0.85 τrep 0.12 τind 0.12 τgen 0.16 k 2 all five fixed a priori across tasks & backbones

Results

Beyond the headline metric: quality, coverage, cost, adversarial stress, and plug-in transfer.

  • Coverage & speed (Qwen3-235B): APD 57.23 vs 50.52, AUOC 71.73 vs 62.78 — 1.16–1.22× faster accumulation of high-quality candidates than PiEvo.
  • Cost frontier: the gate adds only +13.6% wall-clock (134.5 s vs 118.4 s), beating harnesses costing 1.28–1.85× more (AI Scientist v2 172.4 s, AI Researcher 188.2 s, PiFlow 248.6 s).
  • Stress tests: ungated variants admit unreliable evidence at rate 1.0; Triage-PiEvo admits 0.0 — including exploits that reproduce perfectly. After a mechanism reversal at t=17 it retracts, re-adds descendants at t=18, and recovers the new optimum by t=23, where PiEvo stagnates.
  • Plug-in transfer: attached unchanged to three external harnesses (ResearchHarness, AI-Researcher, Claude Code) — false admissions cut >60%, externally verified scores up 31–43%, at extra token/runtime cost.
  • Ablations: removing the gate costs −4.9% SQ; gate alone (Triage-only) falls below PiEvo — see Critical edge. Dropping the held-out criterion raises false revision 4.8% → 34.5%; organic recall is 92.4% (vs 100% in constructed tests).

Strengths

What holds up under scrutiny.

  • Crisp formalization: the source-blind anomaly score (Def. 3.1) plus the asymmetric-loss derivation justify the whole architecture from one principle.
  • An architectural fix, not a prompt-level one — unverified evidence is structurally unable to reach the search policy.
  • Unusually broad evaluation: quality, coverage, speed, cost, adversarial stress tests, organic benchmarks, reversal dynamics, and three-harness transfer.
  • All five gate hyperparameters fixed a priori; the sensitivity phase-map shows the operating point tolerates >~80% hold-out signal attenuation.
  • Honest ablations that expose the system’s own dependencies (Triage-only < PiEvo; 92.4% organic recall).

Limitations

Where the claims stop.

  • Only four GP-modelable, optimization-style discovery tasks (molecules/materials); hypothesis-driven domains (code, text, biology protocols) are untested.
  • SQ’s non-attainable references push scores above 100 and skew cross-task averages; no raw-objective table in the main text.
  • Channel independence is assumed, not verified — correlated evaluator failures are out of scope.
  • Organic recall 92.4% and false revision 4.8% are non-zero; the theory-to-organic gap is explained but not closed.
  • Small regime: 24 evaluations, 3 seeds, “No Thinking” backbone modes; external-harness token cost is unquantified.

Questions for Further Exploration

Open threads left by the paper.

  • Can per-channel Beta trust be extended to detect correlated channel failures (e.g., two surrogates distilled from the same teacher)?
  • Can τgen adapt online for sub-threshold mechanisms without reopening the false-revision floodgate?
  • What replaces the GP residual st in hypothesis-driven loops (code generation, protocol design) where it is a poor anomaly score?
  • Can εFR ≫ εFM be calibrated per domain, and how sensitive is the retain-bias over long horizons?
  • Would an ungated PiEvo+AFE baseline close most of the headline gap at budget 24 — isolating the gate’s true contribution?
