03Strengths

  • Provably antagonistic candidates: choosing the relational match requires inferring the abstract pattern — binary, causally interpretable trials at chance = 50%.
  • A complete causal chain, not correlation alone: RSA → patching → ablation → CoT bypass → ARC-AGI-1 transfer.
  • Confound control rarely seen in benchmarks: nonce cycling, both orderings averaged, shape-coherence ablation (±2 pp), alternative RSA target (r = 0.91–1.00 across 14 checkpoints).
  • Broad, reproducible coverage (7 API + 14 open checkpoints) and honest negative results — scale dips, CoT hurting thin models.

04Limitations

  • Synthetic, narrow stimuli: relations only over color/size patterns on geometric objects — spatial, causal, semantic relations and natural scenes untouched.
  • Full per-head CMA exists for only two Gemma-4 checkpoints (five more on a 30-trial budget); the CoT bypass route is left mechanistically unexplained.
  • ARC-AGI-1 transfer is narrow: 66/80-task subsets, a non-standard cell-wise metric, and a model whose clean exact-match is only ≈0.23.
  • “Circuit” is the authors’ own shorthand — RSA is correlational, and the causal case rests on a handful of heads; weak models near 28–50% are hard to interpret at chance = 50%.

05Questions for further exploration

  1. What language-mediated route lets CoT restore 100% after the relational heads are zeroed — re-implemented comparison, or bypass of vision features entirely?
  2. Do the relational heads emerge during pretraining or visual-instruction tuning — and does the object→relational crossover track the human developmental timeline?
  3. Do the same heads carry spatial, causal, or naturalistic relations — or does the circuit fragment across relation types?
  4. Why does CoT hurt thin models (Qwen3.5-8B −4.0 pp) — reasoning noise, parse failure, or attribute-direction asymmetry?
  5. Can the relational circuit be a lever (steering along L28 H12, relational bottlenecks) that improves ARC exact-match — not just a diagnostic?
