Same Text, Different Prediction: Serving-Context Nondeterminism in Text Classifiers

Santhosh Kumar Kasa · Siva Rajesh Kasa · Sumit Negi — Amazon  ·  2026-10-05

Key metric

~10%

of predicted labels flip when only the padded width changes — the largest single serving factor. All flips sit in the two GPT-2 families, about 1 in 5 of their comparisons.

Why this matters

Invisible deployment choices — padding, batch shape, precision, hardware — can silently change a served classifier’s answers. Monitor probability scores, not just labels.

Critical edge

Headline magnitudes come from small from-scratch GPT-2-style models; the paper’s own pretrained Qwen3 and ModernBERT replications flip zero labels from padding.

一句话总结

来自 Amazon 的作者系统训练了 180 个分类器（判别式 BERT、伪生成式与全生成式 GPT-2 三种范式 × 3 规模 × 5 数据集 × 3 种子），首次系统测量“服务上下文”（batch 大小/组成、padding 宽度、精度、量化、硬件、后端）对文本分类器的影响：仅改变 padding 宽度就翻转约 10% 的预测标签——且全部集中在两个 GPT-2 家族（根因是学习型绝对位置编码随左 padding 位移），而 fp32 下 batch 形状翻转 0 个标签、bf16 下却移动高达 56.7 个百分点的概率质量；GPU→量化 CPU 整机迁移最多让全生成式分类器 39% 的标签翻转，并给出逐机制的缓解手段与形式化理论。

Methodology

  1. 180 classifiersfrom scratch: discriminative BERT, pseudo-generative, fully generative GPT-2 · 3 sizes × 5 datasets × 3 seeds → 158 kept (≥15% margin over chance)
  2. 3,960 serving contextsexactly one recorded setting changed per comparison vs a fixed reference (fp32 GPU, static padding, single-example)
  3. 3 readoutslabel flips vs reference · total variation between probability vectors · max logit change (all targets + label-stable subset)
  4. 2 interventionsreal-token position assignment on/off isolates the padding mechanism; Qwen3 (rotary) & ModernBERT (NoPE) triangulate
  • Bitwise control: repeating an identical context reproduces logits bitwise on every hardware/software config — effects come from the settings, not run-to-run noise.
  • Hygiene: comparisons with missing reference runs are discarded, never substituted; padded width held fixed when comparing peer-sharing interventions.
  • Size analysis: 40 matched dataset/family/seed combos — explicitly descriptive association, not a scaling law (width, depth, training quality confounded).

Labels ≠ scores. A label-only monitor would rank the two combined effects backward — serving load moves raw scores several times more than GPU→CPU migration while flipping the same fraction of labels. The margin certificate predicts exactly this.

Mechanism-matched mitigations (costs measured in-setup, not production)

→ padding flips

Real-token position rule

Restores width invariance on GPU & CPU; must be adopted at training too. Unlocks dynamic padding: 2–2.5× throughput.

→ batch-shape drift

Batch-invariant matmul kernels

Near-zero accuracy cost; 1.3–1.5× standard latency at large batch. Exists for tested stacks, not guaranteed across backends.

→ quantization drift

Fixed offline 8-bit scale

A deployment-level choice that removes per-batch scale recompute drift on the integer path.

→ residual flips

Margin-based recompute

Recompute a reference context for low-margin predictions: detects nearly all of 4,644 held-out flips at 22% recompute.

Formal results

Theorem 5.1

Width invariance is decided by the position rule — with a converse for learned absolute position embeddings.

Proposition 5.1

Margin certificate: if m(x) > 2·TV(x, c, c₀), the predicted label cannot flip. One-sided — silent when violated.

Theorem 5.2

Joint infeasibility: when Bayes error exceeds the accuracy target, no implementation meets speed, accuracy and invariance at once.

Strengths

  • Impeccable experimental hygiene: one recorded setting at a time, bitwise-repetition control, missing runs discarded, manifest-traceable outputs — how nondeterminism should be measured.
  • Causality demonstrated, not correlated: real-token position reassignment removes the effect; re-forcing width-dependent positions restores it; rotary/NoPE models triangulate.
  • Actionable guidance — monitor probabilities, not labels — backed by a formal certificate, and the theory genuinely matches the empirical taxonomy.

Limitations

  • Scope: 180 small from-scratch models on five sentiment/topic datasets; fine-tuned pretrained encoders, larger models and production engines unmeasured — magnitudes may shrink substantially.
  • Mitigations are setup-measured, not production-validated; the position rule must be adopted at training time, so no existing checkpoint can retroactively gain it.
  • Size analysis confounds width, depth and training quality — label-change rate is not monotone in model size; capacity guidance is limited.
  • One-sided certificate: the recompute mitigation’s residual miss rate under reduced precision is deferred to appendices rather than quantified.

Questions for further exploration

  1. Do the ~10% padding flips and the 39% worst-checkpoint effect shrink to practical irrelevance for fine-tuned pretrained encoders, given the zero-flip Qwen3/ModernBERT replications?
  2. How do the structural effects interact with chunked/paged attention and continuous batching (vLLM-style) position bookkeeping?
  3. Can the margin certificate become a cheap online monitor, and what false-negative rate remains under 8-bit dynamic quantization?
  4. Does Theorem 5.2’s infeasibility also hold for fixed positional representations (rotary/NoPE), or is there a jointly achievable regime there?
