Key metric
+2.1 pts

44.4±0.4% vs 42.3±0.5% for the strongest baseline M2PO — Qwen3-Base-1.7B, average over 5 math benchmarks at staleness k=32.

also +2.3 over GEPO on Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct (18.5 vs 16.2)

Why this matters

Hits the core pain of asynchronous LLM RL — heavy-tailed weights, ESS collapse — and the knob drops onto existing GRPO-style stacks at near-zero cost. 可直接叠加到 GRPO/GSPO 类训练栈，开销仅 24 次二分 + O(G) 归一化。

⚠ Critical edge

The “exact geometric-bridge identity” (Thm 1) holds for ratios the experiments never compute — truncated decoding forces a pre-truncation proxy ρ̃ — and there is no κ ablation and no code release.

Methodology

Stale sampler μrollouts lag ≤32 steps behind learner → sequence ratio ρ = product of ~10³ per-token ratios
→
Heavy tail, ESS collapsefew trajectories dominate; ESS ≪ batch size B=512
→
Per-group bisectionα* = max{α: ESS(ρα) ≥ κG}, κ=0.85, 24 iterations, no α floor needed
→
Clipped PPO updatealways-positive weights w̄ = ρα*; α*=1 → self-normalized IS, α*→0 → GRPO-style uniform
  • Smooth, not masked: every weight stays strictly positive (Prop 2) — unlike M2PO’s hard mask, which can zero a whole trust region under mixed staleness.
  • Bridge grounding: ρα/Zα is the exact importance ratio of the geometric bridge πα ∝ πθαμ(1−α) (Thm 1); α=1 recovers πθ, α=0 recovers μ.
  • Exponents on detached ratios; straight-through surrogate gives forward w̄ with gradient w̄·∇ℓ; final loss is a clipped surrogate (ε=0.2) with group-relative advantages.
  • Cost: O(24·MG) bisection + O(G) normalization on already-available log-ratios — negligible next to per-token forward/backward.
Thm 1Tempered weights = exact bridge-policy importance ratio
Thm 2ESS(α) continuous, non-increasing ⇒ unique α*, bisection finds it
Thm 3Deterministic dispersion bound Σ(w̄−1)²/G ≤ 1/κ−1, independent of policy drift
Thm 4α* is the least-tempered exponent minimizing the χ² bridge-target bias

  • No collapse: at k=32 on MATH-500, GEPO collapses near steps ~200 and ~450, M2PO peaks then declines — TETPO rises smoothly to the highest reward.
  • Largest Llama gaps: AMC24 +4.7 over GEPO (14.9 vs 10.2); AIME25 1.6% where GRPO/GEPO score 0.0%.
  • Only method in the comparison that is sequence-granular, smooth (no mask/truncation), and carries a drift-free dispersion bound.

Strengths

  • One clean knob: a single scalar α per group smoothly spans full IS (α=1) to GRPO-style uniform weights (α=0), replacing the brittle mask-or-nothing choice.
  • Real theory: the dispersion bound Σ(w̄−1)²/G ≤ 1/κ−1 is deterministic on the realized batch and independent of policy drift.
  • Always-positive weights directly address M2PO’s documented mixed-staleness mask collapse; no-collapse claim visible in training curves.
  • Essentially free at runtime (24 bisection iterations on existing log-ratios) — trivial adoption on GRPO-style pipelines.
  • Strong, current baselines (M2PO, GEPO both ICLR 2026), shared hyperparameters, decontaminated evals, avg@16 × 3 seeds; unusually candid Appendix B.

Limitations

  • Theory–practice gap: the exact bridge identity and discrepancy bound don’t hold for the truncated-decoding proxy ρ̃ actually logged; only Thms 2–3 transfer — the abstract oversells.
  • No κ sensitivity ablation: everything tuned to κ=0.85; unclear how much performance depends on this single choice.
  • Narrow scope: two small models, math only, one staleness regime (k=32); nothing at low or extreme staleness; no code release.
  • Sequence-level only — token-level credit assignment (M2PO’s target) is future work; bias of the data-adaptive α* selection uncharacterized.
  • Near-floor cells: Llama AIME25 scores span 0.0–1.6%; the +2.3 gain is carried by AMC/MATH-500. Venue is non-archival OPT2026.

Questions for further exploration

  1. How sensitive is TETPO to κ? Is there a principled schedule (e.g. annealing from 1 downward) instead of a fixed 0.85?
  2. Can exact support-restricted ratios under top-k/top-p truncation be logged cheaply at rollout time, so Thms 1 and 4 apply verbatim?
  3. How does it behave at low staleness (k≈4–8) and extreme staleness (k≥64) — does α* gracefully reduce to vanilla GSPO/SNIS on fresh samples?
  4. What is the bias of the data-adaptively selected α* estimator — can a double-robust correction recover unbiasedness while keeping the dispersion bound?
  5. Can tempering compose with per-token mechanisms (à la M2PO) for token-level credit assignment plus groupwise ESS control, and at what variance cost?
  6. Does it transfer beyond verifiable math rewards — e.g. RLHF with learned reward models where R is not θ-independent?
