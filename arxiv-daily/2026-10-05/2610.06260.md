> **一句话总结：** CentriQ 揭示了 DiT 激活中 adaLN 引入的"每 token 均值（DC 分量）"无法被数据无关 Hadamard 旋转均匀打散（三种 DiT 宽度 1152/3072/2240 均不满足正则 Hadamard 要求的 n=4u²），提出旋转前逐 token 去均值、再经全精度秩一项精确回注的免校准 W4A4 量化方案，配合 ℓ_p(p=0.7) 分组权重量化器 RoDS：在 PixArt-Σ/FLUX.1-schnell/SANA-1.6B 上免校准即可追平需校准的 SVDQuant（如 SANA-1.6B MJHQ FID 16.2 vs 19.3），并以 W2A4 的 GenEval 0.647 首次在 2-bit 激活下保持可用画质。

> **TL;DR:** CentriQ is a calibration-free W4A4 post-training quantizer for diffusion transformers built on one structural finding: adaLN conditioning injects a per-token mean (DC component) into activations that data-free Hadamard rotations cannot spread uniformly (no evaluated DiT width admits a regular Hadamard matrix). CentriQ subtracts the per-token mean before rotation, restores it exactly through a full-precision rank-1 branch fused into the GEMM epilogue, and fits grouped INT4 weights with a robust ℓ_p objective (RoDS). Without any calibration data it matches calibrated SVDQuant on PixArt-Σ, FLUX.1-schnell and SANA-1.6B (e.g., SANA-1.6B MJHQ FID 16.2 vs SVDQuant 19.3 and BF16 16.0), while RTN/NF4/HQQ collapse (FID 33–481), and it is the first calibration-free method to hold usable quality at 2-bit activations (W2A4 GenEval 0.647 vs OrbitQuant 0.604), running FLUX.1-schnell at 1.69 s/image vs 1.77 s BF16 on an A100.

## 核心贡献

- **诊断出免校准量化在 DiT 上失效的结构性根因：** adaLN 调制输出 x_t = (1+γ)⊙x̄_i + β 携带随时间步和 prompt 变化的每 token 均值（DC 分量，f_DC 可达 10⁻² 量级），无法折叠进 bias；而能将其均匀打散的"正则 Hadamard 矩阵"（行和相等）仅存在于阶数 n = 4u²，PixArt-Σ（1152）、FLUX.1（3072）、SANA-1.6B（2240）的宽度均不满足——旋转反而把均值能量集中到少数坐标、撑大量化范围。
- **精确均值居中 + 秩一重注入：** 旋转前逐 token 减去均值 x̄_t·1（O(n) 开销），由于层是线性的，被移除的分量通过秩一项 x̄_t·w₁（w₁=W·1，FP32 一次预计算）在 GEMM epilogue 中精确还原。由此建立"偏移不变性"：量化误差与均值彻底解耦，旋转矩阵可只按混合性与宽度自由选取（分解 Hadamard H_m⊗H_{2^k} + 零填充）。
- **免数据的闭式激活标定 + 稳健权重量化器 RoDS：** 激活 scale 由居中后 token 的 p-分位数闭式给出（p=0.9995, γ=2.56，全模型统一）；权重以 ℓ_p（p=0.7）目标按 64 列分组拟成非对称 INT4，经 IRLS + 半二次分裂交替求解（继承 HQQ 初始化），偏好组的稠密模式而非被大离群值撑大的 min-max scale。
- **免校准即可追平校准方法并首次下探 2-bit 激活：** W4A4 下在三大家族上匹配校准的 SVDQuant，且是首个在 W2A4 保持可用画质的免校准方法；FLUX.1-schnell 端到端 1.69s/图（BF16 为 1.77s），rank-1 项与 bias 融入 epilogue 后零额外时延。

## Key Contributions

- Identifies the structural cause of data-free quantization failure on DiTs: adaLN places a per-token mean (DC component) in activations that cannot be folded into a bias, and a Hadamard rotation concentrates it onto few coordinates because a mean-spreading "regular" Hadamard matrix exists only at orders n = 4u² — none of PixArt-Σ (1152), FLUX.1 (3072), SANA-1.6B (2240) qualifies (Lemma 1).
- Proposes exact mean centering with rank-1 re-injection: subtract x̄_t·1 before rotation, restore it through x̄_t·w₁ (w₁ = W·1) fused into the GEMM epilogue at compute precision. This yields an offset-invariance property — quantization error no longer contains terms depending on the rotation's row sums — so any factorized Hadamard (Paley ⊗ Sylvester, with zero-padding) can be chosen for mixing alone.
- Derives a closed-form, data-free per-token activation scale (empirical p-quantile with p = 0.9995, γ = 2.56, fixed across all models and layers) and a robust ℓ_p (p = 0.7) grouped INT4 weight quantizer (RoDS) solved by nested IRLS + half-quadratic alternation, which tracks the dense mode of each group instead of letting heavy-tailed DiT weight outliers inflate min-max scales.
- Empirically: calibration-free CentriQ matches calibrated SVDQuant at W4A4 on three DiT families, is the only calibration-free quantizer producing usable images on all three (RTN/NF4/HQQ collapse), surpasses the strongest prior calibration-free method at W2A4, and adds no net latency over BF16 inference (1.69 s vs 1.77 s per FLUX.1-schnell image on A100).

## Methodology

**Problem setup.** A DiT linear layer y_t = W x_t + b is quantized to W4A4. Activations use symmetric per-token INT4 with one scale; weights use asymmetric INT4 over groups of 64 consecutive input columns with 16-bit scales/zero-points (~4.5 bits/weight). Only the hidden widths differ across models; conditioning all goes through adaLN.

**Why rotation alone fails.** The adaLN output x_t = (1+γ)⊙x̄_i + β has per-token mean β̄_t = β̄ + (1/n)·1^Tγ·x̄_i that varies per token with timestep and prompt, so it cannot be folded into a bias (f_DC is nonzero on every modulated input). A Hadamard rotation maps this DC energy: with a factorized H = (H_m ⊗ H_{2^k})/√n', at least a fraction n/n′ of the DC energy n·x̄_t² falls on m coordinates, one of magnitude |x̄_t|·n/√(n·n′) at least — one spike sets the per-token range for all n′ coordinates. Uniform spreading requires equal row sums (regular Hadamard), which exists only at n = 4u² (Lemma 1), excluding all three evaluated widths.

**Activation path (online).** Per token: (1) center — x′_t = x_t − x̄_t·1 (O(n), mean computed alongside the per-token range); (2) zero-pad to the admissible width n′ = m·2^k and rotate by the fixed factorized Hadamard as two small transforms, O(n′(m+2^k)) ops (m ∈ {1, 12, 36, 44} Paley factors); (3) quantize with a closed-form per-token scale from the empirical p-quantile (p = 0.9995) times γ = 2.56, minimizing combined clipping+rounding error for Gaussian inputs; (4) GEMM in INT4, with the mean and the rank-1 term x̄_t·w₁ plus bias fused into the epilogue (Nunchaku extension). Offset invariance, y(x_t + c·1) = y(x_t) + c·w₁, guarantees the quantized path is independent of the mean.

**Weight path (offline).** The rotated weight W_R = W P^T H^T is quantized by RoDS: per group of 64 columns, minimize Σ_g ‖W_g − diag(s)[(Q_g − z_g)⊙σ_g]‖_p^p with p = 0.7. Under ℓ_p with p < 1 the marginal cost of a residual decays as |r|^(p−1), so a few huge entries no longer hijack the group scale; RoDS tracks the dense mode. Solved by nested alternation — outer loop refits per-row scales via IRLS (≤3 rounds), inner loop refits codes and zero-points via half-quadratic splitting (≤5–20 iterations), initialized from HQQ min-max.

**Evaluation protocol.** PixArt-Σ (20-step DPM-Solver, cfg 4.5), SANA-1.6B (20-step Flow-DPM-Solver, cfg 4.5), FLUX.1-schnell (4-step Euler, cfg 0), all at 1024². MJHQ-5k and sDCI (5,000 prompts each, shared seeds): FID/ImageReward for quality, PSNR/LPIPS against the 16-bit output. GenEval (553 prompts × 4 images) on FLUX.1-schnell. Design choices fixed on a disjoint 1,000-prompt MJHQ dev split. Baselines: RTN, NF4, HQQ re-run in an identical harness; SVDQuant W4A4 rows taken from the published paper (same protocol), with a Nunchaku re-run for GenEval.

## Results & Highlights

- **W4A4 matches calibrated SVDQuant across three families.** On SANA-1.6B (MJHQ-5k), CentriQ reaches FID 16.2 vs SVDQuant 19.3 and BF16 16.0 (ImageReward 1.072 vs 0.935; LPIPS 0.172 vs 0.220); on sDCI the gap is larger: 22.3 vs 28.1 (BF16 22.4). On PixArt-Σ (MJHQ) CentriQ FID 19.1 vs SVDQuant 19.2 (BF16 16.7); on FLUX.1-schnell 18.8 vs 18.3 (BF16 19.45) — both quantizers land below their 16-bit reference, which the authors themselves attribute to FID noise at 5k samples.
- **Calibration-free baselines collapse.** On PixArt-Σ W4A4 (MJHQ), RTN/NF4/HQQ give FID 481.5/479.6/462.6 vs CentriQ 19.1; on FLUX.1-schnell, 43.9/41.3/40.9 vs 18.8; on SANA-1.6B sDCI, RTN hits 43.4 vs CentriQ 22.3. CentriQ is the only calibration-free method producing usable images on all three models.
- **Ablation isolates the components (FLUX.1-schnell, MJHQ).** From no-transform FID 40.62 at W4A4, adding a QuaRot-style data-free rotation alone gives 25.65; adding exact mean centering gives 18.46; adding percentile clipping (full CentriQ) gives 18.80 (BF16 19.45). At W2A4, rotation alone gives 35.84 vs no-transform 363.14, centering improves it to 21.81. Notably, rotation alone *hurts* at W4A2 (437.41 → 455.30); percentile clipping is decisive only there (444.21 → 35.59) while changing W4A4 FID by +0.34.
- **First usable 2-bit activations among calibration-free methods.** GenEval overall on FLUX.1-schnell: CentriQ W2A4 0.647 vs OrbitQuant 0.604, while QuaRot/SmoothQuant/ViTQ-DiT score ≈0.001 (total collapse); CentriQ W4A2 scores 0.573, still above OrbitQuant's W2A4. At W4A4 CentriQ's 0.670 matches BF16's 0.667 and is close to the SVDQuant reproduction 0.682, though concurrent OrbitQuant reaches 0.703.
- **Weight quantizer and latency.** With the activation path fixed, RoDS lowers PixArt-Σ W4A4 FID from 21.04 (HQQ) to 19.13; at W3A16 weight-only, RoDS reaches 29.04 vs HQQ 34.36 and RTN 145.07 (PixArt-Σ). End-to-end, the fused W4A4 FLUX.1-schnell pipeline runs at 1.69 s/image vs 1.77 s BF16 on an A100-80GB (4 steps, CUDA-graphed), so the rank-1 re-injection costs nothing net.

## Authors & Credibility

Nataša Jovanović (Tensorrent / EPFL), Mathieu Salzmann (EPFL, a long-established researcher in computer vision and machine learning), and Saqib Javed (EPFL, previously first author of Q-Drift on diffusion quantization). The mix of an academic group with an inference-kernel company (Tensorrent) shows: the method is validated with a real fused kernel extending Nunchaku on A100 tensor cores, not just simulated quantization at W4A4. The paper is careful about protocol hygiene — design choices made on a disjoint 1,000-prompt dev split, the evaluation split scored once per final configuration, honest flagging of FID noise, and an explicit note that SVDQuant Table-1 rows are quoted from the original paper. ## Critical Assessment

- **The headline comparison is partly against quoted numbers.** The SVDQuant rows in Table 1 are taken from the SVDQuant paper (same MJHQ-5k/sDCI protocol, per App. A), not re-run under identical seeds/prompt code in the authors' harness; only the GenEval SVDQuant (0.682) is the authors' own Nunchaku reproduction. Given that the claimed "matches SVDQuant" margin on PixArt-Σ is 0.1 FID (19.1 vs 19.2), cross-harness comparability is a real caveat; on FLUX.1-schnell CentriQ actually trails SVDQuant on FID (18.8 vs 18.3) and ImageReward (0.939 vs 0.951).
- **PSNR is selectively downplayed.** SVDQuant attains higher PSNR on PixArt-Σ (17.6 vs 16.15) and SANA sDCI (16.2 vs 15.84); the paper reframes this as CentriQ "preferring perceptual structure over pixel-wise intensity" (better LPIPS). That is a fair reading of LPIPS/IR, but the fidelity story is metric-dependent rather than uniformly dominant.
- **The ablation is cumulative, not factorial.** Table 2 adds components one at a time (transform → centering → clip), so there is no "centering without rotation" row and no "rotation + clip without centering" row; the individual contribution of percentile clipping outside the W4A2 regime (+0.34 FID regression at W4A4) and the interaction between centering and rotation are not fully isolated.
- **The closest prior mean-removal method is not compared.** HadaNorm (ICML 2025 workshop) also removes means before a Hadamard transform on PixArt-Σ, but has no public implementation, so no numerical comparison exists; the W4A4 comparison against the mean-removal lineage rests on argument rather than measurement.
- **Sub-4-bit results are simulated.** W4A2 and W2A4 carry no latency claim (no fused kernel for 2-bit activations), so the practical value of the headline W2A4 result (GenEval 0.647) is unproven at the systems level, and microscaling FP4 formats (NVFP4/MXFP4) are explicitly out of scope. Code is promised, not yet released at writing time.
- **Strength of the theory is genuine, though.** Lemma 1 (regular Hadamard orders n = 4u², proof by a row-sum argument) plus the offset-invariance identity make the diagnosis precise and testable, and the paper verifies it empirically (Fig. 1: rotated max/RMS stays at unrotated level; centering + rotation drops it to the incoherence-predicted level).

## Strengths

- A crisp, structural diagnosis (DC component from adaLN + non-existence of regular Hadamard matrices at the evaluated widths) that explains *why* LLM-style data-free rotation fails on DiTs, backed by a short proof and direct measurements.
- The exact rank-1 re-injection is an elegant, provably lossless fix: offset invariance decouples the rotation choice from the mean, and the overhead is fused into the GEMM epilogue with zero net latency (1.69 s vs 1.77 s BF16 end-to-end).
- Fully calibration-free and hyperparameter-frozen across models and layers (p = 0.9995, γ = 2.56, p = 0.7, g = 64), which is the right axis to push on given the per-checkpoint/per-prompt fragility of calibration-based methods.
- Broad evaluation: three DiT families with different attention/conditioning designs, two fidelity benchmarks, GenEval compositional scoring, an ablation table, a weight-quantizer ablation, weight-only W3A16/W4A16 tables, and real-kernel latency.
- Honest reporting in places: flagging FID noise at 5k samples, admitting W2A4 is simulated, and scoring the eval split once after dev-split tuning.

## Limitations

- Gains are specific to adaLN-conditioned DiTs whose activations carry a large per-token mean; the authors concede the centering benefit should shrink on architectures without it, and all three evaluated models do use adaLN.
- Sub-4-bit configurations (W4A2, W2A4) are simulated only — no fused kernel, no latency numbers — and microscaling FP4 (NVFP4/MXFP4) is untested.
- No factorial ablation isolating centering vs rotation vs clipping, and no numerical comparison against HadaNorm (no public implementation) or against a centered-rotation variant of OrbitQuant's block-Hadamard mixing.
- On FLUX.1-schnell W4A4, CentriQ does not beat calibrated SVDQuant on FID/ImageReward (18.8/0.939 vs 18.3/0.951); the "matches SVDQuant" claim is cleanest on PixArt-Σ and SANA-1.6B.
- Code and models "will be made publicly available" — not verifiable at the time of writing; 2-bit claim rests on GenEval + FID only, with a known W4A2 weakness on Counting/Position sub-tasks.

## Relevance

这篇工作直击文生图 DiT 部署的核心瓶颈——采样时延与显存，用"均值居中 + 秩一重注入"这一可证明无损的技巧把免校准量化首次做到与校准方法同档，是 DiT 推理加速方向值得关注的方法论进展。其"诊断结构性失效根因再设计算子"的路径，以及免数据闭式标定 + 稳健 ℓ_p 权重拟合的组合，对任何做低比特推理、post-training quantization 的人都有直接可借鉴之处。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How does CentriQ transfer to video DiTs and to text-to-image models whose conditioning bypasses adaLN (e.g., cross-attention-only or RoPE-heavy designs) — does the DC component diagnosis still dominate the error budget?
- Would a factorial ablation (centering-only, rotation+clip without centering) reveal that centering alone at W4A4 already suffices, making the rotation redundant at 4-bit?
- Can the closed-form per-token scale (p = 0.9995, γ = 2.56) be replaced by per-layer or per-timestep adaptations to close the remaining PSNR gap to SVDQuant on PixArt-Σ and SANA-1.6B?
- Does the rank-1 epilogue survive microscaling FP4 formats (NVFP4/MXFP4), where the scale granularity changes the offset-invariance arithmetic?
- With a released implementation, how does CentriQ compare head-to-head against HadaNorm and against OrbitQuant's post-rotation marginal-codebook mixing under identical seeds and prompts?
