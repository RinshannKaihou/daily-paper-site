Why this matters

If you debias with steering, control for confidence first — falling bias scores may only mean the model abstains more.

用 steering / CAA 做去偏时，必须先排除置信度–弃答混淆，否则偏见分数的下降可能只是模型变不自信的副产物。

Critical edge

The “best layer” is picked by maximizing AUROC on the test set itself, so the headline probe separability numbers are optimistically biased — no nested selection split.

Methodology

Setup: 8 open-weight models — Llama-3.1-8B, Falcon3-7B, Ministral-3-8B, Qwen3-5-9B, each base + instruct (7–9B). Bias data: BBQ (15,960 prompts), StereoSet (2,106), CrowS-Pairs (1,018 one-word-diff pairs). Bias-free controls: MMLU (10k subset), OpenBookQA.

  1. Probe — logistic regression on the final-token hidden state at each model’s per-model “best layer”, trained on biased vs anti-biased options only.
  2. Interrogate — re-score the same probe on highest- vs lowest-probability answers; transfer it to MMLU / OpenBookQA, which contain no societal bias.
  3. Correlate — Spearman ρ between P(biased) and answer probability, ambiguous vs disambiguated BBQ.
  4. Align — build an out-of-distribution confidence direction vc from MMLU/OBQA; cosine-compare with the CAA direction vb = mean(hanti − hbiased).
  5. Steer — h + α·v̂ with α ∈ [−1, 2]; every dataset evaluated with and without an abstain option, tracking bias score, accuracy, and answer entropy.

Baseline bias is real: all 8 models score samb 0.07–0.18 on BBQ, with stereotype-answer rates of 69.0–78.8% (CrowS-Pairs) and 62.7–73.3% (StereoSet) — the confound is not an excuse for biased models.

Strengths

  • Sharp, falsifiable question — what does the separating direction actually encode? — answered by three independent evidence types that agree.
  • Excellent controls: OOD confidence directions, abstain vs no-abstain formats, paired ambiguous/disambiguated BBQ splits.
  • The mechanistic story predicts both signs: accuracy rises on ambiguous BBQ (abstention allowed) and falls everywhere abstention is impossible.
  • Breadth: 8 models × 3 bias datasets × 2 controls × 2 answer formats, all raw magnitudes tabulated.
  • Honest, calibration-focused conclusion with direct guidance: report steering-debiasing results with confidence/abstention controls.

Limitations

  • Scale: 7B–9B open-weight models only; no evidence the confound holds at 70B+ or in closed models.
  • Multiple-choice only: bias measured over answer-letter probabilities; no free-text generation, toxicity, or downstream decisions.
  • CAA-specific: probe-supervised (FairSteer) or INLP-style debiasing untested — listed as future work.
  • Test-set layer selection inflates probe AUROC; a held-out selection protocol would tighten the numbers.
  • Benchmark reformatting (CrowS-Pairs word-diff filter, injected abstain options) breaks comparability with leaderboard numbers.
  • No code release; Qwen3-5-9B-Base is a divergent outlier (final-layer separability, negligible steering effect).

Questions for further exploration

  1. Can a debiasing direction provably disentangled from confidence be constructed — and would it still reduce measured bias?
  2. Do FairSteer-style probe directions or weight-editing methods inherit the same confound, or is it specific to mean-difference CAA?
  3. Does the bias–confidence entanglement persist at larger scales and in RLHF-tuned models with safety-trained abstention?
  4. Would confidence-controlled evaluation — bias metrics at fixed abstention rate or fixed entropy — change published steering-debiasing conclusions?
  5. Do safety steering vectors (refusal, honesty, sycophancy) hide the same artifact, their “target behavior” merely a confidence shift?
