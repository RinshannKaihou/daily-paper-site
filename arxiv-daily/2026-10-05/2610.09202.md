> **一句话总结：** Meta 提出 CanonQ，用固定的 Hadamard-置换-对角（HDP）旋转加能量归一化把权重、激活、KV 缓存四类异构张量映射到统一"规范坐标"，再叠加跨层跨模型复用的高斯参考冻结码本与联合 QAT；在 W2A4KV2 极限压缩下，LLaMA3-1B 的 WikiText-2 困惑度从 ParetoQ 的 90.57 降到 15.42（5.87 倍），8B 上零样本均值精度以 59.81% 对 37.87% 领先 21.95 个百分点，仅比全精度低 0.30 个点。

> **TL;DR:** CanonQ is a quantization-aware training framework that quantizes weights, activations, and KV cache **jointly** (down to W2A4KV2) by separating two decisions: (1) fixed source canonicalization — a frozen Hadamard-based rotation plus energy normalization that pushes all four tensor sources toward a single Gaussian reference N(0,1), and (2) task-aware joint QAT against frozen, offline-fitted reference codebooks reused across layers and models. On WikiText-2 with LLaMA3-8B at W2A4KV2, CanonQ-Omni reaches 9.41 PPL vs 134.36 for the strongest QAT baseline ParetoQ (14.28x lower) and 59.81% vs 37.87% mean eight-task zero-shot accuracy; the joint gain is far larger than weight-only canonicalization (4.18x PPL gap), showing the benefit concentrates exactly when W/A/KV are compressed together. Three theorems bound frozen-codebook transfer error (Wasserstein "canonicalization defect"), local task loss, and STE gradient bias.

## 核心贡献

- **统一的 W/A/KV 联合量化框架 CanonQ**：把标量与向量量化统一到一个"规范接口"——固定 HDP 正交旋转 R_S = P_S H D_S 加能量归一化 ρ(u)，使权重/激活/键/值四类张量在 QAT 前先对齐到 N(0,1) 高斯参考；码本只依赖 (b,d)，一次离线拟合（Lloyd-Max/LBG）后跨层、跨模型复用，QAT 期间完全冻结，模型通过联合训练适应耦合的量化误差。
- **从源对齐到优化误差的量化理论链**：定义"规范化缺陷" Δ_d(P) = W₂(P,Q_d)/√d 刻画实际张量分布与高斯参考的偏差；定理 1 给出冻结码本迁移条件（参考整形增益超过 2 倍规范化缺陷即迁移）；定理 2 给出增益感知的局部任务损失证书；定理 3 证明量化失真控制 STE 梯度偏差，得到失真依赖的 SGD 平稳性界。
- **W2A4KV2 下的大幅质量领先**：1B/3B/8B LLaMA3 与 Qwen3-1.7B 一致领先最强量化基线（ParetoQ、VQLLM-QAT），8B 上与全精度差距缩到 0.30 个精度点；方法迁移到指令微调模型的 GSM8K/HumanEval/MBPP 并在 8K 上下文内保持稳定的相对困惑度。
- **系统性的归因实验**：权重-only → +激活 → +KV 缓存的逐级消融、零更新对照（7043 → 13.84 PPL）、uniform-KV vs canonical-KV 缓存路径消融、标量 vs 向量维度扫描、W₁/KS 边际对齐诊断，全部支持"规范化的收益在联合压缩时最大"这一核心论点。

## Key Contributions

- **CanonQ**, a unified W/A/KV quantization framework built on a common canonical interface: frozen HDP rotations + energy normalization map heterogeneous sources to canonical coordinates; frozen Gaussian-reference codebooks (fit offline to N(0,1)) are reused across all layers and models, while joint QAT adapts the network to the coupled quantization errors.
- **A quantitative theory chain from source mismatch to optimization**: Wasserstein bounds on frozen-codebook transfer (Theorem 1, via the canonicalization defect), a gain-aware local task-loss certificate (Theorem 2), and distortion-controlled STE stationarity bounds (Theorem 3) — each theorem is explicitly connected to a corresponding experiment.
- **Large quality gains in the extreme W2A4KV2 regime**: consistent, order-of-magnitude perplexity improvements over ParetoQ and VQLLM-QAT across LLaMA3-1B/3B/8B and Qwen3-1.7B, with the 8B model landing within 0.30 accuracy points of full precision.
- **Careful attribution studies**: staged W-only → +A → +KV ablations, zero-update controls, cache-path ablations, scalar-vs-vector dimension sweeps, and marginal-alignment (W1/KS) diagnostics that together isolate where the gains come from.

## Methodology

**Canonicalization (fixed).** For each source S ∈ {W, A, K, V}, a block u ∈ R^g is mapped to z = √g · R_S u / ρ(u), where R_S = P_S H D_S is a fixed structured Hadamard-permutation-diagonal orthogonal map (one map per source, shared across layers; K and V share a map) and ρ(u) = ||u||₂ + ε removes magnitude. For ε → 0 the representation is scale-invariant, and Haar-rotated normalized blocks converge to the Gaussian reference Q_d = N(0, I_d), motivating codebook design against N(0,1).

**Frozen reusable codebooks.** For b bits per scalar and tuple dimension d, a table C_{b,d} of K = 2^{bd} codewords is fit offline to the Gaussian reference via generalized Lloyd/LBG iterations (fitting gap ε_vq bounded in Eq. 6). d = 1 gives scalar tables (Lloyd-Max; e.g., 16 levels for 4 bits); d = 4 with 8-bit indices yields 256 four-dimensional codewords, so one index represents four weights. A table depends only on (b,d) and is shared across groups, layers, and models; group gains γ and source maps handle scale/coordinates. Assignment is nearest-codeword; decoding applies a nonnegative gain γ_{S,ℓ}.

**Joint QAT.** All enabled W/A/KV paths are active during training with identity straight-through estimation (STE) at assignment; the norm is recomputed from the current block and differentiable in both encoding and decoding, so the block STE differs from the identity by a distortion-dependent rank-≤1 correction (Eq. 13). Training: FineWeb-Edu, sequence length 2048, effective batch 64, AdamW, ≤240K updates (~31.5B tokens), learning rates from {1,2,4}×10⁻⁵ selected on held-out validation; the 8B configuration reuses the best 3B hyperparameters, and the 8K-context study continues QAT at 8192 for 40K updates.

**Variants.** CanonQ-W canonicalizes weights only; CanonQ-Omni adds canonical A/KV paths (d_A = d_K = d_V = 1 for the reported scalar tables; d_W ∈ {1,2,4} swept). Baselines: ParetoQ (its learned weight quantizer plus uniform A/KV operators), VQLLM-QAT-d4 (raw-space group-fitted weight codebook), plus PTQ references (RTN, GPTQ, QuaRot) and KIVI for cache. Evaluation: WikiText-2 perplexity (2448-token windows), mean zero-shot accuracy over eight tasks (ARC-Easy/Challenge, BoolQ, PIQA, SIQA, HellaSwag, OpenBookQA, WinoGrande), and greedy pass@1 / exact match on MBPP, HumanEval, GSM8K for MobileLLM-Pro-Instruct-1B at W2A16KV16 with a shared-teacher distillation protocol.

## Results & Highlights

- **W2A4KV2, LLaMA3-1B**: CanonQ-Omni-d1 reaches 15.42 WikiText-2 PPL and 53.56% mean eight-task accuracy vs 90.57 / 40.47 for ParetoQ — 5.87x lower PPL and +13.09 points. Against weight-only canonicalization (CanonQ-W-d1, 64.48 / 42.49), it is 4.18x lower PPL and +11.07 points, locating the benefit in extending the interface to activations and caches. (FP: 9.75 / 58.54.)
- **W2A4KV2, LLaMA3-8B**: CanonQ-Omni-d1 reaches 9.41 PPL / 59.81% vs 134.36 / 37.87% for ParetoQ — 14.28x lower PPL and +21.95 points (57.9% relative accuracy gain), only 0.30 points below the FP model's 60.11%. On 3B, CanonQ-Omni-d2 gets 11.16 / 60.04% vs ParetoQ's 92.63 / 39.39% (8.30x / +20.65 points).
- **Cross-family transfer, Qwen3-1.7B W2A4**: CanonQ-Omni-d1 17.80 PPL / 50.96% vs ParetoQ 43.56 / 42.35% — 2.45x lower PPL and +8.61 points with the same frozen-codebook construction.
- **Code and reasoning, MobileLLM-Pro-Instruct-1B W2A16KV16**: GSM8K exact match 47.8% (630/1319) vs 34.3% (453) for ParetoQ and 32.7% (431) for VQLLM-QAT-d4 (+13.4 points, 39.1% relative, retaining 88.9% of the FP score of 53.8%); HumanEval pass@1 62.2% vs 43.9% for ParetoQ (+18.3); MBPP 32.2% vs 28.0% (+4.2). The FP reference scores 60.4% on HumanEval (paired McNemar p = 0.74, bootstrap 95% CI [−5.5, 9.1]).
- **Activation precision is where the separation opens**: A8→A4 costs CanonQ-Omni only +0.59 PPL / −0.89 points vs +14.35 PPL / −6.97 points for ParetoQ; at W2A4 the weight-only canonical path stays near 35 PPL / 44% while CanonQ-Omni-d1 holds 13.84 / 54.71%.
- **The cache path matters**: replacing canonical KV with uniform KV drops LLaMA3-1B CanonQ-Omni-d1 from 15.70/53.45 to 22.82/47.72 (W2A8KV2) and from 15.42/53.56 to 21.65/48.30 (W2A4KV2) — 5.73 PPL / 5.26 points from the cache design alone. Moving W2A4→W2A4KV2 costs the canonical path only 1.58 PPL / 1.15 points vs 58.00 PPL / 3.65 points for ParetoQ.
- **Zero-update control**: applying the canonical W/A codec to the pretrained 1B without any gradient update yields 7043.43 PPL (38.83%) at W2A4; after QAT it is 13.84 (54.71%) — a ~509x PPL drop showing canonicalization and adaptation play complementary roles.
- **Marginal alignment**: HDP reduces layer-mean W₁ distance to N(0,1) by ~90% for activations (0.101→0.010), ~57% for keys (0.060→0.026), and ~40% for values (0.030→0.018); weights barely move (0.015→0.017).
- **8K context**: after 8K continued QAT, LLaMA3-1B W2A4KV2 at 8K gives 14.86 PPL for CanonQ-Omni vs 39.50 (CanonQ-W) and 45.63 (ParetoQ); the ratio to the same-length FP reference stays within 1.69–1.74x across 2K/4K/8K.
- **Honest footprint accounting (Table 5)**: "W2A4KV2" is an index-rate label; stored rates are 2.125–2.250 bits/weight, 4.125–4.250 bits/activation, and 2.250–2.500 bits/KV value after decoding metadata and table bytes.

## Authors & Credibility

The team is from Meta AI, with Kai Yi (correspondence) and Steven Li also behind Jacquant (STE-free QAT via learned Jacobian surrogates, arXiv 2606.25469) and W1pp (accelerating QAT around saddle points, ICML 2026). The group sits inside the Meta low-bit QAT lineage that produced ParetoQ (Liu et al., 2025b) and VQLLM-QAT — meaning the strongest baselines are in-house methods, which cuts both ways: deep familiarity with fair training-budget comparisons, but limited external diversity at the QAT frontier. The paper's credibility practices are strong: explicit index-rate vs stored-bit accounting, McNemar and paired-bootstrap uncertainty analysis on HumanEval, an explicit statement that GSM8K guides learning-rate selection for the reasoning runs, and a one-seed disclosure.

## Critical Assessment

- **The headline bit label overstates the footprint, and no deployment numbers back it.** "W2A4KV2" excludes decoding factors, table storage, and unquantized modules; Table 5 concedes 2.125–2.5 stored bits for W/KV and 4.125–4.25 for activations. The Discussion openly defers exported-model size, KV storage, and prefill/decode throughput to future work — so the paper demonstrates model quality, not serving cost, for a compression claim that is ultimately about memory.
- **Single seed everywhere.** Every reported configuration uses training seed 42 with no variance estimates; with gaps as large as reported this is unlikely to flip conclusions, but for the smaller margins (e.g., 3B scalar-vs-vector d: +1.67 accuracy at the cost of +0.23 PPL) seed noise is unquantified.
- **The 8B recipe is borrowed, not searched.** The 8B model reuses 3B hyperparameters, and the authors themselves expect a dedicated search to "further improve accuracy and narrow the gap to FP" — the 14.28x headline may actually understate the method, but as reported it is a non-tuned configuration compared against baselines under one shared budget.
- **Reasoning/code gains are not in the extreme regime.** The GSM8K/HumanEval/MBPP results are at W2A16KV16 — a mild setting — and GSM8K itself participates in learning-rate selection, so the 39.1% relative GSM8K improvement is partly tuned-on; only MBPP/HumanEval are fully held-out. The paper is transparent about this, but the abstract's juxtaposition of "W2A4KV2" and coding gains invites conflation.
- **Baseline cardinality confound, acknowledged.** At matched index rates, ParetoQ's uniform operators have 15/255/3 active levels vs 16/256/4 for canonical tables, so the comparison is not bitwise-identical; the paper flags this itself (App. A.2), and the huge margins make it immaterial for the headline, but the fine-grained A8 comparisons are less clean.
- **Theory is certificate-grade, not tightness-grade.** Theorems 1–3 assume C³ smoothness, Lipschitz gradients, and bounded sensitivity; the bridge to practice is via diagnostics (W1/KS alignment, zero-update controls, KS diagnostics of assignment distortion) rather than measured bound tightness. The "Connection to evidence" paragraph is a good-faith mapping, but no experiment verifies a bound is anywhere near tight.
- **PTQ baselines collapse by design.** GPTQ/QuaRot rows (37–38% accuracy at W2A4KV2) mostly serve to show the regime is QAT-only; the real comparison set is just two QAT methods, both Meta-affiliated.

## Strengths

- First convincing joint W2A4KV2 numbers: at 8B the quantized model is within 0.30 accuracy points and 1.68 PPL of full precision — previously thought impractical when W, A, and KV are compressed simultaneously.
- The central scientific claim is properly isolated: weight-only canonicalization shows only modest gains (12.66 vs 12.86 PPL at W2A16), while the same interface extended to A/KV produces order-of-magnitude improvements — the ablation chain (W → +A → +KV, uniform vs canonical cache, zero-update) is exactly the right experimental design.
- Frozen, reusable codebooks are a genuinely useful systems property: one offline table per (b,d) shared across layers and models, with no per-tensor refitting during QAT.
- Theory and experiment are actually wired together — each theorem has a designated empirical diagnostic, which is rare in the quantization literature.
- Unusually honest reporting: stored-bit accounting, McNemar/bootstrap intervals, learning-rate-selection disclosures, and a limitations section that names what was not measured.

## Limitations

- No deployment evaluation: no kernel, throughput, latency, or exported-size measurements; stored-bit rates are accounting, not measured bytes.
- Context-length evidence stops at 8K (with 8K-range QAT); no long-context or instruction-following evaluation in the extreme regime, and no reasoning benchmarks at W2A4KV2 itself.
- All headline results use one training seed; the 8B configuration inherits 3B hyperparameters.
- QAT cost is substantial: up to ~31.5B tokens (240K updates) per model, plus 40K more updates for the 8K study — a very different cost model from PTQ methods, and not tabulated against them.
- Evaluation covers dense LLaMA-style and Qwen models only; no MoE, no vision encoders, and K/V share a rotation map, leaving grouped-query attention variants untested.

## Relevance

该工作直击"极端性能优化下的可靠性"关注点：它不只报告 W2A4KV2 的质量数字，还给出了量化失真如何控制 STE 梯度偏差与任务损失的显式界（定理 2/3），以及零更新对照、边际对齐诊断等可靠性证据，是当前低比特量化中少见的"机制+实证"双线工作。对关心低精度数值稳定性与误差传播的读者，冻结码本 + 联合适配这套分解框架值得作为基线范式跟进。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How large is the real serving win once fused dequant kernels, table lookups, and KV decoding-factor traffic are measured — does the 2.125–2.5 stored-bit W/KV footprint still beat uniform INT at equal quality?
- Do the Theorem 3 distortion-controlled STE bounds predict which layers tolerate the most aggressive assignment distortion, enabling per-layer (b,d) allocation instead of uniform configurations?
- The canonicalization defect Δ_d(P) is estimated only via scalar marginals when d > 1; can the joint tuple law be measured cheaply enough during QAT to trigger table refits online?
- Does the frozen-codebook interface transfer to grouped-query attention and MoE, where K/V group geometry and routing statistics differ substantially from dense LLaMA?
- At what context length does the 1.69–1.74x relative-perplexity stability break down, and does energy normalization interact with attention-sink tokens at very long horizons?
