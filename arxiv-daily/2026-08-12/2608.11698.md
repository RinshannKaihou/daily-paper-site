> **一句话总结：** 针对在线蒸馏(OPD)中奖励外推使用单一全局系数λ导致奖励黑客与训练不稳、且最优λ随领域变化的问题，REOPD 提出 token 级兼容性权重 $q_t$ 与 batch 级自适应预算 $\gamma_b$，构造逐 token 有效系数 $\lambda_{b,t}=1+\gamma_b q_t$，在保留教师对齐项的同时仅自适应调整教师-参考残差，无需任何 verifier/奖励/价值模型；在 Qwen3-4B 的数学与代码单/多教师蒸馏上，多数设置超过逐领域调优的最优固定 ExOPD，且数学消融显示去除 token 兼容性使精度下降 4.27 个百分点。

> **TL;DR:** REOPD replaces ExOPD's single global reward-extrapolation coefficient λ with a token-level compatibility weight and a bounded micro-batch budget (effective λ = 1 + γ_b·q_t) that preserve teacher alignment and adapt only the beyond-teacher residual, using no verifier/reward model/extra rollout. On Qwen3-4B it beats the best per-domain tuned fixed-λ ExOPD on AIME/HMMT math (+0.19 pp, 47.66% vs 47.47%) and multi-teacher code (+0.42 pp, 63.32% vs 62.90%), matches on multi-teacher math (+0.03 pp), and trails single-teacher code by only 0.15 pp (63.45% vs 63.60%); ablating token compatibility costs 4.27 pp on math.

## 核心贡献

- **将固定系数奖励外推重构为残差控制问题：** 指出 ExOPD 用同一个全局 λ 放大每个 token 的教师-参考残差，会同时放大隐式奖励的极端尖峰，引发奖励黑客与训练不稳，且最优 λ 随领域(数学/代码/多教师)变化、需要昂贵扫描。
- **token 级兼容性权重 $q_{b,i,t}$：** 基于学生-教师对数概率差构造低方差 $k_3$ 散度代理 $\hat\delta=\exp(x)-x-1$（$x=\log\pi_T-\log\pi_\theta$），再用 $q=\exp(-\hat\delta/\tau)\in(0,1]$ 对残差进行软门控——学生-教师差异小的 token 保留大部分外推残差，差异大的 token 被衰减。
- **batch 级有界自适应预算 $\gamma_b$：** 由数据并行 rank 同步的两个充分统计量——兼容性加权残差占比 $\rho_b$ 与可靠残差 RMS 尺度 $s_b$——经 EMA 平滑后给出 $\tilde\gamma_b=\text{clip}(B_0\bar\rho_b/(\bar s_b+\epsilon),0,\gamma_{\max})$，并再次 EMA 平滑，从而在残差可靠且量级适中时加大外推、量级过大时自动收缩。
- **保留对齐、零额外开销：** 最终 token 代价 $C=a-\gamma_b q_t r_t$ 完全保留标准 OPD 的教师对齐项 $a$，且复用 G-OPD 已计算的学生/教师/参考对数概率，无需 verifier、奖励模型、价值模型或额外 rollout；有效系数满足 $1\le\lambda_{b,t}\le1+\gamma_{\max}$。
- **跨领域/多教师验证：** 在单教师数学、单教师代码、混合多教师蒸馏三种设置下，REOPD 在 4 个设置中的 3 个超过逐领域调优的最优固定 ExOPD，第 4 个(单教师代码)仅低 0.15 个百分点。

## Key Contributions

- **Reframes fixed-coefficient reward extrapolation as a residual-control problem**, exposing that ExOPD's single global λ amplifies extreme teacher–reference log-ratio peaks (reward hacking / instability) and must be re-tuned per domain.
- **Token-level compatibility weight** $q_{b,i,t}=\exp(-\hat\delta/\tau)$ from a low-variance $k_3$ discrepancy proxy $\hat\delta=\exp(x)-x-1$ on already-computed student/teacher log-probs, softly gating the residual so low-discrepancy tokens extrapolate more.
- **Bounded micro-batch budget** $\gamma_b\in[0,\gamma_{\max}]$ derived from synchronized, EMA-smoothed sufficient statistics (compatible-residual proportion $\rho_b$ and reliable-residual RMS scale $s_b$), giving a controller that scales up extrapolation when residuals are reliable and down when their magnitude is large.
- **Preserves teacher alignment at zero extra cost**: the final cost $C_{b,i,t}=a_{b,i,t}-\gamma_b q_{b,i,t} r_{b,i,t}$ keeps the OPD alignment term untouched, reuses the log-probabilities G-OPD already computes, and needs no verifier, reward model, value model, or extra rollout (the only operational additions are all-reduce of two scalar sufficient statistics).
- **Empirically competitive without per-domain λ sweeps**, beating the best per-setting fixed ExOPD coefficient in 3 of 4 settings (single-teacher math, multi-teacher math, multi-teacher code) and trailing by 0.15 pp on single-teacher code.

## Methodology

REOPD sits inside the G-OPD framework that interprets on-policy distillation as dense KL-regularized RL: the teacher–reference log-ratio $r_t=\log\pi_T(y_t|s_t)-\log\pi_{\text{ref}}(y_t|s_t)$ is an implicit token reward, and ExOPD scales its "beyond-teacher" residual by a constant $(\lambda-1)$ at every token. REOPD's only change is to replace that global multiplier with a per-token effective coefficient $\lambda_{b,i,t}=1+\gamma_b q_{b,i,t}$.

**Token path (compatibility).** From the already-computed student/teacher log-probs it forms $x_{b,i,t}=\log\pi_T-\log\pi_\theta$ and the $k_3$-style proxy $\hat\delta=\exp(x)-x-1\ge0$ (a smooth, low-variance discrepancy whose expectation is the reverse KL). The weight $q=\exp(-\hat\delta/\tau)$ lies in $(0,1]$: small student–teacher discrepancy ⇒ $q\approx1$ (keep residual), large discrepancy ⇒ $q\ll1$ (suppress residual). The weight is **detached** (stop-gradient) and measures *local compatibility, not task correctness* — which is exactly why it is applied only to the extra residual and never to the alignment term.

**Controller path (budget).** Because token compatibility alone cannot say how much extrapolation a micro-batch can support, REOPD aggregates two sufficient statistics over valid response tokens, summed across data-parallel ranks before ratios are taken: the compatible-residual proportion $\rho_b=\frac{\sum m| r|q}{\sum m|r|}$ (relative reliable mass) and the reliable-residual RMS scale $s_b=\sqrt{\frac{\sum m(qr)^2}{\sum m}}$ (absolute magnitude). These are EMA-smoothed ($\beta=0.95$) into $\bar\rho_b,\bar s_b$, and converted to a target $\tilde\gamma_b=\text{clip}(B_0\bar\rho_b/(\bar s_b+\epsilon),0,\gamma_{\max})$, then EMA-smoothed again ($\beta_\gamma=0.9$) into $\gamma_b$. The intuition is: more reliable mass ⇒ larger budget; larger residual scale ⇒ smaller budget (so a noisy batch cannot dominate). The scale $B_0$ is either set manually or auto-calibrated over the first $K_0$ controller calls from a scaled alignment-RMS ($\kappa\,\text{RMS}_b(a)$); an optional warm-up holds $\gamma_b$ at a fixed value early.

**Optimization.** The per-token advantage is $A=-C$ (negative cost), masked to valid response tokens, and fed into the same PPO-style surrogate as the OPD baseline. All of $q$, the sufficient statistics, the EMAs, and $\gamma_b$ are stop-gradient control signals — so REOPD changes only how the token advantage is constructed and otherwise reuses the G-OPD student/teacher/reference evaluations. Special cases: $\gamma_b=0$ ⇒ plain OPD; $q=1,\gamma_b=\lambda-1$ ⇒ fixed-λ ExOPD.

## Results & Highlights

- **Single-teacher math (pooled over AIME 2024, AIME 2025, HMMT-Feb-2025, HMMT-Nov-2025, 3,840 completions):** REOPD reaches **47.66%** vs OPD's **46.28%** (+1.38 pp) and vs the best per-domain fixed ExOPD (λ=1.25, **47.47%**) by **+0.19 pp**; on AIME 2024 specifically REOPD scores 61.98% vs OPD 60.10%.
- **Single-teacher code (task-count-weighted over HumanEval+ 164, MBPP+ 378, LiveCodeBench v6 test6 175):** REOPD obtains **63.45%**, beating OPD (**62.55%**, +0.90 pp) and ExOPD at λ=1.25 (**61.72%**, +1.73 pp), but trailing the best fixed sweep point λ=1.5 (**63.60%**) by **−0.15 pp**.
- **Multi-teacher math:** REOPD **47.01%** vs OPD **46.43%** (+0.58 pp) and vs best fixed ExOPD (λ=1.25/1.75 tie, **46.98%**) by **+0.03 pp** — a shared single controller improves both domains.
- **Multi-teacher code:** REOPD **63.32%** vs OPD **61.99%** (+1.33 pp) and vs best fixed ExOPD λ=1.25 (**62.90%**) by **+0.42 pp**.
- **Token compatibility is the central component (math ablation, 3,840 completions):** removing $q_t$ (`no_q`) drops pooled accuracy from **47.66%** to **43.39%** (**−4.27 pp**), with degradation on all four benchmarks (2.40–7.19 pp). Removing only the explicit bound (`no_bound`) costs **−0.50 pp** (47.16%). The fixed-budget sweep (`no_batch`) spans 46.48%–47.66%, and its best point λ0=1.25 (47.66%) matches full REOPD.
- **Controller dynamics (logged trajectories):** mean budget $\gamma$ rises over training — first 10 steps: math 0.608 / code 0.953 / multi 0.957; last 10 steps: 1.000 / 0.986 / 0.990. Mean effective coefficient rises from 1.477/1.710/1.720 to 1.850/1.815/1.826, confirming adaptation is strongest early and that late-stage variation is dominated by the token compatibility weight once the budget saturates near $\gamma_{\max}$.

## Authors & Credibility

Eight authors led by three equal-contribution first authors (Yang Sun, Lichao Ma, Houyuan Qin) spanning Shanghai AI Laboratory (primary affiliation, including its Autonomous Driving and Frontier Discovery Center groups), Peking University, Southwest Jiaotong University, USTC, UESTC, and Renmin University of China. The work builds directly on the G-OPD/ExOPD line (Yang et al., 2026a) and engages seriously with the very recent 2026 OPD-adjacent literature (TIP, Prune-OPD, SCOPE, SG-OPD, reward-gated OPD), positioning its contribution precisely (controlling only the residual, not alignment or rollout). The methodology is mathematically clean — exact-arithmetic bounds on λ, detached control signals, synchronized sufficient statistics — and the experimental protocol is described in enough detail (FSDP on 8×A100-80GB, TP=4, AdamW lr 1e-5, BF16, 1024 prompts/step, step-50 checkpoint, 32 samples/problem for math) to be reproducible in principle. Pre-registration of the step-50 checkpoint and the equal-prompt-budget caveat (token budgets differ across methods because response lengths differ) are honest disclosures.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty** is real but incremental: the core move — replacing ExOPD's global scalar with $\lambda_{b,t}=1+\gamma_b q_t$ and gating only the residual — is a principled, well-motivated engineering refinement of a 2026 baseline rather than a new paradigm. The $k_3$ discrepancy proxy and the two-statistic controller are the genuinely new pieces.
- **Rigor — the main weakness is statistical.** The headline deltas vs the best per-domain fixed ExOPD are **+0.19 / −0.15 / +0.03 / +0.42 pp** on benchmarks that are tiny: AIME 2024/2025 and HMMT-Feb/Nov are 30 problems each (120 math problems total, sampled 32×). Differences of 0.03–0.42 pp on such small problem sets are well within sampling noise, yet **no error bars, no confidence intervals, and no repeated-seed runs are reported** (ablations explicitly use a single "seed 42"). Claims that REOPD "outperforms" the best tuned fixed coefficient in 3 of 4 settings are not demonstrated to be significant.
- **Single-checkpoint, single-model evaluation.** All main results use the **global-step-50 checkpoint only** (no justification given for why step 50, no training curves shown against the baseline), and everything runs on one scale (Qwen3-4B) with one teacher family — generalization to other model families/scales/teacher gaps is entirely untested (the authors concede this in Limitations).
- **Internal tension undercut by the ablation.** The `no_batch` ablation shows that a **well-tuned token-only variant (λ0=1.25) already matches full REOPD (47.66% = 47.66%)** on single-teacher math. So the micro-batch budget's empirical value is mainly "avoid the sweep," not a performance gain — a defensible but narrower claim than the framing implies. Separately, the controller **saturates at $\gamma_{\max}$ late in training** (math reaches 1.000), so the much-emphasized two-level adaptation degenerates into token-only control in the regime that is actually evaluated — weakening the evidence that the batch-level budget is doing real work at convergence.
- **Reproducibility** is moderate-to-good: hyperparameters (τ=0.007, γ_max=1, κ=0.5, β=0.95, β_γ=0.9) and the auto-calibration/warm-up recipe are given, and the method adds no external models. But the non-standard **task-count-weighted code average** (rather than the mean of the three benchmark pass@1 percentages) makes head-to-head comparison with prior distillation numbers awkward, and the equal-prompt-budget-but-unequal-token-budget design means compute is not strictly held constant.
- **Significance** is currently modest: the practical payoff is "drop-in compatibility with G-OPD that removes the per-domain λ sweep," which is useful for practitioners but not yet a decisive accuracy win. No red flags regarding honesty — limitations, the discrepancy-vs-correctness caveat, and the saturation behavior are disclosed.

## Strengths

- Clean separation of concerns: preserving the teacher-alignment term and adapting *only* the beyond-teacher residual is a well-motivated design that sidesteps the reward-hacking failure mode of uniform extrapolation.
- Zero extra infrastructure: reuses existing log-probabilities, needs no verifier/reward/value model or extra rollout, and adds only an all-reduce of two scalars — genuinely cheap and drop-in.
- Strong, decisive ablation isolating token compatibility as the workhorse component (4.27 pp drop when removed).
- Shared controller across math and code in the multi-teacher setting works without per-domain tuning, a practical convenience.
- Honest about scope: explicitly states the compatibility weight is a discrepancy proxy, not a correctness estimator, and lists concrete limitations.

## Limitations

- **Statistical power:** math evaluation rests on only 120 unique problems (4 × 30); the headline deltas (≤0.42 pp) are within plausible sampling noise and are reported without error bars or multi-seed runs.
- **Compatibility ≠ correctness:** $q_t$ cannot detect tokens where student and teacher agree but are both wrong, so REOPD cannot filter jointly-erroneous supervision.
- **Controller saturation:** the micro-batch budget approaches $\gamma_{\max}$ late in training (math hits 1.000), so the batch-level contribution largely vanishes at convergence and late adaptation is carried by the token weight alone.
- **Residual controller hyperparameters** (τ, $\gamma_{\max}$, $B_0$ calibration, warm-up) still exist; statistics depend on micro-batch composition, so the method is not truly hyperparameter-free.
- **Narrow evaluation:** single model family/scale (Qwen3-4B), single teacher configuration, single checkpoint (step 50); generalization to other scales, teacher gaps, and per-teacher vs shared controllers is left to future work.
- The `no_batch` ablation's best fixed-λ0 point matching full REOPD on math means the batch-level budget's benefit is empirically "avoid a sweep," not a robust accuracy gain on this evidence.

## Relevance

直接契合「大模型后训练 / 强化学习 / 知识蒸馏」研究方向：REOPD 属于在线蒸馏（OPD/G-OPD）与 token 级隐式奖励外推这一前沿脉络，提出无需 verifier 的轻量残差自适应机制，对.reasoning 模型的蒸馏后训练与奖励黑客抑制有直接参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How do the +0.03–0.42 pp deltas hold up under multi-seed runs and bootstrap confidence intervals on AIME/HMMT — are any of the "wins" actually significant at 120 problems?
- Once the budget saturates at $\gamma_{\max}$, does the method collapse to token-only control; would a schedule that keeps $\gamma_b$ below saturation (or decays $B_0$) change late-training dynamics?
- Does the compatibility weight generalize to a stronger teacher / weaker student gap (where large-discrepancy tokens are common), and to non-reasoning or open-ended generation where there is no correct answer to align toward?
- Can the two sufficient statistics ($\rho_b$, $s_b$) be replaced or augmented by a cheap correctness signal (e.g. outcome verification) to also suppress jointly-wrong student–teacher tokens?
- Does a per-teacher controller (vs the shared one) help in multi-teacher settings with very different residual distributions per domain?
- What do training curves (not just step-50) look like — is REOPD's advantage stable across training or concentrated at a particular checkpoint?
