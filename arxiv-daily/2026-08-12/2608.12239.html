Critical Edge read cautiously

01

Methodology

four-stage PTQ pipeline

HAMP-LIC is a post-training quantization pipeline for a pre-trained FP32 learned-image-compression model, partitioned into L blocks with per-channel uniform weight quantization. No retraining of the original model.

  1. Hessian sensitivity. Each block’s sensitivity is the Hessian trace Tr(H_l), estimated via Hutchinson’s method (5 Rademacher vectors over a 12-image calibration set) — it predicts quantization damage better than first-order gradients.
  2. Task-aware refinement. The trace is modulated by each block’s relative R-D loss degradation (Ω_l = Tr(H_l)·|L_qp−L_int8|/|L_int8|), tying bit-width choices to the compression objective (λ·D + R), not generic MSE.
  3. Mixed-precision allocation. Minimize total sensitivity under an average-bit-width budget (ε=0.75) over bit-set {2,4,8}. A monotone-partition Pareto search collapses the exponential space (3^50 ≈ 7.1×10^23) to just 1326 candidates.
  4. Block-wise reconstruction. Sequential scaling optimization plus AdaRound-style learnable rounding under a joint task+quantization loss, processed front-to-back with preceding blocks frozen.

03

Strengths

  • Task-aware sensitivity biases allocation toward the real R-D objective, not generic MSE — genuinely useful for entropy-model-heavy architectures.
  • Pareto monotone-partition search provably collapses 3^50 ≈ 7.1×10^23 to 1326 candidates with minimal accuracy sacrifice.
  • Cross-platform error analysis quantitatively exposes an under-discussed FP32 failure mode (up to 98% decode failures) and shows integer PTQ eliminates it.
  • Best-in-class 0.59% Kodak BD-rate at the highest compression ratio (4.85×) among compared PTQ methods.
  • Honest ablations (Hessian-vs-Fisher, bit-allocation-vs-threshold) justify each design choice.
04

Limitations

  • Only two CNN-based codecs; transformer/attention-heavy LIC unverified and deferred to future work.
  • 12-image calibration set leaves out-of-distribution robustness (screen content, medical imagery) unstressed.
  • Budget ε=0.75 chosen empirically; only one alternative (ε=0.65) studied — not automated.
  • No QAT comparison and no sub-8-bit activations; lowest activation precision explored is 8-bit.
  • Minnen2018/Kodak win is compression-ratio-dominant, not BD-rate-dominant (marginally behind FMPQ/RDO-PTQ in absolute BD-rate).

Questions for Further Exploration

open threads
  • Does the Hessian-trace ranking stay stable when calibration expands to out-of-distribution content?
  • How does it transfer to transformer-based LIC (ELIC, SwinT), where attention layers differ sharply from ga/gs/h_a/h_s CNN blocks?
  • Can the Pareto search extend to joint weight-AND-activation allocation at sub-8-bit activations?
  • Can the FP32 decode-failure locations (entropy model? GDN layers?) be localized for cheaper targeted fixes?
  • How does HAMP-LIC compare to a QAT baseline at equal compression, in BD-rate and total engineering cost?
