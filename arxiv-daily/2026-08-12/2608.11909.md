> **一句话总结：** 本文在有限精度软注意力 Transformer 框架下，将旋转位置编码（RoPE）的两类解释统一并区分：分量周期型 RoPE 恰好识别带模谓词的过去时序逻辑 LTL[P, MOD]，而常规非周期 RoPE（旋转永不重复）只能给出依赖精度的、有界长度的固定偏移（look-back）模拟，表现为一种"有界局部性偏置"——实验用受控形式语言证实了这一分离。

> **TL;DR:** This COLM 2026 paper reconciles the two competing accounts of RoPE within fully uniform finite-precision soft-attention transformers: component-periodic RoPE (and periodic SiPE) recognizes exactly LTL[P, MOD] (Theorem 3.1/3.2), while conventional non-periodic RoPE only yields a precision-dependent *bounded* simulation of fixed-offset Y_k operators (hence a bounded piece of LTL[P, Y]). Controlled experiments on formal languages confirm the split — a constructed periodic schedule reaches perfect N*=500 length generalization on (aa)* and (ab)* where conventional RoPE fails at every base, while on the locality language Σ*a conventional RoPE beats NoPE at 6/7 bases (mean N* up to 99.8 vs NoPE 50.8) but on the position-invariant P-language aΣ* it underperforms NoPE (146.9–402.9 vs 470.5), showing RoPE acts as a bounded locality bias rather than a monotonic expressivity gain.

## 核心贡献

- 形式化并区分了 RoPE 的两种"周期性"：**分量周期型 RoPE（RoPE_P）** 的每个旋转分量以有限整数周期重复；而**常规 RoPE（RoPE_NP）** 由于频率（如 g=1）相对 2π 为无理数，旋转永不重复（命题 4.1）。
- 证明分量周期型 RoPE 与分量周期型正弦位置编码 SiPE_P 恰好等价于带模谓词的过去时序逻辑：SMAT[RoPE_P] = SMAT[SiPE_P] = LTL[P, MOD] = PFO₂[<, MOD]（定理 3.1、3.2），并给出该逻辑类在多项式、自动机（PODFA_k）与语法幺半群（QR）层面的多重等价刻画（定理 3.3）。
- 指出 LTL[P, MOD] 与右硬注意力类 LTL[S] **不可比**（(aa)* 在前者不在后者；Σ*abΣ* 在后者不在前者），且不能做模计数（PARITY 不属于该类）。
- 对常规非周期 RoPE 给出**有界**刻画：在精度维持足够分数间隔的长度范围内，它能精确模拟固定偏移算子 Y_k，从而有界模拟 LTL[P, Y]（推论 4.1）；超过该"认证范围"后行为因数值碰撞而变脆。
- 用受控形式语言实验验证了两种解释的分离，并把结论与注意力槽（attention sink / BOS 锚点）和部分 RoPE（partial-RoPE / NoPE-RoPE 混合）等实践现象联系起来。

## Key Contributions

- Formalizes and separates two regimes of RoPE: **component-periodic RoPE (RoPE_P)**, whose every rotary block repeats with a finite integer period, versus **conventional non-periodic RoPE (RoPE_NP)**, whose frequencies (e.g., g=1) are irrational w.r.t. 2π so rotations never repeat (Prop. 4.1).
- Proves that component-periodic RoPE and component-periodic sinusoidal PEs each recognize *exactly* LTL[P, MOD] = PFO₂[<, MOD] (Thm. 3.1, Thm. 3.2), and provides a five-way characterization of that class via left-deterministic modular polynomials, the syntactic-monoid variety QR, and PODFA_k automata (Thm. 3.3).
- Shows LTL[P, MOD] is **incomparable** with the rightmost hard-attention class LTL[S] (star-free): (aa)* is in the former but not the latter, whereas the locally-testable Σ*abΣ* is in the latter but not the former; and that modular predicates count positions, so PARITY is *not* in the class.
- Gives a **bounded** account of conventional RoPE: up to a precision-dependent "certified range" N_max^(k), relative scores can implement exact fixed-offset (look-back-k) heads Y_k, yielding a bounded simulation of LTL[P, Y] (Cor. 4.1) — a formal counterpart to the fixed-offset heads observed in trained LLMs.
- Validates the separation with controlled formal-language experiments and ties the BOS-anchor construction to attention sinks, motivating partial-RoPE / NoPE-RoPE hybrids for long-context "P" reasoning.

## Methodology

The analysis is carried out in the **fully uniform fixed-precision soft-attention transformer** model of Li & Cotterell (2025): a single parameter set processes all lengths, activations/parameters live in a finite set F (mirroring float32), and stable softmax permits exact hard selection whenever the winning score beats alternatives by at least f_large.

**Periodic RoPE (RoPE_P).** For a rational angle θ_d = 2πa_d/m_d, the authors avoid forming the (unbounded) product θ_d·i and instead precompute a lookup table T_d(r) = round_F(Rot_{θ_d,r}) for 0 ≤ r < m_d, indexed by position mod m_d. This keeps the realized map strictly periodic (period m_d) inside the uniform model. They prove the realized entries are definable by unary modular predicates (Prop. 2.1), which yields the upper bound SMAT[RoPE_P] ⊆ PFO₂[<, MOD]; a matching construction (an attention head comparing the returned phase against the fixed BOS phase) gives the reverse inclusion. The same argument applies to periodic sinusoidal PEs (Thm. 3.2), unifying absolute and relative encodings under periodicity.

**Non-periodic RoPE (RoPE_NP).** Conventional RoPE rotates by g/(2π) ∉ ℚ (it contains g=1), so the realized map is non-periodic (Prop. 4.1, via Walters 1982). For an offset k, a single rotary pair with query/key biases yields the score S_{i,j}^{g,C,k} = C·cos(g(i−k−j)), uniquely maximized at j=i−k. The "certified range" N_max^(k) is the largest length at which the target stays ahead of every competitor by ≥ f_large after rounding; within that range stable softmax turns the head into an exact Y_k, and unrotated dimensions independently carry the LTL[P] computation (Cor. 4.1).

**Experiments.** Transformer classifiers (5 layers, dim 64, 8 heads, soft attention, strict future masking) are trained on five probe languages (Table 1): training length ≤40, test length 41–500; 1M steps, batch 128, 512 samples per test length, 5 seeds × 3 learning rates. The metric is longest-perfect length N* (largest N with 100% accuracy at every tested length through N). Conventional bases are swept over {10⁻¹², 10⁻⁸, 10⁻⁴, 1, 10⁴, 10⁸, 10¹²} to check that conclusions do not hinge on the default 10⁴.

## Results & Highlights

- **Periodic RoPE generalizes perfectly on modular languages (Table 2).** On (aa)*, RoPE_P reaches accuracy 1.00 with N*=500, versus NoPE accuracy 0.50 and N*=41 (chance-level, i.e., a delta of +459 in N* and +0.50 accuracy). On (ab)*, RoPE_P reaches accuracy 1.00 with N*=500, versus NoPE accuracy 0.96 and N*=96.
- **Same expressivity does not imply same learnability (Table 2).** Periodic SiPE_P has the same theoretical expressivity (Thm. 3.2) but does *not* learn either modular language: on (aa)* it scores accuracy 0.50, N*=41; on (ab)* accuracy 0.50, N*=44 — the theorem guarantees a parameterization exists, not that optimization finds it.
- **No conventional base recovers the modular construction (Fig. 2 / Table 4).** Across the full seven-base sweep over 24 orders of magnitude, neither RoPE_NP nor SiPE_NP achieves perfect generalization on (aa)* or (ab)*; e.g., the best RoPE_NP on (aa)* is max N*=65 (base 10⁻⁸, mean 50.1) and on (ab)* max N*=98 (base 10⁸, mean 57.1), all far below RoPE_P's 500.
- **Conventional RoPE helps a locality (Y) task but hurts position-invariant (P) tasks (Fig. 3 / Table 5).** On Σ*a (LTL[Y]), RoPE_NP's mean N* beats NoPE (50.8) at 6 of 7 bases, peaking at base 10⁻⁴ with mean N*=99.8 (max 224). In contrast, on aΣ* (LTL[P]) NoPE mean N* is 470.5 while RoPE_NP means range only 146.9–402.9 (worse at *every* base), and on Σ*abΣ* (LTL[P,Y]) NoPE mean N* is 92.9 while RoPE_NP means range 46.1–61.9 (worse at every base).
- **The default base 10⁴ is the weakest on single-operator languages.** Among the seven bases, β=10⁴ gives the lowest mean N* on both Σ*a (45.5) and aΣ* (146.9), reproducing Liu et al. (2024)'s finding that the standard base is the worst tested, with improvements on both sides.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** Strong. It is the first to give an *exact* logical characterization (LTL[P, MOD]) for RoPE under a realistic finite-precision model, and the key insight — that conventional RoPE is provably non-periodic because g=1 is irrational w.r.t. 2π, so its rotations never repeat — is crisp and non-obvious. The incomparability result with LTL[S] and the PARITY non-membership cleanly bound the class.
- **Rigor:** High. All claims are proved; the finite-precision "ideal vs. realized rotation" distinction is handled carefully (Def. 2.1), and the bounded non-periodic result is honestly labeled as *bounded*, not an all-length characterization.
- **The expressivity–learnability gap is a real caveat:** The lower bound for RoPE_P uses a *custom, language-tailored* lookup-table schedule (standard in expressivity work, but explicitly not what standard RoPE or optimization produce). The authors say this directly, but readers should not conclude that stock RoPE-equipped LLMs obtain modular reasoning.
- **Experimentally narrow:** Evaluation is confined to five toy formal languages with tiny (dim-64) classifiers; there are **no natural-language or real long-context benchmarks**, so the jump to "what this means for production RoPE LLMs" is indirect. The negative result that SiPE_P never learns the modular languages despite equal expressivity underscores how far formal expressivity is from practical capability — a gap the paper flags but does not close.
- **Reproducibility:** Reasonable. Architecture, optimization, seeds (5), learning rates (3), base sweep, and per-run means/stds (Tables 4–5) are reported; the periodic scheduler is "explicitly written out in the code," though a public code link is not visible in the PDF.
- **Red flags:** None severe. The claims are well-scoped (the authors repeatedly note RoPE gives "neither general state-tracking nor regular-language recognition," explicitly excluding Flip-Flop/PARITY). The main risk is over-generalizing the bounded-locality story to all long-context failures of real RoPE LLMs, which the controlled setup does not directly demonstrate.

## Strengths

- Reconciles two previously "prima facie incompatible" accounts (modular expressivity vs. local-offset mechanistic bias) within a single clean framework, with each account matching a different *schedule assumption*.
- The finite-precision distinction between ideal and realized rotations is the right level of abstraction and yields the clean provable non-periodicity of conventional RoPE.
- The base sweep (24 orders of magnitude) makes the "no conventional base recovers modular behavior" claim robust rather than an artifact of β=10⁴.
- The connection to attention sinks (BOS as the fixed phase anchor needed to expose modular phase) and the partial-RoPE / NoPE-RoPE hybrid conjecture are genuinely useful architectural takeaways.
- Intellectual honesty: limitations, the bounded nature of the locality result, and the expressivity-vs-learnability gap are stated plainly.

## Limitations

- The periodic lower bound relies on a **tailored custom schedule** and a repeated finite lookup table; it does not show that standard RoPE frequencies or gradient-based optimization discover modular behavior.
- The non-periodic (locality) result is **bounded**, not an all-length characterization — beyond N_max the fixed-offset heads can break due to numerical collisions, and the asymptotic behavior of RoPE_NP is left open.
- Experiments are **small-scale and synthetic** (formal languages, dim-64, 5-layer classifiers); no evidence on natural language, real long-context tasks, or production-scale models, so practical implications are suggestive rather than demonstrated.
- SiPE_P having equal expressivity but failing to learn in practice reveals an optimization story the paper does not explain (it only speculates that absolute sinusoidal features are "more easily overwritten").
- The depth-hierarchy question (how Yang et al. 2025b's RoPE depth hierarchy differs between exact-periodic and bounded-local regimes) is posed but unanswered.

## Relevance

这项工作从形式语言与有限精度角度厘清了 RoPE（当前几乎所有主流 LLM 的默认位置编码）的表达能力边界与"有界局部性偏置"本质，对于理解长上下文建模、长度外推以及 RoPE/NoPE 混合注意力架构设计具有直接的理论指导意义，契合对 Transformer 架构原理与长程推理能力的研究兴趣。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the bounded-locality prediction (RoPE_NP helps Y-tasks but hurts P-tasks) reproduce on natural-language long-context benchmarks (e.g., needle-in-a-haystack, multi-document QA) and at production scale, or is it specific to these formal probes?
- Can the periodic lookup-table construction be realized via a differentiable, learnable schedule that gradient descent actually discovers, closing the expressivity–learnability gap for SiPE_P?
- How does the certified range N_max^(k) scale with precision (fp16 vs fp32 vs fp64) and with model dimension/depth — and can it predict the empirical "effective context length" of real RoPE models?
- Does partial-RoPE / NoPE-RoPE hybrid attention empirically improve long-context P reasoning while retaining the locality bias, as the authors conjecture?
- How does the known RoPE depth hierarchy behave differently in the exact-periodic vs. bounded-local regimes?
