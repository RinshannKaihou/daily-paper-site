> **一句话总结：** 本文证明 LLM 对隐式人口统计学线索的"个性化"行为（输出向刻板印象偏移）可以被定位到特定残差流层的激活对比信号上（per-sample Pearson r 最高达 0.87），并通过对该方向做投影消融，能在多线索场景下选择性抑制某一维度的刻板内容（优于"忽略人口属性"提示词最多 9.2 倍），同时基本保持 MMLU 等通用能力。

> **TL;DR:** Using matched cued vs. neutral 5-turn movie-recommendation dialogues across five 7B–14B LLMs, the authors show the normalized magnitude of the cue-induced residual-stream activation contrast at activation-patched "mediating" layers tracks the per-sample behavioral shift (Pearson r up to 0.87, peak on Llama-3-8B Asian-cue / SED-desc); when two cues co-occur the internal contrast combines near-linearly (mean residual fit 0.576–0.704) yet behavior is strictly sub-additive (28–38% below the additive sum); and projecting out one cue's sample-averaged direction via a layernorm pre-hook suppresses its stereotyped output by up to 9.2× more than an "ignore demographics" prompt while dropping MMLU by at most 2.8 points — though clean per-dimension selectivity holds only for some model/attribute pairs.

## 核心贡献

- **首次把"隐式个性化"行为与模型内部表示建立因果联系。** 以往工作只在输出层观察到 LLM 会因隐式人口统计学线索（如提及 Kwanzaa 庆祝、使用 "no cap" 等年龄俚语）而悄然改变推荐，本文证明一个可定位、可量化的内部信号——匹配线索版与中性版对话在"中介层 L⋆"上残差流激活差的归一化幅值——逐样本追踪这种行为偏移，r 最高达 0.87。
- **刻画了多线索共现时内部表示与外部行为的"解离"。** 内部混合线索对比向量能被该样本自身的两个单线索分量较好地线性重建（平均残差拟合 ρ 在 0.576–0.704 之间），但外部行为偏移却是严格次可加的——比单线索之和低 28%–38%，揭示了线性几何与非线性输出之间的鸿沟。
- **提出一种推理时的因果干预方法。** 在 post-attention layernorm 上以 `h′ = (I − α·v̂·v̂ᵀ)h` 的前向 pre-hook 投影消融某一人口维度对应的样本平均方向，可抑制该维度的刻板内容；该内部消融在 Qwen3-8B 上最多比"忽略人口属性"提示词有效 9.2 倍，而提示词在 Mistral 上甚至会反向加剧刻板化；MMLU 在 α=5 时最多下降 2.8 分。
- **诚实刻画了选择性干预的边界。** 在 18 个多线索消融设置中，仅 3 个能干净地把目标维度压下去而完全不碰共现维度（如 Mistral 的 race×gender），说明"逐维度可控"是高度依赖模型与属性的，Qwen3-8B 在 race×age 上会同时拉低两个维度。

## Key Contributions

- **Bridges implicit personalization to an internal, locatable signal.** For the first time, the paper shows the normalized magnitude of the cue-induced residual-stream activation contrast at activation-patching-identified mediating layers L⋆ tracks the per-sample behavioral shift, peaking at r = 0.87 (Llama-3-8B, Asian-cue, SED-desc) and collapsing toward zero in null-shift conditions (e.g., the White-cue condition, a universal null across all five models).
- **Quantifies a dissociation between internal geometry and external behavior under co-occurring cues.** Internally the mixed-cue contrast is well reconstructed from the sample's own two single-cue vectors (mean residual fit ρ̄ = 0.576–0.704, vs. 0.443 for a single direction and 0.095 when substituting another sample's basis), but behaviorally the shift is strictly sub-additive, 28–38% below the additive prediction, with no super-additive amplification.
- **A causal, inference-time intervention that beats prompting.** Projecting out one cue's sample-averaged direction at the union of each dimension's top-2 L⋆ layers suppresses targeted stereotyped content and outperforms an "ignore demographics" prompt by up to 9.2× on Qwen3-8B, while a matched-norm random-direction sham moves the metric by only +0.001 (confirming the effect is direction-specific, not generic perturbation).
- **Honest boundary on selectivity.** Clean per-dimension removal (target down, co-present dimension untouched) occurs in only 3 of 18 settings; the authors explicitly characterize this as model- and attribute-specific rather than a universal mechanism.

## Methodology

The paper uses a **paired-comparison design**. For each of n = 50 scenarios, GPT-4o generates matched 5-turn user/assistant dialogues that share identical topical scaffolding but branch into: (a) identity-cued variants carrying implicit demographic cues drawn from the Neplenbroek et al. (2025) taxonomy (cultural references, food, hobbies, behaviors — the demographic is never named), (b) an identity-neutral baseline using neutral fillers, and (c) explicit "state the demographic" contexts for calibration. Each set ends with the same query: "Recommend 5 movies. Output only the title and year on each line." Greedy decoding (T=0). Nine single-cue conditions span race (Black/Asian/White), gender (male/female), and age (child/adolescent/adult/older adult); three two-cue pairs (race×gender, race×age, gender×age) use K=4 with two cues from each dimension, each accompanied by dose-matched single-dimension controls.

**Behavioral metrics.** (1) *SED-desc* — taxonomy-free: resolve the 5 titles to TMDB plot overviews, embed with all-mpnet-base-v2, take one minus cosine similarity between cued and neutral concatenations (higher = larger semantic shift). (2) *CAR* (Content Alignment Ratio) — taxonomy-based: GPT-4o tags each item as identity-associated; CAR shift = CAR(cued) − CAR(neutral); human–GPT-4o agreement = 0.8.

**Internal quantity.** Define the raw contrast ∆A_L = A_L(C_cue, x) − A_L(C_neutral, x) at the final query-token post-attention residual-stream activation, and its normalized magnitude s_∆A = ||∆A_L||₂ / ||A_L(x)||₂ (denominator = bare-query activation, identical across conditions, placing layers on a common scale). The condition-specific mediating layer set L⋆ is identified via activation patching (Appendix C); s_∆A is averaged over L⋆.

**Intersectional analysis.** Cross-cue modulation is measured with the difference-of-differences contrast ψ (independent cues ⟹ ψ = 0); internal composition is measured by fitting v_AB ≈ α·v_A + β·v_B per sample/layer and reading the residual fit ρ.

**Causal intervention.** Average single-cue contrasts across samples, remove the partner dimension's component, unit-normalize to v̂_dim; apply directional ablation h′ = (I − α·v̂_dim·v̂_dimᵀ)·h via a forward pre-hook on the post-attention layernorm at the union of the two dimensions' top-2 L⋆ layers, sweeping α ∈ [0.5, 5] (α=1 = exact projection, α>1 overshoots). Compared against random-direction shams and an explicit "ignore demographic" prompt.

**Models.** Five instruction-tuned LLMs (7B–14B): Llama-3-8B, Mistral-7B-v0.3, Qwen3-8B, Qwen3-14B, Phi-4. Correlation + intersectional experiments run on all five; ablation + capability-preservation on the three smallest.

## Results & Highlights

- **Internal activation contrast tracks the behavioral shift per-sample.** On the strongest conditions, Pearson r reaches **0.87** (Llama-3-8B, Asian-cue, SED-desc); for the Black-cue condition r ranges 0.49–0.82 (SED) and 0.72–0.85 (CAR) across the five models, and for child-cue 0.39–0.71 (SED) and 0.48–0.80 (CAR). Tracking strength scales with behavior: across all 45 model×condition combinations, larger SED shifts give significantly stronger correlations (Spearman ρ = 0.54, p < 0.001; for the 35 CAR-scorable combinations ρ = 0.57, p < 0.001). In the universal-null White-cue condition, SED correlations peak at only 0.34 and CAR is unscorable on four of five models — i.e., the signal vanishes exactly where behavior does.
- **Behavior is real but heterogeneous.** A significant personalization shift appears in 7/9 conditions on Llama, 6/9 on Mistral, 4/9 on Qwen3-8B, 7/9 on Qwen3-14B, and 7/9 on Phi-4. Black-cue and child-cue shift on all five models; White-cue shifts on none.
- **Internal composition is near-linear and sample-specific; behavior is sub-additive.** Reconstructing the mixed-cue vector from the two single-cue directions gives mean residual fit ρ̄ = 0.576–0.704 (average 0.646), beating the best single-direction fit of 0.443; substituting a different sample's basis collapses the fit to 0.095, proving the structure is sample-specific. Yet the observed mixed behavioral shift (SED-desc) falls **28%–38% below the strict additive prediction** across models, with no super-additive amplification anywhere.
- **Direction ablation selectively suppresses targeted content and beats prompting.** On Qwen3-8B, ablating the race/age/gender direction drops ∆pure(t) by +0.176 to +0.306 (Cohen's d up to 1.59 on Mistral), outperforming the "ignore demographics" prompt by up to **9.2×** (Ratio column, Table 2); the prompt occasionally backfires and moves Mistral outputs toward the cue. A matched-norm random-direction sham changes CAR by only **+0.001**, confirming the effect is direction-specific.
- **Capability is largely preserved.** MMLU accuracy drops by **at most 2.8 points** even at the maximum strength α=5; at exact projection (α=1) it stays within 0.6 points of baseline.
- **Selectivity is clean in a minority of cases.** Of 18 settings, targeted taxonomy content falls significantly in 7, and in 3 of those the co-present dimension is entirely unchanged — cleanest example: Mistral race×gender, ablating gender lowers gender-tagged content by **0.169** while race-tagged content moves only +0.004. On Qwen3-8B race×age, both fall together (−0.130 race, −0.104 age), showing decoupling is not automatic.

## Authors & Credibility

Authors are from **Indiana University, Bloomington** (Yueru Yan, Siqi Wu, Thai Le). The work follows the well-established causal-mediation tradition (Vig et al. 2020) and representation-engineering / activation-steering lines (Zou et al. 2023; Arditi et al. 2024; Ravfogel et al. 2020; Belrose et al. 2023 LEACE), and is careful to cite the closest concurrent baselines (Karvonen & Marks 2025 on neutralizing attribute directions in explicit hiring; Kantharuban et al. 2025). Methodologically, the design is strong: matched neutral controls, dose-matched single-dimension controls, random-direction shams, Benjamini–Hochberg correction within model, replication across five models, and two complementary behavioral metrics (taxonomy-free SED + taxonomy-based CAR) used in agreement. The authors commit to releasing evaluation/analysis code on publication (though not the raw cue-construction templates, for dual-use reasons).

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty: high and well-scoped.** Connecting implicit (never-stated) personalization to a causally validated activation contrast is a genuine step beyond pure probing (Neplenbroek et al. 2025; Ghandeharioun et al. 2024), which only shows an attribute is *decodable*, not that it *drives* output. The cue→activation→behavior causal chain, isolated by the matched-neutral contrast, is the right experimental move.
- **Rigor: mostly strong, with one notable missing baseline.** The closest conceptual competitor — closed-form linear erasure (LEACE, Belrose et al. 2023) — is explicitly acknowledged as "a stronger natural baseline left for future work." For a paper whose central intervention is rank-one direction removal, not running LEACE is a real gap; iterative projection (Ravfogel et al. 2020) is likewise cited but not compared. The direction-ablation results should therefore be read as proof-of-mechanism rather than as state-of-the-art erasure.
- **Construct validity caveat (self-acknowledged and important).** Demographic cues intrinsically carry topical/lexical content (Kwanzaa ⇒ cultural topics), so the design cannot fully separate *semantic continuation* from *abstract demographic representation*; the authors cite Neplenbroek et al. 2026 showing topic can dominate in real conversations. This means the "demographic signal" located in L⋆ is partly a topical signal — a confound the two-metric (SED + CAR) triangulation mitigates but does not eliminate.
- **Reproducibility: mixed.** Evaluation/analysis code will be released, but the synthetic cue-construction templates will not, so the exact dialogues cannot be regenerated from the released artifacts (n=50 per condition is also modest). Human–GPT-4o tagging agreement of 0.8, concentrated at the adult/neutral boundary, is acceptable but not airtight.
- **Significance: solid but scope-limited.** Main results are on **movie recommendations only**; books and articles appear only in single-dimension form on Llama (Appendix A). Ablation runs on only the three smallest models (7–8B); the two 14B models contribute correlation/composition evidence only, so generalization to larger or base (non-instruct) models is open.
- **Selectivity overclaim risk: handled honestly.** The abstract's "highly model- and attribute-specific" hedge is earned — only 3/18 settings show clean decoupling, and Qwen3-8B race×age drags both dimensions down. This is a genuinely non-rubber-stamp limitation section.
- **Red flag (mild): behavioral-concealment vs. true removal.** The metrics measure *expressed content* and cannot distinguish true geometric removal from behavioral concealment (Gonen & Goldberg 2019); the authors did not run a post-ablation linear probe to test whether the demographic is still recoverable from the residual stream. This is the standard failure mode of projection-based debiasing and should temper any deployment claim.
- **Western/cultural vantage.** Race is operationalized as only {Black, Asian, White}, gender as {male, female}, all in English; the White-cue "universal null" likely reflects the taxonomy's Western framing rather than a language-model universal. Results should be read as evidence about behavior under this specific operationalization.

## Strengths

- Clean causal identification via matched-neutral + dose-matched controls, plus sham and prompt baselines — the experimental controls are above average for this subarea.
- Two complementary, triangulating behavioral metrics (taxonomy-free SED-desc and taxonomy-based CAR) rather than reliance on a single noisy score.
- Replication across five model families (7B–14B) rather than a single model.
- The dissociation finding (internal near-linear composition vs. external sub-additive behavior, 28–38% gap) is a crisp, memorable mechanistic insight with quantified both sides.
- Honest, specific limitations section that names the missing LEACE baseline, the topical confound, and the behavioral-concealment caveat.

## Limitations

- Movie-recommendation domain only in the main paper; books/articles restricted to single-dimension Llama results.
- Direction ablation limited to three 7–8B models; 14B and base models untested.
- n = 50 matched pairs per condition is modest for per-condition correlation claims.
- Construct-validity confound: implicit cues carry topical content that cannot be separated from demographic representation.
- Missing the strongest natural baseline (LEACE / iterative projection).
- Cannot distinguish geometric removal from behavioral concealment; no post-ablation probe test.
- Coarse, Western, English-only taxonomy (race: 3 categories, gender: 2, age: 4); the White-cue "universal null" is likely taxonomy-dependent.
- Selectivity does not generalize: clean per-dimension removal in only 3/18 settings.

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would a **closed-form LEACE erasure** match or beat the hand-tuned α-sweep directional ablation on the same movie-recommendation setup, and does it improve per-dimension selectivity?
- Can a **post-ablation linear probe** still recover the demographic attribute from the residual stream (the Gonen & Goldberg 2019 concealment test the authors did not run)?
- How much of the L⋆ signal survives when cues are **topic-controlled** — e.g., holding the conversation topic fixed while varying only the demographic association, to separate topical continuation from demographic representation?
- Does the cue→activation→behavior chain and its 28–38% sub-additive gap hold on **frontier 70B+/base models**, or is it an artifact of the 7–14B instruction-tuned regime?
- Can the dissociation (linear internal composition, sub-additive behavior) be explained by a specific downstream readout nonlinearity, or by the unexplained internal residual the authors flag as an open question?
- Does the same activation signature govern implicit personalization in **non-English** dialogues, where the cue taxonomy and stereotype lexicon would differ?
