Central Finding H-α · routing/readout boundary PREREGISTERED · NEGATIVE RESULT

Type supervision induces real routing structure — but that structure is functionally decoupled from answer readout: 150/150 structural edits moved outputs by essentially zero.

“Causal Structure is Inducible but Functionally Decoupled: The Routing/Readout Boundary of a Typed Mechanism Library” — arxiv.org/abs/2608.11767

Xining Xun · Tsingjiao Information Science (Beijing) · 2026-08-12 · single-author, industry mechanistic-interpretability model-editing causal-reasoning auditable-state pre-registration
Key Metric
150/150

paired structural flip edits moved the readout by |Δŷ| ≤ 3.4×10⁻⁶ against a frozen bar of ≤1×10⁻³ — with off-path collateral exactly 0.0 on all three seeds.

Why This Matters

Directly tests — and falsifies — the interpretability assumption that exposing or editing a model’s internal structure lets you control its output.

Critical Edge

Synthetic worlds, tiny scales (22.6M–125M) only. Whether this decoupling holds for emergent structure in billion-parameter natural-text models is the explicitly flagged, untested open question.

Methodology

a causal-world testbed with exact interventional ground truth

Every world is a small structural causal model with computable, exact interventional ground truth; each query carries a known evidence type (identity / child / relation / sign / confidence / block-reserved), removing lexical and frequency confounds. The editable object is a typed mechanism library: a tensor of N slots statically partitioned over evidence types (N=600, 6 types at 125M; N=200, 3 types at 22.6M; floor β=0.3), each slot carrying an edge list, sign bits, and scalar parameters — the model’s only writable causal state. A gating head routes tokens under three arms — type (supervised, λg=0.1), emergent (free), blocks (structural prior) — with Gumbel noise, an entropy floor, and load balancing. A 116.66M-parameter causal-LM backbone is trained against a parameter-matched monolith (MONO). Every MI claim uses 2,000-permutation tests, and each headline claim binds to a preregistered, md5-chained criterion archived before its data.

The “moving null.” The blocks structural-prior control sits on the null at 22.6M (z=+0.14) but carries weak stable organization at 125M (z=+2.38, +0.050 nats). The unsupervised null itself moves with scale — a caution for any cross-scale localization claim.

Strengths

why the evidence is hard to dismiss

  • Audit discipline. Frozen criteria, hash-chained scripts, and a fully published failed gate set a methodological bar the field should adopt.
  • Negative results stay prominent. H-α, the failed tgC probe, and the ROME generalization failure (0.855 → 0.124) are all headlined, not buried.
  • Exportable methodology. The “moving null” and the paired-permutation attribution protocol are released as a reusable template.
  • Construction-guaranteed properties. Zero LM cost, exactly-zero collateral, and bit-exact reversibility — toolchain passed its own validation before criterion data existed.
  • Self-scoping framing. Repeatedly states what the evidence does not support rather than overclaiming.

Limitations

where the boundary stops holding

  • Synthetic data only. Conclusions about naturalistic settings require further work the paper explicitly flags.
  • Two small scales, one family. 22.6M and 125M; the scale-window claim is only 5.6×.
  • No behavioral editability claimed. The library is a routing index with exact state-level semantics — not an editable world model.
  • A21 (persistence under continued training) is pending. No data yet; the “trust over time” claim is incomplete.
  • Confounded baseline shift. Scale and type-count co-vary (200→600 slots, 3→6 types), so the moving-null’s cause is unresolved.
  • Decoupling “why” is speculation. The addressing-vs-computation explanation is labeled a hypothesis, not established by data.

Questions for Further Exploration

open threads the paper itself surfaces

Does the boundary hold for emergent structure in much larger natural-text models, or only for the engineered, supervised library?
Can H-α be bridged by training the readout to consume slot codes — and does that trade off against training stability?
Does the moving null generalize beyond this architecture family, and can scale vs. type-count vs. capacity be deconfounded?
What does A21 reveal about persistence of edits under continued gradient updates?
Does the boundary soften, sharpen, or vanish at billion-parameter scale — and does the ≈1.5× amplification continue or saturate?
