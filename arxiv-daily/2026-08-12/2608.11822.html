Located but not Releasable: Silent Gate Inversion and Bounded Linear Release

Xining Xun · Tsingjiao Information Science
mechanistic-interpretability activation-steering representation-behavior-gap negative-results preregistration
The finding

A "knows-but-doesn't-use" circuit is located precisely — yet release fails for two independent reasons: silent gate inversion and a bounded linear family.

Key metric · gate inversion
0/2,400

OOD gate triggers on the CONF50 generations that needed intervention — while firing on 6.9–7.3% of held-out ID40 generations where intervention is harmful (cap 2%).

Why this matters

An end-to-end test of whether located activations can be steered into behavior — a rigorously audited counterexample reshaping activation-steering practice.

⚠ Critical edge

Every number comes from one 25.7M model on a synthetic domain — only the dissociation pattern, not the numbers, is a candidate to generalize.

01

Methodology

方法

C1 is a frozen 25.7M-parameter GPT-style decoder (8 layers, vocab 41) trained on causal-evidence discrimination: prompts present observational evidence under a structural causal model and the model answers interventional queries do(X=x₀) → Y=?. The training set embeds Simpson-trap worlds where observational correlation and causal effect have opposite signs. The suppression phenomenon of interest: on confounded worlds the model tracks the observational association even though the causal structure is linearly decodable from its residual stream. No parameter of C1 is ever updated.

The detect-localize-release pipeline runs four steps: detect a linear probe over PCA(k=8) site features vs. a frozen threshold; contrast a second forward pass with evidence zeroed at observation channels; generate a direction δ (calibration-frozen mean-difference, or per-instance δ(x) with zero learned parameters); inject a residual modification h ← h + λ·δ at the site. Localization uses activation transplant (oracle access). Release is measured by R = (Δ_p − Δ_same)/(Δ_zero − Δ_same). Fixed eval sets: CONF50, ID40, 40-world calibration.

03

Strengths & Limitations

优势与局限

Strengths

  • Preregistration is load-bearing. Every terminal verdict traces to a hashed artifact with a pre-written claim template; decision-tree branches were frozen before data, foreclosing post-hoc re-narration.
  • The decomposition is the insight. Localization, gating, and linear release fail for independent reasons — no single failure licenses the right conclusion.
  • "Silent gating failure" is independently important: any triggered intervention (selective steering, refusal circuits) can fail this quietly; standard held-out metrics miss it unless the trigger itself is audited.
  • Documented self-correction of the B6.5 misread in the audit log — used as evidence for the very recommendation made.
  • Cheap & reproducible: 4.22 GPU-hours, public frozen checkpoint, released per-world records.

Limitations

  • Single small synthetic-domain model. Only C1 (25.7M); numbers are not claimed to transfer — only the dissociation pattern. Larger / NL replication is future work.
  • Linear, single-site, residual-additive only. The negative release claim does not address nonlinear, multi-site, or non-additive mechanisms.
  • Aggressive eligibility filtering. 39/50 Stage-1 worlds excluded; localization eligibility 7.9% (48/608) — rates describe a selected subpopulation.
  • Light detector class. OOD-inverted-gate result uses PCA(k=8)+logistic; more expressive or positive-class-calibrated detectors untested.
  • Oracle access in localization. Transplant uses the released counterpart — it shows sufficiency in principle, not a deployable pipeline.
04

Questions for Further Exploration

待探索
  1. Q1Does the OOD-inverted-detector failure replicate in larger natural-language models and real steering tasks (ITI truthfulness, refusal-direction gating), or is it specific to C1's synthetic domain and light detector?
  2. Q2At the verified L23obs site, can a nonlinear or multi-site release mechanism close the ~0.18 intercept gap the entire linear family could not — under the same preregistered discipline?
  3. Q3How would existing steering benchmarks (CAA, ITI, RepE) change their conclusions if silent gating failures were audited rather than averaged over generations?
  4. Q4Is the bounded-linear-release result a property of the linear parametrization, of single-site resolution, or of C1's geometry? A CKA / SAE analysis could distinguish these.

一句话总结：定位成功（移植激活恢复 89% 行为），但"释放"在两处独立失败——OOD 检测器在 2,400 个需干预样本上触发 0 次，线性方向族剂量–响应在远低于阈值处饱和。
