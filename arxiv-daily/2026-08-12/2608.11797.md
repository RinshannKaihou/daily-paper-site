> **一句话总结：** 通过对合并 LLM 的逐层交叉项做精确分解与因果消融，本文证明 task-vector 干扰在功能上由其**传播方向**承担而非大小——传输放大占逐层通量约 69%，沿正确方向消融可剂量依赖地移除约 19% 的表达干扰，而同范数错误方向反而使其恶化；指令模板会把内部被放大的干扰在输出端"淹没"约 20 倍，使格式包装的评测读到的是"门"而不是干扰本身。

> **TL;DR:** This single-author mechanistic-interpretability paper intervenes on the exact layerwise cross-term `I_ℓ` of LoRA-merged Qwen2.5-1.5B (with Llama-3.2-1B and Qwen2.5-7B replications) to show that merging interference is **transported and amplified along a direction** (transport ≈69% of the flux, gain 1.08/late block) rather than *produced* in magnitude. Cross-term erasure at λ=1 removes ~19% of expressed JSD interference dose-dependently while every norm-matched structural control fails or backfires; task pairs whose float32 local generation differs by ≤1.9× differ by **14×–337× in causally removable interference**. An Alpaca instruction wrapper internally amplifies the cross-term yet collapses expressed interference ~20× (+6.91pp→+0.34pp) by inflating the main-effect denominator ~13×, so format-wrapped evaluations read the gate, not the interference. All 46 predictions were preregistered (14 in the main study), and two of three headline expectations were falsified and reported as such.

## 核心贡献

- **重新定位干扰的因果来源**：把模型合并中 task-vector 的"非加性残差"（逐层交叉项 `I_ℓ`）作为唯一分析对象，提出精确分解 `I_{ℓ+1} = G_ℓ + T_ℓ + M_ℓ`（本层生成 vs 传输 vs 边际路径耦合），证明传输而非新生成主导了通量（Qwen 约 69%，Llama 约 65%，传输增益 >1）。
- **首次激活级因果干预**：对 cross-term 本身做有向消融（`h^AB_ℓ − λI_ℓ`），配 4 个同范数结构性对照（wrong-pair / coefficient-mismatch / cross-prompt / random）和 5 点剂量阶梯，证明效果仅由**方向**决定，并随剂量饱和于精确消融处。
- **"吸引子"机制**：预注册的重生长实验显示，早期层消融后传播会把 cross-term 在范数（ρ≈0.99）与方向（cosine 0.99）上几乎完全重建，basin 测试（6 个起点）收敛于 cosine 0.83–0.99——说明传播主动维护这一方向，且只有靠近输出层的晚期消融才留下持久因果效果。
- **表达门控（expression gating）**：指令模板在内部放大 cross-term 生成（与原始格式反方向），却把输出端表达干扰压低约 20 倍；同一次消融在 wrapper 下只能移除 13 倍更少的干扰。结论：**格式包装的评测读的是"门"而非"干扰"**。
- **数量级警告**：naive bf16 测量的"普适 ±15%"生成本质是量化粗糙度（fp32 复算后缩到 bf16 值的 14–23%），是一个独立的方法学警示。
- **极致的预注册与可复现性**：46 条预测在数据出现前冻结于版本化日志；2/3 的头条预期被证伪并据实报告；附带 1,296+90+54 个不可变 cell 记录、确定性 null、ULP 噪声地板、bitwise 等价审计。

## Key Contributions

- **Relocates the causal locus of interference**: Defines the layerwise cross-term `I_ℓ = h^AB_ℓ − h^A0_ℓ − h^0B_ℓ + h^00_ℓ` (the exact non-additive residue of merging) and an exact per-layer decomposition `I_{ℓ+1} = G_ℓ + T_ℓ + M_ℓ`, attributing ≈69% (Qwen) / ≈65% (Llama) of the flux to transport of the carried term with late-network gain 1.08/block (2.3× the regeneration share), against cumulative magnitudes that exceed even the perfectly-coherent accumulation bound by 2.6×.
- **First activation-level causal intervention on task-vector cross-terms**: Replaces the merged residual state with `h^AB_ℓ − λI_ℓ` over a 5-point dose ladder, against four norm-matched structural controls (wrong-pair, coefficient-mismatch, cross-prompt, distance-matched random). Direction-specific removal saturates near λ=1; structured wrong directions **backfire** monotonically.
- **Establishes the carried cross-term as an attractor**: A preregistered regrowth experiment shows early erasure is rebuilt to ρ≈0.99 / direction cosine 0.99 by propagation; a basin test (six starting displacements including 2× overshoot) reconverges at cosine 0.83–0.99. Durable causal effect concentrates late (depths 20–25), exactly where remaining depth cannot rebuild.
- **Identifies expression gating by prompt format**: An Alpaca instruction wrapper *amplifies* internal cross-term generation (reversing the authors' own 60%-prior prediction, 3/3 seeds) while collapsing expressed interference ~20× (+6.91pp→+0.34pp); the same erasure finds 13× less removable interference when wrapped. The decomposition shows the collapse is denominator-driven (main-effect JSD inflates ~13×: 0.025→0.32) — the wrapper drowns the interaction rather than destroying it.
- **Methodological caution on bf16**: Recomputing `G_ℓ` in float32 shrinks it to 14–23% of its bf16 value with near-zero direction correlation (median cosine 0.15–0.24); 75–90% of naively-measured "local generation" is weight-rounding roughness. Flagged as a standalone warning for activation-difference analyses run at training precision.

## Methodology

The setup follows a companion measurement study (Zhu, 2026, arXiv:2608.09490). **Base models**: Qwen2.5-1.5B (primary), Llama-3.2-1B and Qwen2.5-7B (reduced replications, predictions frozen before any adapter on those points existed). **Task vectors**: six tasks (math via GSM8K-like [Cobbe et al.], code via Alpaca-style data, instruction, safety via PKU-SafeRLHF, summarization via XSum, translation via OPUS), each finetuned as rank-16 LoRA, three training seeds, norm-matched to a global core-median norm before composition. Merged models `θ0 + αΔA + βΔB` are evaluated on a 6×6 interior grid of (α,β) ∈ (0,1.2]².

**Layerwise ledger (§3.2)**: For a prompt, record the residual state at the input of every block under four weight settings (base M00, Mα0, M0β, Mαβ). The cumulative cross-term `I_ℓ` is the four-corner difference. To separate *generated* from *transported*, all four block functions are evaluated on a common additive reference state `h̄_ℓ = h^α0_ℓ + h^0β_ℓ − h^00_ℓ`; the four-corner output difference is `G_ℓ` (local generation). The exact identity `I_{ℓ+1} = G_ℓ + P_ℓ` defines the uninterpreted remainder, which §4.2 further decomposes bitwise into transport `T_ℓ` plus marginal mismatch `M_ℓ`.

**Output estimand (§3.3)**: Expressed interference is the interaction ratio `R = mean JSD(p^αβ, p^add) / mean JSD(p^αβ, p^00)`, a ratio-of-means (never mean-of-ratios). Causal effect of an intervention is `D = R_original − R_patched`, with the unpatched main-effect denominator frozen in both terms so D moves for exactly one reason.

**Cross-term erasure (§3.4)**: At a frozen boundary, replace `h^AB_ℓ` with `h^AB_ℓ − λI_ℓ`, λ ∈ {0, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2}. Five boundaries {3, 8, 14, 20, 25} on the 28-block Qwen. Every control is per-prompt norm-matched to ∥I_ℓ(x)∥ and overlaps `cos(d, −I_ℓ)` are disclosed so direction-mediation is checked rather than assumed.

**Numerical validity (§3.5)**: Because the four-corner difference is a catastrophic cancellation (paths differ at O(α) while `I_ℓ` is O(αβ)), every estimator carries its own propagated-ULP floor (computed in float64, validated by the exact 2¹⁵ ratio between fp32 and bf16 mantissa widths), determinism nulls (1,296/1,296 bitwise zeros), and finite-difference convergence checks. The bf16/fp32 dtype audit (v1.6 freeze) is what surfaced the quantization-roughness finding.

**Preregistration**: hypotheses, thresholds, layer positions, dose ladders, and control constructions were frozen in a versioned log (v1.0–v1.6) before the corresponding data; 46 predictions total, 14 in the main study, with 7 held / 4 failed / 3 split, all reported by the letter of their frozen criteria.

## Results & Highlights

- **Transport dominates generation.** Exact decomposition over 36 preregistered cells attributes ≈69% (Qwen2.5-1.5B, regeneration share 0.307–0.311, 3/3 seeds) and ≈65% (Llama-3.2-1B, regeneration share 0.35) of the layerwise cross-term flux to **transport of the existing term**; late-network transport exceeds regeneration by 2.3× (RMS 0.32 vs 0.14), with transport gain ∥T_ℓ∥/∥I_ℓ∥ = 1.08/block (Qwen) / 1.14/block (Llama), 3/3 seeds. Energy-accounting transport share (0.83–0.89) confirms the attribution is accounting-independent.
- **Cumulative magnitude exceeds the coherent accumulation bound by 2.6×** (Qwen: observed ∥I₂₈∥ = 0.974 vs. perfectly-aligned bound 0.369 and incoherent bound 0.088); the exclusion strengthens at scale — **1.80–8.31×** on Qwen2.5-7B and **2.2–7.9×** on Llama-3.2-1B — so no per-layer injection account survives.
- **Cross-term erasure removes ≈19% of expressed interference dose-dependently.** At λ=1 on code prompts (code+safety), `D_correct = +0.0301 / +0.0289 / +0.0160` across three seeds, while first-token top-1 agreement stays 0.94–0.99 and patched-state JSD from unpatched (0.003) is two orders below the main effect (0.236). The dose ladder saturates near λ=1 (0→+0.017→+0.025→+0.026→+0.020 for λ=0,0.5,1,1.5,2).
- **Every norm-matched structural control fails or backfires.** Specificity contrasts are positive 3/3 seeds: +0.049 to +0.059 vs wrong-pair, +0.058 to +0.072 vs coefficient-mismatch, +0.026 to +0.046 vs cross-prompt, +0.021 to +0.037 vs random; the cross-prompt direction worsens monotonically (0→−0.001→−0.012→−0.034→−0.066), proving the effect sign is decided by direction alone. 27/28 prompt-bootstrap CIs (5,000 paired draws/seed) exclude zero.
- **Causally removable interference differs 14×–337× between task pairs whose float32 local generation differs by ≤1.9×.** On the same code prompts, erasure removes `D = +0.0301/+0.0289/+0.0160` for code+safety but only `+0.0022/+0.0001/+0.0005` for code+math — the paper's central quantitative contrast locating the pair difference in orientation rather than size.
- **Instruction wrappers collapse expressed interference ~20× while amplifying internal generation.** Output-side expressed interference drops from +6.91pp (raw) to +0.34pp (Alpaca-wrapped); the wrapper inflates the main-effect JSD denominator ~13× (0.025→0.32) while the absolute interaction JSD does not shrink. The identical λ=1 erasure finds `D_raw ≈ +0.028` but `D_wrapped ≈ +0.002` — **13× less to remove** under the wrapper, 3/3 seeds.
- **bf16 "universal ±15%" generation is 75–90% quantization roughness.** Recomputing `G_ℓ` in float32 shrinks it to 14–23% of the bf16 value (median fp32/bf16 RMS ratio 0.230 code+safety / 0.136 code+math), with direction cosines median 0.239/0.152 — near-uncorrelated with the bf16 direction.
- **The carried cross-term is an attractor.** Regrowth after λ=1 erasure: ρ = 1.000/0.992/0.944/0.843 erasing at depths 3/8/14/20, direction cosine ≥0.986; a basin test from six starting displacements reconverges to cosine 0.83–0.99 (18/18). Durable causal effect concentrates late: D≈0 at depths 3–8, rising to +0.026 to +0.058 at depths 20–25.
- **Existing magnitude metrics are coarse on pairs and blind to the gate.** CTL linearity is uniformly 0.93–0.98 (separates nothing); parameter cosine held-out Spearman ρ = +0.25 (4/8 bin calls); cumulative ∥I∥ attains held-out ρ = +0.63–0.65 (8/8) but still cannot see the 13× format gate; SurgeryV2's representation bias orders pairs (2.9×) but moves only −1% to +34% on the format axis where expressed interference collapses ~95%.
- **Scale point Qwen2.5-7B sharpens the contrast and closes the gate.** Output pair ratios 6.3–12.2× and format ratios 6.3–7.5× against a G-band spread of ≤1.30; expression gating becomes **total** (`D_wrapped` indistinguishable from zero, all CIs containing zero, 3/3 seeds); pair removable-interference ratios 6.9–22.9×.

## Authors & Credibility

Single author: **Chencheng Zhu**, a student at UNSW Sydney (`chencheng.zhu@student.unsw.edu.au`), explicitly flagged as a student email. The work cites a companion measurement study by the same author (arXiv:2608.09490, "When do task vectors interfere?"), and the present paper's training/measurement protocol is explicitly inherited from that companion — meaning a portion of the experimental scaffolding (e.g., the +6.91pp/+0.34pp format-collapse numbers) is established externally rather than re-derived here. Credibility rests almost entirely on the rigor of the preregistration and numerical-validity apparatus rather than on institutional backing: a versioned freeze chain (six versions, each superseding change logged), 1,296+90+54 immutable per-cell results, bitwise determinism nulls, propagated-ULP floors validated by the exact 2¹⁵ fp32/bf16 mantissa ratio, and the explicit reporting of two-of-three falsified headline predictions. The reproducibility statement commits to releasing the full freeze chain, immutable records, software stack, and pinned base-model commit. No peer review is indicated (preprint, arXiv:2608.11797v1). An "AI usage" statement discloses LLM assistance for code and drafting, with the standard caveat that all hypotheses, predictions, and verdicts were author-specified and no LLM generated or selected experimental data.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: Genuinely high and well-scoped. Prior merging diagnostics (TSV, NeuroMerging, SurgeryV2, APL, RAIN-Merging) operate in *parameter* space or at the *behavioral* level; CTL (Zhou et al. 2024) is the closest prior work and observes the cross-term is small but contains no intervention, no format factor, and no propagation analysis. This paper's claim to be "the first activation-level causal intervention on task-vector cross-terms" (Table 1) is defensible, and the framing of direction-vs-magnitude as the load-bearing distinction is fresh.
- **Rigor**: Exceptional, possibly to a fault. The catastrophic-cancellation handling (per-estimator propagated-ULP floors, determinism nulls, bitwise equivalence audits that caught a real `logits_to_keep=1` GEMM-order bug and an `output_hidden_states` space-mixing bug before any confirmatory data) is a model for the field. The 46-prediction preregistration with reported falsifications — including the authors' own 60%-prior format prediction that *reversed* 3/3 — is exactly the kind of intellectual honesty that rare-mechanism claims require.
- **Reproducibility**: Strong on paper (pinned commits, deterministic kernels, single-A100 hardware lock, released freeze chain) but **three seeds remain the outer replication unit**, and CIs are within-seed. The three-model-point grid (Qwen2.5-1.5B, Llama-3.2-1B, Qwen2.5-7B) is a real strength but all use **rank-16 LoRA only** — full finetuning is explicitly left open, and the headline 13×/20× figures are *scoped to Qwen2.5-1.5B* (Llama shows only ~2× gating; one seed's contrast CI crosses zero). Anyone citing the 13× as a general result is overreading.
- **Significance**: The "format-wrapped evaluations read the gate, not the interference" claim is the most actionable finding for practitioners — it directly indicts standard instruction-template evaluations of merged models and aligns with behavior-level RAIN-Merging observations. The transport/attractor mechanism gives a concrete design corollary (erasure-style mitigation must act late).
- **Red flags / weak points**: (1) **Behavioral validation is a null result, honestly reported**: discrete unit-test/reference-containment scoring was a null instrument at both 1.5B and 7B (e.g., the code expert itself scored 0.00–0.08), and a continuous NLL endpoint passed its validity gate but *falsified behavioral recovery* (11/12 control contrasts went the wrong way). The paper therefore claims only "expressed non-additivity," not task performance — readers wanting "merging works better" will not find it here. (2) **The 14×–337× pair-ratio headline is disclosed to be inflated**: "the middle value is inflated by a near-zero denominator," and the authors flag the direction as unambiguous but the exact magnitude as unstable. (3) **The cheap directional predictor that would make this actionable was not found**: all three preregistered direction features failed on held-out pairs (the best, ρ=+0.62, was contaminated into being ∝ 1/∥I∥, a magnitude signal in disguise). So the causal direction is established only by intervention, not by any deployable metric. (4) Heavy **dependency on the companion paper** for the output-side setup means a reader must consult arXiv:2608.09490 for several load-bearing numbers. (5) Gating evidence rests on **one wrapper template (Alpaca) and two core task pairs** as the primary estimand; the multi-wrapper generalization (Appendix J) is encouraging (17/18 conditions) but one condition missed the frozen threshold.

## Strengths

- Methodological rigor is at the top of the field: per-estimator noise floors, determinism nulls, bitwise equivalence audits that caught two real implementation bugs pre-data, and an exact 2¹⁵-validated ULP-floor computation.
- Preregistration with reported falsifications (2 of 3 headline expectations, plus the bf16 "real signal" prior) sets a standard for mechanism papers that almost no merging literature meets.
- Four norm-matched structural controls plus a dose ladder is a genuinely strong causal-identification design; the cross-prompt derangement is a thoughtful direct null for input-conditioning.
- Multi-scale (7B) and multi-family (Llama) replications with predictions frozen *before any adapter on those points existed* — the cross-family finding that transport dominance and the format inversion are invariants while magnitude-pair-tracking is itself family-dependent is more honest and more informative than a universal claim.
- The bf16-as-rounding-roughness caution is a standalone, broadly useful methodological warning for anyone doing activation-difference analyses.

## Limitations

- **LoRA-only, rank-16**: full finetuning and other adapter parameterizations are untested; the mechanism's strength (especially the 13×/20× gate) is explicitly a family property, not universal.
- **Behavioral endpoints failed to validate**: discrete scoring was a null instrument, and the continuous NLL endpoint *falsified* behavioral recovery. The paper claims only next-token-distribution non-additivity, not downstream task improvement.
- **Three seeds as the outer unit**; within-seed CIs only. The 14×–337× pair ratio is unstable (middle value near-zero-denominator-inflated).
- **No deployable directional metric found**: all three preregistered direction features failed on held-out pairs; erasure as a mitigation method is explicitly "unevaluated."
- **Narrow gating scope**: one wrapper family (Alpaca primary, two extra phrasings in Appendix J), two core task pairs; first-token estimand only; explicit "no safety-behavior claims."
- **Single author, preprint, no peer review**; the load-bearing output-side numbers (+6.91pp/+0.34pp) come from a companion paper that the reader must consult.
- **Heavy computational specificity**: results are locked to a single A100 with sdpa deterministic kernels and TF32 off — the authors warn this is "not pedantry," implying transferability to other hardware is unverified.

## Relevance

与你的"LLM 隐藏状态与可解释性"和"计算可靠性与精度误差"两个方向高度契合：本文正是对合并模型逐层激活做机制可解释性（激活级 patching、方向介导、吸引子分析），而其中 bf16/fp16 量化粗糙度、catastrophic cancellation、ULP 噪声地板的整套数值有效性审计，直接落入你关注的浮点精度/数值稳定性议题；"格式包装评测读的是门而非干扰"这一结论也与你"推理评测一致性与可复现性"的兴趣相关。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the transport-dominance / attractor structure survive **full finetuning** (not just rank-16 LoRA), where the cross-term magnitude and direction structure could differ substantially?
- Can a **cheap, deployable directional predictor** be engineered (all three preregistered features failed on held-out pairs) — e.g., one that probes the late-network carried direction without the four-corner overhead?
- The paper shows erasure controls *next-token-distribution non-additivity* but not behavior: would **late-depth erasure as a merging-repair method** actually improve downstream task accuracy, or does the ~19% interference reduction not project onto the expert direction?
- The 13×/20× gating is scoped to Qwen2.5-1.5B and weakens (~2×) on Llama-3.2-1B — what **architectural property** (GQA, tokenizer, hidden width, norm scale) controls gate strength, and is the total-closure seen at 7B a scale effect or a family effect?
- Does the **expression-gating critique generalize beyond merging** to other format-sensitive evaluations (e.g., RLHF preference benchmarks, safety evals) where a template-pinned main effect could similarly mask internal interactions?
- The companion paper (arXiv:2608.09490) establishes the output-side format collapse — does the mechanism here (denominator-driven drowning, ~13× main-effect inflation) **predict which wrapper templates will gate hardest**, and can it be inverted into a wrapper-design principle?
