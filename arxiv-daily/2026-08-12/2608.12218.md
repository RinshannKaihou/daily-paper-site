> **一句话总结：** 本文提出"信息丰裕悖论"——训练时上下文越相关、越长，模型就越倾向于从上下文取用信息而非把知识写进参数，导致预训练性能呈倒 U 型、且在缺失或误导性上下文下鲁棒性显著下降；机制上梯度压力从 FFN 转向注意力头。

> **TL;DR:** The paper posits an "Information Abundance Paradox": informative long training context lets the model lower loss by reading from context rather than encoding knowledge in weights. In token-matched pretraining on Project Gutenberg (20M–750M Llama-2 models), SuperGLUE/MCQA peak at a context window of W=2048 and language modeling at W=8192, then decline (inverted-U). In LoRA fine-tuning of Qwen3 (0.6B–14B) on MMLU-Pro, raising target-domain documents from k=0 to k=8 improves supporting-context accuracy but cuts no-context accuracy by up to -6.3 pts and widens the supporting–conflicting gap by +18.0 to +42.8 pts. Mechanistically the FFN/SA gradient-norm ratio drops significantly (e.g., 259M slope -0.054 per doubling, p=0.0016; significant in 19/20 SFT comparisons) and causal module-restricted tuning confirms attention updates drive context reliance.

## 核心贡献

- **提出"信息丰裕悖论"(Information Abundance Paradox)**：明确刻画了训练上下文并非中性数据通道——当上下文中存在任务相关信息时，模型会降低损失的方式是直接利用上下文而非把信息编码进参数，从而把学习模式从"参数化内化"推向"上下文化"。该现象的行为表征被命名为"context addiction"（上下文成瘾）。
- **预训练侧的实证（倒 U 型曲线）**：在 token 预算、数据、模型配置完全匹配下，跨 20M–750M 四个规模扫描上下文窗口 W∈{512…32768}，发现 SuperGLUE 与 MCQA 在 W≈2048 达峰、语言建模在 W≈8192 达峰，之后持续下降，且更的大模型容量并不能消除该退化。
- **SFT 侧的相关性可控实验**：固定上下文预算 n=8、只改变其中目标域文档数 k∈{0,4,8}，证明真正驱动成瘾的是上下文的"任务相关性"而非 token 数量本身。
- **理论刻画**：给出"参数信息前沿"(parametric information frontier) 与单调性命题，证明更长上下文可以单调降低达到同一风险阈值所需的权重内任务信息量（可达性结果）。
- **机制层面的多层证据**：合成任务表明成瘾仅在"更短上下文提供更低复杂度解"时出现（梯度范数下降）；模块级梯度分析显示信息性上下文把更新压力从 FFN 转向自注意力；模块受限微调与 token 级注意力分析提供因果证据，表明上下文训练使模型在推理时更重度地依赖上下文 token。

## Key Contributions

- **Formulates the Information Abundance Paradox**, an explicit hypothesis that the train-time context window is not a neutral conduit: when task-relevant information is available in context, the model reduces loss by using it directly instead of encoding it in weights, shifting learning from parametric internalization toward contextualization. The observable behavioral signature is termed "context addiction."
- **Demonstrates an inverted-U in token-matched pretraining** across four scales (20M–750M, Llama-2 architecture, 10B Project Gutenberg tokens, W swept over 7 powers of two), peaking at W≈2048 for SuperGLUE/MCQA and W≈8192 for language modeling, with degradation that greater model capacity does not remove.
- **Isolates relevance rather than length in SFT**: by fixing the context budget at n=8 documents and only varying the number of target-domain documents k∈{0,4,8} across four MMLU-Pro domains on Qwen3 (0.6B–14B), the paper shows the shift is driven by task-relevant information, not extra tokens per se.
- **Provides a theoretical account** via a "parametric information frontier" I_k(ρ) and a monotonicity proposition (longer context weakly reduces the minimum task information that must live in the weights to hit a risk threshold ρ).
- **Supplies converging mechanistic evidence**: a synthetic-task study tying context addiction to lower gradient-norm (lower-complexity) solutions; module-level FFN-to-SA gradient ratios that fall with longer/more-relevant context; causal module-restricted fine-tuning (FFN-only vs SA-only); and inference-time attention-mass analyses showing heavier reliance on context tokens, concentrated in middle layers.

## Methodology

The paper operationalizes "information abundance" differently in two regimes and then explains the findings theoretically and mechanistically.

**Pretraining study (§3.1).** Llama-2-architecture models at 20M/55M/259M/750M parameters are pretrained on 10B tokens drawn from Project Gutenberg, restricted to documents with ≥65536 tokens so that a larger window exposes longer coherent within-document spans (not just cross-document packing). For each scale the context window is swept over W∈{512,1024,2048,4092,8192,16384,32768} while the global batch (~1.05M tokens, 9537 steps), initialization, seed, and optimizer are held fixed — making the comparison token-and-update-matched (but not FLOP-matched, since attention is quadratic). Evaluation is zero-shot on a language-modeling suite (LAMBADA, WikiSPAN, Penn Treebank), SuperGLUE, and closed-book MCQA (ARC, CommonsenseQA, PIQA, etc.).

**Supervised fine-tuning study (§3.2).** Qwen3 (0.6B–14B) is LoRA-tuned (adapters on both attention and FFNs; loss only on answer tokens) on four MMLU-Pro domains (Health, Economics, Law, Psychology), each split 80/20 into train/eval. A fixed context budget of n=8 documents is held constant while the number of target-domain documents k∈{0,4,8} varies (the remaining 8−k come from other domains). Evaluation uses three test-time conditions: supporting context, conflicting context (documents support a wrong answer), and no context.

**Theory (§4).** A latent task variable τ indexes the data-generating distribution; I(W;τ)=H(τ)−H(τ|W) measures task information stored in weights. The "parametric information frontier" I_k(ρ) is the infimum of I(W;τ) over weight posteriors/predictors achieving risk ≤ρ. Because shorter contexts are aligned subwindows of longer ones (projection map T_{k,m}), Proposition 4.2 proves I_m(ρ)≤I_k(ρ): enlarging context weakly expands the feasible set and so can only lower the minimum parametric information needed.

**Mechanisms (§5).** (1) A synthetic pretraining study on four tasks (bitwise ops, string ops, mod10 arithmetic, Caesar cipher) at three scales (0.3M/1.5M/7.5M) tests whether context addiction emerges selectively where longer context lowers solution complexity, using average training gradient norm as a complexity proxy. (2) Module-level FFN-to-SA gradient-norm ratios characterize where update pressure concentrates, in both pretraining and SFT. (3) Module-restricted fine-tuning (update only FFNs or only attention) gives a causal intervention. (4) Token-level attention mass on context tokens during inference localizes the effect to middle layers.

## Results & Highlights

- **Motivating observation on production models (Figure 1):** longer-context Phi-3 (128K vs 4K) and OLMo 3 (65K vs 8K) variants consistently underperform their short-context counterparts; the gap is significant in 20 of 23 reported comparisons, with the worst case being OLMo 3 32B zero-shot MCQA where the 65K variant is Δ = −15.9% behind the 8K variant.
- **Pretraining inverted-U (Figure 2):** under a fixed 10B-token budget, SuperGLUE and MCQA accuracy peak at a context window of W=2048 and language-modeling loss bottoms out at W=8192, then degrade as W grows to 32768. The optimum tracks evaluation-instance length (Table 5/6: MCQA mean length 65 → optimum W=2048; SuperGLUE mean 135 → W=2048; WikiSPAN mean 504 → W=8192; PTB optimum W=512), and the inflection is statistically significant at every model scale (segmented-regression tests, App. F.2). The pattern persists from 20M to 750M parameters, so greater capacity does not remove the degradation.
- **SFT context addiction (Figure 3, Table 8):** raising target-domain documents from k=0 to k=8 in Qwen3 (0.6B–14B) on MMLU-Pro improves supporting-context accuracy but degrades no-context accuracy by −5.4 (0.6B, p=0.0074), −3.6 (1.7B), −5.3 (4B), and −6.3 points (14B, p=0.0062), while the supporting−conflicting accuracy gap widens by +18.0 to +42.8 points; the ordered effect is significant in 9 of 10 comparisons (8B no-context is the lone non-significant case, p=0.0645).
- **Synthetic-task complexity link (Figure 4, Table 9):** context addiction (growing supporting−conflicting gap) appears only for bitwise ops and string ops, which also show decreasing average gradient norm (String Ops slope −0.641, p=6.26×10⁻¹⁰; Bitwise −0.409, p=0.0037), whereas mod10 and Caesar cipher show no significant gradient-norm trend (Caesar p=0.0704) and remain robust — tying addiction to a lower-complexity context-based solution.
- **Module-level gradient shift (Figure 5, Tables 10–11):** the FFN/SA gradient-norm ratio falls significantly with context length in pretraining (259M slope −0.054 per doubling, p=0.0016; significant at all four scales) and is lower with k=8 vs no-context SFT in 19 of 20 model-domain comparisons — i.e., informative context reallocates update pressure from FFNs (parametric knowledge) to self-attention (contextualization).
- **Causal module-restricted fine-tuning (Figure 6, Table 12):** training only FFNs improves no-context robustness and shrinks conflicting-context damage, whereas training only attention heads boosts supporting-context accuracy but increases conflict sensitivity (e.g., Qwen3-4B: FFN-only supporting−conflicting trend +2.2, p=0.0135 vs SA-only −12.0, p=1.49×10⁻⁷), causally confirming the gradient-allocation interpretation.
- **Inference-time attention (Figure 7):** models fine-tuned with k=8 target-domain documents assign significantly more attention mass to context tokens at test time, concentrated in middle layers, across all four domains.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** High and well-scoped. While "two modes of learning" (in-weights vs in-context) is established (Pan et al.; Lin & Lee; Chan et al.), prior work varies inference-time prompts, demo distributions, or scale — not the *training* context window. This paper's framing of the context window as governing the internalization/contextualization allocation, plus the parametric-information-frontier theorem, is a genuine and clean conceptual contribution. The Shi et al. (2026) account of non-monotonic context scaling as a predictive-value-vs-learning-difficulty tradeoff is acknowledged as complementary rather than contradicted.
- **Rigor:** Strong within its scope. Claims are backed by token-and-update-matched pretraining, controlled-relevance SFT, permutation/HAC significance tests, multiple random seeds, and causal module-restricted interventions rather than purely correlational gradient analyses. The theoretical proposition is correctly scoped as an *achievability* (monotonicity of the frontier) result, not a claim that SGD necessarily realizes it — the authors are upfront about this.
- **Reproducibility:** Likely good — code/artifacts badge, nanotron framework, full configs in App. C, deterministic generation, and exact W/k sweeps are specified. The main barrier is compute (pretraining four scales × seven windows), but the SFT and synthetic studies are cheap to reproduce.
- **Significance:** Conceptually important for the long-context-scaling community: it argues, with evidence, that "more long-context data" is not a sufficient answer to near-infinite-context models, and that train-time context trades away context-independent competence. Practically, it warns that retrieval-rich or long-document fine-tuning can quietly degrade closed-book robustness.
- **Red flags / weaknesses:**
  - **Single-domain pretraining data is a real quirk.** All pretraining uses one filtered subset of *Project Gutenberg* (literary text). The reported optima (W=2048 for MCQA/SuperGLUE, W=8192 for LM) are almost certainly domain-dependent — code or web/mixed corpora could shift or even remove the inverted-U. The paper does not test this, which materially limits how far the pretraining claim generalizes.
  - **Scale ceiling of 750M parameters.** The authors flag this themselves; whether the inflection persists, softens, or moves at 7B–70B+ (where most practical long-context training happens) is unverified. The motivating Phi-3/OLMo 3 evidence is correlational and confounded (the long and short variants differ in more than window length, e.g., data mixtures and RoPE scaling).
  - **FFN/SA gradient ratio is a proxy, not a direct measure of parametric knowledge** — the link to "factual knowledge in FFNs" is inherited from prior work and assumed rather than re-validated here.
  - **SFT covers only four MMLU-Pro domains** and uses an 80/20 in-domain split with synthetic document mixes; the magnitude of "context addiction" may be an artifact of how cleanly the target domain is separated from distractor domains.
  - The theory predicts monotonic *possibility* of reduced parametric information, but the empirical inverted-U (degradation, not just plateau) is stronger than what the achievability result alone licenses — the gap between theorem and the sharper empirical decline is not fully closed.

## Strengths

- Clean, falsifiable central hypothesis with a memorable name and a concrete behavioral test (context addiction = poor robustness under absent/misleading context).
- Genuinely controlled pretraining comparison (matched tokens, updates, init, seed, optimizer) — rare and valuable.
- Multi-level evidence (behavioral → theoretical → synthetic-complexity → gradient → causal module intervention → inference attention) that triangulates the same conclusion.
- Honest scoping of the theorem as an achievability result, and honest acknowledgment of the 750M scale limit.
- Practical, actionable implication: long-context/retrieval-rich fine-tuning can quietly erode closed-book competence and conflict-robustness.

## Limitations

- Pretraining experiments use only Project Gutenberg (literary domain); optima and the existence/strength of the inverted-U on code or web data are unknown.
- Scale capped at 750M parameters; behavior at frontier scales (7B+) is untested.
- The theoretical result establishes that longer context *can* reduce required parametric information, not that standard training *does*; the sharper empirical decline (degradation beyond the optimum, not just plateau) is under-explained by the theorem.
- The FFN↔parametric-knowledge and SA↔contextualization mapping is adopted from prior work and treated as assumption rather than re-proven.
- SFT evaluation is limited to four MMLU-Pro domains with constructed supporting/conflicting document sets, which may exaggerate how cleanly "relevant" vs "distractor" context can be separated in real retrieval settings.
- The motivating Phi-3/OLMo 3 observation is correlational; the long and short variants differ in data mixture and positional-encoding setup, not only in context length.

## Relevance

直接命中长上下文训练与参数化知识这一研究主线：它对"上下文窗口越大、长上下文数据越多就一定越好"的主流假设提出系统性质疑，并用受控预训练、可控相关性 SFT 与机制性因果干预给出统一解释，对长上下文模型训练策略、RAG/检索增强微调以及参数化知识的维持都有直接指导意义。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the inverted-U persist (or shift its optimum) on heterogeneous pretraining corpora (code, web mix, multilingual), and does mixing short- and long-context data during training remove the post-optimum degradation?
- At 7B–70B scale, does the inflection point move rightward, weaken, or disappear — i.e., is the paradox a small-model artifact or a fundamental limit?
- Can a curriculum or regularization (e.g., explicitly penalizing the FFN/SA gradient-ratio shift, or up-weighting closed-book loss during long-context training) recover parametric robustness without sacrificing long-context gains?
- How does the paradox interact with retrieval-augmented training and inference (RAG), where context is supplied at both train and test time but is noisy or partial?
- The parametric-information-frontier theorem is an achievability result; can it be strengthened to predict the *location* of the empirical inflection point, or to characterize which optimizers/data distributions actually realize the lower-parametric-information solution?
