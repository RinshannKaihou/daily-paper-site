> **一句话总结：** 首次系统刻画了层交错式混合线性注意力（HLA）大模型内部巨型激活（MA）的两种架构对齐形态——紧贴全注意力层前的"前注意力尖峰"（PAS）和贯通相邻尖峰间的"峰间高原"（ISP）；通过受控预训练证明二者均早期涌现、被全注意力输出门控强烈抑制但不消除，并提出"写入-汇聚-抵消"的统一系统离群值生命周期来同时解释 PAS、ISP 与纯全注意力模型的稳定 MA 形态。

> **TL;DR:** The first systematic study of massive activations (MAs) in layer-interleaved hybrid linear attention (HLA) LLMs identifies two architecture-aligned morphologies — pre-attention spikes (PAS) immediately before full attention layers and inter-spike plateaus (ISP) bridging them — that recur across 5 linear-attention backbones, 6 hybridization ratios, 5 data domains, and 1.2B–397B open-source hybrids (Kimi Linear, Qwen3.5, Nemotron-H, Zamba2). On controlled 340M/1.3B GDN models trained on FineWeb-Edu, sink–spike alignment reaches ~100% at 12:1 ratio, while inter-spike retention for GDN-1.3B climbs from 18.4% (12:1) to 77.8% (3:1); on RULER NIAH-1, early full-attention placement (layer 4) scores only 57.4 vs 90.6 for late placement (layer 20), and adding output gates to full attention collapses NIAH-2 from 99.4 to 16.8 without erasing the morphology. A shared write–sink–cancel systematic-outlier lifecycle, distinguished only by cancellation timing, unifies PAS, ISP, and the stable MA pattern of the full-attention Transformer baseline.

## 核心贡献

- **首次刻画 HLA LLM 的 MA 形态学**：在层交错式混合架构中发现两种此前未被识别的、与架构对齐的 MA 组织方式——紧贴每个全注意力层之前的局部极大值"前注意力尖峰"（PAS），以及随全注意力密度增大而贯通相邻 PAS 的"峰间高原"（ISP）。在纯全注意力极限下二者融合，恢复全注意力 Transformer 的稳定 MA 形态。
- **大规模、跨设定的可复现性证据**：覆盖 RetNet / HGRN / GLA / DeltaNet / GDN 五种线性注意力骨干、ρ∈{3,6,12,24} 四种混合比、五个数据域（WikiText-103、Scientific Papers、GSM8K、CodeSearchNet、FLORES-200），并在 Kimi Linear、Qwen3.5、Nemotron-H、Zamba2 等 1.2B–397B 开源混合模型上重现。
- **受控预训练揭示涌现与门控非对称性**：用 Flash Linear Attention 框架从零训练 340M/1.3B GDN 模型，证明 PAS/ISP 在训练早期（340M 仅 1B token）即涌现并随训练固化；对全注意力层加输出门强力衰减其绝对幅值但不消除层间组织，而移除 GDN 输出门只产生中等幅度放大——表明全注意力是组织 MA 动态的核心。
- **统一的"写入-汇聚-抵消"系统离群值生命周期**：将系统离群值分析从全注意力模型推广到 HLA，提出由"抵消时机"区分的统一机制——快速抵消产生局部 PAS，延迟抵消维持 ISP，全注意力极限下恢复稳定形态，从而把三种形态纳入同一连续谱。

## Key Contributions

- **First systematic characterization of MA morphology in HLA LLMs**: Identifies two previously unrecognized, architecture-aligned organizations — pre-attention spikes (PAS), local maxima forming immediately before each full attention layer, and inter-spike plateaus (ISP), elevated activations bridging successive PAS that strengthen as full attention becomes denser. At the full-attention limit (ρ=1) the two merge into the stable MA pattern of conventional full-attention Transformers.
- **Comprehensive recurrence evidence**: The two morphologies recur across five linear-attention backbones (RetNet, HGRN, GLA, DeltaNet, GDN), four hybridization ratios, five data domains (WikiText-103, Scientific Papers, GSM8K, CodeSearchNet, FLORES-200), and 12 open-source hybrid checkpoints spanning 1.2B–397B total parameters from four families (Kimi Linear, Qwen3.5, Nemotron-H, Zamba2), covering both linear-attention and state-space mixers.
- **Controlled pretraining isolates emergence and gating effects**: Training 24-layer GDN models from scratch at 340M/1.3B on FineWeb-Edu shows PAS and ISP emerge early (visible after 1B tokens) and consolidate during optimization. Full-attention output gating strongly attenuates their absolute magnitudes without eliminating the layerwise organization, whereas removing native GDN output gates produces only modest amplification — an asymmetry that pinpoints full attention as the central organizer of MA dynamics.
- **Unified systematic-outlier lifecycle**: Extends systematic-outlier analysis from full-attention to hybrid LLMs and proposes a single write–sink–cancel lifecycle distinguished by cancellation timing — prompt cancellation localizes MAs as PAS, delayed cancellation sustains them as ISP, and the progression at the full-attention limit recovers the stable MA morphology of full-attention LLMs.

## Methodology

The paper is an empirical/mechanistic interpretability study rather than a new architecture or training method. Its pipeline has four components.

**Attention-sink-guided MA tracking.** Prior work detects MAs by picking the largest-magnitude hidden-state entry per layer, but the authors show this magnitude ranking loses alignment with attention sinks under hybridization: sink tokens drop in rank and the maximally activated token switches far more often between adjacent layers in HLA than in a full-attention Transformer (Figure 2). They therefore replace magnitude ranking with a *consensus attention sink* anchor: for each input they average the causal attention matrices across all full-attention layers and heads (Eq. 5) to pick the dominant sink token t⋆, then trace that fixed token's max absolute activation m_x,t⋆^(ℓ) = ‖X_x,t⋆,:‖_∞ across depth. The attention distribution fixes *which* token is tracked; the magnitude decides whether it is an MA.

**Inference-time characterization (M-A-P suite).** Using 52 public checkpoints from the M-A-P Hybrid Linear Attention Research suite (340M and 1.3B, 24 sequence-mixing layers, FineWeb-Edu pretraining), the authors hold scale fixed (1.3B, ρ=12:1) and vary the linear-attention backbone, then hold the backbone fixed (GDN) and vary ρ∈{24,12,6,3}. They define two metrics: the **sink–spike alignment rate** Align(D) (Eq. 6), the fraction of input–layer pairs where the sink attains its block maximum at the layer immediately before full attention; and the **inter-spike retention score** ISR(D) (Eq. 7), the activation retained between adjacent PAS relative to the weaker of the two, clipped at 1. Both are computed over 500 inputs (100 per domain) with 10,000-stratification bootstrap CIs.

**Large-scale evaluation.** Twelve checkpoints from Kimi Linear, Qwen3.5 (35B/122B/397B), Nemotron-H (8B/47B/56B), and Zamba2 (1.2B/2.7B/7B) are traced the same way, covering linear-attention vs. state-space mixers, periodic vs. nonuniform schedules, and base vs. instruct post-training.

**Controlled pretraining.** New GDN models are trained from scratch (AdamW, cosine LR 3e-4→3e-5, 4096 seq len) to study emergence and gating: a single full-attention layer inserted at depth 4/12/20 for PAS, a 3:1 ratio for ISP, plus two interventions — *GDN-GatedFA* (adds element-wise output gates to full attention, Qiu et al. 2026) and *GDN-NoOutGate* (removes native GDN gates). Downstream evaluation follows the Gated DeltaNet protocol: WikiText-103 perplexity; zero-shot HellaSwag/PIQA/ARC-Easy; real retrieval on SWDE/FDA/SQuAD/NQ in cloze form; and synthetic RULER NIAH-1/2/3 at 4096 tokens.

**Mechanistic (systematic-outlier) analysis.** Signed residual/attention/FFN/block-output updates are decomposed at a *fixed* token–feature coordinate (t⋆, j⋆) through the layers around a full-attention boundary, to verify PAS is one evolving outlier (write at layer f−1, attention-sink coupling at f, opposite-signed cancellation) rather than independent per-layer maxima. ISP is then read as the same lifecycle with deferred cancellation.

## Results & Highlights

- **PAS is near-universal and localized.** Sink–spike alignment at the 12:1 ratio, macro-averaged over five domains, is essentially 100% for every backbone and scale: RetNet 99.9/100.0, HGRN 100.0/100.0, GLA 100.0/99.6, DeltaNet 100.0/99.4, GDN 100.0/100.0 (1.3B/340M, Table 1). Consensus sinks also dominate a continuous "peak excess" metric: the sink-minus-non-sink alignment gap is 6.5–58.6 percentage points and the peak-excess gap 0.97–4.42 log2 units, with every paired 95% bootstrap CI excluding zero (Table 6).
- **ISP grows monotonically with full-attention density.** Inter-spike retention rises as ρ shrinks for every backbone; e.g. GDN-1.3B goes 18.4% (12:1) → 26.6% (6:1) → 77.8% (3:1), and DeltaNet-1.3B 23.1% → 44.6% → 84.4% (Table 2). The monotonic trend is input-consistent: all 20 adjacent-ratio comparisons increase, 12:1→6:1 by 5.5–44.5 pp and 6:1→3:1 by 17.0–52.3 pp, all CIs excluding zero. Mean absolute inter-spike activation rises in 19 of 20 comparisons (Table 7).
- **Morphology recurs at scale (1.2B–397B).** PAS/ISP remain aligned with full-attention placement across all four open-source families despite non-monotonic magnitude variation; matched Kimi Linear Base/Instruct and Qwen3.5 Base/Instruct pairs retain aligned PAS locations and ISP boundaries, showing the organization survives post-training.
- **PAS emerges early and placement controls retrieval.** In 340M GDN with one full-attention layer, PAS is visible after 1B tokens and both scales reach 100% final alignment. Placement sharply affects recall: NIAH-1 is 57.4 (layer 4) vs 61.8 (layer 12) vs 90.6 (layer 20); FDA is 8.02 vs 60.43 vs 53.71, while perplexity (14.00/13.78/13.84) and HellaSwag (~39) barely move (Table 9).
- **Output gating is asymmetric.** Adding output gates to the single full-attention layer (GDN-GatedFA, 340M) collapses NIAH-2 from 99.40 (standard) to 16.80 and NIAH-1 from 78.60 to 61.00, yet the PAS/ISP *organization* persists (ISR even rises to 99.48%). Removing all GDN gates (GDN-NoOutGate) gives a much smaller perturbation — NIAH-2 48.20 — despite touching far more layers (Tables 9–10). Scaling GDN 3:1 from 340M→1.3B lifts ISR 90.56%→94.47%, NIAH-3 63.60→90.00, and HellaSwag 39.56→53.36 (Table 10).

The first-authors and several co-authors have an established track record on attention sinks, KV-cache quantization, and MoE super-experts (Su et al. The experimental design is unusually careful for an analysis paper: it relies on a public, controlled suite (M-A-P) plus freshly trained controlled checkpoints released on HuggingFace, uses paired stratified bootstrap significance, and includes non-sink / first-token / random-layer controls rather than relying on visualization alone. Code is promised at github.com/StartluxLabs/Massive-Activations-HLA and trained checkpoints at huggingface.co/startlux-models/Massive-Activations-HLA.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty — genuine and well-scoped.** This is the first characterization of MAs in layer-interleaved HLA LLMs; prior MA work (Sun et al. 2024; An et al. 2025; Sun et al. 2026) was confined to full-attention Transformers, and the PAS/ISP morphology plus the cancellation-timing unification are non-obvious findings that connect hybrid behavior back to the full-attention limit in a principled way. The consensus-sink tracking procedure is a real methodological contribution, since the paper itself shows plain magnitude ranking breaks down under hybridization.
- **Rigor — strong on controls, weaker on mechanism.** The inference-time claims are well defended (bootstrap CIs, multiple controls, five domains, four model families, 1.2B–397B). However the mechanistic "write–sink–cancel / delayed cancellation" account rests almost entirely on a *single* fixed coordinate in *one* 1.3B GDN model (Appendix E.1, layer f=12) plus layerwise decompositions; there is no closed-form or interventional proof that cancellation *timing* (rather than, say, residual scaling or norm statistics) is the actual regulator. The paper itself flags that "determining what regulates cancellation timing" is future work, which is honest but means the central unifying claim is currently correlational.
- **Reproducibility — high.** All M-A-P checkpoints are public, training uses the open Flash Linear Attention library with a fully specified recipe (tokens, batch size, LR schedule, seq len 4096), and the authors release their own controlled checkpoints and (promised) code. Metrics have explicit equations (5–9).
- **Significance — conceptual more than applied.** The findings do not deliver a better model or a training fix; their value is interpretive — predicting where quantization pain and attention-sink artifacts will appear in hybrid LLMs, and motivating where to place/gate full attention. The placement result (early full-attention layer → poor NIAH/FDA recall despite identical alignment) is the most actionable practical signal.
- **Red flags / weaknesses.** (1) Downstream numbers are at small scale (340M/1.3B, 10B–50B tokens) and somewhat noisy — e.g. NIAH-3 swings from 25.8 to 2.60 across near-identical 340M configs and GDN-GatedFA's NIAH-3 (30.8) exceeds the standard 340M PAS config (2.40), undermining any clean gating→quality story; the authors rightly note ISR "should be interpreted cautiously." (2) No comparison to competing MA-mitigation methods (e.g., gated attention as a baseline) on *downstream* quality at the same compute, so it is unclear whether the surviving weak PAS/ISP under full-attention gating actually matters. (3) The "shared lifecycle" is asserted as unifying but the evidence that PAS and ISP are *the same* process at different cancellation timing, rather than two correlated phenomena, is circumstantial. (4) RULER/NIAH is evaluated only at 4K context, so claims about long-context recall are limited.

## Strengths

- Identifies a clean, previously unseen structural regularity (PAS/ISP) and shows it is invariant to linear-attention backbone, hybridization ratio, scale, post-training stage, and input domain.
- The consensus-sink tracking procedure is a principled fix for the magnitude-ranking fragility the authors themselves expose, and the metrics (alignment rate, ISR, peak excess) are well defined with uncertainty quantification.
- Controlled pretraining (inserting full attention at different depths, ablating gates) isolates causal factors that pure checkpoint analysis cannot — a higher bar than most interpretability papers.
- The cancellation-timing continuum elegantly bridges hybrid and full-attention MA morphologies without requiring two separate theories.
- Strong release posture: public M-A-P suite + own checkpoints + code.

## Limitations

- The unifying lifecycle is supported mainly by one fixed-coordinate trace and layerwise decomposition; there is no interventional or analytical proof that cancellation timing is the governing variable.
- All controlled/downstream results are at 340M/1.3B trained on 10B–50B tokens; whether the gating asymmetry and placement effects hold at production scale (tens of billions of activated params, trillions of tokens) is unverified.
- NIAH is run at a single 4K context length, so long-context retrieval claims are narrow; broader RULER tasks (multi-key, variable tracking, aggregation) are absent.
- Downstream metrics are noisy at this scale (NIAH-3 values are erratic across variants), making fine-grained gating-vs-quality conclusions unreliable.
- No comparison against alternative MA/sink-mitigation baselines on the same downstream benchmarks at matched compute, so the practical payoff of the diagnosis is not quantified.
- ISP onset thresholds (6:1 for DeltaNet vs 3:1 for GDN) are reported descriptively but not explained mechanistically.

## Relevance

高度相关。该论文直接研究混合线性注意力大模型（Qwen3.5、Kimi Linear、Nemotron-H 等当前主流高效长上下文架构）的内部激活动力学与注意力汇聚机制，与"长上下文、高效注意力、线性/状态空间模型、LLM 可解释性与量化鲁棒性"等核心研究兴趣高度契合；其 MA 定位结论对 KV-cache/激活量化、全注意力层放置与门控设计具有直接工程指导意义。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- What variable actually governs cancellation timing — is it residual-stream norm growth, FFN/attention output scale, or the position of the next full-attention sink? An interventional study (e.g., patching the cancellation update) could turn the lifecycle from correlational to causal.
- Do PAS/ISP morphologies predict downstream behavior at production scale and long context (32K–128K)? Extending RULER beyond 4K and to 7B+ hybrids would test practical generality.
- Since early full-attention placement harms NIAH/FDA recall despite identical ~100% alignment, is PAS *presence* functionally inert, or does its magnitude/placement carry recall-relevant computation that the alignment metric hides?
- Can the write–sink–cancel account predict where quantization error will concentrate in a given hybrid model, and thereby guide layer-wise mixed-precision or sink-preserving KV-cache schemes?
- Is the surviving weak PAS/ISP under full-attention output gating (as in Qwen3.5) benign, or does it still degrade low-bit inference quality enough to matter?
- Why does ISP onset differ by backbone (DeltaNet 6:1 vs GDN 3:1)? Relating this to each architecture's state-retention/forgetting rule would tighten the cancellation-timing theory.
