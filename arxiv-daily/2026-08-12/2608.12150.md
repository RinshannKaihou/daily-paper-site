> **一句话总结：** 本文通过 4 个模型 × 3 个推理基准 × 7 个 token 预算层级（共 56,476 次推理）系统性地证明：LLM 排名并非稳定不变，而是随 token 预算剧烈翻转——例如在 GSM8K 上 LLaMA-3.3 70B 在 b=256 领先（62.4%），但 GPT-OSS 20B 在 b=4096 反超（94.8%）；同时作者用预算感知路由器捕获了 14.1% 的 oracle 差距。

> **TL;DR:** This paper systematically shows that LLM benchmark rankings are not invariant to the inference-time token budget: across 4 models (LLaMA-3 8B, Qwen-3 32B, LLaMA-3.3 70B, GPT-OSS 20B) × 3 reasoning benchmarks (GSM8K, MATH-500, GPQA-Diamond) × 7 budgets (64–4096), 56,476 inferences reveal significant ranking reversals (McNemar p<0.01 on all benchmarks), model-specific "overthinking" (3–19% non-monotone items even after filtering truncation), per-item oracle gaps up to +27.8 pp on GPQA, and a budget-aware XGBoost router that beats Best-Per-Budget by +2.67 pp on GPQA (14.1% of the oracle gap), while budget features help within-domain (+1.6 to +5.7 pp) but hurt cross-domain transfer (−1.2 pp).

## 核心贡献

- **首次系统刻画"预算依赖"的评估图景。** 作者在 4 个模型（8B–70B）、3 个推理基准、7 个对数间隔的 token 预算上完成 56,476 次确定性（T=0）推理，提出"模型排名是一个由预算参数化的家族，而非单一序"。
- **提出项目级行为分类法。** 把每个 model–item 轨迹分成 always-correct / monotone-increasing / non-monotone / always-wrong 四类，发现 non-monotone（"想多了变差"）占比 3.6%–25.8%，并且 86–94% 的过度思考只发生在单个模型上——说明这是模型特性而非题目内在属性。
- **用统计检验证明排名翻转。** 在 GSM8K、MATH-500、GPQA 上都出现"谁是最佳模型"的翻转，使用 McNemar χ² 检验给出显著性（多数 p<0.01），并通过三层截断控制（all / stop-only / common non-truncated）排除"截断=错答"的混淆。
- **量化 oracle 差距并给出可工作的路由器。** 实证 per-item oracle 在预算受限时收益最大（GSM8K 在 b=256 达 +16.9 pp，GPQA 在 b=4096 达 +27.8 pp）；训练预算感知的 XGBoost 路由器，跨域（数学→科学）能捕获 14.1% 的 oracle gap，SHAP 分析表明预算特征是支配性信号。

## Key Contributions

- **First systematic study of budget-dependent evaluation landscapes.** A 4 × 3 × 7 grid (56,476 T=0 inferences) shows that model rankings on GSM8K, MATH-500, and GPQA-Diamond are a family of orderings parameterized by the token budget, not a single ordering.
- **Item-level behavioral taxonomy.** Each (model, item) trajectory is classified into always-correct, monotone-increasing, non-monotone ("overthinking"), or always-wrong. Non-monotone behavior ranges from 3.6% (GPT-OSS 20B on GSM8K) to 25.8% (LLaMA-3 8B on GPQA) and is overwhelmingly model-specific (cross-model overlap only 6.4–13.8% on stop-only trajectories), so it cannot be removed by curating "bad" items.
- **Statistically certified ranking reversals.** McNemar tests confirm the best model changes with budget on all three benchmarks (e.g., LLaMA-3.3 70B leads GSM8K at b=256 at 62.4%, GPT-OSS 20B leads at b=4096 at 94.8%, p<0.001). A three-tier truncation analysis (all-items, stop-only, common non-truncated) shows the core findings survive once truncation artifacts are controlled.
- **Oracle gap dynamics and a budget-aware router.** Per-item oracle ensembles reveal complementarity of +3.5 to +27.8 pp, largest under constrained budgets; a per-model XGBoost router using log₂(b) plus text/embedding features captures 14.1% of the cross-domain oracle gap, with SHAP showing the budget feature dominates all others by ~6×.

## Methodology

- **Models (4):** LLaMA-3 8B (`meta-llama/Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct`), Qwen-3 32B, LLaMA-3.3 70B, and GPT-OSS 20B (`openai/gpt-oss-20b`), all served through cloud APIs at T=0 (greedy) for full determinism.
- **Benchmarks (3):** GSM8K (1,319 grade-school math), MATH-500 (500 competition math), GPQA-Diamond (198 graduate-level science), giving 2,017 items total and an IRT-inspired difficulty gradient (mean easiness 0.507 / 0.253 / 0.172).
- **Budgets (7):** max_tokens b ∈ {64, 128, 256, 512, 1024, 2048, 4096}; total 4 × 2,017 × 7 = 56,476 inferences.
- **Evaluation:** Regex-based final-answer extraction (`#### [answer]`) with numerical comparison at 1e-6 tolerance for math, A–D extraction for GPQA; binary correctness per (item, budget).
- **Truncation control (three-tier):** All items (truncated = incorrect); stop-only (per-model items that completed); common non-truncated (all four completed, enabling paired McNemar). Results are flagged unreliable when N<30.
- **Behavioral taxonomy:** A correctness trajectory c_{m,i} ∈ {0,1}^7 is classified by whether it ever has a 1→0 transition (non-monotone / overthinking).
- **Oracle gap:** Per-item oracle ensemble accuracy minus the single best model at that budget, with 95% bootstrap CIs; complementarity is also quantified via pairwise Jaccard similarity of correct-answer sets.
- **Budget-aware router:** Four per-model XGBoost classifiers f_m(x, b) → [0,1] using log₂(b), surface text statistics (char/word counts, special chars, LaTeX presence, entropy, max number magnitude), and 20 PCA dims of all-MiniLM-L6-v2 embeddings; route to argmax_m f_m. Baselines: Random, Largest-Always, Best-Overall, Best-Per-Budget, Oracle. Cross-domain evaluation trains on GSM8K+MATH-500 and tests on GPQA; within-domain uses 5-fold CV per benchmark. SHAP ranks feature importance.

## Results & Highlights

- **Ranking reversals on every benchmark (McNemar χ², p<0.01).** On GSM8K, LLaMA-3.3 70B leads at b=256 (62.4% vs GPT-OSS 20B's 48.6%, χ²=59.57, p<0.001) but GPT-OSS 20B dominates at b=4096 (94.9% vs Qwen-3 32B's 91.7%, χ²=15.86, p<0.001). On GPQA, the smallest model LLaMA-3 8B ranks first at b=512 (21.2%), is overtaken by LLaMA-3.3 70B at b=1024 (40.9%, p<0.01), and matched by GPT-OSS 20B at b=4096 (51.0% vs 50.0%).
- **Non-monotone "overthinking" is real and model-specific.** After filtering truncation, stop-only non-monotone rates stay substantial (LLaMA-3 8B on GPQA drops only 25.8% → 19.1%; LLaMA-3.3 70B on MATH-500 10.6% → 10.3%), and cross-model overlap is just 6.4–13.8%, meaning 86–94% of overthinking items are overthinking for exactly one model.
- **Oracle gaps up to +27.8 pp.** On GPQA at b=4096 the per-item oracle beats the best single model by +27.8 pp (CI [+21.7, +33.8]); on GSM8K the gap is non-monotonic, peaking at +16.9 pp around b=256 and declining to +3.5 pp at b=4096; on MATH-500 it grows monotonically to +12.8 pp (CI [+10.0, +15.8]). Mean pairwise Jaccard similarity climbs from 0.048 (b=64) to 0.741 (b=4096) but never reaches unity, so models stay complementary even at generous budgets.
- **Budget-aware router captures 14.1% of the oracle gap cross-domain.** Trained on GSM8K+MATH-500 and tested on GPQA, Router-Scoring reaches 22.9% vs Best-Per-Budget's 20.3% (+2.67 pp, 95% bootstrap CI [0.94, 4.40]); on the 520/1386 item–budget pairs where models disagree, the gain is +7.12 pp. SHAP shows log₂(b) has mean |SHAP|=2.21, about 6.1× the next feature (LaTeX presence, 0.36).
- **Budget features help within-domain but hurt transfer.** Within-domain 5-fold CV: budget adds +5.7 pp on GSM8K, +3.0 pp on MATH-500, +1.6 pp on GPQA. Cross-domain (math→science), removing budget features actually raises accuracy to 24.2% vs 22.9% (Δ = −1.2 pp), confirming budget–accuracy patterns are domain-specific.
- **Genuine overthinking is not truncation.** 1,193 (low-budget correct, high-budget wrong) pairs exist where both responses had finish_reason="stop" (e.g., LLaMA-3 8B: 550 on GSM8K, 88 on MATH-500, 74 on GPQA).
- **Persistent hard core.** 1.1% of GSM8K, 13.6% of MATH-500, and 10.1% of GPQA items are answered incorrectly by all four models at all seven budgets.

## Authors & Credibility

- Authors are Rodrigo Guedes de Souza (graduate student) and Alison R. Panisson (faculty) at the Federal University of Santa Catarina (UFSC), Brazil. Panisson has a published track record on multi-agent / LLM agent topics; this is a careful empirical methodology paper, not a system paper.
- Methodologically transparent: full model IDs and providers are listed (Table 5), prompt templates are released (Appendix H), 95% bootstrap CIs accompany accuracy tables (Table 6), and the analysis explicitly distinguishes "ability to answer given enough tokens" (stop-only) from "ability to answer within the budget" (all-items).
- Code is described but no public repository link is given in the body text; reproducibility rests on the 56,476-inference count, T=0 determinism, and standard benchmarks/models.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** Genuinely novel framing. Prior test-time-compute work (Snell et al. 2024; s1) studies one model under varying compute; prior overthinking work (Chen et al. 2024; Sui et al. 2025) is single-model. This paper's contribution is the multi-model, ranking-level analysis with a router built around budget as a first-class feature — a clean and underexplored angle.
- **Rigor:** Strong. Pre-registered-style three-tier truncation analysis, paired McNemar tests with explicit χ² values, bootstrap CIs, and a dedicated Appendix G that searches for 1,193 genuine correct→wrong pairs at finish_reason="stop" to defend the "overthinking is real" claim against the obvious truncation confound.
- **Significance / overclaiming check:** Mostly well-calibrated, with two caveats. (a) Some headline reversals are statistically weak: the final MATH-500 reversal at b=4096 has p=0.11, and the final GPQA reversal at b=4096 has χ²=0.02 (p=0.89, n=198) — the paper acknowledges this but the abstract's "rankings reverse on all benchmarks" framing leans on these non-significant points. (b) At least one all-items ranking is admitted to be a truncation artifact (MATH-500 b=4096: GPT-OSS leads on all items at 71.0% but Qwen-3 32B leads on common non-truncated at 86.7%), which slightly undercuts the universality of the reversal phenomenon even though the authors reframe it constructively.
- **Reproducibility:** Mixed. Determinism (T=0), public benchmarks, named model IDs, and exact prompt templates support replication, but there is no code release URL in the paper body, GPQA-Diamond has only n=198 (the authors flag this), and several endpoints (Together.ai, Groq, SambaNova) impose quota/latency variance that could affect exact-number reproduction.
- **Red flags:** GPQA-Diamond sample (n=198) is small enough that some "reversals" are within noise; only 4 models are evaluated and dedicated reasoning models (o1, DeepSeek-R1, QwQ) are deliberately excluded because their dual-stream thinking/output architecture breaks the semantics of max_tokens — a defensible but consequential scoping decision that limits claims about the modern frontier.

## Strengths

- A simple, powerful thesis ("rankings depend on budget") supported by a clean, exhaustive experimental grid (4 × 3 × 7 = 84 configs, 56,476 inferences).
- The three-tier truncation analysis is the right methodological move and is executed carefully; Appendix G's direct enumeration of finish_reason="stop" overthinking pairs forecloses the obvious critique.
- The model-specificity finding (86–94% of overthinking is single-model) has a practical and non-obvious implication: you cannot decontaminate or prune benchmark items to fix overthinking.
- The router ablation honestly reports that budget features hurt cross-domain transfer (−1.2 pp) — a negative result that is more informative than the headline positive one.
- SHAP-based feature attribution supports the central message quantitatively (budget ~6× more important than any other feature), not just rhetorically.

## Limitations

- Only 4 models, all general-purpose chat/instruct models; dedicated reasoning models with internal thinking tokens (o1, DeepSeek-R1, QwQ) are excluded, leaving the most budget-sensitive regime untested.
- GPQA-Diamond has only 198 items, so several "reversals" there (notably b=4096) are not statistically significant and the authors themselves say "results on GPQA-Diamond should be interpreted with caution."
- Budgets are logarithmically spaced; finer-grained budgets might reveal additional structure (and the Qwen-3 32B verbose-`<think>` truncation at 59.7% on GPQA at b=4096 means Qwen's numbers are partly a length artifact).
- Routing features are limited to surface text statistics and static all-MiniLM-L6-v2 embeddings; richer model-internal signals (logit entropy, hidden states) are left to future work, and the router still leaves 19 pp of the GPQA oracle gap unexploited.
- Open-ended / non-verifiable tasks (generation, code) are out of scope; generalization beyond exact-match reasoning benchmarks is untested.
- No public code or data link is provided in the paper body.

## Relevance

直接相关于 LLM 评测方法学与 test-time compute 缩放的研究兴趣：它揭示了一个被广泛忽视的评测脆弱性维度（token 预算），并提供了预算感知路由这一可落地的方向，对评测协议设计、排行榜可信度和受算力约束的模型部署都有指导意义。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Do budget-dependent ranking reversals persist for dedicated reasoning models (o1, DeepSeek-R1, QwQ) once their internal-vs-visible token split is properly accounted for, perhaps by separately bounding thinking and output budgets?
- How stable are the reversals under sampling-based decoding (T>0) and self-consistency — does stochasticity wash out the budget-crossover points or sharpen them?
- Can richer routing features (logit entropy, hidden-state probes, partial-generation signals) close more of the 19 pp residual oracle gap on GPQA?
- Is the cross-domain failure of budget features (−1.2 pp) recoverable with lightweight domain adaptation, or is the budget–accuracy mapping fundamentally task-specific?
- Do the ranking reversals translate into real downstream effects in agentic / multi-step pipelines where the per-call budget is itself a tunable economic knob?
