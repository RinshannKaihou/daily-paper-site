⚠ SCOPE LIMIT
! Critical edge

TensorRT-LLM — the most widely deployed closed serving stack — is excluded. A production shop on TRT-LLM cannot conclude from this audit whether its INT8 path works.

M Methodology

A documentary, source-level audit — deliberately measurement-free.

Every claim is pinned to an artifact and traced through four layers, not benchmarked. The author explicitly reports no throughput, latency, or accuracy numbers — a companion measurement study is "in preparation." The method is a layered trace, cross-checked against shipped release binaries (not just dev branches), with full reproducibility (URLs, commits, digests, access dates all 2026-08-11) centralized in Appendix A.

A functional reroute check on sm_89 (Ada) confirms the Triton escape hatch produces identical greedy output across three kernel configs — verifying the mechanism, not its B300 throughput.

R Results · the four-layer exit

Each layer's decision is locally rational. Combined, INT8 is factually unavailable.

+ Strengths

Explanatory, not just descriptive.

  • ISA root cause (.kind::i8 never on sm_103a) explains the software layers — not merely their symptoms.
  • Within-revision controlled contrast: FP4 extended to sm_103a in PTX 8.8, INT8 not — a clean argument against "oversight."
  • Disciplined pinned sourcing; cross-checks main commits against shipped release binaries.
  • Practical guardrails: IMMA-counter profiler criterion, ~1B smoke-test fixture, digest-pinning.
  • Honest disclaimers: no concealment narrative; the break is B200→B300, not Hopper→Blackwell.
– Limitations

Real scope edges the author flags.

  • No performance measurements by design — the Triton-reroute throughput question is open.
  • TensorRT-LLM (most-deployed closed stack) is not audited.
  • Point-in-time findings on fast-moving repos; the audit caught a within-hours :latest drift (2→3 INT8 backends).
  • Single author; two author-local artifacts (marketplace snapshot, sm_89 run) not independently re-derivable.
  • Rubin-generation behavior out of scope; cannot predict future .kind::i8 re-exposure.
