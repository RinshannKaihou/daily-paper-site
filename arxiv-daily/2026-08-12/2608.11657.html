Semantic Lenia: Emergence of Homeostatic Solitons Within the Semantic Space of Large Language Models

Yoshihiko Kayama · Baika Women's University, Osaka

Key Finding

Mapping Lenia's continuous-life physics onto LLM logits yields stable homeostatic soliton limit cycles instead of degenerative collapse.

Headline Metric
150×
longer trajectory survival than unidirectional linear steering — T > 150 steps vs collapse in T = 1 step (Llama-3.1-70B, α=50; Dist-3 0.978 vs 0.070).
Why This Matters

Reframes why LLMs fall into repetition loops as a continuous-life dynamical problem — and supplies a non-monotonic attraction+repulsion steering mechanism plus a degeneration probe.

Critical Edge

Only unidirectional linear steering is compared — no DExperts / GeDi / CFG / activation-steering numbers — so the claim that Semantic Lenia is "superior" is quantitatively unsupported.

Methodology

The LLM is treated as a hybrid dynamical system: discrete tokens, but continuous trajectories in the macroscopic logit manifold (D=4096 for 8B, D=8192 for 70B). Three components define Semantic Lenia:

  1. Target kernel centroid. For a concept cluster C, the centroid k is the normalized sum of its token embeddings — a multi-token cluster acts as a low-pass semantic filter and avoids collapse onto (or banning of) a single logit.
  2. Semantic potential. U_t = (sim(c_t, k) + 1) / 2 ∈ [0,1] measures the context's distance to the centroid.
  3. Homeostatic growth function. An asymmetric Gaussian G(U_t) that attracts near the centroid, repels when too close, and applies zero force in a dead zone:
Z_steered = Z_base + α·G(U_t)·S_k,    G(U_t) = 2·exp(−(U_t−µ)²/2σ²) − 1,  ∆ = σ√(2 ln 2)

Z_base is interpreted as the model's "Syntactic Inertia." Experiments sweep dense 779-point (µ,σ) grids on Llama-3.1-8B / Gemma-7B at α∈{15,30,50}, plus a Llama-3.1-70B (NF4) scaling run, using two prompt→concept pairs (HAPPY→COMPUTER, BRAIN→SYMPHONY); fixed seed 42, temperature 0.8, Tmax 80/150. Classification pairs an LLM-as-Judge label with a CART decision tree over features (mean U_t, PPL variance, step count T).

Strengths & Limitations

Strengths

  • Original synthesis of continuous Artificial-Life (Lenia) physics with LLM decoding theory; "Semantic Crystallization" is a memorable, pedagogically useful lens.
  • Rigorous within-scope work: dense 779-point grids, explicit orbital metrics (radius, variance, angular velocity, Dist-3), and a principled PPL-variance gauge.
  • Excellent reproducibility — fixed seeds, isolated single-GPU sweeps, quantization disclosure, open data / code / interactive portal (CC BY 4.0).
  • Clean quantitative contrast: linear collapse (T=1, Dist-3=0.070) vs sustained loop (T>150, Dist-3≈0.9).
  • The dual-GPU bifurcation study is a thoughtful contribution on hardware-level non-determinism in LLM generation.

Limitations

  • Intervenes at output logits, where the Habitable Ridge is "exceptionally narrow"; activation-layer steering flagged as necessary future work.
  • Evaluation rests on only two prompt→concept pairs — no held-out set, no downstream task, no human evaluation, no real steering baseline.
  • "Physical scaling law" drawn from a single 8B→70B step on a single prompt; NF4 quantization confounded with scale.
  • Headline lifespan capped at Tmax=150 (runs to 800 show eventual "aging" toward crystallization, unsystematically characterized).
  • Llama-vs-Gemma "material DNA" confounds vocabulary size, architecture, and training data; PPL_var=10.0 threshold is mildly circular (fit on LLM-judge labels).

Questions for Further Exploration

  1. How does Semantic Lenia compare quantitatively to DExperts, GeDi, Classifier-Free Guidance, and activation steering on standard controllable-generation benchmarks (toxicity, topic control, FUDGE)?
  2. Does the required-α scaling law hold beyond one scale transition — generalizing to 1B / 13B / 70B and dozens of concept pairs?
  3. Can the PPL_var<10.0 crystallization threshold be validated on an independent, human-annotated degeneration dataset?
  4. What happens when the growth function targets intermediate hidden states instead of output logits — does the ridge widen and do "abductive leaps" improve?
  5. Is the 18.74% cross-architecture bifurcation specific to the steering regime, or does unsteered FP16 generation show comparable sensitivity that syntactic inertia masks?
  6. Can engineered "soft-decay" homeostatic brakes extend soliton lifespan beyond 800 tokens without degrading output quality?
