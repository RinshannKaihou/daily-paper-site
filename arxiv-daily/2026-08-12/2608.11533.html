§ 01 · The Finding

Performance differences trace to shape — cache capacity, co-tenancy, access pattern — not abstract architecture.

Testing the EPYC Conjecture on Real Hardware: MoA-Guided Dense Matrix Multiplication on NCSA Delta (AMD EPYC 7763 “Milan”)

Lenore M. Mullin· University at Albany, SUNY· 12 Aug 2026
matrix-multiplication Mathematics-of-Arrays cache-blocking NUMA roofline-analysis cs.PF
§ 02 · Key Metric
30–59%

Speedup from zero-fit MC recalibration. Block size MC=256, derived analytically from Delta's real 512 KB L2, beats the M1-Pro-inherited MC=64 at every size — the single largest gain in the project.

§ 03 · Why This Matters

A transferable, shape-first recipe: derive cache block sizes per architecture from capacity and byte-width, not fit them — directly relevant to portable, high-performance kernel design.

§ 04 · Critical Edge

“Beats the hybrid” is not “beats the SOTA.” The headline three-of-four win is over a self-built Strassen–GEBP kernel — never compared against BLIS, LIBXSMM, oneMKL, or AMD AOCL.

§ 05 · Methodology

Predict, then measure — never fit after the fact.

NCSA Delta CPU node · SLURM-exclusive · gcc -march=znver3 · topology verified via lscpu -e

The verified C/OpenMP kernels from the companion paper run on a dual-socket AMD EPYC 7763 “Milan” — 128 cores, eight CCDs per socket (32 MB L3 / CCD, 512 KB private L2, 32 KB L1). The block size MC = 256 is derived analytically from that real L2 capacity before any timing run, using the same no-margin formula that exactly reproduced the M1 Pro's MC=64 from its 128 KB L1. The co-tenancy hypothesis H2 is tested by pinning execution to one CCD (taskset -c 0-7) to reproduce eight-way co-tenancy on a different microarchitecture. The controlled NUMA test fixes P=64 and varies only memory placement via numactl — local (zero cross-socket traffic) vs remote (every access crossing the socket boundary) — measuring STREAM, GEBP, MoA-pipelined, recursive-classical, and the hybrid under both. A roofline sweep covers five sizes by four thread counts; a variance characterization runs one configuration back-to-back to compare bare-metal noise against Graviton4's virtualization shift.

§ 07 · Two-sided read

Strengths & limitations, side by side.

Honest about both — the paper names its own open questions.

✓ Strengths

  • Predict-then-measure. Every parameter (MC, σ, the win pattern) is derived before timing — the strongest defense against post-hoc rationalization.
  • Clean NUMA isolation. Fixing P and varying only placement separates access-pattern shape from bandwidth capacity — a genuinely instructive template.
  • Intellectual honesty. Repeatedly distinguishes “confirms the conjecture” from “speaks only to Milan,” and flags its own 2.29× outlier.
  • Cross-platform pattern. The three-of-four win reproduces on M1 Pro and Milan; the negative Graviton4 result is reported, not suppressed.
  • Re-derivable recipe. Changing data type (e.g. to single precision) would shift every block size by the byte-width ratio — a testable prediction.

! Limitations

  • Wrong target chip. Conjecture named Bergamo (Zen4c, single-socket); Delta is Milan (Zen3, dual-socket) — introducing a cross-socket NUMA confound the original chip lacks.
  • No production BLAS baseline. Compared only against the author's own GEBP / hybrid — not BLIS, LIBXSMM, oneMKL, or AOCL.
  • Negative result understated. The fork/join half is effectively falsified (overhead saturates, doesn't grow) but framed softly.
  • Unexplained flagship. The 2.29× MoA/GEBP ratio is flagged by the authors as possibly an unmatched-utilization artifact.
  • Large variance. 15% mean back-to-back difference undercuts the precision of every other percentage reported.
§ 08 · Open Questions for Further Exploration

Where the work points next.

  • How do MoA-pipelined and GEBP compare against production BLAS (BLIS, LIBXSMM, oneMKL, AMD AOCL) at matched sizes and thread counts — is the advantage robust to a serious baseline?
  • Does the 2.29× MoA/GEBP ratio at n=4096 survive a properly matched utilization fraction, rather than an arbitrary P=8?
  • Would the originally-conjectured Bergamo (Zen4c, single-socket 128-core) reproduce the per-CCD recalibration without Delta's dual-socket confound?
  • What happens to optimal MC, NC, and the MoA/GEBP ratio under single precision (byte width 4) — the paper predicts a deterministic shift, but is it verified?
  • What is the exact mechanism behind the steepest efficiency drop at P=32→64 — Milan's centralized eight-channel memory saturating?
  • Can the 15% back-to-back variance be reduced (frequency pinning, NUMA-bound OpenMP runtime, longer warm-up), and does the scattered noise shape persist beyond two runs?
  • Does MoA's NUMA resilience (2.4% vs 13.9%) generalize beyond dense matmul — to tensor contractions, batched GEMM, stencils?
