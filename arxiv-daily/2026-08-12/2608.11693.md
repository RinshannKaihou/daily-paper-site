> **一句话总结：** 本报告对 NVIDIA Blackwell Ultra (B300) 做"规格表→PTX ISA→CUTLASS→vLLM/SGLang"四层文档级审计，发现 INT8 W8A8 虽在 datasheet 上"存在"，但因硅片 INT8 被限速到 FP8 的 ~1/30、第五代张量核整数指令 `.kind::i8` 从未在 sm_103a 上暴露、CUTLASS 对 103a 构建跳过 INT8 核生成、两大服务引擎均无 Blackwell 级 INT8 GEMM，导致默认部署时 INT8 检查点在模型加载后于首次前向传播才报硬错误——这是一个"全栈属性"而非单一规格问题。

> **TL;DR:** A source-and-spec audit (commit-pinned, deliberately measurement-free) shows INT8 W8A8 is undeployable by default on NVIDIA Blackwell Ultra (B300): although the datasheet lists an INT8 entry, the published dense FP8:INT8 ratio jumps from 1:1 on H200 and B200 to ~30:1 on B300 (4.5 PFLOPS FP8 vs 150 TOPS INT8), the PTX `tcgen05.mma .kind::i8` kind was never extended to sm_103a (while FP4 kinds were in the same PTX 8.8 revision), CUTLASS's generator guards INT8 UMMA out of any 103a build, and both vLLM (commits 6c95a641 / v0.27.1) and SGLang (b20c375) ship no Blackwell-capable INT8 GEMM — vLLM only failing at the first forward pass after a full model load. The one escape hatch is a non-default Triton JIT reroute via `VLLM_DISABLED_KERNELS`, whose B300 throughput is explicitly left unmeasured.

## 核心贡献

- **首次对 B300 的 INT8 做全栈文档级审计**：把 INT8 W8A8 的可用性从规格表一路追到 PTX ISA、NVIDIA 自家 CUTLASS 内核库，以及 vLLM/SGLang 两大开源服务引擎，证明这是一种"四层一致退出"（layered withdrawal）——四层、其中三层在 NVIDIA 内部，都在给定 30:1 速率劣势下做出各自局部合理的决定，组合起来却构成事实上的不可用。
- **定位了不可用性的因果根因（而非仅描述现象）**：核心事实是 PTX ISA 从未在 sm_103a 上暴露第五代整数张量核路径（`.kind::i8`），因此 B300 上唯一架构合法的整数张量核路径只剩 legacy warp-level IMMA（`mma.sync` with `.s8/.u8`），而它在硅片上以 ~FP8/30 的速率运行。这解释了下面所有软件层的行为。
- **纠正了一个正在传播的代际叙事错误**：明确指出断点是 B200→B300，而非 Hopper→Blackwell——H200 与 B200 均为 1:1；并点名一篇预印本 [34] 把 B200 的 INT8 误标为 ~155 TOPS，相对 NVIDIA 公布的 4,500 TOPS dense 低了约 29×。
- **给出测量研究的方法学护栏**：指出在 sm_103 上用 profiler "grep tcgen05 整数指令"判定"原生 INT8"必然产生假阴性（因为该路径根本不存在），给出可靠判据（`sm__inst_executed_pipe_tensor_op_imma.sum` 计数器 + INT8 操作数 dtype + 无 dequantize 桩）；并建议用 ~1B 参数的 INT8 W8A8 检查点做廉价烟雾测试以避免在 405B 级模型上付完整个下载才发现部署失败。
- **全可复现**：每个论断都追溯到官方文档、pinned commit 或发布镜像 digest，URL/commit/digest/访问日期集中列于 Appendix A；审阅的所有源码 pin 在 2026-08-11，CUTLASS 在 dcf215a (2026-08-06)。

## Key Contributions

- **First end-to-end documentary audit of INT8 on B300.** Traces W8A8 INT8 availability through four layers — published specifications, the PTX ISA, NVIDIA's CUTLASS kernel library, and the two major open-source serving engines (vLLM, SGLang) — and shows the format is nominally present on the datasheet yet, by default, undeployable on this hardware.
- **Identifies the causal root, not just the symptom.** The single load-bearing fact is that the PTX ISA never exposes the fifth-generation integer tensor-core path (`tcgen05.mma .kind::i8`) on sm_103a: the manual's integer-kind target list is `sm_100a, sm_101a, sm_110a` (sm_103a absent), and the exclusion phrase "except .kind::i8" appears four times across the tcgen05.mma variants. This explains (rather than merely describes) every software-layer behavior below: there is no B300 instruction for them to target except legacy warp-level IMMA, which silicon runs at ~1/30 the FP8 rate.
- **Controlled-contrast evidence within the same ISA revision.** Table II shows PTX 8.8 (the revision that added sm_103a support) extended the FP4 kinds (`mxf4nvf4`, `mxf4`) to sm_103a and added K=96, but extended `.kind::i8` to nothing — making the INT8 omission a deliberate carve-out rather than an oversight.
- **Corrects an in-circulation generational error.** The discontinuity is B200→B300, not Hopper→Blackwell (both H200 and B200 are FP8:INT8 1:1); the report explicitly flags a recent FP8-centric preprint [34] that tabulates B200 INT8 as ~155 TOPS, low by ~29× against NVIDIA's published 4,500 TOPS dense.
- **Methodological guardrails for the companion measurement study.** Warns of a false-negative profiler trap on sm_103, prescribes the IMMA-instruction counter as the reliable "native INT8" criterion, and recommends a ~1B-parameter INT8 W8A8 smoke-test fixture so the late failure surfaces in minutes rather than after a multi-hundred-GB download at node-hour prices.

## Methodology

This is a **documentary / source-level audit**, not a benchmark study. The author explicitly and deliberately reports no performance, latency, or accuracy measurements ("a companion measurement study is in preparation"). The method is instead a layered trace, with every claim pinned to an artifact:

1. **Spec layer (Section II).** NVIDIA's published per-GPU tensor-core peaks are collected from three official sources (H200 product page, Blackwell Architecture Technical Brief Table 3, Blackwell Ultra Datasheet) into a single basis (dense, per-GPU, HGX air-cooled SKU bin), and compared as FP8:INT8 ratios across H200, B200, B300.
2. **ISA layer (Section III).** The PTX ISA manual v9.3 is read for the `tcgen05.mma .kind::i8` target list, the four "except .kind::i8" exclusion phrases, and the manual's own release-history table (reproduced as Table II) to establish a within-revision controlled contrast (FP4 extended to sm_103a in PTX 8.8; INT8 not).
3. **Kernel-library layer (Section IV).** CUTLASS (pinned at commit dcf215a, 2026-08-06) is read at `python/cutlass_library/generator.py`, where `GenerateSM100()` is shown to guard INT8 UMMA generation behind the absence of the family targets `{100f, 101f, 103a}` while leaving FP8 generation unconditional; the sole SM103-specific generator is FP4 (`GenerateSM103_TensorOp_fp4_ultra_UMMA_gemm_with_block_scaled`), and the count of SM103 generators matching `int8|s8|i8` is zero.
4. **Serving layer (Section V).** vLLM (6c95a641 / release image v0.27.1, digest pinned) and SGLang (b20c375) are read at their CMake/build gates, dispatch tables, and tuning-config directories. Findings are cross-checked against the shipped release binary (`_C_stable_libtorch.abi3.so`), confirming shipped (not dev-branch) behavior.
5. **Functional reroute check (author-local, no perf claim).** On an sm_89 (Ada) GPU with the release image and `RedHatAI/Llama-3.2-1B-Instruct-quantized.w8a8`, the three configurations (default; Cutlass disabled via `VLLM_DISABLED_KERNELS`; Cutlass+Triton disabled) select the Cutlass, Triton, and Humming INT8 kernels respectively and produce identical greedy output — verifying the escape-hatch mechanism, not its B300 throughput.
6. **Corroboration.** The cc-10.3↔B300 correspondence is independently corroborated by a snapshot of all B300 offers on a public GPU marketplace API reporting `compute_cap 1030` (2026-08-11).

Reproducibility (URLs, exact commits, container digests, access dates) is centralized in Appendix A.

## Results & Highlights

The paper deliberately reports **no throughput/latency/accuracy benchmark measurements**; the quantitative findings below are documentary (spec ratios, code-path counts, architecture-tag censuses, failure semantics), presented as the paper's evidence base.

- **Spec-layer ratio (metric: dense per-GPU tensor-core peak; "baseline" = prior generations).** H200 SXM is FP8:INT8 = **1:1** (1,979 TFLOPS FP8 vs 1,979 TOPS INT8); HGX B200 is also **1:1** (4.5 PFLOPS FP8 vs 4.5 POPS INT8); HGX B300 is **~30:1** (**4.5 PFLOPS FP8 vs 0.15 POPS / 150 TOPS INT8**). The ~30× INT8 cut is specific to Blackwell Ultra; the B200→B300 delta is the entire discontinuity. (Datasheet footnote: B300 lists INT8 330/307 TOPS sparse for GB300/HGX bins, dense = ½ sparse.)
- **Co-traveler cut (same metric family).** FP64 tensor throughput falls from **37 TFLOPS (B200) to 1.2 TFLOPS (B300)** — essentially the same ~30× factor in the same product, consistent with a coordinated deprioritization of integer/FP64 paths in favor of the FP4/FP8 inference envelope. INT4 tensor-core types were already deprecated with Hopper, extending a multi-generation integer-format retreat.
- **ISA-layer carve-out (metric: PTX target coverage; baseline = FP4 kinds in same revision).** `.kind::i8` is supported on `sm_100a, sm_101a, sm_110a` only — **sm_103a absent**. In PTX 8.8 (the revision that added sm_103a), the FP4 kinds (`mxf4nvf4`, `mxf4`) and K=96 were extended to sm_103a, while `.kind::i8` was extended to **nothing**; the exclusion "except .kind::i8" appears **4 times** across the tcgen05.mma variants (Table II).
- **CUTLASS-layer gate (metric: SM103 generator count matching int8|s8|i8).** Count = **0**. `GenerateSM100()` generates INT8 UMMA only when the build's feature set does **not** intersect `{100f, 101f, 103a}`, and emits FP8 UMMA unconditionally; any build targeting B300 therefore gets FP4 + FP8 tensor-core GEMMs and **no INT8 UMMA**. The sole SM103-specific generator is FP4.
- **vLLM serving layer (metric: compiled INT8 GEMM sources per arch + failure timing).** The Hopper (9.0a) source list includes INT8 kernels; the Blackwell SM100 and SM120 source lists contain **FP8 sources only**; the SM100 dispatch passes a `nullptr` for the integer case (comment "int8 not supported on SM100") that the dispatch helper turns into a hard error. The Python-side gate `get_min_capability()` returns **75** ("turing and up"), so a B300 (cc 10.3 ≥ 7.5) **passes every load-time check** and the process dies only at the first forward pass — after a complete model load.
- **SGLang serving layer (metric: AOT arch tags + tuning-config device coverage).** `int8_gemm_kernel.cu` carries arch tags Sm75 (×1), Sm80 (×6), a single Sm90, and **none of Sm100/103/120**; the public entry falls through to `TORCH_CHECK_NOT_IMPLEMENTED("No implemented int8_scaled_mm for current compute capability.")`. Per Table III, INT8 W8A8 tuning configs cover only **A100-SXM4-80GB, A800-SXM4-80GB, H20** (3 devices), whereas FP8 tuning already covers **B200, H100, H200, H20, L20Y, L40, MI300X, MI325X, …** — same repo, same naming convention, asymmetric coverage that "falls out of routine maintenance."
- **Escape hatch (functional, unmeasured throughput).** `VLLM_DISABLED_KERNELS=CutlassInt8ScaledMMLinearKernel` reroutes INT8 W8A8 to the JIT-compiled Triton backend (no code patch). On sm_89 the three configurations select Cutlass/Triton/Humbling and produce **identical greedy output**; whether the Triton path emits real IMMA on sm_103, and at what attained throughput, is explicitly deferred to the companion study.
- **Methodological landmine (metric: profiler criterion correctness).** Grepping for tcgen05 integer instructions on sm_103 is a guaranteed **false negative** (the path doesn't exist, so native INT8 would correctly show none and be misclassified as a fallback). The reliable criterion is a nonzero `sm__inst_executed_pipe_tensor_op_imma.sum` counter (Nsight Compute) with INT8 operand dtypes and no dequantize-to-BF16 stubs.

No benchmark performance results are reported (by design).

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty.** High within its niche. To the author's knowledge (and consistent with the cited microbenchmark literature [9], [10], which cover B200/H200/consumer Blackwell but not B300), no third-party measurement or documentary audit of B300 INT8 exists; the ISA-level root-cause analysis (the `.kind::i8` carve-out vs. the FP4 extension in the same PTX 8.8 revision, Table II) is a genuinely original, explanatory contribution rather than a re-description of the datasheet.
- **Rigor.** Strong. The within-revision controlled contrast (FP4 extended to sm_103a, INT8 not) is exactly the right experimental design to distinguish a deliberate carve-out from an oversight. The IMMA-counter profiler criterion is correctly derived from the ISA finding and follows established tensor-core microbenchmarking practice [27]. The recommendation to smoke-test on a ~1B INT8 W8A8 checkpoint before paying for a 405B-class download is a concrete, cost-aware methodological contribution.
- **Scope limits (the real weaknesses).** (i) **TensorRT-LLM is excluded** — the report audits only CUTLASS, vLLM, and SGLang, so the most widely deployed closed serving stack is unaddressed; a production shop on TRT-LLM cannot conclude from this audit whether its INT8 path works. (ii) **No performance measurement by design** — the central practical question ("does the ~30:1 spec ratio hold up, and does the Triton reroute recover any of it?") is explicitly deferred, so practitioners gain a deployment-correctness verdict but not a throughput one. (iii) **Point-in-time fragility** — the author is honest that "the gaps documented here are the kind that a single merged pull request can change," and the documented `:latest` drift from v0.23.0 (two INT8 backends) to v0.27.1 (three) within hours during the audit itself is concrete evidence the window is moving. (iv) **The B300 = cc 10.3 correspondence** leans partly on a marketplace-API snapshot (author-local, not independently re-checkable) rather than an NVIDIA-published statement, though it is consistent with every other signal.
- **Reproducibility.** Excellent for the documentary claims (pinned commits, digests, verbatim PTX strings, file paths with line-level specificity). Weaker only for the two author-local items the author himself flags.
- **Significance.** Practically important for anyone doing capacity planning or quantization-format selection for Blackwell Ultra: it shows that capacity (memory footprint, since INT8 and FP8 are byte-identical in storage) transfers across the H200→B300 migration while compute-path planning silently does not — an asymmetry invisible in any single document and visible only in their conjunction.
- **Red flags.** None on integrity. The main risk is **shelf life** (the author acknowledges this) and the **TRT-LLM gap**, which a reader could over-generalize into "INT8 is dead on Blackwell" when the report's claim is specifically about CUTLASS/vLLM/SGLang defaults on B300.

## Strengths

- Explanatory depth: the ISA root cause (`tcgen05.mma .kind::i8` never on sm_103a) explains the software layers rather than just listing their symptoms.
- Disciplined, pinned sourcing with a dedicated reproducibility appendix; cross-checks main commits against shipped release binaries.
- The within-revision controlled contrast (FP4 extended, INT8 not, in the same PTX 8.8) is a clean argument against "oversight."
- Practical methodological guardrails (IMMA-counter profiler criterion, ~1B smoke-test fixture, digest-pinning) that downstream measurement studies can adopt verbatim.
- Intellectually honest disclaimers: no concealment narrative, INT8 "rate-limited not removed," the discontinuity is B200→B300 not Hopper→Blackwell, and a named correction of an in-circulation preprint error.

## Limitations

- **No performance measurements** by explicit design; the central throughput question for the Triton reroute is open.
- **TensorRT-LLM is not audited**, so the closed-source serving path most production deployments actually use is outside scope.
- **Point-in-time findings** on fast-moving repos; the audit itself caught a within-hours `:latest` drift that changed INT8 backend count from two to three.
- **Single author, no independent re-derivation** of the two author-local artifacts (marketplace snapshot, sm_89 functional run).
- **Rubin-generation claims are out of scope**; the report covers B300 only and is explicit that future-generation INT8 behavior is not established here.
- The documentary method cannot say whether NVIDIA will re-expose `.kind::i8` on a future sm_103 revision or via a microcode/firmware update.

## Relevance

直接命中量化与高效 LLM 推理（INT8 W8A8）与硬件-软件协同这条研究主线：报告解释了为何"规格表上存在"不等于"默认可部署"，并为任何在 Blackwell Ultra 上做 INT8 vs FP8/NVFP4 格式选择、混合精度服务策略或容量规划的工作提供了必须先核对的全栈可用性事实与方法学护栏。对量化研究者，它是一个"格式可用性是全栈属性"的具体警示案例。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- What attained INT8 throughput does the Triton JIT reroute (`VLLM_DISABLED_KERNELS=CutlassInt8ScaledMMLinearKernel`) actually deliver on sm_103, measured against FP8/NVFP4 and against legacy warp-level IMMA on B300?
- Does TensorRT-LLM (the unaudited major stack) ship a Blackwell-capable INT8 GEMM, and if so does it emit IMMA or fall back to a dequantize-to-BF16 path? Would the same audit method apply to its closed binaries?
- How does the vLLM/SGLang picture change once the pinned commits move — specifically, has anyone added an SM103 INT8 tuning config or an SM103 INT8 generator since 2026-08-11?
- Is the ~30:1 spec ratio actually realized in either direction (i.e., does FP8 hit its 4.5 PFLOPS, and does warp-level IMMA hit even the 150 TOPS ceiling) on B300 silicon?
- For phase-aware mixed-precision serving policies that place INT8 W8A8 on certain modules, what is the cost of the silent compute-path asymmetry (capacity transfers, compute does not) across a realistic H200→B300 migration?
- Could a firmware/microcode update or a future PTX revision re-expose `.kind::i8` on sm_103a without a silicon respin, and what would that imply for the "stack property" framing?
