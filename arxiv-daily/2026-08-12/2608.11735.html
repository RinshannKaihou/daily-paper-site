Locating and Controlling Implicit Personalization in Large Language Models

Key finding LLMs’ implicit demographic bias traces to a locatable residual-stream signal that projection ablation suppresses at inference.

Authors Yueru Yan, Siqi Wu, Thai Le | Lab Indiana University, Bloomington | Date 2026-08-12 | Models 5 × 7B–14B (instruct)
mechanistic-interpretability implicit-personalization activation-steering representation-geometry demographic-bias causal-intervention

W Why this matters

A mechanistic-interpretability result: it locates mediating residual layers, turns the activation contrast into a probe signal, and intervenes causally — directly covering representation geometry and causal circuit analysis.

! Critical edge CAUTION

The metrics capture expressed content, not true removal — with no post-ablation linear probe, the demographic attribute may still be linearly recoverable from the residual stream (Gonen & Goldberg, 2019).

src: Critical Assessment · Red flag (mild)

Methodology, distilled

Paired-comparison design. For n = 50 scenarios, GPT-4o generates matched 5-turn dialogues sharing identical topical scaffolding but branching into identity-cued (implicit demographic cues from the Neplenbroek et al. 2025 taxonomy — the demographic is never named), identity-neutral, and explicit “state the demographic” variants. Each ends with the same query: “Recommend 5 movies. Output only the title and year on each line.” Greedy decoding (T=0).

Behavior. Two triangulating metrics — SED-desc (taxonomy-free, embedding cosine shift on TMDB plots) and CAR (taxonomy-based, GPT-4o tagging; human–GPT-4o agreement = 0.8). 9 single-cue conditions span race, gender, age; 3 two-cue pairs use dose-matched single-dimension controls.

Internal signal. The raw contrast ΔA_L = A_L(C_cue, x) − A_L(C_neutral, x) at the final query-token residual stream, normalized as s_ΔA = ||ΔA_L||₂ / ||A_L(x)||₂; mediating layer set L★ found via activation patching.

Intervention. Directional ablation applied through a pre-hook on the post-attention layernorm at the union of each dimension’s top-2 L★ layers:

h′ = (I − α·v̂dim·v̂dimT)·h

Setup at a glance

  • ModelsLlama-3-8B, Mistral-7B-v0.3, Qwen3-8B, Qwen3-14B, Phi-4
  • Scenarios50 matched pairs per condition (modest n)
  • Single cues9 — race {Black, Asian, White}, gender {male, female}, age {child, adolescent, adult, older}
  • Cue pairs3 — race×gender, race×age, gender×age (K=4)
  • MetricsSED-desc (taxonomy-free) + CAR (taxonomy-based)
  • Controlsmatched-neutral, dose-matched, random-direction sham, prompt baseline
  • Sweepα ∈ [0.5, 5] (α=1 exact projection)
  • DomainMovie recommendation (books/articles: Llama only)

Strengths

  • Clean causal ID. Matched-neutral + dose-matched controls, plus sham and prompt baselines — above average for this subarea.
  • Two triangulating metrics (taxonomy-free SED-desc + taxonomy-based CAR) rather than one noisy score.
  • Replication across five model families (7B–14B), not a single model.
  • A crisp mechanistic insight: near-linear internal composition vs. 28–38% sub-additive behavior, quantified on both sides.
  • Honest limitations naming the missing LEACE baseline, the topical confound, and the concealment caveat.

Limitations

  • Movie-rec domain only in the main paper; books/articles restricted to single-dimension Llama results.
  • Ablation on only three 7–8B models; 14B and base models untested.
  • n = 50 matched pairs per condition is modest for per-condition correlation claims.
  • Construct confound: implicit cues carry topical content inseparable from demographic representation.
  • Missing strongest baseline (LEACE / iterative projection) — proof-of-mechanism, not SOTA erasure.
  • No post-ablation probe — cannot distinguish removal from concealment.
  • Coarse Western, English-only taxonomy; clean decoupling in only 3/18 settings.
Authors & credibility
Indiana University, Bloomington (Yueru Yan, Siqi Wu, Thai Le). Sits in the causal-mediation tradition (Vig 2020) and rep-engineering / activation-steering line (Zou 2023; Arditi 2024; Ravfogel 2020; Belrose/LEACE 2023). Cites the closest concurrent baselines (Karvonen & Marks 2025; Kantharuban 2025). Benjamini–Hochberg correction within model; code to be released (cue templates withheld, dual-use).

Questions for further exploration

  1. Would closed-form LEACE erasure match or beat the hand-tuned α-sweep on the same setup — and improve selectivity?
  2. Can a post-ablation linear probe still recover the demographic from the residual stream (the concealment test the authors skipped)?
  3. How much of the L★ signal survives under topic-controlled cues — holding topic fixed, varying only demographic association?
  4. Does the cue→activation→behavior chain and its 28–38% sub-additive gap hold on frontier 70B+/base models?
  5. Can the linear-internal / sub-additive-behavior dissociation be explained by a specific downstream readout nonlinearity?
  6. Does the same activation signature govern implicit personalization in non-English dialogues, with a different cue taxonomy?
