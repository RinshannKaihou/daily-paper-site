01

Methodology

constraint by construction, not penalty

GCPO layers a geometric constraint onto GRPO and adds no new loss term. Before training, the top-k=8 left and right singular vectors (Φ_k, Ψ_k) of each frozen pretrained weight W_ref are computed once. The cumulative adaptation is parameterized in projected low-rank form (rank r=32, α=16, s=α/r):

ΔW(ℓ) = s · Π⊥Φ · L(ℓ) R(ℓ) · Π⊥Ψ
s.t.   Φk⊤ δW = 0  and  δW Ψk = 0 // bilateral orthogonality holds for every value of L, R — overlap cannot enter the update

At each step the raw factors are re-projected, the effective weight W_ref + s·L̄R̄ is synced to the vLLM rollout engine, rollouts are generated (K=16), and only the raw factors are updated by AdamW. The learned update merges back into W_ref for inference. KL controls policy change in output space; GCPO controls feasible directions of parameter change — complementary, not redundant.

The Diagnostic Half

A dimension-corrected, stepwise Principal-Subspace Overlap measure O_excess = O_t − O_null decomposes each realized update into four orthogonal energy blocks.

93.8%Qwen3-8B energy in doubly-orthogonal block (avg)
97.7%GLM4-9B, same

Averages mask intermittent positive spikes that coincide with — and often precede — validation degradation. A controlled dose-dependent intervention (step 150, Qwen3-8B/ToolAlpaca) moves the claim past pure correlation: principal-subspace injection hurts far more than a matched random-subspace perturbation.

Assessment

strengths vs. limitations

Strengths

  • Diagnostic → intervention → constraint arc is the right way to motivate a method; bilateral orthogonality is enforced exactly by construction, with formal guarantees (App. B).
  • Clean ablation grid (Left/Right/Bilateral · Principal/Orthogonal/Random · Soft/Hard/KL) actually explains why each choice matters.
  • Practically attractive: same memory as LoRA, mergeable, no extra rollout/loss — addresses instability, length inflation, and forgetting in one move.
  • Only method that improves tool-use after math-only RL on both backbones — a property more valuable than a small in-domain gain.

Limitations

  • Validated only at 8B/9B scale, three tasks, small held-out subsets, and a non-execution ToolAlpaca metric (action-name + JSON match, no tool runs).
  • Overlap→instability causal chain rests on a single in-text intervention + appendix sweeps; link to length inflation / reward hacking is conjectural.
  • Top-k protection assumes singular directions are a meaningful “structural proxy”; k=8 and protected layers are global hyperparameters, not learned.
  • No comparison to off-policy / preference-based post-training (DPO/KTO/OPD); full-param GRPO itself degrades on GLM4-9B (59.43 < 66.51 base).

Questions for Further Exploration

Does principal-subspace overlap & the bilateral constraint transfer to off-policy / preference-based post-training (DPO, KTO, OPD) and on-policy distillation?
How do gains behave at 30B–70B scale and on the full MATH / HumanEval+ / tool-execution benchmarks rather than the small held-out splits?
Could the protected rank k be chosen adaptively per layer (spectral energy / per-layer overlap variance) instead of a global k=8?
Is length-inflation suppression a direct consequence of shielding length-prior directions, or a side effect of entropy regularization?
Given GRPO itself degrades on GLM4-9B, how sensitive is the ranking to baseline hyperparameter tuning — are the baselines fairly optimized?
Can the diagnostic predict which capability will degrade early enough to enable a dynamic, overlap-triggered intervention instead of a permanent hard constraint?
