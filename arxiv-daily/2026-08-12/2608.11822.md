> **一句话总结：** 在一个已知存在"会表征但不会使用"因果结构的 25.7M 因果判别 Transformer 上，作者用全程预注册的 detect-localize-release 流水线证明：定位成功（移植激活可恢复 89% 的目标行为），但"释放"在两个互相独立的环节上失败——OOD 检测器在 2,400 个真正需要干预的样本上触发 0 次（彻底反转），而即使移除门控、无条件注入每实例线性方向，剂量–响应曲线也在远低于预注册阈值处饱和（截距 0.382→0.264，门限 ≤0.08），表明该位点上的整个线性释放族是被"有界"的。

> **TL;DR:** A fully preregistered end-to-end test of the detect-localize-release pipeline on C1, a 25.7M causal-evidence-discrimination transformer with a documented suppression phenomenon (latent causal structure is linearly decodable but behaviorally unused). Localization passes decisively: activation transplant at the L23obs observation-evidence site restores 89% of the target behavioral shift (paired release advantages 0.563 and 0.854, 97.5% CIs excluding zero) on the 48/608 measurable held-out worlds (CONF50). Release then fails twice and independently: the OOD detector calibrated to fire on zero calibration-OOD worlds triggers on 0/2,400 CONF50 generations that need it (while firing on 6.9–7.3% of held-out ID40 generations), silently reducing the gated pipeline to the base model (release intercept b=0.382 vs. frozen threshold ≤0.08); and with the gate removed entirely, the linear release family's dose-response plateaus far below sufficiency (intercept 0.382 → 0.311 → 0.264 across doses λ=0/0.5/1.0, still >0.18 above the ≤0.08 margin) with per-instance adaptivity adding only ±0.03 over a fixed direction. The representation-behavior gap decomposes into at least three dissociable obstacles, only one of which (localization) is solved.

## 核心贡献

- **提出并执行了一个全程预注册、端到端的 detect-localize-release 流水线**，对"模型表征了但行为上不使用"的结构能否被释放到行为中，给出完整的压力测试。所有阈值、评估集、统计量、逐字声明模板（claim template）和决策树分支在对应数据出现之前都用 MD5 哈希归档。
- **将"表征–行为鸿沟"分解为至少三个互相独立、各自足以使朴素流水线失败的障碍**：(i) 可解码但不与行为耦合（Stage 1）；(ii) 可定位但不可门控（OOD 检测器反转，B6.5）；(iii) 门控可移除但线性方向族被有界（B6.6）。任何单一失败都不能支撑正确的"补救"叙事。
- **证明定位是真实且位点特异的**：在 C1 的中间层观测证据行通道（L23obs）上做激活移植，可恢复 89% 的目标行为偏移（最佳位点平均释放率 0.8886），两对控制对的配对释放优势 0.563 与 0.854，97.5% 置信区间均排除零。
- **识别出一类被标准评估忽略的"静默门控失败"**：检测器在所有校准统计上完美、ID 行为被保留、干预却从不在真正需要的样本上运行——只有把门控的逐生成触发计数作为一阶级指标，才能捕获此类失败（作者自己的审计日志最初都误读了这一点）。
- **在一个经移植验证的因果位点上，给出了线性方向族"剂量–响应单调但有界"的精确刻画**，指出当定位确定无疑时，线性 steering 失败不能再简单归因于"选错了层或方向"——线性参数化本身是候选瓶颈。
- **方法论贡献**：哈希优先的判据归档、逐字声明模板、决策树冻结分支、强制性 pilot 透明披露，以及门控级触发计数审计——为"找到结构≠控制结构"这一日益增长的文献提供了一个难以事后辩解的反例规范。

## Key Contributions

- **Runs a fully preregistered, end-to-end detect-localize-release pipeline** on a system with a known representation-behavior gap. Every threshold, evaluation set, statistic, verbatim claim template, and decision-tree branch was MD5-hashed and archived before any corresponding data existed; missed criteria follow pre-written failure branches and thresholds are never lowered.
- **Decomposes the representation-behavior gap into at least three independent obstacles**, each sufficient on its own to sink a naive pipeline: decodable-but-not-behaviorally-coupled (Stage 1), localizable-but-not-gateable (OOD-inverted detector, B6.5), and gate-removable-but-not-linearly-releasable (bounded direction family, B6.6). Reporting any single failure alone would imply a different (and wrong) remediation.
- **Establishes that localization is real and site-specific.** Activation transplant at the L23obs observation-evidence channel restores 89% of the behavioral shift removed by evidence-zeroing (best-site mean release rate R=0.8886); paired release advantages 0.563 and 0.854 with 97.5% Bonferroni-corrected CIs excluding zero.
- **Identifies a "silent gating failure" hazard that standard held-out metrics miss.** The detector was perfect on every calibration statistic and left ID behavior intact, yet triggered on 0/2,400 generations that needed intervention—GATE ≡ BASE. The paper recommends gate-level trigger counts on the deployment distribution as a first-class metric, and honestly documents that its own audit log initially misread the failure.
- **Gives a precise, monotone dose-response characterization of a linear direction family at a causally verified site**, showing it plateaus at more than three times the release margin with adaptive gain worth at most ±0.03—implying that linear steering failures elsewhere cannot be automatically blamed on "wrong layer/direction."
- **Methodological contribution: a preregistration discipline for ML interpretability** (hash-first criteria, frozen verbatim claim templates, decision-tree branches bound to outcomes, mandatory pilot reporting alongside confirmatory results, gate-level auditing) that the author argues is the contribution most likely to transfer.

## Methodology

The paper studies **C1**, a frozen 25.7M-parameter GPT-style decoder-only transformer (8 layers, naive attention, vocab 41) from prior work (Xun 2026, arXiv:2607.29484), trained on **causal-evidence discrimination**: prompts present observational evidence under a structural causal model and the model answers interventional queries do(X=x0) → Y=?. The training distribution contains **Simpson-trap worlds** where observational correlation and causal effect have opposite signs. The suppression phenomenon of interest: on held-out confounded worlds the model tracks the observational association even though the causal structure is linearly decodable from its residual stream. No parameter of C1 is ever updated.

**Behavioral measurement** rests on a two-point response grid. For a world w with answer span [lo, hi], the unit-span slope is Δ = (parse(r(hi)) − parse(r(lo))) / (hi − lo). Each world supplies three slopes: Δ_same (same evidence), Δ_zero (evidence zeroed at obs channels), Δ_p (under intervention). The denominator D = Δ_zero − Δ_same measures how much behavior zeroing removes (a world is "measurable" iff |D| ≥ 0.05). The release rate R = (Δ_p − Δ_same) / (Δ_zero − Δ_same) is the fraction of the zeroed-evidence shift restored by the intervention (R=1 perfect, R=0 none, R<0 wrong-direction overshoot). For population-level release an offset-gain regression Δ_w = a·ATE_w + b is fit; the preregistered release criterion requires intercept b ≤ +0.08 with ID slope a degrading by ≤ 0.05.

**The CCG (Contrastive Causal Gate) pipeline** has four components: (i) detect — linear probe u(x) over PCA(k=8) of site features, compared to a frozen threshold T; if u(x) < T the prompt follows the ID branch unmodified; (ii) contrast — a second forward pass with evidence zeroed at observation channels; (iii) generate — a direction δ, either a calibration-frozen mean-difference vector δ_ZERO (Eq. 8) or a per-instance δ(x) = MeanPool_raw(h_zero(x)) − MeanPool_raw(h_same(x)) (Eq. 9) with zero learned parameters; (iv) inject — residual modification h^(ℓ)(x) ← h^(ℓ)(x) + λ·δ(x) at the site, followed by a guarded second decoding. The localization stage uses **activation transplant** in the causal-mediation tradition: the site's residual rows of a suppressed world are replaced with those of its released counterpart (oracle access).

**Evaluation sets** are fixed: CONF50 (50 held-out confounded worlds at full power), ID40 (40 held-out non-confounded worlds), and a 40-world calibration set (3 seeds). Statistics use Wilson 95% intervals for rates, bootstrap 95% intervals for intercepts, Bonferroni-corrected CIs for localization, and McNemar exact tests for paired correctness. Total compute was 4.22 GPU-hours on a single consumer GPU.

## Results & Highlights

- **Localization passes decisively (Section 5, B0-rep).** Of 608 screened worlds, M=48 met eligibility (rate 0.0789, Wilson 95% [0.0601, 0.1031], exceeding the preregistered floor of 40). Both control pairs pass criterion C1 with paired release advantages of **L23 pair 0.5628 [0.3932, 0.7302]** and **L345 pair 0.8542 [0.6088, 1.0995]** (97.5% Bonferroni CIs excluding zero, vs. R=0 "no effect"); the best site L23obs attains mean release rate **R_site = 0.8886** (criterion C2 threshold ≥0.5) — i.e., transplant restores 89% of the behavioral shift that evidence-zeroing removes, vs. 0% for an unmodified suppressed model.
- **Failure 1 — the gate inverts out of distribution (Section 6, B6.5).** On the 2,400 CONF50 generations the entire apparatus exists for, the detector triggered **0/2,400 times** (GATE ≡ BASE); simultaneously it fired on **6.9–7.3% (44/44/47 of n_gen=640) of held-out ID40 generations** where intervention is harmful — above the frozen cap of 0.02 (B65e FAIL). The release intercept was **b = 0.3820 [0.3396, 0.4230]** against the frozen threshold b ≤ 0.08 (B65a FAIL), and 25/27 negative-ATE worlds were sign-reversed (B65c FAIL, cap 14/27). The intervention never ran on the worlds that needed it, yet every calibration-visible metric had been perfect.
- **Failure 2 — the linear release family is bounded (Section 7, B6.6).** With the gate removed entirely and the per-instance direction injected unconditionally, behavior moved in the right direction but plateaued far below the margin. The offset-gain intercept fell monotonically with dose — **BASE (λ=0) 0.3820 → ARM-S (λ=0.5) 0.3106 → ARM-R (λ=1.0) 0.2637** — yet the full-strength arm remained >0.18 above the frozen release threshold b ≤ 0.08. Per-instance adaptivity added only **−0.0199 (ARM-R) / +0.0268 (ARM-S)** per world over the always-on fixed direction, against the ~0.18 gap remaining. ID behavior was unharmed (slope drops −0.017 / −0.024, under the 0.05 cap), so the bound is not collateral damage. Verdict: R2_NO_MARGIN.
- **Stage 1 motivates the whole pipeline by failing three gates (Table 1).** A k=32 MLP probe separated world families with in-distribution AUC 0.962, but behaviorally coupled readout existed on only **11/50 worlds** (B0, threshold ≥25), pairwise CKA between confounded and observational features was **0.061–0.068** vs. threshold 0.7 and within-family reference 0.431 (B0.5), and worst leave-one-family-out AUC collapsed to **0.366** with **zero-shot CLadder transfer AUC 0.022–0.292 in reversed direction** (B1) — the probe's ranking anti-predicts external causal difficulty.

Single-authored by **Xining Xun** (Tsingjiao Information Science (Beijing) Co., Ltd.). The work builds directly on the author's own prior artifact (Xun 2026, arXiv:2607.29484, the C1 checkpoint), so the suppression phenomenon under test is independently documented and the checkpoint is public and frozen. Credibility is unusually high for a negative-result paper because of the preregistration apparatus: 25 audit-log entries (B0-REG through B0-REG-19) bind every criteria/code artifact by MD5 hash before any governed data, verdicts are bound to pre-written claim templates (the canonical archived text is Chinese, with faithful English translations in the paper), and the authorship re-ran both terminal experiments' statistics from the released per-world records and reports field-by-field agreement. Notably, the paper voluntarily discloses that its own audit log initially misread the B6.5 failure ("injection perturbs behavior without benefit") and documents the correction in the same append-only log — exactly the kind of error that motivates the gate-level auditing recommendation. The AI-use, ethics, and reproducibility statements are present and appropriate; total cost is a transparent 4.22 GPU-hours.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** High and well-scoped. The decomposition of "knows but doesn't use" into three dissociable obstacles — each independently sufficient — is a genuinely useful refinement over the concurrent "perfect detection, failed control" framing (Anonymous 2026, arXiv:2606.24952). The hash-first preregistration discipline with verbatim claim templates is rare in ML interpretability and is, as the author claims, plausibly the most transferable contribution.
- **Rigor:** Exceptional for the scope. Bonferroni-corrected CIs, Wilson/bootstrap intervals used appropriately, McNemar exact tests, a preregistered ±0.01 guard-band sensitivity check, and independent recomputation of every verdict from released records. The honest disclosure of the B6.5 misread (Appendix A, B0-REG-16 addendum) is exactly the behavior one wants.
- **Generalizability — the main caveat, and the author flags it.** The entire quantitative result is from **a single 25.7M-parameter model on a synthetic causal domain**. The author is explicit that "only the dissociation pattern is a candidate for generality" and that the specific numbers are C1's. Whether the OOD-inverted-detector failure mode and the bounded-linear-family result replicate in larger models and natural-language domains (e.g., truthfulness/refusal steering) is untested; the paper offers convergent evidence from Anonymous (2026) but no direct replication.
- **Selection effects in the positive result.** The localization pass rests on an eligibility rate of only 7.9% (48/608 worlds) after a denominator guard that excludes 39/50 stage-1 worlds. The author reports the rate and interval rather than extrapolating, which is correct, but a reader should weigh the localization claim as characterizing a selected subpopulation, not the full confounded-world distribution.
- **Scope of the negative claim is narrow but honestly bounded.** The "bounded linear release" verdict is explicitly scoped to single-site, residual-additive, linear directions at the tested resolution. The author repeatedly notes it does not touch nonlinear or distributed release, and even pre-commits (via frozen decision-tree clauses) not to launch the B7 escalation experiment once R2 fires. This discipline is commendable but means the negative result cannot rule out that a nonlinear or multi-site mechanism would succeed at the same site — it only rules out the linear family as parameterized.
- **Potential weakness in the detector baseline.** The gate failure is demonstrated with a deliberately light detector (PCA k=8 + logistic regression). A more expressive detector, or one calibrated with held-out examples of the positive class's test-time realization, might behave differently; the author concedes this but argues (fairly) that the no-held-out-positive calibration is precisely the deployment condition the pipeline was designed for. Still, the "gate inversion" finding is somewhat specific to that detector class.
- **Red flags:** None significant. No cherry-picked benchmarks (criteria were frozen before data), no overclaiming (the paper's headline is a failure decomposition), and the compute budget is trivially small and fully disclosed.

## Strengths

- Preregistration is not decorative: every terminal verdict traces to a hashed artifact with a pre-written claim template, and the decision tree's branches were frozen before data, foreclosing the usual post-hoc re-narration (re-thresholding the gate, redefining release around slope instead of intercept, promoting the adaptive-gain sign flip into a story).
- The decomposition itself is the insight. Showing that localization, gating, and linear release fail for independent reasons — and that no single failure alone licenses the right conclusion — is more scientifically useful than any individual positive or negative number.
- The "silent gating failure" finding is independently important for practice: any triggered intervention system (selective steering, refusal circuits, tool-routing heads) can fail this quietly, and standard held-out metrics will not catch it unless the trigger itself is audited on the deployment distribution.
- Honest, documented self-correction of the B6.5 misread in the audit log, used as evidence for the very recommendation being made.
- Cheap and fully reproducible: 4.22 GPU-hours, public frozen checkpoint, released per-world records, code manifests, and figure-generation scripts.

## Limitations

- **Single small synthetic-domain model.** Only C1 (25.7M, synthetic causal-evidence task) is studied; the specific numbers are not claimed to transfer, only the dissociation pattern. Replication in larger models and on natural-language steering tasks (truthfulness, refusal, sentiment) is left to future work.
- **Linear, single-site, residual-additive release only.** The negative release claim is explicitly scoped to that family at that resolution and does not address nonlinear, multi-site, or non-additive release mechanisms.
- **Aggressive measurability/eligibility filtering.** The denominator guard excludes 39/50 Stage-1 worlds from rate statistics, and localization eligibility is 7.9% (48/608); all reported rates describe a selected measurable subpopulation.
- **Detector class is light.** The OOD-inverted-gate result is demonstrated with PCA(k=8)+logistic-regression; a detector calibrated with held-out examples of the positive class's test-time realization, or a more expressive detector, is untested (though the author argues this is the intended deployment condition).
- **Oracle access in the localization stage.** Transplant uses the released counterpart of each suppressed world, so localization demonstrates sufficiency in principle, not that a deployable pipeline can reproduce the transplant — which is precisely what the subsequent (failing) stages test.
- The frozen decision tree's refusal to launch the B7 escalation after R2 is methodologically correct but means the paper stops at "linear is bounded" without probing whether a deliberately nonlinear release could close the gap at the same verified site.

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the OOD-inverted-detector failure mode replicate in larger natural-language models and on real steering tasks (e.g., truthfulness via ITI, refusal-direction gating), or is it specific to C1's synthetic causal domain and the light PCA+logistic detector? Could a detector calibrated with held-out examples of the positive class's test-time realization avoid the inversion while remaining deployable?
- At the same causally verified L23obs site, can a nonlinear or multi-site release mechanism (e.g., a small MLP direction generator, or distributed injection across several layers) close the ~0.18 intercept gap that the entire linear family could not — under the same preregistered discipline?
- The paper recommends reporting gate-level trigger counts on the target distribution as a first-class metric. How would existing activation-steering benchmarks (CAA, ITI, RepE) change their conclusions if silent gating failures were audited rather than averaging over generations?
- Is the bounded-linear-release result a property of the linear parametrization, of the single-site resolution, or of C1's specific representation geometry? A CKA / SAE-based analysis of whether the causal information is distributed nonlinearly across the residual stream could distinguish these.
- Can the three-obstacle decomposition be operationalized as a diagnostic protocol — a fixed sequence of preregistered gates (decodable? behaviorally coupled? aligned across distribution? gateable? linearly releasable?) — that any new "locate-and-release" claim must pass before being reported as control?
