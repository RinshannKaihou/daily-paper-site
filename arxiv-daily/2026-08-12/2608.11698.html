01

Methodology

REOPD operates inside the G-OPD framework, where the teacher–reference log-ratio r_t = log π_T − log π_ref is an implicit token reward and ExOPD scales its beyond-teacher residual by a constant (λ−1). REOPD's only change replaces that global scalar with a per-token effective coefficient λ_{b,t} = 1 + γ_b·q_t.

Token path (compatibility). From already-computed student/teacher log-probs it forms the k3 discrepancy proxy δ̂ = exp(x) − x − 1 (x = log π_T − log π_θ) and a detached weight q = exp(−δ̂/τ) ∈ (0,1]. Low student–teacher discrepancy ⇒ q ≈ 1 (keep residual); high discrepancy ⇒ q ≪ 1 (suppress). It measures local compatibility, not correctness.

Controller path (budget). Two sufficient statistics — compatible-residual proportion ρ_b and reliable-residual RMS scale s_b — are summed across data-parallel ranks, EMA-smoothed, and converted to a bounded budget γ_b = clip(B₀·ρ̄ / (s̄+ε), 0, γ_max). The teacher-alignment term a is left untouched; the per-token advantage is A = −(a − γ_b·q_t·r_t), fed to the same PPO-style surrogate as the OPD baseline.

No verifier, reward model, value model, or extra rollout is needed — the only operational addition is an all-reduce of two scalar statistics. Special cases: γ_b = 0 recovers plain OPD; q = 1, γ_b = λ−1 recovers fixed-λ ExOPD.

03

Strengths & Limitations

Strengths

  • Clean separation: preserves the teacher-alignment term and adapts only the beyond-teacher residual — sidesteps reward hacking.
  • Zero extra infrastructure: reuses existing log-probs, no verifier/reward/value model, adds just an all-reduce of two scalars.
  • Decisive ablation isolating token compatibility as the workhorse (−4.27 pp when removed).
  • One shared controller works across math and code in multi-teacher settings, no per-domain tuning.
  • Honest scope: explicitly states the weight is a discrepancy proxy, not a correctness estimator.

Limitations

  • Statistical power: 120 unique math problems; ≤0.42 pp deltas within plausible noise, no error bars or multi-seed runs.
  • qt cannot catch tokens where student and teacher agree but are both wrong (compatibility ≠ correctness).
  • Controller saturates at γmax late in training — batch-level contribution largely vanishes at convergence.
  • Still has residual hyperparameters (τ, γmax, B₀ calibration); statistics depend on micro-batch composition.
  • Narrow evaluation: one model (Qwen3-4B), one teacher config, one checkpoint (step 50).
04

Open Questions

For Further Exploration

  1. Do the +0.03–0.42 pp deltas survive multi-seed runs and bootstrap CIs at 120 problems?
  2. Once γ saturates, does the method collapse to token-only control — would decaying B₀ change late dynamics?
  3. Does compatibility generalize to a stronger-teacher / weaker-student gap, or open-ended generation?
  4. Can ρb, sb be augmented with a cheap correctness signal to also suppress jointly-wrong tokens?
  5. Would a per-teacher controller beat the shared one across very different residual distributions?
  6. Are REOPD's gains stable across training, or concentrated at the step-50 checkpoint only?
