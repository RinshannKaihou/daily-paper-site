Methodological caveat
01

Methodology — how the premise was tested

The authors ask whether hiding option labels from the model’s commitment step removes positional bias and thereby raises accuracy. They benchmark two label-free strategies — two-stage prompting (free-text generation, then matching back to options) and independent hypothesis scoring (one call per option, argmax of a 0–100 confidence) — against direct MCQ, cyclic permutation (k rotated calls + majority vote), and PriDe (logit-based prior subtraction). A 2×2 grid crosses Stage-1 option visibility with LLM vs. embedding matching to localize where two-stage breaks. Six models (GPT-4.1 mini, Gemini 2.5 Flash, Llama 3.1 8B, Qwen 2.5 7B — both API and local) run on MMLU and ARC-Challenge (~1000 items each, temperature 0, seed 42, ~128 GPU-hours). Diagnostics span per-question flip rate across four rotations, recall standard deviation across gold positions, McNemar tests, Clopper–Pearson and 10k-resample bootstrap CIs, and a 10-seed tie-break sweep.

03

Strengths & limitations, side by side

+ Strengths

  • Honest negative result with a clear, citable takeaway — accuracy and order-sensitivity decouple.
  • The 2×2 diagnostic grid cleanly attributes two-stage failure to option-withholding.
  • Excellent statistical discipline: Clopper–Pearson, McNemar, bootstrap CIs, seed sweep.
  • Full open-source release (MIT) with snapshotted prompts; ~128 GPU-hour budget disclosed.
  • Authors actively argue against their own headline win rather than cherry-pick it.

! Limitations

  • Single-run design — no identical-order controls; provider non-determinism unquantified and may inflate flip rates.
  • Narrow scope: only 4-option English MCQ on MMLU + ARC (1000 items each); no MMLU-Pro / frontier reasoning models.
  • Each strategy tested in one instantiation — no reasoning-enabled Stage 1, no stronger separate matcher.
  • Coverage artifacts: semantic cells score <1000/1000, inflating apparent position-blindness; one cell excluded for API failures.
  • Under-specification mechanism is cited, not tested on the authors’ own data (no stratification by question type).
04

Open questions for further exploration

  • Would a reasoning-enabled Stage 1 recover two-stage losses, or is option-withholding fundamentally lossy?
  • Does the decoupling hold for 10-way MMLU-Pro, GPQA-Diamond, or frontier reasoning models?
  • How much flip rate is provider non-determinism vs. genuine positional sensitivity?
  • Can a stronger separate matcher rescue semantic matching, or is the hidden-options bottleneck matcher-independent?
