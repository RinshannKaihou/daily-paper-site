Why this matters

Any claim that a model "learns X at layer k" can be off by a fifth of the network whenever neighboring layers are correlated.

Scope: layer-wise probing in LLM hidden states as much as in co-folding trunks.

Critical edge

The −19 is an illustration from one synthetic construction — a single gain ramp at a single ρ = 0.9 — while the real-null audit (0.12–0.17 at three layers) is the sturdier evidence.

Critical Assessment · point 1

01

The rule fails, and simulation undersells it

Two measurements of the same error: a synthetic stack with known ground truth, then 100 real null trajectories per regime from a frozen Boltz-1 trunk.

Exact, and one-directional. With an injected onset at stage 20, three-layer persistence at ρ = 0.9 returns stage 1. At ρ = 0 the same weak signal returns stage 22 — the benign direction. Five layers returns 20 exactly.

Intervals fail independently. In the clean scenario the 3-layer point estimate is exactly right while its bootstrap interval is [0, 21]; at five layers it collapses to [20, 21]. A curve can look like a clean pass and still be uninformative.

Real nulls are worse than predicted. The 3-layer rule fires on 12–17% of null trajectories against a nominal 5%. Five layers brings it to 4–7%; the single-layer rule fires on roughly seven in ten.

Onset cannot discriminate. All 48 layers clear threshold in all four regimes and all three feature sets, so the pre-registered onset is layer 0 and Δonset = 0. Peak decodability still ranges from 3.4× to 9.9× a random ordering of residues.

FN is last — not NN. At the common layer on the per-residue track, FN scores 0.230 against NF's 0.601, a 2.6× gap. The ordering holds under peak AUPRC and under ℓ90 in every feature set.

The primary endpoint is null. FF–NN is +0.053 [−0.096, +0.169], p = 0.43, reported as uninformative rather than reinterpreted. Only FN contrasts survive Holm: 9 of 9, largest adjusted p = 0.015; 0 of 9 others.

03

How the estimator was built

Six moves, each doing a distinct job. The persistence length and α were fixed on synthetic data with known ground truth before any real representation was touched.

01

Freeze the trunk

Boltz-1, all 48 Pairformer blocks, no fine-tuning. The question is what is already linearly readable, not what training could produce.

02

Cross two axes

Family familiarity (MMseqs clusters) × pocket familiarity (max qcov against the pre-cutoff pool) gives FF, FN, NF, NN.

03

Split at the cutoff

2021-09-30. From 25,289 post-cutoff systems with pockets, 1,820 are extracted and 534 test proteins evaluated.

04

Probe every block

A linear probe per layer and regime on per-residue s, pooled z, and their concatenation. AUPRC, because positives are roughly 1 in 15.

05

Build a correlated null

Labels permuted within each protein, the same permutation held across all 48 layers — so nulls are trajectories, not independent draws.

06

Threshold, then persist

The 96th of 100 per-layer permutation values (α = 0.05), five adjacent layers, audited leave-one-out against B−1 replicates.

04

What holds, and what it cannot yet claim

The measurement discipline is the contribution. The empirical result is bounded to one trunk, one release, and a linear readout.

Strengths

  • Pre-registration with teeth. Persistence, α, the primary contrast and the 18-test multiplicity family were fixed before real representations were touched.
  • The null preserves the structure under test. Within-protein permutation, held fixed across depth, is what makes the false-positive rate meaningful.
  • The error rate is measured, not asserted. Leave-one-out on the study's own nulls, across four regimes.
  • Honest nulls and a public service. A null primary endpoint is reported as null, and Boltz-2's 128 missing parameters (64 blocks) are documented.

Limitations

  • One trunk, one release, 534 proteins. AlphaFold 3, Chai-1 and Protenix share the cutoff and are the obvious replication.
  • Linear readout only. The capacity ladder and gated head that would separate "not there" from "not linearly reachable" are specified but never run.
  • ℓ90 has no intervals — yet it is the statistic that carries the depth comparison.
  • The 2.6× gap is metric-dependent. Under an alternative pocket metric the FF–FN contrast falls from 0.300 to 0.087.
05

Open questions

Q1

What persistence length does a real LLM residual stream need, given its measured layer-to-layer autocorrelation?

Q2

When does per-layer thresholding actually beat a single global threshold? Here τ moved by only 0.003 across 48 blocks.

Q3

Does the FN ordering survive on a second co-folding trunk trained to the same 2021-09-30 cutoff?
