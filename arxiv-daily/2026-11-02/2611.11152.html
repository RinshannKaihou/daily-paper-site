  1. Dense means dense. Attention and MLP weights in all 12 Transformer blocks of both encoders; norms, embeddings, projections and the logit scale stay frozen.
  2. Same recipe for everyone. SGD (no momentum), SMD, Adam/AdamW at three weight decays, Shampoo and SOAP — plus 11 PEFT baselines, each later retrained under both Adam and SGD.
  3. Diagnose before blaming. Encoder-restricted ratios Rv = ‖vfew‖/‖vfull‖ and Rm, collapse factors, the global update budget ro(t), and direction cosines that separate numerator error from denominator error.
  4. One scalar, kept honest. Adam-M0 rescales the learning rate once at step 0 so rAdam(0) = rSGD(0), leaving Adam's moment recursion intact; Adam-Mt repeats it every step. LR sweeps and warmup test the conventional fixes.

03

Trade-offs

what holds · what doesn't

Strengths

  • Reusable diagnostics. Numerator / denominator / full-data decomposition at encoder and block level — more informative than one accuracy table.
  • Clean causal design. A single scalar swings accuracy 61.5 points; matching the numerator alone does not.
  • Unusual control depth. AdamW ×3 weight decays, Shampoo/SOAP, 11 PEFT ×2 optimizers, LR + warmup sweeps, SigLIP replication, dataset-clustered intervals.
  • Honest negatives. LP++ — their own CVPR 2024 method — hits 21.5% at 1-shot under Adam and 62.5% under SGD; the SGD support gradient is nearly orthogonal to the full-data gradient.

Limitations

  • One recipe. 30 epochs, lr 1e-4, batch 64, no wd/warmup — the abstract's “Adam collapses” reads as a general claim.
  • Missing control. Full-data Adam at 1e-4 is never run, so few-shot miscalibration and generic η√d scaling stay entangled.
  • Protocol-dependent mechanism. Rv(0) is 0.15–0.72 in Fig. 4(a) but 1.256 at K=1 in Table 65; at K=16 the denominator is calibrated (1.037) yet Adam still trails by 52.2 points.
  • Untuned baselines. Rank 2, Adam 1e-4, 30 epochs, never checked against published numbers; the +0.4-point cell at ViT-L/14 16-shot sits inside seed noise.
04

Open questions

the controls still missing
  1. What is rAdam(0)/rSGD(0) for full-data Adam at lr 1e-4 on the same 122M-parameter model? If it is also 10–100×, the few-shot framing needs re-scoping.
  2. Does a blockwise or depth-aware budget schedule beat both global Adam-M0 and SGD — or does the block structure wash out?
  3. Does dense SGD still win on ImageNet-V2/R/A, ObjectNet, retrieval and segmentation, or is +0.76 a property of 11 clean classification benchmarks?
