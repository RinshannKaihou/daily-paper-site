One objective, two knobs

01direction vdifference of means from paired examples
02penalty ω•(θ)expression or reliance
03update θmin R + λω•
04audit L1–L5scored apart from behavior
  • Objective. min R(θ) + λ·ω•(θ) — the task loss plus one constraint on an internal concept score.
  • Expression penalizes the probe score itself: whether the concept shows up in activations or outputs. In RL it is subtracted from the reward.
  • Reliance penalizes squared concept attribution ν(z,v)² along a difference-of-means direction, using one of seven kernels (Gradient, Gradient×Input, IG, SmoothGrad, Noise, Shuffle, Deletion).
  • Why split them? Refusing a harmful request must depend on recognizing harm without expressing it.
  • Direction is refreshed periodically — a frozen direction invites re-encoding. DoM reaches ≈90% WGA where a logistic-probe direction reaches ≈80%.
  • Monitoring is never folded into the behavioral metric; every result carries a held-out detector reading at a declared level.

03

Where it holds, where it slips

both, at full weight

+ Strengths

  • The split earns its keep. Reliance vs. expression explains why “just filter the data” fails for sycophancy: the signal lives in the aggregate, not in any single example.
  • L1–L5 is a protocol, not a worry. “Did the probe die or did the concept move?” becomes measurable and falsifiable, applied across five domains.
  • Detection ≠ shaping. DoM ≈90% vs. logistic probe ≈80% WGA is a concrete trap most practitioners would fall into by default.
  • Unusual breadth. Three modalities, seven model families, four training paradigms — with a monitorability read-out in every one.

− Limitations

  • Single seed on the headline table. Harmfulness results pick the lowest JBB over a large hyperparameter grid — a min-over-noise selection, unquantified.
  • Bespoke benchmark. The 2,066-image perfect-confound split makes 51.6% incomparable to published Waterbirds ERM; the 512-image test set gets no confidence intervals.
  • “Honest solve” may be another shortcut. The Countdown target is always the lowest interval endpoint, so 0.96–0.98 may be a different specification violation.
  • Theory outruns evidence. The product-code account is one seed in toys; in LLMs a nonlinear reader adds only 0.02–0.04, yet the abstract reads as universal.

Still open

what would change the reading
Q1

How much of the 0.96–0.98 “honest solve” is the interval shortcut rather than arithmetic? A randomized-target control would settle it.

Q2

Attention heads are roughly bilinear. Do they read second-order codes directly, collapsing the L3/L4 distinction in real LLMs?

Q3

If gradients through the probe destroy it, is “detach the score” a general prescription or specific to REINFORCE-style estimators?
