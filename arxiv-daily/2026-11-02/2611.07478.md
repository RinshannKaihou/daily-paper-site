> **一句话总结：** 作者在 5 个开源模型 × 2 个任务（4×4 行列式、分部积分）共 10 个 cell 上，用 logit lens + DLA 把"写错一个负号"定位到网络最后四分之一的 MLP 层（72–100% 的错误都在这段峰值），再沿 `WU("−") − WU("+")` 方向做减法干预：Llama-3.3-70B 的 IBP cell 有 93%（13/14）的负号错误被翻转，而同样"定位得很好"的 Phi-4-14B 只有 4.1%（5/122）——定位好并不等于可操纵，真正的预测因子是机制类型（固定"盖章"层 vs 放大器）和冗余度，而不是位置。

> **TL;DR:** Across five open models (Phi-4-14B, Mistral-Small-24B, Gemma-3-27B, Llama-3.3-70B, Qwen-2.5-72B) and two procedural tasks — a 4×4 determinant bank generated to LinAlg-Bench spec plus a custom 1,294-problem poly-trig integration-by-parts bank — the authors replay each model's own wrong solution and show the sign token is committed in the last quarter of depth (72–100% of confirmed errors; only 26/703 = 3.7% commit genuinely early). They then subtract the unembedding sign direction at the identified "habit layers": on held-out minus-written errors at dose α=3 this flips 93% (13/14) on Llama IBP and 70% (39/56) on Mistral IBP, but only 4.1% (5/122) on Phi determinant and 4.4% (3/68) on Phi IBP, while a magnitude-matched random direction flips 1/923 cases (0.1%). Phi is the best-localized model (97% det, 100% IBP) and the least steerable — its wrong-sign margins are a median 17.8 logits and the sign is spread over ~15 late MLP layers, so the same edit cuts the lead roughly in half without crossing zero. 23 of 29 git-frozen preregistered predictions confirmed, misses disclosed. Worth reading if you use activation steering or circuit localization as a repair tool; the caveat is that the "steerability" axis measures minus-token suppression, not error repair.

## 核心贡献

- **定位（§3）：** 在 5 个模型家族、2 个任务、10 个 model×task cell 上，符号决策的 logit-lens 峰值统一落在相对深度 ≥ 0.75 的最后四分之一；错误与正确答案的提交时机一样晚，所以"晚"刻画的是写符号这件事本身，而不是失败。真正早期提交的错误只有 26/703（3.7%），文中逐案列出。
- **机制分型（§4）：** 用 per-layer MLP DLA 按"模型实际写出的符号"分组，得到可证伪的二分：两组柱子同向 = **amplifier**（放大已选符号，如 Llama L79），反向 = **stamp**（无论对错都推「−」，如 Qwen L75/L78）。Qwen 还多一个 rescue layer（L77）专门顶住 minus stamp，Qwen 出错就是"默认的减号没被顶住"。关键规律：**只有减号会被盖章**，没有任何模型有固定的加号 stamp。
- **因果电池（§5）：** 单层消融近零 + 五层联合消融大幅塌陷 = 冗余写入（Llama det −4.25、IBP −7.09 logits，超过全部 40 组随机五层对照）；逐头消融最多只值 0.46 logits，说明注意力不是载体。减法干预带剂量响应、magnitude-matched 随机方向对照、以及四 cohort 的特异性矩阵。
- **核心解离（§6，Figure 5）：** 10 个 cell 的定位率都高（72–100%），可操纵性却横跨 4–93%；定位最好的 Phi 恰恰最不可操纵。这是预先注册并命中的预测，不是事后叙事。
- **预注册（附录 I）：** 每个模型的 6 条预测（P1–P6）、数值通过线、排除规则、分母都在看到 held-out 数据前用 git commit 冻结（含冻结日期与 commit hash），最终 23/29 命中，未命中的（Gemma 4 个 near-miss、Qwen P3）逐条解释并保留原判。

## Key Contributions

- **Localization is universal, mechanism is not.** In all five families the written sign peaks in the final quarter of depth (determinant: Gemma 72%, Qwen 80%, Llama 94%, Mistral 95%, Phi 97%; IBP 76–100%), and correct answers commit just as late — so lateness is a property of sign-writing, not of failure. The authors also run an arithmetic control (48 plain subtractions, 237/240 correct) to show the effect is not an artifact of algebra.
- **A falsifiable mechanistic taxonomy.** Splitting per-layer MLP DLA by the sign the model actually wrote yields stamps (fixed minus vote regardless of correctness) versus amplifiers (support whatever was chosen), plus judges (late layers pushing back toward the true sign) and rescue layers. The typing is re-derived independently from the reverse-patching arm, which is a genuine internal consistency check rather than a relabeling.
- **A cohort-level causal battery, not a single-prompt demo.** 3,253 experiment-ready cases across ten cells, four cohorts per cell (error/correct × wrote-`+`/wrote-`−`), every effect read against matched random and control-layer nulls, and interventions run on correct answers with failures reported at full strength.
- **The dissociation, preregistered.** Localization (share of confirmed errors peaking past depth 0.75) versus steerability (share of held-out minus-written errors flipped at α=3) are uncorrelated across ten cells; the paper predicted in advance that the best-localized model would steer least, and it did.
- **A mechanism-level explanation of resistance.** Phi's immunity is traced to two measured properties: over-determined margins (det median 17.8 logits, max 43.4; IBP clustered at 28–32) and distributed writing (15 MLP layers carry positive credit; the two steered habit layers hold only 19% det / 26% IBP of it). The edit is a proportional cut (~0.58× det, ~0.51× IBP baseline, corr(baseline, Δlead) = −0.90/−0.98), so a 50% cut never crosses zero on a 17.8-logit margin.
- **Full release.** Code, per-case JSONs, frozen predictions and preregistration documents at `github.com/signcircuits-experiments/sign-circuit-scaling`.

## Methodology

**Data.** Determinant problems come from LinAlg-Bench (arXiv:2605.16675), a SymPy-certified 660-problem benchmark where sign errors were first isolated as a structural failure class (33.0% of all failures at 3×3; 270 sign errors total; parity subtypes surge from 3.0% to 61.0% of sign errors at 4×4, which fixes the 4×4 task size). Because the released bank yields too few sign errors for causal work, each model gets its own larger bank generated to the same spec (integer entries in [−5,5], exact symbolic ground truth, deduplicated): Gemma 4,050, Llama 2,850, Mistral 2,050, Phi 2,050, Qwen 675. The integration-by-parts task is new: a custom poly-trig generator, 1,294 problems per model, each forcing at least one application of ∫u dv = uv − ∫v du so the minus must be written explicitly. In total 18,145 problems were graded at temperature 0, producing 5,020 determinant and 4,745 IBP failures.

**Case harvesting.** Exact-match grading against symbolic ground truth sorts responses into error and correct pools. Wrong answers pass LinAlg-Bench's released three-judge pipeline (classification judge → independent validation judge → human adjudication for ambiguous cases), which keeps a case only if the first wrong value has the correct magnitude and the wrong sign. An LLM annotator marks the character offset of the wrong sign with ten characters of context, and a deterministic check verifies the offset and that it lies inside the model's answer. Correct cases enter by exact match alone and are probed at the *matched* sign site (a cofactor or parts-formula operator sign written correctly), so error and correct cohorts are compared at the same kind of token.

**Design.** Two batches, not a random split: a 1,000-case discovery batch used to pick layers, the sign direction and the pass bars, and a 2,253-case held-out batch reserved for the causal tests, with overlap checked at both ID and matrix-content level (Qwen lost 59 of 206 rows to content overlap). Every case is tagged by the sign actually written, giving four cohorts. Everything runs under **teacher-forced replay**: the model's own solution is replayed token by token and measurement happens at position −1 relative to the sign token, so the probe never alters the trajectory.

**Readouts.** (i) Logit lens: decode the residual stream into token scores at every layer; track the *sign lead* = logit(written sign) − logit(alternative), and define the peak as the layer of maximum lead, reported as relative depth layer/(n−1). (ii) Direct logit attribution (DLA): each layer's MLP output projected onto the final-layer probe direction, i.e. its direct contribution in logits to the written sign's lead. Late layers whose DLA tracks the *true* sign rather than the written one are "judges". Only lens-confirmed cases (final-layer lead ≥ 2 logits) enter the localization analysis.

**Interventions.** The sign direction is built from weights alone: d = WU(" −") − WU(" +"), multiplied elementwise by the final layer-norm scale, normalized to d̂. Removal at a layer is h ← h − α(h·d̂)d̂, so α=1 deletes the layer's d-component exactly and higher doses push past zero. Four interventions: single-layer and joint MLP ablation (s05), direction subtraction (s06), activation patching both forward (healthy→error) and reverse (error→correct) (s07), and two-layer dose–response steering at α = 1, 2, 3 (s08), plus a judge test at the Qwen rescue layer (s09). All are run under one fixed ten-stage pipeline s00–s09, identical across models.

**Controls and preregistration.** CTRL_MAG is a magnitude-matched random direction; control layers are fixed per model (e.g. L30/L50 for Llama and Qwen, L15/L25 for Mistral and Phi); joint ablations are compared against 40 matched random layer sets. Six predictions per model (P1 late decision, P2 habit-layer DLA positive, P3 one direction separates −/+ , P4 removing it moves errors dose by dose, P5 it also moves corrects as registered, P6 steering flips errors while control is flat) were frozen in git with numeric bars, denominators and exclusion rules before held-out scoring; 30 cells, 29 scored (Gemma's P5 has no bar after a pre-measurement amendment).

## Results & Highlights

- **Localization is uniformly high.** Across the ten cells, 72–100% of confirmed errors peak at relative depth ≥ 0.75 (Phi 97% det / 100% IBP, Mistral 95% det / 76% IBP, Llama 94% det / 100% IBP, Gemma 72% det / 94% IBP, Qwen 80% det / 100% IBP), against a residual of only 26/703 = 3.7% genuinely early commitments. The exploratory 675B run agrees: 39/51 (76%) of Mistral-Large-675B's 5×5 sign errors are late.
- **Steerability spans 4–93% at the same localization.** On held-out minus-written errors at α=3, Llama IBP flips 13/14 (92.9%) and Mistral IBP 39/56 (69.6%), while Phi flips 5/122 (4.1%) det and 3/68 (4.4%) IBP — a ~20× gap between the best-localized model (Phi, 97%/100%) and a merely well-localized one. Other cells: Gemma 17/41 (41.5%) det, Mistral 39/114 (34.2%) det, Llama 42/121 (34.7%) det, Qwen 6/25 (24.0%) det.
- **The control is essentially null, so the effect is direction-specific.** A magnitude-matched random direction flips 1 of 923 pooled cases (0.1%), and across all 5,100 control evaluations exactly one case flips. Control layers stay at zero throughout.
- **Specificity: the lever moves minus-ness, not wrongness.** At α=3 Llama breaks 131/138 (95%) of correct-minus determinant answers while leaving correct-plus untouched (0/72); Qwen breaks 66/159 (42%) of correct-minus and 0/29 correct-plus; Gemma's plus-written cohorts survive 129/131. The exception is Mistral, which "grips everything": it breaks 42/53 (79%) of correct-plus det and 79/85 (93%) of correct-plus IBP while still posting the highest flip counts (62/185 det, 69/91 IBP).
- **Necessity is set-level, not layer-level.** Removing the direction from any single layer is near-null (except Llama's final amplifier at α=2), and zeroing candidate heads costs at most 0.46 logits. The five-layer joint ablation collapses the wrong sign's lead in Llama (det −4.25, IBP −7.09 held-out) and Qwen det (−2.52), each exceeding all 40 matched random five-layer sets — the signature of redundant writing.
- **Phi's resistance is measured, not asserted.** Its determinant error-site margins are a median 17.8 logits (max 43.4) versus medians of 5.5–7.5 for Gemma/Llama/Qwen and 0.5–3.0 for Mistral; ablating its top 15 DLA layers cumulatively reaches only −1.34 logits, and mean-ablating its entire late window L24–39 moves the det lead by −1.28. At α=3 the edit tracks ≈0.58× (det) and ≈0.51× (IBP) of baseline, so only the two smallest-margin cases (3.5 and 3.75 logits) flip.
- **Preregistration held.** 23 of 29 scored predictions confirmed. Phi's column is fully green *including* three registered nulls (P4 joint ablation mean −0.192 vs a ≥ −1.0 bar; P5 0/146 and 4/146 flips; P6 6/145 = 4.1%). Misses are reported as misses: Qwen P3 separates at L79 (77.9% of pairs) rather than the frozen L75/L78, and Gemma takes four near-misses because its frozen sites L55/L58 were not the best sites (L58/L60).
- **The reverse arrow is asymmetric.** Transplanting an error-run message into correct runs breaks them (Mistral 79/107 = 73.8% at α=3, mean −11.18; Qwen's judge reads the injected content at L79 with +1.61 mean gap on all 123 minus-corrects), but transplanting a healthy message into error runs repairs almost nothing — at stamp sites the healthy and error messages are the same, so there is nothing error-specific to copy in.

## Authors & Credibility

Shradha Agarwal (Missouri University of Science and Technology, CS and Nuclear Engineering) is also first author of LinAlg-Bench, the benchmark this paper builds on — so the data pipeline, the judge protocol and the sign-error taxonomy are the authors' own prior work rather than a borrowed instrument, which explains the unusual level of per-case accounting (offset verification, funnel tables, file-verified counts). Tariq J. (CMPP, NED University of Engineering and Technology, Karachi; also MST) and Deepak Rajbhar (MST) complete a three-author team from a non-frontier-lab institution. That shows in the resource envelope: five models up to 72B, no 675B causal battery ("computationally prohibitive"), and IBP banks capped at 1,294 problems. It also shows in the rigor: a ten-stage fixed pipeline, per-case JSONs with source-file provenance for every number, a ledger cross-check (`paper/appendix/ledger_results.csv`), and a public repo. The self-citation is a real independence concern for the benchmark half (the failure taxonomy and the 92.6% human agreement are quoted from LinAlg-Bench, not re-validated here), but the mechanism and causal claims stand on new experiments.

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty.** The headline — localization ≠ steerability — is a direct extension of Hase et al. (NeurIPS 2023), who showed causal-tracing localization fails to predict weight-editing success; the paper says so and positions itself as the activation-level, cross-model, cross-task, preregistered version. The genuinely new parts are (a) a single failure family clean enough to run a four-cohort design, (b) the stamp/amplifier typing as a *predictor* of steerability, and (c) the Phi result as a concrete counterexample rather than a statistical trend. The self-repair/redundancy finding echoes McGrath et al. (2023) and Rushing & Nanda (2024); the contribution there is confirmation at 70B scale with frozen predictions.

**Rigor.** The preregistration is the strongest part: git-frozen documents with commit hashes and dates (e.g. Mistral-Small frozen 2026-09-14, held-out run 2026-09-19), numeric bars, denominators fixed in advance, one-touch held-out data, and misses left as misses. The Gemma case is exemplary — the frozen sites L55/L58 turned out not to be the best sites, the authors scored them anyway, took four near-misses, and reported the strong effects separately in the main text using held-out-identified sites. The reverse-patching arm independently re-derives each model's mechanism verdict, which is the kind of convergent check most circuit papers skip.

**Weaknesses and red flags.**

1. **The x-axis is saturated, so the dissociation is nearly guaranteed by construction.** Localization ranges 72–100% across cells while steerability ranges 4–93%. A near-constant predictor cannot correlate with anything; plotting the two and observing scatter is close to a tautology. The paper's real claim is stronger and narrower — *the field's standard localization metric carries no information about intervenability* — but Figure 5 dresses it as an empirical dissociation. A finer localization measure (DLA concentration, minimum hitting set, joint-ablation effect) would have been the informative axis, and the paper already has those numbers in Appendix G.
2. **For Phi, "equally well-localized" is never causally established.** Appendix G.7 states it plainly: on Phi det held-out the 40 random five-layer sets average −2.70 logits while the targeted set does not beat random, and the discovery split goes the other way (targeted +0.43 vs null −0.90). So Phi's circuit is localized only by logit-lens peak and DLA — exactly the correlational instruments the paper argues are insufficient. The framing "Phi implements an equally well-localized circuit at the same late depth, yet the identical intervention flips almost nothing" therefore rests on the weaker of the two localization notions. The authors disclose this, but the abstract does not carry the caveat.
3. **The "sign direction" is the unembedding difference, not an activation-estimated direction.** d = WU(" −") − WU(" +") is, up to the layer-norm rescale, the direction that raises the minus logit and lowers the plus logit. The intervention is therefore close to a minus-token suppressor by construction, which is precisely why it cannot separate correct-minus from wrong-minus (Llama breaks 95% of correct-minus answers) and why Mistral's high flip rate coincides with breaking 79/85 correct-plus IBP cases. "Steerability" as operationalized here means "does suppressing the minus token flip the written sign", not "can the error be repaired". The corrective/harmful split is real and reported, but only in a figure caption (Llama det 42 = 33 corrective + 9 harmful; Mistral det 52 + 10, IBP 67 + 2) — a repair-rate metric would be the honest headline and might reorder the models.
4. **The 93% headline rides on a 14-case cell.** Llama IBP held-out is 13/14. The paper marks n < 15 cells as "suggestive only" in Figure 2 and then quotes 93% in the abstract and conclusion. The next-best large-n cells are 70% (39/56) and 42% (17/41). This is not fatal — the direction of the effect is consistent — but the single most quotable number is the least stable one.
5. **Figure 5's y-axis rewards indiscriminate damage.** Mistral has the highest flip counts (62/185 det, 69/91 IBP) and the worst specificity in the paper (42/53 correct-plus det broken, 79/85 IBP). Because the axis counts only minus-written error flips, a model that damages everything scores as "most steerable". The paper's own column-by-column reading admits this ("grips everything, specific to nothing") but the scatter plot does not encode it.
6. **Gemma's main-text numbers and its scorecard use different sites.** The four near-misses are scored at L55/L58; the strong effects in §5/Figure 5 use L58/L60, identified on held-out data. Layer identity is a mild form of selection (no outcome information is used), but it means the headline cell and the preregistered cell are not the same experiment, and the penalty does not propagate to Figure 5.
7. **The oracle-offset problem is unaddressed.** Every measurement and intervention requires knowing the exact character position of the wrong sign, supplied by an LLM annotator plus deterministic verification. A deployable repair would need to detect the wrong sign first; no end-to-end detection-then-steer pipeline is evaluated, and detection precision/recall on unseen errors is not reported. The free-generation arm is acknowledged as unscorable at scale (solutions run ~4,000 characters and often end without a parseable answer at high dose), so all causal claims live at a single teacher-forced token.
8. **The mechanistic predictor has three clean cases out of five.** Qwen (stamp + rescue), Llama (pure amplifier) and Phi (amplifier + redundancy) fit the taxonomy cleanly; Gemma changes role between errors and corrects (L58 stamps on errors, amplifies on corrects) and Mistral is "indeterminate" on errors. A predictor validated on 3/5 models, with the two misfits explained post hoc, is a hypothesis, not yet a law.
9. **Scope.** Two template-like procedural tasks, one failure family, one token position, one direction, one dose family. "LLM mathematics" in the title is broader than cofactor expansion and poly-trig integration by parts. The IBP bank is custom and never validated as a benchmark (accuracies 16.6–33.6%), so some "sign errors" there may be downstream symptoms of method failure that survived the magnitude rule.

**Significance.** High for the mech-interp and steering communities, moderate for practitioners. The practical takeaway is a checklist item rather than a method: a sharply localized site is not yet a lever, and validating a circuit by editing at its site measures localization *and* the network's cooperation, not localization alone. That is a useful corrective to a literature that routinely equates the two.

## Strengths

- **Preregistration done properly.** Frozen numeric bars, denominators, exclusion rules, commit hashes and freeze dates, with 23/29 confirmed and every miss reported — including the awkward ones (Qwen's P3 layer shift, Gemma's four near-misses, Phi's 92.2% vs 95% IBP bar).
- **Cohort-level statistics instead of anecdote.** 3,253 experiment-ready cases across ten cells, four cohorts each, bootstrap CIs behind every verdict, and every effect read against matched random and control-layer nulls rather than against zero.
- **Interventions run on correct answers.** The four-cohort specificity matrix is the paper's best methodological idea: it converts a flip rate into a statement about *what the lever moves*, and it exposes exactly the models where the lever is indiscriminate.
- **A genuine, disclosed null.** Phi's 6/6 confirmations include three registered nulls, and the paper reports that Phi's targeted ablation does not beat random layer sets. Reporting a null that weakens your own localization claim is rare.
- **Mechanism, not just location.** Margins (17.8 logits, max 43.4), redundancy (15 credited MLP layers; habit layers hold 19%/26% of positive credit), proportional-cut dynamics (corr = −0.90/−0.98), and the rescue layer together explain *why* the same edit works in one model and not another — a level of explanation most dissociation papers stop short of.
- **The reverse arrow closes the loop.** "The signal can be written in, not copied out" is a clean, falsifiable triple (removing fixes, transplanting healthy does not, injecting error breaks) that independently re-derives each model's mechanism type.
- **Reproducibility infrastructure.** Public repo, per-case JSONs, source-file provenance for every count, and a ledger cross-check between appendix numbers and raw files.

## Limitations

- **Answer-token readout only.** All causal verdicts are scored as strict flips of the written sign's logit lead at the probe token under teacher forcing; the free-generation arm agrees on small n but could not be scored at scale. Whether steering changes what a model writes in an unprompted rollout is untested.
- **Flips are not always repairs.** At α=3, Llama det's 42 flips are 33 corrective and 9 harmful; Mistral det 52 + 10, IBP 67 + 2. The paper reports this but does not make it the primary metric.
- **A residual early-commit population.** 26 of 703 confirmed errors (3.7%) genuinely peak early, concentrated in Gemma's determinant task (an early band at layers 5–13 that copies the minus ink of the model's own recent text); Qwen's early points are shown to be layer-0 token-table artifacts rather than real early decisions.
- **One-sided intervention design.** Because no model carries a fixed plus stamp at these sites, the experiment is a minus-removal design only; plus-removal steering is never tested, so the symmetry of the dissociation is unknown.
- **Origin hypothesis is under-tested.** The claim that written mathematics teaches models to expect minus ("−67" is marked, "67" is bare) rests on one model, one small completion set (base vs instruct, Appendix F), and three indirect fingerprints (the 2:1 minus skew, Qwen's layer-0 lean, the pre-chat-training stamp). It is a plausible story, not a measurement.
- **Resource-bounded scope.** Five models, two tasks, one failure family, no 675B causal battery, and IBP banks of 1,294 problems per model. The dissociation is demonstrated, not exhaustively mapped — the authors say so.
- **No practical repair evaluated.** The proposed follow-up (LoRA/ReFT restricted to habit layers, screened by the frozen battery) is future work; nothing here shows the identified layers are a better intervention site than a generic one for actually reducing error rates.

## Relevance

直接命中 **LLM Hidden States & Interpretability**：它把"电路定位 → 干预验证"这条机械可解释性的标准工作流拆成两个独立可测的量，并给出一个反例（Phi-4 定位最好却几乎不可操纵），对任何打算用 activation steering / 电路定位做可靠性修复的工作都是一个必须先回答的校准问题。次要价值在于它提供了一个可复用的评测范式——预注册 + 四 cohort 特异性矩阵 + magnitude-matched 随机方向对照——这套设计可以直接搬到其他"单 token 决定性错误"的可靠性研究上。

## Questions for Further Exploration

1. If the y-axis were a repair rate (corrective flips minus harmful flips, normalized by cohort size) instead of a raw minus-written flip rate, does the ranking change — and does Mistral stop looking like the most steerable model?
2. Does the dissociation survive on naturalistic chain-of-thought mathematics (competition problems, multi-line derivations) where the decisive sign is not a single tokenizable character and the probe position is ambiguous?
3. Can an online probe detect the error site without the oracle offset — e.g. a linear probe on the sign lead, or a judge-model trigger — and what precision/recall does that add to the end-to-end repair loop?
4. Is the stamp/amplifier typing stable across prompts, temperatures and non-teacher-forced trajectories, or is it an artifact of replaying the model's own text (which supplies the "minus ink" Gemma's early band appears to copy)?
5. Would a parameter-efficient update (LoRA/ReFT) confined to the habit layers succeed in Phi where activation steering fails, given 17.8-logit margins and 15 redundant writers — or does redundancy defeat weight edits too?
6. Does the frequency of explicit minus tokens at answer positions in pretraining-scale mathematical text predict stamp strength across models, as the origin hypothesis requires?
7. For Phi specifically: is there any intervention family (attention-output zeroing plus MLP subtraction at α=1 moves the lead by −6.70/−7.11 but flips zero cases) that crosses the 17.8-logit margin without destroying the computation — or is "unsteerable at calibrated dose" a hard property of over-determined circuits?
