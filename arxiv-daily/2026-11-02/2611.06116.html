Critical edgeread with care

The 11.5-point headline comes from one model and one contrast, on a benchmark where 7 of 11 families sit at ceiling outside H2 — and the interaction test is only p=0.07.

H4 · reviewer

Same model vets actions and messages.

  • Bank sim: 13 state-changing tools plus 6 lookups. Every forbidden action succeeds, so a silent guardrail cannot rescue a non-compliant agent.
  • Policy: a 1,746-word, 40-section manual paraphrasing EU credit, payments, MiFID II, GDPR and AML rules across 11 rule families.
  • Cases: 39 variants × 2 paraphrases × 2 pressure levels = 156 per cell, hashed before the run; every case has a scripted second customer turn.
  • Scoring: executed trace only. Violations and over-refusals counted separately; 22 unit tests; Cochran’s Q, exact McNemar with Holm, Wilson intervals.

03

Strengths & limitations

side by side

＋ Strengths

  • Clean ablation: same prompts, tools, manual and models; only information supply and control flow move.
  • Two-sided scoring turns the reviewer from “safety at a usability cost” into “safety for free, here”.
  • Failed predictions reported: order rules, pressure and reviewer over-refusal all came back null or reversed.
  • Reproducibility: all 3,120 traces, judge labels, human coding sheet and hashed pre-registration are public.

− Limitations

  • One synthetic bank, one 1,746-word manual, two synthetic customers; several rules are stricter than the law they paraphrase.
  • Four open-weight models, reasoning disabled: “no harness fixes date arithmetic” is a claim about non-reasoning models.
  • 7 of 11 families at ceiling; the effective sample is closer to 39 than 156, so standard errors are optimistic.
  • Scripted second turn presupposes a refusal and probably inflates the 26.1% underclaim rate; no different-model reviewer or false-block audit.

Open questions
