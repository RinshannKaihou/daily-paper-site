04

Method

isolating one shared direction
  1. Read at the generation boundary. Residual stream at the last two prefill tokens, one late layer picked by a fixed rule.
  2. Cross, don't contrast. 96 prompts × 24 matched harm/neutral pairs (bomb/garden). Differencing cancels the prompt and the act of appending; a two-way vector ANOVA leaves a shared µ — 0.30 of the squared norm against 0.15 for all word effects combined.
  3. Cross-fit, then steer. Independent four-fold splits on prompts and words; write a·µ at the boundary at a pre-registered dose of 0.5.
  4. Scan the vocabulary. Project µ̂ over 71,583 words appended to 48 over-refused prompts; rank each word by its shift in standard-deviation units.
  5. Validate on behaviour. Held-out words for dose-response, then table-guided synonym swaps on natural prompts.

07

Strengths

  • Crossed, not contrastive. 96 prompts × 24 matched pairs with an exact two-way vector ANOVA; the prompt axis genuinely cancels.
  • Cross-fitted on both axes. Held-out mean directions keep cosine 0.90–0.97 with the train-fitted one.
  • Two null controls. A KL-matched random direction needs 1.7–4.7× the dose and still loses in every model.
  • Pre-registered, and honest. Frozen dose and endpoints, a detection gate, and negative results in the main text: six hand-picked “courage” classes all fail.
08

Limitations

  • Closed models are out of reach. The table needs activations, so the diagnostic is unavailable for exactly the models whose safety behaviour matters most.
  • Meaning preservation is judged by LLMs. The strict judge accepts 0.61 / 0.58 / 0.33 of the edits, with κ = 0.58 between judges and no human annotation.
  • Words explain less than prompts. The word term is the smallest in the ANOVA (0.15 vs 0.32 prompt, 0.23 interaction); the large deltas are paired effects.
  • Narrow scope. English only, one scan position (end of prompt), and mechanism traced in a single, architecturally unusual model.
09

Open questions

  1. Checkpoint sweep. Does the ranking appear at SFT, at preference tuning, or earlier — and does it reshuffle?
  2. Register, not harm. Why do poetic / whimsical / sardonic lower refusal while containment words raise it?
  3. Meaning drift. A human-annotated sample of 200 substitutions would settle whether the effect is 28 points or 8.
  4. Benchmark re-ranking. Would two XSTests matched on table shift reorder the same eight models?
