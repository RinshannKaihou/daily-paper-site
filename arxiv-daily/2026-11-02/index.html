The metric moves; the capability does not. Across this week’s 102 papers the sharpest failures land on one line of code, one layer, or one gate input: a sub-10⁻⁶ embedding row that silently zeroes a whole gradient batch, an atomic Split-K that changes 17.3% of greedy GSM8K sequences, a release gate that waves through 41% of wrong repairs. Artifact-to-Capability: Diagnosing Breaks in Capability Formation of Self-Evolving Agents in a Controlled Environment

Ziqian Ban, Haoxiang Yang, Yanfei Zhang et al. · Tianjin University; IDEA

五子棋受控环境把 Reflexion、Voyager、DGM 拆到状态、对局、谱系三层：三者都通过“累积 / 行为变化 / 指标上升”，却分别断在合法化增益、组合覆写、过检查点后只丢不增。

★ Must Read/ Inference Reliability & SDC

Pin six values, keep the fast kernels

Detent: Batch-Invariant LLM Serving and RL Rollouts at Near-Stock Speed

Peixu Xin, Runchuan Feng, Jiazhen Guo et al.

批量不变性的缺口在“内核选择”而非内核内部：SelIR + 最大流最小割求出必须钉死的 6 个值，26 格电池全过，p50 延迟仅 1.08–1.11× 原生，而确定性模式是 2.4–5.7×。

Batch invariance is a kernel-selection problem: an executable SelIR plus a minimum vertex cut pins 3 attention and 3 all-reduce values, so the fast kernels survive. 26/26 battery cells pass where Stock fails 5–19; 512/512 seeded rollouts replay bit-for-bit with trainer KL exactly 0.

CaveatThe GRPO stability result is a single-seed sanity test with a lowered learning rate, accuracy is only measured on saturated GSM8K, and there is no released artifact.

★ Must Read/ LLM Training Stability

A silent kill switch in 52.6% of untied checkpoints

Near-Zero Token Embeddings Silently Stall Training in Gated Sequence Models

Xin Liu, Boyuan Sun, Yuxin Wang et al.

291 个仓库普查：20/38 个非绑定门控/混合检查点存在范数 <10⁻⁶ 的可达 embedding 行，梯度放大 30+ 个数量级越过 1.84×10¹⁹ 后 FP32 裁剪把整批梯度静默置零；三行 GroupClip 修复。

CaveatThe 52.6% tying split is confounded by model family and training recipe, and there is no Transformer CPT control for the architecture-conditional claim.

★ Must Read/ Silent Failures

The gate says yes to 41% of wrong repairs

Accepted but Wrong: Silent Failures of LLM Code Repairs after API Migrations

Xiaobo Chen, Genliang Zhu, Ziyuan Wang, Chu Wang

73 个预注册任务、60 处真实运行时变更、全量枚举修复空间：典型输入门放行 41.0% 的隐藏错误修复，加一条 release-note 边界输入降到 6.5%，假拒绝持平 1.0%。

CaveatThe 0.410 headline is menu-dependent (0.406 to 0.271 when the menu grows), and 16.8% of the hard distractors still pass the edge-input gate.

★ Must Read/ Precision & Compression

Sixty years of ocean, one biased increment

FESOM2.8 in single precision: climate fidelity over six decades and performance on CPUs and GPUs

Suvarchal Kumar Cheedela, Nikolay V. Koldunov, Sergey Danilov et al.

60 年 CORE-II 回算：朴素 float32 以 1.2×10⁻⁴ psu/yr 丢盐、累积 −129 ZJ 热亏；把状态存成 64 位反而把漂移符号翻正，证明偏差在增量；厚度加权 Kahan 把 SST RMS 从 0.1649 K 降到 0.0046 K，代价 ≤8%。

Naive float32 loses salt at 1.2×10⁻⁴ psu/yr and accumulates a −129 ZJ heat deficit. Keeping the state in 64 bits reverses the drift sign (+1.85 mpsu), so the bias is in the increment; a thickness-weighted carried remainder restores SST RMS to 0.0046 K against a 0.0040 K double-precision control.

CaveatFidelity is established only at nominal 1 degree, the residual salinity gain is mechanistically unexplained, and the coupled-model test is underpowered by its own 54 ZJ twin spread.

★ Must Read/ Interpretability

Ninety readers, twenty-four brakes, one gate

A Circuit for In-Context Learning

Andrew Gao, William Gao

三个 3–4B 模型上完整因果验证的 ICL 回路：标签 token 作键值槽、约 90 个 reader、标量门控、24 个 brake 头；敲掉 20 个 reader 掉 −0.74 nats（随机 +0.02），敲掉 24 个 brake 涨 +1.39 nats（随机 +0.22），代码开源。

Label tokens act as key–value slots, ~90 upper-layer readers copy the value out, a scalar gate pooled from 16 duplicate-token heads decides whether to read, and ~24 brake heads cancel recency. Knocking out 20 readers costs −0.74 nats against +0.02 for random heads; removing 24 brakes adds +1.39 against +0.22.

CaveatThe completeness percentage is reference-dependent and moved 0.77 nats after a control fix, and circuit-only beating intact is a metric artifact.

★ Must Read/ Compute Infrastructure

Self-scheduling is worth 2–9%. Fusion carries the rest.

KOSMOS: Self-scheduling Persistent Kernels for Communication-Compute Overlap

Alexander Magro, Aristotle Martin, Ye Wang, Wen Chen

480 个微基准格全胜，但一次只改一处的消融把收益拆开：自调度本身只值 2–9%，大部分来自融合与队列顺序（朴素 AG 顺序要付 1.17–1.40×）；8×MI350X 上 1.10–1.28× 端到端，MoE 代理 1.29–1.83×。

Persistent kernels switch between mover and builder roles from in-kernel queues, fusing every TP+SP collective with its GEMM: 1.10–1.28× end-to-end on 8×MI350X/MI355X and 1.29–1.83× over DeepEP on MoE proxies. The ablation is unusually honest: self-scheduling itself is only 2–9%, and a naive AG order costs 1.17–1.40×.

CaveatSingle-node 8-GPU evaluation, proxy-scale 405B (24 of 126 layers), no convergence curves, and no released code.

BitDecoding-FP4: native FP4 KV decoding

DaYou Du, Woon Yee Ng, Guangming Sheng et al.

Dual-residual query quantization cuts the query’s share of logit error from 60% to 1.2%, putting native FP4 KV within 0.42 points of BF16 and 1.81× faster at 256K on B300.

Method

SHq: learnable grids for weight quantization

Michael Helcig, Earl Killian, Dan Alistarh

A two-parameter closed-form grid wins 45 of 50 settings and all 20 at 3–4 bits, cutting NVFP4’s KL 40.7–67.4% at 4.5 bpw, but downstream accuracy gains only 0.1–0.7 points.

Method

AutoEMatic: runtime-adaptive mixed precision

Akshat Ramachandran, Reena Elangovan, Rangharajan Venkatesan et al.

Per-layer precision chosen at runtime from output entropy and residual-update norm lifts Qwen3-8B τ²-bench from 17.5% to 27.2% at 4.9 bits/weight, still 3.5 points behind FP6g.

Method

MACaQ: fast calibrated W4A4 quantization

Lorenz K. Muller, Philippe Bich, Chiara Boretti et al.

Layer-local Hadamard plus Hessian-aware coordinate descent reaches 6.90 WikiText-2 perplexity vs 7.13 for MR-GPTQ on Llama-3-8B, in roughly one seventh of the quantization time.

Method

ResQAdam: residual-preserving QAT optimizer

Liqun Ma, Shengkun Tang, Liangyu Wang et al.

Low-rank optimizers break QAT (30.61 PPL for GaLore vs 20.05); keeping one BF16 momentum plus a sign update on the residual cuts optimizer memory 71–74% within about 1 PPL of AdamW.

Analysis

Twice as fast, until it isn’t

Smit Kadvani, Kriti Faujdar, Spurthi Tallam

Only two checkpoints publish both timings and scores; W4 decode is 2.12× at batch 1 but 1.02× at batch 256, and every HumanEval delta in the audit sits inside sampling noise.

Section 07

INTERPRETABILITY

LLM 隐状态与可解释性
25 papers★ 1 must-read

The largest section, and the most deflationary: decodability is not ownership, localization is not steerability, and a probe that reads the instruction is not reading the decision.

Analysis★ Must Read

A circuit for in-context learning

Andrew Gao, William Gao

A complete causally verified ICL chain across three 3–4B models: label tokens as key–value slots, ~90 readers, a scalar gate from 16 duplicate-token heads, and ~24 brake heads, with code released.

Analysis

Localization does not imply steerability

Shradha Agarwal, Tariq J., Deepak Rajbhar

Sign errors commit in the last quarter of depth 72–100% of the time, yet subtracting the sign direction flips 13/14 on Llama-70B and only 4.1% on the equally well-localized Phi-4.

Analysis

Linear probes to Bayesian manifolds

Anshul Kumar, Neha Prasad, Manoj Kumar et al.

Four separately testable claims: 89% of 1,620 cells decode, all 338 shape tests pick ribbon or torus, but only 19% of shapes are owned and 3.5% of cells clear all four bars.

Analysis

Reducing logprob without causal verification

Chaerin Lee, Wonho Lee, Sohee Park, Daeseon Choi

Of 36 PII-suppression cells flagged by logprob, one survives a rank-aware metric plus a matched random-direction control; in the worst case a random direction beats the chosen feature in 50/50 pairs.

Analysis

Written once, read differently

Marcus Armstrong

Across 22 models and 3,600 question pairs, commitment-direction cosine predicts coupling at ρ=0.43–0.85, and ablating one shared direction removes at most 17% of the effect.

Analysis

The intrusion pathway

Dikshant Kukreja, Gautam Gupta, Avni Mittal et al.

Irrelevant context supplies 52–94% of the distractor logit but almost none of the gold drop; masking ~10 intrusion heads recovers 56.9% of the loss without a clean forward pass.

Analysis

A capitulation direction emerges with scale

Saad Aamir, Muhammad Awais Bin Adil

The direction passes a pre-registered gate only at 7B and above; ablating Qwen2.5-7B’s cuts answer flips from 17.8% to 13.0%, while gate-failing directions move nothing.

Analysis

What do collusion probes read?

Mainul Hasan Mohsin, Mohammad Shahidur Rahman

The published NARCBench probe separates colluders from agents that refused the same brief at AUROC 0.46; the strongest probe falls from 1.00 to 0.61 once the brief is deleted.

Analysis

Which words a model fears

Boyuan Zhang, Mohammad Zandsalimy, Shanu Sushmita

A crossed-difference refusal direction scanned over 71,583 words predicts behaviour at ρ=0.54–0.96, but table-guided synonym choice adds only +0.033 over a random harm-free synonym.

Analysis

Decodability is not validity

Ayal Klein

Probes matched within ±0.01 AUROC differ 1.5–4.7× in nuisance sensitivity; selecting by a conflict criterion cuts test CFN from .69 to .54 at ≤.012 AUROC cost.

Method

Shaping and monitoring models through representations

Thomas Fel, Matthew Kowal, Matt Feiszli et al.

Penalizing concept reliance lifts Waterbirds worst-group accuracy 51.6%→93–97.7%; a five-level monitor hierarchy shows probes usually re-encode rather than lose the concept.

Analysis

All quiet on the neural front

Emilio Ferrara

Across 88,200 reports, injected pain states never exceed what the altered text reveals, yet probes separate pain from sadness at 98–100% and steering moves file deletion 3.5%→48%.

Method

Calibrated concept profiles

Piotr Wilam

Giving neuron readings units and a twin-redraw noise floor: zeroing the 5% carrier set costs +0.40 nats over random at 2.03T tokens, against +0.06 at 4.19B.

Analysis

Whose effect is it?

Toygar Tanyel, Serkan Ayvaz

Orthogonal parts sum to the whole to first order, so beating whole-state patching means the rest pulls back; changing only DAS’s objective drops median readout effect from 1.64–3.00 T to within 3.0%.

Analysis

Represented but not used

Yuzhang Luo, Mohamed Aghzal, Liangming Pan, Ziyu Yao

Linear probes read action value at 98.2% cell accuracy, yet the policy picks the disfavored action in 34.2–58.3% of Grid states; value→activation transport raises success 0.140→0.300.

Method

When are two SAE latents the same feature?

Alexandra Suvorikova, Ilgam Latypov, Anton Trukhan, Yuriy Dorn

An energy-weighted optimal-transport plan with document bootstrap separates dictionary-level differences from noise, but the TinyStories effect shrinks to −2.3%..+2.1% on larger models.

Analysis

One mistake, three layers

Maryam Rahimimovassagh, Michael Brown, Sanjeda Sara Jennifer et al.

The three-consecutive-layer onset rule reports onset 19 stages early and fires on 12–17% of real nulls; permutation nulls held fixed across depth bring that to 4–7%.

Analysis

Probing transformer representations layer by layer

Ambroise Odonnat, Théo Gnassounou, Laetitia Chapel et al.

Across ~144k probes, layer and module must be chosen jointly: LN2@60 recovers 86.0% of the LLM oracle gain (+2.44 points, 21/24 tasks), while vision gains are uneven and ViT-B loses 6.6.

Analysis

Representational drift in a code model

Piotr Wilam

Behaviour settles in the first 4% of training, but 0 of 169 Python constructs ever settle their carrier neurons; the set measured at 84B tokens is indistinguishable from random at the end.

Analysis

Language defaults before content

Shenbin Qian, Yves Scherrer

Patching a single residual vector gives a three-stage order — English default at L15–16, content at L17, target language at L24 — replicated in three models, with SAEs failing to reproduce it.

Analysis

The geometry of ignorance

Adar Peleg, Dvir Alsheich, Tomer Ashuach, Avi Mendelson

Subtracting an anonymized paraphrase collapses known concepts into tight clusters: |d|=21.51 vs 0.92 for raw activations, though robustness correlations flip sign across unlearning attacks.

Method

Interpreting any residual stream direction

Celeste De Schamphelaere, Juan J. Vazquez, Ari Spiesberger et al.

A direction-conditioned generator raises centred cosine from 0.572 for 10M-token corpus search to 0.759 best-of-1 and 0.822 best-of-8, at 3.35 H200-seconds per target.

Analysis

Geometric bottlenecks of cross-lingual editing

Aastha A K Verma, Anwoy Chatterjee, Sparsh Jain, Tanmoy Chakraborty

About 0.90 of the cross-lingual edit error is orthogonal residual mismatch, not key propagation; realigning keys lifts α 0.212→0.858 but costs German efficacy 72.62→61.17.

Analysis

Heads that write, not just point

Junsung Park, Sangwon Yu, Donghun Kang et al.

Pointer and writer top-100 head sets overlap only 35–47 (chance 8.7); ablating the intersection costs 18.0 points on LLaVA-OneVision while writer-only costs 0.5.

Analysis

SAE interprets MoE

Anyuan Zhuo, Shaohua Zhang, Yuan Lin, Pinyan Lu

An SAE on the MoE layer output decomposes every latent exactly over experts: layer 30’s top contribution share is 0.512 vs 0.231 for gating, and expert E50 is 86.7% Java latents.

Section 08

COMPUTE INFRASTRUCTURE

大规模 ML 计算基础设施
10 papers★ 1 must-read

Overlap, route, place, warm. The systems here are judged by end-to-end time, and several of them report the part of the gain that does not belong to the mechanism in the title.

System★ Must Read

KOSMOS: self-scheduling persistent kernels

Alexander Magro, Aristotle Martin, Ye Wang, Wen Chen

Fuses every TP+SP collective with its GEMM in one persistent kernel: 1.10–1.28× end-to-end on 8×MI350X/MI355X and 1.29–1.83× over DeepEP on MoE proxies, with 480/480 microbenchmark cells faster.

System

GC-MoE: overlapping dispatch with grouped GEMM

Yong Li, Kan Liu, Tao Lan

Green Contexts split SMs into 20 communication and 128 compute partitions, launching dispatch and grouped GEMM as independent graph roots: 1.188× on 8×H20 and 1.106× on 8×B300.

Method

AttentionIndex: a causal inverted-file index

Jeongmin Brian Park, Wen-mei Hwu

Indexer scoring takes 53–58% of 1M-token prefill; a causal IVF index gives 1.3–1.8× prefill throughput and recovers 86.6% vs 69.6% of exhaustive top-k at HiSA speed.

System

A per-step load-balancing engine for EP MoE

Jingyuan Li, Maolin Wang, Hayden So

Per-step pairwise halving reaches 99.5–99.8% of the LP optimum; effective utilization at G=16 rises from 72.5% under static EPLB to 86.9% on a CUDA kernel and 99.1% on-die.

System

Look before you launch

Hossein Askari, Wei Sun, Chenguang Zhu et al.

A submission-time throughput predictor reaches median w20 77.0% over 30 nightly retrains and 85.1% vs 53.2% for the lookup table it replaced, saving about 3% of training cost.

System

Firepool: brokering warm GPU capacity

Ankit Goyal, Santosh Jha, Zhuo Zhi et al.

Late binding lets one warm pool serve many environments: agent and RL loops finish 13.0× and 10.8× faster than pod-per-job, and p90 start latency falls from 59.1 s to 3.5 s.

System

INSIGHT-HPC: interpretable system intelligence

Rupak Kadel, Jie Li, Yong Chen

Fusing statistical, job-aware and physics evidence gives F1 0.862 (P 0.913 / R 0.817) on 169 events vs 0.660 for Prodigy — but Phase IV is supervised and 83% of tests are injections.

System

Adaptive scheduling for multi-instance async RL

Salah Chikhi, Luis Zuin, Entong Li, Zeng Li

An offline scheduler cuts mean iteration time 927→215.2 s vs MindSpeed-RL, but only 238→212 s (10.9%) belongs to scheduling; the rest is disaggregating rollout from training.

System

NCCL-BOT: closing the loop on the cost model

Hanan Shteingart, Amedeo Sapio, Chen Avin et al.

A cost-model-warm-started bandit cuts H100 contexts losing more than 10% from 19 to 1 (Holm p=0.047), yet the authors report no net end-to-end win and a loss on GB200 MNNVL.

System

AdaFloor: long-tail MoE rollout planning

Jia Wei, Ziyi Qiu, Yi Xu, Xuehai Qian

Dummy rank-time is 32.83% of a 16-device Qwen3-30B-A3B rollout; bounded leases remove 96.15% of dummy rank-seconds and cut rollout time 24.09%, mostly from batching and guards.
