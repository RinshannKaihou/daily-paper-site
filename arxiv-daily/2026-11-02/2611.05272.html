02

Method

方法 2 forward passes

A population-geometry test, not a similarity score.

  1. 01
    Pair the query with an anonymised twin.

    Mean-pool hidden states at layer ℓ. Rewrite every entity by deterministic substitution — Harry Potter → Alex Ryden, Hogwarts → Crystal Academy.

  2. 02
    Subtract the shared surface form.
    Δvi(ℓ) = hℓ(qi) − (1/K) Σk hℓ(ai(k))

    Syntax and phrasing cancel. What remains is a concept-specific residual, one vector per query.

  3. 03
    Read the geometry of the whole set.

    Across B = 10 batch-mean directions, measure Rényi-2 Gram entropy and dispersion (AUSSL2, AUSScos², AUSSJac). Tight, low-entropy vectors mean known; scattered vectors mean unknown.

The free engineering rule: peak depth sits at 0.80–0.88 of model depth. Fix 0.8× with no per-model search — 8/8 metrics stay significant, and leave-one-model-out transfer hits 72/72.

03

Strengths

优势
  • Theory that earns its place. Lemma 1 is an exact identity, not decoration: S₂ = −log₂[(1+(n−1)cos²)/n]. The authors also concede that low entropy alone cannot rule out anti-parallel collapse.
  • Clean, replicated detection. 10 models across 3 families, 9 metrics, 10/10 direction consistency, BH correction and bootstrap CIs.
  • The ablation is the experiment. Reg S₂ at |d| = 0.92 vs. AUSScos² at 21.51 isolates what anonymisation buys, instead of claiming the whole pipeline as new.
  • A free engineering rule. 0.8× relative depth, validated by 72/72 leave-one-model-out transfer, makes the probe usable without per-model tuning.
  • Reporting discipline. The full 9 × 4 grid is printed, including non-significant cells; MIA is declared unpredictable.
04

Limitations

局限
  • The sign flip is unresolved. −0.51 for ICL vs. +0.48 for steering; the mechanism is inferred from correlations, never checked against generations.
  • Too weak for screening. ρ ≈ 0.5 with no threshold, precision/recall or calibration curve — the “skip the attacks” use case is asserted, not shown.
  • The pool is not independent. 54 runs share 3 models and 3 concepts; effective n is closer to 9 than 45, and no mixed-effects model is fitted.
  • “Known” is confounded. HP QA accuracy is only 4–32%, and no frequency-matched obscure-entity control separates knowledge from entity familiarity.
  • Anonymisation is brittle. 17/100 TOFU and 11/96 HP pairs are unchanged by substitution, pushing null mass into the negative class.
