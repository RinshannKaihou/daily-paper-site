# 周报总览 / Weekly Overview（2026-11-02 .. 2026-11-08）

本周共评估 **3,245** 篇新论文（周一至周日），625 篇进入摘要门控、344 篇通过，最终按领域配额经逐对比较（Bradley-Terry）选出 **102** 篇，分属 9 个领域；26 篇星标候选经全文精读后评出 **8** 篇本周必读。本周主线是**“指标在动，能力不一定在动”——而且断点几乎总能落到一行代码、一层网络或一个门控输入上**。最刺眼的证据来自数值层：291 个开源仓库里，38 个非绑定门控/混合检查点中有 20 个（52.6%）藏着范数 <10⁻⁶ 的可达 embedding 行，梯度放大 30 多个数量级后让 FP32 全局裁剪把整批梯度静默置零，Nemotron-3-Nano-4B 的泰卢固语继续预训练丢掉约 20% 的步数而 loss 曲线毫无异常；vLLM 的原子 Split-K 在 97.61% 的同输入重放里给出不同 FP32 结果，足以把 GSM8K 贪心解码序列改变 17.3%；FESOM2 的朴素 float32 六十年回算以 1.2×10⁻⁴ psu/yr 持续丢盐、累积 −129 ZJ 热亏，而“把状态存成 64 位”这个直觉修复反而把漂移符号翻了过来。代理指标同样在骗人：Reflexion、Voyager、DGM 三个自进化系统都通过了“累积 / 行为变化 / 指标上升”三信号，却分别断在合法化增益、组合覆写、过检查点后只丢不增；73 个预注册的 API 迁移任务（60 个文档变更簇）里，典型输入门放行了 41.0% 的隐藏错误修复，而一条来自 release note 的边界输入就把它压到 6.5%；符号错误能被定位到最后四分之一深度的 MLP（70–100%），但同样定位良好的 Phi-4 只有 4.1% 能被翻转。修复侧也有干净的答案：三行 GroupClip、确定性 Split-K（+1.02–2.62% 延迟）、把批量不变性从“内核”搬到“内核选择”（1.08–1.11× 原生速度）、以及把每一步舍入余项带到下一步的厚度加权 Kahan 更新（≤8% 代价换 1.35–1.66× 加速）。

This week we evaluated **3,245** fresh papers (Mon–Sun); 625 reached the abstract gate, 344 passed, and **102** were selected via within-area pairwise ranking (Bradley-Terry) under per-area quotas across 9 areas; 26 star candidates were fully read and **8** earned Must-Read status. The through-line: **the metric moves, the capability does not — and the break almost always lands on one line of code, one layer, or one gate input.** The sharpest evidence is numerical. Across 291 open-weight repos, 20 of 38 untied gated/hybrid checkpoints (52.6%) carry reachable sub-10⁻⁶ embedding rows whose gradients amplify 30+ orders of magnitude, so FP32 global clipping silently zeroes whole batches — Nemotron-3-Nano-4B's Telugu continual pretraining discards ~20% of its steps with an unremarkable loss curve. vLLM's atomic Split-K returns different FP32 outputs in 97.61% of identical-input replays, enough to change 17.3% of greedy GSM8K sequences. FESOM2's naive float32 loses salt at 1.2×10⁻⁴ psu/yr over a 60-year hindcast and accumulates a −129 ZJ heat deficit, while the intuitive fix — keep the state in 64 bits — *reverses the sign* of the drift. Proxy metrics deceive just as reliably: Reflexion, Voyager and DGM all pass the accumulation / behaviour-change / metric-gain triple yet break at legalization gain, compositional override, and loss-only drift after the checkpoint; across 73 pre-registered API-migration tasks a typical-input gate releases 41.0% of hidden-wrong repairs and one release-note edge input cuts that to 6.5%; wrong-sign tokens localize to last-quarter MLPs (70–100%) yet the equally well-localized Phi-4 flips only 4.1% of the time. The fixers were equally concrete: a three-line GroupClip, a deterministic Split-K (+1.02–2.62% latency), moving batch invariance from kernels to *kernel selection* (1.08–1.11× stock speed), and a thickness-weighted Kahan update that carries each step's rounding remainder forward (≤8% cost for 1.35–1.66× speedup).

### 1. [[2611.04952]] 从工件到能力：受控环境中诊断自进化智能体的能力形成断点（Artifact-to-Capability）
**领域 / Area:** Self-Evolving Agents & Automated Research · **类型 / Type:** 分析 / analysis

- **理由 / Why:** 本周最锋利的“代理指标不等于能力”证据：在五子棋受控环境里对 Reflexion、Voyager、DGM 做状态层（1,876 个可精确重放状态）、对局层（20 局完整对局）、谱系层（逐版本差分）三层解剖，配精确 McNemar 与因果干预；三个系统都通过“累积 / 行为变化 / 指标上升”，却分别断在合法化增益（P(pass) 上升全来自 P(legal)，P(pass\|legal) 反降 3.7 点）、组合覆写（0/20 局成五，去掉 `create_open_three` 后 20/20 胜）、过检查点后只丢不增（OB-7 与 OB-6 在 7,234 个状态里只差 1 个）。P(pass)=P(legal)×P(pass\|legal) 这一审计分解可直接复用。| The week's sharpest "proxy ≠ capability" evidence: a three-scale dissection of Reflexion, Voyager and DGM in a controlled Gomoku environment — state level (1,876 exactly replayable states), game level (20 complete games) and lineage level (per-version diffs) — with exact McNemar tests and causal interventions. ### 2. [[2611.04449]] 近零 token embedding 让门控序列模型静默停训（Near-Zero Token Embeddings Silently Stall Training）
**领域 / Area:** LLM Training Stability & Observability · **类型 / Type:** 分析 / analysis

- **理由 / Why:** 教科书级的“因果链完整”论文：291 个仓库的字节级普查（含往返分词可达性过滤）+ 闭式成因（AdamW 权重衰减按 (1−ηλ) 逐步收缩从未出现的输入行，OLMoE 上实测累积对数衰减 −26.3 对理论 26–27）+ 深度标度定律 + 门控修补 2×2 把“恢复前向读出”与“切断反向路径”分离（34 个数量级的梯度下降，零条反向路径被切）+ ULP 级验证（0/3,562,515,040 个权重与喂入 g=0 相差 1 ULP）。修复是三行 GroupClip，代价未测出，并已报告三个框架的确切溢出常数 1.84×10¹⁹。| A textbook-complete causal chain: a byte-level census of 291 repos with a round-trip reachability filter, a closed-form origin (AdamW weight decay shrinks never-seen input rows by (1−ηλ); OLMoE's measured cumulative log-decay of −26.3 matches the theoretical 26–27), a depth-scaling law, a gate-patching 2×2 that separates restoring the forward readout from severing backward paths (a 34-order gradient drop with zero paths cut), and ULP-level verification (0 of 3,562,515,040 weights differ from feeding g=0). The fix is a three-line GroupClip at no measured cost, and the exact overflow constant 1.84×10¹⁹ is named for three frameworks.
- **保留意见 / Caveat:** 52.6% 的绑定/非绑定对比被模型族与训练配方混淆，且架构条件性主张缺少 Transformer 继续预训练对照。| The 52.6% tying split is confounded by model family and training recipe, and there is no Transformer CPT control for the architecture-conditional claim.

### 3. [[2611.11450]] 同策略蒸馏能容忍多少训练-推理失配（How Much Training-Inference Mismatch Can OPD Tolerate?）
**领域 / Area:** RL Post-Training Dynamics & Failures · **类型 / Type:** 分析 / analysis

- **理由 / Why:** 把量化（bit）、异步（陈旧版本）、权重噪声三种不可比的扰动统一折算成 rollout 引擎与训练器之间的每 token KL（nats/token），用预注册的 17 配置扫描在三对师生上括出 OPD 的失配预算 [0.184, 0.39]——是标准 BF16 引擎 0.0008 的 230–488 倍，而同引擎下 REINFORCE 与 GRPO 分别在约 12× 与 60× 处崩溃；再给一个 24 分钟单卡、8/8 判定正确的准入检查，并把 FP8-KV 失配的 93–99% 定位到第一层（该层留 BF16 即得 1.93× KV 容量）。| Puts three incomparable perturbations — bit width, stale versions, weight noise — on one scale (per-token KL between rollout engine and trainer, nats/token) and uses a pre-registered 17-config sweep over three student–teacher pairs to bracket OPD's mismatch budget at [0.184, 0.39]: 230–488× the standard BF16 engine's 0.0008, while REINFORCE and GRPO break at roughly 12× and 60× under the same engines. It adds a 24-minute single-GPU admission check that called 8/8 verdicts correctly and localizes 93–99% of FP8-KV damage to layer one (keep it in BF16 for 1.93× KV capacity).
- **保留意见 / Caveat:** 主实验是一对 7B 学生跑 200 步，预算区间内没有一次运行，且 RL 对比用的是从未学会的基线。| One main 7B pair for 200 steps, no run inside the budget bracket, and the RL comparison uses baselines that never learned.

### 4. [[2611.10611]] Detent：近原生速度的批量不变服务与 RL rollout（Detent: Batch-Invariant Serving at Near-Stock Speed）
**领域 / Area:** Inference Reliability & SDC Detection · **类型 / Type:** 系统 / system

- **理由 / Why:** 把批量不变性重新表述为“内核选择不变性”，这是本周最有用的一次问题重定位：把引擎的选择逻辑写成可执行 DSL（SelIR），用最大流最小割求出“必须钉死的最少几个值”（FA3 上 3 个注意力 pin + 3 个 all-reduce pin），于是快内核得以保留。26 格电池在两个引擎、两代 GPU、TP 1–8 上全过（Stock 失败 5–19 格），p50 每 token 延迟只有 Stock 的 1.08–1.11×（确定性模式是 2.4–5.7×），512/512 条 seeded rollout 逐 bit 复现、trainer KL 恰好 0，BF16 GRPO 在 Stock 崩到 reward 0.436 时保持 0.843。| Reframes batch invariance as kernel-*selection* invariance — the week's most useful relocation of a problem. Engine selection logic becomes an executable DSL (SelIR), and a max-flow minimum vertex cut derives the fewest values that must be pinned (3 attention + 3 all-reduce pins on FA3), so the fast kernels survive. A 26-cell battery passes on two engines, two GPU generations and TP 1–8 where Stock fails 5–19 cells; p50 per-token latency is 1.08–1.11× Stock versus 2.4–5.7× for deterministic modes; 512/512 seeded rollouts replay bit-for-bit with trainer KL exactly 0; and BF16 GRPO holds reward 0.843 where Stock collapses to 0.436.
- **保留意见 / Caveat:** GRPO 稳定性结论是单种子、且降低了学习率的健全性测试，准确率只在已饱和的 GSM8K 上测，未发布工件。| The GRPO stability result is a single-seed sanity test with a lowered learning rate, accuracy is only measured on saturated GSM8K, and there is no released artifact.

### 5. [[2611.08269]] 接受了但是错的：API 迁移后 LLM 代码修复的静默失效（Accepted but Wrong）
**领域 / Area:** Agent & Pipeline Silent Failures · **类型 / Type:** 分析 / analysis

- **理由 / Why:** 把“这个发布门够不够严”变成一个可穷举测量的量：修复域是有限片段菜单，于是可以在真实 CPython/Node.js/SQLite 运行时上对每个候选修复（405 → 1,491 → 1,689 个片段，最多 2,310 个过程）对照隐藏契约全量评估，得到假接受率而非抽样估计。结论干净且可操作：典型输入 + 全部消费者断言放行 0.410 的隐藏错误程序，加一条 release-note 边界输入降到 0.065 而假拒绝持平在 0.010，并在 15 个事后新建的簇上复现（0.471→0.061，Holm p=0.002）；另有盲写手接近作者水平（0.070）、预注册守卫反而失败（0.157）的诚实报告。| Turns "is this release gate adequate?" into an exhaustively measurable quantity: because the repair domain is a finite menu of documented fragments, every candidate (405 → 1,491 → 1,689 fragments, up to 2,310 procedures) can be evaluated against withheld contracts on real CPython/Node.js/SQLite runtimes, yielding false-acceptance rates rather than samples. The result is clean and actionable: typical inputs plus all consumer assertions release 0.410 of hidden-wrong programs, one release-note edge input cuts it to 0.065 with false rejection flat at 0.010, and it replicates on 15 clusters created after the effect was observed (0.471→0.061, Holm p=0.002) — alongside honest reporting of a blind writer nearly matching the author (0.070) and a preregistered guard that failed (0.157).
- **保留意见 / Caveat:** 0.410 这个 headline 依赖菜单构造（菜单扩大时 0.406→0.271），且 16.8% 的硬干扰项仍能通过边界输入门。| The 0.410 headline is menu-dependent (0.406 to 0.271 when the menu grows), and 16.8% of the hard distractors still pass the edge-input gate.

### 6. [[2611.11248]] FESOM2.8 单精度：六十年气候保真度与 CPU/GPU 性能（FESOM2.8 in Single Precision）
**领域 / Area:** Reliability under Extreme Performance Optimization · **类型 / Type:** 分析 / analysis

- **理由 / Why:** 降精度领域罕见的“六十年配对对照 + 分级消融链”：朴素 float32 以 1.2×10⁻⁴ psu/yr 稳定丢盐、累积 −129 ZJ 热亏、SST RMS 0.1649 K（是双精度孪生散度 0.00463 K 的 36 倍）；关键的受控反例是“64 位状态 + 32 位增量”把漂移符号翻成 +1.85 mpsu，从而证明偏差在增量而非累加。修复（64 位倾向累加 + 厚度加权携带舍入余项）把 SST RMS 降到 0.0046 K、热含量差压到 1.5 ZJ（对照 1.0 ZJ），代价 ≤8%，换 1.35× 回算加速与 GPU 上 40–43% 显存节省，并已进入 FESOM 2.8.0 发布。| A rare "sixty-year paired control plus staged ablation chain" in reduced precision: naive float32 loses salt steadily at 1.2×10⁻⁴ psu/yr, accumulates a −129 ZJ heat deficit and shifts SST by 0.1649 K RMS — 36× the 0.00463 K double-precision twin spread. The decisive control is that a 64-bit state with 32-bit increments *reverses* the drift to +1.85 mpsu, proving the bias lives in the increment, not the accumulation. The fix (64-bit tendencies plus a thickness-weighted carried remainder) brings SST RMS to 0.0046 K and the heat gap to 1.5 ZJ against a 1.0 ZJ control, for ≤8% cost, 1.35× hindcast speedup and 40–43% less GPU memory — shipped in FESOM 2.8.0.
- **保留意见 / Caveat:** 保真度只在名义 1° 分辨率上确立，残余盐度增益机制未解释，且耦合模式检验被自身 54 ZJ 孪生散度削弱了统计功效。| Fidelity is established only at nominal 1 degree, the residual salinity gain is mechanistically unexplained, and the coupled-model test is underpowered by its own 54 ZJ twin spread.

### 7. [[2611.05344]] 上下文学习的一条回路（A Circuit for In-Context Learning）
**领域 / Area:** LLM Hidden States & Interpretability · **类型 / Type:** 分析 / analysis

- **理由 / Why:** 少见的“完整组合而非零件清单”：在三个 3–4B 模型上把任意标签的少样本句子分类拆成可因果验证的链——标签 token 的残差流是键值槽（值走 W_V、键走 W_K，交换标签与替换类别两个孪生 prompt 干净分离）、约 90 个上层 reader 读出、L22h10+L23h26 写查询码、L1–9 的 16 个重复 token 检测头汇聚成标量门控方向压低 attention sink、约 24 个 brake 头抵消近因偏置；敲掉 20 个 reader 掉 −0.74 nats（随机 +0.02），敲掉 24 个 brake 涨 +1.39 nats（随机 +0.22）。还给出闭式读取律（127/140 个 Qwen 头拟合在 0.1 内）、增益上界解释“为何必须人海战术”、以及从零训练 3.4M 模型只在特定训练分布下长出 gate 的因果证据，代码开源。| Rarely does a paper supply the composition rather than a parts list: across three 3–4B models it decomposes arbitrary-label few-shot sentence classification into a causally verified chain — label tokens as key–value slots (values through W_V, keys through W_K, cleanly separated by label-swap and class-swap twin prompts), ~90 upper-layer readers, L22h10+L23h26 writing the query code, 16 duplicate-token heads in L1–9 pooled into a scalar gate direction that lowers the attention sink, and ~24 brake heads cancelling recency. Knocking out 20 readers costs −0.74 nats (random +0.02); removing 24 brakes adds +1.39 nats (random +0.22). It also derives a closed-form read law (127/140 Qwen heads within 0.1), a gain bound explaining why some steps must be a population, and causal evidence from a 3.4M model that only grows the gate under one training distribution — with code released.
- **保留意见 / Caveat:** 完整性百分比依赖参考集，且在一次对照修正后移动了 0.77 nats；“纯回路胜过完整模型”是度量假象。| The completeness percentage is reference-dependent and moved 0.77 nats after a control fix, and circuit-only beating intact is a metric artifact.

### 8. [[2611.07477]] KOSMOS：自调度常驻内核的通信-计算重叠（KOSMOS）

- **理由 / Why:** 系统论文里少见的诚实：480 个微基准格全胜、一次只改一处的消融把“队列顺序”与“自调度”分离（自调度本身只值 2–9%，大部分收益来自融合与队列顺序，朴素 AG 顺序要付 1.17–1.40×），并给出可落地的策略规则——需求单调的集合通信用单向切换、需求交错的 MoE combine 用双向切换，而“越动态越好”的直觉在 TP+SP 上被实测证伪（双向切换 0.951×）。覆盖 TP+SP 前后向 BF16/MXFP8 与整层 MoE megakernel，8×MI350X/MI355X 上 1.10–1.28× 端到端、MoE 代理 1.29–1.83×，另送一个比 RCCL ring 归约误差低 2.21× 且逐次位一致的 RS 实现。| Unusually honest for a systems paper: 480 microbenchmark cells all faster, one-change-at-a-time ablations that separate queue *ordering* from self-scheduling (self-scheduling itself is worth only 2–9%; most of the gain is fusion and ordering, and a naive AG order costs 1.17–1.40×), and a deployable policy rule — one-way switching for monotonic demand, two-way for interleaved MoE combine, with the "more dynamic is better" intuition empirically refuted on TP+SP (two-way switching gives 0.951×). It covers TP+SP forward/backward in BF16 and MXFP8 plus a whole-layer MoE megakernel, reaching 1.10–1.28× end-to-end on 8×MI350X/MI355X and 1.29–1.83× on MoE proxies, and throws in a bitwise-reproducible RS with 2.21× lower reduction error than RCCL's ring.
- **保留意见 / Caveat:** 单节点 8 卡评估、405B 只跑代理规模（126 层中的 24 层）、无收敛曲线、未发布代码。| Single-node 8-GPU evaluation, proxy-scale 405B (24 of 126 layers), no convergence curves, and no released code.
