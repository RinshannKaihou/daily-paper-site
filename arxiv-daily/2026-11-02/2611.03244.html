Why this matters

Turns “will this RM fix help?” from a multi-hundred-GPU-hour gamble into one scoring pass — though the ranking is only reliable for ~30 steps.

One pass of ~16 samples per prompt, scored by the old and the new RM.

▲ Critical edge

The forecast’s ranking reverses by step 200 (Spearman −0.77), expiring long before the 1,000+ step runs practitioners actually ship.

Also flagged: the 82% headline is an in-sample fit, and two high-dose arms were dropped for length runaway.

Method

  1. Price the reward model. Write r = a(x) + Σ pkfk; the KL-regularized optimum turns feature supply into a covariance, S = Cov(f,f)/β.
  2. Forecast the edit. Δsk ≈ κ·Ex[Covx(fk, Δr)] needs only one pass of policy samples scored by the old and the new RM.
  3. Isolate the price channel. Soft-label price shifts are implanted into RMs retrained from the proxy’s exact recipe; a self-distilled placebo holds prices fixed. 2(4−1) factorial, nine arms × six parents, 54 RLOO runs; two arms dropped for length runaway.
  4. Freeze, then test. Predictors, signs, and rankings are frozen before the first run; κ is fitted once on the factorial batch, then held fixed for out-of-sample transfer to three real RM upgrades and to GRPO.

Forecast horizon

0.94 → −0.77. Spearman correlation of the six arms by change in rent: stable early, reversed by step 200. Under GRPO the reversal is −0.83.

Real RM upgrades do not reverse. The chart shows the two reported endpoints; the intermediate path is not reported.

Strengths

  • Almost free. One scoring pass versus hundreds of GPU-hours per RL run.
  • Genuinely causal. Identically retrained RMs plus a self-distilled placebo isolate price from data, architecture, and scale.
  • Pre-registered. Signs, rankings, and numeric criteria frozen before the first run, with a dated decision log.
  • Honest negatives. Six pre-specified hypotheses reported as unsupported or not met.
  • The length result explains, not fits. 0.84 vs 0.07 was predicted before the runs — the price model cannot see unpriced length.

Limitations

  • Single testbed. One 1.7B policy family, Tulu-3, UltraFeedback, RLOO; no 7B–70B sweep.
  • Short horizon by construction. The rent ranking expires by step 200, well short of deployed runs.
  • Missing worst shocks. Two arms ran away to the 1,024-token limit and were dropped — exactly the large summary-price rises.
  • Rule-based goods only. Tone, hedging, sycophancy, and refusal are untested.
  • Not reproducible yet. No code or artifact release; ≈3,450 GPU-hours total.

Open questions

  • Q1

    Does the covariance–price gap survive at 7B–70B, where reward models are better calibrated?

  • Q2

    Why does β0 vanish for price cuts — 0.0029 after a rise, 0 after a cut?

  • Q3

    Can a model of how Cov(f, Δr) evolves with KL replace checkpoint recomputation — and beat a 30-step pilot?

  • Q4

    Is the non-additivity stable across RMs, or an artifact of an undertrained 3,000-pair proxy?
