Why this matters 为什么重要

Same prompt, different batch-mates, different answer. The silent bug behind flaky evals and unreproducible RL rollouts. BlockLock pins the geometry that causes it.

Critical edge 关键质疑

The headline 53.5–55.0% win is inherited: against vLLM STD — what teams actually deploy — BlockLock-Rows is 3.6–9.9% slower, not faster.

Critical Assessment · framing

01

Lock the shape, not the math

方法 · stock PyTorch + vLLM

  1. Bucket Tokenized lengths are bucketed at L = 64; adjacent buckets merge into frozen classes of width W = mL. The class map is fixed for every compared execution.
  2. Lock Rows keeps one [B, W] prefill: the target holds a fixed row and fixed logical positions while all B requests still decode together, at O(BW²). Pack concatenates companions, an isolating dummy segment and the target into [1, BW], anchors the target at the terminal interval, extracts its KV, then decodes it alone at [1, 1].
  3. Gate C1 canonical geometry, C2 repeat-stable ops and collectives, C3 faithful cache handoff; an induction over decode steps yields bitwise-identical raw logits per deployment tuple. cuDNN SDPA and TF32 are disabled.

Stock PyTorch and vLLM — no custom kernels, no CUDA graphs. The guarantee is per-deployment: change B, GPU, TP degree, framework version or class map and it must be re-validated.

Default policy still produced 2/4 (CoRun), 3/3 (Padded), 2/3 (Rows) unique sequences over 320 greedy steps. One logit moved 24.625 → 24.5 at step 280 and the argmax flipped. Disabling cuDNN SDPA and TF32 collapsed every repeat to a single sequence.

03

Strengths

  • The reframing is actionable: pin the geometry, not the arithmetic — a one-sentence idea a serving engineer can implement.
  • No custom kernels: stock PyTorch and stock vLLM. The SlotMapping = −1 trick decouples prefill padding from decode KV storage.
  • Measurement discipline: token IDs, Top-20 raw-logit bits, and full-vocabulary SHA-256, with per-configuration reporting instead of a pooled average.
  • The B = 1 backend finding stands alone: a fixed seed still produced 2/4, 3/3 and 2/3 unique sequences until cuDNN SDPA and TF32 were disabled.
  • Failure regimes are reported: the W = 4096 losses and the CoRun caveat are in the paper, not buried.
04

Limitations

  • Per-deployment only: change the GPU, TP degree, framework version or class map and the guarantee must be re-validated. Outputs are not comparable across batch sizes.
  • Dense models only; MoE capacity-factor token dropping sits outside the contract.
  • Padding waste under variance: +9.0% to +12.1% at W = 4096, and +43.5% on the Blazedit group.
  • Pack scales as O(B²W²), protects only the terminal request, and no memory figures are reported for either path.
  • Greedy only: seeded sampling is argued, not measured. No queueing, tail latency or occupancy sweep; artifact withheld.
05

Open questions

待解问题

  1. What does the restricted backend policy cost in throughput? If it exceeds 10%, the efficiency argument needs restating.
  2. How does CoRun do with a 4-slot decode envelope instead of 256? The 3.28× only means something after that re-run.
  3. Does the contract survive seeded sampling — especially Pack, where decode runs at [1, 1] after a shared prefill?
  4. At realistic occupancy (mean concurrency 2–3), does P = BW reservation still beat CoRun, or does the crossover move to smaller W?
