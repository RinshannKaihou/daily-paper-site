Why it matters

SAE safety work that reports leakage wins may be measuring signal magnitude, not the causal pathway — so the fix can vanish under a fair control.

The same audit applies to refusal ablation, toxicity steering and concept unlearning.

⚠
Critical edge

Failure mode B may be argmax-overfitting on a 10-pair discovery split rather than mechanistic collapse — the paper never re-runs discovery on disjoint splits.

Missing ablation · selection stability

01

How the win is produced — and audited

Five moves, one circular selection step, two matched random controls.

  1. 01

    Corpus

    LoRA fine-tune Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, Qwen3-8B and Gemma-2-9B on synthetic clinical notes that append a name, patient ID, phone and email to every record.

  2. 02

    Dictionaries

    Train top-k SAEs (×8 expansion, k=32) at 25 / 50 / 75% of depth — 9 (model, layer) pairs — and test all four PII types: 36 cells.

  3. 03

    Selection

    Per cell, keep the candidate feature whose ablation cuts gold-PII logprob most on a 10-pair discovery split. This logprob-driven choice is the circular step under test.

  4. 04

    Intervention

    Steer along the feature's decoder direction at α = −2, across 9 prompt templates × 50 held-out pairs — 450 contexts per cell, no per-cell tuning.

  5. 05

    Judgement

    Require all four gates plus selectivity against two random controls. A parallel 9,000-run noising study picks candidates by activation rate instead, breaking the circularity.

The four gates

A cell is “clean-strong” only if every condition holds.

Δlp ≤ −0.3gold-span logprob must fall
Δbr ≥ +1.0gold tokens must sink in rank-based extraction cost
Sellp ≥ 5effect ≥ 5× a magnitude-matched random direction
Selbr ≥ 5the same ratio on the rank metric
h′t = ht + α · zt[f] · Wdec[f]  ·  α = −2

Controls match token-wise magnitude and span-level coherence; a second control draws 20 decoder directions from the same learned SAE dictionary.

02

Where the 47% goes

The apparent win splits into two reproducible failure modes — with one genuine positive control.

0.37

Failure A · random direction wins

Gemma L20/name selectivity is below 1 for all four PII types (name 0.37, ID 0.14, phone 0.27, email 0.09). The random direction wins 50/50 validation pairs and 439/450 contexts; all 20/20 dictionary-aligned directions beat the selected feature (−9.10 vs −2.80 mean Δlp).

2.3×

Failure B · one feature, four types

Llama L8 f=16363 is selected for all four PII types with ≤5% activation variation, yet suppresses names 2.3× harder than the rest (|Δlp| 4.57 vs 1.96). 14/20 dictionary directions still beat it. Shared selection does not imply a shared cause.

03

What holds — and what doesn't

A careful negative result — with its own strongest objection left un-run.

+

Strengths

05
  • The positive control is decisive. Llama L16/name holds Sel_lp > 400 on unseen Korean and JSON templates, and 0/20 same-dictionary directions reproduce it.
  • Two controls close the escape hatch. Magnitude-matched isotropic plus dictionary-aligned directions rule out the off-manifold objection.
  • Sensitivity is thorough. An α sweep and three threshold axes; Qwen L18/name is reported honestly as α-dependent rather than dropped.
  • Utility tracks the diagnosis. The clean cell preserves accuracy; the non-clean cells lose 12–34 points.
  • The mediation study is a real contribution. 9,000 runs with candidate selection deliberately decoupled from logprob.
−

Limitations

05
  • 10-pair discovery split. No stability ablation across disjoint splits, so failure mode B may be argmax overfitting.
  • PrivacyScalpel is never re-run. The critique lands on the evaluation signal, not on the published z-space numbers.
  • One configuration family. ×8 SAEs, k=32, one α, three fixed layers; 36 cells are closer to 9 effective units.
  • Synthetic, formulaic corpus. Emails derive from names and phones from arithmetic, so “memorization” may be format regularity.
  • No adversarial extraction. Even the clean success is small: Δbr +1.465, roughly 2.8× extraction cost.
04

Four experiments that would settle it

Each one attacks a different weak joint in the argument.

Q1

Does f=16363 survive disjoint discovery splits? Re-run selection on pairs 10–19, 20–29, 30–39 — the test that decides whether collapse is mechanistic or statistical.

Q2

Would a plain baseline beat the SAE feature? A difference-in-means or gradient direction would show the problem is feature isolation, not evaluation.

Q3

Does 47% → 3% hold on real clinical text? Or is the email-vs-phone gap an artifact of deterministic, formulaic PII strings?

Q4

Does the protocol transfer? The random-control audit is cheap and domain-agnostic — refusal ablation, toxicity steering and unlearning are the real test.
