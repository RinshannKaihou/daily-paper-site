> **一句话总结：** 论文指出"探针分数连续 3 层超过零假设阈值即判定信号出现"这一常见深度探测规则，在层间相关的 transformer 表示上会把 onset 提前 19 层（合成数据 ground truth 第 20 层报成第 1 层），并给出用"跨深度固定的组内置换零轨迹 + 逐层顺序统计量阈值 + 5 层持续性"标定的估计器；在冻结 Boltz-1 的 48 个 Pairformer block 上，该规则把真实零轨迹的误报率从 12–17% 压到 4–7%，同时发现口袋信息在第 0 层就已可解码，但"熟悉家族 + 结构新颖口袋"（FN）这一格最难，中位 AUPRC 0.230 对 NF 的 0.601（2.6 倍）。

> **TL;DR:** The standard rule for reading a probing depth curve — "first layer where the score clears a null threshold for three consecutive layers" — is broken: on synthetic curves with a known onset at stage 20 and AR(1) depth correlation at ρ=0.9, it reports onset at stage 1 (a −19-stage error), and on 100 real null trajectories from a frozen Boltz-1 trunk it fires on 12–17% of them against a nominal 5%. Replacing it with a within-protein label permutation held fixed across depth, per-layer order-statistic thresholds, and a five-stage persistence requirement brings the empirical false-positive rate to 0.04–0.07 (4 of 4 regimes) at no cost on synthetic recovery. Applied to pocket membership on 534 held-out PLINDER targets (1,820 extracted post-cutoff systems, 48 blocks, linear probe, median AUPRC), the calibrated onset is layer 0 in all four family/pocket familiarity regimes — the endpoint cannot discriminate — but the pre-declared accumulation depth ℓ90 is latest for familiar-family/novel-pocket (FN) in all three feature sets, and FN is the weakest cell: 0.230 vs 0.601 for NF at a common layer on the per-residue track (2.6×), a gap that survives Holm correction (9/9 FN contrasts pass, 0/9 others) but shrinks to 0.087 under an alternative pocket-similarity metric. A side finding with practical bite: the public Boltz-2 checkpoint loads against its own released code with 128 trunk parameters missing (one attention layer-norm per block, 64 blocks), which are silently randomly initialized.

## 核心贡献

- **诊断出一个普遍但未被检查的评测错误**：深度探测中"连续 k 层超过阈值即报 onset"的持续性规则，在相邻层互为残差更新的 transformer 上会因层间分数相关而产生系统性早报。合成数据中真实 onset 在第 20 层，3 层规则在 ρ=0.9 时报第 1 层（−19 层），而每层单独的显著性检验完全看不出问题——错误发生在层与层之间，不在层内。
- **给出误报率被标定而非被假设的估计器**：零假设轨迹由"在每个蛋白内置换标签、且同一置换在全部 48 层固定"构造，从而保留规则必须承受的深度相关结构；阈值取每层 B=100 次置换的有限样本顺序统计量（α=0.05 时为第 96 个值，越界概率 ≤5/101≈0.0495）；持续性要求 p=5 由合成校准预先固定，而非事后挑选。
- **用留一法在真实零轨迹上审计误报率**：每条零轨迹用其余 B−1 条复制品重建的阈值来检验，避免"用自己抬高的阈值检验自己"的 in-sample 偏差。5 层规则在四个 regime 上落在 0.04–0.07（名义 0.05），3 层规则落在 0.12–0.17，单层规则约 0.68–0.71——合成中的失败在真实表示上以同类方式复现。
- **提出累积深度 ℓ90 作为 onset 不可分辨时的次级统计量**：定义为首次达到"第 0 层之上可用增益的 90%"的层，用增益而非绝对高度归一化，因此在信号一开始就已超过阈值、onset 恒为 0 的情形下仍然有定义。
- **把"新颖口袋崩溃"分解为家族轴 × 口袋轴的四格设计**：PLINDER 2026-09、Boltz-1 的 2021-09-30 训练截止、25,289 个通过校验且带口袋的 post-cutoff 系统、抽取 1,820 个、534 个留出评估蛋白。结论反直觉：最难的既不是"双新"（NN），也不是"新家族 + 熟悉口袋"（NF），而是"熟悉家族 + 结构新颖口袋"（FN）。
- **附带报告一个静默的公开权重缺陷**：Boltz-2 的发布 checkpoint 与其发布代码不匹配，缺 128 个参数（每个 trunk block 注意力模块内的一个 layer norm，共 64 个 block），缺失参数被随机初始化，模型照常运行、表示照常可读，但每个表示都掺了噪声。

## Key Contributions

- **Names and quantifies a methodological failure mode in depth-resolved probing.** The persistence rule that most layer-wise probing papers use to declare an onset is anti-conservative when neighbouring layers are correlated. The paper shows this is not a small bias: the error is 19 stages on a 65-stage stack, i.e. the reported onset is essentially at the input embedding, and it comes with a bootstrap interval so wide it cannot contradict the point estimate — a confident-looking number that no uncertainty estimate flags.
- **Provides a calibrated estimator with three separable pieces**, each doing distinct work: (i) null trajectories rather than independent per-layer draws (preserves depth correlation), (ii) per-layer finite-sample order statistics rather than interpolated quantiles or a single global threshold (the null level tracks each layer's capacity to fit noise), (iii) a persistence length fixed on synthetic data with known ground truth before any real representation was touched. The leave-one-out audit is the fourth piece and is what makes the error rate measured rather than asserted.
- **Separates magnitude from timing.** Because a weaker signal crosses a fixed threshold later even when it starts at the same stage, the paper runs paired synthetic arms sharing proteins, partition, noise realisation and signal direction, differing only in gain, to bound how much of a measured Δonset is "delay purchased entirely with signal strength." It explicitly refuses to treat this as a correction term.
- **Decomposes the novelty collapse into two crossed axes** using PLINDER's precomputed annotations, with an asymmetric definition of family familiarity (one familiar chain suffices for familiar; novelty requires every chain resolved and none familiar) that avoids manufacturing novel-family targets out of cluster-index gaps.
- **Reports a null primary endpoint honestly.** The pre-registered contrast FF vs NN returns +0.053 [−0.096, +0.169] and the pre-registered onset contrast Δonset = 0 because all 48 layers clear threshold in all regimes. Both are reported as uninformative rather than reinterpreted, and the paper says so in as many words.
- **Documents a silent checkpoint defect** that would invalidate exactly this class of study, and notes that no public commit of the Boltz-2 repository matches the distributed checkpoint.

## Methodology

**Design.** Two crossed familiarity axes measured against a pre-training reference pool: `Sf` (protein-family familiarity, from PLINDER's MMseqs sequence clustering joined at chain level) and `Sp` (pocket familiarity, the maximum pocket `qcov` between a target pocket and any pre-cutoff pocket, aggregated over a system's ligands by minimum and thresholded at 80). Crossing them gives FF, FN, NF, NN. The trunk is frozen throughout — the question is what is already accessible, not what fine-tuning could achieve.

**Data.** PLINDER release 2026-09. Boltz-1's structural training cutoff of 2021-09-30 (treated as exclusive, since the model is reported as trained "up to" that date) defines the reference pool: 366,197 pre-cutoff systems, 245,732 post-cutoff candidates. PLINDER's composite validation flag holds for 18% of systems, reducing candidates to 25,933, of which 25,289 carry a pocket. 1,820 systems are extracted; 534 test proteins are evaluated (FF 152, FN 122, NF 136, NN 124). The reference pool is deliberately *not* quality-filtered, since the question is what the trunk could have been exposed to.

**Extraction.** Forward hooks on each of the 48 Pairformer blocks capture `s ∈ R^{L×384}` and `z ∈ R^{L×L×128}`; only the final recycle is retained. `s` is kept at full width; `z` is reduced to row mean, column mean and self-pair diagonal (the diagonal being unrecoverable from either marginal). Three feature sets: per-residue `s`, pooled `z`, and their concatenation.

**Probe and metric.** A linear probe is fitted per layer, per regime, per feature set, scored by AUPRC because positives are roughly one residue in fifteen (positive rates 0.065–0.079). Medians are taken across test proteins, with interquartile bands; the random-ranking baseline is each regime's own positive rate, and fold-over-baseline is reported alongside raw AUPRC.

**Null construction.** Labels are permuted *within each protein*, so every protein keeps its own positive rate and only the identity of pocket-lining residues is destroyed. Permuting across proteins would also redistribute pocket size and make the null easier than the real task, depressing the threshold. Replicate `b` uses the same permutation at every layer and for every feature set, so its per-layer null values form a trajectory `(N_0^(b), …, N_L^(b))` rather than L+1 unrelated draws. This is required, not convenient: the onset is defined by a run of consecutive exceedances, so its error rate depends on inter-layer correlation, and independent per-layer permutations would destroy precisely the structure being measured.

**Threshold and onset.** `τ_{ℓ,q}` is the k-th smallest of B=100 null values at layer ℓ, with `k = ⌈(1−α)(B+1)⌉` — for α=0.05, the 96th ordered value, giving exceedance probability ≤ 5/101 ≈ 0.0495. An order statistic is used rather than an interpolated quantile so that the threshold is a value the null actually produced. Onset is the first layer of five *adjacent* layers all exceeding their own-layer threshold; a run of five non-adjacent measurements spans more depth and is a weaker requirement, so only a complete unit-spaced layer set is analysed.

**Empirical false-positive audit.** Each null trajectory `b` is tested against a threshold built from the other B−1 replicates (`τ^(−b)`), and the fraction producing an onset is reported. The leave-one-out construction is necessary because a trajectory tested against a threshold it helped raise is in-sample and understates the rate. Observed curves use the full-B threshold.

**Synthetic calibration (Section 5.3).** n=200 synthetic proteins of 40–90 residues across 65 stages of width 32, ~12% of residues labelled pocket in one contiguous run, signal added along a fixed random direction with per-stage gain that is zero before `ℓ_true` and rises smoothly after. Partitions are formed over proteins, and the null permutes labels within each protein, matching the real setting. Stage noise is an AR(1) process `ε_ℓ = ρ ε_{ℓ−1} + √(1−ρ²) η_ℓ` evaluated at ρ=0 and ρ=0.9, with the signal added on top so `ℓ_true` remains exactly true. Four injected scenarios: clean (20), early (5), weak (20), null (none). Table 1 gives recovered onsets; Table 2 gives protein-level bootstrap intervals.

**Accumulation depth.** `ℓ90,q = min{ℓ : M_ℓ,q ≥ M_0,q + 0.9 (max_ℓ' M_ℓ',q − M_0,q)}` — the first layer reaching 90% of the gain available above layer 0. Pre-declared as secondary while only FF had been inspected. Because it is measured against the observed maximum, it inherits whatever noise placed that maximum, and is an accumulation depth relative to the peak seen rather than an estimate of a latent saturation point.

**Statistics.** All inference is at the protein level, never the residue level. Uncertainty comes from 2,000 resamples of *receptor-family clusters* (taking all of a cluster's proteins when one is drawn), because proteins within a family share sequence and fold; cluster resampling roughly doubles interval widths relative to protein resampling. Contrasts are computed inside each replicate rather than from separately resampled estimates. p-values are two-sided, null-centred on the same resamples, and Holm-corrected across all 18 contrasts (three feature sets × six pairs), a family fixed before the result.

## Results & Highlights

- **The synthetic failure is exact and one-directional.** With an injected onset at stage 20, the 3-layer rule at ρ=0.9 recovers stage 1 — a signed error of **−19 stages** — while at ρ=0 the same weak signal is recovered at stage 22 (+2, the benign direction). Raising persistence to 5 recovers stage 20 exactly (+0) at ρ=0.9, and the clean and early scenarios stay exact at +0 in every configuration, with the null correctly unresolved.
- **Interval width fails independently of point accuracy.** The clean scenario at ρ=0.9 with 3-layer persistence recovers the injected stage exactly yet its bootstrap interval is **[0, 21], width 21 of 65 stages**, with a lower bound at stage 0 — an instrument already degraded in a scenario whose point estimate looks like a clean pass. With 5-layer persistence that interval collapses to **[20, 21], width 1**. Conversely the weak scenario keeps a 22-stage-wide interval in every configuration, including the one where its point estimate is exactly right.
- **The failure reproduces on real null trajectories, at a magnitude simulation did not predict.** On 100 null trajectories per regime from the frozen trunk, the 5-stage rule fires at **0.06 (s/FF), 0.05 (s/FN), 0.04 (s/NF), 0.07 (s/NN)** against nominal α=0.05; the 3-stage rule at **0.17, 0.12, 0.13, 0.12**; the single-layer rule at **0.71, 0.69, 0.71, 0.68** — roughly seven null trajectories in ten.
- **Thresholds are nearly flat with depth, which was not obvious in advance.** Across all 48 layers τ varies by about 0.003 within each regime: **0.094–0.097 (FF), 0.083–0.086 (FN and NN), 0.088–0.092 (NF)**. Each sits roughly 0.016 above its regime's positive rate, which is the margin a positive-rate baseline alone would have missed — and it means a single global threshold would have been a fair approximation *here*, undercutting the necessity of the per-layer machinery on this particular trunk.
- **Pocket membership is decodable everywhere.** Every curve clears its threshold at **48 of 48 layers** in all four regimes and all three feature sets, so the pre-registered onset is **layer 0** and Δonset = ℓ*_NN − ℓ*_FF = **0**. At its peak the weakest regime reaches **3.4×** a random ordering of residues and the strongest **9.9×**.
- **The hardest cell is FN, and the gap is large at the primary operating point.** At the common evaluation layer on the per-residue track: **FN 0.230 vs NF 0.601 (2.6×)**, with FF 0.530 and NN 0.476. By peak AUPRC the ordering on `s` is **NF 0.609, FF 0.540, NN 0.476, FN 0.270** — the same ordering in all three feature sets (pooled `z`: NF 0.704, FF 0.695, NN 0.629, FN 0.303; concatenated: NF 0.724, FF 0.705, NN 0.637, FN 0.353).
- **Only the FN contrasts survive multiplicity correction.** All **9 of 9** contrasts placing FN below another regime survive Holm at α=0.05 in every feature set, the largest adjusted p being **0.015**; **0 of 9** contrasts not involving FN survive. On the primary feature set the three are FF–FN +0.300 [+0.115, +0.403], NF–FN +0.371 [+0.211, +0.470], NN–FN +0.246 [+0.070, +0.374]. Seven raw p-values sit at 0.0005, the floor 2,000 replicates can produce, so the correction is conservative for those.
- **The pre-registered primary contrast returned nothing.** FF–NN is **+0.053 [−0.096, +0.169], p=0.4323** on the primary feature set, and contains zero in all three feature sets. NF–FF (+0.071, [−0.015, +0.188]) also contains zero and is not interpreted. The separation the design was built to detect exists, but between the intermediate cells rather than the corners.
- **FN is lowest wherever the pocket-familiarity boundary is drawn.** Holding metric and aggregation fixed and moving only the threshold: at τ=50 on `s` FN is **0.079** against FF 0.452, NF 0.589, NN 0.219; at τ=80, **0.230** against 0.530 / 0.601 / 0.476; at τ=100, **0.365** against 0.455 / 0.447 / 0.562. The rest of the ordering is *not* stable — NN rises steeply with the threshold while FF falls beyond the primary value, and on the per-residue track NF and FF converge at the strictest boundary.
- **Accumulation depth puts FN last in every feature set.** ℓ90 on the primary feature set: **NF 14, FF 15, NN 17, FN 22**; pooled `z`: FF 19, NF 21, NN 17, FN 27; concatenated: FF 17, NF 19, NN 22, FN 26. Across all twelve curves ℓ90 lies between 14 and 27, so most of what a linear probe can extract from depth is complete before the halfway point of a 48-block stack.
- **The gap is not evaluation geometry.** FN and NF are matched on median protein length (382 vs 367), pocket size (24.0 vs 25.5) and positive rate (0.068 vs 0.071) while differing 2.6-fold in AUPRC. Regressing per-protein AUPRC on length, pocket size and positive rate gives R²=0.321; adding regime indicators raises it by **ΔR²=0.080–0.090**, though the covariates explain **3.7–3.8× more** variance than the regime does beyond them.
- **Nor alignment depth.** FN and NF have near-identical median alignment depths (**7,692 vs 7,966**, a 1.04× ratio) and near-identical fractions at the 8,192 recording ceiling (0.44 vs 0.48), while their AUPRC differs 2.6×. Across all 534 test proteins, depth and AUPRC are essentially uncorrelated: Spearman **0.048** overall, 0.068 among the 287 below the ceiling. NN has the shallowest alignments of the four (5,156) yet scores 0.476, above FN.
- **Fitted continuously, the two axes point in opposite directions.** Pocket familiarity predicts decodability positively, **+0.110 SD [+0.091, +0.129]**; family support predicts it *negatively*, **−0.069 SD [−0.091, −0.048]** — more pre-cutoff relatives go with lower decodability, the opposite of a memorisation account, and invisible when the axis is split in two. The pocket axis is larger by a factor of about 1.6, and the interaction does not separate from zero.
- **Covalency does not close the account, but it is not excluded either.** Pooled across regimes and adjusting for regime, covalent systems score lower by **0.221 [−0.279, −0.160]** (unadjusted −0.245), but the association is not common across regimes (p=0.004): slopes are −0.333 (FF), −0.318 (NN), −0.123 (FN), −0.079 (NF) — FN has the third most negative. Within FN, the covalent-vs-non-covalent contrast against NF does not resolve: **−0.256 [−0.429, +0.242]**.
- **The checkpoint defect is concrete.** Boltz-2's distributed checkpoint lacks **128 parameters** its own model class expects — one layer norm inside the attention module of each of its **64** trunk blocks. No public commit of the repository matches the checkpoint.

## Authors & Credibility

Maryam Rahimimovassagh, Michael Brown, Sanjeda Sara Jennifer and Niloofar Yousefi, all at the University of Central Florida. The group is not one of the established co-folding or mechanistic-interpretability labs; this reads as a careful methods-and-diagnostics paper from a statistics-adjacent group rather than a model-building one, which fits what it does — the trunk (Boltz-1) and the dataset (PLINDER) are both off-the-shelf, and the contribution is the measurement discipline.

Credibility signals are unusually strong for this genre, and they are structural rather than rhetorical: hypotheses and kill criteria are pre-registered in Appendix A and G; the persistence value and α are fixed on synthetic data with known ground truth before any real representation is extracted; the multiplicity family is fixed at all 18 contrasts before the result rather than narrowed to the ones that separate; resampling is at the receptor-family cluster rather than the protein, with the reason stated; the false-positive rate is measured on the study's own null with a leave-one-out construction rather than inferred from the simulation; and the paper repeatedly volunteers the limits of its own claims ("we report the primary endpoint as uninformative rather than reinterpreting it"; "the magnitude does not [survive]"). The discussion of what would have been a self-serving result — an onset difference — and the refusal to take it is the strongest credibility signal in the paper.

Counterweights: the empirical result rests on one trunk, one dataset release and 534 test proteins, and the authors say so. Reproducibility is disclosed but imperfect — `plinder-core` 0.2.27 serves only release 2024-06 through its query interface and *silently substitutes it*, so the tables were read as files from the release directory instead; and the per-ligand pocket-similarity scores were computed in October 2026 with the generating script not retained, so regenerating them means re-implementing from a written rule. Code and results are at https://github.com/maryam-rahimi/Beyond_Pocket. The Boltz-2 claim is asserted with a specific count (128 parameters, 64 blocks) but the verification procedure is described rather than shown.

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty.** The core statistical machinery is imported, and the paper says so: cluster-based permutation inference with consecutive-threshold requirements is standard in neuroimaging ([4], [10]), and the paper cites both. The novelty claim is narrower and defensible — nobody had checked whether *depth* curves, whose index is computational stage rather than time and whose stages are residual updates of one another, need the same treatment. That is a real gap and the demonstration is convincing. The two-axis familiarity design is also borrowed (zPocket/PocketWatch supplies both axes and the structural-over-sequence finding); what is new is the crossing and the FN cell.

**Rigor.** This is the paper's strongest dimension. Pre-registration, a fixed 18-contrast multiplicity family, cluster-level resampling, an out-of-sample false-positive audit, and paired synthetic arms to bound the magnitude-vs-timing confound together constitute a standard most probing papers do not meet. The synthetic calibration is genuinely well constructed — matching the within-protein partition and within-protein null to the real setting, and adding AR(1) depth noise specifically because the persistence rule asks about consecutive stages.

**Where I would push back.**

1. **The headline number is a property of one synthetic construction.** "Nineteen stages early" comes from a single gain ramp, a single ρ (0.9), and a weak-signal scenario whose curve range (0.1218–0.1636) sits almost entirely against τ=0.1317. The paper is candid that the rule's failure "depends entirely on depth correlation," but the abstract leads with −19. The real-data audit (0.12–0.17 at p=3) is the more robust evidence and deserves to be the headline; the −19 is an illustration, not an estimate.
2. **The 2.6× gap is metric-dependent to a degree that undercuts the framing.** Under `sp_pli` at threshold 70 — which reassigns 159 of 534 test systems, 30% — the FF–FN gap falls from **0.300 to 0.087** on the primary feature set, and NF–NN from 0.124 to 0.089. A 3.4× shrink in the headline contrast means "FN is 2.6× harder" is really "FN is hardest, by somewhere between 0.087 and 0.300 depending on how you define pocket familiarity." The paper reports both numbers and says the ordering is the finding and the magnitude is not — correct, but the abstract and Figure 2's visual drama both lean on the larger figure.
3. **The composition confound is narrowed, not eliminated.** FN holds covalent ligands at 0.254 vs NF's 0.110, cofactors at 0.115 vs 0.412, and fewer pocket residues per ligand (18.25 vs 22.0, interval excluding zero). The covalency analysis is post hoc ("these comparisons were decided after seeing the composition result, so they are exploratory"), uncorrected for multiplicity, and the within-FN contrast against NF is unresolved at −0.256 [−0.429, +0.242]. The paper's own caution — that AUPRC is bounded below and FN has the lowest baseline, so a negative association has least room to express itself — is the right one, and it means "covalency does not account for the deficit" is a claim the data support only weakly.
4. **The capacity question is designed and then not run.** Appendix B specifies a five-head ladder and a gated readout whose gate value ḡ would itself be a measurement ("a gate that opens under novelty while performance stays flat indicates that pair information is being recruited and is not sufficient"). None of it is executed; the one nonlinear head "overfitted within an epoch" and the ladder comparison is deferred. This matters because the paper's central empirical claim is bounded to *linear* recoverability, and the difference between "the information is not there" and "a linear probe cannot reach it" is exactly what the missing experiment would settle.
5. **ℓ90 carries no intervals at all.** The claim that FN is latest in all three feature sets (22, 27, 26) rests on point estimates over 122 proteins with no bootstrap, while the cell medians it is compared against do have intervals. The paper states this in Section 7, but it means the one statistic that "carries the depth comparison" is the one statistic without uncertainty quantification — an asymmetry that a reader skimming Figure 2 and Table 18 could easily miss.
6. **Median per-protein AUPRC is an unusual aggregate.** Medians across proteins weight a 40-residue chain and a 900-residue chain equally and are not comparable to the pooled or micro AUPRC that zPocket's 0.745 figure uses. The paper never places its numbers on zPocket's scale, which is defensible (different protocol) but leaves the reader unable to judge absolute quality — only relative ordering.
7. **Threshold flatness is a double-edged result.** τ varies by ~0.003 across 48 layers, and the paper notes a single threshold "would have been a fair approximation here." That is honest, but it also means the per-layer order-statistic machinery — one of the three claimed contributions — does no measurable work on this trunk, and its necessity is argued from principle rather than demonstrated.

**Red flags.** Few, and mostly of the disclosure kind: an unretained script for the pocket-similarity scores, a dependency that silently serves the wrong release, and a checkpoint-defect claim verified by the authors but not shown. None of these threaten the qualitative conclusions; all of them would slow an independent replication.

**Significance.** High for the probing-methods community and moderate for structural biology. The onset-rule critique generalises to any layer-wise probing study that reports "emergence at layer k" — which is a large and growing literature — and the empirical false-positive audit makes the critique concrete rather than theoretical. The FN finding is a genuinely counterintuitive specialisation of the structural-over-sequence axis and lands exactly on the practically important case (new or allosteric pockets on well-studied targets), but it is one trunk, one dataset release, 534 proteins, and a magnitude that moves by 3.4× under a defensible change of metric.

## Strengths

- **The failure mode is diagnosed at the right level.** The insight that the error lives *between* layers, and is therefore invisible to per-layer significance testing, is the kind of observation that is obvious in retrospect and was not being checked. The paper makes it vivid with a case where the point estimate is exactly right and the interval is 21 stages wide.
- **Genuine pre-registration, not a retrospective gesture.** The persistence value, α, the primary contrast, the multiplicity family and the ℓ90 fallback were all fixed before the relevant data were seen, and the paper reports the pre-registered contrast as null when it is null.
- **The synthetic calibration is built to be falsifiable.** Matching the within-protein partition and within-protein permutation to the real setting, then adding AR(1) depth noise *because* that is the property the rule must survive, is a design that could have failed and did.
- **Excellent methodological transparency about the dataset.** Appendix C reports two pocket-similarity metrics that were tried and rejected, *why* each failed (near-identity deduplication; domain annotations that collapse the two axes), and how the adopted metric starves the NF cell — the kind of negative detail that is usually cut.
- **Resampling at the receptor-family cluster** rather than the protein, with the effect size of getting it wrong stated ("roughly doubles the interval widths").
- **The Boltz-2 finding is a genuine public service**, reported precisely (128 parameters, one layer norm per attention module, 64 blocks) and connected to why it matters for this class of study specifically.
- **Clear-eyed about the null endpoint.** "A study that reinterprets its endpoint after seeing the result has stopped measuring and started arguing" is the correct standard, and the paper holds itself to it.

## Limitations

- **One trunk, one dataset release.** Boltz-1 with PLINDER 2026-09; whether the FN ordering is a property of Boltz-1, of co-folding trunks, or of this split is explicitly not addressed. AlphaFold 3, Chai-1 and Protenix share the cutoff and would be the obvious replication.
- **The regime difference is located only to "by the end of block 1."** The measurement starts *after* the first Pairformer block, so the paper bounds where the difference can have arisen without identifying it: input representation, alignment and pretraining are not separated. Alignment *depth* is excluded; alignment *diversity* is not tested.
- **Linear readout only.** Everything is "linearly recoverable under a fixed readout," which is narrower than "present in the representation." The capacity ladder and the gated head that would address this are specified in Appendix B and not run.
- **ℓ90 has no uncertainty estimate**, and its ordering for the three non-FN regimes flips between feature sets (NF earliest for `s`, NN for pooled `z`, FF for the concatenation), so only the FN result is interpreted.
- **"Novel" means "below a similarity threshold against the pre-cutoff pool,"** not "absent from pretraining." A post-cutoff release date establishes that a structure was not in the training set, not that nothing resembling it was.
- **The adopted pocket metric has heavy ceiling mass** (median pocket qcov is 97–100), which compresses the top of the range: 21% of targets sit at or above 99. This limits the resolution of the continuous analysis relative to the binary one.
- **The family-support slope is estimated over a narrow, low-support range.** 49% of the 534 test systems have no pre-cutoff relative and the median count is 1, so the negative family slope says nothing about heavily represented families, and support may track difficulty rather than cause it.
- **Composition differences between FN and NF are real and only partly controlled** (covalent 0.254 vs 0.110, cofactor 0.115 vs 0.412, pocket residues 18.25 vs 22.0), and the covalency test that addresses them is exploratory and uncorrected.
- **The per-ligand pocket-similarity generating script was not retained**, and `plinder-core` 0.2.27 silently substitutes release 2024-06 for its query interface — both disclosed, both friction for replication.
- **The Boltz-2 defect claim is not independently demonstrated in the paper** (no listing of the 128 names, no minimal reproduction), and "no public commit matches the checkpoint" is a strong claim resting on the authors' search.

## Relevance

直接命中「LLM Hidden States & Interpretability」：它讲的是逐层探针、表示深度曲线怎么读，以及"信号何时出现"这个结论在层间相关下如何被系统性高估——这套论证对任何做 hidden-state probing / 层级表示分析的 LLM 工作都能平移，方法部分（跨深度固定的置换零轨迹、逐层顺序统计量阈值、持续性预注册）可以直接借用。另外，Boltz-2 checkpoint 缺 128 个参数却静默运行、表示照常可读的那一段，属于"看起来一切正常但底层已经坏了"的典型样本，与「Inference Reliability & SDC Detection」关注的静默损坏检测高度同构。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the −19-stage failure persist for realistic depth-correlation profiles estimated from an actual LLM (e.g. measured autocorrelation of probe scores across layers), rather than an AR(1) at ρ=0.9? What ρ does a residual-stream transformer actually exhibit, and does the required persistence length scale with it?
- The per-layer thresholds varied by only ~0.003 across 48 blocks here. Under what conditions does the null level actually track depth strongly enough that a single global threshold would fail — different probe capacity, different positive rate, different width?
- What does the gated readout's ḡ actually do across regimes? If the gate opens under novelty while AUPRC stays flat, that distinguishes a readout limitation from a representation limitation far more sharply than any accuracy contrast in this paper.
- Since the regime difference is present at the output of block 1, is it already present in the MSA module's output? Re-extracting with single-sequence inputs would show which regimes the alignment was helping — a sharper question than whether alignment depth correlates with anything.
- How much of the FN deficit is covalent-ligand composition? The within-FN contrast (−0.256 [−0.429, +0.242]) is unresolved at n=31; a matched-composition subsample, or a covalent-stratified re-analysis with proper multiplicity control, would settle it.
- Does the ordering survive on a second trunk with the same cutoff (AlphaFold 3, Chai-1, Protenix)? The cutoff is shared, so the split transfers — only the extraction cost stands in the way.
- Is median per-protein AUPRC the right aggregate for a comparison across cells with different chain-length distributions? A length-stratified or micro-averaged re-analysis would test whether the 2.6× is an artifact of the aggregation as much as of the metric.
