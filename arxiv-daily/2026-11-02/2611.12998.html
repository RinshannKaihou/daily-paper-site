Critical edge03

Ribbon strictly contains helix and circle, and the one 0.1 prior knob that decides ribbon-versus-helix is never swept — so “ribbons win” may be partly a statement about the prior.

Critical assessment · nested shape family

Method

One probe result, four claims

Same cells, four separately testable propositions. Each gate can disagree with the last.

  1. 01

    Decode

    SVD on class centroids; kept directions must beat 1,000 label permutations. 89% of candidate cells pass.

  2. 02

    Shape

    Seven candidate shapes, scored against a cubic baseline. Weights are integrated out, so marginal likelihoods compare fit and complexity on one scale; a 5-nat bar plus Tier A stability.

  3. 03

    Own

    Project out pre-registered related concepts, refit with hyperparameters frozen. Owned only if the evidence gap rises (Δ < 0).

  4. 04

    Use

    Ablate and patch the ≤5-dim manifold basis, the full probe, and rank-matched random subspaces. Retention = manifold drop ÷ probe drop.

Strengths

What holds up

  • A portable checklist. Decodable / shaped / owned / causally used — any probing paper can adopt it tomorrow.
  • Ownership is operationalised. Project out pre-registered related concepts, refit the shape, read the sign of Δ. Not another classifier score.
  • Unusual breadth. 4,860 cells, three model families, 108 concepts, five layers, three preprocessing settings.
  • Controls aimed at the right enemies. Answer-direction removal, norm rescaling, rank-matched random subspaces, permutation guards.
Limitations

What to discount

  • One prompt, one token position. Base models only, 6–9B parameters, single-token answers.
  • Ownership covers 46 of 108 concepts. The other 62 — including every joint concept — never get a verdict.
  • The shape family is nested and the prior favours the richer member; only the weights are truly held out.
  • No mechanism, no necessity test. Code, data and per-cell verdicts are unreleased at review.
Open questions

Three worth chasing

  1. Q1

    Sweep the 0.1 cross-feature prior from 0.01 to 1 — does the ribbon still win every strongly supported comparison?

  2. Q2

    Do the 46 all-four cells survive a change of prompt template, or is the final “=” too specific an assembly point?

  3. Q3

    Why does Llama addition retain ~0.01 at layer 28 — residual-norm scale, block layout, or tokenizer?
