01

Mechanism

3 lines, no new hyperparameter

The paper stops treating entropy as the lever. It reads KL-regularized RL as chasing an implicit target π* ∝ πref·exp(R/β), then measures how hard reward gaps are amplified into probability ratios.

Reward budgeting changes what each prompt is allowed to spend. The group's total reward is fixed at one unit and split in proportion to positive gaps, so success gets cheaper as it becomes common.

  1. 01
    Sample

    G = 8 rollouts per prompt; subtract the group minimum r⁻.

  2. 02
    Budget

    Keep positive gaps only; skip all-right and all-wrong groups; normalize the rest to sum to 1.

  3. 03
    Center

    Ai = ci/k − 1/G. Each correct rollout earns 1/k: many successes, small gap; few, large.

Exact at G = 8

For binary rewards the expected unclipped update is ∇fG−1(p), concave with diminishing returns. At k = 1 the advantage pair equals DrGRPO's — the entire effect lives in prompts solved at least twice.

04

Verdict

read the sign, not the magnitude

+ Strengths

  • κeff is quality-aware, step-resolved, and cheap to log from quantities an RL trainer already computes.
  • The binary-reward objective is exact at the G=8 actually used, not merely asymptotic.
  • Three lines, no new hyperparameter, validated as a plug-in on six optimizers rather than one strawman.
  • 58 of 60 benchmark cells non-negative across 12 model × baseline combinations.
  • Unusual candor: scope notes in A.2/A.4, an LLM-usage declaration, captions that refuse to over-read figures.

− Limitations

  • Single seed per configuration; AMC23 has 25 problems, AIME 30 — one item is 2.5–3.3 points.
  • No pre-RL base-model row, so the absolute level of the Llama3.2-3B results is unanchored.
  • Each method stops at its own unreported plateau time, in a paper whose dependent variable is the plateau.
  • The entropy refutation is two additive bonuses at one coefficient (0.01), one model, one seed.
  • Exact theory only for binary rewards; G is fixed at 8, so the concavity horizon is never swept.

Provenance: University of Cambridge + Qwen Business Unit, Alibaba · code released at github.com/Qwen-Applications/RewardBudget · formal declaration of LLM usage in the paper.
