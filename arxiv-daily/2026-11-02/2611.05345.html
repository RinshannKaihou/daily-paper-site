Why this matters↳

Serving-stack numerics quietly steer long-horizon agents. This turns a hardware artifact into a tunable, distribution-safe accuracy lever.

投机解码在分布上无损，在精度上并非中性——而错位的那几个 slot，恰好可以变成可调的精度预算。

▲ Critical edgecaveat

The slot-0 mechanism is inferred, never observed: no histogram shows committing sentences actually landing there, so the causal story is one experiment short of proof.

0.635 held-out accuracy ⇒ the draft misses the decision point ~36% of the time.

Mechanism

How a numerical gradient becomes a control channel

Four moves: measure the within-block error, explain it, then make the draft spend precision where the agent decides.

  1. 01

    Measure. Freeze the prefix in GPU memory, repeat only the verify forward (R = 4). Slot 0 matches single-token decode — 0.473 vs 0.478 nats — then noise climbs +0.0195 nats per slot (≈4%), leaving slot 7 34% dirtier.

  2. 02

    Explain. Slot s attends to KV entries written in the same launch, so it inherits every earlier slot's rounding. Slot 0 reads cache only.

  3. 03

    Exploit. Rejection sampling lets the draft choose where blocks end: Pr[reject] = TV(p, q). Train it with reverse-direction DPO (β = 10) to prefer the target's undesirable side at the committing sentence, so the target re-samples that decision at slot 0 of a fresh block.

  4. 04

    Deploy. Freeze target weights, serving stack, verifier, sampler and harness. Train 449M/749B (0.06%) or 2.15B/753B (0.28%); decoding slows by ≤2%.

Strengths

What holds up

  • +

    A reusable measurement. Fixing the prefix and repeating only the forward pass removes the prefill confound that would swamp a 0.02-nat/slot effect.

  • +

    Two architectures, one slope. Block-diffusion (W=8) and MoE (W=6) drafts reproduce the same ≈4% per-slot growth.

  • +

    Controls against the thesis. Reversing the preference costs 4.9 points; 27 unrelated-preference runs sit flat at p = 0.83.

  • +

    Distribution-safe by construction. Rejection sampling is untouched, so the target cannot emit outside its own distribution.

  • +

    Deployment realism. QAT on the exported draft means the function evaluated is the function that ships.

Limitations

What the evidence cannot carry

  • −

    The mechanism is unobserved. No histogram places committing-sentence tokens at slot 0, and 0.635 pairwise accuracy means the draft misses ~36% of the time.

  • −

    Small n. Per-run SE ≈ 4.5 points on 113 tasks; +4.0 is inside noise, and +5.8 carries no interval at all.

  • −

    Provenance undisclosed. The fork-mining benchmark is never named, so DeepSWE may be partly in-distribution; only NL2Repo is clean.

  • −

    No “precision everywhere” baseline. W=1 decoding, or bf16/FP16 serving, would price the 2% speed cost.

  • −

    The preference is found by trial. Frames move GLM and do nothing for DeepSeek; the search cost is the real budget.

Open questions

Five experiments the paper leaves on the table

  1. Where do decision tokens land? A slot-index histogram, base vs adversarial, would make or break the mechanism story.

  2. What does W=1 cost? If no speculation recovers most of +7.0 at 30% throughput loss, this is a curve, not a free lunch.

  3. Is fork mining disjoint from DeepSWE? One sentence naming the source benchmark would settle the contamination worry.

  4. Is the gain monotone in rejection reliability? Sweep held-out accuracy 0.55 → 0.85 and plot pass rate.

  5. Does the slope survive other verifiers? Medusa tree attention and EAGLE drafting may not share the ≈4%/slot gradient.
