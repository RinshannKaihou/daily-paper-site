02 · Method / 方法

Two signals, a running mean, and a rule that refuses to act on noise

  1. 01

    Measure

    GE is the normalized Shannon entropy of the top-10 logits. LA is the relative L2 change a layer makes to the residual stream.

  2. 02

    Combine

    M = λ·LA + (1−λ)·GE, with λ = 1 in prefill (no tokens yet) and 0.7 in decode, where entropy lags one step.

  3. 03

    Decide

    Track M’s running µ and σ. Move one level only on sustained trends over two layers: four quiet layers to drop, two active ones to raise. Prefill pins every layer up to the µ + 3σ spike at 6 bits.

  4. 04

    Serve

    One extended-K GEMM per layer; a mixed batch ORs its activation masks, so the whole batch runs a single MMA.

Levels are {2, 4, 6}. 2-bit is weights-only, so realized tuples are W2A4-KV4, W4A4-KV4, W6A6-KV6.

03 · Mechanism / 机制

Refine by offset, then hide it inside one GEMM

NVFP4 base SE₁E₀M 4 b / element · E4M3 scale per 16
Refine mask 4 b / 16 elements · one bit per 4-element subgroup
Offset d −2−10+1 2 b signed · fixed step 2⁻³ on masked subgroups
AutoFP2 −2−10+1+2 e₁e₀ remapped to a quinary alphabet

[Wq | Wqr] · [Aq | Aqr]

One tcgen05.mma, reduction length ≤ 2K.

  • Disjoint N:4 masks (NW + NA = 4) make the second-order cross term vanish.
  • 4δ lands exactly on the E2M1 grid; the factor 4 folds into sW/4.

4 + 0.5 + 2ρ ≈ 4.9 bits/weight at ρ = 0.2 — versus 13.5 for three separately stored copies.

Strengths / 优势

What earns the claim

  • The extended-K GEMM turns a format that should be a memory-layout nightmare into one standard MMA on existing Tensor Cores.
  • It explains why appended mantissa bits fail — round-to-nearest can only refine upward — which is what earns the additive-offset design.
  • Table 4 separates policy, format and calibration, includes a 13.5-bit upper bound, and collapses the policy on an integer format.
  • Real systems work, not simulation only: vLLM 0.15 plugin, FlashInfer attention, two page types on one allocator, 4× GB300.
  • The under-provisioning metric indicts static calibration without depending on any benchmark.

Limitations / 局限

What the evidence will not carry

  • AutoFP2 saves no memory. It is derived on demand, never stored; on GPUs it is emulated as NVFP4.
  • No bandwidth win at all: 4.9 bits versus 4.5 for NVFP4. The real claim is accuracy per bit against FP6g.
  • Accelerator numbers are modeled, not silicon — DRAM assumed at 1 TB/s, with no sensitivity analysis.
  • One run per cell, no seeds; 0.5–1 pt gaps are routine noise at temperature 0.5.
  • No ablation isolates KV-cache adaptation, though the “joint” claim leans on it.

04 · Open questions / 待解

Left on the table

  1. What is accuracy at ρ = 0 — pure AutoFP6 at 4.5 bits, exactly NVFP4’s footprint?
  2. Does the policy still pay off with the KV cache pinned at 8 bits?
  3. How sensitive are the 1.6–2.3× and 7–23% numbers to the assumed 1 TB/s DRAM?
  4. Could GE and LA double as bit-flip or bad-kernel detectors?
  5. At batch 128+ with maximally heterogeneous requests, does ∪MA refine everything?
