> **一句话总结：** 作者提出"激活碎片化（activation fragmentation）"：把每个概念查询的隐状态减去其匿名化改写（anon-vector）后，若模型真"知道"该概念，这批差向量会塌缩成紧致低熵簇，若只是"不知道"则散成高方差方向；在 10 个指令微调模型（Qwen/Gemma/LLaMA，2B–14B）上，AUSScos² 把 Harry Potter（已知）与 TOFU 虚构作者（未知，全部模型 QA=0.00）分开的效应量达 Cohen's |d|=21.51（p_adj=0.002，10/10 模型方向一致），而直接用原始隐状态的 Reg S2 基线只有 |d|=0.92——匿名化相减把信噪比抬了约 23 倍；在下游，碎片化几何与 ICL 恢复率相关 ρ=−0.51，但与 Steering 的相关却是 +0.48（符号相反）。

> **TL;DR:** A two-forward-pass, generation-free probe that subtracts the mean hidden state of identity-stripped paraphrases from a concept query's hidden state, then measures the population geometry of the resulting "anon-vectors" (Rényi-2 Gram entropy, batch-mean L2/cosine/Jaccard dispersion). On 10 instruction-tuned LLMs (Qwen-2.5/14B, Gemma-2B/7B, LLaMA-2/3, 2B–14B), the best metric AUSScos² separates known Harry Potter questions from TOFU's fictitious authors at Cohen's |d| = 21.51 (gap 0.686, BH-adjusted p = 0.002, 10/10 models) versus |d| = 0.92 for the raw-activation Gram-entropy baseline (Reg S2) — a ~23× effect-size gain from the anonymisation step alone, and a leave-one-model-out logistic classifier on the 8-metric peak-layer vector reaches 100% accuracy / AUC 1.00 (9/9 models). As a post-unlearning robustness proxy the story is much weaker and sign-inconsistent: across 54 unlearning runs (5 algorithms × 3 concepts × 3 models) AUSSL2 predicts ICL recovery at ρ = −0.51 (q<0.01) while AnonDiff S2 predicts activation-steering recovery at ρ = +0.48 — opposite signs — and nothing predicts membership-inference attack success. Read this for the RQ1 detection result and the honest theory appendix; discount the "attack-free robustness proxy" headline.

## 核心贡献

- **提出激活碎片化（activation fragmentation）框架**：用 anon-vector（概念查询隐状态 − 匿名改写隐状态）的**群体几何**而非单点相似度来判定"知不知道"，只需 2 次前向、不生成任何 token、不需要 unlearn 前后的双 checkpoint。这是本文相对 Seyitoğlu et al. (2024)（用 anon-vector 做 steering 攻击）与 Skean et al. (2025)（对原始激活算 Gram 熵）的真正增量。
- **给出理论闭环**：Theorem 1 证明高 Rényi-2 Gram 熵强制几何碎片化（$D^2_{L_2} \ge 2 - 2/\mathrm{EffRank}(G)$），Theorem 2 在非负余弦假设下给出反向上界，Proposition 1 把 S₂ 与有效秩夹在 2 倍以内，Lemma 1 给出 $S_2$ 与平均平方余弦的**精确恒等式**（不是不等式）。作者还诚实地指出低 S₂ 单独不足以保证同向（反平行向量也满足 S₂=0），因此必须加非负性假设。
- **RQ1 跨 10 模型 3 家族的稳健分离**：9 个碎片化指标全部在 10/10 模型上方向正确并通过 BH 校正；AUSScos² 最强（|d|=21.51），AUSSL2 次之（|d|=18.91）。用 4 个域（HP-forget / HP-retain / Star Wars / TOFU）扩展，24 个 BH 检验全部显著（q ∈ [0.0063, 0.0260]）。
- **提出 AUSS 三个批量均值指标**：AUSSL2（批量均值方向的平均成对平方 L2）、AUSScos²（1 − 平均平方余弦）、AUSSJac（top-32 坐标的 Jaccard 距离），均严格有界（[0,2] / [0,1] / [0,1]），便于跨模型比较。
- **RQ2 把几何量当作 unlearning 鲁棒性的"免攻击"代理**，并**如实报告了失败**：只有 ICL 在聚合层面稳健（ρ=−0.51），MIA 完全没有信号，GCG 只在全局校正下勉强存活（q_global=0.046），且不同攻击的相关符号相反。
- **一个可直接部署的工程结论**：最优层稳定落在 0.80–0.88 相对深度；固定 0.8× 深度、不做任何逐模型搜索，就在 n=9 上给出 8/8 指标方向正确且显著，留一模型迁移达到 72/72（与 oracle 逐模型搜索完全相同）。

## Key Contributions

- **A population-geometry metric family for parametric knowledge.** Prior anon-vector work (Seyitoğlu et al., 2024) used a single difference vector as a steering direction; prior Gram-entropy work (Skean et al., 2025) applied S₂ to raw activations. This paper applies S₂ and batch-mean dispersion to the *set* of anon-vectors, which is the actual novelty — the paper is explicit that neither component is new in isolation.
- **Theory that closes the loop from spectral concentration to spatial dispersion.** Theorem 1 (high entropy ⇒ fragmentation), Theorem 2 (low entropy + non-negative cosines ⇒ clustering), Proposition 1 (S₂ and effective rank agree within a factor of two on the log scale), and Lemma 1 (an exact algebraic identity $S_2 = -\log_2\frac{1+(n-1)\overline{\cos^2}}{n}$). The remark that the bound is tight at both extremes (all-identical vectors and mutually orthogonal vectors) is correct and verified in the appendix.
- **A clean known/unknown ground truth construction.** Positive class = 96 MUSE-Books Harry Potter QA pairs; negative class = 100 TOFU pairs about GPT-4-invented authors that provably cannot be in any pre-training corpus, with all 10 models scoring 0.00 exact-match on TOFU. This sidesteps the "no true oracle for parametric knowledge" problem the authors themselves articulate.
- **Scale-artifact control.** Because Cohen's |d| is inflated by bounded metric ranges (the authors say so explicitly), they add a leave-one-model-out logistic-regression/MLP classifier on the standardised 8-metric feature vector: 100% accuracy, AUC 1.00, 9/9 models fully correct. This is the right control and it is rarely done in this literature.
- **A 4-attack × 5-algorithm × 3-concept unlearning grid (54 conditions)** with four mechanistically distinct recovery channels (activation steering, 5-shot ICL, GCG suffix, likelihood-ratio MIA), plus a layer-resolved sweep over all transformer layers.
- **A negative result reported as a negative result**: MIA is not predictable by any metric; the sign of the correlation flips between attack families; GCG survives global but not per-column BH correction. Section 7 admits the mechanistic explanation for the sign flip is inferred from correlations alone.

## Methodology

**Anon-vector construction.** For each concept query $q_i$ and layer $\ell$, take mean-pooled hidden state $h_\ell(q_i)$ over the sequence (attention-mask-weighted). Build $K$ anonymised variants $a_i^{(k)}$ by deterministic string substitution over a hand-built entity table (e.g. *Harry Potter → Alex Ryden*, *Hogwarts → Crystal Academy*, *Quidditch → Broomball*), then

$$\Delta v_i(\ell) = h_\ell(q_i) - \frac{1}{K}\sum_{k=1}^{K} h_\ell(a_i^{(k)}).$$

The mean over anonymised variants cancels the shared syntactic/lexical component of the prompt, leaving the concept-specific residual. $K=1$ for RQ1 and for RQ2 forget pairs; $K=5$ for the steering-vector computation.

**Metric family (9 metrics).** Dispersion metrics (lower = knows): `AnonDiff S2` = Rényi-2 Gram entropy of the $\Delta V$ matrix; `NormAnonDiff S2` on row-normalised $\Delta V$; `AUSS_L2`, `AUSS_cos²`, `AUSS_Jac` over $B=10$ batch-mean unit directions $\hat d_b$. Coherence metrics (higher = knows): centroid norm $\|\Delta \hat v\|$, mean pairwise `AnonDiff cos`, mean `AnonDiff L̄2`. Raw-activation baselines `Reg S2`, `Reg cos`, `Reg L̄2` skip the subtraction entirely and serve as the ablation that isolates the contribution of anonymisation.

**RQ1 protocol.** 10 instruction-tuned models: Qwen-7B-Chat, Qwen2.5-7B, Qwen-14B-Chat, Qwen2.5-14B, Gemma-2B, Gemma-7B, LLaMA-2-7B, LLaMA-2-13B, LLaMA-3-8B, LLaMA-3.1-8B. $N=96$ HP pairs (filtered from MUSE-Books `knowmem/forget_qa`, 300 → 96 with non-empty answers) vs $N=100$ TOFU pairs (seed 42, full-retain split). $B=10$ batches. Statistics: two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank across models, Benjamini–Hochberg at α=0.05, bootstrap 95% CIs on the gap. Peak layer per metric chosen as $\arg\max_\ell |\bar m^{HP}_\ell - \bar m^{TOFU}_\ell|$ across all 10 models simultaneously.

**RQ2 protocol.** 3 models (LLaMA-2-7B-chat, LLaMA-3-8B-Instruct, Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct) × 6 methods (GA, DPO, NPO, NPO+KL, RMU, WHP, plus unmodified Base) × 3 concepts (Harry Potter, Star Wars, William Shakespeare) = 54 conditions from ConceptVectors. Forget sets 50–80 Wikipedia-derived pairs; retain sets ~50. A run is "verified" if post-unlearn forget ROUGE-L < 0.10 and retain ROUGE-L ≥ 0.80× baseline. Four attacks: Activation Steering (Seyitoğlu et al.; $c=2.0$, injected at first decode step, ROC-AUC over 500 stochastic samples × 25 pairs), 5-shot ICL (ROUGE-L ≥ 0.30 soft match), GCG via nanogcg (500 steps, 20-token suffix, 5 shortest-answer pairs), and likelihood-ratio MIA (50 forget / 50 retain, AUC).

**Layer-resolved sweep.** Hidden states extracted at all $L+1$ layers in one forward pass; Spearman ρ computed for every (metric, layer, attack) cell — 4,224 tests in total, BH-corrected both within-attack and globally.

**Compute.** 2× NVIDIA L40S (48 GB each), bfloat16, `device_map="auto"`; ~31 min per model for RQ1 extraction, 10–20 min per RQ2 run, ~120–150 GPU-hours total.

## Results & Highlights

- **Anonymisation is what buys the signal.** On the HP-vs-TOFU contrast, AUSScos² reaches Cohen's |d| = 21.51 with a gap of 0.686 [95% CI 0.667, 0.705] and BH-adjusted p = 0.002, while the raw-activation Gram-entropy baseline Reg S2 (no anon-vector subtraction, adapted from Skean et al. 2025) manages only |d| = 0.92, gap 0.117 [0.054, 0.200] — a 23× effect-size difference on the same 10 models and the same layers. AUSSL2 is second at |d| = 18.91 (gap 1.203).
- **All 9 metrics work, but the spread is enormous.** Entropy-family concept-differential metrics land at |d| = 4.21 (NormAnonDiff S2) and 3.50 (AnonDiff S2); coherence metrics are much weaker — centroid norm 1.69, AnonDiff cos 1.29, AnonDiff L̄2 1.21 — and Reg cos is barely above the raw baseline at 1.25. Every one of the 9 is significant at p_adj = 0.002 except AnonDiff cos (0.010), and all 10/10 models point the right way for the top metrics.
- **The separation is not a scale artefact.** A leave-one-model-out logistic regression on the standardised 8-dimensional peak-layer feature vector achieves 100% accuracy, AUC = 1.00, 18/18 correct, 9/9 models fully correct; a small MLP (8 hidden units) gets 94.4%, 17/18. On the four-way domain extension (HP-forget, HP-retain, Star Wars, all vs TOFU) all 24 BH-corrected comparisons are significant with q ∈ [0.0063, 0.0260], and HP-*retain* effect sizes are larger than HP-forget (up to d = 6.20 for Batch_SecondMoment_TopEig vs d = 2.57 for HP-forget).
- **Layer choice is nearly free.** Fixing the extraction layer at 0.8× model depth with no per-model search still gives 8/8 metrics correct-direction and significant at n = 9 (e.g. $\|\Delta\hat v\|$ d = 3.30, p = 0.0039; Batch_Cov_TopEig d = 6.31, p = 0.0039 at r = 0.8); leave-one-model-out transfer of the median peak depth hits 72/72 (model, metric) pairs, identical to the oracle per-model search. Relative peak depth clusters in 0.79–0.98 across all 9 models.
- **Post-unlearning, the geometry predicts ICL — and predicts other attacks with the opposite sign.** AUSSL2 vs ICL ρ = −0.51 (BH-significant), AUSScos² ρ = −0.50, centroid norm ρ = +0.51; but AnonDiff S2 vs Steering is ρ = +0.48 and Reg S2 vs GCG is ρ = +0.45, i.e. *more* fragmentation ⇒ *more* recovery for those two channels. MIA has no BH-significant predictor at all (best uncorrected |ρ| ≈ 0.29).
- **Layer-resolved correlations are much stronger than aggregate ones, but on a smaller sample.** GCG peaks at AUSS_Jac L29 with ρ = −0.853 (p = 0.0008, n = 11, q_global = 0.046); ICL peaks at Batch_Cov_TopEig L28 with ρ = −0.886 (p < 0.0001, n = 15, q_global = 0.009). The two peaks are one layer apart. Across 4,224 (layer, metric, attack) tests, only these two attacks survive global correction; MIA and Steering stay at q_global ≥ 0.22.
- **Suppression is not deletion, quantified.** GA on LLaMA-3-8B drives forget ROUGE-L to 0.000 while AUSSL2 barely moves (0.594 Base → 0.534) and $\|\Delta\hat v\|$ actually rises (0.856 → 0.872). GA is also the only method that pulls steering AUC to chance (0.500) on all three models, whereas DPO/NPO+KL/RMU leave it at 0.62–0.95 — e.g. NPO+KL on Qwen2.5-7B gives Steer-AUC 0.934 and MIA 0.482.
- **Head-to-head vs the published representation-level baseline (Xu et al. 2025/2026).** Of 44 BH-corrected tests, 11 survive and 8 of those are AUSS metrics. On ICL, AUSScos² gives ρ = −0.572 (q = 0.0001) vs Xu's best PCA-similarity ρ = +0.381 (q = 0.0212); on Steering, AUSS_Jac ρ = −0.452 (q = 0.0042) vs Xu's CKA ρ = +0.428 (q = 0.0076). But on GCG Xu's CKA wins (ρ = +0.374, q = 0.0212) against AUSS's best ρ = −0.315 (q = 0.068, not significant), and on MIA neither method survives.

## Authors & Credibility

Adar Peleg and Dvir Alsheich (equal contribution), Tomer Ashuach, and Avi Mendelson, all at the Technion – Israel Institute of Technology. Ashuach has a directly relevant prior paper (CRISP: Persistent Concept Unlearning via Sparse Autoencoders, arXiv:2508.13650, 2026) and a background with Belinkov's group, so the unlearning framing is not opportunistic. The reference implementation is advertised at `github.com/AdarPeleg/geometry-of-ignorance` in the abstract, though Appendix B says "code and data will be released upon acceptance" — a small inconsistency worth noting.

The paper reads like a competent workshop-to-conference submission: heavy appendix (mathematical proofs, three anonymisation procedures with named substitution tables, exact hyperparameters, per-run tables for all 54 conditions), and an unusual amount of self-criticism (they explicitly label $\|\Delta\hat v\|$ as the "exact algebraic dual" of AUSSL2 and therefore "not an independent confirmation", and they flag that the layer-resolved sweep is exploratory rather than a multiplicity-corrected hypothesis test). Compute is modest and fully disclosed (~120–150 GPU-hours on 2× L40S). No evidence of cherry-picking in the reported tables — the full 9×4 metric-attack grid including non-significant cells is printed.

## Critical Assessment

**Genuine strengths.** The theory is real theory, not decoration: Lemma 1's exact identity $S_2 = -\log_2[(1+(n-1)\overline{\cos^2})/n]$ is a correct and useful result, and the authors correctly note that low S₂ alone does not imply alignment (two anti-parallel vectors give S₂ = 0 but $D^2_{L_2} = 4$), which is precisely why Theorem 2 needs the non-negativity assumption. Most papers in this genre would have skipped that caveat. The scale-artefact control (LOMO classifier at AUC 1.00) is the single most convincing piece of evidence in the paper, and the 0.8× relative-depth result — validated by 72/72 leave-one-model-out transfer — is a genuinely useful engineering finding.

**Problem 1: the sign flip guts the headline.** The abstract sells "a single-phase, attack-free proxy for unlearning robustness". But Table 9 shows AUSSL2 → ICL at ρ = −0.51 while AnonDiff S2 → Steering at ρ = +0.48 and Reg S2 → GCG at ρ = +0.45. A proxy whose direction depends on which attack you are worried about is not a proxy; it is three different, individually weak, partially contradictory signals. The Section 5 explanation ("ICL needs one coherent direction; GCG/Steering can exploit many dispersed directions") is plausible and even elegant, but Section 7 concedes it is "inferred from the correlational pattern alone" with no inspection of generations or reasoning traces. That is the paper's central mechanistic claim and it is unvalidated.

**Problem 2: ρ ≈ 0.5 is too weak for the stated use case.** The practical pitch is "if fragmentation remains intact, that is a strong signal that adversarial recovery will succeed without running expensive attacks". At ρ = 0.5 on n = 44–45 runs, a per-run screening decision has very little discriminative power; the paper never reports a threshold, an operating point, precision/recall, or a calibration curve. A screening tool needs a decision rule, not a Spearman coefficient.

**Problem 3: non-independence of the 54 runs.** The correlation pool is 3 models × 3 concepts × 6 methods, treated as n = 44–45 independent observations. Runs sharing a model or a concept are obviously clustered — the same Qwen2.5-7B checkpoint contributes 18 runs, the same Harry Potter forget set appears 18 times. No mixed-effects model, no cluster-robust standard error, no per-model or per-concept breakdown of ρ is reported. With 3 models and 3 concepts, the effective sample size is closer to 9 than to 45, and a ρ of 0.5 could easily be driven by between-model differences in both fragmentation and attack vulnerability.

**Problem 4: what does "known" mean when the model can't answer?** HP QA accuracy ranges from 0.042 (Gemma-2B) to 0.323 (Qwen2.5-14B) in Table 5 — models get 4–32% of Harry Potter questions right, i.e. they mostly *cannot* answer, yet the metric declares them knowledgeable. The authors would say this is exactly the point (suppression). But there is no frequency-matched control: no unknown-but-name-frequent entity, no real-person control who is obscure, no test of whether the metric is tracking "this entity string appeared often in pre-training" rather than "this model can retrieve facts about it". That is the single most important missing ablation, and it is the difference between "detects knowledge" and "detects entity familiarity". Notably, the Star Wars and HP-retain extensions do not address this — they are more of the same high-frequency-fictional-entity class.

**Problem 5: layer selection is partly circular and the reported ℓ\* is odd.** In RQ1 the peak layer is chosen per metric by maximising the HP–TOFU gap — supervised selection on the evaluation contrast, which inflates |d| (the authors admit this and report the label-free 0.8× version, which is good, but the headline |d| = 21.51 is the inflated one). In RQ2 the layer is chosen as $\arg\max_\ell \mathrm{AUSS}_{L2}$ on the post-unlearning checkpoint and then used for *all* metrics, including the entropy metrics whose predictive validity is being assessed — so AUSSL2's own layer choice is privileged. Separately, Table 5's caption states a single "peak layer ℓ\* = 18" for all ten models, which is the final layer of Gemma-2B but mid-network for a 14B model; averaging the argmax across models of different depth is geometrically incoherent, and it sits awkwardly with the later finding that relative depth (0.8×) is what actually matters.

**Problem 6: the aggregate ICL correlation is contradicted by the aggregate ICL data.** Figure 2 shows ICL Δ near zero across all methods and models — the caption says so explicitly ("ICL Δ is near zero across all methods and models; the geometric correlation with ICL recoverability emerges from the layer-resolved analysis, not from this aggregate view"). Yet Table 2 reports ρ = −0.51 for AUSSL2 vs ICL at n = 44–45. If ICL recovery is essentially flat, a ρ of −0.5 must be riding on small absolute differences; the effect may be statistically detectable and practically meaningless.

**Problem 7: anonymisation is hand-built string substitution, partly LLM-generated.** The 83-entry HP table was "created using Seyitoğlu et al. (2024) and expanded with Claude-Opus-4.6", and 11 of 96 HP pairs and 17 of 100 TOFU pairs contain no matchable entity and are therefore *unchanged* by anonymisation. For those pairs the anon-vector is a near-zero vector by construction — which mechanically pushes the TOFU class toward the "fragmented/noise" regime. The paper notes this ("the anon-vector should carry minimal concept-specific signal for unknown concepts") but does not report the metrics with those pairs removed. A 17% null-mass contamination of the negative class deserves a sensitivity check.

**Problem 8: the GCG result is fragile and the benchmark comparison is selective in presentation.** GCG's best cell has q_attack = 0.057 (not significant within its own column) and only q_global = 0.046, and it rests on n = 11 runs with complete layer-resolved data out of 44–45. Meanwhile the abstract's "correlates with adversarial recovery across four attack types" is not supported: MIA has no significant predictor anywhere, and the Xu et al. head-to-head has AUSS losing on GCG outright. The honest summary is "predicts ICL well, Steering partially, GCG marginally, MIA not at all."

**Minor.** Section 4.1 says models "ranging from 7B to 14B parameters" while the Discussion says "2B to 14B" and Table 5 includes Gemma-2B — an internal inconsistency. There is no conclusion section. The abstract's claim of "ten instruction-tuned LLMs from three model families" is accurate, but RQ2 drops to 3 models, all 7–8B, so the cross-family robustness claim does not transfer to the robustness-proxy half of the paper. RQ1 also never benchmarks against the obvious competing intrinsic probe (Gottesman & Geva, 2024), only against raw-activation Gram entropy — a baseline the authors designed to be weak.

## Strengths

- The theoretical section is correct, tight, and actually used: Lemma 1's exact identity, Proposition 1's factor-of-two sandwich, and the explicit acknowledgement that low S₂ alone cannot rule out anti-parallel collapse.
- The RQ1 result is unusually clean for interpretability work: 10 models, 3 families, 9 metrics, 10/10 direction consistency, BH correction, bootstrap CIs, plus a leave-one-model-out classifier that rules out the scale artefact.
- The anonymisation ablation is the paper's best experiment — Reg S2 at |d| = 0.92 vs AUSScos² at |d| = 21.51 isolates the contribution of the concept-differential step rather than claiming the whole pipeline as novel.
- The 0.8× relative-depth heuristic is immediately usable and validated by 72/72 LOMO transfer against an oracle search; this alone makes the paper worth reading for practitioners.
- Reporting discipline: the full 9-metric × 4-attack grid including all non-significant cells is printed (Table 9), leave-one-run-out stability is reported per attack (Table 12), and the $\|\Delta\hat v\|$ / AUSSL2 algebraic-duality footnote pre-empts a criticism most authors would have let slide.
- Honest negative results: MIA is declared unpredictable, GCG is flagged as not surviving per-column correction, and the Section 5 mechanism is labelled as inference rather than measurement.
- Cheap: two forward passes per query, no generation, no reference model, no pre-unlearning checkpoint — usable on API-restricted audits where generation is prohibited.

## Limitations

- **Sign inconsistency is unresolved.** The correlation between fragmentation and recoverability flips sign across attacks (−0.51 for ICL, +0.48 for Steering, +0.45 for GCG), and the mechanistic explanation is explicitly unvalidated against actual generations or chain-of-thought traces.
- **Weak predictive power for a screening tool.** ρ ≈ 0.5 with no operating point, no precision/recall, no calibration; the "skip the expensive attacks" use case is asserted rather than demonstrated.
- **Non-independent correlation pool.** 54 runs from 3 models × 3 concepts × 6 methods are treated as n = 44–45; no clustering correction, no mixed-effects model, no per-model ρ breakdown.
- **No frequency-matched control for the "known" class.** HP QA accuracy is only 4–32%, and no obscure-but-name-frequent entity is tested, so "detects parametric knowledge" and "detects entity familiarity" remain confounded.
- **Layer selection is supervised in RQ1** (argmax of the HP–TOFU gap) and AUSSL2-privileged in RQ2; the reported ℓ\* = 18 shared across models of 18–40 layers is geometrically incoherent.
- **Anonymisation is brittle.** Deterministic string substitution with hand-curated tables (partly LLM-expanded), 17/100 TOFU and 11/96 HP pairs left unmodified, and no tokenization-matched or LLM-paraphrase control. Authors list this first in Section 7.
- **`B ≥ 2` requirement.** The batch-mean construction is undefined for single-sample unlearning requests — a real limitation for the GDPR-style use case the framing implies.
- **Narrow concept coverage.** English-language fictional/factual entities only (Harry Potter, Star Wars, Shakespeare, TOFU authors); no graded knowledge levels, no technical/scientific domains, no multilingual test.
- **RQ2 uses only 3 models, all 7–8B**, so the "architecture-agnostic" claim established in RQ1 is not carried over to the robustness-proxy half.
- **Code not yet released** despite the abstract pointing to a GitHub URL; Appendix B says release is conditional on acceptance.
- No conclusion section, and the internal 7B–14B vs 2B–14B inconsistency in the reported model range.

## Relevance

这篇正好落在"机制可解释性 × unlearning 评测"的交叉点上：如果你关心怎么在不生成 token、不跑攻击的前提下判断一个模型是"真删了"还是"只是压住了"，RQ1 的 anon-vector 群体几何（以及 0.8× 深度这个免调参结论）是可直接复用的工具；但 RQ2 的"鲁棒性代理"要打折看，符号不一致 + ρ≈0.5 + 54 个 run 非独立，离"能替代攻击评测"还有相当距离。对做表征级审计或设计 unlearning benchmark 的工作，这篇的失败模式（MIA 完全不可预测、频率混杂未控制）比它的成功结论更有参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

1. Does the metric survive a **frequency-matched control**? Take obscure real entities that appear rarely in pre-training but whose facts the model also cannot answer — if AUSS separates them from HP as strongly as it separates HP from TOFU, the metric is measuring entity familiarity, not knowledge.
2. What happens if the 17 unmodified TOFU pairs and 11 unmodified HP pairs are dropped? Does |d| = 21.51 shrink materially, or is the effect carried by the 83% that actually get substituted?
3. Can the sign flip be predicted a priori from an attack's mechanism, or is it post hoc? A test: construct an attack that is intermediate between "needs one coherent direction" (ICL) and "exploits many directions" (steering) and see whether ρ crosses zero.
4. What is the actual decision-theoretic value of the proxy? Report a ROC curve for "will this unlearned checkpoint be recoverable by ICL at ASR > X" using fragmentation as the score, with a held-out model.
