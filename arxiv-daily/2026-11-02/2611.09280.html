02 / Method

A corpus study, not a benchmark

Dec 2015 – Jul 2026
  1. 9,099 raw GitHub issues · 7 repositories · open and closed
  2. 3,181 closed, concrete failures after title-keyword and negative-label screening
  3. 300 repo-stratified sample · two coders · κ = 0.87

Seven libraries span the stack: attention (FlashAttention, FlashInfer, xFormers), GEMM and low precision (CUTLASS, DeepGEMM), codegen (Triton), and quantization plus packaging (bitsandbytes). Serving engines such as vLLM and SGLang are deliberately excluded.

  • Sample. Quota proportional to each repository's candidate pool; excluded reports replaced by the nearest same-repo issue until 300 remained.
  • Labels. One primary symptom, one root cause, and the fix per issue; disagreements reconciled by discussion rather than by vote.
  • Framing. Three RQs — symptoms, causes and fixes, testing practice — each closed with a Finding + Implication pair.
  • Evidence. No model or benchmark suite: distributional counts plus measurements quoted from issue threads.
03 / Balance

What holds up, what does not

read the percentages with care

Strengths

  • Actionable by construction. Every finding closes with an implication a maintainer can execute, not just a distribution.
  • Inspectable cases. Six annotated diffs — block-sparse state loss, int16 widening, peer-CTA liveness — make each mechanism checkable.
  • A sharper test oracle. Names differential testing's reference-side failure: an FP16 oracle can itself be the wrong answer.
  • Honest negative result. Only 8/300 numerical cases, framed as a reporting fact rather than a prevalence claim.

Limitations

  • Sampling. The title-keyword screen correlates with the outcome; nearest-timestamp replacement clusters regression bursts. Neither is flagged.
  • Composition. 52% of the corpus is bitsandbytes + Triton; DeepGEMM contributes 4 issues yet receives two-decimal ratios.
  • Missing analyses. No symptom × root-cause matrix, no decade-long trend, no fix size, time-to-fix, or regression data.
  • One label per issue. Multi-cause failures are forced into a single bucket; release of the labeled dataset is unstated.

Open questions

the useful follow-ups

03Is 2.67% numerical a prevalence fact or a reporting fact? The tooling gap that hides these bugs also suppresses the reports.
