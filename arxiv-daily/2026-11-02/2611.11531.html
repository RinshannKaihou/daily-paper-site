Key finding · 核心发现

Method 方法

One pass · eight hook points

block input → block output Act = vision pick · LN2 = language pick
  • A 2-D grid, not a depth scan. 15 vision models × 18 datasets = 270 pairs; 9 LLMs × 24 MTEB classification tasks = 216 pairs. About 144k logistic-regression probes.
  • Extract once. Hooks capture eight tensors per pre-norm block in a single forward pass; a model-agnostic adapter normalizes gated FFNs (SwiGLU) so Act means the same thing everywhere.
  • Probe. Mean-pool content tokens only (CLS, registers, BOS/EOS and PAD masked), standardize, fit ℓ2 logistic regression (L-BFGS, C = 1.0, CuML). No augmentation.
  • Measure. g(ℓ,m) = acc(ℓ,m) − acc(100, RC2). The three oracle gains — layer, module, joint — are test-set upper bounds, not deployable recipes.
  • Controls. RC2@60 for depth, FC1@100 at matched width h = 4d, a ViT-B fine-tuning study, and CIFAR-10-C at severities 1–5.

Strengths 优势

What earns trust
  • 144k probes, one protocol. 24 models, 42 datasets and four pretraining paradigms; per-model, per-dataset appendix tables make every claim auditable.
  • A falsifiable decomposition. Layer / module / joint gains turn “look inside blocks” into three separately estimable numbers and a checkable inequality.
  • Regressions are not buried. ViT-B’s −6.6 pp, DINOv3-S’s −1.0 pp and three failing LLM tasks all appear in the main or appendix tables.
  • The right control. FC1@100 has Act’s width but underperforms even the baseline, killing the “wider features are easier” explanation.
  • Cheap, usable payoff. LN2@60 is a one-line change to a HuggingFace extraction script and brings Gemma3-4B to GritLM-7B parity.

Limitations 局限

Where the evidence stops
  • Classification only. No retrieval, STS, reranking, clustering or attentive probes — exactly where embedding quality is usually judged.
  • Ceilings, not recipes. Oracle gains need test-set access; absolute effects are ~1 pp on strong vision models and ~2.4 pp on a weak 8–16-shot probe.
  • Selected on the test data. Act@100 and LN2@60 are both chosen from the same grid they are evaluated on, with no held-out model or dataset.
  • Partly circular correlation. Gain and “transfer difficulty” share the −acc(baseline) term; near-chance Infograph and RxRx1 are equally weighted in the 18-dataset average.
  • No mechanism, narrow depth rule. Why the nonlinearity helps is unexplained, and 60% is architecture-dependent (Qwen3 ~70%, Llama3/Gemma3 ~50%) with no MoE evidence.
  • Cheap readout, costly cache. Act lives in the h ≈ 4d FFN space, so caching it costs ~4× the residual-stream storage; the saving is a forward pass, not memory.
