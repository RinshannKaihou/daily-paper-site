Why it matters

A lab can build a serious non-English agent from open weights — and Arabic leaderboards were partly measuring broken test items.

Both halves ship: the adaptation recipe and the audited benchmarks are reusable.

Critical edge

The AraIFBench gain is partly a training–test artifact: RLVR's synthetic constraint set uses the benchmark's own verifiers, and no ablation holds a family out.

A 0.9B scoring 91.10 against 33.10 for Qwen3.5-2B is not plausible as general instruction following.

01

The recipe

adapt, don't pretrain

Each model starts from K2 Horizon's final post-trained checkpoint, with architecture and tokenizer frozen. Arabic is added in four passes; 25% replay of the base model's own mid-training data runs through CPT, cooldown and SFT to hold forgetting down.

01 / CPT

Continual pretraining

201.5B tokens, 75% Arabic-side and 25% replay. Natural Arabic is only 19% of the mix; 44% is translated or synthetic. Cooldown adds 3.47B at 3:1.

02 / SFT

Supervised fine-tuning

About 41B tokens, including 2,180 Arabic file-task trajectories across XLSX, DOCX, PDF and PPTX, plus the same 25% replay.

03 / FTPO

Preference pass

A DPO-style objective with tether terms, early-stopped at 0.35 chosen-win. Chiefly a repetition killer: loops fall 13.8% to 1.2%.

04 / RLVR

Verifiable rewards

Five domains — instruction following, math, code, i'rab, tashkeel — scored by deterministic rule-based verifiers, not a reward model.

Side study: mixed-task RLVR beats a five-stage cascade by +25.4 vs +6.7 on AIME 2025, while the cascade loses 6–11 points on short-form math. Tokenizer unchanged: 1.86 tokens per Arabic word, against 4.17 for cl100k.

03 / The bill for reliability

Abstention is not knowledge.

On SimpleQA-Verified-Ar, the 375B declines 93.51% of the questions it gets wrong. Its F-score is 5.49. The abstract calls this fewer hallucinations; the body calls it fewer answers.

With low-effort thinking the non-hallucination rate collapses below 3% at every size. GPT-5.6 Sol abstains on 0.72% of its errors, Gemini 3.8 Flash on 0.00%.

04

The ledger

what holds, what breaks

Holds up

  • Stage-by-stage openness. CPT, cooldown, SFT, FTPO and RLVR checkpoints for all six sizes — rare even among open labs.
  • The audit is a real artifact. 69,168 items reviewed, 3,634 removed, 51% of removals traced to wrong gold answers.
  • Costs are stated in the body. Abstention lowers F-score; the 375B trails frontier models on eight of ten agentic benchmarks.
  • A clean dynamics experiment. Mixed and cascade RLVR arms matched to within 1% exposure, 800 updates, same starting checkpoint.

Breaks down

  • The base model is missing from every main table. Retention appears only as prose: up to 36B, 21 scores rise, 9 tie, 8 fall.
  • The Arabic File-Processing Benchmark is unreleased — yet it supplies the paper's largest agentic margins.
  • Decontamination is lexical 8-gram only. Translated or paraphrased benchmark leakage survives.
  • No compute, wall-clock or cost figures, despite the implicit claim that adaptation beats pretraining.
  • Flat scaling at 375B is unexplained: it trails its own 32B on Arabic file work, and no analysis says why.
05

Questions worth chasing

unresolved in the report
Q1

What share of the AraIFBench gain survives on a constraint family deliberately held out of RLVR?

Q2

Does the abstention policy make the model refuse to act in terminal or file-editing episodes?

Q3

Why is the 375B worse than the 32B on Arabic file work — and was that known before release?

Q4

How much of the Ar-Files gain is format familiarity rather than Arabic document reasoning?
