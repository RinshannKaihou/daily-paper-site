Four moves, one epoch

Planning is offline per epoch; shrinking happens at runtime, inside the step.

  1. Predict each prompt's tail

    Per-prompt max length, smoothed by EMA at α = 0.3 — 30% history, 70% latest epoch.

  2. Sort, then cut contiguous steps

    Prompts ordered by predicted tail into 32-prompt steps, so completions cluster instead of scattering.

  3. Guard the step with TailGuard

    A response cap from the 0.95 quantile of one-sided underestimation over K = 3 transitions, clamped to 4,096–16,384 tokens.

  4. Plan the floor, detach at quorums

    Per-floor KV-safe pair graphs plus a quota-aware ILP pick the shrink depth with maximum release area. At runtime ranks detach at 16 → 8 → 4 → 2 completion quorums, then restore the full world before reward and training.

Release area R(F) = Σ (fs−1 − fs)(T − ts) — the quantity the planner maximizes, not raw floor depth.

Correctness invariants: exits happen only after compute detach and KV release; experts are materialized losslessly; the full EP world is restored before any optimizer step.

Balance02

Strengths

  • The reframing is load-bearing. Rank-level utilisation falls from 14.78% active to 2.04% in the dummy interval — a gap aggregate throughput cannot see.
  • Reporting discipline. Separate capacity / sidecar / latency points, a non-elastic ablation, forecast-perturbation sensitivity, and a controlled quorum experiment.
  • Safety treated as a result. Measured per-floor KV budgets, 93.14% max cap use with 8,994-token headroom, 41 shrink events with no timeout.
  • TailGuard travels alone. Microseconds of bookkeeping buys −6.62% rollout time even with zero ranks released.

Limitations

  • One node, one model, one vendor. 16× Ascend 910C, Qwen3-30B-A3B, EP16; multi-node EP and GPU portability are untested.
  • The elastic gain sits near the noise floor. 2.31% with CI [26.8, 184.0] s on a 4,427 s rollout.
  • No head-to-head against published systems. RollPacker and RhymeRL are absent; the TLT comparison drops draft training and speculative decoding.
  • Points cannot coexist. TailGuard costs ~3× sidecar goodput, and floor2 needs Natural mode because planned group warmup exhausts HBM.

Open questions03

What the paper leaves open

Four threads worth pulling before trusting the 24.09% headline.

  1. Q1Does TailGuard's cap bias the GRPO advantage estimate? Truncation changes the shape of the reward distribution, not only its mean.
  2. Q2Does the 16 → 8 → 4 → 2 ladder survive multi-node EP, where inter-node collective ordering and restore cost may dominate?
  3. Q3Is a 2.8 s MILP worth it when it beats MinSkew on only 8 of 150 replayed steps (5.3%)?
  4. Q4What does an end-to-end run against RollPacker or RhymeRL show on the same DeepScaleR workload?
