Why this matters So what

Gives post-training teams a first-50-step diagnostic (retained request energy, Ξ evolution) to catch a doomed distillation run before burning thousands of GPU-hours.

Critical edge ⚠ Caveat

The healthy/stalled comparison confounds teacher size with recipe (RL-trained vs distilled), so “a stronger teacher transfers less” is not what the experiment manipulates.

01

The loop, decomposed

Conversion · broadcast · preparation

OPD is modeled as a loop: rollouts Πt set the training-prefix distribution µt; each prefix emits a reverse-KL request Bt; the tangent map Φt turns requests into a parameter step; and that step changes predictions at every prefix through Kt(x,s)=Φt(x)Φt(s)ᵀ, reshaping the next rollout distribution.

  • Conversion splits into pressure P=E‖B‖², accessibility A (request-energy-weighted Rayleigh coefficient of the self-kernel), and coherence C=‖Eg‖²/E‖g‖². Collective gain is Ξ=A·C.
  • Identification: freeze B₀, the prefix bank and token coordinates to isolate kernel adaptation from a changing teacher request.
  • Broadcast decomposes exactly into routing (prefix visitation) and drift (fixed-prefix prediction), estimated by symmetric two-way cross-scoring over 20-step windows.
  • Preparation: offline forward-KL on teacher solutions (512 steps × 256 sequences), then OPD on DAPO-Math-17k; 1.5B/7B/Llama-8B, pooled AIME 24+25, one seed, ≈4,000 GH200-hours.

03

What holds, what doesn’t

Read the caveats with the claims
Strengths

What holds

  • Ξ = A·C is diagnostic. It turns “why is this run stuck?” into three measurable numbers, and they dissociate: coherence is the culprit at 7B, accessibility at 1.5B.
  • The frozen-request probe is clean. Freezing B₀, the prefix bank and coordinates separates “the teacher stopped asking” from “the student stopped listening”, and beats the other teacher’s field by 1.79 pp at 1.5B.
  • Routing/drift is exact and cheap. Symmetric two-way cross-scoring sums to the observed change using only frozen rollout banks and checkpoints.
  • The answer/continue intervention is causal. Forcing the branch at 86 prefixes gives 0.34 vs 0.03, isolating execution from decision.
  • Unusually candid. A reported null, a single-seed disclosure, and a math-only, ≤8B scope stated in the conclusion.
Limitations

What doesn’t

  • Recipe confound. Healthy/stalled is RL-trained vs distilled, so “a stronger teacher transfers less” is not the manipulated variable.
  • One seed, tiny counts. 1.5B pass@1 is ≈1.7 of 60 problems and its reliability comparison rests on 3 shared-solved problems; intervals cover problem resampling, not trajectory variance.
  • Small estimator samples. n=80 positions per class; endpoint coherence ratios as low as 0.012 sit near the noise floor.
  • LLM labels, no human agreement. Request semantics carry the control-vs-technical causal story without an annotator-error check.
  • Control arm is not clean. It confounds on/off-policy with forward/reverse KL, and no arm matches total compute.

Open questions

Where the next experiment goes
Q1

Do P, A and C from the first 50 steps predict stalls for other teacher families, code, or 30B+ students, and with what lead time?

Q2

What fills the missing 2×2: forward KL on student rollouts, and reverse KL on teacher solutions?

Q3

Is 512 steps of preparation cheaper than rollout-length caps, entropy penalties, or teacher-confidence gating? None is tested.
