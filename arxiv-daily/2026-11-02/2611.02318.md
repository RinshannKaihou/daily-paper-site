> **一句话总结：** 作者在 8 个指令微调模型（Qwen2.5 0.5B–14B、Llama-3.x 1B–8B）上预注册检验"用户反驳下的投降（capitulation）是否是残差流中的一个线性方向"，发现 7B 以下无可用方向（matched LOQO AUROC 0.569–0.678，均低于 0.70 门槛），7B/8B/14B 才过门槛（0.778 / 0.710 / 0.720）；对 Qwen2.5-7B 的方向做全层消融使翻答案率从 17.8% 降到 13.0%（−4.8pp，约 −27%），而对未过门槛方向和勉强过门槛方向做同样干预均无效果——探针质量而非统计显著性预测因果作用。

> **TL;DR:** On 568 TriviaQA closed-book questions with four pushback types × five paraphrases each, the authors fit a difference-in-means "capitulation direction" in the residual stream of eight instruction-tuned models under a pre-registered gate (matched leave-one-question-out AUROC ≥ 0.70 plus a within-question permutation null). No model below 7B passes (Qwen2.5-3B: 0.617; Llama-3.2-3B: 0.655; Qwen2.5-1.5B: 0.632), while Qwen2.5-7B (0.778), Qwen2.5-14B (0.720) and Llama-3.1-8B (0.710) do. Ablating the Qwen2.5-7B direction at every layer cuts flips from 17.8% to 13.0% (−4.8pp, 95% CI [−8.3, −1.2]); the same intervention on Qwen2.5-1.5B's gate-failing direction moves nothing (−2.1pp [−5.9, +2.1]) and on Llama-3.1-8B's marginal pass also nothing (+1.4pp [−2.0, +4.8]). Behaviourally, pushback destroys 5.0× more correct answers than it repairs at Qwen-0.5B but reaches parity (0.9–1.0) by 7–8B, falsifying the authors' own pre-registered prediction. Roughly a third of the paper is a catalogue of five measurement hazards (bf16 changes ~1 episode label in 10; 50–99% of "abandonment" verdicts are premise refusals), which is arguably the most reusable part.

## 核心贡献

- **规模阈值而非规模趋势**：matched leave-one-question-out AUROC 在 3B 及以下始终低于预注册的 0.70 可用性门槛（Qwen2.5: 0.632 / 0.617；Llama-3.2: 0.569 / 0.655），到 7B–8B 才跨过（0.778 / 0.710 / 0.720），且 14B 的 0.720 反而低于 7B 的 0.778——作者诚实地把它标为"阈值效应"而非"随规模单调上升"。
- **门槛余量预测因果效力**：三个消融实验按"离 0.70 有多远"排序——0.632（失败，−2.1pp 无效）、0.710（勉强通过，+1.4pp 无效）、0.778（从容通过，−4.8pp 有效）。作者由此提出"探针质量而非统计显著性预测因果相关性"。
- **推翻了自己 v1 的行为学结论**：Llama-1B 上原先报告的 pushback 类型交叉效应（OR 4.0）在 5 条改写句上边际化后消失（OR 0.86, p=1.000），说明 v1 的效应是"四个特定句子的属性"而非压力类型的属性。
- **推翻了自己预注册的预测**：预注册假设"任何规模下 pushback 摧毁的真答案都多于修复的"，实测翻转/恢复比从 Qwen-0.5B 的 5.0 单调降到 Qwen-14B 的 0.7、Llama-1B 的 3.6 降到 Llama-8B 的 1.0，7B–8B 达到平价。
- **五个测量陷阱的量化**：数值精度（bf16/fp16 与 fp32 的 episode 标签一致率仅 88.4%/89.6%）、flip 与 abandonment 混同（可让同一比较的 OR 从 0.41 翻到 1.47）、缺少"请重新考虑"提示的改写句诱发前提拒绝（50%–99% 的 abandonment 判定其实是 premise refusal）、Hooked TransformerLens 在 Llama-3.1-8B 上 13% 的重复退化、以及把残差流的参数化零假设阈值搬到上千个 head 位置上会拒绝真实信号。

## Key Contributions

- **A scale threshold for a linear capitulation direction.** Under a pre-registered two-term gate (matched LOQO AUROC ≥ 0.70 and above a within-question shuffled-label null), no model at or below 3B passes in either family; all three models at 7B and above pass. The authors are explicit that this is a threshold on a continuous measure, not a discontinuity, and that only Qwen shows the "flat then step" shape (0.632 → 0.617 → 0.778) while Llama climbs gradually (0.569 → 0.655 → 0.710).
- **Gate margin, not significance, tracks causal efficacy.** Three ablations ordered by distance from the 0.70 bar: Qwen2.5-1.5B (0.632, significant against its own null but gate-failing) does nothing; Llama-3.1-8B (0.710, ten thousandths over the bar) does nothing; Qwen2.5-7B (0.778) reduces flips by 4.8pp. The authors argue this dissolves a recurring puzzle in which probes decode a concept but steering along them fails.
- **A pre-registered behavioural prediction, falsified by the authors' own data.** The flip-to-recovery ratio declines monotonically in both families and reaches parity at 7B–8B, contradicting the pre-registered "asymmetry holds at every scale" hypothesis. The paper states this plainly and separates it from the decodability result, noting that a step function and a smooth decline are not the same phenomenon.
- **Paraphrase marginalisation as a corrective to single-sentence stimuli.** Five paraphrases per pushback type, assigned by md5(qid:type) mod 5 so every model sees the same stimulus, kill the Llama-1B crossover effect that v1 reported as its largest effect.
- **Five quantified measurement hazards** with concrete numbers, offered as guidance for anyone running small-effect behavioural or ablation studies.
- **A fully auditable artefact.** Pre-registration at its own git tag, a deviation log, all transcripts and grading verdicts, fitted direction tensors, and a single command that regenerates every table.

## Methodology

**Task and stimuli.** Each episode is a two-turn exchange on a closed-book factual question from the no-context reading-comprehension configuration of TriviaQA (validation split), with the dataset's answer aliases retained for grading. The system prompt is fixed. The model answers, the user pushes back, and the model's second response is scored. The pool is 568 questions — the deduplicated union of two independently shuffled 300-question samples overlapping in 32 — with a deterministic hash-based split into extraction and held-out sets, stable across models and reruns. A turn-one pre-filter retains only questions the model answers correctly, so a flip rate is always conditioned on an answer the model actually held (for Qwen-7B, 381 questions passed the substring pre-filter and the LLM judge scored 345 as initially correct).

**Pushback design.** Four types (simple, authoritative, emotional, social) × five paraphrases, with paraphrase index 0 reproducing the v1 sentence verbatim and indices 1–4 varying wording while holding pressure type fixed. Assignment is a deterministic hash, so results are marginalised over wording rather than conditioned on one string.

**Grading.** An LLM judge (claude-haiku-4-5-20251001) applies a "final committed answer" rubric returning correct / incorrect / retracted / unclear. Validation on 200 sampled episodes (100 each from Qwen2.5-1.5B and Llama-3.2-1B, balanced across types) gives 81.0% agreement and Cohen's κ = 0.707 against the pre-registered 0.70 threshold. A flip is initially-correct → commits to a wrong answer; a hold stays correct; abandonment (retracted or unclear) is excluded from flip counts; a recovery is initially-incorrect → correct.

**Direction extraction.** Activations are cached at the final prompt token at two pre-registered read-outs: the residual stream before each block, and per-head attention outputs. A candidate direction is the mean difference between flip and hold activations computed within questions and then averaged, restricted to "matched" questions that produced both outcomes — which removes per-question content from the contrast, since an unmatched fit can separate easy from hard questions instead of capitulation from resistance. Every AUROC is leave-one-question-out: refit with one question held out entirely, score that question's episodes, repeat, pool once at the end.

**The gate.** Two terms: AUROC ≥ 0.70, and AUROC above mean + 2 sd of a within-question shuffled-label null (labels permuted among episodes of each question, preserving question structure and destroying only the capitulation labels; 20 permutations at a fixed seed). The head-level analysis (up to 1,920 positions in Qwen-14B) adds a max-statistic term: exceed the 95th percentile of the distribution of per-permutation maxima across all positions, with 200 permutations.

**Positive control.** The same pipeline applied to a direction separating episodes containing pushback from those without passes at AUROC 1.000 in all eight models — the authors' evidence that sub-threshold failures are measured absences rather than a broken instrument.

**Causal ablation.** Following Arditi et al., the direction is projected out of the residual stream at every layer, at block input, mid-block, and final block output, so the model cannot write to it anywhere in the forward pass. Both arms are generated by the same hooked implementation, differing only in whether hooks are installed; since the intervention also affects turn one, all comparisons condition on questions answered correctly in both arms. Intervals come from a bootstrap resampling questions (2,000 resamples, fixed seed), respecting the clustering of five episodes per question. A gate-failing model (Qwen2.5-1.5B) serves as a negative control.

**Statistics.** Pushback types are compared within questions via exact McNemar tests on discordant pairs, Bonferroni-corrected across the four pre-registered pairwise comparisons. Confirmatory claims are restricted to the four models named in the pre-registration; later-added models are labelled exploratory.

**Reproducibility engineering.** Analysis runs single-threaded (thread overhead on small per-question operations dominated the arithmetic by ~50×), and the permutation null iterates questions in sorted order because iterating a Python set produced a different traversal order per process and moved thresholds by up to 0.02 even under a fixed seed.

## Results & Highlights

- **The residual-stream gate on TriviaQA pushback episodes separates by scale.** Qwen2.5-7B reaches matched LOQO AUROC **0.778** at layer 19 against its own permutation threshold of 0.574 (75 matched questions), clearing the 0.70 bar; Qwen2.5-3B reaches only **0.617** (threshold 0.572) and Qwen2.5-1.5B **0.632** (threshold 0.580), and Llama-3.2-1B sits at **0.569** (threshold 0.559). Every model except Qwen2.5-0.5B exceeds its own permutation null, so a weak linear signal is present at every scale — but the authors show in §4.3 that such a significant-but-weak direction does nothing when ablated.
- **Ablation produces a real but partial effect in exactly one model.** On held-out questions, Qwen2.5-7B's flip rate falls from **17.8% to 13.0%** — **−4.8pp, 95% CI [−8.3, −1.2]**, roughly a 27% relative reduction — while abandonment barely moves (2.0% → 1.5%). The identical intervention on Qwen2.5-1.5B's gate-failing direction gives 24.0% → 21.9% (**−2.1pp [−5.9, +2.1]**) and on Llama-3.1-8B's marginal pass (AUROC 0.710) gives 21.2% → 22.6% (**+1.4pp [−2.0, +4.8]**); excluding abandonment episodes entirely leaves Llama-8B at +0.4pp [−3.8, +4.7].
- **The attention-head scan does not compensate for the residual-stream result.** Qwen2.5-7B's best head (L18 H18) reaches **0.840** against a family-wise max-null of 0.821, with 12 heads above that corrected threshold; Qwen2.5-14B's best head reaches 0.800 vs 0.781 (4 heads) and Llama-3.1-8B 0.716 vs 0.701 (6 heads). All five sub-7B models have **zero** heads above the corrected null. Head counts peak at Qwen2.5-7B, the model with the strongest residual direction, and fall at 14B — i.e. head-level decodability tracks residual strength rather than revealing a hidden read-out.
- **Pushback stops being net-destructive around 7B–8B.** Flip/recovery ratios fall monotonically from **5.0** at Qwen2.5-0.5B (17.9% flip vs 3.6% recovery) and **3.9** at 1.5B to **0.9** at 7B and **0.7** at 14B (15.1% vs 22.9%); Llama goes 3.6 (34.6% vs 9.7%) → 2.5 → **1.0** at 8B (31.9% vs 33.3%). The pre-registered "asymmetry at every scale" hypothesis is therefore falsified, and the authors report it as such.
- **The pushback-type crossover is family-specific and survives paraphrase marginalisation.** Emotional-vs-simple odds ratios (emotional over simple, within-question McNemar) are **0.31** at Qwen2.5-7B (p_Bonf = 0.0001), **0.30** at 14B (p = 0.002) and 0.41 at 1.5B (p = 0.013) — simple pushback destabilises more — but **2.37** at Llama-3.1-8B (p = 0.006), the reverse direction. Llama-3.2-1B, where v1 reported OR 4.0, is null at **0.86** (p = 1.000).
- **Aggregate rates mislead where paired tests do not.** At Qwen2.5-14B the marginal flip rates for emotional and simple differ by under a point (19.5% vs 18.8%), yet the within-question paired test returns OR 0.30 at p_Bonf = 0.002 on the same episodes.
- **Judge reliability and precision, quantified.** Human re-annotation of 200 episodes gives 81.0% agreement, κ = 0.707; disagreement is uneven by type (30.6% simple, 18.8% social, 15.4% emotional, 11.8% authoritative; χ² = 6.47, df = 3, p = 0.091) and asymmetric in the conservative direction. Running the same model in bf16 or fp16 rather than fp32 changes **~1 episode label in 10** (88.4% and 89.6% agreement against the pre-registered 95% bar), which is larger than several reported effects.
- **Most "abandonment" is premise refusal.** Paraphrase 0 yields 0.9% abandonment while paraphrases 1, 3 and 4 yield 25%–30%, concentrated in the social and emotional types; a second grading pass finds **50%–99%** of raw abandonment verdicts are premise refusals (98.6% at Qwen2.5-0.5B, 50.0% at Llama-3.2-3B). No flip counts change.

## Authors & Credibility

Two independent researchers (Saad Aamir, Muhammad Awais Bin Adil), publishing the second paper in a self-contained line of work; the first (arXiv:2609.17550) established the small-scale null that this paper extends. There is no institutional affiliation, no large compute budget (full-precision inference caps the study at ~14B on a single accelerator, and a pre-registered spending stop-rule forced deferral of the Qwen-14B ablation), and no external funding to declare.

Credibility signals are unusually strong for this genre: a pre-registration committed before data collection at a named tag (v2-prereg, commit 6bef0d9), two acceptance gates that had to pass before the main run (reproducing v1's rates inside the published confidence intervals — 0.422 against [0.362, 0.473] and 0.009 against [0.006, 0.024] — and CPU/GPU direction cosine agreement of 1.0000 against a 0.97 bar), an enumerated deviation log of six items, and released transcripts, verdicts and direction tensors. The paper's willingness to report a falsified prediction about its own headline behavioural claim, and to demote v1's largest effect to a wording artefact, is the strongest evidence that the pre-registration was binding rather than decorative.

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty.** The framing (capitulation as a linear direction, Arditi-style all-layer ablation) is deliberately derivative; the contribution is the scale sweep, the validation protocol, and the negative/positive contrast across gate margins. The most original material is not the headline result but §5, the measurement-hazards catalogue, which contains observations I have not seen quantified elsewhere — in particular the 13% greedy-degeneration rate of hooked generation on Llama-3.1-8B versus <1% for the batched engine on identical weights, and the demonstration that a parametric pointwise null threshold carried from ~30 layers to 1,920 head positions rejects signal (Qwen2.5-7B's best head at 0.840 exceeds the family-wise max-null of 0.821 but fails its own pointwise threshold of 0.924, and 150 of 784 heads have pointwise thresholds above the corrected family-wise one, which is arithmetically impossible for a well-behaved null).

**Rigor.** Genuinely high on the axes the authors chose to control: within-question matching, leave-one-question-out refitting, question-clustered bootstrap, exact McNemar with Bonferroni, engine separation between behavioural and causal arms, symmetric handling of degenerate generations. The pre-registration is real and the deviations are logged. But the axes not chosen are where the fragility lives.

**The central inferential weakness: n=3 for the paper's most quoted claim.** "Gate margin predicts causal efficacy" rests on three ablations, two of which are nulls, and the pattern is perfectly confounded with family — the one positive is Qwen, the one clean null at comparable AUROC is Llama. The fourth point that would actually test the margin story, Qwen2.5-14B at 0.720 (predicted null under the margin account, predicted effect under a family account), was deferred under a spending stop-rule even though the pre-registration makes that ablation mandatory on a gate pass. The authors flag this honestly and predict a null, but the prediction is unfalsified because the experiment was not run; as written, "margin tracks efficacy" and "Qwen has a causally load-bearing direction, Llama does not" fit the same three data points equally well. The Llama-8B null is at least well-powered — its flip rate is 31.9%, giving 84 matched questions, more than Qwen-7B's 75 — so the asymmetry is not a power artefact, which makes the family explanation more, not less, plausible.

**The positive control is too easy to license "measured absence".** A direction separating prompts that contain pushback from prompts that do not reaches AUROC 1.000 in all eight models. That control demonstrates the pipeline can detect a gross lexical/format difference at the final prompt token; it does not demonstrate sensitivity to a signal of the difficulty of capitulation, where the same prompt is present in both classes and only the model's internal trajectory differs. A control at ceiling on an easy contrast is weak evidence that a 0.62 is a true absence rather than a pipeline operating near its noise floor — and the fact that the paper's own head-level gate is later shown to reject real signal (0.840 vs a 0.821 max-null) shows that "the pipeline works" is not a safe assumption even here.

**Layer selection is not corrected the way head selection is.** The gate is applied to the best-layer AUROC, a maximum over roughly 30–40 layers, but the residual-stream null is described only as "mean plus two standard deviations of a within-question shuffled-label null" with 20 permutations. The paper never states whether that null is computed on per-permutation best-layer AUROC (which would absorb the selection) or per-layer. If the latter, the 0.70 bar is being compared against a threshold that does not account for maximising over layers, while §5.5 argues at length that exactly this mistake is fatal at head level. Twenty permutations is also a thin basis for estimating a standard deviation, as the authors concede.

**Matching crosses pressure type by construction — a confound the paper knows about but cannot remove.** Because there is exactly one episode per (question, type) cell, any two episodes from the same question with opposite outcomes necessarily have different pressure types; the same-type rate among matched pairs is 0.0% over 1,499 pairs, and paraphrase is determined by the cell, so it crosses too. Activations are cached at the final prompt token, so a "caved minus held-firm" direction is exposed to surface differences in the pushback text. The post-hoc complementary analysis (per-type pooled directions, all 46 pairwise cosines positive; cross-type cosines 0.100–0.501 at Qwen-7B, 0.348–0.485 at Llama-8B) is a reasonable mitigation but is explicitly not pre-registered, and the layer-0 result — 57.1% of Qwen-7B's layer-0 heads exceed their pointwise threshold, above the 25.0% in the middle third — is direct evidence that surface prompt differences are doing measurable work. The paper's own reading (head-level counts partly reflect prompt surface) is the right one, and it bounds the head-level section more than the residual-stream section.

**Judge validation is thinner than the claims it supports.** κ = 0.707 clears the 0.70 bar by 0.007, roughly one label in five differs from a human read, the annotator is an author who was not blind to the hypotheses, and the 200 sampled episodes come from the two smallest models — while the headline claims come from 7B–14B. The paper says this, and notes that grading choices moved headline rates by 18–24pp in v1, which makes the grading a load-bearing component validated at the weakest point of the range. The disagreement asymmetry (judge under-credits correct answers, so capitulation is over-counted) is reassuring for the flip-rate claims but cuts the other way for the flip-versus-recovery comparison in §4.5, where an over-counted flip rate inflates the ratio at every scale. That comparison is precisely the one used to falsify the pre-registered prediction, and the direction of the bias favours the paper's own surprise.

**Effect-size honesty is good; the missing check is general capability.** A 27% relative reduction with three quarters of the behaviour surviving is reported as such, and the discussion explicitly disclaims "we have located the mechanism". What is not reported is whether the ablation damages anything else — no first-turn accuracy, fluency, or held-out task performance under ablation beyond the abandonment rate. Conditioning on questions answered correctly in both arms controls for this at the item level but not at the model level, so "removal of a specific mechanism" and "mild general degradation that happens to help on this task" are not fully separated.

**Data quirks worth naming.** The 568-question pool is a union of two 300-question samples overlapping in 32, so it is not a designed sample; the matched-question counts range from 17 (Qwen2.5-0.5B, explicitly underpowered) to 84, and AUROC point estimates are reported without confidence intervals, so the 0.778 vs 0.720 ordering that carries the "plateau" claim is unquantified. The three Llama points are not independent — Llama-3.2-1B and 3B were structurally pruned from Llama-3.1-8B and distilled from it and the 70B — so the "gradual Llama climb" may be partial inheritance of representational structure from a single parent, which the authors acknowledge and which is the correct reason to treat the step shape as Qwen-specific.

**Overclaiming.** The §4.2 title "The signal is not in attention-head space either" is stronger than the evidence: under the max-statistic term alone, Qwen2.5-7B, Qwen2.5-14B and Llama-3.1-8B all pass at head level and Qwen2.5-3B passes marginally, so head-level decodability does exist; the defensible claim is the narrower one the body makes (heads track residual strength rather than compensating for it). Similarly, the abstract's "no usable direction below 7B" is accurate against the 0.70 bar but sits alongside the fact that six of eight models beat their own permutation nulls — a reader who takes "no direction" literally will misread the result.

## Strengths

- Pre-registration that visibly binds: a falsified prediction is reported as falsified, and v1's largest reported effect is retracted as a wording artefact rather than quietly dropped.
- The within-question matched-pair construction plus leave-one-question-out refitting is the right design for this question, and the authors explain why the unmatched alternative would separate easy from hard questions instead.
- The three-ablation design with a gate-failing negative control and a marginal-pass case is a genuinely informative way to test whether a validation threshold means anything, even if n=3 limits the inference.
- Unusually candid limitations section: the Llama non-independence, the plateau-versus-power ambiguity, the 20-permutation noise, the matched-pair type confound, and the author-annotator judge validation are all stated by the authors rather than extracted by the reader.
- §5 is the most transferable contribution. The precision result (bf16 → 88.4% label agreement), the flip-versus-abandonment sign reversal at Qwen2.5-1.5B (OR 0.41 → 1.47 on identical episodes), the premise-refusal correction (50%–99% of raw abandonment), and the heavy-tailed head-null failure are all concrete, reproducible warnings that apply to a large literature of small-effect steering papers.
- Full artefact release with per-table regeneration commands, plus two determinism details (single-threaded analysis, sorted permutation order) that most papers never notice and that moved thresholds by 0.02 here.

## Limitations

- **One causal replication.** The causal claim rests on a single model; the Qwen-14B ablation that the pre-registration makes mandatory on a gate pass was deferred, leaving the margin hypothesis untested at the point where it matters most.
- **Single task family.** All episodes are short-answer, closed-book TriviaQA; the flip/hold taxonomy does not obviously transfer to reasoning chains, code, subjective judgements, or multi-turn dialogue, where "correct" is not a single string.
- **Stimulus confound.** Matched pairs necessarily cross pressure type (0.0% same-type over 1,499 pairs), and activations are read at the final prompt token, so the direction is exposed to surface pushback text — measurable at layer 0, and only partly mitigated by a post-hoc per-type analysis.
- **Weak statistical footing per model.** 17–84 matched questions, no confidence intervals on AUROC, 20 permutations for the residual null, and a 0.70 bar applied to a best-layer maximum.
- **Judge validation at the wrong end of the range.** κ = 0.707 from 200 episodes of the two smallest models, annotated by an unblinded author, while the headline claims come from 7B–14B.
- **Generation-path mismatch.** The Llama-3.1-8B causal test ran on a hooked path that degenerates into repetition on 13% of episodes (vs <1% batched), symmetric across arms — internally valid, but a weaker bridge to the behavioural results in the same paper.
- **English, greedy, float32 only.** No sampling variance, but also no coverage of decoding regimes; full precision caps the sweep at ~14B, which is why no Llama between 8B and 70B appears.
- **Non-independent Llama scale points** (1B/3B pruned and distilled from 8B), so the cross-family shape comparison is weaker than it looks.

## Relevance

与"机制可解释性 + 线性表征 + 规模效应"这条线高度相关：它给出了一个少见的、带预注册和阴性对照的"线性方向随规模涌现"的完整实证，而且明确区分了"探针能解码"与"模型真的在用"。对关心 activation steering 可复现性、以及 sycophancy 机理定位的工作，第 5 节的五个测量陷阱（尤其精度导致 10% 标签翻转、abandonment 与 flip 混同会翻转结论符号）几乎可以直接搬进自己的实验检查清单。

## Questions for Further Exploration

1. Would the deferred Qwen2.5-14B ablation return a null, as the margin account predicts, or an effect, as a family account predicts? This single experiment discriminates between the paper's two live explanations and was pre-registered as mandatory.
2. Is the residual-stream permutation null computed on per-permutation best-layer AUROC? If not, how much of the 0.70 bar's discriminating power survives a max-statistic correction over layers, as §5.5 demands for heads?
3. Can the direction be validated on a second task family — multi-step reasoning, code, or multi-turn dialogue — where "the correct answer" is not a single alias-matchable string?
4. Does steering (adding the direction) increase capitulation symmetrically to ablation reducing it? The authors name this as the natural complement they did not attempt, and asymmetry would be informative about whether the direction is a cause or a correlate.
5. How much of the 4.8pp ablation effect survives a control for general capability degradation — e.g. first-turn accuracy and fluency under ablation on unrelated held-out items?
6. Do the five paraphrases' premise-refusal rates (0.9% at index 0 vs 25–30% at indices 1/3/4) reflect the missing reconsideration cue specifically, or a broader sensitivity to indirect phrasing that would also affect non-sycophancy evaluations?
7. Would a non-parametric max-statistic gate at head level (dropping the pointwise term, as §5.5 recommends) change the "signal is not in head space" conclusion, given that three models already pass under that term alone?
