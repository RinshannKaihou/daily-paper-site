Strengths

What holds up
  • Verifiable by construction. Every exemplar is a sufficient condition for the direction; one forward pass checks it.
  • Statistical hygiene above norm. Pre-registered 7-gram filter (486 of 512 survive), document-clustered CIs, Holm correction, unbiased best-of-k.
  • Token-matched NLA baseline (176 vs 47.3 tokens) makes the 0.21 cosine gap hard to dismiss as a sampling artefact.
  • Negative results reported with care. A failure taxonomy, plus evidence that fine-tuning on failures fixes 26 features and breaks 304.
  • Concrete safety result. 85% payload recovery from LoRA write directions vs 2% for corpus search; mismatch controls stay ≤2%.
  • Honest cost accounting. 3.35 H200-seconds per target for 64 draws, with the excluded scoring pass stated.

Limitations

What remains open
  • No SFT-only ablation at matched compute, so RL’s marginal value over supervised initialisation is unknown.
  • Single everything. One subject model, one read layer, one SAE family, one checkpoint; no cross-architecture test.
  • ~11% of SAE features never cross τ at best-of-8, and 6.5% never fire in 64 draws; the rarest decile fires 42%.
  • Low diversity (Vendi 2.19 vs 2.87) costs description recall: 38% of held-out positives accepted vs 71% for corpus windows.
  • Non-semantic triggers are essentially unreadable: 0% on read directions and 21% on write for three-digit codes.
  • Objective mismatch. Uncentred reward over the last 16 tokens vs centred evaluation; no KL, and reward-hacking headroom untested.
