Critical edge

The title says “compression”; the experiments say “targeted perturbation of one circuit” — simulated 4-bit on a handful of heads, no real quantizer, no perplexity, no downstream benchmark.

01

Methodology

four moves
  1. 1

    Task

    Indirect-object identification: “When Mary and John went to the store, John gave a drink to ___.” 1,000 exploratory prompts, then 2,000 held-out prompts from the same 30 templates, single-token names.

  2. 2

    Partition the circuit

    Retrieval heads T, copy-suppression heads CS, the rest O. The margin change is booked exactly as H = D − R − B − N, each term measured by direct logit attribution.

  3. 3

    Match damage, not head count

    Ten heads remove 1.3–3.7 logits depending on model, so every sweep is interpolated to a shared D = 0.5. Ranking, calibration and receiver choice are frozen on exploratory prompts before held-out evaluation.

  4. 4

    Intervene and probe

    Zero-ablation, simulated per-channel 4-bit on the target heads’ four attention projections, structural half-head pruning — plus path cuts and response-prevention clamps as causal probes.

Pythia 410M–12B (shared data, order, token budget) · GPT-2 XL · OPT 125M–6.7B · Llama 3.1 8B · fp32, TransformerLens.

02

The ledger

logits

H = D − R − B − N

D
direct deficit from the removed retrieval heads
R
copy-suppression released once those heads go quiet
B
net backup from all remaining heads and MLPs
N
final LayerNorm residual

Repair is D − H. The identity closes to ≤ 1.3 × 10⁻³ logits across the Pythia panels — a bookkeeping instrument, not a fitted model.

04

Strengths

  • A reusable instrument. H = D − R − B − N turns “the benchmark didn’t move” into four separately weighable quantities — no retraining, no auxiliary probes.
  • Matched deficit, not matched head count. Ten heads remove 1.3–3.7 logits depending on size; comparing at a shared 0.5 invalidates a chunk of prior pruning comparisons.
  • Frozen before held-out. Target ranking, damage calibration and receiver selection are fixed on 1,000 exploratory prompts before the 2,000 held-out prompts are touched.
  • Two causal probes. The path cut separates reaction-to-input from reaction-to-computation; the clamp tests whether the backup response is actually protective.
05

Limitations

  • One seed per size. Six points, two interior outliers (1B, 1.4B), no slope test; the intervals describe prompt variation, not training variation.
  • Saturated, synthetic task. IOI accuracy is 0.99–1.00, so 0.38 logits is about 9% of a 4.3-logit margin on a task the model still gets right. No prediction flips are shown.
  • Compression is simulated. Per-channel 4-bit on four projections of selected heads, dequantized for fp32; no real quantizer, no KV cache, no perplexity, no downstream suite.
  • Mechanism unresolved. B is a signed net that can hide cancellation, and the copy-suppression channel rests on exactly two heads with no sensitivity sweep.
