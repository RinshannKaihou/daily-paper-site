Why this matters

SAE comparisons lean on decoder cosine. This gives a decomposable, uncertainty-aware measure of where two dictionaries actually differ — and which latents are responsible.

Decoder-cosine scores are exactly +0.0000 on both topics: they never look at the documents.

Critical edge

The headline Δ is measured at ε=0.30, where self-transport is only 68.8% diagonal — so per-latent attributions are substantially an entropic-smoothing artifact; the latent-level analyses retreat to ε=0.10.

01

Method

One plan, three readings

Setup & mass

Two SAEs — possibly different widths — encode the same activations. A latent is the pair (hj, dj); comparison is posed as mass transport between weighted point clouds, not a matching.

aj = E[hj2] / Σj E[hj2]

Mass is energy, not count. A rarely firing but high-amplitude latent is not automatically minor.

Three geometries

Costs come from decoder cosine, uncentered co-activation ρact, or a linearized causal effect. All three are Hilbert-space cosines, so cjk = 2(1 − sjk).

The plan

Pε = argminP∈Π(a,b) ⟨c, P⟩ + ε KL(P ‖ a⊗b)

Solved in the log domain with Sinkhorn. Both marginals stay fixed, which makes the allocation fractions well posed — and asymmetric.

Cost
Dε = ⟨c, Pε⟩ — one number for the dictionary gap.
Decompose
Dε = Σj aj djloc — the same number, split exactly across latents.
Flow
Tjk = Pjk / aj — how j's mass is allocated, directionally.
Influence functions

Costs and both marginals come from the same documents, so variance is taken jointly — not as if marginals were fixed.

Joint CLT

Full covariance Ω across all q = S(S−1)/2 SAE pairs, keeping cross-pair dependence.

Paired bootstrap

Resample documents, using identical indices for every pair; report simultaneous bands.

Strengths & limits

Trust the method more than the finding
+Strengths
  • Equation 5 makes localization a consequence of the same optimization — not a second, differently normalized score.
  • Shared-document dependence is handled correctly: joint Ω over all S(S−1)/2 pairs plus a paired document bootstrap, with coverage checked at 94.5–95.5%.
  • Directionality is a feature: 98.75% vs 54.76% shows a latent can be almost fully absorbed while contributing only half of its partner.
  • Unusual reporting discipline: null results in three of four models, the rare-document coverage collapse, and a 75% self-cost correction at ε=0.60.
−Limitations
  • Scale: the only robust positive result is width 64. Production dictionaries of 16k–1M latents are not addressed.
  • Only activation geometry is validated empirically; causal geometry is never tested, and inference for causal costs is deferred.
  • ε is free and consequential: self-comparison diagonal runs 1.000 → 0.208 across the grid, with no data-driven rule for choosing it.
  • Inference is conditional on trained SAEs: a five-seed jackknife with t₄, i.i.d. documents, and prespecified index sets.

No code or artifact link appears anywhere in the paper — the main credibility gap for a methods contribution whose value is the estimator.

Open questions

What the paper leaves open

Three next experiments

Each one tests whether the method survives contact with real dictionaries.

Q1 How does the estimator scale to Gemma Scope–sized dictionaries — roughly 3.8×107 cross-moments per pair at width 6144?
Q2 Would a centered or IDF-weighted co-activation shrink the 28–40% cost share held by low-energy latents?
Q3 Does causal geometry reorder the top-contributing latents, given Proposition 5's near-parallel sign flip?
