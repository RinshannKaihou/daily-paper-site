01 / Method

A three-stage pathway, then a scalpel

Paired clean / distracted passes

Same query, same scored gold token, with and without a prefix. The measured quantity is the gold-logit shift Δℓg, traced through five open-weight models in TransformerLens: Llama-3.1-8B, Mistral-7B-v0.1, Qwen2.5-7B, Pythia-6.9B and GPT-2 XL.

01 — Enrichment

MLP writes survive

Subject-position MLP enrichment persists under an irrelevant prefix (LRE excess 0.073–0.395, all CIs above zero). Germany is not erased; cancelling these writes still costs 3.10–5.46 gold logit.

02 — Copying

Prefix identity reaches END

Parroting heads copy prefix content to the prediction site: cue removal retains 7–26% of copying-head attribution, versus −1–4% for induction controls. Source-value substitution separates OV copying from QK routing.

03 — Reading

Readers look away — not enough

Relational readers give unrelated filler only 0.008–0.012 attention, versus 0.194–0.273 to a framed gold answer. Selective reading is real, but the residual stream stays polluted.

03 / Verdict

What holds, what doesn't

Critical assessment, condensed

+ Strengths

  • The Ph/Uh source split is a falsifiable claim that overturns the intuitive copying story — backed by source-resolved tables, not one aggregate number.
  • Negative results are reported as results: K20 is worse than K10 on every task, and broad END→prefix masking moves the gold logit by at most +0.17.
  • Head populations are compared before exclusions, with expected-overlap statistics: 0/20 shared with the 36-head entrainment set, expected 0.70 by chance.
  • Prefix construction is unusually careful: a five-level leakage filter, GloVe-neighbour exclusion, four selection rules, plus length and position sweeps.
  • The intervention is decomposed into remove-Ph / amplify-Uh / both / whole-write gain, ruling out the trivial renormalization explanation.

− Limitations

  • Scale: the deployable K10 mask is never tested above 7B; larger-model evidence is oracle patching only.
  • Base models only — no evidence the mask survives chat templates or instruction tuning.
  • Needs a context boundary. In mixed golden + irrelevant prompts it blocks both, costing −0.014 to −0.077 gold log-probability.
  • Supervised head selection: paired clean/distracted passes are needed at identification time; no minimal set or optimal K is established.
  • Uneven recovery: the 56.9% mean hides 48.5% on HotpotQA versus 63.3% on PubMedQA, and Pythia regresses −9 pp on PubMedQA at k=1.
  • Synthetic, separable distractors only; no naturally noisy retrieval corpus, and recovery can exceed 100% (GSM-IC 108.8%).

Bottom line A diagnostic and interpretability result more than a deployable defence: 7B base models, supervised head selection, and a known context boundary.
