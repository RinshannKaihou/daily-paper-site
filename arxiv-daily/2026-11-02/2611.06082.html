Catastrophic overfitting is a sideways walk off a plateau the loss cannot see — not a bad minimum.

02

Instrument, guard, anchor

Sec. 3–4 · method

Instrument

Vanilla FGSM-AT, ResNet18 / CIFAR-10, ε = 8/255. Every 100 batches, save the raw weights w and their EMA w̄ (decay 0.9995) side by side.

  • Line search sweeps 12 pairs, 6 before and 6 after the collapse.
  • Attack is recomputed at every α — freezing it would mis-measure the loss.
  • BatchNorm recalibrated on 4,096 samples before every evaluation.

Guard

Reset to the EMA when validation PGD-10 falls more than θ = 8 points below its running best. Cooldown and fire budget stop a sinking anchor.

if  b*_t − p_t > 8      # deficit
and t − t_fire ≥ 3      # cooldown
and F_t < 5:            # budget
        w ← w̄

Base method

Only an EMA that already sits in the robust region is worth resetting to. The base couples three terms:

L = CEls(zc, y) + γ‖Zc−Za‖* + m‖zc−za‖²

MSE consistency is anchored at the clean input, not the attack init — otherwise the penalty becomes self-referential.

04

What holds, what leaks

Strengths / limitations

+ Strengths the analysis

  • +The cleanest mechanistic measurement of CO to date: paired raw/EMA checkpoints plus a line search along the real drift direction.
  • +±7.5% vs −69–77% turns “blind” into a number, not a metaphor.
  • +The fix is nearly free — the EMA is already maintained by every FAT protocol.
  • +θ is chosen from a measured gap (6.5 vs 9.6), and the failure at θ = 10 is shown, not hidden.
  • +Negative results carry numbers; full AutoAttack + CR, per-seed tables, code released.

− Limitations the method claim

  • −Four seeds. Zero CO in 4 runs is consistent with a true rate as high as 53%.
  • −The guard is exercised on CIFAR-10 only — one seed actually fires.
  • −PGD-50 reverses the headline: 54.63 vs 55.97 for LIET.
  • −Coupling weights were swept on the reporting benchmarks; the CIFAR-100 peak is a narrow inverted U.
  • −Three forward passes per step: 1.05 h vs 0.7–0.8 h for the cheapest fast methods.
  • −A 0.3-weight wrong-logit term exists only on CIFAR-10 — where the guard is validated — and is never ablated.
05

Open questions

For further exploration
01

Does the guard generalise? No setting yet makes CIFAR-100 or Tiny-ImageNet actually collapse under the re-balanced base — so what configuration would test the guard rather than the nuclear term?

02

Why does PGD-50 reverse the ordering? Is robustness concentrated where APGD steps miss it, or is this a checkpoint-selection artifact?

03

What does the 0.3 wrong-logit term cost? If it is inert, why is it there; if not, it belongs in the main text.

04

Can validation memorisation be caught? One seed held validation PGD-10 at 0.592 while test PGD-50 fell to 25.6. Would a second selection statistic — the attack-direction cosine — close the gap?
