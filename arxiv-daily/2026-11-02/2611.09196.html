01

Method — an instrument, then a reading

569,270 prompts · one position each

One reading is one position per prompt: the construct's first token. Everything around it — identifiers, literals, scope, companions, length — is drawn at its rate in 253,398 public Python files, and every draw is logged. The generator never runs the model.

  1. 01

    Declare the population

    A: construct in role. B: the same token out of role — string, comment, docstring, name. C: ordinary code, used only as the yardstick. 720 prompts per set, per twin.

  2. 02

    Standardise per cell

    Divide by each neuron's own mean and spread on C, in the same kind of position: 20 cells = 4 token classes × 5 length bands. An entry of 2 means two ordinary spreads.

  3. 03

    Measure the noise

    Draw the battery twice on the same cores with different surfaces. Twin distance is the noise floor (median cosine 0.986); intervals come from resampling cores, not from a distributional assumption.

  4. 04

    Gate before reading

    Nine checks, two hard nulls, must pass before anything is read. Then zero the top 5% carrier set and measure loss where the grammar fixes the next token.

03

Balance — what holds up, what to discount

read together

Strengths

4 notes
  • +The framing is overdue. A reading is a distribution over a population someone chose; the paper builds the instrument that says so out loud.
  • +The licence is a real artefact. Nine checks, two gates, and an output sentence about which claims are legal — not another score.
  • +Failure is reported, not buried. Three self-caught instrument artefacts, a failed companion check, an owed seed, two scripts that disagree.
  • +Release quality. Full battery, carrier sets at all 61 references, and a manifest mapping every number to a file and script.

Limitations

4 notes
  • −One model, one language, one read site. OpenCoder-1.5B, Python, first token, 55-token prompts. Generality is argued, not measured.
  • −All eight compound statements fail companion matching, and four cannot be re-weighted for lack of per-prompt activations.
  • −The twin unit understates population choice by ~2.3×, yet cross-checkpoint resolution is still stated in twin units.
  • −Causal evidence covers 16 of 169 constructs and compares to the random mean, not its spread. No SAE or attribution-patching baseline.

Open questions

what the next paper owes
Q1

Does the profile survive a second model, or is it a property of this checkpoint series?

Q2

Which unit should cross-checkpoint distance use, if re-weighting moves a profile 2.34 twin units?

Q3

Why does the 20-cell yardstick name neurons with a smaller ablation effect than one pooled cell (+0.08 vs. +0.25 nats at 4.19B)?

Q4

Can the profile become a discovery instrument — does an unnamed SAE feature's top prompts yield a matching carrier set?
