Key finding 核心发现

Pointing at the right image and writing it out are different heads; only their overlap carries the retrieval load.

Why this matters为什么重要

Attention maps show where a head looks, not what it writes into the residual stream. Partition-and-ablate, not top-K overlap, is the transferable test.

Method lesson · 方法论
Critical edge关键质疑

The overlap arm is ablated in pointer-rank order while the pointer-only arm is systematically lower-ranked, so “overlap beats pointer-only” is partly “top pointer ranks beat middle pointer ranks.”

Rank confound · 排序混淆
01Method

Two criteria, one intersection

把“看向哪张图”和“写出了什么”拆成两个可测探针，再用集合划分做因果验证。 / 方法

  1. A probe with exactly one right answer

    6,000 MS-COCO multiple-choice samples (3,000 at N=5, 3,000 at N=6). All four captions name the queried category; only the target image contains it, so category cues cannot answer the question. One labelled target per sample makes a 1,152-head R@1 sweep possible.

  2. Pointer — where the mass lands

    At cattok (last token of the category mention), sum post-softmax attention over each image's visual-token span and rank the candidate images by that mass.

  3. Writer — what the head emits

    For every head, train a one-layer InfoNCE probe on its value-weighted output at cattok, matched against the model's own mean-pooled image tokens in a 256-d space. Is target identity linearly readable from the write?

  4. Partition, then ablate

    Split pointer ∪ writer top-100 into overlap / pointer-only / writer-only. Zero each head's slice of the o_proj input, then compare with matched random-head budgets over N ∈ {2…50}, K ∈ {5…30}.

  5. Transfer to a real haystack

    Visual Haystacks at N=2–50, every image fixed at 448×448 so cross-model comparisons are not confounded by input size; seed 42 fixes filler and ordering.

03Verdict

Strong instrument, one confounded control

贡献真实且可复现；核心因果对比仍有一个未匹配的变量。 / 评价

Strengths 优点

  • A real conceptual cut. “Where a head reads” vs “what it writes” at head granularity, made measurable by a trained probe rather than by inspecting attention.
  • Replicates on seven models across Qwen, InternVL, Mistral and LLaVA backbones — overlap 3.6×–5.4× chance, never identity.
  • Identical head index across models. L21H10 is top-1 in all four main models; 86/80 of the top-100 are shared within backbone pairs.
  • Honest negatives. Writer-only heads are causally inert, the in-context writer ranking is poorly resolved, and N=50 loses dynamic range — all stated plainly.

Limitations 局限

  • Single-target retrieval only. Cross-image comparison, counting and compositional reasoning — where multi-image models actually fail — are untested.
  • Rank confound in the ablation. Pointer-only heads are systematically lower in the pointer ranking, so the headline contrast is not cleanly identified.
  • No per-model significance tests for the four headline models; McNemar appears only for LLaVA in the appendix.
  • Writer is a linear-probe lower bound. No nonlinear probe, and no full-ranking Spearman between writer and pointer; the 35–47 overlap is cut-dependent.
04Open

Three experiments that would settle it

按重要性排序，而非论文顺序。 / 待解问题

Q1

Does the overlap advantage survive a pointer-rank-matched control? Ablate pointer heads at matched rank, split by writer membership. This is the experiment the central claim needs.

Q2

Does a nonlinear writer probe shrink the dissociation? If a 2-layer MLP recovers many more heads, “writing” is a property of the decoder, not the head.

Q3

What happens if you fix the writer side of L21H10? Fine-tune only its value/output projection on the writer objective and re-measure the haystack. A null result would be just as informative.
