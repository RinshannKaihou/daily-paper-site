01 · Method

One error metric, end to end

Measure error where the model actually reads it — after WO, not in raw K/V space.

Step 01

Center, rescale, quantize pre-RoPE

Keys are centered by μ and rescaled by a diagonal A before RoPE, then vector-quantized in 8-channel groups; values stay in raw coordinates.

Step 02

Calibrate against post-WO output

μ, A and both codebooks are fitted jointly with damped Gauss–Newton. Every candidate is cast to FP16, re-encoded and replayed through the native attention kernel; it is kept only if Fit loss falls and Select holds.

Step 03

Weight channels by Fisher sensitivity

Squared gradients of next-token CE give per-channel weights, transformed into the same normalized key coordinates — then clipped and shrunk.

Step 04

Anchor the states that matter online

Each state is scored by its realized reconstruction error under those weights; the top 56 per layer-head stay in FP16, maintained by a heap.

03 · Tensions

Where it holds, where it leaks

Strengths +
  • The coupling is validated, not just asserted: calibration alone 62.56 → 64.03 with Fisher anchors, 61.94 with random ones.
  • Uncommon candor: the Select set is labeled model selection, εx is not estimated, and anchors are explicitly excluded from the bit rate.
  • Implementation discipline: FP16 re-encode and native-kernel replay for every accepted step; a properly controlled same-rate TaSQ comparison.
  • Every reported mean matches its per-task columns on recheck — 7,814 questions, 21/21 cells won.
  • The streaming top-N invariant is proved; the paper also notes that discarded originals cannot be recovered.
Limitations −
  • No systems evaluation: no bytes, decode latency, throughput or kernel — for a compression paper, the largest gap.
  • Only one credible baseline. KIVI, VecInfer and OSCAR are 2-bit designs degraded to 1-bit; KIVI's 0.45 GSM8K reads as a broken port.
  • 56 anchors per layer-head are fixed and excluded from BPE: ~8.3 MB on Qwen3-4B, roughly 2× the advertised cache at 1k tokens (reviewer arithmetic).
  • No seeds or confidence intervals; 198 GPQA questions carry 1/7 of each mean, and more than half the Llama gap comes from that near-chance cell.
  • Fisher anchors lower GPQA 57.07 → 54.54 while random raises it to 58.58; the reversal is unreported.
