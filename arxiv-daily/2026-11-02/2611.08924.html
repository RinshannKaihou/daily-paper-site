01 Key metric

71–74%

less optimizer state than AdamW — 51.81 GB → 13.28 GB on Llama 2-7B.

Optimizer state only. End-to-end peak memory falls 15–19%, at 0.6–1.4% throughput cost.

02 Why this matters

Low-bit QAT shrinks the model but not the optimizer state; this shows why compressing that state breaks quality — and what a fix costs.

A diagnosis first, an optimizer second: the value is knowing which signal low-rank training throws away.

03 Critical edge

The paper never compares against plain 8-bit AdamW, which has the identical two-bytes-per-parameter footprint — so the 71–74% headline is measured against the most expensive possible reference.

Critical Assessment

The failure mode

Why projection fails under QAT

Under the straight-through estimator, the gradient has to cancel quantization error element by element. That compensation is spread across many directions, so a rank-r projection drops real signal at every step — and no learning-rate retune brings it back.

The authors measure the principal-subspace energy ratio on seven weight matrices of one middle layer: it starts high, decays, and settles well below 1 — highest for attention K/V, lowest for the MLP down-projection.

Method

One momentum, two directions

01

One momentum

Keep a single dense BF16 momentum from the STE gradient. No full-space second moment, no fp32 optimizer state.

02

Momentum SVD

Every τ steps, take the top-r singular vectors of the momentum — not the gradient — to define the principal subspace.

03

Adam inside r×r

Project the gradient to UᵀGV, run Adam there, then back-project with a √(nm)/r rescale so the norm matches a dense update.

04

Sign on the residual

From M⊥, keep only the sign, rescaled to ‖Δ∥‖. Cheap by construction — and, by the authors' own note, not exactly orthogonal.

05

Fuse by energy

Weight both directions by momentum energy ρ∥ / ρ⊥, then apply decay. Only the r×r Adam states move on refresh.

Strengths

  • The motivation is measured, not asserted: energy-ratio curves across seven matrices show exactly what projection discards.
  • Unusually broad sweep — two from-scratch frameworks × three bit-widths × two scales, plus three fine-tuning pipelines and three model families.
  • Honest about its own geometry: the sign step breaks orthogonality and the paper says so. Code is released.

Limitations

  • No plain 8-bit AdamW baseline at the same two-bytes-per-parameter cost — the memory win is measured against the priciest reference.
  • Single seed, no error bars; W2A16 fine-tuning cells swing up to 47% in PPL while accuracy barely moves.
  • From-scratch scale stops at 430M; no FSDP or tensor-parallel study; end-to-end savings are 15–19%, not 71–74%.

Open questions

What would change the verdict

  1. At the same two-bytes-per-parameter cost, does 8-bit AdamW close the gap — and what does the residual machinery buy beyond simplicity?
  2. Are the W2A16 PPL swings reproducible, or regime instability? Llama 2-7B moves 24.61 → 12.91; Qwen3 4B moves 33.84 → 46.72.
  3. Why does AdaFactor beat ResQAdam at fine-tuning W4A4G128 (14.80 vs 15.05) yet lose by 13 PPL from scratch?
