01

An exact identity, applied offline

方法

Each 512×512 array holds one weight scale and one ADC range, so every number in a layer must fit a shared window. The paper exploits an exact identity that moves the computation into a rotated, centered frame while leaving the real-valued result unchanged. Only the numbers that meet analog noise change.

  1. 01

    Center

    Subtract a per-channel mean; fold Wμ into the bias. The static component is 25–33% of ‖x‖² on ResNet-50 and Qwen3.

  2. 02

    Rotate outputs

    Block-diagonal regular Hadamard (R·1 = 1) keeps reference error spread; ADC peak-to-RMS falls about 4×.

  3. 03

    Rotate inputs, per tile

    Lowers weight peaks 2.5–2.9× and shrinks the shared scale. Per-tile partial sums stay exact, so calibrated ranges hold.

  4. 04

    Keep it cheap

    Add/subtract kernels only, no multiplies. Under 1% extra operations during model execution.

03

What holds up, what does not

优势 / 局限

Strengths 优势

  • The regular-Hadamard rule is crisp and falsifiable: with a fixed reference, plain blocks collapse ViT-B/16 to 0.0% while regular blocks hold 82.7%.
  • Tile-local rotation names a failure digital PTQ never sees, and prices it: up to 12× ADC error in a single layer.
  • The error-mechanism table is a clarifying abstraction — each fix is motivated by a measured physical effect, not borrowed from digital quantization.
  • Baselines are reproduced in their own settings, including the unflattering step of raising programming noise 10% to match a published AFM baseline.
  • Breadth: 4 CNNs/ViTs, 5 LLMs, PCM and ReRAM, 4/6/8-bit ADCs, read ages from 1 s to 5 days.

Limitations 局限

  • No silicon: AIHWKit simulation with fitted device models. DAC nonlinearity, settling, temperature and array-to-array variation are untested.
  • Partial acceleration: attention, softmax, norms, RoPE and I/O stay digital, so there is no end-to-end speedup or energy number.
  • Hardware add-ons: a programmed reference column for ReRAM and a per-layer probe readout for PCM’s drift gain soften the “unmodified array” claim.
  • Calibration comes from the evaluation distribution (1,024 ImageNet val images; ~8k task tokens). Distribution shift is not studied.
  • The gap grows as models shrink: Llama-3.2-1B stays 6.6 pts below floating point, observed but not explained.
