Why this matters

02

Hold the content fixed

The trick is not a new intervention — it is a contrast where the sentence stays constant and only the successful direction changes.

Hold the sentence fixed. Vary only which translation direction succeeded. Then ask which donor can reproduce the gain.

  1. 01
    Contrast

    FLORES+ multi-parallel segments. Same sentence, two directions: HIGH (xCOMET-XL likes it) vs LOW (it doesn't), margin ≥ 0.10, ≥ 50 instances. 22 MT pairs; 9 Belebele pairs.

  2. 02
    Donors

    Four versions of the last-token residual: matched, shuffled, mean, mean_diff. Only matched contains the test segment.

  3. 03
    Patch

    Overwrite one layer's last-token state at the first decoding step. Layers are patched independently; nnsight, bf16.

  4. 04
    Read

    chrF++, language ID plus target-exclusive characters, Belebele accuracy. Paired bootstrap, 10,000 resamples.

Four donors, one test

Only matched has seen the test segment. A gain shared by all four is content-free by construction.

  • matchedsame instance's HIGH state
  • shuffledanother segment's HIGH state, seeded derangement
  • meanper-layer HIGH average, norm-rescaled by γ
  • mean_diffadditive HIGH − LOW direction
03

What the patch changes

The English flip is content-blind. The content channel opens one layer later and is actively harmed by the wrong sentence.

The three-stage order

Gemma-3-4B, last-token residual, one layer at a time.

At L15–16 the flip direction is least sparse — top-50 R² falls 0.80 → 0.48 — exactly where it is most causal. Sparse add/ablate leaves output language at 0–5%; matched, mean and mean_diff put 76–97% of outputs into English at the same layer.

04

What survives, what strains

The patching result is unusually well controlled. The feature-level story is narrower than the abstract's framing.

+Strengths

  • Clean dissociation. All four donors rise in lockstep at L15–16; only matched separates at L17. That is a textbook control, not a marginal effect.
  • Cross-task replication. Belebele uses a different prompt template and scoring rule, and still puts content onset at L17 — the strongest single piece of evidence.
  • Independent probe. An intervention-free logit lens reproduces the same onset map, separating mechanism from intervention artifact.
  • Honest negatives. The xCOMET-XL non-replication, the n/a cells, and the failed sparse interventions are reported in the main text, not buried.

−Limitations

  • One vector, one position, one layer. The curves show what is causally available there, not where the computation happens; sparse nulls may be site-specific.
  • SAE story is Gemma-only. Qwen's 0.45–0.73 fidelity leaves reconstruction error in charge; Llama's 0.26–0.55 makes the analysis impossible. Sister claims rest on n = 60 / 85.
  • Narrow model band. 4B, 8B, 9B base checkpoints only; no instruct models, no scaling claim — yet the abstract says "three LLMs of different scales."
  • No downstream win. No intervention is shown to raise chrF++ on held-out low-resource pairs; "English" is really English plus high-resource Latin.
