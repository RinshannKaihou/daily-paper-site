Key finding · arXiv 2611.05033 · cs.CL

A subspace’s extra effect is manufactured by its training objective, not by cleaner concept isolation.

超额效应来自目标函数，而不是更干净的概念隔离。

Whose Effect Is It? How Objectives Inflate Learned Subspace Interventions

Toygar Tanyel / Serkan Ayvaz / 02 Nov 2026 / arXiv:2611.05033 [cs.CL]

Mechanistic interpretability Activation patching DAS Evaluation methodology

90 tasks · 14 base models, 124M–32B · 1,168 model–task cases · first-order theory machine-checked in Lean 4

A1 Key metric

1.64–3.00× T

Median readout effect of cross-entropy DAS, per model, in units of the mean total effect T. From the same three initialisations, a per-pair squared error aimed at T lands a median 3.0% from T.

Whole-state patching is the reference: exactly 1.00 T at a complete cut.

A2 Why this matters

If you use activation patching or DAS to claim a site “carries” a concept, your effect size may be measuring your objective, not the network.

The paper’s check: ≤5 forward passes per pair, no gradients.

A3 Critical edge

Whole-state patching is treated as ground truth, but it is also an intervention — one that puts the state off its natural distribution.

Critical Assessment · weakness 3

e(d) = dTMd ∈ [λD, λ1]

Orthogonal parts sum to the whole. A part exceeds the whole only when the remaining components push the output back — not because it isolates a concept more cleanly. Here M = sym E[wΔT].

02

How they got there

One quantity, four losses, one reference. The argument is arithmetic before it is empirical.

01

Fix the reference

A pair is a base input and a source input; z is the log-odds of the source label over the base, and T = z(xs) − z(xb). Every effect is reported in units of mean T. At a complete cut, whole-state patching equals T exactly.

02

Decompose to first order

Patch hb + PPTΔ. A unit direction then has effect e(d) = dTMd with M = sym E[wΔT]. The Rayleigh range [λD, λ1] bounds it, and orthogonal parts sum to the whole.

03

Swap only the loss

Rank-one DAS is trained from the same three initialisations under four objectives: cross-entropy, per-pair squared error at T, CDAS Jensen–Shannon, and mean effect. Same data, same schedule — the objective is the only variable.

04

Run the route test

Each cell’s effect e is compared with G*, whole-state patching in its best context. Report e/G*, declare off-route, or report no evidence of network use. The first-order claims are machine-checked in Lean 4 with Mathlib.

Coverage: 90 tasks from six sources · 14 base checkpoints, 124M–32B · 1,168 of 1,187 model–task cases qualify. Readout on all 14; in-network maps on six small models (78 tasks) and four 7–9B models (three tasks).

03

Where it holds, where to press

The controls are unusually strong. The scope is narrower than the framing, and one reference is left untested.

Strengths

Built to survive scrutiny

  • Predicts before training: θ forecasts the ceiling within 2.1–5.6%, and λ1/T grows with model size in all three families.
  • Causal control, not correlation: three initialisations, four losses, exact test-pair scoring.
  • Confounds run to completion: lemma-only pairs move the output 0.00 T; held-out templates hold in 13/13 IOI and 223/224 RAVEL cases.
  • Machine-checked first order, pinned revisions, hash-stamped run records — plus an upstream CausalGym bug report.

Limitations

And where it stops

  • First order inside the network: rank correlation 0.62, and 4,894 cells exceed the predicted maximum, mostly through attention.
  • Base checkpoints only; above 1B parameters the in-network map covers just three tasks.
  • The reference is itself an intervention: whole-state patching is never tested for opening dormant pathways.
  • Two post-hoc choices, one lab, one preprint — and the 410m sentiment exception carries the surviving 109.8% reading.

Open questions

What the next paper should test

  1. How much of the 0.76–0.97-of-maximum gap survives a stronger search for the maximum?
  2. If whole-state patching is off-distribution, how much excess survives a reference constrained to the natural state manifold?
  3. Does the decomposition survive instruction tuning, where refusal and editing directions are actually applied?
