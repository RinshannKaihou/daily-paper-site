> **一句话总结：** VC-Attention 是一个免训练的低比特 attention 内核：用在线 k-means 对 value token 重排序后减块均值（V-Smooth）消除 value 离群值误差，再用一条 fused multiply-add 把 log 域分数直接写成 E4M3 概率码（ExpCast-FP8）绕过 FP32 指数运算；在 Wan2.2/LongCat/HunyuanVideo/MiniMax-H3 四个视频 DiT 上，比 SageAttention2 高 2.3 dB PSNR（Wan2.2 8-bit），在 B200 上 attention 比 BF16 FlashAttention-4 快 1.59×（端到端快 1.19×）。

> **TL;DR:** A training-free low-bit attention kernel for video diffusion transformers that attacks the two remaining bottlenecks after QK quantization: value outliers (via online k-means token reordering + block-mean subtraction, "V-Smooth") and the FP32 softmax exponential (via a single fused multiply-add that writes the E4M3 probability byte directly from the log-domain score, "ExpCast-FP8"). On 100 MovieGen Bench prompts across Wan2.2-T2V-A14B, LongCat-Video, HunyuanVideo-1.5, and MiniMax-H3, it beats SageAttention2 by +2.3 dB PSNR on Wan2.2 (22.6 vs 20.3 dB, 8-bit, B200) and speeds the attention kernel 1.59× over BF16 FlashAttention-4 on B200 (6.02× over SageAttention2, which runs at 0.29× of BF16 there); end-to-end clip generation is 1.13–1.19× faster on datacenter GPUs and 1.36–1.70× on workstation Blackwell.

## 核心贡献

- **识别出视频 DiT 低比特 attention 的两个真正瓶颈**：(1) 在 QK 平滑（Hadamard 旋转等）之后，**value 量化误差占输出误差的 82%**（Wan2.2 实测），而 value 离群值出现在少数 token 上且其异常通道随 head/layer/step 漂移，旋转或静态布局均无法消除；(2) 在 B200 上两个矩阵乘都进 FP8 后，**FP32 指数与 FP32→FP8 转换成为最长的流水线阶段**（指数运算的 MUFU 周期数是 FP8 矩阵乘 Tensor Core 周期数的两倍）。
- **V-Smooth（value 引导的 token 平滑）**：对每个 batch/head 用轻量在线 k-means 给 value token 打标签，按标签排序得到置换 π，同时作用于 K 和 V（非因果 self-attention 输出不变）；每个 128 行硬件块减去块均值（16-bit 向量，仅 0.125 bit/元素），只量化残差；均值在 online softmax 递推中用已有的行和（row sum）以秩一外积恢复，无需额外 pass 或 buffer。排序后块均值可去除 36% 的块能量（序列顺序仅 8%，DeltaQuant 静态 cube 仅 12%）。
- **ExpCast-FP8（直接概率编码）**：利用 E4M3 字节本身就是其存储值的对数仿射函数这一事实（byte ≈ 8·log2v + 56 + ε），用一条 fused multiply-add `Round(8u + 56 + β)`、β = −0.35（minimax 常数，非拟合）直接从 log 域分数写出 E4M3 概率码，消除 FP32 指数和格式转换；与"先指数再 cast"的标准路径在 79.6% 的区间写出相同字节，其余最多差 1 个码。
- **理论保证**：Proposition 3.1 证明 ExpCast-FP8 的注意力行总变差 TV(p, p̂) < 3.64% + 下溢尾质量 τ；实测 204.8K 行（100 个 Wan2.2 head）平均 TV 1.6%，与 FP32 exp+cast 的 1.1% 接近。
- **全融合 CuTe/CUDA 内核与摊销分组调度**：把 RoPE、Hadamard、平滑均值、gather、量化整链融合进量化 kernel（预处理从 42.2 ms 降到 4.8 ms/次调用，8.74×）；分组只在去噪前 25% 步运行且置换复用 4 步，摊销后分组仅占 attention 时间 3–4%。

## Key Contributions

- **Diagnoses the two real bottlenecks** of low-bit attention on video DiTs: (1) after QK smoothing, the **value quantization term accounts for 82% of output error** on Wan2.2, and value outliers live in a few tokens whose spiking channels drift across heads, layers, and steps — immune to rotations (which preserve token norms) and to static layouts; (2) once both products run on FP8 Tensor Cores, the **FP32 exponential and FP32→FP8 cast become the longest pipeline stage** on B200 (exponentials need 2× the MUFU cycles the products need Tensor Core cycles).
- **V-Smooth**: labels value tokens with a lightweight online k-means, sorts K and V by label (output-invariant for non-causal self-attention), subtracts a per-128-row block mean (one 16-bit vector per block, 0.125 bit per element), and quantizes only the residual. The mean is restored inside the online-softmax recurrence as a rank-one outer product using the row sum already accumulated — no second pass, no extra buffer. Sorting lifts the energy share the mean removes from 8% (sequence order) / 12% (DeltaQuant's static cube) to **36%**.
- **ExpCast-FP8**: exploits that an E4M3 byte is an affine function of the log of the value it stores, and emits the probability code directly from the log-domain score with one fused multiply-add (`Round(8u + 56 + β)`, β = −0.35 read off the encoding's minimax, not fitted). Writes the same byte as the exponentiate-then-cast path on 79.6% of a doubling interval, at most one code apart elsewhere.
- **Theory**: Proposition 3.1 bounds the attention-row error: TV(p, p̂) < 3.64% + underflow tail τ; empirically averages 1.6% over 204.8K rows from 100 Wan2.2 heads, close to the 1.1% of FP32 exp + cast.
- **Fused CuTe/CUDA kernel + amortized schedule**: fuses RoPE, Hadamard, smoothing means, gather, and quantizers so permuted/rotated high-precision tensors never reach HBM (42.2 → 4.8 ms per attention call's preprocessing, 8.74×); grouping runs on the first 25% of denoising steps with the permutation reused over 4 adjacent steps, costing 3–4% of attention time on average. Deployed on B200, B300, H200, RTX PRO 6000, and RTX 5090.

## Methodology

The framework modifies two steps of a standard low-bit FlashAttention-style kernel and leaves the rest unchanged. **V-Smooth** first runs an online k-means over the value tokens of each (batch, head); sorting tokens by cluster label yields a permutation π applied jointly to K and V while Q keeps its order, which permutes the columns of P exactly as the rows of V and leaves P·V (and thus the output of non-causal video-DiT self-attention) unchanged. The permuted values are cut into the kernel's fixed hardware blocks of 128 rows; each block gets one mean µ_j (provably the optimal rank-one subtraction — it minimizes residual Frobenius energy), and only the residual R_j is quantized by the host kernel's value quantizer (per-channel E4M3 at 8 bits, NVFP4 at 4 bits). Substituting V = R + 1µᵀ into the online-softmax numerator update splits the product into a low-bit part (Tensor Core) plus r_ij·µ_jᵀ, where r_ij is the unnormalized probability row sum the normalizer l_i already accumulates — so the mean is restored with one outer product in the same accumulator, exactly, under running-maximum rescaling. Because plain k-means puts each cluster in one contiguous run, at most k−1 blocks mix clusters; balanced k-means would shave a further 8.5% of error but at 2.8× grouping cost and 85% of attention time, so plain (warm-started) k-means is the operating point. **ExpCast-FP8** observes that an E4M3 byte with exponent field e and mantissa field m equals 8·log2v + 56 + ε(m) with |ε| < 1 code; centering ε with the fixed constant β = −0.35 (Mitchell/Schraudolph-style minimax) turns probability production into one FMA that maps the log-domain score u = (s − m′_i)·log2e directly to a valid, clipped E4M3 code. The **implementation** grows fusion backwards from the quantizer until rotary embedding, smoothing means, gather, and Hadamard are all absorbed, with hand-written CuTe/CUDA passes; grouping is scheduled on the first 25% of denoising steps (permutation refreshed every 4 steps), since attention layouts drift little between adjacent early steps. Evaluation covers four open-weight video DiTs (Wan2.2-T2V-A14B 720p, LongCat-Video 480p, HunyuanVideo-1.5 720p, MiniMax-H3 1344×768 with an 8-step distillation LoRA), 100 MovieGen Bench prompts with shared seeds, fidelity scored against the BF16 FlashAttention-4 output of the same model and seed (PSNR/SSIM/LPIPS + two VBench dimensions), and latency measured as isolated kernel launches via CUPTI medians, with end-to-end wall clock pricing all preprocessing.

## Results & Highlights

- **8-bit fidelity (B200, Table 2, vs BF16 FlashAttention-4 output)**: on Wan2.2-T2V-A14B 720p, VC-Attention (V-Smooth) reaches **22.6 dB PSNR vs 20.3 dB for SageAttention2 (+2.3 dB)** and LPIPS 0.152 vs 0.248; on HunyuanVideo-1.5 it reaches 18.4 dB vs 15.6 dB (+2.8 dB); on LongCat-Video 24.5 vs 23.1 dB; on MiniMax-H3 21.0 vs 19.9 dB. LPIPS is reduced 13–29% across models. The fused V-Smooth+ExpCast variant trades 0.7–2.1 dB of that margin for speed and still beats SageAttention2 on all four models (e.g., Wan2.2 20.5 dB).
- **4-bit fidelity (RTX PRO 6000, Table 2)**: V-Smooth improves PSNR over SageAttention3 by **+2.9 dB on Wan2.2 (16.8 vs 13.9 dB)** and **+3.6 dB on LongCat-Video (18.7 vs 15.1 dB)**, and reduces LPIPS by up to 41% (LongCat-Video: 0.226 vs 0.382 for SageAttention3).
- **Speed (Figure 6, Wan2.2 1280×720×81f, 40 steps)**: attention kernel speedup over BF16 FlashAttention-4 of **1.59× on B200 (6.02× over SageAttention2, which itself runs at 0.26× of BF16 there since it ships no Blackwell kernel)**, 1.46× on H200 (1.16× over SageAttention2), 2.27× on RTX PRO 6000 and 3.58× on RTX 5090 at 4 bits. End-to-end single-clip generation speedups: 1.19× (B200), 1.13× (H200), 1.36× (RTX PRO 6000), 1.70× (RTX 5090).
- **B300 (Table 4)**: with both products forced to FP8 (no INT8 QK on the card), VC-Attention runs attention 1.47× vs 1.31× for a naive FP8 kernel and reaches 18.4 dB vs 17.1 dB PSNR on MiniMax-H3 — faster *and* more accurate at matched precision.
- **Where value error comes from (Figure 4 / Table 1)**: the block mean removes 36% of block energy after k-means sorting vs 8% in sequence order and 12% for DeltaQuant's static cube; on Wan2.2 (100 heads), value rMSE drops from 1.81×10⁻⁴ (sequence order) to 1.28×10⁻⁴ with k-means (warm-started, 9.5% attention overhead), while a Hadamard rotation of V changes error by only 0.2%.
- **Ablations (Table 3, Figure 7)**: grouping on the first quarter of denoising steps gives 26.4 dB PSNR / 351.0 s per LongCat clip vs 23.8 dB for the same number of steps placed uniformly (−2.6 dB) and 26.9 dB / 367.0 s for grouping every step — placement matters more than count, and the window saves 16 s. Progressive kernel fusion cuts preprocessing from 42.2 ms to 4.8 ms per attention call (8.74×), reducing a 40-call DiT forward's prep from 1688 ms to 193 ms.
- **Theory meets practice**: measured total variation of ExpCast-FP8 rows averages 1.6% over 204.8K rows (max 17.8% only on rows with underflow; rows without underflow stay below 1.4%), vs 1.1% for the FP32-exp-then-cast baseline.

## Authors & Credibility

The team spans Nunchux AI, MIT, CMU, UC Berkeley, Stanford, and NVIDIA, and includes senior authors well known in efficient-inference research (Song Han — SmoothQuant/AWQ/SVDQuant lineage; Lvmin Zhang and Jun-Yan Zhu) as well as multiple authors of SVDQuant, DeltaQuant, Sparse VideoGen, DC-VideoGen, and FlashAttention-adjacent work, giving them direct hands-on familiarity with both the quantization and kernel sides. The first three authors (Xingyang Li, Dongyun Zou, Shining Zhang) share equal contribution. The paper is unusually transparent about measurement methodology: kernels are modified in place inside the FlashAttention-4/SageAttention codebase so speedups are timed against the pipeline they change, attention latency is captured via CUPTI per-launch medians with preprocessing excluded (and priced separately end-to-end), and SageAttention2's B200 numbers are disclosed as coming from a recompile for sm_100a because it ships no Blackwell kernel. The mathematical claim (Proposition 3.1) comes with a full proof in the appendix, and the β constant is derived rather than fitted.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: The two ingredients are individually grounded in older ideas — Mitchell (1962)/Schraudolph (1999) log-domain approximation for ExpCast-FP8, and token clustering for permutation (echoing Sparse VideoGen 2, which clusters to *skip* attention blocks rather than to quantize). The synthesis is nonetheless genuinely new: no prior training-free method touches the value operand's outlier structure (SageAttention2's optional V mean subtraction is global, not block-wise; rotations provably cannot help since they preserve token norms), and the observation that the E4M3 byte is affine in log-space is a neat, zero-fitted-constant construction.
- **Rigor**: Strong on the whole — four models, five GPUs, 100 prompts with shared seeds, a proven error bound, and honest baseline disclosure. Weak spots: fidelity is measured **against the model's own BF16 output** (PSNR/SSIM/LPIPS), not against human preference or FVD; the two VBench dimensions are admitted to be near-saturated and non-discriminative, so the perceptual story rests on low-level pixel metrics plus qualitative strips. There is no error-bar or significance reporting on the 100-prompt averages.
- **Baseline fairness caveats**: the headline "6.02× over SageAttention2" on B200 is inflated by SageAttention2 lacking a Blackwell kernel (it runs at 0.26–0.29× of BF16 there) — the authors state this openly, and the meaningful number is the 1.59× over BF16 FlashAttention-4. Conversely, **Attn-QAT is run in a training-free configuration that the paper itself shows falls 3.4–6.7 dB below SageAttention2**, i.e., far from its published retrained results — fair for a "training-free" comparison but the table row understates that method's actual ceiling.
- **Reproducibility**: the method is fully specified (block size 128, β = −0.35, 25% grouping window, 4-step permutation reuse), but it depends on hand-written CuTe/CUDA kernels for five GPU targets; no code release is promised in the paper body, and reproducing the kernels from the text alone would be a major undertaking. Fidelity is also tied to a third-party 8-step distillation LoRA for the MiniMax-H3 setting.
- **Significance**: real and practical — a training-free kernel that converts FP8/FP4 Tensor Core throughput into 1.13–1.70× end-to-end video-generation speedup at better fidelity than prior low-bit attention is deployable immediately. The permutation-invariance trick, however, **only holds for non-causal self-attention**, so LLM decoding-style causal attention is out of scope, and ExpCast-FP8 is inapplicable to NVFP4 probabilities (no affine map exists for E2M1 with per-16 microscales), which the paper itself explains in Appendix A.
- **Red flags**: minor — the fused 8-bit variant gives back 0.7–2.1 dB of V-Smooth's PSNR lead, so the "best fidelity" and "fastest" configurations are not the same operating point; and grouping costs 30% of attention time on steps that do group, only amortized to 3–4% by the schedule.

## Strengths

- Attacks the *correct* bottleneck: the Figure 3(a) decomposition showing the PV term dominates residual error (82% on Wan2.2) after QK smoothing justifies the whole design, and the "QK Hadamard shifts PSNR by ≤0.3 dB" control confirms it.
- V-Smooth is mathematically clean: permutation-invariance keeps the exact attention output, the block mean is the provably optimal rank-one subtraction, and mean restoration is free (reuses the online-softmax row sum) with no extra pass or buffer.
- ExpCast-FP8 has a non-fitted, format-derived error bound (TV < 3.64% + τ) that holds for every model, validated empirically at 1.6% average TV.
- Genuine systems work: 8.74× preprocessing fusion, amortized grouping, and deployment on five GPUs including B300's INT8-less FP8-only path, with kernel-in-place modifications for apples-to-apples timing.
- Composability: the method leaves attention patterns and sampling schedules untouched, so it stacks with sparse attention, caching, and distillation.

## Limitations

- ExpCast-FP8 exists only in the 8-bit datacenter kernel (B200/H200); it cannot be applied to NVFP4 probabilities, and no 4-bit datacenter configuration is evaluated at all.
- The fused fast variant sacrifices 0.7–2.1 dB PSNR relative to V-Smooth alone — the accuracy/speed frontier is real, not free.
- V-Smooth's permutation argument requires non-causal attention; causal/autoregressive attention (e.g., LLM decoding) is not covered.
- Fidelity is proxied by PSNR/SSIM/LPIPS against each model's own BF16 output; the perceptual metrics used (VBench S.C./I.Q.) are near-saturated and cannot distinguish methods, and no human study or FVD is reported.
- The online k-means adds 30% attention overhead on grouping steps and its quality depends on the empirical claim that attention layouts drift slowly across early denoising steps — verified here on the four models but a heuristic, not a guarantee (the uniform-placement ablation losing 2.6 dB shows placement sensitivity).
- Evaluation is limited to video DiTs and NVIDIA GPUs; hand-written CuTe/CUDA kernels for five targets make independent reimplementation costly, and no public code commitment is stated in the paper.

## Relevance

直接命中高效推理/量化方向的核心议题：这是首个针对视频 DiT attention 中 value 离群值（而非 QK）做免训练低比特量化的内核级工作，兼具算法洞察（k-means 重排 + 块均值残差量化、对数域直接编码 E4M3）与真实 GPU 内核工程（B200/H200/B300/RTX 5090 上的实测加速），对量化、CUDA 内核优化和视频生成加速的研究都极具参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Can the V-Smooth permutation be shared across heads or layers (or predicted by a tiny network, as SpargeAttn does for sparsity) to further cut the grouping overhead below 3–4% of attention time?
- Does the k-means clustering structure stay stable enough for autoregressive/causal video models, where the invariance argument breaks — e.g., can blocks be re-sorted incrementally over generated chunks?
- Could ExpCast-FP8 extend to E3M4/E2M3 formats or to a two-term affine correction that closes part of the 0.7–2.1 dB gap paid by the fused variant?
- How does VC-Attention compose with sparse attention (Sparse VideoGen 2 uses the same clustering idea for skipping) — do the clusters found for quantization match those that make blocks skippable, enabling one grouping pass to serve both?
- Would the fidelity conclusions change under human preference studies or FVD against ground-truth references rather than PSNR to each model's own BF16 output, given the VBench metrics saturate?
- On the 4-bit workstation path, is the residual NVFP4 quantization error now the binding constraint, and would a low-rank branch (SVDQuant-style) on top of V-Smooth push PSNR further?
