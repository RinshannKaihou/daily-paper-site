> **一句话总结：** 提出 Fixed-SAE Track 框架（在 base 模型与全部 RL checkpoint 的池化激活上训练一个共享 SAE，使特征方向跨训练固定），发现 GRPO 等 RL 后训练对 Qwen2.5-1.5B 的表征漂移小、渐进、集中在第 27 层等晚期层的格式脚手架特征（换行、LaTeX 定界符、答案标签）上；把这些方向的解码向量注入 base 模型即可复现 RL 的绝大部分收益（GSM8K 严格准确率 0.050→0.192，超过 RL 最终模型的 0.175），表明短期 RL 主要是" elicitation "——激发模型已有的能力而非创造新推理。

> **TL;DR:** The authors train one shared TopK SAE on residual-stream activations pooled across the base model and 13 GRPO checkpoints of Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct so feature indices stay comparable across training ("Fixed-SAE Track"), then measure drift, interpret shifted features, and steer/ablate them. RL-induced drift is small and late-layer concentrated (max |Δf| 0.013 at layer 6 vs 0.060 at layer 27); amplified features are formatting scaffolds (step breaks, LaTeX delimiters, `<answer>` tags), not problem content. Steering these directions into the base model lifts GSM8K strict accuracy from 0.050 to 0.192 and tag-rate from 0.092 to 0.600, matching or exceeding the RL-final model (0.175 / 0.533) — recovering "around 80%" of RL's gain per the abstract — while lenient accuracy barely moves; all 29 GSM8K problems fixed by RL are already solvable by the base model at pass@64 (1.00). Short RL mostly teaches answer extractability; a causally necessary arithmetic-reasoning feature set R exists but cannot be injected as a static vector, and only sustained 2000-step training adds modest genuine reasoning (lenient 0.300→0.408).

## 核心贡献

- **方法框架 Fixed-SAE Track**：对 base 模型和所有 RL checkpoint 的残差流激活做池化并训练一个共享的 TopK SAE（k=32，扩展倍数 8，1.5B 模型对应 12288 个特征），冻结后用于重编码每个 checkpoint——使每个特征索引在训练全程指向同一方向，从而严格定义跨 checkpoint 的表征漂移，也使"新出现的特征"可被检测。
- **发现一：RL 漂移小而集中**：在 13 个 GRPO checkpoint 上，表征变化平滑渐进，显著变化集中在约十个特征；漂移随深度单调增加（第 6 层 max|Δf|=0.013 → 第 27 层 0.060），说明 RL 做的是靠近输出端的"聚焦编辑"而非全局重写。
- **发现二：放大的是格式脚手架而非内容**：被放大的特征对应算术运算符、LaTeX 定界符、段落/步骤边界；被抑制的特征对应题目实体、单位与连接性文本。约 86% 的特征放大在前 50 步内完成。
- **发现三：因果充分性**：把被放大特征的解码方向以 α=10 注入 base 模型第 27 层，GSM8K 严格准确率 0.050→0.192、tag 率 0.092→0.600，与 RL 最终模型（0.175/0.533）在置信区间内匹配；随机方向对照无此峰值——短期 RL 的主要收益是让模型"本已会算的答案"变得可被评分器抽取。
- **发现四：推理特征底座存在但不可静态注入**：构造的算术推理特征集 R 的消融大幅降低准确率（GSM8K 0.358→0.242）且跨 GRPO/REINFORCE/RLOO 复现，但把 R 注入 base 模型仅恢复 +0.06（RL 约 +0.34），说明推理依赖动态维持的计算状态而非静态方向；持续训练 2000 步才带来适度的真实推理提升（lenient 0.300→0.408）。

## Key Contributions

- **Method — Fixed-SAE Track.** Instead of per-checkpoint SAEs (arbitrary, non-comparable feature indices) or SAE Track's sequential fine-tuning (inherits each dictionary from the last, ill-suited to detect novel features), the authors pool residual-stream activations from the base model plus all RL checkpoints, standardize with global pooled statistics, and train one shared TopK SAE (k=32, expansion 8, m=12288 for the 1.5B model) that is then frozen and used to re-encode every checkpoint. This makes feature drift Δf = af(T) − af(0) rigorously defined over interpretable latents.
- **Finding — drift is small, gradual, late-layer, and format-dominated.** Across 13 GRPO checkpoints, substantial change concentrates in ~10 features; max drift grows from 0.013 (layer 6) to 0.060 (layer 27). Amplified features fire on arithmetic operators, LaTeX block edges, and paragraph/step boundaries; suppressed features fire on problem entities and connective text.
- **Finding — steering causally reproduces RL's dominant effect.** Adding amplified decoder directions to the base model's layer-27 residual stream yields an inverted-U dose response peaking at α=10 (strict 0.050→0.192; tag-rate 0.092→0.600 vs RL-final 0.175/0.533), with random-direction controls flat; the abstract summarizes this as recovering ~80% of RL's performance gain.
- **Finding — elicitation with a mechanistic qualification.** All 29 GSM8K problems flipped from base-wrong to RL-right are solvable by the base model within 64 samples (pass@64 = 1.00); yet a causally necessary arithmetic-reasoning set R (ablation: GSM8K 0.358→0.242, MATH-500 0.208→0.117, format placebo F ±0.008, MMLU −0.02) cannot be pasted into the base model (only +0.06 recovered of ~+0.34), and sustained 2000-step training adds a modest genuine gain (lenient 0.300→0.408, tag-rate →0.983).
- **Comprehensive robustness evidence.** Findings replicate across GRPO/REINFORCE/RLOO/strong-GRPO (mean off-diagonal drift-vector Spearman 0.42), across 1.5B/3B/7B scales (descriptively), across five SAE families, and via an independent reward-correlation attribution path (|ρ|≥0.8 recovers the same formatting-heavy signature).

## Methodology

The pipeline (Figure 1) has four stages. (1) **Pooling:** residual-stream vectors at the final non-pad prompt token are extracted from the base model LLM0 and every RL checkpoint LLM1..LLMT (e.g., layer 27 of Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct, D=1536), then standardized with pooled mean/RMS statistics. (2) **Fixed SAE:** a single TopK SAE (z = TopK32(Wenc x̃ + benc)) is trained on the pooled set, then frozen; decoder column Wdec[:,f] denotes the same feature direction at every training step, enabling drift, firing-rate, and active-set-turnover measurements. (3) **Interpretation:** features are attributed via token-level strong-fire rates (ρf over generated tokens) and Spearman correlation between mean activation and training reward. (4) **Causal interventions:** sufficiency is tested by adding amplified directions uniformly to the base model's layer-27 residual stream across a swept dose α; necessity is tested by subtracting each feature's reconstruction contribution strictly at the tokens where it fires in the RL-final model. Evaluation uses a **two-grader protocol**: strict accuracy (correct answer inside `<answer>`/`\boxed{}`, matching the RL reward), lenient accuracy (format-agnostic answer extraction), and tag-rate (wrapper emission alone), with the decomposition strict ≈ lenient × tag-rate validated to a residual ≤0.025. Models: Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct (primary), Qwen2.5-3B (layer 34), Qwen2.5-7B (LoRA), Qwen2.5-Math-1.5B base/Instruct; algorithms: GRPO, REINFORCE, RLOO, strong-GRPO; the reasoning set R is built by intersecting four independent criteria (high on rare-but-correct base completions, discriminates hard math prompts from trivial controls, amplified across GSM8K/MATH-500/AMC/AIME, dominates late-generation computation), with a format set F as placebo.

## Results & Highlights

- **Steering recovers RL's dominant gain (GSM8K, internal eval, Wilson 95% CIs):** base strict accuracy 0.050 [.023,.105] → 0.192 [.131,.271] at steering dose α=10, vs RL-final model 0.175 [.117,.253]; tag-rate 0.092 [.052,.157] → 0.600 [.511,.683] vs RL-final 0.533 [.444,.620]; lenient accuracy moves only 0.275→0.333 vs RL-final 0.308. Random-direction steering shows no peak; α≥20 pushes the model off-manifold (strict collapses to 0.000). The abstract summarizes this as ~80% recovery of RL's performance gain.
- **Drift is late-layer concentrated (Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct, GRPO):** max feature drift |Δf| = 0.013 at layer 6, 0.013 at layer 13, 0.017 at layer 20, 0.032 at layer 24, 0.060 at layer 27; active-set Jaccard overlap with the base falls only from 1.0 to 0.716 (~28% turnover) while L0 stays at 32 and FVU only 0.521→0.539, ruling out dictionary degeneration.
- **All RL-fixed problems already solvable by the base model (Qwen2.5-Math-1.5B, GSM8K):** on the 29 problems flipped from base-wrong to RL-right, base pass@k rises 0.235 (k=1) → 0.60 (k=4) → 0.78 (k=8) → 0.91 (k=16) → 0.97 (k=32) → 1.00 (k=64); 0/29 are never solved.
- **Cross-dataset transfer is mostly format (GRPO checkpoints vs base):** GSM8K-trained GRPO improves MATH-500 strict accuracy by +0.13 but lenient accuracy by only +0.03 — the transferable component is format, not reasoning.
- **Reasoning set R is causally necessary but not injectable:** ablating R from the RL model drops lenient GSM8K accuracy 0.358→0.242 and MATH-500 0.208→0.117, while ablating format placebo F changes accuracy by ±0.008 and MMLU by −0.02; the effect reproduces across GRPO/REINFORCE/RLOO/strong-GRPO (−0.34 to −0.37 on GSM8K). Yet clamping zR to a target activation recovers only +0.06 accuracy vs ~+0.34 from RL.
- **Sustained training and timing:** continuing from the plateau for 2000 steps at constant LR raises lenient accuracy 0.300→0.408 and tag-rate 0.108→0.983; ~86% of total feature amplification completes within the first 50 steps, with format changes preceding reasoning gains.
- **Few-shot and RL are partially substitutable (calibrated official protocol):** GSM8K base zero-shot 0.396 vs base few-shot 0.748 vs RL zero-shot 0.760 vs RL few-shot 0.732 (few-shot adds nothing on top of RL and slightly hurts); the few-shot feature-activation shift correlates with RL drift at ρ=0.46.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** The core methodological idea — pool activations across checkpoints and train one shared frozen SAE rather than per-checkpoint dictionaries or SAE Track's sequential fine-tuning — is conceptually simple, though it is the right fix to a real comparability problem and comes with useful formalizations (drift, firing rate, turnover). The elicitation conclusion largely confirms prior behavioral results (Yue et al. 2025 pass@k analysis; Shao et al. 2026 spurious rewards; Wang et al. 2025 high-entropy tokens), so the paper's added value is mechanistic localization (late layers, format features, steering recovery, the non-injectable set R) rather than a new headline phenomenon.
- **A promised experiment is missing.** Both the abstract and introduction state the authors "design a synthetic benchmark with features known by construction to test whether RL can instill genuinely novel features" — the paper's title question — but no such benchmark or its results appear anywhere in the 20-page v1 (the appendix runs A.1–A.13 and covers other ground). The central question of whether RL can create novel features is therefore left empirically unaddressed in this version.
- **The "80%" headline is not derivable from the shown tables.** On the primary steering eval, α=10 steering (0.192 strict) numerically exceeds the RL-final model (0.175) within overlapping Wilson CIs, and tag-rate likewise (0.600 vs 0.533); the main text never shows the computation behind "recovers around 80% of RL's performance gain," which presumably involves a different configuration or aggregate.
- **Rigor:** otherwise high — placebo format set, random-set and random-direction controls, cross-algorithm replication, SAE-family robustness checks, dictionary-health monitoring (L0≡32, FVU 0.521→0.539), and an honest negative result (R is necessary but not sufficient-by-injection, consistent with dynamically sustained computation). The apparent training-prompt "collapse" (205→9 tokens) is properly re-characterized as reward-hacking brevity rather than capability loss, with held-out evidence.
- **Reproducibility:** models are public Qwen2.5 family and training is standard GRPO/REINFORCE/RLOO with checkpoints every 50 steps; but no code or activation-pool release is mentioned, the RL training configs are only sketched (Appendix A.1), and feature-level claims (specific indices like f4974) are by construction tied to the authors' exact pooled dictionary, which third parties cannot recreate bit-for-bit.
- **Scale and scope caveats:** causal ablations are clean only at 1.5B; the 3B/7B results are descriptive ("inconclusive" for ablations), and the 7B model was trained with LoRA — a confound for representation comparisons. The primary analysis uses last-token residual-stream activations at a single layer per model. FVU ≈ 0.52 means the SAE explains only about half the activation variance, so drift in the unexplained subspace is invisible to this method. Exact feature identity is restricted to the sparse SAE family — dense ReLU/JumpReLU dictionaries (L0>5000 vs TopK's 32) do not match across families.

## Strengths

- Solves the cross-checkpoint feature-comparability problem cleanly (one pooled, frozen dictionary), with dictionary-health metrics proving the observed drift is not SAE degeneration.
- The two-grader (strict/lenient/tag) evaluation design is a genuinely useful methodological contribution for separating "answer formatting" from "reasoning" gains, and its decomposition is validated (residual ≤0.025).
- Exceptionally thorough control structure: random-direction steering, format placebo ablation, size-matched random feature sets, MMLU/BBH/ARC specificity probes, cross-algorithm and cross-scale replication, and five SAE families.
- Intellectually honest reporting: retains a superseded lenient-only steering analysis for transparency, reports that the necessary reasoning set cannot be injected, and re-characterizes apparent collapse as reward hacking with held-out evidence rather than milking a dramatic claim.

## Limitations

- The synthetic "novel features by construction" benchmark promised in the abstract/introduction does not appear in this version, so the question of whether RL can instill genuinely new features remains open.
- Primary causal evidence is confined to a 1.5B model; the 3B and 7B causal analyses are inconclusive, and 7B uses LoRA, confounding representation comparisons.
- The SAE's FVU of ~0.52 means roughly half the residual-stream variance is unexplained; drift outside the learned dictionary is unobservable, and cross-family (dense vs sparse) feature matching is near random.
- Analyses are based on a single position (final prompt token) and late layers, which is where they themselves show drift concentrates — but earlier-token dynamics of the same features are not tracked.
- The short-RL regime (600 steps, 13 checkpoints) dominates; the sustained-training extension is a single run, and RLHF (rather than verifiable-reward) settings are not studied.

## Relevance

直接命中"LLM Hidden States & Interpretability"兴趣方向：本文是 SAE/机械可解释性方法（TopK SAE、特征消融与 steering）应用于 RL 后训练表征动态的系统研究，其"训练可观测性"视角（漂移指标、active-set turnover、奖励-特征相关性）也与训练监控/可观测性兴趣有交集。其结论——短期 RLVR 主要改格式抽取而非推理本身——对理解 RL 后训练的收益来源与评测方式有直接参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How do the drift dynamics change for RLHF/preference-based RL, where the reward is not a verifiable format-or-accuracy signal — does the formatting-scaffold signature generalize or dissolve?
- Can the missing "novel features by construction" synthetic benchmark be built to settle whether RL ever creates genuinely new features, and what would the pooled-SAE detection power be at known signal-to-noise ratios?
- Does the non-injectability of the reasoning set R (dynamic computation vs static direction) reflect a fundamental limit of residual-stream steering, or would value-preserving interventions during generation (e.g., clamping zR at every step rather than uniform addition) close the +0.06 vs +0.34 gap?
- How does the format-dominant picture change at 14B–70B scale with full fine-tuning (no LoRA), where pass@k headroom and late-layer geometry may differ substantially?
- Could the two-grader strict/lenient decomposition be adopted as standard practice to re-audit published RLVR accuracy gains that may partly reflect grader-extractability rather than reasoning?
