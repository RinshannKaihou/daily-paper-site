> **一句话总结：** 提出 MiX（Micro-Inverted-Scaling）格式——把微缩放范式"倒过来"，用每元素私有指数共享单一尾数，从而化解 VLM 中视觉/文本 token 约 34 倍动态范围差引发的"微缩放坍塌"；配合可因式分解出共享尾数的无乘法器脉动阵列加速器，在 Qwen2-VL-7B 等模型上以 4.5 bit 达到与 NVFP4 相当或更好的精度（六基准均值 69.3% vs 69.7%），PE 面积效率提升 25%（9.1 vs 7.3 TOPS/mm²，TSMC28），端到端平均加速 9.77×/8.09×（LLaVA-OneVision/MiniCPM-V），并比 SOTA 加速器 Focus 快 2.3–4.5×、省能 1.4–2.9×。

> **TL;DR:** Edge VLM inference is memory-walled by both weights and the vision-inflated KV cache, and standard block formats (MXFP4/MXINT4/NVFP4) suffer "microscaling collapse" when a single vision outlier hijacks the shared exponent (44.8%/38.4%/38.3% of the Layer-27 K-matrix of Qwen2-VL-7B goes numerically dead at B=32 vs 23.9% for MiX). MiX inverts microscaling — per-element private exponents under one shared mantissa — so the shared mantissa factors out of the block dot product, turning PEs into shift-and-add units with a single scalar multiply. On Qwen2-VL-7B's six benchmarks (MMMU/OCRBench/VizWiz/TextVQA/ChartQA/SEED-Bench-2+), 4.5-bit MiX-INT4g16 averages 69.3% vs NVFP4's 69.7% and MXINT4g16's 67.2% (+2.1 pts), while beating NVFP4 by +1.9 pts on MiniCPM-V-2.6 and +0.6 pts on LLaVA-OneVision-7B. Synthesized at TSMC28/500 MHz, the MiX PE is 25% more area-efficient (9.1 vs 7.3 TOPS/mm²) and 15% more power-efficient (16.1 vs 14.0 TOPS/W) than NVFP4 at iso-throughput, delivers average iso-area speedups of 9.77x (LLaVA-OneVision) and 8.09x (MiniCPM-V) over FP16 — versus the token-pruning accelerator Focus which collapses to 1.94x on MiniCPM-V — and cuts energy to ~0.16x of FP16 (5.6–6.6x reduction).

## 核心贡献

- **发现并刻画 VLM 的"微缩放坍塌"（microscaling collapse）**：实证剖析 Qwen2-VL-7B 中视觉 token 与文本 token 存在约 34 倍的动态范围差（均值 0.312 vs 0.0091，99 分位数 2.07 vs 0.061），Q/K 矩阵存在通道级离群、V 矩阵存在早期 token 级尖峰；单一离群值劫持共享指数后，MXINT4/MXFP4/NVFP4 在 Layer-27 K 矩阵上分别有 44.8%/38.4%/38.3% 的元素下溢为死区。
- **提出 MiX（Micro-Inverted-Scaling）倒置数据格式**：与"多尾码共享一个指数"的传统微缩放相反，MiX 让每个元素携带私有指数差 Δe，共享一个小子块的尾码（MiX-4.25b：B=32 共享 4-bit Emax + 3-bit 尾码，EBW=4.25；MiX-4.5b：4 个 b=8 子块各带独立共享尾码，EBW=4.5 对标 NVFP4，且无需其 FP32 张量级缩放）。
- **代数化简驱动无乘法器加速器**：激活用 MiX、权重用 MX 的双格式点积中，共享尾码与指数可从求和中整体提出，逐元素运算退化为"符号翻转 + 桶形移位 + 整数加法树 + 每块一次标量乘"，从而用移位器彻底取代 MAC；输出侧融合单周期 MiX/MX 量化器（硬件指数精调只做一次 0.5 阈值比较），并用"预填充冻结 K 均值"的静态 K-smoothing 解决注意力坍塌。
- **首个端到端低位宽 VLM 加速器（据作者所知）**：与只量化 LLM 线性层的 MBQ/TLQ 不同，MiX 覆盖视觉编码器、投影器、LLM 主干及注意力 BMM（含 KV cache），全流水线可卸载到 sub-8-bit 边缘加速器。
- **系统性评测与开源工件**：3 个 VLM（Qwen2-VL-7B、LLaVA-OneVision-7B、MiniCPM-V-2.6）+ 4 个纯文本 LLM + Qwen2.5-VL 3B–72B 规模扫描，6 个多模态基准、13 种格式的 PPA–精度帕累托前沿；RTL（SystemVerilog, TSMC28HPC, 500 MHz, SAIF 功耗）与精度代码全部开源（Zenodo, MIT 许可）。另发现 OCP MXFP4 规范 floor 取整缺陷（约 40% 块静默裁剪最大元素），ceil 修复可提升 ChartQA 达 +10.9 pts，且 MiX 增益在其上仍可叠加。

## Key Contributions

- **Identify and quantify "microscaling collapse" in VLMs.** Profiling Qwen2-VL-7B reveals a ~34x dynamic-range gap between vision and text tokens (mean |activation| 0.312 vs 0.0091; p99 2.07 vs 0.061), channel-wise outliers in Q/K and early-token spikes in V; a single outlier hijacks the shared block exponent, leaving 44.8% (MXINT4), 38.4% (MXFP4), and 38.3% (NVFP4) of the Layer-27 Key matrix numerically dead at block size 32, versus 23.9% for MiX.
- **The MiX format inverts the microscaling paradigm.** Instead of sharing one exponent across mantissas, MiX shares one mantissa (with a 4-bit block-max exponent) across per-element private exponent differences. MiX-4.25b matches MXFP4's effective bit-width; MiX-4.5b matches NVFP4's 4.5b footprint without its nested FP8+FP32 two-level scaling, and adds hardware exponent refinement (a ±1 search realized as a single comparator against the 0.5 mantissa midpoint) plus asymmetric zero-encoding that reclaims the wasted -0 state.
- **A multiplier-less systolic accelerator co-designed with the format.** In the dual-format A-MiX x W-MX dot product, the shared mantissa and base exponents factor out algebraically, so each PE ingests a whole 32-element block per cycle and computes sign-inversion -> barrel shift -> 32-wide integer adder tree -> one scalar mantissa multiply -> FP32 accumulate. A fused single-cycle output quantizer re-compresses activations, and a static "prefill-frozen K-mean" K-smoother (from SageAttention) fixes catastrophic attention collapse at negligible cost (6258 um², 1.8 mW).
- **First end-to-end low-bit VLM datapath (to the authors' knowledge).** Unlike MBQ/TLQ/Q-VLM/VEQ, which quantize only LLM linear layers and leave the vision encoder, projector, and attention BMMs (including KV cache) in FP16, MiX quantizes the entire pipeline, making full offload to sub-8-bit edge silicon feasible.
- **Comprehensive, artifact-backed evaluation.** Six multimodal benchmarks across three 7B–8B VLMs, scaling from Qwen2.5-VL-3B to 72B, four text-only LLMs, thirteen formats on a PE-efficiency Pareto frontier, RTL synthesis of 15 systolic datapaths (TSMC28HPC, 500 MHz, SAIF-annotated power), plus a public artifact (Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.21530344, MIT license). Bonus finding: the OCP MXFP4 spec's floor-based shared exponent silently clips the largest element in ~40% of blocks; a ceil fix buys up to +10.9 pts on ChartQA, and MiX's gain is additive on top of it.

## Methodology

The paper is a hardware-algorithm co-design with three tightly coupled layers:

1. **Format design (MiX).** A block of B=32 elements stores a 4-bit block-max exponent Emax and a 3-bit shared mantissa mshared (set to the block maximum's mantissa, which empirically beats mean-, scale-weighted, and MSE-based choices). Each element stores only a 1-bit sign and a 3-bit relative exponent difference Δe, reconstructing x = (-1)^s x (1.mshared)² x 2^(Emax-Δe). The MiX-4.5b variant splits the block into four 8-element sub-groups, each with its own shared mantissa, reaching NVFP4's 4.5b EBW without tensor-level FP32 scaling. A ±1 exponent refinement step minimizes local reconstruction error, which in hardware reduces to one comparator because the decision boundary between adjacent power-of-two bins sits exactly at the 0.5 mantissa midpoint.

2. **Dual-format MiX-MX matmul.** Activations are MiX, weights are MX. Factoring the shared mantissa and exponents out of the block dot product leaves per-element work of mw x 2^(-Δe) — i.e., shift the weight mantissa by the activation's exponent — accumulated in an integer adder tree and scaled once per block. This is the key move that makes multipliers unnecessary.

3. **Accelerator architecture.** A 2D output-stationary systolic array (4x4 PEs, 512 MACs/cycle, each PE ingesting one 32-element block per cycle), MiX/MX memories feeding the array, accumulator memory, a fused dual-format quantizer on the output path, and the static K-smoother. Evaluation spans (a) PTQ accuracy with 128 COCO-image calibration, (b) RTL synthesis with Synopsys DC at TSMC28HPC/500 MHz with SAIF power, and (c) a cycle-level system simulator with LPDDR5X (Ramulator) and instance-specific SRAM parameters, evaluated under an iso-area framework (PEs allocated proportional to area savings, so small 4-bit PEs get more parallelism).

A crucial empirical step: naive sub-8-bit quantization collapsed all formats (NVFP4 fell to 29.28% on ChartQA; MXFP4g16 to 0.0%), traced to channel-wise K outliers. Subtracting the token-averaged K mean (softmax-invariant) restores accuracy; freezing that mean at prefill ("static K-mean") matches the dynamic full-sequence scheme within 1 pt at trivial hardware cost.

## Results & Highlights

- **Accuracy vs format baselines (Qwen2-VL-7B, 6-benchmark average):** 4.5b MiX-INT4g16 scores 69.3% vs NVFP4 69.7% (parity within 0.4 pt) and MXINT4g16 67.2% (+2.1 pts); AMXFP4 leads at 70.5% but its dual-FP8-scale PE is ~2x larger. On MiniCPM-V-2.6, MiX-INT4g16 (66.8%) beats NVFP4 (64.9%) by +1.9 pts; on LLaVA-OneVision-7B it leads by +0.6 pts (61.6% vs 61.0%). FP16 reference is 73.0% / 69.4% / 65.7%.
- **End-to-end coverage beats partial PTQ:** fully-quantized MiX-INT5 reaches 70.8% on Qwen2-VL (vs 73.0% FP16), 67.9% on MiniCPM-V, 62.4% on LLaVA-OneVision — versus LLM-backbone-only MBQ (60.0%) and TLQ (61.5%) on Qwen2-VL, a ~9-10 pt gap at comparable effective bit-width.
- **Quantization fidelity (SQNR, Layer 22 activations of Qwen2-VL-7B):** at intra-block dynamic-range ratio 18, MiX reaches ~28 dB vs NVFP4's plateau of ~21 dB, MXFP4 ~14.5 dB, and MXINT4's ~12 dB floor; on the Layer-27 K matrix, MiX's dead-zone rate is 23.9% vs 44.8/38.4/38.3% for MXINT4/MXFP4/NVFP4 at B=32.
- **PE efficiency (TSMC28, 500 MHz, iso-throughput 512-MAC arrays):** MiX-INT4g16 achieves 9.1 TOPS/mm² and 16.1 TOPS/W vs NVFP4's 7.3 TOPS/mm² and 14.0 TOPS/W (+25% area efficiency, +15% power efficiency) at equal-or-better accuracy; AMXFP4 drops to 4.4 TOPS/mm² / 10.7 TOPS/W. Tier-4.25b MiX-INT4 tops the table at 11.0 TOPS/mm² and 19.1 TOPS/W.
- **System-level iso-area speedup (six-benchmark geomean vs FP16):** MiX-INT4g16 reaches 9.77x on LLaVA-OneVision and 8.09x on MiniCPM-V-2.6, ahead of NVFP4 (8.40x/7.55x), MXFP4+ (9.51x/8.04x), and AMXFP4 (5.85x/5.43x); the token-pruning accelerator Focus collapses from 4.32x on LLaVA-OneVision to 1.94x on MiniCPM-V (worse than INT8), because MiniCPM-V's resampler already compresses images to 64 tokens. Headline: MiX delivers 2.3–4.5x speedup and 1.4–2.9x energy reduction vs Focus across the six benchmarks.
- **Energy:** normalized end-to-end energy is ~0.160x FP16 for MiX-INT4g16 (LLaVA-OneVision) and ~0.188x (MiniCPM-V) — a 5.6–6.6x reduction; MiX-INT4 is lowest at 0.152x/0.178x. Memory-bound long-CoT MMMU converges to ~3.8x for all 4-bit formats (DRAM limit), while compute-bound short-generation benchmarks reach up to 15.8x.
- **Pareto and scaling:** on the composite PE-efficiency metric (TOPS/mm² x TOPS/W), MiX-INT4g16 sits at 147 vs NVFP4's 102 (+44% at higher accuracy); the FP16-to-MiX accuracy gap shrinks monotonically with scale on Qwen2.5-VL (8.0/4.1/2.4/2.3 pts at 3B/7B/32B/72B), and MiX-INT4g16 overtakes NVFP4 beyond 3B (+0.2/+1.0/+2.4 pts at 7B/32B/72B).

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty: genuinely high on the format side.** Inverting shared-exponent microscaling into shared-mantissa/per-element-exponent is a clean, non-obvious idea, and — the paper's strongest point — the format was chosen so the mantissa factors out of the dot product, making the multiplier elimination a mathematical consequence rather than an engineering afterthought. The "first end-to-end low-bit VLM accelerator" claim is plausible given Table I's coverage comparison, though "end-to-end" silently includes a borrowed fix (below).
- **A load-bearing crutch: K-smoothing is not MiX's contribution.** All sub-8-bit formats, including MiX, collapse catastrophically without the SageAttention-style K-mean subtraction (NVFP4 at 29.28% ChartQA, MXFP4g16 at 0.0%). The paper is transparent that this training-free trick is imported, and Table IV shows the format's gain is additive (+3.3 pts for MiX over fixed MXFP4), but readers should attribute the recovery jointly to format + K-smoothing, not the format alone.
- **The Focus comparison is the weakest link.** Focus's accuracy numbers are produced under "its authors' default sparsity-pruning configuration," which deletes ~96% of the visual signal on text-rich workloads (ChartQA -21–40 pts, OCRBench -28–40 pts; MiniCPM-V average dragged to 53.2%, -16.2 pts vs FP16). The collapse is real and diagnostic, but the headline "2.3–4.5x speedup over Focus" is measured against a baseline running un-tuned hyperparameters on workloads adversarial to its design; Focus's speedup numbers also come from its simulator overlaid on the authors' FP16 baseline rather than a re-implementation. The MiniCPM-V result (Focus at 1.94x, below INT8) is the more defensible datapoint.
- **Iso-bitness with MBQ/TLQ is only approximate.** The end-to-end coverage comparison pits MiX-INT5 (5-bit-class) against W4A6/4.88b methods whose numbers are quoted from prior works, not re-run; the ~9-10 pt win mixes format quality, coverage, and evaluation-protocol differences.
- **Reproducibility: excellent.** Public artifact with YAML-driven runs, self-checking RTL testbenches, deterministic greedy decoding (±0.1 pt), and honest hardware requirements (a 180 GB B200 for 32B/72B; ~500 GB RAM at 72B). The caveat is that Part 2 needs proprietary Synopsys tools and a TSMC 28 nm library, so exact um² numbers won't transfer — though the authors correctly argue the ordering across formats is the claim.
- **Rigor of the hardware story is standard-but-not-silicon:** everything is RTL synthesis + cycle-level simulation; no tape-out, no measured chip, and the Pareto composite metric (TOPS/mm² x TOPS/W) is a custom product defined by the authors that conveniently weights their design's dual strengths.
- **Significance: strong for the edge-VLM niche.** The OCP MXFP4 floor-vs-ceil spec bug alone (+10.9 pts ChartQA from one rounding-mode change) is a valuable, independently useful finding for practitioners, and it was discovered because the authors actually re-implemented the spec rather than trusting it.
- **Red flags: minor.** FP16 gap remains 2.2–3.7 pts at 4.5b; the 4.5b tier "matches NVFP4" claim holds on average but not uniformly (NVFP4 wins ChartQA/VizWiz on Qwen2-VL); and the abstract's "equivalent or superior accuracy" is accurate only in the aggregate.

## Strengths

- The algorithm-hardware co-design is causal, not correlational: the format inversion exists precisely so multipliers disappear, yielding a shifter-based PE that is 25% more area-efficient than NVFP4's at matched accuracy and bit-width.
- Diagnosis quality is exceptional: the 34x modality gap, Q/K-vs-V outlier topology split, the 44.8%-dead-layer microscaling-collapse visualization, and the MXFP4 floor-rounding bug together form one of the more careful sub-8-bit failure analyses in recent VLM quantization literature.
- Evaluation breadth is well above the norm: 3 VLMs x 6 benchmarks x 13 formats, 4 text-only LLMs for generalization, 3B–72B scaling sweep, 15 synthesized RTL datapaths, and an honest negative result (MiX trails NVFP4 by 1.3–1.7 pts on precision-heavy text-only reasoning and notably on perplexity).
- Static K-mean is a clever systems-flavored fix: freezing the subtraction statistic at prefill makes the smoothing O(1) per decode step and hardware-trivial (6258 um² / 1.8 mW) while matching the dynamic scheme within 1 pt.
- Fully public, MIT-licensed artifact with per-table reproduction paths and self-checking testbenches.

## Limitations

- No silicon validation: all PPA numbers are 28 nm synthesis plus cycle-level simulation with LPDDR5X modeling; the composite Pareto metric is author-defined and the absolute area/power depends on a proprietary standard-cell library.
- Accuracy still trails FP16 by ~2.2–3.7 pts (6-benchmark average) at 4.5 bits, with larger per-benchmark degradation, e.g., MMMU 46.3 vs 50.4 on Qwen2-VL; the shared-mantissa design trades per-element precision for range, which shows up as 3–4 pt ARC-Challenge deficits and clearly worse perplexity on text-only LLMs (Llama-3.1-8B WikiText ppl 8.93 vs NVFP4's 7.94 and FP16's 6.14).
- The attention-collapse rescue depends on the K-smoother being applied; the paper does not explore cases where the frozen prefill K-mean is invalid (e.g., multi-turn conversations whose later images shift the channel statistics, or vision-heavy decode streams).
- Main results cover 7B–8B VLMs only; the 3B–72B sweep uses just three benchmarks, and reproducing the large end requires a B200-class GPU and ~500 GB host RAM.
- Fairness caveats on baselines: MBQ/TLQ numbers adopted from prior work at slightly different effective bit-widths (W4A6/4.88b), and Focus evaluated at its default (untuned) sparsity configuration on text-rich workloads adversarial to token pruning.
- The claimed speedups partially reflect iso-area re-allocation of small PEs (a favorable but defensible methodology for edge deployments); absolute wall-clock wins on a real SoC with real memory controllers would differ.

## 相关性

该工作直接命中"极端性能优化下的可靠性"这一核心关注点：它系统刻画了亚 8-bit 量化在多模态模型中的精度失效模式（微缩放坍塌、离群值引发的静默下溢与注意力图破坏），并给出了格式级与硬件级的修复方案——对量化误差、低精度数值行为和算术正确性方向的研究是高相关的参考；其"数值格式即硬件可靠性设计"的视角也与推理期数值故障检测与缓解的主题互补。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How does the prefill-frozen K-mean behave under multi-turn or streaming-video settings where later tokens shift channel statistics — does the static scheme need a periodic re-estimate, and what accuracy/latency does drift cost?
- Can MiX's per-element-exponent idea compose with rotation-based outlier removal (QuaRot/SpinQuant), or do rotations destroy the intra-block range structure that MiX exploits?
- What is the bit-flip and DRAM-error sensitivity of the MiX encoding — does a single corrupted 3-bit Δe (a 2^7 range error) cause worse silent output corruption than an equivalent fault in MXFP4 or INT4, and can cheap parity checks catch it?
- Does the shared-mantissa precision penalty on text-only models (WikiText ppl +1.0 over NVFP4) shrink with QAT or a learned per-block mantissa split, without giving up the shift-and-add factorization?
- Could the MiX-MX dual-format scheme extend to MoE VLMs (where expert-routing adds another source of activation variance) or to KV-cache-only quantization below 4 bits for very long visual contexts?
