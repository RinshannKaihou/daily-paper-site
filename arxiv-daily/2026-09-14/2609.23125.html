Key finding · arXiv 2609.23125 · quantisation diagnosis

Perplexity barely moves while retrieval collapses — activation-quantisation damage lives in coordinate-aligned sign patterns, not error magnitude.

一句话总结：激活量化的损伤由坐标对齐的误差符号结构（而非误差幅值）决定——在困惑度仅上升 1.2–1.5 倍时检索能力已从 0.991 跌至 0.554，而在旋转基底下量化（3 平均比特）可将归纳准确率从 0.001 恢复到 0.980。

Critical edge

The AWQ W4A4 external validation occurs at perplexity 2.4–8.8× intact — a regime no one would deploy — and the central intervention is a single-block residual-stream hook, narrower than the linear-layer inputs deployed quantisers actually target.

— Critical Assessment / Limitations
01 Methodology — distilled

A forward hook substitutes the quantised activation Q(h) for h at one chosen block of the residual stream — deliberately narrower than the linear-layer inputs deployed W4A4 methods target. Symmetric uniform quantisation runs with per-token (default), per-channel, or per-tensor scales at three depths across 12 models / 4 families (Pythia 70M–12B, GPT-2, Gemma-2 2B/9B, Qwen3 1.7B/8B) on 128-token OpenWebText sequences.

  • Capability probed with random-token tasks — induction, at-distance copying, key-value retrieval (256 positions) — that a language-model prior cannot fake.
  • The causal instrument is the matched control: zero-mean Gaussian noise with the same per-channel second moment as the quantisation error, plus a sign-randomisation ladder holding every magnitude fixed.
  • A second-order Fisher expansion predicts magnitude-matched perturbations cost the same — the controls reject it.
  • SAE dictionaries localise the damage; random / Hadamard rotations test repair; GPTQ, AWQ, SmoothQuant and Qwen2.5-32B check generality.

  • Perplexity ranks, but misdescribes: over 780 within-model comparisons, the arm it prefers keeps more induction 97.9% / more retrieval 96.0% of the time.
  • Standard diagnostics fail: at worst, perplexity hits 7,437,245× baseline while CKA still scores 0.916; ordering errors — CKA 37.6%, MSE 29.0%, subspace 25.1%.
  • Granularity explains the literature spread: the structure premium runs 1.00 → 299.92 (per-token, 8→2 bits) and is already 31.27 for per-tensor at 8 bits, with MSE agreeing to 0.041%.
  • Magnitude-based protection fails: Fisher-protected subspace leaves induction at 0.007; sensitivity ↔ magnitude rank correlation is −0.050 (top-64 overlap just 0.266).
  • Scale & tuning: Qwen2.5-32B keeps induction 0.983 at 3 bits; instruction-tuned Qwen3-8B collapses between 4 and 3 bits, and rotation recovers it; QuaRot's Hadamard beats dense rotation in hard regimes.
03 Strengths
  • Matched-control design (identical per-channel second moment, sign-only randomisation, partial sign-resampling ladder) cleanly isolates which property of the error does the damage — rarely done this carefully.
  • Random-token probes eliminate the language-prior shortcut that makes natural-language benchmarks overestimate quantised capability.
  • Negative results carry concrete numbers: CKA/MSE/subspace diagnostics misorder 25–38% of comparisons; Fisher-based mixed-precision protection fails.
  • Honest scoping throughout — distinguishes established from not, flags its own non-deployable regime, withheld a mechanistic story its data did not support.
04 Limitations
  • Most measurements perturb a single block via a residual-stream hook; deployed W4A4 quantises linear-layer inputs — the gap is bridged only partially.
  • Core analysis uses four base models (≤2B except Qwen3-8B); the bootstrap resamples four, and the scaling claim rests on a single 32B point.
  • Retrieval scored on 256 positions with worst-case ±0.061 noise against intact 0.27–0.54 — resolution-limited; generalisation models measured induction-only.
  • The 51–75% rotation overhead is a dense-matmul upper bound, not a fused kernel; the repair lacks an MSE-matched noise control; no causal account of why retrieval is more fragile.
05 Questions for further exploration
  • Q1Does the induction/retrieval split appear under QuaRot's or SpinQuant's full activation-quantisation pipeline at genuinely deployable 4-bit perplexity levels?
  • Q2Can a probe-level causal restoration pin down which SAE features carry retrieval — and explain why retrieval is more fragile than induction?
  • Q3How do the findings translate to the linear-layer inputs and KV-cache that deployed W4A4 quantisers actually perturb?
  • Q4Does sign-pattern damage interact with known outlier-channel structure — and can a learned rotation (SpinQuant-style) recover the missing retrieval accuracy?
  • Q5Could a cheap capability-targeted probe suite join the perplexity gate in standard quantisation releases, given the 1.2–1.5× band already hides 0.554 retrieval?
