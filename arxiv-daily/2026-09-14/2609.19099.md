> **一句话总结：** 提出 Andromeda 2——一个在结构化自有实验证据（历史配方、溶解/分散/粒径数据、赋形剂行为）之上进行推理、并把概率优化模型当作工具调用的 agentic 自主配方设计系统；在相同的 96 个配方实验预算下于微型自动化实验室中开发紫杉醇口服 SEDDS，高 AUC 命中率达 50%（对比 Andromeda 1 概率优化平台的 17% 与实验 DoE 的 2%），拿到 12 个满足全部四项 TPP 目标的配方（对比 6 与 0），中位 AUC10–240 为 70.1 vs 12.0 vs 3.5 mg·min/mL；消融实验显示去掉历史证据后均值 AUC 下降 34%（62.0 → 46.2），说明"积累可复用的实验证据"而非单点搜索是该系统优势的核心来源。

> **TL;DR:** Intrepid Labs presents Andromeda 2, an agentic system (in-house LLMs + probabilistic models-as-tools + deterministic feasibility engine) that designs and executes SEDDS formulation batches for oral paclitaxel in a miniaturized robotic wet-lab. At a matched 96-formulation budget it beats its non-agentic predecessor Andromeda 1 and a physically executed DoE campaign on median FaSSIF AUC10–240 (70.1 vs 12.0 vs 3.5 mg·min/mL), high-AUC hit rate (50% vs 17% vs 2%), and full-TPP passes (12 vs 6 vs 0), while max AUC stays comparable to Andromeda 1 (157.7 vs 156.4) — the gain is consistency, not a better single optimum. A controlled ablation withholding historical in-house evidence (which contained no paclitaxel data) cuts mean AUC by 34% (62.0 → 46.2) and hit rate from 50% to 31%, and a selected full-TPP formulation shows apparent effective loading of 19 ± 5% w/w, ~3.3× the 5.7% w/w of a published S-SEDDS. Caveats: FaSSIF-only endpoint (no in vivo), one campaign per arm, and a DoE comparator that skipped conventional expert pre-screening.

## 核心贡献

- **证据接地的 agentic 配方设计系统（Andromeda 2）**：将 LLM agent 的"提议—批评—审查—规划—约束检查"循环置于实验室积累的结构化历史证据（历史配方组成、溶解/分散/粒径/PDI 结果、赋形剂行为、平台测量历史）之上，并可把 Andromeda 1 的概率优化模型作为工具调用，生成可由机器人执行的 16 配方批次。
- **同预算、同实验室、同 assay 的公平实物基准**：在紫杉醇（难溶性"硬 API"压力测试）上，用相同的 96 配方预算、相同自动化平台与 FaSSIF assay，对比 agentic 系统、概率优化平台（Andromeda 1）与物理执行的 DoE 战役——而非仅靠仿真比较。
- **关键实证结论——证据积累带来的是"一致性"而非"更高单点"**：Andromeda 2 与 Andromeda 1 的最大 AUC 相当（157.7 vs 156.4），但中位数（70.1 vs 12.0）与高 AUC 命中率（50% vs 17%）大幅领先，说明 agentic 优势在于持续产出高性能配方。
- **受控证据消融**：仅 withheld 历史证据、其余全部不变的消融配置使均值 AUC 下降 34%（62.0 → 46.2 mg·min/mL）、命中率从 50% 降至 31%；且去证据版本在早期批次可与全证据版持平但随后回退，说明历史证据的价值在于更可靠地解释新生成的实验数据，而非提供一个更强的初始批次。
- **跨 API 迁移设定**：历史证据中不含任何紫杉醇配方（目标 API 数据在战役启动时全部排除），检验的是"跨 API 的实验室通用知识能否支持新 API 的配方设计"。
- **工程与科学分层**：确定性可行性引擎（赋形剂合法性、0.05 组成网格、质量归一、批次内唯一性、机器人就绪）负责硬约束，agentic 层负责科学探索；人类配方科学家仅做安全/可行性审查而非重新设计。

## Key Contributions

- **Andromeda 2, an evidence-grounded agentic formulation design system**: an LLM-agent loop (propose → critique → review → plan → constraint-check) grounded in a lab's structured historical evidence (prior compositions, dissolution/dispersion/size/PDI outcomes, excipient behaviour, platform measurement history), invoking Andromeda 1's probabilistic models as tools to produce robot-executable 16-formulation SEDDS batches.
- **A matched-budget, physically executed benchmark**: paclitaxel (chosen as a hard-API stress test) run with the same 96-formulation budget, same automated design–build–test platform, same FaSSIF assay for Andromeda 2, Andromeda 1, and a wet-lab DoE campaign — comparisons are real experiments, not simulation.
- **Consistency, not a higher peak, is the agentic advantage**: max AUC is comparable between Andromeda 2 and Andromeda 1 (157.7 vs 156.4 mg·min/mL), but median AUC (70.1 vs 12.0) and hit rate (50% vs 17%) differ dramatically; Andromeda 2 produced 12 full-TPP formulations vs 6 (Andromeda 1) and 0 (DoE).
- **A controlled evidence ablation with a clean transfer setting**: withholding historical in-house evidence (which contained zero paclitaxel formulations) while holding objective, design space, constraints, batch size, and workflow constant reduced mean AUC by 34% (62.0 → 46.2) and hit rate from 50% to 31%; the reduced-evidence system matched early batches but regressed later, indicating evidence improves interpretation of new measurements rather than seeding a strong start.
- **Clean separation of scientific search and experimental execution**: a deterministic feasibility engine enforces hard wet-lab constraints (valid excipients, 0.05 composition increments, sum-to-one, novelty/duplicate checks, robotic readiness), while a human formulation scientist reviews each batch as a safety/feasibility gate without authoring or retuning compositions.
- **Fully in-house stack**: language models, probabilistic models, and supporting models are trained and operated on Intrepid infrastructure with Intrepid data only — no external commercial model APIs.

## Methodology

All campaigns ran on the same miniaturized design–build–test platform: each wet-lab batch comprised 16 SEDDS preconcentrates prepared by liquid-handling robots and evaluated by automated dispersion/dissolution, dynamic light scattering (droplet size, PDI), HPLC drug quantification, and structured data capture. The assay is a two-stage biorelevant dissolution protocol — a gastric-fluid stage followed by transfer into fasted-state simulated intestinal fluid (FaSSIF), with HPLC quantification at 10/30/60/120/240 min; the primary endpoint is trapezoidal AUC10–240 of apparently solubilized paclitaxel.

**Andromeda 2** organizes its system prompt into science (SEDDS principles, design space, cross-API historical evidence), decision (TPP objectives, verification tools), and execution (agent role as SEDDS formulation scientist, search strategy, structured output) components. With no campaign measurements yet, the first batch explores first-principles hypotheses; subsequent batches update the agent's working context (no model retraining between batches) and preferentially perturb high-performers (e.g., adjusting 1–2 compositional levers of a winner) while retaining informative boundary probes. A deterministic feasibility engine enforces executable compositions before robotic execution.

**Andromeda 1** (the non-agentic comparator) draws candidates from the same constrained composition space and uses probabilistic surrogate models relating composition to outcomes to select each next 16-formulation batch, balancing exploitation against uncertainty — but it starts each campaign essentially de novo without historical formulation data.

**Comparators and ablation**: a formulation scientist designed the experimental DoE (custom + response-surface designs, commercial statistical software) over the same composition grid and constraints, executed end-to-end on the same platform; simulated non-adaptive and sequential-adaptive DoE strategies were additionally evaluated on a validated in-silico emulator (held-out Spearman 0.89, MAE 12.5 mg·min/mL, top-quartile enrichment 3.02 vs ceiling 4.0). The reduced-evidence Andromeda 2 ablation withholds historical evidence only. Each arm received six batches (96 unique formulations). The four-attribute TPP for paclitaxel: AUC10–240 ≥ 8 mg·min/mL, C240 ≥ 0.04 mg/mL, f240 ≥ 10%, C240/C30 ≥ 0.85. Statistics are two-sided Mann–Whitney U tests and percentile-bootstrap 95% CIs, explicitly treated as descriptive because within-campaign observations are not independent.

## Results & Highlights

- **Median AUC10–240 (FaSSIF, 96 formulations each)**: Andromeda 2 = 70.1 vs Andromeda 1 = 12.0 vs experimental DoE = 3.5 mg·min/mL — a ~5.8× improvement over the probabilistic optimizer and ~20× over DoE at identical budget.
- **High-AUC hit rate** (formulations reaching the pooled upper-quartile AUC): 50% (48/96) for Andromeda 2 vs 17% (16/96) for Andromeda 1 and 2% (2/96) for DoE; full-TPP passes were 12 vs 6 vs 0, and formulations-to-first-full-TPP were 12 vs 39 vs "no pass" (reduced-evidence ablation: 31% hit rate, median 46.1, 53 to first full TPP).
- **Evidence ablation**: mean campaign AUC 62.0 vs 46.2 mg·min/mL (+34% with in-house evidence); the best Andromeda 2 formulation maintained apparently solubilized paclitaxel through 240 min with AUC ≈ 18× the unformulated API control (AUC 9), whereas Andromeda 1's best formulation peaked comparably but declined after 120 min, consistent with precipitation.
- **Within the productive Type IIIB/IV composition region** (LFCS taxonomy), Andromeda 2 achieved a 49% hit rate (bootstrap 95% CI [0.39, 0.59]) vs 29% for Andromeda 1 and 3% for DoE — and region-wide hit rates were 0% (Type II), 6% (IIIA), 35% (IIIB), 32% (IV), so the advantage is not merely finding the right region.
- **Loading context**: a selected full-TPP formulation showed apparent effective paclitaxel loading of 19 ± 5% w/w at the first FaSSIF measurement, ~3.3× the 5.7% w/w reported for the published S-SEDDS of Gao et al. and over an order of magnitude above SMEOF#3 (1.6%) and DHP107 (~1%) — though endpoints differ and the authors explicitly warn against direct ranking.
- The descriptive formulation-level Mann–Whitney comparison (Andromeda 2 vs Andromeda 1) yields p = 9.6 × 10⁻⁸, reported as descriptive only due to adaptive within-campaign dependence.

## Authors & Credibility

The team (Craig, Hickman, Ma, Piché-Taillefer, Allen, Bannigan) is from Intrepid Labs, Toronto — an autonomous-formulation company; the Allen/Bannigan group has prior peer-reviewed work on ML-directed formulation development (Adv. Drug Deliv. Rev. 2021; Nat. Commun. 2023) and the group's prior agentic formulation work on clofazimine appeared at the ICML 2026 AI-for-Science workshop (ref. 16), where a similar evidence-ablation effect was observed. Andromeda 1 is described as deployed across dozens of live development projects, strengthening it as a serious baseline rather than a strawman. The paper is unusually candid about statistics (descriptive-only framing, explicit non-independence of adaptive campaigns) and about endpoint limitations (FaSSIF AUC ≠ bioavailability). As a company-authored systems paper, the underlying LLMs and evidence corpus are proprietary and not independently inspectable; this is an arXiv preprint (v1, cs.LG), not yet peer-reviewed.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: The core idea — grounding an agentic design loop in accumulated institutional experimental evidence rather than starting each campaign de novo — is a meaningful increment over Bayesian-optimization-driven self-driving labs (Cosmic, SDL for thin films, mobile robotic chemist) and over the group's own Andromeda 1; the controlled evidence ablation with API exclusion (no paclitaxel in the history) is the paper's most convincing and least common element.
- **Rigor**: Matched budget, same robots/operators/assay, physically executed comparators, and a pre-specified four-attribute TPP are strong design choices. But each strategy was run as a **single independently initialized campaign** — there are no replicate campaigns, so all campaign-level summaries (medians, hit rates) rest on n=1 per arm, and the authors correctly refuse inferential claims; the impressive p = 9.6 × 10⁻⁸ is only descriptive. The high-AUC "hit rate" is defined against the pooled upper quartile, a relative threshold — though the pool-independent full-TPP counts (12/6/0) corroborate it.
- **Baseline fairness**: The DoE comparator explored the shared composition grid directly without the expert excipient pre-screening and prioritization that conventional formulation development would use — the authors acknowledge this, and it likely inflates the contrast vs DoE (median 3.5). The Andromeda 1 comparison is fairer (same lab, deployed system) and is the one that matters.
- **Reproducibility**: Low-to-moderate. The full pipeline depends on proprietary in-house LLMs, an undisclosed historical evidence corpus, and private infrastructure ("Neither platform depends on external commercial model APIs"), so no external group can reproduce the exact system; the wet-lab protocol, TPP thresholds, composition grid, and analysis choices are, however, described precisely.
- **Significance**: Real but early. The demonstrated advantage is on a physicochemical in-vitro endpoint (FaSSIF AUC) for one API in one campaign; translation to digestion-coupled testing, permeability/P-gp effects (notwithstanding the Vitamin E TPGS discussion), and in vivo PK is untested, and generality across chemically distinct APIs is asserted as future work, not shown.
- **Red flags**: Max AUC parity with Andromeda 1 (157.7 vs 156.4) is honest but softens the headline — the system makes good formulations more often, not better best-cases; the 19% w/w "apparent effective loading" is an operational retrospective estimate (nominal loading × max recovered fraction), not measured preconcentrate content; the 3.3× loading comparison crosses different assays and study settings; and the ablation shows evidence contributes, but does not decompose how much of the Andromeda 2 vs Andromeda 1 gap is evidence versus agentic orchestration per se.

## Strengths

- Physically executed, budget-matched comparison against two credible baselines (a deployed industrial optimizer and a scientist-designed DoE) on the same robotic platform and assay.
- The evidence ablation is genuinely controlled (only historical evidence withheld) and uses an API absent from the history, directly testing knowledge transfer rather than memorization.
- Clean architecture separating scientific reasoning (agent), uncertainty modelling (probabilistic tools), and executability (deterministic feasibility engine), with a human safety reviewer that does not redesign.
- Honest reporting culture: peak-AUC parity disclosed prominently, statistics framed as descriptive, literature loadings presented as context rather than ranking, and limitations enumerated without hedging away their severity.
- Composition-space analysis (LFCS taxonomy, hit rate by type, 16 vs 90 oil-surfactant families explored) explains *how* the budget was spent, not just the outcome.

## Limitations

- One campaign per strategy; no replicate campaigns, so campaign-level variability (and hence reproducibility of the 50% vs 17% hit-rate gap) is unmeasured.
- FaSSIF AUC10–240 does not capture digestion, intestinal permeability, first-pass metabolism, or in vivo performance; no pharmacokinetic data.
- DoE comparator lacked conventional expert pre-screening, advantaging the autonomous arms in that pairwise contrast.
- Evidence for a single, deliberately hard API (paclitaxel); generality across chemically distinct APIs and formulation modalities is unproven.
- Proprietary models and evidence corpus make independent verification impossible; system details (LLM scale, evidence schema) are thin.
- The ablation demonstrates that in-house evidence materially contributes, but cannot attribute the full Andromeda 2–Andromeda 1 difference to evidence grounding versus other agentic-design differences.

## Relevance

这篇论文是 "LLM agent 作为科学实验决策核心 + 自主实验室闭环执行" 的一个高质量实证案例：它研究的正是 agentic 系统如何在固定实验预算下利用积累的结构化证据（工具调用、上下文更新、提议-批评循环）超越贝叶斯优化与 DoE，对关注 LLM agent、self-driving lab 与 ML for Science 的工作有直接的方法论参考价值；其"证据积累 → 跨 API 迁移"的消融设计也为 agent 记忆/经验复用研究提供了很好的实验范式。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the 50% vs 17% hit-rate gap survive replicate campaigns — what is the campaign-to-campaign variance of each strategy?
- How much of the Andromeda 2 advantage comes from evidence grounding versus agentic orchestration itself — would an agentic system without evidence-summaries but with richer in-context campaign reasoning close the gap, or would a non-agentic planner given the same historical evidence match Andromeda 2?
- Does the approach transfer to chemically distinct APIs (beyond the prior clofazimine result), to other formulation modalities, and to digestion-coupled or permeability endpoints?
- Can the system's evidence corpus be characterized — how large is it, what schema does it use, and is there a scaling law between evidence volume and hit-rate improvement?
- Since Vitamin E TPGS appeared prominently in winners and also inhibits P-gp efflux, do the identified formulations actually improve oral paclitaxel exposure in vivo?
- Would replacing the proprietary in-house LLMs with open models preserve the advantage, making the evidence-grounding recipe publicly reproducible?
