Why this matters · 为何重要

Clean-context benchmarks hide the real cost of finding evidence. This audit quantifies that hidden workload — and offers a deployable fix: claim-level receipts.

对构建或评估智能体检索问答系统的读者高度相关：量化了"干净上下文评测严重低估真实文档工作负担"，并给出可操作的评估纪律——claim 级回执、条件感知计分、人工对抗校验。

Critical edge · 关键质疑

n = 41 items × 3 seeds means sampling noise of ±7–8 pp: after two defective items are removed, only 2 of 6 labs' burial drops still clearly exclude zero. Read the panel patterns — not any single model's score.

01 Methodology 方法论

room02 is a closed, fully synthetic finance/insurance data room — quarterly prints, credit memos, IC minutes, mandate documents — authored so every fact's provenance is known. The agent gets read-only room access plus shell tools (bash, python, grep) and must retrieve evidence itself; there is no fixed retriever as in RAG.

41 questions with declared hop depth 2–5 run over 3 seeds per model × condition. Two conditions differ only in what is mounted: clean (gold documents only) vs buried (the full noisy room). Answers arrive via a forced-declaration JSON schema — answer + 0–100 confidence + evidence file list — scored by a frozen containment grader with numeric tolerance.

Uncertainty uses Wilson intervals plus a 20,000-draw item bootstrap; a prompt audit verified 0/41 items leak substeps. A secondary chart-reading quiz (34 questions × 3 variants, image input) covers five panel labs plus Grok-4.6.

03 Results beyond the headline 结果全景

Strategy split under burial

Anthropic / OpenAI convert the pressure into forced declarations (55% / 20% of buried trials); Zhipu / Alibaba / Google / Meta absorb it as committed errors (+8.1 to +17.8 pp).

Depth is the killer — buried depth-5 accuracy

Clean depth-2 is 100% for all six labs; Gemini's clean d5 is 57.1%. d5-buried for OpenAI/Zhipu not reported in the source summary. Clean-band 6.5 pts → buried d4/5 spread 13.9 pts.

Receipt, defined

A citation is a pointer the model asserts; a receipt is a pointer a third party can verify against a frozen file.

Strengths

优势
  • The paired clean-vs-buried design isolates the cost of finding evidence from composing answers — a distinction leaderboards erase (6.5-pt clean band vs 13.9-pt buried spread).
  • Multi-metric, cost-aware scoring surfaces what a single accuracy number hides: forced declarations, committed-error splits, $/correct rising to 6.6×.
  • The grader self-audit is a model of intellectual honesty — it found a defective item actively manufacturing between-lab differences and reports which conclusions survive each scoring choice.
  • The receipts discipline (claim ledger, frozen locators, hash manifests) is concretely implementable and directly answers the Box 1 failure mode.
  • Clean separation of three evidence tiers stops provisional re-scorings from silently becoming "corrected" results.

Limitations

局限
  • 41 items × 3 seeds: ±7–8 pp noise swamps most pairwise comparisons; only 2 of 6 burial drops clearly exclude zero after correction.
  • Synthetic corpus authored and graded by the same hand — transfer to real messy data rooms is asserted by design analogy, not demonstrated.
  • The design cannot decompose failure causes (retrieval vs reading vs combination vs format contract); deferred to future work.
  • Multimodal instrument is unmatched: GLM-5.3 absent, Grok-4.6 added, wave-2 run on a different freeze.
  • Fabrication case is n=1 and self-observed — no fabrication-rate estimate is possible.
  • Containment grading penalizes valid surface variants ("$8.4M" vs "$8.4 million") — frozen accuracies are likely underestimates.

05 Open questions 待探索

  1. How much of the burial penalty is retrieval failure vs. misinterpretation of retrieved evidence — can per-hop logging operationalize the decomposition?
  2. Do the deltas survive a pre-registered exact-match + human-adjudicated grading contract, and does the clean/buried index correlation stabilize (currently −0.47 to +0.77)?
  3. Does the positive cross-lab error correlation (φ = 0.43 frozen / 0.21 adjudicated) persist — and what does it imply for real multi-vendor ensemble benefit?
  4. Can source-linked evidence structure plus training on audited trajectories let a smaller open-weight reader match the flagships on buried evidence (the room03 question)?
  5. Would allowing abstention change the confident-wrong profile, given all six models answered every item?
  6. What happens beyond depth 5 — the 6+ hop chains the author believes hold the remaining "alpha" — and in non-text modalities?
