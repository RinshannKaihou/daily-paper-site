A mildly compressed VLA passed every offline gate — then failed all 72 closed-loop trials. Offline metrics are not an acceptance test.

一句话总结：离线指标全绿的温和压缩 VLA 策略（8 层蒸馏 Octo-Base-1.5，teacher-ratio 0.996/1.000），在闭环执行中 0/72 完全崩溃（teacher 40/72）——仅靠部署环境采集的 324 条成功 teacher rollout 即恢复持平，证明离线验收门不足以放行压缩策略。

…despite offline teacher-ratios of 0.996 (gripper accuracy) and 1.000 (translation-direction error) on 600 held-out trajectories.

Why this matters

If you deploy distilled robot policies, green offline dashboards prove nothing — a few dozen closed-loop rollouts are the cheap, decisive acceptance test.

温和压缩的 VLA 可能离线全绿、闭环类别式崩溃，且只有部署分布上的成功 teacher rollout 能修复——压缩策略必须配闭环验收探针。

Critical edge

Existence, not universality: one model family, one task, one simulator, one distillation seed — no claim that other compressed VLAs share this anatomy.

And the repair bundle is confounded: deployment states, success-only filtering, teacher-consistent labels and visual domain all co-vary — the active ingredient is unresolved.

01 Methodology — the setup
Subject
Octo-Base-1.5 teacher (12 transformer layers) distilled to an 8-layer student by layer surgery {0,1,3,4,6,8,10,11} — 86% of parameters kept (202.5M → 174.1M), latency 135.4 → 124.9 ms (1.08×). Trained 300k steps on Open X-Embodiment: hidden-state cosine distillation blended 50/50 with the diffusion action head's loss.
Offline gates
The model family's own validation utilities over 600 held-out trajectories (Bridge / Fractal / Kuka, 200 each) plus three out-of-mixture probes. The student passes everything.
Closed-loop protocol
SimplerEnv / ManiSkill2, PutEggplantInBasketScene-v0, WidowX arm; 72 configurations (seeds {0,1,2} × 24 episodes). The environment's own evaluator logs staged outcomes: moved → grasped → sustained hold → transported. Teacher repeatability ±1/24.
Causal machinery
An 8-hypothesis ledger (H1–H8), each with one decisive test. The repair is a verified minimal pair: two runs identical in every setting except ρR = 0.5 vs 0 — half of each batch replaced by 324 deployment-environment teacher rollouts (38,880 RLDS frames; recording seeds disjoint from evaluation seeds).

03 Strengths
  • Exemplary single-case causal method: matched controls, pre-committed checkpoint selection, an 8-hypothesis ledger with a decisive test for each.
  • The necessity ladder cleanly excludes additive bias, marginal-statistics deficit, single-channel defect, capacity ceiling and harness defect — before any repair is claimed.
  • Substitution, not addition: the repair design kills the "more optimization" confound; the zero-rollout control at floor is the decisive comparison.
  • Recovery shown on multiple axes — success rate, trace signature, perturbation profile, grasp retention — not one scalar.
  • Evidence-grade discipline: counted outcomes carry causal claims; micro-displacements carry none.
04 Limitations
  • Existence, not universality: one family (Octo-1.5), one task, one simulator; the base rate of this collapse among mild compressions is unknown.
  • Repair bundle confound: deployment-state coverage, success-only filtering, teacher-consistent labels and visual domain co-vary; the active component is unresolved.
  • No second testbed: the teacher itself scores only 1/12 and 0/12 on candidate Bridge tasks, so cross-task replication is left to future work.
  • Simulator-only; the 8-layer "expert surgery" subset is unaudited — the lesion could be an artifact of which layers were dropped.
  • Small-n binomial grain: 24–36 trials per condition; paired-trace claims rest on a fixed four-episode diagnostic panel.
05 Open questions — for further exploration
  1. What is the active component of the repair bundle — deployment-state coverage, success-only filtering, or teacher-consistent labels? Would failure-rollout admixture or student-state relabeling alone reproduce the recovery?
  2. Does the collapse survive a different 8-layer subset of the same teacher, or a different distillation seed — i.e. what is its base rate across mild compressions?
  3. Can the negative, late-heavy z residual be detected from a handful of closed-loop rollouts as a cheap pre-deployment alarm?
  4. Does the same graded-to-categorical anatomy appear on other embodiments or real hardware, where teacher competence permits a second testbed?
  5. Since in-domain offline data improved action marginals without touching success, could marginal-statistics metrics be actively misleading as intermediate training signals?
  6. How small can the rollout set shrink before parity on truly novel layouts degrades? (Selection-set margin 24/36 vs teacher 19/36 hints at layout specialization.)
