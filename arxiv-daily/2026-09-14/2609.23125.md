> **一句话总结：** 本文通过对残差流激活的单块前向钩子干预证明：激活量化的损伤由坐标对齐的误差符号结构（而非误差幅值）决定，在困惑度仅上升 1.2–1.5 倍时检索能力已从 0.991 跌至 0.554（归纳仍保持 0.959），而在旋转基底下量化（3 平均比特）可将归纳准确率从 0.001 恢复到 0.980。

> **TL;DR:** Across 12 LLMs from 4 families (Pythia/GPT-2/Gemma-2/Qwen3) with residual-stream activation quantisation at one block, a perplexity rise of only 1.2–1.5x on OpenWebText leaves induction at 0.959 of intact accuracy while key-value retrieval collapses to 0.554 — and matched Gaussian noise of identical per-channel magnitude keeps 0.681 induction where 3-bit quantisation keeps 0.001, proving magnitude alone does not explain the damage. Quantising in a rotated basis repairs induction from 0.001 to 0.980 at 3 average bits (perplexity 33.1 vs 1,310.6 for uniform; intact 21.6), and the induction/retrieval split reappears under deployed AWQ W4A4 (Qwen3-8B-Base keeps 86.3% induction but only 18.8% retrieval).

## 核心贡献

- **能力特异性诊断**：在困惑度仅上升 1.2–1.5 倍的区间内（22 个设定），归纳（induction）探针仍保持 0.959 的完好准确率，而键值检索（retrieval）已跌至 0.554 —— 聚合指标可以完全掩盖特定计算的近失效；但在 780 次模型内比较中，困惑度偏好的方案在 97.9%（归纳）/ 96.0%（检索）的情况下确实保留更多能力，即困惑度**排序可靠、描述失真**。
- **结构化误差证据**：与量化误差逐通道二阶矩匹配的高斯噪声、以及只随机化误差符号而保持全部幅值的替换，都远比量化本身无害（3 比特下归纳保留 0.681 vs 量化的 0.001）——损伤来自跨坐标的**联合符号模式**（坐标对齐性），而非误差大小。
- **损伤定位**：量化把约 5.6% 的损伤集中在最活跃的前 1% 稀疏字典特征上；CKA/MSE/子空间失真等标准诊断分别错序 37.6%/29.0%/25.1% 的比较，最极端设定下困惑度升至基线的 7,437,245 倍而 CKA 仍高达 0.916；Fisher 敏感性与激活幅值的秩相关中位数仅 -0.050，按幅值/敏感性保护通道的保护性干预修复失败。
- **修复与验证**：在随机旋转基底下量化（不改变误差幅值、只改变坐标对齐）在 3 平均比特下将归纳从 0.001 恢复到 0.980、困惑度从 1,310.6 降到 33.1；该现象在端到端量化、最多 32B 参数模型、指令微调模型以及部署级 AWQ W4A4 下均成立（GPTQ W4A4 则同时损伤两种探针，不出现该分裂）。
- **粒度效应**：结构溢价（量化困惑度 / 匹配噪声困惑度）随缩放粒度变化数个数量级——逐 token 从 8 比特的 1.00 到 2 比特的 299.92，逐张量在 8 比特即达 31.27——解释了文献中数比特级别结论分歧的来源。

## Key Contributions

- **Capability-specific diagnosis**: aggregate perplexity can mask near-total loss of a specific computation — in the 1.2–1.5x perplexity band, induction retains 0.959 of intact accuracy while retrieval has fallen to 0.554 (from 0.991 one band below); yet over 780 within-model comparisons perplexity ranks methods reliably (the preferred arm keeps less induction in only 2.1% and less retrieval in 4.0% of cases), so perplexity is a trustworthy *ranking* signal and an untrustworthy *description* of what broke.
- **Structured-error evidence**: a matched Gaussian-noise control with identical per-channel error magnitude, and a sign-randomised substitute holding every |ε| fixed, both preserve far more capability than the quantiser itself (0.681 vs 0.001 induction retained at 3 bits) — the damage lives in the coordinate-aligned joint sign pattern of the error, not its magnitude.
- **Localisation**: with sparse-autoencoder dictionaries, quantisation concentrates ~5.6% of its damage in the top 1% of features (their natural share is 5.7%), a deadzone rounding bias shifts features in the opposite direction to matched noise (-0.0450 vs +0.0250 at 3 bits), and standard similarity diagnostics misorder the damage (CKA 37.6%, MSE 29.0%, subspace distortion 25.1% of orderings; CKA = 0.916 while perplexity is 7,437,245x baseline).
- **Repair and validation**: quantising in a rotated frame restores induction from 0.001 to 0.980 at 3 average bits (perplexity 33.1 vs uniform 1,310.6, intact 21.6), a Fisher-sensitivity-protected subspace fails as a repair (induction 0.007), the split reappears under deployed AWQ at W4A4, and the pattern holds end-to-end, up to Qwen2.5-32B, and under instruction tuning.
- **Granularity explains literature spread**: the structure premium (quantised perplexity over matched-noise perplexity) ranges from 1.00 to 299.92 for per-token scaling (8 to 2 bits) and is already 31.27 for per-tensor scaling at 8 bits — scale-sharing granularity, usually reported only in passing, accounts for much of the several-bit disagreement between published results.

## Methodology

The paper studies quantisation of the residual-stream activation itself, via a forward hook that substitutes Q(h) for h at one chosen block (not the linear-layer inputs or KV-cache that deployed W4A4 methods target), using symmetric uniform quantisation with per-token (default), per-channel, or per-tensor shared scales, evaluated on 128-token OpenWebText sequences at three relative depths (shallow/middle/deep) across 12 models from four families: Pythia 70M–12B, GPT-2 small, Gemma-2 2B/9B, and Qwen3 1.7B/8B. Capability is measured by three probes built from random tokens so a language-model prior cannot substitute for the tested computation: induction (copy the token that followed the current one at its most recent earlier occurrence), at-distance copying (the same operation over a longer span), and key-value retrieval (recall a value bound to a key; scored on 256 positions, worst case ±0.061). Perplexity is evaluated on the same sequences through the same hook, so every number compares one activation read out two ways. The core causal instrument is the **matched control**: zero-mean Gaussian noise η with the same per-channel second moment as the quantisation error (E[η_j²] = E[ε_j²]), plus a ladder of substitutes (sign randomisation holding all magnitudes fixed, errors moved to different tokens, partial sign-resampling in 25% steps) that isolate which property of the error carries the damage. A second-order Fisher-style expansion (Equation 1) predicts magnitude-matched perturbations should cost the same — the controls test and reject this. Localisation uses SAEs trained for Pythia and released dictionaries for the other families (GemmaScope, Qwen-Scope, Gao et al. GPT-2), comparing keep/zero/restore interventions on feature sets, plus CKA and a per-coordinate sensitivity analysis. The repair intervention quantises in a randomly rotated basis (dense orthogonal, then QuaRot's Walsh-Hadamard construction), and an end-to-end variant quantises every block from the intervened one onward. External validation runs GPTQ, AWQ, and SmoothQuant on pythia-1.4b and Qwen3-8B-Base in weight-only and W4A4 configurations, cross-checked against LAMBADA and four multiple-choice commonsense benchmarks; generality is tested on Qwen2.5-32B and instruction-tuned Qwen3-8B.

## Results & Highlights

- **Calibration (Table 2, OpenWebText, pooled over all models/depths/bitwidths)**: where perplexity has risen by a factor of only 1.2–1.5x (22 settings), induction retains 0.959 of its intact accuracy while retrieval in the same band has fallen to 0.554 (vs 0.991 in the 1.0–1.2x band); model-level resampling gives induction [0.946, 0.981] vs retrieval [0.430, 0.720] — non-overlapping intervals, all four families agree in direction.
- **Matched-noise gap (Table 1, 3 bits, median over 4 families)**: quantisation retains 0.001 of induction accuracy while matched Gaussian noise of identical per-channel magnitude retains 0.681; sign-randomised error retains 0.681 (vs quantised 0.001); retrieval is most fragile throughout (0.000 quantised vs 0.272 Gaussian).
- **Rotation repair (Tables 3–4)**: at 3 average bits, rotated-basis quantisation reaches perplexity 33.1 with induction 0.980 and retrieval 0.694, against uniform quantisation's perplexity 1,310.6 with induction 0.001 and retrieval 0.000 (full-precision perplexity 21.6); end-to-end at 4 bits the gap is perplexity 35.7 vs 20,442.3 with induction 0.968 vs 0.003 and retrieval 0.534 vs 0.000.
- **Ranking reliability**: over 780 within-model comparisons between deployable arms, the arm perplexity prefers retains more induction in all but 2.1% of cases and more retrieval in all but 4.0%.
- **Deployed quantisers (Table 5, Appendix A)**: AWQ at W4A4 on pythia-1.4b keeps 16.5% of induction but only 2.8% of retrieval, and on Qwen3-8B-Base keeps 86.3% of induction but only 18.8% of retrieval; AWQ W4A16 on Qwen3-8B-Base moves perplexity only from 19.9 to 21.0 while retrieval falls from 0.480 to 0.383; SmoothQuant W3A16 on pythia-1.4b moves perplexity from 19.7 to 25.1 while retrieval falls from 0.281 to 0.176. GPTQ W4A4 collapses both probes together (no split), and its LAMBADA collapse tracks the mechanistic damage while four multiple-choice commonsense benchmarks barely move.
- **Diagnostics fail (Section 5.1)**: at the most damaged setting measured, quantisation raises perplexity to 7,437,245x baseline while still scoring 0.916 CKA; across 1,080 quantised/matched-noise pairs, 51% of the time the more damaging arm gets the *higher* CKA, and ordering-by-distance error rates are 29.0% (MSE), 25.1% (subspace distortion), 37.6% (CKA).
- **Granularity structure premium (Table 17)**: quantised/matched-noise perplexity ratio runs from 1.00 at 8 bits to 299.92 at 2 bits under per-token scaling, and per-tensor scaling is already at 31.27 at 8 bits (the two arms agree in measured MSE to a median of 0.041%, so every difference is structure, not size).
- **Protective intervention fails; sensitivity ≠ magnitude (Table 3, Section 7.3)**: holding the top Fisher-sensitive directions at high precision reaches perplexity 324.3 with induction still at 0.007 at 3 average bits (uniform: 0.001); the rank correlation between per-coordinate Fisher sensitivity and mean absolute activation has median -0.050 (range -0.84 to 0.68), and the 64 most-sensitive and 64 largest-by-activation coordinates share a median of only 0.266 members.
- **Scale and tuning generality (Tables 8–9)**: Qwen2.5-32B keeps near-intact induction (0.983) at 3 bits where the four-model set had already collapsed, breaking down only at 2 bits (0.086 uniform vs 0.868 matched noise); instruction-tuned Qwen3-8B collapses between 4 and 3 bits (0.940 retained at 4 bits, 0.007 at 3), and rotation recovers induction there too.
- **QuaRot Hadamard (Section 8.1)**: matches the dense random rotation everywhere and exceeds it in harder regimes — induction 0.649 → 0.955 at per-tensor 6 bits and 0.051 → 0.291 at per-token 2 bits — while the dense-rotation hook costs a measured 51–75% per-block forward-pass latency overhead (an upper bound; the deployable fused kernel was not benchmarked).

## Authors & Credibility

Single-author paper by Anish Sathyanarayanan at BITS Pilani, K K Birla Goa Campus (arXiv:2609.23125v1, cs.LG, 19 Sep 2026). The experimental design is unusually careful for a solo work: matched-magnitude controls, a ladder of substitutes isolating the sign pattern, cluster bootstrap over models, an honest AI-use statement (Claude used for writing/formatting only), and a public code link. The paper is explicitly positioned as a diagnosis paper, not a new quantisation method, and its claims are repeatedly scoped ("in the deployed configurations we tested", "we do not claim this generalises to every quantiser"). The scale check tops out at Qwen2.5-32B with one data point above the four-model core set — appropriate caution is stated rather than hidden.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: the core observation that perplexity can hide compression damage echoes Jaiswal et al. (2024), and rotation-based repair is the mechanism QuaRot/SpinQuant already exploit; the genuinely new part is the *decomposition* — showing with matched controls that the damage is carried by the coordinate-aligned sign pattern rather than error magnitude, and that sensitivity-based protection fails precisely because Fisher directions and magnitude channels are disjoint (median overlap 0.266 of top-64 sets). The finding that GPTQ W4A4 does *not* reproduce the induction/retrieval split while AWQ W4A4 does is a useful caveat against overgeneralising.
- **Rigosity**: strong on controls (matched noise, sign randomisation, matched average bitwidth, same sequences for perplexity and probes), but the central intervention is a *single-block residual-stream hook* — a narrower setting than deployed W4A4 activation quantisation, which targets linear-layer inputs; the end-to-end variant (Appendix J) covers only a partial sweep. The AWQ W4A4 external validation occurs at perplexity 2.4–8.8x intact, i.e. in a regime no one would deploy, so the paper itself concedes the split is demonstrated under deployed quantisers "not at modest cost".
- **Reproducibility**: good — public models, public SAE dictionaries (GemmaScope, Qwen-Scope), OpenWebText, per-bitwidth appendix tables, code linked; but SAE-based localisation claims are family-internal by construction (different dictionary widths/corpora per family), and the rotation-latency number times a dense-matmul hook rather than a real kernel, so the "51–75% overhead" is an upper bound that could mislead if quoted as deployment cost.
- **Significance**: for anyone gating quantised-model release on a perplexity target, the calibration table is directly actionable — retrieval-style computations can be at 0.554 while the gate sees a 1.2–1.5x bump. The granularity result (structure premium 1.00 → 299.92) also explains several bits of disagreement across the literature.
- **Red flags**: the rotation repair lacks an MSE-matched noise control (admitted in Section 11), so part of its advantage could still be magnitude rather than alignment; the restoration oracle is a perplexity-only, not probe-level, diagnostic; retrieval's greater fragility has no causal account (an attention-margin measurement at 32B disagreed with the behavioural result and the mechanistic story was withheld); and the headline "restores induction from 0.001 to 0.980" is at 3 average bits on synthetic random-token probes — retrieval recovers far less completely at the same setting (0.694 single-block, 0.534 end-to-end at 4 bits), which the abstract does state but a skimming reader could miss.

## Strengths

- The matched-control design (identical per-channel second moment, sign-only randomisation, partial sign-resampling ladder) is a clean causal isolation of *what property of the error* does the damage — rarely done this carefully in quantisation evaluations.
- Probes built from random tokens eliminate the language-prior shortcut that makes natural-language benchmarks overestimate quantised capability.
- The negative results are as informative as the positive ones: CKA/MSE/subspace diagnostics misorder 25–38% of comparisons, and Fisher-based mixed-precision protection fails, both with concrete numbers.
- Honest scoping throughout — the paper distinguishes what it establishes from what it does not, flags its own AWQ-W4A4 regime as non-deployable, and withholds a mechanistic account that its data did not support.

## Limitations

- Most measurements perturb a single block via a residual-stream hook; deployed W4A4 methods quantise linear-layer inputs, so the gap between the diagnostic setting and deployment is bridged only partially (Appendix J end-to-end variant, AWQ/GPTQ/SmoothQuant checks).
- The core analysis uses four decoder-only base models (one per family, ≤2B except Qwen3-1.7B/8B); the cluster bootstrap resamples only these four, and the scaling claim rests on a single 32B point with an acknowledged unresolved discrepancy.
- Retrieval is scored on 256 positions with worst-case ±0.061 noise against intact accuracy of 0.27–0.54, so fine-grained retrieval comparisons are resolution-limited; induction-only (no retrieval) results for the two generalisation models.
- The rotation repair's latency is measured for a dense matmul hook (51–75% overhead), not a fused kernel, and the repair lacks an MSE-matched noise control; no causal account is given for why retrieval is more fragile than induction.

## Relevance

这篇论文直接命中"极端性能优化下的可靠性"与"LLM 隐藏状态与可解释性"两个核心兴趣：它量化了低比特激活量化在困惑度几乎不动时对特定计算（归纳/检索）的静默损伤，并用 SAE、CKA 与 Fisher 敏感性等机制可解释性工具定位损伤来源，对量化和低精度推理的可靠性评估有直接指导意义。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the induction/retrieval split appear under QuaRot's or SpinQuant's full activation-quantisation pipeline (not just the rotation borrowed as a repair), at genuinely deployable 4-bit perplexity levels?
- Can a probe-level (not perplexity-only) causal restoration pin down which SAE features carry the retrieval computation, and explain *why* retrieval is more fragile than induction?
- How do the findings translate to the linear-layer inputs and KV-cache that deployed W4A4 quantisers actually perturb, rather than the residual-stream activation itself?
- Does the coordinate-aligned sign-pattern damage interact with known outlier-channel structure (massive activations, attention sinks), and can a learned rotation (SpinQuant-style) recover the missing retrieval accuracy (0.694 single-block, 0.534 end-to-end at 4 bits)?
- Could a cheap capability-targeted probe suite (random-token induction/retrieval) be added to standard quantisation releases as a complement to the perplexity gate, given the 1.2–1.5x band already hides a 0.554 retrieval retention?
