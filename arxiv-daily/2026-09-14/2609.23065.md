> **一句话总结：** 本文用同一个稀疏自编码器（SAE）编码"直接预测"和"思维链（CoT）预测"两次前向传播的残差流，提出因果性指标 Δp——消融两次传播共享的 SAE 概念并测量答案概率的下降；在 5 个 LLM、4 个基准上的实验表明，概念重叠（相关性指标）只能弱预测因果忠实性（lift 仅 1.08），且忠实性随层深变化、在的中后层达到峰值，而因果上重要的共享概念并不总在 CoT 中被口头表达。

> **TL;DR:** This paper recasts chain-of-thought (CoT) faithfulness as *internal concept grounding*: a prediction pass and a CoT-derived prediction pass are encoded with one shared sparse autoencoder (SAE), and a causal metric Δp ablates the shared concept set S∩ to measure the drop in answer probability. Across five LLMs (Llama-3.1-8B, Gemma-2-2B/9B, Qwen3-1.7B/8B) and four benchmarks (GSM8K, LogiQA, OpenbookQA, ARC-Easy), correlational concept-alignment metrics (CC-SAE, Jaccard, Recall) are only negligibly-to-weakly associated with causal faithfulness (binarized lift = 1.08, i.e., high alignment raises the odds of high causal effect by only 8%), whereas Δp(S∩) reaches 0.72 on LogiQA and 0.83 on OpenbookQA for Qwen3-8B versus ≈0 for random, count-matched, and norm-matched control ablations. Layer-wise analysis shows faithfulness peaks at mid-to-late (not final) layers and that causally important shared concepts are not always verbalized in the CoT — a direct signature of post-hoc rationalization.

## 核心贡献

- **将 CoT 忠实性重新定义为"内部概念接地"（internal concept grounding）**：不再从输入-输出行为或输入归因推断忠实性，而是直接检验模型的 CoT 是否调用了与直接预测相同的内部概念，以及这些共享概念是否因果地驱动最终答案。
- **单一共享 SAE 使跨传播概念比较成为可能**：对"直接预测传播"和"先生成 CoT 再预测的传播"在答案位置提取残差流，用同一个现成的 SAE（Llama-Scope / Gemma-Scope / Qwen-Scope）编码，从概念上保证两次传播的特征空间对齐——这是与 Chen et al. (2026) 使用两个独立 SAE 做特征注入的关键区别。
- **提出三类相关性指标（CC-SAE、Jaccard、预测锚定 Recall）与一个因果指标 Δp**：Δp 从残差流中减去共享概念 S∩ 的解码贡献，测量答案概率的下降；Δp≫0 表示共享概念因果驱动预测（忠实），Δp≈0 表示答案由其他路径得出（事后合理化的概念级签名）。
- **对 Δp 做了系统性因果验证**：通过随机特征（条件 A）、Spred 内随机（条件 B）、范数匹配（条件 C）、完备性（条件 D）以及 top-k 幅值上界等对照，证明因果效应特异于共享概念而非消融数量、幅值或任意特征选择；并用 SAE 配置消融（宽度 16k→65k、稀疏度 ℓ0=105→46）排除 SAE 伪影。
- **层级化忠实性分析**：发现忠实性在中后层达到峰值而非最后一层；Qwen3 呈"分布式"忠实而 Llama-3.1-8B 呈"局部化"忠实；规模增大不仅提升忠实性幅度，还重塑层级分布（把低忠实计算压缩到更少、更早的层）。
- **概念 verbalization 与因果重要性的分离**：用自然语言概念描述追踪层级概念轨迹（如 LogiQA 中文民俗案例中从中文实体→中英翻译桥接→抽象学术概念），发现因果上重要的共享概念并不总在 CoT 中被说出来。

## Key Contributions

- Recasts CoT faithfulness as **internal concept grounding**: whether the CoT engages the same internal concepts that support the model's direct prediction, and whether those shared concepts *causally* drive the answer — moving beyond input–output intervention and input-attribution approaches.
- Uses a **single shared SAE** to encode the answer-position residual stream of both a prediction pass and a CoT-derived prediction pass, making cross-pass concept overlap well-defined (prior closest work, Chen et al. 2026, trained two separate SAEs and tested feature *steering*; this work tests *dependence* via ablation).
- Introduces **three correlational metrics** (CC-SAE cosine of SAE activations, Jaccard similarity of active feature sets, prediction-grounded recall) and **one causal metric Δp** that subtracts the decoded contribution of the shared set S∩ = Spred ∩ Scot from the residual stream and measures the answer-probability drop.
- **Validates the causal metric** with an unusually thorough control battery: random-feature sampling (Condition A), pred-only random (B), norm-matched (C), completeness check (D), CoT-only-feature ablation (E), a magnitude-ranked top-k upper bound, plus SAE-configuration ablations (width 16k→65k, sparsity ℓ0 105→46) showing layer-wise Δp profiles are robust (Pearson r > 0.8).
- Delivers the central empirical dissociation: **concept alignment (self-consistency) neither guarantees nor precludes causal faithfulness** — high-CC-SAE examples are only 8% more likely (lift = 1.08) to show high Δp — so surface/representational correspondence is insufficient for faithfulness assessment.
- Provides the first **layer-resolved faithfulness profile**: peaks at mid-to-late layers (e.g., L24/28 for Qwen3-1.7B, L31/42 for Gemma-2-9B on LogiQA), distributed vs. localized faithfulness across architectures, and scale-induced reorganization of the profile rather than a uniform shift.

## Methodology

The evaluation pipeline (Figure 1) runs two forward passes per example. In the **prediction pass**, the model answers directly from the input x, and the residual-stream activation r_pred is extracted at the answer token position. In the **CoT pass**, the model first generates a reasoning chain c (zero-shot CoT prompting), then a fresh forward pass runs on (x; c) and the residual r_cot is extracted at the final answer position — the same *functional* (answer-commitment) position in both passes, following Chen et al. (2026) and Dura et al. (2026). Following Tutek et al. (2025), faithfulness is evaluated only on examples where the CoT and no-CoT predictions agree, to avoid confounding CoT-induced accuracy gains with faithfulness.

Both residuals are encoded by one **off-the-shelf residual-stream SAE** (Llama-Scope for Llama-3.1-8B, Gemma-Scope for Gemma-2-{2B,9B}, Qwen-Scope for Qwen3-{1.7B,8B}), yielding sparse activation vectors a_pred and a_cot. Correlational metrics quantify alignment: CC-SAE (cosine similarity of the activation vectors), Jaccard similarity of the active-feature sets, and prediction-grounded recall (how much of the prediction's concept usage reappears in the CoT pass).

The causal metric then ablates the shared concepts: r'_pred = r_pred − W_dec[a_pred ⊙ m_{S∩}], where m is a binary mask over S∩, and Δp = P(ŷ | r_pred) − P(ŷ | r'_pred). Large Δp means the CoT-shared concepts are causally necessary for the answer; Δp ≈ 0 means the model reaches the answer by other means, flagging potential post-hoc rationalization. The causal role of S∩ is validated with four sanity conditions on the prediction pass (random features from the union, random pred-only features, norm-matched pred-only sampling, S∩ + extra pred-only features), a top-k magnitude upper bound, mirrored controls on the CoT pass, and SAE width/sparsity ablations. Finally, shared concepts are mapped to natural-language descriptions via an automated interpretability pipeline (Paulo et al., 2025) to trace layer-by-layer concept trajectories.

## Results & Highlights

- **Alignment is high but barely predicts causal faithfulness.** On LogiQA, binarizing CC-SAE and Δp at per-dataset medians gives P̄(A|B) = 0.53, P̄(B|A) = 0.54, and lift = 1.08 — high concept alignment raises the probability of a high causal effect by only 8% over baseline, across five LLMs and four datasets (GSM8K, LogiQA, OpenbookQA, ARC-Easy).
- **Δp(S∩) is large and specific; control ablations collapse to ≈0.** In the prediction-pass control study (Table 10), random/norm-matched Conditions A–C yield Δp of only ~0.012–0.096 across the five models, whereas ablating the intersection set approaches the magnitude-ranked top-k upper bound (e.g., Qwen3-1.7B: 0.192 for S∩ vs 0.063/0.059/0.034 for Conditions A/B/C) — intersection features are nearly as causally potent as the best magnitude-selected subset despite not being chosen by magnitude.
- **Cross-path consistency and completeness on the CoT pass (Table 14).** On LogiQA, Δp(S∩) on the CoT pass reaches 0.72 ± 0.41 for Qwen3-8B and 0.52 ± 0.47 for Qwen3-1.7B, versus 0.16 ± 0.24 for Llama-3.1-8B; on OpenbookQA Qwen3-8B reaches 0.83 ± 0.34. The completeness ratio Δp(S∩)/Δp(S_pred) ≈ 0.96 for Qwen3-8B (0.90 for Qwen3-1.7B) on LogiQA, and the Condition-D averaged normalized ratio is 0.907 ± 0.108 — pred-only features add almost nothing.
- **Faithfulness peaks at mid-to-late layers, not the final ones.** On LogiQA, peak Δp occurs at L24/28 for Qwen3-1.7B and L31/42 for Gemma-2-9B; the authors attribute low final-layer faithfulness to output formatting / next-token prediction. Llama-3.1-8B shows the highest layer-to-layer variance, with adjacent layers differing by up to Δp = 0.3 (localized faithfulness), while Qwen3 models stay stably high across most layers (distributed faithfulness).
- **Scaling reshapes the layer profile, not just its magnitude.** Scaling Qwen3 from 1.7B to 8B shifts the faithfulness dip from normalized depth 44% (L12–14) to 23% (L6–8) on LogiQA; scaling Gemma-2 from 2B to 9B reduces the layer-wise coefficient of variation from 23% to 12%. Larger models are also more faithful in absolute Δp (e.g., Qwen3-8B 0.72 vs Qwen3-1.7B 0.52 on LogiQA).
- **Robust to SAE configuration.** Changing SAE width (16k→65k) or sparsity (ℓ0=105→46) keeps per-example Δp highly correlated with the baseline configuration (Pearson r > 0.8) and preserves the layer-wise profile shape and peak locations, indicating the findings reflect model reasoning rather than SAE idiosyncrasies.
- **Concept trajectories dissociate causal importance from verbalization.** In a Gemma-2-9B LogiQA case study, shared concepts evolve from Chinese topic entities (L5/L11) through a Chinese-to-English translation bridge (L21) to abstract academic/assessment concepts (L41); layers tied to question content or answer evaluation show high Δp, and some causally central concepts never appear in the spoken CoT (and vice versa).

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** Genuine and well-argued. The single-shared-SAE design solves a real comparability problem that the closest prior work (Chen et al., 2026, two SAEs + feature patching) left open, and flipping the intervention from *sufficiency/steering* (patch CoT features into a no-CoT run) to *necessity* (ablate shared concepts) is a meaningful conceptual step. The correlational-vs-causal dissociation (lift = 1.08) is the paper's sharpest and most quotable result.
- **Rigor:** Above average for this subfield. The control battery (Conditions A–E, norm-matching, top-k upper bound, SAE-configuration ablations, answer-identity and ubiquitous-feature sanity checks) pre-empts the obvious "is this just ablating lots of features?" objection. However, the SAE-robustness study varies only width and sparsity on essentially one backbone in the main text (Gemma-2-2B; others relegated to the appendix), and Δp inherits the usual SAE caveat that reconstruction loss means ablation removes an approximation of the concept, not the concept itself.
- **A specific data quirk:** following Tutek et al. (2025), evaluation is restricted to examples where CoT and no-CoT predictions *agree*. This is defensible (it de-confounds accuracy gain from faithfulness) but systematically excludes disagreement cases — precisely where post-hoc rationalization is most likely to live — so the reported faithfulness levels may be optimistic. The paper does not report how many examples this filter removes per dataset.
- **Benchmark coverage is thin on hard reasoning:** three of four datasets are MCQA (LogiQA, OpenbookQA, ARC-Easy) plus GSM8K; no MATH, code, or long-horizon reasoning, and all models are ≤9B open-weights — no dedicated reasoning models (o1/R1-style), where CoT faithfulness is most contested. The architecture-level claims (Qwen "distributed" vs Llama "localized") rest on a single model per pattern, so they are hypotheses rather than established family traits.
- **The verbalization analysis (Section 5.5) is case-study based** — one worked LogiQA example plus appendix trajectories — so the headline claim that causally important concepts "are not always verbalized" is qualitatively supported but not quantified (no rate of non-verbalization is reported).
- **Reproducibility:** Good — off-the-shelf public SAEs, public models and datasets, formulas fully specified; an AI-use statement is provided. No code link appears in the main text, which slightly tempers this.
- **Red flags:** None serious. One interpretive caution: Δp ≈ 0 is framed as "unfaithful CoT," but it could also mean the answer is computed via a redundant parallel pathway rather than the CoT being rationalization; the necessity-test framing mostly respects this, but readers should note faithfulness here is defined relative to the shared-concept channel only.

## Strengths

- Clean problem reformulation: faithfulness as causal grounding of shared internal concepts, operationalized with a minimal, well-motivated intervention (single shared SAE + intersection-feature ablation).
- Exceptionally thorough validation of the causal metric — count, selection, norm, completeness, and SAE-configuration confounds are each addressed with a dedicated control.
- Layer-wise faithfulness profiling is a genuinely new diagnostic axis that single-number prior metrics (Lanham et al., 2023; Turpin et al., 2023) cannot offer, with actionable reporting recommendations (peak-layer Δp or layer-mean Δp).
- Strong negative result with practical import: representational self-consistency (CC-SAE and kin) is not a proxy for faithfulness — a warning for anyone using activation-similarity as a faithfulness shortcut.
- Interpretable qualitative bridge: natural-language descriptions of shared concepts tie the causal analysis back to readable reasoning trajectories (including a cross-lingual transition finding consistent with Wendler et al., 2024).

## Limitations

- Restricted evaluation set: only examples where CoT and no-CoT predictions agree; only four datasets (three MCQA + GSM8K); only ≤9B open-weight models; no reasoning-specialized or frontier models.
- Δp operates through SAE space, so it inherits SAE reconstruction error and residual superposition; the robustness check covers two hyperparameter axes but not, e.g., different SAE training corpora or training runs.
- The distributed-vs-localized architecture claims rest on one model per pattern; Llama-3.1-8B's erratic profile could reflect its specific SAE (Llama-Scope) quality rather than the architecture.
- The non-verbalization finding is qualitative (case studies) rather than a quantified rate over the corpus.
- The method requires per-layer SAEs and multiple forward passes per example, so scaling it to long CoTs from reasoning models (where sequences run to thousands of tokens) may be non-trivial.

## Relevance

这篇论文正对着"LLM 可解释性与可信推理"方向的核心问题：如何检验思维链是否忠实反映模型内部计算。其"单共享 SAE + 交集特征消融"的因果干预方法与层级化忠实性分析，对做 CoT 忠实性评估、机理可解释性（SAE 特征导向）以及推理模型可信度研究的读者都很有参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How does Δp(S∩) behave on dedicated reasoning models (o1/R1-style) with long CoTs, and on harder benchmarks (MATH, LiveCodeBench) where prediction-pass accuracy is low enough that the CoT/no-CoT agreement filter would remove most examples?
- What fraction of disagreement cases (excluded here) show low Δp, and does including them change the architecture-level distributed/localized conclusions?
- Can the gap between causal importance and CoT verbalization be quantified into a per-example "verbalization rate," and does that rate predict failure under adversarial hint-injection tests (Turpin et al., 2023)?
- Does the mid-to-late layer peak of Δp align with known circuit locations for answer formation found by activation patching, and can the dip layers (e.g., L6–8 in Qwen3-8B) be linked to specific computational phases?
- Could Δp be used online — e.g., as a runtime signal for selective CoT auditing or for training against post-hoc rationalization — given its cost of multiple per-layer forward passes?
