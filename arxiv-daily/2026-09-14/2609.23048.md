> **一句话总结：** 对一个仅压缩到 86% 参数（12 层→8 层蒸馏 Octo-Base-1.5，离线指标全部通过、teacher-ratio 达 0.996/1.000）却在闭环执行中完全崩溃（0/72 vs teacher 40/72）的 VLA 策略做单案例因果解剖：失败集中于"运输到目标"阶段并伴随负向、晚期加重的 z 残差（约为修复后 10 倍），四种标准疗法（继续训练、加域内离线数据、命令级偏移补偿、命令级钳位）在受控条件下全部失效，而仅把一半训练流替换为部署环境中采集的 324 条成功 teacher rollout（38,880 帧）就恢复到 teacher 持平（held-out 18/36 vs 17/36）——证明离线验收门不足以放行压缩策略，几十次闭环试验就足以暴露问题。

> **TL;DR:** A causal case study of a mildly compressed vision-language-action (VLA) policy: an 8-layer distillation of Octo-Base-1.5 (86% of parameters retained, 1.08× latency gain) passes every offline gate — 0.996 teacher-ratio on gripper-prediction accuracy and 1.000 on translation-direction error over held-out Bridge/Fractal/Kuka trajectories — yet scores 0/72 closed-loop success on SimplerEnv/ManiSkill2 PutEggplantInBasketScene-v0 (WidowX) versus the teacher's 40/72. The collapse is stage-localized (moves the object at 90% of teacher rate, grasps at 55%, transports at 0% in every variant) with a persistent negative, late-heavy z residual (~10× its post-repair magnitude: dz = −0.0067 full-rollout / −0.0103 final-third). Four standard therapies fail under matched controls (continued training 0/24; in-domain Bridge data 0/24 despite improving action marginals; z-offset compensation recovers nothing at any of six offsets while the same perturbations degrade the teacher from 19→0/36; command clamping preserves grasping at 16–17/36 but success stays 0–2/36). A minimal-pair intervention substituting half the training batch with 324 deployment-environment teacher rollouts restores held-out parity (18/36 vs teacher 17/36) and eliminates the trace signature, while the matched zero-rollout control stays at floor. The operational lesson: offline gates, even a family's own validation metrics, are insufficient acceptance tests for compressed policies; a few dozen closed-loop trials suffice.

## 核心贡献

- **一个自然发生的失效案例的完整因果解剖。** 8 层蒸馏的 Octo-Base-1.5 学生模型保留 86% 参数（202.5M→174.1M）、延迟仅优化 1.08×（135.4→124.9 ms），通过了模型家族自带的全部离线验收指标（gripper 预测精度 teacher-ratio 0.996、平移方向误差 ratio 1.000，外加三个域外探索数据集上与 teacher 持平甚至四项领先），却在闭环中 0/72 完全崩溃——作者明确声明离线-闭环分离现象本身不是其贡献，贡献是对这一个自然案例的受控解剖。
- **病灶定位：梯度式退化 + 类别式断崖。** 失败不是均匀的能力丧失：早期阶段仅逐渐退化（移动物体为 teacher 的 90%、抓取 55%、保持 58%），而"运输到目标"阶段在所有训练变体中都是 0%（0/24、0/72）。配对动作轨迹分析分离出特征签名：负向、晚期加重的 z 残差（V1 全程 −0.0067、末三分之一 −0.0103，约为修复后的 10 倍），且在两个继续训练分支中持续存在。
- **四疗法的受控证伪（必要性阶梯）。** 继续同数据训练（+5k/+10k 步）成功数不变（0/24、0/36）；加入域内 Bridge 离线数据使边缘动作统计显著改善（如 gripper close-fraction 差距 −0.161→−0.065）但成功仍 0/24；六个固定 z 偏移的命令级补偿无一恢复（而同样的扰动使健康策略单调退化，排除加性偏差解释）；命令级钳位保住了抓取（16–17/36）却仍 0–2/36，排除单通道缺陷解释。
- **最小对照的充分性修复。** 从 V1 检查点出发的两个训练运行除一个参数（ρR=0.5 vs ρR=0）外完全一致（由执行记录验证）：把每批一半替换为部署环境中 324 条成功 teacher rollout，held-out 成功恢复到 teacher 持平（预选检查点 18/36 vs 17/36；held-out 抓取 34/36 vs 未修复的 17/36），z 签名消除，扰动响应曲线变得与 teacher 同形；零 rollout 对照全程处于地板。
- **对评测基础设施的可操作结论。** 600 条离线轨迹返回 teacher 级分数，24 次闭环 rollout 就把两个策略完全分开，一次配对轨迹对比就定位了签名——离线验收门（包括模型家族自己的验证指标）不能单独作为压缩策略的放行测试，而闭环探针可以非常便宜。

## Key Contributions

- **A causal anatomy of one naturally occurring closed-loop collapse.** An 8-layer distillation of Octo-Base-1.5 (layers {0,1,3,4,6,8,10,11} retained; 86% of parameters, 1.08× latency gain) passes every offline gate the model family provides — teacher-ratios of 0.996 (gripper-prediction accuracy) and 1.000 (translation-direction error) on held-out Bridge/Fractal/Kuka trajectories, plus teacher-matching readings on three out-of-mixture probes — yet collapses to 0/72 closed-loop success versus the teacher's 40/72.
- **A localized, structured lesion.** Early stages degrade gradually (object moved at 90% of teacher rate, grasped at 55%, sustained hold at 58%); transport-to-target fails categorically (0% in every training variant). Paired action-trace forensics isolate the signature: a negative, late-heavy z residual (mean dz = −0.0067 full-rollout, −0.0103 final-third for V1), roughly an order of magnitude larger than after repair and persistent across both continuation branches.
- **Controlled falsification of four standard therapies.** (1) Continued training on the unchanged OXE mixture: 0/24 at +5k steps and 0/36 at +10k. (2) In-domain offline data (dual-stream Bridge): visibly improves marginal action statistics (gripper close-fraction gap −0.161 → −0.065) while success stays 0/24. (3) Fixed additive z-offset compensation over six offsets: recovers nothing (1,0,1,0,0,0 out of 36) although the same instrument degrades healthy policies monotonically — excluding an additive-bias account. (4) Phase-gated command-level clamping: grasping preserved (16–17/36) and net commanded z-delta turns non-negative (−0.0066 → +0.0012/+0.0031), yet success stays 0–2/36 — excluding a single-channel defect.
- **A sufficient therapy as a verified minimal pair.** Two runs of the same script resume from the V1 checkpoint differing in exactly one argument (ρR = 0.5 vs 0): substituting half of each batch with 324 successful teacher rollouts collected in the deployment environment (38,880 RLDS frames, recording seeds disjoint from evaluation seeds) restores held-out success to teacher parity (pre-committed step-8k checkpoint: 18/36 vs teacher's 17/36), eliminates the negative late-heavy signature (dz +0.0009 all-phase), and recovers a teacher-like perturbation-response profile, while the matched zero-rollout control stays at floor.
- **An operational lesson for evaluation infrastructure.** Six hundred held-out offline trajectories returned teacher-level scores; twenty-four closed-loop rollouts separated the two policies completely; one paired-trace comparison isolated the failure signature. Offline gates — including a model family's own validation metrics — are insufficient acceptance tests for compressed policies, and closed-loop probes need not be expensive.

## Methodology

The paper is a single-case causal study built on staged instrumentation and controlled intervention:

- **Subject.** Teacher: Octo-Base-1.5 (12 transformer layers). Student: 8 layers via expert surgery (both boundary layers plus a uniform interior sample), trained 300k steps (batch 128, peak LR 3e-5 with cosine decay) on the Open X-Embodiment mixture, with a convex combination (α=0.5) of hidden-state distillation (cosine distance over observation/task/readout token groups) and the diffusion action head's native loss. "Compression" denotes this whole procedure; its components are never separated.
- **Offline gates.** The model family's own validation utilities over 200 held-out trajectories each from Bridge, Fractal, Kuka (gripper-prediction accuracy; end-effector translation-direction error), extended by a retention sweep over three out-of-mixture datasets (fridge-opening, tabletop rotation, pouring). The student passes everything.
- **Closed-loop protocol.** SimplerEnv / ManiSkill2 (SAPIEN), PutEggplantInBasketScene-v0 with a WidowX arm; gripper binarized at 0.75 (calibrated for the Octo-1.5 family, sanity-checked on the older Octo base). 72 configurations (seeds {0,1,2} × episodes 0–23); staged outcomes logged by the environment's own evaluator (moved / grasped / sustained hold ≥5 steps / transport to target). Teacher repeatability 15/24 and 14/24 on identical reruns (±1 attributed to GPU nondeterminism).
- **Evidence grades.** Counted outcomes (24/36/72-trial successes and stage counts) carry every causal claim; paired per-(episode, time-third) trace statistics carry panel-scoped diagnostic claims; unpaired micro-displacements at the 0.002–0.006 scale are dominated by natural per-episode spread (±0.003) and deliberately carry no claims.
- **Hypothesis ledger.** Eight admitted hypotheses (H1–H8: same-data continuation, offline task-domain augmentation, additive z bias, single-channel z defect, state-conditional policy deficiency, simulator physics, 8L capacity ceiling, harness defect), each with a decisive test and verdict; H5 is the supported proximate account.
- **Minimal-pair repair.** 324 successful teacher rollouts recorded in the deployment environment via env-var-gated hooks (recording on selection-set episode IDs with seeds 100–179, disjoint from evaluation seeds {0,1,2}; held-out IDs never recorded), packaged as RLDS (38,880 frames, verified by whole-stack image fingerprinting, zero round-trip error). Two training runs identical in every setting (seed 42, constant LR 1e-6, batch 128, 10k steps, original distillation objective) except ρR = 0.5 (64+64) vs ρR = 0 (128+0). Checkpoint selection pre-committed on the selection set only; held-out evaluated exactly once per checkpoint.

## Results & Highlights

- **Offline gates all pass; closed loop collapses.** Offline teacher-ratios: 0.996 (gripper accuracy) and 1.000 (translation-direction error); on the battery's best probe (fridge-opening) the student actually beats the teacher slightly (13.3° direction error at 0.914 strict accuracy vs teacher's 14.2° at 0.918). Closed-loop: student 0/72 vs teacher 40/72 (diagnostic protocol); on the instrumented slice (seed 0, episodes 0–23) student 0/24 success with moved 18 / grasp 11 / sustained 11 / target 0, vs teacher 15/24 with 20/20/19/15.
- **The lesion is graded-then-categorical.** Move rate 90% of teacher, grasp 55%, sustained 58%, transport 0% in every variant (0/24, 0/72, and 0 after each retraining). Signature residual: dz = −0.0067 full-rollout and −0.0103 final-third (V1), persisting in both continuation branches (−0.0048 / −0.0047 full-rollout) — roughly 10× the repaired model's residual.
- **Therapy 1 inert:** +5k steps on unchanged OXE gives 0/24 (moved 19, grasp 16, target 0), signature persists at dz = −0.0048; +10k further pure-OXE steps end at 0/36.
- **Therapy 2 inert on success despite moving marginals:** +5k dual-stream Bridge shrinks the gripper close-fraction gap from −0.161 to −0.065, raw-open 0.097→0.046, rotation 0.0053→0.0033 — success stays 0/24.
- **Therapy 3 excluded additive bias:** sweeping z-offsets {0.002–0.020}, 36 counted trials each, yields success 1,0,1,0,0,0 (student floor), while the same perturbations degrade the teacher monotonically (19,16,8,3,0,0,0 across offsets 0–0.008); at offsets ≥0.008 the persistent upward bias prevents grasp formation for every policy.
- **Therapy 4 excluded single-channel defect:** phase-gated z-clamp preserves grasping (16–17/36) and flips net commanded z-delta non-negative (−0.0066 → +0.0012/+0.0031), but success remains 0–2/36.
- **Repair restores parity with completeness:** pre-selected step-8k checkpoint scores 18/36 held-out vs teacher 17/36 (higher neighbor 21/36 at step 10k deliberately not reported post-hoc); held-out grasp 34/36 vs unrepaired student's 17/36. On the fixed 4-episode panel the residual drops an order of magnitude (−0.0067 → +0.0009 all-phase; −0.0103 → −0.00065 late) and the repaired student's dose-response (22,22,14,3,0) mirrors the teacher's (19,16,8,3,0) over offsets 0–0.008, while the zero-rollout control stays at floor throughout (0,2,2,1,0).
- **Cost asymmetry:** 600 offline trajectories returned teacher-level scores; 24 closed-loop rollouts separated the policies completely.

## Authors & Credibility

Single-author paper by Fengze Jia (The Ohio State University), accepted as a poster at the IROS 2026 Workshop on Building Scalable Infrastructure for Robot Learning (ScaleInfra). The credibility of the work rests on unusually careful methodology rather than institutional scale: a documented literature-retrieval protocol (three channels, 1,001 deduplicated candidates, tiered full-text review) backs the novelty statement; execution records (experiment-tracking config and command-line metadata) verify the minimal pair; action-normalization statistics are checked bit-identical (max |Δ| = 0) across compared checkpoints; the supplementary repository (github.com/Xanadum/closed-loop-collapse) provides episode records and metric definitions. The author discloses that the research received no external funding and that compute was paid personally — honest, but also signaling a small-scale, single-investigator effort. Every student non-zero score across the project (1–2/36 range) is disclosed rather than hidden.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** Genuine but narrow. The offline–closed-loop dissociation is explicitly not claimed (AutoEval and imitation-learning compounding-error theory predate it); the contribution is the causal anatomy — stage localization, a trace-level marker, an 8-hypothesis falsification ledger, and a minimal-pair repair — which no prior work combines for a naturally occurring compression-induced failure. It is a workshop paper and reads like one: one case, one model family, one task.
- **Rigor:** High for its scale. Pre-registered-style practices are exemplary: checkpoint selection locked before held-out evaluation, the higher neighboring 21/36 number declined, matched substitution (not addition) ruling out gradient-budget confounds, recording seeds disjoint from evaluation seeds, teacher repeatability quantified (±1/24), and a three-tier evidence-grade discipline that explicitly refuses to make claims at micro-displacement scale. The hypothesis ledger (Table III) is a model of adversarial self-testing.
- **Reproducibility:** Good on protocol, inherently weak on generality. Supplementary records and the GitHub repo are promised; all quantitative claims are counted outcomes. But the central object is a single distilled checkpoint whose failure may be idiosyncratic to one training run — no seed variance over the distillation itself is reported (one seed, 42, for repair; one distillation run), so the base rate of this collapse among 8-layer distillations is unknown.
- **Significance:** The operational takeaway (offline gates are insufficient acceptance tests; closed-loop probes are cheap and decisive) is well-argued and useful to anyone deploying compressed VLAs. However, the "existence, not universality" scoping means practitioners cannot yet estimate how often this happens.
- **Red flags / weaknesses:** (1) The repair bundle confound is real and admitted — deployment states, success-only filtering, teacher-consistent labels, and the simulator's visual domain all co-vary, so the "active component" is unresolved (component arms like failure-rollout admixture or student-state relabeling are future work); the covariate-shift interpretation is labeled an interpretation, not a measurement. (2) No second testbed exists because the teacher itself scores only 1/12 and 0/12 on the two candidate Bridge tasks — the anatomy is anchored to a single simulator scene. (3) The 8-layer "expert surgery" layer choice is unaudited (no comparison of alternative 8-layer subsets), so the lesion could in principle be an artifact of which layers were dropped rather than depth per se. (4) The closed-loop n (24–72 trials, ~50–56% teacher success) gives wide binomial confidence intervals; the paper is careful about this but the repair-vs-teacher "parity" (18/36 vs 17/36) is a coarse grain. (5) Single-stage categorical failure at transport is diagnosed behaviorally; the representational origin ("what the pruned layers lost") is explicitly not reached.

## Strengths

- Exemplary single-case causal methodology: matched controls, pre-committed checkpoint selection, an explicit hypothesis ledger with decisive tests for each, and honest refusal to over-claim (the paper repeatedly demotes its own trace marker from "cause" to "most visible projection of the damage").
- The necessity ladder (four falsified therapies ordered by intervention depth) cleanly excludes the deflationary explanations — additive bias, marginal-statistics deficit, single-channel defect, capacity ceiling, harness defect — before the sufficiency arm is presented.
- Substitution-not-addition repair design eliminates the "more optimization" confound; the zero-rollout control run at floor is the decisive comparison.
- Complete recovery is demonstrated on multiple axes (success rate, trace signature, perturbation-response profile, grasp retention), not just a single scalar.
- The evidence-grade discipline (counted outcomes carry causal claims; trace statistics carry panel-scoped claims; micro-displacements carry none) is a practice worth importing into any evaluation-infrastructure paper.
- Strong operational payoff: quantifies how cheap the decisive closed-loop evidence is (24 rollouts vs 600 offline trajectories that said nothing).

## Limitations

- **Existence, not universality:** one model family (Octo-1.5), one task (PutEggplantInBasketScene-v0), one simulator (SimplerEnv/ManiSkill2). No claim that other compressed VLAs share this anatomy.
- **Bundle confound in the repair:** which property of the rollout data does the work (deployment-state coverage, success-only filtering, teacher-consistent labels, or visual domain) remains unresolved; the paper only partially constrains alternatives.
- **No second testbed:** the teacher's own competence bounds the supply of cheap testbeds within this harness (teacher at 1/12 and 0/12 on candidate Bridge tasks), so cross-task replication is left to future work.
- **Simulator-only:** hardware transfer is future work (a physical WidowX platform is in place); the claim is scoped to this simulator's dynamics, with a residual caveat that the student's earlier actions may steer into contact configurations where physics artifacts trigger preferentially.
- **Representational origin unreached:** the study is deliberately behavioral and systemic; what the pruned layers lost is not identified, and the layer-selection surgery itself is not ablated.
- **Small-n binomial grain:** 24–36 counted trials per condition; paired-trace claims rest on a fixed four-episode diagnostic panel, which the authors themselves note "does not make four episodes representative."

## Relevance

对关注 VLA 模型压缩/蒸馏与部署评测的工作高度相关：它实证表明"离线指标全绿"的温和蒸馏模型可能在闭环中类别式崩溃，且只有部署分布上的成功 teacher rollout 数据能修复——这直接支撑"压缩策略必须配闭环验收探针 + 部署分布数据"的基础设施结论，与 covariate shift / 模仿学习误差复合的经典理论呼应。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- What is the active component of the repair bundle — deployment-state coverage, success-only filtering, or teacher-consistent labels? Would failure-rollout admixture or student-state relabeling (on-policy DAgger-style) alone reproduce the recovery?
- Does the collapse survive a different 8-layer subset of the same teacher, or a different distillation seed — i.e., what is the base rate of this failure mode across mild compressions?
- Can the negative, late-heavy z residual be detected from a handful of closed-loop rollouts as a cheap pre-deployment alarm, before a full success-rate estimate is affordable?
- Does the same graded-to-categorical anatomy appear on other embodiment/task families (e.g., non-WidowX platforms or real hardware), where teacher competence permits a second testbed?
- Since in-domain offline data improved action marginals without touching success, could marginal-statistics metrics be actively misleading as intermediate training signals for compressed policies?
- The selection-set margin (24/36 vs teacher's 19/36) suggests specialization to recorded layouts — how large can the rollout set shrink (or grow) before parity on truly novel layouts degrades?
