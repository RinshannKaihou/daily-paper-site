Why this matters

A deployment-grade recipe for self-improving agents where no test oracle exists: skill banks must grow coverage and reliability together — neither works alone.

对「自进化 agent / 免训练持续适配」方向直接可用：无验证判据的开放域中， 部署级持续学习的完整方案与实证。

Critical edge

The same VLM judge drives both evolution and evaluation — internal gains may partly reflect fitting the judge's habits, and no human study is included.

Also: every comparison is EVOLVE vs its own no-skill twin — benchmark, traffic, and skill bank are all withheld.

01 Methodology

Frozen solver, evolving memory

  • System split A frozen frontier model drives 230+ raster / vector / page-layout tools with iterative + parallel calling, parameter repair, multimodal previews, and deterministic re-rendering of recorded XML snapshots. All learning lives outside the model.
  • Skill unit Each skill is a SKILL.md: trigger phrases (the sole retrieval signal), app mode, a 5–10 tool critical path, and a five-section body. The bank is partitioned into three sub-banks by app mode.
  • Retrieval The brief is distilled to ≤6 operation keywords; token-overlap ranking (deliberately not embeddings) scores the bank. Skills enter context only via progressive disclosure — the agent must call load_skill().
  • Evolution loop Prompter · Solver · Grader · Reflector fire every 50 graded records. Graphic-Eval scores rendered PNGs: s = 0.7·completeness + 0.3·aesthetics, τ = 0.6. Absolute scores only decide what to revise, never what ships.
  • Admission Widening mints skills for subtasks recurring ≥3 times; deepening contrastively rewrites skills after ≥2 failures. A matched replay gate replays frozen contexts pairwise (order-randomized) — a change ships iff it wins ≥1 prompt and loses none.

Win rate vs the no-skill twin, 200 held-out briefs (blind two-order pairwise). Completeness: 68.62 / 69.02 / 69.79 / 74.04 — the interaction is superadditive +3.85 (p = 0.025). Neither mechanism alone separates from baseline; cold start actually scores below it (68.62 vs 69.08).

  • Opus regresses on GenEval2 quality (61.24 → 57.92, −3.32) — the replay gate is case-local.
  • Round 4 dips below the no-skill agent at completeness ≥0.3 (90% vs 94%) before round 5 recovers.
  • Despite +28% prompt tokens for retrieval, EVOLVE uses fewer total tokens than cold start (436.6k vs 445.2k) — fewer corrective retries.

03 Strengths

  • Principled admission rule. Proposal separated from admission, absolute scores banned, zero regressions required across matched replay contexts — the right conservative stance for production agents, grounded in safe-policy-improvement.
  • The ablation is the best science. Neither mechanism alone beats baseline; together they are superadditive — a clean, significance-tested causal story about why skill libraries need both coverage growth and reliability refinement.
  • Engineering honesty. Gate rejection counts, non-monotonic per-round curves, per-benchmark regressions, latency/token accounting as first-class outcomes, uncurated output galleries.
  • Rare negative-results appendix mapping exactly where retrieval-and-injection stops working (default strategies, Droste-effect precision, "vibe drawing").

04 Limitations

  • Retrieval can't override strong defaults. Textual skills failed to change a model's default strategy until combined with preference-conditioned retrieval plus tool-set scoping.
  • Geometric precision ceiling. The VLM verifier passes visibly wrong Droste placements; human-in-the-loop remains the fallback.
  • Long procedures leak fidelity. Losses from annotation, distillation, and frozen-model adherence compound — the full tessellated pattern degrades even with a correct hand-annotated skill.
  • The gate is case-local. Passing replay does not guarantee monotonic improvement over the full user-traffic distribution.
  • External validity untested. No human evaluation in the main results, no head-to-head with external skill-evolution baselines; benchmark, traffic, and bank are non-public.

05 Open questions

  • Q1How would the evolved bank fare against ACE, Dynamic Cheatsheet, SkillAudit, or COMFYCLAW run in the same design environment?
  • Q2Does Graphic-Eval-driven evolution overfit the judge — would a small human study confirm the +13 pp gain at completeness ≥0.9?
  • Q3Can the replay gate be made distributionally safe (high-confidence off-policy bounds) instead of case-local, and at what sample cost?
  • Q4Would embedding or graph-of-skills retrieval change the widening dynamics, since mints currently need refined descriptions just to surface?
  • Q5Superadditivity is observed at 5 rounds — does it persist, saturate, or collapse as bank and traffic grow by an order of magnitude?
  • Q6Can numeric verifiers or deterministic macros embedded in skills close the precision ceiling and extend distillation to long constructions?
