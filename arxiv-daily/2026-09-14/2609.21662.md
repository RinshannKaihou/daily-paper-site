> **一句话总结：** 本文发现 COCONUT 式潜在思维链（latent CoT）上的激活转向（CAA）虽然能把隐藏状态推移到与显式 CoT 相当甚至更大的幅度，但对后续语言生成的影响骤降为原来的 1–5%；作者证明任务信息在连续思维中仍可线性解码（AUROC 0.84–1.00），进而提出"潜在—语言过渡鸿沟"假说，并用两项证据支持：语言生成开始处 token 分布突变（归一化 JS 散度比 CoT 高 13–19 倍）、任务方向的双向转向增益减弱 9–70 倍。

> **TL;DR:** The authors apply Contrastive Activation Addition (CAA) steering at layer 16 of COCONUT-style latent-CoT and explicit-CoT variants of Llama-3.1-8B and Llama-2-7B (both trained on ProSQA, evaluated on sentiment / politics-TwinViews-13k / truthfulness-TruthGen). Despite slightly LARGER normalized representation displacement at λ=10 (13.43 vs 11.93 on politics), latent-CoT retains only 1–5% of CoT's stance-token probability shift (e.g., sentiment stance-margin +0.15pp vs +5.20pp), and steering success rate collapses (sentiment 46.0% → 3.0%). Task information stays decodable (AUROC 0.842–1.000), so the failure is a "latent-to-language transition gap": output distributions jump abruptly at the transition (normalized JS ratio 14.77–24.22 vs CoT's 1.13–1.25 on Llama-3.1-8B) and bidirectional steering gain Godd drops 26.6–70.2×. A crisp, well-controlled negative result that any future latent-steering method must evaluate across the transition boundary, not just in latent space.

## 核心贡献

- **首次系统刻画潜在推理中的转向失效模式**：在 Llama-3.1-8B 和 Llama-2-7B 的 COCONUT 式潜在思维链变体上，用经典 CAA 方法做对比激活转向，发现隐藏状态位移幅度与显式 CoT 相当（甚至略大），但语言生成层面的响应骤减至 1–5%。
- **排除了"信息缺失"这一最简单解释**：用仅方向估计集拟合、符号固定、留出集评估的探针协议，证明任务（立场）信息在连续思维中仍强可解码（两个模型家族 AUROC 0.842–1.000）。
- **提出并用两条独立证据支持"潜在—语言过渡鸿沟"假说**：(1) 语言生成开始处的 token 概率分布相对推理内部变化发生突变（归一化 JS 散度比值在潜在模式下为 5.61–24.22，CoT 仅 0.87–1.25）；(2) 任务相关方向的双向转向增益 Godd 在潜在模式下减弱 9.4–70.2 倍。
- **区分了三个常被混为一谈的性质**：任务信息是否被表征、干预是否移动了表征、移动是否控制生成输出——并论证潜在转向方法的评估必须跨越过渡边界。
- **指出可能的成因**：连续思维没有逐步的 token 级词汇监督，因此不必保持语言生成模式所用的语义组织结构。

## Key Contributions

- **Documents a steering failure mode specific to latent reasoning.** Using Contrastive Activation Addition (CAA) at layer 16 (λ ∈ {±1, ±5, ±10}) on explicit-CoT vs COCONUT-style latent-CoT variants of Llama-3.1-8B and Llama-2-7B (same ProSQA training data, five continuous thoughts), the paper shows that comparable-or-larger hidden-state displacement produces a drastically weaker response in language generation.
- **Rules out missing information as the explanation.** A held-out probing protocol (direction fitted only on the estimation split, sign fixed with labels) shows task information remains decodable from continuous thoughts across politics, sentiment, and truthfulness (AUROC 0.842–1.000 on Llama-3.1-8B; 0.859–1.000 on Llama-2-7B).
- **Proposes and tests the latent-to-language transition gap hypothesis** with two independent measurements: (a) an abrupt change of the token probability distribution exactly at the latent-to-language boundary, quantified by a normalized Jensen–Shannon transition ratio R_transition; (b) weak bidirectional control, quantified by a judge-free odd steering gain Godd comparing +λv̄ and −λv̄ interventions.
- **Methodological takeaway for the field:** steering quality during latent reasoning must be evaluated across the transition to language generation, not by in-space decodability or displacement alone.
- **Offers a plausible origin** — supervision asymmetry: continuous thoughts receive no token-level lexical supervision at each step, so they need not preserve the semantic organization used in generation mode — while explicitly leaving the causal mechanism open.

## Methodology

The study compares explicit-CoT and COCONUT-style latent-CoT variants of Llama-3.1-8B and Llama-2-7B, both trained on the same ProSQA data; the latent variants use five continuous thought positions. Steering directions are extracted via Contrastive Activation Addition (CAA, Rimsky et al.): v(l) = mean(h+ − h−) over N contrastive examples at the generation-start position, applied to the layer-16 residual stream with λ ∈ {±1, ±5, ±10}, with generation settings and quality filters fixed across reasoning modes. Evaluation spans three stance tasks: a sentiment dataset, TruthGen (truthfulness), and TwinViews-13k (politics).

Three complementary measurement layers isolate where the failure lives:

1. **Generated output**: steering success rate (SR) judged by an LLM judge (n = 200/cell) on whether the steered response expresses the target stance.
2. **Language generation (judge-free)**: the stance margin mλ(x) = pλ(V+|x) − pλ(V−|x) over target-stance and original-stance token sets at the FIRST generation position — i.e., before any sampling — so decoding stochasticity and judge bias are excluded.
3. **Representation**: normalized displacement dz, the intervention-induced movement along an independently estimated stance coordinate divided by its unsteered standard deviation (n = 100, politics). This separates "did the intervention move the representation" from "did the moved representation change the output".

Section 3 then runs three diagnostics, aligned by distance-to-language-start (latent thought 5 ↔ last explicit CoT position): (A) position-wise decodability via held-out AUROC; (C) adjacent-position JS divergence between token distributions obtained by pushing final-layer hidden states through the model's own final normalization and LM head (logits verified to exactly match native logits), summarized as R_transition = J(last reasoning→E) / median(J(within reasoning)); (D) bidirectional steering gain Godd = (Δm+ − Δm−)/2λ for unit-normalized directions ±v̄ at the last reasoning position, with paired-sample confidence intervals. All three experiments are repeated on Llama-2-7B as a cross-architecture check.

## Results & Highlights

- **Steering success rate collapse (Table 1A, n = 200/cell).** On Llama-3.1-8B, explicit CoT vs latent-CoT: sentiment 46.0% → 3.0%, truthfulness 24.5% → 6.5%, politics 14.0% → 4.0%. On Llama-2-7B: sentiment 33.0% → 14.0%, truthfulness 56.0% → 36.0%, politics 25.0% → 17.0%.
- **Judge-free stance-margin change (Table 1B, λ = +10, Llama-3.1-8B).** Sentiment: +5.20pp (CoT) vs +0.15pp (latent); politics: +2.10 vs +0.02; truthfulness: +1.50 vs +0.07. On Llama-2-7B: +10.24 vs +2.62 (sentiment), +0.96 vs +0.05 (politics), +0.30 vs +0.17 (truthfulness). Overall, latent-CoT retains only 1–5% of CoT's stance-token probability shift at λ = 10.
- **The intervention itself does NOT fail (Table 1C, n = 100, politics).** At λ = 10, normalized displacement is 13.43 (latent) vs 11.93 (explicit CoT) on Llama-3.1-8B — the latent representation is moved slightly MORE, yet the output response is tiny; the same pattern holds at λ = 1 and λ = 5 and on Llama-2-7B (1.62 vs 1.26 at λ = 10).
- **Task information remains decodable (Figure 2A).** Held-out AUROC 0.842–1.000 across the three tasks on Llama-3.1-8B and 0.859–1.000 on Llama-2-7B, with politics decodability staying high at all five continuous-thought positions — so the failure is not absent task information.
- **Abrupt distribution change at the transition (Figure 2C).** Normalized JS transition ratio R_transition: latent-CoT 14.77–24.22 vs CoT 1.13–1.25 on Llama-3.1-8B (13.07–19.38× larger); on Llama-2-7B, latent-CoT 5.61–7.32 vs CoT 0.87–0.91, consistent across all three tasks under paired bootstrap.
- **Weak bidirectional control (Figure 2D).** Godd on Llama-3.1-8B: CoT 4.47×10⁻²–1.20×10⁻¹ vs latent-CoT 1.45×10⁻³–1.71×10⁻³ (26.6–70.2× weaker); Llama-2-7B shows a 9.4–13.7× reduction across all three tasks.

## Authors & Credibility

Gaoxiang Huang (HKUST Guangzhou) and Lei Qi (Southeast University; corresponding author). This is a 6-page preprint (arXiv:2609.21662v1, 18 Sep 2026, cs.CL/cs.AI). The experimental design is careful for a negative-result paper: it uses two model families as cross-architecture validation, complements an LLM judge with a judge-free next-token-distribution margin measured before sampling, verifies that reconstructed logits exactly match native logits, and explicitly separates representation displacement from output response via an independently estimated stance coordinate. Citations are appropriate (CAA/Rimsky et al., RepE/Zou et al., COCONUT/Hao et al., unfaithful CoT/Turpin et al.). No code release is mentioned in the paper.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty: good and genuinely useful.** Negative/characterization results about latent-CoT steering are scarce; cleanly separating "information present / representation moved / output controlled" is a real conceptual contribution beyond "steering doesn't work on COCONUT".
- **Rigor: strong on measurement hygiene, weaker on causal isolation.** The authors honestly flag the key confound themselves: the explicit-CoT and latent-CoT checkpoints "follow the same training lineage but do not have identical weights", so reasoning mode is NOT isolated as the sole causal variable — part of the gap could reflect different weights rather than the latent interface per se.
- **Ablation coverage is thin.** Everything runs at a single layer (layer-16 residual stream), a single steering method (CAA), and a single latent depth (five continuous thoughts); there is no layer sweep or steering-method sweep to show the failure is interface-general rather than a layer-16/CAA artifact.
- **A plausible data quirk weakens external validity.** Both variants are trained on ProSQA (logical reasoning), but steering is evaluated on out-of-domain stance tasks (sentiment, TwinViews-13k politics, TruthGen) — the latent model's continuous thoughts may be under-adapted to these tasks in a way that inflates the gap.
- **The JS-divergence evidence is partially open to a mechanical reading.** Reading "token distributions" at continuous-thought positions through the LM head is exactly the operation latent thoughts were never supervised to support (the authors' own supervision-asymmetry account), so the abrupt R_transition spike may be somewhat expected by construction; the Godd result is the stronger evidence.
- **The explicit-CoT baseline is itself weak in some cells** (e.g., politics SR 14.0% on Llama-3.1-8B, stance-margin +2.10pp), so "failure" is relative to a baseline that is far from reliable.
- **Family dependence:** the gap is much smaller on Llama-2-7B (e.g., truthfulness SR 56.0% → 36.0%; Godd reduction 9.4–13.7× vs 26.6–70.2× on Llama-3.1-8B), which the paper reports but does not explain.
- **Reproducibility: moderate.** Public datasets and open-weight models are used, and the metrics are precisely defined, but no code is mentioned, the sentiment dataset is not named, and the COCONUT-variant training recipe details are not given in this short paper.
- **Significance:** as a diagnosis paper it delivers value — it tells anyone building latent-space control that in-space decodability and displacement are insufficient evaluation targets. It proposes no fix, and the underlying mechanism is explicitly left to future work.

## Strengths

- Clean three-layer measurement (output / generation distribution / representation) that pins the failure to the transition rather than to the intervention or missing information.
- Judge-free stance margin computed before sampling elegantly removes decoding stochasticity and LLM-judge bias — the primary evidence does not depend on the evaluator.
- Cross-architecture replication (Llama-3.1-8B and Llama-2-7B) across three tasks with paired bootstrap and paired-sample confidence intervals.
- Honest scoping: the discussion explicitly states what the experiments do NOT establish (unique training cause, parallel-path hypotheses) and names the checkpoint confound.
- Actionable implication clearly stated: future latent-steering methods should be optimized for their effect on language generation and evaluated across the transition boundary.

## Limitations

- Explicit- and latent-CoT models have different weights, so the reasoning mode is not isolated as the sole causal variable (acknowledged by the authors).
- Single steering method (CAA), single intervention layer (layer 16), single latent depth (5 continuous thoughts); no ablations over these choices.
- Latent variants trained on ProSQA but evaluated on sentiment/politics/truthfulness stance tasks — a domain mismatch that could amplify the failure.
- No code release mentioned; the sentiment dataset is unnamed.
- Purely diagnostic: no transition-aware steering method is proposed or tested, and the causal origin (e.g., supervision asymmetry) remains a hypothesis.
- Modest sample sizes in places (SR n = 200/cell; displacement n = 100) and small absolute margins on some tasks (e.g., politics +0.96pp even for explicit CoT on Llama-2-7B).

## Relevance

直接命中"LLM Hidden States & Interpretability"方向：论文核心是隐藏状态的探针解码（AUROC 协议）、残差流激活转向（CAA）与表征几何（位移归一化、JS 散度过渡比），是对潜在推理（COCONUT）内部表征可解释性与可控性的一次严格负面结果刻画。对任何想在连续思维空间做探测、转向或表征干预的工作，其"过渡边界必须纳入评估"的结论都有直接的方法论参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would the transition gap persist if steering were applied at other layers, with other methods (RepE, SAE-based steering, act-add on multiple layers), or with more/fewer continuous thoughts — i.e., is it an interface property or a CAA-at-layer-16 artifact?
- Can the checkpoint confound be removed, e.g., by steering a single model that can switch between explicit and latent modes, or by comparing against pause-token/soft-thought variants with matched weights?
- Is the abrupt JS spike at the transition partially a mechanical artifact of reading latent positions through the LM head, given that continuous thoughts receive no token-level supervision at those positions?
- Does fine-tuning continuous thoughts with distillation targets tied to the LM head (token-level supervision in latent space) close the gap?
- What happens on in-domain tasks (ProSQA-style logical reasoning) instead of the out-of-domain stance tasks used here?
- Can a "transition-aware" steering direction be learned — one optimized for end-of-transition output change rather than in-space displacement — and would it recover explicit-CoT-level control?
