from Critical Assessment & Limitations

01Methodology — measure, fix, verify

MEASURECancellation → amplification

At each residual add zout = zin + zup, define forward cancellation C and backward amplification A. ViT early layers cancel hardest (C ≈ 1.7 vs ≈ 1.4 in LLMs); C correlates with A at Pearson 0.99 — correlation, not proof, hence the interventions below.

FIXSign-consistent LRP-γ at merges

Branches whose sign matches the output get weight (1+γ); opposing branches are suppressed.

|Rin| + |Rup| ≤ (1 + 2/γ)|Rout|

Exactly conservative for all γ > 0; amplification bounded (A ≤ 3 at γ = 1) where the standard rule has no finite bound. One backward hook per merge; forward pass untouched.

VERIFYThree evidence types + interventions

Occlusion SRG faithfulness, ImageNet-S localization, FunnyBirds causal ground truth, pixel-level VLM attribution, SAE-feature localization — plus channel-wise interventions isolating the mechanism from generic regularization.

Further findings

  • Ground-truth FunnyBirds: single-deletion 0.774 vs 0.682 (+13%); best on all four completeness metrics and correctness.
  • Causal isolation: fixing only the top 0.5% most cancellation-prone channels → +7.9% SRG; a 150× larger low-cancellation set → −5.5%.
  • Register tokens stabilize the residual stream: across four DINOv2 pairs they cut amplification A (e.g. 1.58→1.45) and ResLRP’s gain shrinks accordingly.
  • Zero tuning anxiety: every γ > 0 beats AttnLRP; untuned γ = 1 reaches 98–100% of best-γ SRG on 5/7 models.
  • The smoking gun: AttnLRP localization on ViT-B/16 (0.32) falls below the random baseline (0.37); ResLRP scores 0.51–0.64.

03Strengths

  • Causally validates a concrete, previously overlooked mechanism — not another heuristic heatmap.
  • Minimal, theoretically grounded fix: exact conservation + provable per-merge bound; drops into existing AttnLRP pipelines.
  • Exceptionally broad evaluation: 7 ViT backbones across 5 pre-training families, 3 VLMs, FunnyBirds, SAE localization.
  • Amplification A doubles as an architecture diagnostic, predicting exactly where attribution degrades.
  • Honest reporting: losses (DeiT3-L/16 localization, ViT-L/14 SRG) stay in the tables.

04Limitations

  • Guarantees are local and structural — not faithfulness guarantees; end-to-end conservation still limited by the AttnLRP attention rule.
  • FunnyBirds ground truth confined to one ViT-B/16 checkpoint; interventions cover only ViT-B/16 and DINOv2-B.
  • Not uniformly best: rollout variants lead DeiT3-L/16 localization; LeGrad leads ViT-L/14 SRG and is “n.a.” on SwinV2-L.
  • VLM SRG magnitudes not comparable across models (tokenizer-dependent occlusion); localization uses only correctly classified images.
  • DiTs, SSMs, MoE unexamined; on DINOv2-B low-cancellation treatment also helps (+8.1%), so mild generic stabilization is not fully excluded.

05Questions for Further Exploration

  1. Does residual cancellation similarly destabilize diffusion transformers, Mamba/SSMs, or MoE backbones — and does the γ-rule transfer?
  2. Can amplification A become a training-time regularizer, unifying the register-token and post-hoc correction perspectives?
  3. Why do ViTs develop stronger early-layer cancellation than LLMs — patch statistics, missing registers, or contrastive pre-training?
  4. Cancellation is necessary but not sufficient (Qwen2.5 dissociates) — what downstream conditions turn a cancelled direction into an exploding one?
  5. Does the fix change conclusions of downstream XAI built on AttnLRP — LLM compliance audits, saliency-aligned RLVR rewards?
  6. Could boundedness extend to an end-to-end guarantee by combining with the CP-LRP attention rule, at a quantified quality cost?
