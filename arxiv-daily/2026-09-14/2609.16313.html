01 — How it works

Methodology, distilled

An untrusted agent proposer faces a trusted TCB — policy resolver, evidence evaluator, certificate mint, gateway, replay store. An action is consequential when its worst-case modeled loss crosses a policy threshold; a six-dimensional risk vector (severity, blast radius, irreversibility, observational uncertainty, dependency exposure, adverse plausibility) selects assurance obligations, each declaring a predicate, accepted evidence classes, a freshness horizon, witness-set constraints, and an optional EFD structural-cut requirement κ ≥ k.

SATISFIED → permit VIOLATED → deny UNKNOWN → defer / escalate

Missing evidence becomes a targeted remediation request, not a silent pass — the evaluator separates “no observation” from “counterevidence.” A permit mints a single-use Ed25519 certificate bound to proposal and witness digests; the gateway re-validates guards live and atomically consumes the nonce (CAS) before dispatch. Unknown guards fail closed.

  • Eval: 13 scenarios × 7 controllers × 30 seeds, order rotated
  • Mechanism-isolating ablations (one removal each)
  • Timing sweeps: 9,000 samples, 3 fresh processes

03 — What holds up

Strengths

  • Authority ≠ readiness (Theorem 1): “who may act” is cleanly separated from “is the world safe to act on.”
  • Ternary discharge distinguishes missing observation from counterevidence — binary policy engines conflate the two.
  • Proposition 1, a transferable negative result: any compensatory scalar score (e.g., additive LLM-confidence proxies) can pass threshold while a required conjunct stays unsatisfied.
  • Evaluation hygiene above the preprint norm: independent effect observer, matched fault injection, frozen JSONL traces with SHA-256 manifest.
  • Unusual honesty — repeatedly states what the results do not establish.
04 — Where it breaks

Limitations

  • Policy-relative guarantees: an authentic-but-false sensor reading or omitted dependency still permits harm; obligation sets are never derived from intent.
  • Only Mode A (check-before-forward) is exercised — guard reads are not atomic with execution; Modes B/C unimplemented.
  • No real system: 13 hand-built failover FSMs, local fsynced replay file; no network, partial failures, or post-forwarding races.
  • Combinatorial core: witness search hard-bounded at 12 receipts; cross-process timing variance warns against SLO readings.
  • No head-to-head with Mnemosyne-style agent transaction runtimes despite a directly overlapping enforcement boundary.
05 — Open threads

Questions for further exploration

  1. Does CAC’s edge survive if LivePolicy simply adds the EFD cut — is the certificate machinery load-bearing on realistic workloads?
  2. Can a polynomial witness-selection approximation preserve the ternary discharge semantics?
  3. End-to-end latency and false-refusal rate with real telemetry and a distributed linearizable replay store?
  4. Do DEFER remediation loops cut token cost and hallucinated evidence for an actual LLM proposer?
  5. Can policy adequacy — complete obligation sets — be semi-automated from incident postmortems?
  6. Do Mode B/C guard-preserving modes compose with the authors’ post-deterministic systems model?
