> **一句话总结：** 该文对四个家族的五个检索嵌入模型（Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B、其微调衍生版、EmbeddingGemma-300M、BGE-M3、E5-base-v2）做穷举式 weight-only PTQ 测量后发现：INT4/g16 基本无损但会悄悄替换 13–22% 的 top-10 结果；INT3 下模块敏感度排序在家族间互相矛盾；INT2 下重建误差几乎相同（0.315–0.337）的检查点检索质量保留率却从 1.3% 到 65.9% 不等——混合精度分配器赖以工作的"按模块敏感度分配比特"前提在嵌入模型上并不成立，而"保护嵌入表"这一公认建议在所有家族中都被证伪。

> **TL;DR:** A negative-results measurement paper that stress-tests the premises of mixed-precision PTQ on retrieval embedders. Across five checkpoints (Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B, a fine-tuned derivative, EmbeddingGemma-300M, BGE-M3, E5-base-v2) and three corpora (BEIR SciFact + NFCorpus, plus the SkillRet agent-skill benchmark, metric NDCG@10), W4/g16 quantization is nearly free (98.7–100.4% retention vs the FP32 baseline for every model), but at W2/g16 checkpoints with almost identical weight reconstruction error (0.315–0.337) retain anywhere from 1.3% (Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B) to 65.9% (EmbeddingGemma-300M) of retrieval quality, and reconstruction error — the standard sensitivity proxy used by allocators like HAWQ-style methods — correlates well with damage only along the uniform bit-width axis (Pearson r = 0.80–0.92) and nearly not at all along the module-selection axis (r = −0.04 to 0.42). If you deploy quantized embedders, read this before trusting any "protect attention / protect FFN / protect the embedding table" heuristic.

## 核心贡献

- **三个工作区间的系统性测量**：在 bit 宽度（W8/W4/W3/W2/三值）× group size（g16/g32/g128）网格上穷举测量五个检查点、三个语料，保留 per-query 分数并做配对 bootstrap 置信区间。结论：INT4/g16 下任何单模块量化在任一 model–corpus 对上的代价 ≤ 1.01 NDCG 点（混合精度分配器几乎无可分配的敏感度差）；INT3 下敏感度出现但排序随家族反转（Qwen 系 FFN 最贵，BGE-M3/E5 注意力最贵，EmbeddingGemma 随 group size 切换）；INT2 下模型崩溃且崩溃原因家族依赖。
- **证伪标准敏感度代理的两面性**：参数加权重建误差能很好预测均匀量化的损害（每个检查点 Pearson r = 0.80–0.92，Spearman ρ ≥ 0.89），但在固定工作点上预测模块级损害几乎失效（INT4/g16 时 r = −0.043，INT2 时 r = 0.412）——而后者恰恰是混合精度分配器实际搜索的轴。跨 bit 宽度池化得到的 0.658 是"范围扩张"造成的假象。
- **"NDCG 中性 ≠ 排序不变"**：INT4/g16 下 NDCG 几乎不动，但 13–22% 的 top-10 被替换、约一半 query 的 rank-10 边界文档被挤出；对照相关性标注逐条清点后发现 97.8% 被逐出的文档是非相关的、relevant-only 重叠保持 0.944–0.970，即金标集幸存而尾部翻搅——这是"幸存 INT2"与"崩溃于 INT2"的本质区别（崩溃模型 relevant-only 重叠跌至 0.001–0.006）。
- **三个因果控制实验**：(1) 配对对照（同一检查点微调 127,190 对前后）表明任务微调最多移动量化容忍度 1.0 点（单臂最多 2.28 点），不制造 INT3 衰退或 INT2 崩溃；(2) 仅把嵌入表量化到 INT2 仍保留 97.6–100.5% 质量（含全模型 INT2 下保留不足 2% 的三个检查点），证伪"词表大小/嵌入表脆弱性解释悬崖"；(3) 强制 mean pooling 不能拯救崩溃模型（Qwen3 从 1.6% 仅升至 9.5%），悬崖成因明确报告为"未解释"。
- **由真实失败构建的可复现性契约**：prompt 前缀不匹配曾制造"量化胜过全精度"的假结果（在一个检查点上损失 8.84 NDCG 点）并触发撤稿式重测；byte 记账本（实测文件字节数 vs 算术预测差 0.04–0.45%）、holdout 隔离（曾因按首个 gold 划分导致 100% 泄漏）、以及公开的逐臂 per-query 测量仓库。

## Key Contributions

- **A three-regime measured map of weight-only PTQ on embedders.** Full grid over bit width and group size, five checkpoints from four families, three corpora (SciFact, NFCorpus from BEIR; SkillRet with 4,392 queries / 6,006 docs pinned to a specific Hub revision), per-query paired bootstrap intervals throughout. At INT4/g16 the largest isolated-module effect on any model–corpus pair is 1.01 NDCG points; at INT3 the module ordering inverts between families (FFN costliest for the Qwen lineage, attention costliest for BGE-M3/E5, EmbeddingGemma switches with group size) and module costs stop being additive (interaction residual changes sign across models); at INT2 checkpoints collapse in a family-dependent way.
- **The standard sensitivity proxy works on one axis and fails on the other.** Weight reconstruction error tracks damage from uniform quantization closely (Pearson r = 0.80–0.92 per checkpoint, Spearman ρ ≥ 0.89) but tracks module-level damage poorly at fixed operating points (r = −0.043 at INT4/g16, 0.35 at INT3/g16, 0.415 at INT3/g32, 0.412 at INT2/g16); the pooled module figure of 0.658 is inflated by range extension. The weak axis is exactly the one a mixed-precision allocator searches.
- **NDCG-neutral is not ranking-invariant.** At INT4/g16, while retained NDCG is 98.7–100.4%, 13–22% of the top-10 is replaced and the rank-10 boundary document flips for about half of queries. Counted against qrels: 97.8% of evictions are non-relevant, relevant-only overlap stays at 0.944–0.970, and Recall@10 moves < 1 point on per-model averages (max 2.17 points on any cell) — the gold set survives while the tail churns. Past the cliff, relevant-only overlap falls to 0.001–0.006.
- **Three causal controls.** A paired base/fine-tuned control (127,190 pairs) shows task fine-tuning moves quantization tolerance by at most 1.0 point (2.28 max on any arm) and does not create the cliff; embedding-only INT2 retains 97.6–100.5% in all five checkpoints (including the three that keep < 2% under full INT2), refuting the vocabulary-size/embedding-table-fragility explanation; forcing mean pooling onto collapsing models does not rescue them (Qwen3: 1.6% → 9.5%), so the cliff is reported as unexplained rather than attributed.
- **A reproducibility contract built from measured failures**: a prompt-contract gate (a train/eval prefix mismatch cost 8.84 NDCG points on one checkpoint and manufactured a spurious "quantization beats full precision" result), a byte-verified size ledger (arithmetic predictions run 0.04–0.45% above packed files), holdout isolation over all golds, and a public measurement repository with per-query scores.

## Methodology

Five checkpoints from four architecture families: Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B (causal decoder, last-token pooling), SkillRet-Embedding-0.6B (that exact checkpoint fine-tuned on 127,190 query–skill pairs with a multiple-negatives ranking loss — included as a paired control and excluded from cross-family counts), EmbeddingGemma-300M (bidirectional encoder, mean pooling), BGE-M3 (XLM-R-family multilingual encoder), and E5-base-v2 (BERT-family, 110M, the smallest). Corpora are SciFact and NFCorpus from BEIR plus the SkillRet test split (4,392 queries over 6,006 documents, verified byte-identical to Hub revision a050ad2, with the benchmark version pinned explicitly because v1 used different counts). Headline metric is NDCG@10.

Quantization is group-wise affine (asymmetric) min–max weight-only PTQ, GGUF-style with FP16 scales/zero-points; the symmetric arm (absmax, no zero-point) serves as a control. Activations stay FP32 — all quality numbers are fake-quantized, and the authors make no latency claims. The grid spans W8/W4/W3/W2 and group sizes 16/32/128, plus module-isolation arms that quantize only the embedding table, only the attention projections, or only the FFN block. Every arm is evaluated on all three corpora with per-query scores retained; differences carry per-query paired bootstrap 95% intervals (on SkillRet, 4,392 queries resolve differences of a few tenths of a point), with Benjamini–Hochberg correction across the 45 INT4 module cells as a robustness check. Ranking stability is measured separately: top-10 overlap with the full-precision ranking, the rank-10 flip rate, eviction composition counted against relevance judgements, and relevant-only overlap. The proxy analysis correlates parameter-weighted relative weight reconstruction error against NDCG loss, split into a uniform axis (bit width/group swept over the whole model) and a module axis at fixed operating points, excluding arms that lost more than half their quality (with sensitivity checks that relax this rule). Three interventions probe causes: the paired fine-tuning control, embedding-only INT2, and forced pooling swaps (mean pooling imposed on BGE-M3/Qwen3, removed from EmbeddingGemma). A code audit (Section 6.1) documents deviations found between an earlier draft and the implementation, including a prompt-application bug that forced re-measurement of E5-base-v2 and EmbeddingGemma and the retraction of the symmetric/ternary arms for four of the five checkpoints after a quantizer defect.

## Results & Highlights

- **INT4/g16 is NDCG-neutral for every model** (Table 2): retained NDCG@10 vs the FP32 baseline averages 98.7–100.4% across the three corpora (e.g., Qwen3-Emb-0.6B 100.4%, E5-base-v2 99.4%); isolated-module effects at this operating point cost at most 1.01 points on any model–corpus pair (largest three-corpus average 0.59). But widening the group to g128 already costs more: E5-base-v2 drops to 95.0% and the fine-tuned checkpoint to 96.5% retention.
- **The INT2 cliff is family-dependent despite near-identical reconstruction error** (Tables 2, 5): checkpoints whose weighted reconstruction error is clustered between 0.315 and 0.337 retain between 1.3% (Qwen3-Emb-0.6B) and 65.9% (EmbeddingGemma-300M) of NDCG@10; BGE-M3 and the fine-tuned SkillRet-0.6B keep only 1.8% and 1.7%, E5-base-v2 44.8%. The uniform-axis proxy remains good per model (Pearson r = 0.80–0.92, Spearman ρ ≥ 0.89, ρ = 1.00 in two checkpoints) while the module axis at fixed bit width never exceeds r = 0.42 and is −0.043 at INT4/g16.
- **INT3 module orderings invert between families** (Table 3): at INT3/g16 the FFN is costliest for Qwen3 and its derivative while for BGE-M3 attention costs 2.46 points against 0.01 for FFN (at g32: attention 5.09 vs FFN 1.14 — the largest single-module effect in the grid); EmbeddingGemma's costliest module is attention at g16 (0.71 vs 0.56) but FFN at g32 (1.67 vs 1.13); the interaction residual I = Δjoint − ΣΔmodule is small at INT4/g16 (−0.15 to +0.17) but substantial and sign-flipping at INT3/g16.
- **NDCG-neutral is not ranking-invariant** (Tables 7–8): at INT4/g16, 13–22% of the full-precision top-10 is replaced (11.6–25.2% across cells) and the rank-10 document leaves the list for roughly half of queries, yet 97.8% of evictions are non-relevant (pooled; 94.8% with equal corpus weighting), relevant-only overlap stays at 0.944–0.970, and Recall@10 moves < 1 point on per-model averages (max 2.17 points on any model–corpus cell). Corpus spread matters: for EmbeddingGemma at INT4/g16 the relevant share of evictions is 0% on SciFact but 15.1% on NFCorpus.
- **Controls**: embedding-only INT2 retains 97.6–100.5% of NDCG@10 in all five checkpoints (BGE-M3 reads 100.5%) even though three of them keep < 2% under full INT2 — BGE-M3's attention alone (2.7% retained) nearly reproduces its full collapse; task fine-tuning moves quantization tolerance by ≤ 1.0 point in two-corpus averages (2.28 max on any arm) and the base checkpoint already shows the cliff (1.6% at INT2); forced mean pooling lifts Qwen3's INT2 retention only from 1.6% to 9.5%.
- **Distil-small vs squeeze-large** (Table 10): the distilled 109M student at INT3/g16 occupies 68.4 MB and scores 78.04 NDCG@10 on SkillRet — within 0.44 points of the 0.6B teacher's full-precision 78.48 (1191.6 MB BF16) — and strictly dominates the teacher's own INT3/g32 arm (297.9 MB at 64.46). But out of domain the student scores 7.79 on NFCorpus where every general-purpose model measured scores 31–39, and the 22M student generalizes better than the 109M (16.9 vs 7.5 on NFCorpus).

## Authors & Credibility

Single-author paper by Hyojung Han of ThakiCloud, an independent/industry lab that also maintains the SkillRet benchmark and released the produced checkpoints (SkillRet-Embedding-0.6B, distilled 22M/109M students, quantized variants) on the Hugging Face Hub, with a measurement repository (per-query scores, byte-verified size ledger, prompt-contract gate) promised alongside the arXiv version. Credibility is unusually high for a measurement paper: per-query paired bootstrap intervals everywhere, BH correction, a paired lineage control, intervention tests rather than correlational stories, and an explicit audit section that documents its own retracted results (the symmetric-quantizer defect, the prompt-application bug that forced re-measurement of E5 and EmbeddingGemma, the holdout leak). The candor about what was retracted and what remains pre-fix is exemplary. The main structural caveats: the evaluation includes the author's own benchmark and own fine-tuned models (though pinned to revisions and complemented by BEIR corpora), and cross-family claims rest on four families — a basis the author himself flags as small.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: The individual ingredients (sensitivity-guided allocation from HAWQ, non-separability from LAPQ/BRECQ) are old; the novelty is the target and the rigor — nobody had systematically tested whether these proxies survive when the preserved quantity is retrieval order rather than perplexity/reconstruction, and the two-axis proxy decomposition (uniform vs module) is a genuinely useful framing. The "NDCG-neutral ≠ ranking-invariant" qrels-counted eviction analysis is the most original piece.
- **Rigor**: Very strong on statistics (paired bootstrap CIs, BH correction, sign checks, normalization robustness) and honest about magnitude-vs-significance. Weak spots: correlation claims rest on tiny samples per cell (n = 12–15 module arms at INT2/INT3, n = 15 at INT4), so the r = −0.04 to 0.42 figures carry wide, unreported uncertainty; the pooled-vs-within-bit argument is correct but the paper never gives CIs on the correlations themselves (only one, E5's [0.48, 0.99]).
- **Reproducibility**: Excellent — pinned dataset revision, byte-verified sizes, per-query scores, public checkpoints, and a documented evaluator bug with its downstream effect on every headline number. Missing: the ternary/symmetric arms for four of five checkpoints were retracted after a quantizer defect and never re-measured, so ternary claims rest on a single E5 arm, and three analyses (E5/EmbeddingGemma top-10 turnover, EmbeddingGemma's rank-flip curve, the pooling intervention) still come from the pre-fix evaluator.
- **Significance**: Practically important for anyone serving quantized embedders (INT4/g16 fine, INT2 is a lottery) and conceptually important as a constraint on what mixed-precision PTQ methods can promise. It does not implement or benchmark any allocator — the INT4 conclusion is about headroom, not about whether GPTQ/AWQ-style methods fail.
- **Red flags**: Only three retrieval corpora, no full MTEB, and no multilingual eval even though BGE-M3 is a multilingual model — its multilingual capability, arguably a key reason to deploy it, is never exercised, so the INT2 collapse verdict for BGE-M3 is monolingual-only. The "distil small" comparison is confounded (students distilled on SkillRet pairs alone), which the paper concedes. The correlation between vocabulary size and outcome is dismissed with five points. The embedding-table rule ("never the dominant protection priority") is tested only under min–max affine quantization; other quantizers (e.g., AWQ-style salient-channel protection) are never run, so the refutation targets uniform PTQ, not the stronger methods it is rhetorically aimed at.

## Strengths

- Exhaustive, pre-registered-feeling measurement design: full bit-width × group-size grid, module isolation, symmetric control, per-query scores retained and re-analyzable.
- Statistical care rare in compression papers: paired bootstrap intervals, BH-corrected p-values, explicit magnitude-vs-significance distinction, robustness to normalization choices.
- Causal controls instead of correlations: paired fine-tune control, embedding-only INT2, pooling intervention — and the discipline to report the cliff as "unexplained" when the necessity test could not be run cleanly.
- The qrels-counted eviction analysis (what does NDCG not see) is directly useful to practitioners deciding whether INT4 is "free" for their RAG pipeline.
- Exceptional transparency: an audit section listing its own errors, retractions, and which numbers changed by how much; artifacts and byte-level size ledger released.

## Limitations

- Three corpora only (SciFact, NFCorpus, SkillRet) — no MTEB, no multilingual retrieval despite a multilingual checkpoint; NFCorpus is small and the hardest corpus, dominating eviction variance.
- Weight-only fake quantization with FP32 activations: no integer-kernel latency and no end-to-end deployed accuracy; storage savings do not imply CPU speedup (the paper defers to T-MAC-style kernels).
- No mixed-precision allocator (GPTQ/AWQ/OmniQuant-style) is actually implemented or compared — the paper measures the premise, not the methods.
- Tokenization behaviour (segmentation, vocabulary coverage of the eval set, sequence-length inflation) is explicitly untested, leaving the INT2 cliff mechanism open.
- Four families is a thin basis for cross-family generalization, and the symmetric/ternary grid for four checkpoints was retracted and not re-measured; the ranking-churn table omits E5 entirely (rank-preservation arm never measured).

## Relevance

对我们关注的高效模型部署与检索系统方向高度相关：如果你在边缘端或 agent 关键路径上用量化嵌入模型（INT4 可放心、INT2 需按家族实测），或者在做混合精度量化/检索感知压缩方法研究，这篇论文的实测地图和"哪个前提失效"的结论可以直接指导实验设计与避坑。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would the module-sensitivity orderings and the INT2 cliff persist under stronger quantizers (GPTQ with Hessian compensation, AWQ salient-channel protection, OmniQuant-optimized clipping), or is the failure specific to min–max affine PTQ?
- What mechanistic property actually separates the INT2 survivors from the collapsers — is it pretraining objective, data scale, activation outliers, or representation isotropy — and can it be predicted from the FP32 checkpoint without quantizing?
- Does the "NDCG-neutral but 13–22% top-10 churn" finding matter downstream, e.g., for RAG answer quality, agent skill selection, or LLM rerankers that consume the top-10?
- Does the family-dependence hold on full MTEB and multilingual retrieval (especially for BGE-M3), or do the rankings of robustness change once the eval distribution shifts?
- Can a ranking-aware sensitivity signal (e.g., boundary-margin-weighted or qrels-aware losses in the JPQ/RepCONC spirit) be built that beats reconstruction error on the module axis where the cheap proxy fails (r ≤ 0.42)?
