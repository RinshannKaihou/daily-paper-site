Self-reported · Critical Assessment & Limitations

Methodology · distilled

Population search over three LLM agents

Hypothesis discovery is formalized as finite population search: keep μ=6 structured hypotheses, generate λ=6 offspring per generation via semantic crossover/mutation, score the pooled parent+offspring set by pairwise comparison, and truncate to the top μ ((μ+λ) replacement) for G=3 generations. Three gpt-5.4-mini agents supply the science — their prompts and judging criteria stay decoupled from the search rules, so collaboration design itself becomes an experimental variable.

Generation · seed from literature→ Pairwise scorer · Bradley-Terry fitness→ Evolution · semantic crossover / mutation→ Truncate · top μ of μ+λ

The scorer judges cancer specificity, target evidence, and falsifiability; the evolution agent applies four operators — combination & inspiration crossover, drug-substitution & out-of-box mutation — acting on claims and rationales, not strings. External evidence (DepMap CRISPR selectivity, Open Targets association) is computed strictly post-hoc and never enters fitness.

Open Targets margin over ToT is not statistically resolved (+0.097, p=0.126, 14W/10L); the DepMap margin is (15W/7L, p=0.011).

  • Held-out generalization (27 types, dev set excluded): +0.280 Open Targets (p=8.2×10⁻⁷) and +0.111 DepMap vs single-pass.
  • Search design matters — swapping fitness-guided for uniform parent selection drops the population minimum by 0.088 (DepMap) / 0.218 (Open Targets).
  • Improves across generations: internal fitness rises in 94/94 runs (mean 50.0 → 112.2, +124%); cancer-specificity residual up in 21/31 (DepMap) and 25/34 (Open Targets) types.
  • Largest gain in genetic-association evidence +0.334 vs single-pass — beyond channels that directly document drug–disease pairs.
  • Cost: 206 calls · $0.56 per run — cheaper than co-scientist ($0.71) and reranking ($0.55), 6× Tree of Thoughts ($0.09).
Strengths

Discipline where it counts

  • First controlled ablation of collaboration design — fitness-guided vs uniform parent selection moves real external metrics with everything else fixed.
  • Evaluation hygiene as a field template: post-hoc external metrics, held-out cancer types, paired win/loss statistics, Holm correction, cost accounting, adversarial controls — including ones that favor baselines.
  • The DepMap selectivity metric fixes the degenerate raw-dependency score that ranks THALIDOMIDE first in 30/31 cancer types.
  • Auditable lineages and interpretable mechanism merging (e.g., BAP1-deficiency × YAP-survival crossover for VORINOSTAT in uveal melanoma).
  • Cheaper than co-scientist and static reranking while outscoring all six baselines on both metrics.
Limitations

Read it with eyes open

  • Central mechanism claim under-supported: no crossover/mutation knock-out survives Holm correction (8 types, 1 seed); the no-operator condition mixes signs (+0.034 DepMap, −0.121 Open Targets).
  • Open Targets partly measures drug fame — the frozen-constant-drug control wins held-out; prior LLM exposure may inflate all evidence channels.
  • Single backbone (gpt-5.4-mini), single domain (oncology, 61 FDA drugs) — no transfer experiments despite the general framing.
  • Quadratic pairwise cost (167/206 calls) limits larger populations; μ=10 / G=5 give no significant gain.
  • Retrospective database matching only — case studies are known biology: "rediscovery plus refinement," not novel discovery; no wet-lab validation.
Open questions · for further exploration

Where this line of work goes next

  1. Would fitness-guided selection survive sparse or approximate pairwise schedules — the quadratic cost bottleneck the authors themselves flag?
  2. Can Open Targets be debiased (approval-date stratification, genetic-channel restriction) to remove prior-exposure contamination and the constant-drug failure mode?
  3. Does the framework transfer beyond the 61-drug vocabulary to open-ended molecular proposals, where validation oracles are weaker?
  4. How much crossover benefit is genuine mechanism integration vs reranking pressure — would quality-diversity replacement change the answer?
  5. Can sequential falsification or wet-lab feedback enter the fitness loop without breaking the search/evidence separation that makes the ablations interpretable?
