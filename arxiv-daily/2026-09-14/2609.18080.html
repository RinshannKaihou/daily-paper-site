Why this matters

For anyone attributing model behavior via probes: geometry alone selects the wrong features. Combine probe alignment with activation statistics — and verify the signal survives SAE reconstruction first.

对 LLM 内部表示与因果干预评估：给出了一套干净的可操作规程与需要警惕的陷阱。

Critical edge

Single-case-study design: the lone auxiliary test (reward-hacking code probe) shows the pipeline failing there — the headline dissociation rests on one model, one layer, one toy domain.

SAE reconstruction retained only 26% of that probe's margin; raw-activation flips ≈ 2%.

Methodology, distilled

Runs on Gemma-2-9B-Instruct (bf16) in the instructed-deception setting of Long et al. (2025): factual statements with Truthful/Deceptive/Neutral instructions. Activations are taken at the final prompt token of layer 20, matching the GemmaScope SAE of width 16,384. The probe is the replicated TTPD truth direction (AUROC 0.98); the hyperparameter-unstable LR variant is excluded (top-30 Jaccard 0.07–0.62 across regularizations).

The same features are ranked twice — by geometric probe alignment c = w·d (activation-free) and by dataset-averaged gradients of the True/False margin — then tested by direct ablation: subtracting f·d contributions for probe-attributed, gradient-sensitive, shared, probe-only and random sets at K ∈ {32…1024}. A learned sparse mask (sigmoid weights, ℓ1 penalty, Adam) adds activation awareness.

Gates: output coherence (1.00 throughout), reconstruction cosine 0.89 / MSE 0.21, residual-norm (5.7% removed for shared vs 13.9% geometric) and KL controls (0.11 nats). 1,600 statements, twelve category sets, held-out split, five seeds.

Questions for further exploration

  1. Does the dissociation replicate at other layers (the actual layer-21 probing peak), other SAE widths, other model families (Llama-3.1-8B, layer-14 peak)?
  2. Can the activation-aware sparse mask work as a general feature-selection tool — against supervised dictionaries (Makelov et al.) or activation-magnitude ranking?
  3. Are the True→False-dominant flips specific to the deceptive-instruction condition?
  4. Why does the shared-set effect peak at ~96 features then self-cancel — sign-incoherent counter-directions (polarity, negation)?
  5. Would a finer SAE recover the reward-hacking code-probe margin, or is that signal inherently non-sparse?
  6. Can coherence-gated intervention extend to graded metrics (unfaithful chain-of-thought rates) in open-ended generation?

Strengths what holds up

  • Crisp, falsifiable question; two-ranking-plus-intervention design with every comparison size-matched and coherence-gated.
  • Unusually thorough controls for the venue: chance baselines, five seeds, held-out split, KL/norm/reconstruction checks, a direction-level ceiling, per-category and directional-flip analysis.
  • Constructive, not merely negative: the activation-aware mask shows probe information stays useful when combined with activation statistics.
  • The reward-hacking failure is reported honestly, becoming an explicit validity precondition for the method.

Limitations where it breaks

  • Single model, layer, SAE, probe family and behavioral setting — generalization untested.
  • Toy curated trivia (cities, element symbols, inventors, translations); open-ended generation and agentic deception untested despite the agent-behavior framing.
  • Gradient sensitivity is itself a weak handle (5.4% flips) — circularity risk in defining the "shared" set, only partially mitigated by ablation validation.
  • Moderate effect sizes (peak 31%); intervention does not restore truth (accuracy 0.403 → 0.330), limiting safety relevance.
  • No comparison against activation-magnitude ranking or supervised non-SAE dictionaries — the mask's advantage is incompletely triangulated.
