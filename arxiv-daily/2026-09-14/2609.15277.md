> **一句话总结：** 本文将机制可解释性引入创业研究：用 636 对精心构造的"机会识别存在/缺失"场景在 Llama 3.1 8B-Instruct 的第 12 层残差流中恢复出一个线性"机会识别方向"，并通过激活转向对该方向做首次因果干预——上调使机会判断的 log-odds 提升 +0.401、下调降低 0.716、消融降低 0.556，且该方向在几何上与可行性/合意性/开发利用方向可区分，证明创业构念在大模型内部存在可定位、可因果操纵的表征对应物。

> **TL;DR:** The paper introduces "artificial entrepreneurial cognition" and applies representation engineering to opportunity recognition (OR). From 636 adversarially filtered OR-present/OR-absent scenario pairs (bag-of-words leakage cut from 0.912 to 0.528, near the 0.500 chance level), the authors recover a linear OR direction at layer 12 of Llama 3.1 8B-Instruct that classifies held-out pairs at 0.748 (difference-in-means) versus 0.839 for a logistic-regression probe, well above the 0.528 lexical baseline. Steering this "OR dial" (α=3.5, KL-coherence-gated) shifts forced-choice opportunity judgments by +0.401 (OR-up) and −0.716 (OR-down) log-odds — a 1.117 total swing versus ~0.05 for norm-matched random directions — and ablating it drops the recognition readout by 0.556 log-odds versus −0.002 for random ablation. The direction is geometrically distinct from feasibility (cos 0.174), desirability (cos −0.017), and exploitation (cos 0.328; OR split-half reliability 0.925), yet steering it causally shifts all three downstream judgments. Recovery, signed steering, and geometric separation replicate across four additional LLMs (held-out DiM 0.705–0.744), though construct selectivity replicates only in part.

## 核心贡献

- **提出"人工创业认知"（artificial entrepreneurial cognition）的结构理论**：将创业认知研究的对象从人类扩展到 AI 系统内部，定义其为"创业相关表征与计算在 AI 系统内部的功能性组织"，并给出可证伪的三条判定标准——表征存在性（recoverability）、因果有效性（causal operativity）、表征整合性（systematic organisation）。
- **首次对 LLM 内部的创业构念做因果干预**：不同于以往只考察 LLM 输出的"工具视角"和"模拟器视角"，本文直接在 Llama 3.1 8B-Instruct 的激活空间中定位机会识别（OR）方向，并通过激活转向（steering）与方向消融（ablation）操纵它，绕开了"构念归属问题"（输出正确不等于内部有对应结构）。
- **让"识别 vs. 评估"之争获得实验可操作的形式**：识别方向在几何上与可行性、合意性、开发利用方向可分离（三者联合张成空间仅能解释 OR 方向的 11.6%），但因果上又与之耦合（转向 OR 会同时移动三类下游判断）——为 McMullen & Shepherd（2006）与 Davidsson（2015）关于识别/评估边界的理论争论提供了具体的表征层证据。
- **方法学上为创业研究引入表征工程**：提供了一套"创业理论定义构念边界 + 机制可解释性提供内部访问"的可复用模板，并用严格的词汇混淆控制（AFLite 式对抗过滤）、主题 held-out、随机方向对照、KL 相干性门控等手段保障推断效度。

## Key Contributions

- **A structural theory of artificial entrepreneurial cognition**: defines entrepreneurship-relevant internal representations/computations in AI systems as an object of inquiry, with three falsifiable criteria — representational existence, causal operativity, and representational integration relative to adjacent constructs.
- **The first causal intervention on an entrepreneurship construct inside an LLM**: rather than inferring opportunity recognition (OR) from outputs, the authors locate an OR direction in Llama 3.1 8B-Instruct and steer it up/down and ablate it, directly testing whether the construct has a causally consequential internal counterpart.
- **Makes the recognition–evaluation boundary experimentally tractable**: the OR direction is geometrically separable from feasibility, desirability, and exploitation (their joint span explains only 0.116 of the OR direction), yet intervening on it shifts judgments about all three — giving Davidsson's (2015) "bundling" concern a concrete representational form.
- **A reusable methodological template**: contrastive scenario batteries built under vocabulary/pair/batch-level rules with adversarial filtering (636 pairs from 5,000; bag-of-words accuracy 0.912 → 0.528 vs 0.500 chance), TransformerLens-based activation extraction, difference-in-means direction estimation, KL-gated steering, and theory-guided discriminant geometry.

## Methodology

The study adapts the standard representation-engineering workflow (construct contrastive inputs → record activations → estimate direction → validate via probing, intervention, geometry) to entrepreneurship construct validation, organized in three cumulative parts mapped to the three theoretical propositions.

**Scenario construction.** Claude Opus 4.7 generated 5,000 OR-present/OR-absent scenario pairs under an operational definition of OR as "noticing a generalisable structure that could serve as the basis for a potential value-generating activity, independent of evaluation or action" (following Baron's pattern-recognition account and Grégoire et al.'s structural-alignment view). Both poles depict concrete work; the contrast is whether the actor construes a recurring signal as a generalisable structure (OR-present) or absorbs it into a local routine (OR-absent). Rules at three levels ban construct/role labels (e.g., "opportunity", "founder"), enforce matched incidental features within a token-overlap band, and vary failure modes/openings across batches. Deterministic validation plus an AFLite-style adversarial filter reduced the pool to 636 pairs, cutting bag-of-words classifier accuracy from 0.912 to 0.528 (chance 0.500).

**Direction estimation.** Using TransformerLens, residual-stream activations were recorded for all 32 layers of Llama 3.1 8B-Instruct as it processed each scenario (80/20 train/held-out split: 509/127 pairs). Layer and read position were selected on training data only (layer 12 of 32, read position −5). Two estimators were used: the difference-in-means (DiM) direction (primary) and a logistic-regression probe (corroborating).

**Causal intervention.** Steering adds a scaled copy of the OR direction (α·r) to activations at the final position of each forward pass at the operating layer. The steering coefficient (α=3.5) was selected by a coherence gate — the largest α keeping next-token KL divergence ≤ 0.100 versus the unsteered baseline on a held-out calibration set — explicitly avoiding coefficient selection that maximizes the behavioral outcome. Readouts: (1) forced-choice yes/no log-odds on 665 standalone scenarios (285 entrepreneurial across weak/medium/strong opportunity bands + everyday controls), (2) free-text explanations scored by an independent LLM judge (DeepSeek V4 Pro, order-doubled protocol). Directional ablation removes the component along the OR direction from the residual stream.

**Discriminant geometry and cross-model checks.** Separate DiM directions for feasibility, desirability, and exploitation were estimated from independently constructed batteries, compared to OR via cosines against OR's split-half reliability (0.925) and the random-direction scale (~0.016 in the 4,096-dim space). Robustness covered out-of-distribution scenario rewrites, commerciality erasure, a second scenario generator, and replications in Llama 3.1 70B-Instruct, Qwen3-8B, Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct, and Gemma-3-12B-it. An additional analysis compared steering against entrepreneurial role prompting on a survey battery; an exploratory sparse-autoencoder (Llama Scope) decomposition checked bottom-up feature alignment.

## Results & Highlights

- **Direction recovery (out-of-sample)**: at layer 12, the DiM direction classifies the 127 held-out pairs at **0.748 accuracy (95% CI [0.705, 0.791])** and the LR probe at **0.839 (95% CI [0.795, 0.878])**, both above chance (0.500) and the **0.528 bag-of-words leakage baseline**; a within-pair label-permutation test rejects random labels (p=0.002). A small neural classifier does not beat the LR probe (−0.008, CI [−0.043, 0.028]), consistent with a largely linear representation; holding out each of 15 topic clusters leaves directions nearly identical (mean fold-to-fold cosine 0.996) with held-out-cluster accuracy 0.786 (random-cluster control 0.792).
- **Signed steering (forced-choice)**: on 285 entrepreneurial scenarios, OR-up raises the mean log-odds of "yes" by **+0.401** and OR-down lowers it by **0.716** (total swing **1.117 log-odds**; mean P(yes) 0.891 → 0.924 up / 0.853 down), versus shifts of only **0.050 (SD 0.279)** for 100 norm-matched random directions on the same subset (where the OR direction shifts +0.369). On 400 everyday scenarios the baseline sits at the floor (P(yes)=0.004) and both steering signs move probability by ≤ 0.008 — no construct-carrying effect where OR has no foothold.
- **Directional ablation**: removing the OR direction lowers the recognition readout by **0.556 log-odds versus −0.002 for a matched random ablation** (paired contrast −0.554, 95% CI [−0.658, −0.451], p<0.001), the clearest direction-specific necessity evidence.
- **Free-text readout**: an independent LLM judge prefers the OR-up explanation in **0.656 of 285 entrepreneurial pairs** (95% CI [0.599, 0.709], p<0.001; weak/medium bands 0.705, strong band 0.558 n.s. due to headroom), rising to **0.708** under the stricter order-doubled protocol; OR-up explanations are *shorter* (85.2 vs 92.9 tokens), ruling out a verbosity account.
- **Downstream coupling**: holding the intervention fixed, OR-up shifts log-odds by +0.728 (feasibility), +0.338 (desirability), +0.483 (exploitation); OR-down by −0.456, −0.331, −0.421; ablating OR lowers the three downstream readouts by 0.404 (paired random-ablation contrast −0.400, p<0.001) — coupling without claimed mediation.
- **Discriminant geometry**: OR's split-half reliability is 0.925, whereas cosines with comparators are only **0.328 (exploitation), 0.174 (feasibility), −0.017 (desirability — indistinguishable from the ~0.016 random scale)**; the comparators' joint span explains only **0.116** of the OR direction (R², 95% CI [0.086, 0.138]), leaving 0.884 outside it.
- **Robustness/replication**: DiM accuracy 0.781–0.860 on three out-of-distribution scenario sets; LR accuracy falls only 0.839 → 0.811 after erasing linearly decodable commerciality; cross-generator probe transfer 0.783–0.832; the central pattern (recovery, signed steering, geometric separation) holds in four additional LLMs with held-out DiM accuracy **0.705–0.744**.

## Authors & Credibility

The team pairs entrepreneurship scholars with an AI-interpretability researcher: Christian Fisch, Angela Altmeier, and Pin Ni (SnT, University of Luxembourg), Martin Obschonka (Amsterdam Business School, corresponding author, a leading voice on AI in entrepreneurship and entrepreneurial cognition), and Michal Kosinski (Stanford GSB, known for construct-based LLM studies of "psychology of LLMs"). The manuscript is a preprint under review at a peer-reviewed journal (CC BY-NC-ND), with analysis code and data publicly available on OSF (osf.io/ks2ae) and a detailed technical appendix. The design follows established representation-engineering methodology (Arditi et al. 2024; Marks & Tegmark 2024; Zou et al. 2023) and the paper is unusually forthcoming about negative control results (e.g., failed valence double-dissociation, KL-matched null families) — a credibility strength. All generative instruments (Claude Opus 4.7 generator, Llama analysand, DeepSeek V4 Pro judge) are documented with versions, from mutually independent families.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: For entrepreneurship research, genuinely novel — the first causal intervention on an entrepreneurship construct inside an LLM, and a credible bridge between MI and a non-CS field. For the MI community, the techniques (DiM contrastive directions, additive steering hooks, directional ablation, cosine-based discriminant validity) are standard; the contribution is the application domain, the theory-derived validation logic, and the careful construct-validation controls.
- **Rigor**: High by field standards. The adversarial filter (bag-of-words 0.912 → 0.528), topic holdouts, KL coherence gate for α selection, matched random-direction and label-shuffled null families, and Holm corrections are all above typical practice. The paper also transparently reports where its own evidence is weak rather than burying it.
- **Specificity failures the authors acknowledge but that materially bound the claim**: the causal double dissociation from positive valence is *not supported* (index −0.097, CI [−0.280, 0.081]); OR-up steering fails the KL-matched strengthened null (p=0.091, only the 91st percentile of the label-shuffled family); and in the steer-read matrix the **feasibility direction moves the OR question more strongly than the OR direction itself at a lower realized dose** — so the "dial" is causally involved but not demonstrably selective. The honest conclusion "causally involved, not a demonstrably selective cause" is correct but sits uneasily beside the headline framing.
- **Single-analyst dependency**: the complete causal-selectivity pipeline runs only on Llama 3.1 8B-Instruct; the clean null-tier separation reappears fully in only two of the four additional models, with a weak gap in Gemma-3-12B-it and a *reversal* in Qwen3-8B. Cross-model "replication" therefore covers the coarse pattern, not the key specificity result.
- **Data quirks**: all scenario batteries — including the comparator batteries — derive from one common specification (and ultimately one generator family), so shared-method variance remains a standing alternative the authors flag but cannot eliminate; the OR-present pole "typically expresses a broader offering," confounding recognition with action content (their own candidate explanation for the OR–exploitation cosine of 0.328). No independent human coding of the released battery exists yet, and forced-choice probes always name "yes" before "no", leaving a residual answer-order confound.
- **Reproducibility**: strong — open-weight models, public OSF code/data, greedy deterministic readouts, documented α grids. Effect sizes across the five models are not directly comparable (per-model calibration), which the authors note.
- **Significance**: conceptually significant for entrepreneurship theory (recognition/evaluation becomes experimentable) and a nice demonstration that social-science construct validation can discipline MI analyses; practically preliminary, since whether OR-up steering improves real opportunity search or just inflates yes-rates (more false positives) is untested.

## Strengths

- Transforms a decades-old conceptual debate (recognition vs. evaluation vs. exploitation) into an experimentally manipulable question inside one system, with recovery, intervention, and geometry converging on the same picture.
- Exemplary confound discipline for a steering paper: banned vocabulary, token-overlap bands, AFLite-style filtering, topic holdouts, commerciality erasure, cross-generator transfer, and coherence-gated α selection.
- Multimodal evidence: next-token log-odds, free-text explanations with an order-doubled independent LLM judge, ablation, survey-profile comparison against role prompting, and an exploratory SAE decomposition (strongest single-feature cosine only 0.341; one of top-ten features separates above chance — evidence the direction is distributed, not a monosemantic artifact).
- Unusual reporting honesty: negative results (valence dissociation, KL-matched nulls, Qwen3-8B reversal, feasibility-direction anomaly) are stated in the main text, not hidden in an appendix.
- Cross-family, cross-scale replication of the core recovery-and-steering pattern in five open-weight models.

## Limitations

- Synthetic, third-person, discovery-framed scenarios only: no test on naturally occurring material (pitch transcripts, investor memos), no first-person evaluation, no adjudication of the discovery–creation debate; OR-present/OR-absent labels rest on the generation specification without independent human coding.
- Causal specificity is asymmetric and incomplete: ablation and OR-down are the strong results; OR-up, valence dissociation, and parts of the steer-read matrix are not, and the full pipeline runs on one model with mixed replication of the null-tier separation (Qwen3-8B reverses).
- The steering coefficient is a single calibrated point (α=3.5) under a conservative KL gate; the stronger all-position result (order-doubled win rate 0.971) exceeds the coherence bound (KL 0.671) and is treated only as a ceiling, so dose–response characterization remains thin.
- Coupling ≠ mediation: additive steering cannot establish that OR lies on the computational path from recognition to exploitation; upstream discriminants (alertness, prior knowledge) are untested; shared-method variance across batteries derived from one specification is not eliminated.
- Free-text evaluation rests primarily on an LLM judge that may track the model's framing rather than substance; no downstream utility test (does steering improve opportunity search or just yes-rates?) is conducted.

## Relevance

这篇论文是"机制可解释性 + 表征工程"方法迁移到具体领域构念（创业机会识别）的范例：它演示了如何用理论驱动的对比数据集定位并因果操纵 LLM 内部的领域概念方向，其严格的混淆控制、判别几何与跨模型验证流程对任何想审计/操纵 LLM 内部特定能力或倾向（安全性、领域判断、偏见）的研究都有直接方法论参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would the OR direction survive on naturally occurring, strategically framed material (pitch transcripts, investor memoranda, YC applications), where lexical and rhetorical confounds are far noisier than in the synthetic battery?
- Can the feasibility-direction anomaly (moving the OR readout more than the OR direction itself) be resolved with interchange interventions or circuit-level analysis to establish genuine recognition→evaluation→exploitation causal ordering rather than mere coupling?
- Is OR encoded as a single direction or a low-dimensional subspace — and does the Qwen3-8B reversal and Gemma weak gap reflect different representational geometries, different instruction-tuning regimes, or calibration artifacts from per-model α selection?
- Does calibrated OR-up steering improve real opportunity search quality (novelty, downstream success) or only inflate yes-rates — i.e., what is the false-positive cost of turning the dial up?
- Can the same locate-and-steer pipeline recover upstream constructs (entrepreneurial alertness, prior knowledge, effectuation) and thereby map a full representational architecture of artificial entrepreneurial cognition within one model?
- How do the 636 released scenario pairs fare under independent expert human coding, and does the OR-present pole's "broader offering" phrasing inflate the observed OR–exploitation geometric alignment?
