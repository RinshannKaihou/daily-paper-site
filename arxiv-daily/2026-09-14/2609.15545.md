> **一句话总结：** 本文提出与层类型无关的配对探针（Carrying/Matching 激活修补），发现在 GDN/SWA 混合架构中"携带前驱信息"的计算集中在高效层（聚焦于紧邻历史值前一个 token，即 lag-one），而"按内容匹配"集中在随后的全局注意力层；通过早期掩码、卷积移除和学习率干预可以在不改动架构与语料的情况下重定位整条感应电路，并显著改变最终的召回与自然文本预测表现。

> **TL;DR:** Using layer-type-agnostic activation-patching probes on 8-layer ~77M GDN/SWA hybrids, a 16-layer 318M hybrid, and released Qwen3-4B / Qwen3.5-4B, this paper shows that the induction circuit splits cleanly across layer types: Carrying (predecessor-to-value preparation) concentrates in efficient layers and is dominated by the lag-one token, while Matching (content-based source selection) lands in the following global-attention receiver. On OpenWebText-trained models, ablating the global receiver's access is costly: pure GDN reaches 66.37 multiple-continuation far-recall PPL and 119.23 identifier-reuse PPL versus 24.79 and 27.35 for the GDN-full-attention hybrid (Table S18). Early interventions that preserve architecture and corpus — lag-one channel masking, convolution removal, LR x0.1 on efficient blocks for the first 3K-4K steps — relocate Carrying and Matching to the later L6-to-L7 route (e.g., GDN identifier-reuse PPL 29.00 down to 21.02 after early LR reduction, Table 2) and shift domain PPL (Python 23.79 to 20.64, Wiki 85.52 to 93.80, Table S15), demonstrating that early local predecessor learning causally shapes where and when global retrieval develops.

## 核心贡献

- **层类型无关的配对探针。** 设计了基于统一"块更新接口"（block output minus input，RMS 匹配的 donor 修补）的 Carrying 与 Matching 探针，不依赖注意力权重或递归状态坐标，因此能跨 GDN、SWA、全注意力 Transformer 等异构层进行统一测量；配套 lag 探针扫描历史偏移（1/2/4/8/16/32/64）。
- **混合架构的分工图谱。** 在两类混合模型中系统刻画：Carrying 集中在第一个高效组末端（GDN L2 / SWA 局部层），Matching 集中在紧随其后的全局注意力层（L3）；局部贡献高度集中在 lag-one（紧邻历史值的前一个 token），说明高效层的具体功能是"为远距离检索准备 source key"。
- **干预重定位整条电路。** 早期 lag-one 卷积通道抑制、卷积移除、以及前 3K-4K 步对高效块学习率乘 0.1，都能把 Carrying-Matching 从浅层路线（L2→L3）搬到深层路线（L6→L7）；源键恢复实验证明迁移后的接收器仍通过 source K 读取新 Carrying 位点准备的信息（K 单独转移即可复现几乎全部路径损失，V 几乎无影响）。
- **形成时序受局部支持与训练数据调制。** 更窄的 SWA 窗口（window 2）让 Carrying 更早跨越阈值（(1,2]K 步 vs window 8/16 的 (2,3]K 步），感应增强语料让 Transformer 的电路更早形成——把架构先验与训练证据连接到电路形成时机。
- **扩展到更大模型与行为层面。** 318M 十六层四阶段模型和已发布的 Qwen3-4B / 混合 Qwen3.5-4B 上验证同样分工（Qwen3.5 最强 Carrying 位于 GDN L18、紧邻最强全局 Matching L19）；自然文本实验（10K 序列：2K Wiki、4K Python、4K Math）表明电路重定位真实改变预测与召回画像。

## Key Contributions

- **Layer-type-agnostic paired probes.** Carrying (measured at the historical value) and Matching (measured at the query) probes built on a common block-update interface: patch an RMS-matched donor block update and read out the change in the correct-minus-distractor logit margin. This works uniformly across attention, recurrent (GDN), and convolutional operators, where attention-score diagnostics fail; 200 paired prompts per condition, run, and checkpoint.
- **A division-of-labor map for hybrids.** In both GDN hybrids [GDN3, TF]2 and SWA hybrids [SWA3, TF]2 (full attention at L3/L7, ~77-79M params), Carrying peaks at the end of the first efficient group (L2) and Matching in the next global receiver (L3); the pure Transformer expresses the same roles deeper with higher run-to-run variability. The lag scan shows the local contribution concentrates at offset 1 — the token immediately before the historical value.
- **Circuit relocation under fixed architecture and corpus.** Early lag-one convolution-channel suppression, width-2 convolution, convolution removal, and early LR x0.1 on the efficient blocks (first 3K steps in GDN, 4K in SWA) all reallocate the circuit — convolution removal and LR reduction consistently push Carrying-Matching to the later L6-to-L7 route. Source-key restoration and fixed-value attention-pattern swaps confirm the relocated receiver reads the source K prepared at the new Carrying site.
- **Formation timing from local and data support.** SWA window 2 crosses Carrying onset thresholds (0.1/0.25/0.5 margin effect) within (1,2]K steps versus (2,3]K for windows 8/16; induction-enriched text (bigram frequency/reliability filter) advances Transformer circuit formation, while hybrids rise in a similar early interval under both streams.
- **Scaling and behavioral grounding.** The organization replicates in a 16-layer ~318M four-stage hybrid (LR reduction in different groups favors early/intermediate/late routes) and in released checkpoints: full-attention Qwen3-4B separates the roles across attention layers, while hybrid Qwen3.5-4B puts its strongest Carrying in the GDN layer immediately before its strongest global Matching (L18 to L19), with K restoration recovering nearly all of the sender-patch loss. Natural-text head ablations show the identified receivers causally support recall prediction.

## Methodology

The paper studies how the three classical induction roles identified by Singh et al. (2024) — Carrying (transferring predecessor information into the historical value), Matching (selecting a source by content), and Copying (transmitting its value) — are allocated across heterogeneous layers in hybrid language models.

**Models.** Controlled 8-layer models at width 512 with 8 heads: GDN hybrid [GDN3, TF]2 (78.88M params; GDN = gated delta-rule memory plus width-4 causal convolution), SWA hybrid [SWA3, TF]2 (77.26M; sliding-window attention, default window 4), and a full-attention Transformer (77.26M). All train on packed OpenWebText, context 1024, GPT-2 tokenizer, AdamW (lr 3e-4, cosine decay), 30K steps, learned absolute position embeddings (SWA additionally applies RoPE locally). A 16-layer ~318M four-stage hybrid and released Qwen3-4B (36-layer full attention) and Qwen3.5-4B (32-layer [GDN3, FullAttn]8, width 2560) extend the comparison.

**Probes.** On synthetic prompts AB...CD...Aq (target B; margin m = zB − zD), activation patching replaces a block update (output minus input, including FFN where present) with an RMS-matched donor update, then recomputes later blocks. The Carrying probe replaces the predecessor of the historical value B and patches at the value position; the Matching probe swaps the historical keys (AB...CD...Aq to CB...AD...Aq) and patches at the query. A lag probe scans offsets 1-64. Identity controls (off-site donors, same-predecessor donors) isolate the association-specific effect.

**Interventions.** (1) Early lag-one masking (suppress only the lag-one convolution channel early in training); (2) convolution width-2 and convolution removal; (3) early LR reduction (x0.1 on mixer, FFN, and norm parameters of efficient blocks for the first 3K/4K steps, then resume); (4) SWA window sizes 2/8/16; (5) induction-enriched training text (70% enriched for the first 5K steps, 50% after; paragraphs retained when the frequency/reliability score SIH > 0.10). Tracing tools: source-key restoration (restore clean K at the historical source), fixed-value selection (swap attention patterns holding V fixed), head-level Matching/Copying tests, and natural-text head ablations (delta-NLL on 10,000 sequences: 2,000 Wiki, 4,000 Python, 4,000 Math, with recall target groups for single/multiple continuation and identifier reuse).

**Replication.** Three independent training runs for principal conditions (two for the matched Transformer LR pair); reported values are run means with sample SD.

## Results & Highlights

- **Global receivers are decisive for recall (Table S18, OpenWebText-trained 30K-step models, recall PPL on 10K sequences, lower is better):** the GDN-full-attention hybrid reaches 24.79 multiple-continuation far-recall PPL and 27.35 identifier-reuse PPL, while pure GDN (same size/training) reaches 66.37 and 119.23 respectively — roughly 2.7x and 4.4x worse; pure GDN's historical-source Carrying effect is <0.02 versus 2.60 for the hybrid, and its association lives in the query-time recurrent state instead.
- **Early LR x0.1 on efficient blocks relocates the circuit and changes final recall (Table 2, GDN hybrid, PPL at 30K):** identifier reuse improves from 29.00 ± 1.43 (baseline) to 21.02 ± 1.60 (LR x0.1) and multiple-continuation far from 25.98 ± 1.23 to 23.35 ± 0.34, while single-continuation far worsens from 2.38 ± 0.04 to 2.59 ± 0.05 — same architecture, same corpus, different learned allocation, different prediction profile. The matched Transformer LR intervention hurts everything (single far 2.66 to 3.33, multiple far 25.73 to 33.60, identifier reuse 25.01 to 34.86).
- **Window size trades formation speed against final distant recall (Table 2, SWA hybrid):** window 2 forms Carrying earliest (crossing thresholds 0.1/0.25/0.5 within (1,2]K steps versus (2,3]K for windows 8/16; Appendix D.3) but ends worse on distant recall — multiple-continuation far PPL 28.99 ± 0.43 (window 2) versus 22.40 ± 2.55 (window 16), and identifier reuse 28.05 ± 2.21 versus 20.91 ± 6.76.
- **Domain-level effects of the same interventions (Table S15):** GDN LR x0.1 lowers Python PPL from 23.79 ± 3.04 to 20.64 ± 2.04 but raises Wiki PPL from 85.52 ± 0.92 to 93.80 ± 3.01; the Transformer LR control raises all three domains (Wiki 98.51 to 117.63, Python 22.69 to 30.80, Math 49.32 to 56.14).
- **The relocated receiver reads the relocated source key (Table 1):** in GDN baseline, clean-K restoration at the correct source recovers 5.85 ± 0.45 margin at L3 versus 1.32 ± 0.39 at the off-target L7; after LR x0.1 the effect flips to 9.16 ± 0.95 at L7 versus 0.02 ± 0.07 at L3, and fixed-value pattern swaps show the selection effect moves with the receiver. Transferring K alone reproduces nearly all of the full sender-patch loss; V transfer has little effect.
- **Production-scale confirmation:** hybrid Qwen3.5-4B places its strongest Carrying in GDN layer L18 immediately before its strongest global Matching at L19; restoring the clean K tensor there recovers nearly all of the sender-patch margin loss, while Q, V, gate, attention-output, and FFN-output restoration have little effect (Table S14). Full-attention Qwen3-4B instead separates the roles across distinct attention layers.
- **Natural-text causal role (Appendix F.3):** ablating the identified source-attending/retrieval heads causes larger NLL increases on recall targets and identifier reuse than matched control heads, and the selected heads rank first in their layers for overall NLL damage — the synthetic-prompt circuit matters for natural prediction.

## Authors & Credibility

The team is led by Ke Cheng (Beihang University CCSE lab / Tencent AMS) with Xin Xu, Yixiao Chen, Yue Liu, Jun Zhang (corresponding author), and Jie Jiang from Tencent AMS, plus independent collaborators. The work sits squarely in the transformer-circuits lineage (Elhage et al. 2021; Olsson et al. 2022; Singh et al. 2024) and the hybrid-architecture literature (Griffin/De et al. 2024, Jamba, Qwen3.5), and engages carefully with concurrent work (Qiao et al. 2026 on retrieval-head formation; Arora et al. 2025 on recurrent query-time association; Cabannes et al. 2026 on short-window memorization). Code is released at github.com/ckpassenger/bind-match-copy. Methodological hygiene is strong: identity controls separating association-specific effects from generic context sensitivity, RMS-matched donor updates, prompt-bootstrap intervals, three-run replication with sample SD for most conditions, and calibration experiments for the Qwen prompt pools. The intervention logic — broad LR perturbation backed by predecessor-specific channel-level tests — is explicitly layered to address confounding.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** The layer-type-agnostic probe interface is a genuine methodological contribution — prior induction-head diagnostics genuinely cannot inspect recurrent mixers, and the lag-one finding ("the token before the value is the key") gives locality in hybrids a sharp mechanistic reading. The relocation experiments go beyond correlational circuit mapping. However, the Carrying/Matching/Copying decomposition is inherited from Singh et al. (2024), and the window-size/formation-timing result substantially overlaps Qiao et al. (2026)'s observation that larger local windows delay retrieval-head formation; the delta is the upstream source computation rather than the retrieval head itself.
- **Rigor:** Controls are unusually thorough for this genre (off-site identity donors, same-predecessor donors, lag-2 and late-timing masking controls, random-retention data controls). But the headline LR x0.1 intervention is a broad perturbation of entire efficient blocks (mixer, FFN, and normalization), so the relocation it causes cannot be attributed to predecessor learning per se — only the converging channel-specific masks support the causal story, and even the lag-one mask produces variable outcomes across seeds (runs 42/44 relocate, run 43 does not; Table 1 caption), which is reported honestly but shows the allocation is seed-fragile at this scale.
- **Scale and external validity:** All training results come from 77-79M eight-layer models trained 30K steps on OpenWebText (plus one 318M); the only large models are released Qwen checkpoints studied observationally with fixed-token synthetic prompts, where clean solvability swings wildly across token pools (e.g., mean clean margin −2.551 with only 16.0% solvable prompts for the 150K min-ID pool versus 100% at 120K, Table S13) — the localization is convincing but the training-dynamics claims are untested above 318M. The authors themselves cite evidence that architecture gaps shrink with longer training.
- **Behavioral payoff is mixed and modest:** every intervention that helps some recall metric hurts another (GDN LR x0.1: identifier reuse −7.98 PPL but single-continuation far +0.21 and Wiki PPL +8.28); no overall capability improvement is claimed, which is honest but limits practical design guidance to "early local learning matters" rather than an actionable recipe.
- **Reproducibility:** Good — released code, explicit seeds, exact hyperparameters, and SD reporting; but the synthetic probe protocol (1020-token prompts, needle/distractor spacing heuristics) has many researcher degrees of freedom, and the natural-text evidence is limited to head ablations and recall-PPL conditioning rather than direct causal tracing on natural inputs.
- **Red flags:** minor — Table 2's identifier-reuse SD for window 16 (±6.76) is a third of the mean, so some window comparisons are noisy; the enrichment criterion (bigram frequency/reliability, threshold 0.10) is ad hoc and only weakly motivated by Aoyama et al. (2026).

## Strengths

- The paired block-update probe is an elegant, reusable interface that makes induction circuits measurable in architectures where attention maps do not exist — this directly unblocks interpretability for the hybrid/recurrent model family that is rapidly becoming production-relevant.
- The central finding is crisp and repeatedly corroborated across four independent levers (channel masking, convolution surgery, LR timing, window size) and three model families, then confirmed in released 4B checkpoints.
- The source-key restoration experiment cleanly closes the causal loop: relocation of Carrying is meaningless unless the new Matching receiver actually reads the new site, and the K-versus-V dissociation proves it does.
- Connects circuit-level mechanism to measurable downstream behavior (recall PPL, domain PPL, head-ablation NLL), avoiding the common interpretability trap of stopping at synthetic tasks.
- Transparent statistics (three runs, sample SD, bootstrap intervals, identity controls) and released code.

## Limitations

- All causal training results are at 77-318M scale on OpenWebText for 30K steps; whether the Carrying-before-Matching dependency and its relocation behavior persist at billions of parameters, trillions of tokens, and much longer training is unknown, and cited evidence suggests gaps may shrink with scale.
- The Matching probe measures a composite (selection + value transmission + downstream FFN effects); only the fixed-V pattern swap isolates pure selection, and Copying is analyzed mainly in appendices, so the three-role decomposition is less complete in hybrids than in the original Transformer setting.
- Circuit location under intervention is seed-variable (the lag-one mask splits 2/3 relocated vs 1/3 not), so allocation is a stochastic outcome of early training rather than a deterministic design property — the paper reports but does not resolve this.
- The natural-text linkage is correlational-plus-ablational: no method is given for tracing Carrying/Matching on organic text, and "recall" target groups (single/multiple continuation, identifier reuse) are custom-defined rather than standard benchmarks (e.g., no MQAR, no long-context eval suites).
- The induction-enriched curriculum is a coarse bigram-statistic filter; the mechanism by which it accelerates Transformer but not hybrid formation is asserted qualitatively, not isolated.

## Relevance

对关注混合架构（GDN/SWA + 稀疏全局注意力）与可解释性的研究非常有价值：它把"为什么混合架构能同时提升能力与效率"落到具体电路上——高效层负责 lag-one 前驱信息准备、全局层负责内容检索，且早期训练条件能重定位该分工并改变召回表现，为架构设计与训练调度提供了机制层面的依据。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the lag-one-dominated Carrying and its gate on Matching development persist at 1B+ scale with multi-trillion-token training, or does it wash out as Qiao et al.'s long-training convergence results would predict?
- Can the early LR-reduction recipe be turned into a deliberate curriculum (e.g., staged unfreezing of efficient blocks) that reliably improves identifier-recall-heavy domains like code without the Wiki-PPL regression, instead of the current seed-dependent side effect?
- The probe reads a scalar margin effect per block; could a vector/channel-resolved version of the block-update interface identify the exact subspace in which lag-one information is carried, and is it shared between convolution and recurrent pathways?
- Pure GDN stores the association in the query-time recurrent state while hybrids use historical-source preparation — is there a hybrid ratio or receiver sparsity at which the model learns both routes, and what would the behavioral signature of that redundancy be?
- The enrichment filter advances Transformer circuit formation but leaves hybrids largely unchanged — is this because hybrid efficient layers already saturate the lag-one learning signal, and could an orthogonal data intervention (e.g., long-range repetition rather than bigram reliability) target the Matching stage instead?
- Qwen3.5-4B's strongest Carrying sits in GDN L18 immediately before the L19 receiver, but secondary responses exist deeper; do multiple Carrying-Matching pairs in deep models cooperate, compete, or specialize by distance?
