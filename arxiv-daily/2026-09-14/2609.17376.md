> **一句话总结：** 该工作发现六个开源 LLM（Qwen3.5、Llama-3、Gemma-4）在被输入隐马尔可夫模型（HMM）产生的 token 序列时，其残差流（residual stream）中会线性编码该 HMM 的"信念状态"（对隐藏状态的后验分布，峰值探针 R² 达 0.83–0.99），且通过 steering/patching 干预该子空间能把模型预测定向推向目标信念，为"上下文学习 ≈ 在推断出的生成模型上做近似贝叶斯最优预测"提供了表示层面的证据。

> **TL;DR:** Prompting six open-source LLMs (Qwen3.5-9B/4B, Llama-3.1-8B/3.2-3B, Gemma-4-E4B/E2B) with 40 hand-designed HMM processes (families Mess3, Arch, Wing, Strata), the paper shows that the Bayesian belief state (posterior over HMM hidden states) is linearly decodable from residual-stream activations with peak held-out probe R² of 0.83–0.99, while shuffled/random-belief controls collapse to near zero. Behaviorally, Qwen3.5-9B reaches converged KL of 0.009–0.018 nats to the ground-truth next-token distribution — matching the optimal order-k HMM predictor at k = 4.2–11.0 and beating the 0-HMM/1-HMM baselines. Causally, patching/steering the probe-identified belief subspace shifts the model's predictions toward the injected target belief (final-token KL to the target on the order of the unmodified model), whereas injecting a random belief does not. This extends the computational-mechanics "belief state geometry" account from toy transformers trained on HMM data to production-scale LLMs.

## 核心贡献

- **信念状态可从 LLM 残差流中线性解码。** 对 6 个开源 LLM × 40 个具有非平凡信念结构的 HMM（4 个族：Mess3、Arch、Wing、Strata，其中 30 个为本文新设计），OLS 线性探针能从残差流激活中恢复信念状态几何，峰值 R² 跨所有 HMM-LLM 组合为 0.83–0.99，且在早层到晚层均出现；打乱信念（shuffled）与随机信念（Dirichlet 随机）两个对照探针的 R² 接近于零。
- **排除了多种"平凡解释"。** 论文系统排除了：短后缀启发式（k-HMM 近似，全 HMM 激活的 ΔR² 与理论剩余信息相关性 Pearson r = 0.59–0.86）、下一 token 概率（NTP）/log-NTP 表示（对 Wing/Strata，激活解码 R² 超过从真值 NTP/log-NTP 出发的 R²；ΔR² 与理论对应量相关 r = 0.85–1.00）、以及跨参数化的"族通用"信息（跨参数化迁移 R² 矩阵与真值迁移矩阵相关，r ≈ 0.64–0.78）。
- **信念子空间具有因果作用。** 沿探针学到的嵌入对信念子空间做 steering/patching，能按理论预期改写下游 log-NTP 表示与最终预测：注入目标信念后，模型对目标信念隐含的真值 NTP 的 KL 降到与未篡改模型同量级，而注入随机信念的对照不会下降；沿"保持 NTP 不变的 δ 方向"（δ ∈ Ker(Mᵀ)）steering 也能安装该 NTP 不可见分量，且自修复（self-repair）模式与随机方向对照不同。
- **将计算力学从玩具网络推广到生产级 LLM。** 之前的结论（Shai et al. 2024）仅在专门用 HMM 数据训练的小 transformer 上成立；本文证明对从未见过这些 HMM 的开源 LLM 同样成立，说明信念状态几何是预训练 + 上下文学习的自然产物，并给出了可证伪的理论预测。

## Key Contributions

- **Belief states are linearly decodable from LLM residual streams.** Across six open-source LLMs and 40 HMMs with non-trivial belief structure (four families: Mess3, Arch, Wing, Strata; 30 newly designed), OLS linear probes recover the belief-state geometry with peak held-out R² of 0.83–0.99, spanning early to late layers; shuffled-belief and random-belief (Dirichlet) controls yield near-zero R².
- **A battery of controls rules out trivial explanations.** The authors rule out short-suffix heuristics (ΔR² of full-HMM activations over the 1-HMM/0-HMM decodable floor correlates with the theoretical surplus at Pearson r = 0.59–0.86), NTP/log-NTP as the represented object (for Wing/Strata, R² from activations exceeds R² of linearly predicting beliefs from ground-truth NTP/log-NTP; ΔR² correlates with theory at r = 0.85–1.00), and family-generic information (cross-parametrization transfer-R² matrices correlate with ground-truth matrices, r ≈ 0.64–0.78).
- **The belief subspace is causally used for prediction.** Steering/patching the probe-identified subspace (last 5,000 tokens for steering; suffix lengths k ∈ {1, 5, 10} for prediction control) shifts downstream predictions toward the injected target belief: the final-token KL to the target belief's ground-truth NTP drops to the order of the unmodified model's KL, while a random-belief control shows no such drop; an NTP-preserving δ-direction steer (δ ∈ Ker(Mᵀ), step at 90% of the feasible maximum) installs the NTP-invisible belief component, with a distinct self-repair profile from a magnitude-matched random direction.
- **Computational mechanics scales to production LLMs.** This extends the belief-state-geometry result of Shai et al. (2024) from toy transformers trained explicitly on HMM data to off-the-shelf LLMs that never saw these processes, establishing computational mechanics as a source of falsifiable, theory-derived predictions about activation geometry in real models.

## Methodology

The paper combines a controlled behavioral task, linear probing, causal interventions, and early-exit analysis.

1. **Processes.** 40 edge-emitting HMMs across four families (Mess3 — a known 3-state process from Marzen & Crutchfield 2017; and three newly designed families Arch, Wing, Strata), with 10 parametrizations each, chosen to lie in the entropy/mixing-rate regime where Dai et al. (2025) showed LLMs predict HMM data well and to have non-trivial belief geometry. Mess3/Arch emit three tokens; Wing/Strata emit two; sequences are formatted as space-separated single letters (e.g., "F Q V F Q ..."), with tokenizer alignment verified. All sequences are N = 20,000 tokens; analyses average over 10 sequences.
2. **Models.** Six open-source LLMs: Qwen3.5-9B (L=32, d=4096, 262k context — the main-text model), Qwen3.5-4B, Llama-3.1-8B, Llama-3.2-3B, Gemma-4-E4B, Gemma-4-E2B.
3. **Behavioral evaluation (Sec. 4).** KL divergence between the HMM's ground-truth next-token probabilities (NTP) and the LLM's NTP at each position, compared against the family of order-k optimal suffix predictors (k-HMMs): the 0-HMM (i.i.d. optimum) and 1-HMM (last-token optimum) are the key baselines.
4. **Linear probing (Sec. 5).** At each layer, activations h_{ℓ,t} for t ≥ 15,000 (well past convergence) are regressed (OLS) onto belief states η(x_{1:t}), trained on a random 20% (1,000 tokens) and evaluated on held-out 80% (4,000 tokens), per sequence. Controls: shuffled beliefs, random (Dirichlet) beliefs, cross-parametrization transfer probes, suffix-approximation curves (0-HMM/1-HMM sequences), and NTP/log-NTP regressions.
5. **Causal interventions (Sec. 6–7).** A per-layer affine embedding emb_ℓ of belief space into activation space is learned; steering adds emb(target) − emb(current) to activations, patching overwrites with emb(target) over the last k tokens. Past-consistent targets share the input prefix; past-inconsistent targets conflict with it. Effect measured by probing log-NTP/δ-components downstream (Sec. 6) and by final-token KL to target vs. original belief ground truth (Sec. 7), against random-belief and random-direction controls.
6. **Early-exit analysis (Sec. 8).** A tuned lens is trained per layer toward the ground-truth NTP; the layer-wise KL of early-exit distributions is correlated with the belief probe's R².

## Results & Highlights

- **Peak belief-state probe R² = 0.83–0.99** across all 40 HMMs and all 6 LLMs (residual-stream activations, held-out 80% of a 5,000-token window), versus near-zero R² for the shuffled-belief and random-belief controls across all layers — the headline representational result.
- **Near-optimal behavioral prediction.** For Qwen3.5-9B, converged KL to the ground-truth NTP is 0.009–0.018 nats on the four main-text parametrizations, matching the optimal order-k HMM predictor at k = 4.2–11.0 (median k = 3.4–4.7 across all 10 parametrizations per family; range 2.2–11.0) — far below the 0-HMM (i.i.d.) and 1-HMM (last-token) baselines. KL falls over the first 5,000–10,000 tokens and then plateaus.
- **The smallest model fails the behavioral bar.** Gemma-4-E2B is no more accurate than the 1-HMM baseline on 6/10 Wing and 4/10 Strata parametrizations, and several findings are weaker or absent for it — evidence that belief-state encoding is scale-sensitive.
- **Activations encode more than suffix heuristics.** The gap ΔR² between belief decodability from full-HMM activations and from 1-HMM sequences correlates with the theoretical surplus (belief information the 1-HMM cannot explain) at Pearson r = 0.59–0.86 within every family and LLM; the analogous NTP/log-NTP ΔR² correlates at r = 0.85–1.00 (except 0.43–0.88 for Arch under log-NTP).
- **Cross-parametrization transfer tracks ground truth.** The matrix of transfer-probe R² between parametrizations correlates with the activation-free ground-truth transfer matrix at r = 0.64–0.78 (main-text best layers: Mess3 0.78, Wing 0.67, Arch 0.66, Strata 0.64), showing HMM-specific (not family-generic) belief information.
- **Causal override of predictions.** After patching the belief subspace over the last k ∈ {1, 5, 10} positions, the model's final-token KL to the target belief's ground-truth NTP falls well below its KL to the original belief's — on the order of the unmodified model's KL — while the random-belief control's KL does not drop. The past-consistent advantage over past-inconsistent targets at k = 1 disappears by k = 10, i.e., injected beliefs largely override conflicting context.
- **Early-exit accuracy tracks belief decodability.** A tuned lens trained toward ground-truth NTP achieves KL below the LLM's own output KL at most layers, and per-layer early-exit KL co-varies with 1 − R² of the belief probe, far exceeding shuffled/random-NTP controls.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty: solid, incremental in the right way.** The leap from toy transformers trained on HMM data (Shai et al. 2024) to off-the-shelf LLMs is genuinely new, and the causal-intervention section goes beyond most probing papers. However, the behavioral half (LLMs predict HMM data near-optimally) substantially overlaps with Dai et al. (2025), which the paper openly builds on — the novelty rests on the representational and causal findings.
- **Rigor: high, with an unusually strong control battery.** Shuffled/random-belief probes, cross-parametrization transfer matrices, 0-HMM/1-HMM sequence comparisons, NTP/log-NTP regression baselines, random-direction steering controls, and comparison of every empirical ΔR² against an activation-free theoretical counterpart — this is far above the field norm. The authors are also candid about interpretive limits, e.g., for Mess3 the emission matrix is invertible so beliefs and NTP are provably indistinguishable by linear probes (they exclude Mess3 from those analyses rather than overclaiming), and for Arch under NTP only about half of parametrizations show the activations > NTP gap.
- **Reproducibility: good.** All models are public open-weight LLMs; the HMM families are fully specified by transition matrices in Appendix C.1; sequence lengths, train/test splits (20/80 over 5,000 tokens), and averaging (10 sequences) are stated precisely. Main risk is scale: 20k-token contexts × 6 models × 40 HMMs is a heavy compute job that casual replication may not afford, but no conceptual barriers.
- **Significance: real but scope-limited.** The evidence supports "ICL of low-entropy 2–3 token HMMs approximates Bayesian belief-state tracking," not "ICL in general is Bayesian prediction." All processes emit only two or three distinct tokens — about as far from natural text as a language-model input can be while still being tokenizable.
- **Red flags / weaknesses to note.** (1) HMM selection is curated: the 40 processes were selected for low entropy, non-trivial belief geometry, and LLM predictability (following Dai et al. 2025's entropy-mixing plane); the authors' own Table 3 admits a subset of broader parametrizations shows no gap over 1-HMM/0-HMM decodability — a survivorship-style selection. (2) The probes are fit per-sequence with only 1,000 training tokens against d = 4,096-dimensional activations; the paper itself cites Méloux et al. (2025) warning that linear regression can hallucinate low-dimensional structure, and defers unsupervised corroboration (e.g., SAE-based) to future work. (3) The steering/causality results exclude the Gemma class and rely on layer-indexed heatmaps with qualitative "onset of self-repair" readings rather than a quantitative causal metric. (4) Converged-KL analyses implicitly assume the "two levels of inference" (model identification vs. state tracking) have settled after ~10k tokens; the KL plateau is consistent with, but does not prove, a fixed inferred generative model.

## Strengths

- Theory-derived, falsifiable prediction (computational mechanics) tested on production-scale open models — a rare template for interpretability research.
- The control battery is exceptional: every decodability claim is paired with both an empirical control and an activation-free theoretical counterpart, and the two are correlated quantitatively.
- Causal section does the hard part: patching/steering with past-inconsistent targets and the NTP-preserving δ-direction (Ker(Mᵀ)) steer cleanly separates "belief subspace" effects from generic activation perturbation.
- Honest reporting of negative/weak results (Gemma-4-E2B behavioral failures, Table 3 parametrizations without the decodability gap, Mess3 identifiability degeneracy).
- Full coverage in appendices (all 40 HMMs × 6 LLMs), so main-text cherry-picking risk is low — figures for one illustrative parametrization per family are backed by the full sweep.

## Limitations

- Stimulus class is narrow: 2–3-token alphabets, 3–4 hidden states, low-entropy, slowly mixing HMMs selected partly because LLMs predict them well; no claims transfer automatically to richer processes, hierarchical structure, or natural text.
- Belief geometry here is low-dimensional (2-simplex or 4-state simplex); the paper explicitly does not establish the picture for high-dimensional or structureless belief geometries.
- Linear probes fit per-sequence on 1,000 tokens; susceptibility to spurious linear structure is acknowledged but only partially addressed (no unsupervised/SAE corroboration yet).
- Alternative-feature explanations are only partially ruled out: NTP and log-NTP are excluded as candidate represented objects, but the authors concede other candidate features could make beliefs spuriously decodable.
- Findings attenuate with scale: Gemma-4-E2B (2B effective) fails several behavioral and causal checks, and Gemma models are excluded from the steering results — the boundary of validity in model scale and family is not mapped.
- No scaling-law analysis, no natural-text bridge task, and no code release mentioned in the main text (though all ingredients — models and HMM definitions — are public).

## Relevance

直接命中「LLM Hidden States & Interpretability」方向：这是线性探针 + 因果干预（steering/patching）研究残差流表示几何的当前最系统的代表作之一，其"理论预测 → 表示验证 → 因果确认"的范式对任何做内部表示/隐状态分析的工作都有方法论参考价值。虽然其 HMM 刺激与训练/推理可靠性主题不直接相关，但"探针可解码子空间承载因果有效信息"的论证方式对用内部状态做监控与异常检测亦有借鉴意义。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does belief-state geometry survive for higher-entropy, faster-mixing, or larger-alphabet HMMs — and is there a clean scaling law in HMM complexity (states × tokens × entropy) analogous to the k = 4.2–11.0 effective-Markov-order result?
- The paper leaves the two inference levels (which HMM am I seeing vs. what state is it in) behaviorally entangled: could the cross-parametrization transfer matrices be used to decode *which* parametrization the model has inferred, i.e., an explicit "task vector" for process identification?
- Can the linearly decodable belief subspace be recovered by unsupervised methods (SAEs, PCA on activations), addressing the Méloux et al. (2025) concern about spurious linear structure?
- How does the belief representation interact with position: the probe is fit on t ≥ 15,000 of a 20,000-token window — is the same geometry maintained at 100k+ tokens, and does it persist under context compression (e.g., in models with KV-cache eviction or recurrent memory)?
- Self-repair erased early-layer belief steers — which circuit implements the repair, and is the belief-write mechanism localized to specific attention heads, as the toy-model story would predict?
- Is the belief subspace linearly decodable in quantized or otherwise optimized-for-speed checkpoints, and does degradation of this subspace predict degradation of in-context prediction accuracy?
