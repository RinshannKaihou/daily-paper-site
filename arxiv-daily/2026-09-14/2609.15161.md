> **一句话总结：** EMR 在多智能体医疗问答系统（规划器→科室智能体→汇总智能体）外挂一个三层经验库（诊断原则/推理模式/代表病例），从推理轨迹中自动挖掘"成功经验"与"失败警告"并在后续检索复用，在 MedQA（GPT-4o 上 96.8%，比最强基线 EvolveR 高 4.1 个点）等 9 个医疗基准上稳定超越 MDAgents、EvolveR 等方法，且经验可跨模型、跨数据集迁移。

> **TL;DR:** EMR is a medical multi-agent QA system (planner → department agents → summary agent, mimicking multidisciplinary consultation) that mines "golden" (success) and "warning" (failure) experiences from its own reasoning trajectories into a three-level library (principles / patterns / cases), retrieved at inference via bge-m3 embeddings. On MedQA with GPT-4o it reaches 96.8% accuracy vs 92.7% for the strongest baseline EvolveR (+4.1); averages are 93.9 vs 92.4 (MDAgents) on GPT-4o and 81.1 vs 77.8 on Qwen3-8B. Mined experiences transfer across backbones (GPT-4o-mined experience lifts Qwen3-8B MedQA from 74.3 to 83.9) and across datasets, and adding EMR to MDAgents/RECONCILE improves both — at a modest 1200 tokens and 15s per sample.

## 核心贡献

- 提出 EMR，一个通过"经验挖掘与复用"实现自我进化的医疗多智能体系统：系统在多科室会诊式协作（规划器智能体分配领域、科室智能体做领域推理、汇总智能体综合决策）之外，自动从推理轨迹中蒸馏可复用经验，无需更新模型参数即可持续提升。
- 设计**层次化经验库**，将经验组织为三级：诊断原则（principles，30–50 词的高层临床规则）、推理模式（patterns，可复用的决策模板）、代表病例（cases，保留具体临床细节的实例），兼顾泛化性与针对性。
- 引入**双重经验类型**：从正确轨迹挖掘的"黄金经验"（golden）与从失败轨迹挖掘的"警告经验"（warning，记录错误模式与误导线索），消融显示警告经验贡献 +2.6 个点，起到"护栏"作用。
- 提出基于语义相似度路由 + LLM 语义合并的经验更新机制（阈值 θ=0.85 时新增、否则合并或丢弃），控制库规模增长；推理时进行"原则→模式→病例"的层次化检索（top-3）注入各智能体提示。
- 实证验证经验的**可迁移性**：跨骨干模型（GPT-4o 挖掘的经验给 Qwen3-8B 带来 +9.6）、跨数据集（MedQA 挖掘的经验使 MedMCQA +3.5）、跨架构（嫁接到 MDAgents / RECONCILE 上均提升 2.4–2.7 个点）。

## Key Contributions

- **EMR system**: a self-evolving medical multi-agent framework where a planner agent assigns question-level (m=3) and option-level (n=2) medical domains, department agents produce domain-specific analyses, and a summary agent synthesizes a final answer — mirroring multidisciplinary consultation.
- **Hierarchical experience library**: a three-level abstraction (principles / patterns / cases) that balances transferable high-level diagnostic rules against concrete clinical grounding, replacing the raw-trajectory or flat-summary stores of prior memory methods (ExpeL, ReasoningBank, G-Memory, MDTeamGPT).
- **Golden + warning experience mining**: trajectories are evaluated against ground truth; successful ones yield golden experiences (effective strategies) and failed ones yield warning experiences (failure causes, misleading cues), enabling symmetric learning from both successes and errors.
- **Bounded, structured library update**: new experiences are embedded (bge-m3), compared to retrieved neighbors by cosine similarity, and added only if Sim < θ (default 0.85); otherwise an LLM merges or discards them — preventing uncontrolled growth.
- **Model-agnostic, architecture-agnostic reuse**: retrieved experiences are injected as prompt context into planner, department, and summary agents; EMR's experience module also plugs into MDAgents and RECONCILE, improving both.

## Methodology

EMR comprises three components. (1) **Multi-agent medical system**: given a question q and options O, a planner agent identifies question-level domains D_q and option-level domains D_o (conditioned on retrieved experience E_r); for each domain, a department agent generates an analysis a_d conditioned on q, O, the domain, and retrieved experience; a summary agent integrates all analyses plus retrieved experience via reflective reasoning to output the prediction ŷ. (2) **Experience mining**: each run produces a structured trajectory τ = (q, O, D_q, D_o, A, a_sum, ŷ), labeled successful/failed against ground truth; a collector prompt distills experience entries in natural language, categorized golden or warning. (3) **Experience reuse**: the library L = {L_principle, L_pattern, L_case} is updated by an updater that retrieves related entries R via embedding similarity (Eq. 9–10): if max cosine similarity < θ the entry is novel and added; otherwise an LLM merges it with R or discards it. At inference, retrieval is hierarchical (principles first, then patterns, then cases, top-k with k=3) and the retrieved blocks are injected into all agent prompts. Implementation: bge-m3 embeddings, θ=0.85, 5 mining epochs, Qwen3-8B and GPT-4o backbones; MedQA/MedMCQA/MMLU subsets split evenly between mining and evaluation halves, PubMedQA 500/500; results averaged over 3 runs.

## Results & Highlights

- **Main comparison (Accuracy %)**: on GPT-4o, EMR averages 93.9 across the 9 benchmarks vs 92.4 for MDAgents (+1.5), 90.3 for EvolveR (+3.6), and 85.0 for single-LLM zero-shot; on MedQA/GPT-4o it reaches 96.8 vs EvolveR's 92.7 (+4.1). On Qwen3-8B, EMR averages 81.1 vs MDAgents' 77.8 (+3.3) and single-LLM's 68.6 (+12.5); the abstract reports beating MDAgents by 3.3/2.0/1.5 average points across settings.
- **Ablations (Qwen3-8B)**: removing experience mining drops MedQA from 81.0 to 74.3 (−6.7); removing the hierarchy's principles level costs −4.6 (81.0→76.4), patterns −2.5, cases −1.2; using golden-only experience instead of golden+warning costs −2.6 (81.0→78.4 on MedQA). Keeping experience but removing planner+department agents still yields 78.1 (only −2.9), showing experience partially substitutes for role decomposition.
- **Transferability**: experience mined by GPT-4o lifts Qwen3-8B from 74.3 to 83.9 on MedQA (+9.6) and 71.3 to 81.2 on MedMCQA (+9.9); transfer is bidirectional (Qwen3-mined experience also helps DeepSeek-V3, +5.0 to +6.3). Cross-dataset: MedQA-mined experience applied to MedMCQA gives 74.8 (+3.5 over no-experience 71.3); MedMCQA-mined on MedQA gives 77.5 (+3.2).
- **Efficiency**: EMR uses 1200 avg tokens and 15s per sample, vs MedAgents 1500/18s, MDAgents 1900/23s, RECONCILE 2500/33s (single-LLM CoT: 200/5s); the experience module itself adds only ~300 tokens and ~2s per sample, and mining costs ~$5 per 100 samples (~2 hours for 5 epochs).
- **Robustness / generalizability**: with only 20% of the library retained, MedQA accuracy is 76.8 (above the 75.3 no-experience floor, vs 81.0 full); with 40% injected noise it holds 78.4. Plugging EMR's experience module into MDAgents raises MedQA/GPT-4o from 93.8 to 96.5 (+2.7) and into RECONCILE from 92.1 to 94.8 (+2.7); similarity threshold sweep confirms θ=0.85 optimal (81.0/78.8 vs 80.1/77.9 at θ=0.70 and 80.5/78.3 at θ=0.90).

## Authors & Credibility

Dongsheng Shi, Yue Li, Xin Yi (PhD students/researchers at East China Normal University) and Linlin Wang (corresponding author, ECNU and City University of Hong Kong). The group has a credible track record in NLP and medical LLM agents: the same first-author team has published Surgent (a surgical multi-agent assistance system, arXiv:2605.29368) and an end-to-end TCM clinical-support benchmark (Expert Systems with Applications), plus watermark/security work at ACL. The paper is positioned in a crowded subfield (MedAgents, MDAgents, MDTeamGPT) where the authors know the baseline landscape well. Experiments are averaged over three runs and include ablations, sensitivity sweeps (θ, agent count, retrieval size k), robustness-to-noise tests, efficiency accounting with dollar costs, and a plug-in generalizability study — more thorough than typical for this genre, though no variance or significance statistics are reported in the main text.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty is moderate, not radical**: the recipe — multi-agent role play + distill successes/failures into a memory + retrieve at inference — closely follows ExpeL/ReasoningBank/G-Memory/MDTeamGPT; the genuinely new part is the three-level (principle/pattern/case) hierarchy plus golden/warning duality inside a medical multi-agent loop. The related-work table (Table 9) is fair about this lineage.
- **In-distribution evaluation quirk**: for MedQA and MedMCQA the datasets are "split evenly, using half for experience mining and the other half for evaluation." This is effectively a train/test split from the same distribution, so part of the +6.7 gain may reflect dataset-specific heuristics rather than general clinical wisdom; the cross-dataset transfer numbers (+3.2/+3.5) are noticeably smaller than the same-dataset gains, which is consistent with this concern, though the authors honestly present both.
- **Saturation and contamination risk**: 96.8% on MedQA with GPT-4o leaves almost no headroom on a benchmark whose test questions are widely present in web-scale pretraining corpora; the headline MedQA delta (+4.1 over EvolveR) may partly reflect benchmark memorization interacting with experience prompting rather than improved clinical reasoning. The marginal MedMCQA gain (+0.6 over MDTeamGPT's 88.6) suggests gains shrink on harder, less-contaminated data.
- **Weak spots in evidence**: no error bars or statistical significance despite many sub-1-point deltas (e.g., +0.6 MedMCQA, θ=0.85 vs 0.80 differences of 0.3–0.6); no human evaluation of mined experience quality (only a claim of human-readability); baseline tuning effort for ExpeL/ReasoningBank/G-Memory on GPT-4o is unclear, and these general-purpose methods underperform static MDAgents there, hinting they may not have been adapted.
- **Reproducibility**: prompts for collector/planner/department/summary agents are fully provided in the appendix, hyperparameters (θ=0.85, k=3, m=3, n=2, 5 epochs, bge-m3) are stated, and costs are quantified — good. However, results averaged over 3 runs without variance makes exact reproduction claims hard to check, and the mining/eval split code is not described in enough detail to guarantee identical partitions.
- **Significance**: the plug-in result (MDAgents +2.7, RECONCILE +2.7) and cross-backbone transfer are the most convincing evidence that the mechanism, not the specific architecture, drives gains. Red flags: experience libraries built from model-generated trajectories can propagate hallucinated patterns (acknowledged by the authors); the "collective hallucination" failure mode they catalog is exactly the failure their warning experiences could reinforce if mislabeled.

## Strengths

- Clean decomposition of "self-evolution" into mining, hierarchical storage, bounded update, and hierarchical retrieval — each step ablated separately (components, hierarchy levels, experience types, θ, k, agent count, library size/noise).
- Learning from failures (warning experiences) is principled and empirically validated (+2.6 over golden-only), addressing error repetition that static multi-agent systems suffer.
- Strong transfer results: cross-backbone (+5.0 to +10.0), cross-dataset, and cross-architecture (into MDAgents/RECONCILE) support the claim that the experience library is model- and architecture-agnostic.
- Efficiency-conscious design: drops MedAgents-style multi-round consensus in favor of experience-based conflict resolution; 1200 tokens/15s per sample is cheaper than all multi-agent baselines compared.
- Honest limitations and risk section: text-only scope, noise propagation, offline-only validation, privacy concerns, and explicit "not clinical advice" framing.

## Limitations

- Text-based medical MCQ benchmarks only; no multimodal inputs (imaging, labs, EHR), and no real clinical deployment or human-in-the-loop evaluation.
- Experience quality is bounded by the underlying LLM's reasoning; hallucinated trajectories can inject noisy experiences, with only similarity-based merging as a filter — no independent verification of mined experiences.
- Retrieval is purely semantic-similarity based and may miss causal structure; library maintenance and retrieval efficiency at larger scales are unaddressed empirically.
- Evaluation design (half-dataset mining splits) leaves same-distribution leakage concerns; no significance testing; near-saturated MedQA numbers limit interpretability of headline gains.
- ~6x token cost and 3x latency vs single-LLM CoT (1200 vs 200 tokens; 15s vs 5s), which matters for resource-constrained deployment.

## Relevance

该论文直接命中"多智能体系统 + 自我进化/经验记忆 + LLM 推理"这一核心兴趣组合：它把 test-time 经验积累做成外挂式、模型无关的知识库，并用系统的消融和迁移实验验证了哪些抽象层级真正带来泛化，对理解 agent 记忆机制设计（分层抽象 vs 平铺存储、正负经验并用）很有参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How much of the same-dataset gain survives when the mining half and evaluation half come from genuinely different distributions (e.g., mine on MedMCQA, evaluate on MedQA with disjoint question templates), beyond the reported +3.2/+3.5 cross-dataset numbers?
- Can mined experiences be verified against authoritative sources (clinical guidelines, UpToDate-style knowledge bases) before entering the library, to prevent hallucinated principles from compounding across epochs?
- What is the scaling behavior of retrieval latency and library quality as the library grows 10–100x, and would learned (rather than cosine) retrieval improve causal match?
- Does the golden/warning asymmetry generalize to agentic tasks with delayed or graded feedback (treatment outcomes) instead of instant ground-truth MCQ labels?
- Would the three-level hierarchy help in non-medical multi-agent domains (code generation, web agents), or is the principle/pattern/case structure inherently clinical?
