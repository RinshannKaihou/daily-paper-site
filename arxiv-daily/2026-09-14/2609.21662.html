Key metric — effect surviving the transition

1–5%

of the explicit-CoT stance-token probability shift is retained by latent-CoT steering at λ=10 — despite larger representation displacement (13.43σ vs 11.93σ).

Sentiment steering success, Llama-3.1-8B: 46.0% → 3.0%

Why this matters

If you probe or steer continuous-thought models, in-space decodability and displacement are not enough — evaluation must cross the transition into language.

对想在连续思维空间做探测、转向或表征干预的工作，「过渡边界必须纳入评估」这一结论有直接的方法论参考价值。

⚠ Critical edge

Explicit- and latent-CoT checkpoints do not share weights — reasoning mode is not isolated as the sole causal variable, so part of the gap may come from the weights, not the interface.

Acknowledged by the authors · single layer (16), single method (CAA), single latent depth · out-of-domain stance evaluation

Where the effect dies — the paper in one line

  1. Continuous thoughts ×5

    CAA steering displaces hidden states 13.43σ vs CoT's 11.93σ — the intervention itself works, and task info stays decodable (AUROC 0.842–1.000).

  2. ✂ transition boundary

    Token distributions jump abruptly: Rtransition 14.77–24.22 vs CoT 1.13–1.25 · bidirectional gain Godd weakens 26.6–70.2×

  3. Language generation

    Stance shift collapses to 1–5% of what explicit CoT delivers — information present, representations moved, output control lost.

Methodology, distilled

Explicit-CoT vs COCONUT-style latent-CoT (five continuous thoughts) variants of Llama-3.1-8B and Llama-2-7B — same ProSQA training data — steered with Contrastive Activation Addition at the layer-16 residual stream, λ ∈ {±1, ±5, ±10}, on sentiment, TruthGen (truthfulness), and TwinViews-13k (politics).

  • Layer 1 — output: steering success rate judged by an LLM (n = 200/cell).
  • Layer 2 — generation, judge-free: stance margin p(V⁺) − p(V⁻) at the first generation position, before any sampling — decoding stochasticity and judge bias excluded.
  • Layer 3 — representation: normalized displacement along an independently estimated stance coordinate (n = 100) — separates "moved the state" from "changed the output".
  • Diagnostics: held-out AUROC probing; a Jensen–Shannon transition ratio computed through the model's own LM head (reconstructed logits verified to match native); bidirectional gain Godd with paired CIs, replicated on both model families.

  • The intervention does not fail — displacement is larger in latent mode at every λ and on both models.
  • Information is not missing — AUROC 0.842–1.000; politics stays decodable at all five continuous-thought positions.
  • Directional control is what's lost — Godd falls 26.6–70.2× on Llama-3.1-8B and 9.4–13.7× on Llama-2-7B; the gap is family-dependent and unexplained.

Strengths

  • Three-layer measurement pins the failure to the transition — not the intervention, not missing information.
  • Judge-free margin computed before sampling removes decoding stochasticity and LLM-judge bias from the primary evidence.
  • Cross-architecture replication (Llama-3.1-8B + Llama-2-7B) with paired bootstrap and paired-sample CIs.
  • Honest scoping — the paper itself names the checkpoint confound and what the experiments do not establish.
  • Actionable implication stated plainly: optimize latent steering for its effect on generation, evaluated across the boundary.

Limitations

  • Checkpoints differ in weights — reasoning mode is not isolated as the sole causal variable.
  • One layer (16), one steering method (CAA), one latent depth (5) — no ablations; could be a layer-16/CAA artifact.
  • Trained on ProSQA but evaluated on out-of-domain stance tasks — the gap may be inflated.
  • The JS spike is partially mechanical — reading unsupervised latent positions through the LM head is the very operation they were never trained to support; Godd is the stronger evidence.
  • No code release; sentiment dataset unnamed; COCONUT training recipe not fully given.
  • Purely diagnostic — no transition-aware fix proposed; the explicit-CoT baseline is itself weak in some cells (politics SR 14.0%).

Questions for further exploration

  • Does the gap persist at other layers, with other methods (RepE, SAE steering), or at other latent depths — an interface property or a CAA-at-layer-16 artifact?
  • Can the checkpoint confound be removed — one model switching modes, or matched-weight soft-thought variants?
  • Is the abrupt JS spike partly an artifact of reading latent positions through the LM head?
  • Would token-level distillation targets in latent space close the gap?
  • Does the effect hold on in-domain (ProSQA-style) tasks?
  • Can a transition-aware steering direction — optimized for end-of-transition output change — recover explicit-CoT-level control?
