01

Methodology

fixed budget: 256 seqs
W ≈ A·B per matrix

Every weight matrix becomes rank-r factors under a fixed budget of 256 WikiText-2 sequences — no instruction or recovery data, no new inference-time parameters. Each level warm-starts the next, progressively widening the optimization scope.

L1

Whitened SVD per-matrix · closed form

Truncated SVD of W·S (S = Cholesky of XX⊤) over calibration activations — the Eckart–Young optimum per matrix, but blind to cross-projection interactions.

L2

Block-level joint fit 7 factor pairs / block

Loss = cross-frontier MSE (the compressed block must rebuild the original block's output from an already-degraded input, absorbing upstream drift) + KL on token distributions seen one block ahead through a training-only probe matrix that mimics the full model's final-layer distribution.

L3

End-to-end refinement all factors · warm-started

Standard next-token cross-entropy on the same 256 sequences (AdamW, cosine schedule); validation used only for early stopping.

Evaluated on WikiText-2 / PTB / C4 perplexity plus seven zero-shot tasks (lm-eval-harness v0.4); ρ ∈ {0.2–0.8} on LLaMA-7B, ρ=0.6 on four further architectures. Framing inspired by progressive widening of optimization scope in quantum many-body methods.

Architecture-specific configurations, not one common protocol — cross-row comparisons are descriptive.

  • OOD perplexity roughly halved at ρ=0.6: PTB / C4 = 62.8 / 57.2 vs SAES-SVD's 117 / 94.0; zero-shot 7-task avg 35.6% vs 33.3% (dense 51.6%).
  • The lookahead probe is L2's engine: 2–3× amplification of PPL reduction — without it targets cut Mistral Wiki PPL by 20–23%; with it, 53–59%.
  • L2 is an OOD regularizer: skipping it keeps in-distribution PPL but costs 24 PTB points; the gap widens from Δ22 to Δ72 as calibration shrinks 256 → 32 sequences.
  • Deployment: params −58% (6.74B → 2.85B), peak memory 14.6 → 7.4 GB — but decode runs 6–14% slower (two unfused GEMMs), and L2 costs 13.76 h of a ~14.5 h pipeline.
  • Compute ↔ data: skip-L2 with a 1024-sequence pool ≈ full chain at 256 (Wiki 10.71 vs 11.41), though 1.18 PPL worse on C4.
03

Strengths

  • Clean diagnosis — whitened SVD provably attains Eckart–Young, so the remaining 36.4-PPL gap must live at interaction scales; the three-level design follows almost inevitably.
  • Elegant lookahead probe — inference-free way to give a block-local objective a vocabulary-space, next-block signal; ablations isolate it, not target choice, as the driver.
  • Exceptional honesty — own LiLlama counter-result reported, compute–data trade quantified in nats/token, decode slowdown disclosed, claims explicitly scoped.
  • Actionable guidance — L1 suffices at ρ≤0.2; L2 matters most at 0.4–0.6 and under calibration scarcity; full chain for ρ≥0.8.
03

Limitations

  • Capability not preserved — zero-shot 35.6% vs dense 51.6%; GSM8K 0% exact match; degenerate repetitive SAMSum summaries at ρ=0.6.
  • L2 dominates offline cost (95% of the pipeline) and its advantage exists only in the small-calibration regime; skip-L2 is competitive at 1024+ sequences.
  • Lookahead sees one block ahead only; block matching is pointwise MSE in dense activation space — transition-geometry and SAE-basis alternatives untested.
  • Cross-architecture claims descriptive; LLaMA-2-7B PTB anomaly unresolved; no 70B / instruction / safety / long-context eval; no code release; calibration drawn at random; quantization combos untested.
04

Questions for Further Exploration

from the source summary
  1. Would the full chain at a 1024-sequence pool dominate skip-L2 on all three benchmarks, or does compute–data substitutability persist at larger budgets?
  2. Can the lookahead extend beyond one block — a learned multi-block cascade or shared probe — letting L2 capture what only L3 sees?
  3. Does stacking GPTQ/AWQ-style INT4 quantization on the factored form preserve the perplexity gains at aggressive settings?
  4. Why does a LiLlama-style warm start compose so badly with L3 (step-0 selection on Mistral-7B) — a spectral/rank pathology, and would renormalization fix it?
  5. Can coverage- or sensitivity-based calibration-sequence selection shrink the data price of skipping L2 further?
  6. Do fused AB-kernels or CUDA-graph execution erase the 6–14% decode slowdown — and does the memory win survive at 70B scale?
