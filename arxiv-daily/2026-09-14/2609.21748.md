> **一句话总结：** 通过对 TaxiGPT（在曼哈顿随机游走上训练的 GPT-2）的机制可解释性分析，本文证明该模型内部其实编码了高度忠实的曼哈顿街道地图（99.6% 路口可解码、99.4% 合法移动集可恢复），其此前被归因于"地图混乱"的行为失败实则源于叠加（superposition）表示之间的干扰破坏了定位，并发现"按可供性打包"（affordance packing）的表示组织方式能显著限制定位滑移的行为代价。

> **TL;DR:** Vafa et al. (2024) concluded that a GPT-2 trained on Manhattan taxi rides ("TaxiGPT") was "very far from recovering the true street map of New York City" because its reconstructed maps looked like spaghetti. This paper overturns that behavioral diagnosis with causal mechanistic analysis: the RW-trained GPT2-XL decodes 99.6% of intersections at ≥90% held-out accuracy (layer 18) and recovers the exact legal-move set for 99.4% of intersection features; navigation uses a causal "goal compass" (18.1° median angular error). The apparent maplessness is traced to interference between 4,516 intersection features superposed into ~244 of 1,600 residual dimensions: under the out-of-distribution "deep-and-far" regime of the stress/detour tests, the current-position write weakens and a wrong intersection feature fires. Reinforcing the correct position signal at L11 lifts stress-test legality from 91.5% to 97.3% and detour success from 63.1% to 71.9%. The authors propose mechanistic indicators (decode, causal teleportation, compass, street probes) that dissociate map fidelity from map use, and show across training that navigation capacities (goal compass, legal moves) mature before precise localization.

## 核心贡献

- **推翻"无世界模型"的行为学结论**：对 Vafa et al. (2024) 的 TaxiGPT（GPT2-XL，48 层 × 1600 维，在曼哈顿地图随机游走上训练）做机制分析，证明模型内部存在高度忠实的地图——第 18 层的 diff-means 路口特征在留出数据上以 ≥90% 准确率解码 99.6% 的路口，传送编辑（teleportation edit）因果测试在 99.3% 的可测节点上成功，街道连接（路口—移动—下一路口转移）也能通过 steering 测试（76.6% 街道 top-1、93.3% top-5）与线性探针（89.2% 留出查询）恢复。
- **定位与导航是两个独立的"世界建模能力"**：模型通过"回看窗口"（look-back window）维护运行位置估计；导航由两个互补机制驱动——路口特征提升合法移动 logits（+12.6）、抑制非法移动（−4.5），以及一个编码目标方向的"目标罗盘"（goal compass，循环表示，第 16 层，中位角度误差 18.1°）。消融罗盘保留合法性但严重损害到达远处目标的能力。
- **把失败归因于叠加干扰而非地图缺失**：4,516 个路口方向被叠加压缩到 1600 维残差流的 244 维子空间（最近邻夹角平均 48.1°）；在压力/绕路测试的分布外"深且远"（deep-and-far）状态下，当前位置写入减弱、噪声升高，使错误路口特征被激活。四类失败模式（致命滑移 25%、静默滑移 24%、完全损坏 29%、放弃滑移 16%）解释了 93.5% 的非法移动，且去除噪声 / 强化写入 / 移除错误节点激活均可因果性地大幅恢复合法性。
- **发现"按可供性打包"（affordance packing）及其鲁棒性收益**：合法移动集合相同的路口（104 组）表示相互靠近（91% 的路口与自身组的均值余弦相似度最高），使许多定位滑移"良性化"（错误路口上合法的移动在真实路口也合法），回看窗口随后可恢复正确定位。
- **提出可跨模型比较的机制指标并刻画训练中的涌现顺序**：解码、因果传送、街道探针、罗盘等指标显示导航能力（合法移动、目标罗盘）先于精确定位能力成熟；数据（随机游走）是最重要杠杆，NextLat（下一隐状态预测）目标使街道编码最好；行为分数更好的小模型其实在机制指标上并不更好，说明"会用图"与"有图"可以脱钩。

## Key Contributions

- **Reverses a behavioral diagnosis with mechanistic evidence.** Using diff-means features, linear probes, logit lens, steering, ablations, and "teleportation" causal edits, the authors show the random-walk TaxiGPT (GPT2-XL, 48L × 1600d, from Vafa et al. 2024) contains a faithful Manhattan map: 99.6% of intersections decode at ≥90% accuracy at layer 18; teleportation succeeds for 99.3% of testable nodes; the exact legal-move set is recovered for 99.4% of intersection features via logit lens at layer 31 (1% threshold); streets are recovered by a steering test (76.6% top-1, 93.3% top-5) and held-out linear probes (89.2%).
- **Decomposes "world modeling" into interacting capacities.** Localization (a running position read from a look-back window over past positions — erasing past positions hurts decode while erasing past moves does not) is separated from navigation (a legal-move mechanism writing +12.6/−4.5 logits, plus a circular goal compass decoded best at layer 16 with 18.1° median angular error; ablating it keeps moves legal but breaks far-goal reaching).
- **Traces the spaghetti-map failures to superposition interference.** 4,516 intersection directions live in a ~244-dimensional subspace (90% variance) of the 1,600-dim residual stream; in the out-of-distribution deep-and-far regime (5.4% of stress states vs 0.1% in training) the position write weakens and noise lets a wrong node dominate. Four failure modes (fatal 25%, silent 24%, full corruption 29%, give-up 16%) cover 93.5% of illegal moves, and targeted edits (remove noise, strengthen write, remove wrong-node component) causally repair them.
- **Identifies affordance packing as a robustness strategy.** Intersections sharing legal-move sets cluster (91% assign highest cosine to their own group mean among 104 groups), so many localization slips stay behaviorally benign — a distinct benefit from prior work on correlated/hierarchical packing.
- **Introduces mechanistic indicators of world modeling.** Cross-model comparison (SP/NSP/RW data × NTP/NextLat objectives × model sizes) and training-curve analysis show navigation capacities emerge before localization, data is the strongest lever (RW ≫ SP/NSP), and NextLat best encodes streets — with behavioral proxies shown to mislead (small models behave better yet have comparable-or-worse mechanistic indicators; the large RW model slips 3–6× more).

## Methodology

The study is a mechanistic-interpretability case analysis of an existing trained model rather than a new training method. The subject is the best random-walk TaxiGPT from Vafa et al. (2024): GPT2-XL (48 layers, 1,600 dims) trained on next-token prediction of taxi turns over Manhattan's street graph, where prompts encode origin, destination, and previous turns.

1. **Feature extraction.** Intersection representations are extracted with diff-means (class-mean difference vectors) and linear probes; diff-means features win. Decode accuracy is swept over layers (best at L18); causality is established via a minimal teleportation edit that shifts the encoded position next to the goal and checks whether greedy prediction reaches the goal (best at L11).
2. **Street recovery.** Two complementary tests: a steering test (inject an intersection feature, feed a legal move, measure which intersection feature grows most) and per-move linear probes predicting the next intersection's feature direction during rides, evaluated at held-out intersections.
3. **Geometry of the position code.** PCA variance profiles (244/1600 dims for 90% variance), nearest-neighbor angles (48.1°), and affordance grouping (104 legal-move-set groups; own-group cosine test) characterize superposition and packing.
4. **Localization and navigation circuits.** Ablation contrasts (erase past positions vs past moves) identify the look-back window; logit-lens attribution quantifies the legal-move mechanism's logit writes; the goal compass is found by binning rides into 16 goal-bearing bins, extracting per-bin diff-means directions, and fitting a compass plane — validated by steering over 12 moves and by ablation.
5. **Failure-mode forensics.** The stress, detour, and compression evaluations of Vafa et al. are re-run with internal instrumentation: true-node write strength, position-subspace noise, cosine of the wrongly-activated feature, shared affordance, and depth/goal-distance regimes. Failures are classified into four modes, each causally probed by matched interventions (remove wrong-node component, clear noise, rebuild the write) with random-direction edits as controls.
6. **Comparative indicators.** The same probing suite is applied across five training configurations (SP/NSP/RW × NTP; RW × NextLat; two model scales) and across training checkpoints (384d RW·NTP run), yielding "mechanistic indicators" contrasted with behavioral proxies (stress legality, detour success, compression score).

## Results & Highlights

- **Map fidelity (Manhattan, 4,516 intersections):** 99.6% of intersections decode with ≥90% held-out accuracy at layer 18 (vs the "no coherent map" reading of Vafa et al.); logit-lens recovers the exact legal-move set for 99.4% of intersection features; teleportation edits succeed for 99.3% of testable nodes; street probing recovers the correct successor for 89.2% of held-out state–move queries (steering: 76.6% top-1 / 93.3% top-5).
- **Goal compass causality:** goal bearing decodes at layer 16 with median angular error 18.1° on held-out rides; steering the compass makes the model follow the chosen direction over 12 moves (median deviation 18.1°); the legal-move mechanism writes +12.62/+12.51 logits to legal moves and −4.52/−4.51 to illegal ones.
- **Stress-test failure accounting:** under the stress test the model still reaches the goal on 81% of pairs and its position decodes at 99% accuracy, but 8.5% of rides take an off-graph move; four superposition-interference failure modes (fatal 25%, silent 24%, full corruption 29%, give-up 16%) explain 93.5% of illegal moves; clearing position noise in fatal slips removes 0.80 of illegal probability mass (vs ≤9% for matched random edits).
- **Causal repair of Vafa et al.'s three tests:** continuously reinforcing the correct position at L11 improves stress-test legality from 91.5% to 97.3%, detour success from 63.1% to 71.9%, and compression score from 0.524 to 0.691 — showing the position code is intact and usable, just mis-recruited out of distribution.
- **Compression metric reinterpreted:** in all 146 prefix pairs inspected (including the 94 failing ones) both prefixes decode to the correct shared intersection; lengthening suffixes drops compression from 0.983 to 0.167 while prefixes still decode correctly in 99.8% of conditions, and compression correlates with illegal-move rate at r = −0.957 across seven distance bands.
- **Cross-configuration comparison (Table 1):** random-walk data dominates — RW·NTP decodes 99.6% of intersections vs 12.7% for SP·NTP and 14.9% for NSP·NTP (behavioral stress legality 91.5% vs 71.7%/74.7%); RW·NextLat achieves the best mechanistic scores (99.9% decode, best street encoding); smaller RW models are behaviorally better (96.7%/97.3% stress legality) despite weaker decode, slipping 3–6× less than the 1600d model.
- **Emergence ordering:** in the tracked run, legal-move and goal-compass representations mature before precise localization (decode/causal); the position code expands over training (nearest-neighbor angles grow) and affordance packing rises early, then partially relaxes as intersections differentiate.

Credibility signals: a reproducibility statement with code and instructions at github.com/bepierre/world-modeling plus a project website; matched random-edit controls for every causal intervention; failure-mode statistics reported with medians and held-out intersections; and an explicit AI-use statement disclosing heavy LLM involvement in experiment design, coding, figures, and revision, with the authors taking responsibility. The core claims about Vafa et al.'s benchmarks are verifiable against the original released model and data.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** The headline reversal — a behaviorally "mapless" model actually encoding a near-perfect map — is a genuinely valuable corrective to how behavioral world-model evaluations are read, and the affordance-packing robustness story is a new, specific benefit of feature grouping. That said, the core ingredients (diff-means probing, logit lens, steering/ablation, teleportation edits, interference-from-superposition) are standard interpretability tools; the contribution is synthesis and forensic depth, not new technique.
- **Rigor:** Strong on causal controls (random-direction matched edits, held-out intersections, layered interventions per failure mode). Weaker spots: the teleportation criterion "succeeds on at least one eligible scene for 99.3% of nodes" is a low bar (one scene suffices); the training-dynamics conclusions (Fig. 7) come from a single 384d run, so the claimed emergence ordering could be run-specific; and the compression reinterpretation rests on 146 pairs with seven distance bands.
- **Reproducibility:** Good — public code, project website, appendix protocols, and re-analysis of a publicly released model (GPT2-XL checkpoints from Vafa et al.). The dependency on Vafa et al.'s exact training setup is both a strength (controlled comparison) and a fragility if those checkpoints change.
- **Significance:** The reframing "world modeling as interacting capacities (represent, localize, recruit, guide)" is conceptually useful and the mechanistic indicators are a concrete step toward non-behavioral evaluation. But everything is established on one toy domain: a finite-state, deterministic-transition gridworld (Manhattan street graph) with ~4.5k states and a tiny move vocabulary. The authors concede this explicitly; whether the indicators transfer to LLM-scale or richer environments is untested.
- **Red flags:** The mechanistic indicators are proposed as a general evaluation toolkit but validated only in this domain; the causal-repair patch (adding the correct diff-means vector at L11) uses ground-truth position information, so it is a diagnostic, not a training fix; the "distance-to-goal write weakening is intentional" claim rests on a single destination-token-swap experiment (87% of cases, median decrease 59); and the disclosure that LLMs were used "throughout the research and writing process" warrants extra scrutiny of the numbers, though the causal-control structure mitigates this.

## Strengths

- Turns a well-known negative result (Vafa et al.'s spaghetti maps) into a sharp methodological lesson — behavior underdetermines mechanism — and backs it with causal, not merely correlational, evidence.
- Complete failure accounting: the four failure modes plus unlucky draws explain 99.5% of illegal moves, and each mode is validated by interventions with matched random-edit controls.
- The affordance-packing finding gives a functional (loss-reducing, robustness-increasing) explanation for why states with the same legal moves cluster, reconciling an effect Vafa et al. (2025) viewed critically with genuine world modeling.
- The mechanistic-indicator table cleanly dissociates map fidelity from map use — smaller models behave better while being mechanistically worse at localization — something no behavioral metric could reveal.
- Honest scoping: explicit acknowledgment that the environment is finite-state and deterministic, plus a reproducibility statement and public code.

## Limitations

- Single toy environment (Manhattan street graph, ~4,516 states, six move tokens); no evidence yet that the indicators or the failure taxonomy transfer to richer, stochastic, or partially observable worlds, let alone language models at scale.
- The analysis is a post-hoc study of one pre-existing checkpoint family from Vafa et al. (2024); no new model is trained to test whether the proposed fixes (e.g., training on the deep-and-far regime) actually work.
- The position-reinforcement repair requires knowing the true intersection, so it diagnoses rather than solves the recruitment problem.
- Emergence results come from one training run at 384d; cross-seed variance is unreported.
- Feature extraction relies on diff-means/linear probes under a linear-features assumption; sparse-autoencoder or nonlinear alternatives are not compared.
- Some headline percentages rest on modest sample sizes (146 compression pairs; per-category shares of a few hundred illegal rides), and the goal-compass identification inherits known circularity pitfalls from the 16-bin binning procedure.

## Relevance

对研究 transformer 内部是否形成世界模型及其可解释性评估直接相关：本文提供了"行为失败 ≠ 表示缺失"的因果分析范式、可复用的机制指标（解码/传送/街道探针/罗盘），以及叠加干扰与 affordance packing 的失败—鲁棒性机制，可迁移到 Othello/棋类/迷宫之外的自定义序列环境评估中。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Do the four superposition-interference failure modes (fatal/silent/full-corruption/give-up) recur in other sequence models with superposed state codes (Othello-GPT, chess transformers, maze solvers), or are they specific to random-walk training?
- Would simply augmenting training data with the deep-and-far regime (5.4% under stress vs 0.1% in training) eliminate most illegal moves, as the data-lever hypothesis predicts?
- Can the ground-truth-free version of the position-reinforcement repair (e.g., denoising the position subspace or sharpening the look-back window read-out) recover the same gains (91.5%→97.3% stress legality)?
- Why do smaller models slip 3–6× less than the 1600d model despite worse mechanistic indicators — is later-layer, spread-out localization (L27–L40 vs L11–L18) inherently more robust to interference?
- Does the emergence ordering (goal compass and legality before localization) replicate across seeds, scales, and objectives, and does early compass acquisition causally facilitate later map precision as the authors speculate?
- Can the mechanistic indicators be scaled to natural-language domains where "states" are not enumerable — e.g., tracking entities or locations in narrative text — without hand-labeled state ground truth?
