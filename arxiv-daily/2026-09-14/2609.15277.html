一句话总结

在 Llama 3.1 8B 第 12 层残差流中恢复出线性「机会识别方向」，并通过激活转向完成对创业构念的首次因果干预。

Why this matters

A reusable locate-and-steer recipe for auditing and manipulating any internal LLM capability — safety, bias, domain judgment — not just entrepreneurship.

为审计、调控 LLM 内部能力（安全性、偏见、领域判断）提供理论驱动、混淆受控的可复用方法论模板。

Critical edge

The feasibility direction moves the OR readout more strongly than the OR direction itself — the dial is causally involved, but not demonstrably selective.

OR-up also fails a KL-matched strengthened null (p=0.091); the full causal-selectivity pipeline runs on one model; Qwen3-8B reverses.
Method / how the dial was found
  1. Build contrastive scenarios. Claude Opus 4.7 generates 5,000 OR-present / OR-absent pairs under strict vocabulary, token-overlap and batch rules; an AFLite-style adversarial filter keeps 636, cutting bag-of-words leakage 0.912 → 0.528 (chance 0.500).
  2. Extract & estimate. TransformerLens records residual-stream activations across all 32 layers of Llama 3.1 8B-Instruct; layer and read position chosen on training data only; difference-in-means direction (primary) plus a logistic probe (corroboration).
  3. Intervene. Steering adds α·r at the operating layer; α=3.5 set by a KL coherence gate (KL ≤ 0.100) — chosen for coherence, not maximal effect. Readouts: forced-choice log-odds on 665 scenarios; free text scored by an independent judge (DeepSeek V4 Pro).
  4. Test the geometry. Feasibility, desirability and exploitation directions estimated from independent batteries; cosines compared against split-half reliability (0.925) and the ~0.016 random scale. Replication in four further LLMs.

0.884of the OR direction lies outside the joint span of feasibility, desirability and exploitation (R² = 0.116, CI [0.086, 0.138]) — the constructs are geometrically separable even though they are causally coupled.

Coupling, not mediation: OR-up shifts feasibility +0.728 · desirability +0.338 · exploitation +0.483; ablating OR lowers all three (−0.404 vs random ablation, p<0.001).

Strengths
  • Turns a decades-old conceptual debate — recognition vs. evaluation vs. exploitation — into an experimentally manipulable question, with recovery, intervention and geometry converging.
  • Exemplary confound discipline for a steering paper: banned vocabulary, token-overlap bands, AFLite filtering, topic holdouts, commerciality erasure, coherence-gated α.
  • Multimodal evidence: log-odds, LLM-judged free text, ablation, role-prompting comparison, and an SAE check (top feature cosine 0.341 → distributed, not monosemantic).
  • Unusual reporting honesty — valence-dissociation failure, KL-matched nulls and the Qwen3-8B reversal appear in the main text, not an appendix.
  • Recovery-and-steering pattern replicates across five open-weight models, cross-family and cross-scale.
Limitations
  • Synthetic, third-person scenarios only — no pitch transcripts or investor memos; OR labels rest on the generation spec with no independent human coding.
  • Causal specificity is asymmetric: ablation and OR-down are strong; OR-up fails a KL-matched strengthened null (p=0.091).
  • Full causal pipeline runs on one model; null-tier separation fully reappears in only two of four replications.
  • Single calibrated steering point (α=3.5); the stronger all-position result (win rate 0.971) breaks the coherence bound (KL 0.671) and is treated only as a ceiling.
  • No downstream utility test — does steering improve real opportunity search, or just inflate yes-rates?
Open questions / where this goes next
Q1Does the OR direction survive naturally occurring material — pitch transcripts, investor memoranda, YC applications — where confounds are far noisier?
Q2Can circuit-level analysis resolve the feasibility anomaly and establish genuine recognition → evaluation → exploitation ordering, rather than mere coupling?
Q3Is OR a single direction or a low-dimensional subspace — and do the Qwen3-8B reversal and Gemma weak gap reflect different geometries or calibration artifacts?
Q4What is the false-positive cost of turning the dial up — better opportunity search, or just more “yes”?
