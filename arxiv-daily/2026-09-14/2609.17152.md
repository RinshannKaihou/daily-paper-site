> **一句话总结：** 作者诊断出 ViT 上 LRP 归因噪声的根源是残差相加处的"前向抵消"（attention/MLP 更新与残差流符号相反，导致除以接近零的输出而引发 relevance 爆炸），并提出 ResLRP——在残差合并处改用带符号一致性加权的 LRP-γ 规则（单个超参 γ=1，一个 backward hook 即可实现），在保持精确守恒的同时把逐点放大率限制在 1+2/γ；在 7 个 ViT 骨干 + FunnyBirds 基准上全面超过 AttnLRP（SRG 提升 0.7–4.7 分），在 3 个现代 VLM 上收益最大（定位 +27–29%，忠实度最高 3.4 倍），并通过通道级干预实验证实增益确实来自纠正残差抵消而非一般正则化效应。

> **TL;DR:** This paper identifies residual-path cancellation (attention/MLP updates opposing the residual stream, making the post-addition activation near zero and inflating LRP's division denominator) as the key source of noisy, unstable attributions in Vision Transformers — an effect measured to be far stronger in ViTs (early-layer cancellation C ≈ 1.7) than in LLMs (C ≈ 1.4). The fix, ResLRP, applies a sign-consistency-weighted LRP-γ rule at residual merges: exactly conservative, provably bounded per-merge amplification (|Rin|+|Rup| ≤ (1+2/γ)|Rout|, vs unbounded for the standard rule), one backward hook, one hyperparameter (γ=1, on a broad plateau). On ImageNet/ImageNet-S across seven ViT backbones it beats AttnLRP by 0.7–4.7 SRG faithfulness points (e.g., ViT-B/16: 3.20 vs 1.51, where AttnLRP's localization 0.32 falls below the 0.37 random baseline), wins all four completeness metrics plus correctness on the ground-truth FunnyBirds benchmark (SD 0.774 vs 0.682, +13% over AttnLRP), and delivers its largest gains on three VLMs (Qwen2.5-VL-3B, Qwen3-VL-4B-it, Gemma-3-4B-it): localization +27–29% relative and SRG up to 3.4× (Qwen3-VL: 13.04 vs 3.87). Channel-wise interventions confirm causality: correcting only the top 0.5% most cancellation-prone channels on ViT-B/16 gives +7.9% SRG while correcting a 150× larger set of low-cancellation channels gives −5.5%.

## 核心贡献

- **诊断出 ViT 归因不稳定的根源是残差抵消（residual cancellation）**：当 attention/MLP 更新 z_up 与残差流 z_in 方向相反时，相加后输出 z_out 很小，标准 LRP ε-规则除以这一小量，把少量输出 relevance 放大成巨大的正负对（"relevance 爆炸"）。实测该效应在 ViT 早期层最强（C ≈ 1.7 vs LLM 的 ≈ 1.4），且前向抵消度 C 与反向放大率 A 的 Pearson 相关高达 0.99。
- **提出 ResLRP**：把经典 LRP-γ 规则适配到残差相加——分支符号与输出一致时权重乘 (1+γ)，从而压制相互抵消的矛盾通路。规则精确守恒（Rin + Rup = Rout），并证明每次合并的放大率上界为 1 + 2/γ（γ=1 时 A ≤ 3），而标准规则在单个抵消点就无有限上界。实现上只需在每个残差相加处加一个 backward hook。
- **三重实证 + 因果验证**：在涵盖监督、自监督（DINOv2）、对比（CLIP/SigLIP2）、层次（SwinV2）、多模态五类预训练的骨干上，用遮挡式 SRG 忠实度、ImageNet-S 定位、FunnyBirds 真值基准三种证据一致支持 ResLRP；并通过通道级干预（只纠正最易抵消的 0.5% 通道即 +7.9% SRG，纠正 150 倍多的低抵消通道反而 −5.5%）排除"一般正则化效应"的解释。
- **扩展到两个高价值场景**：把 Sparse Autoencoder 特征定位回输入空间（ActMap 基线在深层完全失效甚至反相关 −0.031，ResLRP SRG 0.449 vs AttnLRP 0.334）；以及对三个现代 VLM 做像素级归因，增益最大（定位 +27–29%，SRG 最高 3.4 倍）。
- **残差放大率 A 作为架构级诊断工具**：发现 register token 本身就在稳定残差流（四对 DINOv2 对照中 A 一致下降，ResLRP 增益相应缩小），并正确预测缺乏 register 的 VLM 抵消最强、从 ResLRP 获益最大。

## Key Contributions

- **Identify residual cancellation as the failure mode**: When an attention or MLP update opposes the residual stream (z_in ≈ −z_up), the post-addition activation z_out is small, so the standard ε-stabilized LRP rule divides by near-zero and amplifies small output relevance into large positive–negative pairs ("relevance explosion"). Cancellation is strongest in early ViT layers (C ≈ 1.7 vs ≈ 1.4 for LLMs), correlates with amplification at Pearson 0.99, and explains why LRP rules that work for language transformers transfer poorly to ViTs.
- **ResLRP, a provably bounded and conservative residual rule**: Adapts the LRP-γ rule to residual additions by up-weighting branches whose sign matches the output (factor 1+γ), suppressing contradictory pathways. It is exactly conservative (Rin + Rup = Rout for all γ > 0) and bounds per-merge amplification at 1 + 2/γ (≤ 3 at the default γ = 1), where the standard rule admits no finite bound even at a single cancellation site. Implementation is a single backward hook per residual addition (full PyTorch code in the appendix, built on zennit + LXT).
- **Comprehensive empirical validation**: Best or tied-best faithfulness (SRG) on six of seven ViT backbones spanning supervised, self-supervised, contrastive, and hierarchical pre-training, improving over AttnLRP by 0.7–4.7 points; best or tied-best localization (0.51–0.64 vs 0.32–0.47 for AttnLRP); best on all four FunnyBirds completeness metrics and correctness.
- **Causal isolation of the mechanism**: Channel-wise interventions applying the γ-rule only to top-cancellation channels (0.5%, 92 channels) improve SRG by +7.9% on ViT-B/16, whereas treating 75% low-cancellation channels (13,824, a 150× larger set) decreases SRG by −5.5%; SRG tracks unstable relevance removed (Spearman ρ = 0.79) far better than channel count (ρ = 0.50). Component ablations show the residual rule carries most of the stability.
- **Two application frontiers plus an architecture-level diagnostic**: ResLRP localizes TopK-SAE features in input space (where naive activation maps become anti-correlated with the latent they visualize), grounds VLM text generation at pixel level with the largest gains of all (+27–29% localization, up to 3.4× SRG), and the amplification measure A reveals that register tokens stabilize the residual stream itself — predicting exactly where attribution degrades.

## Methodology

The paper proceeds in three stages: measurement, rule design, and causal verification.

**1. Quantifying cancellation and amplification.** At each residual addition z_out = z_in + z_up, the authors define over latent entries a forward cancellation score C = Σ(|z_in| + |z_up|) / Σ|z_out| (C = 1 means no cancellation) and a backward amplification score A = Σ(|R_in| + |R_up|) / Σ|R_out|. Comparing ViTs against comparably sized autoregressive LLMs shows cancellation peaking in early ViT layers (C ≈ 1.7 vs ≈ 1.4) and a 0.99 Pearson correlation between C and A. The authors are explicit that this correlation is structurally motivated, not causal proof — cancellation requires downstream relevance to explode (Qwen2.5 shows strong cancellation but little amplification) — and therefore run the interventions described below.

**2. The ResLRP rule.** At a residual addition, relevance is redistributed as R_in = Φ(z_in, z_up) · R_out with Φ(a, b) = (1 + γ·δ[sign(a)=sign(c)])·a / ((1 + γ·δ[sign(a)=sign(c)])·a + (1 + γ·δ[sign(b)=sign(c)])·b), where c = a + b and R_up follows by swapping subscripts. Sign-consistent branches receive the (1+γ) boost; opposing branches are suppressed. Proposition: Φ ∈ [−1/γ, 1 + 1/γ], which with the symmetry Φ(a,b) + Φ(b,a) = 1 yields exact conservation and the per-merge bound |R_in| + |R_up| ≤ (1 + 2/γ)|R_out|, i.e., A ≤ 3 at γ = 1, tightening monotonically in γ (γ → 0 recovers AttnLRP; γ → ∞ gives non-amplifying sign-consistent redistribution). The guarantees are local and structural — full-model attribution quality is established empirically. Implementation uses straight-through-estimator-style `.detach()` tricks so the forward pass is unchanged; only gradients are modified. A composition termed ResLRP+ adds DAVE-style translation averaging over just four forward passes (vs DAVE's 50) to remove patch-grid artifacts at the patch embedding.

**3. Evaluation protocols.** (a) Faithfulness via Symmetric Relevance Gain (SRG): patches ranked by attribution are occluded most-important-first and least-important-first (ImageNet-mean fill, patch grid aligned exactly with the ViT tokenization, 100 steps); SRG is the normalized area between curves. (b) Localization on ImageNet-S segmentation masks: fraction of absolute attribution mass inside the ground-truth object, 500 correctly classified images over 50 seeded classes. (c) FunnyBirds: synthetic birds with removable parts give ground-truth causal part importance (six official metrics). (d) VLMs: attributing a single answer-token logit w.r.t. input pixels on Qwen2.5-VL-3B, Qwen3-VL-4B-it, Gemma-3-4B-it, with per-model occlusion geometry matched to each tokenizer. (e) SAE localization: TopK-SAEs trained at three blocks of a frozen ViT-L/14; the summed post-TopK activation of individual latents is attributed back to pixels and scored with the SRG protocol using the latent activation in place of the classifier logit.

## Results & Highlights

- **Faithfulness (SRG, ImageNet, n = 500)**: ResLRP is the best method on six of seven ViT backbones and within one standard error of the best (LeGrad) on the seventh (ViT-L/14, where LeGrad leads by 0.05), improving over AttnLRP by 0.7–4.7 SRG points. Concrete cells: ViT-B/16 3.20 vs AttnLRP 1.51; ViT-H/14 3.31 vs 1.65; DINOv2-L 4.72 vs 1.65; SigLIP2-L/16 5.26 vs 0.59; SwinV2-L 2.36 vs 0.93 (random ≈ 0).
- **Localization (ImageNet-S, n = 500)**: ResLRP scores 0.51–0.64 vs 0.32–0.47 for AttnLRP, best or tied-best on six of seven models; on ViT-B/16 AttnLRP (0.32) falls below the spatially-uniform random baseline (0.37), directly consistent with the relevance-explosion analysis. Exception: DeiT3-L/16, where attention-rollout variants lead.
- **Ground-truth FunnyBirds (ViT-B/16)**: ResLRP achieves the best score on all four completeness metrics and on correctness — CSDC 0.942 vs 0.926, PC 0.954 vs 0.926, DC 0.916 vs 0.892, Distractor 0.937 vs 0.864 — and single-deletion (causal part importance) SD of 0.774 vs 0.682 for AttnLRP, a +13% improvement, while staying on par with the best on contrastivity (TS 0.950 vs Chefer-LRP's 0.957).
- **Causal channel-wise interventions (ViT-B/16)**: applying the γ-rule to only the top 0.5% most cancellation-prone channels (92 channels) improves SRG by +7.9% over AttnLRP, whereas treating 75% of low-cancellation channels (13,824, a 150× larger set) decreases SRG by −5.5%; across interventions SRG correlates with unstable relevance removed (Spearman ρ = 0.79) much more than with channels modified (ρ = 0.50). Pattern replicates on DINOv2-B (top 2% → +9.9%).
- **Vision-language models (n = 500 per model)**: ResLRP is best on both metrics on all three VLMs — localization improves over AttnLRP by +0.09 to +0.10 absolute (+27% to +29% relative; e.g., Qwen2.5-VL-3B 0.464 vs 0.365) and SRG improves by a factor of 1.9–3.4 (Qwen3-VL-4B-it: 13.04 vs 3.87; Gemma-3-4B-it: 0.41 vs 0.21), with every gap significant under a two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank test (p ≤ 1.7×10⁻²⁶). On Gemma-3-4B-it, AttnLRP does not separate from plain I×G on localization at all (0.352 vs 0.352).
- **SAE feature localization (ViT-L/14 + TopK-SAEs)**: ResLRP attains the best SRG and best mean rank at all three SAE blocks, e.g., 0.449 ± 0.006 vs 0.334 ± 0.006 for AttnLRP at blocks.15, while the conventional ActMap baseline collapses to −0.031 ± 0.006 at blocks.23 — slightly anti-correlated with the very latent it visualizes.
- **Register-token diagnostic (four matched DINOv2 pairs)**: registers consistently reduce amplification A (S: 1.58 → 1.45; B: 1.49 → 1.39; L: 1.32 → 1.28; G: 1.12 → 1.09) and correspondingly shrink ResLRP's localization gain (B: +0.133 → +0.034; G: +0.026 → +0.000), supporting the claim that registers stabilize the residual stream itself.
- **Hyperparameter robustness**: sweeping γ ∈ {0, …, 10} on six models, every γ > 0 beats the AttnLRP baseline (recovered at γ = 0); performance rises to a knee near γ ≈ 0.25 and plateaus, with the untuned γ = 1 achieving 98–100% of best-γ SRG on five of seven models (80–90% on the rest) and 92–100% of best-γ localization on all models.

## Authors & Credibility

The team is from the Fraunhofer Heinrich Hertz Institute (Berend, Achtibat — equal contribution — Schäffer, Samek, Lapuschkin, Dreyer), TU Berlin, ScaDS.AI Leipzig, BIFOLD, Singapore Institute of Technology, and ICGI Oslo. This is the group behind much of the modern LRP literature: they are the authors of AttnLRP (ICML 2024) — the very method this paper improves upon — and of the CRP concept-relevance work in Nature Machine Intelligence; Samek, Binder, Montavon, and Müller are long-standing LRP founders. Evaluating their own prior method as the primary baseline and publishing cases where it fails badly (below-random localization on ViT-B/16, indistinguishable from I×G on Gemma-3) is a credibility signal. Code is released at github.com/jim-berend/ResLRP, built on the maintained zennit/LXT libraries; all checkpoints are public TIMM/HuggingFace models, dataset seeds are disclosed (339306 for SRG sampling, 754068 for localization), FunnyBirds uses the official dataset and metric implementations, and experiments ran on a single RTX 5090 in float32 — the work is clearly reproducible on consumer hardware. Funding is from German public sources (BMFTR, EU Horizon Europe ACHILLES, DFG DeSBi).

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: The diagnosis — residual cancellation as the source of LRP instability in ViTs, with a causal intervention study — is genuinely new; prior transformer-LRP work (AttnLRP, CP-LRP, absLRP) targeted attention and LayerNorm nonlinearities and mostly NLP models. The rule itself is deliberately simple (an LRP-γ adaptation at merges), so the contribution is the analysis, not rule sophistication; this is the right kind of simplicity, but readers should not expect a new propagation paradigm.
- **Rigor**: Above average for an XAI paper. Three complementary evidence types (occlusion faithfulness, segmentation localization, FunnyBirds causal ground truth), Wilcoxon significance tests, a γ sensitivity sweep, component ablations (e.g., with the attention rule removed on SigLIP2, ResLRP still reaches SRG 3.27 vs AttnLRP's −0.004), and a preregistered-style falsification test of the regularization alternative. Weaknesses: the FunnyBirds ground-truth evaluation covers only a single small checkpoint (the ViT-B/16 released by Hesse et al.), so the strongest form of evidence is absent for DINOv2/CLIP/VLMs; SRG magnitudes are comparable within but not across VLM rows due to per-tokenizer occlusion geometry; and results are not uniformly best — LeGrad edges out ResLRP on ViT-L/14 SRG (3.58 vs 3.53) and rollout variants win on DeiT3-L/16 localization (0.69–0.70 vs 0.55), which the paper acknowledges only implicitly via the table.
- **Reproducibility**: Strong — public code, public checkpoints, disclosed seeds, official metric implementations, single-GPU float32 budget, and a copy-pasteable PyTorch hook listing in the appendix. The one hyperparameter sits on a plateau (γ = 1 near-optimal), removing tuning concerns.
- **Significance**: High practical value: a one-hook change to the most widely deployed transformer LRP method, with the largest gains exactly where deployment is heading (VLMs) and a bonus scientific finding (registers stabilize the residual stream) that connects interpretability diagnostics to architecture design. The observation that cancellation is necessary but not sufficient (Qwen2.5's strong C with little A) is handled honestly.
- **Red flags**: Minor. The theory is local and structural — boundedness is not a faithfulness guarantee, a caveat the authors state but abstract-level readers may miss. On the DINOv2-B intervention replication, treating 75% low-cancellation channels still helps (+8.1%), which mildly blurs the "generic regularization doesn't help" narrative established on ViT-B/16 (where it hurt, −5.5%). Localization evaluation is restricted to correctly classified images (defensible, but narrows scope), and FunnyBirds TS is actually won by Chefer-LRP (0.957 vs 0.950), so "on par" is fair but "best correctness + completeness" should not be over-read as a clean sweep. Finally, the authors themselves note the dual-use angle: understanding attribution failure modes enables designing convincingly misleading explanations.

## Strengths

- Identifies and causally validates a concrete, previously overlooked mechanism (residual cancellation) rather than proposing yet another heuristic heatmap method.
- The fix is minimal, theoretically grounded (exact conservation + provable per-merge amplification bound), and drops into existing AttnLRP pipelines with one backward hook and an untuned hyperparameter.
- Exceptionally broad evaluation: seven ViT backbones across five pre-training families, three VLMs, FunnyBirds ground truth, SAE-feature localization, plus intervention and ablation studies that isolate the mechanism.
- The amplification measure A doubles as an architecture-level diagnostic, yielding the non-obvious finding that register tokens stabilize the residual stream and predicting which models benefit most from ResLRP.
- Honest reporting: cases where ResLRP does not win (DeiT3-L/16 localization, ViT-L/14 SRG) remain in the tables, and limitations (locality of guarantees, unexamined architectures like DiTs/SSMs) are stated explicitly.

## Limitations

- Theoretical guarantees are local to residual additions and structural, not faithfulness guarantees; end-to-end conservation of the full pipeline is limited by the AttnLRP attention rule (exact conservation would require the CP-LRP attention rule at a known quality cost).
- Ground-truth-controlled evaluation (FunnyBirds) is confined to one ViT-B/16 checkpoint; the causal-intervention evidence covers only ViT-B/16 and DINOv2-B.
- Not uniformly best: rollout variants lead on DeiT3-L/16 localization and LeGrad on ViT-L/14 SRG; LeGrad is undefined ("n.a.") on SwinV2-L, so some baseline coverage is incomplete.
- VLM SRG magnitudes are not comparable across models due to different image tokenizations and occlusion geometries; localization uses only correctly classified images.
- No examination of other residual-stream architectures (diffusion transformers, state-space models), and possible implications of strong cancellation for training dynamics/generalization are noted but unexplored.
- The DINOv2-B intervention shows low-cancellation treatment also improving SRG (+8.1%), so a mild generic-stabilization component cannot be fully excluded on every backbone.

## Relevance

这篇论文与可解释性研究方向高度相关：它解释了为什么在 ViT/VLM 上 LRP 类归因（包括我们常用的 AttnLRP）会噪声大、不稳定，并给出一个即插即用（一个 backward hook、γ=1 免调参）的修复，同时其残差抵消/放大度量可作为架构级诊断工具用于 SAE 特征定位和多模态模型分析——对做模型归因、机制可解释性和 VLM grounding 的研究都是直接可用的方法升级。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does residual cancellation similarly destabilize attributions in diffusion transformers, Mamba/state-space models, or mixture-of-experts backbones, and does the same γ-rule transfer?
- Can the amplification measure A be turned into a training-time regularizer that reduces residual cancellation directly, unifying the register-token and post-hoc correction perspectives?
- Why exactly do ViTs develop stronger early-layer cancellation than LLMs — is it patch-token statistics, the absence of registers in most ViT recipes, or a consequence of contrastive pre-training (CLIP-trained models showed the highest cancellation)?
- Since cancellation is necessary but not sufficient for explosion (Qwen2.5's dissociation), what downstream-relevance conditions turn a cancelled direction into an exploding one, and can A be predicted a priori from C plus attention structure?
- Does correcting residual cancellation change conclusions of downstream XAI applications built on AttnLRP, such as token-relevance audits of LLM compliance or saliency-aligned RLVR rewards for VLMs?
- Could boundedness be extended to a global (end-to-end) guarantee by combining ResLRP with the CP-LRP attention rule while quantifying the reported quality cost?
