01

How the gate works

方法 · 机制

ARIA keeps the target model entirely frozen and inserts a gate into the decoding stream. Layer-wise sparse autoencoders are pretrained on general-corpus activations — independent of any forget/retain data — then frozen. The model then rolls out forget and retain examples, and for every SAE latent three statistics are computed per trajectory: activation frequency (sustained topic/style cues), max activation (rare salient spikes), and early-window max over the first 64 tokens, where chain-of-thought and answer openings tend to leak the topic. A sparse L1-regularized logistic head g(s) = σ(wᵀφ(s)+b) is trained on these features to separate forget from retain states.

At inference, every generation state is scored: below threshold τ the base model continues unmodified; above it, the response is overridden with “I don’t know.” All statistics update online in a fused CUDA kernel — no stored trajectories, near-zero memory and latency overhead.

Theorem 3.1: expected forget leakage is bounded by the detector’s false-negative rate; retain degradation by its false-positive rate — so benchmark outcomes are literally routing errors.

03

Strengths vs limitations

优势与局限

Strengths

  • Structural decoupling of forgetting from retention, with a simple theorem tying both error types to detector routing errors — an unusually interpretable evaluation story.
  • Robust where it counts: ≤1.4% MMLU loss and <1% forgetting drift under repeated sampling, prompt attacks, and continued SFT — regimes where weight-edit methods degrade or are recoverable.
  • Deployable engineering: streaming statistics + fused CUDA kernel deliver measured latency/memory parity; works as a post-hoc plugin.
  • Load-bearing interpretability: false positives traced to a named latent, exposing concrete style bias in unlearning benchmarks.

Limitations

  • Access control, not removal — white-box access can bypass or strip the detector; the targeted knowledge stays in the weights.
  • Hinges on forget/retain separability in SAE feature space; no a-priori method to verify it in new domains.
  • Narrow scope: two small models (1.5B / 1B), three benchmarks, only the cyber split of WMDP; intervention is a hard refusal with no graduated suppression.
  • Non-adaptive attacks: nothing crafted against the SAE detector itself (latent obfuscation, adversarial prefixes tuned to keep g(s) below τ).
  • Baseline latency costs conflate methods with deployment configs; per-baseline decoding budgets not fully controlled.

Open questions

待探索
  1. Q1Does the forget/retain margin in SAE space (γ of Prop. B.6) scale with model size, SAE width, and domain breadth — can separability be verified cheaply before committing to a detector?
  2. Q2Can an adaptive gray-box attacker — optimizing prompts to keep the detector score below τ while still eliciting knowledge — defeat ARIA, and can adversarial detector training close that gap?
  3. Q3Would softer interventions (steering away forget-related latents rather than hard refusal) recover the ~1.8 ROUGE-L retain loss on TOFU?
  4. Q4Does the response-style bias found in WMDP-cyber (latent L27:33029) generalize to other unlearning corpora — and can style-matched retain calibration remove the false positives it causes?
  5. Q5Can the routing-error framework be extended to bound recovery under quantization or model merging — the deployment-time corruptions that break weight-edit unlearning?
