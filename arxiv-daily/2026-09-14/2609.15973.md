> **一句话总结：** 本文提出"发现基础模型（DFM）"框架：把基础模型的能力边界从"在人类给定的问题结构内求解"扩展到"构造与修订问题、表征、假设、干预和证据的开放式发现过程"，用七种耦合能力（问题发现/形式化/表征构造/假设生成/干预设计/证据驱动修订/跨任务技能进化）形式化定义，给出 Zetema 系统实例（可修订研究状态 + Research World Model 实验门控 + Dry-Lab/Wet-Lab 接地），并用真实治疗肽发现系统 GALILEO 提供经验支撑（两分支 LRRC8C / SLC25A1 经实验验证，IC50 分别为 18.922 μM 和 0.736 μM，五轮湿实验反馈蒸馏出可迁移的"两亲平衡语法"设计规则），最后统一给出能力训练目标 LDFM 与以过程为中心、资源匹配的评测协议。

> **TL;DR:** A position-plus-framework paper (Ling Yang et al., DFM Scientist Collaboration Program) arguing the next scaling frontier is "Discovery Intelligence": model systems that construct and revise the research structure itself (problem, representation, hypotheses, interventions) rather than optimize within a human-specified task Q=(P,R,G,T,V). It formalizes seven coupled discovery capabilities, instantiates them as the Zetema architecture (revisable research state S_t, Research World Model gating, Discovery Skill memory m=(trigger, operation, expected effect, validation)), and grounds the physical loop with GALILEO — a real therapeutic-peptide discovery system where wet-lab feedback revises subsequent decisions: GALILEO-LRC inhibits LRRC8A/LRRC8C currents with IC50 = 18.922 μM in LRRC8C-high patient-derived organoids, and GALILEO-SLC reaches IC50 = 0.736 μM in SLC25A1-high organoids (≈26× more potent than the LRC branch), with five autonomous optimization rounds consolidating into a transferable Amphiphilic Balance Grammar. Note: the DFM training objective and evaluation protocol themselves are formal specifications without new benchmark experiments; all quantitative results are imported from the GALILEO case study. Read it for the formalism and the process-centered evaluation design, not for a working general-purpose DFM.

## 核心贡献

- **提出并形式化"发现智能（Discovery Intelligence）"这一新能力边界**：把智能扩展划分为三阶段——学习已有知识（Chat）、从行动结果中学习（Agent/Coding）、参与新知识的构造与验证（Discovery）；指出当前科学 AI 的结构性天花板在于问题、表征、目标、工具、评估器全部由人类预先给定，模型只能在被给定的结构内优化。
- **给出 DFM 的形式定义与七能力分解**：发现回合 Φdisc: (W, K0, B) → (ΔK, Ξ) 同时产出外部验证的知识进展 ΔK 和证据轨迹 Ξ；能力集合 C_DFM = {C_find, C_form, C_repr, C_hyp, C_int, C_rev, C_cont}（问题发现、问题形式化、表征构造、假设生成、干预设计、证据驱动修订、跨任务持续改进），并以系统边界 D = (π_θ, S, M, T, E, V, H) 界定归因范围（模型策略、研究状态、记忆、工具、环境、验证器、人类监督）。
- **设计 Zetema 系统实例**：以显式可修订研究状态 S_t = (P_t, R_t, H_t, E_t, X_t, B_t, M_t) 为核心；分支（branch）是一等状态对象（可比较、合并、归档、恢复、显式终止）；用 Research World Model W_t 预测观测/状态变化/成本/风险并对高代价行动做验证门控；跨任务经验先归因、再经 held-out/影子模式验证后才晋升为 Discovery Skill（m = (trigger, operation, expected effect, validation evidence)），并版本化、可回滚。
- **用 GALILEO 提供真实 Dry-Lab/Wet-Lab 闭环经验支撑**：物理实验反馈实际改变后续目标信念、检测选择、机制假设与分子设计策略——LRRC8C 分支（GALILEO-LRC，器官模型 IC50 = 18.922 μM）与 SLC25A1 分支（GALILEO-SLC，IC50 = 0.736 μM）均经正交实验验证；五轮优化中弱分支被剪除、被放弃的酸性/极性/非极性组织在第 4 轮被重新打开，最终蒸馏出可迁移的 Amphiphilic Balance Grammar（ABG）设计规则。
- **统一的能力形成与评测方案**：组合式训练目标 LDFM（状态条件行动模仿、研究状态转移学习、偏好优化、过程验证器、世界模型学习、科学反馈 RL、技能检索对比学习）与算法 1 的分阶段流程；评测协议以 P_disc = (ΔK_val, ΔC_future) 双轴报告，定义效率 η_disc = ΔK_val / C_total 与迁移增益 Δ_transfer = DiscPerf(D_unseen | M_1:k) − DiscPerf(D_unseen | M_0)，并显式控制记忆化、基准泄漏、近重复检索与额外算力。
- **治理与递归风险分析**：区分新颖输出 ≠ 科学发现、模型置信 ≠ 科学确定性；按影响半径（任务脚本 < 技能触发器 < 评估器/权限系统）缩放验证、沙箱、回滚与独立审计要求。

## Key Contributions

- **Formalizes "Discovery Intelligence" as the next capability frontier.** The paper cleanly separates intelligence scaling into Chat (learning existing knowledge) → Agent/Coding (learning from action outcomes) → Discovery (constructing/testing/revising the structures through which new knowledge is produced), and pinpoints why current scientific AI hits a structural ceiling: a fixed representation cannot express a variable it omits, and a search objective cannot recover a property its evaluator systematically ignores.
- **Gives DFM a formal problem setting and seven-capability decomposition.** Discovery episodes are maps Φdisc: (W, K0, B) → (ΔK, Ξ) where problem, representation, hypothesis space, intervention strategy, and validation procedure can all change mid-episode; the capability set {C_find, C_form, C_repr, C_hyp, C_int, C_rev, C_cont} is evaluated at the declared model-system boundary D = (π_θ, S, M, T, E, V, H), which makes attribution of human/tool/model contributions explicit.
- **Specifies the Discovery Process over a revisable research state.** Concrete operator formalisms: problem P = (O, Ω, ℓ, C, Y, Q); representation R = (objects, variables, relations, abstractions, constraints); hypothesis H_i = (mechanism, assumptions, validity range, predictions, falsifiers); intervention selection as expected information gain x* = argmax E[ΔI(H|o_x)]; and a revision operator that performs failure attribution (theory vs. measurement vs. protocol vs. confounding vs. simulator misspecification) before updating the right object.
- **Instantiates the framework as Zetema.** Key mechanisms: branches as first-class state objects (merge/archive/restore/explicit termination, including valid negative outcomes); Discovery Skill memory with structural triggers, expected effects, and validation records (extending Buffer-of-Thoughts/ReasonFlux-style template reuse with scientific grounding and negative-transfer protection); a Research World Model that screens counterfactual consequences before costly actions, with staged promotion (archival replay → shadow mode → active use) for cross-task updates.
- **Empirically grounds the physical loop with GALILEO** (Jiang et al., 2026): a real therapeutic-peptide discovery system in which robotic synthesis, multimodal phenotyping, and orthogonal hands-on assays return evidence that revises target beliefs, assay choices, mechanism hypotheses, and design policies — culminating in a transferable Amphiphilic Balance Grammar distilled across five wet-lab rounds.
- **Unifies capability formation and process-centered evaluation.** The compositional training objective L_DFM covers imitation, state-transition learning, preference optimization, process verification, world-model learning, RL from scientific feedback, and validated skill retrieval; the evaluation protocol reports externally validated progress (ΔK_val) and future-capability improvement (ΔC_future) separately, with resource-matched comparisons and explicit controls for memorization, benchmark leakage, near-duplicate retrieval, prompt reuse, and extra compute.
- **Analyzes grounding regimes and governance.** Four horizons (digital, simulation-grounded, embodied/physical, recursive) differ in grounding, consequence, and responsibility burden rather than capability rank; recursive updates (to evaluators, benchmark generators, memory policies) get scaled controls because small distortions can compound while measured performance improves.

## Methodology

The paper is a framework/position paper with one imported empirical case study, built in four layers:

1. **Formalization (Secs. 2–4).** A conventional task is abstracted as Q = (P, R, G, T, V) — problem, representation, objective, tools, evaluator — all supplied. Discovery instead starts from a partially understood world W, initial knowledge K0, and constraints B, and an episode produces both validated knowledge progress ΔK and an evidence-bearing record Ξ. The target is factored into seven capabilities (C_find … C_cont), with the first six operating within an investigation and the seventh (C_cont) a cross-task update mechanism. The Discovery Process is specified as transitions over a revisable research state rather than a fixed pipeline: evidence can send the investigation back to an earlier problem, representation, or explanation. Each research object gets an explicit tuple (e.g., hypotheses carry falsifiers F_i; interventions are selected by expected uncertainty reduction ΔI over the current explanation set).

2. **System instantiation: Zetema (Sec. 5).** One constraint organizes the design: every scientifically consequential operation must leave an inspectable state transition. S_t = (P_t, R_t, H_t, E_t, X_t, B_t, M_t) links hypotheses to the problems/representations under which they are meaningful, observations to the interventions and protocols that produced them, and revisions to the evidence that triggered them. Predicted (Dry-Lab) and observed (Wet-Lab) results are kept at different epistemic status. A Research World Model W_t: (S_t, a) → p(o, ΔS, c, r | S_t, a) predicts observations, state changes, costs, and risks to gate consequential actions (Eq. 14: an action is eligible when every verifier V_j(a) ≥ τ_j, including human authorization for physical steps); world-model disagreement and calibration signals are retained because gating errors have selection effects. Cross-task updates M_{k+1} = Evolve(M_k, Ξ_k, V_k) are attribution-gated and staged (replay → shadow mode → active), versioned, and reversible.

3. **Capability formation (Sec. 6).** Trainable components (policy π_θ, state updater F_φ, world model W_ω, verifier V_ψ, skill selector q_η) receive distinct signals: stage-weighted action imitation L_act (with α_z_t reweighting rare discovery transitions), state-transition learning L_state, state-conditioned pairwise preference optimization L_pref, multi-criterion process verification L_ver, world-model likelihood L_wm, KL-regularized RL from a decomposed scientific reward r_sci = w_K δK + w_I IG + w_R q_rev + w_V q_val − w_C c − w_ρ ρ plus a terminal transfer term λ_T ΔC_future, and a contrastive skill-retrieval loss L_skill using validated positives and negative-transfer cases. L_DFM (Eq. 25) is compositional; Algorithm 1 stages it (trajectory warm-start → verifier/world-model learning → preference refinement → interactive RL → gated skill consolidation). Training environments should hide variables, contain observationally equivalent mechanisms, deliberate evaluator mismatch, and return observations the model does not write itself; success-only trajectories are explicitly insufficient.

4. **Evaluation protocol and analysis (Secs. 7–9).** Discovery progress is two-axis: ΔK_val (externally validated, evidence the proposing system does not control) and ΔC_future (behavioral improvement on held-out tasks). Stage-wise process metrics cover problem discovery precision (not launching programs around every residual), formulation repair of over-broad/proxy questions, representation credit only when downstream operations improve, hypothesis discriminative content (predictions committed before outcomes are revealed), prospective + retrospective intervention scoring, and revision quality under induced counterevidence. Efficiency η_disc = ΔK_val / C_total is reported with non-interchangeable cost components (compute, money, human time, risk); transfer Δ_transfer is a curve over episodes against controls including no memory, fact-only memory, full-trajectory retrieval, and near-duplicate/prompt/compute matching. Sections 8–9 analyze four grounding regimes and governance: scoped action authority, relational provenance, and recursive-update risk scaled by radius of effect.

The GALILEO case study (Sec. 5.7) maps this framework onto a real embodied therapeutic-discovery loop (Jiang et al., 2026): candidate peptides from a clinically informed prior are robotically synthesized and phenotyped; physical outcomes update target beliefs, motif-policy weights, assay selection, and the hypothesis board; five autonomous rounds prune, reopen, and consolidate motif organizations into the Amphiphilic Balance Grammar, whose motifs were then transferred into external peptide-design workflows.

## Results & Highlights

This is primarily a formal framework paper; the quantitative results come from the GALILEO case study it imports as empirical grounding:

- **GALILEO-LRC (LRRC8C branch):** whole-cell electrophysiology showed selective inhibition of LRRC8A/LRRC8C currents relative to other tested LRRC8 heteromers, and patient-derived organoid dose-response in the LRRC8C-high setting yielded an experimentally measured **IC50 of 18.922 μM**; follow-up metabolomic, transcriptomic, perturbational, and immune-context experiments shifted the working mechanism from generic membrane toxicity to an LRRC8C-linked osmolyte/redox-stress and innate-signaling account.
- **GALILEO-SLC (SLC25A1 branch):** confirmed mitochondrial localization (the compartment where SLC25A1 operates) and dose-response in SLC25A1-high patient-derived organoids yielding an **IC50 of 0.736 μM** — roughly **26× more potent than the LRC branch's 18.922 μM** in the corresponding target-high organoid settings; target silencing, acetate rescue, mitochondrial measurements, metabolomics, and extracellular-acidification assays refined the mechanism toward citrate-export-dependent metabolic collapse.
- **Five autonomous wet-lab optimization rounds → transferable rule:** round 1 maintained a heterogeneous motif baseline; rounds 2–3 pruned weakly supported branches and contracted the active motif payload; round 4 reopened previously abandoned acidic/polar/nonpolar motif organizations under revised pore-engagement hypotheses; round 5 consolidated recovered organizations into shared active modules — yielding the **Amphiphilic Balance Grammar (ABG)**, whose motifs improved geometry and energetic profiles when transferred into external peptide-design workflows (improvement reported qualitatively; no numeric delta given in this paper).
- **Framework-level "results" are structural, not benchmark numbers:** the paper delivers a 7-capability decomposition, a capability-comparison table against five existing paradigms (Table 1), one compositional training objective (Eq. 25), one reference training algorithm (Algorithm 1), two evaluation axes with efficiency and transfer metrics (Eqs. 27–29), and a governance model — with no end-to-end DFM training run, no baseline comparisons for the framework itself, and no ablations of the seven capabilities.

For the framework proper, the honest summary is: no quantitative results reported beyond the imported GALILEO case study; the evidence offered is the existence proof that physical evidence can revise downstream scientific decisions and distill into a reusable design rule.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty: real at the conceptual level, modest at the technical level.** The core reframing — evaluate systems by the research structures they can revise (problem/representation/evaluator as model-actionable objects) rather than by final-answer performance — is a genuinely useful synthesis, and the two-axis evaluation (ΔK_val, ΔC_future) with retrieval/compute/resource-matched controls is one of the more careful protocol designs in the AI-scientist evaluation literature. But the individual components are assembled from known parts: explicit state + branching (agent frameworks), thought templates (the authors' own Buffer of Thoughts/ReasonFlux), world-model gating (model-based RL), process verifiers (process supervision), and gated memory updates (Recuris, Voyager). The equations (Eqs. 16–25) are standard loss forms applied to research-state tuples; none is experimentally instantiated here.
- **Rigor: the framework itself is unvalidated.** There are no experiments for Zetema, no training runs for L_DFM, no ablations of the seven capabilities, and no demonstration that Discovery Skill memory beats the paper's own proposed controls (fact-only memory, full-trajectory retrieval) — the control conditions that would make the C_cont claim falsifiable are specified but never run. The λ weights of L_DFM and the optimizer choice are left open ("not committing Zetema to PPO, GRPO, or another particular optimizer").
- **The empirical grounding is borrowed and partially mapped.** GALILEO is a prior domain-specific system mapped post hoc onto the seven capabilities; the authors concede the mapping "is not one-to-one at the level of software modules" and that its problem domain "remains substantially structured." The ABG transfer claim ("improving the geometry and energetic profiles" of external workflows) is carried over without numbers in this paper, and the IC50 values come with no comparison to existing inhibitors or a null/naive-design baseline — 0.736 μM vs. 18.922 μM is an intra-system contrast between two branches, not evidence of discovery competence against baselines. Two validated branches in one modality is a thin empirical base for a category claim spanning digital, simulation, physical, and recursive regimes.
- **Reproducibility:** as a position paper there is nothing to reproduce except the imported case study; no code, data, or model weights are released with this document (the GitHub link is a "plans" repository). The evaluation protocol is specified precisely enough (metrics, controls, split-by-mechanism) that others could implement it — arguably the paper's most actionable artifact.
- **Significance and red flags.** The definitional move has an unfalsifiability risk the authors themselves flag ("the DFM label does not supply capabilities that a system has not demonstrated") — a category this soft can be adopted as branding, and the paper's own presentation (lab collaboration-program splash, roadmap repository) suggests it partly functions as one. Table 1's paradigm comparison is qualitative "typical targets," easy to dispute case by case. Overclaiming is, however, well controlled in the text: the conclusion explicitly denies that general autonomous discovery is solved. The strongest honest claim — "a real physical loop exists where external evidence revises subsequent scientific decisions and distills into a reusable rule" — is properly scoped.

## Strengths

- A crisp, falsifiable-in-principle formalization of what "open-ended discovery" adds over task solving, with the supplied-structure abstraction Q = (P, R, G, T, V) making the structural ceiling argument precise.
- The process-centered evaluation protocol is the standout contribution: two-axis reporting, efficiency with non-interchangeable cost accounting (compute/money/human time/risk), transfer curves over episodes, and explicit controls for near-duplicate retrieval, benchmark leakage, prompt reuse, and extra compute address most known gaming modes of AI-scientist benchmarks.
- Branches as first-class state objects with explicit termination semantics (including valid negative results — failed replications, untestable questions as legitimate outputs) is an unusually honest epistemic design.
- Discovery Skill memory with structural triggers, expected effects, staged promotion, and negative-transfer cases turns "experience" into a testable object rather than an archive.
- The governance analysis of recursive update risk — scaling controls by radius of effect, and keeping audit records/rollback/permission boundaries outside ordinary self-modification — is concrete and ahead of most AI-scientist papers.
- The authors repeatedly and explicitly delimit what GALILEO does and does not establish; the epistemic hygiene (predicted vs. observed evidence, claim-status tiers, provenance as relational record) is exemplary for the genre.

## Limitations

- The framework's training and evaluation machinery (L_DFM, Algorithm 1, the protocol) is entirely unimplemented and unbenchmarked in this paper; every loss and metric is a specification, so the central claim — that discovery behavior can be trained and measured this way — remains a hypothesis.
- All quantitative evidence is imported from a single domain-specific system (therapeutic peptides, Jiang et al. 2026) with two validated branches; cross-domain transfer of Discovery Skills — the defining C_cont requirement — is not demonstrated anywhere.
- The GALILEO numbers lack baseline context (no comparison to existing compounds, random-design controls, or ablated loops without physical feedback), and the ABG transfer benefit is described only qualitatively.
- The seven-capability decomposition and Table 1's paradigm comparison are editorial rather than empirical; no measurement distinguishes, e.g., a system with "Optional" problem revision from one with "Required" problem revision in practice.
- Reward decomposition (Eq. 21) bundles knowledge progress, information gain, revision quality, validation quality, cost, and risk into weighted sums without addressing how these would be measured or traded off in real wet-lab settings where δK_t is rarely available at step time.
- The paper's length and scope (definition + system + training + evaluation + governance) means each part is a research program sketch rather than a validated method; readers looking for a working DFM or a runnable benchmark will find neither.

## Relevance

与"LLM 智能体 / AI for Science / 自主科学发现"方向高度相关：它把 AI-Scientist 类系统从"执行给定研究流程"推进到"修订研究结构本身"的能力定义，其过程中心评测协议（资源匹配、迁移控制、过程轨迹保留）可直接借鉴到自动科研智能体的训练与评估设计中；GALILEO 的 Dry-Lab/Wet-Lab 闭环也为具身科学发现系统提供了真实参照。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- What is the minimal viable instantiation of the evaluation protocol on an existing benchmark suite (DiscoveryBench, MLE-bench, BoxingGym), and do current frontier agents already score nonzero on ΔC_future under the paper's retrieval/compute controls?
- Can Discovery Skill memory be shown to beat full-trajectory retrieval and fact-only memory on held-out tasks — the exact control set the paper proposes — in at least one digital domain before the C_cont claim is credited?
- How should δK_t (externally supported knowledge progress) be estimated at step time for RL credit assignment in settings where external validation is slow, expensive, or only terminal?
- Does the branch-reopen pattern observed in GALILEO (round 4 reopening abandoned motif organizations) emerge in purely digital discovery loops, or is it contingent on physical-evidence structure?
- How sensitive is the Research World Model gating layer to misspecification in practice — what fraction of would-be-valid interventions does a conservative gate suppress, and can that selection effect be measured rather than argued?
- Given the paper's recursive-risk analysis, what concrete audit mechanism could detect a silently degrading evaluator or benchmark generator inside a self-improving discovery loop?
