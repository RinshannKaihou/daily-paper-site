02 · Why this matters

Method · How the readout works

Probe of Internal Recognition (PIR)

Pose a multiple-choice question, take the residual-stream activation at each option’s last token (the “option span”), and score every option by its projection onto an item-contrast direction: mean correct-option span minus mean distractor span, computed only over questions the model itself answers correctly when honest. The highest-scoring option is the answer the model internally recognizes.

Layers are chosen by question-disjoint cross-validation; on Gemma-3, standardizing a few outsized activation dimensions lifts recognition 0.389 → 0.735. No honest reference model, no labeled truth corpus — distractors supply the contrast, and a label-free PCA variant nearly matches the supervised direction (0.812 vs 0.825 on MMLU).

For deployment audits where the correct answer is unknown, a divergence score (max recognition minus the output option’s score) flags concealment, with “known” labels requiring correct answers under every rotation of the option order. Free-form generation is handled by rebuilding the candidate set from the model’s own samples.

Evaluated on gemma-2-9b plus seven more models across five families (Gemma, Qwen, Llama, Mistral, Phi) on MMLU / WMDP / ARC / TriviaQA — with bootstrap CIs, permutation nulls, and two concealment organisms built by other groups.

  • Deployment detection — conceal-vs-unknown AUC 0.718 vs 0.517 text-only (+0.20) and 0.645 combined black-box detector (+0.073).
  • Causal, not correlational — steering along the direction flips answers: 0.189 vs 0.005 for 150 random directions, 0.000 matched control; replicates on phi-4 (0.122).
  • Free-form (TriviaQA) — decode 0.71 vs 0.56 self-consistency; peak-recognition detector AUC 0.776.
  • Adversary — an anti-probe organism evades the readout (0.34, baseline 0.31); the paper retracts an earlier in-sample robustness claim. Audit signal, not defense.

Strengths

  • Reference-free by construction — distractors supply the contrast; the label-free PCA variant loses almost nothing (0.812 vs 0.825).
  • The direction is causal — steering changes answers (0.189 vs 0.000 matched control), replicated on phi-4.
  • Near-lossless cross-dataset transfer — a WMDP-fit direction reads MMLU at 0.815 and ARC at 0.900: one model-level recognition direction, not dataset artifacts.
  • Validated on external artifacts — password-locked and circuit-broken organisms built by other groups (0.93 / 0.90), not just self-built concealment.
  • Statistical hygiene — nested CV, permutation nulls over layers, bootstrap CIs, confound controls, and a public retraction of an in-sample result.

Limitations

  • Needs an enumerable candidate set — multiple-choice and factual QA yes; open-ended reasoning no.
  • Not fully black-box — the deployment detector needs the model’s own base/honest checkpoint.
  • Adversarial evasion — an anti-probe-trained model drives recognition to baseline while keeping capability; tamper detection is future work.
  • Erased ≠ never-had — both give a silent readout; PIR cannot tell them apart.
  • Fragile evidence base — clean erasure rests on one own RMU run; several organisms are single-seed, self-built; free-form shown on one model; code “will be released.”
  • Filtered labels — the strict rotation-consistency “known” gate shapes all detector statistics.

Open questions · For further exploration

  1. Does the hiding-vs-erasure boundary hold for representation-destroying unlearning (rank-one editing, layer surgery), or only RMU/NPO-style methods?
  2. Can the anti-probe collapse signature (self-correlation cosine 0.997) become a practical tamper detector that survives an anomaly-aware adversary?
  3. Is the recognition direction mechanistically upstream of the output direction it steers better than — how does it relate to induction heads and factual-recall circuits?
  4. Does the readout extend to agentic settings, where the “answer” is a plan or action rather than a short option span?
  5. Why does the naturalistic imitate-weak sandbagger read at only 0.63 vs 0.85 for the prefix variant — does imitating weakness blur recognition itself?
  6. Can peak-recognition be calibrated per-domain to avoid false flags on known-but-low-confidence items?
