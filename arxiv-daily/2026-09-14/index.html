Must Read Lead Story · Self-Evolving Agents

ModularRSI: Modular and Generalizable Recursive Harness Self-Improvement

A CLI agent’s harness is split into five independently evolved modules — evolved on a 2,000-task benchmark-disjoint set, merged through validation gates. TerminalBench 2.0 accuracy rises 47.57 → 52.43, SWE-Bench Verified 73.40 → 76.45, with cross-domain and cross-model transfer. The pivotal ablation: evolving the whole harness jointly drops below baseline (44.19) — modular credit assignment is what makes harness RSI generalize at all.

模块化 credit assignment 才是 harness 自我改进可泛化的关键——整-harness 联合进化反而跌破基线；基准不相交协议首次干净回答了 “harness 自我改进到底学没学到可迁移的东西”。

Must Read Self-Evolving Agents

Dream-RSI: Recursive Self-Improvement through Evolving Worlds

Recorded discovery trees become zero-cost replay simulators: exploration policies improve offline — “dreaming” — before redeployment. Lasso solved with 317 calls vs SimpleTES’s 51,200 (~162×); KernelBench up to 2.09× at equal budget. History as interactive replay systematically beats history as prompt guidance.

把发现树历史当作零执行成本的回放模拟器，探索策略离线“做梦”式改进再上线，最多省 162× 调用。
