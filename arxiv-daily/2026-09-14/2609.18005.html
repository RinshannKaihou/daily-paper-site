Why this matters为何重要

A reusable, domain-resolved protocol for pricing the true quality cost of any lossy inference optimization — and proof the proxies you gate on can't see it.

Critical edge批判视角

Only 7–8B models were tested, and the 3-bit arm is a single data-free quantizer (HQQ) — so “3-bit is unsafe” must not be generalized to calibrated methods (AWQ, GPTQ) or to 70B-scale models.

— from the paper's own red-flags list

Methodology · the instrument方法

claude-sonnet-5 rates each response in isolation on a 7-point rubric (7 = complete/correct → 1 = degenerate), plus derailed/recovered flags and a ≤15-word rationale. Five elements calibrate it like a lab instrument:

  1. Dual-reference pairing — two bf16 samples per prompt; each arm scores rating minus the reference mean. Shared prompt variance drops (ICC 0.49 → 0.12).
  2. Exchangeability null — swapping the two reference draws must read exactly zero; observed −0.05 [−0.18, +0.08] pins instrument noise at sd 0.94/prompt.
  3. Implementation null — strict speculative decoding is provably distribution-identical; any nonzero reading indicts the code. It caught two real bugs in development.
  4. Positive controls — early-exit losses registered as predictions before the run; both detected with correct sign (under-predicted ~2×, honestly reported).
  5. Equivalence & power — TOST, 90% CI inside a pre-committed ±0.3 bound; 220 prompts buys ±0.11 pooled.

Ground truth: 27 math problems with parsed answers + 13 programs with hidden assert-tests. Reliability: 86% exact double-judge agreement; cross-judges opus-5 (ρ=0.88) and gemini-3.1-pro (ρ=0.80) preserve arm ordering; judge ratings average 6.07 on execution-correct vs 2.45 on wrong. Noise floor: re-rating identical text moves scores by sd 0.26; a fresh sample at temperature 0.7 moves them by 0.94 — the noise is the model's, not the judge's.

The cheap proxies are blind代理指标失灵

  • EAR Expected acceptance rate predicts judged damage only weakly: Spearman ρ = 0.19 (Qwen) / 0.25 (Llama) — and −0.42 on Llama-Chinese, where high local agreement marks repetition loops.
  • Perplexity Rewards failure: one degenerate looping response scored lower perplexity (1.151) than its reference (1.239).
  • Token fidelity Length/agreement proxies rated as acceptable 37% of responses the judge found unacceptable.
  • Confidence Certainty ≠ consequence: digits vs punctuation emitted at 0.999 vs 0.989 confidence, yet substituting them changed judged outcomes in 23.3% vs 3.3% of positions (n=354).

Strengths优势

  • Evaluation treated as instrumentation — zero points, positive controls, stated resolution, pre-registration; rare discipline in this literature.
  • Two-null design localizes faults (judge vs implementation) and demonstrably caught two real bugs during development.
  • Cross-family, cross-model comparison on identical prompts with per-domain CIs — directly answers “will this hurt my traffic?”
  • Proxy-inadequacy analysis is quantitative, mechanistic, and immediately actionable for acceptance-rule design.
  • Full artifact release: prompts, rubric, hidden tests, registered predictions.

Limitations局限

  • Two 7–8B models only; magnitudes are known not to transfer to larger scales (the method does).
  • 3-bit results characterize data-free HQQ, not calibrated methods such as AWQ or GPTQ.
  • Prose and Chinese rest on the judge alone; primary judge is one model family (secondaries agree on ordering, differ by offsets).
  • ±0.3 per-domain equivalence at 40–60 prompts is coarse; the hard domain would need 80–150.
  • Early exit tested at one depth (15/28) and one refill interval; the Llama null anomaly (−0.14) was bounded but unexplained.
  • Arithmetic domain saturated at rating 7 — judged cells there carry no information.

Questions for further exploration待探索

  1. Do calibrated 3-bit quantizers (AWQ, GPTQ) flatten the domain profile under this instrument, or only shift the level?
  2. Do conclusions change at 30–70B, and what prompt budget resolves ±0.1 per domain there?
  3. Can certainty-vs-consequence (23.3% vs 3.3%) become a validated “pivot-token” classifier for relaxed speculation?
  4. What causes the Llama implementation-null deficit — batched-verification numerics, cache cropping, or EOS handling?
  5. How does the instrument extend to KV-cache eviction, compaction, watermarking, distillation — where the provable-null arm must be built differently?
  6. Would pairwise judging with the same calibration machinery resolve finer differences in ceiling-heavy domains?
