> **一句话总结：** 提出"认知准入控制"（CAC）——在智能体分布式系统中，用策略把有后果的操作映射为带证据类别/新鲜度/见证集约束的"保证义务"，确定性评估器区分已满足/已违反/未决三种状态并签发绑定见证清单与派发守卫的一次性证书；在 2,730 次受控本 地试验中，CAC 完成 120 次无害效果且 0 次有害效果，而 LivePolicy 基线在同一完成数下漏放了 30 次构造的相关见证失败。

> **TL;DR:** A 15-page systems paper from OpenKedge.io that formalizes an admission layer between autonomous agents and external infrastructure: a policy maps a typed action and its risk vector to assurance obligations (predicate, evidence class, freshness horizon, witness-set constraints), a deterministic ternary evaluator (satisfied / violated / unknown) turns missing evidence into targeted remediation requests, and successful admission yields a single-use Ed25519 certificate bound to a witness manifest and dispatch-time guards. On the paper's own CACBench suite (13 local failover state machines × 30 seeds × 7 controllers = 2,730 trials), CAC completes 120/390 intents with 0 harmful effects versus 30 harms for a LivePolicy baseline with the same 120 completions — the entire advantage comes from structural-cut (EFD) enforcement against correlated witnesses. The complete local dispatch path costs 4.65–26.86 ms median (1–8 obligations, 9,000 samples). Honest but limited: no LLM, no real database, no production claim.

## 核心贡献

- ** typed 准入演算**：将"风险条件化的义务选择 → 三值证据清算（满足/违反/未知）→ 定向证据获取请求"形式化为一个完整的准入 calculus（定理 1–3、命题 1–3、性质 1–3），明确区分授权（authority）与就绪性（readiness）。
- **证据生命周期契约**：定义带类别、来源、时间戳、scope、状态版本的"证据回执"（evidence receipt），以及新鲜度区间、见证集约束（distinct-source 基数、EFD 结构割 κ≥k）；重复 ID 不同内容直接拒绝，真实性 ≠ 真值。
- **一次性证书与中介派发**：准入成功后铸造绑定 proposal digest、witness digest、守卫、有效期与 nonce 的单次使用证书；网关做 CAS 式 nonce 消耗保证 at-most-once 派发，未知守卫 fail-closed。
- **可复现的受控评估**：TypeScript 原型 + 独立效果观察器 + 匹配故障调度 + 机制消融（NoGuard / NoEFD / NoRemediation / NoTypedEvidence），并测量完整本地派发路径延迟（9,000 样本）。

## Key Contributions

- A typed admission calculus connecting risk-conditioned obligation selection, ternary evidence discharge (SATISFIED / VIOLATED / UNKNOWN with typed diagnostics), and targeted evidence acquisition (Sections 3–4).
- Formal refinement conditions linking a successful evaluation to a witness manifest, a bounded single-use capability, and guarded dispatch — Theorem 1 (authority–readiness separation), Theorem 2 (budget does not determine admission), Proposition 1 (compensatory scalar scores can bypass a required conjunct), and Properties 1–3 (at-most-once mediated dispatch via nonce CAS, envelope preservation, fail-closed guards).
- Integration of Epistemic Fault Domain (EFD) structural cuts into obligation discharge: a correlated-witness set sharing one modeled root has cut κ=1 and cannot discharge an obligation requiring κ≥2, regardless of how many approving receipts it contains.
- A TypeScript prototype (133 passing tests) with bounded exhaustive subset witness search (≤12 receipts/obligation), persistent file-backed nonce replay protection (verified under an 8-process race and a restarted store), and one test cross-checking the structural-cut routine against direct fault propagation over all 43,561 three-voter profile/rule combinations.
- A corrected evaluation methodology: CACBench (13 constructed failover state machines), 30 seeds each, 7 controllers, independent privileged observer that copies world state and harm counters around every target invocation, matched pre-dispatch fault injection (candidate drift, authorization revocation), and full JSONL traces with a SHA-256 manifest and independent Python analysis.

## Methodology

The system model separates an untrusted proposer (which may generate proofs, telemetry, or verification suggestions but cannot set the evaluator's predicates) from a trusted TCB (policy resolver, evidence evaluator, certificate mint, gateway, replay store). A proposal q = ⟨intent, action, params, scope, principal, state version, constraints⟩ is classified as consequential when its worst-case modeled loss over the environment model's concretization γA(s) crosses a policy threshold. A policy-relative risk vector ρ(q,s) = ⟨severity, blast radius, irreversibility, observational uncertainty, dependency exposure, adverse plausibility⟩ selects a set of assurance obligations ΩΠ(q,s); each obligation declares a predicate, verification kind (observation / verification / simulation / quorum / dual control), accepted evidence classes, freshness horizon Δtmax, set constraints, optional EFD requirement, and guard templates.

Discharge is ternary: positive-only maximal witnesses ⇒ SATISFIED; negative-only ⇒ VIOLATED (deny); both ⇒ UNKNOWN(conflict); neither ⇒ UNKNOWN(c) with a fixed diagnostic precedence (missing observation vs. evidence the predicate is false). Canonical witness selection ranks by policy priority, worst-case freshness, structural cut, minimal cardinality, and encoding tie-breaker. The controller returns PERMIT(certificate) / DENY / DEFER(with acquisition requests) / ESCALATE(for human approval obligations) / ABORT(budget). Certificates are single-use, domain-separated digests, expiring at the earliest witness freshness bound. The gateway re-validates signature, presenter, guards, epoch, and authorization live, then atomically consumes the nonce (CAS) before dispatch. The evaluation uses a 2,730-trial matrix (13 scenarios × 7 controllers × 30 seeds) with controller order rotated across seeds, plus ablations that each remove exactly one mechanism, plus separate timing sweeps (1/4/8 obligations and 1–12 receipts; EFD panel sizes 2–8).

## Results & Highlights

- **Harm prevention on CACBench (13 scenarios × 30 seeds = 390 trials/controller):** CAC completes 120 intents without modeled harm and produces 0 harmful effects across its 390 trials (150 target attempts); the LivePolicy baseline — which live-rechecks authorization and reacquires typed observations at the dispatch boundary — achieves the same 120 completions but admits 30 harmful effects in the correlated-witness scenario (3 approving sources sharing one modeled root, policy requiring structural cut ≥2); the AuthOnly baseline attempts all 390 effects with 180 harmful effects and 180 completions (its completions include physically successful operations after authorization revocation).
- **Mechanism ablations (each isolates one mechanism under a matched fault schedule):** NoGuard proceeds in all 30 candidate-offline-drift trials, each producing an observed harmful effect (CAC and LivePolicy both block it); NoTypedEvidence admits 30 harmful actions when an authenticated wrong-class receipt (static-verification receipt claiming zero lag while the world holds excessive unreplicated data) is substituted for replica telemetry; NoEFD admits 30 harmful correlated-witness transitions (same as LivePolicy); NoRemediation completes 0 of the 30 initially underprepared intents (total completions drop from 120 to 90) — both CAC and NoRemediation correctly refuse all 30 trials where evidence is permanently unavailable.
- **Complete local dispatch-path latency (macOS/ARM64, Apple M4, Node.js v25.2.1, 3 fresh processes × 1,000 samples per workload = 9,000 measured samples, including file-backed guard reads and persistent nonce consumption):** per-process medians 4.65–5.69 ms at 1 obligation/1 receipt, 5.89–9.66 ms at 4/4, and 24.91–26.86 ms at 8/8; p99 ranges 10.28–37.37 / 14.46–22.48 / 41.41–58.39 ms respectively.
- **Witness-search scaling:** full-discharge medians rise from 0.072–0.079 ms with 1 receipt to 67.31–72.69 ms with 12 receipts (the hard bound of the exhaustive subset enumeration; larger pools return UNKNOWN), motivating explicit admission input limits; an EFD sweep over panels of 2/4/6/8 voters with disjoint exposures and a two-of-n rule computes a cut of 2 at every size.
- **Engineering verification:** 133 passing tests plus strict TypeScript checking; the persistent replay store yields exactly one successful nonce consumption under an 8-process race and after a store restart.

## Authors & Credibility

Jun He and Deying Yu are affiliated with OpenKedge.io, an independent research outfit rather than an academic institution. The paper sits at the center of a self-authored research line: it imports its structural-cut machinery from the authors' own prior "Epistemic Fault Domains" paper (arXiv:2609.02925) and frames itself within their "post-deterministic distributed systems" (arXiv:2606.01722), while positioning against Mnemosyne (arXiv:2607.00269, Chang et al.) and Atomix for agentic transaction handling. Artifact hygiene is unusually strong for a preprint: frozen JSONL traces with a SHA-256 manifest, a refusal to overwrite existing output directories, an independent Python analysis that re-checks file hashes and trial IDs before generating tables, and an explicit AI-use disclosure (OpenAI Codex and Google Antigravity assisted with LaTeX, implementation, tests, and analysis; all reported measurements come from executed code). The recurring "corrected" language (corrected suite, corrected matrix, repaired prototype) indicates the authors discarded a previous 5,261-record batch whose controller-dependent labels could not be repaired retrospectively — a transparent revision, though it also signals the evaluation was rebuilt once.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** The core interface — typed obligations with evidence classes, freshness, and ternary discharge — is a careful composition of known ideas (ABAC environmental attributes, proof-carrying code's producer/checker separation, commit-time authorization) rather than a new expressiveness result; the authors say so explicitly ("we do not claim deterministic admission, obligations, or repair in isolation as novel"). The genuinely new pieces are the EFD structural-cut integration into witness validity and the certificate/nonce dispatch binding.
- **Rigor:** The formal results are honestly conditional (Theorem 3 is a refinement of Algorithm 1's permit path, "not a proof that its evidence claims are true"), and Appendix A supplies boundary counterexamples. The empirical methodology (independent observer, matched fault schedules, rotated controller order, mechanism-isolating ablations) is well above the norm for agentic-safety preprints. However, the counts are descriptive with no confidence intervals, by the authors' own statement.
- **The headline comparison is narrow:** CAC's entire advantage over the LivePolicy baseline is the 30 correlated-witness harms; on every other scenario they tie at 120 completions. The paper admits "this suite therefore does not establish a general utility advantage over a competent live-policy check." A reader should treat CAC as "LivePolicy + structural cuts + certificates," and the paper never prices what the certificate machinery buys in scenarios the suite does not contain (e.g., replay, cross-service guard drift after forwarding).
- **Benchmark circularity:** CACBench is "constructed around CAC's mechanisms and is not a representative sample of operational incidents" (verbatim). The one scenario where CAC wins tests exactly the mechanism (EFD cuts) that CAC adds; the wrong-class and guard scenarios similarly test CAC-authored policies. No comparison against a Mnemosyne-style runtime is provided despite direct overlap, which the authors flag as absent.
- **No agent and no system:** despite the "cognitive"/"agentic" framing, no language-model API is invoked, the proposer is scripted, and the target is an in-process callback — "not a deployed PostgreSQL or Kubernetes service." The 4.65–26.86 ms path cost excludes receipt acquisition, model inference, and remote execution, so deployment latency is unmeasured.
- **Scalability cliff:** witness selection is exponential subset enumeration hard-bounded at 12 receipts (0.072 ms → 67.31–72.69 ms from 1 to 12), returning UNKNOWN beyond; cut computation is likewise combinatorial. High-throughput or large-coalition behavior is explicitly not established.
- **Red flags:** single-vendor authorship with three self-citations forming the foundation; the discarded prior evaluation archive; policy adequacy is entirely out of scope ("an omitted dependency can produce an incomplete obligation set"), so the safety story is policy-relative by construction. These are disclosed rather than hidden, which raises rather than lowers trust, but they cap the strength of the claims.

## Strengths

- Crisp separation of authorization (who may act) from readiness (is the world in a state where acting is safe), formalized as Theorem 1 and made operational through obligations and evidence classes.
- The ternary discharge with fixed diagnostic precedence is a genuinely useful primitive: it distinguishes "no observation" from "counterevidence," which binary policy engines conflate.
- Proposition 1 is a clean, transferable negative result: any compensatory scalar admission score (e.g., additive LLM-confidence proxies) can be driven over threshold by unrelated features while a required condition stays unsatisfied.
- Evaluation hygiene is exemplary: independent effect observation decoupled from controller verdicts (harmful transitions can return success ACKs, lost responses counted separately), matched pre-dispatch fault injection applied identically to all controllers, mechanism-isolating ablations, frozen artifacts with hash manifests.
- Unusual honesty: the paper repeatedly states what its results do not establish (production failure rates, LLM capability comparisons, general utility over live-policy checking, EFD scaling).

## Limitations

- Guarantees are policy-relative: a wrong or incomplete policy, an authentic-but-false sensor reading, or an omitted dependency still permits harm; nothing derives the obligation set from natural-language intent.
- Guard reads are not atomic with target execution; preservation until effect requires target-side conditional execution (Mode B) or a transaction protocol (Mode C), neither implemented here — only Mode A (check-before-forward) is exercised.
- The suite is 13 hand-constructed failover state machines with 30 parameter seeds per controller; no real workloads, no distributed replay store (the persistent store is a local file with fsync), no network isolation, no partial failures, no post-forwarding races.
- Witness search and cut computation are combinatorial with hard bounds (12 receipts), and timing variance across the three processes cautions against treating any single median/p99 as an SLO.
- Long-running workflows need fresh admission at each transition; recursive evidence acquisition needs a well-founded discipline that the discharge rules do not imply.
- No implementation comparison against Mnemosyne-style agent transaction runtimes, whose enforcement boundary directly overlaps.

## Relevance

这篇论文直接命中"LLM 智能体安全执行边界"这一方向：它给出了把缺失证据显式化为可检验义务、并用结构割抵御相关见证失败的形式化框架，与自治智能体操作系统、agent 事务运行时（Mnemosyne/Atomix）构成同一谱系。对研究"多智能体系统的安全保证与 quorum 可靠性"的工作（尤其涉及证据类型化、新鲜度与一次性派发能力）有直接参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How much of CAC's benefit survives when the LivePolicy baseline is simply extended with the EFD structural-cut check — is the full certificate/nonce/manifest machinery necessary, or is the cut the only load-bearing addition on realistic workloads?
- Can the exponential witness-subset search be replaced by a polynomial approximation (e.g., greedy canonical selection with a soundness-preserving fallback) without weakening the ternary discharge semantics?
- What is the end-to-end latency and false-refusal rate when receipts come from real telemetry (expensive sensors, network RTT) and the replay store is a distributed linearizable service rather than a local fsynced file?
- How would an actual LLM proposer interact with DEFER remediation loops — does targeted evidence acquisition reduce token cost and hallucinated evidence compared to free-form re-planning, and can model confidence be safely given an evidentiary role under a declared policy?
- Can policy adequacy (the mapping from domain invariants to a complete obligation set) be semi-automated, e.g., derived from failure-mode analyses or incident postmortems with human approval, so "omitted dependency" holes become discoverable?
- Does Mode B/C guard preservation (target-side conditional execution or a transaction protocol) compose correctly with the post-deterministic distributed systems model the authors propose elsewhere, and what does a deployed integration with a capability broker like SAB cost?
