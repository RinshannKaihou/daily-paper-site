Why this mattersRelevance

Critical edgeLimitations

Even after verification-aware retraining, only 2–3 of the top-20 clean features are provably retained — an auditor still cannot rely on interpretation invariance.

And the headline −90% retraining win is demonstrated only on GPT-2's SAE; the 1–2.6B models get verification, not retraining.

01

Method

measure the fragility → certify it → train against the certificate

STEP 1 — ATTACK STUDY

Witness the flips

1,000 WikiText-2 sentences with maximally polysemous targets, paraphrased at three tiers: MINOR (one content word → WordNet synonym), MEDIUM, MAJOR (Gemma 2 2B-it rephrase). Clean-vs-perturbed top-20 feature sets compared via Jaccard across all layers of SAEs and transcoders in five model families.

STEP 2 — FORMAL VERIFICATION

Certify the gap

Adversarial set = convex hull of synonym embeddings. δ-equivalence turns faithfulness into reachability of the difference network MLPk − IRNk: PGD brackets the lower bound δ, zonotope propagation (CORA) certifies a sound upper bound δ̄; soundness is machine-checked in Lean 4. Look-ahead intervals + matrix collapse cut complexity O(d·D²) → O(d²·D), avoiding the 131K-dim zonotope entirely.

STEP 3 — VERIFICATION-AWARE TRAINING

Train against the proof

OpenAI's GPT-2 SAE fine-tuned (50 epochs) under four regimes — STD, PGD-adversarial, SET (reachable-set volume loss), each optionally rank-16 LoRA — then re-verified under the identical per-sample-hull protocol on held-out data. Dedicated zonotope enclosures handle TopK (Llama, Qwen) and JumpReLU (Gemma Scope) activations.

What the certificate buys

≈ −90% — certified faithfulness gap after SET retraining (GPT-2 SAE, layer 6)
5× — sparser code: 13.9 vs 74.4 active features
~1.4 min — median verification per (sample, block) on an RTX 3080 laptop GPU
21% — of MINOR paraphrases flip GPT-2's own next-token prediction: part of the residual gap is model fragility, not IRN error

Beyond the headline

  • Certified at every layer: a substantial δ ≤ δ* ≤ δ̄ gap is verified for GPT-2 at all layers, widening with depth — both bounds rise together, so it is not a verifier artifact.
  • Scale record: the largest IRN verified is Llama 3.2 1B's TopK SAE (131,072 features, K=32): layer-1 δ = 0.055 vs certified δ̄ = 0.225, gap widening across all 16 layers.
  • Cross-model spread: R1-Distill-Qwen 1.5B's SAE is an order of magnitude less faithful than Llama 3.2 1B's; JumpReLU (Gemma Scope) enclosures are looser still.
  • Transcoders verify worse than SAEs, as expected from their harder reconstruction target (MLP output, not identity).
03

Verdict — side by side

what holds · what doesn't

  • First sound formal guarantees for mechanistic interpretability of LLMs, with Lean 4 machine-checked proofs — category creation, not an increment.
  • Laptop-GPU scalability: interval look-ahead + matrix collapse make a theoretically intractable problem runnable for 131K-feature dictionaries.
  • Both-sided honesty: attack bounds and certified bounds reported everywhere; the paper refuses to advertise only the 73%/95% attack numbers.
  • A rare win-win: ~90% tighter certified bound and a ~5× sparser dictionary, without a sparsity penalty.
  • Concrete stakes: the pathogen-"culture" deception demo is fully documented with Neuronpedia feature IDs.
  • Reproducibility: released code, Lean formalizations, exact configs, public HuggingFace artifacts only, disclosed AI assistance.
  • Single-model retraining: SET is shown only on GPT-2 (124M); transfer to Gemma/Llama/Qwen IRNs with non-elementwise activations is untested.
  • Certified retention stays dismal: 2–3 of top-20 features — interpretation invariance remains unusable for auditors even after retraining.
  • Per-layer only: per-sample hulls sidestep attention propagation; the compositional whole-model pipeline is future work and current hulls are ~5× too small.
  • Uneven verifier tightness: TopK/JumpReLU enclosures leave a wide δ–δ̄ gap; several δ̄ searches saturate at the cap (51,200) — max columns are artifacts.
  • Leaky threat model: WordNet "synonyms" of polysemous targets (culture, bank) are not always meaning-preserving, so the hull over-covers.
  • Narrow evaluation: WikiText-2 / English / ≤2.6B models; no safety-specific corpora, no certified-defense baselines; an unexplained rank-32 LoRA quirk (Tab. 9).
04

Open questions

where this line of work goes next

Q1

Does SET-style training transfer to JumpReLU/TopK dictionaries, or do discontinuous, input-dependent gates fundamentally cap certified faithfulness?

Q2

If the base model is first adversarially robustified, how much does the certified Jaccard bound rise — how much of the gap is model fragility vs IRN error?

Q3

Can tighter attention enclosures make the compositional scheme practical, so whole-model faithfulness is verified end-to-end?

Q4

Is the synonym-embedding convex hull the right threat model — would ℓ∞ balls or paraphrase-generator sets give tighter certified bounds?

Q5

Can a persistent large difference set automatically signal "dictionary too small" and trigger dynamic feature expansion?

Q6

Does the certified gap widen on safety-relevant corpora — exactly where auditors need interpretations to hold?
