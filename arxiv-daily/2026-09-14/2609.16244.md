> **一句话总结：** WMHA 是一款延迟优先的扩散 Transformer 推理加速器：利用扩散推理"形状静态、无自回归递归"的特性，用 256 位 VLIW 指令字同时发射 systolic 阵列、向量单元和两路 DMA 四个引擎，配合单遍 online-softmax 注意力与 FP8/BF16 权重驻留数据流；在 sky130 上完成 RTL、UVM 验证与五引擎布线，其"必须真正去噪"的语义验收门以 0.0433 的 MSE 比满足冻结的 ≤0.1 要求（约 23 倍余量），且仅靠软件流水重排指令（不改 RTL）就把典型程序的引擎并发度从 1.83% 提升到 44.66%、每层周期数从 1,605,202 降到 1,081,582（1.484× 加速）。

> **TL;DR:** A single-author technical report describing WMHA, a latency-first diffusion-transformer inference accelerator (VLIW 4-slot issue, 16×16 weight-stationary dual-dot array in FP8 E4M3/BF16 with FP32 accumulation, single-pass online-softmax attention with resident K/V) implemented in synthesizable SystemVerilog and verified by a UVM environment against a double-precision reference. Against its frozen semantic acceptance gate (final-latent MSE ratio ≤ 0.1 vs the starting noise on a real denoising trajectory), the device achieves 0.0433/0.0434 at both synthesized configurations (~23× better than required) with zero failures across 237,214,881 checked values; on the canonical DiT block program, software pipelining alone — with bit-identical engine busy cycles — delivers a 1.484× speedup (1,605,202 → 1,081,582 cycles/layer) over the serialized schedule. Eleven application benchmarks built from published model shapes (DiT-XL/2, Wan 2.1 video, diffusion policy, AlphaFold 3 head, 3DShape2VecSet, SD3 guidance) run on the device; five engines reach routed sky130 layout (76.7–216 MHz post-route) while the full chip is synthesis-only (5.77 M cells, 68.355 mm²). Honest and methodologically rigorous, but with no head-to-head hardware baseline, ~16% array occupancy on full blocks (weight-bandwidth-bound), and no full-chip timing.

## 核心贡献

- **针对扩散推理的静态 VLIW 控制路径**：扩散模型每一步去噪都是全序列、静态形状的前向传播，无数据依赖控制流，因此整个调度可在编译期确定。WMHA 用一个 256 位指令字同时发射四个引擎（systolic 阵列、向量单元 VPU、两路独立 DMA），依赖关系以显式 wait mask 表达，无记分板、无寄存器重命名；循环控制零开销。实测控制开销为每指令字 9 个周期，代表性程序中引擎忙时占比 99.4%。
- **权重驻留的 16×16 dual-dot 阵列 + 单遍 online-softmax 注意力流水线**：每个 PE 可算 4 路 FP8 E4M3 或 2 路 BF16 精确点积、FP32 累加；注意力采用单遍 online softmax（组级标量运行最大值、Newton 倒数归一化），K/V 常驻 stationary 区域，彻底消除 N×N 分数矩阵；生成标签概率环 + beat-valid 互锁使流水线健康度可由硬件计数器观测。
- **先冻结、后实现的语义验收标准**：在写任何 RTL 之前冻结"设备必须执行真实采样轨迹并将最终潜变量 MSE 降至初始噪声的 1/10 以下"的验收门，并论证逐元素容差检查无法区分"算术正确但语义无用"的设备（如全零网络）。实测去噪比 0.0433/0.0434，超出门限约 23 倍，两配置下 2.37 亿检查值零失败。
- **保守可组合的容差模型 + 阴性对照**：每个检查值携带归约树深度、累加链长、分段线性单元包络与存储量化项的元数据，组合成保守带宽；阴性对照（故意扰动的重放）必须以 ≥100× 余量被拒绝，实测以 2131× 被拒绝，证明容差带仍有判别力。
- **纯软件流水的量化结果**：不改一行 RTL，仅将规范程序重排为一层深软件流水，多引擎并发度从 1.83% 升至 44.66%，每层周期从 1,605,202 降至 1,081,582（1.484×），且引擎忙周期位级一致，证明 ≥95% 收益来自真实重叠。
- **对开源 sky130 物理流程局限的定量刻画**：实测布线前静态时序因缺失高扇出缓冲而虚高（epilogue 前仿真 24.2 MHz vs 布线后 76.7 MHz，3.2×），并量化了宿主机内存导致全芯片无法布线的天花板（约 536k 实例/30 GB），给出完成全芯片布局所需的机器配置（256 GB/32–64 核）与设计改造（318 个推断存储器映射为宏）。

## Key Contributions

- **A static VLIW control path justified by the workload**: diffusion inference has no autoregressive recurrence — every denoising step is a full-sequence forward pass over compile-time-static shapes, so the entire schedule is known at compile time. One 256-bit WMHA instruction word issues up to four engines (systolic ARRAY, vector VPU, DMA_LD, DMA_ST); dependences are explicit wait masks resolved at compile time, loop back-edges cost zero cycles, and the measured control cost is 9 cycles per instruction word with engines busy 99.4% of wall time on a representative program.
- **Weight-stationary contraction array with single-pass online-softmax attention**: a 16×16 grid of dual-dot cells (selected by a synthesis-based bake-off over FP8/BF16/INT8 candidates) computes four FP8 E4M3 or two BF16 products exactly, accumulating into FP32; the dot introduces no rounding before normalization. Attention runs as a software-pipelined loop over K/V groups with a group-wide scalar running maximum, resident keys/values, generation-tagged probability ring, and architecturally visible interlock counters — the quadratic score matrix never exists.
- **A semantic acceptance criterion frozen before any RTL existed**: the device must execute a real sampling trajectory and reduce final-latent MSE against a known clean latent by at least 10× — a gate that an all-zero (arithmetically correct but semantically useless) network would fail. Achieved denoising ratio is 0.0433 (16×16) / 0.0434 (8×8), roughly 23× better than the frozen ≤ 0.1 requirement, with 0 element failures across 237,214,881 (16×16) and 234,299,122 (8×8) checked values.
- **A conservative, composable tolerance model with a live negative control**: every recorded value carries metadata (reduction-tree depth, accumulation-chain length, PWL envelope, storage-quantization term) composed into a band via a conservative ℓ1 walk; exact-class values (e.g., DMA copies) are compared bit-for-bit; bands split into self-injected and propagated parts to avoid double counting across chained programs. A deliberately perturbed replay must keep failing (it is rejected with a 2131× margin against a required floor of 100×), otherwise the campaign is declared invalid.
- **A software-pipelining result requiring no hardware change**: restructuring the canonical block program from fully fenced iterations into a one-deep software pipeline raised multi-engine concurrency from 1.83% to 44.66% of cycles and cut cycles per layer from 1,605,202 to 1,081,582 (1.484× speedup), with array/vector busy cycles bit-identical before and after — proving at least 95% of the gain is genuine overlap, not deleted work.
- **Quantified honesty about the open-source physical flow**: five engines were routed to GDS in sky130 with parasitic-annotated timing and measured-activity power (76.7–216 MHz post-route); the paper measures a 3.2× pre-layout timing optimism artifact (epilogue: 24.2 MHz pre-layout vs 76.7 MHz post-route; 98% of one 90.95 ns path sat in a single un-buffered NOR gate driving 6.98 pF), documents the host memory ceiling (~536 k instances on 30 GB) that stopped full-chip place-and-route, and specifies the machine (256 GB RAM, 32–64 cores) plus design work (mapping 318 inferred memories to macros) that would close the gap.

## Methodology

The project follows a specification-first, freeze-before-build discipline. A micro-architecture document was written and frozen before RTL existed, then subjected to five rounds of adversarial review (binding architect brief → targeted rewrite → regression sweep), with blocking findings falling 78 → 29 → 21 → 14 → 8 → 2; uncertain mechanisms carried pre-committed fallbacks (e.g., the attention schedule's failed two-stage skew was replaced by a pre-approved three-stage form without renegotiation), and post-freeze mismatches could only be resolved as RTL bugs or dated specification errata (22 issued).

The architecture itself: a VLIW sequencer issues one 256-bit instruction word containing four 56-bit engine slots plus a zero-overhead loop-control tail; descriptors (base/stride/window) are captured at issue and walked by hardware, enabling ring buffers and double-buffered stationary operands without instruction overhead. On-chip memory is partitioned into four banked regions (activations, stationary weights, accumulator/output, descriptors) behind a crossbar whose per-region port budget (typically 2R1W per cycle) is an architectural contract enforced as a program error. Engine latencies are pinned architectural constants bound to the RTL by assertions (e.g., array operation in flight 14N−1 = 223 cycles; reciprocal 19 cycles; drain normalization 150 cycles at the widest head dimension).

Numerics: FP8 E4M3 (saturation ceiling ±448 with an explicit band term) and BF16 with FP32 accumulation, verified against a double-precision replay reference model that executes the same instruction stream (thereby also validating encoding). The tolerance model composes per-value metadata into conservative bands, treats pure data movement as exact-class bit-for-bit checks, floors flushed-to-zero values, and propagates self-injected bands through normalization Jacobians (bounded by reciprocal row standard deviation times (1 + 2/d)).

Verification: a 34-row UVM suite (24 base rows run at both synthesized configurations as a hard rule, plus 10 application rows) with a scoreboard sweeping device memory at program boundaries; coverage exclusions are hand-authored with reason strings and checked in strict mode that fails if an excluded object is in fact covered. The acceptance row re-ran bit-identically across seven independent runs after every change on its compile path.

Physical implementation: SiliconCompiler/OpenROAD sky130 flow; the synthesized netlist is first fed back through the UVM suite as the device before any synthesis; blocks under the host's demonstrated ~536 k-instance place-and-route ceiling proceed to routed GDS with SPEF extraction, post-route STA, and power at measured switching activity (the assumed 0.05 activity turned out to be within −8% to +4% on three of four engines because clock power dominates); blocks over the ceiling are reported for area and cells only, with no frequency claimed.

## Results & Highlights

- **Semantic acceptance gate (the frozen headline criterion)**: final-latent MSE ratio against a clean latent of 0.0433 (16×16) and 0.0434 (8×8) versus the frozen requirement of ≤ 0.1 — roughly 23× better than required — with 0 element failures out of 237,214,881 checked values (16×16) and 234,299,122 (8×8); trajectory MSE came in at 9.64×10⁻⁶ with 13,836× headroom to the composed per-step bound.
- **Software pipelining, no RTL change (canonical DiT block program)**: cycles per layer 1,605,202 → 1,081,582 (1.484× speedup); multi-engine concurrency 1.83% → 44.66% of cycles; array occupancy 31.24% → 46.37%; DMA-load occupancy 48.70% → 71.29%; non-overlapped overhead 0.63% → 0.26%. Array and vector busy cycles were bit-identical across the two runs, bounding deleted work at ~52.5k cycles against the 1,047,239-cycle saving (≥ 95% genuine overlap).
- **Control-path cost**: measured 9 cycles per instruction word (via a single-word zero-overhead loop); sequencer walk consumes 5.25% of wall time on the canonical program but only 0.006% is ever exposed as idle; engines are busy 99.4% of wall time.
- **Application benchmarks on the 16×16 device (measured, scaled shapes)**: full video block (Wan 2.1, N=192) takes 3,384,210 cycles with load-DMA occupancy 70.3% vs array 16.1% — the block is weight-bandwidth-bound at every shape (a token sweep from 128 to 1024 leaves both figures unchanged; a batched guidance pair at 2×256 tokens scales cycles and load bytes by 1.98× with array occupancy still 15.9%), because the stripe-outer GEMM loop re-streams each weight image at an intensity of 16 MACs per weight byte. Isolated steady-state attention reaches 47.1% array occupancy under the skewed software pipeline with the softmax interlock stall counter reading exactly zero. The DDPM guidance combine (V_CFG, g=7.5) runs in 51,647 cycles with 0.0% array involvement — one native vector operation instead of a host round-trip per sampler step.
- **Ragged head dimension cost (DiT-XL/2's dh=72)**: legal through the ragged closure but costs 1.58× the cycles of dh=64 for only 1.125× the useful work, because the pad chunk is streamed and multiplied like data; the fused attention ring is exactly full at dh=64 (would need 26.6 KB at dh=72), so fused attention serves dh 64/128 and DiT-XL/2's native dh=72 only via the single-group path.
- **Verification coverage**: functional and FSM coverage 100% at both configurations; line 98.23%/97.76%, condition 87.92%/86.12%, toggle 92.39%/86.36%, assertion 96.93%/95.34%, overall 95.60%/94.11% (16×16/8×8) with authored exclusions under strict-mode zero-over-exclusion checks; the negative control is rejected with a 2131× margin against the required 100× floor.
- **Physical results (sky130, typical corner, 10.0 ns SDC)**: post-route fmax per routed engine — CSR 216.0 MHz (0.032 mm²), PE cell 153.4 MHz (0.050 mm²), DMA store 128.5 MHz (0.273 mm²), DMA load 117.5 MHz (0.193 mm²), epilogue 76.7 MHz (1.594 mm²) with measured-activity power from 4.24 mW to 172 mW; the epilogue's pre-layout figure of 24.2 MHz quantifies a 3.2× fanout-buffering artifact in the open flow. The full chip synthesizes to 5,771,328 cells and 68.355 mm² in 46 minutes at 9.7 GB peak but exceeds the host's place-and-route ceiling; the sequencer specifically fails to route (255-level ripple-carry chains; 636 k routing violations at 40% density; host memory exhausted at 18%).

## Authors & Credibility

Single-author work: Shashank (Shashank Chaurasia), an independent, self-funded researcher (contact email at USC alumni domain), stating that the RTL, UVM environment, and functional results were developed on industry-standard simulators and the physical work uses the SiliconCompiler/OpenROAD sky130 flow "proven on our earlier training and inference chips" — those earlier chips are referenced but not published or cited, which is unverifiable. This is a technical report (manuscript prepared August 2026, revised September 2026), not peer-reviewed. That said, the internal credibility discipline is unusually strong for a solo project: frozen pre-implementation acceptance criteria, a live negative control with a measured rejection margin, bit-identical re-runs across seven acceptance runs, authored coverage exclusions with strict-mode over-exclusion rejection, explicit separation of measured occupancy from analytical projections in every benchmark table, and a "What we do not claim" section that declines to quote TOPS, full-chip frequency, or any advanced-node extrapolation.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: moderate on mechanisms, high on methodology. Every component — VLIW issue (Fisher 1983), weight-stationary systolic dataflow (TPU lineage), online softmax (Milakov–Gimelshein / FlashAttention lineage), FP8 with FP32 accumulation — is established; the paper says so itself. The genuinely novel artifacts are the frozen semantic acceptance criterion for generative accelerators (with the all-zero-network argument for why element-wise tolerance is insufficient) and the quantified honesty about the open-source physical flow (the 3.2× pre-layout timing artifact and the host-ceiling analysis).
- **Rigor**: verification rigor is exemplary (double-precision replay reference, 237 M checked values with zero failures, both configurations as a hard rule, negative control). The performance claims are more mixed: the central "latency-first" motivation is undercut by the paper's own Table VI — full blocks run at ~16% array occupancy / ~70% load-DMA occupancy at every tested shape because region W cannot hold one weight matrix, i.e., the hardware spends most of its time waiting on weight streaming at testbench shapes. The authors disclose this candidly and name the fix (weight residency across stripes), but it means the architecture's compute engines are badly underutilized by the shipped compiler's loop order.
- **Reproducibility**: strong on process (frozen spec, five-round adversarial review with finding counts 78→2, 22 dated errata, pinned-latency assertions) but the artifact itself is not stated to be open-sourced, and it is a one-person project with no independent replication of any number. All physical numbers are sky130 (130 nm) — post-route frequencies of 76.7–216 MHz are one to two orders of magnitude below commercial accelerators and the paper explicitly refuses to extrapolate, which is honest but also means the design has no demonstrated competitive latency.
- **Significance**: as a deployable accelerator, limited — no full-chip layout, no fabricated silicon, no end-to-end time-to-image number, and no comparison against any baseline hardware (no GPU, no TPU, no other diffusion accelerator appears in the related-work comparison; the benchmarks compare the device only to its own analytical projections). As a methodological template for accelerator verification and open-flow physical design reporting, it is well above average.
- **Red flags**: (a) the flagship "original diffusion-transformer configuration" (DiT-XL/2, dh=72) is excluded from the fused attention path by the paper's own memory sizing and runs only through the degraded single-group path at 1.58× cycle cost — a sizing miss against the paper's own primary target workload; (b) application benchmarks all run at scaled shapes because the testbench memory is small, so absolute cycle counts (e.g., 9,365,022 for the 2×256 guidance pair) do not correspond to production model sizes; (c) the tolerance model is conservative by construction (no cancellation modeling) and its usefulness rests on a single negative control at one perturbation scale; (d) single-author, self-funded, prior-chip claims uncited.

## Strengths

- The semantic acceptance criterion — "the device must demonstrably denoise, MSE ≤ 1/10 of the starting noise" — frozen before RTL, is a genuinely good idea that generalizes to any generative accelerator, and the all-zero-network argument for why structural checking is insufficient is crisp.
- The software-pipelining section is a model of how to make a performance claim trustworthy: bit-identical busy cycles before/after, an explicit bound on deleted work (~52.5k cycles vs a 1.05 M-cycle saving), and a named limiter (1.403× remaining headroom, bounded by the store fence and engine-granular wait masks) for the next iteration.
- Exceptional disclosure norms: measured occupancy and analytical projections are labelled separately and never mixed; the pre-layout timing artifact (98% of one path in a single un-buffered NOR gate) and the host memory ceiling are quantified rather than hidden; the "What we do not claim" section explicitly refuses TOPS and full-chip frequency numbers.
- Pre-committed fallbacks and dated errata as the only post-freeze edit channel are a lightweight but effective spec-discipline mechanism, and the five-round review finding counts (78→2) make the process auditable.
- Both synthesized configurations (16×16 and 8×8) are independently verified as a hard rule — a cheap practice that catches configuration-specific errors (one erratum was exactly such a case).

## Limitations

- **Weight-bandwidth-bound at the block level**: the emitted stripe-outer GEMM loop re-streams weight images at 16 MACs/byte, holding array occupancy near 16% at every token count and batch; the workload analysis's batching-amortization assumption does not hold for the shipped programs. Fixing it (weight residency across stripes or taller stripes) is future work.
- **Fused attention excludes dh=72**: the resident K/V ring exactly fills region W at dh=64 and would need 26.6 KB at 72, so DiT-XL/2 and PixArt fall outside the fused path — the paper's own headline configuration is served only by the slower single-group route.
- **No full-chip physical realization**: the 5.77 M-cell, 68.355 mm² chip is synthesis-only; four large engines and the sequencer were never routed (sequencer not converging at any tried density), and there is no fabricated silicon or measured silicon result.
- **sky130 flop-lowered memories distort area**: with no memory macros, 318 inferred memories lower to flip-flops (the crossbar alone becomes 530 k flops), so the 68.355 mm² area is dominated by storage (~25 mm² crossbar + memory, ~20 mm² array) and, as the paper notes, is not a meaningful estimate of the same architecture in a process with compiled memories.
- **No external baseline or end-to-end number**: no comparison against GPU/TPU-class or other diffusion-accelerator latency, no time-to-image, and no throughput; all application benchmarks run at scaled shapes on a small testbench memory.
- **Coverage residue and stale merge**: overall code coverage is 95.6%/94.11% with the residue listed rather than waived, and the coverage merge predates the ten application rows.

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would restructuring the GEMM loop for weight residency across stripes (or doubling region W) lift array occupancy from ~16% toward the isolated-attention figure of 47%, and what per-step latency results at production model shapes rather than scaled ones?
- How does WMHA's per-step latency on the canonical DiT trajectory compare against a modern GPU or existing diffusion accelerators running the same trajectory — the paper contains no external hardware baseline at all?
- Does the contractive-sampler argument (iterative denoising absorbs low-precision perturbations) survive flow-matching / rectified-flow schedules with few steps (e.g., SD3's 4–28 step regime), where per-step error has less trajectory to average out?
- Can the sequencer's 255-level ripple-carry chains be restructured (carry-select / prefix adders) to close layout on a commodity host, and what post-route frequency would the control path then sustain?
- How much of the 68.355 mm² flop-dominated area disappears once the 318 inferred memories are mapped to macros, and does that change the crossbar-vs-array area balance enough to alter the next revision's sizing decisions (26.6 KB attention ring at dh=72)?
- Could the frozen semantic acceptance gate (denoise by 10× or fail) be adopted as a standard regression criterion in software-side low-precision diffusion kernels, where the same element-wise-tolerance blind spot exists?
