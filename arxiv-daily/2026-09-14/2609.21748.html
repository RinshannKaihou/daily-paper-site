A behaviorally “mapless” transformer actually encodes a near-perfect Manhattan map — navigation fails from superposition interference, not a missing world model.

TaxiGPT 内部其实编码了高度忠实的曼哈顿街道地图（99.6% 路口可解码）——此前的行为失败源于叠加表示间的定位干扰破坏了导航，而非地图缺失；“按可供性打包”的表示组织方式还能显著限制定位滑移的行为代价。

Why this matters

Behavioral benchmarks can’t tell whether a model has a world model — these causal mechanistic indicators can, separating having a map from using one.

提供“行为失败 ≠ 表示缺失”的因果分析范式与可复用机制指标（解码 / 传送 / 街道探针 / 罗盘），可迁移至自定义序列环境的评估。

⚠ Critical edge

All evidence comes from one toy domain — a finite-state, deterministic gridworld of ~4.5k states and six move tokens. Transfer to richer, stochastic, or LLM-scale worlds is untested.

— Critical Assessment · Limitations

01Methodology — forensic, not new training

  1. Probe the pretrained model. Diff-means features and linear probes extract intersection representations from TaxiGPT (GPT2-XL, 48L × 1,600d, from Vafa et al. 2024), swept across layers.
  2. Edit causally. A minimal “teleportation” edit shifts the encoded position next to the goal; greedy predictions confirm the code is used.
  3. Recover streets. Steering tests and held-out per-move linear probes predict the next intersection’s feature direction.
  4. Map the geometry. PCA (244/1,600 dims ≈ 90% variance), nearest-neighbor angles (48.1°), and 104 affordance groups characterize superposition and packing.
  5. Isolate circuits. Look-back-window ablations localize position; logit lens attributes legal-move writes; a 16-bin circular goal compass is fitted and validated by steering + ablation.
  6. Forensics + comparison. Stress/detour/compression tests are re-run with internal instrumentation; four failure modes are each repaired by matched interventions (random-edit controls). The full suite repeats across five configs and training checkpoints.

Failure accounting — what breaks navigation

  • full corruption 29%
  • fatal slip 25%
  • silent slip 24%
  • give-up 16%
  • other 6%

Four superposition-interference modes explain 93.5% of illegal moves (99.5% with unlucky draws). Clearing position noise in fatal slips removes 0.80 of illegal probability mass — vs ≤9% for matched random edits.

03Strengths

  • Turns a famous negative result into a methodological lesson — behavior underdetermines mechanism — with causal, not merely correlational, evidence.
  • Complete failure accounting: four modes + unlucky draws explain 99.5% of illegal moves, each validated with matched random-edit controls.
  • Affordance packing gives a functional, robustness-increasing explanation for why same-move states cluster.
  • Mechanistic indicators cleanly dissociate map fidelity from map use — invisible to any behavioral metric.
  • Honest scoping: explicit toy-domain acknowledgment, public code, reproducibility statement.

04Limitations

  • Single toy environment (~4,516 states, six moves); no transfer evidence to stochastic, partially observable, or LLM-scale worlds.
  • Post-hoc study of one checkpoint family — no new model trained to test the proposed fixes.
  • The position-reinforcement repair needs ground-truth position: it diagnoses, doesn’t solve.
  • Emergence results from a single 384d run; cross-seed variance unreported.
  • Linear-probe / diff-means assumption throughout; no SAE or nonlinear comparison.
  • Modest samples in places (146 compression pairs); compass inherits 16-bin circularity pitfalls.

05Questions for further exploration

  • Do the four interference failure modes recur in Othello-GPT, chess transformers, maze solvers — or are they random-walk-specific?
  • Would training on the deep-and-far regime (5.4% under stress vs 0.1% in training) eliminate most illegal moves?
  • Can a ground-truth-free repair (denoising the position subspace) recover the 91.5%→97.3% gain?
  • Why do smaller models slip 3–6× less despite worse mechanistic indicators — is late, spread-out localization more robust?
  • Does navigation-before-localization emergence replicate across seeds, scales, and objectives?
  • Can these indicators scale to natural-language domains where states aren’t enumerable?
