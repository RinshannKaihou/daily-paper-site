Why this matters

Explains mechanistically why hybrid architectures deliver both capability and efficiency — and shows early training choices can re-tune that division.

Critical edge

Even the targeted lag-one mask relocates the circuit in only 2 of 3 seeds — the layer allocation is a stochastic outcome of early training, not a deterministic design property.

Methodology 方法01

The three classical induction roles — Carrying (moving predecessor info into the historical value), Matching (selecting a source by content), Copying — are measured with layer-type-agnostic activation patching on a shared block-update interface: patch an RMS-matched donor block update, read the change in the correct-minus-distractor logit margin. This works where attention-map diagnostics fail — recurrent (GDN) and convolutional mixers included.

Interventions keep architecture and corpus fixed: early lag-one convolution-channel masking, convolution removal, LR ×0.1 on efficient blocks for the first 3K–4K steps, SWA window sizes, and an induction-enriched training stream. Source-key restoration and fixed-value pattern swaps verify the relocated receiver actually reads the new Carrying site.

Confirmed at production scale Hybrid Qwen3.5-4B: strongest Carrying in GDN layer L18, immediately before strongest global Matching at L19 — restoring the clean K tensor there recovers nearly all of the sender-patch loss (Q, V, gate, FFN: little effect). Strengths 优势03

  • The paired block-update probe makes induction circuits measurable where attention maps don't exist — unblocking interpretability for the hybrid/recurrent family now shipping in production.
  • Finding corroborated across four independent levers (channel mask, conv surgery, LR timing, window size), three model families, and released 4B checkpoints.
  • Source-key restoration closes the causal loop: K transfer alone reproduces nearly all sender-patch loss; V has almost no effect.
  • Circuit tied to real behavior — recall PPL, domain PPL, head-ablation NLL — not just synthetic tasks.
  • Transparent statistics: three runs, SDs, identity controls, released code.

Limitations 局限04

  • All causal training results at 77–318M / 30K steps on OpenWebText; persistence at billions of parameters and trillions of tokens is unknown — cited evidence suggests gaps shrink with scale.
  • Matching probe reads a composite (selection + transmission + FFN); Copying is mostly appendix material.
  • Relocation is seed-variable (2/3 runs) — reported, not resolved.
  • Natural-text linkage is ablational, not traced; recall target groups are custom-defined, not standard benchmarks (no MQAR, no long-context suites).
  • The induction-enrichment curriculum is a coarse bigram filter; why it advances Transformer but not hybrid formation is asserted qualitatively.

Questions for further exploration 开放问题05

Does lag-one-dominated Carrying persist at 1B+ scale with multi-trillion-token training, or wash out as long-training convergence predicts?

Can early LR reduction become a deliberate curriculum (staged unfreezing) that improves code-like recall without the Wiki-PPL regression?

Could a channel-resolved probe identify the exact subspace carrying lag-one info — and is it shared between conv and recurrent paths?

Pure GDN uses query-time state; hybrids use source preparation — is there a hybrid ratio where models learn both routes?

Would an orthogonal data intervention (long-range repetition vs bigram reliability) target the Matching stage the way enrichment targets Carrying?

Do multiple Carrying–Matching pairs in deep models like Qwen3.5-4B cooperate, compete, or specialize by distance?
