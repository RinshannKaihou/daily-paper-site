Key finding

Every Transformer update splits into a nearly redundant parallel part — and a hyper-fragile perpendicular one.

残差更新的「平行」分量近乎冗余，「垂直」分量极其脆弱 —— 同一几何贯穿推理编辑、压缩诊断与预训练。

Deleting the parallel part of cross-token attention moves the score 73.42 → 73.31 — essentially free. Naive full-aggregate removal instead costs 8–10 pts.

Same asymmetry at 1.7B: −1.5 (exclude-self) vs −8.2 (naive). Perpendicular edits explode PPL to 10⁵–10⁶.

Why this matters

One geometric lens spans the whole model lifecycle — attention editing, compression diagnosis, pretraining bias — a reusable frame for reading representation dynamics.

与门控注意力、QK-norm 等高效架构设计的动机相互印证。

Critical edge

The pretraining gains are thin relative to cost: +0.7 / +1.5 pts on near-saturated benchmarks is within single-run variance — no seed replication, no error bars.

“Redundant” also quietly breaks at small scale: −3 to −5 RULER pts for 0.6B–1.7B models.

Methodology Eq. 1 · two sites

Every sub-layer update Δ is orthogonally projected against a reference z — the incoming hidden state (residual space) or the token's own value vector vt (attention value space) — splitting it into Δ∥ (pure magnitude rescaling) and Δ⊥ (semantic steering).

Interventions are forward-pass-only: ŷ = s∥·y∥ + s⊥·y⊥ via hooks fused into FlashAttention-2 — weights are never modified. The smartest construction is exclude-self: the value aggregate ot = dt + ct, where dt = Attvt is the token's identity carrier, kept intact while only the cross-token part ct is scaled. Naive removal of the full aggregate destroys dt — that, not magnitude, causes the 8–10 pt collapse.

Parallel-only edits reduce to closed-form changes of the attention diagonal, linking the geometry to attention-sink behavior. The same projection decomposes compression error, and from-scratch runs (296M–2.7B, ~104.9B tokens, 8×H100) enforce parallel suppression during both training and evaluation.

Evaluated on: WikiText-2 PPL · 7 zero-shot tasks (LM Eval Harness) · RULER 4k–12k · Qwen3 0.6B–30B-A3B, Llama-3, Gemma-3 · GSM8K / MMLU controls in appendix.

▲ Strengths

  • One idea, three stages. A single geometric formalism carried rigorously through inference editing, compression diagnosis, and pretraining intervention.
  • Exclude-self is genuinely explanatory. It decomposes the naive 8–10 pt collapse into identity-carrier suppression vs cross-token magnitude — cause, not correlation.
  • The audit kills the trivial objection. 49.10% output perturbation with behavior intact rules out “it works because the edit is smaller.”
  • Broad coverage for an interpretability paper: dense + MoE, Qwen3 0.6B–30B-A3B, Llama-3, Gemma-3, contexts to 12k.
  • Honest controls throughout: naive baselines, diagonal-removal, gated ablation, head-level sub-additivity analysis.

▼ Limitations

  • Decoder-only language models only — encoders, other modalities, and post-training/RLHF regimes untested.
  • Pretraining evidence tops out at 2.7B / ~105B tokens, single runs, no seed variance, near-ceiling benchmarks.
  • Compression finding is observational — no direction-aware compression algorithm is actually built; it stays a suggestion.
  • Redundancy is conditional. Costs grow at small scale (−3 to −5 RULER at 0.6–1.7B) and with context length (−2.4 at 12k).
  • Behavior link is indirect — perplexity and zero-shot accuracy only; edits are positioned as analysis tools, not deployable methods.

Questions for further exploration from the summary

Would a compression objective that explicitly penalizes perpendicular error beat isotropic ones at matched bit budgets — as the Discussion implies but never tests?
Why does layer 0 spike under joint all-head removal while deeper layers cancel sub-additively — are early parallel coordinates anchoring positional/identity information?
Does the +1.5 pt gain survive seed replication, stronger evals (MMLU, GSM8K, instruction following), and trillion-token budgets beyond 2.7B?
Can exclude-self structure buy inference-time efficiency — skipping or low-rank approximating cross-token parallel computation — not just diagnosis?
Does identity-carrier preservation still hold when attention sinks dominate streaming / long-context inference?
Do instruction-tuning / RLHF reshape the parallel:perpendicular ratio — and would suppression help in post-training too?
