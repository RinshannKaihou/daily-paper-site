Why this matters

Maps how sub-8-bit quantization silently corrupts vision-language models — and repairs it inside the number format itself.

系统刻画亚 8-bit 量化在多模态模型中的静默失效模式（微缩放坍塌、注意力图破坏），并给出格式级与硬件级修复。

Critical edge

The recovery is a borrowed crutch: without SageAttention-style K-mean subtraction, every sub-8-bit format — MiX included — collapses catastrophically (MXFP4g16 → 0.0% on ChartQA). Attribute the fix jointly, not to the format alone.

And all PPA numbers are synthesis + simulation only — no silicon.

Methodology / 01

Format. Each 32-element block stores a 4-bit block-max exponent and one 3-bit shared mantissa (taken from the block maximum); every element keeps only a sign bit and a 3-bit exponent offset Δe. A ±1 exponent refinement collapses to a single comparator at the 0.5 mantissa midpoint. The 4.5-bit variant splits blocks into four 8-element sub-groups, matching NVFP4's footprint without its FP8+FP32 two-level scaling.

Algebra. With activations in MiX and weights in MX, the shared mantissa and base exponents factor out of the block dot product — per-element work becomes barrel-shift and integer add, with one scalar multiply per block. Multiplier elimination is a mathematical consequence, not an engineering afterthought.

Silicon. A 4×4 output-stationary systolic array (512 MACs/cycle, one 32-element block per PE per cycle), a fused single-cycle MiX/MX output quantizer, and a static prefill-frozen K-mean smoother (6258 µm² · 1.8 mW) that rescues attention collapse for all sub-8-bit formats.

“Microscaling collapse” — share of Layer-27 K-matrix elements numerically dead at B=32 (Qwen2-VL-7B): a single vision outlier hijacks the shared exponent. Cause: a 34× vision/text dynamic-range gap.

  • Beats NVFP4 on other VLMs: MiniCPM-V-2.6 66.8% vs 64.9% (+1.9 pts); LLaVA-OneVision-7B 61.6% vs 61.0% (+0.6 pts).
  • End-to-end coverage wins: fully-quantized MiX-INT5 hits 70.8% on Qwen2-VL vs 60.0% (MBQ) / 61.5% (TLQ) for LLM-backbone-only PTQ — a ~9–10 pt gap at comparable bit-width.
  • Quantization fidelity: ~28 dB SQNR at intra-block range ratio 18, vs NVFP4 ~21 dB, MXFP4 ~14.5 dB, MXINT4 ~12 dB.
  • Scales favorably: FP16 gap shrinks 8.0 → 2.3 pts from 3B to 72B (Qwen2.5-VL); MiX overtakes NVFP4 beyond 3B (+2.4 pts at 72B).
  • Focus collapses where MiX doesn't: the token-pruning accelerator falls to 1.94× on MiniCPM-V (below INT8) because its resampler already compresses images to 64 tokens.
  • Bonus spec bug: OCP MXFP4's floor rounding silently clips the max element in ~40% of blocks; a ceil fix buys up to +10.9 pts on ChartQA — MiX gains stack on top.

Strengths / 03

  • Causal co-design: the format inversion exists precisely so multipliers disappear — a shifter-based PE that is 25% more area-efficient than NVFP4's at matched accuracy and bit-width.
  • Exceptional diagnosis: the 34× modality gap, Q/K-vs-V outlier topology, the dead-layer visualization, and the MXFP4 floor-rounding bug form one of the carefulest sub-8-bit failure analyses in recent VLM quantization work.
  • Breadth above norm: 3 VLMs × 6 benchmarks × 13 formats, 4 text-only LLMs, a 3B–72B scaling sweep, 15 synthesized RTL datapaths — plus honest negative results (trails NVFP4 on text-only perplexity).
  • Systems-flavored fix: freezing the K-mean at prefill makes smoothing O(1) per decode step and hardware-trivial, within 1 pt of the dynamic scheme.
  • Fully public artifact (Zenodo, MIT license) with per-table reproduction paths and self-checking RTL testbenches.

Limitations / 04

  • No silicon validation: all PPA numbers are 28 nm synthesis + cycle-level simulation; the composite Pareto metric (TOPS/mm² × TOPS/W) is author-defined, and absolute area/power depends on a proprietary cell library.
  • Still trails FP16 by 2.2–3.7 pts at 4.5 bits (MMMU 46.3 vs 50.4); the shared-mantissa trade shows up as clearly worse text-only perplexity — Llama-3.1-8B WikiText ppl 8.93 vs NVFP4's 7.94, FP16's 6.14.
  • Frozen K-mean untested where later images shift channel statistics (multi-turn conversations, streaming video).
  • Scope: main results cover 7B–8B VLMs only; the 72B end needs a B200-class GPU and ~500 GB host RAM to reproduce.
  • Baseline fairness: Focus run at its default (untuned) sparsity configuration on text-rich workloads; MBQ/TLQ numbers quoted from prior work at slightly different effective bit-widths.

Questions for further exploration / 05

  • Does the prefill-frozen K-mean drift under multi-turn or streaming-video settings where later tokens shift channel statistics — and what does periodic re-estimation cost in accuracy/latency?
  • Can MiX's per-element-exponent idea compose with rotation-based outlier removal (QuaRot/SpinQuant), or do rotations destroy the intra-block range structure MiX exploits?
  • Bit-flip sensitivity: does one corrupted 3-bit Δe (a 2⁷ range error) cause worse silent output corruption than an equivalent MXFP4/INT4 fault — and can cheap parity checks catch it?
  • Can QAT or a learned per-block mantissa split close the text-only perplexity gap (+1.0 ppl over NVFP4) without giving up the shift-and-add factorization?
  • Does the MiX-MX dual-format scheme extend to MoE VLMs, where expert routing adds another source of activation variance?
  • How far below 4 bits can KV-cache-only MiX quantization go for very long visual contexts before attention degrades?
