Why this matters

A rare physically executed test of LLM agents making real experimental decisions — direct method guidance for anyone building agents, self-driving labs, or ML-for-science systems.

Critical edge

Every campaign-level number rests on a single campaign per arm (n = 1), scored on an in-vitro FaSSIF endpoint — no replicate campaigns, no in-vivo data.

And the DoE baseline ran without conventional expert pre-screening, flattering that particular contrast.

— Critical Assessment · Limitations

01 · Methodology

Platform & endpoint. All arms ran on the same miniaturized robotic design–build–test platform: six batches of 16 SEDDS preconcentrates — 96 unique formulations — prepared by liquid-handling robots, assayed by automated dispersion, DLS droplet sizing, and HPLC quantification. Endpoint: two-stage biorelevant dissolution (gastric stage → FaSSIF transfer), scored as trapezoidal AUC10–240 of apparently solubilized paclitaxel against a pre-specified four-attribute TPP.

The agent. Andromeda 2 runs an LLM-agent loop — propose, critique, review, plan, constraint-check — grounded in the lab's structured history (prior compositions, dissolution/dispersion/size/PDI outcomes, excipient behaviour) and calls Andromeda 1's probabilistic surrogates as tools. Batches update its working context, with no retraining between batches. A deterministic feasibility engine enforces executable compositions (valid excipients, 0.05 grid, sum-to-one, no duplicates); a human scientist gates safety without redesigning.

Comparators & ablation. The deployed Andromeda 1 optimizer (starts de novo), a scientist-designed DoE executed on the same platform, plus simulated DoE strategies on a validated emulator (held-out Spearman 0.89). The ablation withholds historical evidence only. Statistics: Mann–Whitney U and bootstrap CIs, framed as descriptive.

Beyond the headline

  • Evidence ablation: withholding in-house history (which contained zero paclitaxel data) cuts mean AUC 62.0 → 46.2 (−34%) and hit rate 50% → 31%; the reduced-evidence arm kept pace early, then regressed — evidence improves interpretation of new measurements, not the starting batch.
  • Best formulation held apparently solubilized paclitaxel through 240 min at AUC ≈ 18× the unformulated API control; Andromeda 1's best peaked comparably but declined after 120 min, consistent with precipitation.
  • Not just region-finding: within the productive Type IIIB/IV region, hit rates were 49% vs 29% vs 3%; region-wide rates (II 0%, IIIA 6%, IIIB 35%, IV 32%) show the gain survives within-region comparison.
  • Loading context: a selected full-TPP formulation showed apparent effective loading of 19 ± 5% w/w — ~3.3× the 5.7% w/w of a published S-SEDDS; the authors explicitly warn against ranking across differing assays.
  • Statistics: formulation-level Mann–Whitney p = 9.6 × 10−8, reported as descriptive only due to adaptive within-campaign dependence.
03 · Strengths
  • Physically executed, budget-matched comparison against two credible baselines — a deployed industrial optimizer and a scientist-designed DoE — on the same robots and assay.
  • Genuinely controlled evidence ablation using an API absent from the history: tests knowledge transfer, not memorization.
  • Clean architecture separating agent (science), probabilistic tools (uncertainty), and deterministic feasibility engine (execution); human reviews safety only.
  • Unusually honest reporting culture: peak-AUC parity disclosed, statistics framed as descriptive, limitations enumerated without hedging.
  • Composition-space analysis (LFCS types, 16 vs 90 oil–surfactant families) explains how the budget was spent, not just the outcome.
04 · Limitations
  • One campaign per strategy — campaign-to-campaign variance unmeasured, so reproducibility of the 50% vs 17% gap is unknown.
  • FaSSIF AUC captures no digestion, permeability, first-pass metabolism, or in-vivo performance; no pharmacokinetic data.
  • DoE comparator lacked conventional expert pre-screening, advantaging the autonomous arms in that pairwise contrast.
  • Single, deliberately hard API (paclitaxel); generality across chemically distinct APIs and modalities is asserted as future work, not shown.
  • Proprietary LLMs and evidence corpus make independent verification impossible; system details (LLM scale, evidence schema) are thin.
  • The ablation shows evidence matters, but cannot split evidence grounding from agentic orchestration per se.
05 · Questions for further exploration
Q1

Does the 50% vs 17% hit-rate gap survive replicate campaigns — what is the campaign-to-campaign variance of each strategy?

Q2

How much of the advantage is evidence grounding versus agentic orchestration itself — would a non-agentic planner given the same history match Andromeda 2?

Q3

Does the approach transfer to chemically distinct APIs, other formulation modalities, and digestion-coupled or permeability endpoints?

Q4

Can the evidence corpus be characterized — its size, schema, and whether a scaling law links evidence volume to hit-rate improvement?

Q5

Vitamin E TPGS appeared in winners and inhibits P-gp efflux — do the identified formulations actually improve oral paclitaxel exposure in vivo?

Q6

Would replacing the proprietary in-house LLMs with open models preserve the advantage, making the recipe publicly reproducible?
