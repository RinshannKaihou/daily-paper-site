of the aggregate token-mismatch gap between eviction policies is pure post-divergence exposure — divergence timing, not divergence severity, is what separates policies.

SnapKV-512 vs SnapKV-50%: exposure contributes 17.17 of 19.90 pp (Llama) and 14.99 of 16.68 pp (Qwen). All 24 model–stratum–contrast decompositions are exposure-majority: 78.1–96.1%.

Why This Matters为何重要

Eviction design should optimize for delaying or avoiding first divergence — not betting on self-recovery; the branch-aligned protocol transfers to speculative-decoding consistency studies.

Methodology方法 · 三层结构

RSMC= OH+ EH× CH
O_H — occurrence: first-mismatch contribution
E_H — exposure: expected time after divergence
C_H ∈ [0,1] — mismatch rate while diverged

1 · Theory

Reference and evicted kernels are coupled stepwise by overlap–residual maximal coupling, so local disagreement equals local total variation. The first-divergence time τ then splits expected mismatch exactly into the identity above. Theorem 3.2: risks compatible with a finite depth-L divergence-aligned window form the sharp interval [R_L, R_L + B_L] — with minimax corollaries and a shared-reference action-ordering condition.

2 · Estimation — SWRB

Survival-Weighted Residual Branching integrates out the zero-loss full-agreement event (Rao–Blackwell style), jointly estimating occurrence, occupation, and window/tail terms from one replicate. Proposition 4.1 proves per-replicate variance dominance over naive coupling; validated on 24 enumerable systems (median 52.7× variance reduction, 20.0× variance–cost advantage).

3 · Language-model studies

Meta-Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct & Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct over six HELMET/RULER strata (4K/16K/32K), H=128, temperature-1 full-softmax. A 288-document exploratory study (K=64) compares SnapKV-512, SnapKV-50%, and byte-matched recent-token-50%; an independent 288-document confirmatory study at 90% retention uses eight prespecified endpoints (α = .05/8). Dev/eval documents disjoint; document-level stratified bootstrap.

All 36 stratum–model–action 95% lower bounds positive. Confirmatory 90%-retention study: pooled +6.03 to +8.27 pp, all four family-adjusted contrasts positive.

  • Post-divergence mismatch is uniformly high: C_H spans 89.74–93.68% (Llama) and 93.59–96.20% (Qwen) under every policy — once diverged, ~90%+ of tokens mismatch.
  • Two clocks disagree: at 90% retention, generation-clock selector gaps are negative in all HELMET strata (−14.34 to −25.84 pp) but positive in RULER (+0.27 to +7.84 pp); branch-clock contrasts stay positive everywhere — aggregate narrowing masks branch-level discrepancy.
  • Estimator efficiency: median 52.7× variance reduction (range 11.1–289×) at only 1.37× more kernel-pair evaluations.
  • Cheap monitoring: ridge predictors from generation positions 1–32 recover the population-level gap within the prespecified ±2-pp equivalence margin on both models.
  • Not an EOS artifact: EOS components explain only 0.86–3.68% of the Llama HELMET selector difference — discrepancy accrues before termination.

Strengths优势

  • One exact, interpretable identity (first mismatch + exposure × rate) that is directly measurable — rare conceptual clarity for a systems paper.
  • The sharp partial-identification theorem states precisely how much a finite window cannot know about cumulative risk.
  • SWRB carries a proven (not empirical-only) variance-dominance guarantee, checked against exactly-enumerable systems.
  • The two-clock design resolves the paradox of narrowing aggregate gaps alongside rising within-branch TV; the HELMET-vs-RULER sign flip is concrete and memorable.
  • Exceptional hygiene: prespecification, dev/eval separation, family-wise correction, byte-matched budgets, zero-TV full-retention controls, honest AI-use disclosure.

Limitations局限

  • Narrow coverage: two 7B/8B instruct models; SnapKV vs recent-token only — no H2O, RazorAttention, Ada-KV, or quantized caches (KIVI, Palu).
  • Distributional measures only: no accuracy, retrieval scores, or judged quality — the severity of 90% branch TV is left unquantified.
  • Short horizon: H=128, temperature-1, EOS absorbing; greedy, low-temperature, and long generations unexplored.
  • Confirmatory coverage is thin: prespecified study exists only at 90% retention; the practically interesting 50% regime is exploratory.
  • No code or seeds; SWRB needs full-vocabulary distributions and forced-path rollouts — a custom serving stack, nontrivial to reproduce.
  • C_H is coupling-specific and exposure-weighted — not a coupling-invariant quantity; translation to other couplings requires redoing the analysis.

Questions for Further Exploration值得追问的方向

  1. Does exposure dominance persist under greedy or low-temperature decoding, and over horizons beyond H=128?
  2. Can divergence timing rank other eviction families (H2O, RazorAttention, Ada-KV, CAKE) or quantized caches on entry-avoidance rather than aggregate mismatch?
  3. With post-divergence TV stuck near 90–96%, can any intervention — periodic KV restoration, re-prefill on drift, hybrid budgets — genuinely reduce C_H, or is divergence an absorbing failure state?
  4. How does branch-aligned TV map to task-level outcomes (HELMET accuracy, RULER retrieval)? A dose–response link would make the framework decision-relevant.
  5. How short can the observation window L be before the unidentified tail B_L becomes negligible under real LLM kernels, justifying short-window monitoring in production?
  6. Do speculative-decoding acceptance rates — also built on stepwise maximal-coupling arguments — admit the same occurrence / exposure / rate decomposition?
