> **一句话总结：** 把"英文拒绝、译文越狱"的多语言安全漂移问题改写成一个精确的线性代数对象：同义纤维内的拒绝裕度漂移恰是跨 Gram 矩阵泛函，并引入由杠杆对偶 χ²=1/ℓ−1 支配的内在暴露度 χ，把观测漂移确定为三种诊断（读出层可修复 / 病态不可用 / 表征层碰撞），在合成审计中 χ/ρ 以 Spearman 0.972 预测修复下限，且在一个真实多语言编码器（FLORES-200 翻译纤维）上诊断出病态体制——精确不变读出器在样本外有害，而验证集选出的正则化读出器在未见语言上降低 13% 漂移。

> **TL;DR:** This paper turns multilingual safety drift (a model refuses in English but complies in translation) into exact linear algebra on an audited equivalence class ("semantic fiber"): same-fiber margin drift is a cross-Gram functional of the within-fiber contrast, and a new intrinsic exposure χ (via the leverage duality χ²=1/ℓ−1) splits observed drift into a recalibratable reader fault, an exact-but-ill-conditioned fix, or an irreparable representation collision. On matched synthetic benchmarks (ρ=0.880 identical across three families) validation-selected readers cut held-out drift 71.0% vs 12.7% vs −0.0% exactly as the χ/||r_inv|| diagnosis predicts; across 66 geometries χ/ρ predicts the repair floor at Spearman 0.972; an order-swap residual predicts out-of-sample control-composition error at median Spearman 0.964 vs 0.269 for the static cross-Gram baseline. On a real multilingual encoder audited with 300 professionally translated FLORES-200 sentences (d=384, 4 audited + 3 held-out languages), the diagnosis flags the ill-conditioned regime: the exact invariant reader (norm 4.9) is harmful out of sample, while a validation-selected regularized reader (norm 1.07) cuts mean absolute drift from 0.213 to 0.186 (13%) on never-audited languages while keeping English-score correlation at 0.92.

## 核心贡献

- **漂移的精确刻画**：在声明的等价关系（语义纤维）、层、特征字典（writers W）、打分头（reader u）固定后，同纤维内拒绝裕度漂移 M(x)−M(x′) 恰等于跨 Gram 矩阵行作用于纤维内对比的线性泛函；裕度不变性等价于一个零化子（annihilator）条件 v ∈ C_s^⊥，最坏情形漂移是威胁集的支撑函数，可直接给出决策证书（首次接触边界 r* = m/h_A(−v)）。
- **三种预算、三个敏感度常数**：特征坐标球预算下的 ρ_{u,B}、隐藏态距离预算下的 κ_{u,B}（一个 ≤ ∥u∥ 的投影范数，必然饱和）、随机对比模型下的 σ_Δ；论文用数值审计证明三者不可互换——n/d=3.00 时 ρ 把 protected/random/spiked 三族分离为 0.000/0.899/3.464，而隐藏预算 κ 只给出 0.000/0.876/0.991。
- **内在暴露度 χ 与杠杆对偶**：χ_B = min{∥W_B^⊤ r∥ : r^⊤ w_s = 1}，满足 χ_B² = 1/ℓ_B − 1（ℓ_B 为安全 writer 相对干扰块的相对杠杆）；χ_B = 0 当且仅当 w_s ∉ span(W_B)，且精确不变读出器范数为 1/sin θ_{s,B}——可行性（χ）与稳定性（∥r_inv∥）被明确分离，构成"读者问题 / 病态问题 / 表征问题"三向分诊。
- **同暴露、反结论的反例**（命题 7）：两个几何的当前暴露同为 ρ = β√L，但一个 χ=0 完全可修复、另一个 χ=ρ 完全内在——证明黑盒红队测试能测到的行为不一致率与当前暴露都无法确定修复方向。
- **未绑定次序交换恒等式**（定理 8）：读方向与写方向分离（如 SAE encoder/decoder）的校准干预算子的交换子有精确闭式，其实测残差 R_ij 是线性控制接口的因果一致性检验，且在校准态上估计的残差能预测新状态、新目标上三控制复合误差（Spearman 0.964 vs 静态跨 Gram 基线 0.269）。
- **两个闭式修复 + 两个极限**：正则化最小方差读出器 r_λ（沿暴露-条件数前沿的闭式解）与带样本外转移界的最小改动投影 u_D；极限方面给出 reader 优化后总串扰的秩下界 n(n−r)/r（等杠杆框架取等，精化 Welch 界），并用列置换反例证明任何 Gram 谱层面的聚合（超叠水平、全局相干）都不能定位暴露所在。

## Key Contributions

- **Exact drift calculus**: Once the audited equivalence relation (semantic fiber), layer, feature dictionary (writers W), scoring head (reader u), threshold, and contrast model are declared, same-fiber refusal-margin drift is exactly a cross-Gram row applied to the within-fiber contrast; margin invariance is an annihilator condition, and worst admissible drift is a support function yielding decision certificates with a computable boundary-contact radius.
- **Three budget-specific sensitivity constants**: ρ_{u,B} (feature-coordinate norm), κ_{u,B} (hidden-state distance; a projection norm that saturates at ∥u∥ by identity), and σ_Δ (stochastic contrast scale) — shown numerically to be non-interchangeable and to answer different audit questions.
- **Intrinsic calibrated exposure via leverage duality**: χ_B² = 1/ℓ_B − 1 links classical statistical leverage to readout repairability; χ_B separates current (reader) exposure from representation-intrinsic exposure, and ∥r_inv∥ = 1/sin θ_{s,B} prices the noise amplification of the exact fix, giving a three-way triage (recalibrate / regularize / retrain the representation) computable before any repair is attempted.
- **A matched-exposure counterexample**: two geometries with identical tied exposure ρ = β√L and identical deployed scores can be fully removable (χ=0) or fully intrinsic (χ=ρ) — behavioral disagreement rates, the primary observable of black-box multilingual red-teaming, leave the verdict undetermined.
- **Untied order-swap identity as a causal-interface test**: for setters that read along r_i but write along w_j (SAE-style untied systems), the commutator has an exact cross-Gram closed form; its calibration-state residual localizes nonlinear/state-dependent leakage and predicts out-of-sample composition error (median within-system Spearman 0.964 vs 0.269 for the static cross-Gram magnitude baseline).
- **Two closed-form head-only repairs and two sharp limits**: a regularized minimum-variance reader r_λ tracing the exposure-conditioning frontier, and a least-change projection u_D with a held-out transfer bound; a rank floor n(n−r)/r on reader-optimized cross-talk (attained by equal-leverage frames, refining Welch/Benedetto–Fickus), plus a permutation non-identification theorem showing superposition level and global coherence leave block-exposure location completely free.
- **Trained-frame regime analysis**: in 24 toy overcomplete reconstruction models, the superposition level predicts adversarial vulnerability (R² = 0.82) only under uniform feature importance; with geometrically decaying importance the aggregate correlation collapses (pooled R² = 0.11) and important features get protected (interference at 0.248× the median feature's) — locating exactly when aggregate superposition-vulnerability correlations in the literature hold.

## Methodology

The paper works entirely within a declared local linear model: at a fixed layer, h(x) = W z(x) + ε(x) with unit-column writer matrix W (e.g., an SAE decoder), a reader system R (probes/heads, with cross-Gram C = R^⊤ W measuring read/write cross-talk), and a deployed safety head u with margin M(x) = u^⊤ h(x) − τ. Semantic fibers {x ~ x′} are audited equivalence classes (translations/paraphrases) fixed externally before model inspection; the contrast span C_s = span{z(x)−z(x′)} is the object on which everything is computed.

The analysis proceeds as a pipeline of propositions: (1) same-fiber drift equals the cross-Gram functional v^⊤Δz with invariance iff v annihilates C_s, with a 2η∥u∥ residual bound under model error; (2) budget-specific worst-case constants via Cauchy–Schwarz duality (Euclidean, PSD-quadratic, and hidden-state budgets), plus a Gaussian directed-failure prediction Φ(−2m₀/σ_Δ); (3) decision certificates as support functions of the admissible contrast set, robust to an estimated dictionary with an explicit 2η̂∥u∥ pullback residual; (4) the leverage duality theorem proving χ_B² = 1/ℓ_B − 1 with optimizer r* = S_e^† w_s/(w_s^⊤ S_e^† w_s), feasibility χ_B = 0 iff w_s ∉ span(W_B), and conditioning ∥r_inv∥ = 1/sin θ_{s,B}; (5) an explicit two-block construction with matched ρ but opposite χ; (6) the untied order-swap identity for calibrated setters T_i^a(h) = h + (a − r_i^⊤ h) w_j, with commutator (a−r_i^⊤h)C_ji w_j − (b−r_j^⊤h)C_ij w_j; (7) closed-form repairs: the ridge reader r_λ = (W_B W_B^⊤+λI)^{−1} w_s / (w_s^⊤(W_B W_B^⊤+λI)^{−1} w_s) and the least-change projection u_D = (I−P_{L_D})u with held-out bound |u_D^⊤ d| ≤ ∥u_D∥∥(I−P_{L_D})d∥; (8) global floors: reader-optimized off-diagonal cross-talk ≥ n(n−r)/r with equality iff equal leverage, and a permutation argument fixing the entire Gram spectrum while moving block exposure arbitrarily.

Empirically, everything is validated by deterministic synthetic audits (PCG64, CPU float64, eleven pipeline stages in fresh processes): matched benchmarks with 12 rotation seeds and 8,000 fibers per geometry (train/val/test 4000/2000/2000 split by fiber, d=64, n=192, |B|=48), 66 independently rotated geometries for out-of-sample repairability, untied reader-writer systems with deliberately injected nonlinear state-dependent leakage, 24 trained toy superposition models (n=80 features, d=20) under uniform vs decaying importance, and one real-encoder pilot: the mean-pooled final layer (d=384) of a public multilingual sentence encoder, 300 professionally translated FLORES-200 sentences as externally audited fibers (4 languages audited: arb, deu, fra, spa; 3 held back: hin, swh, zho), scored by a zero-shot inner product with a fixed English query direction. Readers are always estimated on training fibers, selected on validation under a pre-specified utility floor, and evaluated once on untouched test fibers.

## Results & Highlights

- **Matched-current-exposure benchmark (Table 1)**: three families with identical deployed scores (sample-level match to 2.22×10⁻¹⁵) and identical ρ = 0.880 but χ = 0.000/0.000/0.880 and ∥r_inv∥ = 1.35/91.59/n/a. Validation-selected readers reduce held-out drift by **71.0% in the stable-removable family vs only 12.7% in the ill-conditioned family and −0.0% in the intrinsic-collision family**, exactly as the diagnosis predicts; the exact invariant reader reaches 99.99% balanced accuracy in the stable family but **collapses to 52.16%** in the ill-conditioned one because its norm 91.59 amplifies endpoint noise (bound 2×0.10×91.59 ≈ 18, vs signal scale 0.78).
- **Out-of-sample repairability across 66 geometries**: the intrinsic fraction χ/ρ predicts the held-out residual-drift floor with **Spearman 0.972**, and on the removable subset log∥r_inv∥ predicts usable repair with **Spearman 0.889** — the two stages of the triage jointly explain the full residual decomposition.
- **Order-swap residual as an out-of-sample predictor**: across 8 untied reader-writer systems with injected nonlinear state-dependent setters, the calibration-state pairwise residual predicts the unexplained three-control composition error on new states/targets with **median within-system Spearman 0.964** (pooled 0.970 over 360 held-out triples), against **0.269** (pooled 0.298) for the static cross-Gram magnitude baseline.
- **Closed-form repairs on untouched test fibers**: empirical projection lowers the tied head's mean absolute drift from **1.394 to 0.008** (data-PCA truncation, rank 43.1; 0.000 with oracle block span) and the directed unsafe rate from **28.79% to 2.16%** (0.04% oracle); at fixed threshold τ=0.65 the rate falls from 33.15% to 0.00%. The invariant ridge (λ=3.00 selected on validation) cuts the frozen-test directed rate from **0.506% to 0.164%** (Wilson interval [0.084, 0.303]%) and mean absolute drift from 0.219 to 0.026.
- **Real multilingual encoder audit (FLORES-200, d=384)**: the audited contrast covariance (rank 221 at 99% energy) contains κ = 0.979 of the deployed score direction, so exact invariance exists in principle but the minimum-norm invariant reader (norm 4.9) is **harmful out of sample — its correlation with the original English score falls to 0.26**; the validation-selected regularized reader (λ = 3.16× mean eigenvalue, norm 1.07) lowers mean absolute drift from **0.122 to 0.114 on audited languages (7%) and from 0.213 to 0.186 on never-audited languages (13%)** while holding English-score correlation at 0.92 — the conditioning regime, correctly flagged by the triage.
- **Budget non-interchangeability (n/d = 3.00)**: feature-budget norms 0.000/0.899/3.464 for protected/random/spiked families vs hidden-budget 0.000/0.876/0.991; directed unsafe rates 0.000%/1.472%/30.721% track the drift scales σ_Δ = 0.000/0.318/1.083, not ρ, and the Gaussian prediction Φ(−2m₀/σ_Δ) undershoots (1.38% vs 1.472%; 25.91% vs 30.721%) because sparsity makes the drift a leptokurtic Gaussian scale mixture.
- **Trained-frame regime result**: under uniform importance, 19 toy superposition models sit on the tight-frame floor (tightness 1.06–1.22) and superposition level predicts attacked loss with **R² = 0.82**; under decaying importance tightness reaches 1.90, the most important feature's interference falls to **0.248× the median feature's**, and pooled over both families neither aggregate predicts vulnerability (**R² = 0.11 for level, 0.19 for worst-case interference**) — aggregate superposition-vulnerability correlations are a property of uniform importance, not a law.
- **Arithmetic saturation checks**: the tied order-swap composition matches the closed form to **4.41×10⁻¹⁶** over 48 feature pairs × 768 interventions; the exact drift identity holds to 2.66×10⁻¹⁵ without noise; block norms reconstructed purely from intervention order-dependence (1.939) match the static value (1.933), with signed Gram-entry recovery at correlation 0.9998.

The paper cites the relevant literature accurately and broadly: multilingual jailbreaks and red-teaming (Yong et al. 2024 on GPT-4 low-resource jailbreaks; Wang et al. 2024; Deng et al. 2024), refusal-direction causality (Arditi et al. 2024; Wang et al. 2025), cross-lingual safety signal attenuation (Oppong et al. 2026), steering transport (Stein et al. 2026), SAE/superposition foundations (Elhage et al. 2022; Bricken et al. 2023; Templeton et al. 2024), concept erasure (Ravfogel et al. 2022; Belrose et al. 2023 LEACE), frame theory (Welch 1974; Benedetto & Fickus 2003; Eldar's oblique dual frames), and causal abstraction (Geiger et al. 2025). Credibility is strengthened by an unusually strong reproducibility statement: a single deterministic command regenerates every number from seeded PCG64 generators, byte-for-byte artifact manifests with SHA-256, a regression suite covering each identity, and the real-encoder pilot isolated as an optional pinned-revision script. No external funding or conflicts statements are given, and the empirical burden rests on synthetic constructions — the real-model evidence is a single small pilot, which the authors state plainly.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: The exact cross-Gram/annihilator calculus itself is elementary linear algebra (Cauchy–Schwarz, projections, rank arguments) rather than new mathematics; the genuine novelty is the *assembly*: the leverage duality χ² = 1/ℓ−1 giving an operational safety meaning to frame-theoretic leverage scores, the feasibility/conditioning split (χ vs ∥r_inv∥ = 1/sin θ), and the untied order-swap residual as a predictive causal-interface diagnostic. The permutation non-identification theorem (12c) is a sharp, useful negative result directly aimed at aggregate superposition-vulnerability claims (Gorton & Lewis 2025).
- **Rigor**: High internal rigor — every identity is proved, and audits are deliberately built around out-of-sample consequences (train/val/test split by fiber; single frozen test evaluation; penalties selected on validation under a pre-specified utility constraint) rather than confirmations. Arithmetic saturation (4.41×10⁻¹⁶) and cross-code-path consistency (∥r_inv∥ = 1/√(1−κ²) agreeing to four significant figures) are honest self-checks.
- **Weak spots in evidence**: The near-total empirical weight is synthetic, where "language" is an abstract block of feature coordinates — no LLM refusal head, no harmful prompts, no jailbreak search is ever audited. The single real experiment uses a *benign* zero-shot similarity score on 300 FLORES-200 news sentences at one layer of one sentence encoder (d=384), so the tie to the motivating multilingual-jailbreak phenomenon is analogy through the linear model, not demonstration. On that real pilot the practical gain is modest (7% drift reduction on audited languages, 13% on held-out), and the intrinsic exposure χ_B, the feature-budget constant ρ, and the order-swap residual were *not* measured on the real model (only the covariance-form subset), so the paper's central three-way triage has never been exercised end-to-end on a deployed safety system.
- **Missing baselines**: The only comparisons are the deployed tied reader, the exact invariant reader, and the static cross-Gram magnitude predictor. Established repairs the paper itself cites — INLP/LEACE-style erasure, BabelSteering-style direction transport, domain-adversarial readouts — are argued against positionally but never run head-to-head on the same fibers.
- **Assumption load**: The local linear model h = Wz + ε, the single scalar safety writer w_s, and the block structure of "language" coordinates are all declared rather than tested (the scalar-writer choice is contested by Wollschläger et al.'s concept cones; the multi-output extension, eq. 16, is stated but never empirically exercised). The audited equivalence relation is external and, in deployment, expensive — the ethics statement concedes native reviewer involvement is needed.
- **Reproducibility**: Excellent — deterministic regeneration, pinned model revision, released code/manifests, regression suite.
- **Significance**: For interpretability practice, the reader/writer cross-Gram viewpoint and the matched-exposure counterexample (Prop 7) are valuable cautions for anyone reading behavioral red-team rates as repairability. For deployment, the significance is currently prospective: the triage is cheap and computable, but whether its verdicts change outcomes on real LLM safety stacks remains unshown.
- **Red flags**: Prose occasionally refers to propositions as "theorems" (e.g., "theorems 1 and 2" for Props 1–2), a numbering looseness; the statistical theory justifying reader estimation from finite audited pairs is deferred to a companion manuscript under review, so some out-of-sample claims lean on external work; and the real-encoder figure shows per-language drift for held-out languages whose selection rationale (hin, swh, zho) is not justified against alternatives.

## Strengths

- Converts a vague deployment symptom ("refuses in English, complies in translation") into closed-form quantities (ρ, κ, σ_Δ, χ, ∥r_inv∥, order-swap residual) computable from tensors a response team already holds, before any reader is trained.
- The feasibility/conditioning separation is the paper's best idea: it explains *why* an algebraically perfect fix (exact invariant reader, 99.99% BAcc stable) can be operationally harmful (52.16% BAcc ill-conditioned) with a single number, 1/sin θ_{s,B}.
- Evaluation discipline is exemplary for a theory paper: out-of-sample tasks the predictor was never fitted on, validation-selected operating points, one frozen test evaluation, and matched-exposure constructions that isolate geometry as the only free variable.
- The non-identification theorem plus the trained-frame experiment jointly locate when aggregate superposition-vulnerability correlations hold (uniform importance) and when they silently fail — a genuinely useful negative result for the interpretability literature.
- Byte-reproducible pipeline with SHA-256 manifests and a regression suite; ethics and scope statements are candid about what is synthetic and what is real.

## Limitations

- Almost all quantitative validation is on synthetic geometry where dictionaries, heads, and contrasts are fixed by construction; the one real-model audit covers only the covariance form on a single layer, single benign score, 300 FLORES-200 sentences, and 7 languages.
- No head-to-head comparison with existing multilingual-safety or erasure baselines (LEACE, INLP, steering transport); the "baselines" are the deployed reader and the static cross-Gram summary.
- Real-encoder repair gains are small in absolute terms (mean |drift| 0.122→0.114 audited, 0.213→0.186 held-out), and the exact-invariant failure it demonstrates is an estimated-annihilator pathology whose statistical treatment lives in a companion paper not included here.
- The linear local model, scalar safety writer, and given block structure are assumptions; the cone-valued (multi-output) extension is provided in closed form but untested.
- The audit presupposes an externally audited equivalence relation (translation fibers), which is itself the costly, governance-heavy step the paper brackets out; professional translation quality and language choice (which languages are audited vs held out) directly determine the measured geometry.
- Notation is dense (twelve numbered results, three sensitivity constants, two reader systems, tied/untied duals); the paper is hard to read without the frame-theory background it cites.

## Relevance

与"LLM Hidden States & Interpretability"方向高度契合：论文本质上是关于 SAE 式 writer/reader 几何、超叠（superposition）与表征空间中方向性读出的闭式分析，其跨 Gram 串扰、杠杆对偶与次序交换残差为探针与干预的可靠性提供了可直接复用的测量工具。虽然动机是安全漂移，但其方法论（表征几何诊断 + 读出侧修复的可行性/条件数分解）对任何基于隐藏状态的线性探针与 steering 研究都适用。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would the three-way triage (χ, ∥r_inv∥) reproduce on a production LLM refusal head with genuinely harmful-prompt translation fibers, where the contrast span is estimated from far fewer audited pairs than the synthetic 4,000-fiber training sets?
- What does the layer-resolved χ_ℓ profile look like across a real multilingual LLM's depth — is there a practical "head-side repair window," and does it coincide with the layers chosen heuristically by multilingual steering work (e.g., Al Ghussin et al. 2026)?
- Do existing repairs — BabelSteering-style direction transport, LEACE/INLP erasure — land in the regime the calculus predicts for them, and can the order-swap residual explain documented steering unreliability (Tan et al. 2024) better than steering-vector geometry alone?
- Does the reader-norm noise amplification bound 2η∥r_inv∥ interact with low-precision inference: under int8/fp8 quantization noise, does the ill-conditioned regime become the dominant failure mode even for geometries that are well-conditioned in float64?
- Can the equal-leverage rank floor n(n−r)/r be used constructively — e.g., as a regularizer or dictionary-design objective for SAE training that allocates leverage to safety-relevant features deliberately?
