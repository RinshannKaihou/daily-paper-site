Key Finding · arXiv 2609.15161

A medical multi-agent system that improves by mining its own successes and failures into a reusable experience library — no retraining needed.

外挂三层经验库（诊断原则 / 推理模式 / 代表病例），从自身推理轨迹中自动挖掘「黄金经验」与「失败警告」，推理时检索复用——无需更新模型参数。

Why This Matters

Turns test-time experience into a plug-in, model-agnostic library — and its ablations reveal which abstraction levels actually generalize.

直接命中「多智能体 + 自我进化 + 经验记忆」：把 test-time 经验积累做成外挂式、模型无关的知识库，系统验证了哪些抽象层级真正带来泛化。

Critical Edge

Mining and evaluation use different halves of the same dataset — effectively a same-distribution train/test split, so headline gains may be dataset-specific heuristics, not general clinical wisdom.

Cross-dataset transfer (+3.2/+3.5) is much smaller than same-dataset gains. No error bars despite many sub-1-point deltas.

How It Works

⟲Every run leaves a trajectory, labeled success/failure against ground truth → distilled into golden & warning experiences → stored in a three-level library → retrieved top-3 and injected into all agent prompts.

The experience library has three levels: principles (30–50-word clinical rules), patterns (reusable decision templates), and cases (concrete exemplars) — balancing transfer against clinical grounding. Growth stays bounded: each new entry is embedded with bge-m3 and added only if its max cosine similarity to existing entries is below θ = 0.85; otherwise an LLM merges or discards it.

At inference, retrieval runs hierarchically (principles → patterns → cases, top-k with k=3) into planner, department, and summary prompts. Mining ran 5 epochs on GPT-4o and Qwen3-8B; results are averaged over 3 runs. No model parameters are ever updated.

Strengths

WHAT HOLDS
  • Clean decomposition of self-evolution into mining, hierarchical storage, bounded update, retrieval — each step ablated separately.
  • Learning from failures is principled and validated: warning experiences add +2.6 over golden-only.
  • Strong transfer: cross-backbone (+5.0 to +10.0), cross-dataset, and cross-architecture into MDAgents / RECONCILE.
  • Efficiency-conscious: 1,200 tokens / 15 s per sample — cheaper than every multi-agent baseline compared.
  • Honest risk section: noise propagation, offline-only validation, privacy, explicit "not clinical advice" framing.

Limitations

WHAT DOESN'T
  • Text-only medical MCQs — no imaging, labs, or EHR; no real deployment or human-in-the-loop evaluation.
  • Experience quality is bounded by the LLM's own reasoning; hallucinated trajectories enter the library with only similarity merging as a filter.
  • Purely semantic-similarity retrieval may miss causal structure; library scaling is unaddressed empirically.
  • Half-dataset mining splits leave same-distribution leakage concerns; near-saturated MedQA limits interpretability.
  • ~6× token cost and 3× latency vs single-LLM CoT — matters for constrained deployment.

Open Questions

FOR FURTHER EXPLORATION
  1. How much same-dataset gain survives genuinely different mining/evaluation distributions?
  2. Can mined experiences be verified against clinical guidelines before entering the library?
  3. How do retrieval latency and library quality scale at 10–100× size? Would learned retrieval beat cosine?
  4. Does golden/warning asymmetry generalize to delayed, graded feedback (treatment outcomes) vs instant MCQ labels?
  5. Does the principle/pattern/case hierarchy help non-medical multi-agent domains — code, web agents?
