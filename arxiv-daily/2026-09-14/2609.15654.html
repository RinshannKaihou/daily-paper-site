Empathy Is Steerable but Multi-Axial: Mechanism Geometry and Persona Effects in LLMs

JuHeon Ha · Byounghan Lee · Yunseo Choi · Kyung-Ah Sohn — Ajou University, Dept. of Artificial Intelligence

Key metric — persona vs. mechanism subspace

2.6–3.1%

of the persona-induced squared activation shift at layer 15 is captured by the ER/IP/EX subspace — vs. <0.1% for a random 3-D subspace. Single-run point estimates, n=200/persona.

Why this matters

Measured dimensions are not independent control axes — any single-direction steering for behavior control or debiasing misses most of what actually moves.

⚠ Critical edge

All headline numbers rest on automated proxies — EPITOME classifiers plus a GPT-5-mini judge sharing the same rubric, with no human ratings; EX/IP classifiers act as 0/2 binaries, possibly inflating magnitudes.

01 Methodology — distilled

EPITOME pool (100 hi / 100 lo per mechanism) → teacher-forced extraction at final response token → v = mean(P) − mean(N) (CAA) → forward-hook injection h ← h + α·v

Contrastive activation steering of the three EPITOME empathy mechanisms — Emotional Reactions (ER), Interpretations (IP), Explorations (EX) — in Llama-3.1-8B-, Qwen2.5-7B-, and Mistral-7B-Instruct. High/low-empathy seeker–response pairs come from filtered Reddit peer-support dialogues (Sharma et al., 2020; ≥100 chars, self-harm posts removed).

A layer sweep ℓ = 3…31 × strength α ∈ {−1, 0, +1, +2} is scored by EPITOME classifiers on n = 200 seekers per cell (temp 0.8, top-p 0.9). Controls include a GPT-5-mini judge with the original rubrics, residualization, exact-matched extraction pools, multi-seed bootstrap CIs (B = 10,000, Holm-corrected), OOD transfer to EmpatheticDialogues, and WikiText-103 perplexity.

Credibility: code released (GitHub) · dual evaluators · unusually thorough controls for the genre · IITP/NRF funded, no industrial COI.

Largest persona projection shift only |ΔpER| = 0.055; top-5 residual PCs explain 27–30% of residual variance — structure remains, but outside the recovered basis.

  • Residualized vectors still steer: ER retains 131.3% of its effect, EX 84.2%, IP 50.0% — shared structure matters beyond its geometric size.
  • Exact-matched pools: target steering survives (+0.610 [0.460, 0.760]) while ER–IP coupling weakens (−0.395 → −0.124) — part of the spillover is a data artifact.
  • OOD transfer (EmpatheticDialogues): direction preserved in every cell; the negative direction transfers more uniformly (−0.250 at α=−1 vs. +0.020 at α=+1, Llama).
  • Cost: WikiText-103 perplexity 8.04 → 10.13 under EX α=+2.
  • Prompting cannot isolate mechanisms: EX-targeted prompt P3 lifts Llama EX 0.120 → 1.220 but shortens responses (123.7 → 93.6 tokens) and lowers Qwen ER (1.365 → 0.895).
  • Race-conditioned check: vER at α=+1 raises ER for all personas (1.14→1.26, 1.11→1.28, 1.03→1.08) yet does not close the gap. All 18 Holm-corrected bootstrap CIs exclude zero (Llama ℓ=15 only).

03 Strengths

  • Layer 15 is a genuinely cross-model intervention point — positive target effect in all 9 cells at α=+1, validated by a second evaluator, OOD transfer, and seed checks.
  • Two well-executed negative results: entangled mechanism geometry (anti-aligned IP directions, spillovers) and persona shifts lying almost entirely outside the recovered subspace with low-rank residual structure.
  • Exceptionally honest framing — repeatedly separates “steerable” from “selectively controllable,” flags classifier bimodality, and warns against single-vector debiasing deployment.
  • The exact-matched-pool control quantifying extraction-label imbalance is a methodological contribution other steering papers should adopt.

04 Limitations

  • Entirely proxy-based evaluation — no human annotation; nothing establishes changes in perceived empathic support.
  • Classifier artifacts: EX/IP behave as effective 0/2 binaries, possibly inflating magnitudes; response-style confounds remain possible for EX.
  • Robustness is Llama-centric — multi-seed and bootstrap machinery confined to Llama layer 15; Qwen/Mistral rest on single runs; persona projections are single-run estimates.
  • Narrow persona set — 11 US-centric personas, 2 controls; no intersectional coverage, no population-level fairness claims possible.
  • Static layer-15 directions only — adaptive, multi-vector, or persona-conditioned controllers untested; final-layer instability explanation hypothesized, not verified (no logit-lens).

05 Relevance · 相关性

与「LLM Hidden States & Interpretability」方向高度相关：以激活转向作为表征结构的因果探针，研究共情机制向量在残差流中的几何关系，正是表征几何与机制可解释性的核心问题。其「评测维度 ≠ 独立干预轴」的结论，对任何试图用单一方向向量控制高阶行为（含去偏）的研究都是方法论警示。

06 Questions for further exploration

  1. Would sparse autoencoder (SAE) features decompose the ~97% residual persona shift better than the contrastive CAA basis — can persona-specific directions be recovered from the low-rank residual PCs?
  2. Does ER–IP anti-alignment persist in larger models or multi-turn emotional-support dialogues, where mechanism expression may span turns?
  3. Can a learned multi-vector or persona-conditioned controller close the race-conditioned ER gap that single-vector steering fails to equalize?
  4. Do human raters perceive α-steered responses as more empathic, and does the EX classifier-bimodality artifact change the effect ranking under human annotation?
  5. Is the layer-15 optimum a general middle-layer property, or partly an artifact of last-response-token extraction (would mean-pooling shift the stable layer)?
  6. How much spillover is token-level competition (question-form EX tokens displacing ER acknowledgment) vs. genuine shared representation — can logit-lens or attention analysis separate the two?
