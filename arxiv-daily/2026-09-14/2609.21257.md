> **一句话总结：** Meta 提出 EvoPilot——一个人类把关的长期在线 autoresearch 框架，用"确定性验证器 + 人工评审"来判定两次实验运行是否构成可采纳的对照比较（而非仅相信 LLM 自报结果）；它在 37 天的 Video Deep Dive 检索系统战役中，识破了一次把 22 个百分点离线跌幅错误归因于 interaction head 的无效比较（实为对照臂 K_eff=3000 vs 处理臂 K_eff=600 的评估缺陷），修复后匹配谱系消融测得离线点击命中率 +3.20 个百分点，线上 7 天 A/B 实验给出 GSRR 相对提升 +0.66%。

> **TL;DR:** EvoPilot (Meta, KDD'27) is a human-gated protocol for long-horizon online autoresearch on the Video Deep Dive two-tower retrieval system (hourly-refreshed index of hundreds of millions of videos): role-scoped agents execute variants, but only a deterministic fail-closed verifier plus human review can admit a control–treatment comparison. It overturned an invalid primitive-autoresearch conclusion (a −22 pp offline hit-rate drop blamed on the interaction head, actually a control K_eff=3000 vs treatment K_eff=600 output-depth defect), and after repair a matched-lineage ablation on offline replay showed +3.20 pp click hit-rate over the head-disabled two-tower baseline, with a separate 7-day randomized online A/B confirming +0.66% relative GSRR (statistically significant, tens of millions of accounts per arm).

## 核心贡献

- **提出"完成 ≠ 有效"的长期在线 autoresearch 协议**：明确区分"工作流跑完"与"构成可解释的对照比较"。协议记录共享基线、声明的处理差异、数据快照、评估器、指标与停止规则，使得数小时级的实验结果在数天后仍可被正确解释。
- **给出可运行的系统 EvoPilot**：六类受限 agent 角色（规划器、基线构建器、实验 worker、结果分析师、故障诊断/修复、周期监控）通过版本化领域技能与类型化适配器执行；编排器与验证器是确定性程序而非 agent；证据采纳公式为 A(c,t;R) = Verify ∧ 人工评审，默认失败即关闭（fail-closed）。
- **"记忆仅供参考，只有可执行检查才有约束力"**：事件账本、程序记忆、含 38 条记录的故障目录跨轮次保存上下文，但任何教训只有被编码为对工件/状态转移的可执行守卫后才具强制力。
- **纵向实地研究**：37 天、7 个方向、20 个人工批准轮次的 VDD 战役清单；配对重放/变异测试逐项检验 10 项守卫；另用独立随机 A/B 实验确立所选模型的下游影响。
- **把真实故障转化为守卫的案例**：人为门控验证发现了历史输出深度不匹配（双方声明 K=600，实际 K_eff 为 3000 vs 600）、评估器版本漂移、训练/评估数据泄漏、no-op 代码编辑等失效模式，并各自催生了新的可执行检查。

## Key Contributions

- A protocol for long-horizon online autoresearch that distinguishes a completed workflow from a valid comparison: every round records the shared base, authorized treatment difference, data pins, evaluator revision, metric, and closure rule, so an hours-long result can still be interpreted days later.
- EvoPilot, a working realization: scoped agent roles with typed inputs/outputs, a versioned model-based-retrieval (MBR) domain skill, a typed execution adapter, isolated variants, persistent evidence/memory, fail-closed comparison admission (A(c,t;R) = Verify_R(c,t) ∧ Review_R(c,t)), and a process that turns incidents into executable guards. The orchestrator and verifier are deterministic programs, not LLM agents.
- The principle "memory is advisory, only executable checks enforce": stored lessons guide agents but acquire binding force only after being encoded as checks over artifacts or state transitions; agent self-reports and proposals are never execution authority or scientific evidence.
- A longitudinal field study: a 37-day campaign inventory over 7 VDD research directions and 20 human-approved rounds (68 configurations with reported metrics), with an outcome-independent record-completeness screen isolating 9 fully auditable rounds, plus paired replay/mutation tests of 10 safeguards and a separate randomized online A/B.
- A documented failure-to-guard loop: the historical funnel mismatch (both arms declared K=600 while realizing K_eff=3000 vs 600), evaluator drift across a metric-aggregation change, train/eval date leakage, and a lineage-rewriting no-op edit each led to a new deterministic check.

## Methodology

EvoPilot treats an online experiment as a protocol rather than a prompt. A human-approved round protocol R names the common base, data pins, required terminal artifacts, metric, closure rule, and one or more authorized contrasts (each contrast = control arm + treatment arm + the single allowed difference). After approval, a base builder materializes the shared source/data/configuration; each variant inherits the base and adds only its declared treatment; isolated workers execute each variant through the versioned MBR domain skill and the typed adapter, emitting terminal results plus execution traces. Once both arms terminate, the deterministic verifier Verify_R checks pair-level eligibility (both arms succeeded, artifact-extracted quantities match the spec, the realized difference equals the authorized treatment, non-treatment stages share equivalence keys), and a human review of metric semantics completes admission. Rejected predicates are persisted and their measurements cannot enter the evidence ledger.

The target system is Video Deep Dive (VDD), a follow-on video discovery experience formalized as a request triple (seed video s, machine-generated pivot query q, user context u). The MBR pipeline is a two-tower dense retriever (normalized dot-product ANN over an hourly-refreshed index of hundreds of millions of videos, trained with out-of-session in-batch InfoNCE, Eq. 2) followed by an optional non-linear interaction head G(h_r, h_d, a(d)) with weighted BCE auxiliary losses that re-scores the top-P candidates; P controls candidates entering the head while K is the output depth. Agents are Claude Opus 4.7/4.8 for planning/modification/synthesis/repair and Claude Haiku for bounded read-only monitoring. Durable state spans three scopes: a run ledger (typed events, atomic state), program memory (prior plans/results), and an incident catalog (38 records, six categories). Artifact reuse is itself a validity control: an equivalence key over checkpoint id, code diff, pinned data, and publication settings lets equivalent variants share one publication. Evaluation of the method has four prongs: (1) paired replay/mutation tests where each of 10 invariants is exercised by one clean fixture and one one-field-mutated fault; (2) execution records measuring terminal coverage, recovery, and reuse over the auditable cohort; (3) the longitudinal case study of the interaction-head direction; (4) a separate 7-day randomized online A/B (account-level randomization and analysis, tens of millions of accounts per arm, regression-adjusted mean using a separate 7-day pre-period) on the VDD slice of Good Search Result Rate for Retention (GSRR, Eq. 5).

## Results & Highlights

- **Overturning an invalid conclusion:** the primitive autoresearch attempt reported a −22 pp offline click hit-rate drop on VDD replay and attributed it to the interaction head; EvoPilot's human-gated verification showed both arms declared (P, K)=(3000, 600) but the control realized K_eff=3000 while the treatment realized K_eff=600. An extreme-K falsification probe (setting K_decl from 600 to 1 moved the two-tower-only hit rate by only ~0.5 pp pre-repair, and near-zero post-repair) confirmed and then validated the fix.
- **Matched offline ablation:** after the repair, a matched-lineage comparison with K=3000 and the trained checkpoint fixed (control: P=3000, head off; treatment: P=6000, head on) measured an offline click hit-rate improvement of +3.20 percentage points over the head-disabled two-tower baseline on VDD offline replay. A separate diagnostic re-test at (P, K)=(3000, 600) measured +4.8 pp, but it also changed the base model, so it estimates a bundled configuration effect, not the head-only effect.
- **Online A/B:** a 7-day randomized experiment with tens of millions of accounts per arm estimated a +0.66% relative increase in the VDD slice of GSRR versus control, statistically significant (regression-adjusted, separate 7-day pre-period); the interaction-head treatment then became the default VDD MBR online configuration.
- **Safeguard testing:** all 10 clean fixtures were admitted and all 10 corresponding one-field mutations were blocked (stale approval, absent code diff, wrong effective setting, one-date drift, incompatible reuse, leakage, failed arm, control realizing (3000, 3000), undeclared P difference, cross-revision evaluator pair).
- **Operational results on the auditable cohort (9/20 record-complete rounds):** 29/29 workflows closed with terminal records (27 scientific results, 2 OOM failures out of 22 training attempts); 71 logical stages (22 train, 22 publish, 27 evaluate); ~97 h summed orchestrator wall-clock; 24 agent retries after local fixes, 6 operator repairs, 6 subworkflows and 1 round recovered after interruption, 1 escalation; artifact reuse let 2 publications serve 7 evaluations, avoiding 5 publications and ~5 GPU-hours.
- **Direction-steering evidence:** removing an unsupervised score term changed click H@3000 by −0.17 pp (within the ±0.36 pp repeat-publication operational tolerance band, so the simpler click-only blend was selected); adding an engagement score reduced click hit-rate by 1.53 pp; results declined beyond 6k candidates (depth fixed at 6k); four in-session-negative weights changed click hit-rate by only [−0.36, +0.07] pp while the nominal best reduced an auxiliary slice by 1.23 pp — admitted results that stopped the additive-score and loss directions.

## Authors & Credibility

All five authors (Hao Fu, Baiting Zhu, Minglei Chen, Yinjie Huang, Shuai Ding) are at Meta Platforms, Inc. (Menlo Park), giving them direct access to the production VDD system, its GPU serving platform, and real traffic — an unusually strong setting for a field study of agentic model development. The paper is conditionally accepted at KDD '27. Credibility is bolstered by unusual epistemic discipline: the authors explicitly refuse to credit a control with preventing the incident that motivated it (post-study hardening is evaluated only by replay), state that the online A/B "is not evidence for EvoPilot's effectiveness" but only for the selected model, and separate scientific results, invalid measurements, and operational failures so an execution failure cannot be reported as negative model evidence.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** The individual ingredients (experiment tracking à la MLflow/TFX, harness engineering, role-scoped agents, procedural skill libraries) are known; the novelty lies in composing them into fail-closed, pair-level comparison admission for the hours-to-weeks online regime. This is a solid systems/harness contribution, not a new model or learning algorithm — the VDD architecture itself is explicitly unchanged.
- **Rigor:** The mutation-test protocol is clean (10/10 clean admitted, 10/10 faults blocked, no LLM judgment in the guards), and the distinction between declared and effective configuration is a genuinely valuable methodological point. However, only 10 paired fixtures are tested — there is no measurement of field recall or false-block rate, which the authors concede. The headline offline numbers (+3.20 pp, −22 pp) are descriptive point estimates with no inferential confidence intervals; the ±0.36 pp "band" is an empirical operational tolerance from repeat publication, not sampling uncertainty, yet decisions (e.g., selecting the click-only blend at −0.17 pp) hinge on it.
- **Reproducibility:** Low. Everything runs on internal Meta infrastructure (SilverTorch GPU platform, proprietary VDD data, internal serving/publishing systems) with no open-source release; the "porting" section lists required components but no artifact. Agent stack details (Claude Opus 4.7/4.8, Haiku, role definitions, prompt hashes) are described but the domain skill and adapter are not published. External replication is effectively impossible.
- **Significance:** The central empirical claim — that EvoPilot's verification rescued a falsely negative research decision — rests on a single retained incident (the primitive autoresearch attempt), not a concurrent randomized comparison of EvoPilot vs. primitive agents vs. manual experts; the paper admits this. Operational statistics are computed over 9/20 record-complete rounds (11 rounds excluded for missing stable-schema fields), an honest but nontrivial filtering. The online A/B lacks guardrail outcomes ("not available in the retained dataset") and covers only the US/Canada VDD-pivot slice.
- **Red flags:** The +4.8 pp "diagnostic re-test" confounds the head with a base-model change (correctly flagged by the authors but easy to over-read); the seven research directions share infrastructure and model lineage, so they are not independent replicates; the 0.66% relative online lift is modest and the paper appropriately does not claim agent-level speedup or cost-benefit (no token cost or GPU occupancy extrapolation). Conversely, the paper's own honesty about these limits is a credibility point in its favor.

## Strengths

- Reframes the unit of scientific evidence from "a completed run" to "an admitted control–treatment comparison," and enforces it mechanically rather than through prompting.
- The declared-vs-effective configuration distinction (P_decl/K_decl vs P_eff/K_eff, evaluator identity, provenance) catches a class of silent invalidity that neither run trackers nor standard ML pipelines detect, and it was validated on a real production incident with a falsification probe (extreme-K test) before and after repair.
- Clean separation of scientific results, invalid measurements, and operational failures prevents execution failures from being misread as negative model evidence.
- Human-gated at the right granularity (per-round approval, metric semantics, exceptions, stopping) while agents do the mechanical work; approval for round n cannot authorize round n+1.
- Longitudinal, production-scale evidence (37 days, hundreds of millions of videos, real A/B with tens of millions of accounts per arm) rather than a toy benchmark, plus auditable control evolution (Table 3 records when each control was introduced).

## Limitations

- Single-company, single-system case study (Meta VDD); no cross-organization replication, and no public code, data, or skill artifacts.
- The primitive-autoresearch baseline is a retained incident rather than a controlled head-to-head comparison, so the claim that EvoPilot beats ungated agentic loops is supported by one documented case, not an experiment.
- Only 9 of 20 rounds have complete operational records; invocation/token-cost and GPU-occupancy figures exist for just two archived directories and are not extrapolated.
- Ten mutation fixtures cannot establish real-world recall or false-block rates of the admission guards; the automatic identity verifier was added post-study and has only replay evidence.
- Offline replay readings are descriptive point estimates without confidence intervals; the ±0.36 pp tolerance band is operational variation, not statistical uncertainty.
- Evaluator semantics beyond recorded software revisions still require human review — the fail-closed machinery does not fully automate metric-meaning drift; online guardrail metrics were unavailable in the retained A/B dataset.

## Relevance

直接命中"Self-Evolving Agents & Automated Research（最高优先级）"方向：这是对长时程 autoresearch / 自主实验循环可靠性与可信性的研究，核心论点（agent 自报不可信，必须用确定性工件验证收口）正是自主科研系统走向实用所需的信任机制。对 LLM 训练可观测性兴趣也有呼应（no-op 变更、数据泄漏、评估器语义漂移等静默失效的检测）。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- What are the field recall and false-block rates of the admission guards at scale — how often does fail-closed verification reject valid comparisons (wasted GPU-hours) or admit subtle invalidity that the 10 invariants do not model?
- Could the human review step (metric-semantics adjudication, exceptions) be partially automated without reintroducing the trust problem the paper is designed to eliminate?
- How does EvoPilot's evidence throughput compare quantitatively against expert-led manual experimentation or an ungated agentic loop under a concurrent randomized protocol, rather than a single retained incident?
- Do the durable-memory mechanisms (advisory incident catalog vs executable guards) change agent behavior measurably, i.e., is there an ablation for memory on/off?
- Can the declared-vs-effective configuration verification be generalized to other multi-stage ML workflows (e.g., LLM training pipelines) where silent no-op edits, data-window leakage, and evaluator drift are equally plausible?
