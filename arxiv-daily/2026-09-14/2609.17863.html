Why this matters为何重要

Aggressive low-precision inference can corrupt outputs silently while throughput stays healthy. Quality-aware benchmarking is the only tripwire that catches it before deployment.

低精度激进优化会引发静默正确性坍塌——纯速度指标无法察觉。

Critical edge批判视角

The 0/200 collapse comes from the naive, uncalibrated KV path — a strawman. Calibrated methods (KIVI, KVQuant, TurboQuant, PyramidKV) exist precisely to avoid it; don't over-generalize to "KV quantization is broken."

Likewise, "speculation adds nothing" really means "n-gram prompt-lookup adds nothing on a 512-in/128-out math workload" — EAGLE-3 was never anchored.

— Critical Assessment · FP8-KV strawman risk

01

Methodology方法

One model, three GPUs, four steps: place every optimization on common axes, measure anchors, calibrate a simulator, compute the frontier.

S1

Axes

Every configuration scored on quality (GSM8K accuracy / agreement with FP16), latency (TTFT & TPOT at P50/P99) and cost ($ per Mtok from measured throughput × GPU hourly price). Quality is always co-reported — nothing wins by silently degrading answers.

S2

Anchors

54 configurations measured: Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct on vLLM 0.12, 512-in / 128-out, across RunPod L4 ($0.39/hr), A100 80GB PCIe ($1.39/hr), H100 PCIe ($2.89/hr) — three campaigns, total spend $18.

S3

Calibrate

Anchors fit a Vidur-style profiled simulator that fills the combinatorial space. All 54 anchors reproduced at anchored batch sizes; cross-campaign drift <1.5% is the honest generalization bound; simulated points carry error bars and are marked on every frontier.

S4

Frontier + atlas

Pareto frontier over (quality, latency, cost) per model/workload; the dominant configuration is named under tight-latency, high-throughput and low-cost constraints.

Scope caveats — NSA sparse attention is simulated only; draft-model speculation (EAGLE-3) cannot run on vLLM 0.12's V1 engine; of four protocol workloads (chat, long-context, code, math), only math is measured.

03

Strengths优势

  • Comparability is the contribution. Isolated speedup numbers are incomparable; dominance under a binding constraint is the decision-relevant question — answered regime by regime.
  • Quality always co-measured with speed — demonstrated by a configuration that looks healthy on throughput and scores 0/200.
  • Honest epistemics: measured anchors vs simulated points marked on every figure, error bars on simulated points, a candid six-item limitations list.
  • Frugal and reproducible: $18 on public cloud GPUs with a fully specified protocol — re-runs after price or kernel changes are practical.
  • Strict-vs-flexible extraction localizes AWQ's loss to formatting, not arithmetic — a reusable deployment diagnostic.
04

Limitations局限

  • n = 1 everywhere: one model, one engine, one workload shape (512-in/128-out), one quality task (GSM8K, n=200, ±3 pp). Chat, long-context and code workloads are listed but unmeasured — the grid structurally underrates KV compression.
  • 18/36 non-dominated says the grid is small — Pareto membership is a weak signal at this scale.
  • Sparsity rests on simulation alone (no stock-vLLM NSA implementation was measured).
  • Speculation is its weakest variant: n-gram PLD; EAGLE-3 could not be anchored at all.
  • Calibration is interpolative by construction; quality is never simulated — frontier cells mix measured quality with simulated latency, and the asymmetry is not propagated.
  • July-2026 price/kernel coordinates will drift; only the dominance structure and protocol are claimed to transfer.
05

Questions for further exploration待探索

  1. Would scale-calibrated FP8 KV (KIVI / KVQuant / TurboQuant-style) survive GSM8K and reclaim frontier seats — especially on the unmeasured long-context workloads (LongBench/RULER, 8k–128k) where KV compression matters most?
  2. Does the AWQ formatting-vs-arithmetic split replicate across models (Llama-3, Mistral) and tasks with different answer conventions (HumanEval, ShareGPT)?
  3. Would a real draft-model speculator (an EAGLE-3 head, or SGLang) overturn the parity verdict where n-gram PLD has no prompt overlap to exploit?
  4. Can the calibration be validated held-out (leave-one-batch-curve-out) rather than relying on cross-campaign drift alone?
  5. Does FP8-weights-as-workhorse persist at other scales (1.5B–70B) and under disaggregated prefill/decode serving?
  6. Could the atlas gain a fourth, reliability-oriented axis — silent output-corruption rate — so quality floors and SDC detection are optimized jointly with cost and latency?
