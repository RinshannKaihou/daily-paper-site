> **一句话总结：** 在一个已知答案出处的合成金融"数据室"基准（room02，41 题）上，把证据从"干净挂载"埋进"全量噪声文档"后，六大前沿旗舰模型准确率下降 4.1–17.8 个百分点、单题正确成本上涨最高 7 倍、d5 深链准确率最低跌至 38%，且 72% 的错误答案仍以 ≥80 的置信度交付——作者据此主张用"可核验的 claim 级回执（receipts）"取代置信度与排行榜来审计智能体问答。

> **TL;DR:** This paper audits six frontier lab flagships (GPT-5.6-sol, Fable 5, GLM-5.3, Qwen3.8-Max, Gemini-3.1-Pro, Muse-Spark 1.2) on a 41-item synthetic finance/insurance data-room benchmark (room02) under clean vs. buried evidence conditions. Burying evidence drops accuracy by 4.1–17.8 pp (e.g., Gemini-3.1-Pro 85.2% → 67.5% on the room02 buried arm), raises cost-per-correct up to 6.6× (Google $0.181 → $1.197), and collapses depth-5 accuracy to 38.1–71.4% versus a 100% ceiling at depth-2 clean. 72% of wrong answers across the six labs were stated at confidence ≥80, and a documented n=1 production incident shows Fable 5 pairing accurate tables with fabricated structural claims — arguing that citations and confidence are not receipts, and agentic QA needs claim-level provenance audited by humans.

## 核心贡献

- 构建了 **room02 数据室基准**：一个封闭、合成、出处完全已知的金融/保险文档库（41 题，声明跳数 2–5，每模型×条件跑 3 个种子），对六大实验室旗舰模型（OpenAI、Anthropic、Zhipu、Alibaba、Google、Meta 各一）做"干净挂载（只挂答案文档）vs 埋藏挂载（全量噪声）"的配对对照，测量准确率、已承诺错误率（committed error）、强制申报、工具调用与成本。
- 量化了**埋藏证据效应**：准确率下降 4.1–17.8 pp，单题正确成本涨 1.6–7.0 倍，工具调用涨 1.6–3.2 倍；不同实验室的应对策略分化——Anthropic/OpenAI 把压力转化为强制申报（buried 下 55%/20%），Zhipu/Alibaba/Google/Meta 则更多直接答错（committed error +8.1～+17.8 pp）。
- 揭示**干净基准的压缩效应**：同样的 41 题在干净条件下把六模型压进 6.5 分的窄带（std 3.0），而埋藏深度 4/5 指数将其拉开到 13.9 分（std 5.6）——干净挂载抹掉的正是"在噪声中定位证据"的真实工作负担。
- 证明**置信度不是回执**：六实验室 72% 的错误答案（120/167）置信度 ≥80，仅 9% 低于 50，无一模型弃权；GPT-5.6-sol 在 clean 条件下 123/123 的答案全落在 90–100 置信桶，过置信指数 +15.6（clean）/ +17.0（buried）。
- 记录并剖析了一起 **n=1 生产环境伪造事件**（Box 1）：Fable 5 在 Claude Code 会话中把精确的数值表格与三条捏造的结构性主张以同等置信度混合输出，事后承认 "The tables were real; the fabrications were mine."——论证"引用（citation）不是回执（receipt）"，主张 claim 级、可由第三方对冻结文件核验的出处。
- 对**评分器本身做了审计**：发现自己的 containment 评分器存在单向失败模式（语义正确但表层形式未列出的答案被判错，如 $8.4M vs $8.4 million），用严格扩展匹配器可翻转 46 个 trial（全部 wrong→right），并诚实标注哪些结论在评分敏感性下存活（如"失败正相关、冗余非独立"方向稳定，但效应量不稳定：φ=0.43 vs 0.21）。

## Key Contributions

- **The room02 data-room benchmark**: a closed, synthetic, provenance-known finance/insurance corpus (41 items, declared hop depth 2–5, 3 seeds per model-condition) evaluated as a paired clean-vs-buried experiment on one flagship per lab (OpenAI GPT-5.6-sol, Anthropic Fable 5, Zhipu GLM-5.3, Alibaba Qwen3.8-Max, Google Gemini-3.1-Pro, Meta Muse-Spark 1.2), scored on accuracy, committed-error rate, forced declarations, tool calls, and cost per correct answer.
- **Quantified burial effect**: accuracy drops of 4.1–17.8 pp, cost-per-correct increases of 1.6–7.0×, tool-call increases of 1.6–3.2×, with a strategy split — Anthropic/OpenAI convert burial into forced declarations (55%/20% buried) while Zhipu/Alibaba/Google/Meta absorb it as committed errors (+8.1 to +17.8 pp).
- **Clean benchmarks compress the panel**: the same 41 items compress six labs into a 6.5-point range (std 3.0) under clean mounting but spread them across 13.9 points (std 5.6) under buried depth-4/5 — clean-context evaluation hides exactly the retrieval burden that regulated document work imposes.
- **Confidence is not a receipt**: 72% of wrong answers (120/167) were stated at confidence ≥80 and 9% below 50, with zero abstentions; GPT-5.6-sol placed 123/123 clean answers in the 90–100 bucket (overconfidence index +15.6 clean / +17.0 buried), while Fable 5 is near-calibrated clean (−0.3).
- **A documented production fabrication (n=1)**: Fable 5 delivered accurate quantitative tables alongside three fabricated structural claims at identical confidence during a Claude Code session, later admitting "The tables were real; the fabrications were mine." The paper generalizes the lesson: a citation is a pointer the model asserts; a receipt is a pointer a third party can verify against a frozen file.
- **An audit of the grader itself**: the containment-based grader's dominant failure mode is one-directional (semantically correct answers in unlisted surface forms scored wrong); a strict extended matcher flips 46 trials across 10 items, every one wrong→right. The paper separates three evidence tiers (frozen historical scores, human-reviewed corrections, agent-provisional sensitivity analyses) and states which conclusions survive.

## Methodology

The evaluation harness mounts a read-only data room at `/work/documents` and gives the agent shell tools (bash, python, read_file, grep, find) — retrieval is performed by the agent's own tool calls, not a fixed retriever as in RAG. room02 is the second-generation synthetic corpus: filing-style quarterly/annual prints, credit memos, investment-committee minutes, mandate documents, and market-event notices, authored so every fact has known provenance (no proprietary material). The 41 items have declared hop counts d2=8, d3=9, d4=17, d5=7, run over 3 seeds per model-condition (~123 scored rows per arm). Two conditions differ only in mounted room contents: clean (gold documents only) and buried (full room with noise); input-token ceilings differed but were non-binding.

Answers are elicited via a forced-declaration JSON schema (answer + confidence 0–100 + evidence file list). Scoring uses a frozen containment-based grader (normalized string containment for string items; numeric tolerance with magnitude factors for numbers) — explicitly not exact-match. A prompt audit (Appendix A) verified 0/41 prompts enumerate substeps or hand over document locators (19 distinct first tokens), licensing "declared hop count" language. A secondary shallow chart-reading quiz (34 questions × 3 variants = 102 rows/model, 1–2 hops, image input) covers five of six panel labs plus Grok-4.6; GLM-5.3 was excluded because the tested configuration did not accept image input.

The receipts discipline: a 420-row claim ledger (paper_claim_ledger.csv) maps every numeric claim to a frozen file and locator; mechanical re-verification confirmed 420/420 cells match and all ten output CSVs regenerate byte-identically. Statistical treatment uses Wilson intervals for arm accuracy and an item-level bootstrap (resampling 41 items, 20,000 draws) for condition differences; a 39-item population (two items ruled defective against their own sources) is used only for §8 statistical comparisons.

## Results & Highlights

- **Burial degrades accuracy across all six labs** (room02, clean → buried): GPT-5.6-sol 82.9% → 78.9% (−4.1 pp); Fable 5 89.4% → 77.9% (−11.6 pp); GLM-5.3 89.4% → 80.5% (−8.9 pp); Qwen3.8-Max 87.0% → 74.0% (−13.0 pp); Gemini-3.1-Pro 85.2% → 67.5% (−17.8 pp); Muse-Spark 1.2 82.9% → 74.0% (−8.9 pp). On the item-level bootstrap over all 41 items the drop excludes zero for 4 of 6 labs, but only 2 of 6 on the corrected 39-item population (Alibaba −13.7 pp [−25.6, −1.7]; Google −17.9 [−29.1, −7.7]) — a sensitivity the author explicitly discloses rather than hides.
- **Cost per correct answer rises 4.7–7.0× for five labs** when evidence is buried (Google $0.181 → $1.197 per correct, 6.6×; Anthropic $0.118 → $0.821; Zhipu $0.108 → $0.590; Alibaba $0.166 → $0.777; OpenAI $0.055 → $0.372); Meta is the outlier at 1.6× ($0.395 → $0.612). Mean tool calls per item rise 1.6–3.2× (Google 9.8 → 22.9; Zhipu 9.2 → 25.7).
- **Depth is the killer**: clean d2 accuracy is 100% for all six labs, but buried d5 ranges from 38.1% (Gemini-3.1-Pro, vs. 57.1% clean — the largest single-cell collapse in Table 4) to 71.4% (Fable 5 buried, exceeding its own d4 of 68.0%); Alibaba buried d5 is 52.4%, Meta 47.6%.
- **Confident wrong dominates**: across the six labs, 120 of 167 wrong answers (72%) were stated at confidence ≥80 and only 15 (9%) below 50; no model abstained. GPT-5.6-sol put 123/123 clean-condition answers in the 90–100 confidence bucket with an overconfidence index (Mean Conf − 100×Acc) of +15.6 clean / +17.0 buried; Fable 5 is near-calibrated clean (−0.3) and mildly overconfident buried (+4.6).
- **Clean scores compress, buried spreads**: the conventional clean index spans a 6.5-point range (std 3.0) across six labs; the buried depth-4/5 multi-hop index spans 13.9 points (std 5.6) — nearly twice the resolution. The correlation between the two indices is unstable (−0.47 to +0.77 across defensible scoring choices), so the paper deliberately draws no ranking conclusion.
- **Redundancy is not independence**: cross-lab correctness patterns on the buried panel are positively correlated — mean pairwise φ = 0.43 under the frozen grader (falling to 0.21 under provisional adjudicated scoring), and 47% of items that at least one lab fails are failed by a majority (≥4 of 6 labs; 14% under adjudicated scoring) — direction stable, magnitude grader-sensitive.
- **Shallow quiz (5 of 6 labs + Grok-4.6)**: four panel labs score 100% at 1-hop and 91–96% at 2-hop; Google is the exception at 67.6% (1-hop) and 92.6% (2-hop), overall 84.3%.
- **Grader audit**: a strict extended matcher (adding only ISO/spelled-date equivalence, magnitude abbreviations, ticker aliases) flips 46 trials across 10 items, all wrong→right; two of 41 items were ruled defective against their own source documents, and a third item's unrequested gold requirement was manufacturing a 12-of-36 vs. 0 split between labs — "a grading contract can be deterministic, reproducible, byte-identical on re-run, and still be wrong in ways that change which model looks better."

Credibility rests on an unusually rigorous verification apparatus: hash-manifested frozen evidence folders (paper_freeze_v1 and a wave-2 addendum), a 420-row claim ledger mapping every table cell to a file+locator with 420/420 mechanical match and 10/10 byte-identical regeneration on independent re-run, a pre-hashed stratified human review of 84 answers (94 recorded decisions), and full AI-assistance disclosure (Claude Opus 5 / Fable 5 used for development and drafting; the Box 1 fabrication incident involves the same model family under study, which the paper flags rather than conceals). Single-author industry papers warrant extra skepticism, but this one documents its own verification gaps more thoroughly than most peer-reviewed evaluations.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: genuinely fills a gap — GAIA/SWE-bench/BrowseComp score open-web or software tasks, HotpotQA/MuSiQue test composition over open corpora, and Lost-in-the-Middle/RULER study evidence position in long context; none combine a closed provenance-known corpus with a claim-level audit and condition-aware (clean vs. buried) scoring on frontier agents. The "receipts vs. citations" framing and the grader self-audit are the freshest parts.
- **Rigor**: exceptionally high for an evaluation paper — frozen artifacts, three explicitly separated evidence tiers, bootstrap uncertainty, refusal to publish a model ranking because every pairwise gap sits within sampling noise, and pre-declared non-claims (§2). The author's own sensitivity analysis kills or narrows several headline results (the 4-of-6 significance finding shrinks to 2-of-6 after removing two defective items) and the paper says so in the abstract.
- **Reproducibility**: strong on the mechanical axis (byte-identical table regeneration, hash manifests, planned deposit with scripts), and the paper is candid that "reproducibility verifies transcription, not grading semantics." The raw logs are excluded from the planned deposit because tool outputs can echo public SEC text, and the fabrication-case pack is reviewers-on-request only — both limit full third-party replay.
- **Significance**: the burial effect, confident-wrong statistics, and positive cross-lab error correlation are operationally relevant to anyone deploying agents in regulated document work. But with 41 items × 3 seeds, Wilson intervals are ±7–8 pp, so most between-lab comparisons (and some within-lab deltas) are underpowered; the durable findings are the panel-level patterns, not any single model's number.
- **Red flags**: (1) the fabrication case is n=1 and involves the author's own working session — vivid, but explicitly not evidence of a general fabrication rate; (2) the corpus is synthetic and authored by the same hand that grades it, so ecological validity for real messy proprietary data rooms is asserted by design analogy, not demonstrated; (3) the shallow quiz is not a matched instrument (GLM-5.3 missing, Grok-4.6 mixed in, wave-2 run later with a different freeze); (4) the containment grader's one-directional failure mode (unlisted surface forms like "$8.4M" vs. "$8.4 million" scored wrong) means the frozen accuracies are likely underestimates, and the buried-arm deltas could partly reflect format friction rather than retrieval failure — the paper acknowledges burial does not separate retrieval, reader, and format-contract components; (5) no interventions are tested — this is a baseline paper whose practical payoff (structured evidence layers, trajectory training, room03 defense corpus) is all future work.

## Strengths

- The clean-vs-buried paired design isolates the cost of *finding* evidence from the cost of *composing* answers — a distinction most leaderboards erase, demonstrated by the compression result (6.5-pt clean range vs. 13.9-pt buried range).
- Multi-metric, cost-aware scoring surfaces effects a single accuracy number hides (forced declarations, committed-error strategy split, $/correct rising up to 6.6×).
- The grader self-audit is a model of intellectual honesty: it found a defective item that was actively manufacturing between-lab differences, and the paper reports which conclusions survive each scoring choice.
- The receipts discipline (claim ledger, frozen locators, hash manifests) is concretely implementable and directly answers the Box 1 failure mode.
- Clean separation of the three evidence tiers prevents provisional re-scorings from silently becoming "corrected" results.

## Limitations

- 41 items (39 after defect removal) with 3 seeds — sampling noise of ±7–8 pp swamps most pairwise comparisons; the paper's own bootstrap shows only 2 of 6 labs' burial drops clearly exclude zero on the corrected population.
- The corpus is synthetic with known provenance; no evidence yet that burial deltas transfer to real proprietary document rooms or to the defense setting (room03 is unbuilt).
- The design cannot decompose failure causes (insufficient retrieval vs. unusable context vs. miscombination vs. format-contract violation) — explicitly deferred to future work.
- The multimodal instrument is unmatched: five of six labs, a supplementary non-panel model (Grok-4.6), and a wave-2 addendum run on a different date with a separate freeze.
- The fabrication case is n=1 and self-observed; no fabrication-rate estimate is possible.
- Containment-based grading penalizes valid surface variants, and scores are frozen historical artifacts of that grader — the paper's mitigations (sensitivity analyses, disclosure) are appropriate but do not replace an exact-match or human-adjudicated contract.

## Relevance

对构建或评估 LLM 智能体（agentic）检索问答系统的读者高度相关：它量化了"干净上下文评测严重低估真实文档工作负担"这一关键问题，并给出了可操作的评估纪律（claim 级回执、条件感知计分、人工对抗校验），对 RAG/多跳问答/智能体评测方法论与幻觉-校准研究都有直接参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How much of the burial penalty is retrieval failure versus evidence misinterpretation once retrieved? The paper's own decomposition proposal (retrieval coverage vs. answer correctness as separate scored quantities) needs an implementation — can it be operationalized with per-hop logging?
- Do the buried-arm deltas shrink under an exact-match-plus-adjudicated grading contract fixed in advance, and does the clean/buried index correlation stabilize (the reported −0.47 to +0.77 range currently makes rank claims meaningless)?
- Does the positive cross-lab error correlation (φ = 0.43 frozen / 0.21 adjudicated) persist with a defined independence baseline, and what does it imply for the actual ensemble benefit of multi-vendor deployment?
- Can an explicitly source-linked evidence structure plus training on audited trajectories let a smaller open-weight reader match these frontier flagships on buried evidence — the central question the announced room03/intervention stage sets up?
- How do the results extend beyond depth 5 (the 6+ hop chains the author believes hold the remaining "alpha"), and into non-text modalities where the buried-evidence effect is hypothesized to be largest?
- Would allowing abstention (not a scored outcome here) change the confident-wrong profile, given that all six models answered every item?
