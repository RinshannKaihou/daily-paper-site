> **一句话总结：** 本文用 EPITOME 框架（ER/IP/EX 三个共情机制）在 Llama-3.1-8B、Qwen2.5-7B、Mistral-7B 三个指令模型上提取对比激活转向向量（CAA），发现第 15 层是跨模型稳定的干预点、目标机制分数可被显著提升，但三个机制方向并不独立（ER–IP 余弦 −0.25~−0.32、EX–IP −0.45~−0.53 反对齐），转向一个机制会引发其他机制的溢出效应；更重要的是 persona 提示引起的共情变化中仅约 3% 落在恢复出的机制子空间内，说明共情"可转向但多轴"，单向量去偏不足以消除 persona 引发的共情差距。

> **TL;DR:** This paper extracts contrastive activation addition (CAA) steering vectors for the three EPITOME empathy mechanisms (Emotional Reactions, Interpretations, Explorations) in Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, and Mistral-7B-Instruct. Layer 15 is a stable cross-model intervention point: at α=+2, EX steering raises the Llama EX score by +0.930 over a 0.150 baseline and IP steering raises Mistral IP by +1.360 over 0.240 (n=200/cell, EPITOME classifier; direction confirmed by a GPT-5-mini judge). But control is not selective — steering EX at α=+1 on Llama simultaneously drops ER by −0.225 — and the three vectors are geometrically entangled (ER–IP cosine −0.29, EX–IP −0.45 on Llama). Persona prompts ("cynical person" lowers Llama ER by −0.550) produce large label shifts whose squared layer-15 activation displacement is captured only 2.6–3.1% by the recovered ER/IP/EX subspace, so single-vector debiasing cannot close persona-conditioned empathy gaps. All conclusions rest on automated proxies (EPITOME classifiers + GPT-5-mini), not human ratings.

## 核心贡献

- **跨模型的激活级共情控制把手**：在三个开源指令模型上系统扫描层（3~31）与转向强度（α∈{−1,0,+1,+2}），确定第 15 层是稳定的中层干预点——在 α=+1 下所有（模型 × 机制）组合的目标分数均正向移动，而末层在三个模型中的两个上不稳定。
- **证明"表达共情是多轴的而非单一可控特质"**：几何分析显示 ER/EX/IP 三个机制向量共享表征结构而非独立轴——ER 与 EX 近乎正交（余弦 +0.08~+0.13），但 ER–IP 与 EX–IP 显著反对齐（−0.25~−0.53），且转向任一机制会产生可测量的跨机制行为溢出（如 Llama 上 EX α=+1 使 EX +0.570 同时 ER −0.225）。
- **persona 引发的共情偏置大多在恢复出的机制基之外**：persona 提示可使 ER 分数大幅变化（cynical person 在 Qwen 上 ΔER=−0.735），但 ER/IP/EX 子空间仅捕获 persona 平均激活位移平方范数的 2.6–3.1%（随机 3 维子空间的期望参考值 <0.1%），残差仍保留低秩结构（前 5 个主成分解释 27–30% 方差），说明单向量去偏不充分。
- **超越响应级打分的因果探针方法**：不同于以往只对生成文本打分的工作，本文通过干预表征（teacher-forced 提取 → 池均值差分向量 → 前向钩子注入）把共情从"打分对象"变成"机制分析的可控目标"，并用提示基线（4 种手工指令均无法隔离单一机制）与残差化、精确匹配对照、多种子/解码种子、OOD 迁移等多重对照加固结论。

## Key Contributions

- A **cross-model activation-level handle on empathy**: a layer sweep (ℓ = 3,7,…,31; α ∈ {−1, 0, +1, +2}) over three open instruction-tuned LLMs identifies layer 15 as a stable middle-layer steering point — at α=+1 every (model, mechanism) cell shows a positive target-mechanism shift, while later layers are model-dependent and the final layer is unstable in two of three models.
- **Empathy is multi-axial, not one controllable trait**: geometry analysis shows the three mechanism vectors share representational structure rather than forming independent axes. ER and EX are nearly orthogonal (cosines +0.08 to +0.13 across models), while ER–IP (−0.25 to −0.32) and EX–IP (−0.45 to −0.53) are anti-aligned, and steering any one mechanism produces measurable behavioral spillovers on the others (e.g., Llama EX at α=+1: ∆EX=+0.570 with ∆ER=−0.225).
- **Persona-induced empathy bias is largely outside the recovered mechanism basis**: persona prompts shift EPITOME scores substantially (e.g., "cynical person" ∆ER = −0.550 on Llama, −0.735 on Qwen) yet the orthonormalized span of {vER, vIP, vEX} captures only 2.6–3.1% of persona-mean squared activation-shift magnitude at layer 15 (vs. <0.1% expected from a random 3-D subspace), positioning single-vector debiasing as insufficient.
- **A causal-probe methodology beyond response-level scoring**: unlike prior work that only scores generated text under prompting/persona/fine-tuning, this paper intervenes on representations (teacher-forced extraction of pool-mean-difference vectors, injected via forward hooks) and triangulates with a prompt baseline (none of four hand-crafted instructions isolates a single mechanism), residualization, exact-matched label controls, multi-seed/decoding-seed checks, and out-of-domain transfer.

## Methodology

The pipeline (Figure 2) has five steps:

1. **Contrastive examples**: for each EPITOME mechanism m ∈ {ER, IP, EX}, high- and low-empathy seeker–response rows are sampled from filtered EPITOME data (Reddit peer-support dialogues from Sharma et al., 2020), with pools |Pm|=|Nm|=100 (Pm: label ym ≥ 1; Nm: ym = 0). Posts shorter than 100 characters or containing suicide/self-harm keywords are removed.
2. **Teacher-forced activation extraction**: each seeker–response sequence is run through the model, and the layer-ℓ residual-stream hidden state at the final response token is recorded.
3. **Vector formation**: v_m^ℓ = mean(h^ℓ over Pm) − mean(h^ℓ over Nm) — a Contrastive Activation Addition (CAA) recipe following Rimsky et al. (2024), contrasting pool-level means rather than paired rows.
4. **Layer/α sweep**: layers ℓ = 3, 7, …, 31 (no 31 for Qwen's 28 blocks), strengths α ∈ {−1, 0, +1, +2}; decoding at temperature 0.8, top-p 0.9; n = 200 evaluation seekers per cell; scored by EPITOME classifiers (0/1/2 per mechanism).
5. **Inference-time steering**: a forward hook updates h_{ℓ,t} ← h_{ℓ,t} + α·v_m^ℓ at each generation step.

Supporting analyses: (a) a GPT-5-mini judge with the original EPITOME rubrics cross-validates the canonical-layer setting; (b) residualization of each vector onto the orthogonal complement of the other two tests geometric dependence; (c) persona-conditioned generation ("You are a {persona}.") with cosine projection of mean-pooled response-token activations onto vER and subspace decomposition of persona shifts; (d) out-of-domain transfer to EmpatheticDialogues (7 negative-emotion categories, 200 situations); (e) WikiText-103 perplexity for fluency; (f) exact-matched extraction pools to control for non-target label imbalance; (g) multi-seed (3 extraction seeds) and decoding-seed (3 seeds) robustness with paired bootstrap CIs (B = 10,000, Holm-corrected) on Llama.

## Results & Highlights

- **Target steering at layer 15 works across all models** (Table 1, EPITOME classifier, n=200/cell): at α=+2, EX steering on Llama-3.1 raises EX mean from 0.150 baseline by +0.930, IP steering on Mistral raises IP from 0.240 by +1.360, and IP steering on Llama raises IP from 0.460 by +1.080; the GPT-5-mini judge preserves the directional pattern (e.g., Mistral ER α=+2: +0.315 over a 1.600 baseline vs. classifier +0.155 over 1.030).
- **Control is not selective — cross-mechanism spillovers** (Table 2): on Llama at α=+1, EX steering raises EX by +0.570 while ER falls by −0.225; ER α=−1 on Llama lowers ER −0.215 while IP rises +0.240. Geometrically (Table 3), ER–IP cosines are −0.29/−0.32/−0.25 and EX–IP −0.45/−0.46/−0.53 on Llama/Qwen/Mistral, predicting the direction of IP-involving spillovers.
- **Residualization confirms shared structure**: steering with the residualized ER vector still works, retaining 131.3% of the ER effect, but EX retention drops to 84.2% and IP to 50.0% (Llama, α=+1, Table 8), so the shared component contributes to behavior beyond its geometric magnitude.
- **Persona shifts bypass the mechanism basis** (Tables 18–19, Figure 4): "cynical person" produces the largest ER label drops (Llama ∆ER=−0.550, Qwen −0.735) yet the largest persona-induced projection shift is only |∆pER| = 0.055; the ER/IP/EX subspace captures only 2.6% (Llama), 3.1% (Qwen), 3.0% (Mistral) of persona-mean squared activation-shift at layer 15, versus <0.1% expected from a random 3-D subspace (d = 4096/3584), while top-5 residual PCs explain 27–30% of residual variance.
- **Robustness and cost checks**: out-of-domain transfer to EmpatheticDialogues preserves direction in every cell but the negative direction transfers more uniformly (Llama ∆ = −0.250 at α=−1 vs. +0.020 at α=+1, Table 6); WikiText-103 perplexity rises modestly at α=±1 but clearly at α=+2 (baseline 8.04 → EX 10.13, +2.09, Table 7); all 18 mechanism–direction–seed bootstrap CIs exclude zero after Holm correction.
- **Prompting cannot isolate mechanisms** (Table 16): the EX-targeted prompt P3 raises Llama EX from 0.120 to 1.220 but simultaneously shortens responses from 123.7 to 93.6 tokens and lowers ER on Qwen (1.365→0.895); P1/P2 shift multiple dimensions at once.
- **Race-conditioned behavioral check**: applying vER at α=+1 raises ER for person/white/black personas on Llama (1.14→1.26, 1.11→1.28, 1.03→1.08) but does not close the black person–person ER gap (Table 17).

## Authors & Credibility

The paper is from the Department of Artificial Intelligence at Ajou University (South Korea), by JuHeon Ha, Byounghan Lee, Yunseo Choi, and corresponding author Kyung-Ah Sohn. The work is methodologically careful for an academic interpretability study: code is released (github.com/hjhj97/Empathy-Is-Steerable-but-Multi-Axial), the methodology is built on the established CAA recipe (Rimsky et al., 2024) and the standard EPITOME framework (Sharma et al., 2020), and the paper includes an unusually thorough set of controls for this genre — dual evaluators (EPITOME classifier + GPT-5-mini judge), exact-matched extraction pools, residualization with norm matching, multi-seed and decoding-seed checks, Holm-corrected bootstrap CIs, OOD transfer, perplexity checks, and an explicit ethics section warning against using single-vector steering for debiasing. Funding is from IITP and NRF (Korean government grants), with no apparent industrial conflicts of interest.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: Moderate-to-good. The core move — extracting CAA vectors for empathy sub-mechanisms — is a competent combination of known techniques rather than a new method; the genuinely novel results are the geometric entanglement finding (mechanism vectors are NOT independent axes, partially rejecting the independent-subspace view reported for logical-reasoning knowledge vectors by Wang & Lei 2026) and the clean 3% persona-subspace decomposition, which is a strong, quotable negative result against "one direction per trait" stories like Persona Vectors.
- **Rigor**: Above average. The exact-matched-pool control (Appendix E) is a check most steering papers omit: it shows target steerability survives label-composition matching (EX target ∆ +0.610 [0.460, 0.760] after matching) while cross-dimension coupling weakens (ER–IP cosine −0.395 → −0.124), honestly quantifying how much of the spillover is a data artifact. However, the seed-robustness and bootstrap machinery is run only on Llama at layer 15; Qwen and Mistral conclusions rest on single runs.
- **Proxy fragility**: All headline numbers are EPITOME classifier scores; the authors themselves note the EX and IP classifiers behave effectively as 0/2 binaries, which plausibly inflates apparent steering magnitudes (e.g., Mistral IP +1.360 at α=+2), and no human evaluation of steered or persona-conditioned generations was collected. The GPT-5-mini judge shares the EPITOME rubric, so it is a partially dependent cross-check, not an independent gold standard.
- **Reproducibility**: Good — public code, full layer-sweep and persona tables in appendices, explicit prompt templates, and precise pool-construction rules (100/100 pools, ym ≥ 1 vs ym = 0, length/self-harm filters).
- **Significance**: The 3% persona-subspace number is the most consequential finding: it directly undermines the plausibility of single-vector empathy debiasing and motivates multi-vector/persona-conditioned controllers. The significance is bounded by the proxy-based, single-model robustness scope.
- **Red flags**: The headline abstract claim "captures only about 3%" is a point estimate from single runs over 200 seekers per persona without CIs; the persona set is small (11), US-centric, and explicitly a controlled probe rather than representative sampling; the residual-PCA analysis is descriptive with no causal interpretation; and the ER steering effect at α=+1 on Llama is small (+0.080) relative to persona effects, yet the layer-selection logic is partly tuned around this canonical setting.

## Strengths

- Identifies a genuinely cross-model, stable intervention point (layer 15) with positive target effects in all 9 (model × mechanism) cells at α=+1, and validates the direction with a second evaluator, OOD transfer, and seed checks.
- The two headline findings are informative negative results executed well: (1) empathy mechanisms are geometrically entangled (anti-aligned IP directions, spillovers), and (2) persona-induced shifts lie almost entirely outside the contrastively recovered mechanism subspace (2.6–3.1%) while retaining low-rank residual structure.
- Exceptionally honest framing: the paper repeatedly separates "steerable" from "selectively controllable," labels its race-conditioned check as non-general, warns against single-vector debiasing deployment, and flags classifier bimodality as a possible inflation source.
- The exact-matched-pool control quantifying how extraction-pool label imbalance contributes to apparent cross-mechanism coupling is a methodological contribution other steering papers should adopt.

## Limitations

- Evaluation is entirely proxy-based (EPITOME classifiers + GPT-5-mini); no human annotation of steered or persona-conditioned outputs, so nothing here establishes changes in *perceived* empathic support.
- The EX and IP classifiers behave as effective 0/2 binaries, possibly inflating steering magnitudes; the length/question-control analysis for EX is lightweight, so response-style confounds remain possible, and EX steering should be read as classifier-level control rather than deeper exploratory reasoning.
- Robustness checks (multi-seed, decoding-seed, bootstrap CIs) are confined to the Llama layer-15 setting; persona projection results are single-run estimates, and Qwen's weak projection response even under direct steering makes its calibration less informative.
- The persona set is narrow and US-centric (11 personas, 2 controls), with no intersectional or non-US personas; no population-level fairness claims are possible.
- Steering vectors are static layer-15 residual-stream directions; adaptive, multi-vector, or sub-module controllers and persona-conditioned vectors are untested, and the final-layer-instability explanation (coupling to next-token logits) is hypothesized but not verified (e.g., no logit-lens analysis).

## Relevance

这篇论文与「LLM Hidden States & Interpretability」方向高度相关：它用激活转向作为表征结构的因果探针，研究共情机制向量在残差流中的几何关系（正交性/反对齐/子空间分解），正是表征几何与机制可解释性的核心问题；其"评测维度 ≠ 独立干预轴"的结论对任何试图用单一方向向量控制高阶行为（含去偏）的可解释性研究都有方法论警示。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would sparse autoencoder (SAE) features decompose the 97% residual persona-shift better than the contrastive CAA basis, and can persona-specific directions be recovered from the low-rank residual PCs?
- Does the ER–IP anti-alignment persist in larger models or multi-turn emotional-support dialogues, where mechanism expression may be distributed across turns?
- Can a learned multi-vector or persona-conditioned controller close the black person–person ER gap that single-vector vER steering (α=+1) fails to equalize?
- Do human raters perceive α-steered responses as more empathic, and does the EX classifier-bimodality artifact change the ranking of mechanism steering effects under human annotation?
- Is the layer-15 optimum a general property of middle-layer residual streams (as prior CAA results suggest), or is it partly an artifact of the last-response-token extraction choice — would mean-pooled or sequence-level extraction shift the stable layer?
- How much of the cross-mechanism spillover is token-level competition (question-form EX tokens displacing ER acknowledgment) versus genuine shared representation, and can logit-lens or attention-level analysis separate the two?
