> **一句话总结：** 在 Llama-3.2-3B、Qwen-2.5-3B、Gemma-3-4B 三个家族上，用节点显著性（LDS）、EAP-IG 边归因、叠加指数和因果消融四种方法对比基座与指令模型的知识冲突回路，发现指令微调是"门控"而非"重连"——同一批后层头仍承担冲突裁决（top-10 头集合 Jaccard 0.67–0.82，中位层偏移 ≤2 层），只是对简短反事实上下文的依赖率大幅下降（Gemma CRR 0.870→0.369，Llama 0.675→0.263），但换成连贯伪造段落时这种抵抗力几乎消失（基座与指令模型都接近 1.00 地跟随假上下文）。

> **TL;DR:** Using a ParaConflict-derived single-token factual conflict set (764–870 prompts per model) and four complementary methods, this paper compares base vs. instruct siblings of Llama-3.2-3B, Qwen-2.5-3B and Gemma-3-4B and concludes instruction tuning **gates** rather than **rewires** the knowledge-conflict circuit: top-10 head overlap between base and instruct stays at Jaccard 0.67–0.82 (Spearman 0.82–0.91, vs. a hypergeometric random null of p ≤ 7×10⁻¹⁵), median conflict-head layer shifts by at most 2 layers, and Contextual Reliance Rate on terse false context drops sharply from base to instruct (Gemma-3-4B 0.870→0.369, Δ −0.50; Llama-3.2-3B 0.675→0.263, Δ −0.41) — yet under a coherent false passage the base/instruct gap nearly vanishes (both ≥0.854 following the false context). Practical payoff: circuit tools calibrated on base models (e.g., PH3-style head pruning) should transfer to instruct siblings, but the added skepticism is narrow — instruction tuning is not a defense against well-written misinformation.

## 核心贡献

- **首个基座 vs. 指令模型冲突回路的机制对比**：把"微调如何改变知识冲突回路"分解为三个正交轴——节点（哪些头重要）、边（如何连线）、行为（是否真的改变），并给出预注册的判定表：头重叠 >0.6 判"门控"、边重叠落入 <0.3 且 CRR 显著变化才判"重连"。
- **C1 门控而非重连**：三个家族中，基座与指令模型的 top-10 头 Jaccard 为 0.67–0.82、边 top-50 Jaccard 为 0.39–0.61，全部落在"门控"带内，无一接近"重连"阈值；Llama 判为严格门控（边 J@50=0.61），Qwen（0.39）与 Gemma（0.54）判为"边子类型含糊的门控"。
- **C2 记忆向的鲁棒性（且是框架效应）**：指令模型更拒绝简短、无支撑的反事实（替换式 CRR 全家族显著下降，p < 10⁻¹⁵），但对连贯的证据式假段落，基座与指令都近乎完全顺从（0.854–1.000）——这种怀疑主义只针对"赤裸裸的矛盾"。
- **C3 定位保持**：冲突头在两种变体中都位于网络上半部（共享头 81–100% 在上半层），中位层偏移 ≤2 层（24→22、32→31、28→28），>95% 的边重要性仍在高层。
- **因果验证与可迁移性结论**：放大/抑制 LDS 选出的 5 个上下文/记忆头，6 个模型变体 36/36 次符号符合先验预测（二项 p ≈ 2⁻³⁶）；因此基座上校准的头级转向、剪枝（如 PH3）与探针应可直接迁移到指令兄弟模型。

## Key Contributions

- **First mechanistic base-vs-instruct comparison of conflict-resolution circuits**, decomposed into three axes — nodes (which heads matter), edges (how they are wired), and behavior (did behavior actually change) — with a pre-registered decision table mapping node/edge overlap and CRR shift to the verdicts null / strict gating / gating-with-ambiguous-edge-subtype / rewiring.
- **C1 (Gating, not rewiring):** top-10 head Jaccard between base and instruct is 0.67–0.82 and edge top-50 Jaccard is 0.39–0.61 across all three families — every cell lands in the gating band, none approaches the rewiring threshold; Llama is labeled strict gating (edge J@50 = 0.61), Qwen (0.39) and Gemma (0.54) "gating with an ambiguous edge sub-type."
- **C2 (Memory-ward robustness as a framing factor):** instruct models reject terse, unsupported counterfactual context far more than base ones (substitution CRR deltas of −0.23 to −0.50, all p < 10⁻¹⁵), yet both variants follow a coherent, evidential false passage at 0.854–1.000 — the added skepticism is specific to bare contradictions.
- **C3 (Localization preserved):** conflict heads stay in the upper half of the network in both variants (shared heads 81–100% in upper half), the median conflict-head layer moves by at most two layers (24→22, 32→31, 28→28), and >95% of edge importance remains in upper layers.
- **Causal validation with a specificity floor:** amplifying/suppressing the 5 LDS-selected context/memory heads matches the a-priori predicted CRR sign in 36/36 cases across six model variants (binomial p ≈ 2⁻³⁶), while random 5-head late-layer ablation moves CRR by only −0.03 to +0.02 — so base-calibrated steering, pruning (e.g., PH3), and probes should transfer directly to instruct siblings.

## Methodology

**Models.** Three families, each a base model paired with its instruction-tuned sibling: Llama-3.2-3B, Qwen-2.5-3B, and Gemma-3-4B. Inference-time analysis only on publicly available weights — no additional training is performed; "instruction tuning" is studied as the delta between released checkpoints.

**Data.** A factual conflict dataset derived from ParaConflict (Li et al., 2025a), filtered so that every prompt's context answer and memory answer are each a single token under the model's own tokenizer (tokenizer-pinned memory target), giving a clean next-token contrast unconfounded by tokenization across the base/instruct pair. Domains: Athlete–Sport, Official–Language, Company–Headquarter, World-Capital; 764 prompts survive for Llama/Qwen and 870 for Gemma. Two conflict forms inject the same false fact: **Substitution** (a terse one-sentence swap, e.g., "Lionel Messi plays the sport of basketball") and **Coherent** (a multi-sentence evidential passage that repeats the distractor).

**Measures.** Four quantities: (1) **Contextual Reliance Rate (CRR)** — the fraction of conflict prompts where greedy generation follows the injected context over parametric memory, with a continuous teacher-forced log-prob variant as a check; (2) **node overlap** — Jaccard of top-k head sets (LDS-ranked) plus Spearman of full head rankings; (3) **edge overlap** — Jaccard of top-50 EAP-IG edges over the full edge graph (87k–390k edges, 5 integration steps), with full-ranking Spearman as a secondary; (4) a per-head **superposition index** S ∈ [−1, +1] from direct-logit-attribution context/memory pulls (+1 pure context head, −1 pure memory head).

**Pre-registered decision rule.** Node overlap >0.6 = high (gating), <0.3 = low; combined with edge overlap bands and a required significant CRR shift, this yields the four-way verdict table (null / strict gating / ambiguous-edge gating / rewiring). Overlap is tested against an exact hypergeometric random-top-k null.

**Validation and controls.** (a) PH3-style **path patching** on the top-20 heads as an independent causal check on the LDS node screen; (b) **causal ablation** — amplify, mean-ablate, or zero-ablate the top-5 context or memory heads and re-measure CRR against an a-priori predicted sign; (c) a **random-ablation floor** (10 random 5-head sets per direction, drawn from the same upper-half layers) to show the effect is specific to LDS-selected heads; (d) robustness of the node screen across saliency flavors (logit-derivative, gradient-norm, gradient×activation). Node/path-patching analyses run on both conflict forms; edge attribution is confined to Substitution because the coherent passage's repeated distractor breaks EAP-IG's position-aligned contrast window.

## Results & Highlights

- **Behavioral shift is large but framing-dependent (C2).** On the substitution form, CRR drops sharply from base to instruct in every family: Llama-3.2-3B 0.675→0.263 (Δ = −0.412), Qwen-2.5-3B 0.445→0.220 (Δ = −0.225), Gemma-3-4B 0.870→0.369 (Δ = −0.501); all substitution deltas are significant at p < 10⁻¹⁵ (z = 16–22). Under coherent passages the effect collapses: deltas are −0.029 (Llama), −0.122 (Qwen), −0.005 (Gemma), with both variants at 0.854–1.000 — i.e., instruction tuning buys resistance to terse contradictions only. The continuous log-prob variant of CRR agrees with the argmax metric within 2.6 points in every cell.
- **The circuit is gated, not rewired (C1).** Top-10 head Jaccard between base and instruct is 0.67 (Qwen), 0.74 (Llama), 0.82 (Gemma) with Spearman 0.82–0.91, far above the hypergeometric random-top-k null (exact p ≤ 7×10⁻¹⁵) but consistent with gating, never rewiring. An independent cross-check via the superposition pipeline gives mean head-set Jaccard 0.74/0.60/0.82, and re-running the node screen on coherent prompts gives J@10 = 1.00 (Qwen), 0.82 (Gemma), 0.67 (Llama) — the node verdict is not an artifact of the terse form. Edge overlap (EAP-IG J@50) sub-types the result: Llama 0.61 (strict gating), Gemma 0.54, Qwen 0.39 (partial band, ambiguous edge sub-type); full-edge ranking agreement is Spearman 0.69–0.72.
- **Causal ablation confirms the same heads are load-bearing in base and instruct.** Suppressing the top-5 LDS context heads collapses context-following — ΔCRR = −0.83 (Gemma base), −0.41 (Qwen base), −0.31 (Llama base) — while amplifying them raises CRR; all six model variants × interventions match the predicted sign 36/36 (binomial p ≈ 2⁻³⁶). The effect is specific: jointly mean-ablating 5 random late-layer heads moves CRR by only −0.03 to +0.02 versus −0.31 to −0.34 for the selected context heads, and ablating an equal number of random heads leaves the LDS-selected heads' effect intact. Path patching agrees with the LDS ranking on top-20 heads (Spearman 0.62–0.85, all six positive), and the LDS-assigned pull direction matches the independent superposition-index sign on 55/60 head assignments.
- **Localization is preserved (C3).** The top-LDS heads sit in the upper half of the network in both variants (shared base ∩ instruct heads 81–100% in the upper half); the median conflict-head layer moves by at most two layers (24→22, 32→31, 28→28 for the 28/36/34-layer models), and >95% of edge-attribution importance stays in upper layers in both variants.
- **An abstention side-effect.** Under terse conflict, instruct models decline to answer with either candidate more often — Gemma's "neither" rate rises from 0 to 48 prompts — bounding the behavioral claim: tuning shifts models toward rejecting false context, not toward ignoring context altogether.

## Authors & Credibility

The six authors (Shubham Pandere and Gautam Ranka as equal contributors, plus Ritika Varshney, Navya Deshmukh, Roushi Sareen, Roshan Kumar Singh) are from **IvLabs, VNIT** (Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology, India) — an academic lab rather than a frontier-industry interpretability team. The work shows unusually disciplined methodology for its scale: a pre-registered decision rule stated before results, exact hypergeometric permutation nulls, an explicit specificity control (random-head ablation floor), cross-method triangulation, and candid reporting of weak spots (e.g., they state that path-patching p-values are individually non-significant and treat triangulation as corroborative rather than overclaiming a single test). Code is released at github.com/IvLabs/Rewired-or-Gated. The paper engages the relevant literature directly (Jin et al. 2024 head taxonomy, Li et al. 2025a ParaConflict, Prakash et al. 2024 entity-tracking enhancement, Wang et al. 2025 edge-drift, Wu et al. 2024 layer shift) and positions its contribution accurately against it. No prior high-profile interpretability track record is evident for this group, but the methodological hygiene here is a strong signal in itself.

## Critical Assessment

- **The headline is cleanest for one of three families.** "Gating, not rewiring" is unambiguous for Llama (edge J@50 = 0.61), but Qwen (0.39) and Gemma (0.54) fall into the paper's own "partial" band and are charitably labeled "gating with an ambiguous edge sub-type." The 0.6/0.3 cutoffs are called pre-registered, but they are the authors' own bands; had the edge threshold sat slightly higher, Qwen's story would be closer to partial rewiring of mid-tail edges. To the paper's credit it reports this honestly (Table 3, Figure 4), yet the abstract presents the blanket verdict without the Llama-only sharpness.
- **The most safety-relevant finding has no mechanistic account.** Edge analysis is confined to the substitution form (the coherent passage breaks EAP-IG's position-aligned contrast window), so the coherent-passage vulnerability — arguably the paper's most important behavioral result — gets a node-level repetition check (J@10 = 0.67–1.00) but no wiring-level explanation. The "why" of framing sensitivity is left open.
- **"Instruction tuning" is treated as a black-box bundle.** The study compares two released checkpoints per family without controlling for what post-training actually changed (SFT vs. RLHF mix, data scale, chat template). The gating conclusion holds for *these* deltas; it cannot attribute the reweighting to instruction tuning per se versus any other post-training difference, and no intervention on training is performed.
- **Narrow evidence base.** Only 3–4B models (the authors flag that 70B+ behavior is open), one source corpus (ParaConflict), and a skewed surviving pool — Athlete–Sport dominates at 70% of Llama/Qwen prompts (61% for Gemma), with Company–Founder and Book–Author relations filtered out entirely. CRR is also measured with a blunt greedy-10-token + case-insensitive substring match, mitigated but not replaced by the 2.6-point-agreeing log-prob variant.
- **Statistics are honest but aggregate.** The 36/36 sign-match binomial treats six cells as independent replications, which the authors themselves qualify; path-patching cell p-values (0.10–0.42) are individually non-significant, so confidence rests on consistency across methods — reasonable, but it means no single number nails the claim.

## Strengths

- **Four-method triangulation** (LDS node screen, EAP-IG edges, independent superposition index, path patching + causal ablation) with each method's known blind spots explicitly discussed — no single attribution method is leaned on.
- **Pre-registered decision rule** with exact hypergeometric nulls turns a qualitative "same heads?" question into a falsifiable verdict table.
- **A real specificity control:** the random 5-head ablation floor (−0.03 to +0.02 vs. −0.31 to −0.83 for selected heads) rules out the "any late-layer perturbation works" alternative.
- **Cross-family replication** (Llama, Qwen, Gemma) with per-family placement rather than a pooled average, including the identical-764-row design that lets Llama and Qwen be scored on the exact same facts.
- **Genuinely useful implications:** base-model circuit calibration transferring to instruct siblings makes interpretability cheaper (open base checkpoint + analysis = deployed-model insight), and the framing dissociation is a concrete, quantified warning that instruction tuning does not defend against coherent misinformation — with a plausible under-trust failure mode for RAG systems.
- **Candor:** ambiguous edge sub-types are labeled as such, non-significant p-values are acknowledged, and Limitations are specific rather than boilerplate.

## Limitations

- **Scale:** all conclusions come from 3–4B models; whether gating persists for substantially larger instruction-tuned models (where post-training is heavier) is untested.
- **Substitution-only edge analysis:** the EAP-IG wiring account exists only for the terse form; the coherent-passage effect — the finding with the clearest deployment relevance — has no edge-level mechanism.
- **Metric scope:** single-token answers only (a constraint of gradient-based attribution); multi-token answers and free-generation conflict resolution are out of scope, limiting ecological validity.
- **Single corpus with domain skew:** everything derives from ParaConflict; Athlete–Sport is ~70% of surviving Llama/Qwen prompts, and two of six relations were dropped entirely by the tokenizer filter; no held-out-domain attribution or second corpus.
- **No causal training-side intervention:** the paper observes a correlational base↔instruct delta; it cannot say *which* ingredient of instruction tuning produces the memory-ward reweighting.

## Relevance

这篇论文与当前对 LLM 可解释性与可靠性的关注高度契合：它首次系统回答了"指令微调到底是重连还是只重加权知识冲突回路"这一机制问题，证明基座模型上校准的头级工具可直接迁移到指令模型，同时量化揭示了"指令模型更可信"这一直觉的脆弱性——面对连贯伪造文本时抗性几乎归零，对 RAG 信任校准、越权注入防御和模型编辑研究都有直接参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the gating verdict (and the Llama-vs-Qwen/Gemma edge sub-type split) survive at 8B–70B scale, where post-training pipelines are substantially heavier?
- What is the edge-level mechanism of the coherent-passage framing effect — do fluent false passages recruit additional early/mid-layer heads that bypass the late-layer context/memory circuit?
- Can the 5-head context set serve as a deployment-time guard for RAG systems — e.g., monitoring or steering those heads to recalibrate context trust per-source?
- Which ingredient of post-training (SFT data scale, RLHF preference optimization, chat templating) drives the memory-ward reweighting? A controlled tuning intervention would convert the correlational delta into a causal one.
- Is the rising "neither" abstention rate under terse conflict a calibrated-uncertainty improvement or an artifact of suppressed context heads?
