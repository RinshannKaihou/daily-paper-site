Why This Matters

The most complete realization of verifiable‑reward recursive self‑improvement beyond text — and proof that reward variance helps only when labels stay faithful.

「可验证奖励 + 自适应课程 + 递归自改进」这条线在音频模态最完整的落地；其消融发现对所有模态的 RL 自进化都有借鉴价值。

Critical Edge

Gains concentrate exactly where the 24 tools mirror benchmark skills — +17.0 on perception but +3.0 on semantic reasoning: skill‑targeted tutoring more than general audio understanding.

Also unquantified: single‑seed runs with no error bars, a promotion gate certified only on the synthetic distribution, and a compute bill never totaled.

01 The Loop · 方法论

  1. Profile the solver

    Score the current model on 47 fixed skills; per‑skill accuracy and round‑over‑round deltas stay comparable across the whole lineage.

  2. Propose priorities

    A DeepSeek‑V4‑Flash proposer reads the profile and returns per‑skill priority ρ plus an easier / harder recommendation.

  3. Compile the curriculum

    q ∝ min(ρ·m, 0.06) — reweight by reward‑variance “mixed rate”, cap any one skill, and hold 12% of budget on mastered skills against forgetting.

  4. Steer difficulty

    Continuous acoustic controls (pitch interval, SNR) step toward a target success rate p* = 0.5, estimated from pilot rollouts.

  5. Construct & verify

    24 tools in 6 families render the waveform so the answer is fixed by construction (e.g. the sign of a drawn pitch ratio). An independent acoustic check re‑measures pitch / rate / loudness / onsets / tempo, Whisper confirms scripts, and a text‑only probe drops shortcut questions. Keep only items solved in 1–3 of 4 rollouts.

  6. Train & promote

    GRPO with +1/0/−1 rewards, KL 0.02 vs parent, lr 1e‑6. Candidates at 32 and 64 steps; promote only if it beats the parent on a fixed, gradient‑free 2,500‑question held‑out set sharing no templates or seeds.

↻  The promoted solver reshapes the next curriculum — 13 rounds · 4 nodes × 8 H800 per run.

03 Strengths · 优势

  • +Fair, budget‑matched baselines — identical GRPO budgets; the SFT variant keeps the exact same curricula.
  • +Verifiability engineered end‑to‑end — construction‑fixed answers, acoustic re‑measurement, Whisper checks, shortcut filtering.
  • +Consistent ordering across five architecturally diverse backbones — it transfers, not exploits one model’s quirks.
  • +Transferable ablation insight — reward variance is necessary but not sufficient; variance toward unfaithful answers is poison for RL.
  • +Honest failure analysis — flat late rounds explained; SFT warm‑start named as the fix, not hidden.

04 Limitations · 局限

  • −Diminishing returns — biggest jump is round 1 on 4/5 backbones; near‑flat by round 13.
  • −Tool library sets the ceiling — semantic / cultural reasoning unreachable (MMAR +3.0 vs MMSU +6.1).
  • −Synthetic‑to‑real gap never measured — all waveforms are TTS / procedural; no held‑out real‑recording evaluation.
  • −Uneven benefit — MiniCPM‑o 4.5 gains only +1.4, with regressions.
  • −Compute‑heavy, cost untotaled — 32 H800 × 13 rounds × 5 backbones × 4 variants.
  • −Single‑seed results, no error bars; the LLM proposer’s marginal value over heuristics is not isolated.

05 Questions for Further Exploration · 探索问题

  1. Would an SFT warm start before GRPO unlock skills where pure RL finds zero reward contrast — the authors’ own stated next step?
  2. How large is the synthetic‑to‑real gap if the curriculum mixes in unmodified real recordings alongside synthesized ones?
  3. Can construction‑based tools ever reach the semantic and cultural reasoning MMAR tests, or is that outside the paradigm?
  4. How much does the DeepSeek‑V4‑Flash proposer add over a purely heuristic learning‑progress curriculum?
  5. Does the loop keep improving past 13 rounds, or does the promotion gate start rejecting all candidates once easy variance is exhausted?
  6. Would a small, contamination‑controlled real‑audio validation probe make promotion decisions more predictive?
