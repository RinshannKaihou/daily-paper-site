# Propose, Don't Judge: An Anytime-Valid Referee for LLM Agents That Mine Investment Factors

> **一句话总结：** 提出"受治理的自演化"分工框架——LLM 智能体只负责提出因子、诊断故障、编写探针，而由一个智能体无法触碰的"冻结统计裁判"（基于 e-过程投注的在线 e-BH 准入 + e-检测器退役）负责判断：在 CSI 500 十年 walk-forward 实验中，冻结裁判每期仅准入 11.7 个虚假因子，而三种泄漏式裁判（窥探 t 检验 / 自适应阈值 / 无门槛）多达 86–196 个（5–11 倍），且任何提议者都无法弥补这一差距；代价是真实因子平均需等待约 500 个交易日才获认证，认证组合净 Sharpe（+0.33–0.50）跑输无门槛组合（+0.59–0.77）。

> **TL;DR:** The paper splits the LLM factor-mining pipeline into "propose" (the agent: scripts, bandits, or LLMs) and "judge" (a frozen statistical referee the agent cannot touch), where admission uses online e-BH over betting e-processes on post-submission daily rank-IC, and retirement uses e-detectors against a registered viability threshold δ=0.015. On a ten-year point-in-time CSI 500 walk-forward (2016–2019 starts, 5 seeds) plus a planted-truth synthetic world, the frozen referee admits 11.7 realised false factors per campaign vs 86.2–196.0 for the three leaky referees (peeking t-test, adaptive threshold, no gate) under a round-robin proposer — a 5–11× reduction no proposer closes — while the LLM proposer's measurable value is yield (13.3 vs 11.7 true factors vs round-robin, beating the script in 6/6 settings but only matching the discounted-UCB bandit) and authoring its own diagnostic probes (intervention regret down 0.388/0.248/0.233 in 3 of 6 evaluable model families). The certificate's price: ~500 trading days median wait per admitted true factor and a certified book net Sharpe of +0.33–0.50 vs +0.59–0.77 for the ungated book, largely because slow daily certification misses momentum.

## 核心贡献

- **可冻结的裁判（C1）：** 裁判只依据提交之后才揭示的市场结果（每日 rank-IC）对候选因子"投注"打分——资本即证据（检验鞅 + Ville 不等式），准入用在线 e-BH（冻结 2000 槽位、α=0.05），退役用以 δ=0.015 为零假设的 e-检测器（目标平均运行长度 A*=1260 天）。由此虚假发现率保证在任意停止时刻、对任意提议策略（包括会自我修改的 LLM）都成立。
- **分离两个效应的因子实验（C2）：** 3 个提议者（round-robin 脚本 / 折扣 UCB bandit / LLM 控制器）× 4 个裁判（冻结 + 窥探 / 自适应阈值 / 无门槛三种刻意泄漏）× 3 个环境（合成真值世界 / 探针环境 / CSI 500 真实数据），用共同随机数做配对比较，把"谁审判"与"谁提议"的影响分开度量。
- **对 LLM 价值的诚实度量（C3）：** LLM 在 6/6 个 family×library 设定中产量超过脚本、与 bandit 大体持平；其 bandit 不具备的能力是"编写自己的诊断探针"——在 3/6 个可评估模型家族中显著降低干预遗憾，其余无差别。
- **十年真实数据研究 + 证书定价（C4）：** CSI 500 上认证组合不亏钱但 alpha 无法与零区分（Newey–West t 从未达到 1），认证与未认证组合的差距被归因于两个机制：等待期错配（真实因子中位等待约 500 交易日）与"每日最强的统计量（短期反转）恰是付费最差的家族"。
- **命题 1（任意提议策略下的有效性）与命题 2（执行层中性）：** 前者给出 LLM 在环不能破坏保证的四个条件；后者证明执行层在所有 e-过程下游、组合可从追加日志零成本重算（220 个 LLM 单元逐项复现裁判层指标）。

## Key Contributions

- **A referee that can be frozen (C1):** Scores each candidate factor only on post-submission data via a betting e-process on daily rank-IC; admission is online e-BH over a frozen 2000-slot universe (α=0.05) and retirement is an e-detector (Shin et al. 2024) whose null is the registered viability threshold δ=0.015 with average run-length target A*=1260 days. The false-discovery guarantee over all admitted factors holds at every stopping time for any proposer, including a self-modifying LLM.
- **An experiment that separates the two jobs (C2):** Three proposers (round-robin script, discounted-UCB bandit, LLM controller) crossed with four referees (frozen plus three deliberately leaky ones — peeking t-test, adaptive threshold, no gate) across three environments (planted-truth synthetic world, probe environment, ten-year CSI 500 walk-forward), with common random numbers so governance and intelligence effects are separately readable.
- **A measurement of what the LLM actually adds (C3):** The LLM beats the script on yield in six of six family×library settings and every start year, is level with the bandit, and its distinctive capability is instrument-making: authoring its own perception probes lowers intervention regret by 0.388/0.248/0.233 in three of six evaluable model families (Holm-corrected), and is indistinguishable from a fixed diagnosis menu in the rest.
- **A ten-year real-data study that prices the certificate (C4):** On CSI 500 the certified book earns money net of registered costs but its alpha (+0.25 to +1.13 bp/day) is never distinguishable from zero (Newey–West t below 1); the certified-vs-ungated Sharpe gap is decomposed into a horizon mismatch (median ~500-day wait) and a bar-selection effect (the fastest-certified family, short-horizon reversal, nets −19.6%/year re-formed daily).
- **Two propositions:** Proposition 1 states the four conditions under which any proposal policy (scripted, bandit, LLM-driven, self-modifying) leaves the guarantees intact; Proposition 2 proves execution-layer neutrality — the book is recomputable from the append-only decision log without re-running the agent (all 220 LLM cells reproduced referee-level metrics exactly).

## Methodology

**Division of labour.** The pipeline is split into a *controller* (swappable agent surface: allocation across factor families, candidate construction, diagnosis, probe authoring, memory) and a *referee* (frozen trust kernel: admission via e-BH, retirement via e-detector), plus a frozen execution layer and an append-only decision log with a cost meter. Anything used on day t must be G_{t−1}-measurable ("alignment invariant").

**The referee (Section 4).** Evidence is treated as capital: each candidate's daily rank-IC X_t ∈ [−1,1] on its post-submission stream is bet on, W_t = Π(1 + λ_s X_s) with G_{s−1}-measurable stakes (aGRAPA stake capped at φ=0.8 of Kelly), making W a test supermartingale under the zero-edge null; admission when capital crosses the e-BH bar N_v/(kα). Because raw-stream betting over-admits under autocorrelation (6.0% at ρ=0.2, 15.4% at ρ=0.4 vs nominal 5%), the referee bets on the AR(1)-whitened stream, restoring 1.8–2.4%. Retirement runs a new e-process from every day j after admission betting against the factor with null "edge ≥ δ_t = δ(1−ρ_t)"; M_t = Σ W^(j) alarms at A*=1260. The viability threshold δ = 2c·TO/κ̄ uses a market-level conversion κ̄=0.018 and 15 bp per-side costs; δ=0.015 was registered before the runs. Proposition 1 gives the four conditions (measurable stakes, post-submission data only, frozen library within an epoch, no foreknowledge of post-submission outcomes) under which an adaptive proposer cannot break FDR or run-length guarantees.

**Execution layer (Section 5).** Each deployed factor forms a quintile long-short sleeve; a deliberately slow grid search picks the holding frequency from five desk frequencies every B=250 days on trailing data; sleeves that cannot pay at any frequency are shelved. Proposition 2 shows the execution layer is downstream of every bet, so books are recomputable at zero marginal cost.

**Controllers (Section 6).** C0 round-robin cycles families in fixed order; C1 discounted-UCB bandit allocates by discounted admission-minus-retirement reward; C2 LLM controller adds three roles under a fixed scaffold — Direction (allocation using family profiles), Diagnosis (labelling retirement causes), and Probe authoring (writing sandboxed diagnostic programs; probes never enter admission or retirement inference).

**Experimental design (Section 7).** (i) *Synthetic world*: four families with true-factor proportions 0.50/0.25/0.10/0.05, AR(1) ρ=0.2, a mid-campaign regime break, 1260-day campaigns, 5 seeds. (ii) *Probe environment*: five held-out fault types (blind spots of the fixed diagnosis menu), 20 episodes each, scored by intervention regret; seven LLM model families sampled. (iii) *Real data*: point-in-time CSI 500, 2015-01 to 2026-08; four-family library (momentum, short-horizon reversal, volatility, liquidity) and nine-family (+value, size, quality, growth, investment); campaigns start 2016–2019, 5 seeds, submissions every 21 days; 540 cells (320 baseline, 220 LLM across DeepSeek/Gemini/GPT), every LLM call recorded so cells replay at zero cost. Every comparison is a paired difference under common random numbers with Holm correction within model family; families are never pooled. Fixed in advance: α, δ, A*, cap fraction, grids, analysis plan; κ̄ and the execution layer were constructed in-sample after a first pass.

## Results & Highlights

- **The referee, not the controller, sets false admissions (synthetic):** Under the frozen referee both LLM families admit 0.000 false factors per submission in every seed; the three leaky referees admit 0.26–0.85 per submission — identical across round-robin, bandit and LLM (Table 8). Realised FDR proportion is 0 in every seed under the frozen referee vs 0.75–0.95 under the leaky ones.
- **The gap survives ten years of real prices (CSI 500, four-family library, per campaign, round-robin):** frozen referee admits 11.7 realised false factors vs 86.2 (peeking), 105.2 (adaptive threshold) and 196.0 (no gate) — a 5–11× reduction; under the LLM proposer it is 11.7 vs 37.4–78.0 (2.6–4.9×). All 100 frozen-referee cells had zero negative-realised-edge admissions, vs 10.8/32.0/90.8 under the leaky referees (round-robin).
- **No proposer substitutes for the judge:** An attacking controller that resubmits unadmitted candidates under new identifiers extracts +0.027 false admissions per submission from the peeking referee and +0.009 from the adaptive threshold, but none from the frozen one. Yield under the frozen referee is identical across controllers (0.095 round-robin, 0.096 bandit, 0.096 LLM, ±0.002); the no-gate arm's yield collapses to 0.042.
- **The LLM's yield value is real but bounded:** It finds more true factors than round-robin in six of six family×library settings and every start year (e.g. 13.3 vs 11.7 admitted true factors per campaign under the frozen referee; realised yield 0.47 vs 0.40, and 0.59 vs 0.37 under no gate — Table 2), but is level with the bandit and loses to it in one nine-family family in 3 of 4 starts. A period split at 2020-01-02 shows the LLM leads the bandit before the split and trails after, in all three model families.
- **Probe authoring is the clearest agent-only capability:** Authored probes lower intervention regret by 0.388, 0.248 and 0.233 in three of six evaluable model families (significant after Holm correction); differences are −0.067, −0.021, +0.074 in the other three; one of seven sampled families never produced a probe within budget. Memory controls: six memory arms differ by at most ±0.0025 yield with 0 of 10 tests significant.
- **The certificate has a price:** An admitted true factor waits a median ~500 trading days (an edge exactly at δ=0.015 needs ~1434 days vs 1356 for an uncapped Kelly bettor — no bettor can do much better). The certified book's net Sharpe is +0.33 to +0.50 (deflated Sharpe 0.49–0.69; alpha t-stats never reach 1) vs +0.59 to +0.77 for the ungated book, which ranks first in every start year.
- **Why the gap (Table 4):** The ungated book held 892 momentum sleeves at +1.69 bp/day while the frozen book held 6 at −4.35 bp/day — momentum's daily rank-IC (0.001) is an order of magnitude below δ=0.015 though it rises to 0.009 by day 63 and pays only at monthly-or-longer holds. Reversal, the fastest-certifying family, nets −19.6%/year re-formed daily. A counterfactual 500-day comparator with the same patience builds a better book (net Sharpe +0.59 to +0.65 vs +0.40 to +0.49, paired gain +0.12 to +0.24 in every start year) while admitting fewer reversals (72.0 vs 87.2), showing the loss is the daily horizon of the certificate, not the wait per se.

## Authors & Credibility

Bo Qu (corresponding author, DeepGrounding), Mingguang Chen (DeepGrounding), Licheng Wang (AlphaAvatar) — industry-affiliated researchers rather than an academic lab. The work shows unusual methodological care: thresholds (α, δ=0.015, A*=1260) registered before the runs, pre-specified design/analysis plan, Holm-corrected paired tests that never pool families, an explicit "what was fixed before the data" section, recorded LLM calls enabling zero-cost replay, honest negative results (the certified portfolio's alpha is not distinguishable from zero; the LLM's edge over the bandit inverts after 2020), and a limitations list that bounds each claim. Credibility is reinforced by correct engagement with the e-value literature (Wang & Ramdas 2022; Waudby-Smith & Ramdas 2024; Shin et al. 2024) and by deliberately building the strawman leaky referees as controlled ablations rather than attacking weakman versions of opponents. Caveats: the paper cites several companion 2026 preprints by overlapping authors (e.g. Chen, Wang & Qu 2026; Qu & Chen 2026), and code/data are available only "on request" with market data non-redistributable (licensed from a commercial vendor).

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty.** The framing — which jobs in a factor lifecycle belong to the agent vs a statistical procedure — is genuinely useful, and applying anytime-valid inference to an *external-hypothesis* stream (rather than to an agent's own modifications, as SEA/PACE do) is a real distinction. But the paper is explicit that it extends no theory: Proposition 1 is assembled from existing results (stopped e-BH, e-detectors, e-value FDR) and "is not a new theorem." The novelty is the system composition and the measurement, not the mathematics.

**Rigor.** High by agentic-ML standards: registered parameters, paired common-random-number comparisons, Holm correction, no pooling of families, two artefact controls (memory arms, decoding forks), and an attacking-controller stress test. Three soft spots: (1) the *realised* truth label on real data overlaps the admission window and leans toward the frozen referee — the paper's own re-judging cuts its headline 5–11× advantage to 4–17× on one label and to two-to-threefold on per-family break-evens; (2) the κ̄=0.018 conversion constant and the execution layer were constructed *in-sample* after a first pass over recorded cells, and the canonical book makes no out-of-sample claim; (3) the fixed-horizon comparator runs counterfactually on the frozen referee's own candidate sequence, not its own campaign.

**Reproducibility.** Weak-to-moderate. Code, manifests, prompts and recorded model calls are available only on request from the corresponding author, and the CSI 500 data cannot be redistributed at all, so the real-data results are effectively un-verifiable by outsiders; the synthetic and probe environments are the only reproducible parts. The "about twenty-five agentic factor-mining systems appeared in six months" motivating claim is asserted "by our count" without a list.

**Significance.** The central finding — that governance (who judges) dominates intelligence (who proposes) for false admissions, while LLM value concentrates in yield and instrument-making — is a crisp, transferable claim for the broader self-evolving-agents field, and the honest pricing of the certificate (~500-day wait; Sharpe below the ungated book) is a service the field rarely performs.

**Red flags.** The LLM real-data arm is a *historical replay*: models trained before or during the campaign years may violate condition (iv) (no information about post-submission streams), which the authors concede means the guarantee does not attach to these runs and results are "replay outcomes." The leakage-direction check (LLM leads pre-2020, trails post-2020) is "an interpretation, not a test." The diagnosis role is never ablated on its own on real data (the LLM bundle also sees family profiles and memory the bandit lacks); the real-data LLM column is one model family per vendor; and one of seven probe families was inevaluable because it never produced a probe — selection among evaluable models is not discussed.

## Strengths

- Clean conceptual contribution: a frozen, anytime-valid referee whose FDR and retirement-run-length guarantees hold at every stopping time for *any* proposer, with the conditions for an agent-in-the-loop stated as an explicit proposition.
- The factorial 3-proposer × 4-referee × 3-environment design with common random numbers cleanly separates governance effects from intelligence effects — the right experiment for the question asked.
- Unusually honest economics: the certified portfolio is shown to *lose* to the ungated book on Sharpe, and the gap is mechanistically decomposed (wait cost + bar selecting short-horizon reversal; momentum held 6 sleeves at −4.35 bp/day vs the ungated 892 at +1.69 bp/day).
- Real measurement of a specific LLM capability (probe authoring) with held-out fault types, Holm-corrected paired effects, and null results reported alongside positives.
- Whitening the stream before betting is a subtle but important fix: it exposes and repairs a real failure mode (6.0%/15.4% over-admission at ρ=0.2/0.4 on the raw stream).

## Limitations

- One market (CSI 500), four start years that share most of their data, and no held-out period beyond the walk-forward; the authors name a second market as "the natural next study."
- The realised truth label on real data overlaps the admission window and favours late-admitting referees; post-admission re-judging shrinks the headline advantage by about a third, and per-family break-even labels shrink it to 2–3×.
- The LLM runs the leaky referees only on the four-family library; the fixed-horizon comparator is a counterfactual on the frozen referee's candidate sequence; the diagnosis role is not separately ablated.
- Real-data LLM results are replays — the validity guarantee attaches only to live deployment where no proposer can know the post-submission stream.
- The guarantee covers the conditional null on the whitened stream; marginal-null control under autocorrelation is measured (1.8–2.4%), not proved; the cluster-merge option is never exercised.
- Retirement is calibrated only on synthetic decay; the ARL guarantee gives no usable bound within a single campaign, and the false-retirement rate rises to 8.5% at ρ=0.4.
- The portfolio supports no investability claim: alpha t-statistics below 1 in every group, a cost model with no borrow fee, and an execution layer constructed in-sample; market data is licensed and non-redistributable, code is on-request only.

## Relevance

这篇论文正处在"LLM 智能体自动化研究"与"统计严谨性"的交叉点：它把 anytime-valid 推断（e-过程、在线 e-BH、e-检测器）用作 LLM 因子挖掘智能体的治理层，并定量回答了"智能体应该保留哪些职责"。对关注 LLM agent 自演化评估、多重检验/FDR 控制或量化因子自动挖掘的读者，这是少数把三者结合并诚实定价的实证工作。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- The authors propose certifying "the horizon that is traded, not the daily one" — what does an e-process on multi-day-horizon rank-IC look like, and does it recover momentum without losing anytime validity?
- Does the 5–11× false-admission gap and the ~500-day certificate wait replicate on a second market (e.g. US equities) with different microstructure and turnover profiles?
- Can the diagnosis role be ablated on its own (allocation vs diagnosis vs probe authoring separately) on real data, given the LLM bundle also receives family profiles and memory the bandit lacks?
- The LLM's advantage over the bandit inverts after 2020 — is this an overtaken prior, or does a periodically refreshed family profile restore the lead?
- How does the referee behave under adversarial proposers with side information (foretelling), beyond the unit-tested Wang et al. 2025 counterexample — e.g. a proposer conditioning on partially overlapping correlated streams?
- Would live (non-replay) deployment with current models, whose training cutoffs precede the campaign, change the controller comparison — and can the recorded-call replay infrastructure be released to let others verify the real-data cells?
