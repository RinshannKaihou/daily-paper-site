Methodology how the loop is braked

A·B·C Propose→ D Screen diff→ Evaluate on Devolve→ E·F·G Accept / prune ↺ ×T — same finite set, reused adaptively

An agent = frozen policy π + mutable harness H (prompts, control flow, tools, memory). Each round re-runs the same evolve set and promotes the argmax — adaptive empirical optimization, where evaluation noise and leakage harden into permanent harness state. RRSI constrains the search trajectory, not the hypothesis space:

Proposal side

  • A · L0 — Annealed edit budget: independently attributable edits per candidate cosine-annealed 4 → 1; late rounds stay sparse.
  • B — Evidence ledger: every edit logged (component, hypothesis, ΔS/ΔC, verdict); falsified mechanisms become negative evidence, never re-proposed.
  • C — Structured exploration: 3 stalled rounds inside noise band δ open a draft slot for unexercised components.

Selection side

  • D — Leakage screening: a critic rejects diffs encoding task names or answers — before they can collect an inflated score.
  • E — Noise floor: accept only if Ŝ ≥ S* − δ, δ calibrated from repeated base-harness runs.
  • F · L2 — Cost-gain rule: ΔC ≤ β₀ + β₁·ΔS — added inference cost must buy measured points.
  • G · L1 — Structural pruning: components without strictly positive recent gain become deletion targets.

Protocol

3 domains (coding / agentic workspace / engineering design) · 8 benchmarks · T = 20–40 rounds, k = 2–4 trials · policies frozen at Claude Opus 4.8 & Gemini 3.5 Flash · all baselines share H₀, evolve set and candidate budget. Results →

Cost & scope of the evidence

2.42M tok/trial · 26.3 steps — cheaper than every baseline (2.69–3.82M · 27.3–34.6); AHE spends 58% more for 4.4 fewer OOD points. Honest caveat: H₀ still cheapest at 1.56M.
Cross-strength transfer: harness evolved on Gemini 3.5 Flash lifts the never-searched Gemini 3.1 Flash Lite 11.2 → 14.6 (+30.4% rel) — a program, not a policy artifact.
Not judge-gaming: gains survive deterministic grading (Frontier-Eng +4.3, EngDesign +4.9) and exact unit tests (SWE-bench +1.8).
Ablation direction: removing acceptance-side regularizers hurts most (OOD 41.0, 3.59M tokens); removing both returns to 40.3 — unregulated selection spends edits on noise and context growth.

Strengths

  • The right problem, stated precisely: harness evolution formalized as adaptive empirical optimization — classical generalization concerns imported into automated harness engineering.
  • Transfer-first design: one evolve suite per domain, harness frozen and replayed on held-out/OOD splits; a deterministic-grading domain included specifically to kill the judge-artifact explanation.
  • Two policy families + cross-strength test (Gemini 3.5 Flash → 3.1 Flash Lite, +30.4% rel) — evidence of a reusable mechanism, the exact property RSI claims to want.
  • Efficiency measured, not asserted: RRSI is Pareto-dominant over all four baselines — cheaper and better OOD.
  • Unusually complete transparency: round-by-round records, accepted/rejected diffs with reasons, and full hyperparameter tables released.

Limitations read before adopting

  • Analogy, not math: the seven “regularizers” are prompt-enforced heuristics; δ varies 5× across domains (0.004–0.020) and no single-hyperparameter sensitivity is reported.
  • Coarse ablation: only two groups, one domain — per-regularizer contributions and the minimal effective set are unknown.
  • The evolve-split loss is real: 90.5 vs 93.0 (Meta-Harness) — practitioners optimizing one benchmark will give up in-distribution points.
  • One evolve environment per domain; frozen-backbone only — “RSI” here is strictly harness-level.
  • Judge-graded share: the biggest OOD deltas (+4.7/+3.5/+3.7) sit on LLM-judged benchmarks; k=2 trials leave residual selection noise; baselines are one-paragraph re-implementations.

Questions for further exploration

  • Which single regularizer carries most of the +3.9 OOD gain — is noise floor + leakage screening alone within a point of full RRSI?
  • Does δ calibration survive k=1 trials, or T ≫ 20 rounds of adaptive reuse?
  • Do gains survive a different harness family (planner-executor, multi-agent) or are they entangled with H₀'s ReAct loop?
  • Can judged-benchmark gains survive adversarial judging — and does the leakage critic catch distribution-level stylistic fitting?
  • What persists once the evolve set is refreshed mid-run, when the search can no longer memorize a fixed finite set?
  • Does the +30.4% cross-strength transfer hold for still-weaker backbones, or shrink with the base harness's headroom?
