Why this matters

按主题分层的基准与按主题平衡的语料，可能漏掉模型内部真正依赖的「解法」轴。
Critical edge

The “approach” taxonomy is judge-invented: two LLM judges author the labels and then certify them on five exemplars per cluster — no human ground truth anywhere.

01 Methodology generation-replay protocol

  1. 1
    Generate

    Greedy decoding, ≤8,196 tokens. 3,800 prompts × 8 models = 30,460 solutions; the identical prompt set is reused across all models.

  2. 2
    Replay

    One teacher-forced forward/backward pass over the exact prompt+generation sequence; causal-LM loss restricted to reasoning tokens only — sampling variance removed.

  3. 3
    Signatures

    Per module: mean L1 activation, RMS, and activation × parameter-normalized gradient norm, concatenated. Raw gradients never used standalone.

  4. 4
    Cluster

    Per model-source cell: ℓ&sub2;-normalize → TruncatedSVD(20) → K-Means, k∈{5…12} by silhouette. Fully unsupervised — topic, sub-skill, and correctness labels withheld.

03 Strengths

  • The dissociation control is decisive: the identical pipeline on TF-IDF text vs activations (81.8% vs 36.9% topic-pure, ARI 0.38 vs 0.10) rules out “just clustering surface similarity.”
  • Pre-registered thresholds for all three tests, stated before the analyzers ran; matched-size random nulls block the “it’s just imbalance” objection.
  • Two independent cross-family judges agree on both the aggregate pattern and its per-source ordering, calibrated with identically-formatted control bundles.
  • C3 is a true intervention, not correlation — and the one failing model (Mistral-7B) is reported honestly rather than hidden.
  • Identical 3,800-prompt set across all 8 models isolates model-specific differences; unusually complete reproducibility package incl. judge prompts.

04 Limitations

  • No human ground truth: “approach coherence” is certified exclusively by LLM judges on 5-exemplar bundles — the taxonomy itself is judge-generated.
  • Modest absolute structure: mean silhouette 0.204; clusters are real vs the null but heavily overlapping.
  • Prompt-based intervention only — no parameters, heads, or circuits are identified.
  • The 36.9% aggregate is inflated: GSM8K (one sub-skill) contributes 43 of 83 topic-pure clusters; genuinely multi-topic sources fall to 9.4%/1.2%.
  • Scope: open-weight math models, five math sources; no closed models, no non-math domains.
  • Correctness is excluded by design, so whether approach-clusters predict accuracy or error modes is asserted, never demonstrated.

05 Questions for further exploration

  • Would human annotators endorse the judges’ approach labels? A small human-validated C2 subset would convert the strongest caveat into evidence.
  • Do approach-cluster memberships predict accuracy or error modes — is the axis causal, not just descriptive?
  • Can the approach axis be localized to specific heads / MLP blocks, linking population-level signatures to circuit accounts?
  • Does the text-topic / activation-approach dissociation hold for closed frontier models, code generation, or non-math reasoning?
  • Would corpora rebalanced over approach clusters (not topics) measurably improve downstream math accuracy or generalization?
  • What explains Mistral-7B’s paraphrase-invariance failure (0.48 vs ≥0.70 elsewhere) — training regime, tokenizer, or scale?
