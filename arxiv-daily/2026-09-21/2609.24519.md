> **一句话总结：** 针对 NVIDIA B200/B300 的 FP4 Tensor Core 无法存 INT8 操作数的问题，提出 AWE（Adaptive Weight Encoding）：抛弃固定的 base-13 位分解，让 limb 携带自由整数权重、用 limb 的线性组合平面做单次乘积项，把无误差整数矩阵乘所需的 FP4 GEMM 次数从 9 次降到 6 次（INT8×INT8，权重 1,29,37）、FP16 尾数从 16 次降到 12 次、FP64 尾数（剩余数系统/CRT 路线）从 75 次降到 59 次，并把剩余数系统占优的输入宽度分界从 11 推到 15；所有编码经独立精确有理数验证器全对校验并开源。

> **TL;DR:** Ozaki-scheme emulation needs error-free integer matrix products, but on Blackwell FP4 tensor cores the storable set S = {0,±1..±4,±6,±12} (scaled) rules out Karatsuba/Toom-Cook because limb sums reach ±24 and fall into the "holes" of S. AWE instead gives limbs free integer weights (e.g., INT8×INT8 uses weights 1,29,37 with coverage 127), turning the 9 FP4 GEMMs of the prior base-13 construction (Hayashi et al.) into 6 (1.5x fewer); FP16-significand products drop from 16 to 12 GEMMs (1.3x) and the FP64-significand CRT route from 75 to 59 at reduction length K=16,384. On B300 — whose 5th-gen tensor cores dropped INT8 — exact INT8×INT8 runs at 1.00x the native BF16 unit rate (2.25 POPS = 13.5 PFLOPS FP4 / 6), and BitNet-style [0,±1]×INT8 at 2.00x; on B200 the same construction is 0.33x the native INT8 unit (1.50 vs 4.5 POPS). Encodings, verification scripts, and environment hashes are released as JSONL records (github.com/TP4-is-All-you-Need/BLAS). All throughput numbers are theoretical peak-rate arithmetic, not measured GEMMs.

## 核心贡献

- **泛化表示（cell (p,q)）**：把"位分解"推广为带自由整数权重 w 的平面（planes）组合：要求 Σ λ_k(α_k·a)(β_k·b) = (w·a)(v·b) 对所有容许输入精确成立，重构系数限定为 λ_k = l_k/D（D 为 2 的幂，最终只是一次移位）。左右两侧编码独立搜索。
- **INT8×INT8 从 9 次 FP4 GEMM 降到 6 次**：权重 (1,29,37)、每侧 6 个平面、coverage 127，重构式 AB = −65P₀₀ − 251P₁₁ + 259P₂₂ + 29P₀₁ + 37P₀₂ + 1073P₁₂（D=1）。同目录还给出 INT4×INT4 用 3 次（直接分解要 4 次）、INT4×INT8 用 4 次（直接分解 6 次）、FP16 尾数 12 次（此前 16 次）。
- **剩余数系统（Ozaki II / CRT）的统一改进**：把恒等式放松为模 m 同余，模 11 与 13 也只需 1 次 FP4 乘积（此前仅到模 9）；FP64 尾数在 K=16,384 下从 75 次降到 59 次，且"剩余数系统 vs 直接分解"的盈亏平衡输入宽度从 11 提高到 15（15 ≤ K ≤ 65,636；宽度 15 处余量仅 2 次：18 对 20）。
- **精确累积条件与硬件 scale 编码**：给出 K ≤ K* = ⌊2^p/(c_A·c_B)⌋ 时 FP32 累积严格无舍入的判据（6-乘积编码封顶 ±6 时 K* = 466,033，用满 S 则只有 116,508）；并分析权重奇部 ≤15 时可吸收进 MXFP4 的 E8M0 / NVFP4 的 E4M3 块 scale，(1,29,37) 奇部超限故并入整数重构。
- **可复现的搜索-验证流程**：离线搜索（Algorithm 2）声明搜索范围 → gcd 过滤 → 精确有理数线性求解 → 覆盖穷举 → 全输入对校验；每个解由独立编写的验证器重新推导，记录（JSONL）、脚本、环境哈希全部公开；发布编码目录并将增量扩展。

## Key Contributions

- A general formulation of error-free integer matrix products on FP4 tensor cores: limbs carry freely chosen integer weights, stored planes are linear combinations of limbs, and the exact product is a weighted sum of FP4 GEMMs with power-of-two reconstruction shift (Definition IV.1, Algorithm 1).
- A verified catalogue of product counts per integer-width pair: INT8×INT8 in 6 FP4 GEMMs (weights 1,29,37) instead of the 9 of the base-13 positional encoding, INT4×INT4 in 3, INT4×INT8 in 4, FP16 significand in 12; the modulo-m relaxation covers residue-number-system (Ozaki II) constructions, cutting the FP64-significand CRT count from 75 to 59 at K = 16,384 and raising the break-even width of the residue approach from 11 to 15.
- A provable exact-accumulation criterion (K ≤ K* = ⌊2^p/(c_A c_B)⌋) and an analysis of which weights can be folded into MXFP4 (E8M0) / NVFP4 (E4M3) block scales versus absorbed into the integer reconstruction.
- An open, incrementally extended repository of encodings (JSONL records declaring the search range, evidence, and verification procedure) plus independent verification scripts; theoretical throughput tables for RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell (sm_120), B200 (sm_100), and B300 (sm_103).

## Methodology

The paper reformulates exact integer products on FP4 tensor cores. Doubling FP4 (E2M1) values gives the storable set S = {0, ±1, ±2, ±3, ±4, ±6, ±8, ±12}, which represents every residue mod 13; hence the prior construction (Hayashi et al., arXiv:2609.00812) stores 3 base-13 limbs (1, 13, 169) per INT8 operand and needs 3×3 = 9 FP4 GEMMs. Classical product-count reductions do not transfer: Karatsuba/Toom-Cook require sums of limbs to be storable, but sums reach ±24 and land in the "holes" of S, and Toom needs a contiguous significand of m ≥ 5 per point while INT8's m = 1.

AWE's four components: (1) **Generalized representation.** A scheme on cell (p, q) is a tuple of planes α_k, β_k, reconstruction coefficients λ_k = l_k/D (D a power of two, so the final division is an exact shift), and integer weights w, v, required to satisfy Σ λ_k (α_k·a)(β_k·b) = (w·a)(v·b) for all admissible inputs; the weight w defines the coverage (the contiguous half-width [-X, X] with X ≥ 2^(M-1) − 1 needed for integer width M). A and B are searched independently. (2) **Product-count reduction.** Combination planes of at most 2 terms (e.g., P_xy = (a_i + a_j)(b_i + b_j)) cover the 9 pairwise limb products with 6 planes per side; admissibility (all plane values in S) constrains the weights — this is exactly where Karatsuba fails and where the search succeeds. (3) **Block-scale encoding.** MXFP4 applies one E8M0 scale per 32 elements and NVFP4 one E4M3 scale per 16; a limb weight whose odd part is ≤ 15 fits E4M3 and can ride the hardware scale with no rounding, otherwise the weight is absorbed into the integer reconstruction (as for (1,29,37)). (4) **Search and verification.** Algorithm 2 declares the cell, candidate planes, and weight range; a gcd filter plus an exact-rational linear solve (fixing one side makes the system linear, avoiding enumeration) decides existence of λ; coverage is decided exhaustively; the identity is then checked over all admissible input pairs. Every solution is re-derived by an independently written verifier, and identity and storability are checked as separate claims. At run time (Algorithm 1) each entry is decomposed by table lookup, combination planes are formed by adds, r FP4-GEMMs are executed, and the accumulator is summed exactly in integer arithmetic before the shift by D. Relaxing the identity to congruence mod m handles residue number systems: moduli 2–9, 11, and 13 need a single FP4 product per modulus (mod 11 and 13 in one product improves on prior work), and the same machinery minimizes moduli products for the FP64 significand (82 moduli by three products each).

## Results & Highlights

- **INT8×INT8: 6 FP4 GEMMs vs 9 of the base-13 baseline (1.5x fewer).** Weights (1,29,37) with coverage 127, reconstruction AB = −65P₀₀ − 251P₁₁ + 259P₂₂ + 29P₀₁ + 37P₀₂ + 1073P₁₂; the catalogue also holds INT4×INT4 in 3 products (direct decomposition: 4) and INT4×INT8 in 4 (direct: 6).
- **FP16-significand exact product: 12 FP4 GEMMs vs 16 in Hayashi et al. (1.3x fewer)**, via weights (1, 71, 9, 639) at width M = 12 with D = 2 and K* = 116,508 — theoretical throughput 0.33x the native FP16 unit on sm_120 and B200, 0.50x on B300.
- **FP64-significand (CRT route): 59 moduli products at K = 16,384 vs 75 of prior work (1.3x fewer)**; the product-count reduction raises the break-even input width where residue systems beat direct decomposition from 11 to 15 (boundary 15 ≤ K ≤ 65,636; it drops to 14 for 1,352 ≤ K ≤ 1,567 and 13 for K ≤ 1,024; the margin at width 15 is two products, 18 vs 20).
- **Theoretical peak rates (Table II, not measurements):** on B300 (sm_103), which has no GPU-wide INT8 tensor-core instruction, exact INT8×INT8 via 6 FP4 GEMMs yields 13.5/6 = 2.25 POPS — equal to the 2.25-PFLOPS BF16 unit, i.e., 1.00x; INT4×INT8 reaches 3.38 POPS (1.50x) and BitNet-style [0,±1]×INT8 (1.58-bit weights in one limb, K* = 2,097,152) reaches 4.50 POPS (2.00x). On B200 the same INT8×INT8 construction is 0.33x the native INT8 unit (1.50 vs 4.5 POPS); on RTX PRO 6000 (sm_120) 0.336 vs 1.008 POPS, also 0.33x.
- **Exactness bound improves with the encoding:** capping every stored value at ±6 (still coverage 148) gives c_A = c_B = 6 and K* = 466,033, versus K* = 116,508 for encodings using all of S — a 4x longer guaranteed-exact reduction length for the same product count.

## Critical Assessment

- **All throughput results are theoretical peak-rate arithmetic, explicitly flagged "NOT MEASUREMENTS" in Table II.** There is no measured GEMM, no kernel, no end-to-end benchmark anywhere in the paper. The stated ratios exclude the run-time costs that decide real performance: forming the 6 combination planes (extra adds and Shared Memory/register traffic per element), table-lookup decomposition, and the integer reconstruction whose width can exceed 64 bits and must be segmented — the paper itself concedes "how much of it an implementation overlaps with other work is outside this paper." The 1.00x/2.00x B300 headlines are therefore ceilings, not achievable numbers.
- **The benefit is hardware-generation-dependent.** On B200 and RTX PRO 6000 the native INT8 unit exists and is exact (INT32 accumulation); AWE's exact INT8 runs at 0.33x of it. The compelling case only appears on B300, which removed the INT8 instruction, or for widths with no native unit (FP16 significand emulation). Buyers of this result should note it is a bet on future FP4-only machines.
- **The catalogue is openly incomplete and optimality is not claimed.** Figure 4 marks widths >17 and 24–53 as "not searched"; entries at M ≥ 13 come from restricted weight families and lifting rules whose cells "have not themselves been searched." Two problems are left open: no 5-product INT8×INT8 encoding was found within the searched plane families (independent components of absolute value ≤ 2, or components ≤ 3 with l1 norm ≤ 5), and whether {0, ±1} components suffice for the planes is a conjecture. The "1.5x" could in principle improve further — or the true optimum could sit between.
- **Baseline coverage is narrow but defensible:** the only meaningful prior construction is the authors' own base-13 encoding [8]; Karatsuba/Toom-Cook are ruled out by a proof (limb sums leave S), not by dismissal. Still, there is no comparison against simply doing 9-product base-13 on the same hardware with measured kernels, so the practical win of 6 vs 9 GEMMs is never demonstrated.
- **Rigor is genuinely high:** exhaustive all-pairs verification over declared ranges, exact-rational re-derivation by a separate verifier, identity and storability checked as independent claims — this is the strongest part of the paper and the reason the unmeasured status is survivable for a catalogue paper.

## Strengths

- A clean generalization (free limb weights + combination planes) that provably subsumes the positional baseline and unifies direct encodings with residue-number-system constructions via the modulo-m relaxation.
- Concrete, immediately usable encodings: INT8×INT8 in 6 GEMMs, FP16 significand in 12, FP64 CRT in 59 — each shipped with full coefficients in the appendix and repository.
- The exact-accumulation criterion K ≤ K* = ⌊2^p/(c_A c_B)⌋ turns "is my sum still exact?" into a one-line check, and the ±6-capped encoding quadruples K* (466,033 vs 116,508) at no product-count cost.
- Exemplary verification discipline: independent verifier, exact rational arithmetic, exhaustive input-pair checks, public records with environment hashes — a model for search-based numerical constructions.
- Timely hardware relevance: it quantifies exactly what is lost (and what is recoverable) when B300 drops INT8 tensor-core instructions, and gives BitNet-style {0,±1}×INT8 a 3-product path with K* = 2,097,152.

## Limitations

- No implementation, no measured performance: plane-production cost, memory traffic, and reconstruction segmentation are excluded from every ratio, so real-world speedups are unknown and likely well below the peak-rate table.
- Throughput ratios below 1.0 (0.33x–0.67x) on B200/RTX PRO 6000 wherever a native INT8 unit exists — the method is only attractive on INT8-less hardware (B300) or for emulated widths (FP16 significand on FP4-only machines).
- Catalogue incompleteness: widths beyond 17 and 24–53 are unsearched; higher-width entries rest on restricted families and lifting, so the counts are lower bounds of the search, not proven optima.
- Open conjectures ({0, ±1} plane components; existence of a 5-product INT8×INT8) leave the theoretical picture unsettled.
- Practical reduction lengths are far below K* for all constructions (the paper notes exactness "as it stands in practice"), so the fine-grained K* boundaries (15 vs 14 vs 13) matter only for very long reductions (K up to 65,636).

## Relevance

该工作直接对应 Blackwell 时代 FP4 Tensor Core（MXFP4/NVFP4）上的精确低精度计算问题，对理解 FP4/INT8 量化推理的精度与硬件边界、以及 BitNet 类 1.58-bit 大模型在 FP4-only 硬件上的高效精确执行很有参考价值；对于关注低精度 LLM 推理系统与 GPU 体系结构的读者，这是一篇少见的高质量底层算术工作。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- What fraction of the theoretical 1.00x–2.00x on B300 survives in a real CUDA implementation once combination-plane production and segmented integer reconstruction are paid — is there any workload where it beats native-int kernels end to end?
- Can the {0, ±1}-components conjecture be proven (or a counterexample found), and does a 5-product INT8×INT8 encoding exist outside the searched plane families (components ≤ 2, or ≤ 3 with l1 norm ≤ 5)?
- How does the 6-product INT8×INT8 encoding compose with real Ozaki-scheme FP64 emulation pipelines, where the per-modulus/product cost includes writing planes to Shared Memory — does the 9→6 saving actually shorten wall-clock DGEMM emulation?
- Does the 3-product [0,±1]×INT8 path (K* = 2,097,152) translate into a practical BitNet inference kernel on B300-class hardware, and how does it compare to simply upcasting 1.58-bit weights to FP8?
