> **一句话总结：** 这是一篇纯理论立场论文：作者论证"线性表示假设（LRH）"并非单一假设，而是由隐式选取的表示等价群所区分的一族假设，并将每个表示主张形式化为四元组 (G, M, F, P)——等价群 G、表示对象所在的 G-空间 M、G-等变的构造程序 F、G-不变的谓词 P——同时要求 G 必须包含架构自身允许的函数保持重参数化在该"读出点"诱导的作用（"架构地板"，例如注意力中 W_Q → ΛW_Q、W_K → Λ⁻ᵀW_K 的规范自由度）；据此作者用一张对称性审计表把余弦/CKA/CCA/范数/干预幅度等常用量各归入其最大不变群（G_sim / G_aff / G_iso），并点名审计了 Arditi et al. 差值均值方向"一量两用"、CAA 与 Activation Addition 归一化约定相反、KeyDiff 在 key 头空间使用非规范不变量、SVCCA 奇异值截断破坏仿射不变性等现行研究中隐式"换群"的失效模式。

> **TL;DR:** A position paper (no experiments, no benchmarks) arguing that the Linear Representation Hypothesis is a *family* of claims indexed by an implicit choice of equivalence group. It formalizes a representation claim as a tuple (G, M, F, P) — a G-space of mathematical objects, an equivariant procedure producing the object, and an invariant predicate — with admissibility requiring (A1) the factorized equivariance/invariance and (A2) that G contain the function-preserving reparameterizations the architecture already realizes. Key derived results: no nonzero GL(V)-equivariant map exists from dual space V* to V (Prop 3.2), the GL(d_head) query/key gauge admits no nonzero invariant bilinear form so per-head cosines and norms are not invariants of the computation (Cor 4.2), and a composed pipeline is admissible only under its most restrictive stage (Prop 4.3). A symmetry audit assigns common quantities to maximal preserving groups (cosine/CKA/grassmann distances only under G_sim; norms and intervention magnitudes only under G_iso; CCA under G_aff while SVCCA's SVD truncation breaks affine invariance), and case studies show Arditi et al.'s refusal direction switching G-space mid-analysis, CAA vs. Activation Addition adopting opposite normalization conventions, and KeyDiff relying on gauge-dependent head-space statistics. The deliverable is a reporting principle: state G, M, F, P, the reading point, and the composite symmetry.

## 核心贡献

- **论证 LRH 是一族假设而非单一假设**：位移表述（特征是 V 中的向量，仿射变换下 v → Av）、线性可解码性表述（探针是 V* 中的对偶向量，变换为 w → A⁻ᵀw）、叠加表述（h = b + Σᵢ xᵢWᵢ，隐坐标在 GL(V) 下本质不可辨识，稀疏惩罚只能将对称性约化为符号置换）把"特征"放进不同数学对象，对应不同变换规律；一维特征自然生活在射影空间 P(V)，k 维特征生活在 Grassmann 流形 Gr(k, V)。
- **形式化"表示主张"为 (G, M, F, P)**：程序 F 必须在 G 作用下等变、谓词 P 必须不变（式 (3) 的分解式条件强于复合谓词 P∘F 的不变性）；给出可容许性条件 (A1)/(A2) 与"可比性"定义——不同 G-空间上的主张即使都正确也不能互相支持。
- **提出"架构地板"并证明读出点依赖性（命题 4.1 / 推论 4.2）**：等价群必须包含架构函数保持重参数化群 Γ 在该读出点的像；在点积注意力的 query/key 位置，规范群为 GL(d_head)，它不允许任何非零不变双线性型（取 Λ = cI 可推出 b ≡ 0），因此以固定内积定义的角度/度量谓词在这些位置不是模型的不变量——RoPE 下规范虽缩小但仍各向异性，结论不变；同一变换在残差流处作用平凡、在 key 处非平凡，故读出点本身是主张的一部分。
- **复合分析流程的对称性演算（命题 4.3）**：整条流水线的可容许性必须对复合程序成立，且受最严格阶段限制——G_aff 下估计的方向 + G_sim 下余弦比较 + G_iso 下范数校准，合成的保证只有 G_iso。
- **对称性审计表（Table 1）与点名案例研究**：把秩/张成/CCA 归入 G_aff，把余弦/主角度/Grassmann 距离/线性 CKA 归入 G_sim，把范数/干预幅度/重构损失归入 G_iso，把坐标级稀疏惩罚归入隐坐标符号置换；并逐案审计 Arditi et al.、Bhalke et al.、CAA vs. Activation Addition、Wollschläger et al.、KeyDiff、Li et al.、SVCCA、Yamawaki et al.。
- **把同一遗漏延伸到 Platonic Representation Hypothesis**：跨模型比较经由由共享输入分布索引的邻域/相似度结构进行，"收敛到同一等价类"中的对象、群与比较程序均未指明；宽度 ≥ 采样数且行满秩时，对任意可逆线性变换不变的相似度无法区分任何两个表示（Kombol et al.），有限样本收敛退化为平凡。

## Key Contributions

- **The family observation, made precise.** Displacement, decodability, and superposition formulations of "linear representation" place features in different mathematical objects (V, V*, latent coordinates in P(V)/Gr(k, V)) with different transformation laws; and a difference-in-means direction and a PCA leading direction can live in the same space while being equivariant under different groups (no inner product required for the former, required for the latter).
- **A formal calculus for representation claims.** Claim = (G, M, F, P) with factorized equivariance/invariance (Eq. 3); admissibility = (A1) internal consistency + (A2) the group contains the architecture-induced equivalences at the declared reading point; comparability requires the same equivalence relation *and* the same G-space, with an explicit equivariant map otherwise. The hierarchy G_iso ⊂ G_sim ⊂ G_aff calibrates claim strength: a larger group leaves fewer quantities invariant.
- **The architectural floor (Prop 4.1, Cor 4.2).** If a predicate varies across the orbit of function-preserving reparameterizations, it is a property of the parameterization, not the model. In dot-product attention, W_Q → ΛW_Q and W_K → Λ⁻ᵀW_K preserve the model function for any Λ ∈ GL(d_head); at the query/key site Im ρ = GL(d_head) admits no nonzero invariant bilinear form, so angular and metric predicates from a fixed inner product are not invariant there — the same transformation acts trivially at the residual stream.
- **Composition (Prop 4.3).** Admissibility must be established for the composite pipeline, which is limited by its most restrictive stage (estimate under G_aff + cosine under G_sim + norm calibration under G_iso ⇒ guaranteed only under G_iso).
- **A symmetry audit + case studies of current practice.** Table 1 maps common quantities to their maximal preserving group; Section 6 documents three recurring failure patterns: identification by storage format (probe weights in V* vs. steering vectors in V, conflated because both are length-d arrays — e.g., Bhalke et al. compare them by cosine and combine additively), hidden normalization choices (CAA normalizes across behaviours but not layers; Activation Addition does not normalize at all, so the same coefficient denotes different displacements under the two conventions; Wollschläger et al. explicitly calibrate the refusal-vector norm and sample within refusal cones), unnamed reading points (KeyDiff's pairwise key cosines, L² norms, key-cache PCA, and log det(KᵀK) are not invariant under the head gauge, whereas Yamawaki et al.'s column-span comparisons are), and mid-analysis inconsistency (Arditi et al. use one difference-in-means estimate both as an unnormalized displacement in V and as a projective direction in P(V); SVCCA's singular-value truncation destroys the affine invariance its CCA stage relies on).
- **Extension to the Platonic Representation Hypothesis** and a **reporting principle**: state the G-space, the procedure and why it is equivariant, the predicate, the reading point, and check composite symmetry — recoverable from the statement of the claim, not from reverse-engineering the implementation.

## Methodology

This is a purely analytical/theoretical methodology paper: there are **no datasets, no trained models, no measurements, and no experiments**. The method proceeds in five steps.

1. **Formulation survey (Sec. 2).** The authors dissect standard LRH formulations — displacement (difference-in-means, contrastive activation addition under the basic model), linear decodability (probing), superposition/dictionary learning — and identify for each the mathematical object (element of V, element of V*, latent decomposition) and its transformation law under affine h′ = Ah + t (v → Av; w → A⁻ᵀw; decomposition reproduces the transformed activation with coefficients unchanged). They observe the metric never enters the hypothesis itself, only the evidence offered for it (cosines and spectral quantities presuppose similarity geometry; norms and intervention magnitudes presuppose a fixed scale).
2. **Group-theoretic formalization (Sec. 3).** Representations are points of S = {φ: X → V} with pointwise group action (g·φ)(x) = T_g(φ(x)); equivalence is orbit membership (Def. 3.1). The transformation groups form the hierarchy G_iso ⊂ G_sim ⊂ G_aff (Eq. 2). Proposition 3.2 (elementary linear algebra, dim V ≥ 2): (i) there is no nonzero GL(V)-equivariant map V* → V; (ii) an inner product induces one under G_iso; (iii) the induced map on projective spaces is G_sim-equivariant — so a metric identifies probes with displacements only under isometries, and projectivization enlarges symmetry to similarities. The ℓ₂ penalty in regularized probing breaks GL(V)-equivariance down to isometric equivariance.
3. **Architectural-floor analysis (Sec. 4).** Define Γ as the group of invertible parameter transformations that preserve the model function (Eq. 4); a parameter symmetry induces a representation action ρ(γ) at a fixed reading point when the transformation descends to activations. Prop 4.1: a predicate that varies across the orbit is a property of θ, not f_θ; admissibility (A2) requires G ⊇ Im ρ. Cor 4.2 works out the Q/K gauge in dot-product attention (invariance of bilinear forms under Λ ∈ GL(d_head) fails: Λ = cI forces b ≡ 0) and notes RoPE reduces but anisotropically restricts the gauge, so the conclusion survives (derivations in Appendix C).
4. **Composition calculus and audit (Secs. 4.2–5).** Prop 4.3 gives a sufficient condition for pipeline admissibility; Table 1 then audits each common quantity (rank, span, CCA, cosine, principal angles, Grassmann distance, linear CKA, norms, Procrustes distance, reconstruction loss, sparsity penalty) against the largest group preserving it. Dictionary learning is analyzed "on two decodes": the encoder side is G_aff-covariant but the reconstruction residual is preserved only under G_iso (AᵀA = I), while the latent side admits W → WB, x → B⁻¹x mixing reduced by coordinatewise sparsity to signed permutations.
5. **Qualitative case studies + extension (Secs. 6–8).** Published analyses (Arditi et al., Bhalke et al., CAA, Activation Addition, Wollschläger et al., KeyDiff, Li et al., SVCCA, Yamawaki et al.) are re-read under the declared symmetries; Section 7 transfers the argument to the Platonic Representation Hypothesis (using Kombol et al.'s degeneracy result: when width ≥ number of sampled inputs with full row rank, invertible-linear-invariant similarities cannot distinguish representations); Section 8 states the reporting principle and pre-empts three objections (narrower conclusions; training-specific θ incurring a cross-seed empirical obligation; "merely relabeling" — answered via Park et al.'s explicit inner-product choice and Goledicha et al.'s argument that near-orthogonality is generic under whitening).

## Results & Highlights

**No quantitative results reported.** The paper contains no benchmarks, datasets, accuracy tables, or ablations; Table 1 is a conceptual audit, not an experiment. Its results are definitions, propositions, and qualitative re-readings of published analyses:

- **Prop 3.2 (identification needs a metric).** For dim V ≥ 2 there is *no* nonzero GL(V)-equivariant map V* → V; an inner product supplies one only under G_iso, and the projectivization [w] ↦ [ι_g w] is equivariant under G_sim. Consequence: comparing a probe direction and a steering direction by cosine can be meaningful under similarity geometry, but reusing the probe weights as an additive displacement is equivariant only under G_iso — a much stronger, rarely-argued claim.
- **Cor 4.2 (reading-point dependence).** At the residual stream the attention Q/K gauge acts trivially; at the query/key site Im ρ = GL(d_head), and since Λ = cI gives b(cu, cv) = c²b(u, v) = b(u, v) for all c > 0, any invariant bilinear form is b = 0. Hence per-head key/query cosines, L² norms, key-cache PCA, and log det(KᵀK) — the exact statistics KeyDiff's supporting analyses report — are *not invariants of the computation*, and gauge-related parameter settings can in principle make a cosine-based key-eviction rule select different tokens. The contrast: Yamawaki et al.'s comparison of column spans of transposed projection matrices (W_Qᵀ spans in the residual stream) is preserved under the same gauge.
- **Prop 4.3 + the "most restrictive stage" rule.** A direction estimated under G_aff, compared by cosine under G_sim, and calibrated by a norm under G_iso yields a composite claim guaranteed only under G_iso unless stronger invariance is explicitly established — i.e., the final, weakest guarantee is usually not the one stated.
- **Mid-analysis G-space switches (Sec. 6.4).** Arditi et al.'s single difference-in-means estimate is used both as an unnormalized displacement in V (its magnitude affects features) and as a normalized projective direction in P(V) (only its span matters); the combined conclusion is stronger than either use alone establishes. Li et al. partially repair this: normalizing the direction and scaling the added displacement by the empirical standard deviation makes the calibrated displacement transform covariantly as A(σŵ) under A = sQ, making the construction G_sim-compatible. SVCCA is the failure at the procedure level: CCA is affine-invariant on centered representations, but SVCCA's singular-value truncation is not preserved by general invertible linear transformations, and the downstream CCA stage cannot restore the lost invariance.
- **PRH degeneracy (Sec. 7).** By Kombol et al.'s result, once representation width ≥ the number of sampled inputs and both activation matrices have full row rank, a similarity measure invariant to arbitrary invertible linear transformations cannot distinguish any pair of representations — so finite-sample "platonic convergence" under permissive linear equivalence is trivial; meaningful convergence would require isometric agreement that different-width/tokenizer/objective models generally cannot satisfy, and existing evaluations actually operate near G_sim with the effective equivalence set by the alignment measure used.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty is consolidation, not invention.** The equivariance lens is standard mathematics, and the paper itself concedes the closest prior art: Park et al. already make the measurement/intervention inner-product choice explicit, architecture-induced symmetries including joint query-key rotations are previously studied (refs [21,22]), and Williams et al.'s generalized shape metrics fix nuisance groups for similarity. The genuinely new elements are the admissibility conditions (A1)/(A2), the reading-point corollary, and the composition proposition packaged into an auditable checklist.
- **The central empirical gap: no demonstration that any published conclusion actually flips.** Every case study is a *possibility* argument. The sharpest one — that gauge-related parameter settings "can cause" KeyDiff's cosine-based eviction to select different tokens — would take a single figure (eviction-set overlap under random Λ ∈ GL(d_head)) to make devastatingly concrete; the paper never runs it. Similarly, for Arditi et al., both uses of the difference-in-means estimate are internally valid, and the authors do not show that any reported refusal-direction result fails under the weaker group — so Table 1 is a list of *unsupported-as-stated* claims, not a list of broken results, and readers should not over-read it as the latter.
- **The architectural floor is worked out only for attention.** The main text (plus Appendix C) covers the Q/K gauge and RoPE; other function-preserving symmetries one expects in a transformer — permutation of attention heads with the matching output-column permutation, or normalization-layer rescale gauges — are not audited, so "what fixes the group" in Table 1 is not claimed (and should not be assumed) to be a complete account of Γ even for the architectures case-studied.
- **The calculus is confined to the affine family.** G_iso ⊂ G_sim ⊂ G_aff plus signed permutations on latent codes covers the quantities in Table 1, but the paper's own examples of non-1D features (circular day/month representations requiring 2D structure) hint at equivalence structure — topology, nonlinear reparameterization — that the affine hierarchy cannot express; the framework may under-describe exactly the multi-dimensional-feature cases it argues matter.
- **Adoption cost is acknowledged but unmitigated.** Section 8 honestly concedes the reporting principle narrows conclusions, forces extra maps between spaces, and attributes some quantities to "representation + metric + parameterization" rather than the representation alone; no checklist, template, or tooling is offered, and no worked re-analysis demonstrates the value of paying the cost.
- **Reproducibility:** N/A — there is nothing to reproduce empirically; the mathematical claims are checkable by inspection and the ones verified for this summary hold. Minor red flags: reliance on a small number of named case studies to characterize "current practice," and one-sided readings that the original authors might contest (e.g., whether Arditi et al. ever claim more than a normalization convenience from the projection step).

## Strengths

- Genuinely clarifying conceptual separation: object space M vs. procedure F catches real confusions, most crisply the "identification by storage format" problem — probe weights (V*) and steering vectors (V) are routinely conflated because both are length-d arrays, with different admissible interventions.
- Cor 4.2 is a one-line-proof result with outsized explanatory reach: it explains precisely *why* per-head attention-geometry analyses (cosines, norms, log det) are gauge-fragile without any experiment.
- The composition proposition matches how interpretability analyses are actually run (estimate → compare → calibrate) and yields a usable rule: the claim is only as strong as the most restrictive stage.
- Intellectually fair to prior work: no audited method is called invalid; the paper distinguishes its stance from fixed-nuisance-group similarity metrics, explicitly answers the "merely relabeling" objection, and credits partial solutions (Wollschläger et al.'s calibration, Li et al.'s σ-scaling, Yamawaki et al.'s gauge-invariant targets).
- Scope discipline and transfer: the same omission is located in the Platonic Representation Hypothesis, and the Kombol et al. degeneracy result is deployed exactly where it bites (finite-sample cross-width comparison).

## Limitations

- Purely analytic: no experiment, code, or quantitative evidence that any specific published interpretability conclusion is materially wrong — only that several are stated under stronger symmetry than they establish.
- The transformation-group taxonomy stops at the affine family; discrete architectural symmetries (head permutations), normalization gauges, and nonlinear equivalences are outside the audited hierarchy.
- The reporting principle imposes a real burden on authors, and the paper offers no lightweight artifacts (checklist, per-module G declarations, automated pipeline checks) to lower adoption cost.
- Case studies are single-auditor readings of others' pipelines (Arditi et al., KeyDiff, SVCCA, CAA); the original authors could reasonably dispute some characterizations, and the paper has no empirical adjudication of the disputes it opens.
- Full reading-point story (RoPE, per-head symmetry derivations) lives in Appendix C, so main-text-only readers get the conclusions without the derivations.

## Relevance

这篇论文对一切依赖线性探针、转向向量、SAE 字典与表示相似度度量（余弦/CKA/SVCCA）的可解释性研究是方法论层面的"对表"：它给出了为什么余弦结论不能随意移植为加性干预、为什么 per-head 注意力几何分析对参数规范敏感的严格判据。若你的工作涉及表示几何、探针/转向方法对比或跨模型表示比较（Platonic 类假设），这篇值得精读并用其报告原则自查。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Can the KeyDiff gauge-instability argument be turned into a concrete experiment — e.g., measuring eviction-set overlap under random Λ ∈ GL(d_head) — to show a published pipeline's behaviour actually changes within one Γ-orbit?
- Do discrete transformer symmetries (attention-head permutations with matching output columns, RMSNorm per-channel rescale gauges) enlarge the architectural floor Γ, and does Table 1's "what fixes the group" column change once they are included?
- For superposition/SAE dictionaries, sparsity reduces latent symmetry to signed permutations — what does that imply for comparing learned features across SAE training runs, seeds, or model checkpoints (i.e., is cross-run feature matching only defined up to signed permutation)?
- When the architecture realizes transformations outside G_iso (condition A2), what statistical protocol should certify that a reported geometry is stable across the Γ-orbit — across seeds, fine-tunes, and gauge choices — before it is attributed to the model function rather than the artifact?
- Could the admissibility calculus be operationalized in interpretability libraries (declaring G, M, F, P per module and validating composed pipelines automatically), and would SVCCA-style failures then be caught at CI time?
