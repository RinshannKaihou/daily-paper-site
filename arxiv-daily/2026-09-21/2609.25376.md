> **一句话总结：** 该工作构建了一个把 RTN 量化器固定不变、从而将"数值格式（W3/W4/W8/W4A4/W4A8/W8A8）× 量化范围（视觉编码器/投影器/LLM/动作头直至单层投影）× 校准"三者解耦的受控基准 VLAQuantBench（409 次评估运行、94,574 个仿真回合，覆盖 OpenVLA-OFT、π₀、π₀.₅、X-VLA 在 LIBERO 四套件及 X-VLA 在 SIMPLER/CALVIN/VLABench），揭示了三个反直觉事实：未校准 W4A4 使三个模型在 LIBERO 全部四套件成功率归零；π₀.₅ 动作头被量化的层从 126 层扩到 167 层反而把成功率从 7.0% 拉回 70.5%（+63.5 pp，重放证实为数值层恢复）；以及仅保护 OpenVLA-OFT 动作头中 28,672 参数（占比约 0.019%）的 fc2 输出投影，即可把 W3 端到端成功率从 8.0% 恢复到 93.5%（基线 92.5）。

> **TL;DR:** VLAQuantBench is a controlled study (409 evaluation runs, 94,574 simulation episodes) of post-training quantization on four released VLAs (OpenVLA-OFT, π₀, π₀.₅, X-VLA) on LIBERO (plus SIMPLER/CALVIN/VLABench for X-VLA), holding a round-to-nearest quantizer fixed so numerical format, quantization scope, and calibration can be isolated. Uncalibrated W4A4 zeroes closed-loop success for three of four models on all LIBERO suites, yet quantizing MORE of π₀.₅'s action head (126 → 167 layers) raises success 7.0% → 70.5% (+63.5 pp, confirmed by fixed-observation replay where chunk MAE drops 0.514 → 0.390 on 217/219 chunks), and protecting one 28,672-parameter output projection (fc2) recovers OpenVLA-OFT W3 success from 8.0% to 93.5% vs a 92.5% baseline. Real-kernel profiling shows every quantized path (e.g., AWQ 6.1 GiB) saves memory over BF16 (15.0 GiB) but runs slower (181.1 ms vs 139.9 ms), and a 1,200-rollout Franka Study confirms W3 collapse (88.0% → 12.0%) while W8/W8A8 stay within ~6 pp.

## 核心贡献

- **发布受控评测框架 VLAQuantBench**：409 次评估运行、94,574 个仿真回合；固定 RTN 量化器，把每个策略拆成 VE / MP / LLM / AH 四个组件范围，再细到层组与单个投影（最细只量化 1 层）；每次运行记录 checkpoint 标识、量化配置、范围与被量化层数/参数量，回合级记录全部公开（github.com/jiuyixu5/VLAQuantBench），支持向新策略、新量化器扩展。
- **揭示非单调、非可加的任务敏感性**：π₀.₅-Spatial 未校准 W4A4 下，单独量化 attention 只掉 1.0 pp、单独量化 adaRMS 只掉 5.5 pp，但二者联合量化暴跌 91.5 pp（99.0% → 7.5%）；而把量化层集从 126 层扩展到完整 167 层动作头，成功率反而从 7.0% 升至 70.5%。20 条 held-out 全精度轨迹的固定观测重放证实这是数值交互而非任务阈值效应：W4A4 的联合误差比 |e_A∪B|/(|e_A|+|e_B|) 中位数达 2.90（W4/W4A8 仅 0.80），残差比 0.82 说明不能用向量加法解释。
- **定位并修复灾难性小投影**：OpenVLA-OFT 动作头中仅 28,672 参数（约头部 0.019%）的 fc2 在 W4A8 单独量化时成功率 18.0%（基线 100.0%，vs 全部四层 20.5%）；保持 fc2 原精度、量化其余 441/442 个合格线性层后，W3 成功率从 8.0% [5,13] 恢复到 93.5% [89,96]（基线 92.5），同一条"保护输出投影"规则也把 W4A8/W8A8 全部四套件拉回基线 2.5 pp 以内。激活 kurtosis 几乎相同的两组层（834.3 vs 835.3）成功率相差 81.5 pp（18.0% vs 99.5%），证明基于局部激活统计的层敏感性排序不可靠。
- **证明"校准是配置的一部分"**：仅两回合数据、α=0.5 的 smoothing+clipping 把 π₀.₅ union 成功率从 1.5% 拉到 97.5%、126 层子集从 7.0% 到 99.0%；但同一配方用在 π₀ 上端到端从 61.0% 跌到 10.5%、LLM-only 从 72.0% 跌到 11.0%；α=0.75 才让 π₀.₅ 端到端达到 91.5%——校准收益不能跨模型迁移，必须连同完整精度配置一起验证。
- **部署侧双补刀**：RTX 4090 真实核测量（OFT LLM-only，batch 1）显示所有量化路径省显存却更慢——AWQ 6.1 GiB vs BF16 15.0 GiB，但完整推理 181.1 ms vs 139.9 ms（LLM.int8() 达 379.5 ms）；Franka Research 3 实机 1,200 次物理 rollout 证实 W3 崩溃（OFT Pick 88.0% → 12.0%）而 W8/W8A8 在 BF16 的 6 pp 以内，W4A8 比 W4 再损失 18–40 pp。

## Key Contributions

- **A released, controlled evaluation framework.** 409 runs / 94,574 episodes over OpenVLA-OFT, π₀, π₀.₅, and X-VLA on LIBERO's four suites (X-VLA additionally on SIMPLER-VM, CALVIN, VLABench), with an RTN anchor quantizer, explicit component/layer-group/projection scopes down to a single 1-layer intervention, per-run recording of checkpoint, scope, and quantized layer/parameter counts, and public code, configurations, and episode records.
- **Non-monotone and non-additive task sensitivity.** Isolated W4A4 action-head tests miss joint failures (attention −1.0 pp, adaRMS −5.5 pp alone; union −91.5 pp), while expanding π₀.₅'s quantized subset from 126 to 167 layers recovers success 7.0% → 70.5% (+63.5 pp, task-clustered 95% CI [+46.0, 80.5]); 20-trajectory fixed-observation replay verifies the recovery numerically (chunk MAE 0.514 → 0.390, lower error on 99.1% of chunks; union-error ratio 2.90 at W4A4 vs 0.80 at W4/W4A8).
- **A targeted remedy for W3 failure.** A single 28,672-parameter output projection (fc2) causes an 82-pp W4A8 loss by itself; keeping it at released precision and quantizing the remaining 441/442 layers restores W3 success to 93.5% [89,96] from 8.0% [5,13] (baseline 92.5), and protected-fc2 W4A8/W8A8 land within 2.5 pp of baselines across all four suites. Kurtosis-matched layer groups (834.3 vs 835.3) differ by 81.5 pp in success, so local statistics miss the real sensitivity.
- **Calibration is part of the configuration.** Two-episode smoothing+clipping (α=0.5) fixes π₀.₅ (union 1.5% → 97.5%) but damages π₀ (end-to-end 61.0% → 10.5%; LLM-only 72.0% → 11.0%); α=0.75 yields 91.5% for π₀.₅ — the recipe does not transfer uniformly across models.
- **Practical deployment facts.** All tested quantized kernels (AWQ 6.1 GiB, NF4 6.3, LLM.int8() 9.0, SmoothQuant 9.0 vs BF16 15.0) reduce memory but increase latency on an RTX 4090 (AWQ 181.1 ms vs 139.9 ms full inference); on a physical Franka Research 3 (1,200 rollouts), W3 collapses success (88.0% → 12.0%) while W8/W8A8 stay within ~6 pp of BF16.

## Methodology

**Benchmark design.** Each policy is partitioned into vision encoder (VE), multimodal projector (MP where present), language backbone (LLM), and action head (AH); module-root lists plus include/exclude patterns define scopes down to named layer groups and single projections. Every result file records the checkpoint, quantization configuration, selected scope, and quantized layer/parameter counts; activation-hook coverage is checked because a missing hook silently degrades an A8 run to weight-only. Six main RTN presets (W3, W4, W8, W4A4, W4A8, W8A8): weights use asymmetric per-output-channel grids with group size 128 (short groups get their own scale), activations use dynamic per-token symmetric absmax; embeddings, biases, normalization, attention-matrix products, and the vocabulary head stay at released precision. Quantization is simulated (round + dequantize; computation stays floating point), so these are accuracy effects, not packed-kernel effects.

**Coverage.** LIBERO Spatial/Object/Goal/Long with official initial states, 200 episodes per configuration (190 for X-VLA); all four models cover all four suites end-to-end. X-VLA additionally runs SIMPLER-VM (864 episodes/setting, hierarchical averaging across variants/tasks/families), CALVIN ABC→D (1,000 chains; 300 for W4A4), and VLABench (50 episodes/task for baseline/W4/W8, reduced to 20/20/10 for W3/W4A8/W8A8); unequal-coverage settings are excluded from precision comparisons rather than pooled.

**Uncertainty and causal controls.** 95% Wilson intervals on binomial episode sampling; task-clustered two-stage percentile bootstrap (5,000 draws) for large contrasts; evaluation-seed replications for selected cells. To separate numerical interaction from task-threshold artifacts, 20 held-out full-precision π₀.₅ trajectories are replayed with identical observations and flow noise (219 predicted chunks); additivity is tested via the ratio |e_A∪B|/(|e_A|+|e_B|) and cancellation via the residual |e_A∪B − e_A − e_B|/|e_A∪B|.

**Alternative quantizers and calibration controls.** AWQ, NF4, LLM.int8(), and SmoothQuant are evaluated on OpenVLA-OFT's LLM scope at matched bit widths (calibrated LLMs retain their own formats — implementations differ beyond the nominal grid). A separate two-episode SmoothQuant-style smoothing+clipping control (α ∈ {0.5, 0.75}, 99.9th-percentile clipping, robot-observation calibration) tests whether the severity of failures is recipe-tied — explicitly a control, not an optimized-method comparison.

**Deployment and physical execution.** Real-kernel profiling of OpenVLA-OFT LLM-only on one RTX 4090 (batch 1, 20 LIBERO-Spatial rollouts/method, three independent repeat sessions; control-call vs full-inference timers). Physical study: OpenVLA-OFT and π₀.₅ each LoRA-fine-tuned on 200 demonstrations for Pick and Pick-and-Place on a Franka Research 3, six precision settings, 50 rollouts per model-task-precision condition (1,200 physical rollouts). Evaluation-stack checks pin MuJoCo versions (3.3.2 vs 3.1.6), verify executed action-chunk lengths, and audit activation-hook coverage.

## Results & Highlights

- **W4A4 collapse is near-universal, but π₀ resists.** End-to-end uncalibrated W4A4 RTN drops success to 0.0% on all four LIBERO suites for OpenVLA-OFT (baselines 100.0/97.5/96.0/92.5% on Spatial/Object/Goal/Long), X-VLA (96.8/97.9/97.4/97.9%), and π₀.₅ (99.0/100.0/94.0/95.0%), while π₀ alone retains 61.0/74.0/83.0/31.0%. Component-only W4A4 flips the sensitivity ordering per checkpoint: π₀.₅ LLM-only gives 0.0% vs AH-only 70.5%, whereas X-VLA is 96.3% LLM-only vs 0.0% AH-only — a universal layer-importance rule cannot exist.
- **Quantizing more layers improves success (+63.5 pp).** On π₀.₅-Spatial uncalibrated W4A4, the 126-layer subset (attention+adaRMS+down_proj) scores 7.0% [4,11]; the full 167-layer head scores 70.5% [64,76] — a +63.5 pp recovery (task-clustered CI [+46.0, 80.5]) that still trails the 99.0% baseline by 28.5 pp. Fixed-observation replay confirms the reversal numerically: normalized chunk MAE falls 0.514 → 0.390 with error lower on 217/219 chunks (99.1%), the W4A4 union-error ratio is 2.90 (IQR 2.27–3.50) vs 0.80 at W4/W4A8, and residual ratio 0.82 shows plain error cancellation cannot explain it. The same six scopes at W4 and W4A8 score 97.5–100.0% (within 1.5 pp of baseline), so the interaction is W4A4-recipe-specific, not a universal W4 property.
- **One 28.7K-parameter projection dominates an entire head.** On OpenVLA-OFT-Spatial W4A8, fc2 alone (28,672 params, ~0.019% of the head) yields 18.0% [13,24] vs 100.0% baseline and 20.5% [15,27] for all four AH layers; the residual-block group (33.6M params) costs only 0.5 pp. Protecting fc2 while quantizing the other 441/442 eligible layers at W3 restores 93.5% [89,96] (replication seeds 91.5%/91.0%) vs 8.0% [5,13] end-to-end; protected-fc2 reaches 97.5/98.0/98.0/94.0 (W4A8) and 99.0/97.5/97.5/96.0 (W8A8) across the four suites — within 2.5 pp of baselines. Weight-group refinement (128 → 64/32) does NOT recover W3 (12.5%/17.5%).
- **Calibration is recipe-dependent, and kurtosis is misleading.** Two-episode smoothing+clipping (α=0.5) lifts π₀.₅ union success 1.5% → 97.5% and the 126-layer set 7.0% → 99.0%, but the same recipe lowers π₀ end-to-end 61.0% → 10.5% (α=0.75: 8.0%) and LLM-only 72.0% → 11.0%; π₀.₅ needs α=0.75 for end-to-end 91.5% (α=0.5 gives 0.0%). Two nearly identical-kurtosis groups (834.3 vs 835.3) produce 18.0% vs 99.5% success — an 81.5-pp gap invisible to activation-statistics-based sensitivity ranking.
- **Memory savings do not buy latency, and the robot agrees.** RTX 4090 real kernels (OFT LLM-only, batch 1): AWQ 6.1 GiB / 181.1 ms, NF4 6.3 / 152.6 ms, LLM.int8() 9.0 / 379.5 ms, SmoothQuant 9.0 / 181.8 ms vs BF16 15.0 GiB / 139.9 ms — every quantized path is slower, with within-method ranges ≤0.38 ms (control) / 3.00 ms (inference) across three repeat sessions. On the Franka Research 3, W3 collapses Pick success to 12.0% (OFT) and 10.0% (π₀.₅) from 88.0%/84.0% BF16, W4A8 costs 18–40 pp vs W4, while W8/W8A8 stay within ~6 pp; alternative LLM quantizers (AWQ/NF4/LLM.int8/SmoothQuant) sit at 98.0–99.0% in near-ceiling simulation where differences cannot be resolved.

## Authors & Credibility

The six authors span academia and industry: Jiuyi Xu (Colorado School of Mines), Qing Jin (independent researcher), Meida Chen and Yang Sui (University of Central Florida), Song Wang (USC Institute for Creative Technologies), and Yangming Shi (Microsoft AI). This is a preprint benchmark/measurement paper rather than the output of a known quantization group, but its credibility rests on unusually rigorous engineering for robotics evaluation: pinned simulator versions, SHA-256 checkpoint identifiers, per-episode records with provenance manifests, Wilson intervals plus task-clustered bootstrap, seed replications, and documented evaluation-stack traps (e.g., MuJoCo version changes silently altering stored initial states; activation-hook omissions silently turning A8 runs weight-only). All code, configurations, and episode records are publicly released (github.com/jiuyixu5/VLAQuantBench), and the limitations section is notably candid — both good signs for reproducibility.

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty.** This is deliberately a measurement paper, not a method paper. Its novelty is the controlled factorial design — an RTN anchor quantizer with scope interventions down to single projections — which cleanly separates effects that VLA-specific PTQ work (QVLA, HBVLA, QuantVLA, DyQ-VLA, cited but excluded from matched comparison) conflates by changing checkpoints, scopes, and algorithms together. The non-monotone 126→167-layer recovery and the 28.7K-parameter fc2 result are genuinely surprising and well-triangulated findings that no prior VLA quantization paper reports.

**Rigor.** Above average for the field: uncertainty quantification (Wilson + task-clustered bootstrap) is rare in robot-manipulation evals, and replay-based numerical verification rules out the obvious "thresholding artifact" confound. However, the headline recovery is established *only* for uncalibrated W4A4 RTN — the paper's own W4/W4A8 controls show no such interaction, so the flagship finding lives in one brittle corner of the configuration space, and the replay (20 trajectories, 219 nested chunks) does not isolate the original cause. The "calibration is part of the configuration" claim rests on a two-episode smoothing+clipping control with α ∈ {0.5, 0.75} from a single task — a narrow slice of calibration space, honestly labeled a control rather than an optimized-method comparison, but still thin support for a section-level claim.

**Reproducibility.** Strong: pinned MuJoCo, checkpoint SHAs, episode-level public records, and implementation checks that other groups will hit (chunk-length conventions differ across released clients; activation-hook coverage must be audited). Main gap: the protected-W3 accuracy result is simulated quantization — no packed-W3 kernel latency/accuracy is measured, and it has only 3 evaluation seeds on one model family.

**Red flags.** (1) The anchor is deliberately naive RTN, so headline failure numbers (W4A4 → 0%) overstate what a practitioner with AWQ/SmoothQuant would see — the paper's own Table 22 shows those methods at 98–99% on the LLM scope; the framing mostly resists overclaiming but the "eight-bit activations can cause severe task losses" section title generalizes an RTN-specific effect. (2) The physical-robot study uses separately fine-tuned policies and different tasks from the simulation checkpoints, so the sim findings' sim-to-real transfer is untested, not demonstrated. (3) The mechanism of the recovery is explicitly unresolved — the skip-path hypothesis is unverified, and the paper's own gripper-bias and absmax diagnostics fail to track the recovery. (4) Only X-VLA extends beyond LIBERO, and near-ceiling LIBERO baselines (99–100%) cannot resolve small differences — the authors say so, but it caps the benchmark's discriminative power exactly where deployment decisions get risky.

## Strengths

- Controlled factorial design with a fixed quantizer isolates numerical-format, scope, and calibration effects that prior VLA-PTQ evaluations conflate — the right experimental template for this subfield.
- Massive evidence base (409 runs, 94,574 episodes) with proper statistical treatment: Wilson intervals, task-clustered bootstrap, seed replications, and explicit statements of what the intervals do NOT cover (training-seed, task-distribution uncertainty).
- The headline finding is triangulated three ways: closed-loop success, fixed-observation replay on held-out trajectories, and error-ratio decomposition — not just a success-rate curve.
- Practically actionable output: the protect-the-output-projection rule is a concrete, cheap precision-allocation heuristic, and the memory-vs-latency kernel measurements deflate the common "quantize for speed" assumption for this workload.
- Unusually honest limitations section and full public artifacts, including the evaluation-stack pitfalls (simulator versioning, chunk conventions, hook coverage) that will save other groups real debugging time.

## Limitations

- Only four checkpoints, none of which span the full VLA architecture space; no autoregressive action-token policy is evaluated, so findings are confined to regression/flow-matching action generation.
- Accuracy results use simulated quantization; real-kernel profiling covers only the OpenVLA-OFT LLM scope on a single RTX 4090, and the protected-W3 result has three evaluation seeds on one model family.
- The non-monotone recovery is established only for uncalibrated W4A4 RTN scopes, with no causal mechanism — the replay verifies the numerical reversal but does not explain why the 41 added layers matter.
- Calibration exploration is minimal: two-episode sets from one task, α ∈ {0.5, 0.75}, three replication sets; no optimized calibration methods are benchmarked, and VLA-specific PTQ methods are excluded from matched comparison.
- Only X-VLA covers benchmarks beyond LIBERO; LIBERO baselines are near ceiling (95–100%), limiting resolution of small but deployment-relevant differences.
- The physical-robot study uses separately fine-tuned policies, 50 rollouts per condition, and no matched sim-to-real controls or replication of the nested action-head interventions.

## Relevance

对同时关注大模型低比特量化与具身智能（VLA、机器人操作）的方向，这篇工作给出了"量化哪些层比量化到几比特更关键"的最直接证据——单层 2.8 万参数投影可毁掉 82 pp 成功率、保护它即可救回 W3，而非单调恢复现象直接挑战了"逐层敏感度排序"类方法的前提假设，对任何打算把 VLA 部署到端侧/实机的工作都有立即可用的精度分配启示。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- What is the causal mechanism of the 126→167-layer W4A4 recovery — does the skip-path hypothesis survive direct intervention on the residual branch, or is error placement (not just input distribution) the deciding factor?
- Does the protect-the-output-projection rule transfer to autoregressive action-token VLAs and to packed low-bit kernels running end-to-end, rather than simulated quantization?
- Would principled calibration (learned scales, GPTQ-style block reconstruction) eliminate the non-additive union failure, or merely move it to different layer subsets?
- Can task-clustered sensitivity be predicted before deployment from a cheap probe (e.g., a few protected-cell rollouts), given that kurtosis and absmax diagnostics demonstrably fail?
- Do the W4A4 collapse and non-monotone recovery persist in the cross-embodiment, long-horizon regime (CALVIN/VLABench), where only one of the four models was tested?
