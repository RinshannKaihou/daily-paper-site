> **一句话总结：** 作者让 16 个相同配置（claude-opus-5 + Claude Code）的自主智能体在冻结的 12,499 个 MOF 数据库上各自独立跑一周甲烷存储筛选：策略几乎立即分化（筛选量 106–5,006 个结构，相差近 50 倍），但最终收敛到同一材料前沿——11/16 个智能体独立报告了同一个铜基 pts 框架（10 个独立高精度值落在 198.9–200.1 cm³ cm⁻³，与独立参考值 199.4±1.3 吻合）；然而 15/16 个智能体同时把同一个因剔除配衡阴离子而虚增孔体积、被预启动审计剔除的 Cu sql 条目选为冠军（206.7–207.2 cm³ cm⁻³，跨智能体标准差仅 0.12）；对半数智能体施加 7 项强制检查把新鲜复现率从 1/8 提升到 8/8（Fisher p=0.001），却对结论正确性无可检测的改善——证明复制智能体间的一致性只能证明"对同一共享输入的重采样稳定"，而非独立验证。

> **TL;DR:** A controlled population study: 16 separately initialized sessions of the same model–harness (claude-opus-5 via Claude Code) each ran a one-week, budget-capped autonomous campaign to find the best methane-storage MOF in a frozen 12,499-structure CoRE MOF 2024 database under a pinned RASPA GCMC protocol. Strategies diverged immediately (screening volume spanning 106–5,006 structures, ~50-fold), yet outcomes converged: 11/16 agents independently reported the copper pts framework as the leading retained material with 10 separately generated high-accuracy values of 198.9–200.1 cm³ cm⁻³ vs the independent reference calculation's 199.4 ± 1.3. A randomized 8-checked vs 8-unchecked design showed enforced gates raised fresh-run reproduction from 1/8 to 8/8 (Fisher exact p = 0.001) and verification acts 19 vs 9 (Mann–Whitney p = 0.012) but did not detectably improve conclusion validity (1 vs 0, p = 1.0): 15/16 agents named the same audit-excluded entry (a BSF-71-derived structure whose charge-balancing [B₁₂Cl₁₂]²⁻ anions were lost in curation, creating ~7 cm³ cm⁻³ of artificial pore volume) as champion. Core lesson: agreement among replicated agents measures stability to resampling of one model on one shared substrate — not independent confirmation.

## 核心贡献

- **人群级 agent 评估范式**：不同于 CORE-Bench/PaperBench 等任务级基准（完成与否、复现已知结果），本文研究 16 个独立初始化的同配置智能体在**同一开放任务**上的**群体行为**——哪些行为稳定、哪些随机、不同策略是否恢复同一科学图景。
- **策略发散、结果收敛**：16 个智能体前 5 个日志动作全部唯一、报告筛选量相差近 50 倍（106–5,006）、形成 4 类策略（描述符优先/代理模型、宽度优先低精度、串行、改造设计），却收敛到几乎相同的材料前沿；参考计算测得的 2,344 个结构中**前九高的点估计全部被至少一个智能体报告**（首个无人命名的结构排在第 10 名，190.2 cm³ cm⁻³）。
- **一致性的证据学边界（核心洞见）**：15/16 个智能体共同选中同一缺陷条目——该条目在数据库精修时丢失了电荷平衡的 [B₁₂Cl₁₂]²⁻ 阴离子，空出的层间空间成为虚假孔体积，模拟值虚高约 7 cm³ cm⁻³；至少 10 个智能体记录了异常迹象（裸露铜中心、极低密度、超高 void fraction、断开的二维片层、极端代理模型残差），但全都把异常解释为"性能卓越的原因"而非"质疑结构的理由"。
- **强制检查的行为学实验**：随机分配 7 项可执行检查给 8 个智能体——复现率 8/8 vs 1/8（p=0.001）、验证行为总数 19 vs 9（p=0.012），但命名审计保留材料为冠军的比例 1 vs 0（p=1.0）——检查改变了行为，却没作用到决定性错误所在的层级（数据有效性而非计算可复现性）。
- **自发性设计行为**：章程允许但不要求结构改造，8 个智能体自发走上这条路，共建 2,253 个假想结构（最大项目：1,065 个互穿母体 → 1,713 个去互穿子结构，测了 250 对亲子，得到 4 个 200–204 cm³ cm⁻³ 的未松弛候选）；脱互穿曾把 75.4 cm³ cm⁻³ 的母体提到 203.7，但同一操作的匹配对照只有 +0.5、+9.6、−23.8——大的改进几乎只出现在远低于前沿的母体上。

## Key Contributions

- **Population-level agent evaluation**: instead of task-success benchmarks, the paper jointly analyzes trajectories, conclusions and failure modes of 16 replicated autonomous researchers pursuing one open objective (best methane-storage MOF) under identical frozen database, pinned protocol and one-week budget — revealing patterns no single trajectory could show.
- **Divergent strategies, convergent outcomes**: the first five logged actions were unique for every agent and reported screening volume varied nearly fiftyfold (106–5,006 structures) across four emergent strategy classes (descriptor-first/surrogate, breadth-first low-fidelity, serial, modification-centered), yet the same legitimate materials frontier recurred: all nine highest reference point estimates among 2,344 independently measured structures were named by at least one agent.
- **The evidentiary limit of agreement**: 15/16 agents named the same audit-excluded Cu sql entry (missing [B₁₂Cl₁₂]²⁻ counter-anions → artificial interlayer pore volume, ~+7 cm³ cm⁻³ over the true frontier) as champion, with cross-agent spread (sd 0.12 cm³ cm⁻³) below simulation uncertainty; ≥10 agents logged warning signs but reinterpreted each as evidence of exceptional performance. Replication rapidly increased confidence in both the frontier and the shared error.
- **What enforcement does and does not buy**: seven enforceable gates (assigned to a randomized half) raised fresh-run reproduction 8/8 vs 1/8 (p = 0.001) and verification totals 19 vs 9 (p = 0.012), but champion validity was 1 vs 0 (p = 1.0) — and the one "valid" champion arose because that agent never examined the excluded entry, not because it recognized the defect. Effective checks must test whether the object under study is valid, not merely whether the calculation is reproducible.
- **Voluntary research programs**: eight agents treated the database-search objective as provisional and ran design campaigns (deinterpenetration, functionalization, solvent removal, cell compression), building 2,253 hypothetical structures — evaluated as evidence of autonomous research behavior, not proposed materials (all simulated as-built, rigid-framework, no relaxation).

## Methodology

- **Benchmark freezing.** The computation-ready CoRE MOF 2024 set was assembled once, frozen at N = 12,499 and pinned by a SHA-256 manifest; coordinate-identical entries scored as one entity left 9,167 distinct structures. A pre-launch three-pass charge-accounting audit (validated on 70 ZIF-like structures) flagged 406 charge-inconsistent entries; 6 combining charge imbalance with anomalous void space were excluded prospectively as capacity artifacts, by mechanical rules recorded in a hidden answer key. Agents received neither the flagged list nor the audit tool.
- **Pinned physics protocol.** Deliverable capacity = absolute GCMC loading at 65 bar minus 5.8 bar (cm³ STP per cm³ framework), 298 K, methane as a single TraPPE united atom, rigid UFF frameworks, 12.8 Å cutoff, no tail corrections, no electrostatics (DDEC6 charges deliberately not read); RASPA v2.0.37 compiled with pinned files. Floor fidelity: 2,000 init / 10,000 production cycles; claim grade: 10,000 / 50,000. Smoke-phase launch required reference agreement within 3σ (e.g., N(5.8 bar) 36.958 ± 0.600 vs reference 36.841 ± 0.183).
- **Agent population.** 16 separately initialized headless sessions of the same frontier model (recorded in every transcript only as claude-opus-5) via Claude Code 2.1.233 (29 Aug–1 Sep 2026), on a dedicated host, one transcript directory per agent. Budgets per agent: 168 h wall clock, 1,610 CPU-hours (vs ~22,873 for naive exhaustive screening), 32 M tokens, US$280 spend cap (the binding constraint for 12/16). Materials-specific packages (Zeo++, pymatgen, MOFid) were absent and unobtainable. Isolation was enforced by charter and audited via scheduler records and transcripts, not OS boundaries.
- **Treatment randomization.** Eight agents received an audit appendix with seven enforceable checks (two capacity thresholds, structural sanity, protocol validity, modification hygiene, finalist reproduction, random auditing); eight received the same scientific norms without enforcement. Agents were not told other agents or groups existed; the investigator made no scientific decisions (58 escalations answered operationally under a pre-registered clause; no scientific guidance).
- **Hidden scoring substrate.** Claims were scored against (a) the pre-launch integrity audit and (b) an independent reference calculation — 1,497 coordinate-distinct retained structures drawn uniformly at random plus 847 descriptor-tail structures (highest void fraction / largest cavity diameter > 15 Å), 2,344 measured under the identical pinned protocol; the analysis was frozen on 6 Sep 2026 by a pre-analysis amendment filed before any report was read. The controller independently re-ran every headline value from archived inputs before scoring.
- **Inference and extraction.** Charter versions, group assignment, budgets, rubric and answer-key census sealed by a public-hash commit before launch; 5 post-seal amendments logged, none touching the scientific task. Conduct measures were extracted from final reports by mechanical text rules; single-row contrasts use Fisher's exact test, totals Mann–Whitney; primary proportions carry Wilson 95% intervals. Strategy classes were assigned post hoc by the investigator from each agent's own description of its search order.

## Results & Highlights

- **Convergence on the true frontier.** On the frozen 12,499-structure database, 12/16 agents named at least one audit-retained material above 180 cm³ cm⁻³; 11 reported the same copper pts framework and 10 placed it first with ten separately generated high-accuracy values between 198.9 and 200.1 cm³ cm⁻³, against the independent reference value of 199.4 ± 1.3 (vanadium srs ≈197–198, ytterbium nia 195–198, indium nuc 195–196 also recurring). All nine highest reference point estimates among the 2,344 measured structures were reported by at least one agent; the first structure no agent named sits at rank ten (190.2). Reference-vs-agent values agreed to −0.2 ± 0.8 cm³ cm⁻³ on average.
- **The shared blind spot.** 15/16 agents named the same audit-excluded copper sql entry (derived from BSF-71, retaining only Cu(dpb)₂ after the charge-balancing [B₁₂Cl₁₂]²⁻ anions were removed in curation) as the best material, reporting 206.7–207.2 cm³ cm⁻³ with a cross-agent standard deviation of only 0.12 cm³ cm⁻³ — about 7 cm³ cm⁻³ above the leading retained entry, the excess tracing to artificial interlayer pore volume. Wilson 95% CI for the excluded-entry-as-champion proportion: 0.72–0.99.
- **Enforcement changed behavior, not validity.** Fresh-run reproduction of headline values: 8/8 checked vs 1/8 unchecked (Fisher exact p = 0.001; Wilson intervals 0.68–1.00 vs 0.02–0.47); total verification acts 19 vs 9 (Mann–Whitney p = 0.012). But naming an audit-retained MOF as best: 1 vs 0 (p = 1.0), and identifying the excluded deposition as incomplete: 0/16. Exploratory behaviors numerically favored unchecked agents (17 vs 10, p = 0.14), with the file-count contrast (2,207 vs 46 built structures) dominated by a single large unchecked program.
- **Frontier recovery does not scale with search effort.** Among the 12 agents stating a screening total, the rank correlation between structures screened and reference top-ten reported was Spearman ρ = 0.10 (p = 0.77). One agent recovered 3 of the top ten with only 30 surviving simulation outputs; the largest-footprint agent (12,465 structures) reported none. Post-hoc class averages of top-ten recovered: descriptor-first 2.4, breadth-first 2.0, serial 1.5, modification-centered 0.
- **The landscape is steep.** In the 1,497-structure uniform random sample, median deliverable capacity was 46 cm³ cm⁻³, 82% of structures lay below 100, only 6% above 150, and the best sampled structure reached 196 — so a result near the 190–200 literature band is plausible while the excluded entry's 207.17 ± 1.24 (pre-launch answer-key value) deserved scrutiny.
- **Design-route economics.** Eight agents voluntarily built 2,253 hypothetical structures; the largest program examined 1,065 interpenetrated parents, generated 1,713 deinterpenetrated children and measured 250 parent–child pairs, finding four unrelaxed candidates at 200–204 cm³ cm⁻³. The spectacular case (75.4 → 203.7 cm³ cm⁻³) did not replicate under matched controls (+0.5, +9.6, −23.8 cm³ cm⁻³ for three parents), and methylation lowered all 20 variants (frontier material 198.3 → 187.1 cm³ cm⁻³).
- **A 3-agent random sample already shows the asymmetry**: 98% chance at least one replica reports the leading retained material, but certainty that a majority names the excluded entry — replication boosts confidence in the shared error as fast as in the truth.

## Critical Assessment

- **Single point of failure in generality: one model, one harness, one task, one database.** The model is identified only by the alias claude-opus-5 with no snapshot pin, so the study cannot be re-run on identical weights — the author concedes this. All headline claims (divergence, convergence, common-mode error) characterize one model–harness on one steep, well-mapped methane-storage landscape where "correct" behavior is comparatively cheap (verification via GCMC is inexpensive); harder domains may expose entirely different failure modes, so the transferable claim is conceptual, not an error-rate estimate.
- **The checked/unchecked contrast is underpowered by construction, and the author says so.** With n = 8 per group, most Table 1 rows are "not significant"; the one descriptive explosion (2,207 vs 46 generated structures) is dominated by a single unchecked agent, and the exploratory-total difference (17 vs 10, p = 0.14) is explicitly flagged as not establishing a budget trade-off. A reader looking for a causal estimate of "do audits hurt exploration" will not find one here — only a manipulation check (fresh-run reproduction) plus suggestive counts.
- **The mandated-reproduction caveat.** The 8/8 vs 1/8 reproduction contrast partly measures compliance with Appendix A's explicit requirement rather than intrinsic diligence; the paper honestly labels it a manipulation check, but the abstract's "raised fresh-run reproduction from one of eight to eight of eight" headline should be read with that framing in mind.
- **The strategy taxonomy is single-coder and post hoc.** Four strategy classes were assigned by the investigator from each agent's final report, without a second coder or inter-rater agreement, and then used for descriptive recovery comparisons (2.4/2.0/1.5/0 top-ten recoveries) — these numbers are illustrative, not tested.
- **The key experiment was set up so only intrinsic capability could pass.** Agents were deliberately given neither the flagged list nor the audit tool, and the 6 prospective exclusions were fixed by mechanical rules; no agent passed. That is the point of the paper, but it also means the "failure" is partly an out-of-distribution probe — one checked agent even built a chemistry audit, correctly computed +8 charge across the four copper centers, and then balanced it with negative groups absent from the file, importing expected chemistry into the deposition. No agent validated its audit tool against independent known cases — a sharp, specific indictment of self-built verification.
- **Isolation was procedural, not architectural.** One confirmed shared-directory failure actually delivered rep09's draft report into rep01's workspace (and overwrote rep03's state file); both agents detected and quarantined it and sensitivity analyses exclude them, but the incident shows how close a "controlled population" study sits to contamination when isolation is a charter rule rather than an OS boundary.
- **Materials-side results are deliberately not science.** All agent-built structures were simulated as-built under a rigid-framework protocol with no geometry relaxation or stability analysis; the 200–204 cm³ cm⁻³ "candidates" are behavior evidence, not materials — the paper is scrupulous about this, but the framing could be misread from the abstract alone.
- **Reference coverage is partial (~16% random coverage outside the descriptor tail)**, so a few frontier members invisible to the porosity rule may remain unmeasured; the ranking within the top nine is by point estimate only, since reference uncertainties overlap.

## Strengths

- A genuinely novel evaluation axis: population-level replicated campaigns with joint trajectory analysis, cleanly distinguished from task-success benchmarks (CORE-Bench, PaperBench, AUTOMAT) — and the design isolates three quantities task-success evaluations conflate: trajectory diversity, conclusion stability, and independence of the scientific substrate.
- Exceptional procedural rigor for the field: public-hash pre-registration, pre-analysis amendments before report reading, hidden answer key with a literature-calibrated ceiling, controller re-running every headline value from archived inputs, Wilson intervals and exact tests, and a smoke phase whose defect ledger (success signals referring to the wrong object, e.g. RASPA exiting 0 after fatal input errors) is documented in detail.
- An independent ground truth most agent studies lack: 2,344 structures re-measured under the identical pinned protocol, with a frozen analysis plan, so agent claims can be scored objectively rather than judged by another LLM.
- The common-mode-error finding is a lasting conceptual contribution: "sixteen agents with that literature available treated a known class of artefact as a discovery" is the crispest demonstration to date that inter-agent agreement is not independent confirmation when inputs, tools and priors are shared.
- Radical transparency: verbatim session transcripts, complete git histories, charters, rubric, answer key and analysis scripts all released; every scored outcome anchored to the hidden key or auditable records.
- Honest negative results preserved inside agent reports (methylation series whose optimum is the unmodified parent; 399/400 candidates with no removable water), and the paper's own interpretation stays measured throughout.

## Limitations

- One model–harness (unpinned claude-opus-5), one database, one task; no second model or cross-domain replication, so nothing can be said about universality of the observed error rates (explicitly acknowledged).
- Small samples: n = 8 per arm leaves most behavioral contrasts nonsignificant; the pre-registered second wave of four deferred agents was never triggered (primary p = 1.0 fell outside the 0.05–0.25 window), so the design's power question remains untested.
- Spend cap (US$280, cache reads 59% of smoke-phase cost) was the binding budget for 12/16 agents, so "one-week budget" conclusions partly reflect token pricing, not scientific resource allocation; deadlines were also extended non-uniformly under standing rules (up to 24.7 h for one agent).
- Isolation enforced by charter and audited post hoc, not by operating-system boundaries; one real cross-contamination incident occurred (detected, excluded in sensitivity analyses).
- Post-hoc, single-coder strategy classes; no inter-rater reliability for the taxonomy used in the recovery comparison.
- Physics scope: rigid frameworks, no electrostatics, no relaxation or stability analysis for agent-built structures; the reference census of all 12,499 structures is still running, so frontier coverage outside the descriptor tail is ~16% sampled.
- General numerical Python libraries were available but standard materials tooling (Zeo++, pymatgen, MOFid) was withheld by the allowlist — agent tool-writing behavior here may not transfer to environments with rich domain tooling.

## Relevance

这篇论文正中用户最高优先级方向（自主科研智能体与长程自主系统的可信性）：它用 16 个复制智能体的人群实验证明"多智能体结果一致 ≠ 独立验证"，并实证展示强制复核门只会把复现率从 1/8 提到 8/8、却无法发现共享数据库层的数据缺陷——这是对 agent 评估方法论（复现 vs 复制 vs 独立确认）的一次关键概念澄清。对任何设计自动科研循环、多智能体交叉验证或 agent 基准的人，这是必读的实验设计范本。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the common-mode failure persist when replicas use **heterogeneous models or harnesses** (the author names this as the natural next test), or when replicas are allowed to communicate — does communication preserve strategic diversity or synchronize errors?
- Can checks be designed to target the **data-validity layer** (e.g., blinded chemistry audits validated against known cases, charge-balance verification against independent structures) rather than the calculation layer that enforced gates demonstrably moved?
- The one agent that avoided the trap did so by never examining the excluded entry — is avoidance-by-ignorance the dominant escape path, and what prior or tool would convert the ten recorded warning signs (bare metal centers, disconnected 2D sheets, extreme surrogate residuals) into rejection or escalation instead of explanation?
- Does the null correlation between search volume and frontier recovery (ρ = 0.10, p = 0.77) hold on flatter, less well-mapped landscapes, or is cheap verification of a steep frontier what makes brute-force screening useless here?
- With all 16 agents' full transcripts released, what mechanical signals (timing of first encounter with the champion, surrogate-residual magnitude, warning-sign logging) best predict downstream conclusion validity — i.e., can a population study yield an online early-warning detector for single-agent deployments?
