> **一句话总结：** 作者在叠加玩具模型（TMS）中构造一对"反极对齐"（编码器余弦 ρ=−1）的特征 A、B，从理论和实验两面证明 LEACE 式线性擦除会连同 A 一起摧毁（A 的重建 MSE 从 0.098 单调恶化到 0.411，即 4.2 倍），而直接对网络做梯度擦除（冻结编码器、微调解码器+bias）虽能把 B 压到≈0 且 A 的 MSE 全程稳定在 0.019–0.021，但收敛结果按 ρ 分岔为"mirror"（解码器近全反射，中位 cos=−0.86）与"shadow"（部分旋转+偏置压制，cos≈−0.40）两个吸引子，且两种解里一个标量因果补丁（shadow：bias 回补 α→1 使 B 读出 0→0.38；mirror：绝对值补丁一步恢复 0→0.13）即可让 B 的表征完全复活——为 LLM 遗忘中"抑制而非删除"的失效模式提供了首个因果充分的机制级解释。

> **TL;DR:** In the Toy Model of Superposition (Elhage et al. 2022), two features sharing one antipodal subspace cannot be separated by linear concept erasure: an LEACE-style rank-1 nullspace projection erases target B (residual ≈0) but drives feature A's preservation MSE from 0.098 at ρ=0 to 0.411 at ρ=−1 — a 4.2× degradation matching an exact ρ² energy-removal argument. Gradient-based excision (frozen encoder, decoder+bias fine-tuned) holds A's MSE flat at 0.019–0.021 across the full entanglement sweep, but bifurcates into two attractors: a "mirror" solution (decoder near full reflection, median cos(w'_A,w'_B)=−0.86) and a "shadow" solution (partial rotation + strong output-bias suppression). In both, a single targeted causal patch revives the erased feature — 0→0.38 readout via bias patch in shadow, 0→0.13 in one step via absolute-value patch in mirror — with zero effect on other features and a null negative control, establishing suppression-not-deletion as a causally sufficient mechanism. Basin prevalence flips sharply (75% mirror at ρ=−1 → 87% shadow at ρ=0, crossover ≈−0.75, Wilson 95% CIs over 100 seeds × 15 ρ values), and the bifurcation survives an extra hidden layer (91% output-layer-dominant suppression) and GELU (though BIC prefers 4 mixture components there).

## 核心贡献

- **线性擦除在反极纠缠下必然失败的精确刻画**：当 ρ=cos(w_A,w_B)=−1 时 A、B 共享同一子空间，秩-1 零空间投影（LEACE 类）只能"连坐"；附录 A.2 给出瓶颈层的精确结果——投影必然摧毁 A 表征能量的 ρ² 比例，无任何自由参数。实验上 A 的 preservation MSE 从 0.098（ρ=0）→0.144（ρ=−0.5）→0.411（ρ=−1），B 则在所有 ρ 下都被擦干净（残差≈0）。
- **发现并刻画"Janus 擦除"分岔**：梯度擦除（冻结编码器，最小化重建+λ·x̂_B² 惩罚，λ=16）不失败，但按初始化/训练动力学收敛到两个不同解——mirror（解码器 B 列近乎全反射，ρ=−1 时平均 cos=−0.933）和 shadow（部分旋转，cos≈−0.40，主要靠输出偏置压制）。二者是一个连续机制（解码器旋转+偏置压制）的不同配比，而非两种独立算法。
- **盆地占比的锐利相变**：15 个 ρ 值 × 100 seeds 的群体扫描显示 mirror 占比从 ρ=−1 的 75% 降到 ρ=0 的 13%（shadow 87%），交叉点 ρ≈−0.75，配 Wilson 95% 置信区间；单个 run 内部归属任意，但群体层面转变锐利——与 grokking 文献中"个体任意、群体锐利"的模式呼应。
- **单标量因果补丁证明"隐藏而非删除"**：shadow 解中把 b_B 从训练值回补向擦除前值（α∈[0,1]），B 的平均读出平滑地从 0 升到 0.38；mirror 解中一步绝对值补丁（z_B→|z_B|）使读出 0→0.13。充分性检查（补丁对其他特征读出零影响，且由加性偏置结构保证）+特异性检查（对随机无关特征打同样补丁，每盆地 20 trials，对 B 读出零效果）。
- **鲁棒性与理论补充**：分岔在加一层隐层后保持（91.0% 输出层主导压制 / 9.0% 隐层主导 / 0 分裂），GELU 下压制仍在但盆地结构变混（BIC 选出 4 组分）；附录证明线性映射因奇函数性无法只在一条射线上归零（ReLU 阈值规则是非线性专属）；并实证反极几何（ρ<−0.9）在普通稀疏训练下高频自然涌现。

## Key Contributions

- An exact account of why linear concept erasure (the LEACE class of rank-1 nullspace projections) must fail on antipodal feature pairs: at entanglement ρ, projection provably destroys a ρ² fraction of the preserved feature's representational energy at the bottleneck; empirically, preservation MSE for A degrades monotonically 0.098 → 0.411 (4.2×) as ρ goes 0 → −1 while B is cleanly erased.
- Identification and characterization of "Janus erasure": gradient-based excision bifurcates into two stable solution types — a mirror basin (decoder rotation to near full reflection, median final decoder cosine −0.86) and a shadow basin (partial rotation, mean cosine −0.40 at ρ=−1, plus strong output-bias suppression) — one continuous mechanism in different proportions.
- A sharp population-level phase transition in basin prevalence: 75% mirror at ρ=−1 vs 87% shadow at ρ=0, crossover at ρ≈−0.75, with Wilson 95% CIs over 100 seeds at each of 15 entanglement values.
- Causal sufficiency for suppression-not-deletion: one targeted scalar patch (bias restore in shadow: readout 0→0.38 graded; absolute-value patch in mirror: 0→0.13 discrete) fully recovers the erased feature with zero measurable side effects and a zero-effect negative control, mirroring the reversal failures reported empirically in LLM unlearning (Yang et al. 2026; Xu et al. 2025).
- Robustness checks (extra hidden layer: bifurcation persists, 91.0% output-layer-dominant suppression; GELU: suppression persists but basin structure blurs to 4 BIC-selected components) plus two appendixed theorems and a demonstration that antipodal geometry emerges naturally under ordinary sparsity training.

## Methodology

The testbed is the Toy Model of Superposition: an encoder W_E ∈ R^{d×n}, decoder W_I, and output bias trained to minimize ReLU-autoencoder reconstruction error on sparse inputs (16 features, bottleneck d ≪ n). For a designated pair (A, B), entanglement is controlled exactly as ρ = cos(w_A, w_B); antipodal geometry (ρ=−1) is enforced via an anti-co-occurrence sampling parameter (x_A·x_B = 0 with probability 1), while the other n−2 features train without geometric intervention. Section 3 establishes the linear baseline: rank-1 nullspace projection h' = h − (h·û)û with û = w_B/‖w_B‖ (LEACE's action for a single direction), sweeping ρ ∈ {0, −0.5, −1} (full 15-point sweep in Appendix B.6), tracking both B's residual activation and A's preservation MSE against a pre-intervention interference floor (0.098).

The excision procedure (Section 4) freezes W_E and fine-tunes only W_I and b_out with loss L = E[Σᵢ(x̂ᵢ−xᵢ)² + λ·x̂_B²], λ=16 (mean-reduced, an effective per-feature weight of 16), using AdamW. Grid construction is two-pass: a 5-point pilot (20 seeds/point) locates the transition zone, then a final 15-point grid (100 seeds/point) densifies it around ρ≈−0.75. Runs are labeled mirror/shadow post-hoc by a two-component Gaussian mixture (EM, full covariance) fit to (cos(w'_A, w'_B), b_B), ranked on bias as the more separating axis; causal-patch runs require posterior >0.9. Basin fractions are reported with Wilson 95% score intervals. Causal validation (Section 5) applies pure forward-pass patches — bias interpolation b_B(α) = (1−α)b_B^trained + α·b_B^pre-excision for shadow; z_B → |z_B| for mirror — with sufficiency checks (no other feature's readout changes, guaranteed structurally by additive per-feature bias) and a specificity negative control (identical patch on a random unrelated feature, 20 trials/basin). Robustness (Section 6) repeats the sweep with an added hidden layer and with GELU in place of ReLU; under GELU, where no hard-zero region exists, erasure is defined functionally (a patch restoring pre-excision parameters must exceed the effect of an identical patch on a control feature), and component count is selected by BIC over k ∈ {2,3,4} rather than forced to two. A compute-budget safeguard reduces grids to 7 points for the new architectures (flagged by the authors as a limitation of those checks). Appendix A proves the ρ² damage result and a sign-separability theorem (no odd linear map can zero one ray while sparing the other — ReLU thresholding is the minimal fix), and Appendix E documents a failure mode motivating the frozen encoder: with W_E unfrozen, descent finds a third route (shrinking B's encoder column toward zero) that is closer to genuine deletion.

## Results & Highlights

- **Linear erasure fails exactly as predicted (Table 1):** LEACE-style rank-1 projection erases B at every ρ (residual activation ≈0) but A's preservation MSE rises monotonically with entanglement — 0.098 (ρ=0.00) → 0.144 (ρ=−0.50) → 0.411 (ρ=−1.00), a 4.2× degradation at full antipodality; Appendix A.2 proves the removed energy fraction is exactly ρ² (a ρ²=0.25 interpolation predicts 0.176 vs the measured 0.144 at ρ=−0.5, and the authors candidly attribute the gap to the one bridging assumption).
- **Gradient excision preserves A flat in both basins (Table 2):** across the same 5-point sweep, preservation MSE for A stays in 0.0184–0.0207 (mirror) and 0.0197–0.0207 (shadow) — essentially at the interference floor — versus linear erasure's 0.098→0.411 rise, while B is still suppressed (non-zero residual only from the x̂_B² penalty's theoretical minimum x̂_B²/17).
- **Sharp basin-bifurcation phase transition (Figure 4):** mirror-basin fraction falls from 0.75 at ρ=−1.0 to 0.13 at ρ=0 (shadow reaching 0.87), crossover at ρ≈−0.75, with Wilson 95% CIs at each of 15 entanglement values (100 seeds each); geometry separates cleanly — mirror cluster mean final decoder cosine −0.933 vs shadow −0.40 at ρ=−1, both moving away from the pre-excision value −0.800.
- **Single-scalar causal recovery (Figure 5):** in the shadow basin, restoring b_B toward its pre-excision value lifts B's mean readout smoothly from 0 to 0.38 as α→1; in the mirror basin, a one-step absolute-value patch (z_B→|z_B|) recovers the readout from 0 to 0.13 — in neither case was any parameter beyond the single targeted scalar touched, and the same patch applied to a random unrelated feature (20 trials/basin) produced zero measurable effect on B.
- **Robustness (Section 6, Appendices C–D):** with an extra hidden layer the bifurcation persists and suppression is output-layer-dominant in 91.0% of seeds (637/700), hidden-layer-dominant in 9.0% (63), with 0 split runs; under GELU suppression still occurs but a BIC comparison prefers 4 mixture components (BIC −5096.1) over 2 (−4946.5), and output bias stays near zero across entanglement — a real departure from the clean two-basin ReLU picture that the authors report rather than hide.

## Authors & Credibility

Akash Samanta (corresponding) and Debasish Chaudhuri are at Techno India University (Salt Lake, Kolkata, India); Manish Pratap Singh is at the DRDO Young Scientist Laboratory - CT (Chennai, India). These are not established ML-interpretability groups, and the paper has no announced code release in the main text (the reproducibility statement instead commits to full procedural detail in appendices). That said, the execution quality is unusually high for an unknown group: exact-controlled entanglement, a two-pass densified experimental grid, Wilson intervals, post-hoc classifier diagnostics verifying that basin labels track raw geometry, negative controls for the causal patches, a candid Appendix E documenting their own earlier experimental artifact (unfrozen encoder → genuine deletion route), explicit GELU-vs-ReLU discrepancy analysis, and formal AI-use, ethics, and reproducibility statements. The work is positioned as a mechanistic complement to empirical LLM-unlearning findings (Yang et al. 2026 ICLR; Xu et al. 2025) and correctly cites the TMS/LEACE/causal-intervention canon.

## Critical Assessment

The core limitation is scale, and the authors say so plainly: everything rests on a 16-feature toy autoencoder with exactly one engineered antipodal pair. The small-scale causal transformer check (Appendix G) shows the phenomenon is not an autoencoder artifact, but the jump from "plausible in a small transformer" to "this explains LLM unlearning failures" (Yang et al. 2026; Xu et al. 2025) remains inferential — the paper never runs TOFU or any real unlearning benchmark, and never compares against actual nonlinear post-hoc erasure methods (RACE, kernelized RCDM), which its own related-work section flags as untested. Second, the frozen encoder is load-bearing: Appendix E shows that when W_E is trainable, gradient descent finds a third route — shrinking B's encoder column toward zero — that constitutes genuine deletion. Since essentially all practical unlearning fine-tunes upstream parameters, the paper's own evidence complicates the headline mapping onto LLM practice, and the authors honestly offer two hypotheses for why deletion appears unavailable in practice without settling one. Third, some apparatus is softer than it looks: "basin"/"attractor" is used informally with no stability proof — labels come from a post-hoc two-component GMM fit after convergence, and Figure 3 concedes that near the crossover (ρ=−0.75) trajectories overlap so heavily that labels are assigned "via the classifier at convergence," not from training dynamics; a formal dynamical-systems treatment is absent. Fourth, the GELU result quietly weakens the clean bifurcation story (BIC selects 4 components, not 2), and both robustness experiments inherit a reduced 7-point grid from a compute safeguard that, by the authors' own admission, under-samples ρ ∈ [−0.5, 0] — so the neat mirror-fraction curve in Figure 6 rests on asymmetric sampling outside the autoencoder baseline. Finally, the excision objective (λ=16 reweighted MSE) is bespoke; whether standard unlearning losses (e.g., representation misdirection, cited as untested) produce the same bifurcation is unknown. None of these undermine the internal results — which are replicated over 100 seeds, interval-quantified, and causally validated — but the paper is a mechanism proposal for a failure mode at scale, not a demonstration of it.

## Strengths

- Rare combination of exact theory and causal experiment: the ρ² damage law is derived without free parameters and then checked quantitatively (0.176 predicted vs 0.144 measured at ρ=−0.5, with the gap explained), and the sign-separability theorem explains precisely why nonlinearity is necessary.
- The causal-validation protocol is exemplary for a mechanism claim: targeted single-scalar patches, structural sufficiency (additive-bias architecture makes zero side effects a property, not a coincidence), an explicit negative control, and recovery demonstrated in both basins with qualitatively different response shapes (graded 0→0.38 vs discrete 0→0.13).
- Statistical hygiene unusual for interpretability work: Wilson 95% intervals at every swept point, 100 seeds per condition, two-pass grid densification around the transition, GMM classifier diagnostics against raw geometry, and BIC instead of forcing a two-basin label onto GELU data.
- Intellectual honesty throughout: Appendix E documents the authors' own earlier artifact (unfrozen encoder → deletion route) as a methodological correction; the limitations section pre-empts the toy-scale, single-pair, frozen-encoder, and untested-method objections rather than waiting for reviewers.
- The framing — "basins whose relative prevalence is a sharp function of entanglement, while individual outcomes are arbitrary" — connects a clean toy phenomenon to grokking-style population-vs-individual discrepancies, giving it broader dynamical interest.

## Limitations

- Toy scale: one antipodal pair in a 16-feature autoencoder; the transformer check is small-scale and does not establish the mechanism in language models, let alone that it explains TOFU-style unlearning failures.
- The frozen-encoder design is what makes suppression the outcome; with all parameters trainable the network deletes genuinely (Appendix E), so the relevance to real unlearning pipelines that fine-tune everything is a hypothesis, not a result.
- No formal attractor analysis: basin labels are post-hoc GMM assignments; near the crossover the two "basins" overlap heavily and the bifurcation's existence/stability is not proven.
- GELU blurs the two-basin picture (4 BIC components), and the robustness experiments use a reduced 7-point grid that under-samples part of the entanglement range.
- Missing comparisons: nonlinear post-hoc erasure (RACE, kernelized RCDM), literature-derived unlearning objectives (representation misdirection), and any standard unlearning benchmark; no code release stated.

## Relevance

这篇论文与机制可解释性和 LLM 遗忘安全两个方向都直接相关：它用叠加玩具模型把"遗忘只是抑制而非删除"这一 LLM 实证发现（Yang et al. 2026、Xu et al. 2025）落实到了单标量可补丁、可证伪的机制层面，并为评估"行为消失 ≠ 知识删除"提供了因果干预的模板。对关注 superposition、concept erasure（LEACE 系）与 unlearning 鲁棒性的研究者的启发在于：判断遗忘是否成功必须用因果补丁检查表征是否仍可恢复。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the mirror/shadow bifurcation survive literature-drawn unlearning objectives (e.g., representation misdirection, gradient-ascent variants) rather than the bespoke λ=16 excision loss?
- Why does deletion (the third route of Appendix E) dominate when the encoder is unfrozen in the toy, yet real LLM unlearning appears to suppress instead — is it optimization geometry, loss design, or the depth of the suppressed feature?
- Can a comparable single-scalar (or single-direction) recovery patch be constructed for SAE/LEACE-style erased features in a production LLM, measured on TOFU or WMDP rather than a synthetic antipodal pair?
- Does the sharp population-level basin transition at ρ≈−0.75 have a dynamical-systems explanation, and does the same finite-size vs population gap hold inside a single training run (grokking-style)?
- How does the bifurcation behave when more than one pair shares entanglement — do mirror/shadow basins remain distinct, or does interference create a third attractor resembling genuine deletion?
