02Why this matters

Safety schemes that monitor CoT lean on behavioral faithfulness tests — these results show they systematically overstate causal faithfulness, most severely on easy reasoning where trust is highest.

03Critical edge

Every headline number comes from one synthetic pseudo-word lookup task and two small Qwen3 models; the 11.4-point gap is quantified only in this regime — transfer to naturalistic CoT is plausible but unproven.

AHow faithfulness was measured

  1. 1Task. Multi-hop lookups (2–6 hops) over in-context knowledge bases of pseudo-word entities, ruling out pretraining leakage. For each hop, a counterfactual KB alters one fact so the step resolves differently; bridge facts keep the implanted answer derivable in-context. A validator re-derives both answers (1,000 passing pairs).
  2. 2Causal probe (CLB). Locate the exact token span where the model states step k; cache residual-stream activations there; patch them into the clean run at layer L. A hit = the final answer flips to the specific predicted counterfactual entity — not just any change.
  3. 3Behavioral baseline. Same items: rewrite the step’s text to the counterfactual entity, truncate, re-continue — the standard deployed paradigm.
  4. 4Controls. Patch the KB fact itself (positive ceiling) · patch after the answer is emitted (sanity, must be 0) · patch a foreign donor entity (specificity).
  5. 5Discipline. Greedy decoding, bf16, fixed seeds; 95% CIs; 221/250 clean runs for Qwen3-4B, 120/250 for Qwen3-1.7B.

Faithfulness tracks capability, not difficulty: 1.7B peaks at 54.8% (best layer) vs 4B’s ~77%, and collapses with depth while 4B stays flat. Effect lives in early–mid layers — gone by layer 20/36 (4B) and 12–16/28 (1.7B).

CStrengths

  • Flip-to-target criterion gives the causal metric specificity that degradation-based audits lack — a flip must land on the constructed counterfactual.
  • Rare three-control rigor for a short paper: empirical ceiling (83%), pipeline sanity (0 flips), specificity (1–15%).
  • Item-matched McNemar design quantifies the gap between deployed measurement practice and causal ground truth — the actionable number.
  • Deterministic task + KB validator: counterfactual answers guaranteed derivable in-context; pseudo-words remove leakage ambiguity.
  • Honest, specific limitations — not boilerplate.

DLimitations

  • Synthetic single-relation lookup only; naturalistic reasoning (math, code) may show different profiles and gaps.
  • One model family, two small scales, greedy decoding — the capability trend is a two-point observation.
  • Severe run attrition for 1.7B (120/250 usable) — survivorship selection likely inflates the weak model’s estimates.
  • Early-layer patches confounded with token-identity replacement; block-output patching localizes coarsely (layer 8 headlined for this reason).
  • One behavioral protocol tested; span-alignment truncation and differing observation channels add asymmetry.
  • No code or data release stated.

EQuestions for further exploration

  1. Does the 11.4-point overestimate transfer to naturalistic CoT — arithmetic, symbolic planning, code — where steps carry inferred content rather than entity lookups?
  2. Would the capability-vs-difficulty trend replicate across families (Llama, Gemma, DeepSeek) and larger scales, or is it a Qwen3-specific artifact?
  3. Do other behavioral protocols — Lanham-style truncation curves, Turpin-style biasing features, paraphrase consistency — also overestimate item-matched?
  4. Does patching attention and MLP outputs separately (not fused block outputs) sharpen the early-layer confound and the layer profile?
  5. Can a CLB-like quantity be estimated when no counterfactual target can be constructed — i.e., where auditing is currently purely behavioral?
