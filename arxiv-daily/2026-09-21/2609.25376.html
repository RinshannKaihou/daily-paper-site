Key Finding · Paper Dashboard

Quantizing more action-head layers lifts π₀.₅ success 7.0% → 70.5% — which layers you quantize matters more than how many bits.

VLAQuantBench: Closed-Loop Evaluation of Post-Training Quantization for Vision-Language-Action Models
Jiuyi Xu et al. · Colorado School of Mines · UCF · USC ICT · Microsoft AI · arXiv:2609.25376 · 2026-09-21
Post-Training Quantization Vision-Language-Action Robotics Benchmark Efficient Inference Model Evaluation
409 evaluation runs 94,574 simulation episodes 4 released VLAs 6 RTN presets 1,200 physical rollouts
Key Metric
+63.5 pp
Success gained by quantizing MORE of π₀.₅'s action head (126 → 167 layers) under uncalibrated W4A4 — confirmed by fixed-observation replay (chunk MAE 0.514 → 0.390 on 217/219 chunks). Task-clustered 95% CI [+46.0, +80.5]. evidence ↓
Why This Matters

Layer selection beats bit-width: protecting one 28.7K-parameter projection rescues a fully collapsed policy — immediate precision-allocation guidance for anyone deploying VLAs on real robots.

⚠ Critical Edge

The flagship recovery holds only for uncalibrated W4A4 RTN — the paper's own W4/W4A8 controls show no such interaction, and the mechanism remains unresolved. → limitations

MMethodology — a controlled factorial, not another quantizer
01

One fixed RTN anchor, six presets

W3 · W4 · W8 · W4A4 · W4A8 · W8A8. Weights: asymmetric per-output-channel grids, group size 128. Activations: dynamic per-token absmax. Embeddings, norms, attention products and the vocab head stay at released precision. Quantization is simulated (round + dequant) — these are accuracy effects, not packed-kernel effects.

02

Scope factorial down to one projection

Each policy is partitioned into vision encoder / projector / LLM / action head, then layer groups, down to single projections — the finest intervention quantizes exactly 1 layer. Every run records checkpoint, config, and quantized layer/parameter counts.

03

Coverage

4 models × all four LIBERO suites (200 episodes/config); X-VLA additionally on SIMPLER-VM, CALVIN ABC→D and VLABench. Unequal-coverage cells are excluded from precision comparisons, never pooled.

04

Uncertainty + causal controls

95% Wilson intervals, task-clustered bootstrap (5,000 draws), seed replications. Twenty held-out full-precision π₀.₅ trajectories are replayed with fixed observations and flow noise to separate numerical interaction from task-threshold artifacts.

05

Reality checks

AWQ / NF4 / LLM.int8() / SmoothQuant on the LLM scope; a 2-episode smoothing+clipping calibration control (α ∈ {0.5, 0.75}); RTX 4090 kernel profiling; 1,200 physical rollouts on a Franka Research 3. Stack audits: pinned MuJoCo, SHA-256 checkpoints, activation-hook coverage (a missing hook silently degrades A8 runs to weight-only).

  • Calibration is part of the configuration — and does not transfer. Two-episode smoothing+clipping (α=0.5) lifts π₀.₅ union 1.5% → 97.5%, yet drops π₀ end-to-end 61.0% → 10.5%; π₀.₅ needs α=0.75 for 91.5%.
  • The physical robot agrees. Franka Research 3, 1,200 rollouts: W3 collapses Pick to 12.0% (OFT) / 10.0% (π₀.₅) from 88.0/84.0%; W8/W8A8 stay within ~6 pp of BF16; W4A8 costs 18–40 pp over W4.
SStrengths
  • The right experimental template: a fixed-quantizer factorial isolates format / scope / calibration effects that prior VLA-PTQ evaluations conflate.
  • Massive, properly-treated evidence: 409 runs / 94,574 episodes with Wilson intervals, clustered bootstrap, and explicit statements of what the intervals do NOT cover.
  • Triangulated headline: closed-loop success + fixed-observation replay + error-ratio decomposition — not just one success curve.
  • Actionable output: the protect-the-output-projection rule is a cheap precision-allocation heuristic; kernel data deflates "quantize for speed."
  • Unusually candid limitations and fully public code, configs, and episode records — including eval-stack traps other groups will hit.
LLimitations
  • Only four checkpoints, none spanning the full VLA space; no autoregressive action-token policy evaluated.
  • Accuracy results use simulated quantization; real-kernel profiling covers one model's LLM scope on a single RTX 4090; protected-W3 has 3 seeds on one model family.
  • The non-monotone recovery is established only for uncalibrated W4A4 RTN, with no causal mechanism — replay verifies the reversal but not why 41 added layers matter.
  • Calibration exploration is thin: two episodes from one task, α ∈ {0.5, 0.75}; VLA-specific PTQ methods excluded from matched comparison.
  • Only X-VLA leaves LIBERO, where near-ceiling baselines (95–100%) limit resolution exactly where deployment decisions get risky.
  • Physical study uses separately fine-tuned policies — sim-to-real transfer is untested, not demonstrated.
QQuestions for Further Exploration
  1. What causes the 126→167-layer W4A4 recovery — does the skip-path hypothesis survive direct intervention on the residual branch?
  2. Does the protect-the-output-projection rule transfer to autoregressive action-token VLAs and to packed low-bit kernels end-to-end?
  3. Would principled calibration (learned scales, GPTQ-style block reconstruction) eliminate the non-additive union failure, or just move it?
  4. Can task-clustered sensitivity be predicted before deployment from a cheap probe, given that kurtosis and absmax diagnostics demonstrably fail?
  5. Do the W4A4 collapse and non-monotone recovery persist in the cross-embodiment, long-horizon regime (CALVIN/VLABench)?
