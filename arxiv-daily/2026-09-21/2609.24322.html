Critical edge

The headline counts neutral flips: 68.8% of W4 changes are between documents with no relevance judgement; mean served-document relevance falls only 0.010.

01 Method, distilled

53 checkpoints→ 6 quantizers→ 2 readouts→ 2,118 configurations
  • Models. ViT-B/16 & ViT-L/16 on 21 image-classification tasks; Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B on 11 MTEB tasks; plus GTE / BGE / E5, zero-shot CLIP on 4,000 Flickr30k images, and a MiniLM cross-encoder reranker.
  • Quantization. Weight-only, 3–4 bits, per-channel scale, group size 128, every linear layer — RTN, HQQ, GPTQ±act-order, AWQ, AdaRound; calibration data always disjoint from evaluation.
  • Key control — self-retrieval. Each ViT is retrieved against its own test split, so both numbers come from the same checkpoint; only the readout differs (linear probe vs cosine nearest-neighbor).
  • Measure. Label-free top-1 change rate vs full precision — a flip between two equally relevant documents still counts. BEIR judgements (nDCG@10, gold-document loss) reported separately.
  • Theory. Lemma 1: if the top-2 gap g₂ ≥ 2ε, no perturbation with ‖δ‖∞ ≤ ε can flip top-1. A split-conformal threshold on 128 unlabeled queries adds a distribution-free violation bound.
  • Fixes. Allocation: 128 calibration queries score each layer by induced gap shift; bits spent greedily to a 3.5-bit budget. Routing: send inputs whose quantized gap falls below τ to full precision — no training, one threshold.

02 Results

Stronger quantizers don’t fix retrieval. GPTQ restores ViT-L/16 classification accuracy 55.5% → 88.6% (11× fewer flips) — yet W3 retrieval on the same model still flips 78.9% of top-1s.

Fix 1 — gap-sensitivity bit allocation (retrieval)

At a 3.5-bit budget, recovers 60–73% of the W3→W4 benefit — first among all 7 forward-only criteria under all 6 quantizers (reconstruction-error allocation: 40–48%).

Fix 2 — conformal routing to full precision (classification)

Routing the lowest-gap 25% of inputs recovers 85–93% of lost accuracy at 47% of FP compute — beating MSP and predictive entropy at nearly every operating point.

03 Strengths

  • A crisp, label-free diagnostic that costs nothing extra — calibration reuses the PTQ pipeline — with both a deterministic guarantee (Lemma 1) and a conformal variant.
  • Exceptionally clean causal design: one checkpoint, one dataset, one quantizer, two readouts; rival explanations (accuracy, corpus size 58×, model scale 13×, quantizer strength, reranking) systematically eliminated.
  • Both fixes are drop-in: allocation improves every one of the six quantizers; routing needs no training and a single threshold.
  • Unusually honest reporting — states where the gap loses to MSP, and that most flips are between equally relevant documents.
  • Rigor at scale: 53 checkpoints, 2,118 configurations, proofs checked by exhaustive enumeration, full scripts for every reported number.

04 Limitations

  • Weight-only, 3–4 bits only; activation / KV-cache quantization and QAT untested — W8A8-style LLM deployments unexamined.
  • The conformal guarantee assumes calibration/deployment exchangeability; it degrades under the distribution shift common in production traffic.
  • Exact cosine search over four small BEIR corpora (~1k–57k docs); approximate nearest-neighbor indexes and their ranking errors are out of scope.
  • The allocation benchmark excludes Hessian-based methods (HAWQ family) — superiority is established only within the forward-only pool.
  • In retrieval the stability check accepts almost no queries (11.3% at W4) — no per-query safety exactly where damage is worst.

05 Questions for further exploration

  1. Does the top-2-gap mechanism survive activation and KV-cache quantization in full LLM-plus-retriever stacks, where perturbations compound?
  2. Can contrastive training add an explicit top-2 gap margin — engineering the separation retrieval currently lacks?
  3. How quickly does conformal calibration break under realistic query drift, and can it be recalibrated online?
  4. Does gap-sensitivity bit allocation transfer to ANN indexes (HNSW / IVF-PQ), where perturbation and approximation interact?
  5. Would mixed-precision retrieval plus per-query FP fallback dominate either fix alone — and how to price it against SLA latency budgets?
