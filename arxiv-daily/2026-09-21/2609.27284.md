> **一句话总结：** 腾讯发布开源 MoE 大模型 Hunyuan-A13B（80B 总参数 / 仅 13B 激活，256K 上下文），通过 20T token 的 STEM 增强预训练 + 四阶段（推理 SFT → GRPO 推理 RL → 全场景 SFT → 全场景 RL）后训练 + 快/慢双模式 CoT 切换，以约 1/2 的激活参数量在 AIME2024 上取得 87.3 分（超过激活参数约为其 2 倍的 Qwen3-A22B 的 85.7 与 DeepSeek-R1-0120 的 79.8），并在智能体工具调用基准 BFCL v3 上以 78.3 vs 70.8 领先 Qwen3-A22B，batch 32 吞吐可达约 1982 tokens/s。

> **TL;DR:** Tencent open-sources Hunyuan-A13B, an 80B-total / 13B-active MoE LLM (1 shared + 64 routed experts, 8 active per token, 256K context) pretrained on a 20T-token corpus with 250B extra curated STEM tokens, then post-trained in four stages (reasoning SFT, GRPO-based reasoning RL with a 36-language code sandbox, all-scenarios SFT, and all-scenarios RL with generative reward models), plus a dual-mode CoT switch ("/think" vs "/no_think"). In slow-thinking mode it hits 87.3 on AIME2024 (vs 85.7 for Qwen3-A22B with ~2x active params, and 79.8 for DeepSeek-R1-0120) and 78.3 on BFCL v3 (vs 70.8), while RULER-QA long-context stays at 73.9 in the 64K-128K bucket (vs 66.6 for Qwen3-A22B); measured throughput reaches 1981.99 tokens/s at batch 32. The gains are not uniform — it trails Qwen3-A22B on SuperGPQA (41.3 vs 44.1), AIME2025 (76.8 vs 81.5), and slow-thinking LiveCodeBench (65.9 vs 70.7), and fast-thinking mode collapses on hard math (AIME2025: 19.2 vs 76.8 slow).

## 核心贡献

- **稀疏 MoE 架构设计**：80B 总参数 / 13B 激活的细粒度 MoE（1 个共享专家 + 64 个专用专家，每 token 激活 1 共享 + 8 专用），配合 GQA（32 注意力头 / 8 KV 头）、SwiGLU、128K 词表、256K 上下文。实验观察：完全去掉共享专家会明显掉点，但共享专家数量超过 1 个后收益递减。
- **20T token 三段式预训练**：复用 Hunyuan-TurboS 数据管线并强化 STEM 数据采集与清洗（额外提取 250B 高质量 STEM token + 知识标注/多维难度分级体系）；Foundation（20T tokens，4K 上下文）→ Fast Annealing（300B tokens，8K 上下文，LR 降至 8e-6）→ 长上下文训练（NTK-aware RoPE，alpha=50 扩到 32K、alpha=1000 扩到 256K）。
- **四阶段后训练管线**：推理 SFT（数学/代码/逻辑/科学四域，拒绝采样验证）→ 推理 RL（GRPO，结果奖励模型 + 支持 36 种编程语言、千级并发沙箱的代码反馈；150K 样本，Math:Coding:Logic:Science = 2:2:1:1；两阶段 24K→32K 上下文，去掉 KL 约束）→ 全场景 SFT（8 大领域）→ 全场景 RL（生成式奖励模型 GRM，16 个子话题、30+ 打分服务，正确性 + 风格双信号）。
- **双模式 CoT（Dual-Mode CoT）**：同一模型统一训练两种推理模式——"/no_think" 快思考（`<think>` 块为空，低延迟）与 "/think" 慢思考（完整反思/回溯链，默认），按任务复杂度与资源约束灵活切换。
- **推理效率与部署**：支持 W4A16 / W8A16 / KV Cache FP8 无损量化，兼容 vLLM、SGLang、TensorRT-LLM 及 TP/EP/FusedMoE；A16W16C16 下吞吐从 batch 1 的 190.84 tok/s 到 batch 32 的 1981.99 tok/s。模型权重开源。
- **评测体系扩充**：引入 ArtifactsBench（前端代码生成）以及内部基准 LengthCtrl、InsCtrl（文本生成）、ComplexNLU、Word-Task（NLU），并在 BFCL v3、τ-Bench、ComplexFuncBench、C³-Bench 四个智能体基准上系统评测。

## Key Contributions

- **Sparse MoE design**: 80B total / 13B active parameters via fine-grained MoE (1 shared + 64 specialized experts, 8 routed experts active per token), GQA (32 heads / 8 KV heads), SwiGLU, 128K vocab, 256K context. Empirical observation: removing the shared expert entirely noticeably hurts performance, but adding more than one shared expert yields diminishing returns.
- **Three-stage 20T-token pretraining**: reuses the Hunyuan-TurboS data pipeline with strengthened STEM acquisition/cleaning (250B extra high-quality STEM tokens, refined knowledge labeling and multi-dimensional difficulty grading); Foundation stage (20T tokens, 4K ctx, peak LR 3e-4) → Fast Annealing (300B tokens, 8K ctx, down to 8e-6) → Long-Context stage (NTK-aware RoPE, alpha=50 for 32K, alpha=1000 for 256K).
- **Four-stage post-training**: reasoning SFT (math/code/logic/science with rejection-sampling verification) → reasoning RL (GRPO with an outcome reward verifier plus a 36-language sandbox sustaining >1000 concurrent executions; 150K prompts at Math:Coding:Logic:Science = 2:2:1:1; two context phases 24K→32K; KL-divergence constraint removed) → all-scenarios SFT (8 domains) → all-scenarios RL (generative reward model across 16 sub-topics and >30 scoring services, dual correctness+style signal).
- **Dual-Mode CoT**: a unified training scheme where "/no_think" yields fast answers (empty `<think>` block, low latency) and "/think" yields full step-by-step reasoning (default when no tag is given), letting users trade latency for accuracy per request.
- **Inference efficiency**: lossless quantization options (W4A16, W8A16, KV Cache FP8), one-click deployment on vLLM / SGLang / TensorRT-LLM with TP/EP/FusedMoE; throughput from 190.84 tok/s (batch 1) to 1981.99 tok/s (batch 32) under A16W16C16. Weights are open-sourced.
- **Extended evaluation suite**: introduces ArtifactsBench (frontend code generation) plus internal LengthCtrl, InsCtrl (text generation) and ComplexNLU, Word-Task (NLU) benchmarks, and systematically evaluates four agent benchmarks (BFCL v3, τ-Bench, ComplexFuncBench, C³-Bench).

## Methodology

**Architecture.** Hunyuan-A13B follows the fine-grained MoE recipe (DeepSeekMoF-style): 32 layers, 1 perpetually-active shared expert plus 64 fine-grained specialized experts of identical intermediate dimension, of which 8 are routed per token; total 80B parameters with 13B active. Attention uses GQA (32 query heads, 8 KV heads) to shrink the KV cache; activation is SwiGLU; tokenizer/vocabulary (128K) is inherited from Hunyuan-Large. The authors report from their MoE scaling experiments that at least one shared expert is needed (models with zero shared experts underperform), while more than one brings only marginal or fluctuating gains.

**Pre-training.** Data curation reuses the Hunyuan-TurboS pipeline (completion dedup, low-quality filtering, topic labeling, model-based plain-text extraction, post-processing with semantic dedup), with the STEM sub-modules upgraded: 250 billion high-quality STEM tokens were extracted, plus a refined knowledge-labeling system and a multi-dimensional difficulty-grading framework. Training runs in three stages: (1) Foundation — 20T tokens at 4096 context, LR warmup to 3e-4 then cosine decay to 3e-5 over 13.5T tokens and constant thereafter; (2) Fast Annealing — 300B tokens at 8192 context decaying to 8e-6; (3) Long-Context — two NTK-aware RoPE phases extending context to 32K (alpha=50) and then 256K (alpha=1000), matching Hunyuan-TurboS.

**Post-training (4 stages).** Stage 1 (reasoning SFT): curated instruction-response pairs with chain-of-thought across math (textbook/competition sources, generative RM + automated verification loops), code (a GitHub-derived pipeline with multi-stage critic/sandbox validation), logic (ZebraLogic-inspired automated synthesis with two-tier validation), and science (LLM-based verifier plus rejection sampling). Stage 2 (reasoning RL): GRPO on 150K unstable-case prompts with two rewards — a lightweight LLM outcome reward model (binary, normalized for formatting/unit/synonym variance) and sandbox feedback (36 languages, >1000 concurrent executions on a distributed CPU cluster with file/network isolation); training excludes multiple-choice/true-false problems so every reward is verifiable; two context phases (24K then 32K); the KL constraint vs the reference policy is removed, with on-policy training, large batches, more rollouts, and temperature 0.6-0.8. Stage 3 (all-scenarios SFT): eight domains — language understanding, creative writing, multilingual, complex instruction scenarios, role-play, knowledge QA, multi-turn dialogues, and agent — the last via a five-role multi-turn synthetic data engine (user, planner, tool, agent, checker) combining sandbox tools, MCPs, and synthetic tools, with ~30 agent system-instruction types and 20,000 format combinations. Stage 4 (all-scenarios RL): a generative reward model (GRM) compares candidates against a reference answer as a semantic anchor, optionally ingesting CoT traces or invoking external tools; 12 domain pipelines (text understanding, translation, long context, creative writing, agents, multi-turn, complex instructions, role-play, safety, knowledge QA, multilingual, finance/legal/medical) over 16 sub-topics with adversarial prompt filtering and dynamic sampling.

**Dual-Mode CoT.** Both modes are trained on the same structured template differing only in the `<think>` block: empty ("\n\n") for fast-thinking, step-by-step reasoning for slow-thinking. Users select via "/no_think" or "/think"; untagged requests default to slow-thinking.

**Evaluation protocol.** Base model: MMLU/MMLU-Pro/MMLU-Redux/BBH/SuperGPQA (general), EvalPlus/MultiPL-E/MBPP/CRUX (coding), MATH/CMATH/GSM8K/GPQA (math/STEM). Post-trained model: AIME 2024/2025, MATH, GPQA-Diamond, OlympiadBench, LiveCodeBench, FullStackBench, ArtifactsBench, BBH, DROP (F1), ZebraLogic, IF-Eval, SysBench, plus internal LengthCtrl/InsCtrl/ComplexNLU/Word-Task and four agent benchmarks; long-context via PenguinScrolls, LongBench-v2, FRAMES, and RULER-QA across length buckets.

## Results & Highlights

- **Math at SOTA-level for its size (slow-thinking, Table 3):** AIME2024 = 87.3, the highest among all compared models — Qwen3-A22B 85.7, DeepSeek-R1-0120 79.8, OpenAI-o1-1217 74.3 — despite having roughly half the active parameters of Qwen3-A22B (13B vs 22B) and ~1/6 its total size. On ZebraLogic it scores 82.2 (vs 80.3 Qwen3-A22B, 78.7 DeepSeek-R1) and on BBH 89.1 (vs 88.7 / 85.7).
- **Base model beats a 5x-larger predecessor (Table 2):** with 13B active params, base Hunyuan-A13B outperforms Hunyuan-Large-388B/52B-active on 12 of 14 benchmarks — e.g., MATH 72.35 vs 69.80, GPQA 49.12 vs 25.18, EvalPlus 78.64 vs 75.69, MBPP 83.86 vs 72.60 — and beats the similar-total-size dense Qwen2.5-72B on almost all (MATH 72.35 vs 62.12, GPQA 49.12 vs 45.90). Against Qwen3-A22B it wins 7 of 12, though it loses SuperGPQA (41.32 vs 44.06), GSM8K (91.83 vs 94.39), and CRUX-O (77.00 vs 79.00).
- **Agent / tool calling is the standout (Table 3):** BFCL v3 = 78.3 (vs 70.8 Qwen3-A22B, 67.8 o1-1217, 56.9 DeepSeek-R1); ComplexFuncBench = 61.2, +13.6 over the best baseline (o1-1217 47.6); C³-Bench = 63.5 (vs 58.8 o1); τ-Bench = 54.7 beats Qwen3-A22B (44.6) and DeepSeek-R1 (43.9) but trails o1-1217 (60.4).
- **Long-context stability (Tables 5-6):** RULER-QA average 76.7 (vs Qwen3-A22B 73.0, DeepSeek-R1 72.0; Gemini 2.5 Pro leads at 81.7); in the hardest 64K-128K bucket Hunyuan-A13B holds 73.9 vs 66.6 (Qwen3-A22B) and 65.6 (DeepSeek-R1) — second only to Gemini 2.5 Pro (80.2). On PenguinScrolls it scores 87.7, edging DeepSeek-R1 (87.5) and Qwen3-A22B (87.1), just under Gemini 2.5 Pro (88.3); LongBench-v2 = 55.0 (second, behind Gemini's 60.9, ahead of DeepSeek-R1 53.8); FRAMES = 81.1 trails DeepSeek-R1 (85.7) and Qwen3-A22B (84.0).
- **Dual-mode trade-off is large (Table 4 vs Table 3):** in fast-thinking mode AIME2024 drops to 40.6 (vs 87.3 slow) and AIME2025 to 19.2 (vs 76.8 slow), yet fast mode still beats Qwen3-A22B fast on IF-Eval (84.2 vs 65.2) and GPQA-Diamond (71.5 vs 62.9). Slow mode also wins instruction following: IF-Eval 84.2 vs Qwen3-A22B 83.4, DeepSeek-R1 83.3, o1-1217 91.8.
- **Throughput (Table 7, A16W16C16, 2048-in/14336-out):** 190.84 tokens/s at batch 1, 1246.54 at batch 16, 1981.99 at batch 32 (1725.95 at 22528-in/22528-out). Quantization options (W4A16/W8A16/KV FP8) are claimed lossless but no accuracy-vs-quantization table is given.

## Authors & Credibility

The paper is authored by the Tencent Hunyuan Team, the industrial lab behind Hunyuan-Large (a 389B/52B-active open MoE, 2024) and Hunyuan-TurboS, and the lineage is explicit: the data pipeline, tokenizer, and RoPE-extension recipe are all inherited from those predecessors, and the PenguinScrolls long-context benchmark cited in the evaluation is a Tencent Hunyuan release. This is a credible industrial group with real large-scale training infrastructure — the described RL stack (distributed 1000+-execution sandbox, 30+ scoring services, 16 RL sub-topics) and the three-stage 20T-token pretrain are consistent with frontier-lab practice. Credibility caveats: this is a team-authored technical report without an individual-author accountability list, several headline benchmarks are internal, and the PDF header carries a 2025-06-27 date while the arXiv stamp is September 2026, so versioning/timeline is slightly murky.

## Critical Assessment

- **"Comparable to much larger models" holds on aggregate, not uniformly.** The paper's own tables show systematic losses to Qwen3-A22B: SuperGPQA 41.32 vs 44.06 (base), AIME2025 76.8 vs 81.5, OlympiadBench 82.7 vs 85.7, and slow-thinking LiveCodeBench 65.9 vs 70.7 — coding is explicitly conceded to be "slightly lower." The strong claims are specifically about math (AIME2024), agents, and long-context stability, and there they are well supported.
- **Four of the winning benchmarks are internal and not yet public.** LengthCtrl, InsCtrl, ComplexNLU, and Word-Task are Tencent-internal, and ArtifactsBench — where the model's 43.0 vs o1's 38.6 is cited — is only "to be made publicly available soon." That is a meaningful fraction of the "second-best or top" claims that outside researchers currently cannot verify, a classic self-benchmarking risk even though the third-party benchmarks (AIME, BFCL, RULER, PenguinScrolls) do carry the story.
- **The throughput claim lacks a baseline.** Table 7 reports only Hunyuan-A13B's own throughput (190.84→1981.99 tok/s across batch sizes); despite the abstract's "superior inference throughput" pitch, there is no measured comparison against Qwen3-A22B or any dense 72B under identical hardware, so the efficiency advantage must be inferred from parameter counts rather than demonstrated.
- **Key training choices are asserted, not ablated.** Removing the KL constraint during RL, the 2:2:1:1 domain ratio, the 250B STEM token boost, and the "one shared expert is optimal" architecture rule are all stated from internal experience with no published ablation tables; two cells (CRUX-I and CMATH for Qwen3-A22B) are simply missing from Table 2. No GPU-hours or training-cost disclosure is given, and no safety evaluation numbers accompany the safety-oriented RL stage.
- **Fast mode's utility is under-characterized.** Fast-thinking AIME2025 collapses to 19.2 (vs 76.8 slow), and no per-mode latency measurements are reported — the entire premise of the dual-mode design (speed-accuracy dial) is never quantified on the speed axis inside the paper.

## Strengths

- Genuinely useful open-source point: 13B active parameters make single-GPU / low-latency deployment realistic while 256K context and 80B total capacity are retained — a rare spot in the open-weight landscape.
- Agent/tool-calling results are strong on independent third-party benchmarks, not just internal ones: BFCL v3 78.3, ComplexFuncBench 61.2, C³-Bench 63.5 all clearly ahead of DeepSeek-R1-0120 and Qwen3-A22B.
- Long-context degradation is unusually graceful: only ~2.8 points of RULER-QA drop from the sub-32K buckets to 64K-128K (73.9), versus ~6.4 for Qwen3-A22B, suggesting the NTK-aware RoPE schedule actually works.
- Verifiable-reward discipline in reasoning RL (multiple-choice and true/false excluded, sandbox execution for code, unit/format normalization in the verifier) reduces reward-hacking risk relative to verbal-reward setups.
- Practical deployment story: quantization formats, mainstream inference engines (vLLM/SGLang/TensorRT-LLM), TP/EP/FusedMoE parallelism, and a documented dual-mode API contract ("/think" / "/no_think").

## Limitations

- Coding remains the weakest axis: slow-thinking LiveCodeBench 65.9 vs Qwen3-A22B's 70.7, and FullStackBench 67.8 vs DeepSeek-R1's 71.6; fast-thinking coding drops further (LiveCodeBench 27.4).
- Science/QA depth lags the frontier: GPQA-Diamond 71.2 (slow) vs o1-1217's 78.0, and base-model SuperGPQA 41.32 vs Qwen3-A22B's 44.06.
- Several headline evaluations rely on internal benchmarks pending public release; no third-party replication is possible yet for LengthCtrl/InsCtrl/ComplexNLU/Word-Task/ArtifactsBench.
- No latency or cost measurements per CoT mode, no throughput baselines, no quantized-accuracy tables, and no training-compute disclosure — the efficiency narrative is only half-substantiated.
- AIME2025 (76.8) trails Qwen3-A22B (81.5) even in the paper's strongest domain, indicating year-to-year variance on competition math is significant for small-active models.
- No safety benchmark results despite a dedicated safety RL track; fine-grained ablations (shared-expert count, KL removal, STEM data boost) are described only qualitatively.

## Relevance

对关注大模型效率与部署的用户高度相关：Hunyuan-A13B 展示了"细粒度 MoE + 少量激活参数 + 双模式推理"如何在数学推理与智能体工具调用上逼近两倍激活规模的模型，是 MoE 缩放律、可验证奖励 RL 与推理系统优化三个方向的实用参考实现，权重开源可直接复现其吞吐与量化配置。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How much of the agent-benchmark advantage (BFCL v3 78.3) comes from the five-role synthetic agent data engine versus the sandbox-based rule+correctness reward — is there an ablation separating data from RL?
- What is the measured latency and cost per token of fast vs slow thinking on the same hardware, and can a learned router (rather than user-supplied "/think" tags) match slow-mode accuracy at fast-mode cost?
- Does the KL-free GRPO recipe remain stable outside the four reasoning domains, or does reward hacking re-emerge on the open-ended all-scenarios tasks?
- How do W4A16/W8A16/KV-FP8 quantizations affect AIME/GPQA accuracy in practice, given "lossless" is claimed without a table?
- Can the graceful 64K-128K RULER retention (73.9) be replicated at 256K with the alpha=1000 RoPE setting, and what does long-context retention cost in short-context benchmark degradation?
- Will Tencent release the missing Qwen3-A22B baselines (CRUX-I, CMATH) and the ArtifactsBench harness so the internal-benchmark wins can be independently verified?
