Why this matters

If you report CKA/mKNN-style similarity or align cross-model representations, this decides which convergence metric to trust — and which is fragile.

Critical edge

The 40.2% global collapse may partly be a statistical-power artifact: MST overlap rests on 1,023 edges vs mKNN’s 10,240 memberships, and the paper never runs a power-matched ablation.

01

Methodology

2×2 controlled design
  1. S1

    De-confound the debate. Prior local-vs-global comparisons mix two axes: structural scale (local / global) and what is compared (relational identity vs metric distance). The 2×2 below separates them.

  2. S2

    Data. 1,024 paired image–text samples (WIT subset of the PRH benchmark); 12 LMs (BLOOM, OpenLlama, LLaMA) × 17 ViTs (MAE, DINOv2, CLIP) = 204 pairs; +143 video–text pairs for replication.

  3. S3

    Identity-only measures. Local: mutual k-NN overlap (k=10). Global (new): H₀ skeleton overlap — minimum-spanning-tree edge overlap, made exact by Prop. 1 (Vietoris–Rips H₀ death edges = MST edges), matched to mKNN in using relation identities only.

  4. S4

    One geometry knob. Shared relations get weight w = exp(−|Δ log D̃|/τ) with 90th-percentile normalization; τ swept 1→10⁻⁴; τ→∞ recovers the pure structure measures exactly.

  5. S5

    Calibration. Per-pair max over layer pairs; 500-permutation null; Benjamini–Hochberg FDR (α=0.05); robustness via a Riemannian (local-covariance) metric and a cosine analogue.

03

Strengths

  • +Genuinely controlled design — the 2×2 plus τ-interpolation separates “which relations are shared” from “how far apart they sit.”
  • +Apples-to-apples rebuttal — run on the critic’s own benchmark (WIT/PRH), models, and permutation calibration, not a strawman.
  • +Statistically careful — 500 permutations, layer-pair max, BH FDR across 204 pairs, family-wise capacity tests, cosine & normalization-quantile robustness checks.
  • +Exact, cheap global measure — Prop. 1 makes H₀ overlap deterministic; two independent generalizations (Riemannian, video–text); code released.
04

Limitations

  • −“Global” is modest — a spanning tree over 1,024 points; no H₁+ topology, no sample-size or k sensitivity.
  • −Fixed-support geometry test — distance agreement is only evaluated on relations both representations already share.
  • −One Riemannian approximation — a local-covariance construction, not a validated intrinsic metric.
  • −Multimodal only — no unimodal vision-only or text-only sweeps, where original PRH evidence was strongest.
  • −No downstream link — whether strict-τ geometric divergence matters for stitching / transfer is unaddressed.
05

Questions for Further Exploration

  1. Q1Does the global-significance collapse (40.2% vs 89.7%) survive a power-matched permutation null between mKNN and MST overlap?
  2. Q2How do higher homology groups (H₁ loops) or larger-radius diffusion neighbors behave under calibration and strict τ?
  3. Q3Is metric-geometry divergence behaviorally meaningful — does it predict model-stitching or cross-model-transfer failures?
  4. Q4Does structure-over-geometry hold for unimodal LM-only or vision-only families?
  5. Q5How sensitive is the τ-sweep to the 90th-percentile + log-gap parametrization vs rank-correlation-based agreement?
