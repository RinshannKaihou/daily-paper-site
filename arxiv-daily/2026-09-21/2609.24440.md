> **一句话总结：** 作者在 1000 万 token 的英文维基语料上分别为 Mamba-130m（自训练 SAE，12,288 特征）与 Pythia-70m（SAELens 预训练 SAE，32,768 特征）提取残差流特征，用二值化共激活向量的最大 Jaccard 相似度做跨架构"孪生特征"匹配，结果 99.98%（12,285/12,288）的 Mamba 特征都与某个 Pythia 特征强对齐（J > 0.35），仅 2 个落入 Grey Matter、1 个落入 Alien 区间——递归状态瓶颈并未破坏语义本体论，只在极端边缘诱导出结构性的"状态复位"地标神经元（如 Wikipedia External Links 页脚、URL 分隔符）和把时间戳与 URL 斜杠、K-12 连字符等语法异常揉在一起的"杂抽屉"式多义神经元。

> **TL;DR:** The authors train/obtain SAEs for Mamba-130m and Pythia-70m, binarize feature co-activations over a 10M-token English Wikipedia corpus, and map each Mamba feature to its max-Jaccard "twin" among Pythia features. Against the hypothesized SSM-specific "Dark Matter" (widespread representational divergence caused by the fixed-size recurrent state), 12,285 of 12,288 Mamba features (99.98%) find a Pythia twin at Jaccard J > 0.35 (global J range 0.234–0.412), with only 2 features in Grey Matter and 1 in the Alien/Structural tail. The rare divergent features are not semantic degradation: they are structural landmarks (e.g., a neuron firing exclusively on Wikipedia "External links" footers, strength 4.197) or one polysemantic "junk drawer" twin — Mamba Feature 4454 (J = 0.324) superimposes table timestamps with URL slashes (strength 6.39) and K-12 grade hyphens (4.58) where its Pythia twin Feature 4309 stays purely monosemantic on timestamps. Complementing Wang et al.'s MPPC-based comparison (arXiv:2410.00672) with a discrete, token-localizable Jaccard metric, the paper argues SSMs preserve the Transformer's semantic ontology and pay for sub-quadratic processing only at rigid syntactic margins.

## 核心贡献

- **对"Dark Matter"假设的否定性证据**：假设预测 SSM 的递归瓶颈会产生大量 Transformer 没有的专属潜在概念；实际映射中 12,285/12,288（99.98%）的 Mamba 特征落入强对齐区（J > 0.35），全库 Jaccard 全距仅 0.2343–0.4116，未发现系统性语义分歧。
- **发现"顺序地标特征"作为结构补偿**：在仅 0.02% 的分歧特征里，Mamba 出现专门编码刚性文档边界的单义神经元——Feature 6888（J = 0.3415，强度 4.197）只在 Wikipedia 文章末尾的 External links 标题处发放，Feature 7378（J < 0.25，Alien 类，强度 6.96）只在 URL 路径分隔符处发放；作者将其解释为 Mamba 在压缩隐状态中显式实现"状态复位"以补偿缺失的位置信息。
- **Twin Test 案例研究：瓶颈在边缘选择性诱导多义性**：Mamba Feature 4454（J = 0.324）与其 Pythia 孪生 Feature 4309 都在表格时间戳处发放，但 Mamba 版本额外叠加了 URL 斜杠（强度 6.39）、K-12 年级连字符（4.58）、外文大写片段如西班牙语 "¡Que v IVAN!"（4.18），而受益于全局注意力的 Pythia 特征保持纯粹单义——提示有限状态容量优先牺牲的是句法边缘的表征纯度，而非核心语义。
- **可复用的跨架构 SAE 共激活分析框架**：针对 12,288×32,768 特征对在 10^7 token 上计算 Jaccard（朴素实现超过 24 GB 显存），给出磁盘流式 + 分块 + 位阵列压缩 + float32 累加器的工程方案，并为 Mamba 中层激活训练了公开仓库中缺失的自定义 L1 稀疏 SAE。

## Key Contributions

- Empirical refutation of the "Dark Matter" hypothesis: over a 10M-token English Wikipedia corpus, 99.98% (12,285/12,288) of Mamba-130m SAE features have a Pythia-70m twin with Jaccard J > 0.35; the global Jaccard range across all 12,288 features is only 0.2343–0.4116, leaving just 2 features (0.02%) in Grey Matter and 1 (0.01%) in the Alien/Structural tail.
- Identification of "sequential landmark features" as structural compensation: within the tiny divergent fraction, Mamba dedicates monosemantic neurons to rigid document boundaries — Feature 6888 (J = 0.3415) fires exclusively on Wikipedia External LINKS headers (strength 4.197) and Alien Feature 7378 (J < 0.25) on URL path separators (strength 6.96) — interpreted as explicit "state resets" compensating for the absence of positional embeddings.
- A Twin Test case study showing the recurrent bottleneck selectively drives polysemanticity at representational margins: Mamba Feature 4454 (J = 0.324) conflates table timestamps with URL slashes (strength 6.39), K-12 grade hyphens (4.58), and capitalized foreign substrings (4.18), while its Pythia twin Feature 4309 is a pristine timestamp-only monosemantic trigger (strengths 4.87/4.83/4.79) — i.e., the cost of the fixed-size state shows up as localized syntactic "concept cramming", not wholesale semantic degradation.
- A scalable, reproducible co-activation framework: disk-streaming + 512-column slab chunking + bit-array masking + float32 accumulators makes the 12,288 × 32,768 pairwise Jaccard over 10^7 tokens feasible on standard GPUs, plus a custom L1-sparse SAE for Mamba mid-layer activations (no public equivalent exists).

## Methodology

The study isolates the effect of attention's global context routing versus Mamba's fixed-size recurrent state by comparing two parameter-comparable models: Mamba-130m and Pythia-70m-deduped, both processed with the GPT-NeoX tokenizer over 10 million tokens of English Wikipedia, chunked into non-overlapping 1,024-token sequences. Residual-stream activations are harvested at the geometric midpoint of depth — `blocks.12.hook_resid_post` for Mamba (24 layers) and `blocks.3.hook_resid_post` for Pythia (6 layers) — a choice meant to capture mid-level semantic/syntactic features while avoiding early-layer tokenization regimes and late-layer prediction-bound regimes.

For Pythia, the authors use a publicly available pre-trained SAELens SAE (`pythia-70m-deduped-res-smi`) with expansion factor 64 over the 512-dim residual stream, yielding 32,768 features. For Mamba, since no robust public SAE exists for mid-layer activations, they train a custom SAE with an L1 sparsity penalty and expansion factor 16 over the 768-dim residual stream, yielding 12,288 features; the authors explicitly flag the asymmetric expansion factors as a confound.

Continuous SAE activations are binarized: any feature with magnitude strictly greater than zero is set True, producing two sparse binary masks per token over the full 10M-token corpus. The pairwise Jaccard similarity J(m,p) = |a_m ∩ a_p| / |a_m ∪ a_p| is computed for all Mamba×Pythia feature pairs; naively materializing M^T P exceeds 24 GB VRAM, so they stream tokens from disk into CPU RAM, process the Pythia feature dimension in contiguous column slabs of width 512, cast masks to bit arrays before device transfer, and keep float32 intersection accumulators. For each of the 12,288 Mamba features, the globally maximizing Jaccard pair designates its cross-architectural "Twin". Alignment categories use empirically derived thresholds anchored on the observed distribution (global min 0.234, max 0.4116): Aligned (J > 0.35), Grey Matter (0.28 ≤ J < 0.35), and Alien/Structural (J < 0.25), with a buffer zone [0.25, 0.28); the authors stress these are descriptive partitions of observed data, not theoretically motivated cutoffs. Finally, a qualitative "Twin Test" compares the top activating contexts of the single most divergent twin pair (Mamba 4454 vs Pythia 4309).

## Results & Highlights

- Universality headline: on the 10M-token Wikipedia corpus, 12,285 of 12,288 Mamba features (99.98%) fall in the Aligned category (J > 0.35) with a Pythia twin; only 2 features (0.02%) fall in Grey Matter (0.28 ≤ J < 0.35) and 1 (0.01%) in Alien/Structural (J < 0.25). Against the "Dark Matter" hypothesis, which predicted widespread SSM-exclusive latent concepts, the observed distribution shows the recurrent bottleneck produces no evidence of systemic semantic divergence — consistent with, but metric-distinct from, Wang et al.'s (arXiv:2410.00672) MPPC-based finding of broad Mamba–Pythia feature alignment.
- Absolute alignment is moderate at best: the global minimum Jaccard across all 12,288 features is 0.2343 and the global maximum only 0.4116 — even the best-matched twin pair shares at most ~41% of its binarized token-level activation support, a fact the "near-identical latent geometries" framing in the conclusion glosses over.
- Twin Test (Table 1): Mamba Feature 4454 (J = 0.324, Grey Matter) fires on the same Wikipedia table timestamps as its Pythia twin Feature 4309, but additionally on URL slashes (strength 6.39), K-12 grade hyphens (strength 4.58, e.g., "Black Hawk County... grades K–12 on one campus"), and capitalized foreign substrings (strength 4.18, e.g., Spanish "¡Que v IVAN!"); Pythia 4309's top-3 contexts are all table-timestamp templates (strengths 4.87, 4.83, 4.79) — a clean monosemantic vs "junk drawer" contrast on an n=1 feature pair.
- Structural landmarks: Mamba Feature 6888 (J = 0.3415, strength 4.197) fires exclusively on the "External LINKS" headers at the end of Wikipedia articles, and Alien Feature 7378 (J < 0.25, strength 6.96) triggers solely on URL path separators — both framed as "state reset" neurons that give the recurrence explicit sequential anchors it cannot obtain from its compressed hidden state.

## Authors & Credibility

All four authors are from the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, PES University, Bangalore, India; Ashwini M Joshi appears to be the faculty author, with the other three (gmail-contact) likely students. This is an IEEE-workshop-style paper with no prior well-known research track record visible from the references or affiliations. The work is creditable in its honesty: the limitations section openly names the asymmetric SAE dictionary capacity confound and downgrades the polysemanticity finding to "hypothesis-generating rather than a confirmed mechanistic result". The engineering is competent (a real disk-streaming solution to a genuine memory blow-up), and the citation apparatus (SAELens, MambaLens, Anthropic SAE line, Platonic Representation Hypothesis, Wang et al.'s prior cross-architecture comparison) shows the authors know the relevant literature. It is, however, a small-scale student study on two sub-100M-parameter models, not a lab-grade interpretability release, and no code or trained-checkpoint link is provided in the paper despite the "fully reproducible" claim.

## Critical Assessment

- The headline number is threshold-dependent and lacks a null baseline. The 99.98% alignment figure depends entirely on drawing the Aligned cutoff at J > 0.35 inside an observed distribution spanning just 0.234–0.412; the authors admit the thresholds are "empirically derived from the observed similarity range rather than theoretically motivated" and that a random-feature-pair null distribution is future work. Without that null, we cannot say whether J = 0.35 constitutes strong alignment or merely beats chance for two sparse binary masks over 10M tokens.
- The "strong alignment" language overstates absolute overlap. A max Jaccard of 0.4116 means even the best twin disagrees on ~59% of its token-level activation support; describing this as models converging on "near identical" representational primitives (Conclusion) is generous. Binarizing at "any activation > 0" further inflates overlap sensitivity to SAE firing-rate differences induced by different L1 penalties and expansion factors.
- The asymmetric SAE setup is a real confound, and the authors' defense is qualitative. Mamba's SAE is custom-trained (expansion 16, 12,288 features) versus Pythia's public expansion-64 SAE (32,768 features); dictionary starvation alone could produce the polysemantic "junk drawer" Feature 4454. The rebuttal — that Mamba's polysemanticity conflates *sequential boundary/formatting tokens* rather than arbitrary co-occurrences — is plausible but rests on a single feature pair (n=1 Twin Test), and the paper itself concedes symmetric-SAE training is "a necessary step for establishing this finding definitively".
- The most striking "divergent" features are corpus artifacts by construction. Features firing on Wikipedia "External links" footers and URL separators reflect English Wikipedia's rigid formatting; on web-scale pretraining data with different document structure, the landmark-feature story might look completely different, limiting the generality of the "state reset" interpretation.
- Everything is correlational. Despite invoking circuit analysis and feature steering as evidence that interpretability transfers, the paper performs no causal intervention (no ablation of Features 6888/7378/4454, no activation patching) to verify the claimed "state reset" or "concept cramming" functions — the mechanisms are narrative readings of top-activating contexts.
- Novelty is incremental and the "first" framing is in tension with its own related work. The Conclusion claims "the first large-scale, feature-level correspondence analysis between a Selective State Space Model and an autoregressive Transformer", yet Section II acknowledges Wang et al. (arXiv:2410.00672) already compared Mamba-130m and Pythia via SAEs with MPPC; the delta here is the discrete Jaccard metric, spatial token-localizability, and a larger (10M vs smaller) corpus. A minor typesetting slip (the Pythia mask is written as {0,1}^{16×10^7} instead of 32768×10^7) also suggests light proofreading.

## Strengths

- Asks a clean, well-posed question — does the recurrent informational bottleneck change *what* is represented, not just *how* — and operationalizes it with a feature-level metric that, unlike RSA/CKA or MPPC, localizes divergence to specific tokens (document footers, URL separators, table timestamps).
- Unusually honest limitation handling: the confound is named, its rebuttal labeled qualitative, and the polysemanticity claim explicitly demoted to hypothesis-generating — a model of calibrated claim strength.
- Real systems contribution: the disk-streaming/bit-array Jaccard scheme makes a 12,288 × 32,768 × 10^7-token intersection computable on commodity hardware, and the custom Mamba SAE fills a genuine tooling gap (MambaLens notwithstanding).
- Findings are specific and falsifiable: named features with numeric strengths, precise firing contexts, and concrete follow-ups (symmetric SAEs, null distributions) stated by the authors themselves.
- The "structural compensation" framing (landmark/state-reset neurons) is a genuinely interesting, architecture-linked phenomenon worth follow-up, and the Primacy-of-the-Data-Manifold discussion connects the result to the Platonic Representation Hypothesis.

## Limitations

- Scale: only Mamba-130m and Pythia-70m at a single mid-layer each; nothing establishes that 99.98% alignment survives at Mamba-790m/Pythia-410m scale, across depth, or on non-W corpora.
- Asymmetric SAE dictionaries (12,288 vs 32,768 features; expansion 16 vs 64; different training runs) confound both the alignment rate (bigger search space inflates apparent twins) and the polysemanticity claim (smaller dictionary can force feature overloading).
- No null distribution or permutation baseline for the Jaccard thresholds; the Aligned/Grey Matter/Alien boundaries are post-hoc partitions of one observed distribution.
- n=1 evidence for the central Twin Test mechanism; no causal ablation or steering experiments; single corpus (English Wikipedia) whose formatting quirks directly generate the "landmark" findings.
- No released code or SAE checkpoint link in the paper, which undercuts the "fully reproducible framework" claim in practice.

## Relevance

这项工作把稀疏自编码器跨架构对比从 Transformer 推广到 Mamba，用可定位到具体 token 的 Jaccard 共激活分析给出了"递归瓶颈不损害语义本体论"的否定性证据，对关注机制可解释性与 SSM/Transformer 表征差异的读者很有参考价值；其诚实的阈值与混淆因素讨论也是好范例，但小模型、单层、单语料的设置意味着结论只能当作假设生成而非定论。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would training symmetric SAEs (same expansion factor and dictionary size) on both models eliminate — or confirm — the Feature 4454 vs 4309 monosemanticity asymmetry?
- What does a random-feature-pair null distribution of Jaccard look like on this corpus and sparsity regime, and would the Aligned threshold of J > 0.35 survive it?
- Does the 99.98% alignment rate hold at larger scale (Mamba-790m vs Pythia-410m), at other depths, or on corpora without Wikipedia's rigid formatting (code, web text, dialogue)?
- Can causal interventions (ablation or amplification of Features 6888/7378) demonstrate an actual "state reset" effect on Mamba's downstream predictions at document boundaries?
- Do the landmark/junk-drawer phenomena appear when comparing Mamba-2 or other hybrid architectures, or when the tokenizer differs between the two models?
