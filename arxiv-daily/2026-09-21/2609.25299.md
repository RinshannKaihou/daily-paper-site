> **一句话总结：** 针对 LLM Agent 重复任务中"同一问题三次运行 38%–74% 答案不一致、95.3%–97.2% 生成 token 花在重新推导已知计划"的问题，提出"技能习惯化"架构：从 Agent 自身执行轨迹中挖掘确定性技能变体（coverage 谓词 + 守卫），经 C0–C3 四道准入门控（可执行性、guardrail、与参考变体跑批方差的非劣性置换检验、人工授权的线上 A/B）挂入技能世系（lineage）并按覆盖门控分发；在自建 text-to-SQL 负载上，习惯化变体在全部 456 次重复分发中逐位复现（四个推理臂仅 11–26/42），非劣于所有被替换臂（p < 0.0001，pass₃ = 1 vs 0.76–0.86），每请求 token 降 13.9%–55.5%（池化 38.1%），7–53 次复用后净收益转正；代价是守卫在 2.6% 自然改写和 26% 边界输入上"认领了本应转交的工作"且中央门控对其中 11/13 不可见。

> **TL;DR:** On a self-authored text-to-SQL workload (14 templates × 3 instances × 3 repeats, 504 calls) over four reasoning arms (Claude Sonnet 5, Kimi K2 Thinking, GLM-4.6, Qwen3-235B-Thinking), the proposed habit-formation pipeline mines deterministic script variants from the agent's own traces and admits them through four gates (executability probe, guardrail conformance, a non-inferiority permutation test against the reference's run-to-run variability, and human-authorized A/B). The habit-formed variant reproduced bit-exactly on all 456 repeated dispatches where the replaced reasoning arms managed only 11–26 of 42 byte-identical triplicates, was non-inferior to every arm (p < 0.0001; pass₃ = 1 vs arms' 0.76–0.86), and cut per-request tokens 13.9%–55.5% (1032/1348/1365/1998 tok/req down to 888), turning net-positive after 7–53 reuses. On a second, third-party retrieval-routing workload (HotpotQA-seeded, frozen 288-question pool), mining raised routing accuracy +0.157 [+0.116, +0.198] from cold start over nine runs. The honest cost: the guard wrongly claims work it should defer on 2.6% of natural paraphrases and 26% of boundary inputs, and the paper's own C2 gate is structurally blind to 11 of 13 such failures.

## 核心贡献

- **问题量化**：42 个任务各跑 3 遍，按模型不同 38%–74% 的重复任务答案不一致；推理 token 占模型生成的 95.3%–97.2%（GLM 95.3% / Qwen 96.2% / Kimi 97.2%），占计费 token 的 62.7%–75.0%——绝大多数算力花在重新推导系统已知的计划上。
- **"技能习惯化"架构**：技能是一个变体世系（lineage）而非单一可变工件；锚点（anchor）作为永不剪枝的兜底，每个变体携带覆盖谓词与确定性、二值的守卫（guard），分发自低环向上走、守卫放行即服务；候选变体从自身执行轨迹挖掘（重放、反事实/扰动重放、有界仿真）。
- **四道准入门控 C0–C3**：可执行性探针、guardrail 静态子集检查、行为-轨迹一致性（对参考变体自身跑批方差做单侧非劣性置换检验、Benjamini–Hochberg 校正）、人工审核终止的线上 A/B。准入成本 O(capabilities) 而候选量可扩展——人审"能力类别"而非逐个候选。
- **经济学与失效测量**：作者化成本实测 7,691 token/模板（十模板包 76,910），断点在 7/16/17/53 次复用（对 Kimi/Qwen/GLM/Sonnet）；把守卫的"错误认领"（false admission）作为一等公民度量——自然改写上 2.6%、边界输入上 26%，且证明 C2 门控因投影只记录参数模式不记录参数值而对 11/13 此类失效结构性失明。
- **两个可迁移发现**：把"选哪个过程"与"填什么参数"拆成两次调用，端到端准确率 0.888 → 0.952（成本仅 43%）；风险按目录累积而证据按候选收集——通过全部门控的模板在旁边准入一个相邻模板后就不再安全（相邻性 + 目录 > 20 同时出现时误准率 15.8%，单独任一为 0）。
- **负面结果同样是贡献**：嵌入式记忆守卫被非劣性门控"锁死"（剂量-响应曲线全平、记忆始终为空）；诊断出挖掘到的纠错是当前策略的单类错误，纯记忆只买到覆盖、买不到泛化（未见意图差异 −0.023 → +0.023）。

## Key Contributions

- Quantifies the consistency gap: asking the same question three times, 38%–74% of repeated tasks return non-agreeing answers depending on the arm (Sonnet byte-identical on 26/42, GLM 13/42, Qwen 11/42, Kimi 11/42), and 95.3%–97.2% of generated tokens re-derive plans the system already holds.
- Proposes **agentic skill habit formation**: a lineage model (anchor + coverage-gated variants with deterministic, binary guards), candidate mining from the agent's own execution history, and four admission gates (C0 reusability, C1 governance, C2 trace-conformance non-inferiority via permutation test against the reference's own run-to-run variability, C3 human-terminated production A/B).
- Demonstrates on text-to-SQL that a habit-formed variant reproduces exactly on 456/456 repeated dispatches, is non-inferior to all four replaced arms, cuts tokens 13.9%–55.5% per request (38.1% pooled), and breaks even after 7–53 reuses against a measured one-time authoring cost of 7,691 tokens per template.
- Measures the architecture's failure mode honestly: guard false-admissions of 2.6% [0.5%, 13.2%] on natural paraphrases and 26.0% [15.9%, 39.6%] on boundary stress items, with a structural proof that the central C2 gate cannot see 11 of 13 of them (its projection records argument schemas, not values).
- Reports a transferable negative result: a non-inferiority gate can deadlock the learner it filters, and autonomously mined corrections are single-class — restoring class balance by mining non-errors recovers +0.131 but buys coverage, not generalization.
- Ships a falsifiable measurement plan for the four claims still unmeasured (achievable coverage, trace opacity, forage yield, realized α_K), rather than overclaiming.

## Methodology

**Lineage model.** A skill is a declared capability contract implemented by an ordered set of variants. The **anchor** is structurally privileged: the slowest retained ancestor, terminal fallback, never pruned, and the reference against which all conformance is measured. Each variant carries a **coverage predicate** and a **coverage guard**; dispatch walks the lineage from the lowest ring upward and the first variant whose guard admits the input serves it. Partial coverage is a feature: it bounds the blast radius of a wrong habit to the slice it claims.

**Candidate generation.** A forge step mines the trace spine for stable structure via three sources in descending trustworthiness: replay of historical traces (per-decision conditional entropy freezes near-deterministic junctions), counterfactual and perturbed replay (cheapest probe of coverage-boundary sensitivity), and bounded simulation. From corrections it also mines **guardrails** — deterministic, binary assertions that enter the tenant set only by human authorization.

**Admission gates C0–C3.** C0: an executable probe must prove the candidate can serve traffic on withdrawal (repair task, or re-anchoring). C1: guardrails are a static subset check against the parent's declarative guardrails. C2: behavior-trace conformance — a one-sided non-inferiority test (5-point margin, α = 0.05, Benjamini–Hochberg corrected across arms) of the candidate's soft run-to-run divergence D_cand against the reference's own D_self, so the reference under test is "no weaker than itself." C3: production A/B under shadow traffic, terminated by human review of a legible delta.

**Economics.** Promotion pays when N·c_R > c_A + N·c_G + n·c_F. The paper measures c_A (76,910 tokens for a ten-template bundle; $0.010–0.012 per shape solo, ~$0.006–0.007 bundled) and c_G ($0.0018–0.0043/call), giving a break-even reuse count per arm and a guard-cost law c_guard(N) = $0.000263 + $0.0000564N fitted over a 5→30 template bundle sweep (R² = 0.999).

**Evaluation.** Two workloads. (1) Self-authored text-to-SQL: 14 templates × 3 instances × 3 repeats (504 calls) across four reasoning arms; replay exactness, token accounting, guard stress sets (40 held-out paraphrases, 80 boundary items), semantic vs regex guard comparison, catalog-size sweep 5→30. (2) Retrieval routing on a third-party agent platform: questions seeded from HotpotQA, frozen 288-question test pool split into seen/unseen intents, three routers (GLM-4.6, DeepSeek-v3.2, Kimi-K2) with dose-response grids to 100/200 mined traces, pre-specified contrasts, and non-habit-forming floors (minimal static classifier, teacher-hardened classifier).

## Results & Highlights

- **Replay is exact where reasoning is not.** The habit-formed variant reproduced on all 456 repeated dispatches (five repeats, 2,280 calls, zero disagreement); the four reasoning arms returned byte-identical triplicates on only 11–26 of 42 questions (Sonnet 26, GLM 13, Qwen 11, Kimi 11), with mean pairwise edit distance 0.05–0.23 across repeats.
- **Non-inferiority at zero marginal cost.** The candidate is non-inferior to all four arms (p < 0.0001 throughout) with pass₃ = 1 against the arms' measured 0.76–0.86; Sonnet was the best arm (accuracy 0.865, pass₃ 0.857) precisely because it planned least (thinking block on only 4 of 126 calls) — "the arm that planned least scored best."
- **Token cost.** A served request costs the reasoning arms 1032 (Sonnet) / 1348 (GLM) / 1365 (Qwen) / 1998 (Kimi) tokens vs 888 for habit-formed dispatch (856 in + 32 out): a 13.9%–55.5% reduction depending on arm, 38.1% pooled. Net of the one-time 7,691-token-per-template authoring charge, curves cross zero at 7 (Kimi), 16 (Qwen), 17 (GLM), and 53 (Sonnet) reuses; at 100 reuses the net saving is 51.7%, 29.3%, 28.4%, and 6.5% respectively.
- **The guard's price, measured.** False admission of work it should defer: 2.6% [0.5%, 13.2%] on 40 natural paraphrases and 26.0% [15.9%, 39.6%] among admitted boundary stress items (50 of 80 admitted; 90.6% of should-decline items correctly declined, including all 13 out-of-scope). 9 of 13 false admissions are correct-template/wrong-parameter, and C2 is blind to 11 of 13 because its projection records argument schemas, not values — the permutation test is "comparing a distribution against itself." One silent failure: correct template, wrong column extraction, confidently returned 0 where the gold answer was 19.
- **Retrieval routing generalizes.** Cold-start-to-t=200 routing-accuracy gain +0.157 [+0.116, +0.198] pooled over nine runs; +0.145 [+0.113, +0.178] overall and +0.132 [+0.082, +0.182] on intents never seen during mining at the pre-specified 200-trace endpoint (two routers, six runs); the pre-specified t=100→t=200 contrast is −0.003 [−0.028, +0.022] (no reliable non-monotonicity). Any effect below the 0.118 same-configuration replication spread is not claimed.
- **Guard error decomposition.** The routing guard's total error falls 0.382 → 0.237 (−0.145, down in all six runs); the entire improvement is the cheap error (retrieving when direct answering would serve, −0.216), while the severe error (answering directly when retrieval was required) moves 0.000 → 0.071 — presented but explicitly not claimed as an effect since +0.071 sits below the 0.118 floor. The guard has no abstain path: 0 of 30,240 scored decisions were ever deferred.
- **Split the call.** Separating routing from parameter extraction raised end-to-end accuracy from 0.888 (joint call) to 0.952 (route-only call at 43% of the cost), with a deterministic schema-grounded extractor reaching 0.925 at zero marginal cost; it recovers 19 wrong answers and converts 16 into visible declines at the price of 2 the joint call had right. The largest residual fault is arithmetic: a mined template emits order_date < {end_date} and the guard fills the month's last day instead of the next month's first, getting 0 of 51 exclusive-window slots right (extractor: 51/51).
- **Scale safety of the catalog.** Across 2,618 dispatches there was not one wrong-template admission, and 0 false admissions in 1,870 covered in-scope dispatches (Wilson upper bound 0.21%); all 29 false admissions were out-of-scope questions that required both an adjacent template present and a catalog above 20 templates (adjacency alone: 0 in 66 attempts; size alone: 0 in 154; both: 15.8%).

## Authors & Credibility

Travis Weber and Rohit Taneja are at Pheo Inc., a company building agent infrastructure; the paper reads as an engineering team's systematized account of a production-shaped design rather than an academic lab's benchmark paper. Credibility signals are unusually strong for an industry preprint: pre-specified endpoint contrasts, confidence intervals over runs (nine runs per interval rather than one), Benjamini–Hochberg correction, explicit refusal to claim sub-floor effects (+0.071 under the 0.118 replication spread), a Wilson upper bound on zero-count events, and a falsifiable measurement plan (§XI) for what remains unmeasured. The related-work grounding is solid — the utility problem (Minton 1990), tracing-JIT guards and deoptimization, non-inferiority testing, and Cadron et al.'s independent evidence of run-to-run variability. Main credibility caveats: not peer-reviewed, both workloads are constructed or labeled by the authors (the SQL workload is self-authored; the routing task uses HotpotQA questions but the authors' own retrieve-versus-answer labels), and no comparison against external agent-memory or skill-library systems (Voyager-style) — the "baselines" are the paper's own four reasoning arms and two deliberately minimal floors.

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty.** The individual ingredients are old — skill libraries (Voyager), workflow-memory induction, caching with guards (tracing JITs), the utility problem (Minton) — and the paper says so. The contribution is the composition: coverage-gated dispatch over a variant lineage with admission gates that treat run-to-run variance as the null, plus the insistence that false-admission rate is a first-class measurable quantity. That framing is genuinely useful and, to my knowledge, not published in this form.

**Rigor — strong by preprint standards, with real gaps.** Pre-specified contrasts, multi-seed grids, bootstrap/Wilson intervals, and refusing to claim below-floor effects are all exemplary. But the headline pilot is a workload the authors authored themselves, with templates "chosen for structural regularity: fixed sentence skeletons with substitutable literals" — close to ideal conditions for regex guards, and the authors concede the regex guard scores 0/40 on held-out paraphrases. The strongest reproducibility claim (456/456 bit-exact) is partly a property of the substrate: 14 deterministic templates with fixed instances leave little to vary, so "reproduces on everything it serves" is near-tautological once the script is correct. The pass₃ = 1 vs 0.76–0.86 comparison is fair but the workload is one where planning "bought nothing" by the authors' own account — the setting most favorable to caching.

**Red flags, mostly self-declared.** (1) The coverage guard is the one component exempt from its own C0–C3 gates and runs unsupervised after training — a quiet-failure mode the authors name but do not fix, and the 2.6% natural-traffic false-admission rate with silent, exactly-reproducible wrong answers (the 0-vs-19 case) is the concrete face of that risk. (2) The routing guard cannot abstain at all — coverage 1.0 by construction, 0 of 30,240 decisions deferred — so "narrow claims tile into wide ones" is an acknowledged design hole. (3) The C2 gate is structurally blind to wrong-parameter serves (schemas, not values), which is 9 of 13 observed false admissions; the authors prove the blindness instead of repairing it. (4) The discovery gate measures fit, not generalization (success falls from 1,000 at one trace to 0.900 at fifty, and the obvious fitting/held-out split "we have not run it"). (5) No production deployment: C3 and the recovery loop are specified, not evaluated; four framework quantities are explicitly unmeasured. (6) Token-denominated savings ignore guard input growth with catalog size ($0.000548→$0.001969 per dispatch from 5→30 templates), which the paper models but a reader skimming the headline 38.1% might miss.

**Significance.** The transferable findings — split routing from value extraction (0.888→0.952 at 43% cost), risk accruing per catalog while evidence accrues per candidate, and non-inferiority gates deadlocking cold learners fed single-class mined corrections — are worth more than the headline system and apply to any procedure cache. This is a good paper that repeatedly refuses to oversell itself; its honesty is verifiable in the numbers, not just the tone.

## Strengths

- The consistency problem is quantified before it is sold: 38–74% self-disagreement and 95.3–97.2% planning-token waste are measured on the paper's own workload with per-arm detail.
- Bit-exact replay (456/456) is an effect any buyer or auditor can verify without understanding the task — no interval or noise floor needed.
- Guard failures are measured with the same care as the wins: false-admission rates with CIs, a boundary stress set, a structural proof of C2's blindness, and a catalog-size sweep isolating the true hazard condition (adjacency + catalog > 20).
- Pre-specified endpoints, multi-run intervals, BH correction, and explicit refusal to claim the +0.071 severe-error shift — statistical hygiene rare in industry agent papers.
- The deadlocked embedding-memory guard is diagnosed to a mechanism (mined corrections are the policy's own single-class errors) and the repair's residual weakness is stated (pure memory buys coverage, not generalization: unseen-intent split −0.023 → +0.023).
- Economics are fully denominated in tokens and dollars, including authoring cost, guard-cost law (R² = 0.999), and per-arm break-even reuses.

## Limitations

- Both evaluations are small and author-adjacent: 42 questions / 504 calls on a self-authored SQL workload, and a 288-question routing pool with the authors' own labels; no public end-to-end agent benchmark (e.g., SWE-bench-style) and no comparison against external skill-library or memory systems.
- The coverage guard is ungated and unsupervised after training; boundary-weighted shadow sampling, a held-out conformance test for the guard, and margin-gated escalation are proposed but "none is evaluated."
- One of the two guards cannot decline (routing guard), so silent wrong serves are structurally guaranteed somewhere in the system; the 26% boundary false-admission rate is on adversarially-shaped-but-benign inputs, and long-run production behavior is untested.
- C2 compares argument schemas, not values, so wrong-parameter serves — the dominant observed failure — are invisible to the central gate by construction.
- The discovery gate conflates fit with generalization (the fitting/held-out split is named as "the obvious repair" and not run); the habit is auditable exactly when it is wrong in exactly the same way.
- Break-even claims depend on provider pricing (the measured c_G assumed an introductory rate had expired), and savings shrink toward zero against the least-planning arm (6.5% net at 100 reuses vs Sonnet) — the benefit is conditional on replacing a wasteful planner.

## Relevance

与你最高优先级的"自进化 Agent 与自动化研究"方向直接契合：这篇论文给出了一条可审计的自改进闭环——Agent 从自身执行轨迹挖掘技能候选、经统计门控自主晋升、并以确定性习惯换取一致性和 38% 的 token 节省，同时把"坏习惯"的静默失效作为一等失效模式来测量。对关注长时自治系统的可靠性与治理（准入门控、覆盖撤回、人工授权点）的读者，这是一份少见的诚实系统报告。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Can the coverage guard itself be brought under C0–C3 — e.g., a held-out conformance test for guards — without an infinite regress of guard-guarding-guard?
- Does the 2.6% natural-traffic false-admission rate hold on non-templated workloads, and would a semantic-classifier guard (Haiku-based, $0.00102/dispatch) keep its 39/40 recovery when templates multiply past the 30-template bundle tested?
- The authors left untested whether habit formation degrades at larger doses (the t=100→t=200 contrast bounds the effect near zero at 200 traces); does the curve hold at 10,000 traces on production traffic?
- The routing guard cannot abstain; what is the measured cost of adding a refusal path — coverage, latency, and the legible-delta pipeline for C3 human review?
- The transferable claim "split routing from extraction, 0.888→0.952 at 43% cost" invites testing on agentic tool-use traces beyond SQL, where the "extraction" half is API-argument filling rather than slot lookup.
- Risk accrues per catalog while evidence is per candidate: what re-admission trigger on catalog change (e.g., re-running C2 for adjacent siblings) catches the 15.8% joint-condition hazard at acceptable O(capabilities) human cost?
