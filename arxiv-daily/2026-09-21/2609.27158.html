— Critical Assessment

How it is argued

01Methodology

Purely analytical — no datasets, no trained models, no measurements. Five moves:

1 Survey the formulations.

Displacement, probing and superposition each place “features” in a different object — V, V* or latent codes — with different transformation laws under h′ = Ah + t.

2 Formalize the claim.

A representation claim becomes (G, M, F, P): an equivariant procedure F into a G-space M, judged by a G-invariant predicate P.

3 Set the architectural floor.

G must contain the function-preserving reparameterizations the architecture already realizes — worked out for attention’s Q/K gauge (WQ → ΛWQ, WK → Λ−⊤WK) and RoPE.

4 Compute composite symmetry.

A pipeline is admissible only under its most restrictive stage; Table 1 then assigns each common quantity its maximal preserving group.

5 Audit and extend.

Nine published analyses re-read under declared symmetries; the same omission is located in the Platonic Representation Hypothesis.

Checkable by inspection: Prop 3.2 and Cor 4.2 are elementary linear algebra.

What it establishes

02Results — a symmetry audit, not a benchmark

Prop 3.2

No nonzero GL(V)-equivariant map V* → V exists — a metric identifies probes with displacements only under Giso.

Cor 4.2

At the Q/K site the only gauge-invariant bilinear form is b ≡ 0 — per-head cosines and norms are not invariants of the computation.

Prop 4.3

Gaff estimate + Gsim cosine + Giso calibration ⇒ the composite claim is guaranteed only under Giso.

Holds up

03Strengths

  • Separates object space M from procedure F — catching the storage-format confusion between probe weights (V*) and steering vectors (V).
  • Cor 4.2 is a one-line proof with outsized reach: it explains why per-head attention geometry (cosines, norms, log det) is gauge-fragile — with no experiment.
  • The composition rule mirrors real practice (estimate → compare → calibrate): a claim is only as strong as its most restrictive stage.
  • Intellectually fair: no audited method is called invalid, and partial solutions (Wollschläger, Li, Yamawaki) are credited.

Watch out

04Limitations

  • No experiment shows any published conclusion is materially wrong — only that several are stated under stronger symmetry than they establish.
  • The taxonomy stops at the affine family: head permutations, normalization gauges and nonlinear equivalences sit outside the audited hierarchy.
  • The reporting principle imposes a real burden, with no checklist or tooling offered to lower adoption cost.
  • Case studies are single-auditor readings; the original authors could reasonably dispute some characterizations.
  • Full Q/K and RoPE derivations live in Appendix C — main-text readers get conclusions without derivations.
