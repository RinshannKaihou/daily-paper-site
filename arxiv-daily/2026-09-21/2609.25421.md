> **一句话总结：** 提出 LARA——一种面向自主科研 agent 的机器可检查"声明-支持"语言与协议：作者把论文声明写成可执行程序（声明+证据叶+严格/可废止推理+三类类型化攻击），确定性检查器将其编译为 Dung 论证框架并按 grounded 语义给出 justified/defeated/contested/gap 四态裁决，配合跨语境"桥"实现可审计的跨论文支持传输；元理论用 Lean 4 机械化（144 个文件、约 117,000 行、公开部分无 sorry、仅用 Lean 三个标准公理），Haskell 生产检查器与编译后的 Lean 参考语义在 673/673 个含裁决输入和 66/66 个畸形输入上达成字节级一致。

> **TL;DR:** LARA turns research claims into executable, checkable certificates: a deterministic checker compiles declared claims, evidence leaves, strict/defeasible inference steps, and typed attacks (rebut/undercut/undermine) into a Dung argumentation framework and reports a four-state verdict (justified / defeated / contested / gap) per claim, plus diamond/box modal queries over "bridges" between papers. The metatheory (decidability, status preservation, backend replacement, conditional cross-context status preservation) is mechanized in Lean 4 — 144 files, ~117k lines, sorry-free public part — and the Haskell checker matches the compiled Lean reference interpreter on 673/673 verdict-bearing and 66/66 malformed inputs with byte-identical reports. Ablations on a 655-input manifest show each removed check admits a distinct defect class (18 / 5 / 32 newly-admitted malformed programs), and an exploratory 60-claim corpus sample from 32 artifacts yields 48 gap / 9 justified / 3 defeated claims, exposing that ~4 in 5 encoded claims lack complete support.

## 核心贡献

- **类型化"声明-支持"演算（LARA 语言与协议）**：声明 = 自然语言散文 + 形式目标原子 + （不受信任的）人工签名绑定；证据叶带 kind/provenance；推理分两层——严格演绎步骤委托给注册后端（nd@1 自然演绎、ra@1 有理算术、ord@1 序比较、insp@1 清单检查）出证明证书，可废止步骤实例化论证图式并必须回答强制性"批判性问题"（如 randomized? adequate_power? external_validity?），否则程序直接被拒。
- **三类位置化类型攻击与四态裁决**：rebut 打结论、undercut 打推理规则、undermine 打证据叶；严格步骤不可被 rebut/undercut。检查器把接受的程序编译为 Dung 论证框架，用 grounded 语义（唯一扩张、多项式可算、怀疑派）聚合出第四态 **gap**（完全没有完整支持论证）——把"支持缺失"与"支持被击败"区分开，这是三态系统做不到的。
- **元理论与状态保持**：证明可判定性（G1）、依赖可追责（G2）、编译可靠性 + 直接源语义与编译框架语义的状态保持（G3）、严格证书隔离与非事实性（G4，证书只证明后端逻辑内的编码结论，绝不消除为源命题真值）、后端可替换（G5）、严格链良构下的一致性（G6）。
- **跨语境可能世界语义**：每个程序是一个"世界"，声明式结构"桥"传输检查过的支持；可执行检查器可判定 diamond/box 模态查询（某声明在某个/所有对比记录中是否 justified）；机械化反例证明传输不等于状态保持，并给出攻击双模拟下的条件状态保持定理（Thm 6.21）。
- **更新演算与复杂度刻画**：add-leaf/tighten/add-attack/add-instance 四种编辑的可达状态转移矩阵（64 格中恰 37 格可达）；grounded 评估的查询次数紧界 n²..n²(1+n)；证明 LARA 可实现框架上的 credulous complete acceptance 是 NP-complete（3SAT 归约，归约本身机械化），而 grounded 核心检查是多项式的。
- **大规模机械化 + 实现验证**：Lean 4 机械化 35 条规范结果中的 34 条（公开元理论无 sorry）；Haskell 生产检查器通过差分测试（673+66 输入字节级一致）、541 个突变程序的变异测试、655 输入消融实验、60 语料单元计时（中位 629.6 µs）验证；语料库探索性研究量化了 60 个采样声明中的支持缺口。

## Key Contributions

- **A typed claim-support calculus**: a checked language where authors declare claims (prose + formal target atom + human-signed binding), evidence leaves with provenance, strict steps backed by replayable certificates from four registered backends, and defeasible steps that must discharge mandatory critical questions or face checker rejection.
- **Four-state semantics with a genuine "gap" status**: compilation to Dung argumentation frameworks under grounded semantics yields justified / defeated / contested / gap, where gap records absent support *before* labelling begins — a distinction three-state or scored accounts collapse.
- **A metatheory with mechanized guarantees**: decidability, dependency accountability, compilation soundness and status preservation (direct source semantics ≡ compiled-framework semantics), strict-certificate isolation (non-factivity: a certified numeric comparison survives defeat of the empirical claim it supported), backend replacement, quarantine non-promotion, and an update calculus with fully mapped reachable transition matrices.
- **Cross-context possible-world semantics**: declared structural bridges transport checked support across papers/contexts; executable deciders answer modal diamond/box queries ("justified somewhere / everywhere"), and a mechanized counterexample shows transport ≠ status preservation, sharpened by a conditional status-preservation theorem via attack bisimulation.
- **Substantial mechanization and implementation evidence**: Lean 4 metatheory (144 files, ~117k lines, sorry-free public audited part, only Lean's three standard axioms) plus a Haskell production checker validated by differential testing against the compiled Lean semantics, mutation suites, ablation studies, and timing measurements.

## Methodology

**Language design.** A LARA program is a claim-support certificate interpreted against four fixed trusted inputs: a logical signature Σ, a versioned policy Π (naming inference schemes, mandatory/optional critical questions, contrary relations, exception conditions, and leaf-admission decisions admit/quarantine/reject), a strict-backend registry R (each backend owing four laws: deterministic replay, normalization reflection, certificate soundness, dependency accounting), and an artifact snapshot. Claims pair prose with a formal ground atom via an untrusted human-signed binding; support is positional identity (normalized syntactic equality, decidable, linear-time) rather than entailment, so no solver enters the trusted base. Propositions are normalized by a structural nf(·) normal form; conflict comes only from a declared contrary relation, never from negation-as-failure.

**Checking pipeline.** Leaf admission (policy table over leaf-kind/provenance, with quarantine that removes leaves from the checking context), then local support and attack judgments (Figure 6/9), then a whole-program completeness scan requiring every ordered contrary pair to be covered by a declared attack. Accepted programs compile to finite Dung argumentation frameworks whose arguments are complete checked support terms; grounded labelling plus a fixed-priority aggregation gives the four claim statuses plus an evidence-blocked public value under quarantine. The trust architecture mirrors proof-carrying code: an untrusted producer's output is checked against a small trusted base, and verdicts record policy/registry versions and artifact digests for byte-for-byte replay.

**Metatheory and mechanization.** Guarantees G1–G6 are stated as named theorems and mechanized in Lean 4 (34 of 35 specification results; the public metatheory is sorry-free with only propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound; three arguments remain on paper: the pipeline cost bound, the NP machine-model bookkeeping, and two clauses of the consistency theorem). Cross-context results include exact support transport (Thm 6.20) and conditional status preservation under attack bisimulation (Thm 6.21), with the transport-vs-status gap witnessed by a mechanized counterexample.

**Implementation and evaluation.** A Haskell implementation covers the full pipeline and all four strict backends. A differential harness uses the compiled Lean semantics as a reference interpreter, requiring byte-identical reports and exit codes; a mutation harness drives 541 mutant programs plus 60 corpus units; three ablations rerun a 655-input manifest with obligation discharge, conflict coverage, or typed-attack checking disabled. An exploratory corpus study machine-annotates a 32-artifact corpus (231 claims, 279 dead ends), samples 60 claims, and measures missing support, scheme coverage, and recorded attacks. Five case studies (rebuttal exchange across three rounds, deterministic verdict-to-review rendering, cross-paper disagreement scoping, a philosophy-of-mathematics debate with no measurements, and axiom withdrawal blocking proof reuse) exercise defeat, reinstatement, contestation, and assumption-dependent certificates; all reuse the existing calculus without new kernel rules.

## Results & Highlights

- **Mechanization scale:** the Lean 4 tree contains 144 files and roughly 117,000 lines; the audited public metatheory is sorry-free and uses only Lean's three standard axioms; 34 of 35 specification results are fully mechanized, with dependency accounting proven for all four registered backends.
- **Differential conformance:** at revision b62d33e0, the Haskell checker agrees with the compiled Lean reference interpreter on 673/673 verdict-bearing inputs (accepted programs and semantic rejections) and 66/66 malformed inputs, requiring byte-identical reports and exit codes; the historical harness agreed on 601/601 inputs (541 mutants + 60 corpus units), and a separate admission differential agrees on all 15 semantic outcomes byte-for-byte.
- **Ablation study (655-input manifest, rev 7d0f7384):** removing obligation discharge newly admits 18 incomplete-support programs; removing conflict coverage admits 5 missing-required-attack programs; disabling attack typing and coverage admits 32 further invalid-target/unlicensed-attack programs (11 in class R10, 21 in R11) — each check catches a distinct, non-overlapping defect class, and no ablation changes any other case's outcome.
- **Corpus study (exploratory, 60-claim stratified sample from 32 artifacts / 231 claims):** 48 claims are gap, 9 justified, 3 defeated, 0 contested — 4 in 5 encoded claims lack complete support under the selected policy; of the 176 mandatory critical questions raised, 71 go unmet (68 as gaps, 3 as defeaters); the nine justified verdicts rest on 38 load-bearing leaves, all agent-produced (20 attested to papers, 18 resolved by theorem).
- **Checker performance (Apple M5 Pro, GHC 9.14.1, Lean 4.32.0):** median end-to-end verdict for a corpus unit is 629.6 µs (worst 675.3 µs), with pre-decoded check+render at 281.3 µs and validate/compile/evaluate each below 2 µs; one full pass over the 601-record harness takes ~247 ms, so the differential suite runs interactively. The authors note compiled frameworks in this corpus have at most 2 nodes and 1 edge, so this establishes low overhead on small inputs, not scalability.
- **Theory highlights:** of the 64 update-kind × source-status × target-status cells, exactly 37 are reachable (Thm 6.14); grounded evaluation issues between n² and n²(1+n) attack queries with tight bounds on the realizable class (Thm 6.17); credulous complete acceptance on LARA-realizable frameworks is NP-complete via a mechanized 3SAT reduction (Thm 6.18), while grounded core checking stays polynomial (Prop 6.2); in the rebuttal case study, claim statuses move non-monotonically across review rounds (c_bench: justified → defeated → justified; c_kurt: gap → gap → justified; c_measure conceded as defeated).

The paper builds directly on the same lab's Agent-Native Research Artifact (ARA) line of work (Liu et al. 2026, "The Last Human-Written Paper"), and the bibliography spans formal argumentation (Dung, ASPIC+, Caminada), justification logic (Artemov, Pandžić), proof-carrying code (Necula, Miller), and recent agentic-science systems (AI Scientist, AgentRxiv-style work), suggesting a group that genuinely straddles the PL/formal-methods and AI-agent communities. The mechanization artifacts (sorry-free Lean, CI audit workflow) and reproducible pinned revisions are unusually strong credibility signals for a language-design paper. Caveat: this appears to be a lab preprint rather than a peer-reviewed venue publication, and both authors are the entire byline.

## Critical Assessment

- **The novelty is real but disciplinary, not semantic.** The argumentation semantics themselves are standard (Dung grounded semantics, ASPIC+-style rebut/undercut/undermine, Walton-style critical questions, Caminada reinstatement); the paper is explicit that its contribution is "a checked instantiation and compilation discipline, not a new abstract device." The genuinely new pieces are the mandatory critical-question gate (an instance with an unanswered mandatory question cannot support a claim), the fourth gap status, status preservation through compilation, and the transport-vs-status-preservation separation. Whether that package is worth ~117k lines of Lean depends on appetite for the research-claims-as-programs vision.
- **The corpus study is weak evidence, honestly labeled.** It is exploratory with a *single machine annotation pass* per artifact and "human review pending"; the only double annotation is the claim-type field on 18 of 60 claims (agreement on 14, κ = 0.73), and the 32-artifact corpus was assembled rather than sampled, so no external-validity claim is possible. Ironically, the annotation pipeline that produced the headline "48/60 claims are gap" is itself an untrusted LLM-mediated judgment — exactly the kind of unauditable step the paper criticizes in peer review. The authors flag this, which is to their credit, but the empirical case that real papers encode cleanly is not yet made: ~90% expressibility is a "judged" (not compiler-accepted) figure with six residual inexpressible shape families (equivalence schemes, distribution-valued leaves, graded predicates, etc.).
- **Performance evaluation borders on token.** The timing table's compiled frameworks have at most 2 nodes and 1 edge per corpus unit, and the paper concedes the timings "establish low overhead on small inputs, not scalability on large argumentation frameworks." The real workload — a literature-scale network of bridged programs with the NP-complete credulous acceptance lurking behind richer queries — is never exercised. The NP-completeness theorem is a genuine warning sign for the vision that the paper meets with a cost model rather than an engineering answer.
- **The hardest problem is placed outside the trust boundary by fiat.** The prose-to-formal binding is untrusted by design, and lowering an ARA artifact to LARA "inherits the same untrusted, model-mediated step" (the ARA Compiler already uses an LLM to reconstruct artifacts). So the checker guarantees are conditional on formalizations whose faithfulness is precisely the contested, judgment-laden part of scientific review. The paper is scrupulously honest about this scope (a "Scope" paragraph in Section 2.3 and again in limitations), but a reader should understand that LARA audits the *consequences* of declared support, not the support's connection to reality.
- **Missing evaluations:** no user study or comparison with human reviewers on rendered verdicts (explicitly deferred as "a separate empirical study"); no evaluation of bridge discovery or automated literature-based review (explicitly out of scope); the rebuttal case study is hand-lowered from a corpus gap, not replayed from a real OpenReview thread at scale; and no other untrusted producer than the deterministic lowering has been exercised end-to-end.
- **Motivating numbers are soft.** The intro's "agents already perform 31.1 workdays of research per workday of human labor (OpenAI, 2026)" and "submissions exceed 20,000 papers" cite an internal OpenAI report and preprints — fine as framing, but not load-bearing evidence.
- **No red flags of dishonesty.** Scope statements are unusually candid, ablations and mutation counts are reported with revision hashes, and degraded/failed cases (corpus claims that stay gap) are kept in the data rather than hidden.

## Strengths

- Genuine formal guarantees with a sorry-free Lean mechanization at real scale (~117k lines, 34/35 results), using only standard axioms, with every public theorem audited by a CI gate.
- A crisp, previously-collapsed distinction — missing support (gap) vs defeated support — that maps directly onto what a reviewer or a rebuttal must actually do (supply an argument vs answer an objection).
- Non-factivity done right: certified strict arithmetic stays justified while the empirical claim above it is defeated, making the checker's epistemic boundary visible in the verdict rather than buried in prose.
- Evaluation discipline rare for language papers: differential testing against an independent mechanized reference interpreter, mutation testing (541 mutants, including 31 localization mutants with a seeded mislocating-checker control), per-check ablations with disjoint failure classes, rejection-class inventories, and per-phase timings.
- Case studies that span empirical review, philosophical debate (no measurements at all), and axiom withdrawal — all on the same calculus without domain-specific kernel rules, demonstrating the abstraction is not overfit to ML benchmark comparisons.
- Honest, repeated scoping of what the checker does and does not establish, with pinned revisions and replayable artifacts for essentially every quantitative claim.

## Limitations

- Prose-to-formal bindings, policy choice, and bridge applicability are trusted or human judgments outside the guarantees; a valid certificate can sit on an unfaithful formalization, and the ARA→LARA lowering inherits an LLM-mediated reconstruction step.
- Strict-chain well-formedness excludes conflicts involving strict-rule conclusions, so some arguments (e.g., headline conclusions resting on defensible steps only) cannot even be expressed without losing consistency guarantees; some unattacked claims remain untouched.
- The exploratory corpus study has no human-reviewed annotation, no sampling frame, and double annotation on only 18/60 claim types (κ = 0.73); its distributions carry no external-validity claim.
- Timings cover ≤2-node frameworks; scalability of checking and grounded evaluation on literature-scale bridged networks is untested, and credulous complete acceptance is NP-complete even on realizable frameworks in one fixed context.
- No evaluation of automated lowering by untrusted producers other than the deterministic path, no evaluation of bridge discovery, and no comparison of rendered review comments with actual human reviewer behavior.
- The surface syntax was designed only "far enough to exercise the calculus end to end"; whether LLM agents can reliably emit valid LARA programs is an explicitly open authoring-language question — which is also the adoption risk for the whole agenda.
- Not yet peer reviewed (arXiv preprint, v1, 21 Sep 2026).

## Relevance

这篇论文与"自主科研 agent"方向的核心痛点——agent 产出结论无法被可靠审计——直接相关：LARA 把研究声明的支持/反驳结构变成可判定、可回放、可跨论文追踪的执行工件，等于给 agent 科研流水线加了一个"认识论内核"（continuous integration for hypotheses）。对于跟踪 agent-native 研究基础设施、可验证科学以及 Lean/形式化与 LLM 结合的读者，这是该子方向上少见的既有严肃机械化又有工程产出的基础设施工作。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Can current LLM agents reliably emit well-formed LARA programs directly (the unexamined surface-syntax question), and does authoring-time checking catch more defects than post-hoc lowering of finished papers?
- Can bridge discovery across a literature be automated and audited, or will the modal network's value always be capped by hand-declared comparisons?
- How do grounded checking and the four-state aggregation behave at literature scale (thousands of programs, dense attack graphs), given the n²..n²(1+n) query bounds and the NP-complete credulous variant?
- Would the guarantee structure survive adversarial producers who choose policies or contraries to game verdicts, given that policy, signature, and registry are trusted inputs?
- Does the deterministic verdict-to-review renderer's output (48 gap + 3 defeated comments over the 60-unit corpus) align with what human reviewers actually complain about — the deferred empirical study?
- How does the framework interact with NL→Lean formalization quality (Zhang et al., 2026): is the untrusted binding boundary stable in practice, or does unfaithful formalization become the dominant failure mode?
