Why this matters

A reusable freeze-and-probe protocol to test whether any self-evolving agent truly improves — plus counter-evidence every skill-library project must confront.

对任何构建技能库或记忆机制的 Agent 项目，这是必须正视的反面证据。

Critical edge

SkillBoost is the authors’ own method, and the comparison is confounded: the main table partly ranks whether a method has a validation gate, not evolution quality.

No control adds a gate to gate-less baselines; all conclusions come from one synthetic trading domain.

01Methodology

K0 K1 K2 K3 K4 K5

freeze artifact at K → probe held-out read-only → attribute the delta

  • Formalization. Base model, tools, and executor stay fixed; only persistent artifacts (memory / skill / workflow) update along an ordered stream. The evaluation target is the update process, not a final score.
  • EvoPathBench. 1,800 streams = 3 templates × 6 task families × 100: Accumulation probes learning generalization, Interference retention after 3 distractor updates, Reversal rule revision under systematic counterevidence. Two market layers: exogenous price paths + an endogenous call auction against 5 opponents (Binance Spot, calibrated synthesis).
  • Probing. Artifacts frozen at six checkpoints K0–K5, evaluated on held-out episodes in near-distribution and transfer variants; score is risk-adjusted wealth after fees, violations, and excess drawdown.
  • Attribution. CEG vs. a non-evolving baseline · SUE via state-off intervention · retention ΔRL / tail ΔCVaR (τ = 0.2/0.1) · revision gains REV1/REV2 — separating “got richer” from “got more capable.”

Four negative findings — plus the bottleneck

  1. Transfer shrinkage. Near-distribution skill gains invert under shift; SkillBoost is the only method positive on both sides.
  2. Tail-dominated forgetting. Worst-10% losses dwarf means; even SkillBoost (best backward transfer, +0.863) declines on 21.3% of trajectories.
  3. No reliable rule revision. Zero effects survive Holm correction; SkillBoost’s post-change CEG collapses 0.383 → 0.001 after its second update.
  4. Selection is the bottleneck. 1→4 candidates lifts best-candidate gain +0.496 yet widens the commit gap +0.163; safeguards cut harmful commits 12.4%→6.2% only by missing 16.5% of improvements.
  5. Instability & cost. Task order swings SkillOpt CEG5 by 9.5 points (+5.615 vs −3.852); token spend is decoupled from quality.

Cross-model footnote: SkillBoost skills add +1.96 to a frozen 27B receiver (near-SFT’s +2.19) but only +0.03 on 9B.

03Strengths

  • First benchmark making “when does a capability appear, persist, and erode” measurable — frozen-artifact probes with paired CEG/SUE attribution that most agent benchmarks lack entirely.
  • Unusually honest statistics: a Holm correction that nullifies its own rankings, CVaR tail reporting, 50-seed repetition, task-order ablation — reported, not discovered by critics.
  • Mechanism-level diagnosis by direct intervention: the selection-vs-generation bottleneck and the safeguard trade-off are quantified, not asserted.
  • Scale & transparency: 1,800 streams, two backbones, audited 1.838B tokens, released code plus complete per-checkpoint artifact transcripts.

04Limitations

  • Single domain: everything lives in one synthetic trading environment; the six families are admitted interventions, not a taxonomy of markets.
  • Short horizons: 5 updates and 24-step episodes per stream; whether tail erosion compounds over long deployments is unknown.
  • Confounded comparison: gate vs. gate-less acceptance policies mix into the ranking; no error bars on the main table.
  • Receiver-scale dependence: distilled skills add +1.96 on a 27B receiver but +0.03 on 9B.
  • Verification is costly: proprietary API backbones and a ≈CNY 36k list-price replication bill.

05Open questions

  1. Do the four negative findings replicate outside trading — SWE-bench-style streams, web navigation?
  2. Can acceptance rules separate “evidence against an outdated rule” from “evidence against a still-valid capability”?
  3. Would adding validation gates to gate-less baselines collapse the gap to SkillBoost?
  4. Does distilling validated artifacts into weights avoid the tail losses of artifact updates?
  5. Does the 96%-negative transfer result persist with more repeats and multi-order evaluation?
