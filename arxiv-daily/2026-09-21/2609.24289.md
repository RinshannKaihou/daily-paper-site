> **一句话总结：** 华为 Poisson Lab 提出 TTSE，把 LLM 智能体的经验知识拆成 FACT（环境事实，“世界是什么样”）和 TIP（条件化执行规程，“在什么条件下怎么做”）两条独立演化的在线轨道，配合 Blame→Retire→Contradict/Synthesize→Induce 四阶段规则生命周期；决策论上把超额风险精确分解为表征遗憾与执行遗憾两项。在 ALFWorld 上 DeepSeek-V3.2 成功率 97.8% 对最强基线 GraSp 83.6%（+14.2pp），ScienceWorld 原生成功率从 53.08% 提到 73.46%（+20.38pp），SOPBench 双轨比单轨规则库净增 +2.9pp（p<10⁻⁴）。

> **TL;DR:** TTSE splits an LLM agent's accumulated experience into two independently governed rule banks — FACT (declarative environmental facts, verified by count-based interaction evidence) and TIP (conditioned procedures of the form `<condition> :: <action>`) — evolving online over task chunks via a Blame → Retire → Contradict/Synthesize → Induce lifecycle. Decision-theoretically, it proves an exact excess-risk decomposition into representation regret (E_FACT) and execution regret (E_TIP), plus a bound of the form R(F,π) − R* ≤ E_TIP + η_F·Δ_max. Empirically, on ALFWorld it reaches 97.8% success with DeepSeek-V3.2 vs 83.6% for the strongest baseline GraSp (+14.2pp) and 98.51% with GLM-5; on ScienceWorld it lifts macro reward from 0.7204 to 0.8534 (+0.133) and native success by +20.38pp; fusing it into Bayesian-Agent on SOPBench adds +3.0pp over a single-track rule bank (permutation p<10⁻⁴, 95% CI [+2.34, +3.50]), with dual-track rules 18% shorter; and on the real end-to-end PinchBench (147 tool-calling tasks, OpenClaw harness) it beats the bare agent in all three runs (mean 0.7822 vs 0.7340) while halving run-to-run variance.

## 核心贡献

- **提出 TTSE 双轨在线自演化框架**：将智能体经验显式分为 FACT（环境事实，陈述式、“世界是什么样”，如“关着的容器在打开前不可见”）与 TIP（任务条件化执行规程、“在 <condition> 下做 <action>”），两条轨道各自拥有独立的归因、验证、退役与矛盾综合生命周期，而非混在单一未区分规则库中。
- **决策论刻画双轨价值**：命题 1 给出超额风险的精确分解 R(F,π) − R* = E_TIP（执行遗憾）+ E_FACT（表征遗憾）；命题 2 证明环境条件化策略价值 H = R*_agn − R*_cond ≥ 0，且当多个环境条件要求不同最优动作时严格为正；命题 3 将下游风险用 FACT 识别错误率 η_F 与跨条件动作错配代价 Δ_max 上界约束。
- **四阶段在线演化管线**：按 chunk 训练（本组 5 chunks），每轮执行 Blame（失败归因至单条规则，防止归因稀释）→ Retire（带审计记录退役）→ Contradict/Synthesize（把相互矛盾的退役规则合成为带显式条件的单一 TIP）→ Induce（对比成功/失败轨迹 + 纯成功轨迹两阶段增量归纳，操作限于 EDIT/AGREE/REMOVE/ADD）。
- **四层互补实验验证**：GDPevo 双轨 vs 单轨消融（机制层）、SOPBench 与 Bayesian-Agent 融合（可迁移性层）、ALFWorld/ScienceWorld 端到端任务适配（经典基准层）、PinchBench 真实多工具任务部署（落地层）；全部实验均为 3 次独立重复，并披露双轨在个别域落后的案例（如 dangerous_goods −0.6pp）。
- **可组合性**：TIP 轨道可直接驱动 Claude-skill 风格的技能发现与调用（PinchBench 中自动调用 claude-skill 完成 GitHub CLI 任务），框架可作为模块化归纳偏置嵌入现有技能自演化方法。

## Key Contributions

- **TTSE, a dual-track online self-evolution framework** that partitions experiential knowledge into FACT (declarative environmental facts whose reliability is continuously verified via count-based interaction evidence) and TIP (task-conditioned implementation procedures in strict `<condition> :: <action>` form), each with an independent lifecycle of attribution, verification, retirement, and contradiction synthesis.
- **A decision-theoretic account of why the split matters**: Proposition 1 gives the exact identity R(F,π) − R* = E_TIP(F,π) + E_FACT(F) (execution regret + representation regret); Proposition 2 proves the value of environmental conditioning H = R*_agn − R*_cond ≥ 0, strictly positive whenever multiple environmental conditions require different optimal actions with positive loss margins; Proposition 3 bounds downstream risk by R(F,π) − R* ≤ E_TIP(F,π) + η_F·Δ_max, coupling FACT identification error (η_F) with cross-condition action-mismatch cost (Δ_max).
- **A chunk-based online evolution pipeline** (cold-start induction, then per-chunk Blame → Retire → Contradict/Synthesize → Induce): failures are attributed to at most one rule (preventing blame dilution), culpable rules are retired with audit records, contradictory retired-rule pairs are synthesized into single conditioned TIPs, and new rules are induced in a contrast stage then a success-only stage under strict operation budgets (max 4 ops/rule/chunk).
- **Four complementary experiment layers** — GDPevo dual-vs-single-track ablation, SOPBench fusion with Bayesian-Agent posterior-driven skill management, end-to-end ALFWorld/ScienceWorld task adaptation, and non-invasive PinchBench deployment in a real agent harness — all with 3 independent runs, statistical testing, and disclosed per-domain losses.

## Methodology

**Dual-track knowledge representation.** TTSE organizes interaction-derived knowledge into two banks. FACT rules are declarative statements about the environment ("what the world is like"), e.g., "closed containers are not visible until the container is opened"; each carries metadata (create/last-verified timestamps, update frequency, support count, truth value, hidden status) and is validated through count-based evidence tracking. TIP rules are conditional action strategies ("under <condition> do <action>", e.g., "under condition: the task requires multiple objects of the same type, after placing the first one, continue searching for remaining objects"). A dual-judgment prompt classifier assigns each induced rule: the **Subject Test** asks whether the rule's subject is the environment/object (→FACT) or the agent (→TIP); the **Necessity Test** is a soft heuristic (violating a FACT hard constraint blocks the task; violating a TIP merely reduces efficiency). The default when uncertain is TIP — a deliberately conservative choice to prevent FACT contamination (e.g., "washing clothes with a sink basin may fail" must be a TIP, not a universal environmental law).

**Online chunk-based evolution.** Training tasks are split into N equally sized chunks (chunk_ratio 0.2 → 5 chunks in the benchmarks). Chunk 0 cold-starts with an empty bank: pure induction from the first batch of trajectories, so initial rules are not biased by prior knowledge. Each subsequent chunk runs four strictly ordered phases: (1) **Blame** — an LLM analyzes each failed trajectory and attributes the failure to at most one existing rule, marked "culpable" only when it actively misled the agent; (2) **Retire** — culpable rules are removed from the active pool with audit records (retirement reason, chunk index, timestamp) but kept as evidence; (3) **Contradict/Synthesize** — pairs of retired rules giving opposite advice about the same environmental property are surfaced, and the LLM synthesizes each pair into a single TIP with an explicit condition, converting conflicting unconditional advice into conditioned behavior selection; (4) **Induce** — incremental updates in two stages: a contrast stage pairing success and failure trajectories to distill improvements, then a success-only stage inducing reusable strategies from successes. Operations are restricted to EDIT/AGREE/REMOVE/ADD with per-rule budgets, so induction performs local optimization rather than global rewriting.

**Theory.** Agent execution is modeled as decision making under latent environmental conditions z: a FACT representation F: O → S and a TIP policy π: O × S → Δ(A). Proposition 1's decomposition is an exact identity (proved by adding/subtracting R(F, π*_TIP)); Proposition 3 assumes the TIP class contains the plug-in conditional policy a*(o, F(o)) and bounds E_FACT ≤ η_F·Δ_max. A binary-flip corollary shows that when two equally likely conditions require mutually incompatible unique actions and the observation carries no information about z, any condition-agnostic policy succeeds at most 1/2 while a condition-identifying system achieves 1. The authors explicitly frame the theory as functional motivation for a modular inductive bias — not a universal-expressiveness claim and not a convergence proof for the LLM implementation.

**Integration modes.** TTSE runs as a full replacement pipeline (ALFWorld/ScienceWorld/GDPevo), fused with an external skill manager by replacing Bayesian-Agent's ~1/r counting with posterior-driven lifecycle while keeping TTSE's FACT/TIP representation and LLM induction (SOPBench), or injected non-invasively into a general agent harness via top-10 FACT + top-10 TIP retrieval (all-mpnet-base-v2 embeddings, kNN; PinchBench).

## Results & Highlights

- **ALFWorld (134 unseen tasks, 6 types; 3-run means; pure action steps):** With DeepSeek-V3.2, TTSE reaches **97.8% success at 10.0 steps vs 83.6%/14.8 for the strongest baseline GraSp (+14.2pp)**, far ahead of Expel (76.1%), ReAct (69.4%), SkillOpt (68.7%), and SkillLLM (56.5%). With GLM-5 (thinking), TTSE hits **98.51%/9.2 steps vs GraSp 94.9%/10.3 (+3.6pp)** — the margin shrinks as the backbone strengthens. SkillOpt and SkillLLM are the authors' own re-implementations under the same setting and are claimed to outperform original skill-optimization baselines.
- **ScienceWorld (30 science-experiment task types, GLM-5, six runs):** TTSE mean **macro reward 0.8534±0.012 vs baseline 0.7204±0.010 (Δ +0.1330)**; native success rate **73.46%±3.2 vs 53.08%±1.74 (+20.38pp)**. Evaluation is deployment-realistic: the rule library is the one retained after the final training chunk (experience accumulation, not a per-task oracle), with same backbones, sharding, and 50-step budget.
- **GDPevo four-condition ablation (12 enterprise task groups across CRM/ERP/finance/HR, GLM-5, 3 runs each):** Full FACT+TIP scores **0.2616 vs TIP-only 0.2390 (+2.4pp), FACT-only 0.1918, and zero-shot base 0.0947** — roughly a 2.8× improvement over base. FACT-only leaves substantial execution regret (environment descriptions alone never specify a conditional procedure); TIP-only beats FACT-only but lacks an independently maintained representation of environment-dependent behavioral branches.
- **SOPBench fusion with Bayesian-Agent (5 domains, 892 tasks × 3 repetitions = 2,676 evaluations, DeepSeek v4-flash):** Zero-shot base 72.15% → single-track unified rules 91.34% → **dual-track 94.38% (+3.0pp over single; paired bootstrap over 892 tasks, 10,000 resamples: +2.9pp, permutation p < 10⁻⁴, 95% CI [+2.34pp, +3.50pp])**. Gains are uneven: warehouse/package inspection contributes 78% of the total (+8.1pp domain-level, +14.4pp attribution), dangerous_goods is **−0.6pp (dual-track worse)**, and excluding warehouse the retained advantage is +0.67pp (p = 0.011). Dual-track rules are also **18% shorter (137 vs 167 chars/rule), cutting ~17% of rule-context tokens** per task injection.
- **PinchBench deployment (147 real end-to-end multi-tool tasks; OpenClaw harness; retrieval injection, TTSE_ENABLE=0 baseline; 3 independent runs):** TTSE wins every run (**per-run gains +4.57pp, +2.67pp, +4.02pp, averaging +3.83pp; overall mean 0.7822 vs 0.7340**), and the run-to-run range drops from 3.31pp to 1.64pp — experience accumulation makes the agent more stable. In one run, strictly perfect (1.0) tasks rise from 65/147 to 70/147. The TIP track also drives Claude-skill discovery: a GitHub-CLI task auto-invokes the claude-skill via the framework's AgentSkill system.

## Authors & Credibility

The eight authors are all affiliated with the **Poisson Lab at Huawei** (first author Ruimin Pei additionally with Tsinghua University, prm21@mails.tsinghua.edu.cn). This is an industrial research team, and the paper reads like one: the evaluation spans an internal-flavored enterprise benchmark (GDPevo), an industry SOP benchmark (SOPBench), and a deployment experiment inside a real agent harness (OpenClaw), which is exactly the kind of infrastructure a lab building production agents would have. Credibility signals are unusually strong for this subfield: every headline experiment uses 3 (ScienceWorld: 6) independent repetitions, the SOPBench comparison gets a paired-bootstrap significance test with disclosed CI, the ethics section pre-commits to reporting all three runs and explicitly discloses domain-level cases where dual-track loses, and Appendix B documents hyperparameters, prompt templates (Blame, Contradict, induction), and environment variables in full. The listed references are current and on-point (Reflexion, ExpeL, Voyager, RaCHET, Mem-α, Memp, Bayesian-Agent 2026, GraSp, SkillOpt), indicating genuine engagement with the agent self-evolution literature. No prior-code-release track record for this exact team is verifiable from the paper itself, and no public code URL is stated, which tempers reproducibility despite the excellent documentation.

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty is moderate but honestly framed.** Separating declarative from procedural knowledge has precedents — CoALA's taxonomy, MIRIX's separate stores, MCSE's "grounded traces vs reusable procedures" partition — and the authors cite all three, positioning TTSE as the first to implement environment-fact and conditional-execution policies as *co-evolving online banks driven by failure attribution and contradiction synthesis*. That framing is defensible; the mechanism-level novelty is in Blame/Retire/Contradict-Synthesize, not the split itself.

**The headline ablation is the weakest link.** In GDPevo, full (0.2616) vs TIP-only (0.2390) — the comparison that directly validates the dual-track claim — is explicitly *not* significance-tested, and the paper itself concedes the gap "is not inconsistent in direction across runs but its magnitude ... should be read as direction rather than a statistically certified margin." Worse, the TIP-only per-run scores are wildly unstable (0.1212, 0.2513, 0.2314 — a 13-point spread), so the +2.4pp mean rests on one catastrophic TIP-only run. A variance-matched comparison could plausibly erase it.

**Concentration of gains.** In SOPBench, warehouse/package inspection alone contributes 78% of the dual-vs-single gain; excluding it leaves +0.67pp (p = 0.011 — significant but thin). Combined with the −0.6pp regression on dangerous_goods, the honest summary is that dual-track helps a lot exactly where hidden environmental conditions flip optimal actions (Proposition 2's regime) and does little elsewhere, which the paper admits but the abstract's "aggregate improvement of +3.4pp" phrasing softens.

**Baseline provenance is mixed.** On ALFWorld, Expel and GraSp numbers are copied from the GraSp paper while SkillOpt and SkillLLM are the authors' re-implementations — three different provenance channels in one table invites apples-to-oranges questions, even though the paper flags it. All baselines are also memory/skill methods; there is no fine-tuning or RL baseline (e.g., GRPO-style adaptation), so "self-evolution vs training" comparisons are absent.

**Missing measurements.** The FACT/TIP classifier is a prompt-based dual test with default-to-TIP, but the paper never measures classification error, nor how often Blame misattributes failures (the prompt instructs blaming at most one rule "conservatively" — a design choice whose error rate is unquantified). Rule-bank size is capped at 20 (max_num_rules), and all evaluated domains are small (≤500 training tasks, ≤147 eval tasks); scalability of the library, retrieval, and contradiction synthesis to long-horizon deployments is untested. Finally, despite the exhaustive reproducibility appendix, no code release is stated, and the appendix reveals training used internal infrastructure (Hydra configs, internal mirrors) that outside researchers cannot fully reconstruct.

## Strengths

- Rare experimental hygiene for the agent-memory subfield: 3–6 independent runs everywhere, a permutation test with CI on the key SOPBench claim, and per-run numbers disclosed rather than only means.
- The theory is modest and precise: an exact identity plus a clean bound (η_F·Δ_max), with Appendix A.7 explicitly disclaiming convergence guarantees and universal-expressiveness claims — the right epistemic posture.
- Mechanism design details show real iteration: blame dilution prevention (max one blamed rule), audit-trailed retirement preserved for contradiction synthesis, cold-start isolation from bank bias, and operation budgets against global rewriting.
- Honest negative disclosure: dangerous_goods −0.6pp and the untested GDPevo gap are stated in the paper, not hidden.
- Practical breadth in one framework: standalone pipeline, fusion with an external skill manager (Bayesian-Agent), and non-invasive retrieval injection into OpenClaw, with evidence the TIP track interoperates with the Claude-skill ecosystem.

## Limitations

- **Blank cold start:** chunk 0 relies entirely on the base LLM's zero-shot induction, so performance is bounded in domains where the backbone is weak; the authors suggest seeding with human examples or a stronger model.
- **No execution verification of new rules:** induced rules are filtered only by post-hoc evidence counting and the Subject/Necessity prompt tests; there is no held-out validation or structured verification before a rule enters the bank, so spurious or over-specialized rules from low-quality trajectories can persist (acknowledged in Section 7).
- **Key ablation lacks statistical certification:** the dual-vs-TIP-only GDPevo margin is untested and built on a high-variance TIP-only run.
- **Gain concentration:** SOPBench improvement is dominated by one domain; ALFWorld's margin over GraSp shrinks from +14.2pp (DeepSeek-V3.2) to +3.6pp (GLM-5), suggesting strong backbones internalize much of the conditional knowledge anyway.
- **Scale and generalization:** small task counts, a 20-rule bank cap, text-centric benchmarks, and no RL/fine-tuning baseline; the binary-flip corollary's clean 1/2-vs-1 regime is never instantiated as a controlled experiment.
- **No public code** despite full hyperparameter/prompt documentation; the theory says nothing about whether the Blame/Retire credit assignment actually converges.

## Relevance

这篇论文与用户关注的 LLM 智能体自演化与经验记忆方向高度契合：它给出了“声明式环境知识 vs 条件化程序知识”的可操作分离方案、配套的规则生命周期管理（归因/退役/矛盾综合）和风险分解理论，可直接对照自己记忆系统设计中的单库混存问题；SOPBench/PinchBench 的融合与非侵入式注入实验也为“如何把这类机制嫁接到现有 agent 框架”提供了模板。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would a held-out execution-verification gate (test each candidate rule on a few validation tasks before banking) close the gap on domains like dangerous_goods where dual-track currently regresses −0.6pp?
- The GDPevo full-vs-TIP-only margin (+2.4pp) rests on a high-variance TIP-only run (0.1212–0.2513 spread) — how many runs would a properly powered comparison need, and does the margin survive?
- How does the 20-rule bank cap behave at 10–100× longer deployments — does contradiction synthesis keep the bank compact, or does retrieval quality degrade as FACTs accumulate?
- Can η_F (FACT identification error) be measured empirically and tracked online, making Proposition 3's bound an actionable monitor rather than a post-hoc explanation?
- The ALFWorld advantage over GraSp collapses from +14.2pp to +3.6pp as the backbone improves — does dual-track evolution become redundant for frontier models, or does it shift value toward longer-horizon, higher-heterogeneity domains like warehouse inspection (where the +14.4pp gain appears)?
- The TIP track already drives Claude-skill discovery — could FACT/TIP separation be applied to the skill files themselves (conditioned skill selection vs environment-model files) in mainstream agent frameworks?
