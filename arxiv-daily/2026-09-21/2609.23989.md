> **一句话总结：** 提出 ACLArena 框架，在 Qwen3-8B 上以"数学→搜索→电商→指令遵循"四阶段 post-training 系统研究 Agent 持续学习：顺序训练导致 AIME26 从 25.8 崩塌至 6.0、NQ 从 45.2 跌至 14.6，而共享权重的三种整合范式（多教师在线蒸馏 MMOPD、自蒸馏 SFT、模型合并）各有明显短板；最终提出的 MLE（SDFT 骨干冻结 + 每阶段一个 LoRA 专家做 RL + 按环境路由）把 τ³-Retail 从 29.6 提到 52.9（+23.3）、多跳搜索从 25.0 提到 38.6，并在 GPQA 上取得所有方法中最高的 42.4。

> **TL;DR:** ACLArena is a controlled testbed for Agent Continual Learning (ACL): a Qwen3-8B-Base passes through Math→Search→E-commerce→Instruction-Following post-training, and plain sequential training collapses AIME26 from 25.8 to 6.0 and NQ from 45.2 to 14.6 as later stages arrive. The paper systematically compares three consolidation paradigms under one framework — multi-teacher on-policy distillation (with a new entropy-gated "Mixed OPD" that fixes reverse-KL collapse on agentic tasks), self-distilled fine-tuning (SDFT), and weight-space model merging — and shows none dominates (MMOPD: AIME26 21.25 / NQ 45.2 but weaker MMLU; merging: best MMLU 80.3 but poor E-commerce/IF). Their recipe, MLE, freezes an SDFT-trained shared backbone and trains one LoRA expert per stage via RL with environment-based routing, lifting τ³-Retail from 29.6 (sequential final) to 52.9 (+23.3) and multi-hop search from 25.0 to 38.6, achieving the highest GPQA (42.4) of all evaluated methods — at the cost of some NQ (40.9 vs MMOPD's 45.2) and MMLU (70.9 vs 78.5).

## 核心贡献

- **ACLArena 基准与流水线**：构建了一个真实感强的多阶段 post-training 测试床（Qwen3-8B-Base，课程固定为 数学 RL → 搜索 SFT+RL → 电商 SFT+RL → 指令遵循 RL），每个阶段配 in-domain 评测（AIME26、NQ、τ³-Retail、IF-Eval）与刻意留出的 OOD 评测（GPQA-Diamond、MMLU-Redux、τ³-Telecom/Mock、IF-Bench、单跳/多跳搜索）。
- **机制层面的诊断**：模型级分析（权重 PCA、任务向量余弦）表明各任务诱导"部分对齐"的参数更新——隔离的各域 RL 增量近乎正交（|cos|≤0.004），而顺序累积向量高度共线（电商与 IF 的 cos=0.98）；token 级分析发现遗忘几乎全部集中在高熵"决策点"上（低熵 token 占 91.9%–95.2%，一致性保持 ≥0.97）。
- **三种整合范式的受控比较 + 新的 Mixed OPD**：把业界路线统一为行为空间在线复用（OPD）、行为空间离线回放（SDFT）、参数空间合并（MM）三类并路由到各自最优配置；发现 vanilla 反向 KL 蒸馏在 agentic 搜索任务上必然崩塌（截断率→100%、有效率→0），并提出按教师熵门控混合前向/反向 KL 的 MOPD。
- **MLE（Mixture of Low-Rank Experts）**：基于三条诊断结论设计的实用 ACL 配方——先用 SDFT 把各 oracle 轨迹回放进共享骨干，再冻结骨干、为每个阶段训练独立 LoRA 专家（RL），推理时用可观测环境（系统提示/工具 schema）路由，在 agentic 任务上大幅领先且避免跨阶段覆写。

## Key Contributions

- **ACLArena benchmark**: a realistic multi-stage post-training pipeline (Qwen3-8B-Base; curriculum Math → Search → E-commerce → IF) with paired in-domain and deliberately held-out out-of-domain evaluations, enabling controlled study of forgetting and generalization in agents.
- **Mechanistic diagnosis**: model-level analysis (weight PCA, task-vector cosine similarity) shows tasks induce partially aligned parameter updates — isolated per-domain RL increments are near-orthogonal (|cos| ≤ 0.004) while sequentially accumulated vectors are highly collinear (E-commerce vs IF: cos = 0.98); token-level analysis shows forgetting concentrates almost entirely in the high-entropy "decision-point" minority (low-entropy tokens, 91.9%–95.2% of positions, keep consistency ≥ 0.97).
- **Controlled comparison of three consolidation paradigms plus a new Mixed OPD**: unifies industry routes into behavior-space online reuse (OPD), behavior-space offline replay (SDFT), and parameter-space merging (MM), each routed to its best configuration; shows vanilla reverse-KL OPD structurally collapses on agentic search (truncation ratio → 100%, valid ratio → 0) and proposes entropy-gated Mixed OPD as the fix.
- **MLE (Mixture of Low-Rank Experts)**: a practical ACL recipe derived from the three findings — replay oracle trajectories into a shared backbone via SDFT, freeze it, train a separate LoRA expert per stage with RL, and route on the observable environment (system prompt / tool schemas); substantially improves agentic capabilities without cross-stage overwriting.

## Methodology

**Testbed.** Base model is Qwen3-8B-Base. Stage 1 (Math): critic-free RL (GSPO) on DAPO-Math-17K (17K integer-answer prompts). Stage 2 (Search): rejection-sampled SFT cold-start to teach the Qwen3 tool-call format, then RL over a Wikipedia-2018 + E5 retrieval environment (3 passages per query). Stage 3 (E-commerce): SFT + RL on the τ³-Bench *retail* domain (multi-turn dialogue + backend API/database tools, database-state-based verifiable reward); *telecom* and *mock* domains are never trained and serve as OOD. Stage 4 (IF): RL with executable verifiers on IFTrain constraints. All RL uses GSPO on the SLIME framework with an agentic rollout loop; evaluation serves models with SGLang (greedy for most benchmarks; AIME26 avg@16 at temperature 1.0). Sequential runs are reported as mean ± std over three independent runs.

**Diagnosis of sequential training (RQ1/RQ2).** Model-level: unit-normalized checkpoints are projected onto the top-2 principal components, and cosine similarities of cumulative task vectors Δθ_k = θ_k − θ_0 are computed; isolated per-domain RL increments (shared-prefix removed) become near-orthogonal. Token-level: one set of token IDs is decoded by the math checkpoint and every later checkpoint scores the same sequence under teacher forcing; positions are split by the math checkpoint's entropy (H = 0.5 nats) and per-checkpoint top-1 consistency is measured per bin.

**Paradigms (RQ3).** (1) *MMOPD*: K frozen stage teachers; the student is initialized from the final sequential checkpoint; prompts are mixed in balanced proportions; the domain-owning teacher scores the student's own rollout (scoring-only forward pass). The per-token loss is reverse KL where the teacher is confident plus a mass-covering forward KL restricted to tokens whose teacher entropy exceeds a threshold α (top-q = 16 renormalization); γ = 0.5, 3,000 prompts per domain, 100 steps. (2) *SDFT*: per-task oracle experts are trained from the base model, rolled out (16 samples per prompt), filtered on final-reward correctness, well-formed tool invocation, protocol compliance, and deduplicated (156,285 math / 157,750 search / 19,573 e-commerce / 225,681 IF trajectories), then SFT on the mix — ablated over initialization (base vs sequential checkpoint) and sampling strategy (Random-Mix vs Balanced-Mix). (3) *MM*: uniform or tuned weighted averaging of the four sequential stage checkpoints (task-vector form, Σλ_k free). (4) *MLE (ours)*: start from the SDFT checkpoint as a shared capability substrate, freeze the backbone, instantiate one LoRA per stage optimized by RL in its own environment; at inference the router uses only the observable interaction context (system instruction + available tool schemas) — no benchmark IDs or ground-truth labels.

## Results & Highlights

- **Sequential training forgets catastrophically and non-monotonically.** Introducing the E-commerce stage collapses AIME26 from 23.33 to 6.04 and NQ from 45.2 to 14.6, and drops OOD multi-hop search from 37.4 to 9.4; the final Seq-IF checkpoint recovers NQ only to 33.5 and multi-hop to 25.0 (while reaching 84.8 on IF-Eval). The final checkpoint dominates none of its predecessors: AIME26 10.21 vs 25.83, NQ 33.5 vs 45.2, τ³-Retail 29.6 vs 34.0 at earlier peaks.
- **MMOPD gives the best shared-weight trade-off.** Relative to the sequential final checkpoint, MMOPD improves AIME26 from 10.21 to 21.25 (+11.0), NQ from 33.5 to 45.2 (+11.7), single-hop search from 44.9 to 53.7, and multi-hop search from 25.0 to 32.8, while largely preserving IF — but with slightly lower MMLU and IF-Bench.
- **Vanilla reverse-KL OPD collapses on agentic tasks; Mixed OPD fixes it.** Distilling the search teacher back into the E-commerce checkpoint drives truncation ratio to 100% and test performance to 0 (the teacher assigns very low probability — advantage ≈ −10 — to critical transitional/termination tokens). With Mixed OPD, the single-teacher student lifts single-hop search to 53.8 vs the teacher's own 50.1 while largely preserving E-commerce capability.
- **MLE wins on agentic and generalization axes.** Vs the sequential final checkpoint, MLE reaches τ³-Retail 52.9 vs 29.6 (+23.3), single-hop search 57.7 vs 44.9, multi-hop 38.6 vs 25.0, and the highest reported GPQA among all methods (42.4 vs 37.9 for Seq-Final); it also approaches per-task oracle experts in-domain (IF-Eval 85.0 vs the IF oracle's 86.2). The trade-off: NQ 40.9 is below MMOPD's 45.2, and MMLU 70.9 sits below Seq-Final (78.5) and merged (80.3) models.
- **SDFT and merging each recover only slices.** Seq+SDFT reaches 48.3 NQ, 56.6 single-hop, and 38.0 multi-hop, but degrades E-commerce/IF badly (IF-Eval 84.8 → 53.6, τ³-Telecom 45.9 → 21.7); Balanced-Mix beats Random-Mix on average. Uniform model merging posts the best MMLU (80.3) yet an in-domain average of only 31.63, and the weight sweep confirms tuning merely shifts the trade-off (Search-heavy: search up to 44.5 but E-commerce down to 17.56).
- **Mechanistic quantifications.** SFT-stage parameter updates are typically 3–7× larger than RL/MMOPD updates (relative magnitude ‖Δθ‖/‖θ‖: SDFT 0.88%, SFT 0.33%, RL 0.07%, MMOPD 0.07%); token-level consistency stays ≥ 0.97 for the low-entropy majority (95.2%/93.6%/91.9% of positions on AIME/GPQA/IF-Eval) across all stages, while the high-entropy minority falls to ~0.6–0.76 — forgetting is decision-point-selective, not uniform.

## Authors & Credibility

The team spans UCLA and UC Santa Cruz, with equal-contribution first authors Haxin Wang and Xiaoxuan Wang; senior authors include Jason Cong and Yizhou Sun (UCLA). Xiaoxuan Wang is a co-author of Afarena (a unified agentic-RL framework), signaling hands-on agentic RL infrastructure experience, and the paper's engineering is credible: GSPO RL backbone, SLIME framework with an agentic rollout loop, SGLang serving, two NVIDIA H200 nodes, three-seed sequential runs with std, and hyperparameters disclosed in the appendix (α = 0.3, top-q = 16, γ = 0.5, batch sizes, trajectory counts). Code (GitHub) and models/data (HuggingFace collection) are publicly released, which materially supports reproducibility. The related-work grounding in real industrial pipelines (DeepSeek-V4 multi-teacher OPD, GLM-5, MiMo-V2, Nemotron-Cascade 2, MAI-Thinking-1, Rio 3.5 Open 397B merge-then-distill) shows the testbed mirrors actual practice.

## Critical Assessment

- **The headline "single model" claim oversells MLE.** MLE is one frozen backbone plus K per-stage LoRA adapters plus an environment-based router. That is parameter isolation — a modular system — not one set of weights that has reconciled all capabilities, which is exactly the route (MMOPD) the paper's own findings show makes smaller but genuinely shared updates. The comparison is fair empirically, but the framing invites over-reading.
- **The router assumption is the load-bearing weakness.** Routing keys on the observable environment context (system prompt + tool schemas). The authors honestly concede (Limitations) that ambiguous contexts, mid-episode context changes, and episodes mixing specializations are untested — which is precisely the regime where real deployed agents live. Every MLE result should be read as conditional on clean domain identifiability.
- **MLE does not dominate, and the prose leans on selective axes.** The claimed wins (GPQA, single-hop, multi-hop, mock) are real, but MLE loses NQ to MMOPD (40.9 vs 45.2), MMLU to both Seq-Final (70.9 vs 78.5) and merging (80.3), and IF-Bench to Seq-Final (29.1 vs 30.8). The abstract's "substantially improving the task's ability to learn across multiple domains" is accurate only for the agentic subset.
- **A surprising unexplained result:** MLE scores 52.9 on τ³-Retail, far above the dedicated retail oracle expert (33.2) that was trained on retail alone. Cross-domain replay during SDFT presumably helps, but no ablation isolates this — it deserves analysis since it suggests SDFT replay data composition, not just expert isolation, is doing work.
- **Curriculum generality is thin in the main results.** All sequential numbers use one fixed order (Math→Search→E-commerce→IF); Appendix G.2 gives order-independent evidence via oracle experts and task-vector geometry, but no full curriculum-permutation experiments, so statements like "math decays gradually" may be order-specific. Scale is also untested beyond Qwen3-8B-Base.
- **Genuine strengths worth crediting:** the Mixed OPD failure analysis is excellent — tracing OPD collapse to the teacher assigning advantage ≈ −10 on transitional/termination tokens is a concrete, actionable finding for anyone doing on-policy distillation on agentic tasks; the entropy-stratified token-consistency analysis (forgetting concentrated in the ~5–8% high-entropy decision points) is novel at this level of care.

## Strengths

- A controlled, fully disclosed testbed (fixed curriculum, matched tool schemas, verifiable rewards) with genuinely held-out OOD domains (telecom/mock never trained) — rare rigor for the ACL-for-agents area.
- Three findings (partial alignment of task updates; shared-weight trade-offs; SFT establishes behavior while RL refines it) each backed by independent quantitative evidence (PCA geometry, cosine matrices, update magnitudes, format/truncation rates), not just headline tables.
- Mixed OPD is a clean, well-motivated contribution: entropy-gated forward KL over top-q-renormalized teacher distributions, closed-form over the truncated set, constant extra scoring passes.
- Fair paradigm comparison: each baseline routed to its best configuration (grid-searched merge weights, both SDFT initializations, both mixing strategies) rather than straw-manned.
- Strong reproducibility: released code, models, data; hyperparameters and eval settings (SGLang, sampling parameters, retrieval setup, user-simulator model) fully specified.

## Limitations

- Single model family and size (Qwen3-8B-Base) and a single fixed four-stage curriculum; no curriculum-permutation or scale ablations in the main results.
- MLE's router requires unambiguous observable environment context; mixed-domain or shifting contexts within an episode are untested, and memory/latency costs of multi-adapter serving are not evaluated (acknowledged by the authors).
- All consolidation methods, including MLE, lose ground on general knowledge (MMLU) and OOD instruction following (IF-Bench) relative to sequential checkpoints' best levels; the forgetting problem is mitigated, not solved.
- The anomaly that MLE beats the dedicated retail oracle (52.9 vs 33.2) is not isolated by any ablation.
- Evaluation of E-commerce OOD depends on an LLM-simulated user (GLM-4.7-Flash), a possible confounder shared across methods but unquantified.

## Relevance

这篇论文与多阶段 agent post-training 的工程实践高度相关：它用受控实验回答了"数学/搜索/工具/指令遵循能力为何互相覆写、该用蒸馏、回放还是合并来整合"这两个所有训练多能力 agent 的团队都会遇到的问题，其中 Mixed OPD 与 MLE 两种配方可直接迁移到自家的多阶段训练流水线中。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- What happens when a single episode mixes domains (e.g., an e-commerce user asks a math question mid-conversation) — can learned routing or dynamic expert composition replace environment-keyed routing?
- Do the near-orthogonality of isolated task vectors and the high-entropy-token forgetting concentration hold at 70B+ scale and with 5–10 post-training stages?
- How sensitive are the paradigm rankings (MMOPD vs SDFT vs MM vs MLE) to curriculum order — does Math-first systematically bias which capabilities survive?
- Could Mixed OPD be applied *after* MLE training to distill the LoRA experts back into one weight set, combining parameter isolation during training with single-weight deployment?
- Why does MLE exceed the dedicated retail oracle on τ³-Retail (52.9 vs 33.2) — is SDFT replay-data cross-pollination the cause, and can it be exploited deliberately?
- How does MLE compare against the simpler alternative of joint multi-task RL from the start with Balanced-Mix sampling, without any staging at all?
