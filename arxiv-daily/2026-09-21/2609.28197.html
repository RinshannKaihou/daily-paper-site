Why this matters

Reframes agent safety from post-hoc verdicts to timed intervention; the lexical-overfitting finding directly guides any team deploying guard models or workflow safety layers.

从事后裁决转向带时间锚点的主动干预；词汇过拟合发现对部署 guard 模型的团队有直接参考价值。

Critical edge

Ground-truth anchors are annotated by GPT-4o itself — GPT-4o topping its own benchmark is exactly what this setup would predict.

Also: the 0.0–32.6% Procedural Safety Gap claim rests on 43 samples.

Methodology how the benchmark works

  • Task: a Guardian model watches a tool-using agent turn-by-turn, sees only the interaction history (no task instruction), and must output continue/interrupt each turn.
  • Temporal anchors: every trajectory marks the Earliest-Signal Turn t_es and irreversible Trigger Turn t_trigger; interventions are graded Perfect / Good / Effective / Early / Late.
  • Taxonomy: 5 Level-1 / 13 Level-2 risk categories (ANSI, SAFT, GDPR grounded), deliberately imbalanced to mirror real-world frequency.
  • Ablation: each of the 1,139 trajectories has a desensitized twin (“transfer”, “steam”, “knife” → neutral words); the “awakening effect” counts Early→OIW corrections.
  • Evaluation: 16 LLMs, zero-shot, greedy decoding; 8 reruns at τ=0.7 give std ≤ 1.22 pp on every metric.

Strengths

  • Genuinely new target: “when to intervene” becomes a first-class, quantifiable metric (OIW), not a vibe.
  • Rigorous QC: dual-model annotation (κ = 0.756), 90.4% human verification, masked risk metadata against leakage.
  • Causal ablation: paired 1,139-trajectory twins isolating lexical cues — a model of benchmark analysis.
  • Broad coverage: 16 models across frontier, reasoning, and four open-weight families.
  • Diagnosis, not leaderboard: early ≤64% / late <1% pinpoints over-defensiveness as the dominant failure.

Limitations

  • Ground-truth circularity: GPT-4o annotates, DeepSeek-R1 synthesizes — capped at GPT-4o’s own risk judgment.
  • Rigid template: normal → signal → trigger always holds; no benign-only runs to price false alarms.
  • Fragile cells: Procedural Safety Gap n=43; one trajectory swings tens of points.
  • Zero-shot only: no fine-tuned guard baseline — the 40.74% ceiling may be prompt insensitivity, not a fundamental gap.
  • Text-only, English-only: physical hazards test reading comprehension as much as safety reasoning.

Questions for further exploration

  1. Would a guard model fine-tuned on OIW labels break the 40.74% zero-shot ceiling — and kill the lexical collapse?
  2. Does the open-vs-closed awakening gap survive rate-matched baseline Early rates — reasoning deficit or over-trigger artifact?
  3. What false-interruption cost does up-to-64% earliness impose on benign-only workflows?
  4. Do the anchors survive independent human re-annotation or real agent incident logs (circularity check)?
  5. Do findings transfer to multimodal settings where risk signals are observations, not text?
