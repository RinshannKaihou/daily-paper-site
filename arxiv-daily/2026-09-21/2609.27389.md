> **一句话总结：** EvoAudio 构建了首个音频领域的闭环自进化系统——当前音频语言模型（solver）的 47 项技能画像由 DeepSeek-V4-Flash 提案器读取并规划下一轮课程，24 种音频工具据此合成"答案由构造过程本身决定"的可验证问答（再经声学复测与文本捷径过滤），GRPO 训练候选模型并仅在固定 2,500 题验证集上胜过父模型才晋升；13 轮进化使 5 个不同骨干模型在 MMSU/MMAU-Pro/MMAR 平均分全面提升，其中 Kimi-Audio-7B 从 53.3 涨到 59.6（+6.3），Qwen2.5-Omni-7B 的 MMSU 感知子项从 44.5 涨到 61.5（+17.0）。

> **TL;DR:** EvoAudio is the first audio system that recursively evolves the model, waveforms, questions, and difficulty in one closed loop: an LLM proposer reads the current solver's 47-skill profile, a deterministic harness composes verifiable QA from 24 audio tools (answers fixed by construction, separately re-verified on the rendered audio, text-shortcut filtered), and GRPO trains a candidate that is promoted only if it beats its parent on a fixed 2,500-question held-out set. Over 13 rounds it raises Qwen2.5-Omni-7B from 59.3 to 65.4 average (+6.1) on MMSU/MMAU-Pro/MMAR — above matched-budget Static-profile GRPO (62.8, +3.5) and Pooled GRPO (62.1, +2.8) — with the biggest jump on Kimi-Audio-7B (53.3 → 59.6, +6.3).

## 核心贡献

- **首个音频闭环自进化系统：** 同时进化模型、波形、问题、难度四要素。与 Absolute Zero（代码任务有执行器验证）不同，音频没有执行器，EvoAudio 把"证据"直接构造进波形，再在渲染后复核——答案永远来自构造参数或数据集原生标签，绝不来自模型标注。
- **求解器自适应课程：** 提案器（DeepSeek-V4-Flash）按 47 项技能给出优先级 ρ，确定性 harness 用"混合奖励率" m 重加权并加帽（q ∝ min(ρ·m, 0.06)），保留 12% 预算给已掌握技能防遗忘；对音高间隔、信噪比等连续声学控制量，按目标成功率 p*=0.5 自动调节难度。
- **可验证性两分离：** 验证器 v 只判回答对错；独立的声学校验复测 F0、语速、响度、onset、节奏，丢弃线索太弱的条目，并用 Whisper 核对合成语音与脚本；再用"纯文本能否答对"探针剔除不需要音频的捷径题。
- **晋升机制：** 每轮存 32/64 步两个候选，在无梯度的固定 2,500 题 held-out 集上与父模型比较，胜者成为下一代 solver 并重塑下一轮课程；败者双弃、父模型留任。
- **跨骨干普适性：** 在 Qwen2.5-Omni、Audio Flamingo 3、Kimi-Audio、MiMo-Audio、MiniCPM-o 4.5 五个不同音频编码器 + LLM 骨干组合上均取得最高平均分，并在同预算对照（Static-profile GRPO、Pooled GRPO、EvoAudio-SFT）下保持一致排序。

## Key Contributions

- First closed-loop recursive self-improvement system for audio understanding that jointly evolves the model, waveforms, questions, and difficulty — audio's analogue of Absolute Zero, where the "executor check" is replaced by constructing the evidence into the waveform and re-verifying it after rendering.
- A solver-adaptive curriculum: an LLM proposer assigns per-skill priorities over 47 skills, a deterministic harness reweights them by reward-variance ("mixed rate"), caps any single skill at 0.06, reserves 12% of the budget for mastered skills (anti-forgetting), and moves continuous acoustic controls (pitch interval, SNR) toward a 0.5 target success rate.
- A two-part verifiability guarantee: the verifier only scores responses, while a separate acoustic check re-measures pitch/rate/loudness/onsets/tempo on the rendered audio and uses Whisper ASR to confirm synthesized speech matches its script; a text-only probe drops questions answerable without audio.
- A promotion gate: each round saves candidates at 32 and 64 GRPO steps and promotes only the one that beats the parent θ_t on a fixed, gradient-free 2,500-question held-out set (sharing no template, source recording, or seed with training items), so the improved solver reshapes the next curriculum.
- Broad empirical validation: highest average on every one of five LALMs (different audio encoders and LLM backbones), under matched optimization budgets against Static-profile GRPO, Pooled GRPO, and an SFT variant of the same curricula.

## Methodology

**Loop structure.** EvoAudio maintains a lineage θ0, θ1, …. Round t profiles the current solver πθt on 47 skills using a fixed held-out set (accuracies p_t,k and deltas Δ_t,k stay comparable across rounds), computes the mixed rate m_t,k from training (share of items whose 16 sampled responses are not all scored alike), and correlates Δ_t,k across skills to detect stalled ones. A DeepSeek-V4-Flash proposer reads this evidence and returns a per-skill priority ρ_t,k plus an easier/harder recommendation.

**Curriculum compiler.** Priorities are reweighted by the mixed rate and capped: q_t,k ∝ min(ρ_t,k·m_t,k, 0.06), normalized to sum 1. The 0.06 cap prevents one skill from dominating a round; 12% of the budget stays with mastered skills to reduce forgetting; stalled skills that no longer co-move with others give up quota. For skills with a continuous acoustic control c (e.g., pitch ratio, SNR), the recommendation becomes a control update c_t+1,k = clip(c_t,k + η(p̂_t,k − p⋆), c_min, c_max) toward target success rate p⋆ = 0.5, estimated from pilot rollouts, with bounds kept inside what the tools render reliably.

**Verifiable item construction.** Each item is a triple x = (a, q, v). A library of 24 tools spanning 6 families (speech prosody 9 types, speakers/dialogue 7, speech content 4, sound events 12, music 10, multi-clip/long scene 5) builds the waveform: Qwen3-TTS + time-stretch/pitch-shift/gain for prosody, multilingual TTS and voice selection for speaker identity, scripted TTS plus LibriSpeech/MELD for content, FSD50K/AudioSet with procedural overlay/mixing for sound events, MIDI/FluidSynth/LeVo for music, and concatenation/reverb/codecs for long scenes. Example: a pitch-comparison item renders one sentence with Qwen3-TTS, resamples a copy by a drawn ratio r, and concatenates — the answer is fixed by the sign of log r and nothing else. Inherited-label items keep their native dataset labels. Verification is split: (1) the verifier v scores responses against the recorded answer; (2) an acoustic faithfulness check re-measures the intended cue on the rendered audio (pitch, rate, loudness, onsets, tempo), discards items whose cue is too weak, and runs Whisper to check synthesized speech against its script; (3) a text-only probe drops questions the solver can answer without audio. Surviving candidates get 4 solver rollouts; only items answered correctly in 1–3 of them are kept (uniform reward groups give no GRPO advantage signal).

**Training and promotion.** GRPO trains on the round's curriculum C_t with rewards +1/0/−1 for correct/wrong/malformed, group-normalized; temperature 0.9, KL coefficient 0.02 against the parent, constant lr 1e-6, fresh optimizer state per round. Two candidates per round (32 and 64 steps; 16 prompts × 16 responses per step) are scored on the fixed 2,500-question held-out set; the better one replaces the parent only if it outscores it, otherwise both are dropped. The full evolution runs 13 rounds; each run trains all audio+language parameters (visual modules frozen) on 4 nodes × 8 H800 GPUs.

## Results & Highlights

- **Headline gains (Table 2, three-benchmark Avg):** On MMSU + MMAU-Pro + MMAR average, EvoAudio (GRPO) lifts Qwen2.5-Omni-7B from 59.3 (Base) to 65.4 (+6.1), beating both matched-budget baselines: Static-profile GRPO 62.8 (+3.5) and Pooled GRPO 62.1 (+2.8) — a +2.6-point edge over the strongest baseline that isolates the value of the model-adaptive profile/quotas/difficulty. EvoAudio (SFT) on identical curricula reaches 63.8 (+4.5), so GRPO adds +1.6 over SFT.
- **Largest backbone gain:** Kimi-Audio-7B-Instruct goes 53.3 → 59.6 (+6.3) vs Static-profile GRPO 58.1 (+4.8) and Pooled GRPO 55.8 (+2.5); MiMo-Audio-7B 58.8 → 64.2 (+5.4); Audio Flamingo 3 58.4 → 62.7 (+4.3); MiniCPM-o 4.5 (9B) only 64.5 → 65.9 (+1.4) with regressions (MMAU-Pro All 65.3 → 65.0; MMAR Semantic 63.4 → 60.0).
- **Where the gains land:** MMSU improves most — averaged across backbones +6.1 points from Base to attempt 13, vs +5.0 on MMAU-Pro and +3.0 on MMAR; on Qwen2.5-Omni, MMSU All jumps 61.4 → 71.9 with the Perception sub-score 44.5 → 61.5 (+17.0). The ordering tracks the tool library's reach (rate/pitch/loudness/event structure vs. semantic/cultural reasoning).
- **Reward-variance ablation (Table 3, Qwen2.5-Omni-7B, Base 59.3):** removing acoustic verification raises the mixed-reward rate from 53.8% to 57.2% but *lowers* accuracy 65.4 → 64.1 — more reward variance is not better when the variance points at unfaithful labels. Freezing difficulty at round one costs 2.1 points (65.4 → 63.3, Var 40.7); keeping every verified question → 63.6 (Var 34.5); uniform skill quotas → 63.7; no retention floor → 64.9.
- **Curriculum freshness matters:** in the final round only 30.8% of Pooled GRPO's questions produce mixed rewards vs 53.8% for EvoAudio — questions written for earlier, weaker solvers fall outside the learning zone and yield no GRPO signal.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty is real but bounded.** The recursive loop (model shapes its successor's curriculum) is genuinely new for audio, and the construction-based verifiability is a clean answer to audio's lack of an Absolute-Zero-style executor. However, each ingredient exists separately: AudioSkills/SARI pre-set skill/difficulty structures, AudioRubrics/AQA-TTRL adapt supervision to model responses, and automatic-curriculum-by-learning-progress dates to Matiisen et al. (2019). The contribution is the integration and the acoustic-faithfulness check, not any single mechanism.
- **The promotion signal is synthetic.** The 2,500-question held-out set shares no template/seed with training items but comes from the same generation pipeline — it certifies improvement on the synthetic distribution, not on real audio. The authors candidly note "a candidate that gains on the held-out set does not always gain on the benchmarks," which means the promotion gate optimizes a proxy that can diverge from the target; no benchmark-aware selection is tried (understandable, to avoid contamination, but the gap is unquantified).
- **Gains concentrate where tools mirror benchmark skills.** The +17.0 MMSU Perception jump on Qwen2.5-Omni sits exactly where the tools directly control rate/pitch/loudness/event structure; MMAR's semantic and cultural reasoning — which the tools cannot construct — moves only +3.0. This is skill-targeted tutoring more than general audio-intelligence gain, and the paper is honest about it, but claims of broad "audio understanding" improvement should be read through that lens.
- **No error bars, single run per cell.** Each of the ~20 configurations in Table 2 appears to be a single run with no seed variance reported; on MiniCPM-o the headline gain is +1.4 with a −3.4 regression on MMAR Semantic, so run-to-run noise (notoriously non-trivial for GRPO at 64 steps/round) is not ruled out for the smaller deltas.
- **Cost is substantial and unstated in total.** 4 nodes × 8 H800 per run × 13 rounds × 5 backbones × 4 methods — the compute budget dwarfs the data savings from avoiding human annotation, and the paper never totals it.
- **The proposer is not fully ablated.** Uniform skill quotas tests quota allocation, but there is no random-priority or heuristic-only-proposer arm isolating how much DeepSeek-V4-Flash's planning adds beyond the deterministic learnability machinery — the strongest ablation ("difficulty fixed") freezes difficulty but keeps proposer priorities.

## Strengths

- Fair, budget-matched baselines: both GRPO baselines get the identical 13 rounds × 64 steps × 16×16 budget; the SFT variant keeps the exact same curricula, isolating the update rule cleanly.
- Verifiability engineered end-to-end: construction-fixed answers, separate acoustic faithfulness re-measurement, Whisper script checks, and text-shortcut filtering together attack label noise and shortcut learning from orthogonal directions.
- Consistent ordering across five architecturally diverse backbones — the method transfers rather than exploiting one model's quirks.
- The ablation table yields a genuinely transferable insight: reward variance is necessary but not sufficient (57.2% Var with acoustic verification removed *hurts* accuracy), because variance toward unfaithful answers is poison for RL.
- Honest failure analysis: flat late-round curves are explained (fewer learnable errors; zero-contrast skills unreachable by RL alone), and an SFT warm start is named as the fix rather than hidden.

## Limitations

- Diminishing returns: the largest jump is attempt 1 for four of five backbones and curves are nearly flat by round 13 — the loop's marginal value decays quickly.
- Ceiling set by the tool library: skills the solver never answers correctly produce no reward contrast and cannot be opened by RL; semantic/cultural reasoning is out of reach of the current 24 tools (MMAR +3.0 vs MMSU +6.1).
- Synthetic-domain training throughout: all waveforms are TTS/procedurally built; the synthetic-to-real transfer gap is never directly measured (no held-out real-recording evaluation beyond the three benchmarks).
- Uneven benefit: MiniCPM-o 4.5 gains only +1.4 with regressions on MMAU-Pro All and MMAR Semantic, so the method is not uniformly safe.
- Compute-heavy: 32 H800 GPUs per run across 13 rounds, multiplied by five backbones and four method variants, with no total-cost accounting.
- Single-seed results with no variance estimates; the LLM proposer's marginal contribution over pure heuristics is not isolated.

## Relevance

这篇论文是"可验证奖励 + 自适应课程 + 递归自我改进"这条线（Absolute Zero、TTRL 一族）在音频模态上最完整的落地，其"奖励方差有用但必须指向忠实标签"的消融发现对所有模态的 RL 自进化都有借鉴价值。如果你关心 LLM 自我改进、RLVR 或多模态感知训练，方法框架和负结果分析都值得精读；若只关心音频感知本身，结论部分（工具覆盖决定增益上限）最具启发。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would an SFT warm start (on construction-recorded answers) followed by GRPO unlock the zero-reward-contrast skills that pure RL cannot open — the authors' own stated next step?
- How large is the synthetic-to-real gap: does the loop transfer if the curriculum mixes in unmodified real recordings (LibriSpeech/FSD50K clips) alongside synthesized ones?
- Can the tool library be extended toward the semantic and cultural reasoning that MMAR tests, where gains were smallest (+3.0 vs +6.1 on MMSU), or is that fundamentally outside construction-based verifiability?
- How much does the DeepSeek-V4-Flash proposer actually contribute compared with a purely heuristic learning-progress curriculum (the uniform-quota ablation only partially answers this)?
- Does the loop keep improving beyond 13 rounds, or does the promotion gate start rejecting all candidates once easy variance is exhausted?
- Since synthetic held-out gains diverge from benchmark gains, would a small, contamination-controlled real-audio validation probe make promotion decisions more predictive?
