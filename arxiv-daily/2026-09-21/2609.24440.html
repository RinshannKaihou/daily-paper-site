Why this matters

First token-localizable, feature-level evidence that recurrence preserves semantic universality — SSMs pay for sub-quadratic processing only at rigid syntactic margins.

递归瓶颈未破坏语义本体论，代价只出现在句法边缘 —— 对机制可解释性与 SSM/Transformer 表征差异研究很有参考价值。

Critical edge

The 99.98% headline rests on a post-hoc J > 0.35 cutoff with no null baseline — and even the best-matched twin pair disagrees on ~59% of its token-level activation support.

— Critical Assessment; authors concede thresholds are "empirically derived, not theoretically motivated".

01 Methodology

  1. Two parameter-comparable models. Mamba-130m (24L, blocks.12.hook_resid_post) vs Pythia-70m-deduped (6L, blocks.3) — both GPT-NeoX tokenized over 10M tokens of English Wikipedia in 1,024-token chunks, harvested at the depth midpoint.
  2. Two SAEs, asymmetric by necessity. Pythia: public SAELens SAE, expansion 64 → 32,768 features. Mamba: custom L1-sparse SAE, expansion 16 → 12,288 features (no public equivalent exists).
  3. Binarize. Any activation > 0 → True, giving two sparse binary masks per token over the full corpus.
  4. All-pairs max-Jaccard twinning. 12,288 × 32,768 masks over 10⁷ tokens; naive MᵀP exceeds 24 GB VRAM → disk-streaming + 512-column slabs + bit arrays + float32 accumulators. Each Mamba feature's global argmax is its "Twin".
  5. Post-hoc bands + Twin Test. Aligned J>0.35 · Grey Matter 0.28–0.35 · buffer 0.25–0.28 · Alien <0.25; qualitative context comparison of the most divergent pair (4454 vs 4309).

⚠ Flagged by the authors themselves: asymmetric expansion factors (16 vs 64) and descriptive (not theoretical) thresholds are open confounds.

03 Strengths

  • Clean question, sharp tool. Asks whether recurrence changes what is represented, with a Jaccard metric that localizes divergence to specific tokens — unlike RSA/CKA/MPPC.
  • Calibrated claim strength. Confounds named, rebuttal labeled qualitative, polysemanticity demoted to "hypothesis-generating".
  • Real engineering. Disk-streaming Jaccard makes a 12,288 × 32,768 × 10⁷-token intersection run on commodity GPUs; custom Mamba SAE fills a tooling gap.
  • Falsifiable specifics. Named features, numeric strengths, precise firing contexts, and concrete follow-ups stated by the authors.
  • Architecture-linked phenomenon. The landmark / state-reset framing connects to the Platonic Representation Hypothesis.

04 Limitations

  • Tiny scope. Two sub-100M models, one mid-layer each, single English-Wikipedia corpus — whose rigid formatting generates the "landmark" findings.
  • Asymmetric SAE dictionaries (12,288 vs 32,768; expansion 16 vs 64) confound both the alignment rate and the polysemanticity claim.
  • No null distribution or permutation baseline; Aligned / Grey Matter / Alien bands are post-hoc partitions of one observed distribution.
  • n=1 Twin Test, purely correlational — no ablation or steering verifies the "state reset" mechanism.
  • No code or checkpoint released, undercutting the "fully reproducible framework" claim in practice.

05 Questions for further exploration

  • Would symmetric SAEs (same expansion, same dictionary size) eliminate — or confirm — the 4454-vs-4309 monosemanticity asymmetry?
  • What does a random-feature-pair null Jaccard look like on this corpus, and does the J > 0.35 Aligned threshold survive it?
  • Does 99.98% alignment hold at Mamba-790m / Pythia-410m scale, at other depths, or on corpora without Wikipedia's rigid formatting?
  • Can ablating or amplifying Features 6888 / 7378 demonstrate an actual "state reset" effect on downstream predictions at document boundaries?
  • Do landmark / junk-drawer phenomena appear with Mamba-2 or hybrid architectures, or when the two models use different tokenizers?
