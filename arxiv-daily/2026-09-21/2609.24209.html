§01 Methodology

Seven deliberately minimal moves — so any shared structure found cannot be credited to alignment machinery.

  1. 44 encoders

    25 ViTs (supervised / CLIP / DINO / MAE / SigLIP × B/L/H) + 19 LLMs (Llama, Gemma, Mistral, OLMo, Bloomz); 10k Flickr30k pairs; concepts from ImageNet-22k ∩ CommonWord79k with WordNet relations.

  2. Whiten + PCA-500

    Center, per-model whitening, PCA to r=500 (stable at 20/100) — minimal pipeline by design.

  3. One rigid alignment per pair

    Orthogonal Procrustes (rotation + reflection) fit on a held-out split; no scaling, no learned mapping; the same rotation serves all metrics.

  4. Three cosine metrics

    Pointwise (positions) vs Cross-Object (architecture floor: 0.87–0.96 even at random init) vs Cross-Model (displacement directions), with random-init nulls.

  5. Consensus decomposition

    Label-free eigendecomposition of the cross-model consensus matrix Scross → shared (linearly aligned) vs private (nonlinearly woven) subspaces.

  6. Causal test by objective

    Fine-tune vs RKD vs FKD, from-CLIP and from-scratch, tracking both similarities per epoch.

  7. ShadowCLIP

    Frozen CLIP + zero-init gated residual modules (DINO feature injection; LLM2Vec-Qwen3-4B relational distillation), CC12M, 3 epochs, 0.5 h on 8 GPUs.

§03 Strengths

  • One decisive formal move (a kernel on displacement pairs) reconciles two opposed 2024–2026 literatures — no new benchmark needed.
  • Exemplary hygiene: held-out alignment fitting, one shared rotation for all metrics, random-init nulls, Wilcoxon + BH correction.
  • Genuinely falsifiable: the §4.5 objective-comparison predictions were confirmed in both from-CLIP and from-scratch regimes.
  • Theory-to-practice arc: ablations fail exactly where the geometry predicts (linear-only adaptation can’t import nonlinear capability).
  • Candid, specific limitations section; full replication code released.

§04 Limitations

  • No frontier-scale models — whether displacement parallelism strengthens or degrades with scale is open.
  • Headline is concept-level; sample-level V–L Cross-Model is only 0.31 (vs pointwise 0.19).
  • Uniqueness open: this geometry may be one of several equally viable consensus modes (“Anna Karenina” scenario).
  • Concepts restricted to nominal WordNet/ImageNet kinds; relations, events, non-English lexicons untested.
  • “Independently trained” models share pretraining corpora — shared data vs shared world structure is inseparable here.
  • ShadowCLIP is a proof of concept: CC12M-scale, CC12M-trained baselines only; trails SAIL-NV2 on fine-grained and T2I.

§05 Questions for further exploration

  1. Does displacement parallelism hold, strengthen, or degrade at frontier scale — the axis where the pointwise counter-evidence emerged?
  2. Is the consensus directional geometry unique among high-performing models, or one of several viable modes — and can models be steered between them?
  3. How much capability can one cached “shadow” forward pass carry, and how entangled is it with the shared semantics?
  4. How sensitive are the headline numbers to the whitening + PCA-500 + rigid-rotation pipeline? No no-whitening ablation is reported.
  5. Do displacement directions for relations, events, or abstract discourse align — or is the shared geometry limited to object kinds?
  6. Is the SAIL-NV2 fine-grained gap (Aircraft 37.5 vs 60.4) a structural limit of residual modules over a frozen CLIP backbone?
