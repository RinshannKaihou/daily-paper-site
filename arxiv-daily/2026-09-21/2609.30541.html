AutoResearch at Production Scale: Failure Modes and a Multi-Agent Framework

Aparajith Chandran et al. · Amazon Books Core Recs Science 2026-09-21 · ICDM 2026 Applied Track
LLM-Agents AutoResearch Multi-Agent-Systems Recommender-Systems Production-ML

12 weeks 220+ experiments 2 production systems ~760× per-iteration cost gap

Key metric

6.90%

Recall@6 — 1.82× the 3.79% hand-tuned baseline (four engineer-weeks), 2.45× the 2.82% deployed retrieval baseline.

Config-level AutoResearch: 3.73% (0.98×) — never surpassed the human baseline.

Why this matters

First insider evidence of what breaks when AutoResearch hits production cost and scale — a borrowable blueprint for self-evolving research loops.

Critical edge

The 1.82× headline rests on an observational ablation and offline Recall@6 never validated against real customer outcomes.

Online pilots were still "underway" at writing time.

01 Methodology

Setup — a natural cost experiment

  • System A: multi-GPU embedding training for 98% of Amazon's active book catalog; 9–19 h/iteration; code-level search; metric = Recall@6 (FAISS IVF+Flat vs actual next purchase).
  • System B: single-GPU RQ-VAE semantic IDs for generative retrieval; 6–52 min/iteration; config-level search; metric = weighted coherence (L1·0.5 + L2·0.2 + L3·0.3).

Three agents around program.md + immutable evaluator

  • 1 · RESEARCHER (claude-sonnet-4-6) — writes a complete, self-contained training script each iteration; full freedom in architecture, loss, optimization.
  • 2 · CODE FIXER (claude-opus-4-6) — one pre-execution rewrite pass; catches semantic bugs static checks miss (DDP misconfig, OOM precursors, wasted recompute).
  • 3 · CRITICIZER (claude-opus-4-6) — fires only when best gain < 8% over a 5-iteration window; emits a 2–5 sentence reframing directive, never code. Auto-revert on OOM-class failures.

Validation & evidence

  • Re-evaluated on 11 distinct purchase dates; agents cannot read or modify evaluation code; System B re-tests cheap-run decisions at 10M scale before locking.
  • Component evidence is observational — natural campaign transitions, not a randomized A/B.

Infrastructure fragility

Broken runs and environment failures waste whole expensive iterations.

"100 epochs" failed on overfitting at least six separate times.

5+consecutive iterations

Search-direction stagnation

The loop circles inside noise with no structural reframe.

15 htreated like a 30-min ablation

Iteration-cost asymmetry

Cheap-iteration habits applied to multi-GPU runs.

150+iters, criteria never jointly met

Metric fixation

Codebook size gamed the metric; hierarchy collapsed to singletons (B only).

4 of 5 modes appeared in both systems despite independent development and a ~760× cost gap — structural, not incidental. Mitigation: prevent (Code Fixer) · redirect (Criticizer) · persist (durable memory + auto-revert) — weighted by iteration cost.

04 Strengths

  • Rare empirical territory: the first account of what breaks when AutoResearch moves from 5-minute loops to 19-hour production jobs, spanning ~760× per-iteration cost.
  • Honest negatives reported plainly: config-AR at 0.98×, System B's Phase-3 regression, the abandoned reviewer design, unsolved metric fixation.
  • Actionable heuristic: "prevent when iterations are expensive, react when cheap" transfers to any long-horizon agent loop.
  • Auditable anecdotes: 14 categorized bugs, the verbatim Criticizer directive with its +50 bps response, the 5.8× coverage story.
  • Validation hygiene: immutable evaluator prevents score inflation; ±0.28pp across-date variance quantified; decisions re-tested at full scale.

05 Limitations

  • No controlled ablation — the 1.10× → 1.45× → 1.82× ladder comes from sequential campaign transitions; accumulated-iteration effects can't be excluded.
  • N = 2 systems, one domain, one organization — the cost principle is a two-point empirical observation framed like a general law.
  • Offline proxies only — neither Recall@6 nor coherence validated against customer outcomes; production significance unproven.
  • Autonomy claim weakens in the multi-criteria regime — System B's two best phases were human-triggered pivots.
  • Proprietary data and untuned thresholds — 8% / 5-iteration gates chosen empirically; no sensitivity analysis, no failed-directive reporting.

06 Questions for further exploration

  • Where is the crossover iteration cost at which pre-execution gates stop paying for themselves — and can it be predicted from failure-mode incidence rates?
  • Can a meta-level Criticizer audit metric validity rather than search direction, breaking metric fixation — or would it just add noise?
  • What is the base rate of Criticizer directives that fail or actively hurt, and how do you tune the 8% / 5-iteration trigger without controlled ablations?
  • Do the five failure modes reproduce outside GPU-heavy recommender settings (LLM fine-tuning, RL loops) at the same relative incidence?
