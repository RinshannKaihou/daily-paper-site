> **一句话总结：** 提出 CELLAUDIT 框架，把 LLM 智能体"发现"的虚拟细胞预测器所登记的输入使用声明拆成三个不等价的审计问题（源代码是否真的消费该输入、拟合预测是否依赖它、该依赖是否提升对观测响应的预测）：在 BBBCC047 形态学-转录组联合扰动基准上，得分选出的预测器 held-out Global PCC 达 0.3153，却对化合物替换精确不变（ΔPCC=0.0000），仅比"只用控制轮廓"的基线高 +0.0011（95% CI [−0.0005, +0.0027]）；48 个候选中 47 个存在拟合依赖、但只有 20 个在两个 held-out 折上有置信区间完全大于零的目标损失增益；以证伪为引导的修订（falsification-guided revision）在恢复化合物贡献（因子分解效应 0.0053/0.0048）的同时保持对控制基线的预测优势（+0.0037/+0.0013）。

> **TL;DR:** CELLAUDIT is a falsification layer for agentic model discovery: it traces each registered input-use claim through three non-equivalent tests — source consumption (AST-level check that the cited computation actually uses the input), fitted dependence (prediction/loss change under input replacement), and target-relevant predictive contribution. On the paired BBBCC036/BBBC047 morphology+transcriptomics perturbation benchmark, the score-selected BBBCC047 predictor attains held-out Global PCC 0.3153 yet is exactly invariant to compound replacement in every refit, beating a control-profile-only baseline by just +0.0011 (95% CI [−0.0005, +0.0027]); source inspection shows its cited compound-conditioned cross-attention has one key and one value, so the compound query cannot move the constant normalized attention weight. In a stratified audit of 48 agent-generated candidates, 47 depend on the compound but only 20 have target-loss-gain intervals entirely above zero on both folds. Falsification-guided residual revision recovers positive compound contributions (mean factorial effects 0.0053/0.0048) while exceeding the control-only baseline by +0.0037/+0.0013 PCC; on matched sci-Plex searches, audit-enriched feedback cuts MSE by 9.58%/6.10% versus score-only feedback, though paired intervals span zero.

## 核心贡献

- **三问式审计框架**：将"模型是否使用了登记输入"分解为三个逻辑独立的问题——源代码消费（引用的计算里是否真的有该输入）、拟合依赖（替换输入后预测/损失是否变化）、目标相关贡献（这种依赖是否改善对真实响应的预测），并给出对应的确定性检测手段（AST 源码检查、输入替换、因子分解效应 + 多层不确定性区间）。
- **发现"预测-声明鸿沟"的具体实例**：在 BBBCC047 上，智能体选出的高分预测器对化合物替换完全不变——根因是被引用的 compound-conditioned cross-attention 只有一个 key 和一个 value，归一化注意力权重恒定，化合物 query 无法传递任何信息；用物理不相交（disjoint）的控制孔重建数据后该失配依然存在。
- **大规模候选审计**：对 48 个生成的候选做分层审计：47 个在两个 held-out 折上预测随化合物变化（拟合依赖普遍存在），但仅 20 个的 target-loss 增益区间在两折上都完全大于零（依赖≠有用）。
- **证伪引导的修订**：不满足审计的候选不必丢弃——在固定的控制轮廓预测器 g(c) 上加残差分支 ŷ = g(c) + αh(c,p,a)（编译器强制参考点处残差为零），使修订后的模型同时具备化合物贡献和预测增益（比 control-only 高 +0.0037/+0.0013 PCC）。
- **预测泛化 ≠ 声明泛化**：把 LINCS-Pilot1 上冻结的模型设计迁移到独立采集的 LKCP Batch 2 重新拟合：预测增益保持（+0.0104/+0.0211），剂量贡献保持，但化合物身份贡献从 +0.0175/+0.0126 坍缩到 −0.000070/+0.0033——输入使用声明不能跨数据采集继承。
- **审计反馈进入搜索回路**：在 sci-Plex 匹配搜索中，把审计测量结果注入智能体提示（audit-enriched feedback），平均 held-out MSE 比 score-only 反馈低 9.58%（Fold 4）/6.10%（Fold 5），化合物与剂量贡献均值更大，但配对区间跨零。

## Key Contributions

- A three-question audit framework (source consumption → fitted dependence → target-relevant contribution) for registered input-use claims in agent-discovered predictors, with deterministic checks: AST-level source verification, input-replacement invariance/loss-gain tests, and factorial effect decomposition with paired-seed and source-scaffold uncertainty intervals.
- A concrete prediction–claim gap: the score-selected BBBCC047 predictor (held-out Global PCC 0.3153) is exactly compound-invariant because its cited compound-conditioned cross-attention has a singleton key-value pair; the mismatch persists under disjoint control wells for both inputs and target references.
- A stratified audit of 48 generated candidates: 47 show fitted compound dependence on both held-out folds, but only 20 have registered target-loss-gain intervals entirely above zero on both folds — dependence and useful contribution are distinct at scale.
- Falsification-guided revision: an executably-compiled residual over a control-profile-only model recovers positive compound contributions (0.0053/0.0048 factorial effects) while retaining predictive gains (+0.0037/+0.0013 PCC over the control-only baseline), moving discovery from "generate→score→revise" to "discover→falsify→revise".
- Claim-vs-predictive generalization separation via independent acquisition: LINCS-frozen designs refit on LKCP keep predictive and dose-use support but lose compound-identity support (35/50 → 10/50 refits passing on Fold 4 across folds; 0/50 at either LKCP boundary).
- Audit-enriched feedback in the agent loop: on matched sci-Plex searches it improves mean held-out prediction (MSE 9.58%/6.10% lower than score-only) and mean compound/dose contributions, with paired intervals honestly reported as spanning zero.

## Methodology

**Problem setup.** Each example has biological context c, perturbation representation p, optional attributes a (e.g., dose), and response y. An agentic search (the CELLSCIENTIST policy, plus AIDE/CellForge/HarmonyCell for comparison) proposes executable predictors within registered permissions and records input-use claims. A prediction–claim gap occurs when the cited source contradicts the claim or fitted behavior is invariant to the input.

**Three audit tests.**
1. *Source consumption*: an abstract-syntax-tree checker verifies that the computation attached to each input-use statement actually resolves the cited locations, classifying candidates as `implemented`, `implementation-contradicted`, or `unresolved` (coverage = fraction fully resolved).
2. *Fitted dependence*: with checkpoint and targets fixed, replace one registered input x_q; prediction distance Δm_q = m(f(x),y) − m(f(x_{−q}, x̃_q), y) and loss gain ΔL_q measure whether predictions depend on the input; exact zero indicates invariance.
3. *Target-relevant contribution*: continuous contributions are averaged factorially over the other input's two states (a 2×2 compound×context audit; Lundberg & Lee transfer analysis separates compound and dose allocations). Uncertainty uses paired training-seed-by-trajectory intervals, fixed-model source-group resampling, and separate fixed-dataset vs population quantiles; known-truth checks run on 6,400 simulated datasets showing map-only intervals undercover population effects while source-group intervals improve coverage.

**Develop–freeze–test discipline.** Data processing, budgets, evaluation and selection rules are fixed before search. Folds 1–2 fit models; Fold 3 provides feedback and selects sources/checkpoints; Folds 4–5 are held out. Source, checkpoint and audit rules are frozen before held-out evaluation. The BBBCC047 control-separation experiment rebuilds shared vs physically disjoint control wells (bank A/B hash-sorted within plate/well-row strata, 60 checkpoint evaluations, all fixed before testing).

**Falsification-guided revision.** A contradicted source path motivates an executably-compiled route; a weak target contribution motivates residual learning: ŷ = g(c) + αh(c, p, a), where g(c) is fitted on Folds 1–2 and frozen and the compiler enforces centered perturbation/dose branches (zero residual at the reference input). BBBCC047 selects by predictive score among qualifying candidates; BBBCC036 prioritizes compound loss gain among non-inferior candidates; each trajectory evaluates anchor plus nine proposals, freezing before Folds 4–5.

**Tasks.** (1) Linked BBBCC036 (known-bioactive subset) and parent BBBCC047 paired morphology+transcriptomics cohorts, Benjamini–Murcko scaffold folds holding out compound families, Global PCC on concatenated responses; discovery-score comparison across CellScientist, AIDE, CellForge, HarmonyCell (ten trajectories per task). (2) sci-Plex3: 2,000-gene well-level responses, holding out cell-line:compound combinations; five paired trajectories per condition with a shared anchor. (3) LINCS-Pilot1 → LKCP Batch 2 transfer of ten frozen designs to an independently acquired cohort. (4) Norman CRISPRa: 5,045-gene responses for unseen double-perturbation combinations with multi-hot pair identity.

## Results & Highlights

- **Compound invariance in a high scorer (BBBCC047).** The score-selected predictor has a source-selection score of 0.3035 and mean held-out Global PCC 0.3153 on replication Fold 5 (0.3035 on Fold 4), yet compound switching changes PCC by 0.0000 in every refit while control replacement lowers PCC by 0.1932 (Fold 4) and 0.2014 (Fold 5). A control-profile-only predictor reaches 0.3142 on Fold 5, so Full − control-only is just +0.0011 (95% CI [−0.0005, +0.0027]) — the headline model adds almost nothing beyond control profiles.
- **Dependence ≠ contribution across 48 candidates.** In the stratified audit, 47 of 48 sources change predictions under compound replacement on both held-out folds (one implemented multi-tower source is exactly invariant), but only 20 have positive target-loss scaffold intervals on both folds; the compound-only control shuffle effect is 0.0111 ± 0.0010 with all five refits above the reference threshold, showing the diagnostic has power.
- **Falsification-guided revision restores compound use with predictive gains.** On BBBCC047 the full predictor exceeds the control-only baseline by +0.0037 (Fold 4) and +0.0013 (Fold 5) with mean factorial compound effects of 0.0053 and 0.0048; the explicit path-constrained route yields even larger compound effects (0.0083/0.0074) but lower predictive scores (0.2822/0.2923 vs 0.3060/0.3173), and none of its ten models passes the reference-relative criterion. Observed-support replacements (3341/4380 and 2927/3878 rows) give compound PCC drops of 0.00534/0.00404 with all-positive source-scaffold intervals.
- **Audit-enriched feedback on sci-Plex.** MSE is 9.58% lower than Score on Fold 4 and 6.10% lower on Fold 5; Audit−Score PCC is +0.0024 (95% CI [−0.0013, +0.0060]) and +0.0015 ([−0.0005, +0.0034]) — paired intervals include zero. Compound target-loss gains rise from 0.0186 to 0.0220 (Fold 4) and 0.0064 to 0.0079 (Fold 5); the cost is 592,347 vs ≥286,995 reported tokens (38 vs 36 logical model calls).
- **Claim generalization fails under independent acquisition.** LINCS-frozen designs refit on LKCP improve over the control-only baseline by +0.0104 (95% CI [+0.0103, +0.0106]) and +0.0211 ([+0.0205, +0.0217]); dose-replacement Sharpe-allocation persists (0.0486→+0.0237 on Fold 4; 0.0467→+0.0341 on Fold 5), but compound-identity allocation collapses from +0.0175/+0.0126 (LINCS) to −0.000070/+0.0033 (LKCP), and 0 of 50 refit endpoints pass the compound-identity criterion at either LKCP boundary (LINCS itself flips from 35/50 passing on Fold 4 to 10/50 on Fold 5).
- **Pair-identity use on Norman CRISPRa.** All five refits pass the registered pair-input-bundle criterion; shuffling the multi-hot pair identity drops PCC to 0.5378/0.6280, and mean PCC exceeds the h0 anchor by +0.0060/+0.0045 and the observed-single additive baseline by +0.0209/+0.0114.
- **Discovered models barely beat the anchor (Table 4).** On BBBCC047 the best discovered model reaches Global PCC 0.1781/0.2036 versus 0.1805/0.2014 for the frozen h0 anchor; on BBBCC036 CellScientist leads at 0.1921/0.1567 versus AIDE 0.1746/0.1497, CellForge 0.1565/0.0102, HarmonyCell 0.0657/0.0102.

## Authors & Credibility

The team spans Sun Yat-sen University (Mengran Li), University of Macau (Bo Li), Sichuan University (Chengyang Zhang), Zhejiang University (Yang Yan, Jinfeng Xu), and Tencent AI Lab (Zhenchao Tang). Notably, Mengran Li and Bo Li are co-authors of CELLSCIENTIST (arXiv 2605.07335), the agentic discovery system that CELLAUDIT audits — giving the audit deep access to the policy being tested, while also meaning the paper is partly a self-assessment of the authors' own framework. The work is well-grounded in the statistics of interference/conditional permutations (Fisher; Caicedo-style matched permutations), factorial attribution (Lundberg & Lee), perturbation-modeling benchmarks (PerturbBen, PertEval, GeoX), and ML-reliability literature (DeGrave shortcut learning, Lapuschkin unfalsifiable predictors, D'Amour underspecification). A project page (https://linemgran98.github.io/CellAudit/), extensive appendices with pre-fixed rules, fold inventories, seeds, and per-table CIs support reproducibility.

## Critical Assessment

- **Genuinely novel framing, assembled from standard parts.** The decomposition into source consumption / fitted dependence / target-relevant contribution is the paper's real contribution and is clearly valuable for agentic discovery; but the individual instruments (replacement tests, factorial averaging, AST checks, residual modeling) are established techniques. Readers should calibrate expectations: this is a discipline-and-evidence paper, not an algorithms paper.
- **The audited predictors have thin predictive margins.** On BBBCC047 the best discovered models barely clear the frozen h0 anchor (0.2036 vs 0.2014 on Fold 5) and the score-selected model beats a control-only predictor by +0.0011 with a CI touching zero — echoing Ahlmann-Eltze et al.'s (Nature Methods 2025) critique that deep perturbation models often fail to beat simple linear baselines. The audit is demonstrated on a target population whose headline metric is nearly saturated by context-only information, which weakens the practical stakes of "recovering compound contribution".
- **The caught defect is an easy one.** The singleton key-value attention flaw is a coarse implementation bug that a code reviewer would likely spot; the paper does not evaluate the source checker's recall on deliberately subtle leakage (e.g., compound information entering through preprocessing or feature selection rather than the cited module). Coverage is also capped by `unresolved` citations.
- **Audit feedback's benefit is not statistically established.** The sci-Plex improvements (MSE −9.58%/−6.10%; PCC +0.0024/+0.0015) all have paired intervals spanning zero, and the audit roughly doubles token cost (592,347 vs ≥286,995). The authors say so plainly, but the abstract's "higher mean held-out predictive performance" should be read as a point estimate, not an established effect.
- **Fragile per-model decision rules.** The LINCS compound-identity criterion flips from 35/50 refits passing on Fold 4 to 10/50 on Fold 5 (mean lower effect quantile 0.0139 → 0.0090), and only 10 of 100 falsification-guided BBBCC047 models pass the reference-relative quantile — threshold choices materially change conclusions, and the paper's own known-truth simulations show map-only intervals severely undercover population effects.
- **Self-audit tension.** The revision mechanism is built and evaluated on the authors' own CELLSCIENTIST policy; AIDE/CellForge/HarmonyCell appear only in the frontier table, so cross-policy generality of falsification-guided revision is untested.

## Strengths

- Conceptually crisp and generalizable: the three non-equivalent questions, and the demonstration that each can hold without the others (e.g., 47/48 dependence vs 20/48 contribution), are immediately reusable beyond cell modeling.
- Exemplary evaluation hygiene: pre-fixed data processing/budget/selection rules, frozen checkpoints before held-out evaluation, physically disjoint control-well reconstruction to kill control-leakage confounds, and independent-cohort (LINCS→LKCP) transfer.
- Honest, unusually detailed uncertainty reporting: paired seed-by-trajectory intervals, source-scaffold resampling, separate fixed-dataset vs population quantiles, and explicit null results (intervals spanning zero, 20/48, 0/50 at LKCP boundaries).
- Constructive rather than purely critical: falsification evidence is fed back to revise models, and the residual-over-anchor formulation with compiler-enforced zero residual at the reference is a clean design.
- Known-truth simulations (6,400 datasets) validating what the intervals do and do not cover.

## Limitations

- Evidence that audit-enriched discovery helps prediction remains suggestive: every paired sci-Plex contrast includes zero, despite roughly doubled token usage.
- Revision experiments use only the CELLSCIENTIST policy family; generalization to other agent policies, other LLM backbones, or other scientific domains is untested.
- Absolute effect sizes are small (PCC deltas ~0.001–0.005; compound effects ~0.005), and the audited models offer little headroom over context-only or anchor baselines on BBBCC047, so the practical payoff for intervention design is unproven.
- The source checker's sensitivity is characterized only via the coarse singleton-attention defect and the 6,400-dataset simulation; no adversarial evaluation on subtle leakage patterns, and `unresolved` citations reduce coverage.
- Domain coupling: fold construction, replacement distributions, and reference thresholds are task-specific, so porting CELLAUDIT requires re-registration of rules per task.

## Relevance

该文与 LLM 智能体自动科学发现的可信性评估直接相关：它给出了把 agent 生成代码的"输入使用声明"落到源码、拟合行为、预测贡献三个可检验层次的完整审计协议，其"发现→证伪→修订"回路和冻结-测试纪律可直接移植到任何 agent 自动建模/写代码的场景；同时对虚拟细胞/扰动建模基准的现状（智能体模型仅勉强超过简单基线）提供了冷静的证据。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Can the AST source checker detect subtler defect classes (compound signal entering via preprocessing, feature selection, or shared encoders) with measurable recall on adversarially constructed leakage?
- Would falsification-guided revision preserve its contribution/prediction trade-off under AIDE, CellForge, or HarmonyCell policies, or with different LLM backbones behind the agent?
- How should the reference-relative criterion be calibrated so that per-model claim decisions are stable across folds (LINCS 35/50 → 10/50 flip) rather than threshold artifacts?
- Is the ~2x token cost of audit-enriched feedback justified when its paired PCC benefit spans zero — are there task regimes (larger effect sizes, noisier feedback) where the audit payoff becomes statistically established?
- Does a model's measured compound/dose contribution predict downstream biological utility (e.g., ranked intervention candidates), or only benchmark loss?
