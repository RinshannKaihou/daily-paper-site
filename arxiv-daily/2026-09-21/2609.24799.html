02 Why this matters

Accuracy metrics alone can hide evidence corruption; this gives low-bit medical deployment a zero-overhead fix — and a reusable rationale-faithfulness evaluation protocol (PredRec / FER / UCR). 量化社区长期忽视“压缩是否破坏可解释性”；该目标可直接套在主流变换型 PTQ 流水线上，对高风险领域的低比特部署尤具参考价值。

03 Critical edge

"Preserves accuracy" is generous: overall accuracy dips vs OSTQuant (35.41 vs 36.96), never beats full precision, and every rationale-aware variant costs 8–10 accuracy points against the unmodified objective.

Plus: FER/UCR rest on a GPT-5.4 judge validated on just 20 in-group cases (κ = 0.58–0.63) — absolute values are not cross-paper comparable.

A Methodology — how the evidence is protected

Two-pass, calibration-only machinery on OSTQuant; everything extra is discarded before deployment. All gains are calibration-matched (same 128-example pool).

  1. Target & scope W4A4KV4 PTQ, 4 open 7B–8B models (OpenBioLLM, Llama-3-8B, BioMistral, Mistral-7B) × 3 medical MCQA benchmarks.
  2. Diagnose the blind spot Between FP and quantized distributions, generic/discourse tokens absorb 83.07% of the KL signal; clinical cues ( 22.19% of tokens) get only 15.56% — the base loss optimizes fluency, not evidence.
  3. Pass 1 · build a faithfulness cache (offline) Keep teacher-correct examples only; Qwen2.5-72B proposes ≤6 candidate spans; spans are scored behaviorally by forced-choice necessity/sufficiency margins, and those whose support weakens under a frozen quantized probe become weighted evidence tokens.
  4. Pass 2 · two extra losses Evidence-weighted top-K rationale-token KL Lrat + evidence-conditioned answer-recovery KL Lrec (λ = 0.1 each), stacked on the base OSTQuant objective.
  5. Fuse & deploy Transformed parameters are merged into the weights: identical W4A4KV4 format and inference path — zero runtime overhead.

C Strengths

  • +The diagnostic is the hook: a quantified, testable motivation (75.34% of tokens taking 83.07% of the KL signal) that maps directly onto the loss design.
  • +Zero runtime overhead: probe, teacher, extractor and cache all vanish after calibration; the deployed model is byte-for-byte the baseline's quantization format.
  • +Careful evaluation: deterministic forced-choice scoring throughout, PredRec content controls, FER/UCR complements, anonymized pairwise judging with an explicit tie, ablations, λ sensitivity, and a second PTQ backbone.
  • +Honest accounting: accuracy trade-offs, the BioMistral exception and metric caveats are reported in the main text, not buried.
  • +Reproducible: released code, full prompts, hyperparameters, GPU budgets and dataset splits.

D Limitations

  • −Narrow scope: medical MCQA only, 7B–8B models, W4A4KV4 only (plus a W4A4 OmniQuant check); other bit-widths and PTQ families (GPTQ/AWQ/SpinQuant) are extrapolation.
  • −Accuracy cost is real: 35.41 vs 36.96 overall; 8–10 pts lost vs the base objective on Llama-3/MedExpQA — "preserves accuracy" means "stays in range".
  • −Benchmark noise: BioMistral beats its FP teacher by ~17 pts on MedExQA; most per-model FER/UCR confidence intervals cross zero.
  • −Judge validated thinly: GPT-5.4 refereed by 2 in-group annotators on 20 cases; FER/UCR absolutes don't transfer across papers.
  • −Teacher inheritance: the cache may entrench wrong-but-consistent clinical justifications; span-extraction errors silently redirect preservation pressure.
  • −Consistency ≠ safety: better answer–rationale agreement does not establish clinical correctness — explicitly not a clinical-use endorsement.

E Questions for further exploration

  • Other backbones: does the objective transfer to GPTQ/AWQ/SpinQuant-style pipelines and other bit-widths (W8A8, W2A4), where the transformation-optimization interface differs or disappears?
  • Recovering the accuracy cost: can the 8–10 pt drop from the auxiliary losses be clawed back via loss scheduling or selective per-layer application?
  • Weak-teacher regime: when the FP teacher is often wrong, does the cache preserve systematically biased rationales — and can a verifier-in-the-loop fix it?
  • Do the metrics mean trust: would FER/UCR hold with a larger, independent (ideally clinician) annotator pool, and correlate with real clinician trust?
  • Beyond multiple choice: does evidence preservation survive long-form clinical generation (discharge summaries, radiology reports) where forced-choice PredRec no longer applies?
