> **一句话总结：** Dartmouth 团队把"柏拉图表征假说"(PRH) 之争从"样本落在哪"改写为"样本之间指向哪"——在仅做一次正交 Procrustes 对齐（旋转+反射、无缩放无学习参数）后，44 个独立训练的视觉/语言编码器之间的**概念级位移向量**高度平行（LLM-Llama/Gemma 的 dog−cat 位移 cos=0.91，视觉-视觉 0.73，跨模态 0.49），而点wise位置相似度只有 0.36/0.41/0.25（Wilcoxon 中位差 0.47/0.11/0.24，均 p<0.0001），一次实验同时调和了 PRH 的支持证据与 2026 年的反例；进一步用训练目标做因果分离——细调 CLIP 让深层 Cross-Model 从 1.0 坍到 0.73 而点wise变化 <0.05，关系蒸馏（RKD）把 Cross-Model 从 0.32 拉到 0.74 而点wise纹丝不动（0.32–0.35）——证明表征可分解为"线性对齐的共享语义 + 非线性缠绕的私有能力"，并据此提出 Shadow Casting：给冻结 CLIP 挂 20M 参数残差模块（视觉侧 SwiGLU 注入 DINO 特征、语言侧对 LLM2Vec-Qwen3-4B 教师做关系蒸馏），CC12M 上 3 epoch、8 卡 0.5 小时训完，MMVP 上较 CLIP-Base +6.7pp（19.3→26.0）、CLIP-Large +9.6pp（20.0→29.6），可训练参数比全量微调少 95%。

> **TL;DR:** This paper shifts the Platonic Representation Hypothesis (PRH) debate from pointwise sample proximity (where convergence is contested and weak) to **displacement vectors** between sample pairs: after a single rigid orthogonal Procrustes alignment, cos(a_i−a_j, b_i−b_j) is substantially preserved across 44 independently trained encoders (25 ViTs: supervised/CLIP/DINO/MAE/SigLIP; 19 LLMs: Llama, Gemma, Mistral, OLMo, Bloomz). Concept-level Cross-Model similarity reaches 0.91 (Llama↔Gemma), 0.73 (vision↔vision), 0.49 (vision↔language), versus Pointwise of 0.36/0.41/0.25 (paired Wilcoxon medians +0.47/+0.11/+0.24, all p<0.0001). Training-objective experiments prove the two similarities are independently controllable: fine-tuning CLIP on ImageNet-1k leaves Pointwise changed <0.05 while deep-layer Cross-Model collapses 1.0→0.73, whereas relational distillation (RKD) raises Cross-Model 0.32→0.74 with Pointwise flat (0.32–0.35). Representations decompose (label-free, via eigendecomposition of a cross-model consensus matrix) into a shared semantic component that aligns linearly and a private capability component that does not. Exploiting this, ShadowCLIP adds <1/20-parameter residual modules to a frozen CLIP (SwiGLU branch injecting DINO features; MLP branch distilled from LLM2Vec-Qwen3-4B), trained on CC12M in 0.5 h on 8 RTX Pro 6000 GPUs, gaining +6.7pp (Base) / +9.6pp (Large) on MMVP over the CLIP baseline with 95% fewer trainable parameters than full fine-tuning.

## 核心贡献

- **测量对象的转变（调和 PRH 双方）**：PRH 支持方（Huh et al. 2024）与反对方（Koepke et al. 2026、Gröger et al. 2026）都在测"位置"——mutual-kNN 或样本相近度，得到相反结论。本文改测**位移方向核** cos(a_i−a_j, b_i−b_j)：独立训练的模型共享的不是样本的绝对位置，而是样本之间的**方向**。点wise相似度低（与反对方一致）与位移几何强收敛（与 PRH 直觉一致）在同一个实验里同时成立——争论的矛盾被消解而非站队。
- **44 模型上的实证刻画**：25 个视觉 Transformer（supervised/CLIP/DINO/MAE/SigLIP-I-JEPA × base/large/huge）+ 19 个 LLM（Llama、Gemma、Mistral、OLMo、Bloomz）。概念级 Cross-Model 相似度 V-V 0.73、L-L 0.91、V-L 0.49，全部在**仅一次正交对齐**下取得（无缩放、无非线性映射、单一旋转对所有三个指标共用，held-out 评估防过拟合）；样本级相似度是概念级更弱、更噪声的反射（概念级增益 +0.28/+0.18/+0.19）。
- **概念正交性的机制解释**：模型内部概念呈近似正交层级几何——父-子概念正相关（cos≈0.72），子概念的变化向量与父概念正交（cos≈0），兄弟概念在该正交子空间内彼此反平行（≈−0.30），且这些规律**逐层持续**。配以受控仿真（A.2）：破坏概念级平行性或正交性，样本级平行性随之坍塌——样本级平行是从概念级几何继承的。
- **可证伪的因果分离**：三种训练体制（细调/RKD/FKD，from-CLIP 与 from-scratch 双设定）各自独立地移动 Pointwise 与 Cross-Model——两者不可相互约减，位移收敛是训练目标可独立控制的量。
- **共享-私有分解 + Shadow Casting**：对跨模型共识矩阵 S_cross 做特征分解（label-free），得到线性对齐的共享语义成分（线性可解码、Cross-Model 高）与非线性缠绕的私有能力成分（线性核 HSIC 低、RBF 核显著高——能力不是与语义正交，而是非线性编织其中）。推论：语义线性对齐而能力不对齐 ⇒ 能力可以经**一次缓存的 forward pass**（"casting the shadow"）从源模型导入目标模型，无需重训。
- **ShadowCLIP 概念验证**：冻结 CLIP 骨干 + 两个零初始化门控的轻量残差模块（视觉 SwiGLU 残差以 CLIP 特征为门、注入 DINO 值特征；语言侧两层 MLP 残差以 KL 对 LLM2Vec-Gen-Qwen3-4B 教师的成对余弦相似度分布做关系蒸馏），CC12M 上 3 epoch 训练仅 0.5 小时（8×RTX Pro 6000），在 11 个 zero-shot 基准上平均超 CC12M 训练的对照 CLIP +4.3pp。

## Key Contributions

- **A measurement-level resolution of the PRH debate**: both PRH proponents (Huh et al. 2024) and critics (Koepke et al. 2026; Gröger et al. 2026) measure *positions* (mutual-kNN / pointwise proximity). The paper instead evaluates a kernel on displacement pairs, cos(a_i−a_j, b_i−b_j), under one rigid orthogonal alignment per model pair — showing that weak pointwise agreement (the counter-evidence) and strong directional convergence (the PRH intuition) are simultaneously true, dissolving rather than adjudicating the contradiction.
- **Empirical characterization across 44 models**: 25 vision Transformers (supervised/CLIP/DINO/MAE/SigLIP-I-JEPA, base/large/huge) and 19 LLMs (Llama, Gemma, Mistral, OLMo, Bloomz). Concept-level Cross-Model similarity is 0.73 (V-V), 0.91 (L-L), 0.49 (V-L) under rotation-and-reflection only — no scaling, no learned mapping, one Procrustes rotation shared by all three metrics, fit on a held-out split and evaluated on a disjoint complement. Sample-level parallelism is a noisier reflection of concept-level structure (+0.28/+0.18/+0.19 gains at concept level).
- **A mechanistic account via within-model concept orthogonality**: hierarchical concepts are approximately orthogonal within each model — parent-child cos ≈ 0.72, child variation vectors ⊥ parent ≈ 0, siblings anti-aligning ≈ −0.30 — persisting at every layer. Controlled simulations (App. A.2) selectively break each concept-level condition and show sample-level parallelism collapses, confirming the inheritance direction.
- **Falsifiable causal separation by training objective**: fine-tuning preserves Pointwise (Δ<0.05) while collapsing deep-layer Cross-Model (1.0→0.73); relational distillation raises Cross-Model (0.32→0.74) with Pointwise flat; feature distillation raises both — the two quantities are independently controllable and irreducible.
- **Shared/private decomposition and Shadow Casting**: a label-free eigendecomposition of a cross-model consensus matrix splits representations into a linearly-aligned shared semantic component (linearly decodable, high Cross-Model) and a private capability component (low linear HSIC, substantially higher RBF HSIC — capabilities are non-linearly woven in, not orthogonal). Because semantics align linearly but capabilities do not, capabilities can be imported via a single cached forward pass of the source model.
- **ShadowCLIP proof of concept**: frozen CLIP backbone + two zero-initialized gated residual modules (<1/20 of CLIP's parameters; vision-side SwiGLU injecting DINO features, language-side 2-layer MLP distilled from LLM2Vec-Gen-Qwen3-4B), trained on CC12M for 3 epochs in 0.5 h on 8 GPUs, outperforming the CC12M-trained CLIP baseline by +4.3pp average zero-shot accuracy across 11 benchmarks.

## Methodology

- **Models and data.** 44 pretrained encoders: 25 vision Transformers spanning three sizes (base/large/huge) and five paradigms (supervised, CLIP, DINO, MAE, SigLIP-I-JEPA), plus 19 LLMs (Llama, Gemma, Mistral, OLMo, Bloomz). Sample-level analysis uses 10,000 paired Flickr30k image-text pairs; concept-level analysis uses the intersection of ImageNet-22k classes and CommonWord79k (each ImageNet-22k class is a concept; parent/child/sibling relations from WordNet), with 10,000 additional random samples for concepts.
- **Minimal preprocessing.** Center, then per-model whitening W = Σ^(−1/2) so the Euclidean inner product matches the intended "causal" inner product (an appendix illustrates how anisotropy makes raw-space similarity misleading: spurious linearity with R²=0.813 drops to 0.509 after whitening). PCA to a common dimension r ∈ {20, 100, 500} (qualitative findings stable; 500 reported). This deliberately minimal pipeline is chosen so that any shared structure found cannot be attributed to sophisticated alignment machinery.
- **Rigid alignment.** For each ordered model pair (a,b), one orthogonal Procrustes rotation+reflection R_ab is fit on a held-out subset (closed-form via SVD; no scaling, no learned nonlinearity) and used for all three metrics on the disjoint complement — no metric is individually optimized.
- **Three cosine metrics.** Pointwise cos(a_i, b_i) (do positions agree); Cross-Object cos(a_i−b_i, a_j−b_j) (a within-model architectural baseline — high, 0.87–0.96, even for random-init models); Cross-Model cos(a_i−a_j, b_i−b_j) (does moving i→j trace the same direction in both models — the directional convergence quantity). Randomly-initialized networks provide a null for learned vs architectural convergence.
- **Cross-model consensus decomposition.** A symmetric matrix S_cross averages cross-covariances over all M(M−1) ordered model pairs; eigendecomposition splits directions into a top-r consensus (shared) subspace and bottom-r private subspace; components are projected and, for downstream evaluation, inverse-transformed back to each model's original feature space. Label-free — labels only used for evaluation.
- **Training-objective experiments.** Two settings: (top) from CLIP ViT-B/16, 10 epochs on ImageNet-1k — (a) fine-tuning, (b) relational distillation (RKD: KL on pairwise cosine-similarity distributions), (c) feature distillation (FKD: MSE on representations); (bottom) 100 epochs from scratch with the same three regimes. Pointwise and Cross-Model are tracked per epoch.
- **ShadowCLIP.** Frozen CLIP-ViT-B/16 or L/14 paired with dimensionality-matched DINOv3; vision side: a residual SwiGLU branch whose gate is driven by the CLIP representation and whose value comes from DINO, added back to the original CLIP branch; language side: a 2-layer MLP residual on the CLIP text encoder trained with KL divergence between teacher (LLM2Vec-Gen-Qwen3-4B, whitened) and student pairwise cosine-similarity distributions. All gates are zero-initialized (ShadowCLIP starts exactly as CLIP). Trained on CC12M with InfoNCE + RKD, 3 epochs, batch 32,768, lr 5×10⁻⁴, on 10 RTX Pro 6000 GPUs (0.5 h on 8); all backbone representations are precomputed once (the "cached forward pass"), which is what collapses the FLOP count. Evaluated on 11 zero-shot classification suites, MSCOCO/Flickr30k retrieval, and MMVP, against CC12M-trained baselines (CLIP, DreamLIP, LiT, ShareLock, SAIL-NV2).

## Results & Highlights

- **Directional convergence dominates pointwise agreement.** Concept-level Cross-Model similarity reaches 0.73 (V-V), 0.91 (L-L), 0.49 (V-L) versus Pointwise 0.41/0.36/0.25 — e.g., the (v_dog − v_cat) displacement in Llama and in Gemma agree at cos = 0.91 after one rigid rotation. Paired Wilcoxon signed-rank tests: Cross-Model exceeds Pointwise by median 0.11 (V-V), 0.47 (L-L), 0.24 (V-L), all p < 0.0001; the 44×44 all-pairs matrices show the pattern for individual pairs, not just aggregates.
- **Training objectives independently control the two similarities.** Fine-tuning CLIP ViT-B/16 on ImageNet-1k (10 epochs) leaves Pointwise changed <0.05 while Cross-Model drops 1.0 → 0.73 in deep layers; RKD raises Cross-Model 0.32 → 0.74 with Pointwise flat (0.32–0.35); FKD raises both (Pointwise 0.5 → 0.7, Cross-Model 0.48 → 0.62). The same pattern reproduces training from scratch over 100 epochs — Pointwise and Cross-Model are independently controllable by objective choice.
- **ShadowCLIP outperforms its CC12M-trained baselines by large margins on the hardest benchmark.** On MMVP (vision-centric understanding), ShadowCLIP scores 26.0 vs CLIP-Base's 19.3 (+6.7pp) at ViT-Base and 29.6 vs 20.0 (+9.6pp) at ViT-Large; it leads zero-shot classification on 11 benchmarks with +4.3pp average over CLIP at both scales (e.g., ImageNet-1k: 70.2 vs 67.0 at Base, 76.5 vs 72.7 at Large), and leads image-to-text retrieval with Flickr30k R@1 of 84.8 (Base) and 89.9 (Large) vs CLIP's 83.2/87.6.
- **Compute efficiency.** ShadowCLIP trains 95% fewer parameters than full fine-tuning of CLIP-Large (20M vs 400M); caching backbone features reduces three-phase FLOPs by a factor of 2×10⁴ (including one-time precomputation), and total FLOPs over the 3 training epochs are 9× less — enabling the 0.5-hour, 8-GPU training run.
- **Robustness across capability asymmetry and modality.** Cross-Model similarity survives extreme scale/capability gaps: 0.376 for CLIP-ViT-Huge vs MAE-ViT-Base, 0.611 for Llama3-8B vs Bloomz-560M; DINO-Large↔Llama3-8B gives 0.306, nearly identical to CLIP-Large↔Llama3-8B's 0.311, ruling out CLIP's vision-language objective as the driver of V-L convergence. Findings are stable across PCA dims 20/100/500 and across seeds; randomly-initialized models show near-zero Cross-Model similarity, isolating learned (not architectural) convergence.
- **Ablations match the geometric prediction.** Fine-tuning CLIP's projections directly on CC12M (Clip-FT) *degrades* performance (−2.5% zero-shot, −2.2% retrieval, −1.5% MMVP vs original CLIP), while removing the relational-distillation Language Guider costs −6.9%/−3.4%/−9.2% — confirming capability transfer operates by *adding relational structure* on top of a frozen backbone, not by modifying its absolute positions.

## Authors & Credibility

The paper comes from the Dartmouth College CS department: Chenning Shang, Yujin Tang, Jun Jie Ou Yang, and Ruize Xu (research students) with Adam Breuer and Nikhil Singh as co-principal investigators. The group demonstrates strong command of the representation-alignment literature: the related-work section engages both the PRH lineage (Huh et al. 2024; Sucholutsky et al. 2023) *and* the 2026 counter-evidence (Koepke et al., Gröger et al.) it aims to reconcile, rather than ignoring critics. The authors of the relational-distillation transfer mechanism it builds on (Tung & Mori 2019) are cited precisely, and the experimental hygiene is above average for this area: held-out Procrustes fitting, a shared rotation across all metrics (no metric-specific optimization), paired Wilcoxon tests with Benjamini-Hochberg correction, random-init null models, PCA-dimension and seed robustness checks, asymmetric-capability pairs, and a DINO-vs-CLIP control against the "it's just CLIP" confound. Full replication code is released (github.com/HelloSCM/RepresentationDisplacements), and the limitations section (§6) candidly concedes scale, uniqueness, and probe-simplicity concerns — a credibility positive.

## Critical Assessment

- **Genuinely novel reframing, with a lineage.** Moving the convergence debate from positions to displacement directions is a clean conceptual contribution, and the "both sides measured positions" diagnosis is well supported by their own data (Pointwise low, Cross-Model high, simultaneously). That said, the ingredients are assembled from known parts — relational distillation (Tung & Mori 2019), within-model categorical concept geometry (Park et al. 2024), and linear representation hypothesis — so the novelty is in the cross-model synthesis and the falsification program, not in any single piece.
- **The headline is a concept-level result; sample-level effects are much weaker.** The dramatic numbers (0.91, 0.73) are concept-level. At the sample level, V-L Cross-Model similarity is only 0.31 vs a Pointwise 0.19, and V-L concept-level is 0.49 — well below the 0.73/0.91 within-modality values. "Shared reality across models and modalities" is therefore substantially an *inheritance from nominal WordNet/ImageNet concept structure*; how much directional agreement exists for relations, events, or abstract content is untested.
- **A large share of directional agreement is architectural, not learned.** The paper's own Cross-Object baseline sits at 0.87–0.96 even for randomly initialized models. The learned component is the gap to this baseline, which is real but means the absolute similarity figures flatter the headline; the authors handle this honestly with the random-init null, but abstract-level claims ("substantially preserved") ride on quantities whose floor is very high.
- **"Independently trained" is doing conventional-heavy lifting.** Appendix D.1 admits that the LLMs share overlapping pretraining corpora (CommonCrawl, Wikipedia, books) and the vision encoders similar web scrapes. The V-L and asymmetric-pair results mitigate this, but the confound of shared *data* versus shared *world structure* — which is the actual PRH claim — is not disentangled, and the authors say so.
- **ShadowCLIP's "outperforms strong baselines" is scoped to CC12M-trained models, with visible losses to SAIL-NV2.** At ViT-Large, SAIL-NV2 beats ShadowCLIP on Aircraft (60.4 vs 37.5 — a 22.9pp deficit), COCO T2I (45.4 vs 44.4), and Flowers is close (75.5 vs 80.6 favors ShadowCLIP); at Base, SAIL-NV2 leads COCO T2I (45.3 vs 39.8) and DTD (61.5 vs 55.5). The paper concedes the SAIL T2I gap and notes SAIL swaps in a full LLM text encoder at inference, but the abstract's "outperforms strong fine-tuned baselines at orders of magnitude less compute" should be read as "beats CC12M baselines on average and on MMVP," not across the board.
- **Missing or thin analyses.** No error bars/CI over concept samples in the main figures (only Wilcoxon medians in App. B); the decomposition rank r is a chosen hyperparameter whose sensitivity is not shown; the whitening step is argued for via toy examples, and while PCA-dim robustness is shown, a no-whitening ablation of the headline numbers is not. The uniqueness question — whether this shared geometry is the only high-performance mode — is acknowledged as open, correctly.

## Strengths

- A simple, decisive formal move (a kernel on displacement pairs) that reconciles two opposed 2024–2026 literatures with one experiment rather than a new benchmark.
- Exemplary experimental hygiene: held-out alignment fitting, one shared rotation for all metrics, random-init nulls, asymmetric-capability pairs, cross-modality controls, PCA/seed robustness, and paired nonparametric statistics.
- Real falsifiability: the §4.5 training-objective predictions (fine-tune vs RKD vs FKD) were stated in advance and confirmed in both from-CLIP and from-scratch regimes — this converts a descriptive claim into a causal one.
- A rare theory-to-practice arc: the decomposition directly motivates ShadowCLIP, and the method's two ablations fail exactly where the geometry says they should (linear-only adaptation can't import nonlinear capability).
- Honest, specific limitations section and public replication code.

## Limitations

- **Scale**: 44 models but none at the frontier; whether displacement parallelism strengthens or degrades with scale is an open empirical question the paper cannot answer (the very axis on which the pointwise-based counter-evidence emerged).
- **Uniqueness**: the shared directional geometry may be one of several equally viable consensus modes ("Anna Karenina" scenario); the analysis is consistent with either.
- **Probe simplicity**: results use the simplest possible directional comparison — cosine of difference vectors after whitening + PCA + rigid rotation; richer decompositions (how directions compose, stratify across layers, or relate to functional subspaces) are unexplored.
- **Concept scope**: concept-level claims rest on ImageNet-22k ∩ CommonWord79k nominal concepts with WordNet hierarchy; generalization to relations, events, and non-English lexicons is not shown.
- **Data confound**: "independently trained" models share pretraining data at scale; shared data vs shared world structure is not separable here.
- **ShadowCLIP is a proof of concept**: CC12M-scale training, evaluation against CC12M-trained baselines only, and capability import demonstrated for one vision-language configuration — the "download capabilities into any model" vision remains untested beyond it.

## Relevance

这篇论文对你的研究兴趣（表征对齐、多模态几何、模型可解释性与高效模型组合）高度相关：它用极简的位移方向核一次性调和了柏拉图表征假说的正反双方证据，并给出了"共享语义线性对齐、私有能力非线性缠绕"的可操作分解。文中 ShadowCLIP 展示的"冻结骨干 + 关系蒸馏残差 + 缓存前向"能力迁移范式，对低算力下的模型增强/蒸馏实验也很有直接借鉴价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

1. Does displacement parallelism hold, strengthen, or degrade at frontier scale — the axis on which the pointwise-based counter-evidence (Koepke et al. 2026) was found? The paper's 44 models stop well short of this.
2. Is the consensus directional geometry unique among high-performing models, or one of several equally viable modes? If multiple modes exist, can a model be steered between them, and what would that imply for Shadow Casting?
3. The private capability component is "nonlinearly woven" into the shared one (linear vs RBF HSIC). Can richer probes characterize this entanglement, and is there a bound on how much capability a cached shadow can carry?
4. How sensitive are the headline numbers to the whitening + PCA-500 + rigid-rotation pipeline? A no-whitening or learned-alignment ablation of the main result is not reported.
5. Concept-level analysis is restricted to nominal WordNet/ImageNet concepts — do displacement directions for relations, events, or abstract discourse align across models, or is the shared geometry limited to object-kind structure?
6. ShadowCLIP trails SAIL-NV2 on fine-grained classification (Aircraft, 37.5 vs 60.4 at Large) and COCO T2I — does scaling the training set or the teacher close this, or is it a structural limit of residual modules over a frozen CLIP backbone?
