Methodology · distilled
  1. The mismatch

    SFT minimizes per-trace cross-entropy, but deployment is search under a budget. The true objective JẸ(θ) needs off-trace info a single demo proof never reveals.

  2. CAT — trace-supported surrogate

    Restrict search to trace-reachable states; fold every deviation into one trace-miss event. Solvable recursion; gradients are just reweighted per-tactic CE (Prop. 1), ω̃t = (pₜ/J̃)·∂J̃/∂pₜ. Cost: one extra epoch.

  3. UA — search-agnostic loss

    Split budget uniformly, K=N/L local tries per step; recovers CE at N=L. One shared adapter transfers across searchers.

  4. Two error channels

    Bypass (alt. proofs deflate weights, Thm 1) vs trap (excursions inflate them, Thm 2). As N→∞, search-aware error →0 while CE error →1 (Thm 3).

  5. Experiments

    Qwen2.5-Math-7B + LoRA on LeanDojo mathlib4 (40,660 proofs); 458 held-out 2–5-step theorems; six strategies; epoch-matched controls; paired McNemar, seed pinned.

Strengths
  • Mechanically derivable. Any branching strategy maps to per-tactic CE reweightings — applicable with one extra epoch.
  • UA is genuinely practical: one adapter, zero knowledge of the deployed searcher, best-or-tied in 5/6 strategies.
  • Theory does real work: explains why gains grow with budget; bypass/trap give directional predictions.
  • Exemplary hygiene: epoch-matched controls, paired McNemar + 95% CIs, pinned seed, abandoned runs documented, no exclusions.
  • Honest scope: authors flag trace-only supervision and single-model limits themselves.
Limitations
  • Narrow base: one 7B model, one benchmark (mathlib4), 2–5-step proofs only, single seed — untested at longer horizons, where the trap channel should matter most.
  • Provably not exact (Prop. 4): alternative proofs and recovery costs are structurally invisible to trace-only training; no rollouts introduced to close the gap.
  • Thin CAT-vs-UA edge: significant over CE in only 2/6 strategies, no CAT-vs-UA test, ~1–2 pt strategy-matched gain.
  • BFS surrogate changes the algorithm: retry-on-singleton-frontier removes score competition; mapping to deployed BFS never validated.
  • Far from the frontier: ~21–27% absolute success is a controlled-science regime, not a SOTA-proving one.
Questions for Further Exploration
  • Can training-time search rollouts break the Prop. 4 identifiability barrier and capture bypass information?
  • Do conclusions transfer to >5-step proofs, miniF2F, or larger models where the trap channel dominates?
  • Is CAT’s edge over UA statistically real? An adequately powered paired comparison is needed first.
  • How do reweighted-CE objectives compare to outcome-reward RL at matched compute — are they complementary?
  • Can trap parameters (κ, qₛ) be estimated from deployment telemetry for per-step weight corrections?
