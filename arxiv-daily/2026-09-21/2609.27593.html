为“行为消失 ≠ 知识删除”提供了可证伪的机制级模板：判断遗忘是否成功，必须用因果补丁检查表征能否复活。

Critical edge

The frozen encoder is load-bearing: unfreeze it and the network genuinely deletes — so the mapping onto real unlearning pipelines stays a hypothesis.

▸ evidence in limitations
01

Method

  1. 1Control geometry. Toy Model of Superposition, 16 features, bottleneck d ≪ n; entanglement set exactly as ρ = cos(wA, wB) via anti-co-occurrence sampling; other features untouched.
  2. 2Linear baseline. Rank-1 nullspace projection (LEACE’s action) swept over ρ ∈ {0, −0.5, −1} (15-point sweep in appendix); track B’s residual and A’s MSE vs the 0.098 interference floor.
  3. 3Excise by gradient. Freeze the encoder; fine-tune decoder + output bias with a λ·x̂B² penalty (λ = 16), AdamW.
  4. 4Map the basins. Two-pass grid (5-point pilot → 15 ρ × 100 seeds); post-hoc 2-component GMM labels runs mirror/shadow on (cos, bB); Wilson 95% CIs.
  5. 5Patch causally. Shadow: interpolate bB back toward pre-excision; mirror: zB → |zB|. Sufficiency (zero side effects, structurally guaranteed) + negative control, 20 trials/basin.
  6. 6Stress it. Extra hidden layer; GELU with BIC-selected components; appendixed theorems: the ρ² damage law and sign-separability (why ReLU thresholding is the minimal nonlinearity).

apparatus — 16-feature ReLU autoencoder · frozen encoder · λ=16 · 100 seeds/ρ · GMM posterior > 0.9 for patch runs

03

Strengths

  • +Exact, free-parameter-free theory: the ρ² damage law is derived then checked (0.176 predicted vs 0.144 measured at ρ=−0.5, gap explained); sign-separability theorem shows why nonlinearity is necessary.
  • +Exemplary causal protocol: single-scalar patches, structural sufficiency, explicit negative control, revival demonstrated in both basins with different response shapes.
  • +Statistical hygiene rare in interpretability: Wilson intervals at every point, 100 seeds/condition, two-pass densified grid, BIC instead of forcing two-basin labels.
  • +Intellectual honesty: Appendix E documents the authors’ own unfrozen-encoder artifact; limitations pre-empt the obvious objections.
  • +Grokking-style framing: individual outcomes arbitrary, population transition sharp — broader dynamical interest.
04

Limitations

  • −Toy scale: one engineered antipodal pair in a 16-feature autoencoder; the small-transformer check is not LLM evidence — no TOFU/WMDP benchmark is run.
  • −Frozen encoder is load-bearing: with all parameters trainable the network deletes genuinely (Appendix E); relevance to real fine-tuning pipelines is a hypothesis, not a result.
  • −No formal attractor analysis: basin labels are post-hoc GMM assignments; trajectories overlap heavily near ρ=−0.75; bifurcation stability unproven.
  • −GELU blurs the picture (4 BIC components), and robustness grids shrink to 7 points, under-sampling ρ ∈ [−0.5, 0].
  • −Missing comparisons: RACE / kernelized RCDM, literature unlearning losses (representation misdirection); bespoke λ=16 objective; no code release stated.
05

Questions for further exploration

  • Does the mirror/shadow bifurcation survive literature-drawn unlearning objectives (representation misdirection, gradient-ascent variants) rather than the bespoke λ=16 loss?
  • Why does deletion dominate when the encoder is unfrozen in the toy, yet real LLM unlearning appears to suppress — optimization geometry, loss design, or feature depth?
  • Can a comparable single-scalar revival patch be built for SAE/LEACE-erased features in a production LLM, measured on TOFU or WMDP?
  • Does the sharp transition at ρ ≈ −0.75 have a dynamical-systems account, and does the individual-vs-population gap hold within a single run (grokking-style)?
  • With multiple entangled pairs, do mirror/shadow basins stay distinct — or does interference create a third attractor resembling genuine deletion?
