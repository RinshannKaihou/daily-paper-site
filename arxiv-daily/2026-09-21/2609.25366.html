Why this matters

CoT-based monitoring is only meaningful where the chain is load-bearing — exactly on hard tasks, where errors propagate before a monitor can intervene.

探针方向是「读出器」而非「控制器」：为「探针准确率 ≠ 因果可操控性」提供了带随机方向对照的大规模实证边界。

Critical edge

The 40.9% BBH headline is likely inflated by label noise — under human labels the C-rate compresses toward 24%, so read it as an upper bound.

Methodology

Perturb one step of a correct multi-step CoT, truncate, and force the model to continue from the corrupted prefix — then classify what the continuation does.

  1. Parse

    Start from questions the model answers correctly with a ≥3-step chain.

  2. Perturb

    Exactly one step: 2 mathematical strategies (GSM8K) or 5 conceptual archetypes (MMLU/BBH), injected at 25% / 50% / 75% depth.

  3. Force continuation

    Truncate after the corrupted step. Unlike Lanham-style re-answering, forced continuation blocks re-derivation from the question — isolating the CoT→answer link.

  4. Classify

    LLM judge assigns each outcome: A silent bypass · B self-correction · C error propagation. Boundary stress-tested; C-vs-non-C human-validated (κ = 1.00).

  5. Disentangle

    Matched 2×2 (text-on-easy + numerical-on-hard, n = 28,584) with a logistic deviance partition separates difficulty from perturbation type.

Readout vs control: linear/MLP probes over 42 layers × 3 token positions (PCA-128, grouped 5-fold CV) predict behavior; steering adds the layer-21 probe direction to the residual stream across 11,556 generations.

Gemma-2-9B-IT · Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct · DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B — 21,238 + 17,346 labeled continuations

Strengths

  • Scale with control — 21,238 + 17,346 matched continuations from a deterministic protocol; reproducible on a single H100 by a small lab.
  • The matched 2×2 / variance partition is the right tool for the type-vs-difficulty confound, executed with unusual thoroughness (strength sweep, entry-order and bucketing sensitivity).
  • Radical label-quality honesty — judge-prompt sweep, blind n=500 human study (κ=0.93 overall, 1.00 on C-vs-non-C), self-reported −24.9pp probe label artifact.
  • A useful null result — detection (86.2%) vs control (≤24.6%) reported with Wilson CIs and dose-response checks.
  • Concrete stakes — confidence injection (36.8% propagation) is a documented attack on CoT-monitored systems; code, prompts, and annotations are public.

Limitations

  • ≤9B models only — no ≥70B run (single-H100 budget); the base-vs-IT control collapsed, leaving instruction tuning vs CoT elicitation confounded.
  • The A/B boundary is operationally subjective — bypass swings 4.0%→89.2% across judge prompts; three-way human agreement is only 76.0%.
  • BBH Type-C over-counting — human-labeled C-rate compresses to 24% vs the paper’s 44% stratum; cross-dataset magnitudes are soft even though ordering holds.
  • Asymmetric correct-baseline conditioning — BBH discards more baselines, over-selecting competence-boundary items; all rates are conditional on this slice.
  • Cross-model fragility — Llama text-on-GSM8K failed to replicate (97.7% empty continuations); the DeepSeek arm uses non-official greedy decoding with small n (1,603).
  • Single-step perturbations only, and the protocol measures only CoT→answer — it is silent on the computation→CoT link.

Questions for further exploration

  1. Does the gradient persist at ≥70B scale, and does reasoning RL causally suppress propagation — or is the distill’s low C-rate a prompt-format artifact?
  2. Would internally consistent multi-step corruption collapse the 98.8% / 0.8% partition, or does difficulty still dominate?
  3. Can causal scrubbing or circuit edits move behavior where additive steering fails — separating “unfaithful” from “unsteerable at the probed layers”?
  4. Is mid-generation self-correction (27% of continuations) detectable prospectively from hidden states, defining a monitor’s intervention window?
  5. Can the same probes detect the confidence-injection attack (36.8% propagation) at inference time?
