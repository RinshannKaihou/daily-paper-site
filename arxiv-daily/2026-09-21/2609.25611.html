Critical Assessment · benchmark asymmetry & undisclosed scale

A Methodology distilled flow

  1. Backbone

    Qwen3.8-Next hybrid sparse MoE: Gated DeltaNet interleaved with full attention; QSA indexer scores compressed micro-blocks, core attention stays on original tokens inside selected blocks.

  2. 1M-token context

    Native 262,144-token pretraining in 4 stages (encoder alignment → ~2.5T tokens → QSA warmup → joint QSA), extended to 1M after post-training.

  3. Three encoders, one Thinker space

    Vision + general AuT (6.25 Hz ≈ 1 token/160 ms) + new Spatial AuT for first-order ambisonics via complex STFT preserving phase/amplitude.

  4. Distill, then unified RL

    Domain-specialist teacher trajectories are distilled into one student; RL then spans text/vision/audio/mixed — rewards from task outcomes, not self-reported completion.

  5. Realtime Talker & open systems

    RVQ tokens + ARIA streaming, 48 kHz Code2Wav, RTF ≈ 0.153 (≈6.5× real time); open Qwen-MM-Plugins (Caption / Video2Note / Memory / Skill-Creator) + Qwen-Live-Harness.

C Strengths

  • Exceptional breadth: ~30+ benchmarks across text coding, vision agents, ASR, A/V reasoning, realtime interaction — with per-benchmark harness configs documented.
  • Gains land on the thesis: +36.5 WildClawBench-MM, +22.3 AgenticVBench, and −45.7% tokens/query at +4.4 accuracy.
  • Credible recipe: multi-teacher distillation → outcome-rewarded RL directly targets cross-modal interference and self-report reward hacking.
  • System-level thinking: abstraction modules attack the real bottleneck (video token budgets); plugins and realtime harness are open-sourced.
  • Honest reporting: regressions and losses to Gemini / Seed / Claude printed beside the wins.

D Limitations

  • Not text-reasoning SOTA: HLE 36.5 vs Claude-Opus-4.6’s 40.0; DeepSWE 57.8 slightly below text-only Qwen3.8-Flash (58.7).
  • Public ASR regressions persist: FLEURS-ASR 9.3 WER, LongAudioASR 6.89→9.27, even as multi-speaker ASR improves dramatically.
  • Long-horizon audio evidence use trails Gemini: 71.8 vs 84.2; Seed 2.0 Lite leads ProactiveVideoQA (68.3 vs 61.5).
  • Reproducibility gap: no parameter count, no compute disclosure, no ablations; several headline speech benchmarks are in-house.
  • Single self-scored demo: the autoresearch CER result is one demonstration, not a repeatable protocol.

E Questions for further exploration

  • Harness or model? How much of the +36.5 survives a same-harness, all-models comparison?
  • Component value: marginal contribution of Spatial AuT and QSA vs the dense Qwen3.8-Next baseline — unmeasured, no ablations reported.
  • Lossless enough? Do evidence-critical answers degrade when forced through caption/memory abstraction instead of raw frames?
  • Deliberate trade-off? General-ASR regressions coexist with multi-speaker gains — recoverable without sacrifice?
  • Reward robustness: does outcome-rewarded RL stay clean when results are noisy or delayed, or invite degenerate “successful” hacks?
