Topographic Training Concentrates Causal Circuits Without Improving Neuron Monosemanticity

Gautam Ranka·Shubham Pandere·Aiden Ross D’souza — IvLabs, VNIT Nagpur ·ViT-S/16 on ImageNet-100 ·code released (IvLabs/toposae)

mechanistic-interpretability sparse-autoencoders activation-patching vision-transformer topographic-loss

Key metric

2.79×

causal sufficiency of top-16 class-neuron clusters under activation patching (cluster |Δlogit| ÷ random-set |Δlogit|), vs 1.68× at α=0.

Δ +1.12 (+66%) n=60 p<0.0001 d=0.95 3/3 seeds >2×

Why this matters

A one-line training regularizer reorganizes circuits in ways SAE and monosemanticity metrics can’t see — challenging “post-hoc SAE is the only path.”

拓扑先验在电路层集中因果质量，单个神经元的叠加却纹丝不动——神经元级指标对这类真实收益存在系统性盲区。

Critical edge

The headline p<0.0001 at n=60 treats non-independent pairs as independent — with only 3 models per α, the honest seed-level test is p=0.082.

also: α=1.0 costs −1.6 pp accuracy (61.2→59.6), and accuracy explains R²=0.72 of the SAE-L0 drop — H2 is not deconfounded.

01 Methodology

Training

Nine ViT-S/16 checkpoints on ImageNet-100. TopoLoss adds α·Σ‖wi−wj‖² Laplacian smoothness over adjacent attn.proj weights on a 16×24 grid, all 12 blocks; α ∈ {0, 0.1, 1.0} × 3 seeds, plus a 4-point dose sweep. A 4-layer TinyViT serves as scale probe.

H1 · monosemanticity

Entropy-based Mu per neuron over 5k val images; TopK-SAE (k=32) feature re-check enlarged to 25k images.

H2 · superposition

One SAE per checkpoint at layer 6 → L0 (active features/token) and dead-feature fraction.

H3 · causal purity

Patch the top-16 class-neuron activations into other-class images; ratio = cluster |Δlogit| ÷ random-16 |Δlogit|. Ratio >1 ⇒ concentrated causal mass.

Controls

OLS of L0 on accuracy; PCA-d95 dissociation at α=0.1; per-seed residuals; αeff weight-norm calibration tested on TinyViT.

02 Results — three hypotheses, three verdicts

* H2 is the weakest result. The authors’ own label is “suggestive but not confound-free”: the −1.6 pp accuracy drop explains R²=0.72 of L0 variance, and a 19× rise in dead features is conventionally an SAE pathology — it can equally mean the SAE fits the new representation worse.

Scale probe (TinyViT, single seed): H2 transfers as the αeff calibration predicts (L0 −2.1%), but H3 does not — it saturates at 1.42× (vs 2.79× in ViT-S) and falls to 1.11× after calibration (α=0.38). Depth is the leading hypothesis; untested.

03 Strengths

  • Clean dissociation (H3 positive / H1 null) argued on two bases (raw neurons + SAE features) and two sample sizes (5k, 25k) — the null is hard to dismiss as underpowering.
  • Unusual statistical honesty for a workshop paper: reports its own underpowered seed-level test (p=0.082) before the inflated n=60, runs explicit confound regressions, labels H2 honestly.
  • Public falsification of its own αeff scaling rule — converts a scale confound into a concrete constraint for future work.
  • Dose-response design (4 α values), monotone effects on H3 and L0, per-seed robustness checks.
  • Cheap and reproducible: one-line, <0.1k-param regularizer; TinyViT + all analysis on a single RTX 3050; code released.

04 Limitations

  • Single dataset, single family: ImageNet-100 + ViT only — untested on LLMs, where mech-interp actually lives.
  • H2 accuracy confound addressed, not resolved: −1.6 pp; R²=0.72; the accuracy-matched ablation is deferred.
  • The H1 null may be a metric failure: Mu’s 100-class basis cannot see features that cut across classes (textures, parts).
  • TinyViT probe is single-seed and confounds depth, width, accuracy and training budget simultaneously.
  • n=60 treats correlated class×seed pairs as independent despite only 3 checkpoints per α.
  • “Cluster” framing leans on a modest −6.1% spatial effect, and α=1.0 pays a real accuracy cost the original TopoLoss did not show.

05 Questions for further exploration

  1. Q1Accuracy-matched ablation: train the baseline to 59.6% — does the SAE-L0 reduction survive deconfounding?
  2. Q2Stronger patching baseline: replace the random set with a size-matched high-activation set at equal |Δlogit| — concentration, or blanket intervention sensitivity?
  3. Q3Controlled depth sweep (TinyViT → ViT-S → ViT-B at fixed width): does depth or total compute gate circuit formation?
  4. Q4Embedding-based monosemanticity score: would it overturn the H1 null, or is per-neuron disentanglement genuinely absent?
  5. Q5Transfer to LLMs: TopoLoss needs a defined unit ordering — and does the circuit-level gain make downstream interp measurably cheaper?
