Key Metric

0.204→0.815

Five-family mean agentic score, Qwen3.6-35B-A3B after 34 GRPO steps (+0.611) — past the Qwen3.7-Max reference (0.429) while written-out solving holds at 0.992.

Why This Matters

Attacks agentic RL's scarcest input — cheap, stateful environments with trustworthy rewards — and ships an instantly reusable F/I/A diagnostic for the can-solve-but-can't-act gap.

「先解题、再造环境」正中 agentic RL 的环境合成痛点；F/I/A 三呈现诊断对任何做 agent 训练的人都可立刻复用。

Critical Edge

Every headline delta rests on a single training run and a single evaluation seed — no error bars — and the decisive external win sits on an in-house benchmark with overlapping authorship.

Methodology — the mechanism-first pipeline

  1. Sample & solve

    Draw θ from 11 catalogued OR mechanism families (inventory DP, capacitated routing, negotiation, knapsack, LP/QP, scheduling) and solve M(θ) exactly — Bellman DP, Held-Karp, scipy LP/QP. No language model in the loop.

  2. Fix the reward first

    The solved pair (u*(θ), u₀(θ)) pins the outcome rule r = clip((u−u₀)/(u*−u₀), 0–1) — default policy scores 0, optimum scores 1. No judge model, no learned reward.

  3. Render, don't align

    A frozen setter LLM (Qwen3.7-Max) turns the solved draw plus one real corpus passage (28 domains) into a relational database, ≥10 executable tools, and a player instruction. θ stays hidden behind probe/commit actions that share one budget.

  4. Admit only what verifies

    Gates C1–C3: executability, a valid default region, and a replay driver that reproduces the precomputed optimum through the generated interface. Audit: 480 executions, zero exceptions; 240/240 repeated outcomes match.

  5. Train cheap

    GRPO on the scalar terminal reward: 34 steps, 16 rollouts per prompt, no KL penalty, 2,176 of 2,200 training environments consumed. Trainee: Qwen3.6-35B-A3B.

Strengths

  • Judge-free, cheat-resistant rewards for stateful multi-turn interaction — verified by replay audits (C1–C3, 480-execution held-out audit), not promised.
  • The F/I/A diagnostic is a clean, reusable instrument: it separates "can't solve the problem" from "can't act on it."
  • Confound controls beyond convention: matched-budget EnvScaler control, Oaxaca–Blinder decomposition, bootstrap CI on the KL shift.
  • Co-scaling shows the setter/trainee asymmetry is a feature — gains grow with environment size (+0.412 → +0.861).
  • Concrete cost accounting and reproduction-useful pipeline documentation (2,200 train / 300 held-out / 800 unseen partitions).

Limitations

  • One training run, one evaluation seed per benchmark — no error bars on any headline delta (self-reported, App. K).
  • Trained on only 3 of 11 families; near-OOD shares their coupled-budget structure; far-OOD is the modest honest number (+0.16).
  • All 11 families are textbook OR models: 28 corpus domains vary the skin, not the mechanism — open-ended environments are untested.
  • The decisive external win (3.4× storefront balance) is an in-house benchmark with overlapping authorship; the EnvScaler contrast is self-run at one starting scale.
  • Some families are scored against hindsight ceilings no online policy can reach — absolute scores are not commensurable.

Questions for Further Exploration

  1. Do the gains survive multi-seed training — what is the run-to-run variance behind +2.84 on BFCL V4?
  2. Does far-OOD (+0.16) close when 5–8 families become training targets, or does the 11-family catalogue cap out?
  3. Can the F−A gap measured on a candidate benchmark predict how much VHD-Play-style training will transfer?
  4. Does the reward normalization stay anchored when u*(θ) must be bounded rather than exact (large MILPs, stochastic control)?
  5. Is the growing-with-scale gain a property of the substrate, or of the 35B model's latent math competence?
