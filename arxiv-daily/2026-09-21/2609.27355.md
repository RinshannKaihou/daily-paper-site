> **一句话总结：** 针对"撤销学习（unlearning）的模型一经 4-bit 量化，'已遗忘'知识就会大量回归"这一部署级失效（MUSE-NEWS 上遗忘集知识保持率从全精度的 ~13% 跳升到量化后的 ~54%，约 4 倍回升），作者用 Taylor 展开证明失效根源是 forget 损失景观在收敛点附近过于尖锐（MUSE-NEWS 可视化显示 forget 盆地远比 retain 盆地陡峭），并提出两段式框架：(1) 以逐参数 Fisher 比值 ρ_i = F_f,i/(F_r,i+ε) 选出最敏感权重（仅 ~0.1%），在其上施加模拟量化网格取整的噪声正则把优化推向平坦盆地；(2) 按 S_l = G_{l,f}/G_{l,r} 选出 forget-critical 层、冻结其余层只更新少数层。在 MUSE-NEWS（LLaMA2-7B）上较此前唯一的量化感知撤销方法 SURE 将 retain−forget 差距最高提升 8.0%（NPO+KLR 4-bit：KnowMem_f 7.4→0.0 且 KnowMem_r 14.5→31.6），并在 AWQ/GPTQ 上把遗忘知识回收压低 8.0%–12.9%。

> **TL;DR:** LLM unlearning that "works" in full precision largely fails after 4-bit post-training quantization: on MUSE-NEWS, forget-set KnowMem recovers from ~13% to ~54% of the target model's knowledge (~4×). The authors attribute this to a sharp forget-loss basin at the unlearned checkpoint (visualized on MUSE-NEWS; retain landscape is far flatter) and prove a 1D quadratic theorem relating retain loss / forget gradient to the Fisher-curvature ratio F_f/F_r. Their fix has two parts: sensitivity-guided noisy regularization — perturb the top ~0.1% of weights (ranked by ρ_i = F_f,i/(F_r,i+ε)) with rounding noise matching the downstream quantizer's per-row scale, acting like SAM-style curvature regularization during forget-loss backprop — and forget-critical optimization, which updates only the top-K layers by gradient-score S_l = G_{l,f}/G_{l,r} and freezes the rest. Against SURE (the sole prior quantization-aware unlearning method) on MUSE-NEWS with LLaMA2-7B, the retain−forget gap improves by up to 8.0% (e.g., NPO+KLR 4-bit: forget KnowMem 7.4→0.0 while retain KnowMem 14.5→31.6); generalization to AWQ and GPTQ cuts forgotten-knowledge recovery by 7.7–12.9% with utility at or above full-precision baselines; on TOFU, critical-layer unlearning alone gains +14.5% (1B) / +19.0% (8B) utility at only +1.5% / +0.5% forget-score cost versus full-layer updates.

## 核心贡献

- **量化撤销失效的机理诊断**：首次从损失景观角度解释"量化 resurrect 遗忘知识"现象——对未学习解 W 处的量化扰动 ΔW 做 Taylor 展开 L(W_O) ≈ L(W) + gᵀΔW + ½ΔWᵀHΔW，指出失效由梯度范数 ‖g‖ 与 forget 损失 Hessian 曲线 H 共同决定；2D 随机方向可视化（MUSE-NEWS）显示 forget 损失在未学习解周围是尖锐盆地、retain 损失则平坦得多，且未学习解还被推离 retain 极小值（ζ = (θ + r₁ + r₂)）。
- **Theorem 1（机理→可操作判据）**：沿连接 retain/forget 两个局部极小（距离 d）的一维二次近似下，收敛解处 retain 损失 ∝ (λ/(F_r/√(F_f−λ)))² d²、forget 梯度 ∝ F_f/(F_r/√(F_f−λ)) d——即 F_f/F_r 大的参数同时导致遗忘鲁棒性差与效用差，为敏感度度量提供理论依据（证明在附录 A.3）。
- **敏感度引导噪声正则**：用小校准集上的对角经验 Fisher 定义逐参数敏感度 ρ_i = F_f,i/(F_r,i+ε)，取 top-τ（τ ∈ {0.05%, 0.10%, 0.50%}，0.10% 最佳）参数，在前向中按下游量化方案同款 per-row scale 做取整式扰动（模仿量化噪声、等价于对 forget 损失的曲率正则化），仅 ~0.1% 参数即可大幅提升鲁棒性。
- **Forget-critical 优化**：逐层梯度范数比 S_l = G_{l,f}/(G_{l,r}+ε) 挑出"对遗忘最关键、对保留影响最小"的 top-K 层（TOFU 1B 取 top-10/16，8B 取 top-20/32），只更新这些层、冻结其余，减少对保留知识的附带伤害，改善 forget–utility 权衡。
- **系统性验证**：覆盖 6 种撤销变体（GA/NPO × GDR/KLR 正则 + TOFU 上的 DPO）、MUSE-Books（ICLM-7B）/MUSE-NEWS（LLaMA2-7B）/TOFU（Llama-3.2-1B、Llama-3.1-8B，forget01/05/10），并从 bitsandbytes 4-bit 泛化到 AWQ 与 GPTQ。

## Key Contributions

- **Mechanistic diagnosis of quantization-induced unlearning failure**: via a Taylor expansion L(W_O) ≈ L(W) + gᵀΔW + ½ΔWᵀHΔW around the unlearned checkpoint, the paper shows failure is governed by the forget-loss gradient norm and curvature; 2D landscape visualizations on MUSE-NEWS reveal a sharp forget-loss basin versus a much flatter retain-loss basin — small 4-bit rounding perturbations inside the sharp neighborhood re-introduce forgotten behavior.
- **Theorem 1 (diagnosis → actionable criterion)**: in a 1D quadratic model along the line connecting the retain and forget minima (distance d), the converged solution suffers retain loss ∝ (λ/(F_r/√(F_f−λ)))² d² and forget gradient ∝ F_f/(F_r/√(F_f−λ)) d; parameters with large F_f/F_r hurt both forget robustness and utility, motivating the sensitivity measure (proof in Appendix A.3).
- **Sensitivity-guided noisy regularization**: a per-parameter sensitivity ρ_i = F_f,i/(F_r,i+ε) from diagonal empirical Fishers on small calibration sets; the top-τ fraction (τ ∈ {0.05%, 0.10%, 0.50%}, 0.10% best) of weights is perturbed during forget-loss backprop with rounding noise using the same per-row scale rule as the downstream quantizer — mimicking quantization noise and acting as curvature regularization, using only ~0.1% of parameters.
- **Forget-critical optimization**: layer-wise gradient-norm scores S_l = G_{l,f}/(G_{l,r}+ε) select top-K "forget-critical" layers (top-10/16 on TOFU 1B; top-20/32 on 8B) to receive unlearning updates while all remaining layers are frozen, reducing collateral damage to retained knowledge.
- **Systematic validation**: six unlearning variants (GA/NPO × GDR/KLR regularizers, plus DPO on TOFU) across MUSE-Books (ICLM-7B), MUSE-NEWS (LLaMA2-7B), and TOFU (Llama-3.2-1B-Instruct, Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct; forget01/05/10), with generalization from 4-bit bitsandbytes to AWQ and GPTQ.

## Methodology

The framework rests on the observation that post-training quantization injects a bounded, deterministic perturbation ε_Q(w) = Q(w) − w into every weight. Expanding the unlearning objective around the converged solution to second order shows the loss change under quantization depends on the gradient g = ∇L(W) and the effective curvature H of the forget-loss landscape. Case-study landscapes on MUSE-NEWS (contour plots over two random orthogonal directions) confirm that unlearning converges into a sharp forget basin whose loss rises rapidly under perturbation, while the retain landscape is flat.

**Step 1 — Sensitivity analysis.** Since the full Hessian is intractable at LLM scale, the diagonal empirical Fisher of the per-sample negative log-likelihood, F_ii(W) = (1/|D|) Σ g_i(x,y)², serves as a curvature proxy on a small calibration set. Theorem 1 (one-dimensional quadratic model connecting the retain minimum at 0 and forget minimum at distance d) shows that the converged solution's retain loss and forget gradient both scale adversely with F_f/F_r, so parameters with large Fisher ratio ρ_i = F_f,i/(F_r,i + ε) are "forget-sensitive."

**Step 2 — Noisy regularization on sensitive weights.** For each sensitive weight row w_{iρ}, weights are replaced in the backprop of the forget loss by a rounding-based perturbation ŵ = s_{iρ} · round(w_{iρ}/s_{iρ}), where s_{iρ} follows the same per-row scale rule as the deployment quantizer. This exposes training to the exact noise distribution quantization will later inject, acting as curvature regularization (in the spirit of Foret et al.'s SAM and Yang et al. 2022) that (i) moves w* closer to retain-loss minima, (ii) decreases the forget-loss gradient norm, and (iii) lowers forget Fisher values in sensitive directions. The selection ratio τ is swept over {0.05%, 0.10%, 0.50%}; 0.10% already yields the best forgetting-utility balance on both MUSE datasets.

**Step 3 — Forget-critical optimization.** Using lightweight first-order information, layer-wise gradient norms G_{l,f} = ‖∇_{θ_l} L_f‖ and G_{l,r} = ‖∇_{θ_l} L_r‖ are computed on mini-batches; layers are ranked by S_l = G_{l,f}/(G_{l,r} + ε) and only the top-K layers are updated by the unlearning objective (with noisy regularization), while all other layers stay frozen. Training uses AdamW (lr 1×10⁻⁵ for MUSE-NEWS; epochs 10–15 on MUSE-NEWS vs 1–5 on MUSE-Books; λ_ret ∈ {1,3,5}), with post-training 4-bit quantization (bitsandbytes as the default; AWQ and GPTQ for generalization; 8-bit is noted as nearly harmless). On TOFU the objective is DPO-based unlearning.

## Results & Highlights

- **Catastrophic recovery quantified**: on MUSE-NEWS, unlearned models retain on average only ~13% of the target model's forget-set knowledge in full precision, but this jumps to ~54% after 4-bit quantization — a nearly 4× recovery. Without regularization, NPO goes from Verb_f/Know_f/Know_r = 0.0/0.0/0.0 (FP) to 16.2/25.4/27.9 at 4-bit, and GA+KLR from 14.1/27.1/23.1 to 33.8/50.9/45.8 — forgetting largely erased.
- **Head-to-head vs SURE on MUSE-NEWS (LLaMA2-7B, 4-bit)**: the method improves the retain−forget gap (retain KnowMem − forget KnowMem) by up to 8.0% over SURE. NPO+KLR: Ours reaches Know_f 0.0 / Know_r 31.6 vs SURE's 7.4 / 14.5 (Verb_f 1.3 vs 12.2); GA+KLR: Ours 22.2/33.0/38.9 vs SURE 19.3/34.6/32.5 — SURE forgets slightly more verbately but retains far less knowledge. Under quantization, GA/NPO without regularization collapse, whereas this method stays closely aligned with the full-precision model.
- **Generalization to AWQ/GPTQ (Table 3, MUSE-NEWS)**: for GA+KLR, the method reduces VerbMem by 7.7% (33.7→26.0) under AWQ and 9.7% (33.2→23.5) under GPTQ, and KnowMem by 12.9% (49.8→36.9) and 11.4% (49.3→37.9); for NPO+GDR, VerbMem drops 8.2% (29.4→21.2, AWQ) and 8.0% (30.1→22.1, GPTQ), KnowMem 8.6% (49.6→41.0) and 11.1% (48.9→37.8) — with utility comparable to or above full-precision baselines.
- **Component ablations**: regularization alone outperforms SURE by ~5% on average on forgetting metrics (Table 2) but fails to fix the trade-off (GA+KLR utility on both MUSE datasets stays below SURE), motivating the second component. On MUSE-Books (ICLM-7B), the full method achieves ~15% stronger forgetting with ~5% better utility than SURE (Fig. 6: e.g., NPO+KLR forget KnowMem 37→17 while retain KnowMem 33→39).
- **Parameter efficiency and TOFU**: perturbing only ~0.1% of parameters (τ = 0.10%) already yields strong robustness on both MUSE datasets (Tables 4/5). Restricting updates to critical layers on TOFU, averaged over forget01+forget05, gains +14.5% utility (1B) and +19.0% utility (8B) over full-layer unlearning at only +1.5% and +0.5% forget-score cost; post-unlearning landscapes become visibly flatter with a broader uniformly-low-loss neighborhood (Fig. 4).

## Authors & Credibility

The author team spans the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill (Jialu Wang, Shuqing Luo, Song Wang, and corresponding author Tianlong Chen — a well-published group in efficient/robust ML and foundation-model compression) and the University of Pittsburgh (Jianing Deng, Yuanzhe Li, Dongwei Wang, Jingtong Hu, Huanrui Yang). Huanrui Yang's prior work on Hessian-enhanced robust optimization (HERO) and Dongwei Wang's noise-perturbation fine-tuning for robust LLM quantization are directly cited as foundations for the curvature-guided noise idea, so the core technique comes from authors with genuine quantization-robustness expertise. The paper is published as a conference paper at COLM 2026, a reputable venue for language-model research, and the experimental protocol follows the established MUSE (Shi et al., 2024) and TOFU (Maini et al., 2024) setups with full hyperparameter tables in the appendix.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty is real but recombinative.** The mechanism-level framing (sharp forget basins) is a genuine advance over SURE, which was an empirical fix. But each ingredient is imported: Fisher-ratio sensitive-weight selection and rounding-noise regularization come from the quantization-robustness literature (explicitly Wang & Yang 2024 "Taming sensitive weights"; Yang et al. 2022 HERO; Foret et al. 2020 SAM), and layer-selective tuning is a standard parameter-efficient trick. The composition is sensible; the Theorem 1 derivation is a heavily idealized 1D quadratic model that motivates ρ = F_f/F_r but makes no falsifiable predictions that are tested (e.g., the distance d between minima is never measured).
- **Thin baseline field.** SURE is the only quantization-aware unlearning baseline compared against. Missing comparisons include generic flatness regularization (SAM on the unlearning objective), quantization-aware training of the unlearned model, and relearn/attack-based evaluations — so it is unclear how much of the gain comes from the specific Fisher-ratio selection versus any curvature regularizer applied to the right weights.
- **Evaluation limited to behavioral metrics on synthetic/copyright benchmarks.** Forgetting is measured only via VerbMem/KnowMem/QA-ROUGE. TOFU is synthetic fictional autobiography, widely considered an easy unlearning target, and no adversarial extraction, beam-search attacks, or relearning attacks verify that knowledge is truly gone rather than merely suppressed — the very failure mode (quantization recovering "forgotten" content) suggests suppression-style metrics can be misleading.
- **Hyperparameter sensitivity and per-dataset tuning.** Epochs differ by 10× between MUSE-NEWS (10–15) and MUSE-Books (1–5), λ_ret and τ require per-dataset sweeps, and no variance over random seeds is reported anywhere. The "up to 8.0%" gap improvement is a best-case framing; typical per-setting gains are smaller (e.g., GA+KLR Know_r 32.5→38.9 is the strong case).
- **Deployment assumption.** The noise scale is matched to the downstream quantizer's per-row grid (bitsandbytes 4-bit); the AWQ/GPTQ transfer result mitigates but does not eliminate the dependence on knowing the deployment quantization scheme in advance.
- **Reproducibility.** Hyperparameter tables (Tables 6–9) and implementation details are commendably complete, but no code release is stated in the paper body I reviewed.

## Strengths

- Addresses a first-order practical problem: unlearned models are routinely deployed quantized, and the ~4× knowledge-recovery effect (13%→54% on MUSE-NEWS) is a striking, well-quantified motivation.
- Clean diagnosis-to-fix pipeline: the landscape analysis (Figs. 2, 4) directly produces the sensitivity criterion, and each component's contribution is isolated by ablation (Table 2 for regularization alone; Fig. 5 for critical-layer alone) — the authors honestly report regularization alone is insufficient.
- Consistent gains across six unlearning variants, three datasets, two model families on MUSE plus Llama-3.x on TOFU, and three 4-bit quantizers (bitsandbytes, AWQ, GPTQ) — much broader coverage than typical unlearning papers.
- Extremely cheap at deployment time: perturbing ~0.1% of parameters suffices (τ = 0.10% is the sweet spot), and critical-layer freezing reduces training cost while improving the trade-off (+19.0% utility at +0.5% forget cost on TOFU 8B).
- Complete hyperparameter reporting and a simple, implementable recipe built only on first-order/Fisher-diagonal information.

## Limitations

- Only behavioral forgetting metrics; no privacy attacks (extraction, relearning, membership inference) to confirm genuine knowledge removal rather than superficial suppression.
- SURE is the sole quantization-aware baseline; no SAM-on-unlearning, QAT, or other robustness baselines; the contribution of the specific Fisher-ratio selection versus generic noise injection is not disentangled.
- TOFU's synthetic nature and the dated MUSE models (ICLM-7B, LLaMA2-7B) limit external validity; no instruction-tuned flagship models on MUSE, and only one model per MUSE dataset.
- Theorem 1 assumes a 1D quadratic landscape with minima separated by a single distance d — assumptions never empirically validated; it motivates but does not quantitatively predict the method's behavior.
- Requires knowing the deployment quantizer's scale rule during unlearning; no variance/seeds reported; per-dataset hyperparameter re-tuning (10× epoch differences) hints at fragility; no compute-overhead accounting for Fisher/gradient profiling.

## Relevance

这篇工作与你关注的 LLM 高效部署与模型隐私方向直接相关：它把"撤销学习 + 后训练量化"这个此前只有一篇 SURE 涉足的交叉问题做成了机理性分析 + 通用框架，且所用工具（Fisher 敏感度、平坦性正则、层选择微调）都是可迁移到压缩/鲁棒性研究的通用技术。若你关心记忆化风险或量化鲁棒性，其损失景观诊断方法与"仅扰动 0.1% 参数即可钉住遗忘行为"的结论都值得借鉴。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the flatter forget basin survive stronger privacy probes — e.g., can GCG-style extraction or fine-tuning-based relearning attacks still recover the forgotten content from the 4-bit model?
- How does the framework behave at lower bit-widths (2/3-bit) or with activation quantization (SmoothQuant-style W8A8), where perturbation magnitudes are far larger?
- Is the Fisher-ratio sensitivity selection actually necessary, or would random 0.1% subset noise injection achieve similar flattening? A random-selection ablation is missing.
- Can the retain−forget minimum distance d in Theorem 1 be measured empirically, turning the theorem into a predictive tool for choosing λ and τ per model?
- How does the method interact with continual unlearning requests (multiple sequential forget sets), where repeated freezing of non-critical layers could starve capacity?
- Would combining this with output-side defenses (e.g., unlearning via contrastive decoding) yield robustness that does not depend on knowing the deployment quantizer in advance?
