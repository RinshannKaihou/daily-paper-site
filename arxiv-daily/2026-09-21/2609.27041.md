> **一句话总结：** 作者提出 generation-replay 协议——让模型先贪心生成解答，再对"提示词+生成序列"做一次 teacher-forced 重放，提取推理 token 上的激活-梯度重要性签名并无监督聚类；在 8 个开源数学模型 × 5 个数学数据源（共 30,460 次生成、40 个 model-source 单元）上发现：所有单元的聚类结构均显著优于同规模随机划分（p<0.005），两个独立前沿 LLM 评判官判定 77–82% 的真实簇呈现"解法连贯性"而同源对照组仅 6–11%，且改写题目措辞不改变簇归属（7/8 模型不变性 ≥0.70）而显式要求换解法会使簇迁移（shift 0.60–0.96）——说明 LLM 内部数学计算按推理方法（approach）而非题目主题（topic）组织。

> **TL;DR:** Across 8 open-weight math LLMs (Qwen2.5-Instruct 1.5B/7B/14B, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen 1.5B/7B/14B, Mistral-7B, Ministral-8B) and 5 math sources (GSM8K, MATH, DeepMind Mathematics, MathQA, NuminaMath, 3,800 prompts each), the authors cluster "generation-replay" activation-gradient signatures of the model's own solutions. Three pre-registered tests agree: (C1) every one of 40 model-source cells beats 200 matched-size random partitions (silhouette mean 0.204, p<0.005); (C2) frontier-LLM judges (Claude Opus-4.7 and GPT-5.4 independently) rate 82.3%/77.3% of the 225 real clusters as approach-coherent vs 6.2%/11.2% for within-source controls (gaps 76.0/66.1 pp); (C3) prompting for a different reasoning approach shifts cluster assignment (shift rate 0.60–0.96, 7/8 models confirmed) while paraphrases preserve it (invariance 0.70–0.84). A TF-IDF text-clustering baseline shows the opposite pattern (81.8% topic-pure, ARI 0.38 vs 36.9% and 0.10 for activations): surface text organizes by topic, internal activations by approach — so topic-stratified benchmarks and topic-balanced training corpora may miss the axis that actually governs math computation.

## 核心贡献

- **提出并检验一个明确问题：** 开源数学 LLM 的内部计算究竟按基准主题/子技能组织，还是按可复用的推理方法组织？据作者所知这是首次在"模型自己生成的轨迹"上系统对比这两种组织假说。
- **Generation-replay 签名方法：** 先用确定性贪心解码（8,196 token 预算）生成解答，再将精确的 prompt+生成 token 序列做单次 teacher-forced 前向/反向重放，仅对推理 token 计算 CLM 重放损失，并按模块提取三个特征（激活 L1 均值、RMS、激活×参数归一化梯度范数），拼接为签名；全程无监督，不使用任何主题、子技能、正确性或参考解标签。
- **三层验证协议（C1/C2/C3，阈值全部预注册）：** 结构性检验（匹配簇大小的随机划分对照）、语义性检验（两个不同厂家的前沿 LLM 独立评判 + 同源对照簇校准）、干预性检验（解法受控提示 vs 改写对照）。
- **反直觉的核心发现：** 225 个激活簇中仅 36.9% 主题纯净（竞赛类来源仅 1.2%），而文本 TF-IDF 聚类 81.8% 主题纯净——模型的"生成文本按主题组织、内部激活按方法组织"的解耦现象。
- **实践含义：** 按主题分层的基准与按主题平衡的训练语料可能漏掉真正重要的轴；论文发布完整 profiling/聚类/分析代码与评判 prompt，可复现。

## Key Contributions

- **A crisp, testable dichotomy:** whether math-capable LLMs organize internal computation by benchmark topic/sub-skill or by reusable reasoning approach — the first systematic comparison of the two hypotheses over model-generated (not reference) solution traces.
- **The generation-replay protocol:** greedy-generate a solution, replay the exact prompt-plus-generation sequence in one teacher-forced forward/backward pass, and extract per-module activation-importance signatures (mean L1, RMS, activation-times-normalized-gradient) over reasoning tokens only; clustering is fully unsupervised — topic, sub-skill, correctness, and reference-solution labels are never used.
- **A three-layer validation design with pre-specified thresholds:** structural (C1, matched-size random partitions), semantic (C2, two independent cross-family frontier-LLM judges calibrated with within-source control clusters), and interventional (C3, approach-controlled prompting vs paraphrase controls).
- **A dissociation result:** only 83/225 (36.9%) activation clusters are topic-pure (1.2% on competition-style sources), whereas TF-IDF clustering of the same traces yields 81.8% topic-pure clusters (ARI 0.38 vs 0.10) — generated text is topic-organized while internal activations are approach-organized.
- **Practical implication:** topic-stratified benchmarks, topic-balanced corpora, and topic-level score reports can miss the axis along which math computation is actually organized; approach-aware re-stratification needs no new annotations, only the released clustering pipeline.

## Methodology

**Models and corpora.** Eight open-weight math-capable models chosen to vary scale and training regime: three reasoning-distilled DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen models (1.5B/7B/14B), three instruction-tuned Qwen2.5-Instruct models (same scales), and Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3 plus Ministral-8B-Instruct. Five sources span an approach-constrained-to-naturalistic axis: DeepMind Mathematics (procedurally generated, 5 sub-skills, 1,000 prompts), GSM8K (grade-school arithmetic, 1 sub-skill, 200), MATH/Hendrycks (competition-style, 6 sub-skills, 1,200), MathQA (operation-annotated, 2 sub-skills, 400), NuminaMath-1.5 (competition-level, 5 sub-skills, 1,000). Up to 200 prompts per sub-skill yield a fixed balanced set of 3,800 prompts per model → 30,460 generations and 40 model-source cells; the identical prompt set is reused across all models so differences reflect trajectories/activations, not problems.

**Generation-replay signatures.** For each (model, problem): (1) generate a solution with greedy decoding, max 8,196 tokens; (2) replay the exact prompt+generation token sequence in a single teacher-forced forward/backward pass, removing sampling variance. Let R(x,y) be the generated reasoning-token positions (prompt tokens and post-answer-detector tokens excluded); the replay loss is the causal LM loss restricted to R. For each module m compute a_abs (mean ‖h‖₁/d_m over R), a_rms (root-mean-square analog), and an activation-times-gradient term i_m = a_abs · g_m where g_m is a parameter-normalized gradient norm; the signature z is the concatenation over modules. Raw gradient norms are never used standalone — they enter only through the product, keeping all analyses in one pre-specified feature space.

**Unsupervised clustering.** Each model-source cell is processed independently: ℓ₂-normalize → TruncatedSVD(20) → ℓ₂-normalize → K-Means with k ∈ {5,…,12} chosen by silhouette (10 initializations, fixed seed). Source, sub-skill, and correctness labels are withheld.

**Validation protocol.** C1 (structural): silhouette of the actual partition vs 200 random partitions preserving the cluster-size multiset, per-cell empirical p-value with finite-sample correction; a cell passes at p_emp < 0.05. C2 (semantic): for each of the 225 clusters, five nearest-centroid exemplars in SVD space are judged; each judge returns a candidate approach label, 3–5 features distinguishing it from sibling clusters, a count of how many exemplars follow the approach, a specificity judgment, and a confidence; generic labels ("algebraic manipulation") are explicitly rejected, and a label that merely recovers the dominant sub-skill does not count. To calibrate the judges, 80 within-source control bundles (five exemplars from *other* clusters of the same cell) are rendered identically. Pre-specified thresholds: real-cluster coherent rate ≥60%, control ≤20%, gap ≥40 pp; a cluster counts as coherent under a strict 4-of-5 exemplar rule. Two frontier judges from different families (Claude Opus-4.7, GPT-5.4, high reasoning effort) evaluate independently, never seeing each other's output. C3 (intervention): an approach bank of 50 base problems from the corpus is expanded into 50 baseline + 100 paraphrase + 130 approach prompts (280 per model, 8,192-token budget); the same replay profiler and clustering pipeline produce clusters, and a model condition is confirmed iff shift_rate ≥ 0.50 AND paraphrase_invariance ≥ 0.70 (rejected if shift < 0.30 or paraphrases shift as often as approach prompts).

**Dissociation control (Appendix A).** The identical clustering pipeline run on TF-IDF/SVD representations of the same generated traces tests whether activation structure is just surface-text similarity.

## Results & Highlights

- **C1 — structure is real everywhere:** across all 40 model-source cells the actual K-Means partition has higher silhouette than all 200 matched-size random partitions (raw exceedance 0/200 in every cell, p_emp = 1/201 < 0.005). Mean silhouette 0.204 (median 0.191, range 0.123–0.323); DeepMind Mathematics is highest (mean 0.283, tightly templated approaches), 225 clusters total, mean k = 5.62.
- **C2 — clusters are approach-coherent, controls are not:** under the strict 4-of-5 rule, Claude Opus-4.7 finds 185/225 (82.3%) real clusters coherent vs 5/80 (6.2%) within-source controls (gap 76.0 pp); GPT-5.4 finds 174/225 (77.3%) vs 9/80 (11.2%) (gap 66.1 pp). Both judges independently clear all three pre-specified thresholds (real ≥60%, control ≤20%, gap ≥40 pp) and recover the same source-level gradient: GSM8K highest (90.7%/88.4% real vs 6.2%/12.5% controls), DeepMind Math 87.0%/81.5%, MATH 77.5%/70.0%, NuminaMath 76.1%/71.7%, MathQA 78.6%/73.8%.
- **Topic purity — the dissociation:** only 83/225 (36.9%) activation clusters are topic-pure (dominant sub-skill purity >0.70), vs 81.8% topic-pure clusters (ARI 0.38) for TF-IDF/SVD text clustering of the same traces (activations: 36.9%, ARI 0.10). On the two competition-style sources only 1/86 (1.2%) clusters are topic-pure. Qualitatively, one DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B MATH cluster spans four sub-skills (precalculus, geometry, algebra, number theory) yet all five exemplars share the same constraint-to-equation reduction approach.
- **C3 — requested approach steers the cluster, wording does not:** shift rates range 0.60–0.96 across all eight model conditions and paraphrase invariance 0.70–0.84; seven of eight conditions are fully confirmed. The exception is Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3, which passes the shift criterion (0.96) but fails paraphrase invariance (0.48) — its signatures are more sensitive to surface wording.
- **Scale:** 3,800 prompts × 8 models = 30,460 generations profiled; reproduction costs one forward/backward pass per problem per model on a single 80 GB GPU (2–15 hours per model depending on size).

## Authors & Credibility

All four authors are at Clemson University (Sajad Goudarzi, Samaneh Zamanifard, Moloud Nasiri, Hamed Rahimian). The paper shows strong methodological discipline: pre-specified success thresholds stated before running the analyzers, matched-size random control partitions, cross-family double-judging to mitigate single-judge bias, and an explicit limitations section that names its own weaknesses (LLM judges instead of human annotation; prompt-based rather than mechanistic intervention). The supplementary material reportedly includes full profiling/clustering/analysis code, raw datasets, and LLM judge prompts with a README — a reproducibility package well above the norm for interpretability papers. The work sits at the intersection of population-level interpretability (probing/sae tradition — Burns, Cunningham, Gurnee are cited) and math-reasoning benchmarking (Saxton, Cobbe, Hendrycks, Amini, Li).

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty.** The question framing (approach vs topic as rival axes of internal organization) is genuinely crisp, and the generation-replay idea — profiling the *model's own executed* trajectory rather than reference solutions — is a sensible and well-motivated move given known CoT-unfaithfulness results (Turpin et al. 2023). The individual ingredients (activation norms, times-gradient importance à la Grad-CAM, SVD+K-Means) are all standard; the contribution is the protocol and the validation design, not new techniques.

**Rigor — mostly strong, with specific soft spots.** The matched-size random partitions in C1 are the right null, and the text-vs-activation dissociation (Appendix A) is the single most convincing control in the paper. But: (1) absolute structure is weak — mean silhouette 0.204 means clusters overlap heavily; the dichotomy rests more on C2/C3 than on geometry. (2) GSM8K's 100% topic purity (43/43) is vacuous — GSM8K has exactly *one* sub-skill label, so *any* partition of it is topic-pure; the paper concedes topic purity concentrates "where topic and approach are mechanically coupled," yet Table 3 still headlines the 100% figure, and the 36.9% aggregate is inflated by single/dual-sub-skill sources. (3) The semantics of "approach" are entirely judge-invented: two LLM judges (themselves LLMs judging other LLMs' traces, with acknowledged position/verbosity/self-enhancement biases in the related-work section) both author the labels and certify them, on only 5 nearest-centroid exemplars per cluster, with no human ground-truth annotation anywhere. (4) C3's approach bank is just 50 hand-expanded base problems (280 prompts/model) — targeted, not exhaustive, as the authors admit. (5) Missing analysis: correctness is deliberately excluded from clustering, and the paper never tests whether approach-cluster membership predicts accuracy or error modes — the evaluation payoff of the discovered axis is asserted, not demonstrated.

**Reproducibility.** Good on paper: full code, judge prompts, raw data, and per-model cost estimates (one forward/backward pass per problem, single 80 GB GPU, 2–15 h/model). The one-line caveat is a single fixed seed for clustering (10 K-Means inits mitigate within-seed variance).

**Significance.** If it holds up beyond math and open-weight models, the finding that topic-balanced corpora can still be approach-imbalanced is a real, actionable point for benchmark and data-curation design. But both the semantic claim (C2) and the transfer claim are one step removed from human verification.

## Strengths

- The core dissociation control — identical pipeline on TF-IDF text vs activation signatures of the same traces (81.8% vs 36.9% topic-pure, ARI 0.38 vs 0.10) — cleanly rules out "you're just clustering surface similarity."
- Pre-registered thresholds for all three tests, stated before the analyzers ran; matched-size random partitions preserve cluster-size multisets, blocking the "it's just imbalance" objection.
- Two independent cross-family frontier judges (Claude Opus-4.7, GPT-5.4) agree on both the aggregate pattern and its per-source ordering; within-source control bundles in identical format calibrate the judges against plausible-but-wrong coherence.
- C3 is a true intervention, not just correlation: requested approach shifts clusters (0.60–0.96) while paraphrases preserve them (0.70–0.84), and the one failing model (Mistral-7B) is reported honestly rather than hidden.
- Held-out prompt distribution (identical 3,800-prompt set across all 8 models) isolates model-specific trajectory/activation differences; comprehensive reproducibility package including judge prompts.
- Honest limitations section that anticipates most of the criticisms above.

## Limitations

- No human ground truth: "approach coherence" is certified exclusively by LLM judges on 5-exemplar bundles; the taxonomy itself is judge-generated, so C2 partly measures whether two LLMs can tell clusters apart, not whether humans would endorse the approach labels.
- Absolute geometric structure is modest (mean silhouette 0.204, range 0.123–0.323); clusters are real relative to the null but heavily overlapping, and k ∈ {5,…,12} selection by silhouette may favor partitioned readings of the space.
- The intervention is prompt-based: it shows the axis is behaviorally steerable, but identifies no parameters, heads, or circuits (explicitly acknowledged).
- GSM8K (single sub-skill) makes 43 of the 83 topic-pure clusters definitionally trivial; on genuinely multi-topic sources purity falls to 9.4%/1.2%, so the aggregate 36.9% overstates topic organization.
- Scope is restricted to open-weight *math-capable* models and five math sources; no evidence on closed models, non-math domains, or whether approach mixtures predict accuracy/error modes — the practical payoff (better evaluation/data curation) is proposed but untested.
- Correctness is excluded from signatures by design, so the paper cannot say whether approach-clusters separate correct from incorrect computation.

## Relevance

这篇论文与你关注的 LLM 推理与可解释性方向直接相关：它给出了"数学推理内部组织轴是解法而非主题"的干预式证据，对评测基准设计（按主题分层可能失真）和训练语料配比（主题平衡 ≠ 方法平衡）都有直接启示；其 generation-replay 签名 + 三层验证协议也可作为可复用的分析工具。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would human annotators endorse the judges' approach labels? A small human-validated subset of C2 bundles would convert the strongest caveat into evidence.
- Do approach-cluster memberships predict error modes or accuracy — i.e., is the axis not just descriptive but *causally* tied to success, as the Discussion hypothesizes?
- Can the approach axis be localized mechanistically (specific heads/MLP blocks), connecting this population-level signature to circuit-level accounts?
- Does the dissociation (text topic-organized, activations approach-organized) hold for closed frontier models, code generation, or non-mathematical multi-step reasoning?
- If corpora were rebalanced over recovered approach clusters rather than topics, would downstream math accuracy or generalization measurably improve?
- What explains Mistral-7B-Instruct's failure of paraphrase invariance (0.48 vs ≥0.70 elsewhere) — training regime, tokenizer, or scale?
