# What Converges in the Platonic Representation Hypothesis? Structure over Geometry

> **一句话总结：** 针对"校准后表征收敛只剩局部邻域一致（mKNN）"的最新质疑，本文构建 2×2 受控框架（局部/全局 × 关系结构/度量几何），用 H₀ 骨架重叠（等价于最小生成树边重叠）作为 mKNN 的全局对应物，发现在 204 个视觉-语言模型对上关系结构在局部和全局尺度都稳健收敛（校准 mKNN 与 H₀ 重叠的 Spearman ρ=0.968），而随着距离一致性要求变严（τ 从 1 收紧到 10⁻⁴），显著的全局对齐比例从 ~100% 跌至 40.2%（局部仍保持 89.7%）——即"收敛的是关系结构，而非度量几何"。

> **TL;DR:** The paper challenges the recent "Aristotelian" reading of the Platonic Representation Hypothesis (Grøger et al., 2026) that convergence survives calibration only in local neighborhoods (mKNN). It argues prior local-vs-global comparisons confound **structural scale** with **what is compared** (relational structure vs metric geometry), and builds a controlled 2×2 framework on the 1,024-sample WIT subset of the PRH benchmark: mKNN (local structure) is paired with a new **H₀ skeleton overlap** — MST-edge overlap, since H₀ death edges of the Vietoris-Rips filtration coincide with MST edges — and both get distance-aware variants whose stringency is set by τ. Across 204 vision-language model pairs (12 LMs × 17 ViTs), relational structure converges at **both** scales after aggregation-aware permutation calibration (Spearman ρ=0.968 between the two measures), whereas increasingly stringent distance agreement flattens capacity-dependent convergence and kills significance: at τ=10⁻⁴, 89.7% of pairs keep significant local alignment but only 40.2% keep significant global alignment. The pattern persists under a Riemannian metric approximation and in 143 video-text model pairs. Code: https://github.com/junwon0/what-converges-prh

## 核心贡献

- **重新审视"局部邻域"解释：** 指出 Grøger et al. (2026) 的"校准后仅剩局部收敛"结论混淆了两个维度——比较的结构尺度（局部 vs 全局）与比较的内容（关系结构，即哪些样本相关 vs 度量几何，即距离/相似度/相关性的数值），先前 local-global 对比无法区分二者。
- **构建 2×2 受控比较框架：** 局部关系结构用 mKNN；提出 **H₀ 骨架重叠**（S_H₀）作为其全局对应物——利用 Vietoris-Rips 滤波的 H₀ 死亡边与最小生成树（MST）边重合这一经典结果（Proposition 1，Kruskal/Skraba），只比较"支撑全局连通结构的样本对身份"，不要求边长一致，从而与 mKNN 在"只看关系身份"这一点上严格匹配。
- **距离感知变体统一结构与几何：** 对共享关系（共享邻居 / 共享 MST 边）施加权重 w = exp(−|log D̃_X − log D̃_Y|/τ)，τ 控制距离一致性严格程度；τ→∞ 时严格退回纯结构度量（公式 6），从而可以在"保持关系不变"的前提下平滑地从结构对齐过渡到几何对齐。
- **主要实证发现：** 关系结构在局部和全局尺度都呈现容量依赖的稳健收敛（校准后全部 204 对仍显著，且两尺度强相关 ρ=0.968）；而距离一致性要求越严，容量依赖的收敛越弱、显著对齐越少，且在最严区间全局比局部更脆弱。
- **鲁棒性与推广：** 该"结构 vs 几何"模式在黎曼度量近似（局部协方差构造）下复现，并在 143 个视频-文本模型对上复现，说明不是欧氏度量或图像-文本设定的特例。

## Key Contributions

- **Revisiting the local-neighborhood interpretation of convergence:** shows that the recent claim "calibrated convergence is only local" rests on comparisons that confound structural scale (local vs global) with what is compared (relational structure vs metric geometry).
- **A controlled 2×2 framework:** introduces H₀ skeleton overlap — overlap of minimum-spanning-tree edge identities (n−1 edges per model), proven equivalent to the H₀ death-edge set of the Vietoris-Rips filtration under deterministic tie-breaking — as a global counterpart to mKNN, matched in using relation identities only.
- **Distance-aware variants with a single stringency knob:** an exponential weight on the gap between log-normalized distances (90th-percentile normalization) shared relations receive; τ→∞ recovers the pure structure measures, so structure-vs-geometry can be swept without changing the underlying relations.
- **Empirical verdict:** relational convergence extends beyond local neighborhoods to global spanning structure (robust to calibration, capacity-dependent at both scales), while increasingly stringent metric agreement substantially weakens convergence and differentially erodes global significance.
- **Robustness beyond Euclidean and beyond image-text:** same structure-geometry pattern under a Riemannian metric approximation built from local covariances, and in video-text representations.

## Methodology

**Setup.** Two representations X, Y of the same n samples with pairwise distance matrices D_X, D_Y. The 2×2 design crosses structural scale (local / global) with comparison content (relational structure / metric geometry). Data: the 1,024 paired image-text samples from the WIT subset of the PRH benchmark (Huh et al., 2024; Grøger et al., 2026). Models: 12 language models (BLOOM, OpenLlama, and LLaMA families) × 17 vision transformers spanning ImageNet-21K (MAE, DINOv2, and CLIP variants), yielding 204 model pairs; for video-text, 143 model pairs (VideoMAE, DINOv2, CLIP families).

**Relational structure (identity-only) measures.** Local: mutual k-NN overlap S_mKNN = (1/n) Σ_i |N_X(i) ∩ N_Y(i)|/k with k=10. Global: H₀ skeleton overlap S_H₀ = |E_H₀^X ∩ E_H₀^Y|/(n−1), where E_H₀ is the set of H₀ death edges from the Vietoris-Rips filtration; Proposition 1 (via Kruskal's construction and Skraba et al., 2020) shows the death-edge set coincides with the MST edge set under a fixed deterministic tie-breaking rule, so S_H₀ = |T_X ∩ T_Y|/(n−1) measures agreement on which sample pairs form the global spanning structure, ignoring edge lengths (deliberately unlike RTD-lite, which uses MST edge lengths).

**Distance-aware (metric geometry) variants.** Distances are normalized by their 90th percentile (D̃ = D/Q₀.₉); a shared relation (i,j) gets weight w_ij^(τ) = exp(−|log D̃_X(i,j) − log D̃_Y(i,j)|/τ). Smaller τ demands tighter distance agreement; τ→∞ recovers S_mKNN and S_H₀ exactly. The distance-aware versions weight only relations shared by both representations (shared neighborhoods / shared MST edges), keeping structural support fixed. Swept τ ∈ {1, 0.1, 0.01, 0.001, 0.0001}. Robustness: a cosine-similarity analogue (Appendix F.2) and alternative normalization quantiles (Appendix F.1).

**Aggregation-aware permutation calibration.** Following Grøger et al. (2026): per model pair, take the maximum alignment over all layer pairs; the null is 500 permutations of sample correspondences (max over layer pairs each); calibrate as T_cal = max((T_obs − ε_α)/(1 − ε_α), 0) with ε_α the (1−α) quantile, α = 0.05; Benjamini-Hochberg FDR control across the 204 pairs, plus family-wise permutation tests for capacity trends (Appendix B/E).

**Beyond Euclidean geometry.** A Riemannian metric approximation is constructed from local covariance information (Singer & Coifman, 2008; Berry & Sauer, 2016) while keeping relation identities fixed, so only the geometry used to judge distance agreement changes (Appendix G).

## Results & Highlights

- **Relational structure converges at both scales.** On the 204 vision-language model pairs (1,024 WIT samples), 100% of pairs remain significant for both calibrated mKNN and H₀ skeleton overlap after Benjamini-Hochberg correction (α=0.05, 500 permutations); the two measures are strongly associated at Spearman ρ = 0.968 (Figure 2c). Calibrated alignment rises with language-model capacity and vision-encoder size for both local and global measures — the capacity-dependent convergence signature Grøger et al. attributed to local neighborhoods extends to global spanning structure.
- **Strict distance agreement collapses metric-geometry convergence.** At τ = 1, local and global distance-aware scores retain 85.6% and 83.0% of their relational-structure baselines; this falls to 39.2% and 34.9% at τ = 10⁻¹, and to 5.8% and 5.5% at τ = 10⁻² — local and global decay at nearly identical rates (Figure 3a).
- **Global significance is differentially fragile in the stringent regime.** All 204 pairs stay significant for local alignment through τ = 10⁻³, while significant global alignment falls to 98.5% at τ = 10⁻², 85.3% at τ = 10⁻¹, and 40.2% at the strictest setting (τ = 10⁻⁴), versus 89.7% for local (Figure 3b). The capacity-dependent sweep becomes progressively less pronounced as τ tightens (family-wise permutation tests, adjusted q < 0.05 for both vision and language capacity at loose-to-moderate τ).
- **The pattern survives a non-Euclidean metric.** Under the Riemannian metric approximation, at τ = 10⁻² only 3.2% (local) and 3.0% (global) of the relational-structure baseline alignment remains — closely matching the Euclidean decay — and the capacity-dependent trend again flattens under stringent agreement (Figure 4a).
- **Video-text replication.** All 143 video-text model pairs show significant calibrated alignment for both mKNN and H₀ overlap with similar capacity-dependent patterns; at τ = 10⁻⁴, 86.0% of pairs retain significant local alignment vs only 46.9% for global (Figure 4b-c).

## Critical Assessment

- **The core measure is more classical than it sounds.** H₀ skeleton overlap is, by the paper's own Proposition 1, minimum-spanning-tree edge overlap — a direct relative of RTD-lite (Tulchinskii et al., 2025) with edge lengths discarded. The novelty is the *controlled framing* (matched support, identity-only vs distance-aware), not a new statistic; a skeptic can reasonably say the paper's contribution is a careful re-interpretation of known tools.
- **Possible power asymmetry behind the headline divergence.** mKNN summarizes ~n·k = 10,240 neighbor memberships while H₀ overlap rests on only n−1 = 1,023 MST edges, and the permutation null is built per-measure. Under extreme τ, all weights shrink toward zero and the calibrated score's dynamic range collapses; the finding that *global* significance dies first (40.2% vs 89.7% at τ = 10⁻⁴) could partly reflect the MST statistic's lower effective power under the shared permutation machinery rather than purely representational facts. The paper controls FDR per level but does not (in the main text) equalize statistical power between the two measures — this is the missing ablation I would most want.
- **Complete dependence on the Grøger et al. calibration.** All conclusions are conditional on aggregation-aware permutation calibration being the right null; if that procedure over- or under-conserves alignment, the structure-vs-geometry split inherits the bias. The paper mitigates this by adopting the critic's own methodology (a good-faith choice) but offers no independent validation of the null itself.
- **Small fixed sample.** Everything runs on n = 1,024 WIT samples with k = 10 fixed; MST overlap on a thousand points is a coarse summary of "global" structure, and no sample-size or k sensitivity appears in the main narrative. The word "global" here means "spanning tree over 1,024 points," not dataset-scale structure.
- **Scope of geometry claims.** The Riemannian extension is a single local-covariance approximation, and the authors honestly flag in the conclusion that H₀ is only one form of global relational structure (no higher homology, no other global summaries). Minor sloppiness: the τ ladder in Section 4.3 contains an inconsistent exponent ("40.2% at τ = 10⁻⁵" in one sentence vs 10⁻⁴ as the strictest swept value elsewhere).

## Strengths

- Genuinely controlled experimental design: the 2×2 decomposition plus the τ-interpolation (Eq. 6) cleanly separates "which relations are selected" from "how far apart they are," which is the right way to adjudicate this debate.
- Adopting the critic's own benchmark (WIT/PRH subset), models, and permutation calibration makes the rebuttal apples-to-apples rather than a strawman.
- Statistically careful: 500 permutations with layer-pair max aggregation, BH FDR control across all 204 pairs, family-wise capacity tests, cosine-similarity and normalization-quantile robustness checks.
- The Proposition 1 (H₀ death edges = MST edges) gives the global measure an exact, cheap, deterministic implementation and a clean conceptual match to mKNN.
- Two independent generalizations (Riemannian metric; video-text modality) support the central structure-over-geometry claim, and code is released.

## Limitations

- Restricted to H₀ spanning structure as the notion of "global"; richer topology (H₁ and above) and other global structures may behave differently — acknowledged by the authors.
- Metric agreement is evaluated only on relations already shared by both representations (fixed-support formulation); distance agreement on non-shared relations is never tested.
- Single Riemannian approximation from local covariances; treated as one alternative geometry, not a validated intrinsic metric.
- All multimodal (image-text and video-text); no unimodal vision-only or text-only model-pair sweeps in the main analysis, so the "Aristotelian" critique is only tested in multimodal settings.
- Practical guidance is limited: the paper shows *what* converges but the strict-τ regime's behavioral relevance (does metric-geometry divergence matter for downstream transfer/stitching?) is unaddressed.

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the global-significance collapse at strict τ (40.2% vs 89.7% local at τ = 10⁻⁴) persist when the permutation null is power-matched between mKNN (n·k relations) and MST overlap (n−1 edges)?
- How do higher homology groups (H₁ loops, not just H₀ spanning trees) or alternative global structures (e.g., diffusion-based neighbors at larger radii) behave under calibration and stringent metric agreement?
- Is metric-geometry divergence behaviorally meaningful — e.g., does it predict failures in model stitching, cross-model transfer, or representation-equilibrium claims?
- Would the structure-over-geometry split hold for unimodal language-model-only or vision-only model families, where the original PRH evidence was strongest?
- The distance-aware weight uses a 90th-percentile normalization and log-gaps; how sensitive is the τ-sweep to this parametrization versus, e.g., rank-correlation-based distance agreement?
