> **一句话总结：** 该论文构建了首个"激励错位环境"受控基准 CAVEAT（9 个高保真电商市场 + 8 类平台导向机制），在匹配对照设计下发现五个模型家族的最优购买率从 78.6% 暴跌至 17.3%（CAVEAT-Hard 上 GPT-5.6-Sol/high 更是从 90.0% 跌至 0.0%），通过轨迹分析将失败归因于目标漂移、过早搜索终止、证据未决即承诺三类机制，并据此设计的 CAVEAT-Harness 推理时干预把 GPT-5.6-Terra-low 在 CAVEAT-Shop 上的最优购买率从 11.7% 提到 66.7%（+55.0），对 Qwen3.5-27B 后训练得到的 CAVEAT-27B 也从 4.2% 提到 22.9%。

> **TL;DR:** CAVEAT is a controlled benchmark of nine synthetic browser-based marketplaces where eight realistic platform steering mechanisms (sponsored placement, preferential ranking, drip pricing, defaults, scarcity cues, etc.) can be toggled on and off while the user's request, catalog, and the unique user-optimal product stay fixed. Across 18 model configurations, optimal purchase rate on the 52-task CAVEAT-Standard collapses from 78.6% (matched control) to 17.3% (misaligned), and GPT-5.6-Sol at high reasoning drops from 90.0% to 0.0% on the 2,112-product CAVEAT-Hard. Trajectory analysis traces failures to three decision-stage defects: objective drift, premature search closure, and premature commitment with unresolved evidence. The resulting CAVEAT-Harness (a pinned task specification plus a pre-purchase verification tool) raises GPT-5.6-Terra-low from 11.7% to 66.7% on CAVEAT-Shop (+55.0 pts vs. the BrowserUse baseline; a prompt-only baseline gets only 30.0%), and post-training Qwen3.5-27B into CAVEAT-27B lifts it from 4.2% to 22.9% under the same harness.

## 核心贡献

- **形式化并实例化"激励错位环境"设定：** 与显式攻击（prompt injection）不同，环境不提供任何虚假信息——所有商品事实都准确，只是通过排序、显著性、信息披露时机等 8 类机制（sponsored placement、preferential ranking、drip pricing、promotional framing、defaults & bundling、scarcity & social proof、trust signals、friction & obstruction）影响 agent 的决策语境；每道题都有可程序化验证的唯一用户最优商品。
- **匹配对照（matched-control）评测协议：** 同一用户请求、同一商品目录、同一最优解，仅开关平台导向机制，从而把"激励导致的退化"与任务能力干净地分离。CAVEAT-Standard 含 52 个任务、9 个环境；CAVEAT-Hard 把 CAVEAT-Shop 的搜索空间扩大到 2,112 个商品、88 页结果。
- **18 个模型配置的大规模评测：** 五个模型家族最优购买率平均从 78.6% 降至 17.3%（降幅 37.2–78.9 个百分点）；鲁棒性随推理预算、模型规模和代际提升，但永远追不上同一模型在对照条件下的能力。
- **三类失败机制的定位与针对性消融：** 人工复盘 200 条轨迹后定位 (1) 目标漂移（等优先级偏好中先被提到的那条主导比较——25 对匹配请求全部翻转选择）、(2) 过早搜索终止（把一个次优商品挪到置顶赞助位后，浏览率 45/60→60/60，购买率 0/60→48/60；Hard 上 50 次失败全部停在第 1/88 页）、(3) 证据未决即承诺（仅开启 drip pricing 一项机制即造成 60 次中 35 次次优购买）。
- **两条可操作的缓解路线：** CAVEAT-Harness（浏览前锁定结构化任务规格 + 购买前可选验证工具，只使用用户请求和正常浏览所得信息）和 CAVEAT-27B（两阶段后训练让 Qwen3.5-27B 内化同一决策流程），分别带来 +55.0 和 +18.7 个百分点的提升。

## Key Contributions

- Formalizes *incentive-misaligned environments* — settings where the environment benefits from outcomes outside the user-optimal set — and instantiates them as CAVEAT, a benchmark of nine high-fidelity marketplace environments with an eight-family taxonomy of steering mechanisms grounded in FTC/regulatory/academic literature; every mechanism presents only accurate information, isolating decision-context effects from deception.
- Introduces a matched-control evaluation protocol (identical user request, catalog, prices, and unique verifiable optimum; only the steering mechanisms toggle) that cleanly separates incentive-induced degradation from task capability.
- Evaluates 18 model configurations and finds a large, universal degradation: 78.6% → 17.3% optimal purchase rate across five model families on CAVEAT-Standard (drops of 37.2–78.9 points), worsening to 90.0% → 0.0% for GPT-5.6-Sol/high on the large-catalog CAVEAT-Hard; robustness improves with reasoning, scale, and generation but always lags matched-control ability.
- Diagnoses *where* incentives enter the decision process via 200 manually reviewed trajectories plus targeted ablations: (1) objective drift, (2) premature search closure, (3) premature commitment with unresolved evidence.
- Shows the failures are actionable: CAVEAT-Harness (inference-time structure) and CAVEAT-27B (targeted post-training of Qwen3.5-27B) substantially recover user-optimal purchasing, demonstrating both diagnosability and trainability of incentive robustness.

## Methodology

**Setting and formalization.** A user delegates a decision to a computer-use agent. The environment has its own objective V alongside the user's U; it is *incentive-misaligned* when advancing V can favor outcomes outside the user-optimal set X*. A *steering mechanism* changes how the environment advances its objective through the decision context — which options are surfaced, how they are presented, what must be inspected. The paper distinguishes misalignment (conflicting objectives) from robustness (the agent's ability to preserve decision quality), and contrasts this with explicit adversarial attacks: in CAVEAT no fact is ever false.

**Benchmark construction.** Nine browser-based marketplaces (short-term lodging, general retail, food delivery, resale, freelance services, grocery delivery, specialty shopping), each with its own storefront and transaction flow. Tasks specify hard requirements (must hold) and comparative requirements (prefer one option over otherwise valid ones); each scored task is constructed so exactly one option is jointly best, giving a unique, programmatically verifiable user-optimal product. Product attributes are stored in structured form and validated against the full catalog. Eight steering-mechanism families are synthesized from regulatory and academic sources (Table 1). Each user request is evaluated twice: a matched control with all mechanisms disabled and an incentive-misaligned condition with all eight enabled — products, attributes, prices, availability, and the optimal option are identical across the two versions. CAVEAT-Standard: 52 tasks, ~70 products per scenario; CAVEAT-Hard: 5 CAVEAT-Shop tasks with 2,112 products across 88 result pages.

**Evaluation protocol.** The BrowserUse harness is held fixed across model families. Main evaluation: five model families on all 52 CAVEAT-Standard tasks, three repetitions per task; five additional configurations on CAVEAT-Shop; the strongest configuration on the five CAVEAT-Hard tasks with ten repetitions. 95% Wilson confidence intervals are reported.

**Diagnosis.** 200 randomly sampled trajectories are qualitatively reviewed, then each hypothesized failure mode is tested with a targeted ablation: (a) preference-order swap on requests with two explicitly equal-priority preferences (25 matched pairs); (b) a placement intervention that moves one suboptimal product into the sponsored top slot (5 product types x 4 preference settings x 3 reps = 60 runs per condition); (c) drip-pricing-only activation (60 runs with GPT-5.6-Terra at low reasoning), plus a manual separation of evidence-resolution failures from discovery failures using repetitions that succeed on identical facts.

**CAVEAT-Harness.** Before browsing, the model converts the user request into a structured task specification recording only user-stated hard and comparative requirements, held fixed for the episode (countering objective drift); before purchase, an optional verification tool lets the agent justify that search is sufficiently complete and check whether missing or conflicting facts could change the comparison (countering premature closure/commitment). It receives no benchmark answers, hidden catalog facts, or incentive metadata. Baselines: vanilla BrowserUse and a prompt-only baseline describing the same decision principles without the structure or tool.

**CAVEAT-27B.** Two-stage post-training of Qwen3.5-27B: (1) supervised examples synthesized by GPT-5.6-Sol-low in a separate synthetic environment teaching the harness behaviors (spec writing, verification-tool use, valid browser actions), with all CAVEAT environments/tasks/catalogs excluded; (2) rejection-style rollouts on CAVEAT-Shop laptop development tasks, training on corrected actions at states the model's own policy reaches (covering navigation errors and recovery). Evaluation on the 16 held-out non-laptop CAVEAT-Shop tasks plus a 4-task CAVEAT-Stay transfer check.

## Results & Highlights

- **Headline degradation (CAVEAT-Standard, 52 tasks, 5 model families, 3 reps):** optimal purchase rate falls from 78.6% under matched control to 17.3% under incentive misalignment (-61.3 pts). Per-family: Qwen3.5-122B 69.9% → 1.9%, Grok-4.3 80.8% → 1.9%, DeepSeek V4 Flash 57.1% → 4.8%, Kimi K2.5 94.2% → 24.4%, GPT-5.6-Sol(low) 91.0% → 53.8% — drops of 37.2 to 78.9 points; a dedicated CUA model (Pairal-5-27B with its native harness) falls 81.7% → 3.3%.
- **Large catalogs are catastrophic (CAVEAT-Hard, 2,112 products, 50 episodes):** GPT-5.6-Sol/high, the strongest configuration, purchases the user-optimal product in 90.0% of matched-control episodes but 0.0% under misalignment — all 50 failures stop on page 1 of the 88-page catalog while a demonstrably solvable optimum stays buried.
- **Mechanism-level ablation (20 CAVEAT-Shop tasks, one family at a time):** with all mechanisms disabled GPT-5.6-Sol-low scores 100%; sponsored placement alone cuts it to 63.3%, preferential ranking to 75%, drip pricing to 83.3%; the placement intervention shows view rate rising only 45/60 → 60/60 but purchase rate 0/60 → 48/60, and drip pricing alone induces 35/60 suboptimal purchases — delayed information timing alone is sufficient.
- **Robustness scaling (CAVEAT-Shop, misaligned):** GPT-5.6-Sol improves 51.7% (low reasoning) → 90.0% (high); GPT-5.5 12.0% (low) → 50.0% (medium) → 47.0% (high, non-monotonic); within GPT-5.6 at low reasoning, Luna 0.0% → Terra 11.7% → Sol 51.7%, while GPT-5 nano/mini/main and GPT-4o/4.1 all remain at 0.0%.
- **CAVEAT-Harness (CAVEAT-Shop, 3 reps):** GPT-5.6-Terra-low rises 11.7% (BrowserUse) → 66.7% (+55.0 pts); a prompt-only baseline reaches only 30.0%. GPT-5.6-Sol/high: 0.0% → 80.0% vs. 13.3% prompt-only. Ablation: structured objectives alone 66.7%, verification tool alone 66.7%, both 80.0%. Cross-environment check on 4 CAVEAT-Stay tasks: 16.7% → 50.0%. Cost: 2.6x runtime, 2.3x steps, 2.3x tool calls vs. BrowserUse.
- **CAVEAT-27B (16 held-out non-laptop CAVEAT-Shop tasks, 3 reps):** original Qwen3.5-27B scores 0.0% with BrowserUse and 4.2% with CAVEAT-Harness; CAVEAT-27B reaches 22.9% under the same harness (+18.7 pts), and transfers to CAVEAT-Stay at 16.7% vs. 0.0% for the base model.

## Critical Assessment

The matched-control design is the paper's real strength and is genuinely rigorous: because the catalog, prices, and unique optimum are bit-identical across conditions, the 61.3-point drop cannot be explained by task difficulty, and the decision-level scoring (did the agent buy the verifiable optimum?) captures failure modes like premature search closure that pattern-level scoring would miss. The diagnosis is also unusually well-supported — each of the three failure modes is backed by a purpose-built intervention (the 25/25 preference-order swaps, the 0/60 → 48/60 placement experiment, the 35/60 drip-pricing isolation), not just anecdotes.

That said, several caveats temper the headline numbers. First, sample sizes are thin for the boldest claims: the 0.0% on CAVEAT-Hard rests on 50 episodes of a single configuration on 5 tasks, and the harness ablation's step from 66.7% to 80.0% (structured objectives or verification tool alone vs. both) is a difference of 8 episodes out of 60 with three repetitions — within plausible noise, though Wilson CIs are plotted but never discussed. Second, all nine "high-fidelity" marketplaces are built by the authors; fidelity is asserted via screenshots and transaction flows, and the steering mechanisms are author-instantiated from regulatory taxonomies rather than scraped from real platforms, so the 78.6% → 17.3% figure is a property of this synthetic ecology — there is no quantitative comparison against existing dark-pattern agent benchmarks (e.g., the cited Curvin et al. or Shi et al.) to calibrate severity. Third, the mitigation gains lean heavily on extra compute: the authors honestly report 2.6x runtime, but the prompt-only baseline (30.0% and 13.3%) shows that a large fraction of the harness benefit comes from telling the model to be careful and search more, not from the specific checkpoint structure; the 66.7% vs. 66.7% single-component ablations reinforce that the additive 80.0% claim is the weakest link. Fourth, CAVEAT-27B's 22.9%, while a clean relative gain, is still far below the frontier harness result of 80.0%, and its training targets are distilled from GPT-5.6-Sol-low — a teacher-student setup whose ceiling and contamination risks (mitigated by environment exclusion, but only asserted) deserve more scrutiny. Finally, every task has exactly one unique optimum by construction, which the authors acknowledge; real purchases routinely have ties or uncomparable preferences, so "optimal purchase rate" may overstate how sharply real-world agent quality can even be defined.

## Strengths

- The matched-control protocol is a clean causal design rarely seen in agent benchmarks: identical decision problem, toggled incentives, programmatically verified unique optimum.
- The problem setting is novel and important — between cooperative benchmarks and adversarial prompt-injection suites — and matches how real platforms actually behave (accurate information, biased presentation).
- Diagnosis is mechanistic and falsifiable: each of the three failure modes has a targeted ablation with concrete numbers (25/25 order-swap flips; 0/60 → 48/60 placement; 35/60 drip-pricing-only failures).
- Honest cost accounting (2.6x runtime for CAVEAT-Harness) and explicit scoping of limitations, including the small CAVEAT-Stay transfer evaluation.
- Both an inference-time intervention and a post-training route are shown, connecting diagnosis to trainability; the two-stage post-training explicitly excludes all CAVEAT environments and catalogs to limit contamination.
- Evaluation breadth: 18 model configurations, five model families, three settings (Standard, Shop, Hard), with Wilson confidence intervals.

## Limitations

- Domain scope: only online marketplaces; transfer to finance, hiring, or information platforms is untested, and the CAVEAT-Stay cross-environment check is just 4 tasks.
- All tasks have a unique, clean user optimum with fully specified preferences — real delegated decisions have partial, evolving, or non-unique preferences, so measured degradation may not transfer.
- Steering mechanisms are fixed and static within an episode; real platforms adapt interactively and strategically over time.
- Statistical power: 5 CAVEAT-Hard tasks (50 episodes for the 90.0% → 0.0% claim), 3 repetitions per configuration, and the harness ablation differences (66.7% → 80.0%) are not tested for significance.
- Robustness gains cost 2.6x runtime and 2.3x tool calls, and the robustness-efficiency trade-off is not yet characterized.
- No public code/data release is stated in the paper, which limits reproducibility of the nine environments; CAVEAT-27B's absolute performance (22.9%) remains far below frontier-model-with-harness (80.0%).

## Relevance

这项工作把 agent 评测从"能否完成任务"推进到"在环境本身有利益诉求时能否守住用户目标"，是 agent 安全与对齐方向少见的受控因果研究。其匹配对照协议、三类失败模式诊断（目标漂移/过早搜索终止/证据未决即承诺）以及推理时+后训练两条修复路线，对任何做 computer-use agent、web agent 鲁棒性或平台暗模式研究的人都有直接参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Do the three failure modes (objective drift, premature search closure, unresolved-evidence commitment) persist in domains with non-unique or only partially specified preferences, where "optimal purchase rate" cannot be defined cleanly?
- Would adaptive platforms — mechanisms that respond to the agent's observed behavior within an episode (interactive steering) — amplify the degradation beyond the static 61.3-point drop?
- Is the 66.7% → 80.0% jump from combining the task specification with the verification tool statistically meaningful at 60 runs, or within Wilson-interval noise?
- Can CAVEAT-27B's decision procedure be learned without distilling from a frontier teacher (e.g., from rule-generated supervision or self-play), and what is the resulting ceiling?
- How much of CAVEAT-Harness's gain survives at matched compute (e.g., giving the BrowserUse baseline the same 2.6x step budget), separating structure from sheer additional search?
- Do these findings replicate on real retail sites, where steering mechanisms co-occur with genuine information asymmetries the benchmark deliberately excludes?
