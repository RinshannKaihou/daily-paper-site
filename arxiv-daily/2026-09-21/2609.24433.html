02  Why this matters

A rare complete path from quantization theory to a native INT4 TensorRT engine and closed-loop real robots — a directly reusable recipe for 4-bit robot-policy deployment.

No retraining anywhere: calibration-time folding + GPTQ only. The transfer recipe — folding consistency, keep o_proj/down_proj at INT8, per-token activation scales — comes with published failure archives.

03  Critical edge

The 92.5% real-robot headline is task-weighted: on ALOHA T1, selective INT8 scores 75.0% vs 85.0% for uniform W4A4 (95.0% BF16) — a regression the paper does not explain.

W4A4 also fails outright on SmolVLA (37.1% vs 71.1% BF16) — consistent folding is not a universal recipe.

Methodology the folding contract

  1. One contract per activation site. A single invertible transform T = D₂ᵀ·R·D₁ (diagonal×rotation×diagonal — only identity-like forms survive ReLU / residuals / attention dot-products) is folded into every consuming weight (W̃ = W T⁻¹) at calibration and executed identically at runtime. Prop 1: bit-exact functional equivalence under integer execution.
  2. Rotation + scaling. Block Hadamard rotation via FWHT (β=64, O(d log d), norm-preserving) + SmoothQuant-style channel scales; normalization gains folded outside the RMS denominator.
  3. Per-token activation quant. Motivated by an audit: worst N1.6 down_proj spread hits 587,588× max/median per token (median layer: 8,650×) — static scales cannot survive.
  4. GPTQ in transformed coordinates (INT8 weights via RTN), INT32 accumulation, clipping ratio 4–8; Prop 2 (timestep invariance) covers the flow-matching action expert.
  5. Selective INT8. o_proj + down_proj stay W8A8 — largest spread, feed the residual stream; costs +1 ms on Orin.
  6. Native engine. TensorRT 10.3 plugins, CUTLASS v3.6.0 (m16n8k64 INT4 MMA), packed INT4 weights in backbone and action expert. Vision encoders, norms, residual arithmetic and the decoder stay floating point.

Strengths

  • Native, not emulated: CUTLASS INT4 MMA inside both backbone and action expert — 2.22× measured vs 1.03× for HoloQ-VLA-style emulation.
  • Decisive cost/benefit point: +1 ms of selective INT8 buys +12.5 pp pooled real-robot success, overtaking BF16 PyTorch and float TensorRT.
  • Principled treatment of flow-matching experts: Prop 2’s timestep invariance means no per-step scale tables; the 587,588× spread audit motivates it concretely.
  • Unusually candid: publishes failure archives, competitor re-run gaps (94.0 vs 96.0), exploratory-test caveats; declines to claim simulator superiority.
  • Breadth with matched controls: 4 checkpoints, 2 embodiments, sim + real, preset-matched arms, deterministic rebuild gates before closed-loop evaluation.

Limitations

  • Architecture-dependent: SmolVLA collapses (37.1 vs 71.1) and Evo-1 degrades (83.0 vs 91.6); no diagnostic or fix is established.
  • Thin statistics where claims are strongest: 20 trials/task, single 40-state campaigns, fidelity from 32 observations, exploratory headline test.
  • Confounded ablation: o_proj-INT8 and down_proj-INT8 evaluated jointly; the ALOHA T1 regression (75.0 vs 85.0) is unexplained.
  • Hardware coverage: native-INT4 latency claims hold on Ada/Orin only — H100 lowers to INT8; single desktop timing run per checkpoint.
  • Large FP residue: vision encoders, norms, residual arithmetic and the decoder stay floating point; speedups do not transfer automatically to other architectures or GPUs.

Questions for further exploration

  1. Do o_proj-INT8 and down_proj-INT8 contribute independently, and can the ALOHA T1 regression be attributed to one specific site?
  2. Is there a cheap offline diagnostic predicting which VLA architectures survive W4A4, before running 800-episode campaigns?
  3. Does Prop 2’s timestep invariance extend to per-step conditioning, or to autoregressive / non-flow action experts?
  4. Can native INT4 survive on H100/B200-class hardware, and how do folding transforms interact with FlashAttention-style fused kernels?
  5. Would behavior-aware metrics (action smoothness, contact forces) change the ranking that binary success rates cannot resolve?
