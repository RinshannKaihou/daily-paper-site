Why this matters

An auditable self‑improvement loop: habits mined from the agent’s own traces trade wasted re‑derivation for consistency and ~38% cheaper tokens.

可审计的自进化闭环：以确定性习惯换取一致性与成本下降，并把「坏习惯」的静默失效当作一等公民来度量。

Critical edge

The coverage guard is the one component exempt from the system’s own C0–C3 gates — and gate C2 is structurally blind to 11 of 13 wrong‑parameter serves.

One silent failure: correct template, confidently returned 0 where the gold answer was 19.

Methodology — mine, gate, dispatch

1 · Mine
Forge candidates from the trace spine. Replay of historical traces (entropy freezes near‑deterministic junctions) → counterfactual / perturbed replay (coverage‑border probe) → bounded simulation. Guardrails are mined from corrections but enter only by human authorization.
2 · Gate
Four admission gates, C0–C3. C0 executability probe · C1 guardrail subset check · C2 one‑sided non‑inferiority permutation test against the reference’s own run‑to‑run variance (α=0.05, Benjamini–Hochberg) · C3 human‑terminated production A/B. Admission cost scales O(capabilities): humans review capability classes, not each candidate.
3 · Dispatch
Skill = variant lineage, not one mutable artifact. Walk from the lowest ring up; the first guard that admits serves. The anchor — slowest ancestor — is never pruned and bounds the blast radius of a wrong habit to the slice it claims.
4 · Price
Economics denominated in tokens. Promotion pays when N·cR > cA + N·cG + n·cF; authoring measured at 7,691 tok/template, guard‑cost law fitted at R²=0.999.
Evaluated on 14 templates × 3 instances × 3 repeats = 504 calls across four reasoning arms, plus a frozen 288‑question retrieval‑routing pool · the guard’s price is measured alongside → see Results

Strengths

  • The problem is quantified before it is sold: 38–74% self‑disagreement and 95.3–97.2% planning‑token waste, per arm.
  • 456/456 bit‑exact replay is verifiable by any buyer or auditor — no intervals or noise floors needed.
  • Failures measured with the same care as wins: CIs on false admissions, a boundary stress set, a structural proof of C2 blindness, and the hazard condition isolated (adjacency + catalog > 20).
  • Statistical hygiene rare in industry agent papers: pre‑specified endpoints, BH correction, refusal to claim the sub‑floor +0.071 severe‑error shift.
  • Economics fully denominated in tokens and dollars, down to a guard‑cost law at R²=0.999.

Limitations

  • Both workloads are author‑adjacent — a self‑authored 42‑question SQL set and a 288‑question pool with the authors’ own labels; no external benchmark, no Voyager‑style baselines.
  • The 456/456 claim is partly substrate: 14 deterministic templates make replay near‑tautological once the script is right, on a workload where planning “bought nothing.”
  • The coverage guard is ungated and unsupervised after training; the routing guard cannot decline at all, so silent wrong serves are structurally guaranteed somewhere.
  • No production deployment — C3 and the recovery loop are specified, not evaluated; four framework quantities explicitly unmeasured.
  • Savings shrink toward zero against the least‑wasteful planner: only +6.5% net at 100 reuses vs Sonnet.

Questions for further exploration

  1. Can the coverage guard itself be brought under C0–C3 without an infinite regress of guard‑guarding‑guard?
  2. Does the 2.6% natural‑traffic false‑admission rate hold on non‑templated workloads, or past a 30‑template catalog?
  3. The t=100→t=200 dose contrast bounds any scaling effect near zero — does the habit curve hold at 10,000 production traces?
  4. What re‑admission trigger on catalog change catches the 15.8% joint‑condition hazard at acceptable O(capabilities) human cost?
  5. What is the measured cost of adding a refusal path to the routing guard — coverage, latency, and the C3 legible‑delta pipeline?
  6. Does “split routing from extraction, 0.888→0.952 at 43% cost” transfer to agentic tool‑use traces beyond SQL?
