01 Methodology training-free · 2-bit KV
01

Diagnose K vs V decoupling

Toggle Key/Value bits (K16V2 vs K2V16) on top of QVG’s centroid–residual scheme. Key MSE is 33× smaller than Value MSE, yet Key-only quantization does 3.3× more output damage — small K errors reorder attention logits and drift which temporal-spatial tokens each query selects.

02

QSAC sensitivity-aware clustering

Assign Keys to centroids that minimize expected attention-logit perturbation, not reconstruction error: per-channel Query sensitivity from running second moments (diagonal E[q²]) combined with residual-group dynamic range via the Widrow bound; shortlist top-M=4 candidates.

03

PSAC rank-8 logit compensation

Historical Query energy concentrates in few directions (top-8 eigendirections hold 60–99%). Store the residual Key error projected onto that subspace as INT8 low-rank factors and correct QKᵣ directly at inference; inactive for the first chunk to keep strict causality.

04

System co-design Triton · bit-packing

Four INT2 codes per byte, fused Triton kernel rebuilds BF16 K/V on demand into native FlashAttention/SDPA, preserving each model’s RoPE and cache-layout conventions. No calibration phase.

Beyond PSNR

  • VBench preserved, not traded: matches or exceeds BF16 on all five models — HY-World 1.5 0.8173 vs BF16 0.8106; Matrix-Game-2 0.7836 vs 0.7831.
  • 1-minute long videos: beats QVG everywhere, e.g. HY-World 1.5 0.6627 vs QVG 0.5949 and above BF16’s 0.6567 — robust to error accumulation.
  • Ablations (HY-World 1.5): QSAC alone 17.990 PSNR, PSAC alone 17.849, both 18.449; rank r=8 is the sweet spot; exhaustive M=256 search buys no quality over M=4 at 2× latency.
03 Strengths
  • Sharp, falsifiable diagnosis: K/V decoupling tables and patch-selection visualizations move the objective from reconstruction error to attention-logit preservation.
  • Principled, training-free method: QSAC’s score derives from expected squared logit perturbation (Widrow bound + Query second moments), not hand-tuning; PSAC exploits a measured 60–99% Query-energy concentration.
  • Real system, not simulation: bit-packed storage, fused Triton on-demand reconstruction, native RoPE/layout preservation, strict causality, zero calibration.
  • Uniform gains: every frame-level metric beats QVG and KIVI on all five models at both resolutions, with VBench intact or slightly above BF16.
  • Honest, thorough ablations plus a candid account of when latency improves vs degrades.
04 Limitations
  • Flicker is never measured directly — no temporal-consistency metric or human study; frame metrics only score fidelity to BF16 outputs (subtle circularity).
  • Only two baselines; the concurrent attention-bias-correction approach targeting the same failure mode is never benchmarked.
  • Long-horizon evaluation reports VBench only, omitting frame-level metrics where error accumulation would show most.
  • Scope confined to causal, chunk-cached autoregressive models; full-attention diffusion generators untested.
  • +5.4–5.9% latency when GPU-resident, and the wins lean on custom fused kernels; no code-release statement, error bars, or seed variance in the main text.
