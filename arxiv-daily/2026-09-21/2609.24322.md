> **一句话总结：** 作者发现量化后分类精度"看起来无损"的模型，其检索 top-1 结果已被悄悄改变 33–46%（同一 checkpoint 做分类仅改变 3–6%，差 5–23 倍），且 nDCG@10/Recall@1 等聚合指标完全掩盖了这一损伤；他们用 top-1/top-2 分数差（gap）这一免标签量解释并预测该现象（2,118 个配置上 Spearman −0.88），并给出两个修复：按层 gap 敏感度分配位宽（3.5 bit 时回收 60–73% 的 W3→W4 收益，超越重建误差等全部 7 个前向准则），以及把低 gap 输入以 conformal 阈值路由回全精度（路由 25% 输入即可恢复 85–93% 的量化精度损失，优于 MSP 与预测熵）。

> **TL;DR:** Across 53 fine-tuned checkpoints (ViT-B/16, ViT-L/16, Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B/4B/8B, GTE, BGE, E5, CLIP) and six weight-only quantizers (RTN, HQQ, GPTQ±act-order, AWQ, AdaRound), W4 quantization changes the top-1 of 33–46% of retrieval queries while the same checkpoint used as a classifier changes only 3–6% (e.g., ViT-B/16: 45.5% retrieval vs 6.2% classification, a 7.9× asymmetry), while nDCG@10 moves by only 2.7–7.6%. A single label-free quantity — the top-1/top-2 score gap — predicts the damage (Spearman −0.88 over 2,118 configurations) and yields two fixes: gap-sensitivity bit allocation at a 3.5-bit budget recovers 60–73% of an extra bit's benefit (vs 40–48% for reconstruction-error allocation), and conformal gap-thresholded routing to the full-precision model recovers 85–93% of lost classification accuracy by routing just 25% of inputs.

## 核心贡献

- **发现"精度保持、排序已坏"的不对称性**：同一 checkpoint、同一量化器、同一位宽下，W4 round-to-nearest 使 top-1 改变率在分类任务上仅 3–6%，在检索任务上达 33–46%（Table 1：ViT-B/16 分类 6.2% vs 检索 45.5%，7.9×；ViT-L/16 为 1.9% vs 27.1%，14.8×；Qwen3-Emb-0.6B 为 4.9% vs 46.2%，9.8×）。标准聚合指标严重低估：CLIP ViT-L/14 W4 的 Recall@1 仅降 1.3%，但 24.6% 的 top-1 结果已改变；Qwen3-Emb-8B W4 的 nDCG@10 仅降 3.1%，但每 6 个原本命中相关文档的查询就有 1 个丢失。
- **机理解释：top-1/top-2 分数差**。交叉熵训练把正确类推离次优类（gap 大），对比学习检索只要求正例与负例分离、从不约束第一名与第二名之间的距离（gap 小）：gap<2ε 的"竞争者集合"在 74.3% 的 ViT-B/16 分类输入上为空，而在检索查询上从不为空。分类的中位 separation ratio 为 5.73，检索仅 0.081。Lemma 1 给出确定性保证（g₂≥2ε 则 top-1 在任意 ‖δ‖∞≤ε 扰动下不变），split-conformal 校准进一步给出分布无关的错误率上界 α。
- **一个量预测两个任务族的损伤**：在 2,118 个（模型×任务×量化器×位宽）配置上，中位 separation ratio 与 top-1 改变率的 Spearman 达 −0.88（单看 gap 只有 −0.58，单看扰动幅度只有 +0.05）；而精度损失、语料规模（放大 58×）、模型规模（13×）、更强量化器（GPTQ/AWQ/HQQ/AdaRound）都无法解释或消除该现象——GPTQ 把 ViT-L/16 分类精度从 55.5% 恢复到 88.6%、改变率降 11 倍，但同模型 W3 检索仍改变 78.9% 的 top-1。
- **两个即插即用的修复**：（1）检索——按层 gap 敏感度分配位宽，3.5 bit 平均预算下在全部 6 个量化器上都排 7 个前向-only 准则第一，回收 60–73% 的 W3→W4 收益（重建误差准则只回收 40–48%）；（2）分类——用量化模型自身的 gap 做 conformal 路由，路由 25% 输入即恢复 85–93% 的精度损失，FP 算力开销仅为 47%。

## Key Contributions

- **A blind spot, quantified**: the same checkpoint at W4 changes its top-1 answer for 3–6% of inputs as a classifier but 33–46% as a retriever (ViT-B/16: 45.5% vs 6.2%, 7.9×; ViT-L/16: 14.8×; Qwen3-Emb-0.6B: 9.8×), while nDCG@10 falls only 2.7–7.6% — CLIP ViT-L/14 loses 1.3% Recall@1 while 24.6% of top-1 results change, and one in ten correctly-answered queries loses its image.
- **A mechanism with a guarantee**: the top-1/top-2 gap. Lemma 1: if g₂(x) ≥ 2ε the top-1 survives every perturbation with ‖δ‖∞ ≤ ε; the contender set is empty for 74.3% of classification inputs but no retrieval queries (median separation 5.73 vs 0.081); a split-conformal threshold τ calibrated on 128 unlabeled queries gives a distribution-free violation bound (α = 0.10: 85.2% of classification inputs accepted at 1.47% violations).
- **One predictor across everything**: median separation ratio correlates with top-1 change rate at Spearman −0.88 over 2,118 configurations; accuracy loss (+0.327, not significant within deployed settings), corpus size (58× sweep), model scale (13×), and stronger quantizers all fail to explain or remove the effect — the same failure re-emerges in a quantized cross-encoder reranker (5–10% flips at W4, Spearman −0.943).
- **Two fixes from one number**: (1) retrieval — allocate bit-width by per-layer gap sensitivity, recovering 60–73% of the W3→W4 benefit at 3.5 bits, first among all seven forward-only criteria under every quantizer; (2) classification — route inputs with quantized gap below a conformal threshold to full precision, recovering 85–93% of lost accuracy at 47% of FP cost, beating MSP and predictive entropy at nearly every operating point.

## Methodology

- **Models and tasks**: 53 fine-tuned checkpoints. ViT-B/16 and ViT-L/16 fine-tuned on 21 image classification tasks (CIFAR-10/100, EuroSAT, Food101, OxfordIIITPet, StanfordCars, TinyImageNet, etc.); Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B fine-tuned on 11 MTEB tasks (Banking77, Emotion, IMDb, MTOPDomain, etc.); later additions: GTE-large, BGE-large, E5-large-v2, Qwen3-Embedding-4B/8B, zero-shot CLIP ViT-B/32 and ViT-L/14 on 4,000 Flickr30k images, and the ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2 cross-encoder reranker. The key control is **self-retrieval**: each ViT is retrieved against its own test split, so the same checkpoint produces the classification and retrieval numbers — only the readout differs (linear probe vs cosine nearest-neighbor).
- **Quantization**: weight-only, 4 and 3 bits, one scale per output channel (group size 128), every linear layer. Quantizers: RTN, HQQ, GPTQ (with and without activation ordering), AWQ, AdaRound (the latter three calibrated on 512 documents; calibration data always disjoint from evaluation).
- **Measure**: top-1 change rate versus the full-precision model (deliberately label-free — a flip between two relevant documents still counts); damage against BEIR relevance judgements (NFCorpus, SciFact, SCIDOCS, FiQA) is reported separately via nDCG@10 and gold-document loss.
- **Theory**: a perturbation with ‖δ‖∞ ≤ ε can overtake the winner only if the top-2 gap g₂ < 2ε (Lemma 1; the contender set C₁(x) = {k : g_k(x) < 2ε}). The separation ratio g₂/2ε is the stability margin. A split-conformal threshold on unlabeled calibration queries turns the deterministic condition into a per-input accept/reject test with P(accepted ∧ top-1 changed) ≤ α. Proofs are checked by exhaustive enumeration (Appendix B).
- **Fix 1 (retrieval allocation)**: run the FP model on n = 128 calibration queries (L−1 forward passes per layer-quantization probe); sensitivity sₗ = median |g₂(x) − g₂^(l)(x)|, the gap shift when only layer ℓ is rounded to 3 bits; spend bits greedily up to a 3.5-bit average budget. Compared against five forward-only criteria (activation norm, SQNR, depth heuristic, activation-weighted error, activation salience) plus the reconstruction-error objective, under all six quantizers.
- **Fix 2 (classification routing)**: route input x to the FP model iff the *quantized* model's own gap g₂^(PTQ)(x) < τ (τ = 25th percentile on an unlabeled slice; the conformal variant calibrates τ for a guaranteed violation rate ≤ α). No training — one tuned hyperparameter; up to order-equivalence with MSP since g₂ is the only score whose threshold set matches the worst-case-flippable set.

## Results & Highlights

- **The asymmetry is large and consistent**: W4 RTN group_128 changes the top-1 of 6.2% of ViT-B/16 classification inputs vs 45.5% of retrieval queries on the same checkpoint (7.9×); ViT-L/16: 1.9% vs 27.1% (14.8×); Qwen3-Emb-0.6B: 4.9% vs 46.2% (9.8×). In the strictest control (one encoder, one dataset, one quantizer, two readouts), Banking77 W4 changes 4.8% of classification top-1s vs 32.0% of retrieval top-1s; Emotion W3: 44.2% vs 97.6%.
- **Aggregate metrics hide the damage**: Qwen3-Emb-8B W4 loses only 3.1% relative nDCG@10 while 21.6% of top-1s change and 15.6% of queries whose top result was relevant lose it; CLIP ViT-L/14 W4 drops Recall@1 by just 1.3% (59.8%→59.1%) while 24.6% of top-1 results change. At W3, Qwen3-Emb-0.6B nDCG@10 collapses from 0.409 to 0.141 (−68.9%) — an unusable retriever — while the classifier survives; 79.6% of gold top-1 documents are lost.
- **The gap predicts damage; nothing else does**: Spearman between median separation ratio and top-1 change rate is −0.88 over 2,118 configurations (gap alone: −0.58; perturbation size alone: +0.05; accuracy loss: +0.327, undetectable within either vision backbone). Fitting on the RTN points (r = −0.860) predicts the 34 points of each of the five other quantizers at R² = 0.60–0.74.
- **Stronger quantizers do not fix retrieval**: GPTQ(act-order) restores ViT-L/16 classification accuracy from 55.5% to 88.6% and cuts the classification change rate eleven-fold, yet W3 retrieval on the same model still changes 78.9% of top-1s. All five advanced quantizers reduce the change rate but none approaches the stability threshold on retrieval.
- **Gap-sensitivity allocation wins everywhere**: at a 3.5-bit budget it captures 73.4% (RTN), 72.8% (HQQ), 60.2% (GPTQ), 63.2% (GPTQ-act-order), 60.7% (AWQ), 69.4% (AdaRound) of the W3→W4 benefit — leading the next-best criterion by 5.5 points (AWQ) to 18.4 (RTN) and beating every competitor in ≥16 of 21 configurations. In absolute terms under RTN: top-1 flip 33.3% vs 43.1% for reconstruction-error allocation, gold top-1 lost 13.8% vs 19.9%, Recall@1 48.5% vs 46.0% (all-W4 reference: 23.5% flip). On CLIP it captures 56.4% vs 34.9% for reconstruction error. Layer-level reconstruction error is nearly uncorrelated with gap sensitivity (mean Spearman −0.04 on text embedders), explaining why the standard objective spends bits in the wrong places.
- **Routing recovers accuracy cheaply**: routing the 25% lowest-gap inputs to FP recovers 84.8% (ViT-B/16), 92.5% (ViT-L/16), 87.8% (Qwen3-Emb-0.6B) of the accuracy lost to quantization, at 47% of FP compute (one quantized pass over everything plus one FP pass over the routed quarter, using GPTQ's reported 4.5× speedup). Recovery rises smoothly from 62–76% at 10% routed to 99–98% at 50–69%. The gap beats MSP and predictive entropy at nearly every operating point (e.g., ViT-L/16 at the 25th percentile: 92.5% vs 83.6% MSP and 69.0% entropy).

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty is real**: quantization papers almost universally certify with perplexity or accuracy; reframing deployment risk as per-query rank stability, and connecting it to classical measurement theory (semioders, Luce 1956; supes 1930/Zipfprajn 1930; Fishburn 1970), is a genuinely fresh and useful lens. The self-retrieval design (same embeddings read through a probe and through cosine NN) is the cleanest possible isolation of the task-family variable — Table 8 shows 4.8% vs 32.0% on Banking77 with *everything* else held fixed.
- **The mixed-precision comparison pool is self-restricted**: the headline "outperforms every criterion under every quantizer" holds only among six *forward-only* criteria. HAWQ/HAWQ-v2 (Hessian-based mixed precision) are cited as related work but excluded because they need backward passes — a defensible deployment constraint, but it means the strongest published allocators were never benchmarked, so "every criterion" overstates the evidence.
- **The headline number counts neutral flips**: "14 to 46% of top-1 retrieval results" includes the 68.8% of W4 flips that move between documents carrying no relevance judgement; the authors' own analysis shows the mean relevance of the served document falls by only 0.010 at W4. The practical harm is real (15.6% judged-relevant loss for Qwen3-Emb-8B) but smaller than the abstract's framing suggests — to their credit, the appendix states this plainly.
- **Small retrieval corpora**: the four BEIR corpora range from ~1k to 57k documents. The corpus-size sweep (up to 58×) partially addresses scale, but production corpora with approximate nearest-neighbor indexes are explicitly out of scope — and ANN ranking errors could interact with the contender-set mechanism in unstudied ways.
- **The routing fix's edge over MSP is thin on some backbones**: on Qwen3-Emb-0.6B at the 25th percentile, MSP recovers 88.2% vs 87.8% for the gap (Table 14). "At nearly every operating point" is accurate but the gap's advantage is not uniform, and the paper is right to hedge.
- **For retrieval, the stability check is a diagnosis, not a fix**: it accepts essentially no retrieval queries at W3/W4 (11.3% at W4, 0% violations), so per-query trust exists only in classification; retrieval safety relies on the global allocation, which offers no per-query guarantee.
- **Scope limits**: weight-only quantization at 3–4 bits with group size 128; activation quantization, KV-cache quantization, and QAT are untested; the conformal guarantee assumes calibration/deployment exchangeability and degrades under distribution shift (acknowledged in Limitations).

## Strengths

- A crisp, label-free diagnostic (top-2 gap) that costs nothing extra — the FP pass for calibration is already part of every PTQ pipeline — with both a deterministic guarantee (Lemma 1) and a distribution-free conformal variant.
- Exceptionally clean causal design: one checkpoint, one dataset, one quantizer, two readouts; plus systematic elimination of rival explanations (accuracy, corpus size, model scale, quantizer strength, reranking architecture).
- Both fixes are drop-in: the allocation criterion plugs into any quantizer (RTN, GPTQ, AWQ, HQQ, AdaRound all improve); routing needs no training, one threshold.
- Honest reporting throughout: states where the gap loses to MSP, that most flips are between equally-relevant documents, that W3 retrieval is unusable regardless of quantizer, and separately scores against BEIR judgements rather than hiding behind agreement-with-FP.
- Breadth and rigor: 53 checkpoints, six quantizers, two modalities plus a reranker, 2,118 configurations, machine-checked proofs, and full scripts for every reported number.

## Limitations

- Weight-only quantization at 4 and 3 bits only; activation quantization and quantization-aware training are untested, leaving W8A8-style LLM deployments unexamined.
- The conformal stability check assumes calibration and deployment inputs share a distribution; the guarantee degrades under distribution shift, which is common in production retrieval traffic.
- Evaluation uses exact cosine similarity over a finite corpus; approximate nearest-neighbor indexes (and their own ranking errors) are out of scope.
- Text retrieval rests on four small BEIR corpora; the allocation benchmark excludes backward-pass/Hessian-based mixed-precision methods (HAWQ family), so the allocation's superiority is established only within the forward-only pool.
- In retrieval the stability check accepts almost no queries, so it cannot provide per-query assurances where the damage is worst; and the ViT self-retrieval setting, while controlled, is a constructed rather than natural retrieval benchmark (mitigated by the CLIP/Flickr30k arm).

## Relevance

这篇论文与用户对高效推理/量化技术和 embedding 检索系统的兴趣高度契合：它揭示了一个极易被忽视的部署盲点——量化后 aggregate 指标几乎不动但检索排序已大面积损坏——并给出可直接落地的按层位宽分配与 conformal 置信路由两个修复。任何打算把检索/embedding 模型压到 4-bit 以下上线（RAG、以图搜图、reranker 服务）的工程决策都应先读这篇文章。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the top-2-gap mechanism survive activation quantization (W4A8/W8A8) and KV-cache quantization in full LLM-plus-retriever stacks, where perturbations compound across components?
- Can training-time objectives (e.g., an explicit margin on the top-2 gap during contrastive training) engineer the separation that retrieval currently lacks — the open question the authors pose in the conclusion?
- How quickly does the conformal calibration break under realistic query drift between calibration and production traffic, and can it be recalibrated online?
- Does gap-sensitivity bit allocation transfer to approximate nearest-neighbor indexes (HNSW/IVF-PQ), where embedding perturbation and index approximation interact?
- Would a joint policy — mixed-precision retrieval with per-query FP fallback for low-gap, high-value queries — dominate either fix alone, and how should it be priced against SLA latency budgets?
