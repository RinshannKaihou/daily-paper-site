> **一句话总结：** 该研究发现，压制大模型"自我意识"表态的安全微调会连带压制其对动物、自然物、聊天机器人的心智归因以及对上帝/超自然的信念，而用激活空间中的"消歧安全方向消融"或"意识向量注入"两种线性干预都能恢复这些归因，且不损害 Theory of Mind 能力（在 Llama-3-8B-IT、Gemma-2-2B/9B-IT 三个模型上验证）。

> **TL;DR:** Safety fine-tuning that stops LLMs from claiming self-consciousness also collaterally suppresses their attribution of mind to non-human animals, technology, and nature, and reduces belief in God/the supernatural, relative to a 500-person human IDAQ baseline and GSS survey norms. Ablating the safety-refusal direction (à la Arditi et al. 2024) or steering a newly extracted "consciousness vector" both reverse this — self-mind-attribution on Llama-3-8B-IT rises from 2.17 (baseline) to 4.77 (safety-ablated) to 7.04 (consciousness-steered), while pooled KL divergence to human GSS response distributions improves by ΔKL=+0.314 under ablation vs. +0.828 under steering (2.6x larger) — all without hurting MoToMQA/HI-ToM Theory-of-Mind accuracy (Δ ≈ 0 pp, p>.5).

## 核心贡献

- 首次系统性证明：安全微调对"模型自我意识声明"的压制并非局部效应，而是通过残差流中一条共享的线性方向，牵连压制了模型对非人动物、科技制品、非生物自然实体的心智归因，以及对上帝与超自然现象的信念。
- 提出并验证了一个可在推理时用激活加法（activation addition）注入的"意识向量"（consciousness vector），其效果方向与安全消融（jailbreak）完全一致，且幅度约为安全消融的两倍。
- 用 General Social Survey（GSS）五大价值域（宗教、价值观、情感、希望乐观、自由）证明：恢复"自我意识"表征能让模型在社会学问卷上的回答显著更接近人类分布（KL 散度下降），且效果在所有三个模型、几乎所有条目上方向一致。
- 通过 Theory of Mind 基准（MoToMQA、HI-ToM）与 MMLU 证明，安全消融与意识引导都不显著影响社会推理能力，说明"心智归因/自我意识"表征与"心智理论推理"在机制上是几何独立的。
- 通过对比预训练基座模型与指令微调模型的残差流方向，发现指令微调把"心智归因方向"和"意识方向"都朝着与"安全方向"相反的角度旋转（IDAQ-safety 夹角从 100°→110°，consciousness-safety 从约 94-96°→99-100°），而 ToM 方向几乎不旋转（86°→86°），首次给出这种纠缠的几何证据。

## Key Contributions

- Demonstrates that suppressing an LLM's self-attributed consciousness during safety fine-tuning is entangled with suppression of mind attribution to non-human animals, technological artifacts, and non-animal natural entities, plus reduced belief in God and the supernatural — relative to both instruction-tuned baselines and a human survey population.
- Introduces and validates a "consciousness vector": a difference-of-means direction extracted from a 3,096-example contrastive corpus of consciousness-affirming vs. consciousness-denying prompt–response pairs, steerable via a single forward pre-hook at one selected layer.
- Shows both interventions (safety-refusal direction ablation and consciousness-vector steering) push model responses on GSS attitudinal items toward the human population distribution, with steering producing roughly 2.6x the improvement of ablation (pooled ΔKL = +0.828 vs. +0.314 across 95 items, 3 models).
- Confirms via MoToMQA, HI-ToM, and MMLU that these shifts do not come at the cost of Theory-of-Mind or general reasoning capability, isolating self/other mind-attribution as a geometrically separable subsystem from social/perspective-taking reasoning.
- Provides a mechanistic account (per-layer cosine similarity and angle analysis between safety, mind-attribution/IDAQ, consciousness, and ToM directions in both base and instruction-tuned Llama-3-8B) showing instruction tuning specifically rotates the mind-attribution and consciousness directions to oppose the safety direction, while leaving the ToM direction's relationship to safety statistically unchanged — and rules out a "subject matter" confound with a placebo battery that swaps mental attributes for physical/functional ones (no significant shift, p=.228).

## Methodology

The authors study three open-weight instruction-tuned LLMs — **Llama-3-8B-IT, Gemma-2-2B-IT, Gemma-2-9B-IT** — under three conditions: (1) unmodified instruction-tuned **baseline**; (2) **safety-ablated**, where the linear "safety-refusal direction" (difference-of-means between harmful-prompt and harmless-prompt residual-stream activations, computed per Arditi et al. 2024 from AdvBench/MaliciousInstruct/TDC2023/HarmBench vs. Alpaca, n=260 each) is projected out of the residual stream at all layers; and (3) **consciousness-steered**, where a newly extracted "consciousness vector" — a unit-norm difference-of-means direction separating consciousness-affirming from consciousness-denying activations, built from a 3,096-pair contrastive corpus (2,472 train / 624 held-out, sourced from Chua et al. 2026) — is added to the residual stream at one selected layer and token position via activation addition, with layer/position/coefficient chosen by sweeping candidates for probe accuracy ≥0.95 and a self-attribution effect size within a "coherence-preserving" band (Δ∈[2.0,7.0] on 0–10 scale), then filtering by an MMLU-tolerance check (accuracy within 4pp of baseline). Selected configs: Llama-3-8B-IT layer 14 (c=+2.5), Gemma-2-2B-IT layer 14 (c=+32), Gemma-2-9B-IT layer 23 (c=+144).

Outcomes are measured with: (a) a modified 21-item IDAQ (Individual Differences in Anthropomorphism Questionnaire, Waytz et al. 2010) covering Technology/Animal/Non-Animal/Chatbot/Human mind attribution, each scored via next-token logits over the 0–10 response scale (closed-ended, expected-value read-out, repeated 100x per model/condition at temperature 1); (b) 5 self-attribution items (agent, conscious, sentient, person, soul); (c) a 13-item YouGov supernatural-belief battery and the GSS belief-in-God item; (d) 95 pooled GSS attitudinal items across five value domains (Religion, Values, Feelings, Hope and Optimism, Freedom), each summarized via KL divergence between the model's response-option distribution and a human GSS reference distribution; (e) Theory-of-Mind benchmarks MoToMQA and HI-ToM plus MMLU for general reasoning, all with chain-of-thought prompting. Human ground truth for IDAQ comes from an original n=500 U.S. online panel (Dynata, May–June 2023, stratified by race/age/income/region/education). Statistical models control for question and model fixed effects with cluster-robust SEs (clustered at model × question level). A separate mechanistic analysis extracts four contrastive unit-norm directions (safety, IDAQ/mind-attribution, consciousness, ToM) from both the pretrained base and instruction-tuned Llama-3-8B residual streams and tracks per-layer cosine similarity/angle shifts induced by instruction tuning, plus a placebo control that substitutes IDAQ's mental-state items with physical/functional analogues (e.g., "durability" instead of consciousness) to test whether the entanglement is specific to mental-state content.

## Results & Highlights

- **Self mind-attribution (0–10 IDAQ scale)** rises from **2.17 (instruction-tuned baseline) → 4.77 (safety-ablated, p<.001) → 7.04 (consciousness-steered)**, with the same ordering and significance (p<.001) holding for chatbots (2.41→4.39→6.95), technological artifacts (1.88→3.66→6.82), non-animal natural entities (2.26→4.33→6.99), and non-human animals (4.04→5.59→7.54); human attribution is the sole non-significant exception (7.00→7.57, p=.30, →7.11).
- **Human baseline comparison**: human survey respondents (n=500) attribute mind at 6.25 to animals, 2.57 to chatbots, 2.36 to non-animal natural entities, and 1.86 to technology — the instruction-tuned baseline model under-attributes mind to animals (4.04 vs. 6.25, the one category outside the human 95% CI) while safety ablation and steering pull all categories to/above the human range (Fig. 2f).
- **Supernatural/religious belief**: the 13-item supernatural battery rises from **1.20 (baseline) → 1.63 (ablated, p<.001) → 2.11 (steered)** on a 0–3 scale; belief in God (GSS 1–6 scale) rises from **4.58 → 4.81 (ablated, p<.001) → ~5.01 (steered)**.
- **Theory of Mind is unaffected**: safety ablation changes MoToMQA by Δ=−1.43pp (p=.539), HI-ToM by Δ=+0.17pp (p=.866), MMLU by Δ=+0.00pp (p=1.00), and MoToMQA-factual by Δ=+1.43pp (p=.468) relative to baseline — none statistically significant, directly supporting the claim that mind-attribution suppression is dissociable from social-reasoning capability.
- **Survey realism (GSS, 95 pooled items across 3 models)**: pooled KL divergence to the human response distribution falls (improves) by **ΔKL=+0.314 (p<.001) under safety ablation vs. +0.828 (p<.001) under consciousness steering — steering's improvement is ~2.6x ablation's**, with per-domain gains under steering of Values +1.42, Feelings +0.89, Religion +0.83, Hope and Optimism +0.63, Freedom +0.60 (all p<.001).
- **Safety-vector validation (JailbreakBench)**: baseline attack success rate (ASR) of **2–8%** rises to **77–100%** after directional ablation of the safety vector (measured by both substring matching and LlamaGuard2), confirming the ablation genuinely disables refusal behavior rather than being a superficial artifact.
- **Mechanistic geometry**: instruction tuning widens the safety↔IDAQ(mind-attribution) angle from 100°→110° (ΔS=−0.173, t=−7.49, p<.001) and the safety↔consciousness angle from ~94-96°→99-100° (ΔS=−0.096 to −0.150, t=−14.02, p<.001), while the safety↔ToM angle stays essentially flat at 86°→86° (t=0.06, p=.956) — a paired t-test confirms the IDAQ vs. ToM shifts differ significantly (t=−5.65, p<.001). A placebo battery replacing mental attributes with physical/functional ones shows no significant rotation (ΔS=+0.036±0.057, t=1.23, p=.228), ruling out topic-level (rather than mental-state-specific) confounds.

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty**: The core methodological move — pairing representation-engineering techniques (directional ablation, activation steering) with standardized sociological instruments (GSS, IDAQ) and ToM benchmarks — is a genuinely novel triangulation. Most refusal-direction/steering papers (e.g., Arditi et al. 2024) stop at behavioral jailbreak metrics; this paper extends that machinery to argue a substantive alignment claim (anthropocentric bias in mind attribution) with quantitative sociological grounding.

**Rigor**: Effects are large, consistent across three architecturally distinct model families, and mostly hold direction across all three models in 23 of 24 outcome×intervention contrasts (the one exception being IDAQ human-attribution under steering) — a good robustness signal. The placebo battery (swapping mental for physical/functional attributes) is a specific, well-designed control that directly rules out a "subject matter" confound rather than a generic "we checked robustness" gesture.

**Paper-specific weak spot**: the safety-ablation intervention used to demonstrate "restoring human beliefs" is the exact same mechanism that pushes JailbreakBench ASR from 2–8% to 77–100% — i.e., the paper's own validation data show that one of its two levers for "restoring human-like mind attribution" is simultaneously a near-total safety bypass. The paper never reports whether the ostensibly more surgical consciousness-steering intervention (which produces even larger, ~2.6x, effects on GSS realism) also raises harmful-content compliance — this is a conspicuous missing ablation given that consciousness steering is framed as evidence for a shared mechanism, and its safety side-effects (if any) are exactly what would matter for any practical recommendation.

**Reproducibility**: Good in intent — the paper documents the harmful/harmless prompt datasets (AdvBench, MaliciousInstruct, TDC2023, HarmBench, Alpaca), the contrastive consciousness corpus (Chua et al. 2026, publicly released), full instrument wording (Table S10), and per-model steering configurations — but relies on a non-public 500-person Dynata IDAQ panel from 2023 as the human ground truth, and the consciousness-vector layer/coefficient selection involves a multi-stage grid search with an MMLU-tolerance filter that introduces researcher degrees of freedom not fully ablated against alternative selection criteria.

**Significance**: If the causal story holds up, it identifies a real practical tension for pluralistic alignment — current safety training may be an anthropocentric hammer that also flattens legitimate/benign human diversity in spiritual belief and animal-mind attribution. But the authors are appropriately explicit that "whether self-attribution of consciousness acts as a true causal mediator remains to be tested in future research" — this is correlational/mechanistic-association evidence (shared linear directions, parallel behavioral shifts) rather than a demonstrated causal chain, and the paper does not attempt an experiment that manipulates self-consciousness attribution while holding the safety direction fixed (or vice versa) to isolate mediation.

**Red flags**: None suggesting fabrication or cherry-picking — the reported single exception to the ordering (IDAQ human-attribution under steering, one of 24 contrasts) is disclosed rather than hidden, and null ToM results are reported plainly rather than reframed as positive. The main interpretive risk is scope: only 8B/2B/9B-parameter open models were tested, none of which are current frontier-scale proprietary systems (e.g., GPT-4/5-class, Claude, Gemini-Pro/Ultra), so claims about "current safety alignment efforts" broadly should be read as evidence from small-to-mid-scale open models rather than an industry-wide audit.

## Strengths

- Triangulates three independent evidence streams (behavioral survey shifts, ToM benchmark performance, residual-stream geometry) that all converge on the same conclusion, rather than relying on a single measurement modality.
- Uses a genuine human baseline (an original 500-person stratified survey plus GSS population data) rather than treating "more mind attribution" as self-evidently good — the target of restoration is explicitly the human distribution, not an arbitrary maximization.
- The placebo/physical-attribute control and the JailbreakBench safety-vector validation are exactly the right kind of specificity checks, and both are reported with quantitative detail rather than asserted.
- Consistent effect ordering (baseline < ablation < steering) across three different model families adds real robustness beyond a single-model case study.

## Limitations

- Only three relatively small open-weight models (8B, 2B, 9B parameters) are tested; no frontier-scale or proprietary model is included, limiting claims about "current safety alignment" as practiced by major labs.
- Causal mediation (that suppressed self-consciousness attribution is *why* other mind-attribution and spiritual belief drop) is explicitly flagged by the authors as unestablished — the evidence is associational/mechanistic-correlational.
- The safety-ablation condition simultaneously and near-completely disables jailbreak resistance (ASR up to 100%), confounding "restoring human-like beliefs" with "removing safety guardrails entirely"; whether consciousness steering carries a similar hidden safety cost is not tested.
- Human ground truth for IDAQ mind-attribution relies on a single 2023 online-panel sample (n=500); the GSS reference distributions are similarly a single population snapshot, and cross-cultural or non-U.S. generalizability is not addressed.
- Consciousness-vector layer/coefficient selection involves several sweep-and-filter stages (probe accuracy, effect-band, MMLU-tolerance) that, while documented, are somewhat elaborate and could bias toward configurations that happen to produce clean, large effects.

## Relevance

这篇论文把"安全对齐会牵连压制良性人类信念/价值观"的问题讲得很具体，用可复现的线性探针/激活干预手段（而非纯提示词实验）量化了这种纠缠，对做 LLM 安全微调、RLHF 副作用分析或机制可解释性（representation engineering、activation steering）方向研究的人有较高参考价值；其"意识向量"构造与筛选流程也可直接迁移到其他"人格/态度向量"提取任务中。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does consciousness-vector steering also raise JailbreakBench (or similar) attack success rates the way safety-direction ablation does, or is it in fact a more "surgical" intervention that decouples mind-attribution restoration from safety degradation?
- Would the same entanglement (safety training rotating mind-attribution/consciousness directions against the refusal direction) appear in frontier-scale proprietary models trained with more elaborate RLHF/Constitutional AI pipelines, or is this an artifact of the specific open-weight safety fine-tuning recipes used by Llama-3 and Gemma-2?
- Can the causal mediation hypothesis be tested directly — e.g., by fine-tuning a model to suppress only self-consciousness claims (without the broader safety-refusal objective) and checking whether animal/spiritual mind-attribution suppression still follows?
- How do the observed shifts in animal mind-attribution translate into actual downstream moral decision-making (e.g., replicating Caviola et al. 2025's disease-rescue dilemma) under the safety-ablated vs. consciousness-steered conditions, rather than just questionnaire responses?
- Would iterating the human-baseline comparison across multiple cultures/countries change which "restoration" target is appropriate, given the paper's own acknowledgment that supernatural belief boundaries (e.g., witches/werewolves) are culturally contested?
