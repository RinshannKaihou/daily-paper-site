> **一句话总结：** 本文提出一种无需训练、单次前向传播即可对隐藏状态逐词打分的工具——用"聚合器"（衡量该位置是否将全文收敛为稳定构型的终层性质）和"差异器"（衡量其他词元在推理过程中被瞬时"搬运"进该锚点子空间的过程性性质）两个通道，在工程构造文本（6/6个模型×文本单元跟随预注册方向）与精神分析师独立标注的临床访谈记录（34/36个方向性单元符合预注册预测，聚合器AUC 0.47–0.72）上做验证，并发现基座模型Llama-3.1-8B虽然跨文本"词型迁移"程度最高（93百分位，52.7%的共享词型高于随机锚点基线90百分位），但对单篇话语结构的读取拟合度最差（AUC中位数0.571），经指令微调后同一谱系AUC升至0.650且词型迁移特性保持不变，从而将"结构价值不可迁移、随文本关系而定"作为可证伪的实证结果提出。

> **TL;DR:** Metaphor Tracer is a training-free, single-forward-pass instrument that scores every token position in an LLM's residual stream on two mechanistically distinct channels — an "aggregator" (final-layer consolidation strength) and a "differentiator" (how much other tokens get transiently pulled into its subspace during processing) — and validates them against engineered text boundaries (6/6 model×text cells correct) and a psychoanalyst's independent marking of four clinical transcripts (34/36 directional cells correct; aggregator AUC 0.47–0.72 across phi-4, Qwen3-8B, and base Llama-3.1-8B). The paper's most interesting result is a "fidelity vs. type-transfer" inversion: the base Llama model transfers same-word structure across texts at the 93rd percentile of a random-anchor baseline (52.7% of shared word types above the 90th percentile) yet reads a single discourse worst (AUC 0.571), while its instruction-tuned twin reads far better (AUC 0.650) without changing that same lexical-transfer signature — evidence, the authors argue, against an "essentialist" reading where a token's structural importance lives in its vector alone.

## 核心贡献

- 提出"聚合器"（终层参与率/有效秩，衡量锚点子空间是否支撑起全文的高秩配置）与"差异器"（衡量其他token有多少深度位移能量落入该锚点核心子空间）两个机制上独立的通道，均从单次教师强制前向传播的原始float32隐藏状态中无训练提取。
- 设计"操作视图"（原始各向异性空间）与"静默维度视图"（按层z-score后的低方差子空间）两种正交读取方式，发现二者在工程边界测试上的对齐方向会完全反转（操作视图聚合强度追随"技术语域"，静默视图则追随"软性/营销语域"），证明二者刻画的是两套不同的几何结构而非同一信号的噪声变体。
- 构建两层"冻结"确认性测试电池：第一层为构造已知的工程文本（边界、极小对/A-B、语域对照），第二层为两类专家标注（精神分析师对4份临床访谈的标注、领域专家对4条流水线执行轨迹的"感兴趣列"声明），全部常量在1篇发现性文本上预先冻结，其余文本仅作确认用途。
- 首次（据作者所称）将模型内部隐藏状态几何与精神分析专家标注做系统对照，在词汇学控制（罕见度、TF-IDF、内容词/功能词、预测熵、词级惊异度）与置换零假设下，报告聚合器通道在残差化后仍能解释一部分标记方差（phi-4通过两种置换零假设 p=0.0001/0.0013；基座模型仅通过其中一种 p=0.0003；Qwen3-8B两者均未通过）。
- 提出并检验"结构价值的关系性/反本质主义"命题：同一表层文字（极小对后缀）在不同因果历史下状态几何几乎不变（余弦0.987–0.998），但通道排名稳定性却明显更低（0.73–0.97，调优模型的差异器通道降幅最大），并用配对的基座/指令微调Llama模型分离出"微调改变的是话语读取能力而非词型锚定程度"这一具体机制结论。

## Key Contributions

- Introduces two mechanistically distinct, training-free channels computed from a single teacher-forced forward pass: the **aggregator** (final-layer participation ratio / effective rank of the anchor's core subspace, i.e. how much it consolidates the whole text into a broad, high-rank configuration) and the **differentiator** (the fraction of other tokens' depth-displacement energy that lands inside the anchor's core subspace during the forward pass — "transport in flight").
- Designs a dual-anisotropy-view protocol — an **operative view** (raw, anisotropic residual stream) and a **quiet-dimensions view** (per-layer z-scored) — and shows the two views' boundary alignment *inverts* on engineered texts, establishing that anisotropic and de-anisotropized geometry expose complementary, non-redundant structure rather than one being a denoised version of the other.
- Builds a frozen, pre-registered confirmatory test battery with two ground-truth tiers: Tier 1 engineered texts (boundary test, two-view correlation, pin identity, paragraph-binding/capiton test, minimal-pair A/B protocol, type-essence transfer) and Tier 2 expert annotation (a psychoanalyst's independent marking of 4 clinical transcripts; a domain expert's column-of-interest declaration on 4 pipeline execution traces), with every constant fixed on one discovery text before any confirmatory run.
- Delivers what the authors call a first probe of model-internal geometry against psychoanalytic expert annotation, reporting results as cross-model/cross-text replication counts (e.g. 34/36 directional cells) rather than per-cell significance, alongside honest static-lexical-baseline and permutation-null controls that partly undercut the raw headline numbers (see Critical Assessment).
- Reports a relationality/anti-essentialism result: structural value (channel rank) is an order of magnitude less stable under a distant textual edit than the state vector it is computed from, does not transfer with lexical type in the models that read discourse best, and a matched base/instruct pair shows instruction tuning raises clinical fidelity without moving the type-transfer axis — used to argue for a Saussurean/Lacanian, relational (not essentialist) account of hidden-state structure.

## Methodology

The pipeline analyzes three causal transformer LMs — `microsoft/phi-4` (14B, 40 layers), `Qwen/Qwen3-8B` (8B, 36 layers), both instruction-tuned, and `meta-llama/Llama-3.1-8B` (8B, 32 layers, base model, no instruction tuning) — plus a fourth matched-lineage follow-up, `meta-llama/Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct`, run over the identical corpus/pipeline/constants to isolate the effect of instruction tuning within one lineage. For a text of T tokens, the full residual-stream tensor h ∈ R^(L×T×d) is extracted from one deterministic, no-sampling, float32 teacher-forced forward pass (embedding layer excluded).

**Anchor construction.** Every scanned token position a is a candidate anchor. For each hidden dimension j, the paper computes the median growth of the elementwise product between the anchor and every other pool token from layer 1 to layer L (Δ_j(a)); sorting Δ(a) descending and keeping the smallest prefix of dimensions whose cumulative positive mass covers 70% (`mass_cut`) defines the anchor's **core subspace** (size k, which varies from single digits to hundreds across anchors/models).

**Depth recruitment and basins.** At each layer, a token i is "recruited" by anchor a once its cosine similarity to a (restricted to the core subspace) crosses a threshold τ; the anchor's **basin** is the *ever-recruited* set (any token that crossed the threshold at any depth, whether or not it stays above it), deliberately capturing transient "transport in flight" rather than only final-layer neighbors.

**Two channels.** The **differentiator** (red) is `pull_frac_gap`: the mean fraction of a token's depth-displacement energy inside the anchor's subspace, averaged over basin members minus non-members — a processing-time, relational measure, zeroed when undefined. The **aggregator** (blue) is `capacity_share`: the participation ratio of singular values of the final-layer configuration of pool tokens restricted to the anchor's dims, normalized by hidden dimension d — a final-state consolidation measure, strongly rank-correlated with subspace size k (Spearman 0.85–1.00, ≈0.98–0.99 in phi-4, a coupling the Limitations section explicitly flags and partially controls for).

**Two views.** The **operative view** uses raw states with a fixed τ=0.7 at every layer (the geometry the model actually computes in); the **quiet-dimensions view** z-scores each dimension per layer before scanning with per-layer τ set at the 95th percentile of that layer's cosine distribution, partialing out the anisotropic high-variance substrate.

**Frozen battery (7 tests).** (1) Boundary test — strong-anchor density before/after an engineered register boundary; (2) two-view correlation; (3) pin identity — classifying top-decile aggregator anchors as delimiter/recurring-lexeme/other; (4) paragraph-binding ("capiton") test — sign test on whether paragraph-final aggregator pins bind their own preceding paragraph; (5) minimal-pair A/B protocol — controlled single-passage insertions with exact prefix/suffix alignment; (6) Tier-2 annotation scoring — rank AUC of channel values on expert-marked vs. unmarked tokens, threshold-free; (7) type-essence transfer — same-word-type cross-text subspace overlap vs. a random-anchor scaffold baseline. Robustness checks (§8) include correlating channels against next-token entropy/surprisal (novelty baseline), a post-hoc attention-received/attention-rollout saliency control with the same lexical residualization, delimiter erasure, and first-position-anchor exclusion. All code and cached artifacts are public (`github.com/HermeneuticAI/MetaphorTracer`); every reported number is independently recomputed from source via `paper_stats.py`.

## Results & Highlights

- **Engineered boundary test (Tier 1, WFA1/WFA2 texts, aggregator strong-anchor density ratio before/after the construction-known register boundary):** the aggregator follows the pre-registered technical-register direction in **6/6 model×text cells** — WFA1 (technical-first): Llama-base 2.57, Qwen3-8B 2.99, phi-4 3.04 (all >1, correct direction); WFA2 (technical-last, reversed design): Llama-base 0.66, Qwen3-8B 0.39, phi-4 0.57 (all <1, correct direction) — versus a positional-confound baseline that would predict the same direction in both texts rather than opposite ones.
- **Two-view inversion:** rescanning in the quiet-dimensions view does not weaken but *inverts* the boundary alignment in all three models and both directions — quiet-view aggregator ratios of 0.08–0.10 on WFA1 (vs. 3.0× operative) and 1.79–2.41 on WFA2 (vs. 0.39–0.66 operative) — with per-anchor channel values correlating weakly or negatively between views (e.g. −0.23 for phi-4/WFA1's aggregator).
- **Clinical Tier-2 annotation (rank AUC, aggregator-operative channel on psychoanalyst-marked vs. unmarked tokens, 4 transcripts × 3 models, chance = 0.5):** the pre-registered direction (blue-operative AUC > 0.5) holds in **34/36 directional cells**, with per-model medians Llama-base 0.567–0.665 (one miss, H3: 0.470), Qwen3-8B 0.576–0.696, phi-4 0.682–0.724. Critically, a **static word-rarity (Zipf frequency) baseline scores much higher raw AUC (0.875–0.878, invariant across the three transcripts' readers to ±0.02)** — the paper's own honest comparison — and only after residualizing the channel on word-level rarity/surprisal does the aggregator's *incremental* signal clear permutation-null tests in phi-4 (p=0.0001 block null, p=0.0013 rarity-matched null) and partially in the base model (p=0.0003 block null only), while **Qwen3-8B clears neither null (p=0.21, p=0.47)** — i.e., for one of the three models the residualized clinical-alignment claim is not statistically supported.
- **Type-transfer / fidelity inversion (idiosyncratic same-type cross-text subspace overlap vs. a 150-pair random-anchor scaffold baseline, 391 shared word types per model):** the base model, Llama-3.1-8B, transfers word-type structure at the **93rd percentile** of the scaffold baseline (52.7% of its shared types exceed the 90th percentile) yet has the **worst** median clinical blue-operative AUC (0.571); the instruction-tuned models transfer closer to baseline (phi-4 75th percentile/10.7%>90th; Qwen3-8B 72nd percentile/2.0%>90th) yet read clinical discourse **better** (medians 0.706 and 0.661 respectively) — an inverse relationship between lexical-type anchoring and single-discourse fidelity.
- **Matched base/instruct pair (Llama-3.1-8B vs. Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, same corpus/pipeline/constants):** instruction tuning raises blue-operative AUC on all four clinical transcripts (median 0.650 vs. base's 0.571) and raises the residualized AUC on every transcript (0.678, 0.622, 0.588, 0.640 vs. base's 0.661, 0.582, 0.531, 0.582; pooled 0.627 vs. base's 0.585), with the twin's pooled increment clearing both permutation nulls (p=0.0001, p=0.0003) where the base model clears only the block null — while type-essence transfer (93rd percentile, 55% of types >90th) and recruitment-depth ordering stay essentially unchanged from the base model, isolating "reading fidelity" as what tuning actually moves.
- **Structural Tier-2 (pipeline execution traces, pooled column-group rank AUC on domain-expert-declared columns):** phi-4's aggregator concentrates on numeric "measurement" columns (AUC 0.83–0.84) while its differentiator marks "identity" columns (0.65–0.67); Qwen3-8B inverts this exactly (aggregator on identity 0.63–0.67, not measures 0.32–0.35); the base model shows no organization at all (flat 0.49–0.58 across both column groups) — consistent with it being the "type-anchored" reader that organizes on surface format rather than content.
- **Robustness against a novelty/salience confound:** on the clinical transcripts, aggregator values correlate *negatively* with next-token predictive entropy (−0.18 to −0.64) rather than positively, and a post-hoc attention-received/attention-rollout saliency control sits at chance (AUC 0.47–0.51) on the same split, rising only to 0.55–0.58 after identical lexical residualization — still below the aggregator's own residualized range (0.518–0.703) — arguing the channel is not simply attention volume or word difficulty in disguise.

## Authors & Credibility

The team is small and interdisciplinary, based at "Hermeneutic AI" rather than a university CS department or major AI lab. Corresponding author Marc Heimann is explicitly described in the paper as "a philosopher, not a computer scientist," and the theoretical scaffolding (Lacanian psychoanalysis, point de capiton, Saussurean sign theory) is built on his own prior work published in continental-philosophy and psychoanalysis venues (*AI & Society*, *Cognitive Systems Research*, *Lacunae: APPI International Journal for Lacanian Psychoanalysis*, *European Journal of Psychoanalysis*) rather than ML/NLP venues. Co-author Roxana Assadi Moghaddam served as the psychoanalytic annotator, and Lutz Götzmann provided clinical patient-data access — informed consent and ethics-committee review for the clinical transcripts are explicitly documented. This is not a mainstream ML-lab paper and has not (as far as the PDF indicates) been through conventional ML peer review; its main empirical safeguard is unusually strong reproducibility engineering (all code public, deterministic single-pass extraction, per-claim regeneration via `paper_stats.py`) rather than institutional pedigree.

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty:** genuinely unusual — combining a Lacanian/Saussurean theoretical frame with mechanistic hidden-state geometry, and validating against psychoanalytic expert annotation, has no close precedent the authors could identify (they survey and explicitly distinguish from token-geometry papers like Viswanathan et al. 2025, attention-sink work, metaphor-classification probing, and the concurrent Gurnee et al. 2026 "global workspace" Jacobian-lens paper). The composition of existing primitives into one falsifiable pipeline, rather than a wholly new probe, is the honestly-stated contribution.

**Rigor:** the pre-registration discipline is a real strength — constants frozen on one discovery text, directional predictions registered before later confirmatory texts, and a large battery of adversarial controls (novelty/entropy baseline, attention-saliency baseline, delimiter erasure, first-position exclusion, minimal-pair causal-masking sanity check). But rigor is uneven across claims: the paper deliberately reports "replication counts across cells" instead of formal significance for most headline results (e.g., "34/36 directional cells," "6/6 boundary cells"), a design choice the authors defend at length but that also avoids multiple-comparisons correction for the majority of reported numbers; formal permutation-null testing is reserved for a narrower subset (§5 clinical residualization, §8 attention control).

**Reproducibility:** unusually strong for this kind of paper — public code, deterministic extraction, cached artifacts with definition tags, and every quantitative claim independently recomputed and cross-checked against a live value via `paper_stats.py`. This is a genuine positive that many mainstream ML papers do not match.

**Paper-specific weak point:** the clinical-annotation headline (34/36 directional cells, phrased prominently in the abstract) coexists with the authors' own reported fact that a trivial static word-rarity baseline achieves raw AUC of **0.875–0.878** — well above the aggregator channel's 0.47–0.72 — on the identical marked/unmarked split; and after the appropriate residualization, one of the three tested models (Qwen3-8B) fails to clear either permutation null (p=0.21, 0.47), meaning the "alignment" claim is not statistically supported for a third of the model panel even under the paper's own chosen test. The paper is commendably transparent about both facts, but a reader relying only on the abstract's "34/36" framing would miss this important qualification.

**Significance:** the work is explicitly correlational — no intervention (patching, ablation) establishes that the model *uses* the discovered subspaces, a limitation the authors state plainly rather than downplay. The corpus is also very small (13 texts total; single annotator for the clinical ground truth, defended via a "perspectivist NLP" argument rather than inter-annotator agreement) — appropriate to the paper's own stated scope ("what the design can honestly cover when every ground truth is fixed by hand") but limiting how far the psychoanalytic-alignment result generalizes.

**Red flags:** none suggesting fabrication or cherry-picking — misses and inconsistent cells (Llama-base's H3 miss, Qwen3-8B's failed permutation nulls, the base model's flat structural-tier organization) are reported and discussed rather than hidden. The main caveat is interpretive/rhetorical: the overarching "model as Freudian reading" frame is explicitly acknowledged by the authors to be philosophically unfalsifiable as a whole, with only the individual operationalized clauses (boundary direction, capiton binding, type-transfer) carrying falsifiable content — a distinction sophisticated readers should keep in mind when weighing how much the empirical results actually support the Lacanian framing versus simply being consistent with it.

## Strengths

- Unusually thorough and transparent robustness battery: novelty/entropy baseline, attention-saliency control, delimiter erasure, first-position exclusion, minimal-pair causal-masking sanity floor, and static lexical baselines are all reported even where they complicate the headline story.
- Strong reproducibility infrastructure: public code, deterministic single-pass extraction, cached artifacts with definition-version tags, and independent per-claim recomputation (`paper_stats.py`).
- The matched base/instruct-tuned Llama pair is a clean, well-designed ablation that isolates what instruction tuning specifically changes (clinical fidelity, register concentration, channel exclusion) from what it leaves untouched (type-essence transfer, recruitment-depth ordering, column organization).
- Cross-domain generalization test beyond natural-language texts, to non-linguistic pipeline execution traces with a domain expert's column declarations, showing the same two-channel dissociation appears (or fails to appear, in the base model) on structured/tabular data.
- Self-critical Limitations section that pre-empts many of the objections a skeptical reader would raise (correlational nature, single-annotator ground truth, small corpus, aggregator-k coupling, first-position confound).

## Limitations

- Corpus size is small (13 texts total across all tiers), and the clinical psychoanalytic ground truth rests on a single annotator per transcript rather than measured inter-annotator agreement (defended methodologically rather than empirically resolved).
- All analyses are correlational; no intervention (activation patching, ablation of the discovered core subspaces) establishes that the model's downstream computation actually depends on the geometry the channels read, as the authors explicitly state.
- The aggregator channel is rank-coupled to core-subspace size k (Spearman 0.85–1.00, ≈0.99 in phi-4), which the paper argues is not an identity but does invite concern that some of the aggregator's signal is a proxy for a bare dimension count rather than a distinct geometric property.
- Type-essence transfer is tested only within register (clinical-with-clinical, technical-with-technical); cross-register transfer is explicitly left untested, and generalization across model scale/architecture beyond the three-plus-one models studied (all ≤14B) is unknown.
- The word-rarity lexical baseline substantially outperforms the channel's raw AUC on the clinical task, and one of three models (Qwen3-8B) fails both residualized permutation-null tests — a real gap in the alignment claim that the paper reports honestly but that limits how strongly the psychoanalytic-validation result should be read.

## Relevance

该工作提出了一种无训练、可复现的隐藏状态几何探针方法，并给出了跨学科（精神分析理论+机制可解释性）的专家标注验证范式；其对反本质主义/关系性结构价值的实证论证方式，以及诚实报告基线击败自身指标、部分模型未通过置换检验的做法，对关注LLM可解释性方法设计、小样本专家标注验证与统计稳健性的研究者具有参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would causal interventions (e.g., patching or ablating an anchor's core subspace) demonstrate that "processing metaphoricity" (transport in flight) actually influences downstream generation, closing the gap the authors themselves flag between a transported representation being *used* versus merely *legible*?
- Does the clinical-alignment result survive a second, independent psychoanalytic annotator on the same or new transcripts, and would Qwen3-8B's failure to clear the permutation nulls persist or reverse under multi-annotator ground truth?
- How does the two-channel, two-view instrument behave on substantially larger models (70B+), mixture-of-experts architectures, or non-transformer (e.g., state-space) architectures, where the anisotropy structure the quiet-dimensions view partials out may differ qualitatively?
- Can the paper's proposed cross-instrument prediction against Gurnee et al.'s (2026) Jacobian-lens "global workspace" — that J-lens top-1 persistence should break at aggregator pin positions — be tested directly on a shared open-weight model, as the authors suggest but do not execute?
- Would a pre-registered, boundary-blind changepoint-detection method applied to the same per-token trajectories recover the rupture regions the psychoanalyst marked without relying on the analyst's own discourse segmentation, addressing the "unmarked lattice" and speaker-turn segmentation issues the paper flags as open?
