Fidelity Is Not Safety: Gently-Compressed LLMs Pass Every Data-Free Quality Guard Yet Invent Procedure Steps in Agentic Execution ↗
I. Kennedy, T. Kennedy · 31 Jul 2026 · no institutional affiliation disclosed
LLM Compression Low-Rank SVD Pruning Agentic Safety Data-Free Fidelity Probes Goodhart's Law in ML Eval

Key Metric

+1.729

invented steps / query

Mistral-7B SVD build at ppl 1.069× — comfortably inside the 1.15× acceptance guard [95% CI 1.076, 2.424]. A perplexity-matched pruning build invents −0.146 (CI includes zero).

Why This Matters

For teams shipping compressed LLMs as tool-use or SOP-following agents: clearing perplexity, MMLU, and fidelity checks does not guarantee the agent won't fabricate steps.

Critical Edge

The canary runs on base models coaxed into a step-listing format, not real instruction-tuned agents on real tasks, so the authors themselves call absolute invention rates "instrument-specific."

Methodology

The instrument is a synthetic "agentic canary": 10-step procedures with a conditional 11th step are placed in context alongside a distractor procedure, and a base model is prompted (two maintenance-mode paraphrases) to enumerate the target's exact steps. The scored failure mode is cross-procedure invention — step-strings drawn from a global SOP vocabulary that belong to neither the target procedure nor its own branch, i.e. fabricated or leaked steps. The bank is pre-registered at 24 SOPs × 3 seeds (144 paired events per arm), with non-diagnostic runs discarded by policy.

Compression operators are compared at matched perplexity dose: coherent low-rank SVD truncation vs. incoherent magnitude pruning (the control), with quantization as an added dose control for mechanism analysis. Every build must also clear three "data-cheap" guards: WikiText-2 perplexity (≤1.15× baseline), MMLU 0-shot accuracy, and a data-free CKA/cosine fidelity probe against random Gaussian activations — the same signal used to allocate the compression in the first place.

The Deployable Screen

A data-free, two-axis statistic computed straight off the weight-difference tensor ΔW = Wbase − Wmod, with no labeled data required:

coherent_fraction > 0.007 error_rate > 0.01

coherent_fraction is the share of ΔW's energy sitting in its top-8 singular directions (structure); error_rate is ΔW's energy relative to W itself (dose). Both fire together only for the toxic arms.

Correctly classifies all six canary-labeled arms and predicts invention onset on an 18-point sweep (9 SVD budgets × 9 prune densities) — but the sweep only covers Mistral-7B and Llama-3.1-8B, and one Llama pruning cell (50% density) breaks the pattern as an acknowledged, unresolved edge case.

  • 18× gap on Llama-3.1-8B between SVD and damage-matched pruning, both intervals clean.
  • Same-weights dissociation (Qwen3-8B): the probe-allocated "b98" SVD build CI-wins a paired output-fidelity test (rand-probe NLL CIs [0.024, 0.036] and [0.062, 0.076]) while CI-failing the invented-step canary (+0.643 [0.171, 1.229]) — on the identical tensors.
  • Mechanism control: a quantization arm with more raw perplexity damage than the failing SVD dose still passes the canary — until pushed to the same error rate, at which point it also fails (+0.10 [0.029, 0.20], n=70). Coherence × rate governs the failure, not damage magnitude, and the effect double-dissociates from MMLU in both directions.

Strengths

  • Falsifiable, pre-registered design with paired confidence intervals rather than point estimates.
  • A genuine within-arm dissociation (same weights, opposite verdicts) hard to dismiss as noise.
  • The coherence × rate mechanism is tested against an active dose-matched quantization control, not just inferred from SVD-vs-pruning alone.
  • Ships an actionable, cheap detector rather than stopping at the negative finding, stress-tested on an 18-point sweep.
  • Unusually candid limitations section that flags its own scope boundaries and soft edges.

Limitations

  • Only three dense 7-8B decoder LLMs tested; no MoE in the controlled battery (one anecdotal MoE data point mentioned outside the experiments).
  • Synthetic-SOP, base-model chat-template-fallback benchmark — invention rates are explicitly called "instrument-specific."
  • The perplexity-guard "evasion" is spectrum-conditional: it holds for Qwen3-8B and Mistral-7B but not Llama-3.1-8B, where the guard catches the toxic build by accident.
  • Detector thresholds are fit to six labeled arms plus a sweep covering only two of three architectures and the SVD/pruning families; one counterexample (Llama 50% pruning) is unresolved.
  • No code, checkpoints, or generation scripts released — numeric results can't be independently verified.

Questions for Further Exploration

  1. Does the invented-step failure replicate on instruction-tuned, RLHF'd chat models on realistic agentic tasks, rather than base-model chat-template fallback prompting?
  2. Would the coherent_fraction / error_rate thresholds hold on MoE architectures, where only an anecdotal data point exists?
  3. Would chain-of-thought or self-verification prompting (checking the step list against the SOP text) reduce or eliminate the confabulation?
  4. Would adding Qwen3-8B to the dense sweep preserve the same fixed thresholds, or force architecture-specific calibration?
  5. Does coherence × rate extend to other coherent-error compression methods — structured/block-sparse pruning, post-hoc-merged low-rank adapters, 2:4 sparsity — left untested here?
