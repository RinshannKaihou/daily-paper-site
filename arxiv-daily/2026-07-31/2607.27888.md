> **一句话总结：** 论文通过反事实重打分实验证明"在线自蒸馏(OPSD)"给出的token级信号本质上只反映token对特权条件的敏感度、而非其对答案正确性的真实贡献，据此提出CSCR方法——仅对高敏感度token的GRPO优势做指数降权并重新归一化，在Qwen3-1.7B和Qwen3-4B上于AMC23/AIME24/25/26/SMT25五个数学推理基准上一致超过GRPO及五种自蒸馏基线（1.7B模型AIME25从GRPO的36.7提升到43.6，提升6.9分）。
>
> **TL;DR:** By re-scoring the same on-policy trajectory under artificially "positive" vs. "negative" verifier prompts, the authors show that on-policy self-distillation (OPSD)'s token-level likelihood shifts are 91–96% same-signed regardless of asserted correctness (CPC ≈ 0.58 overlap) and concentrate on substitutable discourse tokens (*The, But, So, Wait*) rather than reasoning-relevant content — meaning OPSD's dense supervision reflects sensitivity, not answer-aligned credit. Their fix, CSCR, keeps GRPO's verifier-derived direction but exponentially downweights the top-sensitivity tokens and renormalizes advantages; on Qwen3-1.7B it lifts AIME25 from GRPO's 36.7 to 43.6 (Mean@32) and beats the best of five self-distillation baselines (RLCSD at 37.2) by 6.4 points, with similar gains on Qwen3-4B (AIME25: GRPO 65.0 → CSCR 68.3).

## 核心贡献

- **反事实诊断实验**：固定同一条on-policy采样轨迹，分别在"该答案被验证器判为正确"和"判为错误"两种极化特权条件下重新计算每个token的对数似然偏移（z^pos, z^neg），发现91.4%–95.8%的联合显著token在两种相反条件下偏移方向相同（而非GRPO隐含要求的正负反转），且组合信号中约58%的量级同坐标同方向重叠（CPC=0.486–0.671），证明特权分布偏移未能编码可靠的、与答案对齐的方向性信息。
- **偏移量级与内容脱钩**：偏移幅度最大的token高度集中在可替换的表层/话语标记（*The, But, So, Let, Wait, Therefore*等），而非承载具体推理内容（数字、运算符、变量）的token，说明偏移量级反映的是token对反事实条件的"敏感度"，而不是其对最终结果的因果学习价值。
- **提出CSCR**：将token级"反事实敏感度分数" s_i = max(|z^pos|, |z^neg|) 作为GRPO的最小扩展——对敏感度超过阈值λ的token指数式降权（不改变优势符号/方向），再在每条轨迹内重新归一化以保持原有的信用总预算，从而把信用从高敏感token重新分配到低敏感token。
- **系统性消融**：证明(1) 直接信任偏移符号构造的Shift-Directed GRPO（SD-GRPO）在约30步内训练崩溃（奖励趋零、进入token复读循环）；(2) 相比上调权重，适度下调（γ=0.2）效果最好且最稳定；(3) 更强的调制幅度（γ=0.5、1.0）无论升权降权都会破坏优化稳定性。

## Key Contributions

- **Counterfactual diagnosis**: By re-scoring the same fixed on-policy trajectory under two deliberately polarized "privileged" prompts (one asserting the final answer is correct, one asserting it is incorrect — Table 1), the authors show 91.4%–95.8% of jointly-significant tokens shift in the *same* direction under both opposing conditions (rather than reversing sign as GRPO's outcome-conditioned advantages would require), and that ~58% of the combined forward-KL optimization signal (Counterfactual Perturbation Consistency, CPC = 0.486–0.671 across 400 trajectories) overlaps between the two conditions. They formally prove CPC = 0 is the reference point for pure GRPO gradients (Appendix A), making the observed overlap a clean falsification of OPSD's implicit assumption.
- **Sensitivity ≠ content relevance**: Tokens with large privileged likelihood shifts are dominated by highly substitutable discourse/surface-form tokens (*The, But, So, Let, Wait, Therefore*), while near-zero-shift tokens are dominated by digits, operators, and mathematical symbols that carry problem-specific reasoning content — the opposite of what a useful dense credit signal should emphasize. The pattern is replicated across three alternative counterfactual constructions (answer-level, mild outcome-only, reference-solution; Appendix C), ruling out an artifact of the specific polarized wording.
- **CSCR algorithm**: A minimal, drop-in modification to GRPO — compute per-token sensitivity s_{i,t}, map it to a weight w_{i,t} via a saturating exponential (Eq. 14, controlled by onset λ, decay rate α, max attenuation strength γ), then renormalize weights within each trajectory to preserve the total credit budget before substituting the resulting token-level advantage into the standard clipped PPO/GRPO objective (Eq. 16).
- **Empirical validation + ablations**: CSCR wins on all 10 model × benchmark combinations against GRPO and five recent self-distillation baselines (OPSD, SDPO, SRPO, RLSD, RLCSD), and targeted ablations (Shift-Directed GRPO, upweight-vs-downweight sweep) causally confirm the diagnosis: trusting shift *direction* destabilizes training, while moderate downweighting of sensitive tokens is the effective and stable regime.

## Methodology

- **Setup**: Policy π_θ samples trajectory y ~ π_θ(·|x); for GRPO, a group of G on-policy responses per problem receives verifier rewards R_i, normalized into a trajectory-level advantage A_i = (R_i − μ_R)/(σ_R + ε_A), broadcast uniformly across all tokens in the clipped PPO objective.
- **Counterfactual re-scoring**: Given a sampled trajectory, the authors freeze the trajectory and every prefix, then re-score each token under two "privileged" contexts c^pos and c^neg — deliberately polarized prompts (Table 1) telling the model the solution is either verified as absolutely correct/brilliant or absolutely incorrect/flawed. This yields per-token log-likelihood shifts z_i^pos, z_i^neg (Eq. 1) and full-vocabulary probability-shift vectors Δ_i^pos, Δ_i^neg (Eq. 6). They define combined magnitude M, disagreement D, and Counterfactual Perturbation Consistency CPC = 1 − D/M (Eq. 7–9) to quantify same-direction overlap, and prove CPC_GRPO = 0 for antiparallel opposite-sign GRPO advantages at the on-policy reference point (Appendix A) as the theoretical contrast point.
- **Why OPSD fails**: OPSD (on-policy self-distillation) treats the full-vocabulary distribution q_i^c induced by conditioning on privileged information c as a stop-gradient teacher and minimizes forward KL D_KL(q_i^c || p_i) (Eq. 3–4), so the gradient signal at each position is exactly q_i^c − p_i (Eq. 5). The diagnostic experiment shows this signal is dominated by counterfactual sensitivity to the prompt framing rather than by verifier-aligned, answer-relevant information.
- **CSCR construction**: Define token sensitivity s_{i,t} = sg[max(|z_i,t^pos|, |z_i,t^neg|)] — the larger of the two privileged shifts, stop-gradient. Map to raw weight w_{i,t}: identity (w=1) below onset λ, and 1 − γ(1 − exp[−α(s_{i,t} − λ)]) above λ, saturating toward 1−γ as sensitivity grows (Eq. 14). Renormalize weights within each trajectory to define widetilde{A}_{i,t} = (w_{i,t} / mean_j w_{i,j}) · A_i (Eq. 15), preserving the total per-trajectory credit budget while shifting it away from highly sensitive positions. Substitute into the standard clipped GRPO loss (Eq. 16) — no auxiliary teacher, no extra rollout, no value model.
- **Training**: Qwen3-1.7B and Qwen3-4B, full-parameter fine-tuning with the veRL framework on 8×H800 GPUs, trained on DAPO-17K for 300 steps (32 problems × 8 trajectories = 256 trajectories/step), lr = 2e-6, training temperature 0.7/top-p 1.0/max length 20,480 tokens; evaluation temperature 0.6/top-p 0.95/max length 30,720 tokens, Mean@32 over 32 independently sampled responses per problem. CSCR defaults: λ=0.05, α=10, γ=0.2.

## Results & Highlights

- **Main table (Table 4, Mean@32)**: Note the "+X" subscripts printed in Table 4 denote CSCR's gain over the **Base** (untrained) model, not over GRPO. On Qwen3-1.7B, CSCR beats GRPO and every self-distillation baseline on all five benchmarks: AMC23 88.8 (Base 84.1, GRPO 84.1, best baseline RLSD 85.3); AIME24 50.1 (Base 45.5, GRPO 47.4, best baseline RLCSD 47.7); AIME25 43.6 (Base 35.5, GRPO 36.7, best baseline RLCSD 37.2 — CSCR leads GRPO by 6.9 points and the best baseline by 6.4); AIME26 42.2 (Base 35.2, GRPO 37.4, best baseline SRPO 38.3); SMT25 44.0 (Base 38.1, GRPO 39.5, best baseline RLCSD 40.8). On Qwen3-4B: AIME25 68.3 (Base 62.9, GRPO 65.0, best baseline RLCSD 65.7 — CSCR leads GRPO by 3.3 and RLCSD by 2.6); AIME26 67.2 (GRPO 65.3, best baseline RLCSD 65.5); SMT25 59.0 (GRPO 57.4, best baseline RLCSD 57.8).
- **Aggregate claim**: "CSCR improves performance by an average of 3.9 points on Qwen3-1.7B and 1.7 points on Qwen3-4B" compared with the strongest competing method per benchmark — and several self-distillation baselines (e.g., OPSD, SDPO, RLSD) underperform the un-distilled base model on some benchmarks (e.g., OPSD on Qwen3-4B AMC23: 94.6 vs. base 96.2), consistent with the paper's diagnosis that privilege-induced shifts can actively hurt optimization.
- **Counterfactual overlap statistics (Table 2, main-text prompts)**: Combined signal magnitude M mean 0.068, disagreement D mean 0.028, CPC mean/median = 0.583 (range 0.486–0.671) across 400 trajectories — i.e., roughly 58% of the two opposing conditions' full-vocabulary optimization signal is literally identical in direction and coordinate, versus the theoretical CPC=0 for genuine GRPO opposite-sign credit.
- **Token-type evidence (Table 3)**: Top tokens with large positive/negative shifts are dominated by *The*, comma, *But*, *So*, *Let*, *Therefore*, *Wait* (tens of thousands of occurrences each); near-zero-shift tokens are dominated by digits (0–9), "=", "+" — directly supporting the claim that large shifts hit substitutable surface form, not reasoning content.
- **Ablation — trusting shift direction fails**: Shift-Directed GRPO (SD-GRPO), which flips the GRPO advantage sign to match the privileged shift sign for the most-shifted 1.5%/3%/5% of tokens (λ=0.2/0.1/0.05), collapses within ~30 training steps for all three thresholds: reward falls from ~0.8 to ~0, response length hits the 20,480-token cap, and generations enter indefinite token-repetition loops (concrete examples in Appendix E, e.g., repeating "The 0.4855... is the percentage...").
- **Ablation — modulation strength**: Moderate downweighting (γ=0.2) gives the most stable optimization with steady validation Mean@8 gains; stronger downweighting (γ=0.5, 1.0) collapses reward toward zero with response length falling to ~6,000 and ~2,000 tokens respectively; upweighting sensitive tokens (γ=0.2) helps a little over GRPO, but stronger upweighting (γ=0.5, 1.0) drives entropy up to 0.6–0.8 with little validation gain (Figure 5).

## Authors & Credibility

Three authors: Qiangqiang He and Zhongheng Wu (State Key Laboratory for Novel Software Technology, Nanjing University), and ZiJian Wang (Institute of Wireless Communications Technology, Shanghai Jiao Tong University). This appears to be a small, likely student-led team without an established large-scale RLVR track record visible from the paper itself; no code repository or artifact link is mentioned in the paper text. The work is timely and directly engages with a cluster of very recent (2026) self-distillation-for-RLVR papers (OPSD/Zhao et al. 2026a, SDPO/Hübotter et al. 2026, SRPO/Li et al. 2026a, RLSD/Yang et al. 2026a, RLCSD/Pan et al. 2026), all cited and used as baselines, indicating close familiarity with the current state of the field.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: The core contribution is the counterfactual diagnostic protocol itself (Section 2), which is a genuinely useful empirical tool — re-scoring a fixed trajectory under artificially reversed outcome framings to test whether a "privileged" teacher's signal actually tracks correctness. The CSCR fix that follows is intentionally minimal (a monotonic downweighting + renormalization on top of vanilla GRPO), which the paper is explicit about calling "a minimal extension" — this is honest framing rather than overclaiming a fundamentally new algorithm.
- **Rigor of the diagnosis**: The robustness checks are a real strength — the same conclusion (same-sign dominance, surface-token concentration) is reproduced across four different counterfactual prompt constructions (polarized, answer-level, mild outcome-only, reference-solution; Appendix C) and a formal proof that pure GRPO yields CPC=0 at the on-policy reference point (Appendix A), which sets up a clean, falsifiable contrast rather than a hand-wavy analogy.
- **Baseline selection is narrow and self-referential**: All five distillation baselines (OPSD, SDPO, SRPO, RLSD, RLCSD) are essentially concurrent 2026 self-distillation variants from the same narrow sub-literature the paper is critiquing. Other token/step-level credit-assignment approaches explicitly cited in the intro (VinePPO, Grpo-λ, GTPO/GRPO-S with entropy-based reward shaping, process-reward-model methods) are never run as baselines, so it's unclear whether CSCR's gains would hold against a broader class of credit-assignment techniques, not just against the OPSD family it is designed to critique.
- **Scale gap between diagnosis and training experiments**: Figure 1's counterfactual likelihood-shift statistics are reported for four model sizes (Qwen3-1.7B/4B/8B/14B), but the actual CSCR training experiments (Table 4) only cover 1.7B and 4B — leaving open whether the same gains (or the same OPSD failure mode) hold at 8B/14B scale where the diagnostic data was also collected.
- **Single-domain evaluation**: All five benchmarks (AMC23, AIME24/25/26, SMT25) are competition mathematics; no code-generation, general reasoning, or instruction-following benchmarks are tested, despite RLVR being commonly applied beyond math. The improvement pattern (larger gains on the harder/newer AIME25/26/SMT25 vs. smaller gains on the easier, more saturated AMC23) is plausible but also means the biggest reported numbers are on the benchmarks with the most headroom.
- **Hyperparameter sensitivity acknowledged but not fully resolved**: The paper's own ablation shows CSCR's behavior is fragile to the modulation strength γ — values just beyond the "sweet spot" (0.5, 1.0) cause training collapse. This is honestly reported, but it means CSCR trades one fragile hyperparameter regime (self-distillation weighting) for another (three additional hyperparameters λ, α, γ that must be tuned per setting); the paper does not report a sensitivity/robustness sweep for λ or α, only γ.
- **Reproducibility**: No code or model checkpoints are linked in the visible paper text (main body + appendix), the training recipe is fully specified (framework, hardware, hyperparameters, dataset) which aids re-implementation, but independent verification is not currently possible without a released repository.

## Strengths

- Clean, falsifiable diagnostic methodology (fixed-trajectory counterfactual re-scoring + CPC metric + formal zero-overlap proof for GRPO) that convincingly undermines a widely-shared assumption in the concurrent self-distillation-for-RLVR literature.
- Robustness of the diagnosis across four distinct counterfactual prompt constructions and multiple probability thresholds, rather than resting on one cherry-picked prompt pair.
- CSCR is a lightweight, computationally cheap modification (no extra teacher rollout, no value model) that is simple to integrate into existing GRPO pipelines.
- Consistent wins across all 10 model×benchmark combinations, with ablations (SD-GRPO collapse, upweight/downweight sweep) that causally support rather than merely correlate with the paper's central claim.

## Limitations

- Baselines are limited to a cluster of very recent, closely related self-distillation methods; broader credit-assignment families (process reward models, entropy-based token weighting, value-based methods) are discussed but not empirically compared.
- Training experiments cover only two model scales (1.7B, 4B) despite diagnostic data being collected up to 14B, leaving scale generalization of the training method itself unverified.
- Evaluation is exclusively on math competition benchmarks; generalization to code or open-domain reasoning tasks is untested.
- CSCR introduces three new hyperparameters (λ, α, γ); the paper ablates only γ in depth, and shows the method is unstable outside a fairly narrow "moderate downweighting" regime.
- No public code/checkpoint release is evident from the paper.

## Relevance

这篇论文对做RLVR/长链推理训练、尤其是对token级信用分配和自蒸馏方法感兴趣的读者非常相关：它提供了一个可迁移的诊断工具（反事实重打分+CPC指标）来检验任何"密集监督信号是否真的与答案对齐"，以及一个几乎零额外开销、可直接嵌入现有GRPO管线的改进（CSCR），值得在自己的RLVR实验中尝试复现或作为诊断基线。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the same counterfactual-sensitivity diagnosis and CSCR fix generalize to non-math RLVR domains (code generation, tool-use, open-domain QA with verifiable rewards)?
- How does CSCR perform against non-distillation token/step-level credit-assignment baselines (e.g., VinePPO, process-reward-model-guided methods, entropy-based reward shaping like GTPO/GRPO-S)?
- Would the training experiments (not just the diagnostic likelihood-shift analysis) replicate at 8B/14B scale, where self-distillation methods might behave differently due to stronger base capabilities?
- Is there a principled way to set λ, α, γ (e.g., adaptively from the observed sensitivity distribution) rather than manual tuning, given the demonstrated fragility to modulation strength?
- Could the counterfactual sensitivity score itself be used as a diagnostic/monitoring signal during training (e.g., to detect when a policy is drifting toward degenerate surface-token reliance) rather than only as a static per-step reweighting?
