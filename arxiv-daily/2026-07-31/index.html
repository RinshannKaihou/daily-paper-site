Machine Unlearning

Exact Memory Deletion, Verified Bit-for-Bit, Inside a Real 48B Model

Subtract or Replay? Exact Deletion from Language-Model Memory

Vishwajith Ramesh · Vy Labs

This paper turns "exact deletion" into a falsifiable representation-theoretic question (addressable vs. transformed-by-write influence), and is one of the most rigorous empirical tests of machine unlearning to date — validating bit-for-bit deletion on real 18,842-token clinical records inside a 48B hybrid model via checkpointed rewind-and-replay.

论文首次把"精确删除"变成可证伪的表示论问题（可寻址 vs. 被后续写入变换），并在真实480亿参数混合模型上用检查点回退+重放，对18,842 token长度的真实临床病历做到逐位删除验证，是目前对机器遗忘做得最严谨的实证检验之一。

Reflection Loses to Just Sampling More, Study Finds

Sample More, Reflect Less

Iliya Mirzaei · Stony Brook University

A cost-matched, statistically powered replication shows that popular reflective test-time-compute methods (Self-Refine, Reflexion, Best-of-N self-verification) never significantly beat plain repeated sampling with majority voting, from 1.5B to 7B models — a sobering result for the whole self-correction literature.

该复现研究用严格的等token成本配对统计检验证明，Self-Refine、Reflexion、Best-of-N等"反思型"策略没有一个能显著超过最朴素的重复采样+多数投票。

Open-Source 35B Agent Beats GPT-5.5+Codex at ML Engineering

Frontis-MA1: An AI4AI Model for Recursive Self-Improvement

This work open-sources a complete stack (task environments, operator-level training, experience-guided evolutionary search) for a self-evolving ML-engineering agent, whose 35B model reaches 71.21% Medal Average on MLE-Bench Lite on a single RTX 4090 — beating GPT-5.5+Codex's 68.18%.
