> **一句话总结：** 作者发现，通过困惑度（≤1.15×）、MMLU 和数据无关表征保真度探针（CKA）三重把关的"温和压缩"大模型，在作为智能体执行标准操作流程（SOP）时会凭空编造流程步骤——在 Mistral-7B 上，困惑度仅 1.069× 的 SVD 低秩压缩模型每次查询多编造 +1.729 步（95% CI [1.076, 2.424]），而困惑度匹配的幅度剪枝模型编造量为 -0.146（CI 包含零），据此他们提出一个基于压缩误差"相干比例×误差率"两轴统计量的免数据检测器，用固定阈值在三个架构上正确分类了全部六个已标注压缩配置。

> **TL;DR:** Across three 7-8B model families (Qwen3-8B, Mistral-7B, Llama-3.1-8B), gently-compressed low-rank (SVD) builds that pass perplexity (≤1.15× baseline on WikiText-2), MMLU, and a CKA-based data-free fidelity probe still hallucinate procedure steps on a pre-registered 24-SOP × 3-seed agentic canary — e.g., Mistral-7B's in-regime SVD build (ppl 1.069×) invents +1.729 steps/query [1.076, 2.424] versus a perplexity-matched magnitude-pruning baseline at -0.146 [-0.375, 0.035] (CI includes zero), and on Llama-3.1-8B the gap between SVD and matched pruning reaches 18×. The failure is driven by the coherence (low-rankness) of the compression error times its magnitude, not by raw perplexity damage, and the authors ship a two-statistic data-free screen (coherent_fraction, error_rate) with fixed thresholds that correctly labels all six canary-tested compression arms.

## 核心贡献

- 揭示了一个被现有"数据免费"验收流程完全忽视的智能体行为盲区：在困惑度、MMLU 和数据无关保真度探针三项标准把关下都"合格"的压缩大模型，在扮演执行 SOP 的运维智能体时会编造出不在指令中的流程步骤（cross-procedure invention）。
- 通过预注册、统计功效充分的"编造步骤金丝雀"（24 个合成 SOP × 3 个随机种子，每臂 144 对配对事件），在三个模型家族（Qwen3-8B、Mistral-7B、Llama-3.1-8B）上复现该现象，证明其为算子特异性（operator-specific）：相干低秩 SVD 截断会诱发编造，而困惑度匹配的幅度剪枝不会。
- 提出并验证了一个"解离"证据：同一组压缩权重可以在配对保真度检验中置信区间显著"获胜"（更保真），却在编造步骤金丝雀检验中置信区间显著"失败"（行为崩坏），证明保真度探针在构造上就无法看到导致智能体失效的那条轴。
- 定位机制为"相干性 × 误差率"，而非压缩造成的原始损伤大小：在困惑度损伤相当甚至更大的情况下，量化误差不会触发编造，而将量化误差率推高到与失败 SVD 相同水平时，量化也会开始编造步骤。
- 给出一个可部署的免数据两轴检测器（coherent_fraction 和 error_rate 的合取门限），用跨架构固定阈值正确分类全部六个已标注编造/未编造的压缩配置，将该发现从"警示性观察"转化为可在智能体部署前运行的预检脚本。

## Key Contributions

- Identifies an agentic behavioral blindspot invisible to the standard "data-cheap" acceptance battery (perplexity within ~1.15×, MMLU confidence intervals, data-free representational-fidelity probes): compressed builds that clear all three still confabulate SOP steps when driven as operations agents.
- Constructs and pre-registers a powered "invented-step canary" (24 synthetic SOPs × 3 seeds, 144 paired events per arm) and demonstrates operator-specificity across three architectures (Qwen3-8B, Mistral-7B, Llama-3.1-8B): coherent low-rank (SVD) truncation induces invention; perplexity-matched magnitude pruning does not.
- Reports a within-arm dissociation: the same compressed weights (Qwen3-8B's probe-allocated "b98" SVD build) CI-win a paired output-fidelity test on rand-probe NLL while CI-failing the invented-step canary (+0.643 [0.171, 1.229]), showing fidelity and agent safety are decoupled by construction, not by tuning artifact.
- Isolates the mechanism as coherence-times-rate of the compression error (not raw magnitude of damage): dose-matched quantization with larger perplexity damage than the failing SVD dose still passes the canary at moderate dose, but fails once pushed to the same error rate as the toxic SVD cell.
- Delivers a data-free, fixed-threshold two-axis screen (coherent_fraction via randomized subspace iteration on discarded singular tails, and energy-weighted error_rate) that correctly classifies all six canary-labeled arms and predicts invention onset on a dense 9×9 sweep of SVD budgets and pruning densities.

## Methodology

The paper's evaluation instrument is a synthetic "agentic canary": procedures with 10 ordered steps plus a conditional 11th step are generated, a target SOP and a distractor SOP are placed in context together, and the model is asked (under two maintenance-mode paraphrases) to enumerate the target SOP's exact steps. Three metrics are scored per query — step recall, branch (conditional) correctness, and the deciding metric, cross-procedure invention (`invented_x`): the count of step-strings drawn from the global SOP vocabulary that belong to neither the target SOP nor its own conditional branch, i.e., steps the model fabricated or leaked from elsewhere. The canary bank is pre-registered at 24 SOPs × 3 seeds (144 paired events per arm), with a requirement that baselines be diagnostic (clean invention axis, usable step recall) or the run is declared non-diagnostic.

Compression operators are compared at matched perplexity dose: coherent low-rank compression (per-tensor SVD truncation) versus magnitude pruning (uniform, unrescaled) as the incoherent-error control, with quantization used as an additional dose control in the mechanism analysis (§4). All compressed arms are rebuilt in memory from saved allocations and none are re-probed after allocation. Three data-cheap acceptance guards are applied to every build: (i) WikiText-2 perplexity over 40×1024-token windows; (ii) MMLU 0-shot accuracy (513 questions, 57 subjects, letter-logprob argmax); and (iii) a data-free output-fidelity probe — centered kernel alignment (CKA) or cosine similarity between compressed and source-model layer activations under random Gaussian probe inputs — which is also the same signal used to allocate the compression (detailed in Appendix A as "the vehicle," a mixed-precision byte-budget allocator solving a multiple-choice knapsack over per-tensor CKA sensitivity). "In-regime" is defined as perplexity ≤1.15× the uncompressed baseline, the paper's stated standard industry acceptance band.

For the mechanism analysis (§4), the authors define two data-free statistics computed with no labeled data over the weight-difference tensors ΔW = W_base − W_mod, aggregated (energy-weighted) across all linear tensors: `coherent_fraction` = (sum of top-8 squared singular values of ΔW) / (sum of squared Frobenius norms of ΔW) — a low-rankness/structure indicator — and `error_rate` = (sum of squared Frobenius norms of ΔW) / (sum of squared Frobenius norms of W) — a dose indicator. Both are computed data-free via randomized subspace iteration for top singular values, and for SVD builds ΔW is read directly off the discarded singular tail of the factors. The detector is a conjunctive gate on fixed thresholds (coherent_fraction > 0.007 AND error_rate > 0.01) validated against the six canary-labeled arms and an 18-point dense sweep (9 SVD budgets × 9 prune densities per architecture, on Mistral-7B and Llama-3.1-8B).

## Results & Highlights

- **Blindspot on Mistral-7B:** the in-regime SVD build sits at perplexity 1.069× (well inside the 1.15× acceptance guard) yet invents +1.729 invented-steps/query [95% CI 1.076, 2.424] versus baseline, consistent across all three seeds (2.06/1.90/1.85). A magnitude-pruning build matched to nearly the same perplexity (1.176×) invents -0.146 [-0.375, +0.035], a CI that includes zero — i.e., statistically indistinguishable from no invention.
- **Blindspot replicates on Qwen3-8B:** the in-regime SVD cell (ppl 1.17×) invents +0.208 [0.042, 0.375] against a damage-matched pruning arm at 0.000 [0, 0]. On Llama-3.1-8B, the "dose SVD" cell (ppl 1.29×) invents +1.285 [0.743, 1.882] against damage-matched pruning's +0.069 [0.021, 0.125] — an 18× gap with both intervals clean (Table 1).
- **Dissociation on the same weights (Qwen3-8B):** the probe-allocated "b98" SVD build CI-wins a paired output-fidelity test (rand-probe NLL CIs [0.024, 0.036] and [0.062, 0.076]) while simultaneously CI-failing the invented-step canary (+0.643 [0.171, 1.229]) — fidelity-up and behavior-broken hold on the identical tensors.
- **Mechanism control:** a quantization arm carrying more raw perplexity damage than the failing SVD dose still passes the canary at moderate dose, but once pushed to the toxic SVD cell's error rate (1.05×), it also fails (pooled clean-slice Δ +0.10 [0.029, 0.20], n=70) — evidence that coherence × rate, not damage magnitude, governs the failure, and that the effect double-dissociates from MMLU (quantization can fail MMLU CI-cleanly while passing the canary, and vice versa for SVD).
- **Detector validation:** the fixed-threshold gate (coherent_fraction > 0.007 AND error_rate > 0.01) classifies all six canary-labeled arms correctly across both tested architectures (Table 2), correctly handling two "trap" arms — coherent-but-gentle Llama in-regime SVD (passes on the rate gate) and incoherent-but-heavy Mistral pruning (passes on the coherence gate) — though one point (Llama density-0.50 pruning, half the weights removed) breaks the pattern and is flagged as an untested prediction/soft edge.

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty:** The core observation — that Goodhart-style proxy metrics (perplexity, MMLU, data-free fidelity) can be satisfied while a deeper behavioral property (procedural faithfulness under agentic execution) fails — is a genuinely useful and underexplored framing for the compression literature, which the paper correctly notes has historically evaluated compression almost exclusively via perplexity/task accuracy (§6). The "coherence × rate" mechanism and the two-axis data-free detector are a concrete, actionable contribution beyond just naming the blindspot.

**Rigor:** The use of paired confidence intervals, pre-registration language, and a stated non-diagnostic-run policy for baselines is a good, unusually rigorous statistical posture for this kind of finding. That said, several paper-specific weaknesses temper confidence: (1) the canary is entirely synthetic (auto-generated SOPs from a "global SOP vocabulary") and evaluated via "base-model prompted (chat-template fallback)" per the Limitations section — meaning these are not instruction-tuned chat models given realistic agent tasks, but base models coaxed into a step-listing format, which the authors themselves admit makes "absolute invention rates instrument-specific"; (2) the two-axis detector's "fixed thresholds across architectures" claim is validated on only two of the three tested architectures (Mistral-7B and Llama-3.1-8B) in the dense 18-point sweep — Qwen3-8B, one of the three headline architectures for the blindspot itself, is absent from Table 2 and the sweep entirely, which weakens the "generalizes across architectures" framing of the detector specifically (as opposed to the canary finding); (3) the detector has an acknowledged failure point (Llama density-0.50 pruning creeping over the coherence gate) that the authors call an "untested prediction," meaning the gate is not yet validated against its own known counterexample.

**Reproducibility:** No code, model checkpoints, or the SOP-generation script are mentioned as released; the "vehicle" allocator (Appendix A) and canary generator both sound bespoke and are described only in prose, so independent replication would require re-implementing both the canary generation logic and the CKA-based sensitivity/allocation pipeline from scratch.

**Significance:** If the finding replicates on instruction-tuned models and real-world agent benchmarks (rather than the synthetic SOP canary), it has real practical weight for any team shipping compressed models into agentic/tool-use pipelines, since it directly targets the standard "perplexity + MMLU + fidelity" acceptance battery used industry-wide. The explicit scope-limiting honesty (spectrum-conditional guard evasion on Llama, no MoE testing, "we claim only that the accepted guard stack misses this failure, not that the failure exhausts agentic behavior") is a strength that suggests the authors are not overclaiming.

**Red flags:** the missing institutional affiliation combined with the terse, statistically-dense writing style and the "72-hour autonomous campaign" mention raise a mild but real question of how much of the paper's experimentation and possibly drafting was automated/AI-assisted with limited independent human review — worth keeping in mind given the small model count (n=3), synthetic-only benchmark, and the fact that quantitative claims cannot be independently checked without released code.

## Strengths

- Clear, falsifiable, pre-registered experimental design with paired confidence intervals rather than point-estimate claims.
- A genuine within-arm dissociation (same weights, opposite verdicts on fidelity vs. canary) that is hard to explain away as noise or cherry-picking.
- The mechanism claim (coherence × rate) is tested against an active dose-matched quantization control, not just asserted from the SVD-vs-pruning contrast alone.
- Delivers an actionable, cheap, data-free detector rather than stopping at the negative finding, and stress-tests it against an 18-point sweep rather than only the six labeled arms.
- Unusually candid limitations section that explicitly flags scope boundaries (spectrum-conditional guard evasion, synthetic canary, no MoE testing, detector's own soft edge).

## Limitations

- Tested only on three dense decoder LLMs at 7-8B scale (Qwen3-8B, Mistral-7B, Llama-3.1-8B); no MoE architectures were included in the controlled battery (a prior low-bit-expert MoE build reportedly passed QA and failed the canary, but this is mentioned only anecdotally, outside the controlled experiments).
- The canary is a synthetic-SOP, base-model-prompted benchmark (chat-template fallback), so absolute invention rates are explicitly called "instrument-specific" by the authors; it is unclear how this maps onto real-world agentic tool-use failures.
- The perplexity-guard "evasion" effect is spectrum-conditional — it only manifests when a model's weight spectrum admits enough in-guard low-rank headroom (true for Qwen3-8B and Mistral-7B but not Llama-3.1-8B, where the perplexity guard catches the toxic SVD build "by accident").
- The data-free detector's fixed thresholds are fit to only six labeled arms plus an 18-point sweep restricted to two of the three architectures and to the SVD/pruning operator families only; the authors explicitly state it does not resolve "within-quantization subtleties" and flag one known counterexample (extreme 50% pruning on Llama) as unresolved.
- No code or released artifacts are referenced, limiting independent verification of the numeric results.

## Relevance

这篇论文与大模型压缩部署、智能体安全评估直接相关：如果读者在做模型量化/剪枝/低秩压缩后部署为工具调用或流程执行智能体，本文提出的"困惑度和保真度达标≠智能体安全"结论以及免数据两轴检测器可作为压缩验收流程的补充检查项，但其结论目前仅在合成 SOP 基准和三个 7-8B 稠密模型上验证，落地前建议在真实智能体任务上复核。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the invented-step failure replicate on instruction-tuned, RLHF'd chat models given realistic (non-synthetic) agentic tool-use or SOP-following benchmarks, rather than base-model chat-template fallback prompting?
- Would the coherent_fraction/error_rate detector's fixed thresholds hold on MoE architectures, where the authors only have an anecdotal (uncontrolled) data point?
- How does the invented-step rate interact with chain-of-thought or self-verification prompting — would asking the agent to double-check its step list against the provided SOP text reduce or eliminate the confabulation?
- Since the detector was validated on only two of the three headline architectures in the dense sweep, would adding Qwen3-8B to the sweep preserve the same fixed thresholds, or would architecture-specific calibration be needed after all?
- Does the coherence × rate mechanism extend to other coherent-error compression methods beyond SVD (e.g., structured/block-sparse pruning, learned low-rank adapters merged post-hoc), which the paper explicitly leaves untested alongside 2:4 structured sparsity?
