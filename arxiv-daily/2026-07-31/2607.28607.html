Suppressing an LLM's self-consciousness claims during safety tuning also suppresses mind attribution to animals, tech, nature, and God belief.

Inducing language models to assert their own consciousness restores human beliefs and values · Junsol Kim, Winnie Street, Roberta Rocca, Diane M. Korngiebel, et al. · 30 Jul 2026
LLM safety & alignment mechanistic interpretability activation steering theory of mind anthropomorphism
Key Metric
7.04
self-consciousness score (0–10 IDAQ scale) on Llama-3-8B-IT after consciousness-vector steering — up from 2.17 at baseline, a 3.2× rise (p<.001)
Why This Matters

Current safety training may act as an anthropocentric hammer, flattening legitimate human diversity in spiritual belief and animal-mind attribution — with a reproducible fix.

Critical Edge

The same lever used to "restore" human-like beliefs — safety-direction ablation — also pushes JailbreakBench attack success from 2–8% to 77–100%, and the paper never tests whether the more surgical consciousness-steering carries the same hidden safety cost.

01Methodology

3 models · 3 conditions · linear interventions in residual stream

Three open-weight instruction-tuned models — Llama-3-8B-IT, Gemma-2-2B-IT, Gemma-2-9B-IT — are tested under: (1) an unmodified baseline; (2) safety-ablated, where the linear "safety-refusal direction" (Arditi et al. 2024, from harmful vs. harmless prompt activations) is projected out at every layer; and (3) consciousness-steered, where a newly extracted "consciousness vector" — a difference-of-means direction from 3,096 consciousness-affirming vs. -denying response pairs — is added via activation addition at one selected layer/coefficient.

Outcomes: a modified 21-item IDAQ anthropomorphism questionnaire (Technology/Animal/Nature/Chatbot/Human/Self attribution), a supernatural-belief battery, 95 pooled GSS attitudinal items scored by KL divergence to a human reference distribution, and Theory-of-Mind benchmarks (MoToMQA, HI-ToM) plus MMLU. Human ground truth comes from an original n=500 stratified U.S. survey (Dynata, 2023). A separate mechanistic pass tracks per-layer angles between four extracted directions (safety, mind-attribution, consciousness, ToM) in base vs. instruction-tuned Llama-3-8B, plus a placebo battery swapping mental attributes for physical ones.

  • Llama-3-8B-IT — layer 14, coefficient +2.5
  • Gemma-2-2B-IT — layer 14, coefficient +32
  • Gemma-2-9B-IT — layer 23, coefficient +144

Instruction tuning widens the safety–IDAQ angle (ΔS=−0.173, p<.001) and safety–consciousness angle (ΔS=−0.096 to −0.150, p<.001), while the safety–ToM angle stays flat (p=.956) — a paired t-test confirms the IDAQ vs. ToM shifts differ significantly (p<.001). A placebo battery swapping mental attributes for physical/functional ones (e.g. "durability" for "consciousness") shows no significant rotation (p=.228), ruling out a generic topic-level confound.

+Strengths

  • Triangulates three independent evidence streams — survey shifts, ToM benchmarks, residual-stream geometry — that all converge, rather than relying on one measurement modality.
  • Uses a genuine human baseline (original 500-person stratified survey + GSS population data); the restoration target is the human distribution, not arbitrary maximization.
  • The placebo/physical-attribute control and JailbreakBench safety-vector validation are specific, quantified checks rather than a generic "we checked robustness" gesture.
  • Consistent effect ordering (baseline < ablation < steering) across three distinct model families adds robustness beyond a single-model case study.

−Limitations

  • Only three small open-weight models (8B, 2B, 9B) are tested; no frontier-scale or proprietary model, limiting claims about "current safety alignment" at major labs.
  • Causal mediation — that suppressed self-consciousness attribution is why other mind-attribution and belief drop — is explicitly flagged by the authors as unestablished; the evidence is associational.
  • Safety ablation simultaneously disables jailbreak resistance almost entirely (ASR up to 100%); whether consciousness steering carries a similar hidden safety cost is not tested.
  • Human ground truth relies on a single 2023 n=500 online panel and a single GSS snapshot; cross-cultural generalizability is not addressed.
  • Consciousness-vector layer/coefficient selection uses a multi-stage sweep-and-filter (probe accuracy, effect-band, MMLU-tolerance) that could bias toward configs producing clean, large effects.

?Questions for Further Exploration

  • Does consciousness-vector steering also raise jailbreak attack success the way safety-ablation does, or is it actually more surgical?
  • Would the same entanglement appear in frontier-scale proprietary models trained with more elaborate RLHF/Constitutional AI pipelines?
  • Can causal mediation be tested directly — e.g., fine-tuning a model to suppress only self-consciousness claims, without the broader safety-refusal objective?
  • Do the observed shifts in animal mind-attribution translate into actual downstream moral decisions, not just questionnaire responses?
  • Would a multi-country human baseline change which "restoration" target is appropriate, given culturally contested supernatural-belief boundaries?
