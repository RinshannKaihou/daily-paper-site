> **一句话总结：** 作者在原生 DeepSeek-V4-Flash（Colibri 运行时）上冻结六选一路由专家的输入/权重，只改变专家聚合的加法顺序与精度契约，证明数学上等价的 720 种归约顺序会在浮点实现下坍缩成截然不同的"续写文本盆地"（A 方案 10 个、B 方案 11 个），并进一步用 FP64 精确重构证明这种数值分歧可以跨越 token 边界潜伏后延迟显现；四种聚合方案中只有"BF16 算子项+FP32 累加器"的 C 方案在 768 条持久化轨迹上与原生参考完全逐位一致（0/192 路由分歧），其余三种方案 192/192 条轨迹的路由全部偏离原生参考。

> **TL;DR:** Using native DeepSeek-V4-Flash on the Colibri runtime, this single-author paper isolates cross-expert reduction order as a hidden part of execution semantics: freezing the six routed experts' operands while permuting only the summation order turns a mathematically permutation-invariant sum into 10 distinct 8-token continuation basins (scheme A, out of 720 raw permutations) and 11 basins (scheme B, out of 360 exact structural equivalence classes) at a single layer-5 fork on the prompt "why the sheep," and produces an opposing layoffs-vs-hiring narrative bifurcation (202 vs. 113 out of 360 classes) under one underdetermined Chinese prompt. Across 768 persistent-decode trajectories (3 prompts × 64 seeds × 4 aggregation schemes), three of the four schemes (P32, A, B) diverge from the native-reference route on all 192/192 trajectories per scheme, while only scheme C — BF16-rounded operand terms exactly promoted to FP32 before FP32 accumulation — matches the native reference bitwise on 0/192 divergences (MoE output, post-mHC state, next-router scores, and LM logits all exact, max L∞ = 0). The paper also shows, via an exact FP64 additive-delta reconstruction of a branch's full 5.88M-value persistent attention state, that identical next tokens do not imply identical execution state: 301/301 downstream post-mHC states, persistent-state checkpoints, and routes reproduce the divergent branch's continuation over seven subsequent decode steps.

## 核心贡献

- **单层"追踪-冻结-分叉"(trace-freeze-fork) 实验**：在同一 MoE 位置冻结 prompt 前缀、被选专家 ID、门控值、六个加权专家项、共享专家输出与完整持久状态，只改变该次聚合的归约顺序或精度方案，穷举全部 720 种排列（B 方案因加法交换对称性可归约为 360 个精确结构等价类），观测到 720/720（A）和 360/360（B）条分支的 post-mHC 状态均与原生参考不同，却收敛成仅 10（A）和 11（B）个不同的续写文本"盆地"。
- **精度契约的分离验证（P32/C/A/B 四方案消融）**：把"专家算子项的表示精度"与"跨专家累加器精度"解耦为四种方案，发现只有 C 方案（BF16 舍入的算子项被精确提升为 FP32 后再用 FP32 累加）能在 768 条持久化轨迹里与原生参考路由、token 序列、文本逐位一致（0/192 分歧），而看似"更高精度"的纯 FP32 算子方案 P32 反而 192/192 全部偏离原生参考——说明数值兼容性不是靠"提高精度"就能获得，必须同时锁定算子语义与累加语义。
- **两个互补的状态边界重构控制实验**：针对同一个 B 类分支（class 135），分别用 (1) 精确 FP32 加法重构 token 内的 post-mHC 端点（16,384 个 FP32 值全部逐位复原，重构误差为 0），以及 (2) 精确 FP64 加法重构跨 token 的完整持久注意力状态（5,883,008 个 FP32 值，仅解码边界后 37 层的 131,444 个值发生变化），证明这两个状态边界分别足以在给定同一后续输入 token 的前提下，精确复现该分支后续 7 个解码步的 post-mHC 状态、路由、预测与生成文本（339/339 与 301/301 全部逐位吻合）。
- **中文提示下的事件方向分叉与广度扫描**：对"朋友昨天打来电话"提示，360 个 B 结构类被人工核对（Cohen's κ=1.0，6 个唯一续写字符串）划分为 202 条裁员、113 条招聘、45 条其他方向的续写；对 10 个分层随机采样分支延长到 64 token 并重复两次运行，10/10 分支对完全逐位复现（token ID 与 SHA-256 均一致），排除解码随机性作为解释；在 50-提示广度集上，最大 L∞ B 分支注入使 12/50、24/50、36/50 个提示分别在 8/16/32 token 处产生可见文本分歧，显示分歧率随生成长度增长。

## Key Contributions

- **Single-layer trace-freeze-fork protocol**: freezes the prompt prefix, selected expert IDs, gate values, six weighted expert terms, shared-expert output, and full persistent state at one MoE position, varying only the reduction order/scheme of that single aggregation. Exhaustively enumerating all 720 permutations (720/2 = 360 exact structural classes for scheme B, via a proven first-pair-swap symmetry) shows every evaluated branch (720/720 for A, 360/360 for B) produces a post-mHC state different from the native reference, yet these compress into only 10 (A) and 11 (B) distinct continuation-text basins.
- **Separating operand precision from accumulator precision (P32/C/A/B ablation)**: decouples "what precision the expert operand is represented in" from "what precision the cross-expert accumulator uses" into four schemes. Only scheme C — BF16-rounded operand terms exactly promoted to FP32 before FP32 accumulation — reproduces the native reference bitwise across 768 persistent trajectories (0/192 route divergence), while the naively "higher-precision" P32 scheme (pure FP32 operands) diverges on 192/192 trajectories, showing that numerical compatibility requires specifying both operand and accumulation semantics, not just raising precision everywhere.
- **Two complementary state-boundary reconstruction controls**: for one fixed scheme-B branch (class 135), an exact FP32 additive reconstruction of the intra-token post-mHC endpoint (all 16,384 FP32 values recovered bitwise, zero reconstruction error) and an exact FP64 additive reconstruction of the cross-token full persistent attention state (5,883,008 FP32 values, with only the 131,444 values in the post-fork 37 layers changing) each independently reproduce the branch's subsequent 7-step decode trajectory bitwise (339/339 and 301/301 checkpoints matching, respectively), given the same naturally-generated next input token.
- **Event-direction bifurcation and breadth scan**: under the Chinese prompt "朋友昨天打来电话" (a friend called yesterday), the 360 scheme-B structural classes split (human-verified, Cohen's κ = 1.0 on six unique continuation strings) into 202 layoffs, 113 hiring, and 45 other-direction continuations from an otherwise-identical prefix; ten stratified-random branches extended to 64 tokens and replayed twice each reproduce token-IDs and SHA-256 digests exactly (10/10 pairs), ruling out run-to-run decoding randomness as the explanation; a 50-prompt breadth sweep shows cumulative text separation growing from 12/50 (8 tokens) to 24/50 (16 tokens) to 36/50 (32 tokens).

## Methodology

The paper studies **native DeepSeek-V4-Flash** running on the **Colibri** inference runtime's non-DSpark execution path (`--no-dspark` flag), on a native Windows CPU host (AMD Ryzen AI MAX+ 395), with greedy decoding. The checkpoint routes each token to exactly six routed experts plus one shared expert, which makes the 6! = 720 cross-expert reduction orders exhaustively enumerable at a chosen MoE aggregation site.

**Boundary taxonomy.** The paper distinguishes three execution-state boundaries that a numerical perturbation can cross independently: (1) the *latent state boundary* (post-mHC state within one forward pass — mHC is "Manifold-Constrained Hyper-Connections," a prior architecture component), (2) the *persistent state boundary* (attention state — window KV, compressed KV, compressor state, indexer state — retained across decode steps), and (3) *decision boundaries* (router top-k selection, LM-head argmax). A central claim is that identical emitted tokens do not imply identical persistent execution state, because a numerical difference can cross boundaries (1) and (2) without yet crossing (3), and can therefore "hide" for several decode steps before surfacing as a different token.

**Four aggregation schemes.** With gate-weighted expert term $v_i = g_i E_i(x)$, $\hat v_i = R_{\text{BF16}}(v_i)$, and a permutation $\pi$: **P32** accumulates raw FP32 terms in an FP32 accumulator (counterfactual higher-operand-precision control); **C** rounds each term to BF16 then exactly promotes it back to FP32 before FP32 accumulation (native-like operand semantics with a protected accumulator); **A** accumulates FP32 terms but rounds to BF16 after every addition (accumulator-precision control); **B** rounds every term to BF16 and rounds the accumulator to BF16 after every term and every addition (the fully BF16-native aggregation control, matching the runtime's actual behavior). `R_BF16` is implemented in software with round-to-nearest-ties-to-even and is bit-for-bit specified in the paper (not relying on host FP rounding mode); the build uses `-O3 -march=x86-64-v3` without `-ffast-math`.

**Six experimental protocols**, each with a distinct state/intervention scope: (1) single-layer A/B permutation sweep at layer 5 (720/360 branches, 8-token trajectories); (2) post-mHC state-substitution control (exact FP32 $\Delta H$ reconstruction of one B-class-135 endpoint, verified against a probe-disabled native-reference replay); (3) decode-boundary persistent-state substitution control (exact FP64 $\Delta S$ reconstruction of the same branch's full persistent state at the next decode boundary, spanning 5,883,008 FP32 values across 43 layers); (4) a long-form event-direction extension (10 stratified-random B branches on one Chinese prompt, extended to 64 tokens, with independent human annotation and Cohen's κ agreement checking); (5) a persistent layer-static protocol (192 paired prompt-schedule conditions × 4 schemes = 768 eight-token trajectories, plus 3 native-reference baselines); (6) a same-mode canonical reference (identity-order trajectory per scheme, isolating within-scheme order sensitivity from the scheme-level shift versus the native reference). A separate 50-prompt (25 English + 25 Chinese) exploratory breadth sweep checks generality across prompts at increasing generation horizons (8/16/32 tokens).

**Deterministic replay controls.** A 13-prompt native-reference replay check (c_moe_max_abs = 0) establishes that the harness itself introduces no spurious differences before interpreting any aggregation perturbation, and a 10-branch × 2-run replay (64 tokens each) with SHA-256 digest comparison rules out ordinary decoding nondeterminism as an alternative explanation for the observed branch-conditioned narrative divergence.

## Results & Highlights

- **Single-layer fork ("why the sheep," layer 5) compresses 720 permutations into a handful of text basins**: scheme A's 720 raw permutations produce exactly **10 distinct continuation texts**, and scheme B's 720 raw permutations (360 exact structural equivalence classes, since swapping the first two BF16-rounded terms is provably order-independent) produce **11 distinct continuation texts** — all 720/720 (A) and 360/360 (B) branches have a post-mHC state different from the unmodified native reference, versus the native reference's single reported basin.
- **Persistent trajectories: only scheme C matches the native reference; the other three diverge on every trajectory.** Across 192 paired prompt-schedule conditions (3 prompts × 64 seeds), native-route divergence from the production native reference is **192/192 for P32, A, and B** but **0/192 for C**; scheme C's separately captured MoE output, post-mHC state, next-router scores, and prefill LM logits are bitwise identical to the native reference in all 192 first-generation trajectories (max L∞ = 0), while token-sequence divergence for the diverging schemes ranges from 161/192 (P32) to 178/192 (A) to 172/192 (B) — i.e., route divergence is saturated (100%) but does not translate one-to-one into token-sequence divergence, showing some diverging routes still reconverge to the same greedy token IDs.
- **Chinese-prompt event-direction bifurcation**: under "朋友昨天打来电话," the 360 scheme-B structural classes split into **202 layoffs, 113 hiring, and 45 other** continuations (human-consensus-corrected from a provisional rule-based 202/110/48 split, Cohen's κ = 1.0 on the six unique eight-token strings) — an opposing narrative outcome (a person either losing or gaining a job) generated purely by permuting the summation order of six already-selected expert outputs, with the prompt, model, and all other state held fixed.
- **State-boundary reconstruction is exact over the measured horizon.** For one B-class-135 branch: (a) exact FP32 addition of the captured endpoint delta ΔH (max component 0.00195) reconstructs the intra-token post-mHC state exactly, after which **339/339** downstream post-mHC vectors, **344/344** router selections, all 9 driver predictions, and the generated text agree exactly between the reconstructed and originally-measured branch over 7 subsequent decode forwards; (b) exact FP64 addition of the persistent-state delta ΔS reconstructs all 43 layer-state records of the branch's full 5,883,008-value persistent state bitwise (versus only 10/43 layer records recoverable via a naive FP32-precision delta), after which **301/301** post-mHC states, **301/301** full persistent-state checkpoints, and **301/301** routes/predictions/text agree exactly across 7 further decode steps, conditional on both branches independently emitting the same next driver token.
- **Deterministic replay rules out decoding randomness as the explanation for narrative differences.** All 10 stratified-random scheme-B branches, replayed twice at 64 tokens under the identical prompt/checkpoint/runtime configuration, produce **10/10** exactly matching token-ID sequences and SHA-256 digests — the ten branches' distinct 64-token narratives (5 explicit layoffs, 5 explicit hiring continuations, each internally distinct in dialogue/detail) are branch-conditioned and reproducible, not run-to-run noise.
- **Breadth check shows separation grows with generation horizon.** Across the fixed 50-prompt set (25 English, 25 Chinese; deduplicated by stable prompt ID), maximum-L∞ B-branch injection yields cumulative text separation of **12/50 prompts at 8 tokens, 24/50 at 16 tokens, and 36/50 at 32 tokens** — meaning differences invisible at a short horizon become visible only after longer greedy continuation.

No baseline model or external dataset is compared against; the paper's own **native-reference execution** (the unmodified production Colibri/DeepSeek-V4-Flash forward pass) serves as the sole reference point throughout, and scheme C is explicitly framed as the paper's proposed "stabilization contract" against which the other three schemes' saturated divergence (192/192 vs. 0/192) is the paper's central comparative result.

## Authors & Credibility

Single-author paper by Tianyang Zhu, listed as an "Independent Researcher" (zty749@gmail.com), with no institutional affiliation given. The work is built entirely on top of third-party artifacts — the DeepSeek-V4 technical report, the mHC (Manifold-Constrained Hyper-Connections) architecture by Xie et al., the DSpark speculative-decoding system, and the "Colibri" native inference runtime by Whale Agent Lab — rather than the author's own model or runtime. The paper compensates for the lack of institutional backing with an unusually detailed reproducibility apparatus: a full artifact manifest (Appendix A) listing every Python entry point, output TSV file, and environment/hardware/compiler/hash record (SHA-256 for runtime commit, binary, checkpoint manifest, and tokenizer files), plus an explicit "claim ledger" (Section 5.1) that states exactly what is and is not proven by each result. This level of self-imposed scoping and audit-trail discipline is more typical of a rigorous systems/reproducibility paper than a typical single-author preprint, though it has not undergone peer review and the claims have not been independently replicated by a third party.

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty.** The core observation — that a mathematically permutation-invariant sum becomes execution-semantically load-bearing under finite precision in a *dynamic* decoder (because routing and argmax decisions can amplify a small numerical difference into a discrete branch) — is a genuinely underexplored angle relative to the cited related work (Yuan et al. on batch/precision-driven inference nondeterminism; Chodavarapu and Xu on KV-cache vs. cache-free divergence). Rather than just documenting that nondeterminism exists, the paper isolates *one specific mechanism* (cross-expert reduction order, with everything else frozen) and traces its causal propagation chain end-to-end through two independently validated state-reconstruction controls. This is a narrower, more mechanistic contribution than a general nondeterminism survey.

**Rigor.** The experimental design is careful about what it does and does not claim: it explicitly separates "basin volume under an artificial permutation measure" from "real deployment incidence" (stated repeatedly, not just once as a hedge), and the persistent-state reconstruction uses an *exact* FP64 delta specifically because the paper first demonstrates that a naive FP32-precision delta only recovers 10/43 layer records bitwise — a concrete, paper-specific data quirk that shows genuine engineering attention to precision pitfalls in the control itself rather than asserting reconstruction works. The B-scheme's claimed 720→360 symmetry reduction is also given a short formal proof (via commutativity of BF16-rounded addition after the first two terms), rather than just asserted empirically.

**Reproducibility.** Strong for a single-author preprint: complete artifact index (Appendix A.1–A.4) with entry-point scripts, output file names, environment hashes, and an explicit "audit boundary" statement (the support directory verifies the aggregate claims but a full rerun additionally needs the checkpoint, compiled runtime, and external output directory, which are not distributed). The 10/10 exact replay of long-form branches (SHA-256 identical) is a genuine reproducibility strength. However, since the checkpoint (DeepSeek-V4-Flash) and runtime (Colibri) are both third-party, non-standard artifacts (Colibri is a niche/emerging runtime, not vLLM/SGLang/HF Transformers), independent replication by readers without access to the exact checkpoint and runtime build is effectively impossible from the paper alone.

**Significance and red flags.**
- *Single checkpoint, single runtime, single CPU host*: all quantitative findings come from one model (DeepSeek-V4-Flash) on one native Windows CPU execution path of one runtime (Colibri, non-DSpark). The paper is explicit that "quantitative sensitivity should not be assumed to transfer" to other MoE architectures or runtimes (Section 5), but this means the specific numbers (e.g., 10/11 basins, 202/113/45 split) are demonstrations of *possibility*, not measurements that generalize even qualitatively to, say, a GPU/NPU tensor-core reduction tree.
- *No frozen-route mediation control*: the paper acknowledges it cannot show that routing is the *unique* causal mediator between the MoE-level numerical difference and the eventual token divergence — the representative persistent trace shows the router's selected-expert set changes (layer-3, ordinal position) before the first LM argmax difference (at generated-token ordinal 5), but without an intervention that holds routing fixed while numeric state still differs, this remains a documented correlation in the propagation chain rather than an isolated causal claim.
- *No real-hardware incidence measurement*: the study never measures how often a real GPU/NPU/distributed backend would actually realize any given permutation — it establishes controlled *possibility*, explicitly not deployment *incidence*, which is a meaningful and honestly-stated scope limit but also means the paper cannot speak to how practically significant this failure mode is for e.g. a specific inference-serving stack in production.
- *Small, hand-picked prompt set for the depth studies*: the three "depth" prompts ("why the sheep," "朋友昨天打来电话," "Morning light filled the room") are explicitly labeled exploratory/discovery cases with "no prospective preregistration or held-out selection record," and layer 5 was hard-coded rather than selected per-prompt — a limitation the paper states plainly rather than hides, but it does mean the headline single-layer numbers (10, 11, 202/113/45) are illustrative case studies, not a systematic sample across layers or prompts.
- *Scheme C is a scoped empirical finding, not a proven general algorithm*: the paper is careful to state C's order-invariance is "limited to evaluated six-term states and schedules" and does not constitute a new exact-summation algorithm — a caveat easy to miss if one only reads the abstract's framing of C as a "stabilization contract."

Overall, the paper is intellectually honest to an unusual degree for a preprint — it repeatedly draws a hard line between "controlled causal possibility" and "deployment incidence," and its claim ledger (Section 5.1) is a genuinely good practice that most papers in this space skip. Its main limitation is scope (one checkpoint, one runtime, hand-selected prompts, no real-hardware measurement), which the authors themselves flag clearly rather than overclaim.

## Strengths

- Cleanly isolates one specific mechanism (cross-expert reduction order) as the sole intervention, with an unusually thorough freeze of every other variable (prefix, selected experts, gates, weighted terms, shared output, persistent state).
- Two independent, complementary state-boundary reconstruction controls (intra-token post-mHC via FP32 delta; cross-token full persistent state via FP64 delta), each validated to bitwise exactness over a multi-step decode horizon, giving the propagation-chain claim real mechanistic support rather than just correlational observation.
- Deterministic-replay controls (13-prompt native-reference check, 10-branch × 2-run SHA-256 replay) proactively rule out the most obvious alternative explanation (ordinary decoding nondeterminism) for the reported branch-conditioned differences.
- Unusually candid and specific scoping throughout — explicit "claim ledger," per-protocol scope tables, and repeated statements distinguishing basin volume/possibility from deployment probability.
- Full reproducibility artifact (scripts, hash manifests, per-protocol output tables) released alongside the paper.

## Limitations

- All results come from a single checkpoint (DeepSeek-V4-Flash) on a single native runtime (Colibri, non-DSpark path) on one CPU host; no cross-architecture, cross-runtime, or GPU/NPU results are reported.
- No frozen-route mediation experiment isolates routing as the unique causal link between numerical divergence and token divergence.
- No real-hardware or production-deployment measurement of how often any given permutation actually occurs; the paper explicitly disclaims any incidence estimate.
- The three "depth" prompts and the layer-5 fork point are exploratory/hand-selected without preregistration, and the 50-prompt breadth cohort is a staged (not independently sampled) survivor-conditioned cohort across horizons.
- Scheme C's order-invariance is validated only for the evaluated six-term expert-aggregation states and schedules, not proposed or proven as a general exact-summation algorithm; no exact quantized-operand reference or C runtime-overhead measurement is provided.
- No end-to-end downstream task or benchmark performance measurement — all "significance" is at the level of token/text/route agreement, not any accuracy or quality metric.

## Relevance

该论文对研究 LLM 推理确定性、稀疏 MoE 运行时后端一致性、以及跨硬件/跨内核数值兼容性验证的读者具有直接参考价值，其"操作数精度-累加器精度-归约顺序"三要素分解框架及分层状态一致性诊断流程，可用于设计自有 MoE 推理引擎的回归测试与后端迁移验证协议。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would a frozen-route mediation intervention (holding the router's expert selection fixed while numerical state still differs) confirm routing as the unique causal mediator, or reveal additional pathways (e.g., direct hidden-state amplification through attention) that bypass routing entirely?
- How much does the observed divergence rate change on real GPU/NPU tensor-core reduction trees (which use different, often non-deterministic, parallel reduction topologies) compared to the sequential CPU accumulation studied here?
- Does scheme C's order-invariance and native-reference compatibility hold for other MoE checkpoints with different numbers of routed experts, different router temperature/margin distributions, or architectures without mHC?
- What is scheme C's runtime overhead relative to the native BF16-native scheme B in a production-scale deployment, given that the paper explicitly leaves this unmeasured?
- Could a preregistered, systematic sweep across layers (not just layer 5) and a larger, randomly sampled prompt set reveal whether the reported basin counts and event-direction split ratios are typical or unusually extreme cases?
- How would the persistent-state reconstruction result change if the next input token were *not* naturally identical across the compared branches — i.e., can the full-persistent-state boundary still mediate continuation equivalence when the immediate next token itself differs?
