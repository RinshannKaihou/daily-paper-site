Coupling a conjecture's statement to an audited mechanism graph lifts blind-recovery scores by +21 points over drafting alone.

一句话总结：MECA用多智能体框架把"猜想陈述"和"支撑机制（不等式/不变量/分解/归约等证明论证）"作为一对耦合对象共同精化，在20篇论文的盲测复原任务上把综合评分从基线的48.0分提升到69.0分（+21.0），并用该流程构造的100个猜想中有46%被独立的自动证明系统QED给出可验证的证明或反驳。

MECA leads on every scored dimension: Conclusion +6.0 (21.0 vs 15.0), Quantitative Accuracy +5.5 (12.5 vs 7.0), Assumptions +3.5, Boundary +3.5, Setting +2.5. On 100 open-ended conjectures fed statement-only to the QED prover, 46% receive an independently verified proof or refutation (35 proved, 11 refuted, 54 unresolved).

Why This Matters

Provides a template for agent systems that must track partial evidence and unresolved gaps explicitly, plus a reusable anti-confirmation-bias design for multi-agent pipelines.

中文原文（Relevance）：该工作与AI辅助数学研究、多智能体LLM系统设计密切相关，其"机制图"式的结构化中间表示（要求-结论-缺口）思路，对设计需要显式追踪"部分证据+未解缺口"的智能体系统（不限于数学）有参考价值；其严格的信息边界与反确认偏差设计（盲态挑战者）也是多智能体评审/审核流程可借鉴的通用范式。

▲ Critical Edge

The conjecture generator and the target-alignment evaluator both run on the same model configuration (gpt-5.5, "xhigh"), so the reported +21.0-point recovery gain has no independent human or cross-model check.

Methodology

1

Source-grounded seeding — Route A distills reviewed papers into a frozen "source pack" and drafts Raw Seeds (closest known result, bounded delta beyond it, anticipated bottleneck); Route B adapts curated open-problem descriptions. A Seed Validator rejects restatements and direct corollaries, checking both research value and mathematical validity.

2

Mechanism–problem co-exploration — under a frozen target anchor (central object, admissible assumptions, source boundary, forbidden trivializations), an Explorer proposes/revises/splits the candidate statement while a Critic independently audits each mechanism's typed Req/Prov/Gap interface before it enters the dependency graph. A Coverage Judge stops at "SubstantivePartial": meaningful progress with 1–3 precise unresolved cores.

3

Prover-facing projection — accepted (anchor, candidate, mechanism-subgraph) triples are deterministically compiled into a self-contained statement that converts remaining gaps into explicit proof obligations, then screened by a Blind Prover and a Refute Prover before deduplication by mathematical content.

Bias control: a Final Challenger is deliberately denied the mechanism graph, critic history, and exploration trace — it sees only the candidate, anchor, seed, and source pack, and must independently attempt a direct proof or counterexample. Generation/scoring runs on gpt-5.5 "xhigh"; the QED prover on gpt-5.6-sol "xhigh".

Of the 54 unresolved cases (Fig. 5): 44 (81.5%) genuine technical proof difficulty, 6 (11.1%) missing assumptions, 2 (3.7%) insufficient mathematical foothold, 2 (3.7%) ambiguous formulation — so only 10% of all 100 generated conjectures show an identifiable formulation/proof-readiness defect.

  • Domain spread (Fig. 3): the 100 conjectures skew heavily toward optimization and numerical analysis (35% optimization incl. decentralized tracking 12%, derivative-free 10%; 14% variational operators incl. Bregman proximal 10%), reflecting the authors' own specialty rather than broad mathematics coverage.
  • Case study — split under pressure: one candidate evolves via a "split" action into two sibling conjectures after counterexample pressure, retaining an unresolved "technical difficulty" core (Appendix F.2).
  • Case study — repaired to refutation: a first proof attempt fails a numerical audit, is repaired, and QED then delivers a fully verified refutation — treated as a legitimate, checkable adjudication rather than a failure (Appendix F.3).

Strengths

  • Principled, falsifiable formalization of "mechanism" with explicit Req/Prov/Gap semantics and typed composition, rather than an ad hoc prompt for "generate a conjecture with a proof sketch."
  • Strong information-boundary discipline throughout (frozen anchors, immutable target boundaries, blind Final Challenger, statement-only QED submission) — unusually careful about leakage/confirmation bias for an LLM-agent paper.
  • Measures both process quality (article-blind target recovery against a rubric) and downstream utility (independent prover verification), not a single self-reported metric.
  • Transparent, granular failure-mode taxonomy for unresolved cases rather than lumping everything into "prover timed out."

Limitations

  • Same-family model used for generation and evaluation, undermining independence of the +21.0-point recovery-benchmark result.
  • No comparison against any published competing conjecture-generation system (LeanConjecturer, Lemmanaid, COMPOSE, etc.), only an internal baseline.
  • No direct ablation isolating the causal contribution of the mechanism bundle to downstream prover success — QED never sees it.
  • Domains of the 100-conjecture benchmark are heavily skewed toward optimization/numerical analysis, limiting generalizability claims.
  • Source materials (private paper repository, private open-problem collection) are not released, limiting reproducibility.
  • Recovery benchmark size (20 targets) and conjecture set (100, only 8 from the open-problem route) are modest; evaluator variance is not reported beyond the single averaged score.

Questions for Further Exploration

  1. How would the 35%/11%/54% QED outcome distribution change if the mechanism bundle (not just the bare statement) were exposed to the prover?
  2. How does MECA compare quantitatively — not just qualitatively via citation — against LeanConjecturer, Lemmanaid, or COMPOSE on the same article-blind recovery benchmark?
  3. Would a different evaluator model family (not gpt-5.5, the family used for generation) change the target-alignment score gap between MECA and the baseline?
  4. How well does the mechanism-centered approach transfer to non-optimization domains (number theory, combinatorics, algebraic geometry) where "mechanisms" may take very different forms?
  5. What is the human-expert agreement rate with MECA's Coverage Judge and Final Challenger decisions, which currently rely entirely on LLM self-audit?
