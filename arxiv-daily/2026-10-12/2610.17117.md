> **一句话总结：** 该论文把每个释义对（paraphrase pair）当作"两个激活应当一致"的局部见证，在释义图上构建 cellular sheaf，用 sheaf Laplacian 的核 H0 定义内容子空间、其谱补空间定义措辞变化方向：20 维 H0 在 CounterFact 留出事实检索上比 LEACE 保留子空间平均高 +17.1 个百分点（GPT-2 上 85.3% vs 43.1%），谱补方向消融对输出的影响是等变异性对照的 5.6–26.5 倍，而多层网格上的 Hodge 谐波质量以 Spearman ρ=0.90（p=0.014）预测六个架构的 steering 脆弱性——全部无需训练、直接从冻结模型读出。

> **TL;DR:** A training-free interpretability method that builds a cellular sheaf over paraphrase graphs (hidden states on nodes, a learned P^T restriction map into a 128-D edge space) and splits representations into H0 (phrasing-invariant content) and H1 directions (phrasing-dependent variation) via the sheaf Laplacian's spectrum. On held-out ROME CounterFact fact retrieval, 20-D sheaf H0 beats LEACE's full-dimension preserved subspace on all 8 architectures (mean +17.1 pp; GPT-2 85.3% vs 43.1%), and joint multi-paraphrase constraints add another +6.5 pp. Ablating H1 coordinates changes model outputs 5.6–26.5× more than variability-matched controls across 9 models (124M–13B), and a Hodge harmonic mass over multi-layer grids ranks 6 architectures by steering fragility (ρ=0.90, p=0.014) — though the steering advantage itself is confined to α ∈ [0.10, 0.15], and the fragility claim rests on only six data points.

## 核心贡献

- **用 sheaf 条件定义"跨上下文一致性"**：把释义对视为局部一致性见证，sheaf Laplacian L_F = (δ0)^T δ0 的核 H0 就是"换措辞不换内容"的子空间，谱补（"H1 方向"）是随措辞变化的成分——这是对现有工具（SAE、探针、CKA/SVCCA、LEACE）都不测试的"同一特征在等价语境下是否编码同一信息"这一空白的直接回应。
- **H0 内容子空间在留出检索上大幅超过 LEACE**：20 维 H0 在 8 个架构的 CounterFact 硬同关系检索上全部胜过全维 LEACE 保留子空间（平均 +17.1 pp），且限制映射换成随机或恒等映射仍保留大部分收益——说明内容存在于释义差分协方差的核中，收益来自"选核"而非映射本身。
- **H1 方向集中因果影响**：消融最随释义变化的坐标（H1 的坐标级代理）对模型输出的改变是逐坐标变异性匹配对照的 5.6–26.5 倍（9 个架构、124M–13B）；投影掉 sheaf H1 特征基使下一 token 分布的 KL 散度变化达随机子空间的 17.8–398 倍，并把事实回忆打崩到 0–5%。
- **用深度制造环、用谐波质量诊断 steering 脆弱性**：把各层激活堆成网格产生 b1 = N(L−1) 个代数 1-环，模型相关的 Hodge 谐波质量跨越近三个数量级（10⁻⁴ 到 0.08），其排序与六架构在 CounterFact 随机 steering 下的事实保留率排序一致（Spearman ρ=0.90，精确单侧置换 p=0.014）——一种只需 30 个 MRPC 对、一次前向的预筛诊断。
- **同一构造贯通三个用途并连接 SSL**：同一释义图同时给出内容子空间（检索）、影响方向（消融/steering）和脆弱性度量；tr(L_F) − β log det Cov 正则化可在七个架构上把编码器秩稳定在 57–60/64（朴素 MSE 塌缩到 14–42/64）。

## Key Contributions

- **Cross-context consistency as a sheaf condition.** Each paraphrase pair is a local witness that two activations should agree; the kernel H0 of the sheaf Laplacian L_F = (δ0)^T δ0 collects globally consistent (content) directions and its spectral complement ("H1 directions") collects phrasing-dependent variation — a property no existing tool (SAEs, probes, CKA/SVCCA, LEACE) directly tests.
- **Content subspace from the kernel, beating LEACE.** 20-D sheaf H0 beats LEACE's full-dimension preserved subspace on held-out hard same-relation CounterFact retrieval on all 8 architectures (mean +17.1 pp), and the restriction-map ablation (random-P 74.2% / identity-P 77.5% vs PCA-P 81.6%) shows the content lives in the kernel of the within-pair difference covariance whatever P is.
- **Joint paraphrase constraints improve H0.** Replacing pair-chains with a ring over all 5 paraphrases of each fact adds +3.2 to +9.5 pp (mean +6.5 pp) on all 8 architectures; an edge-count-matched acyclic-path control attributes the gain to the joint constraint, not the cycle.
- **Concentrated causal influence in the spectral complement.** Coordinate-wise H1 ablation changes outputs 5.6–26.5× more than variability-matched controls across 9 architectures from GPT-2 (124M) to Llama-2-13B; projecting out the sheaf H1 eigenbasis shifts next-token distributions 17.8–398× more than random subspaces and collapses fact recall to 0–5% while matched random ablation leaves recall at baseline.
- **Depth-induced cycles as a steering-fragility diagnostic.** Multi-layer grids carry b1 = N(L−1) cycles; the model-derived Hodge harmonic mass (fraction of a Procrustes-transported edge cochain's energy on the harmonic subspace) spans [10⁻⁴, 0.08] at L=2 and ranks six architectures by CounterFact fact preservation under random steering with Spearman ρ=0.90 (exact one-sided permutation p=0.014).

## Methodology

The method is entirely post-hoc: hidden states are read from a frozen model, and the decomposition is a spectral computation on a small k×k matrix (k=128) regardless of model width.

1. **Sheaf over the paraphrase graph.** Nodes carry mean-pooled residual-stream activations (layer ℓ ≈ 2L/3), edges connect MRPC paraphrase pairs, and a linear restriction map F_v⊴e = P^T projects each node's d-dimensional state into a shared k-dimensional edge stalk. The coboundary (δ0 h)_e = P^T(h_b − h_a) measures within-pair disagreement, and the empirical sheaf Laplacian is the k×k second-moment matrix L_F = (1/N) Σ Δi Δi^T with Δi the projected pair difference. H0 = span of the bottom m=20 eigenvectors (content), H1 directions = span of the top 20 (phrasing variation). Three choices of P (joint PCA, CCA, contrastive Fisher — the last built from the within-pair difference covariance C_W vs total C_T generalized eigenproblem) are the only knob.
2. **Proposition 3.1 (reduction).** On a matching graph with one shared P and identity transports, L_F = P^T C_W P — the construction provably reduces to projected within-pair covariance, which is why the restriction-map ablation matters: the result survives random/identity P, so the content is in the kernel of C_W, not in the learned map. Remark 3.3 is careful that "H1 directions" on acyclic matching graphs is a mnemonic for high disagreement, not an algebraic cohomology class.
3. **Depth-induced cycles (multi-layer grid).** Vertices are (sentence, layer) pairs; horizontal edges join paraphrases at a layer, vertical edges join adjacent layers via an orthogonal Procrustes transport R_{l→l+1}. The grid carries b1 = N(L−1) elementary squares, each asking whether the layer-to-layer map commutes with paraphrase equivalence. The model-derived edge cochain ω's Hodge harmonic mass ‖Π_ker L1 ω‖²/‖ω‖² (against a 0.25 random-cochain reference b1/|E|) is the fragility signal; because ω would be an exact coboundary under R = I, the fitted transport is what makes the mass model-dependent.
4. **Relation hypergraphs.** Rings over each fact's paraphrases plus relation-level rings (e.g., all country–capital facts) produce dim H1 = 66 from 300 activations — non-trivial topology from relation structure rather than depth.
5. **Steering.** Style direction Δ = mean-target − mean-source at layer ⌊5L/8⌋, either H0-removed (Δ − Π_H0 Δ) or projected onto top H1 dimensions, norm-matched to α = 0.3‖h̄‖; baselines include random, CAA (full style), PIXEL, mean-of-differences, variance top-k, Fisher LDA. Evaluation: style change (fraction of outputs differing) and fact preservation (fraction containing the target entity, greedy decoding, 50 tokens) on CounterFact (n=1000/model).
6. **SSL connection.** A sheaf-regularized objective tr(L_F) − β log det Cov(f) pairs the invariance term (within-pair MSE) with a log-det spread term, preventing dimensional collapse in BYOL/SimSiam-style training.

Statistics: bootstrap 95% CIs (1000 resamples), McNemar's exact test with Benjamini–Hochberg correction for paired steering comparisons, Cohen's d effect sizes, exact one-sided permutation tests for rank/holonomy claims.

## Results & Highlights

- **Held-out CounterFact fact retrieval (Table 2):** 20-D sheaf H0 beats LEACE's preserved subspace at full hidden dimension on all 8 architectures with a mean gap of +17.1 pp, ranging from +1.5 pp (OLMo-2-7B: 82.8% vs 81.3%) to +42.2 pp (GPT-2: 85.3% vs 43.1%); against the dimension-matched LEACE-20D baseline the mean gap is +48.0 pp (27–60 pp).
- **Joint paraphrase constraint (Table 10):** ringing all paraphrases of a fact (b1 = N) beats pair-only H0 by +6.5 pp mean (+3.2 to +9.5 pp) on all 8 architectures; the edge-count-matched acyclic path control recovers +7.7 pp of that and the ring adds only a non-significant +0.5 pp — the joint constraint, not the cycle, drives the gain.
- **Coordinate ablation influence (Table 3):** ablating the most paraphrase-variable (H1-proxy) coordinates changes model outputs 5.6× (Llama-3-8B) to 26.5× (Llama-2-13B) more than ablating control coordinates of matched per-coordinate variability across all 9 architectures, with every bootstrap 95% CI excluding 1 (e.g., Mistral-7B: 19.71×, CI [19.1, 20.3], Cohen's d = 18.45).
- **Eigenbasis ablation (Appendix B.6/B.7):** projecting out the sheaf H1 eigenbasis shifts next-token distributions 17.8–398× more than equal-dimension random subspaces (median over layers, 9 models) and collapses CounterFact fact recall to 0–5%, while matched random ablation leaves recall at baseline on the 7B models — evidence against the dormant-pathway reading of the subspace-patching illusion.
- **Harmonic mass vs steering fragility (Table 1):** at L=2 the harmonic mass spans 10⁻⁴ (Mistral-7B, 39.4% fact preservation under random steering) to 0.079 (Llama-2-7B, 4.2% preservation), and ranks all six architectures consistently with fragility: Spearman ρ = 0.90, exact one-sided permutation p = 0.014; the mass needs only 30 MRPC pairs and one forward pass per sentence.
- **Steering on CounterFact at α=0.3 (Table 4):** on fragile Llama-2-7B, contrastive sheaf steering preserves 31.0% of facts vs 4.2% for random steering (McNemar p < 10⁻⁷⁰), beating PIXEL (21.1%) and CAA (13.7%); on robust Mistral-7B the CCA sheaf and random tie (41.6% vs 39.4%, p = 0.19), and sheaf directions only reliably beat random at α ∈ [0.10, 0.15] (e.g., H0-removed 43% vs random 41% on Mistral-7B at α = 0.10).
- **Retrieval baseline suite (Appendix B.5, Table 12):** sheaf H0 (mean 81.6% across 8 architectures) beats every label-free alternative (CCA 54.4%, PLS 57.0%, PCA-20 30.4%, W-PCA 39.8%) by +9 to +36 pp, and lands within ~2 pp on average of the fact-label-requiring ceiling (LDA 83.7%, NCA 83.3%), winning outright on Phi-2, Gemma-2-9B, and Llama-3-8B.
- **SSL collapse prevention (Appendix B.14):** the sheaf regularizer holds encoder rank at 57–60 of 64 across 7 architectures where naive MSE training collapses to 14–42 of 64.

## Authors & Credibility

Two authors — Bryce Grant (NSF Graduate Research Fellow, Grant No. 2437833) and Peng Wang — both at Case Western Reserve University; the paper extends a non-archival ICLR 2026 workshop version, with the multi-layer grids, relation hypergraphs, harmonic-mass fragility diagnostic, held-out LEACE protocol, restriction-map ablation, PIXEL comparison, and SSL connection all new to this version. It is a NeurIPS 2026 main-track paper with a fully completed checklist. Credibility is high: all datasets are public (MRPC, PAWS, CounterFact), code and a claim-by-claim reproduction guide are released (github.com/CWRU-AISM/gluing-lc, project page cwru-aism.github.io/gluing-lc-page), every headline number carries bootstrap CIs or exact paired tests, negative results (null holonomy, failed node-space H1, neutral MLST training signal) are reported rather than hidden, and total compute is a modest ~200 GPU-hours on a single A100.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty — real framing, partly familiar core.** The sheaf-theoretic packaging is genuinely new for LM interpretability, but the authors' own Proposition 3.1 concedes that on single-layer matching graphs the operator reduces to P^T C_W P — the eigenspectrum of within-pair difference covariance, a close relative of Fisher/LDA-style contrastive subspace analysis. The genuinely novel machinery is the multi-layer grid with Procrustes transports and its harmonic mass; the headline H0 result mostly demonstrates that the *kernel* of the difference covariance is a good content subspace.
- **The LEACE comparison is against a tool outside its design regime.** LEACE erases a *binary* paraphrase-vs-non-paraphrase concept with a rank-1 null space; using its preserved subspace as a content-preserving retrieval basis is an awkward fit, and the +17.1 pp headline partially reflects that mismatch. The authors mitigate this honestly — the dimension-controlled LEACE-20D and PCA-20 baselines (H0 still +27 to +60 pp ahead) and the label-free baseline suite show the win is not only an artifact — but the abstract leads with the least favorable-to-LEACE number.
- **Rigor — above average, with candid caveats.** Variability-matched and edge-count-matched controls, McNemar + BH correction, permutation tests, and an explicit disclosure that the H1 control set carries only 0.13–0.53 of the H1 set's total variability (so part of the 5.6–26.5× ratio reflects removed energy, not selectivity) are all present. The claimed GPT-2/Mistral "near-identical ratios despite 56× size gap" is intriguing but unexplained.
- **Fragility diagnostic is n=6.** Spearman ρ=0.90 with p=0.014 over six architectures is suggestive, not confirmatory; the authors say so themselves ("more architectures are needed before it serves as a predictor"), and the one rank inversion (Qwen-2.5-7B vs Llama-3-8B, 34.3% vs 36.0%) is within sampling error.
- **Practical steering gain is modest and regime-limited.** The dramatic 31.0%-vs-4.2% contrast exists only on the most fragile model at the stress magnitude α=0.3; on robust architectures at the same α, random steering statistically ties every directed method, and the sheaf advantage at α ∈ [0.10, 0.15] is +1–2 pp. The PIXEL comparison also uses the authors' simplified port (single global direction), not the original per-position calibration.
- **Reproducibility — strong.** Public data, released code, all hyperparameters (k=128, m=20, layer rules, α) documented; 4-bit quantization of 7B+ models is disclosed but is itself a mild quirk — activations from quantized models may differ from full precision, and no check of the decomposition's sensitivity to quantization is reported.
- **Data quirks.** The sheaf is fit on 200 MRPC pairs and evaluated heavily on CounterFact (n=500 retrieval facts, 1000 steering prompts); MRPC's news-domain paraphrases are a narrow notion of "same meaning," and the paper's own Appendix B.1 shows cross-corpus P transfer costs ~25 pp — a real domain-dependence concern.

## Strengths

- A single, elegant construction (one paraphrase graph, one spectral decomposition) that simultaneously yields a content subspace, an influence subspace, and a fragility diagnostic — with all three empirically validated.
- Training-free and cheap: the eigenproblem is 128×128 regardless of model width, the fragility screen needs 30 paraphrase pairs and one forward pass per sentence, and it works identically from GPT-2 (124M) to Llama-2-13B.
- Exceptional experimental hygiene: held-out fact splits, edge-count-matched and variability-matched controls, dimension-matched baselines, a full classical-baseline suite (LDA/NCA/CCA/PLS/PCA/W-PCA), bootstrap CIs, McNemar with BH correction, and effect sizes everywhere.
- Honest reporting of negative results (holonomy null with p=0.10–0.33; node-space H1 not transferring; MLST training terms neutral-to-slightly-negative) and a limitations paragraph that matches what the appendices actually show.
- Clear theoretical self-awareness: Proposition 3.1 and Remark 3.2/3.3 delimit exactly where the sheaf machinery adds structure beyond covariance analysis and where the "H1" label is a mnemonic.

## Limitations

- On acyclic (single-layer, pair-only) graphs the construction provably reduces to projected within-pair covariance; the sheaf's distinctive power lives only in the cycle-bearing grids/hypergraphs, where the practical payoff (holonomy, node-space H1) was partly null.
- The steering benefit is architecture- and magnitude-dependent: reliable only at α ∈ [0.10, 0.15] on robust models with +1–2 pp margins; at α=0.3, random perturbation matches directed methods on robust architectures.
- The fragility–harmonic-mass correlation rests on six architectures; it cannot yet serve as a general predictor, and the harmonic mass requires choosing k and the grid depth L, with stability verified only over k ∈ {8, 64, 128}.
- The restriction map P is architecture-dependent (contrastive Fisher wins on Llama-2-7B, CCA on Mistral-7B) and partially domain-dependent (cross-corpus transfer costs ~25 pp); "same meaning" is operationalized narrowly as MRPC-style paraphrase, with only a small cross-lingual probe for translation pairs.
- Fact evaluation is confined to ROME CounterFact-style entity recall with greedy 50-token decoding; logical equivalence, dialogue continuation, and open-ended generation remain untested, and 7B+ activations were extracted under 4-bit quantization.

## Relevance

直接命中"LLM Hidden States & Interpretability"方向：这是对表示几何（representation geometry）与机理可解释性的工作——在冻结模型上以谱方法分解隐藏状态为内容子空间与措辞变化子空间，并给出探针之外的因果验证（消融）与跨架构诊断（steering 脆弱性）。对关注模型内部表征可靠性的人尤其有价值：谐波质量提供了一个廉价、无需训练的预筛信号，可在 steering/编辑部署前判断一个模型的表征路径是否自洽。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the harmonic-mass/fragility correlation hold beyond six architectures — e.g., on MoE models, chat-tuned vs base checkpoints of the same model, or models sharing a family but differing in scale (Llama-2-7B vs 13B already diverge)?
- Can the H0 content subspace replace full hidden states in downstream tasks like fact-editing (ROME/MEMIT) or retrieval-augmented verification, where separating content from phrasing should matter most?
- Why do GPT-2 (124M) and Mistral-7B produce near-identical ablation ratios (18.2× vs 19.7×) despite a 56× size gap — is the ratio controlled by residual-stream width, depth, or training data composition?
- How does the decomposition behave under quantization, pruning, or fine-tuning — could drift of the H0/H1 split serve as an early signal of representation damage in deployed models?
- The paper's own negative results (holonomy null, node-space H1 not transferring) suggest the algebraic cycles carry less practical signal than the spectral split; what richer topologies (attention-head-level graphs, token-position grids) would make genuine cohomological structure functionally consequential?
- Can the paraphrase-graph construction extend to multimodal or cross-lingual "equivalence classes" (the authors' Appendix B.8 translation probe is a start) with per-node maps of different widths, which they leave to future work?
