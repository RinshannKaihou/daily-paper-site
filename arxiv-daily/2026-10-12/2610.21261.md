> **一句话总结：** 本文提出 SAE 可加性残差（SAR）来度量"SAE 特征的下游行为效应能否被一阶求和近似"，证明在独立子集采样下 SAR 恰等于高阶交互能量并界定联盟排序误差；在 18 个模型×行为设置中，学到的 SAE 坐标的一阶残差全部低于同子空间的保和随机旋转（如 Mistral-7B 主谓一致 0.0218 vs 0.1196），但特征效应并非完全可加——成对交互项在全部 18 个设置上提升留出联盟排序（平均秩保真 0.9782→0.9959），在 MMLU-Pro 联合 steering 实验中更把秩保真从 0.5874 提升到 0.7790、排序误差从 0.1444 降到 0.0606。

> **TL;DR:** Sparse autoencoder (SAE) features are linearly decoded, but their downstream behavioral effects need not add up. The authors define SAE Additivity Residuals (SAR): the fraction of behavioral variance (e.g., a target logit difference) over feature-coalition interventions that the best order-r additive approximation leaves unexplained, and prove that under independent Bernoulli subset sampling SAR is exactly the normalized energy of higher-order interactions and bounds coalition-ordering error. Across 18 model-behavior settings (Gemma-3 4B, Mistral-7B, Llama-3.1 8B; six behaviors from simple copying to IOI), learned SAE coordinates show lower first-order SAR than sum-preserving random rotations of the same selected subspace in every setting (e.g., 0.0218 vs 0.1196 median on Mistral-7B subject-verb agreement), yet features are not fully additive: adding pairwise terms improves held-out coalition rank fidelity in all 18 settings (average 0.9782 to 0.9959; selection regret 0.0272 to 0.0051) and beats parameter-matched random third-order models. In a combined steering experiment on Llama-3.1 8B with 16 published CorrSteer directions evaluated on MMLU-Pro (1,400 questions), pairwise modeling raises rank fidelity from 0.5874 to 0.7790 (+0.192) and cuts ordering error from 0.1444 to 0.0606 — though with strong domain heterogeneity (Chemistry +0.396, Law -0.091). Bottom line: SAE bases support comparatively low-order behavioral descriptions, but faithful joint explanations and multi-feature steering still need interactions beyond individual feature effects.

## 核心贡献

- **提出行为组合性（behavioral compositionality）的形式化框架与 SAR 度量。** 对每个 prompt 选定 top-K 个活跃 SAE 特征的解码贡献，保留/移除不同特征子集（联盟）并将修补后的激活通过冻结的上层网络前传，得到标量行为响应 g_x(S)；SAR_r 定义为最优 r 阶（一阶=单特征、二阶=特征对）近似未能解释的行为方差占比。
- **给出精确理论刻画。** 在独立 Bernoulli(p) 子集采样下（Walsh 正交积基），定理 1 证明 SAR_r 恰等于 r 阶以上交互项的归一化能量；定理 2 证明 SAR_r 上界最优 r 阶近似的"行为加权联盟排序误差"；推论 1 给出仿射延续（affine continuation）下 SAR_1=0 的保证。
- **区分"表征的组合性"与"下游计算诱导的组合性"。** 用保持总和的随机旋转（Q1=1 的正交变换）在同一选定子空间内构造对照坐标系：18 个模型×行为设置中学到的 SAE 坐标的一阶 SAR 中位数全部更低，说明组合性依赖于坐标系选择本身，而非仅由子空间和下游延续决定。
- **证明成对交互捕捉真实结构。** 成对模型在全部 18 个设置上提升留出联盟排序（平均秩保真 0.9782→0.9959、选择后悔 0.0272→0.0051），并在参数匹配对照（同样本量的随机三阶 Walsh 项）中全部胜出。
- **扩展到联合 steering 与重建质量的对照。** 在 MMLU-Pro 联合 steering 中成对建模显著优于一阶（秩保真 0.5874→0.7790）；同一位置的 6 个 SAE 匹配比较中，SAR 与符号误差/选择后悔正相关而重建质量 FVUx 几乎不相关——重建质量与行为组合性是不同的表征属性。

## Key Contributions

- **Formalizes behavioral compositionality of SAE representations.** For each prompt, the top-K (K=16) active SAE decoded contributions δ_i = z_i d_i are selectively retained/removed while the reconstruction residual and all other activation content stay fixed; the patched activation is propagated through the frozen upper layers, yielding a controlled set function g_x(S) over feature coalitions for a scalar behavioral readout ψ (logit differences).
- **Introduces SAE Additivity Residuals (SAR) with exact theory.** SAR_r is the fraction of behavioral variance unexplained by the best order-r approximation (an L2(μ) projection onto functions of ≤r features). Theorem 1: under independent Bernoulli subset sampling, SAR_r is exactly the normalized energy of interaction terms above order r in an orthogonal product (Walsh) basis. Theorem 2: SAR_r upper-bounds the behavior-weighted coalition-ordering error of the optimal order-r approximation. Corollary 1: affine downstream continuation forces SAR_1 = 0, so nonzero SAR_1 certifies departure from affinity on the tested states.
- **Separates representation-induced from computation-induced compositionality via matched rotations.** Comparing learned SAE coordinates against sum-preserving random rotations (Q orthogonal, Q1=1, QR-orthogonalized on the all-ones complement) of the *same* selected subspace with the *same* downstream model isolates the role of coordinate choice: learned coordinates achieve lower median first-order SAR in all 18 model-behavior settings.
- **Shows pairwise interactions recover real missing structure.** Pairwise models improve held-out coalition rank fidelity in all 18 settings (average 0.9782 to 0.9959), reduce selection regret (0.0272 to 0.0051), reduce sign error everywhere, remain stable across all 20 refit splits, and beat parameter-matched controls that swap the 120 pairwise terms for 120 randomly chosen third-order Walsh terms.
- **Extends the analysis to combined steering and to SAE comparison.** Using 16 published CorrSteer steering directions on Llama-3.1 8B, pairwise modeling of the aggregate MMLU-Pro response raises rank fidelity from 0.5874 to 0.7790 and cuts ordering error from 0.1444 to 0.0606; across six matched SAEs at a fixed Gemma-3 1B site, SAR correlates with sign error / selection regret (Spearman 0.199-0.430) while reconstruction FVUx does not (-0.114 to 0.028).

## Methodology

The paper builds a "feature-coalition intervention game" and studies its low-order structure.

1. **Controlled subset response.** Let h_x be the activation at a chosen layer/token of model F, and let N_x be the top-K (K=16) active SAE features with decoded contributions δ_i(x) = z_i(x)d_i. For a coalition S ⊆ N_x, the patched activation is h̃_x(S) = h_x - Σ_{i∈N_x\S} δ_i(x): the removed selected contributions are ablated while the reconstruction residual and unselected content stay fixed, so the full coalition S = N_x recovers the original activation exactly. The response g_x(S) = ψ(F_{ℓ←h̃(S)}(x)) is a scalar behavior readout (e.g., logit difference for a target token) after propagating through frozen upper layers.
2. **SAR definition.** With A_r the class of set functions depending on at most r features and Π_r g_x the L2(μ) projection, SAR_r(x;ψ) = ||g_x - Π_r g_x||²_μ / V(x;ψ) — the unexplained fraction of behavioral variance. Under independent Bernoulli(p) sampling, an orthogonal basis u_B(S) = Π_{i∈B}(1{i∈S}-p)/√(p(1-p)) makes the residual exactly the normalized energy of interactions above order r (Theorem 1); at p=1/2 the pairwise share ρ_2 = Δ_2/SAR_1 = Σ_{i<j}(E[Δ_ij g_x])² / (16 V) has a clean finite-difference form. Theorem 2 bounds the probability that a low-order approximation reverses the ordering of two coalitions, weighted by behavioral difference: E(Π_r g_x) ≤ SAR_r.
3. **Estimation.** Exhaustive evaluation is 2^K, so low-order models (17 coefficients at order 1, 137 at order 2) are fit by unregularized least squares on 256 sampled coalitions and evaluated on a held-out 128; reported metrics include held-out residual estimate, Spearman rank fidelity, behavior-weighted ordering error, sign error, and selection regret. Every sampled design was full rank (median order-2 Gram condition number 41.02).
4. **Matched rotation control.** Multiplying the selected contributions by an orthogonal Q with Q1=1 preserves the subspace and its total contribution while randomizing coordinates, giving a coordinate-choice baseline under identical downstream computation. Rotations are drawn by fixing the normalized all-ones direction and QR-orthogonalizing a Gaussian matrix on its (K-1)-dimensional complement (8 per prompt).
5. **Experimental grid.** Main pipelines: Gemma-3 4B (layer 22, Gemma Scope 2 SAEs), Mistral-7B (layer 24, Engels residual SAEs), Llama-3.1 8B (layer 21, Llama Scope); behaviors: simple copying, subject-verb agreement, gendered pronoun preference, CounterFact, IOI, greater-than. Depth analysis spans seven pipelines from GPT-2 Small/Pythia-70M up to Llama-3.1 8B. The steering study fixes 16 published CorrSteer features (with their published coefficients across 12 layers) and samples 1,024 of the 2^16 steering combinations, evaluated on 100 questions from each of the 14 MMLU-Pro domains (1,400 total).
6. **Calibration.** Affine controls (linear readout of the intervened activation) sit at numerical zero (largest prompt-median SAR_1 = 1.55e-13); synthetic games with planted pairwise structure recover target residuals exactly (0.1/0.3/0.5).

## Results & Highlights

- **Learned SAE coordinates beat matched rotations in all 18 settings (first-order SAR, prompt medians, 100 prompts x 8 rotations).** Example cells from Table 10: Mistral-7B subject-verb agreement 0.0218 (SAE) vs 0.1196 (rotation), a ~5.5x reduction; Llama-3.1 8B simple copying 0.0593 vs 0.1661 (~2.8x); Mistral-7B gendered pronoun preference 0.0243 vs 0.1411. The SAE estimate falls below the rotation median for 0.92-1.00 of prompts in 16 of 18 cells (weakest: Gemma-3 4B IOI at 0.66 and Mistral-7B greater-than at 0.54).
- **Pairwise terms improve held-out coalition ordering in every one of the 18 model-behavior combinations.** Mean rank fidelity rises from 0.9782 (first order) to 0.9959 (pairwise); average selection regret falls from 0.0272 to 0.0051 (~5.3x lower); sign error decreases in every combination (e.g., Mistral-7B subject-verb agreement 0.1199 to 0.0482); behavior-weighted ordering error satisfies Ê_2 < Ê_1 in all 18 (e.g., Llama-3.1 8B gendered pronoun preference 0.003579 to 0.0003866, ~9x lower). Improvements persist across all 20 repeated fitting/evaluation splits.
- **Pairwise gains are not just extra capacity:** replacing the 120 pairwise Walsh terms with 120 randomly selected third-order terms (parameter-matched controls, 50 random models per prompt) yields lower rank fidelity and higher ordering error than the pairwise model in all 18 combinations (e.g., Gemma-3 4B IOI rank fidelity 0.9925 vs 0.9690 matched; Mistral-7B gendered pronoun Ê 0.0001367 vs 0.002338 matched).
- **Combined steering experiment (Llama-3.1 8B, 16 CorrSteer features, MMLU-Pro, 1,400 questions, 1,024 coalitions):** pairwise modeling raises rank fidelity from 0.5874 to 0.7790 (+0.1916), reduces behavior-weighted ordering error from 0.1444 to 0.0606 (~2.4x lower), and cuts held-out residual from 0.6424 to 0.3891. Domain-level effects are strongly heterogeneous: rank fidelity change is +0.3960 on Chemistry but -0.0907 on Law, -0.0256 on Math, and -0.0093 on Economics — first-order-only steering compositionality fails hardest where SAR_1 is largest (Physics 0.9071, Chemistry 0.9179).
- **SAR tracks joint fidelity where reconstruction quality does not.** Pooling six matched SAEs at Gemma-3 1B layer 17 (n = 1,800 prompts per behavior), Spearman correlation with sign error is +0.269 / +0.430 / +0.396 (SAR_1) vs -0.080 / -0.114 / -0.013 (FVUx) for simple copying / subject-verb agreement / IOI; for selection regret SAR gives +0.220/+0.235/+0.199 vs FVUx's ~0 (0.011/0.005/0.028).
- **Depth trends are partly computational, partly representational.** Across 9 early-to-late layer comparisons, SAR_1 decreases in 8 and pairwise share ρ_2 increases in all 9 (e.g., Gemma-3 4B IOI 0.2255 to 0.0732, a 3.1x reduction); random rotations show the same first-order decrease in 8 of 9, so part of the trend comes from shorter remaining downstream computation, yet SAE coordinates stay below rotations in all 33 model-behavior-layer comparisons. Llama-3.1 8B simple copying is the exception, increasing 0.2001 to 0.2562 (nonmonotonic near the last sampled layer).

## Authors & Credibility

The paper is by Haotian Ma, Ziye Mei, and Frederic Sala of the Department of Computer Sciences at UW-Madison (Sala leads the MADAI group, known for rigorous work on data pruning/curvature and, with Ma, on scaling-law/data-related theory — this paper continues their style of clean theory plus careful empirics). The experimental design is unusually well-controlled for the SAE-interpretability literature: affine controls that recover numerical zero (≤1.55e-13), synthetic games with planted residuals recovered exactly, parameter-matched third-order capacity controls, 20 repeated-split stability checks, explicit finite-sample diagnostics (full-rank designs, condition numbers, split variation), fresh-coalition redraws (5 sets x 50 prompts), sensitivity to inclusion probability p (0.5 → 0.75), perturbation magnitude (halved removals), prompt count, and coalition budget. Evaluation spans seven published SAE pipelines (Bloom GPT-2, Marks Pythia, Gemma Scope 2, Engels Mistral residual SAEs, Llama Scope) and standard interpretability behaviors (CounterFact, IOI, subject-verb agreement), plus published steering vectors (CorrSteer) on MMLU-Pro. Code is released at github.com/OpenSAE/SAE-Behavioral-Compositionality. The math (Walsh/orthogonal interaction decomposition, coalition-ordering bound) is standard cooperative-game machinery applied in a new, well-motivated setting, with proofs in the appendix.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty: solid but incremental in machinery.** The core instruments — set-function low-order approximation, Walsh bases, finite differences — are classical (Shapley/Faith-Shap territory, which the authors honestly cite in Appendix C). The genuinely new contributions are (a) the intervention design (freezing the reconstruction residual so the full coalition anchors at the true activation) and (b) the sum-preserving-rotation control. The question "do SAE feature effects compose additively downstream?" is timely and this is a credible first systematic answer.
- **Rigor: high, with transparent noise levels.** The authors themselves report that absolute residual magnitudes are noisy (median within-prompt coefficient of variation 13.7% for SAR_1 and 18.2% for SAR_2 across fresh coalition draws, versus 0.37%/0.16% for rank fidelity) and decline to interpret the second-order residual as a high-precision population estimate. The pairwise-improvement conclusion is budget-sensitive: at 160 fitting coalitions it holds in only 9/18 cells, reaching 18/18 only at ≥224 — the main protocol (256) clears this, but the margin is thinner than the headline suggests.
- **The rotation baseline is a weak comparator.** Random rotations establish that *coordinate choice matters*, but they are not an interpretability-relevant alternative basis; comparisons against structured competitors (PCA of the subspace, dense dictionary atoms, or other SAE training objectives at matched sparsity) are absent. The "SAE coordinates are comparatively compositional" claim is only relative to this strawman-ish control.
- **Effect sizes at main layers are small.** Median SAR_1 at the three focal layers ranges 0.015-0.082, i.e., first-order additivity already explains 92-98.5% of behavioral variance, and first-order rank fidelity is already 0.96-0.99. The practical cost of ignoring interactions in coalition games is modest; the steering experiment (SAR_1 = 0.64) is where interactions really bite, but that is one model, one published feature set, and 1,024 of 65,536 coalitions with strong domain heterogeneity (pairwise helps Chemistry +0.396 but hurts Law -0.091).
- **Behavioral readouts are narrow.** Six of seven behaviors are single-token logit differences on template/curated prompts; only MMLU-Pro steering touches aggregate task behavior. Logit-difference readouts sit close to the output layer of linear probes, which may understate interaction for generation-length or multi-step behaviors.
- **Scope conditionality is honestly stated but limiting.** SAR depends on K (steering SAR_1 wobbles non-monotonically from 0.2577 at K=6 to 0.8096 at K=18), on the readout ψ, on the intervention distribution, and on layer — so SAR is a property of an intervention game, not an SAE dictionary. Practitioners wanting "which SAE should I train" guidance get only a six-SAE, one-site, three-behavior comparison, without varying SAE objective or training recipe.
- **Red flags: none serious.** No missing-baseline scandal or overclaiming beyond mildly favorable framing; the abstract's numbers match the tables, the theory matches the estimators, and the released code plus fixed splits/seeds aid reproduction.

## Strengths

- Clean, exact theory: SAR is not a heuristic — under product sampling it is provably the normalized energy of higher-order interactions and provably bounds coalition-ordering error, so the metric means what it claims.
- The sum-preserving rotation control is an elegant causal disentanglement of coordinate choice from subspace and downstream computation.
- Exceptional experimental hygiene: affine and synthetic calibration at numerical zero, parameter-matched capacity controls, repeated splits, budget/sensitivity analyses, and fresh-coalition stability — a model for intervention-based interpretability evaluations.
- Practically actionable finding: individual-feature steering extrapolation is unreliable when composing directions (MMLU-Pro rank fidelity only 0.5874 at first order), and pairwise models partially repair it (0.7790).
- Demonstrates that reconstruction quality (FVUx) does not proxy for joint behavioral fidelity — an important negative result for SAE benchmarking (SAEBench-style metrics miss this axis).

## Limitations

- Absolute SAR values are estimator-noisy (CV 13.7-18.2% across coalition redraws); the paper itself warns against reading second-order residuals as precise population values.
- Only top-K=16 active features per prompt are considered, with default sensitivity up to K=20; interactions involving the long tail of weak features, and feature-splitting/absorption effects across the dictionary, are out of scope.
- Models capped at 8B parameters; behaviors are mostly narrow logit-difference readouts rather than open-ended generation, so extrapolation to frontier-scale steering is untested.
- The steering study is a single (model, feature-set) pair with fixed published coefficients; the 14-domain heterogeneity (pairwise helps 10, hurts 4) suggests conclusions there are fragile.
- No comparison against structured alternative bases (PCA, dense autoencoders, different SAE objectives) — only random rotations — and no link from detected pairwise interactions back to interpretable feature-pair semantics (the examples in Fig. 9 stay descriptive).

## Relevance

对于关注机制可解释性与 SAE 的工作而言，这篇论文直接回答了一个核心实践问题：SAE 特征能否按"单特征效应求和"组合成联合解释或干预。它提供的 SAR 度量、旋转对照协议和"重建质量≠行为组合性"的结论，对设计 SAE 评测基准、多特征 steering 与 feature-circuit 分析都具有直接的方法论价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would structured bases (PCA of the selected subspace, dense dictionary atoms, or SAEs trained with different objectives/sparsity) close part of the gap between random rotations and learned SAE coordinates on SAR_1?
- Can the detected pairwise interaction terms (Δ_ij g) be mapped back to interpretable semantics — e.g., do high-interaction feature pairs correspond to known absorbing/splitting phenomena or to specific circuit motifs?
- How does SAR scale with model size, instruction tuning, and longer-horizon behaviors (multi-token generation, agentic tasks) where the downstream continuation is much deeper than in these logit-difference readouts?
- Is the early-layer-to-late-layer SAR decrease a universal "computational composition" trend, and does the Llama-3.1 8B nonmonotonicity (simple copying 0.2001 to 0.2562) reflect a late-layer re-decomposition of features?
- For steering practice: can pairwise interaction coefficients be estimated cheaply enough (they cost 120 extra terms) to improve combined-vector steering pipelines such as CorrSteer, and does the Law/Math domain failure mode indicate interaction with reasoning rather than knowledge retrieval?
- Does the SAR-vs-FVUx dissociation persist across SAE widths and training data scales, or can reconstruction objectives be regularized toward lower behavioral interaction energy?
