> **一句话总结：** 这篇机制可解释性工作证明 Mamba 的检索失败不能只归咎于固定状态容量：在"查询在前、上下文在后"的 phonebook 检索任务上（此时容量本不应是瓶颈），8B Nvidia-Mamba 仍表现很差，但仅在目标条目前加一个词（0-2 个 token）就能让长列表准确率翻倍以上；通过门控双向 patching 与 oracle 缩放实验，作者证明选择门 Δt 对仅 10 个目标数字 token 的干预就能在近似完美与接近零准确率之间来回切换（3× 缩放→近乎满分，0.8×→近乎零分），而根因是相关性信号要到约 10 层之后才在线性表征中可解码、门又再滞后数层才响应它——即"门有能力但信息到得太晚"，检索关键层（网络早中部）的门尚未与相关性对齐。

> **TL;DR:** Why does Mamba fail at retrieval even when its fixed state capacity cannot be the bottleneck? The authors study query-first key-value retrieval (query precedes the context) on a synthetic phonebook task with two Mamba-2 models (Nvidia-Mamba 8B base, Codestral-Mamba 7.3B). Prepending a single local relevance flag word (0-2 tokens) to the target entry more than doubles Nvidia-Mamba's accuracy on long phonebooks (150-200 entries). Causal interventions show the selection gates Δt fully govern the outcome: patching the gates of just the 10 target digit tokens bidirectionally swaps flagged/unflagged performance, and oracle-scaling those gates to 3× yields near-perfect accuracy while 0.8× dampening collapses it to near zero. The root cause is a depth-timing mismatch: without the flag, relevance only becomes linearly decodable from token representations after ~10 layers (vs 1-2 residual-stream updates with the flag), and the gates lag several layers further behind — so retrieval-critical early/middle layers act on a relevance signal that has not yet arrived. No Transformer baseline is re-run here (that gap is imported from Waleffe et al. 2024), and all evidence is on synthetic retrieval, but the probing-plus-patching methodology is unusually rigorous and the diagnosis (gate miscalibration from late-arriving relevance, not just state saturation) reframes how to fix SSM retrieval.

## 核心贡献

- **推翻"容量是唯一瓶颈"的默认解释**：在查询先于上下文（query-first）的检索设定下，模型理论上只需保留紧凑的目标信息，状态容量不应受限，但 Mamba 仍显著落后于 Transformer——说明存在容量之外的失效机制。
- **构造"局部相关性旗标"对照实验**：仅在目标条目前加一个词（如 `target John Doe: ...`，编码后仅 0-2 个 token），就让 Nvidia-Mamba 在长 phonebook（150/200 条目）上的准确率翻倍以上；两变体只差一个词，从而把瓶颈精确定位到"相关性信息的可获性"。
- **建立门控的因果充分性与必要性**：双向激活 patching 显示，把标记版运行中仅 10 个目标数字 token 的 Δt 门值移植到未标记 prompt，可把准确率恢复到接近标记版基线，反向移植则把标记版拉低到未标记水平；oracle 缩放（3×）达到近乎满分、0.8× 压制到近乎零分——证明选择机制本身足够强大，问题在于校准。
- **刻画"相关性信号沿深度延迟到达"的动力学**：逐层线性探针显示，带旗标时相关性在 1-2 次残差流更新内即可线性解码，无旗标时需近 10 层才平台化；门级可解码性又比表征再滞后数层（约需 10 层渐进上升）。累积式逐层 patching 进一步表明准确率增益集中在网络早中部——恰是"表征已可解码、门尚未对齐"的层段。
- **重新定位失效根源为"时机"而非"组件/容量"**：与既有工作（DeciMamba 的感受野、Gather-Agagate 头、全局通道衰减）不同，本文问的是"负责的组件在它行动的时刻是否拿到了所需信息"，把失败定位于网络内部信息的时序错位。

## Key Contributions

- **Challenges the state-capacity-only narrative for Mamba's retrieval failures.** In query-first retrieval (query before context), the recurrent state need only hold the compact target, so fixed capacity should not bind — yet Mamba still lags Transformers substantially, proving an additional failure mode exists.
- **A minimal, tightly controlled experimental lever: the local relevance flag.** Prepending a single word (0-2 tokens after tokenization) to the target phonebook entry — changing nothing else — more than doubles Nvidia-Mamba's accuracy on long phonebooks, isolating "local availability of relevance information" as the decisive variable.
- **Causal sufficiency and necessity of the selection gates.** Bidirectional activation patching of the Δt gates on only the 10 target-digit tokens transfers the flagged/unflagged outcomes in both directions; oracle-scaling those gates up (e.g., 3×) reaches near-perfect accuracy while dampening (0.8×) collapses it toward zero — the selection mechanism is expressive enough to fully govern retrieval by itself.
- **Quantifies the depth dynamics of the relevance signal.** Layer-wise linear probes show relevance becomes decodable from token representations within 1-2 residual-stream updates with the flag, but only plateaus after ~10 layers without it; gate-level decodability lags the representations by several further layers, and cumulative layer-prefix patching localizes the causal gains to early-to-middle layers where representations already encode relevance but gates do not yet reflect it.
- **Reframes the failure as a timing problem.** Rather than asking *which* component fails (heads, channels, capacity) as prior mechanistic work does, the paper asks whether the responsible component has its required input *when it acts* — locating the bottleneck in the late emergence of relevance across depth.

## Methodology

The authors work backward from the output, tracing Mamba-2's information flow on a synthetic query-first phonebook task (reversed setup of Waleffe et al. 2024, from Jelassi et al. 2024): each prompt gives a task description with two in-context examples, states the target name twice up front, then presents a phonebook (`Name: 10-digit number`) of 20-500 entries and asks the model to complete the target's number. Two variants differ only by whether the target entry carries a `target` prefix (flagged vs unflagged). Models: Nvidia-Mamba (nvidia/mamba2-8b-3t-4k; 8B base, 56 layers, 3T training tokens) and Codestral-Mamba (7.3B instruction-tuned, 64 layers); default evaluation lengths are 150 entries (Nvidia-Mamba) and 400 entries (Codestral-Mamba), 500 samples per setting, greedy decoding, correctness by regex-extracted 10-digit match.

The diagnostic pipeline has four stages: (1) **behavior** — accuracy vs phonebook length for both variants; (2) **state probing** — layer-wise linear probes (1M-dim flattened states, 15k samples, online interleaved training with class-balanced replay) trained to decode the target's identity (profession variant, 20 closed classes) from recurrent states captured after the target and subsequent entries; (3) **gate analysis** — mean difference between pre-activation gate values d_t on target vs distractor digit tokens per layer (pre-activation to avoid Softplus averaging artifacts); (4) **causal interventions** — bidirectional patching of Δt gates (and separately of layer-input representations x_t) restricted to the 10 target digit tokens, plus oracle scaling of those gates by fixed factors (0.8×-3×), and cumulative layer-prefix patching (patch layers {1}, {1,2}, ..., {1,N}) to localize where in depth the causal effect lives. Finally, layer-wise AUC probes on representations and gate vectors at each entry's final digit token measure when relevance becomes linearly decodable at each stage. The methodology follows established causal-mediation / activation-patching guidelines, treating probes as correlative and patching as the causal arbiter.

## Results & Highlights

- **A 0-2 token flag more than doubles long-context retrieval accuracy.** On the query-first phonebook benchmark, prepending `target` to the target entry "more than doubles Nvidia-Mamba's accuracy on long phonebooks" (default length 150 entries; degradation persists but is markedly less severe across 20-200 entries, and across 20-500 entries for Codestral-Mamba, 500 samples per point) — versus the unflagged baseline where accuracy decays toward ~0 as entries grow.
- **Patching just 10 gate values swaps the outcome.** Patching the Δt selection gates of only the 10 target digit tokens in the unflagged prompt with values captured from the flagged run shifts unflagged accuracy to nearly match the flagged baseline (~2× the unflagged baseline on Nvidia-Mamba); the reverse patch drops flagged accuracy back to unflagged levels — a clean bidirectional necessity/sufficiency result (Fig. 3a).
- **Oracle gate scaling spans near-zero to near-perfect.** Scaling the ground-truth target-token gates of the unflagged prompt by 3× achieves near-perfect (~100%) accuracy, while 0.8× dampening drives accuracy to near zero (Fig. 3b, factors 0.8/1.2/1.5/2/3) — the same unflagged prompt, with only ~10 gate values modified.
- **Relevance emerges ~5-10× later in depth without the flag.** Layer-wise representation probes (AUC, 500 samples): with the flag, target-vs-distractor relevance is linearly decodable "within one to two residual-stream updates"; without it, decodability "only plateaus after nearly 10 layers" (Fig. 4a), and gate-level decodability then rises over roughly 10 *further* layers beginning near the representational onset (Fig. 4b) — for both the 56-layer Nvidia-Mamba and 64-layer Codestral-Mamba.
- **Causal gains concentrate where gates lag representations.** Cumulative layer-prefix patching shows accuracy improvements accumulate in a contiguous early-to-middle band of layers (Fig. 4c) — exactly the range where relevance is already representation-decodable but not yet gate-aligned — and representation patching mirrors gate patching, implicating miscalibrated Δt gating (not B_t/C_t miscalibration) as the dominant cause. Total compute: ~1,000 single-GPU hours on one L40S (48 GB).

## Authors & Credibility

Nir Yarden, Amit Ben-Artzy, and Roy Schwartz are from the Hebrew University of Jerusalem; Roy Schwartz is a senior faculty member known for efficiency-oriented NLP work (e.g., "Time and Space efficient NLP" line of research) and leads the schwartz-lab-NLP group. The paper is a preprint (arXiv:2610.19709v1, 14 Oct 2026, cs.CL). Credibility signals are strong: full source code with fixed seeds (seed 42, datasets generated on-the-fly) is released (github.com/schwartz-lab-NLP/beyond_state_capacity); a reproducibility statement covers all results in Sections 4-6; the limitations section is candid (Mamba-2 only, synthetic task); and the AI-use statement is unusually detailed. The methodology consciously follows best practices from the interpretability literature (Alain & Bengio probing; Wang et al. path patching; Heimersheim & Nanda activation-patching guidelines), explicitly treating probes as correlative and patching as causal.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty: high.** The query-first framing is a genuinely clever way to remove state capacity from the equation, and the "gates are expressive but receive relevance too late" diagnosis is a new, precise claim that neither the capacity-bound theory (Wen et al. 2025) nor component-localization work (DeciMamba, Gather-Aggregate, LongMamba) provides. The single-word flag manipulation is elegant.
- **Rigor: high, with one notable gap.** The bidirectional patching + oracle-scaling design cleanly establishes necessity and sufficiency of the gates, and pre-activation d_t is used for averaging to dodge Softplus artifacts (a thoughtful detail). However, the headline "Mamba lags Transformers" is imported entirely from Waleffe et al. (2024) — no Transformer baseline is run in this paper, so the paper strictly establishes a *within-Mamba* mechanism, not a Mamba-vs-Transformer gap explanation; a same-setting Transformer probe/patch comparison (e.g., do attention heads show early relevance alignment?) would have closed the loop.
- **Reproducibility: strong.** Code released, seeds hard-coded, datasets generated at run time, hyperparameters and hardware documented in Appendix A, ~1,000 GPU-hour budget stated. The probe-only state-decodability experiment uses a modified 20-class profession task, which is well motivated but means the "state decodability" claim comes from a slightly different task than the phonebook accuracy claim.
- **Significance: solid mechanistic contribution, limited immediate practical yield.** The oracle-scaling fix requires ground-truth target indices at inference — it demonstrates sufficiency, not a deployable remedy; the paper proposes no training or inference-time intervention that improves unflagged accuracy without oracle knowledge.
- **Red flags / caveats:** (1) All quantitative evidence lives on one synthetic phonebook task; no natural-language retrieval benchmark (needle-in-a-haystack, MQAR, long-context QA) is tested, so generalization is asserted via the "minimal requirement" argument rather than shown. (2) Only two Mamba-2 checkpoints (~7-8B, one base, one code-instruct); Mamba-1 and the newer Mamba-3 are untested (Mamba-3 large checkpoints don't exist yet — acknowledged). (3) Most results are reported as figure curves with relative statements ("more than doubles", "nearly perfect") rather than tabulated exact accuracies, which slightly hampers precise comparison. (4) A subtle confound the patching largely (but not fully) addresses: the flagged prompt is an out-of-distribution input, so the flag's benefit could partly reflect pretraining familiarity with marker words — though gate patching on the unflagged prompt does transfer the gain, which is the right control.

## Strengths

- The query-first + flag manipulation is a model experimental design: one controlled variable cleanly separates "capacity" from "relevance availability".
- Causal methodology is exemplary — bidirectional patching for necessity/sufficiency, oracle scaling, and cumulative layer-prefix patching to localize effects in depth, all consistent with interpretability best practice.
- The diagnosis is actionable at the architecture level: it points at selection mechanisms computed solely from the token's own (insufficiently contextualized) representation, motivating alternative gate inputs.
- Honest and thorough reporting: limitations, AI-use, and reproducibility statements; pre-activation vs post-activation gate-averaging artifacts explicitly handled.
- Findings replicate across two independently trained Mamba-2 models (NVIDIA base and Mistral instruction-tuned), and the delayed-emergence story is quantified layer-by-layer, not just asserted.

## Limitations

- Evidence is confined to a synthetic key-value phonebook task; no natural-language long-context retrieval is evaluated.
- No Transformer model is analyzed in the same framework, so the comparative claim against attention is borrowed from prior work.
- Only Mamba-2 models at 7-8B scale; Mamba-3 dynamics are conjectured, not measured.
- The oracle gate-scaling result establishes sufficiency but offers no practical training-free or training-based fix that works without knowing the answer.
- Exact accuracy numbers are mostly conveyed through figures; the paper's central quantitative claims are stated relatively (e.g., "more than doubles") rather than as tabulated values.
- The flagged variant remains an artificial edit of the input distribution; downstream deployment relevance of the flag effect is not discussed.

## Relevance

对"LLM Hidden States & Interpretability"方向高度相关：这是一项教科书级的探针（probing）+ 激活 patching 因果分析工作，逐层刻画了 Mamba 内部相关性表征与选择门的时序错位；同时对理解高效序列架构（SSM/Mamba）长上下文检索失效根源、以及混合架构中 SSM 层的改进方向提供了机制层面的解释。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would the same late-emergence dynamics appear on natural-language retrieval (needle-in-a-haystack, MQAR, long-context QA), where relevance is fuzzier than an exact key match?
- Do attention layers in a comparable Transformer (or the attention layers of hybrids like Jamba/Zamba) show relevance-aligned behavior in the early-to-middle layers where Mamba's gates fail, directly confirming the comparative story?
- Can a training-time intervention — e.g., auxiliary objectives that push relevance into early-layer representations, or curriculum emphasizing query-first contexts — close the flagged/unflagged gap without oracle knowledge?
- Would alternative selection-mechanism inputs (cross-token or query-conditioned gates, as hinted by Zattra et al. 2025) eliminate the depth delay, and at what cost to the linear-time property?
- Do Mamba-3's redesigned gates (or gated Delta networks with the delta rule) exhibit the same lag between representation-level and gate-level relevance decodability once large checkpoints exist?
- Is the ~10-layer emergence constant a function of depth (fraction of total layers), training data locality, or absolute layer index — i.e., does it scale to 60+ layer Mamba models?
