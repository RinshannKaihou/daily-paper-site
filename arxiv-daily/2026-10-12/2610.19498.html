Why this matters — so what

4-bit quantization is where deployed models lose bit-level reproducibility — a supply-chain blind spot where tampering across up to ~0.2% of weights evades statistical detection.

量化（4-bit GGUF）因浮点收缩、编译器与架构差异导致比特级不可复现，形成最高约 0.2% 权重隐藏容量的篡改检测盲区。

Critical edge — read it critically

The headline 0/198 is partly definitional: GGUF has no field for the build environment, so a recorded-inputs protocol cannot pass by construction.

It conflates “not disclosed” with “not verifiable” for 44/198 artifacts, and the search-based (practically verifiable) rate is bounded only by 3 files — otherwise unmeasured.

Methodology 方法：三次测量，一套协议

  1. Census (M1). GGUF-header-only probes (8–16 MB via HTTP range requests — no multi-GB downloads) score a four-input re-derivation checklist over the top-200 repos; all 198 model cards pattern-scanned deterministically, plus an adversarial 12-card audit with one LLM agent per card instructed to refute the “not verifiable” label.
  2. Trial re-derivation. Four bartowski repositories × {card-only inputs, + pinned per-tensor recipe} × {x86-64/gcc, arm64/clang} × {default, -ffp-contract=off} — every tensor compared by sha256 (key–value region excluded).
  3. Ambiguity localization (M2). One sha256-identical fp16 quantized at pinned llama.cpp b10448 into 4 types, 10 repeats per config; byte-region diff isolates divergence; paired wikitext-2 perplexity prices the fix.
  4. Security floors (M3). Benign fp drift vs one-step nibble tampering measured per-tensor in dequantized weight space; AUC ≥ 0.99 detection floors; adaptive placement, scale sweeps, and a targeted embedding attack probe the blind spots.

Prior art is instrumented, not claimed: deterministic GGUF re-derivation is ggufpacker’s engineering; the fp-contraction divergence + fix is llama.cpp PR #25353; the adversarial-fp16 threat boundary is Mind the Gap (ICML 2025).

Log-scaled bars. Below 0.19%, ≈2×10⁶ uniformly placed one-step edits (of 1.1B Q4_K weights at 1.5B) hide from the calibrated statistics; the floor rises as the admissible environment range widens.

  • Tamper ≠ drift: one-step nibble flips move weights ~20× further than benign fp drift (median |Δw|/σ 0.27 vs 0.014) — yet the floors are still environment-dependent.
  • Targeted embedding edit defeats statistics: a one-step edit to a Q6_K embedding row flips the greedy next-token (“the opposite of hot is” → hot) while evading all three evaluated statistics — so the protocol rests on exact re-derivation, not detection.
  • Determinism is (measurably) free: -ffp-contract=off → PPL 10.750 vs 10.744 on wikitext-2 (Qwen2.5-1.5B Q4_K_M): +0.06%, indistinguishable from zero — though the bytes differ.
  • The imatrix cannot be recomputed: fp16 hop re-derives bit-exact from the provider checkpoint, but regenerated imatrix matches 0/186 entries → it must be shipped and hash-bound.

Strengths +

  • First ecosystem-level, popularity-sampled census of quantized-artifact verifiability, decomposing “inputs hidden” (148/198) from “environment unrecorded” (0/198).
  • Constructive, falsifiable diagnosis: names the exact two card-absent coordinates (FP-contraction setting, per-tensor recipe) that turn 0/23 → 23/23 bit-exact on public artifacts.
  • Independent reproduction + extension of PR #25353: weight-space localization (scales first, nibbles downstream), a size trend, and a paired perplexity cost estimate.
  • Honest blind-spot reporting — the targeted-embedding evasion and environment-dependent hiding capacity are exactly what a less careful paper would bury.
  • Exemplary hygiene: frozen re-runnable census, released harness, raw data behind every figure, explicit assumptions A1–A7.

Limitations −

  • 0/198 measures disclosure, not irreproducibility — structurally forced by the GGUF format’s missing environment field; search-based rate bounded from below by only 3/198 files.
  • One best-case publisher: all five trial repos quantized by bartowski, mostly Qwen family — the ecosystem-wide claim does not follow from n = 5.
  • Narrow environment space: two CPU (architecture, compiler) pairs; GPU imatrix generation, CUDA, MSVC, and GPU-native pipelines (GPTQ/AWQ) untested.
  • Floors are a characterization, not a defense: calibrated on one family vs uniform flips; adaptive placement and targeted edits already expose bounded-to-total blind spots.
  • Forward-looking only: pre-fix artifacts (-march=native) look permanently unverifiable, and adoption of the signed-manifest protocol is an uncosted ecosystem change.

Questions for further exploration 待探索问题

  1. Would -ffp-contract=off attain B = 0 across MSVC, older GCC, CUDA builds, and future llama.cpp releases — and what would make it the upstream default?
  2. What is the ecosystem-wide search-based IVR across all 148 source-reconstructible repositories, and how does it vary across publishers?
  3. Why could the imatrix not be recomputed on any tested CPU build (0/186 entries) — is the accumulation path GPU-based or thread-order-dependent, and can it be made bit-reproducible?
  4. Does the gap extend to GPU-native quantization (GPTQ/AWQ/bitsandbytes) where B = 0 may be unattainable — is TEE-based attestation the only residual route?
  5. Can the targeted embedding evasion scale beyond tied-embedding models, and can Merkle spot-checks lower re-derivation cost without collapsing to the Θ(N/m) bound?
  6. How should non-weight components M (chat-template backdoors are training-free attack vectors) be folded into the signed manifest, given re-derivation reproduces metadata verbatim but does not vet it?
