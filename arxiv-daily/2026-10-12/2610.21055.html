Method & the recipe the agents found

Methodology · distilled

Athanor splits research from execution. Four Claude Opus 5.5 agents — each alone on an 8×80GB-GPU node, public data only, no teacher-model calls — drive the entire post-training loop through four composable verbs:

athanor job athanor eval athanor data athanor artifact

The environment side does everything else: asynchronous GPU jobs (<1% GPU idle-wait), decontamination of every incoming dataset, a frozen evaluator, a sealed held-out twin suite, and a persistent record (journal, checkpoint lineage, transcripts) that survived three mid-campaign node losses with only 23 GPU-hours lost.

Roles are enforced, not conventional: researchers hold all research decisions, the environment holds none, and the human operator gives goal + node recovery but zero research advice. The winning submission rule was fixed before the start — best-of-four checkpoints, multi-seed scored.

Strengths vs limitations

Read both before citing

Strengths

  • Evaluation hygiene rare for the genre: frozen evaluator outside the sandbox, decontaminated data, sealed held-out suite, pre-fixed submission rule, multi-seed means, quantified winner's-curse correction.
  • Decision/execution split works: GPUs busy 93% of hours while agents wait 87% of theirs; externalized state survived three node reclamations (hours 46/70/94) mid-campaign.
  • The record is the science: full lineage reconstruction, 139/287 cross-agent lineage links, verbatim agent notes — findings are checkable, not anecdotal.
  • Cost transparency: itemized GPU/agent accounting, per-phase utilization, capability-vs-dollars curve; honest about what was not achieved.

Limitations

  • One campaign, one 4B base, four identical agents — no independent repeats, no variance estimate, no evidence of transfer to larger or different-family models.
  • Instruct reached, not reasoning: Thinking-2507 stays far ahead (80.5 / 63.0), with residual gaps exactly on math, logic, and code.
  • Profile-dependent parity: exclude ALFWorld and the submission trails by 3.7–4.2 points; the logic gap survives a 32K-token rerun.
  • "No teacher model" only at API level: the dominant corpus was itself distilled from a stronger model; agents also rarely verified training-answer correctness — likely feeding the math/logic deficits.
  • Idea diversity is the bottleneck: all four agents converged on one corpus within an hour; both diversification attempts were dropped.

Open questions

For further exploration
Q1Would a heterogeneous team (different agent models or assigned data priors) break the one-corpus convergence — or does a shared verifier inevitably collapse the search?
Q2Does the "quarter-greedy rollouts" GRPO fix generalize beyond greedy-scored benchmarks, or is it reward-hacking the evaluator's decoding scheme?
Q3What is the autoresearch scaling law — with 10× budget does the frontier keep climbing (new methods) or plateau (idea-basin lock-in)? Does a 30B base change the recipe space?
Q4If answer-correctness verification were forced into the loop, would the math/logic gaps close — or are they a property of the distilled corpus?
Q5What would a human-designed recipe (Tülu-3-style) achieve on the same 32 GPUs and $12K — are the agents at human-team parity, or only vendor-instruct parity?
Q6Can the sealed-suite protocol survive agents that build their own evaluation suites, without reintroducing contamination or evaluator-gaming?
