> **一句话总结：** 该工作在 21 个"训练配方 × 任务 × 骨干"配置（COCONUT/CODI/SIM-CoT-COCONUT × ProsQA/PrOntoQA/GSM8k × GPT-2/Llama-3.2-1B）上，用链删除、内容替换、逐层线性探针与轨迹分析三套互补测试证明：无监督 COCONUT 在逻辑任务上答案在隐链开始前就可读（删除链后 ProsQA 准确率 0.973 纹丝不动，而 GSM8k 从 0.333 跌到 0.103），逐步监督能强制模型依赖链（SIM-CoT-COCONUT 删除后 0.993→0.800）但 Llama 上可出现"删除重伤、替换零影响"的占位符链，据此提出把"链表示什么"与"模型是否用链"分开的六类隐链分类法，调和了此前互相矛盾的解读。

> **TL;DR:** A rigorous interventional study of whether latent CoT models (COCONUT, CODI, SIM-CoT-COCONUT on GPT-2 and Llama-3.2-1B-Instruct) actually use their continuous reasoning chains. Across 21 model-task cells on ProsQA, PrOntoQA, and GSM8k, deleting COCONUT's latent chain on GPT-2/ProsQA changes nothing (accuracy 0.973 → 0.973, "inert" — the answer is linearly decodable from the question's final hidden state), while on GSM8k deletion costs 23 points (0.333 → 0.103) and donor-vector replacement hurts more than deletion itself (−0.307 vs −0.230), proving content use. Per-step supervision installs chain reliance (SIM-CoT-COCONUT/ProsQA 0.993 → 0.800 after deletion, vs an inert loss-off control at 0.970), yet on Llama the same cell is a "placeholder": deletion collapses accuracy 0.997 → 0.077 while 0 of 300 answers move under any content replacement. The paper's six-leaf taxonomy (carrier / empty / bare placeholder / laid-out placeholder / assembling / unstructured) reconciles prior conflicting reports by separating what a chain represents from how the model uses it.

## 核心贡献

- **用"因果使用 × 编码内容 × 状态动态"三个正交问题取代单一测量**：RQ1 用链删除 + 三种内容替换（高斯噪声、数据集均值向量、他人题目的 donor 向量）测试模型是否依赖链；RQ2 在每个 slot × 每层拟合线性探针（对照置换标签零假设）读取最终答案、候选与每个推理步；RQ3 用状态转移聚类与链中编辑（拼接双胞胎题目的后缀）刻画状态演化与可修订性。三者的结论可以互相脱钩。
- **核心实证发现：可读 ≠ 被使用**。逻辑任务上，无监督 COCONUT 的链是"载体"而非"工作区"——答案在问题最后一个隐状态（链开始之前）就已线性可读，链删除几乎不改预测；编码内容最丰富的链恰恰是模型不依赖的链。逐步监督（SIM-CoT）能把推理步 k 有序写进 slot k，并让模型真正依赖链，但 Llama 上同样有序的链可以是"排好版的占位符"：删除重伤、任何替换都不动答案。
- **任务与骨干决定链的角色，而非训练配方本身**：算术任务（GSM8k）上所有配方都依赖链内容（donor 替换比整链删除更伤）；逻辑任务上无监督链可整体跳过。链中编辑实验显示"答案能否被中途修订"由骨干决定：GPT-2 锁定前缀（自适应性随编辑位置单调下降），Llama-3.2-1B 跟随编辑（i=2 处 0.955 vs 0.386，Holm 校正 p=0.0000）。
- **统一的六类隐链分类法**：inert→carrier（可读、早稳定）/ empty（不可读）；consulted→placeholder（bare：早稳定少内容 / laid-out：有序布局且持续变化）与 content-used→assembling（有序且内容被用）/ unstructured（内容被用但无布局）。该分类法是预测性的——每片叶子预测一个未参与定义它的干预结果，并逐一对应了此前互相"矛盾"的文献报告（Zhang et al. 的占位符、Rizvi-Martel 的超叠加坍缩、Li et al. 的分步因果角色）。
- **方法学标准高**：所有实验预先登记判定标准、报告最小可检测效应（MDE）、用置换零假设与训练时长对齐的对照组（辅助损失置零）区分"逐步监督的效应"与"多训练的效应"，不掩盖未定结论（如 CODI/GPT-2/ProsQA 一格记为 unresolved）。

## Key Contributions

- **A three-question experimental framework replacing single-metric verdicts.** RQ1 (causal use) pairs chain deletion with three content replacements — norm-matched Gaussian noise, the dataset-mean vector, and another problem's recorded "donor" thoughts — which separates patterns a single corruption cannot: cells where wrong content hurts more than no content, and cells that resist every replacement while needing the slots (placeholder). RQ2 (encoded content) probes the final answer, both candidates, and each gold reasoning step at every slot and layer against permuted-label nulls. RQ3 (state dynamics) turns "does the chain settle" into a decision against a cross-problem null and adds mid-chain edits splicing a twin problem's suffix.
- **The central empirical dissociation: readable content is not used content.** On ProsQA, inert unsupervised COCONUT chains carry an answer readable from slot 0 (and from the question's final hidden state, before the chain begins), while per-step supervised chains build the answer monotonically and lay out step k in slot k. The chains with the richest readable content are exactly the ones the model does not rely on; conversely, an ordered diagonal layout can sit inside a chain whose contents are never consulted (the Llama laid-out placeholder).
- **Evidence that per-step supervision — not extra training — installs chain reliance.** SIM-CoT-COCONUT on GPT-2 becomes deletion-sensitive on both logical tasks where its COCONUT base was inert, while the matched continued-training control (auxiliary loss weighted to zero) stays inert (0.970 → 0.973 under deletion).
- **Superposition is bounded out, and state movement is mostly not answer-directed.** No ProsQA cell holds both answer candidates in one slot (max both-readable rate 0.113 vs a pre-registered 30% bar); in 11 of 15 cells with a usable answer direction, the along-answer component stays at or below its null — only SIM-CoT-COCONUT/ProsQA shows confirmed answer-directed movement (1.94x its refit null).
- **A predictive six-leaf taxonomy** (carrier, empty, bare placeholder, laid-out placeholder, assembling, unstructured) whose leaves each predict an intervention not used to define them, plus a full 21-cell census (Table 16) that maps earlier conflicting reports onto different leaves instead of contradicting each other.

## Methodology

The study covers twenty-one model-task cells: three latent-reasoning recipes — COCONUT (recycles last hidden states as next inputs, no loss on slot content), CODI (self-distillation aligning the latent pathway with the written one at the answer position, with a projection network), and SIM-CoT-COCONUT (adds per-step supervision: an auxiliary decoder reconstructs written step k from slot k during training, discarded at inference) — crossed with ProsQA (proof-search concept choice), PrOntoQA (syllogistic yes/no), and GSM8k (arithmetic word problems), on GPT-2 and Llama-3.2-1B-Instruct, all at T = 6 latent slots. COCONUT/CODI use public checkpoints (parity-checked against published accuracy to within 0.002 on ProsQA); SIM-CoT-COCONUT was trained by the authors on all cells, with continued-training controls on ProsQA that keep the schedule but zero the auxiliary loss.

Every cell is evaluated on the same frozen set of N = 300 held-out problems. Interventions recompute downstream states under the corruption: deletion skips all latent slots; substitution keeps the slots but feeds noise (magnitude-matched, near-orthogonal), the dataset-mean vector, or a donor problem's recorded vectors (cosine 0.98–1.00 to natural slots), with a direction-by-scale 2x2 control on pre-registered cells. Verdicts are thresholded ("inert" = at most 3 answers change, the rule-of-three bound; "consulted"/"content used" = beyond the cell's minimum detectable effect, MDE). Probes are PCA-30 + logistic regression, 5-fold split by problem, against 200-fold label-permuted nulls, with a full-dimension ridge arm on ProsQA and a nonlinear check (max gain 0.0067). Dynamics use cosine step size and a K-means state-transition label (shared random projection to 128-d; verdicts only where all 5 clustering seeds agree) against a cross-problem null, plus an "along-answer ratio" against a label-permuted refit null. Mid-chain edits keep the first i slots of the original problem and continue on a twin with a different gold answer (480-twin sets fixed by power calculation), reporting the adaptive rate A(i). Confirming/falsifying criteria were recorded before the first forward pass of each experiment, and unresolved cells are reported as unresolved rather than forced.

## Results & Highlights

- **Chain reliance is task-dependent, not recipe-dependent (RQ1).** Deleting the chain on GPT-2 COCONUT/ProsQA leaves accuracy unchanged at 0.973 → 0.973 (0 helped / 0 hurt, "inert"), while GPT-2 COCONUT/GSM8k falls 0.333 → 0.103 (−0.230; 74 problems helped by the chain, 5 hurt). The same logical-vs-arithmetic split holds on Llama-3.2-1B-Instruct (ProsQA 0.983 → 0.987 inert; GSM8k 0.370 → 0.253).
- **Per-step supervision installs dependence that extra training does not.** SIM-CoT-COCONUT/GPT-2/ProsQA drops 0.993 → 0.800 under deletion (58 of 300 correct answers lost; the loss-off continued-training control stays inert at 0.970 → 0.973); the Llama twin collapses 0.997 → 0.077 (276/0). Truncation is monotone in every consulted GPT-2 cell (e.g., SIM-CoT/ProsQA 0.800 at 0 slots → 0.960 at 2 → 0.993 at all).
- **Wrong content is worse than no content on GSM8k.** For GPT-2 COCONUT/GSM8k, replacing every slot with a donor problem's vectors costs −0.307 accuracy vs −0.230 for deleting the chain outright (donor worse in 27/4 discordant pairs, McNemar p=0.000); across the eight content-used cells, donor is more harmful than deletion in five, less in one, undetermined in two.
- **A clean placeholder signature on Llama/ProsQA.** Llama SIM-CoT-COCONUT/ProsQA needs its slots (deletion: 0.997 → 0.077) yet 0 of 300 answers change under any replacement — including a different-answer donor at twice the natural norm — while the identical harness moves 16, 24, and 23 answers on the GPT-2 twins; Llama CODI/ProsQA shows the same pattern (deletion 0.983 → 0.940 with 13/0, all replacements exactly 0.000).
- **The unsupervised chain carries a finished answer; supervision builds one (RQ2).** On GPT-2/ProsQA, the COCONUT answer probe reads 0.597–0.607 uniformly across slots (ridge: 0.760 already at slot 0, and 0.760 at the embedding layer — the question's final hidden state), whereas SIM-CoT-COCONUT starts near chance and reaches 0.963 at the last slot (position-by-layer peak 0.967 at slot 5, layer 7); step probes form a diagonal (step k readable at slot k) in both arms, and the layout appears even in the Llama placeholder cell.
- **No candidate superposition detected.** The highest rate at which both ProsQA answer candidates are readable from the same slot is 0.113, far below the pre-registered 30% bar (co-occurrence null 0.0025, MDE 0.0072) — in every cell and both probe arms.
- **GSM8k answer information accumulates along recycling chains.** The magnitude-bin probe on GPT-2 COCONUT/GSM8k rises 0.310 (slot 0) → 0.587 (last), +0.277, p < 0.0001 (all GPT-2 recycling cells gain +0.23 to +0.29), while distillation-based CODI is flat (0.407 → 0.377, p = 0.452).
- **Unsupervised chains settle early; edits expose a backbone split (RQ3).** COCONUT/GPT-2/ProsQA makes its last state transition at slot 2.07 vs a null of 4.21 (settles early), while SIM-CoT keeps changing (3.78 vs null 3.85); in 11 of 15 cells the along-answer component is at or below null. Under mid-chain edits, GPT-2's adaptive rate falls monotonically with edit position (COCONUT/GSM8k: 1.000 → 0.405 at i=2 → 0.225 at i=4) while Llama follows the edit (COCONUT i=2: 0.955 vs GPT-2 0.386, gap 0.568 > MDE 0.234, Holm-corrected p = 0.0000) — premature commitment is a backbone property, not a mechanism or supervision property.
- **Cross-checks hold up.** An independently trained second COCONUT/ProsQA run agrees with the primary on every condition within 0.010; the released 10-slot SIM-CoT-COCONUT GSM8k checkpoint (kept out of the main grid) replicates the regime: deletion flips 122/300 answers (0.450 → 0.047), donor worse than deletion at pre-registered N = 900 (p = 0.00006).

## Authors & Credibility

The team spans UC San Diego (Jingjie Qiu, Sheldon Yu, Zihan Huang, Julian McAuley), NYU (Xunyi Jiang, Jiajin Liu), University of Virginia (Zhendong Chu), and Adobe Research (Tong Yu, Junda Wu). Julian McAuley's group is a consistent producer of rigorous LLM-reasoning and interpretability work, and the author list includes the authors of the CTRLS state-transition methodology that RQ3 builds on, plus co-authors of the SIM-CoT line being analyzed — i.e., the paper critiques methods its own authors helped develop, which is a credibility plus. The experimental hygiene is unusually strong for an interpretability paper: pre-registered confirming/falsifying criteria recorded before the first forward pass, minimum detectable effects reported per cell, permuted-label and cross-problem nulls, Holm/McNemar corrections, a rule-of-three bound for inertness, public checkpoints parity-checked against published numbers (within 0.002), an independent replication training, matched training-time controls, and explicit "unresolved" verdicts rather than forced conclusions. The paper openly discloses that generative AI tools were used for code, feedback, and drafting.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: The individual instruments (deletion, substitution, probing, trajectory analysis) mostly exist in prior work (Zhang et al. 2025; Dilgren & Wiegreffe 2026; Rizvi-Martel et al. 2026; Li et al. 2026; Aswal et al. 2026). The genuine novelty is the systematic crossing of three recipes x three tasks x two backbones under one protocol, the pairing of deletion with three replacements (which is what isolates the placeholder signature), and the taxonomy that makes prior "conflicting" findings commensurable. It is a synthesis-and-cleanup contribution more than a new-method one — a valuable but incremental genre.
- **Rigor**: High. Pre-registration, MDE reporting, dual probe arms with agreement requirements, donor-vs-deletion McNemar contrasts, direction-by-scale controls that correctly reclassify the GPT-2 CODI/ProsQA noise effect (−0.787) as off-manifold rather than content use, and honest flagging that one pre-registered settling instrument (consecutive-slot cosine) misfired with no body verdict resting on it. Weak spots: the Llama/PrOntoQA per-step checkpoint was selected by a post-hoc sweep over 13 saved checkpoints (checkpoint 9 picked on 60 held-out problems before interventions), and the Llama/GSM8k per-step cell has no matched training-time control — both acknowledged, but both mean one cell of the census rests on a selection quirk.
- **Reproducibility**: Strong. Public COCONUT/CODI checkpoints with parity checks, frozen 300-problem evaluation sets, all thresholds and per-cell tables in appendices, and self-trained SIM-CoT twins described in detail. The main missing artifact is code release, which the preprint does not explicitly promise.
- **Significance**: The central message — readable intermediate states certify representation, not computation — is an important corrective for a subfield that has leaned on vocabulary projections and probe accuracy as evidence of latent reasoning. The taxonomy gives future papers a vocabulary for stating which property (use, content, dynamics) a claim addresses. That said, all conclusions are bounded to small backbones and short chains.
- **Red flags / overclaiming boundaries**: (1) Scale: GPT-2 (124M) and Llama-3.2-1B-Instruct with T = 6 slots are far from frontier latent-reasoning systems; the authors never overclaim, but readers may. (2) The Llama/ProsQA placeholder verdict is bounded by the intervention harness itself — the paper concedes that "no substitute moves an answer (0 of 300)" may partly reflect what the harness can express on that backbone rather than pure placeholder use; the CODI/ProsQA GPT-2 cell is unresolved and excluded from leaves. (3) The per-step backbone comparison rests on small twin sets (53–76 usable problems) and is descriptive only. (4) "Unstructured" GSM8k leaves are defined by the absence of a linearly readable step layout — with probes capped at 30 PCA components and nonlinear gains ≤ 0.0067, "not readable" strictly means "not linearly readable in this readout," which the appendix states but the leaf names do not carry. (5) Synthetic logical tasks (ProsQA, PrOntoQA) have ceiling accuracies of 0.97–1.00, leaving little behavioral room for the chain to matter even in principle.

## Strengths

- The deletion-plus-three-replacements design is the paper's sharpest tool: it separates "needs extra positions" from "needs this content" — a distinction single-corruption studies structurally cannot make.
- The training-time controls (auxiliary loss set to zero, same schedule) cleanly attribute the new chain reliance to per-step supervision rather than additional optimization.
- Every measurement is read against a null (permuted labels, cross-problem slots, label-permuted refit), with MDEs and explicit unresolved verdicts — rare discipline in this literature.
- The taxonomy is predictive, not just descriptive: each leaf predicts an intervention not used to define it, and the 21-cell census places cells in all six leaves.
- Reconciles previously "conflicting" reports (placeholder tokens, collapsed superposition, step-dependent causal roles) by showing each is a different leaf obtained under different task/recipe/measurement combinations.

## Limitations

- Small backbones only (GPT-2, Llama-3.2-1B-Instruct); nothing here tests whether larger latent-reasoning or recurrent-depth models (e.g., Geiping et al.'s scaled systems) behave the same, and both backbones differ in scale and instruction tuning, so "backbone effects" are confounded comparisons.
- Narrow task suite: two synthetic logical datasets with near-ceiling accuracy plus GSM8k; no hard reasoning benchmarks, no natural-language multi-hop tasks.
- Chains are short (T = 6; one 10-slot parity anchor), while the interesting claims about serial computation may only manifest at longer budgets.
- Linear-probe readouts bound what "readable" and "unstructured" mean; nonlinear structure or distributed encodings beyond 30 PCs are not ruled out.
- Several cells remain unresolved or caveat-bound: GPT-2 CODI/ProsQA unclassified, Llama/PrOntoQA per-step checkpoint sweep-selected, Llama/GSM8k per-step cell without a matched control, and the Llama placeholder verdict bounded by intervention expressiveness on that backbone.
- The mid-chain edit driver for recycling models splices the twin's recorded suffix, so edits measure answer response, not re-planning within the chain — the authors state this but it limits what RQ3 says about computation.

## Relevance

对关注"隐式/高效推理"方向的读者高度相关：它直接回答了 COCONUT 类连续思维方法是否真的在隐空间中做推理这一基础问题，指出探针可读性与因果使用必须分开验证——这对评估任何 latent reasoning / 高效推理 / 思维链压缩方法的真实收益都是必要的方法论校准。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Do the six leaves persist at scale — e.g., on 7B+ backbones, longer slot budgets (T = 32+), or recurrent-depth architectures, or does "carrier" give way to genuine assembling as task difficulty forces serial computation?
- Can a training objective be designed that yields the "assembling" leaf (ordered layout + content-used + answer-directed movement) without hand-written step annotations, e.g., via RL or self-distilled step targets?
- Why does Llama-3.2-1B-Instruct follow mid-chain edits while GPT-2 locks onto the prefix — is this instruction tuning, attention-pattern differences, or readout anchoring, and is edit-following actually better reasoning or just weaker prefix commitment?
- The GSM8k leaves are "unstructured" (content used, no linear step layout) — would nonlinear probes, sparse autoencoders, or causal scrubbing recover step structure that linear readouts miss?
- Since carriers show the answer is already computable at the question's final hidden state, what is the minimal extra compute that reproduces the chain's GSM8k benefit — could filler tokens (pause tokens) match COCONUT on arithmetic, making the recycling mechanism itself unnecessary?
- How would the taxonomy extend to hybrid models that interleave written and latent steps, and to agentic settings where a readable-but-unused trace would mislead auditing?
