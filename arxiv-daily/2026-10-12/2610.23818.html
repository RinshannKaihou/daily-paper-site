Why this matters

01How they measured

5 rules × 6 benchmarks × 12 target–drafter pairs · vLLM 0.26.0 patches · one H100

  1. Drafter proposes N=6
  2. Relaxed verify π ≠ p
  3. low-prob tokens commit (p 0.01–0.09)
  4. thinking trace grows
  5. rounds track length (ρ=0.78)

Five published training-free relaxed rules — CACTUS, mentored decoding, reducible fuzzy speculative decoding, and two speculative-cascade variants — are reimplemented as patches to vLLM's rejection sampler, each framed as a relaxed target distribution (πrej, πres, πbonus) replacing p at up to three points in the verify-and-correct round.

Because α means something different in every method, a pilot matches rules at common accepted length ℓ̄, not common α. Four targets (GPT-OSS-20B, Qwen3-8B, Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B) with eight dedicated drafters plus three standalone SLMs; every cell carries a 95% paired-bootstrap interval, and budget-exhausted runs are scored wrong.

saves time only if (ℓ̄relax+1)/(ℓ̄strict+1) > λ · ceiling: λ = (Ndraft+1)/(ℓ̄strict+1)

03Strengths

why the audit carries weight

  • Checkable criterion. The (ℓ̄+1)/(ℓ̄*+1) > λ test is trivially applicable by any future paper evaluating relaxed rules.
  • Breadth. 360 cells, five rules, four targets, eleven drafters, six benchmarks — each with a paired-bootstrap interval; far beyond prior evaluations.
  • Triangulated causality. Relaxed-only tokens carry ~100× lower target probability; a pure temperature increase reproduces the inflation; budget forcing flips 335 wrong runs to correct — the extra length is waste, not thinking.
  • Plain guidance. Says outright that spec_casc_tok (relax least, trust the target more) is the only rule keeping accuracy while cutting rounds to 0.86×.

04Limitations

read the numbers with these in mind

  • Setting convention. No single ℓ̄ is reachable by all five rules; "most relaxed" maximizes observed damage even though gentle settings also inflate (median λ 1.07).
  • Right-censoring. Budgets truncate 11% of relaxed runs (vs 4% lossless) — inflation is understated and part of the accuracy loss is simply unfinished runs.
  • Single run per case outside the primary pairs; AIME24 (30 cases) and MT-Bench (80) have wide intervals.
  • One stack, one sampler. vLLM 0.26.0, temperature 1.0, Ndraft=6 fixed; reimplementations verified only at the strict limit, two drafters apply partial rules.
  • Mitigation is coarse. Budget forcing acts only at whole-run level; no mechanistic account of why unlikely tokens prolong reasoning.

05Open questions

where this line of work goes next

  1. Why does committing a low-target-probability token extend a reasoning trace rather than end it — distribution shift, or predictable from token rank?
  2. Could a length-aware rule (α scheduled down as the trace grows, or relaxation off inside <think>) keep acceptance gains without inflation?
  3. Do training-time alignment losses on dedicated drafters (EAGLE heads) systematically inflate more than standalone SLMs?
  4. Do conclusions hold at realistic serving — batch > 1, recommended samplers, Ndraft swept jointly with α?
  5. What is spec_casc_tok's ceiling with a better trusted-top-set schedule, and does it stack with budget forcing?
