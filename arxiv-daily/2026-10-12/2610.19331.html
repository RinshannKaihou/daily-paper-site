Why this matters

A reference-grade, legally clean roadmap for training open LLMs end-to-end — every mixture decision, dataset, and script is public and reproducible.

Quantified case study: Japanese-leading, English/reasoning-trailing at equal scale.

Critical edge

Headline gains beat only a dated previous-generation comparator; against contemporaneous open-weight models the MoE is merely on par in Japanese and clearly behind in English (0.650 vs 0.714).

Method & evidence
Methodology · the pipeline
  1. 01Pre-train10.5T tokens

    Phase 1 (6.3T) on LLM-jp Corpus v4; Phase 2 (4.2T) swaps in The Stack v2 + MMWPM-OSS for math/code emphasis. Megatron-LM on the ABCI H200 cluster, bfloat16, WSD schedules (a 2T-token study: WSD decay already beats cosine at 100K steps).

  2. 02Mid-train1.26T tokens

    50/50 pre-training corpus + Midtraining-v2 (7 of 8 candidate corpora kept by factorial ANOVA). Context extends 4K → 16K → 64K.

  3. 03SFT~10.83B tokens

    OpenAI Harmony format (analysis channel = reasoning, final = answer). gpt-oss-120b synthesizes traces at 3 effort levels; 5-dimension LLM-as-Judge, all scores must be 5.

  4. 04DPO154,561 pairs

    4 samples/prompt, gpt-oss-120b as judge, length-ratio ≤ 1.5 filter, β = 0.5. No RL anywhere — alignment is SFT + DPO only.

Choices that matter

  • Deliberately conventional architectures — LLaMA-2-style dense (8.59B) and Qwen3-style MoE (32.14B total / 3.83B active, 128 experts · 8 active) for ecosystem compatibility.
  • Tokenizer from scratch — SentencePiece Unigram, 196,608 vocab on a balanced EN/JA/ZH/KO mix; per-digit splitting (ablation: helps math).
  • Statistical mixture selection — fractional-factorial designs + ANOVA (Res III/IV, 8 & 16 runs), pre-registered thresholds p ≤ 0.05 and Cohen's f ≥ 0.4, instead of ad-hoc trial and error.
  • License-clean data rebuilds — FineWeb-Edu → FineWeb-Rescored (Qwen3-32B relabeling; 128 H200 × ~1.5 days over 24.5B docs); MegaMath-Web-Pro-Max → MMWPM-OSS (83M docs / 67.4B tokens).

Strengths
  • End-to-end transparency — every mixture weight, LR schedule, and filter is backed by a described experiment and public artifacts (Apache 2.0, OSAID-aligned).
  • License-clean reproduction of two contaminated workhorse corpora (FineWeb-Edu, MegaMath-Web-Pro-Max) removes a real adoption barrier for derivative open models.
  • Statistical mixture selection (factorial + ANOVA with pre-registered thresholds) — a methodological upgrade over intuition-driven mixing.
  • Honest negative results: rejected superword tokens, no-gain hyperparameters, corpus modifications that failed criteria.
  • Japanese conversational wins over gpt-oss-20b: MT-Bench JA 7.82 vs 7.33, AnswerCarefully 3.70 vs 3.55, llm-jp-instructions 3.61 vs 3.16.
Limitations
  • English trails same-scale peers (0.650 vs 0.714 on llm-jp-eval EN; GPQA-D EN 0.520 vs 0.670) and AIME (0.400/0.300) stays below external baselines.
  • No RL stage — SFT + DPO only; the reasoning gap is unquantified, with no DPO or thinking-vs-non-thinking ablations.
  • Asymmetric comparisons — a 32.14B-total MoE vs 8B/20B baselines; no FLOP- or total-parameter-matched control.
  • Same-family prior — gpt-oss-120b synthesizes the training data and same-family gpt-5.4 judges it; stylistic-bias risk left unexamined.
  • Reproduction needs ABCI-class H200 clusters (~12T tokens per model); safety evaluation is limited to AnswerCarefully, no red-teaming suite.
Questions for further exploration
  1. Would adding RL (RLVR/GRPO) to the 32B-A3B close the AIME/GPQA gap to gpt-oss-20b?
  2. Does the gpt-5.4-judges-a-gpt-oss-120b-distilled-model setup measurably inflate LLM-as-Judge scores vs human or cross-family judges?
  3. Would a total-parameter-matched baseline (e.g., Qwen3-30B-A3B) change the "competitive at similar scale" conclusion?
  4. Could higher-resolution or Bayesian designs de-alias the fractional-factorial mixture experiments at the same compute budget?
  5. How much English capability could be recovered by rebalancing the 0.68-EN tokenizer/corpus mix without sacrificing Japanese parity?
  6. Do the mid-training regressions (GSM8K, XWinograd_en, OpenBookQA) reflect general forgetting — predictable, preventable via replay weighting?
