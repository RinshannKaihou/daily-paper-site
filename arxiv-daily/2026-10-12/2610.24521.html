Why this matters

Before choosing a substrate for circuit analysis or attribution graphs: raw neurons may be the stronger, cheaper basis — transcoders add real value mainly for fine-grained polysemy.

对要做 circuit 分析、归因图或探针实验的工作，这是可直接采用的基线选择依据。

Critical edge

The "neuron feature" label hinges on a cosine threshold hand-tuned per transcoder (0.6–0.9) — headline fractions would likely shift with the cutoff; and faithfulness evidence is first-order (Jacobian) only, not interventional.

  • Faithfulness evals: replacement KL / FVU (Ameisen et al. 2025 protocol), Replacement Score at matched L0 for attribution graphs.
  • Concept evals: 113-task Kantamneni sparse-probing suite (k∈{1…32}); a new 1,500-bigram benchmark (PMI-sampled from FineWeb with hard negatives); LLM-judged sort evals (~500 trials each).
  • Structure probes: rank-1 Jacobian alignment, cross-seed feature matching, distance-from-initialization (TopK vs JumpReLU).
  • Case studies & interventions: re-examines published circuits (position/line-break, base-10 addition, "red" prompt) with steering ablations; tests neuron initialization (NI), NFT, and SNRL.
  • Scope: 13 open transcoders (GemmaScope, mwhanna, LXTC, mntss) on Gemma 2/3, Qwen 3, Llama 3.1/3.2, dense 1B–27B; MoE and cross-layer analyses preliminary.

02Results beyond the headline

  • KL reversal: after training only a linear map, pruned neurons drop below transcoder KL (FVU stays higher) — reversing the prior "neurons Pareto-worse" claim, replicated across models.
  • Faithfulness concentrates: neuron features alone reproduce the full transcoder's Jacobian alignment (≈0.3); the non-aligned rest reaches only ≈0.05.
  • Only stable structure: >50% of cross-seed-stable features are neuron features; widening 32k→1M grows the stable set only 9.8k→22.0k ≈ the 20,480 sign-split neurons. Non-neuron features barely leave init (median cos +0.48 vs 0.03).
  • Honest counter-result — bigrams: transcoder top-1 example match 75.6% vs 0.0% for neurons (Gemma 3 4B); neurons still reach 0.988 mean AUC — probing overstates concept presence.
  • Addition circuit (Llama 3.1 8B): ablating 87 addition features drops accuracy 94.3%→30.6% (28 neurons: →23.6%); mid-aligned features do nothing.
  • Neuron initialization works: aligned features 11%→92%, activation mass 65%→95%, faster convergence, fewer dead features.

03Strengths

  • Stress-tests the field's default substrate against the cheapest possible baseline, apples-to-apples via sign-splitting and matched-L0 evaluation.
  • Three independent evidence lines converge — Jacobian alignment, cross-seed recurrence, distance-from-initialization.
  • Publishes its own counter-evidence: the bigram benchmark transcoders decisively win, and the "red" polysemy case study.
  • Immediately adoptable: TopK≈64 sign-split baselines, neuron initialization, the <4× width-expansion rule.
  • Exceptional appendix depth — 21 appendices with ablations, per-model tables, toy-model controls, compute accounting, candid limitations.

04Limitations

  • Base models only, mostly English; MoE and cross-layer extensions are explicitly preliminary.
  • Only sparse, high-magnitude, single-token activations are characterized; multi-token patterns out of scope.
  • Neuron-feature fraction depends on dictionary architecture and a per-transcoder threshold (0.6/0.7/0.9) without a principled account.
  • Faithfulness argument is first-order (Jacobian), not interventional; no systematic human evaluation.
  • Cross-seed analysis ignores approximately co-occurring features; the "combinations of dozens of neurons" regime is uncharacterized.
  • Open threads: where decoder directions come from, concept→neuron-combination search, safely removing neuron features.

05Questions for further exploration

  1. Does neuron-feature concentration survive instruction tuning / RLHF and non-English data?
  2. Would interventional (not just Jacobian) attribution replicate "non-neuron features contribute ~nothing"?
  3. Is "stuck at init" an artifact of TopK / JumpReLU training recipes rather than a fundamental fact?
  4. Does rising MoE per-expert neuron count close the fine-grained feature gap (Gemma 4 26B-A4B: 20.1% bigram match)?
  5. Can 2–4-neuron "almost features" be recovered at dictionary scale via SNRL, yielding a hybrid basis?
  6. For cross-layer transcoders — largely unfaithful here — is per-layer analysis plus supernode graphs over neurons the better default?
