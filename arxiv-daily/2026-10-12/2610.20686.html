Why this matters

MLA already powers DeepSeek-V3 and GLM-5.3-class models — this makes 2-bit KV caches nearly loss-free and deployable in SGLang today.

Critical edge

The 2.51×/3.96× serving speedup comes from the packed INT2 kernels — which OSCAR also uses — so it is a property of 2-bit caching, not of the calibration; and at 1K–2K inputs RoLaQ is 0.88×, slower than BF16.

crossover ≈ 8K input · single timed run per workload

Credibility Corresponding author Wanxiang Che (HIT NLP). Public code at github.com/noforit/RoLaQ; per-seed tables with stds, explicit calibration hyperparameters, baseline formulas in appendix, AI-assistance disclosure. Evaluation spans MoE + dense architectures and a real SGLang deployment.

Methodology — distilled

  1. Target

    Quantize MLA's shared content latent to 2 bits (group size 128, four-level grid ±ain/±aout); the RoPE key stays BF16. One latent feeds both attention matching (K) and value readout (V).

  2. Rotate

    Refine OSCAR's covariance rotation inside two spectral subspaces of H = HK+HV (leading half holds 62–75% of energy). Skew-symmetric generators: d²/2 trainable params, orthogonal throughout, exports to the same dense kernels.

  3. Range

    Bussgang decomposition: reconstruction = κx + ε. Coupling gain to threshold, g(T) = 1/C(T), forces unit response κ = 1 — the MSE-optimal gain would silently attenuate the signal (κ ≈ 0.88). Per-layer thresholds land near T ≈ 0.91/0.89/0.85, below Lloyd–Max 0.981.

  4. Calibrate & serve

    Weights frozen; BF16 teacher supervises L = LKL + 0.1·Lattn on 1.43M Pile tokens — 22–34 min on one A100, no protected tokens, checkpoints reused across budgets. Read-side rotations fold into MLA's absorbed projections; SGLang + Triton INT2 kernels.

Spotlights

  • GSM8K on DS-Lite: 18.3 → 32.1 (OSCAR → RoLaQ) vs BF16 37.4; RoLaQ solves 277 problems OSCAR misses.
  • RULER @16K (beyond 4K calibration): 73.7 avg vs KVarN 67.4; multi-key MK2 jumps 7.0 → 55.0.
  • RoLaQ without protection (46.7) already beats the strongest baseline with 20 protected tokens (45.3).

Ablations

  • Subspace search with d²/2 params cuts validation KL 6.3% / 9.1% vs full exponential rotation (DS-Lite / Moonlight).
  • κ = 1 coupling: MiniCPM3 KL 0.527 ± 0.008 vs 0.606 ± 0.031 with free gain — lower error and lower variance.
  • KL + attention objective beats KL-only (48.1 vs 47.5 on DS-Lite).

Strengths

  • Built for MLA's one-latent, two-consumer structure — not MHA/GQA tricks ported wholesale.
  • κ = 1 constraint is Bussgang-grounded, costs zero extra parameters, and cuts seed variance (0.527±0.008 vs 0.606±0.031).
  • Compact rotation (d²/2 params) beats unrestricted alternatives and exports to identical inference kernels.
  • Rotation folds into MLA's absorbed projections — no standalone rotation multiplies over cached history.
  • Full-stack validation: 3 models × 6 benchmarks × 2 protection settings × 3 seeds + deployed SGLang; calibration takes 22–34 min.

Limitations

  • Only the content latent is quantized; RoPE key stays BF16 — positional keys + metadata still cost 288 B/token/layer on the 16B models.
  • Sub-8K contexts are slower than BF16 (0.88× at 1K); all serving numbers are single-GPU A100, DeepSeek-V2-Lite only.
  • Residual gap to BF16 at 2 bits: 46.7 vs 49.4 avg; RULER MK3 collapses to 14.3 vs 47.0.
  • Calibration is Pile-only — sensitivity to code-heavy, multilingual, or agentic shift is unmeasured.
  • No head-to-head with the concurrent MLA-specific quantizers it cites (QuantMLA, SnapMLA).

Questions for further exploration

  1. Can the decoupled RoPE key be quantized jointly with the content latent under a coordinated precision scheme?
  2. Would domain-matched calibration data (code, multilingual, agentic traces) close more of the remaining HumanEval/GSM8K gap?
  3. Why do learned thresholds concentrate below Lloyd–Max (T ≈ 0.91/0.89/0.85) — is this general for attention-consumed signals?
  4. Do the capacity/throughput gains persist on H100/Blackwell with FP8/INT4 paths and tensor parallelism > 1?
  5. Does the rotation + range recipe transfer to 1-bit or per-layer mixed precision, and does κ = 1 stay optimal?
  6. How does it interact with prefix caching, speculative decoding, and cache-aware scheduling under distribution drift?
