> **一句话总结：** 该文研究多智能体系统中"任务冲突"场景下的非前缀 KV cache 复用问题（直接复用可致高达 90% 的精度损失），提出 LatentRemedy：用一个轻量 MLP 将目标 agent 前缀的最后隐状态映射为少量潜在 token，插入复用 KV 块之后与共享上下文交互，并通过 full-prefill 蒸馏训练；在自建的 ConflictInstruct（240 个冲突任务族）及 ArgKP / Deal or No Deal / Job Interview 上，精度大幅超越 CacheBlend、EPIC、KVCOMM、RelayCaching 等修复方法（如 Qwen3-4B 无推理 Job Interview 上 65.00% vs 最强复用基线 11.36%，+53.64 分），同时适配器开销仅约 0.2 ms，4,096 token 共享块下 TTFT 仅比直接复用高 5.2%、比 full prefill 低 75.7%。

> **TL;DR:** Non-prefix KV-cache reuse across agents with *conflicting* objectives can crash accuracy (2.27% vs 85.91% for full prefill on ArgKP with Qwen3-4B, no reasoning), and existing repairs (CacheBlend, EPIC, KVCOMM, RelayCaching) recover at most 13.5% of that loss. LatentRemedy instead maps the target prefix's last hidden state through a tiny MLP into ~16 latent tokens placed after the reused KV block, trained via KL distillation from full prefill with the LLM frozen. On the synthetic ConflictInstruct benchmark it beats the strongest reuse baseline by +6.00/+20.83/+21.14 points (Qwen3-1.7B/4B/8B, no reasoning) and on real OOD tasks reaches 100% on reasoning Deal or No Deal (= Full, vs 92.73% for CacheBlend) and +53.64 points over the best reuse baseline on Job Interview — all for ~0.2 ms of adapter overhead (TTFT within 5.2% of direct reuse at 4,096 shared tokens, 75.7% below full prefill).

## 核心贡献

- **识别并界定新问题**：首次系统研究多智能体系统中"目标冲突"（conflicting objectives）场景下的非前缀 KV cache 复用——已有方法（CacheBlend、KVCOMM、RelayCaching、EPIC）均假设智能体间协同合作。实验表明任务冲突时直接复用可造成超过 90% 的相对精度损失，且现有修复方法在无推理预算下最多只能恢复损失性能的 13.5%。
- **揭示失效根因**：通过失败模式分析发现，问题不只是 KV 表征被"污染"，而是源 agent 的任务信息（如错误的效用函数 V 或份额 S）混入目标 agent 的推理轨迹（EPIC 中约 50% 的推理混入源任务变量）；简单的"复用后重复目标指令"即可部分缓解，说明关键是让目标指令与复用上下文发生交互、引导后续生成。
- **提出 LatentRemedy**：用 MLP 将目标前缀最后隐状态映射为 n 个潜在 token（主实验 n=16），置于复用 KV 块之后，通过因果注意力同时作用于目标前缀与复用上下文且不改动缓存本身；以 full-prefill 下一 token 分布为教师、KL 散度蒸馏训练，LLM 全程冻结。
- **构建 ConflictInstruct 基准**：基于 Evol-Instruct 构造 240 个冲突任务族 × 30 对实例（共 7,200 对），按任务族 75:25 划分训练/测试，并以 ArgKP、Deal or No Deal、Job Interview 三个真实任务作为 OOD 评测；代码与数据开源。
- **精度—延迟双优**：在三个 Qwen3 规模上精度稳居复用类方法第一（部分设定超过 full prefill），TTFT 逼近直接复用——适配器开销约 0.2 ms，远低于 EPIC-16 的 18–20 ms 重计算。

## Key Contributions

- **Identify an underexplored failure regime**: first systematic study of non-prefix KV-cache reuse when agents have *conflicting* (not cooperative) objectives; direct reuse causes >90% relative accuracy drops (e.g., 2.27% vs 85.91% full prefill on ArgKP), and existing repairs recover at most 13.5% of the loss without reasoning.
- **Diagnose the failure mechanism**: failure-mode analysis shows source-task variables (utility function V, share S) leak into the target agent's reasoning traces (about 50% of traces in EPIC), and simply repeating the target instruction after reuse recovers much of the loss — evidence that the core need is target-instruction × reused-context interaction, not just cache cleansing.
- **LatentRemedy**: a tiny MLP maps the last hidden state of the target prefix into n latent tokens (n=16 in main results) appended after the reused KV block; they attend to both prefix and reused context while leaving the cache untouched, and the MLP is trained by KL distillation from the full-prefill next-token distribution with the LLM frozen.
- **ConflictInstruct benchmark**: 240 conflicting task families (7,200 pairs) generated via Evol-Instruct with full-prefill answer validation, split 75:25 by family, plus three real-world OOD tasks (ArgKP, Deal or No Deal, Job Interview); code and data released.
- **Best accuracy at near-reuse latency**: consistently the top reuse-based method across Qwen3-1.7B/4B/8B (occasionally beating full prefill), with ~0.2 ms method-specific overhead versus 18–20 ms for EPIC-16 recomputation.

## Methodology

The paper follows a diagnose-then-fix structure.

**Preliminary study.** Two agents share a context block but receive different prefixes (task instructions). Agent A does full prefill; agent B splices A's cached KV for the shared block into its own forward pass. On ArgKP, Deal or No Deal, and Job Interview the authors compare Full, direct Reuse, and four repairs (KVCOMM, CacheBlend, RelayCaching, EPIC). Without a reasoning budget, repairs are nearly useless (best repair 6.82% on ArgKP vs 85.91% Full); with reasoning, direct reuse retains ~80% of full-prefill accuracy on Deal (79.09% vs 100%). A trace-level failure taxonomy (Figure 3) shows models mixing the *source* agent's utility/share into their reasoning. A prompt-level probe (Table 1) shows that *repeating* the target instruction after the reused cache repairs much of the damage (ArgKP 2.27→32.27; reasoning Deal 79.09→99.09) while a generic restatement does not — motivating interaction + guidance rather than recomputation.

**LatentRemedy.** Let H_prefix be the last hidden state of the target prefix. An MLP maps it to latent_n = MLP(H_prefix) ∈ R^{n×d}. These tokens are inserted *after* the reused KV block, so causal attention lets them absorb both the target instruction and the reused context, and subsequent tokens can attend to them; the reused KV itself is never modified. Training minimizes L = KL(q_full ‖ q_remedy) between full-prefill next-token predictions and latent-remedy predictions, with the serving LLM completely frozen — only the MLP adapter is learned. Training uses 30 epochs, batch size 8, AdamW, lr 1e-3, 5 samples per task family, teacher continuations capped at 64 tokens; n=16 chosen on Qwen3-4B.

**Setup.** ConflictInstruct (240 families × 30 pairs; mean instruction 88.3 tokens, mean shared block 260.6 tokens; 180/60 family split, seed 20261005) for training + in-distribution testing; ArgKP/Deal/Job as OOD. Base models Qwen3-1.7B/4B/8B; baselines Full, Reuse, Repeat, EPIC-16, KVCOMM, CacheBlend, RelayCaching under reasoning and no-reasoning settings. TTFT measured on a single RTX 5090 with GPU clock locked to 2400 MHz, mean of 10 runs, with a full pipeline decomposition (prefix forward, method-specific cost, KV splicing, suffix forward, sampling).

## Results & Highlights

- **Task conflict destroys reuse; repairs barely help**: on ArgKP (Qwen3-4B, no reasoning) full prefill reaches 85.91% accuracy while direct reuse collapses to 2.27% and the best repair methods (CacheBlend/EPIC) reach only 6.82% — a >90% relative drop that existing remedies recover at most 13.50% of, and relative drops of 72.75% (Deal) and 86.18% (Job Interview) persist for the best repairs.
- **LatentRemedy dominates reuse baselines in-distribution**: on the ConflictInstruct test set (no reasoning) Latent-16 beats the strongest competing reuse-based method by +6.00, +20.83, and +21.14 accuracy points on Qwen3-1.7B/4B/8B (e.g., 44.47% vs 23.33% for Repeat at 8B, vs 17.28% for direct reuse), and even surpasses full prefill at 8B (44.47% vs 43.78% no-reasoning; 66.58% vs 65.93% with reasoning).
- **Large OOD gains**: on Job Interview (Qwen3-4B, no reasoning) Latent-16 reaches 65.00% vs 11.36% for the strongest reuse baseline (+53.64 points, close to Full's 69.09%); on reasoning Deal or No Deal it hits 100.00%, matching Full and beating CacheBlend's 92.73% and direct reuse's 79.09%. At Qwen3-8B no-reasoning it lifts ArgKP from 2.27% (Reuse) to 78.71% (+76.44 points; Full 89.09%) and Job Interview from 6.82% to 70.73% (+63.91 points; Full 74.55%).
- **Near-reuse latency**: the latent adapter costs only ~0.2 ms regardless of shared-KV length (0.17–0.26 ms across model scales) vs 18–20 ms recomputation for EPIC-16; at 4,096 shared tokens (Qwen3-4B) total TTFT is 55.24 ms — 5.2% above direct reuse (52.50 ms) and 75.7% below full prefill (227.24 ms), while KVCOMM/CacheBlend/RelayCaching balloon to 127.44/122.56/106.17 ms.
- **Data- and token-efficient**: a single latent token already lifts ConflictInstruct accuracy (Qwen3-4B no reasoning) from 16.00% (Reuse) to 40.56%; just 3 training pairs per task family give 42.36% (no reasoning) / 61.61% (reasoning) vs 16.00%/50.94% for direct reuse.
- **Reasoning and task guidance are complementary**: on Label Mapping competing methods stay weak even with reasoning while LatentRemedy stays strong; on Priority Check LatentRemedy is weak without reasoning but near-perfect with it — evidence that a reasoning budget and target conditioning solve different parts of the problem.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: the conflicting-objective reuse regime is a genuinely fresh angle on non-prefix KV reuse, and the diagnosis (task-identity contamination rather than positional/representational corruption) is well supported by the failure-mode taxonomy and the Repeat probe. The fix itself is close to prompt/soft-prompt tuning (P-Tuning lineage) relocated after a reused cache — conceptually simple rather than deep.
- **Rigor**: mixed. Strengths are the 10-run locked-clock latency decomposition and ablations over latent-token count and training-pair count. Weaknesses: (1) the headline "second only to Full" hides that on ArgKP *without reasoning* the residual gap is still huge — 44.55% vs 85.91% at 4B, 24.55% vs 77.73% at 1.7B, 78.71% vs 89.09% at 8B — so recovery is far from complete in exactly the regime the paper motivates; (2) several "surpasses Full" results occur on Deal or No Deal without reasoning where Full itself is near-chance (20.00% at 4B, 31.36% at 1.7B, 20.91% at 8B), making those wins a low bar; (3) KVCOMM behaves anomalously (9.38% on 1.7B ConflictInstruct *with* reasoning, below its own no-reasoning score), hinting at baseline misconfiguration rather than a fundamental weakness; (4) the trainless Repeat baseline actually beats Latent-16 on Qwen3-8B reasoning Job Interview (94.09% vs 90.45%) and ArgKP no-reasoning is close (53.18 vs 78.71), yet the paper never reports Repeat's TTFT in the decomposition tables, so the cost side of that comparison is left implicit.
- **Reproducibility**: good — public code/data, fixed seed (20261005), specified hyperparameters (30 epochs, lr 1e-3, batch 8, 64-token teacher cap), and per-stage latency tables for all three model scales.
- **Significance**: relevant to any multi-agent serving stack that shares context caches across agents, but the evaluation is small-scale: shared blocks average 260.6 tokens (max 2,200; latency tested to 4,096), far below real long-document/code-repo contexts, and there is no batched/multi-tenant serving evaluation.
- **Red flags**: ConflictInstruct labels are validated by "best-effort" full-prefill evaluation (potential label noise feeding the distillation teacher); only Qwen3 tested (acknowledged); the adapter is trained on in-distribution conflicting tasks and the open question of whether a corpus-trained general adapter transfers is left unanswered.

## Strengths

- Clean problem decomposition: preliminary study → failure-mode taxonomy → minimal prompt-level fix → learned remedy, each step empirically grounded.
- The method is genuinely cheap: frozen LLM, one MLP, ~0.2 ms inference overhead, near-direct-reuse TTFT scaling to 4,096-token shared blocks.
- Strong and consistent margins over all four published repair baselines across three model scales, with reasoning and no-reasoning settings reported separately.
- Data-efficient (3 pairs per task family suffice) and generalizes to three real-world OOD tasks not seen in training.
- Public code, data, and a transparent TTFT decomposition with locked clocks and repeated runs.

## Limitations

- Single model family (Qwen3-1.7B/4B/8B); cross-family generalization untested (authors acknowledge this).
- On ArgKP without reasoning the method recovers only about half the loss to full prefill at 4B (44.55% vs 85.91%) — task conditioning alone does not finish the job.
- ConflictInstruct is synthetic and label-validated by the same class of model being evaluated; real-world evaluation is limited to three dialogue/negotiation-style tasks.
- Contexts are short (mean shared block 260.6 tokens; latency up to 4,096); no long-context, batched, or multi-tenant serving experiments, and latency measured only on one RTX 5090.
- The trainless Repeat baseline remains competitive in several settings (even winning one), and its latency cost is not directly benchmarked against LatentRemedy.

## Relevance

与"Agent & Pipeline Silent Failures"高度相关：跨 agent 的非前缀 KV cache 复用正是服务层为省算力而"静默改写"模型实际接收上下文（被源任务条件化）的典型案例，本文给出了失效检测（失败模式分析）与低成本修复方案；同时其对前缀最后隐状态的潜在 token 映射也与 LLM 隐状态可解释性方向的兴趣相呼应。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would a single corpus-trained (task-agnostic) latent adapter retain the conflicting-task recovery, or is per-distribution training essential — and how does it interact with the open question the authors pose about training on large corpora instead of conflicting tasks?
- How does LatentRemedy behave under long shared contexts (32k+ tokens, real code repositories or documents) and under continuous batching / multi-tenant serving, where suffix-forward overhead and KV splicing costs may scale differently?
- Can the method handle *chains* of reuse (agent C reusing a cache already remedied for agent B) or many-agent fan-out from one cached block, where interference could compound?
- Is the ArgKP no-reasoning residual gap (44.55% vs 85.91% Full at 4B) a capacity limit of 16 latent tokens, a distillation artifact of the 64-token teacher cap, or intrinsic to representation-level task confusion?
- Does the failure-mode taxonomy (source V/S mixing) transfer to other cache-sharing setups such as RAG prefix reuse, system-prompt sharing across users, or cross-request cache prefix matching in production serving stacks?
