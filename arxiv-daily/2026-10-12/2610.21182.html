Does Latent Chain-of-Thought Actually Reason? A Single, Unifying Taxonomy for Latent Reasoning
Jingjie Qiu, Sheldon Yu, Xunyi Jiang, Jiajin Liu, et al. (9 authors) / 2026-10-12 / 21 model-task cells · 3 recipes × 3 tasks × 2 backbones
latent-reasoning chain-of-thought mechanistic-interpretability causal-intervention linear-probing
Anchor Metric
0.973 → 0.973

Accuracy after deleting COCONUT's entire latent chain (GPT-2 · ProsQA): zero of 300 answers change. The chain is inert — the answer is already linearly decodable from the question's final hidden state. On GSM8k the same deletion costs 23 points (0.333 → 0.103): chain use is task-dependent, not recipe-dependent.

Why This Matters

Probe-readable chains are being cited as evidence of latent reasoning. This shows readability says nothing about causal use — any evaluation of latent-reasoning or CoT-compression methods must test both, separately.

直接回答“COCONUT 类连续思维是否真在隐空间推理”——探针可读性与因果使用必须分开验证，是评估一切隐式/高效推理方法的方法论校准。

Critical Edge

Every conclusion is bounded to small backbones and short chains — GPT-2 (124M) and Llama-3.2-1B-Instruct at T = 6 slots — far from frontier latent-reasoning systems; nothing here certifies behavior at scale, and readers may overclaim.

Methodology

One protocol, three orthogonal questions方法

COCONUTCODISIM-CoT-COCONUT × ProsQA / PrOntoQA / GSM8k × GPT-2 / Llama-3.2-1B, T=6 slots
RQ1

Causal use. Chain deletion plus three replacements — norm-matched Gaussian noise, dataset-mean vector, and a donor problem's recorded thoughts — separating "needs slots" from "needs this content". N = 300 frozen problems per cell.

RQ2

Encoded content. PCA-30 + logistic linear probes for the answer, both candidates, and each gold step, at every slot × layer, against 200-fold permuted-label nulls.

RQ3

State dynamics. Settling vs a cross-problem null (K-means transition labels), along-answer movement vs a refit null, and mid-chain edits splicing a twin problem's suffix (adaptive rate A(i)).

Confirming/falsifying criteria pre-registered before the first forward pass; per-cell minimum detectable effects; Holm/McNemar corrections; matched training-time controls (auxiliary loss zeroed); unresolved cells reported as unresolved.

  • Finished answer vs built answer: COCONUT's answer probe reads 0.760 (ridge) already at slot 0 — and at the question's final hidden state, before the chain begins; SIM-CoT starts near chance and climbs to 0.963, laying step k into slot k.
  • No superposition: both answer candidates readable from one slot in at most 0.113 of cases, far under the pre-registered 0.30 bar.
  • GSM8k information accumulates: recycling chains gain +0.277 answer-probe accuracy across slots (p < 0.0001); distillation-based CODI stays flat (p = 0.452).
  • Backbone, not recipe, sets revisability: under mid-chain edits Llama follows the splice (adaptive rate 0.955 at i = 2) while GPT-2 locks its prefix (0.386; Holm-corrected p = 0.0000).
  • Replication: an independently retrained COCONUT/ProsQA agrees within 0.010 on every condition; a held-out 10-slot GSM8k checkpoint reproduces the regime (0.450 → 0.047).
The Unifying Move

Six-leaf taxonomy六类隐链分类法

Prior reports "conflict" because each measured a different leaf. The taxonomy splits what a chain represents (encoded content) from whether the model uses it (causal reliance) — and each leaf predicts an intervention that was not used to define it.

Inert — model skips the chain
Carrier
Answer readable from slot 0, settles early; deletion changes nothing.
Empty
Nothing linearly readable in the chain at all.
Consulted — needs slots, not content
Bare placeholder
Early-stable, little readable content; slots used as positions.
Laid-out placeholder
Ordered step-by-step layout that keeps changing — yet no replacement ever moves an answer.
Content-used — reads and needs it
Assembling
Ordered layout and genuine content use; answer built across slots.
Unstructured
Content causally used, but no linearly readable step layout.

The 21-cell census (Table 16) maps earlier contradicting findings — Zhang et al.'s placeholders, Rizvi-Martel's collapsed superposition, Li et al.'s step-dependent roles — onto different leaves of this one tree.

Strengths

What it does well优势

  • Deletion + three replacements separates "needs extra positions" from "needs this content" — a distinction single-corruption studies structurally cannot make.
  • Training-time controls (auxiliary loss zeroed, same schedule) attribute new chain reliance to per-step supervision, not extra optimization.
  • Every measurement is read against a null (permuted labels, cross-problem slots, refit nulls) with MDEs and explicit unresolved verdicts — rare discipline here.
  • The taxonomy is predictive, not descriptive: each leaf forecasts an intervention outside its definition, and all six leaves are populated.
  • Reconciles previously "conflicting" reports by showing each is a different leaf under different task/recipe/measurement combos.
Limitations

Where it stops short局限

  • Small backbones only (GPT-2, Llama-3.2-1B-Instruct); the two backbones also differ in scale and instruction tuning, confounding "backbone effects".
  • Narrow tasks: two synthetic logical suites at 0.97–1.00 ceiling plus GSM8k; no hard or natural-language multi-hop reasoning.
  • Short chains (T = 6, one 10-slot anchor); serial computation may only emerge at longer budgets.
  • Linear probes bound what "readable" and "unstructured" mean — nonlinear or distributed encodings are not ruled out.
  • Caveat-bound cells: GPT-2 CODI/ProsQA unresolved; Llama/PrOntoQA per-step checkpoint sweep-selected; Llama/GSM8k per-step cell lacks a matched control; the placeholder verdict is bounded by intervention expressiveness on that backbone.
Open Questions

For further exploration待探索

  1. Do the six leaves persist at scale — 7B+ backbones, T = 32+, recurrent-depth architectures — or does "carrier" give way to genuine assembling?
  2. Can a training objective produce the "assembling" leaf (ordered + used + answer-directed) without hand-written step annotations, e.g. via RL or self-distilled targets?
  3. Why does Llama follow mid-chain edits while GPT-2 locks its prefix — instruction tuning, attention patterns, or readout anchoring?
  4. Would nonlinear probes, sparse autoencoders, or causal scrubbing recover step structure in the "unstructured" GSM8k leaves?
  5. Since carriers show the answer is computable pre-chain, could mere filler/pause tokens match COCONUT's GSM8k benefit?
  6. How does the taxonomy extend to hybrid written/latent models — and to agentic settings where a readable-but-unused trace would mislead auditing?
