> **一句话总结：** 本文提出"冷启动仅失败进化"（CFOE）评测设定——智能体从空经验库出发、只能从失败轨迹中学习且看不到任何验证器反馈——并在 3 种自进化框架 × 10 个 LLM 骨干 × 4 个交互环境（ALFWorld、τ²-Bench-Verified、WebArena-Verified、WebShop）上系统发现了一致的"修复–泛化鸿沟"：失败经验能部分修复源任务（如 τ²-Bench 上误归因错误高达 53.2% 的经验仍被固化），却几乎不能迁移到新任务（如 WebArena Reddit 上 Qwen3.5-4B 留存集成功率反而从 25.93% 跌至 7.41%），而真正带来泛化收益的是成功轨迹而非更丰富的失败诊断。

> **TL;DR:** A rigorous empirical study asks whether LLM agents can self-evolve from failures alone (Cold-Start Failure-Only Evolution: empty experience store, failures only, no verifier feedback, frozen backbones). Across ReasoningBank / SkillOpt / Evo-Harness and 10 backbones on ALFWorld, τ²-Bench-Verified, WebArena-Verified, and WebShop, failure-derived experience repairs some source tasks (recovery 3.5–15.4% on WebArena) but rarely transfers: e.g., on WebArena-Verified Reddit, Qwen3.5-4B held-out TSR drops 25.93% → 7.41% and Qwen3.5-35B-A3B falls 55.56% → 48.15% despite 11.1% recovery; on WebShop, GPT-5.6-Sol + Evo-Harness declines 31.06% → 23.79%. Manual annotation shows up to 53.2% of reviewed updates contain misattributed failures, and even verified-correct experience leaves held-out performance at/below the no-experience baseline in 4 of 5 configs — while learning from successful trajectories instead yields substantially larger held-out gains.

## 核心贡献

- **提出 CFOE（冷启动仅失败进化）设定**：智能体从空白外部状态（记忆/技能/harness）出发，在失败密集的在线任务流上进化；只有失败轨迹触发更新，成功轨迹、验证器判定、奖励分数、标准答案、专家解全部对智能体和更新器不可见——把"经验驱动自进化"与其通常依赖的正向锚点（成功轨迹、外部评估信号）彻底解耦。
- **发现一致的"修复–泛化鸿沟"（repair–generalization gap）**：在 3 种自进化范式（ReasoningBank 记忆进化、SkillOpt 技能进化、Evo-Harness 运行时 harness 进化）、10 个 LLM 骨干（Qwen3.5 系列、Gemma-4 系列、DeepSeek-V4-Pro、GLM-5.1、GPT-5.6 Sol）、4 个交互环境上，失败经验能部分修复之前失败的任务，但几乎不带来未见任务的持续提升，甚至降低留存集表现；把进化流扩到 800 个 ALFWorld 任务也无法弥合该鸿沟。
- **定位两个根因**：(1) 经验形成不可靠——人工标注出四类错误（虚假成功归因、误归因/幻觉归因、经验固化失真、有害行为指导），例如 τ²-Bench-Verified 上误归因占已审更新的 53.2%（ReasoningBank, Qwen3.5-27B）；(2) 即使只保留人工核实为正确的经验，源任务修复改善但 5 个配置中 4 个的最终留存分仍持平或低于无经验基线——正确的经验也过度绑定源轨迹、难以迁移。
- **通过放松 CFOE 条件回答"缺什么"**：引入成功轨迹（Success）、暴露验证器反馈（Feedback）、反思时允许恢复交互（Resume，10 轮交互 + 1 轮总结）三种放松。成功轨迹带来显著更强的留存集提升（Success > Mix > Fail），验证器反馈与额外交互只有有限收益，且把诊断预算从 5 轮加到 20 轮收益甚微——说明失败揭示"哪里错了"，成功示范"什么可靠地可行"，后者才是可迁移经验的来源。

## Key Contributions

- **CFOE problem formulation.** Formalizes self-evolution as a frozen policy πθ + evolving external state St + updater U + verifier V, where St updates only on failed trajectories (yt = 0) and verifier output is hidden from both agent and updater. This isolates the question: can agents bootstrap capability from failure itself, without privileged guidance?
- **A large-scale controlled negative-result study.** Three paradigms (ReasoningBank memory, SkillOpt skills, Evo-Harness runtime guidance), 10 backbones spanning 4B–31B dense and MoE models plus DeepSeek-V4-Pro / GLM-5.1 / GPT-5.6 Sol, and four verified environments (ALFWorld, τ²-Bench-Verified, WebArena-Verified across 4 sites, WebShop), with backbone-specific failure-dense streams built from tasks a ReAct baseline fails in 3/3 preliminary rollouts.
- **The repair–generalization gap.** A consistent empirical finding: failure-derived experience yields partial source-task recovery but limited, sometimes negative, held-out transfer — robust across frameworks, models, environments, and a 800-task long-horizon ALFWorld run.
- **Error taxonomy for experience formation.** Manual inspection (three annotators) of Qwen3.5-27B (all three paradigms) and GPT-5.6-Sol (Evo-Harness) yields four recurring failure modes — False-Positive Success Attribution, Misidentified & Hallucinated Failure Attribution, Distorted Experience Consolidation, and Poisonous Future Behavior Guidance — quantified per environment and paradigm.
- **Learning-condition analysis.** Systematic CFOE relaxations show successful trajectories ≫ verifier feedback ≈ extra reflective interaction for held-out gains, localizing what missing signal matters for transferable evolution.

## Methodology

The study is an empirical evaluation, not a new method. An online self-evolving agent is defined by a fixed backbone πθ, an evolving external state St (memory repository for ReasoningBank, a natural-language skill document for SkillOpt, compiled harness guidance for Evo-Harness), an updater U, and a verifier V. Under CFOE, St starts empty and is updated only when V(xt, τt) = 0; success labels, reward scores, verifier explanations, ground-truth answers, and expert solutions are visible only to the experimental controller.

**Stream construction.** For each benchmark and backbone, a ReAct agent without external experience attempts each candidate task up to 3 times; tasks failing all 3 runs (with infrastructure/evaluator errors excluded) form a failure-dense online stream, balanced across task type/domain/category. Targets are 200 tasks for ALFWorld, 50 for τ²-Bench-Verified, 80 for WebShop, and 30 per WebArena-Verified site. Held-out sets are disjoint and fixed: 134 ALFWorld unseen-validation tasks, 100 τ²-Bench-Verified test tasks, 47/46/27/45 tasks for WebArena Shopping/Shopping Admin/Reddit/GitLab, 80 category-stratified WebShop tasks.

**Protocols.** The agent processes the stream sequentially; occasional successes are recorded but never used for reflection or updates. Successive checkpoints are evaluated on the held-out set with the frozen experience artifact (reflection disabled, one rollout per task, per-benchmark step budgets: 30 steps ALFWorld/WebArena, 200 τ²-Bench, 20 WebShop). Source-task recovery retries each failed source task once from its initial state immediately after the update that used its trajectory. §4.1 manually annotates experience-formation updates into the four error modes; §4.2 compares full vs. manually verified-correct vs. no experience on identical source/held-out tasks. §5 relaxes CFOE along three axes — trajectory source (Fail / Success / Mix), verifier feedback exposure (Blind / Feedback), and resumable interaction during reflection (Resume: 10 interaction turns + 1 tool-free summary turn; 5/10/20-turn budgets compared) — using Qwen3.5-27B across the three frameworks.

## Results & Highlights

- **Repair happens, transfer does not (ALFWorld, ReasoningBank, 8 backbones).** Source-task recovery ranges 6.8%–16.8% (e.g., Qwen3.5-4B 16.8%, Q9 6.8%, Q27 8.2%, Q35 9.3%, Gemma-4-12B 7.3%, G31 10.3%, G26 10.6%, DeepSeek-V4-Pro 13.1%), yet held-out TSR curves stay flat or decline as experience accumulates — the paper's central repair–generalization gap.
- **Held-out degradation on WebArena-Verified (Reddit).** Qwen3.5-4B + ReasoningBank falls from 25.93% to 7.41% held-out TSR (while recovering 7.1% of source tasks); Qwen3.5-35B-A3B recovers 11.1% of its Reddit source tasks but held-out TSR still drops 55.56% → 48.15%. Across all four WebArena sites, recovery spans 3.5%–15.4% while most configurations finish at or below their initial held-out TSR.
- **WebShop: exceptions exist but do not generalize.** GPT-5.6-Sol + Evo-Harness declines 31.06% → 23.79% mean reward score and GLM-5.1 + SkillOpt is flat (27.40% → 27.70%); one configuration improves (Qwen3.5-27B + SkillOpt, 21.64% → 32.18%), but the gain does not extend across backbones or frameworks; recovery scores range 2.5%–11.8%.
- **Experience formation is unreliable (Table 1, Qwen3.5-27B).** On τ²-Bench-Verified, Misidentified & Hallucinated Failure Attribution affects 53.2% of reviewed ReasoningBank updates, 48.6% for SkillOpt, and 44.2% for Evo-Harness; on ALFWorld, False-Positive Success Attribution dominates (41.3% Memory / 43.8% Skill / 29.9% Harness; 21.2% for GPT-5.6-Sol Harness). Stronger models commit fewer formation errors, and harness evolution is cleaner than memory or skill evolution.
- **Correctness alone does not fix transfer.** After pruning to only manually verified memory entries / harness edits, source-task recovery improves in all five inspected configurations, but final held-out scores remain at or below the no-experience baseline in 4 of 5 — correct experience stays tied to source-trajectory conditions.
- **Long-horizon evolution does not close the gap.** Over an 800-task ALFWorld stream (all tasks failed 3/3 preliminary rollouts), held-out performance declines further for Qwen3.5-27B + Evo-Harness and plateaus for Qwen3.5-4B + SkillOpt despite partial recovery (e.g., SkillOpt recovery up to ~26% for Qwen3.5-27B).
- **Relaxed conditions rank the missing signals.** With Qwen3.5-27B on ALFWorld / τ²-Bench-Verified / WebArena / WebShop, Success yields the largest held-out gains (best final TSR on all four WebArena sites and WebShop + SkillOpt), Mix is intermediate, and Feedback and Resume improve over the Fail + Blind baseline only modestly; scaling the Resume budget from 5 to 20 interaction turns yields small, often identical, final scores (ReasoningBank on Reddit finishes at the same level with 5 and 10 turns). Success + Feedback and Mix + Feedback both improve over initial performance, with feedback helping Mix most.

## Authors & Credibility

The team is from Harbin Institute of Technology (Weixiang Zhao, Yichen Zhang — equal contribution, Yanyan Zhao, Jinming Bai, Bing Qin, Ting Liu), HIT Shenzhen (Ruifeng Xu), and The Chinese University of Hong Kong (Bin Liang) — the same HIT-HITsz group behind substantial prior work on LLM agents and learning from failure (e.g., the ExpeL line of work by the first author). The experimental scope is unusually thorough for a negative-result paper: 3 frameworks × up to 10 backbones × 4 environments with per-backbone failure-dense stream construction, fixed held-out sets, frozen-artifact checkpoint evaluation, plus a 3-annotator manual audit of experience formation and a verified-experience control experiment. The work is framed as an empirical study with explicit limitations, which lends it credibility.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty.** Individual ingredients exist (failure reflection à la Reflexion, ERL's failure-only heuristics, τ²-Bench/WebArena evaluation), but the cold-start + failure-only + fully blind combination, the repair-vs-generalize decomposition, and the Success/Feedback/Resume relaxation matrix are a genuinely new and well-isolated measurement. The negative result is a useful corrective to optimistic self-evolution claims.
- **Rigor.** Strong: verified benchmark releases only, 3-strike stream filtering (excludes luckily-solved tasks), infrastructure errors excluded from failure counts, recovery evaluated with frozen experience and disabled reflection, SkillOpt's validation gate separated from the held-out set. Weaknesses: held-out tasks are evaluated with a **single rollout per checkpoint**, and several held-out sets are small (WebArena Reddit has 27 tasks → ~3.7% TSR granularity, so the 25.93% → 7.41% drop is 7 → 2 successes — directionally alarming but noisy); no confidence intervals or significance tests are reported for any curve or bar.
- **Reproducibility.** Frameworks, backbones, budgets, and stream sizes are specified, and the CFOE protocol is simple enough to reimplement; however, no code or data release is mentioned, the manual annotation criteria live in an appendix (labeling is inherently subjective), and closed-source backbones (GPT-5.6 Sol, DeepSeek-V4-Pro, GLM-5.1) make exact replication impossible.
- **Confounds worth noting.** (1) The Success vs. Fail comparison uses different task streams (3/3-success vs. 3/3-failure tasks), so trajectory difficulty distributions differ — the authors admit conditions "need not share task streams," which softens the causal reading of the Success advantage. (2) The failure-dense stream is adversarially hard for the given backbone; some tasks may be effectively unsolvable within budget no matter what experience accumulates, which inflates the "no generalization" conclusion relative to a natural task mixture. (3) SkillOpt retains its controller-level validation-score acceptance even under "Blind" — so one of the three "blind" frameworks is not fully signal-free.
- **Significance.** High for the agent-learning community: it cleanly shows current failure-reflection pipelines consolidate plausible-but-wrong lessons (up to 53.2% misattribution on τ²-Bench) and that transfer fails even when lessons are hand-verified — pointing at representation/retrieval of experience, not just diagnosis, as the bottleneck.
- **Red flags.** Headline generalization relies on curve shapes more than statistics; the single clearest positive counterexample (Qwen3.5-27B + SkillOpt on WebShop, 21.64% → 32.18%) is dismissed as non-replicating across configurations rather than investigated, which is a missed ablation opportunity (why does skill evolution transfer there and nowhere else?).

## Strengths

- Exceptionally broad and controlled coverage — 3 paradigms × 10 backbones × 4 environments — rare for this genre; conclusions hold across every axis, which makes the repair–generalization gap hard to dismiss.
- The verified-experience control (§4.2) is the paper's sharpest experiment: it cleanly separates "wrong lessons" from "right lessons that still don't transfer," showing the bottleneck is not only failure misdiagnosis.
- The four-mode error taxonomy (false success, misattribution, distortion, harmful guidance) with concrete annotated examples (Appendix D.4) is immediately actionable for anyone building memory/skill-evolution systems — e.g., harmful guidance turning "go back before a new search" into "always go back from a results page."
- Honest, well-scoped claims: the paper resists overclaiming, explicitly reports exceptions (SkillOpt's WebShop gain, DeepSeek-V4-Pro's flat 47.83% on Shopping Admin), and includes a candid Limitations section.

## Limitations

- Benchmarks are text-only interactive environments; no multimodal or multi-agent settings, and the authors acknowledge real deployment distributions may differ (stated in Limitations).
- Held-out evaluation uses one rollout per task per checkpoint on modest held-out sets (27–134 tasks), with no variance estimates — small-set fluctuations (especially WebArena Reddit) could masquerade as declines.
- The 800-task long-horizon study covers only ALFWorld and only 3 backbones; genuinely unbounded evolution horizons remain untested.
- Manual annotation covers 4 model–framework pairs (Qwen3.5-27B × 3 paradigms, GPT-5.6-Sol × Evo-Harness), not the full 10-backbone grid, so error-rate generalization to other models is extrapolated.
- CFOE is deliberately pessimistic: it forbids even the cheap self-consistency or retry signals many practical systems would have, so the gap measured is an upper bound on how badly failure-only learning can go, not a forecast for every deployment.

## Relevance

该论文直接对准"LLM 智能体自进化/经验学习"这一核心方向：它用受控实验证明现有记忆/技能/harness 进化框架在冷启动、只有失败信号时无法产生可迁移能力，并给出了失败经验固化的四类错误分类。对研究智能体经验复用、反思机制与评测协议设计的工作而言，这是设定实验基线和避开"修复≠泛化"陷阱的重要参照。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would cheap internal validation signals the CFOE setting forbids — self-consistency votes, unit-style task re-execution, or a second model as critic — close part of the misattribution gap (53.2% on τ²-Bench) without any verifier access?
- Why does skill evolution transfer for Qwen3.5-27B on WebShop (21.64% → 32.18%) but nowhere else: is it the validation-gated acceptance, the skill-document representation, or the shopping domain's homogeneity — and does the gain survive on a larger held-out set with multiple rollouts?
- Can experience be stored in a form that preserves applicability conditions (e.g., typed rules with explicit preconditions instead of free-text memories), directly attacking the §4.2 finding that even verified-correct experience stays bound to its source trajectory?
- Does mixing a small number of successful trajectories into a failure-dense stream (a few-percent success rate, closer to real cold starts) already recover most of the Success condition's transfer gains, i.e., how much positive evidence is actually needed?
- Do the four experience-formation error modes predict downstream held-out degradation per update — could an online classifier of "is this lesson safe to consolidate" prevent the harmful-guidance failures the taxonomy documents?
