Why This Matters

Aggressive KV-cache sparsification silently corrupts how retained evidence is read; a deterministic ~2% relay repairs it — reliability insurance for extreme inference optimization.

直接命中「极致性能优化下的可靠性」：稀疏化在被保留状态上引入位置性正确性损伤（保留证据「看得见却用不上」），而修复只需 1%–3% 开销的确定性后处理阶段。

Critical Edge

The “32/36 wins” headline counts direction, not significance — several confidence intervals cross zero, and the effect is partly judge-fragile (CaM: +5.0 pp under one judge, +0.2 under another).

Scope is narrower than the framing: on summarization (HelloBench) gains are ≈0 and 4-gram repetition rises (Rep4 0.2005 → 0.2542); all four regressions sit on LongMTBench+ / PersonaMem-BM25.

01Methodology — a relay after selection
1 · SelectAny sparse selector fixes m ≪ L retained states per head →
2 · Compactconsecutive coords p′ᵢ = M − m + i, source temporal order, endpoints aligned at M−1 →
3 · Relocateanalytic key rotation, values untouched →
4 · Syncquestion at M…M+Q−1; decoding continues in the same space

Selectors decide which states survive; RoPERelay reorganizes where they sit. Within each physical KV head, retained states are re-indexed to consecutive coordinates in source temporal order, every head's run ending at a shared anchor M−1. Cached keys are then analytically rotated into the new coordinates — K′ᵢ = R_Θ,p′ᵢ · R⁻¹_Θ,pᵢ · Kᵢ in FP32 — leaving values, masks, and entry counts untouched: no re-selection, no historical re-prefill, no extra budget. The question and all subsequent generation occupy the same compact space, so query and keys share one geometry.

Evaluation is strictly paired: Original vs. RoPERelay runs share identical selections, values, order, and budget — only the coordinate mapping differs, isolating the positional effect. 2 models × 6 selectors (BM25, SnapKV, PyramidKV, H2O, CaM, ChunkKV) × 3 memory tasks; blinded Qwen2.5-32B judge with cluster-bootstrap 95% CIs; overhead timed on 4× RTX 3090.

03Strengths
  • Causal isolation. The paired design holds selection, values, order, and budget identical — only coordinates change — turning an engineering trick into a scientific finding.
  • Instructive negative. The Key-only collapse (8.4% vs 36.2% synced) pins the mechanism on key–query coordination, not span size; the ordering sweep is an equally decisive second decomposition.
  • A small knob, a real win. 1.34–2.70% request overhead, zero extra KV bytes, no re-prefill — and it beats the FullKV reference on LongMemEval-S at a 10% budget.
  • Evaluation hygiene. Six selectors across four families, objective-metric tasks, dual judges, 90.25% human agreement, negative cells printed in the main table, plus a failure-case appendix.
04Limitations
  • Scale and breadth. Only two 7B–8B models; RoPE-only positioning (no ALiBi/NoPE); code promised but not released at v1.
  • Significance. 4/36 comparisons regress (all on LongMTBench+ / PersonaMem-BM25); several winning CIs cross zero; LongMTBench+ has only 88 questions.
  • Judge-fragile magnitudes. All deltas stay positive under gpt-oss-20b, but some shrink sharply (SnapKV +6.2 → +1.8; CaM +5.0 → +0.2 pp).
  • Fixed design choice. Shared endpoint anchor M−1 only — no adaptive or task-aware assignment tested; alignment study covers a disjoint 200-question cohort.
  • Timing evidence is thin. 20 questions × 3 repetitions on one hardware setup.
05Open questions this paper leaves
  1. Does the Key-only collapse (8.4%) generalize to other model families — and can it be predicted from attention-entropy or phase-mismatch diagnostics before applying relocation?
  2. Why does temporal order beat relevance order (38.5% vs 31.5%) when relevance guides membership — would update-aware interleaved layouts close the remaining gap to FullKV?
  3. How does FP32 phase relocation interact with INT8/INT4 quantized KV caches — precision hazards at low cache precision?
  4. The LongMTBench+ regressions are unexplained — is there a cheap predictor for when not to compact?
  5. SelKV reported adverse reassignment effects where this paper finds broad gains — which experimental axis explains the contradiction?
  6. Can the shared M−1 anchor become learned per-layer anchors (RankSync gap: +10.0 pp for Qwen/PyramidKV vs +1.0 for Llama)?
