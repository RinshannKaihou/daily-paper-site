Key metric — checkpoint-surgery recovery

+3.78 3.78 PPL vs control

123.8M checkpoint resume: a function-preserving edit (loss change < 7.4×10⁻⁵ at restart) still costs +3.78 ± 0.42 PPL; SAGGS recovers it to within 0.02 PPL of the 20.84 control — 7 seeds, p = 0.0078.

Why this matters

Lossless-looking checkpoint edits — merges, rescales, imports — can silently cost +3.78 PPL downstream; SAGGS detects and repairs them provably without touching the function.

与「LLM 训练稳定性与可观测性」直接相关：为 checkpoint 可靠性、训练恢复与诊断提供了可证明不改变前向的检测与恢复手段。

⚠ Critical edge

All headline gains come from a deliberately self-imposed imbalance (orbit edit λ=0.2, up to ≈100× rescaling); with no edit, SAGGS is neutral-to-slightly-negative — its proven value is edited/imported checkpoints, not ordinary from-scratch runs.

λ=0 results: −0.025±0.034 (101M) · +0.019±0.024 (31M) · −0.055±0.232 (Lion) — all n.s.

Methodology — three layers

  • 1Symmetry inventory. Table 1 catalogues the exact pairs whose rescaling (A,B)→(cA, c⁻¹B) leaves the forward pass bit-identical: attention Q–K and V–O, ReLU-MLP up–down, SwiGLU's Wup–W2 (GELU excluded). Chain rule: gradients rescale reciprocally (∇A→c⁻¹∇A, ∇B→c∇B).
  • 2Optimizer analysis. Equivariance fails for every block-local rule under a shared learning rate; balanced restart points (‖A‖F=‖B‖F) are unique and forward-preserving.
  • 3SAGGS. A periodic, provably function-preserving rebalance to the geometric-mean norm point; Soft variant damps the correction to spare optimizer state.
Thm 2
AdamW is equivariant iff per-coordinate lr ×si, ε and weight decay ÷si. Covariant moments alone fail: one step leaves error η(c+c⁻¹−2), growing as 2η[cosh(λℓ)−1] with depth.
Thm 5
No standard block-local optimizer — GD, heavy-ball, AdaGrad, RMSProp, Adam/AdamW, signSGD, Lion, zero-stabilizer Shampoo — is orbit-equivariant.
Cor 4
Canonicalizing weights and moments at restart makes resumed runs bit-for-bit identical.
Cor 6
Relative updates of enlarged vs shrunken blocks drift by c1+k per interval (c² for GD, c for Adam/Lion).
# SAGGS — every T_bal = 100 steps, per exact pair (A, B)
ρ  = ‖A‖_F / ‖B‖_F
A ← ρ^(−1/2) · A        # exact orbit move:
B ← ρ^(+1/2) · B        #   function unchanged

Soft SAGGS: use ρ^(α/2), α = 0.5
  # log-norm ratio shrinks ×(1−α) per pass —
  # gentler on sign-based optimizers (Lion)

Natural drift is real, modest, and matters at scale

Pythia 410M: Q/K norms drift apart up to ≈6× in deep layers during ordinary pretraining. GPT-2 774M fine-tuning: after one exact Q–K edit, 200 updates suffice for the run to predict a different top-1 token at 7.3% of held-out positions — canonical restart makes the runs identical.

Strengths

  • A crisp, fully proven negative result (Thm 5) unifying GD, AdamW, Lion, AdaGrad/RMSProp and Shampoo under one block-local impossibility argument — with Cor 6 quantifying each class's distortion (c² vs c).
  • The fix is provably safe: every rebalance is an exact orbit move, so it cannot damage the model's function — it only changes where training continues from.
  • Canonical restart (Cor 4) is a practical, optimizer-agnostic recipe for resuming edited, merged, pruned, or imported checkpoints — trajectory-identical thereafter.
  • Exceptional hygiene: paired-seed design, seed pools fixed in advance, minimum attainable p-values stated, implementation checks on two hardware backends.

Limitations

  • No demonstrated benefit for ordinary training: at λ=0 SAGGS is statistically indistinguishable from vanilla; natural pair drift in the authors' own runs (Q–K log-ratio ≈ 0.15) looks too small to matter.
  • Evidence for real-world imbalance is observational only (Pythia ≈6× drift) — no intervention on a deployed checkpoint family.
  • Scale ceiling: multi-seed results stop at 123.8M–296.7M; the 1.2B run is single-seed over 10K steps.
  • Task scope is LM perplexity only — no downstream benchmarks, RL post-training, MoE, or state-space blocks.
  • Soft-SAGGS's α=0.5 and Tbal=100 are fixed by convention, not swept; weight-decay/clipping interplay lives in appendices.

Questions for further exploration

  1. Does natural pair drift (Pythia's ≈6× Q–K ratio) actually degrade continued pretraining of released models — and would periodic SAGGS help real mid-training recovery or checkpoint merges?
  2. How does SAGGS interact with µP-style width-scaled hyperparameters, tensor-parallel sharding (a pair split across ranks), and QAT/quantization pipelines that also rescale blocks?
  3. Can Thm 2's equivariance conditions be approximated by per-coordinate learning-rate rescaling ("orbit-equivariant AdamW"), and would that beat SAGGS on the resume benchmark?
  4. Does pair imbalance matter in RL post-training — PPO/GRPO value-policy heads, LoRA adapters whose A/B factors are exactly such a pair?
  5. Do the 1.2B single-seed protections hold across a 100B-token run, and does λ=0 neutrality persist or turn positive as drift accumulates?
