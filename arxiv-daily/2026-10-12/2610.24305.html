Muon Operates Beyond the Edge of Stability ↗

Adaptive sharpness ↔ generalization gap within each optimizer (fixed LR, all p ≤ 1e-4) — the Flat Minima Hypothesis extends to Muon, on par with Adam and SGD.

Why this matters

Sharpness-based stability monitors built for Adam/RMSprop will raise false instability alarms on Muon — divergence detection in large pretraining runs needs optimizer-aware thresholds.

为 Adam/RMSprop 建立的基于锐度的失稳预警在 Muon 上会误报；大规模预训练的稳定性监控需要优化器感知的阈值。

Critical edge

The Flat-Minima headline holds only under fixed learning rates — under the realistic linear-decay schedule the correlations mostly vanish (Coupled SGD r = 0.04, p = 0.76).

Relegated to Appendix B / Fig. 3 — the abstract presents FMH unconditionally.

How they probed it

Two experimental tracks + a proof appendix

Track A — Generalization (FMH)

  • Modified Airbench: VGG-like CNN tuned to 94% CIFAR-10 accuracy in seconds; Normalized/Decoupled Muon vs Coupled Adam/SGD.
  • Bayesian HPO (GP surrogate, 128–512 trials), 64 runs per optimizer×scheduler cell, 16 epochs.
  • Per-epoch sharpness: raw λmax(Ht), adaptive (ASAM), effective λmax(PtHt).

Track B — Edge of Stability

  • Cohen et al. (2024) protocol: full-batch tanh MLP (width 200, 5 hidden), 1,000 epochs, MSE loss, fixed LR, no weight decay, 2,000-sample CIFAR-10 subset.
  • Muon's preconditioner reconstructed from consecutive weights; classical threshold (2+2µ)/η (Goh 2017). Normalized Muon excluded "for simplicity."

Implicit preconditioner (Appendix A, Props A.1–A.5)

Mt = UΣVT momentum SVD
↓
Newton–Schulz orthogonalization update = UΣ′VT
↓
Dt = U S−1UT per-layer, S = ΣΣ′−1
↓
Pt = I ⊗ Dt block-diagonal, network-wide

Sharpness via preconditioner-aware power iteration (Pearlmutter trick, τ = 1e-9, ≤ 200 iters) — neither Ht nor Pt is ever materialized.

What they found

Core observation first, numbers after

Beyond the edge: Vanilla Muon's effective sharpness λmax(PtHt) climbs far above the (2+2µ)/η stability bound without diverging, while Adam oscillates around (2+2β1)/η and RMSprop sits slightly above 2/η — exactly replicating Cohen et al. (2024) for the diagonal baselines, and breaking the assumption for Muon.

Raw sharpness: r = 0.27–0.49. Trends do not transfer across optimizers.

  • Regime flip with scheduler: at fixed LR Muon's ASAM sharpness is ~2.4× lower than baselines (0.82 vs SGD 2.06 / Adam 1.93); under LDS the trend reverses (0.71 vs 0.32 / 0.56) with gap staying 0.04–0.08 for all.
  • Post-convergence collapse: Muon's raw sharpness falls rapidly after convergence; Adam/RMSprop plateau — a distinct dynamical regime.
  • ASAM caveat: optimizer-specific clusters persist, so adaptive sharpness is not fully reparametrization-invariant.

Strengths

5

What this paper does right

  • First mover on Muon × Edge of Stability, with a clean, proof-backed derivation of the implicit block-diagonal preconditioner others can build on.
  • Methodologically careful: Bayesian HPO with 128–512 trials, 64 runs per cell, mean ± std on every number.
  • Sanity-first: reproduces the known Adam/RMSprop AEoS phenomenology before claiming the Muon anomaly.
  • Reports honestly what fails: weak cross-optimizer predictiveness, LDS killing the FMH correlation, ASAM clustering.
  • Two genuinely novel observations: beyond-threshold stability, and the post-convergence sharpness collapse.

Limitations

5

Read the claim with these in mind

  • Scale: CIFAR-10 with a VGG-like CNN and a small MLP on a 2,000-sample subset — no transformer LM, nothing where Muon actually matters today.
  • The eigenbasis-misalignment mechanism is hypothesized but never measured or ablated; the true block-diagonal stability boundary is left open.
  • Evidence is correlational and within-optimizer only.
  • FMH confirmation is scheduler-dependent; the practically-used Normalized Muon is excluded from the central EoS analysis.
  • MSE-on-classification and no-weight-decay in the EoS track limit transfer to standard recipes.

Questions for further exploration

Open threads the authors — or you — could pull

  1. Does beyond-EoS behavior persist at LLM-pretraining scale (7B+, where Moonlight/Kimi adopted Muon), or is it a small-MLP/MSE artifact?
  2. Can the eigenbasis-misalignment hypothesis be tested directly — cosine alignment between Muon updates and top eigenvectors of PtHt?
  3. Is there a derivable stability threshold for block-diagonal preconditioners — a Muon-specific Edge of Stability?
  4. Why does Muon's raw sharpness collapse after convergence while Adam's and RMSprop's plateau — implicit regularization?
  5. Does the Flat-Minima correlation reappear under warmup-plus-cosine, or is fixed LR fundamentally special?
  6. How do weight decay and Frobenius normalization (the excluded Normalized Muon) reshape the effective-Hessian geometry?
