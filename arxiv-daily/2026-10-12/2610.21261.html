Why this matters

Tells interpretability practitioners whether SAE feature effects can be summed into joint explanations and steering — and shows reconstruction quality (FVUx) doesn't measure it.

Critical edge

“SAE coordinates are comparatively compositional” is claimed only against a strawman-ish random-rotation baseline — no structured bases (PCA, dense dictionaries, other SAE objectives) are compared.

see limitations ↓

Methodology — the coalition game

  1. Select

    At a chosen layer/token, take the top-K=16 active SAE features, each with decoded contribution δᵢ.

  2. Patch coalitions

    Retain/remove feature subsets; the reconstruction residual stays fixed, so the full coalition restores the true activation exactly.

  3. Read out behavior

    Propagate the patched activation through frozen upper layers → scalar response g(S) (target logit difference).

  4. Score additivity

    SAR_r = share of behavioral variance the best order-r additive approximation leaves unexplained.

  5. Theory

    Under independent Bernoulli sampling, SAR_r is exactly the normalized energy of >r-order interactions (Walsh basis) and upper-bounds coalition-ordering error.

  6. Control

    Sum-preserving random rotations of the same subspace isolate coordinate choice; order-1 (17 coefs) and order-2 (137) models fit on 256 coalitions, tested on 128 held-out.

Grid: Gemma-3 4B · Mistral-7B · Llama-3.1 8B × {copying, subject-verb agreement, gendered pronoun, CounterFact, IOI, greater-than} — 18 model-behavior cells, 100 prompts each.

Strengths

  • Exact theory, not a heuristic. SAR provably equals higher-order interaction energy under product sampling and provably bounds coalition-ordering error.
  • Elegant causal control. Sum-preserving rotations disentangle coordinate choice from subspace and downstream computation.
  • Exceptional hygiene. Affine/synthetic calibration at numerical zero (≤1.55e-13), parameter-matched capacity controls, 20-split stability, budget sensitivity.
  • Actionable. First-order steering extrapolation is unreliable (fidelity 0.5874); pairwise models partially repair it (0.7790).
  • Important negative result. FVUx does not track behavioral compositionality.

Limitations

  • Noisy estimates. SAR coefficient of variation 13.7–18.2% across coalition redraws; authors warn against precise second-order readings.
  • Budget-sensitive conclusion. Pairwise wins 9/18 cells at 160 fitting coalitions — 18/18 only at ≥224.
  • Weak comparator. Only random rotations; no PCA, dense dictionaries, or alternative SAE objectives at matched sparsity.
  • Narrow scope. Top-K=16 features, models ≤8B, mostly single-token logit-difference readouts; long-tail interactions and feature splitting out of scope.
  • Fragile steering study. One model + one published feature set; domain results split 10 help / 4 hurt.

Questions for further exploration

  • Q1Would structured bases (PCA of the subspace, dense atoms, different SAE objectives) close part of the rotation→SAE gap on SAR₁?
  • Q2Can detected pairwise terms Δij be mapped back to interpretable semantics — feature splitting/absorption, circuit motifs?
  • Q3How does SAR scale with model size, instruction tuning, and longer-horizon behaviors (multi-token, agentic)?
  • Q4Is the early→late-layer SAR decrease a universal “computational compositionality” trend, or does Llama's nonmonotonicity reflect late re-decomposition?
  • Q5Can pairwise coefficients (120 extra terms) be estimated cheaply enough for real steering pipelines — and does the Law/Math failure mode indicate interaction with reasoning rather than retrieval?
  • Q6Does the SAR-vs-FVUx dissociation persist across SAE widths and training scales, or can reconstruction objectives be regularized toward lower interaction energy?
