Methodology 方法概要

  1. 1
    Model. ŵ = w + Gq, G unit lower-triangular: past quantization errors are fed forward — noise shaping for weights.
  2. 2
    Assume. Instantaneous errors isotropic, E[qqᵀ] = σ²I ⇒ expected distortion σ²·tr(GᵀHG).
  3. 3
    Solve. KKT conditions give G* = L of the LDL factorization of H⁻¹ — GPTQ/LDLQ feedback recovered as the α=0 special case.
  4. 4
    Extend. Swap in a downstream Jacobian metric: tr(Gᵀ(C̄ + α·s·H_Jac)G); α trades local vs propagated distortion, picked on calibration data.
  5. 5
    Prove. Joint rotation+feedback optimum is n·det(H)1/n, attained constructively via geometric mean decomposition (Proposition 1).
Where theory breaks The trace objective is fixed-statistics — it never sees the rotation-dependent grid step Δ(R) or feedback saturation. Exactly there, finite-level reality diverges (see Results).

Honest negative result · 附录C

The trace-optimal joint design attains its bound to numerical precision (ratio 1.00000) — yet realizes ~3.5× worse metric error at M=2 on MNIST (347.73 vs 98.61), and loses to RHT+EFQ on all three metrics in 16/16 LM comparisons (e.g. Llama-2-7B M=16: 7.563 vs 7.431 PPL).

Strengths 优势

  • One trace formulation unifies GPTQ/LDLQ feedback, Jacobian-aware design and rotations — with a proved joint optimum and constructive attainer.
  • The Jacobian metric is plug-in and pays off: 24/24 matched LM comparisons improved (logit MSE & accuracy), across five language models.
  • Exemplary scientific honesty — ships its own negative-result diagnostic (Appendix C) with mechanism and numbers, rather than hiding it.
  • QAT via straight-through gradients is a fresh angle: feedback designed by descent, not closed-form surrogates.
  • Crowded related work (GPTQ, QuIP, QEP, GuidedQuant, YAQA, Qronos) cleanly positioned via a criterion-mapping table.

Limitations 局限

  • Assumption 1 (isotropic, fixed-variance errors) decouples design from real quantizer behavior — the trace optimum loses to RHT+EFQ 16/16.
  • No external SOTA baselines (official GPTQ/AWQ/QuIP#/QuaRot/SpinQuant); "Local EFQ" is the de facto comparison point.
  • LM regime stops at M ∈ {8,16} uniform grids — the competitive 2–4 bit frontier is untested; no downstream-task accuracy.
  • Edge over covariance EFQ shrinks as M grows; practical value concentrates at very low bits, where it is least tested on LLMs.
  • QAT evidence is toy-scale (2-block, width-64 nanoGPT, 200+100 steps) — scalability to pretrained LLMs is open.
  • Blockwise bound Σ_b n_b·det(H_bb)1/n_b is weaker than the headline full-coordinate bound.

Open Questions 待探索问题

  1. Can a scale-aware term (predicting Δ(R)² per block) make the joint optimum also win at finite levels, closing the Appendix-C gap?
  2. Complementary or redundant with QuIP#'s LDLQ+Hadamard and SpinQuant's learned rotations at 2–3 bits?
  3. Does trainable feedback G scale beyond 2-block nanoGPT to 7B-class models, at acceptable calibration-compute cost?
  4. Would a second-order (curvature-aware, YAQA-style) downstream metric change the α-sweep conclusions?
