> **一句话总结：** 从信息流（information flow）视角诊断扩散多模态大模型（dMLLM）近似 KV 缓存引发的"复读诅咒"（Repeat Curse）：沿 token/step/layer 三个轴定位出注意力锚定被驱散（F1）、KV 状态过期（F2）、深层熵不收敛（F3）三类破坏，并据此提出免训练、即插即用的 CoTA++（CTAR 重锚注意力 + DAR 解码感知刷新 + CTEV 熵引导投票），在 3 个 dMLLM × 2 种缓存后端上将 512-token 长回复的样本复读率从最高 99.6% 降到最差 17.0%（LLaDA-V+dLLM-Cache 上 99.6%→8.2%，最长复读串从 455 token 降到 3），恢复 9 个基准上大部分被缓存损失的精度（LLaDA-V DocVQA 70.5→82.1，无缓存为 83.9），且整体仅增加 15% 延迟（仍比无缓存解码快 2.1 倍）。

> **TL;DR:** Approximate KV caching (dLLM-Cache, SlowFast) — the de facto acceleration for diffusion multimodal LLMs — breaks up to 99.6% of long responses with runaway single-token repeats (the "Repeat Curse"; on 500 MS-COCO val2014 detailed captions at L=512, dLLM-Cache pushes LLaDA-V's sample repetition rate from 2.4% vanilla to 99.6% with one run of 455 tokens). The paper traces the failure along three information-flow axes — attention anchoring dispersed within a step (F1), stale KV states across steps (F2), and deep-layer entropy that never consolidates across layers (F3) — and derives CoTA++, a training-free plug-in with one remedy per axis (CTAR re-forms attention routing, DAR reserves refresh slots for positions about to commit, CTEV discounts confidence by context entropy). CoTA++ cuts LLaDA-V's SRR at L=512 to 8.2% (vs 47.4% for the ICLR'26 conference version CoTA) with no run longer than 3 tokens, recovers 11.6 of the 13.4 DocVQA points and 20.2 of 21.5 LLaVA-Bench points the cache cost, and runs at 1.15× the cache's latency (2.1× faster than uncached). Residual weaknesses: all models are 8B with 32-layer towers, the CTAR band/CTEV window are hard-coded layer ranges, quality is judged by a single LLM judge, and recovery on MathVista is partial (1.3 of 4.5 points).

## 核心贡献

- **定义并量化"复读诅咒"（Repeat Curse）**：近似 KV 缓存使 dMLLM 的回复在相邻位置重复同一 token、形成打断句子的长复读串。在 500 张 MS-COCO val2014 详细描述任务上，无缓存的三个模型（LLaDA-V、MMaDA、LaViDa）复读率至多 4.6%，而 dLLM-Cache 把 L=512 时的样本复读率（SRR）推到 97.4–99.6%，最长复读串达 409–465 token（占 512 预算的 80–91%）。
- **三轴信息流机制诊断**：沿 token/step/layer 三个轴追踪上下文 token 的信息流，把复读定位为三类独立且可叠加的破坏——**F1**（步内路由）：缓存驱散了查询对邻近上下文 token 的局部注意力锚定，复读区域丢失的锚定量是远端区域的 2.8–7.6 倍；**F2**（跨步时效）：按相似度刷新的缓存不感知过期程度，复读位置的 KV 陈旧度中位数为 4 步（正常位置为 1）；**F3**（跨层整合）：复读 token 的深层熵在深层窗口（第 26–30 层）不收敛，79% 的复读位置在第 31 层前未降至平台熵的一半（正常位置仅 36%）。
- **推导出 CoTA++ 免训练即插即用框架**：每个组件对应一类破坏——CTAR（Context-Token Attention Re-anchoring）在深层带对被监视器选中的位置按当前 key 重构注意力路由；DAR（Decode-Aware Refresh）把缓存刷新预算中 r 个槽位保留给即将提交的高置信位置；CTEV（Context-Token Entropy-Guided Voting）用上下文深层熵对置信度打折扣、重排提交顺序。三者作用于一个解码步的不同对象，可与任意 dMLLM/缓存自由组合。
- **全面的实证验证**：3 个 dMLLM × 2 种缓存后端 × 3 种回复长度，SRR 平均降低 88–99%，9 个多模态基准上恢复缓存损失的大部分精度，整体开销仅 1.15×；组件网格消融（所有子集）与设计替代方案分析（重加权 vs 重构、按 F1/F3 信号填充 DAR 槽位、自熵 vs 上下文熵、否决 vs 梯度惩罚）逐一验证了设计选择。

## Key Contributions

- **Names and quantifies the Repeat Curse**: approximate caching makes cached dMLLM responses repeat one token in adjacent-position runs that break the sentence. On 500 MS-COCO val2014 detailed captions, vanilla LLaDA-V / MMaDA / LaViDa repeat in at most 4.6% of responses at any length, while dLLM-Cache drives SRR to 97.4–99.6% at L=512 with the longest run reaching 409–465 tokens (80–91% of the token budget); the paper also localizes the culprit via a cache-component sweep (sweeping suffix interval Es from 1→7 lifts SRR 0.6%→96.8%; similarity fraction α from 1→0 lifts it 0.4%→99.8%).
- **A three-axis information-flow diagnosis** that decomposes the failure into non-redundant, compounding disruptions: F1 (within-step routing — cached attention misses the anchors committed since it was formed; repeat regions miss 1.01 vs 0.40 anchors per position), F2 (cross-step timeliness — similarity-based refresh favors already-committed positions, so positions about to commit decode on stale KV; repetition nearly doubles from 4.9% right after a full refresh to 9.7% four steps later, p=1.2e-13), and F3 (cross-layer consolidation — the top fifth of positions by context entropy repeats 4.6× as often and holds 54% of all repetitions). Tokens hit by all three disruptions repeat at 55% vs 2.4% with none.
- **CoTA++, a training-free, plug-and-play remedy per disruption**: CTAR re-forms the attention distribution of monitor-selected positions against current cached keys in the deep band D={25..32} (monitor: count of newly committed context tokens within ±5, threshold κ=1); DAR reserves r=4 refresh slots for the top-confidence masked positions so they commit at staleness Δ=0; CTEV replaces the commit confidence with Score = c(i) − λ·Ē_ctx(i)/log2|V| (λ=0.25, deep window W={26..30}), reordering but never forbidding unmasking.
- **Broad, honest evaluation**: three 8B dMLLMs × two caching backends × lengths 64/128/512; SRR reduced 95% (LLaDA-V), 88% (LaViDa), 99% (MMaDA) on average, with no run longer than 3 tokens in any setting; most cache-induced accuracy loss recovered on nine benchmarks at ≤0.4-pt cost on short-answer ones; per-component latency/memory accounting (DAR 1.01×, CTAR 1.06×, CTEV 1.07×, full 1.15×) and design-alternative ablations that rule out re-weighting instead of re-forming, own-entropy penalties, vetoes, and wrong-layer readouts.

## Methodology

**Setup.** A dMLLM (running example LLaDA-V-8B: SigLIP vision encoder + 2-layer MLP projector + LLaDA-8B-Instruct diffusion LM) decodes a fully masked suffix of length L by confidence-based remasking: each of N steps predicts all masked positions, commits the k=M/N most confident, repeats. Bidirectional attention invalidates the exact autoregressive KV cache, so practical systems use approximate caches such as dLLM-Cache, which refreshes the prompt every Ep=25 steps, the whole suffix every Es=7 steps, and otherwise only the ⌊αM⌋=25% of suffix positions whose cross-step feature similarity has dropped most.

**Diagnosis.** Repetition is measured by ARR (adjacent-repetition rate over token pairs), SRR (share of responses with ARR>0), and run-length statistics MRL/ARL/95pRL, plus seq-rep-n/distinct-n for phrase-level effects. The analysis traces context-token information flow along three axes, each with observation/meanism/evidence: attention maps show vanilla queries anchor locally on ±5 context tokens and caching disperses exactly where repeats arise (F1); a staleness formalism Δ_i (steps since last recomputation) shows repeats concentrate in the stale tail at the decode moment and similarity-based refresh systematically starves the about-to-commit positions (F2); logit-lens readouts of per-layer entropy show the deep-window consolidation that normally happens fails at repeat positions and high context entropy predicts repetition before it is committed (F3). Controlled comparisons separate the three (staleness explains 81% of the shallow-band anchoring loss but only 5% in the deep band; consolidation failure persists at equal staleness).

**Remedy.** CoTA++ wraps the cached decoding loop in three components that share no state. CTAR fires only when a position's monitor count (newly committed context tokens within ±w=5 since its last re-anchoring) reaches κ=1, then recomputes q=W_Q·LN(h), the softmax attention against the cache's current keys, and the output projection in layers 25–32 — keys, values, and FFN outputs stay cached. DAR keeps the backend's refresh budget size b fixed but fills r=4 slots with the previous step's top-r-confidence masked positions (the rest still chosen by lowest similarity), guaranteeing Δ=0 at commit. CTEV scores each masked position by its confidence minus λ=0.25 times the mean deep-window entropy of its committed ±5 neighbors, normalized by log2|V|; the top-k commit selection uses this score instead of raw confidence.

**Evaluation.** Repeat Curse: 500 MS-COCO val2014 images, "describe the image in detail", L∈{64,128,512}, quality judged 1–10 by gpt-5.6-luna against five human references. General ability: nine benchmarks (DocVQA, ChartQA, MMStar, MME, SEED-Bench, MMBench, LLaVA-Bench-Wild, MathVista, MathVerse) under released protocols. Efficiency: 20 images on one A100 at L=64. Code and the image list are released (github.com/ErikZ719/CoTA).

## Results & Highlights

- **Repeat-Curse mitigation (Table VI, 500 MS-COCO captions, L=512)**: LLaDA-V+dLLM-Cache repeats in 99.6% of responses (ARR 14.70%, longest run 455 tokens) vs 2.4% uncached; +CoTA++ cuts SRR to 8.2% (ARR 0.03%, max run 3 tokens), vs 47.4% for the conference CoTA under the same setting. At L=128 the cache's 96.8% SRR falls to 2.4% (vanilla 0.2%). Worst residual case: LaViDa+SlowFast at L=512 goes 85.6%→17.0%; MMaDA+dLLM-Cache goes 97.4%→0.2%. Averaged over lengths and backends, SRR drops 95%/88%/99% (LLaDA-V/LaViDa/MMaDA) and ARR by 99.5%/97.7%/99.9%; no run longer than 3 tokens survives anywhere.
- **General multimodal benchmarks (Table IX, LLaDA-V under dLLM-Cache)**: the cache costs 13.4 points on DocVQA (83.9→70.5) and 21.5 on LLaVA-Bench-Wild (51.7→30.2); CoTA++ returns them to 82.1 and 50.4 — within 1.8 of the uncached model (conference CoTA: 81.6/50.0). On MathVerse it recovers 2.2 of the 3.1 lost points on LLaDA-V (26.1→28.3 vs 29.2 vanilla) and 3.8 of 6.3 on LaViDa (15.8→19.6 vs 22.1). Recovery is partial on MathVista: 1.3 of the 4.5 lost points (55.9→57.2 vs 60.4). Under SlowFast, LLaVA-Bench-Wild recovers from 37.6 to 53.5, above the uncached 51.7. Short-answer benchmarks (MMStar, MME, SEED, MMBench) stay within 0.4 points of the cached model.
- **Quality is not traded away (Tables VII–VIII, judge gpt-5.6-luna, 1–10)**: caching lowers the judge score by 0.22–1.23 points; CoTA++ raises it in all six model-backend pairs to within 0.34 of the uncached model (within 0.04 in four) — e.g., LLaDA-V+dLLM-Cache at L=512 improves from 3.03 to 4.00 vs 3.93 vanilla. Phrase-level repetition at L=512 under dLLM-Cache (repeated 4-grams) rises from 24.6% to 37.9% on LLaDA-V; CoTA++ brings it to 20.5%, below the uncached level (MMaDA 7.7→2.2 vs 2.2 vanilla; LaViDa 14.5→1.9 vs 2.0). Gains are not bought with shorter responses (L=512 lengths: 510 vs 509 cached).
- **Efficiency (Table XI, LLaDA-V L=64, one A100)**: uncached decoding takes 21.4 s/image (2.9 tok/s, 30.6 GiB); dLLM-Cache 8.8 s (7.1 tok/s, 2.4× speedup); CoTA++ 10.2 s (6.2 tok/s) = 1.15× cache latency and +0.9 GiB — still 2.1× faster than uncached. Per component: DAR 1.01× (it only redirects, never adds, recomputation), CTAR 1.06×, CTEV 1.07× (its logit-lens projections dominate the cost).
- **Ablation grid (Table X, LLaDA-V+dLLM-Cache, SRR at L=64/128/512)**: cache alone 70.8/96.8/99.6; single components — DAR 1.4/4.4/11.2 (strongest), CTAR 8.0/20.6/62.0, CTEV 35.6/59.6/94.0 (weakest) — none removes the L=512 tail (runs of 165/160/420 tokens survive); any pair does (max run ≤3), with DAR+CTAR 0.6/3.0/9.6 vs full CoTA++ 0.4/2.4/8.2 (vanilla 0.4/0.2/2.4). Design alternatives (Table XII, L=128): re-weighting the stored routing fails (additive distance bias SRR 98.0%, gated gain 99.0%) while re-forming gives 21.0%; DAR slots filled by the F1 or F3 signal instead of confidence leave SRR at 98.0%/57.0% vs 3.0% for confidence; CTEV on the position's own entropy leaves 98.0% and a hard veto reproduces the cache (96.0%), while the graded λ=0.25 penalty gives 61.0% (λ=0.5 gives 36.0% but pushes Rep-4 above the uncached level).
- **Mechanistic validation that each component fixes its own finding**: with CTAR the diagonal anchoring band at the decode moment returns as sharp as uncached; with DAR the mean decode-moment staleness drops from 1.89 to 0.003 steps (zero at 99% of decode moments); CTEV's 1,461 changed commit decisions move commitments out of the high-context-entropy region where the repeat rate climbs steeply. A classic no-repeat n-gram decoding rule, by contrast, caps run lengths but leaves nearly all responses carrying a repeat and does not improve the judge score.

## Authors & Credibility

Qiyan Zhao, Xiaofeng Zhang, Xu-Yao Zhang (Senior Member, IEEE), Shijie Han, Zhepeng Li, Shunzhi Zhu, and Da-Han Wang (Senior Member, IEEE), spanning the State Key Laboratory of Multimodal Artificial Intelligence Systems at the Institute of Automation, CAS (NLPR), Shanghai Jiao Tong University, the University of Hong Kong, and UCAS. This is the IEEE TPAMI journal extension of the authors' ICLR 2026 paper ("Context Tokens are Anchors", ref [1]) — the extension adds the cross-step staleness axis (F2), the DAR component, and grows the study from one model/backend to three dMLLMs, two caching backends, three lengths, and nine benchmarks, with per-component efficiency and design analyses. Code and the exact 500-image COCO list are public (github.com/ErikZ719/CoTA). Statistical practice is unusually careful for a systems paper: Wilcoxon signed-rank tests over images throughout (p-values down to 3.4e-15), 95% intervals, error bars, and explicit controlled comparisons separating the three disruptions. The acknowledgment discloses that generative tools (Grammarly, Claude) were used only for grammar polishing.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: the core idea (context tokens as attention anchors; CTAR and CTEV) is inherited from the authors' ICLR 2026 conference paper; the genuinely new journal material is the F2 staleness analysis, DAR, and the scaled evaluation. As a TPAMI-style extension this is legitimate and substantive, but readers should know roughly two-thirds of the framework is not new in this paper. The information-flow diagnosis itself (attention anchoring + logit-lens entropy + staleness formalism) is a solid, genuinely mechanistic contribution in a subfield where most caching papers only report task accuracy.
- **Rigor**: strong. The cache-component sweep (Table II) cleanly isolates suffix approximation as the culprit; the 2×2-style joint diagnosis quantifies that staleness explains 81% of the shallow-band anchoring loss but only 5% of the deep-band loss, justifying separate remedies; the full component-subset grid and the design-alternative table (Table XII) rule out the obvious cheaper variants. The comparison against the no-repeat n-gram rule is a fair negative control that a decoding-time band-aid does not fix quality.
- **Reproducibility**: good — code and evaluation list released, all hyperparameters stated (κ=1, w=5, r=4, λ=0.25, D={25..32}, W={26..30}), models are open-weight 8B, cache settings given (Ep=25, Es=7, α=0.25), and the judge protocol is specified. The main unstated detail is how D and W were chosen for MMaDA's different architecture.
- **Significance**: the Repeat Curse is a real deployment blocker for dMLLMs (99.6% of long responses broken under the default acceleration recipe), and a training-free ≤15%-overhead fix with a mechanistic account is valuable. The broader lesson — a cache must preserve the information flow it accelerates, not just keep states fresh on average — is a transferable design principle.
- **Red flags / weaknesses**: (1) *Depth-hard-coded intervention sites* — CTAR's band D={25..32} and CTEV's window W={26..30} are tied to 32-layer towers; LLaDA-V and LaViDa literally share the LLaDA-8B language tower, so the three "models" are less independent than the count suggests, and no procedure is given for picking the band on other depths. (2) *Narrow testbed for the headline claim* — the Repeat Curse statistics come from one task (English detailed captioning on 500 COCO images with a single instruction); prevalence in dialogue, long-form VQA, or non-English generation is unmeasured. (3) *Single LLM judge* (gpt-5.6-luna) for the 1–10 quality scores with no human-rater check; absolute MMaDA scores (~1.9) are so low that the judge protocol's sensitivity is itself a question. (4) *Partial recovery* on MathVista (1.3 of 4.5 points) and LLaVA-Bench for LaViDa (1.3 of 4.7) — "recovers most of the accuracy lost" overstates a few cells. (5) *Only two caching backends* evaluated (dLLM-Cache, SlowFast); Fast-dLLM, Prefix-KV, and dkv-cache are positioned in Table I but not tested, and no AR-style repetition baselines beyond the n-gram rule (e.g., repetition penalty, DRY-style sampling) are compared. (6) The paper's own limitation statement concedes compute limits: no larger dMLLMs, no further caches.

## Strengths

- A clean diagnosis-to-remedy mapping: each of F1/F2/F3 is tied to a specific information-flow object (attention routing, KV staleness, deep-layer entropy), and each component intervenes exactly where its disruption arises — then per-component analyses (Figs. 10–12) verify the intervention moved its own quantity.
- Training-free, plug-and-play, and architecture-agnostic in the right way: DAR is essentially free (1.01×) because it redirects the existing refresh budget; CTEV only reorders commits and never forbids a token; components compose with both tested backends and all three models.
- The failure characterization is more useful than the fix alone: the facts that a single run can consume 80–91% of the token budget, that function words open 54% of runs (punctuation 33%, content words 14%), and that repetition tracks the refresh cycle (4.9%→9.7% across the 7-step interval) are directly actionable for cache designers.
- Honest reporting: residual failures are shown (17.0% worst-case SRR at LaViDa+SlowFast L=512; doubled periods in the case study), the conference version's weaknesses are quantified (CoTA leaves a 286-token run under SlowFast), and the negative results for cheaper design alternatives are published.

## Limitations

- Evaluation covers only 8B-class dMLLMs and two caching backends; no demonstration on larger towers, other cache designs (Fast-dLLM, dkv-cache), or text-only dLLMs, and the authors explicitly flag compute as the reason.
- The intervention sites are hard-coded layer ranges (CTAR D={25..32}, CTEV W={26..30}); transfer to different depths requires re-derivation, and the paper does not provide an automatic band-selection procedure.
- The headline repetition statistics rest on one captioning task/instruction and 500 images; quality relies on a single LLM judge without human verification.
- Recovery of general ability is incomplete on MathVista and on LaViDa's LLaVA-Bench/ChartQA cells.
- The three budgets (κ, r, λ) are fixed per run; the adaptive single-risk-score allocation the authors sketch (§VII-B) is left to future work, and CTEV's logit-lens projections (O(|W|·M·d·|V|)) dominate its cost — a cheaper entropy estimator is unexplored.

## Relevance

命中"极端性能优化下的可靠性"方向：近似 KV 缓存正是激进的推理加速优化，本文系统地刻画了它引入的输出损坏（复读诅咒）、给出机制级诊断和低开销可插拔修复，是"优化引入正确性问题"的典型案例；同时其信息流分析（注意力锚定、logit-lens 逐层熵读取、KV 陈旧度形式化）也贴合"LLM 隐状态与可解释性"兴趣，展示了可解释性工具如何直接指导推理系统的修复设计。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Do the three signatures (F1–F3) and CoTA++ transfer to cache-free repetition under aggressive step reduction (attributed to mask-prior drift in ref [76]) — i.e., is the Repeat Curse one failure with two triggers, and would DAR-style decode-aware scheduling help there?
- Can the CTAR band and CTEV window be selected automatically (e.g., from the layer-wise entropy plateau profile) instead of hard-coded to layers 25–32 / 26–30, and does the remedy hold on deeper or shallower towers and on text-only dLLMs like Dream 7B or LLaDA2.0?
- Would an adaptive budget that merges the three signals (monitor count, confidence rank, context entropy) into one risk score allocating recomputation per step beat the fixed κ/r/λ budgets, as §VII-B speculates — and can the CTEV entropy penalty be approximated without vocabulary-sized projections?
- Can the F1 anchor analysis inform cache design itself (e.g., staleness- or anchor-aware refresh as the default criterion) so the fix moves into the backend rather than being a wrapper, and does that beat CoTA++'s 1.15× overhead?
- Does training-time use of the CTEV signal (penalizing commits from unconsolidated contexts) teach dMLLMs to be cache-robust, eliminating the curse at the source instead of at decode time?
