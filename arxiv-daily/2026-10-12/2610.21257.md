> **一句话总结：** 本文提出 FPoT——用 base-√2（半八度）分数幂次网格做 4-bit 权重量化、激活与 KV-cache 保持均匀 INT 的免乘法器方案：高斯代理分析证明 k=2 是 4-bit 下近最优的整数分辨率（最坏相对舍入误差从 PoT 的 41.4% 降至 18.9%），配合"近似网格量化"把 1 加法器 √2≈1.5 近似（6.1% 误差）吸收进 GPTQ 校准，使处理单元门数约为 INT4 乘法器的 0.50×、每权重 MAC 能耗约 0.42–0.46×；在 Llama-3-8B W4A4KV4 上 WikiText-2 困惑度开销从 PoT 的 +1.43 降到 +0.24（降 83%），9 项零样本平均仅落后均匀 INT 基线 0.4%p（相对 PoT 提升 8.72%），且 7 个模型 × 3 种位宽共 21 个配置中 20 个的零样本差距都在 1.0%p 以内。

> **TL;DR:** FPoT makes 4-bit multiplier-free LLM inference actually accurate: weights live on a base-√2 "half-octave" power-of-two grid (k=2, worst-case relative rounding error 18.9% vs 41.4% for standard PoT) while activations/KV-cache stay uniform INT, so every MAC becomes shift+add with a 1-adder √2≈1.5 approximation whose 6.1% error is absorbed by quantizing to the real hardware grid during GPTQ calibration (WikiText-2 PPL 7.574 vs 7.560 for the exact-√2 grid, versus 7.729 for naive post-hoc substitution). On Llama-3-8B W4A4KV4 this cuts the PoT perplexity overhead from +1.43 to +0.24 (83% reduction) against the uniform INT4 baseline (PPL 7.36), with 0-shot accuracy over nine benchmarks at 64.8% vs 65.2% (−0.4%p; +8.72% relative over PoT's 59.6%), and the result holds across 7 models (Llama-2/3, Qwen2.5-1.5B/7B/14B, Mistral-7B) × 3 bit-widths (20/21 configs within 1.0%p). The cost model puts the FPoT processing element at ~0.50× the gate count and 0.42–0.46× the energy of an INT4 multiplier (2.2–2.4× per-weight-MAC saving) — though these are analytical/Yosys numbers anchored to a third-party RTL study, with no end-to-end silicon validation.

## 核心贡献

- **√2 基网格的理论论证：** 对 PTQ 预处理（旋转+缩放）后的权重做高斯代理分析，证明对数幅度服从标准差 ≈1.02 个八度高斯、95% 质量集中在 ~4 个八度内；在 N=15 电平下数值最优基底 β*≈2^0.55（等效 k*≈1.8），故整数 k=2 近最优——MSE 仅为均匀 INT4 的 1.27×（PoT 为 3.21×，k=3 为 2.87×，k=4 为 5.37×），最坏情况相对误差从 41.4% 减半到 18.9%。
- **近似网格量化（Approximate-Grid Quantization）：** 奇指数路径需要 ×√2，用 1 加法器实现 x+x≫1=1.5x（6.1% 误差）；关键技巧是让校准直接量化到"真实硬件网格"（奇数指数用 1.5 而非 √2），使 GPTQ 把该近似当作网格的一部分吸收——Llama-3-8B 上 PPL 仅 +0.01（7.574 vs 精确 √2 的 7.560），而朴素事后替换劣化 +0.17。
- **完整整数移位-加法数据通路：** 与保留 FP16 激活的已有 LLM 对数量化工作（PoT-PTQ、LogART）不同，FPoT 权重网格 × 均匀 INT 激活 = 全整数 PE；零码字直接跳过 MAC（k=2 的自适应零阈值 τ≈0.841 隐式剪掉 ~20–22% 权重）。
- **新指标正规化箱熵（NBE）：** H(p)/log₂N 度量固定位预算的信息利用率，FPoT 在高斯代理下 NBE=0.9364，高于均匀 INT4（0.9026）与 PoT（0.8773）；实测 7 个模型的箱占用与理论预测吻合。
- **近无损、跨框架的实证规模：** 7 模型 × 3 位宽共 21 个配置（OSTQuant 预处理 + GPTQ），W4 点 ΔPPL 全部 < +0.3，相对 PoT 开销降低 67–94%；DuQuant 框架迁移下开销仅 +0.05–0.34 PPL；融合 Triton 内核省 82% 中间显存，使单张 A100-80GB 能处理 14B 模型。

## Key Contributions

- **Theoretically grounded base-√2 grid for 4-bit:** A Gaussian-proxy analysis of preprocessed LLM weights (log-magnitude Gaussian with σ≈1.02 octaves, ~95% mass within ~4 octaves) numerically locates the optimum base at β*≈2^0.55 (effective k*≈1.8), making integer k=2 near-optimal: MSE 1.27× uniform INT4 (vs 3.21× for PoT, 2.87× for k=3, 5.37× for k=4), halving worst-case relative error from 41.4% to 18.9%.
- **Approximate-grid quantization:** The odd-exponent path needs ×√2; FPoT implements it as a 1-adder x+x≫1=1.5x (6.1% error) and absorbs the error by quantizing directly onto the actual hardware grid (1.5 for odd e) during GPTQ calibration — PPL within +0.01 of the exact-√2 grid (7.574 vs 7.560 on Llama-3-8B), versus +0.17 degradation for naive post-hoc substitution.
- **Fully integer shift-add datapath:** Unlike prior LLM logarithmic quantization (PoT-PTQ, LogART) that keeps FP16 activations and FP accumulators, FPoT pairs the √2 weight grid with uniform INT activations/KV-cache, so every weight MAC is barrel-shift + conditional add; zero codes skip the MAC entirely (k-adaptive threshold τ₂≈0.841 implicitly prunes ~20–22% of weights).
- **Normalized Bin Entropy (NBE):** A new grid-design metric H(p)/log₂N measuring the information-carrying fraction of the bit budget; FPoT reaches NBE 0.9364 vs 0.9026 (uniform INT4) and 0.8773 (PoT) under the Gaussian proxy, with empirical occupancy matching theory across all seven models.
- **Scale and portability of evidence:** 21 configurations (7 models × 3 bit-widths) with 3-seed mean±std in the main table; W4 ΔPPL strictly below +0.3; PoT-overhead reductions of 67–94%; cross-framework portability to DuQuant (+0.05–0.34 PPL overhead); a fused Triton kernel (82% intermediate-memory reduction) enabling 14B models on one A100-80GB.

## Methodology

FPoT quantizes all linear-projection weights (W_Q, W_K, W_V, W_O, gate/up/down — 6.98 B MACs per token on Llama-3-8B, 80.8% in the FFN) while activations and KV-cache stay uniform INT. The pipeline has four parts:

1. **Preprocessing:** State-of-the-art LLM PTQ transforms (primarily OSTQuant: learned Hadamard rotation + per-channel smooth scales via 400 steps of Riemannian gradient descent with STE) redistribute channel-concentrated outliers into smoother, near-Gaussian weight distributions suited to a logarithmic grid. The transform costs ≈0.02% of weight MACs.
2. **Grid:** Q_FPoT(w) = sgn(w)·s·2^(⌊k·log₂(|w|/s)⌉/k) with resolution k; k=2 gives half-octave spacing 2^0.5≈1.414. Exponent bits split by wire into ⌊e/2⌋ (shift amount) and e mod 2 (odd flag). A k-adaptive zero threshold τ_k=2^(−1/(2k)) maps |w|/s<τ to a zero code that gates the MAC off.
3. **Approximate-grid quantization:** The hardware's √2 for odd exponents is the 1-adder 1.5 approximation; instead of quantizing to exact √2 levels and substituting later, calibration (GPTQ with PoT targets) quantizes to the 1.5-based grid so the approximation is absorbed. A calibration-time grid-alignment search (G=16 phase-shifted per-channel scales, chosen by MSE) is folded into the static scale at zero inference cost.
4. **Hardware analysis:** A naive component cost model (INT multiplier O(W·A) vs shifter+adder O(W+A)) gives FPoT CU = 0.50× INT4 at W4A4 (PoT 0.38×); energy is anchored to Samsung 5 nm RTL synthesis (Przewlocka-Rus et al., 2022), giving 0.42–0.46× per weight MAC at W4A4; Yosys+ABC synthesis confirms the structural ordering INT > FPoT > PoT (cosine similarity 0.988 between analytical and measured cost vectors).

Evaluation: WikiText-2 perplexity and the unweighted mean of nine zero-shot benchmarks (0-shot9: ARC-C/E, HellaSwag, WinoGrande, PIQA, BoolQ, OpenBookQA, LAMBADA, SocialIQa) via lm-eval-harness, on Llama-3-8B, Llama-2-7B/13B, Qwen2.5-1.5B/7B/14B, Mistral-7B under W4A4KV4 / W4A8KV8 / W8A8KV8.

## Results & Highlights

- **Llama-3-8B W4A4KV4 (headline):** FPoT reaches WikiText-2 PPL 7.60±.02 vs 7.36±.07 for the uniform INT4 baseline (+0.24) and 8.79±.04 for PoT (+1.43) — an 83% reduction of the PoT perplexity overhead; 0-shot9 is 64.8±.6% vs 65.2±.4% (uniform, −0.4%p) and 59.6±.6% (PoT, −5.6%p), i.e. an 8.72% relative zero-shot gain over PoT.
- **Consistency at the designed W4 operating point:** across all 14 W4 configurations (7 models × W4A4KV4/W4A8KV8), ΔPPL vs uniform INT ranges from +0.05 (Qwen2.5-14B W4A4KV4: 6.38 vs 6.33) to +0.26 (Qwen2.5-1.5B W4A8KV8: 10.01 vs 9.75); PoT overhead is reduced 71–94% (67–94% over all 21 configurations incl. W8A8KV8). 20/21 configurations land within 1.0%p of the uniform baseline on 0-shot9 (mean gap −0.17%p; all 14 W4 configs within 0.5%p).
- **√2 approximation strategy (Llama-3-8B W4A4KV4):** with the 1-adder 1.5 approximation, approximate-grid quantization achieves PPL 7.574 — within +0.01 of exact-√2 (7.560) — while naive post-hoc substitution degrades to 7.729 and aware-training sits between at 7.625; on cost, the 1-adder PE is 0.50× the INT4 multiplier CU vs 0.75× (2-adder) and 1.00× (3-adder) for at most 0.005 PPL improvement, making 1-adder the Pareto knee.
- **Hardware:** FPoT compute unit ≈0.50× the INT4 multiplier cost (analytical; Yosys measures 0.57–0.59×) and 0.42–0.46× the per-weight-MAC energy at W4A4 (2.2–2.4× reduction), with the √2 unit contributing at most 0.04× overhead at its ~50% duty cycle; the advantage widens at higher bit-widths (quadratic vs linear scaling).
- **Grid theory validated:** under the Gaussian proxy, FPoT k=2 attains MSE 1.27× INT4 (vs PoT 3.21×) and NBE 0.9364 (vs 0.9026 INT4 / 0.8773 PoT); the optimum k*≈1.8 is 4-bit-specific — at 8-bit it shifts to k*≈19.
- **Ablations (Llama-3-8B W4A4KV4):** without PTQ preprocessing FPoT collapses to PPL 181.3, and only learned rotation+scale recovers 7.56 (within +0.12 of the non-PoT baseline 7.444); applying the log grid to activations too is catastrophic (PPL 8.21–52.49 at k=2), justifying INT activations; calibration is robust (|ΔPPL|≤0.065 over 128–512 samples; +0.06–0.10 when switching WikiText-2→C4).
- **vs LogART (concurrent ICLR'26 work):** weight-only W4A16, LogART is better (PPL 6.516 vs FPoT 6.862 on Llama-3-8B), but under post-hoc INT4 activations LogART collapses (PPL 3903 on Llama-3-8B, ∞/NaN on Llama-2-7B, 0-shot9 ≈34%) while FPoT stays stable (PPL 7.60, 64.8%).
- **Cross-framework portability:** swapping the weight grid into DuQuant adds only +0.05–0.34 PPL over uniform INT (vs +0.08–0.42 with OSTQuant), with ≤1.1%p 0-shot9 drop; Qwen2.5/Mistral models actually match or exceed the uniform baseline at W4A4KV4 (+0.7–1.2%p).

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** The core idea — a half-octave (base-√2) grid — is a small but well-motivated delta on APoT-era additive-PoT work; the genuinely novel pieces are (i) the observation that k=2 is optimal specifically at 4-bit because preprocessed weights concentrate within ~4 octaves (this also honestly bounds the method: the optimum shifts to k*≈19 at 8-bit), and (ii) approximate-grid quantization, a neat trick that turns a hardware approximation into part of the calibration target (+0.01 vs +0.17 PPL). Positioning against LogART is fair in weight-only mode (FPoT loses there: 6.862 vs 6.516 PPL) and wins only in the W4A4KV4 setting LogART was not built for — the paper says so, though the "LogART collapses" table uses post-hoc INT4 activations without preprocessing, which overstates the practical contrast.
- **Rigor:** The accuracy evidence is solid (21 configs, seeds, calibration robustness, per-projection ablations showing sub-additive degradation). The hardware evidence is the weak flank: the headline 0.50× CU comes from a deliberately naive gate-count model whose own appendix admits the MUX-cost assumption is off — Yosys actually measures 0.57–0.59× at W4, and the full-MAC ratio including the shared accumulator is only 0.67×. Energy numbers are extrapolated from a 2022 CNN-oriented Samsung 5 nm RTL study at W4A8, not from synthesizing the FPoT PE itself; the 2.2–2.4× "energy reduction" is per weight MAC only, and the paper concedes end-to-end gains shrink in memory-bound decode — no wall-clock latency, throughput, or silicon measurement exists.
- **Reproducibility:** Good on the algorithm side (pseudocode, all hyperparameters, Triton kernel described, LogART reproduced from official code); no code release is mentioned for FPoT itself, and the OSTQuant+GPTQ stack means reproduction requires re-plumbing two external frameworks.
- **Significance:** If the hardware story holds, multiplier-free W4A4KV4 at INT-baseline accuracy is a real systems contribution; as it stands, the paper is primarily a quantization-algorithm result with a projected hardware payoff.
- **Red flags:** Benchmarks are zero-shot classification only (nine tasks) plus WikiText-2 PPL — no generation quality, long-context, or chat evals; Qwen2.5-1.5B's LAMBADA collapses to ≈0% for *all* quantized methods including the uniform baseline (FP: 61.9%), an evaluation fragility the paper notes in passing; the grid-alignment component (G=16) is retained in the default config despite contributing ≤0.034 PPL at k=2 (it mostly helps the k=1 ablation); models stop at 14B due to the fp32-copy memory ceiling.

## Strengths

- Clean problem decomposition: isolates the log grid to static weights (where calibration can absorb error) and keeps dynamic activations/KV-cache on hardware-friendly uniform INT — this is what enables a fully integer PE where PoT-PTQ/LogART still need FP16 accumulators.
- Approximate-grid quantization is an elegant, general lesson: hardware approximations should live inside the quantization grid seen by calibration, not be patched on afterwards.
- Theory is simple but predictive: Gaussian-proxy MSE/NBE optima (k*≈1.8) match empirical bin occupancy across all seven models, and the 4-bit specificity of k=2 is derived, not hidden.
- Unusually thorough ablations for a PTQ paper: preprocessing components, full-vs-weight-only PoT, zero-threshold sweep (ΔPPL≤0.033 across the whole range), rounding mode, calibration size/domain, per-projection sensitivity, cross-framework portability, and a fused-Triton memory engineering note.
- Intellectual honesty: explicit near-lossless criterion, std over seeds, acknowledged scope limits of the hardware claims, and a candid head-to-head loss against LogART in the weight-only regime.

## Limitations

- No end-to-end hardware validation: all energy/area claims are analytical or anchored to a third-party RTL study of CNN MACs; the claimed 2.2–2.4× per-MAC energy saving does not translate to measured system-level speedup, especially in memory-bound decode where arithmetic is a small energy fraction.
- The accuracy benefit is a gap-to-baseline story, not a win: FPoT still costs +0.05–0.42 PPL over uniform INT4 everywhere (Llama family −0.1 to −0.8%p 0-shot9), and it only matches uniform on Qwen2.5/Mistral.
- k=2 optimality is bit-width-specific (8-bit wants k*≈19); the method is tailored to the 4-bit operating point.
- Scale ceiling: fp32 weight copies during preprocessing cap experiments at 14B on one 80 GB GPU; AWQ and plain-RTN compatibility untested; all results couple FPoT to GPTQ-style calibration.
- Evaluation breadth: zero-shot classification + WikiText-2 only; no instruction-following, generation quality, or long-context results; the Qwen2.5-1.5B LAMBADA collapse suggests small-model fragility under any 4-bit scheme.

## Relevance

对"极限性能优化下的可靠性"方向而言，这是一篇典型的量化精度—效率权衡工作：它系统刻画了幂次网格这种激进数值格式在 4-bit 下的误差来源（网格失配、√2 近似的 6.1% 系数误差）并展示校准如何吸收这些误差，直接关系到低精度/非均匀数值格式下推理结果的可信度边界。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How much of FPoT's near-lossless behavior survives at 3-bit weights, where the grid-resolution argument (k* shifts with N) changes qualitatively — is there a fractional-PoT analogue of the W3 regime?
- Can the approximate-grid trick be generalized to other hardware approximations (e.g., truncated adders, approximate multipliers in attention), turning them into calibration-absorbed grid distortions rather than post-hoc noise?
- What is the end-to-end latency/energy on a real shift-add accelerator (FPGA or ASIC) — does the 0.50× CU translate through the memory-bound decode regime, and does zero-gating's ~20% skipped MACs compound the speedup?
- Why does FPoT beat the uniform INT baseline on Qwen2.5/Mistral but trail it on Llama — is the implicit magnitude pruning acting as a beneficial regularizer for certain weight distributions, and does that connect to the observed WinoGrande/LAMBADA gains?
- Does the half-octave grid interact safely with fault-induced bit-flips in stored exponent codes (a single LSB flip changes the weight by a factor of √2 ≈ 41%), and could rotation-based preprocessing make shift-add datapaths more or less sensitive to silent data corruption than INT4 multipliers?
