Why This Matters

Splits any frozen model's representations into content vs phrasing with a 128×128 eigenproblem — plus a 30-pair, one-pass screen for steering fragility.

谐波质量提供了一个廉价、无需训练的预筛信号，可在 steering/编辑部署前判断一个模型的表征路径是否自洽。

⚠ Critical Edge

On acyclic single-layer graphs the construction provably reduces to projected within-pair covariance — the sheaf's distinctive power lives only in cycle-bearing constructions, where the practical payoff was partly null.

  • Fragility–harmonic-mass correlation rests on n = 6 architectures.

01

Methodology

方法
frozen model→ activations @ ℓ≈2L/3→ paraphrase graph (MRPC)→ sheaf Laplacian 128×128→ H₀ content / H₁ phrasing
  • Entirely post-hoc. Mean-pooled residual-stream states read from a frozen model; MRPC paraphrase pairs are edges — each pair a local witness that two activations should agree.
  • Restriction map. A linear Pᵀ projects each node's d-D state into a shared 128-D edge stalk; the empirical Laplacian L_F = (1/N)ΣΔᵢΔᵢᵀ is k×k (k=128) regardless of model width. The only knob is the choice of P (PCA / CCA / contrastive Fisher).
  • Spectral split. Bottom m=20 eigenvectors = H₀ (phrasing-invariant content); top 20 = "H₁ directions" (phrasing variation).
  • Proposition 3.1. On matching graphs with shared P, L_F = PᵀC_W P — a reduction to projected within-pair difference covariance (which is why random/identity-P ablations matter).
  • Depth-induced cycles. (sentence, layer) grids joined by Procrustes transports carry b₁ = N(L−1) cycles; the Hodge harmonic mass of the model's edge cochain is the fragility signal — 30 pairs, one forward pass per sentence.
  • Statistics. Bootstrap 95% CIs, McNemar + Benjamini–Hochberg, Cohen's d, exact permutation tests.

Strengths

优势
  • One construction, three payoffs — the same paraphrase graph yields a content subspace, an influence subspace, and a fragility diagnostic, all empirically validated.
  • Training-free and cheap — a 128×128 eigenproblem regardless of model width; works identically from GPT-2 (124M) to Llama-2-13B.
  • Exceptional hygiene — held-out splits, variability- and edge-count-matched controls, dimension-matched baselines, CIs and effect sizes everywhere.
  • Honest negatives — holonomy null (p = 0.10–0.33), node-space H₁ non-transfer, and neutral MLST terms are reported, not hidden.
  • Theoretical self-awareness — Prop 3.1 and Remarks 3.2/3.3 delimit exactly where the sheaf adds structure beyond covariance analysis.
04

Limitations

局限
  • Reduction on acyclic graphs — provably equals projected within-pair covariance; the sheaf's edge lives only in cycle-bearing grids/hypergraphs, where payoff was partly null.
  • Steering gain is regime-limited — reliable only at α ∈ [0.10, 0.15] with +1–2 pp margins on robust models; at α = 0.3 random matches directed methods.
  • n = 6 fragility correlation — cannot yet serve as a general predictor; harmonic mass needs k and grid depth L (stability checked only for k ∈ {8, 64, 128}).
  • P is architecture- and domain-dependent — cross-corpus transfer costs ~25 pp; "same meaning" is operationalized narrowly as MRPC-style news paraphrase.
  • Narrow evaluation surface — CounterFact entity recall with greedy 50-token decoding only; 7B+ activations extracted under 4-bit quantization.
05

Questions for Further Exploration

待探索
Does the fragility–harmonic-mass correlation hold beyond six architectures — MoE models, chat vs base checkpoints, within-family scale differences?
Can the H₀ content subspace replace full hidden states in ROME/MEMIT editing or retrieval-augmented verification?
Why do GPT-2 (124M) and Mistral-7B give near-identical ablation ratios (18.2× vs 19.7×) despite a 56× size gap?
Could drift of the H₀/H₁ split under quantization, pruning, or fine-tuning signal representation damage in deployed models?
What richer topologies (attention-head graphs, token-position grids) would make genuine cohomological structure consequential?
Can the construction extend to multimodal or cross-lingual equivalence classes with per-node maps of different widths?
