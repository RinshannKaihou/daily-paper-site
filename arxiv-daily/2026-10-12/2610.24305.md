> **一句话总结：** 本文首次证明 Flat Minima 假设同样适用于 Muon 优化器(CIFAR-10 上 adaptive sharpness 与泛化差距的 Pearson r = 0.45–0.69,p ≤ 1e-4),并形式化推导出 Muon 的隐式块对角预条件子,实验显示 Muon 的有效锐度远超经典 Adaptive Edge of Stability 阈值 (2+2µ)/η 却依然稳定收敛——表明它运行在对角型自适应方法(Adam/RMSprop)截然不同的稳定域。

> **TL;DR:** An ETH Zurich team studies the Muon optimizer through the lens of sharpness and the Edge of Stability (EoS). On CIFAR-10 (Airbench VGG-like CNN, 64 runs per optimizer with Bayesian HPO), adaptive sharpness correlates with generalization gap within each optimizer (Pearson r = 0.45–0.69, p ≤ 1e-4), extending the Flat Minima Hypothesis to Muon; and under the LDS schedule Normalized Muon reaches 94% validation accuracy vs 92% for both Coupled SGD and Coupled Adam (+2 pts). The core finding: in full-batch MLP experiments, Vanilla Muon's effective sharpness λmax(PtHt) sits far ABOVE the (2+2µ)/η stability threshold that bounds Adam (which oscillates around (2+2β1)/η) and RMSprop (slightly above 2/η), yet Muon does not diverge — suggesting sharpness-based stability monitoring misjudges non-diagonally preconditioned optimizers, and that Muon's true stability boundary remains an open question.

## 核心贡献

- **首次实证 Flat Minima 假设对 Muon 成立**:在 CIFAR-10 上,每个优化器内部 sharpness 与泛化差距(generalization gap)强相关——adaptive sharpness 的 Pearson r = 0.45–0.69(全部 p ≤ 1e-4),raw sharpness 为 r = 0.27–0.49;Muon 与 Adam、SGD 表现出同等的假设符合度。
- **形式化推导了 Muon 的隐式预条件子**(附录 A,含 5 条命题及证明):Newton-Schulz 正交化后的更新可写为 UΣ′Vᵀ,逐层预条件子为 Dₜ = U S⁻¹ Uᵀ(S = ΣΣ′⁻¹),向量化后为 I ⊗ Dₜ,整个网络构成**块对角**预条件子 Pₜ——这是分析其 Edge of Stability 行为的基础工具。
- **发现 Muon 运行在经典 Adaptive EoS 阈值之外**:全批量 MLP 实验中,Vanilla Muon 的有效锐度 λmax(PₜHₜ) 远超按 (2+2µ)/η 计算的振荡阈值却不发散,而 Vanilla Adam 恰好在 (2+2β1)/η 附近振荡、RMSprop 略高于 2/η(复现了 Cohen et al. 2024 的已知结果)。作者假设原因在于 Muon 更新方向与 PₜHₜ 特征基的**错位**(misalignment),但未做验证。
- **新现象:收敛后 raw sharpness 骤降**——Muon 在收敛后 raw sharpness 快速下降,而 Adam/RMSprop 收敛后趋于平台,进一步佐证 Muon 处于不同动力学区域。
- **对 ASAM 度量的二次发现**:即使使用 adaptive sharpness(Kwon et al. 2021),不同优化器仍各自聚簇,说明该度量并非完全重参数化不变。

## Key Contributions

- **First empirical validation of the Flat Minima Hypothesis (FMH) for Muon**: on CIFAR-10, sharpness correlates with generalization gap within each optimizer — Pearson r = 0.45–0.69 for adaptive sharpness (all p ≤ 1e-4), r = 0.27–0.49 for raw sharpness — showing Muon behaves like Adam and SGD in this respect.
- **Formal derivation of Muon's implicit preconditioner** (Appendix A, five propositions with proofs): the Newton-Schulz update equals UΣ′Vᵀ where Mₜ = UΣVᵀ is the momentum SVD; the per-layer preconditioner is Dₜ = U S⁻¹ Uᵀ with S = ΣΣ′⁻¹; vectorizing gives I ⊗ Dₜ per layer and a **block-diagonal** Pₜ for the whole network, with matrix powers distributing over blocks (needed for computing Pₜ^{1/2}HₜPₜ^{1/2}).
- **Muon operates beyond the classical Adaptive Edge of Stability**: in full-batch experiments, Vanilla Muon's effective sharpness λmax(PₜHₜ) stays far above the (2+2µ)/η threshold without diverging, whereas Vanilla Adam oscillates around (2+2β1)/η and RMSprop sits slightly above 2/η — exactly replicating Cohen et al. (2024) for the diagonal methods as a sanity check. The authors hypothesize (but do not test) that Muon's updates are poorly aligned with the eigenbasis of PₜHₜ.
- **A new trajectory phenomenon**: after convergence, Muon's raw sharpness drops rapidly — absent in Adam/RMSprop, where it plateaus — further evidence of a distinct regime.
- **A caveat on ASAM**: optimizer-specific clusters persist in the adaptive-sharpness plot, so adaptive sharpness is not a fully parametrization-invariant flatness measure even though it removes scale bias.

## Methodology

The paper runs two complementary experimental tracks:

1. **Generalization benchmark (FMH track).** A modified Airbench (Jordan, 2024) — a VGG-like CNN tuned to 94% CIFAR-10 accuracy in seconds — extended with (i) a per-epoch sharpness-measurement callback, (ii) support for a fixed learning rate alongside the original Linear Decay Scheduler (LDS), and (iii) implementations of Decoupled Muon, Coupled Adam, and Coupled SGD besides the original Normalized Muon. Hyperparameters for each optimizer are selected by Bayesian optimization with a Gaussian-process surrogate (128–512 trials maximizing validation accuracy); results aggregate 64 runs per optimizer-scheduler pair over 16 epochs. Three sharpness measures are computed per checkpoint: raw λmax(Hₜ), adaptive sharpness (ASAM perturbation-set formulation of Kwon et al., 2021), and effective sharpness λmax(PₜHₜ) (equal to the eigenvalues of Pₜ^{1/2}HₜPₜ^{1/2}).
2. **Edge-of-Stability track.** Following Cohen et al. (2024): full-batch training of a tanh MLP (width 200, 5 hidden layers) for 1000 epochs on a CIFAR-10 subset (200 samples/class, 0.8/0.2 train-val split) with MSE loss, fixed learning rates, and no weight decay. Vanilla Muon is compared to Vanilla Adam and RMSprop. Muon's preconditioner is reconstructed each epoch from stored consecutive weights via UΣ′Vᵀ = (Wₜ − Wₜ₊₁)/η and Dₜ = UΣ⁻¹Σ′Uᵀ; the stability threshold is the classical (2+2µ)/η (Goh, 2017). Normalized Muon is excluded from this track "for simplicity" since its updates live on a unit Frobenius ball.

Sharpness is computed by a PyHessian-inspired power iteration supporting a preconditioner argument (Hessian-vector products via the Pearlmutter trick; tolerance τ = 1e-9, max 200 iterations), so neither Hₜ nor Pₜ is ever materialized.

## Results & Highlights

- **Muon wins on accuracy**: under the LDS schedule on CIFAR-10, Normalized Muon reaches **0.94 ± 0.002 validation accuracy vs 0.92 ± 0.002 for both Coupled SGD and Coupled Adam (+2 pts)**; under a fixed LR the ordering holds with a larger SGD collapse (Normalized Muon 0.90 ± 0.007 vs Adam 0.87 ± 0.012 vs SGD 0.80 ± 0.057).
- **FMH holds within optimizers (fixed LR)**: adaptive-sharpness vs generalization-gap Pearson correlations are Normalized Muon r = 0.45 (p = 0.0002), Decoupled Muon r = 0.51, Coupled SGD r = 0.69, Coupled Adam r = 0.66 (all p ≤ 1e-4) — up from raw-sharpness correlations of 0.27–0.49; however the trends do **not** transfer across optimizers (weak cross-optimizer predictiveness).
- **Sharpness regime flips with the scheduler**: under fixed LR, Muon's adaptive sharpness is far *lower* than baselines (ASAM 0.82 ± 0.229 vs SGD 2.06 ± 0.911 and Adam 1.93 ± 0.626, i.e., ~2.4× lower); under LDS the trend reverses — Muon is *sharper* (0.71 ± 0.097) than SGD (0.32 ± 0.072) and Adam (0.56 ± 0.074) — despite a generalization gap that stays within 0.04–0.08 for all optimizers.
- **Core EoS finding**: in the full-batch MLP setting, Vanilla Muon's effective sharpness λmax(PₜHₜ) rises far **above** the (2+2µ)/η threshold yet training loss remains stable (Figure 2), while Vanilla Adam oscillates around (2+2β1)/η and RMSprop slightly above 2/η (Figure 4) — replicating Cohen et al. (2024) for the diagonal baselines and breaking the assumption for Muon.
- **Post-convergence sharpness collapse**: Muon's raw sharpness falls rapidly after convergence, unlike Adam/RMSprop which plateau — a phenomenon the diagonal baselines do not show.
- **Fragility of FMH under realistic schedules**: under LDS, sharpness-gap correlations mostly vanish (e.g., Coupled SGD r = 0.04, p = 0.76; only Decoupled Muon adaptive r = 0.34, p = 0.0057 remains significant) — the positive FMH results are a fixed-LR phenomenon in this setup (Appendix B).

## Authors & Credibility

The four authors (Niko Čovic, Baraq Lipshitz, Alexander Padula, Stefan Višnjić) are from the Department of Informatics at ETH Zurich, with equal contribution and alphabetical ordering — a pattern typical of a course/research project rather than a lab's main research line (the public code repo is named "DL-project", and this is a 9-page preprint not yet peer-reviewed). Credibility is bolstered by methodological hygiene: extensive Bayesian hyperparameter sweeps (128–512 GP-surrogate trials), 64 aggregated runs per optimizer-scheduler cell with standard deviations, exact reproduction of the known Adam/RMSprop AEoS behavior as a sanity check before making the Muon claim, and a formal, proof-backed preconditioner derivation. Credibility is weakened by the small scale (CIFAR-10 only) and the absence of any mechanistic verification for the central hypothesis.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: genuine on two counts — nobody had examined Muon through the Edge-of-Stability lens, and the explicit block-diagonal preconditioner derivation (Props A.1–A.5) is a reusable tool. But the headline result is an *observation* (sharpness above threshold, no divergence), not an explanation; the paper itself concedes "we do not explore this and leave this as an open question."
- **Rigor**: strong on the experimental side (HPO discipline, seeds, sanity-check replication); weak on causal evidence. The eigenbasis-misalignment hypothesis is never tested — no measurement of update/eigenvector alignment, no synthetic quadratic or controlled ablation isolating block-diagonality as the cause.
- **A buried fragility**: the Flat Minima headline (r = 0.45–0.69) comes entirely from the fixed-LR setting; under the realistic LDS schedule the correlations mostly disappear (SGD r = 0.04, p = 0.76), yet the abstract presents the FMH confirmation unconditionally while relegating this to Appendix B/Figure 3.
- **Missing pieces**: the (2+2µ)/η threshold is transplanted from diagonal-preconditioner analysis to a block-diagonal one without justification — it is unclear the formula even applies to Muon, which is precisely the open question; and Normalized Muon, the variant actually used in the highly-optimized Airbench and in practice, is excluded from the EoS analysis "for simplicity," so the central claim covers only Vanilla Muon.
- **Data quirks**: the EoS track uses MSE loss on a classification task (chosen because it exhibits AEoS "more clearly") and a 2,000-sample CIFAR-10 subset — both deviations from standard practice that make the phenomenon easier to see but weaken external validity; no LLM-scale evidence despite Muon's main adoption being LLM pretraining (cf. the Moonlight result they cite).
- **Reproducibility**: good — code is public, hyperparameter search procedures and the sharpness power-iteration algorithm (tolerance 1e-9, 200 max iterations, Pearlmutter trick) are fully specified.
- **Significance**: if the beyond-EoS observation holds at scale, it implies sharpness-based stability monitors misjudge Muon runs — practically relevant for divergence detection in large training runs — but at CIFAR-10 scale this is a hypothesis, not a demonstrated fact.

## Strengths

- First mover on Muon × Edge of Stability, with a clean, proof-backed derivation of the implicit block-diagonal preconditioner that others can build on.
- Methodologically careful experimentation: Bayesian HPO with 128–512 trials, 64 runs per cell, mean ± std reported for every accuracy and sharpness number.
- Reproduces the known Adam/RMSprop AEoS phenomenology first, validating the measurement pipeline before claiming the Muon anomaly.
- Reports honestly what does not work: cross-optimizer sharpness predictiveness is weak, LDS kills the FMH correlation, and ASAM's optimizer-specific clustering shows adaptive sharpness is not fully reparametrization-invariant.
- Two genuinely novel empirical observations: beyond-threshold stability, and the post-convergence collapse of Muon's raw sharpness.

## Limitations

- Scale: CIFAR-10 with a VGG-like CNN and a small tanh MLP on a 2,000-sample subset; no ImageNet, no transformer LM, nothing at the scale where Muon actually matters today.
- The mechanism (eigenbasis misalignment of updates vs PₜHₜ) is hypothesized but never measured or ablated; the true stability boundary for block-diagonal preconditioners is left open by the authors' own admission.
- Evidence is correlational and within-optimizer only; raw sharpness varies substantially between optimizers without matching gap changes.
- The FMH confirmation is scheduler-dependent (fixed LR yes, LDS mostly no), and the practical Normalized Muon variant is excluded from the central EoS analysis.
- MSE-on-classification and no-weight-dead restrictions in the EoS track limit how far the phenomena transfer to standard recipes.

## Relevance

该论文直接服务于"LLM 训练稳定性与可观测性"方向:它指出经典的基于 sharpness 的 Adaptive Edge of Stability 阈值对 Muon 这类非对角预条件优化器会给出错误的失稳预警(Muon 远超阈值却稳定收敛),提示在大规模预训练运行中监控优化器稳定性、检测发散时,不能照搬为 Adam/RMSprop 建立的锐度指标。对理解 Muon 在 LLM 训练中的动力学行为及其稳定性边界是初步但有价值的证据。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the beyond-EoS behavior persist at LLM-pretraining scale (e.g., the 7B+ settings where Moonlight/Kimi adopted Muon), or is it an artifact of small MLPs with MSE loss?
- Can the eigenbasis-misalignment hypothesis be tested directly, e.g., by tracking the cosine alignment between Muon updates and the top eigenvectors of PₜHₜ throughout training?
- What is the correct stability threshold for block-diagonal preconditioners — does a Muon-specific Edge of Stability exist, and can it be derived rather than observed?
- Why does Muon's raw sharpness collapse after convergence while Adam's and RMSprop's plateau, and does this indicate implicit regularization of the loss landscape?
- Does the Flat Minima correlation reappear under warmup-plus-cosine or other standard schedules, or is the fixed-LR setting fundamentally special?
- How do weight decay and the Frobenius normalization (the excluded Normalized Muon variant) change the effective-Hessian geometry and the AEoS picture?
