Self-Evolving Agents Analysis ★ Must Read — Lead Story

Agents Learn to Repair Their Failures — But Not to Generalize From Them

Paper: “Self-Evolving Agents Can Repair Failures but Struggle to Generalize from Them”

By Weixiang Zhao, Yichen Zhang, Yanyan Zhao, et al.

罕见的受控负结果：3 种自进化框架 × 10 个骨干 × 4 个可验证环境，用"人工核验经验"对照组干净区分"学到错误教训"与"正确教训仍无法迁移"——修复有效、泛化系统性缺失，泛化收益来自成功轨迹而非更丰富的失败诊断。

A rare controlled negative result: 3 evolution frameworks × 10 backbones × 4 verified environments, with a hand-verified-experience control cleanly separating “wrong lessons” from “right lessons that still don’t transfer” — repair works, generalization systematically fails, and the gains come from success trajectories, not richer failure autopsies.

25.93% → 7.41% held-out WebArena-Reddit success after failure-only evolution — single rollout per checkpoint, no confidence intervals.

※ Caveat留出评测为小集合单次 rollout（仅 27 任务）且无置信区间，头条跌幅方向警示但噪声较大。 / Single rollout on small held-out sets (27 WebArena Reddit tasks), no confidence intervals — directionally alarming but noisy.

Training Stability System ★ Must Read

ATTUNE: An Agent Tunes the Training Fleet — Behind a Correctness Gate It Cannot Cheat

Paper: “ATTUNE: Agentic Throughput Tuning for LLM Training”

By Leo Fan, Hongyang Wang, Wenbo Chen, et al.

真实 64–128 GPU 战役 + 等预算对比；agent 无关的正确性门实测 0/133 误拒、16/24 故障拦截，并对 Mixtral 诚实报告 +0.0% 无效果。

Real 64–128 GPU campaigns with equal-budget baselines, plus an agent-independent correctness gate (0/133 false rejects, 16/24 faults caught) — and an honest +0.0% no-op on Mixtral.

+33.9% training throughput (GLM-4.5 on B200) vs equal-budget OptiCo/Bayesian/random — n=3 campaigns, p=0.05–0.125.

※ Caveat仅模型留出——技术栈、加速器与 agent 即开发所用；+33.9% 部分度量 H100→B200 移植摩擦。 / Only the models were held out; +33.9% partly measures H100→B200 porting friction.

RL Post-Training Method ★ Must Read

Symmetric Reward Aggregation Provably Collapses Into Shortcut Hacking

Paper: “Multi-Reward Hacking: From Aggregation-Induced Drift to Trust-Anchored Defense”

By Yushu Li, Jiin Woo, Xiaoxiao Li, et al.

定理 + 实验双确认：主奖励与更易获得的辅助奖励做对称聚合，会从任意初始化收敛到"只刷辅助奖励"的捷径；TAG（信任锚定门控）是无操作奖励设定下唯一存活的防御。

Theorem plus experiments across 5 models and 2 domains: symmetric aggregation of a main reward with easier auxiliary rewards converges to auxiliary-only shortcuts from any initialization; Trust-Anchored Gating is the only surviving defense.

pass@4 0.581 vs 0.220 (TAG vs additive) under a no-op auxiliary reward, with replies 44% shorter — synthetic binary auxiliary channels only.

※ Caveat所有辅助通道均为合成二值（未测学习型奖励模型或规则裁判）；"所有方法都崩溃"部分依赖简化移植。 / All auxiliary channels are synthetic and binary; the “every method collapses” headline rests partly on simplified ports.

Inference Reliability Method ★ Must Read

Deep-Learning Compilers Silently Corrupt Their Own Cache State

Paper: “Stateful Testing for Deep Learning Compilers” (Rondo)

By Jiatao Ge, Ruixiang Qian, Yibo Hu, et al.

MCTS 引导调用序列 + 三方差分预言机发现 117 个 bug（35 个有状态）；"保留 vs 重置编译器状态重放"干净隔离缓存状态损坏。

MCTS-guided invocation sequences and a three-way differential oracle surface 117 bugs (35 stateful); retain-vs-reset replay cleanly isolates compiler-state corruption.

99.26% alarm precision on 50,074 labeled cases; 39 upstream reports, 18 already fixed — but source-line coverage barely moves (14.17% → 14.94%).

※ Caveat覆盖率大增主要在论文自建行为指标上，且 35 个有状态 bug 中 22 个出自 PyTorch 一家。 / Coverage gains are on the paper’s own metrics; 22 of 35 stateful bugs are in PyTorch alone.

Agent Pipelines Analysis ★ Must Read

When Can an Agent Trust Compiled Memory? Only Under Exact Change Detection

Paper: “Many Steps into One: Reasoning Contraction and the Limits of Safe Memory Reuse”

By Jianghong Zhou, Hao Rong, Chen Lin, et al.

把"智能体何时能信任编译式记忆"变成精确的变化检测理论，并在重放的相同数据流上验证：0 次误认证，陈旧答案比 ExpeL/ACE 式计数器少 7–73 倍。

Turns “when can an agent trust compiled memory” into exact change-detection theory, validated on replayed identical streams — with 7–73× fewer stale answers than ExpeL/ACE-style counters.

0 false certifications across 5,779 at-risk epochs; 7–73× fewer stale answers vs counters — requires exact verifiers (KB execution, unit tests).

※ Caveat自建基准近全量过期、对检测器过于"友好"，ExpeL/ACE 基线为理想化移植。 / The bespoke near-total-staleness benchmark flatters detectors; ExpeL/ACE baselines are idealized transplants.

Extreme Compression Analysis ★ Must Read

A Two-Scalar Counterexample Topples QEP’s Universal Quantization Guarantee

Paper: “When Does Compensating Inherited Quantization Error Help?”

By Tianhao Guo, et al.

用 Lean 形式化的两标量反例决定性推翻 QEP 已发表的普适保证；预注册 8B 实验显示固定 α=1/2 恒胜 RTN，而"自适应选强度"规则过拟合。

A Lean-formalized two-scalar counterexample decisively refutes QEP’s published universal guarantee; pre-registered 8B experiments show fixed α=1/2 always beats RTN while adaptive strength-selection rules overfit.

12.643 → 12.320 WikiText-2 perplexity (Qwen3-8B, 4-bit, fixed α=½ vs RTN) — guarantee covers idealized linear networks only.

※ Caveat正向保证只覆盖无界网格减性抖动下的线性网络（无人实际部署），且所有定理固定校准输入。 / The positive guarantee covers an idealized scheme no deployed system uses; theorems fix the calibration input.

Interpretability Analysis ★ Must Read

1–5% of a Steering Vector’s Energy Actively Fights the Steer

Paper: “The Sharp Directions Are Against You: Curvature Analysis of Activation Steering”

By Seydarmin Azizi, Arya Fayyazi, Parsa Razmara, et al.

CAA 转向向量中仅占 1–5% 能量的高曲率"尖锐分量"是抗转向与行为反转的根源；一行式移除在全部 25 个生成条件下超越标准 CAA。

The 1–5%-energy sharp components of CAA vectors cause anti-steering and behavioral flips; a one-line removal beats standard CAA in all 25 open-ended generation conditions.

78% → 36% Qwen refusal under vanilla CAA (a safety regression); sharp removal restores 98% — from 50 greedy completions per condition.

※ Caveat只与原版（及符号翻转）CAA 对比，未与 conceptor、SAE 引导或优化式转向正面竞争。 / Compared only against vanilla (and sign-flipped) CAA — no head-to-head with conceptors, SAE-guided, or optimization-based steering.

Systems & Infrastructure System ★ Must Read

PivotRL: GPUs That Change Jobs Mid-Training — 1.8–2× Throughput, Same Convergence

Paper: “Efficient Resource-Shiftable RL Post-training with Generative Rewards”

By Yanfeng Zhao, Haoyang Li, Sheng Lin, et al.

本领域最强的实测系统证据：真实 128 卡 H20 集群、同一 veRL 底座上的三个调优基线、3 次重复带标准差、逐组件消融——RL 收敛等价而墙钟约 30h vs >50h。

The area’s strongest measured systems evidence — a real 128-GPU H20 cluster, three tuned baselines on the same veRL substrate, 3 repeats with std, per-component ablations: equal RL convergence in ~30 h vs >50 h.

~30 h vs >50 h wall-clock at equal RL convergence (128× H20, 3 repeats with std) — single math dataset; no code released.

※ Caveat全部结果使用单一数学数据集 + 混合可验证/生成奖励；吞吐目标只计 rollout+奖励 token；未放出代码。 / Single math dataset with hybrid reward; the throughput objective counts only rollout+reward tokens.
