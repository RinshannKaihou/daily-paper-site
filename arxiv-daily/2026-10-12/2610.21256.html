Why this matters为何重要

Separates “the model knows” from “the model says” — enabling instruction-robust internal readouts for hallucination detection, evaluation, and LLM-as-judge pipelines.

对真值方向探测、幻觉检测与鲁棒内部评测有直接方法学价值。

Critical edge批判视角

A supervised pooled baseline matches its held-out numbers — the win is label-freeness, not accuracy.

Methodology 方法

Three binary judgments — correctness (GSM8K · MMLU · ARC-C · TriviaQA), entailment (ANLI), safety (BeaverTails). Primary models: Qwen2.5-7B/14B, Llama-3.1-8B, Mistral-7B → 14 model–dataset cells (~800 Qs each); six more models (to Qwen2.5-32B, Gemma-2-27B) run prospectively after the method was frozen. The judged input stays fixed; only the instruction varies.

1 · Pair final-layer states of the same input under two instructions: hi(a) − hi(b)
▼
2 · Stack & SVD the differences → expression subspace E; rank by split-half stability (every principal angle |cos| ≥ 0.95) → median rank 8. No benchmark labels.
▼
3 · Fit once a class-mean Yes/No probe w (fitted under Yes/No only, then frozen).
▼
4 · Isolate — project expression out: wiso = (I − EET)w → judgment readable regardless of what “Yes” means.

Eval: 5 question-disjoint folds, frozen standardization, AUROC, 10,000-resample clustered bootstrap. Controls: verdict-free pair estimation, LEACE/INLP erasure, PCA & random-subspace removal, cross-wording and cross-instruction-family transfer. Code & protocol: github.com/jchaemin/isolated-judgment.

Prospective replication: five added models (7B–32B) stay within 0.02 of label-fitted readouts everywhere; in Qwen2.5-32B the frozen readout falls to 0.42 AUROC under negation while isolation holds. Honest note: the 0.504/0.480 collapses are the probe’s worst cases — on correctness the drop is milder (0.650 mean / 0.694 median, convention disclosed), and ~3 negation-pair directions alone recover the full rank-8 subspace to within 0.005.

Strengths 长处

  • Crisp reframing — judgment vs expression — of what prompt-format failures mean for evaluation and internal readouts.
  • Fully label-free subspace estimation, yet within 0.01–0.02 of supervised condition-fitted probes on never-seen conditions.
  • Exceptional control battery: verdict-free estimation, LEACE/INLP baselines, PCA & random-subspace controls, cross-wording transfer, prospective 6-model replication.
  • Mechanistic-flavored localization: judgment readable at 0.5 depth, disruption only late; 3 negation directions ≈ full subspace.
  • Transparent statistics: bootstrap CIs on all headline gaps, disclosed mean/median conventions, pre-written predictions, public code + full protocol.

Limitations 局限

  • Binary, single-token judgments only; models 7B–32B (27B+ in 4-bit NF4); open-ended and frontier-scale untested.
  • Representational, not mechanistic — no causal intervention yet dissociates judgment from expression (authors flag as future work).
  • Supervised pooled baseline matches the held-out numbers → value is avoiding labels, not accuracy.
  • Requires white-box hidden-state access; inapplicable to API-only models.
  • AUROCs moderate and uneven (MMLU ~0.72 vs GSM8K ~0.96 near-ceiling); benefit concentrates in word/question negation — digit mappings barely degrade (0.787).

Questions for Further Exploration 进一步探索

  1. Does the expression subspace generalize to open-ended judgments (grading free-form essays) and multi-turn settings with long-generation verdicts?
  2. Can targeted interventions on E causally dissociate judgment from expression — moving from a representational to a mechanistic claim?
  3. Is the subspace shared across models, even universal across architectures — and do the 3 negation directions correspond to identifiable circuits or attention heads?
  4. How does chain-of-thought interact with the separation: does verbalized reasoning shift where in depth disruption appears?
  5. Practical payoff — read the isolated judgment instead of the verdict in self-consistency or LLM-as-judge pipelines when instructions are negation-heavy?
  6. Since readout degradation tracks inverted-mapping application (Spearman 0.62), can instruction-tuning against E reduce verdict fragility without touching judgment?
