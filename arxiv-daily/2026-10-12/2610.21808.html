Why This Matters

Post-training ternary quantization usually destroys model knowledge; this interpretability-derived rule retains most of it for a few GPU-hours, not days.

Critical Edge

The knowledge-Fisher selection signal and the headline MMLU metric come from the same task family — the +6.2-point edge over random placement may partly reflect task-targeted allocation.

Methodology · The RoleQ-T Pipeline
01

Role-aligned scales

One scale per functional vector: residual-stream readers (rows of WQ/K/V, Wgate, Win) scale per output unit; writers (columns of WO, Wout) per input unit. No axis search, no calibration, no stored flags.

placement alone: 14B ternary MMLU 25.4 → 38.5
02

Data-free rotations

s₁ learned with an ℓ₄ kurtosis objective, s₂ with the exact ternary reconstruction objective, plus r₄ Hadamard — on top of QuaRot's rotation slots.

1.38× further ppl cut vs random Hadamard @ 8B
03

Exact ternary GPTQ

Per-vector joint optimum min‖w − at‖² over sign-code supports; readers get a closed-form refit (a* = tᵀHw / tᵀHt) that provably never increases error.

no codebooks · no group metadata
04

Scale distillation

Codes frozen; only scales and norm gains (0.024% of parameters at 8B) train for 400 steps on 3,200 unlabeled RedPajama windows against the fp16 teacher.

2 h · 1× A100 @ 8B
05

Fisher-placed 2nd planes

Units ranked by Fisher-weighted residual saliency get a second ternary code — two multiplication-free ternary matmuls plus an add. K sets the rate in unit-sized steps.

+0.57 bpw → MMLU 47.6 → 62.8

Results · Beyond the Headline
  • Knowledge, cheaply bought — +0.57 bpw of Fisher planes lifts MMLU 47.6 → 62.8 and ppl 11.33 → 9.30 at 2.16 bpw, still under QTEA's 2.281 rate.
  • Scales to 14B — 8.05 ppl (1.26× fp16) and 68.3 MMLU at 2.156 bpw vs QTEA's released 47.8 MMLU at 2.257 bpw.
  • vs learned ternarization — at CAT-Q's own 1.711 bpw: ppl 17.00 vs 27.68, MMLU tied (53.1 vs 52.9), in under ten GPU-hours vs CAT-Q's 8–64 A100-hours.
  • Honest negative result — QTIP's trellis codes lead every metric at 2 bpw on Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct (MMLU 59.9 vs 51.0); the authors claim no parity.
  • Placement ablation — matched solver/seeds/rotation at 14B ternary: 25.25 ppl vs 37.89 (per-output-channel) and 42.60 (per-input-channel); same ordering on every seed from 1.7B up.
  • Resident-rate audit — claimed vs resident bits exposed: PT²-LLM 1.58→1.835, QTEA 1.70→2.281, TWLA 1.58→4.15–4.65.
Relevance · 相关性

如果你关注后训练量化、三值 / 亚 2-bit 推理，或"机制解释视角指导压缩设计"这条路线，RoleQ 值得精读：它把可解释性中的残差流读写角色直接转化为 scale 放置规则，并在知识保留（MMLU）这个 PTQ 最痛的指标上给出数量级改善；其"按功能单元分配额外比特优于按误差幅度"的结论，对混合精度分配也有普适启发。

Strengths
  • One data-free, grid-agnostic rule that wins across 0.6B–14B in two model families — biggest effect exactly where theory predicts (ternary, where the scale is a vector's only continuous degree of freedom).
  • Exceptional cost-quality point: 47.6 MMLU at 1.59 bpw for 3–4 A100-hours — orders of magnitude below learned ternarization.
  • The exact per-vector fit plus provably-safe reader refit is clean, reusable solver engineering.
  • The Fisher-vs-Hessian selector study is a genuinely instructive negative result for mixed-precision allocation.
  • Transparent, self-critical reporting: resident-bpw audit of baselines, baseline reruns in one harness, disclosed negative result vs QTIP.
Limitations
  • Rotation-dependent — the placement advantage does not hold in native (unrotated) coordinates.
  • Thin scaling evidence: four Qwen3 sizes plus one Llama; plane gains shrink from 8B to 14B (MMLU +15.2 → +8.6).
  • Fisher's edge narrows with budget — random placement comes within 1.12× at +0.9 bits.
  • Trellis codebooks (QTIP / GuidedQuant) still lead at 2 bpw on Llama (−8.9 MMLU after distillation).
  • No chain-of-thought or open-generation evaluation; no kernel latency or memory-bandwidth measurements (weight-only, A16).
  • Two-seed averages (four in places); no code release mentioned in the main text.
