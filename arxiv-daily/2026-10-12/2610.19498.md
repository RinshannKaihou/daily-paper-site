> **一句话总结：** 该工作首次测度 GGUF 量化生态的"独立可验证率"（IVR）：在 Hugging Face 下载量前 200（198 个有效）的 GGUF 仓库中，按记录输入协议 IVR = 0/198——没有任何产物或模型卡记录量化构建环境（仅 19/198 的卡用文字写了 llama.cpp 版本）；通过对 bartowski 四个仓库的实测重推导，论文把失败定位到量化这一步，并指出卡上未记载的两个坐标——构建的浮点收缩设置与逐张量量化配方——补齐后可在 x86 上比特级复现 7/8 个目标量化文件（Qwen3.5-0.8B 从 0/23 → 23/23 种量化全部复现），同时给出 B>0 场景的安全刻画：良构浮点非确定性最高可掩护约 0.2% 权重（约 2×10⁶ 次）均匀比特翻转不被统计检测，而定向嵌入编辑可完全绕过检测，因此协议必须依赖 B=0 的精确重推导。

> **TL;DR:** This paper introduces and measures the Independent Verifiability Rate (IVR) — the fraction of the popularity-sampled GGUF ecosystem a third party can re-derive bit-for-bit — over the 200 most-downloaded Hugging Face GGUF repositories (198 in scope, August 2026): IVR = 0/198 under a recorded-inputs protocol, because no artifact or model card records the build environment (compiler, architecture, FP-contraction setting). Trial re-derivation on four bartowski repositories shows card-disclosed inputs alone match only 140/335 (Qwen3.5-0.8B) and 141/339 (Qwen2.5-7B) tensors of the published Q4_K_M files, versus 7/8 target-quant files bit-exact on x86 once the two card-absent coordinates (FP-contraction setting + per-tensor recipe) are supplied. The authors independently reproduce llama.cpp PR #25353's cross-architecture divergence (default builds byte-differ for 3/4 quant types on arm64/clang vs x86-64/gcc; `-ffp-contract=off` restores 4/4 bit-identity at +0.06% perplexity: 10.750 vs 10.744 on wikitext-2, Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct Q4_K_M), and characterize the B>0 security regime: benign fp non-determinism hides up to ~0.2% of weight edits (≈2×10⁶ one-step nibble flips at 1.5B) from the evaluated statistics, and a targeted embedding edit flips a greedy next-token prediction while evading detection entirely — so the proposed protocol rests on exact re-derivation, not statistics.

## 核心贡献

- **首次对 GGUF 生态做流行度采样的可验证性普查**：定义并测量"独立可验证率"（IVR，第三方仅凭公开披露信息能否比特级重推导出部署的量化产物）。在下载量前 200 的 Hugging Face GGUF 仓库（198 个有效、61 个发布者）中，记录输入协议下 IVR = 0/198；把原因分解为"源头可重构"（148/198 = 74.7%，按基座模型规则）与"环境被记录"（0/198，GGUF 格式根本没有对应字段）——环境缺失是结构性、普遍性的缺口，而非个别发布者的疏漏。
- **实测重推导把失败精确定位到量化步骤并找出缺失坐标**：在 bartowski 的 Qwen2.5-7B（b3772）、Qwen3.5-{0.8B,2B,4B}（b9222）与 granite-4.2-3b（b10603）上逐张量比对 sha256：仅凭模型卡披露的信息（版本 + 官方 imatrix + 默认预设）无法复现任何 Q4_K_M 文件（0.8B 仅 140/335 张量匹配）；补上浮点收缩设置与逐张量配方两个卡上没有的坐标后，8 个目标×量化文件中 7 个在 x86 上比特级复现（arm64 上 5/8）；fp16 转换步骤可从模型提供方 checkpoint 逐位复现，而校准 imatrix 无法重算（0/186 条目比特一致），必须随产物发布并做哈希绑定。
- **给出使"验证者需要什么"精确化的词汇表**：把变换写成 Q = T(X̄; E)，其中非环境输入 X̄=(W, θ, C, M)，环境 E 覆盖编译器/架构/浮点设置；定义可接纳输出集 A(X̄) 与 0/1 歧义指示 B。用实验见证 Observation 1：E 是完备规范的必要分量——同一 W、同一 θ 在 x86-64/gcc 与 arm64/clang 默认构建下产生字节不同的输出，而一个编译器开关（`-ffp-contract=off`，即 PR #25353）在测试平台上以 +0.06% 困惑度的代价使 B=0。
- **B>0 场景的安全刻画**：在去量化权重空间度量良构浮点非确定性与均匀比特翻转篡改两种扰动分布——篡改的每权重位移（中位 |Δw|/σ ≈ 0.27）约为良构漂移（0.014）的 20 倍，检测下限为 0.005%–0.05% 权重（已知环境对）；环境范围越宽下限越高，五对环境校准的模型级阈值为 0.19%，即可隐藏约 0.2% 权重（1.5B 上约 2×10⁶ 次）的均匀一步编辑；定向嵌入编辑（一步编辑 Q6_K 嵌入行翻转贪婪解码结果）则完全绕过统计。
- **落地协议与工具**：建议量化方对 (H(W), θ, C, E, M) 签名清单，独立重建者把比对结果发布到 append-only 透明日志；证明抽查式部分验证的开销为 Θ(N/m)，对稀疏篡改趋近全量重推导。发布可重跑的审计工具（census.py、GGUF header-probe、字节区域 diff、检测器）与全部原始数据。

## Key Contributions

- **First popularity-sampled verifiability census of the GGUF ecosystem.** Defines the Independent Verifiability Rate (IVR): the fraction of deployed artifacts an independent party can re-derive bit-for-bit from disclosed inputs. Across the 200 most-downloaded Hugging Face GGUF repositories (198 in scope, 61 publishers, frozen list, August 2026), the recorded-inputs IVR is 0/198; the paper decomposes this into source-reconstructibility (148/198 = 74.7% under the base-model rule; 111/198 if the repo must ship its own fp16 GGUF) versus environment recording (0/198 — the GGUF format has no field for it, making this a structural gap).
- **A constructive trial re-derivation that localizes the failure and names the missing coordinates.** Per-tensor sha256 comparison on four bartowski repositories across three model families and three llama.cpp releases: card-disclosed inputs alone reproduce no Q4_K_M file (140/335 and 141/339 tensors match for the two families); supplying the build's FP-contraction setting and the per-tensor quantization recipe — neither recorded anywhere — re-derives 7/8 target-quant files bit-for-bit on x86 (5/8 on arm64). The fp16 conversion hop re-derives exactly from the provider's checkpoint, but the calibration imatrix does not (0/186 entries bit-identical when recomputed), so it must be shipped and hash-bound.
- **A compact formal vocabulary (X̄/E, admissible set A, 0/1 ambiguity indicator B) plus its empirical witness.** Observation 1 (necessity of E): with X̄ fixed, x86-64/gcc and arm64/clang default builds produce byte-different outputs for 3 of 4 quant types, so any complete re-derivation specification must constrain E. One compiler flag (`-ffp-contract=off`, llama.cpp PR #25353) attains B=0 across the tested (architecture, compiler) pairs at +0.06% perplexity, yet is not the llama.cpp default.
- **A security characterization of the current B>0 regime.** In dequantized weight space, one-step nibble flips move weights ~20× further (median |Δw|/σ ≈ 0.27 vs 0.014) than benign fp drift; the measured detection floor is 0.02% of weights for Q4_K against a known environment pair (AUC ≥ 0.99, every seed), rising to ~0.1–0.2% as the admissible environment range widens — a hiding capacity of ≈2×10⁶ edits at 1.5B. A targeted embedding edit (sub-τ one-step edits to a Q6_K embedding row of the tied-embedding Qwen2.5-1.5B) flips the greedy next token while evading all three evaluated statistics, which is why the protocol rests on exact re-derivation. Adversarial-fp16 attacks (Mind the Gap) are explicitly outside the boundary.
- **A deployable protocol and open harness.** The transforming party signs a manifest committing to (H(W), θ, C, E, M); independent rebuilders publish signed match/mismatch results to a transparency log. A standard Θ(N/m) sampling bound shows partial spot-checking degrades exactly against sparse tampering. The frozen census, re-runnable scoring harness, and all analysis tools are released.

## Methodology

The paper runs three measurements feeding one verification protocol:

1. **Census (M1).** The 200 most-downloaded GGUF repositories from the Hugging Face API (August 2026, trailing 30-day download window; 2 excluded for lacking GGUF files → 198 from 61 publishers). Instead of downloading multi-GB files, the audit fetches only each representative artifact's GGUF header (8–16 MB via HTTP range requests — preferring the Q4_K_M file, first shard for sharded models) and scores a four-input re-derivation checklist: (i) fp16 source W retrievable, (ii) quantizer version recorded, (iii) imatrix C retrievable (tri-state; no imatrix record treated as C=∅ i.e. disclosed), (iv) any execution-environment coordinate recorded. Model cards are audited two ways: an adversarial 12-card LLM-agent audit (one agent per card, instructed to refute the "not verifiable" label) and a deterministic pattern scan of all 198 cards for release/commit identifiers with every hit manually classified.
2. **Trial re-derivation (M1, constructive half).** Four in-sample bartowski repositories — Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct (b3772), Qwen3.5-{0.8B,2B,4B} (b9222), plus granite-4.2-3b (b10603) — each publishing its own fp16 GGUF, imatrix, and Q4_K_M/Q8_0 files. The authors re-derive the deployed files under combinations of {card-only inputs, + pinned per-tensor recipe} × {arm64/clang Apple M4, x86-64/gcc Linux} × {default, `-ffp-contract=off`}, comparing per-tensor sha256 of tensor data (the key–value region excluded because llama-quantize writes local file paths into it). A pilot on Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct (b3772) additionally tests the fp16-conversion hop from the provider's safetensors checkpoint and attempts imatrix regeneration from the card-linked calibration corpus.
3. **Ambiguity localization (M2).** llama.cpp pinned at b10448, quantizing one fixed fp16 (sha256-identical on both machines) into Q4_K_M, Q5_K_M, IQ4_XS, plus Q8_0 as a no-scale-search control, with 10 repeats per configuration (16/16 configurations bit-deterministic same-machine — the baseline, not the finding). Byte-region localization attributes the cross-architecture divergence; a size trend runs at 1.5B/7B/14B (7B/14B via a single-machine proxy validated exact at 1.5B); quality cost is a paired perplexity comparison on wikitext-2 (584 chunks).
4. **Security characterization (M3).** Perturbations measured in dequantized weight space as |Δw|/σ per tensor: benign natural variance (arm64 default vs the contraction-off reference build) versus tampering constructed the way a cheating quantizer would produce it (honest rebuild + uniformly placed one-step nibble flips, 5 seeds). Detection floors at AUC ≥ 0.99 per-tensor across 165–197 tensors per type; adaptive variants (per-block scale multiplication ×(1+ε) sweep, adaptive low-magnitude placement, targeted embedding edit verified by full-precision forward passes) probe the blind spots. Formal scaffolding: Observation 1 (necessity of E) and Proposition 1 (standard PDP spot-checking bound, k = Θ(N/m)), with proofs in the appendix and assumptions A1–A7 delimiting the security-claim boundary.

Throughout, the authors are scrupulous about prior art they build on rather than claim: deterministic GGUF re-derivation is ggufpacker's existing engineering, the fp-contraction divergence and fix are llama.cpp PR #25353, and the adversarial-fp16 attack bounding the threat model is Mind the Gap (ICML 2025). Their contribution is measuring how far current disclosure falls short of what these tools presuppose.

## Results & Highlights

- **IVR = 0/198 under the recorded-inputs protocol** across the 200 most-downloaded Hugging Face GGUF repositories (198 in scope; 0/165 when restricted to text/multimodal LLMs). No standardized GGUF field records the llama.cpp version, compiler, target architecture, or FP-contraction setting, and 0/198 artifacts record any environment coordinate in metadata; only 19/198 model cards state the llama.cpp release in prose (18 of them from two publishers' templates, none stating compiler/architecture/contraction). By contrast, source-reconstructibility is 148/198 (74.7%, base-model rule) or 111/198 (56.1%, own-fp16 rule) — the universal gap is the environment, not the weights.
- **Card-only inputs fail; two omitted coordinates close most of the gap.** From the card's named release + published imatrix + stock preset, no Q4_K_M file re-derives — only 140/335 (Qwen3.5-0.8B/2B) and 141/339 (Qwen2.5-7B) tensors match. With the per-tensor recipe and FP-contraction setting supplied: 7/8 target×quant files bit-for-bit on x86, 5/8 on arm64 (the Q8_0 residuals are one tensor whose all-zero blocks store byte-different but numerically identical codes). Qwen3.5-0.8B is the sharpest demonstration: re-deriving all 23 published quant types gives 0/23 from the card's named type alone but 23/23 bit-for-bit with the recipe. granite-4.2-3b Q4_K_M goes from 263/363 to 363/363. The exception is Qwen2.5-7B Q4_K_M (b3772): its release compiles kernels with `-march=native`, pinning the SIMD ISA, and matched none of six configurations (best 170/339 tensors).
- **The fp16 hop re-derives exactly; the imatrix does not.** For the pilot 1.5B repository, converting the model provider's checkpoint (HF revision 989aa79) with the named converter reproduces the published fp16 bit-for-bit, on a different machine, twice over. Regenerating the 0.8B imatrix from the card-linked corpus (identical sha256) reproduces the structure (all 186 entries' token counts match) but 0/186 entries are bit-identical (median relative difference 5.5×10⁻⁵); re-quantizing with the recomputed imatrix — at otherwise bit-exact settings — changes 141/335 tensors. The imatrix must therefore be shipped and hash-bound.
- **Environment divergence is real, localized in the weights, and removable.** At b10448, the two default builds produce byte-different outputs for 3 of 4 quant types (Q4_K_M, Q5_K_M, IQ4_XS); only Q8_0 matches. `-ffp-contract=off` collapses all 4/4 to bit-identity across both (architecture, compiler) pairs. Byte-localization: the metadata region is bit-identical; divergence concentrates in the k-quant per-block scale parameters (per-byte difference rate 1.2×10⁻³, the highest of any region) and propagates into packed nibbles (73% of differing bytes at 3.4×10⁻⁴). The divergent-byte fraction grows with model size (1.5B → 7B → 14B).
- **The fix is (measurably) free.** Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct Q4_K_M on wikitext-2 (584 chunks): perplexity 10.750 with `-ffp-contract=off` vs 10.744 default — ΔPPL +0.006 (+0.06%, paired SE 0.005, not distinguishable from zero), though the two artifacts byte-differ.
- **Security floors and blind spots (Qwen2.5 family).** Benign fp drift touches 0.22% of Q4_K weights at median |Δw|/σ = 0.014; uniform one-step Q4_K flips move median 0.27 (~20× larger; overlap coefficient 0.036 at 0.5B). Per-tensor detection floors (AUC ≥ 0.99 every seed): 0.02% of weights (Q4_K), 0.005% (Q5_K, IQ4_XS), 0.05% (Q6_K) against the known arm64 contraction pair; the floor rises to 0.1% as benign noise spans pairs, and a model-level threshold calibrated on the five known-release pairs (0.19%) flags every tampered model from 0.2% of weights up, in every pair and seed — i.e., up to ≈2×10⁶ uniformly placed one-step edits (of 1.1B Q4_K weights) hide below it, and more as the admissible environment range widens. The max statistic (honest ≤ 2.36) catches single farthest-code edits (2.40–2.52). But adaptive placement hides a bounded low-magnitude reservoir (0.43% of weights, ≈4.7M), and the targeted embedding attack flips the greedy next token (e.g., "the opposite of hot is" → hot, confirmed by full-precision forward pass) with every edit below τ (max |Δw|/σ ≤ 0.10, pooled contribution ≈10⁻⁶), evading all three statistics.

Prior art is separated with care (they explicitly do not claim ggufpacker's engineering, PR #25353's discovery, or Mind the Gap's attack). The paper is 15 pages, cs.CR, with the depth and honest scoping of a security-venue submission.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: The measurement (IVR over a popularity-sampled GGUF ecosystem) and the two named card-absent coordinates are genuinely new; the deterministic-re-derivation engineering and the fp-contraction fix are explicitly prior work (ggufpacker, PR #25353) that this paper instruments rather than claims. The honest framing is a strength, but it also means the constructive half (how to make quantization reproducible) is largely restating an open upstream PR.
- **Rigor**: High where it counts — bit-exact claims backed by per-tensor sha256 over multiple platforms and builds, 10-repeat determinism checks, paired perplexity with standard error, AUC floors with seeds, and proofs for the two formal statements. The threat model is regime-split (Table I) with stated assumptions rather than a blanket "detectable" claim.
- **The headline 0/198 is partly definitional.** GGUF has no field for the build environment, so a recorded-inputs protocol cannot pass by construction — the authors say this openly ("measures what the format and current disclosure practice allow"), and the census conflates "not disclosed" with "not verifiable" for 44/198 artifacts (23 Q8_0/Q4_0, 8 f16/vision files, 13 custom formats) where the necessity of E is not even established. The search-based rate is bounded below by only 3 files from one trial and left unmeasured; the true practically-verifiable fraction of the ecosystem is unknown.
- **The trial re-derivation generalizes from a single publisher.** All five repositories (including granite-4.2-3b) were quantized by bartowski — explicitly a best case whose "publisher discloses more than most." Whether other publishers' artifacts are as re-derivable with the two coordinates is untested; the per-tensor-recipe underdetermination ("Q4_K_M" on the card vs a custom type map keeping SSM tensors at F32) may be publisher-specific practice.
- **Narrow coverage.** Models: Qwen 0.5B–14B + one Granite 3B. Types: Q4_K/Q5_K/Q6_K/IQ4_XS (+Q8_0 control). Pipeline: llama.cpp CPU quantization, exactly two (architecture, compiler) pairs. GPU imatrix generation, CUDA builds, and GPU-native pipelines (GPTQ/AWQ/bitsandbytes) are all out of scope — and the one imatrix they tried to regenerate failed to match on every CPU build they tested, hinting the ecosystem's actual environment space is wider than measured. The quality check of the fix covers one model/type/task.
- **Reproducibility**: Excellent — frozen sampling frame, released harness, raw data behind every figure, pinned llama.cpp builds referenced by identifier. The one soft spot is the 12-card audit via a closed LLM (anchoring acknowledged; complemented by the deterministic scan).
- **Red flags**: None serious. The paper cannot find the b3772 publisher environment (best 170/339 tensors on the 7B Q4_K_M), which quietly means pre-fix artifacts may never become verifiable — the "closable gap" claim holds only for newly produced artifacts. Their preliminary Mind-the-Gap run on a 0.5B model produced no working attack, which they report but which also means the adversarial-fp16 boundary is argued from prior work, not their own measurement.

## Strengths

- First ecosystem-level, popularity-sampled measurement of quantized-artifact verifiability, with a decomposition that cleanly separates "inputs hidden" from "environment unrecorded" (148/198 vs 0/198).
- Constructive, falsifiable diagnosis: names the exact two coordinates (FP-contraction setting, per-tensor recipe) that convert disclosure failure into bit-exact re-derivation on named public artifacts (0/23 → 23/23 for the 0.8B).
- Independent reproduction plus extension of PR #25353: weight-space localization (scales first, nibbles downstream), a size trend, and a paired perplexity estimate (+0.06%, indistinguishable from zero) quantifying the cost of determinism.
- A security characterization with honest blind-spot reporting — the targeted embedding evasion and the environment-range-dependent hiding capacity are exactly the results a less careful paper would have buried.
- Exemplary prior-art hygiene, explicit assumptions, frozen re-runnable census, and full artifact release.

## Limitations

- The IVR headline measures disclosure, not physical irreproducibility, and is structurally forced to zero by the GGUF format's lack of an environment field; the search-based verifiability rate is bounded from below by only 3/198 files and otherwise unmeasured.
- Trial re-derivation covers one (best-case) publisher, three llama.cpp releases, and mostly the Qwen family; the ecosystem-wide re-derivability claim does not follow from n=5 repositories.
- Environment space is sampled at two (architecture, compiler) pairs on CPU; GPU imatrix generation, CUDA builds, MSVC/older compilers, and GPU-native quantization (GPTQ/AWQ) are untested — and the failed imatrix regeneration suggests unexplored environment coordinates.
- The detection floors are calibrated on five environment pairs of one model family against uniform one-step flips; adaptive placement, scale perturbations, and targeted edits already expose bounded-to-total blind spots, so the statistics are a characterization, not a defense.
- Older artifacts (pre-fix releases with `-march=native`) appear permanently unverifiable by re-derivation — the protocol is forward-looking, and adoption depends on upstream llama.cpp accepting the fix and publishers releasing fp16/imatrix/recipe, an uncosted ecosystem change.
- No evaluation of adversarial-fp16 attacks (out of scope by definition A1); the Mind-the-Gap preliminary run did not produce a working attack, so that boundary rests on cited prior work.

## Relevance

这篇论文正面命中"极限性能优化下的可靠性"方向：它系统刻画了量化（4-bit GGUF）这一激进优化环节因浮点收缩、编译器与架构差异导致的比特级不可复现性，以及由此产生的篡改检测盲区（最高约 0.2% 权重的隐藏容量），对量化产物完整性与低精度数值可靠性的供应链视角研究有直接参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would `-ffp-contract=off` (PR #25353, still open as of September 2026) attain B=0 across untested compilers (MSVC, older GCC), CUDA-enabled builds, and future llama.cpp releases — and what would it take to make it the upstream default?
- What is the ecosystem-wide search-based IVR — re-deriving all 148 source-reconstructible repositories rather than a 4-repository trial — and how does it vary across publishers?
- Why could the imatrix not be recomputed on any tested CPU build (0/186 entries bit-identical)? Is the accumulation path GPU-based or thread-order-dependent, and can imatrix generation itself be made bit-reproducible so that C need not be shipped?
- How does the verifiability gap extend to GPU-native quantization pipelines (GPTQ/AWQ/bitsandbytes) where B=0 may be unattainable — is TEE-based attestation (Attestable Builds) the only residual route, and at what trust cost?
- Can the targeted embedding evasion be scaled beyond tied-embedding models and one-row budgets (their third probe failed to flip), and can re-derivation cost be lowered via Merkle spot-checks without collapsing to the Θ(N/m) bound (~8.1×10⁵ openings, 0.2–0.65 GB proofs for m=100 in a 7B model)?
- How should non-weight components M (chat-template backdoors are training-free attack vectors) be folded into the signed manifest and verified, given that re-derivation reproduces metadata verbatim but does not vet it?
