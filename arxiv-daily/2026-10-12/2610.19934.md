> **一句话总结：** 本文发现剪枝导致的大推理模型（LRM）精度损失主要来自"停不下来"而非"不会推理"——剪枝模型往往已在思维链中写出正确答案却陷入重复循环无法提交（作者称为 reasoning divergence），并基于跨剪枝 checkpoint 一致的"终止方向"提出免训练的激活转向方法 TGAS，在 6 个模型、3 种剪枝流水线、MATH-500/AIME-25/LiveCodeBench 上平均提升精度 1.3–3.7 点、终止率最多提升 11.2 点，同时缩短 12.3% 的生成长度。

> **TL;DR:** A careful diagnosis paper showing that when SparseGPT/Wanda-pruned reasoning models (DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen/Llama 1.5B–14B, OpenThinker3) lose accuracy, the dominant failure is non-termination — on MATH-500 at 60% sparsity, non-termination jumps from 3.8% to 16.8% for DS-Qwen-7B while wrong-but-terminated answers stay flat, and 41.4% of non-terminating traces already contain the correct answer. The stopping signal survives under teacher forcing (p(</think>) > 0.92 at 50–60% sparsity), and a difference-in-means "termination direction" is consistent across sparsity levels (cosine ≥ 0.87). Their training-free steering method TGAS beats the unsteered Base by +1.3 to +3.7 average accuracy points, clearly outperforms the SEAL steering baseline (more accurate on 62 of 72 pairs, since SEAL trades accuracy for termination), and cuts generated tokens by 12.3% at 60% sparsity — but cannot recover genuinely lost capability at high sparsity, where training-based Masked-LoRA is far stronger (e.g., LiveCodeBench 14.7%→33.4% for DS-Qwen-7B).

## 核心贡献

- **重新定性剪枝损失（reasoning divergence）：** 证明中等稀疏度下剪枝 LRM 的精度下降主要源于"无法终止/提交答案"，而非推理能力丢失。在 MATH-500 上，稀疏度从 50% 升至 60% 时，DS-Qwen-7B 的未终止率从 3.8% 涨到 16.8%，而"终止但答错"的比例几乎不变；终止样本的答对率仍高达 93.5%。
- **三层证据的诊断流水线：** (1) teacher forcing 下 `</think>` 停止信号在 50–60% 稀疏度依然完好（p > 0.85–0.92，中位 rank 0）；(2) 剪枝系统性地把决策点处的偏好从"推进新内容"转向"重复旧段落"（∆cyc 从 dense 7B 的 −15.3 移到 70% 稀疏的 −5.0）；(3) 非终止轨迹中 39–41% 已包含金标准答案，答案出现位置中位数仅在轨迹前 ~12%。
- **跨 checkpoint 一致的"终止方向"：** 用同一前缀下"终止 vs 循环"续写的隐状态差均值（difference-in-means）构造方向，不同稀疏度 checkpoint 之间余弦相似度 ≥ 0.83–0.87，说明"提交答案"由表征空间中一个相对稳定的方向编码，单一方向可复用于该模型所有稀疏度。
- **TGAS（Termination-Guided Activation Steering）：** 免训练的推理时干预——把该方向按典型激活范数缩放后加到选定层（按 held-out ROC AUC 选层）的残差流上；α 系数在 held-out OpenR1-Math 划分上调优，提供可控制的"终止—精度"旋钮。
- **大规模验证：** 6 个 LRM（DS-Qwen-1.5B/7B/14B、DS-Llama-8B、OT3-1.5B/7B）、3 种剪枝流水线（SparseGPT+OBC、SparseGPT+RAC、Wanda+C4）、3 个基准（MATH-500、AIME-25、LiveCodeBench-v6）；在全部 24 个模型×稀疏度设置中 22 个超过未干预基线，均值增益从 dense 的 +1.6 点升至 60% 稀疏的 +3.7 点，同时平均缩短 7.6% 生成长度（60% 稀疏时 12.3%，约每条省 1,423 token）。
- **与训练式恢复的清晰对照：** 50% 稀疏度下 TGAS 等免训练方法可匹敌 Masked-LoRA；60% 稀疏度下能力真正受损，只有更新权重才能恢复（Masked-LoRA 在 LCB 上把 DS-Qwen-7B 从 14.7% 拉到 33.4%），为"何时需要重训练"给出实用边界。

## Key Contributions

- **Reframes pruning-induced degradation as a termination failure ("reasoning divergence").** On MATH-500 problems the dense model solves, raising sparsity from 50% to 60% increases non-termination from 3.8% to 16.8% (DS-Qwen-7B) and 2.4% to 16.4% (14B) while the terminated-but-wrong rate stays nearly flat; conditional on terminating, accuracy is still 93.5% / 88.9%. The reasoning is largely intact — the model just cannot stop.
- **A three-pronged causal diagnosis.** (i) Larger budgets do not fix it: going from 16K to 65K tokens cuts non-termination for 7B@50% on AIME-25 from 42.5% to 12.5% but recovers only 0–3 additional correct traces. (ii) The stop signal survives: teacher-forcing dense traces through pruned checkpoints gives p(</think>) > 0.92 (7B) and > 0.85 (14B) with median rank 0 at 50–60% sparsity, collapsing only at 70% (0.036–0.13). (iii) Preference shifts toward repetition: at decision points, the cyclic-vs-progressive log-prob gap ∆cyc rises from −15.3 (dense DS-Qwen-7B) to −5.0 at 70% sparsity, and free-running loop persistence P(cyc|cyc) jumps from ≤ 0.029 (dense) to 0.307–0.324 at 70%.
- **Answer-presence analysis with a validated detector.** Using "Conclusive" text rules (validated against an LLM judge at 96.0% agreement, κ = 0.898), 41.4% / 39.2% of non-terminating traces at 60% sparsity already state the gold answer, at a median of only 11.9–13.0% of trace length (~2k tokens before the budget cap).
- **A shared, reusable termination direction.** Difference-in-means over terminating vs looping continuations of the same prefix, averaged within branch points (so problem content cancels); directions from different sparsity checkpoints of the same model align at cosine ≥ 0.87 (7B, 50–70%) and ≥ 0.83 (1.5B, 40–60%), beating label-shuffled controls in all 15 checkpoint pairs.
- **TGAS, a training-free inference-time fix.** Adds the rescaled direction to the residual stream at the ROC-AUC-selected layer; one direction per model (built at an intermediate 50–60% sparsity) reused across all sparsity levels; 50 sampled continuations per class suffice.
- **Broad, honest evaluation.** Six LRMs, three pruning pipelines, math + code benchmarks, permutation significance tests, a headroom/oracle analysis, and ablations on α, sample count, and cross-checkpoint directions; explicit comparison against training-based recovery to delimit what steering can and cannot fix.

## Methodology

**Setting.** One-shot unstructured pruning of decoder linear layers in distilled/SFT LRMs: DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B/7B/14B, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B, OpenThinker3-1.5B/7B. Pruning pipelines: SparseGPT with OBC calibration (reasoning-step-aware, the default — OBC-Prune is the authors' prior work), SparseGPT with RAC calibration (on-policy CoT), and Wanda with C4 calibration. Benchmarks: MATH-500 (levels 4–5), AIME-25, LiveCodeBench-v6; OpenR1-Math-220K is used only for calibration/steering construction (disjoint from evaluation). Generation: 16,384-token budget, T = 0.6, top-p = 0.95, k = 4 responses averaged.

**Diagnosis.** (1) Evaluate pruned models on dense-correct problems, decomposing errors into non-termination vs wrong-terminated. (2) Measure answer presence in non-terminating traces via the Conclusive detector (answer-like statements after cues like "thus the answer"). (3) Teacher-force dense traces through pruned checkpoints and measure p(</think>) and its rank at the dense STOP position. (4) Define decision points (paragraphs starting with reconsideration cues like "wait"/"hmm"); label paragraphs CYCLIC if their 8-word n-gram set has Jaccard ≥ 0.5 with an earlier paragraph, else PROGRESS; measure teacher-forced preference ∆cyc between the two candidate continuations and free-running transition dynamics P(cyc|cyc), P(cyc|prog) from shared branch points.

**TGAS construction.** From each eligible branch point b of dense correct traces (prefix already sufficient for the answer), sample N continuations with the pruned checkpoint; split into terminating (C+) and non-terminating (C−); represent each continuation by its mean hidden state from b to the dense trace's `</think>` position; compute within-branch difference-in-means and average over branch points to get a per-layer direction r_ℓ (problem/prefix content cancels). Select the steering layer ℓ⋆ by ROC AUC of the projection on held-out branch points; rescale to the mean activation norm (v = n̄·r/‖r‖); add α·v to the residual stream at every token during generation. α is tuned per checkpoint on a held-out OpenR1-Math split. Direction is built once at intermediate sparsity (50% for 1.5B, 60% otherwise) and reused for all sparsity levels of that model.

**Baselines.** Training-free: Forced-Answer (hard stop after D reasoning tokens), No-Repeat (suppress repeated n-grams), SEAL (reflection-suppression steering vector). Training-based: Masked-LoRA (rank-64 LoRA on surviving weights, trained on dense DS-Qwen-14B traces).

## Results & Highlights

- **Non-termination, not wrong answers, drives degradation (Table 1, MATH-500, dense-correct problems):** DS-Qwen-7B at 50%→60% sparsity: non-termination 3.8%→16.8%, terminated-but-wrong 4.5%→5.4% (nearly flat), accuracy-given-termination 95.3%→93.5%. OT3-7B: 13.1%→29.8% non-termination at 50%→60%.
- **Correct answers already present in looping traces (Figure 2, MATH-500, 60% sparsity):** the gold answer appears in 41.4% of DS-Qwen-7B and 39.2% of DS-Qwen-14B non-terminating traces, at a median of 13.0% / 11.9% of trace length (~2.1k / 1.9k tokens) — the model reasons correctly, then loops (one example repeats a "Wait, perhaps I made a mistake" cycle 583 times until the 16k cap).
- **Stopping signal survives moderate pruning (Table 2, teacher forcing at dense STOP):** p(</think>) = 0.998/0.957 at 50%/60% sparsity for DS-Qwen-7B on AIME-25 (median rank 0), vs 0.036 at 70%; DS-Qwen-14B: 0.995/0.854 vs 0.073. Failure is reaching the stop, not recognizing it.
- **Pruning shifts preference toward repetition (Table 3):** ∆cyc at decision points on AIME-25 moves from −15.3 (dense DS-Qwen-7B) to −5.0 at 70% sparsity; OT3-7B: −23.1→−5.6. Free-running loop persistence P(cyc|cyc) reaches 0.307 (7B) / 0.324 (14B) at 70% sparsity vs ≤ 0.029 dense (Table 4).
- **TGAS recovers accuracy across the board (Table 5, avg over MATH-500/AIME-25/LCB):** every model×sparsity setting gains +1.3 to +3.7 points over unsteered Base; e.g., DS-Llama-8B@60%: 14.7→18.3 (+3.6, and +1.6 over the best competitor Forced-Answer at 16.7); DS-Qwen-14B@50%: 60.2→63.9; OT3-1.5B@10%: 52.4→54.9. Best average accuracy among training-free methods in all six main-table settings; comprehensively (Appendix E.1) best or tied-best on 53 of 72 checkpoint-benchmark pairs.
- **TGAS fixes exactly the diagnosed failure:** termination improves on 65 of 72 pairs (from +2.6 points dense to +11.2 points at 60% sparsity) without dropping on any; on MATH-500 at 60% sparsity, DS-Qwen-1.5B termination 21.9%→67.9% and accuracy 15.5%→30.9%. Headroom analysis: TGAS captures 90% (1.5B) and 70% (7B) of the oracle "commit-to-already-stated-answer" gain at 60% sparsity, and turns 25.8%/36.1%/44.2% of non-terminating generations correct (vs 17.1% pooled for an unsteered rerun with another seed), while leaving terminated-wrong generations statistically unchanged (31.2% vs 31.9% rerun) — it restores stopping, not correctness.
- **Beats SEAL, which over-truncates reasoning (Appendix E.1):** SEAL lifts termination by a similar 5.8 points (TGAS: 5.9) but its accuracy falls below Base on 40 of 72 pairs (e.g., DS-Qwen-7B@60% AIME-25: termination 43.3%→60.8% but accuracy 22.5%→16.7%); TGAS is more accurate than SEAL on 62 of 72 pairs.
- **Cheaper inference (Tables 7–8):** TGAS makes ∆cyc more negative in all settings, halves decision-point density at 60% sparsity (7B: 12.3→5.8 per 1k tokens; 14B: 11.5→6.8), and cuts generated tokens by 7.6% on average (12.3% at 60% sparsity ≈ 1,423 tokens saved per generation) while keeping accuracy at or above Base in 64 of 72 settings.
- **Generalizes across pruning pipelines (Table 6):** on DS-Qwen-7B with RAC/SparseGPT and C4/Wanda, TGAS outperforms the pruned Base in all 8 settings and is best in 7 (e.g., RAC@10% AIME-25: 39.2 vs Base 35.8; RAC@50% LCB: 34.5 vs 32.6).
- **Statistical significance (Appendix F.10):** sign-flip permutation tests (K = 16, 20,000 permutations) give significant pooled gains for DS-Qwen-7B on LCB (+1.5, p = 0.016) and OT3-7B on AIME-25 (+2.1, p = 0.046) and LCB (+1.1, p = 0.026); the largest checkpoint-level gain is +4.4 for DS-Qwen-7B@60% on LCB (p = 0.003).
- **Where steering ends and retraining begins (Appendix E.2):** at 60% sparsity Masked-LoRA raises LCB accuracy from 14.7% to 33.4% (DS-Qwen-7B) and 37.6% to 51.8% (14B), well above the best training-free results (19.5% / 42.9%) — genuinely lost capability needs weight updates.

## Authors & Credibility

A five-author team from Nanyang Technological University, Singapore (Minh-Anh Nguyen, Xiyan Fu, Wei Lu) and VinUniversity, Vietnam (Nguyen T. Ha Lan, Dung D. Le). Notably, the same group authored OBC-Prune (Nguyen et al., 2026), the reasoning-step-aware calibration used as the paper's default pruning pipeline — they are deeply embedded in this exact research line, which cuts both ways: strong domain expertise and access to matched infrastructure, but also a default experimental setting built on their own prior method. The paper exhibits unusually strong empirical hygiene for this genre: the answer-presence heuristic is validated against an independent LLM judge (96.0% agreement, κ = 0.898); the steering-direction consistency claim is tested against label-shuffled controls in all 15 checkpoint pairs; gains are backed by permutation tests; and the appendix includes an oracle headroom analysis, cross-checkpoint direction reuse tests, and a Plan-CoT comparison. Models are all public (DeepSeek-R1-Distill, OpenThinker3), benchmarks are standard, and the method needs only sampling-based calibration data — reproducibility looks high.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty: good on diagnosis, modest on method.** The reframe — pruning hurts *commitment*, not *reasoning* — is genuinely illuminating and supported from multiple angles (teacher-forced `</think>` probability, ∆cyc preference shift, free-running transition dynamics, answer-presence). TGAS itself, however, is a fairly direct application of difference-in-means activation steering (Arditi et al., 2024) to a new contrast; the technical novelty lies in the contrast construction (same-prefix terminating vs looping continuations, averaged within branch points), not in the steering machinery.
- **Rigor: high, with honest caveats stated.** The paper itself concedes that per-benchmark gains are often within one standard deviation and that TGAS trails the strongest baseline in 4 of 18 main-table pairs. AIME-25 with k = 4 carries huge error bars (±3–8 points on a 30-problem set), so individual AIME cells should be read qualitatively; the pooled sign-flip tests partially mitigate this.
- **A quiet dependency on the authors' own pipeline.** The default results table (Table 5) is SparseGPT+OBC — OBC-Prune is the authors' method — while RAC and Wanda/C4 generalization is confined to a single model (DS-Qwen-7B, Table 6). The cross-pipeline claim therefore rests on 8 settings from one model family. Also, at the most degraded Wanda/RAC 60% checkpoints (accuracy 0–3%), every training-free method including TGAS fails, so the practical envelope is narrower than the headline.
- **"Training-free" is not hyperparameter-free.** TGAS needs one round of continuation sampling from a pruned checkpoint per model, an ROC-AUC layer search, and per-checkpoint α tuning on a held-out OpenR1-Math split. This is cheap but not free, and α is a per-checkpoint knob rather than a single global setting.
- **Possible conflation with generic anti-reflection steering.** The paper shows TGAS's direction is only weakly aligned with SEAL's reflection direction (Appendix F.8), which is reassuring, but a stronger test — e.g., steering with random or shuffled directions at matched norms, or applying SEAL's vector at TGAS-matched termination rates — would firm up the claim that the *termination-specific* contrast, not just any commit-forward push, is doing the work.
- **Missing ablations worth noting:** no structured or semi-structured sparsity (paper acknowledges), no RL-trained LRMs (all models are distilled/SFT), no tasks beyond math/code, and the Masked-LoRA baseline is trained on dense DS-Qwen-14B traces even for the 1.5B model — the paper flags this distillation confound itself.
- **Significance: practical and conceptual.** The message "moderate pruning breaks stopping before it breaks reasoning, and future pruners should preserve termination behavior" is actionable for the compression community; the accuracy gains, however, are modest (+1–4 points) and cannot substitute for retraining at high sparsity — the paper is admirably explicit about this boundary.

## Strengths

- A crisp, falsifiable reframing (reasoning divergence) backed by four independent measurement angles that all converge on the same story.
- Clean causal isolation: the steering intervention fixes exactly what the diagnosis predicts (termination up, wrong-answer rate unchanged vs rerun, decision points halved) — rare consistency between diagnosis and remedy in this literature.
- One direction per model reused across all sparsity levels, validated by cross-checkpoint cosine similarity and label-shuffled controls; only 50 samples per class needed.
- Strong baseline coverage for a training-free method (Forced-Answer, No-Repeat, SEAL, Plan-CoT, Masked-LoRA), including the training-based method that beats it — and saying so.
- Concrete efficiency dividend: 7.6–12.3% fewer generated tokens (up to 1,423 tokens saved per generation at 60% sparsity) at equal or better accuracy, which matters because non-termination wastes the entire 16K budget.
- Validated measurement instruments (Conclusive detector: 96% LLM-judge agreement, κ = 0.898) and permutation significance testing — methodological care well above the area median.

## Limitations

- Post-hoc recovery only: TGAS does not modify the pruning process, so damage is repaired rather than prevented; the authors call for termination-aware pruning as future work.
- Scope of models: all six LRMs are distilled or SFT-trained and ≤ 14B; whether RL-trained reasoning models (full R1-style), larger models, or open-ended domains show the same divergence is untested.
- Benchmarks are math (MATH-500, AIME-25) and code (LiveCodeBench-v6) only, with a 16,384-token budget; conclusions about non-termination are budget-relative by construction.
- Unstructured sparsity only — semi-structured patterns (2:4 etc.) that SparseGPT/Wanda also support are not studied.
- At high sparsity (60% for RAC/Wanda, 70% for OBC), capability is genuinely destroyed and TGAS recovers only a fraction of the dense gap; Masked-LoRA dominates there but needs a separate adapter per model and sparsity level.
- Requires one calibration pass per model (sampling continuations from a pruned checkpoint) plus per-checkpoint α tuning on held-out data.

## Relevance

这篇文章正落在"极致性能优化下的可靠性"与"LLM 隐状态可解释性"两个兴趣的交叉点上：它把剪枝（激进的推理优化）引发的精度退化精确定性为终止行为失效而非能力丢失，并用隐状态差均值方向做免训练修复——既是压缩模型的可靠性诊断与缓解，也是表征几何/激活转向在推理模型上的扎实应用。其对"压缩改变推理动力学（循环偏好偏移）"的机制分析，对量化/剪枝后推理行为退化这一类问题都有方法论参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Could the termination-direction constraint be built into the pruning objective itself (e.g., a reconstruction loss weighted on positions near `</think>`), preventing reasoning divergence instead of patching it post hoc?
- Does the diagnosis transfer to RL-trained reasoning models (GRPO/RLVR-trained) and to semi-structured (2:4) sparsity, where weight updates are partially hardware-enforced?
- Is the termination direction causally tied to the `</think>` token's output circuit, and can mechanistic analysis (probing, SAEs) explain *why* pruning preserves the teacher-forced stop probability yet shifts free-running preference toward cyclic continuations (∆cyc)?
- Can TGAS be combined with Masked-LoRA at high sparsity — steering to fix termination while the adapter recovers capability — and would a jointly trained termination-aware adapter dominate both?
- How does the direction transfer across model families (e.g., steer DS-Llama-8B with DS-Qwen-7B's direction), and is there a shared "commitment" geometry across all distilled-from-R1 models?
- Does quantization-induced overthinking (Lotfi et al., 2026) share the same cyclic-preference signature ∆cyc, suggesting one common compression-pathology detector across pruning and quantization?
