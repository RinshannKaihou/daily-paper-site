Two's-complement sign extension plants variation error in high-significance conducting cells — differential encoding removes it.

CAFE: Conducting-Cell-Aware Adaptive Differential Encoding for Robust DNN Inference in FeFET-Based Compute-in-Memory ↗

Why this matters

Signed-number encoding becomes a searchable hardware-robustness variable, steered by a simulation-free metric — for anyone deploying quantized models on compute-in-memory accelerators.

Critical edge

The 87-point headline compares against a baseline collapsed to random chance (~1.4% on CIFAR-100); all results are behavioral-simulation only — no silicon measurement.

Methodology 方法

Not a re-training method: CAFE changes the physical number representation inside the FeFET array while preserving quantized arithmetic exactly.

  1. 01 · DEVICE

    Conducting cells carry all the error

    FeFET on/off ratio ≥ 10⁶ makes off-state leakage ≈ 0, so variation error comes almost entirely from conducting on-state cells (1×1 activation-bit × weight-bit events).

  2. 02 · MECHANISM

    Sign extension is the amplifier

    Two's complement turns near-zero negatives (post-GELU activations, Gaussian weights) into leading 1s in high bit planes; shift-and-add scales their errors by 2^(b+k).

  3. 03 · METRIC

    C²AM predicts the damage

    Bit-significance-weighted conducting-cell count, computed straight from encoded bit planes — no Monte Carlo. Tracks output KL and policy-level accuracy drop.

    validation stats ↓
  4. 04 · DEPLOY

    CAFE searches per-layer encodings

    W = W⁺ − W⁻, X = X⁺ − X⁻ unsigned planes, signs applied digitally. Type-wise sensitivity priors seed a 72-gene layer-wise NSGA-II; post-GA pruning masks small-magnitude weights in TC layers.

Error-envelope formalism

Each conducting cell's read current is perturbed as I'on = Ion(1+ϵ), ϵ ~ N(0, σ²) absorbing device/cycle/read variation. Reconstruction weights each bit-pair error by the signed coefficient s(b,k), giving |Δy| ≤ Ion · Σ|s| · Σc·|ϵ| — high-significance conducting events are exponentially amplified, which C²AM (Eq. 8) counts directly. Four per-layer encodings are on offer: TC/TC, Diff-W/TC, TC/Diff-A, Diff/Diff; full differential costs 2(B−1)/B per operand, hence the adaptive search maximizing variation accuracy while minimizing a conservative area-latency proxy C_norm.

Experimental setup

NeuroSim-derived behavioral bit-sliced CIM simulator: bit-plane decomposition, on-current variation, bit-line accumulation, 6-bit ADC, 64 parallel read rows; W8/A8 quantization; σ swept 0–0.20 (step 0.01), 20 seeds (2026–2045) with std reported. Workloads: ViT-Tiny/CIFAR-100 (main), ViT-Base/CIFAR-100, ViT-Tiny/ImageNet, Pythia-70M/160M on WikiText-103. Baselines: TC-W/TC-A, Offset-W/TC-A, CSD-W/TC-A, and the three partial/full differential mixes.

Encoding policy decides everything at σ ≥ 0.10 — and CAFE buys full-differential robustness at ~60–90% of its cost.

AGuided search pays

Seeding NSGA-II with type-wise candidates cuts the cost of the ≥88% target @ σ=0.15 from 1.73× to 1.56× (−10.3%, in 5 vs 20 generations); feasible ≥80% options grow 10 → 18; at σ=0.20 the direct GA finds no feasible solution at all.

BPruning lifts the frontier

Masking small-magnitude weights in residual TC-W layers adds +2.70% accuracy at σ=0.15 at unchanged normalized cost (ratio swept r ∈ {0…0.7}).

CPeripheral hardware relaxes too

Full differential reaches 87.8% with a 3-bit ADC where TC needs 6 bits to hit 51.0%; scaling parallel reads 8→768 rows (ViT-Base), Diff stays 93.4–93.5% while TC falls to ≤2.8% beyond 64 rows. Conducting events drop 8.2× overall, 41× in high-significance regions.

DTransfers across workloads

Pythia-70M/160M perplexity ratio stays 1.01–1.03 vs up to ~1.07 for TC at σ=0.20; ViT-Base/CIFAR-100 and ViT-Tiny/ImageNet trends favor differential modes; robustness ordering survives truncated-normal and lognormal perturbations.

Strengths 优点
  • Physically-motivated mechanism — explains why two's complement fails specifically in FeFET CIM (conducting-cell dominance + sign-extension amplification), not just that it fails.
  • C²AM is cheap and validated — simulation-free predictor with Pearson 0.98 sample-level / Spearman 0.80–0.85 policy-level correlation, honestly benchmarked against weaker activity metrics.
  • Well-engineered search — type-wise priors make the 4⁷² layer-wise space searchable in 5 GA generations; pruning targets exactly the residual error source (small negative TC codes).
  • Systematic evaluation — 20 seeds with std, σ sweeps into an extended stress region, non-Gaussian checks, 4 model/dataset combos, ADC-precision and parallel-read sweeps, multiple encoding baselines.
Limitations 局限
  • Simulation-only — NeuroSim behavioral level; no fabricated FeFET array or measured chip confirms the conducting-cell error model end-to-end.
  • Small models — ViT-Tiny/Base and Pythia ≤160M; no LLM-scale or detection/generation workloads; SwiGLU/GQA/RoPE activation statistics untested.
  • Cost proxy is partial — area-latency estimate, no place-and-route, and it omits the ADC savings the paper itself demonstrates (3-bit sufficiency).
  • Latency unanalyzed — differential activations double bit-serial cycles; streaming/real-time throughput cost is folded into C_norm, not separated.
  • i.i.d. Gaussian variation only — asymmetric drift, retention shift, temperature, and array-level gradients are not modeled.
  • No retraining baseline — orthogonality to noise-aware QAT/calibration is asserted, not quantified.

Questions for further exploration 待探索

  1. Does the layer-type sensitivity ordering (FFN2 ≫ FFN1 > V/Out > Q/K) persist in modern LLM blocks with SwiGLU/GQA/RoPE?
  2. Can C²AM cover correlated/systematic variation (array gradients, retention drift) where the i.i.d. error-envelope argument breaks down?
  3. Are representation-level and weight-level robustness gains additive when CAFE is combined with noise-aware fine-tuning?
  4. Would folding the demonstrated ADC relaxation (3-bit vs 6-bit) into the deployment objective enlarge the reported cost gains?
  5. Can differential overhead be amortized for weight-stationary, latency-sensitive serving (differential weights + clipped TC activations)?
  6. How does C²AM-guided deployment interact with mixed precision (W4A8), where sign extension eats more of the bit budget?
