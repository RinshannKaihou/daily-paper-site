Defines the trust boundary of aggressive numeric formats — exactly how much grid and approximation error calibration can absorb in 4-bit inference.

Critical edge

The hardware payoff is projected, not measured: Yosys measures 0.57–0.59× (not the 0.50× headline), energy is extrapolated from a 2022 CNN RTL study, and no end-to-end latency, throughput, or silicon validation exists.

Per-MAC savings also shrink in memory-bound decode.

Methodology · shift-add, end to end

All linear-projection weights (WQ/K/V/O, gate/up/down — 6.98 B MACs/token on Llama-3-8B, 80.8% in the FFN) move onto the √2 grid; activations and KV-cache stay uniform INT — so every MAC becomes a barrel shift plus one conditional add.

  1. 01

    Preprocess

    OSTQuant-style learned Hadamard rotation + per-channel scales (≈0.02% of MACs) redistributes channel outliers → near-Gaussian weights, 95% of mass within ~4 octaves.

  2. 02

    Quantize to the grid

    Q(w) = sgn(w)·s·2^(⌊k·log₂(|w|/s)⌉/k) with k=2 → half-octave spacing; an adaptive zero threshold τ≈0.841 gates ~20–22% of MACs off entirely.

  3. 03

    Calibrate on the real grid

    GPTQ quantizes onto the hardware’s 1-adder √2≈1.5 approximation (6.1% error) instead of exact √2 — the error is absorbed: PPL +0.01 vs +0.17 for naive post-hoc substitution.

  4. 04

    Integer processing element

    √2 weight grid × uniform INT activations/KV-cache = barrel shift + conditional add; zero codes skip the MAC. No FP16 accumulators, unlike PoT-PTQ / LogART.

  • Consistency: 21 configs (7 models × 3 bit-widths) — 20/21 within 1.0%p of uniform INT4; all 14 W4 runs at ΔPPL +0.05…+0.26; PoT overhead cut 67–94%.
  • vs LogART (ICLR’26): LogART wins weight-only W4A16 (6.52 vs 6.86 PPL) but collapses under post-hoc INT4 activations (PPL 3,903; 0-shot ≈34%) where FPoT holds 7.60 / 64.8% — not LogART’s official configuration.
  • Portable: grid swap into DuQuant costs only +0.05–0.34 PPL; Qwen2.5 & Mistral match or exceed uniform at W4A4KV4.

Strengths

  • +Clean decomposition: log grid only on static weights (where calibration absorbs error), uniform INT on dynamic activations/KV — the only fully integer PE in the PoT line.
  • +Approximate-grid quantization is a general lesson: hardware approximations belong inside the calibration grid, not patched on after.
  • +Theory that predicts: Gaussian-proxy optimum k*≈1.8 matches empirical occupancy across all 7 models; 4-bit specificity derived, not hidden.
  • +PTQ-grade ablations: preprocessing components, rounding mode, calibration size/domain, per-projection sensitivity, cross-framework portability.
  • +Candor: explicit near-lossless criterion (ΔPPL<+0.5, Δacc≥−1.0%p), seed std, concedes the weight-only loss to LogART.

Limitations

  • –No end-to-end hardware validation: analytical gate model + third-party CNN-RTL energy anchor; no wall-clock latency, throughput, or silicon.
  • –Gap-to-baseline, not a win: +0.05–0.42 PPL over uniform INT4 everywhere; Llama family −0.1…−0.8%p on 0-shot9.
  • –4-bit-specific: k=2 optimality fades as bit-width grows — at 8-bit the optimum moves to k*≈19.
  • –Scale & coupling: fp32 weight copies cap runs at 14B on one 80 GB GPU; AWQ / plain RTN untested; results coupled to GPTQ-style calibration.
  • –Narrow evaluation: zero-shot classification + WikiText-2 only — no generation quality, long-context, or chat evals; Qwen2.5-1.5B LAMBADA collapses under every 4-bit scheme.

Questions for further exploration

  1. Q1Does the fractional-grid argument survive at 3-bit weights, where the optimal k shifts qualitatively?
  2. Q2Can the approximate-grid trick absorb other hardware approximations — truncated adders, attention multipliers — as calibration-time distortions?
  3. Q3Does 0.50× CU survive memory-bound decode on a real FPGA/ASIC, and do the ~20% zero-gated MACs compound into speedup?
  4. Q4Why does FPoT beat uniform INT on Qwen2.5/Mistral but trail it on Llama — implicit magnitude pruning as a regularizer?
  5. Q5Fault sensitivity: one exponent-LSB flip changes a weight by √2 (~41%) — how do shift-add datapaths handle silent data corruption vs INT4 multipliers?
