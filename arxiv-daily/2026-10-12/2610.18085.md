> **一句话总结：** 本文证明 Transformer 中存在"精确对称对"（如 Q–K、V–O、SwiGLU 的 up–out 投影，按 (cA, c⁻¹B) 重缩放后函数完全不变但梯度尺度反向变化），并从理论上证明在共享学习率下没有任何标准一阶优化器（GD、AdamW、Lion 等）能保持两个函数等价的模型训练轨迹等价；据此提出 SAGGS（周期性地把每个对称对的两个矩阵的 Frobenius 范数精确均衡，函数保持不变）及其阻尼变体 Soft SAGGS，在 WikiText-103 上 101M 模型 imposed 失衡时降低 PPL 0.374±0.026，在 123.8M checkpoint 续训实验中完全恢复因对称编辑导致的 +3.78 PPL 退化（恢复到与对照组差距 0.02 PPL 以内）。

> **TL;DR:** Two Transformers that compute the same function can train differently: rescaling an exact symmetry pair such as (W_Q, W_K) to (cW_Q, c⁻¹W_K) preserves every prediction but inversely rescales the gradients, and the paper proves no standard block-local optimizer (gradient descent, AdamW, Lion, Shampoo, etc.) is equivariant to this under a shared learning rate — for AdamW even covariantly transformed moments fail (Theorem 2). The proposed SAGGS periodically equalizes the Frobenius norms of each exact pair without changing the function: on WikiText-103 at 101M with a deliberate λ=0.2 rescaling edit it cuts perplexity by 0.374±0.026 vs vanilla AdamW (22.904 vs 23.277, 7 paired seeds, sign-flip p=0.0078); in a 123.8M checkpoint-resume test an edit that changes validation loss by <7.4×10⁻⁵ at restart still costs +3.78±0.42 final PPL, which SAGGS fully recovers to within 0.02 PPL of control. The catch: with no imposed imbalance (λ=0), SAGGS is neutral to slightly negative (−0.025±0.034 PPL at 101M), so the demonstrated value is for edited/imported checkpoints and theory, not for ordinary from-scratch runs.

## 核心贡献

- **识别并系统梳理 Transformer 的"精确对称对"（exact symmetry pairs）**：注意力 Q–K 对、V–O 对、ReLU MLP 的 up–down 对、以及 SwiGLU 的 value 路径 (W_up, W_2)，都满足 (A,B)→(cA, c⁻¹B) 后前向输出逐点不变；GELU MLP 因不具备正齐次性而被排除。同一轨道（symmetry orbit）上的所有参数化函数等价，但由链式法则梯度反向缩放（∇A→c⁻¹∇A，∇B→c∇B）。
- **证明"无一标准优化器等变"定理（Theorem 5）**：在共享学习率下，任何块局部（block-local）一阶优化器——包括梯度下降、heavy-ball、AdaGrad、RMSProp、Adam/AdamW（含 ε=0 极限）、signSGD、Lion、Shampoo（ε=0 极限）——都无法保持两个轨道相关的模型在一步更新后仍等价。等变要求把缩小 c 倍的梯度变成放大 c 倍的更新，而所有标准规则都做不到。
- **AdamW 精确等变条件与"仅变换矩估计不够"（Theorem 2 / Example 3）**：AdamW 保持等变当且仅当学习率按坐标乘 s_i、ε 与权重衰减除 s_i；仅协变地变换一阶/二阶矩仍会失败，反例中一步后的函数误差为 η(c+c⁻¹−2)，随失衡因子 c 无界增长，且在按层深度 c_ℓ=e^(λℓ) 的编辑下误差随层数指数放大。
- **精确规范重启（Corollary 4）**：把权重和矩估计同时规范化到均衡代表点后，从同轨道任意两个 checkpoint 重启的训练轨迹逐字相同——无需等变优化器即可保证。
- **提出 SAGGS / Soft SAGGS**：每隔 T_bal 步把每个精确对称对移到两矩阵 Frobenius 范数相等的唯一均衡点（前向严格不变）；Soft 变体以 α（默认 0.5）的阻尼比例分步纠正，避免破坏已适应失衡尺度的优化器状态（尤其 Lion 的符号更新）。配对实验显示其能在人为失衡、Lion、checkpoint 续训等多种设置下恢复几乎全部退化。

## Key Contributions

- **Identifies the exact reparameterization symmetries of Transformers** (attention Q–K and V–O pairs, ReLU MLP up–down projections, and the linear value path W_up–W_2 of SwiGLU; GELU pairs excluded for lack of positive homogeneity). Any point on a symmetry orbit computes the same function, yet gradients scale reciprocally along the orbit (Dinh et al. 2017, Theorem 3), so the optimizer sees different gradient geometries for identical functions.
- **Proves a block-local obstruction theorem (Theorem 5)**: under a shared learning rate, no standard first-order optimizer — gradient descent, heavy-ball, AdaGrad, RMSProp, Adam/AdamW, signSGD, Lion, zero-stabilizer Shampoo — is orbit-equivariant, because equivariance would require turning c-times-smaller gradients into c-times-larger updates for a c-times-larger block.
- **Characterizes exact AdamW equivariance (Theorem 2) and shows moment covariance alone fails (Example 3)**: equivariance holds iff the learning rate is multiplied by s_i and ε and weight decay divided by s_i per coordinate; with correctly transformed moments but shared hyperparameters, a single Adam step from f(a,b)=ab leaves an unbounded function error η(c+c⁻¹−2), which under the depth schedule c_ℓ=e^(λℓ) grows as 2η[cosh(λℓ)−1], exponentially in layer depth.
- **Establishes exact canonical restart (Corollary 4)**: canonicalizing both weights and moments (multiplying each A block by ρ^(−1/2) and B by ρ^(1/2), ρ = ‖A‖_F/‖B‖_F, with moments transformed by the inverse and inverse-square maps) makes resumed runs from any two checkpoints on the same orbit bit-for-bit identical.
- **Proposes SAGGS and Soft SAGGS**: a periodic, provably function-preserving rebalancing that moves each exact pair to its unique Frobenius-balanced representative (geometric mean of the two norms), plus a damped variant (α=0.5 default) that applies a fraction of the correction per rebalance to avoid abrupt changes to optimizer state adapted to imbalanced scales.

## Methodology

The paper proceeds in three layers. **(1) Symmetry inventory (Sec. 2):** Table 1 catalogues which Transformer matrix pairs admit exact scalar rescalings (A,B)→(cA,c⁻¹B) that leave the forward pass bit-identical: Q–K (bilinear in attention logits), V–O (linear composition), ReLU MLP up–down (positive homogeneity), and SwiGLU's W_up–W_2 (linear in the value path); GELU up–down pairs are excluded. The gradient covariance identity then says ∇_{A'}L = c⁻¹∇_A L and ∇_{B'}L = c∇_B L, so equivalent parameterizations present the optimizer with reciprocal gradient scales.

**(2) Optimizer analysis (Sec. 3):** The orbit of balanced representatives is fixed as the pair state where ‖A‖_F = ‖B‖_F (Proposition 1 shows this point is unique, idempotent under the balancing map, and forward-preserving). Theorem 2 derives the exact necessary and sufficient conditions for AdamW trajectory equivariance under the state transformation (θ', m', v') = (Sθ, S⁻¹m, S⁻²v): per-coordinate learning rate ×s_i, ε and weight decay ÷s_i — violated by any shared learning rate. Corollary 4 shows canonicalizing weights and moments at restart makes two orbit-related runs identical thereafter. Theorem 5 generalizes: any block-local update rule whose direction is positively homogeneous of degree k≠−1 or bounded near zero (which covers every standard optimizer) cannot be equivariant; Corollary 6 quantifies the damage — relative updates of the enlarged vs shrunken block shift by c^(1+k) per rebalance interval (c² for gradient descent, c for Adam/Lion).

**(3) SAGGS (Sec. 4):** every T_bal steps (100 in the main experiments), each exact pair (A,B) with ratio ρ = ‖A‖_F/‖B‖_F is replaced by (ρ^(−1/2)A, ρ^(1/2)B) — an exact orbit move that equalizes both norms at their geometric mean and changes no prediction. Soft SAGGS replaces ρ^(1/2) by ρ^(α/2) (α∈(0,1]), shrinking the log-norm ratio by factor (1−α) per rebalance, which matters for sign-based optimizers like Lion whose EMA momentum buffer retains stale scales after a hard rebalance (a state-covariant variant that also rescales the buffer is ablated in Appendix F.5). Experiments impose imbalance via an "orbit edit" that rescales layer ℓ's pairs by c_ℓ = e^(λℓ) (at λ=0.2 the deepest of 24 layers sees ≈100× scaling), and evaluate from-scratch training (30K AdamW steps, GPT-2-style 101M ReLU Pre-LN on WikiText-103; 31M modern RMSNorm/RoPE/SwiGLU/GQA architecture with T5 SentencePiece tokenization), Lion training (15K steps), checkpoint-surgery resume (123.8M model trained 10K steps, optimizer reset, then four 5K-step branches: control / edited / edited+SAGGS / edited+Soft SAGGS with rebalance at restart and every 50 steps), public-checkpoint case studies (Pythia 410M drift; GPT-2 774M canonical restart), and a single-seed 1.2B run. Statistics use seven paired seeds with sign-flip permutation tests (minimum attainable p = 0.0078) and paired Cohen's d; seed pools were fixed before the experiments.

## Results & Highlights

- **101M WikiText-103, Q–K edit at λ=0.2, 7 paired seeds (Table 2):** SAGGS reduces validation PPL from 23.277 (Vanilla) to 22.904, a mean paired improvement of 0.374 ± 0.026 (paired Cohen's d = 14.21, one-sided sign-flip p = 0.0078, all 7 seeds positive). With SAGGS the edit stops mattering: 22.904 (edited) vs 22.906 (unedited). At λ=0 (no edit) the difference is −0.025 ± 0.034 PPL — slightly negative and non-significant (p = 0.9453).
- **31M modern-architecture replication (RMSNorm/RoPE/SwiGLU/GQA, T5 tokenizer), 7 paired seeds:** Soft SAGGS reduces PPL by 0.780 ± 0.040 at λ=0.2 (p = 0.0078) vs Vanilla, while without an edit the difference is +0.019 ± 0.024 — the benefit is not specific to the GPT-2 architecture or tokenizer.
- **Lion, 101M, all-pairs edit at λ=0.2, 7 paired seeds:** the edit more than doubles mean PPL from 24.15 (unedited Vanilla) to 58.32 (edited Vanilla), while edited runs with Soft SAGGS reach 23.86; the paired difference is +34.465 ± 25.170 PPL (p = 0.0078). At λ=0 the difference is −0.055 ± 0.232 (neutral).
- **123.8M checkpoint-surgery resume, 7 seeds (Tables 3/11):** an exact all-pairs edit that changes validation loss by less than 7.4×10⁻⁵ at restart still degrades final PPL from 20.84 ± 0.25 (control) to 24.62 ± 0.58 — a paired cost of +3.775 ± 0.416 (p = 0.0078); both SAGGS and Soft SAGGS recover to 20.85 ± 0.25, within 0.02 PPL of control. Keeping and transforming the AdamW moments instead of resetting them still leaves a significant gap of 0.058 ± 0.017 PPL on the 31M model, empirically confirming Theorem 2.
- **1.2B single-seed run (10K steps, 4×H100, WikiText-103):** an all-pairs λ=0.2 edit (deepest factor c₂₁ ≈ 66.7) blows up Vanilla PPL from 23.81 to 221.24 (a 9.3× increase; training loss plateaus above 2.7 vs 1.5 baseline; pre-clip mean gradient norm 27.8 vs 0.68, peaking near 4,700), while SAGGS with the same edit reaches PPL 23.70 — slightly better than the unedited baseline — with final max-abs pair log-ratios below 1.3×10⁻⁴.
- **Natural imbalance is real but modest:** in Pythia 410M, query and key norms drift apart by up to a factor of ≈6 in deep layers during ordinary pre-training; on GPT-2 774M fine-tuning, an exact Q–K edit causes the run to predict a different top-1 token than the canonicalized runs at 7.3% of held-out positions after just 200 updates, whereas canonical restart makes the runs identical.

## Authors & Credibility

Single-author paper by Bum Jun Kim (The University of Tokyo, WebLab; sole and corresponding author). This is an unusually careful piece of work for a solo paper: 55 pages with appendices A–K containing full proofs of Theorems 2/5 and Corollaries 4/6, a reference implementation of the rebalancing operator (Appendix D.3), implementation checks verifying exact forward preservation and the closed-form gradient law on both CPU and Intel XPU devices, and a NeurIPS-style reproducibility checklist. The statistics are above field standard: seven paired seeds, seed pools fixed before the experiments (Appendix D.2), one-sided sign-flip permutation tests with the minimum attainable p-value stated (0.0078 for n=7), paired Cohen's d reported, and an explicit pre-declared core contrast separated from secondary comparisons with an unadjusted-p caveat. Compute is disclosed in detail (4×H100 80GB DDP for the largest runs; Intel Data Center GPU Max 1550 "Aurora" tiles for the 31M–296.7M suite; ≈2.8 H100-hours per 1.2B configuration). An AI-use statement discloses generative AI assistance for writing, proof-checking, and code development. No public code release is mentioned in the main text beyond the reference implementation in an appendix listing.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** The core observations are partly old — reciprocal gradient scaling under function-preserving rescaling dates to Dinh et al. (2017) and Neyshabur et al. (2015), and weight balancing ideas exist (WeightNorm, Stock et al. 2019, Kunin et al. 2021) — but the paper's specific contributions are genuinely new: the exhaustive equivariance characterization for AdamW (Theorem 2), the block-local impossibility result covering all standard optimizers (Theorem 5), the exact canonical-restart corollary, and the identification of the SwiGLU value-path pair alongside Q–K/V–O. The framing "no standard optimizer is equivariant" is a strong, clean negative result.
- **Rigor:** High. Proofs are complete, the balancing map is verified to be exactly forward-preserving, and the empirical methodology (paired seeds, pre-registered seed pools, sign-flip tests) is more disciplined than most training-dynamics papers. The honesty about λ=0 results (rebalancing does not help, and is slightly negative at 101M) is commendable.
- **The headline practical claim is narrower than the abstract suggests:** essentially all positive gains are measured under a *deliberately imposed* imbalance (orbit edit at λ=0.2, up to ≈100× per-pair scaling), a synthetic stress test. The authors' own natural-drift measurement shows unedited 101M training ends with a Q–K log-norm ratio of only ~0.15 (vs ~3 after the edit), and SAGGS at λ=0 costs −0.025±0.034 PPL at 101M, +0.019±0.024 at 31M, and −0.055±0.232 with Lion — i.e., always-on SAGGS buys nothing measurable in ordinary from-scratch runs within their budgets. The Pythia-410M ~6× drift observation is correlational; no experiment shows SAGGS improves training of a released or naturally-drifting model. The practical pitch should therefore be "recovery for edited/imported/checkpoint-surgery workflows," which the 123.8M experiment supports well.
- **Scale and scope limits:** multi-seed results stop at 123.8M–296.7M parameters; the 1.2B result is single-seed over only 10K steps. All experiments are language modeling (WikiText-103 plus one OpenWebText replication) — no downstream-task evaluation, no RLHF/post-training settings, no MoE or state-space blocks (acknowledged in Appendix B). The paper's own Limitations appendix concedes the empirical scope is a selected set of architectures, optimizers, and LM corpora.
- **Reproducibility:** Good on paper (fixed seeds, disclosed hardware, reference implementation, public datasets), though no public code repository is cited in the main text, which will slow independent verification. The single-author status means less internal redundancy for such a large experimental suite.
- **Red flags:** minor — the abstract leads with the λ=0.2 gains without immediately flagging that they require an adversarial self-inflicted edit; the Lion headline delta (+34.5 PPL) is driven by the pathological 58.32 PPL of the edited Vanilla baseline with huge variance (±25.17), making the mean delta less informative than the medians.

## Strengths

- A crisp, fully proven negative result (Theorem 5) that unifies gradient descent, AdamW, Lion, AdaGrad/RMSProp, and Shampoo under one block-local impossibility argument, with Corollary 6 quantifying exactly how far each homogeneity class of optimizers distorts relative updates (c² vs c).
- The fix is provably safe: every SAGGS rebalance is an exact orbit move, so it cannot damage the model's function — the method only changes where training continues from, and the 123.8M surgery experiment shows it recovers +3.78 PPL of edit-induced degradation to within 0.02 PPL of control.
- Exact canonical restart (Corollary 4) is a practical, optimizer-agnostic recipe for anyone resuming from edited, merged, pruned, or imported checkpoints: canonicalize weights and moments once, and runs become trajectory-identical.
- Exceptional methodological hygiene: paired-seed design, seed pools fixed in advance, minimum attainable p-values stated, implementation checks on two hardware backends, and a dedicated appendix on optimizer-state handling (reset vs transformed vs stale moments).

## Limitations

- No demonstrated benefit for ordinary training without an imposed edit: at λ=0, SAGGS is statistically indistinguishable from Vanilla (−0.025±0.034 PPL at 101M) or slightly positive only in one replication (+0.019±0.024 at 31M); natural pair drift in the authors' own runs (Q–K log-ratio ≈0.15) appears too small to matter.
- The evidence for natural, real-world imbalance is observational only (Pythia 410M norm drift up to ~6×); there is no intervention showing that rebalancing improves the continued pre-training or fine-tuning of an actually deployed checkpoint family.
- Scale ceiling: multi-seed experiments top out at 123.8M–296.7M; the 1.2B SwiGLU run is a single seed with a 10K-step budget, so long-horizon effects at frontier scale remain untested.
- Task scope is language-model perplexity only (WikiText-103, OpenWebText); no downstream benchmarks, no vision/ViT fine-tuning beyond a small case study, no RL post-training or MoE/state-space architectures.
- Soft SAGGS's damping coefficient α=0.5 and the rebalancing interval (100 steps; 50 in resume) are fixed by convention rather than swept systematically in the main text, and the interplay with weight decay and gradient clipping is analyzed only in appendices.

## Relevance

与"LLM Training Stability & Observability"高度相关：论文刻画了预训练/续训中一类由参数化对称性引起的隐性训练不稳定（checkpoint 看似无损编辑却在续训后 PPL 恶化 +3.78），并给出可证明不改变前向的检测（范数失衡度量）与恢复手段（SAGGS 均衡 + 规范重启），直接服务于 checkpoint 可靠性、训练恢复与诊断这一核心关注点。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does natural pair drift (e.g., Pythia's ~6× Q–K norm ratio) actually degrade continued pre-training or fine-tuning of released models, and would periodic SAGGS improve a real mid-training recovery or checkpoint-merge/rescale workflow rather than a synthetic λ=0.2 edit?
- How does SAGGS interact with µP-style width-scaled hyperparameters, tensor-parallel sharding (where a pair's two matrices may live on different ranks), and QAT/quantization pipelines that also rescale weight blocks?
- Can the equivariance conditions of Theorem 2 be approximated in practice by per-coordinate learning-rate rescaling (an "orbit-equivariant AdamW" using measured norm ratios) instead of weight rebalancing, and would that outperform SAGGS on the checkpoint-resume benchmark?
- Does pair-scale imbalance matter in RL post-training (PPO/GRPO value-policy heads, LoRA adapters whose A/B factors are exactly such a pair), where optimizer restarts and merged adapters are routine?
- What happens over longer horizons and larger scales — does the 1.2B single-seed, 10K-step protection hold across a 100B-token run, and does the λ=0 neutrality persist or turn positive as natural drift accumulates?
