> **一句话总结：** 本文系统测量了五种免训练的宽松（有损）投机解码验证规则（共 360 个实验单元），发现宽松验证虽然把每轮平均接受的 draft token 数提升 1.34 倍，但生成完整度被拉长 1.54 倍——多出来的文本几乎全是低概率 token 触发的冗余思考轨迹，端到端轮数与时延收益大都被抵消甚至反转，且在激进度高时准确率大幅下降；作者因此主张"接受长度"不是有效的速度度量，必须同时报告完成长度与验证轮数。

> **TL;DR:** A University of Waterloo team evaluates five training-free relaxed (lossy) speculative decoding rules (CACTUS, mentored decoding, reducible fuzzy speculative decoding, and two speculative cascade rules) across 360 target-drafter-dataset cells — four targets (GPT-OSS-20B, Qwen3-8B, Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B) with eight dedicated drafters (EAGLE-3/EAGLE-1/P-EAGLE/DSPark) and three standalone SLMs, on six benchmarks (GSM8K, AIME24, HumanEval, LiveCodeBench, MT-Bench, LongBench-v2) against lossless speculative decoding as the baseline. At each rule's most relaxed setting, accepted tokens per round rise 1.34× on average but completions grow 1.54×, so the promised speedup mostly evaporates (verifier rounds only drop to 0.93× median, and 114/360 cells need MORE rounds); accuracy drops in 198/300 graded cells (e.g., cactus on R1-Distill-Llama-8B/LiveCodeBench: rounds cut to 0.44× but accuracy falls 0.58 → 0.02). The extra tokens are reasoning-trace text the target model rates as unlikely (median target probability 0.01–0.09 for relaxed-only tokens vs 0.78–0.93 for shared ones). Forcing the reasoning trace to close near the generation budget recovers correct answers from 126 to 439 across 1,132 affected runs — evidence the added length is waste, not useful thinking.

## 核心贡献

- **识别并命名"长度膨胀"（length inflation）现象**：宽松验证每轮接受更多 draft token，但同时把生成内容（主要是推理模型的思考轨迹）拉得更长，导致端到端时间收益被抵消甚至反转——该效应在接受长度和 tokens/s 这两个常用指标下完全不可见。
- **跨方法、跨基准、跨目标、跨 drafter 的 360 单元系统测量**：5 种免训练宽松验证规则 × 6 个基准（GSM8K、AIME24、HumanEval、LiveCodeBench、MT-Bench、LongBench-v2）× 12 个目标-drafter 对（4 个目标模型配 8 个专用 drafter 与 3 个独立小模型），每个单元都带 95% 配对 bootstrap 置信区间，同时报告完成长度、验证轮数、实测时间与任务准确率。
- **提出在匹配接受长度（ℓ̄）而非匹配 α 下比较方法的协议**，并给出 token 效率记账框架：宽松规则只有在 (ℓ̄relax+1)/(ℓ̄strict+1) > λ 时才省时间，且任何放松都无法突破 λ = (Ndraft+1)/(ℓ̄strict+1) 的理论上限。
- **定位额外长度的来源与性质**：宽松规则专门放进目标模型认为不可能的 token（中位目标概率 0.01–0.09），这些 token 携带的额外文本 90% 以上进入思考轨迹；小学数学上多出的 token 多在已写出答案之后，竞赛数学上则推迟答案出现——两种情况都没有换来准确率。
- **给出简单有效的缓解措施**：在生成预算附近强制闭合思考轨迹，可在 1,132 个受影响运行中把正确数从 126 恢复到 439（+335/−22），证明膨胀的长度是浪费。

## Key Contributions

- Identifies and names **length inflation**: relaxed verification accepts more draft tokens per round but lengthens completions (chiefly reasoning traces), offsetting or inverting end-to-end speed gains — an effect invisible to both accepted-tokens-per-round and tokens-per-second metrics.
- A **360-cell cross-method measurement** (5 training-free rules × 6 benchmarks × 12 target-drafter pairs, vs lossless verification on identical prompts) with 95% paired bootstrap intervals on every cell, reporting completion length, verifier rounds, wall-clock time, and accuracy.
- A **protocol comparing methods at matched mean accepted length ℓ̄ rather than matched α** (since α means something different in every method), plus a token-efficiency accounting: a relaxed rule saves time only if its acceptance gain exceeds its length inflation, and no relaxation can beat λ = (Ndraft+1)/(ℓ̄strict+1).
- **Traces the inflation to its source**: relaxation admits draft tokens the target rates as unlikely (relaxed-only tokens are 7–24% of committed drafts, median target probability 0.01–0.09 vs 0.78–0.93 for tokens both rules accept), and on reasoning models >90% of the extra characters land in the thinking trace (GPT-OSS-20B analysis channel: ≥92% of extra characters in 25/30 primary cells).
- **A simple mitigation with evidence the length is waste**: forcing the reasoning trace to close near the generation budget (Muennighoff et al. budget forcing) raises correct answers from 126 to 439 over the 1,132 runs it touches (335 fixed, 22 broken), across all five rules on both primary pairs.

## Methodology

The paper frames every training-free speculative decoding method as a choice of relaxed target distributions (π_rej, π_res, π_bonus) substituted for the target distribution p at up to three points inside the standard verify-and-correct round; strict (lossless) verification is the special case π = p. Five published rules are reimplemented as patches to vLLM 0.26.0's rejection sampler (each patch ships with closed-form unit tests): CACTUS and mentored decoding (divergence-budgeted rules that boost the drafted token while bounding distance from p), reducible fuzzy speculative decoding (r_fuzzy), and the two speculative cascade rules (spec_casc_opt, spec_casc_tok — the latter is not even strict at α = 0 and is the only rule that can reject what lossless would accept).

Because α is incomparable across methods, a pilot fixes three target values of ℓ̄ and runs each full dataset at the nearest α; tables report each method at its most relaxed tested setting, with robustness checks at the gentlest and best-accuracy-preserving settings. The testbed spans twelve target-drafter pairs: GPT-OSS-20B (two EAGLE-3 heads), Qwen3-8B (EAGLE-3, P-EAGLE, DSpark, standalone Qwen3-0.6B/1.7B), Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct (EAGLE-3, EAGLE-1, Llama-3.2-1B), and DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B (EAGLE-3, Llama-3.2-1B). Everything runs on a single NVIDIA H100, Ndraft = 6 (within 10% of the fastest lossless draft length), sampled drafts, temperature 1.0, top-p 1.0, seed 0. Six datasets cover math (GSM8K, AIME24), code (HumanEval, LiveCodeBench), chat (MT-Bench), and long-context QA (LongBench-v2); budget-exhausted runs are scored wrong. Metrics: ℓ̄ (accepted length), L̄ (completion length), R (verifier rounds), client-side wall-clock at batch size 1, and accuracy — every ratio to lossless computed on identical cases with 10,000-resample paired bootstrap intervals. A key derived quantity is the length-corrected speedup S_relax ≈ (L̄_strict/L̄_relax)·S, and the hardware-free criterion (ℓ̄relax+1)/(ℓ̄strict+1) > λ. Run-log analysis on the two primary pairs splits thinking trace vs answer, and traces per-token target probabilities for both rules' verdicts; a lossless control (raising temperature to 1.2/1.5) confirms that committing low-probability tokens alone reproduces the inflation.

## Results & Highlights

- **Headline trade-off**: at each rule's most relaxed setting, relaxed verification accepts on average **1.34× as many tokens per round but produces 1.54× longer completions**; over all 360 cells λ (length inflation) has median 1.08 / mean 1.54, with completions lengthened in 248/360 cells and the 95% interval above 1 in 172.
- **Rounds barely improve**: verifier-round ratio R/R* has median 0.93 and mean 1.10, and **114/360 cells need MORE rounds than lossless** (67 significantly); the round ratio tracks length, not acceptance (Spearman ρ = 0.78 with λ, only −0.12 with acceptance gain), and the length-corrected model of Eq. (5) predicts rounds from measured length within 2% at the median.
- **Accuracy collapses where rounds are cut deepest**: relaxation loses accuracy in 198/300 graded cells; cactus cuts rounds to 0.44× on R1-Distill-Llama-8B/LiveCodeBench while accuracy falls **0.58 → 0.02**, and to 0.55× on Qwen3-8B/AIME24 while accuracy falls **0.70 → 0.23**; mean accuracy losses are 0.19 (cactus), 0.14 (spec_casc_opt), 0.21 (r_fuzzy).
- **The one rule that keeps accuracy also relaxes least**: spec_casc_tok loses only 0.005 on average over its 60 graded cells with mean λ 0.96 (no growth) and needs 0.86× the rounds of lossless — but its acceptance gain is the smallest (1.11×), supporting "choose which tokens to admit" over "accept more".
- **Inflation is a drafter property, not a target property**: Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct inflates 3.30× on average with its EAGLE-3 head (cactus multiplies its lossless answers by 4.0–13.2×) but only 1.10× with Llama-3.2-1B; Qwen3-8B inflates 1.18× with EAGLE-3 vs 0.90× (deflation) with Qwen3-1.7B.
- **The extra text is thinking, not answer**: GPT-OSS-20B's analysis channel receives ≥92% of extra characters in 25/30 primary cells; Qwen3-8B puts >90% of extra characters inside <think> blocks at the median. Relaxed-only committed tokens have median target probability **0.01–0.09 vs 0.78–0.93** for tokens both rules accept (mean target rank 37–234).
- **Lossless control confirms the mechanism**: raising temperature from 1.0 to 1.2 lengthens GPT-OSS-20B GSM8K completions by 1.27×, and T = 1.5 inflates GSM8K by 3.9× with accuracy 0.31 — committing low-probability tokens alone reproduces inflation.
- **Budget forcing recovers answers**: forcing the reasoning trace closed near the generation budget raises correct answers from **126 to 439 over 1,132 affected runs** (+335/−22); on GSM8K, 50% (GPT-OSS-20B) and 82% (Qwen3-8B) of extra tokens come AFTER the reference answer is already written, while on AIME24 the answer arrives later (median token 3,865 vs 1,721) with ≥59% of extra tokens preceding it.
- **Inflation is understated, not an artifact**: relaxed runs hit the generation budget more often (208/360 cells; median 11% vs 4% under lossless), and keeping only runs that finished under both rules moves λ by ≤0.1 in 45/60 primary cells.

## Authors & Credibility

The paper is by Chiatzen Wang, Bill Xu, Haochen Gu, and Hongyang Zhang of the University of Waterloo (Hongyang Zhang is a faculty member there working on trustworthy/efficient ML). The work is an empirical measurement study, not a method pitch: it releases code, vLLM patches, prompts, and per-run outputs via an anonymized repository, ships closed-form unit tests for each relaxed-acceptance patch, and attaches 95% paired bootstrap intervals to every one of the 360 cells. The framing directly audits recently published methods (CACTUS, mentored decoding, r_fuzzy, speculative cascades), which lends the study practical relevance since lossy speculative decoding is being adopted in production inference stacks. The limitations section is unusually frank (best-effort ℓ̄ matching, budget censoring, one run per case off the primary pairs, single serving stack and sampler, reimplementations not checked against the original authors' code).

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: The core observation — that relaxed verification changes the output length distribution and accepted-tokens-per-round is therefore not a speed proxy — is a genuinely important measurement correction, and the systematic 360-cell sweep with matched-ℓ̄ protocol goes well beyond the "rambling responses" remarks in Xia et al. (2026) and the quality-only analysis of Wang et al. (2026a). However, the ingredients are not new: the length-corrected accounting is essentially Xia et al.'s model validated empirically, and budget forcing is imported from Muennighoff et al. (2025). The contribution is rigor and evidence, not a new algorithm.
- **Rigor**: Strong on uncertainty (paired bootstrap on identical cases, robustness checks at gentlest and accuracy-preserving settings, seed repetition on primary pairs, a second-server timing control, and a temperature-control experiment that isolates the low-probability-token mechanism). Weaknesses are acknowledged: each case runs once per arm outside the primary pairs, so AIME24 (30 cases) and MT-Bench (80 cases) have wide intervals; timing is client-side at batch size 1 and noisy because the first cases of each benchmark ran on a fresh server process. The reimplementations in vLLM's rejection sampler are checked only at the strict limit, not against the original authors' code — a real concern given two of the drafters run through a second sampler path where cactus and spec_casc_tok modify only the acceptance test (a partial-rule deviation disclosed in a footnote).
- **Reproducibility**: Good — anonymized code/patches/prompts/per-run outputs, exact checkpoints named (nebius and RedHatAI EAGLE-3 heads on the Hugging Face Hub), fixed Ndraft = 6, seed 0, vLLM 0.26.0. But the headline "most relaxed setting" presentation could bias the comparison against the relaxed methods; Sections 3.3/3.6 (median λ still 1.07 at the gentlest setting) partially answer this, and the sampler-dependence finding (three rules stop inflating on Qwen3-8B at temperature 0.6 while spec_casc_opt still inflates 1.5×) shows conclusions are conditional on the sampler.
- **Significance**: High for practitioners — it explains why lossy speculative decoding underdelivers in production and prescribes concrete reporting requirements (length + rounds alongside acceptance). The GSM8K budget quirk (2,048 tokens is so tight that 25% of Qwen3-8B's *lossless* runs already hit it) is a confound the authors handle honestly via the finished-runs-only check.
- **Red flags**: The paper does not explain *why* an unlikely token makes a reasoning model think longer (mechanism is correlational); draft length is swept only under lossless verification; the ensemble rule and typical acceptance are excluded; and there is an inherent tension in auditing methods with reimplementations rather than authors' code.

## Strengths

- Reframes the evaluation standard for an actively shipping family of inference optimizations, with a clean theoretical criterion ((ℓ̄relax+1)/(ℓ̄strict+1) > λ) that is trivially checkable by future papers.
- Exceptional measurement breadth: 360 cells, five rules, four targets, eleven drafters, six benchmarks, with per-cell bootstrap intervals — far beyond any prior evaluation of these methods.
- The causal chain is well triangulated: relaxed-only tokens have 100× lower target probability, a pure temperature increase on lossless decoding reproduces the inflation, and the recovery experiment (budget forcing flipping 335 wrong runs to correct) shows the extra length is waste rather than useful thinking.
- Actionable, honest guidance: spec_casc_tok (trust the target more, relax least) is the only rule that keeps accuracy and actually reduces rounds (0.86×); the paper says so plainly instead of burying it.

## Limitations

- Methods are compared at different ℓ̄ because no single value is reachable by all five rules; the "most relaxed setting" convention maximizes the observed damage even though gentle settings also inflate.
- Lengths are right-censored by generation budgets (median 11% of relaxed runs vs 4% lossless hit the cap), so inflation is understated and part of the accuracy loss is just unfinished runs.
- One run per case outside the primary pairs; AIME24 (30 cases) and MT-Bench (80 cases) intervals are wide, forcing reliance on cross-dataset recurrence for interpretation.
- Single serving stack (vLLM 0.26.0), single main sampler (temperature 1.0, top-p 1.0), Ndraft fixed at 6; the rules are reimplementations verified only at the strict limit, and two drafters' sampler path applies partial rules for two methods.
- MT-Bench quality is one judge's first-turn score, not an accuracy; no mechanistic explanation of why low-probability tokens prolong reasoning; per-token guards that force lossless verification failed to shorten completions beyond tuning cases, so the proposed mitigation only acts at the whole-run level.

## Relevance

对关注 LLM 推理加速与投机解码的用户高度相关：本文指出有损投机解码常用的"接受长度/tokens per second"指标会系统性高估加速效果，并给出评估宽松验证规则时必须同时报告完成长度与验证轮数的实践准则——这直接关系到推理服务（vLLM/SGLang/TensorRT-LLM 生态）中是否以及如何部署 lossy speculative decoding 的决策。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Why exactly does committing a low-target-probability token cause a reasoning model to extend its thinking trace rather than terminate — is it a distribution-shift effect on subsequent context, and can it be predicted from the token's rank alone?
- Could a length-aware relaxation rule (e.g., α scheduled down as the reasoning trace grows, or relaxation disabled inside <think> blocks) capture acceptance gains without inflation?
- Does the drafter-dependence finding generalize: do training-time alignment losses on dedicated drafters (EAGLE heads) systematically inflate more than standalone SLMs because they over-concentrate mass on the drafted token?
- How do the conclusions change at realistic serving conditions — batch size > 1, temperature 0.6-style recommended samplers for all models, and Ndraft swept jointly with α?
- Since spec_casc_tok keeps accuracy while cutting rounds to 0.86×, what is its ceiling when combined with a better trusted-top-set schedule, and does it stack with the budget-forcing mitigation on reasoning models?
