The optimizer-switching “cost” across LLM training stages essentially doesn’t exist — apparent penalties are learning-rate artifacts, not mismatch.

从零预训练 1.2B Llama-3 模型，并在预训练 / CPT / SFT 三个阶段按每步概率连续插值 AdamW 与 Muon 更新比例后发现：所谓「换优化器代价」基本不存在——最终性能几乎完全由各阶段优化器的独立边际效应解释，表面切换代价源自学习率错配。

The Ephemeral Cost of Switching Optimizers Across LLM Training Stages ↗

Xinyu Zhao, Davis Wertheimer, Ashutosh Sharma, et al. MIT · MIT-IBM Watson AI Lab 2026-10-15

  • Muon
  • AdamW

Key metric · SFT swap gain

+0.0pts

Muon-SFT over AdamW-SFT on AdamW-pretrained 1.2B models — six-benchmark average 17.2 ± 0.6 vs 15.8 ± 0.4 at 1× Chinchilla. Across the full PT×SFT grid, mismatch interaction residuals stay noise-level (−0.6…+0.3) versus a −0.9…+0.7 SFT main effect.

Why this matters

Post-training teams can pick the SFT optimizer independently of the pretraining optimizer — apparent “switching costs” usually trace to learning-rate misconfiguration.

与「LLM 训练稳定性与超参迁移」方向直接相关：跨阶段训练中表面上的切换代价往往源自学习率错配，而非优化器本身。

⚠ Critical edge — read before citing

The controlled 7×7 mismatch grid exists only at 1.2B / 1× Chinchilla (corners-only at 8×, nothing beyond 14B), interaction terms carry no significance tests, and optimizer identity stays bundled with inherited, unswept hyperparameters.

Muon better in 4 of 5 settings; only Qwen3-8B reverses. All gaps except Llama2-7B sit within run-to-run variation.

  • CPT “switching cost” is an LR artifact: the matched-optimizer advantage at a fixed 5e-5 learning rate disappears entirely after per-setting LR tuning (2.6592 vs 2.6099, monotone in Muon share).
  • Matching ≠ performance: matched optimizers forget less (+0.49 vs +0.73) yet earn no final benchmark benefit — the best cell is Muon/Muon (18.8) on merit, not on match.
  • Counter-evidence kept honest: MMLU dips −1.1 (23.8 → 22.7) under Muon-SFT at 1× on AdamW-PT; “more Muon in SFT is better” is a trend, not a law.

03 · Strengths

§Why the evidence holds

  • Per-step probabilistic optimizer mixture with RMS-matched updates — a clean, reusable instrument for mismatch studies.
  • Two-way decomposition cleanly separates “which optimizer is better” from “does matching matter”.
  • Per-setting LR tuning overturns the paper’s own fixed-LR CPT finding — self-correcting experimental hygiene.
  • Honest reporting: full tables with standard deviations; negative results (Qwen3-8B, the MMLU dip) shown, not buried.
  • Scale triangulation: two Chinchilla ratios from scratch plus five open-weight models up to 14B.

04 · Limitations

§Where the claim thins out

  • Controlled mixed-optimizer grids only at 1.2B; 8× Chinchilla has pure corners only; nothing >14B, no MoE, no Muon-pretrained open checkpoints.
  • SFT and CPT only — RLHF / DPO / on-policy RL, where implicit-bias arguments may matter more, are out of scope.
  • No formal significance test of interaction terms against seed variance; the decomposition assumes additivity.
  • Optimizer identity is bundled with inherited hyperparameters (weight decay 0.2 vs 0.1, momentum, β2) — some “optimizer effect” may be recipe effect.
  • Absolute averages at 1.2B are low (15.8–18.8), leaving floor-effect headroom; forgetting measured only as val-loss increase.

05 · Questions for further exploration

§What this paper opens up

  1. Does the no-mismatch conclusion survive RLHF / DPO / on-policy RL post-training, where optimizer implicit bias meets preference optimization?
  2. Does the flat CPT interaction persist at 70B+ or for MoE models, where Muon’s spectral updates meet routed expert matrices?
  3. Can the matched-optimizer forgetting reduction be bought more cheaply — lower LR or KL anchors during SFT — decoupling forgetting control from optimizer choice?
  4. Is the MMLU dip under Muon-SFT (23.8 → 22.7 at 1×) real knowledge degradation or seed noise — and does it persist under MMLU-Pro / GPQA?
  5. How do the findings transfer to Muon-pretrained open checkpoints (e.g. Moonshot models) post-trained with AdamW — the reverse direction left untested?
  6. Could annealing p over training itself become a recipe, given intermediate mixtures pretrain to lower C4-EN loss than pure AdamW?
