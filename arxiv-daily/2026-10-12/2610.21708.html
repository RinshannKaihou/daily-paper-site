Why this matters

A transferable playbook for agent failures: decodable ≠ causal, scaffold fixes precede weight fixes, and oversight logs become localization data.

⚠ Critical edge

The deliverable is a map, not a deployable method — a toy testbed, claims confined to a 5-of-13 eligibility window, and a simulated-overseer metric that swings 0.45–0.95 across overseer seeds.

01Methodology — distilled

STAGE 1 Collect Twin-pair scenarios in a controlled "clarify" testbed; blocking invariants catch harness bugs.
STAGE 2 Probe Behavior + belief probes at the decision token — then distrust them (canaries, content baseline).
STAGE 3 Patch One coordinate, both directions, doses frozen on training episodes; random / style / belief controls.
STAGE 4 Edit Gated runtime steering vs causal-band LoRA vs behavior cloning — six criteria fixed in advance.
  • Testbed: one-line tasks over 3-item workspaces; every scenario is a twin pair (identical instruction, workspace differs) so ambiguity ground truth is exact — 300 pairs/seed, 3 seeds per model.
  • Labels: a deterministic rule-based overseer issues interventions (validated by blind human annotation, κ = 0.90–0.97); a separate budgeted evaluation overseer defines burden B = (W + 2I)/N and the headline metric IRR.
  • Eligibility: only 5 of 13 screened families produce the ask-vs-act contrast the method needs — all "five for five" claims live inside that window.
  • Discipline: predictions written before outcomes, frozen site/dose selection, a stopping rule for falsified predictors, an external adversarial audit; code, logs, and adapters released (GitHub + Zenodo).

02Two signatures of over-asking

H-caution · 4 of 5 families

The model demonstrably knows the request is unambiguous — the belief is decodable but causally inert, near-orthogonal to the decision axis. It asks anyway. The intuitive fix ("improve the ambiguity detector") targets a component that is not broken.

H-misrep · Phi-4 only (n = 24)

Over-asks track genuinely different internal world-representations (mean U-score −12 vs −41 for acted). The only calibrated over-asker — an entire half of the taxonomy rests on this one small-n family.

Scaffold-before-mechanism guardrail: the re-asking failure (45–67%) collapses to exactly zero once the scaffold shows the agent its own prior question — yet the behavior-cloning edit later re-creates it through the weights. Surface behavior underdetermines the fix level.

04Strengths

  • Reframing executed end-to-end: intervention log as localization dataset — labels → probes → causal patch → runtime steering → weight edit → human validation.
  • Disciplined control stack: random- and style-direction controls under the identical procedure, belief-axis patching in both directions, full-generation validation, frozen site/dose selection — removes the classic forking-paths failure.
  • Negative results as first-class citizens: two pre-written rules falsified with the stopping rule honored; scaffold-level fix shown to precede mechanism.
  • Metric matches the deployment question (did the overseer intervene less?), not probe accuracy.
  • Knowing-doing-gap split is directly actionable for four of five families.

05Limitations

  • External validity: a deliberately minimal testbed — nothing demonstrates transfer to naturalistic agent traces; greedy decoding throughout.
  • Narrow eligibility: 5 of 13 families; small instruct models overwhelmingly sit at a degenerate always-ask/never-ask corner.
  • IRR is a simulated proxy: stochastic overseer trust dynamics, seed-1 point estimate ranges 0.45–0.95 across 1,000 overseer seeds; never directly human-validated.
  • Fragile base for half the taxonomy: H-misrep rests on one family with n = 24 test over-asks; the single stable editing win is one family (Qwen).
  • Adversarial robustness untested; predictions-before-outcomes is self-attested in private notes, not externally registered.

06Questions for further exploration

  1. Does the H-caution / H-misrep split survive on naturalistic multi-turn traces with genuinely noisy ambiguity — and on models above 14B?
  2. What property actually determines the localized-editing regime? Downstream re-absorption capacity is the candidate — how to measure it pre-hoc?
  3. Can the overseer-seed-sensitive burden metric (0.45–0.95 spread) be replaced by a human-validated or deterministic proxy without losing IRR's operational meaning?
  4. Does gated steering stay safe under adversarial inputs crafted to fool the belief probe, and how does the gate degrade under distribution shift?
  5. Do intervention labels localize other failure types (correction-propagation, wrong-object actions) that require multi-step tasks?
  6. Would clamping the six non-shared SAE reader features alone separate readable-but-inert from causal content at feature resolution?
