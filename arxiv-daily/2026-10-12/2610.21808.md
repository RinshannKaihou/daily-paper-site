> **一句话总结：** RoleQ 提出"按功能角色放置量化 scale"的结构性规则——从残差流读取的向量（Q/K/V/gate/in 的行）每输出单元一个 scale、向残差流写入的向量（O/out 的列）每输入单元一个 scale——无需搜索、校准或存储轴选择，配合数据自由旋转、逐向量精确求解的三值 GPTQ 和冻结码字的 scale 蒸馏，在 3–4 个 A100 小时内把 Qwen3-8B-Base 压到 1.59 bpw（WikiText2 困惑度 11.33、MMLU 47.6，而 Qronos/PT²-LLM/QTEA 的 MMLU 仅 25.9–34.8、接近 25% 随机水平），再叠加 Fisher 显著性引导的稀疏第二三值平面（+0.57 bpw）可把 MMLU 恢复到 62.8（约收回一半损失），全面领先同档位后训练三值方法。

> **TL;DR:** A Harvard/Georgia Tech team introduces RoleQ, a structural scale-placement rule for quantization: vectors that read from the residual stream get one scale per output unit, writers get one per input unit — no axis search, calibration, or stored flags. Combined with a data-free learned rotation, ternary GPTQ with an exact per-vector fit, and brief scale distillation (2 h on one A100 at 8B), RoleQ-T converts Qwen3-8B-Base to 1.59 bits/weight at 11.33 WikiText2 perplexity / 60.3 zero-shot / 47.6 MMLU, beating Qronos (25.9 MMLU at 1.591 bpw), PT²-LLM (26.5 at 1.835 bpw) and QTEA (34.8 at 2.281 bpw) — knowledge retention is where the gap is widest. Optional Fisher-saliency second ternary planes (+0.57 bpw → 2.16 bpw) lift MMLU to 62.8 (vs 76.8 fp16), with Fisher selection beating random placement by 6.2 MMLU points and Hessian×weight by 9.0 at matched bits. Honest negative result: on Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct at 2 bpw, QTIP's trellis codes still lead by 8.9 MMLU. Cost is a few GPU-hours vs CAT-Q's 8–64 A100-hours of learned ternarization.

## 核心贡献

- **按功能角色对齐的 scale 放置规则（RoleQ）**：基于 Transformer 机制解释视角（残差流为共享通信通道），把量化 scale 分配给功能向量而非权重矩阵布局——读向量（W_Q、W_K、W_V、W_gate、W_in 的行）按输出单元放置，写向量（W_O、W_out 的列）按输入单元放置。规则是结构性的：无需搜索、无需校准数据、无需存储逐层轴标志，且同时适用于整数网格和三值网格。在 14B 三值设置下，仅换放置方式就把 MMLU 从 25.4（随机水平）提升到 38.5。
- **轻量三值转换流水线（RoleQ-T）**：数据自由的 learned rotation（s₁ 用 OptRot 的 ℓ₄ 目标 + s₂ 用精确三值重构目标 opt + r₄ Hadamard，比随机 Hadamard 再降 8B 困惑度 1.38 倍）→ 逐向量精确求解（联合最优 scale 与支撑集）的三值 GPTQ → 短暂的 scale 蒸馏（码字冻结、只训练 0.024% 参数、400 步、8B 仅需单卡 A100 两小时），几 GPU 小时内完成 1.59 bpw 转换，成本仅为学习式三值化（CAT-Q 60 epochs、8–64 A100 小时）的零头。
- **Fisher 引导的稀疏第二三值平面**：用对角经验 Fisher 加权的一平面量化残差（S_u = Σ F_i δ_i²）给功能单元排序，top-K 单元获得第二个三值码与 scale（ŵ = a₁t₁ + a₂t₂），保持矩阵乘法免乘法（两次三值 matmul + 加法）、无码本无分组元数据。在 8B 上 +0.57 bpw 即可收回约一半的 MMLU 损失。
- **反直觉的分配发现**：在紧张预算下，基于误差幅度的选择（Hessian×权重，与实际误差相关度 ρ=+0.96）表现比随机放置还差，而与误差反相关（ρ=−0.47）的知识 Fisher 表现最好——"把精度花在哪些单元上"比"总位数"更影响知识保留。
- **严格的比较协议**：以"内存驻留 bpw"（含解码必需元数据）对齐比较，揭示了多个基线声称码率与实际驻留率的差距（PT²-LLM 1.58→1.835、QTEA 1.70→2.281、TWLA 1.58→4.15–4.65），并诚实地报告了在 Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct 上输给 QTIP 格点码的负结果。

## Key Contributions

- **Role-aligned scale placement.** Drawing on the mechanistic view of Transformers (residual stream as shared communication bus; Geva et al. key-value memory vectors, Elhage et al. QK/OV circuits), RoleQ assigns one scale per functional vector: readers (rows of W_Q, W_K, W_V, W_gate, W_in) scale per output unit, writers (columns of W_O, W_out) scale per input unit. The rule is structural — no axis search, no calibration data, no stored per-layer flags — and applies unchanged to integer and ternary grids. At 14B ternary, placement alone lifts MMLU from 25.4 (chance) with per-output-channel scales to 38.5.
- **A few-GPU-hour ternary conversion pipeline (RoleQ-T).** Data-free learned rotations (s₁ with OptRot's ℓ₄ objective + s₂ with the exact ternary reconstruction objective + r₄ Hadamard; a further 1.38× perplexity reduction at 8B over random Hadamard), ternary GPTQ with an exact joint scale/support fit per vector (Eq. 1), and scale distillation that trains only scales and norm gains (0.024% of parameters at 8B) for 400 steps (3,200 RedPajama windows) — 2 h on one A100 at 8B, versus 8–64 A100-hours for CAT-Q's 60-epoch learned ternarization.
- **Fisher-guided sparse secondary planes.** Units ranked by a per-unit Fisher-weighted saliency (diagonal empirical Fisher over one-plane residuals) receive a second ternary code and scale, evaluated as two multiplication-free ternary matmuls plus an add — no codebooks, no group metadata, up to nine effective levels at 2·log₂3 bpw. At 8B, +0.57 bpw recovers roughly half the MMLU lost in conversion.
- **A counter-intuitive allocation finding.** At tight budgets, error-magnitude selection underperforms random: Hessian×weight correlates strongly with realized per-unit PTQ error (ρ = +0.96) yet performs worst among selectors, while the knowledge Fisher anti-correlates (ρ = −0.47) and performs best — where bits are spent matters as much as how many.
- **A rigorous comparison protocol.** All methods are compared at memory-resident bits (including strictly necessary decoding metadata), exposing gaps between claimed and resident rates (PT²-LLM 1.58→1.835, QTEA 1.70→2.281, TWLA 1.58→4.15–4.65), and the paper candidly reports a negative result against trellis-coded QTIP on Llama.

## Methodology

The pipeline (Figure 2) converts a full-precision checkpoint in stages, each annotated with memory-resident bpw for Qwen3-8B-Base:

1. **Role-aligned scales (§3.1).** Standard formats place scales by matrix layout (per output channel, i.e., per row). RoleQ instead assigns one scale per functional unit: each vector that reads from the residual stream (rows of W_Q, W_K, W_V, W_gate, W_in) gets its own scale, separating that vector's magnitude from the direction its ternary codes approximate; likewise writers (columns of W_O, W_out) per input unit. This avoids coupling unrelated units through a shared residual coordinate, which is what per-OC scales on writers / per-IC scales on readers do. The distinction survives rotation because r₁ mixes stream coordinates while leaving the unit axis intact. Writer scales rescale input activations once per token at O(d) cost; reader scales fold into stored weights.
2. **Rotations (§3.2).** QuaRot's rotation slots in SpinQuant notation: r₁ on the residual basis, r₂ mixing V→O per key/value head, r₄ on the MLP intermediate dimension. A random Hadamard alone takes 1.7B ternary PTQ perplexity from 1245.9 to 119.8. The paper's contribution is the objective: s₁ is learned data-free with the ℓ₄ kurtosis objective (best perplexity, 89.99 vs 130.3 for the opt objective at 1.7B), s₂ with the exact ternary reconstruction objective; together with r₄ this cuts 8B perplexity by a further 1.38× over random Hadamard at 0 bpw.
3. **Ternary GPTQ solver (§3.3).** GPTQ sweeps input coordinates, quantizing columns and propagating error via the input second moment H. RoleQ-T fits each vector with the exact joint optimum min_{a,t} ||w − at||²: thresholded sign codes over the best support S* (prefixes of |w| sorted descending), scale a*(S) = mean of |w_i| over S. Writers are fitted jointly during the sweep (their scale lies on the sweep axis); readers initialize their grid from Eq. 1, then each scale is refit in closed form at the final codes (a* = t^⊤Hw / t^⊤Ht), which provably cannot increase reconstruction error. The overall sweep remains greedy.
4. **Scale distillation (§3.4).** Codes frozen; only scales (as log a) and re-introduced norm gains are trained (0.024% of parameters at 8B, +0.0007 bpw), minimizing KL divergence to the full-precision teacher's next-token distribution on 3,200 unlabeled RedPajama windows (400 steps). Teacher final residuals are cached once and mapped to logits by the frozen output head, avoiding teacher forward passes. 2 h on one A100 at 8B.
5. **Fisher-placed sparse planes (§3.5).** Unit saliency S_u = Σ_{i∈u} F_i δ_i², where F_i is the diagonal empirical Fisher of per-token NLL over observed targets and δ is the one-plane quantization residual. Two Fisher variants: a text Fisher from C4 windows, and a knowledge Fisher from 720 factual-recall items (MMLU-style multiple choice, TriviaQA cloze, ARC) rendered through the chat template. Top-K units (K sets the rate in unit-sized steps from 1.590 to 3.179 bpw) get a second ternary plane fitted by the same GPTQ to the residual. Ranking stability across disjoint item halves: Spearman 0.87–0.98.
6. **Evaluation.** WikiText2 perplexity, a seven-task zero-shot average (PIQA, ARC-Easy/Challenge, HellaSwag, WinoGrande, BoolQ, OpenBookQA) under lm-eval, and five-shot MMLU — chosen because acceptable perplexity can conceal severe knowledge loss. Models: Qwen3-0.6B/1.7B/8B/14B Base, post-trained Qwen3-1.7B/8B, Llama-3.1-8B-Base/Instruct. All rates are memory-resident (codes + strictly necessary decoding metadata).

## Results & Highlights

- **Ternary conversion at 1.59 bpw (Qwen3-8B-Base, Table 1).** RoleQ-T + scale distillation reaches 11.33 WikiText2 ppl / 60.3 zero-shot / 47.6 MMLU at 1.590 bpw (fp16: 6.99 / 70.42 / 76.82). At matched or higher resident bits, Qronos gets 22.80 ppl / 46.67 / 25.93 MMLU (1.591 bpw), PT²-LLM 67.95 / 44.88 / 26.52 (1.835 bpw), QTEA 15.66 / 50.60 / 34.82 (2.281 bpw). The knowledge gap is the headline: baselines sit at 25.9–34.8 MMLU against a 25% chance floor, while RoleQ-T retains 47.6 at fewer bits.
- **Distillation alone does not explain the gap.** Applying the same scale distillation to PT²-LLM's codes yields 15.51 ppl at 1.835 bpw — still 4.2 points behind RoleQ-T's 11.33 at 1.590 bpw — so the placement/solver advantage survives an identical post-hoc stage.
- **Sparse planes recover knowledge cheaply (8B).** +0.57 bpw (1.590 → 2.157 bpw) lifts MMLU from 33.3 to 56.0 at PTQ and from 47.6 to 62.8 after distillation (fp16: 76.8), i.e., about half the conversion loss returned for a fraction of a bit; perplexity improves from 11.33 to 9.30 — still below QTEA's 2.281 bpw rate.
- **Scaling to 14B.** RoleQ-T + planes + SD reaches 8.05 ppl (1.26× fp16), 69.2 zero-shot, 68.3 MMLU at 2.156 bpw, versus 11.78 / 56.6 / 47.8 for QTEA's released checkpoint at 2.257 bpw — each gap at least 19× the seed range, including on WikiText2, QTEA's own calibration corpus.
- **Placement ablation (Table 2, matched solver/seeds/rotation).** Ternary at 14B: RoleQ 25.25 ppl vs 37.89 (per-OC) and 42.60 (per-IC); MMLU 38.5 vs 25.4 for per-OC (chance). Best-uniform-placement perplexity is improved by up to 2× across sizes 0.6B–14B in both families, with the same ordering on every seed from 1.7B up. On integer grids RoleQ-INT4 matches or improves MXFP4 perplexity at 0.6B/1.7B/8B using 8% fewer bits, and the rule comes within 0.023% of AdaDim's searched reconstruction quality at half the solver work, without calibration or stored axis flags.
- **Against learned ternarization (post-trained Qwen3-8B).** CAT-Q (60-epoch code optimization, 8–64 A100-hours) scores 27.68 ppl / 61.03 zero-shot / 52.92 MMLU at 1.710 bpw. RoleQ-T + planes at 1.990 bpw reaches 15.30 / 64.57 / 61.82 (+8.9 MMLU for +0.28 bpw) in under ten GPU-hours; at CAT-Q's own 1.711 bpw, RoleQ-T-g128 leads perplexity 17.00 vs 27.68 and MMLU 53.10 vs 52.92.
- **Fisher beats magnitude-based allocation (Table 3, post-trained Qwen3-8B at ~1.99 bpw after SD).** Knowledge Fisher: 61.82 MMLU; random units: 55.58 (+6.2 gap); Hessian×weight: 52.80 (+9.0 gap, below random). Notably, Hessian×weight correlates most strongly with realized per-unit error (ρ = +0.96) yet performs worst; the knowledge Fisher anti-correlates (ρ = −0.47) yet performs best.
- **Honest negative result (Appendix Table 10).** On Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct at 2.0065 bpw, QTIP 2-bit (with its QuIP#-style fine-tune) leads on every measure: 8.83 ppl / 68.13 zero-shot / 59.93 MMLU vs RoleQ-T + planes + SD at 11.06 / 63.81 / 51.02 (−4.3 zero-shot, −8.9 MMLU). The authors explicitly do not claim parity with the trellis-codebook class.
- **Resident-rate audit.** TWLA's released code, which reproduces its reported perplexity, is actually resident at 4.15–4.65 bpw (roughly twice RoleQ's planes rate) via a masked two-plane model on 26% of weights that its paper does not describe — used as a reference, not a bit-matched baseline.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty: solid but incremental-adjacent.** The core idea — scales per functional vector, readers per-row and writers per-column — is a clean, well-motivated structural insight, but per-input-channel scales, two-way axis search (AdaDim), folded dual-axis scales (Z-FOLD, D² Quant) and saliency-based extra planes (BiLLM, QTEA, PTQTP) all exist; the contribution is unifying them under a calibration-free rule with an exact solver, not inventing a new primitive. The Fisher-vs-magnitude counter-intuition (Hessian×weight below random) is the most novel empirical nugget.
- **Rigor: high.** Matched-solver placement controls with paired seeds, shared-code-path grids, resident-bpw accounting, baseline reruns, and disclosed seed spreads put this in the top tier of PTQ evaluation practice. The self-reported negative result against QTIP/GuidedQuant on Llama strengthens credibility.
- **A subtle evaluation-affinity concern.** The knowledge Fisher is built from 720 factual-recall items including MMLU-style multiple choice, and the headline metric where Fisher planes shine is five-shot MMLU. The authors are careful — items are drawn with a seed disjoint from their probe, and items "decide where a plane goes but never what it stores" — but the selection signal and the evaluation metric come from the same task family, so the +6.2-over-random MMLU gap may partially reflect task-targeted placement. The text-Fisher variant being best on perplexity and worst on factual recall confirms the item set shapes what is recovered.
- **CAT-Q comparison is not strictly bit-matched in the headline.** The +8.9 MMLU win over CAT-Q costs +0.28 bpw; at CAT-Q's own 1.71 bpw the MMLU advantage shrinks to a tie (53.1 vs 52.9) with the real edge being perplexity (17.00 vs 27.68). The headline "surpasses CAT-Q" should be read with that caveat.
- **Reproducibility: good on protocol, unverified on artifacts.** Hyperparameters, stage costs, rotation objectives, and Eq. 1's closed-form fit are specified in detail; no code release is mentioned in the main text, and the rotation-objective ablations rest on the authors' re-implementations of OptRot/TWLA objectives.
- **Significance: strong for the sub-2-bit post-training regime.** Demonstrating that a few GPU-hours can retain ~62% of above-chance MMLU headroom at ~2 bpw where direct PTQ baselines sit at chance is a practically meaningful advance; the finding that perplexity conceals knowledge loss is well-argued and consistently quantified.
- **Red flags: few.** (1) The placement rule only helps in a rotated basis — the authors admit it does not extend to native coordinates, so the "structural rule" is entangled with the rotation recipe. (2) Plane gains shrink from 8B to 14B (e.g., MMLU +15.2 at 8B vs +8.6 at 14B after SD), an adverse scaling signal under the paper's own observation. (3) No inference-speed or kernel measurements (weight-only A16, storage/quality only), so latency claims are inherited from the ternary-matmul literature rather than measured. (4) Main family is Qwen3 (four sizes) with a single Llama cross-check; a general scaling trend is explicitly not established.

## Strengths

- A single structural rule (readers per-output-unit, writers per-input-unit) that is data-free, grid-agnostic, and consistently wins across 0.6B–14B in two model families — with the largest effect exactly where theory predicts (ternary, where the per-unit scale is a vector's only continuous degree of freedom).
- Exceptional cost-quality point: ~1.59 bpw with useful knowledge retention (47.6 MMLU vs ~26–35 for prior post-training ternary) in 3–4 A100-hours, orders of magnitude below learned ternarization.
- The exact per-vector fit (Eq. 1) is a clean, reusable piece of solver engineering, and the reader refit a* = t^⊤Hw/t^⊤Ht provably never hurts.
- The Fisher-vs-Hessian selector study is a genuinely instructive negative result for the mixed-precision literature: correlating with reconstruction error is not the same as identifying where extra bits pay off.
- Transparent, self-critical reporting: resident-bpw audit of baselines, disclosed negative result vs QTIP, and explicit scope limits in the conclusion.

## Limitations

- **Rotation-dependent rule.** The placement advantage does not hold in native (unrotated) coordinates; the "role-aligned" framing is validated only in a rotated basis (Appendix C shows the advantage depends on rotation).
- **Scaling evidence is thin.** Four Qwen3 sizes plus one Llama model; the best uniform placement varies with size and family, and plane gains shrink at 14B, so the trend to larger models is unproven.
- **Fisher's edge narrows with budget.** In the 1.7B sweep, random placement's perplexity relative to Fisher falls from 1.96× at +0.15 bits to 1.12× at +0.9 — the selector matters most at the tightest budgets.
- **Trellis codebooks remain ahead at 2 bpw.** On Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, QTIP (alone and with GuidedQuant) leads every measure at matched bits (−8.9 MMLU after distillation); no parity is claimed.
- **No generation/reasoning evaluation.** Long chain-of-thought and open-ended generation — where quantization has distinct failure modes — are untested; evaluation is WikiText2 ppl, seven zero-shot tasks, and 5-shot MMLU only.
- **Weight-only, no speed.** Results are A16 (full-precision activations) and measure storage and quality; no wall-clock or memory-bandwidth benchmarks with lookup-table ternary kernels are provided.
- **Two-seed averages** (four in places) with small spreads — adequate given the reported gap-to-range multiples, but per-config sampling is light by some standards.

## Relevance

（与用户兴趣高度相关：这篇是 LLM 极低比特量化的最新进展。）如果你关注后训练量化、三值/亚 2-bit 推理或"机制解释视角指导压缩设计"这条路线，RoleQ 值得精读：它把可解释性的残差流读写角色直接转化为 scale 放置规则，并在知识保留（MMLU）这个 PTQ 最痛的指标上给出了数量级改善，其"按功能单元分配额外比特优于按误差幅度"的结论对混合精度分配也有普适启发。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the reader/writer scale rule transfer to architectures without a clean residual-stream decomposition — e.g., MoE routers, linear-attention or state-space models, or deep MLP towers like modern hybrid models?
- Can the knowledge-Fisher plane selection be made evaluation-agnostic (e.g., Fisher over diverse downstream formats) without losing its edge, to rule out item-set/evaluation affinity?
- How do RoleQ-T models behave on long chain-of-thought reasoning and open-ended generation, where ternary quantization failure modes differ from multiple-choice probes?
- Why do plane gains shrink from 8B to 14B — is saliency concentration, redundancy, or Fisher estimation noise the cause, and does the effect reverse at 30B+?
- What is the realized latency/memory-bandwidth win with actual LUT ternary kernels (à la BitNet-style inference) rather than the storage-only accounting used here?
- Could the exact per-vector fit (Eq. 1) plus reader refit improve 2-bit trellis methods (QTIP/GuidedQuant) as a drop-in scalar front end, closing the remaining gap?
