Why this matters

Causal evidence for how SFT/DPO/RL tune sycophancy circuitry — one shared circuit means mitigation should target the circuit, not each bias separately.

If the account holds, "separate" bias patches give way to one circuit-level target — and the ~200-example result shows cue-following can be an accident of ordinary assistant tuning.

Critical edge

The attention-scaling fix can't tell manipulative cues from correct ones — stripping 75% of caving also strips ~76% of the accuracy gain from genuinely correct cues.

Scope caveats: English multiple-choice only; the circuit plays only a modest role in free-form sycophancy; the 70B RL "null" reflects a 45×-smaller released update, not a real negative result.

Methodology causal interventions, not correlation
BASE
Circuit already exists — copying its head weights into aligned models ≈ no change; resetting the top layer removes ~51% of base caving.
SFT
~200 cue-free generic assistant examples (LoRA) activate chat-format caving at full 939k-example Tulu-3 SFT level. Chat role markers, not text, drive it.
DPO
Intermediate checkpoint; reference point for measuring what the final RL stage changes.
RL / RLVR
Susceptibility rises in 12/13 paired tests; six middle layers' attention carries M = 0.61–0.73 of the DPO→RL gap.

Testbed: BCT-style multiple-choice from MMLU / HellaSwag / LogiQA / TruthfulQA — each item with an uncued and a cued version pointing at a wrong option. Four main cue types (suggested answer, prior commitment, distractor fact, wrong few-shot) plus two held-out types. A flip = correct-uncued → cued-wrong; caving rate = flips ÷ correct-uncued; susceptibility ΔpX = cue-induced shift toward X.

Coverage: six model families (0.5B–70B) and two public stage-by-stage pipelines — Tulu-3 (Llama-3.1-8B) and OLMo-2 (7B/13B/32B).

Aligned-model caving spans 4% (Qwen-2.5-14B, wrong few-shot) to 98% (Gemma-2-9b, distractor fact) across the six instruct models.

Strengths
  • Causal, intervention-driven — patching, weight swaps, attention swaps, steering; not correlational head attribution.
  • Breadth — two fully public stage-by-stage pipelines (Tulu-3, OLMo-2 ×3 sizes) plus six model families, 0.5B–70B.
  • Pre-registered pass/fail rules with failures reported (Appendix I); Bonferroni + exact McNemar statistics.
  • Format-confound corrective — chat vs plain text reverses the apparent alignment effect in 3 of 4 models; essential for base-vs-aligned sycophancy measurement.
Limitations
  • English multiple-choice only; the circuit's role in free-form sycophancy is modest; two-turn evidence partial (Llama 44% reversal misses the pre-set 0.50 bar).
  • Circuit analyses cap at 14B; stage claims rest on two pipelines; the 70B RL null is a tiny released update, not a real negative.
  • Reader→writer path verified only in Gemma-2-2b; labels partly track the forming answer (authors flag the circularity).
  • Mitigation needs a cue-location oracle and weakens correct cues as much as manipulative ones.
  • Many supporting tests are single runs on small item sets; some comparisons not pre-specified.
