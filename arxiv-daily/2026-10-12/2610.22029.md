> **一句话总结：** 本文指出 R-Zero/Agent0/Skill-SP 这类自我进化循环存在"selection-to-update gap"——看起来可学的任务不等于有用的更新（Skill-SP 在 exact-valid 任务上的 96 次真实更新中有 60 次未降低 correct-target NLL），并提出 EVOHEIR：把"参数继承"（HEIRCOMMIT 在全新探针集上对比冻结后继模型与根模型、只在严格更好时提交，三宿主上有害提交占比从 47–77% 降到 13–16%）与"经验继承"（Experience Recovery 用当前+归档参考做修复/保持式蒸馏，被拒轮次的经验仍可帮助后续模型）解耦；四个骨干（Qwen3-4B/8B、OLMo-3-7B、Gemma-4-E4B-it）十轮进化后数学平均分全面领先，较最强基线 Agent0 提升 1.2–7.0%（如 Qwen3-8B 数学均值 36.51% vs 34.11%，+2.40 pp），48 个 benchmark-骨干组合中 45 个胜过 Agent0。

> **TL;DR:** Self-evolving LLM loops (R-Zero, Agent0, Skill-SP) admit tasks by pre-update behavior and inherit every update — yet the authors' audit shows 60 of 96 (62.5%) real Skill-SP updates on exact-valid tasks yield nonpositive correct-target NLL gain, and selected-target optimism inflates scores by 8.60 pp (Agent0) / 4.10 pp (R-Zero) vs ~0 pp for fixed-target controls. The paper's HEIRCOMMIT freezes each successor and commits it only if it beats the root on a fresh confirmation pool (cutting harmful commits from 47–77% to 13–16% across the three hosts, 68–83% below coverage-matched random), while EVOHEIR decouples parameter inheritance from experience inheritance via recipient-conditioned distillation from an archive that persists even through rejected rounds. Over ten rounds on four backbones (Qwen3-4B/8B-Base, OLMo-3-7B, Gemma-4-E4B-it), EVOHEIR posts the best math and general-reasoning averages on every backbone — e.g., Qwen3-8B Math Avg. 36.51% vs 34.11% for Agent0 (+2.40 pp, +7.0%) across 7 math benchmarks (MATH-500, Minerva, OlympiadBench, Omni-MATH-2, MATH-Beyond, AIME 2025/2026) — beating Agent0 on 45 of 48 benchmark-backbone pairs, though the Gemma-4-E4B-it margin is only +0.55 pp and a full round costs 8–9 h on two 96-GB GPUs.

## 核心贡献

- **识别并量化 selection-to-update gap**：任务侧的准入信号（pass rate、分歧度、frontier proximity）描述的是训练前行为，无法预测一次优化器更新产生的迁移。实测证据：Skill-SP 在 exact-valid 任务上的 96 次真实更新中 60 次（62.5%）correct-target NLL 无下降；Agent0/R-Zero 已准入任务在独立新响应下 52.2%/42.0% 离开原生 frontier；selected-target optimism 在 Agent0 达 8.60 pp（固定目标对照仅 0.20/−0.10 pp）。
- **HEIRCOMMIT：源无关的继承规则**：冻结后继模型，在与筛选池不相交的全新确认池上做 paired 打分，严格优于根模型才提交（τ=0 经验模式；正边距给出具证书的模式）。在 R-Zero、Agent0、Skill-SP 三宿主各 64 次原生更新上，有害提交占比从 47–77% 降到 13–16%（相对 coverage-matched random 降低 68–83%），且保留 78–87% 的有用更新，把 R-Zero 和 Skill-SP 的负池增益翻正。
- **EVOHEIR：参数继承与经验继承解耦**：筛选选出"参数接受者"（分支端点或根模型），Experience Recovery 用冻结的、以接受者初始化的教师（修复教师看 donor 参考、保持教师只看题目）在固定接受者轨迹上做 forward-KL LoRA 蒸馏——修失败、保成功；合格经验进入有界归档（≤256 条）而不管本轮是否提交，被拒轮次的参考仍可教后续接受者（10 个比根弱的 donor 中 8 个的经验仍有正吸收增益）。
- **理论刻画**：Proposition 1 证明仅凭训练前观测 Ot 的任何持久化规则存在两个不可区分的学习世界，worst-case 期望 regret ≥ Δ/2；Proposition 2 证明筛选赢家分数被选择偏置抬高；Theorem 1 给出"搜索-继承分离"——复用筛选证据做继承要付 K 项 union bound，而全新确认池只需单项指数尾界；Corollary 1 把 regret 分解为 selection、absorption、confirmation 误差（Rt ≤ 2ϵscr + drec + ϵcon + τ）。
- **经验比模型长寿的实证**：同等 64 条参考下 Recovery 净增 +1.39 pp（新得 2.54、丢失 1.15）而 Cache SFT 为 −0.54 pp（新得 2.08、丢失 2.61）；纳入"被拒轮次"历史使吸收增益从 +0.39（仅当前）→ +0.73（+已提交轮）→ +1.39 pp（全部合格历史），且 matched 替换实验显示非继承来源记录比同龄继承记录多贡献 0.34–0.73 pp（95% bootstrap CI > 0）。

## Key Contributions

- **Identifies and measures the selection-to-update gap**: pre-update behavioral admission signals (pass rate, disagreement, frontier proximity) do not predict the transfer an optimizer step produces. Audit evidence: 60 of 96 (62.5%) real Skill-SP updates on exact-valid tasks show nonpositive correct-target NLL gain; 52.2% (Agent0) / 42.0% (R-Zero) of admitted tasks leave the native frontier under fresh responses; selected-target optimism is 8.60 pp (Agent0) and 4.10 pp (R-Zero) vs 0.20/−0.10 pp for fixed-target controls.
- **HEIRCOMMIT, a source-independent inheritance rule**: freeze the successor, score it against the root on a fresh confirmation pool disjoint from screening, and commit only on strictly positive paired gain (τ=0 empirical mode; positive margin gives a certified mode). On 64 native updates per host (R-Zero, Agent0, Skill-SP) it cuts the harmful share of commits from 47–77% to 13–16% (68–83% below coverage-matched random at the same 23–52% acceptance rate), retains 78–87% of useful updates, and flips negative pool NLL gain positive on two of three hosts.
- **EVOHEIR separates parameter inheritance from experience inheritance**: screening picks the parameter recipient (a branch endpoint or the root); Experience Recovery distills with a frozen teacher initialized from the recipient — the repair teacher conditions on a donor reference, the keep teacher on the problem only — via LoRA forward-KL on fixed recipient trajectories, repairing failures and preserving successes. Qualified experience enters a bounded archive (≤256 records) regardless of the commit decision, so a rejected round can still teach a later recipient (8 of 10 donors weaker than the root still yield positive absorption gain).
- **Theory**: Proposition 1 (task-side non-identifiability — any persistence rule based only on pre-update observations Ot faces indistinguishable worlds with values +Δ/−Δ, worst-case expected regret ≥ Δ/2); Proposition 2 (selected-future optimism E[V̂scr − V] ≥ 0); Theorem 1 (reusing screening for inheritance pays a union bound K·exp(−mscr β²/2σ²) that fresh confirmation avoids); Corollary 1 (regret ≤ 2ϵscr + drec + ϵcon + τ, decomposed into selection, absorption, and confirmation errors).
- **Experience outlives its model, empirically**: from the same 64 references Recovery nets +1.39 pp (gains 2.54, losses 1.15) vs −0.54 pp for Cache SFT (gains 2.08, losses 2.61); widening history eligibility at fixed budget moves absorption gain from +0.39 pp (current only) to +0.73 pp (+committed rounds) to +1.39 pp (all qualified history including rejected rounds), and matched source-replacement shows non-inherited records add 0.34–0.73 pp over same-age inherited ones (95% hierarchical bootstrap CIs above zero).

## Methodology

The self-evolution environment has two co-evolving agents (Curriculum Scout proposes problems; Evolving Learner trains) coordinated by an Evolution Controller; each round starts from a resumable root state St = (θt, ωt) (parameters + optimizer/scheduler/RNG state) and ends with an inheritance decision. Four steps per EVOHEIR round:

1. **Explore**: 1,024 task-generation attempts per round; native admission uses consensus labels from ten learner responses per question (largest answer cluster with support ≥ 0.7 becomes the pseudo-label). Two isolated native branches are trained from the root (each on a 64-question cohort, full-parameter AdamW at lr 10⁻⁶, 15 optimizer calls), giving frozen candidate set Ctnat.
2. **Screen a recipient**: paired root-relative scores on a 16-question screening pool Ptscr select the parameter recipient from candidates ∪ {root} (ties favor root, then fixed branch order). Screening, confirmation, and final evaluation use disjoint question pools with frozen consensus labels.
3. **Recover experience**: current-round records Et and the bounded archive Mt supply qualified problems+references. Recipient attempts (cached exact-checkpoint or one greedy rollout) split material into repair (failed attempt, qualified reference) and keep (successful attempt) groups, disjoint and recipient-specific — an archived success becomes repair material when the current recipient cannot reproduce it. A frozen teacher initialized from the recipient predicts next tokens at the same prefixes as the LoRA student (rank 8, α=16, q/v projections, ≤16 updates, lr 5×10⁻⁵): the repair teacher sees the donor reference (privileged context), the keep teacher only the problem; the student matches each via token-level forward KL over the full vocabulary, weighted by λkeep = 1. Only the merged student is deployed.
4. **Confirm (HEIRCOMMIT)**: freeze the post-absorption proposal St⁺, score paired root-relative gain v on 32 fresh confirmation questions (4 responses each). Commit if v > τt (τt = 0 empirically; τt = σcon·√(2 log(1/δt)/mcon) gives the certified mode). Rejection restores the complete root state (optimizer moments, scheduler, RNGs); the archive is updated with qualified experience either way.

The theory (Section 5) formalizes why this design: Prop 1 shows pre-update observations cannot identify update value (two smooth loss worlds with identical Ot and step size but opposite probe-loss change ±Δ); Prop 2 shows the screening winner's score is optimistically biased by selection even with unbiased per-candidate scores (classic selection-bias/winner's-curse argument); Theorem 1 shows accepting on reused screening scores risks any of K bad branches slipping through (union bound), while fresh confirmation of the single fixed proposal pays only a single sub-Gaussian tail — with adaptive rounds δt = δ/[t(t+1)] supporting an unbounded horizon.

Evaluation: 10 rounds × 3 training seeds on four backbones (Qwen3-4B-Base, Qwen3-8B-Base, OLMo-3-7B, instruction-tuned Gemma-4-E4B-it) against R-Zero, Agent0, and a No-Recovery ablation; 7 math benchmarks (MATH-500, Minerva, OlympiadBench, Omni-MATH-2, MATH-Beyond, AIME 2025/2026 — AIME scored with 32 responses for pass@1, others greedy exact-match) and 5 general-reasoning benchmarks (SuperGPQA, MMLU-Pro, BBEH, LiveBench Reasoning, KOR-Bench), equally weighted averages. Fixed-recipient donor interventions (16 donors, 1,024 items each) isolate experience value; a 2,400-problem diagnostic with three absorption-order seeds isolates the objective; a normalized accounting-budget study (native baseline = 100.0 units, Full = 123.7) compares at equal cost.

## Results & Highlights

- **HEIRCOMMIT audit (64 native updates per host, NLL pool gain in 10⁻³ units)**: harmful-commit share drops from 47–77% (native commit-all) to 13–16% — a 68–83% relative cut vs coverage-matched random inheritance at the same 23–52% acceptance rate — on all three hosts (R-Zero, Skill-SP, Agent0), while retaining 78–87% of useful updates. Pool gain flips from −23.17 to +10.19 on R-Zero and from −3.47 to +2.49 on Skill-SP, and rises from +2.80 to +14.35 on Agent0 (5×).
- **Ten-round self-evolution, Math Avg. over 7 benchmarks (3 seeds)**: EVOHEIR (Full) leads every backbone — Qwen3-4B-Base 31.82% vs 30.01% Agent0 (+1.81 pp, +6.0%; R-Zero 28.79, base 25.81); Qwen3-8B-Base 36.51% vs 34.11% Agent0 (+2.40 pp, +7.0%; base 28.06); OLMo-3-7B 24.94% vs 23.35% (+1.59 pp, +6.8%; base 19.92); Gemma-4-E4B-it 47.21% vs 46.66% (+0.55 pp, only +1.2% — the instruction-tuned start leaves least headroom). General Avg. over 5 benchmarks also leads everywhere (e.g., Qwen3-8B 36.48 vs Agent0 35.24). Relative to the base models, Full raises Math Avg. by 7.9–30.1%, the most of any compared method; across all twelve benchmarks Full beats Agent0 on 45 of 48 benchmark–backbone pairs, the 3 exceptions being 30-problem AIME sets.
- **Task selection does not predict learning**: 60 of 96 (62.5%) real Skill-SP updates on exact-valid tasks show nonpositive correct-target NLL gain; selected-target optimism is 8.60 pp (Agent0) and 4.10 pp (R-Zero) vs 0.20/−0.10 pp for fixed-target controls; 52.2%/42.0% of Agent0/R-Zero admitted tasks leave the frontier under fresh responses.
- **Recovery beats direct fine-tuning and preserves skills**: from the same 64 references, Cache SFT gains 2.08 pp of new successes but loses 2.61 pp (net −0.54 pp), while Recovery gains 2.54 pp and loses 1.15 pp (net +1.39 pp) — a 1.93 pp average advantage held at all four recipient checkpoints. On the 2,400-problem fixed-recipient diagnostic, Recovery nets 3.57 ± 0.30 pp vs 2.26 ± 0.45 (repair-only, λkeep = 0) and 1.11 ± 0.86 (Cache SFT).
- **Experience outlives rejected models**: 8 of 10 donors weaker than the current root still improve the recipient (mean donor gain −1.09 pp vs mean absorption gain +0.71 pp); adding all qualified history (including rejected rounds) lifts absorption gain to +1.39 pp vs +0.39 pp for current-round-only material, and matched source-replacement shows non-inherited records contribute +0.34–0.73 pp beyond same-age inherited ones at ages 1–4 rounds (pointwise 95% bootstrap CIs above zero).
- **Ablations and budget**: removing any mechanism hurts — Same-Lineage 30.77, Random Experience 30.54, Current-Round Only 30.85 (Math Avg. drops of 0.97–1.28 pp vs Full 31.82), and NoConfirm is worst at 29.80 (−2.02 pp math, −1.19 pp general), so the independent inheritance decision matters more than any experience-selection choice. At equal accounting budget Full is the worst method at 0.8× its default cost but the best from 1.0× onward; its default configuration costs 123.7 units vs 100.0 for a native single-stream baseline (23.7% overhead), and a Full round takes 8–9 wall-clock hours on two 96-GB GPUs.

## Authors & Credibility

The work is from MBZUAI (Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence, Abu Dhabi): Haoyi Yang and Kshitij Mishra (equal contribution) with Nils Lukas and Salem Lahlou. Lahlou's group has a track record in self-play and regularization of self-play language models (Alami et al., 2024, on SPIN regularization is co-authored by Lahlou), so the team has genuine prior expertise in this exact area. The paper is a preprint (arXiv 2610.22029, 15 Oct 2026, cs.LG) and does not state acceptance at a peer-reviewed venue. Credibility signals: measured audit data backs every headline claim (the 60/96 and optimism numbers are from their own audits, not citations); three seeds with SDs are reported for all main tables; prompts, hyperparameters, archive policy, budget accounting, and failure modes are documented in unusual detail across seven appendices; and several limitations (round-7 negative absorption gain, verification-dependent repair) are disclosed by the authors themselves. No external reproduction exists yet, and no code release is mentioned in the paper.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: The ingredients individually have precedents — update acceptance in AlphaGo Zero, candidate fine-tuning in APE (Marín, 2025), realized-improvement signals in SOAR, privileged-context distillation in GKD/SDFT — and the paper honestly cites all of these. The genuinely new parts are (a) the formal non-identifiability result plus the winner's-curse/union-bound analysis showing *why* fresh confirmation beats reused screening evidence, and (b) the decoupling of parameter inheritance from experience inheritance with an archive that survives rejected rounds — the donor-intervention experiments (weaker donors still helping) are a clean, non-obvious finding.
- **Rigor**: Strong on audit methodology — disjoint pools for screening/confirmation/evaluation, frozen consensus labels, coverage-matched random controls (not just commit-all), matched-count source replacement with hierarchical bootstrap, and equal-accounting-budget comparisons. Weaknesses: the empirical gate runs at τ = 0, which has no formal guarantee (the certified positive-margin mode of Theorem 1 is analyzed but never run); the decision pools are tiny (16 screening questions, 32 confirmation questions, 4 responses each), so the gate itself is noisy at exactly the granularity the theory treats as sub-Gaussian error; and only 3 seeds back the headline 45/48 sweep.
- **Reproducibility**: Appendices specify hyperparameters (Table 3), LoRA config, archive admission/retention, budgets, and all prompt templates — above average. But no code release is mentioned, and the system has many moving parts (two co-evolving agents, two branches, archive, three disjoint pools, resumable state) plus ~80–90 GPU-hours per ten-round run on 2×96-GB hardware, making independent reimplementation demanding.
- **Significance**: The selection-to-update gap is a real, well-documented failure mode (their audit shows the majority of native updates are useless or harmful), and HEIRCOMMIT is a cheap, source-independent fix that any existing loop could adopt — that part transfers broadly. The full EVOHEIR gains are more modest than the framing suggests: +6.0–7.0% relative over Agent0 on base models translates to +1.59–2.40 pp Math Avg., collapsing to +0.55 pp (+1.2%) on the only instruction-tuned start, and the advantage is budget-fragile (Full is the *worst* method at 0.8× its default budget in Figure 9a).
- **Red flags**: (1) The Curriculum Scout prompt generates only competition-mathematics problems, so the entire self-evolution loop trains on math, and the "general-reasoning" wins (MMLU-Pro, BBEH, LiveBench) ride on math transfer — no code, tool-use, or agentic domain is tested even though Agent0, the strongest baseline, is a tool-integrated method. (2) Confirmation labels are the model's own consensus pseudo-labels (support ≥ 0.7), so a confidently-wrong learner can ratify its own errors; repair quality is explicitly bounded by verification quality (the authors admit a flawed reference that passes the verifier steers the repair teacher). (3) The three losses to Agent0 are conveniently explained away as "30-problem AIME sets", but those AIME sets are part of the headline Math Avg. that EVOHEIR still wins, and 30-item benchmarks are equally noisy when they help. (4) The harmful-update framing partly indicts the baselines' own recipes rather than the comparison — pool NLL gain for native R-Zero commit-all is −23.17 × 10⁻³, so HEIRCOMMIT's improvement has a low bar on that host.

## Strengths

- Diagnoses a real and previously under-formalized failure mode with careful measurement (fresh-response audits, exact-valid NLL audits, selected-target optimism with fixed-target controls) instead of arguing by anecdote.
- The theory directly informs the design: disjoint pools, single-proposal confirmation, regret decomposition with an explicit absorption-loss term, and an anytime-valid adaptive-round bound — analysis and system cohere rather than being stapled together.
- HEIRCOMMIT is source-independent and cheap, and was validated on three *external* hosts' native updates (R-Zero, Agent0, Skill-SP), not just the authors' own loop.
- The parameter/experience decoupling is supported by unusually well-controlled interventions: fixed recipients, matched record counts, same-age replacement controls, and age-resolved bootstrap intervals.
- Exceptional documentation: complete prompt templates, hyperparameters, budget accounting down to per-stage units, and honest failure-mode discussion (including their own round-7 negative absorption gain).

## Limitations

- Evaluation is math-centric by construction (the Scout only writes competition-math problems); no coding, tool-use, or agentic benchmarks despite the baselines (Agent0) being agentic — external validity beyond math-adjacent reasoning is untested.
- Absolute gains over the strongest baseline are modest (+0.55 to +2.40 pp Math Avg.; +1.2–7.0% relative) and shrink to near-noise on the instruction-tuned Gemma-4-E4B-it, the setting closest to practical use.
- Heavy compute: 8–9 wall-clock hours per Full round on two 96-GB GPUs and a 23.7% accounting overhead over native training; the equal-budget study shows the method loses its advantage below its default budget.
- The empirical zero-threshold confirmation gate carries no statistical guarantee, and the 16/32-question decision pools are small relative to the noise the theory itself models; consensus pseudo-labels can entrench confident errors.
- Experience absorption can still hurt (net −0.20 pp at round 7) and repair depends on the verifier's quality; the archive is capped at 256 records with a round-robin policy whose long-horizon (≫10 rounds) behavior is unexplored.
- Three seeds and ten rounds bound the statistical strength of every trajectory claim; no code release is mentioned.

## Relevance

这篇论文正中"Self-Evolving Agents & Automated Research"兴趣区：它诊断并修复自我进化 LLM 循环（R-Zero/Agent0/Skill-SP 一脉）的核心失败模式——"看起来可学的任务不产生有用更新"，其 HEIRCOMMIT 继承门控与经验归档机制对任何自提升/自博弈流水线都是可直接借鉴的可靠性设计。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would HEIRCOMMIT's harmful-commit reduction hold when confirmation labels come from an external verifier or ground truth instead of the model's own ≥0.7-support consensus pseudo-labels — i.e., how much of the gate's benefit survives label noise that is correlated with the model's blind spots?
- Can the loop escape its math-only diet: does EVOHEIR transfer to code, tool-use, or agentic domains where verification is programmatic rather than answer-matching, and where Agent0 (a tool-integrated method) might be a stronger native host?
- The certified positive-margin mode (τt = σcon·√(2 log(1/δt)/mcon)) is analyzed but never run — what acceptance rate and end-task performance does it actually buy at 16–32 confirmation questions, and how large must mcon be before the guarantee is non-vacuous?
- Does the experience archive keep helping at horizons well beyond 10 rounds, or does the 256-record round-robin cap and stale-reference repair eventually saturate or actively mislead later recipients?
- Since native commit-all pool gain is deeply negative for R-Zero (−23.17 × 10⁻³ NLL), could a much simpler early-stopping / per-update validation baseline (e.g., APE-style acceptance without the archive) capture most of HEIRCOMMIT's benefit at a fraction of EVOHEIR's 23.7% overhead?
- The winner's-curse analysis assumes unbiased per-candidate screening scores; how do the conclusions change under correlated errors across branches (shared probe pool, shared training data lineage), which the union bound covers but perhaps too loosely?
