> **一句话总结：** 本文用蒙特卡洛 rollout 把 Qwen3-4B/8B 推理模型的错误轨迹分为能力缺口（capability gap）、近失（near miss）和不可挽回点（PIT）三类，系统测试隐藏态探针能否识别失败类型并定位 PIT 步骤——结果 mostly negative：在 8B 上置信探针漏掉 81% 的 PIT 轨迹、47% 的近失轨迹，没有任何单一探针在定位 PIT 步骤上超过仅用轨迹长度的平凡基线（4B 上 10 次运行 0 次胜出），且换一种训练设计的探针会让两类失败的排序完全反转，说明探针读出的"失败类型排序"依赖训练设计而非模型内在信息。

> **TL;DR:** A NeurIPS 2026 (Interpretability as a Science workshop) study from IBM Research Dublin, Trinity College Dublin, and Umeå University that labels incorrect reasoning traces of Qwen3-4B/8B (thinking mode) on DeepMath-103K and Hendrycks MATH as capability gaps, near misses, or PITs via Monte Carlo rollouts, then tests hidden-state probes against rollout-free baselines. The confidence probe detects wrong final answers with AUROC 0.81 at both scales vs 0.78/0.77 for a gradient-boosted text-feature baseline (+0.035/+0.036, below the prespecified 0.05 margin), but at the 0.5 cutoff misses 81% of PITs vs 47% of near misses at 8B; a combined probe trained on rollout rates reverses this ordering (AUROC 0.76 → 0.40 for PITs vs near misses); and no single probe beats a one-parameter "length rule" at locating the PIT step (pointer probe 0.4632 vs 0.4692 Hit@±2 on 2,289 held-out 4B PITs, above the rule in 0 of 10 runs). The paper is a rigorous, mostly negative result with an identifiability theory explaining why prefix-based probes may fail at localization.

## 核心贡献

- **定义了基于续算价值（continuation value）的三类失败taxonomy**：对每条错误轨迹，通过在每个前缀处做蒙特卡洛 rollout 估计续算价值 V_t，并定义最优可达价值 V*_t = max_{t'≥t} V_{t'}；以阈值 θ=0.1 定义 capability gap（从第 0 步就不可挽回）、near miss（全程可挽回但最终答错）和 PIT（中途某步首次不可挽回），PIT 步骤即恢复性丧失的临界步。这与过程奖励模型关注的"局部步骤有效性"（local validity）刻意区分开。
- **系统性的探针-基线对照实验**：在相同的隐藏态（第 23-24 层，read site）上训练四种探针（最终标签的置信探针、rollout 率的 combined/value 探针、直接预测 PIT 步骤的 pointer 探针），并与最强的无隐藏态基线（11 个文本特征的梯度提升分类器、单参数长度规则）对比，且预设了 0.05 的 AUROC margin 和 0.08 的最小效应量作为判决阈值——这是少见的预注册式可解释性实验设计。
- **核心负面结果 1——失败类型盲区**：训练在最终标签上的置信探针在 cutoff 0.5 处系统性漏掉 capability gap 和 PIT、放过 near miss（8B：漏 81% PIT vs 47% near miss；控制 V*_0、轨迹长度、答案位置、题目难度后差异 +0.125 [+0.104, +0.143]，超过 0.08 最小效应）。即探针"知道答案错了"，但对"错在哪类"有系统偏差。
- **核心负面结果 2——排序可被训练设计翻转**：combined 探针读同样的隐藏态、同样的 read site，却把 PIT 排到 near miss 之下（AUROC 0.76 → 0.40 @8B）。由于两探针在标签之外还差注意力掩码、步加权和训练调度，作者诚实承认无法把反转单独归因于标签。
- **核心负面结果 3——PIT 步骤定位不如长度规则**：长度规则（按轨迹长度预测一个晚步，中位相对深度 0.70/0.75）在 4B 的 2,289 条 held-out PIT 上 Hit@±2 = 0.4692，pointer 探针 0.4632 ± 0.0134（10 次运行 0 次超过）；8B 的 value 探针 5 个种子全部低于规则；只有五种子集成在一种损失加权下以 +0.0262 [+0.0022, +0.0495] 勉强超过。
- **可辨识性理论（命题 1-2）**：证明最终标签监督的总体最优解是 E[Y|h_{1:t}] = E[V_t|h_{1:t}]（仅在表示充分时等于 V_t）；并证明从前缀精确恢复 V*_t 当且仅当 Var(V*_t|s_t)=0——为定位失败提供了理论解释框架（但不直接解释实验结果）。
- **一个正面结果**：读第 19-24 层 transcoder 激活的失败类型分类器（区分 capability gap 与 PIT，按问题评分）在 8B 上超过最强无隐藏态基线 0.055 AUROC，达到预设 margin；4B 上 +0.049 未达标。
- **开放资源**：代码（github.com/SSubhnil/Reasoning-Failures）与 rollout 标注数据集（huggingface.co/datasets/ssubhnil/Reasoning-Failures）公开。

## Key Contributions

- Defines a rollout-grounded failure taxonomy: continuation value V_t estimated by Monte Carlo rollouts at each prefix; best reachable value V*_t = max_{t'≥t} V_{t'}; threshold θ = 0.1 partitions incorrect traces into capability gaps (unrecoverable at step 0), near misses (recoverable at every prefix), and PITs (first unrecoverable prefix at t_PIT). This deliberately diverges from PRM-style "local validity" labels.
- Runs a preregistered-style probe-vs-baseline comparison on identical hidden states (layers {23,24} at both scales) with four probe designs (confidence/combined/value/pointer) against the strongest rollout-free baselines: a gradient-boosted text-feature classifier (11 features) for detection and a one-parameter length rule for localization, with a fixed 0.05 AUROC margin and 0.08 minimum effect as decision thresholds.
- Negative result 1 (failure-type blindness): the final-label confidence probe, calibrated overall, systematically misses capability gaps and PITs more than near misses at the 0.5 cutoff (8B: 81% vs 47%); the controlled PIT-vs-near-miss difference is +0.125 [+0.104, +0.143] at 8B, exceeding the minimum effect; +0.037 [+0.012, +0.064] at 4B, below it.
- Negative result 2 (ordering reversal): a combined probe trained on rollout rates over the same states at the same read site ranks PITs below near misses (AUROC of PIT vs near miss: 0.76 confidence vs 0.40 combined at 8B; 0.67 vs 0.36 at 4B), so either ordering is readable from the states; the probes also differ in attention mask, step weighting, and schedule, so labels alone are not isolated.
- Negative result 3 (localization): no single probe beats the length rule (Hit@±2) at locating the PIT step — pointer probe 0.4632 ± 0.0134 vs rule 0.4692 on 2,289 held-out 4B PITs (0 of 10 runs above); 0.4293 ± 0.0120 vs 0.4462 on the 818 gold PITs (0 of 5); the 8B value probe below the rule in all 5 seeds (0.429/0.405 vs 0.503); only the 5-seed ensemble under inverse-frequency weighting exceeds the rule (+0.0262 [+0.0022, +0.0495]).
- Identifiability theory: Proposition 1 shows outcome supervision's population optimum is E_Q[Y|h_{1:t}] = E_Q[V_t|h_{1:t}], equaling V_t only under representation sufficiency; Proposition 2 shows exact recovery of realized V*_t from a prefix requires Var(V*_t|s_t) = 0 a.s., a condition the experiments do not measure.
- One positive result: a failure-type classifier reading transcoder activations at layers 19-24 of one completed incorrect trace separates capability-gap problems from PIT problems, beating the best no-hidden-state baseline by 0.055 AUROC at 8B (meets the margin) and 0.049 at 4B (does not).
- Releases code and the rollout-labeled dataset (broad collection: 11,260 incorrect 4B and 9,508 incorrect 8B traces; gold collection: 818 reference PITs).

## Methodology

- **Models and data:** Qwen3 in thinking mode at 4B and 8B, on hard mathematics problems from DeepMath-103K and the training split of Hendrycks MATH. Traces are split at blank lines and merged into steps of at least 400 tokens; prefixes are evaluated at steps 0, 1, 2, then every second step.
- **Rollout labeling:** Two collections supply ground truth. The broad collection labels every incorrect trace at both scales (8 rollouts per prefix, 16 in refinement); the gold collection (4B only, 4→8 rollouts) provides 818 reference PIT steps. Failure types come from the envelope of rollout rates against θ = 0.1, chosen above 1/16 so a single success in 16 rollouts does not mark a prefix recoverable. A replayed rollout search (bisection over stored rates) localizes the reference PIT within 2 steps for 80.4% of gold PITs while reading 6.6 evaluated prefixes per trace — the rollout-cost ceiling that probes try to avoid.
- **Probes:** All read frozen-model hidden states at the read site layers {23,24} (selected by detection AUROC on final labels only). The confidence probe is a causal transformer encoder over steps with the final label broadcast to every step (per-step Bernoulli loss, temperature-scaled calibration). The combined probe trains on rollout rates of incorrect traces (final label kept on correct traces); the value probe restricts this to recoverable traces; the pointer probe is a bidirectional transformer over completed traces with a step-distribution head trained on PIT steps, plus auxiliary outputs (failure type under two loss weightings). The failure-type classifier reads transcoder activations (layers 19-24) of one completed incorrect trace per problem.
- **Baselines:** For detection, a gradient-boosted classifier on 11 trace-text features (verification/backtracking sentence frequency, answer position, sentence counts/lengths) plus a logistic regression on the same features. For localization, the length rule t = round(f(L-1)) with f refitted per evaluation set, motivated by median PIT relative depths of 0.70 (4B) and 0.75 (8B).
- **Statistics:** Problem-level train/validation splits (no problem in both), 95% problem-clustered bootstrap intervals, five seeds per probe, prespecified 0.05 AUROC margin and 0.08 minimum effect; controlled comparisons via a logistic miss model over failure type, V*_0, trace length, answer position, and problem difficulty.

## Results & Highlights

- **Detection (final-answer wrongness):** confidence probe AUROC 0.8136 ± 0.0049 (4B) and 0.8088 ± 0.0084 (8B) vs text baseline 0.7783 (4B) and 0.7725 (8B); paired difference +0.0353 [+0.0319, +0.0387] at 4B and +0.0362 [+0.0324, +0.0403] at 8B — significant but below the prespecified 0.05 margin, so the probe does not demonstrate hidden-state-specific information for detection. Adding the probe's score to the text features adds a further +0.0369/+0.0406 AUROC. Calibration is good (ECE 0.0143 at 4B, 0.0243 at 8B before scaling; ~0.01 after).
- **Failure-type miss rates (cutoff 0.5, final step):** at 8B the probe misses 79.7% of capability gaps, 81.5% of PITs, but only 46.6% of near misses; at 4B, 71.7% / 71.4% / 49.0%. After controls for V*_0, length, answer position, and difficulty, the PIT-vs-near-miss difference is +0.125 [+0.104, +0.143] at 8B (exceeds the 0.08 minimum effect) and +0.037 [+0.012, +0.064] at 4B (below it).
- **Ordering reversal:** AUROC of PITs vs near misses by final-step score — confidence probe 0.76 (8B) / 0.67 (4B), combined probe 0.40 (8B) / 0.36 (4B): the same hidden states at the same read site support both orderings depending on training design.
- **Failure-type classification (transcoders):** capability-gap vs PIT problems, exceeding the best no-hidden-state baseline by +0.055 AUROC at 8B (meets the 0.05 margin, mean over 6 repetitions of 5-fold CV) and +0.049 at 4B (misses it); the same classifier on raw hidden states scores lower, which the authors attribute to transcoder width (40-64x) but did not test a width-matched random encoding.
- **Localization (Hit@±2 on identical PITs):** length rule 0.4692 vs pointer probe 0.4632 ± 0.0134 on 2,289 held-out 4B broad-collection PITs (probe above rule in 0 of 10 runs); on the 818 gold reference PITs, 0.4462 vs 0.4293 ± 0.0120 (0 of 5); the 8B value probe scores 0.429/0.405 (two input sets) vs the rule's 0.503, below in all 5 seeds; the final-label probe is also below the rule in all runs (0 of 20 pooled). Only the inverse-frequency ensemble edges out the rule by +0.0262 [+0.0022, +0.0495]; the uniform-weight ensemble (+0.0166, interval contains zero) does not.
- **Population structure:** capability gaps are the most frequent failure type at both scales, and fewer than half of incorrect traces are recoverable at step 0; PIT steps sit late (median relative depth 0.70/0.75), which is what makes the length rule hard to beat. Shares are stable (±2 pp) for θ in [0.05, 0.10].
- **Value-probe fit vs usefulness:** the value probe's prediction at the last evaluated prefix separates near misses from PITs at AUROC 0.91 at both scales — but this target (rollout rate vs θ) is its own training label, so it measures fit, not generalization; its per-step MAE exceeds the error of predicting zero in every seed.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** The failure taxonomy itself is assembled from known pieces (rollout-based recoverability from Self-Explore/Setlur et al.; capability-gap difficulty regions; near misses from resampling studies), but the PIT-step definition via the envelope of best reachable values is crisp and new, as is the head-to-head demonstration that two probes on identical states reverse the PIT/near-miss ordering. The systematic negative evaluation against a length rule is the most novel contribution.
- **Rigor:** Above workshop average — prespecified margins, controlled logistic models for confounds, paired seed-level comparisons, and honest treatment of label noise (two collections assign different types to shared traces; correcting yes/no grading errors shifts the 8B capability-gap miss rate from 0.797 to 0.779, the largest change). Two disclosed defects matter: (1) a read-position bug — for about half of the traces the position read for the final step lies in an earlier step, and results were not re-scored with corrected positions; (2) the final-label probe's training traces were not recorded, so some evaluation PITs may have been in its training set. Both are disclosed rather than hidden, which is commendable, but the first directly weakens every localization claim.
- **Reproducibility:** Good — code, dataset, seeds {42, 137, 271, 314, 577}, and sampling conventions documented in appendices; the trace-generation-once-then-reuse protocol limits runaway variance. Remaining obstacles are compute (two full rollout collections over Qwen3-4B/8B) and the unrecorded confidence-probe seeds.
- **Significance:** The negative localization result is the paper's real payload: it warns that "hidden states know where reasoning failed" (a common implicit assumption in probing and process-reward work) is not established — a one-parameter length rule is the baseline to beat, and nothing here beats it individually. The transcoder classifier's +0.055 at 8B is suggestive but fragile (fails at 4B, no width-matched random-encoding control, problem-level rather than trace-level scoring, and applicable only after a trace has already ended incorrectly).
- **Red flags:** The localization headline could be misread as "hidden states contain no PIT information," but the authors carefully bound the claim to the tested probes, read site, and label noise (4-16 rollouts per prefix at every second step), and Proposition 2 is explicitly stated not to explain the results. The near-miss/PIT AUROC of 0.91 for the value probe is presented with the caveat that it measures fit to its own training target — easy to cherry-pick out of context. Single model family (Qwen3 thinking mode), math-only problems, and success defined by final-answer correctness (so a lucky guess after invalid reasoning counts as recovery) further bound generality.

## Strengths

- Converts a vague question ("can probes find reasoning failures?") into three well-defined, rollout-grounded quantities (failure type, PIT step, final-answer detection) with prespecified success criteria and baselines that are actually strong (a refitted length rule is embarrassingly competitive).
- The ordering-reversal experiment is a genuinely informative control: it shows probe-read conclusions about failure types are design-dependent, undermining naive "read the hidden states" interpretations.
- Theory (Propositions 1-2) is matched to practice without overclaiming — the identifiability gap is named, and the paper does not use it to explain away its negative results.
- Exceptional transparency: read-position bug, label noise, missing run metadata, and per-venue caveats are all disclosed; the 818-PIT gold collection with reference steps is a reusable artifact for future localization work.

## Limitations

- One model family (Qwen3, thinking mode) on mathematics only (DeepMath-103K, MATH train); failure-type shares and probe behavior under other domains, coding tasks, or larger scales are unknown.
- PIT labels rest on 4-16 rollouts per prefix evaluated only every second step; the ceiling on achievable Hit@±2 against these noisy labels is unknown, and the two collections disagree on some shared traces.
- The read-position defect (final-step states read from an earlier step in ~half the traces) was not re-scored, so localization numbers characterize the stored states, not the corrected ones.
- The confidence/combined probe comparison changes labels, attention mask, step weighting, and training schedule simultaneously — the label effect is not isolated; the transcoder-classifier advantage lacks a width-matched random-encoding control.
- At 8B, localization was tested only with the value probe, and the text baseline's failure-type miss pattern was not measured, leaving the failure-type blindness result probe-specific.

## Relevance

与"推理模型的可解释性与失败诊断"方向高度相关：本文提供了 rollout 标定的失败类型 taxonomy、818 条参考 PIT 标注数据集和一套预注册的探针评测协议，对做 process reward model、step-level verification 或 LLM self-monitoring 的研究是直接可复用的基线与负结果警告——尤其是"长度规则即是最强定位基线"这一点，任何声称能从内部状态定位推理错误步骤的工作都应先与它对比。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would the pointer/value probes beat the length rule if PIT labels were derived from far more rollouts (e.g., 64 per prefix), or is the label-noise ceiling the binding constraint rather than probe capacity?
- Does the PIT/near-miss ordering reversal survive when the two probes are matched in attention mask, step weighting, and training schedule, isolating the label's effect?
- Can a probe running causally at generation time (before the trace completes) predict that the *next* step is about to become the PIT step — the practically useful version of localization — even if post-hoc PIT recovery fails?
- Do the failure-type shares and the confidence probe's blindness pattern replicate on non-math domains (code, agentic tasks) and on non-Qwen3 model families, where calibration and verbosity differ?
- Would width-matched random projections of the hidden states match the transcoder failure-type classifier's +0.055 AUROC at 8B, or is sparse feature structure genuinely responsible?
- Since the length rule exploits only the late median depth of PIT steps, would training a pointer probe with a position-debiasing objective (predicting depth relative to the length-rule prediction) reveal any residual step-specific signal?
