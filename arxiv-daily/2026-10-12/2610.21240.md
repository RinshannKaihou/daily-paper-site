> **一句话总结：** 提出"重分配敏感性分数"（RSS），从 SAE 特征中筛出在多种保语义提示变换下方向一致、可复现地发生变化的稀疏特征支撑集，并证明这些不看读出信号选出的特征（中位数仅 19–20 个）能在冻结的 WildGuard 安全线性读出上解释大部分余量侵蚀、以 0.935/0.958 的 AUROC 预测复发性读出失败。

> **TL;DR:** The paper introduces the Redistribution Sensitivity Score (RSS), which selects sparse-autoencoder features whose responses to meaning-preserving prompt transformations recur in a consistent direction, then evaluates them on held-out variants. Across 27 pretrained SAEs (GPT-2 Small to Llama 3.1 8B), context/wrapper transformations redistribute more activity than local edits in 27/27 SAEs (mean normalized gap 0.874, 95% CI [0.867, 0.881]). On two frozen WildGuardMix safety probes (Gemma 3 4B layer 17, Llama 3.1 8B layer 24), clean margin minus RSS-support erosion ranks recurrent readout failure at AUROC 0.935/0.958 with median supports of just 20/19 features, versus 0.965/0.964 using full erosion — i.e., a 20-feature readout-agnostic support nearly matches the full probe's failure prediction, while selected features alone reconstruct held-out logit changes at R^2 ≈ 0.58/0.63 and reversing them restores ~72% of transformed-probe errors.

## 核心贡献

- **RSS（重分配敏感性分数）**：一种三段式特征筛选方法——先按平均绝对响应幅度形成候选池，再要求响应的复现率 r_f ≥ ρ 与方向一致性 c_f ≥ κ（κ > 1/2，平局被排除），最后保留幅度最大的 k 个特征作为"响应支撑集"，并用 MeanRSS 汇总其幅度。支撑与方向仅在选定变体上固定，然后在完全不相交的留出变体上评估。
- **有限样本方向保证（命题 2）**：当 n=5、ρ=κ=0.8 时，被选特征至少在 4/5 个观测上同向、至多 1 个反向，因此改变或删除单个观测不会翻转其主方向——给出了选择阶段的方向稳定性证明。
- **表征变化与下游效应的精确分离**：对固定线性读出，命题 3 给出精确分解 e_f = −y·w_f·s_f·A_f·η_f（侵蚀 = 类别符号 × 读出权重 × 选定方向 × 响应幅度 × 对齐度），说明为什么大响应未必致错、而边界附近的小对齐响应却可以。
- **跨 27 个预训练 SAE 的系统性实证**：六种保语义提示变换（无关上下文、干扰上下文、元语言包装、格式包装、标点噪声、类型化词汇替换）产生可复现的重分配剖面——上下文/包装类变换的重分配在全部 27 个 SAE、全部 1,000 条提示上都大于局部编辑。
- **紧凑支撑的审计价值**：不看读出信息选出的 RSS 支撑（中位数 20/19 个特征，而完整读出有 415/862 个非零系数）在 20% 离线复核预算下捕获 89.2%/92.8% 的错误与 87.3%/94.4% 的有害漏报。

## Key Contributions

- **Redistribution Sensitivity Score (RSS):** a three-stage feature filter — magnitude-based candidate pool from mean absolute change h_f, then recurrence (r_f ≥ ρ) and directional-consistency (c_f ≥ κ > 1/2) constraints, then the k largest eligible responses form the support S, summarized by MeanRSS. Supports and directions are fixed on selection variants V_sel and evaluated without recomputation on disjoint held-out variants V_ho.
- **Finite-sample directional margin (Proposition 2):** with n = 5 selector observations and ρ = κ = 0.8, every selected feature has at least 4/5 observations in the dominant direction and at most 1 opposed, so changing or removing a single observation cannot reverse the selected direction.
- **Exact separation of representation change from downstream effect (Proposition 3):** for a fixed linear readout, signed margin erosion factors as e_f = −y w_f s_f A_f η_f, making explicit how response magnitude, readout weight, directional alignment, and the clean margin jointly determine whether a recurrent change matters; this explains why large changes can be harmless and small aligned changes decisive near a decision boundary.
- **Systematic evidence across 27 pretrained SAEs** (GPT-2 Small, Pythia 70M, Gemma 3 270M/1B/4B, Gemma 2 2B, Mistral 7B, Llama 3.1 8B; Gemma Scope / Gemma Scope 2 / Llama Scope / Pythia / GPT-2 SAE checkpoints) that six meaning-preserving prompt transformations produce a reproducible redistribution profile on held-out variants.
- **Readout-agnostic compact supports with audit utility:** median 20/19-feature RSS supports, selected without any readout information, retain Margin Contribution Retention (MCR) of 43.0%/56.2% at K = 20, reconstruct held-out logit shifts (R^2 ≈ 0.58/0.63), and at a 20% offline review budget capture 89.2%/92.8% of probe errors.

## Methodology

All quantities are defined per pretrained SAE, layer, base prompt, and transformation setting. Given a clean prompt x_0 and transformed variants x_v, the feature change at the aligned target position is δ_{v,f} = a_f(x_v) − a_f(x_0); the mean absolute change h_f forms a top-M candidate pool C_M. Selector observations (per-operator mean changes, 5 operators per setting) yield counts n_f^+ (change > τ) and n_f^− (change < −τ), from which recurrence r_f = (n^+ + n^−)/n, directional consistency c_f = max(n^+, n^−)/(n^+ + n^−), and dominant direction s_f = sign(n^+ − n^−) are computed. Eligible features satisfy r_f ≥ ρ and c_f ≥ κ; the k = min(K, |E_M|) largest-h_f eligible features form the support S, and MeanRSS averages their h_f. Proposition 1 shows this is the constrained magnitude maximizer.

For downstream analysis, a linear readout z(x) = w^T ã(x) + b with margin m(x) = y[z(x) − t] is fit on clean data only and then frozen. Per-feature average response magnitude A_f^U, alignment η_f^U ∈ [−1, 1] with the RSS direction, and signed margin erosion e_f^U = −y w_f · mean(δ̃_{v,f}) are defined; Proposition 3 gives the exact identity e_f^U = −y w_f s_f A_f^U η_f^U, so the sign rule sign(−y w_f s_f) predicts the erosion sign exactly when η_f^U > 0. Full (E) and support (E^S) average erosion combine with the clean margin into residual margins m_res = m_0 − E and m_res^S = m_0 − E^S; MCR measures the fraction of absolute held-out contribution retained by S. Linearity further gives an exact per-variant logit decomposition and a readout-only counterfactual m_cf(x_v) that restores selected readout inputs to clean values — reversing an error (m_cf > 0) without touching the LM's activations. Selective review ranks cases by residual margin computed on selection variants against errors on held-out variants.

Experiments use 1,000 fixed English prompts across 12 content categories and six transformation settings (irrelevant context, distractor context, metalinguistic wrapper, format wrapper = context/wrapper; punctuation noise, typed lexical substitution = local edits), each with 5 operators × 3 realizations. The functional study audits two frozen safety probes trained on 1,800 balanced WildGuardMix prompts (1,200/300/300 train/val/test) at Gemma 3 4B layer 17 and Llama 3.1 8B layer 24; one realization per operator forms V_sel and the other two (10 variants) form V_ho, with "recurrent failure" defined as ≥ 2 of 10 held-out margins negative. Filtering for correct clean prediction, available support, and complete outcomes leaves 1,524 Gemma and 1,445 Llama cases; confidence intervals bootstrap clustered by base prompt.

## Results & Highlights

- **Redistribution is systematic (Section 4.1):** the normalized context+wrapper MeanRSS mean exceeds the local-edit mean in **all 27 of 27 pretrained SAEs**, with an average normalized gap of **0.874 (95% bootstrap CI [0.867, 0.881])**; after aggregation across SAEs the ordering holds for **all 1,000 prompts**, and every context/wrapper setting exceeds both local edits for 997 of them.
- **Persistence beyond selection:** on held-out variants the response rate in the selected direction is **0.952 for RSS versus 0.758 for size- and pool-matched random supports**; in the functional study RSS beats magnitude-only selection on held-out directional response rate (**0.876 vs 0.816 Gemma; 0.871 vs 0.794 Llama**). Intensity calibration: median prompt-level Spearman correlation between edit intensity and MeanRSS is 0.95 (punctuation), 0.74 (irrelevant context), 0.50 (format wrapper).
- **Selected features are functionally loaded, not just loud:** matching RSS features to unselected features by readout-coefficient magnitude |w_f|, mean absolute held-out contribution is **0.240 vs 0.022 (Gemma) and 0.370 vs 0.022 (Llama)** — RSS features carry roughly 11–17x more erosion and are larger in 96.9%/98.0% of matched pairs; the Proposition-3 sign rule agrees with the realized erosion sign in 97.4%/97.1% of cases.
- **Failure prediction with tiny supports (Section 4.3):** although probe AUROC stays above 0.90 under every transformation, 4.2–9.5% of decisions flip. Clean margin alone ranks recurrent failure at AUROC **0.863/0.901**; replacing full erosion with RSS-support erosion (median **20/19 features** vs 415/862 nonzero readout coefficients) yields **0.935/0.958**, close to the full residual margin's **0.965/0.964**. Every case with negative full residual margin shows recurrent failure.
- **Localization and audit utility (Section 4.4):** RSS supports reconstruct held-out logit changes at **R^2 ≈ 0.58 (Gemma) / 0.63 (Llama)** versus ~0.01–0.02 for random or globally unstable features; restoring the ~20 selected readout inputs to clean values reverses **72.2%/71.8% of transformed-probe errors**; at a **20% offline review budget** the RSS residual margin captures **89.2%/92.8% of errors and 87.3%/94.4% of harmful false negatives**. At K = 20, MCR is **43.0%/56.2%** versus 32.6%/43.5% for supports selected for a mismatched transformation (the readout-aware |e_f| ranking retains > 90% but uses readout information).

## Authors & Credibility

Two authors (Ziye Mei, Haotian Ma) from the Department of Computer Sciences at the University of Wisconsin–Madison. The work is careful and unusually self-limiting in its claims: the decomposition is exact (linear readout ⇒ no approximation), the evaluation protocol separates selection variants from held-out variants throughout, and conclusions are consistently framed as applying to the tested transformations and frozen linear readouts rather than to model behavior in general. Code is released at github.com/OpenSAE/Redistribution-Sensitivity-Score, the SAE checkpoints are public (Gemma Scope, Gemma Scope 2, Llama Scope, Pythia SAEs, GPT-2 reformatted SAEs), and the prompt corpus and WildGuardMix are described in reproducible detail, so the main experiments should be reproducible. The paper builds directly on well-established lines — prompt-sensitivity (Sclar et al. 2024; Mizrahi et al. 2024), SAE feature absorption and sensitivity (Chanin et al. 2025; Tian et al. 2025), and SAE probing (Kantamneni et al. 2025) — and states precisely how RSS differs from each.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** the core move — requiring recurrence and directional consistency before calling a feature "sensitive," then freezing the support for held-out evaluation — is a modest but genuinely useful methodological upgrade over single-prompt feature-diffing or aggregate activation distances, and the exact erosion decomposition (Prop. 3) is clean. The framing "representation change ≠ failure" is the paper's most valuable conceptual contribution.
- **Rigor:** strong on evaluation hygiene (disjoint V_sel/V_ho, readout frozen before transformed evaluation, |w_f|-matched controls, magnitude-only and mismatched-transformation controls, bootstrap CIs clustered by prompt). However, the "recurrent failure" label (≥ 2 of 10 held-out margins negative) is a somewhat arbitrary operationalization, and no sensitivity analysis of that threshold appears in the main text.
- **The downstream readouts are the authors' own linear probes, not the model's safety machinery.** "Two frozen safety readouts" are logistic probes trained on WildGuardMix activations — a proxy for downstream computation, not an actual refusal circuit or production classifier; the counterfactual restoration changes only the readout's inputs, not the LM's activations or generated text, so no causal claim at the model level is supported (the paper says this explicitly, but the abstract's "safety" framing invites over-reading).
- **The headline AUROC gain over baselines is moderate:** clean margin alone already achieves 0.863/0.901, so the 20-feature RSS erosion adds roughly +5–7 points to reach 0.935/0.958, still below the full residual margin (0.965/0.964). The honest takeaway — compact readout-agnostic supports nearly match full erosion — is supported, but a reader of only the abstract could overestimate the delta.
- **Holdout scope:** the functional holdout uses new realizations of the *same* five operators, not new transformation types; transfer of supports to genuinely unseen prompt-manipulation families (or adversarial paraphrasers) is untested. MCR also drops sharply for mismatched-transformation supports (43.0% → 32.6% on Gemma), hinting that support specificity may limit generality.
- **Magnitude calibration is uneven:** the intensity–MeanRSS Spearman correlation falls to 0.50 for the format wrapper, so the score does not uniformly track perturbation strength across transformation classes.

## Strengths

- Clean, fully exact theory: Propositions 1–3 give a constrained-optimization characterization, a finite-sample direction-stability guarantee, and an exact per-feature erosion identity — no approximations anywhere in the readout analysis.
- Disciplined evaluation design: selection/holdout splits at the realization level, controls matched on support size, candidate pool, and readout weight |w_f|, and confidence intervals clustered by base prompt.
- Broad empirical base: 27 pretrained SAEs across 8 model checkpoints from 70M to 8B parameters, plus a focused two-model functional study — the 27/27, 1000/1000 universality of the context-vs-local-edit ordering is a robust, quotable finding.
- Practical payoff: ~20 readout-agnostic features that capture ~90% of errors at a 20% review budget and reverse ~72% of transformation-induced probe errors are directly useful for anyone auditing linear classifiers over SAE features.
- Public code and reliance solely on public SAE checkpoints and datasets.

## Limitations

- All downstream conclusions concern frozen *linear* readouts fit by the authors; non-linear or the model's own internal decision mechanisms are out of scope, and the readout counterfactual is offline (it alters the readout computation, not model behavior).
- The functional holdout tests new realizations of the same operators rather than new transformation families, so operator-level generalization of supports is unmeasured.
- The study covers six hand-designed transformation settings on English prompts; natural distribution shift, multilingual inputs, and adversarially optimized prompts are not covered.
- RSS does not maximize contribution retention — the readout-aware |e_f| ranking retains > 90% MCR versus RSS's 43–56% at K = 20 — so if readout information is available, RSS is not the optimal selector; its value is specifically the readout-agnostic setting.
- Intensity calibration varies by transformation type (Spearman 0.95 punctuation vs 0.50 format wrapper), and MeanRSS correlates only weakly with held-out margin erosion on its own.

## Relevance

对关注稀疏自编码器可解释性与提示鲁棒性的研究非常有价值：它把"提示敏感度"从行为层面下沉到 SAE 特征层面，并给出了一套可复用的、带有限样本保证的敏感特征筛选与下游侵蚀归因工具，可直接用于审计安全探针与特征级归因分析。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Do RSS supports transfer across transformation *families* (e.g., supports selected on context/wrapper operators predicting erosion under adversarial paraphrase or multilingual rewrite), or is the 43–56% MCR specificity a fundamental constraint of readout-agnostic selection?
- Can the exact erosion decomposition be extended beyond linear readouts — e.g., to attention-value pathways, multi-layer probes, or the model's own refusal direction — while preserving the sign-rule guarantee of Proposition 3?
- Does the universal context/wrapper > local-edit redistribution ordering persist under natural distribution shift (chat formats, system prompts, tool-use scaffolds) rather than synthetic operators?
- Would restoring RSS-selected feature values inside the LM (true activation-level counterfactual) reverse behavioral errors at rates comparable to the ~72% seen at the readout level?
- How does the recurrent-failure label (≥ 2 of 10 margins negative) affect the reported AUROCs — does the RSS-vs-clean-margin gap survive stricter or looser failure thresholds?
