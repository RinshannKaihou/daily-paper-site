Why this matters

可证明安全、改动极小、已落入 vLLM 系引擎的混合模型 serving 修复方案。

Critical edge

Every result comes from one synthetic serving pattern — candidate-conditioned prefill, four branches per request, one generated token. No decode-heavy, multi-turn, agentic, or production-trace workloads.

Methodology

01 · formalize → fix provably → attribute

The defect. Hybrid LLMs interleave linear-attention (Mamba / GDN) with full-attention layers, so resuming a cached branch needs KV pages plus recurrent / convolutional state at the match boundary. If the producer branch is still computing that state in the same worker round, the consumer is matched but not runnable — and vLLM's scheduler ends its waiting-queue scan at that first failure, stranding every independent branch behind it. Idle batch rows are the paper's "execution bubbles."

arrived branches→ 5 ms flexible admission→ scan in native order ≺
READYselect into batch
BLOCKEDpark consumer; keep scanning · recheck t+1
OTHERnative action (may stop)
execute round → publish state → wake parked consumers

The fix (Algorithm 1). State-ready traversal parks only the blocked consumer for a next-round recheck and keeps scanning for independent READY work. Selection changes; native state checks, cache policy, and request order do not. Each round's batch is a provable superset of the native one, with no avoidable bubble left under monotone readiness.

Harness. A 5 ms admission deadline (~9% of the 52.95 ms native worker-call interval on Qwen3.5-0.8B) with early release; optional +Frag guard. QuALITY + RACE-M candidate scoring: 9,306 requests × 4 branches (37,224 paired branches per model), six-policy ablation on vLLM 0.29 / A800 GPUs, full-attention Qwen3-8B control, exact first-token agreement audit.

Strengths

03 · what holds up

  • Formalizes a real, previously unformalized defect — "cache hit ≠ runnable branch" — that PagedAttention, RadixAttention, Marconi, and SGLang implicitly assume away.
  • Minimally invasive and provably safe: native checks, cache policy, and order untouched; each round's batch is a provable superset of native — attractive for upstreaming.
  • Exceptional hygiene: frozen calibrated budgets, six-arm ablation isolating admission vs traversal, a full-attention control that cleanly nulls the mechanism, identity-based cost accounting.
  • Actionable for practitioners running Qwen3.5 / Nemotron hybrids under prefix-heavy loads — reuse essentially unchanged, tails improved, outputs audited.

Limitations

04 · what to watch

  • One workload family: multi-candidate scoring, one generated token, fixed four branches — no decode-dominant, multi-turn, agentic, or production-trace evaluation, no load sweep.
  • Gains thin out at scale: only −3.1% mean latency on 27B despite −44% rounds; cost shift and overload queueing absorb the savings.
  • Baseline is the authors' instrumented overlay, not stock vLLM+APC; lands in vllm-jev, not mainline; no public artifact link in the paper.
  • Up to ~1.5% decoded-output divergence (0.8B model) is reported but unexplained — no repeat-vs-repeat control, no downstream-accuracy check.

Questions for Further Exploration

05 · open threads

  • Do the gains survive decode-dominant and multi-turn workloads, where long generations replace the four-branch scoring structure?
  • Does traversal still help at larger batch capacities, or with async scheduling and CUDA graphs enabled (the evaluation froze vLLM to eager execution)?
  • Is the ~1.5% worst-case output divergence on Qwen3.5-0.8B batch-shape numerics — and does it move downstream answer accuracy at all?
  • How does RadixFlow compose with chunked prefill (Sarathi-Serve), prefill/decode disaggregation (DistServe / Splitwise), or Marconi-style state retention?
  • Is the fixed 5 ms admission deadline robust across models and offered loads — could an adaptive deadline capture more?
  • Does the same head-of-line blocking defect exist in SGLang's scheduler, and would state-ready traversal port cleanly to it?
