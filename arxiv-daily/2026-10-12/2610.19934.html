Why this matters

Compressed reasoning models fail at terminating, not at thinking — a training-free steering fix, and a template for diagnosing compression-induced reliability loss.

把剪枝引发的精度退化定性为终止行为失效而非能力丢失，并用隐状态差均值方向做免训练修复——既是压缩模型的可靠性诊断，也是激活转向在推理模型上的扎实应用。

⚠ Critical edge

Accuracy gains are modest (+1–4 pts); the default experiments run on the authors' own OBC-Prune pipeline; and at high sparsity only weight retraining (Masked-LoRA) recovers real capability.

Methodology

Diagnose the loop, then steer out of it

Six distilled reasoning models (DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen/Llama 1.5B–14B, OpenThinker3) pruned to 10–70% sparsity via SparseGPT+OBC (default), SparseGPT+RAC, and Wanda+C4; evaluated on MATH-500, AIME-25, LiveCodeBench-v6 with a 16K token budget.

  1. 01Decompose the loss — split errors on dense-correct problems into non-termination vs. wrong-but-terminated.
  2. 02Probe the stop signal — teacher-force dense traces through pruned checkpoints; measure p(</think>).
  3. 03Extract a direction — difference-in-means over terminating vs. looping continuations of the same prefix; pick the layer by held-out ROC AUC.
  4. 04TGAS at inference — add α·v to the residual stream, every token; one direction per model reused across all sparsity levels. Training-free.

Strengths
  • A crisp, falsifiable reframe — "reasoning divergence" — backed by four independent measurements that all converge.
  • The intervention fixes exactly what the diagnosis predicts: termination up, wrong-answer rate unchanged, decision points halved.
  • One direction per model, reused across every sparsity level (cosine ≥ 0.87, label-shuffle controls in all 15 pairs).
  • Includes the training-based baseline that beats it — and says so.
  • 7.6–12.3% fewer tokens at equal or better accuracy.
Limitations
  • Post-hoc repair only — repairs pruning damage rather than preventing it; termination-aware pruning is future work.
  • All six models are distilled/SFT, ≤14B; RL-trained reasoning models untested.
  • Math + code only, with a 16K budget — non-termination is budget-relative by construction.
  • Unstructured sparsity only; no 2:4 semi-structured patterns.
  • Needs a per-model calibration pass plus per-checkpoint α tuning — training-free, not hyperparameter-free.
Questions for further exploration
  1. Bake the termination-direction constraint into the pruning objective itself — prevent divergence instead of patching it?
  2. Does the diagnosis transfer to RL-trained reasoning models and 2:4 semi-structured sparsity?
  3. Is the termination direction causally tied to the </think> output circuit — probing or SAEs?
  4. Can TGAS + Masked-LoRA combine at high sparsity — steering for stopping, adapter for capability?
  5. Does the direction transfer across model families — a shared "commitment" geometry among R1-distilled models?
  6. Does quantization-induced overthinking share the same ∆cyc signature — one compression-pathology detector?
