Why this matters

Per-token halting and KV-reuse in recurrent-depth models assume a stabilized output means a freezable state — direct evidence here says it does not.

本文为循环深度模型的按位置停机与 KV 复用假设，提供了明确的「何时可以冻结状态」负面证据。

⚠ Critical edge

Everything rests on a synthetic FSM suite with one 2-layer, width-128 block — no LLM-scale or real-data evidence is offered.

Methodology

A simulator that scores every position, loop, and counterfactual

Mealy table + 32 actions, in context→ shared 2-layer block × 32 loops→ exit-only readout qk, yk

+E(x) residual each loop · width 128, 4 heads · no positional / loop-depth embeddings

  • Task. Deterministic Mealy-machine execution: a transition table as tokens plus an action sequence; predict state and output at every position. A simulator supplies exact targets and counterfactual continuations, so interventions are scored against a unique correct answer.
  • Objectives differ only in which exit positions are supervised: FINAL-ONLY (last), ALL-PREFIX (all 32), SAMPLED-PREFIX (one uniformly drawn position per example — FINAL-ONLY's budget, ALL-PREFIX's expected loss).
  • Controls. Exit distribution Slack-4 vs Jitter-8; gradient truncation PROGRESSIVE vs MATCHED-RANDOM-EXIT at identical supervised exits. 132k AdamW steps, 35-stage curriculum to H=32.
  • Interventions. Interchange swaps of full hidden vectors (scored against simulator counterfactuals), self-swaps, and per-position freezing at loop k+d — emulating Universal-Transformer halting — vs an external answer cache.

Errors start one step downstream; even the anti-overthinking maze network of Bansal et al. (2022) solves 0/32 mazes frozen at arrival, 32/32 four iterations later.

  • Computation itself is exact. Swapping one 128-d hidden vector at the frontier steers all 24 later positions to the simulator's exact counterfactual — 237/237 eligible pairs, in each of ten models.
  • Exit distribution beats slack at matched budget: Jitter-8 85.6% vs Slack-4 30.0% at loop 64; gradient truncation (PROGRESSIVE adaptation) stalls 2 of 3 seeds below 60%.
  • Retention ≠ extrapolation. At H64/loop-96: MATCHED-RANDOM-EXIT 93.0% vs SAMPLED-PREFIX 82.7% — the best-retaining objective generalizes worse.
Strengths

Why the evidence is convincing

  • A clean compute / retain / reuse decomposition with a design that actually separates the three — not one accuracy-over-loops curve.
  • The 237/237 interchange result (ten models) shows frontier states are causally sufficient for exact symbolic continuation, while answers simultaneously fade.
  • Matched-budget and matched-exit controls (Jitter-8 vs Slack-4; PROGRESSIVE vs MATCHED-RANDOM-EXIT) — rare discipline in this literature.
  • Freeze-vs-cache isolates the exact assumption behind per-position halting and KV-reuse, validated on an external published checkpoint.
  • Unusually transparent limitations: non-independent checkpoints, and what the interventions cannot identify, are stated openly.
Limitations

Read it with these caveats

  • One synthetic task family (Mealy machines ≤ 8 states), one 2-layer width-128 block; the only external check is a single maze checkpoint — no pretrained-recurrent-LM evidence.
  • Full-vector swaps establish sufficiency, not a unique encoding; tests cannot separate “content not yet consolidated” from timing/order cues that freezing also freezes.
  • SAMPLED-PREFIX needs intermediate labels at inference-relevant positions — not a label-free fix for production recurrent LMs.
  • Budget controls match expected loops, not measured FLOPs; six paired seeds for original comparisons, three for new controls.
  • Downstream-reset mechanisms untested on the new controls; finite-window retention does not establish convergence or length extrapolation.
Questions for further exploration
  1. Does the 2–4 loop readability→reusability gap scale with width, depth, horizon, or task complexity — or is it a constant of the architecture family?
  2. Can an unsupervised signal (KV stability, attention convergence, Jacobian criteria) predict when a state becomes reusable, replacing the simulator frontier τ(k)=k?
  3. Would loop-depth or positional embeddings eliminate the freeze fragility by moving timing out of the evolving state?
  4. Do SAMPLED-PREFIX-style objectives transfer to pretrained recurrent-depth LMs where per-position labels are unavailable — e.g., via self-generated readouts at random depths?
  5. Why does MATCHED-RANDOM-EXIT extrapolate better (93.0% vs 82.7% at H64) despite worse whole-trajectory exactness — is retention orthogonal to length generalization?
