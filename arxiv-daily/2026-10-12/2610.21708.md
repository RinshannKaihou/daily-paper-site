> **一句话总结：** 该工作把人类对失控 LLM agent 的干预记录当作"指向失效计算的标签"而非训练数据：在一个受控的澄清-提问测试床（clarify 环境，孪生场景对）上，用规则化监督者（经盲法人类标注验证，κ=0.90–0.97）发出的干预标签训练线性探针，在 5 个合格模型家族中全部实现了对"该问不问 vs 直接行动"决策的因果定位——在残差流单一坐标上外推剂量修补可翻转 72–100% 的 held-out 过度提问（随机方向最多翻转 1/74）；探针门控的运行时转向使模拟监督负担下降（bootstrap 95% CI [0.59, 0.94]–[0.71, 0.94]，全部排除零），而系统提示词指令反而让负担恶化；但把修复固化进权重的结果是"地图而非规则"——仅在 Qwen2.5-3B 上因果带内 LoRA（3.3M 参数）三种子全过预设六项标准、胜过同数据的全层行为克隆（bc 在全部三种子上破坏保留准则 −14/−11/−12 并引入新的重复提问失效），其余四个家族中局部编辑以三种不同方式失败、bc 反而占优，且两条预先写下的几何预测规则均在家族外被证伪（论文按预设停止规则拒绝拟合第三条）。

> **TL;DR:** Reframes human/overseer interventions on misbehaving LLM agents as labels for causal localization. In a controlled twin-pair "clarify" agent testbed (300 pairs/seed, 3 seeds; one-line tasks, 3-item workspaces) covering five eligible model families (Qwen2.5-3B, gemma-2-2b, OLMo-3-7B, Mistral-Nemo-12B, Phi-4; only 5 of 13 screened families produce the needed behavioral contrast), a probe direction patched at one residual-stream coordinate flips 72–100% of held-out over-asks into correct direct actions (e.g., Qwen L28: 97% [91–99] vs 0/74 for random directions; over-ask base rates 84–90%). Probe-gated steering on 200 fresh scenarios/seed cuts overseer interventions from 67/65/72 to 9/0/2 (total failures −74/−80/−83%; bootstrap IRR CIs [0.59,0.94]–[0.71,0.94]) while a prompt-only "don't ask unnecessary questions" instruction makes burden worse on every seed (IRR −20%/−1%/−11%). Weight consolidation is a five-family map, not a rule: causal-band LoRA (3.3M params) beats behavior cloning (15M params, same data) on Qwen 3/3 seeds (bc breaks retention −14/−11/−12 and introduces a de-novo re-ask failure), but loses on three other families in three diagnostically different ways, and both pre-registered geometric rules for predicting the winner were falsified out-of-family. Also: in 4/5 families the model demonstrably knows the request is unambiguous (belief probe decodable but causally inert) and asks anyway — "improve the ambiguity detector" would target the one component that is not broken.

## 核心贡献

- **干预即标签（方法学重构）**：把监督者对 agent 失效的干预记录当作指向失效内部计算的测量信号，而非丢弃或仅作微调数据。干预日志成为定位数据集，监督者的纠正成为因果实验，"编辑是否成功"以监督者负担是否下降（IRR，干预降低率）来评判，而非探针精度——把"可解释性应可行动"（actionability）的标准落到实处。
- **五家族全覆盖的因果定位**：在全部 5 个合格模型家族中，用干预标签训出的探针方向在残差流单一坐标（外推剂量、训练集冻结选择）上翻转 72–100% 的 held-out 过度提问为格式正确、对象正确的直接行动；随机方向在完全相同流程下最多翻转 1/74。位点深度跨家族差异巨大（占网络深度 55–81%），但其存在性与起势尖锐性不变——"杠杆存在"可迁移，"杠杆地址"不可迁移。
- **过度提问的两种机制签名**：4 个家族呈 H-caution（模型明知无歧义仍提问——信念可解码、因果惰性、与决策轴近正交）；Phi-4（唯一校准的过度提问者，36%）呈 H-misrep（其过度提问确实对应不同的内部世界表征）。探针读数可在样本内区分二者，并指向不同修法。
- **门控运行时转向 + 权重编辑五家族地图**：探针门控转向大幅降低模拟监督负担且不伤合法提问与能力电池，而提示词基线全面恶化；权重固化结果为"一胜三败一边界穿越"的地图——Qwen 上因果带 LoRA 稳定击败强模仿基线（据作者所知为首个可复现案例），另三家族局部编辑以三种可诊断方式失败；两条预先写下的预测规则均在家族外死亡，作者按预设停止规则报告证伪并停止。
- **护栏结果（脚手架先于机制）**：最初的候选失效——收到答案后重复提问（45–67%）——根本不在权重里：让脚手架显示 agent 自己上一轮的问题后该失效精确归零；而 bc 编辑后来又通过权重重新制造了同一表面行为。表面行为不足以确定修复层级，廉价的脚手架消融应作为任何机制化流程的前置门槛。
- **罕见的方法学透明度**：预测先于结果写下、约定先于关键模型运行、停止规则被执行；阻断式不变量抓出 5 个 harness bug（其中 2 个本会制造结论）；外部对抗性审计促成冻结选择协议、bootstrap 区间、SAE 读数臂等修正并全部披露；人类盲法标注验证监督者判定（κ=0.900–0.967）。

## Key Contributions

- **Interventions as labels (conceptual reframing).** Human/overseer interventions on agent failures are treated as measurements pointing at the internal computation that just failed, not as discardable events or replayed fine-tuning data. An intervention log becomes a localization dataset; success is scored as reduction of a budgeted overseer's burden (IRR), the "actionability" standard applied literally.
- **Causal localization, five for five among eligible families.** A direction learned from intervention labels, patched at one residual-stream coordinate (projection on the probe direction) at the decision token with the extrapolated dose frozen on training episodes, flips 72–100% of held-out over-asks into well-formed, correctly-targeted actions in every eligible family (Wilson 95% lower bounds above 61%); random directions under the identical procedure flip at most 1/74 anywhere. The causal site's depth varies (55–81% of the stack) — its existence and sharp onset do not.
- **Two mechanistic signatures of over-asking.** H-caution (4 families): the belief that the request is unambiguous is decodable, causally inert in both directions, and orthogonal to the decision axis — the model knows and asks anyway; the natural fix ("improve the ambiguity detector") targets a component that is demonstrably not broken. H-misrep (Phi-4, the only calibrated over-asker): over-asks track genuinely different internal world-representations. A cheap probe readout separates the two.
- **Probe-gated runtime steering vs prompting.** A gate firing only when "belief says unambiguous AND behavior says about-to-ask" removes the majority of simulated oversight burden across seeds while preserving legitimate clarifying questions and the capability battery; a system-prompt instruction makes burden worse on every seed. Cross-model refinement (gemma): specificity lives in the gate; the best lever direction is model-dependent (a style direction beats the behavior direction there, and the geometry explains why).
- **A five-family weight-editing map with two falsified predictors.** One replicated stable win for localized (causal-band) LoRA over the strong behavior-cloning default (Qwen, 3/3 seeds, 3.3M vs 15M params); three diagnostic failure modes elsewhere (unconditional collapse on gemma/Nemo, retention erosion on OLMo-3, criterion-crossing instability on Phi-4); two geometric rules written down in advance both died out-of-family, and per a pre-set stopping rule no third was fit — the map, with ten arm-outcomes and two dead rules, is offered as the constraint any future theory must satisfy.
- **A when-to-edit guardrail.** A frequent first-candidate failure (re-asking an answered question, 45–67%) turned out to be purely scaffold-level (collapses to exactly zero when the agent can see its own prior question) — yet the bc edit later re-created it through the weights: surface behavior underdetermines the fix; cheap scaffold ablations should gate mechanistic pipelines.

## Methodology

The study runs on a deliberately small, fully controlled agent environment ("clarify"): each episode presents a one-line task ("Ship the Sam draft now") over a workspace of three attributed items, and the agent must reply `ACTION: <id>` or `ASK: <one question>`. Every scenario is a twin pair — identical instruction, only workspace content differs, making the request either uniquely resolvable or genuinely ambiguous — and the harness diffs the rendered prompts and refuses to run on any other difference. Ground truth about ambiguity is thus exact, contrasts are matched, and hundreds of balanced episodes are collectable (300 pairs per seed, three seeds per model).

**Labels.** A versioned deterministic rule-based overseer ("rule-v2", the labeling overseer) watches collection episodes and issues verdicts — intervene or not, failure type, canonical correction — as a reproducible, auditable stand-in for a supervising human; its verdicts are validated by blind human annotation (§5.5). A separate **budgeted evaluation overseer** with attention limits and trust dynamics scores fresh evaluation scenarios, defining the burden B = (W + 2I)/N and the headline metric IRR = (B_base − B_edited)/B_base.

**Pipeline (four stages, each trusted only after the next stage's test).**
1. *Collect with the prompt as part of the experiment:* full rollouts with verbatim message lists; blocking invariants — tokenization-identity replay check, pairwise prompt diff, false-positive canary — that caught five harness bugs (two would have manufactured conclusions, e.g., OLMo-3 replies glued to the next role token, deflating the base model).
2. *Probe, then distrust the probe:* at the decision token (final formatting token of the chat template), logistic probes per layer for the upcoming behavior (over-ask vs acted) and for the ambiguity belief (ground-truth label). Splits by pair; permuted-label canaries, a positional canary, and a rich content baseline (bag-of-words + target position + attribute values + distractor counts, reaching 0.93–0.99 AUC on Qwen) accompany every number — decodability is explicitly treated as not probative.
3. *One coordinate, both directions, three controls:* the causal test sets the activation's scalar projection on the probe direction to a value typical of the other class (doses fixed in advance on training episodes: class-mean and extrapolated one inter-class gap past the acting class); the decision is measured as the ASK-vs-ACTION logit gap and flips validated with full generation. Controls: random unit directions (≤1/74 flips anywhere), a style direction (mean-activation difference under ALWAYS-ask vs NEVER-ask system suffixes), and belief-axis patching in both directions.
4. *Edit under one harness:* runtime gated steering (gate = belief-clear ∧ about-to-ask; one-shot projection, continuation unpatched) and weight-level LoRAs trained on the overseer's corrections plus retention examples — the local variant restricted to the causal band, the bc (behavior cloning / interactive imitation) variant over all layers — identical data and hyperparameters, differing in exactly one argument. Six pass/fail criteria were fixed before any run; all evaluations use fresh scenarios, the budgeted overseer, a 50-item capability battery, and base-row audits.

**Discipline.** Predictions were written down before outcomes (self-attested project notes, not registry pre-registration); conventions ("the peak is the first argmax") were declared before the models that made them matter; a stopping rule governed rule falsification. Code, configs, and episode logs are released (GitHub + Zenodo with trained adapters), and an independent adversarial audit by an external frontier-model reviewer produced several of the paper's protocol hardenings.

## Results & Highlights

- **Causal localization (Table 1, frozen-selection protocol, held-out test over-asks):** extrapolated-dose flip rates of 97% [91–99] on Qwen2.5-3B (L28, n=74), 83% [73–90] on gemma-2-2b (L21, n=76), 72% [61–81] on OLMo-3-7B (L21, n=79), 95% [88–98] on Mistral-Nemo-12B (L22, n=81), and 100% [86–100] on Phi-4 (L22, n=24) — versus random directions under the identical procedure flipping at most 1/74 anywhere (0 flips at the chosen site on every family). Under full generation on Qwen, 99% of flips emit well-formed ACTION lines and 95% act on the correct object; on gemma, all 65 generation-confirmed flips act on the correct object (65/65).
- **Gated steering vs prompt baseline (Qwen, 200 fresh scenarios/seed, 3 seeds):** overseer interventions drop from 67/65/72 to 9/0/2 and total failures from 91/86/95 to 24/17/16 (−74%/−80%/−83%); pair-bootstrap 95% IRR intervals [0.59, 0.94], [0.68, 0.94], [0.71, 0.94], all excluding zero. The prompt-only baseline ("do not ask unnecessary questions") moves interventions the wrong way on every seed — 67→80, 65→66, 72→82 (IRR −20%, −1%, −11%) — while legitimate clarifying questions barely move under steering (−2 to −4 of ~93) and the capability battery is unchanged.
- **Two signatures (belief-probe readout):** in the four H-caution families, over-asked episodes carry essentially the same "unambiguous" belief as acted ones (mean U-score as a fraction of the acted class: 0.65–0.84), and patching the belief axis removes almost no questions (at most 1/74 on Qwen, 0/76 on gemma) nor induces them (0/13, 1/12); on Phi-4 over-asks sit far closer to the ambiguity boundary (fraction 0.29; mean U-score −12 vs −41 for acted) — its over-asking tracks genuine internal uncertainty.
- **Weight-editing map (Figure 4, criteria fixed in advance):** on Qwen, the causal-band LoRA (layers 24–31, 3.3M params) passes every criterion on 3/3 seeds with burden reductions of 84/85/86% and an unchanged 50-item capability battery (0.84 base → 0.84/0.86/0.84), while behavior cloning on identical data (15M params, 4.5×) fails the retention criterion on all three seeds (legitimate questions −14/−11/−12) and introduces a de-novo re-ask failure (15/9/11 of 100 episodes). The map inverts on the other families: gemma local-edit collapses to never-asking (100/100 ambiguous episodes acted too) while bc passes and even improves retention (+11); OLMo-3 local fails retention (−15/−12/−12) while bc reaches IRR 86–90%; Nemo local collapses (clarify −51/−92/−81) and bc is mixed 2/3; Phi-4 local is mixed 1/3 with IRR 28%→49.5%→72% across full-pipeline seeds while bc passes (IRR 70–78%).
- **Falsified predictors, honored stopping rule:** the pre-written cosine rule (entangled geometry ⇒ distributed capacity needed) died on OLMo-3 (cos=0.07, Qwen-like geometry, yet behaved like gemma — exact inverse of the prediction); the revised depth rule died on Phi-4 (retention held at −9, killing it, while IRR 28% missed the bar, killing the original). No third rule was fit. A band-width sweep on gemma found two ordered thresholds: below 3 layers the adapter cannot even fit the corrections; 8–12 layers fits them but generalizes as the unconditional "never ask"; only from 18/26 layers (69% depth) can it afford the conditional policy — Qwen passes at 8/36 layers (22%).
- **Human validation and the scaffold guardrail:** blind annotation agrees with the rule overseer at κ=0.900 (independent annotator; κ=0.967 between annotators) and reproduces the steering intervention reduction (−81% human vs −87% overseer on the same sample); the re-asking failure, at 45–67% across three models, collapses to exactly zero on both models tested once the scaffold shows the agent its own prior question — and the bc edit later re-creates it through the weights (15/9/11 of 100 on Qwen; amplified 8→21 on one Nemo seed).

## Authors & Credibility

Three authors from the Dipartimento di Informatica, Università di Pisa (Italy): Cesare Zavattari, Alessandro Tommasi, Giuseppe Prencipe — an academic group, not a frontier lab, but the paper's credibility rests on unusually strong internal evidence rather than institutional weight. Verifiable artifacts are released: code, configs, and episode logs at github.com/bradstallion/interventions-as-labels, plus trained adapters on Zenodo (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23319778). Credibility signals include: an independent adversarial audit by an external frontier-model reviewer whose six findings hardened the protocol (frozen site/dose selection, bootstrap IRR intervals, the SAE reader arm, optimizer-seed retrains), with a follow-up pass reproducing interval analyses from released logs; blind human annotation validating the overseer verdicts (κ=0.900–0.967) with disclosed corrections (three confirmed input errors, κ=0.791 before correction, both values reported); explicit disclosure of five harness bugs caught by blocking invariants, including one whose effect had favored the authors' method; and honest framing of the self-attested (not registry-filed) "predictions before outcomes" discipline. The main credibility caveat is that the headline IRR metric rests on a simulated budgeted-overseer dynamic that was not directly human-validated, and that the one stable editing win (Qwen) is a single-family result.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty.** The label source is genuinely new — oversight interventions on an acting agent, rather than curated contrastive prompts — and the paper honestly delimits it against closest prior work (conditional activation steering, COAST, ClarifySAE, BehaviorBench, ASA). The both-directions causal inertness test on the belief axis, and the five-family editing map with pre-written falsified predictors, are contributions I have not seen elsewhere. The H-caution/H-misrep split converges with concurrent knowing-doing-gap work (Cheng et al. 2026) but adds the causal layer.
- **Rigor.** High by interpretability standards: frozen site/dose selection, matched-procedure random and style controls, full-generation validation of flips, base-row audits, human-validated verdicts, and a disclosed deviation (Wilson interval replaced by pair-cluster bootstrap because the pre-declared criterion was undefined for a burden ratio). The deflationary honesty about probes (rich content baseline reaches 0.93–0.99 AUC on Qwen, so decodability proves nothing) is exactly right.
- **Weaknesses in the evidence chain.** (1) The headline metric IRR is generated by a simulated overseer whose stochastic trust dynamics make it unstable — the paper's own audit shows the seed-1 point estimate ranges 0.45–0.95 across 1,000 overseer seeds — and the burden dynamics were not human-validated (only verdicts and intervention reductions were). (2) The random-direction control is a machinery null, not a matched-norm placebo; displacement norms per arm were not recorded, so "any direction would do" is controlled less strongly than the text sometimes implies — though the style-direction control at gemma (76/76 flips but with different semantics) partially covers this. (3) Phi-4 — the sole H-misrep family and the sole boundary-crossing editing case — has only n=24 test over-asks (Wilson [86,100] on a 100% point estimate), so an entire half of the taxonomy rests on one small-n family. (4) The single stable localized-editing win is one family (Qwen); with ten arm-outcomes across five families, the "map" is more a documented negative result than a usable method.
- **Reproducibility.** Strong: released code/configs/logs/adapters, config hashes, per-arm audit trails, and an external adversarial audit with closed regression-tested gaps. Caveats: predictions-before-outcomes is self-attested in private notes (the authors say so plainly); per-run git SHAs were not recorded for most runs; and the narrow eligibility window (5 of 13 screened families, with 8 excluded for always-ask/never-ask/format failures) means replication requires finding models in the same behavioral window.
- **Significance.** For interpretability methodology this is well above the bar — an existence proof that intervention labels yield causal levers, plus a falsification that could not be reinterpreted after the fact. For practice, the deliverable is a map and a guardrail, not a technique one can deploy: the environment is a toy, and no cheap measurement tested predicts where localized editing wins.
- **Red flags / overclaiming check.** Minimal for a paper of this ambition: scope is repeatedly narrowed (eligible models only; lab proxy for human oversight; burden as automated proxy), the failed rules are reported at length, and the introduction's scoping note pre-empts the title's strongest reading. The one place to read carefully: "five for five" causal localization lives inside a 5-of-13 eligibility filter, and the bc baseline's Qwen retention failures (−14/−11/−12) are what make the localized win look clean — a reader should note the comparison was criterion-frozen in advance but the data recipe (overseer corrections + retention examples) is the authors' own construction.

## Strengths

- The core reframing — an intervention log as a localization dataset — is clean, well-motivated by oversight economics, and executed end-to-end (labels → probes → causal patch → runtime steering → weight edit → human validation).
- Causal claims are backed by an unusually disciplined control stack: random-direction and style-direction controls under the identical procedure, belief-axis patching in both directions, full-generation validation, and a frozen site/dose selection protocol that removes the classic interpretability garden-of-forking-paths.
- The negative results are first-class citizens: two pre-registered predictor rules falsified out-of-family with a stopping rule honored, a headline failure (re-asking) shown to be scaffold-level not weight-level, and eight screened-out model families reported with their failure modes.
- Metric design matches the deployment question (did the overseer have to intervene less?) rather than probe accuracy, with full-attention failure counts reported alongside the proxy IRR.
- The knowing-doing-gap analysis (H-caution vs H-misrep) is directly actionable: for four of five families, the intuitive fix targets a component the paper shows is not broken.

## Limitations

- External validity: a deliberately minimal testbed (one-line tasks, three-item workspaces, a rule-defined overseer); nothing demonstrates transfer to naturalistic agent traces — the authors say so themselves and defer it to future work.
- Narrow eligibility: 5 of 13 candidate families; all "five for five" claims live inside that window. Small instruct models overwhelmingly sit at a degenerate always-ask/never-ask corner.
- Greedy decoding throughout; behavior-probe decodability is matched by a prompt-content baseline (0.93–0.99 AUC), so the correlational results are uninformative by design and everything rests on the causal tests.
- The editing-regime boundary is mapped, not explained (n=5, with the boundary crossing one model's full-pipeline seed variance); the H-misrep signature rests on one family; the SAE analysis is one dictionary, one layer, class-mean dose only.
- The IRR burden metric is a simulated operational proxy (stochastic trust dynamics, sensitive to the overseer's own seed), not directly human-validated, and weights every intervention alike — an over-ask costs the same as acting on a genuinely ambiguous request, the more consequential error; gated steering also slightly increases proceed-when-ambiguous (8→11, 6→8, 5→9 of ~100).
- Human validation rests on two annotators, with disclosed blemishes (three corrected input errors; episode IDs inadvertently visible during one κ study); the predictions-before-outcomes discipline is self-attested, not externally registered.
- Adversarial robustness of any of these edits is untested (concurrent work suggests located-and-edited mechanisms can collapse under adaptive attack), and multi-edit interference is unexplored.

## Relevance

直接命中"LLM Hidden States & Interpretability"方向：这是把探针、因果修补、SAE（Gemma Scope）与表示几何用于 agent 行为失效的完整因果管线，其"可解码≠因果"的控制设计和冻结协议是可迁移的方法学范本。同时与"Agent & Pipeline Silent Failures"相关：它系统展示了同一表面失效可分别源于脚手架（agent 看不到自己已问过的问题）或权重（bc 编辑重新制造该行为），并给出"脚手架消融→提示词→门控转向→权重编辑"的修复层级——对 agent 可靠性工程有直接借鉴意义。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the H-caution/H-misrep split, and the probe readout that separates them, survive on naturalistic multi-turn agent traces with genuinely noisy ambiguity, and on instruction-tuned models above 14B (where the always-ask corner may dissolve)?
- What property actually determines the localized-editing regime? The two falsified rules (cosine entanglement, causal-peak depth) and the two-thresholds band-width result suggest downstream re-absorption capacity as a candidate — how would one measure it on a model chosen before the measurement?
- Can the budgeted-overseer burden metric be replaced by a human-validated or deterministic proxy that is not overseer-seed sensitive (the 0.45–0.95 spread on seed 1), without losing the operational meaning of IRR?
- Would the gated-steering approach remain safe under adversarial inputs crafted to fool the belief probe (the paper notes the gate would then force exactly the proceed-when-ambiguous error), and how does the gate degrade under distribution shift in workspace size and instruction phrasing?
- Do intervention labels localize other failure types in the taxonomy (correction-propagation, wrong-object actions) that require multi-step tasks, and does the causal site remain stable across sampled (non-greedy) decoding?
- The SAE analysis found that only the reader features that also move the style axis flip the decision — would clamping the six non-shared readers alone (the missing arm the authors identify) separate readable-but-inert from causal content at feature resolution?
