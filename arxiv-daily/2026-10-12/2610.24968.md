> **一句话总结：** 提出"视觉充分性景观"（visual sufficiency landscapes）——在固定层与保留预算下，均匀采样 K 个视觉 token 的子集，统计能同时保持稠密模型答案、答案似然与输出分布的比例（p_suf），并在 Qwen2.5-VL-7B / InternVL2-8B / LLaVA-v1.5-7B 上证明：同一图像的不同问题对应的景观差异远超重复采样误差（三个模型各 32/32 张 GQA 图像均为正），且把 p_suf 加入预测器后，LLaVA/GQA 上 FastV / VisionZip / SparseVLM 剪枝失败的 AUC 分别提升 +0.1151 / +0.0683 / +0.1401，新图像上 log loss 降低 9.0%–14.1%。

> **TL;DR:** Rather than proposing another pruning method, this paper measures *how many* visual-token subsets can preserve model behavior: at a fixed layer and budget, it uniformly samples K-token subsets and defines visual sufficiency probability p_suf as the fraction jointly preserving the dense answer, reference-answer likelihood (ΔNLL ≤ 0.10), and output distribution (KL ≤ 0.05). On GQA and TextVQA across Qwen2.5-VL-7B, InternVL2-8B, and LLaVA-v1.5-7B, different questions about the *same* GQA image yield landscapes that differ beyond replication error (all 32/32 images per model, Δ_Q up to 0.270), and p_suf adds real risk signal: on LLaVA/GQA it lifts failure-prediction AUC over a strong feature baseline by +0.1151 (FastV), +0.0683 (VisionZip), +0.1401 (SparseVLM) with log-loss cuts of 11.5%/5.1%/10.0%, and frozen predictors cut log loss on new GQA images by 9.0–14.1%. The measurement needs 128 forward passes per condition plus reference answers, so it is an offline diagnostic — a cheap online estimator does not exist yet.

## 核心贡献

- **提出"视觉充分性景观"这一组合式度量**：在给定（图像，问题，干预层，保留数 K）条件下，从所有 C(N,K) 个子集中均匀采样，定义 p_suf 为同时满足三项保持判据（生成答案一致 + ΔNLL ≤ 0.10 + KL ≤ 0.05）的子集比例，把"剪枝余地有多大"从个案成败变成可估计的概率。
- **给出集合层面的理论刻画**：命题 1 证明两个问题充分性概率之差 |p_q1 − p_q2| 下界化了两个成功子集集合的对称差（即保持结果不一致的子集比例）；附录 D.2 进一步证明 −log p_q 是"只在成功子集上取样的选择分布"偏离均匀分布的最小 KL 散度。
- **量化了同一图像内的查询依赖性**：用两个独立批次（各 M=64 次干预）区分输入差异与蒙特卡洛噪声，在三个模型上，同一张 GQA 图像的三个问题产生显著不同的景观（每模型 32/32 张图像净查询差异为正），且在 ρ ∈ {0.125, 0.25, 0.50} 三个预算下全部持续。
- **证明了该度量对剪枝风险的增量预测价值**：对 FastV、VisionZip、SparseVLM 三种完整剪枝方法、三种受控选择规则、跨架构配置与全新图像队列，加入 p_suf 一致降低 log loss / Brier 并提升 AUC；按最低 p_suf 报警可在新图像上集中捕获失败（宏风险差 17–42 个百分点）。

## Key Contributions

- **A combinatorial reliability measure, not another pruner.** Visual sufficiency probability p_suf(i, l, K) is the fraction of uniformly sampled K-token visual subsets that jointly preserve the dense model's answer, reference-answer likelihood (ΔNLL ≤ 0.10 nats/token), and teacher-forced output distribution (KL ≤ 0.05), measured after a shared dense prefix up to layer l with all non-visual positions frozen.
- **Formal set-level guarantees.** Proposition 1 shows the difference between two queries' sufficiency probabilities lower-bounds the fraction of subsets whose preservation outcomes disagree across the queries; Appendix D.2 shows −log p_q is the minimum KL divergence from uniformity required by any selection distribution supported entirely on successful subsets; Appendix D.3 proves singleton/endpoint observations cannot determine intermediate-budget success mass, formally separating this measure from single-token analyses (Information Horizon) and removability studies (CoRePrune).
- **Query dependence quantified beyond sampling noise.** Using two independent replicates of 64 interventions per condition as an error reference, between-input curve differences (Δ_curve 0.148–0.247) and within-image query differences (Δ_Q 0.177–0.270) are positive with 100% bootstrap directional support on GQA and TextVQA for all three models; all 32/32 GQA images per model show positive net query effects, persisting at all three tested budgets.
- **Actionable risk signal.** Adding p_suf to predictors of joint-preservation failure improves AUC, log loss, and Brier for three full pruning pipelines, transfers to a disjoint image cohort, and flags low-sufficiency questions whose failure rate is 17–42 percentage points above the remainder.

## Methodology

The paper measures local prunability at a fixed budget. Given image and question, the visual encoder/projector produce N ordinary visual tokens entering the LM; at an intervention layer l, a subset S of K = max{1, ⌈ρN⌉} positions is retained in original order (special boundary tokens excluded) and the remaining layers run with identical weights, physically removing the other visual positions per each model's compression pipeline. A subset succeeds under **joint preservation** when the normalized generated answer matches the dense answer with task score at least as high, ΔNLL ≤ 0.10, and dense-to-pruned KL ≤ 0.05 (both per target token; generation/parsing failures count as failures). p_suf is estimated by two independent batches of M = 64 uniform subsets (128 pooled), and arranging estimates over a layer grid (L4–L24 for Qwen2.5-VL; L5–L30 for InternVL2/LLaVA) at ρ = 0.25 yields the "landscape."

Comparisons subtract replication error: Δ_curve contrasts mean between-input curve distance against within-input replicate distance; Δ_Q(x) does the same for three questions on the same image; boundary statistics track the first layer reaching p̂ ≥ 0.80. Inference uses greedy short answers (≤16 tokens), reference answers for NLL/KL, image-cluster bootstrap (5,000 resamples), and reported directional support.

The predictive experiments fit paired predictors B0/B1 (without/with p_suf) for the target "joint-preservation failure": (i) native FastV/VisionZip/SparseVLM on LLaVA with 8-fold image-grouped logistic regression sharing one p_suf measured at layer 9; (ii) architecture-adapted configurations on Qwen2.5-VL and InternVL2; (iii) controlled selection rules (query similarity, feature distance, central position) over 7 layers (2,016 records) with nested image-grouped cross-validation over logistic regression and gradient-boosted trees, plus frozen predictors applied to a prespecified disjoint cohort of 32 new GQA images; (iv) risk alerts flagging the 32/96 questions with lowest p_suf and measuring the failure-rate risk difference versus the remainder.

## Results & Highlights

- **Input heterogeneity is real, not sampling noise (GQA/TextVQA, ρ=0.25, 32 images/cell).** Net between-input curve differences Δ_curve are positive with 95% CIs entirely above zero in all six model-task cells — e.g., InternVL2/GQA 0.247 [0.187, 0.289], LLaVA/GQA 0.221 [0.161, 0.268], Qwen2.5-VL/TextVQA 0.153 [0.095, 0.202] — and first-high-sufficiency boundary disagreement Δ_b reaches 0.78–0.82 on GQA. Average depth trends (mean p_suf rising with depth) conceal widely dispersed individual curves.
- **Same image, different question, different landscape.** Holding the image fixed (32 GQA images × 3 questions), net within-image query differences are Δ_Q = 0.270 [0.219, 0.325] for Qwen2.5-VL, 0.177 [0.133, 0.223] for InternVL2, and 0.177 [0.146, 0.209] for LLaVA, with positive point estimates for **all 32/32 images in each model** and 100% bootstrap directional support; the effect persists at every tested budget (all nine model × ρ ∈ {0.125, 0.25, 0.50} CIs above zero, e.g., Qwen2.5-VL 0.269/0.315/0.217).
- **p_suf predicts pruning failure for full methods (LLaVA/GQA, 96 questions).** Adding p_suf to the baseline B0 raises AUC by +0.1151 for FastV (0.5317 → 0.6468), +0.0683 for VisionZip (0.5952 → 0.6635), and +0.1401 for SparseVLM (0.5938 → 0.7339), with relative log-loss reductions of 11.5% (p=0.026), 5.1% (p=0.105), 10.0% (p=0.042); Brier drops in all three (e.g., SparseVLM 0.2373 → 0.2052). FastV here retains 144/576 LLaVA visual tokens after block 9; VisionZip keeps 64; SparseVLM keeps 39.
- **Gains replicate across architectures — with one honest null.** Five of six architecture-adapted model-method combinations cut log loss by 8.2%–28.1% (best: InternVL2/VisionZip 0.3224 → 0.2317, −28.1%, AUC 0.6677 → 0.8283; Qwen2.5-VL/SparseVLM −20.4%, AUC 0.6974 → 0.8185); the exception, InternVL2/FastV, has a CI spanning zero (−0.1%, p=0.476, AUC 0.7449 → 0.7409).
- **Generalization to new images under controlled selection rules.** Predictors frozen on the original 32-image GQA cohort reduce log loss on 32 disjoint new GQA images by 9.0% (Qwen2.5-VL, 0.4092 → 0.3724), 13.0% (InternVL2, 0.3935 → 0.3424), and 14.1% (LLaVA, 0.4635 → 0.3982), all difference CIs below zero; Brier differences are negative with 100% directional support, and improvements hold for every selector at 6–7 of 7 layers.
- **Risk alerts concentrate failures.** Flagging the 32/96 GQA questions with lowest p_suf yields a macro risk difference (flagged minus unflagged failure rate) of 41.67 pp [29.69, 51.56] on Qwen2.5-VL (main; transfer 38.54), 23.44 pp on InternVL2 (both cohorts), and 17.19 pp [3.65, 30.21] on the LLaVA transfer cohort.
- **Answer preservation ≠ behavioral preservation.** Of 96 LLaVA/GQA questions, VisionZip fails joint preservation on 50 but only 24 fail task preservation — 26 questions keep the dense answer while breaching the NLL/KL thresholds (analogously 19 for FastV, 24 for SparseVLM) — empirically justifying the joint criterion over answer-match alone.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty: genuinely new angle, well-differentiated.** The contribution is a *measurement object* (fraction of successful subsets under a uniform measure) with two clean formal results (Prop. 1's symmetric-difference bound; D.2's minimum-KL characterization of selection concentration). Appendix D.3 is a pointed argument that this object is not derivable from singleton or endpoint interventions — a concrete, correct separation from Information Horizon-style single-token analyses and CoRePrune's conditional removability. It is analysis, though: no new pruning method or acceleration is delivered.
- **Rigor: high, with honest nulls.** CIs are reported even when they span zero (InternVL2/FastV; Qwen2.5-VL original-cohort ΔL −0.0172 [−0.0348, 0.0017]; the LLaVA-vs-Qwen cross-model effect-size comparison −0.0636 [−0.1373, 0.0106] is reported as indistinguishable). Sensitivity checks cover boundary thresholds (0.7/0.8/0.9), isotonic/sustained boundary definitions, and the 9 images where dense answers are all correct. Appendix E.2 even discloses that the original cohort "did not exclude all images used in historical pilot experiments" and that two analyses were follow-ups — refreshingly candid.
- **Weak spots a skeptic should note.** (1) *Tiny cohorts*: 32 images / 96 questions per model-task cell; the effects are consistent but the absolute generality across the full distribution of VQA inputs is untested. (2) *Narrow task surface*: only short-answer GQA/TextVQA; RefCOCOg grounding was attempted and abandoned after interface failures (Qwen2.5-VL produced one invalid dense output; none of six LLaVA check inputs yielded parseable coordinates) — so nothing here covers localization or long-form generation, yet pruning failures are known to concentrate exactly in fine-grained tasks (their own FEATHER citation). (3) *Fixed joint-preservation thresholds* (ΔNLL ≤ 0.10, KL ≤ 0.05) are sensible but arbitrary; threshold sensitivity is checked for the 0.80 boundary, not systematically for the NLL/KL constants. (4) *The measurement is expensive*: 128 interventions per (image, question, layer, budget) plus reference answers — strictly offline; the paper concedes an online decision signal would need a cheap p_suf estimator that does not yet exist, so the practical payoff today is diagnostic. (5) *Baseline-feature coverage is uneven*: single-token information summaries exist only for 12 preselected images (mean-imputed elsewhere), which may weaken the baseline the increment is measured against; and the headline LLaVA AUC gains start from near-chance baselines (FastV B0 AUC 0.5317), so absolute discrimination remains moderate (best B1 AUC 0.8283). (6) *Query "condition" is a bundle* — length, referents, answer format, semantics vary jointly, and the design cannot attribute Δ_Q to any one factor.
- **Red flags:** none serious. The alert quota is fixed at 32/96 with equal weighting across methods; per-method intervals on the LLaVA transfer cohort span zero for FastV (+0.1094 [−0.0473, +0.3281]) and VisionZip (+0.0625 [−0.1719, +0.2656]) — only SparseVLM is individually significant (+0.3438) — so the macro RD there leans on pooling.

## Strengths

- Reframes pruning reliability as abundance of successful combinations, yielding a quantity with an exact combinatorial meaning (successful subsets / all K-subsets) and two useful formal interpretations (disagreement bound; minimum selection concentration −log p_q).
- Exemplary experimental hygiene: independent replication as the noise reference, image-cluster bootstrap, prespecified disjoint cohorts, frozen-predictor transfer, disclosure of deviations and null results.
- The predictive results cover full pruning pipelines (FastV, VisionZip, SparseVLM), controlled selection rules, three architectures, and new images — the increment survives a feature ablation baseline (M0–M3) that already includes dense-output stats, full-visual-removal responses, query-similarity summaries, and Information-Horizon-style single-token information.
- The C.1 decomposition (26/96 VisionZip questions answer-preserving but distribution-distorting) gives concrete evidence that answer-match metrics underreport pruning damage.

## Limitations

- Offline only: 128 forward passes per condition plus reference answers make the landscape a diagnostic, not a deployable runtime signal; the needed cheap surrogate estimator is future work.
- Scale and scope: 32-image cohorts, two short-answer VQA datasets, three ~7–8B models; localization/grounding dropped due to tooling failures; no video, no long-generation, no larger models.
- The query effect is established as a bundle (length/format/semantics jointly) without factor decomposition; budget trends of the effect are model-dependent (Qwen2.5-VL shrinks at ρ=0.50: −0.098 [−0.172, −0.035]; LLaVA shrinks from ρ=0.125: +0.075 [0.034, 0.121]).
- Some intervals span zero (InternVL2/FastV; Qwen2.5-VL original cohort; Qwen late-layer reversal in Appendix B), so per-configuration gains are not universal.
- Joint preservation preserves *dense* behavior — it can certify preservation of an incorrect dense answer; task correctness is tracked separately but the main failure target is deviation-from-dense, not accuracy.

## Relevance

视觉 token 剪枝是多模态大模型推理降本的核心手段，本文首次把"同一输入下有多少保留子集能保住模型行为"变成可估计、可比较的量，并证明它对 FastV/VisionZip/SparseVLM 等主流剪枝方法的失败风险有增量预测能力——这直接服务于高效多模态推理与可靠性感知（risk-aware）剪枝预算决策方向。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Can an amortized surrogate (e.g., a lightweight probe on hidden states) estimate p_suf within a few points of the 128-intervention Monte Carlo estimate, turning the offline landscape into an online budget/fallback signal?
- Which component of the "query condition" — answer format, referent count, question length, or semantics — drives Δ_Q? A factorial design over synthetic queries on fixed images would decompose the effect.
- Do landscape statistics (p_suf, first-high-sufficiency boundary) transfer across model scales or training stages of the same family, enabling cheap measurement on a small model to predict pruning risk on a large one?
- The D.2 bound says a selector must be at least −log p_q nats away from uniform to succeed on low-sufficiency inputs — can this bound be tightened into a selection-strategy difficulty metric that ranks existing pruners a priori?
- Does the within-image query dependence grow for grounding, counting, or long-form generation tasks where multiple visual elements must be jointly preserved (the relation-question gains hint at this)?
- How does p_suf interact with token merging vs. token pruning — merging keeps information in superposed positions, so the fixed-size-subset intervention may understate flexibility for merge-based methods like ToMe/PuMer?
