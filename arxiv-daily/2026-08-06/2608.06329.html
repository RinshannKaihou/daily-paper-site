§ 02 Methodology 方法学

Model a benchmark as a policy + tasks, then judge it on three axes — consistency, complexity, coverage — with no gold reference and no agent runs.

Formal model
B = (P, t₁ … tₙ)
t = (dₜ, eₜ, iₜ)
P = domain policy · dₜ = description
eₜ = expected behavior · iₜ = initial DB state
Meta-metric
BOS = #satisfied pairwise orderings / 8
Top (GPT-5.4, Claude-4.5) > Medium (Llama-70B/8B) > Small (Llama-1B)
M1 Description ↔ Expected Behavior AlignmentDoes eₜ contradict or invent facts beyond dₜ? Consistency
M2 Policy ↔ Expected Behavior AlignmentDoes eₜ comply with P — no invented or missing steps? Consistency
M3 Policy Violations per Taskmean |V(t)| — how adversarial is each task? Complexity
M4 Policy Violations Coveragefraction of policy items violated by ≥ K=3 tasks Coverage
Judges: GPT-5.4 · Claude-4.5-Sonnet · Gemini-2-Flash. Synthetic benchmarks built by swapping generator LLMs inside the IntellAgent pipeline (Airline 100 / Retail 95 tasks × 5 generators = 975 tasks).

  • Perfect Airline ordering. Every metric × every judge hits BOS = 1.00 — the four metrics cleanly separate GPT-5.4/Claude-4.5 > Llama-70B/8B > Llama-1B.
  • Human agreement is significant. Kendall τb = 0.32–0.67, all p < 0.05. Strongest for Violations Count (0.67); weakest for Policy–Expected under Gemini-2 (0.32).
  • Degrades monotonically under perturbation. Desc–Expected falls as expected-behavior swapping rises 0%→80%; cross-domain policy swapping collapses all policy-grounded metrics.
  • τ³-bench application: hand-curated tasks show only 0.38–0.60 violations/task vs 1.5–2.3 for strong synthetic benchmarks — and 54–69% of policy items are never challenged.

§ 05 Strengths vs. Limitations 优势与局限

A genuinely novel, reference-free diagnostic — but the headline ranking rests on a synthetic setting, and the lone natural-data point is confounded.

+ Strengths

  • Truly reference-free. Works on a single benchmark with no gold dataset and no panel of agents.
  • Actionable diagnostics. The five-category failure taxonomy turns a score into a to-do list for benchmark builders.
  • Clean, strong signal on synthetic data — perfect Airline ordering across all four metrics and three judges.
  • Generous appendices — full judge prompts, perturbation definitions, representative failure examples.
  • Honest about confounds — explicitly flags the τ³-bench annotation-granularity artifact rather than hiding it.

! Limitations

  • Narrow scope. Only τ-bench / IntellAgent-style benchmarks with an explicit policy + (d, e, i) task structure. No code-gen, web-agent, or static-QA coverage.
  • Single-pipeline dependency. IntellAgent is the sole synthetic-benchmark source; other generation strategies untested.
  • Capability proxy is an assumption. The human study is one annotator on 50 tasks — no inter-annotator agreement.
  • Sensitive knobs untested. K=3 coverage threshold and semi-automatic policy extraction both lack ablation.
  • LLM-judge bias unanalyzed. GPT-5.4 both generates and judges the top tier — self-preference not measured.

§ 06 Open Questions 待探索

Where the framework could break, generalize, or close the loop — each is a natural next experiment.

Q1
Are the metrics robust to LLM-judge self-preference? Does GPT-5.4 over-score its own benchmarks — and does the 0.97 vs 0.94 GPT/Claude-vs-Gemini gap hint at it?
Q2
Once expected-behavior granularity is normalized (expanding outcome-only annotations into workflow steps), does τ³-bench's "hand-curated is worse" signal survive or vanish?
Q3
How sensitive is Coverage to K (3 vs 1 vs 5)? Can K be set adaptively per benchmark size?
Q4
Does the framework generalize to non-policy-structured benchmarks (SWE-bench, WebArena, AppWorld) where no explicit policy document grounds coverage?
Q5
Can the five failure categories be auto-classified reliably enough to drive a closed-loop benchmark-repair pipeline — and how much does fixing flagged tasks improve downstream evaluation reliability?
Q6
Cost/latency: three frontier judges × 975 tasks is reported nowhere — what is the per-benchmark price tag, and does it scale to per-release CI for benchmark maintainers?
