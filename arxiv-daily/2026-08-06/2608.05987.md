> **一句话总结：** 针对长程多轮智能体 RL 中轨迹级优势无法区分关键决策的问题，AgentOPSD 把 token 级师生概率差聚合为轮级证据，在 log-odds 空间递归更新贝叶斯信念，用信念的边际修正量对 GRPO 优势做有界重塑——无需 critic、无需额外 rollout，在 ALFWorld (Qwen2.5-7B) 上达 89.1%，随任务步数增加的衰减仅 -0.54/步（GRPO 为 -2.91）。

> **TL;DR:** AgentOPSD turns the token-level teacher–student log-prob gap from on-policy self-distillation into history-dependent turn-level credit by aggregating it at environment turn boundaries and recursively updating a Bayesian belief state (initialized from the GRPO group success rate) in log-odds space; the marginal belief revision reshapes the sequence-level advantage via a bounded, sign-preserving multiplier. Critic-free and rollout-free (one extra teacher forward per turn), it raises ALFWorld success on Qwen2.5-7B to 89.1% (vs. 81.2% for GRPO, +7.9), Search-QA average to 49.2% (vs. 42.0%, +7.2), and WebShop Score to 90.2 (vs. 80.9, +9.3), and degrades at only -0.54 success points per extra trajectory turn versus -2.91 (GRPO) and -3.59 (RLSD).

## 核心贡献

- **将轮级信用形式化为信念修正量**：在 log-odds 空间把每轮自蒸馏间隙 $e_k$ 解释为对"轨迹最终成功"这一事件的贝叶斯证据，信用取为信念的边际修正 $\Delta B_k = B_k - B_{k-1}$，而非孤立局部分数。理论上证明：孤立的自蒸馏间隙本身不构成序列信用（Prop. 6 的不可辨识性）。
- **递归信念更新 + 有界优势重塑**：用几何衰减累积证据 $c_k = \gamma c_{k-1} + e_k$，信念初值锚定到 GRPO 组成功率 $B_0 = \text{clip}(\bar R, \epsilon_0, 1-\epsilon_0)$；重塑因子 $w_k \in [1-b, 1+b]$ 严格保号，永远不翻转 GRPO 更新方向（Prop. 2），$\lambda=0$ 时精确退化为 GRPO（Prop. 3）。
- **环境对齐的轮级粒度**：在轮边界（而非 token 级）累积证据，因为环境只在整段动作后给出观测；消融显示轮级比 token 级高 3.2 个点。
- **无 critic、无额外 rollout 的完整方案**：相对 GRPO 的唯一开销是每轮一次 teacher 前向（teacher 与 student 共享参数，仅在训练时以检索到的技能 $c^+$ 作为条件）。在 3 个交互环境、2 个模型规模上稳定优于 GRPO 及 SDAR / RLSD / StepOPSD / Skill-SD 等强自蒸馏基线。

## Key Contributions

- Formalizes **turn-level credit as the revision of a success-belief induced by each turn**: in log-odds space, the per-turn self-distillation gap is evidence that recursively updates a Bayesian belief initialized from the group success rate; credit is the marginal revision $\Delta B_k$, not the raw local gap $e_k$. A non-identifiability proposition (Prop. 6) shows two same-reward trajectories can have different per-turn contributions, so a trajectory return alone cannot recover credit.
- Introduces **AgentOPSD**, which aggregates token-level teacher–student log-prob gaps at environment-aligned turn boundaries, recursively propagates them through a decaying evidence accumulator $c_k = \gamma c_{k-1} + e_k$, and reshapes the sequence-level advantage with a bounded, strictly sign-preserving multiplier $w_k \in [1-b, 1+b]$ (Prop. 1–3).
- Provides a **clean theoretical bridge** between the ideal Bayes factor $\log \frac{p(a_k|s_k,C)}{p(a_k|s_k,\neg C)}$ and the computable self-teacher contrast $e_k = \log \frac{\pi_\theta(a_k|s_k,c^+)}{\pi_\theta(a_k|s_k)}$ (Appendix A.1): under the rare-success limit, $e_k$ recovers the Bayes factor, and even without that assumption it is the pointwise mutual information, preserving both the sign and the ranking of turns by evidential strength.
- Demonstrates that the method is **critic-free and rollout-free** — only one extra teacher forward pass per turn — and is the only design (among OPSD, GRPO+OPSD, Skill-SD, RLSD, SDAR, StepOPSD) that explicitly converts a local gap into a *history-dependent* revision.

## Methodology

AgentOPSD sits on top of standard GRPO and modifies only the per-token advantage. For a task $x$, the agent samples a group of $G$ trajectories; each trajectory $i$ has $K_i$ turns. For trajectory $i$:

1. **Turn-level evidence aggregation.** The student and a self-teacher (same parameters $\theta$) score each student-generated token; the teacher branch additionally conditions on a training-only retrieved skill $c^+$ from the SkillBank (Xia et al., 2026). The detached token-level contrast $\delta_{k,t} = \log \pi_\theta(y_{k,t}|h^+_{k,t}) - \log \pi_\theta(y_{k,t}|h_{k,t})$ is summed within a turn to give $e_k = \log \frac{\pi_\theta(a_k|s_k,c^+)}{\pi_\theta(a_k|s_k)}$, interpreted as the Bayes-factor proxy for the turn.

2. **Recursive belief update.** Initialize $B_0 = \text{clip}(\bar R, \epsilon_0, 1-\epsilon_0)$ where $\bar R = S/G$ is the group success rate (Prop. 7 proves this is the MLE of task difficulty). Accumulate evidence with geometric decay: $c_k = \gamma c_{k-1} + e_k$, $\ell_k = \text{logit}(B_0) + c_k$, $B_k = \sigma(\ell_k)$. The marginal revision is $\Delta B_k = B_k - B_{k-1} \approx B_{k-1}(1-B_{k-1})\,[e_k - (1-\gamma)c_{k-1}]$ (Prop. 4): the $B(1-B)$ gate makes evidence most influential under uncertainty and inert once support saturates.

3. **Outcome-aligned signed credit.** $q_k = \text{sign}(A^{(i)}_{\text{seq}})\,\Delta B_k$. The magnitude says *where* belief changed; the sign records whether that change agrees with the verifier's outcome (consistency with a successful vs. failed trajectory).

4. **Bounded advantage reshaping.** Standardize $q_k$ within the trajectory to $z_k$, clip to $w_k = \text{clip}(1 + b\,z_k,\, 1-b,\, 1+b)$, and form $\tilde A_k = A^{(i)}_{\text{seq}}\big[(1-\lambda) + \lambda w_k\big]$. Each token in turn $k$ inherits $\tilde A_k$. Propositions 1–3 guarantee $|\tilde A_k - A^{(i)}_{\text{seq}}| \le \lambda b |A^{(i)}_{\text{seq}}|$, $\text{sign}(\tilde A_k)=\text{sign}(A^{(i)}_{\text{seq}})$, and exact GRPO recovery at $\lambda=0$.

No separate distillation loss is added; the detached teacher signal enters only through $\tilde A$. A single shared hyperparameter setting ($\lambda=0.5, b=0.2, \gamma=0.95, \epsilon_{\text{low}}=0.2, \epsilon_{\text{high}}=0.24$) is used across all environments and both model scales.

## Results & Highlights

- **ALFWorld (success rate), Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct:** AgentOPSD reaches **89.1%** vs. **81.2%** for GRPO (+7.9 pts), **82.0%** for RLSD, **85.9%** for SDAR, and **88.4%** for StepOPSD. On the 3B model AgentOPSD scores **84.4%** vs. GRPO's **75.0%** (+9.4 pts), tying the strongest baseline (SDAR, 84.4%).
- **Search-QA (7-dataset average accuracy), Qwen2.5-7B:** AgentOPSD **49.2%** vs. GRPO **42.0%** (+7.2 pts), SDAR **49.0%**, RLSD **49.0%**, with the largest held-out win on PopQA (46.8 vs. 44.0) and 2Wiki (45.3 vs. 43.2). On 3B, AgentOPSD averages **46.7%** vs. GRPO **36.4%** (+10.3 pts), including PopQA 48.0 vs. 41.1 and Bamboogle 68.1 vs. 26.4.
- **WebShop, Qwen2.5-7B:** Score **90.2** vs. GRPO **80.9** (+9.3) and Acc **79.7%** vs. **72.6%** (+7.1). On 3B, AgentOPSD is best on both: Score **90.4** vs. GRPO **79.8** (+10.6) and SDAR **85.0**, and Acc **69.5%** vs. SDAR **68.0%** and GRPO **63.3%**.
- **Horizon robustness (Figure 1b, ALFWorld 7B):** success points lost per extra trajectory turn — AgentOPSD **-0.54**, GRPO **-2.91**, RLSD **-3.59**. AgentOPSD is essentially flat as tasks get longer, directly matching the long-horizon credit-assignment motivation.
- **Mechanism ablation (Table 2, ALFWorld 7B):** full method 89.1 → per-token accumulation 85.9 (-3.2) → raw local gap $e_k$ instead of $\Delta B_k$ 82.8 (-6.3) → magnitude-only (drop outcome sign) 80.5 (-8.6) → drop empirical-rate prior $B_0$ 78.9 (-10.2). The signed direction and the group-rate prior anchor are the two largest contributors.
- **Hyperparameter robustness (Figure 3):** $\lambda$ is the only knob with a systematic effect ($\lambda=0.5$ best; $\lambda=0.01$ collapses toward GRPO); $\gamma \in [0.8, 1.0]$ and $\epsilon_{\text{high}} \in [0.2, 0.28]$ move results within a few points with no monotone trend. The spread across all knobs shrinks sharply on the short-horizon Search-QA task, consistent with the method acting where long-horizon credit matters.

## Authors & Credibility

The work is a collaboration between **Tsinghua University** (first author Zi-Han Wang, Yujiu Yang's group), **Zhejiang University**, and **Meituan** (corresponding author Qi Gu). The same author group has produced the closely related SDAR (Lu et al., 2026b), Skill0 (Lu et al., 2026c), and StepOPSD-adjacent work, so they have deep prior context on agentic self-distillation. Code is publicly released at `https://github.com/ZethWang/AgentOPSD`. The paper includes formal propositions (with proofs in Appendix A.2) for boundedness, sign preservation, GRPO recovery, first-order belief-revision decomposition, and the role of $B_0$ as the MLE group success rate — a level of theoretical grounding above most empirical RLVR tuning papers. Experiments use a single shared hyperparameter setting across all three environments and both model scales, which is a strong sign of a real effect rather than per-task tuning.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty — solid but incremental.** The framing of "self-distillation gap as Bayesian evidence, recursively accumulated" is genuinely new and well-motivated, and the Prop. 4 decomposition ($\Delta B_k \approx B_{k-1}(1-B_{k-1})\Delta\ell_k$) cleanly explains *why* history matters. However, the engineering delta over SDAR/RLSD/StepOPSD is small: all of them already use the teacher–student gap to modulate GRPO; AgentOPSD's contribution is (i) aggregating at turn boundaries and (ii) a decaying accumulator + sigmoid gate. The "Bayesian" label is somewhat aspirational — Appendix A.1 concedes $e_k$ recovers the true Bayes factor only under the rare-success limit $\rho_k \to 0$, and under (A1) alone it is just the pointwise mutual information.
- **Rigor — strong on the controlled comparison, weaker on breadth.** The controlled-information setup (all privileged baselines use the same retrieved skills, same backbone, same budget) is commendable and isolates the credit-construction effect. But only three benchmarks are used (ALFWorld, WebShop, Search-QA), all text-only and all from the same agentic-RL testbed lineage; there are no code-generation, math-reasoning, or tool-use-Heavy (e.g., SWE-bench) tasks, and no models beyond 7B. The headline "consistently outperforms" is qualified in the text itself: AgentOPSD exceeds SDAR on only **six of eight** aggregate comparisons, and on WebShop-7B Acc it actually *loses* to SDAR (79.7 vs. 82.8) — a result buried in the table and unmentioned in the narrative.
- **Reproducibility — good.** Single shared hyperparameter table (Table 3), Algorithm 1 pseudocode, public code, standard backbones (Qwen2.5-3B/7B-Instruct), and 8×H800 hardware disclosed. Skill retrieval is via keyword matching on the SkillBank of SkillRL (Xia et al., 2026), which is itself a dependency not everyone will have access to.
- **Significance — conditional on privileged skills.** The method's gains are entirely built on the availability of a good retrieved skill $c^+$. The "OPSD" baseline rows in Table 1 (skills used during training but with naive token-level distillation, no belief mechanism) collapse on Search-QA (0.0% avg on 7B) and barely move ALFWorld (32.8% on 7B) — showing that skills alone, badly injected, are useless. This means AgentOPSD's practical value is bounded by SkillBank coverage and retrieval quality, a caveat the paper does not stress.
- **Red flags / cherry-picking.** (1) The horizon-robustness headline (-0.54 vs. -2.91/-3.59) is computed on **one** benchmark (ALFWorld 7B) and reported as an OLS slope over sub-tasks; no confidence intervals or significance tests are given for any main-table number. (2) The "Bk is treated as relative support rather than a calibrated success probability" admission means the Bayesian interpretation should be read as an analogy, not a calibrated posterior. (3) GiGPO (Feng et al., 2025) — the most natural environment-reward-based turn-level competitor — is discussed as "complementary" but never run, leaving the clearest head-to-head open.

## Strengths

- Clean, well-motivated central insight: a local gap is not sequential credit; what matters is how much it *revises* the accumulated belief. The Prop. 4 gate $B(1-B)$ gives an intuitive, correct story for why pivotal turns (under uncertainty) get amplified and routine turns (saturated support) get suppressed.
- Strictly sign-preserving, bounded reshaping (Prop. 1–3) means AgentOPSD can never destabilize GRPO and recovers it exactly at $\lambda=0$ — a strong safety property.
- Minimal overhead (one teacher forward per turn, no critic, no extra rollouts) and a single shared hyperparameter setting across three environments and two model scales — unusually clean for an RLVR paper.
- Mechanism ablation (Table 2) cleanly decomposes the gain into four orthogonal components and identifies the signed direction and the group-rate prior as the dominant contributors, which is actionable for future work.
- Honest about the approximation: Appendix A.1 explicitly states the two assumptions and the rare-success limit, rather than overclaiming a calibrated Bayesian update.

## Limitations

- **Skill dependency.** The entire teacher signal comes from a retrieved skill $c^+$. In domains without a usable SkillBank (code, math, real web), it is unclear how to construct the privileged branch, and the paper does not address this.
- **Narrow benchmark coverage.** Three text environments, 3B/7B only. No SWE-bench, no code, no multimodal, no frontier models. The "long-horizon" claim rests on ALFWorld, whose episodes are still only a handful of turns.
- **No statistical significance.** All deltas are single-seed-point comparisons; horizon-robustness slopes have no CIs. Several wins (e.g., +0.2 over SDAR on Search-QA-7B, +0.2 over RLSD) are within plausible noise.
- **A real loss is underplayed.** WebShop-7B Acc: SDAR 82.8 > AgentOPSD 79.7. The paper claims superiority "on six of eight" aggregate comparisons, which is a weaker statement than the abstract's "consistently outperforms."
- **Uncalibrated belief.** The authors admit $B_k$ is relative support, not a calibrated probability, so the "Bayesian" framing is analogical; the method is really a sigmoid-gated, decay-weighted accumulator of log-prob gaps.
- **No comparison to GiGPO**, the most direct environment-reward turn-level baseline, dismissed as "complementary."

## Relevance

直接命中"长程智能体 RL 的信用分配"这一核心议题：把 token 级自蒸馏信号转化为历史相关的轮级信用，与 cs.LG/cs.AI 中 RLVR、LLM 智能体后训练、on-policy 蒸馏等方向高度相关，对本项目关注的 agentic post-training 与 credit assignment 研究有直接参考价值，尤其适合关心 GRPO 改进与多轮交互 RL 的读者。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How does AgentOPSD behave when the privileged skill $c^+$ is noisy or absent? An ablation with random/wrong skills would test whether the belief gate degrades gracefully or amplifies misinformation.
- Can the method be combined with GiGPO (environment-reward-based step-level advantages) rather than only GRPO, since the authors frame them as complementary signal sources?
- Does the belief-revision signal transfer to harder, longer-horizon tasks like SWE-bench or multi-file code editing, where a single trajectory spans dozens of decisions and the $B(1-B)$ gate should matter most?
- What happens at larger scale (32B/72B)? The gains narrow between 3B and 7B on some benchmarks (e.g., WebShop Acc), raising the question of whether turn-level credit matters less as the base model gets stronger.
- Is the uncalibrated $B_k$ fixable with a temperature or isotonic calibration on a held-out success set, and would a calibrated belief improve the horizon-robustness slope further?
- The signed-direction ablation (80.5 without sign vs. 89.1 with) is the single largest component — could an explicit outcome-conditioned (success vs. failure) teacher, rather than a skill-conditioned one, recover that gain more directly?
