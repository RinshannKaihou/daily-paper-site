APQF reaches lower Rel. BOPs on 4 of 5 architectures and improves over its own FP32 baseline on 3. VGG-7 at 0.41% BOPs lands within 0.08 pp of Bayesian Bits' 93.23% while using ~20% fewer BOPs, and is the only sub-0.5%-BOPs method to beat its FP32 baseline.

LLM-planner robustness (Swin-Tiny): six different planners — incl. free/open-weight DeepSeek and Gemma — all reach 97.4–97.9% Top-1, matching far more expensive models. The framework is not a "premium-LLM-only" artifact.

03Strengths & Limitations

Side-by-side read of the trade-offs.

Strengthswhat holds up
  • Genuinely end-to-end & architecture-agnostic — one pipeline, no per-architecture rules, validated on both CNN and ViT families.
  • Profiling-grounded — the w/o-profiling ablation cleanly shows measured evidence beats LLM priors alone on every model.
  • Right unified metric — Rel. BOPs captures pruning + quantization in one architecture-normalized number; structured sparsity + integer-friendly bit-widths are deployable.
  • Robust to planner choice — 6 LLMs within a 0.5-point band; OpenRouter design is explicitly future-proof.
  • Honest comparison — GETA reruns are the authors' own; activation-quant limitation and VGG-7 baseline discrepancy are disclosed.
Limitationswhere it strains
  • Abstract overstates "close to baseline" — ResNet-50 / Swin-Tiny lose 6–7 points; the close story is really DeiT/ViT at the chosen budget.
  • LLM nondeterminism unquantified — no per-planner variance, no failure-rate table, no seed study.
  • 200K-image budget is deliberate — disadvantages GETA; a standard full-budget ImageNet top-line for APQF itself is absent.
  • Classification only — segmentation (SAM), detection, LLMs/VLMs are future work, not demonstrated.
  • Compute-heavy & partly manual — repeated prune→recover→QAT cycles; user still picks LLM, distillation hyperparams, aggressiveness.
  • Comparison asymmetries — VGG-7 denominator mismatch; GETA runs weight-only while APQF runs weight+activation.

04Open Questions

What the paper leaves unanswered — and worth probing next.

Does the ~17-point lead over GETA persist at a full 1.28M-image budget, or vanish once GETA has data to converge?
What is the run-to-run variance under LLM nondeterminism, and each planner's strategy-validity failure rate?
How much does each piece contribute — cumulative-replay vs incremental, multi-stage vs single-shot, KD-vs-no-KD, which PEFT variant?
Can it move beyond classification to SAM-style segmentation, detection, and decoder/generative models?
Is the LLM's per-layer bit-width assignment portable across datasets for the same architecture?
What are the real on-device latency/energy numbers, not just Rel. BOPs, on an integer-only edge target?
