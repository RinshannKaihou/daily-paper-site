A training-free, plug-and-play budget-allocation layer extending the FunSearch / AlphaEvolve / ShinkaEvolve line to any fixed-cost program, prompt, or agent search.

Critical edge

Only one cheap–strong model pair (Qwen-3.5-Flash → Plus) is studied — no multi-tier cascade, no alternative cheap model, leaving the central "which cheap model?" question open.

§ 02

Methodology 方法

RelayEvolve runs under a fixed inference budget B and call cap N, with a cheap model mc (Qwen-3.5-Flash) and strong model ms (Qwen-3.5-Plus). A single population-level objective — Relay Gain — coordinates trajectory allocation, adaptive stopping, and seed selection.

PHASE A · CHEAP EXPLORE

Grow-Deepen bandit

A non-stationary UCB bandit splits effort between Grow (new trajectory) and Deepen (extend one). Block length h; per-trajectory & horizon caps prevent monopoly.

→
SIGNAL · RELAY GAIN

gt = F(St+1) − F(St)

Marginal improvement of a quality-diversity bank F = λ·Q + (1−λ)·D — both terms monotone submodular. Normalized relative gain ρ feeds the scheduler.

→
PHASE B · STRONG REFINE

Submodular handoff

On saturation (ρ < εrel for patience p), greedy submodular selection + local search re-optimizes over the full terminal pool — (1−1/e) guarantee — then a single shared strong population refines.

  • Objective FC(S) = λ·Qr(S) + (1−λ)·DCq(S): top-r quality + quality-weighted facility-location diversity — both monotone submodular, so FC is too.
  • Offline + online bank. Online bank streams in greedily per block; at handoff the offline bank is re-optimized over the complete fixed pool Cτ to remove arrival-order dependence. Final S* = argmax{S_g, S_o}.
  • One shared strong population (not separate budgets) — so strong-model refinement recombines discoveries from different cheap trajectories. Best valid program across both phases is returned.

§ 04

Strengths vs. Limitations 优势与局限

STRENGTHS

What lands well

  • Clean central idea — population handoff vs. call-level routing — coordinated by one shared marginal-reward signal.
  • Three quantified empirical findings directly justify each design choice (short blocks, multi-trajectory, cheap-first, set-level reward).
  • Principled submodular curation with the classical (1−1/e) guarantee; offline+online banks remove arrival-order dependence.
  • Honest reporting: the lone non-best setting (Prism 50%) is surfaced, not hidden.
  • Training-free & API-only — immediately deployable on any frontier cheap/strong pair without fine-tuning.
LIMITATIONS

Where it strains

  • Single cheap–strong pair only — no 3-tier cascade, no alternative cheap model. Sensitivity to the capability gap is unmeasured.
  • Narrow task family — four program/algorithm-evolution benchmarks; no prompt, agentic, combinatorial, or symbolic-math tasks.
  • Only 3 runs in a setting the paper itself flags as high-variance; close margins (Prism 50%: 26.2408 vs 26.2440) stay fragile.
  • Hyperparameter opacity: εrel, p, h, λ, η, k, r, and the 0.85 strong-reserve default are described but not fully tabulated; several tuned on one task.
  • API nondeterminism (OpenRouter routing) and no public code/data — independent replication is hard.
  • AdaptEvolve (closest competitor) excluded for needing token-level logits unavailable via API.
§ 05

Open Questions 待探索

  1. Does population handoff generalize beyond a single Qwen pair — to a 3-tier (small → mid → frontier) cascade, or a different family (Llama, Gemma)?
  2. How does Relay Gain behave when the cheap–strong capability gap is smaller (Flash vs Turbo) — does the handoff still fire, or collapse into all-cheap?
  3. Can Relay Gain extend to continuous-control or symbolic-math evolution where fitness is noisier and multi-objective?
  4. Is the 0.85 strong-reserve split & εrel a robust global compromise, or a Circle-Packing-biased artifact? Per-task vs. global sensitivity?
  5. Could the Grow-Deepen bandit be replaced by a learned scheduler (transformer over trajectory embeddings) without losing the training-free appeal — and does it pay off at T ≫ 100?
  6. Do the offline (S_g) and online (S_o) banks actually differ in practice — or is one consistently dominant and the redundant curation droppable?
