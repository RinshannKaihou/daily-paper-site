Why this matters

Reasoning correctness is legible from internal states and transfers zero-shot to unseen tasks and models — even to factual errors the detector was never trained on. A directly usable design pattern for reliable, cross-task validity probes.

Critical edge

The detector loses to the frozen base model's own answer likelihood on all three FACTOR sets (e.g. FACTOR-news 72.3 vs 58.2) — the very benchmarks used to advertise "factual transfer."

01 Methodology

Three readers of one frozen model's residual stream, fused under a pairwise objective.

Selection setting: one forward pass per candidate records h_{t,ℓ} at every layer and token; the detector scores each candidate and the highest-scoring is chosen. No weights are written back to the base model. The three streams deliberately treat depth asymmetrically.

  • Motion reader (unchanged from Truth-as-a-Trajectory). Layerwise displacements d = h_{ℓ+1} − h_ℓ over all tokens/layers, read by a 2-layer bi-LSTM (128 hidden). Differencing attenuates persistent token-specific content — the source of cross-dataset transfer.
  • The trade-off it exposes. Differencing kills label-correlated surface signal (wording, format, topic) but also drops the reasoning state needed to interpret motion; restoring the raw state leaks wording at R²=0.086–0.191 vs 0.007 for displacement. Two restricted views are proposed to escape it.
  • Region reader (coarse). Unit-normalized states pass a depth-shared projection, then a K=128 VQ-VAE-style codebook replaces each with its nearest entry. Straight-through estimator + commitment loss + codebook reset; no continuous code reaches the head.
  • Direction reader (fine). Keeps the unit-normalized per-layer direction over a fixed m=6 mid-to-late layer window (answer tokens only); a depth-shared MLP combines final-token state with the answer-span mean.
  • Fusion. ŷ = σ(f([c_mot; c_dir; c_reg])) per candidate; trained from scratch with a pairwise objective + commitment term. Setup: base models frozen, donors ARC-C/Easy, OpenBookQA, CSQA, SocialIQA, HellaSwag (≤10k each); eval adds Story Cloze + MMLU; FACTOR + VitaminC for factual transfer.

02 Results & detection limits

Best in 47/48 reasoning cells; reads factual errors it was never trained on.

  • Reasoning transfer (Llama-3.1-8B). OOD 75.2 vs TaT 63.2 (+12.0) vs Probe 64.1 (+11.1); in-distribution ARC-C 74.2 vs TaT 62.2 vs Probe 69.0. Across donors, OOD gain is +10 to +21 over the probe and +7 to +12 over motion-only.
  • Factual transfer with NO factual training. FACTOR-news 58.2 vs TaT 44.9 (+13.3) vs Probe 31.2 (+27.0); FACTOR-expert 69.5 (+18.7); FACTOR-wiki 51.9 (+12.5); VitaminC 67.1 (+9.5).
  • Cross-model robustness (incl. MoE). Qwen2.5-14B reasoning 82.3 vs 68.6 (+13.7); Qwen3-30B-A3B reasoning 82.2 vs 71.6 (+10.6), factual 62.2 vs 50.3 (+11.9). Full model is best in every cell except one.
  • Wording-leakage control. Reconstruction R² at 0.007 (motion) / 0.009 (direction) / −0.003 (region) — the two location streams return almost none of the answer wording, vs 0.086 for a raw last-token state.
  • Shared-codebook evidence. Region codes used by factual answers are a strict subset of those used by reasoning answers — supporting the claim the streams read "correctness," not task identity. Code-only majority vote already beats base rate (ARC-C 81.2 vs 75.0).
  • Parameter efficiency. Two location streams add 0.31M params (6.7%); full detector 5.08M = 0.06% of the frozen 8B base — same overhead as the displacement baseline. Scaling motion LSTM to 4× width (25.7M) still reads 10 points below the 5.1M full model.

03 Strengths ↔ Limitations

▲ Strengths

  • Clean conceptual contribution: the state-restoration trade-off is articulated and quantitatively motivated before any method is proposed.
  • Principled minimal-sufficiency design — both location readers are narrow by construction, and post-training wording-leakage verifies the restriction holds.
  • Remarkable efficiency: SOTA transfer at 0.06% of base-model params; width-scaling shows the gain is design, not capacity.
  • Honest controls: surface-confound exclusion (TriviaQA/HaluEval), wording-leakage measurement, MoE replication, and a late-fusion decomposition exposing each stream's unique recovery.
  • The "shared codebook" observation is elegant evidence the signal is about correctness, not task identity.

▼ Limitations

  • Selection-only. Cannot score the absolute correctness of a lone answer — only rank candidates an item already supplies; ceiling bounded by what the base model distinguishes.
  • Loses to raw likelihood on FACTOR (wiki 57.9 vs 51.9; news 72.3 vs 58.2; expert 74.6 vs 69.5). The "ahead of every trained detector" headline is carefully scoped.
  • Chosen 6-layer window is suboptimal by ~3 points vs the middle third (75.6 vs 72.6 reasoning mean); kept for pre-registration honesty.
  • Region stream adds little over direction on most reasoning benchmarks; its independent value is concentrated on VitaminC.
  • Fusion leaves 3–21 points on the table vs a per-item oracle — needs a learned per-item gate, not another fixed combining rule.

04 Questions for further exploration

  1. Can the detector move from selection to absolute per-answer validity (e.g. calibration against a correctness prior), and how does its score behave as a standalone confidence signal?
  2. Would a learned per-item gate over the three streams close the 3–21 point oracle gap — and double as an interpretability instrument stating which reading a decision rests on?
  3. Does restoring residual-stream magnitude as a separate bounded quantity add correctness signal, or mostly re-introduce wording/depth shortcuts?
  4. The middle-third window beats the chosen window by 3 points — does a principled donor-blind layer-selection rule recover that gap without overfitting?
  5. How does the detector behave on free-form chain-of-thought, where candidates are not pre-supplied — and can the trajectory localize which reasoning step is unsound?
