01

Methodology — a three-stage, training-free pipeline

residual-stream SAEs · inference-time
1

SAE decomposition & graph construction

JumpReLU encoder gives sparse c_l. Cross-layer edge added iff features co-active (>τ_act 1.5) and decoder directions align (cos > τ_sim 0.10).

2

Contrastive circuit discovery

Specificity S = P(edge|D+) − P(edge|D−); keep edges with S > 0.05, top K=30. Isolates the behavior-specific multi-layer trajectory.

3

Dense vector synthesis & intervention

Per layer, v_l = mean(d_{l,i}) over participating features; inject h'_l = h_l + λ·v_l at every circuit layer with one shared scalar λ. Per-token cost ≈ CAA.

Decision-point variant for refusal: SAE features are read at the generation step where the model commits to refusal (not at input encoding) — because refusal is a discrete decision, not a continuous representation.

03

Strengths & Limitations

side by side

Strengths PRO

  • Honest framing. “Training-free” is scoped (no in-method gradients; off-the-shelf SAEs required) and co-activation/alignment edges are explicitly called candidate indicators, not proven causes.
  • Load-bearing ablations. NoGeo / NegativeAlign / RandomSAE / SingleLayer genuinely localize the gain; RandomSAE collapsing is a clean control that it’s the learned features, not the scaffolding.
  • Collateral damage measured. Under λ = −3, MMLU and GSM8K barely move; benign-prompt PPL (30.4) is several times lower than CAA (41.8–46.0).
  • Tunable, not brittle. Behavioral reduction saturates around ~30 edges; thresholds are non-critical; vectors stable across re-splits (App. H, J).

Limitations CON

  • Core idea is concurrent. Decoder-direction cosine edges overlap Laptev et al. (ICML 2025); the delta — co-activation, contrastive score, dense synthesis — is incremental.
  • Coverage ≠ peak. On 4 of 8 cells competitors lead by 1.3–2.3×; the “all 8” claim partly depends on the chosen validity window.
  • One marginal cell. Llama–Jigsaw (Δ .034) is not statistically separable from zero.
  • Coarse evaluators. Detoxify toxicity + keyword refusal detection are standard but miscalibrated; the LLM-judge ablation (gpt-5.4-mini) runs only on a subset.
  • Missing baselines. No activation/path-patching circuit methods (ACDC, EAP-IG); no head-to-head with the concurrent Laptev feature-flow steering it cites.

Open questions for further exploration

from the authors
Q1 · DEPTHDoes it hold for genuinely distributed, multi-layer concepts (long-horizon planning, ICL task inference) rather than shallow behaviors like toxicity & sycophancy?
Q2 · SYNTHESISCan the mean-of-decoders rule (Eq. 6) be replaced by a weighted combination (activation magnitude, causal influence à la Arad 2025) without destructive interference?
Q3 · SAE ROBUSTNESSIs the alignment criterion stable across SAE families — JumpReLU vs TopK vs ReLU, and across widths — or is it Gemma-Scope / Llama-Scope specific?
Q4 · CONTRAST CLASSDoes the specificity score generalize when D− is a confusable related behavior (e.g. steer off sycophancy without killing legitimate agreement)?
Q5 · ATTRIBUTIONDoes combining the alignment criterion with gradient-based circuit discovery (Marks 2024) outperform either alone?
Q6 · REFUSALDoes decision-point discovery transfer beyond keyword-detectable AdvBench refusal to broader red-teaming?
