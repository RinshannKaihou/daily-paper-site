Why this matters
Directly kills the production pain of embedding-model migration silently breaking old similarity thresholds — SQP gives a lightweight, annotation-free calibration any team can run on its own corpus.
⚠ Caveat
Critical edge
Conversion functions are fitted and evaluated on the same full dataset — no train/test split. The reported R²/MAE are in-sample fit quality, so deployable accuracy is likely lower than the tables suggest.

The two-regime finding

Calibration behaves differently inside a model family vs. across families.

Stable noise rejection: once each model is calibrated to its own scale, false-positive counts converge (96–104 on SciFact, 72–79 on enterprise) despite wildly different threshold magnitudes.

MMethodology — Synthetic Query Probing

  • Corpus & chunking. SciFact (5,183 biomedical abstracts, homogeneous) and a Pegasystems enterprise corpus (114,648 chunks, 33,492 docs — heterogeneous).
  • Synthetic query generation. Sample 100 chunks/corpus; Claude Sonnet 4.6 emits 10 queries × 3 classes per chunk — PARAPHRASE / RELEVANT / IRRELEVANT → 3,000 labeled pairs/corpus, no human annotation.
  • Scoring. Four configs: Titan V2 at 256 / 512 / 1024-d and OpenAI ada-002 (1,536-d). Cosine similarity computed for every (query, chunk) pair under every config.
  • Threshold sweep. 500-point sweep defines a binary classifier (PARAPHRASE∪RELEVANT = positive); precision/recall/F1 reported at precision 0.93 / 0.95 / 0.97.
  • Conversion. All 12 directional pairs fit with linear OLS, isotonic regression, and quantile (CDF) mapping — evaluated by MAE & R².

Key design choice: map between score distributions, not embedding vectors — sidesteps geometric-alignment assumptions and stays dimensionality-agnostic.

RResults & Highlights

  • Class ordering preserved, scale distorted. PARAPHRASE > RELEVANT > IRRELEVANT holds everywhere — but Titan scores run high while Ada compresses everything into a 0.626–0.930 band (std 2–6× smaller).
  • Isotonic wins all 12 pairs. Captures the monotone-but-non-linear Titan↔Ada curve that linear OLS misses.
  • Thresholds are corpus- AND model-specific. Titan-1024 needs 0.063 (SciFact) vs 0.393 (enterprise) at precision 0.95 — a ~6× swing within the same model.
  • Recall flips by corpus. Ada reaches perfect recall (1.000) on SciFact; Titan wins on the heterogeneous enterprise corpus, where its wider class separation pays off.

▲ Strengths

  • Clean, well-motivated problem with immediate value for embedding migration & threshold calibration in production RAG.
  • Deliberately simple, annotation-free, reproducible in principle — runnable on any corpus with any LLM.
  • The two-regime + directional-asymmetry findings are non-obvious and actionable.
  • Results reported comprehensively across 3 tables and KDE/box/scatter plots.
  • Honest about scope: flags two-corpus limit, synthetic-query bias, static thresholds, and the in-sample fit issue.

▼ Limitations

  • Only two corpora, two model families (effectively one cross-family contrast) — generalization to Cohere/Voyage/Mistral/BGE/E5 unproven.
  • Conversion fit & evaluated on the same data — no held-out split; deployable accuracy overstated.
  • No ablations on query-LLM choice, query count, chunk size, or IRRELEVANT construction.
  • No cheap-normalization baselines (z-score, percentile rank) to quantify what learned mappings actually buy.
  • Assumes normalized embeddings, static thresholds, single-stage retrieval — no hybrid search or rerankers.
  • Proprietary corpus + unreleased synthetic queries limit exact replication; only cosine studied.

?Open Questions for Further Exploration

  1. How do accuracies hold under a proper train/validation/test split, and how stable are isotonic functions when the target corpus drifts over time?
  2. Does the "intra-family ≈ linear, cross-family ≈ S-shaped" pattern generalize to Cohere, Voyage, Mistral, BGE, E5, GECKO?
  3. Sensitivity of per-class distributions to (a) query-generation LLM, (b) # synthetic queries, (c) IRRELEVANT construction?
  4. Can SQP class-separation stats (e.g. IRRELEVANT↔RELEVANT gap) predict expected cross-model R² before any mapping is fitted?
  5. Could SQP drive continuous drift monitoring — detecting when a deployed calibration has gone stale?
