Continual Learning in Transition

▸ Key Finding Reframes continual learning for the LLM/agent era as capability evolution along three orthogonal axes — When, Where, How — with classical CL collapsing to a single point.

Zhiyan Hou, Dan Zhang, Tao Feng et al. (equal contribution) arXiv:2608.06216 · CASIA / UCAS · NUS · Tsinghua · SJTU · Alibaba

1 Key Metric

1B–7B

Forgetting intensifies with model scale during continual fine-tuning — domain knowledge, reasoning, and reading all degrade as instruction tuning proceeds. (Luo et al.)

…vs. near-lossless: SimpleCPT keeps 405M & 10B models ~negligible-degradation with LR rewarming + small replay.

2 Why This Matters

Unifies scattered topics — continual pre-training, test-time training, agent memory/skill/prompt evolution — into one coordinate system; §4.2 & §4.3 directly hit the self-evolving-agent debate.

3 Critical Edge

No comparative method-vs-method tables on standard benchmarks — all comparisons are verbal. Which continual-FT method actually wins on TRACE is not answered.

§ 01

The three axes, unpacked

Each axis is a relaxed classical assumption. Methods are profiles, not frozen grid points (e.g. SKILL0 migrates harness→params).

When

when does learning happen?

T1Pre-training — continual corpus absorption (SimpleCPT).
T2Post-training — SFT → RLHF/RLVR multi-stage alignment.
T3Inference-time — TTT-LM, Titans, reward-driven TTRL.

Where

where is capability carried?

W1Parameters — LoRA family, full FT.
W2Memory — MemoryLLM, MemoryBank, A-MEM, MemRL.
W3Skills — Voyager library, SkillRL, SKILL0.
W4Protocols — Promptbreeder, rule files (thinnest).

How

how are updates driven?

H1Off-policy gradient — SFT, SDFT self-distillation.
H2On-policy — RLHF/RLVR; structurally small-KL anti-forgetting.
H3Beyond-gradient — Task Arithmetic/TIES merge, MeZO/ZeroFlow.
§ 02

Methodology

Taxonomic & synthetic, not experimental. Four construction steps.

STEP 01

Expose three implicit assumptions

Classical CL silently fixes: (i) learning during a training stage, (ii) capability in parameters, (iii) off-policy gradient updates.

STEP 02

Relax assumptions → questions

Each fixed answer becomes a variable, yielding the When / Where / How questions and a per-method profile that can change over time.

STEP 03

Per-axis literature survey

§3.2–3.4 trace each axis through representative methods (~150+), placed by most-salient carrier or mechanism.

STEP 04

Joint-space cross-analysis

§3.5 + Appendix C.1 record combined profiles — explicitly illustrative, not an exhaustive enumeration protocol.

§ 03

Results & cited findings

No original experiments — these are the landmark results the synthesis is anchored on.
  • Shenfeld 2025
    “RL's Razor”

    On-policy RL is structurally anti-forgetting: drift scales with KL from reference policy; off-policy SFT can drift arbitrarily far.

    ∝ KLqualitative
  • ZeroFlow

    Zeroth-order (forward-only) updates match or exceed first-order FT at mitigating catastrophic forgetting — an intrinsic smoothing property.

    ≥ 1st-orderon forgetting bm
  • Luo et al.

    Continual FT degrades domain, reasoning & reading ability, with the effect intensifying across 1B–7B scale. TRACE confirms erosion of safety & instruction-following too.

    1B → 7Bscale hazard
  • SimpleCPT
    Ibrahim et al.

    LR rewarming + small replay buffer keeps degradation on the original distribution close to negligible for 405M & 10B models.

§ 04

Strengths & limitations

The cleanest unified frame available — but a roadmap, not an evidence table.

Strengths

  • Cleanest unified frame of LLM-era CL currently available; makes the field's scatter legible.
  • Each axis tied to a structural reason it matters (KL-minimization, retrieval decay, context ceiling) — not just a label.
  • §4.2's "harness substitutes for but does not constitute CL" is the most provocative analytical move.
  • The four faces of forgetting (param overwrite / memory decay / context overflow / skill drift) reframes forgetting as multi-layer compression.
  • Honest self-limitations section + an honest AGI distance estimate.

Limitations

  • No quantitative meta-analysis — no method-vs-method tables on TRACE / MMLU-drift; all comparisons verbal.
  • Uneven carriers — "protocols" markedly thinner than memory/skills; rests largely on Promptbreeder + engineering practice.
  • Non-exhaustive, curated method set; density claims illustrative, not bibliometric.
  • Subjective placement rules ("most salient carrier"); paper concedes alternative classifications are equally reasonable.
  • Editorial AGI stance ("frontier model first") is contestable and reflects institutional positioning.
  • Rests on recent unreplicated landmarks (Shenfeld, ZeroFlow, SKILL0, AgentEvolver) — would shift if those revise.
§ 05

Open questions

Where the framework exposes gaps rather than fills them.
  1. Can a single forgetting metric cover all four faces, or do they need fundamentally incompatible measurement protocols?
  2. Does on-policy RL's small-KL advantage survive reward-model drift across post-training stages — or does drift just migrate from policy to reward model?
  3. SKILL0 shows harness→parameter internalization. What is the inverse — making latent parametric capability explicit as a retrievable memory/skill — without expensive distillation?
  4. Capability scheduling across carriers ≈ a cache/memory hierarchy. Can OS replacement theory (LRU-K, ARC) be imported, or are access patterns too task-dependent?
  5. Long-horizon error accumulation (§4.4) is core, yet most harness ops are non-differentiable. Can zeroth-order estimators give a tractable error-accumulation model for agents?
  6. Is there a principled reason protocol evolution is harder than memory/skill — or is a Voyager-equivalent baseline for protocols simply still missing?
