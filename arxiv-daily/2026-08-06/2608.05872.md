> **一句话总结：** MACRO 把冻结 LLM 的层执行顺序建模成上下文相关的马尔可夫策略（支持跳层/重复/残差相加），用训练集反馈做分布估计划更新、用 top-k Viterbi 解码出单一任务级路由，不改任何权重就在 GSM8K 上把 Qwen3-1.7B 从 43.44% 提到 69.52%，并比 Dr.LLM 平均高 +7.2 个点、搜索时间快 9.4 倍。

> **TL;DR:** MACRO rewrites a frozen LLM's layer execution order as a context-dependent Markov policy over skip/repeat/residual-add moves, learned via estimation-of-distribution on training data and decoded with a top-k Viterbi algorithm. With zero weight updates it lifts Qwen3-1.7B's GSM8K accuracy from 43.44% to 69.52% (+26.08), beats the prior best dynamic router Dr.LLM by +7.2 pts average across 6 models/13 benchmarks, and cuts route-search time from 14.8h to 1.6h per benchmark (9.4x); gains shrink to <1 pt on the strongest models and vanish (MACRO actually loses to Dr.LLM) on the reasoning-distilled DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B.

## 核心贡献

- **把层路由形式化为上下文相关的马尔可夫策略**：状态 `s_t=(ℓ, φ, δ, o)` 由当前层、预算阶段（early/mid/late）、入向位移、算子上下文构成；动作含局部跳移、加-应用（把旧隐状态 ht-b 加回再做一层）、Rejoin（接回标准后缀）。整套机制对冻结/量化模型兼容，无需反向传播。
- **用 top-k Viterbi 精确解码代替随机采样或昂贵的测试期搜索**：学习完成后用多项式时间的 Viterbi 递归找出概率最高的 k 条可行路由，再用 held-out 验证集选最优；从而做到"无需测试期标签、无需逐样本搜索、无需离线路由标签生成"。
- **分布估计算法更新全局策略**：从 forward-biased 先验出发，每轮采样 K 条候选、在训练子集+replay buffer 上评分，用 softmax 加权最大似然重估转移表；对小验证集天然有结构性正则。
- **跨 6 个开源 LLM、13 个推理/知识基准全面验证**：相对未路由基线平均 +5.0%，对 Dr.LLM +7.2%、对 Dr.LLM+Ext.(等动作空间) +1.5%，路由搜索成本降 9.4×。

## Key Contributions

- Formalizes layer routing over a frozen transformer as a single global, context-dependent Markov policy `πθ(a|s)` with a compact state (current layer, budget phase, incoming displacement, op-context) and three action families (local layer move, add-and-apply previous hidden state, Rejoin), requiring no gradient through the LLM.
- Replaces per-example test-time search and auxiliary neural routers with an **exact top-k Viterbi decode** of the learned policy followed by held-out validation selection, eliminating the need for test labels, per-instance MCTS, or offline route-label generation (Table 1).
- Introduces an **estimation-of-distribution update**: a forward-biased prior keeps the standard pass most probable; each iteration samples K candidates, scores them on a fresh training subset plus a replay buffer, and refits transition counts with a β-softmax over an elite set of size m.
- Provides the strongest evidence to date that the *action space* — not the router-training machinery — is what makes layer routing help: plugging MACRO's action space into Dr.LLM's pipeline (Dr.LLM+Ext.) is "substantially stronger on every model" than Dr.LLM's native skip-and-repeat space.

## Methodology

MACRO keeps pretrained weights frozen and optimizes only over the space of *routes* r — ordered programs of block applications, skips, repeats, and residual additions. The objective is `r* = argmax Acc(r)` over a fixed train/val/test split.

**State and actions.** The transition policy conditions on `s_t = (ℓ, φ, δ, o)`: current layer ℓ, budget phase φ∈{early,mid,late} (fraction of remaining length budget), incoming displacement δ=ℓ−ℓ_prev, and a binary op-context flag. Actions are (1) local move `apply(ℓ+Δ)` for Δ∈{−r,…,r}; (2) add-and-apply `h_t + γh_{t-b}` followed by a local move; (3) Rejoin, which terminates the learned prefix and runs the standard suffix. A feasibility mask prunes invalid moves (out-of-bounds layers, over-budget length, unseen add sources) before every sampling/decoding step.

**Initialization.** The prior π₀ is forward-biased: the standard next-layer move `a→` gets most of the mass (1−ε_loc−ε_op), local deviations and add actions get small exploration mass, and Rejoin is forced at the last layer. Unvisited states thus default to the unmodified network.

**Update rule.** Iteration i samples K candidates from π_θ_i, scores each `q_j = Acc_{S_i}(r_j)` on the current training subset `S_i = D_i ∪ B_{i−1}` (a replay buffer retains a λ_rep fraction), forms an elite set E_i, computes softmax weights `w_j ∝ exp(β(q_j − max_E q))`, and refits `π̂_{i+1}(a|s) = N_i(s,a) / Σ_{a'} N_i(s,a')` from weighted pseudo-counts initialized at απ_θ_i. Optional EMA smoothing `π_{i+1} = (1−η)π_i + ηπ̂_{i+1}` stabilizes learning.

**Final selection.** After convergence, top-k Viterbi decodes the k highest-log-probability feasible routes `V_k(s) = TopK_{a∈A(s)}[log π(a|s) + V_k(T(s,a))]`; the candidates are then scored on held-out validation and the best is deployed uniformly at test time. Exploration is fully halted before deployment.

## Results & Highlights

- **Headline averages across 6 models × 13 benchmarks (Table 2):** MACRO beats the unrouted baseline by +5.0% mean accuracy; beats Dr.LLM by +7.2%; beats Dr.LLM with the matched MACRO action space (Dr.LLM+Ext.) by +1.5%. On the weakest model (Qwen3-1.7B) MACRO lifts the 13-benchmark average from 50.35 to 63.03 (+12.68 pts); on the strongest (Qwen3-14B 4-bit) it goes 78.52 → 79.29 (+0.78).
- **Single best-known route (Figure 2 / Appendix B):** on Qwen3-1.7B GSM8K the route `L0-L7 L3-L9 RJ` (rewind L3–L7 once, 33 block calls vs. 28) raises test accuracy from 43.44% to 69.52% (+26.08 pts) with zero weight changes.
- **Search-cost reduction (Figure 1):** route-search time drops from 14.8h to 1.6h per benchmark averaged over six shared model settings — a 9.4× speedup over Dr.LLM — while *improving* mean accuracy by +7.2 pts.
- **Statistical significance (Table 3):** treating each benchmark's mean accuracy delta as one observation and applying a one-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank test (Holm-corrected over 6 models), every model improves significantly with CIs excluding zero (e.g. Qwen3-1.7B d̄=+12.68, 95% CI [6.70, 21.38], 13/0/0 W/T/L, p=7.3e-4; Mixtral-8x7B d̄=+1.33, [0.86, 1.91], 13/0/0). No benchmark is significantly degraded on any model.
- **Ablations matter (Table 4):** removing the budget-phase factor from the state drops Qwen3-1.7B average accuracy from 63.03 to 59.56 (−3.47); removing op-context drops it to 61.48 (−1.55). Dr.LLM in its original skip-and-repeat action space frequently lands *below* the unrouted baseline (Qwen3-1.7B avg 46.09 vs 50.35 baseline), confirming the action space is the decisive ingredient.
- **Mechanistic finding (Figure 5):** logit-lens analysis on Qwen3-1.7B shows the standard forward pass already represents the correct answer token internally (high log-prob at mid-depth) but *suppresses* it near the output; the MACRO route preserves the signal through the final readout, suggesting routing elicits an already-computed answer rather than adding new capability.

## Authors & Credibility

Authors are from Heinrich Heine University Düsseldorf (Batorski, Pourhadi, Aitaza, Swoboda), Jagiellonian University and IDEAS Research Institute (Spurek). Swoboda and Spurek have a track record in structured/equivariant deep learning. Code is publicly released at https://github.com/Batorskq/MACRO. The evaluation is unusually broad: 6 open-weight models spanning three families (Qwen3 1.7B/4B/8B/14B-4bit, Mixtral-8x7B-4bit, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B) plus Llama-3.2-3B in the appendix, 13 benchmarks across math reasoning, knowledge QA, and BIG-bench tasks, with 3 seeds and proper statistical testing (cluster bootstrap + Wilcoxon, Holm-corrected). The comparison table honestly reports Dr.LLM numbers from the authors' own reproduction, and the Dr.LLM+Ext. ablation isolates the contribution of the action space.

## Critical Assessment

- **The +5.0% headline is dominated by weak models.** The per-model gains collapse as the base model strengthens: Qwen3-1.7B +12.68, but Qwen3-8B +0.76, Qwen3-14B(4-bit) +0.78, and Llama-3.2-3B only +0.35 (Table A.1, where most benchmarks are 0.00±0.00 — MACRO effectively selected the standard forward pass). A reader scanning only the abstract would over-estimate the benefit on capable models.
- **MACRO outright loses on the reasoning-distilled model.** On DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B (Table 2), Dr.LLM reaches 58.62 avg and Dr.LLM+Ext. 62.53, while MACRO scores 55.91 — *below* both router-trained baselines and barely above the 42.87 unrouted baseline only because that base is pathologically weak. The paper flags "every model except the reasoning-distilled one" once, but the abstract claims a clean win. This is the most important caveat for practitioners.
- **The flagship +26.08 GSM8K result is cherry-picked.** It is the single best route on the single weakest model (Qwen3-1.7B); the same model gets +0.00 on MATH500 (standard forward pass selected), and the gains on the 8B/14B models are an order of magnitude smaller. The figure-2 "rewind L7→L3" anecdote is illustrative, not representative.
- **The "No Per-Ex." / "No Labels" claims need scoping.** There is genuinely no *test-time* per-example search and no *test* labels — but MACRO does require labeled *training* data per task and a multi-hour per-task search. Calling it "parameter-efficient" is fair; calling it label-free is a stretch for any task without a train split.
- **Missing comparisons.** The baselines are restricted to other layer-routing methods (Dr.LLM, CoLA, Neuroanatomy, early-exit). There is no comparison to test-time-compute alternatives (more chain-of-thought sampling, self-consistency, best-of-N) at matched wall-clock, nor to weight-editing methods like activation patching. It is therefore unclear whether rewinding five layers is more cost-effective than simply sampling five more reasoning chains.
- **Search cost is still non-trivial.** 1.6h per benchmark is a 9.4× win over Dr.LLM but is still a per-task cost that accumulates; the one-route-per-model experiment (Table 5) shows this cost can be amortized, but at the price of much smaller gains (Qwen3-1.7B out-of-domain +9.02, Qwen3-4B +1.12).

## Strengths

- Genuinely parameter-free and frozen/quantized-LLM compatible: no gradients, no auxiliary network, no quantization-unfriendly operations — a clean drop-in for deployment.
- The Viterbi + validation-selection design replaces sampler luck with principled decoding; the ablation shows both stages contribute (MACRO > MACRO-top-1 ≥ MACRO-no-Vit.).
- Honest, well-powered evaluation: 3 seeds, 6 models, 13 benchmarks, Wilcoxon with Holm correction, and explicit W/T/L counts. The Dr.LLM+Ext. ablation cleanly isolates *why* the method works.
- Mechanistic logit-lens analysis offers a concrete, testable hypothesis (route elicits, doesn't create, the answer) rather than hand-waving.
- Code released, routes table (Appendix B) lets readers inspect exactly what each discovered route does.

## Limitations

- Gains are inversely correlated with base-model strength; near-zero (and occasionally negative vs. router baselines) on already-strong or reasoning-distilled models.
- Per-task search cost remains ~1.6h/benchmark and requires labeled training data; not a zero-cost intervention.
- Single global policy per task — no input-dependent routing, so all test examples for a task share one route (the authors explicitly flag input-dependent routing as future work).
- The logit-lens evidence is limited to one model and four benchmarks; the "elicit vs. create" question is posed but not resolved.
- No comparison against non-routing test-time-compute baselines at matched compute, leaving open whether the gains beat simply spending the same FLOPs on more decoding.

## Relevance

高度相关：MACRO 属于"冻结大模型 + 测试期/任务级算力再分配"这条线，与用户对 LLM 推理增强、动态深度、测试期 scaling 的兴趣直接吻合；其马尔可夫策略 + Viterbi 解码的范式为不改动权重的推理优化提供了新工具，可启发后续的逐样本动态路由研究。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Can the global per-task policy be made input-dependent (e.g. conditioned on a token or prompt embedding) without reintroducing an auxiliary neural router, and would that recover the large small-model gains on 8B+ models where the current global route is near-flat?
- At matched wall-clock/FLOPs, how does rewinding K transformer layers compare to spending the same compute on K extra chain-of-thought samples with self-consistency — does the logit-lens "elicitation" hypothesis imply the gains are orthogonal and stackable?
- Why does MACRO underperform router-trained baselines specifically on DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B — is the distilled model's layer semantics already saturated by reinforcement-learning distillation, leaving no recoverable signal for restructuring?
- Does the Rejoin-terminated prefix structure transfer to non-transformer architectures (e.g. Mamba/SSM blocks), where "layer rewinding" has a different semantics?
- The replay buffer and elite-count hyperparameters are shown to matter (m=1 and m=12 are failure modes); is there an adaptive schedule that removes this knob entirely?
