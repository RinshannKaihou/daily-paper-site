Why this matters

A label-free, plug-in debiaser that bolts onto G-Eval / Prometheus — making open-weight LLM judges track human agreement more closely, and automated leaderboards more trustworthy.

Critical edge
⚠

No significance testing, and the dev-tuned λ carries an unresolved generalisation gap — plus the method can amplify bias on LLM-Alignment where the model's mode already sits on low-bias "3".

01

How it works

STAGE 1 · PROBE

Measure latent number bias

Ask the LLM to "generate a random integer in [min,max]" via 100 paraphrases; keep the top-10 by lowest perplexity. Average per-token probabilities into Prandom(y).

floor pmin = 1e−12
STAGE 2 · ENCODE

Build the correction term

Deviation from uniform becomes a log-space bias term — negative for over-generated numbers, positive for under-generated ones. Deb-T prepends the task instruction to capture task-specific bias.

B(y) = log U(y) − log Prandom(y)
STAGE 3 · CORRECT

Revise logits at scoring time

Apply λ·B(y) to the scoring logits and take the G-Eval probability-weighted sum. Zero-shot, training-free; λ tuned on dev (search 0.1–1.0); temp fixed at 0.2.

P̃(y) = softmax(l(y) + λ·B(y))

  • STS-B Spearman 61.9 → 71.5 (Deb-T), beating CC (68.1) and DC (61.0); weakest evaluator Llama-3.1-8B lifted from 7.45 to 47.0.
  • Deb-T > Deb-R on most settings — confirming latent bias is task- and score-range-dependent (Qwen/Mistral cluster on "1"; Gemma spikes at both ends).
  • SDD metric catches distribution-level gains that Spearman/MSE miss — where the method wins most decisively.
  • One honest failure: on LLM-Alignment / HelpSteer2 the method barely helps and can inflate the already-dominant "3".
03

Strengths & limitations

+ Strengths

  • Label-free at measurement time — needs only the score range; a one-line logit edit bolts onto any decoder judge.
  • Figure 3 visualisation of per-model, per-task bias is genuinely informative (Qwen/Mistral's "1"-fixation; range effect).
  • SDD metric surfaces distribution gains rank/point metrics miss.
  • Candid failure reporting on LLM-Alignment with a mechanistic explanation, not a hand-wave.

− Limitations

  • No significance testing; several headline margins are small and over only five models.
  • λ generalisation gap (§4.5): best dev λ ≠ best test λ — acknowledged, unresolved.
  • Zero-shot only; few-shot / CoT prompts (which shift calibration) deferred.
  • Scale untested: models 7B–14B only; no read on frontier judges already >80 Spearman.
  • Can backfire when the mode sits on low-bias "3"; no per-sample λ gating.
  • RNG prompt set is ChatGPT-o3-generated; only meta-prompt disclosed; Tp=10 "intuitively set."
04

Open questions

Would per-sample adaptive λ (gated to 0 when the model is already confident) rescue the LLM-Alignment failure — estimable without ground truth?

Does latent-number bias transfer: can a cheap proxy task's bias correct an expensive target task, or must Deb-T be re-measured per task?

How does the correction interact with few-shot / chain-of-thought judging prompts, which shift both accuracy and calibration?

Do frontier closed judges (GPT-4 / Claude-class) show the same uniform-deviation, and does correction still help once Spearman > 80?

Can B(y) be learned jointly with a small reward model rather than Monte-Carlo RNG — smoothing the dev/test λ gap?

Is SDD a better proxy than Spearman for downstream ranking usefulness, and does it track per-sample human agreement?
