01 Methodology

clue c→ author task τ→ structural + self-solve→ CALIBRATION LOOP (≤50 rounds)→ retain / discard

CalibForge reframes task construction: solver pass/fail outcomes are construction-time feedback that revises the task — not a post-hoc filter, and not feedback to the solver (à la Reflexion).

  • Two-stage validation before calibration: structural checks (env builds, tests fail-first) + self-solving by the authoring agent. Only candidates with V(τ)=1 enter the loop.
  • Multi-solver calibration γ_multi — K heterogeneous solvers attempt τ; retain when 0 < Σy_i < K (cross-model disagreement).
  • Contrastive calibration γ_con — designated stronger (V4-Pro) / weaker (V4-Flash) pair; retain on y_s=1 ∧ y_w=0 (strong-pass, weak-fail).
  • Trajectory distillation under CALIBFORGE-EVAL scaffold; full-parameter SFT, 10 epochs, identical teacher recipe across all sources.
  • Decontamination against all three evals: 14-gram prompt overlap + 5-shingle Jaccard over instruction + test code.

03 Strengths & Limitations

▲ Strengths

  • Clean conceptual move: solver behavior as construction-time feedback, with two formal retention criteria.
  • Strong controlled design: matched 1,300-task ablation + trajectory-volume control rules out the obvious confound.
  • Convincing 19% → 96% revision-vs-filter evidence under contrastive calibration.
  • Broader, less skewed domain coverage (largest category 25.5% vs 49.9–74.6% for prior sets).
  • Intellectual honesty: published mechanism-level failure cases in the appendix.

▼ Limitations

  • No explicit Limitations section; no RL experiment — only SFT is studied.
  • High construction cost: up to 50 rounds × multiple solvers × 100 steps × 30-min cap; no wall-clock/$ reported.
  • Depends on multiple frontier models (V4-Pro/Flash, GLM-5, Kimi K2.5) that may not be openly available.
  • Diminishing returns at 35B: OOD edge over best baseline is +0.41pp (SWE-Pro, single run) / +1.57pp (Doc2Repo).
  • No ablation on Rmax or solver-pool composition.

04 Questions for Further Exploration

How sensitive are gains to the strong/weak solver pair? Does the “learnable zone” generalize when the deployed model lies outside the solver interval?
What is the wall-clock / dollar cost per retained task, and how does performance degrade if Rmax drops from 50 to 10 or 20? (Funnel shows 93% retained within 20 probes.)
Does adversarial solver calibration also help RL (GRPO/PPO), or does the gain come mainly from higher-quality teacher trajectories?
Can the signal be made self-improving — let the trained student join the solver pool so the learnable zone tracks its rising capability?
How much OOD transfer comes from the broader domain mix (25.5% SWE) vs the calibration criterion itself? A domain-matched comparison would separate them.
