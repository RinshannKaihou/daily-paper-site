Methodology

How a frozen net is re-routed

Policy over routes

Pretrained weights stay frozen; MACRO optimizes only the space of routes r — ordered programs of block apply / skip / repeat / residual-add. Objective: r* = argmax Acc(r) over a fixed split. No gradient flows through the LLM.

State & actions

Policy conditions on s = (ℓ, φ, δ, o): current layer, budget phase (early/mid/late), incoming displacement, op-context flag. Actions: local move apply(ℓ+Δ); add-and-apply h_t + γh_{t−b}; Rejoin — terminate the learned prefix and run the standard suffix.

Estimation-of-distribution update

A forward-biased prior keeps the standard pass most probable. Each round samples K candidates, scores them on a fresh train subset + replay buffer, forms an elite set, and refits transition counts with a β-softmax. EMA smoothing stabilizes learning.

Exact top-k Viterbi decode

No per-example test-time search, no auxiliary router. After convergence, V_k(s) = TopK[log π(a|s) + V_k(T(s,a))] decodes the k best feasible routes in polynomial time; held-out validation picks the deployed one. Exploration halts before deployment.

  • Ablation isolates the action space: Dr.LLM in its native skip/repeat space lands below the unrouted baseline (Qwen3-1.7B 46.09 vs 50.35); plugging MACRO's action space into Dr.LLM's pipeline ("Dr.LLM+Ext.") is stronger on every model.
  • Mechanistic finding (logit-lens): the standard forward pass already represents the correct token at mid-depth but suppresses it near the output — the route preserves the signal, suggesting it elicits rather than creates the answer.

Strengths & Limitations

Side-by-side verdict

Strengths

  • Genuinely parameter-free: no gradients, no auxiliary network, no quantization-unfriendly ops — a clean frozen/quantized-LLM drop-in.
  • Viterbi + validation selection replaces sampler luck with principled decoding; ablation confirms both stages contribute.
  • Well-powered evaluation: 3 seeds, 6 models, 13 benchmarks, Wilcoxon with Holm correction, explicit W/T/L counts.
  • The Dr.LLM+Ext. ablation cleanly proves the action space is the decisive ingredient — not the router machinery.
  • Logit-lens analysis offers a concrete, testable elicitation hypothesis; code + routes table (App. B) released.

Limitations

  • Gains invert with model strength: Qwen3-1.7B +12.68 vs Qwen3-14B(4-bit) +0.78, Llama-3.2-3B +0.35; the +5.0% headline is weak-model-driven.
  • Outright loses to router baselines on DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B (55.91 vs 62.53) — abstract claims a clean win.
  • Flagship +26.08 GSM8K result is cherry-picked (single best route on weakest model); same model gets +0.00 on MATH500.
  • Per-task search ≈1.6h and needs labeled training data — "label-free" claim only holds at test time.
  • No comparison to non-routing test-time compute (more CoT sampling, self-consistency, best-of-N) at matched FLOPs.

Questions for Further Exploration

Open edges
  1. 1Can the global per-task policy be made input-dependent (conditioned on a token/prompt embedding) without an auxiliary router — and would that recover gains on 8B+ models?
  2. 2At matched wall-clock/FLOPs, how does rewinding K layers compare to K extra chain-of-thought samples with self-consistency — are the gains orthogonal and stackable?
  3. 3Why does MACRO underperform on DeepSeek-R1-Distill — is the distilled layer semantics already saturated by RL distillation?
  4. 4Does the Rejoin-terminated prefix transfer to non-transformer architectures (Mamba/SSM), where "layer rewinding" has different semantics?
  5. 5The elite-count hyperparameter matters (m=1 and m=12 are failure modes) — is there an adaptive schedule that removes the knob?
