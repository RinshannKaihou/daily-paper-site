§ METHODOLOGY

Workload, cluster, measurement

Workload
Full fine-tuning of Qwen3-32B (32.76B dense) in bf16, AdamW. FSDP FULL_SHARD (ZeRO-3) across 16 GPUs → 32.8 GB/GPU model states; live footprint 82 GB/GPU (~30% of 275 GB HBM).
Corpus
Nemotron-Personas-Korea, 1,000,000 records, ~1.01B tokens/epoch. ~4 GB total — 1000× smaller than 4 TB per-node RAM, stays resident in page cache.
Cluster
2 nodes × 8 NVIDIA B300 SXM6 (1100 W TGP, 275 GB HBM). InfiniBand NDR + GPUDirect RDMA inter-node, NVLink intra-node. Among the first published operational accounts on B300.
Training config
seq_len 2048, micro-batch 1, grad-accum 8, world 16 → global batch 128 = 262,144 tok/step; ~3,854 steps/epoch; lr 1e-5 cosine; grad-checkpointing off (memory-rich regime).
Measurement
Power bands from nvidia-smi --query-gpu=power.draw + NCCL flight recorder. A/B held fixed over 300 steps, drop_caches before cold runs, medians reported.
Scaling points
Complete measured epochs (not microbenchmarks), medians over n = 1538 / 767 / 385 steps for 4 / 8 / 16 GPU. Positive control: conserved GPU·h + matching loss.
§ RESULTS

What the numbers say

Strong scaling 4 → 8 → 16 GPU throughput; per-GPU tok/s flat within ~1%; GPU·h/epoch conserved; final loss preserved (0.674 / 0.673 / 0.67). Only loss: ~1% two-node IB boundary tax. 99%efficiency @ 16 GPU
Negative result Pretokenize-and-cache pipeline buys ~0% throughput at this scale: NFS cold 52.8k = NFS warm 53.0k = local cache 53.0k tok/s (within ~0.5%). Corpus fits in page cache. Inverts for data > RAM. 0%cache gain
Failure cost One epoch-end deadlock = ~48 GPU·h hang + ~85 GPU·h unrecoverable progress (epoch-end-only save). Triggered by a per-rank block spread of just 53 blocks (0.17% of workload). ~130GPU·h / incident
evenfix cost Truncating all ranks to global-minimum block count (all_reduce(MIN)) drops a negligible fraction of tokens and structurally eliminates the deadlock hazard. 0.085%tokens dropped
Grad checkpointing Cost ~1.6× throughput (33k vs 53k tok/s) with zero memory benefit at 82 GB / 275 GB. “Always enable grad-checkpointing” is wrong in a memory-rich regime. 1.6×throughput tax
Post-fix validation 16-GPU epoch after the fix: 3,854 steps, rc=0 first attempt, final loss 0.67, median 53.0k tok/s, zero watchdog/abort/deadlock traces. Checkpoint loads and generates fluent text. rc=0first attempt
§ BALANCE

Strengths & limitations

+ Strengths

  • Exceptional honesty. Explicit novelty disclaimers, a dedicated prior-art subsection (§5.4), Threats-to-Validity, and a public correction of the authors’ own earlier misdiagnosis.
  • The negative-results A/B (§4) is genuinely useful — exactly the measurement practitioners over-invest in without testing.
  • Textbook-quality deadlock diagnosis (§5): symptom → triage signature → flight recorder → root cause → prior-art → fix → cost.
  • Power-band triage needs no metric infra, unlike concurrent work relying on ~751 Prometheus metrics.
  • Honest SHARP analysis via Amdahl’s law, with explicit statement of why it does not generalize.

! Limitations

  • Scale capped at 16 GPU / 2 nodes — authors themselves warn against extrapolating past the two-node IB boundary.
  • Single model + single dataset. Generality argument is structural, not empirical.
  • Wattages are B300-specific; only the ordering and per-rank shape discriminators transfer to other SKUs.
  • Negative result is scale-conditional — holds only while data fits in page cache (~4 GB vs 4 TB RAM); inverts above RAM.
  • ~130 GPU·h conflates deadlock severity with epoch-end-only checkpoint policy; a more frequent cadence shrinks the headline.
  • SHARP projection is an Amdahl bound at an operating point where SHARP was not actually provisioned.
  • Artifacts “on reasonable request,” not openly released — plus the B300 hardware itself blocks independent reproduction.
Authors &credibility
All four authors are from Samsung SDS, giving credible access to brand-new B300 (Blackwell Ultra, 2025–26) hardware and a production 2-node cluster. The credibility-enhancing feature is intellectual honesty: it explicitly disclaims algorithmic novelty, names the exact prior art each artifact maps to (PyTorch Join since 1.7/1.10, drop_last, HF Accelerate even_batches, NVIDIA DCGM), and corrects the authors’ own earlier “storage bottleneck” misdiagnosis. The framing as a field report with a Threats-to-Validity section is mature systems-writing practice.
§ OPEN

Questions for further exploration

Does the per-rank packing-imbalance deadlock also manifest with FSDP2 fully_shard once a Joinable implementation lands, and would drop_last at the sampler layer change the discarded-token fraction?
How do the B300 power bands shift under 3D parallelism (TP + FSDP), where communication phases are more frequent and overlap structure differs?
At what GPU count does the two-node IB ~1% tax break down, and at what scale does SHARP’s O(log N) tree pay off empirically rather than by projection?
For datasets exceeding RAM (the inverted regime), what is the quantitative throughput cliff, and does locality-optimized packing recover it?
Can the power-band triage generalize into an automated launch-policy controller that auto-aborts on a sustained ~190 W deadlock signature without a separate watcher host?
Would a more frequent checkpoint cadence (e.g. every 125 steps vs 500) cut the ~85 GPU·h “unrecoverable progress” enough that the headline cost is closer to the ~48 GPU·h hang alone?
