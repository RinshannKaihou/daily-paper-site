> **一句话总结：** SEAM 把"局部正确的科学解释能否拼成一个全局自洽的解释"建模为有限层球的局部到全局粘合问题,用一个 1-上链 ω=Ds 度量相邻区域解释在重叠区上的不一致,并按通道/重叠区分解、再用"预算受限干预"逐一证伪候选修复假说;在 19 个实验(含 FNO OOD 监控、UCI 四数据集、USGS 径流、合成 Burgers/金融/工业)中,即便所有局部 R²>0.999 也能检出全局不可容许。

> **TL;DR:** SEAM is a generator-agnostic framework that makes "can locally-valid scientific explanations be glued into one globally admissible explanation" computable via finite cellular sheaves: each region emits a structured explanation (state/closure/observation channels), neighbors are compared on overlaps, disagreement is assembled into an obstruction 1-cochain ω=Ds that is decomposed by channel and overlap, and competing repair hypotheses are tested by restricting each repair to a "budget" subspace and checking feasibility ω∈im(DP). Across 19 experiments (synthetic Burgers PDEs, Fourier Neural Operator OOD monitoring on Burgers where ω correlates with L² error at Pearson r=0.995 vs r=0.71 for a 5-member ensemble-variance baseline, UCI Household Power/Metro Traffic/Air Quality, USGS streamflow at three sites, synthetic financial and industrial fault systems), SEAM detects global inadmissibility even when every local model scores R²>0.999, attributes failures to specific channels, and recovers injected closure sources to within 0.2% of the true location.

## 核心贡献

- **将"解释可容许性"确立为一类新的科学对象。** 作者明确区分"局部精度/残差满足"与"局部到全局的可粘合性":一组各自局部正确、内部自洽、被数据支持的区域解释,可能在重叠区上互不相容——这种不可容许性是"族"的性质,任何区域内残差或留出分数都无法检测。
- **提出 SEAM 范式(五阶段 + 两个扩展)。** Generate → Restrict → Obstruct → Diagnose → Intervene,外加 Identify(可识别性)与 Monitor(流式监控)扩展。这是一个与具体实例化无关的可复用设计模式,任何能给出结构化区域解释的生成器(PDE 求解器、神经算子、回归模型)都可被审计。
- **给出有限维线性实例化 SEAM-Ω。** 用有限层球(stalk 为向量空间,限制映射为块对角线性映射,作用于覆盖神经的 1-骨架)把范式变成闭式可计算的算子:层球边界矩阵 D、原始可容许性缺陷 ω=Ds、按通道/重叠区的分解、预算受限干预。配套定理包括 Theorem 1(最小代价预算干预,闭式解)、Theorem 2(守恒契约可检测性)、Proposition 7.2(可识别性维数)、Corollary 7.3(闭包受限可恢复性)。
- **把"候选修复假说"形式化为可证伪的假设检验。** 每个假说(如"传感器漂移""缺失物理""接口标定")声明它允许修订哪些通道,用正交投影子 P 表示;"硬可行"当且仅当 ω∈im(DP);精确可行集 Fproper(ω) 收集所有未被重叠证据证伪的具体假说,代价仅在各自预算内比较,不跨预算排序。当所有具体预算都被证伪、仅无限制回退可行时,SEAM 报告"未解决"而非把无限制修复当作因果归因。
- **19 个实验跨 8 组科学主张做实证。** 覆盖合成 PDE 守恒律、FNO OOD 监控、四个 UCI 开放数据集、三个 USGS 径流站点、合成金融与工业系统,系统验证"局部正确≠全局可容许""闭包可恢复""通道归因""可识别性""后端互操作""流式监控"。

## Key Contributions

- **Explanation-admissibility as a new scientific object.** The paper sharply distinguishes local accuracy / residual satisfaction from local-to-global gluing: a family of individually plausible, internally consistent, data-supported regional explanations can be jointly inadmissible, and this inadmissibility is a property of the family that no within-region residual or held-out score can probe.
- **The SEAM paradigm (five stages + two extensions).** Generate → Restrict → Obstruct → Diagnose → Intervene, plus Identify (identifiability) and Monitor (streaming). This is an instantiation-agnostic design pattern; any backend that emits structured regional explanations (finite-volume solvers, FNOs, PINNs, regressors) can be audited unchanged.
- **SEAM-Ω, a finite-dimensional linear instantiation.** Finite cellular sheaves (vector-space stalks, block-diagonal linear restrictions on the 1-skeleton of the cover's nerve) yield closed-form diagnostic, intervention, identifiability, and monitoring operators, with Theorem 1 (minimum-cost budgeted intervention), Theorem 2 (conservation-contract detectability), Proposition 7.2 (identifiability dimensions), and Corollary 7.3 (closure-restricted recoverability).
- **Repair hypotheses as refutable hypothesis tests.** Each candidate account (drifted sensors, missing physics, interface miscalibration) is encoded as an orthogonal projector P stating which channels it permits to revise; hard feasibility is the linear test ω∈im(DP); exact feasibility retains only accounts not refuted by the overlap evidence, and costs are reported within each account rather than used to rank across differently-permitted subspaces. When every proper budget fails and only the unrestricted fallback survives, SEAM reports "unresolved" rather than treating unrestricted repair as a causal account.
- **Nineteen experiments spanning eight scientific claims.** Synthetic Burgers PDEs, FNO OOD monitoring, four UCI datasets (Household Power, Metro Traffic, Air Quality, plus USGS streamflow at three hydrologically distinct sites), and synthetic financial and industrial fault systems jointly evaluate local-correctness-≠-global-admissibility, closure recovery, channel attribution, identifiability, backend interoperability, cross-domain consistency, and learned-generator monitoring.

## Methodology

SEAM-Ω instantiates the paradigm with a **finite cellular sheaf F** over the 1-skeleton of the nerve of a finite cover 𝒰 = (U_i) of the domain. Vertices are regions; edges E are nonempty pairwise overlaps U_ij.

- **Local explanation (Definition 3.2):** each region U_i carries e_i = (u_i, c_i, o_i, ε_i) — a state block (predicted field), a closure block (hidden source/subgrid physics/closure parameters), an observation block (sensor means and covariance diagonal), and an optional contract-metadata block (e.g., accumulated conservation-repair magnitudes). The stalk is F(U_i) = ℝ^{d_i} partitioned into these blocks.
- **Restriction maps (Definition 3.4):** linear maps ρ_{i,ij}: F(U_i) → F(U_ij) that act block-diagonally with respect to the channel partition (Assumption A0) — typically spatial interpolation for the state block, identity-into-overlap for closure, sensor selection for observation.
- **Coboundary and defect (Definitions 3.6–3.7):** the coboundary d⁰: C⁰(F) → C¹(F) is (d⁰s)_ij := ρ_{j,ij}s_j − ρ_{i,ij}s_i; its matrix D is assembled from the restrictions. The raw admissibility defect is ω := d⁰s = Ds. A global section is s ∈ ker D.
- **Channel decomposition (Eq. 1):** under A0, ω = ω_state ⊕ ω_closure ⊕ ω_obs ⊕ ω_meta, with calibrated fractions p_• = ‖W_•ω_•‖²/Z; the dominant channel maximizes p_•.
- **Budgeted intervention (Definition 3.9, Theorem 1):** an intervention budget is an orthogonal projector P onto a "permitted revisions" subspace V_P and a positive-definite cost metric C. The constrained QP min ½δ⊤Cδ subject to DPδ = ω, (I−P)δ = 0 is feasible iff ω ∈ im(A_P) where A_P := DPι_{V_P}. When feasible, δ⋆ = ι_{V_P} C_P⁻¹ A_P⊤ (A_P C_P⁻¹ A_P⊤)⁺ ω in closed form (Eq. 6); repaired stalk is s_repaired = s − Pδ⋆. Standard budgets: AllowState, AllowClosure, AllowSensorRejection, AllowAll (=I). Antitonicity: enlarging a budget under a shared metric cannot raise cost.
- **Closure-restricted recovery (Corollary 7.3):** the closure block D_C has minimum-norm correction δ_C⋆ = D_C⁺ ω_closure ∈ row(D_C) when ω_closure ∈ im D_C; physical missing-source increment is ĉ_missing = −δ_C⋆.
- **Identifiability (Proposition 7.2):** the blind admissible subspace 𝒩 := ker D ∩ ker O contains admissibility-preserving directions invisible to the observation map O; dim 𝒩 = dim ker D − rank(O|_{ker D}), and dim 𝒫 = rank(O|_{ker D}). Computed by a two-stage nullspace/SVD routine (Algorithm 3) that explicitly retains structural null directions.
- **Conservation-contract detectability (Theorem 2):** under baseline cancellation, one-sided zero-neighbor contribution, positive rank, and row-space alignment, ‖ω_e^closure‖ ≥ σ⁺_min(ρ^closure_{q,e}) · ε_repair; a projected variant (Eq. 9) applies when row-space alignment fails.
- **Streaming monitoring (Definition 6.1, Algorithm 4):** for a trained generator G_θ on streaming inputs x_t, the streaming obstruction is ω(t) := Ds(t); D and pseudoinverses are cached, so per-call cost is dominated by one matrix-vector product with D.
- **Workflow:** Algorithm 1 (diagnose) bundles obstruction norm, channel/overlap norms, per-budget hard/tolerance/soft records with residuals, and the 𝒩 certificate into a single auditable diagnostic record.

## Results & Highlights

- **FNO OOD monitoring (Burgers, three covariate-shift families):** the Pearson correlation between ‖ω(t_f)‖ and the reference-solver relative L² prediction error is **r = 0.995 ± 0.003** (Spearman ρ = 0.987 ± 0.005, 95% bootstrap CI [0.991, 0.998]) across 500 trajectories per family. The comparator — a **separate 5-member FNO ensemble's predictive variance** — gives only **r_var = 0.71 ± 0.04** on the same OOD test sets, while costing ~5× the compute (one forward pass per region plus a single coboundary multiplication vs. five full FNO evaluations).
- **Household-power cross-framework audit (UCI Individual Household Electric Power Consumption, seasonal R² > 0.999 locally for all models):** SEAM still detects large global obstruction — ‖ω‖_SK = **9.424 ± 0.732**, ‖ω‖_XGB = **9.391 ± 0.719**, ‖ω‖_SM = **9.424 ± 0.732** — with the state channel dominant and the (winter, spring) overlap carrying the largest seasonal gradient (~1.0 kW RMS disagreement per evaluation point). Local R² > 0.999 coexists with nontrivial global inadmissibility across all three officially maintained baseline frameworks (scikit-learn LinearRegression, XGBoost XGBRegressor, statsmodels OLS).
- **Hidden-source recovery for inviscid Burgers with a Gaussian source (A=0.6, x₀=0.5, w=0.06):** the closure channel contributes **99.9992% of ‖ω‖²** (closure norm 2.020986 vs state 0.005873); the recovered source peak is **x̂₀ = 0.501, within 0.2% of the true center x₀ = 0.5 and displaced by 1.7% of the source width**, with closure-channel repair residual **< 5%**.
- **Conservation-contract detectability (Theorem 2 verification):** observed ‖ω¹²_closure‖ = ‖ω²³_closure‖ = **1.172** vs the predicted lower bound σ⁺_min ε_repair = **0.128** (σ⁺_min = 2.828), exceeding the bound by a factor of **9.2**; the closure-revision budget recovers the repair magnitude to within **0.83% relative error**; all **25 parametric configurations** (5 repair magnitudes × 5 seeds) pass the prespecified 10% discretization-tolerant criterion, with minimum observed slack > 0.9.
- **Data–physics conflict attribution (sensor vs closure corruption):** under sensor corruption, the observation-channel soft squared intervention norm is **0.08 vs 1.47** for closure (ratio 18.4 favoring sensor-rejection); under closure corruption, closure is **0.11 vs 1.39** for observation (ratio 12.6 favoring closure-revision). In both conditions the lower-norm budget correctly identifies the injected cause.
- **PDE model-form discrimination (true = linear advection c=0.5):** ‖ω‖_Burgers = **0.84** vs ‖ω‖_advection = **0.031**, a 96% relative obstruction gap selecting the correct governing model.
- **Industrial multi-zone fault detection:** ‖ω‖_normal = **0.491 ± 0.199** vs ‖ω‖_fault = **2.039 ± 0.501** under a 0.3σ step fault, an increase by a factor of **4.1**, with the state channel correctly dominating.
- **Controlled predictor perturbation (USGS Potomac AR(2), 10 random directions × 7 magnitudes):** the empirical monotonicity fraction of ‖ω‖ in perturbation magnitude is **1.00 ± 0.00** (confirmed at 1.00 across all 50 seeds in a robustness re-run).
- **Zero-floor negative control:** the identical-model control gives ‖ω‖ = **0.0000 ± 0.0000**; a 10⁻⁸-scale Gaussian noise perturbation gives ‖ω‖ = (2.71 ± 0.78) × 10⁻⁸, orders of magnitude below the smallest real-world obstruction of 0.0266.
- **Cover-sensitivity ablation:** ‖ω‖ scales as √n_eval and approximately as √|E|, so the edge-count-normalized metric |E|^{-1/2}‖ω‖ is region-count-invariant; GPU vs CPU coboundary agree to relative tolerance 10⁻¹⁰ on a 32-region, 10³-per-region synthetic problem with <5 ms per-call wall time on one A100.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty (genuine):** Framing explanation-admissibility as a cellular-sheaf local-to-global gluing problem, and pairing it with *budgeted, refutation-based* hypothesis testing (where each account is a projector and exact feasibility is decisive), is a real conceptual contribution that is distinct from prior sheaf-consistency work (Robinson's consistency radius, Hansen–Ghrist opinion dynamics, sheaf-Laplacian GNNs) which collapses overlap disagreement into a single scalar or uses it as a diffusion operator inside a learning objective. SEAM keeps ω as a cochain and resolves it by channel, overlap, and budget.
- **Rigor (strong on theory, mixed on empirics):** The theoretical suite is cleanly stated with explicit assumptions (A0–A4) and consolidated proofs; the antitonicity, closure-recovery, and detectability results are correct and non-trivial. However, every reported experiment uses the Euclidean cost metric **C = I** (Remark 7.1 acknowledges this), so the entire C ≻ 0 generalized-metric machinery — including the restricted-inverse subtlety the authors flag as important — is *never exercised empirically*. Claims about weighted closure costs (Remark 4.2) are correspondingly unsupported.
- **Reproducibility (mixed):** Strengths include a fixed 5-seed schedule, deterministic-experiment seed-invariance checks, a 50-seed robustness run for the perturbation study, stated CFL/grid resolutions (N_x = 200/region, CFL = 0.4), and an explicit data-provenance table. The major weakness is that **no public code or data repository is provided** — availability is "on reasonable request from the corresponding author," which is a known failure mode for reproducibility. The FNO training recipe is specified (1000 trajectories, Adam lr 10⁻³, cosine annealing, 200 epochs, 4 spectral layers, mode cutoff m=16, ~0.65M params), but pseudoinverse/SVD tolerances η_svd, η_feas rely on "Appendix C.2" numerical conventions that depend on recorded library versions.
- **Significance:** The framework's claim to be a *generator-agnostic global-consistency audit* that wraps existing PINN/neural-operator/regression pipelines is appealing and the FNO OOD correlation (r = 0.995) is a striking monitoring result. But the only monitoring comparator is **ensemble variance (r_var = 0.71)** — a deliberately weak baseline. Modern OOD detectors (ODIN, energy score, Mahalanobis, residual-based FNO-specific monitors, likelihood-ratio two-sample tests) are not benchmarked, so the practical advantage over established shift-detection methods is unestablished.
- **Red flags and overclaiming:**
  - The authors are admirably careful in places (e.g., they explicitly state that the FNO correlation "does not establish a calibrated probabilistic detector or a universal causal interpretation," and that the Burgers-vs-advection monotonicity is "an empirical observation … not a claim"). This is commendable honesty.
  - But the headline "nineteen experiments" overstates breadth: a large fraction are synthetic Burgers configurations on three-region covers with hand-crafted restrictions, and the real-world experiments use very simple predictors (per-region AR(2) or OLS on 3-region covers). The "cross-domain" claim rests on UCI tabular regression with R²-style local fits, not on genuinely coupled multi-physics or multi-sensor systems where SEAM's channel decomposition would plausibly shine.
  - The exact-feasibility "scientific verdict" (Definition 2.5) is **brittle for single-channel budgets under A0**: a single-channel hard budget is feasible only when obstruction components *outside* that channel vanish, so any obstruction spread across multiple channels refutes *every* single-channel account and the attribution falls back to soft Tikhonov records. In practice, much of the diagnostic value therefore rests on the soft branch (e.g., the Figure 5 attribution ratios of 18.4 and 12.6 are explicitly "empirical soft diagnostics with residuals … not theorem-level full hard-cost comparisons"), which is weaker than the "exact-hard verdict" framing suggests.
  - Theorem 2's row-space-alignment assumption (A3) is restrictive, and the only backend that satisfies it (CT-SEAM, Appendix D) is engineered to do so by applying a uniform scalar correction over a 1D closure coordinate; the bound's tightness in more general closure spaces is unverified.
  - Restrictions are "hand-crafted linear maps" (Remark 3.5) — a modeling choice that materially affects ω but is not itself audited; learned restrictions are mentioned as future work without validation.

## Strengths

- Conceptually original framing: explanation-admissibility as a sheaf-theoretic local-to-global gluing problem, with budgeted hypothesis testing as the diagnostic engine. This is a genuinely new scientific object distinct from local accuracy or residual satisfaction.
- Clean, fully proven theoretical suite (Theorems 1–2, Proposition 7.2, Corollary 7.3) with explicit assumptions and a useful summary table (Table 1) mapping each result to its operational role.
- Generator-agnostic by construction: PINNs, FNOs, DeepONets, finite-volume solvers, and scikit-learn/XGBoost/statsmodels regressors are all wrapped through the same `from_predictor`/backend interface, with the FNO OOD study as a concrete demonstration.
- Honest reporting discipline: separate exact-hard verdicts from tolerance/soft records, residuals always retained, antitonicity caveat about shared metrics, explicit acknowledgment that ensemble variance is a comparator rather than a gold standard, and explicit "unresolved" rather than forced causal attribution when all proper budgets fail.
- Identifiability analysis is a thoughtful addition: separating detected inconsistency (ω) from genuinely unresolved directions (𝒩 = ker D ∩ ker O) prevents over-claiming attribution when the data cannot distinguish admissible alternatives.
- Strong FNO OOD monitoring signal (r = 0.995) at ~5× lower compute than the ensemble-variance comparator, with the ω(t) signal computed from a single forward pass per region plus a cached coboundary multiplication.
- Comprehensive experimental appendix with seed schedules, deterministic-experiment seed-invariance checks, cover-sensitivity and overlap-fraction ablations, GPU/CPU agreement check, and a baseline comparison against scalar disagreement metrics.

## Limitations

- **All experiments use C = I.** The generalized positive-definite cost-metric theory — including the restricted-inverse subtlety the authors highlight as load-bearing — is never empirically validated.
- **No public code or data release.** Availability is "on reasonable request," undermining the otherwise careful reproducibility documentation.
- **Weak OOD baselines.** Only ensemble variance is compared; no comparison with established OOD/dataset-shift methods (ODIN, energy, Mahalanobis, two-sample tests) that the related-work section itself cites.
- **Predominantly synthetic and small-scale.** Real-world experiments are tabular regressions on 3-region seasonal covers; the framework is not demonstrated on large-scale coupled multi-physics, multi-sensor networks, or high-dimensional learned operators where its channel decomposition would matter most.
- **Exact-feasibility verdicts are brittle under A0.** Single-channel hard budgets require obstruction to vanish outside the channel, so multi-channel obstructions push attribution onto the softer Tikhonov branch, weakening the "exact-hard hypothesis test" framing.
- **Hand-crafted linear restrictions.** The restriction maps (and their channel weights W_•) are geometry-defined modeling choices that materially affect ω and the dominant-channel verdict; their validity is assumed, not audited.
- **Pairwise overlaps only.** SEAM-Ω uses the 1-skeleton of the nerve, so guarantees concern pairwise compatibility; higher-order overlap consistency (triple intersections, etc.) is out of scope.
- **Theorem 2's row-space-alignment condition (A3)** is restrictive, and the only satisfying backend (CT-SEAM) is constructed specifically to satisfy it; general closure spaces require the weaker projected bound (Eq. 9).
- **No comparison against established model-discrepancy methods** (Kennedy–O'Hagan Gaussian-process discrepancy, Bayesian model discrepancy) beyond a conceptual discussion in Section 10.4.

## Relevance

这篇论文与科学机器学习(SciML)、神经算子监控、物理约束学习密切相关:它直接处理 PINN/神经算子/领域分解求解器的全局一致性与 OOD 监控问题,且其"层球上的局部到全局粘合 + 通道分解"思想可作为多区域、多模态、多传感器系统中的可解释诊断与归因工具,对关注模型可信性、可识别性与分布偏移监控的研究方向有较高参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How does ω(t) compare (as an OOD/shift monitor) against established detectors such as ODIN, energy-based scores, Mahalanobis distance, or two-sample dataset-shift tests on the same FNO/Burgers benchmark, beyond the single ensemble-variance comparator?
- Does the framework remain diagnostic when the generalized cost metric C ≻ 0 is non-identity — e.g., does a physics-informed or sensor-noise-calibrated C change which budget survives or the dominant-channel verdict?
- Can learned, interpretable restriction maps replace hand-crafted linear ones without breaking the auditability of D, and how sensitive is ω to restriction-map misspecification?
- How does SEAM scale to higher-order overlap consistency (triple and higher intersections via the full nerve rather than its 1-skeleton), and does the exact-feasibility verdict become less brittle when multi-overlap constraints are included?
- On genuinely coupled multi-physics or multi-sensor systems (rather than per-region AR(2)/OLS on seasonal covers), does the channel decomposition (state/closure/observation) yield qualitatively different attributions than a scalar consistency radius?
- Can the streaming ω(t) be turned into a calibrated sequential change-detection statistic (e.g., CUSUM on ω) with a controlled false-alarm rate, rather than reported only as a correlation with L² error?
- Since exact-feasibility for single-channel budgets is brittle under A0, is there a principled way to combine the soft Tikhonov records across budgets into a calibrated posterior over accounts that respects the "no cross-budget cost ranking" caveat?
