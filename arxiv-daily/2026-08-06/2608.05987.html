01

Methodology — belief revision, not raw gaps

方法
1

Turn-level evidence aggregation

A self-teacher (shared params θ, conditioned on a training-only retrieved skill c⁺) scores each student token. The detached contrast is summed within an environment-aligned turn to give the Bayes-factor proxy:

ek = log πθ(ak | sk, c⁺) − log πθ(ak | sk)
2

Recursive belief update (log-odds space)

Initialise from the GRPO group success rate B₀ = clip(R̄, ε₀, 1−ε₀) (Prop. 7: MLE of task difficulty). Accumulate evidence with geometric decay; the marginal revision is gated by uncertainty:

ck = γ·ck−1 + ek  ;  ΔBk ≈ Bk−1(1−Bk−1)·[ek − (1−γ)ck−1]
3

Outcome-aligned signed credit

qk = sign(Aseq)·ΔBk. Magnitude says where belief changed; sign records agreement with the verifier's outcome. Standardise, then clip to a bounded, strictly sign-preserving multiplier.

wk = clip(1 + b·zk, 1−b, 1+b)  ;  Ãk = Aseq·[(1−λ) + λ·wk]
4

Bounded advantage reshaping — provably safe

Prop. 1–3 guarantee |Ãk−Aseq| ≤ λb|Aseq|, sign(Ãk) = sign(Aseq), and exact GRPO recovery at λ=0. One shared setting across all envs and scales: λ=0.5, b=0.2, γ=0.95. No critic, no extra rollout — one teacher forward per turn.

02

The gate & what each piece buys

机制

Belief gate B(1−B) — influence under uncertainty

evidence is amplified near B≈0.5, inert once support saturates

Pivotal turns (belief undecided) get amplified; routine turns (support saturated) get suppressed. This is the central insight a raw local gap cannot express.

04

Strengths & limitations

优劣

Strengths

  • Right central insight. A local gap is not sequential credit; the revision of accumulated belief is. The B(1−B) gate explains why pivotal turns get amplified.
  • Provably safe. Strictly sign-preserving, bounded reshaping (Prop. 1–3); exact GRPO recovery at λ=0 — cannot destabilise training.
  • Minimal overhead. One teacher forward per turn, no critic, no extra rollouts; a single shared hyperparameter set across three envs and two scales.
  • Orthogonal ablation. Table 2 cleanly decomposes the gain and pins it on the signed direction + group-rate prior — actionable for future work.
  • Honest approximation. Appendix A.1 states the rare-success limit openly rather than overclaiming a calibrated posterior.

Limitations

  • Skill dependency. The entire teacher signal needs a retrieved c⁺; in code/math/real-web domains without a SkillBank, the privileged branch is undefined.
  • Narrow coverage. Three text envs, 3B/7B only. No SWE-bench, no code, no multimodal, no frontier models. "Long-horizon" rests on ALFWorld's handful of turns.
  • No significance tests. All deltas are single-seed; horizon slopes have no CIs. Several wins (e.g. +0.2 over SDAR on Search-QA-7B) sit inside plausible noise.
  • A real loss, underplayed. WebShop-7B Acc: SDAR 82.8 > AgentOPSD 79.7. "Six of eight" is weaker than the abstract's "consistently outperforms."
  • Uncalibrated belief. Bk is relative support, not a probability — the "Bayesian" framing is analogical; really a sigmoid-gated decay accumulator.
  • No GiGPO comparison. The most direct env-reward turn-level baseline is dismissed as "complementary" and never run.
05

Open questions

待探
Q1
How does it behave when c⁺ is noisy or absent? A wrong-skill ablation would test whether the gate degrades gracefully or amplifies misinformation.
Q2
Can it combine with GiGPO (env-reward step-level advantages) instead of GRPO, since the authors call them complementary signals?
Q3
Does belief revision transfer to SWE-bench / multi-file code editing, where one trajectory spans dozens of decisions and B(1−B) should matter most?
Q4
Does turn-level credit fade at scale? Some 3B→7B gains narrow — does it matter less as the base model strengthens?
Q5
Is the uncalibrated Bk fixable with temperature/isotonic calibration on a held-out success set, and would that improve the slope further?
