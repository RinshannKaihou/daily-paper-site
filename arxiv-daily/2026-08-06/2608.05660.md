> **一句话总结：** 该论文提出一个三流式内部状态检测器，在残差流轨迹的"运动"(层间位移)之上，叠加"区域"(向量量化粗定位)与"方向"(归一化细定位)两种受限的状态视图，仅在一个推理基准上训练后，即可在未见过的推理基准和事实类任务上将候选答案选择准确率比仅用位移的 SOTA 提升高达 12 个百分点，且只需约 6.7% 的额外参数。

> **TL;DR:** A three-stream internal-state detector for LLM reasoning validity fuses motion (layerwise residual-stream displacements, as in Truth-as-a-Trajectory) with two deliberately restricted state views — a vector-quantized coarse region code and a normalized per-layer direction — to resolve a "state-restoration trade-off." Trained on a single reasoning donor (e.g., ARC-Challenge) on Llama-3.1-8B, it lifts out-of-distribution selection accuracy from 63.2 (displacement-only TaT, +12.0) and 64.1 (linear probe, +11.1) to 75.2 on ARC-C-donor transfer, and — though trained only on reasoning — also leads every trained detector on the unseen FACTOR and VitaminC factual benchmarks (e.g., FACTOR-news 58.2 vs TaT 44.9 vs probe 31.2), while adding only 0.31M parameters (6.7% of the motion LSTM).

## 核心贡献

- **提出"状态恢复权衡"(state-restoration trade-off)**：明确指出基于层间差分(位移)的轨迹方法虽能抑制与标签相关的虚假信号(措辞、格式、主题等)，但同时丢弃了解释"运动"所需的推理相关状态信息；而恢复完整状态又会让虚假信号卷土重来。论文用图 3 的措辞重建实验定量刻画了这一权衡(位移 R²=0.007 vs 单层原始状态 R²=0.086)。
- **设计两个互补的"受限状态读取器"**：(1) 区域读取器用可学习的 K=128 码本对归一化后的多层状态做向量量化，只保留"状态落在状态空间的哪个区域"；(2) 方向读取器保留所选答案 token 在固定 6 层窗口内每一层的单位归一化方向，保留更细的几何信息。
- **三流融合检测器**：将运动(位移 LSTM)、区域(量化码)、方向(归一化方向)三种嵌入拼接后送入 MLP 头，用配对目标从头联合训练；三种读取对深度的处理不对称(运动按层序递归，两个定位流把深度当无序集合)。
- **跨任务与跨模型的强迁移**：在 Llama-3.1-8B、Qwen2.5-14B、Qwen3-30B-A3B(含 MoE)上，仅训练于一个推理基准的检测器在 8 个推理基准和 4 个事实类基准上零样本迁移均优于线性探针与位移基线；消融表明三种信号互补。
- **区域码作为"共享词汇"**：事实类答案使用的码本条目全部落在推理答案使用的条目之内，没有任何"仅事实"的码，这解释了为何训练于推理的检测器能读出未见过的事实错误——它读的是"正确性"而非"某一种推理"。

## Key Contributions

- **Identifies a state-restoration trade-off.** Trajectory differencing (displacement) suppresses stable, label-correlated surface signals but also drops the reasoning-related state needed to interpret the motion; restoring the full single-layer state reintroduces those shortcuts. The paper quantifies the trade-off (Sec. 3, Fig. 3): a last-token state at the probe's layer leaks answer wording at R²=0.086 (retrieval 2.26) and pooled over the answer at R²=0.191 (retrieval 3.13), versus R²=0.007 / retrieval 1.01 for displacement.
- **Two complementary, restricted state readers.** A region reader projects normalized multi-layer states through a learned K=128 codebook (VQ-VAE-style, straight-through estimator, commitment loss, codebook-reset) keeping only a coarse state location; a direction reader keeps per-layer unit-normalized states, giving finer geometry while discarding magnitude, non-answer tokens, and out-of-window layers.
- **Three-reader fusion.** Motion (the displacement bi-LSTM of Truth-as-a-Trajectory, taken unmodified) + region code + direction features are concatenated and passed to a shared MLP head trained from scratch with a pairwise objective. The three streams treat depth asymmetrically (recurrent order vs. unordered set).
- **Strong zero-shot transfer.** Trained on a single reasoning donor and scored on 8 reasoning + 4 factual benchmarks, the full model is best in 47/48 reasoning cells on Llama-3.1-8B, and on all three base models (incl. a 30B-A3B MoE) it beats both the linear probe and the motion-only reader on reasoning and factual transfer.
- **Minimal-sufficiency principle with shared codebook evidence.** Region codes used by factual answers are a subset of those used by reasoning answers, supporting the claim that the streams read "correctness" rather than a kind of reasoning.

## Methodology

The detector operates in the **selection setting**: for each candidate answer, one frozen-model forward pass records the residual-stream state h_{t,ℓ} at every layer ℓ and token t; the detector scores each candidate and the highest-scoring one is chosen. No weights are written back into the base model.

**Motion reader (unchanged from prior work).** Reads layerwise displacements d_{t,ℓ} = h_{t,ℓ+1} − h_{t,ℓ}, stacked over all tokens and layers into a sequence read by a 2-layer bidirectional LSTM (128 hidden units) whose final state is the motion embedding c_mot. Differencing attenuates persistent token-specific content, which is what lets the signal transfer across datasets.

**Shared preprocessing for the two location readers.** Both (i) give up the magnitude (residual-stream norm grows with depth, so magnitudes track depth rather than content) by using unit-normalized states u_{t,ℓ} = h_{t,ℓ}/‖h_{t,ℓ}‖; (ii) are confined to answer tokens (the question is common to every candidate); (iii) read a fixed window of m=6 mid-to-late layers chosen from the literature on layer roles (early layers group by surface form, late layers work in the next-token space, the middle is where representations detach from wording). Each layer's normalized direction is projected by a depth-shared matrix W and concatenated into g_t.

**Region reader (coarse).** A learned K-entry codebook trained jointly with the detector. The answer's final-token projection passes through an encoder to z, then is replaced by its nearest codebook entry q(z)=e_{k⋆}; no continuous code reaches the head. Trained with the straight-through estimator and a commitment term to prevent collapse; unused entries are reseeded.

**Direction reader (fine).** Keeps g_t whole at each of the m depths (rather than compressing to one label). A small depth-shared MLP maps each g_t to p_t; the embedding is c_dir = W_c [p_{t⋆}; (1/|A|)Σ p_t] — combining the final-token state with the mean over the answer span.

**Fusion.** ŷ = σ(f([c_mot; c_dir; c_reg])) for each candidate; the candidate with the highest ŷ is selected. All parts train jointly from scratch under a pairwise objective with the commitment term added.

**Setup.** Base models frozen: Llama-3.1-8B (32 layers, read at 8,12,16,20,24,28), Qwen2.5-14B (48 layers), Qwen3-30B-A3B MoE (48 layers, 3B active). Donors: ARC-Challenge/Easy, OpenBookQA, CommonsenseQA, Social IQa, HellaSwag (capped at 10k items each). Eval: the 6 donors + Story Cloze + MMLU for reasoning; FACTOR-wiki/news/expert and VitaminC for factual transfer. TriviaQA and HaluEval are excluded because a length-only heuristic scores 88.8/95.9 on them.

## Results & Highlights

- **Reasoning transfer (Table 1, ARC-Challenge donor, Llama-3.1-8B, selection accuracy).** Ours OOD = 75.2 vs Motion-only (TaT) 63.2 (+12.0) vs Linear Probe 64.1 (+11.1); in-distribution ARC-C cell Ours 74.2 vs TaT 62.2 vs Probe 69.0. Across all donors, OOD gain over the probe is +10 to +21 points and over the motion-only reader +7 to +12.
- **Factual transfer with NO factual training (Table 2, mean of 3 best donors).** FACTOR-news: Ours 58.2 vs TaT 44.9 (+13.3) vs Probe 31.2 (+27.0); FACTOR-expert: Ours 69.5 vs TaT 50.8 (+18.7) vs Probe 55.5 (+14.0); FACTOR-wiki: Ours 51.9 vs TaT 39.4 (+12.5); VitaminC: Ours 67.1 vs TaT 57.6 (+9.5) vs Probe 62.3 (+4.8).
- **Cross-model robustness (Table 16).** On Qwen2.5-14B reasoning mean 82.3 vs TaT 68.6 (+13.7), factual mean 63.6 vs 46.4 (+17.2); on Qwen3-30B-A3B (MoE) reasoning 82.2 vs 71.6 (+10.6), factual 62.2 vs 50.3 (+11.9). The full model is the best of the three readers on every cell except one.
- **Wording-leakage control (Table 13).** The two location streams hand back almost none of the answer wording: held-out reconstruction R² = 0.007 (motion) / 0.009 (direction) / −0.003 (region), versus R² = 0.086 for a last-token raw state and 0.191 for a pooled raw state at the probe's layer.
- **Ablation (Table 3, ARC-C donor).** Adding the direction stream to motion lifts per-target accuracy by +3 to +20 points; adding region by +4 to +15; the two together are best on every target.
- **Parameter cost (Table 14).** The two location streams add 0.31M params = 6.7% of the motion LSTM; the full detector is 5.08M params = 0.06% of the frozen 8B base model — same overhead as the displacement-only baseline it builds on. Scaling the motion reader to 4× width (25.7M params) still reads 10 points below the full model's 5.1M.
- **Region-code signal (Table 15).** A code-only majority vote predicts correctness above base rate on all four sets tested (ARC-C 81.2 vs 75.0 base; Story Cloze 88.2 vs 50.0).

## Authors & Credibility

The work comes from the Australian Institute for Machine Learning (Adelaide) and collaborators, and carries an AAAI 2027 copyright notice. The lead author (Hamed Damirchi) is also first author of the "Truth as a Trajectory" paper (ACL 2026) that this work extends, so this is a legitimate, well-contextualized continuation rather than a re-derivation. The empirical work is unusually careful for the venue: three base models including a mixture-of-experts, six training donors in a full cross-donor grid, three-seed stability tables, a parameter-count parity control (Table 11), an honest wording-leakage measurement (Table 13), an explicit layer-window sweep (Table 19), and a late-fusion decomposition that surfaces how much each stream uniquely rescues (Appendix J). Benchmarks with trivial length shortcuts (TriviaQA, HaluEval) are explicitly excluded and the heuristic that exposes them is reported (Table 6).

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty.** The framing of a "state-restoration trade-off" and its resolution via two deliberately restricted views (coarse region via VQ + fine direction via normalization) is genuinely new; the motion reader itself is taken verbatim from the authors' own prior work, so the novel technical content is the two location readers and the principle behind them.
- **Rigor.** High. Controls isolate the gain: scaling the motion LSTM to 4× width does not close the gap (Table 11), removing magnitude from the motion reader does not reproduce the gain (Table 12), and the layer-window sweep shows depth matters more than count (Table 19). Seed stability is tight (±0.1–1.0 points, far under the ~8-point margin).
- **Reproducibility.** Likely reproducible but unconfirmed: all three base models and all benchmarks are public, the training recipe (Adam, lr 1e-3, cosine, batch 16×4 accum, 7 epochs, 10k-item donor cap, fixed layer windows per model) is fully specified, and layer selection for the probe is deterministic given the donor split. However, **no code or checkpoint release is mentioned**, and the region codebook initialization / moving-average details rely on Appendix M, which a re-implementer would have to reconstruct.
- **Significance.** Moderate-to-high for the interpretability / hallucination-detection community: the claim that reasoning validity is legible from "state-conditioned motion" and transfers even to unseen factual errors is a meaningful step beyond single-layer probing.
- **Red flags / overclaiming to watch.**
  - The headline "ahead of every detector we compare against" on factual benchmarks is carefully scoped: it compares only against *trained* detectors. The **frozen base model's own answer likelihood actually beats the full model on all three FACTOR sets** — FACTOR-wiki 57.9 vs 51.9, FACTOR-news 72.3 vs 58.2, FACTOR-expert 74.6 vs 69.5 (Table 2). A casual reader of the abstract could miss that the detector loses to raw likelihood on the very benchmarks used to advertise "factual transfer."
  - The main baseline being improved upon (motion-only TaT) is the authors' own prior work; the linear probe is a comparatively weak static baseline, and no modern consistency-based or self-evaluation baseline is included.
  - The FACTOR-expert per-donor cells rest on only ~236 items and are explicitly called noisy; the single cell where the probe beats Ours (FACTOR-expert / ARC-Easy donor) is acknowledged but downplayed.
  - The "three complementary streams" claim is materially weaker on reasoning than on VitaminC: the late-fusion overlap (Fig. 8, Appendix J) shows the region reading sits *almost entirely inside* the direction reading on ARC-C / Story Cloze / FACTOR-expert — region seldom reads an item on its own. The complementarity is real but concentrated on VitaminC.
  - The chosen 6-layer window is *not* the best: the middle-third window reads 3.0 points higher on the reasoning mean (75.6 vs 72.6, Table 19). The authors keep their window for principled pre-registration reasons, but this leaves measurable accuracy on the table.
  - The detector only works in the selection setting — it cannot judge a single answer in isolation — and its ceiling is bounded by what the base model itself distinguishes (raw-state linear read is only AUC 0.61 on FACTOR-wiki).

## Strengths

- Clean conceptual contribution: the state-restoration trade-off is precisely articulated and quantitatively motivated before any method is proposed.
- Principled minimal-sufficiency design — both location readers are *narrow by construction* (region is a single codebook index; direction drops magnitude, out-of-window layers, and non-answer tokens), and the paper verifies post-training that the restriction holds (low R² wording leakage).
- Remarkable parameter efficiency: state-of-the-art transfer at 0.06% of base-model parameter count, and width-scaling shows the gain is design, not capacity.
- Strong, honest controls: surface-confound exclusion (TriviaQA/HaluEval), wording-leakage measurement, cross-model replication on a MoE, late-fusion decomposition exposing per-stream unique recoveries.
- The "shared codebook" observation (factual codes ⊆ reasoning codes) is an elegant piece of evidence that the signal is about correctness, not task identity.

## Limitations

- Selection-only: cannot score the absolute correctness of a lone answer, only rank candidates an item already supplies.
- Requires the residual stream, one forward pass per candidate, a labeled donor benchmark, and per-model training.
- Performance is capped by what the base model distinguishes; on FACTOR-wiki the raw-state AUC is only 0.61, and every method (including this one) reads FACTOR-wiki lower than FACTOR-expert.
- The chosen layer window is suboptimal by ~3 points versus the middle third; the authors keep it for pre-registration honesty but acknowledge the loss.
- Magnitude is discarded and may carry correctness signal; a reading that keeps it as a separate quantity is untested.
- The current fusion leaves 3–21 points of selection accuracy on the table versus a per-item oracle (Table 10), i.e. the three streams jointly hold more signal than concatenation recovers; closing this needs a learned per-item gate, not another fixed combining rule.
- The region stream contributes little beyond the direction stream on most reasoning benchmarks; its independent value is mainly visible on VitaminC.

## Relevance

与"利用 LLM 内部状态检测推理/事实错误"这一研究方向高度相关：该论文把可解释性(残差流轨迹、线性探针的脆弱性)与幻觉/推理错误检测这两个用户关心的主题直接结合，其"运动 + 区域 + 方向"的三流框架对构建更可靠、跨任务可迁移的内部状态检测器具有方法论参考价值，且对理解 LLM 内部正确性表征的几何结构有启发。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Can the detector be extended from the selection setting to absolute per-answer validity scoring (e.g., via calibration against a correctness prior), and how does its score behave as a standalone confidence signal?
- Would a learned per-item gate over the three streams close the 3–21 point oracle gap, and would such a gate also serve as an *interpretability* instrument stating which reading a given decision rests on?
- Does restoring the residual-stream magnitude as a separate, bounded quantity add correctness signal, or does it predominantly re-introduce wording/depth shortcuts?
- The middle-third window outperforms the chosen window by 3 points on reasoning — does a principled (data-driven but donor-blind) layer-selection rule recover that gap without overfitting?
- Since the region stream is largely subsumed by the direction stream on reasoning but not on VitaminC, is there a task-adaptive way to weight the streams, and does the region code encode anything that direction provably cannot?
- How does the detector behave on free-form / open-ended generation (chain-of-thought) where candidate answers are not pre-supplied, and can the trajectory signal localize *which* step in a reasoning chain is unsound?
