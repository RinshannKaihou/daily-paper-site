§ 01 Methodology — closed-form, not gradient descent

GROM edits late-MLP down-projection matrices W ∈ ℝm×n — the residual writers where facts are stored. Up-/gate-projections are excluded because their nonlinearity breaks the additive-shift control.

Two gradient-free forward passes collect per-token keys over forget (X_f) and retain (X_r) sets. The optimal additive update is the unique minimizer of a ridge-regularized least-squares objective:

minP  (wr/r)‖P Xr‖² + (wf/s)‖P Xf − D‖² + μ‖P‖²
P* = (wf/s) · D XfT A−1,   A = (wr/r)XrXrT + (wf/s)XfXfT + μI

Theorem 2 adds a Sherman–Morrison–Woodbury downdate giving the exact per-example deletion influence ΔP_i = L_i R_iT from one cached A−1 — audit any deletion request in seconds, no re-solve.

Layer choice is a logit-lens attribution score S_ℓ = F_ℓ − R_ℓ, not grid search. Only three true hyperparameters: β (edit strength), w_r (retain weight), ρ (ridge, a numerical safeguard).

§ 03 Strengths & Limitations — two axes, one view

▲ Strengths

  • Mathematically clean: one closed-form solve per layer, complete proofs in Appendix A, easy to implement and audit.
  • Genuinely gradient-free — only forward passes; naturally compatible with frozen/quantized inference engines.
  • Survives the 4-bit attack (Zhang et al. 2025) that undoes most current unlearning — the rare, timely property.
  • Specificity weight α_j (suppress only forget-distinctive tokens) is well-motivated; ablating it drops every score.
  • Exact per-example deletion influence → directly useful for GDPR right-to-be-forgotten reporting.

▼ Limitations

  • Not zero-knob: retain weight w_r must be tuned per benchmark ("both ends fail outright").
  • Layer choice is heuristic-then-fixed; no principled per-model automatic selector.
  • Ceiling at ~8B parameters — no 30B/70B experiments; scaling claim is aspirational.
  • Single attack vector tested (4-bit); not relearning/jogging, adversarial prompts, or membership inference.
  • On raw verbatim forgetting (1-Rouge-L) ROME/MEMIT/AlphaEdit/IDKNLL beat GROM on TOFU-10% — GROM wins only via utility preservation.
  • MLP down-projection only; attention/embedding knowledge may be missed (o_proj edges it out on TOFU-5%).

§ 04 Questions for Further Exploration

  1. Does the closed-form solve scale to 30B / 70B models, or does the n×n Gram matrix A become a memory bottleneck requiring approximation?
  2. Behavior under relearning/jogging attacks (Hu et al. 2024) and adversarial-prompt extraction — is the content truly deleted or only removed from the edited MLP write paths?
  3. Can the exact per-example influence (Theorem 2) be used to curate the forget set (flag examples whose removal would damage retain utility), not just audit it?
  4. When forget/retain sets are highly entangled beyond MUSE-Books, does α_j still suffice?
  5. Could the ridge-LS formulation extend to a joint solve over attention o_proj + embeddings to capture FFN-missed knowledge?
