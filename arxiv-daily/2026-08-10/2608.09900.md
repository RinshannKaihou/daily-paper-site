> **一句话总结：** 该文提出 Decoding-Level Taboo——在解码时对"词首"位置屏蔽 top-i 个最高 logit 的零提示压力测试，用注入惊异度（injected surprisal, bits）量化施加的"剂量"；在 GSM8K 上，i=1 时 Gemma-3-12B 的条件保留率从 base 的 25% 升到 instruct 的 90%、Qwen2.5-7B 从 8% 升到 48%，而 Llama-3.1-8B 几乎没有对齐增益（14%→16%，但到 70B 时恢复为 36%→75%），说明"离轨鲁棒性"是后训练对齐 + 规模共同赋予的、且仅体现在多步生成式推理上（MMLU 塌向选择题地板、HumanEval 全线归零）。

> **TL;DR:** Taboo is a zero-prompt, training-free logit-space intervention that masks the top-i candidate tokens only at word-initial decoding steps, forcing "machine circumlocution," and grades the perturbation by injected surprisal in bits. Across Qwen2.5 / Gemma-3 / OLMo-2 / Llama-3 (0.5B–72B) on GSM8K, MMLU, TriviaQA and HumanEval, the comparison is base vs. instruction-tuned checkpoints under identical 2-shot prompts: at the mildest dose (i=1) GSM8K conditional retention goes 25%→90% for Gemma-3-12B (+65 pts), 8%→48% for Qwen2.5-7B (+40 pts), 15%→45% for OLMo-2-7B, but only 14%→16% for Llama-3.1-8B — an exception that disappears at 70B (36%→75%). Scale compounds the effect (Qwen instruct: 6% at 0.5B → 93% at 32B at i=1), MMLU collapses to the MCQ floor at every size (an admitted formatting artifact), and HumanEval Pass@1 goes to 0% for every model (a single 1/102 cell aside). Worth reading if you care about deployment-time robustness diagnostics or about what instruction tuning actually buys; skip if you want a new training method — this is a measurement instrument, not a model improvement, and the headline signal essentially lives on GSM8K alone.

## 核心贡献

- **提出 Decoding-Level Taboo**：一种零提示、零训练的运行时诊断，直接在 logit 空间加二值掩码 $M_t \in \{0,-\infty\}^{|V|}$，把 top-i 名义候选置为 $-\infty$，强迫模型走"次优路径"。关键设计是**只在词首（word-initial）token 步干预**，词内 subword 续写完全不掩码——因为在词中干预只会破坏分词而非施加语义压力。
- **用"注入惊异度"$\Delta S_t$ 把干预强度变成可比较的连续剂量**：$\Delta S_t = -\log_2 P_{nom}(x_t^*) + \log_2 P_{nom}(x_t^{nom})$，即在模型自身信念下被迫吐出替代 token 的额外比特代价；按干预次数归一后可跨架构、跨 tokenizer 比较。
- **提出条件保留率 R（Conditional Retention Ratio）**：只在模型贪心解码本来做对的题目子集 $Q_{correct}$ 上统计 Taboo 后仍正确的比例，把"离轨推理韧性"与"名义能力"解耦。
- **实证结论**：离轨鲁棒性由「规模 × 后训练对齐」共同决定，且**仅特异于多步生成式推理**；四族模型中三族对齐显著提升保留率，Llama-3 在 7–8B 是例外但在 70B 复现增益。
- **机制线索**：Qwen/OLMo/Gemma 的 instruct 检查点在每个 rank 都吸收更多 $\bar{\Delta S}$（下一 token 分布更尖锐）却保留更多，说明增益不是"有效剂量更轻"；Llama 的 instruct 吸收的惊异度并不比 base 多（8B 与 70B 皆然），其 70B 的增益因此机制上与其他三族不同。
- **提出该原语的外延用途**：安全护栏审计（屏蔽拒答 token 看是否立刻越界）、合成 CoT 轨迹多样化采样、结构化输出（JSON/function-call）压力测试、以及把 Taboo 采样嵌入 GRPO rollout 做 taboo-guided 对齐。

## Key Contributions

- A **zero-prompt runtime stress test** that decouples the perturbation from the prompt entirely, avoiding the confound in IFEval / FollowBench-style negative constraints where a failure cannot distinguish instruction-refusal from reasoning collapse.
- The **word-initial-only masking rule**, justified by an ablation rather than asserted: mid-word masking at matched width i=2 collapses GSM8K absolute accuracy to 0.01–0.05 across all four families and both BPE and SentencePiece tokenizers (Gemma-3-12B-it: 0.01 mid-word vs. 0.81 word-initial at a *higher* effective intervention).
- **Injected surprisal** as a graded, model-comparable dose metric, empirically shown to grow roughly logarithmically with the taboo rank i.
- A **base-vs-instruct × scale × benchmark grid** (0.5B–72B, four families, four benchmarks, i ∈ {1,2,4,8}) with 95% Wilson intervals, plus precision, temperature, extraction, and task-constraint controls.
- A clean **negative control**: HumanEval, where formal syntax has near-zero lexical redundancy at word starts, establishing the empirical lower bound of the method.

## Methodology

Under nominal greedy decoding a model picks $x_t^{nom} = \arg\max_j z_{t,j}$. Taboo adds an additive binary mask before softmax, setting $M_{t,j} = -\infty$ for $j \in \text{Top-}i(z_t)$ **only when** a word-boundary predicate $W(x_t, x_{<t}) = 1$, i.e. when the model's own preferred continuation would start a new lexical word. Word-initial status is a static vocabulary-level property, precomputed once as a boolean mask over the vocabulary from tokenizer prefix metadata (`Ġ` for byte-level BPE in Llama-3/Qwen2.5, `▁` for SentencePiece in Gemma-3). Every word therefore has a two-phase lifecycle: masked initiation, then completely unmasked subword completion. Implementation is a `TabooLogitsProcessor` in HuggingFace `transformers`; per-step cost is a vectorized top-i selection plus masked argmax, negligible next to the forward pass. The only material cost is indirect: blocked continuations lengthen generations (~1.4× mean tokens at i=1 on GSM8K).

Models: Qwen2.5 (0.5B–72B), Gemma-3 (12B, 27B), Llama-3 (8B, 70B), OLMo-2 (7B), each in both base and instruct/aligned checkpoints. Benchmarks: GSM8K (multi-step math), MMLU (4-way MCQ, deliberately included to expose a structural boundary since the single-letter answer is itself word-initial), TriviaQA (factual recall), HumanEval (negative control). All conditions use a standardized 2-shot CoT prompt on fixed seeded subsamples (n=500 for the 7–12B comparisons, n=100 for the scale ladder and ≥32B runs). Scoring is deterministic (final-number extraction for GSM8K, SQuAD-style normalization for TriviaQA, sandboxed execution for HumanEval Pass@1). The primary metric is conditional retention R restricted to baseline-correct items; a "marginal retention" ratio Acc_taboo/Acc_base is used for a few auxiliary runs. Main experiments use greedy decoding to permit exact item-level confidence intervals; a temperature-sampling re-run holds the taboo mask fixed and samples only the continuation.

## Results & Highlights

- **Alignment effect on GSM8K at the mildest dose (i=1), conditional retention, base → instruct:** Gemma-3-12B 25% → 90% (+65 pts), Qwen2.5-7B 8% → 48% (+40 pts), OLMo-2-7B 15% → 45% (+30 pts), but Llama-3.1-8B 14% → 16% (+1 pt, overlapping Wilson CIs). At the harshest dose i=8, instruct checkpoints still hold 51% (Gemma), 30% (Qwen), 16% (OLMo) while **every** base model has collapsed below 8%.
- **Scale ladder (Qwen2.5, GSM8K, i=1):** base retention 6% (0.5B), 11% (1.5B), 23% (3B), 8% (7B — a non-monotonic dip), 52% (14B), 59% (32B), then *down* to 39% at 72B; instruct rises 6% (0.5B) → 93% (32B) → 85% (72B). At 32B the aligned model keeps 74% even at i=8 versus 13% for its base sibling (+61 pts), so the alignment gap widens with scale rather than saturating.
- **The Llama exception is bounded by scale, not absent:** Llama-3.1-70B marginal GSM8K retention at i=1 is 36% base → 75% instruct, and 9% → 22% at i=8, recovering the gain missing at 8B. Raising the 8B model's budget from 512 to 1024 tokens does *not* recover it (22% vs. 24% at i=1), so the 8B failure is non-termination, not truncation. These 70B figures are single-run at n=100.
- **HumanEval negative control:** every evaluated model drops to 0% Pass@1 under word-initial Taboo, with exactly one non-zero cell (1/102 for Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct at i=2) — masking `def`/`return`/`for` leaves no valid alternative, versus baselines that solve a substantial fraction unperturbed.
- **Mid-word ablation (GSM8K, n=100, matched i=2):** masking non-start subwords while leaving word starts free collapses absolute accuracy to 0.01–0.05 in all four families; Gemma-3-12B-it falls to 0.01 mid-word versus 0.81 under word-initial masking at a *higher* average intervention. This is the paper's strongest internal-validity result: the drop is semantic stress, not tokenization corruption.
- **MMLU is an artifact, and the paper says so:** absolute accuracy collapses toward the 4-way guessing floor at every size including 72B, with non-monotone dose response (e.g. Qwen instruct retention 60% at i=1 but 19% at i=2 then back to 44% at i=8) — forced re-selection can *raise* retention at large i. TriviaQA degrades gracefully with small base–instruct gaps (Llama 53% base vs. 45% instruct at i=1, i.e. the gap even inverts).
- **Controls.** Precision: median |acc_4bit − acc_bf16| = 0.03 over 50 (model, dose) cells with 47/50 ≤ 0.07; the one outlier (Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct at i=2, 0.62 bf16 vs. 0.44 4-bit) moves to 0.58 in 8-bit, matching bf16. Sampling: at τ=0.7 the base→instruct gap persists (Gemma-3-12B 41%→85%, Qwen2.5-32B 53%→91%, Qwen2.5-72B 38%→86%, Llama-3.1-8B instruct ≤ base), seed-to-seed SD ≤ 0.04; at τ=1.0 Qwen2.5-7B 22%→58%. Extraction: agreement with a Gemini 3.6 Flash judge on a 200-item subsample is 97% on GSM8K (100% on taboo-perturbed outputs) and 79% on MMLU. Task-constraint check on IFEval `forbidden_words`: aggregate compliance net change +0.0 to +0.2 (n=25), so drops are not the intervention mechanically violating task constraints.
- **Token overhead:** answer-length ratio vs. own baseline reaches 1.6× for Qwen-7B-Instruct and 2.4–2.9× for OLMo-2 and Gemma-3 by i=8; ratios ≤1 for Gemma/Llama base and Llama instruct reflect models already saturating the 512-token budget. Compute: a few GPU-days total on one A100/H100 (~4 min per 7B condition at n=100, ~16 min for 70–72B).

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty (moderate).** The logit-mask-at-word-boundary trick is simple and, as far as the framing goes, the "zero-prompt" positioning against IFEval/FollowBench is fair. But the paper cites at least three concurrent 2026 preprints that are extremely close in spirit — Candussio et al., *"Don't say it!": Constraints, compliance and communication when language models play taboo*; Ayoobi et al., *Say anything but this: when tokenizer betrays reasoning in LLMs*; and Jehu-Appiah, *Trivial vocabulary bans improve LLM reasoning more than deep linguistic constraints* — and runs **no empirical comparison against any of them**. The last one in particular reports that vocabulary bans can *improve* reasoning, which cuts against this paper's framing; it is cited once in passing and never engaged. Contribution is therefore best read as "a cleaner, dose-graded instrumentation of an idea already in the air."

**Weak/absent baselines.** There is no competing method to compare against because this is a diagnostic, but that also means every number is a within-paper base-vs-instruct contrast. The only external reference point (IFEval `forbidden_words`) is used at n=25 and explicitly labelled "indicative." A head-to-head of prompt-level bans vs. logit-level bans on the same items would have been the natural, cheap experiment and is missing.

**Effective single-benchmark support.** The paper advertises four benchmarks, but by its own analysis HumanEval is a negative control that pins to 0%, MMLU is "a formatting artifact rather than genuine reasoning resilience," and TriviaQA shows small and sometimes inverted base–instruct gaps. The entire headline claim rests on GSM8K. Presenting the grid as "four benchmarks" overstates the evidential breadth even though the text is candid about each column.

**A specific data quirk the paper flags but does not chase.** The word-boundary predicate W is admitted to "miss the first generated token, punctuation-attached words, and numerals." On GSM8K the load-bearing tokens *are* numerals and equation fragments — meaning the intervention may systematically spare exactly the arithmetic tokens whose corruption would matter most, while stressing only the surrounding prose. The scaffolding-vs-computation split is never measured (e.g. rate of interventions falling on numeric vs. non-numeric words), so it is unclear how much of the "reasoning collapse" is arithmetic failure versus verbal derailment. Appendix A's qualitative modes (derailment, repetition loops) suggest the latter dominates, which is interesting but is a *termination/coherence* failure more than a *logic* failure — a weaker claim than "the reasoning engine unravels."

**Confound between retention and generation budget.** Instruct models expand to 1.6–2.9× their baseline length under Taboo while the budget stays fixed at 512 tokens. More tokens spent circumlocuting is both the mechanism *and* a resource that base models (which derail or already saturate) never get to use. The authors test this only for Llama-3.1-8B at 1024 tokens; a budget sweep for the models that *do* show the effect (Gemma, Qwen) would have been the more informative control.

**Statistical rigor.** The authors themselves note no multiple-comparison correction across the family × size × benchmark × dose grid, and the family-level conclusion rests on n=4 families. The 70B Llama result — the one that rescues the paper's "the exception is bounded by scale" narrative — is a single run at n=100 with only two points on the ladder, so the transition point is unlocatable, as they concede. Conditional retention R conditions on |Q_correct|, which varies by an order of magnitude across the ladder; at 0.5B the denominator is tiny, making the 6% figures very noisy.

**Unexplained mechanism.** The surprisal story (aligned = sharper distribution = absorbs more bits yet retains more) is appealing, and Figure 7 shows the instruct curves sitting up-and-to-the-right in dose space. But the authors admit the dose axes "overlap only partially," making it "indicative rather than a strict dose-match." And Llama-3.1-70B breaks the story outright: flat surprisal signature, large retention gain. The proposed next probe (next-token entropy) is not run.

**Reproducibility (good).** Zenodo code, fixed seeds, named subsample sizes, deterministic scorers, explicit hardware and wall-clock costs, and a stated total budget of a few GPU-days on one accelerator. This is comfortably reproducible by an academic lab — a genuine strength relative to most robustness papers.

**Overclaiming to watch.** Section 5's application list (safety auditing, synthetic CoT generation, structured-output stress testing, taboo-guided GRPO alignment) is entirely speculative — no experiment, not even a pilot, supports any of the four. The abstract's phrasing ("Taboo provides a novel primitive for...") reads as demonstrated capability but is a roadmap.

## Strengths

- The mid-word vs. word-initial ablation is exactly the right control and produces an unusually decisive contrast (0.01 vs. 0.81 for the same model), turning a design choice into an empirical claim.
- The controls are unusually thorough for a short paper: quantization spot-check with an 8-bit tiebreak on the single anomalous cell, temperature sampling at two settings with three seeds, an LLM-judge audit of deterministic extraction, and an IFEval collision check. Each addresses a specific objection a reviewer would raise.
- Intellectual honesty: MMLU is included *and labelled* an artifact, the Llama exception is reported prominently instead of buried, the non-monotonic 72B-base dip is not smoothed over, and the Limitations section names the missing multiple-comparison correction itself.
- Conditional retention R is the right metric for the question, isolating off-path resilience from nominal capability.
- Injected surprisal makes the intervention comparable across tokenizers and architectures, which is what allows the base-vs-instruct claim to survive the obvious "the aligned model was just perturbed less" objection.
- Cheap and immediately usable: a `LogitsProcessor` drop-in with negligible per-step overhead, no retraining, no prompt engineering.

## Limitations

- English-only; word boundaries, subword mechanics, and morphology differ sharply in morphologically rich or non-space-delimited languages, so the word-initial rule may not transfer.
- Only dense open-weight autoregressive models; no MoE, no proprietary APIs (which cannot expose logits anyway), no multimodal.
- A single masking rule (uniform top-i at every word start). Sparse, adaptive, surprisal-thresholded, or non-top-i masking is unexplored, so the reported dose-response describes one point in a large design space.
- Large models run in 4-bit nf4 (chosen for decode speed, not memory), with 8-bit used only as a spot check on one outlier cell.
- Four families, one exception, no multiple-comparison correction over the family × size × benchmark × dose grid; the family-level conclusion is effectively an n=4 observation.
- Main results are greedy-decoding; the temperature ablation covers only GSM8K and a subset of cells, and top-p/top-k sweeps are future work.
- The 512-token generation cap interacts with the 1.6–2.9× length expansion the intervention induces, and is only probed at 1024 tokens for one model.

## Relevance

如果你（用户）关注 LLM 评测方法论、后训练对齐到底带来了什么、以及"benchmark 分数 vs. 真实部署表现"的落差，这篇给出了一个便宜、可复现、直接在 logit 空间施压的诊断原语，且把"干预强度"做成了可比较的连续剂量（bits），这个设计本身值得借鉴到你自己的鲁棒性/护栏评测里。另一方面，它对 LLM-as-a-judge、约束解码、以及 RL rollout 多样性（把 Taboo 塞进 GRPO）都留了明确接口，但这些都还只是设想而非实验，可作为跟进方向而非现成结论。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- What fraction of Taboo interventions land on numerals or equation tokens versus prose scaffolding on GSM8K? If the predicate systematically spares arithmetic tokens, is the measured "reasoning collapse" really a termination/coherence failure rather than a logic failure?
- Does next-token entropy (the direct sharpness measure the authors suggest) explain the Qwen/Gemma/OLMo alignment gain, and does it confirm that Llama-3.1-70B's gain is mechanistically distinct?
- Where between 8B and 70B does Llama-3 acquire taboo-robustness? Llama-3.1-13B/34B-class checkpoints or the Llama-3.2/3.3 line would locate the transition.
- Under a matched generation budget scaled to each model's own expansion factor (e.g. 1024–1536 tokens), does the base-vs-instruct gap on Gemma-3 and Qwen2.5 survive, or does part of it come from instruct models simply having room to circumlocute?
- How does prompt-level forbidden-word banning compare head-to-head with logit-level banning on identical items — does the confound the paper argues for actually change the ranking of models?
- Does the taboo-guided GRPO idea work in practice, i.e. does rewarding successful off-path rollouts improve robustness without degrading nominal accuracy?
- Does an SFT-only checkpoint already confer taboo-robustness, or does it require DPO/RLHF? Isolating which post-training stage produces the effect is the obvious next experiment the diagnostic enables.
- On the safety-auditing application: for models with known jailbreak surfaces, does masking refusal-prefix tokens actually separate shallow template refusals from deep alignment, and does that correlate with red-team attack success rates?
