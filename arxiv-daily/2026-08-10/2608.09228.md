> **一句话总结：** 本文提出 OP²SD，把 OPSD 中教师看到的"配对参考答案"替换为另一道数学题的解答，结果在 Qwen3-1.7B/4B/8B 上仍保持甚至超过 OPSD 的增益，证明 OPSD 的收益并非来自特权答案本身，而主要来自数学解题上下文诱导出的教师 token 级行为。

> **TL;DR:** The authors dissect On-Policy Self-Distillation (OPSD) by introducing OP²SD, which swaps the teacher's paired reference solution for a worked solution to a *different* problem. On Qwen3-1.7B/4B/8B across AIME 2024, AIME 2025, and HMMT 2025, OP²SD matches or beats OPSD — on Qwen3-8B AIME 2024 it reaches 55.83 Avg@12 vs OPSD's 45.00 and Base's 28.47 — showing OPSD's gains stem mostly from context-induced teacher behavior, not privileged-answer transfer.

## 核心贡献

- **揭示 OPSD 的解释混淆：** 指出在教师提示中加入"配对参考答案"同时改变了两个因素——揭示了当前问题的特权答案，以及改变了教师提供 token 级监督时的上下文，而标准对比无法区分二者。
- **提出受控干预方法 OP²SD：** 保持学生 on-policy rollout、冻结的自教师、token 级蒸馏目标完全不变，仅把教师上下文里的"目标题 A 的解答"换成"另一道题 B 及其解答"，并显式声明该示例不是 A 的提示。
- **实证证明配对答案非必需：** 在三个 Qwen3 模型 × 三个数学基准上，OP²SD 持续超过 base 模型且与 OPSD 竞争力相当（甚至更优），九个模型-基准组合中 OP²SD 在八个上取得最高 Avg@12。
- **系统消融上下文的哪些性质起作用：** 证明示例多样性、粗粒度领域匹配、甚至解的正确性都不是必需的（单一固定的代数题、局部出错的解答都有效）；但任意外部文本不充分——琐碎的 "1+1" 示例和跨学科的物理题上下文都失败。
- **结论是诊断性的（非新方法）：** 将 OPSD 增益的解释从"特权答案转移"转移到"数学解题上下文诱导的教师行为"。

## Key Contributions

- **Identifies a confounding factor in the standard OPSD explanation.** Adding the verified reference to the teacher prompt does two things at once: it reveals target-specific privileged information (the answer/derivation to the current problem) and it changes the teacher's context (reasoning structure, notation, style, continuation/termination effects). Standard Base-vs-OPSD comparisons cannot separate these.
- **Introduces OP²SD (On-Policy Self-Distillation from Other Problems).** For target A, the teacher is given a worked solution to a *different* problem B ≠ A (with an explicit instruction that it is not a hint), while the student prompt, on-policy rollout, frozen self-teacher, and clipped Forward-KL objective are held identical to OPSD. This breaks the target–reference pairing while preserving a teacher-only worked-solution context.
- **Shows paired target solutions are not necessary.** Across Qwen3-1.7B, Qwen3-4B, and Qwen3-8B on AIME 2024, AIME 2025, and HMMT Feb 2025, OP²SD improves over Base and stays competitive with (frequently surpasses) OPSD.
- **Isolates which properties of the teacher context matter.** Through a suite of controls (fixed single example, corrupted solution, verbose solution, trivial "1+1", Geometry-for-Algebra, Physics-for-Math), the authors show diversity, coarse-domain match, and even exact correctness are not required, while a substantive mathematical worked solution is.
- **Reframes the mechanism.** The major source of OPSD's gain is argued to be context-induced change in the teacher's token-level behavior, not direct privileged-answer transfer.

## Methodology

The work is built on On-Policy Self-Distillation (OPSD), where a trainable student and a frozen teacher are initialized from the same base model. The student conditions only on the target problem xᵢ and samples a full response ŷᵢ ~ pθ(·|xᵢ). The OPSD teacher additionally conditions on the paired reference solution yᵢ* and scores the student's prefixes, yielding a token-level Forward-KL loss.

The key intervention, OP²SD, changes only the teacher's conditioning context. Instead of (xᵢ, yᵢ*), the teacher sees (xᵢ, xⱼ, yⱼ*) for some j ≠ i, where (xⱼ, yⱼ*) is a worked example drawn from the same training pool (OpenThoughts Math), with a prompt that explicitly labels it as unrelated to the target. The shared objective is:

L_m(θ) = E[ (1/|ŷ|) Σ_t D( p_T^m(·|xᵢ,cᵢ^m,ŷ_{<t}) ‖ p_S(·|xᵢ,ŷ_{<t}) ) ]

where cᵢ^target = yᵢ* and cᵢ^other = (xⱼ, yⱼ*), and D is a clipped Forward-KL surrogate with gradients flowing only through the student. Three model settings are used: Qwen3-1.7B (non-thinking student / thinking teacher, inheriting the original OPSD recipe), and Qwen3-4B and Qwen3-8B (both student and teacher non-thinking). Training uses LoRA (r=64, α=128) on the OpenThoughts Math 30k OPSD split on 4× H100 GPUs, with 12 generations per problem over 4 decoding seeds (48 pooled generations). Evaluation is on AIME 2024, AIME 2025, and HMMT Feb 2025 from MathArena, reported via Avg@12, Pass@12, and Vote@12. A controlled suite of teacher-context variants (Target-only, Answer-only, Fixed correct, Fixed locally corrupted, Fixed verbose correct, Fixed trivial 1+1, Geometry-for-Algebra, Physics-for-Math) isolates the contributing factors.

## Results & Highlights

- **OP²SD matches or surpasses OPSD.** Across the 9 model–benchmark groups, OP²SD achieves the highest Avg@12 point estimate in 8 of 9; the gap is largest at the 8B scale. On Qwen3-8B AIME 2024, OP²SD reaches **55.83 Avg@12 vs OPSD's 45.00 and Base's 28.47** (+10.83 over OPSD); on Qwen3-8B AIME 2025 it is **42.85 vs 32.15 (OPSD) and 20.97 (Base)** (+10.69); on Qwen3-8B HMMT 2025 it is **25.56 vs 16.18 (OPSD) and 11.81 (Base)** (+9.38).
- **Even the smallest model benefits without the paired answer.** On Qwen3-1.7B, OP²SD improves Avg@12 over Base on all three benchmarks (e.g., AIME 2024: 55.35 vs 49.86 Base; HMMT 2025: 27.57 vs 23.61), competitive with OPSD's 55.42 / 25.76.
- **Diversity is unnecessary.** Replacing the stream of varying examples with a single fixed Omni-MATH algebra problem and its concise correct solution (Fixed correct) yields Avg@12 of 35.83 / 31.94 / 17.71 on AIME 2024 / 2025 / HMMT 2025 with Qwen3-4B — slightly *higher* than varying-example OP²SD (31.53 / 30.62 / 16.11).
- **Correctness is not strictly required, but substance is.** A Fixed locally corrupted solution (same 204-token length, wrong final answer) performs comparably or better (36.04 / 34.86 / 17.99). However, a Fixed trivial "1+1" example collapses to 5.21 / 5.28 / 2.29, far below Base.
- **Cross-subject context breaks the gain.** Replacing the teacher's math worked examples with CAMEL Physics pairs drops Qwen3-4B Avg@12 to 18.68 / 20.21 / 8.68 — below Base (23.19 / 21.11 / 11.67) — accompanied by degraded answer formatting and repetitive non-terminating generations.
- **Verbose solutions hurt.** The Fixed verbose correct (742 tokens) lowers Avg@12 by 1.94 / 3.54 / 1.95 points relative to the concise version and shortens student responses by ~10.2%.
- **Mode asymmetry (1.7B) is a separate channel.** In the thinking-teacher / non-thinking-student 1.7B setup, even Target-only and Answer-only beat Base; but in the mode-matched Qwen3-4B setting, Target-only degrades to 1.18 / 0.49 / 0.76 (far below Base) and Answer-only stays 4.51 / 3.26 / 2.43 points below Base — confirming the gain is tied to the worked-solution context, not to thinking mode or generic on-policy distillation.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** The conceptual reframing — separating "target-specific privilege" from "context-induced teacher behavior" — is genuinely insightful and underexplored in the OPSD/privileged-information literature. The OP²SD intervention is a clean, minimal ablation (one prompt substitution, everything else fixed), which is the right experimental design for a mechanistic claim.
- **Rigor:** The control suite is commendable: Target-only, Answer-only, fixed/varying examples, correct/corrupted/verbose/trivial solutions, within-math (Geometry-for-Algebra) and cross-subject (Physics-for-Math) substitutions. Evaluation uses uncontaminated MathArena benchmarks with corrected Monte Carlo standard errors and 4 decoding seeds.
- **A major confound is correctly handled.** The authors notice the 1.7B setup's thinking/non-thinking asymmetry could explain the gain on its own, and they re-run controls on the mode-matched Qwen3-4B to rule it out — a sign of careful experimental hygiene.
- **Weak statistical power.** Each trained condition is a *single* training run; the 4 seeds only measure decoding stochasticity for that one run, not training-run variance. The paper acknowledges this explicitly (Section 6), and it is most consequential for the subtle Fixed/Corrupted/Verbose comparisons where point estimates differ by only 1–4 points within overlapping error bars — some of those "comparable or better" claims are likely not statistically significant.
- **Scope is narrow.** Only math reasoning, only Qwen3 family, only non-thinking (mostly) student/teacher. Whether the context-induced-behavior explanation generalizes to coding, agentic, or thinking-model distillation is untested.
- **Causal mechanism not pinned down.** The paper demonstrates the privileged answer is not necessary but does not isolate *why* a mathematical worked solution helps (format priming? style? termination behavior?). It honestly states "these experiments do not identify a unique mechanism."
- **Potential concern with the physics control:** the Physics condition differs from Math in multiple ways (subject, formatting conventions, dataset source), so the conclusion that "mathematical subject matter is causal" is weaker than implied; the authors flag this.

## Strengths

- Clean, minimal, and well-motivated experimental design that isolates one variable at a time.
- Strong coverage of controls that progressively relax assumptions (pairing, diversity, domain match, correctness, triviality, cross-subject).
- Honest about limitations (single training run, narrow scope, unknown mechanism, unexplained longer-training degradation).
- Reproducible: exact training configs, prompt templates (Appendices), and released code.
- The finding that a single *corrupted* fixed math solution works as well as the correct one is striking and practically useful — it suggests OPSD-family methods may not need verified solutions at all, lowering data-construction cost.

## Limitations

- One training run per condition; reported uncertainty reflects decoding variance only, not training-run variance — several close comparisons are statistically underpowered.
- Restricted to mathematical reasoning on the Qwen3 family; generalization to other domains (code, agents, multimodal) and model families is untested.
- Does not establish the underlying causal mechanism (why does a math worked solution help?), only that privileged-answer transfer is not the whole story.
- The 1.7B thinking-teacher results entangle mode asymmetry with solution context and required a separate 4B re-run to disentangle.
- Observes but does not explain the deterioration under longer OP²SD/OPSD training.
- The cross-subject (physics) control confounds subject matter with formatting and dataset provenance.

## Relevance

This paper is highly relevant to research on reasoning LLM training, knowledge distillation, and the mechanistic understanding of post-training techniques like OPSD/GRPO. It directly challenges the prevailing "privileged information" narrative for on-policy self-distillation and offers a cheaper data recipe insight (verified target solutions may be unnecessary), which matters for anyone building distillation pipelines for math/reasoning models.

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the context-induced-behavior explanation hold for non-math domains (code generation, tool-use/agentic tasks, multimodal reasoning), and does a domain-matched worked solution remain necessary there?
- What specific property of the teacher's next-token distribution changes under a worked-solution context — is it answer-format priming, chain-of-thought style, termination behavior, or something token-level and measurable?
- Would repeating the Fixed/Corrupted/Verbose comparisons across multiple training runs confirm that corrupted and verbose solutions are genuinely as effective as the concise correct one, given the current single-run uncertainty?
- Can the mechanism be isolated by directly measuring the divergence between target-solution-teacher and other-problem-teacher distributions on student prefixes?
- How does OP²SD interact with thinking-mode (reasoning) student/teacher pairs, where Kaur et al. (2026) report privileged-context degradation at long budgets?
- Is the longer-training degradation shared across all OPSD-family methods, and can it be mitigated by adapting ideas from Purified OPSD or ROSD to the context-induced channel?
