Why this matters

Challenges the "privileged information" story behind on-policy self-distillation: verified target solutions may be unnecessary — pointing to cheaper distillation data recipes.

Critical edge

Every condition is a single training run. The four seeds measure decoding noise, not training variance — leaving many close "comparable or better" claims statistically underpowered.

01

Methodology — a one-line surgical edit

OP²SD is a minimal ablation of OPSD: the student prompt, on-policy rollout, frozen self-teacher, and clipped token-level Forward-KL loss are held identical (LoRA r=64 / α=128, OpenThoughts Math 30k, 4×H100, 12 generations × 4 seeds). The only change is the teacher's conditioning context.

L_m(θ) = E[ (1/|ŷ|) · Σ_t D( p_T(·|xᵢ, cᵢ, ŷ<t) ‖ p_S(·|xᵢ, ŷ<t) ) ]

Where OPSD sets cᵢ = yᵢ* (the target's paired reference), OP²SD sets cᵢ = (xⱼ, yⱼ*) — a worked solution to a different problem j≠i, explicitly labeled "not a hint." This severs the target–reference pairing while preserving a teacher-only math context. Tested on Qwen3-1.7B (thinking teacher / non-thinking student) and the mode-matched Qwen3-4B / 8B, on uncontaminated MathArena benchmarks (AIME 2024/2025, HMMT Feb 2025).

Diversity, coarse-domain match, and even correctness are unnecessary — a single wrong math solution works. But substance is required: trivial arithmetic and cross-subject (physics) context both collapse below Base.

+ Strengths

  • Clean, minimal design: isolates one variable, everything else fixed — ideal for a mechanistic claim.
  • Control suite progressively relaxes assumptions: pairing → diversity → domain → correctness → triviality → cross-subject.
  • Honest about limits: flags single-run variance, narrow scope, and unknown mechanism.
  • Fully reproducible: exact configs, prompt templates (appendices), released code.
  • Striking practical takeaway — corrupted solutions work as well as correct ones, lowering data-construction cost.

− Limitations

  • One training run per condition; uncertainty is decoding-only — several close calls are underpowered.
  • Math reasoning on the Qwen3 family only; code, agents, multimodal untested.
  • Shows privileged answers aren't necessary, but never pins down why a math solution helps.
  • 1.7B thinking-teacher setup entangles mode asymmetry; needed a separate 4B re-run to disentangle.
  • Observes but does not explain longer-training degradation under OP²SD/OPSD.
03

Open questions for further work

  1. Q1Does the context-induced-behavior explanation hold for non-math domains — code, agentic tool-use, multimodal reasoning?
  2. Q2Which property of the teacher's next-token distribution actually shifts — format priming, CoT style, or termination behavior?
  3. Q3Would multi-run repeats confirm corrupted & verbose solutions truly match the concise correct one?
  4. Q4Can the mechanism be isolated by directly measuring target-teacher vs other-teacher divergence on student prefixes?
  5. Q5How does OP²SD behave with thinking-mode student/teacher pairs at long budgets (cf. Kaur et al. 2026)?
  6. Q6Is the longer-training degradation shared across OPSD-family methods — and can Purified OPSD / ROSD mitigate it?
