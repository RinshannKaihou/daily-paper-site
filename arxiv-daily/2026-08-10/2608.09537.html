The defensible headline

Why this matters

The missing safeguard for self-improving research agents: a transferable evidence-admission protocol — plus a planted-fault audit (30 traps, 10 real fixes) you can run on your own loops.

Critical edge

The "68% search / 69% GPU" efficiency claim is baselined against a memory-less strawman. Against honest unconditional reuse it shrinks to 4.1→3.6 trials — bare point estimates over 9 runs, no error bars.

01

Methodology

distilled · ~150 words
Characterize→ Retrieve→ Validate→ Accumulate

VERDI runs a continual loop over a campaign C = (model, data, goal, frozen verifier, budget). The guiding contract: retrieval only reorders experiments — it never writes a claim.

Characterize. Reversible, seed-paired, dose-calibrated inference-time probes (action scaling, context retention, first-frame anchoring, sampling noise) measure a paired response Jacobian J, its bootstrap covariance Σ, and induced metric G = JᵀWJ. The fingerprint φ = (J, G, Σ) is cheap (8–11 GPU-h) and comparable across heterogeneous backbones.

Retrieve. An Interventional Repair Geometry (IRG) ranks source repairs as hypotheses, never facts, via an uncertainty-weighted, archive-frozen distance with a support-mismatch penalty.

Validate. Six compatibility certificates can only abstain; a frozen target-side verifier plus multi-seed, held-out confirmation is the sole path to a memory record. Explicit H/I/E (hypothesis / implementation / evaluation) separation prevents the loop that proposes from also settling.

Accumulate. Effect vectors, nulls, harmfuls, and skill interactions enter WMOM; IRG collisions open regret-gated probe-evolution work orders held to held-out replication.

03

Strengths & Limitations

Strengths

  • The principle is stated crisply and the architecture actually enforces it: retrieval reorders experiments, never writes a claim.
  • Effect vectors carry explicit protected-coordinate regressions (e.g. motion smoothness ↑, dynamic degree ↓), stored as boundary annotations that constrain future retrieval.
  • Null and harmful outcomes are first-class memory entries; skill interactions are measured, not inferred from marginals.
  • Rare planted-fault audit (30 traps, 5 failure modes) with a false-conclusion metric — plus a human-expert reference arm.
  • Real downstream validation: +12 to +24 pt closed-loop success in sim, +15/+20 pts on a physical Franka.

Limitations

  • Guarantees are conditional on a valid evaluator; a bad verifier makes the whole certificate chain vacuous, with no test for verifier validity.
  • Fingerprints are local and must be recomputed after "major" architectural changes — detection of "major" is unspecified.
  • Probe evolution is fail-closed in the current replay; §4.4 cannot yet claim end-to-end repair-accuracy gains.
  • Training-loss & data-reweighting interventions are excluded by construction — objective-manifesting failures lie outside the geometry.
  • Overhead is nontrivial: +6.2 GPU-h per audited episode; 96–173 GPU-h per campaign.
04

Open Questions

for further exploration
  1. Does the 3.6 vs 4.1 trial gap survive real confidence intervals beyond 3 backbones × 3 seeds — or is negative-transfer reduction the only statistically defensible result?
  2. How is the certificate's δ_G calibrated when the archive is small, and does the system abstain on everything early in life?
  3. What fraction of retrievals actually abstain, and does abstention spike for genuinely novel architectures — useless exactly when most needed?
  4. Can the outcome schema be decoupled from WorldArena to test whether the fingerprint measures optimization behavior or WorldArena-specific behavior?
  5. Would the paper's own validity gates reject the four sub-0.2 dB ACWM-Phys environments under a stricter τ?
  6. How often are IRG collisions unresolvable due to support mismatch rather than probe-basis mismatch?
