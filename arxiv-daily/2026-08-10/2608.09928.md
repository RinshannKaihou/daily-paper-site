> **一句话总结：** MMDiff 通过把基座 LM 的 SAE 字典"热启动"迁移到多模态模型上，用解码器方向的旋转 + 视觉能量筛出被多模态训练改造过的特征，再用逐 token 对比激活分析定位任务特征——在 PaliGemma 2 上单个特征消融即可让 VSR 空间准确率掉 6–31%（三模型均值 −10.1/−12.3/−14.6%）、OCRBench 类别子集掉 16.9%、VLSBench 攻击成功率降 17–28%，而 VQAv2 波动 ≤1.5%；配套的 MMDiff-CAA 转向比单层 CAA 在空间任务上 +3.6%（+8.96 → +12.59），但其中 +1.82 只来自"改成多层 CAA"，SAE 特征方向本身仅贡献 +1.81。

> **TL;DR:** MMDiff diffs a base-LM sparse autoencoder against its multimodal-adapted counterpart to isolate the ~5–20% of dictionary features that both rotate geometrically and become image-responsive, then narrows them per task via per-token contrastive firing (Fisher exact, OR ≥ 3, Δp ≥ 0.05) plus a lexical-invariance filter. Ablating one discovered feature drops VSR spatial accuracy by 6–31% (means −10.1 / −12.3 / −14.6 on LLaVA-MORE / PaliGemma 2 / InternVL3.5-2B) vs −0.46 for randomly chosen features in the same layers, cuts VLSBench ASR by 17–28% per category, and costs ≤1.5 pts on VQAv2. MMDiff-CAA steering beats vanilla single-layer CAA on VSR (+12.59 vs +8.96 mean, peak +30.77 on "ahead of") and on OCRBench (+4.02 vs +2.21), though the decomposition shows multi-layer CAA alone already gets +10.78 — the SAE direction adds only the last +1.81. Worth reading if you care about SAE-based control of VLMs; skim if you only want interpretability visualizations.

## 核心贡献

- **多模态模型 diffing**：从与骨干匹配的 SAE 套件（LLaMA-Scope TopK / Gemma-Scope JumpReLU / Qwen-Scope TopK）热启动，在冻结 MLLM 的激活上微调出多模态 SAE；用同索引解码器方向的余弦相似度 $c_f$ 衡量"旋转"，用视觉输入下的均方激活 $E_v$ 衡量"视觉响应"，两者交集（$E_v > \epsilon$ 且 $c_f$ 位于最低 25%）定义"适配特征集" A，占字典的 ~5%（Llama）/ ~20%（Gemma）/ ~13%（Qwen）。
- **任务特征发现管线**：在 A 内做目标分布 vs. VQAv2 基线的逐 token 放电频率对比（Fisher 精确检验 + BH 校正，OR ≥ 3，Δp ≥ 0.05），再用"中性提示词库"过滤词汇伪影（空间任务约 60% 候选通过），得到 711（Llama-空间）/ ~1400（Gemma-空间）/ 1061（安全）/ 1070（OCR）个特征。
- **特征级因果控制**：三点全层投影消融（attention 输出、MLP 输出、残差输出，且只在文本 token 位置）实现"移除"；MMDiff-CAA 把多层骨干 CAA 方向与特征自身解码器方向在其所属层的注入结合起来实现"增强"。
- **训练配方的经验结论**：text-only 掩码训练的 SAE 重构误差最低（FVU 均值 0.005，vs full 0.032 / image 0.037 / random-init 0.050），且与基座字典对齐最好，因此被选为 diffing 用字典。
- **辅助分析**：attribution patching 定位驱动特征的注意力头（如 L13H1 在"on top of"类查询上反复出现），GPT-4o-mini 自动解释给出特征标签与 F1 置信度（加注意力热图叠加约 +0.2 F1）。

## Key Contributions

- A stage-wise model-diffing recipe for the LM→MLLM transition: warm-start the base-LM SAE, fine-tune on MLLM activations, and use same-index decoder cosine as the change signal. Index correspondence is explicitly validated — over the full PaliGemma 2 dictionary (26 layers × 16,384), 96.41% of features are "preserved", 3.36% "rotated in place", **0.00% relocated**, 0.23% emergent.
- A task-specific discovery funnel that is behavior-agnostic: only $D_{tgt}$ and the neutral prompt bank change between spatial reasoning, multimodal safety, and OCR.
- Two intervention primitives built on the same discovered directions: orthogonal-projection causal removal and MMDiff-CAA steering, with a full component decomposition of where the steering gain comes from.
- An ablation showing that diffing is load-bearing: a from-scratch SAE trained directly on MLLM activations yields top-10 "spatial" features that all fire on 100% of VSR samples (odds ratio saturates) and whose ablation moves VSR by +0.22 on average, vs −10.11 for MMDiff features on the same model.
- Cross-seed reproducibility evidence: retraining PaliGemma 2 SAEs on 8 layers with a different seed gives mean same-index decoder cosine 0.93 (84% ≥ 0.9), relocation 0.44%; the reported top-10 features average 0.98.

## Methodology

SAEs are attached to residual-stream outputs of each transformer block and trained on cached activations from 50k VQAv2 image–question pairs (Adam, lr 7e-5, 1k-step warmup, 8× A100 80GB, one job per layer). Three masking regimes are trained (full-sequence, image-only, text-only) plus a random-init control. Text-only wins on FVU and on alignment with the base dictionary — the paper's argument is that multimodal capability *emerges in the language backbone* when text tokens attend to visual context, so text-token activations retain the LM basis while still reflecting multimodal change; reconstructing projected visual tokens instead forces large early-layer rotations from the projector-induced distribution gap.

Adapted-feature selection uses decoder cosine (not encoder rows, since decoder columns index semantics) plus visual energy $E_v = \mathbb{E}_{vision}[h_f^2]$; nearly half of features have $E_v = 0$, so a simple cutoff suffices. Because $E_v$ alone cannot separate image-driven from text-driven firing, the authors run fixed-text image counterfactuals (300 samples/feature): blanking the image reduces mean activation by 8.3% (spatial), 21.8% (safety), 36.8% (OCR); shuffling is mildest; noise is intermediate.

For causal removal, the unit-norm decoder direction is projected out at *every* layer and at three insertion points (attention out, MLP out, residual), restricted to text-token positions so image tokens and the projector are untouched. Three deltas are reported: target-task accuracy, ΔVQA on VQAv2, and ΔCtrl on a domain-matched control (non-spatial VSR relations like *has/wears/holds*; the MSSBench-safe split; a VQA-clean non-OCR yes/no subset).

MMDiff-CAA is $h' \leftarrow h + \alpha d_\ell$ at all layers in a task-specific layer set $L_{task}$, with an extra $\gamma_f v_f$ ($\gamma_f \in \{1,3,10\}$) injected at the feature's home layer. For OCR, generation is reframed as 4-way multiple choice with steering applied at the decision token, and the CAA direction is built from (GT, distorted-GT) answer pairs.

## Results & Highlights

- **Spatial ablation (Table 1).** Ablating a single MMDiff feature drops VSR accuracy by 6–31%, with per-model means of **−10.1 (MMDiff-Llama), −12.3 (MMDiff-Gemma), −14.6 (MMDiff-Qwen)**, while |ΔVQA| ≤ 1.5% and ΔCtrl is near zero. Strongest single feature: PaliGemma 2 L9/F387 ("right side of") at **−30.62 VSR with +0.30 VQA and 0.00 control**. The matched baseline — one randomly chosen feature per layer from the same 8 layers, identical protocol, OR = 1.0 by construction — moves VSR by only **−0.46 mean** (Table 13).
- **Diffing is necessary (Table 7).** Contrastive firing alone gives a bigger VSR drop (−15.1) but destroys the model: **ΔVQA = −25.9**. Adding visual responsiveness: −15.9 / −26.3. Adding only the lexical filter: −14.6 / −24.4. The full MMDiff pipeline is the only selective one at **−12.3 VSR / −0.1 VQA**. A from-scratch SAE on MLLM activations yields **+0.22 mean ΔVSR vs −10.11 for MMDiff** on LLaVA-MORE (Table 12).
- **Multimodal safety (Table 4).** On PaliGemma 2, the per-category top feature reduces VLSBench ASR by **17.96% (Illegal Activity) to 28.14% (Self-Harm)**, with |ΔVQA| ≤ 1% and ΔCtrl ≤ 1% on the MSSBench-safe split. Across the full sweep of 1,061 candidate safety features, the mean is **ΔASR = −9.67%, ΔVQA = −0.03%, ΔCtrl = +0.41%** — i.e., the headline "24%" reflects hand-picked top features, not the population mean.
- **OCR ablation (Table 5).** Mean ΔCat across five top features is **−16.9%** (best: L19/F10089 Scene Text at **−28.0**), with |ΔVQA| ≤ 1.6 and |ΔCtrl| ≤ 1.8.
- **Steering (Tables 3, 6, 14).** MMDiff-CAA beats vanilla single-layer CAA on VSR: **mean +12.59 vs +8.96 (Δ = +3.63)**, peak **+30.77 vs +15.38 on "ahead of"**; but it *loses* on L9/F387 (+8.28 vs +9.66) and both methods fail on L11/F9639 (−1.21 vs −1.82). On OCRBench, **+4.02 vs +2.21 mean** (peak +10.58 vs +2.88 on L17/F13602), with L19/F10089 slightly worse than baseline (+0.47 vs +0.63). The decomposition is the most informative number in the paper: SAE direction alone = +2.63 (one feature) / +3.66 (all ten), single-layer CAA = +8.96, **multi-layer CAA = +10.78**, MMDiff-CAA = +12.59. So of the +3.63 headline gain, **+1.82 comes from layer selection and only +1.81 from the discovered SAE direction**.
- **Cross-stage ablation (Table 2).** Ablating the same MMDiff-Gemma features on PaliGemma 2 pt-448 (pretrained, no instruction tuning) vs mix-448 amplifies effects roughly **3×** after instruction tuning (e.g., L9/F387: −2.08 → −30.62), and two features (L13/F15219, L12/F2257) flip sign from positive noise to clear negatives — evidence that these spatial behaviors are *acquired* during multimodal fine-tuning rather than inherited.
- **Reconstruction (Fig. 6).** Text-only FVU mean **0.005** (max 0.037) vs full-sequence 0.032, image-only 0.037, random-init 0.050 — but text-only sees only 3.2M tokens vs ~31.6M for the others.

## Authors & Credibility

Six of seven authors are at University of Oxford (Torr's group / PIXL lab), with Lachin Naghashyar at Microsoft; Philip Torr and Christian Schroeder de Witt are established names in vision and AI safety, and Constantin Venhoff has prior work on VLM internals. Compute was supported by Cosmos Institute, Modal Academics credits, and AMD's University Program. The paper is a preprint with a project page (pixl.cs.ox.ac.uk/mmdiff); no code or SAE checkpoint release is stated in the text, though all base SAE suites used (LLaMA-Scope, Gemma-Scope, Qwen-Scope) and all benchmarks (VQAv2, VSR, VLSBench, MSSBench, OCRBench) are public. A telltale phrase in App. G.1 — "including the absolute-value choice raised in the reviews" — indicates this version has already been through at least one review cycle.

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty (high-moderate).** Model diffing, SAEs on VLMs, and CAA all exist; the specific composition — warm-started base-LM dictionary, decoder-rotation + visual-energy intersection, contrastive firing, and *using the diff as a discovery signal for control* — appears genuinely new, and the related-work section's claim that no prior work diffs base-LM against MLLM-adapted SAEs seems fair.

**Rigor (mixed, with one serious concern).** The controls are unusually good for an interpretability paper: a random-feature baseline with OR = 1.0 in the same layers, a from-scratch-SAE dictionary control, cross-seed dictionary stability, explicit full-dictionary matching (0.00% relocation), image counterfactuals, and per-domain behavioral controls. But the headline spatial numbers are inflated by an evaluation-set construction problem the paper states plainly and never addresses: "each feature is scored on a VSR subset constructed from its top-activating samples." The evaluation set is chosen *by the feature being tested*, so a −30.62 ΔVSR is measured on precisely the examples where that feature is most active. The random-feature control does mitigate this partly (random features are also evaluated on "the same relation subsets"), but a proper control would ablate MMDiff features on a fixed, feature-independent VSR split.

Second concern: **Table 7 is non-monotone and unexplained.** Firing + visual energy + rotation (no lexical filter) gives −1.0 ΔVSR; adding the lexical filter — which can only *remove* candidates — jumps the effect to −12.3. Since the filter is a subset operation, the entire 12× swing comes from re-ranking of which features land in the reported top-k. The paper does not discuss this, and it makes the "each component is necessary" story less clean than presented.

Third: **the steering headline oversells.** "+3.6% over a standard single-layer steering baseline" is true, but Table 14 shows half of it (+1.82) is obtained by simply applying CAA at multiple layers — a change requiring no SAE, no diffing, and no feature discovery. The genuinely MMDiff-specific contribution to steering is +1.81 on 10 features, and the SAE directions alone are weak (+2.63/+3.66). The abstract's framing does not convey this.

**Coverage gaps.** Safety and OCR are evaluated on PaliGemma 2 *only* — the three-model story holds for spatial reasoning alone. Table 8 lists SAE configs for MMDiff-Llama and MMDiff-Gemma but omits MMDiff-Qwen. The MMDiff-Qwen discovered-feature count (~2,800) is an **extrapolation** from a 400-feature sample, flagged with a dagger. VLSBench ASR is judged by Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct with no reported human agreement study — for a safety claim, an unvalidated LLM judge is a soft foundation. The MSSBench-safe control has only 100 samples (76 embodied + 24 chat), so ΔCtrl ≤ 1% has coarse resolution (one sample = 1%). App. D.5 contains a leftover drafting artifact — "and (when results land) for the OCR target distribution" — suggesting some panels were still pending at submission.

**Red flags / overclaiming.** "Reduces attack success rate by 24%" is the mean of hand-picked per-category top features; the honest population number, disclosed in the body, is −9.67% across 1,061 candidates. "No impact on VQA performance" is defended, but only for the *selected* features; the paper also admits a minority of safety candidates cause generation collapse rather than refusal and need a post-hoc filter, with no reported prevalence. Ablating a feature that reduces harmful compliance is also not the same as *improving safety* — a model that has lost a capability is not a model that refuses, and no refusal-rate or over-refusal metric is reported.

**Reproducibility (moderate).** Hyperparameters, optimizer settings, thresholds ($\epsilon$, $p_{cos} = 25\%$, OR ≥ 3, Δp ≥ 0.05, η = 0.01, k = 5), verbatim prompt banks, and auto-interp prompts are all given, and every base SAE suite and benchmark is public. Absent code or SAE checkpoints, though, rerunning means re-training per-layer SAEs across 26–32 layers on 8× A100 — expensive to replicate independently.

**Significance (solid).** The strongest and most durable result is the negative one: a conventionally trained SAE on MLLM activations does *not* recover causally effective features (odds-ratio saturation, +0.22 mean ΔVSR), so the warm-start-plus-diff step buys something real. That plus the cross-stage pt-448 vs mix-448 comparison — showing spatial features are created by instruction tuning, not inherited — are contributions that survive the caveats above.

## Strengths

- Unusually complete control suite for an interpretability paper: random-feature ablation, from-scratch-dictionary ablation, cross-seed stability, full-dictionary index-correspondence verification, image counterfactuals, and per-domain behavioral controls.
- The steering decomposition (Table 14) is self-undercutting in a way that reads as intellectual honesty: the authors report that multi-layer CAA alone reaches +10.78 of their +12.59.
- The same pipeline instantiates three very different behaviors (spatial, safety, OCR) by varying only $D_{tgt}$ and a five-prompt neutral bank, which is a real generality claim rather than three bespoke methods.
- The lexical-invariance filter with a spatial prompt bank that deliberately avoids all spatial vocabulary is a well-designed guard against the most common failure mode of contrastive feature discovery.
- Cross-stage ablation on pt-448 vs mix-448 is an elegant, cheap causal experiment isolating what instruction tuning adds.
- Three backbone families with three different SAE objectives (TopK, JumpReLU, TopK) and three vision encoders (CLIP, SigLIP, InternViT) for the spatial results.

## Limitations

- Safety and OCR results exist for PaliGemma 2 only; the multi-family claim covers spatial reasoning alone, and all backbones are small-to-mid (1.7B–8B) dense models — no MoE, no Qwen-VL/Pixtral-style architectures, no frontier-scale check.
- Per-feature VSR evaluation subsets are built from each feature's own top-activating samples, biasing effect sizes upward.
- MMDiff-CAA requires an instruction-tuned reference model to extract backbone CAA directions; without it the method degrades to plain SAE-feature steering, which the paper's own Table 14 shows is weak (+2.63/+3.66).
- A minority of safety features cause generation collapse instead of refusal when ablated and are excluded by a post-hoc filter of unstated size and criteria.
- ASR is judged entirely by an LLM (Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct) with no human validation; MSSBench control n = 100.
- MMDiff-Qwen's discovered-feature count is extrapolated from a 400-feature sample, and its SAE configuration is missing from Table 8.
- Text-only SAE training uses ~10× fewer tokens (3.2M vs 31.6M), so the FVU comparison across regimes is not token-matched — the conclusion may partly reflect distributional proximity rather than a fair training comparison.
- Ablation removes capability; it is not demonstrated to produce appropriate refusal, and no over-refusal or helpfulness-on-benign-sensitive-prompts metric is reported.

## Relevance

如果你关注 SAE / 机制可解释性向"可操作控制"落地，这篇是当前少见的把 model diffing 当作**发现信号**而非事后描述工具的工作，其 warm-start + 旋转筛选的配方对任何"基座模型 → 领域微调模型"的差异分析（不限于多模态）都可直接迁移。对多模态安全方向，它给出了特征级消融降低 ASR 且不伤 VQA 的量化证据，但 LLM-judge 与 top-feature 挑选带来的乐观偏差需自行折价。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Do the spatial effect sizes survive evaluation on a fixed, feature-independent VSR split rather than each feature's own top-activating subset? What fraction of the −10 to −15 mean remains?
- Why does adding the lexical-invariance filter *increase* the ablation effect from −1.0 to −12.3 when it can only remove candidates? Is the full ranked list monotone, or is this a top-k artifact?
- Does ablation of safety features produce refusal or capability loss? What are over-refusal rates on benign-but-sensitive prompts, and how large is the excluded generation-collapse subset?
- Since multi-layer CAA alone recovers +10.78 of the +12.59, is layer selection the actual contribution? Would attribution patching (Sec. 6.2) suffice to pick $L_{task}$ without training any SAE?
- Does the diffing signal survive when the base LM has itself been RLHF'd, or when the vision encoder is trained jointly rather than bolted onto a frozen backbone?
- Do the discovered features transfer across MLLMs sharing a backbone (e.g., different LLaVA variants on LLaMA-3.1-8B), which would turn per-model discovery into a reusable feature catalog?
- Can multiple features be ablated jointly for stronger safety effects, and does the ΔVQA cost stay additive or blow up?
