> **一句话总结：** 作者在 Qwen2.5-1.5B 上用 7×7 的二维任务向量组合曲面测量「首 token 预测分布的非可加性比值 R」，发现 code+safety 相对匹配对照 code+math 在代码提示上高 +5.96 点、指令提示上高 +7.58 点，但在数学提示上仅 +0.10 点；该粗粒度序关系在 6 任务前瞻性预注册测试中 8/8 方向正确，并在 0.5B 全参数微调、7B LoRA、Llama-3.1-8B 上复现，但把同一批公开代码提示套上 Alpaca 指令模板后对比度从 +6.91 塌缩到 +0.34，EvalPlus pass@1 交互也无法稳健复现——结论是权重空间组合只支持「受输入与格式条件约束的粗粒度」功能陈述，不能当作通用的合并性能预测器。

> **TL;DR:** This paper measures *functional* (not parameter) non-additivity of task vectors by scanning a 7×7 grid θ₀+α∆ₐ+β∆_b (α,β∈[0,1.2]) and comparing the observed first-token distribution against a logit-space additive counterfactual via JSD, normalized by displacement from base. On Qwen2.5-1.5B with norm-matched rank-16 LoRA adapters, code+safety exceeds the matched code+math control by **+5.96 pts on code prompts** and **+7.58 pts on instruction prompts**, but only **+0.10 pts on math prompts** — the sign flips nothing, but the effect vanishes. In a prospectively specified 6-task expansion (12 families, 36 surfaces), **8/8** high-vs-low unseen-pair comparisons have the predicted sign (12/12 with the code+math anchor), though finer high-vs-middle ordering holds only **5/8**, and the exact design-preserving randomization test gives p=0.083 — the *minimum attainable* value for this design. The ordering survives 0.5B full-parameter FT (+4.37/+2.98), Qwen2.5-7B LoRA (+9.52/+10.56), and Llama-3.1-8B (+4.87/+5.30). The honest headline is the failure: wrapping the *identical* 60 public code prompts in an Alpaca template collapses the contrast from **+6.91 → +0.34**, and EvalPlus pass@1 factorial interaction is +4.14 [−2.44, 12.60] on HumanEval+ and −0.15 [−2.65, 2.38] on MBPP+. Read this if you care about whether merging diagnostics generalize; skip it if you want a better merging method — it proposes none.

## 核心贡献

- **区分参数几何与功能几何**：明确指出任务向量是参数空间的位移，而成败由行为判定，两者不必一致；论文不优化合并系数，而是把二维组合曲面当作诊断工具。
- **定义输入条件化的功能交互度量**：在 logit 空间构造「无交互反事实」ℓ_add = ℓ_{α,0} + ℓ_{0,β} − ℓ_{0,0}，先减去每条轴自身的边际非线性，再用 JSD 测量联合交互，并以相对基座的位移做归一化，得到比值 R；输入分布 X 是显式参数，绝不在检验前被聚合掉。
- **前瞻性（预注册式）方向检验**：在训练 summarization / translation 适配器之前冻结 high/middle/low/bridge 分箱与 75% 成功判据，事后 8/8 未见任务对方向正确。
- **多重压力审计**：范数匹配干预（排除「小向量更可加」的混杂）、0.5B 全参数微调（排除 rank-16 LoRA 伪影）、7B 规模、Llama-3.1-8B 跨架构、参数空间聚类诊断（cosine gap / silhouette / 1-NN / TIES 符号冲突率）。
- **主动划定失效边界**：格式桥接诊断（raw vs Alpaca-wrapped 同一批提示）与 EvalPlus pass@1 因子交互，都被当作实质性的效度边界而非「基准失败」来报告。

## Key Contributions

- Separates *parameter geometry* from *functional geometry* and argues that neither low-loss connectivity, permutation alignment, nor tangent-space arithmetic implies a task vector carries input-independent meaning.
- Introduces a first-token predictive-distribution **interaction ratio** R = E[JSD(p_{α,β}, p_add)] / E[JSD(p_{α,β}, p_{0,0})], averaged separately (ratio-of-expectations) over 36 interior grid points and 60 held-out prompts per stratum, then averaged over 3 seeds. The additive counterfactual is a normalized additive displacement in log-probability coordinates, invariant to scalar logit shifts.
- **Core median norm matching** as the primary mechanism control: every rank-16 task vector is rescaled to the median Frobenius norm of the audit block, so radius cannot explain the contrast.
- A prospectively frozen 6-task expansion (math, code, instruction, safety + XSum summarization, OPUS Books en→fr translation) with bins registered *before* adapter training, plus an exact design-preserving randomization test over the 12 distinct label assignments.
- A three-tier external validation ladder: raw public prompts (HumanEval+/MBPP+, GSM8K, AlpacaEval, XSTest) → format-bridge diagnostic (same prompts, Alpaca wrapper) → execution behavior (greedy generation, official EvalPlus sanitization, pass@1 factorial interaction I_S = S_ab − S_a − S_b + S_0).
- An explicit **Claim Boundary Checklist** enumerating what the evidence does *not* support: no global semantic coordinate system, no universal architecture generality, no per-example interference prediction, no downstream-performance prediction, no safety guarantee.

## Methodology

Let θ₀ be a shared base and ∆ₐ, ∆_b be LoRA displacements merged into base weights. The authors evaluate θ(α,β) = θ₀ + α∆ₐ + β∆_b on a 7×7 grid with scales {0.0, 0.2, …, 1.2}; (1,1) is ordinary task arithmetic, while the axes retain each task's full nonlinear path. For prompt x they take first-token logits ℓ_{α,β}(x) and build a no-interaction prediction **in logit space** (Eq. 2), equivalently p_add(v|x) ∝ p_{α,0}(v|x)·p_{0,β}(v|x)/p_{0,0}(v|x). Crucially, softmax is applied only *after* logit composition. Interaction is I_x = JSD(p_{α,β}, p_add) in nats over the full vocabulary; the denominator M_x = JSD(p_{α,β}, p_{0,0}) measures actual displacement from base. Numerator and denominator are averaged separately over prompts and the 36 interior points (α,β>0) before forming a seed-level ratio — the paper deliberately avoids per-prompt ratios with unstable small denominators, and reports an expectation-of-ratios (EoR) sensitivity analysis alongside.

Training: Qwen2.5 at 0.5B/1.5B/7B, rank-16 LoRA on q,k,v,o,gate,up,down projections, α=32, dropout 0.05, lr 2e-4, cosine schedule with 5% warmup, effective batch 16, bf16, 3,000 examples × 2 epochs per task, seeds {42,123,456}. A separate 0.5B audit does full-parameter FT at lr 2e-5 with identical data/seeds/objective. A **response-only** protocol supervises response tokens plus a true EOS and masks prompt/padding; the authors disclose that an earlier pilot protocol shared padding and EOS identifiers, inadvertently masking real EOS targets, and that no pilot checkpoint enters any reported estimate.

Statistics: hierarchical bootstrap resampling seeds as outer unit and prompts as inner unit, with matched contrasts sharing prompt indices. The authors explicitly note that with 3 seeds an exact sign test cannot reach p<0.05, so they report bootstrap intervals and seed-level directional consistency separately rather than pretending to significance. A frozen denominator audit records quantiles per family/stratum/seed, flags but never removes boundary cases, and reproduces all 68 frozen bootstrap summary values at 1e-12 tolerance. Parameter-space diagnostics (global cosine, TIES sign-conflict rate over jointly non-negligible coordinates, layerwise singular-subspace overlap, participation ratio, stable rank) are audited but never used to define the primary metric.

## Results & Highlights

- **Input stratum dominates the contrast (Qwen2.5-1.5B, norm-matched, Table 2).** code+safety vs the matched code+math control: **code prompts 8.49 vs 2.53 = +5.96 pts [4.14, 7.93]**; **instruction prompts 9.31 vs 1.74 = +7.58 pts [5.59, 9.69]**; **math prompts 2.98 vs 2.88 = +0.10 pts [−0.83, 0.91]**; safety prompts 6.20 vs 4.24 = +1.97 pts [−0.03, 4.14] — the safety stratum interval *touches zero* even though safety is half the headline pair. The two families share the same code checkpoint within each seed, so this is a genuinely matched contrast.
- **Not a denominator artifact.** On code prompts the numerator rises 0.00352 → 0.01663 (≈4.7×) while the denominator rises only 0.14048 → 0.19728 (≈1.4×); on math prompts both rise roughly proportionally, so the normalized contrast is near zero. Estimator sensitivity confirms stability: RoE +5.96 vs EoR +5.90 on code, +7.58 vs +8.04 on instruction (Table 8).
- **Prospective transfer is coarse only.** Across 9 unseen-task families, **8/8** high-vs-low comparisons have the predicted sign (12/12 including the registered code+math low anchor), with safety+translation top at R=16.62% and math+translation bottom at 6.06%. But **high>middle holds in only 5/8**, Spearman ρ=0.571 (n=9, nominal p=0.108) among OOS families, and per-stratum accuracy is **37.5% on math and 50% on code** vs 100% on instruction/safety/summarization and 75% on translation — i.e. the "successful" aggregate ordering fails on two of six strata. The exact randomization test gives **p=0.083 = 1/12**, which the authors correctly flag as the smallest value this design can attain.
- **Stress tests hold, with magnitude modulation (Table 5, code / instruction contrasts in pts).** 0.5B LoRA **+6.21 [3.11, 9.04] / +5.26 [3.74, 6.70]**; 0.5B Full FT **+4.37 [2.58, 6.27] / +2.98 [2.43, 3.54]** (weaker than LoRA — parameterization modulates magnitude but does not create the ordering); Qwen2.5-7B LoRA **+9.52 [7.14, 12.77] / +10.56 [9.23, 11.88]** (larger than 1.5B, inconsistent with a simple capacity-bottleneck story); Llama-3.1-8B LoRA **+4.87 [3.86, 5.88] / +5.30 [4.03, 6.59]**, all with 3/3 positive seed differences and intervals excluding zero.
- **Functional structure without parameter clustering.** At 0.5B (4 tasks, 12 checkpoints) the cosine gap is 0.0045 (p=0.1146), silhouette −0.0008 (p=0.0886), 1-NN task accuracy 0.250 (p=0.3755) — *none* significant — yet the functional contrast is +6.21/+5.26. At 1.5B (6 tasks, 18 checkpoints) the same diagnostics are significant but tiny (cosine gap 0.0184, silhouette 0.0122, 1-NN 0.444). Static parameter summaries did not reliably predict held-out families.
- **The format boundary is the sharpest result (Table 4).** On 60 raw public prompts per stratum: math **+0.36 [−0.59, 1.31]** (passes the frozen [−2,+2] practical-equivalence gate, as intended), code **+6.91 [3.71, 10.87]**, instruction **+11.09 [8.34, 13.94]**, safety **+3.96 [1.24, 6.93]**. Wrapping the **identical 60 public code prompts** in the Alpaca instruction template collapses the contrast **+6.91 → +0.34 [−0.16, 0.86]**, i.e. strong equivalence. Provenance is not the boundary; serialization is.
- **Execution metrics do not reproduce it.** Factorial pass@1 interaction contrast is **+4.14 pts [−2.44, 12.60]** on HumanEval+ (bootstrap P(>0)=0.841) and **−0.15 pts [−2.65, 2.38]** on MBPP+. A sequence-level teacher-forcing check gives smaller but positive residuals: public code **+3.72 [2.77, 4.85]**, public math **+1.87 [0.88, 2.93]** — so even the math "equivalence" claim is specific to the first-token estimand.

## Authors & Credibility

Chencheng Zhu (UNSW Sydney), Xiaoyang Li and Taotao Cai (University of Southern Queensland). No lab-scale compute: the 1.5B replication and expansion ran on a single RTX 5090 (32 GB) in a Linux container, with an earlier 0.5B pilot on an A100 40 GB. The methodological hygiene is notably above the norm for a small group — frozen protocols with dated identifiers (`response-only-eos-v2-20260712`), locked checkpoint revisions, prompt hashes, a disclosed-and-discarded pilot bug, an estimator audit reproducing 68 frozen bootstrap values exactly, and a self-authored claim-boundary checklist. Against that: the artifact is *planned*, not released ("machine-readable audits and prompt hashes are planned for the public artifact"), and the authors admit the cached surface evaluation "predates prompt hash fields recorded during generation and has no protocol file," limiting provenance strength. Llama-3.1 weights are not redistributed (license).

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty (moderate-to-good).** The logit-space additive counterfactual that subtracts each axis's own marginal curvature before measuring interaction is a genuinely cleaner operationalization than the usual chord-from-base-to-merge comparison, and the framing of input distribution as a first-class argument of the estimand is the right conceptual move. But it is adjacent to two concurrent works the authors themselves cite — Zhou et al. (2026) on predicting mergeability from checkpoint properties and Sivaramakrishnan et al. (2026) showing parameter-space angles are unstable predictors — and the paper's positive claim ("coarse, input-conditioned ordering exists") is weaker than either.

**Rigor (high on procedure, low on power).** Three seeds is the binding constraint and the authors know it: they state outright that an exact sign test cannot reach p<0.05 with |S|=3, and that the 8/8 randomization result yields p=0.083 *because 1/12 is the floor of the design*. That means the flagship prospective test was structurally incapable of significance before any data were collected — a preregistered design should have been powered to be falsifiable. The anchor-inclusive ρ=0.697 (p=0.0118) is the only sub-0.05 number in the paper and it is explicitly circular (the anchors defined the bins); the authors demote it to secondary, correctly.

**Paper-specific weaknesses.** (1) There is **no same-task or random-direction anchor**, which the authors concede — so R has no absolute scale, and a reader cannot tell whether R=8.49% is "a lot of interference" or noise floor; every claim is comparative by construction. (2) **Individual adapter task accuracy is never reported** — the paper says plainly that "each task label describes training data rather than verified capability." A "safety" adapter that learned refusal-flavored surface style would produce the same first-token interaction signal, and nothing rules this out. (3) The primary metric is **first-token JSD**, and the authors admit it "does not reliably predict decoded behavior" — the sequence-level teacher-forcing check (+3.72 on code) confirms attenuation. (4) Full-parameter FT is audited **only at 0.5B**, and Llama-3.1-8B covers **only 3 tasks**, so "across scales and model families" rests on one 8B point with one prompt protocol. (5) The 1.5B safety-stratum interval **includes zero (+1.97 [−0.03, 4.14])** while the 0.5B safety contrast is **+5.52 [3.21, 8.26]** — a 2.8× non-monotone swing across scale on the stratum most relevant to the safety framing, which goes unremarked. (6) The claimed EvalPlus null is **underpowered, not demonstrated**: HumanEval+ interval [−2.44, 12.60] is compatible with a large positive effect, and the authors say so, but the abstract's "do not robustly reproduce it" reads more definitive than [−2.44, 12.60] warrants.

**Red flags / overclaiming (low).** This is the rare paper that under-claims. There are no comparisons to TIES-Merging, DARE, or AdaMerging — but the paper explicitly disclaims predicting merge performance, which is intellectually honest yet also makes the contribution hard to falsify against the merging literature it is nominally about. There is no ablation of the metric itself: no rank-4/rank-64 LoRA sweep, no alternative divergence (KL, TV), no layer-resolved decomposition (named as future work), and no test of whether the 7×7 grid resolution or the [0,1.2] range matters. Cherry-picking risk is low because the failure cases are foregrounded.

**Reproducibility (medium, pending artifact).** Hyperparameters, seeds, grid, prompt counts, and hardware are fully specified (Table 6); the total is well within a single-GPU budget. The blocker is that the artifact is promised rather than posted, and the authors' own admission of missing token provenance for the cached surface means an independent party would re-run rather than re-verify.

**Significance (medium).** For practitioners the actionable finding is narrow but real: an instruction wrapper can erase a 6.91-point interference signal on identical prompts, so any merging diagnostic must be evaluated in the exact serialization used at deployment. That is a useful warning. It is not a method, and it will not change how anyone merges models tomorrow.

## Strengths

- The matched-control design (code+safety vs code+math sharing the same code checkpoint within seed) is the right comparison and is genuinely hard to argue with.
- Norm matching directly kills the most obvious confound (small vectors look additive because they stay near base), and the numerator/denominator decomposition in Table 7 proves the effect is not a shrinking denominator.
- Prospective specification of bins and success criteria *before* training the summarization/translation adapters, with a dated appendix trace, is real preregistration discipline in a subfield with essentially none.
- Negative and boundary results (format collapse, EvalPlus non-reproduction, non-significant clustering at 0.5B) are placed in the abstract and given their own sections rather than buried.
- Disclosure of the EOS-masking pilot bug and explicit exclusion of all pilot checkpoints.
- The estimator audit (RoE vs EoR, exact reproduction of 68 frozen bootstrap values) preempts the obvious "your ratio estimator is doing the work" objection.

## Limitations

- Three training seeds cap statistical power; the headline prospective test's p-value floor is 0.083 by design.
- No same-task or random-direction anchor, so interaction-ratio magnitudes are uninterpretable in absolute terms.
- Individual adapter capability is never measured — task labels denote training data, not verified skill.
- First-token JSD is a proxy; sequence-level and pass@1 signals are weaker or absent.
- Full-parameter fine-tuning audited only at 0.5B; cross-architecture evidence is a single Llama-3.1-8B run on 3 tasks.
- Shared checkpoints across families make cross-family correlations statistically dependent (the authors label them "nominal").
- Only two adapters are composed at a time; nothing is said about 3+ way merges, which is the practically interesting regime.
- No layer-resolved decomposition of the interaction, so there is no mechanism — only a measurement.
- Artifact not yet public; partial provenance gap for the cached surface evaluation.

## Relevance

如果关注模型合并 / task arithmetic 的可靠性、或在做 LoRA 适配器组合与多能力模型装配，这篇给出的「功能非可加性依赖输入分布与提示格式」以及「参数空间相似度诊断不能预测功能干扰」两条结论是直接可用的负面知识——尤其是 Alpaca 模板让同一批提示的对比度从 +6.91 塌缩到 +0.34 这一发现，提示任何合并前诊断都必须在部署时真实使用的序列化格式下评估。同时，本文的预注册式设计、范数匹配干预、RoE/EoR 估计量审计，可作为 LLM 评测方法论上的一个可借鉴范式。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the code/instruction contrast survive if the adapters are first verified to actually improve their task's accuracy? Reporting per-adapter task performance would separate "learned capability" from "learned output style," which the current design cannot distinguish.
- Is the Alpaca-wrapper collapse a genuine functional fact or an artifact of the first-token estimand — i.e. does the wrapper make the first token nearly deterministic (a template token), mechanically shrinking both numerator and denominator? A distribution-entropy breakdown at the measured token would settle this immediately.
- Why is the 7B contrast (+9.52/+10.56) *larger* than 1.5B while 0.5B Full FT is smaller? Is interference growing with scale, or is R confounded with vocabulary-level entropy that varies with model size?
- What does a same-task control (two adapters trained on the same task with different seeds) yield for R? Without that noise floor, the entire ordering could sit inside seed-to-seed variation.
- Does the ordering extend to 3+ task vectors, where the additive counterfactual would need a higher-order factorial decomposition, and do pairwise interactions predict triple-wise ones?
- Would a layer-resolved or module-resolved decomposition (attention vs MLP projections) localize where code and safety displacements collide, converting this diagnostic into an actionable merging intervention?
- Given HumanEval+ interval [−2.44, 12.60], how many seeds/tasks would be needed to actually distinguish "no pass@1 interaction" from "moderate pass@1 interaction"? The paper's null is currently a power statement, not an evidential one.
