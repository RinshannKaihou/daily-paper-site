> **一句话总结：** 本文提出 GO-MUON，把 Muon 的极分解更新推广为「任意 SPD 左右映射下的加权谱 oracle」，用 OAS 收缩后的 K-FAC 激活/反传二阶矩的 **负四次方根** 作为几何，并每 K=4 步才刷新一次因子；在 PTB 字符语言模型上把三窗口训练损失几何均值相对 Muon 降低 0.38%、Tiny Shakespeare 降低 3.71%、单步耗时降 20.2%，在改造过的模加法 grokking 任务上把达到持续 99% 泛化准确率的步数从 2320/4520 降到 290/220。

> **TL;DR:** GO-Muon reformulates Muon's polar update as an exact linear-minimization oracle for *any* pair of SPD left/right maps (Theorem 1), then instantiates those maps as inverse fourth roots of OAS-shrunk K-FAC activation and backward-gradient second moments, refreshed only every K=4 steps. On a 3-layer 128-width char Transformer it cuts the 3-window training-loss geometric mean by 0.38% on Penn Treebank and 3.71% on Tiny Shakespeare vs. paired Muon controls, cuts mean step time to 0.798x (−20.2%) vs. per-step refresh, and on corrected independent-CLS modular addition reaches sustained 99% held-out accuracy at update 290 vs. 2320 for Muon (mod 103) and 220 vs. 4520 (mod 107). Caveats: single-author, sub-1M-parameter models, 1,000 updates, training loss only (no val/test), asymmetric LR grids, and one screening seed per modulus for the headline 8x/20.5x grokking claim.

## 核心贡献

- **因子无关的加权谱 oracle（Theorem 1）**：对任意 SPD 映射 $P_A, P_B$，方向 $D_0 = P_B \,\mathrm{Polar}(P_B M P_A)\, P_A$ 精确求解约束 $\|P_B^{-1} D P_A^{-1}\|_{op} \le 1$ 下的支撑问题，且 $\langle M, D_0\rangle = \|P_B M P_A\|_*>0$。该结论**不依赖** Fisher 语义、Kronecker 近似或因子新鲜度，因此缓存的旧几何也自动保证正对齐（Corollary 2）。
- **"map-back" 为什么必需**：给出一个 2×2 反例，仅在极分解**之前**做预条件（inside-only）会使 $\langle M, \mathrm{Polar}(Z)\rangle = -17/\sqrt{949} < 0$，即得到上升方向；正标量 graft 无法修正符号。匹配的 map-back 则恒为正。
- **观测标签因子 vs. 真 Fisher 的定量刻画（Prop. 3 / Cor. 4）**：$\|B_{q,z}-B_{F,z}\|_2 \le 3\|q-p\|_2$，隐层版本再乘 $\|J_l\|_2^2$；只有在预测一致性（模型分布收敛到数据条件分布）下经验 Fisher 才收敛到模型 Fisher/GGN，作者明确指出这**不是**任意训练轨迹的性质。
- **四分之一次幂几何 + Frobenius graft**：$\alpha=1/4$ 对应 K-FAC 块的平方根度量的算子范数版本；Prop. 5 给出最坏情况保证 $\langle M,\hat D_\alpha\rangle \ge K_{AB}^{-\alpha}\|M\|_*$ 与 $\kappa_+(\hat D_\alpha)\le K_{AB}^{\alpha}$。
- **懒惰刷新是"方差–滞后"权衡而非降噪（Prop. 6）**：$\beta_B^K$ 系数保留了经过步数的衰减但不复现被跳过的 minibatch 平均；在 $\beta_B=0.97, K=4$ 时稀疏/逐步方差比为 **3.99537**，而平均滞后 $L_4=32.3714$ 对 $L_1=32.3333$ 步——滞后几乎不变，方差涨约 4 倍。作者由此明确否定"陈旧几何=去噪"的常见说法。

## Key Contributions

- A factor-agnostic **matched spectral oracle**: the pull-back/push-forward construction is proved to be the exact LMO for the SPD-weighted operator-norm ball, framed as the matrix specialization of Veprikov et al. (2025)'s preconditioned-norm oracle rather than a new duality principle (the author says so explicitly).
- A **cached-geometry corollary** certifying strictly positive source alignment for *any* stale SPD pair, plus a scale-free efficiency bound (Prop. 7): a cached direction, rescaled to its current weighted radius, retains at least $\Theta_t^{-1}$ of the current oracle value where $\Theta_t = \kappa_2(P_{B,t}^{-1}\tilde P_B)\kappa_2(P_{A,t}^{-1}\tilde P_A)$.
- A **four-term error decomposition** for the backward factor (finite sample + model mismatch + shrinkage/floor + EMA/staleness), separating the Fisher interpretation from regularization and temporal choices.
- The **GO-Muon system**: OAS shrinkage (Chen et al., 2010) with an explicit conditioning bound $\kappa_2(\mathrm{OAS}(S)) \le (d-(d-1)\lambda)/\lambda$, eigenvalue flooring, cached inverse fourth roots, hook-gated factor capture (forward hooks return immediately on reuse steps), and fresh momentum/polar/graft every step.
- Empirical evidence on three protocols: PTB and Tiny Shakespeare char LM trajectories, a matched-map-back ablation vs. an inside-only halfspace baseline, a lazy-vs-per-step refresh ablation, and a modular-addition grokking screen.

## Methodology

The core object is the support problem $\max_D \langle M, D\rangle$ s.t. $\|P_B^{-1}DP_A^{-1}\|_{op}\le \rho$. Substituting $U = P_B^{-1}DP_A^{-1}$ and using operator/nuclear duality gives value $\rho\|C\|_*$ with $C = P_BMP_A$ and maximizer $D_\rho = \rho P_B Q(C) P_A$. The key rhetorical move of the paper is to *decouple* this exactness from everything else: the oracle is exact for whatever geometry you hand it; whether that geometry is *useful* is a separate estimation question.

For instantiation, GO-Muon builds minibatch second moments $\hat A = \frac1N\sum x_ix_i^\top$ (layer inputs) and $\hat B = \frac1N\sum \delta_i\delta_i^\top$ (output gradients at the observed label), applies OAS shrinkage, floors eigenvalues at roundoff scale, and sets $P_A = A_t^{-1/4}$, $P_B = \bar B_t^{-1/4}$. The quarter power is justified as the operator-norm analogue of the *square root* of the K-FAC block, since $\mathrm{vec}(D)^\top (A^{1/2}\otimes B^{1/2})\mathrm{vec}(D) = \|B^{1/4}DA^{1/4}\|_F^2$; $\alpha = 0, 1/4, 1/2$ recover Muon, GO-Muon, and full-factor spectral map-back respectively. After the polar map, a positive Frobenius graft rescales $D_{0}$ to $\sqrt{\min(m,n)}/\|D_0\|_F$, restoring Muon's update energy while preserving the oracle ray and sign (but *not* the unit-radius LMO). The final update adds Muon's $\sqrt{\max(1,m/n)}$ shape factor and a cosine LR schedule; embeddings, norms, and non-matrix parameters use AdamW.

The refresh schedule is the systems contribution. Every $K=4$ steps, hooks are armed before the forward pass, activations are retained, output-gradient hooks fire, moments/OAS/eigendecompositions run, and $\bar B_t = \beta_B^K \bar B_{t-K} + (1-\beta_B^K)B_t$ updates. On the other three steps the hook returns immediately and all factor work is skipped; momentum, the polar map, graft, and parameter update still run every step. $A_t$ has no EMA at all — it is sampled and held. Prop. 6 characterizes the resulting estimator via spectral-measure transfer functions $G_K$ and $H_1$, proving $\mathrm{Cov}(Y_j)\succeq \mathrm{Cov}(Z_{jK})$ in general and giving the exact IID ratio $\frac{1-\beta_B^K}{1+\beta_B^K}\big/\frac{1-\beta_B}{1+\beta_B}$.

Experiments use 3-layer causal Transformers, width 128, 4 heads, SwiGLU width 384, seq len 128, 2,048 tokens/update, 1,000 updates; the grokking model is width 64 with RoPE and an `[a, b, CLS]` input where CLS is an independent learned vector (the "correction" over prior setups where the control token doubled as an unused output class).

## Results & Highlights

- **Penn Treebank char LM (hash-matched, 3 paired seeds)**: GO-Muon at matrix LR 0.080 reaches window means 1.45689 / 1.28167 / 1.17000 (updates 201–300, 501–600, 901–1000) vs. Muon at LR 0.020 with 1.46773 / 1.28084 / 1.17560. Trajectory geometric mean 1.29757 vs. 1.30257 — a **0.38% reduction**, winning the per-seed summary on 3/3 pairs. But the middle window is a *loss*: Muon is lower by 0.00083. The early/late gains are 0.74% and 0.48%, i.e. the headline is carried by two of three windows and the absolute effect is under 0.006 nats.
- **Tiny Shakespeare (3 fresh paired seeds)**: 1.63052 / 1.43613 / 1.31465 vs. the registered Muon+Q/K control's 1.70001 / 1.50427 / 1.34825; trajectory GM 1.45471 vs. 1.51072, a **3.71% reduction**, and GO-Muon is lower in **all 9 paired seed–window comparisons**. On the tuning seed (17401) the same config gave 1.45132 vs. 1.51073 (−3.93%), so the frozen selection transferred to seeds 17402–17404 with only mild shrinkage (3.93% → 3.71%).
- **Grokking on corrected modular addition (one screening seed per modulus)**: mod 103 — GO-Muon reaches sustained 99% held-out accuracy at update **290 vs. 2320** for Muon (**8.0x**); mod 107 — **220 vs. 4520** (**20.5x**). Crucially, *training*-set fitting is nearly identical (64 vs. 66 updates on mod 103; 68 vs. 65 on mod 107), so grokking **delay** falls from 2254 → 226 and 4455 → 152. This is the paper's most striking number by two orders of magnitude relative to the LM gains. The author honestly reports that the mod-107 GO-Muon run *leaves* the 99% region later, with below-threshold evaluations as late as update 4470 before returning at 4480 — a retention failure that the onset metric does not penalize.
- **Matched map-back ablation (PTB, 4 paired seeds)**: in the preregistered 201–300 window, matched map-back gets 1.45513 vs. **1.48882** for the inside-only halfspace baseline — a **2.26% relative reduction**, lower on all 4 paired seeds. Notably this ablation gain (2.26%) is ~6x larger than the headline gain over Muon itself (0.38%), which suggests the halfspace baseline is weak rather than that matched map-back is strong. The allocation was requeued before the final preregistered window and the 901–1000 endpoint is **not imputed**.
- **Lazy refresh ablation (PTB, 4 paired seeds, K=4 vs. K=1)**: early window is a tie (1.45549 vs. 1.45513, lazy *worse* by 0.00036, 2/4 wins); middle window lazy lower by 0.00119 (2/4 wins); late window lazy lower by 0.00320, winning **4/4**. Mean step-time ratio **0.798x (−20.2%)**. So the compute saving is real and, at this scale, free — but the win rate of 2/4 in two of three windows means the loss improvement itself is not established.
- Both refresh arms recorded **zero** negative momentum-source / current-gradient alignments, consistent with Corollary 2.

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty (moderate).** The theory is deliberately modest by the author's own framing: Theorem 1 is "the matrix specialization of the general preconditioned-norm pullback oracle (Veprikov et al., 2025)" and "not a new duality principle." Mousse (Zhang et al., 2026b) already applies "a quarter-power matched polar map with a Frobenius graft," so the *update form* is shared; GO-Muon's differentiators are (a) OAS-shrunk observed-label K-FAC factors instead of Shampoo gradient Grams, (b) the exact 1/4 exponent, and (c) hook-gated factor skipping. Prop. 6's variance–lag characterization and the accompanying refusal to claim staleness denoises are the freshest genuinely-new content. The 2x2 counterexample in §2.2 is a nice, concrete pedagogical contribution.

**Rigor (mixed, with unusual self-honesty).** The paper repeatedly flags its own weaknesses in ways most submissions do not: it labels the PTB comparison **"exploratory"** because the three-window ranking rule was introduced *after* the original late-window rule (which would have selected LR 0.16 instead of 0.08) — a textbook post-hoc selection hazard, self-declared; it states Θ_t "is not measured" in the experiments despite Prop. 7 being conditional on it; it refuses to impute the missing 901–1000 window in §5.3; it calls blockwise geometry coherence "an empirical mechanism hypothesis, not a consequence of Proposition 6"; and it reports the mod-107 retention failure that cuts against its own headline. That said, several confounds remain unaddressed. The **learning rates are not matched**: PTB Muon runs at 0.020 while GO-Muon runs at 0.080, and the grokking screen uses **disjoint-ish grids** — {0.16, 0.32} for Muon vs. {0.08, 0.16} for GO-Muon, so Muon was *never evaluated at 0.08*, the value that won for GO-Muon. Given that grokking onset is famously LR- and weight-decay-sensitive, an 8x–20.5x claim resting on one seed per modulus with an asymmetric two-point LR grid is the weakest link in the paper. **No validation or test loss is reported for the LM experiments at all** ("No validation or test split is read") — every LM number is *training* cross-entropy, so nothing here speaks to generalization. There is no comparison against Shampoo, SOAP, Mousse, or FISMO despite an entire related-work subsection devoted to them; the only measured baselines are Muon and an inside-only halfspace variant the paper constructed itself. There is also no ablation on α (0 vs. 1/4 vs. 1/2) despite Eq. (12)–(14) explicitly parameterizing it, no ablation on K beyond {1, 4}, and no ablation isolating OAS shrinkage from the eigenvalue floor.

**Reproducibility (weak).** No code or artifact link appears anywhere in the paper. Hyperparameters are specified reasonably well (β_B=0.97, K=4, momentum 0.8, 50-step warmup, cosine schedule, seeds 17401–17404), and the deterministic 50/50 modular-addition split is described, but the "hash-matched" seed protocol is not defined precisely enough to replicate, and the "registered campaign control" for Tiny Shakespeare references an external preregistration that is not included.

**Significance (limited at this scale, potentially real).** Every model is a sub-1M-parameter, 3-layer, 128-width Transformer trained for 1,000 updates. A 0.38% training-loss delta at that scale over 3 seeds is within the range where optimizer comparisons routinely reverse at scale. The 20.2% step-time saving is the most portable finding, and it is a systems result that should transfer. The grokking result, if it survives multi-seed replication and matched LR grids, would be the interesting one — a curvature-matched geometry that reaches generalization 8–20x sooner without changing memorization speed is a mechanistically suggestive claim.

**Red flags.** (1) The two headline empirical claims live at wildly different effect sizes (0.38% vs. 20.5x) on wildly different tasks, which invites cherry-pick suspicion. (2) The abstract calls the PTB result a win without the "exploratory" qualifier that §5.1 attaches to it. (3) The strongest ablation gain (2.26%) is against a self-constructed weak baseline. (4) Sampling occurs *before* the refresh-domain EMA, which the paper notes "can alias" high-frequency variation — an acknowledged but unmeasured failure mode. (5) The `2608.*` arXiv ID and the dense thicket of `2602.*`–`2604.*` citations mean essentially none of the closest competitors have been independently validated.

## Strengths

- The separation of "the oracle is exact for the supplied geometry" from "the geometry may be bad" is genuinely clarifying, and Corollary 2 turns it into a usable engineering guarantee: cached factors can never produce a negative source alignment.
- The §2.2 counterexample is concrete and immediately convincing about why inside-only preconditioning (as used by several concurrent Muon variants) can be sign-wrong.
- Prop. 6 is a real result with a clean answer: sparse refresh at β=0.97, K=4 costs 3.995x stationary variance but only 0.038 steps of extra lag. Framing lazy curvature as compute-for-variance rather than as denoising corrects a common folk belief.
- Unusually candid reporting: the post-hoc selection-rule change, the unmeasured Θ_t, the un-imputed missing window, the mod-107 retention failure, and the 2/4 win rates are all disclosed rather than buried.
- The error decomposition in Eq. (20) is a useful mental model for anyone building K-FAC-style optimizers, and the OAS conditioning bound Eq. (19) is a practical guard rail.
- Table 1's taxonomy (statistical geometry / matrix update / curvature amortization) is a clean way to place Muon, K-FAC, Shampoo, SOAP, Mousse, FISMO, and GO-Muon on one grid.

## Limitations

- All LM evidence is **training** loss on 3-layer/128-width models for 1,000 updates; no validation, no test, no scaling study, no wall-clock-matched comparison at any realistic model size.
- Learning rates are unmatched across arms (0.020 vs. 0.080 on PTB), and the grokking LR grids for the two optimizers barely overlap, leaving the 8x/20.5x claim confounded.
- The grokking screen uses **one seed per modulus** and only two moduli (103, 107) — the paper itself calls it a "screen."
- No comparison against the closest concurrent methods (Mousse, FISMO) or the classical structured baselines (Shampoo, SOAP), despite discussing all of them.
- Missing ablations: α exponent sweep, K sweep beyond {1,4}, OAS vs. plain damping, graft vs. no-graft, and the effect of A_t having no EMA at all.
- Prop. 7's guarantee depends on the drift quantity Θ_t, which is never measured, so the cached-geometry bound is untested in practice.
- Theory covers only linear layers `y = Wx`; attention-specific structure, the Q/K "partner metric" used in the Tiny Shakespeare control, and non-matrix parameters are handled by convention (AdamW) rather than by the framework.
- The full K-FAC independence approximation $E[(xx^\top)\otimes(\delta\delta^\top)] \ne E[xx^\top]\otimes E[\delta\delta^\top]$ remains, and near interpolation the softmax Fisher's shrinking eigenvalues mean absolute factor convergence does not imply relative inverse-root accuracy — acknowledged but not empirically probed.

## Relevance

如果你关注 Muon/Shampoo/SOAP 这条二阶与矩阵范数优化器主线，这篇给出了一个把「预条件放在极分解内外」这件事做成坐标一致 oracle 的干净数学框架，并附带一个可直接复用的工程结论：K=4 的懒惰因子刷新在方差涨约 4 倍、滞后几乎不变的代价下省 20% 单步时间。对 grokking / 泛化相变机制感兴趣的话，"训练集拟合时间不变、grokking 延迟从 2254 步降到 226 步" 这个观察值得单独去复现验证——但请注意它只有单种子且学习率网格不对称。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the 8x–20.5x grokking speedup survive a **symmetric** LR grid (both optimizers over {0.04, 0.08, 0.16, 0.32}) and 5+ seeds per modulus? Given that Muon was never run at 0.08, how much of the gap is just LR?
- Why is grokking *delay* the thing that collapses while memorization time is unchanged? Is the quarter-power geometry effectively acting like an implicit weight-decay/norm-control mechanism on the read-out, or is it genuinely reshaping the loss landscape's route to the generalizing circuit?
- What happens at α = 1/2 (full-factor spectral map-back) and α = 1/8? The paper parameterizes α but only ever runs 1/4; Mousse reportedly uses an exponent *below* 1/4 for language models, which hints the optimum may be task-dependent.
- Does the 0.38% PTB gain hold or grow at 100M–1B parameters and 100k+ updates, and does the 20.2% step-time saving survive when the polar map (which is *not* skipped) dominates cost at large widths?
- Can Θ_t be cheaply instrumented online to make K **adaptive** — refreshing only when coordinate drift exceeds a threshold, in the spirit of Eschenhagen et al. (2025)'s adaptive Shampoo refresh criteria?
- The mod-107 run leaves the 99% region around update 4470. Is post-grok instability a systematic cost of the quarter-power geometry, or a single-seed artifact? Onset-only metrics would hide a real regression here.
- How does GO-Muon compare against Muon when both are given equal *wall-clock* rather than equal update count, and against a simple diagonal preconditioner (MALT) that costs far less?
