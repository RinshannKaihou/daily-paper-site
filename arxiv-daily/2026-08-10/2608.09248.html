Critical edge

Methodology · distilled

Offline extraction. For each of 27 GoEmotions concepts, Qwen3-8B writes 100 short stories that embody the emotion without naming it. Token-mean activations (over late tokens) define a concept direction as within-class mean minus global mean, then PCA-denoised by projecting out the top-10 components of neutral stories. Stacking gives an extractor W_e ∈ R^{27×d}; the readout layer is picked by GoEmotions 27-way accuracy — L*=24/36, 37.2% vs 8.2% random.

Online routing. At each step the residual at the Assistant: delimiter is projected through W_e, ℓ2-normalized to e_t, then a 3-layer MLP (<27K params) maps it to a 128-d embedding. An argmax-cosine over 12 learnable prototypes selects one natural-language state template plus a scalar confidence c_t. The template enters the routing prompt only when c_t ≥ τ (τ=0.3; ~72% of steps admitted, else text-only fallback).

Evolution. Trajectory shift points δ_t = 1 − cos(e_t, e_{t−1}) above mean+1σ localize failure steps; 3-step windows around them are verbalized as diagnostics for an SOP rewriter, replacing coarse episode-level success/failure signals across R=3 rounds.

Strengths & limitations
+ Strengths
  • Real gap. Prior skill-selection methods condition only on text; the internal state at routing is genuinely unobserved.
  • Ablations as degradations (class means vs contrastive directions; raw labels vs encoder), isolating each design choice rather than just module existence.
  • Confidence gate is load-bearing: 28% of steps fall back to text-only; τ=0.9 collapses to 20.0%. Reported, not hidden.
  • Falsifiable evolution case study — a single SOP line ("verify the appliance is open…") traced to one T9 transition, credited with much of the Heat gain.
  • Low variance (≤1.6pp) vs ExpeL ±4.7, Reflexion ±7.0.
− Limitations
  • White-box only. Needs residual-stream access; cannot target API-served frontier models.
  • Per-checkpoint re-extraction. Even instruction-tuning degrades directions (37.2% → 28.1%); not a drop-in across versions.
  • ~500-episode warm-up, and the encoder's contrastive positives use episode outcomes — so the "internal" signal is partly reward-supervised.
  • Not uniformly better: loses to MASA on Cool (8B); on 14B loses on Pick, Clean, and badly on Cool (−20.5pp).
  • Only Qwen3 (8B/14B), two benchmarks — no Llama/Mistral/GPT replication of the emotion geometry.
  • "Emotion" may be a label of convenience — no ablation swapping GoEmotions for an arbitrary 27-concept basis.
Open questions for follow-up
  1. Placebo control — does injecting a random template + random confidence at the same rate recover part of the gain? Without it, "emotion" vs "any auxiliary token" is unresolved.
  2. Text-space baseline — how does the emotion channel compare to token entropy, logit margin over skill IDs, or self-reported confidence (no white-box access)?
  3. 14B accounting — why does the 14B ALFWorld average exceed its per-task mean by 5.4pp? Different weighting, split, or transcription error?
  4. Template bottleneck — is the 12-way argmax necessary? A quantized 27-dim vector or top-k with magnitudes may preserve more information.
  5. Does evolution need emotions? δ_t is just change-point detection on a projection — how does it compare to change-points on the raw hidden state or per-step novelty?
  6. Closed-loop risk — could bidirectional coupling let agents learn to induce "confident" states rather than solve the task (the representational analogue of reward hacking)?
