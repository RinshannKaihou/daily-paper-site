Agentic Science

Multi-agent discovery of practical quantum LDPC codes

An LLM multi-agent loop evolves executable generators of balanced-product CSS codes and discovers leading — even record — finite-length quantum LDPC codes across every weight class (e.g. [[234,28,18]] at w=10, Q=38.77), beating bivariate-bicycle baselines under BP-OSD decoding. A hard-result demonstration of agentic AI for science.

By Dongheng Qian & Tianyi Li  ·  → Full briefing

一句话：LLM 多智能体闭环（研究员议会 + 策展人 + worker）演化出 coset-orbit balanced-product CSS 码；w=10 的 [[234,28,18]] 拿到 Q=38.77，BP-OSD 解码下全程优于 bivariate-bicycle 基准。

TL;DR: An LLM multi-agent loop evolves balanced-product CSS code generators, finding leading or record finite-length quantum LDPC codes across weight classes and beating bivariate-bicycle baselines under BP-OSD.

Self-Evolving Agents

Evo-Bench: Can Language Models Improve Agent Harness?

The first benchmark isolating a model’s intrinsic ability to evolve an agent harness — freezing the policy model, seed harness and protocol while varying only the evolver LLM. GPT-5.6-Sol / Opus-4.8 lift Overall from 29.7 to ~46, approaching a human-engineered composite of 47.5.

By Lisheng Huang, Chen Yang et al.  ·  → Full briefing

一句话：首个把"模型能否自改 agent harness"隔离成受控基准的工作；GPT-5.6-Sol/Opus-4.8 把 Overall 从 29.7 推到 ~46，逼近人工 harness 组合的 47.5。
