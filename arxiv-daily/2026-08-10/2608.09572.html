Why this matters

Translates "forgetting" into falsifiable geometry — radial drift = hierarchy loss, cross-modal drift = relational drift — reusable for any Lorentz-space adapter.

Critical edge

The update rule ΔWₛ = δₛ(I − VVᵀ) is exactly GPM / Adam-NSCL null-space projection ported to the space-like block — and those closest Euclidean baselines are never benchmarked.

01

Methodology

Frozen pretrained encoders feed per-modality Lorentz heads W = [w₀ Wₛ] at fixed curvature K = 0.1, trained with symmetric hyperbolic contrastive + Ganea entailment-cone loss (wt 0.2). Tasks arrive sequentially, no replay.

Theorem 1: preserving intra-modal, inter-modal, and hierarchical structure holds iff one shared SO(d) rotation acts on every modality's old embeddings. The canonical solution projects the space-like block off the old-task row space, freezing the time-like row:

ΔWₛᵐ = δₛᵐ (I − VVᵀ) ,   Δw₀ᵐ = 0

One SVD per stage yields basis V from a fixed d×d covariance summary — O(d²) memory, task-count-independent, zero new parameters. AdamW's finite steps are patched by an unprincipled step-pullback toward task-start weights (β = 0.10 / 0.20).

03

Strengths

  • Theory and algorithm are genuinely coupled — Thm 1 → Cor 1 → Prop 1 → Cor 2 → Algo 1 is a derivation chain, not post-hoc justification.
  • Drift analysis falsifies the mechanism — each of four measures is tied to a preservation condition, reductions 57.8–73.8% at p ≤ 3.1×10⁻⁴.
  • Cheap and practical — zero new parameters, one SVD/stage, O(d²) constant memory, no replay buffer, no private data.
  • Honest controls within the set — all baselines share the hyperbolic objective, backbone, optimizer and schedule, isolating the update rule.
  • Rebuttal-driven additions (weight-decay mismatch, shuffled streams, drift stats) are explicitly labelled; the weight-decay result is arguably stronger than the main table.
04

Limitations

  • Frozen backbone, single linear head — the first-order & linearity assumptions may not survive full-backbone adaptation (authors concede this).
  • Closest relative never benchmarked — no Euclidean null-space projection (GPM / Adam-NSCL), no DER++ / ZSCL / MoE-Adapters, no Ayoughi 2025 hyperbolic CL.
  • Step-pullback (β) hand-tuned per backbone, never ablated, given to HMCL only — a missing "Vanilla + pullback" control.
  • Curvature fixed at K = 0.1 with no sensitivity study, despite being the central geometric hyperparameter.
  • Zero-shot retention never measured — though it motivates CLIP-style models; absolute accuracy only 40–46%.
05

Open questions the paper leaves

  1. How much gain survives when w/o-block meets a Euclidean GPM / Adam-NSCL baseline head-to-head?
  2. Why are Vanilla BWT_A and BWT_RK numerically identical (incl. std) in Table 1 — a column-copy error?
  3. Does Vanilla + step pullback close the gap, attributing some stability to the interpolation heuristic?
  4. Does shared-isometry hold when curvature K is learned or varied?
  5. Known hyperbolic dimensional collapse → does the old-task subspace shrink, weakening projection over 15 tasks?
  6. At what stream length does the null space vanish and HMCL lose plasticity entirely?
