Top-i logits sent to −∞ before softmax → model is forced onto a lower-ranked word ("machine circumlocution"). Predicate W fires only when the preferred continuation starts a new lexical word.
② mid-word step · fully unmasked
##ing …
Subword completion inside a word is never masked, so the intervention applies semantic pressure rather than corrupting tokenization. Two-phase lifecycle per word.

Under greedy decoding the model emits x* = argmax z. Taboo injects a binary mask M ∈ {0, −∞} before softmax, sending the top-i logits to −∞ — but only when the next token would start a new lexical word. Word-initial status is precomputed once from tokenizer prefixes (Ġ for byte-level BPE in Llama/Qwen; ▁ for SentencePiece in Gemma). Per-step cost is a vectorized top-i plus masked argmax — negligible beside the forward pass; the only real overhead is longer generations (~1.4× tokens at i=1). Perturbation strength is graded as injected surprisal ΔS in bits (the extra cost, in the model's own belief, of emitting a blocked token's replacement), which grows roughly logarithmically with i and is comparable across tokenizers. The headline metric is conditional retention R — correct-under-Taboo over only the items the model already solved, decoupling off-path resilience from nominal capability.

  • Word-initial-only is justified by ablation, not asserted — mid-word masking at matched i=2 collapses GSM8K absolute accuracy to 0.01–0.05 across all four families and both tokenizer types.
  • Base-vs-instruct × scale × benchmark grid with 95% Wilson intervals, plus precision (4-bit vs bf16), temperature (τ=0.7 / 1.0 × 3 seeds), LLM-judge extraction audit, and IFEval task-constraint controls.
  • HumanEval is a deliberate negative control — near-zero lexical redundancy at word starts (def/return/for) pins Pass@1 to 0%, establishing the method's empirical floor.
  • Standardized 2-shot CoT prompts on fixed seeded subsamples (n=500 for 7–12B; n=100 for the scale ladder & ≥32B). Deterministic scoring; greedy decoding for exact item-level intervals.

0.01 vs 0.81
Mid-word ablation (Gemma-3-12B-it, GSM8K, matched i=2): masking non-start subwords collapses accuracy to 0.01 vs 0.81 under word-initial masking — at a higher average intervention. The paper's strongest internal-validity result: the drop is semantic stress, not tokenization corruption.
Strengths
  • The mid-word vs word-initial ablation is the right control and unusually decisive (0.01 vs 0.81) — turning a design choice into an empirical claim.
  • Thorough controls for a short paper: quantization spot-check with an 8-bit tiebreak on the one anomalous cell, temperature sampling at two settings × three seeds, an LLM-judge extraction audit (97% on GSM8K), and an IFEval collision check.
  • Intellectual honesty throughout — MMLU labelled an artifact, the Llama exception reported up front, the non-monotonic 72B-base dip not smoothed over.
  • Conditional retention R isolates off-path resilience from nominal capability — the right metric for the question.
  • Injected surprisal (bits) makes the dose comparable across tokenizers, killing the obvious "the aligned model was just perturbed less" objection.
  • Cheap and drop-in: a LogitsProcessor with negligible per-step overhead, no retraining, no prompt engineering.
Limitations
  • Cites three concurrent 2026 preprints with the same spirit — including one reporting bans improve reasoning — and runs no empirical comparison against any of them.
  • Headline rests on GSM8K alone; HumanEval pins to 0%, MMLU is an admitted formatting artifact, TriviaQA gaps invert. Presenting "four benchmarks" overstates the breadth.
  • The word-boundary predicate spares numerals & equation fragments — the very arithmetic tokens that matter on GSM8K. Scaffolding-vs-computation split is never measured.
  • n=4 families, no multiple-comparison correction; the 70B Llama result that rescues the narrative is a single run at n=100.
  • 512-token budget vs the 1.6–2.9× length expansion Taboo induces — a confound probed only for one model at 1024 tokens.
  • English-only; dense autoregressive models only (no MoE / proprietary APIs / multimodal); a single uniform top-i masking rule.

Questions for further exploration

Open threads the diagnostic leaves behind
  1. What fraction of Taboo interventions land on numerals / equation tokens vs prose scaffolding on GSM8K — is the measured "reasoning collapse" really a coherence or termination failure rather than a logic failure?
  2. Does next-token entropy (the sharpness measure the authors propose but don't run) explain the Qwen/Gemma/OLMo gain — and confirm that Llama-3.1-70B's gain is mechanistically distinct?
  3. Where between 8B and 70B does Llama-3 acquire taboo-robustness? 13B / 34B-class checkpoints would locate the transition.
  4. Under a generation budget scaled to each model's own expansion factor, does the base→instruct gap on Gemma-3 and Qwen2.5 survive — or does part of it come from instruct models simply having room to circumlocute?
  5. How does prompt-level forbidden-word banning rank models vs logit-level banning on identical items — does the confound the paper argues for actually change the ranking?
  6. Does an SFT-only checkpoint already confer taboo-robustness, or does it require DPO / RLHF? Isolating which post-training stage produces the effect is the obvious next experiment the diagnostic enables.
