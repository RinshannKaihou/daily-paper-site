03 Why this matters

A provably-bounded, control-theoretic route to stable multimodal training when sensors drop out — it never imputes or hallucinates missing values, only regulates motion along evidence-supported directions. 在模态缺失的病态稀疏场景下提供可证明的训练稳定性，且不插补、不"幻觉"缺失值。

04 Critical edge

Every comparison runs against fixed-step SGD only — no Adam, no RMSProp, no imputation baseline — so the marginal gain of the observability geometry over a plain per-coordinate adaptive step remains unproven.

05

Methodology

Three layers

Diagnostic prototype. For masked linear regression the residual splits as rt = −⟨et, x̃t⟩ + dt, where dt is an unobservable disturbance from masked coordinates. Standard masked SGD applies fully-actuated control inputs to a partially-actuated system: observed parameters drift as if the hidden ones were co-evolving, inducing inconsistency and "hallucinated" updates. A scalar gain αt = η/(ϵ+γt), γt=‖x̃t‖², recovers an ISS-style bound.

Directional controller. The rank-one actuation operator At = gtgtT (gt = ∇wŷt, the output Jacobian) captures both energy and direction of actuation. Aggregated into an EMA St = βSt−1+(1−β)At and regularized to Bt = St+ϵI, the update wt+1=wt−ηBt−1∇ℓt normalizes strongly-excited directions and shrinks updates along vanishingly-excited ones.

L1Scalar controller — isotropic throttling αt=η/(ϵ+γt); ISS bound ‖et‖≤ρt‖e0‖+C·sup|dτ|.
L2Directional controller — Gramian-like Bt via EMA of gtgtT; ridge ϵI guards singularity.
L3Stability (Prop. 1) — under bounded sensitivity, sector-bounded feedback, |δt|≤δ, and windowed excitation T≥d: ‖et‖≤Aq⌊t/T⌋‖e0‖+Lδ, q∈(0,1).
≈Scalable approximations — diagonal, block-diagonal (per layer/head), low-rank; deployed on a 368K-param CNN.

▲ Strengths

  • Provable trajectory bounds — rare in a missing-data literature dominated by statistical-consistency arguments.
  • Model-agnostic — same Bt−1∇ℓ machinery spans linear, kernel/RKHS, and gradient networks via the Jacobian gt.
  • Never imputes missing values — only regulates parameter motion in evidence-supported directions; safer in pathologically sparse regimes.
  • Large, consistent gains under heavy missingness (24/24 HAR cells; MAE ≈ half of masked SGD in early sea-ice windows).
  • Candid scope limits (Sec. 5.5) — finite-dimensional restriction and stall behavior stated openly.

▼ Limitations

  • Scalar-output only; multivariate / decoder / transformer outputs are future work.
  • Small-to-mid scale — largest model is a ~368K-param CNN; no large-pretraining eval.
  • Thin baselines — no Adam/RMSProp, no imputation, no Bayesian/ensemble comparisons.
  • Assumption 3 (|δt|≤δ) must be externally certified; the needed small-gain argument is not provided.
  • Windowed excitation needs T≥d for full-space guarantees; chronic missingness stalls the method.
  • No released code; "good/bad-region" occupation metric is internally defined, not standard.

◆ Open questions for further exploration

  1. Does the gain survive against Adam / RMSProp / modern imputation (e.g. ReMasker), or is it just per-coordinate adaptive scaling?
  2. Can Bt−1 extend to multivariate / decoder outputs and transformer scale under block-diagonal or low-rank At?
  3. Can a practical projection / small-gain mechanism make the |δt|≤δ bound verifiable rather than assumed for real deep nets?
  4. Does "controlled beats full SGD" persist once full SGD is properly tuned (Adam, cosine schedule, tuned step)?
  5. How does it behave when a parameter subspace is chronically unobserved — can a low-rank active-subspace realization recover useful guarantees?
  6. Can it compose with self-supervised / streaming objectives, where temporally-correlated missingness is the norm?
