> **一句话总结：** VERDI 把"世界模型优化经验"从可直接复用的配方降级为需要目标端实验验证的假设——用推理期探针构建 Optimization Fingerprint、用 IRG 几何检索候选修复、再用六项证书 + 冻结验证器逐条验证，在 Ctrl-World / Cosmos / RoboCoin 的留一骨干（LOBO）迁移上把首个通过严格门的试验数从冷启动 11.2 降到 3.6、GPU 成本从 312 降到 96 GPU-h，并把负迁移率从无条件复用的 0.34 压到 0.06。

> **TL;DR:** VERDI treats a repair strategy validated on one world model as an *optimization hypothesis*, not a reusable recipe: it fingerprints each model with shared inference-time probes, retrieves ranked candidates via an Interventional Repair Geometry (IRG), and admits nothing into memory until a frozen target-side verifier confirms it. On leave-one-backbone-out transfer across Ctrl-World / Cosmos-Predict 2 / Cosmos3 / RoboCoin, certified reuse hits the first strict-gate positive in 3.6 trials at 96 GPU-h vs 11.2 trials / 312 GPU-h for cold start (the headline "68% search, 69% GPU" numbers) — but vs the much stronger *unconditional reuse* baseline the speedup is only 4.1→3.6 trials and 118→96 GPU-h; the real contribution is cutting negative transfer 0.34→0.06 at 0.83 sign-prediction accuracy. Read it if you care about evidence-gated memory for self-improving research agents; skip if you want a new world-model architecture.

## 核心贡献

- **提出"检索 ≠ 迁移"原则**：在源模型上验证过的优化策略，对目标模型而言最多只是一个假设，必须经过目标端实验验证才能成为可复用知识。这是对 AI Scientist / SWE-agent 一类研究智能体"机会主义复用经验"的直接反驳。
- **Optimization Fingerprint 与 IRG**：用一组共享的、推理期、可逆、带剂量（dose）的语义探针（动作幅度缩放、上下文保留、首帧锚定、采样噪声等）测量成对响应雅可比 J、其不确定性 Σ 与诱导度量 G = JᵀWJ，构成跨骨干可比的"优化指纹"；IRG 用不确定性加权距离 + 支撑集不匹配惩罚定义"优化相似性"，而非架构相似性。
- **六项迁移证书 + 冻结验证器**：证书检查编译可行性、支撑重叠、有效证据量 N_eff、响应对齐误差 e_align、邻居符号一致性、以及校准后效应下界 L_a 是否超过冷启动增益；证书只决定"是否优先做这个实验"，从不认定效果成立。
- **差分修复契约（differential repair contract）**：每次修复记录的是成对变化向量 Δy（主效用 + 受保护指标违规量），而不是单一分数；主指标涨幅不能抵消受保护坐标的回退，回退被显式存为"边界注记"。
- **WMOM / IEM 记忆 + 碰撞驱动的探针演化**：正向、零效、有害、交互四类结果全部落库；当 IRG 中相邻的两个 campaign 对同一技能给出统计上相反的验证效果（IRG collision），系统认为当前探针基缺少一个迁移相关的区分维度，据此按 regret/cost 比生成新探针，并要求在留出 campaign 上复现才准进入下一版探针基。

## Key Contributions

- **A principled stance**: retrieval ranks experiments; only target-side experiments create knowledge. Compatibility certificates explicitly *abstain* rather than forcing a transfer.
- **Inference-time characterization**: fingerprints are built from paired, seed-matched, dose-calibrated probe responses that touch neither parameters nor the objective, so they are cheap (8–11 GPU-h/campaign per Table 12) and comparable across heterogeneous backbones.
- **Effect-vector memory (IEM inside WMOM)**: stores skill descriptors (hook type, transformation, dose, schedule, preconditions, invariants), effect vectors with CIs, cost, artifact lineage, plus null/harmful outcomes and measured skill *interactions* as separate records.
- **Typed compilation with receipts**: an intervention is a typed descriptor, not a free-form patch; compilation fails loudly rather than silently approximating an unavailable mechanism, and every executed change carries a runtime receipt.
- **Counterexample-driven probe evolution**: failed transfers between IRG-adjacent campaigns become evidence that the diagnostic basis is wrong, gated by a frozen regression battery and held-out replication.

## Methodology

VERDI runs a four-stage continual loop: **Characterize → Retrieve → Validate → Accumulate**.

*Characterize.* A campaign is `C = (M, D, G, V, B)` — target model, permitted data, goal (primary utility U_G, validity constraints v_j, threshold τ_G), a verifier frozen for the duration, and a nominal allowance. Each probe `q` applies a reversible inference-only perturbation at dose α; paired central differences over matched contexts and seeds give the response Jacobian `J[k,q] = E[(z_k(M^{q,α}) − z_k(M))/α]` with bootstrap covariance Σ. The fingerprint is `φ = (J, G, Σ)` with `G = JᵀWJ`, W standardizing outcome scale and downweighting uncertain coordinates. Probe paths must be inference-only, reversible, seed-paired, and measurable at ≥2 nonzero doses; training losses and data reweighting are explicitly excluded as non-dose-comparable.

*Retrieve.* IRG distance `d²_IRG = (r_x − r̃_i)ᵀ W_dist (r_x − r̃_i) + λ_c d²_cap` operates on transported repair coordinates `r = vec(W^{1/2} J)`, with the metric frozen from a target-independent archive before evaluation. Candidate effect is a proximity/evidence/context-weighted average of source effects (Eq. 6), producing *ranked hypotheses*, not facts.

*Validate.* Progressive fidelity: a low-cost 512-step paired screen sets search order but cannot create a record; promoted candidates get the official 50-step evaluator plus held-out contexts, longer horizons, and multi-seed confirmation. A trial is positive only when the paired utility improvement's lower confidence bound clears τ_G *and* every validity gate passes. There is an explicit H/I/E separation (hypothesis / implementation / evaluation planes) so the loop that proposes cannot also settle.

*Accumulate.* Settled effects, interactions, and source→target transfer relations are written to WMOM. A collision `d_IRG(φ_x, φ_y) ≤ ε` with `LCB[−r_x(a)·r_y(a)] > 0` opens a probe-evolution work order; a new probe enters the next basis version only if `(Regret(Q) − Regret(Q ∪ {q}))/Cost(q)` is maximal and the benefit replicates held-out.

Evaluation uses ACWM-Phys (an internally built 8-environment suite: rigid contact, articulated motion, deformable memory, granular/fluid transport, target-conditioned control) plus WorldArena-style metrics on real backbones, with matched training steps, identical data, matched HP-trial allowance, and per-component cost accounting (C_train, C_search, C_first+, C_overhead).

## Results & Highlights

- **Held-out cross-backbone transfer (Table 4)**: certified reuse reaches the first strict-gate positive in **3.6 trials / 96 GPU-h** vs **11.2 trials / 312 GPU-h** for cold start (archive cleared) and **4.1 trials / 118 GPU-h** for unconditional reuse. The abstract's "68% search cost, 69% GPU cost" is computed against *cold start*; against the honest baseline (unconditional reuse) the deltas are only −12% trials and −19% GPU-h. The genuine win is **negative transfer 0.34 → 0.06 (−5.7×)** with protected-violation rate 0.03.
- **Fingerprint informativeness (Table 6)**: full IRG fingerprint predicts repair-effect sign on held-out campaign pairs at **0.83**, vs 0.71 for raw response + flat Euclidean distance, 0.65 for static probe covariates, and 0.58 for environment labels — so the weighted induced metric + dose calibration buys +0.12 over the naive interventional representation.
- **Selection-source ablation (Table 14)**: full fingerprint 0.83 sign / 3.6 trials / 0.06 neg-transfer beats learned model embedding (0.74 / 4.6 / 0.17), repair-text embedding (0.62 / 5.8 / 0.24), frequency prior (0.57 / 5.2 / 0.29) and random (0.50 / 8.7 / 0.38).
- **Agent-level controls at matched 200 GPU-h (Table 7)**: VERDI 3.6 trials / 96 GPU-h / 0.06 neg-transfer vs a bare LLM agent with the same repair pool at 5.9 / 187 / 0.31 and a generic research-agent loop at 6.4 / 213 / 0.36 — i.e. unconstrained agents are only ~1.6–1.8× slower to a first positive but 5–6× worse on negative transfer. Ablating certificates alone: 4.3 / 128 / 0.21; ablating IRG: 4.8 / 142 / 0.18.
- **Human reference**: two experienced video-world-model engineers reached first-positive on Ctrl-World in **3.9 trials with 0.05 protected violation** — comparable to VERDI — but needed ~14 h expert wall-clock per campaign and, when moved to Cosmos-Predict 2, restarted at **10.8 trials** vs VERDI's 3.6. Commendably, the authors state they do *not* claim superhuman repair ability.
- **Ctrl-World repair (Tables 2/16/17)**: motion smoothness **68.75 → 81.53**, depth accuracy 93.28 → 96.16, flow 26.54 → 30.35, aesthetic quality 32.75 → 36.12, LPIPS 0.1528 → 0.1432, FID 54.01 → 51.71 — while background consistency drops 86.37 → 85.11, dynamic degree 45.32 → 43.78, subject consistency 84.31 → 83.35, and PSNR is flat (20.5099 → 20.4921). Dynamic degree exceeds tolerance on the open-drawer group, so the confirmed claim is narrowed rather than averaged away.
- **Cosmos-Predict 2 + DINOv2 REP guidance (Table 18)**: depth 95.35 → 97.05, motion smoothness 78.93 → 81.31, flow 29.55 → 30.70, but background consistency **85.35 → 82.73** (−2.62) and subject consistency 83.07 → 81.79.
- **ACWM-Phys (Table 15)**: largest gains are robot_arm **+1.08 dB PSNR**, cloth_move +0.94, pour_water +0.85, push_sand +0.64; the other four environments are +0.15, +0.06, +0.03, +0.02 dB — essentially indistinguishable from noise, yet all eight rows are marked status "formal".
- **Video-quality → action-quality (Tables 19/20)**: the Cosmos3 self-forcing repair lifts closed-loop success on peg insertion **28% → 52% (+24 pts, n=50)**, drawer opening 41% → 59%, pick-and-place 62% → 74%; on a real Franka, drawer opening 35% → 55% (n=20). Expert-action L2 error 0.084 → 0.061, long-horizon success decay −34 pts → −22 pts. Gains grow with horizon, consistent with a repair aimed at compounding rollout error.
- **Validity gates are load-bearing (Table 9)**: removing the negative-correlation gates raises headline primary Δ from **+12.78 to +14.92** while protected violations jump **0.03 → 0.41 (13×)**; re-calibrating all tolerances at 0.5× and 2.0× flips no campaign verdict.
- **Control-plane audit under 30 planted faults + 10 genuine improvements (Table 10)**: full control plane false-conclusion rate **0.07** vs **0.52** for direct settlement (7.4× reduction), at +6.2 GPU-h/episode and a modest missed-genuine increase (0.10 vs 0.04). Removing H/I/E separation quadruples false conclusions to 0.28.
- **Screening (Table 11)**: the cheap screen retains **94%** of exhaustively confirmed repairs at 205 vs 480 GPU-h (43% of cost); a kNN selector matches UCB (0.94, 214 GPU-h) and even random-over-screened beats no screening — the authors explicitly say the acquisition function is *not* the source of the gains.
- **Probe evolution (§4.4, Table 3)**: an action-dimension anisotropy probe is local in 5/8 environments; a cloth_move counterexample with locality residual 3.88 triggers an action-embedding temporal-mix successor that drops the residual to 0.0196 — but on reacher the successor is worse (0.7032), so the library keeps both conditionally, raising local-chart coverage to 6/8. Collision-driven + regret-gated growth: regret 0.21 / calibration error 0.08 with 7 probes, vs fixed canonical frame 0.29 / 0.14 (4 probes) and free growth 0.31 / 0.19 (14 probes) — ungated growth triples the frame with *no* regret reduction.
- **RoboCoin adaptation (§C.6)**: action conditioning improves held-out LPIPS by 0.029 and SSIM by 0.039 over a no-action control; multi-view cross-view subject-consistency variance is 0.029 vs 0.083 for independent per-view adaptation.
- **Cost ledger (Table 12)**: fingerprinting is 8–11 GPU-h/campaign; overhead 96/173/130 GPU-h for Ctrl-World / Cosmos-Predict 2 / Cosmos-Predict 2.5 with net savings +96/+139/+132. Confirmation, not measurement, dominates overhead.

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty (high-moderate).** The *framing* — "retrieval is not transfer", certificates with explicit abstention, failed transfers as evidence that the representation is wrong rather than that the recipe is bad — is genuinely new relative to AI Scientist / Agent Laboratory / AlphaEvolve, which reuse experience opportunistically. The mechanical parts (paired finite-difference Jacobians, uncertainty-weighted Mahalanobis distance, UCB-over-cost, Shapley-style factorial credit) are standard; the contribution is the protocol, not the math.

**Rigor (mixed, with real strengths).** The planted-fault audit (Table 10) is the best part of the paper and is rare in agentic-research work: 30 traps across five explicit failure modes with a false-conclusion metric. The gate-ablation showing they *deliberately keep the smaller +12.78 gain over the ungated +14.92* is intellectually honest and should be copied by others. Threshold sensitivity at 0.5×/2.0× flipping no verdict is a good robustness check. Against that:

- **The headline is baselined against a strawman.** "68% search cost / 69% GPU cost" compares to *cold start with the archive cleared* — a system with no memory at all. The interesting comparison, unconditional reuse, gives only 4.1→3.6 trials. The abstract does not disclose this, and a reader skimming will over-read the efficiency claim.
- **No confidence intervals on the headline tables.** Table 4, Table 7, and Table 14 report bare point estimates over "three backbones and three seeds". A 4.1→3.6 trials difference across 9 runs is almost certainly within noise; only the negative-transfer gap (0.34→0.06) is plausibly outside it. For a paper whose entire thesis is "don't accept effects without confidence bounds", the absence of error bars on its own headline is a self-inflicted wound.
- **ACWM-Phys is the authors' own suite and is not public.** Four of eight environments show PSNR deltas of +0.02 to +0.15 dB — within seed noise by any reasonable standard — yet Table 15 marks all eight rows "formal", while §4.2 warns that "these numbers do not imply that all environments have the same confirmation strength" and the evidence column shows heterogeneous support (`2 receipts` vs `1 checkpoint` vs `800 steps` vs `metric pass` for pour_water). The status column and the prose disagree; by the paper's own contract, pour_water should not read as formal.
- **Circularity risk in the evaluator.** The validity gates and outcome schema are instantiated from WorldArena, and the fingerprint's weighting W is defined over the same outcome schema used by the goal. The verifier is frozen *within* a campaign, which prevents drift, but nothing prevents the whole apparatus from optimizing WorldArena-shaped quantities. There is no independent held-out metric family.
- **Single-lab archive.** The authors admit it: distances, abstention rates, and collision statistics derive from one laboratory's campaigns, and Table 5 lists the archive inventory only at a "role/status" level with fold IDs "recorded in the ledger" — i.e. not in the paper. The LOBO folds cannot be audited from the manuscript.
- **RoboCoin is one dataset family and one base model**, so the embodiment-adaptation claim in the abstract is thinner than it sounds.
- **Manuscript hygiene red flags.** The related-work section contains two verbatim duplicated sentence pairs ("Agent Laboratory... SWE-agent..." and the CORE-Bench/PaperBench sentence repeated back-to-back), and Figure 1 still contains an untouched template bar chart with Chinese placeholder labels (销售/财务/市场营销/HR — "Sales/Finance/Marketing/HR"). Several cited works are 2026-dated and hard to verify (Karpathy's autoresearch loop, RoboClaw, ENPIRE, AutoResearchClaw). None of this invalidates the experiments, but it signals a rushed submission for a paper preaching receipts and provenance.

**Reproducibility (weak as submitted).** Anonymized project page, no code link, an internal benchmark, "detailed budgets, thresholds, seeds, and evaluator contracts are retained with the supplementary experiment artifacts" that are not in the PDF, and campaign-level fold ledgers kept out of band. Someone wanting to rebuild VERDI has the equations but not the probe library, the repair registry, or ACWM-Phys.

**Significance (high if it holds).** If evidence-licensed memory really cuts negative transfer ~6× at negligible speed cost, that is exactly the missing safeguard for autonomous research loops, and the failure taxonomy + planted-fault audit is reusable methodology independent of world models.

## Strengths

- The core principle is stated crisply and the architecture actually implements it: retrieval only reorders experiments, never writes a claim.
- Effect vectors with explicit protected-coordinate regressions (motion smoothness up, dynamic degree down) instead of a favorable scalar average — and the regressions are stored as boundary annotations that constrain future retrieval.
- Null and harmful outcomes are first-class memory entries; skill *interactions* are measured, not inferred from marginals.
- Planted-fault control-plane audit with a false-conclusion metric, plus the human-expert reference arm, plus the admission that the selector (UCB vs kNN vs random) is not where the gains come from.
- Real downstream validation: video-quality repairs translate into +12 to +24 pt closed-loop manipulation success in sim and +15/+20 pts on a physical Franka.
- Limitations section names the actual weaknesses (local fingerprints, single-lab archive, single RoboCoin family, no causal identification from H/I/E controls).

## Limitations

- Guarantees are conditional on a complete goal contract and a valid evaluator; a bad verifier makes the whole certificate chain vacuous, and the paper offers no test for verifier validity.
- Fingerprints are local and must be recomputed after major architectural changes — unclear how "major" is detected in practice.
- Probe evolution is fail-closed in the current frozen-selector replay, so §4.4 explicitly cannot claim improved repair accuracy from evolved probes; the mechanism is demonstrated but not yet shown to pay off end-to-end.
- Only two named source backbones in the transfer study ("representative, not exhaustive"), three backbones × three seeds for all agent-level numbers.
- Probe library excludes training-loss and data-reweighting interventions by construction, so any failure mode that only manifests through the training objective is outside the geometry.
- Overhead is nontrivial (+6.2 GPU-h per audited episode; 96–173 GPU-h per campaign), justified only because cold-start campaigns are expensive.

## Relevance

这篇论文与你（用户）对自演化研究循环（auto-research loop）、LLM-as-a-judge 评审可信度、以及 agent 记忆机制的关注高度契合：它给出了一套可直接迁移到任何"自动优化 campaign"上的证据准入协议——冻结验证器、六项迁移证书、H/I/E 提议/实现/评估三权分立、以及把 null 与 harmful 结果同等入库的记忆设计。其中的 planted-fault 审计（30 个人造陷阱 + 10 个真改进，false-conclusion 0.52 → 0.07）尤其值得借鉴，可以直接用作你自己 judge / optimize 循环的可信度体检方法。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- What happens to the 3.6 vs 4.1 trials gap with proper confidence intervals over more than three backbones and three seeds? Is the efficiency claim over unconditional reuse statistically real at all, or is negative-transfer reduction the only defensible result?
- The certificate's `L_a(φ_t) > δ_G` term (calibrated lower bound beating cold start) carries the second-largest ablation load (0.24). How is δ_G calibrated when the archive is small, and does the certificate degrade gracefully or just abstain on everything early in a system's life?
- Coverage–risk curves are promised in the supplementary but not shown: what fraction of retrievals actually abstain, and does abstention rate correlate with backbone novelty in a way that makes VERDI useless exactly when a genuinely new architecture appears?
- Can the outcome schema be decoupled from WorldArena? An independent metric family (e.g. physics-simulator-grounded rather than perceptual) would test whether the fingerprint measures optimization behavior or WorldArena-specific behavior.
- IRG collisions are defined only for *supported* pairs with comparable data support. How often are collisions unresolvable because the two campaigns differ in support rather than in probe basis, and does the probe-evolution loop misfire in that regime?
- Does the ACWM-Phys "formal" status column survive a re-run — specifically, do the four sub-0.2 dB environments replicate at all, and would the paper's own validity gates have rejected them under a stricter τ?
