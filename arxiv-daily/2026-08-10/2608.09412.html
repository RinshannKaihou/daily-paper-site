Methodology, distilled

Qwen3-8B · bf16 · H200 · greedy decode · one deterministic result per cell

KVDiagnosis pairs each of 59,800 supported compression runs against a same-source FullCache control, then extracts only the C→W rows (correct under full cache, wrong after compression) — 12,520 of them over 2,094 unique sources.

The ordering is deliberate: complete the full run matrix first, then draw each cell's failures from its own results, so no compressor defines another's test set. Each failure is tagged with ten mechanism-valid diagnostics across four axes — cache (evidence retention / coverage), likelihood (ΔNLL, GPR, KL, gold-rank shift), access (EAM / EAR / NEAE), and decoding.

Crucially, ERR/ECov are recorded N/A — never 1.0 for channel-pruning (ThinK) and quantization (QuantizedCache), where "50% retention" means fewer key channels or 4-bit tensors, not half the token positions. A controlled 4× evidence-attention boost vs. sham and C→C safety controls converts the low-EAR diagnosis from correlation toward causation.

  1. Run the matrix8 verified compressors × 3 retention tiers × 2,600 sources, each FullCache-paired.
  2. Extract C→W rows12,520 failures drawn per-cell after the matrix completes.
  3. Attach diagnosticsTen metrics, four applicability states; N/A enforced, never imputed.
  4. Categorize & interveneSix predeclared rules + one causal boost (4× vs sham).

Strengths & limitations

▲ Strengths7

  • Selection-bias-free ordering. Complete the matrix, then draw each cell's own C→W rows — stated precisely enough to adopt.
  • Mandatory N/A semantics. No imputation, no zeros; empty intersections are N/A. Solves a real field-wide problem.
  • Layer–KV-head slot averaging in ERR/ECov blocks the fake near-perfect coverage a cross-head union would create.
  • Causal triad (intervention / sham / C→C safety) with paired McNemar + bootstrap, not an unpaired t-test.
  • PyramidKV exclusion audit — published with hashes & job-distribution evidence (84.2% distinct nodes). Rare negative-result writeup.
  • Honest cross-model framing: the procedure generalizes; magnitudes do not.
  • Refuses composite scoring — consistent with its own argument against cross-mechanism ranking.

▽ Limitations8

  • Single primary model. All category shares, AUROCs, traces, and the intervention are Qwen3-8B-only.
  • Tiny QA denominators. 59 Qasper / 148 HotpotQA FullCache-correct sources drive highlighted percentages — incl. the one Jaccard=0.70 exception.
  • Non-official scorer. Substring containment at a 29.5-pt Qasper baseline makes C→W labels noisy.
  • Partial diagnostic availability. EAR exists on 56.2%, Top-50 on 67.1%; 15.6% are Conflicting/Ambiguous.
  • No efficiency axis. Latency, throughput, bytes, energy all out of scope; cross-mechanism memory normalization impossible.
  • Unswept thresholds. τ=0.5, the 0.50/0.90 boundaries, ΔNLL≥1, EAR<0.50 — none sensitivity-tested; the 63.2% headline is threshold-defined.
  • Coarse setting grid. ThinK's 97.7→0.0 and QuantizedCache's 4b→2b collapse both sit inside an unsampled interval.
  • One intervention magnitude, one category. Only log-4 / low-EAR tested; five of six categories get no causal probe.

Questions for further exploration

  1. Does the 29.2% repair rate survive a magnitude sweep — and where does C→C degradation finally exceed C→W repair?
  2. If ΔNLL (0.853) outpredicts ECov (0.747), is the expensive evidence-alignment pipeline even needed for triage?
  3. The 2,126 structural-drift rows are the biggest unexplained bucket — could per-layer reconstruction error make "fidelity unknown" measurable?
  4. Why is quantization drift so model-specific (2.153 vs 0.021) — outliers, RoPE, group_size, or sheer 24B-vs-8B capacity?
  5. Could the SnapKV–TOVA disjointness support a per-source compressor router, and how much of the 12,520-row failure set is recoverable?
  6. Is ThinK's 97.7 → 0.0 collapse a phase transition or a kvpress window_size=32 artifact under Qwen3's GQA geometry?
  7. How does the protocol extend to reasoning-time compression, where "evidence" is generated chain-of-thought with no ground-truth support map?
