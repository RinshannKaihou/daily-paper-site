A ~14-orders-of-magnitude gradient vanishing, measured within ~10 optimizer steps of the collapse transition. Appendix masks values below 10⁻¹⁰ for readability.

Why this matters

Two loggable early-warning signals — attention amount s and contraction factor c — plus a frequency-loss floor test for detecting collapse in deep pretraining stacks.

Diagnostic, not prescriptive: no fix is proposed. But s and c are quantities any deep stack could log.

Critical edge

Every contraction-factor measurement is taken during or after the transition — so c crossing 1 may be a co-symptom, not the cause, and the diagnostics are never shown to predict the jump.

01Methodology

One scalar state variable, two theorems, one falsifiable intervention.

The state variable is token similarity t_sim(X)=‖Π₁X‖²_F/‖X‖²_F — equal to 1 iff every token row is identical (exact rank collapse). The Post-Norm block analyzed is RMS→Attn→RMS→SwiGLU. Forward: the random causal attention matrix P is replaced by the deterministic prefix-averaging matrix Cn with (Cn)i,j=1/i, yielding a closed-form one-step similarity increment Δattn that is strictly positive and peaks near t_sim≈½; the SwiGLU branch adds a negative but tiny ΔFFN bounded by ≈0.011. A single scalar "amount of attention" s=nd²σ²_W/‖X₁‖²_F unifies both norms — depth-constant for Post-Norm (fast saturation), O(1/l) for Pre-Norm. Backward: RMSNorm's Jacobian multiplies both the skip and the sublayer gradient in Post-Norm, giving a per-sublayer contraction c=α√d/‖y‖₂ that compounds geometrically toward layer 1 once ‖y‖²/d > α². Validated on a 48-layer / 180M-param Llama-2-style model trained on C4 (seq 2048, effective batch 256, AdamW, 40k steps, LR swept 6e-4 → 1.8e-3).

03Strengths & Limitations

Strengths

  • The P − αCn intervention is real mechanistic evidence — not curve-fitting. Theory predicts the (1−α)² suppression; experiment observes it monotonically.
  • Theorem 3.8 converts a qualitative failure into a checkable number: collapsed loss floor = entropy of the label frequency distribution.
  • Reports its own disconfirming detail (layer-1 attention where c does not drop) instead of burying it.
  • Strong controls: the non-collapsing LR 6e-4 run + a second collapsing seed isolate collapse from training-in-general.
  • The "amount of attention" scalar s economically unifies the Pre-/Post-Norm gap in one quantity.

Limitations

  • No explanation of the transition trigger — why similarity and loss jump at one specific step. Acknowledged as future work.
  • Purely diagnostic: no mitigation, fix, or architectural proposal.
  • Headline forward figure agreement is reported only qualitatively ("captures sign and scale") — no fit error or R² where it matters most.
  • The equal-correlation closure is never validated numerically, despite being questionable under causal masking.
  • Single-head theory, no learned RMSNorm gain, no ε — but the trained models have all three.
  • One architecture (48L / 180M), one dataset (C4); no depth/width sweep; no Pre-Norm training-time backward diagnostics.
  • No code release identified in the paper.

04Open Questions

Where the mechanistic story stops — and what would push it further.

Q1 · PREDICTION

Do the diagnostics have predictive power? Does c(y,α) or the residual-norm growth rate drift measurably in the hundreds of steps before the transition — enabling forecast rather than post-hoc explanation?

Q2 · TRIGGER

What actually fires the abrupt jump? Candidates: attention-entropy collapse (Zhai 2023), an Adam second-moment blow-up in W_O/W_2, or an edge-of-stability bifurcation as effective LR crosses a threshold.

Q3 · SCALING

How does the collapse LR threshold scale with depth/width? Does constant-s predict a threshold LR ∝ 1/Ldepth? Does the 48-layer bound (between 6e-4 and 8e-4) move as predicted at 24 or 96 layers?

Q4 · GAIN

Does the learned RMSNorm gain fight or accelerate the contraction? The theory drops it — tracking its per-layer norm trajectory across the transition would test whether it is a failed compensator.

Q5 · CLOSURE

Is the equal-correlation closure defensible under causal masking? Measuring position-dependent structure of X₁X₁ᵀ (early vs late positions) would localize the Figure-1 mismatch.

Q6 · GENERALITY

Does the same c-crosses-1 story explain Pre-Norm loss spikes or collapse in deep vision Transformers — or is whole-path Jacobian multiplication genuinely Post-Norm-specific?
