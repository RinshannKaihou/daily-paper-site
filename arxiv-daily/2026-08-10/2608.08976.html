01 Methodology

Each subject is scored as a deviation from healthy controls in a frozen representation space. PD labels are reserved for evaluation — the reference is the training-control set alone, so a subject can be scored in isolation.

  • Voice — synthetic-dysarthria CAA. Frozen HuBERT-base (layer 8, 768-d) encodes the first 12 s of 16 kHz mono speech. Two DSP degradations reproduce hypokinetic dysarthria: a phase-vocoder TimeStretch ρ=0.82 (22% slow-down, pitch preserved) and envelope-modulated breathy phonation (Gaussian aspiration noise, ×2.5). The contrastive-activation-addition direction d = mean(ϕ(degraded)) − mean(ϕ(original)) is built per degradation, normalized by control-set σ, and summed into a single score.
  • Face — direction-free kNN anomaly. 32 frames per clip through a frozen FER2013 face-expression ViT (MediaPipe FaceMesh crop, 1.6× bbox, 224²); the video embedding is the mean CLS token. A kNN-anomaly score (k=15) against 36 control embeddings recovers the signal where CAA is anti-aligned.
  • Fusion — deliberately equal-weight. Per-modality scores are σ-scaled (leave-one-out for face) and added. Any data-driven weighting would consult PD labels and breach the protocol.

03 Strengths

  • Truly label-free. No PD label touches any fit, so the method generalizes to new populations without relabeling and is encoder-agnostic.
  • Mechanistically interpretable. The alignment principle is a falsifiable, measurable diagnostic for when synthetic-degradation steering will work.
  • Honestly self-audited. Explicit overfitting audit separates a cleanly-transferring voice branch from a potentially-optimistic face branch.
  • Clinically grounded degradation design. Time-stretch and breathy phonation faithfully reproduce the two canonical markers of hypokinetic dysarthria.
  • Integrity audits beyond AUROC — confound correlations, permutation, split-half consistency all pass.

04 Limitations

  • Small dataset. 72 train (36/36) and 157 test videos (20 PD / 137 ctrl); wide CIs (fusion [0.70, 0.89]).
  • Face-encoder not recoverable on dev. The +0.28 test margin needs test labels; face & fused AUROC flagged optimistic.
  • Voice partly inflated by a dev→test speaking-rate gap (−0.37 → −0.88 SD) — a confound for any motor-signal method.
  • Alignment principle is explanatory only — it consumes labels post-hoc, not a usable label-free selection rule.
  • No external / clinical-cohort validation. Not SOTA in absolute AUROC (below 0.86–0.92 supervised). Rule-out only.

05 Questions for Further Exploration

  1. Can a face encoder with cos > 0 be found — e.g. a facial-motion / blink-rate-sensitive model instead of static-emotion FER2013 — so the face branch uses the same activation-steering primitive as voice?
  2. Does the alignment principle generalize beyond PD — can synthetic CAA directions predict detection success for other motor-speech or neurodegenerative conditions?
  3. How robust is voice CAA to language / accent / device shift, given HuBERT was pretrained on LibriSpeech (English read speech)?
  4. Would a pre-registered, externally-validated face-encoder choice reproduce the 0.751 AUROC, or is the face branch partly a test-set artifact?
  5. Can the two synthetic directions (slow, breathy) be decomposed to attribute each one's share of the +0.37 cosine — a per-direction ablation is not reported?
  6. Given PPV ≈ 0.34 at 13% prevalence, what prevalence threshold makes the screen useful as rule-out triage?
