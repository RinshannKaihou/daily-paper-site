> **一句话总结：** DUD 通过噪声诱导的因果干预将 LLM 残差流更新显式解耦为 FFN（参数化记忆）与 Attention（上下文路由）两条流，构造 2L 维双流动态画像训练轻量 MLP 探针，在 HaluEval/SQuAD/TriviaQA/HotpotQA 四个基准上 AUROC 全面超过 Semantic Entropy、SAPLMA、ICR Probe 等 SOTA，LLaMA-3.1-8B 上 HaluEval 达 0.949，并在跨数据集迁移下性能衰减 <5%。

> **TL;DR:** DUD reframes LLM uncertainty quantification as active mechanistic diagnosis: it injects Gaussian noise into embeddings and performs module-specific causal patching to independently restore FFN vs. Attention activations layer-by-layer, producing a 2L-dimensional dual-stream feature that a small MLP probe turns into a faithfulness score. Across LLaMA-3.1-8B, Qwen2.5-7B and Gemma-2-9B on HaluEval, SQuAD, TriviaQA and HotpotQA, DUD-Probe beats the strongest baseline (ICR Probe) by up to +17.8 AUROC points on LLaMA HaluEval (0.9487 vs. 0.7703) and degrades less than 5% under cross-dataset transfer where SAPLMA/SEP frequently fall below 0.6.

## 核心贡献

- **首个解耦式因果 UQ 框架**：首次将 FFN（参数化记忆）与 MHSA（上下文路由）的因果贡献显式分离，证明聚合式残差流方法（如 ICR Probe）会抹平记忆-上下文冲突这一关键不确定性信号。
- **机制脆弱性现象发现**：通过严格因果追踪揭示不确定性表现为特定的时空脆弱模式——早期层 MHSA 路由不稳定、晚期层 FFN 出现"悬崖式坍缩"，为错误检测提供了物理基础。
- **置信度悖论的量化刻画**：通过四象限分析（Q1 Robust / Q2 Context Deviant / Q3 Memory Deviant / Q4 Dual Deviant）实证"Confidence Inversion"现象——错误率从 Q1 的 16.8% 升至 Q4 的 72.0%，而表面 logit 置信度无法追踪该风险。
- **跨架构/跨任务的稳健泛化**：在三种开源 LLM、四个认知任务上 AUROC 均达到 SOTA，跨数据集迁移性能衰减 <5%（对比基线下降 15-20%），证明捕获的是任务无关的内在机制签名。
- **轻量探针 + 完整算法开源**：4 层 MLP（2L→128→64→32→1）即能把解耦动态特征转化为可靠概率，提供完整的因果追踪与训练伪代码。

## Key Contributions

- **Decoupled causal framework**: First UQ framework that explicitly disentangles the causal contributions of FFN (parametric memory) and MHSA (contextual routing), showing internal module dynamics are a more faithful reliability proxy than output probabilities or aggregated hidden-state probes.
- **Mechanistic fragility discovery**: Causal tracing reveals uncertainty manifests as a "double-valley" spatiotemporal signature — early-layer Attention routing instability (layers 0-5) plus a precipitous late-layer FFN collapse (layers 20-28) — providing a physical grounding for error detection.
- **Quantified Confidence Paradox**: Quadrant analysis at layer 24 (SQuAD) demonstrates Confidence Inversion: error rates climb from 16.8% (Q1 Robust) to 72.0% (Q4 Dual Deviant), yet surface logits assign deceptively high confidence to stubborn errors and low confidence to "humble truths".
- **Robust cross-dataset/cross-architecture generalization**: SOTA AUROC across LLaMA-3.1-8B, Qwen2.5-7B, Gemma-2-9B and four cognitive tasks; out-of-distribution transfer degrades <5% versus 15-20% for baselines, indicating task-agnostic mechanistic signatures.
- **Lightweight, reproducible probe**: A 4-layer MLP (2L→128→64→32→1) trained with BCE on Z-score-normalized restoration scores; full pseudocode (Algorithms 1-2) and ~800-1000 A100-hours budget disclosed.

## Methodology

DUD reframes UQ from passive estimation to active mechanistic interrogation through three forward passes on the same input:

1. **Clean Run** — standard forward pass caches per-layer activations (hidden states `h`, MHSA outputs `a`, FFN outputs `m`) and the clean prediction probability `P_clean(y_t)`.

2. **Corrupted Run** — Gaussian noise `ε ~ N(0, ν)` is injected into input embeddings to induce an information bottleneck, producing `P_corr(y_t)`. The average probability drop defines the Total Effect: `TE = (1/T) Σ_t [P_clean(y_t) − P_corr(y_t)]`.

3. **Decoupled Patch Run** — for each layer `l` and module `ϕ ∈ {MHSA, FFN}`, the corrupted forward pass is re-run with only that module's activation replaced by the cached clean value. This isolates each module's causal contribution to restoring the correct prediction. The per-layer **Restoration Score** is `S_ϕ^(l) = (1/T) Σ_t [P_restored^(l,ϕ)(y_t) − P_corr(y_t)] / TE`.

Empirically `S` is predominantly negative (single-module restoration introduces state incoherence); magnitude is interpreted as a fragility proxy — `|S| ≈ 0` indicates mechanistic robustness, `|S| ≫ 0` signals a precarious internal equilibrium.

**Probe construction**: The 2L-dimensional feature vector `v = Norm(Concat(S_attn^(1..L), S_ffn^(1..L)))` feeds a 4-layer MLP (2L → 128 → 64 → 32 → 1) with LeakyReLU, BatchNorm, dropout 0.3, and a sigmoid head. Ground-truth labels come from ROUGE-L of the generation (`y=0` correct if `S_rouge ≥ τ`, else `y=1` uncertain); probe is trained with BCE under 5-fold cross-validation with identical splits across baselines.

The design rests on the mechanistic-interpretability premise that MHSA routes contextual information while FFNs store parametric key-value memory (Geva 2021; Meng 2022), so memory-context misalignment — the actual origin of epistemic uncertainty — is only visible when the two update streams are kept separate rather than aggregated into a single residual-stream signal.

## Results & Highlights

- **Main UQ (AUROC, Table 1)**: DUD-Probe wins all 12 model×dataset cells. On LLaMA-3.1-8B it reaches **0.9487 AUROC on HaluEval** vs. the strongest baseline ICR Probe at **0.7703** (+17.8 points absolute), 0.8959 on SQuAD (vs. SAPLMA 0.7007), 0.8579 on HotpotQA, 0.8031 on TriviaQA. Semantic Entropy is beaten by +17.4 on LLaMA HaluEval; on Qwen2.5-7B DUD beats ICR Probe by +5.0 on average.
- **Cross-dataset transfer (Figure 5)**: Train on TriviaQA, test on SQuAD → DUD **0.868 AUROC** vs. ICR **0.613** (+25.5 points); TriviaQA → HaluEval reaches **0.930**, approaching in-distribution performance. Overall OOD degradation is **<5%** vs. 15-20% drops for SAPLMA and SEP, which frequently fall below 0.6.
- **Calibration (ECE, Table 6, Qwen2.5-7B)**: DUD-Probe achieves **0.0039 ECE on HotpotQA**, orders of magnitude better than SEP (0.2879) and SAPLMA (0.1675); 0.0173 on SQuAD and 0.0425 on HaluEval, the lowest in every column.
- **Rejection efficiency (PRR, Table 7, Qwen2.5-7B)**: DUD-Probe reaches **0.6346 PRR on HaluEval** vs. ICR Probe 0.5003; it is the top method on all four datasets (0.5669 / 0.5187 / 0.5945 on SQuAD / HotpotQA / TriviaQA).
- **Robustness to labeling criterion (Table 2, Qwen2.5-7B)**: DUD stays best across Lenient (ROUGE-L>0.3), Standard (>0.5) and Strict (>0.7) thresholds — e.g., HaluEval 0.8528 / 0.8642 / 0.8135 — while SEP and SAPLMA fluctuate substantially, confirming features capture intrinsic correctness rather than lexical overlap.
- **Mechanistic ablation (Table 3, LLaMA-3.1-8B)**: Full dual-stream (0.9487 HaluEval) beats Attn-Only (0.9144) and FFN-Only (0.8968); removing Early Layers drops HaluEval from 0.95 to 0.85, confirming early-layer instability matters for context-heavy tasks.
- **Quadrant Confidence Paradox (Figure 4, SQuAD layer 24)**: Error rate rises from **16.8%** (Q1 Robust) → 61.7% (Q2 Context Deviant) → 28.0% (Q3 Memory Deviant) → **72.0%** (Q4 Dual Deviant), while mean logit confidence barely separates correct from incorrect within each quadrant (e.g., Q4 confidence 0.337 incorrect vs. 0.364 correct) — directly evidencing Confidence Inversion that surface metrics cannot detect.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: Genuine. Decoupling FFN vs. Attention contributions under noise-induced causal tracing is a sharper operationalization of "memory-context conflict" than aggregated probes (ICR Probe, SAPLMA), and the Confidence Inversion / four-quadrant framing is a memorable diagnostic contribution.
- **Rigor**: Solid on the whole — 3 architectures × 4 datasets, 5-fold cross-validation with matched splits, three labeling thresholds, two alternative correctness metrics (EM, BERTScore in Appendix G), and ECE/PRR not just AUROC. The quadrant analysis is the kind of mechanistic evidence that distinguishes the method from a black-box probe.
- **Reproducibility**: Strong on paper — pseudocode, prompts, hyperparameters, GPU-hours and funding all disclosed. However, **no public code/data release is mentioned**, and the noise level `ν` and ROUGE-L threshold `τ=0.5` are tuned empirically without a sensitivity sweep on `ν`, which is a non-trivial knob for the entire feature pipeline.
- **Significance**: Practically important for hallucination detection and selective generation on open-weight models; the framing of "internal mechanistic fragility orthogonal to surface confidence" is a useful conceptual lens.
- **Red flags / overclaiming**: (1) The near-perfect **0.9487 AUROC on LLaMA HaluEval** is strikingly high and may partly reflect that HaluEval generations are easier to separate via any hidden-state probe — the paper does not test on harder, more adversarial hallucination sets (e.g., TruthfulQA, HaluEval-WildBench). (2) Generalization is only cross-dataset within knowledge QA; there is **no evaluation on reasoning/math/code/multilingual** tasks, so "robust proxy for model faithfulness" is broader than the evidence supports. (3) The strongest baseline set omits recent token-level SAR variants and conformal-prediction UQ methods. (4) White-box access requirement (explicitly listed as a limitation) makes the method inapplicable to closed-source APIs like GPT-4/Claude, limiting deployment scope. (5) Multiple forward passes (clean + corrupted + 2L patched runs) impose a non-trivial latency cost the paper acknowledges but does not benchmark concretely.

## Strengths

- Clean, well-motivated mechanistic story tying FFN/MHSA functional specialization to epistemic uncertainty.
- Comprehensive evaluation: 3 models × 4 datasets, plus ECE, PRR, EM, BERTScore, layer ablations, threshold sensitivity, and scale robustness (Appendix H).
- Strong cross-dataset transfer (<5% degradation) supporting the claim of task-agnostic signatures.
- Honest limitations section calling out white-box requirement and inference latency; concrete future-work direction (distillation to lightweight detectors).
- The four-quadrant Confidence Inversion analysis is a genuinely illustrative diagnostic that explains *why* surface confidence fails.

## Limitations

- **White-box only**: requires internal activations and causal interventions; unusable on closed API models (GPT-4, Claude).
- **Latency overhead**: clean + corrupted + 2L patched forward passes per query is much heavier than logit-based methods; concrete wall-clock numbers are not reported.
- **Benchmark coverage**: evaluation restricted to knowledge QA / reading comprehension (HaluEval, SQuAD, TriviaQA, HotpotQA); no math reasoning, code generation, multilingual, or adversarial truthfulness benchmarks.
- **Tuning knobs under-explored**: noise level `ν` is a single empirically-tuned value with no sensitivity sweep; ROUGE-L labeling is itself a noisy proxy for correctness.
- **No code/release mentioned** at submission, reducing immediate reproducibility.
- The "double-valley" and "late-layer FFN collapse" patterns are Architecture-dependent (Gemma-2-9B often shows Attention, not FFN, as the dominant signal), slightly weakening the universality of the FFN-fragility narrative.

## Relevance

该论文属于大模型可靠性与可解释性方向，将机制可解释性（causal tracing）直接用于不确定性量化和幻觉检测，与对 LLM hallucination detection、selective generation 和 model faithfulness 评估的研究兴趣高度契合；其解耦式 FFN/Attention 因果追踪方法对设计白盒探针和可信生成管线有直接借鉴价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How does DUD perform on harder adversarial / reasoning benchmarks (TruthfulQA, GSM8K, HumanEval, MBPP) where errors are not primarily parametric-memory failures?
- What is the sensitivity of AUROC to the noise level `ν` and to the corruption site (embeddings vs. specific layer activations)?
- Can the 2L patched forward passes be amortized or distilled into a single-pass student probe without losing the cross-dataset generalization?
- Does the Confidence Inversion quadrant pattern persist under reinforcement-learning fine-tuning (RLHF/GRPO) that explicitly optimizes calibration?
- How does DUD interact with decoding-time methods (DoLa, Lookback Lens, SAR) — are the signals complementary or redundant, and can they be fused for a stronger UQ ensemble?
- Is the architecture-dependent module dominance (FFN for LLaMA, Attention for Qwen/Gemma) a stable property or an artifact of the chosen layer count / training data?
