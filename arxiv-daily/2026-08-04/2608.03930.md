> **一句话总结：** 作者提出 Logic-PPT，在自然语言预训练之前用 247 条形式逻辑推演规则（命题/词项/一阶逻辑）通过逆向链式生成下一推演步任务对 254M Qwen3 进行符号化预训练，在 100B FineWeb-Edu token 规模上以比随机初始化少 36B token 达到 80% 语言学任务准确率，最终平均准确率 87.4%（高出最强基线 Formal-PPT 7.1 个百分点），同时诱导出更模块化、更低秩、更谱集中的表示空间，使 Wanda 剪枝在 40% 稀疏度下相对下降仅 14.4%（PT 为 24.7%、Alg-PPT 为 31.6%）。

> **TL;DR:** Logic-PPT replaces narrow symbolic pre-pretraining (Dyck, sorting) with formal logical derivations built from 247 propositional/term/first-order inference schemata via backward chaining, then transfers the 220M Qwen3 backbone into a 100B-token FineWeb-Edu run. It hits 80% mean accuracy on Liu et al.'s 17 elemental linguistic tasks using 36B fewer tokens than the PT baseline, reaches 87.4% final mean accuracy vs. 80.3% for the strongest baseline Formal-PPT (+7.1 points), induces a persistent lower-rank, spectrally concentrated representation geometry, and under Wanda pruning degrades only 14.4% at 40% sparsity versus 24.7% for PT and 31.6% for Alg-PPT, matching dense PT performance at ≈33% sparsity.

## 核心贡献

- **首个以形式逻辑推演为预训练信号的工作**：相对于 Dyck（仅刻画括号嵌套）与排序/集合算法（仅刻画过程式序列变换），Logic-PPT 用同时绑定变量、连接量词与关系依赖、并在长上下文上组合谓词-论元结构的推演数据，覆盖更接近自然语言的表达能力。
- **形式化数据生成管线**：用逆向链式（backward chaining）从目标公式生成深度 L=10 的推演树，再用保持拓扑与规则序列的符号重命名（n=5 变体）扩充多样性，最后通过后序遍历展开为 K≤L 个下一推演步预测样本，构造出由 247 条 schema 驱动（49 命题 + 77 词项 + 121 一阶）的大规模数据集。
- **100B token 规模下的行为-机制-干预三位一体分析**：将先前仅在 <10B token 上观察的现象首次扩展到 100B token，分别从行为（BLiMP + 17 项 elementary 任务）、机制（CKA / RankMe / spectral decay / stable rank）、干预（Wanda 剪枝）三个视角系统刻画预训练动力学。
- **首次将 PPT 诱导的表示几何与压缩鲁棒性挂钩**：发现 Logic-PPT 在中、上层产生最低 RankMe 与最高 spectral decay 的"压缩寻求相"，并首次证明这种几何直接转化为剪枝鲁棒性——在 ≈33% 稀疏度下匹配稠密 PT 基线，40% 稀疏度下相对下降仅 14.4%（其他 PPT 平均 26.8%）。
- **表示重组的持久性与种子稳定性**：通过跨层 CKA、与 PT 的成对 CKA 显示 Logic-PPT 在中段层（4-9）产生与 PT 显著不同且更模块化的结构；3 个种子在 10B token 上 self-CKA 的 Pearson r > 0.99，证明结果非随机种子伪影。

## Key Contributions

- **First use of formal logical derivations as a pre-pretraining signal**: Argues Dyck captures only bracket nesting and procedural algorithms capture only sequence transformations, whereas formal derivations simultaneously bind variables, link quantifiers and relational dependencies, and compose predicate-argument structures over long contexts — closer to the expressive capacity of natural language.
- **A principled data-generation pipeline**: Backward chaining from a sampled goal builds derivation trees (max depth L=10); structure-preserving symbol renaming (n=5 variants per tree) prevents surface-overfitting; post-order traversal expands each tree into up to K≤L next-step prediction examples. The library has 247 inference-rule schemata (49 propositional, 77 term, 121 first-order).
- **Three-pronged analysis (behavioral / mechanistic / interventional) at 100B-token scale**: Extends prior pre-pretraining work — which generally stayed below 10B tokens — to a 100B FineWeb-Edu regime, using BLiMP + 17 elemental tasks for skill emergence, CKA/RankMe/spectral-decay/stable-rank for representation geometry, and Wanda pruning for an interventional test.
- **First link between PPT-induced geometry and compressibility**: Shows Logic-PPT uniquely drives the backbone into a low-rank, spectrally concentrated regime (lowest RankMe and highest spectral decay in mid/upper layers) and translates this into pruning resilience — matching dense PT at ≈33% sparsity and degrading only 14.4% at 40% sparsity versus a 26.8% average for the alternative symbolic methods.
- **Persistent reorganization and seed stability**: Layer-wise CKA shows Logic-PPT produces the clearest block structure with lowest boundary-layer similarity; pairwise CKA against PT shows a distinctive activation geometry concentrated in middle layers 4-9. Three random seeds at 10B tokens yield self-CKA Pearson r > 0.99, indicating the representational pattern is not a seed artifact.

## Methodology

Logic-PPT is a two-stage initialization framework:

**Stage 1 — Logic Pre-pretraining.** The model is trained exclusively on synthetic formal derivations. A library R of 247 inference-rule schemata spans propositional logic (truth-functional composition), term logic (Aristotelian categorical/syllogistic relations), and first-order logic (variables, predicates, universal/existential quantifiers). For each sampled goal φ, backward chaining (inspired by LogicTree, Wang et al. 2025) recursively expands leaves using applicable schemata for up to L=10 steps; the leaves become the premise set Γ and the root remains φ. Structure-preserving symbol renaming (n=5 injective variants per tree) preserves topology, rule sequence, and logical relations while diversifying surface symbols. Post-order traversal yields a leaf-to-root derivation D = ⟨α₁, …, αₙ⟩.

Each derivation step i becomes a next-step prediction example xᵢ = ⟨premises⟩ Γ ⟨derived⟩ α₁;…;αᵢ₋₁ ⟨goal⟩ φ ⟨next⟩ with target yᵢ = αᵢ, processed by a domain-specific character-level tokenizer. The loss is computed only over the target-step tokens. After Stage 1 the backbone parameters Θ^backbone_logic are retained while the symbolic embedding E*logic and head H*logic are discarded.

**Stage 2 — Natural Language Pretraining.** A fresh natural-language embedding E_lang and head H_lang (32K Mistral tokenizer vocabulary) are attached to the pretrained backbone, and all parameters are trained on ≈100B FineWeb-Edu tokens with a standard causal LM objective (sequence length 2048, batch 96, cosine schedule, peak LR 3e-4, 127,160 steps, BF16 on 4× NVIDIA GH200).

**Architecture.** 14-layer Qwen3 backbone, 220M transformer + 254M total parameters; hidden size 1024, 16 attention heads, 8 KV heads, Mistral 32K tokenizer.

**Baselines.** PT (random init, no symbolic PPT), Formal-PPT (Dyck + Shuffle-Dyck, Hu et al. 2025), Alg-PPT (Set + Sort + Union, Jiang et al. 2026). All PPT methods use ≈2.5B tokens balanced via round-robin sampling.

**Evaluation.** FineWeb-Edu held-out perplexity, BLiMP (Warstadt et al. 2020), and the 17 elemental tasks from Liu et al. (2026) that at least one PPT configuration solves to 80% within the 100B budget — covering string operations, morphology, reading comprehension, and composition.

## Results & Highlights

- **Skill acquisition speed (Figure 1A, Figure 3)**: Logic-PPT reaches the 80% mean-accuracy threshold on the 17 elemental tasks using **36B fewer natural-language tokens** than the PT baseline in the 100B-token run, and reaches it earlier than every other PPT configuration.
- **Final skill accuracy (§4.2)**: At the 108.6B-token checkpoint, mean accuracy across the 17 elemental tasks is **87.4% for Logic-PPT vs. 80.3% for Formal-PPT (+7.1 points), 77.3% for PT, and 76.8% for Alg-PPT**; on Morphology, Reading Comprehension, and Compositional tasks Logic-PPT beats the strongest competing condition by **5.5–6.7 accuracy points**.
- **Language-modeling perplexity (§4.1)**: Final FineWeb-Edu test perplexity is **11.183 for PT, 11.088 for Alg-PPT (0.85% reduction), and 11.074 for both Formal-PPT and Logic-PPT (0.97% reduction)** — the PPT advantage appears within the first 1B tokens and persists through training, though perplexity alone cannot discriminate between PPT variants.
- **Pruning robustness under Wanda (Figure 9, §6)**: At 40% sparsity, Logic-PPT mean elemental-task accuracy drops from 0.87 to **0.745, a relative drop of 14.4%**, compared with **24.7% for PT, 22.0% for Formal-PPT, and 31.6% for Alg-PPT** (26.8% average across alternative symbolic methods). At ≈33% sparsity Logic-PPT retains accuracy comparable to the dense PT baseline.
- **Representational geometry (§5.1)**: During the compression-seeking phase Logic-PPT produces the **lowest RankMe and highest spectral decay** in middle (layers 4-9) and upper (10-13) layer groups, and the clearest block-diagonal self-CKA structure with the lowest boundary-layer CKA; pairwise CKA against PT shows Logic-PPT is most dissimilar from PT precisely in the middle layers 4-9.
- **Weight-space dynamics (§5.2, Figure 8)**: Throughout the symbolic PPT stage, Logic-PPT's attention and MLP stable rank **continually decreases with no plateau**, whereas Formal-PPT and Alg-PPT drop only in the earliest steps and then flatten — indicating formal derivations impose a stronger and more persistent structural bias on the weights.
- **Seed stability (Appendix F, Table 4)**: Across three seeds trained for 10B tokens, mean Pearson correlation between vectorized off-diagonal self-CKA entries is **>0.99 for every model (PT 0.9955, Formal-PPT 0.9942, Alg-PPT 0.9941, Logic-PPT 0.9926)**, indicating the layer-wise representational organization is stable.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: Genuine. Framing formal derivations (with variables, quantifiers, predicate-argument structure over long contexts) as a richer pre-pretraining signal than Dyck or sorting is well-motivated, and the demonstration that PPT-induced low-rank geometry *translates into pruning robustness* is, as far as the paper claims, the first such link for symbolic pre-pretraining.
- **Rigor**: Mixed. The three-pronged behavioral/mechanistic/interventional design is exactly the right structure, the seed-stability control in Appendix F (Pearson r > 0.99 across 3 seeds at 10B tokens) addresses the single-seed concern partially, and the RankMe / spectral-decay / stable-rank machinery follows Li et al. (2025) closely. However, **the main 100B-token results rest on a single seed per configuration** — the authors themselves flag this as a limitation, and the headline 7.1-point delta over Formal-PPT comes with no reported variance, so its significance is unclear. The final-checkpoint perplexity differences between PPT variants (11.074 vs. 11.088) are within plausible seed noise.
- **Reproducibility**: Strong on paper. Architecture (14-layer Qwen3, 220M/254M), hyperparameters (LR 3e-4, batch 96, cosine, 127,160 steps, BF16), data (FineWeb-Edu ≈100B tokens), and the 247-schema library (Table 5 lists counts by rule family) are fully specified, and derivation samples are shown in Appendix I. The method builds on the publicly described LogicTree generator. **No public code or data release is mentioned**, which is a notable gap given the pipeline has many moving parts (backward chaining, unification, symbol renaming) where implementation details matter.
- **Significance**: Important as a mechanistic-interpretability contribution (PPT persistently reshapes mid-layer geometry and compressibility) more than as a practical recipe — at 254M parameters and English-only it is a proof of concept rather than a deployable initialization strategy. The connection between representational geometry and pruning resilience is the most transferable finding.
- **Red flags / overclaiming**: (1) The behavioral claim "outperforming existing symbolic PPT methods" leans on a **single-seed 7.1-point delta** with no error bars and no seed replication at 100B tokens — at this scale the headline gap could shrink. (2) **Perplexity, the standard pretraining metric, barely moves** (11.074 vs. 11.183 for PT, <1% reduction), so the practical LM-quality gain is marginal and the meaningful signal lives entirely in the 17 elemental tasks chosen by the 80%-reachability filter, which is a somewhat post-hoc task selection. (3) The 17 elemental tasks are themselves filtered to those at least one PPT method can reach 80% on — a selection that mechanically advantages PPT methods over PT and is not neutral. (4) The pruning-benefit narrative rests on Wanda only; no test of whether the compressibility gain survives **distillation, quantization, or SFT** is provided (explicitly listed as a limitation). (5) Scale is 254M, so all claims about "persistent structural reorganization throughout 100B tokens" describe a small-model regime that may not transfer to billion-parameter pretraining where representations are known to behave differently. (6) The 2.5B-token PPT budget is held equal across methods, but the **information density per token differs sharply** between formal derivations and Dyck/sorting, so a token-matched comparison may not be compute-matched in the relevant sense.

## Strengths

- Clean three-axis framing (behavioral / mechanistic / interventional) that elevates the work above a "yet another symbolic warm-up" paper.
- The formal-derivations data design is well-grounded: backward chaining guarantees validity, structure-preserving renaming controls for surface memorization, and the 247-schema library covers three logically distinct families.
- Mechanistic analysis is the strongest pillar — layer-grouped RankMe / spectral-decay trajectories cleanly reproduce Li et al.'s entropy-seeking → compression-seeking phases and pin Logic-PPT's advantage to the compression-seeking phase in mid/upper layers.
- The compressibility result is a concrete, useful downstream payoff that connects representation geometry to a practical efficiency intervention (Wanda pruning).
- Honest limitations section explicitly disclosing single-seed main runs, 254M scale, English-only coverage, and absence of SFT/downstream transfer evaluation.

## Limitations

- **Scale**: Experiments are at 254M parameters / 100B tokens on FineWeb-Edu only; whether the skill-acceleration and compressibility effects hold at billion-parameter scale and on non-English corpora is untested.
- **Single seed for the headline 100B-token runs**: Three-seed replication is only at 10B tokens and only for self-CKA, not for task accuracy or pruning metrics.
- **Evaluation scope**: No SFT, no downstream reasoning/QA/instruction-following benchmarks, no quantization or distillation stress tests — so the practical value of the induced geometry is asserted, not demonstrated, beyond pruning.
- **Task selection bias**: The 17 elemental tasks are filtered by an 80%-reachability rule that advantages PPT methods by construction.
- **Compute-matching**: All PPT methods are matched at ≈2.5B tokens, but token information density differs across symbolic regimes, leaving open whether Logic-PPT wins per unit of symbolic compute or per token.
- **No released code or data** at submission time.

## Relevance

与"LLM 隐藏状态与可解释性"方向高度相关：论文系统用 CKA / RankMe / spectral decay / stable rank 揭示预训练动力学中的 entropy-seeking→compression-seeking 相变，并将表示几何直接挂钩到剪枝鲁棒性，恰好对应表示几何、探针、机制可解释性的核心议题；同时也对"LLM 训练稳定性与可观测性"中的预训练动力学监控与"ML 鲁棒性与压缩"主题有直接参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the 7.1-point final-accuracy advantage over Formal-PPT survive multi-seed 100B-token replication, and what is the seed-level standard deviation on the elemental-task mean?
- How much of the pruning-resilience gain is specifically due to formal logic versus any pre-pretraining signal that drives low stable rank — i.e., is there a controlled comparison holding final RankMe constant across PPT variants?
- Do the Logic-PPT geometry and compressibility gains persist after supervised fine-tuning, instruction tuning, or quantization (INT8/FP8), where industrial deployment actually happens?
- At billion-parameter scale, does the compression-seeking-phase signature (RankMe drop, spectral-decay rise) still appear, and does it still correlate with Wanda pruning robustness?
- Would token-budget matching (≈2.5B tokens) survive a compute-matching control (e.g., equalized symbolic FLOPs or equalized unique derivation structures), given that formal-derivation tokens carry more information than Dyck tokens?
- Is the mid-layer (4-9) dissimilarity from PT causally responsible for the skill-acquisition gains, or merely correlated — testable via layer-targeted ablation or freezing during Stage 2?
- Can the 247-schema library be extended with second-order, modal, or probabilistic logic to cover additional linguistic phenomena (quantifier scope ambiguity, modality, intensionality) that first-order logic handles weakly?
