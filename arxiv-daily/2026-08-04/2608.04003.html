Agent Benchmark arXiv 2608.04003
Key Finding · Benchmark

Retained experience helps — but the same score gain can come from entirely different persistence pathways.

PAST-Bench is the first trajectory-level, performance-attribution benchmark for personal-agent "online self-evolution." It pairs 26 scenarios / 204 episodes with matched persistence-on/off controls and a trace-level Mechanism-Evidence score — decoupling "did the score go up" from "did it go up via the intended pathway."

§ 04

Strengths & limitations

Read side-by-side; the honesty is itself a strength.

▲ Strengths

  1. Clean attribution. Matched persistence-on/off with shared prompt/grader/seed isolates the persistence substrate.
  2. Mech-Evidence score exposes the "same Δ, different pathway" phenomenon invisible to one-shot scoring.
  3. Capability profiles reveal model-specific strength (GPT-5.4 broad vs. GLM-5.1 Update-heavy vs. Kimi K2.6 Memory-heavy).
  4. Principled taxonomy → 5 disjoint failure modes, one per loop stage, each independently ablatable.
  5. Intellectual honesty: explicit variance reporting, refrains from overclaiming the +0.02 gain.

▼ Limitations

  1. Synthetic, isolated families — no cross-family interference, long horizons, or human-authored scenarios.
  2. Only 26 / 204 — small vs. GAIA/AgentBench; Update's 7 families are thin.
  3. Hermes+ tuned on one model; regresses on DeepSeek-V4-Pro (+0.17→+0.15) and Claude Opus 4.6.
  4. Procedural is fragile: E2↔E3 tension unresolved; removing E2 raises Procedural Δ.
  5. Single LLM judge; Mech is consistency, not causal counterfactual.
§ 05

Verdict

A−Methodology · Novelty
A genuinely new attribution instrument whose value outlives its own scaffold. The matched on/off design + Mech score is the contribution; Hermes+ is honestly framed as a diagnostic, not a win. The honest variance reporting and the unresolved E2↔E3 tension are exactly the signals of a careful benchmark paper.
Read: methodology > result
§ 06

Open questions

Q1Long horizons & cross-family transfer. At 20+ episodes, or reusing experience across families — does interference dominate?
Q2Counterfactual Mech. Delete/replace the candidate artifact and re-measure — does it re-rank the frameworks?
Q3E2↔E3 tension. Can an adaptive router decide which substrate should own a given experience?
Q4Judge sensitivity. Re-running with GPT-5.4 or Claude Opus 4.6 as judge — do borderline cases shift?
Q5Recursive RSI ceiling. Do the five mechanisms saturate once the base model itself is being fine-tuned?
Q6Cost-matched ranking. Under matched token-cost (not task-count) — where does Agent-Zero actually land?
