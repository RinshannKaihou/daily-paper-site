Benchmark · AI4Research · LLM-as-Judge

Can LLMs design high-quality experiments?
A systematic benchmark for autonomous experimental design.

Zejun Liu · Jian Wu · Ru Peng · Yuliang Ji · Dongyuan Li · Renhe Jiang · Yue Zhang (corresponding)  — Westlake · Zhejiang · NJUST · Tokyo

SCOPE — a 300-paper, 19-domain, 6-dimension benchmark — finds seven mainstream LLMs average only 14.81 / 30 on autonomous experimental design. Low-level configuration (datasets, baselines, metrics) is the universal bottleneck, naive web search actively degrades planning, and the proposed OptED agentic workflow recovers much of the gap on six models.

2 Why this matters

Experimental design has been the under-evaluated middle child of AI4Research. SCOPE makes its failure modes diagnosable.

The six-dimension High-Level / Low-Level split turns "the model is bad at planning" into an actionable architectural lesson: grounding in concrete datasets, baselines and metrics — not high-level reasoning — is what future AI co-scientists must solve.

First end-to-end benchmark for the design stage
3 Critical edge
  • Judge
    Single-judge design. GPT-5.2 is the sole LLM-as-Judge — no inter-annotator agreement, no human spot-check, no variance on headline scores.
  • Confound
    Self-referential leakage. GPT-5.2 is both top-2 subject and the judge — a structural confound left unaddressed.
  • Baselines
    No head-to-head. OptED is only compared to CoT / CoT+Search prompting — not AI-Scientist, AgentLaboratory or AAAR-1.0.
  • Skew
    Domain bias. Corpus over-weights high-Github foundation / generative / multimodal papers; learning theory & causality sparse.

The diagnosis, in one line

Models can plan an experiment. They cannot ground one.

That single asymmetry is what SCOPE diagnoses and OptED partially fixes. It is also why unstructured search — which should, in theory, help grounding — instead imports noise and fabricated sources, hurting the dimension that mattered most.

Ablation: stage isolation alone already beats CoT+Search on all three ablated models; the Think-Act-Observe loop delivers the largest jumps (DeepSeek +2.33, GPT-5.2 +1.80).

Methodology at a glance

Corpus. 300 papers selected from >5,200 ICML / ICLR / NeurIPS submissions across 19 domains. GitHub stars + forks are z-normalized into a composite impact score for ranking:

R_i = 0.5·(ln(1+S_i)−μ)/σ  +  0.5·(ln(1+F_i)−μ)/σ

A "summary-then-alignment" pipeline extracts task, method, ground-truth design, datasets, baselines, metrics, constraints. Self-verification + external revision keeps only tasks scoring ≥35/50. Final corpus: 4.2 method modules, 6.8 baselines, 3.1 datasets per task.

Scoring. Six sub-dimensions (3 High-Level: main / ablation / analysis; 3 Low-Level: datasets / baselines / metrics), 0–5 each → 0–30 total. GPT-5.2 judges. A redline mechanism zeroes any sub-dimension with fatal flaws (source hallucination, metric incompatibility, constraint violation).

Limitations & red flags

  • Single LLM judge, no human validation. All scoring — including fatal-flaw detection — relies on GPT-5.2; IAA with humans not reported.
  • Domain coverage skewed. Foundation / generative / multimodal dominate; learning theory and causality sparse.
  • Static, single-shot evaluation. Real experimental design is iterative and outcome-conditioned — abstracted away here.
  • ML-only scope. Wet-lab, physical, safety-constrained natural-science design is out of scope.
  • No head-to-head agentic baseline. OptED vs CoT/CoT+Search only — not vs AI-Scientist, AgentLaboratory, AAAR-1.0.
  • Extraction fidelity ceiling. Despite the 35/50 threshold, ground truth may be distorted, especially for implicit assumptions.

Open questions for follow-up

Does the Low-Level bottleneck shrink for models post-trained on methodology corpora?
Would a diverse multi-judge panel + human spot-checks change rankings or redline rates?
Can the static paradigm extend to multi-turn, outcome-conditioned plan revision?
How does OptED compare to AI-Scientist v2 / AgentLaboratory / AAAR-1.0 on SCOPE tasks?

Could a dedicated grounding module close the residual HL/LL gap OptED leaves open?
