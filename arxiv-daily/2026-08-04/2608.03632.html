When Teachers Mislead:
Spurious-Signal-Aware On-Policy Distillation

Not every teacher token is a lesson. SA-OPD isolates spurious signals — token-level teacher judgments driven by input-agnostic language priors — by measuring how much each token's divergence survives when the prompt is removed, then filters only the weakly-input-grounded, high-impact ones. Beats every selective baseline on all 11 benchmarks.

Method §3

The spurious-signal filter, end to end.

Built on reverse-KL OPD. No trainable parameters; cost is one extra student pass + one extra teacher pass under a residual no-prompt context.

§3.1Diagnose
Decompose sampled divergence A_t = A_t^grd + A_t^prior. Thm. 1: prior-driven gradients align weakly with input-specific direction. Thm. 2: sustained low-SNR updates drift linearly in K steps.
§3.2Probe groundedness
Re-score the same student prefix under a residual no-prompt context (∅, y_≤t). Define Δ_IG = |A_full − A_res|. Small gap ⇒ template-driven.
§3.3Two-axis filter
F = Bottom(Δ_IG) ∩ Top(|A_full|). Both axes required: low-groundedness alone drops low-impact templates; high divergence alone drops useful corrections.
§3.4Dynamic FLMR
Adaptively rescale the filter so filtered loss-mass ratio stays ≤ β=1.8. Prevents over-cleaning unstable VLM tasks.
§3.5Filtered loss
Optimize reverse-KL only on retained tokens V \ F. No new parameters, no backward-pass changes.

Diagnostic insight §5, Fig. 3

VLM Persistent pollution

FLMR trajectories stay elevated throughout training — spurious signals are a dense, continuous contaminant, explaining the larger gains on visual tasks.

LLM · Math Early alignment only

Spurious signals concentrate in the initial alignment phase, then decay — suggesting relaxed filtering late in training could recover compute without loss.

Strengths

  • New failure-mode framing. "Spurious signals" with a clean gradient-SNR foundation (Thm. 1 & 2).
  • Elegant probe. Dual-context divergence is training-free, label-free — no verifier, no reward model.
  • Honest ablations. The Proxy Replacement control cleanly isolates why the student-score proxy beats teacher-logprob.
  • Diagnostic depth. FLMR trajectories + filtered-token stats explain when and why, not just that scores go up.
  • Plug-and-play. ≤7.53% overhead, no new parameters, public code.

Limitations

  • Narrow model coverage. Qwen3 / Qwen3.5 only; students ≤ 2B. Llama / Mistral / 7B+ unverified.
  • Heuristic null context. A single residual context; sensitivity to shuffled/paraphrased/averaged nulls not studied.
  • AIME within noise. Single-digit Avg@8 on small sample sizes — read deltas cautiously.
  • Hard removal only. No soft-reweighting baseline under the SA-OPD criterion.
  • Structured-output bias. All six VLM benchmarks favor template-heavy tasks; open-ended VQA untested.
