A closed-form attention-Jacobian bound, verified zero-violation on a real pretrained model — and an honest mixed result on the IOI circuit it lives in.

Abdallah Khemais (ISITCOM, University of Sousse) · July 2026 · a follow-up to a companion single-block theorem, extended to multi-layer networks and tested on Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct.
Extends a companion paper's exact single-block interaction theorem to multi-layer networks: gives an exact multi-layer decomposition of activation-patching vs. weight-ablation interaction into same-block terms plus one cross-layer remainder, isolates that remainder as an exact double integral (Prop. 2), derives the previously-missing closed-form pointwise attention-Jacobian bound, and verifies it with zero violations across 12 probed sites on Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct. On the emergent IOI circuit, collapse holds on 4/5 instances but visibly fails on Anna/Mike (0.761), and interaction probes only 3/5 — at layer pairs outside the same-block scope the companion theorem actually proves.

01 / THEORY Four load-bearing results

Three exact identities and two closed-form bounds advance the companion paper's program. Each proposition names what it does not close — a discipline rare in circuit papers.

Proposition 1

Exact multi-layer decomposition

For an ablated subset S spanning any number of layers, the true-minus-idealized selector gap decomposes exactly (no approximation) into a sum of same-block terms Δleff plus a single cross-layer remainder R×.

Exact · no curvature
Proposition 2

Cross-layer remainder as a double integral

R× is isolated exactly as a double integral of a mixed second partial — the companion paper's fundamental-theorem-of-calculus argument, applied one dimension up. Identity only; bounding comes next.

Exact identity
Proposition 3

Closed-form attention-Jacobian bound

The missing ingredient to turn the identity into a usable bound: a pointwise operator-norm bound on one attention head's response to a single perturbed key/value token. Derived in closed form; verified on real Qwen weights.

Closed-form · verified
Prop. 4 · Lemma 1

Closed-form curvature constant Λ

The companion paper's second-order interaction bound assumed a curvature constant Λ it never exhibited. This paper computes Λ from three MLP spectral norms and the RMSNorm scale γ alone — discharging the hypothesis rather than leaving it assumed.

03 / EMPIRICAL An emergent IOI circuit on Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct

04 / SCOPE What is established vs. what remains open

Section 8 of the paper separates the two with care. The headline below is the honest split.

✓ Established

  • Proposition 1 — exact multi-layer decomposition, verified to floating-point 0 on 1440 measurements.
  • Proposition 2 — exact double-integral identity for the cross-layer remainder R×.
  • Proposition 3 — closed-form attention-Jacobian bound, zero violations on 12 real-weight sites.
  • Proposition 4 — closed-form curvature Λ discharges the companion paper's assumption; 0 violations in 1200 samples.
  • Pre-registered 4 candidate mechanisms; the magnitude comparator (0/5 clean) is reported as rejected, not silently dropped.

? Open · flagged by the authors

  • Does the per-layer bound compose into a non-vacuous multi-layer bound past 5–10 blocks? Not run; "likely loose."
  • The sign of R× has no identifiable rule (Remark 6's bilinear-structure negative finding).
  • Qwen evidence is one model, one mechanism; no systematic replication on Llama-3 / Pythia or non-IOI tasks.
  • Anna/Mike collapse failure (0.761) — artifact of the 6-site budget, Hydra self-repair, or genuine counterexample? Open.
  • Can the product-of-norms Λ argument be tightened to reduce the 6.5×10²–2.4×10³ slack?
