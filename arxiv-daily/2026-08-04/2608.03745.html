Risky Business:
Measuring the Faithfulness–Safety Tension

Dominik Meier* · Luca Joshua Francis* · Marco B. Kaiser · Terry Ruas · Jan Philip Wahle · Bela Gipp
* joint first authors · University of Göttingen (GippLab) · Meier also at Landeskriminalamt NRW

Across 7 open-weight reasoning models, the most faithful reasoner blindly follows its own tampered, unsafe chain-of-thought — faithfulness and safety are anti-correlated. Two independent internal directions in QwQ-32B can be steered to lift safety +9 pp without touching MMLU.

The tension, in two numbers

97.5%faithful

DeepSeek-R1-Llama-70B is the most faithful model on HazMart — yet rejects tampered unsafe reasoning only 12.3% of the time. QwQ-32B is the lone balancer: 74.7% / 73.9%.

② Why this mattersSO-WHAT

Monitoring ≠ safety

Every CoT-based safety monitor assumes a faithful trace is a safe trace. This paper falsifies that for agentic LRMs: you cannot read the reasoning to keep a model safe if faithfulness is exactly what drags it toward the unsafe action.

③ Critical edgeCAVEAT

One model. 77 scenarios.

All probing and steering claims rest on QwQ-32B alone. HazMart has 77 hand-written items (~7 per category, several rankings within noise). Proprietary frontier models (Claude, GPT, Gemini) are excluded — and rule-based token swaps may leave detectable artifacts.

The method — TRR

§ METHODOLOGY

Targeted Reasoning Replacement intervenes on the model's own generated chain-of-thought, not the prompt. After free reasoning over a HazMart scenario, regex-based swaps rewrite the chosen option reference — preserving case and identifiers (id_a / id_b / id_c) — then feed the tampered trace back.

  • Swapped-safe → faithfulness score (eq. 2): does the model switch to the other safe option?
  • Swapped-unsafe → safety score (eq. 3): does the model reject the injected unsafe option?
  • Deterministic regex chosen over LLM paraphrase — Appendix B shows paraphrasing "introduced significant noise."
  • Run on 4×A100, VLLM, temp 0.6, top-p 0.95, 2048 max tokens, 5 runs / 95% CI.

The benchmark — HazMart

§ DATASET

77 hand-written, non-abstract scenarios. The LRM plays an autonomous shopkeeper optimizing customer satisfaction and profit, acting through function calls take_action / choose_strategy / hire_candidate. Each scenario has two safe options (A,B) and one unsafe option (C), shuffled to kill first-option bias.

  • 11 harm categories, incl. two agentic-specific ones: power-seeking and weight exfiltration.
  • 7 scenario types: inventory, customer service, hiring, competitor interactions.
  • Embedding dispersion analysis confirms semantic diversity, not clustering.

Asymmetric internal architecture

§ ATTENTION-MASK ABLATION

The two axes travel through different circuitry. The safety axis is redundantly wired — knock out one path, the other compensates. The faithfulness axis is a single integrated pathway — fragile by comparison. The finding predicts different attack surfaces for CoT-hijacking across models.

Strengths ↔ Limitations

§ CRITICAL ASSESSMENT

+ What lands well

  • TRR is a clean, reproducible intervention (regex, no LLM variance) — measures faithfulness and safety in the same pipeline.
  • Agentic shopkeeper framing with function calls + dual-safe-option design avoids the MMLU-style faithfulness/truthfulness confound.
  • Unusually careful interpretability: orthogonalization, cross-condition probes, matched-magnitude controls, joint plane sweep, mask ablation.
  • Asymmetric-architecture result is non-obvious and predicts CoT-hijack attack surfaces.
  • Honest limitations section names scale, open-weight-only restriction, artifact risk, inference-only scope.

− Where it frays

  • 77 scenarios — per-category CIs of ±6 pp make several rankings indistinguishable (Malware 47.19% vs Privacy 47.25%).
  • Single-model mechanism — all probing/steering on QwQ-32B; "two independent axes" is not yet a general property of LRMs.
  • Proprietary blind spot — Claude, GPT, Gemini excluded; the systems most likely to be deployed agentic are untested.
  • Substitution-artifact risk — a model may resist the artifact of the swap, not the unsafe content (inflates safety).
  • No defense tested against naturally-arising unsafe reasoning (self-jailbreaks, CoT forgeries) — only TRR-injected swaps.
  • Narrow α range — headline α=+3 sits near a cross-over; full dose-response suggests a thin operating window.
