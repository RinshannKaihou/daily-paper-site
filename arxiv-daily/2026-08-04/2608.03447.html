Key
Finding
Stop truncating at the first mismatch. ASD accepts bounded low-regret draft–target mismatches and reuses the already target-scored greedy suffix — for free. It is training-free, verifier-side only, needs no extra target forward pass, and reduces exactly to strict greedy verification at B = 0.

3 Critical Edge
  • Not lossless. Hash divergence >95% on GSM8K / MATH-500 even when accuracy is flat.
  • Cherry-picked config. (B,g,M)=(8,0.25,2) tuned on GSM8K only; ≤1.52 pp dips elsewhere.
  • Speed ⇄ quality tie. Largest speedups land on cells with the largest accuracy hits.
  • Ledger ≠ error prob. B is an accounting knob, not a calibrated quality tolerance.

The Three Knobs § METHODOLOGY

B= 8
Request-level regret budget. Persistent ledger (B, s) accumulates local target-logit regret of every accepted mismatch across the whole completion.
g= 0.25
Local regret gate. rᵢ / qᵢ ≤ g — regret normalized by remaining proposal length; a training-free proxy for suffix-reuse opportunity. g=0.25 covers 95.3% of requests.
M= 2
Per-block exception cap. Prevents accepted mismatches from clustering inside a single draft block.
B = 0  ⇒  ASD reduces exactly to strict token-ID greedy verification.

Core Contributions § NOVELTY

01 Budgeted prefix selection
Reformulates approximate verification as longest-contiguous-prefix selection under a persistent request-level regret ledger — not a local heuristic.
02 Realized-prefix reuse (Prop. 1)
After an accepted exception, the contiguous suffix that stays target-greedy under the realized prefix is committed with no extra target forward. This is what separates ASD from MARS / Fuzzy.
03 Three-tier control
Local gate, block cap, request budget — independently tunable, with an exact strict-greedy fallback at B=0.
04 O(K) overhead
Vectorized via a per-position feasibility mask (cumsum + cumprod). No extra target forward pass; only target top-token & top-logit needed.

Strengths

  • Drafter-agnostic: works on DSpark / EAGLE3 / Medusa with no fine-tuning.
  • Principled fallback: B=0 is provably strict greedy.
  • Suffix reuse is provable (Prop. 1) — explains why accepting an early mismatch pays off.
  • Strong hygiene: hash-checked matching, CIs on every cell, disjoint H-param selection, separate accuracy / hash audits.
  • 10/10 cross-family cells positive, negligible verifier overhead.

Limitations

  • Explicitly not lossless; needs per-task natural-EOS quality auditing — real operational burden.
  • Accuracy dips on HumanEval, MT-Bench, two GSM8K cells under the single frozen config.
  • g-sweep is single-seed, n=256 — operating point carries understated uncertainty.
  • Scope: single L20 for main tables; H20 reports only A/P (not end-to-end TPS) due to FP4→FP8 path.
  • No head-to-head vs. rejection-sampling SD or distributional verifiers (Judge, Cactus).

Open Questions FOR FURTHER EXPLORATION

Does per-task re-tuning of (B, g, M) erase the HumanEval / MT-Bench dips — and at what throughput cost?
Can the regret ledger be converted into a calibrated probability of quality degradation, giving B a direct tolerance meaning?
How does ASD interact with temperature > 0 and rejection-sampling SD, where the baseline is already distribution-preserving?
End-to-end TPS (not just A/P) on native-FP4 hardware (Blackwell), removing the FP4→FP8 Q/DQ path?
Does suffix reuse compose with tree drafters (SpecInfer, Sequoia, DySpec), where many candidate paths each expose reusable suffixes?
Under batched / continuous serving with heterogeneous lengths, does the ledger still bound aggregate quality drift — or do long-tail requests accumulate uncharacterized regret?
