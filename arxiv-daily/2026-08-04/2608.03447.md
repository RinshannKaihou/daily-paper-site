> **一句话总结：** 本文提出 Approximate Speculative Decoding (ASD)，一种无需训练、纯验证器侧的方法，将标准贪心投机解码的"首次失配即截断"替换为带预算的最长前缀选择——通过局部 regret 门控、每块异常上限和请求级 regret 预算三类约束接受少量低 regret 失配，并复用其后已为目标贪心的连续后缀；在 Qwen3-14B + DSpark-14B 上七任务平均提速 7.78%，最高 15.26%，且 B=0 时严格还原标准贪心验证。

> **TL;DR:** Approximate Speculative Decoding (ASD) is a training-free, verifier-side replacement for strict greedy verification in speculative decoding: instead of truncating at the first draft–target mismatch, it accepts bounded low-regret mismatches (gated by local regret, a per-block exception cap, and a persistent request-level regret budget) and then reuses the already target-scored contiguous greedy suffix without another target forward pass. On Qwen3-14B + DSpark-14B it lifts fixed-workload TPS by 3.05%–15.26% over matched strict SD (7.78% mean across 7 tasks), and improves acceptance ~10%–16% on DeepSeek-V4-Flash + DSpark for GSM8K and MATH-500 under FP4-to-FP8 compatibility — at the cost of small (≤1.52 pp) task-accuracy changes and explicit, non-lossless trajectory divergence.

## 核心贡献

- **将近似贪心验证形式化为"带预算的最长连续前缀选择"问题**：用一个贯穿整个请求的持久 regret 账本 (B, s) 来累计所有被接受失配的局部 target-logit regret，把"接受多少失配"从局部启发式变成可解释的请求级权衡。
- **提出并证明 realized-prefix 后缀复用 (Proposition 1)**：在某位置接受一个失配后，其后那些在"已实现前缀"下仍等于 target argmax 的连续 draft token 可以直接提交，无需额外的近似决策或额外的 target 前向——这是 ASD 区别于 MARS/Fuzzy 等纯局部放松规则的关键。
- **三类互补的近似控制**：局部 regret 门 g（按剩余提议长度归一化，过滤代价过高的失配）、每块异常上限 M（防止失配在同一 draft 块内集中）、请求级预算 B（绑定整段补全的累计偏移）；B=0 时严格等价于 token-ID 贪心验证。
- **跨家族、训练无关的系统性提速**：在 DSpark、EAGLE3、Medusa 三个 drafter 家族与 Qwen3、Llama-3.1、Qwen2.5 四个 target 上，所有 10 个 cross-family 单元均为正收益 (3.05%–15.26%)，且只需标准的 target 验证前向，O(K) 额外算术开销，无需重训 drafter。

## Key Contributions

- Reformulates approximate greedy verification as budgeted longest-contiguous-prefix selection, gated by a persistent request-level regret ledger (B, s) that accounts for every accepted mismatch along the realized trajectory.
- Formalizes and proves realized-prefix suffix reuse (Proposition 1): after an accepted exception, a contiguous suffix of draft tokens that remain target-greedy under the realized prefix can be committed with no additional approximate decisions and no extra target-model forward pass.
- Introduces three complementary, independently-tunable controls — local regret gate g (normalized by remaining proposal length q_i), per-block exception cap M, and request-level regret budget B — and shows ASD exactly reduces to strict token-ID greedy verification when B = 0.
- Demonstrates training-free, verifier-only gains across four targets and three drafter families (DSpark, EAGLE3, Medusa), with ASD improving fixed-workload TPS in all 10 cross-family cells and on all 7 primary tasks, requiring only the target top-token and top-logit.

## Methodology

ASD operates strictly on the verifier side, after the ordinary teacher-forced target pass. For a draft block x_{1:K} scored against target greedy tokens y_i^* = argmax z_i, the method:

1. Computes per-position mismatch indicators d_i and local target-logit regret r_i = z_i(y_i^*) − z_i(x_i) ≥ 0 (equivalent to the conditional log-prob gap, since the softmax normalizer cancels).
2. Normalizes regret by remaining proposal length q_i = K − i + 1 as a training-free proxy for suffix-reuse opportunity, yielding the local gate r_i / q_i ≤ g.
3. Maintains a persistent per-request state (B, s); a candidate prefix x_{1:t} is feasible iff cumulative regret C_t ≤ B − s, exception count N_t ≤ M, and every mismatch satisfies the local gate.
4. Returns a_ASD = max feasible prefix length (the contiguous-prefix requirement is essential — ASD never skips an infeasible token to take a later one, which would desynchronize the verified target rows from the committed cache).
5. Updates the ledger s' = s + C_a^ASD and emits either a recovery token (if a < K) or a bonus token (if a = K); KV caches and ledger advance only along the emitted sequence.

Algorithm 1 is vectorized via a per-position feasibility mask f_i (Eq. 14) computed with cumulative sums and a cumulative product, adding O(K) arithmetic and no target forward pass. The authors prove (Proposition 1) that any committed x_j = y_j^* is target-greedy under the realized ASD history, so suffix reuse introduces no additional token-level exception — though it does not restore equivalence to the strict trajectory. ASD is intentionally approximate: the ledger bounds cumulative local regret but does not guarantee output identity, distributional equivalence, semantic preservation, or task correctness. Hyperparameters (B, g, M) = (8, 0.25, 2) are selected on a disjoint GSM8K slice and frozen before multi-task evaluation.

## Results & Highlights

- **Primary matrix (Table 1, Qwen3-14B + DSpark-14B, frozen config):** ASD improves fixed-workload TPS over matched strict SD on all 7 tasks: GSM8K +10.08% (strict 5.58× → ASD 6.30×), MATH-500 +11.73% (6.88× → 7.32×), HumanEval +6.90% (4.68× → 5.14×), MBPP +3.64% (5.04× → 5.36×), MMLU +4.41% (3.30× → 3.54×), MT-Bench +7.79% (4.68× → 5.16×), Alpaca +9.94% (4.22× → 4.66×); mean +7.78%. Per-task accuracy changes are small: +1.17 pp on MATH-500, −0.61 pp on HumanEval, −0.64 pp on MT-Bench, all others within ±0.73 pp.
- **Cross-family (Table 2, DSpark/EAGLE3/Medusa × Qwen3/Llama-3.1/Qwen2.5):** Every one of 10 cells shows positive TPS gain spanning 3.05%–15.26% (mean 7.52%); the largest gain is **+15.26% on Llama-3.1-8B + EAGLE3 / GSM8K** (strict 4.74× → ASD 5.34×) followed by +11.94% on MMLU. Accepted length rises in every cell (+0.07 to +0.52 tokens). Accuracy deltas are task-dependent and never exceed 1.52 pp in magnitude.
- **Verifier overhead is negligible (Fig. 6d):** synchronized per-token profiling shows verifier logic adds only 0.083 ms/token on Alpaca and 0.045 ms/token on GSM8K, while target verification time drops by 1.479 and 1.509 ms respectively — the gain comes from committing useful target-scored suffixes and reducing verifier rounds, not from hiding overhead.
- **Budget-matched comparison vs. relaxed baselines (Fig. 6c):** under the same maximum-realized-regret criterion, ASD beats MARS-style (+5.3% GSM8K / +6.0% MATH-500) and Fuzzy-style (+5.1% / +7.3%) controls with +6.5% and +9.7% respectively, isolating the value of request-level accounting over purely local permissive rules.
- **Large-model acceptance (Fig. 7, DeepSeek-V4-Flash 284B + DSpark, 8× H20):** on the independent 1,000-example GSM8K-Confirm split, g ∈ {2, 3} improves accepted tokens/proposal by 10.08%–11.48% while changing accuracy by ≤0.30 pp; cross-task acceptance gains on HumanEval and MATH-500 are described as roughly 10%–16% on GSM8K and MATH-500 in the FP4-to-FP8 compatibility setting.
- **Regret-gate frontier (Fig. 5, GSM8K natural-EOS, n=256):** the selected g = 0.25 covers 95.3% of requests and yields +7.2% TPS with no measured accuracy change; more permissive gates produce only small accuracy fluctuations, justifying gated (not unconditional) acceptance.

## Authors & Credibility

Multi-institutional collaboration spanning School of Integrated Circuit Science and Engineering (Beihang), Department of Precision Instrument (Tsinghua), Faculty of Engineering and Department of ECE (University of Hong Kong), and School of Integrated Circuits (Peking University). The paper carries an AAAI 2027 copyright notice ("Copyright © 2027, AAAI"), indicating acceptance. Source code is publicly released at https://github.com/Kissmetothemoon/ASD . The experimental protocol is unusually careful for an inference-acceleration paper: hyperparameters frozen on disjoint data, strict–ASD–strict triplets run sequentially on the same GPU with hash-checked matching token counts, 95% Student-t confidence intervals reported on every TPS delta, and separate natural-EOS accuracy / length / hash-divergence audits. The authors are explicit that ASD is approximate (not lossless), foregrounding hash divergence above 95% on GSM8K and MATH-500 even when accuracy does not drop.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty (genuine):** The core insight — that an accepted low-regret mismatch exposes an already-scored target-greedy suffix that can be reused for free (Proposition 1) — is a real verifier-side contribution that MARS, Fuzzy, Judge Decoding, and other relaxed verifiers do not formalize. The framing as budgeted longest-prefix selection with a persistent request-level ledger is a clean, novel abstraction; the B=0 reduction to strict greedy is a satisfying theoretical anchor.
- **Rigor (strong):** Hash-checked fixed-workload matching, confidence intervals on every cell, disjoint hyperparameter selection, and an explicit natural-EOS audit that separates throughput from behavior preservation are above the bar for the area. The honest disclosure that ASD is not output-preserving (GSM8K/MATH-500 hash divergence >95% despite flat accuracy) is commendable and rare.
- **Reproducibility (good with caveats):** Public code, named software stack (PyTorch 2.8.0+cu128, Transformers 4.55.2, vLLM 0.11.0, Python 3.12.13, Linux 5.4) and single NVIDIA L20 GPU for the main results. However, several ablation sweeps (Figs. 5, 6b) use a single seed and small n (64–256), so the precise magnitude of the g-sweep and draft-horizon curves is under-powered.
- **Significance (moderate-to-high):** Verifier-only, training-free gains that stack on top of already-strong strict SD baselines (1.82×–6.88× target-only, lifted to 1.94×–7.32×) are practically useful and complementary to drafter-side work. The DeepSeek-V4-Flash FP4-to-FP8 results broaden industrial relevance.
- **Red flags / overclaiming to flag:**
  - **Cherry-picked hyperparameters:** (B, g, M) = (8, 0.25, 2) are tuned on GSM8K only and then applied to all six other tasks; the −0.61 pp HumanEval and −0.64 pp MT-Bench drops (and −1.17 / −1.52 pp in two cross-family GSM8K cells) suggest the frozen config is not uniformly safe, and no per-task re-tuning bound is given.
  - **The largest speedups coincide with the largest accuracy hits** in Table 2 (Llama-3.1-8B + EAGLE3 GSM8K +15.26% with −1.17 pp; Medusa GSM8K +6.72% with −1.52 pp), so the headline range should be read alongside the quality column.
  - **Single-GPU, single-seed for several sweeps** understates variance; the regret-gate frontier (Fig. 5/6a) that justifies g=0.25 rests on n=256, one seed.
  - **"Acceptance up 10%–16%" on DeepSeek-V4-Flash** is reported only as verifier-side A/P, not end-to-end TPS, because of the FP4-vs-FP8 Q/DQ compatibility path — a scope limitation the paper acknowledges but readers may miss.
  - **No comparison to exact-sampling alternatives** (rejection-sampling speculative decoding) under the same budget; the speed–quality frontier is plotted only against ASD's own controls and two relaxed variants.

## Strengths

- Training-free and drafter-agnostic: drops into any greedy speculative decoding stack (DSpark, EAGLE3, Medusa) with no fine-tuning and no extra target forward pass.
- Principled, three-tier control (local gate, block cap, request budget) with an exact strict-greedy fallback at B=0.
- Realized-prefix suffix reuse (Proposition 1) is a clean, provable mechanism that explains *why* accepting an early mismatch pays off, beyond mere local tolerance.
- Strong, carefully-audited experimental hygiene: matched fixed-workload, hash verification, CIs, disjoint selection, separate accuracy/hash audits.
- Consistent positive gains across 4 targets and 3 drafter families (10/10 cells), with negligible verifier overhead (0.045–0.083 ms/token).

## Limitations

- **Explicitly not lossless:** ASD does not guarantee output identity, semantic preservation, or task correctness; deployment requires per-task natural-EOS quality auditing, which the paper honestly recommends but which adds operational burden.
- **Accuracy dips on a non-trivial subset of tasks** (HumanEval, MT-Bench, two cross-family GSM8K cells) under the single frozen config; users running code generation or open-ended chat should expect small quality movements.
- **Hyperparameter sensitivity is real:** Figure 5 shows g drives the speed–accuracy frontier, and the g-sweep is single-seed on n=256, so the chosen operating point carries understated uncertainty.
- **Hardware/generalization scope:** main tables are a single L20 GPU; only the DeepSeek-V4-Flash study uses H20, and there it reports acceptance (A/P) rather than end-to-end TPS due to the FP4-to-FP8 Q/DQ path. No CPU/edge or batched-serving regime is reported.
- **No head-to-head against exact rejection-sampling SD** or against distributional-equivalence-preserving relaxed verifiers (Judge Decoding, Cactus) on the same workload — the relaxed-baseline comparison is limited to MARS-style and Fuzzy-style under a matched-regret criterion.
- **Ledger bounds local regret, not downstream error:** the regret budget is an accounting quantity, not a calibrated error probability, so B is not directly interpretable as a quality tolerance.

## Relevance

高度相关于 LLM 推理加速与投机解码方向：ASD 是一种纯验证器侧、训练无关的改动，可叠加在现有 drafter/tree 方法之上，直接提升 fixed-workload 吞吐，且提供了"近似但可审计"的速度-质量权衡新范式，对部署侧的系统研究者有明确价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Is the frozen (B=8, g=0.25, M=2) config robust under per-task re-tuning on each benchmark, or do the HumanEval/MT-Bench accuracy dips vanish with task-specific budgets — and at what throughput cost?
- Can the request-level regret ledger be converted into a calibrated probability of task-quality degradation, so that B has a direct quality-tolerance interpretation rather than being an accounting knob?
- How does ASD interact with sampling (temperature > 0) and rejection-sampling speculative decoding, where the strict baseline is already distribution-preserving — does suffix reuse still help, or does it distort the target distribution?
- What is the end-to-end TPS (not just A/P) gain on DeepSeek-V4-Flash once the FP4-to-FP8 Q/DQ compatibility path is removed on native-FP4 hardware (e.g., Blackwell)?
- Does realized-prefix suffix reuse compose with tree-based drafters (SpecInfer, Sequoia, DySpec), where multiple candidate paths could each expose reusable suffixes after an accepted exception?
- Under batched / continuous serving with heterogeneous request lengths, does the persistent request-level ledger still bound aggregate quality drift, or do long-tail requests accumulate uncharacterized regret?
