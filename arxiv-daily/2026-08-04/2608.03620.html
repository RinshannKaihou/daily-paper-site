Key Finding

Activation patching and weight-space ablation are proven to measure two generally unrelated quantities — patch moves the β·δ contrast, ablation moves the −β·α(xB) level — so a single-carrier patch can flip a decision while every single-carrier ablation stays put. The disagreement is an identity, not a caution.

Why This Matters
Whom to trust: patch or ablation?
For anyone doing circuit analysis — IOI, refusal directions, induction heads, model editing — this turns "patch and ablation sometimes disagree" into a checkable arithmetic identity with named, computable quantities (β, δ, α). It tells you, per carrier, exactly what each intervention measures and when they will diverge.
Critical Edge
The clean threshold does not survive.
A numerical "empty band" of (6.78, 9.95) read off 14 configs fails out-of-sample: 9 of 39 configurations violate it, 7 fall inside, and 3 of the original 14 even flip sides on re-measurement. The author withdraws the strong threshold claim — only the monotone association survives.
§ 01

The dissociation, made arithmetic

What patching measures

Δspatch = βi · δi // contrast

The donor–receiver difference δ, weighted by the receiver's readout coefficient β. Frozen-activation patch moves this.

Equivalence (Prop. 2): the frozen-activation protocol and a genuine weight edit produce identical writes — median gap ≤ 9.6×10⁻⁷, six orders below the signal — even with data-estimated directions.

What ablation measures

Δsablate = −βi · αi(xB) // level

The receiver's own selector magnitude, negated. Ablation removes the level it was sitting at — an entirely different quantity.

Construction (Cor. 4): for any n ≥ 2 carriers, set β=1, α(xB)=−½, α(xA)=1. Every single-carrier patch flips the decision; no single-carrier ablation does. Overshoot / "self-repair" with no repair mechanism.

§ 02

Four results, four scopes

Theorem 1 · iff
Conditional Collapse Criterion
collapse ⟺ q̄S=0  and  ΨS=0
Deleting carriers S maps the matched pair onto s̄ = (gA+gB)/2 with deterministic polarity — independent of which S (Cor. 2).
Prop. 1 · tolerance
Robust Error Identity
sS(xA)−s̄ = −q̄S + ½ΨS
Drops exact-equality for |q̄S|≤ε₁, |ΨS|≤ε₂. Any polarity reversal ⟹ ε₁+½ε₂ ≥ |s̄|.
Theorem 2 · dissociation
Patch ≠ Ablation
βiδi  ≘  −βiαi(xB)
Two named, mutually-unbounded quantities. Cor. 4 builds n≥2-carrier counterexamples certifying the gap.
Theorem 3 · nonlinearity
The one place the additive picture breaks
∆(x) = (I−Q)[−Dg(r1)η − R(x)],   ‖R‖ ≤ Λ‖η‖²
Head composed with its own layer's RMSNorm+MLP is the single architectural site where carrier independence fails. Fundamental-theorem-of-calculus derivation; Λ is closed-form-computable from trained weights (in companion). ∆ vanishes when only the MLP is ablated (Cor. 5), non-zero when a head is.

§ 04

The claim, in two languages

中文 · 一句话总结

在残差流上加性条件计算 + 线性读出的理想模型下，本文严格刻画了何时低秩权重消融会把匹配输入对塌缩到同一无分支输出（充要条件 + 误差恒等式），并证明激活修补与权重消融度量的并非同一量——一个移 contrast (β·δ)，一个移 level (−β·α)。在 head 自身层 MLP 的复合处给出严格二阶余项的非线性交互公式。在小规模合成关联召回任务上：交互幅值与理想模型预测精度呈强单调（Spearman −0.83），但一个初见"干净分界带"在 25 个 held-out 配置上不复存在。

EN · TL;DR

In an idealized residual-stream conditional read out linearly, the paper proves an iff criterion for low-rank weight ablation to collapse a matched pair onto one unconditional branch, gives the exact error identity for near-collapse, and shows patching (β·δ contrast) and ablation (−β·α level) measure two generally unrelated quantities — constructing n≥2-carrier pairs on which every single-carrier patch flips the decision while no single-carrier ablation does. A second-order-bounded formula is derived for the nonlinearity an ablated head introduces into its own layer's MLP, vanishing identically when only the MLP is ablated. On 39 configurations, interaction magnitude tracks fidelity (Spearman ρ = −0.83, p < 1e-5), but a clean numerical separation band read off 14 configurations does not survive on the other 25 held-out.

§ 05

Relevance & what's open

Who should read this

Researchers in mechanistic interpretability, circuit analysis, activation patching, and model editing. It bears directly on the methodological choice — pervasive in IOI, refusal-direction, and induction-head work — of whether to trust the patch or the ablation. The four named quantities (β, δ, α, Λ) are computable per component and turn that choice into arithmetic.

Open questions

  1. Does the iff criterion hold on emergent circuits in real pretrained models (GPT-2 IOI, refusal), where matched-pair structure is hard to enforce?
  2. Can Λ be estimated cheaply enough to flag additive-vs-non-additive components during circuit discovery?
  3. Is there a designable subset-selection procedure (using only β, α, δ, margins) that steers a target collapse polarity?
  4. Does the interaction↔fidelity monotone stabilize into a transferrable threshold, or is the absence of a cutoff itself the finding?
