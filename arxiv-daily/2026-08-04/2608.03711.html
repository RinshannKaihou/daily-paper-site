Attention is Case-Sensitive

Maximilian Dillitzer · Tin Stribor Sohn · Jason J. Corso · Michael Auerbach

Esslingen · KIT · Michigan · Porsche AG·5 model families·3 tokenizer types·13 models

Key
Finding

Letter case is a latent attentional attractor already baked into pretrained transformers. Uppercase target emphasis concentrates attention and lifts accuracy — but the strongest attractor, alternating case, attracts the most attention while degrading accuracy. The steering reflex "more attention on the span ⇒ better answer" is, empirically, false.

03Why This Matters

A zero-access, prompt-only probe — no fine-tuning, no hooks, no activation edits, mean overhead ≈0.0015 ms. It reframes letter case from a tokenization nuisance to normalize away into a latent steering lever anyone can use to audit their own model.

Diagnostic · not a method

04Critical Edge

The headline "reasoning buffer" sub-claim rests on two models whose baselines are clearly broken — equally consistent with an answer-parsing / harness failure, not a real cognitive buffer.

01 Methodology

Causal isolation at the pre-tokenization layer.

Given raw text X and a ground-truth answer span A ⊆ X, construct X̃ = T_C(X) with a per-character case map. A semantic-preservation constraint holds lexical identity invariant even when token IDs change; a two-stage matcher locates A (98.2% exact, 1.8% Levenshtein ≤2).

13 models 15 schemes 6 benchmarks 3 tokenizer families 0 model access

Measured: target attention mass Att_A(f_θ(X̃)) aggregated across layers/heads, and task accuracy — always reported relative to each model's own naturally-cased baseline.

03 The central divergence

Attention attractor strength vs. accuracy gain.

On LLaMA-3.1-8B-Instruct / MMLU-Pro, the schemes that buy the most attention (TA) buy the least accuracy — or cost it. Only Target-Emphasis is reliably productive.

04 Cross-modal transfer

Prompt-only case rewrites reshape image attention — on a dual axis.

On RefCOCOg the image is never altered; only the case of the referring-expression label changes. Yet attention reorganizes along two coupled axes:

Microscopic · target box

+1.55pp

Residual visual budget concentrates on the target box — +23.08% relative.

Macroscopic · whole image

−1.85pp

Overall image engagement drains away — −12.41% relative. The only sign-consistent signature across all 4 VLMs.

Family ordering TA > TE > rest is preserved in the aggregate. The biggest single disruptor is TE2 (uppercase target inside an alternating context): whole-image drain −4.11 pp, cross-model disengagement 33.35% mean.

05 The "reasoning buffer" — and its crack

Thinking dampens the effect in text, amplifies it in vision.

In pure text, reasoning models barely move under case pressure. In VLMs, the effect reverses: reasoning VLMs disengage from vision most strongly — peaking under TE2.

07 Strengths / Limitations

+ Strengths

  • Case is the only variable; formal semantic-preservation constraint.
  • Honest about TA's failure — frames it as the central insight.
  • 13 models · 6 benchmarks · Grad-CAM viz.
  • Practically free: zero access, ~0.0015 ms overhead.
  • Novel dual-axis + reasoning-buffer VLM finding.

− Limitations

  • Reasoning-LLM baselines are broken — sub-claim shaky.
  • "Universal" really means consistent sign, not magnitude.
  • Descriptive only — no pipeline, no head-to-head vs PASTA.
  • LayerNorm mitigation is conjecture, not evaluated.
  • Latin-only; attention-mass metric smooths layer/head detail.

08 Open questions

  1. Does the reasoning buffer survive a parsing-harness fix on gpt-oss-20B / Qwen3-4B-Thinking?
  2. Is case a free complement, substitute, or orthogonal axis to white-box steering (PASTA, AutoPASTA)?
  3. Does the productive-vs-destructive split hold for long-form QA, summarization, agentic tool-use, refusal?
  4. Can case-agnostic LayerNorm neutralize TA without hurting TE — at what cost?
  5. Is VLM macro-disengagement a symptom of text-dominance bias — exploitable to reduce spurious image attention?
  6. Are there non-Latin analogues (full/half-width, ruby) that transformers internalize?
