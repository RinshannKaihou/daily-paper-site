Layer-wise robustness · Mechanistic interpretability

Sensitivity, Causality, and Repair Dissociate.

Nathan Labiosa · David Buff · Ena Nayak · Erica Donno — University of Southern California

Key Finding
The three natural readings of "which layer is responsible for a perturbation failure" — sensitivity, causality, and compensatory capacity — peak at different layers, and on the two gate-passing models they are strongly anti-correlated (ρ = −0.72 to −0.88). The layers your diagnostics point to are the worst places to put a repair adapter, not the best.

01 · The headline dissociation

Three maps of "responsibility" don't agree.

Sensitivity (LRD) — early-mid Causality (patching) — late Compensatory (LoRA) — deepest
Sensitivity

LRD

1 − cos(h̄_clean, h̄_noisy) of mean-pooled hidden states. Where representations first blow up.

Causality

Activation patching

Substitute clean hidden state into noisy pass; fraction of failed examples rescued. Where the fix lives.

Compensation

Per-window LoRA

Δ accuracy from r=4 adapters in a 5-layer window vs no-adapter baseline. Where repair actually helps.

On Phi-3.5 (identity patch 100%) and Llama-3-8B (90%) the three maps dissociate, and sensitivity↔causality are strongly anti-correlated. Gemma-2-9B is the lone exception (ρ = +0.39) — conjectured corrupted by its 60% identity ceiling.

02 · Propagation regimes

Two regimes, scaling-monotone within family.

Models split by how layer-0 divergence resolves by the final layer:

Spike-and-suppress 21–60% recovered

Early divergence absorbed by mid-depth. Phi-3.5, Gemma-2-9B.

Late-accumulation 0–5% recovered

Divergence persists to the end. Llama-3, Mistral, Qwen2.5-7B.

03 · Cascade disruption → validated placement anti-pattern

The diagnostic site is the worst adapter window.

Mechanism. An early-layer adapter perturbs every downstream layer on clean inputs: upstream divergence is bit-identical (exactly zero), disruption onsets at the window and persists to the end. So total clean disruption is largest for the earliest windows — which is exactly where divergence and patching point.

This yields a falsifiable prediction, confirmed on every adjudicable model under a fixed GSM8K harness (six perturbations, mean Δ pp vs no-adapter):

04 · A reversal the authors caught on themselves

The CoT-truncation artifact.

A representation-stability loss (Lstab) the authors previously believed in reported strong gains under a truncated harness. Under an adequate budget, the sign flips.

+7.3 → −3.5
Phi-3.5 L15–19, pp. max_new_tokens 100 → 512.
+11.6 → −7.5
Qwen2.5-7B L24–27, same checkpoint, fixed harness.

Cause: truncated CoT scored as empty — rewarding shorter, often less accurate outputs. Remedy: any CoT intervention needs a generation-length-matched null.

06 · Credibility ledger

Unusually self-critical.

Pre-registered 80% identity-patch gate; separates "confirmed" from "exploratory".
Prospectively registered C3/C4 metric pick on Qwen-7B before its sweep.
Reported its own hypothesis falsified (C3/C4 picked Qwen's worst window) rather than burying it.
Exposed its own previously-believed Lstab gain as a CoT-truncation artifact.
Public code, configs, per-model logs. AI use confined to scaffolding & copy-editing.
