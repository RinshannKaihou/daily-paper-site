> **一句话总结：** 本文提出 SCDG，一种无需训练、有方向性、可逐 token 分解的源条件描述长度增益框架，通过对比冻结语言模型在有无候选源上下文下对可疑文档的编码长度，衡量候选源对预测该文档的增量贡献；在 PAN 2025 配对检测上 F1 达 0.9433（最强基线 0.9041），在 PAN 2026 多源检索上将 nDCG@10 提升超过 0.13，且在 Multi-News 同主题压力测试中假阳性率仅 0.125%。

> **TL;DR:** SCDG is a training-free, directional, token-decomposable framework that measures how much a candidate source shortens a frozen LM's description length of a suspicious document; on the PAN25-derived pairwise benchmark it reaches F1 = 0.9433 vs. 0.9041 for the strongest baseline (PAN12 n-gram), and on PAN26 multi-source retrieval DAAC+SCDG achieves nDCG@10 = 0.8315 / Recall@100 = 0.9625, a >0.13 nDCG@10 gain from reranking alone, while a calibrated gain-distribution classifier yields only 0.125% proxy FPR on the same-topic Multi-News stress set (vs. 71.125% for BM25).

## 核心贡献

- **提出 SCDG（源条件描述长度增益）框架**：从描述长度/概率预测的视角重新定义生成式抄袭检测，将"候选源 S 对可疑文档 P 的增量预测贡献"形式化为冻结自回归语言模型在有无 S 上下文下对同一目标序列的编码长度之差，无需任何任务特定训练或参数更新。
- **token 级精确分解与稀疏证据聚合**：文档级增益可精确分解为每个目标 token 上的对数似然增益，使方法能定位被重用的局部段落，并支持 Top-q 稀疏聚合避免文档级平均稀释证据——这是应对"源仅被部分重用"场景的关键设计。
- **提出 DAAC（Dense-Anchored Abstract Calibration）检索融合指标**：仅基于检索排名（BM25、句级 dense、abstract 级）融合出紧凑候选集，无需额外神经网络推理，与 SCDG 配合实现可扩展的大语料源归因。
- **统一支持配对检测与候选源重排**：同一方向性、可分解信号既可阈值化为二分类决策，又可对一阶段检索的 Top-1000 候选做重排，无需为不同任务单独微调。
- **全面的实证验证**：在 PAN 2025 衍生配对基准、PAN 2026 开放语料多源检索、以及 Multi-News 同主题压力测试三类场景上系统评测，并设计受控源证据保留实验与同主题混淆分析，分别验证对真实源证据的敏感性与对主题混淆的鲁棒性。

## Key Contributions

- Introduces **SCDG (Source-Conditioned Description-Length Gain)**, a training-free, directional framework that recasts generative plagiarism detection as source-conditioned predictability: it contrasts a frozen autoregressive LM's codelength of a suspicious document P with and without a candidate source S in context, yielding a model-relative conditional log-likelihood ratio that quantifies S's incremental predictive contribution.
- Provides an **exact token-wise decomposition** of the document-level gain, enabling localized evidence tracing and a Top-q sparse-aggregation variant that prevents dilution of source evidence when only part of P reuses S.
- Proposes **DAAC (Dense-Anchored Abstract Calibration)**, a rank-only retrieval-fusion indicator combining BM25, sentence-level dense, and abstract-level evidence with gated interactions; it builds compact Top-1000 candidate sets without extra neural inference and pairs with SCDG for scalable source attribution over 86k+ candidate corpora.
- **Unifies pairwise detection and candidate-source reranking** under one signal, with thresholds calibrated on validation data for classification and descending-gain ordering for retrieval reranking—no task-specific fine-tuning.
- Delivers a **comprehensive empirical study** across three settings (PAN25 pairwise, PAN26 full-corpus retrieval, Multi-News same-topic confounding), plus a controlled source-evidence-retention intervention demonstrating monotonic gain response to genuine source evidence and flat near-zero response on newly written content.

## Methodology

**Theoretical foundation.** Building on Shannon source coding and its MDL interpretation, the negative log-probability of a sequence under a fixed model equals its model-relative codelength. SCDG defines:

- Baseline codelength: `L0_θ(P) = −Σ_t log p_θ(p_t | p_<t)`
- Source-conditioned codelength: `LS_θ(P) = −Σ_t log p_θ(p_t | S, p_<t)`
- Description-length gain: `D_θ(P ← S) = L0_θ(P) − LS_θ(P) = log [p_θ(P|S) / p_θ(P)]`

This is a model-relative conditional log-likelihood ratio (PMI-like information-density quantity). The authors ground it in algorithmic information theory as a computable upper bound on conditional Kolmogorov complexity, arguing that a substantial codelength reduction signals model-relative incremental information gain that survives paraphrasing.

**Token-level decomposition.** Because autoregressive codelength is additive, `D_θ(P ← S) = Σ_t g_t(P ← S)` where `g_t = log [p_θ(p_t | S, p_<t) / p_θ(p_t | p_<t)]`. Positive `g_t` marks source-responsive positions. Length-normalized score `D_θ(P ← S) = (1/|A|) Σ_{t∈A} g_t` is computed either over all tokens or over Top-k gain positions (sparse aggregation).

**Pairwise detection.** Predict reuse by thresholding: `ŷ(P,S) = I(D_θ(P ← S) > τ)`, with τ calibrated on validation data.

**Candidate-source reranking.** A first-stage retriever returns Top-K candidates; rerank by descending `D_θ(P ← S)`.

**DAAC fusion indicator.** Combines reciprocal-rank evidence from BM25 (b), sentence-dense (d), and abstract (a) routes with gated interaction `s_DAAC(q,x) = d(x)[1 + U_D(x)·C_A(x)] + b(x)² + a(x)²`, where `U_D` is dense-route uncertainty and `C_A` is abstract-route confidence. Final retrieval score blends normalized DAAC and SCDG: `s_final = (1−λ)·N_q(s_DAAC) + λ·N_q(s_ours)`.

**Backbones.** Three frozen autoregressive LMs: Qwen3-8B-Base, Meta-Llama-3.1-8B, Ministral-3-8B-Base-2512.

**Validation via controlled intervention.** On 300 aligned PAN25 pairs, source context is retained at ratios r ∈ {0, 0.25, 0.50, 0.75, 1}, with removed chunks replaced by length-matched distractors from an external news corpus, five seeds per level. Plagiarism-labeled content shows a strong monotonic gain response to retained evidence; new content stays near zero—confirming SCDG's selective sensitivity.

## Results & Highlights

- **PAN25 pairwise detection (Table 1).** SCDG with Llama-3.1-8B (Top-q) achieves **F1 = 0.9449, Precision = 0.9171, Recall = 0.9707, FPR = 0.2067, MCC = 0.8046, AUROC = 0.9330**, vs. the strongest baseline PAN12 n-gram overlap at **F1 = 0.9041 (Precision 0.8545, Recall 0.9602, FPR 0.3861, MCC 0.6416, AUROC 0.8482)** — a +0.0408 F1 and +0.1629 MCC improvement. Llama Average variant hits F1 = 0.9433 (the headline number).
- **PlagBench prompted-LLM baselines collapse.** All four PlagBench variants (zero/few-shot × vanilla/CoT) predict nearly every pair as positive: **Recall = 1.0000, FPR ≈ 1.0, MCC ≈ 0.0, AUROC as low as 0.5012**, with CoT few-shot reaching MCC = 0.0000 and AUROC = 0.5197—effectively random.
- **PAN26 full-corpus retrieval (Table 2, 200 queries × 86,822 candidates).** DAAC coarse ranking gives nDCG@10 = 0.7678; **adding fixed SCDG reranking raises it to nDCG@10 = 0.8315, MAP = 0.8532, MRR = 0.9925, Recall@100 = 0.9625** — a +0.0637 nDCG@10 gain over DAAC alone and a >0.13 nDCG@10 absolute improvement stated for matched candidate sets. The strongest single-route baseline (Linq sentence-to-chunk) reaches only nDCG@10 = 0.7430.
- **Reranking improves ranking head without recall loss.** SCDG reranking improves nDCG@10 and MAP without changing Recall@1000 (0.9900 for RRF+SCDG, 0.9854 for DAAC+SCDG), showing gains come from reordering, not new retrieval.
- **Cross-backbone consistency.** Performance is broadly stable across Qwen3-8B-Base, Ministral-3-8B-Base-2512, and Llama-3.1-8B, with Llama giving the strongest point estimates—indicating the signal is not model-family-specific.
- **Same-topic robustness (Table 3, Multi-News).** Gain-distribution logistic SCDG achieves **proxy FPR = 0.1250%** on same-cluster article pairs, vs. Positive-gain-rate SCDG at 8.6875%, Average SCDG at 62.8760%, BM25 pair score at 71.1250%, and 4-gram logistic at 29.9375%—demonstrating that topical robustness depends on how the gain distribution is summarized and calibrated.
- **Controlled source-evidence retention (Figure 3).** Across all three backbones, plagiarism-labeled content exhibits a strong monotonic gain response to retained source-evidence ratio (0→1), while new-content gains remain near zero, validating selective sensitivity to genuine reuse.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** Genuine. Reframing plagiarism detection as source-conditioned predictability (a directional PMI-like quantity over a frozen LM) is distinct from both AI-authorship detection (DetectGPT/Fast-DetectGPT/ImBD) and surface/representational similarity. The token-level decomposition + Top-q sparse aggregation is a non-trivial operational contribution beyond the algebraic identity `D = log[p(P|S)/p(P)]`.
- **Rigor:** Strong experimental controls: document-linked group splits preventing overlap, 20 hash-locked test partitions with medians reported, calibrated thresholds, and a controlled source-retention intervention with 95% CIs across five seeds. Multiple metrics (F1, MCC, AUROC, AP, FPR) reported, not just F1.
- **Reproducibility:** Plausible. Three named frozen backbones, fixed candidate sets, development-only hyperparameter selection, and PAN standard benchmarks aid replication. However, full implementation and compute details are relegated to a supplement not present in this text, and the SCDG scoring requires two LM forward passes per pair—scaling behavior on the 86,822-candidate corpus is not characterized in the main text.
- **Significance:** High for the academic-integrity / source-attribution subfield; addresses the realistic PAN26 multi-source generative-plagiarism setting that similarity-based methods fail. Could become a reference signal for scholarly-writing verification pipelines.
- **Red flags / weaknesses:**
  - **PlagBench baselines are alarmingly weak and possibly unfairly configured**: all four variants collapse to all-positive predictions (MCC ≈ 0, AUROC ≈ 0.5). This is presented as evidence of SCDG's superiority, but a baseline that degenerates to "predict everything positive" suggests a prompt/setting mismatch rather than a fair comparison; the authors do not diagnose why PlagBench judging fails here.
  - **No comparison to stronger modern embedding baselines** (e.g., larger BGE, E5-Mistral, GTE) or to supervised pairwise classifiers trained on PAN data; only Linq-Embed-Mistral cosine is represented, and only BM25/BGE-M3 in retrieval.
  - **Same-topic robustness is partly engineered, not inherent.** Average SCDG itself has a 62.8760% proxy FPR on Multi-News; only the calibrated gain-distribution logistic variant reaches 0.125%. The headline 0.125% thus reflects a dedicated calibrator trained on the Multi-News cluster-disjoint split, and the paper notes the calibration→robustness trade-off is relegated to the supplement—making the robustness claim more fragile than the abstract suggests.
  - **Compute cost is a stated limitation** but unquantified; SCDG is "more computationally expensive than conventional similarity measures" and reranks only Top-1000, leaving open how it scales beyond PAN26's 200-query setting.
  - **Domain coverage is narrow**: English scientific documents (PAN25/26) plus one constructed news stress set; no multilingual, cross-domain, or longer-document evaluation.
  - **DAAC's design (squared BM25 term, gated interaction) is introduced without ablation** isolating each component's contribution.

## Strengths

- Principled, training-free formulation grounded in coding theory; the directional, asymmetric nature correctly matches the source→document dependency relation (unlike symmetric similarity).
- Token-level decomposition enables interpretability (where reuse happens) and the Top-q aggregation elegantly handles partial-reuse documents.
- Consistent gains across three independent frozen LMs (Qwen3, Llama-3.1, Ministral-3) demonstrate the signal is not an artifact of one model.
- Strong absolute numbers on community benchmarks (PAN25 F1 ≈ 0.94, PAN26 nDCG@10 ≈ 0.83) with proper group-disjoint splits.
- The controlled source-retention study is a credible mechanistic validation rarely seen in detection papers.

## Limitations

- Computational cost of two LM forward passes per pair; reranking capped at Top-1000 candidates; full scaling analysis missing from the main text.
- Same-topic robustness requires a separately calibrated classifier (gain-distribution logistic); raw Average SCDG is highly confounded (62.8760% FPR), so the robustness headline depends on the calibrator.
- Baseline selection is thin: no supervised pairwise classifiers, no recent large embedding models, and PlagBench LLM-judge baselines appear mis-configured (all-positive collapse).
- Evaluation restricted to English scientific text + a constructed Multi-News stress set; no multilingual, cross-domain, or passage-level localization evaluation.
- DAAC retrieval-fusion components are not ablated.
- SCDG is explicitly a model-relative signal—"evidence of source dependence rather than direct proof of provenance or authorial intent"—and its behavior under adversarial rewriting designed to confuse the frozen scorer is untested.

## Relevance

高度相关于 LLM 安全与学术诚信方向的研究兴趣。SCDG 提供了一种与"检测文本是否由 AI 生成"正交的、针对"源重用"的可解释信号，可作为引用验证、检索增强生成（RAG）中的来源归因、以及学术写作审计管线的候选核心组件，其 token 级分解与稀疏聚合思路对后续多源、跨语言、对抗鲁棒性研究具有方法学启发。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How does SCDG behave under **adversarial rewriting specifically optimized to minimize source-conditioned gain** (e.g., an attacker with white-box access to the frozen scorer)? Is the signal gameable in ways surface similarity is not?
- What is the **real wall-clock and GPU-cost profile** of SCDG reranking on a 86k+ corpus, and can the two-pass scoring be amortized via KV-cache reuse or approximate nearest-neighbor pre-filtering to scale beyond Top-1000?
- Can the **token-level gain profile be aggregated into passage-level evidence spans** that align with human-annotated reuse boundaries, enabling interpretable evidence highlighting rather than only a document-level score?
- How does SCDG **transfer across languages and domains** (e.g., Chinese scientific text, legal documents, code), and does the choice of frozen LM induce systematic bias toward certain rewriting styles?
- Since Average SCDG is heavily confounded by topic (62.8760% FPR) while the gain-distribution logistic calibrator is robust, **what features of the gain distribution are doing the work**, and can a single calibrator generalize across PAN and Multi-News without per-corpus retraining?
- Could SCDG complement **watermarking or retrieval-augmented generation provenance tracking**, providing a post-hoc verification layer when watermarks are absent or stripped?
