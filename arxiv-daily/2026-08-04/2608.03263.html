Pins "ignition" to a measurable commit surface — giving interpretability a geometric handle to test latent reasoning at scale.

Critical Edge

01 · Method

Dual-channel capture, one forward pass.

The core instrumentational idea: a single forward pass per item hooks the final recurrent block, and both channels are deterministic functions of the same captured hidden states hᵣ. This makes the axes commensurable by design — a stated dependence, not an assumed independence.

All estimators (first-hit clock, runway-qualified sharpness, windowed departure, phase-resolved ∥∆h∥) were validated by exact reproduction of reference-class numbers before any verdict capture. Every criterion was frozen before its data existed; every amendment is reference-side and versioned.

02 · Result

The difficulty clock, lawfully ticking.

On 340 pre-committed novel items: the rank-channel first-hit median rises with hop depth — byte-matched to the reference at every k.

04 · Three hypotheses, three verdicts

The dichotomy, corrected to an "interface-constituted commit."

Three candidate readings of what "ignition" is, adjudicated against the captured channels. Select a hypothesis to see the verdict and its evidence.

Not necessary REJECTED
The full ignition signature emerged in a model that has never seen a word of human language.
Trained only on non-verbal synthetic structure, the model still produces the difficulty clock, the margin jump, and the silent walk. Corpus inheritance (MIRROR) is therefore not necessary for this signature in this substrate class — though "sufficient/modulating at scale" remains open.
Not supported in these channels UNCONFIRMED
If composed intermediates were a readable workspace, they should surface through the tied readout.
Relay (intermediate decodability) = 0.00 at every iteration, k, and checkpoint under the pre-registered rank criterion — the "silent walk." This corroborates Lu et al., who found no structured latent pathway in depth-recurrent rank, and sits in tension with DiscoLoop's reported decodable bridge entities.
Insufficient REJECTED
If ignition were merely rank-discreteness, the commit step should look like any other near-threshold step.
Against the identifiable near-threshold null, the arrival step's median |∆| is 7.96 vs 3.15 (in-band 90th pct), with 96.4% of events above it. Against the regression baseline on 5,040 non-event steps, 268/274 event residuals exceed the non-event 90th pct (median residual 6.33 vs 0.36). The transition carries information beyond rank-discreteness alone.

Synthesis. Neither pure projection (MIRROR), nor a legible workspace (WORKSPACE), nor a decode artifact (PRINTER). The ignition is an interface-constituted commit — a real event, but one defined at the readout.

06 · Scope & limits

The beachhead is narrow.

  • Single substrate, single task family. 30M recurrent-depth; pure-depth composition; one closed synthetic world (2,000 facts). The commit-surface geometry is untested at scale, on natural language, or on binding/interference.
  • State-channel fidelity fails. ∥∆h∥ row of C5.5 fails in both pairings; state geometry is realization-dependent under fixed seed, so state-channel claims are caveated to "our realization."
  • Thin counterfactual base. 71 near-threshold controls, all at k=6–10; statistical intervals conditional on the one sampled world.
  • No functional claim tested. Captures run fixed iteration ladders — no halting analysis, no test of whether the event is used. The registered early-exit intervention (forcing readout just before vs. after the commit) remains future work.
  • Magnitude-arc is post-hoc, discovered during capture verification rather than pre-registered; the twin four-cell comparison is confounded.
  • Trust concentration. Single non-academic author, heavy AI-assistance attribution (Fable 5, Opus 4.8) — the receipts repo is doing a lot of the trust work.
07 · Open questions for further work

Where the commit surface breaks — or generalizes.

  1. Does the commit-surface geometry (margin jump + directional snap + radial null) hold in frontier-scale recurrent-depth or universal-thinking models, or is it a small-model closed-world artifact?
  2. The registered early-exit intervention — forcing readout immediately before vs. after the commit step — is the missing functional test. Does perturbing the commit causally change downstream accuracy?
  3. The language-diet arm: does adding verbal training data move the commit surface? Is MIRROR "not necessary" but still "sufficient/modulating" at scale?
  4. Why is state geometry realization-dependent under a fixed seed? Chaotic divergence of training dynamics, or an artifact of the ∥∆h∥ estimator?
  5. relay = 0.00 contradicts DiscoLoop's decodable bridge entities — is intermediate decodability strictly training-regime-dependent, and what regime variable controls it?
  6. Does the radial/angular dissolution generalize beyond LayerNorm decoders, where the ϵ-term / float32 confound disappears?
