A soil-microbiome index, repurposed to keep LLMs from forgetting.

Omega-S turns an ecological resilience index (cluster × density / modularity × degree-concentration) into a drop-in LoRA penalty that needs no previous-task data, no Fisher matrix, no stored old weights. On Llama-3-8B in a code→prose sequential setting it lifts HumanEval pass@1 retention from 62.9% → 84.1% — beating tuned weight decay on 10/10 seeds and tuned EWC on 8/10.

2 Why this matters

A continual-learning fix that beats EWC with strictly weaker operational requirements — no stored optimum, no Fisher matrix, no previous-task data, three-line integration, zero FSDP communication for LoRA. That is the profile production LoRA pipelines can actually deploy.

Statically careful too: 10 seeds, paired sign + Wilcoxon tests, all arms in one session, and a directly measured run-to-run noise floor used to bound every comparison.

3 Critical edge

As implemented, Omega-S does not do what its name promises. The clustering/topology channel is saturated by the logistic map; three of four factors are numerically inert, and the entire effect is carried by degree-variance reduction.

"The name promises topology; the gradient does degree-variance control."
§ 01

How a soil index became a regulariser

Omega-S takes the microbiome resilience index Ω = C·D / (M·Coex) — clustering × density over modularity × degree-concentration — and applies it as a per-layer penalty on the weight-graph adjacency A = W Wᵀ.

Four factors are computed from A: density D = mean(A); clustering C = Tr(A³)/(‖A‖³_F + ε) with the triadic trace estimated by Hutchinson Rademacher probes (dropping per-layer cost from O(N³) to O(N²)); degree concentration Coex = var(k) with k = A1; and an algebraic-connectivity term M = 1/λ₂(L) via three steps of shifted power iteration on the Laplacian.

The per-layer penalty is a log-ratio composite Ω_ℓ = log((M·Coex)/(C·D + ε)), summed across layers and added to the task loss. Two rationales are explicit: the raw trace form is degree-six homogeneous in W (gradient ∝ |W|⁵ — it explodes when weights grow, switches off when they shrink), so the log-ratio + bounded logistic map remove scale sensitivity. λ_Ω is calibrated per-step so the penalty gradient is a fixed 0.03 fraction of the task gradient — making λ_Ω vary per seed (16–41 in the main run).

The headline table is paired with a brutally honest control: a row-norm variance penalty at the same calibration target — the natural "is this just disguised weight decay?" test — reaches only 17.5% retention, losing on 10/10 seeds.

Even the a priori most promising variant, a contrast-preserving cosine construction that revives the clustering channel, loses on all 10 seeds (0.547 vs 0.841) — monotonically ordered by how much the clustering channel moves. The opposite of what a topological reading predicts.

The module-channel ablation seals it: alignment-only (v_proj) at 0.750 beats magnitude-only (q_proj) at 0.499 on 9/10 seeds (p=0.011), but neither alone reproduces the full 0.841. The effect requires acting on both module types.

Generalisation ceiling On GPT-2 124M (full fine-tuning), Omega-S + group lasso does not exceed group lasso alone in structured sparsity (−0.72% vs −0.77% FLOPs). The method should not be claimed to do compression.

Base-weight elasticity / trained-weight elasticity / in-run median relative change. C, D, M are inert (≤10⁻⁴); Coex — degree variance — is ~90× more elastic and alone carries the effect. The logistic map saturates the clustering channel. Verified by python experiments/check_M.py — one command, authors invite replication on other models.

This is the rarest thing in an ML paper: the authors measured their own mechanism and reported that it contradicts their framing. The novel topological content — the triadic Tr(A³) channel — is precisely the channel the measurements show is inert.

They are also open about a text-vs-implementation discrepancy: for non-square W the code uses σ(|W Wᵀ|); for square W it uses σ(|W|). That branch changes the degree-variance term by ~400× on q_proj and is openly declared as a description gap.

Closest antecedent Fiedler regularisation (Tam & Dunson) already minimises λ₂ of a weight graph during training. The authors concede Omega-S's M factor coincides with it. The differentiator — the clustering channel — is the part that turns out to be inert.
§ 04

The design space — three open choices, three honest verdicts

Graph construction Default · bounded logistic map on W Wᵀ
Alternatives enumerated: cosine affinity, rank transform, thresholded graph, doubly stochastic, local scaling. The a priori most promising — contrast-preserving cosine — loses on all 10 seeds (0.547 vs 0.841, p=0.002), monotonically ordered by how much the clustering channel moves.
Objective form Genuine fork — unresolved
Raw trace Tr(A³)/numel(W) vs log-ratio composite. They rank oppositely across retention (log-ratio wins) and pruning (raw trace used earlier) settings. The raw form is the weakest arm on retention. Retained config uses log-ratio.
Modularity orientation M = 1/λ₂ · settled by ecology
Fixed by the originating domain: resistant soil communities show lower modularity, so M = 1/λ₂. The two orientations differ by only +0.075 — below the measured noise floor (std = 0.104), so they are not separable at ten seeds anyway.
§ 05

What this paper cannot claim

  • Single model, single pair, single metric. Llama-3-8B / code→prose / HumanEval only. Plasticity on the second (prose) task is explicitly unmeasured — no Wikitext perplexity — so the stability-plasticity trade-off is only half-characterised.
  • Mechanism ≠ name. As implemented, Omega-S operates by reducing the variance of node degrees. Whether that is separable from row-norm equalisation is only partially resolved (row-norm control beaten 8/10 but at p=0.055).
  • Negative on compression. GPT-2 124M: Omega-S + group lasso does not beat group lasso alone (−0.72% vs −0.77% FLOPs). Do not claim compression.
  • Two objectives under one name. Retention uses log-ratio composite; earlier pruning used raw trace. Different objectives on different architectures.
  • Implementation branch mismatch. Square vs non-square W use different constructions; degree-variance term differs by ~400× between branches.
  • Marginal statistics in a noisy regime. With std = 0.104, the Omega-S vs EWC and Omega-S vs row-norm-control comparisons sit near the edge of distinguishability at ten seeds.
  • EWC mean shifted +0.098 on re-measurement months later — exactly the kind of data quark that should make a reader cautious about any marginal win in this literature.
  • Single-task generalisation untested. Whether degree-variance control helps where weight decay is actually the standard tool is flagged but not measured.
