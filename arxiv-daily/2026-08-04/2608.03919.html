Key finding · NAP

Freeze 98.6% of the network. Optimize only the normalization affine parameters. Recover collapsed low-bit quantization.

Normalization Affine Preconditioning — Peng Xia, Junbiao Pang & Zheng Huang · Beijing University of Technology. A unified, target-aligned framework that treats (γ, β) as a low-dimensional, high-leverage response-control subspace for both BN-based CNNs and RMSNorm-based LLMs.
post-training quantization low-bit W4A4 / W3A4 / W2A4 parameter-efficient CNN + LLM zero inference cost
Key metric
0.33% → 66.11%
MobileNetV2 · W4A4 · ImageNet. Recovered from RTN collapse using only 34.2K trainable params (1.43% of the QAT state). +5.40 pts over QDrop (60.71%), beating full 3.52M tuning (64.28%).
Why this matters

A sub-2%-parameter, zero-inference-overhead adapter that plugs into existing PTQ/QAT pipelines. Where extreme low-bit RTN collapses entirely, NAP recovers usable accuracy — and on LLMs cuts Qwen2.5-3B W4A4 OSCAR perplexity ~3× versus AWQ-style scaling.

One principle, three deployments

Mode i · Pre-PTQ
Proactive conditioning
Before PTQ, optimize affine Φ on the target backend's fake-quant graph G_B with task + KD loss. Cheap preparation that lifts quantization-friendliness.

Lightweight adaptation
Applied to an already-saturated QAT checkpoint to test whether an under-exploited affine direction remains. Sometimes helps, sometimes hurts.

Mode iii · NAP-QAT
Block-coordinate (LLMs)
For RMSNorm LLMs: alternate group-wise quantizer-scale updates (Ω) with RMSNorm-scale updates (Φ). Backbone weights never touched.

04 / THEORY

Why the affine subspace is high-leverage

residual-aware local analysis · Eq. 13–18
Local affine-distortion model
Under per-channel affine distortion, the optimal affine parameters exactly cancel the structured scale-and-shift component:
γ* = γ₀ / α
β* = β₀ − (γ₀ / αρ)·[(α−1)μ + δ]
leaving only the irreducible per-channel residual:
e_c = rounding + clipping residuals
RMSNorm lacks a shift term β, so it can only compensate the multiplicative part — strictly weaker than BN. Distillation NAP then becomes a local projection: minΔΦ ‖ê − J_q ΔΦ‖².
  • 1 Channel-wise broadcast. One or two scalars (Δγ, Δβ) reshape an entire channel across all spatial positions / tokens.
  • 2 Exact affine cancellation. BN's (γ, β) can invert the structured scale-and-shift of quantization distortion — only rounding/clipping is irreducible.
  • 3 Local projection view. KD-NAP corrects only the teacher–student mismatch component expressible in the affine response subspace.
  • 4 Zero added inference cost. BN affine folds into the preceding conv; RMSNorm γ is already part of the model.
05 / BOUNDARIES

What NAP cannot do

honest scope · reported by the authors
Not data-efficient

Requires the full ImageNet training set; 5K/10K images fail catastrophically. Unlike true PTQ methods that need only small calibration batches.

Backend-specific

A NAP checkpoint does not transfer across mismatched fake-quant graphs — users must re-run NAP per deployment backend.

Not universally additive to QAT

NAP degrades the W2A4 OOQ-Freeze checkpoint (67.42 → 62.81). Post-QAT benefit is conditional on the checkpoint's specialization.

Cannot recover non-affine distortion

Severe W3/W2 activation clipping & rounding remain largely uncorrectable; LLM W4A4 still ~10× FP16 perplexity.

Single-run statistics

Several sub-1% QAT deltas (e.g. 70.02 → 70.68) lack variance estimates; statistical robustness unverified.

LLM scope is PTQ-scale

LLM experiments do not perform full-parameter QAT (by design) — NAP-QAT is best read as a lightweight PTQ enhancement, not a QAT replacement.

Authors & provenance

Peng Xia, Junbiao Pang (corresponding), Zheng Huang — School of Information Science & Technology, Beijing University of Technology.

Hardware: RTX 3090 (CNNs) · two MetaX C600-A 72GB (LLMs). Seed 42, single runs unless stated. Code/configs promised upon publication.

Honesty signals

Matched-subset control rules out the "any sparse subset works" null hypothesis. Backend-mismatch ablation proves specificity. Data-size sweep exposes data hunger. One negative case (W2A4 OOQ-Freeze) is openly reported rather than buried.

Closest prior work correctly attributed to Norm Tweaking (Li et al. 2024); authors explicitly disclaim being "the first normalization-only quantization method."
