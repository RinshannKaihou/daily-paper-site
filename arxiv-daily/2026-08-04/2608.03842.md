> **一句话总结：** 本文将"扰动失败由哪一层负责"分解为三种含义——敏感度(LRD散度)、因果性(activation patching恢复)、补偿能力(逐层LoRA)——证明这三张逐层图谱并不一致,在两个通过80%身份补丁门槛的模型(Phi-3.5、Llama-3)上敏感度与因果性呈强负相关(ρ=−0.72到−0.88),并提出"级联破坏"机制:在因果性所指向的早期层放置适配器反而最破坏下游干净计算;固定评测框架下的逐层扫描在GSM8K上验证了诊断标记位点是最差的适配器放置窗口。

> **TL;DR:** The paper decomposes "which layer is responsible for a surface-perturbation failure" into three operationalizations—sensitivity (Layer-wise Representation Divergence, LRD), causality (activation patching recovery), and compensatory capacity (per-layer LoRA effectiveness)—and shows the three per-layer maps dissociate, with sensitivity and causality strongly anti-correlated on the two gate-passing models (Phi-3.5 ρ=−0.88, Llama-3 ρ=−0.72, p<0.001). It proposes *cascade disruption* (early-layer adapters break intact downstream clean computation) and confirms under a fixed generation harness on GSM8K that diagnostic-flagged sites are the *most damaging* adapter windows (e.g., Qwen2.5-7B L08–11 = −7.8 pp vs. deepest L24–27 = +0.3 pp), while a previously-claimed +7.3 pp representation-stability gain reverses to −3.5 pp once a chain-of-thought truncation artifact is fixed.

## 核心贡献

- **三图谱解离(Dissociation):** 首次系统比较"扰动负责层"的三种自然定义——敏感度(LRD)、因果性(activation patching恢复率)、补偿能力(逐层LoRA增益),证明它们峰值出现在不同层;在两个通过80%身份补丁门槛的模型上,敏感度与因果性强负相关(ρ=−0.72 到 −0.88,p<0.001,去相关后有效N≈3–5)。
- **两种传播体制(Regimes)与规模律:** 模型分为spike-and-suppress(Phi-3.5、Gemma-2-9B,早期散度被中深度吸收)与late-accumulation(Llama-3、Mistral、Qwen2.5-7B,散度沿深度持续累积)。Qwen2.5家族内(1.5B→7B→14B)late/early LRD比值1.59→2.90→4.10单调增强,Llama 1B→8B 2.08→5.29,以同家族扫描削弱了"体制即家族"的混淆。
- **级联破坏机制(Cascade Disruption)与已验证的放置反模式:** 早期层适配器破坏其下游所有层的干净计算(上游散度精确为0,从适配窗口起持续到末端),因果性所指向的层恰是最差适配器位点;在固定框架GSM8K扫描中,所有可判定模型上诊断标记窗口都是最破坏性的。
- **方法学警示——CoT截断伪影:** 作者自己曾相信的representation-stability loss(Lstab)在max_new_tokens=100时报告+7.3 pp增益,在修正为512后翻转为−3.5 pp(同一检查点);截断的CoT被按空答案计分,奖励了更短(常更不准)的输出。提出任何CoT干预都应附带"生成长度匹配的零假设"。
- **实用指导:** 训练无关的LRD预筛可在任何训练前排除早/中层的适配器放置;默认放置到最深层窗口(相对排名规则,非正向recipe)。

## Key Contributions

- **Three-map dissociation.** The three natural per-layer notions of "responsible for a perturbation failure"—sensitivity (LRD), causality (activation patching), compensatory capacity (per-window LoRA)—peak at different layers; on the two models passing the 80% identity-patch gate (Phi-3.5, Llama-3), sensitivity and causality are strongly anti-correlated (ρ = −0.72 to −0.88, p < 0.001, post-decorrelation effective N ≈ 3–5).
- **Two propagation regimes plus within-family scaling.** Models split into spike-and-suppress (Phi-3.5, Gemma-2-9B) and late-accumulation (Llama-3, Mistral, Qwen2.5-7B); the late-accumulation signature strengthens monotonically with scale within Qwen2.5 (late/early LRD ratio 1.59→2.90→4.10 across 1.5B/7B/14B) and Llama (2.08→5.29, 1B→8B), every per-perturbation cell monotone within family.
- **Cascade-disruption mechanism and a validated placement anti-pattern.** Early-layer LoRA adapters perturb every downstream layer on clean inputs (upstream divergence exactly zero, disruption onsets at the window and persists to the end); the total clean disruption is largest for the earliest windows. This yields a falsifiable prediction—diagnostic-flagged sites should be the *worst* adapter placements—confirmed by a fixed-harness layer sweep on GSM8K on every adjudicable model.
- **A methodological warning about chain-of-thought evaluation artifacts.** A representation-stability loss the authors themselves previously believed in showed +7.3 pp gain at L15–19 on Phi-3.5 under a truncated harness (max_new_tokens=100); under an adequate budget (512) the same checkpoint reads −3.5 pp. The cause: truncated CoT was scored as empty, rewarding shorter (often less accurate) outputs. The remedy is a generation-length-matched null.
- **Practical placement guidance.** A training-free LRD pre-screen rules out early- and mid-layer adapter placement before any training compute; absent task-specific evidence, default to the deepest window (a relative-ranking rule, not a positive recipe—absolute gains over no-adapter baselines remain small or null).

## Methodology

The paper measures three per-layer signals across a five-model panel (Phi-3.5-mini-3.8B, Mistral-7B-v0.3, Llama-3-8B-Instruct, Gemma-2-9B base, Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct; plus Qwen2.5-1.5B/14B and Llama-3.2-1B for scaling) on GSM8K, MMLU, and BBH, using six surface perturbations (typos 5%, OCR 5%, whitespace 10%, case swap 10%, speech-like 10%, homophones 20%).

1. **Layer-wise Representation Divergence (LRD) — sensitivity.** For mean-pooled hidden states at layer L on (clean, perturbed) pairs: LRD(L) = 1 − cos(h̄_clean^(L), h̄_noisy^(L)). Two summary statistics (linear slope and recovery rate = (LRD(0) − LRD(Lmax))/LRD(0)) classify regimes: spike-and-suppress has high recovery and non-positive slope; late-accumulation has near-zero recovery and positive slope. Per-layer Cohen's d between failing and succeeding examples localizes where the failure signal crystallizes.
2. **Activation patching — causality.** At each layer L the clean hidden state is substituted into the noisy forward pass; recovery = fraction of originally-failed (clean-correct, perturbed-incorrect) examples that now succeed. An all-layer identity patch is the ceiling; a random-noise patch is the floor. Models with identity recovery ≥ 80% are "confirmed," otherwise "exploratory" (a gate that Phi-3.5 at 100% and Llama-3 at 90% pass; Gemma 60%, Qwen-7B 67%, Mistral ≤65% fail).
3. **Per-window LoRA adapters — compensatory capacity.** LoRA (r=4, α=8) attached to q_proj/v_proj (or qkv/o for fused-attention Phi-3.5), base model frozen, trained on perturbed GSM8K with clean supervision and CE on answer positions. Non-overlapping 5-layer windows are swept with three seeds each; the perturbed Δ is mean accuracy change over six perturbations vs. the no-adapter baseline.
4. **Cascade disruption measurement.** For each trained LoRA window, compute LRD between frozen and adapted models on *clean* inputs at every downstream layer; verify upstream divergence is bit-identical (exactly zero) and disruption onsets at the window.
5. **Statistical framework.** Because layer-wise signals autocorrelate (partial-autocorrelation decays to the 95% white-noise band by lags 5–8), correlations use the moving-block bootstrap (Künsch 1989, block size 5, 10k resamples), giving post-decorrelation effective N ≈ 3–5. Per-layer Spearman ρ and Cohen's d are reported across layers per model. A single fixed evaluation harness with adequate max_new_tokens is used throughout (§5.3).

## Results & Highlights

- **Sensitivity–causality anti-correlation on confirmed-gate models.** On Phi-3.5 (32 layers, identity patch 100%) and Llama-3-8B (identity 90%), Spearman ρ between LRD and patching recovery is **ρ = −0.72 to −0.88, p < 0.001** (effective N ≈ 3–5); the two maps peak at different layers. Mistral matches the direction but fails the 80% gate; Gemma-2-9B is the exception (ρ = +0.389), conjectured corrupted by its 60% identity ceiling.
- **Two propagation regimes, scaling-monotone within family.** Recovery rates (fraction of layer-0 divergence eliminated by the final layer): spike-and-suppress models show 21–60% recovery; late-accumulation models show 0–5% (homophones the single exception at ≈35–40%). Within Qwen2.5 the late/early LRD ratio grows monotonically **1.59 → 2.90 → 4.10** (1.5B/7B/14B); within Llama **2.08 → 5.29** (1B/8B); every per-perturbation cell is monotone within family.
- **Qwen2.5-14B clears the patching gate at scale.** Identity recovery rises to **85.0%** (vs. 67% for the 7B variant), with early/mid thirds recovering 73.1%/71.1% of failed examples vs. 42.3% in the late third—consistent with late-accumulation. (A 20.5% random-patch control weakens effect-size attribution but not the identity-ceiling promotion.)
- **Cascade-disruption prediction confirmed by the layer sweep (Table 2, GSM8K, six perturbations, mean Δ pp vs. no-adapter).** Diagnostic-flagged early/mid windows are the most damaging on every adjudicable model:
  - Phi-3.5: every mid-layer window strongly negative (Early-mid −5.3 ± 1.1, Mid −3.5 ± 0.8); the only non-negative window is the deepest, L27–31 (+0.2 ± 0.3 pp, effectively null); All-layers = −8.6 ± 0.7 pp.
  - Qwen2.5-7B: worst window is C3/C4-predicted L08–11 (−7.8 ± 1.7 pp); least-regressive is deepest L24–27 (+0.3 ± 0.0); All-layers = −13.6 ± 0.1 pp.
  - Llama-3-8B: L20–24 (+0.4) and deepest L27–31 (+1.1 ± 0.1) are the only non-negative windows; All-layers = −9.9 ± 1.0 pp.
  - Pre-registered placement tally: **Qwen ✓** (L24–27 beats L08–11 by 8.1 pp; C3/C4's pick is Qwen's *worst* window), **Llama-3 ✓** (L27–31 beats L05–09 by 7.5 pp), **Phi-3.5 ✗** (no window rises meaningfully above zero).
- **Width dose-response (Phi-3.5, widths 3/5/7).** Mid-window damage grows monotonically: **−2.8 → −5.3 → −7.8 pp**; sign structure preserved at every width. Adapting every layer regresses perturbed accuracy more than any single window on all three adjudicable models, and regresses clean accuracy by −8.1 to −14.1 pp.
- **MMLU control: sign-consistent but strongly attenuated.** Only Phi-3.5's diagnostic-flagged mid window L10–14 is reliably nonzero (**−1.8 ± 0.4 pp**); no window on either model produces positive transfer. The attenuation is consistent with cascade disruption, which predicts damage compounds over autoregressive generation steps.
- **CoT truncation artifact reversal.** The representation-stability loss Lstab that previously reported **+7.3 pp** on Phi-3.5 L15–19 under max_new_tokens=100 reads **−3.5 pp** under the fixed harness (max_new_tokens=512); Qwen2.5-7B L24–27 vanilla CE baseline reads **−7.5 pp** where the same checkpoint had read **+11.6 pp**. Toggling only λstab=1.0 under the fixed harness hurts perturbed accuracy by 1.2–3.9 pp across seeds.
- **Cross-task and held-out generalization.** LRD profiles on Phi-3.5 and Mistral are near-identical across GSM8K/MMLU/BBH (**ρ > 0.95**); three held-out perturbation types (char_insert, char_swap, qwerty) reproduce the regime gap at ~5× (e.g., Llama-3 final-layer LRD 0.066–0.105 vs. Phi-3.5 0.016–0.023 on GSM8K).

## Authors & Credibility

Authors are Nathan Labiosa, David Buff, Ena Nayak, and Erica Donno, all at the University of Southern California (USC), listed with institutional email addresses. The paper is unusually self-critical for a preprint: it explicitly flags a previously-believed intervention (the representation-stability loss) as a harness artifact, separates "confirmed" from "exploratory" results via a pre-registered 80% identity-patch gate, prospectively registered the C3/C4 metric pick on Qwen-7B before running its sweep (and reported the falsification honestly), and discloses AI-assistant use confined to code scaffolding and copy-editing with all scientific claims attributed to the authors. Code, configurations, and per-model logs are released at https://github.com/NathanLabiosa/Layer-Wise-Representation-Divergence.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** Genuine. The three-map dissociation framing (sensitivity vs. causality vs. compensatory capacity) is a fresh conceptual contribution; the anti-correlation between where divergence peaks and where patching helps is non-obvious and directly contradicts the implicit assumption of much interpretability-for-intervention work. Cascade disruption is a clean, falsifiable mechanistic hypothesis.
- **Rigor:** High *within its declared scope* but the scope is narrow. The authors are transparent about statistical fragility: the headline anti-correlation rests on effective N ≈ 3–5 after autocorrelation correction (block-bootstrap block size 5, Künsch 1989), drawn from only the two gate-passing models plus Mistral directionally. The LRD × LoRA correlations mostly miss per-model significance individually—the authors claim a sign pattern, not individual coefficients, and say so plainly.
- **Reproducibility:** Strong. All models and benchmarks are public with stated licenses; exact hyperparameters and configs are in Appendix P; code is released. Three seeds per window is light for fine-grained between-window comparisons but adequate for direction.
- **Significance:** Practically important for the interpretability-for-intervention subfield. The central actionable claim—"do not place repair adapters where divergence or patching point; default to the deepest window"—is a *negative* result with a mechanism, and the LRD pre-screen is cheap. The CoT-truncation warning is broadly applicable beyond this paper.
- **Red flags / specific weaknesses:**
  - **Small panel and family–regime confound.** Only five models; regime tracks model family within the panel, and the within-family scans that de-confound it cover only two families (Qwen 3-point, Llama 2-point, the 1B Llama point with a narrow 17-layer bin caveat). No universal-scaling claim is made, but the taxonomy's generality is unproven.
  - **Patching ceiling effects corrupt the causal map on 3 of 5 models.** Gemma (60%), Qwen-7B (67%), and Mistral (≤65%, unstable across configurations) fail the 80% gate; the central dissociation claim is therefore anchored on 2 of 5 models. Qwen-14B's 85% comes with a 20.5% random-patch control that weakens effect-size attribution.
  - **No positive intervention recipe.** All placement claims are relative-ranking under a fixed harness; absolute gains over no-adapter baselines are small (Llama-3 +1.1 pp) or null (Phi-3.5, Qwen). The "default-deepest" rule is a relative ranking, not a recipe that beats the baseline.
  - **Mistral learning-rate sensitivity is hand-waved.** Every Mistral window is strongly negative at the shared-grid lr (5e−5); a single-cell re-run at 1e−5 recovers to +0.13 pp, attributed to an lr artifact—yet only one cell was re-run, and Mistral is then excluded from adjudication rather than fully re-swept.
  - **Task and generation-length scope.** Sweep adjudication is GSM8K only, with MMLU as a short-form control on one model per regime; intermediate generation lengths are untested. All experiments are English-only.
  - **An overclaim risk the authors themselves flag:** the C3/C4 intrinsic metrics showed post-hoc correlation (|ρ| = 0.54–0.75) on the original three models but *failed prospective validation* on Qwen-7B (predicting its "best" window that turned out to be its worst, −7.8 pp). The authors reclassify C3/C4 as descriptive only—a honest correction but a real failure of a hypothesis they had registered.

## Strengths

- Conceptually clean three-way decomposition of an ambiguous question ("which layer is responsible?") with operational, independently-measurable definitions.
- Mechanistic explanation (cascade disruption) that makes a falsifiable, behavioral prediction—then tests it under a fixed harness and confirms it on every adjudicable model.
- Exceptional methodological honesty: pre-registered placement tally with one model scored as a fail; prospective falsification of C3/C4 reported rather than buried; the authors' own previously-believed intervention exposed as a CoT-truncation artifact.
- Cheap, training-free, actionable practitioner output: LRD pre-screen plus a default-deepest placement rule.
- Public code and configs; adequate statistical disclosure (moving-block bootstrap with explicit effective-N).

## Limitations

- Five-model panel with regime–family confound; only two within-family scaling scans (Qwen primary, Llama direction-only); no controlled multi-family sweep varying architecture and scale independently.
- The headline anti-correlation rests on effective N ≈ 3–5 and only two gate-passing models; the LRD × LoRA correlations miss per-model significance and are claimed only as a sign pattern.
- Patching ceilings (60–67%) make three of five models "exploratory," narrowing the base of the central causal claim.
- No positive intervention recipe: placement guidance is a relative ranking, and absolute gains over no-adapter baselines are small (best +1.1 pp) or null.
- Mistral fully re-swept at lr=1e−5 at only a single cell; Gemma is "adapter-insensitive" (every window within ±0.7 pp of zero), so two of five models contribute no placement signal.
- GSM8K-only sweep adjudication; MMLU attenuation is interpreted post-hoc as generation-length compounding but not tested at intermediate lengths; English-only.

## Relevance

对关注机械可解释性(activation patching、逐层功能定位)与LLM鲁棒性的研究有直接价值:本文揭示了一个反直觉但实操重要的结论——诊断(散度/补丁)指向的层恰是最差的修复介入位点,且这种解离随规模在家族内单调增强,这对任何想用解释性诊断指导适配器/表示工程放置的工作都是必须知道的陷阱。对从事LoRA/参数高效微调、扰动鲁棒性评测、以及CoT评测方法论(生成长度匹配零假设)的研究者同样高度相关。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the three-map dissociation (and the sensitivity–causality anti-correlation) hold on encoder-only or encoder-decoder architectures, or on models trained with substantially different objectives (e.g., heavy RLHF, diffusion-LMs), where layer function may differ from the decoder-only panel studied here?
- A controlled multi-family sweep varying architecture and scale *independently* (e.g., two architectures at matched parameter counts) would cleanly separate the regime taxonomy from family identity—the authors explicitly flag this as the missing next step.
- Can cascade disruption be *mitigated* rather than avoided—e.g., by freezing the immediate downstream layers, by residual/gated adapters that are no-op on clean inputs, or by distillation losses that constrain the adapted model's clean-input divergence to zero?
- Does the generation-length compounding account predict a smooth damage-vs-generation-length curve at intermediate lengths (between MMLU single-token and GSM8K long-CoT), and can it quantitatively fit the MMLU-to-GSM8K attenuation?
- The LRD pre-screen is training-free and cheap; could it be combined with a small *causal* probe (beyond identity-patch ceiling) to recover a positive placement recipe that beats the no-adapter baseline, rather than only a least-regressive ranking?
- Is the spike-and-suppress vs. late-accumulation split predictive of *other* robustness phenomena (e.g., adversarial attacks, distribution shift, prompt sensitivity), or is it specific to surface perturbations?
