Paper dashboard · generative plagiarism detection

SCDG.

Source-Conditioned Description-Length Gain — measuring how much a candidate source shortens a frozen LM's encoding of a suspicious document.

Key finding

Generative plagiarism can be detected as source-conditioned predictability: a directional, training-free likelihood ratio over a frozen autoregressive LM. The same signal thresholds for pairwise detection and reranks 86k-candidate retrieval — no task-specific fine-tuning.

training-free directional (asymmetric) token-decomposable PMI-like frozen LM × 3 backbones PAN25 / PAN26 / Multi-News

Why this matters

  • Orthogonal to AI-authorship detection — targets source reuse, not "is this AI-generated".
  • One signal, two tasks: pairwise detection + 86k-candidate reranking, no fine-tuning.
  • Interpretable: token-level gain pinpoints where reuse happens.
  • Fits citation verification, RAG provenance, scholarly-writing audit.

Critical edge

Robustness is engineered, not inherent. Raw Average SCDG suffers a 62.876% FPR on same-topic pairs; only a separately-trained gain-distribution logistic reaches 0.125%.

PlagBench LLM-judge baselines collapse to all-positive predictions (MCC ≈ 0) — possibly mis-configured, not a fair fight. Compute cost of two LM forward passes per pair is stated but unquantified.

01 / METHOD

Codelength as a plagiarism signal

Shannon · MDL · Kolmogorov

The SCDG identity

# baseline codelength of suspicious doc P L0_θ(P) = −Σ_t log p_θ(p_t | p_<t) # source-conditioned codelength, with candidate S in context LS_θ(P) = −Σ_t log p_θ(p_t | S, p_<t) D_θ(P ← S) = L0_θ(P) − LS_θ(P) = log [ p_θ(P|S) / p_θ(P) ] # PMI-like, directional

A substantial codelength reduction means S carries model-relative incremental information about P — and the reduction survives paraphrasing, because it is computed at the probability level, not over surface tokens.

Because autoregressive codelength is additive, the document gain decomposes exactly per token: D = Σ_t g_t. Positive g_t marks source-responsive positions.

Token-level gain profile

Per-token g_t across a 40-token window of a suspicious document. The signal localizes which spans the source helps predict.

high gain (reuse) moderate near zero
  • Top-q sparse aggregation averages only the highest-gain tokens, so partial reuse is not diluted by document-level averaging.
  • Pairwise: ŷ = I(D > τ), τ calibrated on validation.
  • Reranking: order Top-1000 candidates by descending D.
  • Backbones: Qwen3-8B · Llama-3.1-8B · Ministral-3-8B.

03 / MECHANISM

Controlled source-evidence retention

Figure 3 · 300 pairs × 5 seeds

Gain responds monotonically to genuine evidence

Removed chunks are replaced with length-matched distractors. Plagiarism content scales with r; new content stays at the noise floor across all three backbones.

DAAC: rank-only retrieval fusion

To survive an 86k-candidate corpus without per-pair LM scoring, SCDG is paired with DAAC (Dense-Anchored Abstract Calibration) — a rank-only fusion of three retrieval routes that produces a compact Top-1000 shortlist.

# gated interaction s_DAAC(q,x) = d(x)·[1 + U_D(x)·C_A(x)] + b(x)² + a(x)² # final rerank score s_final = (1−λ)·N_q(s_DAAC) + λ·N_q(s_SCDG)
  • b(x) BM25 rank · d(x) sentence-dense rank · a(x) abstract rank
  • U_D dense-route uncertainty, C_A abstract-route confidence
  • No extra neural inference at fusion stage.
  • Caveat: each DAAC component is not ablated in the main text.
04 / VERDICT

What holds up, what to probe

strengths · red flags

+ Strengths

  • Principled & training-free. Directional PMI-like quantity correctly matches the source→document dependency; not symmetric similarity.
  • Token decomposition + Top-q gives interpretable, localized evidence and handles partial reuse without dilution.
  • Cross-backbone consistency across Qwen3, Llama-3.1, Ministral-3 — not a single-model artifact.
  • Strong absolute numbers on community benchmarks with proper document-linked group-disjoint splits.
  • Controlled retention study is a mechanistic validation rarely seen in detection papers.

! Red flags & limitations

  • Same-topic robustness is calibrated, not inherent. Raw Average SCDG: 62.876% FPR; the 0.125% headline depends on a per-corpus logistic trained on Multi-News.
  • PlagBench baselines degenerate to all-positive (MCC ≈ 0). Presented as win, but likely a prompt/setting mismatch — authors don't diagnose why.
  • No modern embedding baselines (BGE-large, E5-Mistral, GTE) and no supervised pairwise classifiers on PAN data.
  • Compute cost stated but unquantified; scaling beyond 200 queries × Top-1000 rerank is open.
  • Domain coverage narrow: English scientific text + one constructed news stress set. No multilingual / cross-domain / passage-level eval.
  • Adversarial rewriting untested — behavior under attacks tuned to minimize gain against the frozen scorer is unknown.

Ming Li is an established information-theory / compression researcher. The framing draws legitimately on Shannon coding, Rissanen's MDL, and Kolmogorov complexity (Li & Vitányi), and cites recent compression-by-LM work (Delétang et al. 2024; Li et al. 2025, Nature Machine Intelligence).

Overview-paper comparisons (Greiner-Petter 2025; Bevendorff 2026) are co-authored by the benchmark organizers.

The core method is simple enough to reproduce from the paper: two forward passes, same target-token IDs. Full implementation and compute details are deferred to a supplement.

06 / NEXT

Open questions

for follow-up work
  1. How does SCDG behave under adversarial rewriting optimized to minimize source-conditioned gain (white-box access to the frozen scorer)? Is it gameable in ways surface similarity is not?
  2. What is the real wall-clock / GPU-cost profile on an 86k+ corpus? Can two-pass scoring be amortized via KV-cache reuse or ANN pre-filtering to scale beyond Top-1000?
  3. Can the token-level gain profile be aggregated into passage-level evidence spans aligned with human-annotated reuse boundaries, for interpretable highlighting?
  4. How does SCDG transfer across languages and domains (Chinese scientific, legal, code), and does the frozen-LM choice bias toward certain rewriting styles?
  5. If raw Average SCDG is topic-confounded yet the logistic calibrator is robust, what gain-distribution features do the work, and can one calibrator generalize without per-corpus retraining?
  6. Could SCDG complement watermarking or RAG-provenance tracking as a post-hoc verification layer when watermarks are absent or stripped?
