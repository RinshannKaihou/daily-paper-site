Logic before Language.

Jo-Ku Cheng · Nikolaos Aletras · Marco Valentino — University of Sheffield
Key
Finding

Pre-training a 254M Qwen3 on 247 formal-logic inference schemata before it ever sees natural language makes it learn linguistic skills 36B tokens faster — and the resulting backbone is so low-rank and spectrally concentrated that Wanda pruning to ≈33% sparsity still matches a dense model.

Key Metric
87.4%
mean acc · 17 elemental tasks

+7.1 pts over Formal-PPT (80.3%), +10.1 over PT (77.3%). At 40% Wanda sparsity the accuracy drop is just 14.4% vs 24.7% for PT.

Why This Matters

First causal link from PPT-induced geometry to a deployable efficiency lever.

Representation geometry stops being descriptive — it becomes something you can engineer for compressibility.

Critical Edge

Headline 100B-token runs are single-seed with no error bars. The 7.1-pt gap could shrink.

Perplexity barely moves (<1%), all claims hold only at 254M, and no code or data is released.

01Two-stage initialisation

Teach the weights to derive before you teach them to read.

STAGE 1 · LOGIC PRE-PRETRAINING

Synthetic derivations only

A library of 247 schemata (49 propositional · 77 term · 121 first-order). For each goal φ, backward chaining builds a depth-L=10 derivation tree; structure-preserving renaming (n=5) diversifies surface form; post-order traversal expands each tree into next-step prediction targets.

➜
STAGE 2 · NATURAL-LANGUAGE PRETRAINING

Drop symbolic head, attach fresh embeddings

Retain the backbone Θ, discard symbolic E*/H*. Attach a 32K Mistral embedding + head and train on ≈100B FineWeb-Edu tokens, causal-LM objective, cosine peak LR 3e-4, 127,160 steps, BF16.

Backbone 14-layer Qwen3 220M transformer · 254M total hidden 1024 · heads 16 · KV 8 seq 2048 · batch 96 hw 4× NVIDIA GH200

What the numbers say

  • Skill speed. Hits 80% mean accuracy using 36B fewer tokens than PT in the 100B run; earliest of any PPT config.
  • Per-task edge. Beats strongest competitor by 5.5–6.7 pts on Morphology, Reading Comprehension, and Compositional tasks.
  • Perplexity is a wash. 11.074 (Logic/Formal) vs 11.183 (PT) — a <1% reduction, so the real signal lives entirely in the elemental tasks.
  • Pruning resilience. At ≈33% sparsity Logic-PPT matches dense PT — the headline deployment payoff.
  • Seed stability (10B). Self-CKA Pearson r > 0.99 across 3 seeds for every model — but only at 10B, and only for CKA.

03Why logic bends the geometry

Formal derivations push the backbone into a low-rank, spectrally concentrated regime — and that regime is exactly what Wanda likes.

RankMe · spectral decay

During the compression-seeking phase, Logic-PPT produces the lowest RankMe and highest spectral decay in mid (4-9) and upper (10-13) layers — the "compressible" signature.

CKA · block structure

Clearest block-diagonal self-CKA with lowest boundary-layer similarity; pairwise CKA vs PT shows Logic-PPT is most dissimilar precisely in middle layers 4-9.

Stable rank · weight space

Attention & MLP stable rank decrease continually, no plateau throughout Stage 1 — Formal/Alg-PPT flatten after early steps. Logic imposes a stronger, persistent structural bias.

04Contributions

Formal derivations as a PPT signal
First use of variables, quantifiers & predicate-argument composition over long contexts — richer than Dyck (brackets) or sorting (sequence transforms).
Principled data pipeline
Backward chaining (L=10) + structure-preserving renaming (n=5) + post-order traversal into next-step targets, from a 247-schema library.
100B-token tri-axis analysis
Behavioural (BLiMP + 17 tasks) · mechanistic (CKA / RankMe / spectral) · interventional (Wanda) — extending prior <10B work.
Geometry ↔ compressibility link
First demonstration that PPT-induced low-rank geometry translates into pruning robustness, matching dense PT at ≈33% sparsity.

05Strengths vs. red flags

A genuinely novel framing wrapped around a single-seed, small-scale, narrow-metric result.

▲ Strengths

  • Clean behavioural / mechanistic / interventional framing lifts it above "yet another symbolic warm-up."
  • Derivation design is well-grounded: backward chaining guarantees validity; renaming controls surface memorisation.
  • Mechanistic pillar is the strongest — cleanly reproduces Li et al.'s entropy-seeking → compression-seeking phases.
  • Compressibility result is a concrete downstream payoff, not just a chart.
  • Honest limitations section: single-seed, 254M, English-only, no SFT.

▼ Red flags

  • Headline 7.1-pt delta is single-seed, no error bars, no 100B seed replication.
  • Perplexity barely moves (<1%) — practical LM-quality gain is marginal.
  • 17 tasks are post-hoc filtered by an 80%-reachability rule that mechanically favours PPT over PT.
  • Pruning story is Wanda-only; no distillation, quantization, or SFT stress test.
  • 254M only; geometry at billion-param scale behaves differently — untested.
  • Token-matched (2.5B) ≠ compute-matched: formal-derivation tokens carry more information than Dyck tokens.
  • No code or data released despite a many-moving-parts pipeline.

06Open questions

Click to expand what a careful reader should press on.

Does the 7.1-pt win survive multi-seed 100B replication?
No variance is reported at scale; three-seed CKA stability at 10B is suggestive but not the same as accuracy replication. The headline gap could be within seed noise.
Is the pruning gain due to logic, or to any signal that lowers stable rank?
A controlled comparison holding final RankMe constant across PPT variants would isolate the cause. Without it, "logic" and "low-rank-inducing pre-training" are confounded.
Do the gains survive SFT, instruction tuning, or INT8/FP8 quantization?
Industrial deployment happens after fine-tuning and quantization — the paper explicitly lists this as a limitation and provides no evidence either way.
Does the compression-seeking signature appear at billion-parameter scale?
Small-model representation dynamics are known to differ at scale; the central mechanistic claim is untested beyond 254M.
Is mid-layer (4-9) dissimilarity from PT causal or merely correlated?
Testable via layer-targeted ablation or freezing during Stage 2 — not done here.
Can the schema library extend to modal, second-order, or probabilistic logic?
First-order logic handles quantifier scope, modality and intensionality weakly; richer logics could cover more linguistic phenomena.
