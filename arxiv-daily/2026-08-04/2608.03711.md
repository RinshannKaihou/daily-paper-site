> **一句话总结：** 本文以纯 prompt 端的大小写变换为唯一干预手段，系统刻画了 13 个 LLM/VLM 中"大小写吸引注意力"这一固有现象：把目标片段用大写/交错大写写在以小写为主的上下文里，会稳定地把内部注意力质量聚拢到该片段（以及对应图像区域），但这种"更专注"并不等于更准——大写目标在 MMLU-Pro/ARC 上可带来 +14.8 pp 的提升，而高熵的交错大小写虽吸引最多注意力却反而使精度下降最多 −13.96 pp；推理（thinking）阶段在纯文本中充当"语义缓冲"削弱该效应，却在多模态中放大了对文本模态的依赖。

> **TL;DR:** Through prompt-only letter-case manipulation (no fine-tuning, no model access), this 13-model empirical study shows that pretrained LLMs/VLMs internalize typographic casing as a latent attentional attractor: formatting answer spans in uppercase against lowercase context reliably concentrates attention and yields large gains (e.g., LLaMA-3.1-8B on MMLU-Pro rises 29.59% → 44.39% under TE1, +14.8 pp; Qwen2.5-14B +9.06 pp; Mistral-7B +15.3 pp), whereas high-entropy alternating case attracts the most attention but degrades accuracy (worst case −13.96 pp on LLaMA-3.1-8B). The textual effect is universal across 7 non-reasoning LLMs and all three tokenizer families (BPE/SentencePiece/BBPE), the "reasoning buffer" of thinking models dampens it in text but amplifies text-modality reliance in VLMs (peak 43.74% visual disengagement on Qwen3-VL-2B-Thinking under TE2), and only Target-Emphasis schemes (TE1/TE3) reliably act as productive attractors.

## 核心贡献

- **首次将"大小写=注意力吸引子"作为预训练 transformer 的固有属性进行系统刻画**：在 7 个非推理 LLM、2 个推理 LLM、2 个非推理 VLM、2 个推理 VLM 上，以 15 种静态大小写方案（U/TE/TT/TA/ADE）作为预分词干预，证明大小写是受训权重里已存在的潜在重要性信号，而非分词器假象。
- **提出"注意力—性能背离"的中心观察**：交错大小写（TA）是最强注意力吸引子（在 Qwen2.5-7B 上把 ARC 的目标注意力质量从 0.061 抬到 0.104，近乎翻倍），但却是"破坏性吸引子"，反而降低精度；大写目标强调（TE1/TE3）是少数能稳定提升精度的"生产性吸引子"。
- **通过对抗式去强调（ADE）做因果验证**：把目标小写、上下文大写会稳定压制目标注意力，证明注意力机制是对"显著性反差"作出响应，而非对任意改动响应。
- **跨模态拓展并识别双轴响应**：在 RefCOCOg 上，prompt 端的大小写重组跨模态注意力——宏观上把整体图像注意力抽走（平均 −1.85 pp，相对 −12.41%），微观上把剩余视觉预算聚拢到目标框（平均 +1.55 pp，相对 +23.08%）；TA > TE > 其余 的家族排序在聚合层面跨模态保持。
- **发现"推理缓冲"这一边界条件及其跨模态反转**：纯文本中 thinking 阶段几乎抹平大小写敏感性（Qwen3-4B-Thinking 的 TA/TE 注意力位移仅 +0.23/+0.16 pp，gpt-oss-20B 仅 +0.07/+0.05 pp）；但在 VLM 中，视觉推理反而放大对文本流的宏观依赖，峰值在 Qwen3-VL-2B-Thinking 达 43.74% 视觉抽离。
- **机制层面证据与缓解方向**：第 24 层隐藏态余弦相似度显示 TA 引发严重语义漂移（0.341）而 TE 保持较稳（0.496）；据此提出"大小写无关的 Layer Normalization"作为概念性架构缓解方向（未实证）。

## Key Contributions

- First systematic empirical characterization of letter casing as a *latent attentional attractor* inherent to pretrained transformers, isolated via a strictly pre-tokenization, ground-truth-span-guided protocol across 9 LLMs and 4 VLMs spanning 5 families and 3 tokenizer types (BPE, SentencePiece, BBPE) — operating with zero model access (no fine-tuning, no hooks, no activation editing).
- A taxonomy of 15 deterministic case interventions (U1–3, TE1–3, TT1–3, TA1–3, ADE1–3) that cleanly separates *productive* attractors (uppercase Target-Emphasis) from *destructive* ones (high-entropy Target-Alternating), turning the study into a causal probe rather than a prescriptive method.
- The central "attention–performance divergence" finding: the schemes that most strongly attract attention (TA) systematically *degrade* accuracy, while only TE1/TE3 reliably improve it, refuting the common steering assumption that "more attention on the relevant span ⇒ better accuracy."
- Causal validation through adversarial de-emphasis (ADE), which suppresses target attention and serves as a consistent negative control across all models.
- Cross-modal transfer analysis on RefCOCOg showing a coupled dual-axis response (macroscopic image disengagement + microscopic concentration on the target box), and a modality-reversing "reasoning buffer" effect that dampens typographic sensitivity in text but amplifies text-stream reliance in VLMs.
- Mechanistic corroboration via layer-24 hidden-state cosine similarity (TA: 0.341 vs TE: 0.496) and a proposed (conceptual, unverified) architectural mitigation using case-agnostic Layer Normalization.

## Methodology

The study is framed as a *characterization study*, not a method paper. The protocol is entirely training-free and input-driven.

**Causal isolation at the pre-tokenization level.** Given raw text `X` and a ground-truth answer span or bounding-box label `A ⊆ X`, the authors construct `X̃ = T_C(X)` where `C` assigns one of {upper, lower, title, alternating} per character. The transformation satisfies a semantic-preservation constraint `∀i, lower(c_i(x_i)) = lower(x_i)`, so lexical identity is invariant even when the tokenizer yields different token IDs. A two-stage matching protocol (exact case-insensitive match for 98.2% of samples; Levenshtein ≤2 fallback for 1.8%, manually verified on a 100-sample subset) locates `A`. Whitespace, punctuation, and token boundaries are preserved; mixed-case entities like "iPhone" are wholly recased (→ "IPHONE").

**Intervention taxonomy (15 schemes).**
- *Global Uniformity (U1–U3):* all-caps / all-lowercase / title-case baselines.
- *Target Emphasis (TE1–3):* target uppercase, context in lower / alternating / natural case.
- *Target Title (TT1–3):* target title-case, context in upper / lower / natural.
- *Target Alternating (TA1–3):* target in alternating case (e.g., CoRrEcT), context in upper / lower / natural.
- *Adversarial De-Emphasis (ADE1–3):* target *lowercased*, context in upper / alternating / natural — the causal negative control.

**Models (13).** LLaMA-3.2-3B-Instruct, LLaMA-3.1-8B-Instruct; Gemma-3-1B-IT, Gemma-2-2B-IT, Gemma-3-4B-IT, Gemma-4-E4B-IT; Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3; Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct, Qwen3-4B-Thinking-2507, gpt-oss-20B (text); Qwen3-VL-4B-Instruct, Qwen3-VL-2B-Thinking (vision). Five families, three tokenizer types, two inference regimes (direct vs reasoning).

**Benchmarks.** MMLU-Pro and ARC-Challenge (factual/scientific MCQ), SQuADv2 (reading comprehension), RefCOCOg (cross-modal visual grounding, where the target label is embedded in a template sentence and the image is *never* altered). Appendix adds XQuAD (cross-lingual) and HumanEval (code).

**Measured quantities.** Mean attention mass on the target span `Att_A(f_θ(X̃))` aggregated across layers/heads (and over the ground-truth bounding box for VLMs), and task accuracy `Acc(f_θ(X̃))`, always reported relative to each model's naturally-cased baseline. Latency overhead is also measured (mean ≈ 0.0015 ms, negligible). Three guiding principles: causal attribution (case is the only independent variable), zero-inference observation (frozen off-the-shelf weights), and phenomenological reporting of both gains and failures.

## Results & Highlights

- **TE1 (uppercase target, lowercase context) yields double-digit gains on MCQ benchmarks.** On LLaMA-3.1-8B-Instruct / MMLU-Pro, accuracy rises from 29.59% (baseline) to 44.39% (+14.8 pp), with target attention mass increasing from 0.017 to 0.022 (+29.4% relative). On Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3 / MMLU-Pro, 29.84% → 45.14% (+15.3 pp). On Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct / MMLU-Pro, 52.70% → 61.76% (+9.06 pp). (Tables 5, 8, 10.)
- **Target-Alternating is the strongest but most destructive attractor.** On the discovery model Qwen2.5-7B, TA3 (+2.77 pp) and TA2 (+2.75 pp) produce the largest gains in mean attention mass on the target, yet on ARC-Challenge the absolute target attention mass under TA2 reaches 0.104 — nearly double the 0.061 baseline — while accuracy drops (e.g., −2.88 pp on MMLU-Pro in the discovery setting; worst case −13.96 pp on LLaMA-3.1-8B-Instruct). This is the headline "attention–performance divergence."
- **Cross-modal transfer is partial and dual-axis.** On RefCOCOg, prompt-side casing induces a microscopic concentration on the target bounding box (mean +1.55 pp absolute, +23.08% relative) *and* a macroscopic disengagement from the whole image (mean −1.85 pp, −12.41% relative). The family ordering TA > TE > rest is preserved in aggregate, but the macroscopic axis is the only sign-consistent signature across all four VLMs.
- **Pattern-conflict drives modality disengagement, peaking under reasoning.** TE2 (uppercase target inside an alternating-case context) is the primary modality disruptor, producing the largest whole-image drain (−4.11 pp) and the strongest cross-model disengagement (mean 33.35%, peaking at 43.74% on Qwen3-VL-2B-Thinking). Uniform lowercasing (U2) minimally perturbs spatial allocation (+0.48 pp).
- **Reasoning buffer: dampens in text, amplifies in vision.** In text, Qwen3-4B-Thinking's peak attention shifts for TA/TE are only +0.23 / +0.16 pp, and gpt-oss-20B's only +0.07 / +0.05 pp, with accuracy fluctuations within ±0.5 pp. In VLMs the effect reverses: reasoning VLMs disengage from vision most strongly (Qwen3-VL-2B-Thinking mean 14.88%, peak 43.74% under TE2; Gemma-4-E4B-IT mean 12.92%, peak 28.83% under TA1), while direct-inference VLMs remain more vulnerable to fine-grained microscopic steering (Gemma-3-4B-IT peaks at +6.23 pp under TA1 and +5.74 pp under TE2).
- **Universality across tokenizer families.** The textual casing effect holds across every evaluated non-reasoning model and all three tokenizer families (BPE, SentencePiece, BBPE), confirming it is a representational rather than tokenizer artifact; scaling does not wash it out (larger Qwen2.5-14B shows more refined TE utilization).
- **Mechanistic corroboration.** Layer-24 hidden-state cosine similarity against baseline is 0.496 under TE but only 0.341 under TA (max localized divergence gap up to 0.381), showing that TE refines token salience while TA distorts the latent geometry.
- **Adversarial validation works.** ADE schemes (target lowercased) consistently yield attention mass equal to or below baseline across all tables, confirming the mechanism responds to salient contrast, not arbitrary change.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** Genuine and timely. Prior attention-steering work (PASTA, AutoPASTA, InstA-Boost, ROME, MEMAT, activation engineering) requires white-box hooks, weight edits, or activation injection; this paper documents that an input-level, pre-tokenization, zero-access variable (letter case) already produces large internal shifts — reframing case from "a tokenization nuisance to normalize away" into "a latent steering lever." The cognitive-typography framing (uppercase salience in human reading) is a nice interdisciplinary hook.
- **Rigor:** The causal isolation protocol is strong — ground-truth-span matching, semantic-preservation constraint, three tokenizer families, adversarial de-emphasis as negative control, and reporting of both gains and failures. The attention–performance divergence is an honest and important nuance that many steering papers gloss over.
- **Reproducibility concerns / data quirks (paper-specific):**
  - The reasoning-model baselines are clearly broken: **gpt-oss-20B scores 0.33% on MMLU-Pro and 0.85% on ARC-Challenge**, and **Qwen3-4B-Thinking-2507 scores 3.74% / 6.84%** — orders of magnitude below their published numbers. The authors attribute the near-zero attention/accuracy sensitivity of reasoning models to a "reasoning buffer," but the data are equally (more) consistent with an answer-parsing or evaluation harness failure for these two models. The central "reasoning buffer" claim rests on models whose baselines are non-functional, which materially weakens that sub-finding.
  - Only one discovery model (Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct) drives the main attention-mass analysis; the cross-model generalization in Fig. 4 is plotted but the per-scheme per-model numbers in Tables 4–12 show real inconsistency (e.g., Gemma-3-1B-IT TE1 on MMLU-Pro is only +2.5 pp, vs +14.8 pp for LLaMA-3.1-8B), so "universal" overstates the magnitude consistency even if the sign is consistent.
  - The proposed architectural mitigation (case-agnostic LayerNorm before late semantic blocks) is explicitly *unverified* — labelled "conjecture" and "an open avenue for future empirical work" — so it should not be read as a contribution.
  - Multilingual (XQuAD) and code (HumanEval) results are relegated to the appendix and only superficially integrated into the main claims.
- **Significance:** Moderate-to-high as a foundational/descriptive contribution. It is unlikely to be deployed as-is (the authors explicitly disclaim an automated pipeline, Sec. 3.5), but it gives the community a cheap, zero-access diagnostic and a vocabulary (productive vs destructive attractors, reasoning buffer) that should inform prompt-engineering, robustness, and interpretability work.
- **Red flags:** (i) gpt-oss-20B and Qwen3-4B-Thinking baseline numbers are anomalous enough to warrant an explicit caveat in the paper (there is none); (ii) "universal" is used for the textual effect when it really means "consistent sign across 7 non-reasoning models"; (iii) the headline +8.95 pp figure in the conclusion aggregates across models/benchmarks and overshadows the high variance in Tables 4–12.

## Strengths

- Clean, principled causal-isolation design: letter case is the *only* thing that changes, with a formal semantic-preservation constraint and a two-stage matching protocol.
- Honest about failures: explicitly reports that the strongest attention attractor (TA) *degrades* accuracy, and frames this as the paper's central insight rather than burying it.
- Broad empirical coverage: 13 models across 5 families and 3 tokenizer types, 6 benchmarks, 15 schemes, with full per-model tables and Grad-CAM visualizations.
- Practically free: prompt-only, zero model access, negligible latency overhead (mean 0.0015 ms), so the diagnostic is trivially reproducible by anyone.
- Novel cross-modal finding (dual-axis response, reasoning-buffer reversal) that opens concrete follow-up questions for VLM interpretability.

## Limitations

- The two reasoning LLMs (gpt-oss-20B, Qwen3-4B-Thinking-2507) have clearly anomalous baselines (0.33–3.74% on MMLU-Pro), undermining the quantitative strength of the "reasoning buffer" claim; a parsing/harness fix is needed before this sub-result is trustworthy.
- Restricted to scripts where letter casing is defined (Latin); the authors explicitly note that non-Latin scripts and causal-mediation analysis are left to future work.
- The study is descriptive by design — no automated steering pipeline, no deployment evaluation, no comparison against existing white-box steering methods (PASTA/AutoPASTA) on the same benchmarks, so it is hard to judge practical utility relative to those baselines.
- The architectural mitigation is conjectured, not implemented or evaluated.
- Per-model magnitude variance is large; "universal" should be read as "consistent sign," not "consistent effect size."
- The attention-mass metric aggregates across all layers/heads, which smooths over the layer-head dynamics the appendix hints at; finer mechanistic attribution is left incomplete.

## Relevance

与 LLM/VLM 可解释性、注意力操控（attention steering）、prompt 鲁棒性和多模态对齐等研究方向高度相关：它给出了一种零模型访问、零训练的诊断探针，可用于评估自家模型对表层正字法扰动的脆弱性，并为后续"无侵入式注意力引导"和抗对抗性 prompt 设计提供了直接切入点；其"注意力≠性能"与"推理缓冲"两个发现对推理模型评测与 VLM 的文本主导性研究尤其有借鉴价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- After fixing the answer-parsing/evaluation harness for gpt-oss-20B and Qwen3-4B-Thinking-2507 (whose MMLU-Pro/ARC baselines of 0.33–3.74% are implausibly low), does the "reasoning buffer" effect survive, weaken, or disappear?
- How does prompt-side casing interact with established white-box steering methods (PASTA, AutoPASTA, InstA-Boost) on the same benchmarks — is case a free complement, a substitute, or orthogonal?
- Does the productive-vs-destructive attractor split (TE helps, TA hurts) hold for generative tasks beyond MCQ/RC, e.g., long-form QA, summarization, agentic tool-use, and safety/refusal prompts?
- Can the conjectured case-agnostic LayerNorm actually neutralize destructive attractors without harming the productive TE gains, and at what compute/quality cost?
- Is the macroscopic image-disengagement axis in VLMs a symptom of the well-documented text-dominance bias, and can it be exploited to *reduce* spurious image attention, or only to amplify it?
- How does the effect behave in non-Latin (CJK, Arabic) scripts where "case" is undefined — are there analogous orthographic salience cues (e.g., full-width vs half-width, ruby annotations) that pretrained transformers internalize?
- Does adversarial exploitation of the casing effect (inducing a model to attend to a misleading uppercase span) constitute a new prompt-injection vector worth characterizing formally?
