> **一句话总结：** 作者提出一种无需梯度训练的逐头闭式岭回归映射器，将同族不同规模 LLM（如 Qwen3 14B→32B）的 KV cache 直接转换复用，跳过接收方的 prefill，在四个匹配-KV 对上保留 73–98% 的独立精度且速度比重新 prefill 快 2.7–25 倍，并提出"误差落点"（而非误差大小）决定可迁移性的诊断视角。

> **TL;DR:** For within-family LLM swaps (e.g. Qwen3 14B→32B, Llama 3.1 8B→70B), the authors show the cross-model KV relationship is largely linear and fit a closed-form, gradient-free per-head ridge mapper (with RoPE-stripped content-space keys and top-k cross-layer source selection) on 500 FineWeb-Edu sequences; it retains 73–98% of the receiver's standalone accuracy on five benchmarks (ARC-C, HellaSwag, WinoGrande, MMLU, GSM8K) on four of six matched-KV pairs while running 2.7–25× faster than re-prefill (up to 25× at 32K on Qwen3 14B→32B vs 6,975 ms re-prefill), and an MLP variant recovers up to +37 pp HellaSwag retention on the two Ministral pairs where ridge collapses.

## 核心贡献

- **闭式跨模型 KV 迁移框架**：首次提出无需梯度训练的逐头岭回归映射，把源模型的 KV cache 线性投影到目标模型，使目标模型无需重新 prefill 即可解码；只需 500 条 FineWeb-Edu（每条 1024 token）的标定数据，单 8×H100 节点约 47–87 分钟拟合一个对。
- **三大设计组件**：(1) 逐目标层按 R² 选 top-k 个最具预测力的源层并拼接特征；(2) 在 RoPE 剥离的"内容空间"中拟合（拟合与位置解耦，跨 RoPE 配置与上下文长度可复用，推理时再用目标 RoPE 重新编码）；(3) 岭正则（λ=0.01）保证数值稳定性。消融显示跨层源选择是贡献最大的单一组件。
- **跨三家族六对的验证**：在 Qwen3、Llama 3.1、Ministral 3 的六个匹配-KV 对（源/目标 KV 头数与每头维度相同）上，四对（Qwen3 14B→32B、8B→32B、Llama 3.1 8B→70B、Ministral 3B→8B）平均保留 73–98% 精度，余下两个 Ministral→14B 对则崩塌至 42–44%。
- **"误差落点"决定可迁移性的机制解释**：跨 12 个匹配-KV 方向评估，注意力输出余弦相似度与 HellaSwag 保留率的相关性 Pearson r=+0.57，远优于校准 R²（r=−0.20）。MLP 在失败对上把误差从注意力敏感子空间重分布到无关方向，带来 +24.3 到 +36.8 pp 的 HellaSwag 保留提升。
- **延迟收益与多轮稳定性**：KV 迁移在 Qwen3 14B↔32B 上比 re-prefill 快 4–25×（32K 时 S→L 277.6 ms 对 6,975.3 ms）；CoQA 多轮交接中 10 轮内漂移可控（L→S 线性增长 0.33 pp/turn）。

## Key Contributions

- **Closed-form, training-free mapper**: First demonstration that within-family cross-model KV transfer admits a small-sample gradient-free fit (per-head ridge regression on 500 FineWeb-Edu sequences of 1,024 tokens each, fit in ~47–87 min per pair on a single 8×H100 node), distinguishing it from prior neural-fuser / adapter approaches (C2C, LatentAlign) that require gradient training.
- **Three-component design**: per-head ridge (λ=0.01) + top-k cross-layer source selection per target layer + RoPE-stripped "content-space" mapping that decouples positional rotation so the fit transfers across context lengths and RoPE configurations.
- **Multi-family empirical validation on six matched-KV pairs**: four pairs retain 73–98% of standalone accuracy across five benchmarks; two Ministral→14B pairs collapse, showing matched KV is necessary but not sufficient.
- **Mechanistic insight — error placement, not magnitude, decides retention**: across 12 matched-KV pair evaluations (six S→L, six L→S), attention-output cosine predicts HellaSwag retention at Pearson r=+0.57 versus R²'s r=−0.20; an MLP recovers up to +36.8 pp on failure pairs by redistributing residual error away from attention-sensitive subspaces.
- **Practical serving wins**: 2.7–25× lower prefill latency than re-prefill (up to 25× at 32K on Qwen3 14B→32B) and stable multi-turn handoff on CoQA.

## Methodology

The paper formulates cross-model KV cache transfer as finding a map f: C_S → Ĉ_T such that the target decoding from Ĉ_T matches its own C_T downstream. The method operates only on **matched-KV pairs** (n^s_kv = n^t_kv and d^s_h = d^t_h, even if layer counts or parameter counts differ).

**Per-head ridge regression (§3.1).** An independent linear map is fit per (target layer l, target head h), separately for K and V. Stacking N≈128K calibration tokens (500 FineWeb-Edu sequences of length 1,024, stride-4 subsampled) yields a closed-form ridge solve W* = (XᵀX + λI)⁻¹XᵀY with λ=0.01; ridge is preferred over OLS because the top-k source layers are correlated and XᵀX is near-singular. Total mapper size ranges 1.01–3.36 B parameters (4–12 GB) per pair.

**Cross-layer source selection (§3.2).** Because source and target have different layer counts, for each target layer l the top-k source layers are chosen by head-averaged R² (averaged over RoPE-stripped K and V); their KV features are concatenated as the regression input. k is swept per pair over {1,2,4,6,8,10,12,16,20,24,all} and selected by mean log-likelihood benchmark accuracy. Ablation identifies this as the largest single contributor (k=8→k=1 drops K R² from 0.79 to 0.56 on Qwen3 14B→32B).

**Content-space (RoPE-factored) mapping (§3.3).** Keys are mapped in a position-free space: source RoPE is stripped, the ridge projection applied, then target RoPE re-applied at inference: K̂_t = (K_s R^−1_Θs(t) W_K + b_K) R_Θt(t). Because R_Θ is orthogonal the inversion is exact. This decouples the fit from the 1,024-token position distribution seen at calibration and extends by construction to longer contexts (serving up to 32k tokens in §4.7).

**Linear-structure probe (§2.3).** Single-source OLS regressions on Qwen3 14B→32B show a single source layer explains 56% of K and 32% of V variance, rising to 79% / 65% with multiple source layers, motivating the top-k design.

**Nonlinear extension (§4.4).** A 2-hidden-layer × 1,024-unit ReLU MLP, trained with Adam on the same MSE loss, is used as a drop-in replacement per (target layer, head, K|V) to probe where ridge falls short.

## Results & Highlights

- **Headline retention (Table 1)** across five benchmarks (ARC-C, HellaSwag, WinoGrande, MMLU, GSM8K): Qwen3 14B→32B (k=8) retains **97.6%** average accuracy / 96.3% floor-normalized (HellaSwag 97.6%, MMLU 95.0%, GSM8K 95.6%); Qwen3 8B→32B (k=12) **87.5%**; Llama 3.1 8B→70B (k=20) **72.8%** (the most extreme parameter ratio, +15.1 pp over chance-floor-normalized); Ministral 3B→8B (k=all) **76.2%**.
- **Tier-2 collapses**: Ministral 3B→14B and Ministral 8B→14B fall to **44.2% / 41.6%** Avg (only **14.7% / 11.1%** once floor-normalized), with GSM8K retention of 3.2% / 1.6% — demonstrating matched KV does not guarantee success.
- **Linear-structure probe (§2.3, Appendix B)**: on Qwen3 14B→32B, single-source K_stripped R² peaks at **0.81**; multi-source k=8 raises layer-averaged K R² to **0.79** and V R² to **0.65** (from 0.56 / 0.32 at k=1).
- **Ablation (Table 2, Qwen3 14B→32B)**: removing inference-time RoPE re-rotation collapses MMLU to 25.79% (chance) and GSM8K to 4.17% while HellaSwag drops only ~5 pp; additionally dropping cross-layer selection (k=1) crashes HellaSwag to 44.81% and PPL to 22.73 (vs 7.33 full).
- **MLP recovery (Table 3)**: on the two failure pairs, an MLP lifts HellaSwag retention from 68.0%→92.3% (Ministral 3B→14B, **+24.3 pp**) and from 58.7%→95.5% (Ministral 8B→14B, **+36.8 pp**), while slightly *underperforming* ridge on already-linear pairs (−0.3 / −1.5 pp).
- **Predictor of cross-pair retention (§4.5)**: across 12 matched-KV pair evaluations, attention-output cosine correlates with HellaSwag retention at Pearson **r=+0.57**, while calibration-domain K R² has r=−0.20 — R² is not a valid cross-pair screen.
- **Multi-turn handoff (§4.6)**: on CoQA, Qwen3 14B↔32B across ~15-turn conversations, S→L F1 gap widens only 1.7 pp from turn 1 to turn 10; L→S drift grows linearly at 0.33 pp/turn.
- **Prefill latency (Table 5, Qwen3 14B↔32B)**: at 32K tokens, the mapper runs **277.6 ms (S→L) vs 6,975.3 ms re-prefill — 25× speedup**; 17× at 8K (67.8 ms vs 1,154.8 ms) and 4× at 64 tokens. L→S speedup widens with length (3× at 64 → 7× at 32K).
- **Calibration robustness (Table 8)**: λ=1 collapses HellaSwag by −15.79 pp but a four-order λ sweep shows wide flat regions; N flattens after 200 sequences (N=50 still within ~1.6 pp); domain is the costly axis — CodeAlpaca costs −5.24 pp on HellaSwag while Wikipedia is within noise.

## Authors & Credibility

All authors are from **NVIDIA** (Sami/hpc-ai systems group). The work targets a concrete NVIDIA-serving problem (cost-quality cascading, mid-conversation model swaps, routing), uses production-grade models (Qwen3, Llama 3.1, Ministral 3), and reports detailed per-pair tables, mapper parameter counts, storage cost, and per-pair fit wall-time. The paper is forthcoming-references style (cites 2026 ICLR/NSDI work), consistent with an Aug-2026 preprint. The honesty about two pairs collapsing and the negative R²-on-failure-pairs finding is a credibility plus.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: Genuine. As the authors tabulate (Table 6), this is the only method satisfying all four of {gradient-free, cross-scale, transfers KV values, closed-form}; C2C and LatentAlign need gradient training, IAM transfers attention patterns rather than KV values, DroidSpeak requires identical architecture. The RoPE-stripped "content-space" decomposition and the top-k cross-layer concatenation are non-obvious and well-motivated by the §2.3 R² probe.
- **Rigor**: Strong on mechanism. The "error placement, not magnitude" finding — attention-output cosine r=+0.57 vs R² r=−0.20 across 12 directions — is a real diagnostic contribution, not a hand-wave. The MLP ablation (Table 4) actually *measures* K-concentration, V-concentration, and cosine shifts to explain the +37 pp gain rather than asserting it.
- **Reproducibility**: Mixed. Closed-form ridge on FineWeb-Edu is reproducible in principle, and calibration sensitivity sweeps (Table 8) are thorough. However, the per-pair mapper is 1.01–3.36 B parameters / 4–12 GB with no stated release plan, k is selected on the reported benchmarks (acknowledged ≤2.49 pp bias, Appendix H), and the MLP results in §4.4 lack any calibration/training-detail in the main text (deferred to Appendix E).
- **Significance**: Practical impact is real for within-family serving swaps (2.7–25× prefill speedup, stable multi-turn), but **scope is narrow**: only matched-KV pairs (same head count and per-head dimension) within a family are tested, and the authors explicitly leave mismatched-KV, cross-family, and hybrid (sliding-window / SSM) architectures to future work — a large fraction of real model swaps will not satisfy the matched-KV constraint.
- **Red flags / overclaiming**: The abstract's "73–98% on four pairs" framing obscures that **two of six pairs collapse to 11–15% floor-normalized** (Ministral 3B→14B, 8B→14B), all six are within a single family-matched-KV regime, and the headline 25× speedup is the S→L direction at 32K while the abstract's "2.7–25× faster" lower bound is not in the reported Table 5 (which shows 4–25× S→L, 3–7× L→S). GSM8K retention on the failing pairs is essentially at floor (3.2%, 1.6%), suggesting the linear mapper cannot preserve chain-of-thought reasoning on hard pairs even though HellaSwag looks passable. The "stable across multi-turn" claim rests on a single pair (Qwen3 14B↔32B) and 100 CoQA conversations, and the authors themselves note L→S drift is linear and would accumulate over long sessions.

## Strengths

- Training-free, closed-form fit — deployable without GPUs/backprop at fit time on a single 8×H100 node.
- RoPE-content decomposition is principled and makes the fit length-portable.
- Strong mechanistic analysis (attention-output cosine, K/V error concentration) that reframes how cross-model mappers should be evaluated.
- Honest reporting of two failure pairs and a clean MLP rescue; doesn't hide the negative result.
- Real production latency numbers across 64–32K token range.

## Limitations

- **Matched-KV only**: requires source and target to share KV head count and per-head dimension; many practical swaps (e.g. across GQA configurations) are out of scope.
- **Within-family only**: cross-family (Qwen3→Llama) transfer is left open.
- **Single calibration domain** (FineWeb-Edu); CodeAlpaca costs 5.24 pp on HellaSwag, and the sweep cannot separate subject matter from register — calibration confined to a single field (medicine, law) is unbounded.
- **k selected on reported benchmarks** (≤2.49 pp bias acknowledged), not strictly out-of-sample.
- **Multi-turn evaluation is single-pair** (Qwen3 14B↔32B); generalization to the failing Ministral pairs is unknown.
- **MLP variant** — while it recovers failure pairs, it requires gradient training and its generalization beyond the four pairs tested is not established.
- **Hybrid architectures** (sliding-window, SSM/Nemotron-style) out of scope.

## Relevance

直接命中"LLM 推理加速 / KV cache 复用"研究方向：作者给出的闭式岭映射器可作为 prefix caching、cross-layer sharing 之外的第三类 KV 复用机制，对多模型级联、会话中切换、routing 服务场景具有实际工程价值，且其"注意力输出余弦预测可迁移性"的诊断视角对后续 cross-model 表示对齐工作有方法论启发。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the closed-form ridge mapper extend to **mismatched-KV** pairs (different head counts or per-head dimensions), perhaps via a head-pooling/projection pre-step before ridge?
- Can **attention-output cosine be predicted before fitting** the mapper, to enable cheap deployment screening across arbitrary family pairs?
- Why do the two **Ministral→14B** pairs collapse while Ministral 3B→8B succeeds — is it training-data overlap, fine-tuning recipe, or a depth-ratio threshold, and does it generalize to other 3-layer-jump transitions?
- How does the mapper behave on **agentic / tool-use workloads** with structured prompts, where attention patterns differ from HellaSwag-style completion?
- Can an **attention-aligned training objective** (optimizing cosine directly rather than MSE) replace the post-hoc MLP rescue and dominate ridge across all pairs?
- Does the approach compose with **prefill-acceleration methods** (speculative prefill, SwiftKV) for compounded latency wins, or do they interfere on the same KV state?
