Intertemporal Preference Steering in Qwen3 via Contrastive Activation Addition
Michal Mráz & Justin Shenk — ICML 2026, Mechanistic Interpretability Workshop, Seoul
CAA Qwen3-32B residual stream linear probes layers 24–48 α ∈ ±128 thinking disabled
Key finding

A short-term vs. long-term temporal-horizon direction in Qwen3-32B's residual stream is linearly readable (96.0% probe accuracy) and causally steerable — injecting its signed mean-mass vector at inference swings binary long-term choice by 59.4 pp, versus only 5.6 pp for a matched-norm random-orthogonal control.

Why this matters SO-WHAT

A clean, reproducible concept-direction → causal-behavior recipe in LLMs, aimed at a novel target — intertemporal preference. The probe + CAA + AEIM + random-orthogonal-control toolkit is a template for steering other abstract preference axes, with direct relevance to alignment, safety, and decision/advisory systems.

Critical edge CAVEAT

Construct validity is the weak point (authors concede it): the MM direction is trained on answer continuations, so it likely conflates temporal horizon with abstraction, urgency, and reactive-vs-proactive framing. Single model, thinking-traces off, no prompting baseline — generalization is unverified.

Method & evidence

Anatomy of the intervention

01Probe direction & CAA injection

datasets   explicit 500  (400 tr / 100 te) → 800 teacher-forced
            implicit 300  (cross-domain eval only)

direction  d_MM = µ_long − µ_short   (mean-answer pooling)
            fitted at layers {24,28,32,36,40,44,48}

intervention  residual += α · d̂_l
            α > 0  →  long-term     α < 0  →  short-term
            at last prompt token + every decode step
            |α| = 128  ≈  36–70 % of residual L2 norm

Unwhitened MM is what CAA actually steers with; LR/WLR/whitened variants are reported only as readout diagnostics. Causal claims are reserved for the intervention, not the probe accuracy.

Critical assessment

What keeps the claim honest

06Limitations & risks

  • Construct validity. MM direction is fit on answer continuations; it likely bundles horizon with abstraction, urgency, and reactive-vs-proactive framing. Authors flag this themselves.
  • Single model. Qwen3-32B only — no transfer to Llama / Mistral / other scales.
  • Thinking traces disabled. Behavior under reasoning-enabled decoding unknown.
  • Missing ablations. No head-to-head CAA vs LR / WLR / WMM steering vectors; no prompting baseline ("think long-term").
  • Weak TravelPlanner evidence. A single aggregate commonsense curve; no item-level analysis of which checks flip.
  • Code/data release not explicitly promised in the text.

07Open questions

  • Does the direction transfer across model families, or shift when thinking is enabled?
  • How much is truly "horizon" vs correlated features — factorial probe needed.
  • Is MM optimal for intervention, or just for readout?
  • CAA vs a prompting baseline — same magnitude?
  • Composable with risk / cooperation directions, or non-additive?
  • Stable under adversarial paraphrase or multi-turn dialogue?
