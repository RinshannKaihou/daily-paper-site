When Attention Goes Blind: Numerical Failure in ALiBi Positional Encodings

Christopher Schröder · Lukas Gienapp · Ferdinand Schlatt · Martin Potthast · Gerhard Heyer

一句话总结：ALiBi 的线性偏置随距离增长触发浮点下溢，使注意力头在某个距离后"失明"；在 BLOOM / Falcon-RW / MPT 中实测确认，并对 passkey / needle-in-a-haystack 关联检索造成显著损害。

Key finding

ALiBi's linearly-scaling bias underflows floating-point precision, blinding attention heads beyond a per-head distance δ_h = |τ_u| / m_h.

Key metric

36.6%
of attention entries have underflowed by token distance 2048 — averaged across BLOOM 560M, Falcon-RW 7B, MPT 7B.
Mitigation C+L lifts out-of-context passkey AUC 0.08 → 0.79 vs. ALiBi baseline (≈ 10×).

Why this matters

Exposes an overlooked numerical failure, hands over immediate training-level fixes (clamping, log-scaling), and shows that standard CS/QA/LG benchmarks miss the breakdown entirely.

Critical edge

Every mitigation finding comes from 148M-parameter decoders. Transfer to billion-scale production ALiBi models is asserted — but not demonstrated.

01 / METHOD Mechanism

ALiBi adds bias B = −m·D to attention logits before softmax. Beyond a head-specific distance, the exponent underflows.

bf16 threshold τ_u ≈ −92.19 · fp32 τ_u ≈ −103.28 · default slopes: partial blindness δ₁=124, full blindness δ_H=22,358

Mitigations Four strategies evaluated alone and across all 11 combinations — C clamping (c=−87), E robust slopes, L log-scaled distances, S soft capping. Implemented on PyTorch FlexAttention because FlashAttention cannot clamp at the bias level.

03a ↑ Strengths

  • Clean analytical result. δ_h = |τ_u|/m_h, verifiable in a few lines and immediately actionable.
  • Honest experimental scope. Steep/Safe/Wide slopes disentangle underflow from generic out-of-context degradation rather than conflating them.
  • Thorough sweep. 4 strategies × 11 combinations — unusually complete for a positional-encoding paper.
  • Transparent. Discloses 9,000 GPU·h, the small-model limit, and per-config variance.
  • Useful artifact. Appendix Table 5: a near-complete inventory of every published ALiBi model and its slopes.

03b ↓ Limitations

  • Single model size (148M). Findings may not transfer to 7B+, where production ALiBi actually lives.
  • Vanilla RoPE only. No YaRN / NTK-aware / π-scaled baselines — weakens the "ALiBi still competitive" framing.
  • High variance. n=3 seeds, σ up to ±0.20 on PK-in; several mitigation rankings are not statistically separable.
  • Fixed architecture & corpus. Encoder-decoder, Mamba hybrids, and non-English corpora unexplored.
  • No backward-pass study. Whether underflow damages gradient flow (zeroed entries carry no gradient) is left as future work.

04 / OPEN Questions for further exploration

Q1Do the C+L dominance on PK and the NIHS regression of L-containing combos hold at 1B+ and 7B+ scales?
Q2Is "default ALiBi strongest on NIHS" robust against modern RoPE extrapolation (YaRN, NTK-aware, π-scaled)?
Q3Does underflow differentially damage backward-pass gradient flow, and would a straight-through estimator recover steep-head capacity?
Q4Can δ_h be inverted into a learned slope schedule that adapts to a target context length and empirical logit distribution?
Q5How does ALiBi underflow interact with GQA, sliding-window, and hybrid SSM/attention architectures?
Q6Is there a retrieval-specific signal (attention entropy, induction-head strength) that predicts when blindness hurts downstream tasks?
