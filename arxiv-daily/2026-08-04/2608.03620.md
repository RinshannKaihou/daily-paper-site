> **一句话总结：** 本文在残差流上加性条件计算 + 线性读出的理想模型下，严格刻画了何时低秩权重消融会把匹配输入对塌缩到同一无分支输出（充要条件 + 误差恒等式），并证明激活修补与权重消融度量的并非同一量（一个移的是 contrast，一个移的是 level），进而在 head 自身层 MLP 的复合处给出严格二阶余项的非线性交互公式，最后在小规模合成关联召回任务上检验：交互幅值与理想模型预测精度呈强单调（Spearman -0.83），但一个初见"干净分界带"在另外 25 个 held-out 配置上不复存在。

> **TL;DR:** In an idealized model where a residual-stream conditional is read out linearly, this paper proves an iff criterion for low-rank weight ablation to collapse a matched pair onto one unconditional branch, gives the exact error identity for near-collapse, and shows activation patching (moves donor–receiver contrast β_i δ_i) and weight ablation (moves receiver level -β_i α_i(x_B)) measure two generally unrelated quantities — constructing n≥2-carrier pairs on which every single-carrier patch flips the decision while no single-carrier ablation does. A second-order-bounded formula is derived for the nonlinearity an ablated head introduces into its own layer's MLP, vanishing identically when only the MLP is ablated. On 39 ablation configurations of small synthetic associative-recall transformers, the measured interaction magnitude correlates strongly with the idealized model's predictive accuracy (Spearman ρ = -0.83, p < 1e-5), but a clean numerical separation band read off 14 configurations does not survive on the other 25 held-out configurations.

## 核心贡献

- **条件塌缩的充要判据（Theorem 1）**：在匹配输入对 (xA, xB) 上，删掉载体子集 S 当且仅当"对称（q̄S=0）且无对比外溢（ΨS=0）"时把两端塌缩到同一值 s̄=(gA+gB)/2；塌缩结果只取决于对本身，与具体 S 无关（Corollary 2：理想不变性）。
- **塌缩判据的鲁棒化（Proposition 1 / Corollary 3）**：把"精确相等"放宽为容差 ≤ (ε1, ε2) 时给出恒等误差分解 sS(xA)-s̄ = -q̄S + ½ΨS，并据此把"两个塌缩相反极性"对应到 ε1+½ε2 ≥ |s̄| 的下界。
- **修补–消融分离定理（Theorem 2 / Corollary 4）**：在单载体级别，patch 移动 βiδi（contrast）、ablation 移动 -βiαi(xB)（level），二者互不界定的充要；显式构造 n≥2 carriers 使得"所有单 carrier patch 都翻转决策，而所有单 carrier ablation 都不翻转"——把 self-repair / overshoot 现象在没有修复机制时复现。
- **非线性交互定理（Theorem 3）+ 单载体精确性（Corollary 5）**：针对 attention head 经 RMSNorm 喂入同层 MLP 这一无独立性的复合结构，给出 ∆(x) 的精确一阶公式与二阶余项界 ‖R‖ ≤ Λ‖η‖²；证明余项在仅消融 MLP 时恒为零，仅在 head 时非零。
- **承载的负面结果**：14 个配置上的"空带"分离阈值 (6.78, 9.95) 在另外 25 个 held-out 配置上失效（9/39 违反，7 个落入带内），论文据此撤回强阈值声明，仅保留强单调关联（Spearman -0.83 全集、-0.85 held-out）。
- **第二任务/架构的复现**：在更小的 3 层 Llama-style transformer 上的真正反向关联召回任务（F/R，99.5%/98.5% 精度）上，三项检验（patch=weight-edit 精确、交互 vs 保真度 ρ=-0.90、极性反转类比）全部以相同形式出现。

## Key Contributions

- **Conditional collapse theorem (Theorem 1)**: An if-and-only-if criterion — symmetric removal (q̄S = 0) and no surviving outside contrast (ΨS = 0) — under which deleting a subset S of carriers maps a matched pair (xA, xB) onto the common value s̄ = (gA+gB)/2, with deterministic polarity and idealized invariance across subsets (Corollary 2).
- **Robust tolerance form (Proposition 1, Corollary 3)**: An exact identity sS(xA)-s̄ = -q̄S + ½ΨS that lets the conclusions of Theorem 1 degrade gracefully under |q̄S| ≤ ε1, |ΨS| ≤ ε2, and converts any polarity reversal between two subsets into the quantitative lower bound ε1 + ½ε2 ≥ |s̄|.
- **Patching–ablation dissociation (Theorem 2, Corollary 4)**: Names the two distinct quantities actually measured — βiδi (contrast) for patching vs. -βiαi(xB) (level) for ablation — and constructs families of n ≥ 2 carriers on which every single-carrier patch is sufficient while no single-carrier ablation is necessary, reproducing overshoot/self-repair without any repair mechanism.
- **First-order nonlinear interaction formula (Theorem 3) with second-order remainder**: For an attention head composed with its own layer's RMSNorm + MLP, the deviation ∆(x) from the additive model is exactly (I-Q)[-Dg(r1)η − R(x)], with ‖R‖ ≤ Λ‖η‖² under a bounded-curvature hypothesis whose constant Λ is closed-form-computable from trained weights; ∆ vanishes identically whenever only the MLP is ablated (Corollary 5) but not when a head is.
- **Patch-equals-weight-edit equivalence (Proposition 2)**: For any single carrier, the frozen-activation protocol and the genuinely weight-edited forward pass produce identical write-ins — including when the ablated direction is data-estimated — because the projector and the estimation error are common to both sides and cancel.
- **A load-bearing negative result (Section 8)**: A numerical "empty band" separation (6.78, 9.95) read off the first 14 configurations fails out-of-sample: 9 of 39 configurations violate the decision rule, 7 fall inside the supposedly empty band, and 3 of the original 14 even change which side they fall on when re-measured. The author withholds the strong threshold claim and retains only the robust monotone association.
- **Second-task replication (Section 8.1)**: A freshly trained 3-layer transformer on a structurally different inverse-lookup task reproduces the patch=edit exactness, the interaction↔fidelity monotone relationship (Spearman ρ = -0.90, p ≈ 3e-5), and a polarity-reversal analogue.

## Methodology

The author works in an **abstract conditional model**: a residual block's output decomposes additively as F(x) = F0(x) + Σi αi(x)·vi, with fixed "carrier" directions vi and scalar selectors αi(x), read out by a linear functional s(x) = ψ(F(x)) + b whose sign gives a binary decision. A **low-rank weight-space ablation** of a component is shown (Remark 1) to be *exactly* equivalent to deleting one or more v·α(x) terms from the residual stream — an identity, not an approximation.

Three standing assumptions frame the theory: residual additivity, low-rank support, and stability of the unconditional F0. The analysis proceeds in three layers:

1. **Conditional collapse (Sec. 5)**: Define removed-mass symmetric part q̄S and antisymmetric part ΦS, and uncaptured outside contrast ΨS. Theorem 1 proves collapse to s̄ iff q̄S = 0 and ΨS = 0. The robust form (Prop. 1) drops the exact-equality requirement and states the error as an identity.
2. **Dissociation (Sec. 6)**: Compare the patched selector shift βiδi to the ablated selector shift -βiαi(xB). Construct counterexamples explicitly (βi=1, αi(xB)=-½, αi(xA)=1, n≥2) to certify the separation.
3. **Nonlinear interaction (Sec. 7)**: For the one composition where carrier independence is architecturally false — an attention head feeding its own layer's MLP through RMSNorm — apply a fundamental-theorem-of-calculus argument to obtain ∆(x) = (I-Q)[-Dg(r1)η(x) - R(x)], with R being an integral remainder bounded by Λ‖η‖² when ‖D²g‖ ≤ Λ.

**Empirical validation** uses 5 independently trained 4-layer Llama-style transformers (d=64, 4 heads, SwiGLU MLP) on a synthetic associative-recall task with two surface formats (markers mA/mB; mB applies a fixed derangement σ⁻¹ before lookup), plus a second smaller 3-layer/3-head/width-48 transformer on a true inverse-recall task. Carriers are found by a greedy activation-patching sweep (r ≥ 0.25), and ablated by projecting data-estimated directions out of weight matrices. The interactions, collapse rates, and protocol gaps are measured against independent probe seeds to avoid shared-sample contamination.

## Results & Highlights

- **Patching–ablation dissociation, measured**: On a single instance, patching the first-layer MLP output yields median recovery κ = 1.043 (range 1.04–1.22) with argmax flip rate 0.85 — i.e. overshoot — while ablating the *weights feeding that same site* leaves the receiver format correct on 57% of inputs: patch-sufficient yet far from ablation-necessary, exactly as Theorem 2 predicts.
- **Conditional collapse with polarity reversal**: On seed 44, two nested subsets SD1 ⊂ SDJ collapse the same trained network onto opposite unconditional readers — D1 (rank-1 head h3) collapses to the inverted reader (inverted-A rate 0.93, format B retained at 0.963); DJ (h3 + same-block MLP) collapses to the literal reader (literal-B rate 0.977, format A retained at 0.997). Corollary 2 forbids this inside the idealized model, so at least one subset violates hypothesis (i) or (ii); for DJ this is confirmed directly: |q̄S| = 6.3 against |s̄| = 4.2.
- **Robust collapse availability is rare**: Taking ε1 = |q̄S|, ε2 = |ΨS| at per-pair measured values over 39 configurations × 120 pairs each, the sufficient condition |s̄| > ε1 + ½ε2 holds on only 10.0% of pair-configurations; no configuration satisfies it on more than 71.7% of pairs, and none on 90%. Median |s̄|/Φ = 0.083, median |q̄S|/Φ = 0.14, median |ΨS|/Φ = 0.21; in the median the condition misses by a factor of 5.3 — yet it is conservatively discriminating (Spearman ρ = 0.66 between certificate rate and realized collapse rate, p ≈ 1e-5).
- **Patch-equals-weight-edit exactness**: Across all five D1 (single-carrier) configurations, the per-input gap between frozen-activation selector and genuinely weight-edited selector has median ≤ 9.6×10⁻⁷ and maximum 7.7×10⁻⁶ logits against a median selector magnitude of 3.9–8.5 logits — single-precision round-off, six orders of magnitude below signal. The falsifiability control (re-estimating the subspace on disjoint data for the patch route only) raises the gap to median 0.69, max 6.6 logits, confirming the protocol would surface real estimation error if present.
- **Interaction tracks fidelity — strongly but only monotonically**: Interaction magnitude vs. agreement rate C1a gives Spearman ρ = -0.83 over all 39 configurations (p < 1e-5), -0.85 over the 25 held-out alone, -0.81 over the 14 held-out with nonzero interaction. Pass rate over configurations with nonzero interaction falls 7/8 → 6/8 → 1/7 across terciles of interaction magnitude.
- **Negative result on the threshold**: 9 of 39 configurations violate the frozen "empty band" (6.78, 9.95); 7 fall inside it; 3 of the original 14 change which side they sit on when re-measured on held-out probes. The clean numerical separation does not survive.
- **Second-task replication**: On the inverse-recall network, patch=edit gap is again ≤ 10⁻⁶ (median 9.5×10⁻⁷, max 3.8×10⁻⁶ against selector scale 6.99), and interaction↔fidelity correlation reaches ρ = -0.90 (p ≈ 3e-5) on 15 configurations, plus 8 instances of nested-subset polarity reversal across 2 probe pairs.

## Authors & Credibility

Single author, **Abdallah Khemais** (ISITCOM, University of Sousse), July 2026 submission. The paper is mathematically careful: every theorem is stated with explicit hypotheses and a proof, every result has a robust/tolerance counterpart, and the author is unusually explicit about which empirical claims survived replication and which did not — the "negative result we consider load-bearing" framing and the explicit withdrawal of the threshold claim are signs of intellectual honesty rather than overclaiming. Reproducibility is taken seriously: full training recipes, exact probe-seed disjointness, JSON-output figure pipelines (no hand-transcription), and a file-by-file code inventory (Appendix A) under `notebook/` are listed. The paper positions itself as Part I of a two-paper programme (multi-layer extension and emergent-circuit validation in a real pretrained model are deferred to a companion analysis), which is appropriately scoped.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: High. The dissociation between activation patching and weight-space ablation has been *observed* empirically (the Hydra effect [7], superposition [6], interpretability illusions [11]) but here it is an *identity* (Theorem 2) inside a deliberately minimal model, and the existence of the strict gap is established by explicit construction (Corollary 4), not just argument. The closed-form first-order formula for the head→MLP interaction (Theorem 3) with a provably second-order remainder is new and identifies the single architectural mechanism through which the additive picture fails.

- **Rigor**: Theoretical proofs are tight and minimal — Assumptions 1 and 3 are explicitly identified as load-bearing while Assumption 2 (low-rank) is honestly disclosed as not used quantitatively. The RMSNorm Jacobian bound (Lemma 2) is correct and the fundamental-theorem-of-calculus derivation of ∆(x) is standard but applied precisely. The author is scrupulous about the difference between ∆(x) (the quantity the idealized model omits) and the frozen-activation measurement gap (which is forced to ≈0 on single-carrier ablations by Proposition 2, *regardless of ∆*) — a confusion that would have overclaimed validation.

- **Reproducibility**: Strong on paper. Code repository, checkpoints (`marker_ckpt/`, `bidir_ckpt/`), training hyperparameters (AdamW β1=0.9, β2=0.999; lr=10⁻³ with 250-step warmup + cosine decay to 0.1×; 5000 steps; batch 64; float32), evaluation thresholds fixed *before* any checkpoint was probed (C1a ≥ 0.90, C1b within ±0.10), and fully disjoint probe seeds per measurement. The tie-corrected Spearman is cross-checked across three implementations (Julia StatsBase.corspearman, Python scipy.stats.spearmanr, R cor) with the earlier order-dependent statistic retained as a documented control. The experiments are, however, limited to *small synthetic-task* transformers; no real pretrained model is studied in this Part I (deferred to the companion).

- **Significance**: Conceptually significant — it sharpens a methodological caution into a checkable arithmetic identity, gives the exact quantity each intervention measures, and isolates the architectural condition (head fed to its own MLP) under which the additive picture breaks. The practical value is bounded by the headline negative finding: the robust certificate fires on only 10% of pair-configurations and the proposed threshold does not transfer out-of-sample, so on these instances the theory is more a *diagnostic that ranks configurations* than a *guarantee that covers behavior*. The honest framing of this ("the right quantity, not yet a usable cutoff") is commendable.

- **Red flags**: (a) Single author with no stated institutional affiliation beyond ISITCOM; the work has not been externally peer-reviewed at submission time. (b) The "companion analysis" that supplies both the closed form for Λ and the emergent-circuit test on a real pretrained model is not included and is repeatedly deferred — a reader cannot fully evaluate the central claim that the curvature constant is checkable on a given trained network without that companion. (c) The five marker-task checkpoints all use essentially the same training recipe and a single fixed derangement seed (42), so the "5/5 instances" redundancy/low-rank claim of Table 1 is really 5 random initializations of one task configuration — narrower than it sounds. (d) The second-task carrier set needed widening past the paper's own r ≥ 0.25 threshold (to r ≥ 0.15) to give the interaction-fidelity check enough points, which the author discloses but which slightly weakens the "replication" framing. (e) All experiments are on synthetic conditional tasks chosen because they make the conditional unambiguous by construction; whether the dissociation arithmetic dominates over real-language phenomena (where the matched-pair assumption may not even apply) is open.

## Strengths

- Makes the patching-vs-ablation disagreement an *identity* rather than a caution, with named quantities (βiδi and βiαi(xB)) that an experimenter can compute.
- Provides both the exact iff collapse criterion (Theorem 1) and its tolerance form (Proposition 1), so the result is not vacuously about a null set.
- Constructs explicit counterexamples certifying that the patching/ablation gap is achievable for any n ≥ 2 (Corollary 4).
- Theorem 3 isolates the *single* architectural mechanism (head→own-layer-MLP via RMSNorm) through which the additive picture fails, and Corollary 5 shows exactly when ∆(x) vanishes.
- Intellectual honesty: explicit withdrawal of the threshold claim after the out-of-sample test, full code/seed inventory, fixed-before-probing thresholds, and a self-administered falsifiability control (re-estimated subspace) that produces the predicted 3-orders-of-magnitude gap.
- Includes a second, freshly trained architecture and conditional mechanism to check that the predictions are not artifacts of the marker task.

## Limitations

- **Only synthetic-task small transformers are studied in this Part I**; extension to a real pretrained model and emergent circuit is deferred to the companion paper.
- The robust collapse certificate (Prop. 1) is rarely available on the trained instances — only 10.0% of pair-configurations — and at most 71.7% of pairs in any single configuration, because s̄ is small by construction (median |s̄|/Φ = 0.083) while tolerances are of margin scale.
- The proposed numerical separation band (6.78, 9.95) does **not** transfer out-of-sample (9/39 violations, 7 inside the band); only a monotone association survives.
- Theorem 3's interaction analysis is closed form only at the block feeding the readout; propagating ∆(x) through subsequent nonlinear layers (Remark 6) is heuristic-only, and multi-layer carrier interactions are not covered.
- The companion analysis supplying the closed form for the curvature constant Λ is not in this paper; the second-order bound depends on a constant the reader cannot yet verify from these results alone.
- Single author, not yet peer-reviewed; five marker-task instances are 5 seeds of one task configuration, not five independent tasks.
- The second-task replication required widening the carrier threshold past the paper's own r ≥ 0.25 to r ≥ 0.15 to obtain enough configurations, and the off-binary ("other") rate there is much higher (up to 47.5%) than on the marker task (1.5%).
- All five marker instances use the same derangement σ (fixed seed 42), so the redundancy/low-rank claim of Table 1 is over initializations rather than tasks.

## Relevance

本文对做 mechanistic interpretability、可解释性评测、circuit analysis 或 activation patching / 模型编辑方向的研究者高度相关：它给出一个可检验的判据，告诉你何时 patching 与 weight ablation 会一致、何时会分歧，并量化了 head→own-layer-MLP 这一架构处非线性交互的大小，直接关系到 refusals、induction heads、IOI 电路等工作中"该信 patch 还是 ablation"的方法论选择。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the iff collapse criterion of Theorem 1 (and the q̄S, ΨS, s̄ diagnostics of Prop. 1) hold on emergent circuits in real pretrained models (e.g. GPT-2 small IOI, refusal direction), where the matched-pair structure is harder to enforce and F0 is not invariant under ablation?
- Can the closed-form curvature constant Λ promised in the companion analysis be estimated cheaply enough to be used as a per-component flag during circuit discovery, separating components where the additive picture holds from those where it breaks?
- Is there a *designable* subset-selection procedure (currently left open by Corollary 2) that uses only measurable quantities (βi, αi(xB), δi, margins) to steer a target collapse polarity, turning Theorem 1 from a descriptive to a constructive tool?
- How does the dissociation βiδi vs. βiαi(xB) interact with the redundant/superposed codes of [6] quantitatively — can a predicted patch-recovery ratio κ > 1 be read off directly from carrier statistics, and used as a redundancy detector?
- Does the monotone interaction↔fidelity relationship (Spearman -0.83 / -0.85 / -0.90) stabilize into a *transferrable* threshold when measured across many architectures and natural-language tasks, or is the absence of a stable cutoff itself the genuine finding?
- How does the same-block interaction ∆(x) propagate through L subsequent layers in practice (Remark 6) — does the alternating RMSNorm-attenuation / linear-amplification product typically shrink, grow, or saturate on trained weights, and does this explain why joint head ablations across many layers violate the additive model so often?
