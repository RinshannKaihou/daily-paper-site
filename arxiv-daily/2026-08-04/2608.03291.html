The Tell-Tale Trace

Detecting Reasoning Failures in Large Language Models Using Chain-of-Thought Dynamics.

Shashwat Sourav — Physics, Washington Univ. in St. Louis  ·  Aishwarya Balwani — Developmental Neurobiology, St. Jude Children's Research Hospital
Key
Finding

Don't just check whether a chain-of-thought is wrong — diagnose how it failed, then prescribe the procedure-specific fix. A targeted proof-search prompt lifts Llama3-70B UNSAT accuracy from 13.3% → 85.0%, correcting 84.6% of failures that a generic retry barely touches (11.5%).

中文速览不假设 CoT 忠实反映内部计算，而是对推理链中句子的功能角色（规划/赋值/验证/回溯/定稿）动态序列建模，在 SAT/UNSAT 上揭示两种失败模式——SAT 的"早熟验证坍缩"与 UNSAT 的"用赋值搜索替代反证"——并用流程专属提示将 Llama3-70B 的 UNSAT 准确率从 13.3% 提升至 85.0%。

Why This Matters

From faithfulness debates to actionable test-time repair.

Treats the visible chain-of-thought as a behavioral trace — no assumption that it faithfully mirrors internal computation. It separates two distinct failure modes (premature verification collapse vs. wrong-procedure selection) and, crucially, ties each diagnosis to a procedure-specific correction rather than a generic retry.

Critical Edge

Oracle-assisted — and the detector is not universal.

Solver labels decide which failures to rerun, so the 85.0% demonstrates correctability of a diagnosed mode, not an end-to-end monitor. The headline "collapse" rests on a cross-family comparison confounded by verbosity, and early-warning coverage drops to 35.7% (zero lead time) on Llama3-70B.

Two failure modes, not one

§ 01 / DIVERGENT-PAIR ANALYSIS

SAT — premature verification collapse wrong < correct

On matched satisfiable instances, wrong traces rush into clause-checking, revisit similar operations, and finalize early — yet cover the same clauses as correct traces.

  • Shorter & more cyclic: 40 vs 60 sentences, cycle rate 0.56 vs 0.45 (Llama3-8B-wrong vs Qwen3-14B-correct, n=105).
  • Lower transition entropy: 0.58 vs 0.85 — repetitive, low-variability reasoning.
  • Early finalization: begins at 77% of trace vs 90%.
  • Clause coverage unchanged (p=0.206) — failure is not skipping the formula.
Dynamics-only AUROC (no answer leakage): 0.800 / 0.795 / 0.905 for Llama3-8B / 70B / OLMo2-13B.

UNSAT — wrong-procedure selection wrong = SAT conclusion

On unsatisfiable instances, models (Llama3-70B especially) search candidate assignments and declare SAT, instead of constructing a contradiction proof.

  • Directional collapse: Llama3-70B frontier accuracy 0.526 (SAT) → 0.159 (UNSAT), wrong-SAT rate 0.800.
  • Prompt-robust: wrong-SAT rate rises under oversight (D1) and attentiveness (D2) pressure vs. T0 — not a baseline artifact.
  • Explainable post-hoc, not mid-reasoning: pre-final-only AUROC is modest (0.507 / 0.570 / 0.569).
The asymmetry is the key: SAT = exhibit one assignment; UNSAT = rule out the entire space.

Diagnosis-informed correction

§ 04 / UNSAT INTERVENTION

The targeted proof-search prompt instructs case splitting, forced-consequence propagation, contradiction search, and a complete-assignment check before any SAT claim. The visible trace shifts in exactly the diagnosed direction:

Method at a glance

§ 05 / DESIGN
Task
Boolean satisfiability in CNF — SATBench levels L2–L6. Solver-certified ground truth on both sides.
Models
Qwen3-8B, Qwen3-14B, Llama3-8B, Llama3-70B, OLMo2-13B.
Prompts
T0 baseline · D1 oversight-pressure · D2 attentiveness-pressure (formula & required answer held fixed).
Capability frontier
Per-model band 0.30 ≤ accuracy ≤ 0.90; 91/127 matched pairs divergent. Acknowledged as ad hoc.
Roles
SAT: planning, assignment, verification, backtracking, finalization. UNSAT adds contradiction_search, unsat_proof, sat_commitment, clause_check. Rule-based regex tagger.
Dynamics features
Row-normalized transition matrix → densities, cycle rate, self-transition, entropy, finalization timing, clause coverage.
Statistics
Paired Wilcoxon + bootstrap CIs over divergent pairs; McNemar for the intervention; 50/50 split, no formula leakage; leakage-controlled AUROCs.
Framing
Explicitly behavioral — no claim that CoT faithfully reflects internal computation.

Strengths & limitations

§ 06 / ASSESSMENT

What lands well

  • Avoids the faithfulness trap. Findings survive the unfaithful-CoT literature by never assuming the trace mirrors internal computation.
  • Clean isolation of failure from difficulty. Capability-matched divergent pairs + SAT/UNSAT asymmetry is an elegant design.
  • Leakage controls. Dynamics-only AUROC stays high after removing answer-related features.
  • Actionable. Existence proof that a diagnosis-informed prompt rescues failures a retry cannot.
  • Intellectual honesty. Authors repeatedly state what the results do not show and quantify model-dependence.

What to push on

  • Confound in the headline. Strongest collapse is cross-family (Llama vs Qwen); within-family Llama3-8B vs 70B does not reproduce length or verification effects (p=1.0000 ns).
  • Detector not deployment-ready. 35.7% coverage on Llama3-70B; no precision / FPR / threshold analysis.
  • Intervention unmatched. Proof-search prompt is longer/more explicit than the retry — verbosity confound uncontrolled.
  • Single domain. Only Boolean SAT; method needs external checkability + a real procedure distinction.
  • No detector baselines. Cheap signals (length, finalization timing, raw densities) not compared against transition structure.
  • Role tagger is a rule-based abstraction; exact regex not fully specified; no code release referenced.

Where it goes next

§ 07 / OPEN
  1. Can the early-warning detector trigger the repair itself — without solver labels — and what happens to false-positive rate and net accuracy under that end-to-end pipeline?
  2. Does premature verification collapse generalize to reasoning-specialized models (o1-style, Qwen3-thinking), and does the within-family length confound vanish at scale?
  3. How much of the 84.6% UNSAT correction survives when the generic retry is length- and detail-matched to the proof-search prompt?
  4. Can the dynamics framework extend to domains without solvers (math, code, planning) via self-consistency or executor-based verification?
  5. Do the role-transition features add power over cheaper baselines (length, finalization timing, raw densities) once those are controlled?
  6. Is the UNSAT procedure mismatch a training-distribution artifact (over-representation of SAT-like examples) — and can targeted post-training mitigate it?
