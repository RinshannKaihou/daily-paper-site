> **一句话总结：** 本文把一篇配套论文中"单残差块内激活修补与权重消融交互项为零/二阶有界"的结果推广到多层情形，给出了多层交互的精确分解、跨层余项的精确双重积分恒等式，以及一个首次以闭式给出的单 token 注意力 Jacobian 上界（在 Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct 的真实权重上 12 个探测点零次违反），并在该模型上发现了无人专门训练的 IOI 电路，对坍缩/分离/交互三项指标给出了"四例部分成立、一例失败"的诚实混合结果。

> **TL;DR:** Extending a companion paper's exact single-block interaction theorem, this work gives an exact multi-layer decomposition of activation-patching vs. weight-ablation interaction into same-block terms plus one cross-layer remainder, isolates that remainder as an exact double integral (Proposition 2), derives a previously missing closed-form pointwise attention-Jacobian bound and verifies it with zero violations across 12 probed sites on Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct, and discovers an emergent IOI circuit in that model where collapse holds on 4 of 5 instances (ratio 0.168–0.396 vs. 0.761 on the exception, Anna/Mike), dissociation gap is non-negligible (0.224–0.852) on all 5, and a measurable relative interaction appears on 3 of 5 (0.066–0.327) — but at layer pairs outside the same-block scope the companion theorem actually proves.

## 核心贡献

- **多层精确分解（Proposition 1）：** 对横跨任意层数的消融子集 S，把"真实权重编辑后的网络选择子"与"理想化模型预测"之间的差距精确地拆成"每个被触及层的同块项 Δ_l^eff"加上一个跨层余项 R_×，全过程中没有任何近似；其中两个边界情形被完全钉死——只消融该层 MLP 时 Δ_l^eff ≡ 0，当该层直接喂给读出时 Δ_l^eff 恰好等于配套论文已界的 ψ(Δ_l(x))。
- **跨层余项的精确恒等式与闭式注意力 Jacobian 上界（Propositions 2 & 3）：** 对两个被触及层，R_× 被写成双参数插值下混合二阶偏导的双重积分（与配套论文在更低一层用微积分基本定理的手法一致，再升一维）。把该恒等式闭合成数值界所需的唯一缺失材料——单 token 注意力子块的局部 Jacobian 上界——以闭式导出，并在 Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct 的真实权重上点对点验证，12 个探测站点零次违反。
- **闭式曲率常数（Proposition 4 / Lemma 1）：** 配套论文二阶交互界依赖一个未给出的曲率常数 Λ，本文只用 MLP 三个谱范数与训练权重中的归一化尺度 γ 把 Λ 闭式算出，从而把"假设 Λ 存在"变成"在训练好的权重上可验证"。
- **在真实预训练模型上测一个涌现电路：** 用 Wang 等人原始的 activation-patching 方法在 Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct（28 层，GQA，d=1536）上搜出 IOI 电路（layer 25/head 9 在全部 5 个实例中出现），并对 collapse / dissociation / interaction 三探针逐项报告——其中 collapse 在 Anna/Mike 上明显失败（0.761），interaction 只在 5 例中的 3 例适用，作者把"混合结果"原样报告，而不是圆滑成"全部确认"。

## Key Contributions

- **Exact multi-layer decomposition (Proposition 1).** For an ablated subset S spanning any number of layers, the gap between the truly weight-edited network's selector and the idealized model's prediction decomposes exactly (no approximation anywhere) into a sum of same-block terms Δ_l^eff plus a single cross-layer remainder R_×; Δ_l^eff is pinned to zero when a layer's ablated carriers are MLP-only, and exactly equals the companion paper's already-bounded ψ(Δ_l(x)) when that layer feeds the readout directly.
- **Exact cross-layer identity + first closed-form attention Jacobian bound (Propositions 2 & 3).** For two touched layers, R_× is isolated exactly as a double integral of a mixed second partial (the companion paper's FTC technique applied one dimension up). The single missing ingredient needed to turn this identity into a usable bound — a pointwise operator-norm bound on how one attention head's output responds to a single perturbed key/value token — is derived in closed form and verified pointwise against Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct's real weights and a real forward pass, with zero violations across 12 probed (layer, head) sites.
- **Closed-form curvature constant (Proposition 4).** The companion paper's second-order interaction bound assumes a curvature constant Λ it never exhibits; this paper computes Λ in closed form from the three MLP spectral norms and the RMSNorm scale γ of the trained weights alone, discharging the hypothesis rather than leaving it assumed.
- **An emergent IOI circuit on a real pretrained model (Section 7.1).** Using Wang et al.'s original activation-patching method on Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct, the authors find a circuit (layer 25/head 9 shared across all 5 IOI lexical instances; 4/5 reach recovery ≥ 0.96, Lucy/Sam plateaus at 0.673) and report the collapse / dissociation / interaction probes honestly, including the Anna/Mike collapse failure (0.761) and the fact that only 3/5 circuits admit an interaction probe at all.

## Methodology

The paper is a mathematical-and-empirical extension of a companion paper. The setup restates (Section 3) an abstract conditional-residual model where a mapping F(x) = F_0(x) + Σ α_i(x) v_i decomposes into unconditional and carrier terms, deletion of a carrier subset S is realized exactly by low-rank weight edits (Remark 1), and a matched input pair (x_A, x_B) is correct iff s(x_A) > 0 > s(x_B). The companion paper's single-block Fact 4 shows the joint-ablation gap Δ(x) equals (I−Q)[−Dg(r_1(x))η(x)] minus a remainder R(x) that is O(Λ‖η‖²) under a curvature hypothesis.

Three new pieces of theory are then built:

1. **Multi-layer decomposition (Section 4, Proposition 1).** Writing the network as a sequential composition x_{l+1} = x_l + Block_l(x_l) and S = ∪ S_l, the true-minus-idealized selector gap is exactly Σ Δ_l^eff(x) + R_×(x). The proof is finite-sum regrouping plus Fact 5 substitution; no curvature argument is needed for the decomposition itself.
2. **Exact cross-layer identity and attention Jacobian bound (Section 5, Propositions 2–3).** For two touched layers, R_×(x) = ∫∫ ∂²H/∂t₁∂t₂ dt₁ dt₂ where H parametrizes a two-axis interpolation between clean and fully-edited networks. Bounding that mixed derivative needs a Jacobian bound for the attention sub-block; Proposition 3 derives ∥∂a/∂u_j∥_op ≤ p_j∥W_V∥_op + p_j∥v_j − a∥·∥W_K^⊤ q∥/√d_h in closed form (tight form) and a weight-only, normalization-closed variant, verified against finite-difference estimates on real Qwen weights.
3. **Closed-form curvature (Section 6, Lemma 1 + Proposition 4).** The second derivative of RMSNorm and the SwiGLU MLP are computed in closed form, giving Λ = ∥γ∥²_∞ ρ(r)² ∥W_1∥∥W_2∥∥W_3∥ (6 + 8s_1 + s_2 √d ∥γ∥_∞ ∥W_1∥) with s_2 = 1/2, s_1 = 1.0998.

Empirically (Section 7), the authors (i) reuse the companion paper's five 4-layer marker-task transformers to verify the multi-layer decomposition on 18 multi-layer configurations × 80 fresh probe pairs = 1440 measurements; (ii) load Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct natively, run a greedy activation-patching circuit search over 364 candidate sites with depth capped at 6, prune redundancies, and apply three probes (collapse via joint zero-ablation, dissociation via patching-vs-ablation gap, interaction via −R_× on the first head/MLP pair) on 5 IOI lexical instances.

## Results & Highlights

- **Attention Jacobian bound — zero violations on Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct (Remark 4).** Checked against finite-difference estimates at 6 (layer, head) pairs spanning layers 1–28, 2 key positions each, 24 random unit perturbations per site: **0 violations in 12 sites**. The tight (real-activation) form of the bound exceeds the empirical estimate by a factor of 12–111 (median 71); the weight-only normalization-closed form is looser by 4–6 orders of magnitude (median 1.7×10⁵, max 4.2×10⁶) — finite and never violated, but not numerically useful as stated.
- **Curvature bound refutation test (Remark 7).** On the five trained marker-task instances, the closed-form curvature bound (6) was checked against finite-difference estimates of D²N and D²g over 1200 random direction pairs: **0 violations in 1200 samples per quantity**. The bound is loose by a factor of 6.5×10²–2.4×10³; Lemma 1's normalization-only bound is loose by only 12.9–15.7×, indicating the slack accumulates at the composition step.
- **Multi-layer decomposition reproduces exactly (Section 7).** On the 18 multi-layer configurations × 80 probe pairs = 1440 measurements, the Proposition 1 identity holds to floating-point 0 on every measurement. Pooled medians: same-block sum 0.31 logits, total interaction 0.30, cross-layer remainder 0.59 (roughly twice either); same-block sum alone exceeds total interaction on 57% of pair-configs, R_× on 63%, so the two routinely partial-cancel rather than one dominating.
- **Emergent IOI circuit on Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct (Section 7.1, Table 1).** Layer 25/head 9 appears in all 5 pruned circuits; layer 19/head 6 in 4/5. Recovery ≥ 0.96 on 4/5 instances; Lucy/Sam plateaus at 0.673. **Collapse ratio (|s_abl(x_A)−s_abl(x_B)| / |s(x_A)−s(x_B)|):** Mary/John 0.245, Alice/Bob 0.224, Sarah/Tom 0.168, Anna/Mike 0.761 (failure — circuit barely narrows the branch gap), Lucy/Sam 0.396. **Dissociation gap (patching effect vs. same-circuit ablation effect on x_A):** 0.678 / 0.489 / 0.546 / 0.852 / 0.224 — non-negligible on every instance. **Relative interaction |Δ_HM − Δ_H − Δ_M| / (|Δ_H| + |Δ_M|):** 0.124 (Mary/John), 0.327 (Alice/Bob), 0.066 (Sarah/Tom); not applicable on the other two circuits (no MLP).

## Authors & Credibility

The paper is explicitly a follow-up to a companion paper by the same author and is unusually disciplined about scope: every theorem names what it does and does not close, every numerical check is framed as a refutation test rather than a confirmation, and Section 8 separates "what is established" from "what remains open" with care. Code is provided as Julia notebooks (verify_crosslayer_decomposition.jl, verify_curvature_bound.jl, verify_attention_jacobian_bound.jl, code4_real_llm_step1_matched_pair_search.jl, code4_step2_ioi_patching.jl). ## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** Genuinely novel along three axes — the multi-layer decomposition (Proposition 1) is an exact, approximation-free identity that was not in the companion paper; the pointwise attention Jacobian bound (Proposition 3) is, per the authors' own related-work section, something they could not find a prior closed-form treatment of and say so plainly rather than fabricate a citation; the closed-form curvature constant discharges a hypothesis the companion paper only assumed. The emergent-circuit check on Qwen is a real-model generalization, two orders of magnitude past the companion paper's synthetic instances.
- **Rigor:** Mathematical proofs are explicit and short (finite-sum regrouping; FTC applied twice). Every bound is paired with a finite-difference refutation test, and the authors are candid about looseness: the curvature bound is 6.5×10²–2.4×10³× loose, the weight-only attention bound 4–6 orders of magnitude loose. The "0 violations in 12 sites" / "0 violations in 1200 samples" framing is the correct epistemic stance (a refutation test, not a confirmation).
- **Reproducibility:** Strong. Concrete file names for every figure-producing script are listed; the marker-task checkpoints are reused (not retrained); Qwen weights are loaded locally; probe seeds (20260803/20260804) are stated and disjoint from the companion paper's seeds. The four candidate mechanisms (IOI, sentiment, temporal, magnitude) were pre-registered before probing, and the magnitude comparator (clean on 0/5) is reported as rejected rather than silently dropped.
- **Significance:** The central open item — whether the chained multi-layer attention Jacobian bound stays numerically controlled past a handful of layers — is **not closed**, and the paper says so repeatedly. This caps the practical impact: the closed-form bound is verified per-layer but, given the 4–6-order looseness at a single layer, "compounding into something vacuous after a handful of layers" is a real risk the authors flag but do not resolve. The Qwen evidence is a mixed result on **one** model and **one** mechanism (IOI), not a systematic replication; the interaction probe applies to only 3/5 circuits and none of those three sits in the same-block configuration the companion theorem actually covers, so it is "at most suggestive" of the paper's own open multi-layer case.
- **Red flags / honest weaknesses worth naming:** (a) The most important claim — a usable numerical bound on R_× — remains open; the paper delivers an exact identity plus an unchained per-layer ingredient. (b) The Qwen result is genuinely mixed, not confirmatory: Anna/Mike collapse ratio 0.761 is a clear failure, and the paper commendably reports it rather than excluding it. (c) Single author, no peer review evident, single pretrained model. (d) The interaction-probe layer pairs (head layer 25 vs. mlp layer 22/23, or head 19 vs. mlp 22) are explicitly outside the same-block configuration of Fact 4, so the "nonzero interaction" numbers do not test the companion theorem directly — they probe this paper's still-open generalization. None of these are dishonest; they are stated in the paper itself, which is itself a positive signal.

## Strengths

- Unusually honest scope management: every proposition names what it does not close (Remark 3, Remark 5, Remark 6, Section 8), and the Qwen result is reported as mixed rather than rounded toward confirmation.
- Three exact identities (Propositions 1, 2) plus two closed-form bounds (Propositions 3, 4) advance the companion paper's program in a way that is mathematically load-bearing, not incremental.
- Refutation-test discipline: bounds are checked against finite-difference estimates with explicit looseness factors, and the framing ("a single violation would indicate an algebraic error") is the correct one.
- Pre-registration of the four candidate mechanisms and reporting of the rejected magnitude comparator (0/5 clean) is a credibility signal rare in interpretability circuit papers.
- The bilinear-structure argument in Remark 6 for why no simple sign rule for R_× should exist is a genuine negative finding, not an unexplored question.

## Limitations

- The central practical question — whether the per-layer attention Jacobian bound composes into a non-vacuous multi-layer bound — is **not resolved**; the per-layer bound is verified, the chained multi-layer one is reported as unverified and "likely loose."
- The Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct evidence is a single model and a single mechanism (IOI); one of five instances (Anna/Mike) shows no real collapse (ratio 0.761), and the interaction probe applies to only 3/5 circuits.
- None of the three applicable interaction pairs sits in the same-block head-then-MLP configuration of Fact 4, so the interaction numbers bear on this paper's open multi-layer generalization, not on the companion theorem directly.
- The closed-form attention bound's weight-only variant is loose by 4–6 orders of magnitude — fine as a certificate, not as a numerical estimate.
- Single author, no indication of peer review.
- The sign of R_× has no identifiable rule (Remark 6), and the three-point sign check (matching sign(Δ_H + Δ_M) on 2/3 instances) settles nothing statistically.

## Relevance

对 mech-interp 可解释性研究者高度相关：本文给出的是 activation patching 与 weight-space ablation 这两条主流因果干预路径在多层真实模型上何时一致/何时不一致的精确理论，以及一个可直接用于估界注意力 Jacobian 的闭式工具，对设计可信的电路消融实验和评估因果归因误差有直接价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the chained per-layer attention Jacobian bound stay numerically controlled past 5–10 intervening blocks on a real model, or does the 4–6-order single-layer looseness compound into vacuity? A multi-layer refutation test (the obvious next experiment, explicitly not run here) would settle this.
- Does the same collapse/dissociation/interaction three-way pattern reproduce on other pretrained models (e.g., Llama-3-1.5B, Pythia-1.4B) and on mechanisms other than IOI (sentiment, temporal order, syntactic agreement), ideally with enough instances to test the sign question statistically?
- Can the closed-form curvature constant Λ be tightened — e.g., by replacing the product-of-norms argument with an actual spectral-norm estimate of the composed SwiGLU map — to reduce the 6.5×10²–2.4×10³ looseness?
- Is the Anna/Mike collapse failure (ratio 0.761) an artifact of the 6-site search budget, evidence of a redundant/self-repairing representation (Hydra effect), or a genuine counterexample to the companion paper's pattern? A deeper search at higher depth would discriminate.
- Can a usable sign rule for R_× be found by exploiting the bilinear pairing structure of Remark 6 (between propagated removed-mass and block l_2's Jacobian), or is the negative finding here definitive?
