> **一句话总结：** 作者从零独立复现了一台 30M 参数的 recurrent-depth 潜推理 Transformer，在严格预注册的双通道测量下证明"组合点火"是真实的、随问题深度合法时钟化的读出接口事件——在 commit 那一步决策 logit 边际骤升 5.8–8.0、方向发生几何对齐与冻结，但中间步从未经词表读出泄露（relay = 0.00），且整个签名不需要人类语言训练数据。

> **TL;DR:** An independent, pre-registered re-realization of a 30M-parameter recurrent-depth reasoner (replicating Kohli et al.'s "Loop, Think, & Generalize" recipe on 2,000 synthetic facts) shows the "compositional ignition" signature is a real, difficulty-clocked event at the vocabulary readout: first-hit iteration rises 1→4 across hop-depth k=1–10, sharpness is 0.805 (in-band), and at commit the decision margin jumps 5.8–8.0 logits in one iteration versus a 3.15 near-threshold null (96.4% of events above it) — while composed intermediates are never recoverable through the tied readout (relay 0.00) and the whole signature emerges without any human-language training data.

## 核心贡献

- **双通道同时测量 + 预注册裁判**：在同一前向 pass 内同时捕获词表读出（rank 通道）与隐状态（∥∆h∥ 通道），所有判据在数据产生前冻结，并把"速度低谷"等自我推翻的头条结果写进 predictions ledger，体现了一种高透明度的证据标准。
- **"点火住在读出层"的实证定位**：在 340 个预提交新项上，rank 通道的首命中中位数随深度 1→4 合法上升（k=1–10），sharpness 0.805 ∈ [0.714, 0.867]，in-window departure ≤ 0.07，crossed-differential floor 0.97–1.00，三个 COHERENCE 合取项全部命中。
- **margin-jump 形式的 commit 刻画**：commit 那一步决策边际单步跳变 5.8–8.0 logits，对最近阈非事件步 90 百分位的超越率达 96%（更精细地 96.4%），且这个有信息量的是"条件幅度"而非符号定义性的零交叉。
- **方向 snap + 径向 complement 的几何分解**：raw 几何里读出相关方向在 commit 处对齐并随后冻结（(1−cos) EVENT/PRE 1.62），commit 之后的位移以径向为主（0.961 平方范数）且对读出近乎零效应（≤5.7e-6）。
- **跨实现鲁棒性 + 语言无关性**：签名在两条同种子但训练轨迹混沌发散的实现、两个 curriculum 阶段上重现；模型从未见过任何人类语言，所以 MIRROR 假设非必要。
- **三假设裁决**：MIRROR（不必要）/ WORKSPACE 强形式（在这些通道里未被证实）/ PRINTER 否认形式（不充分）——把 dichotomy 修正为"接口构成的 commit"。

## Key Contributions

- **Independent, pre-registered replication** of the Kohli et al. "Loop, Think, & Generalize" 30M-parameter recurrent-depth reasoner (768-d state, 4-layer recurrent block, 2,000 synthetic facts = 200 entities × 10 relations, hop-depth curriculum to stage 21), with fidelity certified by a frozen whole-signature gate at two stage pairings.
- **Dual-channel per-iteration capture** in a single forward pass: rank(WU·LN(hr)) for the vocabulary readout and ∥hr∥ / ∥hr+1 − hr∥ for the hidden state, with the explicit acknowledgement that the two channels are coherent-by-construction (commensurable axes, not independent observations).
- **Margin-jump characterization of the commit**: at the first rank-1 iteration the decision margin jumps 5.8–8.0 logits in a single step, and against the identifiable near-threshold null (71 non-event steps inside the event's own pre-margin IQR) the arrival step's median |∆| is 7.96 versus an in-band 90th-percentile of 3.15, with 96.4% of events above it — arguing the transition is not rank-discreteness alone.
- **Directional snap + radial/angular dissolution**: raw-geometry (1−cos) displacement EVENT/PRE = 1.62 with a post-commit freeze (post/pre 0.06), while 0.961 of post-commit squared-norm is radial and readout-null to a measured bound (radial logit effect ≤5.7×10⁻⁵), dissolving the earlier "post-commit motion" paradox as decode-invisible scale.
- **Three dissociations that scope the finding**: cross-realization robustness (two same-seed, chaotically-divergent realizations via byte-matching clocks), developmental emergence (the "settled" signatures are late achievements grown over the route, absent at competence onset), and language-independence (signature appears in a model trained only on non-verbal synthetic structure).
- **Honest retraction infrastructure**: the predictions ledger documents the withdrawn velocity-trough headline (L26–L27b) and a demoted "depth-dependent locus" claim, demonstrating an evidential standard by exhibiting the claims the programme rejected under frozen criteria.

## Methodology

The author grows an **independent realization of the published 30M-parameter recurrent-depth reasoner** of Kohli et al. (arXiv:2604.07822) from scratch under the published configuration and seed: 768-d hidden state, 4-layer recurrent block, trained on a closed world of 2,000 synthetic facts (200 entities × 10 relations) with a hop-depth curriculum to stage 21. Development is filmed at 8-epoch resolution (archive from epoch 328).

**Dual-channel per-iteration capture** is the core instrumentational idea. A single forward pass per item hooks the final block; both channels are deterministic functions of the same captured hidden states hr:
- Rank channel: rank(WU·LN(hr)) for the answer and every walk-verified intermediate.
- State channel: ∥hr∥ and ∥hr+1 − hr∥ at the answer position and whole sequence, plus top-5 margins.

This makes the axes commensurable by design and a stated dependence (not an assumed independence). The padded/eval-true convention is used throughout and the decode fast-path was proven byte-identical before use.

**Pre-registration discipline** is central: all estimators (first-hit clock, runway-qualified sharpness, windowed departure, phase-resolved ∥∆h∥) were validated by exact reproduction of reference-class numbers before any verdict capture; every criterion was frozen before its data existed; every amendment is reference-side, versioned, with evidence blocks. A pre-registered whole-signature gate certifies fidelity (the C5.5 gate passes on clock, departure, sharpness at both stage-12 and stage-21 pairings; the ∥∆h∥ row fails in both and is headlined rather than hidden).

The **commit analysis** uses three nulls against which the margin jump is compared: (i) a within-item same-quantity null (|∆margin| at every non-commit iteration of the same trajectory), (ii) a regression baseline fitted on all 5,040 non-event steps (|∆| ∼ pre-margin + iteration + k), and (iii) the identifiable near-threshold null — the 71 non-event steps lying inside the event's own pre-margin IQR. The radial/angular decomposition uses the model's real readout (LayerNorm scale-invariance only in the ϵ → 0 limit, measured contribution ≤7×10⁻⁵ absolute).

## Results & Highlights

- **Difficulty clock (rank channel, 340 pre-committed novel items, frame 4257):** first-hit median rises lawfully 1 → 4 across k = 1–10 (k=1: 1.0; k=2–5: 1–2; k=6: 3; k=7–8: 3; k=9: 4; k=10: 4), with byte-matching to the reference at every k.
- **Runway-qualified sharpness:** 0.805 ∈ [0.714, 0.867] (in-band on runway-clean cells), versus the published reference band; sharpness is only computable at k≥6 because lower-k items commit in one or two iterations.
- **Arrive-and-hold stability:** in-window departure ≤ 0.07 everywhere; crossed-differential floor 0.97–1.00 (the signal is query-specific everywhere — not decode-side salience, not corpus statistics).
- **Margin jump at commit (Table 1):** the signed target margin goes from negative (e.g. −2.17 at k=1, −2.74 at k=6, −2.31 at k=9) to a single-iteration jump whose unsigned top1−top2 magnitude is 5.89–7.16 (cluster-bootstrap medians), i.e. **5.8–8.0 logits** depending on k.
- **Margin-jump significance:** against the near-threshold null, the arrival step's median |∆| is **7.96 versus an in-band 90th-percentile of 3.15, with 96.4% of events above it**; against the regression baseline on 5,040 non-event steps, **268/274 event residuals exceed the non-event 90th percentile (median residual 6.33 vs 0.36)**.
- **Relay (intermediate decodability) = 0.00** at every iteration, k, and checkpoint under the pre-registered rank criterion — the silent walk: composed intermediates never surface through the tied vocabulary readout.
- **Directional snap (raw geometry):** (1−cos) displacement EVENT/PRE = **1.62** at the answer position with a post-commit freeze (post/pre = 0.06); **0.961 of post-commit squared-norm is radial** and readout-null (radial logit effect ≤5.7×10⁻⁵).
- **Fidelity gate:** the C5.5 whole-signature gate passes on clock/departure/sharpness for both stage pairings (3992 vs. 5019; 4257 vs. 5388) but **fails on ∥∆h∥ in both** — headlined, not hidden.
- **Language-independence:** the full ignition signature emerged in a model that has never seen a word of human language, so corpus inheritance (MIRROR) is not necessary for this signature in this substrate class.
- **Withdrawn headline:** the velocity-trough claim is retracted — pre-registered normalization controls (raw, RMS-scaled, norm-relative, directional) showed it survives only in magnitude-carrying families and inverts under norm-relative (0.40) and directional (0.06) representations against raw's 2.00.

## Authors & Credibility

Single author: **Simon Lam-Muir** of **Prime Calibre, Australia** (ORCID 0009-0001-2442-4479) — an independent/non-academic affiliation, not an established interpretability lab. The paper is unusually transparent about AI-assistance roles: analysis, review, and drafting in collaboration with "Claude Fable 5 (Anthropic; high reasoning effort)"; autonomous build and execution by a delegated coding agent running "Claude Opus 4.8 (Anthropic; high reasoning effort)". Credibility rests on the **pre-registration receipts** (predictions ledger, incident records, developmental film index shipped at https://github.com/primecalibre-research/ltg-replication-receipts) rather than institutional reputation, and the paper explicitly exhibits claims it rejected under frozen criteria (the withdrawn velocity-trough, a demoted "depth-dependent locus", a sign-wrong verbal hedge). The work positions itself against a credible related line: Kohli et al. (the replicated reference), Lu et al. (found no structured latent pathway in depth-recurrent rank — corroborated here), DiscoLoop (Fu et al.), the halting-gates line (Popescu et al.; adaptive depth), and Gurnee et al.'s global-workspace reading of verbalizable representations.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty — moderate, reframing rather than discovery.** The difficulty-clock fact (first-hit iteration tracks hop complexity) was already established by the replicated reference (Kohli et al., their Figs. 8–9); this paper's genuine increments are the margin-jump characterization of the commit, the raw-geometry directional snap plus its radial complement, and the language-independence dissociation. The "ignition lives at the readout" thesis is essentially a **structural re-interpretation** of an existing phenomenon rather than a new phenomenon.
- **Rigor — unusually strong on pre-registration, genuinely weak on the state channel.** The append-only predictions ledger, the frozen C5.5 fidelity gate, and the public withdrawal of the velocity-trough headline are a real evidential standard. However the headline fidelity gate **fails on the ∥∆h∥ row in both pairings** (Table 2) — i.e. the hidden-state channel does not actually reproduce the reference on the only cross-realization test available; the author attributes this to "state geometry is realization-dependent under fixed seed" and reclassifies it as "a new finding," which is a convenient but defensible move. The near-threshold null underpinning the headline margin claim comprises **only 71 control steps, all from k=6–10** (because lower-k items commit too fast to furnish controls) — explicitly flagged as a limitation but worth emphasizing: 274 events against 71 controls drawn from 1.4% of the non-event population is a thin counterfactual base for the central "not rank-discreteness" claim.
- **Reproducibility — high in principle, narrow in scope.** The recipe, seed, code, ledger, and incident records are public; the author explicitly invites byte-matching. But "one substrate class (30M recurrent-depth), one task family (pure-depth composition; interference/binding deliberately excluded)" is a very narrow beachhead — the commit-surface geometry has not been tested at scale, on natural language, or on binding/interference tasks. The author flags the confounded twin four-cell comparison and the missing halting analysis as open.
- **Significance — speculative until cross-scale.** If the commit-surface geometry holds in frontier models it would matter; if it is a small-model, closed-world artifact it is a clean but narrow interpretability result. The author is honest that the scale question "requires partners."
- **Red flags.** (1) The composite decoder-coordinate claim is **not confirmed** (arrival step falls "just below our preregistered bar"), so one of the two coordinate systems fails the pre-registered criterion — only the raw-geometry version of the snap is confirmed. (2) The angular/radial decomposition relies on LayerNorm being scale-invariant only in the ϵ → 0 limit; the author measures the residual at ≤7×10⁻⁵ but concedes this is **not separable from float32 rounding** at that magnitude. (3) Heavy AI-assistance attribution (Fable 5, Opus 4.8) combined with a single non-academic author raises the bar on independent replication; the receipts repository is doing a lot of the trust work. (4) The paper's prose style (self-referential, ledger-citation-heavy, dramatic verdict rhetoric) is unusual for the venue and may slow peer review.

## Strengths

- Pre-registration discipline with public receipts, including a publicly **withdrawn** headline — a model of intellectual honesty rare in this subfield.
- Dual-channel capture from a single forward pass makes the rank/state axes commensurable by design.
- Three-way adjudication of MIRROR / WORKSPACE / PRINTER (and the COHERENCE/PORT founding question) is a clarifying frame.
- Language-independence dissociation (signature emerges with no human-language data) is a clean, decision-relevant result against the MIRROR hypothesis.
- Cross-realization robustness claim is backed by byte-matching clocks across two same-seed realizations.

## Limitations

- **Single substrate class, single task family:** 30M recurrent-depth, pure-depth composition; interference/binding deliberately excluded; one closed synthetic world (2,000 facts, 200×10).
- **State-channel fidelity fails:** ∥∆h∥ row of C5.5 fails in both pairings; state geometry is realization-dependent under fixed seed, so state-channel claims are caveated to "our realization."
- **Thin counterfactual base for the central margin claim:** 71 near-threshold control steps, all at k=6–10; lower-k items commit too fast to furnish controls; statistical intervals conditional on the one sampled world.
- **Composite decoder-coordinate claim not confirmed** (below the pre-registered bar); the radial null bound (≤7×10⁻⁵) is not separable from float32 rounding.
- **No functional claim tested:** captures run fixed iteration ladders, so there is no halting analysis and no test of whether the event is *used* — the registered early-exit intervention (forcing readout immediately before vs. after the event) remains future work.
- **The magnitude-arc observation is post-hoc/exploratory**, discovered during capture verification rather than pre-registered.
- **Twin four-cell comparison is confounded** (basin-sink moves the instrument's own calibration distribution).

## Relevance

对研究 latent reasoning / 反复深度 Transformer 的机制可解释性直接相关：它用预注册+双通道方法把"组合点火"精确定位到读出接口，并提供了"commit surface"作为分析 commit 时刻的新工具，可作为后续在更大模型、自然语言、binding/interference 任务上验证此类签名几何的参考范式。对于关心 test-time compute 与隐式链式推理机制的读者，这篇论文的 margin-jump 刻画与"中间步不可解码"结论是构建功能性解释（vs. 仅描述性）的关键证据起点。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the commit-surface geometry (margin jump + directional snap + radial null) hold in **frontier-scale** recurrent-depth or universal-thinking models, or is it a small-model closed-world artifact?
- The registered **early-exit intervention** — forcing readout immediately before vs. after the commit step — is the missing functional test; does perturbing the commit step causally change downstream accuracy?
- The language-diet arm: does **adding verbal training data** move the commit surface (i.e., is MIRROR "not necessary" but still "sufficient/modulating" at scale)?
- Why is **state geometry realization-dependent under a fixed seed**? Is this a genuine chaotic divergence of training dynamics or an artifact of the ∥∆h∥ estimator?
- The relay = 0.00 result contradicts DiscoLoop's reported decodable bridge entities — is intermediate decodability **strictly training-regime-dependent**, and what regime variable controls it?
- Does the radial/angular dissolution generalize beyond LayerNorm decoders, where the ϵ-term/float32 confound disappears?
