Why this matters

Multimodal LLMs selectively ignore evidence they have already perceived — and multiple-choice benchmarks cannot see the suppression, or the fix.

对理解 LLM 内部表征如何被选择性忽略具有直接参考价值。

Critical edge

The intervention is unconditional: it attenuates the claim whether or not the claim is right — no experiment tests cases where audio is ambiguous and the text should legitimately win.

Also flagged: the mechanistic core rests on a single 7B Whisper-encoder model, and the free-form intervention runs on few items — the authors themselves say it “should not be overinterpreted”.

01Methodology — distilled

Four-stage circuit dissection, gated by capability

  • 01Capability screen first. Only skills each model can perform from audio alone are analyzed (audio-only condition excludes chance-level models, e.g. Baichuan on prosody).
  • 02Linear probes on frozen activations track speaker gender through the Whisper-family encoder → projector → LM input.
  • 03Attribution patching (gradient-based) ranks causal contributions, gated by a faithfulness test: top 20% of components must reproduce 100% of model behaviour — they do.
  • 04Audio-side repairs: back-patching sweep (source layers 4–31, windows 1–7, scales 1.5–5×, norm-rescaled) plus a TTS-resynthesized ALME replication of the original method.
  • 05Attention knockout (γ=0) to the text-claim span: 3 position scopes × layer bands (mid 10–21 / late / all 32) × 2 formats — free-form re-applies the block at every decoding step.
  • 06Damage controls throughout: matched-item accuracy, text-only cost, fluency under the unmodified model. All inference at 16-bit.

03Strengths

Why the evidence holds

  • ＋Clean decomposition — perception (probing) vs use (attribution) vs repair — applied only to capabilities the model demonstrably has from audio alone, forestalling the suppression-vs-absence confound.
  • ＋Honest negative results: back-patching's null is replicated against its own source task and demoted to “uninformative” rather than spun as non-transfer.
  • ＋Sharpest finding: the closed-set vs free-form dissociation — the working intervention is invisible to multiple-choice scoring.
  • ＋Full control battery (matched items, text-only cost, fluency, contrast blocks) rules out “the intervention just damages the model”.

04Limitations

Where the claim thins out

  • －One 7B model, two conflict categories; scale (Audex-30B was behaviour-only), non-Whisper encoders, languages, and conflict types untested.
  • －Stimuli are acted performances (RAVDESS, MUStARD++), English-only — conflicts more categorical than real conversation.
  • －Repair reduces but does not eliminate dominance (gen-TDR 0.375 / 0.433); the all-layer variant damages capability; the block is unconditional on claim correctness.
  • －The back-patching replication diverges from the published +0.160 gain (plausibly TTS/stimulus differences) — the lexical-vs-paralinguistic mechanism link stays open.

05Questions for further exploration

Open threads

  1. Does the intermediate-relay mechanism replicate in non-Whisper encoders (Phi-4-multimodal, Baichuan-Omni) and at 30B scale (Audex) — or is it a Whisper-embedding artifact?
  2. Can the claim-attention block be made conditional, attenuating text only when audio confidently contradicts it?
  3. Why does back-patching fail to replicate: TTS voice identity, layer-indexing conventions, or configurations overfit to specific stimuli?
  4. Would relay-aware evaluation (free-form generation scored for claim-echoing) change published rankings on LISTEN / MCR-Bench?
  5. Does the same relay structure govern other cross-modal suppressions in vision-language models?
