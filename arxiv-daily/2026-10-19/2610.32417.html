Why this matters

Per-token energy metrics mask real cost: quantization errors propagate into the agent tool-call stack and flip system-level energy bills — precision choice is a reliability decision.

「每 token 指标掩盖任务级真实成本」——量化精度问题向 Agent 软件栈传导，改变系统级指标。

Critical edge

The 3.5× headline rests partly on a strict harness — the dominant failure is one dropped closing brace that grammar-constrained decoding would likely fix; the penalty may be interface fragility, not quantization itself.

01 Methodology — the measurement rig

  • Subjects: 4-bit (Q4_K_M) vs 8-bit (Q8_0) GGUF files of two open-weight models — Qwen3-30B-A3B (MoE) and Devstral Small 2 (dense 24B) — served one precision at a time by llama.cpp.
  • Rig: bare-metal Chameleon nodes (dual EPYC 7763, A100 40 GB); CPU-package + GPU-board power sampled every 0.1 s and attributed to every model/tool call. Failed attempts are charged to completions.
  • Tasks: 29 verifiable tasks in six families (SQL analytics, data repair, regression control, data processing, function implementation, NetworkX repo repair) with hidden verifiers, private seeds, Landlock sandboxing, and anti-gaming checks.
  • Measures: E_tok per output token · E_inf per verified completion · E_op whole-task window per completion.
  • Statistics: 5,000-draw bootstrap CIs, McNemar/Fisher completion tests, a label-permutation noise baseline, and a pre-registered confirmatory/exploratory split (held-out BIRD + EvalPerf predictions made before running).
ρ_op = ρ_tok × token factor × boundary factor

Break-even rule: a 6–16% per-token saving is erased once the cheaper precision needs more than ~6–19% extra tokens per completed task. Watch tokens per completed task, not joules per token.

Budgets: primary 16,384-ctx / 24 turns / 600 s; expanded 65,536 / 250 turns / 1,800 s — 1 greedy + 10 sampled repetitions, seed-paired and interleaved.

02 Results ledger — beyond the headline

The per-token saving is real — 4-bit is cheaper per token on 25–29 of 29 tasks — but the token factor (1.6–2.0× more tokens per completion) eats it.

Averages hide the tail: single-task ratios swing from 0.13 (4-bit uses ~⅛ the energy, regression control) to 3.5× — and condition averages flip from +17% (Qwen primary) to −8% (Qwen expanded, Devstral). In the 8,192-row worst case, 4-bit took 8 turns per attempt vs 3 and generated ~4× the tokens.

Strengths

  • Operator-shaped thesis — "the energy bill arrives per completed task, not per token" is crisp and immediately actionable.
  • Exemplary measurement craft: bare-metal, seed-paired, randomized order, drift-monitored (<0.35%), failed attempts honestly charged.
  • The identity turns an observation into a deployable rule of thumb — track tokens per completed task against the per-token saving.
  • Pre-registered held-out predictions both held (EvalPerf favors 4-bit; BIRD shows nothing) — rare discipline.
  • Honest statistics: permutation noise baseline, confirmatory/exploratory split, separation-repetition retest.

Limitations

  • Strict tool-call parser amplifies 4-bit's cost — grammar-constrained decoding or call repair (no ablation run) could change the numbers materially.
  • Narrow serving envelope: one engine (llama.cpp), one GPU generation, one CPU family, single request, no batching, no wall-power cross-check.
  • Conflated comparison: 4-vs-8-bit mixes bit width with quantization scheme, calibration, and kernels — cause can't be isolated.
  • Fragile small families — regression control has 2 tasks; a 0.47 family average conceals a 0.13–1.72 split. Devstral's retest evidence is thin (2 of 5).
  • Artificial repairs: injected single NetworkX defects, not real issue history; open-ended agent work out of scope.

04 Questions for further exploration

Q1How much of the per-task penalty survives grammar-constrained or auto-repaired tool calls — is it output-format fragility rather than energy?
Q2Do findings transfer to batched multi-tenant engines (vLLM, TensorRT-LLM) and newer formats (FP8, AWQ, sub-1-bit)?
Q3Would a budget tuned per precision — giving 4-bit the longer context its smaller file allows — systematically favor 4-bit on hardest repairs?
Q4Does quantization interact with reasoning modes, where token counts already inflate 21–75% and compound the token factor?
Q5Can tokens-per-completed-task be monitored online to trigger automatic precision switching per task family?
Q6Is the 9× malformed-call rate predictable from perplexity or calibration degradation — enabling a cheap pre-deployment screen?
