A Fix for Softmax and Cross-Entropy ↗

一句话总结：所有主流深度学习框架中 softmax/交叉熵实现存在两个浮点 bug——高置信度下目标 logit 梯度被舍入为零（Softmax Collapse）……作者用 softplus/expm1/log1p 在对数空间重写这些函数予以修复。

Key Metric
100.0%

test accuracy for fixed fp32 cross-entropy on a+b mod 31 grokking (ε = 10⁻⁸, 5 seeds) — versus 94.3 ± 5.4% for the standard implementation.

Why This Matters

Silent floating-point bugs corrupt gradients in bf16/fp32 training; this pinpoints the root cause of Slingshot loss spikes and fixes them in ~10 lines.

低精度浮点数值稳定性缺陷引发静默的梯度错误与训练不稳定，直接服务于 LLM 训练稳定性（损失尖峰的根因定位与修复）。

Critical Edge

All evidence comes from tiny algorithmic grokking tasks — no realistic-scale experiment exists, and the "no meaningful slowdown" claim ships without any wall-clock measurement.

01Methodologythe numerics, distilled

  • Asymmetric precision is the root cause: 1+e⁻ᵐ rounds to exactly 1.0 once the logit margin m passes the precision cliff — the target's loss and gradient die while the K−1 non-target gradients (computed from negative differences, never adding 1) stay accurate to underflow.
  • Collapse fix: keep e⁻ᵐ in the numerator and rewrite log-softmax as z̃ₖ − softplus(−m); the target slot becomes a constant 0, so autodiff itself enforces the zero-sum property.
  • Cancellation fix: for distillation, never subtract two rounded softmaxes — compute q − p in log space from δ = z_S − z_T via expm1/log1p, then project g = u − q·Σu so g = 0 exactly when z_S = z_T.
  • Drop-in: no internal fp64 casting, no hyperparameter changes; ~10 lines of JAX each, with stop_gradient grafting the custom gradient onto the standard forward loss.
  • Test bed: established grokking protocols — 1-layer width-64 ReLU Transformers, a+b mod 31 plus three variants (10k-epoch full-batch AdamW), distillation probed at θ_S = θ_T, margins measured with LaProp for ε down to 10⁻¹⁶.

03Strengthswhat holds up

  • A rare triple: one-line root-cause bug report, a mechanistically complete causal story (cliff → v̂ decay → spike), and a predictive law m* ≈ log(1/ε) verified across six orders of magnitude of ε.
  • Genuinely drop-in fixes — no fp64 casting, no hyperparameter changes, ~10 lines — that provably restore the exact math (zero-sum gradients; exactly zero gradient at z_S = z_T) rather than papering over symptoms.
  • Unifies scattered prior observations (Slingshot, low-precision grokking failures, the ε–precision interaction) under one numerical mechanism, and explains why the default ε = 10⁻⁸ is pathological in fp32 — first account of its kind.

04Limitationsread before adopting

  • Evidence confined to small grokking benchmarks (modular arithmetic ≤ 113 classes, width-64 Transformers / 2-layer MLPs) — nothing on vision, language, or any task where the loss isn't driven to numerical zero.
  • Distillation covers only the θ_S = θ_T fixed point: it shows the standard loss can drift, not that it hurts a real teacher→student transfer.
  • No wall-clock or throughput numbers despite the "no meaningful slowdown" claim; fixes are JAX-only, PyTorch/TensorFlow ports left to the reader.
  • Margin-equilibrium analysis assumes the post-memorization regime (|g| ∝ e⁻ᵐ) and stationary Adam statistics; SGD / sign-SGD unexplored.

05Questions for Further Exploration

  • At what realistic scale (vocab size, label smoothing, mixed precision with fp32 master weights) do margins actually approach c₃₂ ≈ 16.6 — and can the cliff be detected in production loss curves as flat plateaus followed by spikes?
  • Does the fixed soft-target CE change outcomes in on-policy distillation / KL-regularized RLHF, where student and teacher logits are close by construction and bf16 is common?
  • Can the implicit ελ L2-equivalence serve as a cheaper knob than explicit weight decay for controlling logit growth in large-scale AdamW training?
  • Can the fix be fused into CUDA/TPU softmax kernels without the memory/latency cost of extra expm1/log1p passes — and at what measured overhead?
  • Does the same target-dies-first asymmetry appear in other saturating losses — sigmoid + BCE on highly separable data, or focal loss at large margins?
