Why this matters✓

2-bit quantization breaks long-chain reasoning; OnQ is a practical recipe to recover it — and a template for reliable low-precision, fault-tolerant training and inference.

01

Methodology

方法 · 蒸馏于自身轨迹
S1

Block-wise calibration

Per-block MSE reconstruction (Block-AP style, 4,096×2,048-token windows) lifts the 2-bit model out of round-to-nearest collapse — 1.7B average 3.91 → 23.35.

S2

On-policy distillation

Each step rolls out the student (128 prompts, temp 1.0) through the quantized forward pass; the frozen full-precision teacher supervises next-token distributions on those same prefixes — training on trajectories the student actually visits, fixing trajectory divergence.

S3

Candidate-wise Dynamic KL

Per-candidate weight α = p/(p+q) mixes forward and reverse KL — more FKL where the student underpredicts, more RKL where it overpredicts (over top-20 tokens + residual). Micro-diagnostic: one gradient step corrects 95.1% / 84.7% of severe errors on both sides, tracking the better single direction each time.

S4

Response-length schedule

Rollout caps 4,096 → 8,192 → 16,384 tokens (steps 1-50 / 51-100 / after), because the fresh 2-bit student degenerates into repetition late in long rollouts (21.1% repeated tokens beyond 8K, fading to ~1% by step 100).

03

Strengths

5
  • Explains why fixed-response QAT fails — prefix mismatch compounding over thousands of self-generated tokens — not merely that it fails.
  • Dynamic KL is motivated by a clean micro-diagnostic (single-gradient-step correction rates on under-/overpredicted candidates), not hand-waving.
  • Gains land where deployment pressure is highest: at 2 bits, a 4B overtakes a 2×-larger 8B running under every baseline recipe.
  • Fixes the generation pathologies that make quantized reasoners unusable — truncation and repetitive looping — measured with a κ-reported labeling protocol.
  • Strong ablation discipline: token-matched comparisons isolate prefix source (+8.40) from objective; GRPO-alone collapse (31.40 MATH-500) shows distillation is the right tool from a 2-bit cold start.
04

Limitations

6
  • Single model family (Qwen3 1.7B–8B), weight-only W2/W3 — no W4, mixed precision, or activation quantization; no Llama/DeepSeek/Mistral.
  • No cost reporting: continuous rollouts plus a concurrently served full-precision teacher burden that fixed-response QAT avoids.
  • Baselines' sequences cut at 8,192 tokens — handicapping them exactly on the long-horizon benchmarks where OnQ's margin is largest.
  • The 4K/8K/16K length schedule is a hand-tuned heuristic, derived from one setting and never ablated against a fixed cap.
  • AIME 24-26 has only 90 problems; per-seed SDs up to ~4.5 points leave several deltas within noise.
  • 2-bit recovery stays far from full precision on the hardest benchmarks (8B AIME 44.07 vs 71.85); repetition errors classified by an LLM judge.

Credibility

Team
Michigan (Zheng, Shen, Mao), UC Santa Barbara, Meta AI — Zechun Liu of the EfficientQAT/LLM-QAT line this paper extends.
Rigor
3 evaluation seeds with SDs; baselines retrained from a shared calibrated checkpoint; explicit counts (AIME 90, LiveCodeBench v6) and fixed decoding settings.
Labeling
Repetition errors judged by blinded dual-pass LLM with author adjudication (κ 0.87–0.88).
Novelty
Moderate, honestly framed: on-policy distillation (GKD) and Dynamic KL (Jung et al. 2025) are adopted, not invented — the contribution is diagnosis + recipe + validation.
06

Questions for further exploration

开放问题
  1. How much survives weight+activation quantization (W2/A8, W4/A4), where activation outliers dominate the error?
  2. What is the true cost ratio (GPU-hours, teacher serving) vs fixed-response QAT — does the 10-point gain survive a compute-matched comparison?
  3. Would the length schedule generalize, or is it an artifact of Qwen3's repetition profile — would a fixed 16,384 cap or entropy-based early stopping match it?
  4. Does self-trajectory training transfer to other degenerate regimes — FP8/FP4 post-training, bit-flip-corrupted models?
  5. Can the residual 2-bit gap be closed by adding GRPO-style RLVR after recovery, given RLVR alone collapses from the cold start?
  6. Does per-candidate Dynamic KL also help full-precision on-policy distillation, or is it specific to quantization-induced error structure?
