Methodology

How the audit works

  • Three orthogonal evidence structures. Parallel (900 cases; an ambiguous mention induces two disjoint 2–4-hop chains from fictionalized MuSiQue), conditional (1,364; an explicit qualifier must survive), imbalanced (1,838; 1,010 science + 828 policy from Serper web retrieval, the minority view grounded in a single sentence).
  • Anti-shortcut design. Every entity is fictionalized, so compressors cannot rebuild omitted evidence from world knowledge.
  • Proposition-level scoring. Gold evidence decomposes into minimal self-contained propositions; a case counts under All-Rec only if all are entailed by the compressed context alone. GPT-5.2 judges, validated at 93% human agreement (100 decisions per setting).
  • EPR metric. Evidence Preservation Return = κ·√(All-Rec / TFLOPs) with prefix-amortized FLOP accounting; proprietary APIs excluded.
  • Controlled protocol. Sentence-level output budgets, per-setting ICL prompts and thinking modes; a position study permutes 400 two-hop parallel cases (14 arrangements each).

Results

Beyond the headline number

  • Rankings are structure- and domain-unstable. Claude-Haiku-4.5 leads parallel evidence 63.8% vs 31.9% for GPT-5.4-mini (2.0×) yet trails it on imbalanced science (45.5% vs 60.3%); moving science→policy flips GPT-5.4-mini 60.3%→22.6% while lifting Llama-3.1-8B 4.1%→34.5%.
  • Budget amplifies inequality. From 0.5× to 2× output budget the top-3 LLMs gain +32.8 All-Rec points on average, the bottom-3 only +3.9; on parallel evidence Gemma-4-31B-it rises 33.3%→71.4% while Llama-3.2-1B stays at 0.0% under every budget.
  • Compute ≠ preservation. EXIT burns 40.2× RECOMP-extractive's compute for +1.5 points; Gemma-4-12B-it uses 35.0% less compute than Qwen3-14B while scoring +20.0 higher. EPR peaks at 4B–8B (Gemma-4-E4B-it 5.163, Qwen3-4B 3.445, Llama-3.1-8B 2.981); below 50 TFLOPs/case nothing reaches 30%.
  • Position effects. Separating the two evidence blocks (Adjacent→Distant) cuts Para-Rec by 4.8–9.0 points; earlier evidence is recalled 2.5–10.6 points more often (primacy bias).
  • Scaling localizes failure, doesn't fix it. Among 991 cases all Gemma-4 models fail, missing exactly-one proposition rises 15.8% (E2B)→61.0% (31B). Failure signatures differ: EXIT keeps fragments of multiple branches without completeness in 70.2% of shared failures; BRIEF-Pro collapses to a single branch in 62.2%.

Strengths

What the paper gets right

  • Measures the right object. Scores the compressed context itself with an all-or-nothing metric matching how evidence loss actually breaks reasoning — validated by the 96.0%→11.5% pilot collapse.
  • Carefully disentangled settings. The cross-setting disagreements (Claude wins parallel 2:1 but loses science; policy flips two models in opposite directions) show single-benchmark compressor rankings are unstable — the most valuable practical finding.
  • EPR reframes the field. Shows the "high-preservation, low-compute" regime is empty for every method — compression is a frontier problem, not a scaling problem.
  • Honest, mechanistic error analysis. Localized omissions under scaling; extractive = breadth-without-completeness vs abstractive = whole-branch collapse gives actionable design guidance.

Limitations

Where to hold skepticism

  • Judge circularity risk. GPT-5.2 judges outputs including GPT-5.4-mini; the 7% disagreement rate is material for a conjunction metric like All-Rec, where one misjudged proposition flips a whole case.
  • Short inputs only. 3.1K-token average is far from the long-context (100K+) regime where compression matters most.
  • Generous default budget. Demonstrations carry >2× the reference evidence tokens — partly a budget-policy artifact framed as compressor failure; the 0.5× column is far bleaker.
  • Paradigm conflated with scale. Only four dedicated compressors (two are 2024 RECOMP variants, ≤775M) pitted against 31B LLMs.
  • Decaying comparability. Imbalanced-setting web evidence rots as sources change; proprietary EPR unreported, leaving the two best models outside the efficiency analysis.

Questions for further exploration

  • Do All-Rec and the structural failure signatures hold at 50K–200K-token inputs — does the ceiling fall further, and does primacy bias amplify with length?
  • Can EPR be computed for proprietary compressors via measured token pricing or published FLOP proxies, closing the efficiency gap?
  • Would a query-aware extractive-then-abstractive hybrid fix the two observed failure modes (breadth-without-completeness; single-branch collapse) at 4B–8B scale where EPR peaks?
  • Is the judge's 7% disagreement uniform, or concentrated on paraphrased propositions — and would a dual-judge protocol change rankings?
  • Can compressors be trained on these three evidence structures without contaminating the now-public benchmark?
