Why This Matters

Silent radiation bit-flips corrupt quantized inference with no warning. A software-only detect-and-repair layer answers the no-ECC hardware niche where reliability otherwise has none.

01 Methodology — the three-part machine

1
Inject realistic MBU faults. Contiguous bit-bursts drawn from a truncated power law P(m) ∝ m−2.2 on {1..256} — 33% multi-bit strikes (E[m]=2.65), calibrated to checkerboard SRAM data — flipped into stored INT8 weights and biases. Per-channel scales stay protected.
2
Train into a “Green” subspace. Fault-Aware Training runs four forwards per step (clean + 3 surrogate corruptions, deliberately mismatched to the test fault model). Hinge penalties push ~95% of clean energy inside a fixed rank-32 DCT-II basis — regenerated in closed form at runtime, never stored. Persistent memory stays 1.0× INT8.
3
Heal by two-pass projection. Per-coordinate syndrome s = residual²/(max|t|²+ε); a row is flagged only if faults are both large and concentrated (max(s)>0.18 and >2·mean(s)). A near-hard sigmoid gate down-weights flagged coordinates; a second reconstruction is built only from survivors, so the repair is never poisoned by the fault it removes. Weight heal on a dequantized working copy (writeback every Q=256); activation heal every inference.

6 INT8 regression benchmarks · 3-hidden-layer MLPs · 400 realizations/point · 8 seeds

  • Runtime heal, isolated: on a frozen FAT checkpoint, toggling only the heal cuts the native-unit excess integral by a mean 23.4% — for just +0.5% clean RMSE.
  • The conceded regime: in the SBU-dominated window (I ∈ [10⁻⁴, 10⁻²]) SECDED wins, median 0.49 vs 0.76; the method’s early-window mean (1.11) is worse than doing nothing.
  • Ablations pin the mechanism: removing weight alignment alone degrades excess AUC 0.71 → 1.83; a per-row MAD despiker is strictly worse on all six datasets.
  • Overhead: 2.5× MACs at batch 1, ~2.0× at batch 256; persistent storage 1.0× (SECDED ~1.0×, TMR 3.0× ROM). LEO translation: CHF +5% tolerance horizon ≈1.7 → ≈6.0 years.

03 Strengths

  • Zero persistent storage overhead — 1.0× INT8 ROM, DCT basis regenerated in closed form and even checksummable in flight. Deployable on COTS parts with no ECC: the actual underserved niche.
  • Tight causal chain: frozen-checkpoint toggle (+23.4%), MAD-despiker control, and no-weight-align ablation (0.71 → 1.83) isolate the runtime mechanism from training effects.
  • Regression, not classification: activation-clipping defenses don’t apply there, and four of six datasets are real domain data (SDSS photo-z, CMS jets, C-MAPSS, critical heat flux).
  • Sensitivity hygiene: multiplicity sweep conceding the sub-10–20%-MBU regime to SECDED, per-bit damage split (bit 7 = 55.5% of damage), gate-threshold sweep, honest no-op disclosure for fc1.

04 Limitations

  • Wrong tool at low intensity: loses to SECDED+interleaving when SBUs dominate (0.49 vs 0.76), and its early-window mean is worse than unprotected.
  • Clean-accuracy tax: median +0.3% RMSE, but +11.8 ± 5.8% on CHF — a per-dataset gamble that must be checked before deployment.
  • No retrofit path: alignment requires training from scratch; pre-trained models cannot adopt it, unlike passive ECC.
  • Cost accounting is analytic: 2.0–2.5× MACs excludes quantization, checksums, and memory traffic; up to ~130× at batch 1 if weights heal every pass; the Q=256 writeback cadence was never exercised under faults.
  • Narrow scope: INT8 MLPs ≤ ~70k params, one-shot snapshot injection, simulated logical-address bursts only, baselines partially proxied.

05 Open Questions for Further Exploration

Q1Does the concentration gate survive address-scrambled SRAM, where a physical burst scatters across logical rows and may never trip max(s) > 2·mean(s)?
Q2Do INT8 CNNs — or transformer blocks, where “neuron ordering is arbitrary” after attention mixing — still admit a rank-32 DCT-aligned subspace at acceptable tax?
Q3Can alignment be imposed by fine-tuning pre-trained checkpoints, lowering the from-scratch adoption barrier the authors themselves flag?
Q4How does the heal behave when strikes interleave with inference over calendar time — and what Q writeback cadence is actually safe at a given flux?
Q5Could the syndrome gate pair with lightweight CRC/quantile SDC monitors to cover the SBU window, yielding one defense across the full intensity range?
