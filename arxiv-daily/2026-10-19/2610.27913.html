01 Methodology — how the audit was run
  • Panel: six open chat-tuned models — Qwen2.5-7/14/32B, Llama-3.1-8/70B, DeepSeek-V2-Lite-16B.
  • Compression: BnB-4bit (on the fly), AWQ-INT4 + GPTQ-INT4 (community checkpoints), and one-shot SparseGPT at 50% sparsity in two forms — 2:4 structured and unstructured — calibrated on alpaca-cleaned. Distillation excluded (it entangles compression with new supervision).
  • Benchmark-native readouts instead of single scalars: strict vs flexible GSM8K, prompt- vs instruction-level IFEval, pass@1 vs pass@100 HumanEval, MC1 vs BLEURT TruthfulQA — the decomposition itself is the diagnostic.
  • Score = parent-relative retention R = S_comp / S_FP16; PsychoBench read as psychometric profile drift.
  • Controlled serving: one vLLM stack, 8× RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell, ~750 GPU-hours; 25,000+ questions, 1,785 role-play dialogues, 13 psychometric scales; standard errors on the main benchmarks.

Beyond the headline

  • Answer-form brittleness under quantization: Qwen2.5-7B GSM8K strict match halves 0.3351 → 0.1691 under GPTQ-INT4 while flexible holds 0.7225 — the answer is recoverable, the canonical format is not.
  • Format death, not just score death: DeepSeek-V2-Lite parseable EQ-Bench answers fall 169.7 → 108.2 under unstructured sparsity — the benchmark score becomes unavailable.
  • Coordination breaks before recognition: IFEval instruction-level retention consistently exceeds prompt-level; whole-response constraint coordination goes first.
  • Valid programs still in distribution: Llama-70B under 2:4 recovers pass@1 0.4334 → pass@100 0.6646.
  • Persona drift: CharacterEval vividness 0.74–0.77 sparse vs 0.87–0.88 overall; PsychoBench dark-triad drift; 16P stability ≈0.88 sparse vs ≈0.91–0.95 quantized.
＋ STR Strengths
  • Broad and internally consistent: 35+ checkpoints, 10 benchmarks, 25k+ questions, 1,785 dialogues, 13 scales — one stack, same GPUs.
  • The decompositions are the real contribution: “score dropped” becomes “coordination broke before recognition”, “answer recoverable but not canonical” — deployer-grade signal.
  • Interpretive honesty: retention > 1.0 never read as improvement; DTDD shift reported as drift, not a safety claim; exceptions surfaced, not buried.
  • Actionable: pick compression by the ability the application critically needs; algorithm targets named (protect belief-action-link activations).
− LIM Limitations
  • Behavioral only — no mechanistic account of why second-order beliefs or constraint coordination are the fragile ones.
  • Confounded panel: family, scale, and method not disentangled (acknowledged); one pruning algorithm, one sparsity ratio.
  • No cost side: no memory / latency / throughput numbers — the ability-vs-cost trade-off can't be closed from this paper alone.
  • Thin slices: EQ-Bench has 60 items; some ToMBench sub-ability counts are small; no significance tests.
  • Dated panel & questionnaire persona: Qwen2.5 / Llama-3.1 generation; PsychoBench/16P ecological meaning debatable.
05 Questions for further exploration
  • Q1Is “pruning is fragile” a property of SparseGPT@50% or of sparsity itself — Wanda, learned masks, lower ratios?
  • Q2Can per-layer ability probes localize second-order-belief / constraint-coordination circuits, enabling targeted mixed-precision repair?
  • Q3Is the Qwen GSM8K strict-match collapse (0.3351→0.1691) a harness artifact — answer-marker prompt sensitivity — and does fixing it re-rank quantization methods?
  • Q4Do fragile-ability findings transfer to 2026-era models and FP8/INT8 on Blackwell-native stacks?
  • Q5Could a few-hundred-item ability-probe CI gate catch “shallow retention” in community checkpoints before deployment?
