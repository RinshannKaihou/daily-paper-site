> **一句话总结：** 上海AI Lab 推出 35B（激活 3B）MoE 智能体模型 Sage-A1.5，通过把"任务分解/并行子代理调度"（Agentic Orchestration）与"证据审查/策略自我修正"（Agentic Verification）内化为模型自身学得的能力，在 FS-Olympiad（81.0 vs GPT-5.6-sol 73.0）、CMT（80.0 vs 50.0）、PinchBench（90.0，全场第一）等基准上以 1/30 的参数量超过 Kimi-K3、GLM-5.3、GPT-5.6-sol，并用从量子光学自纠错到自动化量子化学工作流的真实案例展示了端到端"AI 科研"能力。

> **TL;DR:** A 40-page technical report from Shanghai AI Lab introducing Sage-A1.5, a 35B-A3B MoE agent built on Agents-A1 that trains *capability composition* — learned orchestration of parallel subagents with isolated contexts plus in-model verification of stalled exploration — rather than just broader task coverage. Across 20 benchmarks it beats the far larger Kimi-K3/GLM-5.3/GPT-5.6-sol on FrontierScience-Olympiad (81.0 vs 73.0 for GPT-5.6-sol, +8.0), CMT condensed-matter theory (80.0 vs 50.0, +30.0), PinchBench (90.0 vs 88.2 for Kimi-K3) and IFBench (81.6 vs 72.7 for GPT-5.6-sol), but clearly trails them on DeepResearch-Bench-II (43.7 vs 57.9), SWE-Bench Pro (61.2 vs 64.6) and ALE-CLI (19.1 vs 28.6). Case studies (SeekBrain neuro-science reanalysis, a 12-hour Psi4 quantum-chemistry workflow, PXRD structure refinement) show genuine long-horizon scientific execution, though they are single-run showcases and the unified model loses part of each domain teacher's peak (science teacher FS-O 89.0 → final model 81.0).

## 核心贡献

- **能力组合（Capability Composition）作为训练目标**：区别于只扩展"能力覆盖面"（更多领域/任务），Sage-A1.5 明确把"如何组织、分配、协调计算"变成模型学得的策略，而非外部脚手架里的固定规则。
- **Agentic Orchestration（智能体编排）**：模型自行决定任务分解、向拥有独立上下文的并行子代理委派子任务（区分"探索式组合" A∨B∨C 与"构建式组合" A∧B∧C 两种模式），并通过结构化反馈（answer/status/evidence/unresolved_issues）回收证据、迭代规划。
- **Agentic Verification（智能体验证）**：模型在推理过程中自主识别"搜索停滞/关键中间结论待查"，区分已证实事实与未验证假设并改道；验证通过系统提示中的条件标记 `Verification protocol:enabled` 按需激活，推理时无需在线监督模型或外部验证工具。
- **统一训练管线**：多领域教师（搜索、MLE、SWE、科学、真实工作流）先分别训成，再经"结果 RL + 多教师在线策略蒸馏（OPD）"的样本级混合后训练融合为单一模型；配合 pVisor 沙箱基础设施（暂停/换出/恢复执行环境，静态测试驻留内存约降 80%）支撑超长轨迹训练。
- **开放式科学发现实证**：用 50 频道 YouTube 商业版图调研（13 轮主代理 + 51 个子代理、2.6M tokens、6.7h 研究时间）、Navier–Stokes 深度研究报告（8 节、391 处引用）、Psi4 量子化学 12 小时全流程、Sc₂Au₆ 晶体精修、电化学机理回放、荧光斑点检测迭代优化等案例，展示从"解题"走向"科研"的路径。

## Key Contributions

- **Capability composition as a training target.** Beyond expanding *capability coverage* (more domains), Sage-A1.5 explicitly trains *how execution is organized* — decomposition, delegation to parallel subagents with isolated contexts, and evidence-driven integration — as part of the model's policy instead of hardcoded scaffolding rules.
- **Agentic Orchestration.** The model distinguishes Exploratory composition (multiple agents test rival hypotheses on one question, A∨B∨C) from Constructive composition (complementary subproblems whose outputs must all fit, A∧B∧C), interleaving both dynamically. Subagents return structured feedback (answer, self-assessed status, per-URL evidence, unresolved issues) that the main agent compares to resolve gaps and plan follow-ups.
- **Agentic Verification.** A learned, conditioned capability: when progress stalls or a key intermediate conclusion is reached, the model reviews confirmed facts vs. unsupported inferences and redirects exploration. Activated by appending `Verification protocol:enabled` to the system prompt; needs no online supervisor or external verifier at inference.
- **Unified multi-domain post-training.** Domain teachers (search, science, MLE, SWE, real-world workflows on the ALE recipe) are merged via Stage-I multi-harness SFT (Claude Code, Codex, internal harnesses) and Stage-II sample-level mixing of outcome RL (R = V_d − format − protocol penalties) with multi-teacher on-policy distillation (reverse-KL), under asynchronous rollouts with bounded staleness.
- **Engineering-grade data machinery.** Kaggle-MLE Factory (Kaggle competitions → executable tasks with hidden test sets), SWE-rebench-V2 + in-house SWE tasks with explicit leakage control (Git-object scrubbing, proxy-blocked rollouts, no-op reward checks), recursive long-context task evolution (3,512 hard QA pairs; base-model success rate 31.57%), and a >1,000-environment professional-workflow pool grown from a few dozen seeds with builder/reviewer/judger role separation.

## Methodology

The report frames agent progress along two axes: *capability coverage* (breadth of domains) and *capability composition* (how capabilities are organized during execution). Orchestration is trained via two task families: **Constraint-Guided Long-Horizon Search**, built by extending Agents-A1's relation chains into starlike constraint graphs (pick an answer entity, attach multiple relation branches, mask identifying details — yielding questions solvable by delegating different constraints to parallel investigators), and **Multi-Source Research Synthesis** with complementary parallel assignments. Teacher rollouts run in an orchestration harness; main-agent and subagent trajectories are treated as independent supervised samples (role-specific supervision), and each subagent report is supervised in its sender's trajectory and treated as an observation by its receiver.

Verification is trained by running an execution model on live search tasks while a supervisor model injects reflections at points of repetitive search, stalled progress, or key conclusions; the supervision prompts are then stripped so the reflection lives inside the model's own reasoning text, gated by the conditioning marker. A planned extension uses verification points as natural boundaries for context compression.

Domain teachers: (1) **Science teacher** — problems selected by a strong model's world-model judgments (difficulty / task type / tool benefit, ExoMind-style), a research harness with search/visit/code/scholar tools plus isolated code sandbox and tool-result replay, then trajectory distillation from a strong model, three-dimension filtering (correctness, reasoning depth, tool-call success — deliberately retaining recoverable-failure trajectories), and extended multi-epoch SFT on Qwen3.5-35B-A3B. (2) **MLE teacher** — Kaggle-MLE Factory + MLE-Dojo tasks (excluding MLE-Bench overlap), an experiment-tree harness, staged SFT, then Windowed Interaction RL (WIRL): interactions between successive experiment results form windows with shared outcome-based rewards (medal thresholds + delta vs. last valid submission; execution errors penalized), optimized asynchronously with PPO-clip + KL-to-SFT + entropy, using pre/post-update probability ratios as off-policyness correction weights. (3) **SWE** — mini-swe-agent and Claude Code harnesses, reference-patch and no-op validation, re-grading of zero-reward trajectories, and strict leakage control. (4) **Search** — research questions generated from a search-grounded proposal agent so every question is answerable from actually discoverable web evidence, filtered by per-question evaluation criteria. (5) **Real-world tasks** — environment pool scaled from ~dozens of manual seeds to >1,000 professional environments via controlled evolution with separated builder/reviewer/judger agents and deterministic graders (ALE-style), yielding several hundred million supervised tokens.

Stage II merges everything with per-sample selection among outcome-only RL, process-only multi-teacher OPD, or mixed supervision, sharing one token mask, harness format, and asynchronous training loop, with cross-domain and held-out-harness evaluation to monitor interference.

## Results & Highlights

- **Science benchmarks (Table 1):** on FrontierScience-Olympiad Sage-A1.5 scores **81.0 vs 73.0 for GPT-5.6-sol, 69.0 for Kimi-K3, 67.0 for GLM-5.3** (+8.0 over the best larger model) with only 35B total / 3B active parameters; on CMT (condensed matter theory) it reaches **80.0 vs 50.0 (GPT-5.6-sol), 40.0 (GLM-5.3), 34.0 (Kimi-K3)** — a +30.0 gap — and on FS-R (PhD-level research subtasks) **48.3 vs 46.2 (GPT-5.6-sol)**.
- **Science teacher ablation (Table 2):** SFT on the distilled science trajectories lifts the Qwen3.5-35B-A3B base from **64.5 → 89.0 on FS-O, 2.5 → 70.0 on FS-R, 20.0 → 84.0 on CMT, and 0.9 → 25.7 on CritPt** — enormous gains precisely on research-level tasks.
- **Agentic Verification ablation (Table 5, BrowseComp):** enabling verify mode raises the teacher from **81.0 → 85.5 (+4.5 pts, highest among compared configurations)** while *reducing* mean output tokens from 23,245 → 21,962 (−5.52%) and turns from 84.81 → 82.14 (−3.15%) in paired attempts — verification pays for itself by cutting unproductive exploration.
- **Agentic Orchestration ablation (Table 4):** on the final model, orchestration adds **+1.1 pts on BrowseComp (81.7 → 82.8) and +3.8 on DeepResearch Bench II (43.7 → 47.5)**; the orchestration teacher itself reaches 53.6 on DRB-II vs 42.3 for the raw Qwen3.5-35B-A3B base.
- **MLE teacher (Table 6, MLE-Bench-Lite, mean ± SEM over 3 seeds):** any-medal rate **72.73 ± 2.62% vs 37.88 ± 5.46%** for the Qwen3.6-35B-A3B base (+34.8 pts), with gold-medal rate more than doubling (22.73 → 50.00%).
- **General/agentic benchmarks:** PinchBench **90.0 — best of all evaluated systems** (Kimi-K3 88.2, GPT-5.6-sol 87.3, GLM-5.3 86.6), IFBench **81.6 vs 72.7** (GPT-5.6-sol) and 72.1 (Kimi-K3); GAIA **95.1** (A1: 96.0, GPT-5.6-sol: 88.3); XBench-DS-2510 92.0. Weaker spots: ALE-CLI **19.1 vs 28.6** (GPT-5.6-sol), DRB-II **43.7 vs 57.9**, HLE w/ tools **55.1 vs 64.5**, CritPt **20.0 vs 32.3**, SWE-Bench Pro **61.2 vs 64.6**.
- **Scientific case studies (single-run showcases):** on CritPt #19 (cascaded optical parametric amplifiers) the model detected an analytic-vs-numeric discrepancy of up to 24.16, localized a wrong complex conjugation in its own checker (`conjugate(α)β + conjugate(γ)δ → αβ + γδ`), and reduced the maximum residual to ~3.55×10⁻¹⁵ over 10 tests (Claude Opus 4.8 retained wrong extremum expressions on the same task); the SeekBrain neuroscience reanalysis reproduced the paper's shared decoding axis (PC1 explains **63.2%** of variance over 201 regions vs 24.0% null mean across 5,000 permutations, p ≈ 2×10⁻⁴; r = 0.960 between value- and rank-based PCA); the Psi4 pyridine–NMA workflow completed in ~12 hours (authors estimate ~1 working week for a human); the fluorescence-detection pipeline improved `aggregate_improvement` from −0.0469 to 0.1158 over 8 submissions.

## Authors & Credibility

Large team effort from Shanghai Artificial Intelligence Laboratory ("Sage-A1.5 Team", 100+ listed authors, project co-leads Xiangchao Yan, Wenlong Zhang, Shufei Zhang, Peng Ye, Bo Zhang; scientific directors include Lei Bai, Xiangyu Yue, Wenwei Zhang; advisors Xing Xie, Dahua Lin, Bowen Zhou). The report is unusually detailed about data machinery (leakage control, no-op checks, role-separated environment synthesis) and reports negative/mixed results alongside wins (ALE-CLI and DRB-II gaps, teacher-to-student capability loss, Rwp fit quality caveats). Baseline numbers are taken from competitors' original technical reports where available and re-evaluated under matched protocols otherwise — a reasonable but partly mixed-source comparison. No external model release confirmation or third-party evaluation is given in the PDF itself.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** The core reframing — making execution organization (orchestration) and verification *learned, in-model* capabilities rather than scaffold rules — is a genuine and well-argued contribution; the conditioning marker for verification and the sender-supervised/observer-view treatment of subagent reports are neat engineering ideas. But each ingredient (parallel subagents, self-reflection, outcome+distillation post-training, Kaggle task factories) exists in prior work; the novelty is composition and integration scale, not any single mechanism.
- **Rigor (weak points worth noting):** The headline "matches or surpasses Agents-A1 and Ornith-1.5 on all twelve benchmarks" is generous — Table 1 shows Sage-A1.5 *below* A1 on GAIA (95.1 vs 96.0), HiPhO (45.3 vs 46.4) and MatTools (42.6 vs 47.1). The ablation attributing gains to orchestration on the *final* model is thin: +1.1 on BrowseComp is within plausible run-to-run noise (no variance reported), and the much larger +3.8 on DRB-II conflates the orchestration teacher's strength with the toggle's effect. A no-ablation of the verification *data* (reflection-injected vs. plain trajectories) weakens causal claims about why verification helps.
- **Distillation loss is visible in their own tables:** science teacher FS-O 89.0 vs final model 81.0; MLE teacher 72.7% any-medal vs final MLE-Bench-Lite 65.2; orchestration teacher 53.6 vs final 47.5 on DRB-II — the unified model is consistently worse than its best teachers, which the report honestly flags but does not quantify systematically.
- **Case studies are n=1 showcases:** the PXRD refinement ends at Rwp 86.1% (they admit "substantial remaining mismatch limits conclusions about structural accuracy"); the electrochemistry replay is explicitly non-causal ("model hypotheses, not established causal relationships"); the "~1 working week for a human" chemistry estimate is a rough author guess; no success rates over repeated runs are reported for any showcase. The pVisor ~80% memory-reduction claim rests on "preliminary static tests, with one run per condition."
- **Reproducibility:** Data pipelines (Kaggle-MLE Factory, >1,000 synthesized professional environments, supervisor-injected verification trajectories) are described but not released in the PDF; evaluation harnesses have quirks others must match (BrowseComp context discard + up-to-5 reruns after 300 tool calls; subagents capped at 50 turns). A minor inconsistency: the science/search teachers use "Qwen3.5-35B-A3B" while the MLE table says "Qwen3.6-35B-A3B".
- **Significance:** If the numbers hold, a 35B-A3B open-weight-class model beating GPT-5.6-sol/Kimi-K3 on FS-Olympiad and CMT by 8–30 points is a significant result for accessible scientific agents, and the local-deployment story (FP8/4-bit, Ascend/Apple Silicon) is practically valuable. Red flags: none severe, but cherry-picking risk exists in the intro's benchmark subset ("four benchmarks where it wins") while it trails larger models on roughly half of Table 1.

## Strengths

- Clear conceptual framing (coverage vs. composition; A∨B∨C vs. A∧B∧C parallelism) that will be reusable vocabulary for the field.
- Verification ablation shows the rare result that *added reasoning reduced cost* (tokens −5.52%, turns −3.15% at +4.5 pts accuracy on BrowseComp).
- Serious data hygiene: leakage audits, no-op reward checks, separated task-builder/reviewer/judger roles to avoid self-certification, exclusion of benchmark-overlapping competitions.
- Honest limitations section that names teacher-retention loss, context overhead on ultra-long trajectories, and the simulation/replay-only nature of current scientific showcases.
- Breadth: 20 benchmarks across search, engineering, science, instruction following, and general agentic tasks, plus 10 professional-workflow artifacts and 6 scientist-aligned case studies — far more evidence than a typical model card.

## Limitations

- Trails larger models on roughly half the evaluated benchmarks: DRB-II (43.7 vs 57.9), HLE w/ tools (55.1 vs 64.5), CritPt (20.0 vs 32.3), HiPhO (45.3 vs 64.5), SWE-Bench Pro/Multilingual, SciCode, MLE-Bench-Lite, ALE-CLI (19.1 vs 28.6), VitaBench, ChemCoTBench, MatTools.
- Unified post-training does not retain each teacher's peak (FS-O 89.0 → 81.0; MLE any-medal 72.7% → 65.2).
- Orchestration/verification training still centers on search and research synthesis; ultra-long trajectories accumulate heavy context overhead (the verification-point compression scheme is described as future work, not shipped).
- Scientific showcases operate on simulations and historical data replays — no active physical experiments; no repeated-run statistics for case studies.
- Many training-data assets and evaluation harness details are internal; independent replication will be hard without a public release.

## Relevance

高度契合"Self-Evolving Agents & Automated Research"方向：Sage-A1.5 正是一个面向自主科研（agentic scientific discovery）的长程智能体，其编排+验证内化训练与端到端 auto-research 案例（SeekBrain 复现、Psi4 工作流、机理假设迭代）直接展示了 AI scientist 的当前能力边界；同时它也是上海AI Lab 的官方技术报告，符合"Model System Cards & Technical Reports"关注范畴，可用于校准 35B 级开源智能体与万亿级闭源模型的真实差距。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How much of the FS-O/CMT advantage comes from the science teacher's task-selection heuristics (ExoMind-style difficulty/tool-benefit filtering) rather than from orchestration/verification themselves? A swap ablation is missing.
- Can verification-point-based context compression (the planned extension) close the DRB-II gap (43.7 vs teacher 53.6), or is distillation loss from multi-teacher merging the binding constraint?
- Does the `Verification protocol:enabled` conditioning marker transfer across harnesses, or does it overfit to the training scaffold? Held-out-harness numbers are mentioned but not reported in detail.
- What are repeated-run success rates and cost distributions for the scientific showcases (e.g., does the Psi4 workflow succeed reliably, or was it a curated best-of run)?
- Would the orchestration gains survive if subagent budgets (50-turn cap, 300-tool-call context reset) were removed — i.e., is the model or the harness doing the long-horizon management?
- How does the 3B-active MoE compare against a dense 35B with identical post-training, to isolate whether MoE sparsity itself matters for agentic horizons?
