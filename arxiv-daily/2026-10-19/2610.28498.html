Key Finding · 关键发现

A failed probe is weak evidence of absence: a digit copied from the question is present in hidden states, yet nearly unreadable.

探针失败 ≠ 信息缺失 —— 从题目原样复制的数字确实存在于模型编码中（最小对比对读出 0.92–0.97），常规单提示探针却几乎读不出来。

Why This Matters relevance

Anyone using probes or logit lens to decide whether a model “really reasons internally” is holding an instrument that can fail on present values.

用探针或 logit lens 判断模型“内部是否真的在推理”之前，先校准你的仪器——失效的可能是仪器，不是模型。

Critical Edge sharpest caveat

“Not used” is narrowly scoped: patch and clamp nulls cover one position and class-mean 9-dim subspaces — an a+b code held elsewhere, or in other codes, would be missed.

Also bounds the claim: two ~1.5B models only · computation studies on Ouro alone · not yet peer-reviewed

01 Methodology · 方法

Validate the instrument first

Ten pre-registered studies, thresholds frozen in commits; code, pre-registrations and freeze hashes public.

  • States read, nothing trained — fp32 hidden states at the middle/end of each of Ouro-1.4B’s four 24-layer loops, and after layers 4/7/14/21/28 of Qwen2.5-1.5B; the reimplemented forward pass matches Ouro’s own (max logit diff = 0).
  • Standard probe — standardize → 256 PCs → multinomial logistic regression, scored on held-out folds grouped by template.
  • Core instrument: minimal pairs — ten copies of a question differing in one digit (137 families from 77 questions); subtracting the family mean isolates the digit’s trace.
  • Five-part protocol for computed values — copy ceiling on one-hot input copies; mod-5 labels; ranking against all 31 digit combinations; sign-flipped twins (a+b vs a−b); synthetic mixing nulls.
  • Causal tests — patch or clamp a 9-dim subspace at P1, then read which digit Ouro writes; answer patch as positive control, a−b and random subspaces as negatives.

P1 = token just before the answer’s units digit, gold answer fed in.

  • Swamping, not crowding — the digit’s subspace is no busier than shuffled-label nulls (overlap 0.83–1.07×); a random word in the slot reads at 0.24–0.74 while equal-distance noise stays unreadable (0.11–0.15).
  • Loop gains are front-loaded — GSM8K accuracy 6.5% → 23.0% over loops 1–4, but only +3.0 pts from loop 2–4; the loop-1→2 jump also appears on HellaSwag and MMLU.
  • The unwritten intermediate — a+b shows a small sign-following signal over its copy ceiling (I = +0.157, CI [0.141, 0.172]), but generic mixing alone reproduces roughly half (I ≈ +0.09).
  • The sanity check prior work missed — a standard probe for the largest number in the question (a value that must be copied) scored 40.8% — exactly the majority-guess rate.

03 Strengths · 优势

  • Instrument-first reframing of a live debate (do looped models reason latently?) — and the instrument fails on a known-present value.
  • Elegant presence test — family-mean subtraction turns ten one-token-different prompts into a clean causal contrast, transferable across templates.
  • Reusable protocol — each of the five components targets a specific confound (copies, parity, sign preference, output mixing).
  • Exemplary pre-registration discipline — negative and ambiguous results reported with the same prominence as positive ones (Appendix C self-audit).
  • Properly controlled causal section — positive/negative patch controls, equal-answer pair designs, clamping across loops.

04 Limitations · 局限

  • Scale — two small models only (~1.4–1.5B); computation studies on Ouro alone; no frontier-scale test.
  • Narrow task family — a±b−c two-step word problems, two-digit numbers; probing at P1 requires feeding in the gold answer.
  • Presence test, not a decoder — minimal pairs need all ten variants of a question; single-prompt decoding quantified only up to 5,249 questions, curves still rising.
  • Scoped causal null — one position, class-mean 9-dim subspaces; values held elsewhere, in other codes, or read via attention would be missed.
  • Forking paths — Study 2’s threshold was set after Study 1’s near-miss; several key rankings were post hoc.

05 Questions for Further Exploration

  1. Would the presence/readability gap close with 50k+ training questions — or is single-prompt decoding of small traces fundamentally data-limited at these scales?
  2. Does the swamping analysis generalize to genuinely computed values in multi-hop or implicit-CoT tasks where prior work reported nulls?
  3. Could alignment-search subspaces (rather than class-mean 9-dim ones) find an a+b code at earlier positions whose patching does move the answer?
  4. If generic mixing reproduces ~half the sign-following signal, what mechanism produces the other half — and does the looped architecture prevent any early intermediate from being causally efficacious?
  5. Do loop gains concentrated at loop 2 — mirroring non-reasoning benchmarks — mean the loops buy general state refinement rather than latent chain-of-thought?
