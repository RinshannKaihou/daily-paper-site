> **一句话总结：** 本文提出 ACE-Compression 评测框架，在 6 个开源 LLM 的 35+ 个压缩检查点（4-bit 量化与 50% SparseGPT 剪枝）上、用 10 个基准从"能力"而非"任务分数"的角度评估压缩损伤，发现压缩是重新分配能力而非均匀降分——粗粒度/表面能力存活而细粒度/长尾能力（二阶信念、约束协调、角色扮演生动性）悄然崩塌，且 4-bit 量化（GSM8K flexible 仅 -4.4 pt）远比 2:4 剪枝（-72.8 pt）安全。

> **TL;DR:** Instead of reporting aggregate benchmark scores, ACE-Compression evaluates 35+ compressed checkpoints (BnB/AWQ/GPTQ 4-bit quantization vs. SparseGPT 50% pruning, 2:4 and unstructured) of six open LLMs (Qwen2.5-7/14/32B, Llama-3.1-8/70B, DeepSeek-V2-Lite-16B) across 10 benchmarks and 4 ability dimensions. On GSM8K, Llama-3.1-8B loses only 4.4 pts under GPTQ-INT4 (0.8446→0.8006 flexible match) but collapses by 72.8 pts under 2:4 sparsity (0.8446→0.1168); HumanEval greedy pass@1 falls 0.6951→0.1280 for the same model under 2:4 pruning. The core message: compression redistributes abilities (coarse/surface signals survive; fine-grained, long-tail, context-dependent ones like second-order false beliefs on ToMBench, retention 0.608, quietly break), so a checkpoint with high mean retention can still be unusable for a specific application.

## 核心贡献

- 提出首个**以能力为中心**（ability-centric）的 LLM 压缩评测协议：把 10 个基准映射到四个面向部署的能力维度——程序性推理与约束执行（IFEval/GSM8K/HumanEval）、知识接地与真实性（TruthfulQA/C-Eval/RoleEval）、社会认知与情感理解（ToMBench/EQ-Bench）、人设保真与风格一致性（CharacterEval/PsychoBench）。
- 关键方法论创新是保留**基准原生读出**（benchmark-native readouts）而非合并为单一分数：IFEval 的 prompt 级 vs instruction 级、GSM8K 的 strict vs flexible 匹配、HumanEval 的 greedy pass@1 vs pass@100、TruthfulQA 的 MC1 vs 生成侧 BLEURT、EQ-Bench 的分数 vs 可解析性——这些分解本身就是诊断信号。
- 大规模实证：6 个基座模型、5 类主流后训练压缩方法、35+ 检查点、超过 25,000 道题、1,785 段多轮角色扮演对话、13 个心理测量量表，全部在同一 vLLM 服务栈与同型 GPU（8×RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell，约 750 GPU 小时）上评测。
- 三大发现：(1) 压缩**重新分配**能力画像而非均匀降分；(2) 粗粒度、短程、表面能力比细粒度、长尾、上下文依赖能力更稳健，产生"浅层保留"（shallow retention）——总分尚可而支撑能力已弱化；(3) 量化普遍比剪枝保留更好，2:4 结构化剪枝伤害最大，非结构化剪枝居中。
- 面向部署与算法设计的可操作结论：按应用关键能力（而非平均分）选择压缩方法；为压缩算法提供需保护的脆弱能力靶点（如 ToMBench 的 belief-action-link 子能力）。

## Key Contributions

- First ability-centric (rather than task-centric) evaluation protocol for LLM compression, organizing 10 benchmarks into four deployment-facing ability dimensions: procedural reasoning & constraint execution, knowledge grounding & truthfulness, social cognition & affective understanding, and persona fidelity & stylistic consistency.
- A "benchmark-native readouts" methodology: instead of collapsing each benchmark to one scalar, the paper exploits internal decompositions (prompt-level vs instruction-level IFEval; strict vs flexible GSM8K matching; greedy pass@1 vs pass@100 HumanEval; MC1 vs generation-side BLEURT on TruthfulQA; score vs parseability on EQ-Bench) as diagnostic probes of *which* ability broke.
- A large, controlled empirical study: 35+ checkpoints from 6 open-weight models (Qwen2.5-7/14/32B, Llama-3.1-8/70B, DeepSeek-V2-Lite-16B) under BnB-4bit, AWQ-INT4, GPTQ-INT4, and SparseGPT 50% pruning (2:4 structured and unstructured), spanning 25,000+ questions, 1,785 multi-turn role-play dialogues, and 13 psychometric scales, all served through one vLLM stack on identical hardware (~750 RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell GPU-hours).
- Three findings: compression **redistributes** ability profiles rather than uniformly lowering scores; coarse/surface abilities are more robust than fine-grained, long-tail, context-dependent ones ("shallow retention"); and quantization consistently retains abilities better than pruning, with 2:4 structured sparsity the most damaging and unstructured sparsity in between.
- Actionable guidance for deployers (pick compression by the ability an application critically needs, e.g., GPTQ-INT4 for procedural/knowledge workloads; avoid 2:4 sparsity for fine-grained constraint execution) and for algorithm designers (protect vulnerable ability-linked activations, e.g., ToMBench belief-action links).

## Methodology

The study evaluates six chat/instruction-tuned base models: Qwen2.5-7B/14B/32B, Llama-3.1-8B/70B, and DeepSeek-V2-Lite-16B. The compression panel covers BitsAndBytes 4-bit (applied on the fly from the dense checkpoint), AWQ-INT4 and GPTQ-INT4 (loaded from officially released community checkpoints), and one-shot SparseGPT pruning applied to attention and MLP projection matrices at 50% sparsity, instantiated two ways: 2:4 structured sparsity (two of every four consecutive weights zeroed) and unstructured sparsity (zeros placed anywhere under the same overall ratio). Calibration activations come from yahma/alpaca-cleaned. Distillation is deliberately excluded because it entangles compression with new architectures and supervision.

Ten benchmarks are mapped onto the four ability dimensions: IFEval/GSM8K/HumanEval (procedural), TruthfulQA/C-Eval/RoleEval (knowledge & truthfulness), ToMBench/EQ-Bench (social cognition), CharacterEval/PsychoBench (persona). All higher-is-better readouts are converted to parent-relative retention R = S_comp/S_FP16 (R=1 full retention), computed per model-method pair against its own FP16 parent. PsychoBench is analyzed separately as psychometric profile drift (normalized drift per scale, plus 16P dominant-type stability). Everything runs through a unified vLLM backend on NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell GPUs (Llama-70B with 2-way tensor parallelism), so dense, quantized, and sparse variants share serving conditions. Standard errors are reported for IFEval, GSM8K, HumanEval, TruthfulQA, and C-Eval.

## Results & Highlights

- **GSM8K (flexible match):** Llama-3.1-8B drops from 0.8446 (FP16) to 0.1168 under 2:4 sparsity (−72.8 pts) but only to 0.8006 under GPTQ-INT4 (−4.4 pts); DeepSeek-V2-Lite-16B collapses 0.5929→0.1054 under 2:4. Even Llama-70B falls 0.9469→0.6164 under 2:4 pruning.
- **IFEval (prompt-level strict):** Llama-3.1-8B falls 0.7468→0.3937 under 2:4 sparsity while GPTQ-INT4 costs just 1.7 pts (0.7301); instruction-level retention consistently exceeds prompt-level retention, showing compression breaks whole-response constraint *coordination* before local instruction *recognition*.
- **HumanEval (greedy pass@1):** Llama-3.1-8B falls 0.6951→0.1280 and DeepSeek-V2-Lite 0.5061→0.0549 under 2:4 sparsity; quantization is nearly lossless (Llama-70B BnB-4bit exactly matches FP16 at 0.8171; GPTQ-INT4 reaches 0.8293). Under 2:4 sparsity Llama-70B recovers from sampling pass@1 0.4334 to pass@100 0.6646 — valid programs remain in the distribution even when the first decode fails.
- **Answer-form brittleness under quantization:** Qwen2.5-7B under GPTQ-INT4 keeps GSM8K flexible match at 0.7225 (vs 0.7597 FP16) but strict match halves from 0.3351 to 0.1691 — the answer stays recoverable while canonical formatting breaks.
- **Knowledge dimension:** TruthfulQA MC1 for Llama-3.1-8B drops 0.4039→0.2852 under 2:4 sparsity; C-Eval test accuracy for Qwen2.5-7B falls 0.7931→0.4736 under 2:4 but only to 0.7787–0.7804 under 4-bit quantization (−1.3 to −1.5 pts). Role-grounded long-tail knowledge (RoleEval) degrades more steeply than broad exam knowledge: Qwen2.5-14B RoleEval 0.5913→0.3287 under 2:4, and a positive generation–selection gap (BLEURT up while MC1 down) signals drift toward reference-style output, not improved truthfulness.
- **Social cognition:** 2:4 sparsity retention bottoms out on ToMBench Hinting Task (0.570), Ambiguous Story (0.675), False Belief Task (0.682), and Unexpected Outcome (0.721), while quantization stays at 0.95–1.01 across task families; the most fragile subabilities are second-order beliefs (0.608), location false beliefs (0.612), irony/sarcasm (0.615), and discrepant emotions (0.616), with unstructured sparsity partially recovering them (0.724, 0.768, 0.856, 0.851).
- **EQ-Bench parseability collapse:** DeepSeek-V2-Lite-16B under unstructured sparsity keeps almost no valid emotion-label outputs — parseable answers fall from 169.7 to 108.2 and the benchmark score becomes unavailable; Llama-3.1-8B's score itself drops 65.98→37.60 under 2:4.
- **Persona dimension:** CharacterEval expressive vividness retention is 0.74/0.77 under sparsity vs 0.87/0.88 across all methods; multi-turn consistency falls to 0.77 (sparse) vs 0.89 (all). PsychoBench shows sparse settings drift psychological profiles further than quantization, with DTDD responses shifting toward higher dark-triad traits and 16P dominant-type stability dropping (≈0.88 under sparse settings vs ≈0.91–0.95 under quantization).

## Authors & Credibility

The authors are Jiahui Ning and Fenglei Fan (City University of Hong Kong), Liang Chen (Tongji University), Yuchen Liang (Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute), and Yi Zhang (Sichuan University) — an evaluation/systems-oriented collaboration rather than a frontier-lab team. This is a preprint (arXiv, October 2026). Credibility signals are strong: all checkpoints are evaluated through one vLLM serving stack on identical hardware; standard errors or seed-level variation are reported for the main item-level benchmarks; the appendix provides full per-model tables, an honest audit trail (e.g., explicitly flagging DeepSeek-V2-Lite's missing GPTQ-INT4 checkpoint and keeping auxiliary Trim/GPTQ-INT8 rows out of the main comparison), TruthfulQA 3-shot seed lists, and an estimated ~750 GPU-hour reproduction cost. An anonymized supplementary code package with compression scripts, serving templates, configurations, and processed results is promised.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** The ability-centric framing and, more concretely, the benchmark-native readout decompositions (strict vs flexible matching, pass@1 vs pass@100, MC1 vs BLEURT, score vs parseability) are a genuine step beyond LLMCBench and UniComp, which stay method-centered. However, the headline conclusion "quantization degrades less than pruning" was already well established; the new value is *where* the losses land, not the ranking.
- **Rigor caveats:** The pruning arm is a single algorithm (SparseGPT) at a single 50% sparsity level with one calibration set (alpaca-cleaned). The claim "quantization beats pruning" is really "4-bit quantization beats SparseGPT@50% on Alpaca calibration" — no dose-response over sparsity ratios, no comparison against Wanda or calibration-ablated variants, and no statistical significance testing despite standard errors being available.
- **Asymmetric provenance:** quantized checkpoints are loaded from community releases while sparse models are self-pruned — a heterogeneous-provenance design that confounds who produced the artifact with the compression method.
- **Eval-harness sensitivity:** the Qwen FP16 GSM8K strict-match baselines are already anomalously low (0.3351 strict vs 0.7597 flexible for Qwen2.5-7B — a 42-pt formatting gap before any compression), so the strict/flexible "answer-form brittleness" narrative partly rides on prompt-marker sensitivity of the harness rather than compression alone.
- **Noisy small slices:** above-parent retentions such as knowledge-pretend-play links at 1.622 under 2:4 sparsity sit on small ToMBench subability counts and are likely noise; the paper honestly says these are not improvements, but draws the "redistribution" conclusion partly from exactly such ratios. EQ-Bench has only 60 questions.
- **Missing cost side:** no memory/latency/throughput numbers are reported, so the deployer still cannot complete the ability-vs-cost trade-off from this paper alone.
- The model panel (Qwen2.5, Llama-3.1 generation) is dated relative to 2026 frontier models, and the authors themselves concede family/scale/method effects are not disentangled.

## Strengths

- Unusually broad and internally consistent evaluation: 35+ checkpoints, 10 benchmarks, 25,000+ questions, 1,785 role-play dialogues, 13 psychometric scales, one serving stack, same GPUs.
- The diagnostic decompositions are the real contribution — they turn "score dropped" into "coordination broke before recognition," "answer recoverable but not canonical," and "the model stopped emitting the expected output format," which is exactly what a deployer needs.
- Interpretive honesty: retention above 1.0 is explicitly not read as improvement; the DTDD dark-triad shift is reported as drift, not as a safety claim; exceptions (DeepSeek AWQ, missing GPTQ checkpoint) are surfaced rather than buried.
- Concrete, actionable deployment guidance per ability dimension, plus algorithm-research targets (protect belief-action-link-like activations).

## Limitations

- Behavioral characterization only — no mechanistic analysis of why specific abilities (second-order beliefs, constraint coordination) are fragile.
- Panel confounds family, scale, and compression method (acknowledged); six models and one pruning algorithm at one sparsity ratio limit generality.
- No distillation, quantization-aware training, INT8/FP8 mid-precision points, or efficiency measurements.
- Persona/psychometric conclusions rest on questionnaire simulation (PsychoBench/16P), whose ecological meaning for real deployment is debatable; PsychoBench reporting is representative (DTDD, BFI, ECR-R, LMS, Empathy) rather than exhaustive, and DTDD Narcissism / LMS Overall were unavailable in released files.
- No significance tests; some key readouts (EQ-Bench, ToMBench subabilities) rest on small item counts.

## Relevance

与用户"极端性能优化下的可靠性"研究方向高度相关：本文系统刻画了量化与剪枝这类激进数值优化如何在整体分数基本不变的情况下，让细粒度能力（约束协调、二阶信念、答案规范格式、情感标签输出格式）发生静默退化——这正是低精度计算引入的静默失效模式；其"基准原生读出"方法可直接迁移到压缩/低精度推理栈的正确性审计中。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the quantization-vs-pruning gap persist with stronger pruning (Wanda, learning-based masks), better calibration sets, or sparsity below 50% — i.e., is "pruning is fragile" a property of SparseGPT@50% or of sparsity itself?
- Can per-layer ability probes localize where second-order-belief or constraint-coordination abilities live, enabling targeted mixed-precision or partial-reconstruction repair of exactly those layers?
- Is the Qwen GSM8K strict-match collapse under GPTQ-INT4 (0.3351→0.1691) an interaction with the answer-marker prompt template that a better harness would eliminate, and does that re-rank quantization methods?
- Do the fragile-ability findings transfer to 2026-era models and to FP8/INT8 formats on Blackwell-native serving stacks?
- Could a compact ability-probe suite (a few hundred items per dimension) serve as a cheap CI gate for community-released compressed checkpoints, catching "shallow retention" before deployment?
