> **一句话总结：** 通过预注册的合成探针（PersistGym-lite）与对 ARC-AGI-3 官方 Standard→Provider Adapter 差距的逐组件分解，本文证明：截断上下文窗口只在"关键事实无法被重新观察"时才摧毁成功率（gpt-6-luna 从 200/200 跌到 15/200，两种惩罚相差 92.5 个百分点，claude-haiku-5-5 复现出 +101 点），而在 ARC-AGI-3 上，把"丢弃旧轮次"换成"摘要压缩+笔记提示"即可在没有原生推理回放的情况下挽回 86% 的 62.6 分差距——不过"回放占 57%"这一份额建立在每格单次运行之上，相当脆弱。

> **TL;DR:** A pre-registered study asks when an agent harness's context policy (drop / summarize / replay turns) decides success rather than just cost. In the synthetic PersistGym-lite probe, a 24k-token truncation window drops gpt-6-luna from 200/200 (full history) to 15/200 (7.5%, chance = 10%) when a needed fact cannot be re-observed, but costs nothing in success (200/200, via 201 lookups in 200 episodes) when it can — a 92.5-point contrast (90% CI [89.5, 95.5]), replicated at +101.0 points on claude-haiku-5-5. On ARC-AGI-3 (Gemini 3.8 Flash, 4 games), replacing the Standard harness's drop-oldest policy with summarization plus its note-taking instruction recovers 86% of the 62.59-point RHAE gap to the Provider Adapter (9.24 → 63.08 vs 71.83) without native reasoning replay — but the replay share (57%) hinges on a single replaced run per cell, and run-to-run variance is of the same order as the effects.

## 核心贡献

- **分离"事实丢失"与"事实可重新观察"**：设计合成探针 PersistGym-lite，将"截断上下文是否伤害成功率"分解为两个条件——所需事实能否再次查到。预注册结果表明：窗口截断在事实可恢复时只付出额外查询成本（成功率不变），在事实不可恢复时直接把成功率打到随机水平（7.5% vs 10% 随机，两惩罚相差 92.5 个百分点）。
- **跨厂商复现**：在第二个厂商的模型 claude-haiku-5-5 上预注册复现出同样的对比（ΔH1 = +101.0 点）；且该模型在事实丢失后宁可不作答（0/200）也不乱猜，与 gpt-6-luna 的接近随机猜测行为（387/400 次仍选答案，仅 31 次正确）形成鲜明差异。
- **ARC-AGI-3 差距的逐组件分解**：首次将基准自家 Standard 与 Provider Adapter 线束的差距按"丢弃 vs 摘要的丢失策略"、"笔记提示"、"原生推理回放"逐项分解（S0→S1→S2→S4）。发现摘要压缩+笔记提示（S1，均值 63.08）即可挽回 86% 的 62.59 分差距，无需厂商专属的推理回放。
- **反直觉的成本发现**：截断窗口美元成本反而更高（2.9–4.0 倍），尽管发送 token 更少（0.84–0.90 倍）——因为丢弃旧轮次破坏了提示缓存（全历史 94% 输入 token 命中缓存 vs 窗口 48%，缓存价格仅为未缓存的 1/10）。
- **罕见的透明度**：预注册（PREREG.md v1.0，2026-10-01）、逐条带日期的修正案（R1–R8）、决策日志、公开代码仓库与 750.11 美元成本台账；并主动报告对自身结论脆弱性的分析。

## Key Contributions

- **Isolates re-observability as the decisive variable.** PersistGym-lite factorially crosses whether a needed fact is observed or derived, recoverable or not, and near or far relative to a truncated window. Truncation costs success only when the fact cannot be re-observed: gpt-6-luna falls from 200/200 (full history) to 15/200 (7.5%, chance = 10%) in the not-recoverable far cell, while scoring 200/200 in the recoverable cell by simply looking the fact up (201 lookups across 200 episodes). The two penalties differ by ΔH1 = +92.5 percentage points (90% CI [89.5, 95.5]), confirmed after Holm correction with agreeing model checks (Firth coefficient +7.09; Bayesian posterior median 5.25, 90% interval [2.86, 7.52]).
- **Pre-registered cross-provider replication.** On claude-haiku-5-5 (medium effort, adaptive thinking), the same design yields ΔH1 = +101.0 points (90% CI [100.0, 102.5]): 0/200 vs 200/200 under the window. Notably, once the fact was lost this model chose no answer rather than guessing, whereas gpt-6-luna guessed near chance (right in 31 of 387 answers, 7.75%).
- **First component-by-component decomposition of ARC-AGI-3's own Standard-to-Adapter gap.** Prior reports (OpenAI's "two settings" post; VISTA) gave only combined effects. Here, for Gemini 3.8 Flash at high thinking on four games, each step changes one component: S0 (Standard, drop-oldest + notes instruction) 9.24 mean RHAE → S1 (summarize, keep notes) 63.08 → S2 (drop notes) 36.32 → S4 (Provider Adapter, + native reasoning replay) 71.83. Shares of the G = 62.59 gap: σ_loss = 0.860, σ_notes = −0.427, σ_replay = 0.567. Provider-neutral summarization with notes recovers 86% of the gap without replay.
- **The dollar cost of truncation is inverted by cache breaking.** The window harness cost 2.9–4.0× full history in dollars while sending only 0.84–0.90× the tokens (gpt-6-luna; 3.3–3.7× on claude-haiku-5-5), because dropped turns defeat prompt caching (94% cached tokens for full history vs 48% for the window).
- **Exceptional transparency.** Pre-registration with dated amendments (R1–R8), a decisions log, both pre- and post-fix analysis labels reported, a public repo (github.com/raimondasl/persistgym) where committed scripts re-derive every number, and a $750.11 cost ledger.

## Methodology

The paper runs two complementary designs, both pre-registered (PREREG.md v1.0, 2026-10-01) with deviations logged.

**PersistGym-lite (controlled probe).** Episodes are generated so that a fact needed at a late "need step" (10-way multiple choice, so chance = 10%) is either shown directly ("observed") or derived from clues, is either recoverable (a lookup action re-shows it at the cost of a step) or not, and lies inside or outside a truncated window at the need step — 8 task cells, T = 200 templates, one episode per cell per template. Harness cells: F-0 (full history), W-0 (24,000-token sliding window), W-n-0 (window + the ARC-AGI-3 Standard harness's one-sentence note-taking instruction: "Include any context you want to carry forward in your reply, along with the action you want to take."), Σ-n-0 (model-written summaries replacing dropped turns + notes; exploratory), plus a single-prompt flattened control (H3) and a restated-at-need ceiling control. Confirmatory model: gpt-6-luna (low-cost reasoning model, medium effort, Flex tier). Inference: one-sided template-cluster bootstrap (10,000 resamples) with Holm correction across H1/H3, plus logistic model checks with template fixed effects (Firth's penalized fit for separated cells). A pre-registered validity gate passed: 160/160 correct with the fact restated at need, 19/160 (11.9%, gate 20%) when withheld; a programmatic oracle scored 100%. Amendment R8 added a replication on claude-haiku-5-5, played episode-for-episode on the same templates, reported outside the Holm family with unadjusted p-values.

**ARC-AGI-3 gap decomposition.** Using the benchmark's own harness (upstream commit 4ca00e6, 2026-09-21, plus 18 patches), Gemini 3.8 Flash at high thinking ran in four configurations: S0 = Standard (visible text only, notes instruction, drop oldest past 175k characters); S1 = S0 but summarize instead of drop (trigger 175k provider tokens); S2 = S1 minus the notes instruction; S4 = Provider Adapter (native replay of reasoning items with thought signatures, summaries, no notes). Each step isolates one component. Four games (FT09, SB26, R11L, CD82) fixed by a registered selection rule (published gap ≥ 20 points, per-run cost ≤ $60, normal ending), one counted run per configuration and game under the 12-hour clock. Shares are step changes in mean RHAE (Relative Human Action Efficiency, 0–100) divided by the gap. A third analysis (H5) compares P(clear | reached) at early (levels 1–2) vs deep (4+) levels, on the benchmark's published runs (25 games) and the authors' own, via logistic models with game fixed effects.

## Results & Highlights

- **H1 (probe, gpt-6-luna, confirmatory):** With a 24k-token window, success in the observed-fact far cell was 15/200 (7.5%, chance 10%) when the fact could NOT be re-observed vs 200/200 (100%) when it could — ΔH1 = +92.5 percentage points (90% CI [89.5, 95.5]); full history scored 200/200 in both. All four registered predictions held (manipulation penalty 92.5 pts vs bar 25; zero recoverable penalty; 201 lookups under the window vs 0 under full history; need-phase tokens 1.31× full history's, 45,875 vs 35,088 per episode, and 1.99× vs near episodes, 90% CI [1.97, 2.01], registered bar 1.30).
- **Replication (claude-haiku-5-5):** window success 0/200 (not recoverable; the model chose no answer rather than guess) vs 200/200 (recoverable), ΔH1 = +101.0 points (90% CI [100.0, 102.5], unadjusted p < 0.0001; Firth window-by-recoverability coefficient +8.55). H3 replication: 399/400 vs 400/400 (Δ = −0.25 pts).
- **Truncation is more expensive in dollars despite fewer tokens:** the window cost 2.9–4.0× full history per episode (4.0× in the headline cell, 95% CI [3.95, 4.03]) at 0.84–0.90× the tokens, because cache hit rates collapsed (94% full history vs 48% window; cached input priced at 1/10). On Anthropic the ratio was 3.3–4.0×→3.3–3.7× at 0.84–0.90× tokens (rewritten prompts billed as cache writes at 1.25× input price).
- **Note-taking partially rescues, summaries help derived facts more:** W-n-0 scored 28.5% vs W-0's 7.5% in the not-recoverable far cell (notes held the fact in 21.5% of episodes, used every time when present); exploratory Σ-n-0 succeeded on 92% of derived-fact vs 43% of observed-fact episodes, with the fact retained in 92% vs 38.5%.
- **ARC-AGI-3 decomposition (H4, Gemini 3.8 Flash, 4 games, one counted run per cell):** mean RHAE S0 9.24 → S1 63.08 → S2 36.32 → S4 71.83; gap G = 62.59 (90% bootstrap interval [29.51, 87.44]); shares σ_loss = 0.860, σ_notes = −0.427, σ_replay = 0.567. Summarize-instead-of-drop with the notes instruction recovers 86% of the gap without native replay; prediction B (replay ≥ 1/2) holds on point estimates under amendment R7, prediction A does not.
- **The replay share is fragile:** under the pre-R7 frozen rules, FT09 S2 counts 71.43 instead of 18.06, S2's mean moves 36.32 → 49.67, σ_replay falls 0.567 → 0.354, and neither prediction holds; excluding FT09 gives σ_replay = 0.357, excluding CD82 0.445. Run-to-run variance is huge: FT09 S0's two runs (both ending on the 12-hour clock) scored 58.17 and 18.11 — 40 points apart.
- **H5 (clear rates by depth):** on the benchmark's published runs, the Adapter's advantage in P(clear | reached) is +0.14 early (Standard 39/47, Adapter 47/50) vs +0.44 deep (Standard 15/26, Adapter 63/75), difference C = +0.30 (90% interval [0.16, 0.46]); on the authors' four games, deep-level clears were S0 0/2 vs S4 11/11 (C = +0.75). The authors note this largely restates where runs stopped.
- **Exploratory compaction signal:** summary-based configs compact every 6.3 (S1), 6.9 (S2), 5.6 (S4) steps; unusable replies (empty text / unoffered tool calls) occurred 22× in S1 and 17× in S2 but never in S4 (737 steps); in S1 they were more frequent within 5 steps after compaction (3.4% vs 1.3% of calls) — reported as a lead, not a finding.

## Authors & Credibility

Single-author work by Raimondas Lencevicius of Microsoft (explicitly in a personal capacity, not Microsoft's views). Credibility is unusually high for an empirical LLM-agent paper: hypotheses, thresholds, and analysis code were frozen before the first paid run; every deviation (R1–R8) is listed with date, reason, and effect; analysis bugs found after results were read are disclosed with both the original "inconclusive" labels and the corrected ones; every number is re-derived by committed scripts from a public repository with a $750.11 cost ledger. The paper proactively narrates its own weaknesses ("How firm this is. Not very, and we say so"), which is closer to registered-report norms in experimental psychology than to typical arXiv agent papers. The author discloses that code and drafts were produced with an AI coding assistant (Claude Code) under their direction.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** Genuine on two fronts. No prior work separates "the fact was lost" from "the fact can be re-observed" as the moderator of truncation damage (LOCA-bench, the Fan et al. harness study, and the ALFWorld eviction result of Mehta & Datta conflate them), and no prior work decomposes ARC-AGI-3's own Standard→Adapter gap — OpenAI reported only the combined effect of retained reasoning + compaction, and VISTA added components to its own visual harness rather than the benchmark's.
- **Rigor:** The probe is pre-registered, Holm-corrected, model-checked, replicated cross-provider, and passed a validity gate — strong. The ARC decomposition is much weaker: one counted run per configuration per game, with run-to-run variance of the same order as the effects (FT09 S0: 58.17 vs 18.11 across two runs), only 35 distinct bootstrap resamples with 4 games, and a headline replay share (57%) that flips to 35% depending on which of FT09 S2's two runs counts.
- **Specific red flags:** Amendment R7 — which raised the S1/S2 summary budget from 8,192 to 65,536 tokens and granted FT09 S2 a replacement run — was adopted after that run's levels had been seen, and it is load-bearing for the paper's headline replay share; the mixed budgets also confound the S1→S2 "notes" step in three games (the step behind σ_notes changes the budget too). The games were selected for a large published gap, so the shares describe games where the Adapter is known to help. Probe power fell below the registered target after the cost-driven cap (0.65 for H1, 0.78 for H3 vs 0.80; the power rule would have set T = 290, not 200). H2 (replay in the probe) stopped, so replay is never tested in the controlled setting. H5's contrast largely restates where runs stopped — at most one reached-but-uncleared level exists per run — and the published runs were effectively seen before the analysis was written.
- **Reproducibility:** Excellent on the probe side (public repo, committed scripts, cost ledger), though raw model transcripts are kept out of the repository, and the ARC side depends on a provider model with no dated snapshot run on different days.
- **Significance:** The 92.5/101-point re-observability contrast is a clean conceptual result harness designers should internalize (distinguish recoverable from unrecoverable state; make unrecoverable state persistent), and the cache-breaking cost inversion is an under-appreciated practical finding. The ARC decomposition is directionally useful (summarize-vs-drop dominates; notes matter more than expected) but the replay-vs-notes split should be read as hypothesis-generating.

## Strengths

- Clean factorial design that isolates exactly one conceptual variable (re-observability), with registered manipulation checks, ceiling controls, and a programmatic oracle.
- Cross-provider replication showing the same contrast, plus a meaningful behavioral difference (abstain vs guess) between the two models.
- Decomposes a documented, publicly visible gap (ARC-AGI-3 Standard vs Provider Adapter) using the benchmark's own harness rather than a new bespoke agent.
- Practical, quantified cost analysis: truncation can be 2.9–4.0× more expensive in dollars while sending fewer tokens, purely via cache invalidation.
- Exemplary transparency: pre-registration, dated amendments, both-labels reporting, open code, cost ledger, and frank self-criticism of every fragile number.

## Limitations

- The ARC decomposition rests on one counted run per configuration and game; run-to-run variance is comparable to the effects, and the replay share hinges on a single replaced run (FT09 S2 under R7).
- The replay step bundles native reasoning replay with the provider's item format and (in some games) a summary-budget change, so replay itself is never isolated — and the probe's replay hypothesis (H2) was stopped.
- One model per provider; agreement across providers cannot be attributed to any single factor and says nothing about other models or real tasks.
- The probe is synthetic; the not-recoverable window penalty is partly by construction (a registered manipulation check — the finding is the contrast with the recoverable case, as the authors carefully note).
- The four ARC games were selected for a large published gap; H3 and H5 sit at ceiling or restate stop points; probe power fell below the registered target; costs are provider- and period-specific (prices of 2026-09-30 / 2026-10-17).

## Relevance

直接命中"Agent & Pipeline Silent Failures"方向：agent harness（上下文管理策略）在模型与外部世界之间静默地改变了模型跨轮次能看到的内容（丢弃、摘要、回放旧轮次），并由此而非模型能力本身决定任务成败——本文量化了这类静默失败的触发条件（不可重新观察的状态被丢弃）并评估了检测/修复手段（笔记指令、摘要压缩、推理回放）。对长时程自主 agent 系统的可靠性设计有直接指导意义。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the re-observability contrast transfer to real tasks where state can be externalized into files, code, or tool outputs (e.g., SWE-bench-style coding agents), or does externalization eliminate the not-recoverable case entirely?
- What is the replay share with multiple runs per ARC cell (or a cheaper probe analog of native replay, since H2 stopped), separating configuration effects from the ~40-point run-to-run variance observed on FT09 S0?
- Could a provider-neutral "unrecoverable-fact ledger" (explicitly tracking derived state that cannot be re-read) recover the remaining 14% of the Standard→Adapter gap without native reasoning replay?
- Why do model-written summaries preserve derived facts (92% retained, 92% success) far better than observed facts (38.5% retained, 43% success) — is re-derivation cheaper for the model than verbatim retention?
- Is the deep-level clear-rate advantage a compounding state-loss effect, or an artifact of ordered level play with near-ceiling early rates (a constant advantage compressed by early ceilings)?
- How does the cache-breaking cost inversion scale with providers whose cache pricing differs (e.g., 1.25× write pricing vs 0.1× cached pricing), and does summarization preserve cache better than truncation in practice?
