> **一句话总结：** 该工作在模型生成答案之后、被追问来源之前，对 KV cache 做因果干预：增删文档的缓存条目在四个模型（7B–32B）上都不改变"来源报告"，而在问答 token 完全相同的运行之间移植问答（Q+A）缓存能把报告 100% 移植过去；沿拟合方向操控 Q+A values 可翻转 98.9–100% 的报告（随机方向 ≤1.1%），且仅移植/固定 K+V 在拟合方向上的投影即可近似复现或阻断该效应——说明来源报告因果地依赖于答题过程中写入缓存的"来源痕迹"，而非当时仍可读的文档。

> **TL;DR:** When asked "did you use the document or your own knowledge?", LLMs' source reports are causally driven by cached state from answering, not by re-reading the document. Across OLMo-3-7B/32B and Qwen3-8B/32B on a templated factual task (155 facts, 8 relations) plus a DynamicQA Wikipedia-paragraph replication, dropping or swapping the document's KV entries yields 0% report flips on all four models, while Q+A cache transplants flip 100% of eligible reports (97.2–100% on DynamicQA); on the two smaller models, steering Q+A values along a fitted true-vs-irrelevant direction reverses 98.9–100% of reports at α=3 vs ≤1.1% for norm-matched random directions, and clamping just the K+V projections onto fitted directions blocks transfer (100%→1.1%/0%) while transferring those projections alone reproduces it (97.9%/100%). The same direction applied during generation switches the actual answer source in 51–85% of cases against opposing instructions. The paper explicitly does not claim general reporting accuracy or a full interpretation of this state.

## 核心贡献

- 提出并系统检验了"来源报告"（source report，即模型回答"我用的是文档还是自身知识"）的三种机制假设：H1 重新读文档、H2 用参数知识判断答案、H3 读取答题时写入缓存的"来源痕迹"（source trace）。干预证据系统性地否定 H1、H2 作为充分解释，支持 H3。
- 方法上的关键创新：在答案生成之后、来源问题提出之前编辑 KV cache，从而把"模型看到了什么文本"与"处理这些文本产生的缓存状态"解耦——同一份可见文本可以携带不同处理历史的缓存状态。
- 发现文档缓存完全不影响报告：删除或替换文档条目在四个模型上均产生 0% 的报告翻转，且模型事后仍能逐字引用新文档（100% 引用成功率），证明报告不追踪当前文档是否支持答案。
- 发现 Q+A 缓存移植以 100% 的成功率单向转移报告（所有四个模型、四种供体-受体组合），且仅靠 K+V 在拟合方向上的投影即可近乎复现（97.9–100%）或阻断（降至 0–1.1%）该转移效应。
- 将机制发现与行为连通：同一拟合的 value 方向在答案生成期间施加时，能在与指令对抗的条件下切换实际答案来源（51–85%），把"来源相关缓存状态"与"来源选择"联系起来。

## Key Contributions

- Formalizes the "source report" question (does the model attribute its answer to context or parametric knowledge?) and cleanly separates it from attribution evaluation (whether context supports the answer) and explanation faithfulness, then tests three mechanistic hypotheses (H1 re-read document, H2 judge answer against parametric knowledge, H3 read a cached trace) with sequential interventions.
- Introduces a post-answer, pre-question KV-cache intervention paradigm: edit the cache after the answer is greedily generated but before the source question, so token identities, positions, and the answer string stay fixed while cached state varies — isolating what the report actually reads.
- Shows the report ignores the document at report time: dropping the counterfactual document, replacing it with an irrelevant fact, or inserting a true fact supporting the answer produces 0% flips on OLMo-3-7B, Qwen3-8B, OLMo-3.1-32B, and Qwen3-32B, even though the model can quote the replacement document in 100% of runs.
- Establishes causal dependence on the Q+A cache: joint question+answer cache transplants between runs with identical question and answer tokens flip 100% of eligible reports on all four models; controls (re-splicing a run's own cache, same-condition donors) give 0%.
- Localizes the effect to fitted linear directions: steering Q+A values along a true-fact-minus-irrelevant mean-difference direction (fitted on held-out relation splits, before any source question) flips 98.9–100% of reports at α=3 across four document conditions, with random norm-matched directions at ≤1.1%; clamping K+V projections onto these directions reduces transplant flips from 100% to 1.1% (OLMo) / 0% (Qwen), while imposing donor projections alone flips 97.9% / 100% — near necessity and sufficiency.
- Demonstrates the same value direction, applied during answer generation without refitting, switches which source supplies the answer (70.9% OLMo / 51.2% Qwen toward memory; 85.3% / 72.8% toward the document) even when the instruction opposes the edit.

## Methodology

The task uses 155 candidate subject–relation–object facts across 8 relations (capital, continent, official language, chemical symbol, author, painter, composer, inventor). Each fact has seven one-sentence document variants — a true fact, two counterfactuals (other attested values of the same relation, chosen deterministically), two irrelevant facts about other subjects, and two neutral sentences — token-length-matched per item under each model's tokenizer so that later tokens occupy identical positions and cache entries can be exchanged without padding. A dialogue presents the document and the question; the model answers greedily; a fixed source question then asks for a one-word "context" or "knowledge" report, scored as the label with higher summed token log-probability (this readout matches the model's freely generated reply in 99.6–100% of cases). Runs are filtered to "eligible" ones whose answer and intact report match the expected pattern for the document type (eligibility 92–100%, except OLMo-3-7B irrelevant-fact runs at 81–82%, mainly refusals), so any post-edit change is attributable to the edit.

Interventions happen after answer generation and before the source question. Spans are defined as the document, the question (Q, including template tokens), and the generated answer (A). Edits are (1) drops of document cache entries and (2) swaps (transplants) that replace a span's keys and values with those from another run of the same fact that saw a different document; unedited entries are kept, not recomputed. For steering, per-layer directions are fit as the mean difference of position-averaged value vectors between supporting-fact and irrelevant-fact runs on DEV relations, then evaluated on held-out relations (e.g., fit on {capital, continent, language, symbol}, test on the rest); at strength α, values are shifted as V ± αd, with norm-matched random directions averaged over three draws as controls. Section 6 decomposes the transplant: a clamp copies the donor K+V but restores the recipient's projections onto fitted directions (values via dot-product replacement, keys via a norm-preserving rotation in pre-RoPE space with ~2×10⁻⁷ round-trip error); the reverse operation imposes donor projections on the recipient's cache. Energy-matched control subspaces (3–186 leading principal directions capturing at least the selected direction's squared difference energy) rule out that the effect is merely about removing a large perturbation. Section 7 moves the steering earlier — applying the value direction to question tokens and each generated answer token while the model is answering — with instructions always opposing the edit, and reads out which value ("Tokyo" from the document vs "Paris" from memory) the answer contains. Appendix E replicates document edits and transplants on a length-matched Wikipedia-paragraph adaptation of DynamicQA.

## Results & Highlights

- Document edits never flip the report: on the templated task, drop-counterfactual, counterfactual→irrelevant, and irrelevant→true-fact edits yield 0% report flips on all four models (OLMo-3-7B, Qwen3-8B, OLMo-3.1-32B, Qwen3-32B), while the model can still quote the newly inserted document in 100% of eligible runs — vs the 100% flip rate achieved by Q+A transplants, the paper's central contrast.
- Q+A transplants transfer the report completely: 100% of eligible reports follow the donor on all four models across all four donor-recipient conditions (e.g., neutral ← true-fact becomes "context" despite the recipient's neutral document; true-fact ← neutral becomes "knowledge" despite supporting evidence remaining in context); re-splicing controls give 0%.
- Steering reverses reports nearly perfectly: adding the fitted direction turns irrelevant-document runs into "context" reporters at 100% on both 7–8B models at α=2; subtracting removes "context" from counterfactual runs at 100% by α=3; across all four document conditions at α=3, flips are 100% except OLMo true facts at 98.9%, versus ≤1.1% for norm-matched random directions (0% on all Qwen cells) — a >85× specificity gap over the random baseline.
- Projection clamps localize the carrier: restoring recipient K+V projections after a full Q+A transplant drops flips from 100% to 1.1% (OLMo-3-7B) and 0% (Qwen3-8B); imposing donor projections alone (no full transplant) flips 97.9% and 100% respectively, while an energy-matched control subspace clamp leaves 100% / 94.7% flipped — i.e., the specific 1-D directions, not perturbation magnitude, carry the effect.
- Answer repetition and document quotation survive steering: at the operating points (irrelevant-create α=2, counterfactual-remove α=3), 100% of evaluations on both small models simultaneously flip the report, repeat the original answer verbatim, quote the full document, and (for counterfactuals) retrieve the false value — the edit changes the report, not the cached content readouts.
- Steering during generation changes the chosen source: at α=2 against opposing instructions, value steering toward memory switches 70.9% (OLMo) / 51.2% (Qwen) of answers, toward the document 85.3% / 72.8%, versus random-direction switches of at most 4.1% (one exception: 23.6% random on OLMo toward context, where the fitted direction is still 3.6× stronger); at most 9.3% of answers are damaged.
- DynamicQA replication: document deletion/replacement retains context reports in 98.4–100% of eligible items across model–edit cells; joint Q+A transplants achieve 97.2–100% donor-following across the 16 model–direction cells (e.g., Q+A 100% on OLMo-3-7B true-fact ← uninformative vs Q-only 97.6% and A-only 10.6% on the same subset).
- Span decomposition: answer-only transplants flip just 0–3% of reports on three of four models (question-only: 75–100%), and question-only swaps are strikingly asymmetric at 32B (counterfactual←irrelevant flips 14.2% on Qwen3-32B; irrelevant←counterfactual only 6.7% on OLMo-3.1-32B) — the asymmetry that motivated the joint Q+A design.

## Authors & Credibility

Five authors (Afendulev, Dontsov, Chepurova, Tutubalina, Korznikov); the preprint prints no institutional affiliations on page 1, though the team's framing, tooling and citation graph (KV-cache steering, activation patching, SAE-based knowledge-conflict steering, ContextCite, introspection work) sit squarely in the mechanistic-interpretability community. Credibility signals are unusually strong for a preprint: eligibility rules were fixed before experiments; the forced-choice readout is validated against free generation (99.6–100% agreement); every headline number comes with a matched control (re-splice, same-condition donor, norm-matched random directions averaged over three draws, energy-matched PCA subspaces); Table 16 maps every section to a named script and result artifact (e.g., data/report_steering_checks.json, data/transplant_clamp_results.json), and a reproducibility statement plus an AI-use statement are included. The claims are carefully scoped — the abstract's final sentence explicitly disclaims general reporting accuracy and complete interpretation.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** Good, not groundbreaking. Post-hoc self-attribution is probed with a genuinely new lever (post-answer KV-cache edits that hold all visible text fixed — the "identical-text swap" where question and answer tokens are swapped between runs yet 100% of reports flip is elegant), but the toolkit (activation patching, mean-difference steering, KV-cache steering) and the phenomenon neighborhood (knowledge conflicts, source selection via SAEs in SpARE, ContextCite attribution, Lindsey's introspection experiments) are well established; the contribution is a clean causal decomposition rather than a new method.
- **Rigor:** High on controls, but with structural caveats. Steering and projection results cover only OLMo-3-7B and Qwen3-8B — the two 32B models support only the transplant findings, so the paper's most mechanistic claims (linear directions, projection clamps) are untested at the scale where question-only transplants already become asymmetric (14.2% and 6.7% cells in Table 7). Report-steering rates pool item–split pairs over two retained relation splits rather than independent facts, inflating the apparent sample size relative to the ~90–155 underlying items. Direction-fitting split 1 was dropped for failing the ten-DEV-pair minimum on both contrasts, a quiet survivorship choice. The fixed source-question wording is a single phrasing; report invariance is shown for it, not across paraphrases.
- **Reproducibility:** Strong on paper — scripts, artifacts, prompts and fitting protocols are enumerated per experiment — though no public repository URL appears in the preprint text itself, so "accompanying data" is currently a promise rather than a verifiable release.
- **Significance:** The dissociation is conceptually important for RAG trust: a model can faithfully quote a document it did not use to decide its source report, so source self-reports are not verifiable by checking context at report time. But the ecological validity is limited: one-sentence templated facts with token-length-matched variants are far from multi-passage RAG, and the DynamicQA replication covers only edits and transplants, not steering.
- **Red flags / tensions:** The "trace of answering" (H3) framing overstates the answer's role in the trace — on three of four models answer-only transplants flip 0–3% of reports (Table 10), so the source information is largely written while processing the question+document, not while generating the answer; the paper concedes this in limitations but the headline framing leans on "state produced while answering." The fitting contrast (true-fact vs irrelevant) conflates evidence availability with actual source use, which the authors admit does not yield a pure "use" signal. Behavioural preservation checks (answer repetition, document read-back) were not run for the joint K+V clamp conditions, so near-perfect necessity/sufficiency is established only for the report readout, not for other readouts. The 100%-everywhere transplant table is conditional on eligibility filtering (92–100%, and 81–82% on OLMo irrelevant runs), which excludes exactly the runs where the mechanism might be weakest. Residual-stream vs value steering flips direction between models (Qwen: 75.6% residual vs 51.2% values; OLMo: 16.3% vs 70.9%), showing the intervention story is site- and model-dependent in ways the main text underplays.

## Strengths

- A genuinely clean causal design: holding question, answer, document, and position indices fixed while swapping only cached state is one of the most convincing dissociations between "what the model can see" and "what the model consults" in the self-report literature.
- Exhaustive, well-matched controls (norm-matched random directions, energy-matched PCA subspaces with 3–186× more dimensions, re-splice and same-condition donor controls) that make the 98.9–100%-vs-≤1.1% specificity claims hard to dismiss.
- Cross-model breadth (four models, two families, 7B–32B) for the transplant/edit findings, plus a benchmark-derived (DynamicQA Wikipedia paragraphs) replication rather than only synthetic templates.
- Behavioral preservation checks (answer repetition, document quotation, counterfactual read-back at the steering operating points) showing the intervention changes the report rather than corrupting the cache.
- Honest scoping throughout: hypotheses tested as sufficient explanations rather than exclusive roles, and explicit disclaimers about what is not established (general accuracy, interpretation of the state, one-dimensional source variable).

## Limitations

- Steering, direction fitting, and projection-clamp experiments are limited to the two 7–8B models; the 32B models appear only in edit/transplant experiments.
- The main task is a controlled templated dataset (155 facts, 8 relations, one-sentence length-matched documents); natural multi-document retrieval, long contexts, and multi-turn dialogue are untested, and general attribution reliability "in natural retrieval settings remains untested."
- The paper cannot distinguish whether the fitted directions encode evidence availability, actual source use, or both (the fixed-document contrast leaves this open), and the directions span layers and Q/A positions rather than defining one global source variable.
- Rates are conditional on pre-registered eligibility filtering; ineligible runs (e.g., OLMo-3-7B irrelevant-fact refusals at 18–19%) are excluded, and one source-question wording is used throughout.
- Joint K+V edits lack answer-repetition/read-back checks; separate key-vs-value and question-vs-answer contributions vary by model and condition and remain mechanistically unexplained.
- No public code/data URL is given in the preprint; reproducibility rests on the authors' accompanying artifacts.

## Relevance

直接命中"LLM Hidden States & Interpretability"方向：通过对 KV cache 的因果干预（patching、方向拟合、投影钳制）研究模型自我归因报告的内部机制，属于机制可解释性与知识冲突交叉的前沿工作。对 RAG 场景下"模型自述来源是否可信"这一问题提供了内部状态层面的因果证据，也与方法论上关注的 activation steering / KV-cache steering 检测与操控紧密相关。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the fitted direction encode evidence availability or actual source use? A contrast built from conditions where the document is used but invisible vs visible-but-unused could separate the two.
- Why is the trace concentrated in the question span (answer-only transplants flip 0–3% on three models)? Layer- and position-resolved patching could localize where the "source trace" is written during document processing.
- Do the 32B asymmetries in question-only transplants (14.2% / 6.7% cells) grow with scale, and does the linear-direction account survive at 70B+ or instruction-tuned frontier models?
- Can the direction be repurposed as a runtime monitor — e.g., detecting when a RAG system's citation/self-attribution diverges from its actual context dependence?
- How does this interact with known knowledge-conflict steering (SpARE's SAE features)? Are the KV-value directions and SAE source features the same subspace?
- Does report invariance hold across source-question paraphrases, languages, and adversarial phrasings ("are you sure you used the document?"), where verbal self-reports are known to shift?
