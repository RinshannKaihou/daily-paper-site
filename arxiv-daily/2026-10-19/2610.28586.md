> **一句话总结：** 本文发现 agentic on-policy distillation 失败的真正原因不是复合误差导致学生"漂出"教师能力区，而是教师逐 token 打分看不到回合结局（rollout–scorer gap）从而放任学生拖延；提出的 R2S 方法用教师接管轨迹（无需任务奖励）离线修复教师的打分分布，再冻结为唯一打分器做标准 OPD，在 12 个"教师-学生-环境"组合中全部胜过 TCOD-F2B、Guided-OPD、FutureBridge，平均领先每个组合最强基线 12.8 个成功率点。

> **TL;DR:** The paper shows that in agentic on-policy distillation (OPD) the teacher can still finish tasks from the very states where the student drifts most, but its per-token scores cannot penalize the student's delaying moves because scoring never sees the episode's end (the "rollout–scorer gap"). R2S repairs the teacher reward-free — mine the untrained student's highest-disagreement states, run 8 teacher takeovers each, keep states where enough takeovers pass a consensus/LLM-judge check, and fine-tune the teacher to reproduce its takeover-informed token scores without the takeover in context — then freezes it as the sole scorer for standard OPD. On ALFWorld/WebShop/ScienceWorld across 2 teachers and 4 students (Qwen3.5-9B→2B/4B, Qwen3-30B-A3B→4B/8B), R2S beats vanilla OPD, TCOD-F2B, Guided-OPD, and FutureBridge in all 12 cells, averaging +12.8 success points over the best baseline per cell (e.g., 81.9% vs 63.6% for Guided-OPD on ALFWorld with the 9B→4B pair), and cuts out-of-turn episodes roughly in half (WebShop: 27.5% vs 55.9% for vanilla OPD).

## 核心贡献

- **提出并实证了 "rollout–scorer gap"（接管-打分差距）这一新诊断**：在学生偏离教师最严重的高分歧状态上，教师亲自接管仍能完成任务（ALFWorld 44.2% vs 学生 39.9%、ScienceWorld 27.5% vs 18.6% 成功率），说明"复合误差把轨迹带到教师能力区之外"的旧解释不完整；真正问题是教师逐 token 打分是"近视的"——看不到回合结局，因而放过了结果导向 RL 会用零奖励惩罚的安全拖延动作。
- **量化了"有结局在上下文中时打分会反转"**：把教师的一个成功接管放入上下文后，学生话术中 hedging（犹豫）词概率下降 8.2–13.0%、searching（反复搜索）词下降 6.2–16.6%，而其余思考内容只降 3.5–5.0%——即打分器惩罚拖延是"有信息时"天然会做的。
- **提出 R2S（Rollout-to-Scorer Distillation）**：完全无任务奖励的教师修复流程——挖高分歧状态 → 教管接管 + 无奖励校验（共识投票或 LLM 裁判）筛选 → 用"接管在上下文中"得到的知情打分目标 q+ 微调教师副本（去掉接管上下文也能给出同样打分）→ 冻结为标准 OPD 的唯一打分器。学生端训练目标与流程完全不变，学生从未见过接管内容。
- **大规模统一协议评测**：3 个环境 × 2 个教师 × 4 个学生的 12 个组合中，R2S 全部超过三个 SOTA agentic-OPD 方法（TCOD-F2B、Guided-OPD、FutureBridge），平均比各组合最强基线高 12.8 个成功率点，且常超过教师亲自上阵的成绩。
- **自蒸馏也有效**：Qwen3.5-4B 教自己时，先修复 4B 教师（其自身接管、自身筛选，无更大模型参与）得到 72.7/58.1/42.4%，大幅超过未修复 4B 教出的所有基线（最好 50.6/34.0/29.3%），甚至超过 9B 教师配 vanilla OPD 的成绩。

## Key Contributions

- **Identifies and evidences the "rollout–scorer gap."** From the states where the untrained student departs most from the teacher (measured by OPD's own per-token divergence over the student's thinking), teacher takeovers still finish the task and rescue many states the student goes on to lose — so compounding-error/teacher-reach explanations are incomplete. The failure is that next-token scoring is myopic: it never sees where the episode ends, so it lets safe-but-delaying moves pass that outcome-based RL would punish with zero reward.
- **Quantifies the score reversal under privileged context.** With one finishing takeover in context, the teacher's probability of the student's hedging words drops 8.2–13.0% and searching words 6.2–16.6% across environments, two to three times the drop on the rest of the student's thinking (3.5–5.0%) — the judgment exists in the teacher but plain scores never surface it.
- **Proposes R2S, a reward-free teacher-repair pipeline.** (1) Mine divergence turns from the untrained student's episodes (up to 2,400 states, K=8 takeovers each); (2) keep states where enough takeovers pass a reward-free check (consensus among takeovers or an LLM judge) and keep the shortest passing takeover as reference; (3) fine-tune a copy of the teacher so that, without the takeover in context, it reproduces the takeover-informed token distributions q+ (top-16 renormalized, computed once from the frozen teacher) over the full assistant suffix from the divergence turn onward; (4) refreeze the repaired teacher as the sole scorer for standard OPD. No task reward enters repair or training; the student sees no takeover tokens.
- **Comprehensive shared-protocol evaluation.** Across 12 teacher–student–environment cells (Qwen3.5-9B→2B/4B and Qwen3-30B-A3B-Instruct-2507→4B/8B on ALFWorld, WebShop, ScienceWorld; 150-step runs, final checkpoint, 3 decodes, no checkpoint selection), R2S beats all three re-implemented SOTA baselines in every cell, by 12.8 points on average over the per-cell best baseline, and exceeds the teacher acting alone.
- **Self-distillation setting.** Repairing a Qwen3.5-4B teacher on its own takeovers (no larger model in the pipeline) yields 72.7/58.1/42.4% versus ≤50.6/34.0/29.3% for all baselines learning from the unrepaired 4B, and beats the 9B-teacher vanilla-OPD student on all three environments.

## Methodology

R2S is a pre-distillation repair stage bolted in front of vanilla agentic OPD. The OPD objective is the reverse KL between the student's next-token distribution and the teacher's on the student's own histories, estimated at sampled tokens inside a clipped PPO surrogate (per-token advantage = distillation log-ratio; no extra KL or entropy term). The paper's diagnosis first: from the untrained student's episodes, the divergence turn is the eligible turn (thinking ≥3 tokens, ends in a tool call) whose thinking has the largest average log-probability gap to the teacher; from those states the teacher takes over 8 times and its takeovers both succeed more often than the student and rescue many lost states — while the student trained on plain scores keeps running out of turns in a third to over half of held-out episodes. The repair then proceeds in three steps. Privileged scoring: for each kept state, the frozen teacher T0 scores every student token of the episode suffix (divergence turn to end) with the selected shortest takeover inserted as one reference message; these distributions form the targets q+. Closed-book distillation: a full-parameter copy Tϕ (initialized from T0, one epoch, lr 2e-6, batches of 8 states, 10% of goals held out) sees the same suffix without the takeover and minimizes cross-entropy to q+ over every assistant position. Freeze and distill: Tϕ becomes the sole scorer and vanilla OPD runs unchanged. Selection of takeovers needs no task reward — consensus voting among the 8 takeovers or an LLM judge suffices (ground-truth reward exists only as an ablation upper bound). The whole pipeline requires an environment replayable to a given state (all three benchmarks qualify). Evaluation uses success (%) on ALFWorld and ScienceWorld (mean of seen/unseen splits) and WebShop's 500 canonical goals, 30 turns per episode (15 on WebShop), one fixed worked demonstration per environment, reasoning mode off.

## Results & Highlights

- **Sweep of all 12 cells (Table 2).** R2S outperforms vanilla OPD, TCOD-F2B, Guided-OPD, and FutureBridge in every teacher–student–environment cell; the gain over the best of the three SOTA baselines in each cell averages +17.0 points from the 9B teacher and +8.5 from the 30B-A3B teacher (+12.8 overall), and over vanilla OPD +17.8 / +9.8 points respectively.
- **Largest single gains (Qwen3.5-9B→Qwen3.5-4B).** WebShop success 55.9±1.4% (R2S) vs 34.6±2.9% for vanilla OPD and 35.5±1.3% for the best baseline (Guided-OPD); ScienceWorld 47.8±3.0% vs 34.6±3.5% vanilla and 32.2±1.1% best baseline (Guided-OPD); ALFWorld 81.9±2.4% vs 65.8±2.6% vanilla and 63.6±2.2% best baseline (Guided-OPD) — and above the 9B teacher acting alone (63.1% ALFWorld).
- **MoE teacher cells hold up.** Qwen3-30B-A3B→Qwen3-8B: ALFWorld 54.7±1.0% vs 48.8±1.8% (best baseline TCOD-F2B), ScienceWorld 50.5±1.4% vs 37.1±2.0% (FutureBridge), WebShop 30.4±1.2% vs 23.6±1.2% teacher-alone and 23.4±0.7% best baseline. Notably this teacher alone completes almost no WebShop purchase (94% of its episodes run out of turns; it skips the action tool on 13.7 of 15 turns), yet every student — R2S most of all — lands above it.
- **Self-distillation (Table 3).** Qwen3.5-4B teaching itself with a repaired 4B scorer: ALFWorld 72.7±1.3% vs 50.6±1.9% (FutureBridge, best baseline), WebShop 58.1±2.5% vs 34.0±1.8% (Guided-OPD), ScienceWorld 42.4±3.4% vs 29.3±1.8% (Guided-OPD).
- **Reward-free selection works (Table 4, 9B→4B cells).** Consensus vote: 81.9/63.1/39.5% (ALFWorld/WebShop/ScienceWorld); LLM judge: 82.6/55.9/50.3%; both far above vanilla OPD (65.8/34.6/34.6%) and close to the ground-truth upper bound (85.9/65.0/49.0%) — neither reward-free rule dominates everywhere (consensus wins WebShop, the judge wins ScienceWorld).
- **Informed targets matter beyond the takeovers themselves (Table 5).** SFT on the selected takeovers alone (no informed targets) already gives 76.8/46.7/41.0%, well above vanilla OPD; adding the takeover-informed targets lifts to 81.9/55.9/47.8% — the takeovers help most as context for scoring the student's own tokens, not as SFT data. Supervising the suffix beats the divergence turn alone or the prefix on average (61.9 vs 59.9/60.5, Table 6).
- **Mechanistic long-horizon effects (Tables 1, 7, 8).** Takeover success from high-disagreement states beats the student in 11 of 12 cells (e.g., ScienceWorld 27.5% vs 18.6% for the 4B student); the R2S student runs out of turns about half as often as the vanilla student (WebShop 27.5% vs 55.9%; ALFWorld 19.5% vs 34.3%; ScienceWorld 17.1% vs 31.9%) and in fewer turns per episode (14.7 vs 19.0 on ALFWorld); in won episodes it searches less (8.4 vs 10.5 searching turns on ALFWorld, 2.0 vs 3.0 on WebShop) with task turns roughly unchanged — the saved turns are exactly the hedging/searching turns the informed scores mark down.

## Authors & Credibility

Both authors (Shu Zhao, Tan Yu) are at NVIDIA. Shu Zhao's record includes ParallelSearch (RL for query decomposition, arXiv 2025), MoRA (ICLR 2026), KALAHash (AAAI 2025), and SafeMap (ICML 2025) — a consistent line in RL/learning-based methods, lending credibility to the empirical craftsmanship. The evaluation protocol is unusually careful for this subfield: all three competitor baselines were re-run under the authors' shared models, prompts, estimator, and decode protocol using public code (rather than quoting each paper's self-reported numbers); results are mean±std over three decodes of the final checkpoint with no checkpoint selection; and per-split numbers (Table 12) plus a project page with code are promised. The main analysis (Section 3) is run on one teacher–student pair before generalizing, and its counterintuitive claim (teacher takeovers succeed from the student's worst states) is verified across all twelve cells in Appendix B. The paper is a preprint (arXiv 2610.28586v1, 20 Oct 2026, cs.CL) and not yet peer-reviewed.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty: high and well-motivated.** The rollout–scorer gap is a crisp, falsifiable diagnosis that competes with the compounding-error story underlying TCOD-F2B/Guided-OPD/FutureBridge, and repairing the scorer rather than constraining the student is a genuinely different intervention point. The privileged-context score-reversal experiment (Table 1) is a clean piece of evidence.
- **Rigor: strong on protocol, weaker on breadth.** All 12 cells use one 150-step training run per cell (variance comes only from three decodes of the final checkpoint); multi-seed training variance is shown only for the 9B→4B pair (Figure 3). Ablations (Tables 4–6) likewise cover only the 9B→4B cells.
- **Benchmark selection is dated and narrow.** ALFWorld, WebShop, and ScienceWorld are text-game benchmarks from 2021–2022; there is no modern hard agentic suite (e.g., SWE-bench- or τ-bench-style tool use, real web agents), and WebShop is evaluated on only 500 canonical goals. The delay/out-of-turns failure mode these benchmarks amplify is exactly the one R2S fixes — a favorable fit between method and evaluation.
- **One model family.** Teachers and students are all Qwen3/Qwen3.5 (2B–30B-A3B); the authors admit this. Whether the rollout–scorer gap and its repair transfer to other families or much larger teachers is untested.
- **Teacher-alone degeneracy flatters one claim.** "R2S students end above the teacher acting alone" is partly driven by the 30B-A3B teacher's near-total WebShop collapse (94% of episodes out of turns, action tool skipped on 13.7/15 turns) — a broken control in that cell, which the paper does discuss but which softens the headline.
- **Compute and environment assumptions.** Repair costs the untrained student's episodes plus N×K = 19,200 takeover episodes plus one teacher fine-tune before training starts, and takeovers require an environment replayable to a given state — excluding many real-world, non-resettable deployments. Consensus selection with only 8 takeovers may be noisy in low-success settings (WebShop takeovers succeed ~10%), which could explain consensus underperforming the LLM judge there.
- **No red flags on honesty.** Limitations are stated plainly, the vanilla-OPD control is exact (same estimator and recipe, only the scorer swapped), and ground-truth selection is reported as a non-ranked upper bound rather than hidden.

## Strengths

- Reframes the failure of agentic OPD from "student drifts out of teacher reach" to "teacher's scores are myopic," backed by a decisive takeover experiment rather than intuition.
- The repair is reward-free, modular (scorer-side, combinable with student-side curricula like TCOD), and leaves the student's training objective untouched — easy to adopt in existing OPD pipelines.
- Uniform re-implementation of all baselines under one protocol with public code makes the 12/12 sweep unusually trustworthy.
- Rich mechanistic follow-through: out-of-turn rates, turns per episode, searching-vs-task turn decomposition, and score-drop decomposition (hedging/searching vs other thinking) all point the same direction as the diagnosis.
- Self-distillation result (a repaired 4B self-teacher beating the 9B-teacher vanilla student) is a strong practical payoff for small-model deployments.

## Limitations

- Evaluation confined to three classic text-agent benchmarks and the Qwen3/Qwen3.5 family; no real web/tool-use environments, no other model families, no frontier-scale teachers.
- Pre-training compute overhead (19,200 teacher takeover episodes + one full-parameter teacher fine-tune) and the need for a replayable environment limit applicability.
- Main table reports one training run per cell; training-seed robustness is demonstrated only for one teacher–student pair.
- Ablations only in the 9B→4B setting; sensitivity to the number of mined states (2,400), K=8 takeovers, and the consensus threshold m is not deeply explored in the main text.
- The average +12.8 headline mixes very large gains (WebShop 9B cells) with smaller ones (30B→4B ALFWorld: 54.5 vs 48.8, +5.7), so expected gains on already-strong teacher–student pairs are more modest than the mean suggests.

## Relevance

这篇论文直接命中"RL Post-Training Dynamics & Failures"方向中 on-policy distillation 的失败模式主题：它诊断并修复了 agentic OPD 中教师打分信号"近视"这一具体失败机制，且全程无需任务奖励，对无奖励可用的 agent 训练场景有直接实用价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the rollout–scorer gap exist in reasoning-mode (thinking) models, where the scorer already sees long chains of deliberation, and does R2S repair transfer there?
- Can R2S be combined with student-side curricula (TCOD-F2B, Guided-OPD) for additive gains, as the modularity argument suggests — and does the takeover-informed target quality degrade as the student trains and its states move?
- How sensitive is the repair to K (takeovers per state), the consensus threshold m, and the 2,400-state budget in sparse-reward environments where takeover success rates are near 10%?
- Would the repaired scorer also serve as the distillation term in hybrid reward+distillation systems (e.g., SDAR-style) without confounding, as the authors speculate?
- Does the myopia diagnosis generalize to real, non-replayable environments (live web agents) where takeover mining is impossible — can offline datasets or simulated resets substitute?
