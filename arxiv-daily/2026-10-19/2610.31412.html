Key Finding · 关键发现

Separate straight-through estimators for direction and gain make VQ-QAT a Riemannian optimization on the sphere — beating INT-QAT at 2.25 bits per weight.

将 LLM 权重分解为方向（shape）与增益（gain）、分别施加直通估计器（STE），等价于在球面上做黎曼优化；配合分布式精确最近邻分配（12.8× 加速），首次把精确码本 VQ-QAT 扩展到十亿参数模型与百亿 token 训练预算，并在 2.25 bpw 下全面超越 INT-QAT。

Why This Matters · 为何重要

Shows how quantizer structure and gradient-estimator placement jointly govern accuracy loss at ~2-bit compression — a direct reference for reliability under aggressive numerical compression.

Critical Edge · 保持怀疑

E8S has no efficient de-quantization kernel yet — E8P12's custom CUDA path is fixed at 2.0 bpw — so the fractional-bitwidth advantage (1.75–2.25 bpw) is not yet realizable in deployment.

Methodology · 方法概要

Where the STE is inserted is the whole paper

Each 8-d weight block is quantized in polar form, w = γ·s — gain γ = ‖w‖₂, direction s on the unit sphere S⁷ — and the two parts are trained through separate estimators.

  • Shape quantizer — exact nearest-neighbor against a normalized E8-derived spherical codebook. The new E8S codebook enumerates equal-norm E8 lattice shells (240 / 2,160 / 6,720 / 17,520 points…), discards shells collapsing to duplicate directions, and farthest-point-samples the partial shell to hit K = 2^⌊8·bpw⌋ exactly — unlocking fractional rates 1.75–3.0 bpw.
  • Gain quantizer — closed-form chi-quantile scalar quantizer (block norms of near-Gaussian weights follow χ₈); no learned parameters, consumes exactly bγ/d bits per weight.
  • The core move — separate STEs (∂ŝ/∂s ≈ I, ∂γ̂/∂γ ≈ 1) plus exact differentiation through normalization. The surrogate gradient on the direction becomes (γ̂/‖u‖)·(I − ssᵀ)·g — precisely a Riemannian tangent-space step, with renormalization as the retraction. A standard joint STE is provably blind to the shape-gain structure.
  • Systems — fused Triton kernel (normalize + select + reconstruct), quantized-weight caching across gradient-accumulation steps, and data-parallel sharding that syncs integer indices only — exact assignment at 4.9 s/it.
  • Training setup — QAT from PTQ checkpoints (GPTQ + OmniQuant calibration, Hadamard rotations); DCLM-Edu with Jensen–Shannon distillation from a frozen FP16 teacher; batch 256, context 1024, 0.25–10B tokens. Models: LLaMA-3.2-1B/3B-Instruct, Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507, base Qwen3-1.7B. Eval: WikiText2 PPL@4096, CSR, MMLU, GSM8K, IFEval, LongBench-E.
核心贡献 · Key Contributions (中文)

四件事，一件主线

  • 球面化 VQ-QAT 训练规则：方向与增益分别施加 STE，在方向参数上诱导出"切空间步进 + 归一化回缩"的黎曼更新；标准联合 STE 则完全忽略 shape-gain 结构。
  • E8S 球面码本：由 E8 格等范数壳枚举并做最远点采样补齐，支持 1.75–3.0 bpw 分数比特率，突破 E8P12 固定 2.0 bpw 与标量量化的粒度限制。
  • 分布式精确最近邻分配：融合 Triton kernel + 跨梯度累积步的索引缓存 + 仅同步整数索引，把 LLaMA-3.2-1B 训练从 62.5 降到 4.9 s/it（12.8×）。
  • 2.25 bpw 下的 SOTA VQ-QAT 精度：在相同量化器与初始化下稳定优于标准 STE，几乎全面匹敌或超过成熟的标量 INT-QAT（SEQ）。
  • 系统性消融：相同码本 + 相同 PTQ 初始化隔离训练规则贡献，3 个随机种子的非重叠区间结果，以及增益比特分配的 Pareto 分析。

Reading the fine print

  • Advantage shrinks with budget — PPL gap compresses from −0.37 (1B tokens) to −0.13 (5B); at 5B tokens on LLaMA-3.2-3B CSR the standard STE actually wins, 66.8 vs 66.4. The training-rule edge is largest at small budgets.
  • Still behind FP16 — WikiText2 PPL 13.74 vs 12.15 (L1B, 10B tokens); low-budget MMLU on 1B models degrades steeply (40.3 vs FP16's 45.4).
  • vs. QTIP / QuIP# (52M tokens) — sometimes higher perplexity (10.83 vs QTIP's 10.12 on L3B) but stronger downstream accuracy (L3B MMLU 55.5 vs 49.4); QuIP# rows use unspecified training budgets.
Strengths · 优点

What holds up

  • Theory derived, not asserted — the STE-placement ⇒ Riemannian tangent-plus-retraction identification is worked out explicitly (Eqs. 9–12).
  • Controlled ablations — identical quantizer and PTQ initialization across training rules, plus multi-seed significance checks; rare in this literature.
  • Solves the real blocker — exact NN assignment cost attacked with engineering (not approximation), measured at 12.8× end-to-end.
  • Finer compression dial — E8S fractional bitwidths (1.75 / 1.875 / 2.25 bpw) with a gain-bit Pareto analysis.
  • Honest authors — they themselves flag the Qwen3-4B CSR exception, the E8S dequantizer gap, and the 4B/10B-token ceiling.
Limitations · 局限

Where it frays

  • Scale ceiling — evaluation caps at 4B params and 10B tokens; 7B+ models, other architectures, hierarchical higher-bit schemes untested.
  • Not deployable yet — no efficient E8S dequantizer kernel (E8P12's CUDA path is stuck at 2.0 bpw), so fractional bitwidths remain a paper advantage.
  • Wins are localized — at 2.0 bpw, SEQ often overtakes with longer training; the practical edge concentrates at 2.25 bpw and short budgets.
  • Early-optimization effect? — the spherical-vs-standard advantage narrows (occasionally reverses on CSR) as budgets grow.
  • Gap to FP16 persists — PPL above FP16 at every budget (13.74 vs 12.15 on L1B @10B); low-budget MMLU/GSM8K on 1B models degrade steeply.
  • Accounting gaps — no total-training-compute comparison; speedup timed on LLaMA-3.2-1B only.

Questions for Further Exploration · 待探索问题
  1. Does the factored-STE / Riemannian advantage persist at 7B+ scale and 50B+ tokens, or wash out as PTQ initialization matters less relative to budget?
  2. Would VQ-specific stabilization from INT-QAT (learned clipping, elastic step-size schedules) widen the SQuAT-vs-SEQ gap at 2.0 bpw, where SEQ currently catches up?
  3. How much of the gain is tangent-space geometry versus simple gradient re-scaling by γ̂/‖u‖ — is there an ablation against a Euclidean update with matched preconditioning?
  4. Can E8S get a compact structured decoder (Leech-lattice-style arithmetic) to make fractional bitwidths deployable — and what is the inference-time bandwidth win at 1.875 vs 2.25 bpw?
  5. How does shape-gain behave under activation or KV-cache quantization, where the Gaussianity assumption on block norms may fail?
  6. Does index-only distributed assignment extend to learned (AQLM-style) or non-lattice codebooks, where exact NN is far more expensive?
