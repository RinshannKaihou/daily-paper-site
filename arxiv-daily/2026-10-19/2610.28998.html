01 Methodology · 系统方法

Four 8×B200 nodes (1 trainer + 3 inference; 179 GiB GPUs, ~2 TB host RAM, 400 Gb/s EFA) run a closed loop: vLLM 0.31 engines with MTP3 speculative decoding replay real SWE rollouts, and the trainer replays recorded RL steps packed into 131,072-token bins — throughput measured on real data, no generation noise.

  • Engine topology by replay: TP2 exhausts its KV cache past 10.5 sessions/GPU; TP8 slowest at every load; TP4+EP wins (1,091 output tokens/s/GPU at 26 sessions/GPU).
  • Offload path first: PinnedArena exact-size regions cut the Muon step 66–75 s → 12.4 s; GPU-resident expert gradients remove 63 GB of 1.8 GB/s blocking copies per micro step (2.4–2.7×).
  • Memory budget: StreamingMuon at 12 B/param fits ~2 TB host RAM; AdamW's 16 B/param (2,018 GiB) does not.
  • Attribution: controlled 8-layer cumulative ladder on identical batches (±0.3% noise) + leave-one-out on the final stack; memory via PSS (RSS overstates by 43%).
  • Router replay: engines return per-token top-10 experts (960 B/token) — BF16 routing flips explain ~96% of trainer–sampler mismatch.

03 Strengths · 优势 keep
  • Closes a real gap: no open-source RL stack could train this architecture (GDN recurrence, QSA backward, 640-wide MoE, 95 GiB frozen n-gram table) at 131K context on one node — each bottleneck fixed with measured before/after.
  • Transferable ordering finding: torch.compile costs 20% on the unoptimized stack but gains 2% on the final one — host-offload fixes dominate kernel wins until GPUs stop waiting.
  • Engineering-grade telemetry: preemption counts, prefix-hit rates, per-stage memory dips, leave-one-out sensitivity table.
  • Router replay is a clean, quantified correctness fix (96% of trainer–sampler mismatch) — relevant to all BF16 MoE RL.
04 Limitations · 局限 check
  • No accuracy or convergence evidence: 15 steps, no benchmark scores; long-run convergence, final SWE accuracy, multi-node scaling all left open.
  • Hardware- and model-locked: one config (4×8×B200 + EFA), one model; kernels target sm100; economics change elsewhere. No code release stated.
  • Endpoints are development snapshots, not controlled experiments; several baselines are convenience versions rather than tuned ones.
  • Not full-parameter RL: first 2 layers, n-gram table, QSA indexer and MTP head frozen; MTP acceptance-decay risk over long runs unmonitored.
05 Questions for further exploration · 待探索
  1. How much of the recipe — PinnedArena offload, GPU-resident expert gradients, native-size MoK — transfers to H100/A100 clusters without EFA, or to dense / standard-attention models?
  2. Does MTP speculative-decoding acceptance decay over hundreds of steps as the policy drifts from the frozen draft head — and would periodically re-freezing it pay for itself?
  3. What were the reward / pass@1 trajectories of the 15-step run — does the 331 s generation wait justify a fourth inference node versus a faster trainer?
  4. Could router replay be replaced by a lower-overhead trainer-side correction (stochastic rounding, matched-precision router) instead of shipping 960 B/token of expert ids?
  5. Is per-layer activation checkpointing optimal for the 4-branch gated residual, or would multi-layer segments flip at even longer contexts?
