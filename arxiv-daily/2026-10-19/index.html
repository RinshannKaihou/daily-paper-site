Must Read·Self-Evolving Agents·Analysis

Are Self-Evolving Agents Continual Learners?

On four drifting task streams, every self-evolving paradigm hits the plasticity–stability tradeoff: GEPA buys +3.7pp terminal advantage at −4.3pp stability, global self-edits overwrite earlier knowledge, and retrieval collides across tasks. None of them is a continual learner yet.

r = −0.72 plasticity–stability correlation, n=12 points (p=0.008) — GEPA +3.7pp plasticity / −4.3pp stability; ReasoningBank −1.9pp / +3.3pp

By Xiaoyin Chen, Yizheng Huang, Yinglun Xu et al. · Mila / Google DeepMind

4 条漂移任务流上 ReasoningBank / GEPA / Meta-Harness 全部陷入可塑性–稳定性权衡：全局自编辑覆盖旧知识，检索式方法有外域注入与检索冲突。 The first drift-stream evaluation with fixed backbone, disjoint train/eval streams and prompt autopsies — the negative result the field needed.

CaveatStreams use a single fixed block order, and the plasticity–stability correlation rests on n=12 points (r=−0.72, p=0.008) — suggestive rather than conclusive.

Must Read·RL Post-Training·Analysis

ECHO: Common-Mode Hidden Offsets Drive Vocabulary Collapse in Preference Optimization

Ranking scores climb to 0.995 while a common-mode offset b̄ quietly wells probability mass onto a single token.

89%of released probability mass collapses onto one token within 504 steps, while held-out ranking accuracy reaches 0.995 — across 14/17 model lineages

By Yun Li, Ehsan Javanmardi, Simon Thompson et al.

偏好优化在最终隐层累积共模偏移 b̄，排序损失几乎看不见它，投影成“词表井”导致生成坍缩；注入/掩蔽因果验证。 Injection controls and strip-the-well-row experiments isolate the cause — causal evidence, not correlation, across 510 snapshots.

CaveatSeveral headline numbers rest on single seeds, and the most popular recipe (textbook DPO at β=0.1) does not collapse even at 3× budget.
