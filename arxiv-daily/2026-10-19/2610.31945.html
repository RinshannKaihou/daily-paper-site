Methodology · 方法

How Kolibri is built and trained

Architecture

50-block decoder-only MoE: 40 sliding-window blocks (window 512, RoPE) + every-fifth block full attention with NoPE; GQA 48/4 heads with QK RMS-norm. 384 routed experts, top-6 + 1 shared → 4.4% of parameters active per token; EQB + LEI load balancing.

Data & tokenizer

UniBPE hybrid tokenizer — BPE merging scored by Unigram loss. 20T pre-training tokens, 21.3% German (>2T self-curated: 0.8T organic + 1.1T rephrased, not translated); mix ratios solved as a convex program fitted on 3,354 proxy-model runs.

Training stages

Pre-training 20T @16k → mid-training 3.4T @64k → long-context 200B @256k; serves 1M-token contexts at inference.

Post-training

SFT ~268B tokens × 2 (averaged); one asynchronous RL run across 38 environments / 1,209,965 tasks — group-relative advantages, B-TV trust region, squared log-ratio regularizer, targeted OPSD injected at failed tool-call turns.

Serving-consistent training

Trainer replicates the inference engine's FP8 quantization and expert routing (RL diverges otherwise); per-token logit-gradient bound; orchestrated by the Savanna "Model Training as Code" factory with nightly CI.

Strengths · 优势

What holds up

  • Documentation depth — per-decision ablations, gradient-norm stability plots, failure post-mortems, explicit limitations sections.
  • Genuinely open — Apache 2.0 weights of a competitive sub-4B-active MoE with 1M-token context; few European models of this class exist.
  • Deployment-relevant evaluation — quality plotted against measured bytes/s/GPU at each model's best serving layout.
  • Machinery with evidence — per-token logit-gradient bound vs CISPO; FP8 QAT proven necessary; targeted OPSD for tool-failure remediation.
  • Compliance operationalized — PII redaction stats, copyright due diligence, abstention training with measured hallucination cuts.

Limitations · 局限

Where it falls short

  • English and German only; multilingual extension is listed as future work.
  • Long-context retrieval — trails Qwen3.5 and Nemotron Nano on RULER within its trained window (≤256k); the 1M win comes where almost no rivals are evaluable.
  • Weak rows — RGB Closed-Book last of 14 (73.0), TerminalBench 27.7, BFCL v4 multi-turn 47.5.
  • Single RL run — objective components (B-TV, OPSD) not ablated at target scale.
  • Hard to verify independently — German benchmark translations and Industry RAG proxies are in-house (two proxies: 11 and 26 items); all baselines self-run.
  • Data-mix law fitted on 15M proxies — authors flag optimizer sensitivity and unresolved German-quality misweighting.

Questions for Further Exploration · 延伸问题

Open threads worth pulling

  1. How much of the +21.4 gain over Origin is recipe vs 3× tokens and 2.6× parameters? A compute-matched ablation would isolate it.
  2. Does abstention training causally cause the RGB Closed-Book collapse (73.0)? Track hallucination-vs-recall across RL checkpoints.
  3. Does UniBPE transfer to other morphologically rich languages (Slavic, Turkic), and does the gain persist at larger vocabularies?
  4. Does the per-token logit-gradient bound measurably reduce entropy collapse vs CISPO in a target-scale controlled ablation?
  5. Do residual kernel-level mismatches still bias policy ratios over long FP8-aligned RL runs?
  6. Can fewer, larger proxies replace the 3,354-run mixing-law methodology, or does surrogate noise cap fidelity?
