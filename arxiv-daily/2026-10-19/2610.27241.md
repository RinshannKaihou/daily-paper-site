> **一句话总结：** 提出"实体透镜"(entity lens)——一种在固定层、只用中性提示 "{} is known for" 估计的 Jacobian 读出——能在完全不接触关系本身的情况下预测线性关系嵌入 (LRE) 的忠实度：在 OLMo-3-7B 的 LRE 数据集 28 个关系上，该关系无关得分与 LRE 忠实度的 Spearman 相关达 ρ = 0.7355 (p < .01)，说明线性可解码性主要是主体表征自身的属性，而非被解码关系的属性。

> **TL;DR:** The authors introduce the entity lens, a supervision-free Jacobian readout estimated on same-type entities under the neutral prompt "{} is known for" at one fixed layer (17). With no access to the relation, its template, or any (subject, object) pairs, its score predicts LRE (Hernandez et al., 2024) faithfulness across the 28 entity-subject relations of the LRE dataset on OLMo-3-7B at Spearman ρ = 0.7355 (p = 8.2e-6), far above the logit lens baseline on the same model (ρ = 0.206) and the best OLMo-corpus co-occurrence baseline (object document frequency, ρ = 0.571). Caveats: on Qwen3-8B-Base the plain logit lens actually wins (0.791 vs 0.665), LRE hyperparameters are selected in-sample, and at the fact level the lens predicts poorly (median ρ = 0.235). The paper also diagnoses two dataset flaws — semantically weak first tokens (PERSON_UNIVERSITY: 45% object collapse, model answers "University" for 54/91 subjects) and reference competition — and found a real tokenizer bug in year relations (faithfulness 1.00 was an artifact of scoring a bare space; rescored to 0.74/0.79).

## 核心贡献

- **实体透镜 (entity lens)：** 提出一种新的 Jacobian lens 形式。与跨所有 token 和概念估计的 Jacobian lens (Gurnee et al., 2026) 不同，实体透镜只在某一实体类型 t 的参考实体上、在单一中性提示 "{} is known for" 下估计 Jacobian，从而读出"模型准备好对该类型实体说什么"。给出两种变体：对比式 CEL(e) = mean_i[J_i(s_e − s_i)]（只保留区分性关联）与非对比式 NEL(e) = mean_i[J_i s_e]。
- **关系无关的线性可解码性诊断：** 证明在估计与预测阶段完全不使用任何关系特定信息（无关系模板、无对象、无 (主体, 对象) 对）的前提下，透镜对模型自身 top-1 答案的排名得分即可预测 LRE 忠实度——28 个关系上 ρ = 0.7355。这把"线性"从关系的属性重新定位为**主体表征的属性**。
- **系统性的稳健性检验：** 通过 leave-one-type-out（ρ ∈ [0.696, 0.799]）、对象基数控制（剔除后 ρ 升至 0.757）、答案 token 频率控制（0.784）、外部显著性检验（自由生成 prominence 与透镜 top-5 命中率 ρ = 0.570）以及锚点/主体项重组干预实验，对相关性结论做了多方位验证。
- **事实层面的数据集批判：** 将分析单位从关系下移到单条事实后，发现现有 LRE 数据集的两个系统性缺陷——**语义弱首 token**（如 61% 的 PERSON_MOTHER 对象与他人共享首 token，"University" 这类前缀被算作正确）与**指称竞争**（如演员 Rolf Thommessen 被模型关联到同姓律所的 "whistleblowing"）——并提出未来事实数据集的两条构建要求。

## Key Contributions

- **Entity lens:** A novel form of the Jacobian lens. Instead of estimating the Jacobian across all tokens and concepts (Gurnee et al., 2026), the entity lens evaluates Jacobians at reference entities of one type t under the single neutral factual prompt "{} is known for", yielding a readout of what the model is disposed to say about entities of that type. Two variants: contrastive CEL(e) = mean_i[J_i(s_e − s_i)] and noncontrastive NEL(e) = mean_i[J_i s_e].
- **Relation-agnostic diagnostic of linear decodability:** Without using the relation, its template, objects, or (subject, object) pairs in estimation or prediction, the lens's ranking of the model's own answer predicts LRE faithfulness at ρ = 0.7355 across 28 relations — reframing "linearity" as substantially a property of what the subject's representation already carries, not of the relation.
- **Robustness checks:** leave-one-entity-type-out (ρ ∈ [0.696, 0.799], median 0.728), controls for object cardinality (removing it raises ρ to 0.757) and answer-token frequency (ρ = 0.784 with both removed), an external salience check via free generation (prominence vs. gold-in-lens-top-5: ρ = 0.570, p = 0.033, n = 14), and interventional anchor/subject-term recombination experiments.
- **Fact-level dataset critique:** Moving to the individual-fact level exposes two defects of the LRE dataset — semantically weak first tokens (collapse rate: PERSON_MOTHER 61%, PERSON_FATHER 58%, PERSON_UNIVERSITY 45%) and reference competition (obscure actor names confused with same-last-name non-actor entities) — plus concrete methodological requirements for future fact datasets.

## Methodology

The paper builds on the LRE framework of Hernandez et al. (2024): an affine map o ≈ βWs + bβ, where W = mean_i J_i is the average Jacobian of the model's map f (from a mid-layer subject state to the final-layer state) over reference subjects, and bβ is an anchor. Faithfulness is the rate at which decoding βWs + bβ over the full vocabulary reproduces the model's own top-1 prediction (not the gold object), with layer and β selected per relation on a 6×6 grid (layers 5–23; β ∈ {0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 5}).

The entity lens drops the anchor (Appendix G shows no shared β beats the anchor-free form: selection beat it in only 1% of 2,000 splits) and comes in two forms: CEL subtracts reference-entity states before applying the averaged Jacobian (surfacing what differentiates an entity from same-type peers, e.g. "vodka"/"Kremlin" for Russia), while NEL applies mean_i J_i directly to the entity's state. Jacobians are never materialized: Jacobian-vector products are computed via the double-backward construction (Pearlmutter, 1994) in float32 with eager attention. The per-relation diagnostic is A_r = −median_e log10 rank of the model's answer under the lens, computed at a single fixed layer (17) for all relations.

Setup: primary model OLMo-3-7B (fully open), with six more models in the appendix (GPT-J 6B, Mistral 7B, Gemma-2 9B, Qwen3-8B-Base, Qwen3.8-27B, Pythia-6.9B). Of the 47 LRE-dataset relations, the 28 with named-entity subjects (person, country, company, landmark, etc.) are kept — pre-registered via the dataset's domain_name field — because "{} is known for" is a category error for relations over words or abstractions. Lens and LRE sample their in-context demonstrations and reference sets from independent splits. Section 7.3 adds a recombination intervention: swapping an LRE's subject term for a foreign relation's term, for the neutral-prompt NEL, for the anchor alone, or for a wrong entity's NEL.

## Results & Highlights

- **Headline prediction:** On the LRE dataset's 28 entity-subject relations with OLMo-3-7B, the relation-agnostic lens score correlates with LRE faithfulness at Spearman ρ = 0.7355 (p = 8.24×10⁻⁶), using one fixed layer and no relation information. The reproduced faithfulness spread runs from 0.875 (COUNTRY_CAPITAL_CITY) down to 0.075 (PERSON_FATHER) / 0.05 (COMPANY_CEO).
- **Against baselines (OLMo-3-7B):** NEL reaches ρ = 0.7650 vs 0.2056 for the logit lens and 0.4390 for the differential logit lens (Table 4); the strongest OLMo-corpus-frequency baseline is object document frequency at ρ = 0.5712 (Table 5) — the lens beats all pretraining-data co-occurrence measures.
- **Cross-model honesty check:** the entity lens is not uniformly best — on Qwen3-8B-Base the plain logit lens wins (ρ = 0.7908 vs 0.6647 CEL / 0.6232 NEL), and on GPT-J 6B the logit lens (0.4669) also edges out CEL (0.4346). All seven paired 95% bootstrap intervals for NEL vs CEL overlap zero.
- **Confound controls:** object cardinality k alone predicts faithfulness weakly (ρ = 0.355, p = 0.064) and is nearly uncorrelated with the lens (ρ = 0.086); partialling it out raises the lens correlation to ρ = 0.757, and to 0.784 with answer-token frequency also removed (frequency alone: ρ = −0.111). Leave-one-type-out keeps ρ ∈ [0.696, 0.799].
- **Reference-set sensitivity:** on PERSON_NATIVE_LANGUAGE (61 French subjects), CEL degrades by 1.10 median log10-rank (a factor of 13) when references switch from non-French to French, with the token "French" falling 47 → 115 → 665 in rank; NEL stays stable ([1.71, 1.81]) and even puts "French" at its best rank (55). On the 23 non-French subjects the ordering reverses — no reference set is neutral for every relation.
- **Interventions (β = 3):** swapping in a foreign relation's subject term leaves the anchor's answer largely intact (12/12 capitals, 21/24 pro-sport), and the neutral-prompt NEL substitutes moderately (21/24 sport, 18/24 position, but only 7/12 capitals and 4/12 largest cities); the anchor alone names 0 capitals — the hits are composed, not read off the anchor. Notably the model itself answers all 22 test countries correctly while the operator reproduces only 12.
- **A real bug found:** year relations initially scored faithfulness 1.000 because the tokenizer splits " 1743" so that first-token scoring compared a bare space on both sides; rescored they give 0.74 / 0.79. Including the uncorrected values would have depressed the headline correlation from 0.7355 to 0.416.
- **Fact level is hard:** per-relation correlation between lens rank and LRE rank spans ρ ∈ [−0.401, 0.664] with median 0.235 — the relation-level result does not transfer to individual facts, motivating the dataset critique (PERSON_UNIVERSITY: model answers "University" for 54/91 subjects; 45% object-collapse rate).

## Authors & Credibility

The authors are from LMU Munich's CIS, TU Darmstadt's UKP Lab, MCML, and relAI (Hinrich Schütze's group — a well-established NLP lab; group members have prior interpretability and analysis work). The paper follows ICLR-style conventions with explicit AI-use, acknowledgement, and reproducibility statements: the complete pipeline is public (github.com/jonasrohweder-lab/entity-lens per abstract), one command reproduces every number, and the authors verified it in a fresh Docker container (~35 A100-80GB GPU-hours for all seven models, ~1,200 CPU core-hours, 3.6 TB disk for OLMo corpus counts). The decision to pre-register the 28-relation entity filter via the dataset's domain_name field, and to report the tokenizer artifact they discovered in the standard protocol, both signal above-average care.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** Solid rather than revolutionary. The entity lens is a natural specialization of Gurnee et al.'s Jacobian lens (same first-order machinery, entity-type references, one neutral prompt), and the "linearity lives in the subject" conclusion complements Merullo et al. (2025) and Geva et al. (2023) rather than overturning them. The recombination interventions and the dataset critique are the freshest parts.
- **Rigor:** Generally high (leave-one-type-out, cardinality/frequency controls, external salience check, 500-anchor variance analysis), with candid self-flagging: Figure 1 is correlational; LRE layer/β selection is in-sample on the scored subjects; β = 3 in the interventions was picked by inspecting an earlier version of the same analysis (Appendix E admits this); layer sensitivity is untested (Appendix C).
- **Weak spots the abstract hides:** (1) On Qwen3-8B-Base the plain logit lens (0.7908) beats the entity lens (0.6647) — the headline "relation-agnostic readout predicts faithfulness" is model-dependent, and no analysis is given for why the lens underperforms there beyond a prompt-sensitivity table where "{s} is known for" yields only ρ = 0.3430 vs 0.7331 for "Entity: {s} is known for" — i.e., the "neutral prompt" is itself a tuned hyperparameter per model (four models needed a leading newline for position-zero norm issues). (2) The lens is only a good predictor in the middle of the cardinality range (§6.1, §9): it over-predicts for low-cardinality relations like NAME_GENDER (+0.289 residual, k = 9.5) where an LRE can win by elimination, and under-predicts for hard near-unique-answer relations (PERSON_FATHER −0.381, COMPANY_CEO −0.260). (3) 28 relations with some as small as 19–24 rows make per-relation faithfulness coarse. (4) The fact-level deliverable is essentially negative (median ρ = 0.235) and the "requirements for better datasets" are methodological directions, not evaluated solutions — the authors say so themselves.
- **Reproducibility:** Excellent — pinned seeds/checkpoints/prompts, one-command reproduction, public models and datasets.
- **Significance:** A useful diagnostic and a well-evidenced reframe (decodability ≈ what the subject's representation already carries), plus a genuinely valuable audit of the standard LRE evaluation protocol (the space-token artifact and collapse-rate statistics will matter to anyone reusing this benchmark).
- **Red flags:** None serious. The main risk is over-reading the headline ρ as evidence of mechanism rather than prediction; the authors mostly resist this.

## Strengths

- A clean, well-motivated question (can LRE faithfulness be predicted without the relation?) answered with a deliberately minimal, hyperparameter-free readout (NEL after dropping the anchor has no β at all).
- Unusually thorough control battery: confound partialling (cardinality, answer frequency), leave-one-type-out, external salience measurement via free generation, reference-set composition analysis with a monotone mechanistic account of the CEL cancellation, and interventional recombination that separates anchor (relation) from subject term.
- Intellectual honesty: they surface and fix a scoring artifact in the standard protocol that inflated two relations to 1.000, report the counterfactual correlation (0.416), and report where their method loses to a simpler baseline (logit lens on Qwen3/GPT-J).
- The fact-level analysis converts negative results into concrete, reusable dataset-construction guidance (score complete objects, verify that the intended referent is what the model actually resolves).

## Limitations

- Purely correlational at the relation level; no causal claim that the read-out representation is what the LRE exploits.
- In-sample LRE hyperparameter selection means reported faithfulness is an upper-ish estimate; no held-out evaluation.
- The predictor degrades at both tails of object cardinality (tiny answer spaces → faithfulness by elimination; huge answer spaces → floor effects), with no principled boundary given.
- Only 28 entity-subject relations from one 47-relation dataset; smallest relations have 19–24 examples; results on other models are weaker and prompt-choice-sensitive (the neutral prompt is not a free lunch — it had to be varied for Qwen3).
- Fact-level prediction is weak (median ρ = 0.235), and reference competition / weak-first-token problems are diagnosed but not solved.
- The diagnostic is defined only for entity subjects; relations over words or abstractions are out of scope by construction.

## Relevance

与用户在 "LLM Hidden States & Interpretability" 方向的兴趣（机制可解释性、内部表征与隐藏状态、probing、表征几何）高度契合：本文正是用 Jacobian 读出探测主体隐藏状态中已编码的事实知识，并将其与线性关系解码联系起来。若你关注 factual recall 的机制或 LRE/知识表征几何，这篇论文的实体透镜诊断与数据集审计值得精读。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Why does the entity lens lose to the plain logit lens on Qwen3-8B-Base (0.6647 vs 0.7908) — is it position-zero norm pathologies, tokenizer differences, or genuinely different factual-recall circuitry?
- Would averaging several neutral prompts (rather than one "{s} is known for") broaden the salience the lens captures — e.g., attributes like gender that a name is not "known for"?
- Can the lens be turned into a causal test — e.g., does steering along NEL's top directions change LRE faithfulness for individual facts, closing the correlation-to-mechanism gap?
- How does the diagnostic behave under model editing (ROME/MEMIT) or fine-tuning: does predicted decodability track edited facts?
- Can the cardinality tails be repaired with a normalized score (e.g., rank-within-answer-space rather than global vocabulary rank) so the predictor covers low- and high-k relations too?
- Do the semantically weak first token and reference competition problems affect other knowledge benchmarks (ParaRel, LAMA, PopQA) as strongly as the LRE dataset, and would full-continuation scoring change published conclusions there?
