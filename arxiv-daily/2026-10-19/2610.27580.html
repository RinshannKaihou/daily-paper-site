Key FindingarXiv 2610.27580

Diffusion LMs recall facts through a two-stage circuit — layer-0 relation routing, early-MLP lookup — and one fact occupies a region of state space, not a point.

Factual Access States in Diffusion Language Models: From Mechanism to Knowledge Editing and Retrieval ↗
Yuchen Guo · Zhuoyuan Mao · Yiwen Guo 2026-10-20 · arXiv preprint Models: LLaDA-8B · Dream-7B
diffusion-language-models mechanistic-interpretability knowledge-editing information-retrieval factual-recall

一句话总结：两阶段事实访问——第 0 层注意力把关系词路由进可见实体位置，第 1–8 层 MLP 在融合表征上查表；同一事实占据状态空间的一个区域而非一点，据此写入（Multi-View Edit）与读取（免训练检索）共用同一状态。

Key Metric
+63.31pts

BAKE Reverse-QA, Dream-7B: 20.26% → 83.57% with Multi-View Edit. On LLaDA-8B: +40.89 pts (22.35% → 63.24%).

Why This Matters

First causal-tracing account of factual recall in diffusion LMs — one mechanism drives both knowledge editing and training-free retrieval. The “ROME moment” for DLMs.

机制可解释性方向的核心样本：机制 → 干预（编辑）→ 读取（检索）的完整闭环。

⚠ Critical Edge

The headline “AR editors gain near zero” rests on an uncontrolled comparison — published numbers from different models (GPT-J / LLaMA-2) and edit pools, not matched baselines.

Methodology 三阶段设计

  • Mask-native causal tracing. Corrupt subject+object with the model's own [MASK] operator, restore each state, measure indirect effect; decompose with single-layer ablations + five attention-surgery conditions. The apparent late peak (layers 18–25) is a window-stacking artifact.
  • Factual access state a(e, r, d). Early-block residual at the visible entity after layer-0 routing — a function of relation wording and query direction. Hence a region, not a point.
  • Multi-View Edit. From one statement, build masked views — Vobj, Vrel (ρ∈{0.4, 0.7} × 3 patterns), Vctx (suffixes matter in DLMs), Vrev. Fit one shared value per direction (intersection, not union — rank never grows), write along a whitened key direction into early-MLP down-projections, MEMIT-style. Attention untouched.
  • Reading the state. Per-entity states (block-0 output or a validation-picked layer: L31 / L27) become relation-specific retrieval vectors — cosine scoring, alternating budget, zero training.

Strengths 优势

  • Tracing adapted to how DLMs actually decode — [MASK] corruption, masked-position scoring, not Gaussian noise.
  • Converging evidence: window tracing, single-layer ablation, attention surgery, role reversal, second backbone.
  • Editor directly motivated by mechanism — fit the region of keys, not the single editing sentence; shared-value intersection controls locality and rank growth.
  • Retrieval recycles the same mechanism; the causal subtraction / substitution experiment closes the loop.
  • Unusual candor: measured (non-zero) BAKE floors, matched-budget ablations, honest QAEdit reporting.

Limitations 局限

  • Needs identifiable subject / relation / object spans — arbitrary user statements may not parse.
  • QAEdit paraphrase transfer stays partial: wrong answers remain the majority (weighted 0.187 on LLaDA).
  • No head-to-head with DLM-native editors (e.g. DiffER) beyond the Park & Jo composition; FT+L is the only external DLM baseline.
  • Mechanism verified on two 7–8B DLMs only; behavior at scale is unverified.
  • Retrieval benchmark's regular structure (exactly four relations per entity) may flatter readouts; layer-0 treated as a monolith — no head-level dissection.

Questions for Further Exploration 延伸问题

  • Does layer-0 routing + early-MLP lookup hold at 30B+ scale, or under LLaDA's mixed AR/diffusion pretraining?
  • Which layer-0 heads carry the relation routing — learned per relation type, or generic syntactic position signals?
  • Can Multi-View Edit scale to thousands of sequential MEMIT-style edits without the view family amplifying interference?
  • Would Monte-Carlo view families (fitting a sampled region of key space) also fix paraphrase / reverse brittleness in AR editors?
  • Does the access state support other region-respecting operations — e.g. fact deletion (unlearning) with the same locality guarantees?
