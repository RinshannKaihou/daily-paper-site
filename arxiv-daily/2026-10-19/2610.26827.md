> **一句话总结：** UCLA 提出 KEEP-Bench（4,102 个案例的诊断式基准），绕过下游 QA 准确率、直接检验压缩后的上下文是否完整保留了推理所需的全部证据命题，发现所有专用压缩器和 10B 以下通用 LLM 的全命题召回率（All-Rec）均不足 30%，最强的 Gemma-4-31B-it 也只有 66.8%（输入平均仅 3.1K token），且更多输出预算只让强者更强。

> **TL;DR:** KEEP-Bench (UCLA) directly audits context-compression faithfulness via 4,102 cases in three evidence structures (parallel/conditional/imbalanced), instead of inferring it from downstream QA. On average 3.1K-token inputs, the best open-weight compressor Gemma-4-31B-it preserves all required evidence in only 66.8% of cases and Claude-Haiku-4.5 in 61.6%, while every dedicated compressor (RECOMP, BRIEF-Pro, EXIT) scores below 10% All-Rec — far under EXIT's 41.9% Para-Rec on parallel structure. In a GPT-5.2 pilot, unfaithful compressions collapse downstream both-answer accuracy from 94.0% (original) to 11.5% (−82.5 pts), proving QA metrics can silently mask evidence loss.

## 核心贡献

- 提出 KEEP-Bench：首个直接评估（而非经由下游 QA 间接推断）上下文压缩忠实性的诊断基准，共 4,102 个案例，覆盖三种证据结构——平行证据（一个歧义指称诱导两条必须同时保留的推理链）、条件证据（必须保留推理步的限定条件/qualifier）、不均衡证据（占少数但同样必需的观点，如政策中仅一句话支持的地区性例外）。
- 定义命题级指标：把每个案例所需的全部证据拆解为最小、自包含的命题集合，用 All-Rec（全命题召回，只有全部保留才计 1）以及 Para-Rec、Cond-Rec 分别度量；用 GPT-5.2 作判定器，与人工标注一致率 93%。
- 提出新指标 EPR（Evidence Preservation Return）：整体 All-Rec 与单例推理 TFLOPs 之比的平方根缩放，度量"每单位算力换来的证据保存"；发现 EPR 在每个模型家族的 4B–8B 中等规模处达到峰值。
- 实证结论：现有压缩方法远不可靠——专用压缩器全部低于 10% 整体 All-Rec；算力与保存效果不成正比（EXIT 用 40.2 倍于 RECOMP-extractive 的算力只换来 +1.5 点）；增加输出预算主要让本已强大的模型受益、反而拉大能力差距；证据在上下文中相隔越远、越靠后越容易被丢弃（primacy bias）。
- 开源了基准（GitHub + HuggingFace 数据集 + 网站），可直接用于未来压缩器的诊断。

## Key Contributions

- KEEP-Bench, a 4,102-case diagnostic benchmark that evaluates the compressed context itself (proposition-level entailment) rather than downstream task accuracy, isolating compression fidelity from reader behavior and parametric knowledge.
- Three deliberately orthogonal evidence structures — parallel (two plausible referents inducing two disjoint reasoning chains), conditional (an explicit qualifier that a hop is valid only under a stated condition), and imbalanced (minority perspectives supported by a single sentence amid heavily documented dominant ones) — spanning fictional multi-hop chains built from MuSiQue plus web-retrieved science/policy contexts.
- All Proposition Recall (All-Rec) and structure-specific variants (Para-Rec, Cond-Rec), judged by GPT-5.2 with 93% human agreement, plus Evidence Preservation Return (EPR) = κ·sqrt(All-Rec / TFLOPs) linking preservation to inference compute.
- A 17-configuration evaluation (4 dedicated compressors + 13 general LLMs from 1B to 31B plus GPT-5.4-mini and Claude-Haiku-4.5) with controlled sentence-level output budgets, ICL prompts tailored per setting, and prefix-amortized FLOP accounting.
- Analyses along computation, output budget, and evidence position, including a 200-query pilot showing unfaithful compressions destroy downstream accuracy (96.0% → 11.5%) even though QA-based protocols would not notice.

## Methodology

The benchmark construction splits by setting. Parallel and conditional settings start from qualified MuSiQue examples: minimal per-hop evidence propositions are extracted, then all entities are fictionalized so compressors cannot reconstruct omitted evidence from world knowledge. Parallel instances replace a queried entity with an underspecified mention admitting two referents, each with its own parallel chain (2–4 hops; 900 cases), so the full evidence set is the union of both chains. Conditional instances (1,364 cases) add an explicit qualifier plus a disambiguating paragraph stating the proposition fails when the condition is unmet. Imbalanced settings (1,838 cases: 1,010 science, 828 policy) are built from a retrieval pipeline over web documents via the Serper API: dominant propositions get multiple ~400-token supporting chunks; each minority proposition is grounded in a single inserted sentence.

Evaluation decomposes the gold evidence into self-contained propositions and asks whether each is entailed by the compressed context alone; a case counts only under All-Rec if every proposition survives. For LLM compressors, the authors tuned system prompts per setting, per-setting ICL demonstrations, and thinking modes, selecting ICL-without-thinking (best overall) for the main table; budgets are sentence-level, with the default giving demonstrations >2× the token count of the reference propositions. EPR uses prefix-amortized TFLOPs per case (proprietary APIs excluded). The position study permutes the two branch blocks of 400 two-hop parallel cases across Adjacent/Middle/Distant separations (14 arrangements per case), and the error analysis examines 991 cases all four Gemma-4 models fail and 2,540 shared EXIT/BRIEF-Pro failures.

## Results & Highlights

- On KEEP-Bench overall (avg. input 3.1K tokens vs. 32K–400K context windows), the best open-weight compressor Gemma-4-31B-it reaches only 66.8% All-Rec and the best proprietary model Claude-Haiku-4.5 only 61.6%; every dedicated compressor (RECOMP abstractive/extractive, BRIEF-Pro, EXIT) stays below 10% overall All-Rec, and all general LLMs under 10B parameters stay below 30%.
- Pilot study (200 parallel queries, GPT-5.2 reader, both-answers-required accuracy): 94.0% with original contexts, 96.0% with faithful compressions, but 11.5% with unfaithful compressions — a drop of 82.5 points vs. original, showing downstream QA silently masks evidence loss (a reader can still guess one answer: per-answer match remains 40.0%/27.0%).
- Performance varies sharply by structure and domain: Claude-Haiku-4.5 leads the parallel setting at 63.8% All-Rec vs. 31.9% for GPT-5.4-mini (2.0×), yet trails GPT-5.4-mini on imbalanced science (45.5% vs. 60.3%); moving science→policy flips GPT-5.4-mini from 60.3% to 22.6% while lifting Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct from 4.1% to 34.5%.
- Output budget amplifies gaps: from 0.5× to 2× the default budget, the three strongest LLMs gain +32.8 All-Rec points on average while the three weakest gain only +3.9; on parallel evidence Gemma-4-31B-it rises 33.3% → 71.4% whereas Llama-3.2-1B-Instruct stays at 0.0% under every budget.
- Compute is a poor predictor of preservation: EXIT uses 40.2× the computation of RECOMP (extractive) for only +1.5 All-Rec points; Gemma-4-12B-it uses 35.0% less compute than Qwen3-14B while scoring +20.0 points higher; EPR peaks at moderate scales (best-in-family: Qwen3-4B 3.445, Gemma-4-E4B-it 5.163, Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct 2.981), and below 50 TFLOPs/case no method reaches 30% All-Rec.
- Position effects (400 two-hop parallel cases): separating the two evidence blocks from Adjacent to Distant cuts Para-Rec by 4.8–9.0 points (Qwen3-8B, Llama-3.1-8B), and earlier-presented evidence is recalled 2.5–10.6 points more often than later evidence (primacy bias).
- Error analysis: scaling localizes rather than eliminates failure — among 991 cases all Gemma-4 models fail, the share missing exactly one proposition rises 15.8% (E2B) → 61.0% (31B) while missing ≥4 falls 24.6% → 5.7%; failure signatures differ by type — EXIT keeps fragments of multiple branches without completeness in 70.2% of shared failures, whereas BRIEF-Pro collapses to a single branch/perspective in 62.2%.

## Authors & Credibility

All six authors (Ziqing Man, Di Wu, Jia-Chen Gu, Junyi Zhang, Kai-Wei Chang, Nanyun Peng) are from UCLA; the senior authors (Kai-Wei Chang, Nanyun Peng) lead the PlusLab NLP group with an extensive record on RAG, reasoning, and compression — notably the BRIEF/BRIEF-Pro compression line, which this benchmark evaluates critically rather than favorably (BRIEF-Pro scores below 10% overall). Code, dataset (HuggingFace: uclanlp/KEEP-Bench), and a website are released. The design choices that most inspire confidence are entity fictionalization (blocking parametric-knowledge shortcuts), judge validation against 100 human decisions per setting (93% agreement), and the controlled pilot study that empirically justifies the direct-evaluation premise.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** The core move — scoring the compressed artifact via proposition-level entailment instead of downstream QA — is genuinely diagnostic and well-motivated by the pilot study; the main incremental aspect is that directness itself is not new (Łajewska et al. 2025; Context Codec), so KEEP-Bench's real contribution is the three controlled evidence structures plus the compute-coupled EPR metric.
- **Rigor:** Strong on controls (fictional entities, equal prompts/demonstrations across LLMs, sentence-level budgets, judge validation). But the judge is GPT-5.2 evaluating outputs of GPT-5.4-mini — a same-family judge assessing a competitor-era model; 93% agreement on 100 propositions/setting is decent but the disagreement tail directly moves All-Rec, an all-or-nothing metric where one misjudged proposition flips a whole case.
- **Reproducibility:** Good — released code/data/prompts and only public models. However, the imbalanced setting depends on the Serper API and live web content, which will drift over time, and 856/1,010 science cases being 4-perspective gives the minority-detection statistic a narrow shape.
- **Significance:** The headline (66.8% for the best 31B model on 3.1K-token inputs) is a striking, useful negative result; the EPR framing (compute per unit of preservation) is a fresh angle on compression efficiency.
- **Red flags / weaknesses to note:** (1) The default budget is generous — demonstrations carry >2× the reference evidence tokens — yet this is framed as compressor failure rather than partly a budget-policy artifact; the 0.5× column in Figure 4 is where compression actually compresses, and conclusions there are far bleaker. (2) Compressors are prompted without seeing the query for the fictional settings? No — the setup gives query-conditioned compression, but dedicated compressors like RECOMP (775M, T5-large init) against 31B LLMs is a lopsided comparison that makes "all dedicated compressors < 10%" partly a statement about model scale, not about the dedicated-compressor paradigm. (3) RECOMP (abstractive) is exempted from length control because it cannot follow constraints, so its 0.0% parallel All-Rec partly reflects a protocol mismatch rather than pure preservation failure. (4) Fictionalized MuSiQue chains are much cleaner than real corpora, so the parallel/conditional settings may overstate how often such tidy dual chains appear in practice.

## Strengths

- Directly measures the right object (the compressed context) with an all-or-nothing metric that matches how evidence loss actually breaks downstream reasoning, validated by the 96.0% → 11.5% pilot collapse.
- The three settings are carefully disentangled, and the cross-setting disagreement results (Claude wins parallel 2:1 but loses science; policy flips two models in opposite directions) are the most valuable practical finding — single-benchmark compressor rankings are unstable.
- EPR adds a much-needed efficiency dimension: the paper shows the "upper-left" regime (high preservation, low compute) is empty for every method, reframing compression research as a frontier problem rather than a scaling problem.
- Honest, mechanistic error analysis (localized omissions under scaling; extractive = breadth-without-completeness vs. abstractive = whole-branch collapse) gives actionable design guidance.

## Limitations

- LLM-as-judge (GPT-5.2) for entailment introduces circularity risk and its 7% disagreement rate is material for a conjunction metric like All-Rec.
- Average input is only 3.1K tokens — far from the long-context (100K+) regime where compression matters most, so extrapolating the 66.8% ceiling to production-length contexts is unjustified.
- The default output budget is loose relative to true compression use; compression rates of 10–30× coexist with generous sentence budgets, and the weakest models are never shown under query-agnostic settings.
- Dedicated compressors are represented by only four systems, two of which are RECOMP variants from 2024; the strongest trained compressors in this comparison are small (≤775M or distilled), so the "dedicated compressors fail" conclusion conflates paradigm with scale.
- The imbalanced setting's web-retrieved evidence will rot as sources change, complicating longitudinal comparability; proprietary-model EPR is unreported (excluded), leaving the two best models outside the efficiency analysis.

## Relevance

与"Agent & Pipeline Silent Failures"方向高度相关：上下文压缩正是位于模型与外部世界之间、会静默改写模型所见输入的 RAG/编排层组件，KEEP-Bench 提供了检测这类"静默证据丢失"的直接度量与基准；其发现（最强模型仍有约三分之一案例丢失必需证据、下游 QA 无法察觉）对长时程 agent 与 RAG 管线的可靠性设计有直接指导意义。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How do All-Rec and the structural failure signatures change at 50K–200K-token inputs, where compression is actually deployed — does the 66.8% ceiling fall further, and does the primacy bias amplify with context length?
- Can EPR be computed for proprietary compressors via measured/charged token pricing or published FLOP proxies, closing the gap in the efficiency analysis?
- Would a query-aware extractive-then-abstractive hybrid fix the two observed failure modes (EXIT's breadth-without-completeness and BRIEF-Pro's single-branch collapse) at moderate (4B–8B) scale where EPR peaks?
- Is the GPT-5.2 judge's 7% disagreement with humans uniform, or concentrated on paraphrased/entailed-by-inference propositions — and would a self-consistency or dual-judge protocol change model rankings, given the paper's own claim that rankings are structure-sensitive?
- Can compression models be trained explicitly on the three evidence structures (parallel chains, qualifiers, minority perspectives) without contaminating the benchmark, since the released data may leak into training corpora?
