A²

arXiv 2610.32142  ·  accepted at NeurIPS 2026  ·  2026-10-19

Large LoRA+ learning-rate ratios collapse because ηB amplifies initialization noise — shrinking A's init variance to Θ(n⁻²) removes it.

标准 Init[A] 在高学习率比下把随机初始化噪声与信号一起放大导致崩溃；将 A 的初始化方差降至 Θ(1/n²)（Init[A²]，B₀=0）即可在保持特征学习的同时压低噪声、稳定激进微调。

Key metric
+20.9pts

Accuracy at LR ratio ρ = 4000 (Llama 3-8B arithmetic SFT): 56.7% → 77.6% for Init[A2] vs standard Init[A].

mechanism check: init-noise share of feature energy 117% → 1.5–3.1%

Why this matters

Pinpoints why LoRA fine-tuning blows up at aggressive learning rates — and fixes it with a one-line initialization change in standard PEFT code.

从理论定位初始化引发的高学习率比崩溃，并给出确定性诊断量（噪声/信号能量分解）与一行代码级修复。

Critical edge

After matched LR retuning the headline gap shrinks to ~1.3 pts — the real value is a wider stable LR region, not uniformly higher peak accuracy.

Methodology — distilled

An exact signal–noise decomposition of the LoRA feature update explains the collapse, then prescribes the minimal fix.

  • Setup. LoRA layer Z̄ = WZ + (α/r)BAZ, frozen W, rank r ≪ width n, per-matrix rates ηA, ηB; updates modeled as normalized optimizer directions (covers SignSGN / Adam / AdamW).
  • Lemma 1 (exact). The feature update splits into a signal term built from accumulated optimizer updates and a noise term proportional to random init Ã₀ — ηB amplifies both equally.
  • Theorem 1. Under Init[A] (σA² = Θ(n⁻¹)), noise energy stays Θ(1) in width — never washed out, so large ρ, β, α/r are all brittle.
  • Theorem 2. Init[A2] suppresses noise by an extra 1/n while preserving feature learning (ηA = Θ(n⁻¹), ηB = Θ(1)); an independent spectral analysis recovers the same n⁻².
  • Experiments. MNIST toy MLP (widths 16→4096), RoBERTa-base on GLUE, decoder-only SFT across Llama 3 / 3.2, Qwen2.5 / Qwen3, Mistral + QLoRA; sweeps over β, ρ, r, α/r; 3–5 seeds on headline claims.

THE FIX — σA² : Θ(n⁻¹) → Θ(n⁻²)  ·  keep B₀ = 0  ·  one-line PEFT edit, zero cost

Strengths

  • New mechanism, directly measured. Init noise = 117% of feature energy under Init[A] falls to 1.5–3.1% — matching the theory's dominance prediction.
  • The fix is free. No architecture change, no extra compute, zero initial output preserved — one line in standard PEFT code.
  • Unusual honesty. Seed-level CIs, matched-retuning controls, explicit single-run caveats on the heatmaps.
  • Generalizes. Variance-compressing Orthogonal/EVA/PiSSA rescues them too (2.49% → 77.74%) — A₀'s scale, not Gaussianity, is what matters.
  • Cross-backbone, cross-precision. Llama / Qwen / Mistral, full-precision and QLoRA — not a single-model artifact.

Limitations

  • Single-run sweeps. Most LR-ratio heatmaps have one run; grid-cell differences are not established improvements.
  • SFT only. No RL post-training (RLVR/GRPO), no instruction tuning at scale, models ≤ 8B.
  • No optimal-ratio rule. Stabilizes the high-ratio regime but does not tell you which ρ to pick; rank–multiplier–LR interactions only partially characterized.
  • Equal-LR trade-off. Smaller initial A slows early adaptation (51.5% vs 57.8% at η = 3e−4); over-suppression (Init[A3]/A4) needs huge ratios to recover.
  • Assumptions probed, not proven. Alignment, contraction exponents, T(n) = o(√n) — and the 1/n asymptotic is never directly verified at finite width.

Questions for further exploration

  1. Does Init[A2] change the optimal-ratio landscape for RL post-training (RLVR/GRPO with LoRA), where LR sensitivity is most severe?
  2. Can the noise/signal energy ratio E_N/E_S become an online early-warning probe that flags "ηB too large" before accuracy collapses?
  3. Why does the best ratio in practice (ρ ≈ 200–2000) fall well below the ηB ≈ nηA width guideline (ρ ≈ 4096 for n = 4096)?
  4. How does Init[A2] interact with rsLoRA's α/√r multiplier and rank-transfer rules — is one closed-form (r, α/r, ρ) prescription possible?
  5. Does variance compression extend to nonzero-B₀ initializers (CorDA, LoftQ) and quantized backbones beyond the tested QLoRA setup?
