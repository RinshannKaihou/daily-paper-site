Key Finding 核心发现

Weight sensitivity is a designable quantity — one added regularizer term shrinks a network's dangerous tail 5–13× at ≤0.5 pt accuracy cost.

Why This Matters 为何重要

Stored model weights are one Rowhammer strike or low-voltage SRAM error away from corruption — this hardens edge AI against exactly those faults, cheaply.

直接对应 Rowhammer / 低压 SRAM 位翻转对存储权重的破坏，同时降低纠错开销、改善低比特存储。

▲ Critical Edge 批判视角

The headline storage gain is mostly not LEAN's: 2.4 of its 2.8 points over 3-bit LSQ come from sensitivity-ordered tiering alone (λ=0) — the regularizer itself merely matches LSQ (74.3 vs 74.4).

And the 500-flip survival headline holds in only 2 of 3 training seeds — the seed-robust guarantee is 300 flips, not 500.

Methodology 方法

Four moving parts, one knob

10-epoch fine-tune of a trained checkpoint · λ is the only tuned coefficient

1

Damage model

INT8 storage (one scale per tensor) means a sign-bit flip moves a whole layer by one step Δℓ = 128·sℓ, so first-order damage is dᵢ = |gᵢ|·Δℓ. Baseline tails are heavy: on ResNet-18/CIFAR-10 the top 1% of weights carry ~20% of all flip damage.

2

The regularizer

Fine-tune with L = LCE + λ(Ptail + β·Pclip). The tail term is the mean damage of the currently top-1% most sensitive weights — a Hessian-vector product via double backprop, every 4th step (~1.5× fine-tune cost). The clip term bounds the flip step by clipping layer outliers at the 99th quantile. β=100 fixed.

3

Falsification check

Real single-bit flips confirm the reshape is genuine: worst-100 damage falls 0.031 → 0.009 loss/flip. The tail term alone at λ=400 merely games the proxy (gradient masking); the range term carries most of the real shrink.

4

Deployment

Either INT8 plus a selective BCH(127,43) code over the four MSBs of HIGH weights (parity hidden in LOW-weight bits), or a sensitivity-ordered 8/6/4/3-bit tiered format with an interleaved sign-plane BCH code.

Strengths 优势

  • Attacks the root cause — the size of the sensitive tail — with one interpretable knob; λ sweeps monotonically and a floor analysis explains saturation near 0.1%.
  • Falsification discipline: real-flip verification, term-wise ablation exposing gradient masking, and a gradient-norm control that fails to reproduce the effect.
  • One training term buys three systems wins — attack tolerance, 3.7× cheaper parity, better low-bit storage — reusing the same selective-BCH machinery unchanged.
  • Both deployment routes characterized; the non-obvious finding that the learning-rate schedule, not epoch count, sets how far the tail moves.

Limitations 局限

  • Needs training data and fine-tune access (~1.5× cost, plus a QAT pass for the low-bit format) — not applicable to frozen third-party models.
  • Seed-fragile headline: 94% @ 500 flips holds in 2 of 3 seeds; most tables are single runs, so sub-0.5-pt accuracy costs sit within noise.
  • Idealized code: the BCH decoder is never simulated (covered flips simply modelled corrected), the attacker assumed greedy, random faults tested at a single BER (6%).
  • No transfer to targeted attacks — mixed-data sensitivity does not rank class-conditional sensitivity; zero ImageNet gain.
  • LLM evidence is preliminary (≤1.5B params, 1 epoch, mixed coded-attack results); energy figures are analytical estimates; no code release.

Questions for Further Exploration 待探问题

  • Would a codeword-planning (non-greedy) attacker — budgeting flips across interleaved codewords — invalidate the 300–500-flip survival guarantee?
  • Can the sensitivity ranking be made class-conditional so targeted (T-BFA-style) attacks fall inside the protected set without inflating coverage?
  • Does the first-layer outlier floor predict which architectures (ViT embedders, LLM heads, depthwise CNNs) are fundamentally harder to reshape?
  • Does the INT4 perplexity bonus survive stronger quantizers (GPTQ/AWQ) that exploit the outlier structure LEAN deliberately removes?
