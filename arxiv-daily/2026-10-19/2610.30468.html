Key metric · headline result
+3.78dB PSNR

Nebula3DGS-v2 reaches 36.36 dB vs 32.58 dB for vanilla 3DGS on the nebula scene — a domain no prior 3DGS method addresses.

16 runs · 198 branches · 24–48 h & $150–250 per run — vs a graduate-student cycle of months
Why this matters

First agentic discovery validated end-to-end in a live research field — its claim-to-log binding and standing falsifier target the reliability of long-running autonomous agents.

首个在活跃 CV 子领域端到端验证的 agentic scientific discovery 系统，直接回应长程自主智能体的可靠性与可信性问题。

⚠ Critical edge — read before citing

The +3.78 dB headline is against untuned vanilla 3DGS in a new domain; against tuned published SOTA gains cluster at +0.18–0.84 dB, some within the ~0.1 dB noise floor. The authors' own human reviewer rates the manuscripts borderline 3.5/6 vs 4.1/6 for published papers.

Methodology — the loop

W(x; D) maps a one-line prompt, a paper URL, or nothing to (LaTeX, PDF, claim-to-log ledger, codebase), on a fixed context D: the 3DGS corpus, the verified Splatify backbone, nine EVOLVE-BLOCK hooks, a conference template.

  1. Grounding

    RAG over arXiv/Scholar plus a persistent cross-run paper store; parsed PDFs become limitation IDs (e.g. L-127) and measured bottleneck IDs (e.g. B-04: garden plateaus at 25.65 dB).

  2. Grounded ideation

    Candidates carry (mechanism, category, L-ID, B-ID, predicted Δ); screens for precedent, "Tweak Trap" novelty, saturation, orthogonality; LLM-judged bracket tournament seeds Elo 1200.

  3. Implementation

    Hook Mapper binds each category to the 9 EVOLVE-BLOCK hooks; citation-graph + grammar-constrained diffs; a 100-iteration smoke test gates entry.

  4. Refinement

    Cascade-weighted Elo (smoke .2 → probe .5 → full 1.0, K=32) across 8 islands, 5 mutation operators picked by a bandit; promotion needs Pareto improvement per (benchmark, loss); falsifier attacks cost −30 Elo.

  5. Audit

    Six checks — provenance hash chain, faithfulness, novelty re-resolution, compute-match, cross-codebase port, claim check. A load-bearing mismatch aborts the run.

  6. Synthesis

    Research log (Mode A) or manuscript (Mode B): five writers, VLM figure check, three reviewers under an Editor; the loop ends only when no reviewer demands a new experiment.

System shape

  • 36 agents — Professor / PhD coordinators / undergrad leaves, three levels.
  • No fixed DAG — stages revisited on falsifier veto, audit failure, reviewer ablations.
  • Verified substrate — every candidate mutates a plugin already matching its target's published headline.
  • Real-GPU scoring — Elo computed on measured PSNR, conditioned on (benchmark, loss) pairs.
  • Claim-to-log binding — the run cannot finish while any reported number lacks a logged training run.

Strengths

What holds up
  • Verified-reproduction backbone anchors every mutation to a plugin matching the target's published headline — improvements get a fixed external reference.
  • Claim-to-log binding is a real reliability contribution: 16/16 unbacked numbers without the gate, 0/16 with it.
  • Ranking decided by measured GPU PSNR conditioned on (benchmark, loss) — not free-text LLM scores.
  • Honest negative space: marginal gains labeled against a stated ~0.1 dB noise floor, one recorded failure, a disagreeing human reviewer kept in the paper.
  • Real artifacts — workshop posters with genuinely interesting content (e.g. the attention-entropy sign-flip in AttDis).

Limitations

What to watch
  • Domain-locked — taxonomy, hooks, and backbone are 3DGS-specific; a new subfield means rebuilding the substrate.
  • Big gains sit on new OOD scenes vs vanilla 3DGS; on mature benchmarks improvements are sub-dB, sometimes within noise.
  • Writing lags humans — Presentation 3.0 vs 3.8 (human judge); overall verdict borderline accept.
  • True cost understated — hand-built backbone engineering is uncounted; ~113M tokens and 24–48 h per run strain small labs.
  • Self-evaluation leans on small samples and LLM judges with pro-AI bias; autonomy beyond 48 h untested.

Questions for further exploration

Open threads
  1. Give AI Scientist-v2 or PaperOrchestra the same verified Splatify backbone — is the gain from the system or the substrate?
  2. Can claim-to-log binding and the standing falsifier generalize as a lightweight layer over other autonomous-research pipelines (ML, RL) without a per-field backbone?
  3. Do (B, L)-conditioned Elo and Pareto promotion scale past eight islands, or does the tournament saturate as the design space widens?
  4. Will follow-on runs discover mechanisms outside the seven-category taxonomy, and does the open category close the gap?
  5. At 10× compute, do the soundness and presentation gaps to human papers close — and at what token cost?
