> **一句话总结：** 把 Transformer MLP 通道视为"读者 u_h（何时激活）—写者 v_h（写回什么）"的一对，用预归一化分支 Jacobian 的对称性得分和带符号配对坐标 m_h=⟨ū_h, v_h⟩ 度量二者的耦合：在 FineWeb-EDU 上自训的 28.9M–405M 模型及 OLMo-1B/GPT-2/Pythia 检查点中，每层按 |m_h| 排序的前 16 个核心通道有 98.5% 呈"反向"（写者指向读者响应方向的反方向；初始化时仅约一半），该符号在训练最初几百步、耦合幅度仅达终值几个百分点时即按跨种子共享的固定深度顺序形成，且只在保留语料词序时出现；翻转每层 16 个反向写者使输出向高频常见 token 偏移 0.52（中心化对数词频），相对扰动匹配的对照翻转产生 0.20 nats/token 的额外损失。

> **TL;DR:** An SNU interpretability paper asks whether the reader→writer binding inside each transformer MLP channel is itself learned, measuring it with the exact (square) Jacobian of the pre-norm block update and a signed pairing coordinate m_h = ⟨ū_h, v_h⟩. On models trained from scratch on FineWeb-EDU (tiny 28.9M / small 123.6M / medium 405.3M, 3 seeds each) plus released checkpoints (OLMo-1B 4B→3.1T tokens, GPT-2, Pythia), every trained seed's Jacobian symmetry score exceeds all 999 random writer re-pairings (p ≤ 0.001) while initialization sits inside the re-pairing range; 98.5% of each block's top-16 core channels are opposed (writer > 90° against its own reader) vs roughly half at initialization, and negating 16 opposed writers per block shifts outputs toward common tokens by 0.52 in centered log-frequency at 0.20 nats/token excess loss over activity- and size-matched flips. This is a diagnostics/training-dynamics paper — no downstream benchmark gains are claimed — but its census (71.3% of medium-model channels are "stream couples" read by later blocks rather than the logits) challenges the standard vocabulary-lens reading of MLP neurons.

## 核心贡献

- **提出"耦合对"（couple）视角：** MLP 通道的读者 u_h 与写者 v_h 通过共享索引绑定，而任何只看单侧因子集的统计量（范数、奇异值、直方图）对写者重配对不变——即传统逐因子分析在结构上看不见配对信息。本文首次把"整个分配方案"本身作为估计对象。
- **算子级工具：** 利用 MLP 写回它所读的残差流这一事实，分支更新的精确 Jacobian 是方阵；其对称–反对称分解给出对称性得分 C(J)，其迹分解出每个通道的带符号配对坐标 m_h = ⟨ū_h, v_h⟩（读者面板均值与写者的内积）。
- **重配对置换检验：** 固定两组因子、只打乱写者分配（B=999 次），配合初始化时可交换性校准和"共同置换保持 Jacobian 不变"的精确阴性对照，构成有限样本有效的检验。
- **反向核心现象：** 训练后每层最强的 16 个耦合通道几乎全部反向（写者对抗自己的读者）；该符号在训练最初几百步、耦合增长之前形成，跨种子共享固定的深度顺序，且仅在保留语料词序时出现（打乱词序或 Zipf i.i.d. 流上核心和归零、损失停在 unigram 熵）。
- **通道普查（census）：** 对 medium 三个种子的每个通道读"触发语境—写者返回—后继读者—logit 推动"四位一体：71.3% 是被后层读取的"流耦合"，只有 8.1% 直接作用于下一 token logits，词表投影只能描述十二分之一的通道。
- **干预实验：** 训练中途置换写者后，写者向读者原有配对回生而读者几乎不动；翻转反向写者使输出系统性偏向常见 token；等扰动匹配下破坏对称性更多的重配对损失更高。

## Key Contributions

- **A couple view of MLPs.** The reader–writer correspondence is exposed by comparing the trained assignment against bijective re-pairings of the *same* factor sets — a quantity invisible to any marginal (one-factor-set) statistic by the permutation symmetry of the parameterization.
- **An operator instrument.** Because a pre-norm MLP block writes back into the residual stream it reads, its exact update Jacobian is square; its symmetric–skew split yields a symmetry score C(J), and its trace yields the signed per-channel pairing coordinate m_h = ⟨ū_h, v_h⟩ that ranks a block's "core" (top-16 by |m_h|).
- **Opposed strongest couples.** Trained assignments carry symmetry far beyond any re-pairing, and the strongest couples are opposed — each writer points against the direction its reader responds to — recursing from 28.9M models trained from scratch up to released OLMo-1B/GPT-2/Pythia checkpoints.
- **Formation before growth.** The sign appears within a few hundred steps at only a few percent of final coupling magnitude, block-by-block in a depth order shared across five GPT-2 seeds, and does not form on order-shuffled or Zipf i.i.d. token streams.
- **A census of couples.** Every channel of the three medium seeds is read as a couple under a frozen taxonomy: most act through the readers of later blocks (stream couples), few reach the logits, and flipping opposed writers shifts outputs toward common tokens.

## Methodology

The paper trains pre-LayerNorm GELU decoders with RoPE (AdamW, bfloat16) for next-token prediction on FineWeb-EDU at three scales — tiny 28.9M (ablations), small 123.6M (tied embeddings), medium 405.3M (untied) — with three seeds per setting and 20 tokens/parameter for the two larger sizes. Corpus controls use TinyStories, C4 RealNewsLike, Gutenberg, order-shuffled web text, and frequency-matched Zipf i.i.d. tokens; a parameter-matched SwiGLU family (123.5M) is trained alongside GELU; released models (OLMo-1B across 24 public checkpoints from 4B to 3.1T tokens, Pythia, replicated GPT-2 small) extend the training histories.

The measurement core has four parts. (1) *The update against its transpose:* the symmetry score C(J) = (‖S‖²_F − ‖K‖²_F)/(‖S‖²_F + ‖K‖²_F) with S, K the symmetric/antisymmetric parts; factorization splits it into a paired-channel term D and a remainder O. (2) *The re-pairing test:* with both factor sets fixed, B = 999 writer permutations define a finite-sample-valid upper-tail test (p̂ with +1 correction) of assignment exchangeability; initialization provides the calibration (channels are i.i.d. there) and the common permutation — which preserves every Jacobian exactly — provides an exact negative control. (3) *Seen as pairs:* the trace of the panel-mean pre-norm branch Jacobian decomposes as Σ_h m_h with the effective pairing coordinate m_h = ⟨ū_h, v_h⟩ (ū_h averages the reader — including activation derivative and LayerNorm Jacobian — over an evaluation panel); m_h < 0 means the channel is "opposed," m_h > 0 "amplifying"; a block's core is its top-16 by |m_h|. A cheaper weight-level coordinate m_w,h = ⟨k_h, v_h⟩ is validated against the effective one. (4) *The vocabulary lens and census:* centered writers are projected through the unembedding with k ∈ {0,1,4,8} leading spectral filters removed; each channel's firing contexts, returns to its own and later readers, and logit pushes are labeled jointly under a taxonomy frozen before application. Interventions complement observation: writer permutations at saved training steps with resumed training (regrowth), writer sign flips vs activity-/size-matched comparators, re-pairings matched on weight/Jacobian/output disturbance, and removals of the reader-parallel vs perpendicular writer component.

## Results & Highlights

- **The pairing is learned, not incidental:** on the medium 405M model trained on FineWeb-EDU, 83.2% of all channels and 98.5% of core (top-16 by |m_h|) channels lie on the opposed side of the 90° reader–writer line, versus roughly half at initialization; every trained seed's symmetry score exceeds all B = 999 sampled writer re-pairings (p ≤ 1/1000) while initialization lies inside the re-pairing range and cross-seed combinations stay near it.
- **The block update is one-sidedly contracting:** from medium block 5 through block 21, more than 90% of the symmetric part's eigenvalue energy sits on negative eigenvalues, where random re-pairing restores an exactly one-half split; OLMo-1B stays above 0.97 until its final two blocks, and only OLMo-1B's last block (where the core flips amplifying) and the small model's first block fall below one half. Mid-stack, ⟨J, J⊤⟩ runs about 100× its re-pairing spread, yet only ~one-third comes from couples' own Σ_h m²_h — the rest is mutual (channels reading each other's writers).
- **Flipping opposed writers moves outputs toward common tokens:** negating 16 opposed writers per block in the medium model shifts output by 0.52 in expected centered log-token-frequency and costs 0.20 nats/token excess loss over activity- and size-matched flips, while amplifying-writer flips give no resolved frequency shift.
- **Writers regrow around readers; loss does not wait for the pairing:** after permuting writers (with optimizer moments) at saved steps and resuming training, the loss gap closes nine-tenths before writers are halfway to their plateau; at the three earlier breaks the endpoint loss lands within 0.004 nats of the control while writers stop at cosine 0.2–0.6 with the control writer — a different assignment reaches the same loss. The pairing gap itself stands out within the first ~100 steps, and loss only comes to depend on the reader-parallel writer component between steps 1,000 and 2,000.
- **Sign formation precedes coupling growth and needs token order:** on released trajectories each interior block's strongest pairs turn opposed within a few hundred steps at a few percent of final coupling size, in a fixed depth order shared across five GPT-2 seeds; on order-shuffled web text (unigram counts kept) and on a frequency-matched Zipf i.i.d. stream the core sums end near zero with loss at unigram entropy, while web, stories, news, and books corpora all form the opposed core at depths {4,6,8,12}.
- **Coupling is concentrated:** half of a block's squared pairing coordinate sits in just 133–471 of the medium model's 4,096 channels per block, and the equal-disturbance re-pairing test shows symmetry-damaging re-pairings cost 4–9 millinats more loss per seed (positive in every seed, significant after Holm correction in all six tiny seeds).
- **The census reassigns most MLP work away from the logits:** 71.3% of medium channels are stream couples (contribution returns against their own reader and is taken up by later readers), only 8.1% are logit couples (five-sixths of them in the last quarter of the stack), 7.8% are subject-fixed and 10.0% nudges; a contribution keeps three-fifths of what the residual path predicts four blocks on and one-fifth twelve blocks on. The norm-product top-16 criterion captures only two of the paper's four showcased core channels, whereas the |m_h| census reads all four.
- **Weights-only sign reading transfers to elementwise but not gated models:** the weight-level and effective coordinates agree on 99.2–99.3% of channels at the small scale and 96.3% in medium GELU models, but gated SwiGLU/GEGLU families lose agreement in the input quarter (OLMo-1B's agreement decays from 0.80 at 4B tokens to 0.17 at its final state as closed-gate channels grow).

## Authors & Credibility

Giyeong Oh, Sunghee Ahn, and Youngjae Yu are at Seoul National University. This is a 50-page preprint (arXiv:2610.29340v1, 21 Oct 2026, cs.LG) with ~40 pages of appendices (A–J) backing every main-text claim with per-seed, per-block detail. Methodological credibility is high: decision rules and the census taxonomy were fixed before outcomes were read, permutation tests use the finite-sample-valid inclusive-tie +1 construction with Holm correction, the common permutation serves as an exact negative control, and initialization/cross-seed combinations calibrate the test. Results replicate across three seeds per setting, three trained scales, four natural corpora, and released checkpoints (OLMo-1B's public 24-checkpoint trajectory, Pythia, five replicated GPT-2 small seeds). The authors disclose extensive generative-AI assistance (writing, search, experiment automation, LM-based census labeling) in an AI-use statement while stating that hypotheses, analysis plans, and claims were fixed and reviewed by the authors; a reproducibility statement documents protocols and estimands, though no public code release is referenced in the main text.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** Genuinely new estimand. Prior joint readings — Gurnee et al. (2024)'s same-index input–output cosines, Gerstner et al. (2026)'s gated weight-level cosines against mismatched pairs — read pairs individually; this paper is the first to test the *whole assignment* against its bijective re-pairings and to separate the paired-channel term D from the mutual remainder O in the symmetry score. The signed coordinate m_h (rather than squared cosines) is what recovers polarity.
- **Rigor:** Strong. Exact negative control (permutation invariance), exchangeability calibration at init, pre-registered thresholds, matched-comparator interventions (activity-/size-matched flips, equal-disturbance re-pairings), and honest separation of statistics an intervention does and does not establish.
- **Functional significance is the weak point, and the authors partly admit it:** the equal-disturbance loss effect is only 4–9 millinats, and after writer-permutation breaks a *different* assignment reaches the control loss within 0.004 nats — the pairing is statistically far from random yet functionally near-interchangeable. Their own Appendix H reports within-family writer re-pairings beat writer removal on only 2 of 16 class-axis contrasts with probability shifts around 10⁻⁵, so the suggested pruning/editing applications remain speculative rather than demonstrated.
- **Scale and architecture coverage are limited:** the largest model trained is 405.3M and the largest released is 1B; the weights-only diagnostic — a headline selling point — demonstrably fails for the gated SwiGLU/GEGLU family that dominates modern LLMs (OLMo-1B agreement 0.80 → 0.17 over training), pushing those cases onto the more expensive effective-reader machinery.
- **The central "why" is left open:** the paper explicitly says why training settles into opposed cores "we leave open," and the order-shuffled control conflates removal of token order with collapse to unigram-entropy loss — the authors concede that which property of ordered text the sign follows "stays open."
- **Census labels are LM-generated:** the 71.3%-stream/8.1%-logit taxonomy shares rest on language-model annotation under a frozen protocol, inheriting label noise that is mitigated (protocol, reporting rules) but not eliminated; no human-validated subset is reported.

## Strengths

- Turns a structural blind spot (permutation invariance of marginal neuron statistics) into a clean, well-calibrated measurement with an exact negative control.
- Cross-scale, cross-corpus, cross-family replication — 28.9M to 405M trained plus OLMo/GPT-2/Pythia released trajectories — makes the opposed-core phenomenon hard to dismiss as an artifact.
- Temporal resolution of emergence (sign within a few hundred steps, shared depth order across five GPT-2 seeds, before coupling grows) is a genuinely informative training-dynamics finding.
- Interventional rather than purely observational: regrowth, flips, equal-disturbance matching, and parallel/perpendicular removal each isolate a distinct claim.
- The census quantifies how misleading the standard vocabulary-lens reading is (it describes 1 channel in 12) — a practical correction for interpretability practice.

## Limitations

- Model scale tops out at 405M trained / 1B released; behavior at 7B+ and in instruction-tuned or RLHF models is untested.
- Gated architectures (the modern default) break the cheap weights-only sign reading.
- No causal account of *why* opposed cores form; the mechanism question is explicitly deferred.
- Functional effects of the pairing itself are tiny (millinats), weakening near-term applications to pruning/model editing.
- No downstream-task evaluation; all evidence is next-token-prediction diagnostics on English web-like corpora.
- LM-assisted census labeling introduces unquantified label noise; no public code artifact is cited.

## Relevance

本文 squarely 落在"LLM Hidden States & Interpretability"兴趣区：它研究 Transformer MLP 内部表示的几何结构（读者-写者配对坐标、Jacobian 对称谱），是 mechanistic interpretability 与训练动力学的交叉工作；其对 SAE/transcoder 字典 latent（本身也是编码行-解码列的配对）的推广建议，与表示几何分析的工具箱直接相关。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the opposed core persist at 7B–70B scale, and does instruction tuning / RLHF reshape the reader–writer sign distribution or only the coupling magnitudes?
- Do sparse-autoencoder latents and transcoders — themselves encoder-row/decoder-column pairs — inherit the opposed sign of the couples they replace, as the authors propose to test?
- Given that half the squared coupling concentrates in 133–471 of 4,096 channels, can core-aware pruning match Wanda-style magnitude pruning at equal sparsity on downstream benchmarks?
- Which property of ordered text drives the sign — syntactic structure, topical coherence, or local repetition — and would a syntactically shuffled but n-gram-preserving control preserve the opposed core?
- Is the opposed, contracting core related to residual-stream norm control and attention-sink dynamics, i.e., is the MLP bank acting as a learned stabilizer (damping) of the stream it reads?
- Since the mid-stack symmetry is mostly *mutual* (channels reading each other's writers), is there a low-rank reciprocal circuit structure that a graph-level analysis could extract from the re-pairing statistics?
