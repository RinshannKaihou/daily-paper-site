Why this matters

FP4 training is coming. This quantifies how its numerical noise destabilizes training, what actually repairs it, and why kernel-level metrics mislead end-to-end outcomes.

Critical edge

The decisive 0.007 delta carries no seeds or error bars — the paper's own Table 5 BF16 rows differ by 0.006, the same magnitude as the entire claimed effect.

from Critical Assessment · "No statistical rigor"

Methodology — the recipe, distilled

GPT-OSS-20B pretrained from scratch on C4 under the MLPerf Small MoE protocol: 8,192-token sequences, global batch 16, AdamW, LR 4×10⁻⁴ cosine→4×10⁻⁵, validation-loss target 3.34 checked every 768 iterations. MXFP4 covers all Linear and grouped-expert layers; lm_head and MoE router gates stay high-precision (routing is perturbation-sensitive).

01

Hybrid 1D/2D blocking

Activations in 1D 32-element blocks; weights in 2D 32×32 blocks (beyond the MX spec) so one layout serves both forward and backward without double quantization.

02

Randomized Hadamard transform

Applied to the weight-gradient path only — orthogonal, so the expected gradient is unchanged while outliers are spread across the block.

03

Stochastic rounding

On gradients only (unbiased in expectation); forward weights/activations keep deterministic rounding.

04

Weight de-oscillation — the new piece

A pure AdamW post-step hook: over a P=200 window, elements whose quantized travel exceeds 4× their FP travel (dQ/dw > τ=4.0) snap to their bin center. Enabled at step 2,000 — earlier and more aggressive than Tetrajet-v2. No model surgery, no extra GEMMs.

Faithful numerics, zero speedup: MI300X lacks native MXFP4 GEMM, so every FP4 op is simulated by quantize–dequantize round-trips on BF16 GEMMs. Bandwidth/compute gains are theoretical, not measured.

Results — beyond the headline

  • Full recipe reaches 3.3350 vs BF16 3.3283 (+0.007); without de-oscillation 3.3418 (+0.014). The de-oscillated MXFP4 run also finishes ahead of NVFP4+RHT+SR.
  • The benefit is concentrated late in training — curves separate only near the final checkpoint, matching oscillation's accumulation.
  • Operator-level SNR wins do not transfer: macro-block scaling's +1.16 dB forward SNR buys only −0.004 loss; DGE and dynamic clipping look benign at kernel level yet cost +0.201 / +0.181 end-to-end.

Strengths

  • Real scale, auditable protocol — 20B MoE trained from scratch on MLPerf Small MoE (C4, fixed 3.34 target), not a toy proxy.
  • Honest negative results — DGE, dynamic clipping, low-rank compensation documented with full numbers; the SNR-vs-convergence gap is an actionable practitioner lesson.
  • Clean two-level methodology — synthetic outlier-stressed kernel SNR + end-to-end convergence + steps-to-target; explicitly separates validated numerics from unmeasured speed.
  • Practically deployable hook — no model surgery, no extra GEMMs, modest optimizer memory, and it satisfies MLPerf closed division's exact-AdamW rule.

Limitations

  • Everything is simulated — quantize–dequantize on MI300X; no native FP4 hardware, no wall-clock/memory/bandwidth numbers, unfused kernels. The efficiency premise stays unvalidated.
  • 1.3% of the horizon — 16,128 steps of a 1.2M-step schedule, one model, one dataset, one vendor, GBS=16. Long-run behavior (where oscillation grows) is unestablished.
  • "Closed" means halved — +0.007 remains; no downstream-task evaluation confirms loss parity equals quality parity.
  • Tuning opacity — single point for τ=4.0, P=200, step 2,000 (no sensitivity study); no seed variance anywhere.

Open questions this paper leaves

  • Does the +0.007 gap persist, shrink, or explode over the full 1.2M-step schedule and production batch sizes?
  • Is τ=4.0 safe, or does it snap healthy weights — given Tetrajet-v2 chose a far more conservative τ=16?
  • Is the 0.007 effect outside seed noise, when BF16 baselines themselves differ by 0.006?
  • Do simulated-numerics conclusions transfer to native FP4 hardware (MI355, NVFP4 GPUs) once kernels are fused?
  • Can macro-block scaling's +1.16 dB SNR gain ever convert into an end-to-end win?
  • Does validation-loss parity at 3.34 imply parity on downstream tasks and final model quality?
