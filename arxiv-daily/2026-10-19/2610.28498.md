> **一句话总结：** 用最小对比对（minimal pairs，仅差一个数字的提示）证明：在 Ouro-1.4B（循环 transformer）和 Qwen2.5-1.5B 的"答案起始位置"，一个从题目原样复制来的数字确实存在于跨题目共享的编码中（读出约 0.9），但常规单提示探针几乎读不出来（Ouro 0.36 / Qwen 0.19，即使训练了 5,249 道题）——失败探针不等于信息缺失；对两步算术题的预注册研究进一步发现，未写出的中间值 a+b 有一个随运算符号变化的小信号，但将其钳制到另一题的值并不能改变模型写下的数字（答案钳制却能改变 91%），即"可读但不被使用"。

> **TL;DR:** Probing studies that read a model's state where the answer begins and treat a failed probe as "the value is absent" are using a broken instrument. With minimal pairs (prompts differing in one digit) on GSM8K-style prompts, a digit copied from the question is demonstrably present at the last prompt token in both the looped model Ouro-1.4B (readout 0.92–0.97) and the standard Qwen2.5-1.5B (0.75–0.98, mean 0.88), yet the standard single-prompt probe used by prior negative-result work (e.g., Lu et al. 2025's Huginn study) reads it at only 0.15–0.23 with tens of templates and still only 0.36 (Ouro) / 0.19 (Qwen) after 5,249 training questions (chance 0.10) — the cause is swamping, not absence: the digit's change is small, not crowded. A five-part protocol (copy ceiling, prime modulus, every input combination, sign-flipped twins, synthetic mixing null) applied to two-step a+b−c word problems finds a small sign-following signal for the unwritten intermediate a+b at the position just before the answer's units digit (operation-swap statistic I = +0.157, CI [0.141, 0.172]), roughly half of which generic mixing reproduces; and causal clamping shows the value is readable but not used — clamping the answer moves the written digit in 91% of pairs while clamping a+b moves it in at most 4%.

## 核心贡献

- **证明"探针失败 ≠ 信息缺失"：** 在摘要位置（答案开始处），一个从题目逐字复制的数字在 Ouro-1.4B 和 Qwen2.5-1.5B 中都以跨题目共享的编码存在（最小对比对探针读出 0.92–0.97 / 平均 0.88），但常规单提示探针只能读到 0.12–0.24；即使训练集扩到 5,249 道 GSM8K 训练题，也只能达到 Ouro 0.36、Qwen 0.19（随机水平 0.10）。因此此前"探针读不出中间值 ⇒ 模型没有潜推理"的否定性结论证据力很弱。
- **找到失败的真实原因——"淹没"而非"拥挤"：** 数字所在方向并不比其他任何变化（包括随机噪声）更繁忙；区别在于变化幅度。把数字换成随机词会让状态移动大得多且可读（Qwen 上 0.24–0.74），而等距的随机噪声与数字一样不可读（0.11–0.15）。这与神经科学中的 population coding 极限（Moreno-Bote et al. 2014）同构。
- **指出"探针成功"同样有歧义：** 每个被改变的 token（包括无关句子里的数字甚至颜色词）都会在摘要位置留下可读痕迹，因此读到"计算值"可能只是在读它的输入副本。
- **给出一套检验"计算值"的预注册协议：** 复制上限（copy ceiling）、素数模（mod 5）、对全部 31 种输入组合排序、符号翻转孪生对照（a+b vs a−b）、输出混合对照，以及用于校准的合成混合零假设。
- **对 Ouro 循环模型的实证结论：** 三项预注册研究发现未写出的中间值 a+b 在答案末位数字之前有小的、随运算符号变化的信号（换运算后 I = +0.157）；但因果钳制实验表明该位置的这个值"可读而不被使用"（答案钳制 91% 生效 vs a+b 钳制 ≤4%）。

## Key Contributions

- **A failed probe is weak evidence of absence.** At the summary position (where the answer begins), a digit copied verbatim from the question is present in both the looped Ouro-1.4B and the standard Qwen2.5-1.5B, in a code shared across questions — a minimal-pair probe that compares a question's ten digit variants reads it at 0.92–0.97 (Ouro) and 0.75–0.98, mean 0.88 (Qwen), while the standard single-prompt probe reaches only 0.15–0.23 (Ouro) and 0.12–0.14 (Qwen) with tens of templates, and still only 0.36 / 0.19 after 5,249 distinct GSM8K training questions (chance 0.10). Negative probing results in the latent-reasoning literature (Lu et al. 2025 on Huginn; Yu 2024) therefore rest on an instrument that cannot read even a copy.
- **The mechanism is swamping, not crowding.** The digit's subspace is no busier than a shuffled-label control (overlap ratio 0.83 in Ouro, 1.01 in Qwen) or than random noise (overlap 1.07× the digit's in Qwen); what differs is magnitude. A random word swapped into the digit's slot moves the state far more and reads at 0.24–0.74; random noise of equal starting distance arrives as small as the digit and is unreadable (0.11–0.15). The word swap's effect is almost entirely nonlinear — its linear prediction is orthogonal to the actual change and 4.5–15× too small — so what reaches the last token depends on which token is present, not on input distance.
- **Successful probes are ambiguous too.** Every varied token — an irrelevant number in an added sentence, even a colour word — leaves a readable trace at the same position in a code transferable across templates (a probe trained on needed numbers reads irrelevant ones at 0.78–0.97). A probe that "finds" a computed value may be reading the copied inputs it is a function of.
- **A five-part protocol for computed values:** (1) a copy ceiling via conditional probing on one-hot input copies (plus the written answer digit, and stricter, carry/borrow); (2) a prime modulus (mod 5) so shared parity structure cannot give controls free accuracy; (3) ranking the target against all 31 mod-5 combinations xa+yb+zc rather than chosen controls; (4) sign-flipped twins (a+b vs a−b), disentangled from fixed sign preference by swapping the operation; (5) mixtures of the output being written, calibrated against a synthetic null (random pairwise products, Fourier-feature products, a random one-layer network).
- **What it finds in Ouro, honestly reported.** A small, sign-following signal for the unwritten intermediate a+b at the token just before the answer's units digit — but roughly half of it is reproduced by generic mixing alone, and clamping/patching shows Ouro does not use that value to write the digit: "readable but not used."

## Methodology

The study reads hidden states (fp32, nothing trained; the author's forward pass matches Ouro's own with a largest absolute logit difference of 0) at the middle and end of each of Ouro-1.4B's four 24-layer loops, and after layers 4, 7, 14, 21, 28 of Qwen2.5-1.5B. Prompts are four fixed GSM8K training problems with their final answers, then the test question and "Answer:". The standard probe standardizes the state, projects onto 256 PCs fitted on training folds, and fits multinomial logistic regression (C ∈ {0.01, 0.1, 1}), scored on held-out folds grouped by template. Two positions are probed: P0 (last prompt token) and P1 (the answer token just before its units digit, with the gold answer fed in).

The core instrument is the **minimal pair**: ten copies of one question differing only in one number's last digit (137 families from 77 distinct questions); subtracting the family mean isolates the digit's trace. Because any computed value within a family is a fixed function of that digit, Section 4's protocol moves to a factorial design varying both operands' units digits, and measures every label as its excess over its **copy ceiling** — the same probe applied to one-hot codes of the inputs, so a state holding f(a)+g(b) cannot beat it. Labels are scored mod 5; controls include the sign-flipped twin, all 31 combinations of the digits, forms on the line through the answer (answer + k·c, since a+b = answer + c on units digits), and synthetic states that mix copies and the answer without any a±b term. Causal tests patch or clamp a 9-dimensional subspace (spanned by family-centred class means of a+b, a−b, or the answer, fitted on held-out templates) at P1, then read which digit Ouro writes; the answer patch is the positive control, a−b and random subspaces are negative controls, and same-c vs equal-answer pair designs separate an a+b code from an answer-plus-c mixture. Ten probing studies were pre-registered with frozen thresholds; code, pre-registrations, and freeze hashes are public on GitHub, and Appendix C tabulates the status (pre-registered / post-hoc / exploratory) and outcome of every analysis.

## Results & Highlights

- **Copied digit: present vs readable.** Minimal-pair probe at the last prompt token: 0.92–0.97 in Ouro and 0.75–0.98 (mean 0.88) in Qwen2.5-1.5B; standard single-prompt probe: 0.15–0.23 (Ouro) and 0.12–0.14 (Qwen). Scaling training data to 5,249 distinct GSM8K questions lifts the best single-prompt probe only to 0.36 (Ouro; +0.060 over the last doubling, still below its pre-registered 0.50 level) and 0.19 (Qwen; "partial"), while minimal pairs on held-out questions read 0.96 / 0.91 — presence and single-prompt readability come apart by a wide margin.
- **The sanity check prior work missed.** In a pre-registered study, the standard probe for the last digit of the largest number in the question (a value that must be copied) scored 40.8% — exactly the majority-guess rate.
- **Size, not crowding.** In Qwen, a random word in the digit's slot reads at 0.24–0.74 vs 0.12–0.14 for the digit and 0.11–0.15 for same-size random noise; overlap ratios sit at 0.68–1.24 of each change's own shuffled-label null. Digit input embeddings sit about half as far apart as random words (0.70 vs 1.37 in Qwen; 0.88 vs 1.54 in Ouro).
- **Ouro's loop gains are front-loaded.** Answer-only GSM8K accuracy (n = 200): 6.5% → 20.0% → 22.5% → 23.0% over loops 1–4 — +16.5 points [10.5, 22.5] overall, but only +3.0 [−1.5, +7.5] from loop 2 to 4, and the loop-1→2 jump also appears on non-reasoning benchmarks (HellaSwag 55.2→71.2→74.3; MMLU 41.2→60.4→67.5). On GSM-Symbolic variants accuracy rises 2.8%→9.2% (+6.4 points), below the pre-registered 8.25-point bar (labelled UNCLEAR). Huginn-3.5B stays at 5.5% even at 32 recurrences, matching Lu et al.'s ~4.9%.
- **The intermediate value a+b, against every control.** At P1 after loop 4, a+b's excess over its copy ceiling is +0.205 [0.172, 0.236] (Study 1, missed its 0.15 margin bar) and +0.219 [0.194, 0.244] (Study 2, margin +0.075 [0.052, 0.098] over answer+2c). It beats its sign-flipped twin a−b by +0.087 [0.069, 0.105] and +0.130 [0.105, 0.154] on all 24 templates. Study 3's operation swap (pre-registered) shows the better-read sign follows the needed operation: I = +0.157 [0.141, 0.172] (FOLLOWS), with only a small fixed preference for differences (F = +0.032); adding carry/borrow to the ceiling leaves I = +0.126 at P1 and −0.013 at P0.
- **But mixing explains about half.** Synthetic states containing only copies, the answer, and one generic mixture reproduce the sign-following statistic at I ≈ +0.09 on average (max +0.14) vs Ouro's +0.157, and top the answer line by as much as Ouro does (null margins +0.051/+0.078 vs Ouro's +0.036/+0.045) — so by the pre-registered rule the line test does NOT separate a held a+b from generic mixing.
- **Readable but not used.** Patching the answer's subspace at P1 after loop 3 moves Ouro's predicted digit to the partner problem's in 90.2% of pairs; patching a+b hits in 1.7% (statistic −0.015, NOT-USED). Clamping the answer from the ends of loops 1–3 moves the written digit in 91.4% of pairs; clamping a+b moves it in at most 4%, within 0.02 of the a−b and random controls. At P1, in the subspaces tested, the value is readable but not used.

## Authors & Credibility

Single author, Aur Shalev Merin, an independent researcher (no institutional affiliation). Despite the solo/independent status, the empirical hygiene is well above the norm for interpretability preprints: ten pre-registered studies with thresholds frozen in commits before runs, frozen file hashes, a public repository (github.com/Aur-Shalev-Merin/present-but-hard-to-read) with pre-registrations, per-item outputs, and a FREEZES.md ledger of when each freeze was pushed; 97.5% and Bonferroni-adjusted intervals resampling families; a harness check reproducing Lu et al.'s published Huginn number (5.5% vs their 4.9% at 32 recurrences); and a zero-logit-difference verification that the reimplemented forward pass matches Ouro's own. Appendix C openly tabulates which analyses were pre-registered, post-hoc-with-rule, or exploratory, and reports its own near-misses and reversals (e.g., the earlier mod-10 reading of Study 1 was revised). The work is an arXiv preprint (v2, cs.LG/cs.CL) and appears not yet peer-reviewed.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** Genuinely novel on two fronts. Minimal pairs / counterfactual contrasts are standard in interpretability (Vig et al. 2020; Stolfo et al. 2023; CCS by Burns et al. 2023), but applying them as a *presence test* to show that a standard probe fails on a demonstrably present copied value at a summary position is, per the related-work survey, new; and the composed protocol (copy ceiling + prime modulus + all 31 combinations + sign-flipped twins + output-mixture null) is a substantive methodological contribution beyond any single ingredient. The swamping analysis, connecting probe failure to information-limiting correlations in population coding, gives the field a correct mechanistic vocabulary ("small signal, not busy directions") where "superposition" would be the wrong frame.
- **Rigor:** High. Pre-registration with frozen bars, multiplicity control, resampling at the family/template level, positive and negative patch controls, equal-answer pairs designed so a parity code cannot fake a hit, and a calibrated synthetic null that actually adjudicates against the paper's own positive reading (the line test). The author repeatedly reports results that weaken the headline (Study 1 UNCLEAR; a−c not separable from a difference preference; b−c not behaving like a partial result after loop 3).
- **Weak spots the author concedes and a reader should weight:** (1) only two ~1.5B models, and the computation studies use Ouro alone; (2) the task is a narrow, two-form family (a±b−c, two-digit numbers) read at a position where the gold answer is fed in — P1 is close to the output, and a+b = answer + c as a function, so no probe can distinguish "first step formed" from "answer with c added back"; the paper says so explicitly; (3) the causal null is subspace- and position-limited — class-mean 9-dim subspaces at P1 only; an a+b held at other positions or in other codes would be missed, and the end-of-loop-1 patch was unscorable (later loops undo even answer patches, hit 0.009), so "not used" is scoped to "at that position, in the subspaces tested"; (4) Study 2's margin bar (0.025) was set after Study 1's near-miss of 0.15 — a forking-paths concession; several Section 5.2 analyses were post hoc without rules; (5) the single-prompt learning curves are still rising at 5,249 questions, so the presence/readability gap is a statement about data sizes tested, not a law; (6) the mixing null's third world (random one-layer network) could not be calibrated, and real states may mix differently; (7) the answer-ablation test has no positive control and scrambles output, making its null hard to interpret. None of these are hidden — the Limitations section is unusually candid — but they do bound the strength of "Ouro does not use a+b."
- **Reproducibility:** Strong — public code, pre-registrations, frozen hashes, per-item results, fixed checkpoints (Ouro-1.4B, Qwen2.5-1.5B are public models), deterministic probes. The one friction point is that pre-registration push records live partly in a private dev repo "available to reviewers on request."
- **Significance:** Mainly methodological and cautionary. It directly undercuts the evidential force of failed probes in prior latent-reasoning studies and provides a ready-made checklist; "readable but not used" is a crisp, well-controlled instance of the amnesic-probing distinction. It does not settle whether looped models do latent reasoning — and honestly does not claim to.
- **Red flags:** Solo independent authorship and non-peer-reviewed status are the only real credibility concerns; the artifact quality argues against them. One subtle overreach risk is rhetorical: the abstract's final clause ("the value is readable but not used") could be quoted beyond the paper's own scope qualifiers (one position, class-mean subspaces).

## Strengths

- Reframes a live debate (does Ouro/Huginn reason latently in loops?) by first validating the measurement instrument — and shows the instrument fails on a known-present value.
- Minimal-pair presence test is elegant: family-mean subtraction turns ten prompts differing in one token into a clean causal contrast, transferable across templates.
- The computed-value protocol is reusable and each component targets a specific confound (copies, parity structure, periodic number features, sign preference, output mixing).
- Exemplary pre-registration discipline and self-auditing (Appendix C); negative and ambiguous results are reported with the same prominence as positive ones.
- The causal section is properly controlled: answer patch as positive control, a−b and random subspaces as negatives, same-c and equal-answer pair designs, and clamping across multiple loops to defeat the "later loops undo patches" artifact.
- Connects the phenomenon to population-coding theory (information-limiting correlations) rather than defaulting to superposition language.

## Limitations

- Two small models only (~1.4–1.5B); computation studies on Ouro alone; no test at frontier scale.
- Narrow task family (a±b−c two-step word problems with two-digit numbers), and probing at P1 requires feeding in the gold answer, which limits ecological validity.
- The minimal-pair test needs all ten variants of a question — it is a presence test, not a decoder; single-prompt decoding gaps are quantified only up to 5,249 training questions with curves still rising.
- Patch/clamp nulls cover one position (P1) and class-mean subspaces; values held elsewhere, in other codes, or read via attention from the question sentence would be missed (the follow-up at the end of b's sentence was UNCLEAR, I ≤ +0.018).
- The synthetic mixing null covers three mixing kinds on 64-dim synthetic states; one could not be calibrated, so "half the sign-following is mixing" is an approximation.
- Study 2's threshold was set after Study 1's result; several key rankings (all-combinations, line tests) were post hoc.
- "Not used" cannot exclude that a+b was formed and consumed earlier and survives only as a by-product at P1 — the timing data (answer reads as early as a+b) keeps that door open.

## Relevance

这篇论文直接命中"LLM Hidden States & Interpretability"方向：它研究循环/标准 transformer 的隐状态可读性、探针方法学及其失效模式（swamping、copy ceiling、因果钳制），并给出可直接复用的预注册探针协议——对任何用探针或 logit lens 判断模型"内部是否真的在推理"的隐状态分析工作都是重要的方法论警示。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would the presence/readability gap close with 50k+ training questions, or is single-prompt decoding of small traces fundamentally data-limited at these model scales? What about much larger models, where context variance may be relatively smaller?
- Does the swamping analysis generalize to genuinely computed values (not copies) — e.g., can a minimal-pair-style design verify intermediate values in multi-hop factual or implicit-CoT tasks where Yu (2024) and Lu et al. (2025) reported nulls?
- Can alignment-search or distributed-alignment-search subspaces (rather than class-mean 9-dim subspaces) find an a+b code at earlier positions whose patching *does* move the answer — i.e., is "not used" an artifact of testing the wrong subspace?
- If generic mixing reproduces ~half the sign-following signal, what mechanism produces the other half — and does the looped architecture's re-derived-per-loop state (Wang & Reid 2026) prevent any early-loop intermediate from being causally efficacious at the output?
- Does the digit-vs-word asymmetry (token identity carried, small perturbations damped, word swaps nonlinear) point to a specific attention/MLP mechanism for "last-token token-identity transport," and can it be circuit-level localized?
- Do the GSM8K loop gains that concentrate at loop 2 — mirroring non-reasoning benchmarks — mean Ouro's loops mostly buy general state refinement rather than latent chain-of-thought, and could the copy-ceiling protocol be applied per-loop to test that directly?
