0.642→0.000

Accuracy once Ouro's loop-boundary norm is removed: from its 0.642 peak at the trained budget r₀ = 4 to zero within 2–3 further steps — while the released model still reads 0.583 at r = 32.

State norm moves only ×2.021 throughout — the failure is a decalibrated readout, not state divergence.

Why this matters为何重要

Intra-loop edits to deployed looped models — extra steps, KV-cache, quantization — can silently invalidate the readout; report extrapolation as budget multiples, not step counts.

Methodology

方法 · a two-half design

Two halves that deliberately separate scale (does it happen in released models?) from causality (does the normalisation cause it?).

  • Instrumented audit. Count calls to every normalisation module across forward passes at two loop counts — a module called more often as r grows sits inside the loop. Verdict: Ouro renormalises every step; Huginn only at readout.
  • Exact rearrangement. Restrict Ouro's model-level norm to the final loop step: zero new parameters, readout path bit-identical, identity at r = 1 — only the state re-entering the loop changes.
  • From-scratch causal arm. 19M–76M models (6–24 layers), boundary-norm flag fixed before training, 16 seeds per config, training seed as the statistical unit.
  • Tasks. Synthetic families with exact per-instance depth d*: circuit (Boolean evaluation, floor 0.500), hops (pointer chains, floor 0.072), paragg (parity). Single-token answers; separate generators and verifiers.
  • Measurements & stats. Readout entropy, top1−top2 logit margin, per-step state norm, fresh linear probes, Miller–Madow-corrected MI; exact McNemar + cluster bootstrap + Holm (released weights), exact Mann–Whitney + Cliff's δ (from scratch); dose-response arm applying the norm every 2nd/4th/8th step.

Cliff's δ up to 0.867 (p ≤ 1.1×10⁻⁵, survives Holm). On hops at K = 1: 0.979 vs. 0.556 (p = 2.3×10⁻⁶); at 2× budget δ = 0.844.

  • State scale is not the cause. Norm ratio stays ≤ 2.021 in the broken arms; the from-scratch no-norm arm lets its norm grow ×4.84 (366 → 1772) and holds accuracy — the opposite sign from the state-growth failure account.
  • The readout leaves the alphabet. Entropy 0.786 → 12.470 bits (≈ uniform over the vocabulary), then constant-token commitment (modal share 0.83); linear probes fall to chance.
  • Dose-response is a threshold, not a gradient. Norm every step: 0.642; every 2nd step: 0.496 (p = 3.1×10⁻⁹); every 4th or sparser: 0.000 — readout entropy 0.95 / 3.51 / 12.11 bits on a one-bit task.
  • Damage localised. Refitting unembedding + final norm only recovers 33% of lost accuracy and plateaus; also unfreezing the last recurrent-block layer (4.2M of 19M params) recovers 43% within 30 steps and 100% by 3 000. Caveat: at K = 1 the operation is an identity, yet a retrained head still gains +0.093 — part of the third is generic fine-tuning.
  • Overshoot confound fixed. At 24 layer-applications, comparing at equal loop count flatters looping; at matched overshoot (8× budget, equal compute) the looped-vs-stacked gap on circuit is non-detectable (0.907 / 0.918 / 0.828; δ = +0.33). On hops a gap survives (δ = 0.778, exploratory).

Strengths

优势 · what the paper does right

  • Surgical rearrangement with three built-in validity checks: only the re-entering state changes, the readout path stays bit-identical, and the operation is an identity at r = 1.
  • The two halves falsify complementary predictions — together they cleanly separate "normalisation is required" from "topologies must agree", which neither half could do alone.
  • Rich diagnostics (entropy, logit margin, state norm, probes, mutual information, format statistics) turn "accuracy dropped" into an identified two-stage failure narrative.
  • Statistical care well above the norm for this literature: seed-level units, exact nonparametric tests, Holm correction, cluster bootstrap, pre-registered gates.
  • The overshoot correction for looped-vs-stacked benchmarking (Sec. 4.5) is a genuinely useful methodological fix for anyone evaluating adaptive-compute models.

Limitations

局限 · read with these in mind

  • Scale and causality are split: sign of the effect at 1.4B–3.5B, causal magnitude only at 19–76M on synthetic tasks; paragg and the multi-cell hops grid are absent from the causal half.
  • Released-model competence is weak: hops misses the paper's own 0.30 gate (0.166 / 0.154); Huginn fails every family, leaving the add-norm direction untested on released weights.
  • Arg-max runs over the full vocabulary; the 0.000 reading partly reflects out-of-alphabet collapse — disambiguated by format statistics, not the headline number.
  • The pre-registered primary evaluation cell had to be swapped to tier-A cells (n = 800–900); direction and magnitude matched, sample size did not.
  • State norm reaches 1772 in the widest no-norm configuration — the claim is only that growth does not explain the failure, not that dropping the norm is safe at arbitrary budgets.

Questions for further exploration

待探索 · open threads

01Does the agreement contract hold at pretrained scale — would a from-scratch 1B+ run without boundary normalisation still extrapolate better?
02What does the last recurrent-block layer do that makes it, with the head, the sole locus of damage — and can an equivalent refit repair released models after intra-loop edits (KV-cache, quantization)?
03Would supervising intermediate loop steps (dense per-iteration losses) substitute for architectural agreement — can a readout be trained topology-robust?
04How does the loop-boundary axis interact with the orthogonal readout-normalisation axis of Sharma & Vu (2026) — is there a 2×2 design on the same checkpoints?
05Does matched-overshoot equivalence between looped and stacked depth persist with more seeds, and on natural-language benchmarks rather than synthetic tasks?
06Can the two-stage readout signature (entropy blow-up, then constant-token commitment) serve as an online detector that a deployed looped model's inference topology has drifted?
