> **一句话总结：** 本文证明主流文档转换器（docling、纯文本等）在把企业 Word 政策文档喂给 LLM 智能体时会静默丢弃作者用颜色和高亮编码的"强调"语义，使智能体在恢复作者标记要求（author-marked requirements）的任务上从 micro F1 0.690 崩塌到 0.150（docling）和 0.075（纯文本）且误报率升高 4 倍；逐线索消融把全部损失定位到高亮（+0.302）与颜色（+0.262）两种线索，而预注册实验表明把保留的线索再"类型化"标注给智能体还能额外带来 +0.085 macro F1。

> **TL;DR:** An Amazon team shows that formatting-blind document ingestion silently destroys the meaning enterprise authors encode in colour and highlight: on a 76-real-policy-document task (ground truth from OOXML run properties, deterministic scoring, no LLM judge), recovering author-marked requirements falls from micro F1 0.690 with their formatting-aware parser to 0.150 with docling and 0.075 with plain text, with docling's false-positive rate 4x higher (0.043 vs 0.011); a leave-one-out ablation attributes the entire effect to highlight (+0.302) and colour (+0.262) while bold/italic/monospace/strikethrough contribute nothing (±0.003), and on Microsoft's OCB File-Fidelity Word track their system scores 0.959 vs 0.810 for the strongest baseline docx2python (p < 10^-6). The headline typing effect (+0.085 over merely preserving cues, pHolm = 0.049) is borderline and replicates on only 3 of 6 answering models.

## 核心贡献

- **量化了格式盲（formatting-blind）文档摄取的代价**：在 76 份真实政策文档上构建"列出作者用红色/高亮 singled out 的要求"任务，真值直接取自 OOXML run 属性（非人工判断），用确定性词法匹配打分（连续 4 个实词命中即算检索成功），无 LLM 裁判。格式感知表示取得 micro F1 0.690，而 docling 只有 0.150、纯文本 0.075，且该差距在两个厂商的 6 个回答模型上全部复现。
- **把损失精确定位到两种线索**：span 级 leave-one-out 消融显示高亮（+0.302）与颜色（+0.262）承载了几乎全部效应（pHolm < 10^-4），而粗体、斜体、等宽、删除线各在 ±0.003 以内——把"格式很重要"细化为"颜色与高亮很重要"，且颜色恰是主流转换器不保留、ParseBench/OmniDocBench 等解析基准不评分的线索。
- **区分"保留线索"与"告诉模型线索含义"**：预注册（pre-registered）对照实验表明，在字节相同的内容上，仅展示 `[COLOR:#FF0000]` 不如再附上类型化角色（如 CRITICAL_WARNING），+0.085 macro F1（CI [+0.035, +0.145]，pHolm = 0.049）；论文如实报告该效应偏弱且仅在 3/6 模型上显著复现。
- **发布了验证过的评测工具**：对 6,462 份公开语料库 .docx 做 OOXML run 级扫描（32.8% 文档带刻意的颜色/高亮，10.3% 落在义务性条款上）；标签经渲染页面图像独立模态验证（κ = 0.72），并用有监督纯文本分类器给任务定了"仅靠措辞"的上界（ROC AUC 0.664）。
- **诚实报告失败与修正**：渐进式披露（progressive disclosure）没有降低 token 成本；最初只给黄色高亮赋义是错的（修正后作者标记段落获得 salience 角色的比例从 79.1% 升至 97.6%）；headers/footnotes/文档属性/修订追踪起初也被自家解析器静默漏掉，由外部基准暴露后才修复。

## Key Contributions

- **Quantifies the cost of formatting-blind ingestion on real documents**: a task built from 76 real policy documents asks the model to list every requirement the author singled out; ground truth comes from the file's own OOXML run properties and scoring is a deterministic 4-consecutive-content-word lexical matcher (no LLM judge). Their formatting-aware representation reaches micro F1 0.690 vs 0.150 (docling) and 0.075 (plain text), a gap that replicates across six answering models from two vendors (five Claude models across Opus/Sonnet/Haiku tiers plus GPT-5.6-terra).
- **Localizes the loss to exactly two cues**: a span-level leave-one-out ablation with byte-identical content attributes the whole effect to highlight (+0.302) and colour (+0.262), both pHolm < 10^-4, while bold, italic, monospace and strikethrough each sit within ±0.003 of zero. The ablation is cue-specific: removing colour collapses colour-marked documents from 0.699 to 0.214 micro F1 while leaving highlight-marked ones at 0.694 (and the mirror image for highlight, 0.659 to 0.128). Colour is precisely the cue no mainstream converter preserves and no parsing benchmark scores.
- **Separates cue preservation from cue typing with a pre-registered test**: showing the identical inline cue markers with vs without a typed role token differs by +0.085 macro F1 (CI [+0.035, +0.145], pHolm = 0.049, 13/4/62 sign test on 79 documents, 6 repeats) — a real but modest, borderline effect the authors report as such, including the full execution history (a parser defect forced one re-run; effect size was unchanged while p moved from 0.005 to 0.049).
- **Releases a validated evaluation instrument**: labels derived from OOXML markup, validated against rendered page images through an independent modality (κ = 0.72), with a supervised text-only classifier (TF-IDF + char n-grams, logistic regression) bounding what wording alone can achieve at ROC AUC 0.664 — urgency keywords (0.508) and sentence length (0.492) sit at chance.
- **Shows the failure is silent and precision-degrading**: every arm returns well-formed output that passes schema and token-count checks; an agent that cannot see the cue does not abstain but over-asserts — docling's false-positive rate on genuinely unmarked requirements is 0.043, roughly 4x theirs (0.011).

## Methodology

Stage 1 (the paper's subject) is entirely code-based with zero LLM calls: the parser reads .docx via python-docx, emits a flat sequence of blocks where every contiguous run of text with identical formatting becomes a FORMATTINGSPAN (12 attributes), and applies eleven high-confidence formatting-to-semantics mappings (red text/red highlight to CRITICAL_WARNING, any other visible highlight to WARNING, strikethrough to DEPRECATED, partially-bold spans to KEY_TERM, standalone fully-bold short lines to IMPLICIT_HEADING, etc.). Medium-confidence mappings (e.g., green text to status marker) are deliberately excluded because a wrong hint biases the agent worse than none. Disambiguation rules were corrected against corpus measurements — e.g., colour is matched by channel threshold (r > 180, g < 80, b < 80) rather than hex literal (though 99.7% of red spans turn out to be exactly #FF0000), and highlight is typed by presence rather than hue once cyan/grey highlighting of obligations proved common. Blocks are serialized as hybrid Markdown (what pretraining saw: `**bold**`, `~~struck~~`) plus custom tags (`[COLOR:#FF0000]`, `[HIGHLIGHT:YELLOW]`) plus structural tags (list membership, heading level, table headers) — 15–20% more token-efficient than pure custom tags. Crucially, roles attach at block level while spans carry raw inline markers, which is what makes the salience-vs-typing ablation possible over byte-identical content. The parser also walks content unreachable from a naive `w:body` walk: headers/footers (up to 3 per section), footnotes, document properties, OMML equations, and tracked changes (`w:del`/`w:delText`), where the accepted revision is the text actually in force. Evaluation is three-layered: (1) the 76–79-document real-document task with paired exact sign tests, Holm correction, and 10,000-resample bootstrap CIs over documents; (2) Microsoft's Office Comprehension Benchmark File-Fidelity Word track (326 items, 59 documents, Microsoft-written questions); (3) an equal-preprocessing test running the complete identical downstream pipeline per arm on 68 documents, varying only the parsed text. All experiments use Claude Opus 4.6 at temperature 0 unless stated; question generation and judging use different model families. For PDF, cues are recovered geometrically from character bounding boxes and evaluated on all 126 born-digital formatting rules of ParseBench. Model eligibility was decided by a pre-fixed competence screen on a formatting-free control document (macro F1 >= 0.80, FP rate <= 0.05); all six reported models pass, Mistral Large 3 (0.558 / FP 0.133) is reported out-of-population rather than discarded.

## Results & Highlights

- **Main task (76 documents, 3 repeats, 1,368 calls, paired per-document)**: micro F1 / precision / FP rate — OURS 0.690 / 0.824 / 0.011; OURS-untyped 0.605; docx2python 0.586; docling 0.150 / 0.196 / 0.043; plain text 0.075 / 0.437 / 0.005. The macro-F1 gaps of +0.578 (docling) and +0.689 (plain text) are pHolm < 10^-4; even over docx2python (the strongest, HTML-preserving baseline) the +0.110 gap is significant (pHolm = 0.022).
- **False positives, the autonomy-relevant direction**: docling's FP rate on genuinely unmarked requirements is 0.043 vs 0.011 for the typed representation — roughly 4x — because a system that cannot see the cue guesses; under two other answering models the same missing cue produces the opposite pathology (Claude Sonnet 5: recall 0.09 at precision 0.96).
- **Leave-one-out cue ablation**: highlight +0.302 (pHolm < 10^-5) and colour +0.262 (pHolm = 2e-5); bold, italic, monospace, strikethrough each within ±0.003 of zero despite a single document carrying over a thousand bold markers — common cues carry no intent signal.
- **Pre-registered typing test (79 docs, 6 repeats, 1,422 calls)**: typed cues add +0.085 macro F1 over byte-identical untyped cues (CI [+0.035, +0.145], pHolm = 0.049); the full representation beats docx2python by +0.107 (CI [+0.047, +0.174], pHolm = 0.014). Cross-model: typing replicates significantly on 3 of 6 models, is positive on 5, never significantly negative (e.g., Haiku 4.5 +0.108**, Sonnet 4.6 +0.085*, GPT-5.6-terra +0.019 ns).
- **Microsoft OCB File-Fidelity Word track (326 items)**: overall 0.959 (ours) vs 0.810 (docx2python), 0.720 (docling), 0.668 (unstructured), 0.634 (plain text), all deltas pHolm < 10^-6; on the 38 formatting-dependent items 0.816 vs 0.750, and on the 288 non-formatting items 0.977 vs 0.818; it is the only system that answers Metadata, Comment and Footnote items at all (1.000 each where every baseline scores 0.000).
- **Prevalence at corpus scale**: scanning 6,462 real policy .docx files, 32.8% carry deliberate red text or a non-white highlight, 10.3% on a modal-obligation sentence, and 2.6% carry cues only outside the body (headers/footers/footnotes/comments) where no tested baseline can see them.
- **Equal-preprocessing pipeline test (68 docs, 408 scored calls)**: with identical extraction prompt/model/rendering, micro F1 0.503 (ours) vs 0.258 (docx2python, +0.178, p = 0.0001) vs 0.084 (docling, +0.422, p < 10^-4) — while gold-requirement text retention is near-identical across arms (0.665 / 0.634 / 0.607), proving the gap is salience, not content loss; marked requirements reach the quick-reference file at 0.569 vs 0.245 for unmarked ones (2.3x enrichment).
- **PDF cross-format (ParseBench, 126 born-digital rules)**: theirs and pymupdf4llm both recover 65/126 cues while plain text recovers 0/126 despite locating 107/126 targets; the only statistically distinguishable per-cue difference is highlight, 11/18 vs 4/18 (exact McNemar p = 0.039).
- **Task ceiling**: a supervised text-only classifier reaches ROC AUC 0.664, so 0.690-level performance is not achievable from wording alone; the scorer's structural ceiling is 0.997 and the task is unsaturated (recall 0.60).

## Authors & Credibility

All three authors (Yikai Zhao, Saurabh Pandey, Pradeep Kumar Misra) are at Amazon, which fits the enterprise-agent deployment motivation, though the paper reads as research rather than a product report. Credibility is unusually strong for an evaluation paper: ground truth is taken from the files' own OOXML markup rather than author judgement; scoring is deterministic lexical matching with no LLM judge; question generation and second-opinion judging use different model families from the answering model; labels are cross-validated against rendered page images through an independent modality (κ = 0.72); the typing claim rests on a pre-registered design (hypotheses, arms, repeats, test and correction family committed to version control before execution) whose full execution history — including a parser defect that forced a re-run and made the headline p worse — is disclosed; a negative result (progressive disclosure did not reduce token cost) and an excluded model's numbers (Mistral Large 3) are reported rather than hidden. Scripts, item sets, per-document content hashes and raw result JSONs accompany the paper.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: genuine and well-scoped. Document-conversion benchmarks (OmniDocBench, ParseBench) score text/reading order/tables/formulas and at most bold-italic-strike presence; the closest refereed work (TexTAR, ICDAR 2025) covers bold/italic/underline/strikeout from images and stops at attribute detection — neither covers colour or highlight, which this ablation shows are the only load-bearing cues, and none measures downstream answer correctness. The framing as a *silent ingestion failure* that inverts the usual failure mode (more assertion, not less) is the paper's best idea.
- **Rigor**: high — paired per-document exact sign tests with Holm correction and bootstrap CIs, a pre-registered confirmatory experiment, an equal-preprocessing control isolating representation from pipeline depth, a supervised ceiling (AUC 0.664) that *measures* rather than asserts cue-dependence, and a competence screen for model eligibility fixed in advance. Weak spots: the headline typing effect sits right at the significance threshold (pHolm = 0.049) and replicates on only 3 of 6 answering models — the authors say so, but the abstract's "+0.085" still carries more weight than a borderline p and 3/6 replication warrant. Absolute micro F1 is stable only to about ±0.08 across configurations, so all claims must lean on the paired tests, which they do.
- **Reproducibility**: good on paper — deterministic scorer, code-only parser, content-hash-named corpus, released scripts/item sets/raw JSONs. However, the "public corpus" of 6,462 policy documents is not named in the main text beyond SHA-256 provenance, and the answering models are commercial APIs (Claude Opus 4.6, GPT-5.6-terra), so exact numbers will drift; the deterministic OOXML extraction and matcher are the reproducible core.
- **Significance**: the practical message — reliability is won or lost at ingestion, not reasoning, and the fix is deterministic, code-only and cheap (though ~2x plain-text tokens) — is actionable for anyone running document-consuming agents. The finding that 32.8% of real documents carry a deliberate colour/highlight and that the failure is invisible to pipeline health checks makes this a genuine production-reliability result.
- **Red flags**: relatively minor. The task was written by the same authors who built the parser being evaluated (mitigated by the external OCB benchmark, where questions are Microsoft's); the corpus prevalence of colour/highlight (32.8%) may not transfer beyond English-language policy/SOP corpora; the tracked-changes analysis is honest about being too small to carry a claim (n ≈ 35, 0.818 vs 0.833 forced-choice accuracy, ns); and the OCB win is partly mechanical — they are the only system that parses headers, footnotes, metadata and comments at all, so the 0.959-vs-0.810 overall gap bundles formatting-awareness with simply reading more of the file.

## Strengths

- Ground truth from the document's own OOXML markup plus a deterministic, LLM-free scorer — the result does not depend on judge-model goodwill.
- The leave-one-out ablation converts a vague "formatting matters" into a sharp, actionable claim (colour + highlight only), and the cue-specificity check (removing colour hurts only colour-marked documents) validates the manipulation itself.
- Pre-registration with full execution history, including a re-run that worsened their own p-value — a level of disclosure rare in this literature.
- Failure modes are honestly characterized: over-flagging in some models, extreme abstention (recall 0.09 at precision 0.96) in others, both invisible without ground truth.
- External validation on a benchmark they did not write (OCB) and a cross-format replication in PDF (ParseBench) with the same colour-class asymmetry.

## Limitations

- Labels are validated against rendered page images (κ = 0.72) but not yet by human annotators — the authors name this the most valuable missing addition, since OOXML markup can encode cues the author did not semantically intend.
- The typing benefit is model-conditional: significant on only 3 of 6 answering models, borderline significance overall (pHolm = 0.049), and absent on the two models that barely attempt the task.
- No OCR: scanned PDFs (cue survives as pixels, no text layer) are out of scope; 18 of ParseBench's 126 rules name text absent from the PDF text layer entirely.
- The representation costs roughly 2x plain-text tokens — a deliberate context-budget-for-factuality trade, but a real cost at 50–300-page document sizes.
- Exposure is common but not universal (about a third of documents), English-only so far (cue semantics are culturally inflected), and the internal task's documents skew toward policy/SOP prose where colour/highlight conventions are strongest.

## Relevance

这篇论文直接命中"Agent & Pipeline Silent Failures"方向：它研究的是模型与世界之间的软件层（文档摄取/解析管线）如何静默改变模型收到的信息，使智能体自信地答错而非明显缺失，并给出确定性、可度量的修复方案——正是长时运行自主智能体系统中静默失败检测与修复的核心关切。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would the colour/highlight dominance hold for non-policy corpora (contracts, specs, tickets), where bold-on-obligation conventions might differ, or is the cue hierarchy corpus-specific?
- Can a small supervised model map raw cues to typed roles per organization (learning department-specific colour semantics) instead of the hand-crafted 11 high-confidence rules, and would that close or widen the +0.085 typing gain?
- Does cue typing help *reasoning* models (which might re-derive intent from `[COLOR:#FF0000]` given chain-of-thought) as much as instruction-following models, or is the typing benefit purely an instruction-following phenomenon?
- How would the silent-cue-loss failure interact with RAG chunking — does chunk-level ingestion compound the loss, and can an ingestion-level assertion (recovered cue implies located span) be generalized into a standard pipeline health check?
- OCR-backed cue recovery for scanned pages: can geometric colour/highlight detection survive compression and annotation artefacts at the κ = 0.72 level the born-digital labels achieve?
