> **一句话总结：** 在 Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct 上用统一流水线交叉测试两种探针估计器（均值差/逻辑回归）×三种池化策略×三种安全行为（欺骗、高风险感知、谄媚），发现"逻辑回归读得最好、均值差写得最好"的读写分离规律对前两种行为成立但对谄媚反转，且反转主要源于数据构造（模板化回复只在结尾词上有差异）而非估计器本身。

> **TL;DR:** A single controlled pipeline on Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct crosses two probe estimators (mass-mean vs logistic regression) with three pooling schemes across three safety behaviours (deception, high-stakes awareness, sycophancy), evaluating every direction as both detector (AUROC) and steering vector (NIE + LLM judge). The "LR reads best / mass-mean writes best" dissociation from prior work (Marks & Tegmark 2024, ITI, CAA) holds for deception (OOD pooled: LR-mean 0.722 AUROC vs MM detection; steering: MM NIE +1.53 at coeff +2 vs LR +0.13) and high-stakes (Anthropic-HH OOD: MM steering +1.34 at +2 vs +0.32 for LR), but inverts for sycophancy on the math OOD set (LR-attention steers monotonically from −1.28 to +0.77 while MM dips to −0.92 at +1) — and the authors argue the inversion traces to the templated dataset (only the final answer token differs) rather than the estimators. Steering blocks (layers 6–15) sit well before detection layers (12/19/20), and the NIE readout and judged behaviour can disagree outright.

## 核心贡献

- **统一流水线下的受控比较**：在同一个模型（Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct）、同一套代码里，交叉两种估计器（均值差 MM、逻辑回归 LR）×三种池化（末词、均值、可学习注意力），覆盖欺骗、高风险感知、谄媚三种行为，每个方向同时按"检测器"和"转向向量"两种角色评估——此前各结论来自不同模型、行为、指标，无法区分是方法效应还是设定差异。
- **读写分离规律的行为依赖性**：逻辑回归读得最好、均值差写得最好（read/write dissociation）这一此前文献拼起来暗示的规律，在欺骗和高风险两种行为上成立，但在谄媚上反转（MM 末词检测最佳、LR-attention 转向最佳），且作者给出证据表明反转部分来自数据构造（谄媚回复是模板化的，类别间只有最后一个答案词不同，均值池化把信号稀释掉了）而非估计器本质。
- **池化与层选择的系统性结论**：最佳池化取决于信号在文本中的分布（高风险需要读整个对话→全词池化胜；谄媚差异在结尾→末词池化胜）；检测层随行为复杂度后移（欺骗 12 层、谄媚 19 层、高风险 20 层）；转向最优的层块（6–15 层）可以远早于检测最优层——只有欺骗两者重合。
- **两种转向度量 instrument 的分歧**：NIE 读出（模型对文本的"感知"）与 LLM 评判的生成行为（模型"表现"出的行为）可以完全背离——谄媚在系数 +1 处 MM 的 NIE 为负（−0.92 OOD）而评判得分达到峰值（9.42/10）。监控该看前者，干预该看后者。
- **可交互的透明度工具**：每种行为导出约 0.5 MB 的探针/方向包，驱动一个交互界面，用户可直接对任意文本逐 token 打分或对照生成"未转向 vs 已转向"回复，还支持双行为同时在不同层块转向。

## Key Contributions

- **A controlled factorial comparison in one pipeline.** Prior evidence for "logistic regression detects best but difference-of-means steers best" was scattered across different models, behaviours, metrics and token policies (ITI, Marks & Tegmark 2024, CAA, Goldowsky-Dill et al. 2025, McKenzie et al. 2025); this paper crosses two estimators × three pooling schemes × three behaviours on a single model (Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct) so the interactions, not the setups, explain the patterns.
- **The read/write dissociation is behaviour-dependent.** It holds for deception and high-stakes awareness (LR (mean) detects best OOD; MM steers best by NIE), but reverses for sycophancy, where MM (last) is the best detector and LR-attention is the best steering direction.
- **The sycophancy reversal is partially a data artefact.** Sycophantic vs candid replies share a template and differ almost only in the final answer token; mean pooling dilutes that single differing token, so MM (mean)/LR (mean) directions are diluted, while the attention probe learned to put 0.38 of its pooling mass on the echoed answer token — so the inversion may reflect "which directions the data favours," not the estimators themselves.
- **Layer findings.** Detection layer deepens with behavioural complexity (deception layer 12/32 at 37.5% depth, sycophancy 19, high-stakes 20 at 62.5%); the best steering blocks (contiguous 5-layer windows, layers 6–15 across behaviours) can sit 9–10 layers *before* the detection layer — reading and writing a behaviour are localized at different depths.
- **Two steering instruments measure different things.** The NIE readout (model's judgment of a transcript) responds to steering on every set, while LLM-judged generated behaviour is nearly flat in most OOD settings and can outright contradict the readout (sycophancy at coefficient +1: MM readout negative, judge at its 9.4–9.7 maximum). The metric to report depends on whether one is monitoring or intervening.
- **A practical interface.** Each behaviour exports a ~0.5 MB bundle (5 probes + 3 calibrated steering directions at every layer of the steering block) powering an interactive tool for per-token scoring and side-by-side steered generation; code, datasets and interface are public.

## Methodology

All experiments run on Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct in 8-bit on a single GPU (seed 42, greedy decoding, seed-fixed splits). Each behaviour is defined by one specification (class labels, token-mask policy, readout prompt, judge instruction, coefficient sweep) consumed by a shared pipeline.

**Data (contrastive pairs, with a "detection mask" covering only the tokens where the behaviour lives):**
- *Deception:* training on Instructed-Pairs (Goldowsky-Dill et al. 2025) — byte-identical facts under honest vs deceptive system personas; evaluation on AI Liar (Pacchiardi et al. 2024, 54 context-defined examples) and Roleplaying (Goldowsky-Dill et al. 2025, 742 content-defined examples).
- *High-stakes:* training on Models Under Pressure (McKenzie et al. 2025), 1,500/class with 500/class held out; evaluation on Anthropic-HH (2,984 real multi-turn conversations, GPT-4o labelled) — genuinely OOD against the synthetic single-turn training set.
- *Sycophancy:* training on disentangle-sycophancy (Vennemeyer et al. 2025), 1,368/class with 347/class held out; 29% of negatives are genuine agreements (to defeat an agreement shortcut); evaluation on the held-out math domain (1,000 examples).

**Probes.** Five probes = estimators × pooling: MM (last), MM (mean), LR (last), LR (mean), LR-attention. LR reproduces Apollo's hyperparameters (L2, λ=10) on normalized activations; MM is the closed-form difference of class means θ = μ+ − μ−. Attention pooling trains a query vector to softmax-weight the mask tokens, jointly with the LR head, three seeds averaged.

**Layer selection.** Detection layer = earliest layer maximizing 5-fold cross-validated AUROC of a mean-pooled mass-mean probe (swept over all 32 layers); steering block = contiguous 5-layer window with highest summed NIE when injecting the calibrated MM (mean) direction at coefficient +1. The authors disclose this anchoring as a mild bias toward MM.

**Steering.** Three directions are steered (MM (mean), LR (mean), LR-attention; last-token variants deliberately excluded). Each direction is calibrated so coefficient +1 moves the mean off-behaviour activation exactly onto the mean on-behaviour activation (Marks & Tegmark 2024), making effect curves comparable across directions with different norms; coefficients sweep {−2, −1, −0.5, 0, +0.5, +1, +2} following CAA. Each layer in the block gets its own refit direction (per van der Weij et al. 2024) rather than one shared vector.

**Evaluation.** Detection: AUROC with bootstrap 95% CIs (threshold-free, so comparable across estimators and ID/OOD). Steering: (1) the normalised indirect effect (NIE) — P(YES)−P(NO) shift on a few-shot YES/NO readout of the transcript, normalized by the full class gap; (2) judged open-ended generation — Claude Haiku 4.5 scores 24 generations per (direction, coefficient) pair, 1–10 on behaviour and separately on coherence, following CAA's protocol.

## Results & Highlights

- **Deception detection (ID, Instructed-Pairs):** three of five probes hit AUROC 1.000 (layers 12–13 both at 1.000 cross-validated); OOD pooled across AI Liar + Roleplaying, LR (mean) leads at 0.722 vs LR (last) trailing at 0.571 — but the OOD sets split sharply: on AI Liar every probe except LR (last) exceeds 0.95 (MM (mean) 0.992) while on Roleplaying the best is LR (mean) at 0.706, versus 0.964 for Apollo's LR probe on Llama-3.3-70B (a scale gap of ~0.26).
- **Deception steering (ID):** mass-mean produces the expected monotone NIE response — +1.53 at coefficient +2 and −0.23 at −2 — while logistic regression barely moves on the positive side (+0.13 at +2) and runs the wrong way on the negative side (+0.37 at −2); the MM > LR-attention > LR ordering holds on both OOD sets.
- **High-stakes detection (OOD, Anthropic-HH):** every probe drops 0.20–0.28 AUROC vs ID (LR-attention 0.996 ID → 0.755 OOD); every-token probes dominate (LR (mean) 0.774, LR-attention 0.755, MM (mean) 0.737) with last-token probes last (0.687/0.686), matching McKenzie et al.'s finding that high-stakes labelling "often depends on every token of the conversation."
- **High-stakes steering (OOD, Anthropic-HH):** MM dominates with NIE +0.23 / +0.72 / +1.34 at coefficients +0.5 / +1 / +2, versus +0.68 for LR-attention and +0.32 for LR at +2; judged behaviour moves strongly ID (2.46 at −2 → 4.08 unsteered → 7.54 at +2 for MM) but stays flat OOD (2.38 vs 1.67 baseline at +2) — steering shifts self-report without shifting the generated conversation.
- **Sycophancy — the inversion:** MM (last) detects best on both sets (0.976 ID, 0.879 OOD-math) yet steering flips the ordering to LR-attention > MM > LR: the attention direction is the only monotone readout (−0.75 → +0.81 ID; −1.28 → +0.77 OOD over the ±2 sweep) while MM dips sharply at coefficient +1 (−0.53 ID, −0.92 OOD) before recovering at +2.
- **Instrument disagreement at its starkest:** at sycophancy coefficient +1, MM's NIE readout is negative while the judge spikes to 9.71/10 ID and 9.42/10 OOD from unsteered baselines near 1.0; MM's judged coherence simultaneously collapses (9.67 at +0.5 → 2.88 at +1 → 1.00 at ±2, ID), reproducing CAA's "large multipliers degrade text quality."
- **Layer separation:** steering blocks are early (layers 6–10 for both high-stakes and sycophancy; 11–15 for deception) while detection layers are mid-to-late (12/19/20) — for high-stakes the detection layer sits ten layers *after* the steering block, so the site that best writes a behaviour is far from the site that best reads it.

## Authors & Credibility

Nimun Bajwa and Massimo Poesio are at Queen Mary University of London; Poesio is a well-known professor working on NLP/anaphora/annotation. The acknowledgment thanks "the ARENA 3.0 interpretability" programme, indicating the work grew out of a structured training programme / student project under faculty supervision — credible as careful empirical work, but not a large-lab industrial study. The paper is unusually transparent: limitations are itemized and self-critical (echo-shortcut untested, unvalidated judge, MM-anchored layer selection), the exact hyperparameters of prior work are reproduced deliberately (Apollo's λ=10 LR; CAA's coefficient sweep; Marks & Tegmark's NIE and calibration), and code, datasets, and the interactive interface are released at github.com/NimunB/probing-safety-behaviours. Claims are consistently calibrated to the evidence, with scale (8B vs 70B) offered as a *plausible*, explicitly-to-be-tested explanation for gaps to reference results rather than asserted.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** Moderate but genuine. Individual ingredients (MM vs LR probes, CAA steering, NIE) are all imported from prior work; the contribution is the factorial design that isolates estimator × pooling × behaviour interactions in one harness — exactly the controlled comparison the scattered literature lacked. The sycophancy inversion and its data-artefact explanation is the most novel empirical finding.
- **Rigour:** Good on evaluation hygiene — OOD transfer prioritized over ID accuracy (heeding Levinstein & Herrmann's critique), threshold-free AUROC with bootstrap CIs, coefficient calibration in class-gap units so curves are comparable across directions of different norms, seed-fixed splits, disclosed bias toward MM in layer selection. Weak points: the LLM judge (Claude Haiku 4.5) is unvalidated against humans and is a single judge with no agreement measurement; steering is evaluated with 24 generations per point vs CAA's 50, so judge-curve noise bounds are unclear.
- **Reproducibility:** Strong — public code/data/interface, one model, fixed seeds, 8-bit single-GPU budget that others can match. The one-model scope is simultaneously the design's strength (controlled) and its main external-validity question.
- **Significance:** The read/write dissociation is a load-bearing assumption in the probing-for-monitoring literature (if the best-reading direction is not the best-writing one, monitors and interveners need different probes); showing it is behaviour- and data-dependent rather than universal materially qualifies that assumption. The NIE-vs-judge divergence also warns against using a self-report readout as the sole evidence that steering "worked."
- **Red flags:** (1) The sycophancy inversion conclusion is undercut by the authors' own admission that the best sycophancy detector — MM (last) — was never steered, so "MM writes worst for sycophancy" was not actually tested in the strongest form; the headline inversion partially rests on which directions happened to be available. (2) The sycophancy probes are not checked against an echo shortcut, and 29% genuine-agreement negatives do not fully mitigate it — sycophantic and genuine agreements both echo the user, so the reported 0.879 OOD AUROC may partly measure surface agreement. (3) Both layer and block selection are anchored on mass-mean, a bias toward MM precisely in the comparisons where MM is declared the winner or loser. (4) All conclusions are from one 8-bit-quantized 8B model; the authors themselves note McKenzie et al. measure the same probe at 0.887/0.913/0.916 AUROC on 8B/27B/70B, so the estimator/pooling orderings could shift with scale. (5) Only induction is measured — every steered item is already behaviour-off, so the safety-relevant direction (suppressing an exhibited behaviour) is entirely untested.

## Strengths

- One shared, seeded, released pipeline across all conditions turns a set of anecdotal cross-paper comparisons into a genuine controlled experiment.
- The data-artefact diagnosis of the sycophancy inversion (templated replies differ only in the final token; attention puts 0.38 mass on it; mean pooling dilutes it) is a concrete mechanistic explanation backed by a pooling-mass figure, not hand-waving.
- Dual steering instruments (NIE readout + judged generation + coherence) and the honest demonstration that they can disagree outright is one of the most useful methodological takeaways for anyone evaluating activation steering.
- Layer analysis (detection depth tracks behavioural complexity; steering blocks sit early) gives actionable guidance for practitioners choosing where to probe vs where to intervene.
- Exemplary limitations section and public artifacts, including an interactive interface that makes the probes directly usable rather than purely a paper table.

## Limitations

- Single model (Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, 8-bit quantized); every gap to reference papers is attributed to scale but never tested — the 70B replication is future work.
- The LLM judge is a single unvalidated model (Claude Haiku 4.5) with no human-agreement measurement, and the judged-behaviour conclusions (e.g., "flat OOD") inherit its noise.
- The best sycophancy detector, MM (last), is deliberately not steered, so the inversion claim is about the three steered directions only.
- Sycophancy probes lack an echo-shortcut control (AUROC on sycophantic vs genuine agreements was not measured).
- Layer and block selection are anchored on mass-mean, biasing both sides of the estimator comparison toward MM.
- Only behaviour induction is measured; suppression of an active behaviour — the actual safety use case — is untested, and negative coefficients are shown to have no consistent shape.

## Relevance

对"LLM Hidden States & Interpretability"方向高度相关：这是关于残差流线性探针与转向向量的系统性机理可解释性工作，直接研究隐藏状态中安全行为的表征位置（检测层 vs 转向层分离）、池化与估计器的选择规律，为用内部表征监控与干预模型行为提供了实证依据。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the sycophancy read/write inversion survive when MM (last) — the best detector — is actually steered, and on non-templated sycophancy data where the class signal is not concentrated in one token?
- Do the estimator/pooling orderings and the early-steering/late-detection layer split replicate at 70B, where the reference papers report 0.9+ AUROCs that this 8B pipeline falls well short of?
- How much of the sycophancy AUROC is an echo shortcut? Measuring probes on sycophantic-vs-genuine-agreement subsets (where both echo the user) would quantify it.
- Why does mass-mean's NIE readout go negative at sycophancy coefficient +1 exactly where judged behaviour peaks (9.4–9.7/10) — is the probe direction capturing something other than sycophancy at high injection strengths, or is the readout prompt the weak link?
- Does steering suppress (rather than induce) a behaviour the model is exhibiting, with the NIE denominator reversed — the direction that actually matters for safety deployments?
- Do sparse-autoencoder latents in place of dense linear directions preserve the read/write dissociation and the data-dependence findings?
