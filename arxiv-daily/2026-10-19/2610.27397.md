> **一句话总结：** 本文将 4-bit 文本嵌入器的 PTQ 校准视为一种"剂量"干预：以功能位移 D_func 度量更新幅度，发现同一固定流程在 EmbeddingGemma-300m 上随学习率从 +1.47 一路跌至 −71.53 pp（in-domain NDCG@10），且无标签的 cosine 目标在匹配剂量下同样崩溃（−69.52 vs −71.63 pp）——崩溃源于"可学习校准"而非"读标签"；作者进而提出预注册的验证门控规则，在 6 个独立校准板 × 5 个臂共 30 次决策中 0 次选出低于基线的检查点（代价是 11 次有益更新错过 3 次，平均 oracle 遗憾 1.52 pp）。

> **TL;DR:** Across e5-base-v2, BGE-M3, and EmbeddingGemma-300m at 4-bit weight quantization (161 calibration runs), a fixed LambdaRank-calibration procedure on BEIR-derived data swings from +1.47 to −71.53 pp in-domain NDCG@10 on EmbeddingGemma purely with learning rate; a label-free cosine objective collapses identically at matched functional displacement, so the danger is learnable calibration itself, not retrieval supervision. Objective sensitivity is model-dependent (e5: ranking beats cosine by +14.16 pp at matched dose, 95% CI [+3.37, +24.96]; BGE-M3: unresolved), and a pre-registered per-query paired-CI validation gate on a 25% calibration split made 0 harmful selections in 30 decisions while retaining 8/11 beneficial updates (mean regret 1.52 pp vs oracle). Read it for the dose framing and the deployment-safe gate, not for large accuracy wins.

## 核心贡献

- **把 PTQ 校准明确建模为"剂量"干预**：用功能位移 D_func（校准查询及其候选文档上，校准后与未校准量化嵌入之间的平均余弦距离）度量更新幅度。在模型内部，损伤随 D_func 单调（图 1）；同一流程在 EmbeddingGemma 上随学习率从 +1.47 pp 增益跨越到 −71.53 pp 的灾难性崩溃。
- **证明崩溃是"可学习校准"的属性而非"读相关性标签"的属性**：剂量匹配（dose-matched）比较中，无标签的 cosine 重建目标在最高剂量桶与 LambdaRank 同样崩溃（−69.52 vs −71.63 pp）；预注册比较器给出 not_retrieval_specific 判定。这推翻了"排序监督导致崩溃"与"排序目标更安全"的直觉。
- **揭示目标敏感性的模型异质性**：在匹配剂量下，e5 上排序校准显著比 cosine 更稳健（Δ=+14.16 ± 4.34 pp，3 个配对 slate）；BGE-M3 上方向相反但置信区间跨零（[−5.18, +2.38]），排序不成立；EmbeddingGemma 上两者都崩溃。
- **证明 D_func 是强筛选变量但非充分统计量**：同一 D_func 带内、不同学习率路径的结果相差可达 1.61 pp（约 9 倍 σ_runtime=0.184 pp）；预注册的"标量阈值"假设被拒绝。
- **给出可部署的预注册验证门控**：留出 25% 校准查询做配对 bootstrap 置信区间，仅当下界清零才接受检查点、否则弃权并部署未校准量化器。6 个独立 slate × 5 臂共 30 次决策中 0 次有害选择，同时保留 8/11 有益更新——诚实地表述为安全—机会权衡（平均 oracle 遗憾 1.52 pp，最大单次 11.13 pp）。
- **实施审计纠正了多个结论并写入正文**：包括撤销早期的"标签效率"声明（选择器实际读取了整个候选池的 1,024 个标签）、修复一次误分类为 label-free 的损失、以及把一个判别力仅 54% 的数字门降级为存在性检查。

## Key Contributions

- Reframes learned PTQ calibration of text embedders as a **dose-driven intervention**, measured by functional displacement D_func (mean cosine distance between calibrated and uncalibrated quantized embeddings), and shows the outcome of one fixed procedure spans +1.47 to −71.53 pp of in-domain NDCG@10 on EmbeddingGemma-300m across a four-point learning-rate grid (medians of three optimizer seeds).
- Separates two usually-conflated questions — how far to calibrate, and whether to calibrate at all — and answers both: the dose question has no universal constant (models differ in backbone/pooling/normalization), while the gate question has a usable label-free answer.
- Establishes via a pre-registered, dose-matched comparator that the catastrophic collapse is a property of **learnable calibration under a large enough update, not of reading relevance labels**: the label-free cosine objective collapses in the same highest-dose bin as LambdaRank (−69.52 vs −71.63 pp on EmbeddingGemma, both saturated; 7 qualifying bins, verdict not_retrieval_specific). The MSE weight-reconstruction control never reaches the collapse dose (stops at Dfunc = 0.107 even at lr 10⁻²) — evidence of an objective that cannot deliver the dose, not of safety.
- Documents **heterogeneous objective sensitivity**: paired, step-matched, three-slate comparison gives ranking-over-cosine robustness of +14.16 ± 4.34 pp (95% t-CI [+3.37, +24.96]) on e5-base-v2, but −1.40 ± 1.52 pp ([−5.18, +2.38]) on BGE-M3 — the ordering is model-dependent and unresolved there.
- Rejects the pre-registered strong form "performance is a function of D_func alone": equal Dfunc reached along different paths differs by up to 1.61 pp in calibration-validation NDCG@10 (~9× σ_runtime), while Dfunc itself is highly repeatable (σ/Dfunc = 3.3% at the collapsing dose over 5 seeds) — so the path residual is real and left open.
- Proposes and pre-registers a **validation-gated abstention rule** (accept a step only if the per-query paired bootstrap 95% lower bound on NDCG@10 gain over step 0 clears zero on a held-out 25% calibration split; otherwise abstain and ship the uncalibrated quantizer). Deployed across 6 independent calibration slates × 5 arms: 0 harmful selections in 30 decisions, harm_prevention (pre-registered) = 0.56, opportunity retention 8/11, mean oracle regret 1.52 pp (slate-cluster 95% [0.59, 2.76]), max single-slate regret 11.13 pp.
- Reports a two-pass implementation audit that **changed several conclusions** (a loss misclassified as label-free; a selector that read the whole candidate pool's labels; under-excluded self-matches; a determinism audit measuring the wrong gap) and retracts the earlier label-efficiency claim via a label-budget accounting in the gate's own unit.

## Methodology

Three embedders covering three architectures: e5-base-v2 (BERT backbone, mean pooling, "query:/passage:" prefixes), BGE-M3 (XLM-R backbone, CLS pooling, no prefix), and EmbeddingGemma-300m (Gemma 3 decoder backbone, mean pooling with a dense head, task prefixes). All linear layers are quantized to 4-bit weights with a learned step-size quantizer (LSQ with straight-through estimator); the quantizer parameters are the only trainable variables, so full-precision weights are frozen.

Calibration draws 32 labelled queries from each of four BEIR domains (MS MARCO, FiQA, NFCorpus, SciFact) over a 2,000-document corpus per domain, and runs 12 Adam steps at one of {10⁻⁴, 3×10⁻⁴, 10⁻³, 3×10⁻³}, extended to 10⁻² where a control could not otherwise reach the dose of interest — 161 calibration runs in total. In-domain generalization is measured on 25 disjoint queries per domain; cross-domain on held-out ArguAna and SciDocs (100 queries, 2,000 documents each); TREC-COVID, Climate-FEVER and Quora remain sealed and unevaluated. Since MS MARCO and SciFact are ceiling-limited (>70% of full-precision queries already at NDCG@10 = 1.0), improvement is read on the two unsaturated domains and held-out sets.

The retrieval objective is LambdaRank over positives and mined negatives; two label-free controls (cosine pull toward full-precision embeddings; MSE weight reconstruction) share every other setting. Crucially, cross-objective comparisons are **dose-matched**: trajectory points are binned by Dfunc and objectives compared within bins against a tolerance of 3× the model's own σ_opt, and the decisive cells were re-run on three independent slates, step-matched with a 0.02 Dfunc caliper (Table 2). Noise floors are measured per model and per learning rate (slate variance, not optimizer seed, dominates: σ_slate = 2.54 pp on SciDocs vs σ_opt = 0.192 pp on e5), and every headline comparison is paired within a calibration slate. Validity checks include bit-exact export of every checkpoint to packed INT4 codes (85,524,480 weights across 73 layers on e5 and 101,449,728 across 168 on EmbeddingGemma, zero mismatches), near-tie audits for NDCG ties, shuffled-label nulls, and pre-registration of the selection protocol, comparator, and hypotheses before the corresponding batches ran. All numbers are generated from the experiment ledger by code.

## Results & Highlights

- **Dose-driven collapse (EmbeddingGemma-300m, in-domain disjoint NDCG@10 vs uncalibrated quantized baseline):** the four learning rates give +1.47, −6.28, −30.48, and −71.53 pp (medians of 3 optimizer seeds) — the same procedure that helps at 10⁻⁴ destroys the model at 3×10⁻³. e5-base-v2 shows the same shape at a different scale: +2.07, +4.26, +6.05, −8.60 pp. An earlier "works on e5, fails on Gemma" architectural reading is shown to be an uncontrolled learning-rate comparison.
- **Collapse is not retrieval-specific (dose-matched):** on EmbeddingGemma, the label-free cosine objective collapses in the highest Dfunc bin exactly as LambdaRank does (−69.52 vs −71.63 pp, both saturated); after adding two intermediate learning rates (1.5×10⁻³, 2×10⁻³, n=3 each) the comparator holds 7 qualifying bins and returns not_retrieval_specific. The MSE control plateaus at Dfunc = 0.107 and never reaches the collapse dose even at lr 10⁻².
- **Heterogeneous objective sensitivity (paired slates, step-matched, n=3 slates):** on e5, ranking beats cosine by Δ = +14.16 ± 4.34 pp at matched Dfunc ≈ 0.15 (95% t-CI [+3.37, +24.96]); on BGE-M3 at Dfunc ≈ 0.21 the gap is −1.40 ± 1.52 pp, CI [−5.18, +2.38] — directionally opposite, statistically unresolved. A single-slate comparator on BGE-M3 (slate 0) had favoured cosine (+5.35 vs +2.46 pp in the 0.20–0.35 bin), illustrating why the multi-slate paired design overrides it.
- **Dfunc is necessary but not sufficient:** trajectories reaching the same Dfunc band via different learning rates differ by up to 1.61 pp in calibration-validation NDCG@10 (~9× σ_runtime = 0.184 pp), while Dfunc repeatability is tight (collapsing Gemma config over 5 seeds: Dfunc = 0.686, σ = 0.0227, i.e. 3.3%; e5 low dose: σ/mean = 0.8%) — the path residual is real, not measurement noise.
- **Validation-gated abstention (6 slates × 5 arms = 30 decisions, Table 3):** 0 harmful selections (worst selected checkpoint on any slate ≥ +0.00 pp); e.g. EmbeddingGemma at lr 3×10⁻⁴ goes from ungated final −11.2 ± 2.2 pp to gated +0.66 ± 1.05 pp, and at lr 10⁻³ from −31.9 ± 3.5 pp to gated +0.18 ± 0.43 pp; e5 at 3×10⁻³ from −7.0 ± 4.5 pp ungated to +5.29 ± 2.73 pp gated. Costs: abstention in 14/30 decisions, opportunity retention 8/11, mean oracle regret 1.52 pp (slate-cluster 95% CI [0.59, 2.76]), max 11.13 pp on one e5 slate where the rule abstained from both arms despite a strongly positive oracle. The authors correctly note zero harmful events in six slates bounds the per-slate harmful-selection probability only at ≲39% (one-sided 95%), not near zero.
- **Shuffled-label nulls and label-specificity:** the small EmbeddingGemma in-domain gain (+2.38 pp) sits inside the shuffled-label null tail (null max +2.70 pp over 20 seeds, 1 exceedance), so it is scored as a missed opportunity rather than attributed to retrieval supervision; on e5 the LambdaRank gain exceeds all 50 within-domain shuffled-label controls (calibration-fit retention 1.0369 vs null max 0.7830; randomization p = 0.0196).
- **Label-budget retraction:** 128 selected vs 512 random calibration examples give indistinguishable held-out NDCG@10 point estimates (0.4835 ± 0.0143 vs 0.4831 ± 0.0183 on ArguAna+SciDocs, n=3; difference +0.0004, Welch 95% CI [−0.037, +0.038]), while the selected arm accessed 1,024 labels vs 512 — the earlier "32 labelled queries" label-efficiency claim is retracted.

## Authors & Credibility

Hyojung Han (corresponding author), Ryangkyung Kang, and Seung-Hun Jeon are at ThakiCloud, with Youngeun Kim at Korea University — a small industry-academic team rather than a large quantization lab. Credibility signals are unusually strong for a paper of this scale: pre-registration of the comparator, selection rule, saturation criterion and hypotheses before the batches ran; all numbers generated from an experiment ledger by code with none typed by hand; per-model, per-learning-rate noise floors instead of borrowed thresholds; bit-exact INT4 export verification on every run; shuffled-label and derangement nulls; and a disclosed two-pass audit that retracted one of the paper's own earlier claims (label efficiency) and demoted a weak number gate. Runner, frozen protocol, comparator, selection rule, audit log, and ledger are stated to be released with the paper.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** The individual ingredients (learning rate too high destroys a quantized model; validation-based early stopping; selective prediction abstention) are all known, and the closest methodological relative — early stopping with a validation split — is acknowledged. The genuine novelty is the disciplined combination: separating dose from objective via Dfunc-matched comparison, showing the collapse is shared by label-free objectives (refuting the natural "ranking supervision is dangerous" story), and turning "should we calibrate at all" into a pre-registered abstention decision with a four-number scorecard.
- **Rigor:** Well above average. Dose-matched binning with pre-registered tolerances, paired-within-slate designs, heteroscedastic σ handled per cell, honest treatment of the argmax-rule failure (it once picked a −3.42 pp checkpoint against ~+1.7 pp oracles, which is exactly why the CI rule replaced it), and correct non-independence handling (5 correlated arms per slate not counted as 30 independent trials).
- **Reproducibility:** Strong on paper — ledger-generated numbers, released code/protocol/audit log, sealed test sets reserved for a single post-freeze evaluation. The main gap is that the sealed evaluation (TREC-COVID, Climate-FEVER, Quora) has not been performed in this version, so the cross-domain story rests on ArguAna/SciDocs only.
- **Significance:** The practical wins are modest — best gated gains are +5.53 pp (e5 at 10⁻³) and +0.78 pp (EmbeddingGemma at 10⁻⁴), and the gate's value is mostly harm prevention under aggressive doses that a careful practitioner would avoid anyway. The bigger value is methodological guidance: never compare objectives (or models) at unmatched dose, and treat calibration as an optional, gated intervention.
- **Red flags / weaknesses:** Evaluation scale is small — 25 disjoint queries per domain in-domain and 100 queries / 2,000 documents per held-out dataset, so absolute NDCG is not headlined; three of the seven candidate datasets are ceiling-limited (>70% of MS MARCO/SciFact queries at NDCG@10 = 1.0); BGE-M3 was never pushed to a collapsing dose, so model coverage of the collapse claim is 2 of 3; the pre-registered harm_prevention metric (0.56) and the realized 0-harmful-selections outcome diverge because the metric scores abstention on collapsing arms; no mechanism is offered for the 1.61 pp path residual; and the earlier draft's errors (label-free misclassification, label leakage in the selector) — though caught by the audit — indicate the first-pass implementation hygiene was imperfect.

## Strengths

- The dose-matching methodology (bin by Dfunc, compare objectives within bins, verify on independent paired slates with a caliper) is a clean template for any study comparing training objectives that also differ in effective update magnitude.
- The central negative result — a label-free cosine objective collapses identically at matched dose — kills a plausible-but-wrong narrative and is exactly the kind of claim most papers would not test.
- The validation gate is simple, label-free at decision time, pre-registered, and evaluated on independent slates; the four-number scorecard (0 harmful, 8/11 retained, 1.52 pp regret, 14/30 abstentions) communicates the safety-opportunity trade-off without overselling.
- Exceptional transparency: a section literally titled "What we do not claim" enumerates the unresolved BGE-M3 ordering, the missing mechanism, and the retracted label-efficiency claim, and the audit that changed conclusions is reported as part of the results.

## Limitations

- Small evaluation footprint: 100 queries over 2,000 documents per held-out dataset (ArguAna, SciDocs) and 25 disjoint queries per calibration domain; only two unsaturated in-domain domains carry the improvement claims; the three sealed datasets were never evaluated in this version.
- Only three embedders, one bit width (INT4), one quantizer family (LSQ), 12 calibration steps, and 32 selected queries per domain — generality to W8A8, AdaRound-style objectives, longer schedules, or larger embedders is untested.
- BGE-M3 never reaches a collapsing dose, so the "dose can be lethal" claim is directly demonstrated on 2 of 3 models, and the paper explicitly declines to support "every model collapses if pushed far enough."
- No mechanism for the path residual (up to 1.61 pp at equal Dfunc) — update direction, objective geometry, curvature are listed as unexplored candidates.
- The gate's oracle regret (1.52 pp mean, 11.13 pp max) and 14/30 abstention rate mean a practitioner using it will sometimes ship an uncalibrated model when calibration would have helped substantially; doubling the validation split to 50% did not recover the missed +2 pp updates.

## Relevance

与"极限性能优化下的可靠性"方向高度相关：本文研究的正是 4-bit 量化校准这一激进低精度优化引入的精度/可靠性风险，把"校准到什么程度会崩溃"量化为功能位移剂量，并给出防止静默退化的验证门控与弃权机制——是量化误差与数值可靠性分析在文本嵌入/检索场景的具体落地。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the dose-driven collapse and the Dfunc screening variable transfer to activation quantization (W4A4), other bit widths, or decoder-based embedders at larger scale (e.g., 1B+ parameters)?
- What mechanism produces the path residual — why do trajectories reaching the same Dfunc via different learning rates differ by up to 1.61 pp, and can a path-aware statistic (e.g., update norm history or curvature) close the gap that the rejected scalar-threshold hypothesis left open?
- Would the validation gate improve if the paired bootstrap used a different statistic or if the gate condition incorporated Dfunc itself (a two-factor rule), reducing the 14/30 abstention rate without reintroducing harmful selections?
- Does the ranking-over-cosine robustness ordering (+14.16 pp on e5, unresolved on BGE-M3) correlate with an architectural property (pooling type, prefix conventions, normalization), and can it be predicted a priori for a new embedder?
- What happens on the sealed datasets (TREC-COVID, Climate-FEVER, Quora) in the planned single post-freeze evaluation, and does the gate's zero-harm record survive the larger query counts and corpora of full BEIR?
