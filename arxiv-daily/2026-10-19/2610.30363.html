Key finding · arXiv 2610.30363 · activation probes, read vs write

“LR reads best, mass-mean writes best” is not a law of probes — it inverts for sycophancy, and the dataset decides.

原文·一句话总结（节选）统一流水线交叉测试 2 种估计器 × 3 种池化 × 3 种安全行为（欺骗、高风险感知、谄媚）：“逻辑回归读得最好、均值差写得最好”对前两种行为成立但对谄媚反转，且反转主要源于数据构造（模板化回复只在结尾词上有差异）而非估计器本身。

Key metric · steering strength (NIE)

+1.53VS+0.13

Induced deception at steering coefficient +2: mass-mean vs logistic-regression directions — same model, same layer block, a ~12× gap.

NIE = normalised indirect effect on a YES/NO readout. MM sweeps −0.23 → +1.53 monotonically; LR barely moves and runs the wrong way at −2.

Why this matters

If the best-reading direction isn't the best-writing one, monitors and interventions need different probes — this maps when that holds (one 8B model).

为用内部表征监控与干预模型行为提供实证依据：检测层与转向层位于模型的不同深度，需分别选择。

Critical edge

Only behaviour induction is measured — suppressing an already-active behaviour, the actual safety use case, is entirely untested.

Also untested: the sycophancy "inversion" rests on 3 steered directions — the best detector, MM (last), was never steered · one 8-bit 8B model · a single unvalidated LLM judge.

Methodology · distilled

One shared, seeded pipeline on Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct (8-bit, single GPU) crosses 2 estimators × 3 pooling schemes × 3 behaviours — and evaluates every direction twice: as detector (AUROC) and as steering vector.

  • Data: contrastive pairs per behaviour — Instructed-Pairs (deception), Models Under Pressure (high-stakes, 1.5k/class), disentangle-sycophancy (1.4k/class, 29% genuine-agreement negatives) — each with genuinely OOD eval sets (AI Liar + Roleplaying, Anthropic-HH's 2,984 real conversations, held-out math).
  • Five probes: MM/LR × last/mean tokens, plus LR-attention with a learned token-weighting query (LR reproduces Apollo's λ=10).
  • Layers: detection layer = earliest max 5-fold-CV AUROC across 32 layers; steering block = 5-layer window maximising summed NIE (anchored on MM — a disclosed bias).
  • Steering: directions calibrated so coefficient +1 = the full class gap; sweep −2 … +2; each layer gets its own refit vector.
  • Dual evaluation: NIE readout on the transcript + Claude Haiku 4.5 judging 24 generations per point (behaviour and coherence, 1–10).

Results · beyond the headline metric

Behaviour Best OOD detector Best steering (NIE) Dissociation
Deception LR (mean) — 0.722 pooled OOD MM — +1.53 @ +2 HOLDS (LR only +0.13)
High-stakes LR (mean) — 0.774 on Anthropic-HH MM — +1.34 @ +2 HOLDS (LR +0.32; judged behaviour flat OOD)
Sycophancy MM (last) — 0.879 on math OOD LR-attention — −1.28 → +0.77 INVERTS (MM dips to −0.92 @ +1)
  • Near-ceiling ID detection: three of five probes hit AUROC 1.000 on Instructed-Pairs (layers 12–13); estimators only separate under OOD transfer.
  • Read and write live at different depths: steering blocks sit at layers 6–15, detection at 12/19/20 — ten layers apart for high-stakes.
  • Instruments can contradict outright: at sycophancy +1, MM's NIE readout is negative while the LLM judge peaks at 9.42/10 and coherence collapses 9.67 → 2.88 → 1.00.
  • The inversion is partly a data artefact: templated sycophancy replies differ almost only in the final answer token; the attention probe puts 0.38 of its pooling mass there, mean pooling dilutes it.

Strengths

  • One shared, seeded, released pipeline turns scattered cross-paper anecdotes into a genuine controlled experiment.
  • The data-artefact diagnosis of the sycophancy inversion is mechanistic — 0.38 pooling mass on the one differing token — not hand-waving.
  • Dual steering instruments plus an honest demonstration that they can disagree is a methodological service to anyone evaluating activation steering.
  • Actionable layer guidance for practitioners: where to probe vs where to intervene.
  • Exemplary limitations section; public code, datasets, ~0.5 MB probe bundles, and an interactive per-token scoring interface.

Limitations

  • Single 8-bit 8B model; every gap to reference results is attributed to scale but never tested — the 70B replication is future work.
  • Single unvalidated LLM judge (Claude Haiku 4.5), no human-agreement measurement, 24 generations per point vs CAA's 50.
  • The best sycophancy detector — MM (last) — was deliberately never steered; the inversion claim covers only the three steered directions.
  • No echo-shortcut control: the 0.879 OOD AUROC may partly measure surface agreement.
  • Layer and block selection are anchored on mass-mean, biasing both sides of the estimator comparison.
  • Only induction is measured; suppression — the safety-relevant direction — is untested.

Questions for further exploration

  • Does the sycophancy inversion survive when MM (last) is actually steered — and on non-templated data where the class signal isn't one token?
  • Do the orderings and the early-steering / late-detection split replicate at 70B, where reference papers report 0.9+ AUROCs?
  • How much of the sycophancy AUROC is an echo shortcut? (Measure on sycophantic-vs-genuine-agreement subsets.)
  • Why does MM's readout go negative exactly where judged behaviour peaks (9.4–9.7/10) — probe artefact or readout-prompt weakness?
  • Does steering suppress (not just induce) an exhibited behaviour, with the NIE denominator reversed?
  • Do sparse-autoencoder latents preserve the read/write dissociation findings?

Credibility

Grew out of the ARENA 3.0 interpretability programme under faculty supervision — careful empirical work, not an industrial-scale study.

Unusually transparent: itemised self-critical limitations, prior-work hyperparameters deliberately reproduced (Apollo's λ=10, CAA's sweep, Marks & Tegmark's NIE), and public code / data / interface.
