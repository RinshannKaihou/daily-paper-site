> **一句话总结：** 提出"投影愈合"（Projection Healing）：在故障感知训练中加入低秩 DCT 子空间对齐惩罚，使 INT8 量化网络在推理时能用两轮投影从幸存坐标重建被多位翻转（MBU）损坏的权重/激活；在六个回归基准上，仅切换运行时愈合就使原生单位超额误差积分再降约 23%，+5% RMSE 容忍下的擦洗间隔中位数延长 2.04 倍（SECDED 仅 1.62 倍），代价是干净 RMSE 中位数 +0.3% 的对齐税与约 2.0–2.5 倍的 MAC 开销。

> **TL;DR:** UC Berkeley authors extend Fault-Aware Training (FAT) with a low-rank DCT-II alignment penalty so that, at runtime, a two-pass "Projection Healing" projection can detect and rebuild INT8 weight rows and activations corrupted by multi-bit upsets — no ECC replica, no extra persistent storage. Across six INT8 regression benchmarks (California Housing, Friedman #1, SDSS photo-z, CMS Jet pT, NASA C-MAPSS, NRC Critical Heat Flux) it cuts wide-window relative excess AUC to a median 0.72 vs 0.99 for SECDED+interleaving and ~0.9 for RedNet/DieHard proxies, and extends the median +5%-RMSE scrub interval 2.04x vs 1.62x for SECDED — but SECDED still wins in the low-intensity SBU window (0.49 vs 0.76), the runtime heal alone contributes a mean 23.4% reduction in native-unit excess integral on a frozen FAT checkpoint, and the clean-RMSE tax reaches +11.8% on CHF. Simulation-only, on small MLPs (18k–70k params).

## 核心贡献

- **投影愈合机制**：一种运行时软件修复，通过两轮 DCT-II 低秩投影与"综合征-门控"检测（行级旗标 τ_flag=0.18 + 逐坐标 sigmoid 门控），从幸存的未损坏坐标重建被 MBU 损坏的权重行与前激活；无需任何存储的权重副本，持久存储保持 1.0× INT8。
- **对齐惩罚扩展 FAT**：在故障感知训练目标中加入激活/权重的"红空间能量"铰链惩罚（死区 ρ=0.05，λ_red 线性退火至 8），将约 95% 的干净信号压入固定 rank-32 的 DCT-II"绿子空间"，使运行时投影可分离故障噪声；DCT 基为解析闭式，可校验、可在线重生成，不作为持久模型状态存储。
- **系统性 MBU 基准**：在六个 INT8 回归基准上，以截断幂律多重性模型（α=2.2，m_max=256，33% 多位翻转，E[m]=2.65）注入权重位翻转，与 Clipped、Ranger、RedNet/DieHard 代理、SECDED+交织、SECDED(b7) 定向保护等七种防御对比，并给出逐位损伤分解（位 0–5 占 12.8%、位 6 占 31.6%、位 7 占 55.5%）。
- **完整的消融与敏感性**：冻结 FAT 检查点单独切换愈合、MAD 去尖峰对照、任意正交基对照、逐组件移除（去权重对齐使超额 AUC 恶化到 1.83）、训练期代理故障与真实位翻转 FAT 的 3.3% 差异、幂律参数与内存布局敏感性、以及解析计算开销（批 1 为 2.5×、批 256 约 2.0× MAC）。

## Key Contributions

- **Projection Healing**: a runtime, software-only repair that detects and rebuilds corrupted INT8 weight rows and pre-activations using a two-pass robust projection onto a fixed low-rank DCT-II basis. A per-coordinate syndrome (normalized squared residual) plus a concentration flag (max > 2·mean) gate which coordinates to replace; the second reconstruction is built only from surviving coordinates, so the repair is not poisoned by the fault it is removing. No replica, no extra persistent ROM (the basis is regenerated in closed form, not stored).
- **Alignment-penalized FAT**: extends Fault-Aware Training with hinge penalties on the fraction of activation/weight energy outside the rank-k "Green" subspace (ρ=0.05 dead zone, λ_red ramping to 8 over 40 epochs), plus a row range penalty suppressing peak-to-mean weight ratios that would confuse the gate. Training uses surrogate corruptions (contiguous 25% input-block masks, 25% weight-block zeros, 15% sign inversion) deliberately mismatched to the evaluation power-law MBU distribution to avoid overfitting the fault model.
- **A six-benchmark regression study** — an underserved regime, since clipping-based defenses target classifiers and regression lacks terminal neural collapse: California Housing, Friedman #1, SDSS photometric redshift, CMS Jet pT, NASA C-MAPSS FD001, NRC Critical Heat Flux, all as 3-hidden-layer INT8 MLPs.
- **Honest, mechanism-isolating evaluation**: frozen-checkpoint toggling of the heal, MAD-despiker and arbitrary-basis controls, single-component removals, surrogate-vs-exact fault-model FAT comparison, per-bit damage/detection analysis, multiplicity-spectrum sweeps (10–40% MBU fraction), memory-layout sensitivity, and an explicit analytic overhead model.

## Methodology

The system has three parts. (1) **MBU simulation**: faults are injected into stored INT8 weights and INT8 biases (per-channel scales kept as protected metadata) by flipping a contiguous burst of m bits in the flattened parameter store, with m drawn from a truncated power law P(m) ∝ m^−2.2 on {1..256}, calibrated so 33% of strikes are multi-bit (matching checkerboard SRAM data from Ibe et al.); mean multiplicity 2.65. (2) **Fault-Aware Training with alignment**: each training step runs four forwards — clean, input-block mask (zeroing a contiguous 25% of features), weight-block mask (zeroing 25% of fan-in columns per layer), and sign flip (15% of columns) — regularized toward the clean prediction. On top of this sits the alignment machinery: a normalized orthogonal-complement energy r(v) is computed per pre-activation row and per weight row against the DCT-II basis B (Green rank K=32), aggregated into hinge losses E_red and E_w-red above dead zone ρ=0.05, plus a range penalty on each row's ‖W‖∞/‖W‖1 ratio (readout weighted 3×). The full loss separates the projection-healing terms (alignment) from the FAT terms (fault robustness), with EMA-scaled coefficients tuned once and fixed across datasets. (3) **Runtime Projection Healing**: for each weight row and activation vector t, a provisional low-rank reconstruction gives a per-coordinate syndrome s = (t − tBBᵀ)²/(max|t|² + ε). A row is flagged only if max(s) > τ_flag = 0.18 and max(s) > 2·mean(s) — faults must be both large and concentrated. A near-hard sigmoid gate (centers T = 0.25 for activations, 0.35 for weights; slope 40) down-weights flagged coordinates to form a surrogate clean vector, from which a second reconstruction supplies replacement values; healthy coordinates pass through untouched. Weight healing runs on the dequantized float working copy (healed values are not written back to SRAM; an optional INT8 writeback every Q = 256 iterations amortizes cost), and activation healing runs every inference. Networks are 3-hidden-layer LeakyReLU MLPs (~18k params for CalHousing, ~68k for the other five), post-training quantized to symmetric per-channel INT8, evaluated over MBU intensity I ∈ [10⁻⁴, 1] with 400 realizations per point and 8 seeds.

## Results & Highlights

- **Wide-window excess AUC (I ∈ [10⁻⁴, 1], lower is better, unprotected = 1.00)**: the full method reaches mean 0.71 / median 0.72 across the six benchmarks, vs 0.99 for SECDED+interleaving (72,64 code), 0.90 for the RedNet proxy, 0.91 for the Diehard proxy, and 1.00 unprotected — i.e., a 28–29% reduction in accumulated excess RMSE where MBUs dominate, while single-bit ECC saturates.
- **Scrub-interval extension (Table 2)**: at +5% median RMSE degradation the method extends the tolerable corruption interval by a median **2.04×** over unprotected (SECDED: 1.62×); at +10% it is 2.06× (SECDED 1.43×); at +50% breakdown it is **1.96×** (SECDED only 1.07×). Per-dataset, the +5% extension ranges from 1.53× on SDSS (9.06e−3 → 1.33e−2) to 2.27× on CMAPSS (2.34e−2 → 5.31e−2).
- **Runtime heal isolated (Table 3)**: holding the FAT-trained checkpoint fixed and toggling only the runtime heal reduces the native-unit excess integral by a mean **23.4%** (from 22.3% on SDSS to 33.7% on CMAPSS), and by 13.7% against standalone unaligned FAT — with a mean clean-RMSE change of just +0.5% from switching the heal on.
- **Low-intensity regime is conceded**: in the early window I ∈ [10⁻⁴, 10⁻²] (SBU-dominated), SECDED+interleave reaches median 0.49 vs the method's 0.76, and the method's *mean* there is 1.11 — worse than the 1.00 unprotected baseline, because the clean-accuracy tax inflates the ratio on SDSS and CMAPSS. The authors state plainly that classical ECC is the right tool when SBUs dominate.
- **Ablations pin the mechanism**: removing weight-subspace alignment is the single most damaging variation (mean excess AUC 1.83 vs 0.71 full); a per-row MAD despiker under identical training is strictly worse on all six datasets (1.07×–1.87×); an arbitrary fixed orthogonal basis is near-parity on four datasets but 1.78× worse on CHF, the same dataset with the worst alignment tax.
- **Per-bit analysis vs targeted ECC**: under fixed m=3 bursts, pooled SBU damage splits 12.8% (bits 0–5), 31.6% (bit 6), 55.5% (bit 7); a dedicated SECDED(b7) arm protecting only sign bits (1.6% storage) still loses at high intensity because uncorrected lower bits accumulate and ECC cannot correct multi-bit syndromes, while projection healing repairs any flagged coordinates at once.
- **Overhead**: analytic multiply-accumulate ratio on a square N=128, k=32 layer is 2.5× at batch 1 and 2.0× at batch 256 with Q=256 writeback (vs ~1.0× for SECDED, 3.0× ROM for TMR); persistent storage stays 1.0× INT8 with no long-lived FP32 replica. An LEO translation: at ~10⁻⁶ upsets/bit/day, the CHF +5% intensity of 6.32×10⁻⁴ (unprotected, ≈1.7 years) extends to 2.18×10⁻³ (≈6.0 years).

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: genuine and well-executed. Low-rank subspace regularization is old, and FAT is established (Stutz et al., Cavagnero et al., Vinck et al.), but using a *fixed, closed-form, regenerable* DCT basis as an implicit checksummed redundancy channel — training the network to live inside it so corrupted coordinates can be rebuilt from survivors at inference with zero persistent storage overhead — is a new combination, and the frozen-checkpoint ablation credibly isolates the runtime mechanism from FAT itself.
- **Rigor**: strong on ablations and self-criticism (they concede the low-intensity window to SECDED, disclose that the early-window mean of 1.11 is *worse* than unprotected, and model SECDED miscorrection rather than strawmanning it). Weaknesses: the RedNet and Diehard arms are self-built proxies (LogClip without the confidence head; ReLU6 without activation clamping) rather than the published systems, and two relevant defenses — in-place zero-space ECC and unaligned weight-bit-flip FAT (Stutz et al.) — are explicitly not evaluated, which leaves the "beats established techniques" claim partially untested.
- **Reproducibility**: negative. No public code or data at submission (release gated on acceptance), eight seeds but a single simulator; the many hyperparameters (τ_flag, T, S, K, ρ, six λ's) were tuned once on a dev split and fixed, which reduces per-dataset cherry-picking but makes independent replication harder.
- **Significance / overclaiming risk**: experiments are 18k–70k-parameter MLPs with 5–21 input features on *simulated* power-law bursts; no beam test (unlike the ECON-T ASIC they cite) and no CNN/Transformer — yet the introduction motivates the work with H100 HBM and "space-based data centers," far beyond what is demonstrated. The gate's concentration requirement (spike in a row, fan-in-aligned bursts) is an artifact of row-major logical layout; Appendix F.2 shows column-major is near-parity on average, but real SRAM address scrambling — which the authors admit their injector does not model — could defeat the row-local gate in ways the sweep does not capture. The LEO calendar-time translation (1.7 → 6.0 years) is computed on CHF, the dataset with the +11.8% clean-RMSE tax whose shared-floor +5% threshold is I = 0 on all eight seeds — a favorable framing choice. The Q=256 writeback cadence is analytic accounting, never exercised in the fault campaign itself.
- **Red flags**: none serious — the patent and the AI-assistance disclosures are transparent; the main concern is the gap between tiny-MLP simulation evidence and deployment-scale claims.

## Strengths

- Zero persistent storage overhead (1.0× INT8 ROM; the DCT basis is regenerated in closed form and even checksummable in flight), making it deployable on COTS parts with no ECC — the actual underserved niche.
- Clean mechanism isolation: the frozen-checkpoint toggle (23.4% mean gain), MAD-despiker control, and no-weight-align ablation (1.83 vs 0.71) form a tight causal chain from the alignment penalty to the runtime heal.
- Focus on regression, where activation-clipping defenses are inapplicable, with four real domain datasets (SDSS photo-z, CMAPSS, CHF, CMS jets) rather than only toy tabular data.
- Exceptional sensitivity hygiene: multiplicity-spectrum sweep showing the SECDED comparison reverses below ~10–20% MBU fraction (0.75× at 10%), bit-position breakdown, gate-threshold sweep with FPR/clean-cost trade-off, and an honest F.1 note that the fc1 weight heal is a provable no-op at these widths.

## Limitations

- The method **loses to SECDED+interleaving at low intensity** (median 0.49 vs 0.76 in the SBU-dominated window) and its early-window mean (1.11) is worse than doing nothing; it is only the right choice when MBUs dominate.
- Clean-accuracy tax: median +0.3% / mean ~2% RMSE, but **+11.8 ± 5.8% on CHF**, where the model starts already beyond the +5%/+10% budgets of the unprotected model — a per-dataset gamble that must be checked before use.
- Requires training from scratch with alignment; **pre-trained models cannot be retrofitted**, raising the barrier versus passive ECC.
- Compute cost of 2.0–2.5× MACs (analytic, excluding quantization, checksums, memory traffic, and atomic-write costs), and up to ~130× at batch 1 if weights are healed every pass; the Q=256 cadence is unvalidated under actual radiation.
- Scope: INT8 MLPs ≤ ~70k parameters, simulated contiguous logical-address bursts only; no hardware beam data, no interleaving of strikes with inference over time (one-shot snapshot injection), no convolutional or attention layers, and baselines partially proxied.

## Relevance

正中「Inference Reliability & SDC Detection」核心方向：本文针对推理期硬件位翻转（MBU/SEU）对量化模型的静默损坏，提出检测加运行时修复的软件层方案，与 DRAM/内存错误和加速器故障检测这一主题高度吻合，且 INT8 量化同时触及「极端性能优化下的可靠性」中对低精度可靠性问题的关注。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does projection healing survive address-scrambled SRAM layouts, where a physical burst lands scattered across logical rows and may never trip the concentration flag max(s) > 2·mean(s)?
- Can the approach scale beyond toy MLPs — do INT8 CNNs, or transformer blocks where "neuron ordering is arbitrary" is less obvious after attention mixing, still admit a rank-32 DCT-aligned subspace at acceptable clean-accuracy tax?
- Can alignment be imposed by fine-tuning (rather than from-scratch training) on pre-trained checkpoints, lowering the adoption barrier the authors themselves flag?
- How does the heal behave when strikes arrive interleaved with inference over calendar time (their benchmark injects one accumulated snapshot), and what Q writeback cadence is actually safe at a given flux?
- Could the syndrome gate be combined with lightweight CRC/quantile SDC monitors (Geissler et al.) to cover the low-intensity SBU window where SECDED currently wins, yielding a single defense across the full intensity range?
