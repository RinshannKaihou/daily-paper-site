> **一句话总结：** 本文发现所有主流深度学习框架中 softmax/交叉熵实现存在两个浮点 bug——高置信度下目标 logit 梯度被舍入为零（Softmax Collapse，导致梯度不再求和为零、引发 grokking 中的 Slingshot 尖峰），以及蒸馏时学生/教师 logit 接近梯度却不为零（Softmax Cancellation）；作者用 softplus/expm1/log1p 在对数空间重写这些函数予以修复，在 a+b mod 31 等四个 grokking 基准上让 fp32 固定交叉熵达到 99–100% 测试精度（标准版仅 93–96%），并证明 logit margin 在 AdamW+权重衰减下收敛于平衡点 log(1/ε)。

> **TL;DR:** Standard softmax and cross-entropy implementations in JAX/PyTorch/TensorFlow silently zero out the target logit's gradient once the logit margin exceeds a precision cliff (≈5.5 in bf16, ≈16.6 in fp32, ≈36.7 in fp64), producing gradients that no longer sum to zero; the authors give drop-in fixes built on softplus/expm1/log1p that restore exact zero-sum gradients without fp64 casting. On the modular-addition grokking benchmark (a+b mod 31, 1-layer width-64 Transformer, AdamW, 5 seeds), fp32 fixed cross-entropy reaches 100.0±0.0 / 99.6±0.7 / 99.0±2.3% test accuracy at ε = 10⁻⁸/10⁻¹²/10⁻¹⁶ versus 94.3±5.4 / 96.4±5.1 / 96.5±4.3% for standard cross-entropy, and at ε=10⁻¹² it beats the Zero-sum Projection baseline of Liu et al. (48.9%) and StableMax of Prieto et al. (95.6%, with margins blowing up to 3200–21252). The fix also eliminates Slingshot loss spikes, explains them via a margin equilibrium m* ≈ log(1/ε) between weight decay and Adam's ε, and stops student networks from drifting away from the teacher in bf16 soft-target distillation — though all evidence is on small algorithmic grokking tasks, not large-scale training.

## 核心贡献

- **识别并命名两个被广泛忽视的浮点 bug：** (1) **Softmax Collapse** —— 当目标 logit 的 margin m 超过精度悬崖 c_d（bf16 ≈ 5.5，fp32 ≈ 16.6，fp64 ≈ 36.7）时，标准实现中分母 1+e⁻ᵐ 被舍入为 1.0，导致前向损失和目标梯度 g̃_t 同时归零，而 K−1 个非目标梯度却保持正值直到指数下溢（m ≈ 88），于是梯度不再满足求和为零；(2) **Softmax Cancellation** —— 软目标蒸馏中分别计算 q = softmax(z_S) 与 p = softmax(z_T) 再相减，当 z_S → z_T 时精度丢失，且前向 softmax 与反向 log-softmax 的运算顺序差异使 z_S = z_T 时梯度仍非零。
- **给出即插即用的修复实现：** 只对最大（或目标）logit 做平移、用数值稳定的 softplus(−m) 组织分母、绝不清算 1+e⁻ᵐ；蒸馏情形则直接从 logit 差 δ = z_S − z_T 出发，用 expm1/log1p 在对数空间计算 q − p。全程无需内部升级到 fp64，可无显著减速地替换现有实现（论文附完整 JAX 代码）。
- **首次把 Slingshot 机制完整归因于 Softmax Collapse：** 记忆化后 margin 增长越过悬崖 → 目标梯度归零、Adam 二阶矩 v̂ 指数衰减 → 权重衰减把样本拉回悬崖下方 → 恢复的梯度除以极小的 √v̂+ε 产生巨步 → 损失尖峰并循环。修复后尖峰完全消失。
- **推导并验证 margin 平衡律 m*(ε) ≈ log(1/ε)：** 记忆化后梯度 |g| ∝ e⁻ᵐ，Adam 的归一化步长在 |g| ≪ ε 时衰减为 g/ε，与权重衰减 λθ 平衡得到 m* ≈ log(1/ε)。该定律解释了为何默认 ε = 10⁻⁸ 恰好使 fp32 出现 Slingshot（18.4 刚好越过悬崖 16.6），而 ε ≥ 10⁻⁷ 则不会；并用 LaProp 在 ε 低至 10⁻¹⁶ 的范围内逐点验证。

## Key Contributions

- Identifies and names two numerical bugs present in the standard softmax, log-softmax, and cross-entropy implementations of all major deep learning frameworks: **Softmax Collapse** (the target logit's gradient rounds to zero once the logit margin m exceeds a precision cliff c_d — ≈5.5 in bf16, ≈16.6 in fp32, ≈36.7 in fp64 — while non-target gradients survive to exponent underflow, so gradients no longer sum to zero) and **Softmax Cancellation** (subtracting separately-rounded softmax(z_S) and softmax(z_T) loses precision as z_S → z_T and leaves a non-zero gradient even at z_S = z_T).
- Provides **drop-in fixed implementations** that shift only the argmax (or target) logit, express the denominator as softplus(−m), and never form 1+e⁻ᵐ directly; for distillation, the student–teacher difference q − p is computed in log space from δ = z_S − z_T using expm1/log1p. No internal fp64 casting is needed, and exact gradient sums of zero are preserved.
- **Traces the "Slingshot" loss-spike phenomenon** (Thilak et al., 2022) mechanistically to Softmax Collapse interacting with Adam/AdamW: above the cliff the target gradient vanishes while Adam's second moment v̂ decays; weight decay eventually pulls examples back below the cliff, where the restored gradient divided by a tiny √v̂+ε produces an outsized parameter step — repeating the cycle. The fix removes the cliff and the spikes with it.
- Derives and verifies a **margin equilibrium law m*(ε) ≈ log(1/ε)**: after memorization |g| ∝ e⁻ᵐ, and Adam's normalized step shrinks to g/ε once |g| ≪ ε, balancing weight decay λθ like an implicit L2 regularizer of weight ελ. This predicts why the default ε = 10⁻⁸ causes Slingshot in fp32 (log(1/ε) ≈ 18.4 sits just above c32 ≈ 16.6) and why ε ≥ 10⁻⁷ does not; the law is validated with LaProp across orders of magnitude of ε down to 10⁻¹⁶, with ε = 0 growing until fp32 gradient underflow at m ≈ 81.2.

## Methodology

The paper is a numerics-first study. Starting from the max-shifted softmax/cross-entropy of Equation 1 (Higham, 2002), the authors analyze where floating-point rounding diverges from the exact gradient g = q − p. For Softmax Collapse, the key observation is asymmetric precision: floating point is fine-grained near 0 but loses e⁻ᵐ when it is added to 1.0, so the standard denominator 1+e⁻ᵐ rounds to exactly 1.0 once m > c_d, zeroing the forward loss and g̃_t while the non-target gradients e^(z_j−z_t)/(1+e⁻ᵐ), evaluated from negative differences without adding 1, remain accurate to underflow. The fix keeps e⁻ᵐ in the numerator (g_t = −e⁻ᵐ/(1+e⁻ᵐ)) and reorganizes log-softmax as z̃_k − softplus(−m) where the margin m is the LSE of all logits excluding the argmax (Equation 5); for hard-target cross-entropy the target t replaces the argmax and the t-th slot is replaced by a constant 0, so autodiff itself enforces the zero-sum property (Equations 6–7). For Softmax Cancellation, the log-ratio s_k = log(q_k/p_k) and difference u_k = q_k − p_k are computed from the centered logit difference via expm1 and log1p (Equation 9), and a final projection g = u − q·Σu removes residual drift so g = 0 exactly when z_S = z_T. Complete JAX implementations (~10 lines each) are provided, using stop_gradient to graft the custom gradient onto the standard forward loss.

Experiments use established grokking protocols: 1-layer width-64 ReLU Transformers on a+b mod 31 (50% train split, 10,000 epochs full-batch AdamW, η = 10⁻³, β1 = 0.9, β2 = 0.98, λ = 1, following Varma et al. 2023) across bf16/fp32/fp64 and ε ∈ {10⁻⁸, 10⁻¹², 10⁻¹⁶}; three additional grokking tasks (a+b mod 113, a²+b² mod 97, a³+b mod 97); and a distillation test where the student is initialized with the teacher's weights (a+b mod 53, temperature T = 4, 2,000 Adam steps, ε = 10⁻⁸), where the true gradient at initialization is exactly zero. The margin-equilibrium analysis uses LaProp (updating v̂ without squaring, to avoid fp32 underflow of g² below |g| < 2⁻⁶³) and compares measured margins against the analytic m*(ε).

## Results & Highlights

- **Main table (a+b mod 31, λ = 1, 5 seeds):** fp32 fixed cross-entropy reaches 100.0±0.0 / 99.6±0.7 / 99.0±2.3% test accuracy at ε = 10⁻⁸/10⁻¹²/10⁻¹⁶ with final margins 17.9 / 27.4 / 36.8 (matching the predicted log(1/ε) ≈ 18.4 / 27.6 / 36.8), versus 94.3±5.4 / 96.4±5.1 / 96.5±4.3% for fp32 standard cross-entropy whose margins stall at 13.0 / 15.1 / 12.7 — below the fp32 cliff c32 ≈ 16.6.
- **Precision sweep (same setup):** bf16 fixed CE gets 100.0±0.1 / 99.8±0.2 / 98.9±2.4% vs 93.3±7.4 / 94.9±4.9 / 94.2±8.3% for bf16 standard CE, which stalls at margins 5.1–5.8 (cliff c16 ≈ 5.5); even fp64 standard CE fails at ε = 10⁻¹⁶ (97.2±3.2%, margin 30.1 < c64 ≈ 36.7) while fp64 fixed CE holds 99.0±2.0% at margin 36.8.
- **Baseline comparison (Table 1):** at ε = 10⁻¹² the Zero-sum Projection fix of Liu et al. (2026) collapses from 97.9±3.2% (ε = 10⁻⁸) to 48.9±2.6% test accuracy because it stalls the margin near 17–18.9; StableMax (Prieto et al., 2025) reaches 95.6±2.0% but lets margins blow up to 3200–21252 (vs 27.4 for fixed CE), because its piecewise-rational function grows linearly instead of exponentially.
- **Destructive single spike without weight decay (a²+b² mod 97, λ = 0, ε = 10⁻¹⁶, Doshi et al. setup):** standard CE tracks the fixed version up to 61% test accuracy, then one Slingshot spike when m hits c32 around step 1250 drops it to 23% (60% → 21% on a³+b mod 97), while fixed CE continues smoothly to 85%.
- **Grokking speed with weight decay:** on a+b mod 31 fixed CE groks faster on all 5 seeds and saturates at 100±0% vs 94±5% for standard CE; across all four grokking benchmarks at ε = 10⁻¹², fp32 fixed CE matches fp64 standard CE and reaches 100% test accuracy.
- **Distillation with θ_S = θ_T initialization:** the residual gradient of standard soft-target CE is ~10⁻¹⁷ in fp64 (below ε = 10⁻⁸, student stays near KL = 0), ~10⁻⁸ in fp32 (brief KL spike), and ~10⁻³ in bf16 (drives the student away from the teacher and degrades test accuracy); fixed soft-target CE returns g = 0 exactly, holding held-out KL = 0 and 100% test accuracy in all three precisions.
- **Equilibrium verification (LaProp, 3 seeds per setting):** measured margins level off at the predicted m*(ε) = log(1/ε) for every ε > 0, while ε = 0 keeps growing until fp32 gradients themselves underflow at m ≈ 81.2.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** The individual numerical ingredients (softplus, expm1, log1p, max-shifting) are classical (Higham, 2002); the genuine novelty is the *diagnosis* — that the standard implementation's target gradient alone dies at the precision cliff while non-target gradients survive, breaking the zero-sum property — and the consequences traced from it: a complete mechanistic account of the Slingshot phenomenon and the m* ≈ log(1/ε) equilibrium, which is a crisp, falsifiable, and independently interesting result about AdamW's implicit regularization (ελ acting as an effective L2 weight).
- **Rigor:** High. Every claimed mechanism is demonstrated (gradient asymmetry in Fig. 1, spikes and their absence in Fig. 2, equilibrium vs analytic prediction in Figs. 3–4), with 3–5 seeds, error bars, and comparisons against the two most relevant recent alternatives (Zero-sum Projection, StableMax) rather than strawmen.
- **Reproducibility:** Excellent — ~30 lines of self-contained JAX code, exact hyperparameters, seeds, and public task definitions; the a+b mod 31 protocol is standard in the grokking literature.
- **Significance caveats:** All evidence comes from tiny algorithmic grokking tasks (width-64 one-layer Transformers, two-layer MLPs, modular arithmetic); there is no experiment on any realistic workload (no CIFAR/ImageNet distillation à la Hinton, no small LM pretraining). The authors admit this honestly in the conclusion. The practical relevance for frontier-scale training — where label smoothing, high entropy, and bf16 mixed precision with fp32 master weights make margins of 16+ on *every* class unlikely — is plausible but unquantified.
- **Missing evidence / red flags:** (1) The distillation experiment only tests the degenerate initialization θ_S = θ_T; a realistic distillation run where the student genuinely learns from a teacher (and whether the fixed loss changes final quality) is absent. (2) The claim of "no meaningful slowdown" is asserted without any wall-clock or throughput measurement. (3) The Softmax Collapse fix's behavior is a provable no-op for margins below c_d, so the paper cannot and does not show any benefit for standard large-scale training where margins stay modest — the title's breadth slightly outruns the evidence. No signs of cherry-picking; failures of standard CE are explained rather than just exhibited.

## Strengths

- A rare combination of a one-line-root-cause bug report, a mechanically complete causal story (cliff → v̂ decay → spike), and a predictive analytical law (m* ≈ log(1/ε)) that is verified across six orders of magnitude of ε.
- The fixes are genuinely drop-in: no fp64 casting, no hyperparameter changes, ~10 lines of code, and they provably restore the exact mathematical properties (zero-sum gradients; exactly zero gradient at z_S = z_T) rather than papering over symptoms.
- Unifies and reinterprets prior scattered observations (Slingshot, grokking failures in low precision, the ε-vs-precision interaction) under a single numerical mechanism, and gives the first explanation I have seen for why the specific default ε = 10⁻⁸ is pathological in fp32.

## Limitations

- Evidence is confined to small-scale grokking benchmarks (modular arithmetic with ≤113 classes, width-64 Transformers / 2-layer MLPs); no experiments on vision, language, or any task where the loss is not driven to numerical zero.
- The distillation study covers only the θ_S = θ_T fixed point; it shows the standard loss *can* drift but not that it hurts a real teacher→student transfer, nor at what student–teacher gap the effect becomes negligible.
- No performance/overhead numbers are given for the fixed kernels despite the "without meaningful slowdown" claim, and the fixes are JAX-only in the paper (PyTorch/TensorFlow ports are left to the reader).
- The margin-equilibrium analysis assumes the post-memorization regime |g| ∝ e⁻ᵐ and stationary Adam statistics; its applicability to SGD or sign-SGD-style optimizers beyond the Adam family discussed (RMSProp, LaProp) is not explored.

## Questions for Further Exploration

- At what realistic scale (vocabulary size, label smoothing, mixed-precision training with fp32 master weights) do logit margins actually approach c32 ≈ 16.6, and can the cliff be detected in production loss curves as anomalously flat plateaus followed by spikes?
- Does the fixed soft-target cross-entropy change outcomes in on-policy distillation / KL-regularized RLHF, where student and teacher logits are close by construction and bf16 is common?
- Does the implicit ελ L2-equivalence of the margin equilibrium offer a cheaper knob than explicit weight decay for controlling logit growth in large-scale AdamW training (cf. Wortsman et al., Molybog et al.)?
- Can the Softmax Cancellation fix be fused into fused-softmax CUDA/TPU kernels without the memory or latency cost of the extra expm1/log1p passes, and what is the measured overhead?
- Does the same asymmetry (target gradient rounding to zero while others survive) appear in other losses with saturating tails, e.g., sigmoid + BCE on highly separable data, or focal loss at large margins?
