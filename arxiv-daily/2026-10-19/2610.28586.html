Key Finding · 关键发现 · arXiv 2610.28586

Agentic on-policy distillation fails because the teacher's scores are myopic — they never see where the episode ends.

教师逐 token 打分看不到回合结局（rollout–scorer gap），从而放任学生拖延；R2S 无需任务奖励即可离线修复教师的打分分布。

Why This Matters

Diagnoses and repairs a concrete OPD failure mode using no task reward at all — directly useful wherever agent rewards are unavailable.

诊断并修复 agentic OPD 中教师打分"近视"这一失败机制，且全程无需任务奖励，对无奖励可用的 agent 训练场景有直接实用价值。

⚠ Critical Edge

"The delay/out-of-turn failure mode these 2021–22 text-game benchmarks amplify is exactly the one R2S fixes — a favorable fit between method and evaluation."

Also: one training run per cell (no multi-seed except 9B→4B) · Qwen-only family · 19,200 takeover episodes + replayable environment required.

01 Methodology — R2S, distilled

A reward-free repair stage bolted in front of vanilla agentic OPD. The OPD objective is a reverse-KL between student and teacher token distributions on the student's own histories, estimated inside a clipped PPO surrogate. The student's training is untouched; only the scorer changes.

  1. Mine divergence states from the untrained student's episodes — turns where its thinking diverges most from the teacher (up to 2,400 states).
  2. Teacher takeovers, K=8 each; keep states where enough takeovers pass a reward-free check (consensus vote or LLM judge). Keep the shortest passing takeover.
  3. Privileged scoring: the frozen teacher scores the episode suffix with that takeover in context → informed targets q⁺ (top-16 renormalized).
  4. Closed-book fine-tune a teacher copy to emit q⁺ without the takeover in context (1 epoch, lr 2e-6), then refreeze it as the sole scorer for standard OPD.

9B teacher acting alone on ALFWorld: 63.1% — the repaired 4B student (81.9%) beats its own teacher. Beats TCOD-F2B, Guided-OPD and FutureBridge in all 12 cells.

  • Out-of-turn episodes roughly halved: WebShop 27.5% vs 55.9% vanilla; ALFWorld 19.5% vs 34.3%; ScienceWorld 17.1% vs 31.9%. Won episodes also finish in fewer turns and search less.
  • MoE teacher holds up (Qwen3-30B-A3B→8B): 54.7 / 50.5 / 30.4 vs best baselines 48.8 / 37.1 / 23.4 — even though this teacher alone completes almost no WebShop purchase (94% of episodes run out of turns).
  • Self-distillation works: a repaired 4B teaching itself gives 72.7 / 58.1 / 42.4 vs ≤ 50.6 / 34.0 / 29.3 for every baseline learning from the unrepaired 4B — no larger model in the pipeline.
  • Reward-free selection ≈ oracle: consensus vote 81.9/63.1/39.5 and LLM judge 82.6/55.9/50.3 vs ground-truth upper bound 85.9/65.0/49.0 (vanilla OPD: 65.8/34.6/34.6).
  • Informed targets, not SFT data: takeovers alone as SFT give 76.8/46.7/41.0; adding takeover-informed targets lifts to 81.9/55.9/47.8 — takeovers help most as scoring context.

Strengths

  • Reframes the failure from "student drifts out of teacher reach" to "teacher scores are myopic" — backed by a decisive takeover experiment, not intuition.
  • Reward-free and modular: scorer-side repair combinable with student-side curricula; student's objective untouched, easy to bolt into existing OPD pipelines.
  • Unusually trustworthy sweep: all three SOTA baselines re-implemented and re-run under one shared protocol with public code; 12/12 cells won.
  • Mechanistic follow-through (out-of-turn rates, turn budgets, searching-vs-task decomposition) all points the same direction as the diagnosis.
  • Practical payoff: a repaired 4B self-teacher beats the 9B-teacher vanilla student.

Limitations

  • Dated, narrow benchmarks — three 2021–22 text games (ALFWorld, WebShop, ScienceWorld); no SWE-bench-/τ-bench-style or real web/tool-use environments; WebShop only 500 canonical goals.
  • One model family: all teachers and students are Qwen3/Qwen3.5 (2B–30B-A3B); transfer to other families or frontier-scale teachers untested.
  • One 150-step run per cell; training-seed robustness shown only for the 9B→4B pair; ablations also confined to those cells.
  • Compute + environment assumptions: 19,200 teacher-takeover episodes and one full-parameter teacher fine-tune before training; requires a replayable environment — excludes live, non-resettable deployments.
  • The +12.8 average mixes very large and modest gains (WebShop 9B cells vs 30B→4B ALFWorld +5.7) — expect less on already-strong pairs.
Credibility: both authors (Shu Zhao, Tan Yu, NVIDIA) have a consistent RL-methods publication line; baselines re-run under a shared protocol rather than self-reported; the counterintuitive takeover claim is verified across all twelve cells; ground-truth selection honestly reported as a non-ranked upper bound. 03 Questions for Further Exploration

  1. Does the rollout–scorer gap exist in reasoning-mode (thinking) models, where the scorer already sees long deliberation chains — and does R2S transfer there?
  2. Can R2S stack with student-side curricula (TCOD-F2B, Guided-OPD) for additive gains, and does target quality degrade as the student's states move during training?
  3. How sensitive is repair to K (takeovers per state), the consensus threshold m, and the 2,400-state budget when takeover success is near 10%?
  4. Would the repaired scorer also serve as the distillation term in hybrid reward+distillation systems (e.g., SDAR-style) without confounding?
  5. Does the myopia diagnosis generalize to real, non-replayable environments (live web agents) — can offline datasets or simulated resets substitute for takeover mining?
