> **一句话总结：** Aleph Alpha 发布的开源（Apache 2.0）英德双语 MoE 模型 Kolibri（78.1B 总参数 / 每 token 仅激活 3.46B），通过自研 UniBPE 分词器、>2T 自建德语数据、混合滑动窗口注意力与 1.2M 任务的 RL 后训练，在英德双语的"质量—推理成本"帕累托前沿上超越所有同规模开源 MoE（英文总分 75.5 vs Qwen3.5 的 74.7），并以 1M token 上下文和显著的幻觉抑制（错误时 44% 选择弃答）面向欧洲受监管场景的自主部署。

> **TL;DR:** A 182-page open-weights technical report from Aleph Alpha: Kolibri is an English-German MoE (78.1B total / 3.46B active, Apache 2.0) trained on 24T tokens with >20% German data and post-trained with SFT + RL over 1.2M curated tasks across 38 environments. Post-trained Overall scores are 75.5 (EN) / 70.8 (DE) on its unified protocol — +0.8 / +1.0 over Qwen3.5 35B-A3B at similar active parameters and +21.4 / +24.4 over its predecessor Kolibri Origin at 2.7x decode throughput — and it beats every 3B-active MoE baseline on AIME 2026 (96.0 vs Nemotron 3 Super's 90.4), though the dense Qwen3.8 27B still leads nearly everywhere (80.2 EN Overall). Especially valuable for the training-machinery detail: UniBPE tokenizer (11.2% fewer German tokens than GPT-5's), proxy-model data-mix optimization (3,354 runs), FP8 quantization-aware RL, and per-token logit-gradient bounds.

## 核心贡献

- **主权开源模型**：Kolibri 是由德国团队端到端（数据、架构、训练、后训练、评测全链路自控）开发的 78.1B-A3.46B 英德双语 MoE，以 Apache 2.0 许可开放权重，面向公共行政、工业、航天等受监管领域在自有基础设施上部署，从设计之初就考虑 EU AI Act 与 GDPR。
- **UniBPE 分词器**：结合 BPE 自底向上合并与 Unigram 损失打分的混合训练法，在德语 FineWeb-2 上达到 4.90 bytes/token（GPT-5 为 4.35，德语文本少用 11.2% token），词素边界命中率 65%（外部最好 49%）。
- **德语数据工程**：德语占预训练 token 的 21.3%；自建 CC 管线产出 0.8T 有机德语 + 1.1T 改写德语（Mistral-Nemo 改写为百科/问答/教学体），并刻意以改写替代翻译以保留德语的文化与制度语境。
- **架构与训练配方**：40 个滑动窗口（512）+ 10 个无位置编码全注意力块的混合注意力；384 专家取 6 + 1 共享专家（每 token 仅激活 4.4% 参数）；EQB+LEI 负载均衡；用 3,354 个 15M 代理模型的混合律优化 78 个数据源配比。
- **RL 后训练机制**：38 个环境、1,209,965 个任务的单次异步 RL：组相对优势 + B-TV 信任域 + 平方对数比正则 + 针对工具调用失败的 targeted OPSD；用逐 token logit 梯度范数上界与 FP8 量化感知训练保证训练-推理数值一致（不匹配时 RL 发散）。
- **幻觉抑制与合规**：训练弃答行为——AA-Omniscience 答错时 44% 弃答/部分作答（前代仅 15%），RGB 无答案文档时 86% 弃答（前代 74%）；含政治平衡、版权合规、安全拒答等 RL 环境。
- **Model Factory（Savanna）**：将全流程表达为版本控制代码（MTaC），夜间 CI 端到端训练小模型防回归，并让 AI 代理在受约束的动作空间内自主审查实验、分流失败运行。

## Key Contributions

- **A sovereign open-weights LLM**: Kolibri (78.1B total, 3.46B active per token, 4.4% sparsity) is built end-to-end in Germany — data, architecture, training infrastructure, post-training, and evaluation all in-house, on German and Finnish hardware — and released under Apache 2.0, explicitly designed for EU AI Act / GDPR-constrained deployment in public administration, industry, and aerospace.
- **UniBPE tokenizer**: a hybrid that keeps BPE's greedy bottom-up merging but scores each merge by Unigram loss (closed-form ΔL). On German FineWeb-2 it reaches 4.90 bytes/token vs 4.35 for GPT-5 (11.2% fewer tokens for German web text) and 65% morpheme-boundary precision vs 49% for the best of nine external tokenizers, without losing English efficiency (4.58 vs GPT-5's 4.67).
- **German-first data pipeline**: German is 21.3% of the 20T pre-training tokens (>2T self-curated: 0.8T organic CC German web with language-specific filtering thresholds, 1.1T Mistral-Nemo-rephrased German preserving German institutions and register rather than translating); own CC pipeline distills 308B extracted documents into 14.9B quality-scored ones via Luxical embedding classifiers distilled from a Qwen3 32B judge.
- **Data-mix optimization at scale**: log-linear mixing laws fitted on 3,354 15M-parameter proxy trainings over the 78-source pool, solved as a convex program with a KL pull to a token prior and 3-epoch repetition caps.
- **Efficient architecture**: hybrid attention (40 SWA blocks with window 512 + RoPE, every fifth block full attention with NoPE), 384 routed experts at top-6 + 1 shared expert, sigmoid routing without renormalization, EQB+LEI load balancing, QK normalization that both stabilizes high learning rates and bounds the FP8 KV cache.
- **RL training machinery**: a single asynchronous RL run over 38 environments / 1.2M tasks with group-relative advantages (Dr. GRPO-style), a Binary Total Variation trust region (DPPO-style), squared log-ratio regularization, targeted On-Policy Self-Distillation that injects remedial hints at the exact turn where a tool call failed, a per-token logit-gradient norm bound (shown to dominate CISPO caps), FP8 quantization-aware training plus routed-expert replay to keep trainer and inference engine numerically aligned (they observe divergence otherwise).
- **Pareto-frontier evaluation methodology**: quality measured under one unified protocol and plotted against measured decode throughput (bytes/s/GPU on 8x B200, best parallelism layout per model), not parameter counts alone.

## Methodology

Kolibri is a decoder-only MoE transformer with 50 blocks: 40 use sliding-window attention (window = 512 preceding tokens) with RoPE (θ = 10,000); every fifth block (10 total) uses full attention with no positional encoding (NoPE). Attention is GQA (48 query / 4 KV heads, dim 128) with per-head QK RMS-normalization. Each MoE layer routes every token to 6 of 384 routed experts plus 1 shared expert; expert loads are balanced with Exact Quantile Balancing and Load-Error Injection.

Training proceeds in three stages: pre-training (20T tokens at 16k context from a 78-dataset, 29.3T-token pool across nine domains), mid-training (3.4T tokens at 64k context on curated math/code/reasoning data), and long-context extension (200B tokens at 256k context). The model supports 1M-token contexts at inference, extrapolating past its trained window. Ablations are run on two proxy scales (7.8B-A0.6B and 30.6B-A2.1B) with tuned learning rates before target-scale validation.

Post-training has two phases. SFT (~268B tokens x 2 runs, averaged) mixes curated German and English data, removes English reasoning traces from non-English exchanges, and injects synthetic German teacher data with German "openers" to cold-start German reasoning. RL is a single asynchronous run on 38 environments (31 EN + 7 DE; 1,209,965 tasks) spanning SWE/terminal (36.1% of sampling weight), math/reasoning (17.8%), instruction following (15.9%), code generation (12.9%), tool use (9.8%), retrieval/long-context QA (4.9%), identity/safety and hallucination reduction. The objective combines a group-relative policy-gradient term with a B-TV trust-region mask, a squared log-ratio regularizer (no KL to a reference model), and targeted OPSD for low-reward groups. Infrastructure-wise, the trainer performs FP8 quantization-aware training replicating the inference engine's quantization and replays the engine's expert routing decisions; weights are pushed to inference replicas via RDMA after every step without dropping in-flight requests, at up to 18,900 concurrent sandboxes and 16,900 B300-equivalent GPU hours. Everything is orchestrated by Savanna, their "Model Training as Code" factory with declarative TOML run configs, checkpoint lineage, and nightly end-to-end CI runs.

## Results & Highlights

- **Post-trained Overall (unified protocol, Tables 28-29):** Kolibri scores 75.5 (EN) / 70.8 (DE) vs Qwen3.5 35B-A3B's 74.7 / 69.8 (+0.8 / +1.0 at 3.46B vs 3B active parameters) — best of all compared MoE models in both languages. The dense Qwen3.8 27B still leads at 80.2 / 79.9 while activating ~8x more parameters per token.
- **vs predecessor Kolibri Origin:** Overall jumps from 54.1 to 75.5 (EN, +21.4) and 46.4 to 70.8 (DE, +24.4) at ~2.7x decode throughput per GPU (Figure 1 headline); base-model Overall goes 58.4 to 81.1 (EN) at ~1.6x throughput.
- **Base model (Table 17):** Kolibri Base Overall 81.1 (EN) / 81.5 (DE) is the best model under 12B active parameters and second overall, behind only Nemotron 3 Super 120B-A12B (83.1 / 85.0, >3x the active parameters); GLM-4.5 Air 106B-A12B scores lower in both languages (77.1 / 79.4). It is first of ten models on the English math average (84.9).
- **Math (post-trained):** AIME 2026 (EN) 96.0 — best among all MoE baselines, ahead of Nemotron 3 Super's 90.4, with only dense Qwen3.8 higher (97.7); AIME 2025 (DE) 87.5 and AIME 2026 (DE) 90.0 beat every 3B-active model.
- **Long context (RULER):** Kolibri Base trails Qwen3.5 35B-A3B and Nemotron 3 Nano at every length up to 256k (e.g. 69.8 vs Nemotron Nano's 80.1 at 256k), but degrades least beyond its trained window and is best at 1M tokens (63.2 vs Nemotron Nano 57.5, Qwen3.5 58.5).
- **Hallucination reduction vs Kolibri Origin:** abstains or partially answers on 44% of AA-Omniscience questions it gets wrong (Origin: 15%); abstains on 86% of RGB questions whose documents lack the answer (Origin: 74%); Merlin-Arthur grounding score on SQuAD 23.4% where Origin and several baselines score 0.
- **Industry RAG proxies (unseen corpora):** English average 89.7, leading every MoE baseline by +2.7 over Qwen3.5 (87.0); German average 67.5, below Qwen3.5's 70.0; dense Qwen3.8 leads both (93.2 / 80.2). On Honeypot (EN/DE agentic retrieval) Kolibri scores 80.8, +6.5 over Qwen3.6 35B-A3B.
- **Tokenizer:** 4.90 bytes/token on German FineWeb-2 (best of twelve tokenizers; GPT-5: 4.35) with 65% morpheme-boundary precision (best external: 49%); at scale-M proxy training it matches plain BPE on BPB while improving RULER VT and HELMET JSON-KV recall at every context length.
- **RL training-inference consistency:** serving the policy with FP8 weights + FP8 KV cache raises rollout throughput 2-3x, and without FP8 quantization-aware training in the trainer, the RL run diverged — the per-token gradient bound and QAT are what make the 2-3x speedup usable.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: No single component is fundamentally new — hybrid SWA attention, sigmoid routing, EQB balancing, log-linear mixing laws (Olmix-style), GRPO/B-TV/OPSD are all adapted from prior work. The genuine novelty is (a) UniBPE (a small but clean contribution with closed-form merge scoring), (b) the German data strategy (rephrasing over translation, with an articulated cultural-preservation argument), and (c) the FP8-aligned trainer-inference setup with routed-expert replay, which is documented at a level of detail most labs do not publish.
- **Rigor**: Strong on ablations (proxy-scale studies with tuned learning rates before target-scale validation) and unusually honest — they report that their mixing-law optimizer is sensitive to swarm size, that German quality buckets were down-weighted contrary to their labels, and that byte-vs-token miscounts had to be patched mid-solve. However, all baseline numbers are self-run under their own protocol (they say so), and several "German versions" of benchmarks (Ellamind translations) plus five in-house Industry RAG customer proxies are not publicly available, which makes the German headline wins hard to verify independently. The BFCL v4 web-search backend differs from the official leaderboard's, so those scores are not comparable; two Industry RAG proxies (11 and 26 items) are admitted to be too small for trends.
- **Reproducibility**: Weights and model card are public; the data pipeline and RL environments are not. The report gives enough hyperparameter detail (Appendices I.4-I.5) to reimplement the objective, but the 1.2M curated RL tasks are the actual moat.
- **Significance**: The "Pareto frontier" claim is about quality-per-throughput, not raw quality — the dense Qwen3.8 27B beats Kolibri on 17+ rows including English math average, τ3-bench, and both Industry RAG averages. The claim holds for the evaluated open models, but the model does not match 12B-active MoEs everywhere (trails Nemotron 3 Super on TerminalBench 27.7 vs 39.7 and overall base aggregates).
- **Red flags**: Kolibri's weakest rows are telling — RGB Closed-Book 73.0 is the lowest of all 14 evaluated models (several at 92-93), AA-Omniscience accuracy 14.8 trails most baselines, and multi-turn BFCL splits lag (47.5 vs 62.7 for Nemotron 3 Nano). The abstention-heavy training that wins hallucination benchmarks appears to trade off closed-book recall — a real cost the report acknowledges only implicitly. The +21-point jump over Kolibri Origin conflates recipe improvements with a 3x token budget and 2.6x total-parameter scale-up, so it is not evidence of recipe quality alone. Kimi-K2.6 as the retrieval-benchmark judge is a competitor's model judging everyone, which is defensible but arbitrary.

## Strengths

- Exceptional documentation depth: per-decision ablations (attention pattern, window size, sparsity sweep, QK-norm stability analysis with gradient-norm plots), failure post-mortems, and explicit limitations sections.
- Genuine open release (Apache 2.0 weights) of a competitive sub-4B-active MoE with 1M-token context — few European models of this class exist.
- The quality-vs-throughput evaluation methodology (bytes/s/GPU at best serving layout) is more deployment-relevant than pure parameter or benchmark comparisons.
- Training-machinery contributions with evidence: per-token logit-gradient bound analyzed against CISPO; FP8 QAT shown necessary (RL diverges without it); targeted OPSD for tool-failure remediation is a practical trick.
- Regulatory posture is operationalized, not just stated: PII redaction statistics, pirated-content filtering, a legal due-diligence deep-dive on copyright, and abstention training with measurable hallucination reductions.

## Limitations

- Only English and German; the report itself lists multilingual extension as future work.
- Trails on RULER long-context retrieval within its trained window (up to 256k) behind Qwen3.5 and Nemotron 3 Nano; its 1M-token win comes where almost no competitors are evaluable.
- Weakest on closed-book knowledge (RGB Closed-Book last of 14 models), terminal agentic work (TerminalBench 27.7), and multi-turn function calling (BFCL v4 multi-turn 47.5).
- RL is a single run without published ablation of the objective components at target scale (B-TV vs clipping, OPSD contribution are argued at analysis level, not via controlled final-scale ablations).
- Industry RAG proxies and several German benchmark translations are in-house artifacts; two proxies have 11 and 26 items.
- Data-mix law fitted on 15M proxies; authors themselves flag optimizer sensitivity and unresolved German-quality misweighting.

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How much of the +21.4 EN Overall gain over Kolibri Origin is recipe vs. scale (3x tokens, 2.6x total parameters)? A compute-matched Origin-to-Kolibri ablation would isolate this.
- Does the abstention training that wins RGB/AA-Omniscience causally cause the RGB Closed-Book collapse (73.0, last of 14)? A Pareto analysis of hallucination-vs-recall along RL checkpoint progression would quantify the trade-off.
- How well does UniBPE transfer to other morphologically rich languages (e.g., Slavic or Turkic), and does the morpheme-alignment gain persist at larger vocabularies?
- The per-token logit-gradient norm bound is argued analytically vs CISPO — does it measurably reduce entropy collapse or staleness pathologies in a controlled RL ablation at target scale?
- Since the trainer replicates FP8 quantization and expert routing of the inference engine, does residual kernel-level mismatch (non-batch-invariant kernels) still produce measurable policy-ratio bias over long runs?
- Can the 3,354-run mixing-law methodology be replaced with fewer, larger proxies, or does the noise the authors report fundamentally cap 15M-parameter surrogate fidelity?
