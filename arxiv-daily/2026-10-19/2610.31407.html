Why this matters

A methodological playbook for anyone studying “the model knows but answers wrong”: probing, calibration, and causal-site identification on a real multilingual failure.

Critical edge

A plain scalar offset already reaches a nearby operating point (0.312 TPR @ 0.012 FPR vs the vector’s 0.335 @ 0.015) — most of the celebrated fivefold gain is a threshold move, and script parity, the motivating goal, is never achieved.

01 · Methodology

Only the script varies; everything else is pinned.

A three-tier diagnostic built on a benchmark where script is the single manipulated variable.

  • Controlled benchmark. 601 manually audited Wikidata person pairs (404 observed-alias + 197 name-property relations), each name attested in both Devanagari and Latin. Four script conditions (Dev–Dev … Lat–Lat) hold person, relation, and question language fixed.
  • Hard negatives. Every positive pair gets a different-person foil matched by occupation (99.8% shared), country, and relation tier.
  • Measurement. Final logit margin m = zyes − zno, no sampling; 2,000 item-clustered bootstrap CIs; counterbalanced answer keys; 3 paraphrases × Marathi/Hindi/English; fixed 361/120/120 split.
  • Tier 1 — behavior. Accuracy, margin AUROC, and signal-detection d′ across five models, plus romanization controls.
  • Tier 2 — information. Ridge probes (α=10) trained on Lat–Lat states only, tested on Dev–Dev; five-feature string baseline; scalar margin calibration.
  • Tier 3 — causality. Activation patching at E1/E2/readout under a strict causal gate (must fail Dev–Dev and succeed as donor across paraphrases and keys); rank-one direction patching; a frozen shared steering vector over layers 12–17, scale tuned on validation only.

Also on the record

  • Tokenization is not the culprit. Five romanization schemes cut mean name length from 16.5 to 7.7–10.4 tokens with no reliable recovery; substituting the attested Latin form reproduces Lat–Lat within 0.004 across all nine model–language cells.
  • Probes beat string features only under similarity-matched negatives (0.908 vs 0.796) — but tight matching is possible for just 144/601 items, tested on a 25-item slice.
  • Name form, not prompt language, carries the correction: an English Lat–Lat donor moves the Marathi margin by 6.26 (vs 5.97 for a Marathi donor); an English Dev–Dev donor gives only 0.11.
  • The vector also lifts Lat–Lat items (Marathi 34.0% → 62.9%); across models, Mistral-Small-24B shows a 25.8-pt gap vs 6.9 for Indic-adapted Sarvam-M-24B.

03 · Strengths

  • Attested — not transliterated — name forms in both scripts; occupation-matched hard negatives; counterbalanced answer keys; a causal gate against format shortcuts.
  • The three-level chain (behavior → decodable information → causal site) is made operational with AUROC, d′, and calibration — not left as rhetoric.
  • Generality pre-checked: Malayalam replication, five models, romanization and donor-language controls.
  • Unusual candor — offset-only and oracle-threshold negative controls are reported up front, not buried.

04 · Limitations

  • Person names in two scripts and three prompt languages; organizations, locations, and other relations untested.
  • All mechanistic results (probe, patching, steering) concern Qwen2.5-7B only.
  • Gated flip rates rest on 12–24 items per language; the matched-negative probe test has 25 items.
  • Steering sweep is validation-only; shared test items mean the controls are not independent replications.
  • A diagnostic lever, not a fix: the script gap itself does not close (AUROC unchanged; disparity widens in one arm).

05 · Questions for further exploration

  1. Does the E2 middle-layer site replicate in non-Indic pairs (Cyrillic–Latin, Han–Latin), or is it Qwen- and script-specific?
  2. Can a discrimination-targeting intervention close the residual AUROC gap (0.023–0.065), or is some loss an irreducible consequence of fragmented tokenization and reduced familiarity?
  3. Does the 87% calibration recovery survive generation-based evaluation, where the model must produce rather than score the answer?
  4. Do retrieval-augmented or entity-linking pipelines inherit the same criterion shift — and can the margin offset be deployed safely at a fixed FPR in production?
  5. How much probe signal is genuine identity association vs orthographic regularity — could a causal probe dissociate the two?
