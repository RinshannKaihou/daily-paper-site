Why This Matters

Anyone routing or abstaining on model confidence today: verbal confidence alone is unsafe, and early-layer "internal signal" may just restate the question's surface.

Critical Edge

In 9 of 11 retrieval cells, the "early" probe does no better than a prompt-only TF-IDF classifier — the early internal signal may be a surface artifact of the question.

The headline +21.3 pp depth gap also comes from a post-hoc 21-tap sweep, not the marginal pre-registered test (p = 0.052).

Methodology 方法

Grid
5 Qwen2.5 models × 6 task tiers (PopQA hi/lo popularity, SimpleQA, GSM8K, MATH L1–2, L4–5) = 30 cells; 500 questions each, fixed seed, 60/20/20 split.
Channel 1
Verbalized confidence, three formats: 0–100 integer, 5-level ordinal labels (fixed mapping), answer-or-pass scored +1/−2/0.
Channel 2
Prefill-state probes: logistic regression on the last prompt token's hidden state (single forward pass, hooks verified leak-free), trained to predict whether the model's own 10 sampled answers (T = 0.8) disagree. Depth swept 0–100% in 5% steps; onset = AUROC ≥ 0.65 on two consecutive layers.
Channel 3
Sampling consensus: semantic-entropy agreement across the 10 sampled answers.
Gates
Four audit gates — human-audited grading (195/200), verbal generalization ρ ≥ 0.60, probe validity (beat a label-shuffle null, a prompt-only TF-IDF baseline, and a 0%-depth negative control), matched base/instruct rows. Hypotheses H0–H4 pre-registered in a version-controlled protocol.
Analysis
Brier score split via the Murphy (1973) decomposition — reliability, resolution, uncertainty — going beyond ECE.

Credibility notes: no known lab or affiliations given, but process rigor is high — an activation-hook token-leakage bug was disclosed, fixed, and regression-tested; code and generations are released; the five-tap run is unversioned (authors flag it themselves); all on one RTX PRO 6000.

13,121 / 15,000 answers sit at ≥ 90/100 — yet 26.8% of those "high-confidence" items are voluntarily passed in the answer-or-pass format.

  • Probe-onset depth gap: retrieval probes informative at ~5–7% of layer depth vs. ~28–30% for reasoning; Δ = +21.26 pp [95% CI +9.72, +33.05], permutation p = 0.004; peak AUROC 0.88 (retrieval) vs. 0.79 (reasoning).
  • The TF-IDF collapse: onset probes fail to beat a prompt-only TF-IDF baseline in 12/24 cells overall — including 9/11 retrieval cells (e.g. 1.5B PopQA-high: probe 0.864 vs. TF-IDF 0.882). Excluding GSM8K shrinks the gap to +12.63 pp.
  • Cross-signal coherence: raw verbal–probe correlation ρ = 0.086 (nearly uncorrelated); after per-cell calibration it rises to 0.83 — but mostly reflects between-cell difficulty, not item-level agreement.
Strengths 优势
  • Pre-registration with falsifiable gates — two hypotheses honestly reported falsified; rare in LLM evaluation.
  • Murphy decomposition cleanly explains why low ECE ≠ useful confidence — the paper's most transferable lesson.
  • Prompt-only TF-IDF control — deceptively simple, devastating, and omitted by much of the probing literature.
  • Full 30-cell grid kept — no cherry-picked accuracy band; out-of-band cells recorded, not dropped.
  • Bug disclosure, 0%-depth negative controls, human-audited grading (195/200) raise pipeline trust.
Limitations 局限
  • Single model family (Qwen2.5, 0.5B–7B) — depth gap and format fragility may not transfer; no frontier models.
  • Probe predicts self-consistency, not correctness — "knows when it will disagree" is easy to over-read.
  • The clean 21-tap depth-gap result was designed after seeing the marginal pre-registered test — exploratory-confirmed at best.
  • Gap is carried mainly by GSM8K; MATH L4–5 onsets are early and unexplained; dropping GSM8K nearly halves it (lower CI +1.31).
  • Small per-cell samples (100 test items); complete-case three-signal analyses cover only ~1/3 of cells; H3 never ran — the base model's verbal confidence was degenerate.

Questions for Further Exploration 待探索

  1. Does the onset gap — and its TF-IDF collapse — replicate in Llama-4, Gemma-3, or Qwen3 families?
  2. Would probes predicting correctness (not self-consistency) show the same depth profile?
  3. Can a stronger surface baseline (frozen embeddings, small MLP) fully explain retrieval onsets — or does any layer show genuine recall?
  4. Why are MATH L4–5 onsets early while GSM8K is uniformly late — difficulty, answer-format entropy, or chain-of-thought structure?
  5. Does the 2× resolution edge of sampling consensus survive more samples, lower temperature, or cheaper approximations, given ~10× inference cost?
  6. Could hierarchical item-level calibration recover a genuinely shared latent confidence behind the 0.83 pooled correlation?
