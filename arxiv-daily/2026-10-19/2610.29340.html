Why This Matters

Marginal per-neuron statistics are structurally blind to how MLP readers and writers are paired — this paper turns that blind spot into a measurable, learned quantity.

Practical correction: the standard vocabulary lens describes only 1 channel in 12.

Critical Edge · Read Before Citing

The pairing is statistically far from random yet functionally near-interchangeable — break it, and a different assignment recovers the control loss within 0.004 nats.

Critical Assessment: functional significance is “the weak point” — equal-disturbance effects are only 4–9 millinats.

Methodology 01

Pre-LayerNorm GELU decoders with RoPE trained from scratch on FineWeb-EDU at 28.9M / 123.6M / 405.3M (3 seeds each, 20 tokens/param), plus corpus controls (order-shuffled text, Zipf i.i.d., stories/news/books), a SwiGLU family, and released checkpoints. Four-part instrument:

  1. Symmetry score C(J). A pre-norm MLP writes into the stream it reads, so its update Jacobian is square; split it symmetric/antisymmetric.
  2. Re-pairing test. Fix both factor sets, permute writers only (B = 999); initialization calibrates exchangeability; the common permutation is an exact negative control.
  3. Signed coordinate m_h = ⟨ū_h, v_h⟩. Negative means “opposed”; a block’s core is its top-16 by |m_h|.
  4. Census + interventions. Project writers through the unembedding under a taxonomy frozen before labeling; writer regrowth, sign flips vs matched comparators, equal-disturbance re-pairings.

Strengths 03

  • Turns a structural blind spot (permutation invariance of marginal neuron statistics) into a clean, well-calibrated measurement with an exact negative control.
  • Cross-scale, cross-corpus, cross-family replication — 28.9M to 405M trained plus OLMo / GPT-2 / Pythia released trajectories.
  • Temporal resolution of emergence: sign within a few hundred steps, shared depth order across five GPT-2 seeds, before coupling grows.
  • Interventional, not just observational: regrowth, flips, equal-disturbance matching each isolate a distinct claim.
  • Quantifies how misleading the vocabulary lens is (1 channel in 12) — a practical correction for interpretability.

Limitations 04

  • Scale tops out at 405M trained / 1B released; 7B+ and instruction-tuned / RLHF models untested.
  • Gated architectures (the modern default) break the cheap weights-only sign reading.
  • No causal account of why opposed cores form — the mechanism question is explicitly deferred.
  • Functional effects of the pairing itself are tiny (millinats) — pruning / model-editing applications remain speculative.
  • No downstream-task evaluation; all evidence is next-token diagnostics on English web-like corpora.
  • LM-assisted census labeling noise unquantified; no public code artifact cited.

Questions for Further Exploration 05

  • Does the opposed core persist at 7B–70B, and does instruction tuning / RLHF reshape the sign distribution?
  • Do SAE latents and transcoders inherit the opposed sign of the couples they replace?
  • Can core-aware pruning match Wanda-style magnitude pruning at equal sparsity, given coupling concentrates in 133–471 channels?
  • Which property of ordered text drives the sign — syntax, topical coherence, or local repetition?
  • Is the opposed, contracting core a learned stabilizer of the residual stream, related to attention-sink dynamics?
  • Is the mid-stack mutual symmetry a low-rank reciprocal circuit a graph-level analysis could extract?
