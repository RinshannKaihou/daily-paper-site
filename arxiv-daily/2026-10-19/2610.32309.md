> **一句话总结：** 该论文用"干净决策余量 + 位移 + 增益"三个标量在 Qwen3-32B/Gemma 4 31B 上跨扰动族预测语义脆弱性（AUROC 0.953/0.910），再通过精确的纵向-横向分解与一系列匹配干预证明：标量增益丢弃了方向信息——交换横向朝向可在保持标量几何完全不变的情况下翻转行为，但失败方向高度依赖输入与选项布局（32 个失败改写中 28 个只在四种选项顺序之一失败），因此"共享失败动力学"指共享的预测规律，而非普适的失败向量。

> **TL;DR:** Why does a task-preserving rewrite flip a correct MMLU answer while a larger internal change does not? A compact 3-scalar score (clean margin, incoming displacement, local gain) predicts correct-to-incorrect failures across held-out RUPBench perturbation families with AUROC 0.953 on Qwen3-32B (vs 0.869 for clean margin alone) and 0.910 on Gemma 4 31B, and transfers frozen to ReClor rewrites (endpoint form AUROC 0.978). But an exact longitudinal–transverse decomposition plus matched interventions (equal-norm orientation swaps, Gemma mirror controls, same-answer-letter donors) show scalar summaries omit consequential direction: own-direction attention repairs strictly rescue 13/27 failures vs 0/108 matched random draws, yet 28/32 failing rewrites fail in only one of four option orders — direction matters, but is context-dependent, not universal. A second axis on MonitorBench fixed-CoT replay finds the readout-aligned attribution transfers across math/health domains (ρ=0.434) while the frozen positive-mean causal criterion fails; post hoc signed-effect prediction reaches 73/82 (89.0%). Exceptional evidential hygiene, with several honest negative results.

## 核心贡献

- **跨扰动族的紧凑预测规律：** 仅用三个标量——干净答案余量 M、窗口入口位移范数 D_in、局部位移增益 G_W——的逻辑回归，即可在严格"留出扰动族 + 留出题目"协议下预测任务保持型改写导致的正确→错误翻转。Qwen3-32B 上 AUROC 达 0.953（L52–54 窗口），远超仅用干净余量的 0.869；Gemma 4 31B 在更早的 L38–45 窗口达 0.910（余量基线 0.787）。加入扰动族标识符几乎无增益（0.952→0.951），支持"共享预测规律"而非"共享表面触发器"。
- **精确的纵向–横向分解暴露标量丢弃的信息：** 对窗口两端位移差 δb = (1+q_W)δa + ρ_W·D_in·r̂_W 的恒等分解（纯代数恒等式，不依赖训练），配合作者证明的命题 1（增益与范数相同的端点集合可被线性读出赋予不同值），表明标量传输增益系统性丢弃了朝向信息。
- **多层级匹配干预确立方向因果性：** 等范数方向控制、固定标量几何的横向朝向交换（MMLU 双向效应 +1.702，翻转安全样本 34.1% vs 随机控制 1.1%）、Gemma 入口镜像控制（镜像方向反而比经验供体方向更强抑制下游位移，是供体方向"特权稳定器"假设的实质负结果）、以及注意力组件级冻结规则在 250 个不相交新题上的验证（严格修复 13/27 失败 vs 0/108 匹配随机方向）。
- **答案顺序与答案字母控制划定结论边界：** 四种循环选项布局下，自身方向在全部 37 个失败条件下超过随机与同字母供体控制均值（严格修复 16/37 vs 供体 10/148、随机 0/148），但 28/32 个受影响改写只在单一布局下失败，且每次修复都需重算当前布局的干净参考——方向信息是局部的、上下文依赖的。
- **固定 CoT 回放中"未披露影响"的读出相对度量：** 在 MonitorBench 未披露延续上，横向–读出对齐归因与提示对比的关联跨 math/health 域迁移（Fisher 聚合 ρ=0.434），但冻结的"正均值因果判据"失败；事后符号分析达 73/82（89.0%），作者明确标注其为 post hoc。
- **罕见的证据编年史透明度：** 论文以表格逐项记录每个研究的冻结阶段、选择历史与剩余暴露（如窗口选择属历史发现而非嵌套验证、Qwen 端点参数化事后开发、负结果全部保留），并区分预测、因果敏感性与带符号读出影响三种不可互换的证据。

## Key Contributions

- **A compact cross-family predictive law for semantic fragility:** a 3-feature logistic score (clean answer margin M, incoming displacement D_in, local gain G_W) predicts correct-to-incorrect failures under strict held-family/held-item exclusion — AUROC 0.953 on Qwen3-32B (vs 0.869 margin-only) across six RUPBench families on MMLU, and 0.910 on Gemma 4 31B (vs 0.787) at an earlier window (L38–45 of 60 blocks vs Qwen's L52–54 of 64), showing the measurement structure ports across architectures while the normalized layer location does not.
- **An exact decomposition exposing what scalar gain discards:** the longitudinal–transverse identity δb = (1+q_W)δa + ρ_W·D_in·r̂_W (pure algebra, checked numerically in bfloat16), plus Proposition 1 proving that all endpoints with identical norm and gain can receive different values from any readout with a transverse component — a possibility result that motivates every orientation experiment in the paper.
- **Matched causal interventions at three granularities:** (i) endpoint orientation swaps that hold D_in, q_W, ρ_W, displacement norm, and gain fixed in exact arithmetic, rescuing 33/44 MMLU failures (75.0%) and inducing failure in 34.1% of safe receivers vs ~1.1% for matched random controls; (ii) Gemma entry interventions with mirror controls (u_mir = 2(e·u)e − u, matching entry norm, clean-axis projection, and resulting entry distance) where mirrors out-suppress empirical donor directions on 38/48 targets — a genuine negative result for donor directions as privileged stabilizers; (iii) a frozen failure-conditioned rule targeting Qwen's L54 attention output on 250 identity-disjoint new ReClor items, strictly rescuing 13/27 failures vs 0/108 matched random-direction draws.
- **Answer-order and answer-letter controls that bound the claim:** across four cyclic option layouts, the current-order own direction beats both random and same-gold-letter donor means in all 37 failure conditions (strict rescue 16/37 vs 10/148 same-letter, 0/148 random), but 28/32 affected rewrites fail in exactly one layout and none in all four — directional information is real yet layout-relative, and every repair uses a newly computed clean reference.
- **A readout-relative account of undisclosed cue influence:** on 348 nondisclosed MonitorBench continuations (82 bases), the signed transverse attribution J_trans correlates with the prompt contrast across held-out math/health domains (aggregate ρ = 0.434, CI [0.133, 0.687]) and tracks finite patch effects almost perfectly (ρ = 0.994/0.933), while the pre-registered positive-mean causal rule fails (macro α=1 effect −0.0022); a post hoc zero-threshold sign rule predicts 73/82 patch-effect signs (89.02%, CI [81.7%, 95.1%]) — explicitly labeled as a changed inferential target, not a passed test.

## Methodology

The paper pairs a reference input P0 (clean MMLU question) with a contrast P1 (RUPBench rewrite: checklist, compound, it_cleft, red_herring, stress, wh_cleft — 500 base questions, all six variants; Qwen retains 399 clean-correct bases → 2,394 variant rows, 193 failures; Gemma 428 bases → 2,568 rows, 185 failures). Residual-state differences δ_ℓ,t = h1 − h0 are measured at the final prompt token between window endpoints a < b, yielding incoming displacement D_in, output displacement D_out, and gain G_W = D_out/(D_in + ε). A compact logistic scorer over (M, D_in, G_W) is fit with training-only median imputation and standardization under five-fold item-disjoint splits that simultaneously hold out an entire family.

The exact decomposition splits the accumulated update u_W = δb − δa into a longitudinal coefficient q_W along δa and a transverse residual r_W ⊥ δa with magnitude ρ_W, so G² = (1+q_W)² + ρ_W². This is applied descriptively to natural trajectories (within-item failure-minus-safe contrasts), and causally via interventions: endpoint contraction h54 ← h_C + α(h_T − h_C) with α from 0 to 1.5; equal-norm random/orthogonal direction controls; fixed-geometry orientation swaps that transplant a donor's transverse direction into the receiver's transverse plane while preserving every scalar coordinate in exact arithmetic; a natural-dose factorial over (q*, ρ*); Gemma entry edits at L38 with the downstream L45 state left unpatched, controlled by mirror constructions that match all entry scalars; and attention-component patches (add clean-minus-rewrite output difference at L53/L54.attn/MLP, recompute suffix) against random directions matched for update norm and resulting component norm.

Eligibility is decided from natural states before edited logits are read; no outcome-conditioned dropping or tolerance relaxation is allowed; bfloat16 feasibility gates can make a comparison literally unestimable (the ReClor magnitude factorial has zero feasible groups and is reported as unavailable, not null). Uncertainty uses item/base-cluster bootstrap (10,000 or 2,000 resamples) with Bonferroni-style interval allocation across planned contrasts. A second axis replays fixed generated CoT under a standard cue prompt vs an exact-target lexical control (same target text framed as unrelated), scoring mean target-sequence log-probability, attributing signed transverse influence J_trans = Σ g_t^T r_t at target-prediction positions, and patching H ← H + αR at frozen doses.

The evidence chronology is documented in an explicit table: window selection was historical discovery (not nested in prediction folds); the Qwen endpoint parameterization was developed post hoc after the initial ReClor ratio result but fit only on historical MMLU; the failure-conditioned component rule was frozen before acquiring 250 disjoint original items; the CoT signed-effect outcome was chosen after the mean-effect failure. Negative results (initial all-pair component validation, Gemma CoT replication uncounted, positive-mean criterion failure) are retained rather than replaced.

## Results & Highlights

- **Prediction:** the compact (M, D_in, G_W) score reaches strict held-family/held-item AUROC 0.953 on Qwen3-32B vs 0.869 for clean margin alone (+0.084), and 0.910 on Gemma 4 31B vs 0.787 (+0.123); per-family held-out AUROCs span 0.945 (stress) to 0.959 (checklist, compound). Within-question conditional logistic regression (removing all item-constant difficulty) gives log-gain odds ratio 5.46 (p = 1.05×10⁻¹⁶) and log-displacement odds ratio 2.54 (p = 1.37×10⁻⁹).
- **Frozen MMLU→ReClor transfer:** on 604/680 AI-screened paraphrase variants, the ratio score transfers at AUROC 0.926 but is *outperformed* by clean margin alone (0.956); the endpoint form (M, D_in, D_out) — invertibly related to the ratio features — reaches 0.9782 (Brier 0.0189). Both ratio scorers overestimate mean risk (Qwen predicted 0.0999 vs 0.0248 observed), so ranking transfer ≠ calibrated probabilities.
- **Dose–response causality:** contracting the L54 endpoint toward clean rescues 17.6% of failures at α=0.75, 51.3% at α=0.5, 69.0% at α=0.25, and 80.7% at α=0 (mean gold-margin +4.793); reverse amplification to α=1.5 induces failure in 9.63% of safe variants. All six family-specific contraction slopes are positive (3.03–5.59).
- **Direction specificity:** equal-norm own-clean correction improves margin by +2.153 and rescues 51.4% of 72 failures, vs +0.013 / 2.78% for isotropic random and −0.005 / 1.56% for orthogonal controls; own-clean beats the mean random update on 70/72 cases. Layer-localized correction with a fixed absolute norm peaks at L54 (+0.465) vs L52 (+0.269) and L56 (+0.417), refuting a generic "later is easier" account.
- **Orientation beyond scalars:** transverse orientation swaps with every scalar coordinate held fixed give a signed bidirectional effect of +1.702 on MMLU (44 items; failed-receiver contrast +1.201, safe-receiver −2.204), rescue 33/44 failures (75.0%), and induce failure in 34.1% of safe receivers vs ~1.1% for matched random rotations; the fixed-rule ReClor replication retains the effect (+1.161, CI [0.813, 1.549]) but on only seven primary items. On natural trajectories at L52–54, transverse energy accounts for ~87.2% of the failure-vs-safe squared-gain difference (Δρ²=0.3843 vs ΔG²=0.4408), and the pattern reverses at L54–56 (−0.0446).
- **Component-level:** the initial all-pair validation is negative (all three 98.33% intervals include zero; pooled score 0.0184) despite reducing exit displacement 10.49%; the revised failure-conditioned rule, frozen before 250 disjoint items, strictly rescues 13/27 failures vs 0/108 matched random draws, with family effects 0.583/1.200/1.369 — but the all-clean-correct reference analysis remains null (−0.017, 0.106, 0.091; all intervals include zero). In the option-order study, own corrections strictly rescue 16/37 vs 10/148 same-gold-letter donor and 0/148 random draws, and 28/32 failing rewrites fail in only one of four layouts.
- **Limits of local replay:** on 601 ReClor pairs, path integration cuts the one-point local patch error from 0.1753 to 0.1300 (−25.8%) but a remaining-context discrepancy of 0.3942 (natural prompt effect 1.1451 vs patch effect 0.8995) persists — a finite last-token patch is not the full rewrite effect.
- **CoT axis:** the observational gate passes (J_trans vs prompt-contrast Spearman ρ = 0.331 math / 0.526 health, aggregate 0.434), the frozen positive-mean causal criterion fails (α=1 macro effect −0.0022, CI [−0.0121, 0.0099]), and the post hoc sign rule predicts 73/82 base-level patch-effect signs (89.02%, CI [81.71%, 95.12%]; math 19/20, health 54/62).

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** the exact longitudinal–transverse decomposition and Proposition 1 are elementary linear algebra, and the authors say so; the novelty lies in the disciplined combination — scalar prediction, fixed-geometry orientation swaps, entry-matched mirrors, and component-level donor controls — which goes meaningfully beyond prior robustness work (RUPBench, latent adversarial paraphrasing) and standard activation patching by matching the exact quantities a scalar summary keeps.
- **Rigor:** among the most self-critical papers in this literature — chronology, frozen rules, feasibility gates decided before outcome inspection, item-cluster bootstraps, Bonferroni allocations, and explicit retention of negative results. But several causal stages rest on tiny eligible cohorts: the ReClor orientation replication has 7 primary items, the MMLU equal-dose magnitude cohort 17, and the component/order failure cohorts 21–23 failure items, all below the paper's own frozen twenty-per-family interpretive threshold — the honest labeling does not manufacture precision that is not there.
- **Reproducibility:** models, prompts (non-thinking bfloat16), windows (Qwen L52–54 of 64; Gemma L38–45 of 60), doses, tolerances, and rules are specified to an unusual degree, and the revision was assembled from saved-output archives. However, no code or archive release is mentioned, some historical intervention values are inherited from prior summaries because original archives were not re-openable, and the ReClor magnitude factorial is unestimable under bfloat16 — a real and disclosed, but real, reproducibility gap.
- **Semantic validity of stimuli:** the whole Axis I reading depends on rewrites being task-preserving, yet ReClor rewrites are AI-screen-passed with generator and reviewer sharing the same Qwen3.8-27B checkpoint (correlated screening, no blinded human audit), and the retrospective audit's conservative criterion rejects the compound, it-cleft, and wh-cleft families outright while overlapping the historical fitting data. The stability of AUROC under filtering (0.957 → 0.953) mitigates but does not close this.
- **Red flags / overclaiming controls:** the paper itself flags the two biggest ones — the CoT signed-effect 89.02% is a post hoc target change after the frozen mean-effect rule failed, and every "repair" requires the item's own clean reference (plus gold labels and readout gradients for the diagnostic features), so none of this is a clean-free detector, monitor, or deployable defense; the stale-reference diagnostic (old-order reference flips 24.8% of changed-order conditions to the old letter) shows how easily a letter-level mechanism could masquerade as directional information. Window selection was historical discovery, never nested, so the 0.953/0.910 AUROCs are not fully external.

## Strengths

- Separates three evidence types — scalar prediction, causal directional sensitivity, signed readout influence — that the robustness literature usually conflates, and never lets one stand in for another.
- Matched controls at every level: equal-norm random and orthogonal updates, fixed-scalar orientation swaps, Gemma mirror constructions matching entry norm/projection/distance, same-gold-letter donors, and a stale-reference diagnostic — each closes a specific confound the previous experiment left open.
- The negative results are load-bearing: contraction alone does not imply rescue (Gemma mirrors suppress displacement more yet rescue less), displacement reduction does not imply answer benefit (10.49% reduction with null margin effect), and the initial all-pair component validation is kept alongside the refined positive rule.
- Cross-model and cross-dataset triangulation (Qwen vs Gemma at different depths; MMLU → frozen ReClor transfer; math/health CoT transfer) with parameterization limits (ratio vs endpoint) analyzed rather than hidden.
- The behavior-blind prospective search (20.02% beam failure rate vs 2.49% low-risk controls, 8.04× enrichment) demonstrates the scalar score has practical triage value, not just retrospective fit.

## Limitations

- All causal interventions and diagnostic features require the paired clean reference computation, gold answers, and sometimes readout gradients — nothing here works without privileged access, so deployment as a robustness filter or monitor is unaddressed (and the 89.02% sign accuracy explicitly cannot be converted into monitor detection rates).
- Small eligible cohorts dominate the causal conclusions (7, 17, 21–27 items at the decisive stages), with correlated draws (cyclic orders, donor trials) that bootstrap cannot fully de-correlate.
- Only two ~30B open-weight models; Gemma lacks an identical replication of the Qwen orientation/magnitude/norm experiments and has no completed CoT signed-effect replication; the Qwen L52–54 window is inherited into the CoT axis without re-justification.
- Rewrite semantic preservation is certified only by correlated AI screening; the CoT lexical control changes cue framing rather than achieving semantic equivalence, and fixed-CoT teacher-forced replay says nothing about free-running generation or about what originally caused the recorded rationale.
- bfloat16 precision makes some planned comparisons unestimable and forces tolerance-based (not exact) matching; the paper's central "shared dynamics" claim is, by its own conclusion, a shared predictive regularity — not a universal failure direction, a complete explanation of natural rewrites (0.39 remaining-context discrepancy), or a validated detection method.

## Relevance

直接命中"LLM Hidden States & Interpretability"方向：这是对 LLM 残差流表示几何（纵向-横向分解、窗口增益、读出对齐）与注意力组件级激活干预的系统 mechanistic interpretability 研究，同时其"语义脆弱性的可预测失败规律"视角与推理可靠性/失败模式检测的兴趣相呼应。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Can the (M, D_in, G_W) law be estimated without gold labels — e.g., with self-consistency margins — so the predictor becomes usable as a clean-free fragility triage filter at inference time?
- Does the transverse-dominance signature (≈87% of excess squared gain at L52–54, reversing at L54–56) appear in other architectures and at other scales, or is the window band an accident of Qwen/Gemma training?
- Since 28/32 failures occur in only one option order, what would an unchanged-direction transport test (patching order A's direction into order B) show — full breakdown, or partial transfer that the current per-order references cannot reveal?
- Could the same-gold-letter donor control be strengthened to a full answer-readout basis (all four letters plus distractor geometry) to rule out letter-level mechanisms entirely?
- Does the post hoc 89.02% signed patch-effect prediction replicate under the prepared Gemma L38–45 protocol with a pre-registered sign threshold and dead-zone stability checks?
- Is the remaining-context discrepancy (|P − C_b| = 0.394) reducible by patching multi-position state tuples, or is it an irreducible consequence of attention recomputation over the full rewritten prompt?
