Why this matters

Where probes read risk is not where interventions work — control placement must be established causally, not inferred from probe accuracy.

The paper's probing + activation-steering study of reasoning-model hidden states yields a split with direct methodological weight for interpretability: monitoring signal and causal lever evolve along different trajectories.

Critical edge

Every safety verdict rests on a single judge (LlamaGuard-3-8B), the baseline arms are "controlled adaptations, not reproductions," and nothing is tested on non-distilled reasoners or adaptive adversaries.

Methodology · four frozen stages

1

Distill a direction

170 exact-prompt safe/unsafe CoT–answer pairs are re-encoded by the frozen model; direction = mean safe−unsafe difference, refined by a signed continuation-NLL loss on 32 pairs (lower NLL for safe continuations, higher for unsafe).

2

Calibrate strength

Per-model edit strength α tuned against a relative-perturbation budget (r ≤ 0.55) — endpoint test labels never touched. 8B reference: layer 20, α = 1.

3

Train the gate

Linear gate g(h) = 1[(h−c)ᵀw ≥ τ] on 900 prompts (450 harmful / 450 no-op), reading natively generated pre-edit t2 states; threshold frozen at ≤ 5% dev false-fire rate.

4

Edit once, then leave it alone

At t2 the gate reads the unedited hidden state and either adds αδ a single time or does nothing; all later tokens decode normally. LlamaGuard-3 judges CoT and answer separately — Safe only if both pass.

weights frozen throughout · ≤ 1 edit per trajectory · 8B study = six arms × 3 seeds × 1,223 prompts (22,014 units)

Strengths

  • Dissociation established causally — full post-edit regeneration with within-seed deltas; the negative result (t4 readable but uncontrollable) is as informative as the positive one.
  • Edit vs gate cleanly separated by a token-identity ablation: identical harmful-set scores with and without the gate — the gate's entire job is suppressing benign cost.
  • Careful hygiene: pre-frozen gate thresholds, no endpoint labels in calibration, malformed outputs can't fake safety, exact-text overlap audits, a candid limitations appendix.
  • Replicated across scale (1.5B / 7B / 8B), depth (layers 9–32), seeds, and two harmful sets.

Limitations

  • Distill-only evidence: DeepSeek-R1 distills (Qwen2.5 / Llama-3.1 bases); RL-native or frontier reasoners untested, and window boundaries shift ± 1 token across models.
  • Mechanism unresolved: the norm-matched diagnostic is teacher-forced and 8B-only; the t2 KL advantage fades after the first future token, so why t2 works in free-running generation remains open.
  • Ecology leakage: the SORRY pool shares its source with training data (88 prompts overlap direction/gate sets) — StrongREJECT-273, a single 273-prompt set, is the only source-independent evidence.
  • Benign evaluation is Alpaca-500 short instructions: no math, coding, or long-reasoning capability check; judge reliance on LlamaGuard; gate never tested against adaptive adversaries.

Questions for further exploration

  1. Does the early control window exist in natively RL-trained reasoners, or is it an artifact of distillation training?
  2. What dynamical property of the t2 state lets one edit propagate through hundreds of subsequent tokens — is a commitment direction consolidating between t2 and t3?
  3. Can an edit family (multi-site, low-rank, richer gate features) restore steering effectiveness at t3/t4, where probes read risk best?
  4. How robust is the ~5%-fire-rate linear gate to paraphrase attacks, domain shift, and adaptive adversaries?
  5. Would combining REACT with prefill steering give complementary coverage — or would the two interventions interfere at the representation level?
