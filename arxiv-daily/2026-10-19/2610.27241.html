Beyond the headline
Methodology · distilled

How the lens works

First-order machinery, deliberately minimal

  • Baseline framework — LRE (Hernandez et al., 2024): affine map o ≈ βWs + bβ from a mid-layer subject state; faithfulness = decoding reproduces the model's own top-1 answer (not the gold object), layer & β from a 6×6 grid.
  • Entity lens — Jacobians evaluated only at reference entities of one type, under the single neutral prompt "{} is known for" at one fixed layer (17). Contrastive CEL subtracts same-type reference states; non-contrastive NEL applies the mean Jacobian directly.
  • Anchor dropped — NEL keeps no β at all; a shared β beat the anchor-free form in only 1% of 2,000 splits (Appendix G).
  • Cheap gradients — Jacobian-vector products via double-backward (Pearlmutter, 1994); full Jacobians never materialized. Per-relation diagnostic: A_r = −median_e log₁₀ rank of the model's own answer.
  • Setup — OLMo-3-7B primary (6 more models in appendix); 28 of 47 LRE relations kept, named-entity subjects pre-registered via the dataset's domain_name field; lens and LRE draw demonstrations from independent splits.
  • Reproducible — public pipeline, one command, pinned seeds; ~35 A100-80GB GPU-hours across all seven models.

Assessment · 1 of 2

Strengths

What this paper does unusually well

  • Clean question, minimal readout — after dropping the anchor, NEL has no β hyperparameter at all.
  • Control battery — confound partialling (cardinality, answer frequency), leave-one-type-out, external salience via free generation, reference-set analysis, interventional recombination separating anchor from subject term.
  • Intellectual honesty — surfaced and fixed the protocol artifact (1.000 → 0.74/0.79), reported the counterfactual ρ = 0.416, and reported where a simpler baseline wins.
  • Reusable guidance — negative fact-level results converted into concrete dataset-construction requirements (score complete objects; verify referent resolution).
Assessment · 2 of 2

Limitations

Read it with these in view

  • Correlational at the relation level — no causal claim that the LRE exploits the read-out representation.
  • In-sample selection — LRE layer/β chosen on the scored subjects; faithfulness is an upper-ish estimate, no held-out evaluation.
  • Cardinality tails — over-predicts where elimination wins (NAME_GENDER), under-predicts near-unique answers (PERSON_FATHER); no principled boundary given.
  • Narrow base — 28 relations from one dataset, smallest 19–24 examples; other models weaker and prompt-choice-sensitive ("{} is known for" had to be varied for Qwen3).
  • Fact-level weak (median ρ = 0.235); weak-first-token and reference-competition problems diagnosed, not solved. Entity subjects only, by construction.
