Why this matters

Harness context policies silently decide what an agent can see across turns — task success hinges on harness design, not model capability.

Methodology

Two pre-registered designs

PersistGym-lite · controlled probe

Synthetic episodes where a fact needed at a late step (10-way multiple choice, chance = 10%) is observed or derived, recoverable via a lookup action or not, and inside or outside a 24,000-token sliding window — 8 cells × 200 templates. Harness arms: full history, window, window + the benchmark's one-sentence note-taking instruction, model-written summaries. Confirmatory model gpt-6-luna; episode-for-episode replication on claude-haiku-5-5 (amendment R8). One-sided template-cluster bootstrap (10,000 resamples) with Holm correction, Firth logistic checks, and a passed validity gate (160/160 with the fact restated at need).

ARC-AGI-3 · gap decomposition

The benchmark's own harness (commit 4ca00e6 + 18 patches), Gemini 3.8 Flash at high thinking, four games fixed by a registered rule (published gap ≥ 20 pts, ≤ $60/run). Four configurations S0 → S1 → S2 → S4 each change exactly one component — drop-oldest → summarize, remove notes, add native reasoning replay. One counted 12-hour run per configuration per game; shares are step changes in mean RHAE divided by the total gap.

Other registered findings
  • Cost inversion: the window harness cost 2.9–4.0× full history in dollars while sending only 0.84–0.90× the tokens — dropped turns break prompt caching (94% → 48% cached input; cache priced at 1/10).
  • Notes partially rescue: the one-sentence note instruction lifted the not-recoverable cell from 7.5% → 28.5%.
  • Summaries favor derived facts (exploratory): 92% retained / 92% success for derived vs 38.5% / 43% for observed.
  • Depth effect (H5): Adapter edge in P(clear | reached) grows +0.14 early → +0.44 deep; authors' games 0/2 vs 11/11 deep clears — though it largely restates where runs stopped.
  • Behavioral split under fact loss: claude-haiku-5-5 abstains (0/200 answered); gpt-6-luna guesses near chance (31/387 correct).
Strengths

What holds up

  • Clean factorial probe isolating one conceptual variable — re-observability — with registered manipulation checks, ceiling controls, and a programmatic oracle.
  • Cross-provider replication of the same +92/+101-point contrast, plus a meaningful abstain-vs-guess behavioral split.
  • Decomposes ARC-AGI-3's own documented gap using the benchmark's own harness, not a bespoke agent.
  • Quantified, counterintuitive cost finding: truncation is 2.9–4.0× pricier via cache invalidation alone.
  • Exemplary transparency: pre-registration, dated amendments, both-labels reporting, open code, cost ledger, frank self-criticism.
Limitations

Where it wobbles

  • ARC decomposition rests on one counted run per cell; run-to-run variance is comparable to the effects themselves.
  • Replay share hinges on a single replaced run (FT09 S2 under R7, adopted after levels were seen); replay is never isolated — probe hypothesis H2 stopped.
  • One model per provider; agreement says nothing about other models or real tasks.
  • Probe is synthetic — the not-recoverable penalty is partly by construction (the finding is the contrast, as the authors note).
  • Games selected for a large published gap; H3/H5 at ceiling or restate stop points; probe power fell below target (0.65 / 0.78 vs 0.80); costs are provider- and period-specific.
Questions for further exploration
01Does the re-observability contrast transfer to real tasks where state is externalized into files, code, or tool outputs (SWE-bench-style agents)?
02What is the replay share with multiple runs per ARC cell — or a cheaper probe analog of native replay, since H2 stopped?
03Could a provider-neutral "unrecoverable-fact ledger" recover the remaining 14% of the Standard→Adapter gap?
04Why do summaries preserve derived facts (92%) far better than observed facts (38.5%) — is re-derivation cheaper than verbatim retention?
05Is the deep-level clear-rate advantage compounding state loss, or an artifact of ordered level play near early ceilings?
06Does the cache-breaking cost inversion scale across providers with different cache pricing (1.25× writes vs 0.1× cached)?
