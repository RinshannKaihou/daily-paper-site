Key finding 关键发现

Post-training doesn’t erase what a model knows about authorship. It makes the model read that knowledge out backwards.

Why this matters 为何重要

Probes can decode what a model knows without proving it uses that knowledge. Fine-tuning can invert the readout while the representation survives — an audit target.

Practical upshot: compare the state at the question and answer-cue positions, not just at the text.
Critical edge 批判视角

All three headline authorship corpora are unreleasable, so the central claim rests on data no reader can inspect or re-analyze.

Only derived tables are public, and the fully reproducible public task runs in the opposite direction.

Methodology 方法

A lens-model framing: separate encoding (text cues → internal state, measured by probes) from readout (state → answer, measured by d′ and criterion c).

04 · ANSWERd′ = √2·z(AUC)
05 · INTERVENEstitch + patch + steer
  • Corpora. 300 student chat messages (61 human / 239 AI, truncated to 25 words); 103 news segments from 14 never-digitized local articles (38 / 65); 238 textbook summaries (119 matched pairs). Human original plus all derived texts form one resampling group, so results cannot ride on topic recognition.
  • Models. Gemma 3 4B and 12B, Qwen3 8B, OLMo 2 7B, Apertus 1.5 8B, OLMo 3 7B (Think / Instruct / RL-Zero from one base), each with stage-wise checkpoints from SFT through DPO / RL.
  • Measurement. d′ from the ROC of the log-odds difference removes threshold miscalibration: a criterion shift lowers accuracy but cannot manufacture below-chance sensitivity. Grouped bootstraps over whole source texts, 1,000–2,000 resamples.
  • Controls. Character and word n-grams, 14 surface statistics, a probe on the raw input embedding, an external English detector (EditLens), and a label-shuffle probe.
  • Interventions. Steering against 100 norm- and scale-matched directions; model stitching justified by CKA and perplexity; activation patching at 5 position types × 12 depths.

The base-rate test is the paper’s defence against “the threshold was just miscalibrated”: moving the stated prior moves the criterion, not the sensitivity, but post-trained models overshoot the ideal-observer evidence shift by 1.3–3.9×.

When it happens 何时发生

Stage-wise checkpoints put the change in supervised fine-tuning; DPO and RL barely touch it.

d′ = 0

OLMo 2: −0.12 base → −0.50 after SFT; DPO and final RL change nothing material. The median effect is already complete at the first post-training checkpoint.

Where it lives 定位

Stitching puts the carrying weights in early layers. Patching puts the transfer in the positions after the text. The two answers differ, deliberately.

At layer 16, swapping text-token states changes nothing in either direction. Swapping only the question positions moves the entire difference: base states into SFT give +0.41 (Apertus) and +0.04 for OLMo 2 (from −0.50); SFT states into base give −0.42 and −0.61. Inside the after-text span, the answer cue carries most of it (−0.75 across all after-text positions vs −0.46 at question tokens alone). The pattern holds from roughly layer 12–14 onward.

Chats get worse than random labels (C1 − C3: −0.36 [−0.69, −0.06]); held-out news lands at the subset ceiling. At item level, chat answers track a base-model news authorship probe (ρ = .68 [.60, .73]; −.11 for the untrained baseline) and survive controlling for word n-grams (partial ρ = .63), while n-gram scores add .06 [−.06, .18]. Training data can set a readout direction that then generalizes to the wrong genre.

Strengths and limits 优势与局限

What holds up

  • Signal detection does real work: a criterion shift cannot produce below-chance sensitivity, so the threshold explanation is ruled out by argument, not assertion.
  • Stage-wise checkpoints are used properly; the SFT-only timing replicates across OLMo 2, Apertus and three corpora.
  • Two causal interventions on the same checkpoints, plus probe transfer at text vs. answer positions, triangulate a claim probes alone could not carry.
  • The public replication is a model for unreleasable data: same analyses, one script, opposite sign. The item-level training prediction is tested against n-grams and wins.

Where it strains

  • No headline corpus is shareable; external reanalysis of the central claim is impossible. The reproducible case runs the other way.
  • “Below chance” is about graded log-odds: in 23 of 36 plain-prompt conditions the model gave the same binary answer to over 95% of texts.
  • The misread information is lexical — n-grams match every probe, and an input-embedding probe reaches d′ 1.48. This is surface style, not rich authorship.
  • Stitching hybrids overshoot; the matched near-chance control was never run. Scope is 4–12B, German prompts, 14 news articles, one LoRA study.

Open questions 待解问题

01

Does below-chance authorship readout survive scaling to 30–70B, or is it a small-model artifact?

02

Which SFT conversations install the misreading? The census found no authorship requests, so the carriers must be indirect — AI-talk or style-heavy rewrites.

03

Can a matched near-chance, unchanged judgment serve as the stitching control the paper lacks?

04

Is “formal nominal style = AI” a stale stereotype learned from text about AI rather than from AI text?

05

Does intervening at answer-cue positions at inference restore d′ without damaging instruction following?

06

How much of the inverse ordering is German-specific? The AI texts were judged more verbal and lexically diverse.
