只改训练题的顺序、或给每题加一段学出来的前导语，不改模型、不改奖励函数、不改训练配方：Llama-3.2-3B 在 SafeRLHF 上三轮之后，答案质量从 88.6% 掉到 32.1%。作者把这种崩塌归因于「裁判与策略共享同一套参数」的耦合。

Understanding and Mitigating the Fragility of Self-Rewarding Optimization

Rongyi Zhu · Tanqiu Jiang · Fenglong Ma · Ting Wang Stony Brook CS / Penn State IST

Key metric · AQ drop

−56.5PTS

88.6% → 32.1% answer quality. Llama-3.2-3B on SafeRLHF, SRLM, three rounds, under a Preamble attack that only edits the prompts. Re-Ranking lands at 39.9%.

Why this matters

Any self-reward loop fed from an external question pool is exposed — and SRF tells you, with 32–256 oracle labels, whether it is about to collapse.

自奖励训练的攻击面是数据管道，不是模型。

Critical edge

On the headline model, simply freezing the judge beats the proposed defense — 87.7% vs 85.2% AQ — and needs no probe gradients at all.

01Methodology

One model, two jobs

  1. Shared-parameter SRO. πθ answers, rθ judges; K=5 samples per prompt become (y⁺, y⁻) pairs fed to DPO (β=0.1, 3 rounds). Instantiated as SRLM and CREAM.
  2. Threat model: prompts only. Re-Ranking resamples 3,000 questions weighted by judge–oracle disagreement κ; Preamble DPO-trains a prefix that lifts the self-score while leaving the oracle score flat. 攻击者只能动「问哪些题、怎么措辞」。
  3. Diagnostic. Self-reward fidelity SRF(θ)=E[1−|o−rθ|] — on-policy agreement between the model's verdicts and a GPT-5 oracle, in [0,1].
  4. From correlation to intervention. Flip ε of the self-reward labels: ε<0 purifies disagreement, ε>0 injects error. Model and recipe held fixed, only label fidelity moves.
  5. Defense. 16 fixed probes → verdict-score gradients → orthonormal basis Q; every Adam step θ←θ+Δ−αQ(QᵀΔ), α=0.5. No oracle labels at defense time.

03Strengths

What survives scrutiny

  • Minimal threat model. Change only which questions are asked; SRO still dies. Sharper than poisoned labels or a corrupted reward model.
  • The negative-ε control. Denoising at ε=−0.8 lifts AQ to 95.6% — one knob, both directions, so the threshold reading isn't just "noise bad."
  • Frozen-judge control with a matched first update. The gap isolates amplification from label corruption — the right ablation.
  • Candid cost accounting. Reports that 44% of removed update energy was beneficial, that probes transfer only +2.8/+3.2 pts cross-domain, and that 16→128 probes buys 1.3 pts for 8.5× compute.
  • Cheap diagnostic. 256 oracle labels land within 0.1% of full evaluation; 32 labels still separate clean (90.5%) from attacked (73.3%).

04Limitations

Where to push back

  • One task carries every claim. All main defense tables are SafeRLHF; "SRO robustness" here means safety-alignment SRO robustness.
  • SRF has a distribution-shift confound. Stronger policies produce harder answers, which lowers agreement even with an unchanged judge — never controlled for.
  • Theory is one step. Props. 4.1–4.2 are local, fixed-batch statements; no multi-step collapse theorem, so the threshold is documented, not predicted.
  • Single seed, no code. The authors state the point estimates "do not establish statistical significance"; several conclusions rest on ~1-point margins.
  • Attack realism. Re-Ranking needs oracle labels plus K rollouts across the whole question pool. The oracle-free variant is weaker and sits in Appendix F.
  • Signal-blind projection. α damps all sensitive directions regardless of sign — a plasticity tax that is harmless at 8B and decisive at 3B.

05Open questions

What the paper leaves on the table

  1. Freeze the answer set and re-score it with the current judge each round — does SRF still fall before AQ, or is the apparent drop mostly harder on-policy answers?
  2. Why does a frozen judge win on Llama-3.1-8B (87.7 vs 85.2) but lose on Qwen-2.5-7B (79.4 vs 81.7)? Can round-1 SRF drop predict which defense to pick?
  3. Can projection be made sign-aware with a cheap correctness proxy, so the ~44% beneficial removed energy survives clean training?
  4. How does the threshold scale with model size and with K? Only two points on the scaling curve (3B near 74–77%, 8B near 72–81%).
  5. Does the same prompt-level fragility appear when the judge is a separate model — and if not, does that confirm shared parameters as the causal mechanism?
  6. What happens under an adaptive attacker that knows the probe set and optimizes preambles into the null space of Q?
