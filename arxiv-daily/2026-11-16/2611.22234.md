> **一句话总结：** 本文系统审计了"推理重要性"这一被剪枝方法默认信任的信号，发现它在三个环节都不可靠——用最终答案 token 做归因会在所有 CoT 模型上饱和（NLL 仅 10⁻⁴–10⁻³，据此剪 attention head 在 D1/N1 上比用推理 token 归因低 27.6 和 32.2 个准确率点），单神经元因果重要性低于测量噪声底且正负相互抵消（90% 的组出现符号反转），"推理专属"重要性也无法与通用重要性分离；由此给出四条测量规则：对推理 token 打分、在 ≳100 单元的粗粒度上归因、不要做推理专属对比、用准确率而非困惑度评估。

> **TL;DR:** Auditing the importance signal that reasoning-aware pruning consumes, this single-author study shows that scoring final-answer tokens saturates (answer-token NLL 9×10⁻⁵ to 3.6×10⁻³ across four models) because the CoT already states the answer, and that head pruning guided by this target costs **+27.6 points on DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B and +32.2 points on Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct** on GSM8K versus scoring CoT tokens (5 seeds, p<0.001), while CoT perplexity barely moves and even reverses sign at 7B; single-neuron attribution is below the measurement floor (median 2.4×10⁻⁵ vs a 1.0×10⁻⁴ sham floor) and only becomes reproducible at ~100-neuron groups.

## 核心贡献

- **目标脆弱性（Target fragility）**：以最终答案 token 作为归因目标（T_ans）在全部四个模型上饱和——答案 token 的 NLL 仅 9×10⁻⁵ 到 3.6×10⁻³——原因是 CoT 文本内部已经写出了答案（answer-in-CoT leakage，用 T_rec − T_ans 度量，四个模型为 1.24–1.65）。
- **后果可测量且很大**：在各自 f₁/₂ 工作点上，用 T_ans 而非 T_cot 指导 head 剪枝，D1 损失 27.6 点（95% CI [+18.8, +37.3]）、N1 损失 32.2 点（CI [+26.4, +38.6]），五个种子全部为正；格式成功率从 97%+ 掉到 56–64%。
- **粒度脆弱性（Granularity fragility）**：单神经元因果重要性在数值上不可测（中位效应 2.4×10⁻⁵，低于 1.0×10⁻⁴ 的 sham 扰动噪声底），跨数据划分的排序是噪声（G=1 时 split-half Spearman 0.059，G=3 时 −0.366），且效应会相互抵消（50 个 100 神经元组中，45 组的组效应为正而成员效应符号和全为负，符号不一致率 0.90）。可复现的重要性只在 G*≈100 出现（split-half Spearman 从 G=30 的 0.367 升到 G=100 的 0.781）。
- **特异性脆弱性（Specificity fragility）**：对比式隔离出的"推理专属"重要性无法与原始重要性区分——其与原始排序的差异只有自身噪声底的 1.00/1.08/0.97 倍（k=5/10/20%）；而保护"专属"集合反而比保护"通用"集合更伤推理（ΔNLL 0.42 vs 0.70 @1%、0.98 vs 1.31 @5%、1.47 vs 1.68 @10%）。
- **一个预注册的负结果**：蒸馏导致的重要性集中度并不能解释剪枝代价（四模型上 rank correlation 为 −0.80，但 n=4、两个 7B 点噪声主导、且集中度与规模混淆），作者将其报告为"未解决"而非"被推翻"。
- **准确率–困惑度解耦**：损伤只在准确率上可见，在困惑度上几乎不可见，7B 上两者符号相反——对任何只报告 perplexity 的压缩工作都是警告。

## Key Contributions

- Reframes reasoning-aware pruning as resting on an unexamined measurement assumption and turns that assumption into the object of study, auditing it along three axes (scoring target, granularity, specificity) rather than proposing yet another mask.
- Establishes answer-in-CoT leakage as a *universal* property of step-by-step-then-answer generation, not a distillation artifact: leakage is 1.54 in non-distilled N1 versus 1.46 in distilled D2. What tracks distillation is concentration of the saturated signal (top-1 head mass 0.969/0.996 for D1/D2 vs 0.260/0.598 for N1/N2).
- Quantifies the downstream cost with a proper power analysis: seed standard deviation is 5.9–10.4 points at 1.5B but 17.5–20.5 at 7B, which is exactly why the 7B arm is labelled unresolved rather than null.
- Separates the pathology from a confound with a T_rec control (answer tokens with CoT removed), showing the problem is the answer-after-CoT copy effect, not answer tokens in general (Δ(T_cot − T_rec) = +27.6 on D1, +35.9 on N1).
- Provides a measurement-floor argument for granularity: two independent criteria (reproducibility onset, numerical-floor crossing) both place G* at 100, and the cancellation statistic (0.90 sign disagreement, group effect a median 0.105× the summed singletons) shows single-unit effects do not compose.
- Pre-registers a mechanism hypothesis and reports its failure in full, including the confound that makes it uninterpretable either way.

## Methodology

**Design.** A 2×2 grid over distillation and scale: DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B (D1) and 7B (D2) versus size/family-matched Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct (N1) and Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct (N2). D1/D2 are distilled from the Qwen2.5 family, so N1/N2 are near-matched controls that isolate distillation from scale and tokenizer.

**Importance estimator.** Head importance = increase in NLL on a chosen token set when a query head's output is mean-ablated (replaced by its calibration-set average). Three scoring targets are compared: T_ans (final-answer tokens with the CoT present, the naive default), T_cot (the CoT reasoning span, the corrected choice), and T_rec (answer tokens with the CoT removed, a control separating "answer tokens" from "answer-after-CoT"). Neuron importance uses attribution patching over G-neuron MLP groups (post-SwiGLU activations feeding `down_proj`; 8,960 per layer, 250,880 total for D1).

**Pruning mechanics.** Whole query heads are removed by zeroing the rows of W_Q and columns of W_O; with grouped-query attention, K/V projections are shared and left untouched (max ‖W_K‖, ‖W_V‖ norm change asserted to be exactly 0.0 in all four models). Head-level granularity is chosen because (a) the audited attribution methods operate at comparable coarse units, (b) heads are structured, hardware-friendly units, and (c) the paper's own granularity result shows neurons are below the trustworthy scale.

**Evaluation.** GSM8K, ≥500 held-out problems, disjoint from the 128 correctly-answered CoT calibration trajectories per seed (permissive exact-match extractor accepting `\boxed{}`, "the answer is X", or the final number). Metrics: accuracy, CoT-token NLL, format-success rate. Greedy decoding, 1024-token budget, bfloat16, so calibration resampling is the only stochasticity.

**Operating points.** Accuracy under head pruning does not decay smoothly — it holds near dense and then falls off a cliff — so a single sparsity level is a poor comparison basis. The paper locates f₁/₂ (the pruned-head count at which accuracy reaches half of dense) per model, with a **pre-registered gate** requiring the swept curve to contain a point in the 25–50%-of-dense band; all four models pass (D1: 40 heads/11.9%, N1: 44/13.1%, D2: 118/15.1%, N2: 118/15.1%). For the 1.5B models the coarse sweep initially skipped the band and the gate rejected it; refining to single-head steps recovered a genuine slope.

**Statistics.** Five seeds at 1.5B, three at 7B; each seed resamples calibration, recomputes importance, re-prunes, and re-evaluates. Intervals are hierarchical bootstraps resampling both seeds and test items; seed standard deviations are reported instead of item-only p-values on seed-averaged accuracy. Everything runs on a single H100 80GB, ~75 GPU-hours total, PyTorch 2.13 / Transformers 5.13.

## Results & Highlights

- **Answer-token NLL saturates in all four models** (Table 2): D1 6.5×10⁻⁴, N1 2.0×10⁻⁴, D2 9×10⁻⁵, N2 3.6×10⁻³ — three to four orders of magnitude below the CoT-token NLL the corrected target optimizes. The residual signal is also degenerate: the top-1 head holds 0.969 of the T_ans mass in D1 and 0.996 in D2, versus 0.260 (N1) and 0.598 (N2); T_ans ranks heads nearly orthogonally to T_rec (Spearman −0.008 to 0.119) and only weakly agrees with T_cot (0.120–0.347).
- **The saturating target costs 27.6 and 32.2 accuracy points at 1.5B** (Table 3, 5 seeds): D1 dense 63.0% → Δ = +27.6 (CI [+18.8, +37.3], p<0.001, seed s.d. 10.4); N1 dense 74.0% → Δ = +32.2 (CI [+26.4, +38.6], p<0.001, seed s.d. 5.9). Positive in all five seeds of both models. The T_cot-guided sweep holds format-success above 97% at the same sparsity while the T_ans masks collapse to 56–64%, so this is loss of reasoning, not loss of output structure.
- **At 7B the evidence does not resolve**: N2 shows +20.5 (CI [−7.4, +38.4], p=0.07) and D2 shows −1.1 (CI [−18.6, +21.2], p=0.79) with per-seed values that flip sign (−7.2, +22.8, −18.8); seed s.d. is 20.5 and 17.5 points respectively. The 7B absolute accuracy gaps, however, still point the same direction as 1.5B for N2, so the direction is consistent even where the test is underpowered.
- **Accuracy and perplexity decouple, and reverse sign at 7B**: on D1, T_ans-guided pruning inflates CoT-NLL by +3.18 alongside the accuracy collapse, but on D2 and N2 the NLL gap is **negative** (−0.20, −0.35) even where the accuracy gap is positive (N2 +20.5). Any pruning paper reporting only perplexity could therefore rank two pruners backwards.
- **The gain is not a weight-magnitude proxy**: a target-agnostic head-magnitude baseline (sum of W_Q-row and W_O-column norms) sits at or near the floor at f₁/₂ in all four models — 1.2% and 0.8% accuracy on D1/N1 at 40 and 44 heads — so T_cot's advantage is not recoverable by a reasoning-blind scorer. (The text says "on D1 at 40 and 44 heads"; 44 is N1's operating point, a minor slip.)
- **Concentration tracks distillation but does not explain the cost**: the dominant T_ans head is identical across all five calibration resamples and decisive over the runner-up by 113× (D1) and 6539× (D2), unstable in N1 (two distinct winners) and only 16× in N2; D1 and D2 concentrate onto the *same head position* across a 4.6× scale gap. Yet the pre-registered prediction that concentration predicts Δ fails: rank correlation −0.80 across four models, with the most concentrated model (D2, 0.996) showing the smallest, non-significant gap.
- **Single-neuron importance is below the floor and irreproducible** (Section 7): median |ΔNLL| = 2.4×10⁻⁵ for G=1 against a 1.0×10⁻⁴ sham-perturbation floor; split-half Spearman is 0.059 at G=1, −0.366 at G=3, 0.367 at G=30, and 0.781 at G=100, stabilizing above 0.8 thereafter. The mechanism is cancellation, not redundancy: across 50 groups of 100 neurons the group ablation effect is positive in 45/50 (90%) while the signed sum of member effects is negative in 50/50, with the group effect a median 0.105× the summed singletons.
- **Reasoning-specific importance is not separable**: the divergence between the "specific" ranking and raw importance is 1.00/1.08/0.97× its own split-half noise floor at k=5/10/20%. With a length- and token-type-matched shuffled-CoT control (itself reproducible at split-half Spearman 0.983), the rank correlation with raw importance rises to 0.509 and top-k overlap is 0.71–0.79, failing the pre-set 0.7 separation bar. Direct ablation agrees: the top raw-importance neuron group damages reasoning more than the top reasoning-specific group at every budget (ΔNLL 0.70 vs 0.42 at 1%, 1.31 vs 0.98 at 5%, 1.68 vs 1.47 at 10%; random groups 0.007–0.085).
- **Preliminary probe (explicitly no claim)**: ablating the concentrated head alone drops GSM8K from 60.4% to 2.6% on D1 and 68.8% to 1.4% on D2, but random early-layer heads are wildly variable on D2 (+1.2, −1.8, +45.4), which is why the authors decline to draw a single-head causal conclusion.

## Authors & Credibility

Single-author paper by Himanshu Mishra (University of British Columbia, student email). No prior large-lab track record is evident from the paper itself, but the work reads as methodologically mature beyond the usual single-author submission: pre-registration of a mechanism hypothesis, an explicit power analysis (five versus three seeds, seed s.d. reported instead of inflated item-level p-values), a pre-registered gate on the accuracy cliff, a control target (T_rec) and a reasoning-blind baseline, and an appendix that reports the uncontrolled probe while refusing to draw a claim from it. The citations are appropriate and, notably, the author verified *from source* that the two closest methods (Lucas et al. 2025's CoT reconstruction; Sheth et al. 2026's causal attribution) do **not** use the saturating target, and says so explicitly — a rare and credibility-enhancing refusal to strawman prior work. Code, seeds, calibration draws, held-out test identifiers, per-job artifact hashes and figure scripts are promised at `github.com/himans-iitk/Reasoning_in_Pruning`.

## Critical Assessment

**Genuine strengths.** This is a well-designed audit rather than a method paper, and the design choices are unusually disciplined. The T_rec control is the key one: without it, a reviewer would immediately object that the T_ans failure is just "answer tokens are hard"; with it (+27.6 on D1, +35.9 on N1 relative to T_cot), the pathology is pinned to the answer-after-CoT copy effect. The power analysis is honest to the point of self-harm — reporting D2 as *unresolved* rather than null when its per-seed values span −18.8 to +22.8 is precisely the behaviour the field claims to want and rarely exhibits. The accuracy–perplexity sign reversal at 7B (−0.20, −0.35 NLL alongside a +20.5 accuracy gap on N2) is the single most transferable finding here, because it invalidates a widely used evaluation shortcut.

**Weak baselines and missing comparisons.** The negative controls are thin in two places. First, the weight-magnitude baseline sits at 1.2%/0.8% accuracy — essentially floor — at f₁/₂; a head-level L2-magnitude pruner being that bad is plausible, but a **random-head-pruning baseline is never reported for the main sweep** (it appears only for neuron groups in Section 8), so the reader cannot tell how much of the T_cot curve's advantage over T_ans is "any sensible ranking" versus "specifically CoT-token attribution." Second, no comparison against an actual reasoning-aware pruner (Lucas et al.'s reconstruction objective, Wanda/SparseGPT calibration) is run end-to-end; the paper is careful to say it audits the *signal*, not the method, but that framing also means the practical magnitude of the problem for deployed pipelines is inferred rather than measured.

**Estimator circularity in Section 7.** The cancellation statistic (90% sign disagreement, group effect 0.105× summed singletons) compares a *true group ablation* against the *signed sum of attribution-patching singleton estimates* — and the paper itself reports that attribution patching failed its per-neuron validation gate (rank-stratified Spearman 0.65 against true ablation). Mixing an unvalidated linear estimator into a claim about whether effects "compose" is a real confound: some of the observed cancellation could be estimator bias at the neuron level rather than a property of the network. The reproducibility argument (split-half Spearman rising to 0.781 at G=100) is estimator-agnostic and carries the claim better than the cancellation panel does; the paper leans on both as if equally strong.

**Generality.** "Universal" saturation is measured on **four models, all from the Qwen2.5 lineage** (D1/D2 are Qwen2.5 distillations, N1/N2 are Qwen2.5-Instruct). That controls the confound beautifully but bounds the claim hard: the evidence cannot distinguish "all CoT models saturate" from "Qwen2.5-family CoT models saturate." The same caveat applies more strongly to Sections 7–8, which are run on one model family and, for the neuron experiments, largely on D1. Task coverage is GSM8K only — grade-school math with short, highly templated CoTs, the regime most likely to state the answer verbatim inside the reasoning. Whether leakage-driven saturation survives on long-form CoT (competition math, code, agentic traces) is exactly the question a practitioner would ask and the paper does not answer.

**Scope honesty versus framing tension.** The limitations section is candid (no end-to-end latency, head pruning in tension with 2:4 semi-structured sparsity, math only, 7B underpowered), and the "Scope of the claims" paragraph is exemplary. But the title and abstract still use "pruning language models" and "universal," which sit uneasily with a 1.5B, GSM8K, one-family evidence base. A stricter title would have been "…for pruning small math reasoners."

**Minor.** The "1.2% and 0.8% on D1 at 40 and 44 heads" sentence conflates D1's and N1's operating points. f₁/₂ is located from the T_cot sweep, so the T_ans-versus-T_cot comparison is evaluated at an operating point chosen on T_cot's curve — a mild asymmetry, though Figure 2's full sweeps mitigate it. The pre-registered negative (concentration) occupies a full section for a result the authors themselves say is uninterpretable at n=4 with scale confounded; it is admirable transparency but thin as a section.

## Strengths

- Audits an assumption that the reasoning-aware pruning literature genuinely leaves unexamined, and does so *before* proposing anything — the framing as "prior to" rather than "instead of" is correct and productive.
- The T_rec control cleanly separates the mechanism (answer-after-CoT copying) from the surface description (answer tokens), which is what turns an observation into a causal story.
- Rigorous statistics for the scale of the study: pre-registered gate on the accuracy cliff, five seeds at 1.5B, hierarchical bootstraps over seeds *and* items, seed s.d. reported, and explicit refusal to interpret underpowered 7B results.
- Two independent criteria (reproducibility onset, numerical-floor crossing) converge on G*≈100, which is much stronger than a single-threshold argument.
- The accuracy–perplexity decoupling result is immediately actionable for anyone reviewing compression papers, and the sign reversal at 7B makes it impossible to dismiss as noise.
- Unusually good citation hygiene: verified from source that the two closest methods avoid the saturating target, and says so.
- Fully specified compute (~75 H100-hours), versions, seeds and artifact hashes; a single-GPU reproduction is realistic for an academic group.

## Limitations

- Evidence base is four models from one family (Qwen2.5 and its R1 distillations) and one benchmark (GSM8K); "universal" and "language models" overstate the reach.
- The headline pruning cost is established only at 1.5B with five seeds; the 7B arm (three seeds, seed s.d. 17.5–20.5 points) cannot resolve D2's sign at all, so the strongest claim is deliberately narrow.
- No random-head-pruning baseline in the main sweep and no end-to-end comparison against a real reasoning-aware pruner, so practical impact is inferred rather than demonstrated.
- Neuron-level results rest on an estimator (attribution patching) that failed its own per-neuron validation gate (Spearman 0.65), leaving the cancellation statistic partly confounded with estimator bias.
- Head-level pruning is orthogonal to the 2:4 semi-structured sparsity that actually produces GPU speedups, and no latency or memory numbers are reported — the paper is about the signal, not about achieved compression.
- The pre-registered concentration test is uninterpretable as run (n=4, concentration perfectly confounded with scale), so Section 6 consumes space without producing knowledge either way.
- Task coverage excludes long-form and non-math CoT, which is where answer-in-CoT leakage may behave very differently.

## Relevance

直接命中"Reliability under Extreme Performance Optimization"——它正是剪枝在推理任务上造成能力退化的工作，且给出了"用困惑度评估会看错方向"的硬证据（7B 上准确率与 NLL 符号相反）。对"LLM Hidden States & Interpretability"也有价值：它用量化手段说明因果归因在单神经元粒度不可测、可复现重要性只在 ~100 单元尺度出现，这对任何基于重要性/电路的剪枝或可解释性流水线都是必须先满足的测量前提。

## Questions for Further Exploration

1. Does answer-in-CoT leakage survive on long-form CoT (competition math, code generation, agentic traces) where the final answer is not restated verbatim, or is saturation an artifact of GSM8K's short templated solutions?
2. Is the T_ans-versus-T_cot gap still 20–30 points at 7B and above with enough seeds to resolve it, or does scale genuinely buy robustness to a bad importance target?
3. What happens with a random-head-pruning baseline at each f₁/₂ — how much of T_cot's advantage over T_ans is "any coherent ranking beats a degenerate one" rather than CoT-specific signal?
4. If the cancellation in Section 7 is recomputed with true per-neuron ablations (not attribution patching) on a small subset, does the 0.90 sign disagreement persist, shrink, or invert?
5. Can the importance signal be fixed rather than only measured better — e.g. a leakage-aware target that subtracts the CoT's own answer mention, or a token-reweighted CoT objective that emphasizes intermediate steps over restated answers?
6. Does the accuracy–perplexity decoupling generalize to quantization and KV-cache eviction, where perplexity is likewise the default sanity metric?
7. Would a de-confounded concentration test (concentration varied within a fixed scale, powered 7B arm) show the inverse relation the −0.80 correlation hints at, or does the mechanism simply not exist?
