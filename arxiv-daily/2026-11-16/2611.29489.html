Why this matters

If you compress visual tokens in production, a ten-line logit bias recovers grounding accuracy that pruning destroys — no retraining, no new selector.

对任何做 MLLM 部署、视觉定位、高分辨率或视频输入的工程，即刻可外挂。

Critical edge caution

The head probe is tuned on 500 samples from RefCOCO val — one of the eight reported evaluation splits — and the flagship 66.8 depends on a grounding-specific configuration.

Methodology training-free · two knobs

01 — Decompose

Each decoder output is a mixture of modality summaries, O = σ·v̄_img + (1−σ)·v̄_txt. Pruning shifts it by an information term plus a share term — so pruning becomes output-shift minimisation, not token ranking.

02 — Select (WCS)

Greedy maximisation of a saliency-weighted facility location, F(S)=Σ w_i max_{j∈S} s_ij with 1−1/e guarantee. Saliency comes from whichever ViT head a label-free probe picks; grounding adds a spatial kernel whose scale widens as the budget tightens.

03 — Reallocate (AR)

Add b = log(N/k) to every retained visual logit: retention-ratio only, features untouched, so v̄_S is unchanged. For VQA, a capped per-cell version returns content mass instead.

04 — Deploy

The bias is injected through PSR inside the attention kernel, so FlashAttention-2 stays intact. Incremental gains cut selection on 32-frame video from 5.07 s to 0.61 s.

Baselines are re-implemented from released code and budget-matched by the mean number of visual tokens per decoder layer. Probe cost: ~500 samples per task family, offline.

Esum

Information lost. Bounded by attention-weighted coverage — what WCS maximises. Cuts the measured summary error 17.2 → 14.3.

Ealloc

Share lost. When visual tokens vanish, softmax renormalises over the survivors and the text side takes the freed mass. Bias cuts this 13.6 → 3.8.

At layers 13–19 the image's share sits at 51–64% of its unpruned value — even under random sampling. CAR's total output error is 14.0 against 17.4–22.4 for prior methods; output KL 0.150 against 0.194–0.302.

+ Strengths

  • One mechanism, two symptoms. It explains why VQA survives pruning while grounding collapses, verified layer-by-layer (51–64% share) and term-by-term (Fig. 3).
  • Genuinely plug-and-play. Closed-form, retention-ratio-only, feature-preserving — a practitioner can add it in ten lines and gain twenty points.
  • Unusual breadth for a pruning paper. Four backbones including DeepStack and dynamic tiling, 8 VQA benchmarks, 8 grounding splits, 3 video benchmarks.
  • Ablations that test the theory, not just the method — plus the released "bias on every baseline" table, which changes how the field should report results.

− Limitations

  • Not calibration-free. Fixing the probe head needs ~500 labelled samples per task family, and the grounding probe overlaps a reported evaluation split.
  • Task-specific configs carry the headline. Uniform bias plus kernel for grounding, capped per-cell plus no kernel for VQA; a unified setting costs 3.0 points.
  • Theory-to-practice bridge is correlation. A loose triangle-inequality bound with equal-norm assumptions; the surrogate is encoder-side, the bound decoder-side.
  • Small margins, no error bars. InternVL +1.2, video +0.5, LLaVA +2.2 — and 9.9% grounding is still 22% below unpruned.

Questions for further exploration open problems

When does the bias over-restore? The exchangeability argument assumes E[ρ] = k/N, but the retained tokens are the salient ones, so their true share is higher. The per-query distribution of σ′/σ is never measured.

Does the probe gain survive a leak-free probe set? Probe on RefCOCO train and evaluate on val: the +7.7-point head-probe contribution deserves that replication.

What if pooling were retuned? The +17.7 over biased pooling assumes pooling gets no per-cell bias and no matched budget schedule.

Can the bias form be chosen automatically? Measure whether unpruned attention is spatially concentrated or diffuse, instead of declaring the task family up front.

Why does the bias help every selector by the same amount? If the gain is nearly selector-independent, the residual gap should be attributable to Esum alone — re-plot Fig. 3 to test that additivity.

Does this reach decoding? Quantisation and KV-cache eviction also distort attention mass; the same reallocation argument may apply beyond prefill.
