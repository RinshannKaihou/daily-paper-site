Mean-replacing 5,000 neurons cuts evaluation awareness to ≤1.4% — but refusal falls through a separate channel.

Disentangling Verbalized Evaluation Awareness and Refusal through Neuron-Level Intervention

Jingyuan Feng, Gouki Minegishi, Andrew Gambardella, Takeshi Kojima, et al. · University of Tokyo · 4 × 32B models · 64 layers · 1.77M gated-MLP neurons scanned
causal intervention evaluation awareness refusal QwQ-32B / OLMo 3 mediation analysis
中文速览一句话总结

在同一批 CoT 内对比 VEA 句与非 VEA 句的神经元激活，均值替换把 VEA 率压到 ≤1.4%；保留拒答方向分量的干预又把拒答从 0.504 拉回 0.548——可见 VEA 文本只解释拒答下降的 4–10%。

Key metric
≤1.4%
from a 12.6–51.8% baseline

VEA rate after mean-replacing 5,000 selected neurons — QwQ-32B on AgentHarm, OLMo 3 SFT/DPO/RLVR on SorryBench & StrongREJECT. A count- and layer-matched random control stays near baseline.

Why this matters

A recipe for causally dissecting a high-level model behaviour neuron by neuron — and a warning that one LLM judge can end up as both measuring instrument and intervention target.

Critical edge

The VEA rate is a single gpt-5-mini draw at temperature 1, truncated at 40k reasoning characters — and the intervention is validated on the very annotator that defined the target.

No human agreement study on the Yes/No label; only a 1–11% verbatim-quote mismatch audit.

Methodology within-sample contrast, not a steering vector

  1. Label inside one CoT. Take rollouts already judged VEA-positive. The sentence containing the quoted awareness text becomes V; every other token in that same rollout becomes O. Task, prompt and sample identity stay fixed.
  2. Rank all MLP units. Compute Cohen's d per neuron per layer between V and O activations (64 × 27,648 units), pool ranks across layers, take top-N for N ∈ {1k … 30k}. Both signs eligible, so the set mixes promoters and suppressors.
  3. Intervene by mean replacement. Overwrite each chosen activation with its non-VEA mean mj before the down-projection, on prompt prefill and every decode step. Control: random neurons outside the top 50k, matched in count and layer distribution.
  4. Isolate one component. Build a variant that projects the intervention delta off the prompt-end refusal direction r̂, preserving the MLP write along r̂ exactly — the only difference from the plain intervention.
  5. Mediate. 440 SorryBench questions × 3 seeds under no / plain / preserving intervention split the refusal drop into VEA text, the refusal-direction write, and a residual.

Independent corroboration

  • Word frequency: “testing” falls 13.86% → 0.19% of rollouts at N = 30k, while “instructions” holds at 43.56% vs 47.50% — a targeted shift, not global muting.
  • Probe transfer: a frozen layer-63 probe on 262 fixed VEA segments drops 4.089 vs 0.484 for random controls; on prompts before any reasoning, 1.977 vs 1.069.
  • No VEA left to delete: on rollouts with no test-category VEA under either condition, 30k neurons still cut refusal 0.565 → 0.464 (random control 0.545).
  • Weight space: VEA neurons align with the layer refusal direction at 0.37% vs 0.09% (4.1–6.5× enrichment) — but only 111 of 30,000 neurons, mean |cos| 0.0152 vs a 0.012 floor.

Strengths
  • Controls built right. Same count, same layer distribution, drawn outside the top 50k by |d| — and run at every intervention size, so the claim is about these neurons, not perturbation magnitude.
  • The “only one component” trick. A variant differing from the plain intervention solely along the refusal direction makes pathway (iii) identifiable instead of argued.
  • Honest tail statistics. Reporting mean |cos| barely above the random floor, alongside the enrichment, is more informative than a flattering average.
  • Nulls are printed. The continuation experiment's null, the random control's large probe effect, and the non-significant steering result all appear in the text.
Limitations
  • One model carries the mechanism. Mediation, the preserving intervention and the probe are QwQ-32B only; OLMo checkpoints get VEA rates and neuron counts, no refusal analysis.
  • Judge-defined target. One draw, no repeated annotation, no inter-draw agreement, 40k-character truncation that could bias the SFT→RLVR comparison.
  • No polysemy diagnostic. Sentence-level labels can absorb adjacent clauses; 58% of top-1k neurons sit in layers 32–52, suggesting a distributed mechanism.
  • Underpowered and unreleased. Generality is 100×2; EvalAwareBench has no random control; no code or artifact release is mentioned.

Questions for further exploration

01

Does leave-one-benchmark-out localisation hold — neurons discovered on SorryBench, evaluated on AgentHarm in QwQ-32B — or does in-domain selection carry the ≤1.4%?

02

Only 111 of 30,000 neurons are strongly refusal-aligned, yet pathway (iii) is credited with ~half the drop. What happens if the intervention is restricted to those 111?

03

Are trace lengths and VEA-sentence positions systematically different across SFT / DPO / RLVR — and does the 40k cap therefore bias the post-training trend?

04

Could the refusal-component-preserving intervention serve as an evaluation-time correction, and would “VEA-suppressed, refusal-preserved” scores change model rankings on safety benchmarks?
