Why this matters

Sinks, massive activations and gradient sinks stop being three separate stories: one operator, plus diagnostics you can reuse to judge VGA, Softpick or DyT.

Critical edge

Every Pythia training comparison confounds the gate with an MLP-width change (4096 → 3584), and no width-matched baseline was trained — so part of the effect may be capacity, not gating.

01 — How it works

Sink → covariance spike → contraction

  1. A, Vrow-stochastic attention × values
  2. CY = ⅟d YYᵀE[CY|A] = σV² Σj A:,jA:,jᵀ
  3. spike along ājrank-one mode vs the MP edge λ+
  4. Γ = diag(G)post-gate energy a²αs ≤ a²
  • Sink as an eigenvector, not a heatmap artifact. With C_Y = ⅟d YYᵀ and an isotropic-value approximation, every high-incoming-mass source column enters as a rank-one term, so its direction is the normalized attention column ā_j. Detection becomes alignment against a data-adaptive projector P_sink (≤ 8 positions); strength becomes “is the spike supercritical vs the Marchenko–Pastur edge”.
  • The gate never touches A. ỹ_i = G_i ⊙ y_i with G_i = σ(x_i W_g) ∈ [0,1]^d, head-specific and elementwise, applied after SDPA. That yields Γ_i = diag(G_i), 0 ⪯ Γ_i ⪯ I.
  • One operator, two halves. Forward: the sink signal is scaled by exactly α_s = ‖Ds‖₂² ≤ 1. Backward: ∇v_sL = Σt≥s atsΓ_tᵀδ_t gives ‖∇v_sL‖ ≤ B·M_seff ≤ B·M_s, with M_seff = Σ_t atsκ_t, κ_t = ‖Γ_t‖op ≤ 1.
  • What is actually run. Public Qwen-1B baseline / gated checkpoints, inference only, on 16 × 512-token WikiText blocks; a matched Pythia-410M pair trained on 15B FineWeb-Edu tokens at seq-len 4096, AdamW @ 4e-4 — the gated twin’s MLP narrowed 4096 → 3584 to pay for gate parameters.

02 — Claims, graded

Proved vs. idealized

Theorem 3.1 (forward, deterministic).

1/d ‖Ỹsig‖F² = a²αs ≤ a²

Holds for any learned gate, no distribution assumed. But it only contracts a signal component that already exists — it says nothing about how the sink formed.

Theorem 4.1 (backward, “gradient valve”).

‖∇v_sL‖ ≤ B Mseff ≤ B Ms

The sink column need not be removed; filtering each query→source value path once is enough to weaken its gradient statistics. A second-moment version follows when upstream gradients are uncorrelated across positions.

Proposition 3.2 (the BBP part).

αs > σ²√c′ ⇒ outlier vanishes

Proved only for a random row-selection surrogate whose retained noise stays i.i.d. Gaussian. A learned elementwise gate is neither a row selector nor isotropic, so this is flagged by the authors as an “idealized spectral reference”, not a theorem.

06 — The number that cuts back

Gating blocks the sink at the output, not at birth

The gated model’s pre-gate sink alignment is 0.220 versus 0.062 for the baseline — 3.5× higher before any gating is applied.

If the gate had suppressed sink formation, its upstream representation should be less sink-aligned, not more. The consistent reading is that the model still builds the mode and the gate blocks it downstream — a result that would strengthen the gradient-valve story, but the paper never remarks on it, and the abstract’s “suppresses the co-emergence” language reads more strongly than the data.

A permutation control makes the same point about geometry: swapping the attention-column / value pairing while holding A, V and Psink fixed raises baseline alignment 0.062 → 0.196 (100% of layers), so the aligned direction comes from column structure, not from a privileged sink value.

07 — Strengths

What holds up

  • One operator, two stories. The forward and backward halves share Γt; this is the part that will outlive the specific diagnostics.
  • Honest theorem scope. 3.1 is deterministic for learned gates, 3.2 is explicitly labelled an idealized reference, and the appendix spells out exactly what fails (noise modulation, higher-rank subspaces).
  • The right counterfactual. Caching δt from the real backward pass and recomputing with G ≡ 1 isolates the valve without changing weights; r = 0.91 is predictive, not merely directional.
  • Controls allowed to fail. The attention–value permutation, the value-anisotropy interpolation (alignment 0.97 → 0.64) and the probe-standardization check that dissolves the apparent gated probe advantage are all reported.
  • Robustness is real: 410M and 2.8B, 4K and 8K contexts, plain vs EOS-separated formatting, plus a sink-position sweep.

08 — Limitations

What to discount

  • Capacity confound. MLP 4096 → 3584 across every Pythia table (2, 7, 8, 9, 11, 12); a baseline-3584 is never trained, and it is unstated whether the 2.8B pair reused the same compensation.
  • BBP is decoration. No experiment varies αs within a layer to show λ₁ crossing σ²√c′ — the proposition’s actual prediction goes untested.
  • Forward headline is between-checkpoint (two separately trained Qwen-1B models), so value anisotropy, attention entropy and MLP allocation all fold into “the effect of gating”. The within-model contrast exists but gets less weight.
  • Thin selectivity base: a 22% relative retention gap on 64 modes of one model family, and the scalar control removes token- and feature-selectivity together. No feature-shuffled gate.
  • Alignment values are small in absolute terms (0.062–0.280) and move with k, T and normalization choice — 0.97 → 0.64 under a different covariance normalization.
  • M₂ / M∞ are layer- and sample-averaged maxima, so the 81× / 136× Qwen ratios read louder than they are; the single 17,536 coordinate is the clearer statement.
  • Coverage gaps: Softpick only descriptively, VGA with no experiment, nothing on DyT, no wall-clock or memory cost of the gate, no functional long-context or retrieval task.

09 — Open threads

Questions for further exploration

  1. Does a single layer’s λ₁ actually cross σ²√c′ when αs is swept by construction (interpolating Γ from I to the learned G)? This is the central prediction and appears untested per layer.
  2. Why is the gated model’s pre-gate alignment 3.5× the baseline’s — does the valve make upstream sink modes cheaper to maintain?
  3. How much survives a width-matched baseline (MLP 3584, no gate)? A three-way 4096 / 3584 / gated-3584 run separates gate from capacity in one shot.
  4. Can a feature-shuffled gate (permute G across the feature axis, keep token structure) isolate feature-direction from token-position selectivity?
  5. Trained Softpick drops mtopk 24.3× while λ₁/λ₊ moves only 15.39 → 13.74 — is the residual spectral mode a different object, e.g. a value-anisotropy artifact?
  6. Corollary 4.2 assumes uncorrelated upstream gradients; how much of the measured ρ₁ drop comes from the coherent bound versus the gate changing δt itself?
  7. Would any intervention that suppresses massive activations reproduce the +1.10 downstream gain, or is the spectral mechanism doing work?
