Chain-of-Thought Hidden States Reveal Task-Solving Directions for Direct Answers

Houman Mehrafarin, Ioannis Konstas | 2026-11-16 | Heriot-Watt · Edinburgh
activation patching chain-of-thought linear probes representation steering LLaMA 3.1 8B · Qwen 2.5 7B

把每条 CoT 隐状态 patch 进 direct prompt，用“能否答对”作因果标签；再取每题两类状态的均值构造对比方向，一次性旋转过去。

Key metric

46→85%

Macro accuracy over 25 tasks, LLaMA 3.1 8B (CoT: 69%). Qwen 2.5 7B: 52% → 86%. Single SLERP rotation, 19 generated tokens vs 197 / 245 for CoT.

Why this matters

It measures how much computation sits unused inside a direct prompt's last state — and shows a per-question direction can reach it.

direct prompt ≠ no computation

Critical edge

Building the direction needs the question's own chain, a patched forward pass per cached token, and the gold answer — so the headline is an oracle upper bound, not an inference-time method.

reported as such in Limitations, not in the abstract

Methodology

Two 7–8B instruct models, 25 arithmetic / logical / temporal / spatial / causal / linguistic tasks (GSM8K, StrategyQA, 23 BIG-Bench-Hard subtasks), 9,120 questions. Greedy decoding, seed 42, ~8,000 GH200-hours.

  1. Chain first. Generate a CoT (≤400 tokens) and cache hidden states at three late layers — 27/29/31 (LLaMA), 23/25/27 (Qwen).
  2. Patch for labels. Write each cached state into the direct prompt's final position and re-decode. Gold answer appears → recovering; otherwise failed. Only 17% (LLaMA) / 28% (Qwen) of states are recovering.
  3. Probe the labels. Standardised L2 logistic probe, class-balanced, 5 folds split by question so no chain is scored by a probe that saw it. Out-of-fold AUROC 0.836 / 0.796.
  4. Build a per-question direction from the class means c⁺, c⁻ (only 27° / 21° apart): d_q(λ)=‖c⁺‖(c⁺−λc⁻)/‖c⁺−λc⁻‖, λ = 0.5.
  5. Rotate once. SLERP the last prompt state toward d_q with t = 1 — direction changes, norm is preserved — then decode normally.

Questions lacking a recovering or failed state (350 LLaMA / 192 Qwen; 96% / 97% coverage) get no direction and are scored incorrect under every intervention.

  • Where both prompts fail, it still works. Of 1,867 questions where direct and CoT are both wrong (LLaMA), the direction fixes 1,192 (64%); Qwen 1,014 / 1,441 (70%).
  • Decodable where the model cannot answer. LLaMA scores 0.4% on Multistep Arithmetic without CoT, yet its probe reaches AUROC 0.88 on those states; LLaMA AUROC spans 0.709 (Boolean Expressions) to 0.921 (Temporal Sequences).
  • Not a position artefact. Within-position selectivity 0.284 / 0.263, positive in all 50 task×model probes; the position-only control probe stalls at 0.552 / 0.532.
  • Partly task-general. 587 of 600 cross-task pairs beat chance; same-reasoning-type transfer 0.710 / 0.672 vs 0.654 / 0.623 cross-type, permutation p < 10⁻⁵.

Strengths

  • Causal per-token labels (does the patched state produce gold?) instead of correlational probe targets.
  • Probing done right: out-of-fold by question, nested penalty selection, two Hewitt–Liang controls with selectivity reported.
  • Builds the direction from class means and measures why probe weights are wrong (pattern vs weight cosine 0.99 vs 0.30; fold stability 0.99 vs 0.82).
  • Honest accounting: missing-direction questions counted wrong, dropped subtasks named, the failed regressor reported, GPU budget disclosed.
  • Negative results get the same prominence as positive ones — failed-mean 9%, per-token 6%, task averaging 0, cross-task −18.

Limitations

  • Not deployable: each question needs its own chain, patch labels and gold answer; amortised cost exceeds CoT.
  • No within-task cross-question swap and no random / shuffled-label direction control for the intervention itself.
  • Direct baseline is hobbled — “do not show steps” leaves LLaMA at 9% on GSM8K — so +39 is measured against a floor.
  • No comparison to CAA, ITI, latent CoT vectors or spherical steering; only direct and CoT prompts.
  • Single seed, greedy decode, no error bars; λ, t and layer are tuned per model on the evaluation tasks, and Qwen prefers layer 27 (85 vs 82 at 25).
  • “A tenth of CoT's tokens” is composition-driven: 4.6× on GSM8K, 55 vs 140 tokens on Multistep Arithmetic, and it excludes the chain needed to build the direction.
  • Probe–intervention tension unexplained: Qwen Causal Judgement at AUROC 0.524 yet 56% → 86%.

Questions for further exploration

  1. Apply q's dq(0.5) to a different question q′ of the same task, matched on answer type and difficulty. If that also gives ~85%, the specificity claim collapses.
  2. How much survives a random unit direction at the same layer and norm, or a direction from shuffled recovering/failed labels? The failed-mean control is only a weak version of this.
  3. Can a small trained head predict the direction from the direct prompt alone? One attempt failed; what architecture and how much data?
  4. Does it hold for reasoning-trained models (R1-distills, QwQ) where the direct prompt may already be internally aligned — and beyond 8B?
  5. Why does the direction work when the probe is at chance? Nonlinear subspace, or a separate “commit and compute” axis?
  6. On GSM8K the direction still emits 39 tokens of arithmetic: latent computation, or just suppressed verbal boilerplate? A terse-CoT baseline would separate the two.
