Why this matters

Routing entropy is a diagnostic, not a goal: healthier MoE routing can coexist with flat accuracy, so balance interventions need independent evidence.

路由健康度是诊断指标而非优化目标；负载均衡干预是否值得做，需要单独的收益证据。

Critical edge

The null is underpowered: n = 6 leaves a 95% CI of −4.7 to +7.3 points, so the title's “without improving accuracy” claims more than the interval supports.

Also — no external or dense-head baseline, so 66–68% weighted accuracy has no absolute meaning.

Methodology two arms, one head

  • Backbone, no foundation model. Text: frozen 300-d GloVe → BiLSTM → one Transformer block. Speech: 34 acoustic descriptors → 2× Conv1D → BiLSTM → Transformer. Motion: head/hand/facial markers as a 200×189 map → 3 strided Conv2D. Each branch emits 128-d.
  • Identical expert head in both arms. Fused f ∈ ℝ²⁵⁶ → softmax router over 7 experts → Σ wₖ Eₖ(f) → 4 classes. Deliberately no auxiliary load-balancing loss.
  • Control (EMoE). Convex scalar gate gt, gs, gm (Σ = 1), then projected to 256-d.
  • Treatment (CMET+EMoE). Before fusion, every modality attends to both others across all six directed pairs (4 heads, dk = 32); a GELU gate α blends own and cross-modal content. α is affine, not convex in [0,1] — the authors flag it.
  • Data & runs. IEMOCAP 4-class subset: 4,936 utterances; sessions 1–4 train, session 5 (unseen speakers) held out. Six seeds for accuracy, routing statistics on three retained checkpoints.
  • Measurement. Per-sample normalised entropy plus effective experts as mean per-sample perplexity (not exp of mean entropy), with argmax share and >1% coverage.

Strengths

  • A genuinely controlled comparison: identical head, router, optimiser, schedule and data; only the pre-fusion representation changes.
  • Routing measured directly — per-sample entropy, per-sample perplexity, argmax share, coverage — rather than inferred from accuracy.
  • Effect sizes and confidence intervals reported next to p-values; an F-test, not eyeballing, for the variance claim.
  • Honest counter-evidence kept in: the most collapsed run scored highest, corroborating the decoupling within-condition.
  • The 13.4-point seed spread is quantified and tied to the common single-run IEMOCAP comparison.
  • Actionable framing: router health is a diagnostic, and load-balancing losses should not be assumed to transfer to low-resource regimes.

Limitations

  • No external or dense-head baseline, so accuracy carries no absolute meaning — 66–68% could be good, bad, or just this backbone.
  • Confounded intervention: CMET changes expert inputs as well as router inputs and adds parameters; no router-only ablation identifies the mechanism.
  • Underpowered null: n = 6 with CI −4.7 to +7.3 does not exclude a small real gain.
  • Thin routing statistics: three seeds; the per-class routing figure is one seed with expert indices unmatched across runs.
  • Test-session checkpoint selection inflates absolutes; single split, fixed K = 7, four-class subset with “happiness” excluded.
  • No load-balancing-loss arm, though the paper argues such objectives may not transfer — asserted by reasoning, not experiment. Per-run routing data only “on request.”

Questions for further exploration

  1. Run the router-only ablation: CMET-fused input to the router, scalar-fused input to the experts. Does diversity persist when experts still see the impoverished representation?
  2. Add one auxiliary load-balancing arm — does it repair routing and leave accuracy flat too, or behave unlike the architectural fix?
  3. Is 1.88 effective experts a property of the data regime or of K = 7? Sweep K ∈ {3, 5, 7, 15} at fixed data.
  4. Does the decoupling survive more data — full 5-fold IEMOCAP, or MELD / CMU-MOSEI?
  5. Why did the most collapsed run score highest: noise, or a useful inductive bias when the training signal is scarce?
  6. Would expert-output probes (representation similarity, output disagreement) show that CMET's “specialists” are functionally redundant?
