Why this matters

Critical edge

Elicit & label

Forced choice between two groups on a trait (“Who is more trustworthy, men or women?”) under an adversarial persona, in Direct-Answer and Chain-of-Thought. 60 questions for identification, 80 balanced held-out for evaluation; 4-bit NF4, greedy, English. Responses are unfair, fair, or unclassifiable; the labeller agrees with blind human annotation at 0.867.

Score & mask

Each head is scored by the mean squared projection of its o_proj slice onto a reference vector, separately over fair and unfair sets, standardised within layer, over at most the first 64 response tokens. The prescribed set is top-20(unfair) \ top-20(fair) → 10 heads, zeroed at every decoding position with a KL > 10⁻⁴ fidelity check.

Intervene

Four prompts — DA, CoT, verbose, terse — break the length–label coupling while the ten heads stay fixed; nothing is re-identified. Four outcome predictions were pre-registered, and two failed.

The layer story also flips

Selectivity sits earlier than chance: centre of mass 11.72 vs 13.5 (p = 0.011) on Qwen, 13.67 vs 15.5 (p = 0.021) on Llama. Seven of eight model × reference × labeller cells are earlier; none reproduce the reported late-layer concentration.

A single random baseline is uninformative about both magnitude and sign. The stored prescribed sets sit at the 33rd and 62nd percentiles; the Llama complement sits at z = +2.13, outside its band, so its claim rests on item-level asymmetry rather than rate movement.

Strengths

audit design
  • A control map falsified by its own intervention: the obvious fix for this confound returns ρ = −0.950, and every extra correlational control agrees with the wrong answer.
  • A proper positive control: the complement set replicates on near-disjoint reselections (2–3 of 10 heads shared) while the prescribed set never helps, and the single counter-directional flip is disclosed.
  • Denominator discipline: 31% unclassifiable and an apparent Δ = −0.127 show how the wrong convention manufactures debiasing — caught in the authors’ own earlier analysis.
  • Nulls as distributions: ten layer-matched draws, not one point, plus two reference readings and two labellers for every headline conclusion.
  • Pre-registration: four predictions stated, two failures reported.

Limitations

self-reported + audit
  • Scale: 60 identification questions against the original’s 2,516 — “the method does not replicate” cannot be fully separated from “the reduction loses the effect”.
  • One family member: the length intervention tests only DiffHeads’ generative, judge-labelled subfamily; the critique is argued to generalise, the evidence is not.
  • One arm for collinearity: ϕ = 0.951 comes from terse alone (n = 60); the converse direction is arm-dependent and unmeasured elsewhere.
  • Prompt is a bundle: wording, instruction position and response format co-vary, so the 64% is a bound, not an identified cause.
  • CoT is not measurable here: 20/60 and 24/60 unclassifiable, token counts censored at the cap, elicitation analysis resting on nine unfair examples.
  • Exposure & provenance: 0.867 agreement inside 250 characters vs 0.696 beyond; all labels by one annotator; scoring never independently re-implemented; 4-bit NF4.
中文 relevance · 为什么值得读

与「LLM Hidden States & Interpretability」高度相关。它审计的不是某一个去偏方法，而是「头归因 + 消融」这一整类方法的结构效度：相关性对照可能给出 ρ = −0.950 的错误答案，必须用干预打断混淆。对任何「定位某个内部单元负责某个行为」的研究都可直接迁移；对评测可靠性、测量陷阱（退化生成被算作去偏、单次随机基线不可用）同样有参考价值。
