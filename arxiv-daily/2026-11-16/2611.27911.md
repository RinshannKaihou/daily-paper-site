> **一句话总结：** 作者用层集合上的容斥分解（Möbius 变换）定义"连通分量" Û_T——只有当某一组层同时参与、且无法被任何真子集的单独前向解释时才出现的那部分计算——并在 Pythia/OLMo/Amber 七个公开检查点上追踪发现：该量相对末层实际写入的强度在全部 35 个"模型×阶数"单元中都比初始化或最早检查点更大（Pythia-6.9B 的 k=6 达 R₆=64.4），删掉它带来的 WikiText-103 困惑度损失在 35/35 单元中大于同位置等范数随机方向（Pythia-6.9B k=3 为 809× vs 2.0×），但它与 CKA 表示相似度只是负相关（ρ=−0.66…−0.19）而不可互换——在 Amber-7B 上按 CKA 选块删除的排序甚至方向反转。

> **TL;DR:** The paper turns "depth" from an architectural count into a measurable learned property: for any layer set T it computes the exact inclusion–exclusion (Harsanyi/Möbius) component U_T of the residual write, normalizes it by the final participating layer's actual write (Û_T), and tracks it across public checkpoints of Pythia (410M–6.9B), OLMo-1B and Amber-7B on WikiText-103. Û_T rises above its baseline in all 35 model–order cells (R_k from 1.62 for Pythia-1B at k=2 to 64.4 for Pythia-6.9B at k=6), with Pythia-1.4B staying statistically indistinguishable from R_k=1 until ~2.1B tokens and separating afterwards. Deleting U_T at the structurally determined site hurts perplexity more than a norm-matched Gaussian direction with identical magnitude in all 35 cells (Pythia-6.9B, k=3: 809× vs 2.0×), while pairwise CKA is negatively correlated with pairwise Û_{i,j} in all seven models and, on Amber-7B, ranks candidate removal blocks in the wrong direction (CKA ρ = +0.18/+0.01/−0.07 vs coordination ρ = +0.57/+0.49/+0.32).

## 核心贡献

- **给出一个精确、可干预的"跨层联合计算"估计量**：把每层视为恒等 + 写入 f_ℓ(h)=h+g_ℓ(h)，对层集合 T 做容斥求和 U_T(h)=Σ_{S⊆T}(−1)^{|T|−|S|}F^S(h)（F^S 为只保留 S 中层的子前向）。该式与直接展开前向得到的 δ_ij 完全相等，不是近似，且在恒等消融下任何真子集都无法解释它。这是把 Harsanyi 红利/Möbius 变换第一次系统用于"层"这一玩家集合，并保留向量值写入与非交换映射。
- **提出"写相对"归一化，使跨训练阶段可比**：因为残差写入幅度本身会随训练变化，作者用末层在实际上下文中的写入作分母，Û_T=‖U_T‖/(‖g_{ℓ_k}(F^{T<ℓ_k}(h))‖+ε)，把交互强度表示成"相对于该层自己写多大"的无量纲量，并定义训练前后比 R_k=Û_T^final/Û_T^base。
- **发现功能深度是"学出来的"而非架构给定的**：全部 35 个模型×阶数单元 R_k>1；初始检查点上 Û_T 每升一阶约减半（Pythia-410M：0.35/0.14/0.06/0.03/0.02），训练后各阶都回到 O(1)（0.62/0.54/0.58/0.72/0.98），即训练抹掉了"高阶交互天然衰减"这一初始化特征。
- **证明该分量在因果上承重**：在 U_T 结构上确定的位点（ℓ_k 输出后）同时做"删 U_T"与"删等范数随机方向"两种干预，35/35 单元前者更伤困惑度，排除了"这只是代数余项"的解释；并给出 R_k 脱离 1 的训练时间点（Pythia-1.4B 约 2.1B token）。
- **厘清表示相似度与跨层协调的关系**：七个模型上成对 CKA 与成对 Û 均为负相关（−0.66…−0.19），但两者给出的块删除排序在 Pythia 上常有 8% 以内的一致、在 Amber-7B 上则背离甚至反向，说明"表示像"不等于"计算独立"，相似度不是剪枝的充分信号。

## Key Contributions

- **An exact estimand for joint computation, not a proxy.** U_T is the Möbius transform of the set function induced by identity ablation, so U_T(h) is by construction the part of the forward pass that no proper subset of T reproduces; the paper shows (Eq. 5) that for |T|=2 this equals the forward-pass expansion term g_j(h+g_i(h))−g_j(h) exactly, and generalizes the inclusion–exclusion to arbitrary order.
- **A scale-free normalization tied to a structural site.** Splitting the alternating sum by whether the last layer ℓ_k is included (Eq. 9) shows every surviving term is an update produced by ℓ_k under a different subset-conditioned input; the denominator is exactly the S=T_{<ℓ_k} term, so Û_T is measured in units of that layer's real write and the deletion site is determined structurally rather than by approximation.
- **A developmental claim with a control at initialization.** The baseline Û_T is non-zero even at random weights (0.35→0.02 as k goes 2→6 for Pythia-410M), so the finding is not "coordination appears from nothing" but "the decay with order is removed by training": final values 0.62/0.54/0.58/0.72/0.98 are O(1) at every order.
- **A matched-norm causal control rather than an ablation-only argument.** Both interventions subtract a vector of identical per-token norm at the identical residual site, so the perplexity gap is attributable to direction alone; the paper reports all 35 cells rather than a favorable subset.
- **A concrete, actionable negative result for similarity-based pruning.** CKA-selected blocks and coordination-selected blocks agree within 8% in 10 of 15 Pythia cells and pick the same block at k=2 on Pythia-6.9B and k=4 on OLMo, but on Amber-7B the CKA ranking has ρ=+0.18/+0.01/−0.07 — i.e. the sign is wrong — while coordination keeps ρ=+0.57/+0.49/+0.32.

## Methodology

**Setup.** A pretrained transformer with L layers is written as a residual stream h^{(ℓ)} with each block f_ℓ(h)=h+g_ℓ(h), where g_ℓ is the full attention+MLP write. For a layer set T=(ℓ_1<…<ℓ_k) and subset S⊆T, F^S(h)=f_{s_m}∘…∘f_{s_1}(h) is the forward pass retaining only S in original depth order, every unselected layer replaced by identity; F^∅(h)=h.

**Estimand.** U_T(h)=Σ_{S⊆T}(−1)^{|T|−|S|}F^S(h). Interpretation: the change produced by all of T that is not explained by the forward pass of any proper subset. The paper is careful that U_T≈0 covers two distinct cases — ℓ_k writes the same update regardless of predecessors, *and* the case where each predecessor does change the update but the changes add as a sum of per-layer contributions (lower-order coordination cancels in the alternating sum).

**Normalization.** Decomposing by whether the last layer is included gives U_T(h)=Σ_{S⊆T_{<ℓ_k}}(−1)^{k−1−|S|}g_{ℓ_k}(F^S(h)), so Û_T=‖U_T‖/(‖g_{ℓ_k}(F^{T_{<ℓ_k}}(h))‖+ε) is the component in units of the last participating layer's actual write. The authors explicitly warn that Û_T is **not** a fraction (components need not be orthogonal) and is **not** bounded by 1. The training ratio is R_k=Û_T^final/Û_T^base, where base is the init checkpoint when available (Pythia) and otherwise the earliest released checkpoint (OLMo 4B tok; Amber ≤1.26T tok). Because baseline Û_T shrinks with order, R_k is only comparable within an order.

**Models and data.** Pythia 410M/1B/1.4B/2.8B/6.9B (8 checkpoints each, 0–64k steps, 134B tokens), OLMo-1B (7 checkpoints, 4B–3T tokens), Amber-7B (8 checkpoints, ≤1.26T tokens). All measurements on WikiText-103, averaging U_T(h) per token position over contiguous chunks; the CKA and perplexity measurements use the same corpus and token sample so coordination, similarity and deletion damage are compared on one distribution.

**Probed tuples.** Only adjacent depth windows T={ℓ,…,ℓ+k−1} (L−k+1 per model), so subset passes and the full pass share the same input state and no intermediate layer is bypassed — this keeps the intervention site well defined.

**Interventions.** Record base point h at the input of ℓ_1, run the 2^k subset passes, subtract U_T from the residual immediately after block ℓ_k's output, continue the rest of the model. The control subtracts, at the same site, a Gaussian direction rescaled per token to exactly ‖U_T‖. Reported factors are post-intervention perplexity divided by clean perplexity. Block removal compares k consecutive layers chosen by coordination score vs by CKA, with Spearman ρ between each criterion's block score and post-removal perplexity (correct ranking: ρ>0 for coordination, ρ<0 for CKA).

## Results & Highlights

- **Coordination strengthens in every cell.** On WikiText-103, R_k>1 in all 35 model–order cells. Pythia-410M's Û_T goes 0.35/0.14/0.06/0.03/0.02 at baseline to 0.62/0.54/0.58/0.72/0.98 at the final checkpoint, giving R_k=1.76/3.86/9.06/21.3/47.1 for k=2…6; Pythia-6.9B reaches R_6=64.4 (0.02→1.40) and Pythia-1.4B R_6=60.8. The weakest cell is R_2=1.62 for Pythia-1B — still above the R_k=1 null.
- **Coordination arrives at a training phase, not as smooth drift.** For Pythia-1.4B (134B tokens) all orders sit near R_k=1 through the earliest checkpoints, and the 95% bootstrap intervals over adjacent windows stop containing 1 only after ~2.1B tokens; OLMo-1B starts from a later baseline (4B tok) yet still reaches R_2=2.05, the largest k=2 ratio of the seven models.
- **Deleting the connected component is far worse than deleting a matched random direction.** All 35 cells have the same sign, with large margins in Pythia: Pythia-6.9B k=3 gives 809× perplexity vs 2.0× for the norm-matched control (404× ratio of damages) and k=5 gives 1472× vs 162×; Pythia-1.4B k=6 gives 224× vs 19.8×; Pythia-2.8B k=5 gives 167× vs 3.9×. The control leaves clean-model perplexities of 34.7/30.4/26.1/22.0/19.3/24.4/11.0 for 410M/1B/1.4B/2.8B/6.9B/OLMo/Amber as reference.
- **The margin is real but thin exactly where it matters for the broadest claim.** Amber-7B shows 2.3× vs 1.20× at k=2, 1.8× vs 1.05× at k=3, and essentially nothing at k=4 (1.1× vs 1.04×). Conversely, at high order in the largest model the *control itself* is catastrophic: Pythia-6.9B k=6 is 1253× for U-deletion vs 898× for the random direction, so "direction matters" there rests on a 1.4× margin.
- **Similarity and coordination are anti-correlated but not substitutes.** The model-level Spearman correlation between pairwise CKA and Û_{i,j} is negative in all seven models, from ρ=−0.66 (Pythia-1B) to ρ=−0.19 (Pythia-6.9B). Block-removal rankings nevertheless diverge: coordination correlates positively with removal damage in all 18 Pythia/OLMo cells (ρ=+0.05 to +0.94), whereas on Amber-7B CKA's ρ becomes +0.18/+0.01/−0.07 at k=2/3/4 — wrong-signed — while coordination stays +0.57/+0.49/+0.32. Concretely, high-CKA Amber blocks yield perplexities of 55.7/32.1/49.9 versus 26.9/25.7/34.8 for the low-CKA blocks that CKA itself would have preferred to keep.
- **Pythia is where the two criteria mostly agree, which is why the Amber divergence matters.** Low-coordination and high-CKA blocks agree within 8% in 10 of 15 Pythia cells, and the low-CKA blocks fall in the early layers in 13 cells, frequently overlapping high-coordination regions. Also notable: Pythia-6.9B has both the weakest similarity–coordination association (ρ=−0.19) and the largest k=3 ratio (R_3=4.84), i.e. the model with the most higher-order coordination looks *least* redundant by CKA.

## Authors & Credibility

JaeSeong Kim (Semyung University) and KyungTae Lim (KAIST), with the work done during an internship at MLP Lab, KAIST; Lim is corresponding author. The paper is a single-author-team theory/measurement paper in the mechanistic-interpretability style, positioned against Elhage et al.'s residual-stream framework, Belrose et al.'s tuned lens, Kornblith et al.'s CKA, and the layer-pruning line (ShortGPT, "The Unreasonable Ineffectiveness of the Deeper Layers", Transformer Layers as Painters, LayerShuffle). The citation base is appropriate and current through 2025, and the estimand is explicitly connected to the Shapley-interaction literature (Harsanyi 1963; Grabisch & Roubens 1999; Sundararajan et al. 2020; Faith-Shap) rather than presented as entirely new mathematics. Everything measured comes from publicly released checkpoints, which is a genuine credibility asset; the absence of any code or artifact link is the counterweight.

## Critical Assessment

The mathematical core is sound and honestly scoped: Eq. (5) is an identity, not a model, and the paper repeatedly refuses the tempting over-readings (Û_T is not a fraction, not bounded by 1, and R_k is only meaningful within an order). The matched-norm intervention is the right control and reporting all 35 cells rather than a flattering subset is good practice.

But the headline "all 35 cells" conceals a sharp heterogeneity that the abstract does not surface. Amber-7B — the only model outside the Pythia/OLMo lineage and the one with the lowest clean perplexity (11.0) — has deletion margins of 1.1× vs 1.04× at k=4, i.e. the causal claim is at the edge of measurement resolution precisely in the model family that is supposed to guard against "this is a Pythia property." The paper does note the narrow Amber margins, but it does not reconcile them with the abstract's confident "behaviorally consequential." A plausible unstated explanation is that Amber's coordination formed *before* its earliest released checkpoint (≤1.26T tokens, so the baseline is already organized), which would also explain its low R_k values (1.71…14.1, the smallest of the three families) — but that is my inference, not the paper's analysis, and it means the cross-family R_k comparison is not apples-to-apples: Pythia uses initialization as base, OLMo 4B tokens, Amber an unknown point.

The R_k construction has a structural fragility the paper does not confront. Because baseline Û_T decays roughly by half per order, the high-order ratios divide by values at the numerical floor (0.02–0.03), so R_6=64.4 is a ratio of two small numbers computed with an ε-stabilized denominator. Bootstrap intervals are over adjacent *windows* only — not over token samples, not over seeds — and Pythia is a single training run per size, so there is no seed-level replication anywhere in the paper. The most dramatic numbers in the abstract are therefore the least statistically supported.

Two missing controls stand out. First, the deletion experiment's only control is a Gaussian direction; a stronger one would delete a norm-matched direction drawn from another layer's actual write (or the Û of a randomly chosen non-adjacent window), which would separate "this specific connected computation" from "any structured, high-variance direction." This matters concretely at Pythia-6.9B k=6, where the Gaussian control already produces 898× damage — at that scale norm, not direction, is doing most of the work. Second, the CKA–Û negative correlation is pooled over all layer pairs within a model with no control for depth position; since early-layer pairs are plausibly both more similar and more coordinated, ρ=−0.66 could be a layer-index confound reported as a functional fact. A partial correlation controlling for |i−j| and layer index is the obvious ablation and is absent.

Scope limits are acknowledged but still binding: adjacent windows only (so coordination between distant layers — arguably the interesting case for "functional depth" — is unmeasured), k≤6 because exact U_T needs 2^k subset passes (64 forward passes at k=6), one corpus (WikiText-103) with perplexity as the only behavioral readout, and no downstream task anywhere. The paper also cannot distinguish coordination-as-cause from coordination-as-signature, and says so. Reproducibility is mixed: public checkpoints plus a fully specified estimand should make this replicable, but no code, no per-cell raw values beyond Tables 2–4, and no released implementation of the norm-matched intervention are provided.

Novelty is real but incremental in the mathematical ingredient (Möbius/Harsanyi dividends are standard; the authors say so) and genuinely new in the application: write-relative normalization, exact intervention site, and checkpoint-wise tracking of functional depth. The strongest scientific contribution is arguably the CKA result — a clean demonstration that representation similarity and computational dependence can rank layer removals in opposite orders — and that result would stand even if the developmental R_k story were weakened.

## Strengths

- The estimand is exact rather than a proxy: U_T is defined by inclusion–exclusion over identity-ablated subset passes, so "the computation that requires all of T" has a precise operational meaning, and the intervention site follows structurally from Eq. (9) instead of being chosen post hoc.
- The write-relative normalization solves a real confound (residual magnitude drifts during pretraining) and is derived, not tuned — the denominator is literally the S=T_{<ℓ_k} term of the alternating sum.
- Seven models, three families, five interaction orders, 35 cells, with consistent sign throughout: this is a breadth of evidence unusual for an interpretability paper and it makes the qualitative claim hard to attribute to one architecture.
- The matched-norm control is the correct design, and the paper's willingness to report Amber's near-null cells and Pythia-6.9B's inflated random-control damage shows intellectual honesty rather than cherry-picking.
- The CKA analysis has direct practical consequence: on Amber-7B, similarity-based block selection yields 55.7/32.1/49.9 perplexity where coordination-based selection yields 23.8/33.3/40.7, i.e. a pruning criterion already used in the literature can be actively misled.
- The discussion of the two causal orderings (coordination enables capability vs capability demands coordination) and the explicit statement that the data cannot separate them is better epistemics than most papers in this genre.
- Figure 1's per-checkpoint, per-order arc visualization makes a genuinely abstract object (order-k interaction over depth) legible, and the finding that coordination concentrates into an early-interior band is a concrete, checkable claim.

## Limitations

- Adjacent-window restriction: only T={ℓ,…,ℓ+k−1} is measured, so any coordination spanning distant layers is invisible; the authors flag this but it bounds the interpretation of "functional depth" substantially.
- Exact computation costs 2^k subset forward passes, capping the study at k=6 and leaving k>6 (where the largest R_k values occur and where the interesting "many layers must agree" regime lives) unmeasured, with no approximation or sampling study.
- Single corpus (WikiText-103) and single behavioral metric (perplexity); no downstream task, no generation-quality measure, and deletion damage is measured on the same distribution used to define U_T.
- Baseline checkpoints differ in kind across families (init for Pythia, 4B tok for OLMo, unspecified early point for Amber), so R_k values are not directly comparable across families even though they are tabulated side by side.
- Normalization accounts only for the final participating layer's write magnitude, not the preceding layers'; the paper acknowledges this, but it means Û_T can exceed 1 and comparisons across tuples with very different preceding-write scales are not scale-free in the way the name suggests.
- No seed replication (one Pythia run per size), bootstrap intervals over windows only, and high-order ratios computed against baseline values near the ε floor.
- No code or artifact release, and no partial-correlation control for depth position in the CKA analysis.

## Relevance

属于"把深度从架构超参数变成可测的学习性质"这一类工作，对理解 Transformer 的残差流、层间依赖与剪枝/压缩有直接方法学价值；其"表示相似度 ≠ 计算独立"的结论对任何按 CKA/层相似度做层剪枝的流水线都是可操作的警示。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the CKA–coordination negative correlation survive a partial correlation controlling for layer index and |i−j|, or is ρ_sim largely a depth confound?
- Would replacing the Gaussian control with a norm-matched direction drawn from another layer's actual write (or from a random non-adjacent window's Û) preserve the 35/35 sign, especially at Pythia-6.9B k=5–6 where the Gaussian control already costs 162×–898×?
- Can a sampled or Shapley-approximation estimator recover the k>6 ordering cheaply enough to test whether coordination keeps growing or saturates near the top of the stack?
- On Amber-7B, where deletion damage is within ~1.05–1.2× of the control, does removing the k=3 connected component change any downstream task metric at all, or is the effect confined to perplexity on an English corpus the model may not prioritize?
- If coordination is a signature rather than a cause, does R_k at a fixed token budget predict which of two equal-depth models will acquire a multi-step capability (e.g. compositional generalization) faster?
- Do the high-coordination early-interior bands identified in Figure 1 coincide with the "sink"/early-layer regions that pruning work already treats as redundant, or are they a distinct phenomenon — and do they persist after instruction tuning or RLHF?
