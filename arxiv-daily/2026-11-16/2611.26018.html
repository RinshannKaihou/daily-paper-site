Why this matters

Ablation under-reports use: a workspace that looks almost unnecessary can still do the causal work. This protocol separates relaying from actually computing.

Critical edge

The calibration is closer to a unit test than to validation: no false-positive rate for a standard two-value patch is reported, so “patching cannot tell these apart” is argued by example, not measured.

The protocol — four rungs, each paired with the hypothesis it rules out

RUNG 1

Dependence

Zero every received broadcast, rounds 1–3, at every token.

Rules out

A decorative channel: composition accuracy falls 69–78 points, to 21–27%.

RUNG 2

Content use

Transplant the donor’s write-2 packet; leave competition and everything else free.

Rules out

Pure relay: the third answer Z appears in 97.2%, not Y in 1.0%.

RUNG 3

Mechanism

2×2 over broadcast (native / transplanted) × queries (native / induced): restore or install.

Rules out

Any perturbation works: 2,047/2,048 in both directions, round-2 restores 0.

RUNG 4

Breadth

One packet feeds three receivers with locations, colours and access rights.

Rules out

Single-receiver artefact: 84.0% joint hybrid vs 3.1% native; 0/1,024 copy the donor.

Methodology — a world small enough to enumerate every route

  • Organism. 4 recurrent modules (192-d private state), 4 rounds per token, one 640-d packet (128-d control + two 256-d lexical blocks) contested against a NULL option. E1/E2 read binding vs location records; E0/E3 hold no memory. The output head sees the question, prefix, final state and final lexical read — never the workspace.
  • Task. Four objects, four people, four locations; 432 templated questions per family (108n); 2,048 held-out composition cases. Training is a two-stage curriculum: 2,048 local updates, then 2,048 mixed updates with readers restricted to one record type.
  • The identification trick. A symbolic rule, fixed before any model output is seen, keeps only cases where ignoring (X), relaying (Y) and recombining (Z) predict pairwise-distinct strings. A two-value patch cannot separate Y from Z; a three-value one can.
  • Discipline. Panels, intervention site (write 2 → read 3 → first answer token), metrics and training lengths fixed before evaluation. Controls: self-replacement, transplant at the never-read write 3, matched-norm noise, matched-total-variation attention rows, record-order permutation.

Both nets score ≥511/512, both collapse without the workspace, and a standard two-value patch moves both to the donor’s answer in every case. Only the three-value transplant separates them. They differ in nothing but whether the receiver’s answer-bearing records are resampled during training.

Controls that hold

  • Self-replacement and a transplant at write 3 — which is never read — change 0/2,048 outputs.
  • Matched-norm packet noise keeps 92.9–96.9% of the effect, so content, not magnitude, carries it.
  • Restoring round-2 queries instead returns 0 changed cases to native — site-specific in 8/8 runs.
  • Exact match and teacher-forced log-probability agree in 32/32 comparisons; retained failures stay in the counts (2,013/2,015).

Strengths

  • Turns a real identification problem into an identifiable test: X, Y and Z selected from inputs only, never from model behaviour.
  • Calibrates on networks whose computation is known, with pre-registered panels, sites and metrics — rare in mechanistic interpretability.
  • Triangulates necessity (restoration), sufficiency (installation), specificity (round-2 returns nothing) and a positive control (a third donor, 572/586).
  • Generality is tested rather than asserted: eight entities, dual route, uniform routing, point-to-point, slot workspace, order-invariant reader, three receivers, a pretrained Transformer.
  • States each rung’s validity conditions explicitly and keeps its own failures in every denominator.

Limitations

  • Scale. Headline result is a 6.9M-parameter model on a four-object world with 432 templated questions; the Qwen check is 128 hand-built cases.
  • Rung 1 is not a clean measure of use. OFF accuracy (21–27%) sits 25 points under the memory-only ceiling (46.5–52.9%), so dependence is confounded with an under-trained own-memory path.
  • Preconditions. An exclusive route, an identifiable carrier, exact three-value counterfactuals and a receiver-side read interface. The slot workspace degrades to 82% Z with 12.6% noise reversal.
  • Selection. Only three-value cases are scored; the share of natural composition questions surviving that filter is never reported.
  • Power and artefacts. Four main models, two corpora, one generator; no code, data, checkpoints or intervention scripts.

Questions for further exploration

  1. What fraction of composition questions passes the three-value filter, and does the 97.2% hybrid rate survive on the excluded Y = Z cases under a different identification strategy?
  2. Can the protocol be inverted — given a frontier model and no known carrier, is there a search procedure that reliably finds one, or must it be specified by construction?
  3. Why is OFF 25 points below the memory-only ceiling? A control that trains the receiver’s own-memory path on composition questions would separate learned over-dependence from under-training.
  4. Does read redirection hold when a packet must carry more than one fact — or on MoE routing, where experts are sources and the router is the workspace?

What this does not show

  • No frontier model. A synthetic-task organism; the 1.5B check is one task, one carrier.
  • No carrier discovery. The Qwen carrier is the layer-4 residual of a generated name token, chosen by construction.
  • No natural distribution. Pairwise-distinct cases only; the excluded share is unmeasured.
