Why this matters

Verifiable inference is stuck on float non-associativity. Proving a canonical, order-independent product unblocks it — and yields a cheap probe for kernel-level numerical drift. 可验证推理一直卡在浮点非结合性上；把被证函数换成规范语义，它与累加顺序无关，同时给出一个廉价的内核数值漂移探针。

Critical edge

Soundness (< 2−85) is bounded only for the interactive protocol, yet every number comes from a Fiat–Shamir build; privacy rests on an unproved masking hypothesis — argued, not established. 已证明的 soundness 只覆盖交互式协议，而全部实测跑在 Fiat–Shamir 实现上；零知识依赖未证明的 masking 假设——有论证，无结论。

  • Ziv test, not a circuit. Compute an approximation ŝ with a certified bound β̂; if RN(ŝ−β̂) = RN(ŝ+β̂) the rounding is already determined, so nothing is recomputed. With binary64 accumulation only 1.7·10⁻⁴ of GPT-2 entries get flagged.
  • Hundreds-of-bit products → exponent buckets. Significands split into w = 12 bit limbs indexed by binary exponent; one sumcheck at a random z checks Σt ztD̃(t) = Σl ÃzB̃z, recovering every coefficient.
  • Field equations must lift to ℤ. Limb range checks plus a signed carry chain turn each field identity back into an integer identity (Lemma 7), with explicit field-size conditions.
  • Rounding must be exact, not approximate. The residual V = σN − (M−½)U captures ties by significand parity, the binade boundary at λ = u/4, and underflow, in 7 constraint families of degree ≤ 5 plus 4 lookups.

ncell = 52–62 per output, independent of the inner dimension k. That independence is the whole cost argument: the saving is 64k/ncell, not a benchmark artefact.

06The case against error bounds

What a prover can do with the slack that an “error-bounded” relation leaves open.

41%
of product outputs admit a 1-ulp choice under the binary32 bound
47/48
greedy tokens flipped by a coordinated adversary (median logit gain 3.50)
0/48
flips once the bound is tightened to binary64 level

Random choices inside the same slack flip only 1 of 48 targets, so the attack is not noise — it is coordination across outputs. The result is a designer's argument, not a footnote: a verifier that accepts “within a certified bound” accepts a different model, and generation will diverge.

Hence zero slack. The relation must be canonical: one rounding, exact dot product, no freedom for the prover to spend. That requirement — not efficiency — is what forces the whole construction.

07The theorem is about a nearby model

Three gaps between what is proved and what a deployment would run, stated in the paper's own scope section.

Format

Full bf16 (H = 254) is not an instance of Theorem 1: the exponent offset 133 shares no w ≥ 2 divisor with the window. Every measurement uses windowed Fl(e₀, 48) with 4 buckets and a per-tensor e₀ calibrated on the evaluation input.

→ which also leaks a 12-bit magnitude bound per tensor

Architecture

Heads padded 12→16, widths 768→1024, 3072→4096. The verifier does not enforce that padded parameters vanish, so “a prover may use the padded coordinates as hidden capacity.” The fix — a public mask Z∘θ = 0 — is described, not implemented.

→ the proof is about the widened model, not GPT-2 as shipped

Numerics

Canonical LayerNorm is a different function: ε̂ = 71·2⁻¹⁰ and five roundings where the reference rounds once. A block errs 0.43% against 0.37% for both CR and native bf16 — 1.2× less accurate.

→ “at least as accurate” holds for the full-model logits (0.37% vs bf16 0.52%), not the block

08Strengths

  • The idea is right and defended on three axes — canonicality, legitimacy (≤ ½ ulp is a legal IEEE execution), algebraicity — not merely on cost.
  • The rounding gadget is exact where these things are usually hand-waved: ties by significand parity, λ = u/4 at a binade boundary, odd-significand strictness, underflow via sub·(1−E₀) = 0.
  • Commit once, read through views. Lemma 13 (views commute with the multilinear extension) is what makes “no copy constraints” sound rather than convenient.
  • The cost model is structural, not measured. ncell = 52–62 with no k dependence means the 64k/ncell advantage can be checked without trusting the benchmark.
  • Unusual honesty: four scope limits up front, an explicit “we claim no end-to-end privacy level,” a refusal to claim Fiat–Shamir or quantum soundness, and seven self-found design flaws documented in S-IV-F.
  • The 47/48 experiment is a contribution in its own right — a concrete argument that certified-bound verification is the wrong guarantee for generation.

09Limitations

  • Privacy is conditional. ZK holds only under an unproved masking hypothesis H(ε_H) and an unproved leaf-entropy condition E(ε_RO). Rank condition (12) has known counterexamples; the linear-algebra terms alone reach ≈2⁻⁴⁷·².
  • Soundness bounds are interactive only. The 2⁻⁸⁵ figure covers the interactive protocol; every measurement uses a BLAKE3 Fiat–Shamir transcript with no derived bound.
  • Proof size is 0.83 GB with O(√N) openings; the paper's own open problems ask for a polylogarithmic commitment.
  • One model, one prompt. GPT-2 small (124M), 2 cores, 7 GB; no 1B+ scaling, no batching, no serving latency — despite the cost model being the scalability argument.
  • No ablations of w = 12, nc = 4, or bucket count G; G = 6 and G = 12 are costed in entry counts only, never in prover time.
  • Deployment needs a wider accumulator or a repair pass — a GPU bf16 kernel cannot produce CR directly. Unary tables are binary64 values, not certified correct roundings.
Cell count ÷ constraint count is not wall-clock. The 861× is 64k/ncell; the honest timing headline is 18.7 min on 2 cores, with 625 s of one-time setup and an 846 MB transfer left out of the marginal 0.61 s/token/block figure.

10Questions for further exploration

  1. What does G = 6 (GELU tails) or G = 12 (141-bit attention probabilities) actually cost in prover time? Only entry-change counts are reported, and attention is where the bucket count explodes.
  2. Can H(ε_H) be discharged with independent masks per revealed value instead of a fresh-pad order? The authors call this the main open step — is there an obstruction, or only 21 phases of bookkeeping?
  3. Does the 1.7·10⁻⁴ Ziv flag rate survive a distribution shift to heavier cancellation? Every extra flag multiplies the exact-recomputation cost, and it was measured only on GPT-2 weights.
  4. What does “hidden capacity” in padded coordinates really buy a dishonest prover — could a second, smaller model be encoded there, or is it only a definitional gap?
  5. If LayerNorm rounded once instead of five times, how much of the block's 0.43% vs 0.37% error gap would disappear? That ablation separates the CR products from the re-specified normalisation.
  6. Is the 47/48 attack robust to sampling instead of greedy decoding? The reported number is for argmax with median margin 0.72; the sampling regime is unmeasured.
