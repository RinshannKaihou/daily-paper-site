01 / Methodology

Replace the basis, not the parameters

mHC-lite already keeps Hres exactly doubly stochastic by writing it as a convex combination of all n! permutation matrices. cmHC leaves that parameterization alone and smooths the bases themselves:

P′m = λ·Pm + (1−λ)/(n−1)·(1n1n⊤ − Pm)
⟹ c(H) = λ′H + (1−λ′)Jn,   λ′ = (nλ − 1)/(n − 1)

Since Σmαm = 1, the residual matrix becomes an affine contraction toward the barycenter Jn with factor λ′; its unique fixed point is Jn. Setting λ → 1 recovers mHC-lite exactly, so this is a config flag, not a new architecture.

  • Zero learnable parameters. P′m is precomputed once per layer and cached; the kernel receives bases, not a scalar λ.
  • Exactness preserved. ℬn(λ) ⊆ ℬn, ‖H′res‖2 ≤ 1, and every entry is ≥ min(λ, (1−λ)/(n−1)) > 0 — permutation vertices become unreachable.
  • Contract shallow only. One shared λ = 0.97 on the first K layers (K = 2 at 45M–360M), mHC-lite everywhere after; deep contraction is measurably worse.
  • Why it propagates. The bound at layer k depends only on λ′0…λ′k−1, so early contraction reshapes every later stream difference, while late contraction only flattens the tail.
vertex Pm→ λ-blend→ interior P′m→ first K layers→ cumulative → Jn

05 / Strengths

What earns the read

  • Drop-in, not a re-architecture. One line, no learnable parameters, exact double stochasticity, mHC-lite recovered at λ = 1.
  • Names a failure mode the field ignored. Prior work pushed harder toward the polytope interior; λ = 0.70 losing more with depth is a useful corrective.
  • The butterfly-effect theorem. A 0.01 per-entry floor measurably reshapes α in layers that were never contracted — checkable, not hand-waved.
  • Falsifiable ablations. Shallow-vs-deep at fixed λ is the experiment the theory predicts, and the blow-up is reported rather than buried.
  • Honest reporting. States where mHC-lite and KromHC win, and runs an unprompted second seed.
  • Industrial evidence. 300B-MoE plus hand-written Ascend C kernels with a full Pre/Post breakdown.
06 / Limitations

What the tables do not show

  • Perplexity only. Two web corpora, zero downstream benchmarks; the practical value of 0.002–0.007 nats is unestablished.
  • Short runs. 10k iterations at 45M–360M; only the 6B run is reasonably trained.
  • 300B is a probe. 33.55B tokens ≈ 0.11 tokens/param, single run, mHC-only.
  • Theory is loose where it matters. 0.01 entry floor, 0.96L = 0.14 at L = 48, unmeasured Lipschitz constant.
  • Two hand-tuned knobs. λ and K drift with scale (K = 2 / 4 / 6) and no selection rule is stated.
  • Narrow stream count. n = 4 throughout, 24 bases; the O(n!) growth is never analyzed.
  • Single-platform systems. Ascend 910C only, B = 1, sublayer excluded, no end-to-end throughput.
07 / Open questions

What to test next

  1. Do the 0.002–0.007 nat gaps become downstream gains at 1B+ scale and 100k+ steps, or wash out?
  2. Why is best K = 2 for 12/24/48-layer nanoGPT but 4 at 6B and 6 at 300B — depth fraction, tokens, or width?
  3. Can λ or K be learned or annealed (λ → 1 over training) instead of grid-searched?
  4. Does cmHC compose with KromHC’s Kronecker factorization or oHC’s SO(n) parameterization?
  5. Is high inter-stream cosine similarity actually harmful, or only representationally redundant? Ablate one stream at a time.
  6. What is the end-to-end tokens/sec cost of the extra [T,24]×[24,16] projection on Ascend and on H100?
  7. How much does the residual-bias init drive under-mixing, and is a non-symmetric init additive or redundant with cmHC?
  8. Is there a depth beyond which no λ ∈ (1/n, 1) helps at fixed K, and can τ trigger contraction changes mid-run?
One-line verdict

Worth reading for the over-mixing diagnosis and the shallow-contraction ablation. Treat the nats-level deltas as suggestive, not conclusive.
