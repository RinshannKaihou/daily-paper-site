Why this matters

Grounding failures are decided inside the residual stream, not in the retriever. The paper makes “the model ignored the source” a locatable, measurable event.

Relevance: LLM hidden states & interpretability, hallucination as a mechanistic failure.

Critical edge

The paper's most quotable number (61.5–62.3% → 7.7–13.5%) rests on roughly 26 hallucinated slots, and that table reports no confidence intervals.

Related confound: on SST-2 the permuted-label control beats the real task for Qwen (86.7% vs 77.1%), so the “broken rule” contrast is not clean.

01Methodology

One intervention, measured at three levels

matched counterfactual→ Δh (rank-10)→ Bfℓ,i→ ablate · attenuate · amplify
  1. Matched counterfactual. Rewrite only the span that carries the role (JSON vs Markdown output rule; “bake 20 min” vs “bake 30 min”). Every other position is held fixed, so content, length and query stay constant and only the function changes.
  2. Representation. Δhℓ at the final prompt position is low-dimensional — a rank-10 PCA subspace fitted on one split carries far more energy on the other than the k/H ≈ 0.2% random baseline — and its leading direction aligns with the unembedding; re-injecting it restores behaviour.
  3. Prediction. Stepwise score shift Φ(y) and a preference margin M on a minimal candidate pair, teacher-forced.
  4. Mechanism. Bfℓ,i = Σa Σs∈Sf αi←s Vℓ(s) WO: the slice of token i's attention update sourced from role positions. Prop. 1 shows only attention moves information across positions, so the account is attention-indexed by design.
  5. Decomposition. Theorem 1 expands each step shift into magnitude × local sensitivity × direction cosine. A role matters when its contribution is large and its signed terms aggregate coherently across layers instead of cancelling.

Interventions: pathway ablation (zero Bf), sustained attenuation (1−α), amplification (β), random-direction and matched non-role controls, plus attention-vs-MLP at three depths. Models: Qwen2.5-7B, Mistral-7B, Llama-3.1-8B.

04Strengths

What survives scrutiny

  • Clean counterfactual. Only the role span changes, so content, length and query are fixed across conditions.
  • Three-level triangulation. Probe, score decomposition and causal intervention all carry the same Bfℓ,i; unusually coherent for an interpretability paper.
  • Honest negative result. Rule failures are reported as unrecoverable at 16× amplification, not smoothed over.
  • A prediction, then a check. Prop. 2 is quantitative and tested at both decision and task level.
  • Non-obvious timing claim. Grounding is often decided 6–8 tokens before the emitted fact, by indirect terms.

05Limitations

Where to discount the claims

  • n ≈ 21–26 per cell. No CIs in the headline table, no per-item breakdown, no seed variance.
  • Three 7–8B models. No frontier scale, no instruct-vs-base ablation, one decoding setting.
  • Attention only, by construction. MLP transport of role information is excluded, and Table 7's gap (Qwen rule, attention 30.79 vs MLP 26.14) is real but not decisive.
  • Weak reference class. Controls are random directions and matched spans, not function vectors, retrieval heads or attention knockout.
  • Theory drift. Theorem 1's remainder is unbounded, so magnitude + sign coherence constrain the first-order term, not influence itself.
  • No mitigation. Amplification needs the target token and decision position, i.e. an oracle; the paper is explicit that this is a diagnostic.

06Questions for further exploration

Open threads worth pulling

  1. How large is the per-step remainder |Rt| against the first-order terms, and does sign coherence survive step by step rather than aggregated?
  2. Does the rule/evidence asymmetry hold on a frontier model where format-following is near-ceiling, or is it an artifact of 7–8B checkpoints?
  3. Can the amplification diagnostic drop the oracle and locate the decision position with a learned probe?
  4. Does “influence precedes the fact by 6–8 tokens” replicate on long-context, multi-sentence RAG?
  5. At matched decision counts rather than matched deficits, does rule accuracy decay exponentially in k, as the sustained-support account implies?
