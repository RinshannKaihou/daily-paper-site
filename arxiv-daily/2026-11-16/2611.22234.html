01 The audit, not another mask design

The paper reframes reasoning-aware pruning as resting on an unexamined measurement assumption, then audits that assumption along three axes: what you score, at what granularity, whether “reasoning-specific” is separable.

Importance = NLL increase when a query head is mean-ablated, measured on three token sets:

Tans · answer + CoT vs Tcot · reasoning span vs Trec · answer, CoT removed

Tans is the naive default. Tcot is the correction. Trec is the control that separates “answer tokens” from “answer after CoT” — the move that turns an observation into a mechanism.

  • Prune: whole query heads — zero WQ rows and WO columns; K/V untouched (asserted exactly 0.0 norm change).
  • Compare at f½: accuracy holds then cliffs, so a single sparsity is a bad basis. A pre-registered gate requires a sweep point in the 25–50%-of-dense band; all four pass (D1 40 heads/11.9%, N1 44/13.1%, D2 & N2 118/15.1%).
  • Evaluate: ≥500 held-out GSM8K problems, disjoint from 128 calibration trajectories; greedy, 1024-token budget, bf16 — calibration resampling is the only stochasticity.
  • Statistics: 5 seeds at 1.5B, 3 at 7B; hierarchical bootstrap over seeds and items; seed s.d. reported rather than item-level p-values.

02 Three fragilities one signal

Target

9×10−5…3.6×10−3
Answer-token NLL in all four models — 3–4 orders below CoT-token NLL. The residual mass concentrates on one head (0.969 in D1, 0.996 in D2).

Granularity

2.4×10−5
Median single-neuron effect vs a 1.0×10−4 sham-perturbation floor. Group effects cancel: 45/50 groups positive while 50/50 signed sums are negative.

Specificity

0.97–1.08×
“Reasoning-specific” importance diverges from raw importance by no more than its own noise floor — and protecting the “specific” set hurts reasoning more (ΔNLL 0.42 vs 0.70 at 1%).

Reproducible importance appears only at G* ≈ 100 units — two independent criteria (reproducibility onset, floor crossing) agree.

Not a formatting failure. At the same sparsity the Tcot sweep holds format success above 97%, while Tans masks collapse to 56–64% — the reasoning is gone, not just the output shape.

Not a magnitude proxy. A reasoning-blind head-norm baseline sits at 1.2% / 0.8% accuracy at f½, so Tcot’s gain is not recoverable from weight size.

7B is unresolved, honestly. N2 +20.5 (CI [−7.4, +38.4], p=0.07); D2 −1.1 with per-seed values −7.2 / +22.8 / −18.8 and seed s.d. 17.5–20.5. The paper labels this unresolved, not null.

06 Strengths

  • The Trec control. Without it, “answer tokens are hard” would be the obvious objection; with it (+27.6 on D1, +35.9 on N1 relative to Tcot) the pathology is pinned to the answer-after-CoT copy effect.
  • Disciplined statistics. Pre-registered gate on the accuracy cliff, pre-registered mechanism hypothesis, hierarchical bootstrap over seeds and items, seed s.d. on the table.
  • Honest to the point of self-harm. D2 is reported as unresolved rather than null; the uncontrolled single-head probe (60.4%→2.6%, 68.8%→1.4%) is shown without a claim.
  • Citation hygiene. The author checked from source that the two closest methods do not use the saturating target — and says so instead of strawmanning.
  • Reproducible scale. ~75 H100-hours, pinned versions, seeds, artifact hashes; a single-GPU academic reproduction is realistic.

07 Limitations

  • One lineage, one task. D1/D2 are Qwen2.5 distillations and N1/N2 are Qwen2.5-Instruct; GSM8K only — short, templated CoTs, the regime most likely to restate the answer.
  • No random-head baseline in the main sweep and no end-to-end comparison against a real reasoning-aware pruner (Lucas et al., Wanda/SparseGPT), so practical magnitude is inferred.
  • Estimator circularity in §7. The cancellation statistic mixes a true group ablation with signed sums of attribution-patching singletons — an estimator that failed its own per-neuron gate (ρ=0.65).
  • Signal, not speed. Head pruning is orthogonal to 2:4 semi-structured sparsity; no latency or memory numbers.
  • Section 6 is uninterpretable as run (n=4, concentration confounded with scale) — the pre-registered negative occupies space without yielding knowledge either way.

08 Four rules the paper would impose

If you prune a reasoner, these are measurement preconditions — not preferences. Violating the first one alone costs 27.6–32.2 accuracy points that a perplexity check will not show you.

RULE 1

Score the reasoning tokens, never the final answer tokens. The CoT has already said the answer.

RULE 2

Attribute at ≳100 units. Single neurons fall below the 10−4 sham floor and their effects cancel.

RULE 3

Skip reasoning-specific contrasts. They do not separate from raw importance; protecting them hurts more.

RULE 4

Report accuracy, not perplexity. At 7B the two disagree in sign.

09 Open questions

  1. Does answer-in-CoT leakage survive long-form CoT (competition math, code, agentic traces), or is saturation a GSM8K artifact?
  2. Is the Tans–Tcot gap still 20–30 points at 7B+ with enough seeds, or does scale buy robustness to a bad target?
  3. With a random-head baseline at each f½, how much of Tcot’s edge is “any coherent ranking beats a degenerate one”?
  4. Recomputed with true per-neuron ablations, does the 0.90 sign disagreement persist, shrink, or invert?
  5. Can the target be fixed rather than only measured better — e.g. subtract the CoT’s own answer mention?
  6. Does accuracy–perplexity decoupling also hold for quantization and KV-cache eviction, where perplexity is likewise the default?
  7. Would a de-confounded concentration test confirm the −0.80 correlation, or does the mechanism not exist?
