01Methodology

The mechanism is a ratio, not a constant

Diagonal A splits the scan into independent scalar cells. Everything then rides on how you measure a change in Δ.

With λ = |Ai,n| and an exact zero-order hold, each cell is a convex combination of the previous state and an equilibrium ct = Btut/λ:

the cell ht = Āt ht−1 + (1 − Āt) ct,   Āt = e−λΔt ∈ (0,1)

The weight 1 − Āt is the input weight. Cao et al. measure a Δ-perturbation additively, so the time-warp term grows linearly in length unless Δ gets a positive floor — and the floor costs a factor 1/Δlo. Here the hypothesis is instead |log Δt − log Δ̃t| ≤ ρ.

Lemma 2 is three lines of chord-slope monotonicity, and it is the whole trick: the transition moves by a fixed fraction of that step's own input weight, not by an absolute amount. At λΔ = 10−4 the additive measure is 99.5× the input weight; the relative one is capped by eρ − 1.

Subtract the two updates and the disturbance arrives carrying the same (1 − Āt) as the contraction. Tolerance and disturbance therefore cannot separate, the convex recursion has a bounded equilibrium, and the telescoping sum collapses to 1 − Pt. An independent Appendix B derivation — Cao et al.'s Lemma B.2 after a time change aligning both runs on one grid — lands on the same bound.

  • Cell theorem. Unroll |et| ≤ Āt|et−1| + (1 − Āt)E with |h̃t−1 − c̃t| ≤ 2M. No Δ floor, no Δ ceiling.
  • Lower bound. Alternate c̃t between ±M and pair it with Δ̃t = Δte∓ρ so the larger input weight always sits on the c̃t = M step; let the step size τ → 0.
  • Block certificate. Push an input box through conv + SiLU → x_proj → dt_proj (Lemma 6: d log Δ/dz ≤ 1 for softplus) → per-cell Theorem 3 → readout and gate.
  • Experiments. Two 2-block Mamba models, SPY order book, 28 Jan 2021; the only difference is the timescale map — bounded [10−3,10−1] versus softplus.

02Theorem & price

The bound, and what it costs

Theorem 3 |ht − h̃t| ≤ Pt|h−1 − h̃−1| + (1 − Pt)·E
E = η + 2M(eρ − 1),  Pt = Πk≤t Āk
Proposition 4 (matching) gap ≥ η + 2M·tanh(ρ/2)  ⇒  E is within eρ + 1 of optimal

Since 2M(eρ−1) = (eρ+1)·2M tanh(ρ/2), the constant degrades gracefully: the proved factor is 2 in the small-ρ limit, and the numerical search measures exactly that.

Released-code gap. Mamba ships exponential-Euler steps, not the exact ZOH. Corollary 7 covers it at cost Gηb + 3GM(eρ−1) with G = Ψ(λΔhi): no floor, but a ceiling is now required, and the constant 2 becomes 3.

04Strengths

What holds up

  • A real mechanism, cheaply proved. Contraction and disturbance share the factor (1 − Āt) — that shared factor, not a clever constant, is why the floor disappears.
  • The lower bound earns its keep. The alternating construction is non-obvious, and its constant is measured at 2.0 (ρ = 0.01) and 2.8 (ρ = 0.6), not merely asserted as O(1).
  • The baseline is derived generously. Keeping the e−λΔlo that Cao et al.'s Lemma B.3 drops makes the comparison kinder to the baseline, not to the authors.
  • Two independent derivations — convex recursion, and a time change adapting Lemma B.2 — agree, which is a genuine algebra check.
  • Self-undercutting done precisely. Equivalence to first order is proved, then measured at 0.034%, instead of being hidden.
  • Well-chosen formal fragment. Isabelle/HOL covers exactly the load-bearing plumbing: ZOH update, invariant, LayerNorm coordinate bound, fused projection.

05Limitations

What it does not deliver

  • Real arithmetic only. No outward rounding; float32 scan error accumulates to ≈1100× per-step error and exceeds the certified change in 49%/61% of cells at δ = 10−6. Slow transitions round to exactly 1, breaking the convex combination outright.
  • Per block, against the normalised input. LayerNorm's sup-norm Lipschitz constant is 3.9 at realised inputs but ≥653 at a constant input, so no raw-input or end-to-end statement exists.
  • Optimality is not proved for the natural class. Under ct, c̃t ∈ [−M, M] the reachable gap is only η + (2M − η)tanh(ρ/2); the stated theorem allows equilibria outside the box.
  • One dataset, one day, one instrument, one run per arm — and checkpoints at different steps (750 vs 2000), so the softplus comparison supports no causal claim about timescale maps.
  • Narrow baseline set. One re-derived bound plus the trivial 2M + η; no reachability, invariant search, or Lyapunov competitor is run.
  • Experiments use exact ZOH, not released Mamba. Corollary 7 is never evaluated, and its M → GM blow-up with G = Ψ(λΔhi) is materially weaker.

06Open questions

What a follow-up should settle

  1. Is η + 2M tanh(ρ/2) the exact worst-case gap from a common start, and what is the exact constant once both equilibria are confined to [−M, M]?
  2. Can a time-varying Lyapunov function beat the constant E and close the ≈2× gap at small ρ, which is tight only for a constant E?
  3. Do zonotopes or affine arithmetic recover the 98–147× lost at the gate + output projection — the single largest slack term?
  4. Can the 3.9× LayerNorm constant at realised inputs be turned into a valid data-free certificate by bounding over the image of the normalisation rather than a box containing it?
  5. Does Mamba-3's complex transition survive at all? Lemma 2 needs concavity of 1 − e−y for real y, and it is not obvious the (1 − Ā) factor persists under a modulus argument.
Scope. Diagonal A with a real transition in (0, 1) per cell. Rank IC of the testbed is 0.13 with bootstrap CI [0.075, 0.197] — the model is a vehicle for the certificate, not evidence about deployed Mamba.
