bf16 and fp32 lower-layer gradients in Gemma-4 are nearly orthogonal — the backward path, not the bit width, is broken.

在 Gemma-4 的长上下文 LoRA 微调里，模型下三分之一的 bf16 与 fp32 首步梯度几乎不相关（最低两块中位余弦 0.02 / 0.01），而上层稳定在 ≥0.98。逐元素噪声实验证明 fp32 本身是可信参考，只是 bf16 的前向状态误差已经把这条反向通路击穿了 30–130 倍；把第 19–21 层图像 token 的 MLP 输出在反向中断开（前向完全不变），验证 NLL 从 0.336±0.019 降到 0.296±0.003，in-box 从 75% 升到 88%。

Same prompt, same weights, same math — only arithmetic precision differs. Upper layers agree; the bottom third is computing another direction entirely.

Why this matters今天为什么值得看

Anyone fine-tuning long-context VLMs in bf16 may be training on near-noise gradients in the lower third — and the fix is a six-line hook.

如果这个现象复现，那么“训练不稳定就上 fp32”这条默认反应是错的——该查的是反向图的条件数，不是位宽。

Critical edge！

The detached gradient is biased — cosine 0.01–0.35 with the standard fp32 gradient. It trains better; it does not estimate the gradient better.

Methodology

方法 diagnose → localize → patch
  1. Compare first-step gradients. LoRA’s B matrix starts at zero, so only ∂L/∂B is non-zero at step 1. Cosine is computed in float64 over every B matrix of a layer block, on CT lesion-grounding prompts of 2,400–3,600 tokens (2,100–3,200 of them image tokens, 2–3 slices).
  2. Rule out the boring explanations. Repeated fp32 runs agree at ≥0.9989, repeated bf16 at ≥0.9989, and an fp32 backward pass over bf16 forward states reproduces the bf16 gradient at ≥0.996 — so the disagreement lives in the forward states, not in backward rounding.
  3. Measure the tolerance budget. Inject element-wise relative noise (h + σ·h·ε) into the fp32 forward and find the σ at which the gradient stops agreeing. Controls swap CT images for natural photos, or drop them and pad text to ~2,260 tokens.
  4. Localize by substitution. Feed bf16 states into selected layers (h = stored + (h − h.detach())), then ablate three detach points — MLP output, gate activation, up-projection — at image-token positions in layers 19–21.
  5. Train it. Gemma-4-12B SFT, 300 steps, batch 8, AdamW, lr 1e-4, global-norm clip 1.0, 65.6M trainable parameters; four arms × four LoRA-init seeds under one data order, plus a single fp32 run.

Read right to left: the fp32 gradient tolerates about 10⁻⁴ of relative element-wise noise. bf16 forward states differ from fp32 by 0.3–1.3% per token — 30–130× past that cliff. The upper-layer agreement (≥0.98) is therefore not evidence that bf16 is safe — it is evidence that the damage is localized, and that a global precision upgrade is the wrong instrument for it.

Three things this table says at once: the patch is free (forward values bit-identical, step time unchanged); the gain is real across seeds (Detach NLL 0.291–0.298 vs Standard 0.320–0.364, no overlap); and removing the lower-layer updates entirely (Frozen) is as good as Standard — which is either “these layers learn nothing useful in bf16” or “300 steps were too few for them to matter.”

The dominant patch is not the lesion — it is a dark, low-contrast patch on the image border. Two other prompts concentrate on blank patches with 105 and 212 identical copies.

And the path is forward-dispensable: zeroing these image-token MLP outputs in the base model moves validation NLL only 0.653 → 0.697, versus 1.331 for zeroing text tokens in the same layers and 1.274 for image tokens in layers 4–6.

Relevance / 相关性

这篇工作直接对应大模型微调中的数值稳定性与训练效率问题：先测 fp32 梯度的扰动容忍度、再比较 bf16 前向状态误差的诊断流程，以及“反向断路径优于加精度”的结论，对做 LoRA / SFT 与多模态长上下文训练的人有可操作的参考价值；同时也是一份关于如何诚实报告小样本训练实验的范例。

If the effect replicates, the recipe is portable: measure the noise budget of the reference gradient, compare it with your low-precision state error, and only then decide whether to pay for bits.

Strengths / 站得住的部分

  • The diagnostic is the contribution. Measure the fp32 gradient’s tolerance, compare it to the bf16 state discrepancy, then decide — this is reusable on any stack tomorrow.
  • Every link has its own probe. Noise injection (tolerance) → state substitution (which layers) → detach variants (which component) → token shares (which positions) → forward ablation (does the path matter).
  • Controls that kill the easy explanation. fp32 backward on bf16 states ≥0.996; repeated runs ≥0.9989; two competing noise models printed side by side, including the one that contradicts the story.
  • Honest about the shape of its own evidence. Per-seed tables, post-hoc statistics labelled post hoc, biased-gradient admission, and the Frozen counter-explanation stated instead of buried.

Limitations / 站不住的部分

4 seeds / arm1 data order1 fp32 seed 80 val lesions5 image promptsprivate CT
  • The statistics are thin by construction. p = 1/16 is the floor for four pairs — it can only say “all four moved the same way” — and it was added after three seeds were seen. The complete rank separation carries the argument, not the p-value.
  • “Better conditioning” is not separated from “less clipping.” Clipping fires on 99.7% of Standard steps, and there is no per-layer-clipping arm — the one baseline the paper itself names as the obvious alternative.
  • No sweep over the fix. Layers 19–21 / MLP output / image tokens was frozen from five first-step prompts, then trained in that single form; the gate-only detach (0.86) was never trained.
  • The cross-model contrast is confounded. 31B and Qwen run in 4-bit NF4 (31B “does not fit in fp32 on our GPU”), so “Qwen is spared” is measured in a different numerical regime.
  • Evaluation hygiene. Patient-level train/validation overlap was never checked, and center error is unscaled voxel-index distance — not physically meaningful across scanners.

Questions for further exploration

待解问题 6 open threads
Q1

Does the L19–21 MLP amplification survive in other Gemma-4 sizes once the 4-bit NF4 confound is removed — e.g. 31B sharded across GPUs in bf16 or fp32?

Q2

Would per-layer gradient clipping, or freezing only image-token MLP LoRA, reproduce the Detach gain — i.e. how much is conditioning versus norm control?

Q3

Why is Qwen3.8-27B spared? Attention-sink structure, MLP gating family, or how image patches are injected?

Q4

Can the dominant-token pathology be predicted from activation norms, and would soft token reweighting match a hard detach while staying unbiased?

Q5

Does the fix transfer to long-text-only fine-tuning, where the depth profile already differs and agreement is poor at L21 for two of five prompts?

Q6

Beyond 300 steps and more than one data order — does Detach’s advantage persist, shrink, or reverse once lower layers have adapted?
