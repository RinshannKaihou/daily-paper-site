Why it matters

Self-evolving agents forget, pollute memory, and silently overwrite their own rules. GUARD's gate and [iff] narrowing are directly borrowable fixes.

Critical edge

80.80% is the best of five GUARD configurations on that same 43-task split, and the gain over Meta-Harness is p = 0.11 with a CI reaching −0.4pp.

Read the win as “gating prevents collapse”, not as “GUARD wins everywhere”.

01Methodology — four stages, no gradients

Stage 1

Turn-0 checklist

Before the solver's first action, a self-reviewer reads only the task prompt and public tool schemas and compiles 4–8 atomic binary predicates: execution-verifiable ones graded in a throwaway sandbox, semantic ones graded one at a time against the trace. No references, verifiers or rubrics.

Stage 2

Analyze

Obedience per criterion, compared across the last two incumbents. Δhurt = newly broken, Δgap = still unsolved; priority is their sum in [0, 2], which collapses to exactly {2 = regressed, 1 = gap, 0 = mastered} on binary verdicts.

Stage 3

Propose

M ≥ 2 candidate playbooks, all from one domain-agnostic seed and scored on the identical task batch so difficulty cancels. Fitness R = Perf − 3.5|Q⁻| + 1.5|Q⁺|; a best-vs-worst contrast rewrites next round's prompts.

Stage 4

Gate — Soft-HURT

Promote only if ΔS̄ > Hurt. One isolated regression triggers a single [iff] scope repair rather than deletion; rejected edits go to a deduplicated JSONL ledger that is read back next round.

Theorem 1The self-review objective telescopes non-decreasing across rounds on each gated buffer.
Theorem 2Dense per-criterion grading removes the 1/ε cost of passively waiting to observe a rare invariant.
Proposition 1Paired priority is the unique parameter-free ordering where a fresh break beats an old gap.

Serving is cheap by construction: at test time evolution freezes — one harness, one rollout, pass@1, O(1) context, no candidate pool.

05Strengths

  • The abstraction is right. Splitting regressions from gaps is a real diagnosis, and Prop. 1 shows the sum is the unique parameter-free ranking with both properties.
  • Small mechanisms that travel. [iff] narrowing plus a rejection ledger answers the oscillation that plagues reflective self-editing — even if the ledger size is never ablated.
  • Serving realism. Frozen harness, one pass, O(1) context, −54.5% tokens and −22.5% tool calls. Most harness papers optimise a benchmark and ignore the bill.
  • Uncommon candour. p = 0.11, a CI touching zero, “by abstention”, and best-of-five are all disclosed in text.

06Limitations

  • Selection met the test split. 80.80% is the max of five configs spanning 75.57–80.80%, on 43 tasks; the +0.82pp Drift margin has no significance test.
  • Abstention looks like stability. FWT = 0.00 “by abstention”, W_final = W₀ on 2/5 local-gym domains — conservative, or unable to find an edit?
  • The reviewer is the weak link. One frozen model ranks, reviews and gates itself; no experiment uses a deliberately miscalibrated reviewer.
  • Adaptation cost is unpriced. M = 2 doubles rollouts, no code is released, and several knobs are tuned per suite.

07Open questions

  1. Q1Does the Drift Gauntlet edge survive multiple seeds, when the five hosted configs already span 5.2pp?
  2. Q2What happens when the reviewer is weaker than the solver — the regime most practitioners actually run?
  3. Q3Is abstention ever permanent, and does the rejection ledger slowly poison the proposal prior?
  4. Q4How much of the −54.5% token cut is harness quality versus simply avoiding 8-step budget exhaustions?
  5. Q5Can the checklist be derived incrementally as constraints appear mid-trajectory, instead of fixed at Turn 0?
