Why this matters

A cheap, released recipe for converting a pretrained AR model into block diffusion, plus a 16GB single-GPU deployment that actually runs.

Architecture delta: 0.002% of parameters.

Critical edge

No component is ablated, so the +7.8 could just as well be the agent-trajectory SFT corpus; meanwhile HLE falls 8.4 points against the same backbone.

Methodology

Write the past into a state. Read the present through one softmax.

STEP 1Clean prefillCausal GDN writes committed tokens into a delta-rule state Sb.
STEP 2Denoise blockBidirectional softmax over Nb noisy tokens + 1 history slot.
STEP 3CommitConfident tokens land; the causal GDN verifies self-speculative drafts.

Clover keeps the Qwen3.6-35B-A3B text stack: 40 layers, 30 BD-GDN recurrent mixers alternating with 10 full-attention layers, each followed by a 256-expert dMoE (top-8 plus a shared expert). The new mixer does not concatenate history to the block. The compressed readout rj = Sbq̂j/√dk occupies exactly one logit beside the Nb block logits, and a single softmax divides probability between prefix memory and in-block tokens.

Because one slot competes against Nb of them, a fixed history bias starves the prefix as blocks grow. A learnable term bh + ch·log(Nb/32) removes that dependence at ch=1. A three-tap right-context convolution is summed onto the pretrained four-tap causal kernel before one SiLU, with right taps zero-initialized, so step 0 is numerically the original checkpoint. Routing aggregates per-block mass and keeps the smallest expert coreset covering τ = 0.9 before token-level top-8.

Training packs two streams, [xt | x0], samples block size from {4, 8, 16, 32}, and optimizes L = λBDLBD + λARLAR. Deployed decoding is self-speculative with an entropy acceptance rule (δ = 0.6).

Strengths

Clean formulation, disciplined engineering

  • One history slot competing inside the block softmax is the cleanest "read the past cheaply, read the present fully" formulation in this line of work.
  • The block-size dilution argument (Eq. 12) is correct, non-obvious, and fixes a real normalization defect rather than tuning a constant.
  • The intervention is honest about cost: 740K parameters, zero-initialized, numerically identical to the checkpoint at step 0.
  • Unfavorable numbers stay in the paper: MATH-500 throughput below the baseline, with the drafting cost explained.
  • The on-device result is the most checkable claim: routing-aware expert placement reaches 97.5 tok/s on a 16GB card at no accuracy cost.

Limitations

Attribution is asserted, not demonstrated

  • Zero ablations for BD-GDN, the calibration term, the right taps, or dMoE coresets, so architecture is a story rather than a cause.
  • λBD and λAR are never reported; no data mixture, token counts, compute budget, RL disclosure, or frozen/trained split.
  • The regression on HLE and AIME goes undiscussed, though it is exactly the axis that separates redistribution from representational gain.
  • No confidence intervals on 18 benchmarks; Terminal-Hard is 21 tasks, so +6.3 is about 1.3 tasks.
  • Agent replay measures decode speed, not rollout success; no cost-per-resolved-task is given.

Relevance / 相关性

Directly useful to anyone engineering discrete diffusion language models: it is a concrete recipe for turning a pretrained AR backbone into block diffusion while touching 0.002% of its parameters, with a reproducible 97 tok/s run on one 16GB GPU. Cite the agent numbers only alongside HLE −8.4 and the 23% MATH-500 throughput shortfall.
