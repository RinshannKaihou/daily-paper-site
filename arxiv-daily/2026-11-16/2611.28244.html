Method

One loop, one variable at a time

For each task the agent runs a scored attempt loop 100 times (MLE-Bench) or 50 times (KernelBench). Six configurations share the identical loop — same prompts, tools, action space, and a deliberately weak seed solution — and differ only in the history policy Φ and the skill policy.

action selector→skill picker→coder · 32K→benchmark scores→skill writer↺
C_t = [ T , R_t , K_t , Φ(H_t) ]  ·  |C_t| ≤ 32,000 tokens
  • Baseline — recency truncation: keep the longest suffix of attempts that fits, discard the rest permanently.
  • Folding — every attempt becomes a 2–4 sentence digest; the agent unfolds any subset in full until the history budget fills, so all attempts stay addressable.
  • Compaction — a rolling structured summary plus a recent tail; older attempts are folded into the summary and become irretrievable.
  • Skill policies — deletion (drop after 5 unpicked iterations), merging (cosine ≥ 0.8), promotion (frequent winners become standing rules).

MLE-Bench: 10 of 67 competitions (6 low / 4 medium complexity), gold-normalised score. KernelBench: a 50-problem stratified subset (20 L1 / 20 L2 / 10 L3) of 238 eligible problems, geometric-mean speedup over PyTorch Eager on a GH200. Three backbones: gpt-oss-120b, gpt-6-luna, gpt-6-sol.

Folding keeps ~7× more attempts while spending ~34% fewer context tokens than the baseline. Late-run new-best rate: 12.0% vs 10.2% (baseline) and 9.7% (compaction); early rates are statistically indistinguishable.

Skill numbers are gains on top of an unmanaged library, which itself adds only +2.1% over No-Skill — so curation, not accumulation, carries the benefit. One run per cell, no seeds or error bars.

Strengths

+Why the result is believable

  • Genuinely controlled isolation. One component swapped inside one harness, with prompts, tools, action space and seed solution held fixed — the design most memory papers skip.
  • All six cells reported, including the ones that weaken the story: compaction −1.7%, folding only +1.6%, deletion only +1.0% on MLE-Bench for gpt-6-sol.
  • A mechanism, not just a delta. 42 attempts retained vs 6.2, fewer tokens (12.1k vs 18.4k), higher late-run new-best rate, with the trace in Fig. 3a.
  • No-Skill ablation separates "having a library" (+2.1%) from "curating it" (+4.1%) — a distinction most skill-library papers blur.
  • KernelBench protocol is unusually thorough: 28-stratum sampling frame, seeded subset, documented exclusions, median timing justified by measured CV, five-part AST anti-cheat gate plus reference-buffer isolation.
  • Failure traces explain why merging and promotion underperform — a merged skill lost the missing-parenthesis fix and the syntax error returned at iteration 79.

Limitations

−Where to push back

  • No variance reporting anywhere. One run per cell; the +1.0% to +1.6% cells cannot be distinguished from noise, and "wins all six" leans on two of them.
  • Budget is counted in iterations, not tokens or cost. MLEvolver adds selector, picker, writer and summariser calls per iteration; AIDE's step is cheaper. No dollar or wall-clock accounting.
  • Hyperparameters asserted, not tuned. τ_d = 5, θ = 0.8, digest length 2–4 sentences, 32K. Deletion's +4.1% rests entirely on an untested constant.
  • Missing control: a raw-history baseline capped at the same 12.1k tokens with more, shorter attempts — digest vs. equal-token coverage is untested.
  • Benchmark coverage: 10 of 67 MLE-Bench competitions with hand-set weights; a KernelBench subset whose strata weights come from pilot runs of the same configurations.
  • Median-timing deviation from the KernelBench protocol changes the scale of every reported speedup, and the bias direction is not analysed.
  • Not reproducible: two of three backbones are proprietary, version-pinned API models; the harness is not released.

Relevance / 相关性

本文系统拆解长时程自演化 agent 的 harness 记忆设计，直接回答「history 该怎么留、经验该怎么剪」，结论可迁移到任何长跑 agent。它同时是 harness 层静默失真的清晰实例：recency truncation 会无声丢弃最早期的实验记录，compaction 则逐轮稀释每次实验的细节，而作者用 new-best rate（后期 12.0% vs 9.7%）把这种信息损失量化了出来。它不是新机制，而是少见的「一次只改一个组件」的受控对照。

Open questions

What would change the conclusion

  1. With ≥3 seeds per cell, do the gpt-6-sol folding (+1.6%) and promotion (+1.2%) cells survive?
  2. Equalise budgets in tokens or model calls rather than iterations — what fraction of the +22%/+24% remains?
  3. Is folding's gain addressability or coverage? Cap a raw-history baseline at the same 12.1k tokens.
  1. How sensitive is deletion to τ_d (1, 3, 5, 10, 20), and does the optimum track retrieval volume?
  2. Does a deliberately lossy or adversarial digest destroy the advantage — is digest faithfulness worth measuring?
  3. Does folding plus deletion still win in a parallel tree-search harness, or with cross-task skill libraries?
