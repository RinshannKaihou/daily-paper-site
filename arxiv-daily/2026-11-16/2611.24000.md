> **一句话总结：** 论文把"规则"与"证据"统一视为上下文中的功能性角色（role span），用匹配反事实对照在残差流中分离出角色表征，再把它拆成逐层注意力贡献（一阶分解 + 幅度×符号一致性两个条件），发现规则主要靠格式/实体选择类关键 token 决策起作用、证据主要靠事实取值决策起作用；消融证据通路使 RAGTruth 真实幻觉位置的事实保留率从 61.5–62.3% 掉到 7.7–13.5%，而放大证据贡献在 β=4 时把真实幻觉的目标 token 选择从 ~10% 提到 32.5%。

> **TL;DR:** This paper explains why the same model follows a prompt-specified rule yet ignores supplied evidence by treating both as "functional role spans" and measuring their attention contribution to each decoding step. Matched-counterfactual perturbations of only the role span show a low-dimensional, output-aligned residual response (rank-10 PCA captures far more than the 0.2% random baseline; injecting the matched Δh recovers next-token behavior 0.98 for rules and 0.81 for evidence vs 0.08–0.21 for controls). Ablating the role-span attention pathway on Qwen2.5-7B/Mistral-7B/Llama-3.1-8B drops rule-format retention from 100% to 2.4–4.8% and RAGTruth hallucinated-fact retention from 61.5–62.3% to 7.7–13.5%; amplifying it at failing factual decisions (β=4) lifts target selection to 32.5% vs 5.0% for a random direction, while rule failures stay at 0% even at β=16.

## 核心贡献

- **统一视角**：把 ICL 的"规则"和 OOC 幻觉语境下的"证据"都抽象为动态上下文中的功能角色（role span），指出关键现象——角色信息可以被内部表征，却不一定能有效约束输出。
- **表征层面证据**：匹配反事实（只改角色 span 内容、其余 prompt 完全不变）诱发的残差流差分 Δh 具有系统性低维结构（10 维 PCA 子空间在留出样本上的能量占比远高于随机基线 k/H≈0.2%），且主方向与 unembedding 输出方向对齐，注入后能恢复原行为。
- **理论层面**：把 Δh 落到逐层注意力中"来自角色位置的贡献" B^f_{ℓ,i}，给出步级 score 的一阶分解定理，把角色效应拆成"匹配差分幅度 × 局部敏感度 × 方向余弦"，从而给出两个条件——贡献幅度足够大、且跨层带符号项一致聚合而非相互抵消。
- **因果层面**：按决策类型定位角色作用点——规则作用于格式与实体选择，证据作用于事实取值；并给出"支持量 vs 决策亏损"的可恢复性判据（Prop. 2），在任务级上被验证（证据类任务成功率预测 23.3%→69.9%，实测 25.2%→58.3%）。

## Key Contributions

- A unified framing of ICL and out-of-context hallucination as different *organizations of the same role-span contribution mechanism*, rather than two separate phenomena.
- A matched-counterfactual methodology that isolates a role's effect by editing only the role-bearing span, applied consistently at three levels: representation (probe/PCA), prediction (score shift), and behavior (generation + intervention).
- Theorem 1: an exact substitution identity plus a first-order expansion of the stepwise score shift into role-span contribution coordinates, with an explicit remainder bound; the decomposition yields magnitude and sign-coherence as the two conditions for a role effect to matter.
- Causal evidence that rule and evidence contributions land on *different kinds of key token decisions* (structure/selection vs factual value), including a temporal asymmetry: 84–87% of evidence-driven divergences occur several tokens *before* the fact, while 44–53% of rule divergences occur exactly at the decision.
- A decision-boundary crossing proposition (Prop. 2) that predicts the amplification factor needed to flip a failing decision, tested against observed recovery (79% agreement on decision outcomes).

## Methodology

**Setup.** Three 7–8B decoder-only models (Qwen2.5-7B, Mistral-7B, Llama-3.1-8B); greedy decoding for free generation, teacher forcing for fixed trajectories. Rule tasks are a controlled benchmark derived from CL-bench task patterns (format, selection, include/omit instructions); evidence tasks use the RAGTruth train split, plus SST-2/TREC (few-shot induced rules) and MuSiQue/competing-source prompts in the appendix.

**Matched contexts.** For each role f ∈ {rule, evidence}, the counterfactual prompt C̃^f changes only the token content of the role span while every other prompt position is held fixed (e.g. JSON vs Markdown output rule; "bake 20 min" vs "bake 30 min"). This gives a clean intervention on function without confounding content or length.

**Three measurements.** (1) *Representation*: Δh^f_ℓ = h_ℓ(P^f) − h_ℓ(P̃^f) at the final prompt position; rank-10 PCA fitted on one split, energy fraction evaluated on the other; projection through the unembedding and behavior-recovery by adding Δh back into the counterfactual state. (2) *Prediction*: trajectory score shift Φ^f(y) = log p(y|C^f⊕x) − log p(y|C̃^f⊕x), and preference margin M^f = Φ^f(y⁺) − Φ^f(y⁻) for a minimal candidate pair. (3) *Mechanism*: role-span attention contribution B^f_{ℓ,i} = Σ_a Σ_{s∈S_f} α_{i←s} V_ℓ(s) W_O, isolated as a source-indexed sub-sum of the attention update; the paper proves (Prop. 1) attention is the only channel by which a role-position difference can enter a new position.

**Interventions.** Pathway ablation (zeroing B^f during free generation), sustained attenuation by (1−α), amplification by β, random-direction and matched-non-role controls, plus attention-vs-MLP update-family comparisons at three fractional depths.

## Results & Highlights

- **Representation and recovery (Table 1).** Injecting the matched role residual response restores the original next-token behavior in 98% of cases on average for the rule role (0.94/1.00/1.00 for Qwen/Mistral/Llama) versus 8% when no role content is injected and 15% for a mismatched role response; for evidence the same comparison is 81% vs 34% and 21%. So the Δh carries genuinely usable role information, not just a norm shift.
- **Causal effect on real hallucinations (Table 2b).** Removing the evidence-span attention pathway during generation drops the rate of selecting the evidence-supported fact at RAGTruth annotated hallucination slots from 61.5–62.3% to 7.7–13.5% across the three models, while rule-format retention collapses from 100.0% to 2.4–4.8% and rule entity retention from 53.3–58.1% to 18.1–33.3%. Pathway removal changes token selection in 92–100% of generations.
- **Attenuation and recovery asymmetry (Tables 3–4).** Full attenuation (α=1) flips 76.5–84.0% of rule-decided tokens and drops rule task accuracy to 22.3/32.4/28.6% from 100.0/94.1/86.7%, versus 17.0–32.4% flips and unchanged accuracy (100.0/94.1/86.7%) under the non-role comparison. Amplifying evidence contributions at failing task-defined decisions restores 43.7% of selections at β=2 (task success 0.0%→47.6%), and on real hallucinated slots reaches 32.5% selection / 57.5% grounded continuations at β=4 versus 5.0% / 28.8% for an equal-norm random direction — but rule failures recover 0.0% selection even at β=16, with the median margin only moving from −14.6 to −11.7.
- **Where the effect lives (Table 6, Table 14).** Substituting only the direct ΔB term recovers 73.5–93.9% of the exact role effect (rule 73.5/88.3/87.7%, evidence 85.9/92.0/93.9%), while attention reweighting ΔW recovers 0.0–1.9% and matched non-role positions −0.9% to 1.4%. At a fixed prefix, removing only the direct evidence term at the factual decision changes the selected fact in 31.6% of cases versus 1.7% for a matched non-evidence span, while 91.8% of *earlier* first divergences are carried by indirect terms.
- **First-order adequacy (Table 5).** Summed first-order contributions track exact stepwise score shifts with median Spearman 0.85–0.94, and the sign of the accumulated contribution matches the exact trajectory shift in 77.1–91.4% (rule) and 94.0–97.0% (evidence) of responses.
- **Multi-source evidence (Tables 11–12).** On two-hop MuSiQue items, grounded preference is 82.7/89.8/98.0% (original) vs 46.9/43.9/50.0% (counterfactual answer) and 63.3/69.4/76.5% (no evidence); removing the answer passage costs 4.50–5.82 nats of margin while removing the bridge passage costs only 1.07–1.98 — the answer depends on distributed evidence but the final choice is governed by the fact-bearing passage.

## Authors & Credibility

Tianxiang Chen, Jingyuan Zhou, Guoyao Yang and Kaidi Yang are at the Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering, NUS (Kaidi Yang corresponding). This is an unusual home department for mechanistic interpretability, and the group has no visible prior track record in this specific literature; the paper reads as a careful, internally consistent first entry rather than a lab's flagship result. The writing is dense but disciplined, the appendix carries full proofs (Prop. 1, Theorem 1, Prop. 2), and the AI-use statement plus reproducibility statement are explicit (code in supplementary, to be released). The reference base is appropriate (function vectors, task vectors, retrieval heads, RAGTruth, CL-bench, CLASHEval) though engagement with circuit-level ICL work (induction/semantic heads) is thinner than the topic warrants.

## Critical Assessment

The strongest part is the intervention discipline: every representational claim is paired with a causal test, and the paper does not hide the awkward result that rule failures are *unrecoverable* even at 16× amplification while evidence failures are often recoverable. That asymmetry — plus the 79% agreement between predicted and observed decision outcomes — is the most convincing evidence that the framework has real content rather than being post-hoc description.

The weakest part is scale and control design. **Sample sizes are small and unevenly reported**: Table 2b's values (100.0 → 4.8/2.4/4.8; 53.3/57.1/58.1; 61.5/62.3) imply denominators of roughly 21 and 26 items, yet Table 2 carries no confidence intervals, while Tables 8 and 11 do — the paper's most quotable number ("61.5–62.3% → 7.7–13.5%") rests on ~26 hallucinated slots total. **The control set is thin relative to the claims**: comparisons are against random directions, matched non-role spans, and pathway zeroing, but never against published interpretability interventions (Todd et al.'s function vectors, Wu et al.'s retrieval heads, attention knockout), so "attention is the dominant route" is only dominant relative to random. The MLP numbers in Table 7(b) make this concrete: rule Qwen attention 30.79 vs MLP 26.14, evidence Qwen 8.76 vs 7.18 — the gap is real but far from decisive, and the decomposition *by construction* excludes MLP transport of role information.

**A genuine confound in the few-shot ICL section**: on SST-2, permuting the label assignments gives Qwen *higher* accuracy than the original task (86.7% vs 77.1%; Mistral 82.9% vs 82.9%), while "mapping follow" is 84.8%. If breaking the input–label pairing helps Qwen, the "broken rule" control is not a clean contrast, and the reported accuracy drops (e.g. 25.7 pp for Qwen) may partly reflect sensitivity to label-slot statistics rather than rule induction. TREC numbers are also low (35.2–52.4% on six classes), and Qwen's TREC accuracy-drop CI includes zero ([−1.9, 20.0]).

**Theory-to-claim drift.** Theorem 1 is an honest Taylor expansion whose remainder is explicitly *not* assumed small (the authors say so in B.3), so "sufficient magnitude + coherent signed aggregation" are necessary conditions for the *first-order term* to be large, not for the role effect to exist. The abstract and conclusion nevertheless slide toward presenting them as necessary conditions for influence. The 22.9% sign-mismatch rate for rule responses on Qwen (77.1% sign agreement) is a substantial fraction of cases where the accumulated contribution points the wrong way, which is in tension with a mechanistic account that treats sign coherence as the organizing principle.

**The "unified" claim is comparative rather than unifying.** Rules are operationalized as synthetic format/selection templates and evidence as RAGTruth factual slots; the two roles differ in decision frequency and difficulty *by construction*, so "different decision organization explains the opposite outcomes" partly restates the design. Table 10(b) is the tell: at matched decision deficits, single-label rule decisions recover 6.5% of the time versus 47.6% (task-defined) and 58.8% (real-hallucinated) for evidence — deficit alone does not explain the asymmetry, and the "sustained support across repeated decisions" explanation is never quantified (no model of how per-decision support compounds over a trajectory).

Finally, **no deployable mitigation is offered**. Amplification requires knowing both the target token and the decision position, i.e. an oracle; the paper is explicit that this is a diagnostic, but a reader looking for a hallucination fix will not find one here. All models are 7–8B, single-family per vendor, greedy-decoded, with no seed variance and no statement of whether instruct-tuned checkpoints were used — which matters a great deal for both role-following and hallucination behavior.

## Strengths

- Matched-counterfactual design is genuinely clean: only the role span changes, so content, length, and query are held fixed across conditions.
- Three-level triangulation (probe → score decomposition → causal intervention) with the same quantity B^f_{ℓ,i} carried through all three; unusually coherent for an interpretability paper.
- Honest reporting of a negative result (rule failures not recoverable at 16× amplification) and an explicit, pre-registered-style quantitative prediction (Prop. 2) that is then checked (79% decision-level agreement; task-level 23.3→69.9% predicted vs 25.2→58.3% observed).
- Full proofs in the appendix, plus a well-designed appendix program: few-shot induced rules, distributed multi-hop evidence, competing sources, depth-resolved carry, attention-vs-MLP.
- The temporal finding (evidence influence concentrates 6–8 tokens *before* the emitted fact, carried by indirect terms) is a non-obvious, testable claim about where grounding failures are actually decided.

## Limitations

- Small evaluation sets for the headline causal numbers (n ≈ 21–26 per cell in Table 2), no CIs there, and no per-item breakdown.
- Only three open 7–8B models; no frontier or larger model, no instruct/base ablation, single decoding configuration.
- Role-span contribution is defined over attention only; MLP-mediated transport of role information is excluded by construction and only partially probed via update-family interventions.
- Controls are random/non-role ablation, not competing published attribution methods, so claims of "dominance" are relative to a weak reference class.
- Few-shot ICL results are confounded by the permuted-label control being easier than the original task on SST-2 for Qwen.
- Remainder term R in Theorem 1 is unbounded in practice; no empirical quantification of how much of Φ^f is *not* captured by the selected coordinates (the 73.5–93.9% recovery is for the trajectory-level effect but per-step remainder is not reported).
- No mitigation strategy, and the recovery analysis relies on oracle knowledge of the target token.

## Relevance

直接对应 **LLM Hidden States & Interpretability**：它给了一套可复用的"按来源位置切分注意力贡献 → 一阶分解 → 消融/衰减/放大"的归因配方，且把表征探测与因果干预绑在同一个量上，比多数只做 probing 的工作更可用。对关心"内部表征是否真的约束输出"这一可靠性问题的人（包括把 hallucination 当作机制性失效而非安全问题的读者）值得精读方法与前两节，结果部分的样本量需要打折看。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How large is the per-step remainder |R_t| relative to the first-order terms in practice, and does the sign-coherence condition survive at the step level (not just aggregated over responses)?
- Would the same rule/evidence asymmetry hold on a frontier model where format-following is near-ceiling — i.e. is the rule failure mode an artifact of weak instruction-following in 7–8B checkpoints?
- Can the amplification diagnostic be turned into a non-oracle intervention, e.g. by selecting the decision position from a learned probe rather than ground-truth fact location?
- Does the "evidence influence precedes the fact by 6–8 tokens" pattern replicate on long-context RAG with multi-sentence evidence, or is it an artifact of short single-span prompts?
- What happens at matched *decision counts* rather than matched deficits — if rule following needs k decisions of support, does accuracy degrade exponentially in k as the sustained-support account predicts?
