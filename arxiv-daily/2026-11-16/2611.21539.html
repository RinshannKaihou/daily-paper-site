Why it matters

Splits VLM two-image matching failures into “computed but not read out” versus “never compared”, giving a reusable head-level localize-and-repair recipe for multimodal interpretability.

对 VLM 诊断、注意力头干预与参数高效微调都有直接方法借鉴价值；Transplant/Upstream 分解可作为机制干预实验的模板。

Critical edge

“0.02% of parameters” is a mechanistic-insight headline, not an efficiency result: a generic all-layer rank-2 LoRA beats the targeted repair by 8.0 and 12.3 pp at essentially the same parameter count.

Methodology

2 images + drawn ref4-way letter answerreadout ladderrank headsmass-fixed knockouttargeted Q/K LoRAtransplant

Measure what is readable, separately from what is said

Stimuli. Every item is a four-way multiple choice over two images: image 1 carries a drawn reference marker, image 2 holds candidates A–D. Four tasks — Syn-squiggle (unnameable closed splines), Syn-known (ten nameable outline classes), HP (same-instance points from homographies), SP (semantic keypoints from SPair-71k). 1,000 dev + 1,000 test items each; distractors at least 8% of the image diagonal away.

Readout ladder. Acc → Acccal (dev-fitted multinomial calibration) → Probeans (input-matched probe at the answer position) → Probereg (best 30×30 px region readout). Only the last one is privileged to see the annotated correspondence locations, which is what makes an “it is in there” claim checkable.

Localization. Rank heads by how strongly option tokens attend to their matching image-1 region; take C32 (Qwen3-VL-8B) / C16 (InternVL3.5-8B). Knock them out three ways — Block (remove mass), Uniform (spread the same mass evenly), Shuffle (permute it) — against 10 layer-matched random sets and 3 attention-mass-matched sets.

Repair and decomposition. Train Q/K of Ck only (A), with query-only, random-head, mass-matched, value-only and all-layer controls. Then transplant the trained image-2 attention rows into the base model and back, splitting the gain into Upstream (inputs changed) and Local (weights changed).

Strengths

Why the claims are hard to wave away

  • Matched controls everywhere: random heads, layer-matched heads, attention-mass-matched heads, magnitude-matched subspaces, shuffled labels, image ablation, residual-norm bands.
  • Uniform/Shuffle at fixed mass is the best methodological idea here — it turns “these heads matter” into “the pattern of their attention matters”.
  • Uncommon honesty: Appendix C enumerates every pre-registered criterion that failed, including the failure of the positive control that the probe direction should steer the output.
  • The transplant decomposition answers a question most adapter papers never ask: did fine-tuning change the weights, or the inputs?
  • A probe-free diagnostic: selectivity Δref on a fixed head set rises .0107 → .0426 (A) / .0771 (D) and orders accuracy with Spearman 0.89–0.96 — usable on any fine-tune immediately.

Limitations

Read the headline with these attached

  • The task format carries part of the claim. Two images, one drawn marker, four letters. The heads are salience-gated — move the marker to a distractor and the answer follows it. Language-specified references are untested.
  • No PCK@0.1, no standard SPair-71k numbers. “+28.3 pp” means this 1,000-item 4-way proxy, not progress on correspondence as the field measures it.
  • Semantic causality is post-hoc. Ck is localized on exact copies (where SP knockouts are null) and then reused as an intervention site.
  • The lost exact-copy answer channel is unidentified — only a linear probe direction is ruled out; the actual pathway is future work.
  • Generic adapters win. D beats A by 8.0 / 12.3 pp at the same parameter count; C beats A on Qwen.
  • Collateral damage is under-weighted. A drops InternVL exact-copy accuracy 15–16 pp, and no MMMU/MMBench/OCR evaluation bounds the side effect.
  • Small effects, narrow support: several headline comparisons are +2.1 to +3.6 pp with lower bounds of 0.2–0.8 pp; the selectivity correlation is fit over 7–9 arms.
  • Two 8B SigLIP2 models carry the mechanism. Single author, no replication, artifacts promised rather than released.

Open questions

Where this line of work goes next

  1. Does the two-locus taxonomy survive when the reference is named in language (“the red cup on the left”) rather than drawn?
  2. What happens on standard PCK@0.1 — does training Ck’s Q/K improve keypoint transfer, or only this letter-choice proxy?
  3. Which channel does carry the exact-copy answer, if not the probe’s 3-D subspace? A multi-layer or MLP-pathway search is the obvious next step.
  4. What is the minimal union of “change the comparison” and “change the compared features” that matches D at A’s parameter budget?
  5. Can InternVL’s collateral drop (.926 → .776) be avoided by mixing synthetic exact-copy items into training, or by regularizing toward base attention off-distribution?
  6. Does Δref predict downstream fine-tuning quality outside geometric matching — OCR, chart reasoning — or is it specific to correspondence?
  7. Is the 16-head InternVL set stable across head-ranking seeds, or does the criterion pick a different but equally functional set each time?

Verdict

High for interpretability of VLMs; moderate for practitioners.

The two-locus split is a real conceptual contribution, and Upstream/Local is a reusable template for any adapter. The targeted repair is neither the strongest nor the safest option available — the authors say so in §6.1, even if the title-level framing invites the other reading.
