The spurious agreement trap

A termination-token mismatch (<|endoftext|> vs <|im_end|>) suppresses the student's mode without ever teaching the teacher's token. Overlap looks perfect while generation degrades.

EOS mismatch→mode suppressed→length explosion

Fixing it — align the terminator or mask its gradient — cuts max-length rollouts from 0.95 to 0.25 and validation length from 7,234 to 3,939 tokens, with overlap essentially unchanged (0.988 → 0.989).

Overlap was never the diagnostic. Coverage of the teacher's mass was.
01

How it works

exact steps

The paper runs a tight loop: derive a local bound, check it on real tokens, then act only where the diagnosis fires.

  1. Local theory. One SGD step on the sampled-token OPD loss at a fixed state gives Δθ = ηA(eu − πθ). Prop. 1 expands the softmax Jacobian into an advantage, the current probability, and a coupling term B(c,u;s).
  2. Two consequences. The sampled token's change is clamped by p(1−p)², so rare teacher-preferred tokens get almost no correction. Non-sampled tokens split at a watershed τ(u;s)=‖πθ‖²−πθ(u); Theorem 1 shows the student mode always sits above it and therefore moves against the sampled advantage.
  3. Measure on 786,374 tokens. Δlog π is binned by pre-update π(u) and |A|; forward-KL change is measured on a fixed held-out set of teacher prefixes and cross-tabulated against student entropy, teacher entropy, and Mtop-p.
  4. Account for the rest. Cross-position terms Mij=ηAj⟨hi,hj⟩ explain the gap between the single-state story and realized updates.
  5. Route, don't replace. Only at low-coverage or high-underestimation states swap sampled-token reverse KL for unnormalized top-K (K=16) forward KL.
Δlog π(c|s) = ηA(s,u)·B(c,u;s) + O(η²)
|A|p(1−p)² ≤ limη→0 |Δπ|/η ≤ 2|A|p(1−p)²

Routing rule. Route when Mtop-p < 0.2 or V(θ;st) > 0.3, where V is the teacher top-p mass the student underestimates by more than 6×. Observed trigger rate: 6.7% of positions.

04

Strengths

what holds up
  • Theory generates predictions rather than decorating the intro: the p(1−p)² clamp, the watershed, and the mode-direction theorem are each checked on real tokens.
  • Appendix B.1 admits the sampled objective truncates reward-to-go and is unbiased for Eq. 2, not the exact gradient of Eq. 1 — a distinction most OPD papers blur.
  • The EOS case study has two independent controls and an injected-mismatch test showing ordinary tokens are harmless (\n\n 1.8% → 0.02%).
  • Mtop-p is proven a better routing signal than teacher entropy, not asserted: 18.12 vs 16.12 at matched ~6.7% FKL.
  • Cost accounting is specific and reproducible: named checkpoints, full hyperparameter tables, baseline thresholds, bootstrap definition.
05

Limitations

what to discount
  • Theory is single-state and assumes independent per-state logits; the authors' own ~50% sign agreement shows that assumption fails at LLM scale.
  • Training is short — 200 steps base, 100 steps SFT — so it is unknown whether routing fixes collapse or merely delays it.
  • No All-FKL table row, no full-vocabulary reverse KL run; TrOPD, TIP, Teachability-Aware OPD and REOPOLD are discussed but never trained.
  • Every method experiment is Qwen3-8B teaching Qwen3; cross-family teachers appear only in diagnostic figures, narrowing the claimed mismatch regime.
  • Best@8 is a bootstrap estimate, not pass@8, so headline numbers are not comparable with most OPD papers; no seed or variance reporting anywhere.

Open questions

for the next run
  • How much of the 1.7B gain survives if ε₁, ε₂, ε₃ are chosen on a held-out set such as MATH-500 rather than AIME/AMC?
  • Does the advantage persist past 200 steps, or does routing only delay the collapse? Plot completion ratio and Mtop-p at 600+ steps.
  • What does All-FKL score at matched compute? If it is within noise, the contribution is the diagnosis, not the method.
  • Is Mtop-p predictive for GRPO/RLVR collapse too, or is it specific to distillation?
  • Why does the gain concentrate on LiveCodeBench and vanish on HumanEval+/MBPP+ — long-generation effect, or simply unsaturation?
  • Why isn't the free termination-token fix (align EOS or mask its gradient) the headline recommendation instead of a single-table case study?
