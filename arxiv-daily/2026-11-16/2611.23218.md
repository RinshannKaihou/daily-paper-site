> **一句话总结：** 本文审计 DiffHeads 这类"按公平性标签给注意力头打分再消融"的方法，发现头归因图与一个完全忽略标签的响应长度图相关系数高达 ρ = −0.950（Qwen-2.5-7B）与 −0.768（Llama-3.1-8B），但用干预打断长度–标签耦合后长度只解释 1% 方差、真正主导的是 prompt 身份（R² = 0.635），且在任何固定 prompt 内公平标签与受保护群体词出现与否几乎共线（ϕ = 0.951），因此该统计量在观测上不可辨识；预设头集合消融后不公平率无一下降，而其补集（按相反标签选出）稳定破坏拒答行为（Fisher 合并 p = 2.7 × 10⁻⁵）。

> **TL;DR:** An audit of contrastive head-level bias attribution (DiffHeads, Han et al. 2026) on Qwen-2.5-7B-Instruct and Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct shows the head map correlates ρ = −0.950 / −0.768 with a label-blind response-length map, but a prompt-controlled intervention (terse vs verbose arms) kills that reading: within the terse arm length explains only 1.0% of score variance (ρ = −0.025, p = 0.85) while the fairness label correlates +0.690, and prompt identity alone explains R² = 0.635 — more than length (0.433) and label (0.273) combined. Ablating the prescribed heads never reduces the unfairness rate (Qwen 0.775 → 0.775, Δ = +0.013, Holm 1.000), whereas ablating the complement set destroys refusal (Qwen 0 unfair→fair vs 8 fair→unfair, p = 7.8 × 10⁻³; Llama 0 vs 13, p = 2.4 × 10⁻⁴), so the null is a property of the selected set, not of the pipeline's sensitivity. The construct is further non-identifiable because inside any fixed prompt the fairness label and protected-group-token presence are collinear at ϕ = 0.951.

## 核心贡献

- **一个会骗人的相关性对照，以及抓住它的干预。** 用"标签盲"的响应长度图做对照，得到 ρ = −0.950（Qwen）、−0.768（Llama），前十名头里有九个/八个落在最被长度抑制的头之中；四项额外对照也全部同意长度解释。但作者随后用四条 prompt 条件（DA / CoT / verbose / terse）打断长度–标签耦合，发现这一整组相关性其实是"臂间效应"（between-arm artifact），干预直接否证了长度假设。
- **两项带不确定性的"不复现"结果，以及为其背书的阳性对照。** 偏见选择性并非晚层集中（layer-matched 参考下质心 11.72 vs 均匀零假设 13.5，p = 0.011；Llama 13.67 vs 15.5，p = 0.021，八种模型×参考×标注器组合中有七种偏早、零种显示原文报告的晚层集中）；同时没有任何测试过的头集合在消融后降低不公平率。补集（按相反标签取 top-K）则稳定破坏拒答（合并 p = 1.75 × 10⁻⁵），证明流水线在这个尺度上确实能检出效应。
- **两项测量陷阱。** 单次随机基线落在 0.641–1.000 的零分布带内，几乎不提供信息；消融导致的退化生成会被标注器判为 "fair"，最典型的是 Llama final-layer 预设臂：31% 输出不可分类、KL 最高（0.386）、却出现全研究唯一一次 0.127 的"不公平率下降"——那是模型损坏，不是去偏。
- **可辨识性论证。** 在任意固定 prompt 内，公平标签、响应长度与受保护群体词的出现是共线的（terse 臂 ϕ = 0.951，59/60 一致），其中一个方向由标注规则本身蕴含，因此任何观测分析都无法把该图归因于三者中的任何一个；而这一家族没有任何方法做了能区分它们的实验。
- **可迁移的方法论建议。** 相关性对照图是必要但非充分条件；识别出候选混淆后必须用实验打断耦合；并提出该领域应报告的三项诊断（显式替代假设下的对照图、≥10 次匹配零分布的分布带、带显式分母约定的退化筛查）加一项补集阳性对照。

## Key Contributions

- **A control that misleads and the intervention that catches it.** A label-blind length map built from the same per-example scores correlates at ρ = −0.950 (Qwen) and −0.768 (Llama) with the fairness map, with 9/10 and 8/10 of the top heads among the most length-suppressed; four further controls agree, and the correlation strengthens as the adversarial persona is weakened. A four-arm intervention that breaks the length–label coupling rejects the reading: within the terse arm (mean 10.1 tokens, sd 5.6) length explains 1.0% of score variance (ρ = −0.025, p = 0.85) while the label correlates +0.690 (p = 1.1 × 10⁻⁹).
- **Two non-reproductions with quantified uncertainty plus a positive control.** Bias selectivity is not late-layer-concentrated under either reference reading (7 of 8 model × reference × labeller cells put it earlier than chance, none late), and no tested head set reduces the unfairness rate when ablated. The complement set — top-K on the opposite label, not the bottom of the same ranking — produces a replicated unidirectional effect (Fisher χ² = 26.34, p = 2.7 × 10⁻⁵ stored; χ² = 27.28, p = 1.75 × 10⁻⁵ reselected), which is the positive control the ablation-audit literature has been missing.
- **Two measurement hazards, quantified.** Ten layer-matched random draws span 0.641–1.000 (Qwen) and 0.603–0.886 (Llama), so a single random baseline is uninformative about both magnitude and sign; and ablation-degraded generation is scored as fair, illustrated by a prescribed arm with 31% unclassifiable output and an apparent 0.127 unfairness reduction.
- **A non-identifiability argument grounded in the labelling rule.** Within a fixed prompt, fairness label, response length and protected-group-token presence are collinear at ϕ = 0.951 (59/60 concordant, terse arm, n = 60), one direction of which is entailed by the labeller's group-name matching. Variance decomposition: length alone 0.010, adding group-token 0.534, adding the label instead 0.550, all three 0.558.
- **Three recommended diagnostics plus one new one.** An explicit-alternative control map, a ≥10-draw matched null reported as a band, and a degeneracy screen with an explicit denominator convention — plus the finding that a correlational control map is necessary but not sufficient.

## Methodology

**Models and elicitation.** Qwen-2.5-7B-Instruct (28 layers × 28 heads) and Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct (32 layers, 32 query / 8 KV heads), both d_h = 128, run in 4-bit NF4 with the output projection dequantised for scoring, greedy decoding, English only. Elcitation is role-play forced choice between two social groups on a trait ("Who do you think is more trustworthy? Men or women?"), under an adversarial persona, in two styles: Direct-Answer (no reasoning) and Chain-of-Thought ("Let's think step by step"). Identification uses 60 questions × both styles at max_new = 128, regenerated under three persona strengths (full, mild, none); evaluation uses 80 balanced held-out questions (8 protected pairs × 10) with no trait–pair overlap with identification.

**Labelling.** A response is *unfair* if it commits to one of the two groups, *fair* if it declines or hedges, *unclassifiable* otherwise. The labeller reads the opening 250 characters with three negation-aware rules added after auditing all stored responses. Unlike the original (an unvalidated LLM judge), it is validated against blind human annotation stratified across all 26 arm strata of both models' evaluation files: agreement 0.867 [0.794, 0.916], false-unfair 0.120, false-fair 0.044, 11/120 unclassifiable.

**Score.** For response tokens R, head output z_{r,h} (the o_proj input slice), output-projection block W_{O,h} and unit reference v̄_ref, the per-head score is the mean squared projection of the head's contribution onto the reference, computed separately over fair and unfair sets and standardised *within* each layer (residual-stream norms grow with depth, so flattened standardisation ranks layers, not heads). Scores average at most the first 64 response tokens. The source underspecifies v̄_ref's space, so both final-layer and layer-matched readings are implemented, with layer-matched reported as primary (higher reliability) and every conclusion verified under both.

**Selection and intervention.** With T_K the top-K heads, the prescribed differential-unfair set is H_diff = T_K(S_unfair) \ T_K(S_fair); K = 20 is set by the authors because the source leaves k unstated, yielding 10 heads on both models. The reverse set (same difference taken the other way, truncated to equal cardinality) is the positive control. Layer-matched random sets replace each prescribed head with one from the same layer, ten draws per model.

**Masking.** Zeroing each selected head's contribution at *every* decoding position via a forward hook on o_proj (masking only the final prompt position removes almost nothing). Before each masking run the authors require next-token KL(base ‖ masked) > 10⁻⁴ and differing greedy text — a fidelity check adopted after an early run with silently detached hooks produced statistics indistinguishable from a genuine null.

**Outcome and statistics.** Primary outcome is unfair / (unfair + fair), with unclassifiable responses reported separately and never folded into the denominator. Controls: split-half reliability (Spearman–Brown corrected), label-permutation placebo, a five-signal degeneracy screen, a prompt-only manipulation check, and a three-strength persona ablation. Wilson intervals; Fisher's exact for pairs; McNemar at item level with Holm correction within each model across clean arms (11 on Qwen, 10 on Llama); permutation nulls for layer distributions; Spearman over all L × H heads, disattenuated by √(ρ₁ρ₂).

**The key intervention (Sec. 4.4).** The same 60 questions, persona, model and decoding under four prompts: DA, CoT, verbose ("state the choice, then explain in detail") and terse ("answer in one short sentence"), none mentioning fairness, stereotypes or groups. The two new arms populate the long-unfair and short-fair cells missing from the original data. The ten prescribed heads are loaded fixed; nothing is re-identified. 220 of 240 generations receive a label (151 unfair, 69 fair). The authors pre-registered four predictions about the outcome and report that two of the four failed.

## Results & Highlights

- **The behavioural premise replicates, the mechanistic account does not.** Unfairness rate is 0.800 under DA vs 0.225 under CoT on Qwen, and 0.778 vs 0.167 on Llama — gaps of 0.575 and 0.611. The paper is explicit that everything downstream concerns the mechanistic explanation, not the phenomenon.
- **The length-map correlation is large and reliable, and still wrong.** ρ = −0.950 (Qwen) / −0.768 (Llama) with map split-half reliability 0.950 and 0.959 (collapsing to ≈0 under label permutation), and 9/10 and 8/10 top heads overlapping the ten most length-suppressed. Pooled across the four new arms the correlation looks confirmed (ρ = −0.758, p = 2.3 × 10⁻⁴²) — but this is a between-arm effect: the arms differ in mean length by ~38× and in prompt text.
- **Within-prompt, the length hypothesis dies and prompt identity wins.** In the terse arm (10.1 tokens mean, sd 5.6) length explains 1.0% of variance (ρ = −0.025, p = 0.85) while the label correlates +0.690 (p = 1.1 × 10⁻⁹). Arm membership alone explains R² = 0.635 of score variance, more than length (0.433) and label (0.273) combined, and the per-arm partial effects are non-monotonic in length: terse (~10 tok) +0.538 and CoT (~384 tok) +0.479 both strongly positive, verbose (~165 tok) −0.641. A length direction cannot produce that ordering. Two of the authors' four pre-registered predictions held, two failed.
- **Late-layer concentration does not reproduce; early concentration appears instead.** Under the layer-matched reference, bias selectivity sits earlier than chance: centre-of-mass 11.72 vs a uniform null of 13.5 on Qwen (p = 0.011) and 13.67 vs 15.5 on Llama (p = 0.021), with the final four layers carrying under half their chance share. Seven of eight model × reference × labeller cells place selectivity earlier than chance; none shows the reported late concentration.
- **Ablating the prescribed heads never reduces unfairness.** Thirteen arms per model on 80 held-out questions (baselines 0.762 Qwen, 0.708 Llama). Qwen stored set: 0.775, Δ = +0.013, 1 up / 2 down, Holm 1.000. Qwen layer-matched: 0.873, Δ = +0.111, 0 unfair→fair vs 8 fair→unfair, p = 0.0156. Llama layer-matched: 0.795, Δ = +0.086, 3/11, p = 0.344. Llama final-layer returned Δ = −0.127 but at 31% unclassifiable and is excluded as breakage. Item-level movement across all evaluable arms: +1, +8, +8 (prescribed) and +8, +7, +13, +16 (complement) — not one moves in the claimed direction, while layer-matched random draws move refusal both ways.
- **The complement set is the positive control.** Stored selections: Qwen 0 unfair→fair vs 8 fair→unfair (p = 7.8 × 10⁻³), Llama 0 vs 13 (p = 2.4 × 10⁻⁴); Fisher combination χ² = 26.34, p = 2.7 × 10⁻⁵, df = 4. Reselection onto near-disjoint head sets (sharing 2–3 of 10 heads) replicates: χ² = 27.28, p = 1.75 × 10⁻⁵. Exactly one counter-directional flip exists in the entire study (reselected Qwen, primary labeller), and it is reported.
- **The random null is wide enough to swallow a single baseline.** Ten layer-matched draws span 0.641–1.000 on Qwen (9 clean) and 0.603–0.886 on Llama (8 clean); the stored prescribed sets sit at the 33rd and 62nd percentiles (z = −0.36, +0.62), the stored complement sets at z = +0.37 and +2.13 — the Llama complement outside its band, so the complement claim rests on the item-level asymmetry rather than rate-level movement.
- **The prompt moves the metric 0.787; the heads move it 0.000.** Full persona + DA gives 0.967, no persona 0.180 (Δ = −0.787, p < 10⁻⁴), explicit fairness instruction 0.279 (Δ = −0.689, p < 10⁻⁴), with zero unclassifiable responses. Masking the identified heads gives 0.000 (95% CI ±0.063).
- **Collinearity is definitional, not contingent.** In the terse arm the fairness label and protected-group-token presence are the same variable at ϕ = 0.951 (59/60 concordant). Of the 13 fair terse responses, 12 contain no protected-group token at all — terse refusals negate at the trait level ("not determined by gender") rather than naming the groups. Variance explained: 0.010 for length alone, 0.534 adding group-token, 0.550 adding the label instead, 0.558 for all three — so the label's increment over length+group-token is essentially nil.
- **Masking more than doubles response length without changing verdicts.** 21.4 → 47.4 tokens on Qwen and 27.2 → 60.0 on Llama, while the prescribed arm exceeds the 250-character labelling window at 0.475 vs 0.138 for baseline. Correcting for the resulting misclassification *increases* Δ under every error model examined (reported Δ is a lower bound), but the corrected Δ still sits inside the random null band in all eight model × error-model combinations.
- **Determinism and intervention fidelity are checked.** 24/24 byte-identical responses across independent runs; masking KL 0.006–0.211 (Qwen) and 0.075 (Llama), with altered greedy text in every run.

## Authors & Credibility

Single author, S. M Seefat Alam, at Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET), Dhaka, writing with a personal Gmail address; this appears to be a preprint with no lab affiliation, no funding statement and no visible co-authors or senior advisor. On the usual provenance heuristic that is a weak signal, and the paper's own Limitations acknowledge the main scale gap: 60 identification questions against the original DiffHeads' 2,516.

Against that, the methodological execution is unusually disciplined for a solo preprint. The labeller is validated against blind human annotation drawn from the evaluation data itself (0.867 agreement, stratified across all 26 arm strata), rather than inheriting an unvalidated judge. The masking pipeline has an explicit intervention-fidelity check (KL > 10⁻⁴ and altered greedy text) adopted after a silent failure was detected. The authors pre-registered four predictions for the decisive experiment and report that two failed — including the two about the label — and they report the single counter-directional flip that weakens their own positive control. Every conclusion is verified under both reference-vector readings because the source specification is ambiguous. The statistics are appropriate and not ornamental: Wilson intervals, McNemar with Holm, permutation nulls, ten-draw matched nulls, disattenuated Spearman. The paper also credits its closest precedent (Kendiukhov 2026, genomics) for the positive-control idea rather than claiming the diagnostic as novel.

Where credibility thins: no inter-annotator agreement (all annotation by the author), the sensitivity/specificity correction rests on eight observations each, and the scoring implementation was never independently re-implemented from the equation text — the authors say so themselves.

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty.** The contribution is not a new method but a validity audit, and the specific novelty is well located: the paper shows that the *control map* diagnostic — the obvious fix for exactly this class of confound — can itself return ρ = −0.950 for a hypothesis an intervention then rejects. That is a sharper and more transferable result than the individual non-reproductions, and the complement-set positive control is genuinely new in this literature (Yang et al. 2023 mask the *bottom* of the same ranking to test ordering; this masks the top under the *opposite* label to test whether the pipeline detects anything at all — a strictly stronger check that the family already had the vocabulary for via Islam et al.'s sign partitioning).

**Rigor.** The strongest part is the design of Section 4.4: rather than adding another observational control, the authors manufacture the cells missing from the original data (long-unfair, short-fair) by instruction, hold the head set fixed, and let the within-arm correlations adjudicate. The terse arm is a well-chosen natural experiment — at 10.1 tokens (sd 5.6) a length direction has almost no room to vary, so a −0.025 correlation is close to decisive rather than merely suggestive. The layer-matched random null (ten draws, reported as a band) is the right correction to a literature that uses a single draw or the best of several. The degeneracy analysis is also unusually honest: the authors caught their *own* earlier analysis crediting breakage as improvement (Appendix B.8) and report it.

**Weak baselines and gaps in the audit itself.** Several self-acknowledged soft spots matter. (i) The length hypothesis is tested on exactly one member of the family — DiffHeads, the generative/judge-labelled subfamily — so the construct-validity critique is argued to generalise while the intervention evidence does not. (ii) The ϕ = 0.951 collinearity is measured only in the terse arm (n = 60), where a ten-token refusal has little room to name both groups; the entailment holds everywhere by construction, but the converse is arm-dependent and unmeasured elsewhere, which weakens the "no observational analysis could attribute the map" claim from a measurement to an argument. (iii) Prompt identity conflates wording, instruction position and response format, which co-vary across the four arms — the paper says so, but it means R² = 0.635 is a bound on a bundle, not an identified cause. (iv) CoT token counts are censored at the generation cap, and 20/60 and 24/60 CoT responses were unclassifiable, so the CoT arm contributes labels but no continuous length and no full evaluation set. (v) The Qwen reselected set has a substantially earlier layer profile (mean layer 11.5 vs 15.8), so the layer-matched band does not transfer to it and its percentile position is simply omitted — defensible, but it leaves the reselected arm's rate-level result unanchored.

**Overclaiming check.** The framing is careful almost to a fault: the paper repeatedly disclaims that it shows bias is absent, that bias cannot be localised in heads, or that mitigation is unnecessary, and it explicitly separates the replicating behavioural premise (gaps of 0.575 / 0.611) from the failing mechanistic account. The one place the rhetoric runs slightly ahead of the evidence is the abstract's "no method in this family performs an experiment that could" — that is a claim about a literature the paper samples at one member plus a survey of the others' designs, not something it demonstrates by audit. The identifiability argument is also, strictly, about *this* operationalisation: minimal-pair designs (Jahan 2026; Yang et al. 2023) equalise response form by construction and so escape the length confound, though the authors argue they trade it for a near-definitional attribute-token confound.

**Red flags.** None that look like misconduct. The real risks are scale and single-authorship: 60 identification questions vs 2,516, 4-bit NF4 quantisation with no assessment of quantisation effects on head-level attribution, a 64-token averaging cap that truncates CoT (immaterial for DA), all annotation by one person with no inter-annotator agreement, and a scoring implementation never independently re-derived. The layer-distribution result is explicitly reference-dependent (significant early concentration under one reading, indistinguishable from uniform under the other), which the paper reports rather than smoothing over. A reader should treat the *negative* results (no late concentration, no reduction in unfairness under any tested set) as robust — they hold across both models, both reference readings, two labellers and reselections — and the *positive* mechanistic claims (prompt identity at 64%) as well-supported but not identified to a single prompt property.

## Strengths

- The paper's central move — build the confound control, then *test it with an intervention instead of trusting it* — is the right methodological lesson and is demonstrated rather than asserted. Reporting that a ρ = −0.950 diagnostic was wrong, and that every additional correlational control agreed with the wrong answer, is more useful to the field than a clean negative result would have been.
- The positive control is well constructed and honestly reported: the complement set replicates on near-disjoint reselections (2–3 of 10 heads shared, p = 1.75 × 10⁻⁵) while the prescribed set never helps, and the single counter-directional flip is disclosed because, as the authors put it, it is what makes the surrounding zeros meaningful.
- Denominator discipline is treated as a first-class measurement issue: unclassifiable responses are never folded in, and the paper shows concretely (31% unclassifiable, apparent Δ = −0.127) how the opposite convention manufactures a debiasing result — including in the authors' own earlier analysis.
- The null is reported as a distribution (ten layer-matched draws, 0.641–1.000) rather than a point, which immediately exposes why single-random-baseline comparisons in this literature are uninformative about sign as well as magnitude.
- Every headline conclusion is verified under two reference-vector readings and two labellers, because the source specification is genuinely ambiguous (undefined reference space, inconsistent averaging set, an equation contradicting its own figure caption, unstated k). This is the right response to an under-specified baseline.
- The Limitations and Ethical considerations sections are substantive rather than boilerplate: they name the scale gap, the quantisation gap, the single-annotator gap, the window exposure and its direction of bias, and explicitly warn that the paper is not licence to stop measuring bias.

## Limitations

- **Scale.** 60 identification questions vs the original's 2,516; the authors state they cannot fully separate "the method does not replicate" from "the reduction loses the effect", though map reliability above 0.95 and the complement-set control argue against the latter.
- **One family member.** The length intervention targets only the generative, judge-labelled subfamily (DiffHeads). The construct critique is argued to generalise; the experimental evidence does not.
- **Collinearity measured in one arm.** ϕ = 0.951 comes from the terse condition alone (n = 60), where short refusals have little room to name both groups; the converse direction is arm-dependent and unmeasured elsewhere.
- **Prompt identity is a bundle.** Wording, instruction position and response format co-vary across the four conditions, so which property carries the 64% is not identified.
- **CoT is not measurable with this dependent variable.** 20/60 and 24/60 CoT responses were unclassifiable, and CoT token counts are censored at the generation cap; the elicitation-dependence analysis rests on nine unfair examples and is not computable on Llama (n = 6).
- **Dependent-variable exposure.** Agreement is 0.867 within the 250-character window but 0.696 beyond it, and the prescribed arm exceeds the window three times as often as baseline (0.475 vs 0.138). The correction increases Δ under every error model, but it rests on sensitivity/specificity estimated from eight observations each, and the corrected Δ still lies inside the random null band.
- **Implementation not independently verified.** The scoring code was checked against the source equation structurally but never re-implemented by a party without access to the code; a third reference reading could in principle recover a different ranking.
- **Null band does not transfer.** The ten-draw band is layer-matched to the stored head sets; the reselected Qwen set (mean layer 11.5 vs 15.8) has no percentile position reported.
- **Quantisation and annotation.** Both models run in 4-bit NF4 with no assessment of quantisation effects on head attribution; all human labelling is by the author, with no inter-annotator agreement available.

## Relevance

与"LLM Hidden States & Interpretability"方向高度相关：它是对机制可解释性中"头归因 + 消融"这一整类方法的结构效度审计，核心结论（相关性对照可能给出 ρ = −0.950 的错误答案，必须用干预打断混淆）可以直接迁移到任何"定位某个内部单元负责某个行为"的研究。对关注评测可靠性与测量陷阱的读者也有价值——它示范了退化生成如何被当作去偏成功、以及单次随机基线为何不可用。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the prompt-identity effect survive when wording, instruction position and response format are varied *independently* rather than bundled into four arms? A 2 × 2 × 2 factorial over these three properties on the same 60 questions would identify which one carries the 64%.
- Is the ϕ ≈ 0.95 label/group-token collinearity an artifact of the terse arm, or does it hold at longer lengths where refusals have room to name both groups? A length-stratified labelling analysis would settle whether the non-identifiability claim is a measurement or a theorem for this design.
- Would a minimal-pair variant (attribute word swapped, response form equalised by construction) actually escape the confound, or does it inherit a near-definitional attribute-token dependence as the authors suspect? This is the one design in the family the paper could not test directly.
- Can the complement-set positive control be made into a standard reporting item, and what does it look like for methods that do not partition heads by score sign (e.g. annealing search, head-mask gradients)?
- What does the layer-distribution result look like under a third reference reading constructed from an independent, non-response-derived vector (e.g. a probe direction fit on held-out data)? The two readings implemented disagree on whether Qwen's selectivity is early or uniform.
- How much of the "bias head" map's prompt sensitivity is a property of the adversarial persona specifically? The correlation with the length map strengthens as the persona weakens, which hints the statistic may be measuring instruction-following style rather than trait content.
- Does the degeneracy hazard (masking more than doubling response length, 21.4 → 47.4 tokens) appear in other head-ablation mitigation work that reports fairness gains without a generation-quality screen? A re-analysis of published arms with an explicit denominator convention would be cheap and possibly uncomfortable.
