> **一句话总结：** 本文将 on-policy distillation 中"出错后是否保留前缀"重新表述为反事实可恢复性问题，通过对每个错误状态执行预算匹配的教师续作分支和回滚分支比较其经验成功率，将状态分为可恢复/不可逆但可避免/歧义三类，并以 oracle 控制器在 AIME2025 冻结评估上达到 0.578 成功率（强于最佳基线 0.517），证明可恢复性是比散度更优的选择性监督决策变量。

> **TL;DR:** The paper reformulates on-policy distillation (OPD) post-error supervision as counterfactual recoverability: it replays each error state through budget-matched teacher-continuation vs. rollback branches, labels states as recoverable / irreversible-but-avoidable / ambiguous, and shows an oracle controller built on these labels lifts held-out AIME2025 success to 0.578 (vs. 0.517 for the best SOD baseline) and improves AIME2024–2025 average@32 from 0.2656 to 0.3125, while a recoverability proxy hits AUC 1.000 versus 0.392 for divergence alone.

## 核心贡献

- **提出反事实可恢复性框架**：把 OPD 中"出错后该继续还是回滚"的形式化为一个有预算约束的执行性问题，重建错误前的前缀并分别运行教师续作分支和回滚分支，按经验成功率差 Δ_action(s) = p_cont − p_roll 将状态分为可恢复、不可逆但可避免、歧义三类。
- **设计三向 oracle 控制器**：可恢复状态保留前缀并追加教师纠错视野，不可逆但可避免状态触发回滚重采样，歧义状态回落到普通 SOD，从而把因果标签与候选条件化的 OPD 主干解耦。
- **引入候选条件化主干消除混淆**：让师生模型在同一可接受动作集合 A(s) 上打分并各自归一化，规避了不同 tokenizer、无效动作与不可比较 log-prob 带来的伪信号。
- **可恢复性代理远超散度判别力**：在 99 个非歧义 AIME 状态上，分支派生的可恢复性代理取得 AUC=1.000，而散度单独仅 0.392（甚至低于随机基线 0.500）。
- **跨协议的冻结评估增益**：在 disjoint 30-task AIME2025 多步集达到 0.578 成功率，60-task AIME 多步集 0.583，并在自由文本 AIME2024–2025 与 GPQA-Diamond 协议上同步提升 average@32 与 pass@32。
- **消融定位最大贡献来源**：组件消融显示"保留教师可纠错前缀"是单步贡献最大的部件，去掉 retain 后 held-out-60 从 0.583 跌到 0.483。

## Key Contributions

- **Counterfactual recoverability formulation**: reframes the post-error OPD decision as an executable question — under matched remaining budget, is teacher continuation more successful than rollback — and categorizes each error state as recoverable, irreversible-but-avoidable, or ambiguous based on Δ_action(s) = p_cont − p_roll with pre-registered thresholds (p_cont ≥ 0.50, p_roll ≥ 0.50).
- **Three-way oracle controller**: maps audited labels to retain / roll-back / default-to-SOD training actions, deliberately separating the causal label from the backbone loss so a missing label or budget mismatch cannot silently become a strong intervention.
- **Candidate-conditioned distillation backbone**: both teacher and student score a shared admissible action set A(s) and normalize within it, eliminating invalid-action confounds and incomparable cross-tokenizer log-probabilities; the SOD weight w_k still operates on the sequence-mean log-prob gap as a diagnostic, never equated with recoverability.
- **Diagnostic superiority over divergence**: a branch-derived recoverability proxy reaches AUC = 1.000 on 99 non-ambiguous AIME states, versus 0.392 for divergence alone and 0.500 for budget-only, with a proxy-minus-divergence AUC gap of 0.608 (group-bootstrap interval [0.424, 0.817]).
- **Frozen cross-protocol evaluation**: oracle-assisted adapters (n = 3 seeds) reach 0.578 on the disjoint AIME2025 multi-step set and 0.583 on the 60-task AIME set, plus consistent gains on free-text AIME2024–2025 and GPQA-Diamond at 32 samples.
- **Ablation isolating retention as the largest contributor**: removing retention alone drops held-out-60 success from 0.583 to 0.483; removing rollback drops it to 0.550; the monotonic pattern identifies retaining teacher-correctable prefixes as the larger single contribution.

## Methodology

The paper targets a precise local decision inside on-policy distillation (OPD): after an observed erroneous action in a sequential environment, should training retain the realized prefix and apply corrective teacher continuation, or roll the action back and resample a fresh branch?

**Counterfactual recoverability labels.** For a replayed error state s, the method reconstructs the pre-error prefix and runs k teacher-continuation branches and k rollback branches under a matched remaining budget B(s) (environment steps, tokens, temperature, sampling). Letting p_cont(s) and p_roll(s) be their empirical success frequencies, it computes Δ_action(s) = p_cont(s) − p_roll(s). With pre-registered (non-learned) thresholds, states are labeled:

- recoverable: p_cont(s) ≥ 0.50
- irreversible-but-avoidable: p_roll(s) ≥ 0.50 and p_cont(s) ≤ 0.25
- ambiguous: everything else (kept as a substantive third class, not forced into a binary)

**Candidate-conditioned OPD backbone.** At each state the environment exposes a shared admissible action set A(s). Teacher T and student θ each score every a ∈ A(s) using their own sequence-average log-probabilities, then normalize within that set with a distillation temperature τ_d. The rollout action is sampled from the student candidate distribution; the teacher target is a*(s) = argmax P_T; the retained-target loss is L_OPD(s) = −log π_θ(a*(s) | s). Candidate JS divergence D_JS(s) is logged as a local disagreement summary, while the SOD reproduction retains its official cumulative-ratio weighting rule over the sequence-mean log-prob gap d_k. The two quantities have different roles and neither is assumed to equal recoverability.

**Oracle control rule.** Recoverable states retain the prefix and assign a corrective horizon; irreversible-but-avoidable states trigger rollback/resampling; ambiguous states fall back to ordinary SOD. The controller only consumes the audited record and reverts to the ambiguous path on any missing label or budget mismatch, making the oracle an upper-bound mechanism with observable failure behavior.

**Safeguards.** (1) Replay reconstructs the pre-error prefix before either branch; (2) branch checks assert matched remaining budgets; (3) the E3 diagnostic separates the probe branches used to build the recoverability feature from held-out branches used as the prediction target, preventing leakage.

**Evaluation pipeline.** Five pre-specified questions (E0–E5) gate the study: E0 verifies training health, E1 checks reliable state replay, E2 tests opposite branch effects per label, E3 evaluates recoverability as a predictor, E4 audits oracle feasibility under a 2× cost ceiling, and E5 runs frozen adapters on disjoint AIME2025, a 60-task AIME set, AIME2024–2025 free-text at 32 samples, and GPQA-Diamond (198 questions) at 32 samples. Models are a Qwen3.5-9B student and a Qwen3.5-27B teacher.

## Results & Highlights

- **Opposite action effects by label class (E2)**: across 99 non-ambiguous recorded AIME states, the mean continuation-minus-rollback effect Δ_action is **+0.185** for the 65 recoverable states and **−1.000** for the 34 irreversible-but-avoidable states, a between-group difference of 1.185 with group-bootstrap interval [1.092, 1.277] — non-overlapping intervention preferences that divergence alone cannot separate.
- **Recoverability proxy dominates divergence as a diagnostic (E3)**: on the 99-state, 18-task-group diagnostic, the branch-derived recoverability proxy reaches **AUC = 1.000** (balanced accuracy 1.000), while divergence alone scores **0.392** (below the 0.500 no-skill reference), budget-only 0.500, and action-type-only 0.000. The proxy-minus-divergence AUC gap is **0.608** with bootstrap interval [0.424, 0.817].
- **Frozen AIME2025 multi-step success (E5)**: the recoverability oracle reaches **0.578 ± 0.019** on the disjoint 30-task AIME2025 multi-step set, versus **0.517 ± 0.017** for the best E0 baseline (SOD reproduction) — a +0.061 delta. On the larger 60-task AIME multi-step set it reaches **0.583 ± 0.017** vs. best-E0 **0.492 ± 0.008**.
- **Free-text AIME2024–2025 average@32**: the oracle improves **average@32 from 0.2656 (strongest E0) to 0.3125** and **pass@32 from 0.3917 to 0.4556**, a +0.0469 / +0.0639 improvement.
- **GPQA-Diamond (198 questions, 32 samples)**: the oracle improves **average@32 from 0.2702 to 0.3070** (+0.0368) and **pass@32 from 0.3939 to 0.4461** (+0.0522).
- **Component ablations (3-seed mean, held-out-60)**: full oracle 0.583; removing rollback → 0.550; removing retain → 0.483; removing both (label-only) → 0.450, identifying retention as the largest single contributor.
- **Oracle feasibility audit (E4)**: seeds 0/1 completed 300 updates with zero budget mismatches, processing 2,454 and 2,525 labeled states; teacher-token inflation was 1.543× / 1.819× and environment-step inflation 1.546× / 1.820×, all under the configured 2× ceiling. The oracle used ~662.8k teacher tokens vs. ~220k for each E0 control.
- **Online training summary (Table 4)**: the oracle achieves the highest online task-success mean (0.539) but also the lowest teacher–student agreement (0.573, vs. 0.625–0.781 for E0 controls), consistent with selective correction rather than maximizing imitation at every state.

## Authors & Credibility

All four authors are affiliated with Tsinghua University (corresponding emails under `mails.tsinghua.edu.cn` and `sz.tsinghua.edu.cn`), with Kehong Yuan and Shaohua Ma based at the Shenzhen campus. The paper is posted on arXiv (2608.04408). The work is unusually disciplined about scope: the authors repeatedly emphasize that the oracle is an upper bound, not an online deployment method, that all performance claims are bounded by the supplied manifests and seed counts, and that the candidate-conditioned normalization is an experimental control rather than a universal objective claim. The methodology is pre-registered into five gated questions (E0–E5), with explicit decision rules and an integrity-check table rather than a leaderboard. References cover the relevant OPD lineage (Agarwal et al. 2024 on OPD; Zhong et al. 2026 on SOD; Li et al. 2026a/b on curriculum and phenomenology) and counterfactual RL explanations (Amitai, Septon, and Amir 2024).

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: genuinely novel framing. The central insight — that a divergence score is a property of two policies, not a decision variable for whether to retain an error prefix — is sharp and well-motivated, and the matched-budget replay protocol (with ambiguity as a substantive third class) is a cleaner instrument than prior divergence-only reweighting.
- **Rigor**: high. Pre-registered thresholds (p_cont ≥ 0.50, p_roll ≥ 0.50) are explicitly analysis-population definitions, not learned parameters; probe-target split in E3 prevents branch-outcome leakage; budget-mismatch counts are logged per seed; and the candidate-conditioned backbone neutralizes the cross-tokenizer confound between Qwen3.5-9B and Qwen3.5-27B (conservatively treated as unaligned token sequences, so d_k is only a sequence-mean log-prob gap, not token-level KL).
- **Reproducibility**: moderate-to-good. Manifests, models (Qwen3.5-9B/27B), training-update count (300), parse/admissible/target rates, and NaN counts are reported, and the SOD weight formula is reproduced with the official bounded-amplification form. However, only 3 training seeds (0–2) are recorded, held-out set sizes are small (30 / 60 AIME tasks, 198 GPQA questions), and training cost is not matched between oracle and baselines, so the headline deltas are not from an equal-budget study.
- **Significance**: meaningful as a diagnostic proof-of-concept, but the deployable claim is deferred. The recoverability labels are assembled from extra teacher branch probes (~3× teacher tokens), 101 of 200 E2 states are ambiguous, and the paper is explicit that "a deployable state-only estimator must abstain and be tested under matched budgets." So this is an upper-bound mechanism, not a production method.
- **Red flags**: (1) the AUC = 1.000 result on 99 states in 18 task groups is suspiciously perfect and the authors themselves caveat it as "diagnostic evidence from teacher branch probes, not a learned online estimator"; (2) divergence-only AUC of 0.392 sits below the 0.500 no-skill reference, which the paper flags but readers should weigh carefully — the diagnostic may overstate the gap if the recorded divergence is a degenerate sequence-mean proxy rather than true token-level SOD; (3) the headline AIME2025 0.578 vs 0.517 delta comes from unequal-cost runs (662.8k vs ~220k teacher tokens), so it does not establish cost-matched superiority; (4) single teacher (Qwen3.5-27B) and single student (Qwen3.5-9B) family limit generalization claims.

## Strengths

- Reframes a vague "when to intervene in OPD" question into a concrete, executable, budget-matched counterfactual with a clean three-way label (including an explicit ambiguous class that a deployable controller could abstain on).
- Candidate-conditioned scoring over a shared admissible action set is a genuinely useful methodological control that other agent-distillation papers could adopt to remove invalid-action and tokenizer confounds.
- Honest, almost unusually disciplined scope: explicit about the oracle being an upper bound, about cost inflation (1.5×–1.8× under a 2× ceiling), about 101/200 ambiguous states, and about unequal-cost comparisons.
- Strong diagnostic separation: opposite signs of Δ_action (+0.185 vs −1.000) with non-overlapping bootstrap intervals is a clean falsifiable finding.
- Five-gate evaluation structure (E0–E5) makes the integrity checks legible instead of burying them in an appendix.

## Limitations

- **Single teacher / single student family**: only Qwen3.5-9B student and Qwen3.5-27B teacher; no test of whether the recoverability labels transfer across model families, sizes, or tool environments.
- **Small seed count and unequal cost**: 3 training seeds (0–2) with standard deviations over fixed task sets; the oracle uses ~3× the teacher tokens of E0 controls, so the AIME/GPQA deltas are not cost-matched.
- **Ambiguity is large**: 101 of 200 E2 states lack enough branch evidence to prefer continuation or rollback — a deployable state-only estimator must abstain on roughly half the states, which the authors flag but which materially bounds practical impact.
- **Perfect AUC under caveat**: the E3 AUC = 1.000 comes from teacher branch probes, not a learned online predictor, and the 0.392 divergence baseline may be a degenerate sequence-mean proxy rather than true token-level SOD.
- **Domain coverage**: only mathematical reasoning (AIME) and one science benchmark (GPQA-Diamond); no agent/tool-interaction tasks where the recoverability framing is arguably most motivated (the introduction sells sequential tool interaction, but the experiments are exam-style QA).
- **No learned online controller**: the paper explicitly leaves the deployable state-only estimator as future work; what is evaluated is an oracle that consumes additional teacher rollouts at training time.

## Relevance

这篇论文与"on-policy 蒸馏 / agent 蒸馏 / 推理模型训练"方向高度相关，其核心观点——"出错后是否保留前缀应由可执行的反事实成功率决定，而非散度阈值"——对 reasoning 模型的选择性监督、错误恢复与多轮 agent 训练有直接启发，尤其适合关注 SOD、trajectory-level 蒸馏与 self-correction 机制的研究者跟踪。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Can a state-only predictor (e.g., a small classifier over context + budget features) approximate the oracle labels well enough to retain most of the gain while staying within the E0 cost budget?
- Does the recoverability signal transfer across model families (e.g., Llama ↔ Qwen teacher/student pairs) and across tokenizer alignments, or is the candidate-conditioned normalization doing most of the work?
- How does recoverability interact with inference-time recovery mechanisms (Reflexion, self-refine) — does training-time rollback supervision reduce or complement the need for inference-time retry?
- On genuine tool-use / agent environments (not exam-style AIME/GPQA), what fraction of error states fall into each label class, and does the 50% ambiguity rate observed here shrink or grow?
- Would a soft or graded recoverability signal (rather than the pre-registered hard thresholds) give a smoother trade-off between intervention aggressiveness and abstention?
