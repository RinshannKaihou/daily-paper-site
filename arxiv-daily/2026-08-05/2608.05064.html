Provable Limits and Certified Deferral for Verbalized Uncertainty in Small Language Models ↗

Why this matters
Couples verbalized confidence, post-hoc calibration, and finite-sample risk certificates into a laptop-runnable pipeline — telling us when small open LLMs should abstain.
Runs on one MacBook Air M4 · 0.14–2.55 s/question · auditable (c, ĉ, τ, action) tuples
Critical edge
The certificate is never compared against any baseline selective-prediction method — conformal risk control, Geifman–El-Yaniv, nothing. The empty 10% outcome may be Clopper–Pearson conservatism, not model failure — and that confound is never isolated.

Methodology / 4 stages

verbalize → calibrate → certify → defer

  • Problem setup. Model emits label ŷ + raw confidence c ∈ [0,1] (verbalized integer / 100). Defer when calibrated ĉ < τ; otherwise answer. Deployment fixes max tolerable risk rmax.
  • Elicitation. Single prompt, letter,number format, one generation pass per question — no token logprobs. Strict + lenient parsers; refusals auto-defer.
  • Post-hoc calibration. Compares temperature scaling, Platt sigmoid, isotonic. Quality = ECE over 10 equal-width bins; fit on disjoint 200-question Dcal.
  • Certify. τ* = min{τ : CP-upper ≤ rmax}. Proposition 3: with n(τ) retained i.i.d. examples and k(τ) errors, R(τ) ≤ BetaInv(1−δ; k+1, n−k) at prob ≥ 1−δ (δ = 0.05). Union bound δ → δ/|T| covers data-dependent threshold.

AUROC ceiling
  • TS floor matched to a fraction of a point. Qwen2.5-0.5B on TruthfulQA hits ECE 0.283 vs. Corollary 1 bound 0.276 (gap 0.7 pp); Llama3.2-1B 0.254 vs. 0.252.
  • Platt dominates temperature scaling everywhere. Gemma3-1B on ARC: ECE 0.687 → 0.030 under Platt; Qwen2.5-0.5B on ARC → 0.022, the table minimum.
  • Certified autonomy is rare. At 20%: Qwen2.5-14B/ARC (99.8% coverage, 8.1% risk), Qwen2.5-7B/ARC (93.3%, 10.8%), Qwen2.5-14B/TruthfulQA (43.6%, 14.2%). At 10%: none.
  • AUROC stays weak. Sub-2B models ~0.5; Llama3.2-3B on TruthfulQA 0.468; largest models only 0.63–0.66. Gemma3-12B hits 89.2% ARC accuracy at AUROC 0.509.
  • Thresholds don't transfer cross-task. ARC→TruthfulQA at 20% breaches budget by 1.3–30.6 pp for every model; calibration fixes scale, not discrimination.

Strengths & Limitations / side by side

+ Strengths

  • Theory and measurement agree quantitatively — TS infeasibility floor met within a percentage point, a falsifiable prediction rare in calibration work.
  • Honest negative results (no certification at 10%, weak AUROC, cross-task transfer failure) reported prominently, not buried.
  • The TruthfulQA answer-ordering audit (all 817 correct options first) is a concrete, immediately reusable methodological fix.
  • Fully local M4 setup + transparent (c, ĉ, τ, action) rationale broadens replicability and auditability.

− Limitations

  • Both benchmarks are multiple-choice; open-ended generation (where elicitation is harder) is untested.
  • The CP certificate assumes i.i.d. and is void under distribution shift — a strong assumption for real edge deployments.
  • 200-question calibration sets cap certifiable coverage; the empty 10% result may be sample size as much as model quality.
  • Models capped at 14B under one default Ollama quantization; no precision sensitivity, no code release referenced.
  • No comparison to conformal prediction or other guaranteed-risk baselines.

Open questions / for further work

  1. Would conformal risk control (Angelopoulos & Bates 2023) or the Geifman–El-Yaniv guaranteed-risk bound certify strictly more autonomy than Clopper–Pearson at the same 200-question budget on these 22 pairs?
  2. Does the Corollary 1 infeasibility floor extend to open-ended generation, where verbalized confidence is harder to elicit and the accuracy floor shifts?
  3. How sensitive is the sparse-certification finding to quantization — do FP16 builds of the same models cross the 10% threshold?
  4. The TruthfulQA position artifact is repaired by a hash permutation — how many published TruthfulQA MCQ numbers are inflated, and by how much, when re-scored?
  5. Can a learned (rather than fixed-prompt) elicitation push AUROC above the ~0.66 ceiling and translate into measurably more certified coverage at fixed rmax?
