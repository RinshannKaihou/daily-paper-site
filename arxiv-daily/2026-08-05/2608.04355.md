> **一句话总结：** 论文将"自我修正后准确率变化"精确分解为"内容变化边距"(content margin，两次回答都可解析) 与"格式边距"(format-recover/loss，可解析性变化)，在 Qwen3.5 (0.8B-9B)、Gemma-4-12B 及 Tencent Hy3、Nvidia Nemotron-3-Ultra-550B 共 29 个主网格 + frontier 臂上证明：中大规模模型上多数显眼的"自我修正增益"其实是答案抽取格式修复造成的伪信号 (Wilcoxon p=1.7e-3)，用语法受限解码因果控制可在中位水平关闭原始总效应与内容边距之间 71% 的差距；calibration-floor 分析揭示"小模型有内容变化但缺少可用的置信信号，大模型有信号但缺少内容变化余地"的双向挤压。

> **TL;DR:** The paper introduces an exact additive decomposition of the always-revise accuracy shift into a content margin (both answers parseable) and two format margins (parseability gains/losses), and shows across 29 (model, task) cells (Qwen3.5 0.8B-9B, Gemma-4-12B) plus a frontier arm (Tencent Hy3, Nvidia Nemotron-3-Ultra-550B) that format effects dominate content effects on the 12 active-extraction-channel cells (Wilcoxon p=1.7e-3); a grammar-constrained re-extraction causal control closes a median 71% of the total-vs-content gap on 14 cells, and a verbatim replication of Li et al.'s IoE protocol on Qwen3.5 does NOT reproduce its reported gain (GPT-3.5: 74.9%→78.5%, +3.6; Qwen3.5-4B: 81.8%→81.5%, Qwen3.5-9B: 89.5%→88.2%) but instead reproduces this paper's near-zero content-margin pattern. The calibration-floor criterion reveals a scale squeeze: at frontier scale all 5 cells have exactly zero content margin despite total effects up to +0.275, while floor-scale (0.8B/2B) cells have real harmful content flips (GEE odds ratios 15.9×/21.0×, p<1e-7) but no signal that clears the floor.

## 核心贡献

- **精确可加的边距分解 (Margin Decomposition)**：将"总是修正"策略下观察到的总准确率变化 Δ_total 严格分解为 Δ_content (前后两次回答都可被解析器抽取时的答案改变) + Δ_format-recover (初答抽取失败、修正后成功) + Δ_format-loss (初答成功、修正后失败)。该分解对整个样本是代数精确的可加恒等式，适用于任何使用答案抽取器的"总是修正"轨迹。
- **从相关走向因果的语法受限解码控制 (Causal Control)**：冻结模型已生成的推理文本，仅在最后通过 grammar-constrained decoding 强制产生可解析答案。在 14 个网格单元上，该因果控制可关闭原始总效应与内容边距之间中位 71% 的差距 (2B/4B ARC 完全收敛)，把对 C1 (格式伪信号主导) 的证据从观察性提升为因果性。
- **收敛性的六重独立证据**：通过 (1) 提示词制度符号反转、(2) 强制延续探针、(3) 提示词复述臂、(4) 因果控制、(5) 跨家族复现 (Gemma-4-12B)、(6) 逐字文献协议复现 (IoE on Qwen3.5)，六个独立检查共同支持"中规模及以下模型的显眼自我修正效应主要来自格式边距"。
- **尺度对比 + 聚类统计模型**：用聚类 GEE (clustered by cell, controlling for task) 给出 floor-scale (0.8B/2B) 相对 capable-scale (4B-12B) 在内容变化/有害翻转上 15.9× / 21.0× 的优势比 (p=8.2e-8 / 7.9e-11)，并配以任务配对符号检验、精确 checkpoint 排列、checkpoint 聚类自助法三层稳健性检查，诚实报告"4 个 checkpoint 的显著度天花板"。
- **重导出的 calibration-floor 准则 + Squeeze Plane**：把 δ-校正 (δ=0.01) 的 AUROC* 准则重新应用到内容边距上，并用非参数自助法纠正了两个 binormal 假阳性。在 (内容余地, 信号盈余) 平面 (Figure 5) 上，可行门控象限只有 1 个边缘成员 (9B TriviaQA)，其封存 holdout 策略实现增益仅 +0.005、oracle-regret 比例 0.83 (>0.50 阈值)。
- **可复现的轨迹库与仪器**：发布完整的离线可重算 JSONL 轨迹库、抽取门、边距分解、统计检验、因果控制、atlas 图表的代码 (cf_core.py, decompose_margins.py 等)；主网格在 Apple Silicon Mac Mini 上零 API 成本完成，约 40 小时墙钟。

## Key Contributions

- **Exact additive margin decomposition.** An algebraically exact identity Δ_total = Δ_content + Δ_format-recover + Δ_format-loss over the full sample (not just the parseable subset), applicable to any always-revise trajectory with an answer extractor. The decomposition is not a causal estimate (bothok membership is post-treatment-selected) but is exact bookkeeping; only Δ_content can be attributed to a genuine answer change.
- **Causal test via grammar-constrained re-extraction.** Freezes already-generated reasoning text and forces a guaranteed-parseable final answer through logits-masking, closing a median 71% of the total-vs-content gap on the 14 cells with an active extraction channel (2 cells converging exactly), moving the evidence for C1 from observational to causally probed.
- **Six convergent independent checks for format dominance.** (1) Same-cell sign reversal between truncation-prone and repaired prompt regimes, (2) forced-continuation probe, (3) three-template paraphrase arm, (4) the constrained-decoding causal control, (5) cross-family replication on Gemma-4-12B, (6) verbatim IoE protocol replication on Qwen3.5 that reproduces this paper's pattern rather than its source's reported gain.
- **Scale contrast backed by a clustered model with honest significance ceilings.** A logistic GEE (exchangeable correlation, clustered by cell, controlling for task) on 3,815 bothok rows gives floor-tier odds ratios of 15.9× (content change) and 21.0× (harmful flip) with p < 1e-7; checkpoint-level robustness checks (task-paired sign test, exact 4-checkpoint permutation, checkpoint-clustered bootstrap) honestly report the significance ceiling forced by only 4 distinct checkpoints.
- **Calibration-floor criterion recomputed on the content margin.** A δ-corrected (δ=0.01) AUROC* floor that blocks the degenerate binormal corner where AUROC>0.5 nominally crosses λ at FPR→0 with vanishing Δ; a nonparametric bootstrap re-derivation reverses two binormal false positives on 0.8B GSM8K and 2B MMLU, tightening rather than weakening the floor-side squeeze.
- **Released instrument and trajectory library.** Fully offline-reproducible extraction gate, margin decomposition, statistical tests, causal control, atlas figures; primary 29-cell grid runs at zero API cost on a single Mac Mini (~40 h wall-clock).

## Methodology

**Frozen-trajectory offline evaluation.** For each (model, task, prompt template) the authors run ONE always-revise trajectory a0 → review → a1 → review → a2 (T=2, early-stop if answer unchanged) under greedy decoding, and record per-node text, correctness, four intrinsic confidence signals (A: token-logprob aggregate; A': Kadavath-style teacher-forced P(True); B: length-normalized sequence log-likelihood; C: verbalized 0-100). Because gating only decides keep-a0 vs adopt-af (it does not alter revision content), all gating policies — NEVER, ALWAYS, every (signal, τ), per-sample oracle — are evaluated offline from this single trajectory at zero additional generation cost.

**The per-sample identity (§3.2).** For a gate triggering revision below threshold τ: Δ(τ) = (1-acc0)·TPR(τ)·r(τ) - acc0·FPR(τ)·d(τ), where r is the repair rate and d the damage rate. Profit at τ requires TPR/FPR > λ(τ) with λ = acc0·d/((1-acc0)·r). At the ALWAYS point (TPR=FPR=1) profit requires λ<1, giving Huang et al.'s negative result as the high-λ open-reasoning regime. A δ-corrected floor AUROC* is solved numerically: max_τ Δ(τ) ≥ δ with δ=0.01 fixed in advance, blocking the degenerate binormal-ROC corner.

**The format/content margin decomposition (§3.3).** Every (a0_answer, af_answer) pair falls into exactly one category: bothok (CONTENT margin), recover (FORMAT-RECOVER), loss (FORMAT-LOSS), or dead. Scoring is unconditional (unparseable = wrong, as in any accuracy pipeline), giving the exact additive identity Δ_total = Δ_content + Δ_format-recover + Δ_format-loss. Only Δ_content can be attributed to a genuine answer change; the format margins are attributable to whether the free-form text happened to contain something the extractor could read off, sensitive to token budget and prompt wording.

**Admission gates (§4.2, §4.9).** Floor gate (acc0 ∈ [0.20, 0.85] with ≥60 wrong/correct on dev), rigidity gate (change rate ∈ [0.03, 0.97]), signal gate (non-zero variance), and a post-hoc extraction-completeness gate (both a0-bad and af-bad rates <0.25; 29 of 31 generated cells pass). Two prompt regimes — truncation-prone vs repaired — are run on 8 cells to provide C1's sign-reversal evidence.

**Causal control via grammar-constrained decoding (§4.12).** For MCQ cells: append "Final answer (a single letter):" and force exactly 1 token from valid option letters; for GSM8K: append "Therefore, the final numerical answer (digits only) is" and force ≤8 tokens from {0-9, '.', ',', '-', space, EOS, newline}. Implemented as a logits_processor masking disallowed vocabulary entries; the preceding CoT is never constrained. Run on 14 admitted cells built on GSM8K or MCQ tasks (not MATH/TriviaQA — answer space not enumerable in a small character-class grammar).

**Six synthetic sandbox checks (§5.4).** Before pointing the instrument at real models, the authors verify on data with known ground truth that (1) the per-τ identity holds to <1e-12, (2) the ALWAYS endpoint and Huang localization match analytic predictions, (3) the δ-floor separates pass/fail cells with 100% agreement, (4) offline gating equals brute force to <1e-15, (5) explicit dominates implicit ROC, (6) the δ-correction blocks the degenerate corner.

**Statistical analysis (§7).** Row-level percentile bootstrap (B=10^4); Wilcoxon signed-rank on |Δformat|−|Δcontent| across active-channel cells; logistic GEE (exchangeable correlation, clustered by cell, controlling for task) for the floor-vs-capable contrast; BH-FDR 0.05 within the C1/C2 rank-test family; nonparametric percentile-bootstrap floor call as a distribution-free re-derivation. Sealed holdout evaluation: holdout labels encrypted at generation time, decrypted only after dev τ* selection is frozen.

**Models and tasks.** Qwen3.5-{0.8B, 2B, 4B, 9B} (4-bit MLX, thinking mode off), Gemma-4-12B (ollama, think:false), plus Tencent Hy3 and Nvidia Nemotron-3-Ultra-550B via OpenRouter free API. Tasks: GSM8K, MATH L1-3, HumanEval, MBPP-sanitized, MMLU (4 subjects), ARC-Challenge, TriviaQA, CommonsenseQA, TruthfulQA-MC1; n=400/cell (300 at floor scale), 50/50 dev/holdout split, seed 2026 frozen before generation.

## Results & Highlights

- **Format effects dominate content effects on active-channel cells (C1).** Across the 12 admitted cells with a0-bad ≥ 5%, |Δformat| exceeds |Δcontent| with median difference +0.055; one-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank p=1.7×10^-3 (BH-adjusted within the C1/C2 family to 2.6×10^-3).
- **Causal control closes a median 71% of the gap (C1, causal).** On the 8 cells with a0-bad ≥ 8% under the original extractor, grammar-constrained re-extraction produces median closure 71% (mean 71%, range 55-100%) of the total-vs-content gap; 2B ARC closes exactly (−0.023→−0.053 vs content −0.053, 100%) and 4B ARC closes exactly (+0.030→+0.003 vs content +0.003, 100%).
- **Headline gains collapse under the decomposition.** The single largest apparent gain in the grid — 9B MMLU at +0.145 Δ_total — has Δ_content of only +0.007 and is +0.138 format-recover; under constrained decoding it falls to +0.053 (closure 67%). The second-largest — 4B MMLU at +0.105 Δ_total — has Δ_content exactly 0.000; under constrained decoding it falls to +0.048 (closure 55%). The residual on these two cells is honestly reported rather than dismissed.
- **Cross-family replication on Gemma-4-12B.** Content change rate is exactly 0.000 on all three tested tasks (GSM8K/MMLU/MATH) despite total effects of +0.005/+0.040/+0.030; on MMLU the 12 "changed" rows are all a0-bad rows (mostly on abstract_algebra, plausibly LaTeX-heavy), not genuine bothok reconsiderations.
- **Scale contrast with clustered model (C2).** Logistic GEE on 3,815 bothok rows across 12 matched-task cells: floor-tier rows have 15.9× higher odds of a content-level change (95% CI [5.8, 43.6], p=8.2×10^-8) and 21.0× higher odds of a harmful content-level flip (95% CI [8.4, 52.6], p=7.9×10^-11) than capable-tier rows. Matched-task cell-level Mann-Whitney on change-rate medians 0.115 (floor) vs 0.005 (capable): p=1.1×10^-3 (BH-adjusted 2.6×10^-3).
- **IoE protocol replication does not reproduce its source gain (C1 external).** Running Li et al.'s verbatim IoE protocol on Qwen3.5 GSM8K: Qwen3.5-4B is flat (81.8%→81.5%→81.5%); Qwen3.5-9B drops then partially recovers (89.5%→85.5%→88.2%). The original paper reported GPT-3.5-turbo-0613: 74.9%→77.1%→78.5% (+3.6 from IoE alone) — that model snapshot was retired by OpenAI on 2024-09-13 and cannot be tested by anyone. Margin decomposition of the Qwen runs gives Δ_content of 0.000 (4B) and +0.005 (9B); the small net-negative totals (−0.003, −0.013) are format-loss artifacts of Qwen using ## for markdown headers colliding with IoE's own delimiter.
- **Frontier check: zero content margin at ~55B-active scale (C1, exploratory).** Across all 5 frontier cells (Tencent Hy3, Nvidia Nemotron-3-Ultra-550B), Δ_content is exactly 0.000 while total effects range +0.080 to +0.275 (the single largest apparent gain anywhere in the paper, on Hy3 × MMLU). All effects are entirely inside the format margin; extraction-failure rates (22-49% a0-bad) are higher than any admitted 4B-12B cell. This arm is lower-powered and 4/5 cells exceed the 25% admission gate.
- **The squeeze (C3, exploratory).** All six floor cells fail the content-margin floor under the binormal criterion; the nonparametric bootstrap re-derivation reverses the two binormal false positives (0.8B GSM8K, 2B MMLU) so all six floor cells fail under the nonparametric criterion too, tightening rather than weakening the floor side. Across 19 capable-scale cells, the maximum 95% CI upper bound on content headroom is 0.036. Exactly one cell (9B TriviaQA) sits in the viable quadrant, but its sealed holdout policy realizes only +0.005 over the better endpoint with oracle-regret fraction 0.83 (target <0.50).
- **Grader sensitivity (MATH, §4.13/§9.1).** Re-grading with a sympy CAS checker flips 92/326 (4B), 86/313 (9B), 54/185 (Gemma-4-12B) bothok rows from wrong to right (~28% of such rows under-counted by the string normalizer), yet Δ_content is unchanged to three decimal places on all three cells (4B: +0.0061→+0.0061; 9B, Gemma: 0.0000→0.0000), confirming the format/content confound is not itself a grader artifact.

## Authors & Credibility

Mingguang Chen (DeepGrounding), Bo Qu (DeepGrounding), Licheng Wang (AlphaAvatar). The work sits in a series of companion preprints ([16] error-structure manipulation, [17] excess self-confirmation drift) that share the same frozen-trajectory harness and offline policy-evaluation engine. Credibility signals are unusually strong for a measurement/methodology paper: sealed holdout isolation (labels encrypted at generation, decrypted only after dev τ* frozen), Benjamini-Hochberg FDR correction within the prespecified C1/C2 rank-test family, six synthetic-instrument checks before any real-model run, and three layers of checkpoint-level robustness checks that honestly report the significance ceiling imposed by only 4 distinct checkpoints (exact permutation p=1/3). The authors explicitly disclose a coauthor code-review bug found on 2026-07-26 in the ROC/AUROC routines (incorrect threshold-walk direction, missing tie-averaging) that affected every AUROC-based number in §9.3-9.4 and Figures 2-5, and re-ran all affected analyses; they also disclose that the project was never formally preregistered (internal git tags only) and that the 25% extraction-completeness admission gate was added post-hoc after inspecting the first grid.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty.** Genuine. The exact additive decomposition Δ_total = Δ_content + Δ_format-recover + Δ_format-loss is novel as a measurement instrument for self-correction, as is the observation that extraction-failure rates differ between a0 and af and silently contaminate acc0, r, d in the standard calibration identity. The mistake-finding/mistake-fixing literature (Tyen et al. [9]) does not raise this layer because benchmarks report accuracy directly rather than accuracy conditioned on successful parsing. The causal control (grammar-constrained re-extraction on frozen reasoning) is a clean move from correlational to causal evidence within the narrow sense the authors define.
- **Rigor.** High on the design side: six synthetic-instrument checks, sealed holdout, BH-FDR, clustered GEE, nonparametric floor re-derivation correcting two binormal false positives, and an explicit "what the GEE does and does not fix" paragraph that surfaces the 4-checkpoint non-independence and reports the honest p=1/3 permutation floor. Honest reporting of the two residual cells (4B/9B MMLU retain +0.048/+0.053 against a near-zero content estimate) and of the 0.8B GSM8K exception (low change rate, small positive effect) without hand-waving.
- **Reproducibility.** Strong. All 29 cells' trajectories, the extraction-gate audit, margin decomposition, statistical tests, LOO, constrained-decoding causal control, grader sensitivity, GEE, floor redo, IoE replication, frontier arm, and all figures are reproducible offline from the released JSONL trajectory library with one-line uv invocations (NumPy/SciPy only; no GPU, no LLM for the analysis layer). Pinned seeds (2026), pinned environment manifest (ENV.lock), and a documented freeze order. Full trajectories omitted from the arXiv source archive only due to size, to be deposited separately.
- **Significance.** Methodology papers live or die by adoption; this one ships a drop-in instrument (cf_core.extraction_completeness, decompose_margins.py) that any frozen-trajectory pipeline scoring free-form generations can reuse. The non-reproduction of a cited confidence-gating protocol (Li et al.'s IoE on Qwen3.5 not reproducing its GPT-3.5 gain) is a concrete, falsifiable warning to the literature. The frontier check is suggestive but not sealed.
- **Red flags and weaknesses (paper-specific, not generic).** (1) The 4B/9B MMLU residual is real and the paper's two candidate explanations (imputation under-estimate vs. a probe-like effect of the forced continuation itself) are explicitly not adjudicated — readers cannot treat C1 as fully causally settled on these two cells, and they are the two largest apparent gains in the entire grid. (2) The frontier arm has severe data-completeness problems: Hy3 was retired by OpenRouter mid-collection (HTTP 404 on all subsequent requests) so its MATH file is missing and MMLU stopped at 153/200; 4 of 5 frontier cells exceed the 25% admission gate (22-49% a0-bad), so the "format-dominance intensifies with scale" claim rests on a self-selected bothok subset rather than the prespecified admitted-cell pool. (3) The 25% extraction-completeness admission gate itself was introduced post-hoc after inspecting the first grid — technically motivated but data-informed, so all analyses depending on the admitted-cell set inherit that limitation. (4) The MATH grader is an approximate string normalizer that under-counts raw accuracy by ~28% of bothok rows (though Δ_content is empirically unchanged to three decimals). (5) The IoE replication is protocol-fidelity, not literal — the original GPT-3.5-turbo-0613 snapshot was retired by OpenAI on 2024-09-13, so a non-replication on Qwen3.5 is evidence about protocol generality, not about whether Li et al.'s original numbers were wrong. (6) Within-cell signal ranking is out of scope because the self-consistency subsample fails the extraction gate, and τ-quantile transfer across tasks and quantization robustness are untested.

## Strengths

- Honest, paper-specific disclosures: the 2026-07-26 coauthor code-review bug in ROC/AUROC routines is disclosed in §9.3 and §13 with full blast-radius audit, and all affected numbers were regenerated; the post-hoc admission gate and the probe parser repair are disclosed as data-informed.
- Six independent convergent checks for C1 (prompt-regime sign reversal, forced-continuation probe, paraphrase arm, constrained-decoding causal control, Gemma cross-family replication, IoE verbatim replication) — each individually falsifiable, jointly much harder to explain away.
- Sealed holdout evaluation with physical isolation of dev/holdout (separate files, encryption) — a procedural safeguard against analyst peeking that most self-correction papers lack.
- The nonparametric floor re-derivation corrects two binormal false positives (0.8B GSM8K, 2B MMLU) on real data, exactly the failure mode the synthetic Check 6 warned about — the instrument caught its own predicted pathology.
- Drop-in reusable instrument with one-line reproducibility; zero API cost for the primary 29-cell grid.

## Limitations

- Not formally preregistered (internal git tags prereg-opt3-v2 and prereg-opt3-v3-pivot, never publicly posted); several retained cells predate the pivot amendment, four historical seals are retrospective.
- The causal control closes a median 71% of the gap, not 100%; 4B/9B MMLU retain a +0.048/+0.053 residual against a near-zero content estimate, with two candidate explanations left unadjudicated.
- Frontier arm is not sealed, carries no confidence signals (OpenRouter free tier has no logprobs), and 4 of 5 cells exceed the 25% admission gate; Hy3 was retired mid-collection and its MATH data could never be collected.
- Family generalization is broader than a single cross-check (Qwen3.5, Gemma-4, Tencent Hy3, Nvidia Nemotron via three different backends) but still short of a systematic architecture × scale grid; cross-family comparisons are directional rather than controlled backend replications.
- The 6-vs-6 cell-level Mann-Whitney treats cells as independent despite shared checkpoints; the GEE addresses row-level clustering but the checkpoint-level permutation test floors at p=1/3 with only 4 checkpoints, and model scale is confounded with checkpoint identity by design (one checkpoint per scale point).
- Two gating estimands that are not interchangeable: the sealed policy fixes p_norm and evaluates the ordinary benchmark margin; the exploratory squeeze analysis chooses the best of five dev signals and evaluates the content margin (optimistic, dev-set selection optimism).
- The IoE replication is protocol-fidelity on a different model family, not a literal reproduction of the original GPT-3.5-turbo-0613 result.
- Honest exceptions (TriviaQA at both scales, 9B HumanEval, 0.8B GSM8K, two probed cells 4B MMLU/4B CommonsenseQA) deviate from the clean pattern and are reported in situ rather than buried.

## Relevance

对自我修正与置信度门控评估方法论高度相关：论文提出的"内容边距 vs 格式边距"分解与抽取完整性门，可直接用于审查任何基于自由文本生成 + 答案抽取器打分的 LLM 评测流水线，避免把"格式修复"误读为"推理修复"。对研究 inference-time self-correction、confidence-aware gating、selective prediction 的读者是必读的测量层警示，与 Huang et al. 的负面结果、Kamoi et al. 的综述、Tyen et al. 的 finding-vs-fixing 分解构成互补的因果诊断链。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the residual on 4B/9B MMLU (closures of only 55%/67%) reflect imputation under-estimation of a genuine content effect on originally-unparseable rows, or a probe-like elicitation effect of the forced continuation itself? A design that independently elicits the latent answer on unparseable rows (e.g., a different decoder temperature, or a second teacher-forced continuation) could adjudicate.
- Does the squeeze ever open up beyond ~55B-active parameters, or only tighten? A sealed, logprob-equipped frontier run (paid API tier or a hosted 70B-class model) on a richer task grid would close the scale question the way the primary grid closes the 0.8B-12B range.
- Does the format/content split interact with quantization? All primary Qwen runs are 4-bit MLX; an FP16 or bfloat16 control would test whether quantization itself inflates the format margin (e.g., via more truncation-prone decoding).
- Can the margin decomposition be applied post-hoc to existing published self-correction benchmarks (Self-Refine, Reflexion, IoE on GPT-4) to retroactively separate format from content effects in their reported gains?
- Is there a revision-prompt family that produces both useful counterfactual answers AND preserves extraction stability, breaking the squeeze on the headroom side for capable-scale models?
- Does the format-dominance pattern generalize to verifier-in-the-loop self-correction (with external feedback), or is it specific to the no-external-feedback intrinsic setting studied here?
