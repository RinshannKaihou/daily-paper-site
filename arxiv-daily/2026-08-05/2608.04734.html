Methodology

architecture · Chebyshev + Horner

A dedicated activation systolic array sits beside the GEMM/conv array. Each PE computes IR_i = IR_{i+1}·x + a_i and pushes results downward — so a column of n+1 PEs evaluates a degree-n polynomial via Horner's method, mapping Chebyshev approximations directly onto multiply-add hardware.

Shared coefficients. All neurons in a layer share one activation function, so every PE in a row uses the same coefficient. Coefficients are hard-coded constants selected by a mux — no coefficient RAM, and the same hardware serves tanh, sigmoid, ReLU and softmax.

Softmax is not directly polynomial-approximable, so only eˣ is approximated: shift by max(x) for numerical stability, polynomial-exp per element with a running sum, then a hardware divider for the reciprocal. Vector length is bounded by the intermediate-register count (8 or 256).

Two-step quantization-aware selection. Candidates are first optimized per-function with minimum integer bits, then re-ranked under a unified Q3.12 format — exposing the counter-intuitive result that highest degree ≠ best accuracy in fixed point.

Design space
  • Parallelism: 8 or 16 columns
  • Softmax regs: 8 or 256
  • Polynomial degree: 1–12
  • Format: signed 16-bit, Q3.12
  • Process: FreePDK45 45nm
Final polynomials
  • tanh: degree-9, clip [−2,2]
  • sigmoid: degree-5, clip [−3,3]
  • exp: degree-3, clip −5
  • ReLU: comparator bypass
Baselines
  • 8-parallel fixed-point CORDIC (Raghuram et al.)
  • ONE-SA PLA, widths 0.25 & 1.0

Strengths & Limitations

side-by-side verdict
Strengths 5
  • Unified hardware for univariate activations and multivariate softmax on one systolic polynomial evaluator — a real resource-sharing edge over per-function LUT/PLA.
  • Strong, quantified long-vector softmax win at N=256, the regime that matters for transformers; KL-driven polynomial selection for exp is a thoughtful touch.
  • Feature-matched 8-parallel CORDIC baseline and synthesized (not estimated) area/power/latency under a standard PDK and commercial flow.
  • Honest design-space discussion — two-step quantization, clipping-vs-interior-error tradeoff, and a demonstration that higher degree can hurt under fixed point.
  • Extensible to other functions by adding coefficient constants and a mux setting.
Limitations 8
  • No end-to-end model accuracy — despite transformer/BERT motivation, no task-level results (accuracy, perplexity, BLEU).
  • Synthetic-input evaluation only — softmax measured on N(0,σ²) / U(−a,a), not real attention logits which can be far more peaked.
  • ONE-SA softmax comparison is assumed (not documented), weakening the 79.0% KL claim.
  • CORDIC is a weak baseline at large |x| — favorable comparisons partly reflect its narrow convergence range.
  • Unified Q3.12 sacrifices tanh/sigmoid accuracy (Q1.14 would be optimal) for softmax compatibility; per-function variant described but never built.
  • No clock frequency, throughput, or energy-per-op — only per-output latency under a filled-pipeline assumption.
  • Single node (FreePDK45 45nm) — no modern node, no place-and-route, no post-synthesis timing closure.
  • Softmax vector length hard-capped by register count (8 or 256); larger N requires a redesign.

Open questions

for further exploration
Q1
Does the 3.252 max KL at N=256 actually preserve task quality on a real transformer — e.g. BERT/GLUE or a decoder-LM perplexity at N=128/256/512/1024?
Q2
Is the 79.0% KL win robust when ONE-SA's softmax is implemented faithfully instead of the assumed 4-step variant?
Q3
What happens on real attention-logit distributions (heavily peaked) — does the clipped eˣ under-count long-tail probability mass?
Q4
How much extra tanh/sigmoid accuracy does a per-function quantization variant (Q1.14) buy, and at what binary-point-alignment cost?
Q5
What are the clock frequency, throughput, and energy-per-softmax, and how do they scale to 7nm/5nm with real place-and-route?
Q6
Can the architecture support on-the-fly coefficient re-fitting (e.g. for a calibration set) without losing the hard-coded-coefficient area advantage?
