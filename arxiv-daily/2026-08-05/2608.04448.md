> **一句话总结：** 作者将"下采样训练步是否保留原生梯度方向"这一问题分解为受缩放比控制的噪声相关数据项与受目标网格 token 数控制的 σ 无关"图基底"(graph floor)，并构造去偏估计算子测量出 (route, σ) 安全图；据此设计选择性低分辨率 LoRA 训练器，在 Anima (2B DiT) 上以 1024→896 路由、σ∈(0.5, 0.94] 的安全窗训练，在固定步数下减少 14.6% 训练时间且权重空间端点接近原生再训练。

> **TL;DR:** The paper decomposes the gap between a downscaled ("demoted") and native training gradient in a flow-matching DiT into a ratio-governed, σ-dependent data term plus a σ-independent, token-count-governed "graph floor", and debiases the cosine-based estimator (Spearman correction for attenuation) needed to measure it. On Anima (2B-param DiT, Qwen-Image VAE), the resulting (route, σ) map reveals a safe window (1024→896, σ∈(0.5, 0.94]) that the spectral account of SPD (Xiao et al., 2026) does not predict; using it as an opt-in σ-conditional LoRA trainer cuts training time by 14.6% at fixed step budget while keeping the weight-space endpoint at cosine 0.75 (late-scheduled) vs. the native retrain, against the spectral family's 0.147–0.355 RMSE vs. this account's ~0.07–0.09 on held-out routes.

## 核心贡献

- **将"降采样训练步梯度偏差"形式化并分解为两项**：定义了梯度层级的"demotion 距离" `d = 1 − cos(ḡ_src, ḡ_dem)`，并通过 `g = Jᵀ r` 的扰动展开把它分解为(a)受下采样比例控制的噪声相关"数据项"，与(b)受目标网格绝对 token 数控制、与 σ 无关的"图基底"(graph floor, RoPE 部分 + 残差部分)。这一分解在测量之前就在结构上与谱方法假设不同。
- **构造了必需的去偏估计算子**：证明朴素有限次抽样会在余弦估计算子上制造正偏置（且 arm 相关），其签名恰好与待测的 floor 不可区分；用每 arm 的自重绘自余弦 + Spearman 衰减修正 + D 外推两套独立校验来抵消，把重绘 floor 外推到自余弦 1.005 (95% CI [0.994, 1.016])，确认原生梯度是固定方向、整个 redraw floor 纯属抽样噪声。
- **在统一测量曲线上对比两种解释并对两者打分**：构造 debiased 的 (route, σ) 安全图，发现在 1024→768 路由上存在 (0.65 < σ < 0.95) 的低 excess 窗口，是任何容差下的谱准则都无法预测的。谱方法在 768/512 路由上 RMSE 为 0.147/0.355，本方法在留出路由上 RMSE 仅 ~0.07–0.09；并因果定位了两个 governor：比例定振幅、绝对 token 数定 floor。
- **提出选择性低分辨率 LoRA 训练器并验证**：仅 3 行代码的 opt-in σ 门控（σ > 0.5 时把 1024-tier 潜变量替换为缓存的下采样版本，附带 YaRN 风格 σ 门控分带 RoPE 对齐），在固定 480 步、两个单作者语料上实现 −14.6% 训练时间，端点 ΔW 与原生再训练的余弦达 0.753/0.770 (late-scheduled)，堆叠 1024→768 低 excess 窗口几乎不下降 (0.753/0.771)；图 2 的渲染对照证实端点接近原生。

## Key Contributions

- **Formalization and two-term decomposition of the "demotion gap".** Defines a gradient-level substitution distance `d = 1 − cos(ḡ_src, ḡ_dem)` (with a `reenc` control subtracted to isolate the grid change from the VAE round trip), then expands `g = Jᵀr` perturbatively into (a) a data branch governed by the downscale ratio and the noise level, and (b) a σ-independent "graph floor" set by the target grid's absolute token count (split into a RoPE part and a non-positional Resid part). The decomposition disagrees with the spectral account structurally, before any measurement.
- **A debiased estimator the question actually requires.** Shows that naive finite-draw cosines carry an arm-dependent positive bias whose signature is indistinguishable from the floor under test, and removes it via per-arm self-redraw self-cosines with Spearman's correction for attenuation, cross-checked by draw-count `c/D` extrapolation. The native redraw floor extrapolates to self-cosine 1.005 (95% CI [0.994, 1.016]), confirming it is pure draw noise.
- **Head-to-head scoring of both accounts on one measured object.** Builds the debiased (route, σ) map and discovers a low-excess window on 1024→768 (σ ∈ (0.65, 0.95)) that no spectral criterion predicts at any tolerance. The spectral family errs at RMSE 0.147 (768) / 0.355 (512), while the proposed account predicts held-out routes at ~0.07–0.09 RMSE. Two causal probes cleanly divide the governors: ratio sets the amplitude (iso-severity 1280→1120 reproduces 1024→896 bin-for-bin), absolute target size sets the floor (1024→768 and 896→768 share the ~2,160-token grid and land on the same floor despite different ratios).
- **Selective low-resolution LoRA trainer with weight-space validation.** An opt-in σ-gated path (σ drawn first; if σ > 0.5 and image is on-route, the cached demoted latent replaces the native one; plus σ-gated banded RoPE alignment à la YaRN/Zhao et al. 2026), specified in 3 lines inside a standard flow-matching step (Table 2), delivers −14.6% training time at fixed 480 steps and a weight-space endpoint cosine of 0.753/0.770 vs. the matched-seed native twin, with the stacked 1024→768 window essentially unperturbed (0.753/0.771).

## Methodology

**Estimand.** A flow-matching DiT regresses constant velocity `v = ε − x` from `z_σ = (1−σ)x + σε`; the per-image, per-bin population "demotion distance" is `d_{e}(σ) = 1 − cos(ḡ_src(σ), ḡ_dem(σ))`, reported as the **re-encoding excess** `gap_e(σ) = d_e(σ) − d_reenc(σ)` so that the VAE round trip is subtracted out.

**Two accounts in the same units.**
1. *Spectral account* (the inference-side literature's working model, sharpest form = SPD, Xiao et al. 2026): demotion is an ideal low-pass at the coarse Nyquist frequency ω_e = ½·e/e₀; the gap is the destroyed-band contribution, hard-gated at the band's tolerance-parameterized activation time t_ω. By construction it is (a) totally ordered by one tolerance, (b) ratio-only (blind to absolute size), (c) bias-free (vanishes above the crossover).
2. *Gradient-perturbation account* (this paper): write `δg = ġ_dem − ġ_src = J̄⊤Δr̄(σ) + ΔJ̄⊤r̄ + …` (data branch B_e, graph branch C_e, remainder R_e), project orthogonally to ġ_src via an exact angular link `d_e = 1 − 1/√(1+κ_eff²)`, and reduce under four tested assumptions to `gap_e(σ) ≈ ½a²_e·‖Δr̄(σ)‖²/‖ġ_src(σ)‖ + RoPE_e + Resid_e` — a route amplitude a_e on a universal mismatch curve plus a per-route constant floor.

**Instrument.** All measurements on Anima (2B-param DiT, 28 blocks, 3D RoPE, Qwen-Image VAE) with a plain LoRA adapter fine-tuned on an illustration corpus, bucketed into tiers e ∈ {512, 768, 896, 1024, 1280}. Verdict grid: N=40 images, D=12 stratified noise draws per arm per bin, 14 bins in (0,1) plus a σ=1 endpoint "pure-ε" mode and an x-zero companion mode. Arms: redraw null, reenc control, one demote arm per target edge, plus per-arm self-floors.

**Debiasing.** Finite-draw attenuation is arm-dependent (per-draw gradient variance grows as token count falls), inflates every squared norm, and does not cancel in the excess; corrected via `ĉ = cos(g_src, g_a) / √(cos_self,src · cos_self,a)` (Spearman, 1904) plus `c/D` extrapolation. Pre-registered strict margin ε = 0.02; bin-level verdict resolution ε* = 0.02–0.07 (≈0.01–0.02 at endpoint sweeps); safety = simultaneous (Bonferroni) one-sided non-inferiority UB95(gap) < ε.

**Causal probes.** σ=1 endpoint mode (kills input-mediated data term) and x-zero mode (kills target-mediated survivor) isolate the floor; a target-strength sweep `ε − αx` shows the survivor lands parallel to ġ_src and is invisible to the angular estimand; positional-interpolation of fractional rotary coordinates erases the 768 endpoint floor and removes ~30% of the 512 floor, isolating RoPE_e from Resid_e; an iso-severity synthetic route 1280→1120 (ratio matched to 1024→896 at 1.6× absolute size) and a tier-transfer route 896→768 separate ratio (amplitude) from absolute size (floor).

**Trainer.** σ-first draw from unchanged density; if σ > σ*=0.5 and image is on-route, replace cached native latent with demoted-grid counterpart; apply σ-gated YaRN-style banded RoPE alignment on demoted forwards only (full PI stretch for low-rotation bands, native spacing for high-frequency bands, sigmoid-scheduled ramp). 3 seeds × 2 single-artist corpora (60 / 15 images) × 480 steps with deterministic kernels (incl. FlashAttention deterministic backward) and paired RNG.

## Results & Highlights

- **Account head-to-head, held-out RMSE:** the gradient-perturbation account predicts held-out routes at **~0.07–0.09 RMSE** vs. the spectral family's **0.147–0.355 RMSE** (0.147 at the 768 route, 0.355 at the 512 route); committed-region (above-crossover) RMSE 0.027 / 0.049 / 0.218 for 896/768/512 shows the spectral floor still does not vanish where it predicts zero.
- **Measured (route, σ) safety windows:** 1024→896 (floor 0.02–0.04) is **safe on σ ∈ (0.5, 0.94] at margin 0.09** (simultaneous over 7 window bins); 1024→768 (floor 0.06–0.09) is unsafe at the strict margin but retains a narrow **low-excess window σ ∈ (0.65, 0.95)** with paired excess +0.02–0.04 per bin (pointwise 0.072), which the two-term reduction does *not* predict and which a vector-resolved probe traces to negative data–graph interference; any route to 512 (floor ≈ 0.30) is unsafe in all 15 bins. Debiasing flipped roughly half the 768 route's high-σ plateau from "unsafe" to the narrow window.
- **Two governors cleanly separated:** absolute target size, not ratio, sets the floor — 1024→768 and 896→768 share the ~2,160-token target grid and land on the same floor despite different ratios, while near-equal-ratio 896→768 gives a high-σ residual ~2× the 1024→896 plateau. Ratio, not absolute size, sets the amplitude — synthetic iso-severity 1280→1120 (ratio exactly matched to 1024→896 at 1.6× its absolute target size) reproduces the 1024→896 curve bin-for-bin, crossover included.
- **Floor existence at σ=1:** debiased draw-limit endpoint floors +0.019 / +0.056 / +0.304 for 896/768/512; the 512 value clears the pre-registered bar `gap∞ ≥ 0.15` on **12 of 12 probe images individually**. Positional-interpolation of fractional rotary coordinates erases the 768 endpoint floor and removes ~30% of the 512 floor; Resid768 ≈ 0 after re-anchoring, leaving a genuine non-positional Resid512 ≈ 0.2 bulk.
- **Debiasing validation:** first-order variance predicts spurious iso-direction endpoint gaps of ≈ 0.02/0.05/0.15 for 896/768/512; the uncorrected 1024→896 endpoint gap indeed decays as +0.100/+0.035/−0.016 at D=4/8/16 (clean c/D); debiased fits come out D-flat (|c| ≤ 0.05 on the 512 route, vs. c ≈ +0.29 uncorrected); native redraw floor extrapolates to self-cosine 1.005 (bootstrap 95% CI [0.994, 1.016]).
- **Training-time savings at fixed 480 steps (Anima, two single-artist corpora):** always-gated (σ>0.5) arm realizes **−14.6% training time** and −15.1% forward-compute footprint. Weight-space endpoint cos(ΔW_arm, ΔW_native) at matched seed: native same-seed non-deterministic retrain 0.264 / 0.427 (corpus A/B, the kernel-nondeterminism floor); demoting at every σ falls below it (0.183/0.236); gated σ>0.5 demotion matches/exceeds it (0.365/0.432); **late-scheduled demotion reaches 0.753/0.770**; stacking the 1024→768 window leaves it essentially unchanged (0.753/0.771).
- **Banded RoPE alignment is in-window on-manifold:** passes both pre-registered legs, erasing the static variant's low-σ liability (+0.064 → +0.033 ± 0.025) while preserving its high-σ gains (paired −0.05 vs plain demotion at σ ≥ 0.59).

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** High and sharply scoped. The contribution is not "train at lower resolution" — that idea is well-trodden (progressive growing Karras 2018, PixArt-α Chen 2024, Simple Diffusion Hoogeboom 2023, pyramidal flow matching Jin 2024) — but a *gradient-level* account of when a downscaled step is a faithful substitute under an unchanged objective. The decomposition of `g = Jᵀr` into a ratio-governed data branch and a token-count-governed floor, and the observation that the floor's RoPE component can be erased by fractional positional interpolation while a non-positional Resid bulk remains, are genuinely new reframings.
- **Rigor:** Strong. Pre-registered bars, a measurement-instrument debiasing that the paper itself admits was motivated by catching its own earlier failure (split-half reliability indistinguishable from zero for per-image rankings), two mutually checking debiasing methods (Spearman self-cosines and c/D extrapolation), and Bonferroni-corrected simultaneous non-inferiority verdicts. The honest disclosure that the spectral account's *ratio* governor is correct for the data term — only its *scope* is wrong (mistaking one term for the whole gap) — is a sign of intellectual honesty rather than strawmanning.
- **Reproducibility:** Mixed-to-good. Code is public; deterministic kernels and paired RNG make the weight-space reads exact (identical retrain reproduces its twin at 1.000). However, the probe grid is small (N=40 images, D=12 draws per bin) on a single model family (Anima) with a single native-tier-trained adapter; the iso-severity, tier-transfer, depth-split and positional-intervention probes remain *pre-debiasing* reads and enter the held-out validation on that basis, which is a real gap between the strength of the central claims and the strength of the evidence for them.
- **Significance:** If the two-term reduction generalizes beyond Anima, it gives the field a cheap, general pre-test that any "train part of the time at lower resolution" proposal should pass — explicitly framed as such in the conclusion. The −14.6% training-time saving at fixed steps is real but modest, and is demonstrated only at LoRA-fine-tuning scale (480 steps, 60/15 images), not at pretraining scale or with full-weight training.
- **Red flags / specific weaknesses:**
  - **One-model, one-operating-point.** All gradient probes ran on one DiT (Anima) with one adapter checkpoint trained at native tiers; how far the map generalizes to other DiT families (PixArt-Σ, SD3, FLUX), to full-weight training, or to mixed-resolution-trained adapters is unmeasured.
  - **The reduction's domain excludes the very window the trainer stacks.** The two-term reduction does *not* predict the 1024→768 mid-σ low-excess window (σ ∈ (0.65, 0.95)) that the trainer stacks as a second route; the mechanism is traced post-hoc to negative data–graph interference. The trainer is therefore licensed by the *measurement*, not by the *model*.
  - **The A(ratio) governor rests on only two distinct ratio values.** The iso-severity 1280→1120 probe matches one ratio (1024→896's); the ratio-axis coverage is thin.
  - **Estimand-specific floor reading.** The target-mediated perturbation is real at the vector level but lands parallel to the native gradient, so an un-normalized optimizer (e.g., AdamW with magnitude-sensitive updates) could apportion the endpoint gap differently than the angular cosine read does — a caveat the paper itself raises in §6.
  - **Underpowered upper tail.** The two non-endpoint bins of the safe route above σ=0.94 resolve only margins above ≈ 0.05, and the σ=1 endpoint carries a small real gap (+0.034 ± 0.011); the safe condition is therefore stated as σ ∈ (0.5, 0.94], not (0.5, 1].

## Strengths

- A genuine *measurement instrument* (debiased cosine estimator with two cross-checks) for a question the field had previously answered only by looking at final samples through a full training/sampling pipeline.
- Clean causal separation of two governors (ratio vs. absolute token count) via purpose-built probes (iso-severity synthetic route, tier-transfer route, fractional positional interpolation), rather than correlational fits.
- Honest, non-strawman treatment of the competing spectral account: explicitly states what survives of it (ratio is the right governor for the data term) and where its scope is wrong.
- A trained, deployable artifact (the opt-in σ-conditional LoRA trainer) whose pseudocode fits in 9 lines and whose only on-route change is 3 lines, validated in weight space with deterministic kernels rather than only by sample quality.
- Pre-registered bars, simultaneous Bonferroni-corrected verdicts, and explicit reporting of where the instrument is underpowered.

## Limitations

- Single model family (Anima 2B), single adapter checkpoint trained at native tiers, single illustration corpus; generalization to other DiTs, to pretraining, or to mixed-resolution-trained adapters is the designated-but-unrun bound.
- The two-term reduction the paper sells does *not* predict the 1024→768 mid-σ low-excess window the trainer stacks as a second route; that window's mechanism (negative data–graph interference) is traced but not predicted, so the trainer's second route is licensed by measurement, not by theory.
- The A(ratio) governor for the data term rests on only two distinct ratio values; the floor law's functional form is unidentified at the operating points tested.
- Several probes (iso-severity 1280, tier-transfer 896→768, depth-split, positional-interpolation intervention) remain pre-debiasing reads and enter held-out validation on that basis.
- Training-scale validation is LoRA-only at 480 steps on 60/15 images; no pretraining-scale or full-weight-training experiment.
- The angular estimand is blind to a parallel rescaling of the gradient, so an un-normalized optimizer could see a different endpoint gap than the cosine read reports.
- The safe route's upper tail (σ ∈ (0.94, 1]) is underpowered and carries a small real gap (+0.034 ± 0.011 at the σ=1 endpoint); the trainer's one-sided gate σ > 0.5 lets demoted steps land in this region anyway.

## Relevance

这篇论文对"高效扩散模型训练 / DiT 加速"方向高度相关：它给出了一个梯度层级的判据，告诉我们何时可以把高噪声步的潜变量下采样以节省训练算力而几乎不改变 LoRA 梯度方向，并提供了一个可直接接入现有 flow-matching 训练器的 3 行 opt-in 实现。对研究 DiT 训练效率、分辨率课程学习、低秩适配或位置编码外推的工作都有方法论和实证参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the two-term reduction (ratio-governed data term + token-count-governed floor) transfer to other DiT families (PixArt-Σ, SD3, FLUX) and to full-weight pretraining rather than LoRA fine-tuning, where the Jacobian `J` and its token-count dependence may differ?
- The 1024→768 low-excess window is measured but *not* predicted by the reduction (mechanism = negative data–graph interference). Can the reduction be extended to predict such negative-interference windows a priori, so the trainer's second route is theory-licensed rather than measurement-licensed?
- The angular cosine estimand is blind to parallel gradient rescaling. How does the demotion gap read change under an un-normalized optimizer (AdamW with magnitude-sensitive updates), and does the safe window shift?
- The floor law's functional form is unidentified at the tested operating points. Could a sweep over many more target-token counts (not just ~3,000 / ~2,160 / ~1,000) identify whether the floor scales as a power law, logarithm, or step function of token count?
- The banded RoPE alignment erases the 768 endpoint floor but only ~30% of the 512 floor. Can a more aggressive frequency-selective scheme (e.g., per-block learned interpolation, or NTK-aware YaRN variants) push the 512 route into a usable window?
- The trainer is validated at 480 LoRA steps on 60/15 images. At production scale (millions of images, hundreds of thousands of steps), does the −14.6% wall-clock saving hold, and does the weight-space endpoint cosine to the native retrain degrade?
- The map is built from per-step gradients. Does integration over a full optimizer trajectory preserve the safe-window verdict, or does a small per-step bias compound into a meaningful trajectory drift?
