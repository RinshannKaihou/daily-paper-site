02 Why this matters

For multi-agent and LLM-as-judge pipelines, the “social packaging” around a peer answer is itself a source of quality loss — and no single judge can be trusted as a neutral default.

03 Critical edge
bundled residual

The peer-presentation residual bundles attribution, authority labels, consensus structure, and prompt length — so the paper cannot say which feature drives the harm, and leaves it open.

§ 01

Methodology

Branched Round-2 · Four-Channel Decomposition

Four open-weight generators (Qwen2.5-7B, Mistral-7B, Gemma-2-9B, Llama-3.1-8B) — which also serve as the four judges — answer 500 questions each from TruthfulQA, MMLU-Pro, and ARC-Challenge under vLLM greedy decoding.

R1
Round-1 baseline aq — same realized answer branches into all arms.
R2
Seven arms from one baseline: no-peer re-answer · 3 content-only (correct/wrong/mixed) · 3 peer-presented (6-speaker attributed block, authority ±).
∆
Decomposition: total revision shift is split into ordinary re-answering, candidate-content exposure, and a bundled peer-presentation residual.
γ
Paired blind / informed judging on the identical generated answer isolates evaluator-side directional sensitivity γj from generator-side revision.
// estimands ∆sp = Q(no-peer) − Q(Round 1) ∆content,k = Q(content,k) − Q(no-peer) ∆pp,c = Q(peer,c) − Q(content,k(c)) ∆total,c = ∆sp + ∆content + ∆pp

Latent quality is fit with a hierarchical ordinal model (θi = bt + riηc + σθεi) with correct/incorrect anchors pinned at ±1.5 to fix origin and unit. NUTS, 4 chains, 1500 warmup + 1500 sampling, target_accept 0.95; R̂ ≤ 1.007, zero divergences, min bulk ESS 715.

§ 02

Strengths & Limitations

▲ Strengths

  • First clean separation of evaluator bias from generator conformity via paired design on identical answers.
  • Honest about greedy decoding, anchor identification failure, and the bundled presentation residual.
  • External validation with fixed non-generative classifiers (RoBERTa-MNLI, BERTScore, TruthfulQA truth-judge) reproduces the condition ordering.
  • Per-judge γ with posterior probabilities lets practitioners pick near-neutral judges (γ≈0) or model sensitivity.

▼ Limitations

  • No observable ground truth for open-ended quality; the latent scale depends on the measurement model.
  • 7–9B open-weight generators only; frontier models as revisers (GPT/Claude-class) are untested.
  • Bundled residual: attribution vs. authority vs. consensus vs. length cannot yet be isolated.
  • Judge–generator overlap is a structural confound the design cannot fully remove (self-exclusion refits preserve ordering).
  • Cell-average pattern: mixed is sometimes worse than all-correct per-generator, so “all-wrong worst” is not a per-item law.

§ 03

Open Questions

Q1 · Unbundle
Which component of the peer-presentation residual — attribution, authority, consensus, or length — actually carries the negative effect?
Q2 · Scale
Do frontier generators (GPT / Claude-class) show the same all-wrong-worst pattern, or does scale attenuate conformity?
Q3 · Panel
Would a human + LLM mixed panel change the γj distribution — and can per-judge sensitivity be predicted a priori?
Q4 · Generalize
Can the paired blind / informed protocol audit evaluator reactions to retrieval, rubric exemplars, or chain-of-thought scaffolding?
