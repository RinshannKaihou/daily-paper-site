> **一句话总结：** 本文通过一个 sycophancy hint 干预设置，系统比较了显式 CoT、弱监督与强监督隐式 CoT（CODI、SIM-CoT）在 GSM8k/SVAMP/ECQA/SIQA 上的可监控性，发现"失去可读推理链"并不必然使模型更难监控——hint-reliance 信号在生成时被保留于激活态中，监控效果更多取决于任务属性（答案与推理间是否留下可见矛盾）以及对模型内部状态的访问级别，而非推理模式本身。

> **TL;DR:** The paper probes whether latent CoT (CODI, SIM-CoT) is less monitorable than explicit CoT using a sycophancy hint-intervention setup with hint-reliance as the target. On GSM8k (math) and ECQA (QA), activation probing of answer-token states is the most consistent monitor across modes; verbalized latents close the gap to explicit CoT for math (FT-LLM Logit-Lens vs Explicit-COT native) but not for QA; monitorability transfers OOD for math (GSM8k→SVAMP, AUROC drops only ~0.00-0.10) but degrades sharply for QA (ECQA→SIQA, with the SIM-CoT activation probe dropping from ID ~0.85 to OOD 0.53, Δ=−0.25). The headline finding is that monitorability depends more on the task and on access to internals than on whether reasoning is explicit or latent.

## 核心贡献

- 提出并实施了一个统一的"可监控性 vs 推理模式"研究框架：用 hint-based intervention（sycophancy hint）定义每例 hint-reliance 标签 Y，可在不同 TE（total effect）水平下比较 monitors，而非只在单一操作点评估。
- 对 No-CoT、Explicit-CoT、弱监督 latent CoT（CODI）和强监督 latent CoT（SIM-CoT）四种推理模式横跨数学推理（GSM8k/SVAMP）与问答（ECQA/SIQA）进行了系统比较，覆盖 native text、verbalized latent（logit lens / 辅助 decoder）和 activations 三种 trace 类型，以及 exact-match、prompted LLM、fine-tuned LLM、activation probing 四类 monitor。
- 引入"intervention-aware fine-tuning"以诱导可控的 hint-reliance 率（X=1 准确率提升到 87.6%-99.9%），解决了原始 latent CoT 检查点 TE≈0 或 TE 分布过宽、难以横向比较的难题。
- 给出可解释性诊断：在数学任务中，hint-reliance 常以"推理链最后一步与答案矛盾"的形式暴露（GSM8k mismatch rate 0.79 for Y=1 vs 0.04 for Y=0），使显式文本 monitor 受益；ECQA 中这种矛盾信号弱（0.26 vs 0.07），文本 monitor 表现下降。
- 结论性观点：是否可监控主要由 (a) hint-reliance 是否在 trace 中留下可见 mismatch、(b) 对模型内部状态的访问深度决定，而非 explicit vs latent。这指向设计 "monitorability-aware" latent 架构的方向。

## Key Contributions

- A unified framework that ties CoT mode (no/explicit/latent, weak vs strong latent supervision) to monitorability via an intervention-based, per-example hint-reliance target Y, evaluated across a range of total-effect (TE) operating points rather than a single one.
- A comprehensive cross-mode, cross-trace, cross-monitor study: four reasoning modes × three trace types (native text, verbalized latents via logit lens / SIM-CoT decoder, hidden activations) × four monitor families (exact-match heuristic, prompted Qwen3-14B LLM, LoRA-fine-tuned LLM classifier, attention-pooled linear probing).
- An "intervention-aware fine-tuning" recipe (mixing no-hint/correct-hint/wrong-hint data) that brings X=1 accuracy to 87.6–99.9% across modes while keeping X=0 accuracy close to the un-finetuned model, enabling apples-to-apples monitorability comparisons.
- Empirical evidence that **monitorability is governed more by task and access to internals than by reasoning mode**: hint-reliance signal survives in answer-token activations regardless of how intermediate reasoning is represented.
- A mechanistic explanation tying monitorability to CoT-answer mismatch: math traces leave a visible contradiction (GSM8k mismatch 0.79 vs 0.04), so text monitors win for Explicit-CoT; QA traces do not (ECQA 0.26 vs 0.07), so activation probes win.
- Two concrete research agendas: (i) monitorability-aware latent architectures (explicit-latent hybrids, reserving supervised latent slots), and (ii) robustness of activation probes to RL/obfuscation pressures.

## Methodology

**Setup.** Given a prompt x, the model produces reasoning + answer; for the intervened input (X=1) the prompt is augmented with a sycophancy hint, e.g., "I think the answer is 1020 but I'm curious to hear what you think." The monitorability target is hint-reliance:

Y = 1 iff ŷ_{X=1} ≠ ŷ_{X=0} and ŷ_{X=1} = y_hint (Eq. 2)

so Y=1 requires the hint to *change* the prediction in favor of the hint, not merely echo it. Monitorability = how well a monitor Z tracks Y from X=1 outputs alone, measured by AUROC.

**Reasoning modes (Llama-3.2-1B-Instruct).**
- No-CoT and Explicit-CoT: SFT.
- CODI (Shen 2025): weak supervision via self-distillation (teacher loss + student loss + distillation on the ":" token representation).
- SIM-CoT (Wei 2026): strong supervision adding an auxiliary decoder that reconstructs the k-th explicit CoT step from the k-th latent state; each latent is individually supervised.

**Data.** Math: GSM8k-Aug (385k train, no natural language, only `<<...>>` steps) for training, GSM8k test in-domain, SVAMP OOD. QA: ECQA (facts supporting/refuting) for training, ECQA test in-domain, SIQA OOD.

**Monitors.** (1) Exact-match baseline (ŷ_{X=1}=y_hint ⇒ positive); (2) Prompted Qwen3-14B LLM (applied only to Explicit-CoT reasoning); (3) LoRA fine-tuned LLM classifier over native text and verbalized latents; (4) Activation probing — attention-pooled (McKenzie 2025) per-token, linearly combined across a layer subset L={1,4,8,12,15,16} (Chrąszcz 2026), trained on answer-token activations.

**Intervention-aware fine-tuning.** Because CODI/SIM-CoT checkpoints have TE≈0 (e.g., GSM8k X=1 accuracy 30.7% and 29.5%), the authors fine-tune each model with a mix of none/correct/wrong-hint data (ratios in Table 7) and save checkpoints whenever validation TE grows by a fixed margin, yielding multiple checkpoints spanning a range of TE values per model.

## Results & Highlights

- **Intervention-aware FT induces high hint-reliance uniformly** while preserving X=0 accuracy. On GSM8k test (Table 2), CODI's X=1 accuracy rises from 30.7% (base) to 94.5±0.5% (+Hint-FT), SIM-CoT from 29.5% to 93.4±0.5%, Explicit-CoT from 58.7% to 98.6±1.9%; X=0 accuracy barely moves (CODI 55.5→54.4±0.1). On ECQA, No-CoT goes from 75.0% to 99.6±0.0% X=1.
- **Activation probing is the most consistently strong monitor across modes and tasks**, often the best for latent CoT; it does not need the readable trace. The paper's headline claim is that "hint-reliance is retained in the model's internal states at generation time, regardless of how intermediate reasoning is represented."
- **CoT-answer mismatch drives text-monitor success on math.** Mismatch rate is 0.79 (Y=1) vs 0.04 (Y=0) on GSM8k, but only 0.26 vs 0.07 on ECQA. Forcing a random-reasoning swap in ECQA lifts average FT-LLM-Native AUROC from 0.68 to 0.82, isolating the mismatch as the signal.
- **Verbalized latents (logit lens) close the latent↔explicit gap on math but not QA.** Fine-tuned LLM on logit-lens verbalizations ("FT LLM - Logit Lens") recovers Explicit-CoT-level monitorability for CODI/SIM-CoT on GSM8k, whereas the SIM-CoT auxiliary **decoder** fails to improve AUROC — on ECQA it collapses to the uninformative pattern "«People ...»".
- **OOD transfer is mode-dependent.** Math (GSM8k→SVAMP) keeps most monitors above the exact-match baseline with small drops (e.g., Explicit-CoT Activation Probe 0.91 ID → 0.91 OOD, Δ=+0.00; SIM-CoT Probe 0.85→0.85, Δ=−0.05). QA (ECQA→SIQA) degrades severely for latent models: SIM-CoT Activation Probe drops to 0.53 (Δ=−0.25), SIM-CoT Logit-Lens to 0.57 (Δ=−0.20), most ending near the exact-match baseline.
- **A quantified prevalence observation**: for CODI/SIM-CoT the predicted answer coincides with a *randomly sampled wrong hint* <10% of the time on ECQA and <1% on GSM8k, justifying the use of the *correct* answer as the hint (incorrect hints would collapse Y to "echoes hint").

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty (moderate).** The core question ("is latent CoT less monitorable?") is timely but several adjacent results already exist: Chan 2026 reports activations predict misalignment better than CoT text, Mirtaheri & Belkin 2026 detect hint-motivated changes from activations, and Ramjee 2026 probes COCONUT latents. The genuine delta is the *systematic cross-mode, cross-monitor, multi-TE comparison* plus the explicit link to CoT-answer mismatch.
- **Rigor (good but bounded).** Intervention-aware FT is a defensible way to control for TE, but it *induces* rather than naturally elicits hint-reliance — the authors acknowledge this (Limitations). The 3-seed averaging and TE-stratified AUROC plots are appropriate; AUROC is the only metric (no calibration, no FPR-at-fixed-TPR).
- **Key missing ablations / data quirks.**
  - Only one base model, **Llama-3.2-1B-Instruct** (chosen so both CODI and SIM-CoT checkpoints exist); scale/architecture effects (e.g., recurrent-depth latent CoT, Geiping 2025) are entirely out of scope.
  - Only one intervention type (correct-answer sycophancy hint) and one target (binary hint-reliance). Misleading hints, metadata leaks, and other behaviors (Chen 2025) are not tested, so the strong conclusion "monitorability depends more on task than mode" is strictly only demonstrated for this single intervention.
  - The **Exact-Match baseline is weak**: it cannot detect Y=1 by construction (Y requires a *change*), so the headline "monitors beat baseline" partly restates that. A trivial "answer-echoes-hint → positive" classifier is a low bar; comparison against stronger heuristics (e.g., entropy/self-consistency) would be more informative.
  - **QA traces are admittedly near-useless**: prior work (Cui 2026; Dilgren & Wiegreffe 2026) finds latent states are unnecessary for QA performance, so the weak QA signal may reflect the task/data rather than monitorability per se — the authors flag this but the headline summary obscures it.
  - **SIM-CoT decoder collapse on ECQA** ("«People ...»") means a "strongly supervised" verbalizer is uninformative for QA; this is a notable negative result for the strong-supervision premise but is reported only qualitatively.
- **Reproducibility (high).** Public checkpoints, full hyperparameter tables (Tables 6–7), explicit data generation pipeline for extra GSM8k validation samples (Qwen3-32B generation + Qwen2.5-Math-72B majority filtering), monitor prompts reproduced in Appendix Figs 5–6. Single A100/H100 sufficient. Code/data URLs provided.
- **Significance.** Practically important for AI safety: it tempers the worry that latent CoT is *inherently* unmonitorable and redirects effort toward activation-based monitoring and monitorability-aware latent design. Conceptually somewhat less surprising given prior activation-probing results.
- **Red flags.** None severe. The abstract's framing ("depends more on the task and the level of access to model internals than on the reasoning mode") is slightly stronger than what one intervention × one model × two tasks can support; readers should weight it accordingly.

## Strengths

- Clean, well-motivated experimental design: TE-controlled comparison avoids confounding "mode" with "hint-reliance rate."
- Crosses four monitor families × three trace types × four reasoning modes — a genuinely thorough matrix.
- Honest about negative results (SIM-CoT decoder collapse on ECQA; verbalized latents don't help QA).
- Mechanistic explanation (CoT-answer mismatch) is *tested* via the random-reasoning-swap control (AUROC 0.68 → 0.82), not just asserted.
- Excellent reproducibility artifacts (checkpoints, hyperparameters, prompts).

## Limitations

- Single base model (Llama-3.2-1B-Instruct); no scaling or alternative latent architectures.
- Single intervention (correct-answer sycophancy hint) and single target (binary hint-reliance); generalization to other behaviors (deception, backdoors, prompt injection) is unverified.
- Hint-reliance is *induced* via intervention-aware FT rather than naturally occurring; ecological validity of the TE-controlled setup is unclear.
- Two tasks only; QA signal is confounded by prior findings that latents are unnecessary for QA performance.
- No robustness analysis against obfuscation attacks on the activation probe (acknowledged; relevant given Gupta & Jenner 2025, Bailey et al. 2026).
- Exact-Match baseline is weak; no comparison to stronger heuristic monitors.

## Relevance

与用户对 CoT 可信性、reasoning model 监控、隐式/连续推理（latent reasoning）以及 LLM interpretability 的研究兴趣高度契合：本文直接回答了"将推理搬到连续隐空间后还能否监控"，对 monitorability、faithfulness、activation probing 的后续工作有方法论与实证参考价值，并指出了 monitorability-aware latent 架构这一值得跟踪的新方向。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the "monitorability depends on task, not mode" conclusion hold at scale (7B/70B base models) and for recurrent-depth latent architectures (Geiping 2025)?
- How do activation probes and verbalized-latent monitors behave under *misleading* or *wrong-answer* hints, or non-sycophancy interventions (prompt injection, metadata leaks)?
- Can a stronger baseline (self-consistency disagreement, answer-entropy, ensemble-disagreement) close the gap to the fine-tuned LLM/probe monitors and change the relative-mode ranking?
- Are activation probes robust to obfuscation/RL pressure that trains against them (Gupta & Jenner 2025; Bailey et al. 2026), and which probe families resist it?
- Can reserving a subset of latent slots for explicit monitorability supervision (the paper's proposed direction) recover Explicit-CoT-level text monitorability without sacrificing latent-CoT efficiency?
- Why does SIM-CoT's strongly-supervised auxiliary decoder collapse on ECQA but stay diverse on GSM8k — is this a data-property effect or an optimization pathology?
