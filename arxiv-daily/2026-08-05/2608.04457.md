> **一句话总结：** Eigenius 提出一个开源的强类型知识图谱 DBMS,将依赖类型理论(EigenTT)、Grothendieck institutions 与内容寻址存储统一在一个内核中,使"科学审计链"成为数据模型的结构不变量;在对 Chan 等 Nature 论文(WRN 解旋酶合成致死)的端到端重算中,52 条结论全部从哈希固定的源数据中复现成立,并暴露出原文与自有数据之间的 4 处差异(含样本量 n=54→51 与相差 13 个数量级的伪重复 p 值)。

> **TL;DR:** Eigenius is an open-source typed knowledge-graph DBMS that owns its type system, storage, and integration protocol in one kernel, turning scientific audit (provenance + epistemic grade) into a commit-time invariant; in an end-to-end recomputation of Chan et al.'s WRN-helicase Nature study, all 52 derived conclusions hold from hash-pinned data while four discrepancies surface (including a corrected sample size of n=54→51 and a pseudoreplication whose honest p-value differs by 13 orders of magnitude), at a typed-ingestion throughput of ~5,200 validated resources/s on one core of a 22-CPU laptop on a 9.19M-resource WordNet+UMLS load.

## 核心贡献

- **统一类型化数据模型 (C1):** 用依赖类型理论 EigenTT(CIC 族,基于 Mini-TT)将"经验科学的证成逻辑(justification logic,非全 factive)与形式数学证明(Lean 4)"整合为同一资源;认知分层 Declared / Observed / Derived / Verified 在 commit 时作为强制不变量,VerifiedResource 形如依赖对 (T, t)。
- **Institutions 作为强类型存储过程的推广 (C2):** 把 Goguen–Burstall institutions 引入数据库世界,跨系统翻译(comorphisms)在 commit 时被静态类型检查,其输出以确定性内容哈希 IRI 直接物化回链上,成为一阶、可查询、可验证的图成员。
- **消除 O(N²) polystore 适配器瓶颈 (C3):** 通过把共享的强类型中间表示(如数学负载语言 FormulaTerm)原生存入图 schema,使所有共享该 IR 的 institutions(五个 Julia:Symbolics、IntervalArithmetic、Catalyst、OrdinaryDiffEq、JuMP-HiGHS)之间的 comorphism 退化为恒等变换。
- **端到端实证 (C4):** 对 Chan 等 Nature(WRN 解旋酶在微卫星不稳定癌症中的合成致死)论文做端到端重算与重编码,从内容寻址、哈希固定的源数据出发,52/52 条派生结论成立,并暴露 4 处与原文不符的差异,证明此类复杂论证需要数据库级架构。
- **进程内形式验证:** 用 Rust 写的最小 term checker `nanoda_lib` 直接链接进内核二进制,避免 IPC 开销;Lean 4 仅作为链下创作环境,以不透明 blob 形式携带 proof payload,并在 commit 时做三段式校验(proof validity、mirror correspondence、anchor consistency)。
- **MCP 原生 + 闭式审计链:** 通过 MCP server 把内核操作暴露给自治 agent;每条边都是内容寻址链引用,从 Verdict 回到本体类的环路可仅凭图谱被审计员重新走查与重新校验。

## Key Contributions

- **Unified typed data model (C1):** EigenTT, a dependent type theory in the CIC family (built on Mini-TT), unifies empirical justification logic (deliberately non-factive warrants) with formal mathematical proof (Lean 4); epistemic stratification into Declared / Observed / Derived / Verified is enforced as a strict commit-time invariant, with VerifiedResource modeled as a dependent pair (T, t).
- **Institutions as typed generalization of stored procedures (C2):** Goguen–Burstall institutions are ported into the DBMS world; cross-system translations (comorphisms) are statically type-checked at commit and their outputs are materialized back into the chain at a deterministic content-hash IRI as durable, queryable, first-class resources rather than ephemeral transport intermediates.
- **Collapse of the O(N²) polystore adapter cost (C3):** By lifting a shared strongly typed IR (e.g., the math payload language FormulaTerm) natively into the graph schema, comorphisms between any institutions sharing that IR — here the five Julia institutions Symbolics, IntervalArithmetic, Catalyst, OrdinaryDiffEq, and JuMP-HiGHS — degenerate to identity, eliminating pairwise adapter cost.
- **End-to-end replication of a Nature study (C4):** A full recomputation and re-encoding of Chan et al.'s WRN-helicase synthetic-lethality study from hash-pinned source data discharges 52 of 52 derived conclusions and surfaces four discrepancies versus the original paper, demonstrating the need for a database-grade architecture for complex scientific arguments.
- **In-process formal verification without IPC:** Lean 4 is used strictly off-chain as an authoring environment; the kernel links a minimal Rust term checker (`nanoda_lib`) directly into the database binary, with a three-part commit-time check (proof validity, EigeniusFFI mirror correspondence, anchor hash consistency) gating promotion to VerifiedResource.
- **Agent-native MCP substrate with closed audit chain:** An MCP server exposes kernel ops to autonomous agents; every edge is a content-addressed chain reference, so an auditor can re-walk and re-check the cycle from a Verdict::Holds back to the chain class using the graph alone.

## Methodology

Eigenius is a Rust prototype built around RocksDB key-value storage, a gRPC API, a React notebook, and an MCP server exposing kernel operations to autonomous agents. The architecture rests on three tightly-coupled pillars.

**Typed data model.** Every entity is a Resource identified by IRI with class-membership claims and typed properties, extending Atomic Data with dependent types. The unit of commit is a layer — an immutable resource set with a parent pointer — whose identity is the SHA-256 of canonical CBOR, forming a Merkle commitment over graph history. Branches and merges form a layer lattice whose joins are typed merges (Witness, Rename, SchemaQuotient, Restructure), treated as functorial data migrations. Epistemic status is computed from the provenance graph: Declared (authority only), Observed (external provenance recorded), Derived (produced by a typed computation, e.g., a statistics institution), and Verified (carries a Lean proof term re-checked at commit).

**Institutions and comorphisms.** External reasoners (ODE solvers, theorem provers, statistical packages) are integrated via Goguen–Burstall institutions — strongly typed generalizations of stored procedures for knowledge graphs. An institution registers ExportFormats, ImportFormats, and Comorphisms as ontology resources, exposing three trait methods: `extract_typed`, `reify`, and `query`. A comorphism pipeline extracts a payload of type S, applies a transformation m : S → T, and reifies T as a target resource. Because this runs inside the kernel, the engine statically type-checks at commit that m's signature matches payload(export) → payload(import); type-incorrect translations are rejected like schema violations. Outputs are reinserted into the chain at a deterministic content-hash IRI, so identical translations deduplicate via content addressing. Institutions run either out-of-core in containers (Julia/R) or are linked directly into the kernel binary (Lean 4).

**EigenQL.** A typed stratified Datalog with aggregation, DEFINEd recursion, and two institution-specific clauses: FIBER dispatches execution to an external institution mid-query, and INTO pins the external engine's response back into the chain at a named IRI, lifting it through the commit pipeline.

**Formal verification as a commit invariant.** To enforce VerifiedResource, the kernel represents the proposition T in EigenTT but stores the verbatim Lean-4 proof bytes as an opaque blob. At commit, a minimal Rust term checker (`nanoda_lib`) linked into the binary re-checks the proof, then a three-part check verifies (1) proof validity, (2) mirror correspondence mapping each EigeniusFFI constant to a chain class IRI, and (3) anchor consistency hashing the mirror archive. All three must pass to yield Verdict::Holds.

**Shared IR collapse.** The math payload language FormulaTerm is a typed AST modeled natively in the graph; its operator catalog is a set of chain resources rank-checked at commit. Because all five Julia institutions consume FormulaTerm natively, comorphisms between them are identity.

## Results & Highlights

- **End-to-end replication of Chan et al.'s WRN-helicase Nature study:** On a clean database, **52 of 52 derived conclusions hold** from hash-pinned source data (no baseline number reported because the comparison is qualitative correctness, not a delta — every analysis class in the paper runs live, from a limma differential-dependency call over a **187 MB matrix** to per-cell mechanism assays).
- **Discrepancies surfaced by recomputation:** **four machine-checked discrepancies** between the paper's prose and its own data, including a corrected sample size (n = 54 → 51, rows dropped as missing) and a pseudoreplication whose honest p-value **differs by thirteen orders of magnitude** from the published value.
- **Typed-ingestion microbenchmark on WordNet + UMLS:** the lexicons materialize as **9.19M chain resources at 361 bytes each (6.6M lexical entries, 4.8M surface forms)**, sustaining roughly **5,200 validated resources/s on one core of a 22-CPU laptop** through the standard commit path with no bulk-load bypass.
- **Commit-gate cost decomposition:** structural class layers load at **~28k resources/s**, while lexical entries (which rigorously type-check each sense against its category) load at **~4k resources/s**, locating the cost squarely in the commit gate.
- **Kinase-screening empirical path:** lifts **24 IC50 measurements** to typed reasoning through identity comorphisms across the Julia stack.
- **System surface:** eight institutions run live — five Julia (Symbolics, IntervalArithmetic, Catalyst, OrdinaryDiffEq, JuMP-HiGHS), Lean 4 in-process, plus statistics and reasoning institutions.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** Genuine novelty is in the *synthesis*, not in any single mechanism. Dependent type theory (Mini-TT/Coquand et al. 2009), Grothendieck institutions (Goguen–Burstall 1992), justification logic (Artemov 2008), functorial data migration (Spivak 2012), content-addressed layers (Datomic, Dolt, TerminusDB), and in-process Lean checking (Lean4Lean, nanoda_lib) are all pre-existing. The paper's claim is that owning all of them in one kernel yields structural guarantees a federated stack cannot — but this is argued qualitatively against the 2008 Pharos system rather than demonstrated against any modern polystore or graph DBMS.
- **Rigor:** The single flagship macro-evaluation (Chan et al. WRN) is compelling as an existence proof but is also a single n=1 study; there is no second replication to show the framework generalizes across scientific domains beyond oncology genomics. The microbenchmark measures only ingestion throughput on one lexicon corpus (WordNet/UMLS), with no query-latency, concurrency, or distributed-scale evaluation, and no isolation of the per-institution overhead.
- **Reproducibility:** Open-source Rust prototype at github.com/eigenius/eigenius with named dependencies (RocksDB, Lean 4, nanoda_lib, five Julia packages) is a strong signal. However, the macro-evaluation depends on access to Chan et al.'s underlying data, the licenses for which are not discussed, and the "52 of 52" claim is reported without the per-conclusion chain IRIs or a machine-checkable artifact pointer for the discrepancies.
- **Significance:** If the AI-Scientist-over-MCP thesis is correct, a kernel that makes audit a structural invariant is plausibly load-bearing. But the paper does not connect to any concrete AI-Scientist system (OpenAI Rosalind, Claude Science are named only as motivation), and the agent loop is explicitly future work — the present contribution is the database, not the closed agent loop.
- **Red flags and weak spots:**
  - The four "discrepancies" — sample size n=54→51 and a pseudoreplication with a p-value off by 13 orders of magnitude — are striking, but the paper presents them as architectural vindication without acknowledging that any competent reanalysis script (or a static data-versioning tool like Dolt) would also surface a row-count mismatch; the architectural-necessity claim is under-argued relative to a simpler provenance-vocabulary or SHACL baseline.
  - Throughput of ~5,200 validated resources/s on one core of a 22-CPU laptop is modest for a DBMS and the paper does not report scaling behavior across the other 21 cores or under concurrent commits.
  - Foundational soundness is explicitly unproven: "proving that a typed merge over the layer lattice preserves well-typedness is our central foundational target" — i.e., the core merge invariant the architecture leans on is currently asserted, not theorem-proved. The validator and comorphism type-checker are themselves trusted (not mechanized), so the trust root is the Rust kernel, not the type theory.
  - Kernel/orchestrator boundary is described as "in flux" and "ongoing technical debt," and the originally-Wasm design was abandoned — signs that the integration story is still moving.

## Strengths

- Architecturally coherent: the unification of justification logic (empirical) and Lean proof checking (formal) under one epistemic lattice is a clean and original framing, and the four-warrant taxonomy (Declared/Observed/Derived/Verified) maps cleanly onto real scientific practice.
- The identity-comorphism collapse for O(N²) polystore cost is an elegant concrete payoff of putting the IR in the schema rather than in middleware.
- Honest self-assessment: the "What we got wrong" section openly records the Wasm-to-container reversal and the unproven merge-soundness target, which is rare and valuable.
- End-to-end replication on a real, high-profile Nature paper — including surfacing concrete, named discrepancies — is far more convincing than a toy benchmark.
- Open-source artifact plus MCP server makes the agent-native claim testable rather than aspirational.

## Limitations

- **Theoretical gaps:** Typed-merge well-typedness preservation over the layer lattice is not proven (stated as the central open foundational target); the validator and comorphism type-checker are trusted rather than mechanized.
- **Single flagship study:** Only Chan et al. WRN is replicated end-to-end; generalization across domains (physics, chemistry, clinical trials) is asserted, not shown.
- **Narrow microbenchmark:** Ingestion-only throughput on WordNet/UMLS; no query latency, no concurrency story, no distributed or multi-node evaluation, no comparison numbers against Dolt, TerminusDB, Datomic, or a SHACL/RDF stack.
- **Agent loop is future work:** The motivating "AI Scientist over MCP" scenario is not demonstrated end-to-end; the paper delivers the kernel but not the closed autonomous-verification loop.
- **Narrative not yet on chain:** The prose argument connecting study steps remains off-chain; the dependently-typed categorial-grammar engine to land it is described as the next step, not delivered.
- **Technical debt:** Kernel/orchestrator boundary in flux; the original Wasm-component design was abandoned in favor of sibling containers, narrowing the sandboxing story.
- **Data access for replication:** Re-running the Nature replication requires access to Chan et al.'s source data and tooling; licensing and accessibility are not discussed.

## Relevance

对于关注 LLM/Agent 驱动科研自动化与可信审计的基础设施研究者,Eigenius 提供了一个把"证明链、溯源与认知分层"做进 DBMS 内核的具体方案,直接对接 MCP 协议,与 AI Scientist、神经符号化 LLM 输出验证等方向高度相关;其依赖类型 + institutions + 内容寻址的组合也适合作为评估 LLM 生成知识可信度的底座参考。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does typed-merge preservation of well-typedness actually hold, and can the merge validator itself be mechanized inside EigenTT rather than trusted as Rust?
- How does Eigenius's commit-time verification throughput scale beyond one core, and what is the per-institution overhead breakdown between the Lean in-process path and the Julia containerized path?
- Against a modern graph DBMS with provenance vocabulary (PROV-O) plus SHACL constraints, how many of the four Chan et al. discrepancies would a simpler, non-typed stack also surface — i.e., how much of the win is the architecture versus simply re-running from pinned data?
- Can the framework replicate a second study in a different domain (e.g., a clinical trial or materials-science pipeline) to test whether the institution/comorphism model generalizes beyond oncology genomics?
- When the categorial-grammar narrative engine lands prose as chain-resident terms, does the controlled-English stage introduce a new trust surface (the parser) that must itself be verified?
- What is the concrete cost model for an AI Scientist operating purely over the MCP endpoints, and does re-walking the audit chain from Verdict back to chain class stay sub-second at 10M+ resources?
