02 Why this matters

A single RSS pass on a frozen ViT replaces an expensive LoRA-depth sweep — V1 neuroscience turned into a reusable engineering prescription.

Relevance: a benchmark for how self-supervised / contrastive / masked / auto-regressive pretraining lay down low-level visual code.
03 Critical edge

The fine-tuning predictor rests on only 6 backbones — several with broad, flat pooled-performance curves near the optimum, so "peak lands almost exactly" is sometimes within a near-tie.

— Critical Assessment §Red flags / overclaiming

04 Methodology

Probing frozen ViTs with Gabor gratings.

IRIS probes frozen, pretrained ViTs with synthetic sinusoidal gratings (36 orientations × 4 phases × 5 spatial frequencies, bounded below per-patch Nyquist), reading activations at 9 relative depths d ∈ {0…1.0} via block(d,L)=round(d·(L−1)) — keeping cross-architecture comparison fair.

RSS
Basis-invariant population similarity to the orientation manifold (Eq. 3)
OSI
Per-unit vector selectivity, 1 − circular variance of 2θ tuning
ORS
Fraction of units surviving a permutation null (α=0.05)
HWHM
Von Mises tuning bandwidth, comparable to V1 (15°–40°)

Three readout bases protect the conclusions from a single-basis artifact: raw residual coordinates (assumption-light), post-GELU MLP neurons (privileged basis, for trajectory tracking), and SAE features (monosemantic, primary unit).

06 Results & Highlights

  • Cross-scale. RSS curves overlay almost exactly within every family across S/B/L/H/G tiers when aligned by relative depth — except AIMv2, which fixes depth=24 and scales width alone. Depth preserves composition; width changes content.
  • Trajectory (OpenCLIP ViT-H/14). Early layers mature orientation selectivity fast; deep layers keep losing it. Training reshapes the code, not just sharpens it.
  • RSS predicts LoRA unfreeze depth. Peak on the frozen backbone coincides with empirical optimum — DINOv3 S/B/L both peak at d=0.25; AIMv2-L/14 at d≈0.1.
  • Per-task (L-tier). ADE20K: DINOv3 0.465 vs AIMv2 0.342 mIoU (+0.123). COCO inst-seg: 0.688 vs 0.531 mAP@50. COCO keypoints PCK@0.5: 0.873 vs 0.790.
  • Beats 18 alternatives. RSS at block k ranks 1st of 18 a-priori predictors — shape sim 0.76, |Pearson|=0.83, |Spearman|=0.92, argopt-∆=0 (lands exactly on k=4).
  • MAE consistency check. Monotonically rising RSS matches known behavior: linear-probe ≈75% vs fine-tuned ViT-L ≈87.8% top-1.

07 Strengths

  • Conceptually clean: orientation selectivity is the canonical V1 feature with a precise tuning-curve characterization — an ideal interpretable probe.
  • Methodologically careful: basis-invariant RSS, three readout bases, permutation null that mirrors the frequency-selection step (no cherry-picking).
  • The within-contrastive control (OpenCLIP / MetaCLIP / SigLIP2) cleanly isolates data, loss, and patch-size confounds.
  • The cross-scale conclusion (relative depth governs the code; width vs depth play distinct roles) is a crisp, novel scientific result.
  • A genuinely cheap prescription: one RSS pass replaces an expensive LoRA-depth sweep.
  • Honest framing — conjectures labeled as such (e.g., progressive-freezing "not validated").

08 Limitations

  • Synthetic grating stimuli only; natural-image orientation processing not directly probed.
  • Single low-level feature (orientation) and a single cortical-area analog (V1); no spatial frequency, color, motion, or higher ventral/dorsal streams.
  • Fine-tuning prescription validated on 6 backbones on Taskonomy-tiny only, with broad pooled curves — production benchmarks unverified.
  • SAE hyperparameters (dictionary size, λ, steps) stated as fixed but actual values not given in main text.
  • Pooled frozen benchmark penalizes models that need fine-tuning (notably MAE), conflating "frozen-readout quality" with "quality of orientation code."
  • AIMv2 cross-scale behavior complicates the single-L-tier dismissal of it as "not general-purpose."

09 Open questions for further exploration

Does the RSS-predicted optimal LoRA unfreeze depth generalize beyond Taskonomy-tiny to full COCO / ADE20K / NYU-v2, and to non-LoRA adaptation (full fine-tuning, prefix tuning)?
How do the metrics behave on naturalistic stimuli vs gratings — does the inverted-U and the DINOv3 > AIMv2 ordering survive?
Can the suite extend to other V1 features (spatial frequency, color, motion, disparity) and ventral/dorsal analogs, and do they co-localize with orientation-selective layers?
Is "depth preserves composition, width changes content" reproducible when depth and width are varied within a single training run, rather than across families?
Does orientation maturity across checkpoints actually enable a progressive-freezing schedule that preserves performance and cuts compute?
Do the RSS-derived predictions transfer to non-ViT architectures (CNNs, Mamba-style vision backbones, hybrid CNN-ViT)?
