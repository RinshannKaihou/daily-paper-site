§ 01

Methodology — the mismatched-cache audit

Intervene exactly at the relay boundary. Six arms, two regimes, one pre-registered estimand.

The audit swaps the relayed KV-cache for deranged (mismatched-example), zeroed, and moment-matched random counterparts. Every arm delivers a cache with identical geometry — same layers, sequence length, dtype — to the same receiver under the same prompt, so any accuracy gap is attributable to cache content.

The deranged arm is a fixed-point-free permutation that preserves the marginal cache distribution exactly. It is the load-bearing design choice — and genuinely novel as an audit primitive for latent communication.

Primary estimand τ = E[Y | do(C := Ci)] − E[Y | do(C := Cπ(i))]
with identity  τTZ = τTI + τIZ  (only the τTI, τTD terms are the “latent thoughts” attribution)

● Calibrated regime
Procedurally generated registry of 4 entity–attribute bindings, sampled fresh at eval — absent from any training corpus. Receiver cannot solve without the relay.
◆ Natural regime
GSM8K, ARC-Challenge, MedQA-USMLE. Receiver could solve alone. A frozen TOST rejects both one-sided nulls to bound |τ| < δ.

§ 04

Strengths & limitations

Side-by-side, not buried.

Strengths

  • Clean dissociation between generic cache-interface value and example-pairing value, via an accounting identity over arm contrasts.
  • Pre-registered, Holm-corrected TOST with a margin anchored to the audited system’s own claim — turns nulls into quantitative bounds.
  • Cross-family replication of the calibrated ceiling: Qwen3-4B/8B/14B, Mistral-Nemo-12B, phi-4; registry lines and prose paragraphs.
  • Honest scoring of the Mistral-Nemo-12B cell exactly as pre-registered (equivalence not established) — not reframed as a near miss.
  • Same instrument ported to two further released systems, producing three qualitatively distinct readings under one test.
  • Public release of harness, calibration generator, and per-cell raw ledgers.

Limitations

  • Training-split items for GSM8K & ARC-Challenge. Memorization would inflate receiver redundancy and push toward the null the bound reports; a test-split replication weakens but doesn’t eliminate the concern.
  • Interpretability-limited cell: Mistral-Nemo-12B on ARC-C sits at 13.44%, below 4-choice chance (parse failures scored wrong). Within-cell contrasts valid; absolute levels not cross-paper comparable.
  • Single registry construction (4 bindings, 7-letter strings). Prose stress test partially addresses surface-form generality; no claim on human-authored documents.
  • Porting changes: KVComm port replaces calibration mechanics, not crossed with a receiver-sufficient condition. C2C audited in only its released configuration.
  • Margin anchor convenience: the ±2.8-pt margin uses the system’s aggregate gain because per-task figures are negative or variance-free. On 8B GSM8K the CI excludes the tabulated +1.5-pt gain.
  • Portability boundary: Llama-3.2-3B could not consume the relay — a narrower portability claim than it appears.
§ 05

Open questions

Where the audit points next.
iDoes natural-regime equivalence hold on benchmarks where the receiver genuinely cannot solve alone — long-context multi-hop reasoning, private tool-use traces — and where does the ceiling/bounded boundary actually fall?
iiHow sensitive is C2C’s “no detected transfer” reading to projector capacity, training-data scale, or a different sender/receiver pair — and can the audit itself become a training signal for the projector?
iiiCan the deranged-arm protocol combine with mechanistic localization (which layers/heads carry the donor label) to explain why KVComm’s 30%-layer subset recovers only ~1/10 of the ceiling?
ivWould extending to hybrid latent-text protocols (HyLaT) and compressed relays (QKVShare) reproduce the ceiling / partial / none taxonomy — or does compression change which term of the accounting identity dominates?
vIs there a principled way to set the equivalence margin when per-task gains are negative or variance-free — e.g. a ROPE derived from human-judge agreement rather than the system’s own claim?
