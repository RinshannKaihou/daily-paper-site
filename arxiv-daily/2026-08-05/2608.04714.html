§ 01

Methodology — a fully-crossed test-bed

One RTX 3090, fp16, no quantization, fixed seed. Four axes varied in full factorial (~7.2M generations per model), isolating the serving stack as the sole variable.

  • Models3 Llama-3.2-1B-Instruct (Meta), Gemma-3-1B-IT (Google), Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct (Alibaba) — chosen at ~1B scale to make exhaustive factorial tractable.
  • Backends5 hf_raw (reference generate) · hf_pipeline (shares model object) · langchain_hf (orchestration layer) · vllm (separate CUDA context, PagedAttention) · ollama (llama.cpp + GGUF, own runtime).
  • Gen modes4 Deterministic (greedy, isolates structural effect) → Fix (matched sampling) → Token=256 (defaults creep in) → Default (each framework's own config, most realistic).
  • Benchmarks6 Factual: MMLU, TriviaQA, TruthfulQA-MC1, TruthfulQA-Gen. Bias: BBQ (11 demographics), StereoSet (A/B/C adaptation). 14 metrics, all calibrated to [0,1].
  • Stats— McNemar / Wilcoxon paired tests, Benjamini–Hochberg FDR (α=0.05), Friedman omnibus, Fleiss/Cohen κ against hf_raw.

Per-model sensitivity (Deterministic)

Llama-3.2-1B diverges 0.055 on Ollama (9/11 sig.); Qwen 0.025 on vLLM (10/11, |r|=0.38, PagedAttention under non-associative FP); Gemma stays ≤0.010 (0/11).

Strengths

  • Disciplined factorial design — hardware, precision, seed all held constant; exemplary setup for isolating one nuisance factor.
  • Clean variance separation via the four generation modes — turns a vague reproducibility worry into a quantified 39/61 split.
  • Honest framing of confounds (Ollama GGUF, StereoSet format-vs-bias, single-GPU generalizability) instead of hiding them.
  • Triangulated evidence — Cohen/Fleiss κ, error-set overlap, and score-space clustering all converge on the same model ranking.
  • Low-cost, adoptable recommendation: disclose backend+version+config, use greedy for cross-backend comparison.

Limitations

  • Only ~1B scale evaluated; scaling behavior deferred to Pape et al. (2026).
  • Single GPU + CUDA/driver — FP non-associativity means absolute magnitudes may not transfer to other accelerators.
  • Deterministic = one greedy run; stochastic modes only 5 reps — bounds precision of the variance decomposition.
  • English MC / short-form only — no long-form, code, math, multi-turn, or agentic tasks; no INT4/INT8 quantization study.
  • Code promised only on acceptance — not yet independently reproducible; StereoSet numbers not leaderboard-comparable.
§ 02

Open questions for further work

1Does the ~39% share hold, shrink, or grow at 7B/70B where kernel-selection and batching differences amplify?
2Would running llama.cpp on identical fp16 SafeTensors (not GGUF) isolate Ollama's weight-conversion vs. template/tokenizer effects?
3Does the factual-vs-bias asymmetry persist on multilingual bias (CrowS-Pairs) or code/math (HumanEval, GSM8K) with longer decoding?
4How much of the vLLM-Qwen divergence (10/11 sig., |r|=0.38) survives a single attention backend + fixed batch=1?
5Can the decomposition become an exact causal attribution (ANOVA-style orthogonal) vs. the current sequential additive one?
6After full config matching, do residual structural differences (chat-template rendering, tokenization edge cases) remain statistically detectable?
