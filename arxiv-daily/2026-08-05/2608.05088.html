02Why This Matters

The cheapest known way to inject curvature into Muon — O(m+n) state, ~1% wall-clock — for anyone chasing second-order LLM pretraining gains.

03Critical Edge

Only AdamW and Muon are actually run. Concurrent curvature-aware Muon variants (Mousse, FISMO, NewtonMuon) and any billion-parameter scale are absent.

04 · Methodology

04Preconditioned Muon, distilled

Muon orthogonalizes momentum in the gradient's singular-value spectrum but ignores curvature anisotropy. MALT builds a positive-definite surrogate B_t and orthogonalizes inside B_t^{1/2}-induced space, so curvature and spectrum are corrected jointly.

Δ_t = B_t−1/2( Orth( B_t−1/2(∇f(X_t)) ) )

The trick: replace dense Shampoo factors with two diagonal vectors — EMAs of row- and column-wise squared gradient norms, raised to the −1/8 power.

EMAl_t , r_t
buildL_t , R_t
precondM̃ = L·M·R
orthogO = Orth(M̃)
map backD = L·O·R
graft‖O‖_F scale

MALTER adds one scalar ν_t tracking EMA of ‖L_t G_t R_t‖_F², scaling the step by a NAMO-style signal-to-noise ratio α_t ∝ ‖M̃_t‖_F / √(ν_t+ϵ) — a near-free upgrade that consistently beats MALT.

06 · Strengths

+What lands well

  • Almost-zero-overhead curvature awareness — O(m+n) state and ~1% wall-clock, both measured, not asserted.
  • Clean, teachable derivation of "preconditioned Muon" from a local quadratic model; the polar-equivariance result that kills the "SOAP+Muon" strawman is genuinely clarifying.
  • Full convergence proof in Appendix A, with honest disclosure of where it is weaker than prior Muon bounds.
  • MALTER is a principled, near-free upgrade — one extra scalar, consistently beats MALT, supporting the thesis that direction and noise scale are complementary.
  • Fair empirical protocol: shared LR grids, best-LR-by-10K-validation, separate larger grid for MALTER (whose optimal LR is ~10× the others).
07 · Limitations

−Where it falls short

  • Baseline set is thin: only AdamW and Muon are run. Concurrent curvature-aware variants (Mousse, FISMO, NewtonMuon) and Shampoo/SOAP/NAMO are discussed only.
  • Scale ceiling is 774M; no billion-parameter runs despite the "LLM pretraining" framing, and no downstream-task eval.
  • Convergence bound uses a weaker stationarity measure than the standard gradient norm, with worse polynomial dependence on n.
  • No ablations on the −1/8 exponent, the damping ε, grafting target, one-sided vs two-sided, or MALT-vs-NAMO-only.
  • Key hyperparameters deferred to appendix; code release not mentioned.

Q1 · HEAD-TO-HEAD

How does MALTER compare against Mousse, FISMO, NewtonMuon at matched compute above 1B parameters, where dense factors become expensive?

Q2 · THE −1/8 EXPONENT

Inherited from Shampoo without ablation. Sensitivity to −1/4, −1/2? Any principled choice from row/column gradient statistics?

Q3 · PRECONDITION vs NAMO

How much of MALTER's gain comes from diagonal preconditioning vs NAMO-style noise adaptation alone? An ablation would isolate it.

Q4 · DOWNSTREAM

Do the validation-loss gains transfer to HellaSwag, ARC, LAMBADA, zero-shot perplexity — or only to the pretraining-loss curve?
