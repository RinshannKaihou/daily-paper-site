> **一句话总结：** 本文提出 Leak-resistant Unlearning 基准，通过 6 种逻辑启发的多跳推理结构和 3 种恢复攻击（采样、FocusOnKey、量化）对 3 个 LLM、6 种遗忘方法进行评测，发现现有遗忘方法在非链式多跳路径下大量泄漏知识且易被恢复，并揭示了遗忘质量、鲁棒性与模型效用之间不可兼得的"不可能三角"。

> **TL;DR:** The authors introduce Leak-resistant Unlearning, a benchmark that stress-tests LLM machine unlearning along two neglected axes — six logic-inspired multi-hop reasoning structures (HS, MP, LL, CC, DS, MT) and three recovery attacks (Probab, FocusOnKey, Quantization) — across Llama-3.1-8B, Qwen3-14B and Qwen3-32B. On the MQuAKE and Books datasets, 6 unlearning methods (GA, NPO, RMU, TV, PALU, AS) all leak: e.g., Task Vector's Forget Quality collapses from 63.5 under Hypothetical Syllogism to 27.6 under Modus Tollens on Llama-3.1-8B, and PALU reaches 93.0% forget quality with 87.1% robustness but crashes BBH utility to 49.9% of the original model, exposing an "impossible triangle" among forget quality, robustness and utility.

## 核心贡献

- **提出 Leak-resistant Unlearning 概念与基准**：将遗忘评估从"无法回答孤立事实"扩展为两条更严格的判据——路径一致性（Path Consistency，跨多种多跳推理路径知识均不可达）与恢复鲁棒性（Recovery Robustness，在恢复攻击下仍不可恢复）。与 Faithful Unlearning、Deep Unlearning 相比，本工作更强调对抗泄漏的鲁棒性。
- **引入 6 种逻辑启发的推理结构**：基于 LogicBench 筛选并扩展，定义 Hypothetical Syllogism (HS)、Modus Ponens (MP)、Modus Tollens (MT)、Disjunctive Syllogism (DS)、Leibniz's Law (LL)、Conjunctive Composition (CC) 六种路径，使路径一致性可以被细粒度、可比较地评测；并给出了排除 MI、CT、BD 等类型的理论依据。
- **构建可控的数据生成流水线**：知识抽取 → 结构匹配 → LLM（GPT-4o）问题生成 → 双重自动质量校验（分解一致性 + 答案唯一性）。在 MQuAKE 与 Books 上各类型严格控制为等量（MQuAKE 每类 500 条共 3000 条，Books 每类 100 条共 300 条），避免跨类别数据偏差。
- **大规模系统评测**：覆盖 3 个 LLM、6 种遗忘方法、3 种恢复方法、2 个数据集，并定义三项指标——Forget Quality（相对遗忘率）、Recovery Rate、Reasoning Ability（BBH 相对值），三者构成"理想三角"评估框架。
- **揭示三大实证发现**：(1) 非传统推理路径（尤其 MT、DS）显著比常用的 HS/链式更难遗忘；(2) 遗忘知识普遍可被恢复，且多跳问题往往比单跳更易恢复；(3) 现有方法无法同时达成高遗忘质量、鲁棒性与模型效用，呈现"不可能三角"。

## Key Contributions

- Introduces **Leak-resistant Unlearning**, a benchmark reframing unlearning success around two conditions — *Path Consistency* across diverse multi-hop reasoning paths and *Recovery Robustness* against post-hoc recovery — going beyond the isolated single-hop/chain-style QA of MUSE, TOFU, WMDP, MQuAKE, FaithUn, Eval-DU and GONE (their Table 1 shows prior benchmarks lack the six-type logic + recovery-attack combination).
- Formalizes **six logic-inspired multi-hop reasoning categories** (Hypothetical Syllogism, Modus Ponens, Modus Tollens, Disjunctive Syllogism, Leibniz's Law, Conjunctive Composition), each with an explicit join condition for fact composition, and justifies the exclusion of MI/CT/BD on the grounds that they test pattern-matching rather than genuine semantic reasoning.
- Builds a **controllable data-construction pipeline** (knowledge extraction → structural matching → GPT-4o question generation → two LLM-based quality checks: decomposition consistency and answer uniqueness) producing equal-sized categories (500/category for MQuAKE, 100/category for Books) to eliminate cross-category bias.
- Conducts the most comprehensive cross-method evaluation to date for this threat model: **3 LLMs × 6 unlearning methods × 3 recovery methods × 2 datasets**, with three metrics (relative Forget Quality, Recovery Rate, BBH-based Reasoning Ability) arranged as an "ideal triangle."
- Empirically surfaces three vulnerabilities missed by prior benchmarks and frames the central **forget-quality/robustness/utility trilemma** ("impossible triangle") that no method escapes.

## Methodology

**Benchmark definition.** An unlearning procedure is *leak-resistant* iff (1) *Path Consistency* holds — target knowledge stays inaccessible across the six reasoning structures; and (2) *Recovery Robustness* holds — the knowledge cannot be elicited by the three recovery attacks without extra external information. Leak-resistance is an assessment target, not a new training objective.

**Six reasoning structures** (each defines a distinct fact-composition join):
- HS `(A→B)∧(B→C)⊢(A→C)` — transitive chains (object–subject chaining).
- MP `A→B∧A⊢B` — rule instantiation with semantically matched relations.
- MT `(A→B)∧¬B⊢¬A` — backward inference, inverted direction.
- DS `(A∨B)∧¬A⊢B` — elimination over ≥3 candidate values.
- LL `x=y∧A(x)⊢A(y)` — alias/co-reference transfer.
- CC `x∈A∧x∈B⊢x∈A∩B` — intersection of facts sharing (relation, object) across subjects.

**Data pipeline.** (a) Knowledge extraction chunks raw text and prompts an LLM for (subject, relation, object) triples. (b) Structural matching pairs facts per the join rules above, then GPT-4o produces 3 paraphrases + 0–3 answer aliases per pair with the bridge entity masked so the forgotten fact stays implicit. (c) Two independent LLM judges verify decomposition consistency (the multi-hop question decomposes back to the source single-hop questions) and answer uniqueness (rejecting multi-answer prompts).

**Unlearning methods evaluated:** Gradient Ascent (GA), Negative Preference Optimization (NPO), Representation Misdirection (RMU), Task Vector (TV), PALU, Attention-Shifting (AS).

**Recovery methods:** Probab (multinomial sampling, T=0.8, top-p=0.95, 5 trials, correct if ≥1 hit), FocusOnKey (greedy + key-token repetition), Quantization (INT4, exploiting that unlearning weight deltas fall below quantization step size).

**Metrics.** Forget Quality `FQ = 1 − Acc(θ_u)/Acc(θ_p)` (relative to the pre-unlearning model; negative ⇒ unlearning backfired); Recovery Rate over the success set; Reasoning Ability = relative BBH score normalized to the original model.

## Results & Highlights

- **Reasoning structure matters more than method choice.** On Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct with Task Vector, Forget Quality is 63.5 under Hypothetical Syllogism (HS) but collapses to 27.6 under Modus Tollens (MT) — a ~36-point swing showing that high HS scores do not imply robust unlearning across alternative paths (Table 2). For parameter-updating methods (GA, NPO, TV) the Disjunctive Syllogism (DS) structure is consistently the most resistant across all three models.
- **Some structures produce *negative* Forget Quality** (unlearning makes the model answer *better* than before): e.g., PALU on Llama-3.1-8B MP scores −26.0 and Attention-Shifting (AS) on MT scores −43.9, indicating these token-specific methods can actively amplify retrievability along certain paths.
- **Unlearned knowledge is widely recoverable, and multi-hop is easier to recover than single-hop.** On Qwen3-32B, GA's FocusOnKey recovery rate is 95.0 (single-hop) / 93.3 (multi-hop), and Probab recovery for GA on Qwen3-32B is 82.6 (SH) / 87.5 (MH). Across GA, NPO, TV, RMU and PALU on most models, the multi-hop recovery rate exceeds the single-hop rate.
- **The "impossible triangle."** On MQuAKE with Llama-3.1-8B, PALU achieves 93.0% forget quality and 87.1% robustness against recovery but crashes BBH Reasoning Ability to 49.9% of the original; RMU preserves utility almost perfectly (99.2%) yet fails to remove knowledge and stays highly vulnerable to recovery. No method across any model/dataset reaches the ideal outer triangle (Figure 4, §6.3).
- **Base model accuracy** on the curated sets: Llama-3.1-8B 82.9% SH / 65.8% MH, Qwen3-14B 82.5% / 60.6%, Qwen3-32B 84.8% / 64.3% (Table 8), confirming models can solve the multi-hop questions before unlearning so any post-unlearning drop is meaningful.
- **Dataset quality validated** by human annotators (98.4 on MQuAKE, 99.2 on Books) and three LLM judges (DeepSeek-v4 93.4/97.6, gemini-2-flash 92.1/98.9, kimi-K2 91.7/97.1) on a 0–100 scale (Table 6). Curated data is also more diverse: Self-BLEU drops from 0.506→0.285 on MQuAKE and answer entropy rises 5.49→6.85 (MQuAKE) and 6.27→8.01 (Books) (Table 7).

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty** is genuinely incremental over the recent cluster of multi-hop unlearning work (MQuAKE, FaithUn, Eval-DU, GONE, Deep Unlearning). The differentiator is the combination of *six* logic-typed reasoning structures (vs. the single chain type used by all prior benchmarks in their Table 1) *and* a recovery-attack axis; the formal justification for *which* logical types to include/exclude (Appendix C, e.g., excluding Material Implication because it reduces to pattern matching) is a substantive design contribution rather than an ad-hoc list.
- **Rigor** is strong on data control (equal 500/100 per category to suppress cross-category bias), independent LLM-judge verification with two distinct checks (decomposition consistency + answer uniqueness), and independent hyperparameter tuning per (method, model) pair to avoid unfair catastrophic-collapse artifacts. The relative FQ metric correctly normalizes for differing base-model accuracies.
- **Reproducibility** is high: open-source code/data site, A800 hardware specified, exact decoding configs (Probab T=0.8/top-p=0.95/5 trials/≥1 correct; Quantization INT4) and learning-rate/epoch ranges (8e-7–6e-5, 2–5 epochs, TV α∈{1.0,4.0}) reported. A real gap is that the curated datasets and GPT-4o generation prompts are described but the *generated questions themselves* depend on a proprietary LLM (GPT-4o), so byte-identical reproduction is not guaranteed.
- **Significance** is high if the community adopts the six-structure + recovery framing as standard; the "impossible triangle" finding is a clear, citable negative result. However, several reported Forget Quality *negatives* (e.g., AS −43.9 on MT) are striking enough that they deserve an ablation isolating whether the artifact is in the unlearning method, the multi-hop question phrasing, or the LLM-judge grading — the paper presents them as findings but does not fully diagnose the mechanism.
- **Red flags / data quirks.** (1) Evaluation is restricted to **open-weight** models (Llama-3.1-8B, Qwen3-14B/32B) because recovery methods Quantization/FocusOnKey require parameter or logit access; conclusions about black-box commercial APIs (GPT-4) are explicitly out of scope, so the "current methods are not robust" claim does not transfer to API-only deployment threat models. (2) The LLM-as-judge recovery grading rule "if the ground-truth answer appears anywhere in the response, judge CORRECT" (Appendix E.2) is lenient and could inflate Recovery Rate, yet no sensitivity analysis on this criterion is provided. (3) All six reasoning structures are auto-generated from an LLM, and although human + 3 LLM judges score ≥90, the "LLM-as-judge validating LLM-generated data" loop is exactly the failure mode the authors themselves flag in §8.

## Strengths

- First benchmark to jointly combine a rich taxonomy of multi-hop reasoning structures *and* recovery attacks, with a clean formal distinction from Faithful/Deep Unlearning.
- Principled, well-justified selection of the six logical types, with explicit exclusion rationale.
- Controlled, fair category sizes and a relative metric that neutralizes base-model-accuracy confounds.
- Honest, citable negative results: no method achieves the ideal triangle; multi-hop is systematically more recoverable than single-hop.
- Strong transparency: prompts, hyperparameters, recovery configs, hardware, and quality-assessment protocols all disclosed.

## Limitations

- **Open-weight only.** Cannot speak to black-box/commercial API unlearning dynamics (GPT-4, Claude), which the authors acknowledge.
- **Factual knowledge only.** Privacy memorization, copyrighted spans, and implicit harmful behaviors are out of scope and have different representation mechanics.
- **English only.** Cross-lingual multi-hop leakage (e.g., querying in a non-English language) is explicitly flagged as underexplored.
- **LLM-driven data and grading.** The pipeline relies on GPT-4o for generation and on LLM judges for quality/recovery grading, inheriting their known biases; the lenient "answer appears anywhere → CORRECT" rule may overstate recovery.
- **Limited model/method sweep.** 3 LLMs, 6 unlearning methods, 3 recovery methods cannot exhaust the rapidly growing space; the authors frame their claims as "strong evidence of general trends" rather than universal laws.

## Relevance

直接对齐大模型安全/机器遗忘与多跳推理评估方向：提供了比 MUSE/TOFU/WMDP 更严苛的"多跳推理 + 恢复攻击"双轴评测协议与可控数据构造流水线，对设计更鲁棒的遗忘算法、以及对评估 LLM 中敏感知识残留的研究者具有直接方法论价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Do the negative Forget Quality cases (e.g., AS −43.9 on MT, PALU −26.0 on MP) survive under strict human grading and under the stricter "exact-match" criterion rather than "answer appears anywhere"?
- How do the six reasoning structures behave under cross-lingual multi-hop queries — can unlearned English facts leak through Chinese/French reasoning chains?
- Can a single unlearning objective be designed to explicitly trade along the "impossible triangle," and where is the Pareto frontier between forget quality, recovery robustness, and BBH utility?
- Do the same leakage patterns hold for black-box API models where only Probab-style (decoding) recovery is available — i.e., is parameter-level recovery (Quantization/FocusOnKey) the dominant threat or a minor one in practice?
- Could the structural-matching pipeline be inverted into a *training-time* regularizer that explicitly suppresses the six reasoning paths, turning the benchmark into an algorithmic contribution?
