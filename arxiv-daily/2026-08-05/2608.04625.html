01 Methodology — one closed loop, three moving parts

i.

Knowledge Organization

Raw experiment reports are atomized into self-contained strategy chunks (motivation / mechanism / params / context / outcome / risk) and placed on a hierarchical experience tree domain → scenario → stage → objective. A chunk can attach to multiple paths for cross-branch transfer.

π(c) = [d, s, l, o]
ii.

Target-Aware Generation

A new request is mapped to a tree path; sparse + dense multi-path retrieval is fused, then hierarchy-aware boosting up-weights structurally matched candidates and gives partial credit to nearby branches. Qwen-Reranker reranks; agent-judges filter on feasibility, gain, and guardrail risk.

B_tree = Σ β_l · exp(−γ_l · d_T)  (Eq.4)
iii.

Self-Evolution

Each A/B result is confidence-adjusted and appended to an experiment tree. A utility score rewards core gains and penalizes guardrail loss; parent-child diffs isolate one change, siblings pick the winner. On saturation, a new mechanism is pulled from the experience tree and a new branch spawns.

U(v) = Σ wⱼfΔyⱼ − λΣwₖmax(0,−fΔyₖ)  (Eq.8)

03 Strengths & Limitations — side by side

✓ Strengths

  • Tackles a real, under-addressed industrial problem — strategy-level A/B iteration, not the crowded user-item space.
  • Genuine closed loop: knowledge organization → target-aware generation → feedback-driven self-evolution, with results written back.
  • Guardrail-aware utility (Eq. 8) explicitly balances core gain against ecosystem-risk penalty — essential for production, often missing in academia.
  • Controlled baselines: all RAG variants share the GLM-5.1 backbone, isolating knowledge-organization effects.
  • Honest, granular error analysis (Fig. 5) and per-stage breakdown (Table 6) rather than hiding weaknesses.

! Limitations

  • Offline evaluation relies solely on GPT-5.5 as judge — no human gold set, no inter-annotator agreement.
  • Headline Tree-RAG ablation is only +0.015 points (0.2%) — smallest of any component — undercutting the central architectural claim.
  • Dataset, experience tree, and code are proprietary and unavailable, blocking independent reproduction.
  • Only one online scenario reported; the other two are offline-only.
  • No statistical significance, confidence intervals, or variance over seeds for any metric.
  • 43.1% of failures are "solution mismatch" — scenario/objective alignment remains the dominant unsolved weakness.

04 Questions for Further Exploration

Q1 · EVALUATOR BIAS How sensitive are Overall scores to the LLM judge — would Claude-Sonnet-4.6 or a human panel rank methods differently than GPT-5.5?
Q2 · LOAD-BEARING? Given the Flat-RAG ablation differs by only 0.015 points, is the hierarchical tree actually decisive — or is the gain coming from the knowledge base + reranker?
Q3 · ONLINE VARIANCE What are the confidence intervals on the +4.829% GMV lift, and how does it hold up across multiple traffic buckets and time windows?
Q4 · CROSS-DOMAIN Can the experience tree transfer beyond short-video e-commerce (live streaming, ads) without re-chunking — and how is transferability measured?
