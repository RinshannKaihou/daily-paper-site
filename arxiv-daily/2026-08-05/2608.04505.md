> **一句话总结：** LG AI Research 通过"模型 upcycling"将 K-EXAONE 沿深度（48→78 层）和宽度（128→256 专家）扩展并继续训练，得到 750B 总参 / 37B 激活 / 256K 上下文 / 10 语言的 MoE 基础模型 K-EXAONE 2.0；在 24 个基准 9 类评测上相比前代平均提升超 10%，SWE-Bench Verified 从 49.4 跃升至 68.2（+18.8）、OpenAI-MRCR 从 52.3 跃升至 94.4，并在长上下文检索与韩国本土安全（KGC-Safety 99.8 / ROK-Fortress 89.5）上对 Qwen3.5、GLM-5.1、DeepSeek V4 Pro 等开源对手取得最明显优势。

> **TL;DR:** K-EXAONE 2.0 is an open-weight (Apache 2.0) Mixture-of-Experts foundation model from LG AI Research, built by upcycling its predecessor K-EXAONE along both depth (48→78 layers) and expert width (128→256 experts) rather than training from scratch, yielding 750B total / ~37B activated parameters, a 256K-token context window, and 10-language coverage (up from 6). Across 24 benchmarks in 9 categories, it improves over K-EXAONE by >10% on average, with the largest gains in agentic coding — SWE-Bench Verified 68.2 vs 49.4 (+18.8), Terminal-Bench 2.1 43.8 vs 30.3 (+13.5) — and long-context understanding — OpenAI-MRCR 94.4 vs K-EXAONE's 52.3 and ahead of GLM-5.1's 71.5; its clearest edges over Qwen3.5, GLM-5.1, and DeepSeek V4 Pro open-weight rivals are in long-context retrieval and Korean sociocultural safety (KGC-Safety 99.8 vs 92.0/69.3/82.8; ROK-Fortress 89.5 vs 86.1/73.2/47.6), though it trails them on raw world-knowledge and math benchmarks like HLE (18.3 vs 28.7/31.0/37.7).

## 核心贡献

- 提出 **模型 upcycling 路线**作为通向前沿规模基础模型的实用方法：不从头训练，而是将 K-EXAONE 在 LLLG 块级别重复扩展深度（12→19 块、48→78 层）并复制专家扩展宽度（128→256），保留已有参数与训练投入，再以 8T tokens 继续预训练，使总参数从 236B 增至 750B（3.2×）而激活参数仅从 23B 增至 37B。
- 为深层 MoE 训练稳定性引入 **Clamped SwiGLU**（阈值 τ=7.0，作用于最后 16 层专家），将最后一层峰值激活从 6862 压到 silu(τ)·τ=48.96，使低精度训练与低精度推理同时稳定。
- 提供两条共享目标权重的 **自投机解码路径**：与骨干共同训练并经 total-variation loss 微调的 MTP 头，以及新训练的 DSpark 半自回归 drafter（5 层、block size 7）；在相同 γ=7 预算下 DSpark 接受长度比 MTP 高 32–66%，端到端加速从 1.27–1.77× 提升到 1.81–2.57×。
- 构建 **难度聚焦的 mid-training 两阶段**（8K→64K→256K，每阶段 400B tokens），引入 Active Reading 合成数据、thinking-augmented + latent-thought 数据、长上下文多跳数据与 agentic workflow 数据（含 SWE、terminal、Korean public-API、depth/breadth agentic search），使 HLE 相对 Mid-Stage 1 提升 5.66 分。
- 将多语言从 6 种扩展到 **10 种**（新增法、意、波兰、葡），并配套构建 FineWeb2 过滤语料、翻译数据与合成 QA。
- 把安全作为开发全流程的一环：通过与 UNESCO APCEIU 合作的 46 人 Safety Teacher Advisory Council 进行红队与人工评审，将 **K-AUT 安全分类法从 226 扩展到 296 个风险领域**（新增 70 个），并据此训练与评测。

## Key Contributions

- Establishes **model upcycling** as a practical path to frontier-scale foundation models: instead of training from scratch, K-EXAONE is expanded at the LLLG-block level along depth (12→19 blocks, 48→78 layers) and along expert width (128→256 experts via duplication plus norm-preserving rotation noise to break symmetry), then continually pre-trained on an additional 8T tokens, reaching 750B total / 37B activated parameters (3.2× total, 1.6× activated) while preserving the prior generation's parameters, data and practices.
- Introduces **Clamped SwiGLU** (threshold τ=7.0 on the gate and linear branches of the last 16 layers' experts) to tame deep-MoE activation blow-up that breaks low-precision training and serving, capping the last-layer peak activation at silu(τ)·τ=48.96 versus 6862 without the clamp.
- Provides **two self-speculative decoding paths on the same target weights**: a Multi-Token Prediction head refined with an end-to-end total-variation loss, and a newly trained DSpark semi-autoregressive drafter (5 layers, block size γ=7); at equal draft budget, DSpark raises acceptance length by 32–66% over MTP and lifts end-to-end speedup from 1.27–1.77× to 1.81–2.57×.
- Designs a **difficulty-focused two-stage mid-training** (8K→64K→256K context, 400B tokens per stage) combining Active Reading synthetic knowledge data, thinking-augmented plus latent-thought data, synthetic multi-hop long-context data, and agentic-workflow data (SWE PRs, terminal workspaces, Korean public-API, and depth-/breadth-oriented agentic search); HLE improves by 5.66 points over the Mid-Stage 1 checkpoint when followed by SFT.
- **Expands multilingual coverage from 6 to 10 languages** (adding French, Italian, Polish, Portuguese) with in-house FineWeb2 filtering, translation data and synthetic QA, achieving balanced per-language gains without single-language dominance.
- **Integrates safety across the whole pipeline** by partnering with UNESCO APCEIU to run a 46-teacher Safety Teacher Advisory Council for red teaming and human review, expanding the Korea-Augmented Universal Taxonomy (K-AUT) from 226 to 296 risk areas (70 new), and folding the revised criteria into both safety training and evaluation.

## Methodology

**Architecture and upcycling.** K-EXAONE 2.0 is a fine-grained sparse MoE with 78 layers (2 dense + 76 MoE); each MoE layer routes to top-8 of 256 experts plus 1 shared expert, using sigmoid scoring, sequence-level load balancing, and a dropless policy. The hybrid attention interleaves sliding-window (window 128, except 4096 in the second layer) and global attention; QK-Norm and SWA-only RoPE are retained. Depth scaling repeats LLLG blocks copied from the *middle* of the parent (empirically better than input/output copies); width scaling duplicates each expert plus its router row and adds norm-preserving rotation noise to break gradient symmetry. The resulting upcycled model is healed on late-stage K-EXAONE data, then continually pre-trained on 8T tokens of the K-EXAONE 2.0 mixture.

**Continual pre-training data.** The pipeline adds two synthetic-data methods: Active Reading (AR) for knowledge-intensive domains (selected because AR beats textbook-style generation on ARC-C, +1.54 vs −0.42, while textbook-style wins on MMLU), and a thinking-augmented pre-training extended with latent intermediate reasoning. High-quality Korean cultural/historical data is sourced from K-DATA, NIA, the National Institute of Korean Language, and the Northeast Asian History Foundation; new languages are built from FineWeb2 via an in-house filtering pipeline plus translation and synthetic QA.

**Mid-training (two stages).** Stage 1: 400B tokens at 64K context. Stage 2: another 400B tokens with >64K contexts, increasing the proportion of long-form reasoning, repository-level code, and agentic workflows, and introducing retrained classifiers for graduate/STEM-level knowledge plus internally searched challenging knowledge data. Long-context adaptation is verified with Needle-in-a-Haystack up to 256K (perfect retrieval).

**Post-training.** SFT on 350B tokens (reasoning, world/expert knowledge, instruction following, agentic systems) with the router frozen to preserve expert specialization, jointly training thinking and non-thinking modes. Agent SFT covers general tool-calling (with persona-driven MCP servers and NVIDIA Nemotron-Personas), Korean public-API tool use (LG CNS), agentic search (depth- and breadth-oriented, plus hybrid), SWE (Python/Go/JS/TS Docker-verified PRs with F2P/P2P tests), and terminal environments; a preserved-thinking mode carries reasoning blocks across turns. Preference Learning uses two stages of GROUPER (Group-wise SimPER): multi-task preference optimization (math/code via verifiable signals, proof tasks via LLM-judge, chat via instance rubrics) and safety-aware preference optimization organized under K-AUT-V2.

**Inference.** Two drafters attach to the same target: the MTP head (refined with an end-to-end total-variation loss that directly optimizes multi-step acceptance) and a post-hoc DSpark drafter (5-layer block-diffusion backbone conditioned on target hidden states, plus a sequential refinement module).

**Evaluation.** 24 benchmarks across 9 categories — World Knowledge (MMLU-Pro, GPQA-Diamond, HLE text-only), Math (AIME 2026, HMMT Feb 2026, IMO-Answer-Bench), Coding/Agentic Coding (SciCode, SWE-Bench Verified, Terminal-Bench 2.1), Agentic Tool Use (τ3-Banking, Claw-Eval), Instruction Following (IFEval, IFBench), Long Context (AA-LCR, OpenAI-MRCR, Ko-LongBench), Korean (KMMLU-Pro, CLICK, HRM8K-KSM), Multilinguality (MMMLU, Global MMLU-Lite, PolyMath), Safety (KGC-Safety, ROK-Fortress) — in REASONING mode against K-EXAONE, Qwen3.5, GLM-5.1, and DeepSeek V4 Pro.

## Results & Highlights

- **Agentic coding is where K-EXAONE 2.0 makes its biggest jump over its predecessor.** On SWE-Bench Verified it scores **68.2 vs K-EXAONE's 49.4 (+18.8 points)** and on Terminal-Bench 2.1 **43.8 vs 30.3 (+13.5 points)** — the report explicitly describes these as ~30% relative gains; SciCode moves from 35.6 to 40.1.
- **Long-context understanding shows a dramatic improvement.** OpenAI-MRCR rises from **52.3 (K-EXAONE) to 94.4**, beating Qwen3.5 (93.0) and DeepSeek V4 Pro (92.9) and far ahead of GLM-5.1 (71.5); Ko-LongBench improves to 89.6 (vs 86.8) and AA-LCR to 56.2 (vs 53.5). Needle-in-a-Haystack is perfect across all positions up to 256K tokens.
- **Safety is the clearest advantage over open-weight rivals.** KGC-Safety reaches **99.8 vs K-EXAONE 96.1, Qwen3.5 92.0, GLM-5.1 69.3, DeepSeek V4 Pro 82.8**; ROK-Fortress reaches **89.5 vs 60.9 / 86.1 / 73.2 / 47.6** — K-EXAONE 2.0 leads every compared model on both.
- **Korean and multilingual math strengthen.** HRM8K-KSM 91.1, KMMLU-Pro 69.1, CLICK 84.2; PolyMath improves from **57.4 to 71.3 (+13.9 points)** with per-language POLYMATH gains of +7.4 (Vietnamese) to +18.1 (Korean) and +15.3 (Japanese).
- **Speculative decoding delivers large real speedups.** With γ=7 on TP8/8×H200, DSpark achieves acceptance length 5.25/5.20 (GSM8K non-thinking/thinking) vs MTP 3.58/3.13, and end-to-end speedup **2.49×/2.56× vs MTP's 1.72×/1.55×**; on HumanEval DSpark reaches 2.57× vs MTP 1.77×.
- **Reasoning gains are present but smaller.** GPQA-Diamond 82.2 (+3.1 over K-EXAONE), HLE text-only 18.3 (+4.7), IMO-Answer-Bench 78.6 (+2.3); on AIME 2026 (92.3) and HMMT Feb 2026 (78.4) K-EXAONE 2.0 is roughly on par with or slightly below K-EXAONE.
- **Where it still trails the frontier.** Against DeepSeek V4 Pro it lags substantially on HLE (18.3 vs 37.7), HMMT Feb 2026 (78.4 vs 95.2), SciCode (40.1 vs 50.0), SWE-Bench Verified (68.2 vs 80.6), and KMMLU-Pro (69.1 vs 80.5); against Qwen3.5 it is behind on most world-knowledge and math benchmarks (e.g., MMLU-Pro 83.5 vs 89.8, HLE 18.3 vs 28.7).

## Authors & Credibility

The report is authored by **LG AI Research** (full contributor list in Appendix A, with ~33 "Core Contributors" and ~40 additional "Contributors"), the industrial AI lab that also produced the EXAONE lineage and the predecessor K-EXAONE under a Korean government program providing compute. The team discloses model weights, architecture details (layers, experts, dimensions, router policy), the exact upcycling recipe (LLLG-block copying from the middle, expert duplication plus rotation noise), the Clamped SwiGLU threshold (τ=7.0) and its measured effect (last-layer peak 6862 → 48.96), the speculative-decoding acceptance lengths and speedups, and per-benchmark numbers with asterisks marking which baselines come from official reports vs internal runs. The model is released under Apache 2.0 on Hugging Face and GitHub, which materially raises credibility and reproducibility compared to a closed report. The safety section is unusually detailed, naming the partner (UNESCO APCEIU), the council size (46 teachers), the selection process (gender/age/level/subject/region-balanced from 400+ applicants), and the resulting K-AUT-V2 expansion (226 → 296 areas). The limitations section, however, is a generic boilerplate about harmful/biased/stale outputs rather than a substantive discussion of the evaluation gaps.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty** is concentrated in engineering rather than new ideas: the four upcycling building blocks (LLLG-block depth copying, expert duplication + rotation noise, Clamped SwiGLU, dual MTP/DSpark drafters) are individually drawn from or mirrored by prior work — the report itself cites gpt-oss and DeepSeek-V4 for the SwiGLU clamp, block-diffusion for DSpark, and Komatsuzaki-style expert duplication for width scaling. The genuine contribution is the *combination* and the demonstrated stability of the recipe at 750B scale.
- **Rigor** is uneven. Strong points: per-benchmark numbers with baseline-provenance asterisks, an explicit upcycling-vs-textbook ablation (Table 3), a Mid-Stage 1 vs Mid-Stage 2 ablation on HLE (Table 5, +4.71 / +5.66), Korean data-source ablation (Table 4), and full speculative-decoding acceptance-length tables (Table 2). Weak points: many headline comparisons against Qwen3.5/GLM-5.1/DeepSeek V4 Pro mix internally-run and officially-reported (*) numbers without a unified evaluation harness, and several baselines are evaluated "with recommended parameters" — leaving room for setup asymmetry.
- **Reproducibility** is above average for a 750B technical report because weights are Apache-2.0 released, but several ingredients are not: the K-AUT-V2 risk taxonomy details, the Korean institutional data (K-DATA/NIA/NIK/NAHF), the internal agentic-search and SWE task pools, the Nemotron-Persona-driven MCP trajectories, and the LG CNS Korean public-API dataset are all either private or only partially described. The "more than 10% average improvement" headline is not pinned to a specific averaging protocol across the 9 categories.
- **Significance** is real for the sovereign-models and MoE-scaling communities: it is a credible demonstration that a 3.2× upcycled MoE can be stabilized (Clamped SwiGLU) and made servable (DSpark 2.5× speedup) while delivering >10% average gains, and it pushes Korean-context safety taxonomy work (K-AUT-V2) forward. But on absolute capability it does not claim frontier leadership — it explicitly trails DeepSeek V4 Pro on HLE, HMMT, SciCode, SWE-Bench Verified, and KMMLU-Pro.
- **Red flags / specific concerns.** (1) **KGC-Safety and Ko-LongBench are explicitly labeled "in-house"** — the model is benchmarked on private tests it could have been tuned against, which inflates the safety and Korean long-context headline advantage over Qwen3.5/GLM-5.1. (2) **τ3-Banking is essentially flat (14.2 → 14.2)** and is far behind DeepSeek V4 Pro (25.8), undercutting the "agentic tool use" narrative — the gains are concentrated in SWE/Terminal and Claw-Eval, not in τ3. (3) **HMMT Feb 2026 regresses (80.7 → 78.4)** despite a 3.2× scale-up, and AIME 2026 barely moves (92.2 → 92.3), so the math-scaling story is weak relative to compute spent. (4) The compute-scaling figure (Fig. 4) plots GLM-5.1/5.2 and EXAONE 4.0 only as "external references" explicitly *not* comparable, yet visually invites comparison — a framing risk. (5) The "average improvement of more than 10%" and "approximately 30% on three benchmarks" are reported as round numbers without a table breaking down the per-category averages. (6) The boilerplate Limitations section does not discuss any of the above, which is a missed honesty opportunity.

## Strengths

- A genuinely resource-efficient scaling story: upcycling reuses a trained 236B model instead of discarding it, with a clean ablation-backed recipe (middle-block depth copying, rotation-noise width expansion, Clamped SwiGLU for stability).
- Strong and well-measured speculative-decoding results: DSpark delivers up to 2.57× end-to-end speedup with acceptance length gains of 32–66% over MTP, all on the same target weights — directly relevant to serving a 750B MoE.
- Open Apache-2.0 release of a 750B/37B model with 256K context and 10-language coverage, lowering the barrier for sovereign and multilingual deployment.
- Substantial and transparent safety methodology: a 46-teacher UNESCO-partnered advisory council, an expanded 296-area K-AUT-V2 taxonomy, and multi-category ROK-Fortress evaluation with a disclosed judge ensemble (Kimi K2 Thinking, o4-mini, GPT-5.2).
- Honest about not leading everywhere: the conclusion explicitly states "K-EXAONE 2.0 does not lead on every benchmark."

## Limitations

- Headline safety (KGC-Safety) and Korean long-context (Ko-LongBench) benchmarks are in-house/private, creating a risk of train-test proximity that the report does not address.
- Math scaling is disappointing for a 3.2× parameter increase: HMMT Feb 2026 regresses (80.7 → 78.4), AIME 2026 is flat (92.2 → 92.3), and the model trails DeepSeek V4 Pro by ~17 points on HMMT.
- Several key training assets are not open or fully specified: the K-AUT-V2 risk criteria details, the Korean institutional corpora, the agentic-search query generation framework, the SWE/terminal task pools, and the LG CNS Korean public-API dataset.
- Baseline evaluation is heterogeneous: official (*) numbers are mixed with internally-run numbers under "recommended parameters," and τ3-Banking baselines come from official reports while K-EXAONE 2.0 is run internally — not a fully unified harness.
- τ3-Banking agentic tool-use gain is zero over the predecessor and the model is far behind DeepSeek V4 Pro (14.2 vs 25.8), so the "agentic" advance is concentrated in code/SWE rather than general tool calling.
- The "more than 10% average" and "~30% on three benchmarks" summary statistics are not backed by an explicit per-category averaging table.
- The Limitations section is generic boilerplate (harmful/biased/stale outputs) and does not engage with the specific evaluation gaps above.

## Relevance

对关注 MoE 大模型训练、长上下文与多语言基础模型的研究者，K-EXAONE 2.0 提供了一份工程价值很高的"upcycling + 稳定化 + 投机解码"落地范例，其中 Clamped SwiGLU、DSpark drafter、mid-training 两阶段与 K-AUT-V2 安全分类法等细节可直接借鉴到自研大规模 MoE 的稳定性、推理效率与本土化安全建设中；对关注 agent 与长上下文评测的工程师，它在 SWE-Bench Verified / Terminal-Bench / OpenAI-MRCR 上的跃升以及安全子基准的设计也具备直接参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How much of the KGC-Safety (99.8) and Ko-LongBench (89.6) advantage survives when the same private benchmarks are released and independently re-run, and how much is train-test proximity from in-house safety taxonomy curation?
- Is the Clamped SwiGLU clamp (τ=7.0) necessary only because of upcycling-induced instability, or does it also help a from-scratch 750B MoE — and what is the sensitivity of final quality to τ?
- Why does math barely improve (HMMT Feb 2026 regresses) despite 3.2× scale and dedicated reasoning mid-training; is this a data mixture problem or a reasoning-RL reward-design problem?
- Could the DSpark drafter's 2.5× speedup be combined with quantization (FP8/INT4) and expert-offloading to make a 750B MoE servable on a single 8×H200 node, and what is the acceptance-length degradation under quantization?
- How do the depth (middle-LLLG-block copying) and width (duplication + rotation noise) upcycling choices compare to identity-initialized block insertion and untied expert initialization in a controlled from-scratch vs upcycled paired study?
- Does the preserved-thinking mode (Appendix D) translate to measurable Terminal-Bench / SWE-Bench gains, and what is its context-cost trade-off at 256K?
- Can the K-AUT-V2 methodology (teacher advisory council expanding 226→296 risk areas) be replicated for other sovereign contexts, and does it generalize beyond Korean sociocultural risks?
