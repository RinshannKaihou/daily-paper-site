The Calibration Floor: Format Repair Can Masquerade as Self-Correction at Small-to-Mid Scale

Mingguang Chen · Bo Qu · Licheng Wang DeepGrounding · AlphaAvatar arXiv:2608.04355 01 Jul 2026

Key finding

Format repair masquerades as self-correction — apparent gains at mid-to-large scale are extraction artifacts, not reasoning fixes.

格式修复伪装成自我修正——中大模型上多数显眼的"增益"实为抽取伪信号，而非推理修复。

metric causal control

71%

Median share of the total-vs-content gap closed by grammar-constrained re-extraction on 14 active-channel cells (2 cells converging exactly), promoting format dominance from observational to causal evidence.

Wilcoxon |Δformat|−|Δcontent|: p = 1.7×10⁻³

why this matters

A drop-in audit for any LLM eval that scores free-form generations through an answer extractor.

The content/format decomposition and extraction-completeness gate slot directly into existing frozen-trajectory pipelines — stopping the field from misreading format fixes as reasoning fixes.

critical edge

71% — not 100%.

The causal control leaves a residual on the two largest apparent gains in the entire grid (4B & 9B MMLU): two competing explanations — imputation under-estimate vs. a probe-like elicitation effect — are explicitly left unadjudicated.

§ 01

The instrument

Per-sample additive identity · §3.3
Δtotal  =  Δcontent  +  Δformat-recover  +  Δformat-loss
Δcontentboth answers parseable → genuine answer change (only attributable one)
Δformat-recovera₀ unparseable, af parseable
Δformat-lossa₀ parseable, af unparseable

Algebraically exact over the full sample (not the parseable subset). Scoring is unconditional: unparseable = wrong, as in any real accuracy pipeline.

  • ⟲Frozen-trajectory offline eval
    One always-revise pass a₀ → a₁ → a₂ (T=2, greedy, early-stop). Every gating policy — NEVER, ALWAYS, every (signal,τ), oracle — is replayed offline at zero extra generation cost.
  • ⊗Causal control · grammar-constrained
    Freezes the already-generated CoT and forces a guaranteed-parseable final token via logits-masking (1 letter for MCQ; ≤8 digits for GSM8K). The reasoning text is never constrained.
  • ⊞Six synthetic sandbox checks
    Verified against known ground truth before touching real models: identity holds to <1e-12, δ-floor separates pass/fail at 100%, offline gating equals brute force to <1e-15.
  • ⛨Sealed holdout
    Holdout labels encrypted at generation time, decrypted only after dev τ* is frozen — a procedural safeguard against analyst peeking.
  • ≋Models & tasks
    Qwen3.5 {0.8B–9B} (4-bit MLX), Gemma-4-12B, + frontier arm (Tencent Hy3, Nvidia Nemotron-3-Ultra-550B). 9 tasks, n=400/cell, seed 2026. Runs on a Mac Mini (~40 h, zero API cost).

§ 03

The squeeze — a double bind by scale

The calibration-floor criterion recomputed on the content margin reveals a two-sided trap. Small models genuinely flip answers but carry no usable confidence signal; large models carry signal but have almost no content headroom left to flip.

Across 19 capable-scale cells the max 95%-CI upper bound on content headroom is 0.036. All six floor cells fail the floor under the nonparametric bootstrap — the instrument caught its own predicted pathology (Check 6).

Exactly one cell lands in the viable quadrant: 9B TriviaQA. Its sealed holdout policy realizes only +0.005 over the better endpoint, oracle-regret fraction 0.83 (target < 0.50). The door is, at best, ajar.

§ 04

Verdict — strengths & limitations

+ Strengths

  • Six independent convergent checks for format dominance (sign reversal, forced-continuation probe, paraphrase arm, causal control, Gemma cross-family, IoE replication) — each falsifiable, jointly hard to dismiss.
  • Honest self-disclosure: a 2026-07-26 coauthor code-review bug in ROC/AUROC routines is reported in §9.3/§13 with full blast-radius audit; every affected number was regenerated.
  • Sealed holdout with physical isolation (separate files, encryption) — a safeguard most self-correction papers omit.
  • The nonparametric floor re-derivation corrects two of its own binormal false positives (0.8B GSM8K, 2B MMLU) — the instrument caught the failure mode Check 6 predicted.
  • Drop-in, one-line-reproducible instrument (cf_core, decompose_margins.py); the primary 29-cell grid costs zero API dollars.

− Limitations

  • 71% closure, not 100%. The 4B/9B MMLU residual (the two largest gains) has two competing explanations left unadjudicated — C1 is not fully causally settled on its biggest cells.
  • Frontier arm is not sealed: no logprobs (OpenRouter free tier), 4/5 cells exceed the 25% admission gate, and Hy3 was retired mid-collection (HTTP 404) — its MATH data never collected.
  • Not formally preregistered (internal git tags only); the 25% extraction-completeness gate was added post-hoc after the first grid.
  • Scale is confounded with checkpoint identity (one checkpoint per scale point); the checkpoint-level permutation floor sits at p=1/3 with only 4 checkpoints.
  • Generalization is broader than one cross-check but short of a systematic architecture × scale grid; quantization (all 4-bit MLX) and τ-quantile transfer across tasks are untested.
§ 05

Open questions

Is the 4B/9B MMLU residual imputation under-estimate of a genuine content effect on unparseable rows, or a probe-like elicitation effect of the forced continuation itself? A second teacher-forced continuation could adjudicate.

Does the squeeze ever open up beyond ~55B-active parameters, or only tighten? A sealed, logprob-equipped frontier run (paid tier or hosted 70B-class) would close the scale question.

Does the format/content split interact with quantization? All primary runs are 4-bit MLX — an FP16/bf16 control would test whether quantization itself inflates the format margin.

Can the decomposition be applied post-hoc to published benchmarks (Self-Refine, Reflexion, IoE on GPT-4) to retroactively separate format from content in their reported gains?

Is there a revision-prompt family that yields useful counterfactual answers AND preserves extraction stability — breaking the squeeze on the headroom side?

Does format-dominance generalize to verifier-in-the-loop self-correction with external feedback, or is it specific to the no-feedback intrinsic setting studied here?
