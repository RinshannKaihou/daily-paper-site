The headline claim
When Does Training on Downscaled Images Yield the Same Gradients?

A downscaled step is a faithful substitute when its gradient gap splits into a ratio-governed data term + a token-count floor.

Why this matters — so what

Gives the field a cheap, general gradient-level pre-test for when a high-noise downscaled step stays faithful — plus a 3-line opt-in LoRA trainer any low-resolution proposal should pass.

Critical edge — sharpest caveat

The two-term reduction does not predict the very 1024→768 window the trainer stacks — that route is licensed by measurement, not theory, on a single DiT.

01 / METHOD

One gap, two governors, debiased

gradient-level · pre-registered bars

The estimand is the re-encoding excess — gradient cosine distance between a downscaled ("demoted") and native step, with the VAE round-trip subtracted out: gap_e(σ) = d_e(σ) − d_reenc(σ).

Against the inference-side spectral account (SPD; an ideal low-pass, ratio-only, bias-free), this paper writes δg = J̄⊤Δr̄ + ΔJ̄⊤r̄ + … and reduces under four tested assumptions to a data branch plus a constant floor:

data branch
a_e · ‖Δr̄(σ)‖² — governed by the downscale ratio & noise level; sets the amplitude.
graph floor
RoPE_e + Resid_e — governed by absolute target token count, σ-independent; sets the floor.
debiasing
per-arm self-redraw cosines + Spearman attenuation + c/D extrapolation; native floor → self-cosine 1.005 (95% CI [0.994, 1.016]).
verdict
Bonferroni simultaneous non-inferiority, pre-registered margin ε = 0.02; bin resolution ε* = 0.02–0.07.

The instrument is N=40 images × D=12 draws × 14 σ-bins on Anima (2B DiT, 28 blocks, 3D RoPE), tiers {512, 768, 896, 1024, 1280}. Two causal probes — an iso-severity synthetic route (ratio-matched, 1.6× size) and a tier-transfer route — separate ratio from absolute size.

Other measured results

▸ Two governors separated. Absolute target size, not ratio, sets the floor — 1024→768 and 896→768 share the ~2,160-token grid and land on the same floor. Ratio sets amplitude — iso-severity 1280→1120 reproduces 1024→896 bin-for-bin.
▸ Floor existence at σ=1. Debiased endpoint floors +0.019 / +0.056 / +0.304 for 896/768/512; the 512 value clears the pre-registered bar gap∞ ≥ 0.15 on 12 of 12 probe images.
▸ RoPE vs Resid isolated. Fractional positional interpolation erases the 768 endpoint floor and removes ~30% of the 512 floor; a genuine non-positional Resid₅₁₂ ≈ 0.2 bulk remains.
▸ Banded RoPE alignment. Erases the static variant's low-σ liability (+0.064 → +0.033 ± 0.025) while preserving its high-σ gains — in-window, on-manifold.
▸ Stacking the 768 window leaves the weight-space endpoint essentially unchanged: cosine 0.753 / 0.771 vs 0.753 / 0.770 unstacked.
03 / ASSESSMENT

Strengths against limitations

novelty high · rigor strong · significance modest

Strengths

05
  • 01A genuine measurement instrument (debiased cosine estimator, two cross-checks) for a question the field had answered only by inspecting final samples through a full pipeline.
  • 02Clean causal separation of two governors via purpose-built probes (iso-severity route, tier-transfer route, fractional positional interpolation) rather than correlational fits.
  • 03Honest, non-strawman treatment of the competing spectral account: states what survives (ratio governs the data term) and where its scope is wrong.
  • 04A trained, deployable artifact — opt-in σ-conditional LoRA trainer, 3-line on-route change — validated in weight space with deterministic kernels, not only by sample quality.
  • 05Pre-registered bars, Bonferroni-corrected simultaneous verdicts, and explicit reporting of where the instrument is underpowered.

Limitations

07
  • 01One model family (Anima 2B), one native-tier adapter, one illustration corpus; generalization to other DiTs, pretraining, or mixed-resolution adapters is unrun.
  • 02The reduction does not predict the 1024→768 mid-σ window the trainer stacks; its mechanism (negative data–graph interference) is traced, not predicted.
  • 03The A(ratio) governor rests on only two distinct ratio values; the floor law's functional form is unidentified at the tested operating points.
  • 04Several probes (iso-severity 1280, tier-transfer 896→768, depth-split, positional interpolation) remain pre-debiasing reads.
  • 05Training-scale validation is LoRA-only, 480 steps, 60/15 images; no pretraining-scale or full-weight experiment.
  • 06The angular estimand is blind to parallel gradient rescaling; an un-normalized optimizer (AdamW) could see a different endpoint gap.
  • 07The safe route's upper tail σ∈(0.94,1] is underpowered and carries a small real gap (+0.034 ± 0.011); the σ>0.5 gate lets demoted steps land there anyway.
04 / OPEN

Questions for further exploration

transfer · mechanism · optimizer · scale
Q1 · TRANSFER

Does the two-term reduction transfer to PixArt-Σ, SD3, FLUX and to full-weight pretraining, where the Jacobian J and its token-count dependence may differ?

Q2 · MECHANISM

Can the reduction be extended to predict negative-interference windows a priori, so the trainer's second route is theory-licensed rather than measurement-licensed?

Q3 · OPTIMIZER

How does the demotion gap read change under an un-normalized optimizer (AdamW with magnitude-sensitive updates), and does the safe window shift?

Q4 · FLOOR LAW

Could a sweep over many more target-token counts identify whether the floor scales as a power law, logarithm, or step function of token count?

Q5 · RoPE

Banded alignment erases the 768 floor but only ~30% of the 512 floor. Can a more aggressive scheme (per-block learned interpolation, NTK-aware YaRN) push 512 into a usable window?

Q6 · SCALE

At production scale (millions of images, hundreds of thousands of steps), does the −14.6% saving hold, and does the endpoint cosine to the native retrain degrade?
