> **一句话总结：** 本文指出 Perception Encoder (PE) 的冻结特征中已蕴含比 DINOv3 更清晰的"真/伪溯源结构"，但标准线性探测无法利用它；通过提出 Semantic Prototype Calibration (SPC)，用文本编码器得到的 "AI art" / "a real photo" 语义作为可训练类原型起点再做校准，PE-SPC 在 GenImage、In-the-Wild、AIGI-Now、AIGI-Holmes、Blur-and-JPEG 五大基准组上均刷新 SOTA，并在 1.9B 参数下反超 6.7B 参数的 DINOv3-Linear。

> **TL;DR:** The authors show that the vision-language model Perception Encoder (PE) already encodes stronger forensic (real-vs-generated) structure in its frozen features than DINOv3, but standard semantic-agnostic linear probing wastes it (PE-Linear = 89.9% vs DINOv3-Linear = 94.0% avg on In-the-Wild). They propose **Semantic Prototype Calibration (SPC)**, which initializes the two columns of the binary linear head from PE's text embeddings of "AI art" / "a real photo" and then calibrates them on GenImage SD1.4. The resulting **PE-SPC** sets state-of-the-art on GenImage (98.1%, +1.7 over DINOv3), In-the-Wild (94.0%, matching DINOv3), AIGI-Now (95.2%, +8.8 over DINOv3), AIGI-Holmes (98.7%, +1.5 over DINOv3), and Blur-and-JPEG (95.2%, +4.5 over DINOv3) — all while using a 1.9B ViT-G/14 backbone instead of DINOv3's 6.7B ViT-7B/16.

## 核心贡献

- **揭示 PE 的潜力被线性探测低估**：通过 LDA 投影 + 重叠系数分析证明，PE 冻结特征中真/伪图像的分布重叠系数（1.1%）远低于 DINOv3（12.3%），但 PE-Linear 在 In-the-Wild 上的平均准确率（89.9%）反而低于 DINOv3-Linear（94.0%），说明标准线性头浪费了 PE 的溯源结构。
- **提出 Semantic Prototype Calibration (SPC)**：将二分类线性头的两列视为"溯源类别原型"，用 PE 文本编码器对 "AI art" 与 "a real photo" 的嵌入作为可训练起点，再在 GenImage SD1.4 上做小幅校准。文本编码器仅用于初始化，推理时不增加任何开销。
- **全面刷新 SOTA**：PE-SPC 在跨生成器、后处理、真实世界、最新生成器四类共五个基准组上均达最优；尤其 AIGI-Now +8.8 pp、Blur-and-JPEG +4.5 pp、AIGI-Holmes +1.5 pp 全面超越 DINOv3-Linear。
- **更小更高效**：以 1.9B 参数（ViT-G/14）反超 6.7B 参数的 DINOv3-Linear，推理显存降 52.1%、吞吐提升 173.3%（4.5 → 12.3 images/s）。
- **深入的可解释性分析**：通过 prompt 替换实验（Opposite Prompt 掉 26.2%、Unrelated Prompt 掉 7.8%~9.1%）证明 SPC 的收益来自"类别对齐的溯源语义"而非任意文本起点；并揭示 SPC 只对在预训练中已大量接触 AI 生成图像的模型（PE、MetaCLIP2）有效，对 OpenCLIP/SigLIP2（基于 2022–2023 的 LAION/WebLI）反而有害。

## Key Contributions

- **Identifies that linear probing under-exploits PE**: A pooled 1-D LDA + overlap-coefficient analysis on four In-the-Wild datasets shows PE's real/generated feature distributions overlap by only 1.1% vs 12.3% for DINOv3, yet PE-Linear still trails DINOv3-Linear by 4.1% on In-the-Wild — proving conventional semantic-agnostic probing wastes PE's forensic structure.
- **Proposes Semantic Prototype Calibration (SPC)**: Reinterprets the two columns of a binary linear head as learnable "forensic category prototypes," initializes them from PE's text embeddings of "AI art" / "a real photo," then calibrates with cross-entropy on GenImage SD1.4. The text encoder is used only for initialization, so inference architecture and cost are identical to PE-Linear.
- **Sets new SOTA across five benchmark groups**: PE-SPC improves every group over PE-Linear and beats the strong DINOv3-Linear baseline on GenImage (98.1% vs 96.4%), AIGI-Now (95.2% vs 86.4%), AIGI-Holmes (98.7% vs 97.2%), and Blur-and-JPEG (95.2% vs 90.7%), while matching it on In-the-Wild (94.0%).
- **Demonstrates a favorable accuracy/efficiency trade-off**: PE-SPC's 1.9B-parameter ViT-G/14 backbone delivers 96.2% five-group average accuracy vs DINOv3-Linear's 92.9% from a 6.7B ViT-7B/16, with 52.1% lower memory and 2.7× higher throughput.
- **Provides rigorous ablations explaining *why* SPC works**: Prompt-substitution experiments isolate the effect of class-aligned forensic semantics (Opposite Prompt collapses by −26.2%, Unrelated Prompts by −7.8%/−9.1%); a cross-model study (OpenCLIP, SigLIP2, MetaCLIP2, PE) shows SPC helps only models whose pretraining corpus already exposed them to AI-generated images.

## Methodology

**Problem framing.** AIGI detection generalization fails because detectors latch onto generator-specific shortcuts. Recent work (*Simplicity Prevails*, Zhou et al. 2026) showed a frozen DINOv3 + linear probe already beats specialized detectors. The authors argue this focus on vision-only VFMs under-uses vision-language models.

**Geometric reinterpretation.** Standard linear probing on a frozen image encoder is `x = N(f_I(I)), z = xW`. For a binary head, `W = [p1, p2]`, so each column is a class prototype whose dot product with the (ℓ2-normalized) feature gives the class score — a view borrowed from face-recognition losses (SphereFace, NormFace, CosFace). Conventional probing learns `p1, p2` from scratch on a single source (SD1.4), biasing them toward that generator's cues.

**Semantic Prototype Calibration (SPC).** Because PE shares an image-text embedding space, the prototypes are given a semantic head start:
- `p1 ← N(f_T("AI art"))` (fake prototype)
- `p2 ← N(f_T("a real photo"))` (real prototype)

These text-derived prototypes lie near task-optimal directions in PE's space, so calibration needs only small updates. The head is then trained by minimizing cross-entropy `L_CE([x·p1, x·p2], y)` on GenImage SD1.4 with the image encoder frozen, AdamW, batch 128, 2 epochs, lr 2e-5 (much smaller than the 1e-3 used for random-init probing), and bias terms initialized to (1, 0) for stability.

**Inference.** Identical to PE-Linear — frozen image encoder + binary linear head; no text encoder is needed. SPC therefore adds zero deployment overhead.

**Evaluation protocol.** Trained only on GenImage SD1.4; evaluated on five benchmark groups: GenImage (6 unseen generators), In-the-Wild (Chameleon, WildRF, SocialRF, CommunityAI), AIGI-Holmes (autoregressive + diffusion-transformer generators like LlamaGen, VAR, FLUX, SD3.5-L), AIGI-Now (closed-source GPT-4o, FLUX-Pro, with pix/sem splits), and Blur-and-JPEG (Gaussian blur σ∈{0.5,…,2.0}, JPEG quality ∈{95,…,65} on Chameleon).

## Results & Highlights

- **GenImage (Table 1):** PE-SPC reaches **98.1% average accuracy**, beating PE-Linear (93.8%) by **+4.3 pp** and DINOv3-Linear (96.4%) by **+1.7 pp**; its largest gains over PE-Linear come on **ADM (+21.1 pp)** and **Midjourney (+5.7 pp)**, and it ranks first on 4 of 7 unseen-generator subsets.
- **In-the-Wild (Table 2):** PE-SPC lifts PE's average from **89.9% → 94.0%**, matching DINOv3-Linear; the gain is concentrated on the weak subsets — **WildRF +11.2 pp (83.6 → 94.8)** and SocialRF +3.2 pp — while preserving Chameleon (94.7) and CommunityAI (97.2).
- **AIGI-Now (Table 3):** PE-SPC achieves the best overall **95.2%**, exceeding DINOv3-Linear (86.4%) by **+8.8 pp**; gains over PE-Linear are much larger on the sem split than the pix split (**+9.0 pp vs +3.3 pp**), e.g., MiniMax sem **+19.7 pp**, FLUX-Kontext sem **+14.2 pp**, GPT-4o pix **+11.7 pp**.
- **AIGI-Holmes (Table 4):** PE-SPC ranks first with **98.7%**, beating DINOv3-Linear (97.2%) by **+1.5 pp**, with notable improvements over PE-Linear on **VAR (+4.8 pp)**, **SD3.5-L (+4.4 pp)**, and **FLUX (+2.0 pp)** — spanning both autoregressive and diffusion-transformer generators unseen during pretraining.
- **Blur-and-JPEG (Table 5):** PE-SPC achieves the best average **95.2%**, beating DINOv3-Linear (90.7%) by **+4.5 pp**; under the strongest Gaussian blur σ=2.0 it scores **95.9%**, exceeding PE-Linear (77.8%) by **+18.1 pp** and DINOv3-Linear (89.1%) by **+6.8 pp**.
- **Minimum-accuracy stability (Fig. 3):** SPC's main effect is raising the *worst-case* accuracy within each group — minimum accuracy improves by **+21.1 pp on GenImage**, **+16.0 pp on Blur-and-JPEG**, and **+10.2 pp on AIGI-Now**.
- **Efficiency (Table 7):** PE-SPC (1.9B params, 12.5 GiB, 12.3 img/s, 96.2% avg) vs DINOv3-Linear (6.7B, 26.1 GiB, 4.5 img/s, 92.9%) — **−71.6% params, +173.3% throughput, +3.6 pp accuracy**.
- **Prompt-semantics ablation (Table 6):** "Our Prompt" (+3.9 avg Δ) and a semantically equivalent "Similar Prompt" (+3.7) both help; "Opposite Prompt" (swapped labels) collapses to −26.2; content-only "Unrelated Prompts" degrade by −7.8/−9.1 — proving gains come from class-aligned forensic semantics, not arbitrary text initialization.
- **Cross-model applicability (Fig. 4):** SPC improves models with large-scale AIGI pretraining exposure — **MetaCLIP2 +3.9, PE +3.9** — but *hurts* OpenCLIP (−3.1) and SigLIP2 (−15.8) whose 2022–2023 LAION/WebLI corpora predate the synthetic-content explosion.

## Authors & Credibility

Authors are from the **State Key Laboratory of Multimodal Artificial Intelligence Systems, Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences** (Cai, Tan, Wan), the **University of Chinese Academy of Sciences**, **Baidu Research's Institute of Deep Learning** (Tan), and **Purple Mountain Laboratories** (Gao). The group has a strong track record in face/forgery detection. The work positions itself explicitly against the very recent *Simplicity Prevails* (Zhou et al. 2026) and *DINOv3* (Siméoni et al. 2025) lines, and benchmarks against current 2025–2026 detectors (DDA, OMAT, AIDE, FGTS, STAL) on up-to-date evaluations (AIGI-Now with GPT-4o/FLUX-Pro, AIGI-Holmes with VAR/LlamaGen/SD3.5-L). All compared foundation models use the largest public checkpoint (Table 8), reducing scale-confound bias. Appendices report hardware (single V100S-32GB), learning-rate sweeps, per-dataset LDA analyses, and the full text-concept matching protocol — a level of detail consistent with a rigorous vision-conference submission.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** Genuine but modest. Reinterpreting a linear head's columns as class prototypes is a known idea from face recognition (SphereFace/CosFace/NormFace, which the authors cite); the actual contribution is the *initialization recipe* — seeding those prototypes from the frozen text encoder of a VLM. This is a clean, low-dimensional insight rather than a new architecture.
- **Rigor:** Strong. The prompt-ablation (Table 6) is the key credibility test and is done well: "Opposite Prompt" crashing to 44.7% on In-the-Wild is a convincing falsification of the "any text init helps" hypothesis. The cross-model study (Fig. 4) honestly reports that SPC *degrades* OpenCLIP/SigLIP2, which is a non-trivial negative result many papers would omit.
- **Reproducibility:** Good. Single GPU (V100S-32GB), 2 epochs on a public SD1.4 subset, exact lr/batch/optimizer given, biases initialized to (1, 0). The only friction points are the closed/proprietary evaluation generators (GPT-4o, FLUX-Pro, Jimeng, Keling, MiniMax in AIGI-Now) and the 2025-dated foundation checkpoints whose pretraining corpora are not fully public.
- **Significance:** The headline comparison is **1.9B PE-SPC vs 6.7B DINOv3-Linear**. This is a real and favorable trade-off, but note the unfairness in the *opposite* direction that the paper does not stress: PE was pretrained on a 2025 web corpus with abundant labeled synthetic images and aligned text, whereas DINOv3 is image-only — so part of PE-SPC's edge plausibly comes from pretraining-data recency rather than from SPC per se. The authors partially address this via the OpenCLIP/SigLIP2 negative result, but a same-era, same-scale vision-only control is missing.
- **Missing ablations:** No comparison against (a) initializing prototypes from a *pool* of forensic concepts rather than two hand-picked prompts, (b) SphereFace/CosFace-style angular margin losses on top of the prototypes, or (c) applying SPC to DINOv3 by borrowing an external text encoder. The hand-chosen strings "AI art" / "a real photo" are a mild red flag — Table 6's "Similar Prompt" mitigates this, but a small automatic prompt-search ablation would close it.
- **Data quirk:** Training is exclusively on GenImage SD1.4 (single generator, single processing pipeline). Generalization claims therefore hinge entirely on the foundation model's pretraining; the paper is honest about this but it limits how much "SPC" itself (vs PE's pretraining) is being validated.
- **Baseline strength:** DINOv3-Linear is the right strong baseline and is beaten fairly; however, the very recent DINOv3-enhanced detectors FGTS (Huang et al. 2025) and STAL (Li et al. 2026) are discussed in related work but *not* included in the result tables, leaving the strongest in-family competitors unevaluated.

## Strengths

- Simple, deployable method: SPC changes only the initialization of a 2-column linear head and adds zero inference cost — easy to drop into any VLM-based detector.
- Convincing mechanistic story: the LDA overlap-coefficient analysis (1.1% vs 12.3%) plus the Opposite/Unrelated prompt ablations jointly explain *why* PE-Linear underperforms and *why* SPC fixes it.
- Breadth of evaluation: five benchmark groups covering cross-generator, in-the-wild, latest closed-source (GPT-4o, FLUX-Pro), and post-processing robustness, all trained on a single source.
- Honest negative results: SPC degrades OpenCLIP and SigLIP2, and the paper reports this rather than hiding it.
- Strong efficiency result: 71.6% fewer parameters and 2.7× throughput vs the main baseline, with higher accuracy.

## Limitations

- **Hand-crafted prompts.** "AI art" / "a real photo" are fixed; the paper shows wording invariance for *one* alternative but does not automate or robustly search the prompt space.
- **Single training source.** Only GenImage SD1.4 is used; the method's generalization is therefore inseparable from PE's pretraining quality.
- **Depends on pretraining-era exposure to synthetic content.** SPC fails on OpenCLIP/SigLIP2 — so it is not a universal upgrade for vision-language models, only for those (PE, MetaCLIP2) trained on recent web corpora.
- **Recent in-family competitors (FGTS, STAL) not benchmarked.** These DINOv3-enhanced detectors are the natural rivals and are absent from the tables.
- **Evaluation on closed generators (GPT-4o, FLUX-Pro, Jimeng, Keling, MiniMax)** depends on the AIGI-Now benchmark's curation, whose real-image controls and access are not fully under the authors' control.
- **No exploration of margin/loss variants** that the prototype framing naturally invites (CosFace/SphereFace-style), and no multi-class (per-generator) extension despite the prototype formulation supporting it.

## Relevance

对于关注**伪造/深度伪造检测、视觉基础模型的可泛化性、以及视觉-语言模型在取证任务中的应用**的研究方向，本文提供了一个简洁且可即插即用的方案（SPC），并给出了一套针对"线性探测为何低估 VLM"的诊断方法论（LDA + 重叠系数 + 文本概念匹配），可作为后续 VLM 取证工作的基线和分析范式参考。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Can SPC's prompt dependence be removed by **automatically mining** the best forensic concept from a candidate pool (e.g., via the Top-1 text-concept matching protocol in Appendix F) instead of hard-coding "AI art" / "a real photo"?
- Does adding an **angular-margin loss** (CosFace/SphereFace) on top of the calibrated prototypes further widen the real/generated separation, given the prototype-geometric framing?
- How does PE-SPC behave when **trained on multiple generators** (multi-source SD1.4 + ADM + Midjourney) rather than SD1.4 alone — does the SPC advantage over random-init probing shrink, stay, or grow?
- Could SPC be **ported to vision-only VFMs** like DINOv3 by borrowing a separate text encoder for initialization, and would it still help, or is the shared image-text embedding space essential?
- The AIGI-Now "sem" split gains (+9.0 pp) dwarf the "pix" gains (+3.3 pp); does this mean SPC is **complementary** to methods that target low-level pixel traces (frequency/spectral detectors), motivating a two-head ensemble?
- Given SPC fails on OpenCLIP/SigLIP2, is there a **lightweight pretraining-corpus screening test** that could predict in advance whether a given VLM will benefit from SPC, without running the full benchmark suite?
