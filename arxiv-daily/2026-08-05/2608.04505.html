Why This Matters

A working, open-weight recipe — upcycling + Clamped SwiGLU + DSpark drafter — that stabilizes and serves a 750B MoE while reusing prior compute. Directly transferable to sovereign and multilingual MoE projects, not just a leaderboard entry.

Critical Edge

01 / METHODOLOGY

Four engineered moves, not four new ideas

↑ Depth scaling

Middle-block copying

LLLG blocks are duplicated from the middle of the parent (empirically better than input/output copies), expanding 12→19 blocks, 48→78 layers.

48 → 78 layers
→ Width scaling

Expert duplication + rotation noise

Each expert and its router row is duplicated, then norm-preserving rotation noise breaks gradient symmetry — 128→256 experts without relearning.

128 → 256 experts
≡ Stability

Clamped SwiGLU (τ=7.0)

Clamps the gate and linear branches of the last 16 layers' experts, crushing the last-layer activation peak from 6862 → 48.96 and enabling low-precision train + serve.

6862 → 48.96 peak
⚡ Inference

Dual self-speculative decoding

Two drafters share the same target weights: an MTP head (TV-loss refined) and a new 5-layer DSpark semi-autoregressive drafter (block size γ=7).

up to 2.57× speedup
◷ Mid-training

Difficulty-focused, two stages

8K → 64K → 256K context, 400B tokens per stage, blending Active Reading synthetic data, thinking-augmented + latent-thought data, and agentic-workflow traces.

⌘ Post-training

SFT + two-stage GROUPER

350B-token SFT with router frozen (preserves expert specialization); then multi-task and safety-aware preference optimization under K-AUT-V2.

03 / ASSESSMENT

Strengths vs. limitations, side by side

Strengths

  • Resource-efficient scaling. Upcycling reuses a trained 236B model with a clean ablation-backed recipe — middle-block depth copying, rotation-noise width expansion, Clamped SwiGLU.
  • Well-measured speculative decoding. DSpark delivers up to 2.57× end-to-end speedup with +32–66% acceptance length over MTP, all on the same target weights.
  • Open Apache-2.0 release of a 750B / 37B model with 256K context and 10-language coverage — lowering the barrier for sovereign deployment.
  • Transparent safety methodology: 46-teacher UNESCO-partnered council, 296-area K-AUT-V2, disclosed judge ensemble (Kimi K2 Thinking, o4-mini, GPT-5.2).
  • Honest about scope: the report explicitly states "K-EXAONE 2.0 does not lead on every benchmark."

Limitations & Red Flags

  • Train-test proximity risk. KGC-Safety and Ko-LongBench are in-house/private — the headline safety & Korean lead may be inflated.
  • Math scaling disappoints for 3.2× params: HMMT Feb 2026 regresses 80.7 → 78.4; AIME 2026 flat at 92.2 → 92.3; trails DeepSeek V4 Pro by ~17 pts on HMMT.
  • Key assets not open: K-AUT-V2 criteria details, Korean institutional corpora, agentic-search framework, SWE/terminal pools, LG CNS public-API dataset.
  • Heterogeneous baselines. Official (*) numbers mixed with self-run "recommended parameters" — not a unified harness; τ3-Banking baselines come from official reports.
  • "Agentic" advance is narrow: τ3-Banking is flat (14.2 → 14.2) and far behind DeepSeek V4 Pro (25.8) — gains concentrate in SWE/Terminal, not general tool calling.
  • Round-number headlines (">10% average", "~30% on three benchmarks") are not backed by a per-category averaging table.
04 / OPEN QUESTIONS

What the report leaves unresolved

01

How much of the KGC-Safety (99.8) and Ko-LongBench (89.6) advantage survives when the same private benchmarks are released and independently re-run?

02

Is Clamped SwiGLU (τ=7.0) necessary only because of upcycling-induced instability — or does it also stabilize a from-scratch 750B MoE? What is the τ sensitivity?

03

Why does math barely improve despite 3.2× scale and dedicated reasoning mid-training — data mixture problem, or reward-design problem?

04

Can DSpark's 2.5× speedup combine with FP8/INT4 quantization and expert offloading to make a 750B MoE servable on a single 8×H200 node?

05

How do depth (middle-LLLG copying) vs width (duplication + rotation noise) upcycling choices compare to identity-init block insertion in a controlled paired study?

06

Does the K-AUT-V2 teacher-council methodology (226 → 296 risk areas) replicate for other sovereign contexts, or does it generalize only to Korean sociocultural risks?
