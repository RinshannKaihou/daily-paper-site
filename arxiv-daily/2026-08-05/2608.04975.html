01

Methodology — an audit, not a model

STEP 01
Re-derive every target
Two physicist/mathematician authors independently re-derive all 65 test problems; the 15-problem dev split is excluded (public gold).
STEP 02
Adversarial re-check
A second pass re-verifies each flagged defect to kill false alarms. Approved fixes land in a ledger/.
STEP 03
Regenerate & md5-pin
Release files are rebuilt from the ledger and md5-verified at harness startup — original upstream text & h5 pinned identically.
STEP 04
Matched re-evaluation
12 models, identical harness (background, pass@1, two-env OR grading). Only the benchmark data differs — isolating the instrument's contribution.

Why a single defect is costly here

SCICODE subproblems form a cumulative chain: step k may call gold functions of steps 1…k−1, and main-problem scoring is all-or-nothing. One defective subproblem vetoes the whole main problem, and an early defect propagates downstream.

Library-version-robust grading

Each saved solution is graded in two pinned NumPy/SciPy environments; "correct if either passes" rescues 7–9 of 287 subproblems per model, with near-identical rescue sets across models — so the gain is not version luck.

04

Strengths & limitations, side by side

Strengths

  • Closes a real measurement gap on a benchmark feeding public leaderboards and national-lab suites.
  • Adversarial, provenance-tracked design — second reviewer, diff-grounded, md5-verified release.
  • The taxonomy generalizes — nature × direction with seven scientific subtypes transfers to other research-level benchmarks.
  • Honest about limits: single pass@1, near-saturation top end, author-only review.
  • Tightens lenient tests too — gain is not grade inflation.
  • Fully open: harness, ledger, manifests, analysis files.

Limitations

  • Only the 65-problem test split — the 15-problem dev split (public gold) is untouched.
  • Mostly single pass@1 — small inter-model gaps among saturated top models sit inside run-to-run noise.
  • No external blind validation — all reviews by the authors.
  • Original-benchmark numbers don't fully match the Artificial Analysis leaderboard; harness gap not fully reconciled.
  • Single-problem coding only — no long-horizon agentic workflows (named future work).
  • With-background subproblem accuracy near saturation (≥95%) — limited headroom on that metric.
