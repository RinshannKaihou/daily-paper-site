Relational Response Fields: A General Theory of Black-Box LLM Response Consistency and Recovery

01 Methodology finite-dim linear inverse problem

α Formal Setup

Transformed queries form a directed graph G=(V,E). Each node holds a parsed response zi in a Hilbert space; each edge carries a typed transport Te. The relation defect (Dz)e = √we(zj − Tezi); trusted evidence forms the anchor operator A. Both stack into B=[D;A].

β Central Object

γk(D,A) := min1≤|S|≤2k σmin(BS) — nonincreasing in k, nondecreasing when informative rows are added. Positive iff the group spark of B exceeds 2k. The factor 2k is unavoidable: two k-sparse fields differ on up to 2k nodes.

γ Theorems

Th.2 consistency blindness: any h∈ker D is observationally invisible. Th.3 γk>0 ⟺ uniform identifiability. Th.4 stability ∥ê−e*∥ ≤ 2ε/γk. Th.5 minimax lower bound — no estimator beats 1/γk dependence. Prop.6 separates this from convex null-space recovery.

δ Algorithms & Experiments

Three decoders: exact ℓ0,g, convex group-ℓ1 (proximal gradient), discrete candidate-field. Returns (γk, ∥Bê−s∥) as an instance certificate. Tested on synthetic fields, Qwen2.5-{0.5B,7B,14B} & Phi-3-mini (128 math + 64 code), and authentic-error replays (3 correct + 1 wrong node).

Decision-rule trichotomy

γk = 0acquire information — no margin exists.
γk ≈ εimprove measurement — margin is uncertain.
γk ≫ εimprove computation — recovery is well-posed.

Table 2 numbers (e.g. Qwen2.5-0.5B code 31.2%→75.0%) characterize candidate generation under unequal query budgets — not a compute-matched leaderboard.

03 Strengths & Limitations side-by-side verdict

Strengths

what lands cleanly
  • One quantitative object γk with three matching roles — identifiability threshold, stability scale, minimax lower bound — exactly what an "intrinsic difficulty" should be.
  • Cleanly formalizes why consistency ≠ truth and why anchors, not more paraphrases, remove gauge directions. Directly actionable for self-consistency / reflection practitioners.
  • Falsifiable, prediction-first experiments: the four phenomena are pre-specified by theory, not post-hoc rationalizations.
  • Honest separation of information-theoretic recoverability from convex-decoder conditions — resists a common overclaim.
  • The decision-rule trichotomy is genuinely useful engineering guidance for when to acquire vs. iterate vs. compute.

Limitations

where it stops
  • Theory is finite-dimensional and linear; nonlinear transports get only a local Jacobian guarantee (Appendix G).
  • Exact γk is combinatorial (enumerate supports ≤2k); large fields need relaxations.
  • Sparse-node corruption models localized failures, not diffuse representation bias — a major real-world error class is out of scope.
  • Empirical scale is small and synthetic-leaning (128 math + 64 code, sub-frontier models); effect is modest (ρ≈0.45, AUC +0.033); no frontier-model or open-ended NL evaluation.
  • Certificate is only as reliable as parser, relation spec, and anchors — paper itself recommends symbolic substitution / code execution over LLM-scored relations.
04 Questions for Further Exploration open frontiers

Does γk retain cross-task predictive power on open-ended NL QA, where typed transports are hard to define and parsers are lossy?

How does γk compare as a repair-difficulty predictor against semantic entropy, verifier confidence, or self-consistency variance — head-to-head, compute-matched?

Can the combinatorial cost of computing γk be replaced by a tight, efficiently computable lower certificate (group-RIP / SDP) reliable enough for online query selection?

For diffuse/systematic biases — the excluded regime — is there an analogous observability object over a distribution of corruptions rather than sparse support?

Which typed transport families (identity/scaling/translation vs. decomposition vs. code refactor) carry most predictive signal — and can transport selection itself be optimized against the margin witness?

Does a combined feature (γk + semantic entropy + verifier) beat either alone on a deployable selector for natural fields?
