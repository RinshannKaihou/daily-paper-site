03 / ASSESSMENT

Strengths & limitations

Strengths

Where the work earns trust

  • First study pairing production hyperscaler telemetry with a reproducible controlled study — ecological validity and causal isolation in one paper.
  • The taxonomy → characterization → mismatch → principle → prototype pipeline is unusually tight and self-consistent.
  • Honest negative results — CORAL backfires; private per-task cores lose to shared; partitioning by tool class gives no benefit.
  • Concrete, silicon-relevant future agenda: scheduling offload, way-partitioned/tagged caches & branch predictors, morphable cores — grounded in PMU evidence.
  • Dense quantitative results consistently tied to specific metrics, frameworks, and operating points.
Limitations

Where the scrutiny bites

  • Fleet study details (framework mix, model sizes, absolute volumes, geography, beyond 24h) withheld for confidentiality — capping external scrutiny.
  • GPU harvesting evaluated on only two of four frameworks; SWE-Agent & Trae GPU results are sparse.
  • No head-to-head with agent-aware serving systems (Pythia, ThunderAgent, Murakkab, Agentix) — marginal value over SOTA unquantified.
  • Agora not released; MILP formulation, profiling horizon and auto-tuning controller abbreviated — hard to reproduce exactly.
  • Harvester sensitivity tested only with DCPerf; energy numbers rely on GPU power proxies, not measured wall-power.
  • A single 24h window may miss diurnal/weekly seasonality and rare bursts that dominate production tails.
04 / OPEN

Questions worth chasing

Q1 · SCHEDULER

How much of Agora's gain survives against a strong agent-aware scheduler (Pythia, ThunderAgent, Murakkab) rather than only static, workload-agnostic baselines?

Q2 · GENERALIZATION

Does the Owl GPU-harvesting win extend to SWE-Agent & Trae (untested on GPU), and scale beyond 8 A100s to larger DGX-class pods?

Q3 · HARVESTER MIX

How sensitive are the numbers to the choice of co-located harvester — DCPerf was the only one tested — across CPU- vs bandwidth-bound profiles?

Q4 · ORCHESTRATION

Can orchestrator hints extend to LLM-orchestrated workflows (no proactive graph) via learned speculative burst prediction, avoiding conservative reactive retreat?

Q5 · SEASONALITY

How do role-level CPU signatures & tool-call heavy tails behave over multi-week traces with diurnal seasonality — do the tail-driven provisioning rules change?

Q6 · SILICON CEILING

For proposed future silicon (tagged caches, offload engines, morphable cores), what is the quantified upper bound on IPC & context-switch reduction left on the table?
