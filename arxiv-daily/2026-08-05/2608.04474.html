02

Geometry & the sampling wall

Why one solve suffices

Half-space collapse

Under Gaussian perturbation, the violation set collapses to half-space S₀. The local rate is just the 1-D Gaussian tail beyond the Mahalanobis distance d*_Σ — one scalar, one Φ evaluation.

Sampling cost vs one solve

Each "sample" is a full LP solve. The closed-form rate is independent of ν — a single solve returns it regardless of how rare the event.

03

What the numbers say

Results & highlights

Dispatch certificate, end-to-end

On a 5-generator system under a 428.5 tCO₂ emissions cap (forecast demand 500 MW, margin 19.5 tCO₂), the certificate yields d*_Σ = 2.00, ν_local = 0.023 with basis-stability margin ρ_min = 6.8 ≫ d*_Σ — so Assumption 5.1 holds comfortably.

Severity & feature attribution

Expected emissions overshoot given a violation: 3.6 tCO₂. The typical violating forecast shift concentrates on the load feature (+0.36) vs renewable (+0.02) — load drives roughly 3× the rate sensitivity.

Tightness of the collapse

Gaussian mass leaking past the J=2 basis facets is ≈ 2×10⁻¹⁰, so the polyhedral and half-space measures of S₀ agree to all reported digits (0.0227) — validating the collapse Proposition 5.1 makes exact.

Where it breaks, honestly

When the marginal generator's headroom shrinks (ρ_min < d*_Σ), the merit order flips inside the boundary. The single-region certificate then overstates 0.023 vs true 0.008 — ~3×, a concrete demonstration of where the assumption binds.

04

The trade, stated plainly

Strengths  ⇄  Limitations

+ What it buys you

  • Sampling-free, with explicit bounded error — against a proven, not assumed, Ω(1/ν₀) barrier.
  • Per-decision interpretability: sensitivity ∂ν/∂x₀ and direction v* = Σa/‖Σa‖ give real feature-level attribution.
  • The violation half-space is derived from the cost structure & active basis (a = 0.55 Bᵀ = (66,−22)ᵀ), not configured.
  • Decision-space size is essentially free — only input dimension p enters at O(p²).
  • Intellectually honest about the failure regime (the 0.023 vs 0.008 overstatement example).

! What it costs you

  • Linearity is load-bearing: nonlinear predictors, θ-dependent costs, MIP all break Lemma 4.1.
  • Single-basis assumption (ρ_min ≳ d*_Σ): near vertex degeneracy the certificate overstates the rate.
  • Gaussian perturbations only — heavy-tailed or skewed forecast errors are not covered.
  • One toy example; no importance-sampling / subset-simulation / cross-entropy comparison.
  • No released code or artifacts — reproducible in principle, not in practice.
  • Global certification unsolved: regions can grow as C(d, m); construction left open.
