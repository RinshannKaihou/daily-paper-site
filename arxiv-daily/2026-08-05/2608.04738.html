§ 01

Methodology — three phases on one shared graph

1
Construct

Initial evidence-graph construction

Task Analyzer turns goal q into context C. A Hypothesis Filter clusters candidates by semantic similarity and runs small pilot experiments (e.g. 10 AG News samples / direction) to keep the best-supported group H*. Graph Builder assembles a provisional, intentionally incomplete graph G₀.

2
Inspect · Repair

Cross-stage inspection & rollback repair

A node is weak if its attributes and a neighbor’s violate the edge relation’s semantic constraint. Graph Inspector returns repair groups (weak node + downstream subordinates); Repair Planner regenerates them in topological order. Checkpointed RollbackToBest(MS, Gbase) rejects repairs that add weak nodes or break a valid evidence chain.

3
Gate · Write

Evidence-gated manuscript generation

Writing fires only when Ready(G) holds — schema valid, ≥1 retained Claim, deliverables covered, every retained Claim backed by a valid chain with no weak node. Paper Reviewer audits structure, novelty framing, citations, graph faithfulness. Otherwise the run returns Incomplete.

§ 03

Strengths & Limitations

+ Strengths
  • Architecturally principled. A single typed graph as operational state unifies cross-stage consistency in a way pipelines cannot.
  • Evidence-readiness gate directly attacks the most cited failure mode — unsupported or overstated claims.
  • Checkpointed rollback with an explicit non-degrading criterion (preserve valid evidence chains) is a clean safety mechanism.
  • Honest reporting of the CSR/EDC trade-off and the Alignment gap rather than cherry-picking.
  • Strong engineering docs — Algorithm 1, weak-node patterns (Table 2), schema & prompt appendices.
− Limitations
  • No quantitative ablation (acknowledged) — components only argued qualitatively via Table 5 + one trace.
  • Only two baselines; AI Scientist-v2, AutoSci, ARIS, InternAgent cited but not run.
  • EDC 87.73% < NanoResearch 96.15% — does not strictly dominate on numerical fidelity.
  • Alignment drift (6.6 vs 8.8) — repair can move research off the original task.
  • Small benchmarks (25+20 tasks) from the baselines’ own lineage; no variance or significance bars.
  • CSR denominator depends on stochastic LLM claim extraction; long-term library ML not empirically isolated; single backbone (qwen-3.6-plus).
§ 04

Questions for Further Exploration

i.

How does EviGraph behave with weaker open-source backbones (7B–14B), where the Inspector and Repair Agents themselves may be unreliable?

ii.

Can dependency-aware repair be ported to non-research pipelines — long-horizon coding or data-analysis agents where claim–evidence consistency matters?

iii.

What is the marginal contribution of the long-term library ML in a warm-start regime — and at what risk of importing stale claims?

iv.

Is the Alignment regression a fundamental tension between evidence-driven repair and task fidelity — could a task-anchoring constraint in Ready(G) recover it?

v.

How sensitive is the CSR comparison to the choice of claim-extraction LLM? Would a shared, fixed claim set change the ranking?

vi.

Could a formal ablation isolate the Graph Inspector, rollback, and Hypothesis Filter on a frozen graph?
