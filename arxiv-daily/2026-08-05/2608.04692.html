Suppression Sticks, Locality Is Fragile: A Closed-Loop Target-and-Control Audit of Task-Vector Negation in VLA Policies

Key finding

Task-vector negation suppresses the target VLA skill — but silently preserves collapses unrelated skills to 52% control retention.

Locality is a cliff, not a floor. A target-only success metric scores all five edits as “successful” while control collapses to 4–52%.

02Why this matters

01

A closed-loop target-and-control audit, not a loss probe.

Built on MergeVLA: a multitask goal model θGoal trained on N skills, with per-skill LoRA experts (rank 64, seed 42). The task vector is the component-wise weight delta τi across three parts — VLM backbone (Qwen2.5-0.5B + dinosiglip, ~1.25B), action head (0.16B), proprio projector.

The edit is a single closed-form subtraction: data-free, gradient-free at edit time (<1s, one CPU tensor op). A pre-registered taxonomy then sorts every cell of the 10×10 matrix before any analysis — correcting the bias of prior static-loss / token-likelihood probes.

Subtracting τ0 → target 0% (control 100%). Adding the identical vector leaves target at 50% (control 90%). A norm-matched random direction achieves only ~25% reduction. The effect is direction-specific, not generic norm damage.
Relearning probe · masking, not deletion

Negated t0 recovers to 85% (17/20, Wilson [64,95]%) in 250 fine-tune steps; the task-naive base stays at 0% (0/20). Consistent with rapidly reversible behavioral masking, not durable weight-level unlearning.

03

What holds up — and where it frays.

+ Strengths
  • S1The right lens. Closed-loop target-and-control protocol with pre-registered thresholds is itself a methodological contribution, reusable beyond this paper.
  • S2Brutally honest reporting. Full 10×10 matrix, worst-control column, explicit post-hoc caveat, and a minimum-reporting-unit checklist (target x/n, every control x/n, baseline-normalized mean & worst control, selection/eval split).
  • S3Matched-panel baselines. NegGrad, NegGrad+, Gradient-Difference, retrain oracle, plus a norm-matched random-direction control confirming the effect is direction-specific.
  • S4Boundary honesty. Replication on three action-head families and four LIBERO suites — with the Spatial/Object/Long10 and multi-skill failures reported as carefully as the positive Goal result.
  • S5Relearning probe tests the claim. Directly pits masking vs. deletion; rapid recovery is a genuinely informative negative result for “certified unlearning” claims.
! Limitations & red flags
  • L1Single training seed for the full matrices. Only a three-seed t0/t1 probe is reported, with a run-dependent threshold. The 5/3/2 split is not claimed seed-robust.
  • L2Simulation only. LIBERO MuJoCo; no real-robot or non-LIBERO validation — a stated limit.
  • L3Needs a precomputed expert bank. “Data-free, gradient-free at edit time” is conditional on already having experts (~6–18 GPU-hr each). Negating an off-the-shelf multitask vector is broadly destructive.
  • L4Cross-suite failure. Sharpest claims are calibrated only on MergeVLA-Goal; Object & Long-horizon do not separate.
  • L5Thin baseline panel. E1 covers one target (t0), one seed, one budget per gradient method — descriptive cost–retention comparison, not an architecture-wide ranking. OpenVLA cells use only 10 rollouts and t3 is non-diagnostic.
  • L6No mechanism, only correlation. Diagnostics co-vary with outcomes but establish nothing; mean weight-cosine (0.011) fails to predict control survival; Alignment-Score threshold does not transfer across suites.
04

Open questions the audit leaves on the table.

Q01

Does the 5/3/2 regime persist across multiple seeds and on real-robot LIBERO transfers — or is the split seed- and simulator-specific?

Q02

Can a behavioral-overlap metric (shared trajectory / subgoal overlap) predict control survival where weight-cosine (0.011) fails — and explain why Spatial/Object/Long10 collapse?

Q03

Is there any multi-skill negation rule that preserves selectivity for k≥2 — or is single-skill selectivity a fundamental boundary of closed-form arithmetic?

Q04

How does negation compose with input-side efficiency methods (keyframe search, adaptive frame pruning) that also change the VLA's visual-token budget?

Q05

Can locate-then-edit (ROME-style layer surgery) recover selectivity, given no single depth band carries the edit at native norm — or does VLM distributed editing rule them out?

Q06

Does rapid relearning (85% by 250 steps) hold for safety-relevant / hazardous skills — where re-exposure cost is the risk the edit was meant to remove?
