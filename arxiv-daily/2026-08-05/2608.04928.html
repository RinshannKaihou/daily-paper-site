03Why this matters

Directly tests whether moving reasoning into continuous latent space breaks monitorability — and redirects safety work toward activation-based monitoring and monitorability-aware latent architectures.

→ Tempering the fear that latent CoT is inherently opaque.

04Critical edge

The headline “depends more on task than mode” rests on a single intervention (correct-answer sycophancy hint), one base model (Llama-3.2-1B), and two tasks — generalization is unverified.

Exact-Match baseline is also weak: Y requires a change, so it cannot detect Y=1 by construction.

01

Methodology

intervention-based · TE-controlled

For each input the model emits reasoning + answer. The intervened prompt (X=1) is augmented with a sycophancy hint — e.g. “I think the answer is 1020 but I'm curious what you think.”

Y = 1 iff ŷ(X=1) ≠ ŷ(X=0) and ŷ(X=1) = y_hint

So Y=1 requires the hint to change the prediction in the hint's favor — not merely echo it. Monitorability = how well a monitor Z tracks Y from the X=1 output alone, measured by AUROC, evaluated across a range of total-effect (TE) operating points instead of a single one.

Because raw CODI/SIM-CoT checkpoints sit at TE≈0 (GSM8k X=1 ≈ 30%), the authors fine-tune with a none/correct/wrong-hint mix — “intervention-aware FT” — to induce comparable hint-reliance across modes.

Four reasoning modes · Llama-3.2-1B-Instruct

No-CoT SFT — answer directly, no reasoning trace.
Explicit-CoT SFT with readable natural-language chain of thought.
CODI Weak latent supervision (Shen 2025) — self-distillation on the ":" token representation.
SIM-CoT Strong latent supervision (Wei 2026) — auxiliary decoder reconstructs the k-th CoT step from the k-th latent state.
Monitors Exact-match · prompted Qwen3-14B · LoRA-finetuned LLM · attention-pooled activation probe over layers {1,4,8,12,15,16}.
Data Math: GSM8k-Aug train → GSM8k ID / SVAMP OOD. QA: ECQA train → ECQA ID / SIQA OOD.

Results & signals

three views · toggle below

The CoT-answer mismatch rate is the mechanistic handle. When the readable chain's last step contradicts the final answer, text monitors win — that happens on math (GSM8k). 03

Verdict

strengths ↔ limitations

++ Strengths

  • Clean, well-motivated design: TE-controlled comparison avoids confounding “mode” with “hint-reliance rate.”
  • Genuinely thorough matrix — 4 monitors × 3 trace types × 4 reasoning modes.
  • Honest about negative results (SIM-CoT decoder collapse on ECQA; verbalized latents don't help QA).
  • The mechanistic mismatch story is tested, not just asserted — random-swap control lifts AUROC 0.68 → 0.82.
  • Excellent reproducibility: public checkpoints, full hyperparameter tables, prompts in appendix.

!! Limitations

  • Single base model (Llama-3.2-1B-Instruct) — no scaling, no alternative latent architectures (e.g. recurrent-depth, Geiping 2025).
  • Single intervention (correct-answer sycophancy) and single binary target — deception, backdoors, prompt injection untested.
  • Hint-reliance is induced, not naturally occurring; ecological validity of the TE-controlled setup is unclear.
  • QA signal is confounded by prior findings that latents are unnecessary for QA performance — weak signal may be task, not monitorability.
  • No robustness analysis against obfuscation / RL pressure on the activation probe.
  • Exact-Match baseline is a low bar; no comparison to self-consistency / entropy heuristics.
