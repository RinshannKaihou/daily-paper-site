> **一句话总结：** 该工作提出一种"错配缓存审计"(mismatched-cache audit)对 LatentMAS、C2C、KVComm 三个已发布系统的 KV-cache 中继做因果检验：在接收方确实需要发送方私有信息的校准任务上，对齐中继准确率达 100% 而错配仅 23–25%（差异达约 +75 分）；但在 GSM8K / ARC-Challenge / MedQA 等标准基准上，预注册的五种子等价性检验(TOST, ±2.8 分)表明 example-pairing 效应被统计上界定在系统自身宣称增益之下——即基准提升并不能单独证明"隐式思想"被真正传递。

> **TL;DR:** The paper audits three released latent-communication systems (LatentMAS, C2C, KVComm) by swapping the relayed KV cache for deranged (mismatched-example), zeroed, and moment-matched random counterparts. Where the receiver needs sender-private content, the aligned relay reaches 100% vs 23–25% for answer-irrelevant relays on Qwen3-8B (+~75 pts). On GSM8K, ARC-Challenge and MedQA, a pre-registered five-seed Holm-corrected TOST bounds the example-pairing effect within ±2.8 points (margin anchored to LatentMAS's own claimed 2.8% sequential gain); on Qwen3-8B the pairing delta is +0.00 (GSM8K) and −0.36 (ARC-C) points. Under the same test, LatentMAS's KV-prepend reads ceiling, KVComm's 30%-layer subset reads partial (~+9 pts pairing), and C2C's released projector reads no detected example-specific transfer.

## 核心贡献

- **接收方需求的双分离 (receiver-necessity dissociation)**：在三个模型家族、五个 checkpoint、三类任务表面（数学、选择题、prose 反事实 QA）上，同一缓存干预电池在读到需要发送方私有信息时打到 ceiling，在读到接收方可独立解决时被统计界定在 ±2.8 分之内。
- **提出"错配缓存审计"作为因果证据标准**：在 relay 边界上用 deranged（错配样本、保持边缘分布）、zeroed、moment-matched random 三种干预，配合一个程序化生成的 sender-private 校准仪器，分离"接收方冗余"与"通道失败"。
- **可移植性**：把同一审计移植到 C2C（学习型跨模型投影器）和 KVComm（层级选择中继）上，在同一信息不对称测试下分别读到 ceiling / 部分 / 无检测传递三种不同的内容传递水平。
- **预注册的等价性检验协议**：用种子级推断 + Holm 校正的 TOST，将 example-pairing 项绑定在系统自己宣称的 +2.8% 总体增益之下，使零结果成为"校准过的零"而非"哑仪器"。
- **公开校准仪器与审计 harness**：发布仪器生成器、审计代码与逐 cell 的运行清单（含 per-arm 原始账本与 GPU venue 表）。

## Key Contributions

- **A receiver-necessity dissociation.** The same cache-level intervention reads at-or-near-ceiling effect where the receiver needs the sender's private content, and a bounded/undetected pairing term where the receiver solves the task alone, across five checkpoints (Qwen3-4B/8B/14B, Mistral-Nemo-12B, phi-4) and three task surfaces.
- **A causal evidence standard and the audit methodology that operationalizes it.** Gains credited to transmitted content require a mismatched-content control; the audit combines cache-level interventions (derangement, zeroing, moment-matched randomization) with a procedurally generated sender-private calibration instrument that separates receiver redundancy from channel failure.
- **Portability of the audit to two further released latent-communication systems.** Under the same information-asymmetry test, LatentMAS's native KV-prepend, KVComm's layer subset, and C2C's learned projector read three distinct levels of detected content transfer (ceiling / partial / none detected).
- **A pre-registered equivalence protocol.** Holm-corrected TOST at a ±2.8-point margin anchored to the audited system's own reported 2.8% sequential gain turns a bare natural-regime nonrejection into an affirmative bound on the example-pairing term.
- **Released calibration instrument and audit harness** with per-cell run manifests and per-arm raw outcome ledgers.

## Methodology

The audit intervenes exactly at the relay boundary of the delivered LatentMAS pipeline (and ports to C2C and KVComm). Agents are instances of the same released checkpoint arranged sequentially; after the sender processes its context, its layer-wise key–value cache is extracted and prepended to the receiver's cache before generation. Every arm delivers a cache with identical geometry (same layer count, sequence length, dtype) to the same receiver under the same prompt, so any accuracy gap is attributable to cache content.

**Six intervention arms (per-cell pre-registers the subset):** *True* (paired-example cache); *Deranged* (cache of a different example in the same batch, a fixed-point-free permutation that preserves the marginal cache distribution exactly — the canonical content control); *Zero* (all entries zeroed); *Random* (Gaussian entries rescaled per example/layer/K/V tensor to match true cache moments); *Intact-no-private* (calibrated regime only — sender sees a format-matched but answer-irrelevant registry, isolating answer-relevant content from generic sender computation); *Direct-text reference* (sender-private info placed verbatim in the receiver prompt — a validity calibration, never a text-channel baseline).

**The primary estimand** is the pairing effect τ = E[Y | do(C := C_i)] − E[Y | do(C := C_{π(i)})], with an accounting identity τ_{TZ} = τ_{TI} + τ_{IZ} in the calibrated regime (and τ_{TZ} = τ_{TD} + τ_{DZ} naturally). Only the content terms (τ_{TI}, τ_{TD}) are the "latent thoughts" attribution under audit.

**Two regimes:**
- *Calibrated regime.* A procedurally generated registry of four entity–attribute bindings (e.g., "invyqqz → zevrlel"), sampled fresh at evaluation time so the bindings are absent from any training corpus; the sender observes the registry, the receiver only the query, so the receiver cannot solve the task without the relay. A reading far above control-arm accuracy proves the channel delivers answer-relevant private content.
- *Natural regime.* Standard benchmarks (GSM8K, ARC-Challenge training-split items; MedQA-USMLE test split) where the receiver could in principle solve alone. A frozen TOST rejects both one-sided nulls to give |τ| < δ; the random-arm collapse confirms the intervention point is causally coupled (a calibrated zero, not a dead instrument).

**Statistical conventions (frozen before unblinding).** n = 500 examples per arm per seed in a fixed 25×20 batch geometry; three receiver-sampling seeds (42/43/44), expanded to five only for the pre-registered natural-audit equivalence family; receiver decoding at the audited system's released sampling config (temp 0.6, top-p 0.95) — the sole seed-varying randomness; Holm step-down correction within each pre-registered family; equivalence margin ±2.8 points anchored to LatentMAS's reported aggregate sequential gain; seed (not example) as the unit of inference. All numbers recomputed from raw per-example records; frozen wholesale-seed replacement rule for incomplete grids.

## Results & Highlights

- **Calibrated regime, primary cell (Qwen3-8B):** the delivered aligned relay reaches 500/500 on all three seeds; the matched answer-irrelevant relay (intact-no-private) reaches 126/122/117 (23.4–25.2%); the paired true-minus-intact difference is **+74.8 / +75.6 / +76.6 points per seed**.
- **Family replication of the ceiling contrast:** true-minus-intact differences of **+75.6 to +78.8 points on Qwen3-4B and Qwen3-14B**, and **+58.2 to +63.4 on Mistral-Nemo-12B**; phi-4's true-minus-zero contrast ≈ **+1.00**, the strongest ceiling observed.
- **Receiver consumes relayed content even when it misleads:** under deranged relays, **64.2–64.8% of outputs match the donor example's label vs 20.8–23.4% under the matched answer-irrelevant control**.
- **Natural regime, all seven Qwen3 cells establish equivalence within ±2.8 points** (Holm-corrected TOST within each backbone's pre-registered family; exact over the primary backbone's three tasks, raw TOST p = .0018 and .0010 on GSM8K and ARC-Challenge). On Qwen3-8B the two-sided test rejects on neither task (GSM8K T−D = 0.00, ARC-C T−D = −0.36) and seed signs are mixed.
- **One small detected advantage inside the margin:** Qwen3-4B GSM8K, T−D = **+1.52 points** (90% CI [+1.08, +1.96]), about half the operationalized ±2.8-point margin.
- **A large cache effect need not be a pairing effect (MedQA 8B):** true-minus-zero = **+14.7 points**, but true-minus-deranged (the pairing term) only **+0.4 points** (90% CI [−1.34, +2.14]).
- **Random caches collapse accuracy** in every natural cell (e.g., 8B: ≤2/500 per seed on both GSM8K and ARC-Challenge; 4B: 0/500 on ARC), confirming the intervention reaches the receiver's computation.
- **C2C (Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct → Qwen3-8B, released projector):** no detected example-specific transfer — true 114/114/117, deranged 104/111/126, true-minus-deranged **+2.0 / +0.6 / −1.8** points (sign-mixed); all cache arms sit only ~6% above the zero arm.
- **KVComm (Qwen3-8B port, 11 of 36 effective layers):** detected but partial transfer — true 165/153/156 vs deranged 111/117/111; true-minus-deranged **+10.8 / +7.2 / +9.0 points**, roughly a tenth of LatentMAS's ~+75-point ceiling effect on the same construction.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty.** The contribution is not a new relay but a measurement: extending activation-patching logic from model internals to the inter-agent communication boundary while preserving the cache distribution. The deranged arm (a marginal-preserving fixed-point-free permutation) is the load-bearing design choice and is genuinely novel as an audit primitive for latent communication. The dissociation between "large cache effect" and "pairing effect" (MedQA: +14.7 true-minus-zero vs +0.4 true-minus-deranged) is the cleanest single result and is not something any of the audited systems' own ablations could have produced.
- **Rigor.** Pre-registered, Holm-corrected, seed-level TOST with a margin anchored to the system's own claimed gain is a meaningfully higher bar than typical benchmark-delta comparisons. The choice of seed (not example) as the inferential unit is correctly motivated — it reduces power and makes equivalence *harder* to establish, not easier — and the authors disclose that this is exactly what costs Mistral-Nemo-12B its equivalence verdict.
- **Specific weaknesses.**
  - *Training-split items for GSM8K and ARC-Challenge.* The natural-regime cells use fixed training-split items (MedQA uses test). Memorization would inflate receiver redundancy and push *toward* the null the bound reports; the authors flag this and partially address it with a replication on the official GSM8K test split (seed-mean T−D = −0.64, signs mixed), which weakens but does not eliminate the concern.
  - *Interpretability-limited cell.* Mistral-Nemo-12B on ARC-Challenge sits at 13.44%, below four-choice chance, because parse failures are scored wrong under the frozen convention. Within-cell contrasts are still valid (parser is uniform across arms), but absolute levels cannot be compared across papers — and this is precisely one of the two cells where equivalence is not established, which limits how strongly the second-family null can be read.
  - *Single registry construction.* The calibrated ceiling rests on one generator (four bindings, seven-letter strings). The prose-document-QA stress test on Qwen3-8B partially addresses surface-form generality (true-vs-intact lower bound 0.918) but is still procedurally generated; the authors explicitly make no claim about human-authored documents.
  - *Porting changes that could move numbers.* The KVComm port replaces evaluation-prefix calibration with a pre-specified disjoint 100-example split, and layer selection is calibrated once under the true-private condition — so KVComm effects are conditional on a task-matched selector and do not establish task-agnostic layer selection. KVComm is also not crossed with a matched receiver-sufficient condition, so no within-KVComm necessity-by-relay interaction is identified. C2C is audited in only its released Qwen2.5-7B → Qwen3-8B configuration.
  - *Margin anchor choice.* The ±2.8-point margin uses the system's aggregate claimed gain because per-task figures are variance-free point estimates and some are negative (e.g., −1.6 on 4B GSM8K, −0.2 on 8B ARC-C), which would yield no margin at all. This is defensible but conveniently the most favorable anchor available; on Qwen3-8B GSM8K the pairing CI [−0.98, +0.98] actually excludes the system's tabulated +1.5-point per-task gain.
  - *Portability boundary, not result.* Llama-3.2-3B could not consume the relay in either delivered realignment configuration, so the audit's precondition of a functioning channel was not met — meaning the portability claim covers a narrower family than it might appear.
- **Reproducibility.** Strong on paper: harness, calibration-instrument generator, per-cell run manifests with per-arm raw ledgers, disclosed GPU venues and pinned software stack, and a disclosed run-deviation record (including the wholesale seed-46 → seed-47 replacement on Qwen3-8B GSM8K/ARC-C). The frozen replacement rule and "no post-hoc arm acceptance" commitment are stated.
- **Significance.** The paper does not kill latent communication — it explicitly rules out the reading that relayed caches carry nothing (the calibrated ceiling is unambiguous). What it does is raise the evidence bar: a benchmark delta alone cannot establish that the relayed cache transmits example-specific content the receiver uses, and builders should demand a mismatched-cache control. This is a meaningful methodological contribution for the LatentMAS / C2C / KVComm / HyLaT line of work.
- **Red flags.** None fatal. The most important thing to watch is scope: the audit bounds the *example-pairing* value of delivered relays in *one released configuration each*, and explicitly issues no latent-vs-text verdict. Headlines claiming "latent communication doesn't work" would overread this paper; headlines claiming "LatentMAS's standard-benchmark gains are not by themselves evidence of latent-thought transmission" would read it correctly.

## Strengths

- Clean dissociation between generic cache-interface value and example-pairing value, operationalized via an accounting identity over arm contrasts.
- Pre-registered, Holm-corrected equivalence protocol with a margin anchored to the audited system's own claim — turns nulls into quantitative bounds.
- Cross-family, cross-checkpoint, cross-surface replication of the calibrated ceiling (Qwen3-4B/8B/14B, Mistral-Nemo-12B, phi-4; registry lines and prose paragraphs).
- Honest scoring of the Mistral-Nemo-12B cell exactly as the pre-registered rule scores it (equivalence not established), rather than reframing it as a near miss.
- Same instrument ported to two further released systems, producing three qualitatively distinct readings (ceiling / partial / none) under one test.
- Public release of harness, calibration generator, and per-cell raw ledgers.

## Limitations

- External systems audited in one released configuration each; retrained or retuned variants could behave differently.
- The calibrated regime uses one registry construction; other sender-private constructions could modulate the ceiling (the prose cell partially addresses this).
- Absolute accuracy levels in some cells are interpretability-limited (notably Nemo-12B ARC-Challenge below four-choice chance due to parse failures scored wrong) and should not be compared across papers; only within-cell contrasts remain valid.
- KVComm port changes calibration mechanics and is not crossed with a receiver-sufficient condition, so it cannot identify a within-KVComm necessity-by-relay interaction.
- Llama-3.2-3B is a portability boundary (receiver could not consume the relay), not an audit result, so the portability claim is narrower than it might appear.
- The audit bounds the example-pairing value of delivered relays and explicitly issues no latent-versus-text verdict.

## Relevance

对研究多智能体 LLM 通信与表征对齐的工作高度相关：它直接审视"中继 KV cache 等价于传递隐式思想"这一被 LatentMAS 等系统作为信息保持定理提出的因果主张，并给出可复用的"错配缓存审计"协议。对于关心智能体间信息流、激活/表示迁移机制可解释性、以及 LLM 评测方法论（等价性检验、因果干预）的研究者，这是值得纳入工具箱的评测范式。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the natural-regime equivalence hold on benchmarks where the receiver genuinely cannot solve alone without being a synthetic lookup task — e.g., long-context multi-hop reasoning or private-tool-use traces — and where would the boundary between "ceiling" and "bounded" actually fall?
- How sensitive is the C2C "no detected transfer" reading to projector capacity, training data scale, or a different sender/receiver pair, and can a mismatched-cache audit be used as a training signal for the projector itself?
- Can the deranged-arm protocol be combined with mechanistic localization (which layers/heads carry the donor's registry label) to explain *why* KVComm's 30%-layer subset recovers only ~1/10 of the full-cache ceiling effect?
- Would extending the audit to hybrid latent-text protocols (e.g., HyLaT) and compressed relays (e.g., Honavar & GVSL's QKVShare) reproduce the same ceiling / partial / none taxonomy, or does compression change which term of the accounting identity dominates?
- Is there a principled way to set an equivalence margin when the audited system reports *per-task* gains that are negative or variance-free, other than falling back to the aggregate — and how would the verdicts change under a region-of-practical-equivalence derived from human judge agreement rather than from the system's own claim?
