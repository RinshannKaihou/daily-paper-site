Towards Physics of Multimodal Pretraining: Knowledge Flow, Modality Synergy, Early Unification, and Recipes

Key finding

Cross-modal knowledge flow is asymmetric: language and visual understanding drive visual generation — but generation barely transfers back.

Junlin Han · Shengbang Tong · David Fan · Minghao Chen · Philip Torr · Filippos Kokkinos · Mike Lewis
FAIR Meta · Reality Labs · University of Oxford
multimodal-pretraining unified-models early-fusion knowledge-flow mixture-of-experts scaling-laws
Key metric
5×
fewer generation tokens, yet GenEval 0.482 vs 0.467 and DPG 0.689 vs 0.676 against the balanced L50/U25/G25 baseline — validated at 2T tokens / 13.5B MoE.
Why this matters
Mechanistic guidance on data mixing, parameter sharing, and training scheduling — immediately actionable for next-generation unified understanding + generation models.
Critical edge
The “5% generation” win quietly trades pixel fidelity — a small diffusion-loss and FID regression (5.234 vs 5.131). The authors themselves flag a later generative-midtraining stage. Not a free lunch.
01 / METHOD

Bottom-up empirical dissection

A systematic study of unified Transfusion pretraining — discrete next-token prediction for text plus continuous flow-matching for image generation, fused in a single Llama-3-style decoder — stress-tested across synthetic CLEVR and web-scale real data.

  • Backbone1.5B model (2.3B params w/ split FFNs), 16 layers, GQA 32Q/8KV, SwiGLU, RoPE, pre-RMSNorm, QK-norm, 4096 ctx, bf16 under FSDP-2.
  • ObjectiveDiffusion flow-matching loss up-weighted 3× vs text CE; x-prediction with on-the-fly velocity conversion; log-normal timestep sampling; 25-step Euler, CFG=5.0.
  • TokenizersFour families tested for generality: RAE (SigLIP-2 ViT-400m/14, default), Raw Pixels, CLIP+VAE, AR UniTok.
  • DataDCLM (text), ~350M Shutterstock pairs (SSTK), and an extended-CLEVR corpus (~1M scenes) for controlled per-concept ablation via regex filters.
  • Eval11 LM benchmarks; 16 VQA benchmarks (General/Knowledge/OCR&Chart/Vision-Centric) after Cambrian-7M SFT; GenEval, DPG, CLIP-sim, held-out diffusion loss, FID@50k.
  • Scale run13.5B MoE (1.5B active, 256 experts, top-16 routing, 2 fixed modality experts) trained on 2T tokens, single-variable ablations.

03 / CONTRIBUTIONS

Four load-bearing claims

01

Asymmetric knowledge flow

非对称的知识流动规律

Language is a universal booster for all visual tasks; visual understanding is a strong prior for generation; generation yields near-zero backward transfer.

语言是所有视觉任务的通用增强器，视觉理解是视觉生成的强先验，而视觉生成对语言/理解几乎无反向迁移，且迁移是“概念相关”的。

02

Locating synergy vs. competition

协同与竞争的机制定位

Task complexity decides synergy-or-competition; inside the Transformer, shared attention + norm promote synergy, decoupled FFNs isolate competition — across 4 tokenizers.

任务复杂度决定模态间协同或竞争；共享注意力与归一化层促进协同，解耦 FFN 隔离竞争，且跨 RAE/Raw Pixels/CLIP+VAE/AR 四种 tokenizer 通用。

03

Early unification & “vision laziness”

早期统一与“视觉惰性”

Delaying vision systematically harms it. Four independent probes show the vision pathway goes progressively “quiet” the longer the language warm-up runs.

延迟引入视觉会系统性损害视觉能力；四条独立机制性测量（激活 L2、嵌入范数、对图像 token 的注意力比例）显示语言预热越长，视觉通路越“沉默”。

04

Asymmetric recipe, validated at scale

高效配方与大规模验证

Grid search yields L70/U25/G5; on 13.5B MoE / 2T tokens it matches the balanced recipe on generation with 5% of the generative compute budget, with stronger language + understanding.

导出极端非对称配比 L70/U25/G5；在 13.5B MoE / 2T tokens 上验证，仅用 5% 生成 token 即获得与均衡配方相当的生成质量，语言与理解指标全面更优。

04 / ASSESSMENT

Strengths & limitations

Strengths

  • Systematic & mechanistic. Replaces heuristic arguments with controlled, single-variable evidence at multiple scales.
  • CLEVR concept-ablation converts “language helps vision” correlations into causal per-concept statements — a reusable diagnostic tool.
  • Four-axis “vision laziness” diagnosis is a concrete mechanistic story for why late-fusion underperforms.
  • Immediately deployable recipe (L70/U25/G5 + MoE + early unification) with a strong efficiency story.
  • Honest about the minor fidelity/efficiency trade-off in the generation results.

Limitations

  • Static images only. Video, audio, 3D are left to future work despite an “omnimodal” conclusion.
  • Validated only at 13.5B. Behavior at frontier (>1T param) scales is unverified; bidirectional flow is speculation.
  • Proprietary Shutterstock data limits reproducibility for academic groups; weights/code not stated to be released.
  • Not a free lunch: the 5% generation win carries a measurable diffusion-loss + FID regression; a later generative-midtraining stage is recommended.
  • Some baselines favor the design (3.5B dense vs 13.5B MoE; 60% late-fusion cutoff); several numbers are read off bar charts, not tabulated.
  • CLEVR concept vocabulary is far simpler than natural-image semantics; how cleanly it maps to real concepts is unclear.
05 / OPEN

Questions for further exploration

Q1

Does L70/U25/G5 hold or invert once video and audio bring their own understanding + generation complexity profiles?

Q2

Would a moderate late-fusion schedule (20–30% rather than 60%) erase the vision-laziness gap, and does optimal warm-up scale with model size?

Q3

At frontier (>1T) scales, does visual generation finally give backward transfer to understanding/language — detectable with the same four probes?

Q4

Can the CLEVR concept-ablation methodology extend to natural images via controlled synthetic scene editing?

Q5

Is the shared-attention / decoupled-FFN conclusion stable under alternative routing (soft merging, expert choice) vs. top-16 MoE?

Q6

Does “vision laziness” also explain production MLLM hallucination/blindness failures — and can early-unification correct them via continued pretraining?
