Methodology & construction

A pure theory paper: nonsmooth analysis + Fourier analysis on the cyclic group Cp = Z/pZ. No experiments. A single-neuron ReLU network has parameters u, v, w ∈ R^{C_p}; gate z_{a,b} = u(a)+v(b), activation h = ReLU(z). Training minimizes full-table cross-entropy with no weight decay, formulated as the Clarke differential inclusion θ̇(t) ∈ −∂_C L(θ(t)).

Plant one highly symmetric seed so exactly one table entry — (0,0) — is active, every other gate inactive. Then argue the dynamics die or slide.

The construction (Thm 1.5): set u(0)=v(0)=ε/2, u(a)=v(a)=−B for a≠0, w(0)=−A, rest zero, with B > ε/2, A² > pε²/(2(p−1)). On this single-active-gate chamber the dynamics reduce to a smooth 4D ODE. A conserved quantity g² − p·s²/(2(p−1)) = Γ > 0 forces ė < 0, so the central gate hits zero transversely in finite time. Picking Clarke slope ReLU′(0)=0 freezes every parameter.

At the frozen endpoint all three centered parameter blocks are scalar multiples of the centered point mass q = e₀ − 𝟙/p, whose Fourier transform has q̂(ζ) = 1/√p for ζ≠0 — hence each real class carries exactly 2/(p−1) of the energy. A transversality + implicit-function-theorem argument upgrades the single seed to an open neighborhood U, and since Gaussians charge every open set, the failure has positive conditional probability.

Strengths & limitations

Strengths 5

  • Crisp, elementary main proof: single active gate → finite-time death → centered-point-mass limit → flat spectrum. Memorable mechanism chain.
  • Honest scoping: explicitly states the open question for softplus and non-vanilla optimizers rather than overclaiming.
  • Appendix removes the obvious "you cherry-picked the Clarke selection" objection — every trajectory, ReLU′(0)=0, smooth dead-zone, discrete GD.
  • Transparent AI-tool disclosure per the Leiden Declaration, with the underlying note cited as [GPT26].
  • Cleanly situated against the strongest prior theorems: Morwani et al.'s m ≥ 4(p−1) margin result provably excludes the m=1 case studied here.

Limitations 5

  • Both mechanisms exploit ReLU's exact dead half-line; does not extend to softplus. Central smooth-activation question unresolved.
  • GD result (Thm A.4) is vanilla full-batch only — momentum, Adam, weight decay, minibatches out of scope; weight decay is standard in real grokking.
  • Highly symmetric construction (single active gate). Proves positive-probability failure, not measure-1 — "typical" inits unaddressed.
  • No numerical experiments illustrate trajectories or check robustness to discretization noise.
  • Appendix's strengthened results are AI-drafted; local semiconvexity of the summed loss warrants independent verification.
04

Open questions

Q1 · smooth activations

Does single-frequency alignment hold for softplus?

The dying-ReLU mechanism is unavailable for everywhere-positive activations. The paper leaves this explicitly open.

Q2 · practical optimizers

What happens with weight decay, Adam, minibatch SGD?

The GD result is restricted to vanilla full-batch; weight decay is ubiquitous in real grokking experiments.

Q3 · typicality

Is the failure measure-typical or a thin symmetric island?

A numerical study over random Gaussian inits for small p (5, 7) would clarify practical relevance.

Q4 · grokking link

Does the sliding trajectory explain the memorization phase?

Connect loss → (p²−1)/p²·log p to wider networks; does its flat spectrum predict a pre-grokking non-emergent pattern?

Q5 · S_3 extension

Does the strategy extend to the symmetric group S_3?

The author speculates (Remark 1.7) it may transfer to the S_3 purity problem of [Cla26c].

Q6 · verification

Can the AI-drafted uniqueness proof be replaced?

Independent check of local semiconvexity + Gronwall uniqueness on the sliding face (Prop. A.16) is needed.
