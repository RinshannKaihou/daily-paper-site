> **一句话总结：** 通过 3 个指令微调模型 × 5 个推理框架 × 6 个基准 × 4 种生成模式的全因子实验，本文证明即使在使用贪心解码（无采样噪声）的情况下，仅切换推理后端（HuggingFace / vLLM / Ollama / LangChain）即可显著改变 LLM 基准得分，结构性后端效应约占"开箱即用"方差的 39%，且该效应在事实型任务上比社会偏见任务更显著、强依赖于模型家族。

> **TL;DR:** In a fully-crossed study (3 instruction-tuned models × 5 inference backends × 6 benchmarks × 4 generation modes) on a single RTX 3090 in fp16, the authors show that the serving framework is a genuine, model-dependent source of benchmark variance: under greedy decoding on MMLU/TriviaQA/TruthfulQA/BBQ/StereoSet, swapping from HuggingFace `generate()` to Ollama yields a mean calibrated divergence of up to 0.055 for Llama-3.2-1B (vs. ≤0.010 for Gemma-3-1B), the structural backend effect alone accounts for ~39% of the variance seen under default settings (0.00074 vs. 0.00190), and Llama-3.2-1B hits "severe" (>5% flipped items) on 14 of 16 backend×mode cells for MMLU/TriviaQA/TruthfulQA/BBQ — leading the authors to recommend mandatory disclosure of backend, version, and full generation config plus greedy decoding for cross-backend comparisons.

## 核心贡献

- **构建了一个完全可复现的全因子测试床**：在固定硬件（单卡 RTX 3090，fp16，无量化）、固定权重精度和评测协议的前提下，系统变化推理后端（hf_raw、hf_pipeline、langchain_hf、vllm、ollama）、生成模式（Deterministic / Fix / Token=256 / Default）、基准与模型家族四个维度，每模型约 7.2M 次生成。
- **证明后端效应在无采样噪声时依然存在**：即使在贪心解码下，仅切换推理栈也会改变模型答对/答错的具体题目集合，说明这是结构性效应而非采样噪声，且强依赖于模型家族。
- **量化了后端对总方差的贡献**：通过逐步累加方差源的分解，结构性后端效应约占"开箱即用"（Default）设定下方差的 ~39%，其余 61% 来自采样噪声与各框架的默认生成参数——后者通过披露并匹配生成配置即可消除。
- **揭示任务家族与模型家族的交互**：后端效应对事实型基准（MMLU、TriviaQA、TruthfulQA、TruthfulQA-Gen）比对社会偏见基准（BBQ、StereoSet）更显著（12 个 model-mode 配置中分别有 7 个 vs. 4 个出现显著背离），Llama 最敏感、Gemma 最稳健。
- **提出可操作的报告标准建议**：要求披露后端名称及版本、完整生成配置；跨后端比较时强制使用贪心（确定性）解码；对偏见基准同时报告格式合规率以分离偏见方向与格式遵从性。

## Key Contributions

- A **fully-crossed, reproducible test-bed** (3 instruction-tuned models × 5 inference backends × 6 benchmarks × 4 generation modes) that isolates the inference backend as the sole variable, holding weight precision, GPU type (RTX 3090), CUDA version, and evaluation protocol constant (~7.2M generations per model).
- An empirical demonstration that **the backend is a genuine, structural source of score variance even under greedy decoding** — i.e. with sampling noise removed, swapping the serving stack still changes which individual items a model answers correctly.
- A **variance decomposition** showing the structural backend effect (Deterministic mode, 0.00074) accounts for roughly 39% of the variance a practitioner sees out-of-the-box under Default settings (0.00190), with the remaining 61% attributable to sampling noise and each framework's own default generation hyper-parameters — both of which are avoidable through disclosure and parameter matching.
- A **task-family × model-family interaction analysis** showing backend effects are more pronounced on factual benchmarks (significant in 7/12 model-mode configurations) than on social-bias benchmarks (4/12), with Llama-3.2-1B-INST the most sensitive, Gemma-3-1B-IT the most robust, and Qwen2.5-1.5B-INST task-dependent.
- A **minimum reporting recommendation**: disclose backend name and exact version, the complete generation configuration, use greedy decoding for any cross-backend comparison, and report format-compliance alongside bias scores.

## Methodology

**Controlled four-factor design.** The authors fix weight precision (fp16, no quantization), hardware (single NVIDIA RTX 3090, 24 GiB, CUDA 12.6, PyTorch 2.6), and evaluation protocol, then vary:

- **Models (3)** at the ~1B scale: Llama-3.2-1B-Instruct (Meta), Gemma-3-1B-IT (Google), Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct (Alibaba). The 1B scale is chosen to make the exhaustive factorial tractable.
- **Inference backends (5)**, ordered by separation from raw PyTorch: `hf_raw` (reference, `AutoModelForCausalLM.generate()` with `apply_chat_template`), `hf_pipeline` (shares the in-process model object, isolating the pipeline abstraction), `langchain_hf` (adds LangChain's HuggingFacePipeline prompt/orchestration layer), `vllm` (v0.8.5 V0 engine, separate CUDA context, PagedAttention, independent decoding implementation), `ollama` (standalone OS process backed by llama.cpp + GGUF + own tokenizer/runtime). The four HF-ecosystem backends load identical fp16 SafeTensors; Ollama loads a fp16 GGUF variant, so its observed differences mix serving-stack behavior with weight-representation artifacts — explicitly framed as part of "the backend cost" rather than a confound.
- **Generation modes (4)** that progressively add variability: *Deterministic* (greedy, temperature=0, do_sample=False, seed=42, max_new_tokens=256 — isolates the structural backend effect); *Fix* (sampling enabled but parameters matched: temp=0.7, top_p=0.9, top_k=50, rep_penalty=1.1 — adds sampling noise); *Token=256* (only max_new_tokens standardized, all else at framework defaults — adds default-hyperparameter effects); *Default* (each backend's own generation configuration — least controlled, most realistic). Five repetitions are collected for all stochastic modes; Deterministic collects one run.
- **Benchmarks (6)** spanning two task families: factual (MMLU 14,042 MC questions; TriviaQA 11,313 QA with exact_match + token_f1; TruthfulQA MC1 817 adversarial questions; TruthfulQA-Gen free-form scored by ROUGE-L against curated answers) and social bias (BBQ 58,492 questions across 11 demographic categories with accuracy, disambig_accuracy, ambig_unknown_rate, ambig_bias_score; StereoSet 4,229 items adapted to an A/B/C shuffled generation format, with LMS, SS, ICAT, invalid_rate). StereoSet's original fine-tuned classifier is intentionally not used because it is not backend-agnostic.

**Metrics.** All 14 metrics are calibrated to a common [0, 1] scale where 1 is best. For each (model, backend, benchmark, mode) cell they report mean divergence |Δ| vs. the `hf_raw` baseline, disagreement rate (fraction of binary items where backends differ), severity (disagreement > 5% of items), backend-induced within-cell variance σ² averaged over (model, benchmark) pairs, and significance via McNemar (binary metrics) / Wilcoxon (continuous) with Benjamini–Hochberg FDR control (α=0.05) and a Friedman omnibus test across the five backends. Per-question agreement is further analyzed with Fleiss κ and Cohen κ against `hf_raw`.

## Results & Highlights

- **Structural backend effect is non-negligible even under greedy decoding (Table 1, Deterministic mode).** Mean |Δ| from `hf_raw` averaged over 14 metrics: Llama-3.2-1B-INST diverges by 0.049 for langchain_hf (6/11 metrics BH-significant) and 0.055 for ollama (9/11 significant); Gemma-3-1B-IT stays ≤0.010 across all backends (0/11 significant); Qwen2.5-1.5B-INST diverges by 0.025 for vllm (10/11 significant), attributed to vLLM's PagedAttention kernel under non-associative floating-point arithmetic, with effect size |r| = 0.38. `hf_pipeline` is exactly 0.000 by construction (shares the model object).
- **Backend accounts for ~39% of out-of-the-box variance (Table 3).** Backend-induced within-cell variance rises from 0.00074 (Deterministic, structural only) to 0.00112 (Fix, +sampling noise) to 0.00118 (Token=256) to 0.00190 (Default, +framework default hyper-parameters); the structural component alone is ~39% of the Default variance.
- **Effect is large enough to flip evaluation conclusions (Table 4, "severe" = >5% items flipped).** Llama-3.2-1B-INST is severely affected on 14/16 backend×mode configurations for MMLU, TriviaQA, TruthfulQA, and BBQ, 8/16 for TruthfulQA-Gen, 9/16 for StereoSet; per-item disagreement reaches ~48% on StereoSet and ~40% on TruthfulQA. By contrast, Gemma-3-1B-IT is severe on at most 4/16 configurations on any task (0 on TriviaQA) and its disagreement never exceeds 13%.
- **Significant divergence grows sharply once framework defaults enter (Table 2).** Out of 33 primary-metric comparisons (3 models × 11 metrics), ollama diverges significantly from `hf_raw` in 20 (Deterministic) → 23 (Fix) → 23 (Token=256) → 27 (Default); vllm grows from 10 → 12 → 19 → 22. Under Default mode, all backends except `hf_pipeline` diverge on the majority of metrics.
- **Factual tasks are more backend-sensitive than bias tasks (Table 5).** Across 12 model-mode configurations, factual benchmarks show significant divergence in 7 cases vs. 4 for bias benchmarks; on Llama, factual |Δ| under Default reaches 0.0789 while bias stays at 0.0380.
- **Inter-backend agreement corroborates the model ranking (Cohen κ).** Agreement with `hf_raw` averages 0.84 for `hf_pipeline` but drops to 0.45 for ollama on Llama-3.2-1B-INST, vs. 0.79 on Gemma-3-1B-IT — the same Llama-most-sensitive ordering appears in error-set overlap and score-space clustering analyses.

## Authors & Credibility

All four authors (Shahed Masoudian, Passant Elchafei, Monorama Swain, Markus Schedl) are affiliated with Johannes Kepler University Linz. The group has a track record in NLP/IR and recsys-style empirical work (Schedl is well known for the MediaEval/MIREX evaluation tradition). The methodology is unusually disciplined for a reproducibility study: a single fixed GPU, fixed precision, fixed seed, fully-crossed factorial design, automatic FDR control (Benjamini–Hochberg), McNemar/Wilcoxon paired tests, and pre-registered-style RQs. The code/analysis repository is promised "upon acceptance," which weakens the immediate reproducibility claim until public release.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty (moderate):** The broad claim that "serving framework matters" overlaps heavily with the closely-related and contemporaneous Pape et al. (2026, "The silent hyperparameter"), which the authors themselves cite and against which they position this work. The genuine delta is the addition of the **generation-mode axis** and the **task-family split (factual vs. bias)** with variance decomposition — but the headline 39% figure is a sequential additive attribution, explicitly not an exact causal decomposition, and interactions between backend and sampling hyper-parameters are left unquantified.
- **Rigor (strong but bounded):** Statistical machinery is solid (paired McNemar/Wilcoxon + BH-FDR + Friedman omnibus + Fleiss/Cohen κ). However, **Deterministic mode rests on a single greedy run** whose within-environment reproducibility is assumed, not tested; and the stochastic modes use only **5 repetitions**, which the authors concede "bounds the precision of our sampling-noise and variance estimates." The "severe > 5%" threshold is borrowed from prior work (Mekala et al. 2025; Sclar et al. 2024) but is still an arbitrary convention.
- **Reproducibility (mixed):** Hardware/software stack is fixed and disclosed (RTX 3090, CUDA 12.6, PyTorch 2.6, transformers 4.57, vLLM 0.8.5, Ollama 0.31.1), and a single CUDA/driver version is used — yet because floating-point non-associativity is hardware-dependent, the authors warn the absolute magnitudes "may differ slightly on other accelerators." Critically, **code is only promised on acceptance**, so the result cannot yet be independently reproduced, and the StereoSet adaptation to A/B/C format means its numbers are **not directly comparable to the StereoSet leaderboard**.
- **Significance (high for evaluation practice, low for model design):** The takeaway is a methodological one — benchmark scores are jointly a property of model + backend + generation config, not of the model alone. This is an important procedural correction for the community, but it does not change how models should be built.
- **Red flags / data quirks:** (1) Ollama's effects are a confounded blend of GGUF weight conversion, template handling, and tokenizer differences that the authors deliberately do **not** disentangle, so a reader cannot attribute Ollama divergence to any single cause. (2) StereoSet's composite ICAT/SS score "conflates format compliance with bias direction," which the authors report but cannot separate. (3) Only the ~1B parameter scale is studied; the scaling question is explicitly punted to Pape et al. (2026). (4) All bias benchmarks are US/English-centric (BBQ, StereoSet), limiting generalization of the "bias is less affected" claim. (5) No code/math/multi-turn/agentic tasks are evaluated, where output-length and sampling effects could compound differently.

## Strengths

- Disciplined fully-crossed factorial design with hardware, precision, and seed held constant — an exemplary setup for isolating one nuisance factor.
- Clean separation of variance sources via the four generation modes, turning a vague reproducibility worry into a quantified 39% / 61% structural-vs-avoidable split.
- Honest framing of confounds (Ollama's GGUF weights, StereoSet's format-vs-bias conflation, single-GPU generalizability) rather than hiding them.
- Practical, low-cost recommendation (disclose backend+version+generation config; use greedy for cross-backend comparison; report format-compliance on bias benchmarks).
- Complementary agreement analyses (Cohen/Fleiss κ, error-set overlap, score-space clustering) that triangulate the same model-ranking conclusion.

## Limitations

- Only the ~1B parameter scale is evaluated; scaling behavior is not investigated and is deferred to Pape et al. (2026).
- Single GPU (RTX 3090) and single CUDA/driver version — absolute magnitudes may not transfer to other accelerators or precisions.
- Deterministic mode uses a single greedy run; stochastic modes use only 5 repetitions, limiting the precision of the variance decomposition.
- Benchmarks are English-only and dominated by multiple-choice and short-form generation; no long-form, code, math reasoning, multi-turn, or agentic tasks.
- Quantization (a likely large source of cross-backend disagreement) is held constant by using fp16 everywhere, so the paper cannot speak to INT4/INT8 deployment divergence.
- The generation-mode axis samples only a few points; temperature, top-p, top-k, repetition penalty, batch size, attention backend, KV-cache settings, and random seed are not swept.
- Code and full analysis artifacts are promised only upon acceptance, not yet public.

## Relevance

高度相关于 LLM 评测可复现性与基准有效性这一研究主题：本文直接量化了推理后端（HuggingFace / vLLM / Ollama / LangChain）作为"隐性超参数"对基准分数的影响，并为评测报告标准提供了可立即采纳的建议，正好契合关注 LLM evaluation、reproducibility 与 benchmark validity 的读者群。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How does the structural backend effect scale beyond 1B parameters — does the ~39% share hold, shrink, or grow for 7B/70B models where kernel-selection and batching differences are amplified?
- Would an exact-cause decomposition for Ollama (running llama.cpp on the same fp16 SafeTensors rather than GGUF) isolate weight-conversion effects from template/tokenizer effects?
- Does the factual-vs-bias divergence asymmetry persist on multilingual bias benchmarks (e.g., CrowS-Pairs in other languages) or on code/math benchmarks (HumanEval, GSM8K) where longer decoding trajectories give backends more room to diverge?
- How much of the vLLM-specific Qwen divergence (10/11 metrics significant, |r|=0.38) survives when forcing a single attention backend and fixed batch size of 1?
- Can the variance decomposition be turned into an exact causal attribution (e.g., via ANOVA-style orthogonal decomposition) instead of the current sequential additive approximation?
- Does matching generation config across backends fully close the gap, or do residual structural differences (chat-template rendering, tokenization edge cases) remain statistically detectable even after config matching?
