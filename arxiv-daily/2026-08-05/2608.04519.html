Leak-Resistant Unlearning: A New Benchmark for Evaluating Multi-Hop Reasoning Consistency and Recovery Robustness

Haoting Qian · Qingjie Zhang · Zhicong Huang · Cheng Hong · Han Qiu Aug 5, 2026 Tsinghua University × Ant Group
The finding

Current unlearning methods leak knowledge along non-chain multi-hop paths — and stay recoverable — exposing an impossible triangle.

Six logic-typed reasoning structures × three recovery attacks, run across 3 LLMs and 6 unlearning methods, show no method simultaneously achieves forget quality, robustness, and utility.

Key metric · Forget Quality swing
63.5 → 27.6

On Llama-3.1-8B with Task Vector, Forget Quality scores 63.5 under Hypothetical Syllogism but collapses to 27.6 under Modus Tollens — a ~36-point swing proving high chain-style scores don't imply robust unlearning.

Why this matters

A stricter multi-hop-reasoning + recovery-attack evaluation protocol for building robust unlearning — and for detecting residual sensitive knowledge buried inside deployed LLMs.

Critical edge

Evaluation is open-weight only (Llama-3.1-8B, Qwen3-14B/32B). Conclusions do not transfer to black-box commercial API threat models.

§01

Methodology

An unlearning procedure is leak-resistant iff (1) Path Consistency holds — target knowledge stays inaccessible across six reasoning structures — and (2) Recovery Robustness holds — the knowledge resists three post-hoc recovery attacks. Leak-resistance is an assessment target, not a training objective.

HS
Hypothetical Syllogism
(A→B) ∧ (B→C) ⊢ (A→C)
Transitive chains via object–subject chaining.
MP
Modus Ponens
A→B ∧ A ⊢ B
Rule instantiation with semantically matched relations.
MT
Modus Tollens
(A→B) ∧ ¬B ⊢ ¬A
Backward inference, inverted direction.
DS
Disjunctive Syllogism
(A∨B) ∧ ¬A ⊢ B
Elimination over ≥3 candidate values.
LL
Leibniz's Law
x=y ∧ A(x) ⊢ A(y)
Alias / co-reference transfer.
CC
Conjunctive Composition
x∈A ∧ x∈B ⊢ x∈A∩B
Intersection of facts sharing (relation, object).

Data construction pipeline

  1. Knowledge extraction — chunk raw text, prompt LLM for (subject, relation, object) triples.
  2. Structural matching — pair facts per each join rule above.
  3. GPT-4o generation — 3 paraphrases + 0–3 answer aliases per pair, bridge entity masked so the forgotten fact stays implicit.
  4. Two LLM-judge checks — decomposition consistency (multi-hop question decomposes back to source single-hops) + answer uniqueness (rejects multi-answer prompts).

Equal-sized categories suppress cross-category bias: 500 / category on MQuAKE (3,000 total), 100 / category on Books (300 total).

The impossible triangle

No method reaches the ideal outer triangle. PALU buys forget quality + robustness but crashes BBH utility to 49.9%; RMU preserves utility (99.2%) yet fails to remove knowledge. Hover a point for its trade-off.

§04

Strengths & limitations

▲ Strengths

  • First benchmark to jointly combine a rich taxonomy of multi-hop reasoning structures and recovery attacks, cleanly distinguished from Faithful / Deep Unlearning.
  • Principled selection of the six logical types, with explicit exclusion rationale (e.g. Material Implication rejected as pattern-matching).
  • Controlled equal category sizes + a relative metric that neutralizes base-model-accuracy confounds.
  • Honest, citable negative results: no method hits the ideal triangle; multi-hop is systematically more recoverable.
  • Strong transparency — prompts, hyperparameters, recovery configs, hardware, QA protocols all disclosed.

▼ Limitations

  • Open-weight only — cannot speak to black-box / commercial API unlearning dynamics (GPT-4, Claude).
  • Factual knowledge only — privacy memorization, copyrighted spans, implicit harmful behaviors are out of scope.
  • English only — cross-lingual multi-hop leakage (e.g. querying via Chinese/French) is underexplored.
  • LLM-driven data & grading — GPT-4o generates; LLM judges grade. Lenient "answer appears anywhere → CORRECT" rule may overstate recovery.
  • Limited sweep — 3 LLMs × 6 methods × 3 recoveries can't exhaust the space; framed as "strong evidence of trends," not universal laws.
§05

Questions for further exploration

  1. Do the negative Forget Quality cases (AS −43.9 on MT, PALU −26.0 on MP) survive strict human grading and an exact-match criterion?
  2. How do the six structures behave under cross-lingual multi-hop queries — can unlearned English facts leak through Chinese / French reasoning chains?
  3. Can a single unlearning objective trade explicitly along the trilemma — and where is the Pareto frontier between forget quality, robustness, and BBH utility?
  4. Do the same leakage patterns hold for black-box APIs where only Probab-style decoding recovery is available — is parameter-level recovery the dominant threat in practice?
  5. Could the structural-matching pipeline be inverted into a training-time regularizer that suppresses the six paths — turning the benchmark into an algorithm?
