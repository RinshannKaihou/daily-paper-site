01 / METHODOLOGY

Three pillars, one kernel

Typed data model · EigenTT

A dependent type theory in the CIC family (built on Mini-TT) unifies empirical justification logic with formal Lean-4 proof. Epistemic status — Declared / Observed / Derived / Verified — is computed from the provenance graph and enforced at commit. Layers form a Merkle-committed lattice; merges are typed (Witness, Rename, SchemaQuotient, Restructure).

Institutions as stored procedures

Goguen–Burstell institutions are ported into the DBMS world. Comorphisms are statically type-checked at commit; their outputs are materialized back into the chain at a deterministic content-hash IRI, becoming durable, queryable, first-class graph members rather than ephemeral transport intermediates.

Shared-IR collapse · C3

The math payload language FormulaTerm is modeled natively in the graph schema. All five Julia institutions (Symbolics, IntervalArithmetic, Catalyst, OrdinaryDiffEq, JuMP-HiGHS) consume it natively — so comorphisms between them degenerate to identity, collapsing the O(N²) polystore adapter cost.

Formal verification, no IPC

A minimal Rust term checker (nanoda_lib) is linked directly into the kernel binary. Lean 4 stays off-chain as an authoring environment. Promotion to VerifiedResource requires a three-part check: proof validity, EigeniusFFI mirror correspondence, and anchor hash consistency — all three must yield Verdict::Holds.

Declared→ Observed→ Derived→ Verified · Verdict::Holds closed audit chain · re-walkable from graph alone

03 / ASSESSMENT

Strengths vs. limitations

+ Strengths

  • Architecturally coherent. Justification logic (empirical) and Lean proof checking (formal) unified under one epistemic lattice; the four-warrant taxonomy maps cleanly onto real scientific practice.
  • Elegant C3 payoff. Identity-comorphism collapse for O(N²) polystore cost is a concrete win from putting the IR in the schema rather than in middleware.
  • Honest self-assessment. The "What we got wrong" section openly records the Wasm-to-container reversal and the unproven merge-soundness target — rare and valuable.
  • Real, high-profile replication. End-to-end on a Nature paper, surfacing named discrepancies — far more convincing than a toy benchmark.
  • Testable agent-native claim. Open-source artifact plus MCP server makes the substrate verifiable rather than aspirational.

! Limitations

  • Central invariant unproven. Typed-merge well-typedness preservation is stated as the open foundational target; the validator is trusted Rust, not mechanized inside EigenTT.
  • Single flagship study. Only Chan et al. WRN is replicated end-to-end; cross-domain generalization (physics, chemistry, trials) is asserted, not shown.
  • Narrow microbenchmark. Ingestion-only on WordNet/UMLS — no query latency, concurrency, distributed scale, or head-to-head with Dolt, TerminusDB, Datomic, or a SHACL/RDF stack.
  • Agent loop is future work. The motivating "AI Scientist over MCP" scenario is not demonstrated end-to-end; the kernel ships without the closed autonomous-verification loop.
  • Narrative still off-chain. The dependently-typed categorial-grammar engine to land prose arguments as chain terms is described as next step, not delivered.
  • Technical debt. Kernel/orchestrator boundary "in flux"; original Wasm-component design abandoned for sibling containers, narrowing the sandboxing story.
04 / OPEN

Questions for further exploration

Q.01

Does typed-merge preservation of well-typedness actually hold — and can the merge validator itself be mechanized inside EigenTT rather than trusted as Rust?

Q.02

How does commit-time verification throughput scale beyond one core, and what is the per-institution overhead breakdown between the Lean in-process path and the Julia containerized path?

Q.03

Against PROV-O + SHACL on a modern graph DBMS, how many of the four Chan et al. discrepancies would a simpler non-typed stack also surface — architecture win, or just re-running from pinned data?

Q.04

Can the framework replicate a second study in a different domain (clinical trial, materials pipeline) to test whether the institution/comorphism model generalizes beyond oncology genomics?

Q.05

When the categorial-grammar narrative engine lands prose as chain-resident terms, does the controlled-English stage introduce a new trust surface — the parser — that must itself be verified?

Q.06

What is the concrete cost model for an AI Scientist operating purely over MCP endpoints, and does re-walking the audit chain stay sub-second at 10M+ resources?
