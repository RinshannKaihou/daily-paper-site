Key Finding

Runtime procedures lift a 27B model but not a 9B — only teacher-concretized code supervision closes the abstraction–execution gap.

From Execution to Capability: Scientific Experience Consolidation via Procedural Knowledge Synthesis

Key Metric · SciCode

+3.89/+6.25pts

sub-step / main gain of procedure-guided SFT over a pipeline-matched no-procedure control — both 9B students deployed procedure-free. Versus the original 9B base: +5.62 / +11.25.

Why This Matters

A clean template for turning execution feedback into durable model capability — and the sharpest evidence yet that procedure utility is consumer-dependent, not intrinsic to the text.

Critical Edge

The “self-improving” framing is undercut: the 9B cannot itself produce the labels or procedures it learns from — it leans on GLM-5.1 (failure labeling) and Qwen3.6-27B (concretization). The authors concede this themselves.

01

The SciConsolidate Pipeline

execution → abstraction → guided synthesis → learning
STEP 01

Verified experience + failure attribution

Roll out the 9B target K times per task; executable eval sorts success / partial / failure at task and sub-step level. Within-task contrasts cluster into cross-task failure families {F1 spec/interface, F2 array/numerical, F3 logic/dependency, F4 knowledge/capacity}.

STEP 02

Validation-gated procedure synthesis

Procedures are versioned artifacts. A candidate revision is promoted only if it beats the prior version on a paired utility gate G over Dval — making “transfer” part of the operational definition of knowledge, not just textual plausibility.

STEP 03

Answer-free query synthesis

Seed pool is small and pattern-concentrated, so specs, interfaces, deps, and tests are generated under z(x) with balancing over source concentration, domain share, and failure-family coverage. No reference answers needed.

STEP 04

Procedure-guided concretization

A stronger Qwen3.6-27B teacher receives concat(x, s*) and writes a code solution. The student input stays procedure-free, so the training pair is (x, y^T). Only verifier-passing solutions survive; the supervised target is the final code, not the teacher’s reasoning trace.

STEP 05

Matched no-procedure control

Identical construction protocol with the procedure stripped at teacher generation. This isolates procedural guidance from generic 27B distillation — the experiment that makes the headline numbers interpretable.

STEP 06

Procedure-free SFT

Full-parameter SFT, 1 epoch, lr 2e-6, cosine / 0.03 warmup, AdamW (wd 0.1), bf16, max seq 49,152, eff. batch 32 (8 GPUs × 4 accum), DeepSpeed ZeRO-2, gradient checkpointing, seed 42.

03

Strengths & Limitations

honestly reported, single-testbed study

Strengths

  • Clean conceptual contribution. The operational, consumer-dependent definition of the abstraction–execution gap is a lens future skill-internalization work can build on.
  • Methodologically careful. Three-way split with a genuinely frozen held-out set; a pipeline-matched no-procedure control separates “procedural guidance” from “generic stronger-model SFT.”
  • Intellectual honesty. Limitations are itemized in detail (single testbed, one pass, external-model dependence, non-uniform transfer, final-answer-only supervision). No overclaiming of autonomous self-evolution.
  • Fine-grained diagnostics. Task-type, domain, and failure-family breakdowns locate where the method works (simulation, array/numerical) and where it stalls (numerical-method, spec/interface).

Limitations

  • Single testbed. SciCode only — no cross-benchmark, cross-model-family, long-horizon-tool-use, or physical-experimentation evidence.
  • One pass only. “Sustained continual self-improvement” is explicitly not demonstrated; iteration behavior (compound / saturate / interfere) is unknown.
  • Gap is operational, not mechanistic. Two differently-pretrained sizes (Qwen3.5-9B vs Qwen3.6-27B) cannot isolate capacity as the causal driver — no within-family scaling study.
  • Heavy external dependence. GLM-5.1 for labeling, Qwen3.6-27B for concretization — limits how “self” the improvement really is.
  • Narrow knowledge scope. Computational practices only (interface / array / numerical / dependency-flow discipline), not scientific discovery or hypothesis generation.
  • Non-uniform gains. Numerical-method reasoning and spec/interface behavior remain weak or negative; future iterations risk negative interference.
04

Open Questions

what the next paper must answer
1

Does the abstraction–execution gap hold when capacity varies within a single model family and recipe (Qwen3.6-7B / 14B / 27B), isolating capacity from pretraining distribution?

2

Over multiple consolidation rounds with the updated student as the new target, does improvement compound, saturate, or interfere with already-consolidated failure families?

3

Can the validation gate’s contribution be isolated by comparing gated vs random vs plausibility-only procedures at matched size?

4

How much of the +3.89 / +6.25 gain survives if guided and control pools are force-matched to 1,985 each, killing the 110-example yield confound?

5

Can labeling and concretization move onto the improving 9B itself (or a same-size sibling) without collapsing the gap — testing the autonomy claim?

6

Do the induced procedures transfer to other scientific-computing benchmarks (ResearchCodeBench, CFD-LLMBench, FEM-Bench) beyond SciCode?
