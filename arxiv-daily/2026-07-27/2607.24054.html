01

How AcquaBench turns a question into a matched intervention

For a fixed qid × action-channel unit, binary success Y (exact match or token-F1 ≥ 0.80) is recorded under three matched conditions. SHAM is explicitly not a no-information baseline — it preserves the source structure and exposure opportunity while substituting only the value, so any GOLD−SHAM gap must be attributable to value correctness.

CLEAN
Only benchmark-authorized information — HotpotQA distractor set's official context paragraphs.
GOLD
A controlled source makes the correct target value aᵢ available, via a verified short declarative template.
SHAM
Same base source, template, exposure, channel — but the value slot holds a type- & length-matched decoy from a frozen item.

Two paired contrasts

  • ΔGCtotal score response to target availability — GOLD − CLEAN ranges +11.3 to +18.0pp
  • ΔGScorrect-vs-incorrect substitution — the headline attribution test
  • RG−RSconditional net rescue over shared CLEAN-failed population: +31.9 to +38.8pp

Three acquisition regimes

  • D0direct single-source sufficiency — 1 answer-sufficient claim / qid (135/150, seed 13)
  • D1surface-form / scorer boundary — verified alias replaces canonical value (38 qids)
  • D2distributed two-source sufficiency — value split across 2 sources, neither alone sufficient (36/50 qids)

Models: Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, Qwen2.5-14B / 32B-Instruct, deterministic (temperature 0, ≤8 steps, top-5 BM25, ≤1800 chars/source). All intervals are 95% two-sided percentile bootstrap CIs from 5,000 qid-clustered replications (analysis seed 13).

03

Strengths & limitations, side by side

Strengths

  • Conceptually clean layering. Three audit layers (task eval · outcome evidence · success provenance) and three regimes (D0/D1/D2), each with a stated inferential role rather than pooled.
  • SHAM is a strong control. It forces any GOLD−SHAM gap to be attributable to value correctness, not generic source-exposure effects.
  • Intellectual honesty. A claim-boundary matrix (Table S10) explicitly enumerates what each result does not imply; the model-comparison section refuses to overclaim rank inversion.
  • Direct confound attack. Style-controlled validation rules out planted wording — base-doc/template match 1.0, coloc AUROC 0.944.

Limitations

  • Single benchmark family. HotpotQA distractor dev set only — short, multi-hop, Wikipedia-fact answers. Behavior on GAIA, BrowseComp, τ-bench, or code/web agents is unknown.
  • Small, length-selected populations. D1: 38 qids; D2: 36 qids — wide intervals (e.g. D2 Llama net rescue +8.3pp [−6.9, 26.9]); several surface estimates descriptive only.
  • Channels aren't independent. The four action surfaces are interface variants over one shared BM25 store — no channel-generalization claim supported.
  • Detector not deployable outside D0. D2 coloc AUROC below 0.5 — practical detection of distributed acquisition is open work. No human naturalness evaluation of artifacts.
04

Questions this opens

Q1 · GENERALIZATIONDoes success provenance behave similarly on longer-form / open-ended benchmarks (GAIA, BrowseComp, τ-bench), where answer sufficiency is hard to localize in one short claim?
Q2 · DETECTIONCan a detector whose observation unit matches distributed (multi-source) acquisition recover AUROC > 0.5 in a D2-like regime — without post-hoc score reversal?
Q3 · SCALE & TRAININGHow do the CLEAN/GOLD/SHAM gaps interact with scale and post-training (RLHF, reasoning)? Does instruction tuning amplify target-value dependence?
Q4 · RANKINGIs the Qwen-32B-vs-14B compression an artifact of two sizes, or does it replicate across more families and parameter counts?
Q5 · LIVE AGENTSCan the audit extend to live/web agents where the "authorized information state" is the open web? How would SHAM controls even be built there?
Q6 · CONTAMINATIONWhat is the relationship between success provenance and training-data contamination — does parametric knowledge dominate CLEAN enough to mask acquisition effects?
