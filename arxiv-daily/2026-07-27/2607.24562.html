Why this matters

Standard marginal CRC silently breaks per-group risk budgets under mild population shift. HG-CRC is a retraining-free post-hoc fix with formal simultaneous guarantees.

Critical edge

Methodology

post-hoc, retraining-free, score-agnostic
Setup. Given a pre-trained LM with uncertainty score s(x) (lower = more confident; default = selected-option NLL over MC options), a labeled calibration set D_cal, and a group-membership hierarchy H (a rooted tree of nested masks), HG-CRC picks per-node thresholds {τv} so that, with probability ≥ 1−δ over the calibration set, the selective risk on every node's deployed residual is ≤ α simultaneously.
01

Bonferroni correction

A union bound sets per-node confidence to δ̂ = δ/|H|, controlling family-wise error across all nodes. Node v is certified iff the Clopper–Pearson upper bound U_v(τ) ≤ α; the optimal τ* is the largest grid value (100 quantiles) satisfying it.

Nodes below Nmin = 30 are pruned.

02

Residual calibration

Because leaf-first routing deploys a node only on examples not claimed by deeper certified descendants, each node is calibrated on its residual ρv — aligning calibration and deployment populations.

Two variants: split (unconditionally valid) and in-sample (deployment default, covered by Lemma 1 with measured slack εn).

03

Leaf-first selection

At inference, each example walks its ancestry path leaf→root and returns the prediction of the first certified node whose threshold it satisfies; otherwise it abstains.

Well-characterized subgroups get the most tailored threshold.

Strengths & Limitations

Strengths

what holds up
  • Exceptional intellectual honesty — equal-participation comparison, εn caveats, endogeneity analysis, and corrected prior anomalies are all in the paper, not buried.
  • Clean formal treatment distinguishing unconditional (split) from deployed (in-sample) guarantees, with slack actually measured via jackknife.
  • Transferable robustness findings — label noise → graceful conservatism; quantization safe if recalibrated; prompt shift needs cheap recalibration.
  • No retraining, model- and score-agnostic, transparent assumptions; reproducible (public models, fixed seeds, all hyperparameters stated).

Limitations

what to worry about
  • Benchmark- and model-specific headline — holds only on ARC, only for models with base accuracy well above 1−α; abstains or fails on MMLU-Pro.
  • WGER rewards abstention — Gemma's near-zero WGER coexists with ~8% participation (near-degenerate); must always read jointly with participation.
  • Deployed procedure only empirically valid — worst-case jackknife slack εn ≈ 0.34 on the small ARC pool is material.
  • Nmin = 30 prunes most leaves (57 MMLU-Pro subjects × 3 difficulties), collapsing the hierarchy where domain structure is real.
  • Bonferroni is conservative — Holm-Bonferroni / hierarchical testing could recover participation; left as future work.
05

Open questions

where this leads next
?

Can Holm-Bonferroni, Benjamini-Hochberg, or Yekutieli hierarchical testing recover a meaningful share of the 22–37 pp participation cost while preserving the simultaneous guarantee — especially on wide hierarchies like MMLU-Pro's 57 subjects?

?

How does HG-CRC behave on generation tasks (not multiple-choice), where selected-option NLL is unavailable and uncertainty must come from self-consistency, token entropy, or a meta-learner?

?

Does the headline extend to larger/stronger models (70B-class) on harder benchmarks, or does the participation cost shrink toward zero as base accuracy improves?

?

Can the equal-participation result (§8.2) be inverted into a coverage-adaptive global baseline that finds HG-CRC's safe operating point without the hierarchy — and how much would that erode HG-CRC's case?

?

What happens under adversarial group shift (an attacker choosing the test mixture), and does the simultaneous guarantee still hold at the measured εn slack?

?

Is there a cheap hybrid (split at sparse upper nodes, in-sample at well-populated leaves) that is both unconditionally valid and participation-efficient, fixing the εn ≈ 0.34 worst case?
