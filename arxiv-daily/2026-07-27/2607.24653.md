> **一句话总结：** Moonshot 发布了首个开源 3T 级 MoE 大模型 Kimi K3（总参 2.8T、激活 104B、1M 上下文），基于 Kimi Delta Attention + Attention Residuals + Stable LatentMoE 三项新架构实现较 K2 约 2.5 倍的 scaling 效率提升，并在长期编码、Agent、知识与多模态评测中除略逊 Claude Fable 5 / GPT-5.6 Sol 外全面领先其他开源与闭源模型，权重已完全开放。

> **TL;DR:** Kimi K3 is the first open 3T-class Mixture-of-Experts model (2.8T total / 104B activated params, 1M-token context) built on Kimi Delta Attention, Attention Residuals, and Stable LatentMoE, achieving ~2.5× scaling-efficiency over Kimi K2. Across ~40 benchmarks it trails only Claude Fable 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol overall—e.g. 91.2% on BrowseComp (beating GPT-5.6 Sol's 90.4% and Claude Fable 5's 88.0%), 77.8% on ProgramBench (#1), and 42.0% on SWE-Marathon (+7 points over Claude Fable 5)—while consistently beating Claude Opus 4.8, GPT-5.5, and GLM-5.2. Full weights are released.

## 核心贡献

- **首个开源 3T 级预训练基础模型**：训练 2.8T 参数的原生多模态 MoE 模型，104B 激活参数、1M token 上下文窗口；KDA + AttnRes + Stable LatentMoE + 改进数据/训练配方合计带来较 Kimi K2 约 2.5× 的整体 scaling 效率。
- **多维信息流架构**：序列维度用 3:1 的 KDA 与 Gated MLA 混合注意力；深度维度用 Block Attention Residuals 实现层间选择性检索；宽度维度用 LatentMoE 在 896 路由专家中激活 16 个（稀疏度 56），并提出 SiTU-GLU 激活与 Quantile Balancing 负载均衡以稳定极端稀疏下的优化。
- **多努力等级的强化学习后训练**：在通用、Agent、编码三域 × {low/high/max} 三种推理努力等级共训练 9 个专家模型，再用 Multi-Teacher On-Policy Distillation (MOPD) 融合为单一统一模型；训练环境涵盖可验证搜索、SWE、GPU kernel 优化、视觉推理、长期个人助理与自主执行任务。
- **关键基础设施创新**：KDA 的算法–系统协同设计（FlashKDA、KDA Context Parallelism、状态感知前缀缓存）；MoonEP 实现完美平衡的专家并行（每 rank 恰好 S×K token，零拷贝通信、静态计算形状）；为 1M token agentic RL 构建持久 rollout + 可恢复 microVM 沙箱（AgentENV，全程创建 5100 万个沙箱）。
- **开放前沿模型**：完整发布模型权重，并提供 MiniTriton 编译器、nano-kpu 芯片原型等多个开源工件，便于社区研究与部署。

## Key Contributions

- **First open 3T-class pre-trained foundation**: A 2.8T-parameter native-multimodal MoE with 104B activated parameters and a 1M-token context window; KDA, AttnRes, Stable LatentMoE, and refined data/training recipes together yield ~2.5× overall scaling efficiency over Kimi K2.
- **Multi-axis information-flow architecture**: Sequence mixing via a 3:1 hybrid of Kimi Delta Attention (linear, channel-wise forget-gated delta rule) and Gated MLA; depth mixing via Block Attention Residuals that let each layer selectively attend to all preceding blocks; width mixing via LatentMoE activating 16 of 896 routed experts (sparsity 56) stabilized by RMSNorm, the new bounded SiTU-GLU activation, and Quantile Balancing for load balancing.
- **Multi-effort RL post-training**: Nine expert policies are trained (3 domains × {low/high/max} reasoning effort) then consolidated into one unified model via Multi-Teacher On-Policy Distillation (MOPD). Environments span verifiable search, SWE, GPU-kernel optimization, vision-in-the-loop reasoning, persistent personal-assistant workflows, web development, and autonomous-execution (verify-in-the-loop) tasks.
- **Infrastructure co-design**: FlashKDA kernels and KDA Context Parallelism make the recurrent attention efficient within and across devices; MoonEP achieves perfectly balanced expert-parallel training (every rank receives exactly S×K tokens) with zero-copy communication and static computation shapes; a co-located RL system with external KV-cache pool, auto-throttling, and resumable Firecracker microVM sandboxes (AgentENV, 51.2M sandboxes created) sustains million-token agentic rollouts; KDA-aware prefix caching packs the fixed-size recurrent state into the same paged pool as MLA KV.
- **Open frontier release**: Full model weights are released on HuggingFace (moonshotai/Kimi-K3), alongside MiniTriton (a from-scratch Triton-like compiler) and nano-kpu (an INT4 inference-chip prototype) as additional open-source artifacts.

## Methodology

**Architecture.** Kimi K3 scales information flow along three axes. (1) **Sequence**: each block has 3 Kimi Delta Attention (KDA) layers followed by 1 Gated MLA layer (3:1 ratio); KDA extends the delta-rule recurrence with a channel-wise forget gate, and Kimi K3 changes its decay parameterization from unbounded negative-Softplus to a lower-bounded scaled-sigmoid (g_min = −5) so all chunk tiles—including the diagonal—can use dense Tensor Core matmuls. Gated MLA layers use NoPE (no positional encoding) since KDA already provides position-sensitive mixing. (2) **Depth**: Block Attention Residuals partition the 93 layers into 8 blocks of 12, letting each layer attend (via learned pseudo-queries and an RMSNorm-softmax kernel) to block-level summaries rather than every prior layer, reducing O(Ld) memory to O(Nd). (3) **Width**: Stable LatentMoE separates full-width shared experts (Ns=2) from compact-latent routed experts (16 of 896 active, latent dim = 0.5× hidden), inserts RMSNorm before the up-projection, replaces SwiGLU with the bounded SiTU-GLU (softcap with β1=4, β2=25) to suppress activation outliers, and uses Quantile Balancing—setting each expert bias to the (1−k/n)-quantile of routing margins estimated from an all-reduce histogram—to achieve target-perfect load balance without auxiliary loss. Vision is native: MoonViT-V2 (27 layers, ~0.4B params) is trained from scratch with next-token prediction (not SigLIP init) for stability, and shares parameters between images and videos. Per-Head Muon orthogonalizes each attention head's momentum block separately.

**Pre-training.** A curated four-domain text corpus (Web, Code, Math, Knowledge) plus large-scale vision data, with rephrased knowledge/math corpora, programmatic multimodal data (SVG, 3D, Webpage, Game, CAD), and absolute + normalized coordinate supervision. A dedicated scaling-law study retunes batch size, learning rate, tokens-per-parameter, and model shape; cosine decay is chosen over WSD after independent hyperparameter searches. Training starts at 8K context and is progressively extended through a four-stage curriculum to 1M tokens during cooldown; NoPE allows direct extrapolation without RoPE rescaling or YaRN.

**Post-training.** Three stages: (1) SFT for cold-start, serialized with an XTML chat template and using MXFP4 QAT from the start; (2) RL across general/agentic/coding domains and three reasoning-effort levels, using partial rollouts with a λ-completion threshold, per-token off-policy regularization, per-problem token-budget control, and an Agentic Generative Reward Model (GRM) with mandatory rubric + verbosity-control protocols for non-verifiable tasks; (3) MOPD consolidates the nine experts into one student via a clipped log-ratio dense reward. RL environments include a unified white-box harness (instantiating Kimi Code / Claude Code / Codex / OpenClaw / Hermes), knowledge-graph-guided task synthesis, kernel-optimization tasks with hacking detection, mock personal-assistant apps (Gmail/Notion/Slack/Canvas), and autonomous-execution verify-in-the-loop tasks. Deployment-aware post-training keeps MXFP4 expert weights / MXFP8 activations and fine-tunes the MTP layer into an EAGLE-3 draft model using an LK loss that directly optimizes speculative-decoding acceptance rate.

## Results & Highlights

- **BrowseComp (agentic web search)**: Kimi K3 scores **91.2%**, beating GPT-5.6 Sol (90.4%) and Claude Fable 5 (88.0%)—i.e. +3.2 points over the strongest baseline—while costing ~$2.03/task vs. an order of magnitude more for Claude at max effort.
- **ProgramBench (coding, Vals AI)**: Kimi K3 is #1 at **77.8%**, ahead of GPT-5.6 Sol (77.6%) and Claude Fable 5 (76.8%).
- **SWE-Marathon (GPU-kernel-oriented coding)**: Kimi K3 scores **42.0%**, +7 points over Claude Fable 5 (35.0%) and +29 points over GPT-5.5 (14.0%); note this is on an H20-calibrated branch and Claude Fable 5 hits fallbacks on 35% of tasks.
- **Terminal-Bench 2.1**: Kimi K3 reaches **88.3%**, within 0.5 points of GPT-5.6 Sol (88.8%) and ahead of Claude Fable 5 (88.0%).
- **FrontierSWE (long-horizon SWE)**: Kimi K3 ranks #2 with **81.2%** behind only Claude Fable 5 (86.6%) and far ahead of GPT-5.6 Sol (71.3%) and GLM-5.2 (67.3%).
- **GPQA Diamond (graduate-level reasoning)**: Kimi K3 scores **93.5%**, tied with GPT-5.5 and ahead of Claude Fable 5 (92.6%); however on research-level **HLE-Full with tools** it scores 56.0%, trailing Claude Fable 5 (63.0%) and GPT-5.6 Sol (58.0%), and on **CritPt** 23.4% vs. GPT-5.6 Sol's 32.3%.
- **Agentic suites**: Kimi K3 leads MCPMark-Verified (94.5% vs. Claude Fable 5 87.4%), AutomationBench (30.8%), SpreadsheetBench 2 (34.8%), τ³-Banking (33.4% vs. GPT-5.6 Sol 33.0%), Harvey Lab-AA (94.6% criterion pass), and DeepSearchQA (95.0% F1); it places 3rd on GDPval-AA v2 Elo (1686 vs. Claude Fable 5 1747) and 2nd on AA-Briefcase (1548 Elo).
- **Third-party rankings**: Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v4.1 = **57.1** (#4/580, behind Claude Fable 5 59.9 and GPT-5.6 Sol 58.9); Vals AI Index = 74.7% (#2/39, behind Claude Fable 5 75.1%); **#1 on WebDev Arena** (1678 Elo, first open model to top it, ahead of Claude Fable 5 at 1634).
- **Cybersecurity (exploit suite vs. GLM-5.2)**: Kimi K3 solves **14/36 tasks (38.9%)** vs. GLM-5.2's 8/36 (22.2%); at Tier 1 it identified ~70% confirmed genuine vulnerabilities including 16 previously unknown Linux-kernel bugs, but the independent UK AISI / NIST CAISI assessment notes it achieves arbitrary code execution on **0 of 41** tasks vs. frontier cyber-capable models.
- **GPU-kernel optimization case study**: Kimi K3 cut AttnRes latency from 283.6 ms → 114.4 ms, reduced DSA runtime 55.1% and KDA runtime 73.6%, matching Claude Fable 5 and beating GPT-5.6 Sol (+17.3%) and GPT-5.5 (+30.8%); it autonomously built MiniTriton (a Triton-like compiler reaching ~90% of cuBLAS roofline) and a nano-K3 inference chip (8,700+ tok/s decode, 1.46M cells, closes timing at 100 MHz in a single 48-hour run).

## Authors & Credibility

Authored by the **Kimi Team at Moonshot AI**, with a very large contributor list (hundreds of named researchers across the appendix). Moonshot AI is the company behind the Kimi K1.5, K2, and K2.5 line of frontier models, giving the team a credible track record on large-scale pre-training, long-context RL, and open-weight releases. The technical report is detailed, includes both algorithmic derivations (chunkwise KDA, Quantile Balancing proofs in appendices) and substantial systems engineering (MoonEP, AgentENV, KDA Context Parallelism), and is accompanied by released weights, open-sourced infrastructure (MoonEP, FlashKDA, AgentENV, MiniTriton, nano-kpu), and independent third-party evaluations (Artificial Analysis, Vals AI, LMArena, UK AISI/NIST CAISI). Benchmarks are mostly standardized public suites, and the report transparently notes where proprietary baselines (Fable 5) use fallbacks or where the SWE-Marathon branch was H20-calibrated.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: The combination is genuinely novel at this scale—a 3T-class open model with a hybrid linear/global attention (KDA:MLA = 3:1) plus depth-wise Attention Residuals plus extreme-sparsity LatentMoE (896 experts, sparsity 56) is not found in prior open releases. Several individual ingredients (Quantile Balancing for auxiliary-loss-free routing, lower-bounded decay enabling all-Tensor-Core KDA tiles, from-scratch non-contrastive vision encoder, perfectly balanced MoonEP) are substantive contributions. However, KDA and AttnRes are explicitly cited as built on the team's own prior work (Kimi Linear [63], Attention Residuals [57]), and SiTU-GLU is a softcap variant reminiscent of several recent bounded-activation proposals (PowLU [51])—so the architecture is more "synthesis + scale" than fundamentally new primitives.
- **Rigor**: Strong on systems rigor (MoonEP's E/R redundant-expert bound is proven tight; KCP's associative prefix-scan is derived; QAT and draft-model training are described end-to-end). The scaling-law comparison of cosine vs. WSD is commendably fair, running independent hyperparameter searches per schedule. The cybersecurity section is unusually candid, including an independent UK AISI/NIST assessment and explicitly stating 0/41 arbitrary-code-execution on hardened targets.
- **Reproducibility**: Weights are fully released, and multiple infra components (MoonEP, FlashKDA, AgentENV, MiniTriton, nano-kpu) are open-sourced. However, the pre-training corpus, the exact RL task mixtures, the GRM, the mock application implementations, and the "in-house" benchmarks (KCB 2.0, KAET, Swarm Bench, Deep Research Bench, etc.) are not released, so the post-training pipeline cannot be reproduced. Many "headline" agentic benchmarks (JobBench, Toolathlon, OSWorld 2.0) are very recent and themselves evolving.
- **Significance**: This is plausibly the strongest open-weight frontier model at release time and the first credible 3T-class open model, with a genuine cost-efficiency story (e.g. BrowseComp at ~half GPT-5.6 Sol's cost, WebDev Arena #1). It narrows but does not close the gap to Claude Fable 5 / GPT-5.6 Sol, especially on Elo-rated knowledge-work (GDPval-AA v2, AA-Briefcase) and research-level reasoning (HLE-Full, CritPt).
- **Red flags / caveats**: (a) Baseline comparability is uneven—Claude Fable 5 results include "potential fallbacks" and GPT-5.6 Sol "potential cyberguards," the SWE-Marathon branch is H20-calibrated (not the official v1.1) with Fable 5 hitting fallbacks on 35% of tasks, and PostTrainBench is run on H20 instead of the official H100. (b) Several "wins" are on benchmarks the authors either created or co-maintain (ProgramBench is a Vals AI suite, but Kimi Webdev Bench, Swarm Bench, KAET, Deep Research Bench, KCB 2.0 are all in-house and not independently auditable). (c) Missing ablations: there is no clean ablation isolating the contribution of KDA vs. a pure softmax baseline at the 2.8T scale, no ablation of AttnRes vs. standard residuals at final scale, and no head-to-head of SiTU-GLU vs. SwiGLU at scale (only scalar-response curves). (d) The cyber-evaluation explicitly omits frontier proprietary models (they refuse the tasks), making the "we outperform" framing on that suite less informative. (e) The "first open 3T-class" claim is time-stamped and could be overtaken quickly.

## Strengths

- Genuine open-weight frontier: 2.8T params, 1M context, native vision, released under a permissive weight license with substantial infra also open-sourced.
- Strong engineering rigor in the systems stack—MoonEP's provably-balanced EP, KDA Context Parallelism with associative prefix scan, KDA-aware paged prefix caching that unifies linear and softmax caches, and resumable Firecracker microVM sandboxes with 133 ms checkpoint / 49 ms resume.
- Consistently beats Claude Opus 4.8, GPT-5.5, and GLM-5.2 across nearly all suites, and is competitive with or ahead of Claude Fable 5 / GPT-5.6 Sol on a meaningful subset (BrowseComp, ProgramBench, SWE-Marathon, MCPMark, several agentic suites).
- Excellent cost-efficiency frontier—near-top scores at a fraction of proprietary-model cost, and the first open model to top WebDev Arena.
- Candid about weaknesses (research-level reasoning, Elo-rated knowledge work, hardened-target cyber exploitation) and supplements internal evals with independent third-party assessments.

## Limitations

- Still trails the strongest proprietary models (Claude Fable 5, GPT-5.6 Sol) on overall intelligence (Intelligence Index 57.1 vs. 59.9 / 58.9) and specifically on research-level reasoning (HLE-Full, CritPt), Elo-rated knowledge work (GDPval-AA v2, AA-Briefcase), and some computer-use suites (OSWorld 2.0, SaaS-Bench, OfficeQA Pro).
- Post-training pipeline (SFT data, RL environments, GRM, mock apps, in-house benchmarks) is not released, so the recipe is not reproducible end-to-end.
- Several key comparisons are confounded by baseline configuration differences (Fable 5 fallbacks, GPT-5.6 Sol cyberguards, H20 vs. H100, harness-specific results, SWE-Marathon calibration).
- No final-scale ablations isolating KDA, AttnRes, or SiTU-GLU contributions; the 2.5× scaling-efficiency claim rests on fitted scaling-law curves rather than a controlled same-FLOP bake-off at 2.8T.
- Cyber-capability framing is one-sided since frontier proprietary models refuse the tasks; the 0/41 hardened-target ACE result underscores that the "frontier" claim is relative to the open-weight ecosystem, not to all deployed systems.
- Quantization (MXFP4 weights / MXFP8 activations) is applied throughout post-training; the report does not provide a clean FP-vs-quant quality comparison at release.

## Relevance

对关注大模型架构创新、长上下文与 Agent RL 的研究者高度相关：Kimi K3 是首个开源 3T 级 MoE 模型，其 KDA + Attention Residuals + Stable LatentMoE 的混合架构、Quantile Balancing 负载均衡与 MoonEP 完美平衡专家并行，为线性注意力 + 极致稀疏 MoE 的工程化提供了可复现的参考实现。对从事长程编码、Agent 编排与多模态推理评测的人而言，它也是当前最强开源基线之一。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How much of the 2.5× scaling-efficiency gain comes from KDA vs. AttnRes vs. Stable LatentMoE vs. data/recipe changes? A controlled same-FLOP ablation at smaller but representative scale would clarify which components are load-bearing.
- Can the lower-bounded decay parameterization (g_min = −5) and all-Tensor-Core KDA tiles generalize to other gated-linear-attention families (e.g., RWKV-7, Mamba-2), and does the g_min value interact with context length?
- Does Quantile Balancing's histogram estimator degrade when expert counts grow beyond ~1000, or when token distributions become highly skewed (e.g., long-tail routing)?
- How sensitive is the MoonEP "E/R redundant experts" guarantee to non-uniform expert capacities or heterogeneous expert sizes?
- To what extent do the in-house agentic benchmarks (Swarm Bench, Deep Research Bench, KAET) overfit to the team's own harness conventions, and how would Kimi K3 perform under fully independent harnesses?
- Given the 0/41 hardened-target ACE result, what is the marginal cyber-risk posed by releasing full open weights vs. the defensive-research upside from the Tier-1 vulnerability-discovery capability?
- Does MXFP4 QAT throughout post-training measurably hurt long-horizon agentic reliability (e.g., tool-call argument precision) vs. an FP8/BF16 post-trained control?
