> **一句话总结：** 本文提出 AcquaBench，通过在同一 qid/模型/通道上匹配的 CLEAN/GOLD/SHAM 三条件值替换审计（覆盖检索、工具、记忆、子智能体四类通道），揭示智能体基准的正确答案可能依赖评估期间获取的目标值而非授权信息，在 HotpotQA 上的 D0 中 GOLD 比 SHAM 高 19.1–25.9 个百分点，且能使 Qwen2.5-32B 相对 14B 在 CLEAN 下 +5.0pp 的优势在原始 GOLD 下压缩为 -0.6pp。

> **TL;DR:** The paper introduces AcquaBench, a within-qid CLEAN/GOLD/SHAM value-substitution audit (across retrieval, tool, memory, subagent channels) that tests whether agent-benchmark success on HotpotQA tracks the correct target value rather than matched source exposure. In the D0 regime, GOLD exceeds SHAM by 19.1–25.9 percentage points across Llama-3.1-8B and Qwen2.5-14B/32B, and under distributed two-source sufficiency (D2) the behavioral effect persists (+11.8/+14.6pp) even though a single-source coloc detector collapses (AUROC 0.376/0.142). A supported CLEAN gap of +5.0pp for Qwen2.5-32B vs Qwen2.5-14B compresses to a raw GOLD difference of -0.6pp, showing exposed scoring can distort supported model rankings without establishing rank inversion.

## 核心贡献

- 提出"成功溯源"（success provenance）这一缺失的评估对象：在智能体基准中，正确性只能证明结果发生，不能证明成功依赖的是基准授权信息还是评估轨迹内获取的目标值。
- 设计 AcquaBench：在同一 qid、模型、参考目标、动作通道、评分器下匹配 CLEAN/GOLD/SHAM 三条件值替换审计，其中 GOLD 注入正确目标值、SHAM 保留源结构与暴露机会但替换为匹配的错误值；GOLD−CLEAN 测度目标可用性的总响应，GOLD−SHAM 检验该响应是否超出源暴露跟踪目标正确性。
- 将审计实例化在四个标准化通道（检索、工具、记忆、子智能体），并对每个 qid 联合 qid-cluster 分析，避免将四个通道当作独立重复。
- 引入三种获取机制 D0（直接单源充分性）、D1（表面形式边界）、D2（分布式两源充分性），分离"行为依赖"与"单源可见性"，并揭示二者并非同一性质。
- 证明暴露评分对模型比较的后果：Qwen2.5-32B 在 CLEAN 下对 14B 的 +5.0pp 支持性优势，在原始 GOLD 评分下压缩为 -0.6pp，提示基准应将"成功"与"评估信息状态是否支持该成功"一并报告。

## Key Contributions

- Defines **success provenance** as the missing evaluation object for agent benchmarks: outcome correctness establishes that a result occurred, but not whether the score depended on target information acquired inside the evaluated trajectory rather than the authorized information state.
- Introduces **AcquaBench**, a matched within-qid CLEAN/GOLD/SHAM value-substitution audit that holds qid, model, reference target, action channel, and scorer fixed; GOLD−CLEAN measures total score response to target availability, and GOLD−SHAM tests whether that response tracks target correctness beyond matched source exposure.
- Instantiates the audit on four standardized action surfaces (retrieval, tool use, memory access, subagent messaging) sharing one BM25 evidence store, analyzed jointly by qid-cluster rather than as independent replications.
- Distinguishes three acquisition regimes — **D0** (direct single-source sufficiency), **D1** (surface-form/scorer measurement boundary), **D2** (distributed two-source sufficiency) — separating behavioral value dependence from single-source detector visibility.
- Demonstrates an evaluation consequence: a supported +5.0pp CLEAN gap for Qwen2.5-32B over Qwen2.5-14B compresses to a -0.6pp raw GOLD difference, arguing that agent benchmarks should report success together with whether the evaluated information state supported it.

## Methodology

AcquaBench turns "did the score follow an evaluation-time-supplied target value?" into a matched intervention. For a fixed qid-action-channel unit `i`, binary success `Y_i^c` (exact match or token F1 ≥ 0.80) is recorded under three conditions:

- **CLEAN** — only benchmark-authorized information (HotpotQA distractor development set's official context paragraphs).
- **GOLD** — a controlled source making the correct target value `a_i` available, appended via a verified short declarative template.
- **SHAM** — the same base source, template, exposure opportunity, and action channel, but the value slot is filled with a deterministic decoy matched on answer type and length, drawn from another frozen item.

The two paired contrasts over the frozen population are `Δ_GC = E[Y_G − Y_C]` (total response to target availability) and `Δ_GC = E[Y_G − Y_S]` (matched correct-vs-incorrect value substitution). SHAM is explicitly *not* a no-information baseline; it preserves the opportunity to observe and use a matched source while substituting the value, so a positive Δ_GS shows the score tracks correctness beyond generic source-exposure interaction. Conditional net rescue `R_G − R_S` is computed over the shared CLEAN-failed population.

Three regimes probe different boundaries. **D0** appends one answer-sufficient claim per qid (135/150 eligible, frozen with seed 13). **D1** replaces the canonical value with a verified alias/surface form, jointly changing evidence surface and answer form (38 qids). **D2** decomposes the target across two sources — one maps an opaque SHA-256-derived handle to the value, the other states the relation — so neither source alone is answer-sufficient, retained only when the maximum single-source "coloc" score is ≤ 0.25 (36/50 qids). Coloc is a prespecified single-source detector score: the max over observed sources of the product of question-token coverage and predicted-answer-token coverage.

Models are Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct and Qwen2.5-14B/32B-Instruct, run deterministically (temperature 0, ≤8 action steps, top-5 BM25 retrieval, ≤1800 chars/source). All intervals are 95% two-sided percentile bootstrap CIs from 5,000 qid-clustered replications with analysis seed 13, preserving within-qid dependence across channels and conditions.

## Results & Highlights

- **D0 target-value attribution (Table 2):** GOLD exceeds matched SHAM by **+25.9pp [19.6, 32.4]** for Llama-3.1-8B, **+23.9pp [18.0, 30.2]** for Qwen2.5-14B, and **+19.1pp [13.5, 24.8]** for Qwen2.5-32B on HotpotQA; all 12 prespecified model-surface Δ_GS point estimates are positive, and all model-level intervals exclude zero. GOLD−CLEAN ranges +11.3 to +18.0pp, and conditional net rescue ranges +31.9 to +38.8pp.
- **D0 single-source visibility (Table S5):** the coloc detector separates operationally attributable GOLD trajectories from earned CLEAN successes at AUROC **0.929 [0.881, 0.966]** (Llama-8B), **0.925** (Qwen-14B), and **0.968 [0.938, 0.990]** (Qwen-32B), aligning behavioral effect with detector discrimination in the regime coloc was designed for.
- **D2 boundary (Table 3):** when answer sufficiency is distributed across two sources, the behavioral Δ_GS persists at **+11.8pp [2.8, 21.5]** (Llama-8B) and **+14.6pp [5.6, 25.0]** (Qwen-32B), yet coloc AUROC drops to **0.376 [0.210, 0.555]** and **0.142 [0.040, 0.327]** — below 0.5, meaning the prespecified high-coloc rule no longer transfers in its intended direction even as behavioral dependence remains.
- **Style-controlled D0 validation (Table S7):** with matched base document, template, length, and coverage (median paired token-count and question-coverage differences both zero; base-document/claim-template match rates 1.0 over 135 qids), CLEAN accuracy is 40.7%, GOLD 63.0%, SHAM 38.5%, and coloc AUROC is **0.944 [0.895, 0.980]** vs earned success while naive string-match AUROC stays at chance (0.509).
- **Model-comparison consequence (Table 4):** under CLEAN, Qwen2.5-32B beats Qwen2.5-14B by **+5.0pp [0.2, 10.2]**; under raw D0 GOLD the gap reverses to **-0.6pp [-5.2, 4.1]**, with D0 inflation difference **-5.6pp [-10.9, -0.2]**. The operational audit-cleaned view returns +4.4pp (66.3% vs 70.7%) but is descriptive, not an inferentially established corrected ranking.

## Authors & Credibility

Jingkun Luo and Da-Tian Peng (corresponding author) are at Northwestern Polytechnical University. The work is presented in AAAI-27 submission style with a thorough 17-page PDF including a full supplementary appendix (A–H) covering audit construction, estimands, frozen results, reproducibility manifests (Python 3.12.3, PyTorch 2.11.0, vLLM 0.23.0, single NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 96GB), artifact prompts, claim-boundary matrix, and SHA-256 traceability. Code is released at https://github.com/luojingkun22/acquabench. The statistical protocol is unusually careful: prespecified contrasts, qid-clustered bootstrap, frozen populations with explicit eligibility audits (e.g., D0 selects toward shorter answers — accepted 2.46 words/16 chars vs rejected 8.2 words/49 chars), and a claim-boundary matrix that lists what each result does *not* prove.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** Genuine. The framing of "success provenance" as a distinct audit layer between contamination detection and outcome verification is fresh; SHAM as an attribution control (preserving source structure/exposure while substituting the value) is more disciplined than typical robustness or contamination tests, and the within-qid matched triple design is clean.
- **Rigor:** High for an evaluation paper. Paired bootstrap with qid-clustering, prespecified contrasts, frozen seed-13 populations, a style-control that rules out the strongest "planted wording" alternative, and a D2 regime that deliberately tests the single-source detector's observation-unit boundary. The authors are transparent that D1 is a measurement boundary (its Δ_GS for Qwen-32B is -2.0pp with only 2 detector positives) and that D2 AUROCs below 0.5 mean the *rule* fails, not that structure is absent.
- **Reproducibility:** Strong on paper — deterministic decoding, public checkpoints, fixed analysis seed, archive-relative paths, SHA-256 manifests, and a reproduction sequence (steps 1–7). Caveat: model weights are referenced by public checkpoint identifier without an immutable revision, which the authors themselves flag as a limitation ("do not retain an immutable repository revision for every model").
- **Significance:** The model-comparison finding (a CLEAN-supported gap compressing/reversing under GOLD) is the most consequential claim and is appropriately hedged — the interval does not establish rank inversion, only two Qwen sizes are compared (no scaling law), and operational cleaning is explicitly not a corrected leaderboard. Scoped to one benchmark family (HotpotQA), 135/38/36 qids, three models.
- **Red flags / weaknesses:** The eligible populations are small and heavily length-selected (D0 keeps 135/150; D1 only 38; D2 only 36), so generalization beyond short-answer multi-hop QA is unproven. The four "action surfaces" share one BM25 evidence store and differ only in action name/serialization, so the "12 positive Δ_GS estimates" are not independent replications despite being descriptively reported as such. The headline model-comparison result rests on a single model family and two sizes; the -0.6pp reversal has a CI straddling zero, so "compresses" is fair but "distorts ranking" is not established. No human evaluation of the constructed artifacts' naturalness beyond automated verifiers. The coloc detector is prespecified and not tuned, but its below-0.5 D2 AUROC means it cannot be recommended as a practical detector outside D0.

## Strengths

- Conceptually clean separation of three audit layers (task evaluation, outcome evidence, success provenance) and three regimes (D0/D1/D2), each with a stated inferential role rather than pooled.
- The SHAM control is a genuinely strong design choice: it forces any GOLD−SHAM gap to be attributable to value correctness, not to generic source-exposure effects.
- Intellectual honesty throughout: a claim-boundary matrix (Table S10) explicitly enumerates what each result does *not* imply, and the model-comparison section repeatedly refuses to overclaim rank inversion or a corrected leaderboard.
- Style-controlled validation directly attacks the most plausible confound (planted title/wording), with base-document and claim-template match rates of 1.0 and coloc AUROC of 0.944.

## Limitations

- Scoped to HotpotQA distractor development set only; short, multi-hop, Wikipedia-fact answers. Eligibility selects strongly toward short answers (D0 ≈2.46 words), so behavior on longer-form, open-ended, or code/web agent tasks is unknown.
- Small frozen populations (D1: 38 qids; D2: 36 qids) yield wide intervals; D2 net rescue for Llama is +8.3pp [-6.9, 26.9] and several surface-level estimates are descriptive only.
- The four action channels are interface variants over one shared evidence store, not independent retrieval systems, so they cannot support claims about channel-generalization.
- Model comparison uses only two Qwen sizes from one family; no scaling law, no rank inversion established.
- D2's below-0.5 coloc AUROC means the detector is not deployable outside its intended D0 regime, leaving practical detection of distributed acquisition as open work.

## Relevance

这篇论文直接服务于"智能体基准可信度"这一核心议题：它给出了一套可操作的成功溯源审计方法，可用于审视检索增强、工具调用、多智能体系统中模型分数是否被评估期间注入的目标值所"注水"。对于关注 LLM agent 评估方法论、benchmark 污染与排行榜稳健性的研究者具有较高参考价值，其 within-qid 匹配对照设计也可迁移到自研基准的内部审计流程中。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does success provenance behave similarly on longer-form or open-ended agent benchmarks (e.g., GAIA, BrowseComp, τ-bench), where answer sufficiency is harder to localize in a single short claim?
- Can a detector be designed whose observation unit matches distributed (multi-source) acquisition, recovering AUROC above 0.5 in a D2-like regime without post-hoc score reversal?
- How does the CLEAN/GOLD/SHAM gap interact with model scale and post-training (RLHF, reasoning training) — does instruction-tuning amplify target-value dependence relative to base models?
- Is the Qwen-32B-vs-14B ranking compression an artifact of two sizes, or does it replicate across additional model families and parameter counts?
- Could the audit be extended to live/web agents where the "authorized information state" is not a frozen corpus but the open web, and how would SHAM controls be constructed there?
- What is the relationship between success provenance and training-data contamination — does parametric knowledge dominate CLEAN performance enough to mask acquisition effects in highly-contaminated models?
