> **一句话总结：** 本文指出 coding agent 的"生成-测试-修订"循环本身不保证可靠性——在 30 个 HumanEval 修复任务的密封五种子研究中，强制修订会让当前正确率从一次修订后的 0.820 降到二次修订后的 0.673（而 ever-correct 反而升到 0.847），并且 14B 模型上"陈旧证据"会使 34/135 个已正确的起点重陷错误（对比 current traces 的 4/135，差 +22.2 个百分点，Holm 校正后 p=0.0337）；作者据此提出一个证据-状态绑定、带类型修订动作、保留 checkpoint、风险感知停机的可执行"修订循环契约"及参考实现 StateSeal，但坦诚这只是合规规范而非修复能力提升的证据。

> **TL;DR:** The paper reframes coding-agent "generate–test–revise" loops as a *completion reliability* problem distinct from *proposal search*. In a sealed five-seed study on 30 HumanEval repairs (FeedbackEval subset), forced revision drives current correctness from 0.820 after revision 1 down to 0.673 after revision 2 while ever-correct still rises to 0.847; in a prespecified 14B common-state replication on 2,430 frozen-state branches, stale execution traces harm 34/135 already-correct starts vs 4/135 with current traces (a +22.2-point gap, exact Holm p=0.0337). Verifier quality does not imply independence (Qwen-7B/Qwen-14B share φ=0.641 conditional dependence among incorrect revisions at 70% coverage). The authors derive an evidence-bound typed loop contract and ship a reference implementation (StateSeal) that mechanically binds verifier evidence to exact code states, preserves checkpoints, and emits auditable admission receipts — but repository-agent factorials over 24 bugs and four coder stacks (Qwen2.5-14B, Qwen3-Coder-Next, Devstral Small 2 24B) hit a severe completion floor and yield no Holm-significant contract effect, so the work is honestly framed as an executable specification/conformance artifact rather than proof of improved repair competence.

## 核心贡献

- **提出"搜索 vs. 完成可靠性"的区分**：论证了 generate-test-revise 循环里"是否曾出现过正确补丁"（proposal search / ever-correct）与"orchestrator 是否留住、按当前状态证据修订、并正确停机"（completion reliability）是两回事，并用 Q_t+1−Q_t = (1−Q_t)α_e − Q_t·β_e 的转移分解把"修复率 α"与"回归率 β"显式分离。
- **轨迹级非吸收性的实证（RQ1）**：900 条强制修订轨迹显示当前正确率非单调（0.820 → 0.673 → 0.693），第 3 次修订时 16.0% 的轨迹"曾对过又丢了"；证明"多轮一定更好/更坏"都不是无条件的普适规律。
- **共态干预实验去除 post-treatment 偏倚（RQ2）**：在 27 个任务上冻结同一份 wrong/correct 代码状态，再分支不同证据条件，得到 2,430 条单步分支；14B 上"陈旧 trace"使正确起点回归 34/135（0.252）对比 current traces 的 4/135（0.030），+22.2 个百分点（CI [8.9, 37.0]，Holm p=0.0337），且案例显示陈旧证据可"复活"已修好的 bug（HumanEval/10、/89）。
- **把 verifier 风险与依赖耦合到 agent 完成决策（RQ3）**：在匹配覆盖（70%）下 Qwen-14B 风险 0.041 远低于 DeepSeek-6.7B 的 0.240，但 Qwen-7B/Qwen-14B 的条件 φ=0.641（CI [0.096, 0.935]），说明"模型族多样性"既不保证低风险也不保证错误独立；并形式化条件误接受率 P(AiAj|Y=0)=fi·fj+D_ij，证明乘积误差论证只在 D_ij=0 时成立。
- **推导并实现"证据-有界类型化修订契约"与 StateSeal 参考实现**：把循环变成小状态机——证据信封 E=(h_code, h_suite, execution_id, payload)、类型化动作 Keep | Patch(code) | Escalate、last-known-good checkpoint、风险/依赖感知停机；StateSeal 在 agent 进程外用 admission broker 重算状态哈希、跑 verifier、保留 checkpoint 并回执；35 个确定性故障注入用例全部通过合规测试。
- **仓库级 factorial（RQ4/RQ5）诚实暴露 floor effect**：24 个 bug × 四个 coder stack 的契约 ON vs OFF 测试中，OFF 臂 sound completion 仅 0–2.1%，joint intersection-union decision 全部失败；随机化组件消融（B/A/C）与等预算深度 factorial（cap 1/3/5/8）都没有 Holm 显著效应，作者明确不把它当作契约有效性的正面证据。

## Key Contributions

- **Reframes agent loops as completion reliability, not just search**: separates "ever-correct" from "current-correct" and formalizes repair (α_e) and regression (β_e) as a transition kernel, showing that continuation is harmful in expectation once Q_t > α_e/(α_e+β_e).
- **Empirical non-absorption of correctness (RQ1)**: over 900 forced-revision trajectories, current correctness is non-monotone (0.820 → 0.673 → 0.693) even as ever-correct climbs to 0.853; 16.0% of revision-3 trajectories had been correct and then lost it.
- **Common-state evidence interventions removing risk-set bias (RQ2)**: 2,430 single-step branches from identical frozen wrong/correct states; the prespecified 14B replication finds stale traces harm 34/135 correct starts vs 4/135 for current traces (+22.2 pts, CI [8.9, 37.0], exact Holm p=0.0337), with concrete bug-resurrection cases (HumanEval/10, /89).
- **Connects verifier risk–coverage to dependence to completion decisions (RQ3)**: at matched 70% coverage Qwen-14B risk (0.041) is far below DeepSeek-6.7B (0.240), yet Qwen-7B/Qwen-14B conditional φ=0.641 among incorrect revisions — model-family diversity is no proxy for independence; product-of-errors holds only when D_ij=0.
- **An auditable typed loop contract and the StateSeal reference implementation**: evidence envelope bound to code+suite hashes, typed actions `Keep | Patch(code) | Escalate`, last-known-good checkpoint, risk/dependence-aware stopping; passes all 35 deterministic conformance cases and intercepts the recorded 12 (7B) and 34 (14B) stale-condition harms on replay.
- **Honest repository factorials over 24 bugs × four coder stacks (RQ4/RQ5)**: the contract removes observed unsafe completions but the joint intersection–union superiority test fails under a severe completion floor (OFF-arm sound completion 0–2.1%); no component (state binding / admission / certification) or loop-depth (cap 1/3/5/8) ablation reaches Holm significance — explicitly not claimed as an effectiveness result.

## Methodology

The paper runs a battery of sealed, hash-locked studies on a controlled function-repair harness drawn from the HumanEval portion of FeedbackEval, with visible/challenge/hidden test partitions from HumanEval+ kept sealed until all trajectory matrices validate.

- **Reliability decomposition (Section 3).** Define Y_t∈{0,1} as executable correctness after revision t and e_t as evidence. Repair rate α_e=P(Y_{t+1}=1|Y_t=0,e), regression rate β_e=P(Y_{t+1}=0|Y_t=1,e), with the dynamic equation Q_{t+1}−Q_t=(1−Q_t)α_e − Q_t·β_e. Effective evidence responses are contrasts R_W(e)=α_e−α_{e0}, R_C(e)=β_{e0}−β_e against a matched nonactionable control e_0. Verifier behavior is reported as coverage Cov_v(c)=P(A_v=1), risk Risk_v(c)=P(Y=0|A_v=1), excess joint false acceptance D_ij(c), and conditional φ — all operating-point-dependent.
- **Trajectory study (4.2).** Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct (temp 0.3, top-p 0.9), 30 tasks × 6 evidence conditions × 5 seeds × 3 forced revisions = 900 trajectories / 2,700 revisions. Evidence conditions: current binary, current trace, stale binary, stale trace, unavailable, flipped-binary stress.
- **Common-state intervention (4.3).** Freeze one wrong and one correct code state per task (27 tasks have both), then branch 9 state×evidence conditions (current binary/trace, unavailable, matched control; correct state also gets a stale wrong trace); 5 seeds → 1,215 branches for Qwen2.5-7B, mirrored by a prespecified Qwen2.5-14B replication (another 1,215). This removes the post-treatment risk-set bias of free-running trajectories.
- **Verifier study.** Native self-declaration, Qwen2.5-7B/14B (same family), DeepSeek-Coder-6.7B (cross-family), and an executable challenge verifier; 13,500 verdicts and 18,000 paired policy outcomes via four stopping-rule replays.
- **Prospective paired online comparison (4.5).** 540 rollouts (27 tasks × 2 start states × 5 seeds × baseline/guarded arms) with baseline committing every parseable patch and the guarded arm binding evidence to current state, gating admission on the challenge suite, and retaining a last-known-good checkpoint.
- **Repository-agent factorial (4.6).** 24 historical bugs from 9 Python projects, hash-locked local Qwen2.5-14B in typed actions (search/view/edit/shell/test/submit/escalate), contract OFF vs ON × current vs stale diagnostic evidence × 3 seeds = 288 rollouts, with the co-primary intersection–union decision requiring unsafe-completion superiority and sound-completion non-inferiority at a −5-point margin. Three additional frozen replications (Qwen2.5-Coder-32B-AWQ, Qwen3-Coder-Next-FP8, Devstral Small 2 24B), a 2×2×2 component ablation (576 rollouts), and a depth factorial (caps 1/3/5/8, 576 rollouts) were all preregistered before outcomes.
- **Inference.** Task (not revision) is the sampling unit; task-cluster bootstrap (10,000–200,000 draws), paired sign-flip exact tests, and Holm correction over preregistered families; all artifacts carry SHA-256 lineage.
- **Contract + StateSeal (Section 6).** The reference implementation was developed *after* the empirical results as an executable realization of the mechanically enforceable subset, and is validated only by deterministic conformance cases, not by the study data.

## Results & Highlights

- **Correctness is non-absorbing (RQ1).** With current traces, current correctness falls from **0.820 (rev 1) → 0.673 (rev 2) → 0.693 (rev 3)** on 900 HumanEval trajectories, while ever-correct rises to **0.847 → 0.853**; at revision 3, **16.0%** of trajectories had been correct and then regressed. Current-binary loss is lower (8.0%) but still nonzero.
- **Stale evidence resurrects fixed bugs (RQ2, 14B replication).** From correct starts, stale traces harm **34/135 (0.252)** vs **4/135 (0.030)** for current traces — a **+22.2-point** contrast with task-cluster 95% CI **[8.9, 37.0]** and exact Holm **p=0.0337**. Concrete cases: HumanEval/10 and /89 regress in 5/5 stale seeds for 7B (in /89 a stale Caesar-shift-12 report flips the fixed shift-4 back to 12 every seed).
- **Repair is payload-, provenance-, and coder-dependent (RQ2).** For 14B, *unavailable* evidence repairs **126/135 (0.933)** wrong starts — more than current trace (120/135), binary (115/135), or control (119/135) — so more feedback is not monotonically useful to the stronger coder.
- **Verifier quality ≠ independence (RQ3).** At matched 70% coverage, risks are Qwen-7B **0.050**, Qwen-14B **0.041**, DeepSeek-6.7B **0.240** (Qwen-14B − DeepSeek = −19.9 pts, bootstrap CI [−26.4, −9.5]). Yet Qwen-7B/Qwen-14B conditional φ = **0.641** (CI [0.096, 0.935]) with excess joint false acceptance **0.060** (CI [0.001, 0.133]); Qwen-14B/DeepSeek φ is only **0.021** but DeepSeek's marginal risk is much higher. All three pairs show positive excess-joint intervals at 75% coverage.
- **Executable gating shifts errors without raising sound completion (RQ4 replay).** Self-only stopping accepts 99.8% at **29.5% risk** and **70.3% sound completion**; a visible gate cuts risk to **2.4%** and raises sound completion to **77.8%**; the challenge gate gives **0.000 observed risk** at 77.8% coverage (one-sided 95% zero-event bound over 30 clusters = 9.5%). Strict freshness (Qwen-14B + current-evidence challenge) costs coverage: **38.4%** coverage, 2.27 mean rounds.
- **Prospective bundled guard.** From correct starts, baseline harms **13/135** branches and finishes correct on 122/135; the guarded arm has **0 observed harm** and retains correctness on **135/135** (−9.6-pt harm contrast, CI [−20.7, 0.0], sign-flip p=0.25). From wrong starts repair drops **100/135 → 93/135**; the joint unsafe+sound criterion fails.
- **Repository factorials hit a floor (RQ5).** Contract OFF yields 5 accepted completions (3 unsafe from current evidence, 2 unsafe from stale); Contract ON yields **0/72** in each evidence arm — it removes every observed unsafe completion but also the only observed sound one. Only **1/288** rollout fully resolves; **276/288 (95.8%)** exhaust the 15-action budget. Unsafe-completion contract effect −2.78 pts (CI [−8.33, 0.00]), missing strict superiority; sound-completion −0.69 pts (CI [−2.08, 0.00]), passing the floor-limited −5-pt non-inferiority margin. Component ablation: certification has the largest descriptive sound-completion effect **+6.25 pts** (CI [0.69, 13.19]) but Holm p=0.753; depth cap-1-to-cap-8 sound effect −0.23 pts (Holm p=1.0).
- **StateSeal conformance.** All **35** deterministic failure-injection cases and 3/3 managed Go/Python/Node.js fixtures pass; replay shows state binding intercepts all 12 (7B) and 34 (14B) stale-condition harms, reducing total harm **19→2** and **69→33**.

## Authors & Credibility

- Authors are **Xueping Gao, Jianwei Yang, Qiang Yang** at **Alibaba Cloud** (Hangzhou); the work is to appear at the **AgenticDev 2026 workshop** (Oct 12, 2026, Munich). The framing is unusually disciplined for an industry paper: sealed test partitions, hash-locked candidates, SHA-256 lineage on every artifact, task-as-sampling-unit inference, task-cluster bootstrap with exact dynamic-programming sign-flip tests, and Holm correction over **preregistered** families — including separate preregistration of the 14B replication, the three stack replications, and the component/depth factorials before outcomes were inspected.
- Credibility is reinforced by the explicit negative framing: the paper repeatedly states the reference implementation is **not** evidence of improved competence, calls out its own floor-limited non-inferiority passes as weak evidence about liveness, and discloses that private suites and licensed source are withheld (only a numerical reconstruction package and the StateSeal conformance artifact are public).
- The reference list is appropriately broad (Huang et al. on self-correction failure, Kamoi et al. survey, Tyen et al. on locate-vs-fix, Zhang et al. on small-model verifiers, FeedbackEval, EvalPlus, SWE-bench, SWE-agent, Agentless, Self-Refine, Reflexion, EviACT, Mnemosyne, FixedBench, SpecBench, AgentLens, in-toto/SLSA/OPA).

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty.** The core conceptual move — distinguishing *proposal search* from *completion reliability* and decomposing it into preservation / provenance / verification / stopping — is genuinely useful and not a restatement of "feedback quality matters." The authors are careful to scope what is novel (state-bound evidence provenance tied to transitions, plus the typed-action state machine) against a dense related-work field that already includes EviACT (evidence guards), Mnemosyne (transactional admission), FixedBench (action bias), and PAIR-Bench (preservation). The conditional-dependence framing (P(A_iA_j|Y=0)=f_i·f_j+D_ij) is a clean, citable formalization.
- **Rigor.** High. Task-clustered bootstrap, exact sign-flip tests, Holm correction over preregistered families, sealed hidden partitions, frozen candidates, an ITT denominator that keeps invalid generations, and a separate secondary 14B-minus-7B paired analysis. The post-treatment risk-set bias in free-running trajectories is correctly diagnosed and fixed by the common-state design. This is the strongest part of the paper.
- **Reproducibility.** Mixed and honestly disclosed. Prompts, private suites, and full lineage are retained internally and **not** released (licensing/evaluator privacy); the StateSeal artifact and a numerical reconstruction package are public. Task identifiers, candidate indices, sources, and hashes are recorded. A reader can audit conformance and re-derive the statistics but cannot fully rerun generation from scratch.
- **Significance.** Moderate and appropriately scoped. The headline mechanism (stale evidence resurrects fixed bugs; quality ≠ independence; looping is non-absorbing) is real and demonstrated with a Holm-surviving contrast on the 14B stale-trace condition. But the practical payoff — the contract improving repository repair — is **not** demonstrated: the four repository stacks are dominated by a completion floor (95.8% budget exhaustion, OFF-arm sound completion 0–2.1%), and every co-primary joint test fails. The paper's value is therefore conceptual/evaluative (a better measurement vocabulary and a falsifiable contract) rather than a deployed reliability gain.
- **Red flags / overclaiming checks.** The authors largely police themselves: they explicitly say StateSeal is "an executable specification and conformance artifact, not evidence of improved repair competence," and they do not pool coders or claim a capacity effect from the 14B-minus-7B interaction (p=0.3615). Two residual concerns worth noting: (1) **the only Holm-significant human-subject-style result (the +22.2-pt stale-trace harm) rests on a 14B model and 27 tasks pre-selected for 7B-observed repairability** — external validity to other model families and to non-HumanEval code is untested, and HumanEval is notoriously in training data; (2) **one-step staleness is an experimenter-constructed intervention, not the natural prevalence of staleness in real agent runs**, so the absolute harm rate should not be read as a field deployment number. The paper acknowledges both, but the abstract's prominent placement of "34/135 vs 4/135" could be misread as a population estimate.

## Strengths

- A clean, transferable reliability vocabulary: current-vs-ever-correct, repair (α) vs regression (β), risk–coverage, conditional dependence, sound completion — with the dynamic equation showing exactly when continuation is harmful in expectation.
- Methodologically tight common-state design that removes the post-treatment risk-set bias plaguing free-running trajectory studies; the prespecified 14B replication that actually *changes the transition kernel* (unavailable evidence beats trace for the stronger coder) is a nice demonstration that evidence effects are coder-specific, not payload-only.
- Intellectual honesty is exceptional for the genre: preregistered families, floor-limited non-inferiority passes labeled as weak, the reference implementation explicitly decoupled from the empirical claims, and a Threats section that names the construct-validity limits of one-step staleness and exact-code churn.
- The typed-action state machine (`Keep | Patch(code) | Escalate`) is a small but immediately useful engineering prescription: the 7B current-trace harms on HumanEval/137 are all `STATUS: DONE` outputs with no code, and a fail-closed parser mapping them to `Keep` would have retained the checkpoint.

## Limitations

- **External validity is narrow.** HumanEval/FeedbackEval tasks (likely in training data), 27 tasks pre-selected for 7B repairability, one scaffold, four coder stacks that vary jointly (model/tokenizer/adapter/runtime confounded), 24 repository bugs from only 9 projects. The authors flag all of this.
- **The headline stale-trace harm is induced, not observed.** One-step staleness is an intervention; its real-world prevalence and magnitude in production agent runs are unknown.
- **No demonstration of practical repair improvement.** Every repository co-primary joint test fails; the contract suppresses unsafe completions but also suppresses the only sound ones under a near-zero completion floor. Component and depth ablations are all Holm-nonsignificant.
- **Verifier family and capacity are confounded**, and self-acceptance (declarative) differs from repository acceptance (which adds the ON-arm's frozen checkpoint rule), muddying direct comparisons.
- **Reproducibility is partial** — private suites and licensed source withheld; full trajectory re-execution is not possible for reviewers.
- **Statistical power is genuinely thin** in the repository studies (5, 12, 6, 4 accepted completions across the four stacks), so the −5-point non-inferiority margin "weakly constrains liveness" by the authors' own admission.

## Relevance

对关注 coding agent / LLM 自我纠错可靠性、修订循环停机策略与 verifier 依赖性的研究方向高度相关：它把"循环 ≠ 可靠性"形式化为可测的转移核与证据-状态绑定契约，直接呼应了 self-correction 失败、verifier 偏好与弱停止策略等核心痛点，并提供了一套可借鉴的密封评估协议（current-vs-ever-correct、共态干预、风险-覆盖-依赖联合报告）。对于做 agent benchmark、agent 框架可靠性工程或形式化 agent 契约的研究者，这份工作的方法论与负面的 repository 结果都值得引用与复现。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the **stale-evidence resurrection** mechanism generalize beyond HumanEval — e.g., to SWE-bench-style multi-file tasks where execution traces are larger and provenance tracking is harder? The 14B result is striking but rests on 27 toy tasks.
- How often does **one-step (or k-step) staleness arise naturally** in production agent loops (Reflexion/SWE-agent style), and does the induced +22.2-pt harm rate overestimate or underestimate field reality?
- Can the **conditional dependence φ** between same-family verifiers be broken cheaply — e.g., by test-time augmentation, prompt-order randomization, or genuinely orthogonal oracles (property-based tests, formal specs) — and how does that interact with the D_ij additive penalty in Eq. 8?
- The repository factorials are dominated by a **95.8% budget-exhaustion floor**. Would a more capable base coder (or longer action budgets) lift the floor enough for the contract's unsafe-completion superiority to become identifiable, or does the contract's abstention inherently cap liveness?
- The typed-action prescription (`Keep | Patch | Escalate`) intercepts the `STATUS: DONE`-without-code failures on HumanEval/137. How much of the observed regression in modern agents is similarly **parser/action-schema failure** rather than semantic regression, and is a fail-closed action parser a higher-ROI intervention than the full evidence-binding contract?
- The paper treats verifier risk at a *fixed* coverage; in deployment, **calibration drift** over time and across projects is the real risk. How should the contract's "declared operating point" be re-calibrated online without leaking hidden-test signal back into generation?
