> **一句话总结：** 本文将 MLLM 中的"视觉注意力汇聚（attention sink）"现象重新表述为一种跨文本到图像的、与查询无关的广义"结构偏置"，并提出无需训练、即插即用的 SPAR 方法——通过显著性引导净化结构噪声并自适应重分配注意力预算，在 POPE/CHAIR/GaslightingBench 等基准上以极低延迟同时缓解自发幻觉与诱导式"gaslighting"幻觉。

> **TL;DR:** SPAR reframes MLLM "visual attention sinks" as a query-invariant structural bias diluting the semantic visual signal, and removes it via a training-free, plug-and-play two-stage intervention (saliency-guided purification + confidence-aware redistribution) injected only into the first 2 Transformer layers. On LLaVA-v1.5, POPE Accuracy rises 76.69→85.20 and F1 79.72→85.42 over Regular decoding; on GaslightingBench, after-negation accuracy reaches 41.74% (vs. 24.71% baseline and 40.95% for GasEraser) at near-zero extra latency (0.42 vs. 1.05 s/it for GasEraser), and SPAR further stacks on top of AGLA/ONLY/VAR.

## 核心贡献

- **形式化"语义-结构解耦"假设（SSD）**：从信息论与贝叶斯推断视角，将注意力流形建模为"潜在语义注意力 + 刚性结构偏置 + 随机噪声"的叠加，并证明当视觉信噪比（SNR）趋于零时，后验分布会向后坍缩到语言先验，统一解释了自发幻觉与诱导式幻觉两种现象。
- **提出 SPAR 训练免修方法**：包含"显著性引导的结构偏置净化"（用视觉特征方差构造显著性图，调制性地减去结构偏置模板）与"自适应概率重分配"（按稀疏度置信度恢复被移除的概率预算）两个阶段，避免单纯抑制高频注意力带来的语义擦除与噪声放大。
- **极低开销的高效注入**：只需在 MLLM 的前 2 层注入 SPAR 即可达到峰值性能（对比 GasEraser 需要修改 16 层），推理延迟几乎与原生解码持平。
- **强兼容性与广覆盖评测**：可与 AGLA、ONLY、VAR 等已有解码期方法叠加增益；在 POPE、CHAIR、GaslightingBench，以及 MMMU/AI2Diagram/MMBench 三大扩展基准上同时缓解自发与诱导式幻觉。
- **揭示结构偏置的普适性**：通过 Pólya 球模型类比和 InternVL2-8B（1792 个动态视觉 token）的实验，论证结构偏置在固定分辨率与动态分辨率模型中均普遍存在。

## Key Contributions

- Formalizes the **Semantic-Structural Decoupling (SSD)** hypothesis using information theory and Bayesian inference, modeling the attention manifold as latent semantic attention plus rigid structural bias plus noise, with a corollary showing the posterior collapses to the linguistic prior as visual SNR→0 — a unified account of both spontaneous and induced hallucination.
- Proposes **SPAR**, a training-free, plug-and-play intervention combining (i) saliency-guided structural-bias purification that uses visual feature variance to modulate the subtraction of a query-invariant bias template, and (ii) confidence-aware adaptive probability redistribution that restores the removed attention budget only to confident semantic signals.
- Demonstrates **extreme efficiency**: peak performance with injection into only the **first 2 Transformer layers** versus GasEraser's 16, yielding near-zero extra latency and positioning SPAR as "fast & robust" on the efficiency–performance frontier.
- Shows **broad compatibility and coverage**: SPAR stacks on top of AGLA, ONLY, and VAR, and is evaluated across spontaneous hallucination (POPE, CHAIR) and induced gaslighting (GaslightingBench, MMMU, AI2Diagram, MMBench) on both LLaVA-v1.5 and LLaVA-NeXT.
- Provides **mechanistic analysis** (Pólya-urn self-reinforcement, layer-wise spurious-attention budgets, persistence of bias in InternVL2-8B's 1,792 dynamic tokens) arguing the structural bias is a general phenomenon rather than an isolated sink-token artifact.

## Methodology

SPAR is grounded in a **Semantic-Structural Decoupling** view of the MLLM attention manifold. The authors treat the observed text-to-image attention matrix `A_obs` as an additive noise model: `A_obs(T,V) = A*_sem(T,V) + λ·A_struct(V) + ζ`, where `A_struct` is the query-invariant component (Eq. 2, estimated by marginalizing over the text dimension), `λ` is a per-head modulation coefficient, and `ζ` is stochastic approximation error. Under the unit-sum constraint and softmax's exponential amplification, structural bias acts as static noise that lowers the visual signal-to-noise ratio `SNR = ‖A*_sem‖_F / ‖λ·A_struct + ζ‖_F`. They prove (Corollary 1, sketch in Appendix A) that as SNR→0 the posterior `P(y|V,T)` converges in KL to the linguistic prior `P(y|T)` — formally explaining both spontaneous hallucination (visual likelihood attenuated by internal bias) and induced/gaslighting hallucination (a deceptive prompt biases the prior that the weak visual likelihood cannot counteract).

**Stage 1 — Saliency-Guided Structural Bias Purification.** Building on the observation that high-feature-variance visual tokens act as attention sinks, SPAR derives a per-token saliency score `S_j` from the variance of visual hidden states `h_j`, normalized and squashed by a sigmoid (Eqs. 9–10). A structural bias template `Ā_j = E_{i∼T}[A_{i,j}]` is estimated by marginalizing the text dimension (Eq. 11). Purification then subtracts the bias modulated by saliency: `A^pur_{i,j} = ReLU(A_{i,j} − λ·(1+βS_j)·Ā_j)` (Eq. 12). The saliency gating prevents semantic erasure of legitimately attended background context and avoids amplifying low-frequency noise.

**Stage 2 — Adaptive Probability Redistribution.** Purification reduces total attention mass, so naïve renormalization would amplify residual noise. Instead SPAR computes a per-text-token sparsity ratio `ρ_i = max_j A^pur_{i,j} / Σ_j A^pur_{i,j}` and a confidence score `γ_i = Sigmoid(k·(ρ_i − τ_sparsity))` (Eq. 13). The removed budget `Δ_i` is restored proportional to `γ_i`, strengthening weak-but-correct semantic alignments while keeping ambiguous noise suppressed (Eq. 14).

**Implementation.** Injected into only the first 2 Transformer layers of LLaVA-v1.5 and LLaVA-NeXT. Hyperparameters: λ=1.0, β=1.0, τ_sparsity=0.1. Default decoding is multinomial sampling (Regular). GPU: NVIDIA RTX A6000.

## Results & Highlights

- **POPE (LLaVA-v1.5, Table 1):** SPAR lifts Accuracy from **76.69 → 85.20** (+8.51) and F1 from **79.72 → 85.42** (+5.70) over Regular decoding; combined with VAR it reaches the best F1 of **85.59** and the lowest CHAIR_I of **12.4**.
- **CHAIR (LLaVA-v1.5, Table 1):** SPAR reduces CHAIR_S from **55.0 → 49.0** and CHAIR_I from **16.3 → 14.0** under Regular decoding; stacked on ONLY it cuts CHAIR_S to **31.4** (from ONLY's 49.8) and CHAIR_I to **12.5**.
- **GaslightingBench (Table 2):** After-negation accuracy on LLaVA-v1.5 reaches **41.74%** (vs. 24.71% baseline, 33.89% with prompt hardening, and **40.95%** for GasEraser), while pre-negation accuracy is preserved (63.71% vs. 63.25%); on LLaVA-NeXT SPAR reaches **34.04%** after negation vs. GasEraser's 30.58%.
- **Inference latency (Table 6):** SPAR adds **3.40 s/it** end-to-end latency on CHAIR, below ONLY (3.49), far below AGLA (8.55) and VAR (59.58); per-iteration cost on LLaVA-v1.5 is **0.42 s/it vs. GasEraser's 1.05 s/it**, and GasEraser needs **16 layers** whereas SPAR peaks with just **2** (Fig. 7).
- **Cross-domain gaslighting (Table 9, MMMU/AI2Diagram/MMBench):** SPAR raises average after-attack accuracy to **36.93%** vs. LLaVA-v1.5 baseline **26.14%** and GasEraser **33.34%**, with gains on every individual dataset (e.g., MMBench after-attack **46.05%** vs. GasEraser's 41.77%).
- **Ablations (Tables 4–5):** Text-side marginalization (After-Negation 41.74%) matches joint image+text marginalization (41.18) and far exceeds image-only (34.28); removing Sparsity Guidance costs 5.85 points before-negation and removing Probability Redistribution drops after-negation to 33.91, confirming each component's necessity.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty (genuine):** The reframing from isolated "sink tokens" to a *generalized, query-invariant text-to-image structural bias* is a real conceptual advance over VAR (which reallocates specific sink tokens) and GasEraser (which targets sink tokens under pressure). The Bayesian posterior-collapse proof gives the empirical claim a clean theoretical hook. However, the additive noise model `A_obs = A*_sem + λ·A_struct + ζ` is an *assumption*, not derived; `A*_sem` is never independently measurable, so the SNR quantity is essentially a motivational construct rather than a quantity that is computed and validated against hallucination rates.
- **Rigor (mixed):** Headline baselines are strong and current (AGLA CVPR'25, ONLY ICCV'25, VAR ICLR'25, GasEraser 2025), and SPAR is shown to *stack* on them rather than only compete head-on — a credible, non-cherry-picked framing. The GasEraser comparison is fair: hyperparameters for GasEraser are reported (τ=20, ρ=0.6, α=0.01/0.1, p=0.6) and tuned to avoid degrading non-gaslighting accuracy. **Missing ablations worth flagging:** no evaluation on closed/proprietary models (GPT-4V, Gemini) or on stronger open models beyond 7B (InternVL2-8B is analyzed only for the *bias phenomenon*, never as a hallucination-mitigation target), and no human evaluation of CHAIR captions despite CHAIR being noisy.
- **Data quirk:** The gaslighting protocol explicitly adopts a "Hardened System Instruction" as its *baseline* (Table 2's "+Preemptive Prompt Hardening" row), meaning the 41.74% includes a +9.18-point prompt-engineering boost that is not SPAR's contribution — the SPAR-only marginal gain over prompt hardening is 41.74 − 33.89 = ~7.85 points, which is meaningful but smaller than the headline delta over the 24.71% raw baseline suggests.
- **Reproducibility (good):** Fixed hyperparameters (λ=1.0, β=1.0, τ=0.1, first 2 layers), open models (LLaVA-v1.5/NeXT), single GPU type, and a sensitivity analysis (Table 8) are all provided. A project page is referenced. Code release is not explicitly promised in the text, which is the main gap.
- **Significance:** Plausible. The "first 2 layers suffice vs. GasEraser's 16" efficiency result is the most practically valuable claim, and the stacking result implies the bias SPAR removes is orthogonal to prior interventions.
- **Red flags:** The Pólya-urn and RoPE-asymmetry origin story (Sec. 3, Appendix B.1) is presented as analysis but is essentially illustrative — no causal intervention (e.g., ablating RoPE) verifies it. CHAIR is evaluated with max generation length 128, which can mechanically change object-invention rates; this standardizes the comparison but should be noted.

## Strengths

- Unified theoretical framing (SSD + posterior-collapse corollary) that explains *both* spontaneous and induced hallucination from a single mechanism.
- Training-free, plug-and-play, and strikingly cheap: 2-layer injection, ~0.42 s/it overhead, lower end-to-end latency than ONLY/AGLA/VAR.
- Genuinely complementary — measurable gains when stacked on VAR/ONLY/AGLA, indicating it removes a bias class that prior methods miss.
- Broad and consistent evaluation: 6 benchmarks spanning discriminative, generative, and adversarial gaslighting settings on two model families.
- Honest efficiency story backed by the layer-sweep (Fig. 7) showing GasEraser degrades if not given ~16 layers while SPAR needs only 2.

## Limitations

- Evaluated only on 7B-class open MLLMs (LLaVA-v1.5, LLaVA-NeXT); no results on proprietary frontier models or larger (e.g., 13B/70B) open models, so scalability is unproven.
- The structural-bias *origin* (Pólya urn / RoPE asymmetry) is argued by analogy and visualization, not by causal ablation — it remains a hypothesis.
- `A*_sem` and SNR are defined formally but never measured or used as a predictive signal, so the theory is motivating rather than operational.
- Headline gaslighting gains conflate SPAR with a hardened system prompt used as the shared baseline; the SPAR-attributable portion (~7.85 pts) is smaller than the table's top-line delta implies.
- CHAIR evaluated at max length 128; code release not explicitly stated; no human evaluation of caption fidelity.
- Per-layer "first 2 layers" choice is empirically tuned for LLaVA; whether it generalizes to architectures with different early-layer roles (e.g., InternVL2) for *mitigation* (not just bias visualization) is untested.

## Relevance

本文与多模态大模型幻觉缓解及注意力机制可解释性研究方向高度相关：它提出的"结构偏置 vs. 语义注意力"解耦视角，对理解 MLLM 中注意力汇聚、视觉接地失效以及对抗性"gaslighting"鲁棒性具有直接参考价值，可作为训练免修推理期干预方法的代表性工作。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does SPAR's first-2-layer injection generalize to larger (13B/70B) and proprietary frontier MLLMs, or is the layer-1/2 bias locus specific to LLaVA's architecture?
- Can the formally defined SNR `‖A*_sem‖_F / ‖λ·A_struct + ζ‖_F` be operationalized (e.g., estimated via the purification residual) and shown to correlate empirically with per-sample hallucination probability?
- Would a causal test — ablating RoPE or replacing it with ALiBi — actually reduce the structural bias as the Pólya-urn hypothesis predicts, providing direct evidence for the claimed origin?
- Since SPAR stacks on VAR/ONLY/AGLA, what is the optimal *combination* and ordering of inference-time interventions, and is there diminishing returns or interference beyond two stacked methods?
- How does SPAR interact with structured visual token compression / token pruning (e.g., in InternVL2-8B's dynamic-tokenization regime) where the bias is shown to persist but mitigation was not measured?
- Is the confidence-aware redistribution (`γ_i` from sparsity) robust to prompts that deliberately dilute attention sparsity, i.e., can an adaptive adversary defeat SPAR by manipulating the very sparsity signal it relies on?
