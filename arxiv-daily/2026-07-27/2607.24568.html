Why this matters
Operators that "work" in floating-point can become pure hardware overhead once quantized to fixed-point silicon — so re-evaluate every learned operator after the cost is real, not before.
Critical edge
Absolute accuracy ≈47% sits far below the 60%+ of learned I/Q CNNs on the same benchmark — and the result rests on only two training seeds, with QAT's sign flipping between them.

What the numbers say

  • Ungated wins all four matched comparisons. Per-seed PTQ deltas: −0.868 (seed 20260716), −0.700 (20260719); QAT deltas: −0.607, −0.625 pp.
  • Bit-exact verification: every one of 352,000 board decisions matches an independent integer reference; 3,760 captured intermediates from seed-20260716 also match.
  • QAT gives no consistent gain — it flips sign between seeds (gated +0.389 vs −0.275 pp; ungated +0.127 vs −0.350 pp), unresolved with only two seeds.
  • SNR profile: high-SNR (10–18 dB) plateaus ~75–77%; low-SNR (−20 to −2 dB) sits near chance (~20%).
  • Dominant confusions: WBFM→AM-DSB 43.61%, QAM64→QAM16 30.73%; AM-SSB is over-predicted (31.20% of all calls).

Strengths & limitations

side-by-side
Strengths

Disciplined to a fault

  • Genuinely controlled: every variable except the gate held constant; 8-bitstream × 2-seed × 2-quant grid isolates the gate and QAT effects.
  • Verification is the standout — 352,000 bit-exact board/reference matches plus 3,760 intermediate-value matches, far beyond typical FPGA-ML reporting.
  • Non-overclaimed: authors state the result is specific to this gate/representation and list when it might not hold.
  • Full cost breakdown — ALMs, registers, DSP hierarchy, cycles, latency, Fmax, slack, with DSP-hierarchy attribution of the +4 blocks.
Limitations

The caveats that bite

  • Only two training seeds; QAT's seed-dependent sign flip cannot be resolved at this sample size.
  • Dated synthetic benchmark — RadioML 2016.10A; no captured-RF, external-dataset, or channel-shift evaluation.
  • Board-level prototype with Virtual JTAG transport, sequential scheduling, register buffering, no M10K — streaming throughput and power/energy uncharacterized.
  • Reproducibility gap: frozen board_test_payloads.hex & feature-cache are missing from the retained checkout (authors' own flag).
  • Low absolute accuracy (≈47%) by design; no test of richer features, lower-rank gating, or different gate precision that could overturn the verdict.

Open questions

where it could flip
Q1

Does the verdict flip with a richer front end (64+ features, temporal I/Q attention), where the dense layer can no longer absorb feature-dependent scaling?

Authors flag, do not test
Q2

Is the seed-dependent QAT sign flip a real instability, or an artifact of starting QAT from a single PTQ-selected checkpoint?

Needs a multi-checkpoint QAT study
Q3

How much of the WBFM→AM-DSB (43.61%) and QAM64→QAM16 (30.73%) confusion is from missing features (FM trajectory, constellation structure) vs. MLP capacity?

Needs targeted feature ablation
Q4

Would moving parameters + I/Q buffer into M10K and adopting a pipelined/streaming schedule shift the resource/latency trade-off enough to change the gate's cost-benefit?

Uncharacterized in this prototype
Q5

Does the gate fail on captured RF or under channel-distribution shift — or is the negative result specific to RadioML 2016.10A's synthetic channel?

No external-data evaluation
