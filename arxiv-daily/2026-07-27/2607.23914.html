The Phase Transition in Online PCA Depends on n/d log(d), not n/d — arxiv.org/abs/2607.23914 ↗

Apratim Dey  ·  Washington University in St. Louis (partly at Stanford, hosted by David Donoho)

Key Metric

γ* = 5.26 ρ* = 0.95   at (θ=1, δ=0.1)

Critical iteration n* scales with d·log d: 16,354 → 484,755 as d goes 500 → 10,000.

Why This Matters

An intrinsic log(d) gap makes Oja statistically suboptimal versus offline PCA whenever n = Θ(d).

Critical Edge

Single rank-one spike, Gaussian isotropic noise, random init — "log d is non-negotiable" is initialization-specific; Wang & Lu hit Θ(d) with warm starts.

01 / METHOD

A drift–martingale recursion on the signed overlap

Pure theory in the proportional-asymptotics tradition. The analytical engine is a Taylor-expanded recursion for the signed overlap ρₖ = ⟨v̂ₖ, v₀⟩, decomposed into drift, cubic, martingale, and remainder.

Setting
Σ = θ²v₀v₀ᵀ + I  ∈ ℝ^{d×d}
Xₖ ~ N(0, Σ)  iid
ṽₖ = ṽ_{k−1} + (δ/d)⟨Xₖ,ṽ_{k−1}⟩Xₖ
v̂ₖ = ṽₖ / ‖ṽₖ‖
Overlap recursion (Lemma 3.2)
ρₖ = (1+α/d)ρ_{k−1} − βρ³_{k−1}/d
      + Mₖ/d + Rₖ/d²
α = δ(θ² − δ/2)
β = δθ²(1 + δ/2)
  • Three regimes by theorem: (1) α<0 ⟹ powerless for all n; (2) subcritical γ<γ* ⟹ overlap → 0; (3) supercritical γ>γ* ⟹ overlap → ρ*.
  • Key device — iid surrogate martingale: replace correlated Mₖ with M̃ₖ = δAₖCₖ via Lemma 6.2, then apply a martingale CLT to obtain the Gaussian G in the critical limit law.
  • Moment bound tames the cubic: E|ρₖ|³ ≤ C·exp(3αk/d)/d^{3/2} (Lemma 6.1) kills the cubic drift in subcritical and critical regimes.
  • Supercritical stability: a Lyapunov/contraction argument on eₖ = ρ²ₖ − ρ²* pins the fixed point.
  • Universality (Thm 5.1): spherical-Oja has identical α, β, and leading-order M̃ₖ^sph — whole proof transfers verbatim.

03 / ASSESSMENT

Strengths & Limitations

Strengths

  • Crystallizes a previously order-only result into exact, interpretable closed-form constants (γ*, ρ*) and a fully specified critical limit law.
  • Honest about scope: explicitly states log d is intrinsic, not proof looseness, and that ρ* plateaus regardless of further samples.
  • Clean self-contained proofs via a reusable drift + martingale + CLT template that plausibly extends to related online eigenvector problems.
  • Satisfying negative result (Thm 5.1): ordinary Oja = spherical Oja at the phase-transition level, foreclosing a natural "use more geometry" improvement.
  • Experiments test the limit distribution via a variance-stabilizing transform T — a higher evidentiary bar than typical theory-paper sims.

Limitations

  • Model rigidity: single rank-one spike, isotropic Gaussian noise, constant step size. Universality to non-Gaussian / non-isotropic noise is unproven here.
  • Top-1 only: no top-k or multi-spike generalization; the recursion exploits rank-one structure.
  • Initialization gap: bridge between random init (n·log d) and Wang & Lu's warm-start (Θ(d)) is left open.
  • Speed vs. accuracy tension: δ*=θ² minimizes search time but simultaneously depresses ρ* — documented, not jointly optimized.
  • Reproducibility: experiments need up to ~1.45M iters on a cluster; no public code release mentioned. Validation is confirmatory, not adversarial.
