Why This Matters

Reported EEG-FM "gains" can collapse — and even reverse to random-init — once you add targeted controls and an out-of-distribution cohort.

Critical EdgeReproducibility gap

Code, split indices, and per-fold results are not released at submission — promised "on request." For a benchmark whose value depends on others re-running it, that is a real gap.

  • CAUEEG is recording-level (no public patient ID)
  • Single-author + LLM-assisted coding
  • BENDR / LaBraM confounded by bandwidth & channel mismatch
01 / METHOD

Six encoders, four benchmarks, one Korean stress test

Models

LaBraM (5.8M), EEGMamba (3.3M, ported to pure PyTorch), CBraMod (4.9M), REVE-Base (69.4M, 60k h on 25k subjects), BENDR (157M), BIOT (3.2M). LEAD excluded — downstream-training exposure unclear.

Datasets

CHB-MIT (ictal, 23 subj), TUH TUAB (253), OpenNeuro ds004504 (AD/FTD), Sleep-EDF (14), plus CAUEEG — Korean, 1,379 recordings / 1,155 patients — as the cross-population stress test. TUH is in-domain for REVE (44% of pretraining).

Protocol

Frozen linear probing (L2 log-reg, C fixed a priori). CHB-MIT & Sleep-EDF use LOSO; TUAB & ds004504 use 5-fold group CV (zero subject overlap); CAUEEG uses recording-level CV with a patient-disjoint held-out bound.

Targeted controls

Random-init (matched arch), raw-signal random features, label permutation, scrambled-label fine-tuning, PCA-vs-Gaussian-random-projection, plus a dataset-identity probe. Subject-level aggregation reported where clinically primary.

03 / ASSESSMENT

Strengths & limitations, side by side

▲ Strengths

  • Intellectual honesty: preregistered correction of the 0.920 CHB-MIT headline → 0.793, rather than silently adopting the lower number.
  • Cross-population stress test is exactly the external-cohort generalisation test the field has been missing.
  • Random-init-beats-pretrained + whitening analysis = a representational (not architectural) indictment.
  • Subject-level aggregation correctly deflates the epoch-level AD advantage.
  • Runs entirely on consumer M3 hardware — no industrial compute barrier.

▼ Limitations

  • CAUEEG is recording-level (no public patient ID); patient-disjoint bound is a single fixed split with wide intervals.
  • Dataset-identity probe cannot isolate site / device / preprocessing / population — establishes membership only.
  • BENDR & LaBraM confounded by sampling-rate, bandwidth, channel-padding mismatches.
  • CHB-MIT uses approximate bipolar coords for REVE; per-epoch z-scoring handicaps the classical comparator.
  • Thin statistical power (n=5 folds); code & splits not released at submission.
04 / OPEN

Questions this leaves open

Does the random-init-beats-pretrained reversal on Korean dementia generalise to other non-Western or clinically heterogeneous cohorts, or is it specific to CAUEEG's preprocessing/sensor combination?

Would an amplitude-aware classical baseline on CHB-MIT erase REVE's +9.3 pp ictal advantage, given per-epoch z-scoring already removes the most discriminative ictal property?

Can the dataset-identity signal be decomposed — by harmonising preprocessing, matching hardware, or controlling clinical composition — to isolate which factor drives AUROC-1.000 separability?

How do conclusions change under fine-tuning (LoRA r=8) vs frozen probing on the Korean cohort — does adaptation recover the pretrained advantage or merely overfit recording-level structure?

Do more recent or larger EEG-FMs (LEAD, EEGPT, post-REVE) pass the same targeted-control suite, or repeat the dataset-identity dominance and random-init deficit?
