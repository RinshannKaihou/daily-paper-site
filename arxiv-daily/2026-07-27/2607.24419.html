Why this matters

Reframes LLM use: an offline agent produces an auditable, clinically-grounded, inference-free classifier — escaping the cost and hallucination of online LLM inference.

Critical edge

Retrospective-only — no prospective or clinical validation, and the refinement loop's total compute (LLM calls + repeated retrains) is not reported.

How the loop works

Three stages · fixed problem contract
STAGE 01

Criteria-to-Measurement Compilation(CMC)

Clinical ECG criteria are formalized as (concept, measurement, condition) — e.g. PR > 200ms → 1°AV block. The LLM emits deterministic measurement functions, validated on a representative subset until tests pass.

key → reference path per function; emits "indeterminate" on weak evidence.
STAGE 02

Evidence-Grounded Failure Review(EGFR)

Selects a targeted failure batch (labels that most move validation score), builds per-case evidence (X, y, probs, ŷ, measurements), then contrastively diagnoses against comparable correctly-classified cases.

key → isolates systematic limits, not idiosyncratic errors.
STAGE 03

Diagnostic Classifier Revision

Conditioned on the diagnosed limitation, the LLM writes an executable code revision (architecture + training), not prose. The candidate is kept only if it runs and beats validation; accepted edits enter an audit trail.

key → frozen output; zero LLM at deployment.

Strengths & limitations

Honest read

Strengths

  • Clean split: LLM-as-Designer (offline, frozen) vs. LLM-as-Classifier (online, costly). Deployment needs zero LLM inference.
  • Deterministic, reference-backed measurement functions are genuinely auditable; "indeterminate" fallback is a thoughtful safety mechanism.
  • Contrastive failure review vs. comparable correct cases isolates systematic limits, not idiosyncratic errors — the right epistological move.
  • Consistent gains across three independent benchmarks with controlled variance and a backbone-sensitivity check (Qwen3.5-27B stays competitive).
  • The evolved architecture (unified ResNet → lead separation → cross-lead + temporal attention) shows interpretable, ECG-specific inductive biases.
  • Open-source code and a real cardiologist co-author (Fuwai Hospital) ground the criteria curation.

Limitations

  • Retrospective-only; the paper itself flags no autonomous diagnosis without external clinician oversight. No prospective trial.
  • Total compute of the refinement loop (LLM calls + k=3 candidates × 5 iterations of retrains) is unreported — hard to compare on a total-cost basis.
  • Inherits bias from both the LLM and the curated criteria corpus; the corpus is acknowledged as a risk but not empirically audited.
  • Gains are uneven — largest where waveform criteria are crisp (NST_, ISCA); labels without crisp criteria are not separately analyzed.
  • Backbone identifiers (DeepSeek-V4-Pro, Qwen3.5-27B) are forward-dated; exact revision trajectories may not be reproducible if versions shift.

Open questions

For further exploration
Q1

What is the total compute budget (LLM tokens + GPU-hours) of the full loop, and how do gains degrade under a tighter candidate/iteration budget?

Q2

How sensitive are results to the curated criteria corpus — can the agent detect and reject noisy or conflicting criteria during CMC validation?

Q3

Does EGFR transfer to non-ECG time-series (ICU waveforms, EEG) where deterministic clinical criteria are less crisp?

Q4

Does contrastive diagnosis find truly systematic limits, or overfit the selected failure batch — how stable is ℓ across random batches?

Q5

Since the classifier is frozen, how does it handle distribution shift vs. an LLM-as-Classifier that could adapt online?

Q6

Can the audit trail drive prospective failure attribution on new data — turning a one-time design loop into online monitoring?
