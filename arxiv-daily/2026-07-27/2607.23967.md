> **一句话总结：** 本文为线性模型（heavy-ball + L2）的 grokking 给出严格可解的"权重衰减时钟"机制，证明只有经验零空间中 population-active 的 grokking 子空间贡献慢速 population-risk 尾部，导出精确离散速率 ρ₀ 及 (1−β)/(ηλ) 迭代尺度律，并在合成模型（无拟合参数）与模加任务（log-log 斜率 1.01）上验证。

> **TL;DR:** The paper provides an exactly solvable late-time relaxation theory for grokking under full-batch heavy-ball + coupled L2 weight decay. It decomposes the empirical null space into a "grokking subspace" S (the metric-dependent representative of Ker G_N / Ker G_pop) plus a genuine population-null part, proves only S drives the slow ρ₀^k (or ρ₀^{2k}) tail of the population-risk excess while the training loss is provably blind to it, and recovers the iteration-scale law k_grok ≈ (1−β)/(ηλ)·log(‖Π_g θ₀‖/ε) from exact discrete roots. All identities are verified without fitted parameters on a synthetic linear model (max relative deviation 8.4×10⁻⁵ over a 36-config sweep; coupled vs. decoupled weight-decay rate ratio exactly (1−β)⁻¹), and a modular-addition (p=17) MLP benchmark shows genuine delayed generalization with the measured test-loss excess decay at 4.5×10⁻⁴/step versus the parameter-free prediction 6.0×10⁻⁴/step and the k-at-test-acc-0.9 scaling following (1−β)/(ηλ) with fitted log–log slope 1.01.

## 核心贡献

- **可解机制**：在线性-in-参数模型 + 平方损失 + 全批 heavy-ball + 耦合 L2 下，给出 grokking 晚期松弛的严格可解理论——完整离散模式谱（Theorem 4），无连续时间近似。
- **Grokking 子空间分解**：把插值流形的切空间 Ker G_N 分裂为 S ⊕ Ker G_pop（grokking 子空间 + 真正 population-null），并证明只有 S 进入慢速 population-risk 渐近尾部（Theorem 20），训练损失对所有零空间方向"看不见"（Eq. 36）。
- **权重衰减时钟**：导出精确慢根 ρ₀ 及迭代尺度律 k_grok ≈ (1−β)/(ηλ)·log(‖Π_g θ₀‖/ε)（Corollary 5），并把弱正则极限 ηλ/(1−β)、连续时间 λ/γ 展示为精确根的渐近近似。
- **软 Noether 定律**：把经验零空间平移荷的"软破缺守恒律"直接化为慢坐标的运动方程 m q̈ + γ q̇ + λq = 0，并给出旋转荷的更快的、λ-无关的角度时钟（精确离散对应 Q_k = β^{k−1} Q_1，Remark 14）。
- **优化器可证差异**：耦合 L2 与解耦 weight decay 在相同 (η, λ, β) 下慢速率相差因子 (1−β)（Proposition 6）。
- **因果/可证伪预测 P1–P4**：速率坍缩、对 grokking 分量的对数延迟干预、gauge 控制实验（N_pop/S/N_N^⊥ 三个方向产生可区分的 train/test 签名）、优化器差异。
- **无拟合参数验证**：合成线性模型所有定理身份验证（速率一致到 8.4×10⁻⁵ 相对偏差）；模加 MLP benchmark 测得 log-log 斜率 1.01。

## Key Contributions

- An exactly solvable linear core: full discrete mode spectrum (Theorem 4) of heavy-ball + coupled L2, including the exact slow root ρ₀, with no continuous-time or overdamped approximation.
- A kernel-mismatch decomposition S = Ker G_N ∩ (Ker G_pop)^⊥ (the "grokking subspace", metric-dependent representative of the intrinsic quotient Ker G_N / Ker G_pop), with a theorem (Theorem 20) proving only the S-component enters the slow ρ₀^k / ρ₀^{2k} tail of the excess population risk; training loss is provably blind to all null directions (Eq. 36).
- An iteration-scale grokking clock k_grok ≈ (1−β)/(ηλ)·log(‖Π_g θ₀‖/ε) (Corollary 5) recovered from exact roots, plus continuous-time companion with exact rate r_slow = (γ − √(γ² − 4mλ))/(2m).
- A "soft Noether law" framing: translation charges Q_b = ⟨p, b⟩ along empirical-null directions satisfy a softly broken conservation law that *is* the slow-mode oscillator; rotation charges give a faster, λ-independent angular clock Q_k = β^{k−1} Q_1.
- A falsifiable optimizer-dependence result (Proposition 6): coupled L2 vs. decoupled weight decay differ in slow rate by the factor (1−β) at fixed (η, λ, β).
- Four causal predictions (P1–P4): rate collapse onto −log ρ₀, logarithmic shift under rescaling of Π_g ξ, gauge-control signatures distinguishing N_pop / S / N_N^⊥, and the optimizer distinction.
- No-fitted-parameter verification on a synthetic solvable model (max relative deviation 8.4×10⁻⁵ across 36 hyperparameter configs; coupled/decoupled ratio matches (1−β)⁻¹) plus a first nonlinear test on modular addition (p=17).

## Methodology

The paper proceeds from an exactly solvable core outward as controlled perturbations:

1. **Setup.** Model f(x; θ), squared loss, full-batch heavy-ball with coupled L2: v_{k+1} = βv_k − η∇U(θ_k), θ_{k+1} = θ_k + v_{k+1}, with U = L_N + (λ/2)‖θ‖². Empirical and population observability Gram matrices G_N, G_pop are defined via Jacobians J_x(θ). The nested kernels satisfy Ker G_pop ⊆ Ker G_N almost surely (Lemma 3), and the parameter space splits as R^P = N_N^⊥ ⊕ S ⊕ N_pop, where S = Ker G_N ∩ (Ker G_pop)^⊥ is the "grokking subspace".

2. **Exact linear theory (Sec. 3).** For f = ϕ(x)^⊤ θ the recurrence decouples along the eigenbasis of G_N. On the empirical null space the recursion q_{k+1} = (1+β−ηλ)q_k − β q_{k−1} is data-independent with characteristic roots ρ₀ > β > ρ₁. Theorem 4 establishes a strict rate hierarchy: under √(ηλ) < (1−β) and a no-sign-flip condition, every training-visible direction relaxes strictly faster than every null direction.

3. **Soft Noether law (Sec. 4).** A Hamiltonian formulation on phase space with kinetic metric mI, m = (1+β)/2, casts heavy ball as a dissipative Hamiltonian flow. The affine prediction-preserving symmetries form the Euclidean algebra euc(r_N) = R^{r_N} ⋊ so(r_N) (Theorem 10). The translation charge Q_b = ⟨p, b⟩ satisfies dQ_b/dt = −(γ/m)Q_b − λq_b, which is literally the slow-mode oscillator m q̈ + γ q̇ + λq = 0 (Theorem 12). Rotation charges relax on the λ-independent clock τ_ang = m/γ (Corollary 13, Remark 14).

4. **Population-risk tail (Sec. 5).** Because L_pop is quadratic, L_pop(θ_k) − L_pop(θ_eq) decomposes into A ρ₀^k + B ρ₀^{2k} + R_k, where A = ⟨Π_g ∇L_pop(θ_eq), w₀⟩, B = ½ s₀^⊤ G_pop s₀, and R_k is bounded by a faster scale ϱ^k. A compatibility condition Π_g ∇L_pop(θ_eq) = 0 selects the doubled-rate ρ₀^{2k} regime (Theorem 20), producing the excess-risk relaxation time k_grok ≈ (1−β)/(2ηλ)·log(B/ε) (Corollary 22).

5. **Local nonlinear extension (Sec. 6).** First-order identities acquire explicit remainders under assumptions on Jacobian Lipschitzness, constant rank, and spectral gap, yielding conditional two-sided bounds with an explicit perturbation floor F (Proposition 29); nothing is claimed below the floor or in discrete time.

6. **Verification (Sec. 7).** E1–E3 are identity checks in a synthetic model (P=60, d=20, N=8) where every subspace and rate is closed-form. E1 verifies the rate hierarchy and the two population tails. E2 sweeps 36 (λ, η, β) configs checking collapse onto the exact −log ρ₀ and the iteration-scale prediction. E3 tests causal interventions (rescaling Π_g shifts k_grok by log c/(−log ρ₀); gauge-control signature of N_pop/S/N_N^⊥; coupled vs. decoupled ratio). A nonlinear modular-addition (p=17) benchmark provides the first external test of (P1).

## Results & Highlights

- **Exact rate collapse (P1, E2):** Across 36 configurations of λ ∈ {3×10⁻⁴, 10⁻³, 3×10⁻³}, η ∈ {0.02, 0.05, 0.1}, β ∈ {0.5, 0.8, 0.9, 0.95}, measured slow decay rate of ‖Π_g ξ_k‖ agrees with the exact discrete prediction −log ρ₀ with **maximal relative deviation 8.4×10⁻⁵**, while the asymptotic rate ηλ/(1−β) visibly deviates at large ηλ/(1−β)². The grokking-iteration collapse k_grok vs log(‖q₀‖/ε)/(−log ρ₀) agrees within 2.2% with no free parameter.
- **Two population tails (E1):** For the compatible teacher, excess population risk decays at the doubled rate ρ₀^{2k} = (predicted −2 log ρ₀ = 4.1694980×10⁻³ per step; measured 4.1703601×10⁻³); for the generic teacher, at ρ₀^k (predicted −log ρ₀ = 2.0847490×10⁻³; measured 2.0848660×10⁻³). Training loss contains no slow component, exactly as Eq. 36 requires.
- **Angular clock (E1):** Discrete rotation charge Q_k follows the exact Casoratian law β^{k−1}Q_1 down to numerical floor (predicted −log β = 5.1293083×10⁻²; measured 5.1293294×10⁻²).
- **Causal intervention (P2, E3):** Rescaling the grokking component at k₀ = 3000 by c ∈ [0.03, 30] leaves training predictions unchanged to 3.0×10⁻¹⁴ and shifts k_grok linearly in log c with measured slope 479.67405 versus predicted 1/(−log ρ₀) = 479.67405.
- **Gauge control (P3, E3):** Unit perturbations along N_pop change neither training nor population predictions (both at roundoff); along S change only population predictions; along N_N^⊥ change both.
- **Optimizer dependence (P4, E3):** At (η, λ, β) = (0.05, 2×10⁻³, 0.95), coupled L2 slow rate −log ρ₀ = 2.0847490×10⁻³ vs. decoupled decay rate −log(1−ηλ) = 1.0000500×10⁻⁴, a ratio of exactly (1−β)⁻¹ ≈ 20×, both measured to within 10⁻⁷ of prediction.
- **Modular addition (Sec. 7.5, nonlinear):** p=17, width-128 two-layer MLP with quadratic activation. Training accuracy reaches 1.0 at k ≈ 400; test accuracy passes 0.5 at k ≈ 7000 and 0.9 at k ≈ 1.05×10⁴ (delay ratio ≈ 26). Across a 27-config factorial sweep (η ∈ {0.2, 0.5, 1.0}, λ ∈ {1,2,3}×10⁻⁴, β ∈ {0.8, 0.9, 0.95}), the iteration at which test accuracy first reaches 0.9 scales with (1−β)/(ηλ) with fitted log–log slope **1.01**. Test-loss excess decays at 4.5×10⁻⁴/step versus the parameter-free prediction 6.0×10⁻⁴/step (≈25% agreement).

## Authors & Credibility

Single author: **Taeyoung Kim**, Center for AI and Natural Sciences, Korea Institute for Advanced Study (KIAS), supported by a KIAS Individual Grant (AP102201). The paper is mathematically dense, with full proofs delegated to appendices (B.1–B.3) and a general-metric Noether-law appendix (A). The framing is unusually disciplined: the author explicitly enumerates what the mechanism does *not* explain (grokking without weight decay; new population-visible directions from feature learning; mini-batch noise; the abrupt accuracy jump; adaptive optimizers) and states the nonlinear results as conditional bounds with an explicit error floor rather than as equalities. The related-work comparison (Table 3) is honest about the theorem-level differences versus the closest dynamical analyses (Boursier et al. 2025; Xu et al. 2026; Khanh et al. 2026).

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty.** Genuine. The 1/λ slow timescale of post-interpolation norm-minimizing flow is *not* new (Boursier et al. 2025). The novelty is the decomposition of the interpolation-manifold tangent into S ⊕ N_pop with a proof that only S enters the slow empirical-null tail (Theorem 20, including the ρ₀^{2k}-vs-ρ₀^k dichotomy governed by an explicit compatibility condition), the exact discrete root ρ₀ rather than a continuous-time approximation, and the coupled-vs-decoupled optimizer distinction (Proposition 6) — none of which are visible at the level of τ ∝ 1/λ. The soft Noether framing is conceptually elegant and connects the slow-mode ODE to a symmetry-breaking law.
- **Rigor.** High in the linear core. Theorems are stated with explicit assumptions (Assumptions 16–19, 25–28), and proofs are provided in appendices. The honest treatment of the nonlinear regime as conditional bounds with an error floor F and no claim below the floor (Proposition 29) is commendable.
- **Reproducibility.** Strong for the linear experiments: every subspace, projector, root, and tail coefficient is closed-form, so E1–E3 are deterministic identity checks rather than statistical claims (the author explicitly labels them as such). The synthetic hyperparameters (P=60, d=20, N=8, η=0.05, λ=2×10⁻³, β=0.95) and the modular-addition protocol (p=17, width 128, quadratic activation, 0.55 train fraction, heavy-ball β=0.9, η=0.2, λ=3×10⁻⁴) are fully specified. An "accompanying experiment package" is referenced for the larger protocol of Sec. 7.6.
- **Significance.** A real but bounded contribution. It clarifies *which* slow direction matters and gives computable signatures testable beyond the solvable regime, but it does not explain the abrupt test-accuracy jump (the paper explicitly leaves the loss-tail-to-margin conversion open), nor grokking without weight decay, nor stochastic/adaptive optimizers.
- **Red flags / weaknesses.** (a) The nonlinear benchmark is **single-seed and single-architecture**; the frozen-projector description of Section 6 is shown to degrade quantitatively on modular addition — the measured population-visible null component decays "several times more slowly" than the test-loss excess, which the author attributes to Jacobian rotation/feature learning but which also means the central nonlinear prediction (P1) is only approximately satisfied (25% rate discrepancy, not the 10⁻⁵-level identity of the linear checks). (b) Predictions P2–P4 are verified only in the linear model; their nonlinear validity is explicitly stated as **open**. (c) The grokking subspace S and Π_g are metric-dependent and not reparameterization-invariant — the author is honest about this but it limits the geometric claim. (d) No quantitative dataset-size N-law is given; N enters only through subspace dimensions and amplitudes. (e) G_pop is idealized; any practical estimator uses a finite held-out Jacobian with its own sampling error, and the modular-addition kernel-mismatch measurement depends on SVD cutoff (10⁻³–10⁻⁵) and holdout size.

## Strengths

- Mathematically exact core: full discrete mode spectrum, no continuous-time hand-waving in the headline iteration-scale law.
- Clean, falsifiable causal predictions (P1–P4) going beyond shared τ ∝ 1/λ scaling.
- Honest scope statement that explicitly lists what the mechanism does not explain.
- No-fitted-parameter verification in the solvable regime (identity-level agreement).
- Elegant unification of Noether symmetries, Hamiltonian dynamics, and NTK/Fisher observability Grams under one framework.

## Limitations

- Linear-in-parameter + squared loss + full-batch + heavy-ball only; nonlinear results are local, continuous-time, with explicit remainder and floor.
- Nonlinear benchmark is single-seed, single-architecture; the central P1 prediction holds only approximately (≈25% rate mismatch); P2–P4 are unverified for nonlinear networks.
- Does not address grokking without explicit weight decay, mini-batch noise effects, adaptive/preconditioned optimizers, or the loss-tail-to-accuracy-jump margin conversion.
- Grokking subspace S is metric-dependent and not reparameterization-invariant.
- No quantitative dataset-size N-law; G_pop requires a finite held-out estimate in practice.

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Can the soft Noether law be extended to mini-batch (stochastic) gradients, where noise projects onto N_N and competes with the λ-drift — does it merely renormalize the clock or change its functional form?
- Does the coupled-vs-decoupled (1−β) rate ratio (Proposition 6) survive for AdamW/Nesterov, and can it be measured empirically on realistic grokking tasks (e.g., transformer modular arithmetic)?
- How does the abrupt test-accuracy jump map onto the smooth ρ₀^{2k} loss tail — what is the missing margin argument, and can it predict the jump iteration?
- Under feature learning, the frozen-projector description degrades (modular addition shows several-fold slower null-component decay than the test-loss excess); can a time-varying S(θ_k) recover the agreement, and does the compatibility condition Π_g ∇L_pop(θ_eq) = 0 still select the doubled-rate regime?
- Is there a quantitative N (dataset-size) law for the delay, given that N currently enters only through subspace dimensions, amplitudes, and the compatibility condition but not through the rate ρ₀ itself?
