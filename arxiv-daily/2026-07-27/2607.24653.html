02

How it's built

three axes of information flow
Sequence

Hybrid attention

KDA : Gated MLA = 3 : 1

Kimi Delta Attention extends the delta-rule recurrence with a channel-wise forget gate; decay is reparameterized to a lower-bounded scaled sigmoid (gmin = −5) so every tile — including the diagonal — runs on dense Tensor Core matmuls.

  • Gated MLA layers use NoPE; KDA already provides position-sensitive mixing.
Depth

Block Attention Residuals

8 blocks × 12 layers

Each layer attends — via learned pseudo-queries and an RMSNorm-softmax kernel — to block-level summaries rather than every prior layer, cutting cross-layer memory from O(Ld) to O(Nd).

  • Enables selective inter-block retrieval.
Width

Stable LatentMoE

16 of 896 routed experts

Shared experts (Ns=2) plus compact-latent routed experts (latent dim = 0.5× hidden), RMSNorm before the up-projection, and the bounded SiTU-GLU activation suppress outliers.

  • Quantile Balancing sets each expert bias to the (1−k/n)-quantile of routing margins — target-perfect load balance with no auxiliary loss.
Pre-training

4-domain corpus + vision

Web / Code / Math / Knowledge + programmatic multimodal (SVG, 3D, Webpage, Game, CAD). Cosine decay beats WSD after independent searches. Context grown 8K → 1M across a four-stage cooldown; NoPE extrapolates without RoPE/YaRN.

Post-training · RL

9 experts → 1 model

SFT cold-start (XTML, MXFP4 QAT) → RL across general/agentic/coding × {low/high/max} effort, with partial rollouts, per-token off-policy regularization, and an Agentic GRM for non-verifiable tasks.

Distillation

Multi-Teacher On-Policy (MOPD)

Nine expert policies consolidated into one student via a clipped log-ratio dense reward. Deployment-aware: MXFP4 weights / MXFP8 activations, MTP layer trained into an EAGLE-3 draft model optimizing spec-decode acceptance.

04

The trade-offs

strengths vs. limitations

Strengths5

  1. Genuine open-weight frontier — 2.8T params, 1M context, native vision, permissive license, with MoonEP / FlashKDA / AgentENV / MiniTriton / nano-kpu also open-sourced.
  2. Deep systems rigor — provably-balanced MoonEP, KDA Context Parallelism with associative prefix scan, unified linear+softmax paged prefix cache, resumable Firecracker microVMs (133 ms ckpt / 49 ms resume).
  3. Beats Opus 4.8 / GPT-5.5 / GLM-5.2 nearly everywhere, and is competitive with Fable 5 / GPT-5.6 Sol on BrowseComp, ProgramBench, SWE-Marathon, MCPMark.
  4. Cost-efficiency frontier — near-top scores at a fraction of proprietary cost; first open model to top WebDev Arena.
  5. Candid about weaknesses — research-level reasoning, Elo-rated knowledge work, hardened-target cyber — supplemented by independent third-party evals (UK AISI / NIST CAISI).

Limitations6

  1. Still trails the strongest proprietary models overall (Index 57.1 vs 59.9 / 58.9), and specifically on research-level reasoning (HLE-Full, CritPt) and Elo-rated knowledge work.
  2. Post-training pipeline unreleased — SFT data, RL envs, GRM, mock apps, in-house benchmarks — so the recipe is not reproducible end-to-end.
  3. Conounded comparisons — Fable 5 "fallbacks," GPT-5.6 Sol "cyberguards," H20 vs H100, SWE-Marathon calibration.
  4. No final-scale ablations of KDA, AttnRes, or SiTU-GLU; the 2.5× scaling claim rests on fitted curves, not a same-FLOP bake-off.
  5. Cyber framing is one-sided — frontier proprietary models refuse the tasks; 0/41 on hardened-target ACE shows "frontier" is relative to the open-weight ecosystem only.
  6. MXFP4/MXFP8 quant throughout post-training with no clean FP-vs-quant quality comparison at release.

Open questions

for further exploration

How much of the 2.5× scaling gain comes from KDA vs. AttnRes vs. Stable LatentMoE vs. data/recipe? A controlled same-FLOP ablation would isolate the load-bearing pieces.

Does the lower-bounded decay (gmin=−5) and all-Tensor-Core KDA generalize to other gated-linear-attention families (RWKV-7, Mamba-2), and does gmin interact with context length?

Does Quantile Balancing's histogram estimator degrade past ~1000 experts, or under highly skewed long-tail routing?

How sensitive is MoonEP's E/R redundant-expert guarantee to non-uniform or heterogeneous expert capacities?

Do the in-house agentic benchmarks (Swarm Bench, Deep Research Bench, KAET) overfit to the team's harness conventions? How does K3 do under fully independent harnesses?

Given the 0/41 hardened-target ACE result, what is the marginal cyber-risk of open weights vs. the defensive upside from its Tier-1 vulnerability-discovery capability (16 new Linux-kernel bugs)?

Does MXFP4 QAT through post-training measurably hurt long-horizon agentic reliability (e.g., tool-call argument precision) vs. an FP8/BF16 control?

How quickly does the "first open 3T-class" claim erode — and does the KDA+AttnRes+LatentMoE recipe scale cleanly past 3T, or hit new instabilities?
