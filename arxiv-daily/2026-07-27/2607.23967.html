Strengths & Limitations

Strengths

  • Exact, not asymptotic. Full discrete mode spectrum and an exact slow root ρ₀ — no continuous-time hand-waving in the headline iteration-scale law.
  • Clean falsifiability. Four causal predictions (P1–P4) go beyond shared τ ∝ 1/λ scaling; each names a measurable signature.
  • Honest scope. Explicitly enumerates what the mechanism does not explain (grokking without weight decay; abrupt accuracy jump; mini-batch noise; adaptive optimizers).
  • Identity-level verification. Every subspace, projector, root, and tail coefficient is closed-form — E1–E3 are deterministic identity checks, not statistical claims.
  • Elegant unification. Noether symmetries, Hamiltonian dynamics, and NTK/Fisher observability Grams under one framework.

Limitations

  • Linear core only. Linear-in-parameter + squared loss + full-batch + heavy-ball; nonlinear results are local, continuous-time, with explicit remainder and error floor F.
  • Thin nonlinear evidence. Modular-addition benchmark is single-seed, single-architecture; central P1 prediction holds only approximately (≈25% rate mismatch); P2–P4 are unverified for nonlinear networks.
  • Scope gaps. No grokking-without-weight-decay, no mini-batch noise, no adaptive/preconditioned optimizers, no loss-tail → accuracy-jump margin argument.
  • Metric-dependent object. Grokking subspace S is not reparameterization-invariant — limits the geometric claim (author is candid).
  • No dataset-size law. N enters only through subspace dimensions and amplitudes, not through ρ₀; G_pop requires a finite held-out estimate in practice.

Questions for Further Exploration

  1. Does the soft Noether law extend to mini-batch (stochastic) gradients, where noise projects onto N_N and competes with the λ-drift — does it merely renormalize the clock or change its functional form?
  2. Does the coupled-vs-decoupled (1−β) rate ratio survive for AdamW/Nesterov, and can it be measured empirically on realistic grokking tasks (e.g., transformer modular arithmetic)?
  3. How does the abrupt test-accuracy jump map onto the smooth ρ₀2k loss tail — what is the missing margin argument, and can it predict the jump iteration?
  4. Under feature learning the frozen-projector description degrades (modular addition shows several-fold slower null-component decay than the test-loss excess); can a time-varying S(θ_k) recover the agreement, and does the compatibility condition still select the doubled-rate regime?
  5. Is there a quantitative N (dataset-size) law for the delay, given that N currently enters only through subspace dimensions, amplitudes, and the compatibility condition — not through ρ₀ itself?
