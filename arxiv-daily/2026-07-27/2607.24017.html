Methodology

SPAR · 2-stage · training-free

The attention manifold is modeled as additive noise: latent semantic attention plus a rigid, query-invariant structural bias plus stochastic error. As visual SNR→0, the posterior provably collapses to the linguistic prior — one mechanism explaining both spontaneous and induced hallucination.

A_obs(T,V) = A*sem(T,V)  +  λ · A_struct(V)  +  ζ     // structural bias marginalized over text dim (Eq. 2/11)

Stage 1

Saliency-Guided Bias Purification

High-variance visual tokens act as attention sinks. A per-token saliency score Sj is derived from visual hidden-state variance (sigmoid-squashed, Eqs. 9–10) and gates the subtraction of the bias template Āj.

Apuri,j = ReLU( Ai,j − λ·(1+β·Sj)·Āj )   // Eq. 12

Saliency gating prevents semantic erasure of legitimate background context and avoids amplifying low-frequency noise.

Stage 2

Adaptive Probability Redistribution

Purification drops total attention mass; naïve renormalization would amplify residual noise. SPAR computes a sparsity ratio ρi and confidence γi, restoring the removed budget only to confident signals.

γi = Sigmoid( k·(ρi − τsparsity) )   // Eq. 13
budget Δi restored ∝ γi   // Eq. 14

Weak-but-correct semantic alignments are strengthened while ambiguous noise stays suppressed.

Strengths & Limitations

side-by-side

[+] Strengths

  • Unified theory — SSD + posterior-collapse corollary explains both spontaneous and induced hallucination from one mechanism.
  • Strikingly cheap — 2-layer injection, ~0.42 s/it overhead, lower end-to-end latency than ONLY / AGLA / VAR.
  • Genuinely complementary — measurable gains when stacked on VAR/ONLY/AGLA, indicating a bias class prior methods miss.
  • Broad evaluation — 6 benchmarks across discriminative, generative, and adversarial gaslighting on two model families.
  • Honest efficiency story — Fig. 7 layer-sweep shows GasEraser degrades without ~16 layers; SPAR peaks at 2.

[!] Limitations

  • Scale unproven — only 7B-class open MLLMs; no proprietary or 13B/70B results.
  • Origin is a hypothesis — Pólya-urn / RoPE-asymmetry argued by analogy, not causal ablation.
  • Theory is motivational — A*sem and SNR defined formally but never measured or used predictively.
  • Headline gain conflated — shares a hardened-prompt baseline; SPAR-attributable portion ~7.85 pts.
  • Open gaps — CHAIR at max-len 128; code release not stated; no human caption eval; "first 2 layers" tuned for LLaVA.

Open Questions

for further exploration
Q1 · Generalization

Does first-2-layer injection hold on 13B/70B and proprietary frontier MLLMs, or is the bias locus specific to LLaVA's architecture?

Q2 · Operationalize SNR

Can the defined SNR be estimated from purification residuals and shown to correlate with per-sample hallucination probability?

Q3 · Causal origin

Would ablating RoPE (or replacing with ALiBi) actually reduce the structural bias, as the Pólya-urn hypothesis predicts?

Q4 · Stacking limit

What is the optimal combination/ordering of inference-time interventions — diminishing returns, or interference past two stacked methods?

Q5 · Token compression

How does SPAR interact with dynamic visual-token pruning (e.g. InternVL2-8B), where bias persists but mitigation was never measured?

Q6 · Adaptive adversary

Is the sparsity-confidence signal robust, or can an adversary defeat SPAR by deliberately diluting the attention sparsity it relies on?
