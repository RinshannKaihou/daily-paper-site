> **一句话总结：** 针对视频扩散模型中 MXFP4 注意力的两大数值失效模式（power-of-two 共享缩放导致的截断/下溢权衡、在线 softmax 中量化路径不一致导致的行归一化误差），本文提出无数据 PTQ 框架 MXAttention：通过闭式推导得到分布无关的最优缩放边界 Qmax=7.25（UOS）并对未归一化 softmax 指数做先于求和的量化（PNQ），在 Wan2.2/HunyuanVideo 上恢复 FP16 与 OCP MXFP4 之间至少 95% 的 VBench Imaging Quality 差距，部分指标甚至超过 FP16。

> **TL;DR:** MXAttention is a fully data-free post-training quantization (PTQ) framework for MXFP4 attention in video diffusion models. It attacks two failure modes of direct MXFP4 attention — the clipping/underflow trade-off of power-of-two block scaling, and the row-normalization breakage inside the FlashAttention online-softmax loop — via Universal Optimal Scaling (UOS), which derives the closed-form, distribution-independent boundary Qmax = 7.25 without calibration, and Pre-Normalization Quantization (PNQ), which quantizes the unnormalized softmax tile before both the row-sum and output-accumulator updates. On Wan2.2 it recovers 95.4% of the VBench Imaging Quality gap between vanilla OCP MXFP4 (0.6414) and FP16 (0.7085), reaching 0.7054 (only 0.0031 below FP16), and on HunyuanVideo it exceeds FP16 (0.6380 vs. 0.6185), staying within 0.01 of FP16 on all reported VBench metrics while matching strong NVFP4-based baselines such as NVFP4 + SageAttention at negligible kernel overhead.

## 核心贡献

- **诊断两类数值失效模式**：系统刻画了直接对注意力做 MXFP4 量化的两大问题——power-of-two 共享缩放在 32 元素块内造成"截断 vs. 下溢"的权衡（OCP 规则下约 19.27% 的块最大值落入 (7,8) 溢出舍入区被饱和为 6）；以及 FlashAttention 在线 softmax 循环中，行和更新用未量化指数、输出累加更新用量化指数，导致行归一化被破坏（Wan2.2 上行和均值 0.9336，低于 1）。
- **通用最优缩放（UOS）**：证明 power-of-two 微缩放引入的"对数周期密度"使最优缩放边界与块最大值分布无关，从而闭式求得全局最优边界 Qmax = 7.25（既不是 OCP 的 8，也不是 TFS 的 6），无需校准、QAT 或逐层搜索。
- **预归一化量化（PNQ）**：把未归一化的 softmax 指数 tile 在行和与输出累加两次更新之前统一量化、复用同一份量化结果，构造性地保证诱导出的注意力概率行和精确为 1，消除了行依赖的额外缩放误差。
- **强实证与低开销**：在 Wan2.2-14B 与 HunyuanVideo-13B 上分别恢复 95.4% 与 100% 的 Imaging Quality 差距，部分指标超过 FP16；UOS/PNQ/固定 Hadamard 旋转在完全 4-bit 配置下逐项消融；算法仅修改缩放选择与 tile 复用，几乎零额外开销，已集成进 MindIE-SD 主分支并开源。

## Key Contributions

- **Two failure modes of direct MXFP4 attention** are precisely characterized: (i) the clipping-underflow trade-off of power-of-two shared E8M0 scaling — under the OCP rule the normalized block maximum falls in [4,8), but since 7+ is not representable in E2M1, ~19.27% of blocks enter an overflow-rounding region and saturate to 6; (ii) normalization mismatch inside the online-softmax recurrence, where the row-sum update uses the unquantized exponential tile while the output-accumulator update uses its quantized counterpart, yielding induced attention weights that no longer sum to one (mean row sum 0.9336 on Wan2.2).
- **Universal Optimal Scaling (UOS)** proves that power-of-two microscaling induces an exact log-periodic density relation (g(q/2)=2g(q)), which removes any dependence on the block-maximum distribution from the derivative sign of a global quantization-error objective; the E2M1 grid then pins the global minimizer at the closed-form Qmax = 7.25 — strictly between OCP's 8 and TFS's 6 — with no calibration, QAT, or per-layer search.
- **Pre-Normalization Quantization (PNQ)** quantizes the unnormalized softmax exponential tile *before* both the row-wise sum ℓ and the output-accumulator Ǒ updates, reusing the same quantized tile in both; Proposition 1 shows the induced weights satisfy P̃·1 = 1 in exact arithmetic, eliminating the row-dependent scale error without any extra pass over the attention matrix.
- **Strong empirical validation with negligible overhead** on Wan2.2-14B and HunyuanVideo-13B: UOS, PNQ, and a fixed Hadamard rotation are shown to be complementary under fully 4-bit attention; the changes only modify the shared-scale selection rule and reuse the already-produced quantized tile, so they fuse cleanly into a tiled low-precision attention kernel. Code is integrated into the MindIE-SD main branch and open-sourced.

## Methodology

MXAttention is a data-free PTQ pipeline that inserts three components into the FlashAttention tiled online-softmax loop.

**1. Fixed Hadamard rotation.** An orthogonal Hadamard transform R_H is applied to Q and K (online only; the corresponding V-side transform is folded into the model weights) for outlier suppression, following QuaRot/SpinQuant. Rotation and block quantization operate locally on Q/K tiles and can run in parallel on orthogonal compute units, so they incur no resource contention.

**2. Universal Optimal Scaling (UOS).** MXFP4 quantizes B=32 E2M1 elements per block under a shared E8M0 power-of-two scale σ=2^e. Different scaling rules are unified through a "boundary" Qmax: OCP uses a floor rule with Qmax=8 (normalized max in [4,8)); TetraJet's Truncation-Free Scaling uses a ceiling rule with Qmax=6 (normalized max in (3,6]); UOS derives a third ceiling rule. UOS defines a *data-free, distribution-independent* objective J(q) = E[D(X_q)] where D(x) is the mean-squared E2M1 projection error over [0,x] normalized by x². The key insight is a **Boundary Density Ratio lemma**: because the periodized log-density H has period 1 and the endpoints q/2 and q are exactly one octave apart, g(q/2) = 2·g(q). This lets the entire block-maximum distribution factor out as a nonnegative multiplier g(q), leaving the sign of J'(q) determined purely by the grid-dependent difference D(q) − D(q/2). Exact integration over the E2M1 decision intervals gives Δ(q) = E(q) − 8E(q/2): constant −1/8 on [6,7] and (4q−29)(4q−27)/8 on [7,8], so the unique admissible zero crossing is q* = 29/4 = **7.25**. Theorem 1 proves this is the global minimizer for *any* absolutely continuous law of U=log₂ M (unique if H is positive a.e.). UOS therefore changes only the shared-exponent selection rule `e = ⌈log₂(M/7.25)⌉`, with normalized block maxima landing in (3.625, 7.25] and the overflow-rounding interval narrowed from OCP's (7,8) to (7,7.25].

**3. Pre-Normalization Quantization (PNQ).** The naive direct placement quantizes P̃_ij only for the output-accumulator update (δǑ = P̃_b V_j) while updating the row sum from the unquantized tile (δℓ = P̃ 1). PNQ instead quantizes the unnormalized exponential tile once, P̃_b = Q*(P̃_ij), and feeds *that same* P̃_b into both the ℓ update and the Ǒ update. After all key/value tiles are processed, both states can be written as ℓ_i = P̃_b^eff 1 and Ǒ_i = P̃_b^eff V, so O_i = Diag(ℓ_i)⁻¹ Ǒ_i yields induced weights P̃^PNQ = Diag(P̃_b^eff 1)⁻¹ P̃_b^eff with P̃^PNQ 1 = 1 by construction (Proposition 1). Importantly, PNQ does not change the element-wise quantizer and requires no extra traversal — it reuses the quantized tile already produced for the low-precision P V GEMM.

The overall forward pass (Algorithm 2) rotates Q/K, MXFP4-quantizes Q, K, V, and each P̃_ij with the UOS boundary, and reuses the quantized P̃_b in both softmax-path updates.

## Results & Highlights

- **Imaging Quality recovery on Wan2.2 (hybrid-precision main comparison):** FP16 = 0.7085, direct OCP MXFP4 = 0.6414, **MXAttention = 0.7054** — recovering **95.4%** of the FP16→MXFP4 gap and only 0.0031 below FP16, while NVFP4 = 0.6973 and NVFP4 + SageAttention = 0.6980.
- **Imaging Quality on HunyuanVideo (fully 4-bit):** FP16 = 0.6185, OCP MXFP4 = 0.4459, **MXAttention = 0.6380** — MXAttention *exceeds* FP16 (+0.0195) and beats NVFP4 (0.6221) and NVFP4 + SageAttention (0.6063).
- **Aesthetic Quality:** MXAttention is best among quantized methods on both models (Wan2.2 0.6229 vs FP16 0.6042; HunyuanVideo 0.6344 vs FP16 0.6242), exceeding FP16 on both.
- **Frame-level similarity to FP16 (Wan2.2):** MXAttention leads 5 of 6 metrics — Cosine **0.9536**, SSIM **0.6319**, PSNR **17.92 dB** — beating NVFP4+SageAttention (0.9510/0.6177/17.72) and OCP MXFP4 (0.9290/0.5076/15.58).
- **Validation that Qmax=7.25 matches the empirical optimum:** on Wan2.2, 7.25 is the per-block empirical MSE optimum for **95.0% of Q blocks, 90.0% of K blocks, and 90.0% of V blocks**; it is the mode or co-mode in 5 of 6 tensor–model combinations, despite the sweep minimizing finite-sample MSE while UOS minimizes the data-free analytical objective.
- **Quantified normalization pathology of the direct baseline:** across 80 Wan2.2 attention tensors the mean row sum of quantized P averages **0.9266** (all 80 below 1.0); error decomposes as zeroing −0.0541, saturation −0.0190, round-down −0.0486, round-up +0.0483 — not a single global scale factor, which is why pre-quant global scaling cannot restore row normalization.
- **Sequential ablation (fully 4-bit):** UOS lifts Wan2.2 cosine/SSIM/PSNR from 0.9143/0.5084/14.90 to 0.9329/0.5519/15.96; PNQ is the single largest Imaging Quality contributor (Wan2.2 0.6352→0.6822; HunyuanVideo 0.5321→0.6423); Hadamard alone raises Wan2.2 Imaging from 0.5452 (OCP) to 0.6352.

The work is tied to a production-grade inference stack: the implementation is integrated into the **main branch of MindIE-SD** (Huawei's Ascend inference engine for stable-diffusion/video) and is publicly released at `gitcode.com/Ascend/MindIE-SD`. The theoretical claims (Theorem 1, Lemma 1, Proposition 1) are stated with proofs and supported by appendices on wrapped-distribution analysis, exact E2M1 error integration, and an extended domain sign analysis. Baselines include the relevant strong alternatives (NVFP4, NVFP4 + SageAttention3, QuaRot/SpinQuant-style rotation, TetraJet TFS), and the comparison is paired (same fixed prompts and generation seeds). Hardware context is multi-vendor (NVIDIA Blackwell, AMD MI350, Ascend 950), consistent with the OCP MX standard motivation.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty (high):** The distribution-independence argument is the centerpiece and is genuinely non-obvious. The Lemma-1 density-ratio trick (g(q/2)=2g(q)) cleanly factoring the unknown block-maximum distribution out of the gradient sign, followed by *exact* E2M1 interval integration yielding the rational constant 29/4, is a real analytical result — not the usual "we tuned a hyperparameter and it worked." PNQ is more incremental conceptually (it is a placement/coupling fix rather than a new quantizer), but it is the right fix and is proven, not just argued.
- **Rigor (strong):** Proofs are provided; the closed-form 7.25 is cross-checked against an independent per-block empirical MSE sweep that agrees on 90–95% of Wan2.2 blocks. The row-sum pathology is quantified (mean 0.9266, full error-source decomposition) rather than asserted.
- **Reproducibility (mixed):** Code is public in MindIE-SD, prompts and seeds are fixed, and the algorithm is fully specified (Algorithms 1–2). However, the headline Wan2.2 comparison uses a *hybrid-precision policy* (Block 0 and denoising steps 38–39 kept in high precision) that is not used anywhere else in the paper — this is a non-trivial configuration choice disclosed only in the Table 2 footnote, and the gain attributed to MXAttention partly rides on this fallback. HunyuanVideo is fully 4-bit, which is the more honest headline.
- **Significance (targeted):** Scope is narrow — only attention quantization, only two video-diffusion models, only MXFP4. The "data-free" framing is fair but is a point in design-space (OAS/SearchBFP-style per-layer search is more flexible), not a universal win; the authors acknowledge this by comparing only against direct NVFP4 and not against the more recent adaptive-search MXFP4 methods head-to-head on the same models.
- **Red flags / overclaiming:** Some framing overstates generality. "All reported VBench metrics within 0.01 of FP16" is technically true but Subject Consistency actually *improves* over FP16 in several rows (0.9517 MXFP4 < 0.9562 FP16 on Wan2.2, but 0.9781 > 0.9665 on HunyuanVideo), which is suspicious and likely reflects VBench noise/seed effects rather than genuine 4-bit improvement — the paper does not discuss this. No latency/wall-clock end-to-end numbers are reported despite the "low-cost inference" motivation; only "negligible algorithmic overhead" is claimed. The NVFP4 + SageAttention baseline is reimplemented by the authors rather than taken from an official kernel, so that comparison is not on perfectly equal footing.

## Strengths

- A genuinely elegant, distribution-free derivation that turns an empirical knob (Qmax) into a provably optimal constant (7.25), with the proof machinery (wrapped-phase density, exact E2M1 interval integration) reusable for other microscaling formats.
- PNQ is a simple, kernel-friendly, provably normalization-preserving placement rule that costs nothing at runtime and is the dominant Imaging Quality contributor in ablation.
- Honest fully-4-bit ablations on both models, plus mechanism-level diagnostics (row-sum decomposition, entropy dependence, pre-quant-scaling counterexample) that explain *why* the fixes work.
- Public, production-integrated code in MindIE-SD lowers the barrier to reuse on Ascend hardware.

## Limitations

- Only attention is quantized; non-attention components stay at original precision, so end-to-end memory/latency gains are not quantified.
- Only Wan2.2-14B and HunyuanVideo-13B are evaluated — no image diffusion, no LLM, no smaller models to test format generality.
- The headline Wan2.2 result depends on a hybrid-precision policy (Block 0 + steps 38–39 in high precision) that is not ablated in isolation; readers cannot tell how much of the 95.4% gap-closure comes from the fallback vs. from UOS/PNQ.
- No comparison against the most directly competing adaptive MXFP4 methods (SearchBFP, Unveiling-MXFP4, M2XFP) on these exact video models — only NVFP4-family baselines are run.
- No wall-clock or memory measurements; "negligible overhead" is asserted algorithmically, not measured.

## Relevance

对关注视频扩散模型推理加速与低比特量化的研究者高度相关：本文给出了一套可立即落地的 MXFP4 注意力 PTQ 方案（已集成进 MindIE-SD），其"分布无关最优边界 + 在线 softmax 行归一化保持"的思路对任何使用 microscaling 格式做注意力量化的工作都有方法论参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How much of the Wan2.2 95.4% gap-closure survives if the hybrid-precision fallback (Block 0 + steps 38–39) is removed — i.e., what is the isolated contribution of UOS+PNQ under fully 4-bit attention on the *main* (not ablation) comparison?
- Does UOS's distribution-independence argument extend to other microscaling element formats (E3M0/E2M2/E4M3) and block sizes (16, 64), and what closed-form Qmax would each yield?
- Can PNQ be combined with NVFP4's finer 16-element blocks to beat both NVFP4+SageAttention on similarity *and* MXAttention on cost?
- Why does Subject Consistency *improve* over FP16 under 4-bit quantization on HunyuanVideo — is this a VBench evaluation artifact, a seed effect, or a real regularization phenomenon?
- What are the actual wall-clock latency and HBM-memory savings of MXAttention vs. FP16 and vs. NVFP4+SageAttention on Blackwell/MI350/Ascend 950, given the stated motivation is inference cost?
- How does MXAttention interact with caching/distillation samplers (e.g., TeaCache, pyramid attention broadcast), which change the distribution of attention inputs the quantizer sees?
