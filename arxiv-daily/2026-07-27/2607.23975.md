> **一句话总结：** Plato-Bio 是基于开源 Denario/Plato 的生物学路由扩展，通过显式工作流状态、来源记录、引用校验、声明-证据链接与发布门控构建"验证优先"的科学代理基础设施；在历史文献重发现任务中独立 A-B/B-C 证据桥将鱼油-雷诺现象关系排到第一（MRR 1.000），并在 15 个蛋白的 AlphaFold-实验结构对比中实现中位核心 RMSD 0.501 Å、输出 27 个可追溯差异候选，但作者明确仅作为可审计基线，不主张代理效能或生物学新发现。

> **TL;DR:** Plato-Bio extends the open Plato/Denario research-agent stack with biology routing, citation-validation, claim-to-evidence linking, and publication gates, and ships a frozen historical temporal-rediscovery benchmark plus a 15-target AlphaFold-vs-experiment structural screen. On the single curated fish-oil/Raynaud historical task, an evidence-aware A-B/B-C bridge ranker reached MRR 1.000 and Recall@1 1.000 versus 0.333 / 0.000 for corpus frequency and 0.500 / 0.000 for TF-IDF; across 15 proteins 11/15 had high-confidence-core Cα RMSD below 1 Å (median 0.501 Å) while 27 traceable discrepancy regions were emitted as unvalidated hypotheses. The author is explicit that the work is software-contract reproducibility plus one retrospective case, not a general efficacy or discovery claim.

## 核心贡献

- 提出生物学路由工作流：将文献源（PubMed、Europe PMC、OpenAlex 等）、MeSH 关键词提取、执行默认值与期刊选择统一为显式 domain profile，避免默认使用天文学源。
- 引入可检验的证据与安全控件：引用校验报告、claim/evidence 矩阵（支持/反驳/中立/不明）、科学一致性检查、运行清单（manifest），并区分"草拟声明"与"来源派生声明"。
- 修复并回归测试了 3 个可能扭曲评估的测量缺陷：默认工厂丢失 task domain（全部退化为天文学 profile）、golden 声明的 method signals 未被评分、evidence sidecar 缺少 Claim 分母（导致 unsupported-claim rate 假性归零）。
- 实现冻结的时间重发现基准：明确截止日期、anchor 概念、候选集、后期验证论文与 pre-cutoff 记录，对齐 ScienceAgentBench / BioDSA-1K / BixBench 的评估范式。
- 将 AlphaFold-vs-实验结构筛选从 3 个球蛋白扩展到 15 个蛋白（2688 个匹配残基），提供源哈希、置信度掩蔽与全链统计、残基级 CSV 与 27 个仅作为假设的差异区域。

## Key Contributions

- A biology-routed research-agent workflow built on the open Plato/Denario codebase, exposing explicit LangGraph state machines for idea → method → analysis → paper with domain-aware source selection (PubMed, Europe PMC, OpenAlex, Crossref, DOAJ, DataCite, OpenCitations, Semantic Scholar), MeSH keyword extraction, and scoped file writes.
- Inspectable evidence/provenance controls: citation-validation reports, claim/evidence matrices classifying source relations as supporting/refuting/neutral/unclear, heuristic scientific-consistency verifiers, run manifests recording hashes/models/seeds/token counts, and a publication gate that requires references and applies a configurable threshold (policy, not observed accuracy).
- Three source-audit repairs with failing-test-first regression coverage: (1) default evaluation factory dropped `task.domain` so all tasks used astronomy defaults; (2) declared `expected_method_signals` were never scored (added `method_signal_recall`); (3) evidence producer wrote only links so an absent Claim denominator made unsupported-claim rate spuriously report 0.0.
- A frozen temporal-rediscovery benchmark with hard cutoff dates, A-B/B-C bridge rules, abstention on unsupported candidates, hard failure for post-cutoff or missing dates, DOI/PMID deduplication, prompt-injection quarantine, and four declared ranking conditions (corpus frequency, TF-IDF, bridge-only, evidence-aware) with bootstrap intervals.
- An expanded deterministic AlphaFold-to-experiment structural screen over 15 human proteins with source SHA-256 hashes, Needleman-Wunsch sequence alignment, Kabsch superposition, both whole-chain and pLDDT≥70 high-confidence-core RMSD, residue-level CSVs, and 27 predeclared discrepancy regions all labeled `novelty_status=not_established`.

## Methodology

The paper is a software-engineering and reproducibility study rather than an efficacy trial. The author (i) conducted a source-level architecture audit of the Plato fork, (ii) repaired three measurement defects under failing-test-first regression discipline, (iii) ran five deterministic validation suites (biology domain, genomics adapters, evidence/citation, adversarial safety, full Python tree) via `preprint/experiments/run_software_validation.py`, and (iv) executed two evaluation lanes.

**Temporal rediscovery.** Each task freezes a cutoff date, an anchor concept A, candidate concepts C, a later validation publication, and pre-cutoff records with explicit PMIDs/URLs. A candidate is "temporal-novel" only if no direct pre-cutoff A-C record exists and at least two records form an A-B and B-C path; direct records are labeled "known pre-cutoff"; candidates lacking a bridge abstain. Ranking conditions: corpus frequency, TF-IDF, unweighted bridge support, and an evidence-aware score combining normalized bridge pairs (0.45) + TF-IDF (0.25) + source diversity (0.20) + provenance completeness (0.10), with a 1.0 penalty for direct prior art. Primary endpoints: MRR, Recall@1, Recall@10, false-novelty rate, with 10,000 task-level bootstrap resamples.

**Structural screen.** Experimental coordinates from the RCSB PDB and AlphaFold DB models were hash-verified; Cα atoms parsed, three-to-one-letter mapped, globally Needleman-Wunsch aligned (match 2, mismatch -1, gap -2), Kabsch superposed, then both whole-chain RMSD and pLDDT≥70 core RMSD were computed. Discrepancy regions required pLDDT≥90 AND core-aligned Cα error ≥2 Å, with adjacent qualifying residues grouped. Kabsch/core-transform code was regression-tested against synthetic rigid transforms. The diverse panel (15 proteins, 2,688 residues) was declared in `preprint/experiments/diverse_structure_panel.json` before the run.

## Results & Highlights

- **Software validation (full Python suite at commit d809f2b):** 937 tests, 931 passed, 6 skipped, **0 failures, 0 errors**. Targeted subsets: Biology domain 12/12, Genomics adapters 11/11, Evidence & citations 46/46, Adversarial safety 56 passed / 1 skipped / 0 failures.
- **Historical temporal rediscovery (single curated fish-oil/Raynaud task):** corpus frequency MRR 0.333 / Recall@1 0.000; TF-IDF MRR 0.500 / Recall@1 0.000; **A-B/B-C bridge MRR 1.000 / Recall@1 1.000; evidence-aware MRR 1.000 / Recall@1 1.000**. Held-out validation PMID 2536517 (1989); three Raynaud-nifedipine and one Raynaud-PGE1 records correctly labeled known pre-cutoff (false-novelty rate 0.000 under every condition).
- **Globin panel (3 targets):** Hemoglobin α Cα RMSD 0.270 Å (mean pLDDT 98.30), Hemoglobin β 0.520 Å (97.55), Myoglobin K45R 0.501 Å with 146/147 residues within 2 Å (97.71). All α/β hemoglobin residues within 2 Å.
- **15-target diverse screen (2,688 matched residues):** median whole-chain RMSD 0.520 Å (9/15 below 1 Å); after pLDDT≥70 masking, **median core RMSD 0.501 Å with 11/15 below 1 Å**. Compact proteins (transthyretin, Hbα, cytochrome c, SOD1, cyclophilin A, carbonic anhydrase 2, lysozyme) ranged 0.258–0.420 Å. CDK2 dropped 1.873 → 0.786 Å and MAPK1 1.705 → 0.740 Å after masking.
- **Four high-discrepancy targets (core RMSD > 2 Å):** KRAS 2.085 Å, SUMO1 2.576 Å, TP53 3.889 Å, estrogen receptor α 4.961 Å. SUMO1 (an NMR ensemble with a flexible N-terminus) was reduced from **16.610 Å whole-chain to 2.576 Å** when fitting only its 74 high-confidence residues; TP53 and ERα experimental chains cover only 49.4% and 40.0% of their AlphaFold sequences.
- The predeclared rule emitted **27 high-confidence discrepancy regions** (9 with ≥2 adjacent residues), all labeled `novelty_status=not_established` and retained as unvalidated hypotheses for review.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** Genuine engineering contributions to the open Plato/Denario fork (biology routing, the three repaired defects, the frozen temporal benchmark design), but the biology pieces are largely assembly and routing over existing tools (PubMed/Europe PMC/OpenAlex, AlphaFold DB, PDB) rather than new methods. The Kabsch/Needleman-Wunsch pipeline is textbook.
- **Rigor:** Strong internal rigor on the software side — failing-test-first repairs, deterministic suites, hash-pinned inputs, manifest-based provenance. But the headline biological benchmark rests on a **single manually curated temporal task** with a degenerate bootstrap interval, and the structural panel is only 15 targets dominated by well-behaved compact globins; four of the high-RMSD cases (SUMO1 NMR ensemble, TP53/ERα partial-domain constructs) are explained away as context artifacts rather than serving as a fair stress test of AlphaFold.
- **Reproducibility:** High. Code, manifest, hashes, residue CSVs, coordinate files, and scripts are shipped at a pinned commit (d809f2b), and the paper states that re-running reconstructs Tables 1-3 and Figures 3-6. The CompBioBench catalog is correctly stored separately as metadata-only to avoid being mistaken for executed performance.
- **Significance:** Modest as a scientific finding, higher as a reproducibility / evaluation-design template. The three repaired measurement defects (especially the spurious 0.0 unsupported-claim rate from a missing Claim denominator) are a useful cautionary tale for the LLM-agent evaluation literature.
- **Red flags:** The paper's own reviewer roles are same-model self-critique using the configured drafting model, and the default evaluation harness stops after idea/method generation rather than running end-to-end paper scoring — both honestly disclosed but limiting. No live LLM agent runs, paid retrieval, or autonomous research cycles were executed in the recorded environment. The structural correlations (pLDDT vs error, ρ≈0.24-0.35) are reported with nominal P values but explicitly not corrected for multiple comparisons and rest on a narrow pLDDT range (mean 97.5-98.3), so they are descriptive only.

## Strengths

- Honest, narrow claim boundaries: the author repeatedly distinguishes "software-contract reproducibility" from "agent efficacy" or "biological novelty," and labels all 27 structural candidates `not_established`.
- Transparent disclosure of the audit defects — documenting that an unsupported-claim metric could silently report 0.0 because the denominator was never persisted is a real methodological contribution.
- Reproducibility packaging is exemplary: source hashes, manifest, residue-level CSVs, frozen temporal fixtures, pinned commit.
- The evidence-aware ranking is mechanistically interpretable: every score traces to explicit PMIDs (58309, 4015748) and known pre-cutoff controls remain visible rather than being hidden.
- Benchmark design aligned with ScienceAgentBench / BioDSA-1K / BixBench (executability, abstention, provenance, task-level metrics) rather than generic text similarity.

## Limitations

- The temporal rediscovery benchmark contains only **one** manually curated historical task; its bootstrap interval is degenerate and it was constructed with knowledge of the later literature, so it cannot estimate general discovery performance.
- The 5 synthetic "engineering smoke" tasks are implementation fixtures, not biological evidence.
- The structural screen omits cryo-EM density fitting, lDDT, TM-score, side-chain accuracy, ligand geometry, oligomeric interfaces, domain segmentation, and conformational-ensemble comparison; the high-confidence mask relies on AlphaFold pLDDT rather than an independently defined domain.
- Several PDB entries are awkward (3RGK is a K45R myoglobin mutant; 1A5R is an NMR ensemble first model; several chains are partial constructs), which the paper flags but which constrain any RMSD interpretation.
- No LLM calls, paid retrieval, Modal, E2B, hosted PostgreSQL, or authenticated Hugging Face paths were available in the recorded environment; reviewer roles are same-model self-critique, not independent review.
- The CompBioBench v1 catalog is imported as metadata only — no claim of performance is made, which is honest but leaves the most directly comparable computational-biology agent benchmark unexecuted.

## Relevance

对于关注"科学代理可复现性与评估"方向的研究者，本文提供了显式工作流状态、来源哈希、声明-证据链接与三个测量缺陷修复的可借鉴工程模板；其"verification-first"理念（visible workflow stages ≠ output correctness）与自批判审稿机制的局限，对构建可信赖的生物医学 LLM agent 评估基准有直接参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How does the evidence-aware ranker scale beyond one task — e.g., on a preregistered set of dozens of historical rediscovery targets curated by multiple blinded experts?
- Would replacing same-model self-critique reviewer roles with a stronger independent model (or human reviewers) change the publication-gate rejection rate?
- Does the `unsupported-claim rate` defect pattern generalize to other LLM-agent evaluation harnesses (AI Scientist, Agent Laboratory, Denario), and can the failing-test-first repair be ported as a shared contract?
- On the structural side, do the 27 discrepancy regions survive once lDDT, TM-score, ligand/construct matching, and alternate-structure searches are added?
- Could the temporal-rediscovery A-B/B-C bridge design be coupled with a prospective wet-lab or curated-validation step to convert "retrospective ranking" into a real discovery loop?
