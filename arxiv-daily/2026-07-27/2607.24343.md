> **一句话总结：** 该论文提出一种按语义角色分层的逐字段共形风险控制（per-field CRC）方法，把工具调用的参数划分为 target / credential / command / content 等角色并分别设定阈值与风险预算，证明聚合（action-level）控制会让稀有高风险角色的违规率被 1/p_r 放大、有效预算被压缩到 α·p_r；在 AgentDojo 和 InjecAgent 上用六个模型做 trace-replay 评估，per-field CRC 在保持 0%–0.3% 违规、9.4%–14.9% 效用的同时，在 8 个迁移/漂移/自适应攻击条件下达到 100% 预算合规，远超 PACT-风格（61%）与 FIDES-风格（48%）代理。

> **TL;DR:** This paper introduces **role-stratified per-field conformal risk control**, a detector-agnostic calibration layer that assigns each LLM tool-call argument a semantic role (target, credential, command, content) and gives each role its own threshold and finite-sample risk budget. It proves that action-level "aggregate" control inflates a rare role's violation rate by 1/p_r and shrinks its effective budget to α·p_r (the "price of coarseness"), then shows on **AgentDojo** and **InjecAgent** across six models that per-field CRC holds target violation at 0.0%–0.3% (vs up to 21.2% for single-threshold baselines) and reaches **100%±0% budget compliance** on eight shifted conditions, versus **61%±33%** for a PACT-inspired per-argument threshold and **48%±48%** for FIDES-inspired labels — at a utility cost (9.9% abstain / 25.2% revert utility vs 27.2%/32.3% for the PACT proxy).

## 核心贡献

- **指明聚合控制的理论缺陷（"粒度代价"）**：形式化证明若只观察 action-level 平均损失，稀有角色 r 的违规率会被放大 1/p_r（Proposition 1），而要保证 V(r) ≤ α 必须把聚合有效预算压到 α·p_r（Proposition 2）；Theorem 1 用 Blackwell 实验比较给出聚合通道 Φ_agg、角色通道 Φ_role、字段通道 Φ_field 之间的可证预算前沿。
- **提出角色分层的逐字段共形风险控制方法**：把每个参数标定为 target/credential/command/selector/control/content 语义角色，对每个角色用 Mondrian/类条件共形风险控制（Angelopoulos et al. 2024）单独求阈值（Algorithm 1），稀有角色（如 credential）样本不足 1/(n_r+1) 下限时自动并入 pooled high-risk 分层。
- **给出有限样本并同时成立的证书**：Theorem 2 用 Clopper-Pearson 上界 + Bonferroni 分配 δ，使所有最终分层以 ≥ 1−δ 概率同时满足各自预算；零预算极限下方法退化为检测器相关的 noninterference（Proposition 3）。
- **标注无关的可部署打分**：默认部署分数 s_i = sim(x_i, untrusted context) − sim(x_i, trusted prompt)，不读取攻击者文本，ROC-AUC 0.71（vs label-coupled 上界 0.93），但安全效果相当。
- **在迁移、漂移、未见工具集、自适应攻击下系统验证鲁棒性**：per-field CRC 在 8 个 frozen-shift 条件下 100% 合规、0% 最差违规，2×2 消融显示分层是鲁棒性的主要来源（conformal recalibration 仅在漂移后补回有限样本有效性）。

## Key Contributions

- **A theory of the "price of coarseness."** Formal proofs that aggregate-measurable controllers inflate a rare role r's violation rate by 1/p_r (Proposition 1) and must shrink the effective budget to α·p_r to certify V(r) ≤ α (Proposition 2). Theorem 1 orders observation channels Φ_agg ⪯ Φ_role ⪯ Φ_field via a Blackwell-style comparison and gives the closed-form utility bound u*(α·p_r).
- **Role-stratified per-field CRC.** A calibration layer wrapping any per-field detector that assigns each argument a semantic role (target, credential, command, selector, control, content) and applies Mondrian / class-conditional conformal risk control per role with budget α(r) and floor 1/(n_r+1) (Algorithm 1). Rare roles (e.g., credential) fall back to a pooled high-risk stratum when individually uncertifiable.
- **Simultaneous finite-sample certificate.** Theorem 2 gives a Clopper-Pearson + Bonferroni high-probability certificate holding jointly over all final strata; Proposition 3 shows the α(r) → 0 limit recovers detector-relative noninterference, so the method is a tunable relaxation of classical IFC.
- **Annotation-free deployable score.** Default score s_i = sim(x_i, untrusted) − sim(x_i, trusted prompt); does not read attacker literals, ROC-AUC 0.71 vs the label-coupled upper bound of 0.93, yet with comparable safety.
- **Robustness study under shift, unseen suites, adaptive attacks.** 100%±0% budget compliance across 8 frozen-transfer conditions and 24/24 unseen-suite conditions, with a 2×2 ablation isolating stratification (not recalibration) as the main driver of frozen-shift robustness.

## Methodology

**Setup.** A tool call is a structured action A = (op, x_1, …, x_k); each argument x_i gets a semantic role r(i) ∈ {target, credential, command, selector, control, content}. A field is *violated* when untrusted input changes it against user intent and *allowed* when the controller emits it. Per-role violation rate:

V(r) = Pr[violated ∧ allowed | role = r]

The certified unit is the **field**, not the action; calibration strata are **semantic roles**. The deployable score is annotation-free:

s_i = sim(x_i, untrusted context) − sim(x_i, trusted user prompt)

shifted/rescaled to [0,1]; a field is allowed when s_i ≤ τ.

**Calibration (Algorithm 1).** For each role r with n_r calibration fields, choose the largest threshold whose inflated empirical risk stays within budget α(r):

τ̂(r) = sup { τ : (1/(n_r+1)) · (Σ_i L_r(x_i;τ) + 1) ≤ α(r) }

If 1/(n_r+1) > α(r) the role is pooled into a shared high-risk stratum g(r); otherwise g(r) = r. One threshold is fit per final stratum. Enforcement is per-field: if s_j ≤ τ̂(g(r_j)) allow, else revert to a trusted value or abstain/escalate.

**Theoretical apparatus.** Proposition 1 (concentration gap V(r) ≤ α_agg/p_r), Proposition 2 (aggregate control must use effective budget α·p_r), Theorem 1 (certification-granularity frontier with Blackwell ordering), Proposition 3 (conformal relaxation of noninterference), Theorem 2 (Clopper-Pearson + Bonferroni simultaneous certificate over final strata), Proposition 4 (per-role shift degradation bounded by TV(P_r, Q_r), removable by recalibration). Label-conditional calibration on attacked fields further yields a prevalence-invariant certificate on V_att(r).

**Evaluation.** Controlled **trace-replay** on real agent traces from six frontier models (GPT-4o, GPT-4o-mini, Gemini 2.5 Pro/Flash, Llama 3.3 70B, Qwen2.5-7B) under live prompt injection on **AgentDojo** (banking, workspace, Slack, travel) and **InjecAgent**. Scores replayed from cached trajectories; 20 random calibration/test splits; budgets α(r)=1% in-distribution and 2% under shift, α=0.10 aggregate for whole-action baselines. Baselines are controlled CaMeL-/FIDES-/PACT-inspired proxies under a common detector (explicitly *not* full reproductions), plus mean-/risk-/inverse-prevalence-/max-risk-weighted single-threshold losses and aggregate CRC.

## Results & Highlights

- **Table 1 (channel ablation, 1% target budget, label-coupled score):** Per-field CRC is the *only* method staying within budget, at **0.0%–0.3% target violation with 9.4%–14.9% utility**, whereas mean-aggregate CRC reaches 1.1%–5.0% violation, risk-weighted 0.6%–3.2%, inverse-prevalence-weighted 0.0%–3.2% (utility collapsed to 0.4%–32.6%), and max-risk up to **21.2% violation**.
- **Figure 1b / Table 2 — shift compliance (8 frozen-transfer conditions, 2% budget):** Per-field CRC reaches **100%±0% compliance with 0% worst-case target violation**, vs **61%±33%** for the PACT-inspired per-argument threshold and **48%±48%** for FIDES-inspired labels; paired Wilcoxon p = 7.8×10⁻³ vs PACT.
- **Table 2 (six-model safety-utility, deployable score):** Per-field CRC attains **0.0% ASR** with 9.9% abstain / 25.2% revert utility and 30.6% over-intervention, vs PACT-inspired 2.1% ASR (27.2/32.3 utility), FIDES-inspired 2.1% (48±48% shift), CaMeL-inspired 1.0% ASR (44.8% over-intervention), and Aggregate CRC **6.0% ASR** (no per-role certificate).
- **Table 3 (2×2 ablation under shift):** Frozen fixed per-role and CRC per-role both hit **100% compliance / 0% worst-case violation**; CRC aggregate single-threshold is **42.5 pp lower** in compliance (p = 8.2×10⁻⁵). After recalibration, conformal correction raises compliance by 8.1 pp (p = 2.8×10⁻²) and cuts worst-case violation 2.9% → 0.8%; even recalibrated, per-field CRC leads fixed per-role by **36.2 pp** (p = 1.2×10⁻⁴).
- **Price-of-coarseness numerics (AgentDojo, GPT-4o, 10% aggregate budget):** Aggregate CRC allows **10.5% target violations** while per-field CRC allows 0%; on **InjecAgent** (GPT-4o-mini, 10% aggregate) aggregate CRC hits only 2.9% overall violation but lets **100% of attacked target fields through**, vs 0% for per-field CRC.
- **Detector-quality robustness (Table C.2):** Despite ROC-AUC 0.71 (deployable) vs 0.93 (label-coupled), worst-case target violation stays ≤ 0.3% with ≥ 95% held-out compliance across all six models — "calibration determines validity, detector quality determines utility."
- **Adaptive & non-verbatim attacks:** Under adaptive least-detectable injection from unseen families, the PACT proxy reaches **3.6%–6.5% target violation (up to 3.25× the 2% limit)** while per-field CRC stays at **0.0%**; with attacker literal removed, the frozen PACT threshold hits 4.8% mean / up to 60% worst-case violation, while recalibrated per-field CRC holds ≤ 0.06%.
- **Unseen tool suites (24 model×suite conditions):** Frozen per-field CRC compliant in **24/24** with 1.5% worst-case violation, vs 14/24 (PACT single-threshold) and 12/24 (aggregate CRC).

The framing of three explicit claim levels (certified under exchangeability; empirically robust but not freshly certified under frozen shift; re-certified after recalibration) is more honest than typical conformal papers. Code/archive and reproduction scripts are referenced (Supplement F).

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty — solid but incremental.** The core move (use the semantic role as the Mondrian conformal stratum) is a natural specialization of class-conditional / Mondrian conformal prediction (Vovk et al. 2003; Ding et al. 2023) and of conformal risk control (Angelopoulos et al. 2024); the authors are explicit about this lineage. The genuine new contributions are the **closed-form price-of-coarseness characterization** (Propositions 1–2, Theorem 1) and the per-role Clopper-Pearson simultaneous certificate (Theorem 2), not the stratification idea itself.
- **Rigor — high.** Proofs are complete (Supplement D); statistics use paired Wilcoxon tests with reported p-values; the 2×2 ablation cleanly separates stratification from recalibration; an independent D_alloc/D_cert split avoids the confidence-allocation double-dip. Honest three-tier framing of guarantees (certified / empirical-but-uncertified / re-certified) is exemplary.
- **Comparability caveat — the biggest red flag.** The "PACT-, FIDES-, CaMeL-inspired" rows in Table 2 are explicitly **controlled proxies under a common detector, not full reproductions of the original systems**. CaMeL and FIDES provide deterministic noninterference/IFC guarantees by design; a stripped proxy that only mimics their granularity cannot represent their true safety-utility frontier. Headline numbers like "0.0% vs 1.0% ASR for CaMeL-inspired" must not be read as "per-field CRC beats CaMeL."
- **Detector-quality dependence understated for utility.** The deployable annotation-free score has ROC-AUC only **0.71**, driving 30.6% (and up to 84% under non-verbatim obfuscation) over-intervention; the paper frames this as "detector quality determines utility," which is correct, but it means real-world utility hinges on a detector the paper does not improve.
- **Assumption fragility in the simultaneous certificate.** Theorem 2's Clopper-Pearson exactness requires **field-level independence within each role stratum**; when episodes emit multiple same-role fields the all-field n_r can overstate effective sample size and the interval becomes anti-conservative. The authors disclose this (Supplement A) and offer a one-per-episode fallback, but main results use the all-field version and are described as "field-independence-based rather than episode-level exact."
- **Prevalence coupling.** The headline certified quantity V(r) is mixture-dependent (V(r) = π(r)·V_att(r)); benchmarks are attack-heavy (π ≈ 85%–94%), so V ≈ V_att there, but in a low-attack-prevalence deployment the same certificate permits a larger fraction of attacked fields to leak. The prevalence-invariant V_att certificate requires label-conditional calibration on attacked fields, which is a stronger data requirement.
- **Scope of the guarantee.** The certificate covers field-value integrity only — not tool selection, omitted actions, call ordering, or leakage through low-risk fields; utility is measured on frozen traces with no live replanning after a block/revert. These are honestly listed in Limitations but materially limit deployment claims.

## Strengths

- Clean, correct formalization of *why* aggregate action-level control fails for rare high-risk roles, with a tight closed-form price (1/p_r inflation, α·p_r effective budget).
- Detector-agnostic: the calibration layer wraps any per-field detector and improves monotonically with detector quality — genuinely composable with StruQ/SecAlign/Spotlighting etc.
- Disciplined, reproducible protocol (trace-replay, 20 splits, paired Wilcoxon, independent confidence-allocation set) and refreshingly honest three-tier guarantee framing.
- Strong robustness story across transfer, unseen suites, detector noise, gradual drift, and adaptive attacks — exactly the failure modes that matter in practice.
- Graceful handling of rare roles via automatic pooling rather than silent failure.

## Limitations

- Utility cost is real: 25.2% revert utility vs 32.3% for the PACT proxy, and over-intervention up to 84% under obfuscated attacks with the weak deployable detector.
- Comparisons to CaMeL/FIDES/PACT are proxies, not full system reproductions — strong claims against those systems are not warranted by the data.
- Credential role cannot be individually certified at 1% (only 0–22 calibration fields; per-model mean floors 6.7%–63.6%); the pooled certificate covers the high-risk stratum average, not credential alone.
- Theorem 2's exactness relies on field-level independence that can be violated by multi-same-role-field episodes.
- No interactive deployment evaluation; no treatment of tool selection, omitted actions, or call ordering.
- V(r) is mixture-dependent, so the guarantee's tightness changes with attack prevalence in deployment.

## Relevance

对关注 LLM agent 安全与可信推理的研究者，这篇论文直接命中 prompt-injection 防御与工具调用风险控制这一核心议题，把共形预测的"分层校准"思想用到 tool-call 参数的语义角色上，既有理论（price of coarseness）又有可复现实验，是构建可信 agent 的实用校准层，可作为引用与基线。对做 conformal prediction / uncertainty quantification 的人也提供了一个面向真实安全场景的应用案例。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How does role-stratified per-field CRC behave under **interactive deployment with live replanning** after a block/revert, where a blocked field changes downstream tool selection and call ordering?
- Can the certificate be extended to **tool selection and omitted actions** (currently explicitly out of scope), perhaps via a role-like stratification over the operator `op` itself?
- What is the right way to handle **within-episode same-role field dependence** without resorting to the conservative one-per-episode construction — e.g., cluster-robust conformal bounds?
- How sensitive is the prevalence-coupled V(r) guarantee in **low-attack-prevalence deployment** (π ≪ 85%), and can the prevalence-invariant V_att certificate be made practical with cheaper attacked-field labels?
- Since the PACT/FIDES/CaMeL comparisons are proxies, how does per-field CRC stack up against **full reproductions** of CaMeL's dual-LLM IFC or FIDES's value-tagged labels under identical detectors?
- Can stronger deployable detectors (e.g., learned provenance models beyond similarity differences) reduce the 30.6%–84% over-intervention while preserving the 0% violation floor?
