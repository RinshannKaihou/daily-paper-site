> **一句话总结：** 本文证明大语言模型使用的概念关系几何（如环/树拓扑）并非固定的"世界模型"，而是由上下文中的声明式规则即时设定并因果性使用的——当规则与预训练先验冲突时，能力足够强的模型（Gemma-31B、Qwen-27B）会以上下文设定的几何压倒先验（RSA 0.6–0.9 对 ~0），激活修补实验证实该映射是因果操作的而非探针相关物。

> **TL;DR:** This paper shows that the relational geometry an LLM uses over a set of concepts (cycle vs. branching tree) is set on-demand by an in-context declarative rule, not fixed in the weights. On the days/weeks benchmark, when a conflicting "redefined order" is declared, Gemma-31B's imposed-order representational similarity (RSA) reaches +0.87 versus the pretrained ring's −0.03, a +0.90 dominance. The same tokens flip from a cycle (RSA +0.72) to a depth-stratified tree (depth-RSA +0.67) purely by changing the stated relations. Activation patching demonstrates this map is causally used: an entity-substitution patch yields a clean crossover (imposed-context → imposed successor 1.00; natural context → pretrained successor 1.00; the other order at chance 1/7), but only in the largest models — clean, faithful use is scale-gated, so a small-model finding must be re-verified before being trusted to transfer up.

## 核心贡献

- **上下文设定拓扑类型，而非仅平移固定形状。** 声明式规则不仅能重新排序，还能决定几何的拓扑类型——同一组 token 在指令下可形成环或分支树，这无法用"重标记存储形状"解释（在任意无意义 token 上同样成立，排除了任何先验）。
- **冲突时上下文几何占优，且被因果使用。** 当声明规则与强预训练先验（如星期、月份）冲突时，能力足够的模型使用的几何遵循上下文而非先验（RSA 0.6–0.9 对接近 0），并通过实体替换激活修补证明该映射是因果操作的，而非探针相关物。
- **形成廉价，忠实使用受能力门控——这是迁移警示。** 小模型和基座模型能形成粗略的施加几何（甚至 2B 基座模型在 12 点概念上能通过零假设），但干净可遍历的映射和完整因果双分离仅在更大模型（Gemma-12B/31B、Qwen-27B）中出现，因此不能将小模型的机制发现直接外推到大模型。

## Key Contributions

- **Specification sets the topology type, not just a shift within a fixed shape.** A declarative rule fixes whether a concept's geometry is a cycle or a branching tree, and this is the geometry the model uses — even appearing under prompts that ask nothing structural. The same seven weekdays form a ring (effective dim ≈4) under one stated relation and a depth-stratified tree (effective dim ≈2) under another, with the tokens never changing.
- **On conflict, the context-set geometry dominates and is causally used.** The used geometry follows the context, not the pretrained prior (demonstrated via anisotropy and crossover controls), and an entity-substitution activation patch shows this map is causally operative rather than a probe correlate — answering the "is a concept's structure stored or assembled?" question in favor of assembly under conflict.
- **Forming is cheap; faithful use is capability-gated (a transfer caution).** A rough imposed geometry forms even in small and base models (incl. a non-instruction-tuned Gemma-31B base), but the clean, traversable map and the full causal double dissociation emerge only with scale, so a mechanistic finding in a small model must be re-verified at scale before assuming it transfers up.
- **Methodological contribution:** First (to the authors' knowledge) use of representational similarity analysis (RSA) with a permutation null and an imposed-vs-natural dominance test as the primary readout for concept geometry, plus the first use of persistent homology (H1 bar detection) for concept-level topology in language models, with convergent validity against PCA planarity (Spearman ρ=0.93) and behavior (ρ=0.83).

## Methodology

The paper frames one question — is a concept's relational geometry fixed in the weights, or set by the context's specification? — and tests it through a controlled taxonomy of imposed structures.

**Imposed specifications.** A declarative rule (no exemplars) asserts the relations among a set of entities: either an order ("the only valid order is: Wednesday, Monday, …") or a hierarchy ("P is the parent of A and B"). Specifications vary along three axes: structure type (open line / closed cycle / tree), relation to the prior (congruent vs. conflicting scramble), and entity type (strong cyclic priors like weekdays/months/clock hours, weak prior like zodiac, non-cyclic like musical notes, and arbitrary structure-free English nouns).

**Entity-layout probe (RSA).** Borrowed from computational neuroscience (Kriegeskorte et al., 2008) but uncommon in mech-interp: per-entity centroids are formed from the residual stream at the last prompt token, immediately before generation (the "integrated state" the next-token computation consumes), averaged over neutral query templates. The representational dissimilarity matrix (RDM) uses mean-centered cosine distance `D_ij = 1 − cos(c̃_i, c̃_j)`, and RSA is the Spearman correlation of its off-diagonal entries against a theory template (e.g., `T_ij = min(|i−j|, N−|i−j|)` for a cycle). The probe layer is fixed at `L = round(0.75·n_layers)` from a full-layer sweep showing a broad late-network plateau, chosen conservatively below peak. Mean-centering is essential to remove the anisotropic residual-stream cone.

**Activation patching (causal test).** Entity-substitution: at one layer, overwrite the orig entity's residual with a donor entity's activation `h_E_orig ← h_E_patch`, then generate and score whether the answer equals the patch's successor under the imposed vs. pretrained order (chance 1/N). A wrong-slot control (injecting the operand one token off the entity) and a random-vector/norm control (Gaussian rescaled to the real patch's norm) rule out trivial vector-injection and norm confounds. Swept across depth fractions {0.1…0.95}×n_layers on a fixed a priori grid.

**Models & sampling.** Eight instruction-tuned models across three families and ~1 order of magnitude: Gemma-4 (E2B, E4B, 12B, 31B), Qwen-3.5 (4B, 9B, 27B), and Llama-3.1-8B; plus Gemma-31B base for the instruction-tuning control. Geometry RSA averaged over 10 scrambles per concept; patching uses 2 scrambles × 42 operand pairs per model in both direct and chain-of-thought regimes.

**Convergent validity.** Four independent measures (fitted circular probe, PCA ring planarity, one-shot behavior accuracy, persistent-homology H1 bar) corroborate the RSA readout.

## Results & Highlights

- **Context dominates the pretrained prior on conflict (Gemma-31B, Figure 1/Table 7).** On the seven weekdays under a scrambled "redefined order," imposed-order RSA = +0.87 while residual natural-order RSA = −0.03 (a +0.90 dominance gap under direct prompting; +0.58 even free-form). For months: +0.81 vs +0.05; for clock hours: +0.82 vs −0.05.
- **Topology type changes on command (Figure 6, Table 10).** Holding the seven weekdays fixed, an imposed cycle yields ring RSA +0.72 (Gemma)/+0.71 (Qwen), effective dim ≈4, whereas an imposed tree on the same days yields depth-RSA +0.67/+0.68 with cyclic structure gone, effective dim ≈2 — a distinction also confirmed by persistent homology (dominant H1 loop for the cycle, none for the tree).
- **Trees build on arbitrary, meaning-free tokens (Figure 5).** A declarative binary tree on structure-free nouns gives depth-RSA +0.92/+0.89 (Gemma/Qwen) at depth-2, holding to +0.73/+0.63 at depth-4; same-depth nodes band together while parent/child are the farthest pairs.
- **The map is causally used — a clean crossover (Figure 7, Gemma-31B days).** Imposed context → patched answer follows the donor's imposed-order successor at rate 1.00; natural context with the same patch → pretrained-order successor at 1.00; each other order only at chance (1/7 ≈ 0.14). A Gaussian-vector control at matched norm flips at only 0.14, ruling out a norm confound.
- **Clean use is scale-gated (Table 4).** The double dissociation (both arms at ceiling at a deep, clean locus passing the wrong-slot control <0.2) emerges only at Gemma-12B/31B and Qwen-27B (clean locus ≈0.45 depth). Below that, small Qwen (4B/9B) carries both arms only at early leaky layers, and small Gemma never carries the natural arm. Behavioral one-shot accuracy (k=1) reaches 1.00 for Gemma-12B/31B and Qwen-27B but the smallest models revert (Llama-8B never builds a usable map; Qwen-9B days RSA −0.33, ring retained).
- **Not an instruction-tuning artifact.** Gemma-31B base (non-RLHF) still reorganizes on conflict: imposed-order RSA +0.62 on months, +0.67 on zodiac.
- **Convergent validity across the eight-model ladder.** Imposed RSA rank-tracks PCA ring cleanliness at Spearman ρ=0.93 and one-shot adjacency accuracy at ρ=0.83; persistent-homology H1 persistence on arbitrary-token cycles is 8–11× the random baseline (Gemma-31B 11.5×), absent for trees (2.0×, at floor).
- **Extends to 2-D grids (Appendix J).** On a 3×3 QWERTY block, an imposed shuffled layout overrides the native keyboard geometry: imposed-layout RSA +0.78 vs native +0.09 at Qwen-27B (dominance +0.69).

## Authors & Credibility

Elad David and Max Fomin are affiliated with Zenity (an AI security/governance firm), writing in the mechanistic-interpretability tradition. The work is positioned carefully against the strongest realist evidence — Engels et al. (2024), Gurnee & Tegmark (2023), Park et al. (2024) on stored concept geometry; Li et al. (2022) / Nanda et al. (2023) on Othello-GPT causal probes; Park et al. (2025) which found pretrained structure resists in-context exemplars — and explicitly distinguishes its setup (declarative rule with no exemplars, integrated pre-generation readout) from Park et al. (2025), plausibly explaining the shift from "resistance" to "dominance." All code, cached data, and interactive 3D explorers are released at `github.com/eladd-ai/context-is-king`. The paper engages honestly with two concurrent works (Hosseini et al. 2026; Xiong et al. 2026) that corroborate context-induced reshaping without forcing conflict. The claims are appropriately scoped to two model families over an eight-model ladder rather than asserted as a universal law.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty: Genuine and well-argued.** The topology-type result (same tokens → cycle vs. tree purely by changing the stated relation, Figure 6) is a clean refutation of the "relabelled stored shape" null that prior probing-only work could not rule out, and the use of RSA with a permutation null plus persistent homology as primary readouts is a methodological step beyond PCA inspection. The honest distinction from Park et al. (2025) — declarative rule + integrated readout vs. random-walk exemplars + top-component readout — is the most plausible explanation for why context "dominates" here but only "resists" there, and it is testable.
- **Rigor: Strong on confound controls, weaker on universality.** The relabeling crossover (geometry fits the imposed order best among all 5040 orderings of seven days), the anisotropy-matched label-shuffle null (10/10 scrambles clear on every concept; 12-point concepts at p<0.001), and the wrong-slot / norm controls on patching are exactly the controls this kind of claim needs. However, the natural-context arm of the patch is one-sided or absent in small Gemma (Table 4: Gemma-E2B/E4B/E12B Nat on days ≤0.48), and the "clean locus" onset sits near the 0.9/0.2 thresholds the authors themselves flag as an interval rather than a point — so the scale threshold is real but soft.
- **Reproducibility: High.** Released code/data/interactive explorers, fixed a priori patching grid (not post-hoc selected), full per-model layer indices (Table 3), verbatim prompt templates (Appendix A), and per-scramble 95% CIs. The probe layer choice is shown to be conservative (0.86 at 0.75 vs. a 0.88 peak for Gemma-31B). The main reproducibility risk is the narrow object: at most 12 centroids, one late layer, pre-generation token — reproducing at a different layer or token could change the picture.
- **Significance: Conceptually important, operationally bounded.** It reframes a "recovered world-model geometry" as context-dependent rather than intrinsic, which is a substantive correction to how the linear-representation-hypothesis literature is often read. But the authors are explicit that they do not resolve whether, when a strong prior exists, the model builds the geometry anew or reconfigures the stored one — on arbitrary tokens it must be built, but where a prior exists the two are indistinguishable in their readout. The intervention localizes the operator and its depth, not the manifold axis (a linear displacement cannot traverse a cycle), leaving the question of *which geometric direction* carries the map open.
- **Red flags / caveats:**
  - The headline dominance numbers come from the two largest models that "reliably build" the map (Gemma-31B, Qwen-27B); the paper's own framing is that dominance is a "capable-model claim, not a universal one," and Qwen-9B on days actually *reverses* (RSA −0.33). The abstract's "0.6–0.9 vs near-zero" is a best-case summary.
  - Small-N topology: at N=7 the anisotropy null has a structural floor from cycle symmetry (14 of 5040 orderings are equivalent), so days scrambles clear at p≤0.008 rather than p<0.001; the authors disclose this honestly but it means the strongest p-values come only from the 12-point concepts.
  - The tree result is "only metric" among same-depth leaves (Figure 5C) — depth structure is robust, fine branch structure is partly surface-driven (co-occurrence control removes part of sibling tightness), so "faithful tree embedding" is explicitly *not* claimed.
  - The paper leaves attention-vs-MLP attribution and richer relations (partial orders, DAGs) to future work, and the causal signature is characterized only on the Gemma/Qwen ladders.

## Strengths

- The double-dissociation crossover (Figure 7b) is the decisive evidence: the *same* patch yields different successors depending only on the context's stated order, which a fixed lookup table cannot do. This is the strongest possible form of evidence for "computed, not stored."
- Topology-type control (cycle ↔ tree on identical tokens, Figure 6) closes the "relabelled stored ring" loophole that pure reordering experiments cannot.
- Exceptionally thorough confound battery: relabeling against all 5040 orderings, anisotropy-matched null, wrong-slot patch control, norm-matched Gaussian control, four-measure convergent validity (probe + PCA planarity + behavior + persistent homology).
- Honest scoping: explicitly an "existence proof over these models rather than a universal law," with the build-vs-reconfigure question left open rather than overclaimed.
- Open artifact release (code + cached data + interactive 3D explorers) and a fixed a priori patching grid that is not post-hoc selected.

## Limitations

- **Small representational object:** at most twelve centroids, a single late layer, the pre-generation token; RSA captures rank-agreement rather than metric shape, so metric-shape claims rest on corroborating measures.
- **Causal localization, not axis identification:** entity-substitution patching pins the operator and its depth but not which manifold direction carries it; nonlinear steering and attention-vs-MLP attribution are out of scope.
- **Scale-dependent and family-specific:** the clean crossover appears only at Gemma-12B/31B and Qwen-27B; the effect reverses (Qwen-9B) or fails (Llama-8B) below, and the native 2-D grids (keyboard, chess) are weaker and more model-dependent than the cyclic rings.
- **Open mechanistic question:** whether the model builds the imposed geometry anew or reconfigures a stored one is unresolved wherever a prior exists (distinguishable only on arbitrary tokens, where it must be built).
- **Behavioral traversal gap:** one-shot direct reading degrades with hop count k even for capable models (k≥2 accuracy falls while step-by-step reasoning recovers ceiling), and Qwen-27B's free-form reasoning over-runs the 2048-token budget on 81% of generations — the map is traversable but free-form termination is brittle.

## Relevance

This paper is a direct hit on the user's "LLM Hidden States & Interpretability — mechanistic interpretability, representation geometry, information encoding in hidden states, probing, representational similarity" interest: it uses RSA (representational similarity analysis), activation patching, residual-stream probing, and persistent homology to show concept geometry is context-set and causally used. It also bears on the "self-evolving agents / automated research" and "LLM Inference Evaluation" interests by establishing that a recovered "world-model" geometry is not an intrinsic, transferable model property but a context-dependent computation — a caution for anyone treating small-model interpretability findings as facts about larger systems.

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Which residual-stream direction/manifold axis actually carries the imposed ordering? The patch localizes the operator and depth but not the carrier; would a nonlinear (manifold) steer along that axis reproduce the traversal the linear displacement cannot?
- Does the build-vs-reconfigure distinction become resolvable with weight-level interventions (e.g., ablating the pretrained ring direction) rather than activation patching?
- How does the context-set geometry behave during *native* chain-of-thought reasoning read along the trace, rather than only at the pre-thinking token?
- Do richer relations (partial orders, DAGs, weighted graphs) form faithful geometries, or does fidelity collapse beyond trees and cycles?
- Is the scale threshold for clean use a smooth function of capability, or a phase transition — and can it be shifted by instruction-tuning or by targeted fine-tuning on relational reasoning?
- Could the declarative-rule dominance be exploited as a controllable interpretability lever — e.g., forcing a target topology to make a concept's structure cleanly readable in a small model that otherwise fails to build it?
