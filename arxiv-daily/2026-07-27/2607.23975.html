Methodology

software-engineering + reproducibility study · not an efficacy trial

A source-level architecture audit of the Plato fork, three defect repairs under failing-test-first discipline, five deterministic validation suites, and two evaluation lanes.

Temporal rediscovery

Freeze a cutoff date, anchor concept A, candidates C, a later validation paper, and pre-cutoff records with explicit PMIDs. A candidate is "temporal-novel" only with no direct A–C record and an A–B + B–C bridge; else abstain.

score = 0.45·bridge + 0.25·tfidf
      + 0.20·diversity + 0.10·provenance
      − 1.0·(direct prior art)

Structural screen

RCSB PDB + AlphaFold DB models hash-verified; Cα parsed, 3-to-1 mapped, Needleman–Wunsch aligned, Kabsch superposed. Whole-chain and pLDDT≥70 core RMSD reported.

discrepancy ⟺ pLDDT ≥ 90
      AND core-aligned Cα err ≥ 2 Å

Provenance controls

Citation-validation reports, claim/evidence matrices (support / refute / neutral / unclear), run manifests (hashes, models, seeds, tokens), and a publication gate requiring references.

  • DOI/PMID dedup
  • prompt-injection quarantine
  • hard-fail on post-cutoff dates

Strengths · Limitations

read side-by-side

Strengths

what holds up

  • Honest, narrow claim boundaries — repeatedly separates "software-contract reproducibility" from "agent efficacy" / "biological novelty."
  • Transparent defect disclosure — the silent 0.0 unsupported-claim rate from a missing denominator is a real methodological contribution.
  • Exemplary reproducibility packaging — source hashes, manifest, residue-level CSVs, frozen fixtures, pinned commit.
  • Mechanistically interpretable ranking — every score traces to explicit PMIDs; pre-cutoff controls stay visible.
  • Benchmark design aligned with ScienceAgentBench / BioDSA-1K / BixBench (executability, abstention, provenance).

Limitations

where it over-reaches / under-delivers

  • One manually curated task with a degenerate bootstrap — built knowing the answer, so no general-discovery estimate.
  • Structural screen is only 15 targets, dominated by well-behaved compact globins; high-RMSD cases are explained away as artifacts.
  • Omits cryo-EM fitting, lDDT, TM-score, side chains, ligands, oligomeric interfaces, domain segmentation, ensembles.
  • Same-model self-critique reviewers, not independent review; no live LLM runs, paid retrieval, or autonomous cycles recorded.
  • CompBioBench v1 imported metadata-only — the most directly comparable agent benchmark is left unexecuted.

Questions for further exploration

open fronts

Does the evidence-aware ranker scale beyond one task — e.g. on a preregistered set of dozens of targets curated by multiple blinded experts?

Would swapping same-model self-critique for a stronger independent model or human reviewers change the publication-gate rejection rate?

Does the unsupported-claim rate defect generalize to AI Scientist / Agent Laboratory / Denario — and can the failing-test-first repair port as a shared contract?

Do the 27 discrepancy regions survive once lDDT, TM-score, ligand/construct matching, and alternate-structure searches are added?

Could the A–B/B–C bridge design couple with a prospective wet-lab step to turn retrospective ranking into a real discovery loop?

Are the pLDDT-vs-error correlations (ρ ≈ 0.24–0.35) meaningful at all, given the narrow pLDDT range (mean 97.5–98.3) and no multiple-comparison correction?
