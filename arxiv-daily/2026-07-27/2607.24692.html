Names a new attack class: network-layer contention converted into silent AI accuracy loss — invisible to availability monitoring, and directly safety-critical for autonomous driving and routed LLMs.

3Critical edge

§ 01Methodology

A conceptual abstraction + a two-stage simulation (latency, then accuracy replay).

Threat model

A workload-shaping adversary interacting only through the normal request interface, submitting valid inputs (complex scenes likely to trigger slow-path assistance) as an ordinary user.

No infrastructure control, no access to model weights, routing thresholds, or merger rules. Leverage is asymmetric: modest average rates → disproportionate delay (Yo-Yo-style bursts, not volumetric DDoS).

Testbeds & models

Latency sim (§4.1): two-tier model of the NSF COSMOS urban testbed — finite edge station + FCFS cloud with warmup/cooldown autoscaler; benign Poisson at λ_b = N·f·p; 250 ms SLO.

Accuracy replay (§4.2): edge YOLOX-Nano, cloud YOLOX-X on Argoverse-HD; Kalman + camera-motion-compensation + SORT; metric = HOTA.

Attack mechanism → 4 steps

01
Attacker sends bursty valid requests likely to invoke slow-path assistance.
02
Router forwards them to the shared slow path (cloud queue).
03
Queueing + delayed scale-out pushes results past the freshness deadline → merger discards as stale.
04
Bursts spaced so workers reclaim after cooldown → each burst re-hits warmup lag.

Sharpest insight

Attack severity depends on which predictions are late, not just how many — the same latency trace yields −2.0 vs −18.7 HOTA depending on the video interval it lands on.

§ 03Strengths & Limitations

What lands hard · what to read with caution.

Strengths

  • Cleanly names a new failure mode ("accuracy collapse") invisible to availability-only monitoring — the fast path keeps the service "up."
  • General router/merger abstraction backed by a cross-domain survey spanning vehicle perception, robot planning, mobile video analytics, and LLM routing.
  • Strong headline latency result: 92 ms → >2 s p99 amplification on a real testbed's parameters.
  • The temporal-variation finding (2.0 vs 18.7 HOTA from identical traces) is non-obvious and reframes how distributed-attack potency should be measured.
  • Honest about limits (replay-based accuracy, weak-adversary assumption, no demonstrated defense) — no overclaim of model compromise.

Limitations

  • Accuracy impact is offline replay of simulated latencies, not a live two-tier system running YOLOX-X under real contention; autoscaler/queue dynamics are modeled, not measured.
  • Per-class claims rest on very low-frequency classes (stop signs, motorcycles ~0.6% of detections) — "nearly half" losses carry wide uncertainty.
  • Key simulator params (N, f, p, warmup/cooldown, attacker distribution) under-specified; no code/config released.
  • No defense evaluated — admission control, priority scheduling, isolation, overprovisioning are discussed qualitatively only.
  • The threat model's claim that an attacker can infer routing via probing is asserted, not demonstrated empirically.
  • Evaluated on one domain (autonomous-driving perception); predictions for routed LLMs and robot VLA pipelines remain untested.

§ 04Open questions

Where this line of work goes next.

How does accuracy collapse manifest in routed/cascaded LLM pipelines (RouteLLM, FrugalGPT, Hybrid-LLM), where the merger picks one of two text answers — does latency-driven fallback to the SLM measurably degrade answer quality on hard queries?

Which concrete defense (admission control, per-tenant isolation, slow-path priority scheduling, predictive autoscaling, deadline-aware merge buffers) most cheaply bounds worst-case HOTA loss, and by how much in simulation?

Can the attack be detected online without ground-truth labels, using only observable signals (slow-path discard rate, p99 latency, autoscaler churn) — and at what detection latency vs. burst duration?

Does the temporal-variation effect (2.0 vs 18.7 HOTA from identical traces) generalize beyond Argoverse-HD, and can it be predicted from input-difficulty features available at the router?
