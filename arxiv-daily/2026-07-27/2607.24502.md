> **一句话总结：** 本文严格分析了将 RoPE（旋转查询/键、值不变）注入归一化球形自注意力后的连续时间动力学，证明自然 RoPE 能量不存在统一的 Lyapunov 符号律，但在共振环上给出了精确的 Bessel-混叠谱与共识速率、在若干几何区域给出了显式收缩率，并刻画了 RoPE 选出的扭转平衡分支与多频共识速率的非单调依赖。

> **TL;DR:** This single-author theory paper studies the continuous-time limit of normalized self-attention on the sphere when RoPE rotates queries/keys but leaves values unrotated. Relative to the closest baseline (vanilla spherical attention without positional encoding, e.g. Karagodin et al. and Kuehn-Yoon), it proves that the natural RoPE interaction energy admits no uniform Lyapunov sign, derives an exact Bessel-aliasing consensus spectrum on a resonant ring giving rates such as γ(β) ~ 2Δ_L e^{-βΔ_L} for L≥3 (versus vanilla γ=1 at β=0), and shows the local consensus gap γ(a) is non-monotone in plane-energy allocation (analytic 3-token example with γ(2/3)=3/4 strictly above both endpoints). The model is an idealized identity-QKV limit rather than a trained Transformer; there is no benchmark dataset, and the quantitative content consists of closed-form constants and a table of deterministic numerical cross-checks at near machine precision (e.g. full-spectrum error 1.554×10⁻¹⁵).

## 核心贡献

- **建立标准 RoPE 球面流模型**：在 (S^{d-1})^n 上写出"旋转 Q/K、不旋转 V、归一化残差一阶极限"的连续动力学，证明全局适定性、可逆（细致平衡）与最 sharp 的注意力下界 a_{β,n}=1/(1+(n-1)e^{2β})，并给出同一系统内 E_+ 导数同时取正负的显式反例，否定统一的能量符号律。
- **共振环上的精确 Bessel-混叠谱**：在 ω=2πm/n、g=gcd(m,n) 的精确共振下，给出循环共识核的全部 Fourier 特征值公式，正确处理非互素情形（g∤q 的模式严格为零），并区分"固定环大 β 渐近"（已证）与"密集相位联合极限"（未证，仅作猜想）。
- **显式几何区域收缩定理**：固定闭半球前向不变；成对非钝角区域与严格开半圆区域均以显式半角界与单点尾界收缩，量化了"局部精确速率 vs 全局一致界"的差距 γ ≥ n·a_{β,n} ≥ 2a_{β,n}。
- **RoPE 选出的扭转分支与稳定性**：给出扭转平衡 θ_j=c-ωj 的充要条件（ω∈πZ 或 nω∈2πZ）；通用共振族为非双曲、线性不稳定（谱含 1/2 重数 2 与 0）；奇反极族在商掉全局旋转后成为双曲鞍。
- **多频共识速率的非单调性**：证明共识间隙 γ(a) 对平面能量分配 a 一般非单调（解析 3-token 例 γ(2/3)=3/4 严格超过两端点），且不存在按频率幅值的普适排序（两 token 解析反例与有限上下文数值观察）。

## Key Contributions

- **Formulates and structurally analyzes the standard query/key-only RoPE spherical flow**: proves global well-posedness, detailed-balance reversibility, and the sharp state-uniform softmax floor a_{β,n}=1/(1+(n-1)e^{2β}); gives same-parameter witnesses (n=3, p=(0,1,2), β=1, ω=π) showing the natural interaction energy E_+ has derivatives of both signs, so neither E_+ nor -E_+ is a Lyapunov function.
- **Exact Bessel-aliasing consensus spectrum on a resonant single-frequency ring**: closed-form Fourier eigenvalues via congruence-filtered modified Bessel functions I_k(β), correctly handling gcd(m,n)>1 (unreachable modes vanish exactly); separates the proved fixed-L large-β asymptotics from an explicitly unproved dense-phase joint limit.
- **Regional global convergence with explicit constants**: fixed closed hemispheres are forward invariant; pairwise non-obtuse configurations and strict open semicircles contract with half-angle/tail bounds; the relation γ ≥ n·a_{β,n} ≥ 2a_{β,n} quantifies the gap between the exact local rate and the uniform global guarantee.
- **RoPE-selected twisted branch and its linear stability**: an if-and-only-if resonance condition for the score-flattening twisted equilibrium; the generic resonant family is non-hyperbolic and linearly unstable (spectrum {1/2 (mult. 2), 0 (mult. n-2)}), while the odd antipodal family becomes a hyperbolic saddle after quotienting global rotation.
- **Multi-frequency non-monotonicity**: proves γ(a) is generically non-monotone in plane-energy allocation (analytic 3-token example γ(2/3)=3/4 > max{γ(0), γ(1)}), and that no position-independent frequency ordering exists (two-token analytic counterexample plus finite-context observations).

## Methodology

The author derives the model as the first-order depth-time limit of a normalized residual attention layer with Q=K=V=I except for prescribed block rotations R_p on queries and keys (equation 5): ẋ_i = Σ_j A_ij(x) P_{x_i^⊥} x_j, with A the row-normalized softmax of rotated inner products. Orthogonality of block rotations makes W symmetric and yields detailed balance π_i A_ij = π_j A_ji, while scores lying in [-1,1] give the sharp floor a_{β,n}=1/(1+(n-1)e^{2β}) = e^{-2β}/(n-1+e^{-2β}).

The technical machinery proceeds in three layers. **(1) Local analysis:** the consensus manifold C={(x⋆,...,x⋆)} is shown to consist of equilibria; its transverse tangent linearization is J_C = (A⋆ - I_n) ⊗ I_{d-1}, where A⋆ is a row-normalized reversible Markov operator whose kernel depends on x⋆ only through the plane-energy vector a (Prop. 4.1, Thm. 4.3). On a resonant ring the kernel is circulant, so Fourier diagonalization yields the Bessel-aliasing spectrum (Thm. 5.1); positive-semidefiniteness follows from the Schur product theorem after writing the consensus kernel as a Gram matrix (Thm. 5.2). **(2) Global analysis:** a kernel-generic positivity argument using only row stochasticity and the floor a_{β,n} gives forward invariance of hemispheres (Prop. 6.1) and explicit contraction in pairwise non-obtuse / strict-semicircle regions via nonsmooth minima handled with finite active-set Dini calculus (Thms. 6.2, 6.3). **(3) Equilibria beyond consensus:** the score-flattening twisted configuration θ_j = c - ωj is characterized by an if-and-only-if resonance condition (Prop. 4.2), its Jacobian spectrum computed (Props. 7.1, 7.2), and the multi-frequency consensus kernel shown positive semidefinite with a plane-energy rate γ(a) that is provably non-monotone (Thm. 7.3, Prop. 7.4) and admits no frequency-magnitude ordering (Prop. 7.5).

All theorems are deterministic and proved in the appendix; numerical computations (matrix spectrum, congruence-filtered Bessel spectrum, FFT, finite-difference Jacobian, nonlinear ODE integration) are reported as cross-checks of convention-sensitive constants, never as proof steps.

## Results & Highlights

- **Sharp softmax floor** (Thm. 3.1): For all states, A_ij(x) ≥ a_{β,n} = 1/(1+(n-1)e^{2β}); the constant is sharp over the model class and strictly stronger than the coarser e^{-2β}/n bound for β>0.
- **Energy-sign no-go with exact witnesses** (Prop. 3.2): In the single fixed system n=3, p=(0,1,2), β=1, ω=π, the natural energy derivative takes Ṗ_+(0,π/6,π/3) ≈ +1.666364304459315 > 0 and Ṗ_+(0,π/6,0) ≈ -0.1296131359239394 < 0, so no uniform Lyapunov sign law exists.
- **Exact Bessel-aliasing consensus spectrum** (Thm. 5.1, Fig. 1a): For n=16, m=4, β=2, the congruence-filtered Bessel formula matches the direct FFT circulant spectrum to machine precision while 12 modes are excluded by the exact gcd(m,n)=4 reachability mask; the structural-zero count is exact rather than counted from floating-point zeros.
- **Fixed-ring large-β consensus slowdown** (Thm. 5.3, Fig. 1b-c): With effective period L=2 the local gap is γ(β) = 1 - tanh β = 2e^{-2β}/(1+e^{-2β}) ~ 2e^{-2β}; for L≥3 with Δ_L=1-cos(2π/L), γ(β) ~ 2Δ_L e^{-βΔ_L}, giving exponentially slow consensus distinct from vanilla γ=1 at β=0.
- **Local-vs-uniform rate separation** (eq. 38-39): On a fixed resonant ring γ/(2a_{β,n}) ~ n-1 for L=2 and ~ (n-1)Δ_L e^{β(2-Δ_L)} for L≥3, so the exact local rate and the state-uniform global guarantee should not be numerically identified.
- **Analytic non-monotonicity of multi-plane rate** (Prop. 7.4): For n=3, β=log(4/3), (ω_1,ω_2)=(π/2,π), the interior value γ(2/3)=3/4 strictly exceeds both endpoints γ(0)≈0.602 and γ(1)≈0.597 — so a→γ(a) is not generally monotone.
- **Analytic frequency-order reversal** (Prop. 7.5): With two tokens at separation Δ, taking ω_low=π/2 and ω_high=π, the high frequency is slower at Δ=1 yet completely aliased (γ_high=1, strictly faster) at Δ=2.
- **Twisted-state linear spectra** (Props. 7.1-7.2): The generic resonant twisted equilibrium has spectrum {1/2 (mult. 2), 0 (mult. n-2)} — non-hyperbolic and linearly unstable; the odd antipodal family (n=2r+1, ω∈(2Z+1)π) becomes a hyperbolic saddle with r stable and r unstable dimensions after quotienting global rotation.
- **Independent numerical cross-checks** (Table 2): 144 resonant spectra with full-spectrum error 1.554×10⁻¹⁵ and Bessel-Fourier gap error 6.051×10⁻¹⁵; geometric contraction over 7057 evaluations with minimum Dini residual -3.886×10⁻¹⁶ and maximum trajectory excess 2.961×10⁻¹⁰; twisted-state drift-formula error 3.764×10⁻¹⁶ and Jacobian error 3.070×10⁻⁹; multi-frequency three-way gap disagreement 1.527×10⁻¹⁵ and relative nonlinear-rate error 2.805×10⁻⁶.
- **Frequency reversal in finite contexts** (eq. 48): For standard two-plane frequencies (1, 0.01), β=4, the high-ω endpoint is slower at n=256 (γ_low=0.240298 vs γ_high=0.134730) but faster at n=384 (γ_low=0.112756 vs γ_high=0.135707), empirically confirming no universal frequency ordering.

The work is positioned in the active mathematical-transformers line (Geshkovski-Polyanskiy-Rigollet, Karagodin et al., Kuehn-Yoon) and includes a careful contribution-boundary table (Table 1) distinguishing it from the closest prior work. ## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: Genuinely incremental rather than paradigm-shifting. The honest Table 1 concedes that polygonal equilibria, spherical spectral analysis, Toeplitz/RoPE phase structure, and the oscillator dictionary are all prior art; the genuinely new pieces are (i) the exact non-coprime Bessel-aliasing spectrum for the *normalized* query/key-only flow, (ii) the explicit same-system energy-sign no-go, and (iii) the analytic non-monotonicity of the multi-plane consensus gap. This is a defensible but narrow novelty claim, and the author is commendably explicit that it is "deliberately narrower than claiming the first RoPE dynamics."
- **Rigor**: High. Theorems are precisely stated with sharp constants (e.g. the softmax floor is proved optimal over the model class, and the bipodal counterexample in Prop. 6.4 shows the strict-semicircle condition cannot be weakened). However, two asymptotic claims are *not* theorems: the dense-phase limit 1-I_1(β)/I_0(β) ~ 1/(2β) is explicitly flagged as requiring an unproved uniform aliasing error bound, and the quantitative nonresonant finite-chain limit is admitted to be a conjecture. The author does not silently promote these to results, which is good practice.
- **Reproducibility**: Deterministic closed-form results plus a clearly described numerical appendix (parameter grids, error definitions, integration tolerances) make the claims reproducible in principle; reported errors at the 10⁻¹⁵ level are consistent with double precision. No code release is mentioned, which is a gap for a paper that leans on numerical cross-checks as validation.
- **Significance**: The model is an extreme idealization — identity Q/K/V, no FFN, no multi-head, no causal masking, no finite-step/finite-depth effects beyond the first-order residual limit. The author states this limitation directly (Section 9), but it sharply limits what the theorems say about a *trained* Transformer using RoPE. The "consensus slowdown" and "twisted instability" are properties of a controlled dynamical limit, not empirical predictions about real models.
- **Red flags**: (i) Heavy reliance on deterministic numerical cross-checks framed as "validation" — these verify internal consistency, not external predictive power, since there is no comparison against an actual trained network. (ii) The multi-frequency non-monotonicity (Prop. 7.4) is established only on a hand-picked 3-token, β=log(4/3) configuration; whether the phenomenon is typical or a measure-zero curiosity is unaddressed. (iii) The generic resonant twisted family has a large center subspace; linear analysis proves instability but the author explicitly disclaims any center-manifold result, so the dynamical role of these equilibria is genuinely open.

## Strengths

- Mathematical honesty: sharp constants, explicit counterexamples, and a contribution-boundary table that resists overclaiming.
- Clean separation of three regimes (local spectrum, regional global contraction, equilibrium branches) with quantitative bridges between them (the γ vs 2a_{β,n} comparison).
- The non-coprime Bessel-aliasing formula with the exact gcd(m,n) reachability mask is a non-trivial technical refinement that prior spectral analyses of normalized attention lack.
- Numerical cross-checks use independent methods (matrix spectrum, congruence-filtered Bessel, FFT, finite-difference Jacobian, nonlinear flow) and report worst-case residuals rather than cherry-picked examples.

## Limitations

- Idealized model: identity Q/K/V, single head, no FFN, no masking, first-order residual limit — far from a trained Transformer. Architectures that rotate values (RoVE) or use learned harmonic couplings are explicitly out of scope.
- All convergence theorems are *regional* (pairwise non-obtuse or strict open semicircle); bipodal stationary states show the boundary is real and cannot be removed by continuity. The hemisphere-invariance result alone does not imply consensus.
- Two asymptotic questions are left open: the dense-phase joint limit (β,L→∞ with L/√β→∞) and the quantitative nonresonant finite-chain limit; both are explicitly partial claims or conjectures.
- The generic resonant twisted branch has a large center subspace; only linear instability is proved, not the nonlinear organizing role of these equilibria.
- No code release and no empirical comparison against any trained Transformer, so the practical implications for RoPE-equipped LLMs remain conjectural.

## Relevance

对研究 Transformer 内部动力学、位置编码（尤其 RoPE）与归一化机制的理论建模高度相关：它把 RoPE 纳入连续时间球形注意力框架，给出可证明的共识速率与扭转稳定性结论，为理解真实模型中 RoPE 对 token 聚合/塌缩行为的影响提供了严格的数学基线，对关注 LLM 长上下文与位置编码理论的读者有参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Can the dense-phase joint limit (γ ~ 1-I_1(β)/I_0(β) ~ 1/(2β)) be promoted from conjecture to theorem via a uniform aliasing error bound, and what does it imply for very-long-context RoPE models?
- Do the twisted and odd-antipodal equilibria persist — and play an organizing role in nonlinear transients — when learned Q/K/V maps, multiple heads, and causal masking are added back?
- How robust is the multi-plane non-monotonicity (Prop. 7.4) beyond the specific n=3, β=log(4/3) example: is it a typical phenomenon or a measure-zero curiosity, and can a center-manifold analysis characterize the generic resonant twisted family?
- Can the deterministic numerical cross-checks be replaced by or paired with empirical measurements on a trained RoPE Transformer to test whether the predicted exponential consensus slowdown and frequency-reversal effects actually appear in practice?
- Does rotating values (as in RoVE) restore a uniform Lyapunov sign law, and how does its consensus spectrum differ from the query/key-only case analyzed here?
