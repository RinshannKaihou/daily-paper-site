Key Finding · Mechanistic Interpretability

A concept's relational geometry in an LLM is set on demand by the in-context rule — not fixed in the weights — and is causally used.

Methodology

One question: is a concept's geometry stored, or assembled? A controlled taxonomy of imposed structures.

Imposed Specifications

A declarative rule (no exemplars) asserts relations among entities — an order ("the only valid order is…") or a hierarchy ("P is parent of A and B"). Three axes: structure type (line / cycle / tree), prior relation (congruent vs. conflicting scramble), and entity type (strong cyclic priors like weekdays/months/hours, weak like zodiac, non-cyclic like musical notes, and arbitrary structure-free nouns).

Entity-Layout Probe (RSA)

Per-entity centroids from the residual stream at the last prompt token, pre-generation, averaged over neutral templates. RDM uses mean-centered cosine distance D_ij = 1 − cos(c̃_i, c̃_j); RSA is Spearman correlation against a theory template (e.g. T_ij = min(|i−j|, N−|i−j|) for a cycle). Probe layer fixed at L = round(0.75·n_layers).

Activation Patching (causal test)

Entity-substitution at one layer: overwrite the orig entity's residual with a donor's activation, then score whether the answer equals the patch's successor under imposed vs. pretrained order (chance 1/N). Wrong-slot control and Gaussian/norm control rule out vector-injection and norm confounds. Swept on a fixed a priori depth grid {0.1…0.95}×n_layers.

Models & Convergent Validity

Eight instruction-tuned models across three families over ~1 order of magnitude: Gemma-4 (E2B–31B), Qwen-3.5 (4B–27B), Llama-3.1-8B; plus Gemma-31B base. Four independent measures corroborate the RSA readout: fitted circular probe, PCA ring planarity, one-shot behavior, and persistent-homology H1 bar.

Strengths & Limitations

Decisive causal evidence, honest scoping — against a small object and an unresolved build-vs-reconfigure question.

Strengths

What the evidence buys
  • Decisive double-dissociation. The same patch yields different successors depending only on the context's stated order (Fig. 7b) — the strongest possible form of evidence for "computed, not stored."
  • Topology-type control. Cycle ↔ tree on identical tokens (Fig. 6) closes the "relabelled stored ring" loophole that pure reordering cannot.
  • Exceptional confound battery. Relabeling against all 5040 orderings, anisotropy-matched null, wrong-slot patch control, norm-matched Gaussian control, four-measure convergent validity.
  • Honest scoping. Explicitly an "existence proof over these models rather than a universal law"; the build-vs-reconfigure question is left open rather than overclaimed.
  • Open release. Code + cached data + interactive 3D explorers; fixed a priori patching grid, not post-hoc selected.

Limitations

Where the claim is bounded
  • Small representational object. At most twelve centroids, a single late layer, the pre-generation token; RSA captures rank-agreement, not metric shape.
  • Causal localization, not axis ID. Patching pins the operator and its depth but not which manifold direction carries it; nonlinear steering & attention-vs-MLP attribution are out of scope.
  • Scale- & family-dependent. Clean crossover only at Gemma-12B/31B and Qwen-27B; reverses (Qwen-9B) or fails (Llama-8B) below; native 2-D grids weaker than cyclic rings.
  • Open mechanistic question. Build-anew vs. reconfigure-a-stored-one is unresolved wherever a prior exists; distinguishable only on arbitrary tokens.
  • Behavioral traversal gap. One-shot accuracy degrades with hop count k≥2; Qwen-27B's free-form reasoning over-runs the 2048-token budget on 81% of generations.
Authors & credibility. Elad David & Max Fomin (Zenity, AI security/governance), writing in the mechanistic-interpretability tradition. The work positions itself carefully against the strongest realist evidence (Engels et al. 2024; Gurnee & Tegmark 2023; Park et al. 2024/2025) and distinguishes its declarative-rule + integrated-readout setup from Park et al. (2025)'s random-walk-exemplar + top-component readout — the most plausible explanation for why context "dominates" here but only "resists" there. Code, cached data, and 3D explorers at github.com/eladd-ai/context-is-king.

Questions for Further Exploration

Open directions the paper explicitly leaves to future work.
Which manifold direction carries the imposed ordering? The patch localizes the operator and depth but not the carrier — would a nonlinear steer along that axis reproduce the traversal a linear displacement cannot?
Does build-vs-reconfigure become resolvable with weight-level interventions (e.g., ablating the pretrained ring direction) rather than activation patching?
How does the context-set geometry behave during native chain-of-thought read along the trace, rather than only at the pre-thinking token?
Do richer relations — partial orders, DAGs, weighted graphs — form faithful geometries, or does fidelity collapse beyond trees and cycles?
Is the scale threshold for clean use a smooth function of capability or a phase transition — and can it be shifted by instruction-tuning or targeted fine-tuning on relational reasoning?
Could the declarative-rule dominance be exploited as a controllable interpretability lever — forcing a target topology to make a concept cleanly readable in a small model that otherwise fails?
