Key Finding

In LLMs, the ability to tell self-generated from externally given information is not a fixed model property — it bends with the shape of conversation memory.

Reality Monitoring in Large Language Models: Self-Knowledge That Transforms with Conversation Memory

Saurabh Ranjan · Konstantina Sokratous · Brian Odegaard 2026-07-27 reality-monitoring metacognition hallucination conversation-memory
Key Metric
2.28
d′ rose ↑  ·  Type 2 AUC 0.34 ↓ below chance
Under corrective feedback, Llama3.3:70b improved its source discrimination while its metacognitive sensitivity collapsed — an accuracy / confidence split no calibration benchmark detects.
Why This Matters

Single-turn accuracy overstates multi-turn safety. Feedback can invert source mappings and decouple confidence from correctness — a concrete risk for agentic deployment that standard benchmarks miss by design.

Critical Edge
Under-powered claim

The headline scaling conclusion — that active expert-network size, not aggregate parameters, drives source attribution — rests on a single MoE model (Llama4:16x17b) compared against a handful of dense models. Inferring a scaling law from n=1 is a stretch the authors acknowledge but still headline.

01

Methodology

Paradigm

Johnson–Raye reality monitoring: a word-pair task where the second word is either externally provided (perceived) or model-generated (imagined). The model later attributes the source. Stimuli: 36 Deese–Roediger–McDermott lists — same materials as a validated human RM study, enabling direct human–LLM comparison.

Models

Six instruction-tuned models via Ollama, temperature = 0 (greedy):

  • Gemma3 12B & 12B-QAT
  • Gemma3 27B & 27B-QAT
  • Llama3.3 70B
  • Llama4 16×17B (sparse MoE)

Memory architectures

  • Single-Turn (Exp.1): 4 subtasks in one interaction, fresh context per trial.
  • Trial-Chain (Exp.1): 4 subtasks across 4 sequential turns, history retained.
  • Episodic-Chain (Exp.2): encoding block, then a delayed source-test phase; 2×2 design over set size (20 / 40) and feedback (absent / present).

Statistics

GLMMs (binomial logit) with random intercept for trace, LMMs for γ, Cumulative Link Models for confidence; Type-II Wald χ², emmeans with Bonferroni; signal-detection measures (d′, criterion c, Type 2 AUC) per trace. Sensitivity analyses re-fit with model as a crossed random intercept — all primary inferences replicated.

Sample sizes

Exp.1: N = 3,456 trials (1,728 perceived).  ·  Exp.2: N = 72,000 test-phase observations across 4,800 traces (200 per cell). Tooling: open-source PsychScanner v0.1, JSON-serialized trials, counterbalanced response-option order.

03

Strengths · Limitations

+ Strengths

  • Clean experimental design — isolates conversational memory as a manipulated variable, rare in LLM evaluation.
  • Direct human comparability via shared DRDM stimuli and the Johnson–Raye framework, grounding findings in cognitive-science theory.
  • Two deployment-relevant failure modes (Type 1/Type 2 dissociation in Llama3.3:70b; response inversion in Gemma3:12b) no calibration benchmark surfaces.
  • Honest statistical reporting — pathologies openly flagged, full sensitivity analyses.
  • Fully open artifacts (code, data, stimuli, PsychScanner) and disclosed AI-assistance.
  • Mechanistic interpretation tied to the competing-biases account (Kumaran et al.) and human calibration research.

− Limitations

  • Minimal word-pair paradigm — low ecological validity vs. real agentic sources; the Exp.1 ceiling is partly a paradigm artifact.
  • Narrow model coverage — six models, two families, all instruction-tuned via Ollama; the MoE-scaling claim rests on n=1.
  • Behavioral only — no activation-level interpretability explaining why feedback inverts mappings or confidence decouples.
  • Single decoding setting (temperature=0) and a single feedback form (binary right/wrong); alternative modalities untested.
  • ~54% of metacognitive-γ observations excluded due to ceiling/floor accuracy or invariant confidence (4,410 of 9,600 computable).
  • Human–LLM comparison is qualitative (different designs / trial counts), limiting cross-species claims.
04

Open Questions

Q1

How do source-attribution and confidence-decoupling effects transfer to deployment-realistic sources — retrieved documents, tool outputs, prior assistant turns — beyond minimal word pairs?

Q2

Is active expert-network size truly the operative scaling variable? Varying expert count vs. expert size independently would settle the headline scaling claim.

Q3

What activation-level mechanism causes Gemma3:12b's response inversion and Llama3.3:70b's d′/Type-2-AUC dissociation under feedback?

Q4

Do alternative feedback modalities (delayed, partial, confidence-targeted, explanation-bearing) preserve the accuracy–confidence link that binary right/wrong erodes?

Q5

Can a source-memory probe be integrated as a runtime safety metric in deployed multi-turn agents — and does it predict downstream errors better than surface accuracy?

Q6

Do sampling temperature and presentation order modulate the reversal and inversion effects observed at temperature=0?
