> **一句话总结：** 本文在 Intel Cyclone V FPGA 上对固定点自动调制分类器（24 个稀疏 DFT 能量特征 + 8 个相位/统计特征 → 32-128-11 MLP）做了比特精确的对照实验，发现加入一个学习型 32 元素 8 比特特征门不仅没有提升分类精度（在 PTQ 下平均反而低 0.784 pp、QAT 下低 0.616 pp），还额外占用 4 个 DSP 块、约 1.3k ALM 并增加 3,140 个处理周期——结论是：在该压缩特征表示下，学习型门控只增加硬件代价而无收益。

> **TL;DR:** A bit-exact FPGA ablation on RadioML 2016.10A compares an ungated vs. a learned-gated fixed-point AMC classifier across PTQ/QAT and two training seeds (8 bitstreams, 352,000 board classifications). The ungated model wins all four matched comparisons (mean gate penalty −0.784 pp PTQ, −0.616 pp QAT; best accuracy 47.257% ungated-PTQ-seed20260719 vs. ~46.26% gated), while the gate adds +1,318 ALMs, +4 DSP blocks (17→21), and +3,140 core cycles (+12.96%) on a 50 MHz Cyclone V. All 352,000 board predictions match an independent integer reference exactly, and the take-away is that for this 32-feature representation, learned gating is pure hardware overhead with no accuracy benefit.

## 核心贡献

- 在 Intel Cyclone V 上实现了固定点 AMC 分类器，前端使用 24 个稀疏 DFT 能量特征 + 8 个相位/统计特征，分类头为 32→128→11 MLP，并提供了完整的定点数值契约（Q1.15 输入、48 比特累加、Q16 特征接口、int16 权重等）。
- 设计了一个受控的对照实验：唯一的架构差异是是否插入一个学习型 32 元素、8 比特的输入相关特征门；在两种量化方式（PTQ / QAT）× 两个训练种子下，独立编译了 8 个 FPGA 比特流，从而隔离出该门的真实代价。
- 进行了物理板级评估：在 Terasic DE1-SoC 上完成 8 × 44,000 = 352,000 次真实板级分类，且所有板级预测与独立整数参考实现比特一致（352,000/352,000），其中 seed-20260716 的 3,760 个中间值也完全匹配。
- 给出了成对的精度、类别级、SNR 级、资源与时序量化结果，明确给出该门的硬件开销（ALM / 寄存器 / DSP / 周期 / 延迟）并讨论了门控失效的可能原因（已压缩的 32 维表示中，后续 dense 层本身已能学习特征缩放）。

## Key Contributions

- A fixed-point Cyclone-V AMC implementation whose front end reduces 128-sample I/Q windows to 24 sparse DFT-energy features plus 8 phase/statistical features, feeding a 32→128→11 MLP, with a fully documented fixed-point contract (signed Q1.15 I/Q, 48-bit DFT accumulators, signed 32-bit features at an effective Q16 interface, int16×2^10 dense weights, 64-bit accumulators, 32-bit logits).
- A matched ablation isolating a single variable: the presence of a learned 32-element 8-bit input-dependent feature gate. Two training seeds (20260716, 20260719) × two quantization schemes (PTQ, QAT) yield 8 independently compiled Quartus bitstreams that are otherwise identical in datapath, dataset split, clock target, and arithmetic widths.
- Physical-board evaluation over 352,000 real DE1-SoC classifications with bit-exact verification: every board prediction agrees with an independent integer reference (352,000/352,000), and 3,760 captured intermediate values (features, gate, hidden, logits) from seed-20260716 also match exactly.
- Paired accuracy/class-level/SNR/resource/timing measurements that quantify both the (negative) value and the (positive) hardware cost of the learned gate, supported by 95% Wilson intervals and paired McNemar tests with p-values down to ~2×10⁻¹².

## Methodology

The study is an operator-level ablation built around a compact AMC classifier. **Input**: 128 complex I/Q samples per window. **Host preprocessing**: per-window RMS normalization followed by signed Q1.15 conversion, transferred via a 4,096-bit Virtual JTAG payload. **Feature front end** (identical for both architectures): 24 sparse DFT-energy features sampled at approximately uniform 128-point bins K = {0,5,11,…,123}, computed with Q1.15 sine/cosine coefficients and 48-bit accumulators, plus 8 phase/statistical features (real/imaginary parts of Σz², Σz⁴, the conjugate autocorrelation Σz*z_{n−1}, plus Σ|z|⁴ and ΣI′Q′). These form a 32-element integer feature vector x ∈ ℤ³².

**Ungated path**: x feeds directly into ReLU(W₁x + b₁) → W₂h + b₂ → argmax. **Gated path** inserts a learned 32→32 affine transform (int16×2¹⁰ weights), an 8-bit clipped gate g = clip(p+128, 0, 255), and a feature product xg = (x ⊙ g) ≫ 8 before the same dense layers. The gate performs continuous attenuation, not binary selection. The gated model has 6,699 parameters (13.74 kB) versus 5,643 (11.56 kB) for ungated (+18.7%).

**Training**: base training is 45 epochs of Adam (batch 2,048, lr 0.0025, multiclass cross-entropy) in floating point; the checkpoint with the best *integer* validation accuracy is selected. For PTQ the floating model is converted each epoch to the deployed fixed-point arithmetic and re-evaluated. For QAT, training continues 15 epochs from the selected base checkpoint (lr 0.0005) with fake-quantized weights/biases and the deployed truncations, using a straight-through estimator through the unsaturated gate range.

**Dataset**: RadioML 2016.10A (220,000 windows, 11 modulation classes, 20 SNR values from −20 to +18 dB), split with a frozen class/SNR-stratified permutation (seed 20260716) into 132,000 train / 44,000 val / 44,000 test. **Hardware**: Terasic DE1-SoC with Intel Cyclone V 5CSEMA5F31C6, Quartus Prime Lite 18.1, 50 MHz target, sequential schedule reusing MAC hardware. **Verification**: a 17-vector ModelSim arithmetic stress suite plus the board/reference agreement checks.

## Results & Highlights

- **Ungated beats gated in all four matched comparisons.** Mean gated-minus-ungated accuracy differences are **−0.784 pp under PTQ** and **−0.616 pp under QAT**. Per-seed PTQ deltas are −0.868 pp (seed 20260716) and −0.700 pp (seed 20260719); per-seed QAT deltas are −0.607 pp and −0.625 pp. Paired McNemar p-values are as low as 2.04×10⁻¹².
- **Best single-run accuracy is 47.257%** (ungated-PTQ-seed20260719, 20,793/44,000 correct, Wilson 95% CI [46.791, 47.724]). Two-seed means: Gated PTQ 46.259%, Gated QAT 46.316%, Ungated PTQ 47.043%, Ungated QAT 46.932%, with seed-to-seed standard deviations of 0.421, 0.048, 0.302, and 0.035 pp.
- **Hardware cost of the gate** on Cyclone V: +1,318 ALMs (12,334→13,651, +10.7%), +1,557 registers (21,188→22,745, +7.3%), **+4 DSP blocks (17→21, +23.5%)**, +3,140 core cycles (24,234→27,374, +12.96%), and **+62.80 µs core latency** (484.68→547.48 µs at 50 MHz). M10K usage is 0 in both; maximum clock frequency is essentially unchanged.
- **Bit-exact deployment verification**: all 352,000 board decisions match the integer reference (352,000/352,000), and 3,760 intermediate values from seed-20260716 also match exactly, confirming the FPGA executes the intended fixed-point checkpoints.
- **QAT gives no consistent gain**: it changes gated accuracy by +0.389 pp (seed 20260716) but −0.275 pp (seed 20260719), and ungated accuracy by +0.127 pp vs −0.350 pp — the direction flips between seeds.
- **SNR behavior**: high-SNR (10–18 dB) accuracy plateaus around 75–77% (gated/ungated PTQ 75.37/76.50%); at low SNR (−20 to −2 dB) accuracy is near chance (~20%). The QAM64 high-SNR recall is only 18.8%, WBFM 32.2%, QAM16 57.2%, while AM-SSB accounts for 31.20% of all predictions despite each true class being 9.09% of the test set.

## Authors & Credibility

Both authors (Gawthaman Senthilvelan and Luthira Abeykoon) are at the University of Toronto. The paper is unusually disciplined for a hardware ablation: it reports 95% Wilson intervals, paired McNemar counts with explicit p-values, a bit-exact board/reference agreement claim (352,000/352,000), a ModelSim stress suite, and intermediate-value captures — exactly the verification rigor that FPGA ML papers often omit. The authors are also candid that the gate's parameters live outside M10K (implemented as synthesized logic) and that the design is sequential, so the comparison is architecture-faithful rather than throughput-optimized.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: Modest but well-scoped. This is a negative-result operator ablation, not a new architecture; its value is in the rigor with which a "learned gate helps" assumption is tested after fixed-point mapping. The framing — that signal-processing operators must be re-evaluated once their hardware cost becomes real — is a useful methodological point.
- **Rigor**: High for the experiment that was actually run. The bit-exact verification, McNemar tests, and matched-seed design are exemplary. However, only **two training seeds** are used, so "architecture-level variance" is barely estimated; the authors acknowledge this. The QAT result flipping sign across seeds is exactly the kind of finding that two-seed studies cannot resolve.
- **Reproducibility**: Strong on artifacts (CSVs, integer references, bitstreams, Quartus reports, checkpoints, manifests) but the paper itself flags a concrete gap — the exact frozen `board_test_payloads.hex` and feature-cache file used during the board campaign are **absent from the retained checkout**, linked only via a payload hash. The authors recommend restoring these before archival/submission. Power and energy were not measured.
- **Significance**: The headline accuracies (≈47% on RadioML 2016.10A's 11-class task) are **much lower than the 60%+ figures typical of learned I/Q CNNs on the same benchmark**. This is a direct and honest consequence of the deliberately compact 32-feature front end, but it means the absolute performance is not competitive; the paper's claim is strictly about the *incremental* effect of the gate, not about SOTA accuracy. A reader should not cite these numbers as representative of AMC capability.
- **Red flags / specifics**: (1) The dominant confusion is **WBFM→AM-DSB at 43.61%** and **QAM64→QAM16 at 30.73%**, with AM-SSB massively over-predicted — the authors attribute this to the coarse feature representation but provide no targeted feature ablation to confirm it. (2) The gate's captured values stay in the 122–192 range and never approach the 0/255 clipping boundaries, so the gate acts as a mild rescaling rather than a selector — yet there is no experiment varying gate precision or rank, which the authors admit could change the conclusion. (3) RadioML 2016.10A is a synthetic, now-dated benchmark with no captured-RF or channel-shift evaluation.

## Strengths

- Genuinely controlled design: every variable except the gate is held constant, and the 8-bitstream × 2-seed × 2-quantization grid lets the gate effect and the QAT effect be separated.
- Verification is the paper's standout contribution — 352,000 bit-exact board/reference matches plus 3,760 intermediate-value matches are far beyond typical FPGA-ML reporting.
- Honest, non-overclaimed conclusions: the authors explicitly state the result is specific to this gate/representation and list the conditions under which it might not hold.
- Full hardware cost breakdown (ALMs, registers, DSP hierarchy, cycles, latency, Fmax, slack) tied to DSP-hierarchy attribution of exactly where the 4 extra DSP blocks come from.

## Limitations

- Only two training seeds; QAT's seed-dependent sign flip cannot be resolved at this sample size.
- RadioML 2016.10A is synthetic and dated; no captured-RF, external dataset, or channel-distribution-shift evaluation.
- Board-level prototype with Virtual JTAG transport, non-overlapped inference, sequential scheduling, register-based buffering, no M10K — so streaming throughput and power/energy are uncharacterized.
- The frozen `board_test_payloads.hex` and feature-cache file are missing from the retained checkout, which the authors themselves flag as a reproducibility gap to repair before archival.
- Absolute accuracy is low (≈47%) by design due to the 32-feature front end; no experiment tests larger feature representations, lower-rank gating, different gate precision, or temporal I/Q attention, any of which could overturn the conclusion.

## Relevance

对关注高效推理、硬件友好模型设计与量化训练（PTQ/QAT）的研究者有直接参考价值：它用严格的 FPGA 比特精确实验，展示了"软件里有效的学习型算子在定点硬件上可能纯属开销"这一重要方法论结论，是做边缘端信号处理 / 射频智能模型部署与消融实验设计的良好范例。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would the gate's verdict flip with a richer front end (e.g., 64+ features or temporal I/Q attention), where the dense layer can no longer absorb feature-dependent scaling? The authors flag this but do not test it.
- Is the seed-dependent QAT sign flip a real QAT instability or an artifact of starting QAT from a single PTQ-selected checkpoint? A multi-checkpoint QAT study would help.
- How much of the WBFM→AM-DSB (43.61%) and QAM64→QAM16 (30.73%) confusion is attributable to specific missing features (FM trajectory, constellation structure) versus the MLP capacity? A targeted feature ablation is needed.
- Would mapping parameters and the I/Q buffer into M10K and moving to a pipelined/streaming schedule change the resource/latency trade-off enough to alter the gate's cost-benefit conclusion?
- Does the same gate fail on captured RF data or under channel-distribution shift, or is the negative result specific to RadioML 2016.10A's synthetic channel?
