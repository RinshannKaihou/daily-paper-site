Key Finding · failure-mode analysis

A bounded "predict-next-obs" reward still collapses every GRPO agent to 0% — the culprit is std-normalization, not the signal.

Turning off one flag flips the same reward from 0% → 51.6% success. The real +20pt gain, though, comes from routing the identical signal through an auxiliary-loss channel instead of the reward channel.

01 · Key Metric · single-factor rescue
0%→51.6%
ALFWorld success, Qwen3-4B. Flipping only the std-normalization flag in GRPO recovers the collapsed arm to baseline parity (4B baseline = 49.5%); no-signal control lands at 52.6%, so the signal's net contribution is ≈ 0.
02 · Why this matters
Tells anyone mixing dense shaping into GRPO-family RL exactly which normalizer and which channel to trust — and gives a checkable safety rule.
03 · Critical edge
"Every endpoint is single-seed, 32-episode validation noise is ±8.4pt … all cross-arm comparisons are 'descriptive' pending seed replication and a 140-game unified evaluation."

Methodology

§ 01 / mechanism + design

Agent & signal. Step-independent multi-turn rollouts (group size 4) over ALFWorld, 30–50 step horizons, near-constant context. GRPO with a sparse terminal reward (success = 10 / fail = 0) plus invalid-action penalty. A dense prediction-sufficiency (PS) signal asks the policy to emit a <predict> block over a task-agnostic feature schema Φ (location, objects-visible, receptacle state); rule-parsed, verified against the actual next observation, no judge model. Per-step reward is the potential difference r_pred(t) = λ(Φ_t − Φ_{t−1}), λ = 0.1.

The mechanism (Prop. 1 & 2). In sparse-success regimes, all-fail groups are common. Within such a group the only within-group return variation is the ε-scale shaping term; GRPO divides by the group's own σ, so the normalized advantage becomes invariant to λ — a bounded reward gets stretched to full-scale pressure (measured advantage fingerprint ±5–7 vs honest ±0.1). Prop. 2 generalizes: the amplified term is governed by the signal's within-group variance at saturation.

Rescue & channel matrix. The single-factor rescue swaps to Dr.GRPO's mean-only normalization. A 9-arm signal-delivery matrix holds the prediction signal, environment, model, and budget fixed and varies only the consumption mechanism. A synthetic POMDP (HiddenRule-Gym) with exactly computable feature coverage C = I(Φ;s)/H(s) dissociates coverage × dynamics × capacity.

Strengths & Limitations

§ 03 / side-by-side

▲ Strengths

  • Clean single-factor causal localization. Collapsing vs rescued arms differ in exactly one flag; the no-signal control attributes the rescue to the normalizer, not the signal.
  • Two compact, verifiable propositions explaining why bounded returns produce unbounded advantages — non-obvious mechanism, transparent algebra.
  • Registered-predictions ledger (SHA256 digests, Table 3): author committed before running, reports falsifications honestly, revised theory in response.
  • Shuffled-gold placebo in the delivery matrix — actively tries to kill its own preferred explanation.
  • Conditional guidance (§8) that resists universal claims, noting dense reward-channel supervision with different variance trajectories has succeeded on the same benchmark.

▼ Limitations

  • Single seed per arm; small validation. All endpoints seed-0, 32-episode, ±8.4pt noise; the paper itself labels cross-arm comparisons "descriptive." Seeds 42/96 and a 140-game eval pending.
  • Only two environments. ALFWorld + a synthetic POMDP; WebShop / InterCode / browsing untested.
  • Collapse-timing law rests on three scale points; the 8B baseline (32.8%) is anomalously low and unexplained.
  • Group size n=4 is collinear with the std pathology; a constant-budget group-size ablation is preregistered but not yet run.
  • Placebo leakiness. ~10% same-group initial-state overlap + household vocabulary bound the non-content effect from above; decisive disjoint-vocabulary placebo still running.
  • Scope is GRPO-family only — critic-based PPO with learned value baselines deliberately untested.

Open Questions

§ 04 / further exploration
  1. Does the variance-profile criterion survive seed replication and the 140-game unified eval, or do the single-seed gaps shrink below noise — especially the ~20pt auxiliary-loss gain?
  2. Does the disjoint-vocabulary placebo (in progress) confirm world-model transfer, or reveal the aux-loss gain as regularization / extra-token-budget — reframing §7?
  3. How does the failure generalize along group size: does n=8/16 at constant budget dilute the std pathology, or merely delay it by stabilizing σ̂?
  4. Can the criterion become an adaptive normalizer that switches between std and mean-only based on measured within-group variance of the dense signal?
  5. Does the same λ-invariance pathology appear under critic-based PPO with group-relative or comparison-based advantage, or is it truly specific to GRPO's empirical-σ normalization?
  6. The 8B baseline anomaly (32.8% < 4B's 49.5%) is unexplained — ALFWorld scaling quirk, hyperparameter mismatch, or a distinct instability in larger models?
