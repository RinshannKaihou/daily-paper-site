Barzilai–Borwein Fails Superlinear Convergence on an Open Set of Quadratics for Every Dimension n ≥ 4↗

Dawei Li · Xiaotian Jiang · Mingyi Hong | University of Minnesota, Twin Cities | 2026-07-23
Key finding

Proves BB1 fails superlinear convergence on a positive-Lebesgue-measure open set of strictly convex quadratics — for every dimension n ≥ 4.

Rules out superlinear convergence: lim inf ‖g_k‖^{1/k} ≥ 10⁻⁶ > 0. Objective gap decays at ρ̄² ≈ 0.372 per step.
Why this matters

Resolves a long-standing open question: BB1 is not superlinear almost-everywhere in n ≥ 4 — via an attracting 7-cycle mechanism, not a null-set counterexample.

Critical edge

The load-bearing dimension-4 certificate rests on an unreleased 200-digit interval-arithmetic script. Independent verification of the whole theorem requires re-implementing that black box.

01Methodology

One certified orbit, enlarged into an open family by stability + continuity

Not a generic-trajectory estimate. The proof builds one rigorously certified orbit of a low-dimensional dynamical system, then lifts it — five stages:

01
Exact reduction
Per-spectral multiplier dᵢ^{k+1} = (aₖ−λᵢ)/aₖ · dᵢ^k; all of Thm 2.1 reduces to one inequality 10⁻⁶ ≤ |aₖ−λᵢ|/aₖ ≤ 0.61.
02
Certified 7-cycle (dim 4)
Newton-like operator H=u−RG in directed interval arithmetic on a box of radius 10⁻⁵⁵; Banach fixed point ⇒ unique exact root.
03
Attraction
Discrete Lyapunov P − A₇ᵀPA₇ ≻ 0 ⇒ ‖A₇‖_P < 1: the 7-cycle is Schur-stable with an open basin.
04
Rates & openness
Translation λ↦λ+41 puts spectrum in (4.99, 8.01); hyperbolicity + implicit-function theorem open up an L₄ × P₄ product.
05
Lift to all n
Extra eigenvalues in (7.49,7.51) with zero weight; transverse Floquet τ(ν)<0.049 attracts the new directions. Three changes of variables reach problem data.

03Strengths vs. Limitations

Robust mechanism on one hand; a black-box certificate on the other

Strengths

  • First positive-measure lower-rate result for BB1 in all n ≥ 4 — closes an explicitly posed open question (Li & Sun 2021).
  • Provides a mechanism: attracting period-7 projective cycle + 15-step admissible landing, not just an existence count.
  • Computer-assisted certificate is rigorously framed (directed rounding, Banach, discrete Lyapunov); all constants published.
  • Exceptionally honest scope & AI-assistance disclosure; no overclaiming of universality or optimality.
  • Clean architecture (Figure 2) separating certified numeric core from the analytic lift.

Limitations

  • BB1 only — not BB2, cyclic, or safeguarded variants practitioners actually use. BB2 left open.
  • The bad set An is a small neighborhood of one spectrum; size is not uniform in n.
  • Dim-4 certificate depends on a 200-digit script that is not released; full verification needs reimplementation.
  • ρ, ρ̄ are extreme safety margins (observed ≈ 0.144); they certify the qualitative conclusion, not tight rates.
  • Constructed spectra are clustered; no word on whether bad sets exist for well-separated or random spectra.
