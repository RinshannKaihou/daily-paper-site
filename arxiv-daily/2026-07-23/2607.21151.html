02 Why this matters

Reframes video safety as an understanding–refusal decoupling failure. The three-stage diagnosis and refusal-direction method transfer to image/audio LLM alignment, inference-time defenses, and safety-subspace interpretability.

03 Critical edge

Judged by a single GPT-4o-mini with no human validation — so ASR = 0 is judge-relative, not absolute. The headline 0.14% vs SEA-SFT 0.34% margin sits inside plausible judge noise.

Also: SEA comparison runs on one model only (Qwen2-VL-7B); Qwen2.5-VL-7B's 8-shot 0.32% ASR pairs with just 26.2% refusal — possibly rephrased, not refused.

§Methodology · V-DEAL, four stages

Three diagnostic stages plus one intervention, run on 5,020 videos (8 frames each) merged from Video-SafetyBench, the VA-SafetyBench video subset, and the Omni-SafetyBench unimodal video subset, across six open-source Video LLMs (Qwen2/2.5-VL-7B, InternVideo2.5-8B, InternVL3.5-8B, MiniCPM-o/V-4.5).

STAGE 1 · BEHAVIOURFour attack conditions disentangle textual from visual harmfulness: T-HQ, T-BQ, VH-HQ, VH-BQ. A fixed GPT-4o-mini judge labels each output HARMFUL / REFUSAL / SAFE.
STAGE 2 · UNDERSTANDINGRe-run under a benign prompt emitting structured JSON labels (5 attributes, exact-match Acc / Macro-F1) + a neutral summary scored by an LLM-judge. Tests whether VH-BQ is just weak perception.
STAGE 3 · REPRESENT.Layer-wise refusal direction w_ℓ = (μ_refusal − μ_other)/‖·‖ from the last-text-token hidden state, estimated on external XSTest + MM-SafetyBench; top-m=4 layers averaged, projected onto a balanced 1,250-sample subset.
INTERVENEPrompt-injection realignment: force the model to first summarize the video's harmful visual evidence, then decide jointly with the query — optionally with 8 in-context refusal demos. Weights & decoding unchanged.

§Results & highlights

  • VH-BQ is the highest-ASR condition in 5 of 6 models. ASR jumps 35.84% → 48.33% (+12.49 pp); refusal collapses 53.05% → 14.22%. Exception MiniCPM-V-4.5 still posts 55.2% ASR at 2.9% refusal.
  • Models do perceive the harm. Structured-label accuracy 0.811–0.955; on a 300-video subset, 89.94% of outputs match reference harmful intent — yet the two best perceivers still hit VH-BQ ASRs of 49.1% & 36.1%.
  • Refusal-direction projection is mechanistic. VH-BQ scores sit below T-HQ (Qwen2-VL-7B: −0.1516 vs +0.0803); external→video transfer AUC 0.853–0.965, above the random-direction P95 control.
  • The intervention is decisive. Zero-shot ASR 0.80%, 8-shot 0.09%; InternVL3.5-8B falls 49.1% → 0.08%; three models reach 0.00%.
  • Competitive with fine-tuning. On the matched SEA setting (Qwen2-VL-7B, full VA-SafetyBench): 0-shot 0.14% / 8-shot 0.24% — under SEA-SFT (0.34%) and SEA-DPO (3.56%).

§Strengths vs. Limitations

Strengths

  • Mechanistic diagnosis linking three independent evidence types, not a lone ASR number.
  • Broad, fair coverage: 6 open Video LLMs across 4 families, 3 public benchmarks, unified manifest.
  • Intellectually honest: explicitly states strong understanding ≠ strong refusal.
  • Training-free intervention, 0.00% ASR on 3 of 6 models.
  • Detailed appendix: prompts, layer selection, parsing, transfer audit with bootstrap CIs.

Limitations

  • Single LLM judge, no human or second-judge cross-check; ASR=0 is judge-relative.
  • Only 8 frames/video; temporal evidence in long videos missed, not ablated.
  • Top-m=4 layer choice and last-text-token extraction not sensitivity-analysed.
  • SEA comparison limited to Qwen2-VL-7B — "competitive" is a one-model claim.
  • Intervention inference cost & over-refusal / benign-utility impact unreported.

§Open questions

  • Would a stronger / ensemble judge (GPT-4o, Claude) move the 0.00% claims and the razor-thin SEA margin?
  • Does the intervention trigger over-refusal on benign pairs, and what is its real latency cost?
  • Is "visual evidence activates weaker refusal than text" an artifact of text-heavy safety recipes — would vision-centric tuning close the VH-BQ vs VH-HQ gap?
  • Does the anomaly survive longer (>8 frames) and audio-bearing videos where harm lives in speech?
  • Can the latent refusal-direction score serve as an online detector that triggers the heavier prompt only when needed?
