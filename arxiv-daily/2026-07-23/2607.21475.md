> **一句话总结：** 本文先用一个不可识别性定理证明：确定性 KV-cache 驱逐在结构上无法在线估计自己引入的注意力误差（"silent failure"），随后用经典抽样理论设计 certainty+Poisson 尾部驱逐配 Hájek 对数偏置修正与 Sen–Yates–Grundy 方差估计，得到每步误差证书（25% 预算下覆盖率 97.2%、与真实误差 Spearman 0.965、且无精度代价），并在 LongBench/RULER 上以预注册方式验证七个可证伪命题——三条（含"证书预测任务失败"）诚实被毙，存活下来的是"归因"结果：证书在区分"驱逐致错 vs 任务本身难"上 AUC 0.73–0.75，远超输出置信度的 0.47–0.54，并据此把重计算调度做得比随机/置信度门控更准。

> **TL;DR:** The paper proves (Theorem 1) that no estimator computable from a *deterministic* KV-eviction scheme's online state can consistently estimate the eviction-induced attention-output error — the retained set is provably blind to what it destroyed. Replacing the tail with Poisson sampling at known inclusion probabilities restores identifiability: a single logit offset `+log(1/π_i)` does the Hájek correction inside the softmax, and a Sen–Yates–Grundy variance estimator (O(|tail|) per step, no training, no new matrices) becomes an empirical-Bernstein per-step error certificate. At the attention level it works as advertised (coverage 96.9/97.2/97.7% at 12.5/25/50% budgets, Spearman 0.943/0.965/0.979 vs true error, and median relative error 0.0317 at 25% vs 0.0447 for top-k). On a pre-registered, two-scale LongBench study (≈74k generations, Qwen2.5-1.5B/7B, Llama-3.1-8B, Mistral-7B) the authors killed 3 of 7 claims — including that the certificate predicts task-level failure (pooled AUC 0.555 at 6k / 0.572 at 16k, below the 0.6 kill line), because question-aware eviction at 25–50% budgets is nearly free on LongBench (full vs Poisson on HotpotQA 0.515 vs 0.516) and output log-probability predicts failure better. What survives is attribution: the certificate separates eviction-induced from inherent failures at AUC 0.749/0.727 vs output-confidence 0.542/0.469, and certificate-gated recomputation beats random and confidence gating (12.5% budget: 0.33 vs 0.29 random vs 0.28–0.29 confidence). Randomization buys attribution, not prediction.

## 核心贡献

- **不可识别性定理（Theorem 1，Silent Failure 现象）：** 给出两行构造性证明——只要驱逐集非空，把被驱逐的 value 替换为 `v_i + c·u`（保持 score 与保留集逐位不变），真实注意力输出可任意平移，而所有"在线可计算"的统计量比特不变。因此任何 Ft-可测的确定性驱逐误差估计量都不一致；Remark 1 进一步说明即便 score 读 value 的有限维摘要（如范数）也救不了，因为保范旋转仍能在保留集看不到的球面上自由移动被驱逐质量。
- **可识别的随机化设计 + 在线证书（Theorem 2）：** certainty（sinks+recent+top slice, π=1）+ Poisson 尾部，借助一个 logit 偏置 `+log(1/π_i)` 让 softmax 分母自动完成 Hájek 重归一；Sen–Yates–Grundy 方差在 Poisson 下塌成单和，O(|S∩T|)/head/step，无训练、无新矩阵、每个保留尾部 token 仅多一个标量。经验 Bernstein 半径（δ=0.1）把它做成每步误差证书，并给出面向解码轨迹的 e-process/Ville 任意时刻有效扩展（Remark 2，sketch 而非定理）。
- **实证量化 Silent Failure（Figure 2 的 self-signal 面板）：** 对 H2O/SnapKV/StreamingLLM/aware-top-k 四种确定性方法，在数千次带评分生成上系统评估它们能"看到自己驱逐致错"的信号——保留注意力熵 0.43–0.51（基本等于随机）、keep-boundary margin 0.49–0.50、被驱逐 score mass 0.59–0.73（部分有信号），而本文证书在同样 yardstick 上达到 0.77–0.86，普遍领先 10 个百分点以上。
- **两尺度、预注册、全披露的真实任务研究：** 7 个可证伪命题附 kill 条件全部预注册（Appendix C，时间戳与 Slack 提交记录随包发布），3 条（R1 证书预测任务失败 / R4 证书门控扩预算 / R3 证书不输输出置信度）诚实被毙，并把"为什么死"的机制讲清楚——aware 驱逐在 25–50% 预算下几乎无损（LongBench 上 full vs Poisson 接近），任务失败主要由任务本身难度驱动，缓存侧信号物理上看不到。
- **存活下来并被推到中心的结果——归因 + 调度：** 在失败样本中区分"驱逐致错 vs 本身难"，证书 AUC 0.749 (6k) / 0.727 (16k)，输出置信度仅 0.542 / 0.469（结构性低于随机，因为低置信度把"难"和"被压坏"混在一起）；据此做重计算门控，12.5% 预算下证书门控精度 0.33，显著优于随机 0.29 与置信度 0.28–0.29（每种子 95% CI 排除 0）。

## Key Contributions

- **An impossibility theorem (Theorem 1) with a two-line constructive proof:** for any value-blind deterministic eviction, two cache configurations generating bit-identical online information can have true attention-output errors differing by more than any preset M (replace evicted values with `v_i + c·u`); hence no Ft-measurable estimator of eviction error is consistent. Remark 1 extends this to value-aware scores (e.g. norm-based, as in VASE/CAOTE): rotations preserve norms yet move evicted mass on a sphere the retained set cannot see. The paper realizes this constructively in a real model — permuting evicted values makes top-k's true error range over 0.014–0.898 (a factor of 64) while every online statistic is bit-identical across all six worlds.
- **A randomized design that restores identifiability plus an online variance estimator (Theorem 2):** certainty set (sinks + recent + score-top slice, π=1) plus Poisson tail at known inclusion probabilities; a single logit offset `+log(1/π_i)` makes the softmax denominator perform the Hájek correction exactly (one line, no training, no new parameters, one extra scalar per retained tail token). Under Assumptions 1 (known design, π ≥ ε) and 2 (bounded normalized weights), the Sen–Yates–Grundy variance collapses to a single sum, `V̂_t`, computable from the retained set alone, unbiased for the linearized error up to O(B²/m²).
- **A per-step error certificate with empirically validated coverage:** the empirical-Bernstein radius (variance + range term, δ=0.1) averaged over a head/layer subsample and maximized over the first six decode steps; Remark 2 sketches an anytime-valid e-process extension via Ville's inequality and arithmetic e-value averaging, explicitly labeled a sketch (vector-to-scalar reduction and one-draw-reuse are open ends).
- **The "silent failure" phenomenon measured at scale (Figure 2, Table 3):** a panel of four online self-signals for deterministic evictors, evaluated on thousands of scored generations — retained-attention entropy 0.43–0.51 (chance), keep-boundary margin 0.49–0.50, evicted score mass 0.59–0.73 (partially sighted), versus the certificate at 0.77–0.86 on the same yardstick, consistently ≥10 points ahead.
- **A two-scale, pre-registered real-task study with all verdicts disclosed (Table 1):** seven falsifiable claims with kill conditions committed before any shard ran (timestamped in Appendix C); three died honestly — certificate's task-failure prediction AUC (0.555 at 6k, 0.572 at 16k, both under the 0.6 kill line), certificate-gated 25%→50% escalation (no headroom aware; ≈ random stream), and the certificate matching output log-prob on induced-failure prediction (log-prob wins at 16k). What survived is the attribution result.

## Methodology

- **Theory (Section 3).** Fix one attention head at decode step t; cache C={(k_i,v_i)}, scores s_i, weights a_i, full output y_t = Σ a_i v_i / Σ a_i. A value-blind deterministic evictor maps prefill state to retained set S; the online information F_t is the retained pairs, design, query history, and everything computed from them (evicted values are not F_t-measurable). Theorem 1 proves unidentifiability by perturbing evicted values by c·u. The fix (Section 3.3) keeps a certainty set A (π=1) and applies independent Bernoulli retention to the tail with `π_i = clip(m · score_i / Σ score_j, ε, 1)` (Poisson sampling, ε=1e-6). The Hájek estimator `ŷ_t = Σ (a_i/π_i) v_i / Σ (a_i/π_i)` is implemented by adding `log(1/π_i)` to the retained logit. Theorem 2 derives the single-sum variance `V̂_t` (Sen–Yates–Grundy under Poisson factoring), with an O(B²/m²) remainder from plugging in ŷ_t and N̂.
- **Certificate (Section 3.4).** Per-step radius `r_t = √(2V̂_t log(1/δ) + b̂_t log(1/δ)) / (‖ŷ_t‖ + ε_0)`, with `b̂_t` a range term; δ=0.1, averaged over every fourth head of every layer during the first six decode steps, maximized over steps. The deployed guarantee rests on measured coverage, not a theorem.
- **Harness (Appendix B).** Shared offline-replay harness: prompts prefilled once, importance scores computed H2O-style (accumulated attention), SnapKV-style (last-64 observation window), or question-position (aware); each arm gets its own compressed DynamicCache and generates greedily with per-step instrumentation. Protected positions (4 sinks, 32 recent) never evicted. Poisson arms draw Bernoulli retention with the certainty-layer mass matched to deterministic budget accounting and apply the +log(1/π_i) offset.
- **Models and suites.** Qwen2.5-1.5B/7B-Instruct, Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3. 6k suite: LongBench HotpotQA, 2WikiMQA, MultiFieldQA-en, PassageRetrieval-en (100 examples each) + a 3-passcode needle anchor; budgets {12.5%, 25%, 50%}. 16k suite: HotpotQA, PassageRetrieval-en, MuSiQue, NarrativeQA; budgets {12.5%, 25%, 50%}. Roughly 74,000 generations total, plus 12,096 attention-level replay cells on Qwen2.5-1.5B.
- **Signals and scoring.** Retained-attention entropy (normalized by log retained size), evicted score mass (1 − retained share of importance mass), keep-boundary margin (min retained − max evicted in SD units), mean output log-probability. Success: token-F1 ≥ 0.5 for QA (0.3 threshold moves no verdict), exact match for retrieval/needle. "Eviction-induced failure" = full-cache correct AND compressed wrong. Statistics: Mann–Whitney AUCs, 95% cluster bootstrap resampling examples (500 draws), paired-signal comparisons bootstrap AUC difference on shared examples.
- **Pre-registration (Appendix C).** Four claims R1–R4 written 2026-07-22 before any full 6k shard; three scale hypotheses S1–S3 before any 16k shard. Post-hoc additions (recomputation analysis, per-seed replication, exact random-gating expectations, two-draw disagreement baseline) explicitly labeled as such.
- **Cost.** Single H100/H200 per shard, ~70 GPU-hours total; prototype computes the certificate in Python on a head subsample during the first six decode steps; median decode 0.043 s/token vs 0.023 (deterministic) vs 0.015 (full cache) — ~2× overhead in this instrumentation, far from the asymptotic O(|tail|) the design admits.

## Results & Highlights

- **Attention-level coverage, correlation, and no accuracy tax (Section 4, Qwen2.5-1.5B, 12,096 cells):** certificate covers the realized error in 96.9% / 97.2% / 97.7% of cells at 12.5% / 25% / 50% budgets (δ=0.1, pass line 0.85); Spearman correlation between certificate and true error = 0.943 / 0.965 / 0.979 (pass line 0.3); median relative error at 25% budget = 0.0317 for Poisson+Hájek vs 0.0447 for top-k vs 0.2386 for uniform sampling (ordering identical at every budget; ablating the Hájek offset forfeits roughly half the gap).
- **The impossibility realized constructively (Section 4):** six random permutations of the evicted values make top-k's true error range 0.014 → 0.898 (a factor of 64), while every retained-set statistic of top-k is bit-identical across all six worlds; the certificate covers the realized error in all permutations.
- **Synthetic suites transfer to task level (Section 5):** needle retrieval with generation-time eviction, H2O retained-entropy AUC = 0.405 (n=24+24, z=−1.15, statistically chance) vs certificate AUC = 0.812 (n=192, z=10.2, median 0.99 on failures vs 0.57 on successes). Across 4 models × 4 RULER-style tasks (640 generations/cell), certificate–failure AUC positive in 16/16 cells, mean 0.836, range 0.65–0.97, each 95% CI excluding 0.5; thresholding gives error 0.229 at 30% coverage vs 0.575 at full coverage.
- **R1 killed (prediction does not transfer to real tasks):** pooled LongBench certificate-to-own-failure AUC = 0.555 at 6k and 0.572 at 16k, both below the pre-registered 0.6 kill line. Reason: question-aware eviction barely damages anything — full-cache vs Poisson at 25% budget on HotpotQA = 0.515 vs 0.516, on PassageRetrieval = 0.820 vs 0.815; aware-condition eviction-induced failure rate at 16k = 6.2% @ 25% and 10.5% @ 12.5%, with S1 ruling out the truncation artifact (4.0% at 16k/25% vs 5.6% at 6k/25% — no rise) and a no-context control (75–84% of full-correct examples are context-dependent) ruling out memorization.
- **Streaming is where the damage lives:** streaming eviction breaks 40–58% of answerable examples on the same LongBench tasks at the budgets tested, versus 6–10% for question-aware eviction — i.e., the compression-damage regime is multi-turn assistants and agent memory, where history is compressed before future queries arrive.
- **Silent-failure panel (R2 survived, Figure 2 / Table 3):** every deterministic retained-set signal evaluated for predicting that evictor's own induced failures sits at chance or near it — retained-attention entropy 0.43–0.51, keep-boundary margin 0.49–0.50; the partial exception, evicted score mass, reaches 0.59–0.73 but still trails the certificate (0.77–0.86) by ≥10 points. R2's kill condition (any deterministic signal reaching the certificate's lower CI) was not triggered.
- **R3 conceded to output log-probability (Section 6.2):** mean output log-probability beats every cache-side signal on every prediction axis — overall failure 0.73–0.80 vs 0.56–0.57 LongBench-pooled; induced failure 0.782 vs 0.778 at 6k and 0.806 vs 0.768 at 16k (paired difference −0.038, 95% CI [−0.065, −0.010]). Selective answering should be gated on output confidence.
- **What survives — attribution (Section 6.4, Figure 3):** restricted to failures, the certificate separates eviction-induced from inherent failures at AUC 0.749 (6k, n=7,924 failures, 1,133 induced) and 0.727 (16k, n=11,717, 1,776 induced); output log-probability scores 0.542 and 0.469 (at or below chance). A certificate-free two-draw disagreement baseline attributes at 0.632/0.687 (6k) and 0.626/0.620 (16k), behind the single-draw certificate and at double the generation cost with no validity statement.
- **Attribution is actionable — recomputation scheduling (Section 6.4, Table 4, Figure 4):** under streaming compression with a 25% re-run budget, certificate gating recovers accuracy 0.33 vs 0.29 (random) vs 0.28–0.29 (confidence) at the 12.5% budget (per-seed advantage over random +3.7 points, 95% CI [+2.3, +5.0]; over confidence +4.4 to +4.9 points, CI excluding zero); the ordering holds at 25% (+1.9 to +2.2 over random) and reproduces at 6k (+2.7 to +3.0 over random). Confidence gating falls below random at the harsh budget because it re-runs inherently hard examples recomputation cannot save.

## Authors & Credibility

- Single author: **Peng Xie**, Technical University of Munich (p.xie@tum.de). Experiments run on the CIT-TUM-HN cluster at TUM Campus Heilbronn; full evidence chain ~70 GPU-hours on single H100/H200 GPUs.
- Unusually strong methodological hygiene: a two-stage pre-registration (R1–R4 before any 6k shard, S1–S3 before any 16k shard) with timestamped files and Slurm submission records packaged for release; all seven verdicts disclosed including the three that died; per-run JSON logs (~98,000 scored generations + 12,096 replay cells), exact prompts, and analysis scripts that regenerate every number and figure from logs; the recomputation analysis, per-seed replication, and two-draw baseline are explicitly labeled post-hoc.
- The paper concedes the prediction axis to the output-confidence baseline the KV-compression literature does not report, and frames the result as "randomization buys attribution, not prediction" — a rare instance of a paper redefining its central claim downward to match what the data support.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty — genuine and sharp.** The framing is not "another eviction score" but a property the entire score-race literature leaves unexamined: whether the system can *know* its own eviction-induced error. Theorem 1's two-line constructive proof, the value-aware extension (Remark 1), and the design-based-inference fix (Hájek by logit offset) are a clean conceptual contribution orthogonal to H2O/SnapKV/MagicPIG/Nexus/VASE, all of which Remark 4 locates in the (design, estimator, variance-handling) taxonomy as having no variance layer.
- **Rigor — high, with a candid weakness the author concedes.** Pre-registration with kill conditions and full disclosure of killed claims is the gold standard and almost unheard of in this subfield. But the deployed per-step certificate is explicitly *not* a theorem: the empirical-Bernstein reduction from vector-valued linearized error to a scalar supermartingale involves "choices whose constants we have not settled," and the anytime extension is a sketch with two named open ends (vector-to-scalar reduction, reuse of one tail draw across steps). Coverage is therefore measured (96.9–97.7%), not proven, and rests entirely on the 12,096-cell Qwen2.5-1.5B replay.
- **Missing ablations — explicitly flagged.** The head and layer subsample (every 4th head), the six-decode-step window, and the normalizer floor ε_0=1e-9 are "unablated implementation choices"; fixed-size sampling designs (rejective / conditional Poisson) that keep the budget exact are untested, leaving open whether the budget-randomness of plain Poisson matters in practice. The Section 3 prediction that merging error should scale with within-stratum dispersion of evicted values (not with evicted attention mass) is stated but not tested.
- **A data quirk worth scrutiny.** Question-aware eviction at 25–50% budgets is "nearly free" on LongBench — full vs Poisson on HotpotQA 0.515 vs 0.516 — and this is precisely what kills R1 and makes the streaming regime the only place the certificate's prediction value survives. The paper argues this with a no-context control (75–84% of correct examples are context-dependent, so memorization is not the cause) and S1 (no 6k→16k rise), which is convincing; but it also means the headline attribution result is concentrated in the streaming/agent-memory regime approximated here by single-turn tasks whose question arrives after compression. Genuine multi-turn agent benchmarks are the untested next step the paper itself names.
- **Reproducibility — strong.** Public models (Qwen2.5, Llama-3.1, Mistral-7B), public benchmarks (LongBench, RULER-style generators), per-run JSON logs, analysis scripts, exact prompts, and timestamped pre-registration files packaged for release; every reported number regenerable from released logs. The main thing a reproducer needs is H100/H200 hardware (~70 GPU-hours).
- **Significance — moderate-to-high, scoped.** The impossibility theorem is a durable conceptual result for the KV-compression literature; the certificate is the first online, design-tied attributor of eviction-induced error, and the recomputation-scheduling product is concrete and deployable. Significance is bounded by the fact that the prediction axis (where most practitioners want a trust signal) is conceded to free output confidence, and the attribution value is concentrated in streaming/agent-memory settings not yet tested on genuine multi-turn benchmarks.
- **Red flags — minor.** Median certificate is roughly 3× the median realized error at the 25% budget (0.0997 vs 0.0317), so it is loose as an absolute bound — the paper is honest that it is "strong as a ranking signal" rather than a tight certificate. The 2× measured decode-time overhead in the unoptimized Python prototype is far from the asymptotic O(|tail|) and the paper reports the measured number rather than the promise.

## Strengths

- The impossibility theorem is short, constructive, and load-bearing for everything that follows; it reframes what a KV-eviction method can ever know about its own damage.
- The Hájek-by-logit-offset trick makes the variance-corrected estimator literally one line of code with no training and no new matrices — the engineering barrier to adoption is minimal.
- Pre-registration with kill conditions and full disclosure of the three claims that died is exemplary empirical conduct, rare in the LLM-efficiency literature.
- The honest concession that output log-probability (a baseline the KV literature does not report) wins the prediction axis, and the reframing of the surviving contribution as attribution rather than prediction, is exactly the kind of intellectual honesty the field needs.
- A usable, deployable product at the end: certificate-gated recomputation beats both random and confidence gating at matched compute, with per-seed replication and exact random-gating expectations.

## Limitations

- Contexts reach only 16k tokens and models only 8B parameters; longer contexts and larger models could shift the damage-onset curve (though the 6k→16k direction moved it *down*, not up).
- The stream condition approximates multi-turn memory with single-turn tasks whose question arrives after compression; genuine multi-turn agent benchmarks are the right next test and are not run here.
- The certificate concerns per-layer attention error; its task-level meaning is established empirically, not by a bound that crosses LayerNorm, residual streams, and autoregressive sampling.
- The anytime-valid extension (Remark 2) is a construction with a sketch, not a theorem; the deployed per-step certificate rests on measured coverage.
- Head/layer subsample, six-step window, normalizer floor ε_0, and fixed-size sampling designs (rejective / conditional Poisson) are unablated or untested.
- Measured certificate overhead is ~2× decode time in an unoptimized Python prototype, far from the asymptotic O(|tail|) scalar cost.

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the certificate's attribution value (AUC 0.73–0.75) and the recomputation-scheduling advantage survive on genuine multi-turn agent benchmarks where history is compressed across turns, rather than the single-turn-after-compression approximation used here?
- Can the deployed per-step certificate be upgraded from "measured coverage" to a true theorem by closing the two open ends of the anytime construction — the vector-to-scalar reduction and the conservative handling of one tail draw reused across decode steps?
- Do fixed-size sampling designs (rejective / conditional Poisson) that keep the budget exact match or beat plain Poisson, given their double-sum variance estimator and non-factoring joint inclusion probabilities?
- Does the Section 3 prediction hold — that merging error (CaM, MomentKV) should scale with within-stratum dispersion of evicted values rather than with evicted attention mass — and could a stratified-ratio variance layer be attached to deterministic merging methods?
- At longer contexts (32k+) and larger models (70B+), does the damage-onset curve shift enough to make the certificate's prediction axis clear the 0.6 kill line that LongBench at ≤16k with ≤8B models did not?
