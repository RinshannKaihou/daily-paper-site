Reexamining zero-shot summarization: trustworthiness of LLM-summarizers

Vasudha Bhatnagar Purnima Bindal Vikas Kumar Raj Kumari Bahl · Univ. of Delhi · CS + Statistics
Key Finding

Zero-shot LLM-summarizers are unstable under static input — run the same document 100× and scores swing widely. Factuality (AlignScore) is the weak link; BERTScore and Gemma3-4B are the most trustworthy of three small open models.

Key Metriccorpus-level ϵ
0.7957

Gemma3-4B BERTScore stability on CNN/DM — highest of three models (vs. Llama 0.6461). Semantic stability is real.

Why This Matters

Single-run summarization benchmarks hide variance — report stability + confidence intervals or be misled by point estimates.

Critical Edge

Temperature, top-p, and seed are undisclosed — the decoding stochasticity under study is itself uncontrolled.

AlignScore is the sole factuality metric; its known noise could be driving the “factuality is unstable” result, not the summaries. Conclusions rest on 3 small (<4B) open models.

01 / METHODOLOGY

A two-stage Stability Analysis Protocol

Each LLM-summarizer is run 100 times on the same document with one fixed prompt and default decoding — so all score variation is attributable to decoding stochasticity, not prompt or parameter confounds.

STEP 01
Generate
100 zero-shot summaries per document, fixed prompt, default params.
STEP 02
Score
BERTScore (semantic), AlignScore (factual), Semantic Consistency.
STEP 03
Document ϵ
Stability coefficient = 1 − max pairwise score gap (pessimistic).
STEP 04
Corpus E
Min ϵ over 32 docs + Levene, Friedman/Nemenyi, bootstrap CIs.

Scale of the study

  • 3 small open LLMs — Gemma3-4B, Llama-3.2-3B, Qwen-2.5-3B
  • 3 genres — CNN/DM (news), PubMed (medical), IN-Abs (legal)
  • 32 length-stratified documents per genre (Z-test / χ² validated)
  • 100 repeated generations per document → >28K summaries

Statistical machinery

  • Dispersion: Gini, CV, IQR, skew, kurtosis
  • Variance homogeneity: Levene's test
  • Normality check: Shapiro-Wilk before non-parametric tests
  • Benchmarking: Friedman + Nemenyi with CD diagrams; 10,000-resample bootstrap CIs
02 / RESULTS

Semantics are stable. Factuality is not.

On CNN/DM, BERTScore corpus-level stability is healthy for all three models — but AlignScore stability collapses to near zero, exposing factuality as the unstable dimension. Gemma3 ranks #1 on BERTScore stability for every genre.

03 / ASSESSMENT

Strengths & Limitations

Strengths

  • Principled statistics. Named tests, stated null hypotheses, Shapiro-Wilk gating before non-parametric tests.
  • Representative sampling. Length-stratified 32-doc sample validated by Z-test & χ²; honest handling of IN-Abs outliers.
  • Multidimensional. Semantic (BERTScore, SC) and factual (AlignScore) stability analyzed separately — isolates factuality as the unstable axis.
  • Cross-genre + pre-registered significance. Friedman/Nemenyi rather than eyeballed comparisons.
  • Actionable recommendation. Distribution-based, multi-run evaluation with CIs; stability as a new evaluation dimension.

Limitations

  • Narrow model coverage. Only 3 small (<4B) open-weights models — no GPT-4/Claude, no 7B/70B. “LLM-summarizers” overgeneralizes.
  • Decoding opacity. Temperature, top-p, seed undisclosed — the stochasticity under study is uncontrolled.
  • Single factuality metric. AlignScore alone; its noise may be driving the headline finding. No FactCC/DAE/QAG cross-check.
  • Pessimistic ϵ. Min-of-doc, max-pairwise form is outlier-sensitive; sensitivity to k not reported.
  • No human evaluation. Link between low stability and user-perceived trust is asserted, not measured. 32 docs limits corpus power.
04 / OPEN QUESTIONS

Where this goes next

  1. Does the AlignScore-is-unstable finding replicate under FactCC, DAE, QAFF, or SummaC? If not, the instability is in the metric, not the summaries.
  2. How do stability coefficients move when temperature, top-p, and seed are explicitly swept? Can low-temperature decoding recover factuality stability?
  3. Do the conclusions transfer to larger / proprietary models (Llama-3.1-70B, GPT-4, Claude)?
  4. Is there a cheaper robust estimator (IQR-based, trimmed) that converges faster than the pessimistic min-of-doc coefficient?
  5. Does low stability cascade downstream when summarization is an intermediate step in a RAG / agent pipeline?
  6. Can expert/user judgments validate that measured low stability maps to perceived untrustworthiness in medical and legal domains?
