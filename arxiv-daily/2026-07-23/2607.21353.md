> **一句话总结：** 本文通过一个匹配计算量的受控消融实验证明，SalUn 风格的梯度显著性掩码在 ResNet-18/CIFAR-10/100 类遗忘中并不决定表征层面的遗忘效果——显著性掩码、等稀疏随机掩码与无掩码更新三者产生统计等价的可恢复性，真正驱动这一现象的是遗忘梯度在末层（CIFAR-10 上约 92% 的平方梯度能量）的集中分布以及显著性掩码极弱的类别特异性（特异性指数 0.09–0.11）。

> **TL;DR:** This paper runs the first matched-compute ablation of SalUn's gradient-saliency masking on CIFAR-10/CIFAR-100 with ResNet-18, holding the random-label unlearning objective, optimizer, and budget fixed while varying only the weight mask. The saliency mask, an equal-sparsity random mask, and no-mask RL-full all yield statistically equivalent (TOST, δ=±5pp, p<0.001) representation-level recoverability: prototype recovery of ~93.2/93.9/93.6% on CIFAR-10 versus 41.5% for the Gold retrain — a ~52pp gap. The mechanism is that forget gradients are already concentrated in late layers (~92% of squared gradient energy) before masking, and saliency masks show weak class specificity (index 0.09–0.11, Jaccard 0.83–0.94 with a diagonal-Fisher mask). A causal early-mask intervention confirms the late subspace is unnecessary for output-level forgetting (all conditions reach 0% forget accuracy) but uniquely displaces prototype geometry toward Gold without erasing linear separability.

## 核心贡献

- **首个匹配计算量的受控消融：** 首次在 SalUn 风格类遗忘中将显著性掩码与等稀疏随机掩码、无掩码全更新进行隔离对比，固定随机标签遗忘目标、优化器、学习率、修复轮数与计算预算，唯一变量是掩码身份，从而把"权重选择"从"遗忘目标函数"中解耦出来。
- **统计等价性的严格证据：** 在 CIFAR-10/CIFAR-100 + ResNet-18 上，三种掩码条件在线性探针召回与（CIFAR-10）原型恢复上均处于预设 ±5pp 等价区间内（TOST p<0.001），意味着显著性权重的"身份"并不解释表征层面的可恢复性。
- **梯度集中机制：** 定量揭示遗忘梯度在施加任何掩码前就已高度集中于末层（CIFAR-10 末层约 92%、CIFAR-100 约 81% 的平方梯度范数），导致所有掩码都作用于同一表征子空间；该集中性在保留集梯度上也同样成立（≈95%/≈79%），表明这是收敛网络的几何性质而非遗忘集诱导。
- **掩码类别特异性近乎为零：** 提出特异性指数 (J_other−J_0)/(1−J_0)，CIFAR-10=0.096、CIFAR-100=0.107，且显著性掩码与对角 Fisher 掩码的 Jaccard 重叠高达 0.83–0.94，说明所选权重对遗忘类别几乎不敏感。
- **因果放置干预：** 设计 Early mask（冻结 layer4+head，仅更新 stem+layer1-3）及其 3× 学习率变体，证明末层子空间对输出级遗忘并非必要（60 次运行遗忘准确率均降到 0–4%），但其唯一作用是把原型几何推向 Gold 状，而非真正擦除——线性可分性在任何放置下都存活。
- **方法论启示：** 指出仅靠输出级（遗忘准确率=0%）评估会严重误导删除质量判断，并强调未来方法应直接作用于潜在表征几何，而非不断精进权重选择。

## Key Contributions

- **First matched-compute ablation of saliency masking in class unlearning**, isolating the contribution of weight selection from the random-label forgetting objective by keeping objective, optimizer, learning rate, repair epochs, retain term, sparsity, and data splits fixed across the saliency mask, an equal-sparsity random mask, and unconstrained RL-full updates.
- **Statistically rigorous equivalence claims** using the two one-sided tests (TOST) procedure at a pre-specified ±5pp margin (calibrated to the ~3–4pp inter-seed noise floor) — a protocol the authors note is absent from prior unlearning equivalence claims, which typically assert "similar" by inspection.
- **Identification of gradient concentration as the explanatory mechanism**: the forget-loss gradient carries ~92% (CIFAR-10) / ~81% (CIFAR-100) of its squared L2-energy in layer 3+4+head before any mask is applied, so all masks act on the same late subspace; the retain-set gradient shows the same late concentration, confirming it is a generic property of the converged network rather than forget-set induced.
- **Quantification of weak class specificity** via a specificity index (0.09–0.11) and Jaccard analysis showing the SalUn mask overlaps an other-class mask (0.397/0.405) almost identically to a retain-set mask (0.395/0.400), and overlaps a diagonal-Fisher mask at 0.83–0.94 against the 1/3 chance null — establishing class-agnosticism for the whole gradient-derived mask family.
- **A causal placement intervention** (Early mask, freezing layer 4+head; plus a 3× LR forced variant) that falsifies the sufficiency of the late subspace for output-level forgetting while refining the mechanism: late-subspace updates uniquely produce Gold-like prototype geometry but never constitute erasure, since linear-probe separability survives at base level under every placement.
- **A clear methodological directive**: output-level metrics alone are insufficient for deletion-quality claims, and effective representation-level unlearning should use objectives acting directly on latent structure rather than more sophisticated weight-selection.

## Methodology

The study fixes a single-factor experimental design on CIFAR-10 and CIFAR-100 with a ResNet-18 backbone, the configuration used by the closest representation-level auditing work (Gao et al., 2026; Ha et al., 2025).

- **Unlearning objective (held fixed):** the random-label (RL) objective of Golatkar et al. (2020), combining a forget term that pushes Df samples toward randomly sampled wrong labels with a retain term (weight λ) that preserves Dr accuracy (Eq. 2).
- **Three matched-compute conditions** branched from one identically trained base model, differing only in the binary mask m applied to the gradient in θ_{t+1} = θ_t − η(m ⊙ ∇L_RL): (i) the SalUn saliency mask selecting the top-k=⌊sp⌋ weights by absolute forget-gradient magnitude, (ii) a random mask of identical cardinality ‖mR‖₀ = ‖mS‖₀, (iii) RL-full with m=1. Two extra conditions sharpen the analysis: a diagonal-Fisher mask (top-50% by second-moment importance) to test dependence on the selection rule, and an Early mask that freezes layer 4+head and makes only stem+layers 1–3 trainable (24.8% of parameters), with a 3× LR gradient-clipped variant.
- **Reference models:** Gold = retrain from scratch on Dr (exact removal, Eq. 1); RandInit = untrained network serving as a texture/low-level floor.
- **Representation-level metrics:** (a) linear probe on frozen average-pool features; (b) prototype recovery (k=5 few-shot nearest-centroid attack); (c) layer-wise linear CKA between original and unlearned models; (d) head-only relearning; (e) membership-inference permutation test. Plus forget-class cosine silhouette and t-SNE.
- **Gradient-energy and mask-overlap diagnostics:** share of forget-gradient squared L2-norm by network region; Jaccard overlap of the saliency mask against other-class, retain-set, and random-label masks; a specificity index (J_other − J_0)/(1 − J_0) with null J_0 = 1/3.
- **Statistical protocol:** TOST equivalence testing at δ = ±5pp (vs paired t-tests), 10 classes × 3 seeds (42, 1337, 999) = 30 runs per condition; each class averaged over seeds before pairing (n=10). For the high-variance CIFAR-100 prototype metric (per-class SD ≈13pp), the authors report a power calculation (≈40 classes needed) and rely on the lower-variance linear probe as the decisive test.

## Results & Highlights

- **Prototype recovery equivalence (CIFAR-10):** SalUn, Random mask, and RL-full produce 93.2±0.3%, 93.9±0.5%, and 93.6±0.8% mean prototype recovery, against 41.5±0.1% for Gold retrain and 25.5±1.2% for RandInit — so all three approximate-unlearning conditions sit ~52pp above Gold, and pairwise TOST differences are at most 0.6pp (p<0.001) (Table 1, Table 2).
- **Linear-probe equivalence on both datasets:** on CIFAR-10 all pairwise mask differences are ≤0.2pp; on CIFAR-100 they are ≤0.6pp; all TOST-equivalent at p<0.001, confirming the saliency mask yields no distinguishable representation-level advantage over random or unconstrained updates (Table 2).
- **Output-level forgetting succeeds everywhere yet leaves info recoverable:** in all three conditions forget accuracy drops to 0.0% (the model stops predicting the forgotten class), while ~93% of the class remains prototype-recoverable on CIFAR-10 — demonstrating that zero forget accuracy does not imply representation-level erasure.
- **Gradient concentration (the mechanism):** before any mask, layer 3+layer 4+head account for ~92% of the squared forget-gradient norm on CIFAR-10 (per-seed range 87–99%) and ~81% on CIFAR-100 (range 77–88%); the random-label gradient SalUn actually optimizes is even more concentrated (~99% / ~89%); the retain-set gradient shows the same late concentration (~95% / ~79%) (Figure 5, §4.5).
- **Weak class specificity of masks:** specificity index 0.096 (CIFAR-10) and 0.107 (CIFAR-100); Jaccard of SalUn mask vs other-class masks (0.397 / 0.405) is indistinguishable from vs retain-set masks (0.395 / 0.400); the diagonal-Fisher mask overlaps SalUn at J=0.83 (CIFAR-10) and J=0.94 (CIFAR-100) against the 1/3 chance null (Table 3, §4.7).
- **Fisher mask reproduces the outcome:** the second selection rule lands at 93.4% (CIFAR-10) / 25.6% (CIFAR-100) recovery, statistically equivalent to SalUn (TOST p<0.001 / p=0.01) and equivalent to all three conditions on the linear probe (p<0.001), because both rules inherit the same late-concentrated gradient and select nearly the same weights.
- **Causal placement test (§4.8, Table 4):** the Early mask (late subspace frozen) still drives forget accuracy to 0–4% across all 60 class-seed runs while retain accuracy stays at Gold level (72–74% on CIFAR-100) — output-level forgetting does not need the late subspace. On the linear probe the Early mask is TOST-equivalent to SalUn on both datasets (96.8 vs 98.0% on CIFAR-10; 92.2 vs 92.5% on CIFAR-100; p<0.001), sitting ~5pp above Gold. On prototype recovery, placement matters only where the metric has room to move: on CIFAR-100 the Early mask leaves 59.0±1.4% recovery, +33.5pp above SalUn's 25.5% (t-test p<10⁻⁵), while every late-subspace condition sits at the Gold floor.
- **Sparsity robustness:** a sweep over sparsity {0.1, 0.3, 0.5, 0.7} on three representative classes shows SalUn and random-mask outcomes stay close with small mixed-sign differences; raw-mask overlap matches the analytic chance null r/(2−r) to within 0.0005 at every ratio on both datasets (§4.3, Table C.5).
- **Geometric coherence preserved:** SalUn/Random/RL-full/Fisher all keep the forget-class silhouette on CIFAR-10 at ~0.6–0.7 (like the original model), whereas Gold pushes it toward −0.09 — the cluster survives compact and separable under all approximate methods (§4.6).

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** Genuine and well-scoped. This is explicitly a mechanistic *explanation* paper, not a new method, and it is the first to isolate SalUn-style saliency masking under a matched-compute protocol. The contribution is a re-framing (mask identity is not the load-bearing factor) backed by three converging lines of evidence (matched ablation, gradient-energy localization, mask-specificity analysis) plus a causal placement intervention. The honest framing — that the late subspace is sufficient but not necessary for output-level forgetting while being uniquely responsible for Gold-like prototype geometry — is a substantive and non-obvious refinement.
- **Rigor:** Above average for the unlearning auditing literature. The TOST equivalence protocol at a pre-specified ±5pp margin calibrated to the seed-noise floor is a meaningful upgrade over the field's typical "looks similar by inspection" claims, and the authors openly report a power calculation showing the CIFAR-100 prototype metric is under-powered (would need ≈40 classes vs their census of 10), correctly down-weighting it in favor of the linear probe. Multiple seeds (42, 1337, 999) and a full 10-class census on CIFAR-10 strengthen the main claims.
- **Reproducibility:** Strong. Matched conditions branching from one base model, public datasets, public code on Zenodo + GitHub, and explicit hyperparameters (sparsity, repair epochs, learning rate, the 3× forced variant). The one soft spot is that the contrastive-mask diagnostic is reported for only one class and one seed and is correctly demoted to "supporting diagnostic."
- **Significance:** The paper does not kill SalUn — it explicitly does not challenge SalUn's reported output-level or generative behavior — but it does undercut the common assumption that the saliency mechanism is what provides representation-level deletion, which has direct consequences for privacy/compliance claims built on it. The causal result that "geometry-sensitive scores can be driven toward retrain-like values by any late-subspace update without any loss of class separability" is an important warning for auditors.
- **Red flags / overclaiming:** Bounded well. The authors repeatedly scope conclusions to "the ResNet-18 class-unlearning setting on CIFAR-10/100," explicitly note they do not study transformers, LLMs, diffusion models, or random data-point forgetting, and flag the forced variant as supporting-only because it pays 3–6pp retain accuracy on CIFAR-100 and 2/30 runs missed the forget-accuracy criterion. Two genuine weaknesses: (i) the CIFAR-100 prototype comparison is statistically indeterminate (TOST p=0.08–0.20) rather than supportive, so the headline equivalence there rests entirely on the linear probe — a single-metric dependence the authors acknowledge but do not fully de-risk; (ii) the Gold retrain itself reaches 25.5% prototype recovery on CIFAR-100 (tied with the unlearning conditions), which means prototype recovery cannot distinguish approximate unlearning from exact retraining on that dataset, materially limiting what the CIFAR-100 results can say about deletion quality. The specificity index, while clean, is only a structural overlap measure and the authors concede it is not a complete theory of mask function.

## Strengths

- Clean single-factor experimental design that genuinely isolates mask identity from the objective, optimizer, and budget — a rarity in a literature that usually compares whole pipelines.
- TOST equivalence testing with an a priori margin calibrated to seed noise, replacing inspection-based "similar" claims; paired with a transparent power analysis.
- Mechanistic triangulation: matched ablation + gradient-energy decomposition + mask-overlap (Jaccard, specificity index) + a second selection rule (Fisher) that reproduces both the outcome and the selected weights.
- A genuinely causal intervention (Early mask + forced variant) that falsifies one part of the mechanism story (late subspace not needed for output forgetting) and sharpens another (late subspace uniquely moves prototype geometry without erasing separability).
- Honest scope and limitations; transparent AI-use and funding declarations; public code/data archive.

## Limitations

- Scope restricted to single-class unlearning on CIFAR-10/100 with ResNet-18; no random data-point forgetting, no ImageNet/transformer/multimodal/LLM/diffusion settings — so the gradient-concentration mechanism is an empirical finding, not a theorem.
- CIFAR-100 prototype recovery is non-discriminative (Gold itself reaches 25.5%, tied with the unlearning conditions, and the metric is under-powered with n=10), so the CIFAR-100 equivalence rests almost entirely on the linear probe.
- The forced 3× LR Early-mask variant is supporting-only: it loses 3–6pp retain accuracy on CIFAR-100 (below the utility gate on all 30 runs) and 2/30 runs failed the forget-accuracy criterion.
- Jaccard overlap / specificity index is a structural diagnostic, not a complete functional-equivalence measure; two masks could overlap heavily yet interact differently with gradient magnitudes and the local loss landscape.
- The contrastive-mask diagnostic covers only one class and one seed, so the claim that class specificity cannot easily be increased is preliminary.
- The authors do not test whether objectives that act directly on representations (the design direction they advocate) actually escape the gradient-concentration trap; the recommendation is motivated but not demonstrated.

## Relevance

该工作通过严格的匹配计算量消融，揭示 SalUn 等基于权重显著性的机器遗忘方法在表征层面并不真正实现类擦除，这与用户对 cs.LG 中机器遗忘、隐私合规评估与表征分析的关注高度契合；其 TOST 等价性检验、层级 CKA 与线性探针审计协议，对构建可信的遗忘评估基准具有直接方法论参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the late-layer gradient concentration (≈92% energy) generalize beyond ResNet-18 to ViTs, CLIP-style encoders, and decoder-only LLMs, or is it an artifact of residual-CNN + global-average-pool geometry?
- Would a matched-compute ablation on *random data-point* (rather than class) forgetting still show mask-identity equivalence, given that per-sample gradients are typically less localized?
- Can a representation-level objective (prototype collapse, contrastive shaping, or orthogonal projection) actually drive linear-probe separability toward Gold, or does any logit-anchored update leave the class separable regardless of the objective?
- The Fisher mask overlaps SalUn at J=0.83–0.94; do all gradient/Fisher-derived selectors provably converge to the same late-layer subset, and is there a non-gradient selection rule that selects early/intermediate weights and breaks the equivalence?
- Is the 0.09–0.11 specificity index a floor forced by the architecture (lottery-ticket-style critical weights), and could contrastive (forget − retain) masking, replicated across all classes/seeds, lift it enough to matter functionally?
- How should unlearning benchmarks combine linear probe, prototype recovery, CKA, and MIA into a single deletion-quality score that is robust to the fine-grained-geometry confound the authors expose on CIFAR-100?
