> **一句话总结：** 作者在 Qwen3-14B 上以"分阶段潜在思考课程 + GRPO 强化学习"训练国际象棋模型，合法走子率从 48%（RL 前）单调提升到 61% 并彻底消除"将杀幻觉"，但一套六条件因果干预实验揭示：模型并不真正依赖潜在思考向量的内容，强化学习的真正作用是把鲁棒性固化进权重（主要在 MLP 的 gate_proj），而非让模型在推理时"读取"自己的隐式草稿。

> **TL;DR:** On a Qwen3-14B chess model trained through a staged latent-thought (Coconut-style) curriculum plus GRPO, legal-move rate climbs from 48% pre-RL (Stage-2) to 61% post-RL (Rung-3) on a frozen 100-position Stockfish-verified harness while checkmate confabulation falls 19→0, even though move-quality accuracy stays flat at 9-10% (the same ceiling as prior chess-RLVR work). A six-condition causal intervention battery run on the *same* model before and after RL shows neither checkpoint relies on the *content* of its latent thoughts (fixed-vector and matched-noise substitution cost ~0 legality); what RL adds is robustness to disruption, most clearly under exact-zero corruption (legality 1% pre-RL vs 9% post-RL, p<0.001). The conclusion: in this setting latent reasoning shapes the weights during training rather than functioning as an actively consulted inference-time scratchpad.

## 核心贡献

- **行为层面的发现（在棋类领域跑通了潜在推理+RL）**：在 Qwen3-14B 上通过分阶段潜在思考课程（Stage-2，4 个思考位置）再加 GRPO（Rung-3），合法走子率从 SFT 的 38%、显式 CoT+RL 的 52%、Stage-2 的 48% 一路升到 61%；这与数学/逻辑领域 Ouro、Coconut 直接套 GRPO 无增益的结论相反，是首次在棋类中跑出 RL 增益。
- **机制层面的因果证据（RL 增加的是鲁棒性而非对思考内容的依赖）**：在同一模型 RL 前后跑同一套六条件干预（Baseline / Substitute / Noise / Ablate / Lenmatch-Ablate / Zero）。内容替换类条件几乎零代价，删除类轻微下降，只有置零才会崩溃；并且 RL 后的检查点在每个破坏条件下都比 RL 前保留更多能力，唯一通过显著性的是 Zero（1%→9%，p<0.001，Bonferroni 校正后仍显著）。
- **"模板化幻觉"（Templated Confabulation）现象**：错误将杀解释并非针对具体棋盘推理出错，而是高度刻板的固定模板——跨棋盘文本相似度 0.68，而真实走子解释仅 0.27。
- **"幻觉截断"（Confabulation Cutoff）**：门控合法性奖励（非法/无法解析/假将杀都直接 -1.0）在没有专门反幻觉信号的情况下，把假将杀从 28（SFT）→19（Stage-2）→0（Rung-3）；两条独立训练路径（显式 CoT+RL 与潜在+RL）都收敛到 0。
- **权重空间定位**：RL 引起的 ΔW 集中而非弥散——68.9% 在 MLP 投影中，仅 gate_proj 一项就占 34.8%，注意力占 31.1%；与独立训练的显式 Rung-1 ΔW 的整适配器余弦相似度 +0.034（前 27 层 +0.052，后 13 层 ≈0），说明该鲁棒性效应部分是奖励的通用结果、部分与深度相关。
- **J-lens 校准式机制探针**：校准过的 Jacobian lens 在普通文本上以约 94% 恢复即将出现的棋子（比普通 logit lens 高约 90 个百分点），但读潜在思考向量时只能返回噪声——跨棋盘余弦相似度约 0.99，证明思考向量是固定的、与棋盘无关的支架，而非承载棋盘特定内容的"草稿"。

## Key Contributions

- **A behavioral finding in a domain where latent-reasoning+RL had been failing.** Legal-move rate rises monotonically through a staged latent curriculum (Coconut-style continuous thoughts) plus GRPO on Qwen3-14B: 38% (SFT) → 52% (explicit CoT+RL) → 48% (Stage-2, latent pre-RL) → 61% (Rung-3, latent+RL). This contradicts the reported null results for latent+GRPO in math/logic (Ouro, Coconut-direct-GRPO).
- **A mechanistic, before/after causal case.** A six-condition intervention suite (Baseline, Substitute, Noise, Ablate, Lenmatch-Ablate, Zero) is run on the *same* model before and after RL, so any difference isolates what RL did to the weights. RL adds robustness to disruption, not reliance on thought content.
- **Templated Confabulation.** False-checkmate justifications are stereotyped: 0.68 cross-board text similarity vs 0.27 for genuine move explanations — evidence of a fixed learned template, not board-specific reasoning gone wrong.
- **The Confabulation Cutoff.** A single gated-legality reward (illegal / unparseable / false-mate all score −1.0, Stockfish never consulted) drives false-mate claims 28→19→0 with no dedicated anti-hallucination signal, reaching zero under two independently designed training recipes.
- **Weight-space localization.** The RL-induced ΔW concentrates in MLP (68.9%, gate_proj alone 34.8%) vs attention (31.1%), and shows a small but consistently positive structural overlap (+0.034 whole-adapter cosine) with an independently trained explicit-reasoning RL delta across the first two-thirds of the network.
- **Calibrated mechanistic probe (J-lens).** A Jacobian lens that recovers ~94% of upcoming piece tokens on ordinary text returns only noise at the four thought positions; cross-board cosine similarity of raw thought vectors is ~0.99 — a fixed board-invariant scaffold, not load-bearing content.

## Methodology

**Training ladder (4 checkpoints, all LoRA adapters on Qwen3-14B).**
- *SFT baseline:* imitation on 12,002 Stockfish-annotated chess positions, each with a five-section written explanation ([KING SAFETY][CHECKS][CAPTURES & TRADES][THREATS][IMPROVEMENT]). Reaches 38% legal, 28 false-mate / 100.
- *Rung 1 (explicit + GRPO):* GRPO on top of SFT with reasoning still in words. Reward **gates** on legality (illegal / unparseable / false-mate ⇒ −1.0; correct mate ⇒ +1.0; otherwise `exp(−cp_loss/120)` with cp_loss = best_eval − move_eval at depth 12). G=4, LR 5e-6, KL 0.02, 500 steps → 52% legal, 0 false-mate. (The gated design replaces an earlier weighted-sum reward that Goodharted: policy gamed the cheap legality term and move quality regressed.)
- *Rung 2 (staged latent curriculum):* starting from SFT, replace each written section with 2 silent Coconut-style thought positions, resetting the optimizer at each stage boundary; pure imitation, no reward. Curriculum stopped after stage 3 when legality tripped a pre-registered <20% stopping rule (stage 1: 46%, stage 2: 48%, stage 3: 19%). **Stage-2** (4 thoughts) is the pre-RL checkpoint used throughout.
- *Rung 3 (latent GRPO):* GRPO on Stage-2 with the same gated reward; only the decoded move is scored (no ground truth for thoughts). 4 thought positions fixed; KL reference is Stage-2 itself (not the base model); LR 2e-6 (5e-6 destabilized), G=4, KL 0.02, 150 steps → **Rung-3**, 61% legal, 0 false-mate.

**Frozen evaluation harness.** 100 positions fixed once by seed 3407, never changed. Greedy decoding; latent checkpoints run at batch size 1 (bf16 dequantization order makes output batch-dependent). Four metrics: Format, Legal (parsed move legal in FEN), Accuracy (exact match vs Stockfish top move at depth 12; ~10% is random-legal-move floor), False Mate (confident checkmate on a non-mate position). The harness contains no genuine checkmate position, so *every* checkmate declaration recorded is necessarily false.

**Six-condition causal intervention suite** (applied identically to Stage-2 and Rung-3, on the same four thought positions): Baseline (real thoughts); Substitute (single fixed averaged vector T* at all positions); Noise (independent matched-norm random vectors); Ablate (remove the 4 positions); Lenmatch-Ablate (replace with pad-token embeddings, attention unmasked); Zero (exact-zero vectors).

**Supporting analyses.** (1) *Gumbel reparameterization control (SofT-GRPO-style):* top-20 logits, τ=4, straight-through Gumbel-Softmax gives RL an actual gradient into thought content — tests whether a working gradient there would beat the deterministic recipe. (2) *J-lens probing* fit directly on the live 4-bit+LoRA model, calibrated on real chess text (layers 26-29 useful band; layer 28 ≈94% piece recovery). (3) *ΔW localization:* reconstruct the LoRA weight delta at all 280 (layer, projection) slots and compare Frobenius-norm shares and cosine similarity against the independently trained Rung-1 delta.

## Results & Highlights

- **Headline legality gain (frozen 100-position harness):** Rung-3 reaches **61% legal moves vs 48% for Stage-2 (pre-RL latent)**, **52% for the explicit-CoT+RL baseline (GRPO v2)**, and **38% for SFT** — the latent-curriculum-then-RL recipe is the only one reaching the top. Move-quality **Accuracy stays flat at 9-10%** across every checkpoint (vs ~10% random-legal floor), reproducing the accuracy ceiling of prior chess-RLVR work.
- **Confabulation eliminated with no dedicated signal:** false-checkmate claims fall **28 (SFT) → 19 (Stage-2) → 0 (Rung-3)**, and independently **28 → 0** under explicit-CoT RL (GRPO v2). Two different training recipes converge on zero.
- **Only exact-zero corruption reaches significance in the before/after battery:** legality collapses to **1% (Stage-2, pre-RL) vs 9% (Rung-3, post-RL)** under the Zero condition (McNemar p<0.001 for each), with the between-checkpoint retention-ratio gap **+0.134, bootstrap 95% CI [0.050, 0.231]**, surviving Bonferroni correction across the five-condition family (**CI [0.031, 0.263]** at a 99%-per-test threshold). The four milder conditions trend the same direction (Substitute +0.000, Noise +0.034, Ablate +0.066, Lenmatch-Ablate +0.101) but do not independently clear significance at n=100 — the paper states this plainly rather than overclaiming.
- **Content substitution costs nothing on either checkpoint:** Rung-3 baseline 58% vs Substitute 58% and Noise 60%; Stage-2 baseline 48% vs Substitute 48% and Noise 48% (the Rung-3 Table-2 baseline of 58% vs the Table-1 headline 61% is attributed to NF4/bf16 dequantization run-to-run variation, with all within-table comparisons using the internally consistent 58%).
- **Templated Confabulation:** false-checkmate explanations average **0.68 pairwise text similarity across 96 cross-board completions**, vs **0.27 for real, distinct move explanations** built on the identical five-section template — the gap is content, not format.
- **J-lens null on the thoughts:** cross-board cosine similarity of raw thought vectors ≈**0.99** (slot-to-slot within a board 0.31-0.64, so not one vector repeated); J-lens recovers the upcoming piece ~**94%** on real text at layer 28 vs ~0% for plain logit lens — a calibrated instrument returning a null, not an inconclusive one. Stage-2 (0.992) vs Rung-3 (0.992) identical to three decimals: the scaffold is already fixed before RL.
- **Weight localization:** **68.9% of ΔW in MLP** (gate_proj 34.8%, up_proj 19.9%, down_proj 14.1%) vs **31.1% in attention** (q_proj 12.4%, o_proj 9.2%, k_proj 5.1%, v_proj 4.4%); cross-recipe whole-adapter cosine similarity with independent Rung-1 ΔW is **+0.034** (+0.052 over layers 1-27, −0.011 over layers 28-40).
- **Gumbel control underperforms:** giving RL a working gradient into thought content yields only **44% → 50% legal in 150 steps** (vs Rung-3's 13-point climb to 61%), and false-mate plateaus at 15/100 rather than reaching zero — a second independent line of evidence that thought content is not the lever.

## Authors & Credibility

Single-authored by **Ishan S. Kshirsagar (Independent Researcher)**, dated July 24, 2026. Despite sole authorship and no institutional affiliation, the work shows strong methodological discipline: a pre-registered curriculum stopping rule (halt if legality <20%), a frozen evaluation harness fixed after Rung 1 and reused unchanged, a gated reward explicitly motivated as a fix to a documented Goodhart failure in an earlier weighted-sum version, matched-budget controls (Rung-3 vs Gumbel both 150 steps), and Bonferroni correction applied across the five-condition family rather than cherry-picking the one significant test. Related-work coverage is unusually even-handed, engaging directly with the closest prior work (Switch, ref 21) and reporting the quantitative disagreement rather than glossing it. The paper repeatedly reproduces prior ceilings (the chess-RLVR accuracy plateau from Hwang et al. ref 6; Ouro/Coconut-direct-GRPO null results) rather than claiming to beat them.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** Genuinely novel on two axes: (1) the *before-and-after RL* application of a causal intervention battery on the same model (Switch and other prior latent-reasoning causal work run on a single post-RL checkpoint), and (2) a working RL gain for latent reasoning in a *non-math/logic* domain (chess), where multiple independent groups had reported null results. The weight-space ΔW localization by projection type is a nice addition that gives the mechanistic claim a physical location.
- **Rigor:** High. Statistical testing uses McNemar with bootstrap CIs and Bonferroni correction; the six-condition suite is explicitly motivated by the methodological literature showing that a single corruption type can be misleading. The retention-gap analysis is reported with its CI, and the paper is honest that only Zero clears significance at n=100 while the milder conditions merely "trend the same direction." The J-lens null is made trustworthy by a calibration step showing ~94% recovery on real text.
- **Reproducibility:** Mixed. The frozen 100-position harness (seed 3407), greedy decoding, deterministic scoring, full hyperparameter list (G, LR, KL coefficient, step counts), and the explicit reward formula are all fully specified. LoRA rank and several data-construction details are stated. However, **every number comes from a single training run per checkpoint — no seed variance is reported**, a limitation the paper flags itself. Compute requirements for the 14B 4-bit base are not detailed, and the 12,002-position training dataset is not clearly released.
- **Significance:** Narrow but real. The central claim — RL's effect is on the weights, not on inference-time consultation of thoughts — is scoped tightly to "this pipeline" and the paper is careful not to generalize to latent reasoning writ large. It is one data point in an active debate (Coconut-skeptical refs 16,17 vs Coconut-positive refs 18,19,20), landing on the skeptical side.
- **Red flags / weak points:**
  - **The headline legality metric lives on an axis orthogonal to playing strength.** Accuracy is flat at 9-10% (essentially the random-legal-move floor) for *every* checkpoint including Rung-3, and a paired centipawn-loss comparison on 42 boards shows median diff 0cp, mean 19cp *favoring Stage-2*. So Rung-3 plays more legal moves but not measurably better moves — the paper is honest about this but it sharply limits practical significance.
  - **n=100 evaluation harness** with **no genuine checkmate position**, meaning the confabulation result only shows the model learns to *withhold* a false claim, never that it can correctly identify a real one. The paper states this caveat explicitly.
  - **Single-seed, single-domain, single-base-model (14B LoRA)** — no claim about full fine-tuning, frontier scale, or non-chess domains. The 61% vs 58% Table-1/Table-2 discrepancy is attributed to NF4/bf16 dequantization, which is plausible but unverified beyond assertion.
  - **The Gumbel control is inconclusive.** The straight-through Gumbel estimator is a known-biased gradient, so the 50%-vs-61% underperformance cannot cleanly separate "thought content genuinely doesn't help" from "this optimization path is structurally harder" — the paper concedes this.

## Strengths

- Pre-registered stopping rule for the latent curriculum; frozen harness fixed before any comparison.
- Six-condition causal battery run *identically* before and after RL on the same lineage — the cleanest possible isolation of RL's effect.
- Statistical honesty: Bonferroni-corrected CIs, explicit admission that only one of five conditions clears significance, and a retention-gap staircase reported with uncertainty.
- The gated-legality reward is presented as a documented fix to a real Goodhart failure (the earlier weighted-sum reward measurably worsened move quality), not as a hindsight justification.
- Calibrated J-lens null (~94% true-positive rate on text) is a quantified negative, far stronger than an uncalibrated logit-lens reading.
- Direct, quantitative engagement with the closest prior work (Switch), including the unresolved point of disagreement (their matched-noise costs 9.5 points; this paper's costs ~0).
- Confabulation eliminated as a side effect of the legality gate under two independent recipes — a robust, non-cherry-picked result.

## Limitations

- **Single training run per checkpoint; no seed variance.** The retention-gap CIs quantify sampling variance over the fixed 100 boards, not seed-to-seed variance — the paper is explicit these are not substitutes.
- **Accuracy/playing strength does not improve.** 9-10% exact-match vs Stockfish for every checkpoint; paired centipawn-loss comparison favors Stage-2 slightly. The gain is legality and hallucination-suppression only.
- **Evaluation harness has no real checkmate position**, so the model is never tested on correctly calling a true mate — only on withholding a false one.
- **Narrow scope by design:** one 14B LoRA adapter, one domain (chess), one training recipe. No claims about full fine-tuning, frontier scale, or transfer.
- **Gumbel control is a biased-gradient approximation** and cannot disambiguate "content is useless" from "this path is hard to train."
- **Base-model and SFT cross-board cosine similarities (0.874, 0.812)** may partly reflect an unfamiliar-input effect, since those checkpoints were never trained on hidden-state feedback; the Stage-2-vs-Rung-3 comparison (both 0.992) is the one the claim actually rests on.
- **The 61% (Table 1) vs 58% (Table 2 baseline) discrepancy** for Rung-3 is attributed to NF4 dequantization order under bf16 — plausible but not independently verified.

## Relevance

对关注"潜在/隐式推理（latent reasoning）+ 强化学习"与"机制可解释性"方向的研究者高度相关：它在数学/逻辑之外的新领域（国际象棋）首次报告了潜在推理+GRPO 的正向增益，并用"同一模型 RL 前后"的因果干预设计，对"连续思考向量是否真被当作推理时草稿读取"这一 Coconut 默认假设给出了有力的反例证据，直接呼应 Coconut、Ouro、Switch 等一系列工作的开放争论。对做 RLVR 奖励设计（门控 vs 加权、Goodhart 失效）和 LoRA 权重增量定位的读者也有方法论参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the "RL adds robustness, not content-reliance" finding replicate with multiple seeds, larger base models, and full fine-tuning (not just 14B LoRA)?
- Would adding genuine checkmate positions to the harness change the confabulation story — can Rung-3 ever correctly call a real mate, or only withhold a false one?
- Is the flat 9-10% accuracy ceiling breakable with a stronger pretrained chess understanding (the Hwang et al. limitation), or is it intrinsic to LoRA-scale RLVR on a general-purpose base model?
- Does an SVD of each layer's ΔW show the gate_proj concentration is low-rank (a few directions) or diffuse across the full LoRA rank — and do those directions overlap with the layers most sensitive to the Zero intervention?
- Would the before/after-RL causal battery design, applied to Switch's math setting or CODI's recurrence, reconcile the "same pattern, opposite conclusion" disagreement, or does the domain (chess vs math) genuinely flip the content-reliance verdict?
- Why does Rung-3's thought vector collapse to a near-constant board-invariant scaffold (cosine 0.99) — is this an inevitable attractor of hidden-state-as-input-embedding feedback, or an artifact of the staged-imitation curriculum that a different curriculum could avoid?
- Can the templated-confabulation signature (0.68 vs 0.27 similarity) be used as a cheap, training-free detector for hallucinated justifications in other domains?
