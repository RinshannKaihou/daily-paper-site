Key finding

Routine math saturates at 256 thinking tokens — yet on AIME, 43.5% of generations never converge, and their fate is already encoded in internal activations before the failure is observable.

Token Budget Saturation and Mechanistic Early-Detection of Reasoning Non-Convergence in Chain-of-Thought Models ↗

AUC-ROC, activation probe at layer 20 · token 150. Beats behavioral baseline at 8 / 9 checkpoints (mean ΔAUC +0.035).

Why this matters

The 256-token saturation is directly deployable for cutting inference cost on routine math — and internal-activation early-exit reframes how we detect reasoning failure before it surfaces.

Critical edge

The headline probe AUC ≈ 0.61 is barely above chance and operationally useless. The sweep-level significance test (p = 0.063) fails to clear 0.05 — so early-detection is provocative, not actionable.

Methodology

ModelDeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B
Precisionfloat16 · RTX A5000 24GB
Decodinggreedy (do_sample=False)

S1Budget forcing

A custom LogitsProcessor counts tokens within <think>…</think>; once budget B is hit it forces </think> by setting all other logits to −∞. Budgets swept: {256, 512, 1024, 2048, 4096} + "uncapped" (10k ceiling). 200 problems each from GSM8K, MATH-500, AIME 1983–2024 (seed 42). Key metric B* = smallest budget ≥95% of uncapped accuracy, CIs via 1,000-iter bootstrap.

S2Early detection via probing

Two forward hooks: hidden-state on all 28 layers @ token 150, and entropy @ 9 checkpoints (50→300 tokens). Three logistic-regression probes (activation / behavioral / combined), 5-fold stratified CV, AUC-ROC with label-flip correction. Significance via paired permutation tests (per-checkpoint B=10k; sweep-level B=100k, label flips shared across positions).

Results & highlights

  • 256tokens · B* GSM8K (acc 0.740) & MATH-500 (0.625) reach 95% of uncapped accuracy at the smallest budget — "essentially flat" beyond. Directly deployable.
  • 43.5%AIME of generations never emit </think>, burn the full 10k budget, score 11.5%. The 56.5% converged hit 96.5%. Oracle early-exit saves ~43.5% of AIME compute.
  • r² 0.19difficulty Problem difficulty explains <19% of convergence variance. On the hard tercile: converged 100% (n=18) vs non-converged 0% (n=41) — nearly co-extensive.
  • L20peak layer Signal concentrates in upper-middle layers, not output-adjacent. Peak AUC 0.608 ± 0.080 at layer 20 (21: 0.606); falls to chance at layer 0 (0.536) and 27 (0.541).
  • +0.074ΔAUC · 50 tok Activation probe beats behavioral baseline at 8/9 checkpoints; strongest early advantage at 50 tokens. Sweep-level p = 0.063 — underpowered.

Strengths  ·  Limitations

Strengths

  • Honest statistics: bootstrap CIs, permutation tests, explicit "n=200 underpowered" callout.
  • 256-token saturation is immediately actionable for routine-math inference cost.
  • Real post-cutoff contamination control (AIME 2025) + fresh-instrumented-run sanity check.
  • Mechanistic detail — signal at layer 20, not output-adjacent — guides future probe design.
  • Clean separation of "signal exists" vs "strong enough to deploy"; no overclaim.

Limitations

  • Single model only — no test of generalization across families, scales, or training.
  • Greedy decoding against vendor's temp-0.6 advice; 43.5% non-convergence may be a decoding artifact.
  • "Uncapped" is capped at 10k — some "non-converged" might converge at 20k.
  • Probe shows predictive, not causal, information (no activation patching).
  • Behavioral baseline deliberately weak; richer features might erase the gap.

Open questions

  1. Does bimodal convergence + 256-token saturation replicate across QwQ, full R1, o1-class — or is it R1-distill-greedy specific?
  2. At temp 0.6 with multiple seeds, is 43.5% non-convergence intrinsic or a decoding artifact?
  3. Can nonlinear / multi-layer probes push early-detection AUC meaningfully past 0.61?
  4. Would activation patching at layer 20 causally flip convergence — determining, not just predicting, fate?
  5. Would stronger behavioral baselines (partial-answer, plan-tracking) close the gap with the probe?
  6. At 20k–32k ceilings, do some "non-converged" terminate and does the bimodal split soften?
