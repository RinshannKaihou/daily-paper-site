> **一句话总结：** 作者提出 MisKnow-Agent 框架，构造 5,933 条可控的"看似可信但事实错误"的知识实例，注入到 DeepResearch Bench 任务中评估主流深度研究智能体；实验发现 DeerFlow/WebThinker + 三种 LLM 以及 Gemini Deep Research 都会在长程工作流中把误导性证据当作最终结论采纳，且越靠近最终综合阶段暴露越危险（默认条件下 FCAR 从冷启动的 34.5% 飙升到综合前的 85.0%），事前/事后防御只能缓解、无法根除。

> **TL;DR:** This paper introduces MisKnow-Agent, a framework that builds 5,933 quality-controlled "apparently credible but factually misleading" knowledge instances on top of DeepResearch Bench and injects them into the retrieval pools of DeerFlow, WebThinker (each × DeepSeek-V4 Pro / Qwen3.5-397B / Intern-S1-Pro), and Gemini Deep Research. Even one misleading document can be adopted as a final conclusion; the dominant factor is *lifecycle timing* — false-conclusion adoption rate (FCAR) rises from 34.5% at cold start to 85.0% immediately before final synthesis (a +50.5 pt jump), framework choice (DeerFlow 66.7% vs WebThinker 29.3% mean FCAR) shifts results more than backbone LLM choice, and combined pre-/post-research defenses only reduce (not eliminate) adoption (FCAR 60–76% → 15–62%).

## 核心贡献

- 提出 **MisKnow-Agent**：一个分三阶段（蓝图构造 → 可控实例生成 → 检索引导的跨模型精修与质量过滤）的误导性知识构造框架，在 DeepResearch Bench 上产出 5,933 条经 GLM-5/Kimi-2.6/DeepSeek-V4 Pro/Qwen3.5-397B/Intern-S1-Pro 五个搜索增强判别器一致判为 "Mis" 的高质量实例。
- 定义并量化了 **False-Conclusion Adoption Rate (FCAR)** 这一新指标，只统计最终报告把虚假结论采纳为自己的结论，而把"仅仅引用/提及/反驳"视为非采纳，更贴合内容层可靠性语义。
- 系统刻画了误导知识传播的 7 个维度：检索排序、生命周期入口点、机构权威度、来源风格、文档预算、框架设计、骨干 LLM，揭示"框架设计 > 骨干 LLM"的颠覆性结论，以及 LLM 排名会随框架翻转。
- 评估了 pre-research 验证提示、post-research 精修 Agent 及二者组合三种防御，证明它们只能缓解不能消除，且对 Intern-S1-Pro 组合防御反而劣化（62% > 57%/58% 单独）。
- 把"集中式校验能识别 vs 长程工作流仍被采纳"的对立作为关键发现：同样的检索增强判别器在聚焦校验中 100% 判 Mis，但同样的实例在工作流中仍被采纳，凸显"验证-工作流"鸿沟。

## Key Contributions

- Introduces **MisKnow-Agent**, a three-stage pipeline (blueprint construction → controlled instance generation across High/Medium/Low authority tiers and Paper/News/Blog/Post styles → search-guided cross-model refinement and quality filtering) that produces **5,933 quality-controlled misleading-knowledge instances** on DeepResearch Bench, all unanimously classified as "Mis" by GLM-5, Kimi-2.6, DeepSeek-V4 Pro, Qwen3.5-397B, and Intern-S1-Pro.
- Defines **False-Conclusion Adoption Rate (FCAR)**, counting only cases where the final report endorses the seeded false conclusion as its own conclusion — not mere retrieval, citation, mention, or refutation — to cleanly isolate content-level reliability from adversarial instruction attacks.
- Provides the first systematic factorial study of misleading-knowledge propagation across **two open-source DR frameworks (DeerFlow, WebThinker) × three backbone LLMs (DeepSeek-V4 Pro, Qwen3.5-397B, Intern-S1-Pro) + one closed-source system (Gemini Deep Research)**, varying search-rank, lifecycle entry point, authority tier, source style, document budget, framework, and backbone.
- Shows that **framework design shifts FCAR more than backbone LLM choice**, and that the LLM ordering *reverses* across frameworks (Intern-S1-Pro worst under DeerFlow, DeepSeek-V4 Pro worst under WebThinker).
- Evaluates pre-research verification prompting, post-research claim-by-claim refinement, and their combination, showing each mitigates but does not prevent adoption, with the combination actually *hurting* Intern-S1-Pro (62% combined vs 57% pre / 58% post alone).

## Methodology

**Threat model.** The authors deliberately model *ambient content-level* risk in open information environments, *not* instruction-level attacks (no prompt injection, no instruction hijacking, no parameter or system-prompt tampering). Model parameters, system prompts, tool implementation, and the base evidence environment E_q are unchanged; only the augmented environment E_q ∪ M_q exposes misleading instances. To avoid contaminating the real web, misleading documents are injected into isolated retrieval pools rather than published online (closed-source Gemini uses its local-source interface with native online search left on).

**MisKnow-Agent (three stages).**
1. *Blueprint construction*: for each task q_i define a target false conclusion c_f^i, an institution pool H_i stratified by authority level L = {High, Medium, Low} (5 institutions per tier), and an evaluation instruction ψ_i that tells the judge how to recognize adoption of c_f^i.
2. *Controlled instance generation*: a document generator G_doc produces m_{i,ℓ,s,k} = G_doc(q_i, c_f^i, h_{i,ℓ,k}, s) for each (authority ℓ, style s ∈ {Paper, News, Blog, Post}, institution k), all preserving the same canonical false conclusion while varying authority and style. DeepSeek-V4 Pro is selected as the generator after a qualitative pilot (Appendix A) against Kimi-2.6, Qwen3.5-397B, and GLM-5.
3. *Search-guided refinement + quality filtering*: each candidate is judged by J search-enabled verifiers; a candidate is retained as "Mis" only if *all* verifiers agree (Equation 4), otherwise it is iteratively refined (Equation 5) up to a budget, then manually screened. Result: 5,933 instances for 100 DeepResearch Bench tasks.

**Defenses.** (i) *Pre-research*: append a fixed, task-agnostic verification prompt p_ver to the user query, instructing the agent to treat retrieved info as unverified until corroborated, to back conclusion-critical claims with primary/independent sources, and to qualify/omit weak evidence. (ii) *Post-research*: an additional search-enabled refinement agent A_ref (same backbone) verifies and rewrites the final report claim-by-claim in the same augmented environment E_q ∪ M_q. (iii) Combined.

**Evaluation.** FCAR over valid completed reports; tool/API failures are rerun; DeepSeek-V4 Pro is the judge model. Judge–human agreement on 300 reports: 99.0% raw, Cohen's κ = 0.978, accuracy 99.0%, precision 98.46%, recall 100.0%, F1 99.22% (Appendix B). Temperature 0 throughout; default config = High authority + Paper style; Serper backend with top-k=10.

## Results & Highlights

- **Lifecycle timing dominates.** Mean FCAR is 34.5% at cold start, 34.5% at mid-research, and **85.0% immediately before final synthesis** (a +50.5 pt jump); late-stage FCAR is 77–94% in every configuration. By contrast, search-result rank (front/middle/back) varies FCAR only from 65.2% → 65.7% → 64.2% (a 1.5 pt spread, ≤6 pt in any single config).
- **A single document suffices.** Raising the misleading-document budget from 1 to 5 changes mean FCAR only from 46.2% to 48.7% (2.5 pts) with no monotonic dose–response — repeated exposure is not necessary.
- **Paper-like presentation is the most reliable persuasive cue.** Mean FCAR: Paper 48.0% > Blog 43.7% > News 37.0% > Post 29.7% — an 18.3 pt paper-to-post gap, and the highest in 5 of 6 configurations.
- **Authority matters but less.** Mean FCAR: High 48.0% > Medium 43.3% > Low 36.8% — an 11.2 pt high-to-low gap.
- **Framework > backbone LLM.** Mean FCAR across LLMs: DeerFlow 66.7% vs WebThinker 29.3%. DeerFlow beats WebThinker by 53, 34, and 25 pt for Intern-S1-Pro, Qwen3.5-397B, and DeepSeek-V4 Pro respectively — each larger than the 16 pt within-framework LLM range. LLM ordering *flips*: worst is Intern-S1-Pro under DeerFlow but DeepSeek-V4 Pro under WebThinker.
- **WebThinker's robustness is a delay, not verification.** At cold start WebThinker 15.7% vs DeerFlow 53.3%; at mid-research 19.3% vs 49.7%; but before synthesis they converge (86.7% vs 83.3%) — early robustness comes from opportunities to displace evidence, not from synthesis-stage verification.
- **Closed-source generalization (Gemini Deep Research).** FCAR by authority: 54% (High) → 46% (Med) → 8% (Low); by style: Paper 54% > Blog 41% > News 34% > Posts 28%; by budget: 27% (1 doc) → 54% (3 docs) → 50–56% (4–5). Substantial adoption persists even with native online search enabled.
- **Defenses mitigate but do not eliminate (DeerFlow, default config).** No-defense baselines 60–76%; pre-research 37–57%; post-research 20–58%; combined 15–62%. Combined is best for DeepSeek-V4 Pro and Qwen3.5-397B but yields **62% for Intern-S1-Pro — higher than either defense alone (57%, 58%)**, because post-research refinement re-retrieves misleading evidence from the same pool.
- **Verification-vs-workflow disconnect.** The same search-enabled verifiers (GLM-5, Kimi-2.6, DeepSeek-V4 Pro, Qwen3.5-397B, Intern-S1-Pro) classify **100% of retained instances as Mis** in focused corpus validation, yet those same instances are still adopted during long-horizon research.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** Genuinely fresh angle. Prior reliability work (PoisonedRAG, SafeSearch, ClashEval, Deep-Research-Brings-Deeper-Harm) focuses on short-horizon RAG, explicit adversarial corpus poisoning, or claim-level factuality auditing. This paper isolates *ambient, non-adversarial* misleading knowledge in long-horizon DR workflows and introduces the clean "adoption ≠ mention" FCAR criterion — a meaningful conceptual contribution.
- **Rigor:** Strong on experimental hygiene (temperature 0, one-factor-at-a-time variation, rerun on tool/API failure, manual report validity checks, 5-verifier unanimous gate for corpus construction). The judge–human agreement study (Appendix B) is the right thing to do and the numbers are convincing.
- **Reproducibility:** Mixed. MisKnow-Agent's construction recipe, prompts, and the DeepResearch Bench task set are well-specified, but the **5,933 generated misleading documents are not stated to be released**, and without them the FCAR numbers cannot be exactly reproduced by a third party. The pipeline itself is reproducible in principle.
- **Significance:** High. The "framework > backbone" finding and the LLM-ordering reversal are actionable for DR system designers, and the "lifecycle timing dominates" result points to a concrete design lever (intermediate-state verification) rather than yet another search-ranking hack.
- **Red flags / specific weaknesses:**
  - *Simulation vs real web.* All misleading knowledge is injected into isolated retrieval pools; the authors are upfront that this avoids web contamination, but real exposure paths (SEO, UGC) differ and the FCAR magnitudes may not transfer.
  - *Self-bias in judging.* DeepSeek-V4 Pro is simultaneously the **document generator, one of the backbone agents, and the judge model**. Although human–judge agreement is 99.0%, the overlap is a confound a reviewer would flag — a third-party judge would strengthen the claim.
  - *Only 100 tasks.* The factorial study is dense but rests on 100 DeepResearch Bench tasks; per-cell sample sizes for the lifecycle/authority/style/budget figures are not reported, so some of the small deltas (e.g., 1.5 pt rank effect) rest on thin n.
  - *Closed-source setup is asymmetric.* Gemini's misleading docs enter via its local-source interface while native online search stays on — a different exposure regime than the open-source systems, making direct FCAR comparison across the two regimes soft.
  - *Defense "combined can hurt" is under-explained.* The Intern-S1-Pro 62% combined degradation is interesting but only diagnosed in one sentence (re-retrieval in the same pool); no ablation isolates *why* combining prompts amplifies rather than damps the effect for that specific backbone.

## Strengths

- Clean, well-motivated threat model that deliberately avoids conflating "attack" with "ambient risk."
- FCAR is a *better* metric than recall/mention-rate for this problem and is operationalized carefully (judge gets task + c_f + claim identifiers + report; adoption requires endorsement).
- The factorial design (rank × timing × authority × style × budget × framework × backbone) is unusually thorough for a reliability paper.
- Honest negative result: combined defense can *hurt*, and WebThinker's apparent robustness is exposed as a delay rather than true verification.
- Strong practical takeaway: invest in intermediate-state verification, not stronger models / broader search / final-stage auditing.

## Limitations

- Misleading corpus is injected into isolated pools, not the live web; ecological validity of FCAR magnitudes is unproven.
- Generator = backbone = judge overlap (DeepSeek-V4 Pro) is a confound despite the 99% human agreement check.
- Small task base (100) and unreported per-cell sample sizes for some factorial conditions.
- Closed-source Gemini uses a different (local-source) injection path, complicating cross-system comparison.
- No release commitment for the 5,933-instance corpus, limiting exact reproducibility.
- Defense study is restricted to DeerFlow under the default High/Paper config; WebThinker and other regimes are not covered.

## Relevance

高度相关：本文直接研究 Deep Research 智能体在长程信息检索任务中的可靠性失败模式，与「智能体评估」「检索增强生成可信度」「LLM 对抗与防御」三个研究兴趣同时相交，其 MisKnow-Agent 构造范式与 FCAR 指标可作为后续做 agent benchmark、misinformation robustness 与 evidence verification 工作的直接基线。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the lifecycle-timing effect (85% FCAR before synthesis) persist when intermediate research states are themselves *re-verified* by a search-enabled critic at each Plan/Analyze/Synthesize step, rather than only pre- or post-research?
- Would a third-party judge model (e.g., GPT-5 or Claude) reproduce the 99% agreement, or does the DeepSeek-V4-Pro generator=backbone=judge overlap bias FCAR systematically in one direction?
- How do the FCAR numbers transfer from isolated-pool injection to *live-web* exposure through SEO-poisoned or UGC-injected content, where retrieval rank and authority cues are noisy rather than controlled?
- Is the "framework > backbone" finding stable across more than two frameworks (e.g., OpenAI Deep Research, Kimi Researcher, SciResearcher), or is it an artifact of DeerFlow's direct evidence-transfer pipeline vs WebThinker's compression-heavy one?
- Can the 5,933-instance corpus and blueprints be released under a responsible-disclosure license to enable community red-teaming, and does fine-tuning DR backbones on *counter-examples* (reports that refute the same c_f) improve robustness beyond the prompt-based defenses?
