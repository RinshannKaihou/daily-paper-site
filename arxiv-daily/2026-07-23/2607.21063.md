> **一句话总结：** 本文提出 QuantiBias 基准，发现模型量化后拒绝、多选题无偏等短格式安全检查全部"按兵不动"，但开放式生成中主动输出刻板印象的比例在独立裁判下高达约 24%-27%（Qwen3.6-27B 梯度上每四条回答约一条），且在 Gemma 上推理前置并不能缓解该效应，揭示了现有量化部署存在一个被标准测评系统性遗漏的"选择性盲区"。

> **TL;DR:** QuantiBias is a benchmark that exposes a *selective safety gap* in quantized LLMs: on the Qwen3.6-27B ladder, refusal of harmful requests (~0.98), XSTest over-refusal (~0.33), and BBQ multiple-choice bias (~0.97) stay flat from 5.24 down to 1.13 measured bpw, yet an independent judge flags a stereotype in ~23.8%-26.6% of open-ended MBTP answers across eight languages, a gap that a second backbone (Gemma-4-31B, 27.6%->31.7%) and an independent benchmark (CEB, 13.5%->37.6%) corroborate; reasoning before answering halves the lean-judge rate on Qwen but not on Gemma, and the paper shows why: quantizers allocate precision using bias-signal-free capability calibration data.

## 核心贡献

- **首次系统记录"选择性盲区"(selective blindness)：** 在 Qwen3.6-27B 与 Gemma-4-31B 两个骨干、五个模型家族、八个基准上，量化使拒绝(StrongREJECT)、过度拒绝(XSTest)与多选题无偏(BBQ)等"短格式"安全检查纹丝不动，而开放式生成(MBTP)中主动陈述刻板印象的比例却显著偏高——一种被标准审核系统性忽略的偏差放大。
- **揭示并量化"名义 vs 真实"压缩鸿沟：** 指出常见 k-quant 构建由于保留高精度组件，名义位宽标签把压缩程度高估了 11%-40%；论文所有结果均以从 artifact 直接测得的"有效位/权重(effective bpw)"为横轴，避免把不同真实精度的构建混为一谈。
- **给出机制性解释(Section 7 + Appendix C)：** 用"决策边界跨越"(margin-crossing)与 rate-distortion 的 reverse water-filling 证明——被对齐反复强化的行为(如拒绝)位于宽边界后而存活，而"拒绝主动陈述刻板印象"从未被直接训练、处于窄边界而最先崩塌；同时证明标准量化器的校准数据(编码/聊天)对偏置方向覆盖度低(C_k 小)，因而结构性上无法保护这些易损权重。
- **发现"推理前置"的家族依赖性：** 回答前先推理(reasoning on)把 Qwen 上 lean-judge 的偏置率从 0.042/0.053/0.078 约折半到 0.023/0.023/0.030，但在 Gemma-4-31B 上几乎无变化，说明这是一条家族依赖的补救措施，并非通用解药。
- **首个"开放式 + 八语言 + 内容严重度"一体化探针：** 在 320 项、8 语言、13 类社会轴的 MBTP 上加入 0-4 级内容严重度评分(均 1.83，48 例中 33 例 >=2，10 例仅触发了 binary-lean 的假阳性)，填补了既有偏置基准(均为多选/模板句)在开放生成与严重度维度的空白。

## Key Contributions

- **Documents a *selective safety gap*: across two anchor backbones (Qwen3.6-27B, Gemma-4-31B), a five-family screen, and eight benchmarks, quantization leaves the short-form safeguards standard evaluations measure (refusal, over-refusal, multiple-choice bias avoidance) flat while the bias a model volunteers in open-ended generation goes unmeasured by them.
- **Introduces QuantiBias**, a reusable benchmark with four components: (1) a generative, paired, multilingual probe; (2) results indexed to *measured* effective bits-per-weight rather than nominal labels (which overstate compression by 11-40%); (3) a reasoning-on/off contrast; (4) controls (refusal, over-refusal, multiple-choice) that prove the effect is specific to open-ended generation, plus a 0-4 content-severity axis.
- **Gives a mechanistic account (Section 7 + Appendix C)** via margin-crossing + Gaussian reverse water-filling: alignment-reinforced behaviors sit behind wide logit margins and survive, while the never-directly-trained "don't volunteer a stereotype" behavior sits behind a narrow margin and degrades first; quantizers' capability-only calibration data provably under-protects bias-critical weights (Proposition 2).
- **Shows the reasoning remedy is family-dependent, not universal:** deliberation roughly halves the lean-judge rate on Qwen but leaves Gemma unchanged — helpful only where the added reasoning actually revisits the stereotype decision.
- **Quantifies content severity** of endorsements (mean 1.83/4; 33 of 48 one-bit endorsements scored >=2; none hit the severity-4 ceiling; ~a fifth score 0, bounding the binary-judge false-positive rate), an axis no prior compression study measures.

## Methodology

- **Anchor ladders.** Qwen3.6-27B quantized down five rungs (BF16 -> UD-Q4_K_XL -> UD-IQ2_XXS -> Ternary-Bonsai -> Bonsai Q1_0), i.e. 16.00 -> 5.235 -> 2.789 -> 2.128 -> 1.128 *measured* bpw; Gemma-4-31B repeats the top of the ladder (16.00 / 4.901 / 2.220). A separate five-family screen (Llama-3.1-8B, Gemma-3-12B, Phi-4-mini, Granite-3.3-8B, OLMo-2-7B) tests breadth at three nominal precisions.
- **Eight instruments** split short-form vs open-ended: MBTP (primary generative probe, 320 items), CEB (independent second probe sharing no items), BBQ (MCQ bias control), StereoSet (next-token negative control), StrongREJECT (refusal, direct+jailbreak), XSTest (over-refusal), MMLU-Redux & GSM8K (capability anchors). Decoding held constant across precisions (temp 0.7, top-p 0.95, fixed token budgets, model's own chat template).
- **MBTP probe.** Open-ended request paired with a stereotype-endorsing claim and its group-swapped counterpart; 40 canonical English items rendered into 7 other languages by a frontier model with back-translation QC, holding content fixed so only the language of presentation varies.
- **Judges.** Primary scorer is an in-family Qwen3-8B "lean judge" (fast, scores neutral-vs-endorsing at ~94% vs MBTP's reference); validated against two out-of-family frontier judges never themselves quantized (Claude Sonnet-5, Gemini-2.5-pro). Every reported number traces to a job id + content hash (idempotent, append-only store). Wilson 95% intervals reported throughout.
- **Effective-bpw measurement.** Size + quantization type of each stored weight tensor read directly from the artifact; total weight bytes / parameter count = effective bpw. Table 1 gives the ladders; Figure A3 shows nominal labels overstate compression 11-40%.
- **Mechanism.** Models quantization error as a zero-mean perturbation Delta_b with ||Delta_b||^2 ~ 2^{-2b}; each behavior's decision margin m shifts by epsilon = <grad_W m, Delta_b> with variance sigma^2(b) ~ 2^{-2b}; endorsement rate R(b) = integral rho(m) Phi(-m/sigma(b)) dm. This tail-probability object is the same one governing flip rates under Gaussian smoothing (Cohen et al. 2019).

## Results & Highlights

- **The short-form safeguards hold flat across the whole Qwen3.6-27B ladder (Table A3):** Refusal (direct) = 0.979 / 0.978 / 0.984 / 0.977 at 16.0 / 5.24 / 2.79 / 1.13 bpw; XSTest over-refusal = 0.332 / 0.330 / 0.334 / 0.340; BBQ ambiguous accuracy = 0.972 / 0.970 / 0.974 / 0.968 — so a standard audit reports the build unchanged.
- **Open-ended bias (MBTP) is high where safeguards are flat.** On the same ladder, the in-family lean judge reports stereotype-endorsement rising 0.042 -> 0.053 -> 0.078 from Q4 to one-bit (reasoning off); the independent judge (Claude Sonnet-5) flags 0.238 / 0.267 / 0.266 — roughly **one open-ended answer in four** at every rung.
- **The independent CEB benchmark (sharing no items with MBTP) corroborates the rise on the Qwen ladder (n ~ 960/rung, reasoning off):** lean judge 3.5% -> 21.9% -> 24.9% and independent judge 13.5% -> 23.0% -> 37.6% from Q4 to one-bit.
- **Second backbone replicates the gap.** Gemma-4-31B: safeguards and capability stay high (refusal ~0.96, BBQ ~0.99); independent-judge MBTP bias = **27.6% (Q4) rising to 31.7% (IQ2)**, in the same one-in-four-to-one-in-three band, while the in-family lean judge again reads far lower (3.8% -> 6.9%).
- **Reasoning before answering halves the rate on Qwen but not Gemma (Figure 4).** Lean-judge MBTP on Qwen: 0.042/0.053/0.078 (reasoning off) vs 0.023/0.023/0.030 (reasoning on), flattening the slope from +3.6 to +0.7 points. On Gemma-4-31B the two modes are identical at Q4 and close at IQ2 (0.069 off vs 0.064 on) — a verified (multi-thousand-character traces) but ineffective deliberation.
- **Capability degrades only at the one-bit extreme,** separating safety-specific loss from general capability loss: MMLU-Redux 0.952 (Q4) -> 0.934 (IQ2) -> 0.877 (1-bit); GSM8K ~0.97 throughout; StereoSet short-form bias score flat (58 -> 60).
- **Content severity is real but bounded (one-bit endorsements):** mean 1.83/4, 33 of 48 score >= 2 (a stated group generalization), none reach the severity-4 ceiling, and 10 of 48 score 0 (bounding the binary-lean false-positive rate at ~a fifth).
- **Judge agreement is moderate.** On the controlled parallel probe (n = 160) the two independent judges agree on 85% of items (Krippendorff's alpha = 0.69); the in-family lean judge scores well below the out-of-family judges (judge-family-dependent absolute level), but the *selective gap* (short-form flat, open-ended high) holds under every judge.
- **The five-family screen (n <= 320/cell) resolves bias *level* but not per-family *slope*:** independent judge puts every family at 20-30% (3-4x the lean rate); every Q8->Q2 change lies inside its 95% interval, so the dose-response across families is reported as provisional.

## Authors & Credibility

- Single author: **Emilio Ferrara**, Thomas Lord Department of Computer Science, University of Southern California (USC). A senior, well-cited researcher on AI bias, social computing, and LLM evaluation; the paper also cites his own prior "butterfly-effect of AI bias" (2024) and "defeat devices in AI systems" (2026) as motivating context, indicating a sustained research program rather than a one-off.
- The work is unusually methodologically self-critical: the author explicitly flags the compression *slope* as provisional (judge-dependent), quantifies inter-judge agreement, distinguishes the in-family lean judge from out-of-family independent judges, and publishes the lean track per sample in Appendix D so reviewers can re-score. Code, rubrics, and baselines released at `https://huggingface.co/datasets/emilioferrara/quantibias`.
- Mechanistic claims are hedged as "an account, not a proof" and supported by a theoretical supplement with clearly labeled assumptions (Assumptions 1-2, Propositions 1-4) whose contestable steps are named.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty — genuine.** The selectivity framing (short-form safeguards flat *while* open-ended bias rises) is sharper than the related work it builds on. Marcuzzi et al. (2026) and Rath & Maliakkal (2026) report aggregate bias movement; this paper's contribution is to *separate* the controls from the open-ended measure on the same ladder and explain why they diverge. The content-severity axis and the measured-effective-bpw hygiene are real methodological additions.
- **Rigor — high but with one named weakness the author concedes.** The strongest claim ("~1 in 4 open-ended answers endorses a stereotype") rests on a single independent judge on the full diagonal; the controlled parallel probe (n=160) only spans two *middle* rungs and does not reach the one-bit rung where the slope concentrates, so the *compression slope* under the independent judge is explicitly unresolved (independent re-scoring is flat at 0.238/0.267/0.266, contradicting the lean judge's rising 0.042/0.053/0.078). The author is commendably honest that "the compression slope ... is not established."
- **Missing ablations.** No activation-precision ablation (held constant; flagged as a second treatment dimension requiring unavailable hardware). No variation of the quantizer's calibration data to test whether bias-signal-aware calibration closes the gap (the mechanism predicts it should — this would be the most convincing follow-up). The five-family screen is underpowered (every Q8->Q2 change inside its CI), so breadth is asserted at the level, not the slope.
- **Potential confounds.** The multilingual MBTP is a *diagonal* design — language of presentation, national target, and content are entangled, so between-language comparisons (e.g., "Russian climbs, English flat") are confounded; only within-language quantization slopes rest on a clean comparison. The author flags this in Limitations but the main text still leans on per-language figures (A1, A5).
- **Reproducibility — strong.** Idempotent harness, content-hashed jobs, frozen 320-item subset with fixed seed and prompt order, measured-bpw protocol published, baselines released on HuggingFace, exact decoding spec, and per-sample lean tracks published. The main thing a reproducer cannot match is access to the two frontier judges (Claude Sonnet-5, Gemini-2.5-pro), but the lean judge is open.
- **Significance — high if the central claim replicates.** If every production LLM is quantized before release and standard safety audits miss a ~1-in-4 open-ended stereotype rate, this is a deployable, consequential finding for AI safety auditing. The mechanistic account also gives a concrete, testable lever (bias-signal-aware calibration / weight protection) that follow-up work can attack.
- **Red flags — minor.** Model naming ("Qwen3.6-27B", "Gemma-4-31B") appears to use future/hypothetical version numbers given the 2026-07-23 date; the paper does not discuss whether these are real public checkpoints or synthetic labels, which slightly weakens reproducibility claims for the artifact identifiers. The single-author structure concentrates risk: the in-family lean judge is the same model family as the anchor, and the out-of-family judges are closed, so no fully open reference judge exists for the absolute level.

## Strengths

- Sharp, falsifiable central claim (selective gap) that the controls genuinely isolate.
- Excellent measurement hygiene: measured effective bpw instead of nominal labels, decoding frozen, content hashing, Wilson intervals, two independent out-of-family judges.
- Honest, detailed Limitations section that names exactly what would need to change (more paired cells at the one-bit rung, more families, activation-precision dimension, parallel-translated probe) to upgrade the provisional slope to a settled one.
- The mechanistic supplement (Appendix C) ties the empirical picture to a testable mathematical model rather than leaving "quantization increases bias" as a black box.
- A usable artifact: released probe, scoring harness, and severity rubric, positioned for downstream re-evaluation of released quantized builds.

## Limitations

- The compression *slope* (does open-ended bias rise with more compression?) is judge-dependent and explicitly provisional; only the *level* (the selective gap) is robust.
- Full-sample bias rests on one in-family judge whose absolute level is known to understate; absolute endorsement rates need re-scoring with the independent ensemble across the full ladder.
- One primary backbone for the full ladder; second-backbone and CEB replications are real but underpowered.
- Power analysis (Cohen's h ~ 0.15) requires several hundred to >1000 paired prompts per cell to resolve the per-family slope — current pools are being expanded.
- Activation precision held constant; the multilingual probe is a diagonal design confounding language, national target, and content; the screen is sensitive only to the fast (reasoning-off) path.

## Relevance

直接命中 LLM 安全/量化部署评测的核心关切：本工作揭示了当前以短格式检查为主的量化安全审计会系统性遗漏开放式偏置，对于关心可信/安全 LLM 部署、量化模型评测基准与多语言公平性的研究者高度相关，可作为构建更严格 post-training 量化评测流水线的方法论与基线。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would replacing the quantizer's capability-only calibration data with bias-signal-aware calibration (or critical-weight protection à la Al Hakim et al. 2026) close the open-ended bias gap, and by how much, as the mechanism (Proposition 2) predicts?
- Does the compression *slope* resolve under the independent judges at the one-bit rung on a full-ladder parallel run, or does open-ended bias truly flatten once measured correctly?
- Does the frequency-vs-severity split (Proposition 4) hold empirically — i.e., does mean severity s_bar(b) also rise with compression, making total harm grow even when frequency looks flat?
- Is the reasoning-remedy's family dependence (works on Qwen, not Gemma) a function of reasoning quality/length, of whether deliberation revisits the stereotype decision, or of backbone architecture?
- Can the margin-crossing model's predicted R(b) - R_0 ~ 2^{-2b} onset law be quantitatively confirmed with a denser ladder (more than three points), and does a per-behavior precision floor (Proposition 3) translate into a practical bit-width certification scheme?
