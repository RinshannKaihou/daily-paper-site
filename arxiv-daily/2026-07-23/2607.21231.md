> **一句话总结：** 本文提出 progressive cramming（逐 token 增长目标前缀、强制 100% 重建的压缩方法），在 PG19 上让 Llama-3.1-8B 的贪心重建准确率从 full cramming 的 ~40% 提升到 100%，并通过注意力消除实验证明：cramming 高容量并非语义编码，而是通过早期层对模型的脆弱"转向"，导致 HellaSwag/ARC/MMLU 下游能力显著下降甚至崩溃。

> **TL;DR:** The authors introduce **progressive cramming**, which grows the reconstruction target one token at a time and demands 100% reconstruction, exposing that prior "full cramming" (99% teacher-forced accuracy) catastrophically collapses under autoregressive greedy decoding (Llama-3.1-8B: 99.96% TF but only 40.44% greedy on PG19, vs. progressive's 100%/100%). Optimization trajectories live in a low-dimensional subspace (30–100 PCA components explain 99% of variance in 2048–4096-dim spaces), but attention-knockout causal analysis shows the embedding's downstream damage (HellaSwag -21 pts, 5-shot MMLU full-prefix → 0% accuracy) is driven by its early-layer interactions, not the late-layer attention concentration (which is a symptom). Capacity scales with reconstructor depth and width jointly, but perfect reconstruction via brittle steering encodes nothing transferable.

## 核心贡献

- **提出 progressive cramming 方法**：逐 token 扩展目标前缀并 warm-start 优化，直到无法在固定预算内完美重建，从而把"压缩是否成功"从一个模糊的 99% 阈值变成清晰的 0/1 边界，并保证自回归贪心解码下 100% 重建。
- **揭示 full cramming 的脆弱性**：证明先前工作报告的 ~1% 残差并非良性，而是集中在序列前 1-2 个 token（Llama-3.1-8B 位置 0/1 错配率 100/100），一旦早期出错就会触发自回归雪崩，使贪心准确率坍塌到 ~0%。
- **发现优化轨迹是低维的**：跨 Pythia / Llama 3 / SmolLM2 / Gemma 3 四个模型族，30–100 个 PCA 主成分即可解释 2048–4096 维嵌入空间中逐样本轨迹 99% 的方差；但这种低维性是 warm-start 优化路径的性质，而非解集本身（独立优化的等价解彼此近乎正交）。
- **因果定位下游能力崩溃的来源**：用 attention knockout 三种协议证明，下游退化由压缩嵌入在**早期层**的交互驱动；承载最多注意力质量的**晚期层**反而因果影响很小——注意力集中是症状而非原因。
- **证明容量受限于冻结的重构器架构**：截断预训练模型到前 N 层（再用短微调修复），压缩容量随保留深度和宽度单调上升并可部分互相替代（8 层 Qwen3-8B 的 625 tokens 超过完整 36 层 Qwen3-4B 的 512）。
- **公开代码与 progressive-cramming 优化轨迹数据集**。

## Key Contributions

- **Progressive cramming protocol**: replaces fixed token budgets + 99% accuracy thresholds with a token-by-token growing target that stops only when perfect reconstruction is unachievable within budget, yielding a sharp success/failure boundary and guaranteeing 100% greedy-decoding accuracy.
- **Diagnosis of full-cramming brittleness**: the residual ~1% error is concentrated at the first 1–2 token positions (Llama-3.1-8B: 100/100 mismatch at indices 0/1), which under greedy decoding triggers irreversible autoregressive collapse to ~0% accuracy.
- **Low-dimensional trajectory finding**: across four model families, 30–100 PCA components explain 99% of per-sample trajectory variance in 2048–4096-dim embedding spaces; this is a property of the warm-started optimization path, not of the (wide, near-orthogonal) solution set.
- **Causal localization via attention knockout**: per-layer, forward-cumulative, and reverse-cumulative knockout on Llama-3.1-8B (replicated on Pythia-1.4B and SmolLM2-1.7B) show downstream collapse is driven by the embedding's **early-layer** interactions; the **late-layer** attention concentration (20–60% of mass) has negligible causal effect and is a symptom, not the cause.
- **Capacity bounded by reconstructor architecture**: truncating models to their first N layers shows cramming capacity rises monotonically with both retained depth and model width, with partial substitution between the two axes.
- **Released code and progressive-cramming trajectory dataset** (https://github.com/FusionBrainLab/progressive_cramming).

## Methodology

**Problem setup (Background, §3).** Given a frozen autoregressive LM M and a target token sequence x = (x1,…,xn), cramming optimizes a single embedding e ∈ R^d to minimize cross-entropy over the sequence: e* = argmin L_cram(e; x, M), where L_cram = -Σ log pM(xi | e, x1,…,xi-1). The prior "full cramming" protocol (Kuratov et al., 2025) used a fixed token budget and reported 99% teacher-forcing accuracy.

**Progressive token addition (§4.1).** Rather than optimizing for a fixed length, the method (i) initializes with x(1) = (x1) and optimizes e(1) until perfect reconstruction; (ii) extends to x(k) = (x1,…,xk), warm-starting e(k) ← e(k-1); (iii) repeats until perfect reconstruction is no longer achievable within the per-stage optimization budget (default 1,000 steps/new token; up to 10,000 total steps). This produces a trajectory {e(k)} and a well-defined maximum compressed length.

**Low-dimensional projection (§4.2).** Motivated by the observed low-rank trajectories, optimization is optionally constrained to an affine subspace e = Wz + b with z ∈ R^k, k ≪ d (e.g., k=256 for Llama-3.1-8B, k=32 for Gemma-3-4b), with (W,b,z) jointly optimized per sample. A shared-projection variant trains a single (W,b) across a corpus with only per-sample z free, to probe whether the low-rank subspace is corpus-universal or sample-specific.

**Information gain metric (§4.3).** Following Kuratov et al. (2025), capacity is measured as CH = H_LM − H_comp+LM (reduction in total cross-entropy on the target sequence in bits when conditioning on the compression embedding), which is more stable across datasets than raw token count.

**Causal attention knockout (§7).** To separate correlation from causation, the authors mask the pre-softmax attention logits targeting the compression embedding (position 0) at selected layers, completely removing its influence on the residual stream while leaving all other computation intact. Three protocols on Llama-3.1-8B (L=32 layers): per-layer knockout at single layer l; forward cumulative (mask layers 0..k); reverse cumulative (mask layers k..L-1). Each is evaluated on both teacher-forced reconstruction accuracy and downstream HellaSwag accuracy.

**Depth/width capacity probe (§8).** Pretrained models are truncated to their first N ∈ {1,2,4,8} decoder layers, the cut is repaired with a short causal-LM finetune on fineweb-edu, and progressive cramming is run on each truncated checkpoint across five families (1.7–8B params, 24–36 layers) to separate the depth axis (within a family) from the width axis (across families at matched truncated depth).

**Evaluation.** PG19 (50 samples, primary) with replication on Fanfics; downstream on HellaSwag and ARC-Easy (4-way multiple choice, chance=25%) under two conditions (original prefix; compression embedding + original prefix), plus 5-shot MMLU (512 samples) for generative evaluation. Four model families: Pythia, Llama 3, SmolLM2, Gemma 3.

## Results & Highlights

- **Progressive vs. full cramming on PG19 (Table 2, Llama-3.1-8B):** Full cramming achieves 1568 compressed tokens at 99.96% teacher-forcing accuracy but only **40.44% greedy-decoding accuracy**; progressive cramming reaches 1438±380 tokens at **100.00% TF and 100.00% greedy** — trading ~8% fewer tokens for a guarantee of usable autoregressive reconstruction. Pythia-1.4b shows the same pattern (512 tokens, 99.71% TF, 44.19% greedy → 430±65 tokens, 100%/100%).
- **Reconstruction brittleness (Table 1):** Despite 99.0–99.1% teacher-forcing convergence, greedy accuracy collapses to 0.2–0.4% across Llama-3.2-1B/3B and Llama-3.1-8B, with mismatch rates of **86–100% at token index 0 and 100% at index 1** — the residual ~1% error is concentrated at the earliest positions and triggers autoregressive divergence.
- **Low-dimensional projection boosts capacity (Table 3):** Constraining Llama-3.1-8B to dim=256 raises compressed tokens from 1437.6 → 1697.2 and information gain from 4391 → 4814 bits; SmolLM2-1.7B dim=256 jumps from 335.1 → 957.4 tokens and 1208 → 3271 bits; Gemma-3-4b dim=32 goes from 213.6 → 697.4 tokens. PCA-99% (trajectory dimensionality) drops correspondingly (Llama 82.78 → 58.58).
- **Sample-specific (not universal) low-rank subspace (§5.2):** A shared (W,b) trained on 50 PG19 samples reaches 742±177 tokens on SmolLM2-1.7B, but **freezing it and transferring to unseen samples collapses capacity to 8.7±6.8 tokens and 36±29 bits** — the low-rank subspace is sample-specific, not a universal model property.
- **Downstream multiple-choice degradation (Table 4, converged subset only):** HellaSwag accuracy drops Llama-3.1-8B 61.91% → 40.80% (-21.1 pts), SmolLM2-1.7B 53.56% → 37.01%, pythia-1.4b 44.00% → 37.63%; ARC-Easy drops Llama-3.1-8B 63.54% → 43.31%. Progressive and full cramming perform comparably here. Gemma-3-4b fails to converge (0% conv. on HellaSwag progressive).
- **Generative collapse on 5-shot MMLU (Table 26):** Compressing the full prefix drives Llama-3.1-8B from 63.22% → **0.00% accuracy with 0% valid parseable answers**; SmolLM2-1.7B 50.98% → 0.00%; gemma-3-4b 58.13% → 0.00%. A random control embedding leaves accuracy nearly intact (Llama 62.71%, -0.51 pts), confirming the degradation is specifically caused by the optimized compression embedding.
- **Causal localization (§7, Figures 6–7):** Forward cumulative knockout restores HellaSwag accuracy to the uncompressed baseline (0.598) after masking only the first few early layers while destroying reconstruction; reverse knockout leaves accuracy near the crammed level (0.395) until masking finally reaches the early layers. The late layers carrying 20–60% attention mass have negligible causal effect on either metric.
- **Capacity scales with depth and width (Table 5):** Qwen3-8B cramming capacity roughly doubles per layer-doubling (119 → 180 → 304 → 625 tokens at 1/2/4/8 retained layers; full model 774); at matched truncated depth the larger model always crams more (Qwen3-8B > Qwen3-4B > SmolLM3-3B). The 8-layer Qwen3-8B (625 tokens) exceeds the full 36-layer Qwen3-4B (512).
- **Trajectory dimensionality grows sublinearly (Figure 4):** For Llama-3.1-8B, PCA-99% components grow approximately logarithmically with prefix length and are largely insensitive to learning rate.

## Authors & Credibility

The paper is authored by Dmitrii Tarasov, Timofei Lashukov, Elizaveta Goncharova, and Andrey Kuznetsov, affiliated with FusionBrain Lab (Moscow), HSE University (Moscow), and the Artificial Intelligence Institute at Innopolis University — groups with a track record in mechanistic and compression-oriented LLM analysis. The work is a direct, methodologically careful follow-up to Kuratov et al. (2025) "Cramming 1568 tokens into a single vector," and is **accepted at ICML 2026 (PMLR 306, Seoul)**. Credibility is strengthened by: (i) replication of every main finding on a second dataset (Fanfics, Appendix D.5); (ii) cross-family evaluation on four model families; (iii) honest reporting of negative/anomalous results (Gemma-3-4b's failure to converge); (iv) public release of code and optimization trajectories. Total compute is disclosed (~2800 GPU-hours on A100 80GB).

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty (high):** The progressive-cramming protocol is a genuinely useful methodological refinement that converts a fuzzy 99% threshold into a binary success/failure boundary, and the attention-knockout causal analysis is the paper's strongest conceptual contribution — it cleanly dissociates attention mass from causal importance and reframes cramming as "brittle steering" rather than semantic encoding. This is a non-trivial mechanistic insight.
- **Rigor (strong):** Multiple ablations (tokens-per-stage Δ, fixed-prefix, cross-entropy temperature, BOS-removal, recovery finetune), replication on Fanfics, and three knockout protocols with forward/reverse asymmetry. The authors are unusually careful to separate properties of the optimization path from properties of the solution set (noting that independently optimized solutions are near-orthogonal).
- **Reproducibility (strong):** Frozen LMs only, public checkpoints (Pythia, Llama 3, SmolLM2, Gemma 3, Qwen3), disclosed hyperparameters (AdamW, LR sweep {0.01,0.1,0.5,1.0}, 10k steps), released code and trajectory data. The 50-sample PG19 eval set is small but std is reported throughout.
- **Significance (moderate-high):** The central claim — "perfect reconstruction is insufficient for meaningful compression" — is important and well-supported, and directly tempers the strong capacity claims of the original cramming paper. The depth/width capacity result is a useful design hint for compressible reconstructors.
- **Red flags / weaknesses:**
  - **Missing baseline comparison on downstream tasks.** The paper distinguishes cramming from semantic compression methods (Activation Beacon, AutoCompressors, ICAE) only conceptually; it never measures those baselines on HellaSwag/ARC/MMLU, so the implicit claim that cramming is uniquely brittle is not benchmarked against alternatives that actually preserve semantics.
  - **Anomalous Gemma-3-4b behavior is not resolved.** Progressive cramming achieves 0% convergence on HellaSwag (Table 4) and Gemma shows consistently lower information gain; the authors attribute this to logit softcapping but explicitly state they "do not isolate softcapping from other architectural and training-time differences, and leave confirming the mechanism to future work" — a real gap given Gemma is one of four headline families.
  - **Progressive underperforms full on raw token count.** On Llama-3.1-8B, progressive reaches 1438 tokens vs. full's 1568 — an honest trade (budget for accuracy guarantee), but it means progressive is not strictly dominant on the headline capacity metric.
  - **Causal analysis is deepest on a single 32-layer model.** Knockout is fully detailed on Llama-3.1-8B and only summarized as "consistent" on Pythia-1.4B and SmolLM2-1.7B; the forward/reverse asymmetry curves are not shown for the smaller models.
  - **Downstream eval is narrow.** Only HellaSwag, ARC-Easy, and 5-shot MMLU; no long-context, reasoning (e.g., GSM8K), or instruction-following benchmarks, despite the paper's own call for "broader capability benchmarks."
  - **Free-generation LLM-as-judge study (Appendix D.6) uses only 16 samples with binary criteria** — acknowledged by the authors as a "qualitative complement rather than a benchmark," but it is the only open-ended generation evidence.
  - **Cost not adequately addressed as a limitation of the method itself:** progressive cramming is ~2× slower than full cramming and resists batching (each sample converges at its own rate); the geometric/back-off variants recover some speed at an 18–28% token-count shortfall.

## Strengths

- Converts a brittle, threshold-dependent evaluation (99% TF) into a principled binary boundary, exposing a failure mode (early-token autoregressive collapse) that prior work missed.
- The attention-knockout causal analysis is rigorous and delivers a clean, counterintuitive result: high attention mass ≠ causal importance; the late-layer attention sink is a symptom, and the early-layer interactions are the cause.
- Honest about the limits of its own central finding — explicitly states the low-dimensional trajectory is a path property, not a solution-set property, and that perfect reconstruction does not imply transferable semantics.
- Broad, well-controlled experimental coverage: four model families, two datasets, multiple ablations, shared vs. sample-specific projection, depth/width truncation.
- Public code and trajectory release; accepted at ICML 2026.

## Limitations

- **No comparison to semantic-compression baselines** (Activation Beacon, ICAE, AutoCompressors) on the same downstream benchmarks, so cramming's relative brittleness is not quantified against alternatives.
- **Gemma-3-4b anomaly unresolved** — the softcapping hypothesis is stated but not isolated.
- **Progressive cramming is ~2× slower and resists batching**; adaptive schedules recover speed but at an 18–28% capacity shortfall.
- **Narrow downstream evaluation** — only multiple-choice (HellaSwag, ARC) + 5-shot MMLU; no long-context, math/reasoning, or instruction-following tasks.
- **Causal knockout fully detailed on one model only** (Llama-3.1-8B); smaller-model replications are summarized, not shown.
- **Small sample for the only open-ended generation study** (16 samples, binary criteria, single judge setup — though cross-validated with a second judge).
- **Central open problem unsolved:** the authors explicitly state they "do not yet know how to make a single embedding both perfectly reconstructable and semantically faithful."
- **50-sample PG19 evaluation set** is modest; variance is reported but the absolute capacity numbers carry wide std (e.g., Llama-3.1-8B 1438±380).

## Relevance

与本研究兴趣（上下文压缩、长上下文 LLM、机械可解释性）高度相关：progressive cramming 既是一种可复现的"极限压缩"探针，又通过 attention knockout 揭示了 transformer 如何在嵌入空间中存储与使用信息，其"完美重建≠可迁移语义"的结论对设计真正可用的学习型上下文压缩方法具有直接的警示与方法论价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Can cramming embeddings be regularized (e.g., via activation alignment or a semantics-preserving auxiliary loss) to remain perfectly reconstructable **and** semantically faithful, or is there a fundamental trade-off between exact reconstruction and downstream utility?
- How do semantic-compression baselines (Activation Beacon, ICAE, AutoCompressors) perform on the same HellaSwag/ARC/MMLU protocol — is cramming uniquely brittle, or do all embedding-based methods show early-layer steering pathologies?
- Does the early-layer causal locus generalize to much larger models (e.g., 70B+) and to non-Llama architectures (Mistral, Qwen at full depth), where the attention-sink dynamics may differ?
- Can a fast, single-forward-pass compressor (amortized over a corpus) approximate the per-sample progressive optimum, and at what cost in reconstruction fidelity and downstream accuracy?
- Is the depth/width substitutability result actionable for designing reconstructors specifically optimized for compressibility (e.g., wider-but-shallower variants), and does it transfer to encoder-decoder or MoE architectures?
- What is the role of logit softcapping (suspected in Gemma 3) in limiting cramming capacity, and can it be mitigated without harming base LM quality?
