AREX: Towards a Recursively Self-Improving Agent for Deep Research

Shuqi Lu · Chaofan Li · Kun Luo · Zheng Liu · Zhongyuan Wang · Qiwei Ye · Zhicheng Dou · Di He + 16 authors Beijing Academy of AI
Posted 2026-07-23 · 24 authors · arXiv:2607.21461
Key finding

AREX reframes deep research as recursive self-improvement — an outer loop audits confidence-scored answers and restarts targeted search — driving a 10B-active MoE to 82.5 on BrowseComp.

01 Key metric
82.5
BrowseComp · AREX-Base (122B / 10B-active MoE)

Best reported WideSearch-en: 82.0, ahead of Kimi-K2.6 (80.8), DeepSeek-V4-Pro (78.0) and GPT-5.4 (77.5). ACU + outer loop together add +22.9 BrowseComp points over the bare inner loop.

02 Why this matters

A 10B-active MoE beats a 397B dense Qwen — and frontier closed systems — on five deep-research benchmarks.

The recipe — recursive self-improvement, an autonomous update_context tool, and key-step-focused RL — is clean and transferable to other long-horizon agents.

03 Critical edge

All ablations are BrowseComp-only. Whether the +22.9-point ACU/outer-loop gain replicates on GAIA, HLE or DeepSearchQA is unknown.

And MiroThinker-H1 actually beats AREX-Base on both flagship benchmarks — BrowseComp 88.2 vs 82.5, GAIA 88.5 vs 85.4. "Dominant" overstates it.

Methodology

Bi-level · RSI

Inner loop · research

Maintains an interaction trajectory of analysis → action → observation under a research policy πθ. When the objective is investigated, a structured finish call externalizes a provisional answer, supporting evidence, and a confidence score s ∈ [0,100].

Autonomous Context Update (ACU). The model itself decides when to invoke update_context, collapsing the trajectory into a compact improvement state — verified findings, source IDs, rejected candidates, unresolved constraints, next-step plan — discarding redundant observations. Used in 80.3% of episodes at ~25K tokens, far below the 128K cap.

Outer loop · self-improvement

If s ≥ τ the answer is Accepted. Otherwise a trajectory assessment flags recoverability, preserves useful info, lists remaining issues, and emits a sharper next objective — Refine (recoverable) or Restart (not). Verification becomes the transition function between rounds, not a post-hoc filter.

Training. Progressive multi-round capability training (browse-heavy then reasoning-heavy), key-step focused supervision (loss applied only to evidence-discovery / redirection / context-update steps, which are 19–29% harder), and step-aware GRPO with hierarchical normalization plus bounded key-step reward shaping.

Strengths & limitations

Side by side

Strengths what holds

  • Clean theoretical framing. Discovery–verification asymmetry motivates every component; nothing is decorative.
  • ACU is a real advance. Self-timed, objective-aligned state compression replaces fixed-token heuristics — empirically a research operation, not a memory crutch.
  • Key-step supervision works. −8.4 when ablated, justified by step-level loss (annotated steps 19–29% harder).
  • Strong efficiency story. 10B-active MoE competitive with 397B dense and frontier closed systems.
  • Artifacts released — HuggingFace models, GlobeApp, homepage.

Limitations what's thin

  • BrowseComp-only ablations. Component gains on GAIA, HLE, DeepSearchQA, xbench are entirely unknown.
  • Load-bearing hyperparameters undisclosed — τ, max outer rounds, λ_key, dataset sizes, training compute.
  • Not dominant. MiroThinker-H1 beats AREX-Base on both flagship benchmarks (BrowseComp 88.2, GAIA 88.5).
  • Calibration gap. Even with ACU, 33.0% of incorrect outputs still carry confidence 90–100 — unanalysed.
  • No cost analysis. 300 inner turns × 5 outer rounds is a large inference budget; no latency/$ comparison vs single-pass baselines.

Open questions

For follow-up
1Do the ACU and outer-loop gains replicate on GAIA, HLE, DeepSearchQA, or is +22.9 BrowseComp-specific? A per-benchmark ablation is the most important missing evidence.
2How sensitive is performance to τ, λ_key, and key-step detector precision? Is there a regime where a miscalibrated τ causes over-Restart?
3Given 33% of wrong answers still score 90–100, what would a calibration-aware gate (ensemble finish calls, external verifier) buy?
4How does inference cost (tokens, latency, $/query) compare against single-pass Qwen3.5-397B or Kimi-K2.6?
