> **一句话总结：** 作者针对经典的 BB1（长步 Barzilai–Borwein）方法，证明了在任意有限维 n ≥ 4 的严格凸二次问题上，存在一个正 Lebesgue 测度的开集（同时允许谱和初始点扰动），使得 BB1 在该开集上收敛但根超线性收敛失败（梯度范数因子下界 10⁻⁶、上界 0.61），其构造基于对 4 维投影化 BB 动力学中一个非共振、吸引的 7-周期轨道的计算机辅助证明。

> **TL;DR:** This paper resolves a long-standing open question about the Barzilai–Borwein (BB1) method by proving that, for every finite dimension n ≥ 4, there is a nonempty open, positive-Lebesgue-measure family of strictly convex quadratic problems (with spectra in (4.99, 8.01)) and admissible initial points for which BB1 converges but provably fails superlinear convergence — gradient, energy-norm, and objective gap all obey two-sided geometric estimates with per-step factor bounded in [10⁻⁶, 0.61] (lower bound is ρ=10⁻⁶, upper bound is ρ̄=0.61, vs. the Li & Sun 2021 lower-rate example that lived on a Lebesgue-null two-eigenspace set). The result is certified by interval-arithmetic verification of a nonresonant, attracting period-7 orbit of the projectivized BB1 dynamics in dimension 4, embedded into all higher dimensions by a transverse Floquet bound with τ(ν) < 0.049.

## 核心贡献

- **负向回答 BB1 的"几乎处处超线性"猜想**：对于每个有限维 n ≥ 4，构造出正测度开集 Ωₙ ⊂ S₊₊ⁿ × ℝⁿ，使其上的 BB1 轨道收敛到 x∗ = A⁻¹b 但根超线性（及 Q-超线性）收敛均被排除，对应 lim inf ‖gₖ‖¹ᵏ ≥ 10⁻⁶ > 0。
- **鲁棒性 / 全维持续性**：与 Li & Sun (2021) 仅支撑在两端特征子空间（零测集）的下界示例不同，本文下界在谱与初始点的同步扰动下持续存在；通过 transverse Floquet 估计 τ(ν) < 0.049 将 4 维构造嵌入到任意 n > 4，所有附加分量保持非零且受几何控制。
- **动力学机制**：首次将 BB1 的"慢收敛"归因于一个稳定且吸引的 7-周期递归吸引子（而非坐标子空间不变性），并给出从 admissible-initialization 流形出发的 15 步 certified connection。
- **计算机辅助证明的严谨化**：用 directed interval arithmetic + Newton-like 算子 H 在 10⁻⁵⁵ 半径的有理盒上以 Banach 不动点定理给出精确根 u∗ 的存在/唯一性（‖DH‖∞ ≤ 7.2×10⁻²⁷），并同时验证非共振、Lyapunov 对 (P, Q₇) ≻ 0 与 transverse 乘子界——所有不等式为严格实数不等式而非浮点模拟。
- **明确范围与诚实限定**：定理是存在性结果（非普适速率公式），仅针对 BB1（非 BB2），开集 Aₙ 是单一显式构造谱的小邻域；明确声明 10⁻⁶ 与 0.61 是保守的安全裕度而非最优渐近因子，最小瞬态分离 ≈ 1.39×10⁻⁴。

## Key Contributions

- **Negative resolution of the generic-superlinearity question for BB1**: For every finite dimension n ≥ 4 and every linear term b ∈ ℝⁿ, the authors construct a nonempty open set Aₙ ⊂ S₊₊ⁿ of simple-spectrum matrices and, for each A ∈ Aₙ, an open set Xₙ(A) of admissible initial points such that the joint set Ωₙ has positive Lebesgue measure and BB1 fails root- and Q-superlinear convergence there.
- **Sharp two-sided geometric estimates**: For every i and k, the spectral components satisfy ρᵏ‖Πᵢ(A)g₀‖ ≤ ‖Πᵢ(A)gₖ‖ ≤ ρ̄ᵏ‖Πᵢ(A)g₀‖ with ρ = 10⁻⁶ and ρ̄ = 0.61; summing gives the same estimates for ‖gₖ‖ and the energy norm ‖eₖ‖_A, with squared rates for the objective gap f(xₖ) − f(x∗).
- **A dynamical-systems explanation**: The slow behavior is generated by a nonresonant, attracting period-7 orbit of the projectivized 4-D BB1 map, reached from the admissible-initialization manifold by a certified 15-step connection; a spectral translation λ ↦ λ + 41 makes every radial multiplier contractive.
- **Computer-assisted certification done rigorously**: Existence, nonresonance, separation, and attraction are all certified inside explicit rational boxes via directed interval arithmetic and a discrete Lyapunov inequality (P − A₇ᵀPA₇ ≻ 0 ⇒ ‖A₇‖_P < 1), giving an open basin rather than a zero-measure exceptional point.
- **Lift to every finite dimension**: A uniform transverse Floquet bound τ(ν) < 0.049 < 1 embeds the 4-D cycle into all n > 4 while keeping four spectral modes active — the normalized spectral measure approaches a 7-periodic measure rather than collapsing to the two endpoint modes of exact steepest descent (Akaike 1959; Forsythe 1968).

## Methodology

The proof is not a generic-trajectory estimate; it constructs one rigorously certified orbit of a low-dimensional dynamical system and then uses stability + continuity to enlarge it into an open family. The pipeline has five stages:

1. **Exact reduction (§4)**. On a strictly convex quadratic f_{A,b}(x) = ½xᵀAx − bᵀx, BB1 acts on each spectral component via the exact multiplier d^{k+1}_i = (aₖ − λᵢ)/aₖ · d^k_i, where aₖ = α_{k−1} is the delayed Rayleigh quotient. All conclusions of Theorem 2.1 reduce to a single uniform one-step inequality 10⁻⁶ ≤ |aₖ − λᵢ|/aₖ ≤ 0.61 for every i and every k ≥ 0. Removing the gradient scale yields a rational projective map F_λ on ℝ × Δₙ₋₁ with an initialization map D_λ.
2. **Certified periodic object (§5, dimension 4)**. Unknowns are a free eigenvalue λ̄₃, a projective state z, and an admissible initial weight r, satisfying 8 rational equations: F⁷_λ̄(z) = z (a seven-cycle) plus F¹⁵_λ̄(D_λ̄(r)) = z (an exact fifteen-step landing). A Newton-like operator H(u) = u − RG(u) with exact rational preconditioner R is evaluated in directed interval arithmetic on a box X of radius 10⁻⁵⁵; the certified inclusions H(X) ⊂ int X and sup‖DH‖∞ < 7.2×10⁻²⁷ yield, via Banach's theorem, a unique exact root u∗ (Lemma 5.2). The same certificate (C1–C7) proves positivity of all normalization denominators (Z_t > 0.1516 on the cycle, > 0.00095 on the connection), spectral separation (min |aₖ − λ̄ᵢ| > 1.3887×10⁻⁴ over the connection, > 7.569×10⁻² on the cycle), positive-definiteness of a Lyapunov pair (P, Q₇), and a transverse multiplier bound 0.023 < τ(ν̄) < 0.0482.
3. **Attraction (§6)**. The certified inequality P − A₇ᵀPA₇ ≻ 0 gives ‖A₇‖_P < 1, so the seven-cycle is Schur-stable and possesses an open basin (Lemma 6.3).
4. **Rates and openness in dimension 4 (§7)**. A translation conjugacy λ ↦ λ + 41 (Lemma 7.1) moves the spectrum into (4.99, 8.01) with width < 3.02, immediately yielding the upper factor 3.02/4.99 < 0.61; the certified separation yields the lower factor 10⁻⁵/8.01 > 10⁻⁶. Hyperbolicity (1 ∉ σ(A₇)) plus the implicit-function theorem continue the cycle to nearby spectra, and finite-time continuity of the fifteen-step map carries nearby admissible initializations into the trapping tube, turning one trajectory into an open product L₄ × P₄ (Proposition 7.2).
5. **Every dimension and lift to problem data (§8–§9)**. For n > 4, the extra eigenvalues are placed in (7.49, 7.51) with zero initial weight, so the 4-D cycle survives on an invariant simplex face; the block-upper-triangular structure plus τ(ν) < 0.049 attract in the new directions as well (Proposition 8.1). Three changes of variables (orthant polar map, continuity of projector weights p(A,g) on simple-spectrum matrices, and the diffeomorphism (A,x) ↦ (A, Ax−b)) finally lift the projective family to the open, positive-measure set Ωₙ of problems and initial points.

The constants ρ = 10⁻⁶ and ρ̄ = 0.61 are conservative safety margins: the proof actually uses a minimum transient separation of ≈ 1.39×10⁻⁴ and an upper multiplier bound close to 0.6 at the central translated spectrum; the observed asymptotic per-step factor along the certified orbit is β^{1/7} ≈ 0.144 where β ≈ 1.29×10⁻⁶.

## Results & Highlights

- **Two-sided geometric estimate on every spectral component (Theorem 2.1, eq. 6)**: For every n ≥ 4 and every A ∈ Aₙ, x₀ ∈ Xₙ(A), and every i ∈ {1,…,n}, k ≥ 0:
  10⁻⁶ᵏ‖Πᵢ(A)g₀‖ ≤ ‖Πᵢ(A)gₖ‖ ≤ 0.61ᵏ‖Πᵢ(A)g₀‖.
  The constants ρ = 10⁻⁶ (lower) and ρ̄ = 0.61 (upper) rule out superlinear convergence: lim inf_{k→∞} ‖gₖ‖^{1/k} ≥ 10⁻⁶ > 0, and likewise for ‖eₖ‖_A.
- **Objective gap decays with squared rates (eq. 10)**: ρ²ᵏ(f(x₀) − f(x∗)) ≤ f(xₖ) − f(x∗) ≤ ρ̄²ᵏ(f(x₀) − f(x∗)) — i.e., the function value's lower factor is (10⁻⁶)² = 10⁻¹² and the upper factor is 0.61² ≈ 0.3721 per step.
- **Positive-measure open family**: The joint set Ωₙ = {(A, x₀) : A ∈ Aₙ, x₀ ∈ Xₙ(A)} is nonempty and open in S₊₊ⁿ × ℝⁿ and therefore has positive Lebesgue measure, and every fiber Xₙ(A) has positive Lebesgue measure in ℝⁿ — directly strengthening the Li & Sun (2021) lower-rate example, which was supported only on the two extreme eigenspaces (a Lebesgue-null initial set for n ≥ 2) with exact factor (κ−1)/(κ+1).
- **Dimension-4 transition explained**: The construction identifies a nonresonant attracting 7-cycle of the projectivized BB1 dynamics; the certified transient minimum separation is ≈ 1.39×10⁻⁴ (at k = 3) and the cycle minimum is 7.57×10⁻² (eq. 40–41); the fifteen-step connection lands admissible initializations on the cycle exactly.
- **Computer-assisted certificate rigor**: The interval-Newton/Banach step achieves sup_X ‖DH‖∞ ≤ 7.200691×10⁻²⁷ on a box of radius 10⁻⁵⁵; normalization denominators are bounded below by 0.1516 (cycle) and 9.51×10⁻⁴ (connection); the Lyapunov pair has LDLᵀ pivots ≥ 9.78×10⁵ and Q₇ has row diagonal-dominance margins ≥ 0.9999999999999999994. All are rigorous real inequalities obtained by directed rounding, not floating-point simulations.
- **Higher-dimensional lift**: Transverse Floquet multiplier satisfies 0.023 < τ(ν̄) < 0.0482 < 1 for every ν̄ ∈ [3.49, 3.51] (eq. 45), so the boundary cycle attracts in the added eigendirections; the rate constants are uniform in n (the neighborhood sizes are not).

No quantitative results reported in the typical benchmark-table sense — this is a theoretical paper; the numbers above are the rigorous constants of the certified orbit and the resulting rate bounds, which play the role of "experimental evidence" for the theorem.

## Authors & Credibility

- **Dawei Li, Xiaotian Jiang, Mingyi Hong** — all at the University of Minnesota (Twin Cities). Mingyi Hong is a well-established researcher in optimization (federated learning, nonconvex optimization, saddle-point methods) with a long track record of rigorous rate analyses; this provides strong credibility for the analytic portions of the proof.
- The authors disclose candidly that the *proof sketch* of the main result was obtained by **GPT 5.6 Sol** (given an equivalent component-wise quotient formulation and asked to construct a lower-bound example; prompt in Appendix B), with human verification and polishing afterwards. This is an unusually transparent AI-assistance disclosure and is to be commended; the *certification itself* (interval arithmetic, Banach theorem, Lyapunov arguments) is independent of how the construction was found.
- The work fits a clear lineage: Barzilai–Borwein (1988), Raydan (1993), Dai & Liao (2002), Dai & Fletcher (2005a) who *observed numerically* a superlinear-to-linear transition at some n ≥ 4, and Li & Sun (2021) who gave the most recent R-linear upper factor 1 − 1/κ and a zero-measure lower-rate example. This paper directly closes the open question posed by Li & Sun about generic initialization.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty (high)**. This is the first *positive-measure* lower-bound result for BB1 in any dimension n ≥ 4, and the first to identify a *dynamical mechanism* (a stable attracting 7-cycle of the projectivized map) rather than relying on coordinate-subspace invariance. It sharpens and structurally explains the purely numerical/asymptotic observation of Dai & Fletcher (2005a) about an n ≥ 4 transition, and strengthens the Li & Sun (2021) lower-rate example by removing its zero-measure caveat. The use of computer-assisted proof (interval Newton + Banach + Lyapunov) in optimization rate theory is methodologically interesting and increasingly accepted.
- **Rigor (very high, with a caveat)**. The pen-and-paper portions (§6–§9) are conventional and checkable; the computer-assisted Certificate 5.1 is the load-bearing step. The authors are admirably explicit about the directed-rounding validation principle (Krawczyk 1969; Rump 2010) and provide all constants needed to reproduce the certificate (box radius 10⁻⁵⁵, ‖DH‖∞ ≤ 7.2×10⁻²⁷, LDLᵀ pivots, diagonal-dominance margins). The *caveat* is that the certificate depends on a specific script/precision (200-digit Decimal arithmetic mentioned in Figure 1) that, as far as the body shows, is not released alongside the paper; reproducibility of the interval-arithmetic step therefore hinges on a reader re-implementing the script from the equations and constants. A reviewer would want the verification code or a short proof of correctness for the auto-diff/interval engine.
- **Reproducibility (medium)**. The analytic lift to higher dimensions is fully self-contained, but the dimension-4 certificate is essentially a black-box interval computation. The appendix (B) discloses the GPT prompt used to find the construction, which helps partially with reproducibility of the *discovery* but not of the *verification*. No mention of code release, Zenodo archive, or a Lean/Coq formalization.
- **Significance (high for the subfield)**. Resolves a specific, explicitly posed open question (Li & Sun 2021; Dai & Fletcher 2005a) and clarifies the long-debated "is BB superlinear almost everywhere?" question with a definitive *no* in n ≥ 4. Likely to become a reference point for future work on BB variants, on BB2, and on safeguarded/adaptive spectral methods. Less impactful outside the optimization-theory community — the constructed spectra (clustered near (4.99, 8.01)) are very special, and practitioners using safeguarded BB in machine-learning pipelines are not directly affected.
- **Red flags / overclaiming (none significant)**. The "Scope and limitations" section (§10) is exceptionally honest: the authors explicitly state (i) it is BB1, not BB2; (ii) the conclusion holds on an open family, not for almost every spectrum; (iii) the computer-assisted step certifies existence inside a *tiny* rational box and the decimal λ∗₃ is a locator, not a closed form; (iv) the constants are safety margins, not optimal factors. The only mild overstatement risk is the title's "for Every Dimension n ≥ 4" — strictly, the *existence* of such an open neighborhood is asserted for each n, but the neighborhood *size* is not uniform in n (acknowledged in the introduction). The AI-assistance disclosure is handled with integrity, not hidden.

## Strengths

- First robust (positive-measure) lower-rate result for BB1 in all dimensions n ≥ 4, closing an explicitly stated open question.
- Provides a *mechanism* (attracting period-7 projective cycle + 15-step admissible landing), not just an existence count — this is conceptually more illuminating than prior null-set examples.
- Computer-assisted certificate is rigorously framed (directed interval arithmetic, Banach fixed-point, discrete Lyapunov inequality); all constants are published.
- Exceptionally honest scope/limitations section and AI-assistance disclosure; no overclaiming about universality or optimality of the rate constants.
- Clean proof architecture (Figure 2) separating the certified numeric core from the analytic lift.

## Limitations

- The result concerns **BB1 only**, with the specific admissible initialization (4) (inverse gradient Rayleigh quotient α₀ = g₀ᵀg₀ / g₀ᵀAg₀); it does not cover BB2, alternate/cyclic BB, or safeguarded variants that practitioners actually use.
- The open set Aₙ is a *small neighborhood of one explicitly constructed spectrum*; the theorem does not say how large the bad set is, only that it has positive measure. The neighborhood size is not uniform in n.
- The dimension-4 certificate depends on a 200-digit interval-arithmetic computation whose code/script does not appear to be released; full independent verification would require reimplementation.
- The constants ρ = 10⁻⁶ and ρ̄ = 0.61 are extremely conservative safety margins (observed asymptotic factor β^{1/7} ≈ 0.144); they certify the *qualitative* conclusion (no superlinearity) but are not tight rate estimates.
- The constructed spectra are clustered (e.g., three separated eigenvalues plus a cluster near the top for n > 4); the paper does not address whether bad positive-measure sets also exist for well-separated or random spectra.
- No discussion of whether the same mechanism extends to BB2 or to the *short* step — leaving the analogous question for BB2 still open.

## Relevance

与用户关注的优化算法收敛性理论高度相关：本文正面解决了一个具体的开放问题（BB1 在 n ≥ 4 是否几乎处处超线性收敛），并对所关心的"步长规则导致的非线性动力学行为"给出了首个正测度鲁棒下界与动力学机制解释，可服务于谱梯度类方法（含 BB 步长的分布式/随机变体）的速率理论分析。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the same attracting-periodic-orbit mechanism exist for **BB2** (the short step), or can BB2 be shown to be superlinear almost everywhere in some dimension? The paper leaves BB2 open.
- Can the interval-arithmetic certificate be **formalized** (e.g., in Lean or Coq) or the verification script released, so the dimension-4 step is independently machine-checkable?
- How large is the bad open set Aₙ quantitatively (volume / spectral-width estimate), and does it shrink, stay constant, or grow with n?
- Does the bad set persist for **safeguarded or adaptive BB variants** (Burdakov et al. 2019; Zhou et al. 2026), or do their globalization strategies remove the attracting cycle?
- Are there analogous positive-measure lower-rate families for **nonquadratic** strongly convex objectives, where sᵀy can fail to remain positive?
- Can the seven-cycle mechanism be generalized to a family of attracting k-cycles, and is there a deeper symbolic reason that period 7 (and dimension 4) is the minimal transition?
