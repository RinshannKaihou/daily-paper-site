Why this matters

First open recipe to RL-tune real deployment harnesses (Codex / Kimi-Agent / Molmo-Web) for tool-use & GUI agents — closing the train-deploy gap.

Critical edge

Frontier gap stays 20–40 pts (OSWorld 37.7 vs. GPT-5.4 75.0). “Beats larger models” holds only vs. open baselines, and every SFT teacher & judge is proprietary.

Reproducing exact models needs paid API access — only the training infra is fully open.
01

How it works

proxy interception · remote rollout orchestration

Two obstacles, two components

Harness inference is stateful & multi-process (nested tool calls, subagents, long-horizon context) that trainers can't natively express — and rollouts need dedicated containers that can't co-locate on training nodes.

Proxy + Orchestrator

A proxy server wraps the inference server, intercepts every generation request, routes it to the RL engines, and records (prompt, response) pairs + terminal reward rT. A Kubernetes orchestrator (on Orchard/Azure) elastically spins up rollout pods.

Trajectory & credit

Reconstructs τ = (s₀ᴴ,a₀,r₀)…(sₜᴴ,aₜ,rₜ) with rₜ = γᵗ·rT (γ=1.0); GRPO advantage from group-mean comparison. Following DAPO, any rollout ending in env/network error is discarded — partial-credit assignment deferred.

Wall-clock, not turn caps

Codex exposes no turn cap, and caps are inconsistent across harnesses. So each rollout gets a generous wall-clock timeout; on timeout the job dies, returns an error signal, and training keeps collecting from survivors.

Harness difficulty (ClawEval pass@1)

Simple harnesses learn far better: ZeroClaw 48.5, ReACT* 45.1, Codex 32.5 — but OpenClaw only 20.9 despite far longer prompts. Complexity hurts learnability.

Cross-harness transfer

Multi-harness training is best: lifting Codex to 32.5 (+20.3 over base). Single-harness training still transfers to unseen harnesses (+3.3 to +4.6).

03

Strengths & limitations

side by side

Strengths PRO

  • Genuinely general: one interface for text tool-use, MCP, browser & full desktop — both 30B-A3B LLM and 8B VLM backbones.
  • Ends train-deploy mismatch by training inside the real harness, not a reimplemented loop — prerequisite for the behavioral analysis.
  • Data-efficient: GUI gains from ~2.5k tasks vs. MolmoWeb's 200k+; claw gains from only 343 RL tasks.
  • Harness-difficulty & cross-harness studies (Tables 4–5) are the most insightful part — only possible via harness-agnostic design.
  • Honest reporting: admits WebVoyager loss, admits error recovery stays weak, scopes claims to open baselines.

Limitations CON

  • Frontier gap large on every benchmark (OSWorld 37.7 vs. GPT-5.4 75.0) — narrows, doesn't close it.
  • Error recovery weakest learned capability even after RL; needs dedicated data/methods.
  • Proprietary teachers & judges (MiniMax-M2.5, Kimi-K2.5, GPT-4.1/4o, o4-mini, Claude Opus 4.6) undermine the “open” story.
  • High compute bar: 8×B200 + Azure containers — out of reach for most academic groups.
  • No design ablations (timeout, error-discard, γ); partial-rollout credit assignment deferred.
  • MCPAtlas rests on 89 tasks; Online-Mind2Web delta vs. a borrowed, possibly differently-judged MolmoWeb number.
04

Open questions

for further exploration
Q1 · CREDIT

Would partial-rollout credit assignment (vs. DAPO discard-all-on-error) change sample efficiency — and specifically the learning of error-recovery behavior?

Q2 · GENERALIZATION

Does proxy-interception generalize to non-deterministic harnesses, or those that cache/fuse model calls (speculative subagents) where prompt-response pairing is ambiguous?

Q3 · ERROR RECOVERY

Can targeted synthetic failure-and-recover trajectories close the recovery weakness — and does that transfer across harnesses?

Q4 · DATA PIPELINE

What is each stage's marginal value — is the test-and-refine loop doing the work, or is proposal-pool quality (ClawHub, AgentNet) dominant?

Q5 · THE GAP

With closed models leading by 20–40 pts, is the open-vs-closed gap in harness agents primarily a base-model, data, or harness-engineering gap?

Q6 · ROBUSTNESS

How stable are browser results given dependence on stealth-browser anti-blocking — does performance degrade on sites that successfully detect the agent?
