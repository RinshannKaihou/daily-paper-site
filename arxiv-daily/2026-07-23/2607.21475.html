Error Certificates for KV-Cache Eviction via Randomized Design

Peng Xie Technical University of Munich 2026-07-23 ~70 GPU-hours · single H100/H200

Key finding

Deterministic KV-cache evictors are provably blind to their own error; randomizing the tail with Poisson sampling restores identifiability and yields a one-line online certificate — buying attribution, not prediction.

Key metric · from results

0.749

AUC separating eviction-induced failures from inherently hard ones (6k suite).

vs 0.542 for output log-probability · n=7,924 failures

Why this matters

Gives KV-cache evictors what the entire score-race literature lacks — a way to know their own error — unlocking trustworthy long-context inference and agent-memory recomputation scheduling.

relevance → long-context LLM · agent memory · KV mgmt

Critical edge

△

Coverage is measured, not proven — the anytime-valid extension is only a sketch, and the surviving attribution result is untested on genuine multi-turn agent benchmarks, resting on a single-turn-after-compression approximation.

scope: ≤16k tokens · ≤8B params · one replay (12,096 cells)
01

Methodology

A theory + design fix + per-step certificate, with a fully pre-registered empirical harness. No training, no new matrices — one extra scalar per retained tail token.

Theorem 1 — Impossibility (silent failure)

For any value-blind deterministic evictor, perturbing evicted values by c·u leaves every online statistic bit-identical while shifting the true attention error arbitrarily. Hence no Ft-measurable estimator is consistent. Realized constructively: six value permutations move top-k's true error 0.014 → 0.898 (×64) across worlds every online signal sees as identical.

Theorem 2 — Randomized fix

Certainty set (sinks + recent + score-top, π=1) + Poisson tail at known inclusion probs. A single logit offset +log(1/πᵢ) makes the softmax denominator perform the Hájek correction exactly. Sen–Yates–Grundy variance collapses to a single sum V̂ₜ, O(|tail|) per step.

Certificate

Empirical-Bernstein radius per step (variance + range, δ=0.1), averaged over every 4th head of every layer, maximized over the first 6 decode steps. The deployed guarantee rests on measured coverage, not a theorem.

Harness & pre-registration

4 models (Qwen2.5-1.5B/7B, Llama-3.1-8B, Mistral-7B); 6k & 16k suites; ~74,000 generations + 12,096 replay cells. Seven falsifiable claims committed before any shard ran (timestamped); 3 honestly killed.

02

Results & verdicts

survived · coverage
Certificate covers realized error in 96.9 / 97.2 / 97.7% of cells at 12.5 / 25 / 50% budgets (pass line 0.85); Spearman 0.943 / 0.965 / 0.979. Median rel. error at 25%: 0.0317 Poisson+Hájek vs 0.0447 top-k vs 0.2386 uniform.
survived · silent-failure panel
Every deterministic self-signal sits at chance — retained-attention entropy 0.43–0.51, keep-boundary margin 0.49–0.50; the certificate reaches 0.77–0.86 on the same yardstick (≥10 points ahead).
survived · attribution
Restricted to failures, the certificate separates eviction-induced from inherent failures at AUC 0.749 (6k) / 0.727 (16k); output log-probability scores 0.542 / 0.469 (at or below chance).
survived · recomputation
Under streaming compression, certificate-gated recomputation at 12.5% budget recovers accuracy 0.33 vs 0.29 random vs 0.28–0.29 confidence (per-seed advantage over random +3.7 pts, 95% CI excludes 0).
killed · R1 prediction
Certificate's task-failure prediction AUC 0.555 (6k) / 0.572 (16k) — both below the pre-registered 0.6 kill line. Cause: question-aware eviction at 25–50% budgets is nearly free (HotpotQA full vs Poisson 0.515 vs 0.516); failures are task-difficulty-driven, invisible to the cache.
conceded · R3 to log-prob
Mean output log-probability beats every cache-side signal on prediction (induced failure 0.782 vs 0.778 at 6k). Selective answering should gate on output confidence — the baseline the KV literature does not report.
03

Strengths & limitations

Strengths 5

  • Short, constructive, load-bearing impossibility theorem that reframes what any KV-eviction method can ever know about its own damage.
  • One-line Hájek-by-logit-offset fix — no training, no new matrices; engineering barrier to adoption is minimal.
  • Pre-registration with kill conditions and full disclosure of three honestly killed claims — exemplary empirical conduct.
  • Honest concession of the prediction axis to output confidence; surviving contribution reframed as attribution, not prediction.
  • Deployable product: certificate-gated recomputation beats random & confidence gating at matched compute.

Limitations 6

  • Contexts reach only 16k tokens and models only 8B params; damage-onset curve could shift at scale (6k→16k moved it down).
  • Streaming condition approximates multi-turn memory with single-turn tasks; genuine agent benchmarks not run.
  • Certificate concerns per-layer attention error; task-level meaning is empirical, not a bound crossing LayerNorm / residual streams.
  • Anytime-valid extension (Remark 2) is a sketch, not a theorem; two named open ends (vector-to-scalar reduction, draw reuse).
  • Head/layer subsample, six-step window, normalizer floor ε₀, and fixed-size designs (rejective / conditional Poisson) unablated.
  • ~2× measured decode-time overhead in an unoptimized Python prototype vs the asymptotic O(|tail|) scalar cost.
04

Questions for further exploration

Does the certificate's attribution value (AUC 0.73–0.75) and recomputation advantage survive on genuine multi-turn agent benchmarks where history is compressed across turns?

Can the per-step certificate be upgraded from measured coverage to a true theorem by closing the two open ends of the anytime construction?

Do fixed-size sampling designs (rejective / conditional Poisson) that keep the budget exact match or beat plain Poisson?

Does merging error (CaM, MomentKV) scale with within-stratum dispersion of evicted values rather than with evicted attention mass — and could a stratified-ratio variance layer attach to deterministic merging?

At longer contexts (32k+) and larger models (70B+), does the prediction axis clear the 0.6 kill line that ≤16k / ≤8B did not?
