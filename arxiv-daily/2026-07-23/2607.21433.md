> **一句话总结：** 在 DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B 上,作者通过 budget-forcing 发现 GSM8K/MATH-500 仅需 256 思考 token 即达饱和,而 AIME 呈现 56.5%/43.5% 的双峰收敛模式(非收敛样本吞掉约 43.5% 算力却只贡献 11.5% 准确率),并训练线性探针在第 20 层(token 150)以 AUC≈0.608、在 9 个 checkpoint 中 8 个位置超越行为基线,证明收敛命运在行为失败前就已编码于内部表征中。

> **TL;DR:** Studying DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B across GSM8K, MATH-500, and AIME 1983–2024, the authors show that thinking-token budgets saturate at just 256 tokens on GSM8K and MATH-500 (reaching 95% of uncapped accuracy), while AIME exhibits a bimodal failure mode where 43.5% of generations never emit `</think>` and waste ~43.5% of inference compute for only 11.5% accuracy; linear probes on layer-20 activations (token 150) achieve AUC 0.608 ± 0.080 and beat behavioral baselines (entropy + n-gram repetition) at 8 of 9 checkpoints (mean ΔAUC = +0.035, strongest at 50 tokens with Δ = +0.074), indicating convergence fate is partially encoded internally before it becomes observable, and a contamination check on AIME 2025 (converged accuracy 100% vs non-converged 0%) rules out memorization.

## 核心贡献

- **预算饱和的实证刻画:** 用自研 token-level logits processor 实现 budget-forcing,证明 GSM8K 与 MATH-500 在仅 256 个思考 token 即达到无上限准确率的 95%,直接挑战"思考越长越好"的常见假设。
- **AIME 双峰收敛模式的系统化描述:** 揭示 43.5% 的 AIME 生成永远不会终止(`</think>` 不被触发),消耗全部 10,000 token 预算却只贡献 11.5% 准确率;而 56.5% 收敛样本达到 96.5% 准确率。该双峰结构仅由难度解释约 19%(r² ≈ 0.186)。
- **跨 28 层的层扫描:** 在 token 150 处遍历全部 28 层,定位第 20 层为最强信号层(AUC 0.608 ± 0.080),揭示收敛信息集中于中上层表征,而非靠近输出的层。
- **九位置 checkpoint 扫描:** 在 50–300 token 范围内,激活探针在 9 个位置中 8 个超越行为基线(熵 + n-gram 重复),50 token 处优势最强(ΔAUC = +0.074),证明内部表征在表层信号出现前就已携带收敛信息。
- **污染检验(反记忆化):** 在训练截止后的 AIME 2025 全部 30 题上验证,收敛组准确率 100%、非收敛组 0%,排除记忆化作为收敛-正确性关系的主因。

## Key Contributions

- **Empirical characterization of budget saturation:** A custom token-level `LogitsProcessor` implements budget-forcing (adapting Muennighoff et al. 2025's s1 mechanism) and shows that GSM8K and MATH-500 reach 95% of uncapped accuracy at the smallest tested budget of 256 tokens, directly challenging the "more thinking is always better" assumption.
- **Systematic description of bimodal convergence on AIME:** 43.5% of AIME generations never emit `</think>` and consume the full 10,000-token ceiling while achieving only 11.5% accuracy, versus 56.5% converged generations at 96.5% accuracy. Problem difficulty explains only r² ≈ 0.186 of convergence variance.
- **Full 28-layer sweep:** Probing every layer at token 150 identifies layer 20 as the strongest signal carrier (AUC 0.608 ± 0.080), revealing that convergence information concentrates in upper-middle representational layers, not output-adjacent ones.
- **Nine-position checkpoint sweep (50–300 tokens):** Activation probes outperform behavioral baselines (entropy statistics + bigram/trigram repetition) at 8 of 9 checkpoints, with the largest early advantage at 50 tokens (ΔAUC = +0.074), demonstrating that internal representations encode convergence fate before surface signals emerge.
- **Contamination check ruling out memorization:** On all 30 AIME 2025 problems (released post-training-cutoff), converged generations achieve 100% accuracy and non-converged 0%, confirming the convergence–correctness relationship is not a memorization artifact.

## Methodology

The paper runs two interconnected studies on **DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B** (float16, NVIDIA RTX A5000 24 GB, greedy decoding `do_sample=False` for reproducibility).

**Study 1 — Budget forcing.** A custom `LogitsProcessor` counts tokens within the `<think>…</think>` region; once the budget B is reached, it forces emission of `</think>` by setting all other logits to −∞. The processor initializes in thinking mode because the chat template already opens `<think>\n` in the prompt. Budgets swept: {256, 512, 1024, 2048, 4096} plus an "uncapped" condition with a practical 10,000-token ceiling. Benchmarks: 200 problems each from GSM8K, MATH-500, and AIME 1983–2024 (seed=42). The key metric is **B\***, the minimum sufficient budget defined as the smallest budget achieving ≥95% of uncapped accuracy sustained at all higher budgets, with 95% CIs via 1,000-iteration bootstrap. Answer extraction uses cascading regex (`\boxed{}` → "the answer is" → final numeric token).

**Study 2 — Early detection via probing.** All 200 AIME problems are regenerated with two forward hooks: (1) a hidden-state hook on all 28 layers at token 150, and (2) a logit hook computing entropy at nine checkpoints (50, 75, 100, 125, 150, 175, 200, 250, 300 tokens). Care is taken to fire hooks only on autoregressive steps, not prefill. Three logistic-regression probes (C=1.0, L-BFGS, 2,000 max iter) are trained on:
- **Activation probe:** the 3,584-dim hidden-state vector at a given layer/checkpoint.
- **Behavioral baseline:** seven surface features — entropy mean/max/std/slope, bigram and trigram repetition rates, raw token count.
- **Combined probe:** concatenation of activation vector and seven behavioral features.

Evaluation uses 5-fold stratified cross-validation with AUC-ROC (with `max(AUC, 1−AUC)` correction for label-flip ambiguity). Per-checkpoint significance uses paired permutation tests (B=10,000); a sweep-level permutation test (B=100,000) preserves dependence across checkpoints by flipping labels identically at all positions. A fresh instrumented run (Table 4) is compared against the original budget-forcing run to confirm qualitative consistency (bimodal split preserved, minor quantitative drift within sampling variability for n=200).

## Results & Highlights

- **Budget saturation at 256 tokens:** GSM8K reaches B\* = 256 (95% CI [256, 512], uncapped accuracy 0.740); MATH-500 reaches B\* = 256 (95% CI [256, 4096], uncapped accuracy 0.625). Accuracy is "essentially flat" across all budget levels — marginal compute beyond 256 tokens yields no measurable gain on routine math. AIME yields no finite B\* because non-convergence drags down aggregate accuracy at every budget.
- **Bimodal AIME convergence:** Out of n=200, 113 generations (56.5%) converge with 96.5% accuracy averaging ~4,100 tokens; 87 (43.5%) never emit `</think>` within the 10,000-token ceiling, achieving only 11.5% accuracy while consuming the full 10,000 tokens. An oracle early-exit on non-converging generations would save ~43.5% of AIME inference compute.
- **Difficulty explains little:** Point-biserial correlation between convergence and AIME problem number gives r = 0.431 (p < 0.0001), but r² ≈ 0.186 means difficulty explains <19% of convergence variance. Within the hard tercile, converged accuracy is 100% (n=18) while non-converged is 0% (n=41) — convergence and correctness are nearly co-extensive on hard problems.
- **Layer sweep peak at layer 20:** Across all 28 layers at token 150, AUC rises from near-chance at layer 0 (0.536), peaks at layer 20 (AUC 0.608 ± 0.080) and layer 21 (0.606 ± 0.070), then falls back toward chance at layer 27 (0.541). Convergence information concentrates in upper-middle layers.
- **Activation probes beat behavioral baselines at 8/9 checkpoints:** Mean ΔAUC = +0.035; strongest early advantage at 50 tokens (activation AUC 0.615 ± 0.066 vs behavioral 0.541 ± 0.044, Δ = +0.074). Behavioral baseline stays near chance (0.533–0.595) throughout. Sweep-level permutation test gives p = 0.063 — modest signal that n=200 is underpowered to confirm at conventional thresholds.
- **Contamination check on AIME 2025 (all 30 problems):** Convergence rate drops to 36.7% (vs 62.0% historical), but converged accuracy rises to 100% (11/11) and non-converged falls to 0% (0/19) — the convergence–correctness relationship holds, even strengthens, on data the model cannot have memorized (training cutoff July 2024; AIME 2025 released February 2025).

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** Genuine incremental contribution. Budget-forcing itself is not new (s1 / Muennighoff et al. 2025), and the R1-distill non-termination loop is already documented (Pipis et al. 2025). The novelty lies in (a) the systematic three-benchmark budget sweep with a token-level logits processor, (b) the explicit bimodal-decomposition framing, and (c) combining internal-activation probing with the convergence-prediction question. The finding that the layer-20 probe peaks in upper-middle layers (not output-adjacent) is a non-obvious mechanistic detail.
- **Rigor:** Strong on honesty. The authors correctly note that n=200 is underpowered (sweep-level p=0.063, not <0.05), that some bootstrap cells reach n=4, and that greedy decoding is explicitly advised against by DeepSeek (recommended temp 0.6). They include a fresh-instrumented-run sanity check (Table 4) and a contamination check on post-cutoff AIME 2025. However, the **headline AUC of ~0.61 is barely above chance and is operationally useless** — the paper is careful to frame this as a "feasibility foundation" rather than a deployable system, but readers should not over-read the early-detection results.
- **Reproducibility:** Mixed. Greedy decoding, fixed seed (42), and detailed probe hyperparameters help. But "uncapped" is actually capped at 10,000 tokens (a caveat the authors flag), code is not yet public ("upon acceptance"), and the central claim about activation probes rests on a permutation test that does not clear conventional significance. A direct comparison against the temperature-0.6 setting the model vendor recommends is acknowledged as missing.
- **Significance:** Practically important for inference-cost reasoning: the 256-token saturation result alone is a deployable efficiency claim for GSM8K/MATH-500-class workloads, and the ~43.5% wasted-compute figure on AIME quantifies a real inefficiency. The early-detection result is more provocative than actionable at AUC 0.61.
- **Red flags:** (1) The probe AUC (0.608) is close to chance and the statistical test (p=0.063) does not meet the 0.05 threshold — the "consistent directional advantage" framing is fair but soft. (2) Single-model, single-decoding-strategy evaluation means the bimodal 43.5% non-convergence rate may be a greedy-decoding artifact specific to R1-distill (Pipis et al. note this loop is distillation-specific). (3) "Uncapped" with a 10,000-token ceiling conflates true non-convergence with "would converge at 20k tokens." (4) Behavioral baseline is deliberately weak (entropy + n-gram repetition only); stronger behavioral features (e.g., partial-answer markers, plan-tracking heuristics) might narrow or erase the activation-probe advantage.

## Strengths

- Honest, rigorous statistical reporting with bootstrap CIs, permutation tests, and explicit acknowledgment that n=200 is underpowered.
- Clean experimental design with a fresh-instrumented-run consistency check and a genuine post-cutoff contamination control (AIME 2025).
- The 256-token saturation finding on GSM8K/MATH-500 is immediately actionable for inference-cost reduction on routine math.
- Mechanistic insight (layer 20, not output-adjacent layers, carries the signal) gives concrete guidance for future probe design.
- Clear separation between "evidence a signal exists" and "the signal is strong enough to deploy," avoiding overclaim.

## Limitations

- Single model only (DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B); no test of whether the bimodal pattern, saturation point, or layer-20 signal generalizes across model families, scales, or training procedures.
- n=200 with cross-validation folds of ~40; some cells reach n=4–18, limiting power and confidence-interval tightness.
- Greedy decoding is used against the model vendor's own recommendation (temp 0.6); the authors cannot rule out that the 43.5% non-convergence rate is a greedy-decoding artifact rather than an intrinsic model property.
- The "uncapped" condition imposes a 10,000-token ceiling — some "non-converged" generations might converge with substantially more tokens.
- The probe demonstrates *predictive* information, not *causal* determination; causality would require intervention (e.g., activation patching), which is out of scope.
- The behavioral baseline is deliberately minimal; richer behavioral features were not tested.
- The 6.6% non-converged accuracy on historical AIME likely reflects partial answer-value memorization (absent on AIME 2025), so historical baselines slightly overestimate true novel-problem capability.

## Relevance

对关注推理模型(test-time compute scaling、CoT、推理效率、可解释性)的研究者高度相关:文章既提供了可直接落地的预算裁剪结论(256 token 饱和),又提出了"用内部表征做提前退出"的机制化思路,与早退(exit-early)、探针、推理模型机制可解释性等方向形成直接对话。对部署 DeepSeek-R1 系列或类似推理模型、关心推理成本与失败模式检测的工程实践同样有参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the bimodal convergence pattern and the 256-token saturation point replicate across other reasoning models (e.g., QwQ, full DeepSeek-R1, OpenAI o1-class models), or is it specific to R1-distill under greedy decoding?
- How do the results change at temperature 0.6 (DeepSeek's recommended setting) with multiple seeds — is the 43.5% non-convergence rate an intrinsic property or a decoding artifact?
- Can nonlinear probes (MLP/attention-based) or multi-layer / sliding-window activation aggregation push the early-detection AUC meaningfully above 0.61 toward operationally useful discrimination?
- Does activation patching at layer 20 (intervention rather than observation) causally flip convergence outcomes, establishing that the representation *determines* rather than merely *predicts* convergence fate?
- Would stronger behavioral baselines (e.g., partial-answer detection, plan-completion heuristics, latent-repetition detectors beyond n-grams) close the gap with the activation probe, weakening the mechanistic-early-exit case?
- What happens at higher token ceilings (20k, 32k) — do some "non-converged" generations eventually terminate, and does the bimodal split soften?
