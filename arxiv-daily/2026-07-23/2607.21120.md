> **一句话总结：** 本文提出相对价值学习（RV），直接学习状态对之间的反对称价值差 Δ(si,sj)=V(si)−V(sj) 而非绝对状态值，构造了 γ-压缩的成对 Bellman 算子并从成对差分重建广义优势估计（R-GAE），作为 PPO 的即插即用 critic 在 49 个 Atari 游戏上达到与标准 PPO 相当的竞争力（在 61% 的游戏上超过 PPO）。

> **TL;DR:** Relative Value Learning (RV) replaces the absolute state-value critic with an antisymmetric pairwise difference function Δθ(si,sj)=V(si)−V(sj), backed by a proved γ-contraction pairwise Bellman operator and a reconstructed, unbiased Relative GAE (R-GAE); integrated as a drop-in PPO critic on the 49-game Atari/ALE benchmark, PPO+RV beats standard PPO on 30/49 games and DAE on 37/49 games while requiring only ~65 min/run on a single A100 (530 GPU-hours total for all 490 runs), confirming that only value differences — not absolute magnitudes — are needed for control.

## 核心贡献

- **成对 Bellman 算子（理论核心）：** 在有界反对称函数空间 F 上定义了成对 Bellman 算子 Tπ，证明它是 γ-压缩映射，且其唯一不动点恰好等于真实价值差 Δπ(si,sj)=Vπ(si)−Vπ(sj)，从理论上根除了绝对价值中无意义偏移（gauge 自由度）。
- **良定的相对价值目标：** 针对 1-step、n-step、λ-return 三类目标，给出了仅需可观奖励差分和非终止成对差分即可构造的良定 bootstrap 目标，特别处理了两条采样轨迹中后继状态终止/非终止的四种组合情形，避免了在 RV 框架下无法访问绝对值导致的病态目标。
- **相对广义优势估计（R-GAE）：** 证明广义优势估计可仅从成对价值差分重建（通过将每个 rollout 锚定到零并望远镜累加），并给出 R-GAE 与标准 GAE 的精确关系 Ãt=At+Bt（Bt 为轨迹常数），证明用 R-GAE 替换优势所得的策略梯度估计是无偏的（∇φJ̃=∇φJ）。
- **轨迹排序初始化：** 提出基于批次内起始状态成对差分行平均的偏移估计与排序方法，用于将每个状态的相对价值锚定到合理的 episode 级偏移上，抑制轨迹常数 Bt 带来的方差膨胀，并通过消融验证其必要性。
- **实证验证：** 将 RV 作为 PPO 的即插即用 critic，在 Atari 49 个游戏、每游戏 10 个种子的设置下，与标准 PPO 和 DAE 相比取得有竞争力的整体表现，并提供完整可复现代码。

## Key Contributions

- **Pairwise Bellman operator (theory core):** Formalizes a Bellman operator Tπ on the Banach space of bounded antisymmetric pairwise functions, and proves it is a γ-contraction whose unique fixed point equals the true value differences Δπ(si,sj)=Vπ(si)−Vπ(sj) — eliminating the behaviorally meaningless gauge/offset degree of freedom at the model level.
- **Well-posed relative value targets:** Derives 1-step, n-step and λ-return bootstrapping targets that depend only on observable reward differences (ri−rj) and non-terminal pairwise terms Δθ(·,·); handles the four terminal/non-terminal successor cases so targets are never ill-defined even though absolute values are inaccessible in the RV formulation.
- **Relative GAE (R-GAE):** Reconstructs Generalized Advantage Estimation entirely from pairwise differences by anchoring each rollout to zero and telescoping; proves the exact relationship Ãt=At+Bt to standard GAE and that R-GAE yields an unbiased policy-gradient estimator (∇φJ̃=∇φJ).
- **Trajectory-ranking initialization:** Proposes a data-dependent offset that ranks trajectories against each other via row-averaged pairwise differences of start states, anchoring each state to an episode-specific offset to reduce the variance injected by the trajectory constant Bt; an ablation confirms it is generally beneficial.
- **Empirical validation:** Integrates RV as a drop-in PPO critic on the 49-game Atari/ALE benchmark (10 seeds per game, 40M frames), reporting competitive aggregate and per-game scores versus PPO and DAE, with open-source code.

## Methodology

RV reframes value learning around the observation that, for γ<1, the absolute scale of Vπ is behaviorally irrelevant: advantages Aπ(s,a) and greedy action selection argmaxa Qπ(s,a) are invariant to additive shifts of Vπ. Only value differences matter for control, so RV makes differences the primary learning objective.

**Representation.** RV learns an antisymmetric function Δθ: S×S→R with Δθ(si,sj)=−Δθ(sj,si) and Δθ(s,s)=0 by design, targeting Δπ(si,sj)=Vπ(si)−Vπ(sj). For Atari it is implemented with the standard shared PPO CNN encoder fenc(s) and a siamese difference head with a single bias-free learned vector w, computing Δθ(si,sj)=Φ(fenc(si)−fenc(sj)); this guarantees antisymmetry and zero self-difference without target networks or stop-gradients.

**Pairwise Bellman operator.** On the Banach space F of bounded antisymmetric pairwise functions, the operator (TπΔ)(si,sj) = Δrπ(si,sj) + γ·E[Δ(s′i,s′j)] is shown (Theorem 3.1) to be a γ-contraction in the sup norm, hence by the Banach fixed-point theorem it has a unique fixed point equal to the true value differences. This is the central well-posedness result.

**Targets.** Because absolute values are unavailable, naive bootstrapping Δ(si+1,sj+1) is ill-posed when a successor is terminal. The authors rearrange all targets into observable-reward + non-terminal-pair form: the 1-step target y^(1)_ij = (ri−rj)+γδij with δij following a four-case (di,dj) branch; analogous n-step and λ-return targets are derived (full derivation in Appendix A).

**Relative GAE.** Relative values along a rollout are built by telescoping, Ṽθ(s0)=0 and Ṽθ(st)=Σ_{k<t}Δθ(s_{k+1},s_k). Relative TD residuals δ̃t=rt+γṼθ(s_{t+1})−Ṽθ(st) then yield R-GAE Ãt=Σ(γλ)^l δ̃_{t+l}. Lemma 3.2 shows Ãt=At+Bt with Bt a trajectory-constant offset depending on C=Vπ(s0), and Corollary 3.3 proves replacing advantages with R-GAE advantages leaves the policy gradient unchanged (unbiased) because the score-function expectation of the Bt term vanishes.

**Trajectory ranking.** To shrink Bt's variance contribution, start states across the batch are ranked by row-averaging the N×N matrix of pairwise differences and subtracting the batch minimum; each state's relative value becomes V̄θ(s)=V̂θ(s_start)+Δθ(s,s_start).

**Training objective.** PPO's clipped surrogate uses relative advantages Ãt; the critic is trained with an MSE on the n-step pairwise target; standard entropy bonus and shared CNN encoder are kept, and all PPO hyperparameters are left unchanged across the 49 games.

## Results & Highlights

- **PPO+RV beats standard PPO on 30 of 49 Atari games (61%) and DAE on 37 of 49 games (75%)** on final mean scores (last 100 episodes) after 40M frames, with 10 independent seeds per game, demonstrating RV is competitive as a drop-in critic rather than strictly dominant.
- **Large per-game wins over PPO:** PPO+RV substantially exceeds PPO on several games — e.g. RoadRunner 43,346.3±6,741.1 vs PPO 25,076.0±10,851.2; VideoPinball 138,564.8±77,425.7 vs 37,389.0±15,876.6; UpNDown 113,991.7±21,370.6 vs 95,445.0±21,584.1; Gravitar 1,441.0±561.4 vs 737.2±150.9; Robotank 19.5±3.1 vs 5.5±0.7.
- **Notable per-game losses versus PPO:** PPO+RV underperforms on Centipede (1,226.1±104.5 vs PPO 4,386.4±165.6), Zaxxon (845.8±1,567.9 vs 5,008.7±1,261.3), Tennis (−31.7±3.4 vs −14.8±3.8), DoubleDunk (−23.3±5.3 vs −14.9±1.3) and Pong (16.8±3.1 vs 20.7±0.2), indicating RV is not uniformly better and the siamese critic can struggle on some games.
- **Aggregate metrics (Figure 3):** Under the Agarwal et al. (2021) evaluation protocol with 95% stratified bootstrap CIs, PPO+RV achieves higher median, interquartile mean (IQM) and mean human-normalized score, and a lower optimality gap, than both PPO and DAE (PPO+RV sits to the right/higher of PPO and DAE on median/IQM/mean and to the left/lower on optimality gap).
- **Compute footprint:** Each 40M-frame run completes in ~65 minutes on a single A100 GPU with 12 CPU cores; the full 49 games × 10 seeds = 490 runs cost ~530 GPU-hours (≈22.1 A100-days), an unusually modest and reproducible budget for an Atari-wide study.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** The framing is principled and somewhat fresh: making value *differences* the primitive representation with an explicit pairwise Bellman operator, rather than treating differences as a derived quantity. However, the authors themselves note the idea echoes Bertsekas's differential training of rollout policies (1997) and relative value iteration in average-reward MDPs (Abounadi et al., 2001; Puterman, 2014); the contribution is less a wholly new idea than a clean Bellman-consistent, on-policy instantiation with an unbiasedness proof.
- **Rigor:** Theory is solid — the γ-contraction proof is short and correct, and the unbiasedness of R-GAE is properly shown. Empirically, 10 seeds and the Agarwal et al. aggregate-metrics protocol are above the bar for Atari work. A specific honest touch: the authors flag that the alternative non-antisymmetric two-argument function Δγ(s′,s)=γV(s′)−V(s) that would give *pointwise* equality with GAE actually performs *worse* in practice than R-GAE — a candid negative result.
- **Weak/missing ablations:** The pairwise critic head is essentially a single bias-free linear projection Φ (a learned vector w); the paper says a non-linear antisymmetric MLP head "so far" showed no improvement but does not provide that ablation table. There is no ablation on n (the n-step target horizon) or on the subsampling ratio used to tame the O(B²) pairwise cost, both of which are load-bearing design choices. The trajectory-ranking initialization ablation (Figure 4) is present but only qualitative (per-game curves), without a single aggregate number quantifying the gain from offset vs zero initialization.
- **Baseline strength:** The baselines are PPO and DAE (Pan et al., 2022) only — there is no comparison to dueling architectures, distributional critics, or stronger/more modern Atari PPO variants (e.g., with the CleanRL/SB3 tuned hyperparameters or IMPALA-style setups). DAE itself is shown to be quite weak on this setup (e.g., Enduro 0.0, Boxing 25.9), so "beats DAE on 37/49" overstates the bar; the more meaningful comparison is the mixed PPO result (win on 30, lose on 19).
- **Generalization claims vs evidence:** The motivation heavily invokes preference-based / human-in-the-loop RL and reward shaping as natural homes for relative values, yet **no preference-based or reward-shaping experiment is run** — all evaluation is PPO on discrete-action Atari. The conclusion's framing of RV as a building block for "algorithms that reason natively in the relative domain" is forward-looking rather than demonstrated.
- **Data quirk:** Trajectory ranking assumes post-baseline values are on a comparable scale across trajectories and that the ranking signal remains informative; the authors concede this can break down, but provide no diagnostic or failure-case analysis for when it does.
- **Red flags:** None severe. No cherry-picking of headline numbers beyond the standard "competitive" framing; per-game losses (Centipede, Zaxxon, Tennis) are reported honestly. The 530 GPU-hour budget is modest and reproducible.

## Strengths

- Theoretically clean: a genuine γ-contraction result and a crisp proof that R-GAE is an unbiased policy-gradient estimator, clarifying the exact (Bt) relationship to standard GAE.
- Principled motivation: the gauge-freedom argument (only differences matter for control) is correct and the antisymmetric-by-construction critic removes an unnecessary degree of freedom.
- Well-posed targets: the four-case handling of terminal successors is a nice, non-trivial detail that makes the framework actually deployable.
- Strong reproducibility: open code, modest compute, identical hyperparameters across all 49 games, 10 seeds, and the field-standard aggregate-metric protocol.
- Honest reporting: candid about the Δγ alternative performing worse, and about per-game losses versus PPO.

## Limitations

- **Pairwise cost:** Naive pairwise training is O(B²) in batch size and relies on subsampling that trades compute for estimator variance; no complexity/throughput numbers are given for the critic step itself.
- **Critic expressivity:** Enforced antisymmetry plus a near-linear difference head may underfit complex value differences; the non-linear-head ablation is asserted but not shown.
- **Variance from Bt:** Gauge fixing injects a trajectory-constant Bt that inflates variance for long horizons or as γλ→1, only partially mitigated by trajectory ranking.
- **Breadth:** Evaluation is limited to PPO on discrete-action Atari; continuous control, off-policy regimes, and the motivation's preference-based/human-in-the-loop settings remain undemonstrated.
- **Baseline coverage:** No comparison to dueling networks, distributional critics, or more recent/tuned PPO configurations; "competitive with PPO" is a relatively modest empirical bar.

## Relevance

对于关注强化学习中价值函数与优势估计的研究者，RV 提供了一个理论扎实、与 PPO 即插即用的"相对价值"视角，可作为探索偏好/人类反馈 RL、奖励塑造以及 critic 表征不变性时的参考基线；但其当前仅在离散动作 Atari 上验证，对连续控制与离策略场景的直接适用性仍待考察。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does RV transfer to continuous-control benchmarks (MuJoCo/DM Control) and to off-policy actor-critic methods (SAC, TD3), where the pairwise Bellman target and replay-buffer pairing raise new design questions?
- Can the pairwise critic be applied to preference-based / RLHF-style learning where only pairwise comparisons are observable — the setting the motivation invokes but never tests?
- How sensitive are results to the n-step horizon and the O(B²) subsampling ratio, and can the authors supply the missing ablation tables for both?
- Would a non-linear antisymmetric difference head (tanh + bias-free linear layers) help on the games where the linear head underperforms PPO (Centipede, Zaxxon, Tennis)?
- How does the trajectory-constant Bt scale with horizon and γλ, and is there a principled variance-reduction scheme (beyond row-averaged ranking) that drives E_t[B_t]→0 without per-trajectory anchoring artifacts?
- Can RV be combined with distributional or dueling critics, or does the antisymmetry constraint conflict with those representations?
