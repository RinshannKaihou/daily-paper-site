Exact Aumann–Shapley attribution for GNNs, by co-designing the architecture.

APEX turns GNN path-attribution into a finite exact computation: a polynomial GNN (PolyGIN) makes the Aumann–Shapley integral provably exact at a fixed 8-point Gauss–Legendre rule — zero quadrature error.

Converts GNN path attribution from a sampling approximation into deterministic exact computation — a reusable baseline for molecular and text-graph explainability.

03 Critical edge

The exactness guarantee dies on contact with ReLU, attention, softmax, or any non-polynomial GNN.

  • No released code; modern Shapley/SubgraphX baselines omitted.

Methodology

A model–attribution co-design: instead of approximating the Aumann–Shapley path integral on an arbitrary GNN, the architecture is constrained so the integral becomes exactly computable.

The attribution target

For a trained graph model producing a scalar pre-softmax logit, the straight-line path is γ(τ)=X′+τ(X−X′). Aumann–Shapley / IG attribution of feature xᵢ:

φᵢ = (xᵢ − x′ᵢ) · ∫₀¹ ∂f(γ(τ))/∂xᵢ dτ

Key design choice — attribute the logit, not the post-softmax probability (softmax is non-polynomial and would break exactness).

PolyGIN building blocks

  • PolyActivation σ(z)=z+θ⊙z² — identity path plus learnable quadratic interaction; stays polynomial.
  • PolyScaleNorm Norm(z)=s⊙z — per-dim linear scaling that avoids the data-dependent division which would destroy polynomiality.
  • PolyGIN layer — GIN injective sum-aggregation aᵥ=(1+ε)hᵥ+Σhᵤ, then hᵥ=W₂·σ(Norm(W₁aᵥ)).

Degree-bound theorem (Thm. 1)

By induction: aggregation, linear maps, and PolyScaleNorm are degree-preserving; PolyActivation maps degree d → ≤2d. After L blocks the output degree is ≤2ᴸ, so ∂y/∂xᵢ along the path is degree ≤2ᴸ−1.

m-point Gauss–Legendre is exact for degree ≤ 2m−1
⟹ choose m = 2^(L−1) → φᵢ exact up to FP32 roundoff

Node aggregation

Φᵥ=Σᵢ∈Fᵥ φᵢ kept as a signed score (positive = supporting the class, negative = opposing), with ΣᵥΦᵥ=f(X)−f(X′) up to FP precision.

Attribution fidelity

Against GNNExplainer, PGExplainer, GradCAM, FlowX and 50-pt IG on correctly-classified graphs, APEX gives higher Fidelity+ (selected nodes more necessary) and lower Fidelity− (more sufficient) across sparsity 0.5–0.9 on all five benchmarks.

Proven equivalence (Appendix C)

For Riemann-sum IG, completeness error = numerical quadrature error — formally grounding why APEX's exact quadrature exactly recovers the completeness property.

Strengths & limitations · side by side

Strengths

  • Provable exactness with a concrete, deterministic budget (2^(L−1) points) — no N_step sweep, no convergence tuning.
  • Signed, complete node attributions give supporting/opposing direction that nonnegative scores cannot.
  • Negligible overhead — +0.12%–0.17% params, essentially no accuracy loss vs GIN.
  • Honest scoping — explicit about what the guarantee covers (poly arch, logit) and what it doesn't.
  • Clean separation of three concerns: polynomial exactness, 1-WL expressivity, magnitude stability.

Limitations

  • Architecture-restricted — exactness holds only for polynomial GNNs; no transfer to ReLU/attention/softmax; requires retraining.
  • Expressiveness ceiling — PolyActivation is degree-2 only; only L=4 used; deeper nets risk exponential degree growth and instability, with no principled remedy beyond PolyScaleNorm.
  • Baseline gaps — omits SubgraphX / RG-Explainer / GraphMASK and exact-Shapley methods; only one IG config; "SOTA" rests largely on FlowX & PGExplainer.
  • No semantic validation — exactness is mathematical completeness, not human-ground-truth agreement (a non-goal, but limits trust).
  • No released code; some stability-critical hyperparameters under-specified.

Open questions for further work

  1. Can the realized (not just upper-bound) polynomial degree of a trained PolyGIN be measured post-hoc, enabling an adaptive, smaller Gauss–Legendre budget than 2^(L−1)?
  2. How does APEX scale to deeper or attention-based architectures (GAT, GraphTransformer) — can polynomial attention or higher-degree PolyActivation recover expressiveness without exploding the quadrature budget?
  3. Do APEX attributions align with human/chemical ground truth (toxicophores in Mutagenicity, functional groups in BBBP) beyond model-faithfulness Fidelity?
  4. What is the cost/exactness trade-off vs Hesse et al.'s single-pass axiomatic attribution for positively-homogeneous nets — can the two be combined?
  5. Is the small consistent accuracy drop vs GIN fundamental to the quadratic-only activation, or can learnable higher-degree / piecewise-polynomial activations close the gap while preserving exactness?
