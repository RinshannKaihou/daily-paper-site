01 Methodology — bias as an instrument problem

Instead of a single shuffle per question (which confounds bias with content noise), inspect_permute runs exhaustive permutation: all k! orderings of each MCQ. For MMLU's k=4 that is 24 calls per question — a strict drop-in to inspect_ai.

permute_choices
Solver; permutation index = (epoch−1) % k!, so Epochs(k!) yields every ordering once. Preserves original_position invariant.
position_bias_score
Scorer; records presented vs. picked position, permutation vector, epoch, #options per run.
generate_bias_report
Contingency table over (correct_position × correct/incorrect) → Pearson χ², p-value, Cramér's V, per-position accuracy.
shape_diagnostics
Adds Spearman ρ (position vs. accuracy), 1000-resample bootstrap 95% CIs on V and ρ, mechanism classifier.
Why Cramér's V, not pχ² scales with N, so a marginal effect on a large set matches a large effect on a small one. V is sample-size-invariant and comparable across cells.
Why Spearman ρ, not just Vχ² says whether bias exists, not what kind. Monotone A→D drop vs. non-monotone D-drop share a χ² signature but split in shape.
# refined mechanism classifier (Section 3.3) inactive if V < 0.05 OR p ≥ 0.05 processing_load if V ≥ 0.05 AND ρ ≤ −0.5 AND p < 0.05 content_ambiguity otherwise # v0.1 rule (no p-gate) preserved as classify_preregistered

Sweep: 4 × 5 × 50 × 24 = 24,000 temperature-0 calls. Falsifier predictions + per-cell V ranges locked in preregistration.md, SHA-256 posted publicly before the Google/xAI half was observed — verifiable by any third party in three shell commands.

+ Strengths

  • Verifiable pre-registration. Public hash before half the data observed; per-cell predictions scored unmodified against the v0.1 classifier even when the refined rule would have raised the hit rate.
  • Unusually honest self-correction. The boundary case that broke the v0.1 rule is named; the fix ships as a separate deliverable; the old rule is preserved.
  • Instrument-vs-property distinction ("is the benchmark exposing bias, or is the model biased?") cuts through a real ambiguity in single-shuffle literature.
  • Reproducibility surface (eval logs, git tags, 31 unit tests @ 99% coverage, sensitivity appendix) exceeds the norm for a single-author preprint.
  • Cost asymmetry (7.5× avg / 18.5× peak) is a concrete, measured finding with real implications for independent evaluation capacity.

− Limitations

  • Benchmark scope. Only 5 MMLU subjects, one family. Whether the 60/95 thresholds transfer to BBH, AGIEval, GPQA, FrontierMath, TruthfulQA is open.
  • n=50 per subject. Cell-level χ² rests on 1200 runs but only 50 distinct items — bounding achievable precision and inflating single-question leverage.
  • No frontier-tier model in the band. GPT-5 / Opus 4.8 / Gemini 3 Pro / Grok 4 are not measured; "above-the-band" is shown for two thinking-trace vendors only.
  • Floor edge unobserved. The below-0.60 prediction is extrapolation; lowest swept accuracy is 0.61.
  • Reasoning-trace opacity. Cannot distinguish genuine mitigation from ceiling saturation or "bias laundering" without trace access.
  • Conventional thresholds. V_floor=.05, ρ=−.5, α=.05 are not theory-derived; α=.10 would flip one borderline cell.
