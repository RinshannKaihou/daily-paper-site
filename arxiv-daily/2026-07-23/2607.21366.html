01

Methodology

5 stages · greedy progressive encoding
1

Effective parameters

Each neuron absorbs BatchNorm into w_in,eff and b_i, cancelling magnitude inflation before the non-linearity.

2

Data-free surrogate

MaxEnt Gaussian P_X = N(μ̂, Σ̂) built only from BN statistics → closed-form ReLU self- and cross-kernels.

3

Hilbert-Schmidt ops

Neuron = rank-1 operator f_i = g_i ⊗ w_out. Capacity ∥f_i∥ = ∥w_out∥√K(i,i) is scale-invariant.

4

Unified cost

One closed-form J_bound specializes to J_prune, J_merge (Eckart-Young α=β=1), and J_evict.

5

Greedy loop + DEFT

Rate-distortion picks the best action per step. DEFT reuses J_prune to split frozen core vs. plastic slack.

Authors & credibility Rigor is asymmetric. The derivation chain (axioms → continuous relaxation → bounded closed-form proxy → axiomatic-consistency proof, Prop. C.5) is carefully constructed, and DEFT guarantees (Static Shock O(τ), Dynamic Decoupling, Appx. H) are properly stated. But the empirical half is thin and self-described as “proof-of-concept … rather than exhaustive, large-scale benchmarks.”

Strengths & Limitations

side by side

Strengths

  • S1One unified object. The rank-1 Hilbert-Schmidt operator subsumes pruning, merging, and block eviction under a single distortion metric — no ad-hoc per-action heuristics.
  • S2Genuinely data- & hyperparameter-free for BN networks: MaxEnt Gaussian surrogate + closed-form ReLU kernels evaluate all integrals analytically.
  • S3Scale/shape invariance proven, not asserted — Magnitude Neutrality and Connectivity Preservation survive the bounded-proxy approximation.
  • S4DEFT has a real theorem: Dynamic Decoupling guarantees zero interference to the frozen core — stronger than EWC/PEFT’s implicit guarantees.
  • S5Exemplary fairness: per-example (not mini-batch) Fisher for EWC, hyperparameter sweeps locked on Task 0, H-Score tuned on held-out validation.

Limitations

  • L1Narrow empirical scope. A VGG-8 on CIFAR-100→SVHN and one ResNet-50 on ImageNet. No Transformers, no LLMs, no ImageNet-scale transfer, no LoRA/adapter comparison.
  • L2“Data-free” breaks for BN-free architectures — including most modern Transformers and LLMs, the very models where compression matters most industrially.
  • L3Central compression claim is unverifiable: presented only as a plot, with no tabulated top-1, FLOPs, or density points.
  • L4Approximations untested: Gaussian surrogate, zero-bias cross-kernel, and straight-line J_bound justified asymptotically but never stress-tested against ground-truth integrals.
  • L5No ablation isolating merging vs. pruning vs. eviction, nor surrogate vs. real-data kernel. Transfer benchmark is low-resolution / low-shift.

Open Questions

for further exploration
  1. Q1How does HOPE behave on BN-free architectures (ViTs, LLMs) where the MaxEnt surrogate must become a one-time calibration pass — and how large must that batch be?
  2. Q2Can the compression claim be quantified: ImageNet top-1 at 50/30/20% density vs L1, BN-scale, DepGraph, TCP?
  3. Q3Is the Gaussian / zero-bias cross-kernel empirically accurate? Surrogate K(i,j) vs Monte-Carlo over real activations would test the “Post-ReLU Support Paradox.”
  4. Q4Does DEFT’s H-Score gap survive harder shifts (ImageNet→CUB, natural→medical/sketch) and modern PEFT (LoRA, orthogonal-subspace)?
  5. Q5What does an ablation over {prune, merge, evict, all three} reveal about each primitive’s marginal contribution?
  6. Q6Can the HOPE-induced core/slack partition be mechanistically validated (probing, feature attribution) — linking it to complementary learning systems?
