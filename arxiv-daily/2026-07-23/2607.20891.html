Is Deep Research Reliable?
Misleading Knowledge Induces False Conclusions ↗

Pengyu Zhu · Lijun Li · Longju Yang · Sen Su · BUPT / Shanghai AI Lab · 2026-07-23 · MisKnow-Agent
Core finding

Deep research agents adopt misleading knowledge as their own final conclusions — false-adoption hits 85% right before synthesis. Lifecycle timing dominates, not search rank.

1 Key metric — FCAR
85.0%
False-Conclusion Adoption Rate immediately before final synthesis (late-stage band: 77–94%).
+50.5 pt vs 34.5% at cold start
2 Why this matters

First systematic study of ambient, non-adversarial misleading knowledge in long-horizon research agents. MisKnow-Agent's 5,933 instances and the clean FCAR metric become direct baselines for agent benchmarks, misinformation robustness, and evidence verification.

3 Critical edge

Self-bias confound. DeepSeek-V4 Pro is simultaneously the document generator, one backbone agent, and the judge — despite 99% human–judge agreement. The 5,933-instance corpus isn't released, so FCAR can't be exactly reproduced.

All misleading docs are injected into isolated pools, not the live web — ecological validity of the magnitudes is unproven.
01

Methodology

Threat model. Ambient content-level risk in open information environments — explicitly not instruction-level attacks. Parameters, system prompts, tools, and base evidence E_q are unchanged; only E_q ∪ M_q exposes misleading instances, injected into isolated retrieval pools.

MisKnow-Agent — three stages:

1Blueprint construction — per task, define a target false conclusion c_f, an institution pool stratified by authority (High/Med/Low, 5 each), and an adoption-detection instruction.
2Controlled generation — generator G_doc emits docs across authority × style {Paper, News, Blog, Post}, holding the same false conclusion while varying surface cues. DeepSeek-V4 Pro chosen via qualitative pilot.
3Refinement + filtering — each candidate kept only if all 5 search-enabled verifiers (GLM-5, Kimi-2.6, DeepSeek-V4 Pro, Qwen3.5-397B, Intern-S1-Pro) unanimously judge "Mis". → 5,933 instances over 100 tasks.

FCAR counts only cases where the final report endorses the seeded false conclusion as its own — not mere retrieval, citation, mention, or refutation. Judge–human agreement on 300 reports: κ = 0.978, accuracy 99.0%. Temperature 0 throughout.

  • Paper styling is the strongest persuasive cue — Paper 48.0% > Blog 43.7% > News 37.0% > Post 29.7% (18.3 pt gap).
  • WebThinker's robustness is a delay, not verification — cold 15.7% vs 53.3%, but it converges to 86.7% vs 83.3% before synthesis.
  • Defenses mitigate, not eliminate — combined pre+post drops DeerFlow FCAR to 15–62%, yet for Intern-S1-Pro combining hurts (62% > 57/58% alone).
03

Strengths

  • Clean threat model that refuses to conflate "attack" with ambient risk.
  • FCAR is a better metric than recall/mention-rate for this problem — adoption requires explicit endorsement.
  • Unusually thorough factorial design: rank × timing × authority × style × budget × framework × backbone.
  • Honest negative results: combined defense can hurt; WebThinker's robustness exposed as a delay.
  • Actionable lever: invest in intermediate-state verification, not bigger models or wider search.
04

Limitations

  • Corpus injected into isolated pools, not the live web — ecological validity of magnitudes unproven.
  • Generator = backbone = judge overlap (DeepSeek-V4 Pro) is a confound despite the 99% check.
  • Small task base (100) and unreported per-cell sample sizes for some factorial conditions.
  • Closed-source Gemini uses a different (local-source) injection path — cross-system FCAR comparison is soft.
  • No release commitment for the 5,933 instances — limits exact reproducibility.
  • Defense study limited to DeerFlow / High+Paper only; other regimes uncovered.
05

Open questions

Q1 · VERIFICATIONDoes the 85% pre-synthesis effect persist if a search-enabled critic re-verifies intermediate states at each Plan / Analyze / Synthesize step — not just pre- or post-research?
Q2 · JUDGE INDEPENDENCEWould a third-party judge (GPT-5 / Claude) reproduce the 99% agreement, or does the generator=backbone=judge overlap bias FCAR one direction?
Q3 · LIVE WEBHow do FCAR magnitudes transfer from isolated pools to SEO-poisoned / UGC-injected live content, where rank and authority cues are noisy?
Q4 · GENERALITYIs "framework > backbone" stable beyond DeerFlow vs WebThinker — e.g. OpenAI Deep Research, Kimi Researcher, SciResearcher?
