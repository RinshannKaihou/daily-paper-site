Training Large Language Models for Self-Explanation Faithfulness

Key finding

First to recast the Phi-CCT counterfactual-faithfulness metric into a per-sample GRPO reward — directly optimizing LLM self-explanations instead of relying on prompting or external gold labels.

Why this matters

Turns “faithfulness” from an evaluation metric into an optimizable RL target — directly bearing on whether chain-of-thought reasoning can be trusted.

Critical edge

Influence labels are frozen off-policy — computed once from the T0 model, never resampled. Reported gains may partly reflect decisions drifting toward stale labels, not better disclosure.

acknowledged · not quantified · not ablated

01Methodology

A single-factor counterfactual Δ is inserted into a prompt; a faithful explanation must mention Δ exactly when Δ flipped the decision.

  • Faithfulness as correlation. Influence I = 1{Y′ ≠ Y} and mention M = 1{Δ in Z′}. Dataset-level Phi-CCT = Corr(I, M) (Siegel et al., 2025).
  • Metric → reward. The correlation is decomposed into a per-sample binary r = 1{M ⇔ I} covering all four TP/FN/FP/TN cells — robust to both “always-mention” and “never-mention” static hacks (both yield Corr 0).
  • Class-balanced data. A separate set is built per (model × dataset) pair, with I=1 vs I=0 at parity, so any fixed strategy plateaus at reward 0.5 / Phi-CCT 0 — forcing genuine discrimination.
  • Two intervention families. Random-word insertions (RI, POS-constrained, semantically arbitrary) and user-bias insertions (UB, explicit social-appeal hints).
  • Two training stages. (1) Influence-introspection SFT — predict Yes/No on whether Δ flips the decision; (2) influence-mention alignment via GRPO (16 generations × 4 accumulation steps), reward applied only to explanation tokens.
  • Evaluation. Phi-CCT with 95% percentile bootstrap CIs (5,000 resamples, ~2,000 examples/split). Reward-gaming tracked via completion length and overlap ratio.

  • Detection ≠ disclosure. SFT lifts predicted-vs-true influence correlation strongly (e.g. Llama RI: −0.013 → 0.537) yet those detected factors do not appear in the model's explanations — only RL with the mention-alignment reward closes the gap.
  • Reward-gaming ruled out. No overlap-ratio spike for true-positives (no parroting); explanations shorten for predicted-negatives (precision-raising conciseness) without collapsing to silence.

03Strengths

Clean conversion of a dataset-level metric into a non-trivially-gameable training signal.

  • Decomposes a dataset-level correlation into a per-sample binary reward that resists both always-mention and never-mention equilibria.
  • The detection-vs-disclosure dissociation is a sharp, instructive finding about what SFT can and cannot do.
  • Class-balanced construction removes the two easiest reward-hacking shortcuts by design.
  • Non-defensive limitations section — the off-policy label-staleness issue is stated plainly.
  • Cross-dataset OOD transfer (up to 0.691) is the most encouraging result, reported with CIs.
  • Asymmetric, non-replicating cells are reported rather than cherry-picked.

04Limitations

Workshop-paper scope; the off-policy gap is the central threat to the claim.

  • Off-policy labels: I is frozen from T0; as the policy drifts, reward and evaluation targets diverge — drift not quantified.
  • Mention detection is morphological only; synonyms/paraphrases are missed, possibly inflating gaps.
  • No comparison to FRODO, MARIO, FLARE, or decomposition prompting on Phi-CCT.
  • Only two 8B models; 6k balanced subsets of four datasets; no scale or family sweep.
  • No plausibility / human-judgment scores; no task-accuracy or calibration analysis.
  • Random insertions restricted to single words; naturalness ranking cannot guarantee zero semantic shift.

05Open questions

Where the proof-of-concept most needs to be stress-tested.

Does a fully on-policy variant that resamples I each GRPO step preserve the gains, or do numbers shrink once labels track the current policy?
How do FRODO, MARIO, FLARE, and decomposition prompting compare on Phi-CCT under matched compute?
Does the method scale beyond 8B (70B, frontier), and does the random→bias transfer asymmetry persist or invert?
Can the reward extend to multi-factor / multi-token interventions and free-form paraphrased mentions?
What is the plausibility–faithfulness trade-off — do humans still judge the more faithful explanations acceptable?
Does faithful-disclosure training improve adversarial robustness to prompt-injection / jailbreak hidden tokens?
