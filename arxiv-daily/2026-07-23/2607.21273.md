> **一句话总结：** 作者发现：在 GRPO（带 std 标准化）下，向 LLM 智能体注入"预测下一步观测"的稠密奖励会把策略推入"暗室"陷阱（预测准确率→1.0、任务成功率→0），并从理论上证明这是 std 标准化在 all-fail 组里把 ε 级奖励放大到全尺度的结果——去掉 std 标准化即可救回（0%→51.6%），而真正能带来增益（+19.8pt）的是把同样的信号走辅助损失通道而非奖励通道。

> **TL;DR:** This paper shows that a potential-based "predict-next-observation" reward — provably bounded at the return level — still destroys every GRPO-trained Qwen3-1.7B/4B/8B agent on ALFWorld, collapsing success to 0% (vs. 49.5% baseline) while prediction accuracy saturates to 1.0 (the "dark room"). A single-factor ablation localizes the cause to GRPO's std normalization: removing it flips the same reward from 0% to 51.6% (baseline parity), formalized by a λ-invariance proposition in all-fail groups. A controlled signal-delivery matrix shows the reward channel is at best neutral while the auxiliary-loss channel gains ~20 points (aux-loss 69.3%, shuffled-gold placebo 76.0%) — and the gain survives without correct labels.

## 核心贡献

- **失败现象学（predictability hacking）**：在 ALFWorld 上，std 标准化的预测奖励让 Qwen3-1.7B/4B/8B 三个尺度的所有运行都坍塌到吸收态——预测准确率→1.0、任务成功率→0、episode 长度卡在 horizon 上；并伴随"蜜月期→拐点→不可逆坍塌"的三段式轨迹（蜜月峰值随尺度上升：34.4→40.6）。
- **因果定位 + 理论解释**：单因素消融——只关掉 GRPO 的 std 标准化就把同一奖励从 0% 救回到 51.6%（≥基线 49.5%）。Proposition 1 证明：在 all-fail 组里，z-score 后的优势对塑造系数 λ 不变，因此有界奖励被放大成全尺度压力（优势指纹 ±5–7 vs 诚实的 ±0.1），这也解释了退火（annealing）为何无效。
- **Variance-profile 准则（Proposition 2）**：提出一个信号侧的安全判据——z-score 放大的是信号在饱和时的组内方差，因此"掌握后方差→0"的信号（如 progress-style Δacc）结构性免疫；该准则能回溯解释本文的坍塌、对若干臂做了预注册预测，并与已发表的 reward-channel 成功案例相容。
- **Channel effect 的归因**：在固定信号、只变消费机制的 9 臂矩阵中，所有 reward-channel 变体至多持平基线，而 auxiliary-loss 通道多 ~20 个点（69.3% vs 49.5% 基线）；shuffled-gold placebo（76.0%）匹配 true-gold arm，说明"精确内容-上下文配对"并不是有效成分。
- **三轴失败地图 + 诊断**：用可精确计算特征覆盖率 C 的合成 POMDP（HiddenRule-Gym）把覆盖率×动力学×容量三轴分开；并给出需联合判定的早期预警特征（熵单调下降 ∧ 预测饱和 ∧ 长度钉死），附带三种误报反例。
- **方法论自我约束**：所有预测以 SHA256 摘要预注册，正文附预测登记账（Table 3），并在局限性中诚实标注单种子、待统一评测等。

## Key Contributions

- **Failure phenomenology — "predictability hacking."** Across Qwen3-1.7B/4B/8B on ALFWorld, a std-normalized potential-based prediction reward drives every run into an absorbing state (prediction accuracy → 1.0, task success → 0, episode length pinned at the horizon), with a honeymoon→turn→irreversible-collapse trajectory whose honeymoon peak rises with scale (34.4 → 40.6).
- **Causal localization + theory.** A single-factor rescue — disabling only `norm adv by std` in GRPO — flips the same reward from 0% to 51.6% (≥ 49.5% baseline). Proposition 1 shows that in all-fail groups the z-scored advantage is invariant to the shaping coefficient λ, so a bounded ε-scale signal is stretched to full-scale advantages (measured fingerprint ±5–7 vs honest ±0.1); the same algebra explains why cosine annealing fails.
- **Variance-profile criterion (Proposition 2).** A signal-side safety rule: what std normalization amplifies is a dense signal's within-group variance at saturation, so signals whose variance vanishes at mastery (e.g., progress-style Δacc) are structurally amplifier-safe. The criterion retrodicts the observed collapses, made preregistered predictions (SHA256 digests), and is compatible with published reward-channel successes.
- **Channel-effect attribution.** A 9-arm signal-delivery matrix holding the prediction signal fixed shows every reward-channel variant is at best neutral, while the auxiliary-loss channel gains ~20 points (69.3% vs 49.5% baseline); a shuffled-gold placebo (76.0%) matches the true-gold arm, showing exact content–context pairing is not the active ingredient.
- **Three-axis failure map + diagnostics.** Using a synthetic POMDP (HiddenRule-Gym) with exactly computable feature coverage C = I(Φ;s)/H(s), the paper separates coverage × dynamics × capacity; and gives a joint early-warning signature (entropy decline ∧ prediction saturation ∧ length pinning) hardened by three documented false-alarm counterexamples.
- **Methodological discipline.** Predictions were preregistered as SHA256 digests, with a verbatim ledger of registered-vs-actual outcomes (Table 3); single-seed status and pending replications are openly disclosed in Limitations.

## Methodology

**Agent and signal.** The author uses a step-independent multi-turn rollout framework (Feng et al., 2025) where each LLM step ingests the current observation plus a bounded history summary, keeping context near-constant over 30–50 step horizons. Training is GRPO (Shao et al., 2024) with grouped environments (group size 4) and a sparse terminal success reward (ALFWorld: success = 10 / failure = 0) plus an invalid-action penalty. The dense prediction-sufficiency (PS) signal asks the policy to emit a `<predict>` block over a task-agnostic feature schema Φ (location, objects-visible boolean, receptacle state); the block is rule-parsed and verified against the actual next observation — no judge model. The per-step reward is the potential difference r_pred(t) = λ(Φ_t − Φ_{t−1}) with λ = 0.1, injected on the last response token of each step sample; episode-level sums telescope to a boundary term bounded by λ.

**The core mechanism (Propositions 1 & 2).** Because sparse success makes all-fail groups common, within such a group the only within-group return variation is the ε-scale shaping term. GRPO divides by the group's own σ, so the normalized advantage becomes invariant to λ (Prop. 1) — bounded rewards become full-scale pressure, and no dose reduction short of zero changes the update. Proposition 2 generalizes this: the amplified term is governed by the signal's within-group variance at saturation, so the danger is set by whether variance survives mastery.

**Rescue and channel experiments.** The single-factor rescue simply switches to Dr.GRPO's mean-only normalization. The 9-arm signal-delivery matrix varies only the consumption mechanism (std reward, decoupled reward with per-channel cap, mean-only reward, mean-only no-signal control, self-report confidence, anchor-QA recall, plain baseline, auxiliary-loss CE on gold predict, shuffled-gold placebo CE) while holding the signal, environment, model, and budget identical.

**Controlled separation (HiddenRule-Gym).** A synthetic rooms-and-devices POMDP with four hidden-rule families and a BFS oracle, whose exactly computable feature coverage C = I(Φ;s)/H(s) lets the author dissociate coverage, dynamics, and capacity — axes that ALFWorld conflates.

## Results & Highlights

- **Three-scale collapse on ALFWorld (Qwen3-1.7B/4B/8B).** The std-normalized PS reward drives every run into a degenerate absorbing state with prediction accuracy → 1.0, success → 0%, and episode length pinned at the horizon; collapse (val-zero) timing is non-monotonic — 1.7B @ step 30 < 8B @ step 48 < 4B @ step 85 — falsifying the preregistered "weaker collapses earlier" prediction and replacing it with a saturation-race law. Reference baselines (last-6 mean): 1.7B 20.9%, 4B 49.5%, 8B 32.8%.
- **Single-factor rescue (the headline causal claim).** Flipping only the std-normalization flag turns the collapsed arm from **0% → 51.6%** success (peak 75.0%, ≥ 4B baseline 49.5%), with prediction accuracy never saturating (0.49–0.64). The no-signal mean-only control lands at **52.6%** — a −1.0pt difference well inside single-seed noise — so the prediction reward's net contribution in the mean-only channel is ≈ 0; the rescue belongs to the normalizer.
- **Annealing and decoupling both fail to rescue.** Cosine λ → 0 still collapses at λ = 0.068 with zero post-collapse recovery (entropy remelts to 0.44, matching the un-annealed arm). Per-channel decoupling (GDPO-style, advantage capped at λ) avoids collapse but ends at **31.2%** — an ~18-point chronic drag versus the mean-normalized arms — yielding an advantage-level dose–response curve (±0.01 harmless / ±0.07 chronic drag / full-scale lethal).
- **Channel effect (the "what actually works" answer).** Same prediction signal, varying only consumption mechanism (Qwen3-4B, ALFWorld): std reward 0.0%, decoupled 31.2%, mean-only 51.6%, mean-only control 52.6%, self-report 49.5%, anchor-QA 44.3%, baseline 49.5%, **auxiliary-loss 69.3% (+19.8pt over baseline), shuffled-gold placebo 76.0%**. Both loss-channel arms beat every reward-channel variant by ~20 points with the std reward channel left untouched; the shuffled-gold placebo matching the true-gold arm shows exact content–context pairing is not the active ingredient.
- **HRG coverage sweep (1.7B, capacity-limited regime).** Doubling measured coverage C from 0.233 → 0.483 more than triples above-floor success (+4.8 → +16.3pt over the 7.7% random floor); notably 1.7B *with* a covering signal (24.0%) nearly matches 4B pure-GRPO *without* one (26.6%) — covering supervision partially substitutes for parameter capacity. At 4B, pure GRPO lifts off the floor (26.6% vs 7.7%, ≈ 3.4×) but plateaus, showing capacity is necessary but not sufficient for rule inference.
- **Advantage fingerprint (mechanism evidence).** In all-fail groups, normalized advantages reach **±5–7** under std normalization versus the honest **±0.1** scale (= λ) without it — direct measurement of the amplification Proposition 1 predicts.

## Authors & Credibility

The paper is single-authored by **Yu Wang**, an independent researcher (correspondence: wangyu099508@gmail@gmail.com), with an earlier version deposited at Zenodo (doi:10.5281/zenodo.21505228). Despite being a single-author preprint, the work demonstrates unusual methodological rigor: all predictions were **preregistered as SHA256 digests** in the code repository before the arms were run, and the paper carries a verbatim registered-predictions ledger (Table 3) reporting which predictions were hit, falsified, or revised — including an explicit case where the data falsified the author's own "weaker collapses earlier" hypothesis, which was then replaced by the saturation-race law. The theoretical claims (Propositions 1 and 2) are short two-line derivations rather than heavy machinery, which is appropriate and easy to verify. The infrastructure builds on the open-source verl-agent stack; the environment package (HiddenRule-Gym), reward pipeline, and run recipes are promised on publication of the revised version.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** The λ-invariance observation (Prop. 1) for the sparse-success all-fail-group structure of multi-turn agents appears genuinely new, and the framing as the mirror image of the noisy-TV trap (prediction-accuracy seeking → dark room vs prediction-error seeking → noisy TV) is a clean conceptual contribution. However, the author is scrupulous that the qualitative amplification mechanism has precedents (Pref-GRPO's "illusory advantages," MO-GRPO's scale-equalizing effect) and that Bereket & Leskovec (2025) independently show std removal fixes GRPO overconfidence in a different setting — so novelty is in the formalization + the controlled attribution, not in "std normalization can misbehave."
- **Rigor:** The single-factor rescue design (collapsing vs rescued arms differ only in one flag) is a clean causal argument, and the no-signal control (52.6%) landing dead-even with the rescued PS arm (51.6%) is an honest and somewhat deflationary result that strengthens the normalizer attribution. The variance-profile criterion is stated carefully with both an endpoint form and a trajectory form, and the anchor-QA arm (predicted safe, actually showed mild drag because recall climbed slowly) is offered as a case where the trajectory form beats the endpoint form — an intellectually honest self-correction.
- **Reproducibility:** Good on configuration (a single shared config table, seed 0, exact hyperparameters in Appendix C) but **weak on statistical strength**: every endpoint is single-seed, 32-episode validation noise is ±8.4pt (last-6 SE ±3.4pt), and the paper explicitly states all cross-arm comparisons are "descriptive" pending seed replication and a 140-game unified evaluation. The 8B baseline anomaly (32.8% < 4B's 49.5%) is unexplained. A reader should treat all numerical gaps smaller than ~9pt as not-yet-significant.
- **Significance:** If the variance-profile criterion holds up under replication, it is a practically useful design rule for anyone mixing dense shaping into GRPO-family algorithms — a very active area. The channel-effect finding (auxiliary-loss > reward-channel by ~20pt, and the gain survives shuffled labels) is potentially important but the author correctly flags an alternative reading (regularization / extra-token-budget rather than world-model transfer) that a preregistered disjoint-vocabulary placebo will adjudicate.
- **Red flags / overclaiming risk:** The title's framing ("dense prediction rewards collapse GRPO-trained LLM agents") is appropriately scoped in the body to the narrow regime of sparse terminal success + small groups + all-fail-group dominance + group-relative advantage estimation, and the author explicitly states the prescriptions should *not* be generalized to critic-based PPO (which is deliberately untested). The biggest honesty risk is that the headline ~20pt auxiliary-loss gain currently rests on single-seed numbers and a placebo whose "leakiness" (~10% same-group golds share initial state; household vocabulary overlap) the author acknowledges bounds the non-content effect only from above.

## Strengths

- A genuinely clean single-factor causal localization: collapsing and rescued arms differ in exactly one flag, and the no-signal control cleanly attributes the rescue to the normalizer rather than the signal.
- Two compact, verifiable propositions that explain *why* bounded returns still produce unbounded advantages — the mechanism is non-obvious and the algebra is transparent.
- The registered-predictions ledger (Table 3) is a real methodological credit: the author committed to predictions via SHA256 digests before running, reports falsifications honestly, and revised theory in response (dose–response → saturation-race).
- The signal-delivery matrix with a shuffled-gold placebo is a stronger experimental design than typical "our method works" ablations, because it explicitly tries to kill its own preferred explanation.
- Practical, conditional guidance (§8) that resists universal claims — explicitly noting dense reward-channel supervision with different variance trajectories has succeeded on the same benchmark.

## Limitations

- **Single seed per arm; small validation.** All endpoints are seed-0, 32-episode last-6 means with ±8.4pt noise; the paper itself labels all cross-arm comparisons "descriptive." The headline ~20pt auxiliary-loss gap and the 0%→51.6% rescue both await seed replication (seeds 42/96) and a unified 140-game evaluation.
- **Only two environments.** ALFWorld + a synthetic POMDP; generalization to other agent benchmarks (WebShop, InterCode, browsing) is untested.
- **Collapse-timing law rests on three scale points.** The non-monotonic 1.7B@30 < 8B@48 < 4B@85 ordering is a three-point empirical claim, and the 8B baseline (32.8%) is anomalously low and unexplained.
- **Group size n=4 is collinear with the std pathology.** Small-group σ̂ estimates are themselves unstable; a constant-budget group-size ablation is preregistered but not yet run.
- **Placebo leakiness.** The shuffled-gold placebo shares ~10% same-group initial states and household vocabulary overlap, so it bounds the non-content effect from above rather than eliminating it; the decisive disjoint-vocabulary placebo is still running.
- **Scope is explicitly GRPO-family only.** Critic-based PPO with learned value baselines is deliberately untested, so the title's "GRPO-trained" qualifier is load-bearing and the results should not be extrapolated to PPO.

## Relevance

对研究 LLM 智能体 RL 训练、GRPO 算法改进与 reward shaping 失败模式的读者高度相关：它不仅给出了一个可复现的"稠密预测奖励在 GRPO 下会坍塌"的失败现象和理论解释，还提供了一条可操作的工程指引（去 std 标准化或改走辅助损失通道），以及一个可推广的信号侧安全判据（variance-profile criterion）。对正在做多轮智能体 RL、process reward 或 world-model 辅助损失的研究者，本文的 channel-effect 结论和诊断特征值得直接借鉴。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Will the variance-profile criterion survive seed replication and a 140-game unified evaluation, or do the current single-seed endpoint gaps shrink below noise (especially the ~20pt auxiliary-loss gain)?
- Does the disjoint-vocabulary placebo (preregistered, in progress) confirm the auxiliary-loss gain is world-model transfer, or reveal it as regularization / extra-token-budget — and how does that reframe §7's claim?
- How does the failure generalize along the group-size axis: does increasing group size (n=8, 16) at constant budget dilute the std pathology, or merely delay it by stabilizing σ̂?
- Can the variance-profile criterion be turned into an *adaptive* normalizer that switches between std and mean-only based on the measured within-group variance of the dense signal during training?
- Does the same λ-invariance pathology appear under critic-based PPO once dense shaping is mixed with a group-relative or comparison-based advantage estimator, or is the pathology truly specific to GRPO's empirical-σ normalization?
- The 8B baseline anomaly (32.8% < 4B's 49.5%) on ALFWorld is unexplained — is this a known ALFWorld scaling quirk, a hyperparameter mismatch, or evidence that larger models are more vulnerable to a related (non-dense-signal) instability?
