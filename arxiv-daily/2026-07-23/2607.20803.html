The Geometry of Personality:
Activation Steering with Jungian Cognitive Functions

Key finding

Cast LLM personality as eight Jungian cognitive functions, not static Big Five traits — all eight are monotonically steerable in Llama-3.1-8B, and multi-dimensional directions are not linear combinations of single ones.

Liu Zai · Yumeng Wang · Junchen Fu · Joemon M. Jose · University of Glasgow & Leiden University · arXiv:2607.20803 ↗ · 23 Jul 2026

Why this matters

Multi-dim steering directions are not linear combos of single-dim ones — direct evidence of activation-space entanglement, plus a reusable dataset (NarrationDB).

Critical edge

Single model only (Llama-3.1-8B), no head-to-head Big Five baseline, and a partially circular evaluation: the test used to extract the vector is also the test used to score it.

Open methodological gaps
  • Layer 7–12 localization & PCA geometry could be Llama-specific artifacts.
  • Effectiveness metric rewards floor/ceiling shifts, not true control.
  • No downstream behavioral validation of the steered personality.

Section 01

01Methodology — an encode · compute · decode pipeline

STEP 01

Jungian Evaluation Protocol

132-item open typology test, 4 batches × 33, shuffled over 30 seeds. Score S = 100·(w−1)/4, w = 0.7P + 0.3(6−N).

STEP 02

NarrationDB Construction

Llama-3.1-70B role-plays 2,100+ SWCPQ characters; 95%-margin tag selection yields 8-D Jungian scores, dense across function space.

STEP 03

Difference-in-Means Extraction

Top/bottom-k (k=10) clusters; T=5 open prompts each; V = Lat(H)−Lat(L) at layer n, averaged over 50 dialogues.

STEP 04

Single-Layer Injection

Unit-normalized V added at one layer, strength λ ∈ [−6.875, +6.875], step 0.25. Combinations <80% task-completion discarded.

STEP 05

31-Layer Sweep

Per-function most-effective layer n* recorded; high-effectiveness volume clusters at layers 7–12.

STEP 06

Geometry & Backtracking

PCA on V₁₂; multi-dim V_{Ne+Ti} extracted directly, then Least-Squares-fit against single-dim subspace — residual r quantifies entanglement.

Headline results

  • All eight functions steerable monotonically; range 19.20 → 56.70.
  • Baseline Llama-3.1-8B is strongly Ti-biased (90.77), weakest on sensing (Si 72.53).
  • Most-effective layers cluster at 7–12 — encode-compute-decode signature.
  • Fi is asymmetric: only the negative direction works (λ ∈ [−2.38, −0.12]). Post-training pinning suspected.
  • PCA: rational functions (Te/Ti/Fe/Fi) sit nearer their E/I twins; irrational (Ne/Ni/Se/Si) farther — matching Jung (1971).
  • Multi-dim non-linearity: 12 pairs, LS residual r = 0.23 (Ni+Te) → 0.65 (Si+Ne).

Section 03

03Strengths & limitations, side-by-side

+Strengths

  • Clean, traceable pipeline from questionnaire adaptation → dataset → vector extraction → geometric analysis. Parameter-light at every step.
  • Honest reporting: per-function effectiveness for all eight, including the weak Fi result; residuals and λ ranges published.
  • Multi-dim backtracking is the most interesting conceptual contribution, supported by Least-Squares across 12 combinations.
  • Releases NarrationDB (2,100+ characters) and an LLM-usable Jungian protocol — both reusable.
  • Connects mechanistic findings back to a named psychological theory, not just "vectors work."

−Limitations

  • Single architecture (Llama-3.1-8B); no transfer to Qwen/Mistral/Gemma or other scales.
  • No quantitative Big Five baseline on a common downstream benchmark — concept argued, not measured.
  • Fi positive-direction failure unverified — base-model ablation not run.
  • Partially circular evaluation: extraction test = scoring test.
  • Jungian typology contested in modern psychology; treated as ground truth.
  • No code/data URL in paper; "80% task-completion" filter under-specified.

Section 04

04Open questions

Q1Do layer 7–12 localization and rational/irrational geometry replicate across Qwen, Mistral, Gemma — or are they Llama-3.1-specific artifacts?
Q2How does Jungian steering compare quantitatively to OCEAN steering on a shared behavioral benchmark (dialogue style, moral-dilemma responses)?
Q3Does the Fi positive-direction failure disappear in a base (non-RLHF) model, confirming the post-training-pinning hypothesis?
Q4Can the multi-dim non-linearity be exploited — joint vectors outperforming any single-dim combination on a measurable task?
Q5How sensitive are layer rankings to seed averaging and the 80% task-completion filter?
Q6Does an independent, non-Jungian behavioral evaluation move in the predicted direction when a function is steered — breaking the circularity?
