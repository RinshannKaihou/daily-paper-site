The Weight of Silence ♞
A Causal Case for Weights Over the Scratchpad in Latent Chess Reasoning

Ishan S. Kshirsagar·Independent Researcher·2026-07-23

Key finding

Latent-thought RL lifts chess legality 48%→61% and erases checkmate confabulation — yet a six-condition causal battery proves the model never reads its thoughts; the gain is welded into the weights.

Key metric · legal-move rate

61%

Rung-3 (latent + GRPO) on the frozen 100-position Stockfish-verified harness.

↑ +13 ptsvs 48% pre-RL (Stage-2)
false-mate claims: 0/100 (was 28)

Why this matters

First positive latent-reasoning + RL gain outside math/logic — and a same-model before/after causal battery that directly challenges the Coconut assumption that thoughts are read at inference time.

Critical edge

Move-quality Accuracy stays flat at 9–10% — the random-legal-move floor — for every checkpoint. Rung-3 plays more legal moves, not better ones; paired centipawn-loss slightly favors pre-RL. n=100, single seed, no real checkmate in the harness.

  • Gumbel control underperforms. Giving RL a real gradient into thought content yields only 44%→50% legal in 150 steps (vs Rung-3's climb to 61%), false-mate plateaus at 15/100 — a second independent line that thought content is not the lever.
  • Honest nulls. Four milder conditions trend the right way but do not clear significance at n=100; the paper states this rather than overclaiming.
03

Strengths & limitations

What holds up

strengths
  • Pre-registered curriculum stop rule; frozen harness fixed before any comparison.
  • Six-condition battery run identically before & after RL on the same lineage — cleanest possible isolation of RL's effect.
  • Statistical honesty: Bonferroni-corrected CIs; explicit that only one of five conditions clears significance.
  • Gated-legality reward presented as a documented fix to a real Goodhart failure, not a hindsight justification.
  • Calibrated J-lens null (~94% true-positive on text) is a quantified negative, far stronger than an uncalibrated logit-lens read.
  • Confabulation eliminated as a side effect under two independent recipes — robust, not cherry-picked.

Where it frays

limits
  • Single training run per checkpoint; no seed variance. CIs quantify board-sampling variance only.
  • Playing strength does not improve. 9–10% exact-match vs Stockfish everywhere; paired cp-loss slightly favors Stage-2.
  • Harness has no real checkmate — model is never tested on calling a true mate, only on withholding a false one.
  • Narrow scope by design: one 14B LoRA, one domain, one recipe. No full-FT, frontier-scale, or transfer claims.
  • Gumbel control is a biased-gradient approximation — can't separate "content useless" from "path hard to train."
  • The 61% (Table 1) vs 58% (Table 2) gap is attributed to NF4/bf16 dequantization — plausible but unverified.
04

Open questions

  1. Q1Does "RL adds robustness, not content-reliance" replicate with multiple seeds, larger bases, and full fine-tuning?
  2. Q2Would adding real checkmate positions let Rung-3 ever correctly call a true mate — or only withhold a false one?
  3. Q3Is the flat 9–10% accuracy ceiling breakable with stronger pretrained chess understanding, or intrinsic to LoRA-scale RLVR?
  4. Q4Does an SVD of each layer's ΔW show gate_proj concentration is low-rank — and do those directions overlap Zero-sensitive layers?
  5. Q5Would the before/after-RL battery, applied to Switch's math setting, reconcile the "same pattern, opposite conclusion" split?
  6. Q6Why do Rung-3 thought vectors collapse to a near-constant scaffold (cosine 0.99) — inevitable attractor, or curriculum artifact?
