Methodology

controlled minimal pairs → EAP-IG → 5-axis validation

Existing datasets don't fit circuit discovery: BLiMP's subject_passive puts the contrastive noun inside the input (no patchable target), and Gregorio et al. (2025) lacks minimal-pair structure. So the authors build their own dataset from the template The [patient] was [verb p.p.] by the.

Each frame is instantiated across every (animate_verb × inanimate_verb) pair to produce minimal contrasts like "The victim was examined by the" (clean → favors animate) vs. "The victim was blinded by the" (corrupt → favors inanimate). A Llama 3.3 70B plausibility filter keeps pairs only when both prefixes exceed 0.90 Yes-probability.

With no single ground-truth next token, they define a set-based average logit difference over animate target set A vs. inanimate set I. A pair counts as solved when clean > 0 and corrupt < 0 (random baseline 25%). Only solved pairs with margin > 0.5 feed circuit discovery — 500 high-confidence examples per model.

They run Edge Attribution Patching with Integrated Gradients (EAP-IG) across four open-weight models, then validate along five axes: sufficiency, necessity, random-edge controls, seed-stability IoU, and three transfer setups.

Dataset pipeline

  1. Generate semantic frames (patient+verb) with GPT 5.4, manually inspected.
  2. Instantiate template × patients × verb pairs → minimal pairs.
  3. Filter with Llama 3.3 70B at 0.90 Yes threshold → 16,305 pairs (81.5% of 20k).
  4. Targets: ~500 nouns from WordNet (noun.person vs. event/phenomenon/state/artifact/substance/object).
  5. Single-token filter per model → shared cross-model subset of 9,532 pairs.

Necessity & robustness

Ablating just the top-20 EAP-IG edges drops faithfulness below 0.1 in all four models; size-matched random edges do nothing comparable.

Discovered-vs-random faithfulness gap: 87.6% (GPT-2), 93.5% (Llama), 87.9% (Gemma), 85.6% (Qwen). Cross-seed IoU sits between 0.8–1.0.

No backup / shadow circuit is rediscovered — max reduced-graph faithfulness ~0.0004.

Composition & transfer collapse

Circuits are MLP-dominated (MLP0 prominent) and spread across early-to-late layers — unlike compact Greater-Than / IoI / Country-Capital circuits.

Under Setup 3 (new data and named-entity targets) the original circuit breaks even though full models still solve the task (>0.84 acc): GPT-2 → 0.05, Llama → 0.17, Qwen → 0.52, Gemma → 0.47.

Setup 2 (named entities, original prefixes): Llama strongest (faithfulness 0.910); independently discovered circuits share IoU ~0.4–0.7 with the originals.

Strengths & Limitations

read side by side

Strengths PRO

  • First mechanistic, causal account of animacy in LLMs — fills an explicit literature gap beyond behavioral and surprisal analyses.
  • Methodologically careful: random-edge controls, seed-stability IoU, shadow-circuit tests, and three distinct transfer setups — exactly what recent critiques (Mueller 2024; Zhang & Nanda 2024) demand.
  • Honest, non-overclaiming framing: the circuit is repeatedly framed as task-relative, not a universal animacy mechanism.
  • Reusable controlled minimal-pair dataset (16,305 pairs) that fixes BLiMP's patching incompatibility and Gregorio et al.'s non-minimal-pair structure.
  • Cross-model consistency — MLP-dominated, distributed, early-layer routing via MLP0 — across four architecturally diverse models.

Limitations CON

  • Single syntactic construction (passive by-prefixes) and person-vs-inanimate only — no active voice, coreference, agreement, animals, or discourse-level animacy.
  • Dataset relies on LLM-generated frames and automatic plausibility filtering; no large human-validated or lexically-disjoint evaluation split.
  • Circuit discovery is conditioned on the ~45–50% of pairs models actually solve, plus a margin > 0.5 subset — it does not explain model failures.
  • EAP-IG gives only an approximate edge ranking; different metrics, contrasts, or graph representations could surface different structures.
  • Functional interpretation of the necessary core (what MLP0 routes) remains preliminary — no feature-level causal characterization.
  • GPT-2 Small is an order of magnitude smaller and six years older, confounding cross-model compactness comparisons.

Open Questions

where this leads next
Q1
Does an animacy circuit recovered from active-voice SVO frames overlap with this passive by-frame circuit — or do the two rely on distinct mechanisms?
Q2
How does the circuit change when targets extend down the animacy hierarchy (animals, imaginary beings) instead of only noun.person?
Q3
Can a feature-level method (SAEs, dictionary learning on MLP0 outputs) identify the verb-level features MLP0 routes — moving past the current "routing vs. writing" speculation?
Q4
Would circuits discovered on human-validated, lexically-disjoint sets look structurally different from the GPT-generated, Llama-filtered data used here?
Q5
Why is Qwen 3 4B's circuit an order of magnitude more compact (1.74%)? Artifact of EAP-IG on its architecture, genuine mechanistic difference, or a less distributed representation that also explains its poor transfer?
Q6
Do these animacy circuits align with "attention sink" / early-layer MLP patterns (Gu 2025; Xiao 2024) — suggesting a generic early-layer routing role rather than an animacy-specific one?
