Medical LLM Evaluation · Behavioral Auditing

Auditing Evidence Use in Medical LLM Diagnosis

Key finding

A behavioral audit mines low-order evidence interactions in diagnostic margins — most are legitimate differential diagnosis, not misuse; accuracy ≠ evidence-use fidelity.

Junchi Liao · Jiawen Deng · Fuji Ren* · University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC), Chengdu · arXiv:2607.20848 ↗ · 23 Jul 2026

Key metric — legitimate interaction strength

86.6–89.6% of DDXPlus strength is faithful

Faithful support + differential conflict/cancellation → across 5 models

Negated/absent misuse stays at only 1.2–3.0%. Conflict/cancellation alone: 47.1% DDXPlus, 45.8% CupCase, 52.2% MedCase.

Stability filtering lifts adjudicated precision 0.55 → 0.80, cuts false-positive rate 0.20 → 0.08. Reviewer agreement Fleiss' κ = 0.82.

Why this matters

Accuracy ≠ evidence-use: a beyond-accuracy audit pinpointing shortcut failures in negated/absent and clinically local evidence — directly useful for medical AI safety and red-teaming.

Critical edge

Only open-weight 7–8B models and multiple-choice option-letter scoring — frontier proprietary models (GPT-4 / Med-Gemini) and free-form clinical deployment are entirely out of scope, so the failure profile may not transfer.

Other structural caveats
  • 130-item clinical review is enriched, not probability-sampled — "11 invalid" is descriptive, not a base rate.
  • No head-to-head baseline audit (CheckList / SHAP); order-3 value argued via retrieval gap, not direct comparison.
  • Option-letter log-probs carry token/position bias; permutations reduce but cannot calibrate it away.

Section 01

01Methodology — score · role · mine · adjudicate

STAGE 01

Diagnostic scoring under interventions

Decompose case into evidence units E = {e₁…eₙ} + candidates C. Score every candidate by option-letter log-prob s_T(cⱼ); audited margin v_d(T) = s_T(d) − max_{cⱼ≠d} s_T(cⱼ).

STAGE 02

Diagnosis-relative evidence roles

Tag each unit relative to d: supporting, competitor-supporting, negated/absent, background-risk, likely-irrelevant/local. Directional expectations — not global good/bad labels.

STAGE 03

Low-order interaction mining

Möbius/Shapley form I_d(S) = Σ_{T⊆S} (−1)^{|S|−|T|} v_d(T). Target order-3 (interpretable, cheap); rank interactions by |I_d(S)| strength.

STAGE 04

Provisional → confirmed labels

Deterministic priority rules assign candidate-misuse vs. mechanism labels. Stability filters (prompt wording, candidate order, deletion-vs-neutralization) + targeted counterfactuals feed blinded 5-reviewer adjudication.

SETUP

Datasets & models

DDXPlus (500 cases, 8 units, 255 interventions), CupCase (200, 6, 63), MedCase (200, 6, 63). Five open-weight LLMs scored with vLLM at temp 0, one token, option-letter log-probs.

SETUP

Models evaluated

Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct · OpenBioLLM-8B · MedGemma-4B · BioMistral-7B · Llama3-Med42-8B. DDXPlus carries structured role labels; CupCase/MedCase are coarser external checks.

Headline results

  • 86.6–89.6% of DDXPlus strength = faithful support + conflict/cancellation; negated/absent misuse only 1.2–3.0%.
  • Accuracy ≠ evidence-use: model ordering by accuracy does not match faithful-evidence or conflict strength.
  • 130-item blinded review (κ=0.82): 111 valid / 8 questionable / 11 invalid — all 11 invalid from the negated/absent bucket.
  • Counterfactual: removing myasthenia family-history cue → margin −9.50 (Qwen); skin fields for hernia +2.25 (local incoherence, not reliance).
  • Stability funnel 300 → 120 candidates; precision 0.55 → 0.80, FP 0.20 → 0.08 (random control: 0.10 / 0.75).
  • OpenBioLLM-8B tops accuracy (72.2% avg, 87.5% CupCase); MedCase hardest (best 55.0%, BioMistral 44.5%).

Section 03

03Strengths & limitations, side-by-side

+Strengths

  • Clean discovery-vs-assignment separation — prevents the "any sensitivity = unreliability" overclaim that plagues attribution work.
  • Diagnosis-relative roles handle the same-evidence-different-diagnosis subtlety (skin findings: red flag for hernia, legitimate for SLE).
  • Layered validation: prompt/order robustness, deletion-vs-neutralization semantics, targeted counterfactuals, blinded 5-reviewer adjudication (κ=0.82).
  • Concrete failure taxonomy — negated/absent misuse, clinically local evidence — actionable for curators and red-teamers, not vague "bias."
  • Honest epistemics — repeatedly states what the numbers do not prove (margins are preferences, not calibrated probabilities).

−Limitations

  • Only 5 open-weight 7–8B models; no GPT-4 / Med-Gemini / frontier — failure profile may not transfer.
  • Multiple-choice option-letter scoring only; free-form clinical deployment out of scope; token/position bias acknowledged, only mitigated.
  • Enriched, non-probability review sample — precision and mechanism shares are descriptive, not population estimates.
  • Fine-grained failure claims anchored to DDXPlus alone; CupCase/MedCase support only coarse structure labels.
  • No baseline comparator audit (CheckList / SHAP) head-to-head; order-3 sufficiency checked only on DDXPlus.
  • No code/data release mentioned; evidence-unit selection rules only partially formalized.

04Open questions

Q1How does the audit profile change for frontier proprietary models (GPT-4/5, Med-Gemini) and free-form diagnostic outputs rather than option letters?
Q2Can the negated/absent-misuse cluster (no-travel, skin/rash, no-peeling) reproduce under probability sampling — and what is its true base rate?
Q3Do targeted fine-tuning or decoding-time interventions on discovered shortcuts reduce invalid interactions without hurting accuracy?
Q4Does the order-3 budget trade-off generalize beyond DDXPlus's 8-unit structure to cases with 12–20 evidence units?
Q5Could the diagnosis-relative role taxonomy be automated via a clinical-role LLM, extending fine-grained claims to narrative datasets?
Q6Do the confidence-amplification shortcuts (e.g. disease-name family-history cues) persist under instruction-tuning or RLHF, or are they base-model artifacts?
