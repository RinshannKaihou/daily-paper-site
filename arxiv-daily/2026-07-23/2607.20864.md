> **一句话总结：** 作者开源了 `inspect_permute`（基于 inspect_ai 的穷举置换诊断工具），通过 4 个厂商 × 5 个 MMLU 学科共 24,000 次 temperature-0 API 调用，发现位置偏置只有在 60–95% 基线准确率的"Goldilocks 区"内才能被统计检测到，并把可检测单元清晰分离为 `processing_load`（单调 A→D 下降）与 `content_ambiguity`（非单调 D 位下跌）两种机制；当前所有前沿模型在标准 MMLU 上都落在检测带之上，所以"无偏置"信号应被解读为"仪器测不出"而非"模型无偏置"。

> **TL;DR:** This single-author paper releases `inspect_permute`, an MIT-licensed drop-in extension to the `inspect_ai` framework that exhaustively permutes MCQ answer order (all 24 orderings for k=4) and reports chi-squared / Cramér's V with bootstrap CIs and a Spearman-ρ shape classifier. Across a pre-registered (public SHA-256 hash) sweep of 4 vendors (gpt-4o-mini, claude-haiku-4-5, gemini-2.5-flash, grok-3) × 5 MMLU subjects × 50 questions × 24 permutations = 24,000 calls at temperature 0, position bias is detectable only inside a ~60–95% base-accuracy "Goldilocks zone." gpt-4o-mini shows monotone A→D `processing_load` bias (V up to 0.18 vs ≤0.05 inactive baseline on gemini/grok), haiku shows non-monotone `content_ambiguity` D-drop on two subjects, and the two flagship-tier models (gemini-2.5-flash, grok-3) sit above 95% accuracy everywhere, making their "no bias" verdict unverifiable on MMLU rather than evidence of unbiasedness.

## 核心贡献

- **开源诊断工具 `inspect_permute`**：作为 `inspect_ai` 框架的严格 drop-in 扩展，对每道 k=4 选择题枚举全部 24 种答案顺序，记录 per-run 位置元数据，并产出卡方 / Cramér's V 报告 + Spearman ρ 形状诊断 + bootstrap 95% CI。MIT 许可，31 个单元测试、99% 行覆盖。
- **首次跨厂商跨学科的穷举置换扫描**：4 厂商 × 5 MMLU 学科 × 50 题 × 24 置换 = 24,000 次 temperature-0 调用，是首个以穷举置换作为主测量工具的矩阵式实证研究。
- **轻量级预注册协议**：通过 git tag + 文件 SHA-256 + LinkedIn 公开时间戳锚点，在观测到一半数据前公开锁定证伪预测，任何研究者无需注册报告期刊即可复现验证链。
- **Goldilocks 区与两机制理论模型**：发现位置偏置仅在 ~60–95% 基线准确率带内可被统计检测；该带外要么被处理负荷主导（低端），要么被天花板效应压缩（高端）。带内单元分离为两种机制——单调 `processing_load`（低端模型）与非单调 `content_ambiguity`（窄能力带的本征噪声学科）。
- **诊断自带的"自我修正"贡献**：sweep 暴露出 v0.1 分类器（仅 V-floor 规则）在 grok-3 × elementary_mathematics 单元上的盲区，作者公开披露并加入了卡方 p 值门控作为修正分类器，旧规则在包中保留为 `classify_preregistered`。
- **从"扫描"延伸出的生态系统观察**：报告了 thinking-trace 厂商（gemini-2.5-flash）相对 gpt-4o-mini 的 7.5× 平均、18.5× 峰值壁钟时间不对称，把成本不对称定位为 eval 生态系统级的结构性问题（独立评估者难以复现），并提出 `diagnose_band` 校准侦察协议（约 50 次调用、~1% 全扫描成本）作为补救。

## Key Contributions

- **`inspect_permute` open-source package** — a strict drop-in extension to `inspect_ai` that runs exhaustive (or sampled) k! answer-order permutations per question, writes per-run position metadata via a custom scorer, and produces a chi-squared / Cramér's V report with bootstrap CIs plus a Spearman-ρ shape classifier. MIT-licensed, 31 unit tests including a hand-derived numerical reference and adversarial edge cases, 99% line coverage.
- **First cross-vendor, cross-subject exhaustive-permutation sweep** — 4 vendors × 5 MMLU subjects × 50 questions × 24 permutations = 24,000 temperature-0 API calls, the first matrix-shaped empirical study to use exhaustive permutation as the primary instrument rather than single-shuffle or pairwise comparison.
- **Lightweight pre-registration protocol** — falsifier predictions and per-cell expected V ranges committed to a git tag (commit ad33083), with the file's SHA-256 hash posted publicly on LinkedIn (2026-06-30 16:28 JST) before the second half of the data (Google, xAI cells) was observed. Verifiable by any third party in three shell commands without a registered-report venue.
- **Goldilocks-zone finding** — position bias is statistically detectable only within a roughly 60–95% base-accuracy band. Below it (predicted, not directly observed) processing-load dominance swamps subject-specific signal; above it, ceiling effects compress variance below chi-squared resolution. Every frontier-tier cell in the sweep sits above the band, so "no signal" must be read as "not measurable on this instrument," not "model is unbiased."
- **Two-mechanism classification** — detectable cells separate into `processing_load` (monotone A→D accuracy decrease, Spearman ρ → −1, found in all four active gpt-4o-mini cells) and `content_ambiguity` (non-monotone D-drop with ρ ≈ −0.4, found in both active haiku cells), distinguishable only via the V × ρ plane and invisible to a chi-squared p-value alone.
- **Honest methodological self-correction** — the sweep itself exposed a blind spot in the v0.1 classifier (the grok-3 × elementary_mathematics boundary case at V=0.058, p=0.26). The author discloses this rather than silently substituting a refined classifier that adds an explicit p-value gate, and preserves both classifiers in the package so the pre-registration hit rate is scored against the original rule.
- **Cost-asymmetry observation and `diagnose_band` scout** — same protocol costs ~7.5× (peak 18.5×) more wall-clock on gemini-2.5-flash than gpt-4o-mini due to reasoning-token generation. The author proposes a 50-call scout protocol (identity + reverse permutation on 25 questions, ~1% of full sweep) to classify cells into high/middle/low bands before committing to an expensive sweep.

## Methodology

The diagnostic reframes position-bias measurement as an instrument problem. Instead of a single shuffle per question (which confounds bias signal with content noise and sampling stochasticity), `inspect_permute` runs **exhaustive permutation**: for a k-option question, all k! orderings are presented to the same model. For MMLU's k=4, that means 24 calls per question, exactly k! times a single-shuffle protocol.

Implementation is built around four objects inside `inspect_ai`'s Task/Solver/Scorer abstractions:

1. **`permute_choices` solver** — replaces the standard `multiple_choice` solver. In exhaustive mode the permutation index is `(epoch - 1) % k!`, so requesting `Epochs(k!)` yields every permutation exactly once. In sample mode the index is a deterministic function of `(seed, sample_id, epoch)` via Python's `Random`. The solver preserves `inspect_ai`'s `original_position` invariant, so the framework's built-in unshuffling still works and the eval log reconstructs the permutation without storing it explicitly.
2. **`position_bias_score` scorer** — records five per-run fields: presented position of the correct answer, presented position the model picked, the permutation vector, epoch index, and number of options.
3. **`generate_bias_report`** — produces the contingency table over (correct_position × correct/incorrect), Pearson chi-squared statistic + p-value, Cramér's V = sqrt(χ²/N), per-position accuracy and pick distributions. Total: returns χ²=0, p=1, V=0 on degenerate tables instead of raising.
4. **`shape_diagnostics`** — adds Spearman ρ between presented position and accuracy at that position, 1000-resample bootstrap 95% CIs on both V and ρ, and the mechanism classifier.

**Why Cramér's V, not just p:** the chi-squared statistic scales with N, so a marginal effect on a large eval set produces the same p-value as a large effect on a small one. V is sample-size-invariant and lets cells with different N be compared. The author deliberately skips the Yates continuity correction because its conservative bias is substantial on k=4 tables.

**Why Spearman ρ, not just V:** the chi-squared test reports *whether* bias is present, not *what kind*. Two mechanisms — monotone early-position preference vs non-monotone last-position avoidance — produce the same chi-squared signature but differ in shape. ρ → −1 indicates monotone `processing_load`; ρ near 0 with a final-position drop indicates `content_ambiguity`. ρ is used descriptively (with k=4 positions the Spearman test cannot reach significance), so the bootstrap CI is an honest precision statement, not a hypothesis test.

**Mechanism classifier (refined):** `inactive` if V < 0.05 OR p ≥ 0.05; `processing_load` if V ≥ 0.05 AND ρ ≤ −0.5 AND p < 0.05; `content_ambiguity` otherwise. The pre-registered v0.1 rule (no p-gate) is preserved as `classify_preregistered` for reproducibility.

**Sweep protocol:** 4 × 5 × 50 × 24 = 24,000 calls. Temperature 0 everywhere supported. `max_connections=10`, `retry_on_error=2`. Falsifier predictions and per-cell expected V ranges locked into `preregistration.md`, whose SHA-256 was published on LinkedIn before the Google and xAI cells completed (matrix snapshot at the hash-time commit shows only 10 of 20 cells observed).

**Conceptual frame (with explicit caveats):** the author borrows item-discriminability vocabulary from Item Response Theory [Lord 1952, Birnbaum 1968] to name the phenomenon — a measurement instrument that fails to discriminate at either end of the latent scale — while explicitly stating the IRT formal apparatus does not transfer to LLMs (no stable latent trait; position cues enter via attention weights, not via probabilistic response distributions over a latent ability).

## Results & Highlights

- **Headline matrix (4 vendors × 5 MMLU subjects, n=1200 runs per cell):** gpt-4o-mini shows significant position bias on 4/5 subjects with V from 0.10 (professional_medicine) to 0.18 (elementary_mathematics and formal_logic); claude-haiku-4-5 shows bias on 2/5 subjects at V=0.10 (elementary_mathematics) and V=0.09 (philosophy); gemini-2.5-flash shows V ≤ 0.05 with all p > 0.45 on every subject; grok-3 shows V ≤ 0.06 on every subject. The strongest detected bias (gpt-4o-mini V=0.18) is ~3.6× the inactive floor of 0.05.
- **Within-cell position spread (gpt-4o-mini × formal_logic):** accuracy falls monotonically from 0.73 at position A to 0.50 at position D — a 23-point gap inside a single question under different orderings of identical content, with Spearman ρ = −1.0 in all four active gpt-4o-mini cells.
- **Two-mechanism separation in the V × ρ plane:** the four `processing_load` cells (all gpt-4o-mini) cluster at V ≥ 0.05, ρ = −1.0; the two `content_ambiguity` cells (haiku × elementary, haiku × philosophy) cluster at V ≈ 0.09–0.10, ρ = −0.40 with near-ceiling accuracy (0.94–0.99) at positions A–C and a 3–6 point drop at D specifically.
- **Goldilocks zone:** every cell with detected bias lies inside the 0.60–0.95 base-accuracy band. Twelve cells with accuracy ≥ 0.95 cluster below V=0.05. Inside the band, ten cells span V=0.02 to V=0.18 with a non-monotonic accuracy-bias relationship (gpt-4o-mini × formal_logic at 0.61 accuracy and gpt-4o-mini × elementary_math at 0.81 accuracy both reach V=0.18).
- **Pre-registered falsifier outcomes:** Falsifier 1 (college_math null across all vendors: V < 0.10 AND p > 0.05) — CONFIRMED (observed V = 0.08, 0.03, 0.02, 0.02 with p = 0.057, 0.83, 0.80, 0.80). Falsifier 3 (flagship ceiling on elementary_mathematics: V < 0.10) — CONFIRMED (V = 0.05 for gemini, V = 0.06 for grok). Falsifier 2 (philosophy non-monotone for flagships) — INCONCLUSIVE (no flagship bias detected, V = 0.02 and V = 0.05). Per-cell prediction hit rate on the 10 cells unobserved at hash time: 6/10 on V range, 5/10 on mechanism class.
- **Robustness under credit interruption (Section 4.7):** after re-running all five anthropic cells against a credit-positive account, max |V_v2 − V_v1| = 0.0058 (formal_logic); philosophy agrees to four decimals (V = 0.0909 in both); per-position accuracy agrees within 0.003. Measurement integrity preserved.
- **Wall-clock cost asymmetry (Section 4.8):** per-cell averages — gpt-4o-mini ~2.0 min, claude-haiku-4-5 ~4.3 min, gemini-2.5-flash ~15 min (range 5–37 min), grok-3 ~5 min. For gemini × college_math 85% of total tokens were reasoning tokens vs 0% for gpt-4o-mini and haiku. Peak asymmetry 18.5× (gemini college_math vs openai college_math); average 7.5×.
- **Classifier threshold sensitivity (Appendix B):** across a 3×3 sweep of V_floor ∈ {0.04, 0.05, 0.06} and ρ_processing ∈ {−0.4, −0.5, −0.6}, 19 of 20 cells keep the same label; the only flip is the grok-3 × elementary boundary case, which the refined p-gate classifier relegates to inactive in every combination.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** Genuinely methodological. Exhaustive answer-order permutation is not new as a concept, but packaging it as a strict drop-in to `inspect_ai` with reproducibility-tested V/ρ/bootstrap reporting is a real infrastructure contribution that did not exist before. The Goldilocks-zone framing — that bias detectability is a property of the (model, benchmark) pair, not of the model — is a substantive empirical reframing, even though it leans on a well-known IRT structural parallel that the author is careful not to overclaim.
- **Rigor:** High on process. Pre-registration with public hash anchor before half the data was observed, per-cell prediction hit rate reported without retroactive improvement (6/10 V-range, 5/10 mechanism class), disclosed classifier refinement driven by the sweep itself, and an Appendix-B sensitivity sweep over thresholds. Weak on sample size: **n=50 questions per subject** is small for cell-level chi-squared inference on 4×2 contingency tables (n=1200 runs per cell but only 50 underlying items × 24 permutations), which inflates the leverage of any single anomalous question and bounds the precision of the V estimates the whole framework rests on.
- **Reproducibility:** Strongest aspect. Temperature 0, deterministic (seed, sample_id, epoch) permutation mapping, eval logs preserved, git-tagged data snapshot, 31 unit tests with 99% line coverage, an actual re-run of the contaminated Anthropic cells quantifying residual nondeterminism (max |ΔV| = 0.0058). The verification chain (git clone + sha256sum + matrix.json read) is genuinely executable in under a minute.
- **Significance:** The central claim — "absence of bias signal on MMLU for frontier-tier models is a measurement-resolution artifact, not evidence of unbiasedness" — is important and well-argued, and directly undercuts a common vendor-claim pattern. But the empirical scope is narrow: **only 5 MMLU subjects, one benchmark family, four vendors, n=50 per subject**. The Goldilocks band thresholds (60%, 95%) are derived from this single matrix and are explicitly not validated on BBH, AGIEval, GPQA, FrontierMath, or TruthfulQA.
- **Red flags:**
  - The headline "every frontier-tier model sits above the detection band on standard MMLU" rests on only two flagship cells (gemini-2.5-flash, grok-3) — neither is GPT-5 / Claude Opus 4.8 / Gemini 3 Pro / Grok 4, which the paper itself names as the real targets in the conclusion. The framework's central question is acknowledged as still open.
  - The floor edge of the Goldilocks zone (below 0.60 accuracy) is **predicted by extrapolation but not directly observed**; the lowest base accuracy in the sweep is 0.61. The "three-band model" is therefore partly hypothetical on the low end.
  - The reasoning-trace vendors' zero detected bias could equally be (i) genuine mitigation, (ii) ceiling saturation, or (iii) "bias laundering" where the thinking trace carries the bias but the surface output overrides it. The paper is honest that it cannot distinguish these without trace access, but the marketing-friendly reading ("flagship models show no bias") is exactly the misuse the paper warns against, and the paper's own headline matrix can be read that way at a glance.
  - The classification thresholds (V_floor=0.05, ρ_processing=−0.5, α=0.05) are acknowledged as "conventional rather than derived" — the sensitivity sweep helps but is narrow (3×3) and the α=0.10 perturbation would flip gpt-4o-mini × college_math (V=0.079, p=0.057) into active.
  - A LinkedIn post as the public pre-registration anchor is non-standard; the author mitigates this by making the protocol platform-agnostic, but readers from a strict registered-report tradition may discount it.

## Strengths

- Pre-registration is genuinely verifiable, not theatrical: public hash before half the data observed, per-cell predictions scored unmodified against the v0.1 classifier even when the refined classifier would have improved the hit rate.
- The methodological self-correction (Section 3.3) is unusually honest — the boundary case that broke the v0.1 rule is named, the fix is presented as a separate deliverable, and the older rule is preserved for reproducibility.
- The instrument-vs-property distinction ("is the benchmark exposing bias, or is the model biased?") cuts through a genuine ambiguity in the existing position-bias literature that relies on single-shuffle protocols.
- Reproducibility infrastructure (eval logs, git tags, unit tests, sensitivity appendix) exceeds the norm for single-author preprints.
- The cost-asymmetry observation (7.5× average, 18.5× peak wall-clock on thinking-trace vendors) is a concrete, measured finding with real implications for independent evaluation capacity, not a vague complaint.

## Limitations

- **Benchmark scope:** only 5 MMLU subjects, one benchmark family. Whether the Goldilocks thresholds transfer to BBH, AGIEval, GPQA, FrontierMath, TruthfulQA is an open question the paper explicitly names.
- **Per-subject sample size:** n=50 questions per subject is small; cell-level chi-squared inference rests on 1200 runs but only 50 distinct items, bounding achievable precision.
- **No frontier-tier model in the detection band:** the paper's central target (GPT-5, Opus 4.8, Gemini 3 Pro, Grok 4) is not measured; the "above-the-band" conclusion is demonstrated only for two thinking-trace vendors.
- **Floor edge unobserved:** the lowest-accuracy prediction (below 0.60) is extrapolation, not measurement.
- **Three-band model is post-hoc:** the Goldilocks framing and the band thresholds emerged from post-hoc inspection and are labeled as such, but readers may still over-weight them as confirmed.
- **Reasoning-trace opacity:** cannot distinguish genuine bias mitigation from ceiling saturation or "bias laundering" without reasoning-trace access.
- **Self-citation dependency:** the bracketing argument (with arXiv:2606.26185) and the eval-ecosystem argument (Tamba 2026b, under review) rest on the same author's adjacent work.
- **Classifier thresholds are conventional:** V_floor=0.05, ρ_processing=−0.5, α=0.05 are not theory-derived; the 3×3 sensitivity sweep is narrow and the α=0.10 case would flip one borderline cell.

## Relevance

对评估 LLM 在多选基准上的位置偏置、benchmark 校准与"无偏置声明"可信度评判感兴趣的研究者，本文提供了一个可直接 drop-in 的开源工具（`inspect_permute`）与一套可验证的预注册协议，并定量揭示了"看似无偏置"在标准 MMLU 上往往只是测量仪器分辨率不足而非模型真正无偏置，对设计严谨的 LLM 评测与判读厂商评测声明都有直接参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the Goldilocks zone (60–95% base accuracy) and its two-mechanism structure replicate on non-MMLU benchmarks (BBH, AGIEval, GPQA Diamond, FrontierMath, TruthfulQA), particularly on benchmarks engineered to place frontier-tier models inside the band?
- With n=50 questions per subject, how stable are the cell-level V estimates under bootstrap-over-items (resampling questions rather than runs)? Does the small item count inflate the leverage of any single anomalous question?
- For thinking-trace vendors (gemini-2.5-flash, grok-3), can a `position_bias_score` variant that ingests reasoning-trace metadata distinguish genuine bias mitigation from ceiling saturation or "bias laundering"?
- How does the wall-clock cost asymmetry (7.5× average, 18.5× peak) scale as reasoning-trace lengths grow, and what does this imply for the minimum institutional resources required for independent pre-deployment evaluation?
- Can the `diagnose_band` scout (50-call identity+reverse protocol) be validated as a reliable band classifier against a denser (subject, accuracy) grid before being used to skip expensive full sweeps?
- Does the post-hoc three-band capability model survive a pre-registered replication on a held-out vendor × subject matrix, including cells below 0.60 accuracy that the present sweep never observed?
