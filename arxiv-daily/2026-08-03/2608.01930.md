> **一句话总结：** 作者通过匹配反事实框架在 16 个 VLM 上证明,视觉自反思失败的根因是模型复用了先前 CoT 中承载证据的"文本捷径",并提出训练-free 的 Fresh-State Attention Firewall (FSAF),将 5 个 Qwen VLM 的视觉更新率从 35.28% 提到 53.61%,先前答案率从 39.22% 降到 3.67%。

> **TL;DR:** Across 16 VLMs on the VS-Bench counterfactual benchmark, the paper shows that evidence-bearing content in a prior chain-of-thought acts as a reusable "textual shortcut" that competes with fresh visual recomputation; removing this evidence shifts the answer-preference margin by +2.010 (positive in 16/16 models) and lowers prior-answer rate by 62.58 pp versus the length-matched control, while a proposed training-free Fresh-State Attention Firewall (FSAF) raises visual update rate from 35.28% to 53.61% and cuts prior-answer rate from 39.22% to 3.67% on five Qwen VLMs.

## 核心贡献

- **诊断出"文本捷径"机制**:首次定位视觉自反思失败的根因——先前 CoT 中承载证据(数值、关系、推导)的内容构成一条可复用的文本路径,与基于当前图像的重新计算形成竞争关系,而非简单的"视觉注意力不足"。
- **匹配反事实评估框架**:在 800 对人工校验的 VS-Bench 图像对(来自 MathVista-MINI、MathVerse-MINI、MathVision、MMMU-Pro)上,通过保持任务固定、对比"无历史直接推理"与"带先前 CoT 反思"两条路径,引入 VUR、PAR 和教师强制答案偏好 margin 等可解释指标。
- **内容特异性的因果证据**:在 16 个 VLM 上,移除证据性内容(E)相对长度匹配的非证据上下文(C)的 margin 变化为 +2.010,16/16 模型均为正;且随移除比例从 0% 到 100% 单调增强,打断证据顺序也削弱了捷径控制(14/16 模型为正)。
- **"答对 ≠ 捷径退役"**:通过匹配支持撤回(matched support withdrawal)证明,即使在已"恢复"的样本上,撤回当前视觉支持后先前答案率仍从 1.34% 跳到 69.01%(直接路径仅 7.17%),Extra Old Lift 达 +53.38 pp,说明回答正确可能只是当前证据的临时覆盖。
- **提出 FSAF 干预方法**:一个训练-free、单次前向传播的注意力防火墙,通过新的 user-assistant 边界和注意力掩码,在 prefill 和生成阶段都阻止新计算访问先前 CoT,无需目标答案或证据解析器。

## Key Contributions

- **Identifies the "textual shortcut" mechanism**: reframes VLM self-reflection failure not as insufficient visual attention but as a competition between fresh visual recomputation and a reusable evidence-bearing path inside the prior CoT.
- **Matched counterfactual evaluation framework**: 800 human-checked VS-Bench pairs (drawn equally from MathVista-MINI, MathVerse-MINI, MathVision, MMMU-Pro) where each question is paired with two structurally similar images implying different answers, enabling behavioral (VUR, PAR) and teacher-forced answer-preference (margin) readouts.
- **Content-specific causal isolation**: removing evidence-bearing spans shifts the answer-preference margin by +2.010 over the length-matched non-evidence control with a positive effect in 16/16 models; the effect is graded with retained stale evidence and is disrupted by evidence reordering (positive in 14/16).
- **"Correctness is not shortcut retirement"**: a matched support-withdrawal stress test shows that even on recovered samples, withdrawing current-image support raises prior-answer rate from 1.34% to 69.01% on the history path (only 7.17% on the matched direct path), yielding an Extra Old Lift of +53.38 pp — meaning a correct reflected answer can coexist with detectable prior-history dependence.
- **FSAF training-free intervention**: a single-pass attention firewall that couples a fresh user-assistant boundary with a prefill- and generation-time mask preventing fresh-suffix queries from attending to the stale assistant span, requiring neither target-answer access nor an evidence parser.

## Methodology

The paper builds a **matched counterfactual framework** to trace prior-CoT influence. For each current task T=(I,Q,y), it constructs a counterfactual T⁻=(I⁻,Q,y⁻) sharing the question and answer space but changing the task-relevant visual evidence (y⁻≠y). The evaluated model M first produces a coherent incorrect prior CoT R⁻ from I⁻ (Eq. 1); eligibility requires ŷ⁻=y⁻ and direct inference on I returns y, ensuring R⁻ coherently supports y⁻ under I⁻ but conflicts with y under I.

The contrast compares (i) history-free direct inference (Eq. 2) against (ii) prior-conditioned reflection with a fixed reflection prefix (Eq. 3), holding I and Q fixed. To localize the carrier of prior control, R⁻ is decomposed by textual role: E⁻ (evidence-bearing observations, quantities, relations, deductions), A⁻ (explicit final-answer span), and the remainder (non-evidence context). Four conditions are compared at fixed model/image/question/prompt: intact prior (N), evidence removal (E), length-matched non-evidence control (C), and answer-span removal (A), reporting margin contrasts Δm_{X−Y} and analogous VUR/PAR contrasts.

Behavior is measured via free generation using **VUR** (visual update rate, agreement with current answer y), **PAR** (prior-answer rate, agreement with y⁻), and an "Other" residual, judged by Qwen3-VL-235B-A22B-Instruct. Answer preference is measured via teacher-forced mean-token log-probability scoring (Eq. 5) and the margin m(S)=ℓ(y|S)−ℓ(y⁻|S) (Eq. 6), which is judge-independent. Two evidence-preserving continuations (equivalent re-query, derived computation) further separate recurrence from premise transfer.

The intervention, **Fresh-State Attention Firewall (FSAF)**, appends a fixed user re-query asking the model to answer Q again using only the current image, then applies an attention mask (Eq. 8–9) that sets attention logits to −∞ for fresh-suffix queries against stale CoT keys at every language layer, during both prefill and generation, while leaving all other causal edges intact (the fresh suffix can still attend to I, Q, system tokens, U_f, and its own earlier tokens).

Evaluation covers 16 VLMs (Qwen2.5/3-VL, Qwen3.5/3.6, InternVL2-8B, Gemma-4-12B/31B base & IT, Kimi-VL-A3B Instruct & Thinking) on 800 VS-Bench pairs, with FSAF evaluated on five Qwen models restricted to pairs both correctly answerable under direct inference.

## Results & Highlights

- **Prior CoT controls reflection even without the current image** (Table 1): on the Q+R⁻, no-I condition, VUR is only 0.6%–3.5% while PAR reaches 53.3%–97.2% across the three representative model families, confirming R⁻ alone sustains prior-answer control; restoring I shifts predictions toward the current answer heterogeneously (VUR 22.6%–88.0%, PAR 4.4%–53.9%).
- **Evidence-bearing content is the robust cross-model carrier** (Table 2): removing E⁻ shifts the answer-preference margin by +2.069 over intact (N) and +2.010 over the length-matched non-evidence control (C), with a positive E-minus-C effect in all 16/16 models; in free generation, ΔVUR_{E−C}=+43.76 pp and ΔPAR_{E−C}=−62.58 pp (positive VUR effect in 16/16), whereas removing only the final-answer span A⁻ yields a much smaller model-equal shift of 0.074 with heterogeneous model-level effects.
- **Graded evidence effect** (Fig. 3): as evidence removal increases from 0% to 100%, the proportion of samples with m(S_α)>0 increases monotonically at the model level in all 16 VLMs.
- **Evidence organization matters** (Table 3): reversing the evidence-bearing sentences shifts the margin by +0.449 over the intact prior and produces an additional +0.317 over a non-evidence-only reorder (positive in 14/16 models), with free-generation VUR +5.84 pp and PAR −7.55 pp.
- **Correctness does not certify retirement** (Table 4): under matched support withdrawal, aggregate PAR rises from 9.81% to 69.01% on the history path but only from 1.34% to 7.17% on the matched direct path, giving an Extra Old Lift of +53.38 pp (model-equal 49.74 pp, positive in 16/16).
- **State-dependent residual influence** (Table 5): for active-stale outcomes the equivalent re-query yields Δm=−3.896 with PAR +35.05 pp, and derived-computation prior-target generation rises by +45.28 pp; for recovered outcomes the effects are much weaker (Δm=−0.547 and −0.391), showing premise transfer is concentrated where the shortcut remains active.
- **Robust to construction choices** (Table 6): across ten semantically matched reflection-prompt paraphrases the standard deviations are at most 3.1 pp (VUR) and 3.0 pp (PAR); replacing the reflection prefix with a neutral continuation changes VUR/PAR by at most 0.8 pp; naturally occurring errors (no image swap) reduce correction rates by 18.2–26.4 pp when the erroneous CoT is retained versus the matched prompt-only condition.
- **FSAF consistently improves visual updating** (Table 7): pooled over five Qwen VLM model–sample pairs, VUR rises from 35.28% to 53.61% (+18.33 pp), PAR drops from 39.22% to 3.67% (−35.56 pp), and the answer-preference margin shifts from −5.49 to +2.97 (+8.46), with improvements on every model and readout; the largest PAR collapses are on Thinking variants (Qwen3-VL-8B-T: 56.17% → 1.03%; Qwen3-VL-32B-T: 47.21% → 1.64%).
- **A prompt-only re-query variant provides no reliable VUR gain while increasing PAR**, isolating attention control (not merely asking the model to look again) as the active ingredient of FSAF.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: genuinely useful reframing. The contribution is the isolation of an *evidence-bearing* shortcut (via the E vs. C length-matched control) inside prior CoT, distinct from generic context persistence or final-answer anchoring — the +2.010 E-minus-C margin effect with 16/16 sign consistency is a clean result that prior work on visual re-attention did not establish.
- **Rigor**: high. The matched counterfactual design, paired (within-sample) estimands, teacher-forced margin that is independent of the semantic judge, and ten-paraphrase / neutral-prefix robustness checks address the most obvious confounds. The honesty about "Other" rate rising from 8.35% to 26.50% under evidence removal (i.e., weakening stale control does not guarantee recovery) is a notable sign of intellectual integrity.
- **Reproducibility**: strong on the diagnostic side — public checkpoints, recorded provenance, deterministic seeds, fixed decoding within pairs, and full protocol details in nine appendices. The FSAF implementation relies on Transformers eager-attention hooks (Appendix F.2) and exact re-query text, which should be reproducible from the paper.
- **Significance**: the diagnosis is broadly applicable (16 VLMs), but the *mitigation* (FSAF) is evaluated on a much narrower surface — only five Qwen models, only VS-Bench, only pairs both correctly answerable under direct inference. The 35.28%→53.61% VUR improvement is meaningful but still leaves nearly half of eligible pairs un-updated, and the authors concede FSAF "does not guarantee current-answer recovery on every pair."
- **Red flags / concerns**: (1) **No explicit Limitations section** — limitations must be inferred from caveat sentences ("does not by itself guarantee recovery", "descriptive, not paired sample-level scaling estimates"). (2) **Single benchmark dependence**: the entire diagnosis rests on VS-Bench's 800 pairs, all math/visual-reasoning oriented; whether the textual-shortcut phenomenon generalizes to non-math VQA, OCR, or real-world image-editing reflection is untested. (3) **FSAF generalization is unproven**: only Qwen models are evaluated for FSAF, despite 16 models being diagnosed; the attention-mask approach assumes the implementer can identify the stale assistant span exactly, which may be brittle in multi-turn agentic settings with tool calls. (4) **Operational E⁻ identification is rule-based** (numbers, symbols, a fixed visual lexicon of ~30 terms — Appendix C.3), which is reasonable for math-style VS-Bench but would underperform on free-form visual reasoning; the paper does not ablate this rule set against an alternative parser. (5) The baseline "ordinary prior-conditioned reflection" already uses a fixed reflection prefix whose 49.1% VUR (Qwen3-VL-8B) suggests substantial room, so FSAF's gains are partly relative to a weak baseline rather than the strongest known visual-re-attention method (e.g., it is not compared against Jian et al. 2025 "Look Again, Think Slowly" or Yang et al. 2026 "Look-Back" as actual interventions, only cited).

## Strengths

- Clean, falsifiable central claim with a length-matched control (E vs. C) that isolates evidence content from generic context length.
- Cross-model breadth (16 VLMs, four families, Base/Instruct/Thinking) for the diagnostic portion, with sign-consistent effects.
- The "correctness ≠ retirement" stress test (matched support withdrawal, Extra Old Lift) is a genuinely novel diagnostic that reframes how the field should evaluate self-reflection.
- FSAF is training-free, single-pass, requires no evidence parser or target-answer access, and produces large PAR reductions (down to ~1–2% on Thinking variants).
- Strong reproducibility hygiene: public checkpoints, deterministic seeds, judge-independent teacher-forced scoring, recorded provenance, coverage audits.

## Limitations

- No explicit Limitations section; caveats are scattered.
- FSAF is evaluated on only five Qwen models on a single benchmark (VS-Bench); generalization to other families and non-math VQA is untested.
- FSAF is restricted to pairs both correctly answerable under direct inference, a filtering step that may inflate apparent gains relative to the unfiltered diagnostic cohort.
- The rule-based evidence-span identifier is tailored to math/diagram content and is not ablated against alternatives.
- FSAF is not compared head-to-head against recent training-free visual re-attention methods (Look Again, Look-Back, visual contrastive decoding) as actual interventions — only cited.
- Post-FSAF VUR (53.61% average) still leaves roughly half of eligible pairs un-updated; a non-trivial Thinking-mode VUR gap remains even after the firewall (Qwen3-VL-32B-T: 31.8% → 53.77%).
- The framework requires exact identification of the stale assistant span, which may not transfer cleanly to multi-turn agentic or tool-augmented reflection.

## Relevance

对研究 VLM 自反思、思维链忠实性与多模态推理可信度的方向高度相关——它把"为什么 VLM 看了图却不更新"从"视觉注意力不足"重新归因到"文本捷径复用",并提出可即插即用的注意力防火墙 FSAF,直接呼应了视觉 grounding、CoT 因果干预与自校正稳定性等核心议题。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does FSAF generalize beyond Qwen to Gemma-4, InternVL2, and Kimi-VL-A3B, and does the attention-mask assumption hold for models with different positional/tokenization schemes?
- How does the textual-shortcut effect behave on non-math VQA (e.g., real-world scene editing, OCR, chart QA) where the rule-based evidence-span identifier may fail?
- Can FSAF be combined with visual re-attention training (Look Again, Look-Back) for compounding gains, or do they target overlapping failure modes?
- Does the "Extra Old Lift" stress test predict downstream behavior in multi-turn agentic settings where stale premises could propagate across tool calls?
- What is the minimal sufficient evidence-span definition — would a learned, outcome-independent parser change the magnitude of the E-minus-C margin effect?
- The "Other" rate triples (8.35%→26.50%) under evidence removal: are these genuine abstentions, format failures, or a third answer class worth characterizing?
