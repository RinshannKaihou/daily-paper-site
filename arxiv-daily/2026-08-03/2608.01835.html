02Results & Highlights
Sparse Scaffolds Suffice

20–61 BACs predict repetition across 23 models

On-policy classification accuracy reaches 0.9658–0.9879 on real BACs vs. random-coordinate controls at 0.367 (ACT) / 0.317 (NOC).

Cross-Architecture Core

NOC exposes a more architecture-robust core

Primary CCA across all 23 models: NOC 0.974 vs ACT 0.938; NOC top-2-PC transfer 0.958 within-family, 0.950 cross-family.

Behavior Moves, Chart Doesn't

DPO shifts behavior at near-1.0 overlap

DPO moves repetition rate to 0.918/0.885 (up/down) with overlap 0.998–0.999 and Fisher retention ≈ 1.0 — reweighting, not rewriting.

Causally Validated

Steering moves repetition 0.78 → 0.93 / 0.40

Subspace & BAC-level intervention over α ∈ [−2, 2] shifts repetition while PPL stays in a controlled band — the chart is functional, not just predictive.

03Strengths & Limitations

+ Strengths

  • Unified geometric object tracked through post-training — directly addresses ambiguity in interpreting behavior-rate changes.
  • ACT/NOC split cleanly separates "frequently active" from "functionally causal," resolving confounds in pure activation interpretability.
  • 23-model cross-family atlas plus controlled single-base trajectories — both ecological breadth and causal-style rigor.
  • Honest boundaries: explicit leakage controls, flagging of occupancy/gain noise, and archived-pipeline limitations.

− Limitations

  • Only two behaviors (repetition, sycophancy); reasoning, hallucination, toxicity unproven.
  • "Reward optimization" = DPO only — no RLHF/PPO, no RLVR, despite the broad title.
  • Single base model (Llama-3.1-8B) for controlled claims; cross-family rests on confounded public pairs.
  • Local linear charts — dose-response saturates and mildly reverses at high steering strength.
  • Sycophancy dataset has a flagged prompt-level contamination; archived artifacts lack per-example logs.
04Open Questions
Q1
Does the asymmetry survive true RLHF (PPO + learned reward) or RLVR with verifiable rewards, rather than DPO?
Q2
Can the framework scale to hallucination, chain-of-thought style, refusal, or toxicity — and do those admit sparse low-rank charts too?
Q3
Can a "chart-regularized" DPO loss (penalizing projector-overlap drift) recover base geometry while still changing behavior?
Q4
Could the ACT/NOC four-quadrant taxonomy diagnose "latent but strong" (NOC-high, ACT-low) behaviors at training time — pre-deployment risks?
