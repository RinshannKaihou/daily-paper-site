01

Methodology

Decompose the residual as x = r·u with u ∈ S^{d−1}. Projecting the continuous-depth residual flow onto the tangent space gives a sphere-valued ODE in which attention and FFN play distinct dynamical roles:

u̇_i = (1/s_i(t)) · P_{u_i} [ A_i(U) + Φ_i(u_i) ] (1)

Here A_i(U) is the non-local attention aggregation field and Φ_i(u_i) is the local, position-wise FFN steering field. Setting Φ = 0 recovers prior attention-only particle dynamics (Geshkovski et al.; Karagodin et al.). The theory covers any Lipschitz FFN, including ReLU and SwiGLU, while experiments use real checkpoint MLP/SwiGLU modules.

The practical parallelization hinges on the sequential-to-parallel defect: replacing x → x + A(x) + Φ(x + A(x)) with x → x + A(x) + Φ(x). Proposition 2 shows the leading defect is τ²·DΦ(x)·A(x), motivating the per-layer FFN input-sensitivity score:

C_ℓ ≈ ‖Φ_ℓ(x + A_ℓ(x)) − Φ_ℓ(x)‖ / (‖Φ_ℓ(x)‖ + ε) Prop. 2

Small C_ℓ means the FFN barely cares whether attention has already been applied — so the parallel surrogate should be cheap and accurate. Six pretrained models are evaluated on OpenWebText: GPT-2, GPT-2-large, Pythia-410M, Pythia-1.4B, Mistral-7B, Llama-3-8B.

03·04

Strengths & Limitations

Strengths Pros

  • Clean, falsifiable framing. The theory explicitly reduces to prior attention-only dynamics when Φ = 0.
  • Persuasive ablation. Fig. 3 directly tests the angular decomposition; the catastrophic radial-only result (+7.19–12.96) is hard to explain away.
  • C_ℓ is both motivated and actionable. Derived from Prop. 2's leading term, with clear low- vs high-C_ℓ separation in Fig. 5.
  • Architectural breadth. GPT-2, Pythia (parallel-residual), Mistral (SwiGLU), Llama-3-8B — Pythia acts as a built-in control.
  • Honest framing. Diagnostics vs. validations are explicitly distinguished; theory/decoder gap is acknowledged.

Limitations Cons

  • Theory/model mismatch. Mean-field limit covers full attention only; causal decoders are addressed empirically, not by a prefix-indexed limit theorem.
  • Speedups are estimates. Submodule-time, not wall-clock throughput on optimized kernels.
  • Few anchor points. No variance bars, seeds, or calibration sizes; the 0.41 → 0.63 scale trend rests on limited points (Llama-3-8B is the only 8B-class model).
  • Weak baselines. Gate/editing experiments are not benchmarked against MEMIT/ROME or fine-tuning baselines.
  • Loose thresholds. Table 2 saddle labels use a 0.05 zero-score cutoff — local-linearization descriptors, not refined stability classifications.
05

Open Questions

Can a rigorous prefix-indexed (Volterra-type) mean-field limit be proven for causal decoders, closing the gap between theory and the models actually tested?
Does the angular-deficit trend (0.41 → 0.63) continue, saturate, or reverse at 70B+ scale — and is it driven by depth, width, or data?
How does C_ℓ-guided parallelization interact with speculative decoding, quantization, and layer skipping on optimized kernels — what wall-clock gains are real?
Can the tangential-only finding be exploited for compression — pruning or low-rank approximating the radial FFN component — without quality loss?
Does the saddle-dominated FFN critical geometry predict which layers are most editable with ROME/MEMIT, or explain hallucination / prompt-sensitivity modes?
