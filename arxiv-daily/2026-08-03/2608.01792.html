Can You Trust the Confidence? ConfBench for Vision-Language Models on Document Extraction

Priyashree Roy, Sujitha Martin, Mohammad Rostami, Spencer Romo, Renhao Xue, Bob Strahan, Diego A. Socolovsky, Boyi Xie, Md Moflijul Islam — Amazon Web Services
ConfBench vision-language-models confidence-calibration key-information-extraction intelligent-document-processing benchmark
▲ Key Finding

ConfBench is the first calibration-specific benchmark for VLM document KIE — across 7 models, OCR+Image dominates and model capability, not size, governs confidence quality.

Why This Matters

For anyone deploying VLMs for document intelligence, confidence trustworthiness is the gating concern for routing automation vs. human review — i.e. IDP ROI.

Critical Edge

Cross-model comparisons are only partially controlled: 40K vs 8K token budgets and variable-length LineItems — and two headline claims rest on n=2 models.

Methodology01

Benchmark construction. 75 manually verified FCC invoices → 1,346 variants via Augraphy with 20 controlled degradation pipelines (8 archetypes + 12 custom) across three phases.

  • Ink: bleed-through, mottling, low ink, dithering
  • Paper: staining, watermarks, colored paper, moiré, bindings
  • Post: JPEG compression, shadows, lighting, scanner defects, geometric distortion

OCR-SAFE PARAMS

rotation ≤ ±5° · JPEG q 70–95 · brightness 0.7–1.3 · blur ≤ 200px / <8 iters · overlay alpha < 0.3. Filtration drops >80% null-entity docs.

CONFIDENCE ESTIMATION (2 FAMILIES)

  • Verbalized (1S-TopK): single call extracts entities + emits top-4 candidates w/ probabilities; works on any instruction-following VLM.
  • Logprob: greedy T=0 pass; aggregations = first-token, mean-token, first-token margin. Restricted to two self-hostable open models.

FACTORIAL

{Verbalized, Logprob} × {OCR-only, Image-only, OCR+Image} → 6 conditions per model. 7 VLMs: 4 proprietary (Claude Opus 4.6 / Sonnet 4.5 / Haiku 4.5, Kimi K2.5) + 3 open (Qwen 3 VL-235B, Qwen 3.6-27B, Gemma 3-12B).

METRICS

Extraction: WOA, F1. Calibration: AUROC (discrimination), ECE (M=5 quantile bins), Brier, and the new ECARB@b = errors caught at top-k lowest confidence vs. random — operational HITL lift.

MODALITY & STRATEGY

  • OCR+Image uniformly best. Gap vs. Image-only: ~3 pts (Opus) to ~6 pts (Gemma) — weaker models benefit most from OCR overlay.
  • Verbalized vs. logprob is model-dependent. Qwen 3.6-27B: verbalized 0.72 > logprob-first 0.62. Gemma reverses: logprob 0.64 > verbalized 0.58.
  • First-token aggregation consistently beats margin and mean.

Strengths+

  • Fills a genuine gap: first calibration-specific KIE benchmark with full-accuracy-spectrum coverage; all prior benchmarks target clean docs and report accuracy only.
  • Clean factorial design: 2 confidence families × 3 modalities, with within-model token-budget control and four complementary calibration metrics.
  • ECARB is a well-motivated, deployment-relevant metric bridging statistical calibration and HITL economics; largely budget-stable.
  • Above-average intellectual honesty: explicit scope-limiting language, detailed Limitations, public dataset release, refusal to over-claim from n=2.
  • Concrete practitioner guidelines: shortlist on WOA + AUROC, add post-hoc calibration only for threshold routing, read ECARB at your own review budget.

Limitations!

  • Single-domain seed: 75 FCC invoices, one layout family, one language — findings may not transfer to multi-column, handwritten, non-English, or naturally-degraded scans.
  • n=2 for two headline claims: verbalized-vs-logprob (Qwen 3.6-27B, Gemma) and MoE-vs-dense (Kimi K2.5, Qwen 3 VL-235B).
  • Partial cross-model control: 40K vs 8K token budgets + variable-length LineItems → absolute metric values indicative, not matched.
  • No post-hoc calibration experiments (temperature scaling) — deferred to future work; the operationally critical "how much does calibration close the gap?" stays open.
  • Augmentation realism gap: bleed-through, letterpress, fax are implausible for modern FCC invoices; absolute ECE/Brier ≠ expected production calibration.
  • 1S-TopK extraction-confidence coupling slightly contaminates within-model verbalized-vs-logprob WOA comparison.
  • Proprietary model drift: 4 of 7 models are commercial APIs whose behavior can shift between evaluations.
  • Deployment risk: treating high confidence as a substitute for review silently passes through the errors the model is most certain about; recalibrate on your own data.

Open Questions for Further Exploration?

  1. How much of the Opus 4.6 → Gemma 3-12B ECE gap survives a per-model temperature scaling fit on a held-out degradation tier, and does a High-severity-fitted calibrator transfer to the Mild tier?
  2. Does the verbalized-vs-logprob reversal between Qwen 3.6-27B and Gemma 3-12B persist across more open-weight models (Llama 3.x-Vision, Mistral-Doc, Phi-VLM) once token probabilities are exposed?
  3. When ConfBench extends to non-invoice domains (DocILE receipts, multi-column forms, handwritten, non-English), do the OCR+Image dominance and within-Claude monotonic-scaling findings replicate?
  4. Can verbalized and logprob signals be ensembled into one score that strictly dominates both per model, given they draw on different information?
  5. How stable are ECARB rankings across review budgets (5%, 30%, 50%) for models whose reliability diagrams cross y=x non-monotonically (Sonnet 4.5, Haiku 4.5)?
  6. Does naturally-occurring production degradation produce calibration behavior consistent with synthetic Augraphy tiers, or do implausible-but-useful pipelines bias absolute estimates?
