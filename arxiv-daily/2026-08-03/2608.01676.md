> **一句话总结：** 本文提出一套稠密校准的反事实审计协议（Gold/Poison/Benign 三哨兵卡 + 六排列对称 + 同核稠密基线），首次用因果证据证明稀疏注意力会系统性地改变特定内容对模型输出的影响力——压缩比是调节"信号集中"与"整合损失"两种竞争模式的关键控制变量。

> **TL;DR:** The paper introduces a dense-calibrated counterfactual audit protocol (Gold/Poison/Benign sentinel cards under six-layout symmetry with a within-kernel dense baseline) and shows causally that sparse attention changes the behavioral influence of specific content: on SCBench-KV and SciFact across Llama-3.1-8B, Mistral-7B, Qwen2.5-7B, and Qwen3-8B, BSFA route replay flips/increases wrong-answer margins in 13/16 cells, cross-block ablation collapses the probe effect from 4.48 logits to zero, and the calibrated contrast Δ on Qwen3-SCB reverses sign from −0.31 (c=0.25) to +0.93 (c=0.75) — a phenomenon aggregate accuracy cannot detect.

## 核心贡献

- **因果验证稀疏路由改变内容影响（BSFA 路由重放）：** 在四种开源架构（Llama-3.1-8B、Mistral-7B、Qwen2.5-7B、Qwen3-8B）上，强制错误证据路由 vs. 强制金标路由使错误答案相对裕度在 16 个模型-任务-压缩比单元中有 13 个显著为正，恒等重放在所有运行中标签翻转次数为零，且所有干预共享同一 BSFA 内核路径——消除了核路径混淆。
- **稠密校准的反事实审计协议：** 提出 Gold/Poison/Benign 三张 128-token 哨兵卡（仅在冻结索引 64 处差一个标签 token），在六种排列对称下平均以去除槽位与顺序混淆，并用打补丁的 eager attention 在 c=0 下提供"同核、同前向路径"的稠密基线；主量 Δ = H3_sparse − H3_dense 度量稀疏专属的内容影响残差。
- **两种竞争模式的直接因果证据：** "信号集中"由路由收据证明——四个模型-任务对中均出现 G≈P≫B 的选择率模式；"整合损失"由跨块注意力消融证明——在 1,536 个单位中将探针块的影响力从 4.48 logits 完全塌缩到零，形成稠密→稀疏→完全隔离的单调剂量响应。
- **压缩比作为仲裁者：** 在 c∈{0.25, 0.50, 0.75} 全扫描中，四个模型-任务对里有三个随压缩增大朝更正 Δ 移动；Qwen3-SCB 完成完整符号反转（−0.31→+0.16→+0.93），Qwen3-SF 出现第二次符号反转（+0.19→+0.08→−0.08）。
- **来自 KV 缓存驱逐的会聚证据：** 用 KVPress（Expected Attention、SnapKV）在 token 级别而非块级别重复实验，八个单元全部显示同方向（中毒率成对下降），证明该现象不是单一选择算子的伪迹。
- **可部署的开放测量框架：** 协议仅需行为访问（暴露块标识 + 强制路由重放），无需修改权重或架构，适用于任意暴露块标识的模型；在四类最广泛部署的 7B–8B 开源模型上验证。

## Key Contributions

- **Causal validation that sparse routing changes content influence (BSFA route replay).** Across Llama-3.1-8B, Mistral-7B, Qwen2.5-7B, and Qwen3-8B, forcing a wrong-evidence route versus a gold route yields a significantly positive wrong-answer margin gap in 13 of 16 model–task–ratio cells (range 0.022–2.078 logits), with zero identity-replay label flips and zero kernel-path confounds because all interventions share the same BSFA kernel path.
- **A dense-calibrated counterfactual audit protocol.** Three matched 128-token sentinel cards — Gold (G), Poison (P), Benign (B) — differ at exactly one frozen index; the protocol averages over all six card permutations to kill slot/order confounds and uses patched eager attention at c=0 as a within-kernel dense baseline. The difference-in-differences Δ = H3_sparse − H3_dense isolates the sparsification-specific component.
- **Direct causal evidence for two competing patterns.** Signal concentration is supported by routing receipts showing G≈P≫B selection rates in all four model–task pairs; integration loss is supported by a cross-block attention ablation that collapses the probe card's influence from 4.48 logits to exactly zero across all 1,536 units, producing a monotonic dense→sparse→isolated dose-response.
- **Compression ratio as a control variable.** A three-ratio sweep (c∈{0.25, 0.50, 0.75}) shows three of four model–task pairs moving toward more positive Δ at higher compression. Qwen3-SCB reverses from −0.31 to +0.93 and Qwen3-SF reverses from +0.19 to −0.08 — two complete sign reversals driven only by varying the compression ratio.
- **Converging evidence from KV-cache eviction.** Replicating the audit with KVPress (Expected Attention, SnapKV) under real SciFact poisoning passages, all eight cells show negative paired compression-minus-dense poisoning-rate changes, confirming the phenomenon under a structurally different compression mechanism.
- **Open, deployable measurement framework.** The protocol needs only behavioral access (block identities + forced route replay); it works on any model exposing block identities and is validated on the four most widely deployed 7B–8B open-weight architectures.

## Methodology

The paper builds a four-layer measurement stack, each layer attacking a distinct confound.

**1. BSFA route replay (causal, same-kernel).** Using native Block Sparse Flash Attention, the authors replay different evidence routes under the *same* kernel path. The metric is `gap = M_wrong-route − M_force-gold` in the wrong-minus-gold log-probability margin. Identity replay verifies that the replay operator itself does not perturb the model (zero label flips). Non-identity replay tests whether the answer margin depends on which evidence blocks are visible.

**2. Dense-calibrated counterfactual audit (systematic, within-kernel).** Each prompt embeds three 128-token cards differing only at frozen index 64: Gold carries the correct label token, Poison the wrong label token, Benign a whitespace filler. A natural capture pass records the selector's route; four forced routes then add exactly one anchor (G, P, B, or neutral R) onto a frozen K−1 core, producing equal-cardinality routes with pairwise Hamming distance two. The primary sentinel effect is `H3 = M_P − M_B`. The dense arm reruns the same prompts/layouts/scoring under patched eager attention at c=0 and computes H3_dense by content replacement, giving the difference-in-differences `Δ = H3_sparse − H3_dense`. A kernel-path check at c=0 documents a mean |H3| discrepancy of 0.044 logits between patched eager and native SDPA (100% label agreement), retained as a quantified baseline in every Δ estimate — 11×–49× smaller than the main effects (0.47–2.15 logits).

**3. Six-layout symmetry and inference.** Both arms use the complete permutation set {GPB, GBP, PGB, PBG, BGP, BPG}; every card occupies every slot twice. Units are (item, label mapping, layout, route seed) tuples; the item is the sampling unit. Confidence intervals use 10,000 item-clustered bootstrap replicates; p-values use 100,000 blocked sign flips. Three preregistered pooled hypotheses (Holm-corrected) test for a uniform directional effect; a post-hoc 32-test stratified family is treated as exploratory.

**4. Convergent evidence from KV-cache eviction.** Paired dense/sparse runs with KVPress (Expected Attention, SnapKV) on Qwen2.5-7B and Qwen3-8B at two compression ratios use real SciFact poisoning passages and report the paired compression-minus-dense change in poisoning rate. Cross-block attention ablation isolates the probe block (full self-attention but no cross-block communication) to provide direct mechanistic evidence for integration loss.

Tested models span the four most widely deployed 7B–8B open-weight architectures; tasks are SCBench-KV (key-value retrieval from shared long contexts) and SciFact (scientific claim verification).

## Results & Highlights

- **BSFA route replay — causal content influence.** Across 16 model–task–ratio cells, the wrong-route-minus-force-gold margin gap was significantly positive in 13 cells, never significantly negative, with **zero identity-replay label flips** in all runs. Significant positive mean gaps range from 0.022 to **2.078 logits** (Qwen3-8B, SciFact, c=0.25); Qwen2.5-7B on SciFact reaches +1.550 [+0.759, +2.379] at c=0.25 and +1.827 at c=0.50.
- **Stratified significance after correction.** Of the 32-test post-hoc stratified family (8 cellwise tests each for H1, H2, H3 plus 8 SciFact label-stratified H3 tests), Holm correction rejects **31 of 32 nulls** in the positive direction; the only exception is Qwen3–SciFact at c=0.50 in the SUPPORTS stratum.
- **Signal concentration — routing receipts.** Natural selection rates for label-bearing blocks exceed matched benign rates in all four model–task pairs: Gold/Poison/Benign rates are 0.758/0.759/0.470 (Qwen3-SCB), 0.750/0.750/0.670 (Llama-SCB), 0.781/0.781/0.521 (Qwen3-SF), 0.792/0.792/0.666 (Llama-SF) — the G≈P≫B pattern holds uniformly.
- **Integration loss — cross-block ablation.** On Qwen3-SCB across all 1,536 units, isolating the probe block from cross-block attention collapses |H3| below 10⁻⁴ (mean exactly 0.0000), producing a monotonic dose-response: dense H3 = **4.48 [4.10, 4.87]** → sparse (c=0.25) H3 = 4.09 [3.92, 4.26] → isolated H3 ≈ 0.
- **Compression-ratio sweep with sign reversals.** Thin-probe Δ across c∈{0.25, 0.50, 0.75}: Qwen3-SCB moves **−0.31 → +0.16 → +0.93 [+0.81, +1.04]** (full sign reversal, c=0.75 interval entirely above zero); Qwen3-SF moves +0.19 → +0.08 → −0.08 [−0.18, +0.02] (second sign reversal); Llama-SCB moves −0.73 → −0.70 → −0.60 [−0.64, −0.56]; Llama-SF moves −0.53 → −0.46 → −0.29 [−0.41, −0.17]. Three of four cells move toward more positive Δ at higher compression.
- **Real-evidence probes are uniformly negative.** All eight real-evidence Δ point estimates are negative even though label-token signs are mixed, e.g., Qwen3-SCB = **−2.15 [−2.67, −1.63]** at c=0.25 vs. label-token Δ of −0.31, showing that selected-block content drives the observed sparse-dense contrast.
- **KVPress convergent direction.** All 8 cells show negative paired compression-minus-dense poisoning-rate changes, every cellwise 95% CI below zero; Qwen3-8B changes are an order of magnitude larger than Qwen2.5-7B (e.g., Qwen3 Expected Attention c=0.50: **−0.1644 [−0.1796, −0.1504]** vs. Qwen2.5 Expected Attention c=0.50: −0.0122 [−0.0226, −0.0026]).
- **Aggregate-accuracy invisibility.** All three preregistered pooled tests returned null after Holm correction (p = 0.995, 0.771, 0.541) precisely because cells with opposite calibrated signs cancel in the aggregate — the exact failure mode the paper targets.
- **Coexistence within a single cell.** On Qwen3-SCB at c=0.25, the selector prefers the signal-bearing block at rate 0.994 (signal concentration at the routing layer) yet Δ = −0.31 (integration loss at the output layer) — the two patterns operate at distinct layers and can coexist.

## Authors & Credibility

The work is co-authored by Xingyu Ren (CUHK) and Youran Sun, Chugang Yi, and Haizhao Yang (University of Maryland, College Park), with Yang as corresponding author. The authors have prior arXiv output on autonomous research systems (Agon) and multi-agent orchestration benchmarks (PerspectiveGap). The paper adopts a confirmatory/exploratory distinction (Kimmelman et al. 2014), preregisters three pooled hypotheses with Holm correction, and reports *null* results for those pooled tests as a feature rather than a gap — an unusually disciplined stance. Statistical machinery is thorough: 10,000 item-clustered bootstrap replicates, 100,000 blocked sign-flip randomization p-values, complete Latin label mappings, structural and readout gates that invalidate (not impute) failed units, and a quantified 0.044-logit kernel-path baseline reported alongside every Δ estimate.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** The contribution is methodological and conceptual rather than a new selector. It reframes sparse-attention evaluation away from throughput/perplexity/aggregate accuracy toward *content-specific behavioral influence*, and explicitly excludes attention-weight diagnostics as a category error (citing Serrano & Smith 2019, Jain & Wallace 2019, Wiegreffe & Pinter 2019). The Gold/Poison/Benign sentinel design borrows cleanly from RAG-poisoning and contextual-entrainment literatures but repurposes them as calibrated contrasts rather than attacks.
- **Rigor:** Strong. Same-kernel forced routing eliminates the most dangerous confound; the 0.044-logit kernel-path diagnostic is reported everywhere and is 11×–49× smaller than main effects. The honest reporting of three null pooled tests (p = 0.995, 0.771, 0.541) followed by explicitly post-hoc stratified analysis is methodologically clean, though readers should note that 31/32 rejections come from the *exploratory* family — a point the authors flag but which limits confirmatory strength.
- **Reproducibility:** Reasonable. Protocol requires only behavioral access plus a BSFA-based selection interface (citing Ohayon et al. 2025) and patched eager attention; the authors state the framework is "open" and "deployable on any model exposing block identities." However, no code release URL is given in the body, exact seed handling and the full Latin mapping schedule live only in the appendix, and the patched-eager-vs-SDPA numerical equivalence is the linchpin of the entire Δ construct — any reimplementation must reproduce the 0.044-logit agreement.
- **Significance:** Practically important for anyone deploying sparse attention in production: the paper shows a deployed sparse model can preserve aggregate accuracy while *amplifying misleading content and suppressing corrective content* (the shifts cancel). The operational finding that filler/padding content affects the selector and is therefore not neutral is directly actionable.
- **Red flags / scope limits:**
  - The controlled protocol is tied to a fixed 128-token block size; larger or variable block sizes are untested.
  - The thin one-token probe is intentionally minimal and is complemented only by n=16-item real-evidence cells — small per-cell samples for the larger effect sizes.
  - Tested models are only 7B–8B; scaling to 70B+ frontier models is unaddressed.
  - KVPress uses a different outcome metric (poisoning-rate change) than Δ, so convergence is *directional, not quantitative* — magnitudes are not directly comparable across the three arms.
  - Only the final query row is instrumented; selection at other layers/positions is untested.
  - One named weak coverage gap: only Qwen3-SF is the exception in the directional sweep, and the c=0.50 SUPPORTS stratum is the sole non-rejection in the stratified family — a content/label interaction that is acknowledged but not deeply explained.

## Strengths

- Triangulation across three structurally independent methods (BSFA same-kernel routing, controlled block-top-k, token-level KV eviction) is exactly the right epistemic design for a measurement claim.
- The cross-block attention ablation providing a clean monotonic dose-response (4.48 → 4.09 → 0) is a compelling mechanistic complement to the behavioral Δ.
- Six-layout symmetry plus Latin label mappings is a principled solution to the slot/order/label-token confounds that plague position-sensitive long-context probes.
- The paper practices intellectual honesty: it reports null pooled tests, labels post-hoc tests as exploratory, retains the kernel-path baseline in every estimate, and states limitations (block size, thin probe, n=16, scaling) without softening.
- Operational takeaway — that filler content is not neutral and that compression ratio can flip the sign of sparse-specific content influence — is directly useful for sparse-attention deployment and RAG safety.

## Limitations

- Generalization is bounded: fixed 128-token blocks, final-query-row selection only, 7B–8B parameter class, and inference-time only (no training-time sparsification dynamics).
- Real-evidence cells use n = 16 items, so the largest, most deployment-relevant effect sizes rest on small samples.
- The thin label-token probe isolates a single token and cannot capture compound semantic signals.
- The three arms use different outcome metrics, so convergence is directional rather than quantitative.
- No study of how the effect scales with context length beyond what SCBench-KV/SciFact exercise, and no frontier-scale (70B+) replication.
- The paper does not propose a fix or a defense — it is a diagnostic framework only.

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the calibrated Δ behave the same way at 70B+ scale and at context lengths (e.g., 128k–1M tokens) well beyond SCBench-KV/SciFact, where attention-sink and "lost-in-the-middle" effects compound?
- How does the balance between signal concentration and integration loss shift when selection happens at layers other than the final query row, or under learned token-level routing (NSA/GSA) rather than score-based block-top-k?
- Can the protocol be turned into an online monitor that flags content-specific influence shifts in production without requiring matched dense counterfactuals?
- Is the Qwen3-SF c=0.50 SUPPORTS-stratum exception (the lone non-rejection in 32 tests) a label-content interaction worth a dedicated study, or noise from n=16?
- Does the finding that filler/padding content is not neutral imply that standard prompt-formatting conventions silently bias sparse selectors, and can prompt-side mitigations recover the lost cross-block connectivity?
- Could the cross-block isolation ablation be generalized into a per-head "integration-loss map" to predict which architectures are most fragile under aggressive compression?
