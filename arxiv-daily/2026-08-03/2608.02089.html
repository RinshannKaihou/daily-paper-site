The experiment crosses five open-weight target models (Qwen3-4B / 8B / 14B and gpt-oss-20b / 120b) with three benchmarks (GPQA-Diamond n=198, MMLU-Pro n=12,032, Omni-MATH-2-Filtered n=4,181) — 15 model–benchmark settings. Each item produces one completed reasoning run; correctness is option-match for the MC benchmarks and GPT-5-mini equivalence judging for Omni-MATH-2.

Each display is encoded as surface features (word/sentence counts, digit & math-symbol density, uncertainty / self-correction / answer-framing markers) concatenated with a 512-dim Cohere embed-v4.0 embedding, fed to an L2-regularized logistic probe. To handle order dependence (S derives from C), monitors are fitted to all seven nonempty subsets of {R,S,C} and a Shapley attribution averages each component's marginal contribution. The unit of inference is the model–benchmark setting (n=15), with 95% descriptive setting-bootstrap intervals alongside mixed-model sensitivity intervals.

Three additional controls are central: (1) a repeated-run comparison generating two extra MMLU-Pro runs per item and measuring within-item AUROC on 939–1,389 outcome-discordant items; (2) a length-matched comparison replacing the summary with Last90 / First90 / Random / HeadTail trace extracts at equal word budget; (3) a GPT-5-mini reader sensitivity on gpt-oss-20b discordant items.

03

Strengths & limitations

A reader-relative verdict, honestly bounded.

Strengths what holds

  • Clean experimental design — isolates information content by holding the run fixed and varying only the display.
  • Large control suite — Shapley attribution, masking, prompt-overlap stripping, length residualization, leave-one-out, label permutation, decoding sensitivity, chunk-level pooling.
  • Two monitoring regimes cleanly separated — across-item ranking vs. within-item run discrimination, each with the right metric.
  • Reader-relativity finding is the most decision-relevant result for the AI-safety community.
  • Honest framing — every claim tagged with the reader that produced it and the limits of the summary proxy.
  • Fully open — code, data, weights/hashes, parser diagnostics, version pins.

Limitations what's bounded

  • Summary is a controlled proxy — same model, trace-only, one instruction, no filters, no adversarial pressure. Does not describe deployed commercial summaries.
  • Linear readers dominate — the only stronger-reader test (GPT-5-mini) covers one model, one benchmark, one judge instruction.
  • Length is entangled with linear signal; residualization flips the trace-minus-summary sign.
  • Repeated-run subset is unrepresentative — 23–30 pp lower accuracy and 2.5–3× longer traces than typical MMLU-Pro items.
  • Three benchmarks, two model families — intervals are sensitivity summaries, not population intervals.
  • Correctness only — the ladder measures final-answer correctness, not faithfulness, reward hacking, or causal computation.
  • No closed-API evaluation (OpenAI / Anthropic / Google) — where the practical monitoring question is most acute.
04

Open questions

Where the ladder could extend next.
1
How do results change when the summary is written by a different model, or with the prompt and response visible to the summarizer — closer to deployed provider summaries?
2
Does the reader-relativity result extend beyond GPT-5-mini to other frontier judges, and is there a capability threshold above which summaries become sufficient?
3
Can the design extend to faithfulness, reward hacking, or deception using labels defined independently of the reader?
4
How much of the trace's residual signal is semantic vs. superficial length/markers under non-linear readers with joint controls?
5
What happens on closed API models (OpenAI o-series, Anthropic extended thinking, Gemini thinking) that expose only summaries?
6
Are the near-chance within-item summary readers a property of linear probes, or do richer item-conditional monitors recover signal the pairwise control misses?
