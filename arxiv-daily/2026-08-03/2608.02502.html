§ 01Methodology — what CMuon actually changes

diagnose · formalize · patch

The bug. DiT implementations fuse functionally heterogeneous parameters — AdaLN scale/shift, QKV, FFN gate/up — into a single 2D tensor for throughput. Vanilla Muon runs one Newton–Schulz orthogonalization over the whole fused matrix, forcing unrelated subspaces to share a preconditioner.

The fix. Split those tensors back into their original sub-matrices, orthogonalize each chunk independently, concatenate — a reshape plus a per-chunk NS call. The Frobenius norm is provably preserved under Moonlight scaling; chunking only redistributes norm.

Formal contrast. Stacked: U_i = G_i·(Σ_j G_jᵀG_j)⁻¹ᐟ² — covariances mix. Chunked: U_i = G_i·(G_iᵀG_i)⁻¹ᐟ² — each block keeps its own descent geometry.

Scope. Muon/CMuon touch only 2D attention/FFN/AdaLN weights; embeddings, 1D params and the final layer stay on AdamW. Batch 1024, bf16, EMA 0.9999, constant LR.

§ 03Strengths & limitations

side-by-side · not equal weight

Strengths + pro

  • Mechanism-level diagnosis — names the exact failure mode (subspace coupling through fused tensors) instead of an empirical trick.
  • Zero-overhead change — a reshape + per-chunk NS call; throughput stays at Adam-parity on 8× H100.
  • Ablation discipline — every claim (which blocks, which LR scaling, rescale switch, LR value, VAE) is backed by a controlled table.
  • Principled, not heuristic — norm-preservation holds under Moonlight scaling; chunking only redistributes Frobenius norm.
  • Sustained, not transient — gains hold to 400 epochs, unlike vanilla Muon which plateaus.

Limitations – con

  • Eval is narrow — only class-conditional ImageNet (256 & 512); no text-to-image, no video, no >1B scale.
  • Theory is thin — subspace-interference is a toy example + Frobenius identity; no convergence or coupling-distortion bound.
  • PixelDiT hand-waved — the only non-latent setting is a negative result, explained away without an independent test.
  • No public code mentioned in the paper text — a reproducibility concern.
  • Absolute FID gain is modest — 0.03 over fully-trained AdamW; the headline value is compute halving, not asymptotic quality.
  • Chunk granularity unjustified — why 6 for AdaLN? No per-layer chunk-count ablation; could be sub-optimal.

§ 04Open questions

where this could go next

Does chunking also help Muon variants — Scion, Gluon, PolarExpress — or is the effect specific to vanilla Newton–Schulz?

Can the subspace-interference diagnosis become a convergence-rate bound that predicts when chunking helps vs. when it is neutral?

Does the same trick transfer to fused QKV / gate-up in decoder-only LLMs trained with Muon (Moonlight, Kimi-K2 setup)?

What is the optimal per-layer chunk count — is there a principled rule from singular-value spectra or initialization variance?

How does CMuon behave at >1B scale and on text-to-image / video, where AdamW + REPA currently dominates?

Can the PixelDiT negative result be reproduced on a non-Adam-tuned architecture search to actually test the "overfit to Adam" claim?
