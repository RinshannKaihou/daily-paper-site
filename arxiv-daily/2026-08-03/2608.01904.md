> **一句话总结：** CoEvoKG 把知识图谱的多跳实体链同时用作可验证训练任务的来源和持久化证据记忆，求解器在答对且路径被图证据支持时把检索证据写回图节点/边，在 6 个 QA 基准上相对底座平均提升 +10.1~+11.6 分，并在匹配训练预算下比 Search-R1/SSP 自博弈基线高 +2.6~+3.7 分。

> **TL;DR:** CoEvoKG jointly trains a proposer (REINFORCE++) and solver (GRPO) over a knowledge-graph chain pool that both generates verifiable multihop QA tasks and stores evidence written back from correct, path-supported solver trajectories; across NQ/TriviaQA/PopQA/HotpotQA/2WikiMultiHopQA/Bamboogle on Qwen2.5-3B/7B and Llama-3.1-8B it lifts macro-average accuracy by +10.1 to +11.6 over the base models and by +2.6 to +3.7 over Search-R1 and Search Self-Play (SSP) baselines under a matched 314-step budget.

## 核心贡献

- **知识图谱链驱动的任务生成**：用从 Wikipedia/KILT 知识图谱离线随机游走得到的 2–3 跳实体链（带关系标签和节点段落）作为可验证多跳问题的结构化来源，并由 LLM 质量门（去泄露、忠实于链、多跳、单答案、清晰）做硬过滤，避免无约束生成或固定人工 QA 的不稳定性。
- **路径支持 + 难度奖励**：求解器奖励 = 答案正确性 R_ans × (1 + β·S_path)，其中 S_path 由实体路径每跳在图证据上的支持度几何均值得到（β=0.2，仅在答对时计入），把"答对且有证据支撑"与"猜中"区分开；提议器奖励是带退火目标成功率 p*(t) 的钟形难度奖励，把问题推到求解器当前能力边界。
- **可验证图记忆写回**：仅当 R_ans=1 且 S_path≥τ_path 时，把求解器检索到的证据附到原链对应节点/关系，并用小写实体序列哈希去重；丰富后的链池进入下一轮提议采样与验证，闭合模型自演化与知识积累的循环。
- **完整的自博弈 RL 训练栈**：在 veRL + SGLang 后端上做异步多轮 rollout，求解器用 GRPO（组内相对优势、无 critic），提议器用 REINFORCE++（全局批归一化），并共享 token 概率仿射阻尼作为稳定器；代码开源于 https://github.com/lazzy1225/CoEvoKG。

## Key Contributions

- **KG-chain task generation**: Multihop (≥2 relational hops) entity chains from offline random walks over a Wikipedia-based KG (KILT source) provide a verifiable, leakage-controlled source of search tasks, replacing both fixed human-written QA pools and unstable unconstrained generation; an LLM quality gate enforces no-leakage, chain faithfulness, genuine multihop requirement, single-answer focus, and clarity before any task is admitted.
- **Path-support process reward**: A deterministic, no-extra-LLM path-support score S_path verifies each hop of the solver's extracted entity path against the current evidence graph (direct + one-hop-bridge support), and is combined with answer correctness as R_s = R_ans·(1+β·S_path) (β=0.2, process term only on correct trajectories), separating evidence-grounded reasoning from lucky matches.
- **Frontier difficulty reward**: The proposer is rewarded with an annealed bell-shaped reward R_diff peaked at a target group success rate p*(t) that anneals from p*_s to p*_e, discouraging trivially easy or unsolvable questions and tracking the solver's improving competence frontier.
- **Verified graph memory write-back**: Only correct AND path-supported trajectories (R_ans=1, S_path≥τ_path) have their retrieved evidence written back into the originating chain's nodes/edges, deduplicated by a lowercased-entity-sequence hash; the enriched pool advances G_t→G_{t+1} and closes the self-evolution loop so later rounds sample richer chains.

## Methodology

CoEvoKG couples two LLM policies (proposer π_p and solver π_s, on a shared backbone) around an evolving, entity-indexed chain pool G_t ("graph memory"). The pool is seeded offline by 2–3 hop random walks over a Wikipedia-based KG (KILT knowledge source) held in MongoDB, with each node carrying its Wikipedia passage and each edge a typed natural-language relation label.

Each training round runs in fixed order:
1. **Task generation**: The proposer samples M candidate (question, answer) pairs per chain, with the answer entity chosen anywhere along the chain (not only the terminal) to reduce positional leakage. An LLM quality gate Q(q,a,c) enforces a hard no-leakage constraint plus verifier scoring over faithfulness/multihop/focus/clarity; a deterministic prefilter removes broken candidates. Chains producing no admitted question fall back to verified seed questions from a human-written pool, keeping batches well-formed (the seed-fallback rate declines from ≈0.40 to ≈0.25 during training).
2. **Solver rollout**: The solver answers q via multi-turn search (up to 8 turns, interleaving reasoning with dense E5 retriever calls returning top-3 Wikipedia passages). For each admitted question it samples G=8 trajectories (GRPO group). Solver reward R_s = R_ans·(1+β·S_path): R_ans∈{0,1} is exact-match correctness; S_path is the geometric mean of per-hop support scores M(ê_i, ê_{i+1}) computed deterministically (no LLM) from current chain evidence, combining direct (order + textual mention + cooccurrence) and one-hop-bridge support. The geometric mean ensures any unsupported hop drops the score sharply, so a trajectory passes S_path≥τ_path only when every hop is traceable. Process credit applies only to correct trajectories.
3. **Proposer scoring**: Difficulty p_s = n_c/G (fraction of GRPO rollouts with correct final answer, NOT the process-augmented reward) yields a bell reward R_diff = exp(-(p_s - p*(t))²/2σ²) annealed from p*_s to p*_e; R_p = R_diff (failed extraction gets a small fixed penalty). Quality Q is upstream as a gate, not in R_p.
4. **Write-back & update**: Correct, path-supported trajectories attach retrieved evidence to matching nodes/relations of the originating chain, deduplicated by hash; enriched chains return to the pool advancing G_t→G_{t+1} for the next round.

Optimization uses a clipped PPO surrogate with a low-variance (k=3) KL penalty to a frozen reference policy; the solver uses GRPO group-relative advantages (Eq. 11, no value critic) and the proposer uses REINFORCE++ cumulative discounted returns whitened over the global batch (Eq. 12). A token-probability affine scaling stabilizes both. All methods (CoEvoKG, Search-R1, SSP, ablations) are trained for the same fixed 314 optimization steps; checkpoint selected on a held-out validation split, then evaluated once on SSP held-out subsets.

## Results & Highlights

- **Macro-average gains over base models** on six benchmarks (NQ, TriviaQA, PopQA, HotpotQA, 2WikiMultiHopQA, Bamboogle): +11.2 (Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct: 30.9→42.1), +10.1 (Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct: 44.0→54.1), and +11.6 (Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct: 39.8→51.3) points, achieving the highest macro average of all methods under the matched 314-step training budget.
- **Improvement over self-play/RL baselines** under matched budgets: e.g., on Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, CoEvoKG macro avg = 54.1 vs SSP 50.9 (+3.2) and Search-R1 50.1 (+4.0); on Bamboogle/Llama-3.1-8B, CoEvoKG reaches 45.6 vs SSP 41.6 and Search-R1 43.2. The reported range of macro-average gains over the best competitive baseline is +2.6 to +3.7 across the three backbones.
- **Largest per-dataset gains on multihop/search-intensive settings**: On Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct, +19.2 on Bamboogle (12.0→31.2), +14.4 on 2Wiki (18.8→31.2); on Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, +16.8 on Bamboogle (28.8→45.6) and +11.0 on 2Wiki (22.6→33.6). On Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, PopQA improves +10.0 (45.0→55.0) and HotpotQA +10.0 (42.2→52.2).
- **Generated-task diagnostics (Qwen2.5-7B)**: admitted generated questions reach an average of 2.23 hops vs ≈1.75 for the seed pool, while keeping comparable solver success rate (p_s = 0.405 vs 0.410) — a stronger multihop bias consistent with the larger multihop-benchmark gains.
- **Build-up ablation (Qwen2.5-7B, macro avg)**: SSP baseline 50.9 → +KG task generation 52.4 (Δ=+1.5, p=0.0011 paired sign test) → +path/difficulty rewards 53.5 (Δ=+1.1, p=0.0141) → +evidence write-back (full CoEvoKG) 54.1 (Δ=+0.6, p=0.0314), over 2,625 held-out examples.
- **Training dynamics**: CoEvoKG warms up slower than SSP for the first ~100 steps but then keeps improving to the highest validation accuracy, while SSP degrades after mid-training and Search-R1 saturates early; KL divergence and policy entropy rise early then stabilize without divergence or collapse; the verified chain pool grows then plateaus as deduplication admits fewer new chains.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: The core idea — letting a KG chain pool serve double duty as both a verifiable task source AND a persistent evidence memory updated from correct trajectories — is genuinely new relative to SSP (which discards retrieved evidence) and DeepDive (offline KG random walks for a fixed set). The path-support process reward computed deterministically from graph records (no LLM, modular scorer) is a non-trivial design. However, the build-up ablation shows the write-back pillar (the headline novelty vs SSP) contributes only +0.6 macro points (p=0.0314), so the paper's empirical case rests more on KG-grounded task generation + path/difficulty rewards than on the evidence-write-back loop that the title and framing emphasize.
- **Rigor**: Strengths include matched 314-step budgets across all methods, three backbones spanning two model families and three scales, six benchmarks covering single-hop and multihop, a dependency-aware build-up ablation rather than leave-one-out, and paired sign tests over 2,625 examples. Weaknesses: only one checkpoint is selected on validation and evaluated once (no error bars, no seeds); the strongest baselines still win individual cells (e.g., Llama-3.1-8B TriviaQA: SSP 75.2 > CoEvoKG 74.4; Search-R1 on Qwen2.5-7B PopQA shows a negative -2.4 delta), which is disclosed but slightly undercuts the dominance narrative.
- **Reproducibility**: Strong on paper — code released, full optimization/system/threshold settings and pseudocode promised in appendix, KILT + MongoDB + E5 + veRL/SGLang stack is standard. Caveat: re-running requires the KILT Wikipedia KG in MongoDB and an LLM verifier for the quality gate, both of which impose non-trivial infra cost not reflected in the 314-step budget figure.
- **Significance**: The framing closes a real gap in self-evolving search agents (they typically throw away evidence). Gains over SSP/Search-R1 are consistent if modest (+2.6 to +3.7 macro), and concentrated on multihop benchmarks where the field most needs progress. The dependency on a Wikipedia-based KG (and the explicit future-work note about extending beyond Wikipedia to scientific/legal/biomedical domains) bounds generality.
- **Red flags**: (a) The paper's headline contribution (evidence write-back / co-evolution) is empirically the weakest of its three pillars (+0.6, only marginally significant), while task generation and the path/difficulty rewards do most of the lifting — a mismatch between framing and evidence. (b) Path-support verification relies on a hand-tuned deterministic scorer with weights (α, β, γ, η) and thresholds tuned on the same chain pool; no sensitivity analysis over these is reported. (c) Difficulty p_s is computed from final-answer correctness by design, but the choice (vs. process-augmented reward) is asserted rather than ablated. (d) The seed-fallback mechanism never goes away (plateaus at ≈0.25), so a quarter of solver batch slots come from human-written seeds even at the end of "self-evolution."

## Strengths

- Tight conceptual framing: KG chains as both task source and evidence memory cleanly addresses the "discard successful evidence" failure mode of prior self-play search agents.
- Deterministic, LLM-free path-support scorer keeps process reward grounded in the graph and avoids reward hacking from plausible-but-unsupported entity surfacing.
- Difficulty reward with annealed target success rate and geometric-mean hop scoring directly targets the too-easy/too-hard instability of self-play curricula.
- Strong, matched-budget empirical protocol across three backbones and six benchmarks; build-up ablation respects the component dependency stack; code and recipe released.
- Largest gains land exactly where the community needs them (multihop: Bamboogle, 2Wiki, HotpotQA).

## Limitations

- Evidence write-back (the "co-evolution" in the title) is the smallest empirical contributor (+0.6 macro, p=0.0314); the paper would be more honestly framed around KG-grounded generation + path rewards.
- Tied to a Wikipedia-based KG (KILT in MongoDB); extension to scientific/legal/biomedical corpora is left as future work, so generalization beyond Wikipedia is unproven.
- No multiple-seed runs or error bars; one checkpoint per method selected on validation and evaluated once.
- Hand-tuned deterministic path-support weights/thresholds with no sensitivity analysis reported.
- Seed-fallback plateaus around 25%, meaning a non-trivial fraction of training signal still comes from human-written QA — the loop is not fully self-generated.
- Strongest baselines remain competitive on individual datasets (e.g., Llama TriviaQA), so dominance is a macro-average story rather than a clean sweep.

## Relevance

与"基于 RL 的搜索/检索智能体"及"自演化/自博弈训练"研究方向高度相关：CoEvoKG 提供了把知识图谱同时作为可验证任务源和持久化证据记忆的闭环范式，直接对应到 RAG/search-agent 自训练、过程奖励设计和多跳推理评测等核心兴趣点，可作为后续在垂直语料（科研/法律/生物医学）上扩展此类自演化智能体的参考基线。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How much of the +2.6 to +3.7 macro gain survives if the path-support weights (α, β, γ, η) and thresholds (τ_path, p*_s, p*_e, σ) are perturbed — i.e., is the method robust or tuned to a narrow operating point?
- Would replacing the hand-built deterministic path-support scorer with a learned/entailment-based scorer (the paper explicitly allows this) change the relative ranking vs SSP/Search-R1?
- What happens when the underlying KG is NOT Wikipedia (e.g., a scientific or biomedical KG) — does task generation quality and write-back utility transfer, or is the gain a Wikipedia-specific artifact?
- Can the evidence-write-back pillar be strengthened (e.g., explicit chain-merge policies, cross-chain evidence linking) to lift its ablation contribution above the marginal +0.6?
- How sensitive is the result to the seed-fallback rate — does forcing 0% fallback (pure self-generation) degrade, match, or improve the macro average?
- Does the method compose with inference-time KG reasoning (e.g., Think-on-Graph) or does the trained behavior already subsume graph-walking at inference?
