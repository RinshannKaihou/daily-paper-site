Does Accuracy Equal Evidence?

Reasoning Faithfulness under KV Cache Compression

Mengting Ai · Jingrui He · Yue Guo UIUC · University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign
kv-cache-compression reasoning-faithfulness chain-of-thought large-reasoning-models llm-evaluation
“

KV-cache compressors can keep the final answer right while quietly breaking the reasoning chain that should support it — making accuracy an asymmetric diagnostic that flatters bad compressors.

Key metric
ρ=−0.95

Spearman correlation between final accuracy and reasoning-chain validity on AIME26. Methods that rank higher on accuracy rank worse on evidence — an accuracy leaderboard would actively select evidence-destroying compressors.

Why this matters

Final-answer accuracy alone systematically overestimates compression quality, indicting the metric every KV-compression leaderboard currently uses.

— evaluation lens, not another compressor
Critical edge

The headline 256-token budget is far below typical deployment, chosen precisely to surface the gap; at realistic budgets (§4.5), chain consistency on GPQA / MedCalc largely closes — the catastrophe is essentially an AIME story.

M·01Methodology

A controlled protocol that fixes the trace and isolates the cache.

  1. Fixed-trace replay. The uncompressed model first generates <think> r </think> + answer for each question.
  2. Cache isolation. Prefill q ‖ <think> r </think>, protect the question cache, compress only the cache over r, resume decoding after </think>.
  3. Component-level diagnostic. Differences across compressors reflect how well each KV state preserves usable information from an already-present trace — not reasoning-content availability.
  4. Three axes of measurement — final accuracy; answer–chain consistency (RWAC via Claude judge, human-validated κ=0.89); perturbation faithfulness (Fidelity & BiasRate after injecting a wrong answer at start/middle/end).

Setup: Qwen3-8B (validated on DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B & Qwen3-30B-A3B) · 256-token default budget · AIME24/25/26, GPQA-Diamond, MedCalc-Bench, RULER QA · 3-seed means.

S·LStrengths & limitations, side by side

A disciplined diagnostic study — with one structurally thin causal claim.

+ Strengths

  • Clean conceptual contribution: "answer–evidence gap" and "accuracy is asymmetric" reframe how reasoning compressors should be benchmarked.
  • Fixed-trace protocol isolates the right variable; matched-answer and wrong-chain-span controls rule out the obvious confounds.
  • 11 methods across all major compression families, plus a quantization control that separates memory reduction from coverage loss.
  • Rigorous judge validation: dual-human + dual-LLM agreement, Cohen's κ ≈ 0.88–0.92.
  • Public code, named models, standard datasets, fully reproducible setup.

! Limitations

  • 256-token default is far below deployment; GPQA/MedCalc gap mostly closes at realistic budgets.
  • Fixed-trace replay ≠ deployment — end-to-end behavior can diverge sharply either way.
  • Single quantization control (KIVI-2bit) underpowers the "coverage vs. memory" causal claim — KVQuant, GEAR untested.
  • RWAC rankings hinge on an LLM judge whose thresholds can swing eviction-method ordering.
  • No proposed remediation — the paper is diagnostic only, no new compressor.
  • RULER mostly a control where accuracy already collapses; the story is AIME/GPQA/MedCalc.

Q·04Open questions

Where the gap might close, widen, or flip under different conditions.

  • Does the gap survive at realistic budgets (2048–8192), or is it an artifact of aggressive 256-token eviction?
  • Can dependency-/provenance-aware signals be operationalized into a compressor that closes the gap without re-expanding the cache?
  • How does it interact with test-time scaling (longer traces, best-of-N, verification loops) — does scaling amplify or repair evidence loss?
  • Is the eviction-method ranking stable across judges and thresholds, given the κ≈0.89 ceiling?
  • Does the gap appear for other coverage-preserving compression (KVQuant, GEAR, residualized KV) beyond the single KIVI data point?
