01 / METHOD

Relocate grounding from the body to the representation

The opponent is stated precisely: ENT claims continuous online sensorimotor embodiment is necessary for scientific abduction. Everything else about embodiment — sufficiency, development, origin of conventions — is explicitly conceded. The move is to shift the operative requirement from the provenance of a representation to its sufficiency.

  • Representational grounding. R₂ grounds R₁ for inference class I if some inference in I is computable from R₂ but not from R₁ — while structural invariants are preserved.
  • Convention space. Independently evolved drawing conventions (spin-2 fields as headless line elements; complexes as boxes-and-arrows) are forced by shared math into a quotient space — a pre-computed canonical form.
  • Identity abduction, scored. Four conditions (unsupplied correspondence, explicit mapping, verifiable consequence, verification downstream) turn a philosophical dispute into a benchmark-decidable question.
  • DAB-30. 30 cross-discipline instances; seeded positives, adversarial decoys, open-world; ten ablation conditions including a symbolic-invariant fingerprint baseline that tests the deflationary "it's just better indexing" reading.

03 / ASSESSMENT

Strengths & limitations, side by side

Strengths

  • Exceptional epistemic discipline. Three explicit scope commitments, a "what this does not establish" section, every limitation mapped to a benchmark component.
  • Convention space is original and buildable. A concrete answer to cross-vocabulary scientific search that lexical and generic embedding retrieval provably do not solve.
  • Operational identity-abduction definition. Four scoreable conditions, with condition (i) — correspondence not supplied in context — isolating the very recognition event ENT defenders will contest.
  • Failure-mode analysis as a feature. Five predicted failure regimes (stylization drift, convention collision, pedagogical topology…) become DAB-30's decoy class.
  • Ablations target the strongest deflationary readings — symbolic-invariant fingerprint and shared-abstract-term baselines test "it's just better indexing" head-on.

Limitations

  • One retrospective, uncontrolled episode. Contamination channels the author concedes cannot be excluded post hoc; the search strategy was human-supplied (scaffolded, not autonomous).
  • No prevalence, reliability, or autonomy claims can be made from a single episode.
  • DAB-30 is designed but not executed. No benchmark numbers exist — every empirical claim is deferred to "Paper 2."
  • Single, non-institutional author, central episode generated with a commercial LLM during their own research program — the highest-risk configuration for unrecognized contamination.
  • Equivalence may not be novel to joint GW/weak-lensing experts; the author explicitly hedges ("not located in our search" rather than "new").
  • Single quantitative number is a synthetic self-check, not an independent measurement. "Claude Fable 5" / 2026 dates should be checked against actual model availability.
04 / OPEN

Questions worth chasing

Does convention-space retrieval actually win? Once DAB-30 runs, does it beat the CLIP-class and symbolic-invariant fingerprint baselines on cross-domain precision — and by how much?
Is the equivalence genuinely novel? Independent expert adjudication (weak-lensing / gravitational-wave) is needed — the author hedges.
Can the loop originate its own search policy? When to invoke visual analogy, at what motif granularity, with what queries — without human scaffolding, and at what precision cost?
Does the L3→L4 gate hold? Or do narrative-plausible candidates routinely get promoted past their motif-level evidence?
Projection-as-motif conjecture (§11). Does what a dynamical-systems figure discards recover retrieval performance where first-layer convention space fragments?
Beyond diagrams? Does the mechanism extend to tensor networks, categorical diagrams, and sonification at comparable precision — or is diagram-first a fundamental constraint?
