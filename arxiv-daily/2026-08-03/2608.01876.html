03

Strengths & Limitations

side-by-side

Strengths

  • Provides a clean class-level (semantic) lens that TGO-I and TGO-II lacked, completing a coherent three-paper geometric narrative.
  • The co-occurrence of global manifold expansion with local per-class compression is non-obvious and intellectually interesting.
  • Layer-wise analysis showing semantic discrimination concentrated at Block 12 is concrete and useful for interpretability, pruning, and early-exit research.
  • Public release of checkpoints and observatory code promised, lowering the barrier to extension.
  • Clear methodology with explicit covariance formulations and the feature/token covariance duality argument.

Limitations

  • Single architecture (ViT-Small/16), single dataset (ImageNet-100), single seed — generalization is untested.
  • No tabulated numbers, error bars, or statistical tests; results are communicated only via figures and prose.
  • Training hyperparameters (optimizer, LR schedule, batch size, augmentation) are not reported, hindering exact reproduction.
  • The three "hypotheses" are qualitative; the claim to "validate or rule out" them overstates figure-based evidence.
  • Heavy self-citation (TGO-I, TGO-II) and a future-work section that rewrites the previously promised roadmap.
  • No baseline comparison and no downstream-task benefit demonstrated — purely an analysis paper.
04

Questions for Further Exploration

open fronts
Q1 · Generalization

Do the Semantic Expansion and local-compression findings generalize beyond ViT-S/16 — to ViT-Base/Large, DeiT, Swin, or to convolutional architectures as a control?

Q2 · Scale

Does the Block-12 concentration of Fisher Ratio and probe accuracy hold on full ImageNet-1k or other datasets (CIFAR-100, iNaturalist with many fine-grained classes)?

Q3 · Robustness

What happens to the Local PCA Rank trajectory under different optimizers, learning-rate schedules, and data augmentations — is the first-quarter peak robust or an artifact of one recipe?

Q4 · Quantitative link

Can the global-expansion / local-compression co-occurrence be linked quantitatively to the Effective Rank explosion of TGO-I via a predictive relationship, not a qualitative narrative?

Q5 · Statistics

Would adding statistical tests and confidence intervals over multiple seeds change any of the three hypotheses' empirical support?

Q6 · Falsifiability

Since future work pivots to computational/gradient/optimization observatories (TGO-IV/V/VI), what concrete experiment would falsify the Semantic Expansion Hypothesis rather than merely corroborate it?
