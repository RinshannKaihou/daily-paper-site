Toward Plasticity-Preserving KL Regularization for Capability Retention in LLM Reinforcement Learning

Li Wang · Xiaodong Lu · Xiaohan Wang · Jiajun Chai · Wei Lin · Tianhao Peng · Guojun Peng · Guojun Yin

Meituan & Nanyang Technological University · github.com/Lumina04/CoKL ↗

CoKL narrows the KL constraint from the full output distribution to the correctness-conditioned distribution over verified-correct responses — provably decoupling total correctness from how correct mass is allocated, avoiding the strict optimal-correctness gap that full forward / reverse KL imposes.

02 / METHODOLOGY

Keep only the term that matters

The full-policy forward KL decomposes into three pieces. CoKL argues only one of them — the KL between correctness-conditioned distributions — is the right preservation lever.

TERM 1 · BERNOULLI
KL on the correctness event
Dropped
TERM 2 · CORRECT-CONDITIONAL
KL between correct-response distributions
Kept → CoKL
TERM 3 · INCORRECT-CONDITIONAL
KL between wrong-response distributions
Dropped

The Bernoulli term pulls total correctness mass Zθ back toward an imperfect reference — directly fighting the reward objective. The incorrect term anchors the policy to erroneous modes. Only the middle term preserves what should be preserved.

CoKL is defined as the forward KL between correctness-conditioned reference and current policies. A log Zθ(x) normalization-correction term removes the implicit correctness-mass optimization that naive correct-only replay would otherwise reintroduce.

LCoKL(x) = DKL( π⁺ref(·|x) ‖ π⁺θ(·|x) )
≡ − 𝔼y∼π⁺ref[ log πθ(y|x) ] + log Zθ(x)
Ltotal = LGRPO + β · LCoKL (β = 1.0 optimal)

Off-policy clipped surrogate with self-normalized weights αij / γij (sequence-level importance ratio clipped to [1−εIS, 1+εIS]). Reference-correct buffer D⁺ pre-generated once; when a current-policy group has zero correct responses, the surrogate falls back to the reference-only term — an implicit adaptive gate that strengthens recovery pressure exactly when correctness is low.

THEOREM 1

CoKL decouples total correctness probability Zπ from the correctness-conditioned distribution. Its global optimum can reach Z = 1.

THEOREM 2

Full-policy forward and reverse KL force a strict optimal correctness gap Z* (between q and 1, shrinking toward q as β grows) whenever the reference is imperfect.

03 / ABLATION

Both loss terms earn their place

On Qwen3-1.7B (Table 2), removing the reference-anchor term collapses Overall to 52.34 — catastrophic math forgetting. Removing the normalization-correction term reduces CoKL to Correct-only Forward-KL (85.86 vs CoKL's 88.28).

An explicit correctness-floor constraint (CoKL + Lfloor, Table 3) slightly hurts: 87.71 (vs 88.28) on 1.7B and 71.64 (vs 74.90) on 4B. This negative result supports the central claim — forgetting acts through drift within the correctness-conditioned distribution, not proportional loss of correctness mass.

STRENGTHS

  • Clean theoretical motivation — explicit KL decomposition plus a decoupling theorem that crisply separates CoKL from full forward / reverse KL.
  • Two-term loss is justified by ablation: dropping term 1 → forgetting (52.34); dropping term 2 → reverts to Correct-only F-KL.
  • Consistent gains across three scales (0.6B / 1.7B / 4B) — not a single-scale cherry-pick.
  • Controlled bandit experiment visualizes the mechanism (coverage@64, conditional-KL dynamics) the LLM runs can only measure indirectly.
  • Honest negative result on the correctness-floor variant — supports the mass-coupling-is-the-wrong-lever claim.

LIMITATIONS

  • Two-stage Math→Chat protocol is a narrow "capability retention" probe; longer sequences (code→math→safety→chat) and other domain pairs untested.
  • Reference-correct buffer needs πref to produce ≥1 correct response per prompt — filters out prompts where the reference is uniformly wrong (bias toward easy prompts).
  • Binary verifier feedback only; the continuous-score threshold τ extension is described but not empirically validated.
  • No empirical comparison with closest concurrent methods (Kruszewski, UCPO, DSDR) — conceptual contrast only.
  • Scale capped at 4B; 7B+ behavior unknown. Tied to GRPO; DAPO / GSPO integration only conceptual.
04 / OPEN QUESTIONS

Where CoKL goes next

How does CoKL behave on longer continual sequences (math → code → safety → chat), and must the reference-correct buffer be regenerated per stage?
What happens at 7B–70B, where both the buffer cost and the diversity of correct modes grow substantially?
Can the continuous-score threshold τ be tuned online rather than fixed — and does it shift the trade-off versus binary verification?
How does CoKL interact with non-GRPO optimizers (DAPO, GSPO), and does the decoupling theorem still translate under their normalization?
A direct head-to-head with UCPO, DSDR, and Kruszewski et al. under matched compute — which verifier-aware principle wins?
Does the implicit adaptive gate (zero-correct fallback) introduce unwanted bias on hard prompts where the current policy rarely produces correct responses early on?
