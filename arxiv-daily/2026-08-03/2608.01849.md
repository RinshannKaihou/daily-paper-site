> **一句话总结：** 本文首次在多模态大模型机器遗忘中形式化了"知识空洞"(knowledge holes)现象——即遗忘有害内容时对与之共享通用模式的良性输入产生的附带损伤,并提出 SPAR 方法(基于锚定 SVD 的激活过滤 + 实体遮蔽增强),在 LLaVA-1.5-7B 上将响应质量恢复到接近原始模型水平(Res. Q 7.07 vs. 原始 7.17),同时保持 0% 攻击成功率。

> **TL;DR:** The paper formalizes "knowledge holes" in unlearned MLLMs—collateral capability degradation on benign inputs whose representations lie adjacent to the forget set—and proposes SPAR (Anchored SVD activation filtering + entity-masked enhancement), which on LLaVA-1.5-7B recovers >98% of vanilla response quality (Res. Q 7.07 vs. vanilla 7.17, vs. RMU's 1.47) while matching the strongest baselines (RMU/PO) at 0% ASR on the SafeEraser benchmark; standard utility benchmarks (MMVet/POPE/VizWiz) cannot see this damage.

## 核心贡献

- **首次形式化多模态场景下的"知识空洞"概念**:将知识空洞定义为模型在遗忘后,对那些特征表示邻近遗忘集 Df 的无害输入产生的非预期能力退化,并将其扩展到跨模态交互场景(此前 Ko et al. 2025 仅在纯文本 LLM 中讨论)。
- **构建知识空洞探测基准**:通过从遗忘集响应中抽取粗粒度模式(整体回答框架)和细粒度模式(无害组合短语),并将其嫁接到良性主题上,形成 4 类探测提示(Response Framework / Syntactic Structure / Action Pattern / Method-Example),用于揭示传统基准无法捕获的附带损伤。
- **实证证明知识空洞的系统性**:在 LLaVA-1.5-7B 与 Qwen2.5-VL-3B 上,PO、RMU、KL-Min、NPO 等代表性遗忘方法虽在标准多模态基准上保持竞争力,但在探测基准上响应质量分数下降 50%–80% 以上(RMU 在 LLaVA-1.5-7B 上损失超过 79% 的原始响应质量)。
- **提出 SPAR 框架**:包含 Anchored Forget Loss(基于冻结参考模型的 SVD 锚定基向量,过滤通用模式以保护其免受遗忘损失惩罚)与 Abstracted Enhancement Loss(实体遮蔽 + 视觉 Dropout,从抽象化输入中强化非实体 token 预测),三损失联合训练解耦遗忘与附带损伤。
- **揭示关键边界条件**:SPAR 在 LLaVA-1.5-7B(隐维度 4096)上有效,但在 Qwen2.5-VL-3B(隐维度 2048)上失败(ASR 80.33%),提示零空间投影方法存在依赖于表征容量的下限。

## Key Contributions

- **Formalization of knowledge holes for MLLMs**: Defines a knowledge hole in πθ̂ as unintended capability degradation on harmless probing inputs whose representations are adjacent to the forget set Df in feature space, extending prior text-only LLM work (Ko et al. 2025) to the multimodal setting where cross-modal interactions make the landscape more complex.
- **A probing benchmark that exposes an invisible failure mode**: Constructs Dprobe by extracting coarse-grained (overall response framework) and fine-grained (innocuous compositional phrases) generic patterns from forget-set responses and transplanting them onto benign topics, yielding four prompt types (Response Framework, Syntactic Structure Reuse, Action Pattern Continuation, Method/Example Enumeration). All prompts are manually inspected and post-filtered to exclude any information derivable from Df.
- **Empirical proof that knowledge holes are systematic**: Across PO, RMU, KL-Min, and NPO on LLaVA-1.5-7B and Qwen2.5-VL-3B, response-quality drops of 50%–80%+ on probing inputs coexist with competitive scores on MMVet/POPE/VizWiz—damage invisible to standard utility benchmarks.
- **SPAR (Selective Protection with Anchored Regularization)**: Two complementary losses—(1) Anchored Forget Loss computes SVD on a frozen retain-tuned reference model to obtain an immutable basis Vref, projects it out of the trainable model's hidden states H before applying the forget loss, shielding generic patterns; (2) Abstracted Enhancement Loss masks entity tokens (POS tagging + ~150 function-word inverted list) and replaces the image with zeros, reinforcing non-entity token prediction without reintroducing harmful content. Combined as L = α·Lretain + β·LAFL + λ·LAEL.
- **Identification of a dimensionality-dependent boundary**: SPAR succeeds on LLaVA-1.5-7B (hidden dim 4096) but fails on Qwen2.5-VL-3B (hidden dim 2048) because compact leading singular directions conflate structural and content-specific signals, and the strong safety alignment causes refusal expressions to be captured as generic patterns.

## Methodology

**Knowledge hole definition & probing benchmark.** A knowledge hole is triggered when a benign probing input xp = (tp, Ip), whose answer cannot be derived from Df, elicits a degraded or refusal response from πθ̂ despite being correctly answered by πθ. Patterns are extracted at two granularities (coarse response frameworks and fine compositional phrases) and instantiated into benign tasks in two formats: structured generation guided by a pattern, and continuation following a short pattern-conformant example.

**Evaluation metrics (three axes).**
- Forget Quality: Attack Success Rate (ASR) on the SafeEraser test set, judged by GPT-4o.
- Model Utility: MMVet, POPE, VizWiz.
- Knowledge Hole Severity: Refusal Rate (RR%) and Response Quality Score (Res. Q, 1–10) on Dprobe, both GPT-4o judged (manual spot-check shows >95% agreement with humans).

**SPAR — Anchored Forget Loss (AFL).** For each forget sample with L response tokens, hidden states H ∈ R^(L×d) from the trainable model and Href from the frozen reference model (fine-tuned on the retain set) are extracted. SVD on Href (no gradient tracking) yields an immutable top-k basis Vref = [v0_ref, …, v_(k−1)_ref]. Per-token alignment scores are computed via clamped inner products, normalized to [0,1] weights wjr, and used to construct Sr = (H ⊙ (Wr·1d^⊤))·vr_ref·vr_ref^⊤; the filtered hidden state is Hs = H − Σ Sr. Hs then replaces H in the forget loss, so the gradient steers only content-specific representations while generic patterns are preserved. The anchoring is critical: a trainable model could otherwise rotate its feature space to disguise harmful content along filtered directions.

**SPAR — Abstracted Enhancement Loss (AEL).** Entity tokens E are detected via a multi-strategy pipeline (POS tagging + inverted function-word list of ~150 English words, with image placeholder tokens explicitly preserved) and masked in both input (replaced with the tokenizer's mask token) and labels (excluded from loss). Visual-Dropout replaces the image I with zeros Ĩ = 0 to eliminate gradient conflict with the forget loss on the visual encoder. AEL is per-token cross-entropy on non-entity positions S = {0,…,L−1}\E.

**Training.** Total objective L = α·Lretain + β·LAFL + λ·LAEL, where Lretain is MSE between current and frozen model activations on the retain set. Defaults on LLaVA-1.5-7B: α=600, β=1.0, λ=0.5, k=2. All methods trained with LoRA on NVIDIA RTX 3090 GPUs. Forget set from SafeEraser (6 unsafe VQA categories: Weapon, Illegal Activity, Hate Speech, Physical Harm, Fraud, Pornography), retain set from ScienceQA, 1:1 split yielding 6,128 forget samples.

## Results & Highlights

- **SPAR recovers near-vanilla response quality on LLaVA-1.5-7B (50% forget ratio):** Res. Q = 7.07 versus vanilla 7.17 (a >98% recovery), while the strongest forgetting baseline RMU collapses to Res. Q = 1.47 (a >79% quality loss) and PO drops to Res. Q = 3.96 with a refusal rate of 32.50%—despite all baselines reducing ASR below 10% of vanilla and preserving MMVet/POPE/VizWiz.
- **SPAR matches strongest baselines on forgetting on LLaVA-1.5-7B:** ASR = 0.00% (tied with RMU), MMVet = 25.38 (above vanilla's 23.80 and RMU's 25.32), POPE = 83.25 (vs. vanilla 84.70), VizWiz = 37.90 (vs. vanilla 39.56), RR = 2.17%.
- **Knowledge holes are architecture-independent and method-independent:** On Qwen2.5-VL-3B, PO reduces ASR to 5.61% with MMVet = 56.85 (even exceeding vanilla's 47.25), yet its Res. Q falls to 2.67 and RR surges to 30.54%, confirming the hole is not specific to LLaVA or to a particular forget loss.
- **Anchored SVD is essential — standard SVD on the trainable model fails completely:** Replacing Anchored SVD with standard SVD leaves ASR at 70.83% (vs. vanilla 65.00%), i.e., unlearning simply fails because the trainable model rotates harmful content into the filtered directions. Anchored SVD achieves 0.00% ASR.
- **Visual strategy ablation:** zeros and random Gaussian noise both yield 0.00% ASR with Res. Q differing by only 0.03 (7.11 vs. 7.08); real images collapse unlearning (ASR 70.94%), confirming the analyzed gradient conflict.
- **Retain weight α on LLaVA-1.5-7B:** α=200 → Res. Q collapses to 3.24; α=600 → Res. Q 7.07 with 0.00% ASR (the chosen default); α=1800 → ASR rises to 66.67%, showing excessive retain regularization overrides forgetting.
- **Honest negative result on Qwen2.5-VL-3B:** SPAR's ASR = 80.33% (worse than vanilla's 17.00%), traced via a k-ablation to over-filtering in Qwen's compact 2048-dim hidden space where structural and content-specific directions are inseparable; AFL strength k=0 reduces ASR to 18.67% but raises RR and lowers Res. Q.

## Authors & Credibility

- Authors are from the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences (CASIA), the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, and Cloudspace Technology. Junxiang You and Junkai Chen are co-first authors; Shu Wu (CASIA) is the corresponding author—a group with a sustained publication record in multimodal learning and recommendation.
- The work is positioned as a direct multimodal extension of the "knowledge holes" concept introduced by Ko et al. 2025 for text-only LLMs, and builds on the publicly released SafeEraser benchmark (Chen et al. 2025), increasing verifiability.
- Evaluation uses GPT-4o as judge with a fixed temperature and seed (42), each non-utility input queried 3×N times and averaged; a manual spot-check reports >95% agreement with human annotators on binary ASR/RR decisions.
- All experiments use LoRA on RTX 3090 GPUs, with full hyperparameter tables per method (learning rates, batch sizes, epochs) provided in Appendix B, supporting reproducibility.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** Genuine. Formalizing knowledge holes for MLLMs (including cross-modal interactions) and constructing a dedicated probing benchmark fill a real gap left by MLLMU-Bench, PEBench, and SafeEraser, whose retain-set/real-world utility probes are far in feature space from the forget set. The Anchored SVD idea—using a frozen retain-tuned reference model as the SVD basis to prevent the trainable model from rotating harmful content into filtered directions—is a non-obvious and well-motivated fix.
- **Rigor:** Mixed. The ablations on the SVD variant, visual strategy, β, k, λ, and α are thorough and include the right "failure" configurations (e.g., standard SVD → ASR 70.83%, real images → ASR 70.94%). However, several rows in Table 2 are marked "TBD" (Real images POPE/VizWiz), indicating incomplete reporting in the version reviewed.
- **Reproducibility:** Reasonable. Prompt templates for all four benchmark construction types and for ASR/RR/Res. Q are fully disclosed (Appendix D), hyperparameters per method are tabulated (Appendix B), and code/data sources are standard (SafeEraser, ScienceQA, MMVet, POPE, VizWiz). The reference model used for Anchored SVD is described only as "fine-tuned on the retain set"—the fine-tuning recipe and checkpoint are not fully specified, which is the main reproducibility gap.
- **Significance:** High if the finding holds up. The central claim—that every major MLLM unlearning method incurs >50% response-quality loss on adjacent benign inputs that no standard benchmark detects—is consequential for trustworthy deployment. SPAR's strong LLaVA-1.5-7B results (>98% quality recovery at 0% ASR) are compelling.
- **Red flags / specific concerns:**
  - The headline "0% ASR with 98% quality recovery" is demonstrated on a single architecture (LLaVA-1.5-7B); SPAR **fails on Qwen2.5-VL-3B (ASR 80.33%, worse than vanilla 17.00%)**, and the authors attribute this to the 2048-dim hidden space but do not test any mid-scale model (e.g., 8B with larger hidden dim) to localize the failure to dimensionality rather than to Qwen-specific alignment or tokenization.
  - The forget set is small (6,128 samples from SafeEraser) and the retain set is sourced from ScienceQA, which is topically distant from safety content; the "well-separated from harmful content" guarantee is asserted but not measured, raising the question of whether SPAR's generic-pattern protection would survive on harder, entangled forget/retain splits.
  - NPO is reported as degenerate (0.00 across all utility metrics on both models), which suggests a configuration failure rather than a meaningful baseline; including a clearly broken baseline without diagnosis inflates SPAR's relative position.
  - The probing benchmark relies on **manual** pattern extraction and prompt construction, so its coverage of "generic patterns" is subjective and likely incomplete; the authors acknowledge this and list automated discovery as future work.
  - All unlearning is via LoRA adapters; whether SPAR transfers to full fine-tuning unlearning (where feature rotation is even more flexible) is untested.

## Strengths

- Identifies and rigorously names a real, previously under-examined failure mode (knowledge holes) in MLLM unlearning, with a clean formal definition and a constructive probing methodology.
- SPAR's design is well-motivated: anchoring the SVD basis on a frozen retain-tuned model elegantly closes the "feature rotation" loophole of naive null-space projection, and the ablation confirming standard SVD's failure (ASR 70.83%) is one of the most convincing single experiments in the paper.
- AEL's entity masking + visual dropout is a simple, low-overhead (CPU-only entity detection) mechanism that directly attacks the complementary risk of losing organizational pattern competence.
- Honest reporting of the Qwen2.5-VL-3B failure and the α/k ablations that explain it; the authors do not hide the boundary conditions of their method.
- Comprehensive disclosure of prompt templates, hyperparameters, and evaluation protocols supports reproduction and auditing.

## Limitations

- Method efficacy is architecture-dependent: SPAR fails on Qwen2.5-VL-3B (compact 2048-dim hidden space), and the dimensionality threshold for null-space projection methods is characterized only on two points, not systematically.
- The probing benchmark currently covers only text-side generic patterns; visual knowledge holes (e.g., unlearning weapon images degrading tool/sports-equipment recognition) and cross-modal alignment holes are explicitly listed as unaddressed.
- Benchmark construction depends on manual pattern extraction and prompt authoring, limiting coverage and scalability; the four prompt types may not span the space of generic structures affected by unlearning.
- Reference model details (retain-set fine-tuning recipe, checkpoint) are underspecified, and entity detection is rule-based (~150 function words + POS tagging), which may mis-mask domain-specific entities.
- NPO appears misconfigured (all-zero utility), and several ablation cells are marked TBD in Table 2, indicating the manuscript is not fully polished.
- LoRA-only evaluation leaves full-fine-tuning behavior unexplored.

## Relevance

本文与"机器遗忘 / 模型安全 / 多模态大模型"研究高度相关:它系统揭示了当前 MLLM 遗忘方法在标准基准上无法察觉的"知识空洞"附带损伤,并提出基于零空间投影与锚定 SVD 的 SPAR 缓解框架,对评估范式设计、安全对齐与可信 AI 部署都有直接参考价值。对关注大模型遗忘评测与安全治理的工作尤其值得跟踪。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does SPAR transfer to full fine-tuning unlearning (not just LoRA), where the model has even more freedom to rotate its feature space and potentially evade the anchored basis?
- What is the minimum hidden dimensionality at which Anchored SVD reliably separates structural from content-specific directions—can the Qwen2.5-VL-3B failure be reproduced on an 8B-class model with controlled hidden size?
- How do knowledge holes behave when the retain set is topically entangled with the forget set (rather than drawn from a distant source like ScienceQA)? Does SPAR's protection still hold when generic patterns overlap with retained content?
- Can visual and cross-modal knowledge holes (e.g., weapon-image unlearning degrading tool recognition) be probed and mitigated by extending AFL into the visual encoder pathway?
- Would replacing the manual pattern-extraction pipeline with automated (RL-based or adversarial) discovery reveal a larger or qualitatively different set of vulnerable generic structures?
- Is the "frozen reference model fine-tuned on the retain set" a strict requirement, or could a frozen copy of the original model serve as the anchor—thereby removing an extra training stage?
