01 · Methodology

The invisible failure mode, made measurable.

A knowledge hole is unintended capability degradation on harmless probing inputs whose representations sit adjacent to the forget set D_f — damage no retain-set or real-world utility benchmark can see, because those probes are far away in feature space.

The forget set's generic patterns (response frameworks, syntactic structures, action patterns, method/example templates) bleed into benign queries whose features lie nearby — so unlearning them cripples unrelated competence. The probing benchmark transplants these patterns onto harmless topics to surface the damage.

Probe Type 1

Response Framework

Overall answer skeleton lifted from a forget-set reply, applied to a benign subject.

Probe Type 2

Syntactic Structure

Innocuous compositional phrases reused to scaffold an unrelated generation.

Probe ×2

Action Pattern · Method/Example

Continuation and enumeration prompts that mirror forget-set habits on safe topics.

SPAR — two losses that decouple forgetting from collateral damage

AFL · Anchored Forget Loss

Filter generic patterns via a frozen basis

Run SVD on hidden states H_ref from a frozen retain-tuned reference → immutable top-k basis V_ref. Project those directions out of the trainable model's H before applying the forget loss, so gradients steer only content-specific signals. Anchoring closes the "feature rotation" loophole a trainable model could otherwise exploit.

+
AEL · Abstracted Enhancement Loss

Reinforce structure without entities

Mask entity tokens (POS tagging + ~150 function-word inverted list) in input and labels, and zero-out the image (Ĩ = 0) to kill gradient conflict at the visual encoder. Cross-entropy on non-entity positions strengthens organizational competence without reintroducing harmful content.

Total objective:  L = α·L_retain + β·L_AFL + λ·L_AEL  ·  α=600, β=1.0, λ=0.5, k=2 (LLaVA-1.5-7B)

Forget set: SafeEraser (6 unsafe VQA categories). Retain set: ScienceQA. 6,128 forget samples, 1:1 split. All methods trained with LoRA on NVIDIA RTX 3090. Eval: GPT-4o judge (temp fixed, seed 42, 3×N queries averaged; >95% human agreement on ASR/RR).

02 · Results & Evidence

What the probing benchmark reveals.

Near-vanilla recovery on LLaVA-1.5-7B Res.Q 7.07 · ASR 0.00%

SPAR recovers >98% of vanilla response quality (7.07 vs 7.17) at 0% ASR. RMU collapses to 1.47 (>79% quality loss); PO drops to 3.96 with 32.50% refusal — yet all baselines preserve MMVet/POPE/VizWiz.

Forgetting matches the strongest baselines ASR 0.00% · MMVet 25.38

Tied with RMU on ASR; MMVet 25.38 (above vanilla 23.80), POPE 83.25 (vs 84.70), VizWiz 37.90 (vs 39.56), Refusal Rate 2.17%.

Holes are architecture- & method-independent Qwen PO · RR 30.54%

On Qwen2.5-VL-3B, PO cuts ASR to 5.61% with MMVet 56.85 (above vanilla 47.25) — yet Res.Q falls to 2.67 and refusal surges. Not a LLaVA-specific or loss-specific artifact.

Anchored SVD is essential std-SVD → ASR 70.83%

Replacing Anchored SVD with standard SVD on the trainable model leaves ASR at 70.83% (vs vanilla 65.00%) — unlearning simply fails because harmful content rotates into the filtered directions. Anchored SVD: 0.00%.

Visual strategy ablation real image → ASR 70.94%

Zeros and Gaussian noise both yield 0.00% ASR with Res.Q within 0.03 (7.11 vs 7.08). Real images collapse unlearning — confirming the predicted gradient conflict at the visual encoder.

Honest negative on Qwen2.5-VL-3B ASR 80.33%

SPAR's ASR is worse than vanilla's 17.00%. A k-ablation traces it to over-filtering in Qwen's compact 2048-dim space, where structural and content-specific directions are inseparable; k=0 lowers ASR to 18.67% but raises RR.

03 · Critical Assessment

What it gets right — and where it breaks.

Strengths

  • Names a real failure mode with a clean formal definition and a constructive probing methodology — a gap left by MLLMU-Bench, PEBench, and SafeEraser.
  • Anchored SVD is well-motivated: using a frozen retain-tuned reference as the basis elegantly closes the feature-rotation loophole of naive null-space projection. The standard-SVD ablation (ASR 70.83%) is among the most convincing single experiments.
  • AEL is simple and low-overhead (CPU-only entity detection), directly attacking the loss of organizational competence.
  • Honest boundary reporting — the Qwen2.5-VL-3B failure and α/k ablations are not hidden.
  • Comprehensive disclosure of prompts, hyperparameters, and eval protocols supports auditing.

Limitations

  • Architecture-dependent: fails on Qwen2.5-VL-3B (2048-dim). The dimensionality threshold is sampled at only two points — no mid-scale control model tested.
  • Probes cover text-side patterns only. Visual holes (e.g., unlearning weapons degrading tool recognition) and cross-modal alignment holes are explicitly unaddressed.
  • Manual pattern extraction limits coverage and scalability; four prompt types may not span the affected generic structures.
  • Reference model under-specified (retain fine-tuning recipe, checkpoint); rule-based entity detection may mis-mask domain entities.
  • NPO appears misconfigured (all-zero utility) and several ablation cells are TBD — the manuscript is not fully polished.
  • LoRA-only evaluation leaves full-fine-tuning behavior (where feature rotation is more flexible) unexplored.
04 · Open Questions

Where this leads next.

Does SPAR transfer to full fine-tuning unlearning, where the model has more freedom to rotate its feature space and evade the anchored basis?

What is the minimum hidden dimensionality at which Anchored SVD reliably separates structural from content-specific directions? Can the Qwen failure be reproduced on an 8B model with controlled hidden size?

How do knowledge holes behave when the retain set is topically entangled with the forget set, rather than drawn from a distant source like ScienceQA?

Can visual and cross-modal holes be probed and mitigated by extending AFL into the visual encoder pathway?

Would automated (RL or adversarial) pattern discovery reveal a larger or qualitatively different set of vulnerable generic structures?

Is the frozen retain-tuned reference strictly required, or could a frozen copy of the original model serve as the anchor — removing an extra training stage?
