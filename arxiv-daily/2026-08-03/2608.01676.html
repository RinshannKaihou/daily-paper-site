Understanding Sparse Attention Selectivity in Long-Context Foundation Models via Counterfactual Evaluation

Xingyu Ren·Youran Sun·Chugang Yi·Haizhao Yang (corresponding, UMD)

KEY FINDING

Sparse attention causally reshapes which content drives a model's output while aggregate accuracy stays flat — and the compression ratio alone can flip the sign of that influence.

01 Key metric
4.48→0
logits · probe influence · n=1,536 units

Cross-block ablation collapses the probe card's effect to exactly zero — a monotonic dense → sparse → isolated dose-response.

02 Why this matters

A deployed sparse model can hold its accuracy score yet amplify misleading passages and suppress corrective ones — a failure mode no throughput or perplexity metric will flag.

03 Critical edge

31 of 32 rejections come from the exploratory post-hoc family, not the three preregistered confirmatory tests (which returned null). Confirmatory strength is limited.

01

A four-layer measurement stack — each layer kills a confound

L1

BSFA route replay causal · same-kernel

Force wrong-evidence vs. gold routes under the same Block Sparse Flash Attention kernel. Metric: wrong-minus-gold log-prob margin gap. Identity replay verifies zero perturbation (zero label flips).

L2

Dense-calibrated audit systematic · within-kernel

Three 128-token sentinel cards (Gold / Poison / Benign) differ at one frozen index. Six-layout symmetry; patched eager attention at c=0 gives a within-kernel dense baseline. Primary measure Δ = H3sparse − H3dense.

L3

Six-layout symmetry + inference statistical

Full permutation set; every card hits every slot twice. 10,000 item-clustered bootstraps, 100,000 blocked sign-flip p-values, Holm-corrected. Three preregistered pooled tests + one exploratory stratified family.

L4

KV-cache eviction convergence mechanistic

Replicate with KVPress (Expected Attention, SnapKV) at the token level on real SciFact poison passages, plus a cross-block isolation ablation proving integration loss directly.

Compression ratio as arbiter

calibrated contrast Δ across c ∈ {0.25, 0.50, 0.75}

−0.80+1.0

Qwen3-SCB performs a full sign reversal: −0.31 → +0.16 → +0.93 — driven only by turning the compression dial.

  • 13 / 16 model–task–ratio cells show a significantly positive wrong-route margin gap (0.022–2.078 logits), never significantly negative; identity replay flips zero labels.
  • Routing receipts show the G≈P≫B selection pattern in all four model–task pairs — Poison is picked as often as Gold, both far above Benign filler.
  • Signal concentration and integration loss coexist in one cell: Qwen3-SCB at c=0.25 selects the signal block at rate 0.994 yet posts Δ = −0.31. Two layers, two effects.
  • All 8/8 KVPress cells show negative paired poison-rate change — converging direction under a structurally different compression mechanism.
  • The three preregistered pooled tests return null (p = 0.995, 0.771, 0.541) precisely because opposite-signed cells cancel — the exact blind spot the paper targets.
03

Strengths & limitations — side by side

Strengths

  • Triangulation across three structurally independent methods (same-kernel routing, controlled block-top-k, token-level KV eviction) — the right epistemic design for a measurement claim.
  • The 4.48 → 4.09 → 0 ablation is a compelling mechanistic complement to the behavioral Δ.
  • Six-layout symmetry + Latin label mappings solve the slot/order/label confounds that plague long-context probes.
  • Intellectual honesty: null pooled tests reported, post-hoc tests labelled exploratory, 0.044-logit kernel baseline retained in every estimate.
  • Operational takeaway — filler is not neutral; compression can flip content influence — is directly deployable for RAG safety.

Limitations

  • Generalization bounded: fixed 128-token blocks, final-query-row selection only, 7B–8B class, inference-time only.
  • Real-evidence cells rest on n = 16 items — small samples for the most deployment-relevant effects.
  • The thin one-token probe cannot capture compound semantic signals.
  • The three arms use different outcome metrics, so convergence is directional, not quantitative.
  • No context-length scaling beyond SCBench-KV/SciFact, no 70B+ frontier replication.
  • It is a diagnostic framework only — no proposed fix or defense.
04

Open questions

Q1

Does calibrated Δ hold at 70B+ scale and 128k–1M context, where attention-sink and lost-in-the-middle effects compound?

Q2

How does the signal-concentration vs. integration-loss balance shift when selection happens at other layers, or under learned token-level routing (NSA/GSA)?

Q3

Can the protocol become an online monitor that flags content-specific influence shifts in production without matched dense counterfactuals?

Q4

Is the Qwen3-SF c=0.50 SUPPORTS-stratum exception a real label-content interaction, or noise from n=16?

Q5

If filler/padding is not neutral, do standard prompt-format conventions silently bias sparse selectors — and can prompt-side mitigations recover connectivity?
