Why this matters

A methodological template for the gap between what models know and what they say — directly relevant to hidden-state interpretability, decoding failure modes, and cross-lingual reliability.

Critical edge

NL prompts are LLM-assisted forward-then-back translations, not native-speaker validated, for 7 of the 9 non-English languages that drive the cross-lingual claim.

Methodology§3

Parallel cultural substrate

A (motif × culture) grid: 27 Thompson folklore motifs × 10 cultures = 270 entities, each with a citation anchor to a primary text (Eddas, Kalevala, Rig Veda, Kojiki, Pyramid Texts…) or monograph. Four cells flagged as structural absences.

Four mechanistic instruments

Each answers exactly one question. The argument is the conjunction.

E1 · PROBE
Linear probing
Is culture linearly readable from the residual stream?
E2 · LENS
Logit lens
At what depth does the gold token enter top-k?
E3 · PATCH
Activation patching
Where in the network is output causally bound?
E4 · GEN
Greedy generation
Is the right name actually emitted? (EN + NL)

2×2 decomposition

Combining E1 (probe) × E4 (output) yields four cells. Preserved: recognized & emitted. DecodingSuppressed: recognized, wrong name emitted. SurfaceLuck: right name without internal disambiguation. RepresentationallyFlat: neither.

Models

18 open-source decoder-only LLMs, fp16, ≤35B params, across 8 families: Llama 3.x, Gemma 4, Phi 4, Qwen 1.x / 3.6, Yi 1.5, OLMo 3.1, Aya. No quantization — to avoid confounding cultural recall with quantization noise.

+Strengths

  • Mechanistic, not correlational. Activation patching localizes readout to the same late band as lens onset — encode-vs-decode is causal.
  • Parallel-by-construction substrate. Citation anchors, structural-absence flags, contested-fill adjudication — exactly what probing-plus-generation needs.
  • Zero-cost mitigation falls out of the language-conditioning finding (+36% relative recovery, no training).
  • Honest scope. Headline uses English-only prompts/activations; cross-lingual claim held by three design-derived controls.
  • Rare robustness audit — 48,568 generations rerated under three stricter matchers, Pearson ≥ 0.87 ranking preservation.

!Limitations

  • NL prompts not native-speaker validated for 7/9 non-English languages. Directional deltas trustworthy; absolute calibration pending review.
  • 270-cell substrate, cell-level inference. Ten cultures exclude sub-Saharan African, Native American, Polynesian, Australian, Arctic indigenous traditions.
  • Linear ridge probe by design; raw (untuned) logit lens — onset is a lower bound, not a calibrated probability.
  • Greedy chat-template generation only; sparse (every-4th-layer) patching on 5 motifs × 4 culture pairs to fit A100 budget.
  • Open-source models only. Headline white-box claim not tested on closed-source frontier models.
  • Transfer beyond folklore (biomedical misconceptions, emotion concepts) is conjecture — analogous parallel substrates don't yet exist.
