Key finding

Activation-derived, layer-wise FFN width schedules cut 440M validation loss about 6.3× more than the TLM cosine taper — under a fixed parameter budget.

Why this matters

Replaces a hand-designed cosine with a closed-form, transferable allocator derived from optimal transport + topology — giving FFN capacity design an interpretable, geometry-grounded knob.

Critical edge

The headline "6.3×" is a descriptive mean ratio over 5 paired seeds. Paired-difference SD/CI are not reported — statistical separation from cosine is unestablished.

01 / METHOD

Measuring the "geometric work" of every FFN layer

The setup

Replace the global FFN intermediate width d_ff with a layer-dependent schedule w_ℓ, derived from a reference model's activations, under a fixed total FFN parameter/FLOP budget.

For each layer ℓ, record the post-attention residual cloud Z_ℓ and post-FFN output cloud H_ℓ; a cloud comparison D gives the layer's work Ŵ_ℓ = D(π(Z_ℓ), π(H_ℓ)).

Three metric spaces

Each work statistic is evaluated under three embeddings π:

· Raw Euclidean — identity embedding.
· Spherical — unit-normalized, angular distance.
· Hyperbolic — fixed-radius 0.99 on the Poincaré ball; provably a fixed monotone transform of angular separation, not a learned radial hierarchy.

Three work statistics

· Shift Ŵ_sh — correspondence-preserving mean transported distance (collapses to mean FFN branch-output magnitude in raw geometry, Eq. 8).

· Metric Ŵ_met — entropic-regularized quadratic Gromov–Wasserstein discrepancy (Sinkhorn, fixed ε_GW).

· Topological Ŵ_top — 2-Wasserstein distance between degree-one persistence diagrams (Vietoris–Rips, diagonal matching).

Budget-optimal allocator (Lemma 1)

Three assumptions — local additive loss, error–width power law ϵ_ℓ(w) ≈ C_ℓ w^(−a_ℓ) with a_ℓ = s/d_int(ℓ), s=3, and geometric proxy — reduce the schedule to a convex program with unique minimizer:

w*_ℓ = max{ w_min , (a_ℓ c_ℓ / μ*)^(1/(a_ℓ+1)) }

With depth-dependent d_int, a single layer-independent θ is invalid; μ* is found by bisection. Widths are quantized and rebalanced so every rule is iso-parameter by construction.

Local additivity (Fig. 1a)

Joint perturbation loss vs. sum of single-layer losses across 7 pretrained decoders gives R² ≈ 0.72 — supporting the locally additive surrogate that Lemma 1 relies on.

Sensitivity association (§3.2)

After within-model standardization, Gromov work correlates with perturbation sensitivity λ_ℓ more consistently than topological work — stronger in 5 of 7 models. Pooled topological association is weak (finite-sample estimator).

Profile shape (Fig. 2)

Raw work rises toward the network's end (tracking residual-norm growth). Under spherical / hyperbolic geometry the ordering largely reverses — work becomes front-loaded, recovering TLM's broad direction with non-monotone, model-specific departures.

Intrinsic dimension (Fig. 1c)

TwoNN d_int stays far below ambient hidden dim, changes smoothly with depth, is smallest in early layers, reaching ~4–5 mid-stack in larger Gemma. With s=3, the exponent θ_ℓ = d_int/(3+d_int) compresses large work differences rather than transferring them one-for-one into width.

03 / TRADE-OFFS

Strengths & limitations, side by side

Strengths

  • Principled, theory-driven allocator with a clean closed-form optimum (Lemma 1), including honest treatment of the lower-bound and depth-dependent-exponent cases.
  • Comprehensive 3×3 work/geometry design space evaluated on seven real pretrained models from three families (Llama, Gemma, Mistral) — not toy data.
  • Paired-seed design and explicit iso-parameter construction (quantize + rebalance to recover the exact budget).
  • Direct, fair comparison to TLM at the same 440M scale under a controlled pipeline; cosine treated as in-protocol baseline, not strawman.
  • Unusually transparent reporting: appendix documents every figure panel, axis by axis.

Limitations

  • No direct calibration of geometric work to per-layer error–width curves — Assumption 3 is only indirectly tested.
  • Headline 440M result lacks paired-difference SDs / CIs; statistical separation from cosine is unestablished.
  • Small-seed screens (n=3 at 128M/256M) have wide, overlapping intervals.
  • Anti-topological/raw control conflates profile reversal with a geometry switch — cannot cleanly isolate alignment.
  • "Hyperbolic" geometry is a fixed monotone reparametrization of angular distance, not a genuine hierarchical embedding.
  • Single corpus, single GPU, max 440M; no downstream-task or scaling-law evaluation, no non-decoder / non-FFN test.
04 / OPEN

Questions worth asking next

  1. Does Ŵ_ℓ actually predict the fitted coefficient C_ℓ of an empirically estimated per-layer error–width curve ϵ_ℓ(w)? How tight is that relationship across models?
  2. What are the paired-difference confidence intervals for the 440M runs — do topological/hyperbolic and Gromov/spherical remain separated from cosine once paired uncertainty is reported?
  3. Does the front-loaded normalized-work profile persist at 1B+ scale, across modalities (vision, audio), and for non-FFN pointwise computations such as MoE experts?
  4. How do geometry-derived schedules interact with downstream-task fine-tuning, instruction tuning, or quantization-aware training — beyond validation perplexity?
  5. Can a matched reversed/permuted normalized profile (holding estimator and width histogram fixed) cleanly isolate the value of alignment from the choice of geometry?
  6. Is the fixed expansion order s=3 justified, or does fitting s per stack materially change the allocator?
