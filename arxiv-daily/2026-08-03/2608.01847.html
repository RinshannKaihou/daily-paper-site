FOCUS: FP4 Optimization via Coupled-Relaxation & Dual-Granularity Scaling

FOCUS learns the FP4 quantization scale in continuous space — exploiting that it is never stored at inference — for SOTA accuracy with zero runtime overhead.

Methodology

The key insight The dequantization scale is the only one stored at inference — so it must obey the hardware format. The quantization scale is discarded, and is therefore free of precision and granularity constraints. No prior learnable FP4 method had optimized that freedom.

Coupled-Relaxation Scaling CRS

Insert a learnable full-precision coefficient c_i (init = 1) between the two scales. Lifting optimization from a discrete power-of-two grid into continuous space — while keeping the coupling that prevents the oscillation seen under full decoupling.

S_q = S_dq · c_i,   S_dq = Q_fmt(S_fp)

Dual-Granularity Scaling DGS

Partition each hardware block into sub-blocks of size 8 (K=4 for MXFP4, K=2 for NVFP4), each with its own coefficient — capturing intra-block weight variation that one per-block coefficient cannot. K=1 reduces DGS to CRS.

per sub-block: S_q^k = S_dq · c_i^k

Deployment

After training, all coefficients and quantization scales are discarded; only FP4 weights and hardware-compliant S_dq remain — a standard MXFP4/NVFP4 model with no inference overhead and orthogonality to Hadamard rotations. Weights frozen; only scales learned via STE with KL-Top distillation on 1,248 WikiText2 samples (seq 2048), 1 epoch, single H20 GPU.

Strengths & Limitations

Strengths PRO

  • Exploits a genuinely overlooked asymmetry with a clean, minimal formulation.
  • End-to-end gradient-based FP4 scale learning — a real advance over rule-based per-layer heuristics.
  • Zero inference overhead; deployed model is bit-exact standard MXFP4/NVFP4.
  • Orthogonal to Hadamard methods → composable with QuaRot / SpinQuant style rotations.
  • Faster calibration than the previous SOTA while delivering better accuracy.
  • Consistent gains across Qwen3 and LLaMA-3 on perplexity, zero-shot, and CoT reasoning.

Limitations CON

  • No evaluation beyond 8B parameters; behavior on 70B+ architectures unverified.
  • Reasoning gap to FP16 stays large (~12 pts on GSM8K), masked by relative framing.
  • Peak calibration memory ~1.7× MR-GPTQ (77 vs 45 GB) — limits who can reproduce.
  • DGS yields marginal gains on NVFP4 — block size 16 leaves little room past K=2.
  • No head-to-head with the authors' own SOAR, the closest decoupled-scale prior.
  • Only 1,248 WikiText2 calibration samples; OOD sensitivity (code, multilingual) unstudied.

Open Questions

Q1 · SCALING

How does FOCUS behave at 30B–405B, where per-block weight distributions are heavier-tailed — are DGS gains larger, or smaller?

Q2 · HEAD-TO-HEAD

What is the real accuracy/speed trade-off vs SOAR — the authors' own decoupled-scale NVFP4 method, cited but never benchmarked?

Q3 · MEMORY

Can c_i be replaced by a low-rank or shared structure to cut the 77 GB calibration spike without hurting accuracy?

Q4 · STACKING

Since FOCUS is orthogonal to Hadamard transforms, what is the cumulative accuracy when stacked with QuaRot/MR-GPTQ rotations on long-context, code, and math?

Q5 · PLATEAU

Why does DGS plateau at sub-block size 8 for NVFP4 (K=2 = K=4)? Is it bound by the native block size, and would a per-element coefficient help?

Q6 · ROBUSTNESS

How sensitive are the 98–100% recovery numbers to the 1,248-sample WikiText2 set when evaluated on multilingual, code, and long-context tasks?
