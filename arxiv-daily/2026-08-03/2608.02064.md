> **一句话总结：** 作者将每个 Transformer FFN 层视为对 token 表示点云的几何传输，用对应保持位移、Gromov-Wasserstein 失真与一度持续同调在原始/球面/双曲三种度量下量化"几何功"，并由此推导出一个固定参数预算下的精确逐层宽度最优分配；在 440M 五种子训练中，拓扑/双曲分配相对 uniform 的验证损失降低（−0.019）约为 TLM 余弦锥化（−0.003）的 6.3 倍。

> **TL;DR:** The paper replaces the global FFN width constant with a fixed-budget, layer-dependent schedule derived from forward-pass "geometric work" (correspondence shift, Gromov-Wasserstein distortion, degree-one persistent homology under raw/spherical/hyperbolic metrics) measured on a reference model; on a five-seed 440M iso-parameter comparison on OpenWebText, topological/hyperbolic allocation reduces mean validation loss by 0.019 below uniform versus 0.003 for the TLM-style cosine taper (a ~6.3× larger mean reduction), while an anti-topological/raw control is worse than uniform.

## 核心贡献

- 定义了三种互补的前向"几何功"统计量（对应保持位移、二次 Gromov-Wasserstein 失真、一度持续同调的 2-Wasserstein 距离），并在原始欧氏、球面角距离、固定半径 Poincaré 双曲三种度量下系统比较，明确区分了"原始幅度"与"尺度归一化后的形变"。
- 在局部可加损失、按内在维度缩放的误差-宽度幂律（ϵℓ(w)≈Cℓ w^(−aℓ)，aℓ=s/d_int(ℓ)）、几何代理三条假设下，导出了带下界约束的、固定 FFN 预算下连续最优宽度分配的唯一闭式解（Lemma 1），并指出在 d_int 逐层变化时不能简单写成 wℓ∝(λℓCℓ)^θ 的单一比例常数形式，需用一维二分求 μ*。
- 在七个预训练解码器（Llama-3.2 1B/3B、Llama-3.1 8B、Gemma-2 2B/9B、Gemma-3 1B、Mistral-7B v0.3）上诊断了尺度敏感性、局部损失可加性（R²≈0.72）、内在维度（TwoNN）与扰动敏感性关联，发现 Gromov 功与扰动敏感性的关联比同调功更稳定（七个模型中五个更强），而归一化几何功普遍呈"前重"分布。
- 在 128M/256M（三配对种子，11 种规则）和 440M（五配对种子，五种规则）iso-parameter 训练中，几何导出的归一化调度在多数比较中优于 uniform；440M 下 topological/hyperbolic 与 Gromov/spherical 的均值损失下降均大于 TLM 余弦锥化，反拓扑/原始控制则显著劣于 uniform。
- 提供了详尽的复现细节（单卡 RTX A6000、OpenWebText、byte-level BPE、AdamW、配对种子设计、宽度量化与再平衡以严格保持参数预算）并对每一个图/表的轴、估计量、归一化与误差条含义逐项说明。

## Key Contributions

- Defines three complementary forward-pass "geometric work" statistics (correspondence-preserving mean shift, entropic-regularized quadratic Gromov-Wasserstein distortion, degree-one persistent-homology 2-Wasserstein distance between persistence diagrams) and evaluates each under three metric spaces (raw Euclidean, spherical angular, fixed-radius hyperbolic Poincaré), explicitly separating raw magnitude from scale-normalized reshaping.
- Derives the unique continuous budget-optimal FFN width allocation under lower bounds (Lemma 1), via a layerwise approximation surrogate (locally additive loss, intrinsic-dimension-scaled error-width power law with fixed expansion order s=3, geometric-work proxy for the latent approximation coefficient); correctly notes that with depth-dependent d_int a single layer-independent proportionality constant is invalid and μ* must be found by bisection.
- Runs a broad diagnostic suite across seven pretrained decoders from three families (Llama, Gemma, Mistral): scale sensitivity, local loss additivity (joint-vs-sum R²≈0.72), TwoNN intrinsic dimension, and layer perturbation sensitivity; finds Gromov work correlates with sensitivity more consistently than the topological estimator (5 of 7 models), and normalized work is broadly front-loaded across families.
- Trains iso-parameter 128M/256M (3 paired seeds, 11 rules spanning the full 3×3 work/geometry grid plus uniform, TLM cosine, anti-topological/raw control) and 440M (5 paired seeds, 5 rules) Transformers from scratch on OpenWebText; normalized-geometry schedules reduce mean validation loss relative to uniform in most comparisons.
- At 440M, demonstrates within the same five-seed protocol that topological/hyperbolic (Δ=−0.019) and Gromov/spherical (Δ=−0.016) allocations achieve substantially larger mean loss reductions than the TLM-style cosine taper (Δ=−0.003), while the anti-topological/raw control degrades to Δ=+0.066.
- Provides unusually transparent reporting: paired-seed design, explicit acknowledgment that marginal standard deviations do not determine paired-difference uncertainty, and an appendix that documents axis-by-axis how every figure and table is produced.

## Methodology

The paper proposes replacing the global FFN intermediate dimension d_ff with a layer-dependent schedule w_ℓ derived from a reference model's activations, under a fixed total FFN parameter/FLOP budget.

**Geometric work.** For each FFN layer ℓ, the post-attention residual cloud Z_ℓ and the post-FFN output cloud H_ℓ are recorded on a held-out OpenWebText sample. A cloud comparison D gives the layer's work Ŵ_ℓ = D(π(Z_ℓ), π(H_ℓ)) under an embedding π into one of three metric spaces:
- Raw Euclidean (identity embedding);
- Spherical (unit-normalized states with angular distance arccos⟨u(z),u(z')⟩);
- Hyperbolic (clip-normalized states placed at fixed radius 0.99 on the unit Poincaré ball with curvature −1, which the authors note is just a fixed monotone transform of angular separation, not a learned radial hierarchy).

Three work statistics are computed in each geometry:
- Shift Ŵ_sh: mean pointwise transported distance between matched input/output tokens; in raw geometry this reduces exactly to the mean magnitude of the FFN branch output (Eq. 8) and so is not algebraically rescaled by residual norm.
- Metric Ŵ_met: entropic-regularized quadratic Gromov-Wasserstein discrepancy (Sinkhorn, fixed ε_GW across layers) between intra-cloud distance matrices, treating clouds as unlabeled metric-measure spaces.
- Topological Ŵ_top: unsquared 2-Wasserstein distance between degree-one persistence diagrams (Vietoris–Rips filtration, ℓ∞ birth-death ground metric, diagonal matching).

**Approximation surrogate and budget-optimal width.** A latent coefficient C_ℓ captures how hard the trained FFN map is to approximate on the residual distribution; λ_ℓ is the loss consequence of an approximation error; and d_int(ℓ) (estimated by TwoNN, used raw without clipping/smoothing) sets the error-width exponent a_ℓ = s/d_int(ℓ) with fixed s=3. Three assumptions — local additive loss (ΔL ≈ Σλ_ℓϵ_ℓ), intrinsic error-width rate (ϵ_ℓ(w) ≈ C_ℓ w^(−a_ℓ)), and geometric proxy (Ŵ_ℓ informs C_ℓ) — reduce the schedule problem to a convex program whose unique continuous minimizer (Lemma 1) is w*_ℓ = max{w_min, (a_ℓc_ℓ/μ*)^(1/(a_ℓ+1))} with c_ℓ = λ_ℓC_ℓ and a shared multiplier μ* found by bisection. In the experiments λ_ℓ = 1 (pure-work variant) and perturbation sensitivity is kept independent as a diagnostic. Widths are then rounded to hardware granularity and rebalanced so every rule is iso-parameter by construction.

**Training protocol.** 128M/256M runs sweep all nine work/geometry combinations plus uniform, TLM cosine, and an anti-topological/raw control (11 rules, 3 paired seeds each). 440M runs focus on 5 rules with 5 paired seeds. Within a seed, all rules share initialization and data order, so D_s = L_rule,s − L_uniform,s isolates the width profile. Reference profiles are smoothed across relative depth ℓ/L and transferred to the target depth.

## Results & Highlights

- **440M five-seed mean validation loss (Table 1, OpenWebText):** uniform = 3.449 ± 0.022 (PPL 31.47); TLM cosine = 3.446 (Δ = −0.003, PPL 31.37); topological/hyperbolic = 3.430 (Δ = −0.019, PPL 30.88); Gromov/spherical = 3.433 (Δ = −0.016, PPL 30.97); anti-topological/raw = 3.515 (Δ = +0.066, PPL 33.62). The topological/hyperbolic mean reduction is ~6.3× the cosine mean reduction.
- **Local additivity diagnostic (Fig. 1a, seven pretrained decoders):** joint perturbation loss vs. sum of single-layer losses gives R² ≈ 0.72, supporting the locally additive surrogate.
- **Sensitivity association (Section 3.2):** after within-model standardization, Gromov work is more strongly associated with perturbation sensitivity λ_ℓ than topological work in 5 of 7 models; the pooled association of the finite-sample topological estimator is weak.
- **Work-profile shape (Fig. 2):** raw Gromov and raw topological work rise toward the end of the network tracking residual-norm growth, whereas under spherical and hyperbolic geometry the ordering largely reverses — work is front-loaded (high in earliest layers, decreasing through the middle), recovering TLM's broad direction while preserving non-monotone, model-specific departures.
- **Intrinsic dimension (Fig. 1c):** TwoNN estimates stay far below ambient hidden dimension, change smoothly with depth, are smallest in early layers, and reach roughly 4–5 in the middle of larger Gemma stacks; with s=3 the exponent θ_ℓ = d_int(ℓ)/(3+d_int(ℓ)) compresses large work differences rather than transferring them one-for-one into width.
- **128M/256M three-seed screen (Fig. 3):** most normalized-geometry schedules have negative mean paired deltas relative to seed-matched uniform; topological/hyperbolic has the lowest mean validation loss among the tested configurations; intervals (n=3) overlap zero, so this is reported as exploratory only.
- **TLM cross-paper context (Section 3.5):** TLM reports its cosine taper improving validation perplexity from 16.28 to 14.44 in its own pipeline; the present within-protocol 440M experiment cannot be compared absolutely to those numbers but indicates an activation-derived profile can outperform a fixed cosine in mean loss under identical data/tokenizer/optimizer/seeds.

## Authors & Credibility

Timur Mudarisov and Radu State are at the University of Luxembourg; Mikhail Burtsev is at the London Institute of Mathematical Sciences. The work is positioned as a direct, methodologically careful follow-up to Tapered Language Models (Bayat et al., 2026), reusing the 440M scale and treating the cosine taper as an in-protocol baseline rather than a strawman. The authors are unusually explicit about statistical limitations: they repeatedly state that the 440M table omits the paired-difference standard deviation (marginal SDs do not determine it without within-seed covariance), so all 440M claims are framed as descriptive mean differences rather than inferential superiority claims. The full experimental appendix specifies data, models, estimators, budgets, seeds, hardware (single RTX A6000, ~26 GiB peak), and panel-by-panel figure construction, supporting reproducibility.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** Genuine. Combining Gromov-Wasserstein distortion, degree-one persistent homology, and correspondence-preserving shift across three explicit metric spaces to derive a closed-form fixed-budget FFN width allocator is novel relative to TLM's hand-designed cosine family. The Lemma 1 derivation, including the lower-bound and depth-dependent-exponent case where w_ℓ ∝ (λ_ℓC_ℓ)^θ with a single θ is invalid, is a real theoretical contribution.
- **Rigor:** Mixed. The surrogate rests on three unproven assumptions; the authors correctly flag that C_ℓ and λ_ℓ are distinct and need not correlate, and that Assumption 3 (geometric proxy) is model-dependent. Critically, there is **no direct layerwise width sweep** to test whether Ŵ_ℓ actually predicts the coefficient of an empirically fit error-width curve — the central modeling hypothesis is validated only indirectly via sensitivity association and end-to-end training.
- **Reproducibility:** Strong on paper. Single-GPU setup, code/data identifiers, paired-seed design, exact quantization-and-rebalancing procedure, and an appendix that documents every figure panel. The main missing artifact for reproducibility of the headline claim is the paired-difference standard deviation s_D for the 440M runs.
- **Statistical reporting — the main red flag:** Table 1 reports only marginal SDs and explicitly omits the SD/CI of the paired differences D_s. With n=5 paired seeds the headline "6.3× larger mean reduction than cosine" is a descriptive mean-ratio, not an inferential claim; the authors say so repeatedly, but readers will inevitably read it as superiority. The 128M/256M intervals (n=3) overlap zero and each other. A reviewer should require the paired-difference CIs before accepting any estimator ordering.
- **Confounded control:** The anti-topological/raw control simultaneously reverses the profile direction **and** switches geometry, so it cannot cleanly isolate alignment from estimator choice; a matched reversed normalized profile would have been a stronger control (the authors acknowledge this).
- **Hyperbolic embedding is cosmetic:** The fixed-radius Poincaré construction is provably a fixed monotone transform of angular distance (Eq. 6) and adds no learned radial hierarchy; the "hyperbolic" label overstates the construction, which is essentially a reweighting of spherical work.
- **Scale and scope:** Single GPU, max 440M, single corpus (OpenWebText). No scaling-law extrapolation, no instruction-tuning/downstream evaluation, no non-decoder or non-FFN test. Whether the front-loaded normalized profile transfers to much larger models or other modalities is open.

## Strengths

- Principled, theory-driven allocator with a clean closed-form optimum and honest treatment of the lower-bound and depth-dependent-exponent cases.
- Comprehensive 3×3 work/geometry design space evaluated on seven real pretrained models from three families, not toy data.
- Methodological honesty: paired-seed design, explicit iso-parameter construction (quantize + rebalance to recover the exact budget), and repeated disclaimers distinguishing within-protocol descriptive comparisons from cross-paper absolute claims.
- Direct, fair comparison to TLM at the same 440M scale under a controlled pipeline.
- Strong reproducibility documentation in the appendix.

## Limitations

- No direct calibration of geometric work to per-layer error-width curves; the core Assumption 3 remains only indirectly tested.
- Headline 440M result lacks paired-difference standard deviations / confidence intervals, so statistical separation is unestablished.
- Small-seed screens (n=3 at 128M/256M) have wide, overlapping intervals.
- Anti-topological/raw control conflates profile reversal with geometry switch.
- "Hyperbolic" geometry is a fixed monotone reparametrization of angular distance, not a genuine hierarchical embedding.
- Single corpus, single GPU, max 440M parameters; no downstream-task or scaling-law evaluation.
- Schedule is measured on a reference model and transferred to a from-scratch target, assuming profile stability under architectural change.

## Relevance

这篇文章与"高效 Transformer 架构设计 / 结构搜索 / 表示几何"方向高度相关：它把最优传输、拓扑数据分析与 FFN 容量分配结合，提供了一个可解释、可迁移的逐层宽度调度方案，对感兴趣于"用表示空间的几何/拓扑信号指导架构设计"以及"在小模型上诊断、向大模型迁移"的研究路线都有直接参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does Ŵ_ℓ actually predict the fitted coefficient C_ℓ of an empirically estimated per-layer error-width curve ϵ_ℓ(w), and how tight is that relationship across models?
- What are the paired-difference confidence intervals for the 440M five-seed runs, and do topological/hyperbolic and Gromov/spherical remain separated from cosine once paired uncertainty is reported?
- Does the front-loaded normalized-work profile persist at 1B+ scale, across modalities (vision, audio), and for non-FFN pointwise computations (e.g., MoE experts)?
- How do geometry-derived schedules interact with downstream-task fine-tuning, instruction tuning, or quantization-aware training, beyond validation perplexity?
- Can a matched reversed/permuted normalized profile (holding estimator and width histogram fixed) cleanly isolate the value of alignment from the choice of geometry?
- Is the fixed expansion order s=3 justified, or does fitting s per stack meaningfully change the allocator?
