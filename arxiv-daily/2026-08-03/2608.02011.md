> **一句话总结：** 本文将 Agentic RAG 中"搜索后未读证据就直接作答"的失败模式从"读后仍错"中分离出来，提出 READ-GATE 运行时不变式强制"先读后答"，在 HotpotQA / 2WikiMultiHopQA / MuSiQue 的零读取轨迹上把 LLM-Acc 提升 14.9–19.9 个百分点，证明证据检索应作为轨迹级控制问题独立于答案侧推理。

> **TL;DR:** The paper decomposes wrong answers in agentic RAG into pre-evidence discipline failures (finalizing after search without reading any chunk) versus post-gold-read failures, validates that they are largely non-redundant across 12,000 trajectories on HotpotQA, 2WikiMultiHopQA, and MuSiQue, and shows that a runtime invariant READ-GATE (forcing a read before final) recovers +14.9–19.9 LLM-Acc points on zero-read trajectories and +3.2–9.4 points on full minimal-reasoning cells, while a hidden thinking-budget increase on Gemini 2.5 Flash paradoxically raises zero-read finalization.

## 核心贡献

- **首次形式化"证据前程序性失败"（pre-evidence procedural failure）**：提出轨迹级错误分解框架，将错误答案按优先级归入 discipline（搜索后未读证据即作答）/ post-gold-read（读了金证据仍错）/ retrieval / ambiguity 四类，使"程序合规性"可被确定性测量，而非依赖对模型内部推理的猜测。
- **提出 READ-GATE：一个不改变模型、检索器、索引、判官或推理预算的最小运行时不变式**——若智能体在 read count=0 时尝试 final，环境拒绝该动作并返回纠正提示。这是一个推理时（inference-time）的执行级约束，而非训练策略。
- **实证证明两类失败的非冗余性与差异化控制轴**：在 12,000 条 OpenAI 控制器配对轨迹上，both-trigger 率稳定在 [11.2%, 13.1%]（regex 与 spaCy 两种实体抽取器一致），远低于 60% 合并阈值；READ-GATE 主要修复 Pdisc（OR 0.38），而提高推理努力（minimal→medium）同时降低 Pdisc（OR 0.22）和 Ppost（OR 0.51），二者交互显著（OR 2.02, p<10⁻⁴）。
- **揭示"更大隐藏思考预算不等于更强证据检查"的反直觉发现**：在 Gemini 2.5 Flash 上开启 1024-token thinking budget 反而使三个数据集的零读取终结率上升（MuSiQue +42.6 pp），并伴随净正确数下降（−44/−67/−73）。
- **开放可复现资源**：发布完整的智能体循环与 READ-GATE 实现、复现脚本、33,950 条原始轨迹（含 12,000 条配对轨迹及每问 discipline/post-gold-read 标签），并附缓存分析输出，无需额外 API 调用即可复现全部数字。

## Key Contributions

- Operationalizes a previously implicit failure mode — agents that retrieve candidate snippets then finalize without ever calling `read` — through a deterministic trajectory-level decomposition computed purely from saved tool calls, retrieved snippets, read passages, gold-evidence annotations, and final-answer correctness.
- Introduces READ-GATE, a single runtime invariant ("after search, the agent must read before final") that is orthogonal to model weights, retriever, index, judge, decoding temperature, and reasoning budget, making it the lightest possible intervention targeting this failure class.
- Validates three falsifiable predictions (H1 non-redundancy, H2 conditional benefit tracking residual discipline error, H3 differential signatures from reasoning effort) using multi-label reclassification, paired McNemar tests, and question-clustered logistic regressions.
- Shows that instruction-only (strict prompt) controls can reduce the surface zero-read behavior but cannot recover READ-GATE's accuracy gain, and that context injection of the same chunk text (ctx-inject) recovers at most a minority of the gain and is net-negative on 2Wiki (−7.4 pp), isolating the value of the self-issued `read` action itself.
- Demonstrates via a Gemini 2.5 Flash thinking-budget contrast that hidden deliberation and external evidence inspection can vary independently — more compute does not guarantee procedural compliance.

## Methodology

The paper works within a two-tool agentic RAG interface exposing discrete `search(query, k)`, `read(evidence_id)`, and `final` actions. Hybrid search combines BM25 and a 0.6B dense retriever (Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B) via reciprocal-rank fusion (k=60) and returns the top-5 chunk IDs with five-sentence snippets; `read` expands a chunk ID into its full text.

**Trajectory decomposition.** Each wrong trajectory is assigned to the first matching bucket in a fixed priority order: `E_wrong = E_disc ⊎ E_post ⊎ E_retr ⊎ E_amb`. Discipline failure `E_disc` has three subtypes — (a) no-read final (read count=0, the primary signal requiring no entity heuristic), (b) snippet-only final (answer entity appears only in search snippets, never in a read chunk), (c) low-evidence final (question named entities covered in read chunks at <80%). Post-gold-read failure `E_post` fires when gold evidence was retrieved and at least one gold-supporting chunk was read but the answer is still wrong. A complementary multi-label version (Pdisc, Ppost) is used for overlap tests.

**READ-GATE invariant.** A deterministic environment rule: if the model emits `final` with read count=0, the action is rejected and a corrective hint ("You attempted to answer before reading any evidence. You MUST call read(evidence id) on a promising chunk before giving the final answer.") is appended to the next observation; the loop resumes. The intervention operates only at inference time.

**Setup.** Main experiments use gpt-4o-mini and gpt-5-mini at minimal and medium reasoning effort on HotpotQA, 2WikiMultiHopQA, and MuSiQue, with n=1,000 paired questions per dataset–condition pair (12,000 OpenAI trajectories total). All conditions share temperature 0.0, max loop budget 10, max token budget 128k, and a fixed gpt-5-mini judge; Gemini 2.5 Flash is used only as an external thinking-budget diagnostic and Gemini 2.5 Pro for cross-judge robustness (κ=0.924). Statistical testing uses exact paired McNemar tests and question-clustered standard errors in logistic models.

## Results & Highlights

- **Forced reading rescues zero-read trajectories (Table 1).** On the self-selected zero-read subset under gpt-5-mini minimal, forcing a read raises LLM-Acc by **+14.9 pp on HotpotQA** (58.1→73.0, n=215), **+19.9 pp on 2WikiMultiHopQA** (42.1→62.1, n=261), and **+14.9 pp on MuSiQue** (22.5→37.4, n=617); all paired McNemar p<10⁻⁴.
- **READ-GATE improves full minimal-reasoning cells (Table 2).** On gpt-5-mini minimal, READ-GATE lifts LLM-Acc by **+3.2 pp on HotpotQA** (79.6→82.8), **+5.3 pp on 2WikiMultiHopQA** (64.4→69.7), and **+9.4 pp on MuSiQue** (34.2→43.6), while the ctx-inject control is −0.1 / −7.4 / +3.9 pp respectively and significantly worse than READ-GATE (McNemar p≤10⁻⁴).
- **Discipline and post-gold-read failures are largely non-redundant (H1).** Across 3,807 wrong cases the both-trigger rate P(disc ∧ post | wrong) sits in **[11.2%, 13.1%]** across regex and spaCy en_core_web_sm extractors (binary indicator Cohen's κ=0.628, agreement 82.1%), far below the 60% collapse threshold; the discipline-only share dominates post-gold-read-only by more than 2-to-1 (regex 46.5% vs 21.4%).
- **READ-GATE's gain tracks residual discipline error, not reasoning level (H2).** Correlation between baseline E_disc and Δ LLM-Acc strengthens under partial control for retrieved tokens (partial r=+0.881, n=6); under medium reasoning (low residual E_disc: 4.5/5.7/21.7%), gains collapse to +0.0/−7.0/−4.0 pp.
- **Different signatures over (Pdisc, Ppost) (H3, Table 4).** READ-GATE reduces Pdisc (OR 0.38 [0.35, 0.41]) without changing within-read-exposure Ppost (OR 1.00 [0.84, 1.19], n=1,863); reasoning effort (minimal→medium) reduces both (Pdisc OR 0.22, Ppost OR 0.51) with significant interaction OR 2.02, p<10⁻⁴.
- **More hidden thinking budget increases zero-read finalization (Table 5).** On Gemini 2.5 Flash, switching thinking budget from off to 1024 raises zero-read rate by **+5.7 pp on HotpotQA** (net −44 correct, p=0.0025), **+24.8 pp on 2Wiki** (net −67, p=0.0001), and **+42.6 pp on MuSiQue** (net −73, p=3.1×10⁻⁵).
- **Cost–accuracy implication.** Adding READ-GATE to gpt-5-mini at minimal reasoning closes 20–24% of the LLM-Acc gap to gpt-5-mini at medium reasoning, at modest overhead in reads and loop count.
- **Strict no-read-only lower bound preserves the discipline hump** (HotpotQA 9.3%, 2Wiki 15.4%, MuSiQue 51.5%), confirming the signal is not an artifact of snippet-only or low-evidence heuristics.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** High and well-scoped. The framing of "pre-evidence procedural failure" as a trajectory property distinct from answer-side reasoning is genuinely new; prior agentic RAG work (IRCoT, FLARE, A-RAG) makes retrieval controllable but does not isolate the execution-time case where retrieval is invoked and then bypassed. The READ-GATE invariant itself is intentionally minimal — almost trivially so — and the paper is honest that its contribution is the diagnostic framing, not engineering sophistication.
- **Rigor:** Strong on paired statistics and robustness. The decomposition is operationalized deterministically, and the authors report a strict no-read-only lower bound and threshold sweeps (low-evidence 0.6–0.9) rather than relying on a single heuristic. The multi-label reclassification with two independent entity extractors is a credible non-redundancy test. The cross-judge agreement (κ=0.924) mitigates LLM-as-judge concerns.
- **Reproducibility:** Excellent. Cached analysis outputs and per-question labels allow every number to be reproduced without API access; code and 33,950 trajectories are released. The two externally-sourced A-RAG comparison rows are documented in the release.
- **Significance:** Practically useful. The "zero-read finalization rate in unlabeled logs" is a deployable trigger that does not need gold labels, and the cost-accuracy framing (closing 20–24% of the minimal→medium reasoning gap) is actionable.
- **Red flags / weaknesses:**
  - The `E_post` analysis depends on per-chunk gold-evidence annotations, which the processed MuSiQue export lacks, so MuSiQue's post-gold-read analyses are restricted; the most important non-redundancy test (H1) is therefore fully established only on HotpotQA and 2WikiMultiHopQA.
  - The H2 dose-response correlation rests on very few cells (n=6 main OpenAI cells; n=13 cross-family), which the authors themselves flag as a robustness check rather than a confirmatory test.
  - The ctx-inject ablation shows the self-issued `read` action matters more than passive text delivery, but the four-arm channel-vs-action probe is run only on MuSiQue with a single backbone (gpt-5-mini minimal, n=500), so the action-commitment mechanism is not conclusively isolated.
  - The main controllers are limited to gpt-4o-mini and gpt-5-mini (with Gemini 2.5 Flash only as an external diagnostic); behavior of larger frontier controllers and non-English / non-Wikipedia domains is left to future work.
  - On medium-reasoning cells READ-GATE can be net-negative (2Wiki −7.0 pp, MuSiQue −4.0 pp) because forced reads exhaust the loop budget of an already-competent agent — a real deployment hazard.

## Strengths

- Clean, falsifiable three-prediction structure (H1/H2/H3) tested with appropriately paired statistics rather than aggregate bars.
- Mechanism ablations (prompt-only, ctx-inject, four-arm channel probe) genuinely attempt to rule out the most plausible alternative explanation (more context in the window).
- Honest about scope: the paper repeatedly states READ-GATE is a diagnostic, not a universally optimal decoding policy, and explicitly warns it should not justify deploying low-effort agents without downstream verification.
- Stable under multiple robustness perturbations: low-evidence threshold sweeps, strict no-read-only lower bound, paired bootstrap intervals, label-permutation null, cross-family (Qwen2.5 3B/7B/14B) and cross-judge (Gemini 2.5 Pro) replication.
- Deployable insight that the unlabeled zero-read rate is a sufficient operational trigger for enabling the gate.

## Limitations

- Main experiments are restricted to cost-sensitive OpenAI controllers; larger frontier models, additional model families, and non-English settings are untested.
- All three datasets are English Wikipedia-style multi-hop QA where snippet-grounded shortcutting makes discipline failures especially visible; entity-coverage thresholds and gate behavior would need recalibration per domain.
- The framework presumes discrete `search`/`read`/`final` tool actions; agents that interleave retrieval and generation implicitly (no observable read boundary) are out of scope.
- READ-GATE assumes search returns useful candidates often enough that forcing a read exposes relevant evidence; it is not a retrieval-quality fix and modestly increases reads-per-question and retrieved tokens.
- The post-gold-read bucket requires per-chunk gold annotations, which MuSiQue's processed export lacks, so the strongest non-redundancy claim does not fully extend to MuSiQue.
- The action-commitment mechanism (self-issued read > passive context) is isolated on a single dataset and backbone, leaving generality open.

## Relevance

This paper is highly relevant to research on agentic RAG evaluation and reasoning-control loops: it provides a trajectory-level decomposition that exposes when agents fail procedurally rather than reasoning-wise, directly informing better evaluation harnesses, runtime safeguards, and the design of search-augmented LLM agents. The READ-GATE invariant and the unlabeled zero-read trigger are concrete, low-cost interventions practitioners can apply immediately to multi-hop QA pipelines.

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the non-redundancy between Pdisc and Ppost hold for larger frontier controllers (gpt-5, Claude Opus-class) and for non-Wikipedia RAG domains (e.g., legal, biomedical) where snippet semantics differ?
- Can READ-GATE be combined with retrieval-quality monitoring and answer-side verification into a layered runtime policy that avoids the medium-reasoning regression where forced reads exhaust the loop budget?
- Why does a larger hidden thinking budget *increase* zero-read finalization on Gemini 2.5 Flash — is the model substituting internal deliberation for external inspection, and does this generalize across reasoning models?
- Does the self-issued `read` action's advantage over passively delivered context generalize beyond MuSiQue and gpt-5-mini, or is it an action-commitment artifact specific to this backbone?
- Could the trajectory-level decomposition be extended to agents that interleave retrieval and generation without an explicit read boundary, by synthesizing an observable inspection signal from attention or generation-state probes?
