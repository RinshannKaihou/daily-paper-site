Removing evidence-bearing spans from prior CoT shifts the teacher-forced answer-preference margin — vs. a length-matched non-evidence control. The cleanest causal isolation in the paper.

Across 16 VLMs on the VS-Bench counterfactual benchmark, evidence-bearing content in a prior chain-of-thought acts as a reusable "textual shortcut" that competes with fresh visual recomputation. Removing this evidence shifts the answer-preference margin by +2.010 (positive in 16/16 models) and lowers prior-answer rate by 62.58 pp vs. the length-matched control. A training-free Fresh-State Attention Firewall (FSAF) raises visual update rate from 35.28% → 53.61% and cuts prior-answer rate from 39.22% → 3.67% on five Qwen VLMs.

Methodology

Matched counterfactual → causal isolation

The paper builds a matched counterfactual framework. For each task T=(I,Q,y) it constructs T⁻=(I⁻,Q,y⁻) — same question and answer space, but swapped visual evidence. The model first produces a coherent incorrect prior CoT R⁻ from I⁻; eligibility requires ŷ⁻=y⁻ and correct direct inference on I. So R⁻ supports y⁻ under I⁻ but conflicts with y under I.

To localize what in R⁻ carries prior control, the prior is decomposed by textual role: E⁻ (evidence — quantities, relations, deductions), A⁻ (explicit final-answer span), and remainder. Four conditions are contrasted at fixed model/image/question/prompt: intact prior (N), evidence removal (E), length-matched non-evidence control (C), answer-span removal (A).

Behavior is measured by free generation via VUR (visual update rate) and PAR (prior-answer rate), judged by Qwen3-VL-235B at temperature 0. Answer preference is read out through a judge-independent teacher-forced margin m(S)=ℓ(y|S)−ℓ(y⁻|S).

The intervention — Fresh-State Attention Firewall (FSAF) — appends a fixed re-query ("answer Q again using only the current image") and applies an attention mask that sets logits to −∞ for fresh-suffix queries against stale CoT keys, every layer, in both prefill and generation. All other causal edges stay intact; it is single-pass, training-free, and needs no evidence parser or target answer.

Strengths & Limitations

side-by-side

Strengths

  • Clean, falsifiable central claim — the length-matched E vs C control isolates evidence content from generic context length.
  • Cross-model breadth — 16 VLMs, four families, Base/Instruct/Thinking variants, with sign-consistent effects.
  • The "correctness ≠ retirement" stress test (matched support withdrawal, Extra Old Lift) is a genuinely novel diagnostic.
  • FSAF is training-free, single-pass, needs no evidence parser or target-answer access, and drives PAR to ~1–2% on Thinking variants.
  • Strong reproducibility hygiene — public checkpoints, deterministic seeds, judge-independent teacher-forced scoring, recorded provenance, coverage audits.

Limitations

  • No explicit Limitations section — caveats are scattered through the text.
  • FSAF evaluated on only five Qwen models on a single benchmark (VS-Bench); other families & non-math VQA untested.
  • Restricted to pairs both correctly answerable under direct inference — a filter that may inflate apparent gains.
  • The rule-based evidence-span identifier is tailored to math/diagram content and is not ablated against alternatives.
  • Not compared head-to-head with recent training-free visual re-attention methods (Look Again, Look-Back, visual contrastive decoding) as actual interventions — only cited.
  • Post-FSAF VUR (53.61% avg) still leaves ~half of eligible pairs un-updated; a non-trivial Thinking-mode gap remains (Qwen3-VL-32B-T: 31.8%→53.77%).
  • Requires exact identification of the stale assistant span — may not transfer to multi-turn agentic / tool-augmented reflection.

Q1 · GENERALIZATIONDoes FSAF transfer beyond Qwen to Gemma-4, InternVL2, Kimi-VL-A3B — and does the attention-mask assumption hold for different positional/tokenization schemes?
Q2 · DOMAINHow does the shortcut behave on non-math VQA (real-world scene editing, OCR, chart QA) where the rule-based evidence-span identifier may fail?
Q3 · STACKINGCan FSAF combine with visual re-attention training (Look Again, Look-Back) for compounding gains — or do they target overlapping failure modes?
Q4 · AGENCYDoes Extra Old Lift predict downstream behavior in multi-turn agentic settings where stale premises propagate across tool calls?
Q5 · PARSERWhat is the minimal sufficient evidence-span definition — would a learned, outcome-independent parser change the E−C margin magnitude?
