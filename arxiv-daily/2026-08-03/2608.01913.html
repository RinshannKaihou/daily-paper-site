02 · METHODOLOGY

One shared harness, one retriever — only the policy varies

830 questions
~100K-doc corpus
Paradigm
ReAct loop. Each step emits reasoning τₜ + action aₜ ∈ {search, visit, answer}; a trajectory ξ is the full trace.
Benchmark
BrowseComp-Plus — 830 EN questions, fixed ~100K-doc corpus, two-layer human qrels: evidence E(q) ≈ 6.10 docs/q, gold G(q) ≈ 2.90 docs/q.
Retriever
Held fixed: Qwen3-Embedding-8B, top-K=5, 512-token snippets. Only the agent policy varies across the six agents.
Harness cap
128 turns / 150 min. Exceeding either is flagged "incomplete."
Episode split
Trajectory cut into episodes (1 search + visits). Rₖ=evidence, Cₖ=cumulative, k*=last productive episode, M−k*=wasted tail.
Failure labels
RG (retrieval gap): failed & G*(ξ)=∅. UG (utilization gap): failed & G*(ξ)≠∅. Decided by qrels — no LLM judge.
Oracle ladder
Strip tools, inject E(q)/G(q) into GPT-4o reader at temp 0 → Oracle-E ceiling.
Open-web check
200-question BrowseComp-en sample, live commercial API; LLM judge supplies per-step productive/redundant labels (no qrels).

02

Failures split cleanly into RG vs UG — opposite fixes

Retrieval gaps account for 51.6–64.1% of errors in 5 of 6 agents; Kimi K2.6 is the exception with 52.0% UG. Same-accuracy agents can need opposite interventions: better queries vs better verification.

03

Early evidence or never — wasted tails dominate

After the first gold hit, 57.9–74.0% of later episodes add no new evidence; 77.5–93.6% of all episodes are non-productive. A K=20 cap retains 86.3–92.4% of accuracy; K=40 retains 97.0–99.8%.

04

The oracle ceiling is 87.5% — headroom is query-side

Oracle-E = 87.5%, Oracle-G = 87.4% (distractor density doesn't bind). Net Gain ranges from +13.4pp (GLM 5.1) to +49.5pp (gpt-oss-120b), with Spearman ρ > 0.95 to the qrels-RG ranking.

05

Repeat-querying is the cleanest behavioral failure signal

gpt-oss-120b repeats on 5.0% of moves vs ≤1.5% for the other five; repeat rate correlates with accuracy at ρ = −0.83 and cuts gold-hit probability by 1.9pp per SD.

06

Within-question panel: recall absorbs the stronger-agent edge

On 366 gold-hit questions, retrieving agents answer correctly 73.8% of the time vs 2.2% for non-retrievers. Recall alone absorbs 56–60% of each stronger agent's edge over gpt-oss-120b (4,919 trajectories, question FE, clustered SEs).

07

Open-web replication confirms the dissociation

BrowseComp-en, 200 questions, 4 agents: GLM 5.1 tops at 64.0% (~65 calls) vs Deepseek V4 Pro 61.5% (~81 calls). Accuracy-coverage r=0.99 vs accuracy-calls r=0.12; RG dominates 68–88% of errors.

04 · ASSESSMENT

Strengths & limitations

side by side

Strengths what lands

methodology · rigour · honesty
  • Deterministic, qrels-grounded attribution — the failure taxonomy does not rely on an LLM judge, a methodological advance over prior process analyses.
  • Clean experimental control: shared harness + shared retriever → the 26-point recall spread is provably query-side.
  • Layered evidence: cross-agent correlations → bootstrap → within-question panel with FE → oracle reader → open-web replication.
  • Honest about limits: six-point correlations, intervention gains are upper bounds, Tongyi-DR schema artifact, underpowered open-web judge.
  • Actionable per-agent prescriptions rather than one-size-fits-all leaderboard takeaways.
  • Reproducible: open-weights agents, public benchmark, promised code/data release.

Limitations what to doubt

scope · causality · confounds
  • No experimental validation of the prescribed fixes — headroom is quantified, not realized. Numbers are upper bounds.
  • Single fixed-corpus benchmark (BrowseComp-Plus); generalization to multi-hop QA, math, code agents untested.
  • Frontier models via proprietary APIs → drift; the GPT-4o end-to-end judge is a single point of failure, not double-judged.
  • Six agents is small N for cross-agent correlation; bootstrap and panel analyses mitigate but do not eliminate.
  • Within-question regression cannot separate query skill from parametric-knowledge-informed querying — the causal story is an upper bound.
  • Tongyi-DR harness mismatch (JSON-array query schema, 11.3% Bad-call) inflates its apparent failure rate.
  • No human evaluation of subtle/format error categories; SequenceMatcher thresholds (0.85 / 0.6) are heuristic.
05 · OPEN

Questions for further exploration

from the paper & its gaps
Q1 · Interventions
Do reformulation guards, snippet dedup/aging, evidence-driven stopping, visit gating, answer normalization actually reclaim the predicted headroom when implemented and ablated in isolation?
Q2 · Multi-hop
How do RG/UG ratios shift on multi-hop benchmarks (MuSiQue, FRAMES) where question structure forces sequential evidence dependence?
Q3 · Open-web qrels
Can the deterministic qrels taxonomy be extended to open-web settings without an LLM judge — via cached page-level annotations or retrieval-aware synthetic qrels?
Q4 · Causality
Is the within-question "73.8% vs 2.2%" gap partly parametric knowledge guiding queries? How would a closed-book control separate the two?
Q5 · Query batching
Does query batching (the only behavior surviving agent fixed effects) transfer causally — or merely mark models that are strong for other reasons?
Q6 · Durability
How stable are these findings as frontier models update? Will GLM 5.1-class agents still sit near the oracle ceiling in 6–12 months, or will the bottleneck shift back to retrieval?
Q7 · Training signal
Can the wasted-tail metric (M−k*) be used as a training signal for RL-based stopping policies, complementing BrowseConf-style confidence scaling?
Q8 · Replication
Will the open-web r=0.99 (4 agents, LLM-judged) hold up at N ≥ 8 agents with human-annotated qrels?
