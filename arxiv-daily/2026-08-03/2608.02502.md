> **一句话总结：** CMuon 通过将 DiT 中被拼接为统一张量的功能异质权重（QKV/FFN/AdaLN）拆分回独立子块后再做 Newton–Schulz 正交化，消除了 Muon 的子空间耦合，使 675M DiT 在 ImageNet 256×256 上 200 epoch 即达 FID 1.18，相比 AdamW（400 epoch 才到 1.21）取得 2× 以上加速，并修复了原始 Muon 后期收敛停滞的问题。

> **TL;DR:** CMuon fixes Muon's late-stage plateau in Diffusion Transformer (DiT) training by chunking fused weight tensors (QKV/FFN/AdaLN) back into their functionally independent sub-matrices before Newton–Schulz orthogonalization, eliminating cross-subspace coupling; on ImageNet-1K 256×256 with a 675M-parameter DiT-XL, CMuon hits FID 1.18 at 200 epochs versus AdamW's 1.21 at 400 epochs (a >2× speedup) and beats vanilla Muon's 1.29 at the same 200-epoch budget (Δ −0.11 FID).

## 核心贡献

- **揭示根因**：定位了将 Muon 直接用于 DiT 时后期收敛停滞的原因——标准 DiT 实现为效率把功能异质的参数（AdaLN 的 scale/shift、QKV、FFN 的 gate/up）沿长维度拼接成单一 2D 张量，Muon 对整个拼接矩阵做 Newton–Schulz 正交化，导致无关子空间共享一个 preconditioner，梯度协方差被错误混合。
- **提出 CMuon**：在正交化前把上述融合矩阵沿长维度切回原始功能子块，对每个 chunk 独立做 Newton–Schulz 正交化再拼接，从理论上证明可保持整体更新 Frobenius 范数不变（仅重新分配范数）。
- **SOTA 结果**：675M DiT-XL + VA-VAE 在 ImageNet 256×256 上 200 epoch 取得 FID 1.18（30 NFE），无需 REPA 等额外加速技巧，相比 AdamW（400 epoch 才达 1.21）实现 2× 以上加速。
- **系统性消融**：覆盖 chunking 块选择、学习率缩放策略（Vanilla / KellerJordan / MoonLight）、学习率 rescale 开关（√N_chunk）、学习率取值（1/2/3 × 10⁻⁴）、不同 VAE（VA-VAE / SD-VAE / DC-AE）、像素空间 DiT、512×512 分辨率及系统级吞吐优化。
- **工程落地**：给出并行 Newton–Schulz + 通信重叠、Triton fused kernel、batched NS 等系统优化，使 CMuon 在 8× H100 上达到与 Adam 几乎相同的吞吐（DiT-B 9.07 vs 9.51 it/s；DiT-XL 2.30 vs 2.32 it/s）。

## Key Contributions

- **Root-cause diagnosis**: The authors pinpoint why vanilla Muon plateaus on DiTs — standard DiT implementations fuse functionally heterogeneous parameters (AdaLN scale/shift, QKV, FFN gate/up) into a single 2D tensor for efficiency; applying one Newton–Schulz orthogonalization to this whole fused matrix forces unrelated subspaces to share a single preconditioner, mixing their gradient covariances and distorting each block's descent geometry.
- **CMuon algorithm**: A minimal, near-zero-overhead modification that splits these fused matrices back into their original semantically independent sub-matrices and orthogonalizes each chunk independently (then concatenates). The paper shows analytically that under Moonlight scaling the total Frobenius norm is preserved — chunking only redistributes norm across chunks.
- **State-of-the-art DiT result**: A 675M-parameter DiT-XL with VA-VAE reaches FID 1.18 on ImageNet 256×256 at 200 epochs / 30 NFE, more than 2× faster than AdamW (which needs 400 epochs to reach FID 1.21), with no auxiliary tricks like REPA.
- **Comprehensive ablations**: Chunk-block selection, LR scaling strategy, LR rescaling switch, LR value sweep, multiple VAEs (VA-VAE / SD-VAE / DC-AE), pixel-space DiT, 512×512 resolution, and system-level throughput optimizations are all studied.
- **Engineering deliverables**: Parallelized Newton–Schulz overlapped with communication, Triton fused kernels for unchunked params, and batched NS for chunked params, bringing CMuon throughput on 8× H100 essentially on par with Adam.

## Methodology

**Background.** The paper adopts the continuous-time Flow Matching formulation: a linear interpolation path x_t = (1−t)x + tz (t∈[0,1]) gives a constant-velocity target field (z−x), and the network F_θ is trained by minimizing ‖F_θ(x_t,t,c) − (z−x)‖². The Muon optimizer maintains a Nesterov-style momentum buffer M_t = µM_{t−1} + G_t, then updates each 2D weight via O_t = Orth(µM_{t−1} + G_t), where Orth(M) = UV^⊤ is the polar factor approximated by Newton–Schulz iterations, scaled by 0.2·√max(m,n).

**Subspace interference formalization.** Stacking N heterogeneous gradient blocks G_1,…,G_N into G ∈ R^{Nd×d}, vanilla Muon yields U_i = G_i·(Σ_j G_j^⊤G_j)^{−1/2}, whereas chunked Muon yields U_i = G_i·(G_i^⊤G_i)^{−1/2}. The shared preconditioner in the stacked case mixes gradient covariances across blocks; when dominant principal directions of G_i and G_j are misaligned, the summed covariance distorts each block's preferred descent geometry. This is the explicit mechanism the method removes.

**Chunking scheme** (for DiT-XL, hidden dim d = 1152):
- QKV projection [3456, 1152] → 3 chunks of [1152, 1152]
- MLP gate+up [6144, 1152] → 2 chunks of [3072, 1152]
- AdaLN modulation [6912, 1152] → 6 chunks of [1152, 1152]

**Norm preservation.** Under Moonlight scaling α_c = 0.2·√d_out for each chunk, the Frobenius norm of the stacked update equals 0.2·√(N·d_out·d_in), matching the unchunked α = 0.2·√(N·d_out). An optional rescaling switch multiplies chunked-component LR by √N_chunk, accelerating early-stage convergence.

**Optimizer scope.** Muon/CMuon apply only to 2D weights in attention/FFN/AdaLN projections; 1D parameters, embeddings, and the final layer still use AdamW (β=(0.9,0.95), wd=0). Training uses batch size 1024, bf16, gradient clip 1.0, EMA decay 0.9999, constant LR.

## Results & Highlights

- **Headline (DiT-XL 675M, VA-VAE, ImageNet 256×256, 30 NFE)**: CMuon reaches FID **1.18 at 200 epochs**, versus AdamW's **1.21 only at 400 epochs** (>2× wall-clock speedup) and vanilla Muon's **1.29 at 200 epochs** (Δ = −0.11 FID over Muon at the same compute budget).
- **DiT-B 130M (VA-VAE, 30 NFE)**: At 200 epochs CMuon FID **3.03** vs Muon **3.31** vs AdamW **3.49**; gap widens at 400 epochs (CMuon **2.57** vs AdamW **2.78**).
- **DiT-B with SD-VAE (200 epochs)**: CMuon FID **3.06** vs Muon **3.30** vs AdamW **3.60** — robustness across VAE choice.
- **ImageNet 512×512 (675M DiT-XL, DC-AE, 400 epochs)**: CMuon FID **1.60** vs Muon **1.65** vs AdamW **1.67** — gains transfer to higher resolution.
- **Chunk-block ablation (DiT-B, 200 epochs)**: Chunking FFN+QKV+AdaLN jointly gives FID **3.02** vs no-chunking baseline **3.31**; any single-block chunking improves by at most 0.1, confirming complementary contributions.
- **LR scaling strategy (DiT-B, 200 epochs)**: MoonLight scaling wins for both Muon (FID **3.31**) and CMuon (FID **3.03**); Vanilla unscaled gives catastrophic FID 8.73/6.94; KellerJordan intermediate (7.40/6.00). CMuon consistently beats Muon across all three.
- **LR rescaling √N_chunk**: Complementary to chunking — at 40 epochs CMuon+rescale achieves FID **3.78** vs CMuon alone **5.32** and Muon baseline **5.67**; by 80 epochs CMuon+rescale reaches **1.46**, the best.
- **LR sensitivity (DiT-XL, 200 epochs)**: 2×10⁻⁴ is near-optimal for both AdamW (FID 1.29) and CMuon (FID 1.18); CMuon at 140 epochs (FID 1.27) already matches AdamW at 200 epochs, confirming the speedup is not an LR-tuning artifact.
- **System throughput (8× H100)**: With system-level optimizations, CMuon reaches **9.07 it/s (DiT-B)** and **2.30 it/s (DiT-XL)**, versus Adam's **9.51 / 2.32** — essentially no throughput penalty despite the per-chunk NS iterations.
- **Pixel-space DiT (PixelDiT-XL 797M, 160 epochs)**: CMuon FID **2.56** vs Muon **2.70** vs AdamW **2.50** — AdamW wins here, which the authors attribute to their PixelDiT architecture search having overfit to the Adam optimizer.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty (moderate)**: The chunking idea is conceptually simple and arguably an "obvious in hindsight" fix once the subspace-coupling mechanism is named. The contribution is the diagnosis + formalization (Eq. 11 vs Eq. 12) rather than a new optimizer family; it modifies Muon's *application* to fused tensors rather than the orthogonalization itself. Still, identifying the fused-tensor coupling in AdaLN/QKV/FFN as the specific failure mode of Muon-on-DiT is a non-trivial empirical insight.
- **Rigor (good)**: Multiple controlled ablations — chunk-block combinations (Table 3/9), LR scaling strategies (Table 4), rescaling switch (Table 5), LR sweep (Table 6), VAE swap, 512×512 transfer, system throughput (Table 12). The main comparisons hold AdamW's LR (2×10⁻⁴) fixed across optimizers, which the paper itself flags as a caveat favoring MoonLight scaling.
- **Reproducibility (strong)**: Chunk configurations for DiT-XL (d=1152) are tabulated; Algorithm 1 + Algorithm 2 + Algorithm 3 (Newton–Schulz with quintic coefficients a=3.4445, b=−4.7750, c=2.0315) are fully specified; default hyperparameters for each (model, VAE, optimizer) triple are in Table 8. Code release is not mentioned in the text I read, which is a small concern.
- **Significance (high for the DiT-training practitioner community)**: As Muon becomes the default hidden-layer optimizer for foundation models, this paper offers a concrete, near-zero-overhead recipe for getting sustained (not just early-stage) gains on DiTs. The 2× AdamW speedup at 675M is the kind of claim that will be rapidly re-tested by the community.
- **Red flags / specific weaknesses**:
  - **Evaluation is narrow**: Only class-conditional ImageNet-1K at 256×256 and 512×512; no text-to-image, no COCO, no >1B-parameter scale-up. The 675M DiT-XL is the largest model tested.
  - **Final FID delta over AdamW is small in absolute terms** (1.18 vs 1.21 = 0.03 FID at 400-epoch AdamW parity); the practical value is the *speedup*, not the asymptotic quality gain. The paper is honest about this in framing.
  - **PixelDiT negative result** (Table 11: AdamW 2.50 beats CMuon 2.56) is explained away as "architecture search overfit to Adam" — plausible but not independently verifiable, and it is the only non-latent-space setting tested.
  - **Theory is thin**: The "subspace interference" argument is made via a toy stacked-matrix example and a Frobenius-norm-preservation identity; there is no convergence-rate analysis or formal bound quantifying the coupling distortion. The Appendix B.2 derivation is essentially a re-statement of Moonlight RMS≈0.2.
  - **AdaLN chunk count (6) is not justified**: Why 6 chunks of [1152,1152] for the [6912,1152] AdaLN modulation rather than, say, 2 or 12? No ablation over per-layer chunk count is reported.
  - **Headline FID 1.18 is without REPA**, but the SOTA-claim context (vs REPA-augmented DiTs) is not directly tabulated; comparing to other published ImageNet 256 FID numbers would strengthen the "SOTA" framing.

## Strengths

- Diagnoses a concrete, mechanism-level failure mode of Muon on DiTs (subspace coupling through fused tensors) rather than reporting an empirical trick.
- Zero-overhead algorithmic change — only a reshape + per-chunk NS call — yet yields a sustained, not transient, improvement over vanilla Muon.
- Excellent ablation discipline: every claim (which blocks to chunk, which LR scaling, whether to rescale, which LR value) is backed by a controlled table.
- System-level optimizations bring throughput back to Adam-parity, defusing the obvious "but Muon is slow" objection.
- Norm-preservation analysis shows chunking is principled under Moonlight scaling, not just heuristic.

## Limitations

- Evaluated only on class-conditional ImageNet (256 and 512); generalization to text-to-image, video DiTs, or >1B scale is unverified.
- Theoretical analysis stops at a toy example; no formal convergence or coupling-distortion bound.
- Negative result on PixelDiT is hand-waved away without an independent test of the "overfit to Adam" hypothesis.
- No public code release mentioned.
- Final FID advantage over a fully-trained AdamW baseline is modest (0.03 FID); the headline value is the compute halving, not asymptotic quality.
- Choice of chunk granularity (especially 6 for AdaLN) is not ablated and could be sub-optimal.

## Relevance

对关注扩散模型训练效率与优化器设计的研究者高度相关：CMuon 是 Muon 路线在视觉生成领域的具体落地，给出了可直接复现的 chunk 配置和系统级加速方案，对 DiT 类大模型训练成本下降有立竿见影的工程价值，也暗示了在 LLM 中类似 fused-tensor（QKV / gate-up）上做 chunk-aware 正交化的可迁移性。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does chunking fused tensors also help Muon variants (Scion, Gluon, PolarExpress) on DiTs, or is the effect specific to vanilla Newton–Schulz Muon?
- Can the subspace-interference diagnosis be formalized as a convergence-rate bound, predicting when chunking will help vs. when it will be neutral?
- Does the same chunking trick transfer to fused QKV / gate-up projections in decoder-only LLMs trained with Muon (e.g., Moonlight, Kimi-K2 setup)?
- What is the optimal per-layer chunk count (e.g., 6 for AdaLN) — is there a principled rule based on singular-value spectra or block initialization variance?
- How does CMuon behave at >1B-parameter scale and on text-to-image / video benchmarks where AdamW+REPA currently dominates?
- Can the PixelDiT negative result (AdamW 2.50 < CMuon 2.56) be reproduced on a non-Adam-tuned architecture search to test the "overfit to Adam" explanation?
