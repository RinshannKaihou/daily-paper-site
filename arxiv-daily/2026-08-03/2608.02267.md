> **一句话总结：** 本文针对"智能体是否能从内部检测自身压缩表示的充分性"这一问题，提出四层自我认证理论：静态层用 Bayes 风险分组恒等式定义决策论充分性并以精确 TV 阈值定价单次外部验证，序列层把认证建模为以任务损失为货币的最优停止问题，证明任意 δ-正确策略满足信息-任务损失下界 R(π,E_i) ≥ C_i·kl(δ,1−δ)，并给出 CTS（Certification Track-and-Stop）策略使成本渐近匹配该下界，极限常量 C(E_i,f) 可作为有限线性规划精确求解。

> **TL;DR:** For agents acting on a compressed representation whose aliasing may hide action conflicts, this paper builds a four-layer theory of self-certification: a Bayes-risk adequacy label, an exact one-shot TV threshold c\*=(Tr/2)(1−TV) for buying a verification, and a sequential layer proving R(π,E_i) ≥ C_i·kl(δ,1−δ) ≥ C_i·log(1/(2.4δ)) for any δ-correct policy (Theorem 8) and a matching Certification Track-and-Stop policy (Theorem 9). On a four-environment finite instance (Tables 2–3) the exact LP gives C(E_i,f) ∈ {0.6050, 0.6264, 0.4657, 0.4783}, and simulated CTS cost at δ=0.01 is still 1.89–2.48× the asymptotic constant; the boundary section proves C(E,f) does not govern kernel-switching policies, which can achieve O(1) instead of Θ(log(1/δ)).

## 核心贡献

- **决策论充分性标签 Adeq_E(f)**：将"表示是否充分"严格定义为风险层等式 R\*_E(U)=R\*_E(H)，并通过 Theorem 1（基于 aliasing 单元的 Bayes 风险分组恒等式）将其归约为"每个单元内正质量历史共享同一 Bayes 最优动作"。
- **单次认证不可能性与精确阈值**：Theorem 3 用 Le Cam 两点法给出内部认证 Bayes 错误 ≥ (1−TV)/2；Theorem 6 给出外部验证价值阈值 c\* = (Tr/2)(1−TV)，并明确该闭式对 A1 对称性、实验增强、等先验三项假设的不可缺失性。
- **序列认证常量 C(E_i,f)**：以覆盖线性规划（LP）形式定义，与一个 max-min 成本加权特征时间形式等价（强对偶 + Sion 极小极大），退化为两点情形时还原 Nitinawarat-Veeravalli (2015) 的二元受控检验常量。
- **匹配的上下界（核心结果）**：Theorem 8 给出信息-任务损失下界 R(π,E_i) ≥ C_i·kl(δ,1−δ)；Theorem 9 给出 CTS 策略 lim sup R/log(1/δ) ≤ C_i；Corollary 10 给出极限 lim R\*\_δ/log(1/δ) = C(E_i,f)。
- **诚实的边界与证伪**：用 Counterexample A 反驳了早先关于"有限态系统若有动作改变世界状态则不能自证"的"状态闭合"猜想；NT1 说明相同信息核但不同任务损失会改变最优设计；NT2 说明 kernel-switching 类策略以 O(1) 逃脱固定核公式，明确 Verify/Repair/表示修复留作开放问题。

## Key Contributions

- **Decision-theoretic adequacy label Adeq_E(f)** defined as a Bayes-risk equality R\*_E(U)=R\*_E(H), reduced by Theorem 1 (a cell-wise aliasing-regret grouping identity, explicitly attributed to Blackwell/Le Cam) to the absence of action conflicts within every representation cell.
- **One-shot internal certification impossibility (Theorem 3)** with the Le Cam two-point bound err(C) ≥ (1−TV)/2 between label-aggregated transcript laws, plus the **closed-form verification threshold c\*=(Tr/2)(1−TV)** (Theorem 6) along with an indispensability analysis showing the equal-prior, augmentation, and symmetry assumptions are each load-bearing.
- **Certification complexity constant C(E_i,f)** given in two equivalent forms: a covering LP min Σ g_i(a)·n_a s.t. Σ n_a·d(K_i^a‖K_j^a)≥1 for all j∈Alt(i), and a max-min cost-weighted characteristic-time form proved equivalent via LP strong duality and Sion's minimax.
- **Matching lower/upper bounds (core, Section 3.3)**: Theorem 8 (every δ-correct policy pays ≥ C_i·kl(δ,1−δ) ≥ C_i·log(1/(2.4δ))), Theorem 9 (CTS matches to first order), Corollary 10 (limit R\*\_δ(E_i)/log(1/δ) = C(E_i,f)).
- **Boundary honesty**: Counterexample A refutes the author's earlier "state-closure" conjecture; NT1 shows the same observation kernels but different task losses give different LP-optimal actions (factor up to 100× in loss-per-nat), and NT2 explicitly states the fixed-kernel constant does not govern policy-dependent (kernel-switching) transcripts, leaving Repair open.

## Methodology

The paper is a self-contained theory paper (108 pages, single independent author, no empirical datasets). It builds a **four-layer framework**:

1. **Static representation layer (Section 2.2).** On finite history H and action A, define the representation map f:H→U with aliasing cells H_u. Bayes risk under U-vs-H observation is compared and the gap decomposed cell-by-cell (Theorem 1). Adequacy is the binary label Adeq_E(f) = 1[R\*_E(U)=R\*_E(H)].

2. **One-shot certification layer (Section 2.3–2.5).** An internal certification rule C maps the loss-free transcript T_t to {0,1}. Le Cam's two-point method applied to label-aggregated mixtures P̄_0^t, P̄_1^t yields the TV bound (Theorem 3). Theorem 6 prices a single external Verify action: under equal label priors, A1 symmetric switching, and augmentation, a perfect Verify is worth buying iff c ≤ c\* = (Tr/2)(1−TV(P̄_0^t,P̄_1^t)). Counterexample A is constructed to refute a strong state-closure conjecture.

3. **Sequential certification layer (Section 3, core).** Model M1 specifies: K known environments E_1…E_K each carrying binary label Θ_i; per round the agent picks A_t∈A, observes Y_t∼K_i^{A_t}, pays task loss g_i(A_t); it must stop at finite τ and output Θ̂ with max_i P_i(Θ̂≠Θ_i)≤δ, minimizing R(π,E_i)=E[Σ_{t≤τ} g_i(A_t)]. The **certification complexity C_i** is the value of a covering LP (Eq. 40) over action counts n_a, equivalently a max-min over allocations and alternative environments (Eq. 41). The **CTS policy (Algorithm 1)** has four components: MLE/KL-projection environment estimator (Eq. 47); plug-in cost-ratio optimizer w\*(î) (Eq. 48); C-tracking sampling with √t-forced exploration (Eqs. 49–50); GLR stopping rule τ = inf{t: max_i min_{j∈Alt(i)} LLR_{i,j}(t) > β(t,δ)} with β(t,δ)=log(2t(K−1)/δ) (Eqs. 51–52). The lower bound uses the Kaufmann-Kopta-Kaufmann (2016) transportation inequality + change-of-measure against each j∈Alt(i); the upper bound follows the Garivier-Kaufmann (2016) Track-and-Stop template; proofs are in Appendices A and B.

4. **Audit-and-repair boundary (Section 4).** Works out an explicit kernel-switching example (NT2) where a single near-perfect Verify purchase truncates the loss stream, giving O(1) total cost vs the Θ(log(1/δ)) predicted by the static formula; identifies the open theorem needed to extend the analysis to policy-dependent kernels and to representation Repair.

Numerical illustrations (Section 5) are on a fully specified four-environment finite instance (Tables 2–3): exact LP solves via scipy.optimize.linprog (HiGHS dual simplex), CTS Monte Carlo simulation with 120–150 independent replications per (environment, δ) cell, parametric LP sweep over Verify price c_v∈[0.1,2.5] for Figure 2, and boundary instances B1 (LP infeasible → C=+∞), B2 (zero-loss action → C=0), B3 (binary degeneration recovers Nitinawarat-Veeravalli).

## Results & Highlights

- **Exact LP constants** for the four-environment base instance (Tables 2–3, c_v=1.0): C(E0,f)=0.6050, C(E1,f)=0.6264, C(E2,f)=0.4657, C(E3,f)=0.4783 (Eq. 55), each attained bang-bang on Verify (n\*\_Verify=C_i, n\*\_Act=n\*\_Ask=0). LP outputs from four-decimal parameters reproduce full-precision values to within 2.5×10⁻⁴.
- **CTS convergence to the LP constant (Figure 1, Table 5).** Normalized cost R(π\*,E_i)/log(1/δ) decreases monotonically toward C(E_i,f) at all four environments. At δ=0.01 the simulated ratio is 1.146 (E0), 1.217 (E1), 1.157 (E2), 1.151 (E3); these are 1.89×, 1.94×, 2.48×, 2.41× the respective LP constants, with the gap attributed to the conservative GLR threshold β(t,δ)=log(2t(K−1)/δ) and stopping-time overshoot. At δ=0.005 (log(1/δ)≈5.3) curves lie in band 1.0–1.2.
- **δ-correctness holds with margin.** Empirical error frequency was ≤0.01 in every (environment, δ) cell, including cells with target δ=0.1.
- **Lower bound form (Theorem 8):** for every δ-correct policy π, R(π,E_i) ≥ C_i·kl(δ,1−δ) ≥ C_i·log(1/(2.4δ)).
- **NT2 quantitative escape:** kernel-switching policy pays exactly 1.0 (one perfect Verify at c_audit=1.0) at every δ, while the static formula predicts Θ(log(1/δ)); the switching class beats the fixed-kernel optimum at E1 by ≥1.87× at δ=0.01 and ≥4.17× at δ=10⁻⁴, unbounded asymptotically.
- **NT1 task-loss coupling:** with two actions a1,a2 having identical observation kernels (d=0.5341 nats under both) but different task losses, the LP-optimal certification action swaps between loss profiles A and B; a kernel-only Chernoff criterion mis-prescribes and pays 50×–100× the optimal loss-per-nat ratio.
- **Audit-price frontier (Figure 2):** each C(E_i,f) curve is piecewise linear in c_v — pure-Verify line c_v/d_Verify^i through the origin up to environment-dependent kinks at c_v≈1.9–2.5, after which Ask-based plans take over and flatten at no-Verify LP values {1.130, 1.239, 1.083, 1.182}.
- **Boundary B1/B2 on numbers:** setting K_3^a←K_0^a for all a yields linprog status 2 (LP infeasible, C(E0,f)=+∞); setting g_0(Act)←0 with positive Act divergences 0.0081, 0.0010 yields LP value exactly 0 (free-ride).

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty (modest, narrowly scoped).** The mathematical shape of C(E,f) — a covering LP / max-min cost-weighted characteristic time — is admitted by the author to coincide with the constants of Nitinawarat-Veeravalli (2015, Theorem 5.1) in the binary case and Kanarios et al. (2024) in shape. The genuine novelty is the **intersection** of three features the audit found uncovered: (i) an alternative set defined by the self-referential adequacy label Θ_j≠Θ_i; (ii) costs that are task losses of the same environment defining the label; (iii) a δ-correct stopping objective priced in that currency. This is honestly framed but is a refinement of existing Track-and-Stop/active-testing machinery rather than a new technique.
- **Rigor (high).** Theorems stated under explicitly tracked assumption sets (H1–H4 vs H1–H3); lower and upper bounds have full proofs in Appendices A–B; LP values verified by both dual-simplex solves and grid-based max-min evaluation to within 2.5×10⁻⁴ on the instance and <1% relative error on randomly generated five-environment three-action instances; the binary specialization (B3) is verified numerically to recover Nitinawarat-Veeravalli exactly.
- **Reproducibility (high for theory, weak for empirical claims).** All instance parameters are tabulated (Tables 2–3, Eqs. 56, 58); no external datasets, no learned models, no hyperparameter sweeps beyond stated grid; CTS simulation uses standard SciPy tools with per-cell seeded streams. However, the asymptotic regime is not reached: at δ=0.01 the normalized cost is still 1.9–2.5× C(E_i,f), and the author concedes "asymptotic convergence to C(E,f) requires smaller δ than we simulate". The numerical section is honest verification of finite instances, not a benchmark against competing policies on a realistic task.
- **Significance (theoretical, narrow audience).** The framework targets the ESD (Event-State-Decision) memory paradigm motivated by dialogue agents, but contains no dialogue data, no learned-representation experiments, and no comparison against existing drift-detection (FLORA/Widmer-Kubat 1996) or information-directed-sampling methods on a common task. Significance is therefore conditional on the eventual resolution of the open Repair/switching program; the paper itself claims no results there.
- **Red flags.** (a) Single independent author with no institutional affiliation, email-domain qq.com, and a 108-page self-published theory tract — the barrier to independent verification of correctness is high. (b) The whole sequential theory is restricted to a **fixed-kernel** model (M1.2), and Section 4 is explicit that Verify-driven policy switching, Repair, and representation dynamics all violate this premise and can collapse the cost to O(1) — i.e., the proven constant does not bound the cost of the certification procedures the agent would actually want to deploy. (c) The "no loss feedback" assumption (Section 2.1) is the source of every unidentifiability result; the author concedes this is an ESD modeling choice, not a fundamental barrier. (d) The numerical CTS gap (1.9–2.5× the LP constant at δ=0.01) is large enough that practitioners operating at typical δ∈[0.01,0.1] will see substantially worse cost than the asymptotic constant suggests.

## Strengths

- Exceptional honesty about prior art, assumptions, and scope; the five-direction audit (Section 1.4) and Table 6 explicitly enumerate which adjacent line each tool comes from and which element of the present setting is uncovered.
- Full proofs, explicit assumption dependence per theorem, and a self-falsified conjecture (Counterexample A) reported in the body rather than buried.
- Clean mathematical structure: the constant C(E,f) has two equivalent characterizations (LP and max-min) with the duality spelled out, and the lower-bound technology composes transparently.
- Boundary statements B1–B3 and NT1–NT2 are stated precisely and instantiated numerically, demarcating exactly where the framework ends.
- Reproducibility package: all instance tables and channels are tabulated to four decimals and the LP/simulation pipeline is described in Section 5.7.

## Limitations

- **Fixed-kernel restriction.** Model M1 fixes one channel K_i^a and one loss g_i(a) per action for the whole horizon; the kernel-switching example (NT2) and Repair action are explicitly out of scope and remain open.
- **No empirical evaluation on real agents or representations.** Section 5 verifies finite hand-crafted instances only; no dialogue, RL, or learned-embedding experiments connect the theory to practice.
- **Asymptotic-only matching.** The constant C(E_i,f) governs only lim_{δ→0}; at δ=0.01 (a typical operating point) the simulated CTS cost is 1.9–2.5× the LP constant, with no tight second-order analysis.
- **"No loss feedback" assumption** is load-bearing for every unidentifiability result; with loss feedback (Proposition 5(b)(ii)) the picture changes, and the relationship to concept-drift methods (FLORA) is acknowledged as orthogonal but not integrated.
- **Composite-class complexity.** With K≥3 and shared labels the max-min form is genuinely a zero-sum game and single-action bang-bang allocations are generically suboptimal; the paper gives the LP but no closed-form design rules.
- **Single author, no peer review.** Correctness of the longer concentration and plug-in stability arguments in Appendices A–B rests on author-provided proofs.

## Relevance

对研究"智能体能否从内部检测自身表示/记忆是否仍然充分"这一基础问题有直接的理论价值，尤其与表示学习、状态聚合、POMDP/信念状态充分性、主动假设检验等方向相关；但其纯理论框架与固定核限制意味着对实证表示学习研究者更多是概念启发而非可直接应用的方法。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Can the C(E,f) characterization be extended to policy-dependent (kernel-switching) transcripts, and does a finite constant survive in the form of a dynamic program over posterior-dependent regimes, as Section 4.2 hints?
- What is the exact sub-logarithmic scale of R\*\_δ(E_i) in the B2 free-ride regime (C(E_i,f)=0)? The paper states CTS still pays Ω(√log(1/δ)) there and leaves tightness open.
- Can the framework incorporate loss feedback (Proposition 5(b)(ii)) without collapsing to standard online aliasing-regret estimation, and what does the resulting certification complexity look like?
- How do the LP and CTS policy behave when the environment class is large or continuous (K→∞), where Alt(i) becomes a continuum and the covering LP is no longer finite?
- Is there a non-asymptotic, mixture-threshold variant of the GLR stopping rule (in the spirit of Kaufmann-Koolen 2021) that closes the 1.9–2.5× gap between simulated CTS cost and the LP constant at δ∈[0.01,0.1]?
- Can the Repair action be brought inside the decision problem with a priced acquisition, given that a successful repair changes the alternative sets Alt(i) of all future certification problems (Section 4.3)?
