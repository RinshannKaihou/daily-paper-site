Hector Zenil & Luan Ozelim · arXiv:2608.01575

Across 46 frontier models, answering correctly has nothing to do with reasoning toward the known Bayes-optimal posterior — a new axis, “posterior fidelity,” is orthogonal to accuracy and unmoved by scale.

46 个前沿模型上“答对 ≠ 按最优后验推”，准确率与后验保真度 ρ=−0.19，且不随规模改善、被指令微调放大。   Two years of frontier releases show no fidelity gain; 45/46 models sit below a uniform-keystroke reference.
