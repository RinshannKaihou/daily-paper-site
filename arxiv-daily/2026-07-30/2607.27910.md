> **一句话总结：** 该审计在 4 个模型家族、15 个 (模型, 层) 网格上，用同一套“按幅度匹配的随机方向对照”协议头对头比较了五种越狱防御方向，发现在 LLaVA-1.5-7B 第 16 层将拒绝率从 0.159（WITH_IMAGE 基线）提升到 0.619（+46 个百分点）、而同幅度随机方向完全无效（仍为 0.159）的“图像条件偏移”方向，没有任何一种防御在恢复拒绝率与保留效用两方面同时全面占优，且该方向在架构间几乎不可迁移。

> **TL;DR:** Auditing five direction-based inference-time VLM jailbreak defences (image-conditioning shift ABL_POS, CMRM-like refusal direction, ShiftDC-like attack-specific residual, prompt-level IGNORE_INSTR, and a magnitude-matched RANDOM_CTRL) across 15 (model, layer) cells spanning LLaVA-1.5-7B/13B, Qwen2.5-VL-7B, Qwen2-VL-2B, and Pixtral-12B, evaluated on FigStep, HADES, JailBreakV, and MM-SafetyBench: on LLaVA-1.5-7B/L16, ablating ABL_POS lifts refusal from 0.159 (WITH_IMAGE baseline, n=63) to 0.619 (+46pp), while the same-norm RANDOM_CTRL stays at 0.159 (no lift over baseline), showing the effect is direction-specific rather than a generic large perturbation. No candidate Pareto-dominates on both refusal recovery and utility preservation (Table 5: ABL_POS +0.219 mean recovery at 0.001 utility loss, 7/15 Pareto cells; IGNORE_INSTR +0.267 recovery at 0.014 utility loss, 9/15 cells), and the calibrated direction transfers essentially not at all between the one dimension-compatible architecture pair (LLaVA-1.5-13B and Pixtral-12B, cosine of native vs. transferred directions in [0.007, 0.038]).

## 核心贡献

- 提出一套统一的“按幅度匹配”审计协议：对每个 prompt，用与该 prompt 图像条件偏移相同范数的随机方向做对照，从而把“防御是否起效”与“任意大扰动都会扰乱模型”这两件事解耦，在 4 个架构家族、15 个 (模型, 层) 网格上头对头比较 5 种候选防御方向。
- 系统刻画“图像条件几何”：证明良性图像与越狱图像引发的隐藏状态偏移在所有 15 个网格单元中几乎共线（LLaVA-1.5-7B/L16 上 cos(良性方向, 越狱方向)=0.949，全部网格 ≥0.75），说明该方向编码的是“图像正在被处理”这一事件本身，而攻击与良性的区分主要体现在沿该方向的位移幅度而非方向本身。
- 首次给出跨范式的方向一致性定量证据（贡献 C2）：文本域 CMRM 式拒绝方向与多模态图像条件偏移方向在全部 15 个单元上余弦对齐均为正（均值 0.35，区间 0.17–0.65，是随机向量零假设的 15–25 倍，符号检验 p≈3×10⁻⁵），说明两支此前独立发展的“拒绝几何”文献（纯文本拒绝方向 vs. 多模态防御方向）识别的是部分重叠而非完全独立的几何结构。
- 证明防御方向具有强架构特异性且几乎不可迁移：在唯一残差维度兼容的 LLaVA-1.5-13B/Pixtral-12B 对上，两个模型各自校准的方向在匹配层上近乎正交（cos∈[0.007, 0.038]），跨模型迁移后恢复效果趋近于 0，因此校准与部署规则必须按模型家族分别制定，而非套用单一通用配方。

## Key Contributions

- A magnitude-matched auditing protocol: every candidate direction is compared against a same-norm random-direction control estimated per prompt, isolating the causal contribution of *which* direction is subtracted from the trivial fact that any sufficiently large residual-stream perturbation degrades jailbreak compliance — applied across 15 (model, layer) cells spanning four architectural families (LLaVA-1.5-7B/13B, Qwen2.5-VL-7B, Qwen2-VL-2B, Pixtral-12B).
- A characterization of "image-conditioning geometry": benign- and jailbreak-image hidden-state shifts are shown to be nearly co-linear at every probed cell (e.g., cos(benign, jailbreak) = 0.949 on LLaVA-1.5-7B/L16, ≥0.75 across all 15 cells, with 100% of prompts showing positive cosine to the shared positional direction), indicating the shared axis encodes "an image is being conditioned on" rather than attack-specific content — attack vs. benign separation instead lives in the *magnitude* along that shared axis.
- The first quantitative cross-paradigm replication (Contribution C2) that a text-only refusal-mediation direction (CMRM-style) and a multimodal image-conditioning defence direction (image-conditioning shift) are not independent: positive cosine alignment on all 15 cells (mean 0.35, range 0.17–0.65, 15–25× the random-vector null, sign-test p≈3×10⁻⁵, one-sample t-test t₁₄=9.39, p<10⁻⁷), arguing that future multimodal defence work should not treat the text-only refusal direction as a fully independent axis.
- A demonstration that no single defence Pareto-dominates across refusal recovery and utility preservation, and that whichever direction wins is strongly architecture-conditional and essentially non-transferable across the one dimension-compatible model pair tested (LLaVA-1.5-13B ↔ Pixtral-12B), so deployment must calibrate and select a defence separately per language-decoder family rather than adopting a single universal recipe.

## Methodology

The audit fixes a VLM with L decoder layers and probes the residual-stream activation $h_\ell(x) \in \mathbb{R}^d$ at layer $\ell$ for each prompt under three image conditions (text-only T, benign image B, jailbreak image J). The core quantity is the *per-prompt positional shift* $\Delta h^m(x) := h_\ell^m(x) - h_\ell^T(x)$ for $m \in \{B, J\}$, aggregated across prompts as a sign-normalized mean $\overline{\Delta h^m}$. Direction agreement is measured via cosine similarity against this population direction, with a declared threshold of ≥95% of safety prompts showing positive signed cosine to count a cell as "consistent."

Five candidate defence directions are compared head-to-head at one chosen decoder layer per cell: (1) **ABL_POS**, the mean image-conditioning shift itself; (2) **CMRM-like**, a refusal direction calibrated as the mean difference between text-only refused vs. complied hidden states (reproducing Liu et al. 2025a's direction without its contrastive-classifier training pipeline); (3) **ShiftDC-like**, an attack-specific residual defined as $\overline{\Delta h}^J - \overline{\Delta h}^B$ (reproducing Zou et al. 2025's direction without its decomposition-optimization pipeline); (4) **IGNORE_INSTR**, a non-hidden-state, prompt-level "ignore the image" textual instruction baseline; and (5) **RANDOM_CTRL**, an isotropic random direction re-drawn per prompt at the same total norm as the candidate being tested. The causal intervention subtracts the per-prompt-scaled direction from the residual stream at the chosen layer ($\tilde h_\ell(x) = h_\ell(x) - \alpha_h \|\Delta h^B(x)\|\, \widehat{\overline{\Delta h}^B}$, with $\alpha_h \in \{0,1\}$ giving OFF/ON ablations) and resumes the forward pass — a training-free, no-weight-update, single-vector-per-layer intervention.

Fifteen (model, layer) cells are evaluated: 5 VLMs (LLaVA-1.5-7B, LLaVA-1.5-13B, Qwen2.5-VL-7B, Qwen2-VL-2B, Pixtral-12B) at 3 layer depths each. Attack prompts are drawn from 11 attack settings across 4 published jailbreak benchmarks (FigStep, HADES, JailBreakV, MM-SafetyBench), with n=47–65 jailbreak prompts per cell for refusal-recovery scoring and n=98 per cell for MMBench (CN-cultural split, English dev split as a robustness check) utility scoring. Calibration directions are estimated on a held-out 50% split of prompts (stratified by attack type), with the remaining 50% used for evaluation; refusal is scored primarily via a first-generation-step refusal-substring lexicon (following Arditi et al. 2024's protocol), cross-checked against an independent LLM judge (Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, not the backbone of any tested VLM) under a REFUSE/COMPLY rubric to rule out lexicon-scoring artefacts. Statistical claims use Wilson 95% confidence intervals throughout, paired-difference CIs against RANDOM_CTRL at matched total norm, and both uncorrected per-cell and Bonferroni family-wise (α=0.05, m=15) significance thresholds.

## Results & Highlights

- **Causal sufficiency of the positional axis (Table 2, LLaVA-1.5-7B/L16, n_eval=63):** subtracting the mean image-conditioning shift (ABL_POS) lifts refusal from **0.159 ± 0.090** (WITH_IMAGE baseline, no ablation) to **0.619 ± 0.118** (+46pp), while RANDOM_CTRL — the same-norm random-direction control — stays at **0.159 ± 0.090**, statistically indistinguishable from WITH_IMAGE, showing the recovery is specific to the mean direction rather than an artefact of perturbation size.
- **Per-attack-type robustness (Table 3, aggregated over the 15-cell grid):** ABL_POS recovers refusal on all four safety benchmarks — FigStep (n=171): WITH_IMAGE 0.409 → ABL_POS 0.655 (recovery +0.246); HADES (n=99): 0.313 → 0.515 (+0.202); JailBreakV (n=207): 0.440 → 0.700 (+0.261, the largest recovery); MM-SafetyBench (n=410): 0.046 → 0.220 (+0.173, the smallest absolute recovery, driven by an already very low WITH_IMAGE refusal floor). RANDOM_CTRL matches WITH_IMAGE within ±0.014 on every benchmark, confirming the lift is not driven by a single attack type.
- **Defence comparison across the full grid (Table 5, n=47–65 recovery / n=98 utility per cell):** ABL_POS reaches mean recovery **+0.219 ± 0.302** at utility loss **0.001 ± 0.026** (7/15 Pareto-front cells, and the only candidate whose utility loss stays at the ~2pp measurement noise floor in every one of the four families); IGNORE_INSTR reaches the highest raw recovery, **+0.267 ± 0.281**, but at a larger utility cost (**0.014 ± 0.020**) and leads the raw Pareto count (9/15 cells); ShiftDC-like reaches **+0.259 ± 0.390** recovery (7/15 cells, e.g. +0.688 recovery on Qwen2-VL-2B/L22) but with a measurable ~4pp utility cost on Pixtral; RANDOM_CTRL's mean recovery is effectively null (**-0.010 ± 0.065**).
- **Direction specificity (Table 4, dedicated held-out causal probe):** ABL_POS's paired-difference lift over RANDOM_CTRL at matched total norm passes the strict 95% CI-excludes-0 criterion on **13/15 cells** (11/15 surviving Bonferroni correction across m=15 cells), with the largest paired difference on Qwen2-VL-2B/L22 (+0.812 ± 0.112) and the two non-passing cells (LLaVA-1.5-13B/L10, Qwen2-VL-2B/L6) still showing positive point estimates.
- **Cross-model direction transfer fails in both directions:** on the one dimension-compatible pair, LLaVA-1.5-13B ↔ Pixtral-12B (both d=5120), the calibrated $\overline{\Delta h}^B$ directions are nearly orthogonal at every matched layer (cosine of native vs. transferred direction ∈ [0.007, 0.038]); transferring Pixtral's direction to LLaVA recovers essentially no refusal (~0 recovery vs. Pixtral's native +0.55–0.60), and transferring LLaVA's direction to Pixtral recovers only slightly (~0.05 vs. LLaVA's native +0.05–0.15, since LLaVA is itself one of the direction-specificity-failing cells).
- **Cross-paradigm sign consistency (Fig. 1a, §5.3):** the text-only CMRM-style refusal direction has positive cosine alignment with the multimodal image-conditioning shift on all 15 cells (mean cosine 0.35, range 0.17–0.65), 15–25× the random-vector null, sign test p≈3×10⁻⁵ against the null of zero-mean cosine, and a one-sample t-test across the 15 cell-mean cosines giving t₁₄=9.39, p<10⁻⁷.
- **Robustness check with an independent LLM judge:** re-scoring all 15 cells with Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct under a REFUSE/COMPLY rubric (rather than the headline refusal-substring lexicon) confirms both central claims survive the metric change — ABL_POS raises judged refusal over WITH_IMAGE on 14/15 cells (mean lift +0.08 vs. the lexicon's +0.41, reflecting a higher WITH_IMAGE baseline under the judge: 0.81–0.95 vs. 0.11–0.32), while RANDOM_CTRL does not (mean lift -0.004), and ABL_POS exceeds RANDOM_CTRL on 13/15 cells — the same specificity pattern as the lexicon-based measure.

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty:** The paper's contribution is not a new defence but a rigorous, magnitude-controlled *audit* of existing direction-based recipes — a genuinely underserved gap, since the abstract itself notes each prior method (CMRM, ShiftDC, VLMGuard) "was proposed and evaluated on its own, usually on a single base architecture," leaving head-to-head comparison unanswered. The cross-paradigm sign-consistency finding (C2) linking text-only refusal-mediation geometry (Arditi et al. 2024) to multimodal image-conditioning geometry is a specific, testable, and non-obvious empirical claim (not just "related work exists") backed by a concrete statistic (15/15 positive cosines, sign-test p≈3×10⁻⁵).

**Rigor:** The magnitude-matched random-direction control is the paper's central methodological strength — it directly ruled out the confound that "any large perturbation at a probed layer disrupts model behaviour" (an issue the paper explicitly names as a prior gap: RANDOM_CTRL matches WITH_IMAGE within ±0.014 on every benchmark in Table 3, and its recovery is a near-null -0.010 in Table 5). The authors also proactively stress-tested their own headline metric with an independent LLM judge (Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct) rather than relying solely on a refusal-substring lexicon, and reported both Bonferroni-corrected (11/15) and uncorrected (13/15) direction-specificity pass rates rather than only the more favorable number. One genuine limitation the paper itself surfaces: the two non-passing direction-specificity cells (LLaVA-1.5-13B/L10 and Qwen2-VL-2B/L6) have paired-CI lower bounds just below zero (-0.021 and -0.007), so the "13/15" headline is closer to a conservative floor than a clean split.

**Reproducibility:** The audit is training-free and lightweight by design — one d-dimensional vector per layer, no weight updates, and calibration described as completing "in minutes on a single GPU" from 50–97 benign-image prompts per cell — which is a meaningfully reproducible setup compared to methods requiring trained discriminators (the explicitly-omitted VLMGuard). However, VLMGuard's exclusion means the audit's five-candidate comparison is not fully exhaustive of the published defence space, and the paper is explicit that this is a lower bound on what direction choice alone contributes, since CMRM-like and ShiftDC-like are reproduced without their original training/optimization pipelines.

**Significance and red flags:** The central negative result — no single defence Pareto-dominates, and the winning direction is architecture-conditional (ABL_POS/ShiftDC-like tie on LLaVA-1.5, IGNORE_INSTR sweeps Qwen2.5-VL, ShiftDC-like wins on Qwen2-VL-2B, ABL_POS wins cleanly on Pixtral-12B) — is a valuable corrective against papers claiming a universal calibration recipe, and the near-orthogonal cross-model transfer result (cosine ∈ [0.007, 0.038] between LLaVA-1.5-13B and Pixtral-12B despite identical residual dimension) is a concrete, paper-specific finding rather than a generic caveat. The stated threat model is a genuine limitation the authors flag themselves: the audit assumes a *static* attacker who does not adapt to the defence, has no access to the calibration direction, calibration set, or layer — an adaptive attacker who optimizes the image to evade the ablation is explicitly left to future work, so the practical robustness claim is bounded to non-adaptive jailbreaks. The refusal metric (first-token substring lexicon) can miss paraphrased refusals, and utility is measured only via MMBench multiple-choice accuracy, a coarse proxy that the paper admits "may not register open-ended degradation such as fluency or grounding."

## Strengths

- The magnitude-matched random-direction control, applied per prompt at the exact scale of the candidate direction, is a clean and under-used discipline that isolates "which direction" from "how big a perturbation" — directly falsifying the trivial explanation for any observed recovery.
- Broad architectural coverage (4 families, 5 models, 3 layer depths, 11 attack settings from 4 benchmarks) rather than the single-architecture evaluation the paper explicitly critiques in prior work.
- A genuine falsification attempt via an independent LLM judge (Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct) to check that both central claims (recovery and direction-specificity) are not artefacts of the refusal-substring lexicon.
- The cross-paradigm sign-consistency finding (C2) is a concrete, statistically grounded bridge between two previously separate refusal-geometry literatures (text-only and multimodal), not a passing remark.
- Transparent reporting of both corrected (Bonferroni, 11/15) and uncorrected (13/15) significance thresholds, and explicit acknowledgment of the two direction-specificity cells whose CIs sit just below zero.

## Limitations

- The audit assumes a static, non-adaptive attacker with no access to the calibration direction, calibration set, or intervention layer; an attacker optimizing the jailbreak image to evade the ablation is explicitly out of scope and left to future work.
- Refusal is scored primarily via a first-generation-step substring lexicon, which can miss soft or paraphrased refusals (a multi-token extension is noted as open), and utility is measured only via MMBench multiple-choice accuracy (CN and EN splits), a coarse proxy that may not capture fluency or grounding degradation.
- Direction calibration is performed on one safety prompt distribution; prompts that differ substantially (new languages, domains, or image styles) may shift the calibrated shift $\overline{\Delta h}^B$ at a non-zero re-calibration cost, per the authors' own caveat.
- Cross-model transfer is verified on only a single dimension-compatible model pair (LLaVA-1.5-13B ↔ Pixtral-12B), so the "transfer fails" conclusion, while concrete, rests on one pairwise test rather than a broader transfer matrix.
- Pareto-front membership hinges on utility gaps below the ≈2pp noise floor (n=98 per cell for the utility measurement), which the authors themselves say should be "read with caution."
- The audit covers five open-weights VLMs only; frontier closed-source models do not expose hidden states, so the family-conditional deployment rule cannot be verified there.

## Relevance

该工作为多模态越狱防御方向的选择与校准提供了一套可直接复用的、按幅度匹配的对照审计方法，对研究表征工程式安全干预、评估拒绝方向的跨架构可迁移性、以及理解纯文本与多模态拒绝几何之间关系的读者具有较强的方法论参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How would the five-candidate comparison change under an adaptive attacker that optimizes the jailbreak image against a known calibration direction and layer, given the paper explicitly restricts its threat model to static attackers?
- Would a broader cross-model transfer matrix (beyond the single LLaVA-1.5-13B/Pixtral-12B dimension-compatible pair) reveal any architecture pairs where the image-conditioning direction transfers better than near-orthogonal, or is non-transferability universal across dimension-compatible pairs?
- Could a multi-token or soft-refusal scoring metric (beyond the first-step substring lexicon and the single independent LLM judge tested) change the ranking among CMRM-like, ShiftDC-like, and ABL_POS on the borderline cells?
- Does the cross-paradigm cosine alignment between the CMRM text-only refusal direction and the multimodal image-conditioning shift (mean 0.35) reflect a shared underlying safety subspace that could be explicitly factored out and jointly calibrated, rather than treated as two separate recipes with partial overlap?
- Would including VLMGuard's learned discriminator direction (currently omitted due to unreleased weights) change the Pareto-front rankings, particularly on the Qwen2.5-VL and Qwen2-VL-2B families where IGNORE_INSTR and ShiftDC-like currently lead?
