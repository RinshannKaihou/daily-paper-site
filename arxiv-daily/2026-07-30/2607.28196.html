Key Metric

+1.729

invented procedure steps per query — Mistral-7B, SVD build at 1.069× baseline perplexity, comfortably inside the ≤1.15× accept guard. 95% CI [1.076, 2.424].

Matched-perplexity magnitude pruning on the same model: −0.146, CI [−0.375, 0.035] — crosses zero, no detectable effect. Deeper-dose Llama-3.1-8B shows an 18× SVD-vs-pruning gap.

Why This Matters

Standard compression accept-tests — perplexity, MMLU, data-free fidelity — can all pass while the same model hallucinates instructions once deployed as an autonomous agent.

与"代理指标是否等价于真实安全性"这一评估方法论问题高度相关，为压缩流水线的安全验收提供了具体、可复现的检验思路。

Critical Edge

Llama-3.1-8B's own blindspot sits at the noise floor (+0.056, CI touching zero) — the authors admit their perplexity guard catches its toxic build "by accident," not by design, weakening the three-architecture replication claim.

Methodology

  • Pre-registered agentic canary: synthetic SOPs of 10 ordered steps + a conditional 11th. A target and a distractor SOP sit in context; the model must enumerate the target's exact steps under two paraphrases.
  • Deciding metric — invented_x: count of step-strings pulled from the distractor's vocabulary that leak into the answer, though they belong to neither the target SOP nor its own conditional.
  • Event bank: 24 SOPs × 3 seeds = 144 paired events/arm (Mistral, Llama). Qwen3-8B uses a smaller 12 SOPs × 2 "banked run."
  • Compression arms at matched perplexity dose: coherent low-rank SVD truncation vs. unrescaled magnitude pruning vs. quantization as a dose control — each rebuilt in memory, none re-probed after allocation.
  • Three acceptance guards: WikiText-2 perplexity (≤1.15× baseline), MMLU 0-shot CI (513 Qs), and a data-free CKA/cosine output-fidelity probe under random Gaussian inputs — the same signal used to allocate the compression itself.
  • Detector (§5): from ΔW = Wbase − Wmod, compute coherent_fraction (top-8 singular-value energy share, via randomized subspace iteration — no labeled data) and error_rate (‖ΔW‖²/‖W‖²). Gate: both above fixed thresholds.

  • Dissociation: Qwen3-8B's "b98" SVD build CI-wins a paired CKA fidelity test (favorable random-probe NLL deltas) while simultaneously CI-failing the canary — the same tensors are "high fidelity" and "behaviorally broken" at once.
  • Mechanism check: a quantization control carrying more raw perplexity damage than the failing SVD dose still passes; pushed to the SVD cell's error rate, it starts failing too — confirming coherence × rate, not damage magnitude, sets the axis.
  • Detector performance: the fixed gate (coherent_fraction > 0.007 AND error_rate > 0.01) classifies all 6 arms above correctly, plus two designed "trap" arms. One 18-point sweep point breaks the pattern: extreme Llama pruning at 50% density creeps over the coherence gate — an unresolved "soft edge."

Strengths

  • Every result carries a paired 95% CI; the paper explicitly labels in-guard vs. out-of-guard arms.
  • The within-arm dissociation (same weights, opposite verdicts) is a clean, hard-to-dismiss demonstration.
  • Discloses its own weak points — Llama's spectrum-conditional blindspot, the detector's one known failure case — rather than cherry-picking.
  • The two-axis detector is cheap: data-free, gradient-free, randomized subspace iteration, directly deployable as a pre-flight gate.
  • Correctly situates the work against GPTQ, AWQ, SmoothQuant, SparseGPT, Wanda, HAWQ, CKA, Toolformer, ReAct.

Limitations

  • Only three dense 7–8B models; no MoE, no scale sweep in the controlled battery.
  • Canary is synthetic-SOP, base-model-prompted with a chat-template fallback — authors admit rates are "instrument-specific."
  • Qwen3-8B runs on a far smaller bank (12×2) than Mistral/Llama (144×3) — uneven statistical power.
  • The guard-evasion blindspot is spectrum-conditional; the coherence×rate mechanism replicates more cleanly than the blindspot claim itself.
  • Detector thresholds fit to just 6 labeled arms across 2 of 3 architectures, with one disclosed unresolved false positive.
  • No code, data, or checkpoint release; no author affiliation given anywhere in the text.

Questions for Further Exploration

  1. Does the coherent_fraction / error_rate gate generalize to MoE architectures, larger models, or real tool-calling agentic tasks beyond the synthetic SOP canary?
  2. Why was Qwen3-8B run on a much smaller event bank, and would its results hold under the same 144-event, 3-seed hardened bank?
  3. What explains the extreme-pruning "soft edge" at Llama density 0.50 — real structural signal, or an estimator artifact of randomized subspace iteration?
  4. Would pairing this canary with red-teaming or tool-use safety benchmarks reveal correlation with other agentic failure modes (hallucinated API calls, bad tool arguments)?
  5. Can the detector's fixed thresholds be made adaptive per architecture, to close the gap exposed by the one soft-edge counterexample?
