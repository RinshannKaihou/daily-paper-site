Why This Matters

Any pipeline where an LLM consumes an upstream sensor's derived label — wearables, diagnostic assistants, sensor-to-language systems — risks this same instance-specific over-trust.

Critical Edge

The paper's own shuffled-teacher control shows roughly half its headline AUROC gain could be generic regularization, not ECG-specific privileged information.

Methodology

Case study: PPG-only wearable feeds a downstream LLM; ECG is training-only "privileged" ground truth
1 · SignalsPPG (deployment, 50Hz) + ECG Lead-II (privileged, 125Hz, offline-only)
2 · ECG teacherHierarchical Transformer over 8×30s windows → rhythm logits, AUROC .9912
3 · PPG studentB2 = plain weighted-BCE; K2 = B2 + window/global KD from frozen teacher
4 · Evidence textLLM sees only HIGH/LOW + p_low(4dp) + fixed sentence — never ECG
5 · ChallengeD1 conflict / D2 misleading history → COTR/CIR/CRR/ESRM/UHR
  • Matched-vs-shuffled intervention: case, prompt, history and timing held fixed; only the evidence-source patient is swapped, isolating instance-specific revision from generic "reliability language" caution.
  • Negative control: K2-global-shuffled permutes teacher targets across records — same architecture and losses, wrong correspondence.
  • 50,000 records / 1,275 patients (MIMIC-III Matched Waveform DB v1.0), patient-disjoint Train/Val/Locked-test split; hyperparameters (T=2, KD weights 0.15/0.15) frozen on validation before test access.
  • Protocol-locked replication (E3) on never-before-queried locked-test patients; robustness checked across 3 LLMs (Qwen3-8B, DeepSeek-V4-Pro, GPT-5.5) and 3 prompt templates (E1/E2).
17-arm ablation (B1/B2/K1–K5/H1/H2/S4) selected K2's recipe on validation only, before any locked-test access.

Honest null: TPR@1/2/5% FPR — the clinically stringent low-false-positive region — shows K2 vs B2 CIs crossing zero everywhere (e.g. TPR@1%: −0.0008 [−0.176, 0.162]). K2 improves global ranking, not hidden-irregularity sensitivity where it matters clinically most.
Cross-model / cross-prompt: pooled ESRM stays positive across Qwen3-8B, DeepSeek-V4-Pro, GPT-5.5 and 3 prompt templates (D1 +0.064 [0.029,0.097]; D2 +0.071 [0.035,0.108]) — a tested panel, not a universality claim.

Strengths

  • Clean, falsifiable DFOT formulation with fixed-denominator estimands — COTR/CIR are manipulation checks, CRR/ESRM/UHR are the real scientific quantities.
  • Matched-vs-patient-disjoint-shuffled evidence intervention directly tests instance-specificity, not mere presence-of-evidence effects.
  • Full protocol-lock discipline: recorded SHA-256 lock hash, frozen hyperparameters, genuinely independent E3 replication.
  • Reports unconfirmed/negative results (low-FPR TPR, a failed tail-objective control) rather than suppressing them.
  • Cross-LLM (3 models) and cross-prompt (3 templates) checks, scoped honestly as panel persistence, not universal invariance.

Limitations

  • B2 baseline has zero distillation-style regularization — the K2-vs-shuffled control (+0.0115) shows roughly half the headline AUROC gain (+0.0250) may be generic, not ECG-specific.
  • 55.17% of D1's deliberately induced conflict errors remain unrepaired by K2's best matched evidence.
  • No confirmed gain at safety-critical low-FPR operating points (TPR@1/2/5%) — a ranking improvement, not a demonstrated safety upgrade.
  • Evidence-interface ablation isn't factorial: ESRM gains can't be attributed to label vs. score vs. wording specifically.
  • ECG teacher's class-index/semantic alignment isn't fully recovered — limits mechanistic (not just behavioral) reproducibility claims.
  • Single case study (PPG→ECG cardiac rhythm); transfer to other modalities is a stated research agenda, not demonstrated here.

Questions for Further Exploration

  1. Would CRR/ESRM gains hold against a stronger non-privileged baseline (ensemble, calibrated selective prediction) instead of plain-BCE B2?
  2. Can a factorial evidence-interface study isolate which component — label, score, or wording — actually drives ESRM?
  3. Is the resistant low-FPR region a fundamental PPG-signal limit, or an artifact of the KD objective/temperature choice?
  4. How would COTR/CIR/CRR/ESRM/UHR transfer to non-physiological domains (RAG QA, vision-to-language, code-analysis tools)?
  5. Does the unresolved ECG-teacher index ambiguity (Appendix C) affect clinical interpretability beyond its behavioral effect on the LLM?
