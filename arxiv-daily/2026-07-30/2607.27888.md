> **一句话总结：** 作者用对立的"正面/负面特权提示"对同一条采样轨迹重新打分，发现 on-policy self-distillation（OPSD）诱导的 token 级似然变化在 91.4%–95.8% 的显著 token 上方向一致（而非按对错反转），且主要集中在"But/So/Let/Wait/Therefore"这类可替换的表面 token 上——据此提出 CSCR，只对这些"高敏感"token 衰减信用并重新归一化优势，在 Qwen3-1.7B 上把 AIME25 的 Mean@32 从 GRPO 的 36.7 提升到 43.6（+6.9），在五个数学竞赛榜单上全面超过 GRPO 及五种自蒸馏基线。

> **TL;DR:** The paper shows that on-policy self-distillation's token-level "privileged" likelihood shifts are not reliable answer-aligned signals — under two deliberately opposite (positive vs. negative) verifier-conditioned prompts applied to the same fixed trajectory, 91.4%–95.8% of jointly significant token shifts point in the same direction rather than reversing with correctness, and large shifts concentrate on substitutable discourse tokens (e.g., "But", "So", "Wait") rather than problem-specific content. Building on this diagnosis, the authors propose Counterfactual Sensitivity Credit Reallocation (CSCR), a minimal GRPO extension that downweights only high-sensitivity tokens and renormalizes the trajectory-level advantage; on DAPO-17K-trained Qwen3-1.7B/4B models it raises AIME25 Mean@32 from GRPO's 36.7 to 43.6 (+6.9) and beats the strongest of five self-distillation baselines by an average of 3.9 points (1.7B) and 1.7 points (4B) across five math benchmarks (AMC23, AIME24/25/26, SMT25).

## 核心贡献

- **提出"反事实敏感度"诊断范式**：固定同一条 on-policy 轨迹及其所有前缀，分别用极端正面（"完美解答"）和极端负面（"彻底错误"）两种特权提示重新打分，量化 token 级似然变化的方向一致性（Same-Sign）与幅度重合度（CPC，Counterfactual Perturbation Consistency）。
- **证伪 OPSD 的核心假设**：在 4 个模型规模（Qwen3-1.7B/4B/8B/14B）、400 条 DAPO-17K 轨迹上，82.4%–84.7% 的 token 在两种条件下均不显著；显著 token 中 77.2%–79.1% 同号，仅 13.2%–15.3% 反号；CPC 均值/中位数达 0.583（标准 GRPO 的对立优势理论上 CPC=0，见附录 A 证明），说明特权似然变化主要反映"对特权条件的敏感度"而非"与答案对齐的学习价值"。
- **定位敏感 token 的身份**：大幅变化的 token 高度集中在"The/But/So/Let/Therefore/Wait"等可替换的话语标记/句首词，正负两条件下 Top-50 高敏感 token 的 Jaccard 相似度达 88.4%；而近零变化 token 集中在数字、运算符等携带具体计算内容的 token，该模式在三种替代性反事实提示构造（答案级、温和结果级、参考解级）下依然稳健。
- **提出 CSCR**：定义敏感度分数 s_i,t = max(|z_pos|, |z_neg|)，对超过阈值 λ 的 token 用指数衰减函数降低原始权重（衰减强度由 α、上限 γ 控制），再在轨迹内重新归一化以保持总信用预算和 verifier 判定的优化方向不变。
- **实验与消融**：在 DAPO-17K 上训练 Qwen3-1.7B/4B，CSCR 在 AMC23/AIME24/AIME25/AIME26/SMT25 五个榜单上全面优于 GRPO 及 OPSD/SDPO/SRPO/RLSD/RLCSD 五种自蒸馏基线；消融证明"信任特权方向"（SD-GRPO）会在约 30 步内导致奖励崩溃和重复生成，而"适度下调"（γ=0.2）是最稳定有效的策略，过强的上调或下调都会破坏优化。

## Key Contributions

- **A counterfactual-sensitivity diagnostic protocol**: fixing a sampled trajectory and every token prefix, the paper re-scores each token under two deliberately polarized privileged prompts (Table 1: "flawless solution" vs. "completely wrong solution") and measures directional agreement (same-sign shifts) and magnitude overlap (Counterfactual Perturbation Consistency, CPC) between the two opposing conditions.
- **A falsification of OPSD's core assumption**: across four model scales (Qwen3-1.7B/4B/8B/14B, 400 on-policy trajectories from DAPO-17K), 82.4%–84.7% of tokens are insignificant under both conditions; among significant tokens, 77.2%–79.1% shift in the same direction versus only 13.2%–15.3% in opposite directions, and mean/median CPC is 0.583 (vs. a proven CPC=0 for standard GRPO's opposite-sign advantage assignment, Appendix A) — meaning privileged likelihood shifts largely encode sensitivity to conditioning, not verifier-aligned credit.
- **Identification of which tokens are being perturbed**: large shifts concentrate on highly substitutable discourse markers/sentence starters ("The", "But", "So", "Let", "Therefore", "Wait") — Top-50 high-sensitivity token lists under positive vs. negative conditions share 88.4% Jaccard overlap — while near-zero-shift tokens are dominated by digits, operators, and other problem-specific computational content; the pattern is robust across three alternative counterfactual constructions (answer-level, mild outcome-only, reference-solution).
- **CSCR**: a minimal GRPO extension defining per-token sensitivity s_i,t = max(|z_pos_i,t|, |z_neg_i,t|), applying an exponential-attenuation weight w_i,t (controlled by onset λ, decay rate α, and cap γ) to tokens above threshold, and renormalizing weights within each trajectory so the total credit budget and the verifier-determined sign of A_i are preserved exactly.
- **Ablations pinpointing the mechanism**: Shift-Directed GRPO (trusting the sign of the largest shifts) collapses reward and entropy within ~30 steps and produces degenerate repetition loops (Appendix E); sweeping γ shows moderate downweighting (γ=0.2) gives the most stable reward growth and gradual response-length increase, while stronger down- or up-weighting destabilizes training (entropy spikes, reward collapse, or length collapse to ~2,000–6,000 tokens).

## Methodology

The paper works within the standard GRPO framework: for a problem x, G responses are sampled from the old policy, each verifier reward R_i is converted into a group-normalized advantage A_i = (R_i − μ_R)/(σ_R + ε_A), and this single scalar is broadcast uniformly across all tokens of response y_i via the clipped PPO-style objective.

**Diagnosing OPSD.** On-policy self-distillation (OPSD) instead conditions the policy on privileged information c (e.g., a verified answer) and minimizes the forward KL between the resulting full-vocabulary distribution q_i^c and the unprivileged policy p_i at every prefix, implicitly treating the induced probability difference q_i^c − p_i as a valid, answer-aligned optimization signal. To test this, the authors construct two deliberately polarized "privileged prompts" (Table 1: an ecstatic, maximally affirmative evaluation of the trajectory vs. a maximally damning one) and re-score every token of 400 fixed on-policy trajectories (100 DAPO-17K questions × 4 responses) under both. They define per-token likelihood shifts z^pos and z^neg (Eq. 1), a full-vocabulary shift Δ_i^c = q_i^c − p_i, and trajectory-level statistics: combined magnitude M, disagreement D, and CPC = 1 − D/M, which would equal 0 for standard GRPO's opposite-sign advantages (proved by an antiparallel-logit-space argument in Appendix A) but is measured at 0.486–0.671 (mean/median 0.583) for OPSD's opposing conditions — showing the two "opposite" verifier framings actually push probabilities in largely the same direction.

**CSCR.** Motivated by this, the paper reinterprets shift magnitude s_i,t = max(|z_pos_i,t|, |z_neg_i,t|) as a measure of counterfactual sensitivity rather than answer-aligned value, and modifies only how GRPO's advantage is distributed across tokens (not the OPSD/KL machinery at all). Tokens with s_i,t below onset threshold λ keep unit weight; tokens above it get an exponentially attenuated weight w_i,t = 1 − γ(1 − exp(−α(s_i,t − λ))), with α controlling decay rate and γ ∈ [0,1] the maximum attenuation. Weights are then renormalized within each trajectory (w̃_i,t = w_i,t / mean_j w_i,j) and multiplied by the original trajectory advantage to produce the token-level advantage Â_i,t = w̃_i,t A_i, which is substituted directly for A_i in the GRPO loss. This preserves the total credit budget and the verifier-determined direction while reallocating credit away from highly sensitive (largely substitutable, surface-form) tokens toward less sensitive ones. Default hyperparameters: λ=0.05, α=10, γ=0.2.

## Results & Highlights

- **AIME25, Qwen3-1.7B, Mean@32**: CSCR reaches 43.6 vs. GRPO's 36.7 (Δ+6.9) and vs. the strongest baseline RLCSD's 37.2 (Δ+6.4) — the largest single-benchmark gain reported for this model.
- **AMC23, Qwen3-1.7B, Mean@32**: CSCR 88.8 vs. the strongest baseline RLSD's 85.3 (Δ+3.5), vs. GRPO's 84.1 (Δ+4.7).
- **AIME24, Qwen3-4B, Mean@32**: CSCR 74.4 vs. the strongest baseline RLCSD's 72.3 (Δ+2.1), vs. GRPO's 71.5 (Δ+2.9).
- **SMT25, Qwen3-4B, Mean@32**: CSCR 59.0 vs. GRPO's 57.4 (Δ+1.6) and RLCSD's 57.8 (Δ+1.2).
- **Aggregate**: the paper reports CSCR beats the strongest per-benchmark competing method by an average of 3.9 points on Qwen3-1.7B and 1.7 points on Qwen3-4B across all five benchmarks (AMC23, AIME24, AIME25, AIME26, SMT25), and it is the single best method in all ten model–benchmark combinations in Table 4.
- **Diagnostic evidence for the mechanism**: mean/median Counterfactual Perturbation Consistency (CPC) across 400 trajectories is 0.583 (range 0.486–0.671), meaning ~58% of the combined full-vocabulary optimization-signal magnitude points in the same direction under opposing positive/negative verifier framings — in stark contrast to the proven CPC=0 for GRPO's own opposite-sign advantage assignment (Appendix A).
- **Training dynamics**: CSCR maintains a higher smoothed reward than GRPO throughout 300 steps on Qwen3-1.7B and grows average response length gradually from ~10,000 to ~11,500 tokens (well under the 20,480-token cap), whereas GRPO plateaus near 9,500–10,000 tokens. Several self-distillation baselines (e.g., OPSD on Qwen3-4B: AMC23 94.6 and AIME26 62.7) underperform the un-tuned base model (96.2, 62.8) on multiple benchmarks, which the authors attribute to overemphasizing large privilege-induced shifts.
- Several baselines (OPSD, SDPO) notably fall below the untrained base model on multiple benchmarks/scales, a result the paper reports honestly rather than hiding.

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty**: The reframing of OPSD's privileged likelihood shifts as "counterfactual sensitivity" rather than answer-aligned credit is a genuinely useful diagnostic contribution, and the zero-CPC proof for GRPO's own advantage assignment (Appendix A) gives a principled reference point rather than an arbitrary baseline for the empirical CPC≈0.583 finding.

**Rigor**: The diagnosis is unusually thorough for this kind of paper — it is replicated across four model scales (1.7B–14B) and, in the appendix, across three additional counterfactual prompt constructions (answer-level, mild outcome-only, reference-solution) that all reproduce the same same-sign-dominance pattern (CPC 0.533–0.789 depending on construction), which meaningfully strengthens the claim that this is not an artifact of the specific polarized wording in Table 1.

**Reproducibility**: Uses public data (DAPO-17K) and open-weight models (Qwen3-1.7B/4B) with a named framework (veRL) and stated hyperparameters (λ=0.05, α=10, γ=0.2, lr=2e-6, 300 steps, 256 trajectories/step) — reasonably reproducible on paper, though no code link is given in the excerpted text.

**Significance / red flags**: A specific, paper-verifiable gap: the diagnostic analysis (Figure 1, Table 2) is run on Qwen3-1.7B/4B/8B/14B, but the actual CSCR training experiments (Table 4) are only reported on the two smallest models (1.7B and 4B) — it remains untested in the paper whether CSCR's gains persist at the 8B/14B scale where the same sensitivity pathology was diagnosed. The main results table also uses a single Mean@32 seed-averaging scheme without reported variance/confidence intervals, so the reported deltas (e.g., +1.2 on SMT25 for Qwen3-4B) are not statistically bracketed. Additionally, the ablation studies (Figures 4–5) show qualitative training-dynamics curves (reward, entropy, response length) for varying λ/γ but do not report a corresponding full per-benchmark accuracy table for those hyperparameter sweeps, so the sensitivity of the headline numbers to (λ, α, γ) choices beyond the single reported default is not directly quantified.

## Strengths

- Diagnostic methodology is careful and adversarial toward the authors' own hypothesis: they deliberately construct maximally polarized "opposite" conditions (Table 1) to stress-test whether OPSD-style privileged shifts could still encode reliable answer-aligned polarity, and report a formal proof (Appendix A) showing what a truly polarized (GRPO-style) signal would look like (CPC=0) for comparison.
- Robustness checks across four alternative counterfactual prompt constructions (Appendix C) and four model scales substantially reduce the risk that the central finding is a one-off artifact of prompt wording.
- CSCR is explicitly framed and implemented as a minimal, single-line modification to the existing GRPO advantage (token reweighting + renormalization), making the proposed fix easy to audit and to compare mechanistically against the diagnosis.
- Honest reporting of baseline weaknesses: the paper explicitly notes several self-distillation baselines underperform the untrained base model on some benchmarks, rather than cherry-picking only favorable comparisons.
- Ablations (SD-GRPO sign-trust experiment, up/down-weighting sweep) directly test the two most natural alternative hypotheses (trust the shift's sign; upweight instead of downweight) and show concrete failure modes (repetition loops in Appendix E, entropy collapse) rather than just reporting that the alternatives "underperform."

## Limitations

- CSCR training experiments are limited to Qwen3-1.7B and Qwen3-4B; no results at the 8B/14B scales used in the diagnostic analysis, and no non-Qwen model family is tested.
- Evaluation is restricted to competition-style mathematical reasoning benchmarks (AMC23, AIME24/25/26, SMT25); despite the introduction citing "math and code" as RLVR domains, no code-generation or other verifiable-reward domain is evaluated.
- CSCR requires two additional full-vocabulary re-scoring passes per token (positive and negative counterfactual conditioning) to compute the sensitivity score, adding non-trivial extra compute over plain GRPO that is not quantified in wall-clock or FLOP terms.
- No variance/confidence intervals are reported for the Mean@32 results, and no ablation table quantifies how sensitive the main accuracy numbers are to the (λ, α, γ) hyperparameters beyond the single chosen default plus qualitative training-dynamics plots.

## Relevance

该工作直接触及"信用分配"这一 RLVR/CoT 强化学习的核心问题，其对 on-policy self-distillation 有效性的证伪分析和轻量级修复方案（CSCR）对关注 GRPO 变体、token 级优势设计、以及长链推理训练稳定性的研究者具有较高的参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does CSCR's improvement persist when scaled to the 8B/14B models where the same sensitivity diagnosis was run, or does the attenuation mechanism need re-tuning at larger scale?
- How does CSCR interact with non-math verifiable domains (code execution, tool-use rewards) where "substitutable surface-form tokens" might differ qualitatively from math discourse markers?
- Would combining CSCR's sensitivity-based downweighting with an actual (non-counterfactual) OPSD teacher signal yield further gains, or does the paper's diagnosis imply the two signals are redundant/conflicting?
- What is the wall-clock/compute overhead of the extra positive/negative re-scoring passes relative to plain GRPO, and does it remain favorable at longer generation lengths?
- How sensitive are the main Table 4 results to the choice of (λ, α, γ) beyond the single default configuration, given that the ablations only report training-dynamics curves rather than a full accuracy sweep?
