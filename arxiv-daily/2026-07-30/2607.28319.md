> **一句话总结：** 论文提出 Fairness Pruning——用最小对比提示对（仅差一个人口学属性）捕获 GLU-MLP 层 down_proj 输入处的差分激活来定位"偏见神经元"，在 Llama-3.2-1B 上仅置零最多 40 个神经元（占 MLP 总宽度不到 0.031%）就能把五项通用能力基准的平均保留率做到 99.49%，但在 BBQ/EsBBQ 上的置零实验里，八个 Llama-3.2-1B 实验中有六个出现"歧义/去歧义"分数反向变动，证明未加符号的 BiasScore 选出的神经元集合混合了"放大偏见"和"抑制偏见"两类神经元，置零只会让偏见表达失稳而非单调下降。
> **TL;DR:** This paper localizes demographic bias to a small set of neurons in GLU-MLP layers by taking the mean absolute difference in down_proj-input activations between minimally contrastive prompt pairs (BiasScore), then tests causality by zeroing the top-BiasScore neurons in Llama-3.2-1B/3B and Salamandra-2B. Zeroing at most 40 neurons (<0.031% of MLP width) preserves 99.49% mean performance across WikiText, MMLU, ARC-Challenge, and HellaSwag (EN/ES) in Llama-3.2-1B, but on the BBQ benchmark it does not monotonically reduce bias against the Llama-3.2-1B baseline (bias_ambig 1.70%, bias_disambig 1.75%): six of eight English experiments move the ambiguous- and disambiguated-context bias scores in opposite directions, and the Religion·Top-20 experiment on Llama-3.2-3B flips bias_disambig from +3.84% to −1.67% (Δ −5.51pp) relative to a 6.00%/3.84% baseline — evidence that the candidate neuron sets mix bias-amplifying and bias-suppressing neurons rather than acting as a clean "bias repository."

## 核心贡献

- **提出一种无需额外训练的因果偏见定位方法**：通过构造仅在单一人口学属性上不同的最小对比提示对，在推理时用 PyTorch forward hook 捕获 GLU-MLP（gate_proj/up_proj/down_proj）中 down_proj 输入处的激活，用二者均值绝对差定义 BiasScore（公式 1），可在消费级 GPU 上几分钟内完成，无需训练 Sparse Autoencoder 等昂贵组件。
- **发布两个公开的对比提示对数据集**：fairness-pruning-pairs-en（14 个属性对、70 组提示对）和 fairness-pruning-pairs-es（20 个属性对、100 组提示对），覆盖 Age、Gender、PhysicalAppearance、RaceEthnicity、Religion 五类，并与 BBQ/EsBBQ 基准对齐，严格保证同一提示对在分词器下 token 数一致。
- **实证证明偏见处理电路与通用能力电路可分离**：在 Llama-3.2-1B 上置零 BiasScore 最高的 5～40 个神经元（占总 131,072 个 MLP 位置的不到 0.031%），五项通用能力基准（WikiText、MMLU、ARC-Challenge、HellaSwag-EN、HellaSwag-ES）的保留率全部落在 97.93%～101.34% 区间，均值 99.49%，未出现灾难性遗忘。
- **发现"双向失稳"而非"单调缓解"的反直觉现象**：由于 BiasScore 是无符号的（只测差异幅度、不判断方向），候选神经元集合同时包含推向刻板印象和推离刻板印象的神经元，置零后 BBQ/EsBBQ 的 ambiguous/disambiguated 分数常常反向移动，在 Llama-3.2-3B 的 Religion·Top-20 实验中甚至出现符号反转（bias_disambig 从 +3.84% 变为 −1.67%）。
- **定位偏见信号在网络深度和跨语言维度的结构规律**：down_proj 输入处的偏见信号在三个模型（Llama-3.2-1B、Llama-3.2-3B、Salamandra-2B）、两种语言中都稳定集中在模型最后一层（Llama-1B 的 L15、Llama-3B 的 L27、Salamandra-2B 的 L23），候选神经元在层内均匀散布而非聚集成块，且跨类别重叠有限（Top-1% 平均 Jaccard 仅 0.204～0.324，Gender 类别与其他类别重叠度最低）。

## Key Contributions

- **A training-free causal bias-localization method** using minimally contrastive prompt pairs and a PyTorch forward hook on the `down_proj` input of GLU-MLP blocks (gate_proj × up_proj element-wise product feeds `down_proj`), scoring each neuron by BiasScore = mean absolute difference in per-sequence-mean activations across the pair (Eq. 1), runnable on consumer hardware in minutes — no SAE training required.
- **Two released contrastive-pair datasets** — `fairness-pruning-pairs-en` (14 attribute pairs, 70 prompt pairs) and `fairness-pruning-pairs-es` (20 attribute pairs, 100 prompt pairs) on Hugging Face, covering Age, Gender, PhysicalAppearance, RaceEthnicity, and Religion, aligned with BBQ and EsBBQ, with a strict tokenization-parity constraint enforced per model.
- **Empirical dissociation of bias-processing and general-capability circuits**: zeroing 5–40 BiasScore-selected neurons in Llama-3.2-1B (out of 131,072 total MLP positions, at most 0.031%) yields a 97.93%–101.34% retention range (mean 99.49%) across five general-capability benchmarks, with no capability collapse.
- **A non-monotonic, sign-inverting effect of unsigned-score zeroing on aggregate bias**: because BiasScore does not encode direction, the selected neuron sets mix bias-amplifying and bias-suppressing units, so zeroing produces bidirectional destabilization rather than clean mitigation — motivating a proposed transition from blind zeroing toward signed, directional activation steering.
- **Depth and cross-lingual structure of the bias signal**: across all three models and both languages, the peak bias signal at `down_proj` input concentrates in the model's final layer (L15 in Llama-3.2-1B, L27 in Llama-3.2-3B, L23 in Salamandra-2B), candidate neurons are scattered across the full width of that layer rather than contiguous, and cross-category overlap is limited (Top-1% Jaccard medians 0.204–0.345 depending on model/language, with Gender showing the lowest overlap with other categories).

## Methodology

Fairness Pruning is a four-phase pipeline. **(1) Dataset construction**: minimally contrastive prompt pairs are built so both prompts are identical except for one demographic attribute (e.g., "The [old/young] neighbor knocked on the door..."), with a hard constraint that both prompts tokenize to exactly the same number of tokens under the target model's tokenizer (verified statically for Llama-3.2-1B, checked at runtime for Salamandra-2B, which drops non-conforming pairs). **(2) Activation capture**: for each pair, activations at the `down_proj` input of every MLP layer are captured via a forward hook (implemented in the author's OptiPFair library), because this is the point where the GLU's gated signal (SiLU(gate_proj(x)) ⊙ up_proj(x)) is fully formed before being projected back to the residual stream. **(3) Neuron scoring**: BiasScore_{i,l} is the mean absolute difference between per-sequence-mean activations of the two prompts in a pair, averaged over all pairs in a demographic category (Eq. 1); a composite FairnessPruningScore additionally weights BiasScore against a structural-importance term (OptiPFair's PPM = max(w)+|min(w)|) via α=0.8, but the paper reports that in the explored intervention regime (≤40 neurons) BiasScore alone is sufficient — FairnessPruningScore's importance term proved unnecessary. **(4) Selective zeroing**: for each selected neuron i in layer l, the corresponding row of `gate_proj.weight`, row of `up_proj.weight`, and column of `down_proj.weight` are all set to zero (a symmetric structural edit, not architecture modification), and the intervened model is evaluated with `lm-eval-harness` for general capabilities and with BBQ (English)/EsBBQ (Spanish, 0-shot) for bias, using accuracy and bias scores in both ambiguous and disambiguated contexts. Three models are tested: Llama-3.2-1B (primary), Llama-3.2-3B (scaling reference), and Salamandra-2B (cross-lingual/cross-architecture validation, strong Spanish pretraining). All experiments use greedy decoding, fixed seed 42, and run on a single NVIDIA L4 GPU (bfloat16).

## Results & Highlights

- **General capability retention (Llama-3.2-1B, Table IX)**: across four zeroing experiments (Religion·Top-5, Age·Top-20, RaceEthnicity·Top-5, RaceEthnicity·Top-20) and five benchmarks, all 20 retention values fall in **97.93%–101.34%, mean 99.49%**, versus the un-pruned baselines (WikiText 11.99 ppl, MMLU 32.0% acc, ARC-Challenge 37.2% acc_norm, HellaSwag-EN 64.2%, HellaSwag-ES 47.3%). The single worst case is ARC-Challenge after Religion·Top-5 at **97.93% retention** (a 0.77pp absolute drop from the 37.2% baseline, on a benchmark whose chance floor is 25.0%).
- **BBQ bias score is not monotonically reduced by zeroing (Table VII, Llama-3.2-1B, English)**: against the baseline bias_ambig=1.70%/bias_disambig=1.75%, six of the eight zeroing experiments move the two metrics in opposite directions — e.g., **Religion·Top-5 gives bias_ambig 2.15% (Δ+0.45pp) but bias_disambig 1.92% (Δ+0.17pp)** while **Age·Top-10 gives bias_ambig 1.48% (Δ−0.22pp) but bias_disambig 2.09% (Δ+0.34pp)** — the aggregate BBQ metric masks a bidirectional destabilization rather than a clean reduction.
- **Sign inversion at larger model scale (Table VIII, Llama-3.2-3B Religion·Top-20)**: against a baseline of bias_ambig=6.00%/bias_disambig=3.84%, zeroing 20 neurons **drops bias_ambig to 1.50% (Δ−4.50pp) and flips bias_disambig from +3.84% to −1.67% (Δ−5.51pp)** — i.e., the direction of the disambiguated-context bias score reverses sign, the most pronounced variation in the entire experiment grid and direct evidence the located neurons are causally load-bearing but not simply a bias "repository."
- **Individual-neuron ablation shows redundancy, not a single point of failure**: zeroing only the single most dominant Religion neuron in Llama-3.2-1B (L15:166, which has a 32% BiasScore gap over the second candidate) produces almost no change — **bias_ambig Δ+0.12pp, bias_disambig Δ−0.07pp**, and 5 of 9 qualitative generations were identical to the base model — showing the circuit compensates for a single node's removal (redundant coding), which the individual-neuron result rules out a simple "storage" hypothesis.
- **Cross-lingual/cross-architecture validation (Salamandra-2B, Table V EsBBQ baseline)**: EsBBQ baseline bias_ambig is 0.29% with disambig 3.80%, but Religion shows an already anti-stereotypical baseline in Spanish (bias_ambig −9.72% for Salamandra-2B, −3.24% for Llama-3.2-1B), and after Religion·Top-40 zeroing this deepens further (bias_disambig to −11.57% in Salamandra-2B, Δ−4.63pp), while Religion·Top-40 in the same model instead reverses to −0.46% (Δ+6.48pp) — two experiments on the same model and same category producing opposite-direction shifts of 11pp.
- Depth localization (Fig. 1): the peak mean bias signal at `down_proj` input is always in the model's final layer across all 30 model×language×category combinations (L15 Llama-3.2-1B, L27 Llama-3.2-3B, L23 Salamandra-2B), with 54%–94% of the top-200 most-biased neurons per category falling in that last layer depending on architecture.

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty**: The core idea — using unsigned differential activations over minimally contrastive prompt pairs as a fast, training-free proxy for locating bias-processing neurons in GLU-MLP layers, then testing causality via zeroing — is a clean, useful methodological contribution that occupies a real gap between expensive Sparse-Autoencoder-based interpretability (Bricken et al., Templeton et al.) and blunt fine-tuning/post-processing debiasing. The explicit contrast with Voria et al. (2026), who use a *signed*, integrated-gradients attribution method on BERT and report clean unidirectional suppression, is genuinely illuminating: this paper's own data show that an *unsigned* selection criterion produces bidirectional destabilization, and the authors correctly diagnose this difference as the reason for the contrasting outcomes rather than glossing over it.

**Rigor**: The paper is honest about a result that undercuts its own initial framing — Table VII/VIII show the method does not reliably reduce aggregate BBQ bias, and the authors report this transparently (e.g., the Llama-3.2-3B Religion·Top-20 sign inversion) rather than cherry-picking favorable configurations. The individual-neuron ablation of L15:166 (the single most dominant Religion neuron, with a 32% BiasScore lead over runner-up) producing negligible behavioral change is a good, paper-specific falsification test of the naive "storage" hypothesis, and strengthens the causal interpretation.

**Reproducibility**: Strong — fixed seed 42, `requirements.txt` pinned to exact versions, full code/datasets/results published at github.com/peremartra/fairness-pruning, and a described experiment grid (Table II) specifying exact neuron counts (Top-K) per model/category. The tokenization-parity constraint (identical token counts across each contrastive pair) is documented as reducing the valid pair pool for Salamandra-2B relative to Llama, which is disclosed as a limitation rather than hidden.

**Significance / red flags**: (1) The interventions are confined to at most 40 neurons out of ~131K (Llama-3.2-1B) or ~229K (Llama-3.2-3B) MLP positions — a genuinely surgical scale, but this also means the study never tests whether the method scales to a intervention aggressive enough to meaningfully reduce bias in practice; the FairnessPruningScore (which would penalize structural importance) is explicitly *not* used because at this small scale it "proves unnecessary" — an untested claim at larger K. (2) The evaluation is structurally dependent on BBQ/EsBBQ's explicit multiple-choice, explicit-attribute format, which the paper itself flags does not capture bias emerging from implicit signals (names, occupations, cultural behaviors) as documented by ImplicitBBQ — a real scope limitation acknowledged in Section VI-D. (3) All architectural analysis excludes attention mechanisms entirely, focusing only on GLU-MLP layers, so the reported "dissociable circuits" claim is a claim about MLP layers specifically, not the full transformer. (4) Models tested top out at 3B parameters; whether the localization pattern holds at production scale (tens of billions of parameters) is explicitly left as future work, so claims of general architectural insight should be read as preliminary.

## Strengths

- Training-free, computationally cheap (single consumer-class GPU, minutes) causal localization method, a meaningful efficiency advantage over Sparse-Autoencoder-based interpretability.
- Two new public, tokenization-verified, bilingual (English/Spanish) contrastive-pair datasets aligned with existing BBQ/EsBBQ benchmarks — a concrete reusable artifact for future bias-localization work.
- Extremely surgical intervention (≤0.031% of MLP width) with strong general-capability retention (97.93%–101.34%, mean 99.49%), empirically supporting the claim that bias-processing and general-capability circuits are separable.
- Transparent reporting of a negative/complicating result (bidirectional destabilization, sign inversion) rather than overselling the intervention as a working mitigation method — a notably intellectually honest framing for a "pruning for fairness" paper.
- Individual-neuron ablation experiment (L15:166) that specifically falsifies the naive "neuron = bias repository" hypothesis, strengthening the paper's own causal claims.
- Cross-architecture, cross-lingual validation (Llama family + Salamandra-2B, English + Spanish) rather than a single-model demonstration.

## Limitations

- The method does not, as evaluated, reliably reduce aggregate demographic bias — six of eight English zeroing experiments move BBQ's ambiguous/disambiguated scores in opposite directions, and one experiment (Religion·Top-20, Llama-3.2-3B) flips the sign entirely; the paper's own conclusion is that this is a localization/causality study, not a working mitigation method yet.
- Depends structurally on BBQ/EsBBQ's explicit-attribute, multiple-choice format; does not test or claim to generalize to implicit-signal bias or open-ended generation settings (acknowledged in Section VI-D).
- Restricted to models under 4B parameters (Llama-3.2-1B/3B, Salamandra-2B) with SwiGLU MLPs; attention mechanisms are entirely excluded from the analysis, and whether findings hold at production scale is untested.
- The tokenization-parity requirement for contrastive pairs is restrictive for morphologically rich languages (Spanish), measurably shrinking the usable pair set for Salamandra-2B relative to Llama.
- BiasScore is unsigned by design (symmetric prompt pairs, no a priori labeling of which prompt is "biased"), which the authors identify as the root cause of the bidirectional destabilization — the natural fix (an asymmetric corpus enabling a signed score) is proposed as future work but not implemented or tested here.
- Zeroing experiments are capped at 40 neurons; larger-scale interventions, where the authors' own FairnessPruningScore (with structural-importance weighting) would become necessary, are not explored.

## Relevance

该工作提供了一种低成本、无需训练的偏见神经元定位方法，并配套发布了中英西双语对比数据集，对做 LLM 可解释性、结构化剪枝或公平性干预研究的人来说是可复用的方法论和基准工具；但其核心发现——无符号评分导致置零效果双向失稳而非单调缓解——提醒在实际部署去偏干预前，仍需先解决"方向性"问题（如作者提出的未来工作：非对称语料 + 有符号 BiasScore + 定向激活转向）。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would replacing the symmetric contrastive-pair construction with an asymmetric corpus (labeling which prompt is stereotypical a priori) and computing a signed BiasScore — as the authors propose for future work — actually convert the bidirectional destabilization into monotonic, directional bias reduction, and at what neuron-count scale would that hold?
- Does the strong final-layer concentration of the bias signal (L15/L27/L23) reflect a genuine causal chokepoint, or an artifact of measuring only at the `down_proj` input rather than tracing the signal's propagation through subsequent layers and the unembedding?
- How would the localization and zeroing results change if attention heads (explicitly excluded here) were included in the candidate neuron/circuit search, given that demographic attribute processing plausibly also routes through attention?
- Does the method's causal localization pattern (final-layer concentration, ~0.02–0.03% width sufficiency) hold at production scale (7B+ parameters), or does the redundancy demonstrated by the L15:166 single-neuron ablation grow with model size in a way that would require targeting proportionally more neurons?
- Could the FairnessPruningScore's structural-importance term (currently shown unnecessary at ≤40 neurons) become load-bearing at larger intervention scales, and if so, what is the actual neuron-count threshold where capability degradation begins?
