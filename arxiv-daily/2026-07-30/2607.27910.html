A Cross-Architecture Audit of Direction-Based Inference-Time Defences in Vision-Language Models

Key Metric +46pp refusal 0.159 → 0.619 Ablating the image-conditioning direction (ABL_POS) on LLaVA-1.5-7B / layer 16 (n=63). A same-norm random direction stays flat at 0.159 — the lift is direction-specific, not just a big perturbation.
Why This Matters

Gives a reusable, magnitude-controlled protocol for choosing and calibrating multimodal jailbreak defences, and clarifies how text-only and multimodal refusal geometries relate.

Critical Edge

The audit assumes a static, non-adaptive attacker with no access to the calibration direction, prompt set, or layer — an attacker optimizing the image against a known defence is explicitly untested.

01 Methodology

  • Probe the residual-stream activation at a chosen decoder layer under three conditions per prompt: text-only, benign image, jailbreak image. The core quantity is the per-prompt positional shift Δh = h(image) − h(text-only), aggregated into a mean direction.
  • Five candidate defence directions are compared head-to-head at one layer per cell: ABL_POS (mean image-conditioning shift), CMRM-like (text-only refusal direction), ShiftDC-like (attack-specific residual, jailbreak minus benign shift), IGNORE_INSTR (prompt-level "ignore the image" baseline), and RANDOM_CTRL (isotropic random direction, re-drawn per prompt at matched norm).
  • Intervention: subtract the per-prompt-scaled direction from the residual stream at the chosen layer and resume the forward pass — training-free, no weight updates, one vector per layer.
  • Grid: 15 (model, layer) cells across 5 VLMs / 4 architecture families (LLaVA-1.5-7B/13B, Qwen2.5-VL-7B, Qwen2-VL-2B, Pixtral-12B) × 3 layer depths, evaluated on 4 jailbreak benchmarks (FigStep, HADES, JailBreakV, MM-SafetyBench) plus MMBench for utility.
  • Calibration on a held-out 50% split, evaluation on the rest; refusal scored by a first-token substring lexicon, cross-checked against an independent LLM judge (Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct).

  • Direction specificity: ABL_POS beats RANDOM_CTRL at matched norm on 13/15 cells (11/15 surviving Bonferroni across m=15); the two non-passing cells still show positive point estimates.
  • Cross-model transfer fails: on the one dimension-compatible pair (LLaVA-1.5-13B ↔ Pixtral-12B, both d=5120), native vs. transferred directions are near-orthogonal, cosine ∈ [0.007, 0.038]; transferred recovery collapses toward 0.
  • Cross-paradigm alignment: the text-only CMRM refusal direction and the multimodal image-conditioning shift are positively aligned on all 15 cells (mean cosine 0.35, 15–25× the random-vector null, sign-test p≈3×10&supminus;⁵).
  • LLM-judge robustness: re-scoring with Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct (REFUSE/COMPLY) confirms the same specificity pattern — ABL_POS beats WITH_IMAGE on 14/15 cells, RANDOM_CTRL does not.

Strengths

  • Magnitude-matched random-direction control isolates "which direction" from "how big a perturbation" — directly falsifies the trivial explanation for any recovery.
  • Broad coverage: 4 architecture families, 5 models, 3 layer depths, 11 attack settings across 4 benchmarks — not a single-architecture study.
  • Genuine falsification attempt via an independent LLM judge, not reliance on one refusal-substring lexicon.
  • Cross-paradigm sign-consistency (C2) is a concrete, statistically grounded bridge between text-only and multimodal refusal geometry.
  • Transparent reporting of both Bonferroni-corrected (11/15) and uncorrected (13/15) thresholds, including the two borderline cells.

Limitations

  • Static, non-adaptive attacker only — no access to calibration direction, prompt set, or layer; adaptive attacks are future work.
  • Refusal scored primarily via first-token substring lexicon (misses soft/paraphrased refusals); utility measured only via MMBench multiple-choice accuracy.
  • Direction calibrated on one safety prompt distribution; new languages, domains, or image styles may shift it at unknown re-calibration cost.
  • Cross-model transfer verified on a single dimension-compatible pair (LLaVA-1.5-13B ↔ Pixtral-12B) — one data point, not a matrix.
  • Pareto-front membership hinges on utility gaps below the ≈2pp measurement noise floor (n=98/cell) — authors flag this as read-with-caution.
  • Covers five open-weights VLMs only; closed frontier models expose no hidden states to audit.

03 Questions for Further Exploration

  • How would the five-candidate comparison change under an adaptive attacker optimizing the jailbreak image against a known calibration direction and layer?
  • Would a broader cross-model transfer matrix (beyond the single LLaVA-1.5-13B/Pixtral-12B pair) find any architecture pair where transfer works better than near-orthogonal?
  • Could a multi-token or soft-refusal scoring metric change the ranking among CMRM-like, ShiftDC-like, and ABL_POS on the borderline cells?
  • Does the 0.35 mean cosine between the text-only refusal direction and the multimodal image-conditioning shift reflect a shared safety subspace that could be jointly calibrated?
  • Would including VLMGuard's learned discriminator direction (omitted here due to unreleased weights) change the Pareto-front rankings on Qwen2.5-VL and Qwen2-VL-2B?
