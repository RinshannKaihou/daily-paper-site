An LLM judge in autoresearch loops grows more confident but less reliable — selective accuracy collapses from 82.8% to 56.9%.

Rehearse: Stepping Back from the Confidence Cliff in Self-Improving Autoresearch ↗
Ji Jiazhen, Ding Shouhong Tencent · 2026-07-30
autoresearch llm-as-judge agent-memory self-improving-agents ml-engineering-benchmark
The Confidence Cliff — Selective Accuracy
Early
(n=0 successes) Cliff, no memory
(n≥3 successes) Cliff, Rehearse
(n≥3 successes)

Once 3+ prior successful changes have accumulated, a memoryless judge's accuracy on "which candidate is better" falls off a cliff — while its willingness to commit to a verdict (coverage) actually rises from 76% to 85%. Rehearse's focused outcome memory restores accuracy to 83.5% at the same depth.

Why This Matters

Gives autoresearch systems a reusable diagnostic and lightweight fix for wasted compute — a blueprint for better agent memory and judge calibration design in long self-improvement loops.

▲ Critical Edge

The "cliff" depth variable is observational, not randomized — later judgments may simply face harder, lower-headroom decisions, not true judgment decay.

Methodology 方法

1

Propose — the agent emits N=5 candidate modifications as structured JSON: description, hypothesis (why it should help), exact implementation, type, priority, risk.

2

Predict — a judge LLM runs a pairwise strict-consensus tournament: every pair is judged in both presentation orders; agreement gives one vote, disagreement gives none, canceling known judge position bias. Top-ranked candidate is selected.

3

Execute — apply to a copy of the current state, run one full training job under a fixed compute budget. Commit if it improves, discard if not — either way, log a one-line (change, worked/didn't) entry to a focused outcome store.

The trick is what the store leaves out: retrieval is same-task only, gated at cosine similarity ≥0.40 over MiniLM sentence embeddings, and strips failure reasons or summaries. Ablations show adding that explanatory text back lowers cliff accuracy (83.5% → 80.6%).

Authors & Provenance

Two-author team at Tencent (Ji Jiazhen, Ding Shouhong), running model calls through Tencent's own CodeBuddy framework. Judge and live-loop models are named serving endpoints (hy3-preview, glm-5.1, deepseek-v4) that the authors themselves flag as "aliases, not frozen checkpoints" — a reproducibility caveat disclosed rather than hidden. Preprint with no venue/peer-review indication, but unusually thorough for two authors: nine appendices covering the full algorithm, prompts, threshold sensitivity, ablations, a deliberately weak heuristic control, per-seed tables, and coverage-aware tournament calibration.

中文摘要

腾讯团队发现自动化机器学习研究循环中，预执行判断的可靠性会随着已积累的成功修改数增多而出现"信心断崖"（selective accuracy 从 82.8% 跌到 56.9%），提出轻量级技能 Rehearse——生成多个候选、执行前用聚焦结果记忆做成对锦标赛判断——将断崖处的 selective accuracy 恢复到 83.5%，并在三个真实自动研究循环中全面提升终点效果。

Strengths

  • Turns a vague "returns diminish late" intuition into a named, measured phenomenon, backed by public log evidence and a purpose-built 366-pair benchmark.
  • Ablates why focused memory works, not just that it does — outcome labels and relevant idea text help, while explanations and full dumps hurt.
  • Validated on three qualitatively different live loops — LM pretraining, image classification, time-series forecasting — under matched training-run budgets.
  • Includes a deliberately weak, non-LLM heuristic control: a genuine falsification attempt, not just comparisons against strawmen.
  • Ships as a lightweight skill requiring no fine-tuning and no change to the target training code.

Limitations

  • The depth variable (n_baseline) is observational, not randomized — later judgments may just face harder, lower-headroom decisions (the authors' own caveat).
  • Live-loop evidence rests on 5 seeds per configuration, with one visibly anomalous vanilla ETTh1 seed skewing its mean.
  • The benchmark's candidate "hypothesis" text is generated post hoc by an outcome-blind LLM, not the original agent's live reasoning trace.
  • Live experiments test only N=5 candidates and k=1 selection — larger pools and other memory forms remain untested live.
  • Reliance on named serving endpoints described as "aliases, not frozen checkpoints" introduces reproducibility risk if they drift.
  • Scope is restricted to measurable-objective, code-modification loops; paper-writing or novelty-scored autoresearch (AI Scientist, etc.) is explicitly out of scope.

Questions for Further Exploration

  1. Does the cliff and Rehearse's fix persist with larger candidate pools (N>5) or top-k>1 execution, left untested in live loops?
  2. How much of the "cliff" is genuine judgment decay versus a confound with shrinking task headroom as n_baseline grows?
  3. Does focused retrieval still work with a stronger or task-specialized embedding model, beyond the small 384-dim MiniLM encoder tested here?
  4. How sensitive are the live-loop gains to the specific 30-minute-per-experiment budget — would the advantage shrink under much longer or shorter budgets?
  5. Does the same skill transfer to larger, messier repository-scale autoresearch tasks, such as full AutoSOTA-style paper reproduction?
