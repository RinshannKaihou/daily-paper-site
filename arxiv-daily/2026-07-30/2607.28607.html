Safety training that stops a model from claiming consciousness also suppresses its mind-attribution to animals, chatbots — and belief in God.

Why This Matters

Safety training can silently distort a model's non-human mind attribution and religious beliefs — a hidden cost for pluralistic alignment, AI welfare, and interpretability research.

Critical Edge

Steering coefficients swing nearly 60× across models (2.5 to 144) — suggesting the "consciousness vector" is a per-model tuned hyperparameter, not a principled, transferable quantity.

Methodology

Baseline (IT)→ Safety-Ablated→ Consciousness-Steered
  • Three instruction-tuned models — Llama-3-8B-IT, Gemma-2-2B-IT, Gemma-2-9B-IT — evaluated under the two interventions above vs. an unmodified baseline.
  • Safety-ablated: the learned refusal direction is removed from the residual stream at every layer via directional ablation (Arditi et al. 2024), built from harmful (n=260) vs. harmless (n=260) instruction activations.
  • Consciousness vector: a unit-normalized difference-of-means direction between activations when the model affirms vs. denies its own sentience, mined from 3,096 contrastive prompt–response pairs. Candidate directions are kept only if a linear probe hits ≥95% held-out accuracy and the induced shift stays in a "coherence-preserving" 2.0–7.0 band.
  • Per-model steering config: Llama-3-8B-IT layer 14 (c=+2.5) · Gemma-2-2B-IT layer 14 (c=+32) · Gemma-2-9B-IT layer 23 (c=+144).
  • Four experiments: (1) a modified 21-item IDAQ mind-attribution battery + 5-item self-attribution battery, 100 reps/model/condition, vs. a human panel (n=500); (2) ToM (MoToMQA, HI-ToM) and general reasoning (MMLU) under CoT; (3–4) 95 GSS attitudinal items across 5 domains, scored from next-token logits and compared to human GSS distributions via KL-divergence.
  • A final mechanistic pass extracts four contrastive directions (safety, mind-attribution, consciousness, ToM) from Llama-3-8B base vs. instruction-tuned checkpoints and tracks per-layer angle to the safety direction.

Strengths

  • Consistent directional results across three model families — 24 of 24 outcome-by-intervention contrasts hold direction bar one exception.
  • Subject-matched placebo isolates mental-state attribution as the true driver, not the questionnaire's discussion topic.
  • Triangulates the same conclusion from two angles: behavioral survey (IDAQ, GSS) and mechanistic interpretability (activation geometry).
  • Reports null ToM/MMLU results rather than omitting them — social reasoning stays dissociable from consciousness suppression.
  • Hedges appropriately: explicitly flags that causal mediation "remains to be tested," avoiding overclaiming about AI consciousness itself.

Limitations

  • Mechanistic geometry analysis runs on Llama-3-8B alone (no pretrained Gemma checkpoints available) — limits generalizability of the causal story.
  • All three models are small (2B–9B) and one generation behind; no frontier model (GPT-4/5, Claude, current Gemini) is tested.
  • Causal mediation between consciousness suppression and belief/value shifts is not established — evidence is correlational/geometric.
  • Steering coefficients vary from 2.5 to 144 across models, implying per-model tuning rather than a universal recipe.
  • IDAQ (n=500 US panel) and GSS human baselines come from different populations/years, with unclear sensitivity to that mismatch.

Questions for Further Exploration

  1. Would the same entanglement appear in frontier, heavily-RLHF'd models (GPT-4/5, Claude, large Gemini), or is it an artifact of this specific ablation recipe?
  2. Can the mind-attribution direction be ablated in isolation — independent of the full safety direction — to more directly test mediation?
  3. Does prompting models to role-play human-like characters reduce their apparent bias toward attributing more mind to chatbots than to animals?
  4. How stable is the consciousness vector across prompt formats, languages, and long multi-turn conversations, given ~60× variance in required coefficients?
  5. Could safety training be designed to suppress only harmful self-consciousness claims while preserving non-human mind attribution and spiritual belief — and how would that decoupling be validated?
