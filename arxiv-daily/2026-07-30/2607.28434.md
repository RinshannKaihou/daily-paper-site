> **一句话总结：** 论文提出一个无需训练、仅需一次前向传播的"聚合器/差异器"双通道读数工具，在 phi-4、Qwen3-8B 与基座 Llama-3.1-8B 上对隐藏状态几何评分，结果显示聚合器通道在 6/6 工程化文本-模型对中追踪预注册的语域边界，在精神分析师独立标注的临床访谈记录上 34/36 个方向性单元命中（偶然水平 AUC=0.5，中位 blue-operative AUC 为 phi-4 0.706、Qwen3-8B 0.661、基座 Llama 0.571），且按词汇类型跨文本迁移程度最高的基座模型（93rd 百分位）恰恰读得最差，指令微调后的同源模型在类型迁移不变的情况下把汇总残差化 AUC 从 0.585 提升到 0.627。

> **TL;DR:** Without any training, the paper scores every token of a text on two mechanistically distinct hidden-state properties — an *aggregator* (final-layer participation ratio inside a token-specific subspace) and a *differentiator* (transient cross-token recruitment during the forward pass) — across phi-4, Qwen3-8B, and base Llama-3.1-8B. The aggregator tracks a pre-registered engineered register boundary in 6/6 model×text cells and a psychoanalyst's independent markings on four clinical transcripts in 34/36 directional cells (chance = AUC 0.5; median blue-operative AUC 0.571–0.706 across models), while a same-split word-frequency baseline alone reaches AUC 0.875–0.878 — a gap the paper argues is expected and subtracts out, but a gap worth knowing about before trusting the raw numbers. A transfer test shows the model that reproduces anchor subspaces most by lexical type across texts (base Llama, 93rd-percentile transfer) reads the singular clinical discourse worst, and a matched instruction-tuned twin raises pooled residualized clinical AUC from 0.585 to 0.627 without moving type-transfer at all.

## 核心贡献

- 提出两个机制上彼此独立的隐藏状态通道：**聚合器**（aggregator，蓝色）衡量某 token 位置是否把全文汇聚成一个稳定的、终态的高秩配置（参与率/有效秩指标）；**差异器**（differentiator，红色）衡量在前向传播过程中有多少其它 token 被瞬时"搬运"进该锚点的子空间（隐喻的词源义：metá + phérein，"搬运过去"），二者分别对应"终态属性"与"处理过程属性"。
- 构建了一个训练自由、单次前向传播即可运行的工具：先在一篇"发现文本"上冻结全部超参数（核心子空间的正质量截断 μ=0.7、恢复阈值 τ=0.7），再在其余 12 篇文本上做纯确认性检验，避免事后调参。
- 设计了"操作视角"（原始各向异性空间，固定阈值）与"静默维度视角"（逐层 z 标准化，逐层第 95 百分位阈值）两种几何读法，发现二者在工程化语域边界上的对齐方向完全反转，说明两个视角揭示的是不同的子结构而非同一信号的噪声版本。
- 首次（据作者所知）把模型内部几何直接拿去对照精神分析师对临床访谈记录的独立标注（在工具建成前约九个月完成的标注），在词汇学基线（罕见度、长度、TF-IDF 等）与置换零假设的双重控制下报告残差对齐效果。
- 给出一个"关系性"结果：token 的结构价值（聚合器/差异器排序）在跨越一次远距离编辑后的稳定性（Spearman 0.73–0.97）明显低于其原始隐藏状态向量的稳定性（余弦 0.987–0.998），且按词汇类型的跨文本迁移程度与临床话语读取质量成反比——为"结构价值是位置的函数而非向量内在属性"的反本质主义论断提供了可证伪的量化证据。

## Key Contributions

- Two mechanistically distinct channels scored per token from a single forward pass: the **aggregator** (blue) — the participation ratio / effective rank of the final-layer configuration of all other pool tokens restricted to an anchor's core subspace (a final-state, consolidation property) — and the **differentiator** (red) — the "pull-gap" in how much of other tokens' depth-displacement energy transiently falls inside the anchor's subspace during the pass (a processing, transport property; metaphor in its etymological sense of "carrying across").
- A training-free, single-pass instrument whose constants (core-subspace mass cutoff μ=0.7, recruitment threshold τ=0.7) are frozen on one discovery text before any confirmatory run, keeping the rest of the 13-text corpus purely out-of-sample.
- Two anisotropy "views" — an operative view (raw, fixed threshold) and a quiet-dimensions view (per-layer z-scored, per-layer 95th-percentile threshold) — whose boundary alignment is shown to invert between them, establishing that they expose different strata of the representation rather than noisy variants of one map.
- A first (to the authors' knowledge) validation of model-internal geometry against a psychoanalyst's independent annotation of clinical transcripts, fixed roughly nine months before the instrument existed, scored with lexical-frequency baselines and two permutation nulls (a block null and a rarity-matched null) to separate the channel's genuine increment from what a word list already explains.
- A relationality result: a token's structural value (its channel rank) is measurably less stable under a distant textual edit (Spearman 0.73–0.97) than the hidden state it is read from (cosine 0.987–0.998), and the model whose per-anchor subspace content transfers most by lexical type across texts reads the singular discourse worst — operationalizing a Saussurean/Lacanian anti-essentialism about transformer representations as a testable, falsifiable claim.

## Methodology

The instrument analyzes three causal transformer LMs in a single deterministic, no-sampling, teacher-forced forward pass each: **microsoft/phi-4** (14B, 40 layers), **Qwen/Qwen3-8B** (8B, 36 layers, both instruction-tuned), and **meta-llama/Llama-3.1-8B** (8B, 32 layers, base/no instruction tuning). A fourth run set, **Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct**, is added as a matched-lineage follow-up over every corpus text with the same pipeline, isolating what instruction tuning changes versus what the base/instruct lineage shares; it is reported separately and never pooled into the main replication counts.

For every scanned token position *a* (the "anchor"), the method computes, for each hidden dimension *j*, the median growth `Δ_j(a)` of the elementwise product `h_L(i)_j · h_L(a)_j − h_1(i)_j · h_1(a)_j` over all other pool tokens *i* (excluding special tokens and bare tokenizer space-markers, but keeping punctuation, since delimiters are a hypothesized aggregation site). Sorting `Δ(a)` and keeping the smallest prefix of dimensions whose cumulative positive mass covers μ=0.7 defines the anchor's **core subspace**. At every layer, a token *i* is "recruited" into anchor *a*'s **basin** the first time the cosine similarity between `h_ℓ(i)` and `h_ℓ(a)`, restricted to the core subspace, crosses τ=0.7; the basin is the *ever-recruited* set (transient membership counts, even if the token later falls back below threshold).

Two scores are then computed per anchor: (1) **differentiator strength** (`pull_frac_gap`) — the mean fraction of a basin member's depth-displacement energy that lands inside the anchor's subspace, minus the same fraction for non-members, floored at zero; and (2) **aggregator strength** (`capacity_share`) — the participation ratio `(Σσ)²/Σσ²` of the singular values of the final-layer configuration of all pool tokens restricted to the anchor's core subspace, normalized by the hidden dimension. Both are computed in two anisotropy **views**: the **operative view** (raw states, fixed τ=0.7 at every layer — "the geometry in which the model actually computes") and the **quiet-dimensions view** (every dimension z-scored per layer before scanning, with τ set per layer at the 95th percentile of that layer's cosine distribution — partialling out the dominant-variance substrate to expose low-variance structure).

A frozen battery of seven confirmatory tests is then run over every completed model×text×view cell: (1) a **boundary test** on two engineered texts with a known technical/soft register split; (2) **two-view correlation**; (3) **pin identity** — classifying top-decile aggregator anchors as delimiters, recurring lexemes, or other; (4) a **paragraph-binding ("capiton") test** for whether paragraph-final aggregator pins bind their own preceding paragraph; (5) a **minimal-pair (A/B) protocol** on texts differing by one inserted passage, exploiting causal masking to align shared prefixes/suffixes exactly; (6) **Tier-2 annotation scoring** — rank AUC of channel values on expert-marked vs. unmarked tokens, against static lexical baselines (Zipf frequency, length, TF-IDF, word-level surprisal) and two permutation nulls (a block null re-placing marked blocks at random, and a rarity-matched null); and (7) a **type-essence transfer test** comparing same-word-type cross-text anchor-subspace overlap against a random-anchor scaffold baseline. Ground truth has two tiers: engineered texts (construction known to the character) and expert-annotated texts of two kinds kept strictly separate — a psychoanalyst's discourse/rupture markings on four clinical transcripts (linguistic ground truth) and a domain expert's declaration of the columns of interest on four pipeline execution traces (structural/data ground truth). All code, cached activation tensors (>40GB), and a script that recomputes every reported number from the same artifacts are public at github.com/HermeneuticAI/MetaphorTracer.

## Results & Highlights

- **Engineered register boundary (Tier 1, 2 texts × 3 models).** The aggregator's before/after strong-anchor density ratio follows the pre-registered register direction in **6/6 model×text cells**. On WFA 1 (technical register first), the ratio is 3.04 (phi-4), 2.99 (Qwen3-8B), 2.57 (Llama-base); on WFA 2 (technical register last, reversed direction), it flips to 0.57, 0.39, 0.66 respectively — versus a null ratio of 1.0 for no boundary effect.
- **Two-view inversion.** Rescanning the same boundary in the quiet-dimensions view does not weaken the alignment, it *inverts* it: phi-4's WFA 1 ratio drops from 3.04 (operative) to 0.10 (quiet), Qwen3-8B from 2.99 to 0.07 — over a 30× reversal — replicated (more weakly) in the base model (2.57 → 0.82).
- **Clinical transcript annotation (Tier 2, 4 transcripts × 3 models, psychoanalyst ground truth).** Blue-operative rank AUC on analyst-marked vs. unmarked tokens exceeds chance (0.5) in 11/12 cells, with medians of 0.706 (phi-4), 0.661 (Qwen3-8B), and 0.571 (Llama-base); the one miss is the base model on transcript 3 (AUC 0.470). Overall, 34/36 pre-registered directional cells (blue above chance, red and quiet-blue at/below chance) come out correct.
- **Lexical baseline is stronger in raw terms, and one model fails significance.** On the identical marked/unmarked split, a pure word-rarity baseline reaches pooled AUC **0.875–0.878** — higher than any channel's raw AUC. Under residualization on word-level surprisal and rarity within content words, phi-4's increment clears both permutation nulls (p=0.0001 block, p=0.0013 rarity-matched) and the base model clears the block null only (p=0.0003 vs. 0.103), but **Qwen3-8B fails both nulls (p=0.21, 0.47)** — an explicit negative result the paper reports rather than hides.
- **Transfer/fidelity inversion (Fig. 7).** The base model's same-type cross-text anchor-subspace overlap sits at the **93rd percentile** of a random-anchor scaffold baseline (53% of shared word types above the 90th percentile), yet its median clinical blue-operative AUC (0.571) is the lowest of the three models; the instruction-tuned models transfer far less by lexical type (72nd/75th percentile, 2.0%/10.7% of types) while reading the discourse better (0.661/0.706).
- **Matched base/instruct pair.** The Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct twin raises pooled residualized clinical AUC from the base's **0.585 to 0.627** (clearing both permutation nulls, p=0.0001/0.0003, where the base clears only the block null), while type-transfer stays at the base's regime (93rd percentile, 55% vs. 53% of types above the 90th) — instruction tuning adds discourse-reading fidelity without touching the underlying token-type geometry.
- **Execution-trace column dissociation (Tier 2, structural ground truth).** phi-4's aggregator ranks "measurement" columns at AUC 0.83–0.84 vs. "identity" columns at 0.65–0.67, while Qwen3-8B inverts this exactly (identity 0.63–0.67, measures 0.32–0.35); the base model shows no such organization (flat 0.49–0.58).

## Authors & Credibility

The corresponding author, Marc Heimann, is a philosopher (not a computer scientist, by his own description in the paper) affiliated with Hermeneuic AI, who has been developing the Lacanian/transformer theoretical program this paper operationalizes across several prior venues — *AI & Society*, *Cognitive Systems Research*, *Lacunae: APPI International Journal for Lacanian Psychoanalysis*, and the *European Journal of Psychoanalysis* (Heimann and Hübener 2024, 2025; Heimann 2026a–c). Co-author Roxana Assadi Moghaddam is the psychoanalyst who produced the clinical annotations; Lutz Götzmann provided access to the patient data (informed consent and ethics-committee approval are documented in the Ethics Statement); Olga Brovkina and Mark Pettifor built the domain-table test material and performed app testing. All five authors share one affiliation (Hermeneuic AI), an independent research collective rather than a university lab or major AI company, and the paper is explicit that its primary contribution is an *interpretation* (the model read as a Freudian-tradition theory of language) rather than a practical tool — an unusual framing for a mechanistic-interpretability paper that readers should weigh when assessing generalizability of the claims.

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty:** the two-channel (aggregator/differentiator), two-view (operative/quiet), per-text (not corpus-averaged) framing is a genuinely distinctive addition to a crowded token-geometry literature, and the explicit tie to Lacanian "point de capiton" theory gives it a falsifiable, clause-by-clause structure rather than a vague analogy.

**Rigor:** the discovery-text/confirmatory-text split, the frozen battery, the dual permutation nulls, and the public `paper_stats.py` script that recomputes every claim from cached artifacts are unusually disciplined for this kind of interpretability work. That said, the rigor cuts both ways — the paper itself reports that Qwen3-8B's clinical-tier alignment fails both permutation nulls (p=0.21 block, p=0.47 rarity-matched), which undercuts the "three unrelated models" framing of universality even though the authors are transparent about it. A second concrete weak spot: the lexical-rarity baseline alone reaches AUC 0.875–0.878 on the same split the channel is scored on, dwarfing the channel's own raw AUC (0.57–0.72); the paper's response — subtracting the shared lexical plane and reporting only the residual increment — is methodologically defensible but means the headline "34/36" count is doing a lot of work relative to what a much simpler frequency heuristic already explains.

**Reproducibility:** code, cached float32 activation tensors, and per-claim recomputation scripts are public; every non-extraction analysis is deterministic and re-runnable on CPU in minutes. This is a real strength.

**Significance:** the corpus is honestly tiny — 13 texts total, with hyperparameters (μ=0.7, τ=0.7) frozen on a single discovery text, only 4 clinical transcripts from a single psychoanalyst annotator (no inter-annotator agreement is reported, a choice the Discussion defends at length on methodological grounds but which still limits external validity), and only 3 base models plus one matched instruct pair (insufficient for factorial claims about scale or architecture, as the Limitations section itself states). The type-essence transfer test is also restricted to within-register text pairs (clinical-with-clinical, technical-with-technical); cross-register transfer is explicitly left untested.

**Red flags / overclaiming check:** the paper is unusually self-critical for a piece with this much philosophical scaffolding — it labels many secondary findings (channel exclusion, delimiter erasure, first-position elevation, the rupture "typology") as "post-hoc, descriptive" rather than confirmatory, and explicitly states the overall Lacanian "reading" is not itself falsifiable even though its individual operationalized clauses are. This is intellectually honest, but a reader should not mistake the wealth of exploratory findings in §3 and §8 for pre-registered confirmatory results — only the boundary test, the Tier-2 annotation scoring, and the minimal-pair protocol carry that status.

## Strengths

- A training-free, single-forward-pass instrument requiring no probe training or fine-tuning, applied at full per-token resolution rather than averaged corpus statistics.
- Careful confirmatory design: constants frozen on one discovery text before any other text is touched, replication counts reported per cell rather than a single pooled p-value, and a public script recomputing every number from cached artifacts.
- An unusually rich set of adversarial controls: lexical/rarity baselines, two flavors of permutation null, an attention-saliency rival instrument, delimiter-erasure and first-position-elevation checks, and a causal-masking sanity check (prefix identity floor of exactly 1.0 in float32).
- Reports negative/mixed results candidly (Qwen3-8B's failed nulls, the base model's weakest clinical tracking, an inverted sign in one reversed-order control) rather than only favorable cells.
- Draws a testable, falsifiable cross-instrument connection to concurrent Anthropic circuits work (Gurnee et al. 2026's "global workspace" J-lens), proposing a specific prediction (top-1 persistence should break at pin positions) for future validation on open-weight models.

## Limitations

- Entirely correlational: no causal intervention (e.g., patching an anchor's core subspace) establishes that the model actually *uses* the structures the channels read, as the Limitations section itself states.
- All analyses use a single teacher-forced forward pass; free-running generation is untested and may organize differently.
- Very small, single-annotator corpus (13 texts; 4 clinical transcripts marked by one psychoanalyst with no second-annotator agreement check) — enough for replication counts but not for statistical generalization claims.
- Only 3 base models plus 1 matched instruct pair; insufficient for factorial claims about model size, architecture, or alignment regime.
- Type-essence transfer is tested only within register (clinical-with-clinical, technical-with-technical); cross-register transfer is explicitly left untested.
- The base model (Llama-3.1-8B) is simultaneously the paper's architectural control and its weakest performer on the clinical tier (below-chance AUC in 1/12 cells), so claims of cross-model generality lean more heavily on the two instruction-tuned models.

## Relevance

这篇论文为可解释性研究提供了一种全新的、无需训练的单次前向传播读数方法，用两个几何量化的通道刻画单篇文本内部的组织结构，并首次将模型内部几何与精神分析专家标注做了系统对照，对关注隐藏状态几何、token 级结构以及跨学科可解释性方法的研究者具有较高参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would a causal intervention (patching an anchor's core subspace, as the authors themselves propose as a natural follow-up) confirm that "transported" content is actually used downstream, rather than merely traversed during the forward pass?
- Does the aggregator/differentiator framework generalize to larger models, other architectures (mixture-of-experts, state-space models), or free-running generation instead of a single teacher-forced pass?
- Would the "34/36" directional-cell result and the reported permutation-null failures for Qwen3-8B hold up with a second independent clinical annotator, given the paper's deliberate choice not to compute inter-annotator agreement?
- Can the proposed cross-instrument prediction against Gurnee et al.'s (2026) J-lens "global workspace" (that top-1 persistence should break at pin positions) be tested directly on an open-weight model?
- Does the type-essentialism-vs-discourse-fidelity trade-off shown for the Llama base/instruct pair (Fig. 7 and the matched-lineage follow-up) hold across other model families and base/instruct pairs, or is it specific to this one lineage?
