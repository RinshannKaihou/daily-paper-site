Stage-Replay Divergence Follows the KV Cache: Fixed-Prefix Precision Controls and Bidirectional Cache Transplantation ↗

Alexander Boesgaard Lorup · affiliation: “Openhagen” — no university or lab named · 30 Jul 2026
kv-cache fidelity replay diagnostics numerical precision (bf16 / fp32) activation transplantation llm reproducibility

Key metric

83%

166/200 suffixes diverge in BF16 on identical integer token prefixes → 0/200 in FP32 (Wilson upper bound 1.88%).

Whole-cache transplant: 24/24 & 43/43 divergent rows follow their cache donor 100% of the time.

Why this matters

Challenges the common assumption that replayed intermediate states mirror the model’s true decoding path — key for counterfactual attribution, distillation, and multi-stage reasoning evaluation.

Critical edge

The 24-row transplant primary set was deliberately conditioned on already-observed divergence, so its 100% recovery rate estimates sufficiency within a selected stratum — not the population-wide frequency of this failure mode.

01Methodology

A 14B Qwen2.5-derived multi-branch reasoner — three CoT branches (Gθ), a merge trace (Mθ), an answer (Aθ) — flattened into six role-tagged token blocks with a six-role visibility mask enforced at every attention layer.

Boundary studied The point immediately after the model’s unique <|merge|> token — maximal accumulated state.
Three cache constructions live (retained decode cache) vs. incremental (teacher-forced rebuild) vs. prefill (single forward pass) — independent replicas measure the within-construction noise floor.
Five gated experiments (1) 200-item live-vs-prefill matrix, BF16 & FP32 · (2) fixed-prefix precision crossing · (3) live→incremental bridge (12 rows) + 200-row saved-ledger audit · (4) bidirectional 48-layer K/V transplant (32 rows) · (5) outcome-blind replication on a distinct, later checkpoint (48 rows).
Statistics deterministic answer matcher, Wilson 95% CIs, 20,000-replicate paired bootstrap, exact two-sided McNemar tests.

Strengths

  • Disciplined evidentiary layering — noise floor → contract equivalence → fidelity → causal sufficiency — kept as separate, independently gated experiments rather than one aggregate comparison.
  • The fixed-prefix BF16/FP32 crossing cleanly isolates precision from construction method, a cleaner design than either closest prior work.
  • Bidirectional transplant replicated outcome-blind on a second, later checkpoint before any result was observed; negative-control rows (0/8, 0/5) are reported honestly.
  • Explicitly disclaims a “universal BF16/FP32 law” or kernel cause, and rounds Experiment 5 down to “conditional” replication when it narrowly misses its own pre-registered threshold.

Limitations

  • Transplants swap entire 48-layer K/V pairs jointly — cannot localize the effect to one layer, or to keys vs. values.
  • All evidence comes from one Qwen2.5-derived model family, one GPU (B200), one attention backend (SDPA), greedy decoding only.
  • The 24-row transplant primary set was conditioned on already-diverged rows; small control strata (8, 5 rows) leave wide Wilson upper bounds (32.4%, 43.4%).
  • No external or classical baseline — can’t compare this failure mode’s severity to other known nondeterminism sources (batch size, tensor-parallel reduction order).

?Questions for Further Exploration

  • Would key-only vs. value-only transplantation localize the divergence to one tensor, or a specific subset of the 48 layers?
  • Does the same divergence pattern hold on non-Qwen2.5 architectures, non-merge boundaries, or non-greedy (sampled) decoding?
  • What is the actual low-level mechanism — attention-kernel reduction order? RoPE differences? — and would a certified batch-invariant kernel suppress it the way FP32 does?
  • What fraction of an unconditioned random sample of boundaries would actually diverge and be recoverable via transplantation?
  • Given only 7/10 and 6/10 preservation on the stock public checkpoint, how much divergence is batch-composition vs. live/prefill construction per se?
