> **一句话总结：** WEP 从同一次白盒 prefill 中逐层估计支持/反驳当前候选答案的"有符号视觉证据"，用问题绑定的证据溯源（provenance）和证据集中度（concentration）两族可解释路由、经嵌套分组验证选出更可靠的一族并拟合稀疏 top-k 路由组合，再与候选置信度融合，在 3 个 MLLM×4 个基准共 12 组配对上把错误检测 AP 相对置信度基线平均提升 +0.134（12/12 组均为正），其中 InternVL3.5-8B 在 AMBER-D 上从 0.468 提升到 0.822（+0.354）。

> **TL;DR:** WEP augments a closed-answer MLLM's confidence margin with signed, layer-wise internal visual evidence read from the same instrumented prefill pass — no image perturbation, no decoding change, no backward pass, no external verifier. Across Qwen3-VL-8B, LLaVA-1.5-7B, and InternVL3.5-8B on four hallucination/visual-reasoning benchmarks (AMBER-D, Causal-HalBench, VSR, HallusionBench), WEP improves mean error-detection AP over the negative-candidate-margin confidence baseline from 0.468 to 0.602 (+0.134), with the largest gain on InternVL3.5-8B/AMBER-D (0.468→0.822, +0.354) and the smallest on Qwen3-VL-8B/VSR (0.373→0.411, +0.038, CI crossing zero). Against a matched-protocol adaptation of the published HALP-QT hallucination detector, WEP's provenance-only fallback is statistically indistinguishable in-domain (0.592 vs. 0.610±0.003, gap −0.018) but transfers better to unseen datasets (+0.089 vs. −0.065 AP over confidence).

## 核心贡献

- **提出"有符号决策证据"视角**：不同于只测候选间置信度差距的传统方法，WEP 从解码位置对每个候选做 one-vs-competitors 的 logit 方向读出（式 3），把每层每个视觉源 token 对当前预测候选的贡献分解为支持（+）或反驳（−），并通过一阶完备性恒等式（式 5）和端到端 patching 审计验证该线性读出与真实注意力更新的一致性（median closure error < 0.1%，rank correlation ≥ 0.975，sign agreement 94.6–97.2%）。
- **两族互补的可解释路由**：溯源族（provenance）比较证据与问题绑定"见证者"（witness map）相对均匀视觉参照的重叠，残差族进一步扣除全局重叠只留问题特定部分；集中度族（concentration）用归一化熵衡量支持/矛盾证据是聚集还是弥散。两族在不同数据格局下各有优劣（溯源在 37/60 outer fold 被选中，集中度在 23/60），单独使用分别只带来 +0.129 / +0.124 的 AP 增益，而嵌套验证选出更可靠一族后达到 +0.134。
- **零学习参数的稀疏路由组合器**：验证阶段只做一个离散的"选族"决策 + 稀疏 top-k 路由挑选（k∈{1,2,3,5,8,13,21,36}）+ 一个融合权重 β，不学习任何逐路由系数，因而结果在样本和组合层面均可审计（Figure 6 展示了具体样本上哪些层/符号路由驱动了风险分数）。
- **系统性验证覆盖校准、迁移、鲁棒性**：50 个随机种子重跑完整嵌套选择流程仍有 581/600 个 cell-seed 组合为正增益；跨数据集零目标标签迁移（不用目标集做任何选择）宏平均 ΔAP 仍有 +0.095（11/12 为正）；标签置换零假设检验中，真实增益 (+0.130) 与零假设均值 (~0.000) 有 11/12 cell 的经验 p ≤ 0.039，排除了"分数尺度伪影"的解释。
- **诚实的失败模式定位**：VSR（空间关系）上的增益明显弱于其他三个基准（因为 WEP 只衡量证据是否覆盖两实体，不编码几何/方向谓词本身），作者用 Grounded-VSR 的 oracle 边界框几何分数做诊断，证明加入显式几何信息能把 VSR 宏平均 AP 从 0.540 进一步提到 0.562（2D 空间关系子集从 0.555 到 0.581），从而验证了"WEP 缺的是几何推理信号"这一诊断而非笼统的"方法失效"。

## Key Contributions

- **A signed decision-evidence formulation of closed-answer risk.** For a predicted candidate ŷ, WEP computes a one-vs-competitors readout direction d_y at every layer (Eq. 3–4), transports source-token contributions into visual-token space (Eq. 6), and verifies the linear local decomposition against the true nonlinear attention update via an exact first-order completeness identity (Eq. 5) plus end-to-end value-patching audits — a step most attention/saliency-based hallucination detectors skip, leaving them unable to confirm the identified tokens actually move the final logits.
- **Two complementary, interpretable route families with a low-capacity selector.** Provenance routes (global-anchor and question-residual, Eq. 9) measure evidence overlap against a question-independent uniform visual reference vs. question-bound witness maps; concentration routes (Eq. 10) measure whether signed support/contradiction evidence is spatially concentrated or diffuse. Nested grouped validation (inner image-grouped folds within each outer calibration split) picks one family per fold — never both jointly — and the paper shows (Table 10) that pooling both families into one selectable pool is *not* better (−0.006 mean AP), i.e., the family-first structural constraint is doing useful work, not just adding capacity.
- **A zero-learned-coefficient sparse portfolio construction.** Route ranking, standardization statistics, portfolio size k, and fusion weight β are chosen on calibration folds via grid search and frozen — no per-route weights are fit — which keeps the resulting score inspectable both at the example level (Figure 6: named layer/sign routes with numeric contributions) and the portfolio level (Table 12: route-selection stability across folds, Jaccard 0.424–0.668 depending on backbone).
- **Comprehensive validation spanning calibration efficiency, stability, transfer, and matched-budget baselines.** 50-seed reselection of the entire nested algorithm (family choice, route identities, portfolio size, normalization, fusion) yields mean ΔAP +0.129 [+0.126, +0.134] with 581/600 positive cell-seeds; leave-dataset-out transfer with zero target labels retains +0.095 [+0.085, +0.102] mean ΔAP (11/12 positive targets); a label-permutation null control shows the real gain (+0.130) is far outside the null distribution's mean (~0.000) in 11/12 cells.
- **Honest failure-mode analysis on VSR (spatial relations).** The paper explicitly diagnoses that WEP's smallest gains occur on VSR because its routes test whether evidence covers the two named entities in a spatial-relation question, not whether the geometric/directional predicate itself is satisfied; adding an oracle Grounded-VSR bounding-box geometry score on top of WEP's held-out risk raises macro AP from 0.540 to 0.562 (and 0.555→0.581 restricted to 2D spatial relations), confirming the diagnosis rather than papering over the weak result.

## Methodology

WEP operates entirely within one instrumented forward (prefill) pass over an image I, question q, and closed candidate set Y (the four main benchmarks use {yes, no}; a four-choice MMStar pilot and a six-token multi-answer VSR prototype are reported in the supplement). The confidence baseline is the negative candidate margin r_conf(x) = −(z_ŷ − max_{y≠ŷ} z_y) (Eq. 1).

**Witness maps and decision evidence.** A question-bound witness map P_Q(v) aggregates each question token's visual binding distribution B_j(v), weighted by how much the decision position attends to that question token; a rule-based span parser recovers subject/object entity spans S(v), O(v) (falling back to P_Q when parsing fails), and a question-independent uniform reference U(v)=1/|V| is also defined. Separately, for each candidate y and layer ℓ, a one-vs-competitors readout direction d_y (Eq. 3, computed analytically via RMSNorm gradients without backpropagating through the network) yields a per-source-token contribution C_{ℓ,y}(s) (Eq. 4) via attention weights, value vectors, and the output projection. Source contributions are transported into visual-token space, weighted by each question token's "visually sourced fraction" ρ_j, to give signed visual evidence E_{ℓ,y}(v) (Eq. 6).

**Two route families.** Provenance routes (Eq. 7–9) score min/max histogram-intersection overlap between positive/negative evidence and the entity spans S, O, then subtract the question-independent global-reference score to isolate question-specific alignment (residual routes). Concentration routes (Eq. 10) compute normalized entropy of the (separately normalized) positive/negative evidence distributions over visual tokens, flagging narrowly concentrated support or diffusely spread contradiction as risk signals. All routes are scaled by a candidate-level factor κ_y capturing how much of the layer's margin change is visually sourced.

**Nested selection and fusion.** For each outer held-out calibration fold, three inner image-grouped folds independently fit a provenance portfolio and a concentration portfolio; out-of-fold AP on the pooled inner folds picks the family. Using all outer-calibration images, individual routes in the winning family are ranked by error AP; portfolio size k ∈ {1,2,3,5,8,13,21,36} and fusion weight β ∈ {−3.00, −2.75, …, 3.00} are chosen over predefined grids. The frozen selection is applied to the held-out fold: selected routes are standardized with calibration-fold statistics, averaged (Eq. 11), and fused with standardized confidence (Eq. 12) to produce the final r_wep(x) score. No per-route weights are learned; the only tunable additions beyond the base sparse portfolio are one discrete family choice, the route set, and one scalar fusion weight.

**Baselines and controls used for comparison.** Extra-path contrastive controls (VCD-margin, ICD-margin, a diagnostic VCD sweep) require an additional image or prompt-perturbation path and score a different construct (contrast, not signed evidence). Matched-budget internal baselines include a dense all-layer-provenance-route logistic regression, L1-regularized sparse route learners, full-vocabulary confidence scalars (candidate NLL, entropy, energy, top-1 uncertainty), and a matched-protocol re-implementation of HALP (Kogilathota et al. 2026), a published query-token MLP hallucination detector, refit under WEP's own image-grouped five-fold protocol and labels.

## Results & Highlights

- **Main result (Table 1, 12 model×dataset pairs):** mean error-AP rises from 0.468 (confidence) to 0.602 (WEP), **ΔAP +0.134**, with the largest single gain on **InternVL3.5-8B / AMBER-D: AP 0.468 → 0.822, ΔAP +0.354 [+0.339, +0.370]**, and AUROC 0.711 → 0.909 (+0.198). InternVL3.5-8B / Causal-HalBench likewise rises AP 0.617 → 0.887 (+0.270). All 12 cells are positive; 10/12 have image-cluster bootstrap 95% CIs strictly above zero (the two exceptions are Qwen3-VL-8B/VSR, ΔAP +0.038 [−0.001, +0.074], and — implicitly — the borderline cases with wide intervals due to small n).
- **Review-budget precision (Figure 2):** at fixed review fractions, WEP raises error precision by **+0.196 [+0.115, +0.274] at a 1% review budget, +0.206 [+0.173, +0.246] at 5%, and +0.179 [+0.153, +0.202] at 10%**, averaged across all three backbones — direct evidence that WEP concentrates errors earlier in an audit/routing queue than confidence alone.
- **Ablations isolate where the gain comes from (Table 2, image-grouped out-of-fold AP gains over the confidence-only baseline, which is +0.000):** unsigned evidence alone gives only **+0.014** (8/12 positive); dropping visual transport (using source-token space directly) gives **+0.054**; a uniform (unselected) route mean gives **+0.081** (11/12); provenance-only reaches **+0.129** (12/12); concentration-only reaches **+0.124** (12/12); the full nested family-selecting WEP reaches **+0.134** (12/12) — showing that both the *sign* of evidence and the *validated route selection* (not just averaging more routes) are load-bearing.
- **Extra-path contrastive baselines are weak or negative (Table 3, strict same-prediction subset):** confidence margin AP delta is 0 by definition (1/9 cell wins); **VCD-margin: −0.101 AP (0/9 wins)**; **ICD-margin: −0.167 AP (0/9 wins)**; best diagnostic VCD sweep: −0.023 AP (0/9 wins); **WEP: +0.127 AP (8/9 wins)** — the contrastive-decoding-style baselines that require an extra image/prompt path actually *underperform* raw confidence on this task, underscoring that WEP's single-prefill signed-evidence approach targets a genuinely different, and here more informative, construct.
- **Closest published detector (Table 23):** a matched-protocol adaptation of **HALP-QT** (Kogilathota et al., 2026), a 2.26M-parameter query-token MLP, reaches in-domain AP **0.610 ± 0.003** versus WEP's zero-target-label provenance fallback at **0.592** — a small, non-significant gap (−0.018, 95% CI [−0.036, +0.001]) — but HALP-QT **transfers worse**: leave-dataset-out AP change is **−0.065 ± 0.006** for HALP-QT vs. **+0.089** for the provenance fallback, with WEP's provenance winning on 10/12 transfer targets vs. HALP-QT's 7/12 in-domain wins.
- **Deployment overhead (Table 25):** measured absolute latency increment is **0.050–0.107 seconds/example** relative to candidate-only scoring (e.g., Qwen3-VL-8B goes from 0.046s to 0.099s/example, a 116.4% relative overhead; InternVL's high-token mp12 setting has a lower 28.9% relative overhead), with incremental peak GPU memory of 14 MB to 244 MB depending on backbone/config — no extra model forward pass, image perturbation, or backward pass is required.

**No quantitative results reported** does not apply — the paper reports extensive quantitative results (AP, AUROC, AURC, review-precision, bootstrap CIs) across the main tables and 29 supplementary tables.

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty:** The core idea — deriving a complementary white-box risk score from the *same* fixed prefill path that produced the candidate answer, decomposed into signed provenance (does evidence support/contradict via question-bound witnesses relative to a uniform reference) and concentration (is signed evidence localized or diffuse) — is a genuinely new framing distinct from perturbation-based (VCD/ICD), generic internal-state classifiers (HALP, VIB-Probe), and pure attention/saliency methods. The explicit numerical audit of the linear evidence decomposition against the true nonlinear attention update (closure error < 0.1%, sign agreement 94.6–97.2%, Table 17) is a rigor step that most attention-attribution hallucination papers omit, directly answering the classic "attention is not explanation" critique the paper itself cites (Jain & Wallace 2019; Wiegreffe & Pinter 2019).

**Rigor:** The validation is unusually thorough for this literature: image-grouped nested folds (avoiding image leakage across train/test), 50-seed stability reruns, leave-dataset-out zero-target transfer, a label-permutation null (route-search-alone control), an evidence-shuffle sanity control, and paired image-cluster bootstrap intervals throughout. The label-permutation null in Table 15 is a particularly strong rigor check — it directly tests whether the *route-search freedom itself* (rather than genuine example-matched evidence) could manufacture the observed gain, and shows the real mean ΔAP (+0.130) sits well outside the null distribution (mean ~0.000) in 11/12 cells.

**Reproducibility:** Code is released; datasets are public benchmarks (AMBER-D, Causal-HalBench, VSR, HallusionBench) with deterministic probe construction, fixed seeds, and documented image-hash dedup across train/test splits (zero shared SHA-256 hashes across all six dataset pairs). This is a genuine strength.

**Significance and paper-specific red flags:**
- The closest published competitor, **HALP-QT**, is essentially tied with WEP's provenance fallback in-domain (0.610 vs 0.592, gap not statistically significant), which somewhat undercuts the headline claim that WEP's *fused* score (+0.134 over confidence) represents a large absolute advance over prior internal-evidence detectors specifically — WEP's edge over HALP is concentrated in *transfer* robustness (+0.089 vs. −0.065), not raw in-domain detection power.
- **VSR is a genuinely weak benchmark for WEP** (Qwen3-VL-8B ΔAP +0.038 with a CI crossing zero; the paper's own oracle-geometry diagnostic shows WEP is missing a geometric-reasoning signal entirely) — this is disclosed prominently rather than hidden, which is commendable, but it does mean the "12/12 positive" headline includes at least one practically negligible, non-significant cell.
- The **four-choice MMStar pilot** (Table 28) shows only a marginal, non-significant gain (confidence AP 0.575 → WEP 0.586, ΔAP +0.011, 95% CI [−0.057, +0.070]), and category-level results are mixed (logical reasoning −0.092, science/technology −0.080) — the paper is upfront that this is a pilot outside the binary main setting, but it is a meaningfully weaker result than the headline binary numbers and worth weighing before generalizing WEP to richer candidate sets.
- **Candidate-adapted Overthinking and its trajectory LR baseline are weak comparators** (0.334 and 0.528 AP respectively, both well below the 0.468 confidence baseline on average), so their inclusion, while transparent about relative strength, does not stress-test WEP as much as the HALP-QT comparison does.
- The method is fundamentally scoped to **white-box, closed-answer, labeled-calibration settings** — it cannot be applied to black-box APIs or free-form generation without the claim-segmentation extensions the paper explicitly flags as future work, and the entire framework depends on a labeled calibration slice, which is a nontrivial deployment requirement compared to zero-shot confidence baselines.

## Strengths

- Rigorous internal-consistency audit (closure error, rank correlation, sign agreement, end-to-end value-patching) verifying that the claimed evidence readout actually reflects the model's true decision path, not just a plausible-looking attention heatmap.
- Extensive, well-designed control suite: label-permutation nulls, evidence-shuffle sanity checks, matched-budget learned-selector comparisons, and a matched-protocol reproduction of a published competing method (HALP-QT).
- Zero-target-label transfer and 50-seed stability analyses give genuine confidence the gains are not fold-specific artifacts, with quantitative positive-rate reporting (11/12, 581/600) rather than only point estimates.
- Honest, self-critical failure-mode analysis on VSR, including a follow-up oracle-geometry diagnostic that pinpoints exactly what kind of signal WEP's routes cannot capture.
- No architectural retraining, extra forward passes, backward passes, or external verifiers required — measured latency overhead (0.05–0.11s/example) and memory overhead (14–244MB) are modest and explicitly reported rather than left as an unaddressed practicality question.

## Limitations

- Requires a labeled calibration slice for nested route/family/portfolio selection — not a zero-shot method, unlike raw confidence.
- Restricted to white-box models with accessible attention/value internals and closed (small, finite) candidate sets; the paper explicitly scopes free-form generation, black-box APIs, and claim-level aggregation as out of scope.
- Spatial/geometric reasoning errors (VSR) are only partially addressed, since WEP's routes test evidence coverage of named entities, not the geometric predicate itself — confirmed by the paper's own oracle-geometry ablation.
- Gains on the four-choice MMStar pilot and multi-token VSR prototype are smaller or statistically marginal compared to the binary main-benchmark results, suggesting the strongest evidence for WEP is currently limited to binary yes/no closed-answer settings.
- Relative latency overhead is substantial in percentage terms (up to 116% on Qwen3-VL-8B) even though absolute overhead is small, which may matter for very high-throughput deployment.

## Relevance

该论文与多模态大模型幻觉检测、可解释性归因（logit lens、注意力/数值读出）以及选择性预测/风险路由方向高度相关，其"信号来自同一次白盒 prefill、无需额外前向或扰动"的设计思路，对希望以低成本给闭集视觉问答结果加装置信度之外的可审计风险分数、并用于审核/路由决策的场景具有较强的参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Can the provenance/concentration route framework be extended to free-form generation via claim segmentation, and would the same nested-validation-based family selection remain stable across claim boundaries?
- Would a richer, learned (rather than hand-selected top-k) combination of provenance and concentration routes close the gap to the dense all-layer logistic regression's in-domain +0.163 mean ΔAP without sacrificing WEP's demonstrated transfer robustness?
- How would WEP's witness-map construction generalize to non-English or code-mixed questions, given its reliance on a rule-based subject/object span parser?
- Could an explicit lightweight geometric-relation module be added as a third route family to address the diagnosed VSR gap, rather than relying on oracle bounding boxes only for diagnosis?
- Does WEP's advantage over HALP-QT in leave-dataset-out transfer generalize to genuinely different model families/scales beyond the three 7–8B backbones tested, or larger MLLMs where internal readouts may behave differently?
