> **一句话总结：** 作者在一个 Qwen2.5-14B 衍生的三分支推理系统上证明：即便重放（fresh-prefill）用的是和原始实时解码完全相同的整数 token 前缀，BF16 下仍有 83%（166/200）的后缀会与保留的实时 KV 缓存分叉，而把 48 层 K/V 张量整体"移植"给对方后，24/24 及 43/43 的分叉样本都 100% 跟随缓存捐赠者的轨迹——证明边界处的完整 KV 缓存是分叉轨迹的因果充分载体，数值精度（FP32 下分叉降为 0/200）则调节这一效应是否表现出来。

> **TL;DR:** On a 200-item frozen GPQA Main holdout, replaying a reasoning-stage boundary via one-shot fresh-prefill of the *exact same integer token prefix* diverges from the retained live `DynamicCache` on 166/200 (83.0%) suffixes and 49/200 (24.5%) final answers in BF16, even though duplicate replicas of each construction are bit-exact on all 200/200 items and the net accuracy gap is a non-significant +1.0pp (93/200 live vs 95/200 prefill, paired 95% CI [-3.5, +5.5], McNemar p=.824). A fixed-prefix BF16-vs-FP32 crossing on the same 200 prefixes shows the divergence is precision-moderated: FP32 gives 0/200 suffix/answer/correctness-label disagreement (Wilson upper bound 1.88%) versus BF16's 166/49/20 out of 200 (all differences significant, p ≤ 3.55e-15 to 2.14e-50). Bidirectional transplantation of all 48 key/value layers at a divergent boundary makes the recipient follow its cache donor's continuation on 24/24 selected rows and, in an outcome-blind replication on a later checkpoint, 43/43 rows — establishing the boundary KV cache as a causally sufficient carrier of the divergent trajectory, while 0/8 and 0/5 non-divergent control rows respectively show no spurious new trajectory from the swap.

## 核心贡献

- 提出并执行了一套"匹配的实时缓存 vs 一次性重放前填充（fresh-prefill）"四臂矩阵实验：在完全相同的整数 token 前缀、角色、掩码、位置、缓存位置、生成上限和物理 batch 构成下，仅改变"边界状态是保留实时缓存重建，还是重新前填充重建"这一个变量，同时用重复副本（replica）单独度量每种构造内部的噪声下限。
- 设计了一个"固定前缀 2×2"精度交叉实验：把 200 个 BF16 前缀原封不动地搬到 FP32 下重跑，从而把"数值精度效应"和"缓存构造方式效应"解耦——结果显示 BF16 下的分叉在 FP32 下完全消失（0/200，Wilson 上界 1.88%），证明分叉是精度调制的，而非纯粹由构造方式决定。
- 首次对边界处完整的 48 层 K/V 张量做**双向整体移植**（不是单层或残差流层面的局部干预），在两个独立的检查点（含一次结果盲选的复现实验）上验证：把某构造的完整缓存整体替换成对方的缓存后，续写内容 100%（24/24、43/43）跟随缓存捐赠者，而非接收者自身的构造方式，从而把"缓存是分叉的因果充分载体"从相关性证据提升为直接干预证据。
- 明确划定了本文不主张的边界：不宣称重放精度的普遍提升或损害、不宣称 BF16/FP32 的普适规律、不宣称唯一的内核或调度器成因，只做"在测得的状态下"的操作性结论，并诚实指出移植实验联合替换 key 和 value，因此无法区分二者各自的作用，也未定位到具体某一层。

## Key Contributions

- **Matched fidelity and precision controls**: a four-arm retained-live-cache-vs-fresh-prefill matrix over 200 fixed integer-token prefixes (GPQA Main), with duplicate replicas on each side to isolate within-construction noise from live-vs-prefill state divergence, plus a fixed-prefix BF16/FP32 crossing that removes the discrete-state confound.
- **Direct state intervention**: bidirectional whole-cache (all 48 key/value layer pairs) transplantation between incremental and prefill constructions at a divergent boundary, tested on a selected primary-checkpoint set (24/24 rows) and replicated outcome-blind on a distinct, later 14B checkpoint (43/43 rows), with non-divergent control rows (0/8, 0/5) showing no spurious new trajectories.
- **A compact fidelity protocol**: separates replica-floor noise, token/role/mask/position identity, live-state fidelity, and causal state sufficiency into distinct, independently gated experiments (Experiments 1–5), plus a prospective live-to-incremental "bridge" (12/12 bit-exact rows) and a 200-row saved-ledger audit confirming trajectory/fingerprint reproduction without new decoding.
- A secondary cross-check on the public, stock `Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct` checkpoint under vLLM's batch-invariance mode shows singleton-vs-paired execution preserves token trajectory on only 7/10 and log-probability traces on 6/10 prompts — evidence that batch-composition sensitivity exists even without this paper's custom role-embeddings/visibility-mask machinery, bounding external validity of the main claim.

## Methodology

The target system is a 14B multi-branch reasoning model derived from `Qwen/Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct` after a merge-targeted policy update: for a problem x, it generates three branch traces via `G_θ`, a merge trace via `M_θ`, and a final answer via `A_θ`, flattened into six ordered, non-nested token blocks (`<|prompt|>`, three `<|cot_i|>` branches, `<|merge|>`, `<|answer|>`) with role-conditioned embeddings and a six-role visibility contract (Table 2) enforced at every attention layer; branch/merge/answer content is capped at 768/512/64 tokens, decoded greedily (temperature 0, top-p 1, top-k 0). The "stage-replay boundary" studied is the point immediately after the unique `<|merge|>` token has been processed. Three constructions are compared: a **live** cache retained from ordinary autoregressive generation, an **incremental** cache rebuilt token-by-token under teacher forcing, and a **prefill** cache built in a single forward call from the same integer tokens; **replicas** (independent storage-isolated repeats) measure the within-construction noise floor.

Five experiments build up the causal claim: (1) a matched 200-item (frozen GPQA Main holdout) four-arm live-vs-prefill matrix in both BF16 and FP32, with replica pairs and an eight-row preflight integrity gate requiring exact agreement on tokens/roles/masks/positions/cache-tensor storage disjointness; (2) a fixed-prefix 2×2 that reuses the same 200 BF16 integer-token prefixes under FP32, isolating precision from construction; (3) a prospective live-to-incremental "bridge" on 12 evenly-spaced newly-decoded rows plus a 200-row saved-ledger audit, testing whether ordinary live decoding and incremental (teacher-forced) reconstruction of a *freshly reached* prefix agree bit-exactly; (4) bidirectional whole-cache transplantation — swapping all 48 key/value layer pairs between the incremental and prefill reconstructions of the same fixed prefix, in both directions, on 32 frozen rows (24 known-divergent + 8 known-exact controls) at the primary checkpoint; (5) an outcome-blind replication of experiment 4's design on 48 rows (selected by prefix length and problem ID only, before any suffix/transplant outcome is observed) using a distinct, later 14B checkpoint from the same training family. Scoring uses a deterministic answer matcher, Wilson 95% intervals for binary disagreement/recovery rates, 20,000-replicate paired bootstrap CIs for accuracy differences, and exact two-sided McNemar tests for directional imbalance. Reproducibility artifacts (SHA-256 digests of weights/config/tokenizer/role-embeddings, frozen software/hardware manifest in Table 3) are released, though training corpora and the actual checkpoint weights are excluded.

## Results & Highlights

- **Live-cache vs. fresh-prefill diverges sharply in BF16 despite exact replicas**: on the matched 200-item GPQA Main matrix, both replica pairs are bit-exact on 200/200 items, yet live and prefill continuations diverge on 166/200 suffixes (83.0%, Wilson 95% CI [77.18, 87.57]), 49/200 final answers (24.5%, [19.06, 30.90]), and 20/200 correctness labels (10.0%); net accuracy is 93/200 (live) vs 95/200 (prefill), a non-significant +1.0pp gap (paired 95% CI [-3.5, +5.5], exact McNemar p = .824) built from 9 live-only and 11 prefill-only successes — i.e., large item churn cancels out in aggregate accuracy.
- **FP32 eliminates the same construction-level disagreement that BF16 shows on identical prefixes**: the fixed-prefix 2×2 (same 200 integer-token prefixes crossed with precision) reproduces BF16's 166/200 suffix, 49/200 answer, and 20/200 correctness-label disagreement, but FP32 yields 0/200 on all three (Wilson 95% CI [0, 1.88]%); paired FP32-minus-BF16 reductions are 83.0pp (suffix, bootstrap CI [77.5, 88.0]), 24.5pp (answer, CI [18.5, 30.51]), and 10.0pp (correctness, CI [6.0, 14.5]), each significant at p = 2.14×10⁻⁵⁰, 3.55×10⁻¹⁵, and 1.91×10⁻⁶ respectively. Cache relative L2 drift between incremental and prefill states falls from a median 0.01224 (BF16) to 4.27×10⁻⁶ (FP32).
- **Whole-cache transplantation causally redirects the divergent continuation in both directions and on two checkpoints**: at the primary 14B checkpoint, all 24/24 selected divergent rows follow their cache donor after a bidirectional full 48-layer K/V swap (Wilson 95% CI [86.2, 100]%), while 0/8 non-divergent control rows produce a new (third) trajectory (CI [0, 32.4]%); an outcome-blind replication on a distinct later checkpoint (48 rows, selected by prefix length/problem ID before any outcome is observed) shows 43/48 rows (89.6%) naturally diverge, with 43/43 donor recovery in both directions (CI [91.8, 100]%) and 0/5 exact-control rows creating a new trajectory (CI [0, 43.4]%) — one control short of the paper's own pre-registered "strong replication" threshold of six, which the authors flag explicitly rather than round up to a full claim.
- **The prospective live-to-incremental bridge and saved-ledger audit rule out a trivial confound**: on 12 newly-decoded rows, teacher-forced incremental reconstruction is bit-exact to the retained live cache on all 48 layers, boundary logits, and complete suffixes (12/12); a separate 200-row saved-ledger join independently reproduces every retained trajectory, cache, and logit "fingerprint" from Experiment 1 with no new decoding, confirming the incremental construction used in the fixed-prefix crossing is a faithful stand-in for live decoding.

## Authors & Credibility

Sole author Alexander Boesgaard Lorup, affiliation listed only as "Openhagen" — no university, company, or lab is named, which is unusual for a paper making detailed infrastructure-level claims about a specific (undisclosed) production-style multi-branch reasoning system. The paper cites and closely engages with two very recently posted (2026) same-topic arXiv preprints — Chodavarapu & Xu on cache-on/cache-off FP16 divergence, and Matteson on re-feeding vs. state resumption — and also self-cites the same author's own prior 2026 preprint on counterfactual-likelihood tests in private reasoning channels (arXiv:2605.19092), framing this paper as a direct follow-up motivated by an earlier self-training analysis whose central claim the author says "this audit... invalidated." The acknowledgements state LLM tools were used only for language editing/drafting, not analysis or claims. Full reproducibility manifests (SHA-256 digests, frozen problem order, per-item scores) are released, but the underlying checkpoint weights and training corpora are withheld, so an independent end-to-end rerun is not currently possible from the released artifacts alone.

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty**: The core observation — that identical-token fresh-prefill is not behaviorally equivalent to a retained live decoder cache — is a genuinely useful negative result for anyone building stage-replay diagnostics (counterfactual token-credit, distillation from privileged intermediate states, ablation probes), and the bidirectional whole-cache transplantation goes a clear step beyond the two closest prior works (which either report the divergence without a stated fix confound, or intervene only on the residual stream and leave direct KV-tensor intervention as future work). Table 1's explicit design comparison against Chodavarapu-Xu and Matteson is a strong, unusually transparent way to stake out the paper's specific contribution.

**Rigor**: The staged-gate design (replica floor → fixed-prefix crossing → prospective bridge → bidirectional transplant → outcome-blind replication) is careful about not conflating different levels of evidence, and the paper is explicit about what each experiment does and does not establish (e.g., "the third statement does not by itself imply the fourth"). A concrete, paper-acknowledged limitation is that the transplant experiments swap keys and values jointly, so the study "does not separate their roles or identify one layer" — meaning the causal-sufficiency claim is at the level of the whole 48-layer cache, not a mechanistic account of which layer or which of K vs. V actually drives the divergence. The outcome-blind replication (Experiment 5) is honestly reported as falling one control row short (5 of a registered minimum 6) of the paper's own "strong replication" bar, and is explicitly downgraded to "conditional replication with an underpowered control stratum" rather than rounded up.

**Reproducibility**: Strong relative to typical single-checkpoint LLM studies — exact SHA-256 digests for weights/config/tokenizer/role-embeddings are given for both checkpoints, along with a frozen software/hardware manifest (Table 3: exact CUDA/cuDNN/PyTorch/driver versions, B200 GPU, deterministic-algorithm mode disabled). However, since the checkpoints themselves (a "merge-targeted policy update" of Qwen2.5-14B and a distinct later checkpoint from an unspecified internal training family) are not public, true end-to-end reproduction is gated behind access the paper does not provide.

**Significance and red flags**: The vLLM batch-invariance side-check (7/10 and 6/10 preserved trajectories on the *stock*, public Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct checkpoint with no custom role-embeddings or visibility masking) is a useful, honestly-reported external bound showing the phenomenon isn't wholly an artifact of this paper's bespoke six-role architecture — but it also means the main experiments, run on one undisclosed multi-branch checkpoint family, cannot claim architecture- or task-general validity, a limitation the authors state directly rather than downplay. The GPQA Main population was "selected after an earlier breadth screen" not used as evidence in this paper, and the KV-transplant subset is explicitly conditioned on prior-observed suffix divergence (24 of 32 rows chosen because they already diverged) — a design the authors flag themselves as estimating recovery *within a selected sensitive stratum*, not population-wide divergence prevalence, so readers should not extrapolate the 83%/89.6% suffix-divergence rates as a general estimate of how often this failure mode occurs across models or tasks.

## Strengths

- Unusually disciplined evidentiary layering: separates "replicas are exact" (noise floor), "tokens/roles/masks match" (contract equivalence), "live state is faithfully reconstructed" (fidelity), and "the candidate state variable is causally sufficient" (transplantation) into distinct, independently-gated experiments rather than conflating them into a single aggregate accuracy comparison.
- The fixed-prefix BF16/FP32 crossing is an elegant confound-removal step: by freezing the exact same 200 integer-token prefixes and only crossing precision, it isolates "does construction method matter" from "does numerical precision matter" — a cleaner design than either prior closest work achieves.
- Whole-cache bidirectional transplantation, replicated outcome-blind on a second, later checkpoint before any outcome was observed, is a genuinely causal (not merely correlational) test, and the paper explicitly reports the negative-control rows (0/8, 0/5) that could have falsified the sufficiency claim.
- Honest, non-inflated claims throughout: the paper explicitly disclaims "a universal BF16/FP32 law," "a universal kernel or scheduler cause," and rounds the Experiment 5 replication down to "conditional" rather than "strong" when a control-count threshold is narrowly missed.

## Limitations

- The transplant intervention swaps entire 48-layer K/V caches jointly; it cannot attribute the divergence to specific layers, to keys vs. values separately, or to a specific low-level kernel/reduction-order mechanism — the paper is explicit that "reduction order, kernel selection, cache implementation, and their interactions remain candidates."
- Both checkpoints belong to one Qwen2.5-derived multi-branch model family, one GPU (B200), one attention backend (Hugging Face SDPA), and one sampling regime (greedy, temperature 0); there is no cross-architecture, cross-runtime, or cross-temperature evidence, so the "boundary KV cache is causally sufficient" claim is scoped to this tested surface only.
- The KV-transplant primary set (24 of 32 rows) is deliberately conditioned on already-observed suffix divergence, so its 24/24 recovery rate estimates sufficiency within a selected sensitive stratum, not the population-wide frequency of cache-mediated divergence; the small procedural-control strata (8 and 5 rows) yield wide upper-bound Wilson intervals (32.4% and 43.4%) that cannot rule out a non-trivial spurious-trajectory rate.
- The prospective bridge (Experiment 3) establishes tensor equality only on 12 *newly reached* prefixes, none of which is token-identical to an earlier saved prefix — so it does not (and does not claim to) establish retrospective equality for the discarded cache tensors from Experiment 1.
- No named external, non-neural, or classical baseline is used anywhere; all comparisons are internal (live vs. prefill, BF16 vs. FP32, incremental vs. prefill), so the paper cannot speak to how this failure mode compares in severity to other known sources of LLM inference nondeterminism cited in related work (e.g., batch-size effects, tensor-parallel reduction order).

## Relevance

该论文对"用重放/重建的中间状态代表模型真实经过的解码状态"这一常见诊断假设提出了具体、可操作的反例和修复路径，对做反事实归因、蒸馏特权中间状态、多分支/多阶段推理系统评测和 KV 缓存工程的研究者有直接参考价值；其"移植缓存即可让轨迹跟随捐赠者"的因果实验设计也可作为后续机制定位（如区分 K/V 各自作用、定位具体层）工作的起点。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would separating key-only versus value-only transplantation (rather than swapping both jointly) localize the divergence to one of the two tensors, or to a specific subset of the 48 layers?
- Does the same live-vs-prefill divergence pattern (166/200 suffix disagreement in BF16, eliminated in FP32) hold on non-Qwen2.5 architectures, on non-merge-boundary stage transitions, or under non-greedy (sampled) decoding?
- What is the actual low-level numerical mechanism behind the BF16 cache drift (median relative L2 0.01224) — is it attention-kernel reduction order, RoPE application differences between incremental and batched prefill, or something else — and would a certified batch-invariant kernel (as used in the paper's vLLM side-check) suppress it the way FP32 does?
- Since the 24-row transplant primary set was conditioned on known divergence, what fraction of an *unconditioned* random sample of boundaries would actually diverge and be recoverable via transplantation — i.e., what is the true population-wide prevalence of this failure mode?
- Given the vLLM batch-invariance side-check shows only 7/10 and 6/10 preservation on the stock public checkpoint, how much of the observed divergence in this paper's custom six-role architecture is attributable to batch-composition effects versus the live/prefill construction difference per se?
