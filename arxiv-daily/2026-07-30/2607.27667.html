Interpretability · White-Box Hallucination Risk Detection for Multimodal LLMs

WEP augments a closed-answer MLLM's confidence margin with signed, layer-wise internal visual evidence read from the same instrumented prefill pass — no image perturbation, no extra forward or backward pass.

一句话总结：WEP 从同一次白盒 prefill 中逐层估计支持/反驳当前候选答案的"有符号视觉证据"，用问题绑定的证据溯源（provenance）和证据集中度（concentration） 两族可解释路由、经嵌套分组验证选出更可靠的一族并拟合稀疏 top-k 路由组合，再与候选置信度融合，在 3 个 MLLM×4 个基准共 12 组配对上把错误检测 AP 相对置信度基线平均提升 +0.134（12/12 组均为正），其中 InternVL3.5-8B 在 AMBER-D 上从 0.468 提升到 0.822（+0.354）。

Why This Matters

Lets closed-set visual QA systems flag likely-wrong answers using only the existing forward pass — an auditable risk score for review or routing pipelines, with no retraining or extra compute.

Critical Edge

The closest published detector, HALP-QT, is essentially tied with WEP's provenance-only fallback in-domain (0.610 vs. 0.592, gap not statistically significant) — WEP's real edge is transfer robustness (+0.089 vs. −0.065), not raw in-domain detection power.

01 Methodology

  1. 1
    Signed decision evidence. At every layer, a one-vs-competitors readout direction d_y (computed analytically, no backprop) decomposes each visual token's contribution to the predicted candidate into support (+) or contradiction (−). A first-order completeness identity plus end-to-end value-patching audits verify this linear readout actually tracks the true nonlinear attention update (closure error <0.1%, sign agreement 94.6–97.2%).
  2. 2
    Witness maps. A question-bound witness map aggregates visual bindings of question tokens weighted by decision-position attention; a rule-based parser recovers subject/object entity spans, alongside a question-independent uniform reference.
  3. 3
    Two route families. Provenance routes score evidence overlap against entity spans, minus the question-independent global baseline (residual routes). Concentration routes measure normalized entropy — is signed evidence spatially localized or diffuse?
  4. 4
    Nested selection, zero learned weights. Inner image-grouped folds pick one family per outer calibration fold (never pooled); routes are ranked and a sparse top-k portfolio (k∈{1,2,3,5,8,13,21,36}) plus fusion weight β are chosen by grid search — no per-route coefficients are fit, keeping the score auditable at the example level.

02 Results

  • +0.196 / +0.206 / +0.179 precision gain at 1% / 5% / 10% review budgets (Fig. 2) — WEP surfaces errors earlier in an audit queue than confidence alone.
  • Extra-path contrastive baselines underperform raw confidence: VCD-margin −0.101 AP, ICD-margin −0.167 AP (0/9 wins each), vs. WEP's +0.127 AP (8/9 wins).
  • 50-seed full reselection: 581/600 cell-seed combinations positive; leave-dataset-out zero-target-label transfer retains +0.095 mean ΔAP (11/12 positive targets).
  • Deployment overhead: 0.050–0.107s/example latency, 14–244MB peak GPU memory — no extra forward, perturbation, or backward pass.
  • Honest failure mode: on VSR (spatial relations), gain shrinks to +0.038 (CI crosses zero) — WEP checks evidence covers named entities, not the geometric predicate itself.

Strengths

  • Rigorous internal-consistency audit (closure error, rank correlation, sign agreement, value-patching) verifies the evidence readout tracks the model's real decision path, not just a plausible-looking heatmap.
  • Extensive control suite: label-permutation nulls, evidence-shuffle sanity checks, matched-budget learned-selector comparisons, and a matched-protocol reproduction of a published competitor (HALP-QT).
  • Zero-target-label transfer and 50-seed stability analyses report positive-rate counts (11/12, 581/600), not just point estimates — genuine confidence gains aren't fold-specific artifacts.
  • Honest, self-critical VSR failure-mode analysis with a follow-up oracle-geometry diagnostic pinpointing exactly what signal the routes miss.
  • No retraining, extra forward/backward passes, or external verifiers; modest latency (0.05–0.11s) and memory (14–244MB) overhead is explicitly measured.

Limitations

  • Requires a labeled calibration slice for nested route/family/portfolio selection — not a zero-shot method, unlike raw confidence.
  • Restricted to white-box models with accessible attention/value internals and closed, finite candidate sets; free-form generation and black-box APIs are explicitly out of scope.
  • Spatial/geometric reasoning errors (VSR) are only partially addressed — routes test entity coverage, not the geometric predicate, confirmed by the paper's own oracle-geometry ablation.
  • Four-choice MMStar pilot gain is marginal and non-significant (+0.011, CI crosses zero); category results are mixed (logical reasoning −0.092).
  • Relative latency overhead is substantial in percentage terms (up to 116% on Qwen3-VL-8B), which may matter for high-throughput deployment.

Questions for Further Exploration

  • Can provenance/concentration routes extend to free-form generation via claim segmentation, with stable nested-family selection across claim boundaries?
  • Would a learned (not hand-selected top-k) combination of routes close the gap to the dense all-layer logistic regression's +0.163 in-domain ΔAP without losing transfer robustness?
  • How does witness-map construction generalize to non-English or code-mixed questions, given its reliance on a rule-based span parser?
  • Could an explicit lightweight geometric-relation route address the diagnosed VSR gap, instead of relying on oracle bounding boxes only for diagnosis?
  • Does WEP's transfer advantage over HALP-QT hold for model families/scales beyond the three 7–8B backbones tested here?
