Why This Matters

Directly tackles credit assignment in RLVR/CoT training — its diagnosis and lightweight fix matter to anyone building GRPO variants or token-level advantage schemes.

Critical Edge

CSCR is trained only at 1.7B/4B scale, leaving it unverified whether gains persist at the 8B/14B scale where the same sensitivity pathology was diagnosed.

Methodology

1. Samplefix an on-policy trajectory + every prefix (400 trajectories: 100 DAPO-17K questions × 4 responses)
→
2. Rescorere-score every token under two polarized "privileged prompts": flawless-solution vs. completely-wrong
→
3. Sensitivitys = max(|z_pos|, |z_neg|) — a counterfactual-sensitivity score, not an answer-aligned signal
→
4. Attenuatetokens above onset λ get exponentially decayed weight (rate α, cap γ)
→
5. Renormalizeweights renormalized per-trajectory; multiplied into GRPO's advantage A_i
  • Baseline (GRPO): one scalar advantage A_i = (R_i − μ_R)/(σ_R + ε) is broadcast uniformly across every token of a response.
  • The diagnosis: on-policy self-distillation (OPSD) treats the probability shift induced by privileged conditioning as a valid, answer-aligned learning signal. Under two deliberately opposite framings applied to the same fixed trajectory, the resulting shifts should cancel if they were truly answer-aligned — they mostly don't.
  • CSCR's fix: leaves the OPSD/KL machinery untouched and only reshapes how the single trajectory-level advantage is spread across tokens — downweighting exactly the high-sensitivity, substitutable discourse tokens ("But", "So", "Wait", "Therefore") while preserving the total credit budget and the verifier-determined sign. Default hyperparameters: λ=0.05, α=10, γ=0.2.
  • Ablation guardrail: trusting the shift's sign directly (Shift-Directed GRPO) collapses reward and produces repetition loops within ~30 steps; moderate downweighting (γ=0.2) is the stable regime — stronger up- or down-weighting destabilizes training.

Reading the Mechanism

CPC (Counterfactual Perturbation Consistency) is the paper's yardstick: it is provably 0 for standard GRPO's own opposite-sign advantage assignment (Appendix A), so any non-zero value under OPSD's opposing framings is direct evidence the two "opposite" verifier prompts are not actually pulling probabilities apart.

Strengths & Limitations

Strengths

  • Deliberately adversarial diagnostic design: maximally polarized "opposite" conditions stress-test the authors' own hypothesis, backed by a formal CPC=0 proof (Appendix A) for what a truly polarized signal should look like.
  • Robustness checked across four model scales (1.7B–14B) and four counterfactual prompt constructions (Appendix C) — reduces the odds this is a one-off artifact of Table 1's specific wording.
  • CSCR is a minimal, single-line modification to GRPO's advantage — reweight + renormalize — making it easy to audit and mechanistically compare against the diagnosis.
  • Honestly reports baseline weaknesses (some self-distillation methods underperform the untrained base model) rather than cherry-picking favorable comparisons.
  • Ablations test the two most natural alternative hypotheses (trust the shift's sign; upweight instead of downweight) and show concrete failure modes, not just "underperforms."

Limitations

  • Training experiments cover only Qwen3-1.7B/4B — no 8B/14B results despite the diagnosis being run at those scales, and no non-Qwen model family.
  • Evaluation is restricted to competition math (AMC23, AIME24/25/26, SMT25); the paper's own framing cites "math and code" as RLVR domains, but no code-generation domain is tested.
  • Requires two extra full-vocabulary re-scoring passes per token (positive + negative counterfactual conditioning); the added compute is never quantified in wall-clock or FLOPs.
  • No variance/confidence intervals on Mean@32 results, and no full accuracy table across the (λ, α, γ) sweep — only qualitative training-dynamics plots.

Questions for Further Exploration

  1. Does CSCR's improvement persist at 8B/14B, where the same sensitivity diagnosis was run, or does the attenuation mechanism need re-tuning at larger scale?
  2. How does CSCR interact with non-math verifiable domains (code execution, tool-use rewards) where "substitutable surface tokens" may differ from math discourse markers?
  3. Would combining CSCR's sensitivity-based downweighting with an actual (non-counterfactual) OPSD teacher signal yield further gains, or are the two signals redundant/conflicting?
  4. What is the wall-clock/compute overhead of the extra positive/negative re-scoring passes relative to plain GRPO, especially at longer generation lengths?
  5. How sensitive are the headline Table 4 numbers to (λ, α, γ) beyond the single reported default, given ablations show only training-dynamics curves, not a full accuracy sweep?
