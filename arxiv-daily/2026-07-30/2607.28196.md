> **一句话总结：** 在 Mistral-7B、Qwen3-8B、Llama-3.1-8B 三个模型家族上，用 SVD 低秩截断压缩的模型即便困惑度（perplexity）落在标准 1.15× 验收阈值内、且通过数据无关的保真度探针（CKA），在执行标准操作规程（SOP）智能体任务时仍会虚构出未出现在指令中的步骤（Mistral-7B 上每次查询多编造 +1.729 步，95% CI [1.076, 2.424]）；而困惑度损伤相当的幅度剪枝（magnitude pruning）几乎不出现这一问题（-0.146，CI 包含零），说明压缩误差的"相干性 × 误差率"而非损伤大小才是决定智能体安全性的轴，论文并给出一个两轴数据无关探测器来提前筛出危险构建。
>
> **TL;DR:** Across Mistral-7B, Qwen3-8B, and Llama-3.1-8B, an in-regime SVD (low-rank) compressed Mistral-7B build sits at perplexity 1.069× baseline (inside the standard ≤1.15× WikiText-2 guard) yet invents +1.729 procedure steps/query [95% CI 1.076, 2.424] on a pre-registered synthetic-SOP agentic canary, while a magnitude-pruning build matched to the same perplexity shows no effect (−0.146 [−0.375, 0.035]). The same SVD-compressed Qwen3-8B weights that CI-win a paired CKA output-fidelity test simultaneously CI-fail the canary (+0.643 [0.171, 1.229]), demonstrating that perplexity + MMLU + data-free fidelity checks can all pass while an agent still confabulates instructions it was never given. The paper proposes a cheap two-axis data-free detector (coherent_fraction × error_rate) that correctly classifies all 6 labeled test arms.

## 核心贡献

- **发现一个验收盲区**：当前压缩模型的标准验收流程——困惑度（≤1.15×基线）、MMLU 置信区间、数据无关保真度探针（CKA/cosine）——全部通过，但模型在作为智能体执行 SOP 时仍会"编造"不在指令中的步骤（cross-procedure invention）。
- **操作符特异性（operator-specificity）**：同等困惑度损伤下，连贯的低秩（SVD）截断会诱发编造行为，而非结构化的幅度剪枝几乎不会，这一现象在三个架构（Qwen3-8B、Mistral-7B、Llama-3.1-8B）上均复现。
- **同一组权重的分离现象（dissociation）**：Qwen3-8B 的某个 SVD 压缩构建（b98）在配对保真度检验上"胜出"（CI 落在优良区间），却在编造步骤金丝雀测试上"失败"，证明保真度探针在设计上无法看到这一失效轴。
- **机制定位**：失效轴是压缩误差的"相干性 × 误差率"乘积，而非损伤幅度本身——量化误差幅度更大却不触发失效，而相干的低秩误差在很小剂量（<2% 困惑度）即触发失效；且该效应与 MMLU 表现发生双重分离。
- **可部署的数据无关探测器**：提出 coherent_fraction（误差是否低秩/结构化）与 error_rate（误差幅度是否足够大）两个统计量，固定阈值（>0.007 且 >0.01）在三个架构的全部六个标注实验组上正确分类。

## Key Contributions

- **Identifies a validation blind spot**: models that clear perplexity (≤1.15× baseline), MMLU confidence-interval, and data-free representational-fidelity (CKA/cosine) guards can still fabricate procedure steps that never appeared in their instructions when run as SOP-executing agents.
- **Operator-specificity**: at matched perplexity damage, coherent low-rank (SVD) truncation triggers step invention while magnitude pruning does not — replicated across Qwen3-8B, Mistral-7B, and Llama-3.1-8B.
- **A within-arm dissociation**: the same Qwen3-8B SVD-compressed weights that CI-win a paired CKA fidelity test simultaneously CI-fail the invented-step canary, showing the fidelity probe is structurally blind to this failure mode.
- **Mechanistic account**: the governing axis is compression-error coherence times error rate, not the magnitude of damage; a quantization control with more raw perplexity damage than the failing SVD dose still passes, while quantization pushed to the SVD failure cell's rate also fails.
- **A deployable data-free screen**: coherent_fraction and error_rate, computed with no labeled data via randomized subspace iteration, jointly gate all six canary-labeled arms correctly with fixed cross-architecture thresholds.

## Methodology

The paper builds a **pre-registered agentic canary**: synthetic standard operating procedures (SOPs) of 10 ordered steps plus a conditional 11th step. A target SOP and a distractor SOP are placed in context, and the model is prompted (base-model, chat-template fallback) to enumerate the target's exact ordered steps under two paraphrases (maintenance-mode on/off). Three metrics are scored per query: step recall, branch correctness, and the deciding metric — **cross-procedure invention (invented_x)**, the count of step-strings drawn from the *distractor* SOP's vocabulary that leak into the answer despite not belonging to the target SOP or its own conditional. The bank is 24 SOPs × 3 seeds = 144 paired events per arm (Qwen3-8B instead uses a smaller "banked run" of 12 SOPs × 2).

Compression operators are compared at **matched perplexity dose**: coherent low-rank (per-tensor SVD truncation), magnitude pruning (uniform, unrescaled, "damage-matched" to the same perplexity as the SVD arm), and quantization as an additional dose control. Each arm is rebuilt in memory from saved allocations; none are re-probed after allocation.

Three guards are applied to decide "acceptance": (i) WikiText-2 perplexity (40×1024 windows), (ii) MMLU 0-shot (513 questions, 57 subjects, letter-logprob argmax), (iii) a data-free output-fidelity probe — CKA or cosine similarity of compressed-vs-source layer activations under random Gaussian probe inputs (the same signal used to allocate the compression, described in Appendix A). "In-regime" is defined as perplexity ≤ 1.15× baseline.

The **mechanistic analysis** (§4) isolates coherence vs. rate by comparing quantization (incoherent, but able to carry more raw perplexity damage) against SVD (coherent) at matched perplexity, then pushing quantization to the SVD failure cell's error rate to check whether it also starts failing.

The **detector** (§5) is built from two data-free statistics of the compression error ΔW = W_base − W_mod, aggregated energy-weighted over a model's linear tensors: `coherent_fraction` = top-8 singular-value energy fraction of ΔW (computed via randomized subspace iteration, no data needed; for SVD builds ΔW is literally the discarded singular tail), and `error_rate` = ||ΔW||²_F / ||W||²_F. The gate `coherent_fraction > 0.007 AND error_rate > 0.01` is tested against the 6 labeled arms and an 18-point sweep (9 SVD budgets × 9 prune densities per architecture).

## Results & Highlights

- **Blindspot on Mistral-7B**: in-regime SVD build at perplexity **1.069×** baseline (inside the ≤1.15× guard) invents **+1.729 steps/query [95% CI 1.076, 2.424]** over baseline (consistent across all 3 seeds: 2.06/1.90/1.85), while a magnitude-prune build matched to the same perplexity (1.176×) invents **−0.146 [−0.375, +0.035]** — a CI that includes zero, i.e., no detectable effect.
- **Replication on Qwen3-8B**: in-regime SVD (ppl 1.17×) invents **+0.208 [0.042, 0.375]** vs. damage-matched pruning at **0.000 [0, 0]** — the fidelity guard passes, but the canary fails.
- **Replication on Llama-3.1-8B**: at a deeper dose (ppl 1.29×), SVD invents **+1.285 [0.743, 1.882]** vs. damage-matched pruning **+0.069 [0.021, 0.125]** — an **18× gap** with both confidence intervals clean of zero. (Llama's shallowest in-guard SVD only reaches ppl 1.089× and invention there sits at the noise floor, +0.056 [0.000, 0.167], because Llama's weight spectrum has little low-rank headroom — the perplexity guard "catches" the toxic build there only by accident.)
- **Dissociation (same weights, opposite verdicts)**: the Qwen3-8B probe-allocated "b98" SVD build **CI-wins** a paired output-fidelity test (random-probe NLL deltas [0.024, 0.036] and [0.062, 0.076], both favorable) while **CI-failing** the canary (+0.643 steps/query [0.171, 1.229]) — the same tensors are simultaneously "high fidelity" and "behaviorally broken."
- **Mechanism check**: a dose-matched quantization control that carries *more* raw perplexity damage than the failing SVD dose still passes the canary at moderate dose, but quantization pushed to the failing SVD cell's error rate (1.05×) also fails (pooled clean-slice Δ +0.10 [0.029, 0.20], n=70) — confirming coherence×rate, not magnitude of damage, sets the axis.
- **Detector performance**: the fixed gate (coherent_fraction > 0.007 AND error_rate > 0.01) classifies **all 6 labeled arms correctly** (Table 2), including two designed "trap" arms — Llama's coherent-but-gentle in-regime SVD (coh. frac 0.0090, rate 6.5e-3, correctly passes) and Mistral's incoherent-but-heavy prune (coh. frac 0.0054, rate 3.19e-2, correctly passes). An 18-point sweep (9 SVD budgets × 9 prune densities per architecture) supports the boundary, though one point breaks the pattern: extreme Llama pruning at density 0.50 (half the weights removed) creeps just over the coherence gate — flagged by the authors as an untested, unresolved "soft edge."

No claim of "no quantitative results" applies here — the paper is dense with paired-CI numbers as summarized above.

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty**: The core observation — that perplexity/MMLU/data-free-fidelity acceptance criteria are structurally blind to an agentic "invented-step" failure mode, and that this failure is specific to *coherent* (low-rank) compression error rather than compression magnitude — is a genuinely interesting and underexplored angle on compression evaluation. Framing it via Goodhart's law (a fidelity probe becomes the optimization target and is gamed) is apt and the within-arm dissociation (same Qwen3-8B weights CI-win fidelity and CI-fail the canary) is the paper's strongest and most convincing piece of evidence.

**Rigor**: The use of paired confidence intervals, a pre-registered event bank, and per-seed reporting is good practice, and operator-specificity replicating cleanly across three architectures is a real strength. However, several details limit confidence:
- The three architectures are evaluated with **inconsistent statistical power**: Mistral-7B and Llama-3.1-8B use the "hardened bank" of 144 paired events/arm across 3 seeds, but Qwen3-8B uses a much smaller "banked run" of only 12 SOPs × 2 (24 paired events) — the paper does not explain why Qwen wasn't run on the hardened bank, and its narrower/looser CIs (e.g., +0.208 [0.042, 0.375]) are less convincing than Mistral's.
- The canary is **synthetic SOPs with base-model prompting via "chat-template fallback"** rather than real operational procedures executed by instruction-tuned chat models — the authors admit "absolute invention rates are instrument-specific," which is honest, but it also means external validity to real deployed agentic systems is unestablished.
- The detector's **fixed thresholds (0.007, 0.01) are fit to only 6 labeled arms plus an 18-point sweep across just 2 of the 3 model families** used elsewhere in the paper (Table 2 has no Qwen3-8B row) — a very small calibration set for a claim of "fixed thresholds across architectures," and the authors themselves flag one clear counterexample (extreme Llama pruning at density 0.50 breaches the coherence gate) as an unresolved "soft edge," i.e., a known false positive the current gate does not handle.
- Llama-3.1-8B's blindspot claim is weaker than Mistral's/Qwen's: the in-regime SVD build's invention rate (+0.056 [0.000, 0.167]) sits at the noise floor, and the paper's own "honest scope boundary" concedes the perplexity guard catches Llama's toxic SVD build "by accident" rather than by construction — this somewhat undercuts the strength of the three-way replication claim for the *blindspot* specifically (though not for the *operator mechanism*, which does replicate cleanly).
- No ablation on **model scale beyond 7–8B dense models**, and the paper explicitly states MoE architectures were not tested under the controlled battery (only mentions a "prior low-bit-expert MoE build" anecdotally, with no numbers).

**Reproducibility**: No code or data release is mentioned. The SOP canary is described only at the level of "10 ordered steps plus a conditional 11th," which is specific enough for a rough reimplementation but not enough for exact replication without the actual SOP bank.

**Significance**: If the core finding generalizes, it has real practical importance — it argues that the standard compression-acceptance pipeline used across the industry (perplexity + MMLU + data-free fidelity) is insufficient for agentic deployment decisions, and offers a cheap, actionable pre-flight screen. The significance is currently bounded by the narrow scope (three 7–8B dense models, synthetic SOP task, SVD vs. pruning vs. quantization only).

**Red flags**: The unusual lack of author affiliation is worth noting though not necessarily disqualifying. The inconsistent sample size for Qwen3-8B relative to the other two architectures, and the single unresolved "soft edge" in the detector (which the authors disclose rather than hide), are the main technical caveats — to the paper's credit, both are stated explicitly rather than glossed over, which is a mark of intellectual honesty rather than a hidden flaw.

## Strengths

- Clear, falsifiable claim structure: every result is stated with a paired 95% CI, and the paper explicitly distinguishes "in-guard" (bold in Table 1) from "out-of-guard" arms.
- The within-arm dissociation (same compressed weights, opposite verdicts on fidelity vs. canary) is a clean, hard-to-dismiss demonstration that fidelity metrics cannot stand in for behavioral/agentic safety checks.
- Honest, well-marked limitations section that discloses the spectrum-conditional nature of the blindspot (Llama doesn't show it because of insufficient low-rank headroom) and the detector's one known failure case (extreme pruning), rather than cherry-picking only favorable results.
- The proposed two-axis detector is cheap (data-free, no gradients, randomized subspace iteration) and directly actionable as a pre-deployment gate.
- Good situating in related work (Goodhart's law framing, correct citation of GPTQ/AWQ/SmoothQuant/SparseGPT/Wanda/HAWQ/CKA as the relevant prior art).

## Limitations

- Only three dense decoder LLMs at 7–8B scale (Qwen3-8B, Mistral-7B, Llama-3.1-8B); no MoE, no larger/smaller scale models tested in the controlled battery.
- Canary is synthetic-SOP and base-model prompted with a chat-template fallback, so absolute invention rates are explicitly acknowledged by the authors as "instrument-specific," limiting generalization to real-world agent procedures.
- Qwen3-8B is evaluated on a smaller, less-powered event bank (12 SOPs × 2) than Mistral/Llama (144 paired events × 3 seeds), creating an inconsistency in evidentiary strength across the three "replications."
- The blindspot (guard evasion specifically) is spectrum-conditional — it requires the model to admit enough in-guard low-rank headroom, which Llama-3.1-8B does not have, so the three-architecture "replication" is stronger for the coherence×rate mechanism than for the perplexity-guard-evasion claim itself.
- Detector thresholds are fixed from only 6 labeled arms plus an 18-point sweep across 2 architectures, with one explicit unresolved false-positive case (extreme pruning at 50% density); the authors correctly label the onset predictions as "falsifiable tests, not established operating points."
- No code, data, or checkpoint release mentioned, and no author affiliation given, limiting independent verification.

## Relevance

这篇论文与模型压缩、LLM 智能体可靠性评估、以及"代理指标是否等价于真实安全性"这一评估方法论问题高度相关，其"相干性×误差率"机制和数据无关探测器为压缩流水线的安全验收提供了一个具体、可复现的检验思路。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the coherent_fraction/error_rate gate generalize to MoE architectures, models beyond 7–8B, or other agentic task formats (real tool-calling, multi-turn workflows) beyond the synthetic SOP canary?
- Why was Qwen3-8B evaluated on a much smaller event bank than Mistral-7B and Llama-3.1-8B, and would the Qwen results hold up under the same 144-event, 3-seed hardened bank?
- What explains the extreme-pruning "soft edge" (Llama density 0.50) mechanistically — is coherent_fraction picking up a real structural signal at very high sparsity, or is it a statistical artifact of the randomized subspace iteration estimator at that regime?
- Would combining this behavioral canary with existing red-teaming or tool-use safety evaluations (e.g., ToolBench-style benchmarks) reveal whether the "invented step" phenomenon correlates with other known agentic failure modes (hallucinated API calls, incorrect tool argument fabrication)?
- Can the detector's fixed thresholds be made adaptive/learned per architecture rather than fixed constants, to close the gap exposed by the one soft-edge counterexample?
