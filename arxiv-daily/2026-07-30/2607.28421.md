> **一句话总结：** 论文提出并量化"衍生特征过度信任"（DFOT）这一下游 LLM 失效模式——用 PPG-ECG 心律案例研究证明，把训练期才可见的 ECG"特权"信号蒸馏进部署期 PPG-only 可靠性评分（K2 方法），能在锁定测试集上把 AUROC 从基线 B2 的 0.8927 提升到 0.9042（+0.0250, 95% CI [0.0118, 0.0274]），并在协议锁定复现（E3）中把"匹配证据修复率"从 40.11%提升到 44.83%（+4.72pp）。

> **TL;DR:** This paper formalizes derived-feature over-trust (DFOT) — when a downstream LLM treats an instance-dependent derived measurement (e.g., a PPG-derived heart rhythm label) as ground truth or misuses it outside its valid scope — and builds a five-estimand metric chain (COTR, CIR, CRR, ESRM, UHR) plus a matched-vs-patient-disjoint-shuffled evidence intervention to test whether LLM behavior revision is instance-specific rather than generic caution. On a protocol-locked 50,000-record MIMIC-III PPG-ECG benchmark, a privileged-distillation reliability estimator (K2, ECG-teacher → PPG-student) beats a conventional PPG-only baseline (B2) on locked-test AUROC (0.9042 vs 0.8927, +0.0250) and on downstream repair (E3 replication: D1 matched CRR +4.72pp [0.98,8.18], D2 ESRM +6.69pp [2.60,7.19]), while leaving 55% of induced conflict errors unrepaired and showing no confirmed gain at the safety-critical low-false-positive operating points (TPR@1%/2%/5% CIs all cross zero).

## 核心贡献

- 提出"衍生特征过度信任"（Derived-Feature Over-Trust, DFOT）概念：下游 LLM 把上游模型产生的、有效性依赖具体实例的衍生测量（如 PPG 推断的心律）当作直接事实来使用，或在其有效范围之外使用它——这是一种独立于具体可靠性生成器的接口级失效模式，不同于幻觉、校准误差或选择性预测。
- 设计两种互补的诱导机制：D1（当前测量冲突：PPG 显示规律但 ECG 参考显示不规律，测试 LLM 是否无条件接受）与 D2（误导性历史：严重房颤病史诱导 LLM 拒绝一个实际上 PPG 与 ECG 一致可靠的当前测量）。
- 形式化五个可复现的量化指标链：冲突过度信任率 COTR、情境诱导错误率 CIR、正确修复率 CRR、证据包特异性修复边际 ESRM、不必要验证/伤害率 UHR，均建立在"冻结"分母（固定错误集合）之上，把 DFOT 从模糊概念转化为可证伪的比较。
- 提出"匹配 vs. 患者不相交打乱"证据干预法：保留病例、提示词、历史与时序，仅替换证据来源患者，从而把"修复行为是否依赖当前实例的具体证据"与"仅仅因为出现了可靠性措辞就产生的通用谨慎"区分开。
- 构建协议锁定的 PPG-ECG 基准（5万条配对记录，1,275名患者，MIMIC-III Matched Waveform Database v1.0）及一个特权蒸馏基线方法（ECG 教师 → PPG 学生，K2），为后续 DFOT 缓解方法提供可复现的参照点，代码已开源。

## Key Contributions

- Introduces **derived-feature over-trust (DFOT)**: a downstream LLM assigns the epistemic status of a direct fact to an upstream-derived value (e.g., a PPG-derived rhythm label) whose validity is instance-dependent, or uses it outside its intended scope — framed as an interface-level failure distinct from hallucination, calibration error, or selective prediction.
- Formalizes two complementary challenge mechanisms: **D1** (current-measurement conflict — a PPG-derived "regular" rhythm contradicted by a synchronized ECG reference) and **D2** (misleading history — a severe atrial-fibrillation history that induces rejection of a currently reliable, PPG-ECG-agreeing measurement).
- Defines a five-estimand metric chain with fixed ("frozen") denominators to disentangle failure induction from repair: **COTR** (conflict over-trust rate), **CIR** (context-induced error rate), **CRR** (correct repair rate), **ESRM** (evidence-specific repair margin = matched CRR − shuffled CRR), and **UHR** (unnecessary verification/harm rate on baseline-usable HIGH-reliability cases).
- Introduces a **matched-versus-patient-disjoint-shuffled evidence intervention**: swaps only the evidence source (from a different, matched patient) while holding the case, prompt, decision history, and timing fixed, isolating whether a downstream revision is driven by instance-specific evidence content rather than the mere presence of reliability language.
- Delivers a **protocol-locked PPG-ECG benchmark** (50,000 records, 1,275 patients from MIMIC-III Matched Waveform Database v1.0) together with a privileged-distillation reference baseline (K2: an ECG teacher distilled into a PPG-only student), establishing a reproducible comparison point for future DFOT mitigation work; code released at github.com/Zongheng-Guo/When-Derived-Measurements-Mislead.

## Methodology

The authors instantiate DFOT with physiological sensing as a concrete case study. An upstream estimator maps deployment-only PPG signals to a derived feature D (PPG heart rate + categorical rhythm label), while a synchronized, higher-fidelity ECG signal serves only as an offline reference and as privileged training supervision — it is **never shown to the downstream LLM** (`X_p,i ∉ input(M)`). The downstream LLM M receives a task description, context, the derived feature D, and, optionally, rendered reliability evidence E, and must output a decision O.

**Data and cohort.** Source: MIMIC-III Matched Waveform Database v1.0. A channel-level scan of 1,297 candidate patients with simultaneous PLETH/Lead-II recordings, after requiring complete 240-s paired intervals (≤20% missing samples) and valid beat-interval extraction, yields a frozen cohort of 50,000 four-minute records from 1,275 patients, split patient-disjointly into Train (36,115 records / 925 pts), Validation (6,464 / 163 pts), and a Locked test (7,421 / 187 pts). PPG is resampled to 50 Hz, ECG Lead-II to 125 Hz. A four-way clear-case rhythm label (both regular / both irregular = HIGH reliability; PPG-regular+ECG-irregular = "hidden irregularity" LOW; PPG-irregular+ECG-regular = "false alarm" LOW) defines the reliability ground truth used only for evaluation.

**Hierarchical ECG teacher.** Processes eight contiguous 30-s ECG windows (overlapping 0–25s/5–30s views) through a shared encoder, gated multi-view fusion, and a two-layer temporal Transformer, producing window- and record-level three-class rhythm logits plus HR/RMSSD/SDNN estimates. Trained from scratch (a public pretraining init did not help); three-seed mean record-level rhythm AUROC = 0.9912 ± 0.0007. This is a privileged reference model, used only offline.

**PPG-only student and privileged distillation.** Initialized from SIGMA-PPG (an 18-block encoder, prior work by the same group), the student patches 12,000 PPG samples into 120 tokens (patch size 100), pools into 8 windows aligned to the teacher's windows, and passes them through a 2-layer, 256-dim, 8-head temporal Transformer to produce a scalar reliability score p_low. Three arms are compared: **B2** (conventional baseline, trained with weighted BCE only, `L_rel`, no privileged information); **K2** (proposed) adds window-level and global-level knowledge distillation from the frozen ECG teacher's rhythm logits: `L_K2 = L_rel + 0.15·L_winKD + 0.15·L_globalKD` with temperature T=2; and **K2-global-shuffled**, a negative control that permutes teacher targets across records while preserving architecture, losses, and training schedule — testing whether gains require correct instance correspondence rather than just extra regularization. All arms share training schedule, optimization budget, data order, and three random seeds (42, 123, 2026); hyperparameters (T=2, 0.15/0.15 weights) were frozen on validation before locked-test access.

**Evidence interface.** The downstream LLM only ever sees a fixed textual rendering of the PPG-only student's output (a HIGH/LOW label + p_low to 4 decimals + a fixed explanatory sentence) — never the ECG waveform, ECG-derived label, teacher logits, or embeddings.

**Frozen execution stages.** E0: Qwen3-8B under the primary prompt (P1), characterization + mitigation. E1: cross-LLM robustness — Qwen3-8B, DeepSeek-V4-Pro, GPT-5.5, all under P1. E2: prompt robustness — three semantically equivalent prompt formulations (P1 formal, P2 clinical narrative, P3 reliability/status separated) within Qwen3-8B. E3: an independent, protocol-locked replication of the D1/D2/repair/UHR endpoints on the previously unqueried locked-test patients (5,000 patient-cluster bootstrap replicates). Inference uses vLLM 0.25.1, temperature 0, ≤96 output tokens, deterministic parsing, thinking disabled; all cases, prompts, parsers, thresholds, and bootstrap settings were frozen before E3 access (protocol lock hash recorded in the paper). A 17-arm ablation (B1/B2/K1-K5/H1/H2/S4, Table 5 in supplement) was used to select K2's design during validation, before locked-test access.

## Results & Highlights

- **Locked-test upstream AUROC transfer (primary discriminative result):** K2 = **0.9042** vs baseline B2 = **0.8927** on the 7,421-record patient-disjoint locked test, a gain of **+0.0250** (95% CI [0.0118, 0.0274], excludes zero). K2 also beats the K2-global-shuffled control (+0.0115, 95% CI [0.0095, 0.0455]), showing the gain requires correct teacher–student instance correspondence, not just extra regularization. Per-seed: seed 42, K2 .93061 vs B2 .90171 (+.0289); seed 123, K2 .92600 vs B2 .90090 (+.0251); seed 2026, K2 .93262 vs B2 .91162 (+.0210) — direction of effect preserved for all three seeds.
- **D1 downstream repair (conflict scenario), E3 protocol-locked replication:** matched-evidence Correct Repair Rate (CRR) rises from B2 = **40.11%** to K2 = **44.83%**, a gain of **+4.72 pp** (95% CI [0.98, 8.18], criterion met); Evidence-Specific Repair Margin (ESRM) rises from B2 = **21.67%** to K2 = **26.17%** (+4.50 pp, 95% CI [0.98, 8.18] on validation, positive on E3). Notably, **55.17%** of the deliberately induced D1 conflict errors remain unrepaired even with K2's best evidence — substantial mitigation headroom remains.
- **D2 downstream repair (misleading-history scenario), E3:** matched CRR rises from B2 = **94.39%** to K2 = **96.21%** (+1.82 pp); ESRM rises from B2 = **15.82%** to K2 = **22.51%** (+6.69 pp, 95% CI [2.60, 7.19]) — a much larger specificity gain than the CRR gain, since B2 already repairs the D2-induced errors at near-ceiling (94.7%) — ESRM is the more diagnostic endpoint here.
- **Interface harm (UHR):** unnecessary verification of already-usable HIGH-reliability cases rises from B2 = 2.89% to K2 = 3.56% on E3 (+0.67 pp, 95% CI [−0.40, 1.70] — CI includes zero, not statistically confirmed), staying under the paper's own pre-specified exploratory 2-pp interface-engineering limit (validation estimate +0.87 pp, 95% CI upper bound 1.40 < 2 pp — "Pass").
- **Failure induction is essentially total when triggered:** D1 COTR = 1.000 (300/300 reference-confirmed conflict cases accepted by the no-evidence baseline across 37 patients) and D2 CIR = 0.942 (292/310 neutral-correct records flipped to an error by injected severe-AF history); E3 replicates both exactly (300/300 and 297/297 respectively), confirming the challenge mechanisms are non-degenerate and fully reproducible.
- **Cross-model / cross-prompt robustness:** K2–B2 ESRM gains stay positive under Qwen3-8B, DeepSeek-V4-Pro, and GPT-5.5 (pooled D1 ESRM +0.064 [0.029, 0.097]; D2 ESRM +0.071 [0.035, 0.108]) and across three semantically-equivalent prompt templates (P1–P3) within Qwen3-8B — though the paper explicitly caveats this is a fixed 3-model/3-prompt robustness panel, not a claim of invariance across all LLMs.
- **Honest negative result at safety-critical operating points:** at the extreme low-false-positive-rate region relevant to clinical screening, K2 vs B2 differences in TPR@1%/2%/5% FPR all have 95% CIs crossing zero (e.g., TPR@1%: −0.0008 [−0.1763, 0.1620]) — "Not confirmed." The paper states this plainly: K2 improves global ranking and calibration but not hidden-irregularity sensitivity in the stringent low-FPR region.
- **Negative control on a follow-up tail-aware objective:** a tail-aware training objective improves the development-tail mean by +3.06 pp (95% CI [1.28, 5.56]) over K2, but shows no reliable advantage over a patient-deranged shuffled-tail control (+1.31 pp, 95% CI [−1.70, 4.50]; pAUC difference 0.0057, 95% CI [−0.0105, 0.0212]) — the authors conclude this gain cannot be attributed to label-specific privileged information and do not pursue it further, an example of the paper policing its own claims.

## Authors & Credibility

Nine authors spanning Politecnico di Milano (Dept. of Electronics, Information and Bioengineering), Zhejiang University (State Key Laboratory of Industrial Control Technology), Shenzhen Institutes of Advanced Technology (Chinese Academy of Sciences), Emory University's Nell Hodgson Woodruff School of Nursing, and China-Japan Friendship Hospital's Dept. of Integrated Traditional Chinese and Western Medicine Cardiology — a mix of biomedical engineering, control theory, and clinical/nursing informatics backgrounds, appropriate for a physiological-sensing case study. First author Zongheng Guo has several recent first/co-authored papers cited in this same reference list on PPG/physiological signal modeling (SIGMA-PPG, QualityFM, remote heart-rate CNNs), indicating the case-study infrastructure (SIGMA-PPG backbone, teacher/student pipeline) builds directly on the authors' own prior work rather than being borrowed from an independent third party — worth noting since the baseline comparison is partly "the authors' new method vs. the authors' own conventional variant." The retrospective, de-identified MIMIC-III data use is documented with an ethical statement noting no prospective recruitment or clinical intervention, and the paper explicitly disclaims clinical-decision-making use.

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty:** The core contribution is a *problem formulation and measurement framework* (DFOT + the 5-estimand chain + matched-vs-shuffled intervention), not a new estimator — the paper is explicit that "DFOT is not to be declared solved by one ECG-PPG method." This framing is a genuine and useful conceptual contribution: existing selective-prediction/calibration literature addresses whether an upstream model's confidence matches accuracy, whereas DFOT specifically asks whether a *downstream* consumer model uses a derived value appropriately for a specific instance — a distinction that is real and, as far as this reviewer can tell from the related-work section, not previously isolated this way.

**Rigor:** Above average for this space — the protocol-lock discipline (cases, prompts, parsers, thresholds, and bootstrap settings frozen before test access, with a recorded SHA-256 lock hash) and the inclusion of a shuffled-evidence *and* a globally-shuffled-teacher negative control are exactly the right instruments to rule out "the LLM/model just likes seeing reliability language" confounds. The paper's willingness to report an unconfirmed result (TPR@1/2/5% FPR CIs crossing zero) and to walk back a promising-looking tail-aware objective after a negative control failed is a strong positive signal of intellectual honesty, rare in ML papers making efficacy claims.

**Paper-specific weak point (named, not generic):** The primary baseline B2 is a plain weighted-BCE PPG-only student with **no** privileged information — a comparatively weak baseline for isolating the value of ECG-teacher distillation specifically, since B2 doesn't even use any distillation-style regularization. The paper partially addresses this by comparing K2 against K2-global-shuffled (same architecture/losses, shuffled correspondence), which is the more informative ablation — but the headline AUROC number (+0.0250) is reported against the weaker B2, and the shuffled-control gain (+0.0115) is roughly half that size, meaning close to half of the reported "improvement" could be attributed to generic architectural/regularization effects rather than genuine ECG-specific privileged information transfer.

**Missing ablation, acknowledged by the authors themselves:** the supplementary audit explicitly states it "does not recover the three class names/index order, the exact teacher-label generation rule, or whether the window/global classifier parameters are shared," and that a common permutation of aligned teacher/student indices leaves the KL objective unchanged — meaning the paper cannot make an index-specific mechanism claim about *why* distillation helps, only that a frozen recipe produces reproducible gains. This is a real reproducibility limitation candidly disclosed in Appendix C.

**Significance:** Practically important for any pipeline where an LLM consumes a wearable-derived label (PPG-based AF/rhythm screening is a real deployed use case per cited JAMA Cardiology / npj Digital Medicine work), but the reported effect sizes are modest in absolute terms (repair rates moving from ~40% to ~45% on D1, leaving over half of induced errors unrepaired) — this is presented candidly as "clear headroom for stronger methods," not a solved problem.

**Red flags:** None indicating fabrication or cherry-picking; if anything the paper under-sells its own positive results by foregrounding the negative low-FPR and tail-objective findings. The main caveat for readers is scope: all quantitative claims are specific to this one ECG-teacher/PPG-student instantiation on MIMIC-III waveform data with three specific LLMs (Qwen3-8B, DeepSeek-V4-Pro, GPT-5.5) and three prompt templates — generalization to other modalities, other LLM families, or other derived-measurement domains is explicitly framed as future work, not demonstrated.

## Strengths

- Clean, falsifiable problem formulation (DFOT) with fixed-denominator estimands that avoid the trap of population-prevalence framing — COTR/CIR are manipulation checks, CRR/ESRM/UHR are the actual scientific quantities.
- The matched-vs-patient-disjoint-shuffled evidence intervention is a well-designed causal control that directly tests instance-specificity of behavior change, not just presence-of-evidence effects.
- Full protocol-lock discipline with a recorded hash, frozen hyperparameters selected only on validation, and a genuinely independent E3 replication on never-before-queried locked-test patients.
- Reports and interprets negative/unconfirmed results (low-FPR TPR, tail-objective negative control) rather than suppressing them — a meaningful rigor signal.
- Cross-LLM (3 models) and cross-prompt (3 templates) robustness checks, appropriately scoped as "persistence across the tested panel" rather than overclaimed as universal invariance.

## Limitations

- Baseline (B2) for the headline AUROC/CRR gains lacks any distillation-style regularization, so roughly half the discriminative gain is attributable to generic architecture/optimization effects rather than ECG-specific privileged information (per the K2-vs-K2-global-shuffled comparison).
- 55.17% of D1's deliberately induced conflict errors remain unrepaired by the best (K2 matched-evidence) condition — the method leaves substantial headroom rather than solving over-trust.
- No confirmed improvement at clinically stringent low-false-positive operating points (TPR@1%/2%/5%), meaning the reliability signal is a global-ranking improvement, not a demonstrated safety-relevant classifier upgrade.
- The evidence-interface ablation is not factorial: the paper cannot separately attribute ESRM gains to the categorical label, the numeric score, the threshold, or the wording template — only to "the complete matched package vs. shuffled package."
- Index/class-semantic alignment of the ECG teacher's 3-way logits is not fully recovered in the supplementary audit, limiting mechanistic (not just behavioral) reproducibility claims.
- Single case study (PPG→ECG in cardiac rhythm monitoring); transfer of the DFOT framework to other modalities or derived-measurement types is asserted as a research agenda, not empirically validated in this paper.

## Relevance

该框架对任何"上游感知模型→下游 LLM 决策"的模块化系统都有直接参考价值（可穿戴健康监测、多模态诊断助手、传感器到语言推理管线），其五指标评估法和匹配-打乱证据干预可直接迁移到评估其他衍生特征场景下的 LLM 过度信任/过度谨慎问题。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would ESRM/CRR gains hold if the baseline B2 were replaced by a stronger non-privileged baseline (e.g., an ensemble or a calibrated selective-prediction student), to isolate privileged distillation's marginal value more cleanly from generic architectural gains?
- Can the framework be extended to a factorial evidence-interface study (label alone vs. score alone vs. wording alone) to determine which component of the reliability rendering actually drives ESRM?
- Why does the low-false-positive-rate region resist improvement even though global AUROC and calibration improve — is this a fundamental limit of the PPG-only signal, or a limitation of the KD objective/temperature choice?
- How does DFOT manifest and how would COTR/CIR/CRR/ESRM/UHR transfer to non-physiological derived-measurement settings (e.g., retrieval-augmented QA, vision-to-language pipelines, code-analysis tool outputs) where the "privileged" ground-truth channel is harder to define than a synchronized ECG?
- Does the unresolved index/class-semantic ambiguity in the ECG teacher (noted in Appendix C) affect the clinical interpretability of the K2 reliability signal in a real deployment, beyond its behavioral effect on the downstream LLM?
