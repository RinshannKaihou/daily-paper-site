> **一句话总结：** 论文在原生 DeepSeek-V4-Flash（Colibri 运行时）上冻结 MoE 局部状态、仅改变跨专家归约方式，发现单层分叉处 720 种数学等价的专家累加顺序会收敛到 10～11 个不同的续写文本"盆地"；在 192 条持久化解码轨迹上，P32/A/B 三种归约方案对原生参考的路由轨迹偏离率均为 192/192，而作者提出的 C 方案（BF16 算子精确提升到 FP32 后用 FP32 累加）偏离率为 0/192，并通过 post-mHC 端点（16,384/16,384 值零误差）与跨 decode 步的 FP64 持久状态（301/301 检查点）精确重构，验证了这两个状态边界对复现分叉轨迹是充分的。

> **TL;DR:** Using controlled fork-freeze interventions inside the native DeepSeek-V4-Flash/Colibri runtime (not a Transformers reimplementation), this solo-authored paper shows that on a single layer-5 MoE fork under the "why the sheep" prompt, 720 mathematically-equivalent expert-reduction orders compress into only 10 distinct continuation-text basins (scheme A) or 11 basins over 360 exact structural classes (scheme B); across 192 paired persistent-decoding trajectories per scheme, schemes P32, A, and B each diverge from the unmodified native-reference route in 192/192 cases, while the paper's proposed scheme C (BF16 operands exactly promoted to FP32, FP32 accumulator) matches the native reference bitwise in 0/192 cases — a stark divergent-vs-conformant delta the paper then causally validates via exact post-mHC endpoint reconstruction (16,384/16,384 values, zero error) and an FP64 full-persistent-state reconstruction reproducing 301/301 downstream post-mHC states, routes, and predictions across seven subsequent decode steps.

## 核心贡献

- **单层"追踪-冻结-分叉"（trace-freeze-fork）干预协议**：在固定提示前缀、token 位置、被选专家、门控值、六个加权专家项、共享专家输出和完整持久状态的前提下，只改变一次 MoE 归约的操作数表示或累加精度/顺序，把由此产生的 post-mHC 状态空间映射到续写文本"盆地"上。
- **四种归约方案（P32/C/A/B）把"操作数表示"和"累加器精度"两个自由度解耦**：P32 是反事实的高精度操作数对照；A 是 FP32 操作数但每次加法后 BF16 舍入；B 是完全 BF16 原生累加；C 是关键提案——BF16 舍入后的项被精确提升为 FP32 表示，再用 FP32 累加器归约。实验显示只有 C 在全部评测状态（MoE 输出、post-mHC、路由、语言模型 logits）和调度上与原生参考逐位一致，而 P32、A、B 都会引入路由级差异。
- **两个互补的状态边界重构对照实验**：对同一条 B 分支，(1) 对 post-mHC 端点做精确 FP32 加法重构，证明该端点对一次前向内的下游轨迹是充分的（intra-token 边界）；(2) 对下一个 decode 边界的完整持久状态做精确 FP64 加法重构，证明该端点对跨 token 的续写轨迹是充分的（cross-token 边界）——首次明确区分并验证了这两类状态边界的充分性。
- **持久化解码下的路由/文本双重分叉证据**：在中文提示"朋友昨天打来电话"上，360 个 B 结构类经确定性分层抽样扩展到 64 token 后，产生了截然相反的"裁员"与"招聘"两种叙事方向（202/113/45 的类别分布），并通过独立重放验证其是可复现的分支特异性差异，而非解码随机性。
- **提出运行时数值一致性的分层诊断程序**：从 decode 边界的完整持久状态指纹开始，逐步下探到 layer-indexed post-mHC 状态、再到具体算子中间量，为跨后端（GPU/NPU/分布式）验证专家归约的数值兼容性提供了一套可操作的核对层级（L0-L4）。

## Key Contributions

- A **single-layer "trace-freeze-fork" intervention protocol**: with the prompt prefix, token position, selected experts, gate values, six weighted expert terms, shared-expert output, and full persistent state all held fixed, only the cross-expert reduction's operand representation or accumulator precision/order is changed at one MoE aggregation point, and the resulting post-mHC state space is mapped onto continuation-text basins.
- **Four aggregation schemes (P32/C/A/B) that decouple operand representation from accumulator precision**: P32 is a counterfactual higher-operand-precision control; A uses FP32 terms but rounds to BF16 after every addition; B is fully BF16-native accumulation; C — the key proposed contract — exactly promotes the already BF16-rounded terms to FP32 representation before FP32 accumulation. Only C matches the native reference bitwise across every evaluated state (MoE output, post-mHC, router scores, LM logits) and schedule, while P32, A, and B all introduce route-level differences.
- **Two complementary state-boundary reconstruction controls**: (1) an exact FP32-additive reconstruction of the post-mHC endpoint shows it is sufficient to reproduce the downstream trajectory within one forward pass (an intra-token boundary); (2) an exact FP64-additive reconstruction of the full persistent state at the next decode boundary shows it is sufficient to reproduce the continuation across subsequent token boundaries (a cross-token boundary) — the first paper, to the authors' claim, to isolate and separately validate these two boundary types.
- **Route/text bifurcation evidence under persistent decoding**: for the Chinese prompt "朋友昨天打来电话" ("a friend called yesterday"), a deterministic stratified sample of 10 of the 360 B structural classes extended to 64 tokens produces opposing "layoffs" vs. "hiring" narrative continuations (a 202/113/45 class split), independently confirmed as branch-specific and reproducible via paired replay rather than decoding nondeterminism.
- A proposed **hierarchical runtime-conformance procedure** for cross-backend MoE validation, moving from a decode-boundary full-persistent-state fingerprint down through layer-indexed post-mHC state to specific operator intermediates (levels L0–L4), giving hardware/runtime engineers an actionable checklist for qualifying expert-reduction semantics across GPU/NPU/distributed backends.

## Methodology

All experiments run inside the native DeepSeek-V4-Flash checkpoint on Whale Agent Lab's **Colibri** runtime (native non-DSpark execution path, `--no-dspark` flag), executed on a single native-Windows CPU host (AMD Ryzen AI MAX+ 395, MSYS2 UCRT64 GCC, `-O3 -march=x86-64-v3`, no `-ffast-math`) with greedy decoding — deliberately not a Transformers reimplementation, so the authors have implementation-level control over expert aggregation, layer states, routing, and persistent decode state. The checkpoint routes each token to six experts plus one shared expert, making all 6! = 720 cross-expert reduction orders exhaustively enumerable.

The core intervention is the **single-layer trace-freeze-fork protocol**: at one MoE aggregation site (layer 5), the prompt prefix, token position, selected expert IDs, gate values, the six weighted expert terms, the shared-expert output, and the full incoming persistent state are all frozen; only the permutation/aggregation scheme of that one reduction is varied, after which downstream mHC propagation, attention, routing, and greedy decoding evolve normally and are observed layer-by-layer. Four schemes are compared: **P32** (FP32 terms, FP32 accumulator — a counterfactual higher-precision control), **C** (BF16-rounded terms exactly promoted to FP32, FP32 accumulator — the paper's proposed native-compatible-plus-protected-accumulation contract), **A** (FP32 terms, BF16 rounding after every addition), and **B** (BF16-rounded terms, BF16 rounding after every term and every addition — fully BF16-native). For scheme B, a proven algebraic symmetry (swapping the first two BF16-rounded operands leaves the second-step accumulator bitwise unchanged under the tested finite operands and BF16 rounding rule) collapses 720 raw permutations into 360 exact structural equivalence classes.

Beyond the single-layer sweep, the paper runs: (1) a **post-mHC state-substitution control**, where the captured difference ΔH between a B-branch and native-reference post-mHC endpoint (for one catalogued B class, "class 135") is added back to the native-reference endpoint via a literal FP32 addition, and the reconstructed trajectory is compared bitwise against the true B branch across 339 post-mHC vectors, 344 router selections, and 8 driver tokens; (2) a **decode-boundary persistent-state substitution control**, where the full persistent state (window KV, active compressed KV, compressor state, indexer state — 5,883,008 FP32 values) difference ΔS between production and the B branch is computed in FP64 (a literal FP32 delta reconstructs only 10/43 layer records; the FP64 delta reconstructs all 43) and added back, then compared over seven subsequent decode inputs (301 checkpoints each for post-mHC states, persistent-state checkpoints, and routes); (3) a **long-form event-direction extension**, stratified-sampling 10 of 360 B classes under a Chinese prompt and extending generation to 64 tokens, cross-checked by a blind two-annotator consensus and by exact token-ID/SHA-256 replay; (4) a **50-prompt exploratory breadth study** (25 English, 25 Chinese) checking cumulative text separation at 8/16/32-token horizons; (5) a **persistent layer-static protocol** running 3 prompts × 64 seeds × 4 schemes = 768 eight-token trajectories, with a deterministic per-layer permutation schedule reused across the whole request; and (6) a **same-mode canonical reference** (identity-order trajectory per scheme) that isolates within-scheme order sensitivity from the scheme-level shift versus the native reference. All artifacts (scripts, hash-based replay manifests, per-experiment TSV tables) are documented in an appendix for external audit, though raw logs, full tensors, and model weights are kept out of the released support set.

## Results & Highlights

- **Single-layer fork collapses 720 permutations into a handful of text basins ("why the sheep" prompt).** Scheme A's 720 raw permutations produce 720 distinct post-mHC states but only **10 distinct continuation texts**; scheme B's 720 raw permutations (360 exact structural classes) produce 360 distinct post-mHC states but only **11 distinct continuation texts** — i.e., large numerically-distinct branch spaces compress into small text-outcome basins, with basin membership, not a single reference execution, determining the output text.
- **Chinese-prompt event-direction bifurcation.** Under "朋友昨天打来电话," the 360 B structural classes reduce to 6 unique 8-token continuation strings, which a blind two-annotator consensus (κ = 1.0 on 6/6 items) maps to **202 layoffs-direction classes, 113 hiring-direction classes, and 45 other classes** — opposite workforce-direction narratives arising from a single frozen-prefix underdetermined prompt, with the direction confirmed stable to 64 tokens on 10 stratified-random branches.
- **Persistent decoding: only scheme C matches the native reference.** Across 192 paired prompt-schedule conditions per scheme (768 trajectories total), native-route divergence from the unmodified reference is **192/192 for P32, A, and B, but 0/192 for scheme C**; native token-sequence divergence is 161/192 (P32), 178/192 (A), 172/192 (B), versus **0/192 for C** — establishing C as the only evaluated scheme that is empirically order-invariant while reproducing native-reference behavior over the six-term states and schedules tested.
- **C intermediate check confirms bitwise nativity.** A separate 192-trajectory C-only check (3 prompts × 64 schedules) finds the MoE aggregation output, post-mHC state, next-router scores, and prefill LM logits **bitwise identical to the native reference in every case (0 missing records, maximum L∞ = 0)** — the strongest single piece of evidence for C as a stabilization contract, versus P32/A/B's saturated 192/192 route divergence.
- **Post-mHC endpoint reconstruction is exact and sufficient (intra-token boundary).** For B class 135, adding the captured endpoint delta ΔH (max component 0.001953125) to the native-reference post-mHC state reconstructs the B endpoint with **16,384/16,384 values bitwise identical, reconstruction error 0**; downstream, all **339/339 recorded post-mHC vectors, 344/344 router selections, and 9/9 driver predictions** agree exactly with the true B branch, versus a materially different native-reference continuation ("are not in the fold. The sheep" vs. B's "are not in the pen. The sheep").
- **Persistent-state (cross-token) reconstruction is exact given the same next token.** At the decode boundary after position 3, an FP64-encoded delta ΔS reconstructs all **43/43 layer records bitwise** (a literal FP32 delta reconstructs only 10/43); over the next seven decode steps this yields **301/301 matching post-mHC states, 301/301 persistent-state checkpoints, and 301/301 matching routes**, with exact predictions and generated text — versus the unmodified production continuation, which the paper confirms differs from the reconstructed branch, ruling out the trivial explanation that reconstruction just reproduces the native path.
- **Deterministic replay rules out run-to-run noise as the explanation for long-form divergence.** Replaying the 10 stratified-random 64-token B branches twice each (20/20 successful runs) yields **10/10 branch pairs with identical token-ID sequences and matching SHA-256 digests**, and the 50-prompt breadth study shows cumulative text separation growing with generation horizon — **12/50 prompts separated at 8 tokens, 24/50 at 16 tokens, 36/50 at 32 tokens** — meaning branch-conditioned divergence is reproducible and its detectability increases with how far decoding is allowed to run.

## Authors & Credibility

The paper is single-authored by Tianyang Zhu, listed as an "Independent Researcher" with a personal Gmail contact address and no institutional affiliation. The work is built on and instruments an actively-developed open native inference runtime, **Colibri** (Whale Agent Lab, GitHub), and studies a real, contemporaneous checkpoint, **DeepSeek-V4-Flash**, together with its **mHC** (manifold-constrained hyper-connections) architecture and the **DSpark** speculative-decoding system, all cited as 2026 arXiv/technical-report sources — placing the work squarely inside a fast-moving, non-peer-reviewed open-source MoE tooling ecosystem rather than an established academic lab. There is no indication of external peer review beyond arXiv posting; credibility instead rests on the paper's own unusually extensive artifact index (hash-based replay manifests, per-protocol TSV outputs, a documented audit boundary explaining what can and cannot be independently re-verified from the released support set), which is a meaningful substitute for institutional weight but not equivalent to it.

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty:** the core idea — holding every MoE input constant except the pure mathematical reduction order/operand-precision contract, then tracing the divergence through post-mHC state, routing, and greedy decoding to the emitted text — is a genuinely narrow and well-isolated question that prior work (e.g., cited studies on batch-size/precision-driven LLM inference nondeterminism, or KV-cache/cache-free divergence) does not test at this operator granularity. The four-scheme P32/C/A/B decomposition, and specifically the demonstration that C (not the intuitively "safer" higher-precision P32) is the scheme that stays native-compatible, is a non-obvious and useful finding.

**Rigor:** the bitwise/SHA-256 verification discipline is unusually strong for this kind of systems paper — post-mHC and persistent-state reconstructions are checked to exact floating-point equality (0 max L∞) rather than approximate tolerance, and the native-reference replay is independently checked first (13 prompts, c_moe_max_abs = 0) before any perturbation is interpreted, guarding against a broken harness masquerading as a real effect. A concrete paper-specific rigor caveat, however: the paper itself documents that its **own breadth-study tooling had a bug** — "an earlier single-line extractor truncated embedded newlines and undercounted extended-breadth text separation," recovering 12 (not 7) separations at 16 tokens and 17 (not 3) at 32 tokens after correction — a data-quirk the authors disclose candidly but which underscores how fragile long-horizon text-comparison tooling is in this domain. Another concrete limitation: the event-direction annotation relies on only **two annotators reaching 6/6 consensus on just six unique items**, explicitly described by the authors as too small a sample for real inter-annotator-agreement statistics (kappa is reported "descriptively rather than as strong statistical evidence").

**Reproducibility:** strong on paper — an appendix (Section A) lists every Python entry point, seed/schedule, and paper-facing output table, with SHA-256 identity for runtime, checkpoint, tokenizer, and compiler recorded in `replay_environment.tsv`. The catch is that a full rerun additionally requires the (unreleased) checkpoint, compiled runtime, and external output directory — the released artifact verifies the paper's aggregate claims but "is not sufficient to rerun native inference by itself," per the authors' own audit-boundary statement.

**Significance:** the paper is explicit and disciplined about scope — it states multiple times that results establish "controlled causal possibility, not deployment incidence," that C's order invariance is "limited to evaluated six-term states and schedules," and that the study covers exactly one checkpoint, one native CPU runtime, and does not measure real GPU/NPU/distributed-backend incidence, frozen-route mediation, or an exact quantized-operand reference. This makes the practical takeaway conditional (relevant mainly if a real accelerator's expert-combine unit sums partial results in arrival order with low-precision sequential rounding) rather than a demonstrated hazard in any specific deployed system.

**Red flags / overclaiming check:** none found that aren't self-disclosed. The paper is unusually careful to distinguish basin "volume" from posterior probability or deployment frequency, to state that identical tokens need not imply identical persistent state (its central mechanistic claim), and to flag that routing is an "observed discrete boundary" but not proven to be "the unique causal mediator" absent a frozen-route control it does not run. The main thing a skeptical reader should note is that the entire empirical base is one checkpoint, one CPU runtime, three "depth" prompts, and 50 breadth prompts — generalization to other MoE architectures, top-k values, or real accelerator hardware is explicitly not established, only motivated as future work.

## Strengths

- Extremely tight causal isolation: freezes every MoE input except the pure reduction order/precision contract, then verifies the frozen inputs really are unchanged (pre-fork bitwise checks) before interpreting any downstream difference.
- Two independent, exact (zero-error) state-boundary reconstructions — post-mHC (intra-token) and full persistent state via an FP64 delta (cross-token) — that convert an observational correlation ("branches differ") into a validated sufficiency claim ("this specific state boundary is sufficient to reproduce the branch").
- Discloses its own tooling bugs (multi-line text truncation, repeated-event miscounting in the breadth study) and corrects them transparently rather than silently.
- Deterministic replay (10/10 branch pairs, exact SHA-256 agreement) explicitly rules out the natural alternative explanation (ordinary decoding randomness) for the long-form narrative divergence.
- Proposes a concrete, actionable hierarchy (L0–L4: token/text → logits/margins → full persistent state → layer-indexed post-mHC → operator intermediates) for future cross-backend MoE conformance testing, not just a one-off empirical curiosity.

## Limitations

- Single checkpoint (DeepSeek-V4-Flash) and single native CPU runtime (Colibri, non-DSpark path); the authors explicitly caution that quantitative sensitivity "should not be assumed to transfer unchanged to other MoE architectures or runtimes."
- No measurement of real-world incidence: the study does not test actual GPU/NPU/distributed backends, does not perform frozen-route mediation, and does not construct an exact quantized-operand summation reference — so it cannot say how often production hardware actually realizes any given permutation.
- Persistent-state reconstruction is demonstrated for exactly one controlled branch and one decode boundary; it establishes endpoint sufficiency, not uniqueness (distinct computation histories could converge to the same endpoint), and the FP64 additive delta is an exact intervention representation, not a compact or storage-efficient checkpoint format.
- The extended breadth result (12/24/36 out of 50 across 8/16/32 tokens) is a staged, selection-conditioned cohort (survivors carried forward), not three independent 50-prompt experiments, so its cumulative counts are descriptive rather than probability estimates.
- The event-direction annotation is based on a single blind two-annotator consensus over only six unique items, with no annotator-identity or language-background metadata retained — insufficient for a real inter-annotator-agreement claim, as the paper itself notes.
- Everything hinges on the six-routed-expert structure of this specific checkpoint (which makes all 720 permutations exhaustively enumerable); architectures with different top-k or expert counts are not tested and may behave differently.

## Relevance

这篇论文对关注大模型推理系统数值一致性、MoE 稀疏路由与运行时可复现性的研究者很有参考价值：它给出了一套可操作的分层诊断方法（post-mHC/持久状态边界充分性验证），可以直接迁移到评估其他 MoE 推理后端或量化/融合内核的行为一致性问题上。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would the same phenomenon (basin compression, route/text bifurcation, persistent-state divergence) be observed on real GPU/NPU or distributed all-to-all MoE dispatch (e.g., DeepEP, DeepSpeed-MoE), where reduction order is often hardware- or scheduler-determined rather than software-chosen?
- Does scheme C's empirical order-invariance (BF16 operand promotion to FP32 + FP32 accumulation) hold for MoE checkpoints with different top-k, expert counts, or non-mHC architectures, or is it specific to this six-term, DeepSeek-V4-Flash setting?
- Can a frozen-route mediation control (holding routing fixed while still perturbing the reduction) establish whether routing is the necessary causal pathway from numerical divergence to token divergence, as the paper suggests but does not test?
- What is the actual incidence rate of behaviorally divergent reduction orders under realistic production conditions (varying batch size, kernel fusion, thread scheduling) rather than under the paper's controlled single-permutation forks?
- Would a second independent annotator or a larger annotated sample change the 202/113/45 layoffs/hiring/other split, and would the same underdetermined-prompt bifurcation reproduce under other prompts or languages beyond the three depth cases tested?
