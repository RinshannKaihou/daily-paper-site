> **一句话总结：** 针对逐组件归因打分被 backup/自修复交互效应扭曲的问题（Hydra 效应），本文提出 witness-integrated set effect（WISE）集合级因果估计量与基于 Gumbel-top-k 松弛、集合尺寸退火下降的梯度电路发现方法 JuntaLearner：在 GPT-2 small / Qwen3-1.7B / Llama-3.1-8B × 5 任务的 15 个组合上，CRS 必要性（kill）均值 0.67、任务特异性（net）均值 0.54，均超过 EAP、EAP-IG、MAttr 与签名 exact per-head NIE（MAttr net 仅 0.36），并在 MIB Circuit Localization 公开测试集上单次未调参运行即超越 MAttr 发布电路的 kill 与 net，同时在 Llama-3.1-8B 上以 14–16 分钟训练替代 97 分钟精确打分（4–7× 提速）。

> **TL;DR:** Per-component attribution (even exact NIE patching) misranks circuits when backup heads mask primaries — on GPT-2 IOI, backups alone score NIE 0.74 vs 0.14 for name movers, but witness-integrated scoring flips this to 1.16 vs 0.60. The paper introduces WISE, set-level causal estimands that pin downstream "witnesses" to block self-repair, and JuntaLearner, a Gumbel-top-k gradient learner over candidate sets with a descending set-size ladder. Across 15 model-task cells (GPT-2 small, Qwen3-1.7B-Base, Llama-3.1-8B on IOI/SVA/addition/induction/entity tracking), tuned JuntaLearner reaches mean kill CRS 0.67 vs 0.64 for signed exact per-head NIE and 0.55 for MAttr, and mean net CRS 0.54 vs 0.36 (MAttr) / 0.45 (EAP-IG signed); on the MIB Circuit Localization test split a single untuned run beats MAttr's released circuits on kill and net while training 4–7× faster than exact scoring on Llama-3.1-8B (14–16 min vs 97 min). It also proposes kill/net/CRS metrics showing that CPR vs CMD can reverse circuit-quality verdicts.

## 核心贡献

- **WISE 估计量族**：把实际因果性（actual causality）中的"证人"（witness）概念引入因果中介分析——在打分组件集合 M 时，将 M 下游的 witness 组件固定在干净提示上的取值，从而阻断集合外的自修复（self-repair）对 NIE/PIE 的掩盖；对候选池上的中介-见证集合分布取期望，得到 witness-integrated NIE/PIE 及其软最小值（bottleneck score）。
- **JuntaLearner 电路发现方法**：为每个候选组件学习一个 inclusion logit，通过 Gumbel-top-k 松弛可微地采样不同尺寸的组件集合，配合 straight-through 梯度估计与 Adam 优化 WISE；训练沿集合尺寸递减的"梯子"（ladder）退火，单次运行输出一条组件排序，可截取出任意尺寸的电路；成本 Θ(T·B·P) 与候选池大小无关。
- **电路评测三元组**：kill（必要性：patch 电路本身的任务性能损失）、net（任务特异性：目标任务增益减去对其他任务的附带损伤，附带效应封顶）、CRS（circuit recognition score：对数尺度上加权折损累计增益式聚合，偏向小电路）；证明 CPR 与 1−CMD 会给出相反结论（Qwen3 SVA 一例中 CRS 0.76 对 0.59，而 CPR 1.01 对 0.94）。
- **实证验证**：15 个模型-任务组合 + MIB 基准 12 个组合，JuntaLearner 在 kill 与 net 上超越所有归因基线；witness pinning 的收益呈模型依赖性（仅 Qwen3 上净收益为正 +0.7，GPT-2/Llama 上反而下降）。

## Key Contributions

- **WISE (witness-integrated set effect)**: a family of set-level causal estimands (over NIE, PIE, or their soft minimum) that score components by averaging causal effects over sampled mediator sets while *witness pinning* holds specific downstream sites at their clean values — blocking the self-repair from backup components that distorts per-component NIE. Witnesses are imported from actual causality (Halpern 2016) and generalized to the causal mediation setting.
- **JuntaLearner**: WISE maximization over a learned distribution of candidate sets via differentiable Gumbel-top-k relaxation with a dynamically annealed (descending-ladder) set size; a single run yields a component ranking from which circuits of any size are read off. Cost is Θ(T·B·P), independent of candidate-pool size, versus Θ(|M|·N) forward passes for exact set scoring.
- **A trio of circuit-quality metrics**: kill (necessity), net (task specificity with capped collateral damage), and CRS (log-scale discounted-gain summary emphasizing small circuits) — demonstrating that CPR vs 1−CMD verdicts can reverse on the same data (e.g., the two Qwen3-SVA faithfulness curves: CRS 0.76 vs 0.59, CPR 1.01 vs 0.94, 1−CMD 0.96 vs 0.59).
- **Empirical validation** on 15 model-task cells (3 models × 5 tasks) plus the MIB Circuit Localization track (12 cells, 4 models × 6 tasks), with tuned and default configurations reported side by side and per-cell variation shown with 95% intervals.

## Methodology

**WISE estimands.** Given a candidate pool C of model components, each draw splits variables into mediators M ⊂ C (patched to alternative-prompt values), witnesses W ⊂ reach(M) (all downstream components outside M, pinned to their values on the original prompt for NIE / counterfactual prompt for PIE), and free variables F that recompute. The witness-informed NIE and PIE are single-set contrasts Y(a, M(a), W(a)) − Y(a, M(a'), W(a)); WISE_𝒮 is the expectation of a per-set score 𝒮 (NIE, PIE, or the soft-min "bottleneck" score with temperature τ) under a distribution p(M, W | C). Pinning a backup head as a witness prevents it from compensating when the primary is patched — the rock-thrower "Suzy/Billy" preemption structure made operational for LMs.

**JuntaLearner.** Because enumerating sets is infeasible, each candidate C_i gets a trainable inclusion logit θ_i. Per step, B mediator sets are drawn by adding Gumbel noise to the logits and keeping the k largest (Equation 8); witnesses are sampled uniformly from the eligible downstream pool with fraction m (m = 0 recovers plain NIE/PIE). The forward pass uses hard selection (components patched, pinned, or recomputed); the backward pass uses a straight-through estimator over the Gumbel-top-k relaxation, optimized with Adam and gradient clipping. Training follows a descending ladder of set sizes k_1 > … > k_R; a rung ends when the top candidates' gaps stabilize, and logits carry across rungs. The final ranking uses clipped, per-step-normalized gradient contributions (explicitly flagged as engineering choices), so circuits of size ℓ are simply the top-ℓ of one ranking.

**Evaluation.** Faithfulness patches the circuit's complement; kill patches the circuit itself; net = min(1, Kill_target) minus mean capped Kill on the model's other tasks (controlling for tasks that floor each other's damage). Curves across circuit sizes are summarized by CPR (area under faithfulness curve), CMD (distance to the full-pool curve), and CRS — a discounted-cumulative-gain aggregate over MIB's ten log-spaced proportions with weights from log-spacings, rewarding early (small-circuit) recovery. Experiments use GPT-2 small, Qwen3-1.7B-Base, and Llama-3.1-8B with attention heads as candidates and the correct-minus-incorrect answer logit as outcome; tasks are IOI, subject-verb agreement, two-digit addition, induction, and entity tracking. MIB evaluation extends to 12 cells with attention heads, MLPs, and embeddings, using a single untuned configuration selected on GPT-2 IOI validation data.

## Results & Highlights

- **Table 1 (CRS over 15 model-task cells):** tuned JuntaLearner leads five of six columns with mean kill 0.67 (vs 0.64 signed exact per-head NIE and 0.55 MAttr) and mean net 0.54 (vs 0.47 signed per-head NIE, 0.36 MAttr, −0.11 EAP-IG). The separation is widest at worst-case net: 0.28 for JuntaLearner vs −0.03 (MAttr), −0.22 (EAP signed), −0.11 (EAP-IG). MAttr keeps the best worst-case faithfulness (0.42 vs JuntaLearner's 0.38).
- **Witness interactions in GPT-2 IOI (Figure 2):** scored alone, backup name movers outrank primaries (NIE 0.74 vs 0.14); with witness integration the ordering flips to primaries 1.16 vs backups 0.60 — the per-component signature of the Hydra/preemption effect the method targets.
- **MIB Circuit Localization test split:** across 12 model-task cells, a single untuned JuntaLearner run beats MAttr's released circuits on aggregate kill and net and exceeds exact per-node NIE and MAttr on CRS for faithfulness, kill, and net; summed 1−CMD is 9.1 vs MAttr's 1.0 because CMD penalizes MAttr's overshoot (faithfulness above the intact model).
- **Speed:** on Llama-3.1-8B, JuntaLearner trains in 14–16 minutes on the slowest task versus 97 minutes for exact per-head NIE (4–7× speedup, holding across all timed runs); exact scoring costs Θ(|M|·N) forward passes scaling with the pool, training Θ(T·B·P) independent of pool size. On smaller models exact patching wins (training is 2–8× slower).
- **Metric choice reverses verdicts (Figure 3):** two Qwen3-1.7B-SVA faithfulness curves score CPR 1.01 vs 0.94 (preferring the late riser) but CRS 0.76 vs 0.59 and 1−CMD 0.96 vs 0.59 (preferring the early one).
- **Baselines are metric-specific:** on Qwen3-1.7B, signed EAP-IG matches the strongest kill on 4 of 5 tasks, yet its net is negative on all three — its top heads recur across tasks (mean IoU 0.28 vs 0.12 for exact per-head NIE), i.e., high removal without task specificity. MAttr beats the JuntaLearner default on faithfulness in 8/15 cells but loses on kill in 12/15 and net in 13/15.
- **Witness pinning is model-dependent:** on GPT-2 and Llama, pinning all eligible witnesses lowers all three scores; on Qwen3 it improves net (+0.7 normalized) while reducing kill (−1.8) and faithfulness (−2.5), and witness-pinned configs top all five Qwen3 tasks.
- **Ladder training helps:** descending set-size ladder improves predicted faithfulness, kill, and net on all three models versus training on single-component sets throughout (verified with expected-improvement sweeps double-checked by CatBoost).

## Authors & Credibility

Sankaran Vaidyanathan (Basis Research Institute / UMass Amherst) and Rafal Urbaniak (Basis) are co-first authors, joined by Emily Bunnapradi, Michelangelo Naim, and Daniel Wainman (Basis / MIT). Basis Research Institute is an interpretability-focused lab, and the authorship shows clean division: SV+RU led the project and math formulation, RU designed the training algorithm and ran experiments, DW contributed the WISE theory. The paper includes a formal contribution statement and an unusually transparent AI use statement (Claude Sonnet 5/Opus 5 and GPT-6 Astra used for engineering under human-approved plans, with every number and figure regenerated by a script from saved run artifacts) — a good reproducibility signal, though no code/artifact URL appears in the main text pages. The citations are on point (Wang et al. 2023 IOI backups, McGrath et al. 2023 self-repair, Halpern's actual causality, MIB/Mueller et al. 2023), suggesting deep familiarity with the circuit-analysis literature.

## Critical Assessment

- **The headline "outperforms attribution baselines" is thinner than it sounds.** Against the strongest baseline — signed *exact* per-head NIE, which is already interaction-blind — the mean kill margin is 0.67 vs 0.64 (0.03 CRS), and the paper itself concedes that on GPT-2 small the default is "within a few hundredths" of signed per-head NIE on nearly every panel. The convincing separation is in worst-case net (0.28 vs −0.03/−0.22), which is a task-specificity story, not a faithfulness story.
- **Objective-evaluation circularity is half-acknowledged but not resolved.** JuntaLearner trains toward NIE (necessity) and is evaluated on kill (necessity), while MAttr patches the complement and wins faithfulness — the paper's own explanation ("MAttr patches the complement of the circuit, which is what faithfulness measures; JuntaLearner patches the circuit itself, which is what kill measures") implies each method's best CRS column is the one aligned with its training objective. Cross-objective generalization is only weakly demonstrated.
- **The "Tuned" rows are selected on the evaluation metric.** Task-specific configurations are chosen by highest CRS on that goal from the sweep — that is test-set selection. Reporting the fixed Default row alongside is the right call, but the bold Table-1 means come from the Tuned rows.
- **Witnesses — the titular conceptual contribution — lack a principled account and empirical support is 1-of-3 models.** Pinning helps only on Qwen3 (+0.7 net, −1.8 kill, −2.5 faithfulness, i.e., a trade-off, not a win) and *hurts* on GPT-2 and Llama; the limitations section admits there is "no principled account of why witnesses will help identify context-dependent mechanisms." For a paper named after killing the Hydra, the witness mechanism is doing less work than the framing suggests — the ladder training and set-level scoring appear to carry most of the improvement.
- **Scope is narrow:** candidates are attention heads only in the main experiments (MLPs/embeddings only in the MIB appendix run), and the five tasks (IOI, SVA, addition, induction, entity tracking) are the standard synthetic circuit canon — no natural-language tasks where the Hydra effect's prevalence is itself unmeasured.
- **Positive:** the reporting hygiene is genuinely good — default vs tuned configs, per-cell margins with 95% intervals, a dedicated section on how metric choice reverses verdicts, and scripted regeneration of every number. This is not a rubberstamp evaluation.

## Strengths

- A real, well-documented problem: per-component NIE misranks circuits under preemption/self-repair (GPT-2 IOI backups: 0.74 vs 0.14 alone, flipped by witness integration to 1.16 vs 0.60), and prior escapes required task-specific priors (Wang et al. 2023) or redefined interaction structure.
- Principled bridge from actual causality (witnesses) to causal mediation, with the WISE family cleanly parameterized by any effect measure including the NIE∧PIE bottleneck score.
- JuntaLearner's cost profile is structurally sound: Θ(T·B·P) independent of pool size, realized as 4–7× faster than exact scoring on Llama-3.1-8B, and one run yields a full ranking usable at any circuit size.
- The kill/net/CRS metric trio is a contribution in its own right — the demonstration that CPR and CMD rank two Qwen3-SVA curves oppositely (0.76 vs 0.59 under CRS) should change how circuit papers report.
- Honest end-to-end: explicit limitations, model-dependence of witness pinning reported rather than hidden, and MIB evaluation uses a publicly released-competitor's circuits as the comparison point.

## Limitations

- Attention heads only in the main experiments; no MLP layers, residuals, or SAE features, so applicability to richer component spaces is untested outside one MIB appendix run.
- Witness pinning's benefit is model-dependent (positive only on Qwen3) with no theory predicting when it will help — the mechanism the paper is named for is its least validated piece.
- Slower than exact per-head patching on the smaller models (2–8×), so the speed argument only holds once the candidate pool is large (Llama-scale).
- Mean gains over signed exact per-head NIE are marginal (0.67 vs 0.64 kill CRS); tuned results involve selection on the evaluation metric itself.
- Single untuned configuration on MIB; hyperparameters of the ladder (rung stopping, clipping, ranking normalization) are acknowledged engineering choices without ablation from first principles.
- All five tasks are small synthetic algorithmic tasks on models ≤ 8B; generalization to open-ended behaviors is unaddressed.

## Relevance

与你关注方向中的 "LLM Hidden States & Interpretability"（机制可解释性 / circuits）高度契合：它直击电路发现中 backup 头与自修复交互效应这一已知痛点，给出的 WISE 估计量、JuntaLearner 方法与 kill/net/CRS 评测体系都可作为后续 circuit 分析的工具与评测参考。其"组件集合打分对交互效应敏感"的思想对长时运行系统的可靠性诊断也有边缘参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Can witness selection be made principled — e.g., choosing witnesses by predicted self-repair activity rather than uniform sampling over the downstream pool — and does that extend the benefit beyond Qwen3?
- Does JuntaLearner scale to non-head components (MLPs, SAE features, residuals) at 7B–70B scale, where exact per-head NIE baselines become computationally impossible rather than merely slow?
- Is the NIE-vs-kill circularity removable by training on the bottleneck score (NIE∧PIE) and evaluating on faithfulness — i.e., does the method win on the metric it did not optimize?
- The Qwen3 witness result hints many backup heads are multi-functional; can witness-pinned rankings be used to map function-sharing structure across tasks systematically?
- How prevalent is the Hydra effect on natural (non-synthetic) tasks, and does the interaction-aware advantage grow with model scale and task diversity?
