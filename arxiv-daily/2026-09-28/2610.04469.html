01

Method

three roles, one policy, zero labels

The loopTrinity turns one policy into a three-role loop over raw images. The Questioner samples image-conditioned QA pairs; the Solver attempts each with K chain-of-thought completions; the Verifier — an EMA copy of the policy (φ = 0.999, inference-only, refreshed every outer step) — stands between them and cannot be exploited by the learner because it always lags.

The gatesBefore any pair becomes supervision, the Verifier checks it: every premise must be visible in the image, the reference answer must be correct, and difficulty is rated on a four-level rubric. Solver answers earn rule-based correctness plus a Verifier-scored reasoning grade that is multiplied by correctness — fluent-but-wrong chains earn nothing. When ≥75% of Solvers agree against the reference, the Verifier re-solves and adjudicates; siding with them overwrites the reference and zeroes the Questioner's credit.

The updateBoth roles' rewards feed one GRPO/GSPO-style group-relative step through shared LoRA weights (rank 32); the vision encoder stays frozen. The RL loader reads raw pixels only — the corpora's QA annotations are never touched.

RQ = 0.3·difficulty + 0.7·challenge ungrounded ⇒ 0 overturned reference ⇒ ≤ ½ RS = 0.5·correct + 0.3·reason×correct + 0.2·format malformed / non-terminating ⇒ 0

Trade-offs

what the paper does well — and what it concedes

Strengths

  1. Drops both structural dependencies — no human labels, no external judge; verification becomes a free byproduct of the policy (EMA copy), so stronger backbones mean stronger verifiers at no extra cost.
  2. Principled verifier-gated rewards: grounding and reference-correctness are hard zeros; difficulty is the Solver's realized failure rate, not a subjective rubric; reasoning credit is multiplied by correctness.
  3. Dispute resolution is a concrete fix for the majority-vote blind spot of V-Zero/EvoLMM-style consensus rewards, with explicit reward consequences when a reference is overturned.
  4. Exceptional reproducibility hygiene for a workshop paper: per-backbone RL settings, judge prompts, verbatim episode transcripts, released evaluation protocol, and the Vero-600K corpus on HuggingFace.
  5. Limitations paired with experiments that would fix them — a research roadmap, not a shrug.

Limitations

  1. Circularity: shared policy–verifier errors are invisible in-loop; the audit found false positives and negatives, and no error rate is quantified.
  2. Cost: 8×A100 80GB, three co-resident roles, 10–20 min per 8B outer step, days per run; Verifier calls scale with G×K — pricier per example than supervised RL.
  3. Joint vs alternating optimization of the two roles is un-ablated, so the joint update's contribution is unquantified.
  4. Coverage: two backbones, one family (Qwen), 7–8B only — no cross-family evidence; no benefit on multi-image questions.
  5. Gain scope: MME-SCI improvement is essentially multiple-choice only; benchmarks measure components of scientific reasoning, not discovery utility.
  6. Mixed-protocol baselines: several numbers copied from other papers; a sampling-vs-greedy decoding asymmetry in the re-run science comparison.

What's still open

the paper's own questions, sharpened

What is the measured Verifier error rate across training (false negatives on grounded questions, false positives on lenient reasoning), and does it correlate with final gains? The audit is mentioned, never quantified.

Would a fixed, stronger external Solver of a different size recover the missing 7B gains — i.e., is the Verifier, not the curriculum, the binding constraint on weaker backbones?

How much of MMMU +12.6 comes from the supervised thinking-mode warm-up versus the RL loop itself? The warm-up-only ablation is absent.

Would a lightweight external anchor — retrieval over figure captions, or a symbolic numeric checker — inserted at dispute resolution eliminate systematic shared errors without reintroducing annotation?

Does the loop transfer beyond Qwen 7–8B and to multi-image scientific questions, where it currently shows no advantage over the base model?
