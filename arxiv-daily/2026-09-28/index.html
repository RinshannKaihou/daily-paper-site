Recursive Self-Improvement · Lead Story

Audit the Scaffold, Not the Checkpoint

A stationarity dichotomy for recursive self-improvement in agentic coding — machine-checked in Lean 4, with no axioms and no sorry.

By Sebastian Bobadilla-Suarez, Bob Suh & Ryan Fortin

Fix an agent’s reachable edit set and recursive self-improvement strictly saturates: per-round gains on SWE-bench Lite (55 tasks × 5 seeds) decay 0.097/0.156 → near zero, and churn across 401 production coding sessions decays with the same shape as 9,395 pre-AI human sessions (ρ=0.77 vs 0.86). Frozen weights are not a safety boundary — the audit target is the scaffold: tools, verifiers, task decomposition.

Lean 4 机器验证的「平稳性二分法」：可达编辑集合固定，迭代自我改进必然严格边际递减——当前冻结权重的 agentic coding 处于「有界精炼」而非「逃逸式 RSI」；审计对象应是脚手架，而非权重检查点。

Automated Research

Research-Native by Construction: When Misconduct Becomes Unrepresentable

By the IdeaHorizon Team (Di Wang, Yu Liu, et al.)

The AeS platform makes research integrity mechanical instead of prompt-enforced: frozen preregistrations gate experiments, an append-only SHA-256 hash-chained ledger makes freezing irreversible, and closure tallies are recomputed from ledgers rather than model reports. In an 8.85-hour unattended 2D-Ising campaign it reported Tc = 2.268776±0.000148 — a 2.8σ deviation it honestly recorded as refuting its own preregistered hypothesis.
