# Principles that Guide, Actions that Inform: Agent Evolution via Knowledge Abstraction

> **一句话总结：** 提出 SAGA 框架，把交互轨迹逐级抽象为 L0 原始轨迹 → L1 情节知识 → L2 可复用程序 → L3 带显式适用条件的"经验原则"四级证据链记忆，并通过任务条件化实例化 + 原则编译成可执行谓词的动作校验（违规时有界重采样 ≤3 次）形成"执行-抽象"自进化闭环；在 ScienceWorld (strata175) 上把 Qwen3.8-Max 从 48.4（无记忆）/63.7（轨迹检索）提升到 67.0，超过同协议下 GPT-5（43.0）与 Claude-4.5-Sonnet（57.4），Qwen2.5-7B 训练管线从 9.6 提升到 66.9（SFT+GRPO）；配对显著性分析还揭示直接注入 L3 原则显著有害（配对差 -0.0155, t=-3.83），而实例化后转为 +0.0365。

> **TL;DR:** SAGA turns raw agent trajectories into a 4-level evidence-linked memory (episodes → procedures → principles with explicit applicability conditions, each backed by traceable execution evidence) and consumes it two ways: task-conditioned instantiation of principles into operational constraints, plus principle-compiled predicates that veto/resample violating actions (≤3 bounded attempts). On ScienceWorld (strata175, strict protocol), it lifts Qwen3.8-Max from 48.4 (no memory) and 63.7 (raw-trajectory retrieval baseline) to 67.0 avg, surpassing GPT-5 (43.0) and Claude-4.5-Sonnet (57.4); the Qwen2.5-7B training pipeline goes from 9.6 → 58.1 (SFT) → 66.9 (SFT+GRPO), vs 57.0 for the strongest training-based baseline (ScalingAgent). The most instructive finding: directly injecting L3 principles into prompts is significantly harmful (-0.0155 paired delta, t=-3.83 on n=738 episodes) — only contextual instantiation (+0.0365) makes principle-level knowledge pay off.

## 核心贡献

- **四级证据链知识体系（L0–L3）：** 将每次交互轨迹（L0）经 LLM 总结为情节知识（L1）、从共享任务模式的情节中抽取可复用程序（L2）、再从多个程序及其支撑证据中推断带"条件-效果-机制-范围"字段的经验原则（L3）；各级之间保留父子证据链接，记忆为 append-only 存储 + Qwen3-0.6B-embedding 余弦检索，任何原则都可回溯到支撑轨迹。
- **任务条件化实例化 + 原则编译的动作调节：** 检索到的 L3 原则先按当前任务/场景实例化为单条可操作约束（或判定 NOT_APPLICABLE），再编译为谓词 φᵢ(aₖ)∈{0,1} 对候选动作做逐条校验；违规时有界重采样最多 3 次，若无候选满足谓词则执行原动作——软性约束不替换底层生成策略。
- **闭环自进化机制：** 每轮只采纳每个任务变体下按"终局分/峰值分/新近度"排序最优的轨迹入库；跨任务合并阈值 0.75 且原则证据需覆盖 ≥2 种任务类型；验证失败的原则进入排除登记表防止回流；评估前冻结记忆并剔除评估轨迹，杜绝评估泄漏。
- **双范式评估与严格消融：** 同时覆盖 prompt-based（冻结模型，DeepSeek-v4-Flash / Qwen3.8-Max）与 training-based（Qwen2.5-7B 的 SFT + GRPO，LoRA，22,962 条知识条件化 SFT 样本）两种适配方式；提供双骨干六配置消融、n=738 配对 t 检验和逐轮回归诊断。
- **诚实的负结果：** 直接把 L3 原则塞进 prompt 显著降低得分（-0.0155, t=-3.83）；在完整 7B ALFWorld 配置中 R1–R3 轮没有任何一条 L3 原则通过验证——原则级知识目前只在强骨干上被证明有效。

## Key Contributions

- **Evidence-linked hierarchical knowledge abstraction (L0–L3):** raw trajectories (L0) are summarized into episodic records (L1), distilled into reusable procedures (L2), and lifted into experience-grounded principles (L3) with condition/effect/mechanism/scope fields; append-only stores with parent-child links and Qwen3-0.6B-embedding retrieval preserve a full audit trail from any principle back to its supporting trajectories.
- **Task-conditioned instantiation + principle-compiled action regulation:** retrieved principles are instantiated per-episode into one operational constraint (or NOT_APPLICABLE), then compiled into executable predicates φᵢ(aₖ)∈{0,1}; violations trigger bounded resampling (≤3 attempts), falling back to the original action — a soft intervention that constrains behavior without replacing the generative policy.
- **A disciplined self-evolving loop:** per task variant only the best trajectory (terminal score → peak score → recency) is admitted per round; cross-task merging (threshold 0.75) requires evidence spanning ≥2 task types; failed candidates go into an exclusion registry; memory is frozen and evaluation trajectories excluded before evaluation, avoiding evaluation contamination.
- **Dual-paradigm evaluation with unusually careful analysis:** prompt-based (frozen DeepSeek-v4-Flash / Qwen3.8-Max, 6–7 evolution rounds) and training-based (Qwen2.5-7B SFT on 22,962 knowledge-conditioned stepwise targets, then GRPO on a LoRA adapter), plus a six-configuration dual-backbone ablation, paired significance tests (n=738), and round-wise regression diagnostics.
- **Honest negative results:** naive direct injection of L3 principles is significantly harmful; in the complete 7B ALFWorld configuration no L3 principle passed validation in rounds R1–R3, so the principle-level story is currently demonstrated only with large backbones.

## Methodology

**Hierarchical memory construction.** Each level is an append-only store with an embedding index. L1 = Summarize_LLM(τᵢ) (goal, key actions, outcome, obstacle; 1:1 with its L0 trajectory). L2 = Extract_LLM over groups of L1 records sharing a task pattern, keeping pattern/strategy/outcome citations. L3 = Infer_LLM over related L2 procedures plus their supporting evidence, producing a principle with condition, effect, mechanism, scope, and citations; a candidate is admitted only if its evidence spans ≥2 task types, and L2 records with insufficient support remain procedures. Retrieval uses Qwen3-0.6B-embedding cosine similarity. Algorithm 2 governs incremental updates: per task variant at most one trajectory per round (ranked lexicographically by terminal score, peak score, recency); ALFWorld additionally admits executed failures (including zero-score episodes) as negative evidence; duplicates merge only when similarity ≥0.75 AND evidence spans ≥2 task types; validated principles are committed while rejected candidates enter an exclusion registry to prevent reintroduction.

**Knowledge utilization (Algorithm 1).** The actor first emits a one-sentence plan with scalar confidence. Plan confidence sets the principle injection budget: no principle if confidence ≥0.8, at most 1 in [0.5, 0.8), at most 3 otherwise. Retrieved principles are instantiated once per episode into a single operational constraint or NOT_APPLICABLE; the presented context bundles a similar case (episodic evidence), a procedure (strategy), and the mechanism (instantiated constraint). During execution, harmful principles are compiled into predicates; a candidate action is accepted only if all predicates pass; on violation the semantic information of the violated principle is fed back for regeneration, with at most three resampling attempts, after which the original action executes. Post-task, the trajectory flows back through the abstraction pipeline to update memory; memory is frozen before evaluation.

**Training-based variants.** SFT: prompt-based evolution trajectories are replayed into 22,962 stepwise action targets (8,788 full-score; 4,882 zero-score prefixes up to the first scoring peak; 9,292 partial-score peak prefixes), with knowledge-conditioned prompts appending the retrieved L0–L3 context. GRPO: LoRA adapter, group size 8, within-group RMS advantage normalization without mean subtraction, KL 0.02, lr 5e-6, 16,384-token context; dense per-step change reward on ScienceWorld (1,890-item pool, 2 epochs, 30,240 sampled episodes) and shaped environment-success feedback on ALFWorld (2,432-game pool, four seen task types). Crucially, in all training-based settings Qwen3.8-max-0902 serves as the offline builder of abstractions while Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct is the optimized actor ("API-assisted memory evolution").

## Results & Highlights

- **ScienceWorld, prompt-based (strata175, strict StraTA-aligned protocol, 200 items):** SAGA lifts Qwen3.8-Max from 48.4 (no memory) and 63.7 (raw-trajectory retrieval) to 67.0 avg (+3.3 over trajectory retrieval, +18.6 over no memory); with DeepSeek-v4-Flash: 25.0 → 54.8 → 56.2 (+1.4 over trajectory retrieval). The 67.0 surpasses every prompt-based baseline in the same table, including Claude-4.5-Sonnet at 57.4, Gemini-2.5-Flash at 49.6, GPT-5 at 43.0, and ReAct (DeepSeek-flash) at 20.0.
- **ScienceWorld, training-based (Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct):** starting from a 9.6 self-measured base score, knowledge-conditioned SFT reaches 58.1 and GRPO raises it to 66.9 — the best training-based result in Table 1, +9.9 over the strongest compared baseline ScalingAgent (57.0), and well above SKiRL (43.3), ExpTeL (47.8), and plain GRPO (22.1).
- **ALFWorld (valid_unseen, 134 games):** with Qwen3.8-Max, SAGA improves prompt-based success from 96.3 (no memory) to 99.3 (+3.0), versus Memento at 53.0, ExpeL at 46.3, and ReasoningBank at 41.1; with Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, memory augmentation improves success from 45.5 to 57.5 (+12.0). Training-based, SFT+GRPO reaches 93.3 vs Evolving-RL without skills at 93.1, and the SFT+GRPO+External variant (test-time skill acquisition, explicitly not zero-shot) reaches 96.3, matching Evolving-RL with skills (96.0 vs 96.3).
- **Ablation (ScienceWorld, six configurations, dual backbone):** raw-trajectory retrieval (R2) already yields most of the gain (48.4→63.7 Qwen3.8-Max; 25.0→54.8 DeepSeek); L1+L2 (R3) adds +0.8/+0.2; direct L3 injection (R4) DROPS below R3 (63.9 vs 64.5; 54.8 vs 55.0); instantiation (R5) restores and exceeds it (66.2/55.8); adding action regulation (R6) reaches the full 67.0/56.2.
- **Paired utilization statistics (Qwen2.5-7B, n=738 episodes):** direct L3 injection vs no injection is -0.0155 (t=-3.83, significantly negative), while instantiated injection is +0.0365 (t=+5.90); the isolated instantiation effect is +0.0520 (t=+7.74); under instantiation, L3 beats L2 by +0.0238 (t=+3.50).
- **Regression diagnostics (7 rounds, Qwen3.8-Max):** full hierarchical memory causes 11–14 evaluation episodes to regress below a previously attained score vs 12–22 for trajectory retrieval (estimated self-induced regression rates 0.024 vs 0.045), with higher round-7 perfect-score counts (41 vs 35) — suggesting principles mainly reduce execution-time score regression rather than raising the attainable ceiling.

## Authors & Credibility

The team spans Alibaba Group (Hangzhou) — Bowen Ye, Junkai Ma, and corresponding author Wenzhao Li (project lead) — plus Shanghai Jiao Tong University (Yongchao Xu, Xiang Yin) and USTC. This is a credible industrial research line on agentic memory: the first author previously published CoEvo-Mem (co-evolving retrieval policy and memory bank, arXiv 2608.01739), and the related-work section cites several 2026 works from the same ecosystem (e.g., Remember Me, Refine Me; experience compression spectrum). The group has the resources to run repeated API evolution rounds with Qwen3.8-Max-0902 and DeepSeek-v4-Flash and to train 7B models with SFT + GRPO. Methodological hygiene is above average for this area: strict protocol alignment with StraTA on ScienceWorld, explicit clarification that the "Avg" column is a category-weighted mean (Measure 28 / Test-Cond 83 / Find 44 / Chem-Mix 5 / Lifespan 15), paired significance testing, and an appendix that volunteers unflattering results.

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty.** The individual ingredients are familiar: skill libraries (Voyager), cross-task insight induction (ExpeL), rule/manual construction (AutoManual), context-aware guideline selection (AutoGuide), and abstraction-spectrum memory views (Zhang et al. 2026c). The distinctive combination is (a) explicit evidence links preserving traceability from L3 principles down to raw trajectories, (b) post-retrieval task-conditioned instantiation, and (c) predicate-compiled action regulation with bounded resampling. The single most novel empirical contribution is the paired demonstration that direct principle injection is significantly harmful (-0.0155, t=-3.83) while instantiation is positive — a genuinely useful corrective to the "retrieve and stuff into context" default. However, the paper's own related-work section concedes AutoManual and AutoGuide differ mainly in emphasis, so the architectural delta is incremental rather than a new capability class.

**Rigor.** Evaluation care is a strength: strict terminal-step scoring on strata175 aligned to StraTA, baseline numbers reproduced from Xue et al. (2026), paired t-tests, regression diagnostics, and round-wise evolution tables. But several issues temper the headline claims. First, the marginal gain of the full principle machinery over plain trajectory retrieval is modest — +3.3 (Qwen3.8-Max) and +1.4 (DeepSeek) on ScienceWorld — since any memory already recovers 25.0→54.8; the entire L1–L3 apparatus is worth single digits. Second, the 99.3 ALFWorld headline is achieved with Qwen3.8-Max, a far stronger backbone than the training-based baselines (7B-class) it is tabled against in the same "best" bolding; the fair same-backbone comparison is 96.3→99.3. Third, training-based ALFWorld is essentially a tie with Evolving-RL: 93.3 vs 93.1 without skills, and the 96.3 parity requires the External test-time variant that the authors themselves flag as "not a zero-shot result." Fourth, the builder/actor split means 7B training gains may partly reflect Qwen3.8-Max's pretrained knowledge embedded in the abstractions — the authors admit a same-model builder=actor control is missing. Fifth, in the complete 7B ALFWorld configuration no L3 principle passed validation in R1–R3, so principle-level knowledge is only demonstrated to work with large backbones; the complete-hierarchy 7B improvement (45.5→59.4) is explicitly not attributable to validated principles. Sixth, the SAGA ALFWorld gain includes a regression on unseen Pick2 (100.0→94.1, game pick2/17 exhausting the 32,768-token context budget), and the authors concede a substantial subset of residual failures are context-bound rather than knowledge-bound. Finally, serving concurrency differs across protocol families (32 vs 8) — a self-admitted implementation-level confound.

**Reproducibility.** Anonymized supplementary code with complete prompt contracts (Table 4) is promised, and hyperparameters are fully tabulated. But reproduction requires API access to Qwen3.8-max-0902 and DeepSeek-v4-Flash, 6–7 evolution rounds, and 30,240 GRPO episodes — a nontrivial budget — and hand-tuned constants (merge threshold 0.75, confidence cutoffs 0.8/0.5, ≤3 resampling attempts) have no sensitivity analysis.

**Significance.** For practitioners building self-evolving agents, the actionable lessons — abstract, instantiate, then regulate, rather than naively inject — are likely to hold beyond these two benchmarks; whether the specific L3 machinery generalizes is unverified, and the authors honestly label their principles "explanatory hypotheses... not established causal laws."

## Strengths

- Unusually honest and careful evaluation: paired significance tests, regression diagnostics, strict protocol alignment, explicit flagging of non-zero-shot variants and protocol confounds, and negative results (harmful direct injection; no validated 7B principles) reported in the main narrative.
- Full evidence traceability: every principle links back through procedures and episodes to raw trajectories, with an exclusion registry preventing unsupported abstractions from re-entering memory — a real auditability improvement over flat experience pools.
- The instantiation-before-injection finding is immediately actionable for anyone designing memory-augmented agents.
- Covers both adaptation paradigms (frozen-model memory evolution and parameter-based SFT+GRPO) under one knowledge representation, with the builder/actor division made explicit.
- Action regulation is a soft intervention: bounded resampling with fallback to the original action preserves the generative policy instead of overriding it.

## Limitations

- The full principle machinery adds only +1.4 to +3.3 over plain trajectory retrieval on ScienceWorld; most of the headline improvement comes from having any memory at all, so the cost-benefit of the L1–L3 pipeline is not established.
- The builder (Qwen3.8-max-0902) ≠ actor (Qwen2.5-7B) confound is uncontrolled; no same-model abstraction experiment exists.
- Principle-level knowledge is only validated with large backbones — in the complete 7B ALFWorld pipeline zero principles passed validation in R1–R3.
- Only two embodied/household-style benchmarks (200-item strata175; 134-game valid_unseen), small evaluation sets; generality beyond ScienceWorld/ALFWorld is unverified by the authors' own admission.
- Comparability friction: the Avg column is a category-weighted mean; ALFWorld mixes protocol families (token-prefix continuation for 7B vs multi-turn for Qwen3.8-Max) and concurrency levels (32 vs 8).
- Residual failures are often context-budget-bound rather than knowledge-bound (e.g., the pick2/17 regression from 37 to exhausted budgets), and the External 96.3 variant permits test-time skill acquisition so is not comparable as a zero-shot result.

## Relevance

与首要研究方向 Self-Evolving Agents & Automated Research 高度相关：论文给出了不更新参数的经验驱动自进化闭环（证据链记忆 + 原则实例化 + 动作调节）以及 SFT+GRPO 参数化变体的完整对照，其中"直接注入原则有害、任务条件化实例化才有效"的配对实证，对设计自进化 agent 的记忆抽象与知识利用机制有直接可借鉴的价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would L3 principle validation succeed with a same-model 7B builder, or is principle-level abstraction fundamentally a large-backbone capability? A builder=actor control is the obvious missing experiment.
- Can the "direct injection is harmful, instantiation is positive" result be replicated on other memory frameworks (AutoManual, AutoGuide, Memento), or is it an artifact of SAGA's prompt format?
- The merge threshold (0.75), confidence-based injection budget (0.8/0.5), and ≤3 resampling cap are hand-tuned constants — how sensitive is the 67.0/99.3 headline to them?
- Does predicate-compiled action regulation transfer to environments without cleanly executable per-step checks (e.g., web or SWE agents), where compiling principles into predicates may be impossible?
- Since principles are explanatory hypotheses rather than causal laws, can counterfactual rollouts or the paired-episode machinery be used to promote/refute principles over long horizons instead of the current evidence-span heuristic?
