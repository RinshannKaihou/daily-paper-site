## 本周必读 / Must Read This Week

### 1. [[2609.34924]] Audit the Scaffold, Not the Checkpoint: A Stationarity Dichotomy for Recursive Self-Improvement in Agentic Coding
- **中文：** 用 Lean 4（无公理、无 sorry）机器验证了递归自我改进的「平稳性二分法」：只要智能体的可达编辑集合固定，迭代精炼必然严格边际递减——SWE-bench Lite 55 任务×5 种子的精炼循环中每轮平均改进从 0.097/0.156 衰减到近零，401 个生产会话的 churn 衰减形态与 9,395 个 pre-AI 人类会话完全一致（ρ=0.77 vs 0.86）。结论：冻结权重不构成安全边界，审计对象应是脚手架（工具/验证器/任务分解）而非权重检查点。
- **English:** Machine-checked in Lean 4 (no axioms, no sorry), this paper proves a stationarity dichotomy for recursive self-improvement: whenever the agent's reachable edit set is fixed, refinement strictly saturates — per-round improvement on SWE-bench Lite (55 tasks × 5 seeds) decays from 0.097/0.156 to near zero, and churn across 401 production coding sessions decays with the same shape as 9,395 pre-AI human sessions (ρ=0.77 vs 0.86). Frozen weights are not a safety boundary: audit the scaffold, not the checkpoint.

### 2. [[2609.35182]] Research-Native by Construction: Minimal Nodes, Re-verifiable Workflows, and Compounding Memory for Long-Horizon Scientific Agents
- **中文：** AeS 平台把机器科研的可信性从「提示模型守规矩」改为「让不合规状态无法被表示」：冻结预注册门控实验、SHA-256 哈希链工件台账让冻结不可逆且可检伪造、闭包清单由框架从台账现算。35 次端到端战役中，8.85 小时无人值守的 2D Ising 复现得到 T_c=2.268776±0.000148，与 Onsager 精确值偏差 2.8σ——系统如实推翻了自己的预注册假设。
- **English:** AeS makes research integrity mechanical rather than prompt-enforced: frozen preregistrations gate experiments, an append-only SHA-256 hash-chained ledger makes freezing irreversible and forgery detectable, and closure tallies are recomputed from ledgers rather than model reports. Across 35 end-to-end campaigns, an 8.85-hour unattended 2D Ising reproduction landed at T_c = 2.268776±0.000148 — a 2.8σ deviation from Onsager's exact value that the system honestly reported as refuting its own preregistered hypothesis.

### 3. [[2609.35107]] DoAtlas-2: A Foundation for Self-Evolving Causal Biomedical Discovery
- **中文：** MBZUAI 的 DoAtlas-2 用 GPT-5.6 多智能体闭环做自进化因果发现：70 天在 8,724 个候选问题中系统评估 2,031 个（约 1.587 万亿 token、62,677 个实验单元），通路目录从 175 扩到 4,014 条；发现的脂肪–肝–血压–动脉硬化长链（WHR–ALT–DBP–PWV 乘积 0.01073）在两个外部队列 12 条路径检验中复现 9 条；冻结的 63 个方程构成误差 ~1e-14 可精确复算的可解释预测基座（NHANES 死亡率 AUC 0.793，逼近 GBT 的 0.803）。
- **English:** DoAtlas-2 runs a self-evolving causal-discovery loop with GPT-5.6 agents: 2,031 of 8,724 candidate research questions systematically evaluated in 70 days (~1.587T tokens, ~62,677 experimental units), growing the pathway catalog from 175 to 4,014. Its headline adiposity–liver–blood-pressure–arterial-stiffness chain (WHR–ALT–DBP–PWV product 0.01073) replicates in 9 of 12 path tests across two external cohorts; freezing the network into 63 explicit equations yields an interpretable predictor (recomputation error ~1e-14) at AUC 0.793 for NHANES mortality, near GBT's 0.803.

### 4. [[2609.34272]] Broken Symmetry in BF16 Attention: Why FlashAttention Gradients Blow Up Late in Training
- **中文：** 450M 模型 50B token 预训练在 25B token 后梯度范数暴涨千倍、loss 高 0.2 nats 且全程无 NaN——根因是 FA3 的 BF16 反向破坏了 softmax 梯度「每行和为零」守恒律，舍入残差沿注意力加权均值键泄漏，注意力越接近 one-hot 泄漏相对越大。GProj（gauge projection）用每行两次 rank-one 修正恢复零行和：中位 dQ/dK 误差从 773%/85.6% 降到 0.34%/0.37%（与 FP32 持平），每步仅 +4.7%。
- **English:** A 450M-model, 50B-token pretraining run blew up a thousandfold after 25B tokens — no NaN, loss 0.2 nats high — because FA3's BF16 backward breaks the softmax gradient's zero-row-sum conservation law: rounding residue leaks along the attention-weighted mean key, dominating as attention collapses one-hot. GProj restores the zero sum with two rank-one corrections per row, cutting median dQ/dK error from 773%/85.6% to 0.34%/0.37% (FP32 parity) for just +4.7% step time.

### 5. [[2609.36525]] Reliability Testing of Medical Model Performance under Distributed Deployment
- **中文：** 首次把「部署诱导不一致性」确立为独立的医疗模型可靠性维度：同一 checkpoint、同一输入，仅换推理栈（DeepSpeed/FSDP/vLLM/TensorRT-LLM）就改变预测——未加扰动的两卡 DeepSpeed 推理已使 HuatuoGPT2-7B 在 MedQA 子集上 25.52% 答案与 HuggingFace 参考不同（第三语料 45.82%）；仅把发散放大最严重的前 5% 关键层提到 float32，即可把 16 组模型-框架对的平均测试成功率从 0.56 降到 0.21。
- **English:** Establishes deployment-induced inconsistency as its own medical-AI reliability dimension: same checkpoint, same inputs, but swapping only the serving stack (DeepSpeed/FSDP/vLLM/TensorRT-LLM) flips predictions — unperturbed two-GPU DeepSpeed execution already changes 25.52% of HuatuoGPT2-7B answers vs the HuggingFace reference (45.82% on a third corpus). Elevating just the top-5% divergence-amplifying layers to float32 cuts mean differential-test success from 0.56 to 0.21 across 16 model-framework pairs.

### 6. [[2609.35596]] SEABench: Benchmarking Endogenous Misalignment In Self-Evolving Agents
- **中文：** 首个审计「内生错位」的纵向配对基准：无任何外部攻击者，自进化 agent 为局部任务成功写下的持久化更新（controller 文件/记忆/工具技能）在未来不相关任务中造成安全越界。48 条序列、480 个任务实例上，进化使任务完成率 35.7%→47.2%，但安全失败率 43.9%（316/720），而配对不进化基线 0/720；工具/技能面最脆弱（55.83%）；思维链风险线程监控能以 9.7% 误报拦截 70.9% 有害输出。
- **English:** The first paired longitudinal benchmark of endogenous misalignment — with no adversary involved, persistence-worthy updates a self-evolving agent writes for local task success (controller files, memory, tools/skills) cause safety failures on later unrelated tasks. Across 48 sequences and 480 task instances, evolution lifts completion 35.7%→47.2% but incurs 43.9% safety failures (316/720) versus 0/720 for paired non-evolving baselines, concentrated on the tools/skills surface (55.83%); a chain-of-thought risk-thread monitor blocks 70.9% of harmful outputs at 9.7% false positives.

### 7. [[2609.34069]] Towards Certificate-Driven Software Porting: A Self-Improving Agentic Harness for Scientific Program Optimization
- **中文：** 把 AlphaEvolve 式进化搜索扩展为自进化系统：评估 harness 检出的失败（Lean 证明、CBMC 越界检查、差分/物理守恒测试、Compute-Sanitizer）固化为可机器执行的「证书」，控制逻辑强制拒绝冲突组合并定向修复，无需微调。Geant4 两个粒子物理函数 CPU→CUDA 移植获 13.78×/23.54× 函数级加速，Compton 吞吐超专家手写 Celeritas 14.9%；证书反馈把候选通过正确性检查的比例从 55% 提到 90%。
- **English:** CDES extends an AlphaEvolve-style evolutionary loop with an enforceable certificate database: failures caught by the evaluation harness (Lean proofs, CBMC bounds checks, differential/physics tests, Compute-Sanitizer) become machine-enforced certificates used by a control logic to reject conflicting candidates and target repairs — no fine-tuning. Porting two Geant4 particle-physics functions from CPU to CUDA yields 13.78×/23.54× speedups, beats expert-written Celeritas by 14.9% on Compton throughput, and certificate feedback lifts correctness-passing proposals from 55% to 90%.

### 8. [[2609.34279]] Direct Self-Evolving Optimization: Evolving LLMs without Challenger Training
- **中文：** 证明 KL 正则化挑战者目标有指数倾斜闭式解，因此「出题器」不必是可训练模型：冻结 LLM 变异题目（泛化/组合/反转/换目标/对偶五种操作）+当前 solver 打分+Metropolis 接受即可在能力边界附近采样任务，只训 solver。Qwen3-4B/8B 数学推理以少 52–61% 墙钟时间匹配或超越 R-Zero（AVG7 47.57 vs 45.93）；换用闭源 Claude Haiku 4.5 当生成器还能进一步提升（49.60）——训练挑战者的方法做不到。
- **English:** DEO proves the KL-regularized challenger objective has a closed-form exponential-tilt solution, so the task proposer need not be trained at all: a frozen LLM mutates tasks (generalize/compose/invert/re-objective/dualize), the current solver scores them, and Metropolis acceptance samples near the capability boundary — only the solver trains. It matches or beats R-Zero on Qwen3-4B/8B math with 52–61% less wall-clock (AVG7 47.57 vs 45.93), and swapping in API-only Claude Haiku 4.5 as generator pushes further (49.60) — impossible for challenger-training methods.
