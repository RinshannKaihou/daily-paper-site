Why this matters

让搜索策略本身——记忆、变异器、课程——与 harness 一起被 LLM 进化，是「自改进 agent 架构 + 自动化发现」方向的代表性系统工作。 The search strategy itself — memory, mutators, curriculum — evolves alongside the harness: a representative system for self-improving agents and automated discovery.

Critical edge

Equal wall-clock is not equal compute. MILO runs 3 islands with a 6-mutator frontier-agent pool; per-method search-side cost is never reported. Part of the headline deltas may be bought with more search compute, not a better algorithm.

Also: every experiment uses a single DeepAgents Python genome — the “framework-agnostic” claim is by construction, not by experiment. Full audit ↓

01 Methodology distilled

Automated Harness Discovery (AHD): freeze the model M, search the space of runnable harnesses h (a single DeepAgents/LangGraph Python file) to advance the accuracy–token–latency Pareto front, with held-out validation and k = 3 rollouts per task. Three cooperating components:

1

Hierarchical lineage memory

K = 3 append-only island trees. Rejected children persist with their patch and Δacc/Δcost verdicts, so failure evidence prunes the space and pushes mutators toward bolder structural rewrites. Islands evolve in isolation, preserving diversity.

↓ evidence feeds
2

Evidence-driven mutator agents

One coding agent per island from a pool of six (Claude Code, Codex, four DeepAgents variants). Receives parent source, failure traces, and an island snapshot — all as on-disk paths; diagnose-first, whole-harness rewrite, 45-min cap.

↓ stall signals
3

Meta-evolutionary orchestrator

On a 3-round stall, diagnoses the bottleneck (weak mutator / inert or exhausted lineage / crowded niche / global plateau) and applies at most two of: REASSIGN · GRAFT · SPECIATE · CURRICULUM.

admission · Pareto hyper-volume growth > 0 (ε=0.02)
held-out re-scoring · no task-tailored logic (constraint C)

03 Strengths what holds up
  • +The orchestrator is a real mechanism, not a slogan: the round 6–14 stall-and-recover trace and the weakest-seed-wins outcome (39.2% → 59.9%) are concrete evidence.
  • +Keeping rejected mutations as first-class negative evidence is a clean, cheap counter to LLM mutators’ exploitation bias and diversity decay.
  • +Disciplined evaluation: three long-horizon benchmarks, two backbones, 8 harness + 6 search baselines under identical seeds, budget, and evaluator; official verifier for the math records.
  • +Honest systems findings independent of MILO: SoTA harnesses can be counterproductive (PaperBench), harness gains flip sign across backbones (4/8 on gpt-oss-120b), token spend does not predict accuracy.
  • +Strong cost story: better accuracy and fewer tokens (0.74×/0.70×), plus robustness under a handicapped model (81.1 vs 34.4 RR@5).
04 Limitations audit notes
  • □One genome in practice: a single-file DeepAgents Python harness; no cross-framework search experiment despite the representation-agnostic framing.
  • □Equal wall-clock hides unequal search compute; no per-method search-side token/cost accounting.
  • □Single 72-hour run per configuration — no seed-level variance for the evolutionary process itself; the smallest margin (DeepSWE +10.3) is the most fragile.
  • □Self-Harness, DarwinX, AIDE² absent (code unavailable) — exactly the prior strategy-level self-improving systems MILO claims to subsume.
  • □PaperBench metrics come from a GPT-5.5 rubric judge (RR threshold ≥ 0.9); no judge-reliability analysis for the +28.3 headline.
  • □Four orchestrator actions are hand-mapped to four diagnoses; no explicit limitations section; code release only implied.
05 Questions for further exploration
  1. Does the lineage memory’s prune-inducing effect survive across heterogeneous genomes (YAML, tool repos, multi-framework sub-agents), or does it depend on single-file code?
  2. Under equal compute rather than equal wall-clock, how much of the +12.0 / +28.3 / +10.3 advantage over GEPA and Meta-Harness remains?
  3. What is the run-to-run variance of a full 72-hour search on DeepSWE, where the margin is smallest and the “no prior gain” baseline makes the claim most fragile?
  4. Can the diagnosis→action mapping be learned or expanded (e.g., a CURRICULUM that adapts the verifier or rollout count k), or does performance degrade once interventions are no longer hand-designed?
  5. Do the EinsteinArena optimizer-writing harnesses transfer to other instance-level domains (TimeSeriesGym, AutoML), or are they tuned to mathematical optimization traces?
  6. The 4K-cap result (81.1 vs 34.4) suggests harnesses can substitute for model capability — can MILO harnesses compose with quantization/distillation for cheap-but-reliable agents?
