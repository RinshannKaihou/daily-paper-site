01

Methodology

Discovery as constrained search over an explicit evidence ledger — six mechanisms decide what may be called a discovery.
M1

Task contract

Every run signs C = (D₂ , Dₚ , E, B, A, S): a public data view for proposing, a terminal view reserved for judging, fixed evaluators, budget, editable artifact space, and the claim scope.

M2

Append-only typed memory

A ledger keeps successes and failures with attribution and rejection reasons. Infrastructure incidents trigger bounded recovery without counting as evidence against the method.

M3

Active falsification

Challenge inputs — boundary cases, coefficient mismatches, counterexample searches, proof obligations — target the proposal’s stated mechanism, not just the objective.

M4

Three-valued admission

Gates check schema, scope, correctness, budget, replay — only a full pass replaces the incumbent. A higher public score alone can never displace a verified endpoint.

1 pass0 fail⊥ unresolved
M5

Phase control

Chooses among explore / refine / recover / finalize — later shown to be the single most load-bearing component: removing it costs 55.59 normalized points.

M6

Independent replay

The frozen endpoint is re-evaluated outside the search loop and sealed: Seal = h(C, c*, Mₜ, ξ*, σ*, S), distinguishing finite computation, general argument, and formal kernel.

Safety invariant

Protocol-level guarantee: retained-endpoint correctness never decreases — every admission replacement carries passing credentials.

Evidence layers

Case studies stack four non-interchangeable layers: semantic reconstruction → exact / exhaustive replay → mutation controls → formal or analytic bridge — each claim records which layers it reached.

Frozen evaluation

Pre-registered: backbone (Deepseek-v4-Flash), seeds 2026081901–03, task cohorts and adapters all fixed before any run; held-out replay on 32 never-used official inputs.

04

Verdict

Where the protocol genuinely advances the field — and where the evidence is thin.

+Strengths

  • Checkable gates, not rhetoric — operationalizes a named failure mode of AI research agents: promoting fragile candidates as discoveries.
  • Rare evaluation hygiene — pre-registered frozen protocol, held-out replay of frozen endpoints, paired ablations with sign tests, trace-level mechanism diagnostics.
  • Intellectual honesty — NMF reversal, the Convolve2D tie, the no-memory variant’s better worst-task floor, and “not new algorithms” labels are all self-disclosed.
  • Independently checkable artifacts across four domains with per-claim evidence boundaries instead of one undifferentiated “verified” stamp.
  • Causally informative ablation — every component’s removal hurts; proof admission wins 6/6 paired tasks; phase control most load-bearing (−55.59).

−Limitations

  • Single backbone (Deepseek-v4-Flash) for all five methods; no evidence the ranking transfers.
  • Three seeds, no variance estimates on benchmark means; AlgoTune / Math14 aggregates are descriptive; the Math14 win is +0.01 with a losing paired record.
  • Small task counts (12 / 14 / 6) compressed into normalized means; the no-memory variant dominates on worst-task floor, so the full system is not uniformly better.
  • Search-cost efficiency unmeasured — no paired fixed-budget comparisons or resource accounting vs baselines.
  • Open bridges — heterogeneous case budgets, unresolved external priority, a missing normalization bridge for the hypercube theorem; extension to non-exact-evaluator domains is future work.
05

Open questions

What would settle the remaining doubt.
Q1

Does the evidence-governance advantage persist on a different LLM backbone, given the entire comparison ran on Deepseek-v4-Flash?

Q2

What is the wall-clock and call-budget overhead of challenge, admission, and replay vs baselines at equal fixed budgets — the authors flag missing resource accounting as limitation #1?

Q3

Can the no-memory variant’s superior worst-task floor be reconciled with full EPOCH’s mean advantage — e.g. task-adaptive memory projections — or is memory inherently a mean-over-variance trade?

Q4

Do the 54-comparison median-of-23, R(Tss444) ≤ 20, and the Hβ counterexample survive external priority review and independent formalization?

Q5

Can the protocol extend to domains without exact evaluators — simulation, wet-lab science — where the terminal view is noisy and the finite-certificate proposition does not apply?
