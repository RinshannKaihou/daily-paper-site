> **一句话总结：** 针对 LLM Agent 迭代自蒸馏中跨轮次性能崩塌问题，人大高瓴提出即插即用增强 ReSAIL：用教师特权信息敏感度（JSD）挑选高信息量交互步骤做蒸馏（SGS）+ 轨迹间损失均衡（TLB）+ 特权视角保留正则（PR）守住下一轮"教师能力"；在 ALFWorld/TextCraft 上使 SDPO/OEL 三轮持续提升，第三轮成功率较母方法平均提高 22.5 个百分点（如 Qwen3-8B ALFWorld OOD：OEL 51.3% → OEL+ReSAIL 78.4%），PR 还把 PI 条件化成功率从崩塌的 44.8% 拉回到 70.3%。

> **TL;DR:** Iterative privileged-information (PI) self-distillation methods (SDPO, OEL) collapse across deployment cycles on ALFWorld/TextCraft — e.g. OEL on ALFWorld OOD (Qwen3-8B) falls from 66.7% to 51.3% success by cycle 3. ReSAIL, a plug-in fix, distills only the steps where PI most shifts the frozen teacher's predictions (sensitivity-guided selection with ρ=0.05), balances losses per trajectory, and adds a KL regularizer keeping the student's PI-conditioned outputs at the frozen teacher's. Result: OEL+ReSAIL hits 78.4% vs OEL's 51.3% on ALFWorld OOD cycle 3 (+27.1 pts), final-cycle gains average 22.5 points across six model-benchmark settings, and PI-conditioned competence rises to 70.3% instead of collapsing to 44.8% — at 1.23× the training-loop compute of OEL.

## 核心贡献

- **首次实证并缓解迭代自蒸馏的性能崩塌**：在固定评测任务与特权信息（PI）的条件下，SDPO 和 OEL 的部署成功率与 PI 条件化成功率随轮次双双下滑；本文首次证明仅通过更稳健的学习机制（无需额外环境交互）即可有效缓解该崩塌。
- **提出 ReSAIL**——面向迭代 PI 自蒸馏的即插即用增强，包含两个互补模块：
  - **TBSD（轨迹均衡选择性蒸馏）**：以教师模型的"PI 敏感度"（同一交互步骤下有/无 PI 两种视角输出分布的 Jensen–Shannon 散度）为打分，仅保留敏感度最高的 top-ρ 步骤做蒸馏（SGS），并按轨迹均衡损失（TLB），每条轨迹总权重恒为 1/|B|。
  - **PR（特权保留）**：由于更新后的学生要充当下一轮的 PI 条件化教师，PR 用 KL 正则把学生的特权视角输出分布约束到冻结教师的分布上（覆盖被选中和未被选中的全部步骤），防止"教人能力"在轮次间退化。
- **三轮、双模型规模、双基准的持续增益**：ReSAIL 使 SDPO/OEL 在 ALFWorld（ID/OOD）与 TextCraft 上每轮都优于母方法且均值单调不降；第三轮成功率较母方法平均提升 22.5 个百分点。
- **方法学的普适性验证**：SGS 作为离线数据过滤器也能提升多模态 GUI Agent（AITZ 上 Qwen3-VL-4B）的动作预测精度，说明 PI 敏感度选步准则不局限于文本 Agent 迭代训练。

## Key Contributions

- First evidence that performance collapse in iterative agent self-distillation (SDPO, OEL) can be mitigated purely by a more robust learning mechanism — no extra environment interaction, working entirely offline on already-collected deployment trajectories (unlike the off-policy context-distillation fix of Chen et al. 2026a).
- ReSAIL: a plug-in augmentation combining (1) TBSD — Sensitivity-Guided Selection (teacher PI sensitivity = JSD between privileged and ordinary views, keep top-ρ steps) plus Trajectory Loss Balancing — and (2) Privileged Retention, a KL regularizer that keeps the student's PI-conditioned output distributions at the frozen teacher's so it can still supervise the next cycle.
- Substantial, sustained gains: final-cycle success improved by 7.0–26.1 points over SDPO and 20.3–37.0 points over OEL (average +22.5 points over six model-benchmark settings) across three cycles on ALFWorld ID/OOD and TextCraft with Qwen3-4B/8B.
- Controlled ablations showing the two mechanisms act at different timescales: selection drives first-cycle learning (Top vs Random selection: +12.5/+11.5 pts ID/OOD at ρ=0.05), while privileged-view retention specifically preserves cross-cycle PI-conditioned competence (70.3% vs 44.8% without retention at cycle 3).
- Generality check: SGS as a static offline filter lifts multimodal GUI-agent action accuracy on AITZ (65.95% vs 64.89% for OEL), and a full cost breakdown shows the trade-off (selection alone is cheaper than OEL; full ReSAIL costs 1.23×).

## Methodology

**Setup.** In cycle r, a starting policy π_r has a fixed trajectory dataset D_r (no further environment interaction). Each policy has two views sharing parameters: an *ordinary view* π(·|h) (deployment) and a *privileged view* π(·|h, c) conditioned on PI c (e.g., a summary extracted from the complete trajectory by the cycle-start policy). The student π^S is trained from π_r against a frozen teacher π^T = π_r; the updated student becomes π_{r+1} and initializes both roles next cycle. The base objective (shared with OEL/SDPO-offline integrations) is a token-averaged student-to-teacher KL matching the ordinary-view student to the privileged-view teacher at logged steps, uniformly averaged.

**TBSD (Trajectory-Balanced Selective Distillation).**
- *SGS:* PI sensitivity of a step is the token-averaged JSD between the frozen teacher's ordinary-view and privileged-view distributions at that step (same response prefixes). Steps are ranked batch-wide and only the top ⌈ρ|U|⌉ are distilled (default ρ=0.05 on ALFWorld, 0.25 on TextCraft).
- *TLB:* selected losses are averaged within each trajectory, then summed with weight 1/|B| per trajectory, so trajectories with many selected steps do not dominate.

**PR (Privileged Retention).** A second KL term matches the *student's privileged-view* distributions to the frozen teacher's at **all** steps in U (selected and unselected), with the same trajectory-level reduction. Total loss L = L_sel + λ·L_ret (λ=0.5 ALFWorld, 1.0 TextCraft). Sensitivity scores are recomputed per update and held fixed during backprop. KL/JSD computations use a Top-20-plus-residual-tail vocabulary approximation.

**Protocol.** Qwen3-4B/8B (thinking disabled) on ALFWorld (128 ID + 128 OOD episodes) and TextCraft (100 episodes); 3 cycles × 30 updates; success rate over 3 decoding seeds. Baselines: ReAct, RFT, offline GRPO (action-matching reward), EPD, offline SDPO (with TIS correction), OEL — all sharing the cycle-1 corpus. Qwen3-VL-4B-Instruct on AITZ: one cycle of 100 updates, OEL vs OEL+SGS (fixed global top-80% steps). Training on slime + Megatron-LM + SGLang, 8×H800.

## Results & Highlights

- **Final-cycle collapse reversed (Table 1):** On ALFWorld OOD with Qwen3-8B at cycle 3, OEL+ReSAIL reaches **78.4%** success vs **51.3%** for OEL (+27.1 pts) and SDPO+ReSAIL **75.3%** vs **49.2%** for SDPO (+26.1 pts), while the strongest non-PI baselines sit at 63.8% (RFT), 62.2% (GRPO), and 46.6% (EPD). Averaged equally over six model-benchmark settings, final-cycle gains over the parent method are 7.0–26.1 pts (SDPO) and 20.3–37.0 pts (OEL), averaging **22.5 points**.
- **Gains compound rather than fade:** the advantage over the parent method *grows* from 6.8 to 18.3 pts (SDPO) and from 5.0 to 26.6 pts (OEL) between cycle 1 and cycle 3; augmented runs are non-decreasing in every setting, whereas OEL finishes below its own cycle-1 score in all six settings and SDPO in five.
- **Component ablation (Qwen3-4B, ALFWorld, Table 2):** SGS alone lifts cycle-1 success by +11.5 (ID) / +14.4 (OOD) over OEL, but SGS+TLB still declines by cycle 3 (56.5 ID / 45.6 OOD); adding PR yields the best cycle-3 numbers (**74.2 ID / 67.2 OOD**), beating TBSD by +17.7/+21.6 pts and PR-alone by +9.9/+13.3 pts. Selection criterion matters, not just sparsity: at the same ρ=0.05, Top selection beats Random by +12.5/+11.5 pts, and Bottom selection is *worse* than Random.
- **Retention preserves teaching ability (Table 9):** without retention, PI-conditioned success on the fixed 128 ALFWorld OOD tasks falls from 61.5% (cycle 1) to **44.8%** (cycle 3); with PR it rises from 58.9% to **70.3%**. Ordinary-view retention only reaches 62.2% under PI — an 8.1-pt gap for PR under PI vs just 1.3 pts without PI, supporting the view-specific design.
- **Cost (8×H800, cycle-1 training loop):** SGS cuts student optimization from 0.91 to 0.24 GPU-hours (−74%) and total loop cost from 4.33 to 4.08 GPU-hours; full ReSAIL (PR at all steps) costs 5.32 GPU-hours, i.e. **1.23× OEL**.
- **Multimodal generalization (AITZ):** OEL+SGS (fixed top-80% of steps by initial-model PI sensitivity) finishes at **65.95%** step-level action accuracy vs **64.89%** for OEL (+1.06 pts), same 100-update budget on a fixed 843-step monitoring subset.

## Authors & Credibility

All five authors are from the Gaoling School of Artificial Intelligence, Renmin University of China; Zhiwu Lu (corresponding) is a well-established professor there with a long track record in multimodal learning and LLM research. The work is a single-institution academic effort. Credibility positives: the paper builds directly on very recent PI-distillation lines (SDPO/Hübotter et al., OEL/Ye et al., EPD/Gou et al.), discloses exact hyperparameters, seeds, prompts, per-component GPU-hour costs, and negative details (e.g., λ=1 underperforms; error bars measure only decoding variability). No code release is mentioned for ReSAIL itself, though it builds on open infrastructure (slime, Megatron-LM, SGLang).

## Critical Assessment

- **Single training seed per configuration.** The reported SDs are over three *decoding* seeds only — Appendix C.1 states this explicitly ("decoding variability, rather than variation across independent training runs"). For a paper whose central phenomenon is *instability across cycles*, not re-running training with different seeds is a genuine weakness: some "collapse" and "recovery" gaps (e.g., OEL 38.5% vs OEL+ReSAIL 67.2% on Qwen3-4B OOD cycle 3) are plausibly robust, but cycle-to-cycle wiggles of ±2–5 pts in Table 1 could be training-run noise.
- **Benchmark breadth is thin.** The iterative result rests on two synthetic text environments (ALFWorld, TextCraft) and three cycles only. There is no evidence the retention benefit persists past cycle 3, and no web/search/tool-agent setting where deployment distributions drift more strongly. The AITZ experiment is a much smaller effect (+1.06 pts, one decoding seed, 843 evaluation steps) and only tests SGS-as-filter, not the full method.
- **Per-benchmark hyperparameters without a stated selection protocol.** ALFWorld uses ρ=0.05, λ=0.5 while TextCraft uses ρ=0.25, λ=1.0; the paper does not say how these were chosen (and evaluation has no validation-based checkpoint selection), leaving mild tuning-on-test ambiguity. The sweep in Figure 4 is only on ALFWorld.
- **Honest cost accounting, but the headline framing favors the method:** selection alone is *cheaper* than OEL, yet the full configuration that produces the sustained multi-cycle gains costs 1.23× OEL training-loop compute — a fair trade, but "selection improves cost–performance" and "retention sustains gains" are different claims that headlines can blur.
- **PI is a single, self-generated modality for text tasks** (trajectory summaries extracted by the cycle-start policy). The method's premise — PI-conditioned behavior is worth preserving — is only as good as the PI; no experiment varies PI quality/type (e.g., demonstrations vs hindsight feedback) for the retention claim.
- Novelty is incremental-but-real: sensitivity-based data selection and KL-to-reference regularization are each familiar; the contribution is the diagnosis (teacher degradation through view coupling) and the specific privileged-view retention target, backed by the ordinary-vs-privileged retention control (Table 3(b)), which is exactly the right control.

## Strengths

- Clear, well-motivated diagnosis: separates "deployment collapse" from "teacher competence collapse" via a dual-view evaluation with fixed PI and matched initial states (Table 9) — a protocol other iterative-learning papers should copy.
- Strong, recent, and reasonably adapted baselines (offline SDPO with TIS correction, OEL, EPD, offline GRPO, RFT), with shared cycle-1 corpora and matched update budgets.
- Unusually complete ablations: component on/off, selection-strategy control (Random/Bottom/Top), retention-view control (None/Ordinary/Privileged), ρ and λ sweeps, cost breakdown, and PI-sensitivity dynamics on a fixed probe.
- The plug-in design works on both OEL (fresh student responses) and SDPO-offline (cached responses), showing it is not tied to one response-sampling regime.
- Honest reporting of costs and of settings where things get worse (λ=1, ρ≥50%).

## Limitations

- Only two synthetic text benchmarks and three deployment cycles; scalability of the collapse-mitigation claim to longer horizons and realistic agent environments is unverified.
- One training seed per configuration; variance across training runs is unquantified.
- Gains require a usable PI channel: if summaries are poor or tasks lack extractable experience, both the teacher signal and the retention target degrade — not tested.
- Extra teacher-side forward/backward passes for retention add ~23% training-loop compute on top of the base method.
- Models limited to the Qwen3 family (4B/8B text, 4B VL); no evidence at other scales/families.
- The 22.5-point headline is an equal-weight average over six settings that mixes very different deltas (7.0 to 37.0 pts), and the strongest single-setting result (OOD, 8B) is also where baselines collapse hardest.

## Relevance

这篇论文正中你最高优先级的 "Self-Evolving Agents" 方向：它研究自进化 Agent 迭代自蒸馏中的性能崩塌及修复机制，同时 "PI 条件化教师能力随轮次退化、用 KL 正则保住监督质量" 的诊断与 "单训练种子、方差只算解码种子" 的可复现性问题也直接关联你关注的 LLM 训练稳定性与长期自主系统可靠性。若你在做自进化 Agent 或训练不稳定监测，这篇的 dual-view 评测协议和保留正则思路都值得借鉴。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the retention benefit hold beyond cycle 3, and does an adaptive or annealed λ (rather than a fixed 0.5–1.0) prevent the cycle-3 degradation seen at λ=1 over longer horizons?
- How sensitive is SGS to PI quality — if trajectory summaries are generated by a weaker model or degraded noisily, does privileged retention preserve a *bad* teacher just as faithfully as a good one?
- Would averaging over multiple independent training runs (not just decoding seeds) change the collapse curves for SDPO/OEL, and what fraction of the reported 22.5-point average gain survives run-to-run variance?
- Can privileged retention be applied to on-policy RL or experience-replay pipelines (e.g., GRPO-based agent training) where the "teacher" signal is a reward-conditioned reference rather than an explicit PI view?
- The Top-20-plus-residual-tail KL approximation trades fidelity for compute — at what vocabulary support size does retention stop preserving PI-conditioned competence?
