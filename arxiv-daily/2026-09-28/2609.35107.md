> **一句话总结：** MBZUAI 的 DoAtlas-2 用 GPT-5.6 驱动的多智能体闭环（提出机制问题→预注册研究设计→在以色列 HPP 队列上执行分析→把"支持/挑战/未决"结果写回因果证据状态并生成下一轮问题），70 天内在 8,724 个候选研究问题中系统评估了 2,031 个（约 1.587 万亿 token、62,677 个实验单元），把通路目录从 175 扩到 4,014 条，并在 259 条有队列证据的关系中判定 81 条支持、2 条挑战、117 条未决；发现脂肪–肝–血压–动脉硬化的长链通路（如 WHR–ALT–DBP–PWV 乘积 0.01073），在两个外部队列中 9/12 条路径复现，最终把发现的血管网络冻结成 63 个方程构成的可精确复算（误差 ~1e-14）的"可解释预测基座"，在 NHANES 60 个月死亡率上以 AUC 0.793 接近 GAM(0.798)/GBT(0.803)。

> **TL;DR:** DoAtlas-2 is an LLM-agent "AI scientist" that runs a closed discovery loop — hypothesis generation, prespecified causal analysis on the Human Phenotype Project (HPP) cohort, and evidence-state revision — across three research rounds that grew its pathway catalog from 175 to 4,014 questions (2,031 fully evaluated by GPT-5.6 agents, ~1.587T tokens, ~62,677 experimental units). Compared against a literature-derived evidence network of 149,383 candidate causal relations, HPP data adjudicated 259 relations into 81 supported / 2 challenged / 117 unresolved. The headline discovery (WHR–ALT–DBP–PWV adiposity–liver–blood-pressure–arterial-stiffness chain, serial product 0.01073, 95% CI [0.00758, 0.01389]) replicates in 9 of 12 path tests across two external cohorts (Holm-adjusted P < 0.0012). Freezing the 19-node vascular network into 63 explicit equations gives exact closed-form decomposition (recomputation error ≤1.42×10⁻¹⁴) and AUC 0.793 for 60-month NHANES mortality, within 0.001 of a GAM and 0.010 below a gradient-boosted tree (0.803) — interpretability at near-parity accuracy.

## 核心贡献

- **自进化因果发现闭环**：把科学状态形式化为 S_t = (E_t, Q_t, A_t)（因果证据状态、发现前沿、外部证据需求），每次状态转移 S_t → S_{t+1} 都由外部证据的选择记录（selection records）归因，可审计、可复现——区别于以往"一次性"假设生成系统。
- **规模化执行**：三轮研究将候选通路问题从 175 → 1,079 → 4,014 扩展（8,724 个候选 RQ，系统评估 2,031 个），由 GPT-5.6（xhigh reasoning）并行执行，共产出 88,731 个结果估计；首轮 1,079 个问题中 756 个获得筛选级统计支持。
- **真实人口证据裁决**：以 HPP 深度表型队列（13,072 人完成首访、6,300 万+字段级测量）为裁决环境，产生 2,169 条边级证据记录；259 条有队列证据的关系中 81 条获支持、2 条被挑战、117 条未决，其中 16 条支持关系与文献方向一致、49 条无文献先例。
- **代表性机制发现**：确认血压是连接代谢与血管表型的汇聚节点——WHR–ALT–DBP–PWV 通路乘积 0.01073（95% CI [0.00758, 0.01389]）；睡眠血氧通路 Waist–SpO₂–SBP–cIMT = −0.00252；证明"脂肪分布–炎症–血压"通路在联合校正 BMI 与吸烟后从 0.01218 衰减至 0.00222（Δ = −0.01016），即该联系主要反映整体肥胖与吸烟而非腰臀比本身。
- **可解释预测基座**：把发现的 19 节点/29 连接血管网络固化为 63 个（61 个可估计）显式方程，每个预测都是可逐项分解的闭式表达，独立复算 14,079 个预测的最大偏差仅 1.42×10⁻¹⁴；在 NHANES 死亡率迁移任务上用冻结的 HPP 关系参数达到 AUC 0.793 / IPCW Brier 0.0473。

## Key Contributions

- **A self-evolving causal foundation, not one-shot hypothesis generation.** Scientific state S_t = (E_t, Q_t, A_t) is revised only by attributable selection records from external evidence, so every claim, revision, and next question has an explicit causal-audit trail — the paper's central conceptual contribution over prior autonomous-science systems.
- **Scale with discipline.** Three research rounds expanded the candidate catalog 175 → 1,079 → 4,014 pathway questions (8,724 candidate RQs, 2,031 systematically evaluated, 88,731 result estimates), executed concurrently by GPT-5.6 agents in a 70-day run (~62,677 terminal experimental units, mean 5.26 h per RQ), with every evaluated question carrying a prespecified design, estimand, and complete denominator.
- **Population evidence as the arbiter.** HPP longitudinal deep phenotyping (13,072 first-visit completers; 63M+ field-level measurements including CGM, sleep, imaging) adjudicates 2,169 edge-level evidence records: of 259 relations tested, 81 supported, 2 challenged, 117 unresolved — and 49 supported relations have no counterpart in the 149,383-relation literature-derived network.
- **Representative mechanistic findings.** Blood pressure is the convergence node linking adiposity (WHR, hip, waist) via liver (ALT) and lipid (ApoB, TG) routes to arterial stiffness: WHR–ALT–DBP–PWV serial product 0.01073 (95% CI [0.00758, 0.01389]); sleep-oxygenation paths reach cIMT/PWV (Waist–SpO₂–SBP–cIMT = −0.00252); and the adiposity–inflammation–BP link collapses from 0.01218 to 0.00222 (Δ = −0.01016) under joint BMI+smoking adjustment.
- **A completely interpretable predictive foundation.** The discovered vascular network (19 nodes, 29 connections, 15 pathways) is frozen into 63 explicit equations; every prediction is a closed-form identity decomposable into node/interaction/branch contributions, verified by independent recomputation of 14,079 predictions to max discrepancy 1.42×10⁻¹⁴, and transfers to NHANES 60-month mortality at AUC 0.793 (IPCW Brier 0.0473) without refitting HPP relationship parameters.

## Methodology

The system composes three agent roles over a shared causal foundation. A **causal discovery agent** examines the contextualized causal evidence state (built from ~4.7M literature-derived records over 93,566 concepts and 149,383 candidate relations), proposes testable mechanism questions q = (m, c) with traceable parent results, and prioritizes the frontier by causal coherence, unresolved uncertainty, and discriminative value. **Research agents** turn each selected question into a full design D(q) = (population, exposure, outcome, temporal windows, causal estimand, identification conditions) grounded in HPP measurements, then an analysis agent executes prespecified units (serial/strictly-ordered, weakly-ordered, and cross-sectional design classes; reverse-direction, falsification, negative-control, and sensitivity analyses) with verification/refinement loops; all outcomes, including non-estimable states with named missing-evidence reasons, are reported. A **scientific learning agent** compares outcomes against prior evidence and sets record states (supported / challenged / context-dependent / unresolved) under explicit rules (interval vs. null, equivalence margin, Holm-style multiplicity, literature direction reference ρ_xy), then emits the four follow-up motifs: extend pathways, test alternatives, test boundaries, resolve gaps. All evaluations ran on GPT-5.6 with xhigh reasoning (~781.5M tokens per completed RQ; ~1.587T tokens over 70 days; ~62,677 terminal experimental units). Discovery analyses use serial path products β_π^D = Π β_k^D across HPP chains (±30 to ±365-day measurement windows, age/sex adjustment), with Holm correction, participant bootstrap, negative controls (e.g., left-minus-right PWV), within-person fixed-effects models, reverse-direction checks, off-path outcomes, and Cinelli–Hazlett omitted-variable robustness values. The predictive foundation composes the 63 estimated source equations (15 pathways, 22 source contexts, standardized/centered/quadratic/interaction terms) with per-task fitted weights; causal mediation of BMI–SpO₂–cIMT follows VanderWeele–Vansteelandt with exposure–mediator interaction; transfer evaluation freezes HPP relationship coefficients and fits only task-specific parameters on NHANES development data, scored by AUC and IPCW Brier on a temporal test split (1,587 participants, 110 deaths).

## Results & Highlights

- **Discovery-loop throughput and yield (the self-evolution evidence):** across the 70-day run, 2,031 of 8,724 candidate RQs were fully evaluated, producing ~62,677 terminal experimental units (60,646 computation-complete) and 88,731 result estimates at a mean of 781.5M GPT-5.6 tokens and 5.26 h per RQ (Table 1); the cumulative catalog grew 175 → 1,079 → 4,014 pathway questions, and 756 of the first 1,079 screened questions received screening-level statistical support.
- **Evidence-state adjudication vs. the literature network:** HPP contributed 2,169 edge-level evidence records; of 259 relations with population evidence, 81 were supported, 2 challenged, and 117 unresolved. Where the literature is divided (e.g., BMI→LDL: 7 increasing vs 4 decreasing reports; BMI→HDL: 1 vs 9), HPP provides a population direction. Of the 81 supported relations, only 16 match a directional literature reference and 49 have no literature counterpart at all (Table C.7) — the system's claimed novel-discovery surface.
- **Headline pathway:** the WHR–ALT–DBP–PWV chain reaches a serial product of 0.01073 (95% CI [0.00758, 0.01389]) under the ±365-day joint model, agreeing with the Round-2 primary design estimate 0.01099 (±180/±90/±30-day windows) and the screening association 0.0149 (n=7,452); the full-minus-ALT-bypass contrast is 0.008542 (95% CI [0.00290, 0.01419], Holm-adjusted P = 0.01207). The parallel Hip–ALT–DBP–PWV (0.01174) and Hip–ApoB–DBP–PWV (0.004717) branches survive Holm correction.
- **External replication:** in two independent cohorts — 912 adults (Fukuda 2014) and 879 adults with diabetes (Ning 2022, brachial-ankle PWV) — the metabolic-to-pressure routes replicate in 9 of 12 path tests at Holm-adjusted P < 0.0012 (e.g., standardized DBP–baPWV coefficient 0.29, 95% CI [0.22, 0.36], in the 879 cohort). Caveat: in the 879-cohort, neither the BMI–TG–DBP nor the TG–DBP RQ-level test replicated (both Holm-adjusted P = 1, Table D.24) — replication strength is cohort-dependent.
- **Confounder stress test of the adiposity–inflammation–BP link:** jointly adjusting BMI and smoking attenuates the WHR–GlycA–SBP–PWV serial estimate from 0.01218 to 0.00222 (Δ = −0.01016, 95% CI [−0.01537, −0.00494]) — the paper honestly reports that its own headline-style link is largely explained by overall adiposity and smoking. The weakest-edge robustness value of the WHR–ALT–DBP–PWV joint model is RV = 0.1081 (partial R² an unmeasured confounder would need on both predictor and outcome to null it).
- **Interpretable prediction at near-parity accuracy (NHANES mortality transfer):** with frozen HPP relationship parameters the foundation reaches AUC 0.793 (95% CI [0.730, 0.859]) and IPCW Brier 0.0473 (95% CI [0.0382, 0.0572]) on the NHANES temporal test (1,587 participants, 110 deaths), vs. a target-domain refit at 0.799, a generalized additive model at 0.798 (paired ΔAUC +0.001 [−0.002, 0.005]) and a gradient-boosted tree at 0.803 (−0.003 [−0.049, 0.033]).
- **Held-out HPP phenotype prediction (Table 2):** across six tasks on 10,401 held-out participants, R² is 0.378 for future PWV (NRMSE 0.959, test n=166) and 0.176 for future cIMT (n=172); queried tasks range 0.155 (ATT) to 0.346 (SpO₂); under 70% random input masking, five-seed mean R² falls to 0.248 (PWV) / 0.136 (cIMT).
- **Exact interpretability:** independent recomputation reproduces all 14,079 predictions with maximum discrepancy 1.42×10⁻¹⁴ (12,492 HPP and 1,587 NHANES predictions: 1.42×10⁻¹⁴ and 5.55×10⁻¹⁶) — the decomposition is an identity, not an approximation.
- **Closed-form causal mediation:** for a BMI contrast 22.92 → 28.40 kg/m² in 425 HPP participants, the BMI–SpO₂–cIMT total effect is 0.02306 mm (95% CI [0.00400, 0.04285]) = positive direct 0.02678 + negative mediated component −0.00372, partially offsetting (Eq. 11).

## Authors & Credibility

The paper is a technical report from the DoAtlas Team at MBZUAI (Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence). Co-corresponding authors **Imran Razzak** (machine learning) and **Eran Segal** (computational biology, Weizmann-affiliated co-author of the Human Phenotype Project itself — Reicher et al., Nature Methods 2025) give the project dual credibility: ML systems-building plus direct access to and deep knowledge of the HPP cohort that serves as the evidence arbiter. First authors Yulong Li, Rong Xia, and Yuxuan Zhang contributed equally. The author list spans clinicians, statisticians, and ML researchers (including Jionglong Su, Hagai Rossman), and the reference list shows command of both the autonomous-science literature (Ghaareeb et al. and Gotweis et al., Nature 2026; King et al., Science 2009) and the causal-inference canon (Pearl; Bareinboim; Cinelli–Hazlett; VanderWeele–Vansteelandt; Holm; Graf IPCW). The statistical care visible in the appendices (prespecified designs, negative controls, participant bootstrap, preregistration-style estimands) supports the team's credibility for this hybrid of agentic AI and epidemiology.

## Critical Assessment

- **"Foundation model" is doing heavy lifting.** The predictive foundation is 63 frozen linear equations, not a learned representation; on the only external benchmark with baselines (NHANES 60-month mortality), it sits *below* both the target-domain refit (0.799 vs 0.793 frozen) and a gradient-boosted tree (0.803, Δ −0.003, CI spanning −0.049 to 0.033). The honest claim is "interpretability at small accuracy cost," but the branding invites overreading — no representation learning, no emergent capability, no scaling behavior is demonstrated.
- **The discovery loop is evaluated internally.** There is no comparison against a human-expert pipeline or any other autonomous-science system on the same data, and no expert audit of question quality. The 70% screening pass rate (756/1,079) is computed on the *same cohort that generated the hypotheses* (HPP), so "support" mostly certifies that a large Israeli cohort contains the expected associations — a descriptive yield, not a validated discovery rate. The strongest external evidence for novelty is indirect: 49 supported relations lack literature counterparts, but absence from a literature network ≠ novelty worth pursuing.
- **Replication is genuinely mixed and the text softens it.** Table D.24 shows that in the 879-patient Chengdu diabetes cohort neither RQ-level test replicated (BMI–TG–DBP and TG–DBP, both Holm-adjusted P = 1); the "9 of 12 paths" headline pools the healthier 912-person Japanese cohort's 5 of 7 successes with the diabetes cohort's results. External cohorts are also small and cross-sectional, so temporal-order claims (the paper's key causal lever) cannot be checked there.
- **Effect sizes are tiny and fragile.** Headline serial products are ~0.004–0.011 in standardized units; the weakest-edge robustness value is 0.1081, meaning a single unmeasured confounder with partial R² ≈ 0.11 with both predictor and outcome could erase the WHR–ALT–DBP–PWV pathway. Most supported relations (and 33 of the 38 supported-but-unresolved ones) rest on cross-sectional or weakly ordered contexts where direction is not identified.
- **Cost and reproducibility are unexamined.** 1.587 trillion GPT-5.6 tokens for 2,031 questions (mean 781.5M tokens/RQ) is reported as a footprint but never justified: there is no ablation of what the LLM contributes beyond deterministic screening, no cost-per-validated-discovery, and the pipeline's dependence on a proprietary model makes the "self-evolution" non-replicable by others as-is.
- **Data bookkeeping quirks:** Figure 1's caption concedes participant totals (>720,000) are *not deduplicated* across the 771 resources, so the headline coverage number overstates unique individuals; primary population evidence comes overwhelmingly from one cohort (HPP, Israel), which the 48-country map visually amplifies.

## Strengths

- **Genuine closed-loop self-evolution with attribution:** every state transition S_t → S_{t+1} carries source records, complete denominators (8,724 candidates, all selections logged), and prespecified designs with estimands declared before outcomes — a preregistration-grade discipline rare in autonomous-science work.
- **Real external evidence, not LLM self-play:** hypotheses are adjudicated against a longitudinal deep-phenotyped cohort, replicated in two external datasets, and stress-tested with reverse-direction, within-person fixed-effects, negative-control (left-minus-right PWV), off-path, specification-grid (21 alternatives), and formal confounder-robustness analyses.
- **Honesty machinery:** the system's own taxonomy surfaces 117 unresolved and 2 challenged relations; the paper reports that joint BMI+smoking adjustment *guts* one of its headline links (0.01218 → 0.00222) and prints non-replicating external tests rather than hiding them.
- **Exact interpretability as an identity:** closed-form decompositions verified to 10⁻¹⁴ by independent recomputation, plus closed-form direct/mediated effects for temporally ordered questions — a concrete, checkable answer to the "black-box AI science" objection.
- **Engineering scale that is actually finished:** 62,677 executed experimental units, concurrent dispatch, mean 5.26 h end-to-end per question, and per-unit diagnostics — not a demo, a completed 70-day campaign.

## Limitations

- All population evidence is observational; causal identification rests on declared temporal windows and adjustment sets, with no interventional validation of any discovered mechanism.
- Single primary cohort (Israeli HPP; 13,072 deep-phenotyped participants) supplies most adjudications; the two external cohorts are small (912 / 879), cross-sectional, and partially non-replicating.
- Predictive performance is modest: held-out future PWV R² = 0.378 and cIMT R² = 0.176 on test sets of only 166–172 participants; queried-task R² 0.155–0.346; NHANES AUC slightly below a simple GBT.
- Enormous, non-reproducible compute footprint (1.587T proprietary-model tokens, 70 days) with no cost-benefit or LLM-contribution ablation; the loop cannot be rerun independently as published.
- No expert evaluation of the scientific value, priority, or actionability of the 4,014 generated questions; 1,824 of 8,724 candidates remain unevaluated, and 117/259 tested relations are unresolved.
- Reported effect sizes (standardized path products ~10⁻³–10⁻²) are far from clinical decision thresholds; robustness values (weakest edge RV = 0.1081) indicate sensitivity to moderate unmeasured confounding.

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would an ablation replacing the GPT-5.6 discovery agent with deterministic frontier prioritization (e.g., uncertainty + coherence scoring alone) produce the same 4,014-question catalog and 81 supported relations — i.e., where exactly does the LLM earn its 781.5M tokens per question?
- Can the 49 supported relations with no literature counterpart survive adversarial expert review, or are they mostly cohort-specific artifacts of the Israeli HPP population?
- The 879-patient diabetes cohort failed both RQ-level replications (Holm P = 1): is metabolic-to-pressure pathway structure population-heterogeneous, or were the external tests underpowered and mis-standardized?
- How does the evidence-state formalism (S_t with attributed transitions) transfer to domains where the arbiter is an experiment rather than a cohort — e.g., could the same loop close over wet-lab or simulation feedback?
- Does the frozen-equation predictive foundation scale beyond the vascular module (19 nodes) to the full 93,566-concept space, or does exact closed-form decomposition break down as the equation system grows?
- With a weakest-edge robustness value of 0.1081, what instrumental-variable or natural-experiment designs could the system itself propose to break the unmeasured-confounding symmetry?
