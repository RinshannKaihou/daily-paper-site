> **一句话总结：** 在经典阈值 τ=Ku 因 Ku≈32 而失效的 bfloat16、K=4096 区间，本文用约 7.99×10⁷ 条干净 per-row 残差拟合广义 Pareto 尾部、外推到每-GEMM 假阳性率 10⁻⁶，首次给出 checksum ABFT 的经验阈值 τ=3.1445×10⁻⁶；随后按比特位刻画检出能力——单翻指数位仅 76%（304/400，低于预注册的 90% 门槛，判为失败），低尾数位翻转 0/600 完全不可见，且检出率对收缩长度 K 不变（符号位 79/75/73/81/77% @ K=512..8192），证伪了研究自身的设计机制，并完整报告了四项撤稿。

> **TL;DR:** Checksum ABFT has no principled threshold at bfloat16 because the classical τ=Ku bound needs Ku≤0.01 while K=4096 gives Ku≈32 — which is why Ma et al. 2025 could only evaluate it at float32. This paper measures the clean per-row residual distribution (~7.99×10⁷ rows at bf16, K=4096 on a Tesla T4), fits a Generalized Pareto tail, and extrapolates τ=3.1445×10⁻⁶ at a stated 10⁻⁶ per-GEMM false-positive rate. Against this threshold the one-sided detector catches 76% (304/400) of single exponent-bit flips — failing its pre-registered 90% bar — and 0 of 600 low-mantissa flips (a hard floor, not a tuning problem). Detectability is governed by bit position, not contraction length: sign-flip detection is flat at 73–81% across a 16× K range because the clean noise floor falls as K^-0.4999 (theory: K^-1/2) and signal scales with the same normalizer, falsifying the study's own registered mechanism. Four failed pre-registered predictions and four retractions (including a comparison bug that silently discarded Inf residuals, understating EXPONENT_HIGH detection by up to 14 pp) are reported in full; conclusion: use checksum ABFT as a cheap first filter with known blind spots, not a complete SDC detector.

## 核心贡献

- **填补阈值空白**：首次在 bfloat16、K=4096（经典 τ=Ku 界未定义的区间，Ku≈32 ≫ 0.01）上对单侧 checksum ABFT 做经验标定：对约 7.99×10⁷ 条干净 per-row 残差做超阈值（peaks-over-threshold）GPD 拟合，外推到每-GEMM 假阳性率 10⁻⁶，得 τ=3.144535556×10⁻⁶，恰好落在两次观测的干净样本最大值（2.684525×10⁻⁶ @ n=500 与 3.245921×10⁻⁶ @ n=2000）之间——即该阈值不是任何一次采样运行的伪影。
- **检验而非假设换算前提**：per-row→per-GEMM 换算依赖行独立性；实测每-GEMM 超数计数均值 40.42、方差 39.93（Binomial(4096, 0.01) 期望 40.96 / 方差 40.55，方差比 0.985），行间平均 Pearson 相关仅 0.000126，支持换算在拟合阈值处成立。
- **按比特类别的检测地图**：bfloat16 五个比特类的检出率分层稳定——指数位最好（单翻 76–84%）、符号位次之（~81%）、高尾数位罕见（14/200 = 7%）、低尾数位恒为 0；并验证低尾数盲区不是注入器伪影（50 样本/类中 0 个被舍入抹掉，相对变化 0.00407–0.0313 严格为正）。
- **证伪自身机制**：本研究为演示"更长收缩更难检"而设计，但预注册证伪条件命中——单翻符号位在 K=512..8192 上检出 79/75/73/81/77%（均值 77%，8 pp 波动 ≈ n=100 二项噪声）；机制解释：干净噪声底按 K^-0.4999（锁定预测区间 [0.4, 0.6]，PASS）下降，信号与噪声底同速缩放，比值与 K 无关。
- **过程透明度本身是贡献**：全部头条数字均有预注册（含数值区间与证伪条件，commit 时间戳定序）；完整报告 4 项失败预测与 4 项撤稿——包括 scalar 归约造成的"结构性不可见"误判（0/1800 → 修正后符号位 155/200）和比较算子把 Inf 残差静默判假的 bug（10/15 个 EXPONENT_HIGH 格子被低估最多 14 pp），后者对所有浮点残差阈值检测器都有普适警示意义。

## Key Contributions

- **First empirical false-positive calibration for bf16 ABFT**: a GPD tail fit over ~7.99×10⁷ clean per-row residuals at bfloat16, K=4096, extrapolated to a 10⁻⁶ per-GEMM false-positive rate, yields τ=3.144535556×10⁻⁶ — sitting between the two observed clean sample maxima (2.684525×10⁻⁶ and 3.245921×10⁻⁶), in the regime where the classical τ=Ku bound is undefined (Ku≈32).
- **The independence assumption is tested, not asserted**: per-GEMM exceedance counts have mean 40.42 / variance 39.93 vs Binomial(4096, 0.01)'s 40.96 / 40.55 (variance ratio 0.985), and the mean pairwise per-row correlation is 0.000126 (SD 0.0225), supporting the per-row→per-GEMM conversion at the fitted threshold.
- **Bit-position governs detectability**: exponent flips caught 76–84%, sign ~81%, MANTISSA_HIGH 7% (14/200), MANTISSA_LOW 0/600 at τ and 0/1500 across the K-scaling study — verified as a real hard negative, not an injector artifact (relative deltas 0.00407–0.0313 strictly positive after the cast back to bf16).
- **K-invariance falsifies the study's design mechanism**: single sign flips detected at 79/75/73/81/77% across K=512..8192; the clean noise floor scales with exponent −0.4999 over the full 16× range (median 1.02719×10⁻⁶ → 2.57563×10⁻⁷), and signal scales against the same normalizer, so their ratio — what the detector tests — is K-independent.
- **Process transparency with teeth**: every headline number was pre-registered with numeric ranges and falsifiers; 4 of 5 K-scaling mechanism predictions failed (Q2–Q4 FAIL), the 90% detection bar was written into the README before any code and never moved, and four retractions are documented — most notably a comparison-operator bug that silently evaluated non-finite residuals as false, understating EXPONENT_HIGH detection in 10 of 15 cells by up to 14 percentage points.

## Methodology

**Detector (residual-v3).** One-sided Huang–Abraham checksum: for C=AB, form d=Ce and d′=A(Be); the per-row residual is rᵢ=|dᵢ−d′ᵢ|/sᵢ with sᵢ=|A|(|B|e), scored as r_max=maxᵢ rᵢ; all operands promoted to float64 and every accumulation done in float64. Two earlier formulations were discarded before this one: a signed grand-sum normalizer (max-to-median ratio 389, residual correlated 0.794 with its own denominator's reciprocal) and a scalar reduction of the length-M checksum vector — the latter destroyed detectability (0/1800) and produced a wrongly-"structural" explanation, retracted in Section 4.8. The chosen normalizer spans only 1.7169×10¹⁰–1.7193×10¹⁰ across n=2000 draws (max-to-median 1.0007) and its terms are nonnegative, so sign cancellation cannot shrink the denominator.

**Perturbation model (versioned spec, docs/SPEC-PERTURBATION-MODEL.md v1).** After C=AB completes, distinct (element, bit) pairs inside C are XOR-flipped — output tensor only; operands, accumulator intermediates, instruction encodings, and control flow are untouched. Bits are drawn from five bfloat16 classes: SIGN [15], EXPONENT_HIGH [14:11], EXPONENT_LOW [10:7], MANTISSA_HIGH [6:3], MANTISSA_LOW [2:0], with 1, 2, or 4 flips per sample. The paper is explicit that this model says nothing about silicon fault incidence.

**Platform.** Single Tesla T4 (sm75), torch 2.13.0+cu130, CUDA 13.0, driver 580.159.04; factors are uniform [−1,1] from a deterministic seed mixer (base seed × workload ID × shape index × sample index, no wall-clock input). cuBLAS was verified bitwise-deterministic across repeated launches in all 44 checked cases, justifying treating the clean floor as accumulation error rather than kernel-selection variance — a property of this exact hardware/software tuple, not of cuBLAS generally.

**Threshold and predicate.** 20,000 clean GEMMs at K=4096 yield ~8×10⁷ row observations. The 95th/99th/99.9th percentiles were frozen from an initial 500-GEMM pass (1.058×10⁻⁶ / 1.393×10⁻⁶ / 1.777×10⁻⁶); a GPD is fitted by MLE to exceedances above each. A pre-registered sensitivity rule requires that refits differing by more than 2× be reported as untrustworthy rather than averaged (the two evaluable fits agreed at 1.063×). The canonical threshold τ=3.144535556×10⁻⁶ comes from the 99th-percentile fit, extrapolated to the 1−2.44×10⁻¹⁰ quantile (= 10⁻⁶ per GEMM over M=4096 rows). Detection predicate: r_max > τ **or** r_max non-finite — the non-finite clause was added only after a pre-publication audit found the naive comparison silently scored diverged checksums (the loudest possible signal) as undetected.

**Pre-registration.** Predictions (numeric ranges + explicit falsifiers) were committed to the repository before the corresponding data existed, with commit history establishing ordering independently of the authors' word: the noise-floor and sensitivity sets (Q/P), the K-scaling mechanism predictions, the extreme-value set (F), and one later mechanism prediction about operand asymmetry.

## Results & Highlights

- **Calibrated threshold vs undefined classical bound**: τ = 3.144535556×10⁻⁶ at a stated 10⁻⁶ per-GEMM false-positive rate, from a GPD fit over ~7.99×10⁷ clean per-row residuals (bfloat16, K=4096, Tesla T4). It lands between the two observed clean maxima (2.684525×10⁻⁶ @ n=500; 3.245921×10⁻⁶ @ n=2000), i.e. no rule change would be needed at either sample size. In this regime the classical τ=Ku is undefined (Ku≈32 vs the required ≤0.01) — the exact reason Ma et al. 2025 restricted their production-node ABFT study to float32.
- **The headline negative**: pooled single exponent-bit-flip detection is 304/400 = **76%** against τ — below the pre-registered **90%** bar (75.25% = 301/400 and 80% = 320/400 at the two sample maxima). Stratified (Table 2, K=4096, n=200/cell): EXPONENT_HIGH 1-flip 168/200 (166/200 at τ, −1.00 pp); EXPONENT_LOW 1-flip 152/200 clean-max but **138/200 = 69% at τ (−7 pp)**; SIGN 1-flip 162/200; MANTISSA_HIGH 1-flip **14/200 = 7%**; MANTISSA_LOW **0/600** at τ and **0/1500** across the K-scaling study at per-K clean maxima.
- **K-invariance kills the design mechanism**: sign-flip detection is 79%, 75%, 73%, 81%, 77% at K = 512, 1024, 2048, 4096, 8192 (mean 77%, spread 8 pp ≈ binomial noise at n=100); the registered falsifier ("flat across a 16× range ⇒ mechanism wrong") fired. The clean median floor falls monotonically 1.02719×10⁻⁶ → 2.57563×10⁻⁷ with fitted exponent **−0.4999** (locked prediction [0.4, 0.6], the study's only PASS in set Q), matching the derived K^-1/2 ratio of √K accumulation error to linear-in-K normalizer.
- **Fit diagnostics and their fragility**: GPD shape ξ = −0.078 / −0.053 (CIs exclude zero ⇒ bounded upper tail); extrapolated thresholds 3.1445×10⁻⁶ and 3.3420×10⁻⁶ agree at 1.063× (bar: 2×), but the implied upper endpoints differ 1.23× (3.74×10⁻⁶ p99 vs 4.60×10⁻⁶ p99.9), so the hard ceiling is less well determined than the quantile. The p95 refit was NOT EVALUABLE: the harness stored only the top 64 residuals per GEMM (mean 205.3 exceedances at p95), truncating two-thirds of p95 exceedances.
- **Retracted result that flipped sign**: the naive comparison bug understated EXPONENT_HIGH detection in 10 of 15 cells by up to 14 pp (e.g. a fabricated non-monotonicity of 91% @ 2 flips vs 85% @ 4 flips at K=1024); with the corrected predicate, detection is monotone in flip count at every K (78/97/100 at K=512; 88/97/100 at 2048; 83/97/100 at 4096). Similarly, the retracted scalar-reduction evaluation showed 44.75% (179/400, exact binomial CI [39.8%, 49.8%]) pooled exponent detection; the per-row form gives 155/200 on the same workload and seeds.

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty — honestly scoped but narrow.** The detector is textbook Huang–Abraham ABFT; the paper explicitly disclaims novelty in applying it to transformer-shaped GEMMs. The real contribution is calibration in the one regime nobody had covered: Xie et al. 2025 fit statistical thresholds for LLM inference, but on INT32/INT8 datapaths where the checksum identity is exact, so no false-positive floor exists and their optimization criterion (avoid unnecessary *recovery*) is the opposite constraint; the numerical-bound line stops at float32 by its own stated condition. Filling precisely that hole is genuine, incremental-but-real novelty.

**Rigor of process vs narrowness of measurement.** The pre-registration discipline is exemplary — yet the measurement is a single point in a vast space: one GPU (Tesla T4, sm75), one driver/CUDA/torch tuple, one workload, uniform [−1,1] factors, M=N=4096. The authors themselves state the clean floor is "per-configuration by construction" and must not be transferred to other hardware — so the headline τ is a case study, not a constant a practitioner on H100/TPU bf16 can adopt. The claimed strongest external anchor is convergent validation: Ma et al. 2025 found checksum ABFT missing real corruption on 14 of 15 genuinely faulty production nodes at float32; this paper finds ~three-quarters of single exponent flips and zero low-mantissa flips at bfloat16 under controlled injection. Compatible conclusions from different corruption sources, precisions, and methods — but the agreement is qualitative, not a replication.

**Extreme extrapolation, partially self-inflicted.** The GPD fit reaches from ~10⁻² observed exceedance probability to the 2.44×10⁻¹⁰ quantile — several decades beyond the data. The p95 fit is unevaluable because the data collector stored only the top 64 residuals per GEMM, a data-collection limitation discovered *after* the method was locked; the fitted upper endpoints disagree by 1.23×. The paper flags all of this, but it means τ's far-tail guarantee is softer than "10⁻⁶ false positives" reads.

**Specific weaknesses worth naming.** (1) Uniform [−1,1] factors are numerically benign; real LLM weights/activations are heavy-tailed with outliers, and the residual distribution — hence the calibrated quantile — may shift substantially under realistic inputs; no real-model tensor is tested. (2) Flip cells are n=100 (K-scaling) or n=200, giving SEs of ~4 and ~3 pp, so fine deltas (e.g. the −1.00 pp EXPONENT_HIGH change at τ) are noise-level, and sub-percent detection probabilities are explicitly unresolved. (3) Two checksum reduction backends in the released implementation (float32 vs float64 accumulation) disagree, and the discrepancy is unresolved — a threshold calibrated on one backend should not be assumed to transfer. (4) The operand-asymmetry mechanism prediction (retraction #3) was untestable because the harness injects into the output tensor only, so the 0.5247 shared mantissa minimum remains unexplained — an honest but real gap in the mechanistic story. (5) The "residual-v3" history means earlier numbers from this project circulating in repo docs are void; anyone reusing the artifact must read docs/RESIDUAL.md and Section 4.8 first.

None of these undermine the headline claims — the 76%-vs-90% failure and the 0/600 blindness have ample margin over the noise — but they do bound how far the calibration generalizes.

## Strengths

- Fills a precisely-stated, practically-important hole: an empirically calibrated, false-positive-annotated threshold for bfloat16 ABFT where the classical Ku bound is undefined — exactly the precision regime modern LLM workloads run in.
- Negative results with teeth: the 90% detection bar was written into the README before any code existed, the detector failed it (76%), and the bar was never moved; three of five K-scaling mechanism predictions failed, including the one the study was designed around.
- The bit-class stratification produces an immediately actionable deployment map: trust checksum ABFT for exponent-class corruption, do not trust it for low-mantissa corruption at any flip multiplicity, because the threshold is bounded below by the false-positive requirement — this is a floor problem, not a tuning problem.
- The comparison-bug retraction generalizes beyond this paper: any threshold detector operating on floating-point residuals must handle non-finite values explicitly, since an Inf checksum divergence is the loudest signal and a naive `<` silently discards it.
- Maximal reproducibility: deterministic seeding, versioned perturbation spec, released data and predictions with commit-timestamp ordering, and retractions preserved as evidence rather than deleted.

## Limitations

- Single hardware/configuration point: one Tesla T4 (sm75), one driver/CUDA/torch tuple, bfloat16, M=N=4096; by the authors' own statement τ and the clean floor do not transfer to other GPUs or builds.
- Output-tensor-only perturbation model: no operand, accumulator-intermediate, instruction-level, or micro-architectural faults; no estimate of fault incidence in real silicon.
- Extreme tail extrapolation (observed ~10⁻² → 2.44×10⁻¹⁰ quantile) with the p95 refit unevaluable due to top-64-per-GEMM data truncation, and fitted upper endpoints spanning 1.23×.
- Statistical power: n=100/200 flip cells give SEs of ~4/3 pp; sub-percent detection probabilities and small cross-threshold deltas are unresolved.
- Benign input distribution (uniform [−1,1]); no realistic LLM weight/activation distributions, no real model tensors, no training-loop context.
- Unexplained shared mantissa minimum (0.5247) and an unresolved float32-vs-float64 checksum-backend discrepancy in the released implementation.

## Relevance

这篇论文正中你的核心方向「推理可靠性与 SDC 检测」与「极限性能优化下的可靠性」：它给出了 LLM 实际运行的 bfloat16 GEMM 上 checksum ABFT 的第一个经验假阳性阈值（τ=3.14×10⁻⁶ @ 10⁻⁶ FPR），并量化出按比特位分类的检测盲区（低尾数位 0/600 完全不可见），与 Ma et al. 2025 在真实生产节点上的阴性结论互相印证——可直接校准"ABFT 作为廉价初筛器"的部署预期，其 Inf 残差比较 bug 的教训对所有浮点阈值检测器都适用。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Do the calibrated quantile and the K^-1/2 floor scaling transfer to tensor-core accumulation paths (split-K, FP32 accumulate in TC) and to datacenter GPUs where cuBLAS kernel selection may not be launch-stable across repeated launches?
- How does the residual distribution — and hence τ — shift under realistic heavy-tailed LLM weight/activation tensors with outliers, rather than uniform [−1,1] factors?
- What complementary cheap detector can cover the MANTISSA_LOW blind spot (two-sided checksums, dithered rounding guards, periodic full-precision spot checks), and at what overhead relative to the O(MK+KN) one-sided check?
- Is the shared 0.5247 mantissa-cell minimum derivable analytically from the |A||B|e normalizer's distribution, or is it an artifact of the float64 promotion path?
- At what real-silicon incidence of low-mantissa-class corruption does the 0% detection cell flip the cost-benefit of deploying checksum ABFT as a first filter?
