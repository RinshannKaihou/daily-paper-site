A working template for AI scientists: autonomous agents ran a preregistered, auditable discovery loop over real biomedical data — a finished campaign, not a demo.

Critical edge 关键批评

The loop is graded by the same cohort that generated its hypotheses — no expert or system baseline — so “support” certifies expected associations, not validated discovery.

— Critical Assessment

01

Methodology 方法

DoAtlas-2 formalizes science as a state Sₜ = (Eₜ, Qₜ, Aₜ) — causal evidence, discovery frontier, external-evidence needs — where every transition is attributed to logged selection records. Three agent roles share the foundation: a discovery agent mines a literature network (~4.7M records, 93,566 concepts, 149,383 candidate relations) to propose mechanism questions; research + analysis agents compile each question into a prespecified design — population, exposure, outcome, causal estimand, identification conditions — and execute it on the Human Phenotype Project cohort (13,072 first-visit completers, 63M+ field-level measurements); a learning agent adjudicates outcomes into supported / challenged / context-dependent / unresolved states under Holm-style multiplicity rules, then emits follow-up motifs: extend pathways, test alternatives, test boundaries, resolve gaps.

Claims are stress-tested with serial path products across HPP chains, participant bootstrap, negative controls (left-minus-right PWV), within-person fixed effects, reverse-direction checks, and Cinelli–Hazlett robustness values. All 2,031 questions ran concurrently on GPT-5.6 (xhigh reasoning). The discovered 19-node vascular network is frozen into 63 explicit equations that recompose every prediction exactly (max error 1.42×10⁻¹⁴) and transfer to NHANES mortality with HPP coefficients held fixed.

03

Strengths 优势

  • +Attribution-grade discipline — complete denominators, prespecified designs and estimands declared before outcomes are seen.
  • +Real evidence, not LLM self-play — adjudicated on a longitudinal deep-phenotyped cohort, replicated in two external datasets, stressed with negative controls and robustness batteries.
  • +Honesty machinery — surfaces 117 unresolved and 2 challenged relations, and prints the BMI + smoking adjustment that guts one of its own headline links (0.01218 → 0.00222).
  • +Interpretability as an identity — closed-form decompositions verified by independent recomputation to 1.42×10⁻¹⁴.
  • +Finished engineering scale — 62,677 executed units, 5.26 h mean per question, a completed 70-day campaign rather than a demo.
04

Limitations 局限

  • −Observational only — causal identification rests on declared temporal windows and adjustment sets; no interventional validation of any mechanism.
  • −One primary cohort — Israeli HPP supplies most adjudications; external cohorts are small (912 / 879), cross-sectional, and the diabetes cohort failed both RQ-level replications (Holm P = 1).
  • −Modest prediction — held-out PWV R² 0.378, cIMT R² 0.176 on test sets of 166–172; NHANES AUC 0.793 sits below a simple GBT (0.803).
  • −Unjustified cost — 1.587T proprietary-model tokens with no ablation of what the LLM contributes; the loop cannot be rerun independently as published.
  • −Fragile, tiny effects — standardized path products ~10⁻³–10⁻⊃, weakest-edge robustness RV = 0.1081; far from clinical decision thresholds.
  • −Unaudited novelty — no expert review of question value; 1,824 candidates unevaluated; the 49 literature-absent “supports” may be cohort artifacts.
05

Questions for further exploration 延伸问题

  1. Would deterministic frontier prioritization — no LLM — reproduce the same 4,014-question catalog? Where exactly does the agent earn its 781.5M tokens per question?
  2. Can the 49 literature-absent supported relations survive adversarial expert review, or are they artifacts of the Israeli HPP population?
  3. The 879-patient diabetes cohort failed both RQ-level replications (Holm P = 1): population heterogeneity, or underpowered, mis-standardized external tests?
  4. Does the attributed state formalism Sₜ transfer to domains where the arbiter is a wet-lab or simulation experiment rather than a cohort?
  5. Does exact closed-form decomposition scale beyond the 19-node vascular module toward the full 93,566-concept space?
  6. With weakest-edge RV = 0.1081, what instrumental-variable or natural-experiment designs could the loop itself propose to break the confounding symmetry?
