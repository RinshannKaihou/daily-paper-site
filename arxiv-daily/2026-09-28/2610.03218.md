> **一句话总结：** Arm AI Research 系统性研究了 Microscaling（MX）块缩放格式下视觉模型的后训练量化（PTQ），定位出三类误差来源——非均匀浮点元素网格与"仅相邻两级取整"的错配、块缩放格式（UE8M0 幂次缩放）过粗、以及 ReLU 后非负激活浪费有符号码本负半区（约损失 1 bit 码空间）；据此提出 VisionMX：RangeRound（在非均匀网格的有界窗口内学习取整决策，配 AdaRound 式多阱正则与 FP4 候选窗口充分性证明 δ=4）+ AAC（把 LSQ+/HPTQ 仿射激活校正适配到 MX 块结构、逐块定标并折叠进后续层）+ 学习偏置与 MX 块粒度 QDrop，在 ImageNet-1K W4A4 下把 MXFP4-UE8M0 直接转换后崩溃的 EfficientViT-L2 从 2.54% 恢复到 81.89%（NVFP4 下 0.08%→83.82%），MobileNetV2 从 41.20% 提升到 65.58%（最强基线 QDrop 57.99%），并在 COCO 检测（较 RTN +5.64~9.59 AP）和 ADE20K 分割（+3.04~48.51 mIoU）上全面超越 RTN/AdaRound/QDrop。

> **TL;DR:** On ImageNet-1K W4A4 MXFP4 quantization, direct RTN conversion collapses compact convolutional/hybrid vision models (EfficientViT-L2: 2.54% top-1 vs 85.06% float; MobileNetV2: 41.20%), while ViTs stay within 5% relative loss. Arm's VisionMX combines RangeRound (learned rounding over a bounded window of the nonuniform FP4 grid) with Activation Affine Correction (per-block affine shift/scale that reuses the wasted negative codes of post-ReLU activations, folded into the next layer). Against the strongest baseline QDrop (57.99% on MobileNetV2 under MXFP4-UE8M0), VisionMX-BW reaches 65.58%, and it restores EfficientViT-L2 from 2.54% to 81.89% (83.82% under NVFP4 vs RTN's 0.08%). On COCO detection it beats RTN by 5.64–9.59 AP (FCOS R50-FPN: 8.10→34.03 AP); on ADE20K segmentation by 3.04–48.51 mIoU. The catch: the ablation shows most of the lift comes from AAC + bias + blockwise reconstruction, not RangeRound (+0.69 on MobileNetV2), and AdaRound/SpinQuant still win a few cells (DeepLabV3-R50 NVFP4, DeiT-B).

## 核心贡献

- **系统性敏感性分析：** 覆盖卷积（MobileNetV2、ResNet、ConvNeXt）、Transformer（DeiT、ViT、Swin、EfficientFormer、EfficientViT）与混合架构 × 分类/检测/分割/低光增强 4 类任务，发现敏感度高度依赖架构：视觉 Transformer 对 MX 量化最鲁棒（DeiT-B、Swin-T、ViT-S/16、ConvNeXt-Tiny 在权重/激活单侧 MXFP4-UE8M0 RTN 下相对损失 <5%），而 MobileNetV2 在权重单侧下相对精度损失高达 97.46%，EfficientViT-L2 两侧均接近完全崩溃（top-1 仅 0.10，float 85.06）。
- **三个误差来源的诊断：** (1) 块缩放格式本身影响巨大——ResNet-18 权重单侧 RTN 下 UE5M3（5 指数位+3 尾数位）比 UE8M0（纯 2 的幂）高 3.44 个点（65.87 vs 62.43），消融扫描遍历 UE0M8~UE1M7 证明指数位是主导因素；(2) 浮点元素网格非均匀，相邻格距 0.5/1/2 不等，传统"只在最近的下/上取整间二选一"不再保优——定理 4.1 证明在对角占优 Hessian 假设下 round-down/up 集合对 INT4 保优、对 FP4 未必（构造性反例：全网格最优 (2,4)ᵀ 重建损失 0.315 vs 上下取整最优 0.365），命题 5.1 进一步证明有界窗口 δ≥2·g_max（无缩放 E2M1 FP4 即 δ=4）即可恢复保优；(3) ReLU 后非负激活使有符号码本的负半区约一半码字永久闲置（ResNet-18 末层残差 ReLU 输出的码字占用直方图为证），等效损失约 1 bit 表示容量。
- **VisionMX 方法：** ① RangeRound——把 AdaRound 式学习取整推广到非均匀网格：每个归一化权重在 δ 宽度的候选窗口（含最近上下格点）内选码，用整流 sigmoid 门控 + 多阱正则（β 从 20 退火到 2）优化；② AAC（Activation Affine Correction）——把仿射重参数化（源自 LSQ+/HPTQ）适配到 MX 块结构：逐通道平移+缩放、逐块（考虑共享块缩放）校准，逆变换折叠进后续线性层，使负码区对非负分布重新可用；③ 学习偏置更新 + 把 QDrop 的逐元素丢弃改为 MX 块粒度丢弃。提供 Layerwise（AdaRound 式，VisionMX-LW）与 Blockwise（BRECQ 式，VisionMX-BW）两个变体。
- **大规模实验验证：** 在 MXFP4-UE8M0/UE5M3、NVFP4、MXFP8 四种格式、5 个随机种子下，VisionMX-BW 在几乎所有 CNN/混合模型-格式组合上取得最优，并在 ViT 上与 MR-GPTQ、SpinQuant 对比多数占优。

## Key Contributions

- **Architecture-resolved sensitivity study of MX PTQ:** across convolutional, transformer, and hybrid vision models on classification, detection, segmentation, and low-light enhancement, ViTs are robust (DeiT-B, Swin-T, ViT-S/16, ConvNeXt-Tiny stay within 5% relative loss under single-sided MXFP4-UE8M0 RTN), whereas MobileNetV2 loses 97.46% relative accuracy with weight-only quantization and EfficientViT-L2 collapses to near-zero accuracy (0.10 top-1 from 85.06 float) on either side.
- **A mechanistic diagnosis of three error sources:** (1) the block-scale format matters — UE5M3 beats the power-of-two-only UE8M0 by 3.44 pts on weight-only ResNet-18 RTN (65.87 vs 62.43), with a full UE0M8–UE1M7 sweep isolating exponent bits as the dominant factor; (2) the FP element grid is nonuniform, so restricting rounding to the two nearest levels can be provably suboptimal — Theorem 4.1 shows the round-up/down set preserves the quadratic-loss optimum for INT4 but not always for FP4 (worked example: full-grid optimum loses 0.315 vs 0.365), and Proposition 5.1 proves a bounded window with δ ≥ 2·g_max (δ=4 for unscaled E2M1) suffices; (3) post-ReLU nonnegative activations leave roughly half of the signed-grid codes permanently unused (code-occupancy histogram of ResNet-18), an effective loss of ~1 bit of code space.
- **VisionMX, an MX-specific PTQ pipeline:** RangeRound (AdaRound-style learned rounding over a bounded window of nonuniform grid levels, with a multi-well regularizer annealed β=20→2) + Activation Affine Correction (adapts LSQ+/HPTQ affine reparameterization to the MX block structure, calibrated block-by-block against shared scales and folded into the following layer) + learned bias updates + a block-granular QDrop. Two variants: VisionMX-LW (layerwise) and VisionMX-BW (blockwise reconstruction).
- **Comprehensive evaluation:** on MXFP4-UE8M0/UE5M3, NVFP4, and MXFP8 with 5 seeds, VisionMX-BW is best in nearly all CNN/hybrid model-format pairs and mostly beats MR-GPTQ and SpinQuant on ViTs.

## Methodology

The paper proceeds in two stages: an empirical/theoretical analysis, then a method designed directly from the diagnosis.

**Analysis.** (1) Architecture behavior: weight-only vs activation-only MXFP4-UE8M0 RTN is measured separately for 15+ models across 4 tasks, exposing which side dominates degradation per architecture (weight-only dominates for MobileNetV2 and DeepLabV3-MobileNetV3; activation-only dominates for the low-light models). (2) Weight rounding: using a second-order Taylor expansion of the task loss with a Hessian diagonal-dominance assumption (factor-two strengthened), Theorem 4.1 shows the rounding-up-down set contains the full-grid optimum for INT4, but an explicit 2-weight FP4 example (grid spacings 0.5/1 under block scaling) shows the FP4 optimum can lie strictly outside it — motivating optimization over a *bounded window* rather than the full grid; Proposition 5.1 (with Corollary C.1 for fixed-scales MX) proves a window of half-width δ ≥ 2·g_max contains every full-grid minimizer, δ=4 sufficing for unscaled E2M1 FP4. Validated by exhaustive enumeration on a 2-weight example plus a correlation sweep over ρ∈[0,0.5]. (3) Activations: comparing FP4 element-code occupancy histograms of the pre- and post-ReLU distributions shows the nonnegative output cannot use negative signed codes regardless of the shared block scale, since scaling changes range but not code *utilization* — motivating an affine transform that recenters part of the distribution onto the negative codes.

**Method.** VisionMX quantizes weights and activations of all linear ops (including input/output layers) to 4-bit MX formats. RangeRound normalizes each weight by its deployed block scale, then learns a per-weight logit via a rectified sigmoid (γ=-0.10, ζ=1.10) that interpolates between any two grid endpoints within the δ-window, with AdaRound's multi-well regularizer R_round = Σ(1-|2u_i-1|^β) annealed β: 20→2 driving discrete convergence; the objective is asymmetric block-wise reconstruction with teacher inputs from the float prefix and student inputs from the already-quantized prefix. AAC stores per-channel scale α and shift γ per quantized layer, applied before dynamic block-wise activation quantization, with parameters selected by minimizing reconstruction error after mapping back to the original domain, and W' = W·diag(α)⁻¹, b' = b + Wγ folded for deployment. QDrop is adapted to sample one Bernoulli(0.5) bypass decision per MX block rather than per element. Two variants: VisionMX-LW optimizes layer-wise (AdaRound-style); VisionMX-BW optimizes complete executable blocks (BRECQ-style) with bias updates inside each block. Calibration: 2,048 images for ImageNet-1K (class round-robin), 256 for COCO, 256 crops for LOLv2; 10k–20k iterations per layer/block on a single NVIDIA H100; results averaged over 5 seeds.

## Results & Highlights

- **ImageNet-1K W4A4 classification, MXFP4-UE8M0:** EfficientViT-L2 (float 85.06) collapses to 2.54% top-1 under RTN and QDrop recovers only to 4.80%, while VisionMX-BW reaches 81.89% (+79.35 pts over RTN; under NVFP4 the gap is even larger: RTN 0.08% vs VisionMX-BW 83.82%). MobileNetV2 (float 72.15): RTN 41.20 → VisionMX-BW 65.58 vs strongest baseline QDrop 57.99 (+7.59). ResNet-18: VisionMX-BW 68.72 vs RTN 65.73 under UE8M0, and 68.23 vs RTN 62.28 under UE5M3 (+5.95). Gains reach ~10 pts in several settings but shrink for already-robust models (ConvNeXt-Tiny: 80.28 vs QDrop 79.75, +0.53).
- **ViT comparison (joint W4A4, MXFP4-UE8M0):** VisionMX-BW is the best reported method on DeiT-T (67.37±0.13 vs MR-GPTQ 66.50 / SpinQuant 66.40) and Swin-T (79.45±0.06 vs SpinQuant 78.29 / MR-GPTQ 78.12), and on ViT-S/16 (78.41±0.20 vs SpinQuant 77.90), but loses to SpinQuant on DeiT-B (80.30±0.05 vs 80.49).
- **COCO object detection (W4A4, MXFP4):** FCOS R50-FPN rises from 8.10 AP (RTN, UE8M0) to 34.03 AP (VisionMX-BW), above the 29.10 float reference loss-adjusted baselines AdaRound 31.96 / QDrop 31.90; Faster R-CNN R50-FPN: RTN 5.45 → 33.15; RetinaNet R50-FPN: RTN 10.60 → 32.19 (float 28.80). The paper summarizes VisionMX-BW improving detection by 5.64–9.59 AP and segmentation by 3.04–48.51 mIoU relative to RTN.
- **ADE20K segmentation (W4A4, MXFP4):** DeepLabV3-MobileNetV1 (float 60.00 mIoU) goes from RTN 20.46 (UE8M0) / 11.60 (UE5M3) to 53.31 / 56.35 respectively — up to +44.75 mIoU over RTN; DeepLabV3-R50: 59.72 → 67.13 under UE8M0. However, under NVFP4 AdaRound (67.84) beats both VisionMX variants (64.35 / 64.46) on DeepLabV3-R50.
- **Ablation (Table 4, blockwise, MobileNetV2):** starting from reconstruction-only 52.73, learned bias alone gives 64.54, AAC alone 64.84, RangeRound alone only 54.91; the full combination reaches 65.53 (+12.80 total) — AAC contributes +8.11 on top of RR+bias while RangeRound adds just +0.69 over bias+AAC. In the layerwise ablation without QDrop (Table 12), bias+AAC *without* RangeRound (65.46) actually beats the full method (65.11) on MobileNetV2, though RR+full is best on CaiT-XXS24 (66.21).
- **MXFP8 appendix results:** RTN already retains near-float accuracy for most models (e.g., ResNet-18 69.16 vs float 69.76), but EfficientViT-L2 remains format-sensitive (56.44 under UE5M3, 0.41 under UE8M0); VisionMX-BW restores it to 84.93/84.94 vs 85.06 float.

## Authors & Credibility

The five authors — Elad Dror Cohen, Ofir Gordon, Lior Dikstein, Idan Achituve, and corresponding author Hai Victor Habi — are all with Arm AI Research (AAIR), Arm Ltd. This is a strong fit: Arm is a founding member of the OCP Microscaling consortium and builds the NPUs/CPUs where MX formats will actually ship, so the team has both the motivation and the format-level expertise (their own HPTQ, EPTQ, and LATMr works on PTQ are cited and reused). The group has a consistent publication record in efficient-quantization research (HPTQ at ECCV 2021, EPTQ at ECCV 2024, LATMr at ICML 2026), which lends credibility to the implementation quality of the AdaRound/QDrop baselines, re-integrated with block-scaled quantizers. The experimental rigor (5-seed means with standard deviations, exhaustive enumeration to validate theory, detailed hyperparameters in appendices) is above average for PTQ papers. No code release is mentioned in the paper.

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty is incremental at the component level; the real contribution is the diagnosis.** RangeRound is AdaRound's learned rounding re-targeted from uniform INT grids to a bounded window over a nonuniform FP grid (prior work FAAR already did adaptive rounding for NVFP4), and AAC explicitly adapts LSQ+/HPTQ affine activation correction to the MX block structure. The genuinely new material is the empirical/theoretical characterization of *why* MX breaks vision models: the round-up/down suboptimality on nonuniform grids (Thm 4.1 + Prop 5.1, with proofs and exhaustive 2-weight enumeration) and the code-occupancy argument for post-ReLU activations (Fig 1c) are clean and convincing.

**The headline method's lift is mostly not RangeRound.** The paper's own ablation undercuts its second contribution: on MobileNetV2 (Table 4), bias+AAC gives 64.84 and adding RangeRound yields only 65.53 (+0.69), while Table 12 shows bias+AAC *without* RangeRound (65.46) beating the full three-component method (65.11) in the layerwise setting. The abstract leads with the novel optimizer, but the load-bearing components are the re-purposed affine correction, learned bias, blockwise reconstruction, and block-granular QDrop — i.e., mostly known machinery. The text does not acknowledge this hierarchy explicitly.

**Win/loss record is not uniform, and the prose is quieter about losses than the tables.** On DeepLabV3-R50 under NVFP4, AdaRound (67.84 mIoU) clearly beats both VisionMX variants (64.35/64.46); AdaRound also has the best PSNR on RetinexFormer under UE8M0 (22.59 vs 22.45); SpinQuant wins DeiT-B. To the authors' credit, all losing cells are visible in the tables with standard deviations — but the narrative ("outperforms established PTQ baselines ... in most evaluated settings") elides them.

**Baselines are solid but dated, and MX-native competitors are absent.** RTN/AdaRound/QDrop date from 2019–2021 (though fairly re-implemented for block-scaled formats with matched calibration), and MR-GPTQ/SpinQuant appear only for the ViT classification appendix. The related work discusses MX-specific efforts (MicroScaleQuant, MR-Qvits, TensorJet) that are not empirically compared, so "best PTQ method for MX vision models" is established against an integer-era baseline suite rather than the MX-specialized frontier.

**Cost and reproducibility.** Blockwise reconstruction re-optimizes each executable block for 10k–20k steps on an H100 with 2,048 ImageNet calibration images — a generous budget compared to the 128–1,024 images typical of AdaRound/QDrop-era studies, and a real deployment cost for large models. No code release is stated, no end-to-end latency/memory/energy numbers on actual MX hardware are reported (the hardware motivation is asserted, never measured), and the theory leans on a factor-two-strengthened diagonal-dominance Hessian assumption whose validity in deep networks is heuristic.

## Strengths

- Unusually broad empirical coverage: 15+ models spanning CNN/Transformer/hybrid families, 4 tasks (classification, detection, segmentation, low-light enhancement), 4 MX formats, all with 5-seed mean±std reporting.
- The error-source analysis is mechanistic and actionable: the scale-format sweep (UE0M8–UE1M7), the FP4 rounding counterexample with exhaustive enumeration and a correlation sweep, and the ReLU code-occupancy histogram each map directly onto one method component.
- Real theory with proofs (Theorem 4.1, Proposition 5.1, Corollary C.1) that actually constrains the algorithm design — the δ=4 window for E2M1 is derived, not tuned arbitrarily.
- Spectacular recoveries where it matters: EfficientViT-L2 from 2.54%→81.89% (UE8M0) and 0.08%→83.82% (NVFP4) top-1 — nearly the full float accuracy restored at W4A4.
- Honest tables: per-cell standard deviations, losing cells (vs AdaRound, SpinQuant) printed rather than hidden.

## Limitations

- RangeRound — the paper's nominally novel optimizer — contributes marginally (+0.69 pts on MobileNetV2 over bias+AAC) and can even hurt in the layerwise setting (65.11 full vs 65.46 without RR); the practical recipe is dominated by affine correction + bias + blockwise reconstruction.
- Best results require BRECQ-style blockwise reconstruction: 10k–20k optimization steps per block on an H100 with 2,048 calibration images — costly at scale and not compared against cheaper reconstruction settings.
- Only classification results are reported against GPTQ/SpinQuant-style methods, and no MX-specialized PTQ competitor (MicroScaleQuant, MR-Qvits, TensorJet) is compared despite being cited.
- Purely an accuracy study: no hardware-measured latency, memory, or energy on MX-capable hardware; AAC's per-channel shift/scale parameters add a small storage/compute overhead that is asserted negligible but not quantified in deployment terms.
- No code release mentioned; the theory's factor-two diagonal-dominance and negligible-first-order assumptions are unverified for the deep networks the method is applied to.

## Relevance

这篇论文正中你「极端性能优化下的可靠性」关注点：它细致刻画了 MX 低精度块缩放格式在视觉模型上的量化误差来源——非均匀浮点网格上的取整错配、块缩放格式（纯 2 的幂缩放）的表达力不足、以及非负激活对有符号码本负半区的系统性浪费——本质上是低精度数值表示与真实张量分布之间的精度/表示错配问题，而非单纯的速度收益；对评估 4-bit MX 推理在哪些架构上可信（ViT 稳、紧凑卷积与 EfficientViT 会崩溃）很有参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the wasted-negative-codes diagnosis (and AAC) transfer to LLM blocks, where activations after SwiGLU/GELU are signed but asymmetric — and does AAC interact badly with KV-cache quantization since the folded shift changes downstream tensor statistics?
- RangeRound adds +0.69 pts at best; can the bounded-window idea be justified on *uniform* grids too (where Theorem 4.1 says up-down suffices), or is the FP4 window the only regime where learned-window rounding pays for its optimization cost?
- How do these results change on real MX hardware (OCP-specified UE8M0 pipelines, e.g., future NPUs) where the UE5M3/UE5M2 scale formats the analysis favors may simply not be supported — is UE8M0's 2.54% collapse on EfficientViT-L2 an argument against hardware mandating power-of-two scales?
- The 97.46% weight-only collapse of MobileNetV2 is attributed to misalignment between small conv weights and the nonuniform grid; is there a closed-form characterization of which architectures' weight distributions fall into the bad cells (e.g., via the H_w spectrum), enabling pre-deployment sensitivity prediction without quantizing?
- Can SpinQuant-style rotations compose with VisionMX on the models where it loses (DeiT-B), and does block-granular QDrop remain necessary when rotations already whiten activations?
