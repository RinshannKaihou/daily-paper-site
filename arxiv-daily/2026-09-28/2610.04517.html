Critical edge

“Success” is graded by EvoCast’s own canonical pipeline — the same yardstick the system is architected around. And the end-to-end claim rests on just three forecasting tasks, one of which actually regresses on MAE.

Also unresolved: no foundation-model ablation (every call is MiniMax-M3), no efficiency accounting of the evolved architectures, and a suspicious table row — AutoResearch printed as “86.7% (28/30)” when 28/30 = 93.3%.

01 · Method

One frozen protocol, two kinds of power

Distilled from the paper’s Methodology — the loop, the boundary, and the ledger behind every claim. Full detail: markdown source.

One forecasting task, one frozen protocol: P = (D, y, L, H, mode, B) never changes during a run, so baseline selection, diagnosis, candidate evaluation and promotion all compare on a single stable surface. Around it, EvoCast splits who may think from who may decide.

Four cognitive roles generate hypotheses and edit code — but only inside a bounded model-side source region; dataset, evaluator, metric computation, training budget and promotion logic are untouchable. Four deterministic program authorities validate contracts, execute the sandbox, check causal validity, and adjudicate promotion with a fixed multi-seed rule — no accepted result can bypass it.

Each of 20 formal rounds runs five stages: characterize the dataset → establish one formal baseline → ablate its mechanisms (Δ(a) = m(a;P) − m(b*;P)) → plan and implement one direction → adjudicate. Every outcome lands in exactly one of four states (below), and negatives are fed back as evidence for the next round’s planning rather than discarded. After the budget, a narrator emits an auditable report with the current-best model, source, and full resource usage.

Setup — MiniMax-M3 backbone ($0.30/M in, $0.06/M out) · 1× RTX 3080 Ti (12 GB) · PyTorch 2.7.1 / CUDA 12.8 · 20 rounds · ≤3 repairs/round · code: github.com/1860-x/EvoCast

04 · Verdict

Where it holds, where it cracks

Strengths

  • The right trust architecture. Deterministic gates on source boundaries, evaluation and promotion directly target protocol contamination; the dedicated unstable state treats seed variance with unusual honesty.
  • Negatives are first-class evidence. Rejected, unstable and failed rounds feed the next plan — the loop learns from what did not work instead of hiding it.
  • Rare cost transparency. Calls, tokens, dollars and wall-clock sit beside quality in every table — and EvoCast wins on both axes against most baselines.
  • Unusually reconstructable. Formal protocol, prompts (App. B), budget accounting (App. C) and released code make the whole system auditable end to end.

Limitations

  • One domain. Everything is forecasting-architecture evolution; transfer to other ML or science domains is asserted by analogy, not shown.
  • Thin end-to-end base. Three real tasks, one LLM backbone, and no seed variance reported for the RQ2 headline tables — the Melbourne/Steel margins carry unknown error bars.
  • Accuracy-only comparison. Evolved architectures report no parameter count, FLOPs or latency; part of the gain may simply be bought capacity a bigger baseline would also enjoy.
  • Self-referential metrics, drifting ground. Success is defined by the system’s own validation pipeline, and MiniMax-M3 pricing/behavior makes every dollar figure and success rate time-sensitive.
05 · Open questions

What to probe next

Q1

Does cognition–authority separation transfer beyond forecasting — to tabular/vision ML engineering or algorithm discovery, where the “editable model-side region” is much harder to delineate?

Q2

How does the loop behave with a weaker or stronger foundation model? Is there a minimum capability below which the authority gates merely reject everything?

Q3

A3 (no grounded planning) achieved the best valid-round ratio, 20/20 — what exactly makes grounded planning reduce execution reliability, and is there a planning-depth/validity Pareto frontier?

Q4

Which evidence-bundle components drive the RQ3 differences — valid negatives, unstable gains, or failure memory? The paper removes them jointly; a finer-grained ablation is missing.

Q5

Do the evolved module insertions survive out-of-distribution tests, longer horizons, or parameter/FLOPs-matched baselines — i.e., how much of the gain is added capacity rather than mechanism?
