01

Method — evidence-only coupling of Bayesian branches

Distilled · ~170 words

Each of K = 3 branches keeps a Bayesian posterior over a shared finite Universal Principle Space (inherited from PiEvo). Tested hypothesis–score pairs become local evidence; candidate records — hypothesis, observation, source context, metadata — publish to an append-only shared pool. Branches never see each other's posteriors or search histories.

The coordination core runs Collect–Merge–Score–Route–Inject each round. Scoring values a record by expected entropy reduction net of transfer cost (Eq. 1), evaluated exactly by cloning the target posterior — no sampling. Routing admits only records clearing a calibrated gate V̂ − r > η, ranks by certified value per unit cost, and caps quota at B=3/round with a token-Jaccard redundancy limit of 0.3. Injection multiplies the target likelihood by a discount α = source accuracy × context match × replication status — a generalized-Bayes update where local evidence counts fully, imports fractionally.

Four theorems cover posterior concentration under discounted imports, negative-transfer control, parallel coverage ∝ K, and discovery-time hazard tails exp(−tΣλj). The harness regime adds sealed graders and server-side budget enforcement; self-reported numbers are discarded.

04

Trade-offs — strengths vs limitations

Side by side

Strengths

  • Clean conceptual contribution: an evidence-only interface (records cross branches, posteriors never do) with discount α as a principled knob between full pooling and full isolation.
  • Rare theory–experiment discipline: Appendix C maps each of four theorems to a measurable signature — and the signatures hold.
  • Broad, strong baselines: 8 systems including InternAgent-1.5 and EvoScientist, two backbones, fixed query budget — not strawmen.
  • Exemplary cost accounting & honesty: near-parity token premium disclosed, Welch/Bonferroni failures reported, sealed graders, self-reported numbers discarded.
  • Thorough ablations that isolate why the system wins, pairing SQ with import volume per hyperparameter.

Limitations

  • Thin statistical power: 3 seeds/cell; after Bonferroni only 2 of 12 task-backbone comparisons vs PiEvo stay significant.
  • Mechanism idle on half the suite: transfer concentrates on MBO/AMP; on saturated NHO the win is convergence speed (1.5× faster), not quality.
  • Conditional guarantee: gate radii are heuristic hand-set constants under GP misspecification; sensitivity to cost constants only partially explored.
  • Fixed K=3: landscape-dependent returns (inverted-U on MBO, flat on AMP); no adaptive branch-count rule.
  • Latency win, not cost win: 0.97× SQ-per-token; 1.55× billable tokens in the harness regime.
  • Closed-world only: surrogate validators, no wet-lab validation; code promised but not released at v1.
05

Questions for further exploration

Open threads
  1. Under large genuine context shift between branches, calibrated radii reduce to heuristic constants — how far does negative-transfer control stretch before the gate must be learned rather than set?
  2. Can K and the per-branch budget be chosen adaptively online, given the landscape-dependent inverted-U response (MBO peaks at K=3, flat AMP, TMC plateau)?
  3. Does evidence transfer stay beneficial when branches run heterogeneous backbone LLMs, with source accuracy ρ and context match s estimated across models of different reliability?
  4. The 0.97× token conversion ratio means quality-per-token parity — does that hold at larger budgets, or with a cheap verifier model replacing exact enumeration of V̂?
  5. How does the framework behave when replication υ is expensive or impossible (real experiments, not surrogates) — and does the uplift statistic become causal with branch-level randomization of import availability?
