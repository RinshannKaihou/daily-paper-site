06 Methodology distilled

Five agents form a closed evidence loop (§01). The calculation-design agent is Qwen3.5-27B fine-tuned with SFT (MatRL tool-use episodes + execution-checked physics trajectories), then GRPO in a bounded environment of small electron-hole and Hubbard calculations — terminal reward from numerical accuracy and evidential support, a compute-cost penalty on successful episodes, 4 rollouts per task. Hypothesis / execution / critique / synthesis run on Claude Opus 4.6, over an 18-tool registry: Quantum ESPRESSO 7.4.1 (PBE + DFT+U+J on Ru 4d, U = 3.6 eV, J = 0.4 eV), BerkeleyGW 4.0 (Hybertsen–Louie GW with 440-band sums; BSE on Tamm–Dancoff), GP-UCB Bayesian tuning, Paperclip literature tools.

For α-RuCl₃ — monoclinic, zigzag antiferromagnetic order — GW corrections are applied as a fixed two-branch scissors; the principal result uses a 64-point spinor grid, convergence probed at 96. Humans set direction and performed all measurement: an hBN-encapsulated, graphene-contacted junction, differential-reflection and photocurrent spectroscopy at 8 K (Montana cryostat, Bruker IFS 66v/S FTIR).

08 Strengths

  • Genuine closed evidence loop — hypotheses must survive controlled tests, not free-form LLM generation.
  • 2 of 3 routes rejected with physical reasons — a documented negative-result trail, rare in agent papers.
  • Deterministic numerics (QE, BerkeleyGW) + GP-UCB anchor reasoning; the critique agent distinguishes numerical failure from physical disproof.
  • Outcome-based reward design (accuracy + evidential support, compute penalty) avoids per-step human annotation.
  • Transparent cost accounting and unusually candid limitations in Methods.

09 Limitations

  • Single material — no evidence the pipeline or GRPO-trained policy generalizes to a second system.
  • No agent-evaluation numbers reported — SFT+GRPO vs SFT-only vs Opus comparisons described but never quantified.
  • SOC ablation mixes grid + treatment changes; the ~3,000× figure is not a clean causal estimate.
  • Nothing released — no code, data, prompts, or checkpoints at publication time.
  • Theory–experiment agreement never quantified with residuals; the α′ dissociation story is imported from WSe₂ without direct evidence.

  2. Can SOC be isolated at matched k-grids and treatments, turning 0.093 vs 3×10⁻⁵ into a clean ablation?
  3. Will the polarization-rotation prediction (α′ enhanced over α) be confirmed experimentally?
  4. Does the pipeline transfer end-to-end to a second material — is the design policy material-agnostic?
  5. Can quantitative residuals and explicit binding energies turn peak assignment into a statistical test rather than a visual match?
