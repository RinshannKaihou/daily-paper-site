> **一句话总结：** 浙大+港大团队提出证据治理型发现架构 EPOCH,用任务契约、只增式证据记忆、主动证伪、三值声明感知准入和搜索循环外独立重放五个机制约束 AI 研究代理:在 AlgoTune 12 任务上平均归一化分 0.65 vs 最强基线 AdaEvolve 0.53(+22.6%),在 Math14 上以 0.57 居首,并产出 54 次比较的 median-of-23 选择过程(改进 1969 年 Hadian–Sobel 55 次经典界)、AES 可逆电路 T 门数 10,746→9,234(−14.1%)等十项可验证发现。

> **TL;DR:** EPOCH reframes AI research agents as evidence-governed discovery systems: each task runs under an explicit contract (data views, evaluators, budget, claim scope), a typed append-only memory keeps both successes and failures, proposals are actively falsified, admission is a three-valued claim-aware gate, and endpoints are sealed by independent replay outside the search loop. On a frozen 12-task AlgoTune subset it reaches 0.65 mean normalized score vs 0.53 for the strongest baseline AdaEvolve (+22.60% relative; AI-Scientist v1 0.52, OpenEvolve 0.50), leads Math14 at 0.57 (OpenEvolve 0.56, only +0.60%), and on six AgentHPOBench tasks scores 77.48 normalized mean where removing memory/proof-admission/falsifier/phase-control costs 13.17/37.28/41.75/55.59 points. Its ten case studies yield verifiable artifacts: a 54-comparison median-of-23 procedure (beating the 55-comparison Hadian–Sobel 1969 bound), a 1,512-T-gate (14.10%) reduction for AES reversible circuits, 11 improved tensor border-rank upper bounds incl. R(T_5w4414) ≤ 30 with 45,242 exactly replayed coordinates, a Lean-checked hypercube determinant inequality with equality characterization, and a nine-element matroid counterexample refuting the proposed H_β coefficient ordering.

## 核心贡献

- **证据治理发现架构**:提出把发现过程组织为约束搜索——任务契约 C = (D_pub, D_term, E, B, A, S) 固定公开/终端数据视图、评估器、资源预算、可编辑工件空间和允许的声明范围;只增式类型化记忆同时保留成功与失败轨迹(含失败归因);challenge 阶段针对声明机制主动证伪;准入是三值门(pass/fail/unresolved,只有全部门通过才替换 incumbent);终端重放在搜索循环外评估冻结端点,并封存 Seal = h(C, c*, M_T, ξ*, σ*, S)。由此得到协议级安全不变量:任何准入替换都带通过凭证,正确性永不回退。
- **搜索性能达到 SOTA**:AlgoTune 冻结 12 任务、5 方法、3 种子、48 次语义调用预算下,EPOCH 平均归一化分 0.65,超最强基线 AdaEvolve 0.53(+22.60%),对 AI-Scientist v1/v2 和 OpenEvolve 领先 24.80%–30.90%;冻结端点在 32 个从未用过的官方测试输入上重放,对全部四个基线保持正向平均效应。
- **跨域迁移**:Math14 内部套件(14 个数学目标 × 5 系统 × 3 种子 = 210 cells)平均分 0.57 排名第一(相对提升 +0.60% ~ +10.90%)。
- **组件贡献的因果消融**:六个 AgentHPOBench 任务上,完整系统归一化均值 77.48、平均任务排名 1.67;去掉记忆/证明准入/证伪器/相位控制器分别降到 64.32(−13.17)、40.21(−37.28)、35.74(−41.75)、21.89(−55.59);证明准入对 6/6 任务全胜。
- **十个案例的可验证发现**:11 个张量 border-rank 上界严格改进(含 R(T_5w4414) ≤ 30 的 30 条路径构造、R(T_ss444) ≤ 20,45,242 个坐标独立重放);AES 可逆电路 T 门 10,746→9,234(matched-PyZX 下 −1,512 门、−14.10%,消除 256 个输入克隆并恢复所有 ancilla);median-of-23 的可执行 54 次比较决策过程(改进 Hadian–Sobel 1969 年的 55 次界);全维超立方体行列式不等式 SD(w)W^{d−1} ≤ ∏ᵢ mᵢ,d ≥ 3 时等号当且仅当质量支撑在一个 facet 上(Lean 检查);九元素拟阵反例 H_β = (1,9,27,37,9,1) 使 h₂^β − h₃^β = −1,直接推翻 h₂ ≥ h₃ 前置排序猜想,并通过 principal-extension 扩成无穷族。
- **证据分层方法学**:发现案例的证据按四层构造——语义重建、精确/穷举重放、突变控制(扰动因子/系数/门并要求被拒绝)、形式或解析桥接——并以命题形式给出有限证书充分性条件,明确区分"有限计算"、"一般论证"与"形式化核"三种证据边界。

## Key Contributions

- **Evidence-governed discovery**: a domain-general architecture in which a task contract (public/terminal data views, evaluators, budget, editable artifact space, claim scope), an append-only typed memory retaining both successful and failed trials, active falsification targeting the stated mechanism, three-valued claim-aware admission (pass/fail/unresolved), and independent terminal replay jointly determine which candidates are retained as discoveries — with a protocol-level safety invariant that retained-endpoint correctness never decreases.
- **State-of-the-art search performance**: 0.65 vs 0.53 mean normalized score on the frozen 12-task AlgoTune subset against AdaEvolve (+22.60%), and +24.80%–30.90% over AI-Scientist v1/v2 and OpenEvolve; favorable held-out replay on 32 previously unused official test inputs against all four baselines.
- **Transfer across domains**: highest mean score on the internal Math14 suite (0.57; relative gains +0.60% over OpenEvolve to +10.90% over AI-Scientist v1).
- **Causal component evidence**: on six AgentHPOBench tasks, removing memory, proof admission, falsification, or phase control reduces the normalized mean by 13.17–55.59 points (77.48 → 64.32/40.21/35.74/21.89), with proof admission winning 6/6 paired tasks.
- **Ten documented discoveries with explicit evidential scope**: eleven strict tensor border-rank improvements (incl. a 30-path construction establishing R(T_5w4414) ≤ 30, verified over 45,242 replayed coordinates), a 14.10% matched-PyZX T-count reduction for AES reversible circuits (10,746 → 9,234), an executable 54-comparison median-of-23 selection procedure improving the classical 55-comparison Hadian–Sobel bound, an all-dimensional hypercube determinant inequality with equality characterization (Lean-checked), and a nine-element matroid counterexample refuting the proposed H_β front-loading property (extended to an infinite family).

## Methodology

**Architecture.** EPOCH (Evidence-conditioned Proposal Optimization through Cumulative Hypothesis-governance) frames discovery as constrained search over an explicit evidence ledger. (1) *Task contract* C = (D_pub, D_term, E, B, A, S) separates the public view used for proposal generation from the terminal view reserved for evaluating the selected endpoint, and fixes the claim scope S. (2) *Typed append-only memory* records artifact/evaluator hashes, scores, correctness, resource use, failure attribution, and rejection reasons, so scientific failures constrain later proposals while infrastructure incidents trigger bounded recovery without counting as evidence against the method. (3) *Active falsification*: challenge examples (semantic boundary inputs, coefficient mismatches, cokernel witnesses, counterexample searches, proof obligations) target the proposal's stated mechanism, not just the objective. (4) *Claim-aware admission* is a three-valued gate (schema, scope, correctness, budget, replay); gates take values {1, 0, ⊥} and only a full pass replaces the incumbent, giving protocol-level safety (a higher public score alone can never replace a verified incumbent). (5) *Phase control* chooses among explore/refine/recover/finalize. (6) *Independent replay* evaluates the frozen endpoint outside the loop and seals the result, Seal = h(C, c*, M_T, ξ*, σ*, S), distinguishing finite computation, general argument, and formal kernel.

**Discovery-case evidence protocol.** Each case study builds evidence in four non-interchangeable layers: semantic reconstruction (rebuild the target independently from its definition), exact or exhaustive replay (check every coordinate or every member of the declared finite universe), mutation controls (corrupt a factor/coefficient/gate and require rejection), and a formal or analytic bridge to the stated theorem — with a stated proposition guaranteeing finite-certificate sufficiency only when the independent enumeration and a sound exact checker both hold.

**Evaluation setup.** All systems, adapters, task definitions, and official data were frozen before evaluation (pre-registered schedule, model label Deepseek-v4-Flash, seeds 2026081901–03). Three complementary settings: (a) AlgoTune — a frozen 12-task subset, five methods (EPOCH, AI-Scientist v1/v2, OpenEvolve, AdaEvolve), three seeds, 48-semantic-call budget; held-out official replay re-runs frozen endpoints on 32 unused official test rows (600 s timeout, fresh process, 1 warmup + 3 timed repetitions). (b) Math14 — a project-frozen suite of 14 mathematical objectives, 5 methods × 3 seeds = 210 cells. (c) AgentHPOBench — six heterogeneous tasks (2048 win rate, ModernBERT MNLI accuracy, NoisyGL Cora accuracy, RankUp UTKFace MAE, SparseTSF ETm1 MSE, TabM California RMSE) used for component ablations with cross-task normalized means and paired W/T/L sign tests; mechanism diagnostics (prior-evidence citation rate, repeated-configuration rate, admission rejections) are computed from traces.

## Results & Highlights

- **AlgoTune (12 frozen tasks, 3 seeds, 48-call budget):** EPOCH achieves 0.65 mean normalized score vs 0.53 for the strongest baseline AdaEvolve (+22.60% relative; complete-development effect +0.12 normalized units), 0.52 for AI-Scientist v1 (+24.80%), and 0.50 for both OpenEvolve (+30.10%) and AI-Scientist v2 (+30.90%). It reaches ≥ 0.60 on five of twelve tasks, compared with at most one for any given baseline.
- **Held-out official-test replay (32 unused inputs):** EPOCH keeps a positive mean replay effect against all four baselines (replay effects +0.04 to +0.11 in normalized units), with the largest task-level gains on maximum common subgraph (+0.47 to +0.48 vs every baseline) and Convolve2D (+0.16 to +0.49, though exactly 0.000 vs AdaEvolve, which also later produced a clean FFT solution); NMF consistently reverses to −0.27 vs every baseline.
- **Math14 (14 objectives × 5 methods × 3 seeds):** EPOCH takes rank 1 with mean 0.57 vs OpenEvolve 0.56 (+0.60%), AdaEvolve 0.53 (+7.70%), AI-Scientist v2 0.52 (+9.20%), AI-Scientist v1 0.51 (+10.90%) — but paired task-level records are mixed: 7/4/3 vs AI-Sci v1, 7/5/2 vs AI-Sci v2, yet 4/3/7 vs OpenEvolve and 5/3/6 vs AdaEvolve, so the aggregate lead rests on larger gains on a subset of objectives.
- **AgentHPOBench ablations (6 tasks):** Full EPOCH attains the highest normalized mean 77.48 and best mean task rank (1.67); removing proof admission drops it to 40.21 (−37.28, W/T/L 6/0/0 for full), removing phase control to 21.89 (−55.59, 5/0/1), removing falsifier to 35.74 (−41.75, 4/1/1), and removing memory to 64.32 (−13.17, 4/0/2). Mechanism diagnostics align: prior-evidence citation rises to 80.00% vs 42.50% without memory (+37.5 pp), repeated configurations fall by 10–16 pp with falsifier/proof/phase components, and admission rejections go from 0.00% to 34.67% with proof admission.
- **Discovery artifacts:** the median-of-23 endpoint is an executable decision procedure with worst-case cost ≤ 54 comparisons (V_{1:23}(23) ≤ 54), improving the classical 55-comparison Hadian–Sobel (1969) bound by one; the AES construction drops T-count from 10,746 to 9,234 under matched PyZX (−1,512 T gates, −14.10%, all 256 input clones eliminated and every ancilla restored); the 2-adic transfer scans 86 rational formulas, of which 43 are applicable and all 43 transfer, yielding 11 strict border-rank improvements (incl. R(T_5w4414) ≤ 30 and R(T_ss444) ≤ 20) over 45,242 independently replayed coordinates; the hypercube inequality SD(w)W^{d−1} ≤ ∏ᵢ mᵢ holds with equality iff all mass is supported on a facet for d ≥ 3 (Lean-checked kernel); the nine-element matroid H_β = (1,9,27,37,9,1) has h₂^β − h₃^β = −1, refuting the proposed ordering and extending to an infinite family.

## Authors & Credibility

The four authors — Binjie Guo, Aisheng Mo, Ruitong Li (Zhejiang University), and Xinle Deng (Zhejiang University / The University of Hong Kong) — are not established names in the autonomous-discovery literature, so track-record weight cannot be assigned from the paper alone. Credibility instead rests on unusually strong procedural hygiene: a pre-registered, frozen evaluation (model backbone, seeds, and task cohorts fixed before running); an explicit AI Use Statement disclosing generative-AI assistance with verification responsibilities; a reproducibility statement pointing to frozen configurations, exact/exhaustive verification procedures, formalization scopes, and replay interfaces in the supplement; and disciplined claim hygiene (Table 4 / Table S9 record the evidence boundary of every case, with several bridges honestly marked partial or open). The breadth of executed artifacts — quantum-circuit T-count optimization, tensor border-rank certificates, comparison-complexity selection procedures, Lean-checked inequalities and counterexamples — demonstrates genuine cross-domain competence.

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty.** The contribution is a protocol, not a new optimizer: evaluator-guided search exists (AlphaCode, AlphaTensor, FunSearch, AlphaEvolve, OpenEvolve, AdaEvolve), and domain-specific verification exists (AlphaGeometry, AlphaProof, Lean states). What is genuinely new is treating claim scope as a first-class output — a three-valued admission gate plus sealed, independently replayed endpoints with an explicit evidence-boundary table. That framing is a real, if conceptual, advance for the "AI scientist" field, where conflating finite certificates with theorem-level claims is a live failure mode.

**Rigor — good but with specific soft spots.** (1) All five compared methods run on a single backbone (Deepseek-v4-Flash); the architecture ranking could be backbone-dependent, and no cross-backbone check is reported. (2) Three seeds and no confidence intervals on the headline 0.65 vs 0.53 gap; the AgentHPO ablation gets paired sign tests, but AlgoTune/Math14 aggregates are descriptive. (3) The Math14 lead is thin: +0.01 mean over OpenEvolve with a losing 4/3/7 task-level record against it — the rank-1 claim is an aggregate-mean statement, not task-level dominance. (4) The no-memory variant actually beats full EPOCH on ART 2048 (win rate 0.15 vs 0.07) and ModernBERT MNLI (49.79 vs 47.72 accuracy) and attains the best worst-task floor (Fig. 2a) — memory buys mean performance at a per-task cost the authors disclose but do not resolve. (5) The NMF reversal (−0.27 vs every baseline on held-out replay) shows development gains transfer unevenly, and the Convolve2D replay "advantage" is exactly 0.000 vs AdaEvolve.

**Discovery claims.** The benchmark-side endpoints are, by the authors' own candid admission (Supplement E), established techniques — zero-padded real FFT for Convolve2D, the LSOda stiff solver for Robertson ODE, association-graph bitset Bron–Kerbosch for max common subgraph — i.e., competent engineering rather than new algorithms. The genuinely novel objects (54-comparison median-of-23, R(T_ss444) ≤ 20, the H_β counterexample) are verifiably constructed, but the paper concedes "external record priority remains a separate question": whether the 54-comparison bound is truly new to the selection-complexity literature is not established, and the ten case studies ran under heterogeneous budgets (Supplement B.4), so the discovery portfolio cannot be read as budget-uniform output. The hypercube theorem also still requires a normalization bridge (Averkov et al., 2026) before the polynomial-theorem connection is complete.

**Reproducibility.** Above average for the field — frozen schedules, seeds, endpoint-selection rules, mutation controls, and replay interfaces are documented — but exact replication of the discovery runs depends on access to the specific (unnamed-version) LLM backbone.

## Strengths

- The evidence-governance framing addresses a real, named failure mode of AI research agents (promoting fragile candidates as discoveries) and operationalizes it with checkable gates rather than rhetoric.
- Rare evaluation hygiene: pre-registered frozen protocol, held-out replay of frozen endpoints on unused official test inputs, paired component ablations with sign tests, and trace-level mechanism diagnostics that tie each component to intended behavior (+37.5 pp prior-evidence citation from memory; 34.67% admission rejections from proof admission).
- Intellectual honesty throughout: NMF reversal, the AdaEvolve Convolve2D tie, the no-memory variant's better worst-task floor, and the "not new algorithms" labeling of AlgoTune endpoints are all disclosed by the authors themselves.
- Concrete, independently checkable artifacts across four domains (exact coordinates, Lean kernels, executable selection procedures, gate-count reductions) with per-claim evidence boundaries instead of a single undifferentiated "verified" label.
- The ablation is causally informative: every component's removal hurts, with proof admission at 6/0/2-free 6/0/0 paired dominance and phase control the single most load-bearing piece (−55.59 points).

## Limitations

- Single LLM backbone (Deepseek-v4-Flash) for all five methods; no evidence the ranking transfers to other backbones.
- Three seeds and no variance estimates on benchmark means; AlgoTune/Math14 aggregate comparisons are descriptive, and the Math14 win over OpenEvolve is +0.01 with a losing paired record.
- Small task counts (12 AlgoTune, 14 Math14, 6 AgentHPO) and heterogeneous native metrics compressed into normalized means; the no-memory variant dominates on worst-task floor, so the full system is not uniformly better.
- Held-out replay covers endpoint quality only; the authors acknowledge the absence of larger paired evaluations with fixed budgets and comprehensive resource accounting, so search-cost efficiency vs baselines is unmeasured.
- Discovery case studies use heterogeneous budgets and unresolved external priority; the hypercube result needs a still-missing normalization bridge, and four cases retain open or partial proof bridges (Table S9).
- Extension beyond executable optimization and formal mathematics (simulations, experiments, databases, scientific instruments) remains future work.

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the evidence-governance advantage persist across a different (weaker or stronger) LLM backbone, given the entire comparison ran on Deepseek-v4-Flash?
- What is the wall-clock and call-budget overhead of challenge, admission, and replay relative to baselines at equal fixed budgets — the authors flag the missing resource accounting as the first limitation?
- Can the no-memory variant's superior worst-task floor be reconciled with full EPOCH's mean advantage, e.g., via task-adaptive memory projections, or is memory inherently a mean-over-variance trade?
- Do the 54-comparison median-of-23 procedure, R(T_ss444) ≤ 20, and the H_β counterexample survive external priority review and independent formalization, and can the missing normalization bridge for the hypercube inequality be closed?
- Can the protocol extend to domains without exact evaluators (simulation, wet-lab science), where the terminal view is noisy or expensive and the finite-certificate proposition does not apply?
