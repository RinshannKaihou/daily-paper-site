> **一句话总结：** 提出 AI Theorist 多智能体系统（Claude Opus 4.6 负责假设/执行/批判/综合，GRPO 强化学习微调的 Qwen3.5-27B 负责计算设计，配 18 个工具的注册表：Quantum ESPRESSO、BerkeleyGW、GP-UCB 贝叶斯调参、Paperclip 文献检索），在 α-RuCl₃ 光反射/光电流谱上自主跑通"假设→设计计算→执行→批判→修正→综合"闭环：提出并**有据否决** 3 条解释路线中的 2 条，把 GW 屏蔽更新后的计算带隙从固定屏蔽基线的 1.326 eV 迭代到 1.800 eV，用 BSE 将实验 L/α/α′ 峰指认为 1.077/1.179/1.291 eV 的紧束缚激子，并定量给出光学选择定则（α 的 armchair:zigzag 振幅平方比仅 9×10⁻⁵，α′ 反而偏好 armchair、比值 2.9、强度仅 α 的 ~5%）与可检验预测（偏振旋转应增强 α′/α 相对强度），全程耗 153.98M tokens（约 US$335）+ 约 69 小时选择性计算。

> **TL;DR:** This paper introduces AI Theorist, a five-agent LLM system (hypothesis, calculation-design, execution, critique, synthesis) that autonomously develops and computationally tests physical models against previously unpublished experimental data, demonstrated on differential-reflection and photocurrent spectra (8 K, hBN-encapsulated, graphene-contacted) of α-RuCl₃, a Kitaev quantum-spin-liquid candidate. The calculation-design agent is a Qwen3.5-27B fine-tuned with SFT (MatRL tool-use episodes + execution-checked physics trajectories) and GRPO with terminal rewards for numerical accuracy and evidential support; other agents run on Claude Opus 4.6 over an 18-tool registry (Quantum ESPRESSO DFT+U+J, BerkeleyGW GW-BSE, GP-UCB parameter search). The system proposed 3 explanatory routes, rejected 2 with documented physical reasons (local-correlation model failed to jointly explain gap and optics; multiplet route predicted an unobserved dominant peak), iterated GW screening to move the calculated gap from 1.326 eV (fixed-screening baseline) to 1.800 eV against a 1.8710 eV quasiparticle limit, and used BSE to assign the experimental L/α/α′ peaks to compact bound excitons at 1.077/1.179/1.291 eV (α–α′ spacing 0.112 eV) with quantified optical selection rules: α couples almost purely to zigzag light (armchair:zigzag squared-amplitude ratio ≈9×10⁻⁵) while α′ favors armchair (ratio ≈2.9) at only ~5% of α's amplitude — explaining why α′ shows up in photocurrent but barely in reflection. Total cost: 153.98M tokens (≈US$335) and ~69 h of selected calculations; an independent human corroborated the numerics, and the model yields a falsifiable prediction (rotating polarization from zigzag to armchair should enhance α′ relative to α).

## 核心贡献

- **AI Theorist 多智能体架构**：五个角色（假设、计算设计、执行、批判、综合）构成"假设生成 → 计算设计 → 执行 → 批判 → 修正 → 综合"的迭代闭环，首次演示 AI 系统对**未发表的量子材料实验观测**自主发展微观物理模型并给出可检验预测；人类科学家只负责设定研究方向、做实验和提供样品语境。
- **按科学结论训练的计算设计智能体**：Qwen3.5-27B 经 SFT（MatRL 工具调用语料 + 经执行校验的物理轨迹）+ GRPO（以"数值精度 + 证据支撑度"的终局奖励训练、附加计算成本惩罚、每任务 4 个 rollout）微调，使其在受限的小型电子-空穴/Hubbard 计算环境中学会选择能区分假设、且数值收敛的计算序列——而非照搬某个固定动作序列。
- **α-RuCl₃ 激子结构的自主解释**：系统否决 2/3 条路线（局域关联模型无法同时解释带隙与光学特征；多重态路线产生实验中不存在的支配峰），迭代 GW 屏蔽把计算带隙从 1.326 → 1.585 → 1.729 → 1.800 eV，BSE 给出 L/α/α′ → 1.077/1.179/1.291 eV 紧束缚激子的逐一指认，并定量给出对比鲜明的光学选择定则（zigzag vs armchair）。
- **定量的 SOC 依赖与网格敏感性**：SOC 计算中卫星峰-参考峰跃迁振幅平方和之比约 0.093，标量相对论参照仅 3×10⁻⁵（约 3 个量级）；k 网格 36→64 点使 α/α′ 能量移动 −13.2/−12.2 meV，64→96 点质心变化 ≤8.3 meV。
- **可检验预测与成本透明**：预测偏振从 zigzag 转到 armchair 时 α′ 相对 α 增强；全程成本公开——153.98M tokens（Qwen 87.70M in/0.41M out，Claude 65.60M in/0.27M out，约 US$334.75）+ 2.5/38.5/27.9 h 三段计算，并由独立人类研究者复核数值方法与微观诠释。

## Key Contributions

- **AI Theorist architecture**: a five-agent loop (hypothesize → design calculations → execute → critique → refine → synthesize) that, for the first time, autonomously develops a microscopic physical model explaining previously unpublished experimental observations in a quantum material, with humans setting direction and performing measurements.
- **A scientifically-rewarded calculation-design agent**: Qwen3.5-27B, fine-tuned with SFT (MatRL tool-use episodes + execution-checked physics trajectories) and GRPO (terminal reward from numerical accuracy + evidential support, computation-cost penalty, 4 rollouts/task), trained in a bounded environment of small electron-hole and Hubbard calculations to select discriminating, numerically sound calculation sequences.
- **Autonomous excitonic interpretation of α-RuCl₃**: the system proposed 3 routes and rejected 2 with documented reasons, iterated GW screening (gap 1.326 → 1.585 → 1.729 → 1.800 eV vs 1.8710 eV quasiparticle limit), and assigned the experimental L/α/α′ features to bound excitons at 1.077/1.179/1.291 eV with quantified contrasting selection rules (α zigzag-pure at ratio ≈9×10⁻⁵; α′ armchair-preferring at ≈2.9 with ~5% of α's amplitude).
- **SOC dependence and grid sensitivity quantified**: satellite-to-reference summed squared transition-amplitude ratio ≈0.093 with SOC vs 3×10⁻⁵ scalar-relativistic; 36→64-point refinement shifts α/α′ by −13.2/−12.2 meV and 64→96-point changes centroids ≤8.3 meV.
- **A falsifiable prediction and transparent costs**: polarization rotation from zigzag to armchair should enhance α′ relative to α; total budget of 153.98M tokens (≈US$334.75) and 2.5/38.5/27.9 h of named calculations, with independent human corroboration of the numerics.

## Methodology

**Agent architecture and loop.** AI Theorist comprises a hypothesis agent (analyzes observations + literature, proposes and revises competing physical explanations), a calculation-design agent (proposes calculations that discriminate between explanations or resolve numerical uncertainty), an execution agent (runs the tools), a critique agent (checks numerical reliability, quantitatively compares predictions against the experimental probes, distinguishes numerical failure from evidence against a model), and a synthesis agent (combines supported results into an interpretable model with stated limits of validity and derived predictions). Hypothesis/execution/critique/synthesis run on Claude Opus 4.6; the calculation-design agent is a Qwen3.5-27B adapted via SFT + GRPO. Role-specific prompts, scientific-tool supports, and rubric-based critique guides govern each agent.

**Tool registry (18 tools).** Four Paperclip interfaces for literature search/paper inspection; three Quantum ESPRESSO 7.4.1 interfaces (electronic-structure, orbital projections, export); seven BerkeleyGW 4.0 interfaces (wavefunction conversion, dielectric screening, quasiparticle corrections, electron-hole kernels, excitonic spectra, quasiparticle interpolation, real-space exciton analysis); a GP-UCB module for parameter search; NumPy 2.4.6/SciPy numerical analysis; SciPy-based model fitting; Matplotlib visualization.

**Calculation-design agent training.** SFT on public MatRL tool-use episodes plus execution-checked physics trajectories, then GRPO in a bounded environment of small electron-hole and Hubbard calculations: terminal rewards derive from numerical accuracy and evidential support of the scientific conclusions, a computation-cost penalty applies only to successful episodes, four rollouts per task, group-relative advantages update the planner while the executor and numerical scorer stay fixed. The outcome-based reward deliberately avoids requiring human annotation of every intermediate decision. Evaluation compared SFT-only, SFT+GRPO, and Claude Opus 4.6 policies on prespecified synthetic tasks and Quantum ESPRESSO transfer cases with matched tools/budgets, excluding all RuCl₃ case-study data from training and checkpoint selection.

**GP-UCB parameter search.** For expensive DFT+U/many-body parameter tuning (Hubbard U, J against HSE reference band gaps/structures), a Gaussian-process surrogate with upper-confidence-bound acquisition θ_{t+1} = argmax[μ_t(θ) + κ_t σ_t(θ)] trades agreement with measurements against exploration; failed calculations return diagnostics without counting as improved agreement.

**Physics pipeline for α-RuCl₃.** Monoclinic bulk, zigzag antiferromagnetic order; PBE + DFT+U+J on Ru 4d (U = 3.6 eV, J = 0.4 eV) in Quantum ESPRESSO; GW quasiparticle corrections in BerkeleyGW (Hybertsen-Louie generalized plasmon-pole with static-remainder correction, 440-band sums, 3D periodic Coulomb truncation), applied as fixed two-branch scissors; BSE on the Tamm-Dancoff approximation. The principal result uses a 64-point spinor calculation; scalar-relativistic reference uses 36 points; convergence probed at 96 points. Observables: polarization-resolved dielectric response, discrete excitation energies, optical matrix elements, conditional electron distributions (2.1) with shared fixed hole position, normalized per map.

**Experiment (performed by humans).** hBN-encapsulated α-RuCl₃ vertical junction between graphene electrodes; differential-reflection and photocurrent spectroscopy at 8 K under vacuum (Montana cryostat, Bruker IFS 66v/S FTIR, tungsten-halogen source); photocurrent spectra normalized by source spectra from InGaAs/Si reference detectors.

## Results & Highlights

- **Discovery loop with documented route rejection**: AI Theorist proposed and tested 3 explanatory routes — (1) local correlations, (2) multiplet excitations, (3) a constrained electronic starting state with relaxed orbital occupations + updated screening + electron-hole interactions. Route 1 was rejected because the local-correlation model could not jointly explain the gap and optical features; route 2 was rejected because it produced an unobserved dominant optical peak. Route 3 was refined: releasing the occupation constraint restored α as the dominant low-energy resonance.
- **Screening-update sequence (GW, α-RuCl₃)**: the calculated gap rises 1.326 → 1.585 → 1.729 → 1.800 eV (fixed-gap → iterated-W iterations; final value 1.8004 eV) against a frozen-screening baseline of 1.326 eV and a quasiparticle limiting value of 1.8710 eV — a +0.47 eV correction attributable to screening updates alone. The DFT+U+SOC indirect gap (0.811 eV) is raised to 1.770 eV by the fixed two-branch GW scissors.
- **Exciton assignments vs quasiparticle gap (BSE, 64-point spinor grid)**: L, α, α′ are assigned to states at 1.077, 1.179, 1.291 eV — all ≈0.6–0.8 eV below the ~1.8 eV quasiparticle reference gap, with α–α′ separation ≈0.112 eV. The β-region representative state (1.904 eV) extends across much of the supercell, contrasting with the compact L/α/α′ conditional electron distributions — the paper's bound-exciton evidence.
- **Optical selection rules explain probe contrast**: α's armchair-to-zigzag squared-transition-amplitude ratio is ≈9×10⁻⁵ (near-pure zigzag coupling), while α′ favors armchair with a ratio of ≈2.9 and a total amplitude of only ~5% of α's — quantitatively accounting for α dominating differential reflection while α′ is weak there yet resolved in photocurrent. The contrast persists under tested kernel/screening grid refinements.
- **SOC sensitivity (headline number, with a caveat)**: the satellite-to-reference ratio of summed squared transition amplitudes is ≈0.093 in the SOC calculation vs 3×10⁻⁵ in the scalar-relativistic reference (a ~3,000-fold difference). Note the comparison uses a 64-point Γ-centric unshifted SOC grid vs a 36-point shifted scalar-relativistic grid — the authors explicitly state it "does not isolate SOC alone."
- **Grid sensitivity quantified but convergence not claimed**: 36→64-point refinement moves α/α′ by −13.2/−12.2 meV (values 1.179386/1.291078 eV on the 64-point grid); dominant-channel centroid changes are ≤8.3 meV at 64→96 points; mean-field and screening convergence is not established.
- **Budget**: 153.98M tokens total (Qwen3.5-27B: 87.70M in / 0.41M out; Claude Opus 4.6: 65.60M in / 0.27M out), estimated token cost US$334.75, plus 2.5 h (fixed-screening), 38.5 h (screening-update sequence), and 27.9 h (36-point calculation; 16 MPI processes) of selected scientific calculations.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty is in the orchestration, not the physics ingredients.** That SOC and electron-hole interactions shape α-RuCl₃'s optical response, and that GW-BSE describes its excitons, was already established by prior human work the authors cite (e.g., Li & Yang's PRB 2020 quasiparticle/excitonic study of α-RuCl₃, ref [21]; Plumb 2014 and Sandilands 2016 on spin-orbit-assisted optics). What is genuinely new is the autonomous peak-by-peak assignment of L/α/α′ to specific excitons with quantified selection rules and the dissociation-based account of the reflection/photocurrent contrast — an incremental but real scientific contribution, and a credible "first" as scoped ("autonomously developing a physical model for previously unpublished observations").
- **The headline SOC ablation is confounded.** The 0.093 vs 3×10⁻⁵ satellite/reference contrast compares a 64-point Γ-centric unshifted spinor grid against a 36-point shifted scalar-relativistic grid, with pseudopotential/quasiparticle-treatment differences on top; the authors admit in Methods 6.7 that this "does not isolate SOC alone." A clean SOC-only ablation at matched grids is missing — this weakens the paper's most quotable number.
- **The agent-training contribution is unverifiable from the paper.** Methods 6.4 promises comparisons of SFT-only, SFT+GRPO, and Claude Opus 4.6 calculation-design policies on prespecified synthetic tasks and Quantum ESPRESSO transfer cases — yet no win rates, success rates, or cost figures for these evaluations are reported anywhere in the paper. For an ML/agents audience, the claim that GRPO training improved calculation selection is therefore unsupported by evidence in the text.
- **Theory–experiment agreement is never quantified.** The calculated 1.077/1.179/1.291 eV assignments are not compared against experimental peak positions with residuals or uncertainties; the reader must eyeball Fig. 2b. The α′ photocurrent-visibility explanation (higher energy → weaker binding → field-assisted dissociation) is qualitative and imported from monolayer WSe₂ literature (ref [31]) without direct evidence here.
- **Reproducibility is currently incomplete.** Code, role prompts, tool configurations, checkpoints, data, and calculation tables are all promised "once available" — nothing is released with the preprint, so neither the agent loop nor the calculation audit trail can be independently re-run today.
- **Red flags are modest but real**: single-material case study (generalization asserted only as future work), convergence of the mean-field/screening steps explicitly not established, and the "first demonstration" framing leans on the word "autonomously" while humans chose the material, performed all measurements, and an in-team human re-checked the numerics.

## Strengths

- A genuine closed evidence-driven loop, not free-form LLM generation: hypotheses must survive controlled computational tests (SOC on/off, fixed vs updated screening W, 36/64/96-point grids), and 2 of 3 proposed routes were explicitly rejected with physical reasons — a documented negative-result trail that is rare in agent papers.
- Deterministic scientific tools (Quantum ESPRESSO, BerkeleyGW) plus GP-UCB anchor the LLM reasoning in reproducible numerics; the critique agent distinguishes numerical failure from evidence against a physical model, a subtle and important design point.
- The calculation-design agent is trained on outcome-based rewards (numerical accuracy + evidential support with a compute-cost penalty) in a bounded environment, avoiding per-step human annotation — a sensible reward design for long-horizon scientific decisions.
- Produces a falsifiable, specific prediction (polarization-rotation test of the α/α′ assignment), and the real-space exciton distributions plus selection-rule ratios give the interpretation content beyond "energies match."
- Transparent cost accounting (tokens, dollars, CPU-hours) and unusually candid limitations (SOC comparison confound, non-established convergence) in the Methods section.

## Limitations

- Single-material demonstration; no evidence the pipeline or the GRPO-trained calculation-design agent generalizes to a second material.
- No quantitative agent evaluation results reported (SFT+GRPO vs SFT-only vs Claude Opus 4.6 comparisons are described but their numbers never appear).
- The key SOC ablation mixes grid and treatment changes, so the ~3,000× sensitivity figure is not a clean causal estimate; mean-field/screening convergence is unestablished.
- No code, data, prompts, or checkpoints released at publication time; all reproducibility rests on promised artifacts.
- Theory–experiment agreement (peak positions/intensities) is never quantified with residuals or error bars; the α′ dissociation explanation for the reflection/photocurrent contrast is speculative and untested in this system.

## Questions for Further Exploration

- What were the actual evaluation numbers for SFT-only vs SFT+GRPO vs Claude Opus 4.6 calculation-design policies on the prespecified synthetic and Quantum ESPRESSO transfer tasks — does GRPO training measurably help?
- Can the SOC effect be isolated with matched k-grids, pseudopotentials, and quasiparticle treatments, turning the 0.093 vs 3×10⁻⁵ contrast into a clean ablation?
- Will the falsifiable polarization-rotation prediction (α′ enhanced relative to α as polarization goes from zigzag to armchair) be confirmed experimentally?
- Does the pipeline transfer to a second quantum material end-to-end, and is the GRPO-trained calculation-design policy material-agnostic or does it need per-material fine-tuning?
- Can quantitative theory–experiment residuals and explicit exciton binding energies be extracted to turn the peak assignments into a statistical test rather than a visual match?
