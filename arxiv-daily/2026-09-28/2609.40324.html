Key Finding

An adversarial multi-agent harness solved five open research problems — including a 20,000→2 constant collapse — at ~100 model calls each.

Cogentic 以「编排者—并行证明者—对抗验证者」的轮次迭代累积已验证引理，仅用每题 O(100)（最难 O(1000)）次模型调用，独立解决了线上学习、拍卖理论与机制设计中 5 个开放研究问题，全部经专家验证。

Why It Matters

A reference blueprint for agentic scientific discovery: adversarial verification, a verified lemma ledger, and dead-end registries tackle long-horizon agent reliability.

Critical Edge

No ablations, no baselines: all five problems sit in the authors' own research program, verified by coauthors — architecture asserted, not measured.

The Harness § Methodology

Cogentic is a group of agents sharing an on-disk workspace, coordinated by an orchestrator that decides which agent runs, when, and on what. Its defining discipline is role hygiene: the orchestrator and process advisor are barred from mathematical opinions — no guessing answers, recommending techniques, or declaring directions promising or dead — while verifiers start from the assumption that every step and every citation is wrong until justified.

Memory does the long-horizon work: a verified ledger of confirmed intermediate lemmas means later rounds never re-prove accepted fragments, and a dead-end registry of counterexample-killed bounds means they are never revisited. Long documents circulate as file paths, so each prover reads selectively and contexts stay small. Runs used Gemini 3.1 Pro or an early Gemini 4 Argon, starting from the bare problem statement; domain experts verified every proof afterwards.

01PlanOrchestrator partitions prover slots across directions: bounds, constants, constructions, counterexamples.
02BriefEach prover gets an individually written briefing — independent readings of the same history.
03DraftProvers work in parallel, contexts kept small via path-passed documents.
04AttackEvery draft: solo read, then joint read — everything assumed wrong until justified.
05BankAuditor extracts accepted lemmas → verified ledger; killed bounds → dead-end registry.
06AdviseProcess advisor tunes standing instructions; coordinators hold no mathematical opinions.
07CloseComparator picks the strongest proof → formal writer → final audit; experts verify after.

Strengths ▲

  • Real, quantified wins over named SOTA: 20,000→2, 5.2→3.52, √2 eliminated, O(d ln T)→O(d), 1.8→tight 1.5 — each expert-verified and companion-papered, not just claimed.
  • Extreme inference frugality: Ø(100)–Ø(1000) calls per problem, orders of magnitude below brute-force agentic-science compute, with scaling headroom deliberately unused.
  • A reliability recipe for long-horizon agents: rejection-biased dual verification, ledger so work is never redone, dead-end registry so failures are never retried, and a strict no-math-opinions firewall between control flow and content.
  • Demonstrated independence: proposed the pFPA₂ₙ mechanism and analysis for a part authors had never studied, with no hints.
  • Honest discussion: admits human post-processing, the expert-verification bottleneck, and the proof-throughput vs understanding gap.

Limitations ▼

  • Only five problems, all from the authors' own subfields; no standardized benchmark and no success-rate over attempted problems — survivorship bias is unquantified.
  • QA rests on coauthors reading natural-language proofs — no Lean/Isabelle formalization — leaving residual risk of subtle undetected errors.
  • No component ablations, no baseline harness comparisons, no failed-run analysis: the architectural contribution is asserted rather than measured.
  • Reproducibility is limited: proprietary models including an unreleased "early Gemini 4 Argon"; prompts, round counts and retry stats unreported — nobody outside Google can re-run this.
  • Proofs live in companion papers; this document alone contains neither the mathematics nor enough run-level detail to audit system behavior.
