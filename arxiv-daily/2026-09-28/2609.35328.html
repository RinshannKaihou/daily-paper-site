01

How the loop works

Methodology, distilled

In Meta-Black-Box Optimization (MetaBBO), a learnable meta-policy makes online design decisions for a low-level optimizer — yet every existing variant still requires humans to hand-craft that policy, its features, the training pipeline, and the optimizer beneath it.

HADA hands the whole design job to two coding agents on a DeepSeek-V4 backbone, run for H = 100 evolution steps. Each step samples a parent from the Algorithm Evolution Tree (selection probability ∝ performance, penalized by child count), lets the hyper agent rewrite the task agent's prompt — and its own — then lets the task agent edit the MetaBBO codebase itself, from the low-level optimizer to the meta-policy. The child is scored with a 0–1 normalized Perf metric (5 training epochs, 3 proxy runs; the final winner is re-trained 20 epochs, re-tested over 10 runs) and written back into the tree.

The “zero” start is deliberately naive: DQN-DE — a 36,761-parameter MLP whose only job is tuning DE's mutation factor F and crossover rate Cr. Nothing else is human-designed.

03

Strengths & Limitations

Critical assessment, condensed

Strengths

  • First-mover on a real gap — the MetaBBO design loop was still fully hand-crafted; HADA removes the human with minimal input.
  • Unusual protocol depth — 4 domains, 17 tuned baselines, OOD generalization, cross-domain transfer, 3 ablations, token-matched LLaMEA control.
  • Interpretability done right — complete evolution logs released, including the honest 0.877 dip and rollback narrative.
  • The thesis is tested, not preached — the modifiability ablation (0.9674 vs 0.9039 frozen) directly substantiates open-endedness.
  • Backbone-robust — every tested LLM lands ≥ 0.95; no single-vendor quirk.

Limitations

  • “From zero” is overstated — the start, DQN-DE, already scores 0.8831 officially; net gain over its own start is ≈ +0.08.
  • One evolution run underpins everything — variance across independent HADA runs is never reported; constrained/multi-objective shown only as box plots.
  • No statistical significance testing — several per-function gaps sit within ~1 std.
  • Hand-set AET sampling rule — the exploration/exploitation heuristic is exactly what the paper criticizes elsewhere.
  • Benchmark-coupled, continuous-only — combinatorial and expensive surrogate settings left as future work; no wall-clock or dollar budget reported.
04

Open questions

For further exploration
Q1

Is 0.9674 a typical trajectory — what variance appears across independent evolution runs?

Q2

Can the hyper agent learn to rewrite the AET sampling rule itself, closing the last hand-crafted loop?

Q3

Does the advantage survive expensive optimization, where proxy budgets must shrink drastically?

Q4

What is the true token-and-dollar cost per step — and the compute-optimal stopping point on the 0.577→0.971 curve?

Q5

Do self-edited prompts stay stable beyond 100 steps, or harden into over-constrained rules that plateau novelty?
