01 · Setup

Methodology, distilled

Three frozen backbones — EEG-native CBraMod, REVE (≈25,000-subject pretraining), and vision DINOv3 as a cross-modal control — are probed at early/middle/late blocks on three 200 Hz EEG tasks (motor imagery, mental-arithmetic workload, imagined speech): 27 settings.

Per setting, a TopK SAE (k = 32, 16× dictionary, MSE + AuxK) is trained on ℓ2-normalized activations (test FVU 0.059–0.228). Clean trials get perturbed copies: spatial channel dropout and spectral band-stop notches — the 8–12 Hz alpha notch against a width-matched 30–34 Hz sham.

Because EEG spectra are 1/f, the alpha notch deletes 11.8–57.2× more energy than the sham, so every response is divided by time-domain distortion and by removed spectral energy — two normalizations that bound, not remove, the confound. Controls: size-matched fragile, random, and activity-matched (firing 0.374 vs 0.373). Stage-5 correlates clean activation with trial-wise relative alpha power — a quantity never used in selection.

02 · The deliverable

The five-stage validation ladder

The paper’s operational contribution: an ordered protocol with an explicit evidence→claim mapping. The ablation-selected candidates clear stage 1 — and stop there.

04a · What holds up

Strengths

  • A clean, well-posed confound — 1/f removed-energy asymmetry — bounded by two complementary normalizations, applied uniformly across 27 settings with a cross-modal control.
  • Genuinely out-of-sample check: stage 5 uses a quantity untouched during selection — the step most interpretability papers skip.
  • Obsessive controls: size-matched, random, and activity-matched (0.374 vs 0.373 firing); frozen vs retrained probes expose baseline-activity artifacts.
  • Explicit falsification table mapping evidence tiers to claim tiers, with a stated “negative reading” of what the result does not show.
  • Reports inconvenient details: fragile latents out-correlate specialists, orderings are seed-dependent, alpha wins only 15/27.
04b · Where it breaks

Limitations

  • No positive control — the protocol is shown to reject, never to accept a true feature.
  • Single SAE seed in 24/27 settings; ablation orderings proved seed-dependent where 3 seeds were run.
  • Band-stop hits the whole 8–12 Hz band; relative band power tests only a global reading — structured mu-rhythm codes stay unexcluded.
  • Narrow readout: logistic probes on mean-pooled codes only; task analyses cover 21/27 (REVE MA/Speech unavailable); Δfi contaminated by TopK rank displacement.
  • Normalizations bound, not remove, the confound (0.28× vs 1.32× straddle 1).
  • No LLM experiments despite the LLM-interpretability framing; text ablations have no clean spectral-energy analogue.
06 · Open questions

What to ask next

  1. Run the missing positive control — e.g. a latent tracking a sinusoid injected into EEG — so the ladder must accept something; calibrate its false-negative rate.
  2. How do JumpReLU, BatchTopK, or larger dictionaries move stage-2/5 verdicts across SAE seeds?
  3. Would an electrode-wise mu-rhythm test rescue a structured alpha reading that global relative band power cannot see?
  4. What is the analogue of “fraction of signal deleted” for token/feature ablations in text — and do published LLM “concept” features survive it?
  5. Would token-level or attention-head readouts overturn the stage-4 null that selectivity is not importance?
05 · The rulebook

Evidence → claim mapping

  • 1 Latent responds to ablation
    ⇒ may claim “alpha-sensitive”
  • 2 Survives distortion control
    ⇒ “alpha-selective”
  • 3 Specific across perturbations
    ⇒ not generically fragile
  • 5 Co-varies with alpha on clean data
    ⇒ evidence of “alpha-representing”
  • 4 + readout intervention
    ⇒ task-relevant representation
