> **一句话总结：** 该文把"部署诱导不一致性"（同一 checkpoint、同一输入、同一决策规则，仅因换用 DeepSpeed/FSDP/vLLM/TensorRT-LLM 等分布式推理栈而产生的输出改变）确立为医疗 AI 评测的独立可靠性维度：用 VDST（L∞≤0.03 视觉扰动差分测试）和 TDST（隐形 Unicode 后缀的梯度引导离散搜索）构建配对基准，发现未加扰动的两卡 DeepSpeed 推理在 MedQA 子集上就已使 HuatuoGPT2-7B 有 25.52%（12,409/48,522）的答案与 HuggingFace 参考执行不同（第三语料上达 45.82%），多模态模型 VDST 测试成功率最高达 0.98（BLIP2-OPT-2.7B + vLLM），且仅将相对发散提升（RL）前 5% 的关键层（主要是 `self.rotary_emb`、`self.norm`）提升到 float32，即可把 16 组模型-框架对的平均成功率从 0.56 降到 0.21。

> **TL;DR:** A medical model that passes centralized HuggingFace evaluation can change its prediction when served through a distributed framework. The paper builds a paired differential-testing benchmark where checkpoint, input, prompt, and decision rule are fixed and only the execution stack changes (HuggingFace reference vs DeepSpeed/FSDP/vLLM/TensorRT-LLM). Natural, unperturbed two-GPU DeepSpeed execution already flips 25.52% (12,409/48,522) of HuatuoGPT2-7B answers on MedQA-derived subsets vs the HuggingFace reference, and 45.82% (7,517/16,407) on the third QA corpus. Gradient-guided VDST pushes test success rate (SR) to 0.98 for BLIP2-OPT-2.7B on vLLM (vs 0.21-0.27 for ResNet-18), invisible-suffix TDST reaches 100% SR for 3 of 4 medical LLMs on MedMCQA/PubMedQA (Llama-3.2-3B only 53.33%/46.67%), and elevating only the top-5% divergence-amplifying layers to float32 cuts average SR from 0.56 to 0.21 across 16 supported model-framework pairs.

## 核心贡献

- **问题形式化：** 首次把"部署诱导不一致性"定义为一个独立的医疗模型可靠性维度——checkpoint、prompt 模板、决策策略完全相同，仅执行栈（算子内核、精度策略、张量并行、通信归约顺序）不同即产生不同预测；这与传统的模型错误（衡量预测是否临床正确）正交。
- **VDST（视觉部署敏感测试）：** 在 L∞=0.03 球内用 Adam 搜索加性扰动 δ，成功条件刻意不对称：单卡执行必须保持正确预测（y_ref = y），而分布式执行必须给出不同预测（f_m(x') ≠ y）；损失为 L_single − λ·L_multi + β‖δ‖₂²（λ=1.0, β=0.002），兼顾图像分类与 VQA。
- **TDST（文本部署敏感测试）：** 一个精巧的测试原语——向医疗多选题问题追加"隐形后缀"（仅保留单 token 稳定的不可见 Unicode 字符：Cc/Cf/Zs/Zl/Zp 类别与变体选择符），用梯度引导在离散 token 空间搜索，使 top-2 选项概率在边界处拉平（等式 5 的均衡+支配损失），参考执行与分布式执行给出不同选项，而人眼可见的问题文本完全不变。
- **部署敏感型医疗基准：** 语言赛道 4 模型（Llama-3.2-3B、Qwen2.5-3B、HuatuoGPT2-7B、Qwen3-8B）× MedMCQA/PubMedQA × DeepSpeed/vLLM 两卡；视觉/多模态赛道 5 模型（ResNet-18、RCLIP、BLIP2-OPT-2.7B、Qwen2.5-VL-7B、Qwen3-VL-8B）× ROCO/VQA-RAD × 4 框架 × 2/4 GPU，并同时报告成功率（SR）与搜索步数（Avg. Step）。
- **定位与定向缓解：** 用逐层 MAD（式 6）和相对发散提升 RL_i（式 7）定位放大执行差异的关键层（top 5% 分位，式 8），集中在 `self.rotary_emb` 和 `self.norm`；仅对这些模块做 float32 精度提升，16 组支持的模型-框架对平均 SR 从 0.56 降至 0.21。

## Key Contributions

- Formalizes **deployment-induced inconsistency** (y_ref(x) ≠ y_dist(x) with identical checkpoint, prompt, and decision policy) as a directly measurable reliability dimension for medical foundation models, orthogonal to ordinary model error and to hallucination/robustness benchmarks (MedQA, PubMedQA, MedMCQA line of work).
- **VDST**, an execution-aware paired image test generator with an asymmetric oracle (reference must stay correct; distributed execution must flip), covering classification and VQA-style objectives.
- **TDST**, a gradient-guided discrete search over an *invisible-token pool* (tokenizer-stable Unicode variation selectors / format characters) that nudges answers to the decision boundary while leaving the visible medical question unchanged — a reusable testing primitive for LLM serving stacks.
- A **cross-framework benchmark matrix** (4 frameworks × 2/4-GPU × 9 models across language, vision, and multimodal) reporting both Success Rate and search cost (average optimization steps), plus an RQ1 natural-discrepancy measurement without any test generation.
- A **localize-then-mitigate** loop: per-layer Mean Absolute Difference and Relative Divergence Lift identify precision-amplifying layers (dominated by `self.rotary_emb` and `self.norm`); targeted float32 elevation of these modules reduces average SR from 0.56 to 0.21 at minimal added cost.

## Methodology

**Paired design.** For every test case, HuggingFace single-device execution is the centralized evaluation reference; each distributed execution (DeepSpeed, FSDP, vLLM, TensorRT-LLM on 2 or 4 GPUs) is the matched deployment variant. Checkpoint, input semantics, prompt template, and decision rule are held fixed, so any output difference is attributable to the serving stack rather than data variation.

**VDST (visual).** For input image x with gold label y, search an additive perturbation within an L∞ ball of radius ε=0.03 (Adam, step size α=0.01, max T steps, projected back into the ball after each update) such that: (1) the reference prediction stays correct (f_s(x')=y) and (2) the distributed prediction differs (f_m(x')≠y). The objective (Eq. 3) is L_vst = L_single(x',y) − λ·L_multi(x',y) + β‖δ‖₂² with λ=1.0, β=0.002; for VLMs the per-token negative log-likelihood against the ground-truth short answer replaces cross-entropy. Success Rate (SR) is the fraction of inputs for which this condition is reached within budget; Avg. Step is the mean number of steps among successes. Because the image is minimally perturbed while reference semantics are preserved, a success exposes an error that appears only after execution configuration changes.

**TDST (textual).** The candidate pool contains invisible Unicode characters only (categories Cc, Cf, Zs, Zl, Zp plus variation selectors such as U+FE00–U+FE0F), filtered to characters with stable single-token encodings (Eq. 4): the suffix must map to exactly one token ID under repetition and keep the visible prompt unchanged. TDST computes gradients over current suffix tokens, restricts replacements to invisible candidates, and picks top-£ high-impact candidates per iteration. The loss (Eq. 5) is L_txt = λ_eq(P(1) − P(2))² − λ_dom((P(1)+P(2))/2 − P_rest): equalize the top-two answer probabilities while keeping both above all distractors, driving the question to a narrow decision boundary where a tiny execution-level difference flips the selected answer. Search stops when the two executions produce different letters.

**RQ protocol.** RQ1: natural discrepancy without any perturbation (HuatuoGPT2-7B, HuggingFace vs two-GPU DeepSpeed, three medical QA corpora). RQ2: TDST on four medical LLMs × MedMCQA/PubMedQA × DeepSpeed/vLLM. RQ3: VDST on the full vision/multimodal matrix. RQ4: localization (per-layer MAD, Eq. 6; Relative Divergence Lift RL_i = (μ_i − max_{k<i} μ_k)/(max_{k<i} μ_k + ε), Eq. 7; critical set = top-5% percentile of RL, Eq. 8) followed by targeted precision elevation: run only detected modules (mainly `self.rotary_emb`, `self.norm`) in float32 while everything else stays unchanged.

## Results & Highlights

- **Natural discrepancy is large without any attack (RQ1):** HuatuoGPT2-7B under two-GPU DeepSpeed vs HuggingFace differs on 3,571/14,369 MedQA-US samples (24.85%), 8,838/34,253 MedQA-Chinese samples (25.80%) — 12,409/48,522 (25.52%) across the two — and on 7,517/16,407 samples (45.82%) of the third medical QA corpus.
- **TDST on medical LLMs (RQ2, two-GPU):** on MedMCQA/PubMedQA, Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct, HuatuoGPT2-7B, and Qwen3-8B all reach **100% SR** on both datasets under DeepSpeed, with Qwen2.5-3B needing only 8.57/10.70 average steps, while Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct lags at 53.33% SR (41.13 steps) on MedMCQA and 46.67% (57.14 steps) on PubMedQA. vLLM preserves the model-level pattern but changes difficulty: Llama-3.2-3B drops to 43.33% SR with 40.08/51.23 steps; HuatuoGPT2-7B and Qwen3-8B need 11.43–12.20 steps under DeepSpeed vs 21.30–25.60 under vLLM.
- **VDST on vision/multimodal (RQ3, Table 1):** single-modality models are moderate — ResNet-18 SR 0.21–0.27 and RCLIP 0.38–0.43 across 2/4-GPU DeepSpeed/FSDP — whereas multimodal models are far more sensitive: BLIP2-OPT-2.7B hits **0.98 SR on vLLM and 0.96 on TensorRT-LLM** (2-GPU), Qwen2.5-VL-7B reaches 0.93 on vLLM, versus abstract-reported ranges 0.21–0.43 (single-modality) and 0.32–0.98 (multimodal). Search cost diverges sharply: BLIP2 needs 16.72 steps on vLLM while Qwen2.5-VL needs 156.16 steps to reach only 0.47 on TensorRT-LLM.
- **Targeted mitigation (RQ4, Table 2, 2-GPU post-elevation SR):** averaged over the 16 supported model-framework pairs, SR falls from 0.56 to **0.21**. ResNet-18 drops to 0.00–0.01 and RCLIP to 0.01 under DeepSpeed/FSDP; BLIP2 falls from 0.51→0.19 (DeepSpeed), 0.47→0.30 (FSDP), 0.98→0.45 (vLLM), 0.96→0.07 (TensorRT-LLM); Qwen3-VL falls from 0.81→0.17 (vLLM) and 0.81→0.01 (TensorRT-LLM). Residual disagreement stays high for Qwen2.5-VL on vLLM (0.93→0.86) and TensorRT-LLM (0.47→0.46). Detected critical modules concentrate in `self.rotary_emb` and `self.norm`.

## Authors & Credibility

The seven authors span Shanghai Jiao Tong University (Yifei Wang, Youtao Ding, Li Pan — corresponding author), Beihang University (Xiaohan Zhang), Nanyang Technological University (Tianlin Li, Xiaoyu Zhang), and Tongji University (Yida Yang); the first two authors contributed equally. The copyright notice indicates an AAAI 2027 submission/camera-ready. The team's citation pattern sits firmly in the software-testing-for-ML literature (CRADLE cross-platform validation, differential testing, DL compiler/inference-engine bug studies) and NTU co-author Tianlin Li has an established record in deep-learning system testing, so the methodology is credible. None of the authors appear to be clinical/medical-informatics specialists — the medical content comes entirely from public benchmarks (MedQA, MedMCQA, PubMedQA, ROCO, VQA-RAD) — so the paper is best read as an ML-systems reliability contribution with a medical application skin, not a clinical validation study.

## Critical Assessment

- **The headline natural-discrepancy number rests on a single model.** RQ1 (the unperturbed 25.52%/45.82% flip rates that anchor the paper's real-world significance) is measured only for HuatuoGPT2-7B; the other three language models and all vision models are never tested for natural disagreement, so it is unknown whether 25-46% is typical or an outlier property of one checkpoint.
- **SR near 1.0 measures search power as much as fragility.** TDST reaching 100% SR for three of four LLMs is partly a consequence of an effectively unbounded gradient-guided search against a razor-thin top-2 margin; there is no random-perturbation or fixed-budget control showing that gradient guidance (or the invisible suffix at all) is necessary, so SR cannot be read as deployment risk prevalence.
- **The HuggingFace reference is an arbitrary oracle.** The asymmetric success condition (reference must be correct, distributed must differ) treats centralized single-GPU execution as truth, but the paper never runs an fp32/CPU oracle to determine which execution is actually closer to exact arithmetic — "inconsistency" is symmetric, yet the benchmark's success definition is not.
- **The mitigation is real but partial and half-evaluated.** Qwen2.5-VL-7B on vLLM remains at 0.86 SR after targeted float32 elevation (vs 0.93 before), and mitigation is never attempted on the language track despite TDST results opening the paper's motivation; the claim that targeted elevation is cheaper than global precision increase is asserted without any latency/memory/throughput measurement, and the effect on ordinary task accuracy (does fp32 rotary embedding change benchmark accuracy?) is unreported.
- **Ecological validity gap.** An L∞=0.03 adversarial image or an invisible Unicode suffix will never occur in a clinic; these are excellent canaries for boundary fragility, but the clinical-risk rhetoric ("a different confidence shift can become a different medical prediction") leans on the one-model RQ1 experiment while most of the benchmark measures artificially searched boundary cases.
- **Reproducibility details are thin:** no code or data release is mentioned, GPU model, framework versions, per-model test-set sizes, and the step budget T are unspecified, which will hamper independent replication of the 16-pair mitigation table.

## Strengths

- The paired design is clean and genuinely isolates the execution stack — a well-executed operationalization of an evaluation-deployment mismatch that the serving-systems literature discusses but medical-model evaluation has largely ignored.
- The invisible-token construction in TDST (tokenizer-ID-stable, visually imperceptible, and explicitly represented in the token sequence) is a clever, reusable primitive for testing inference engines on text models.
- Breadth with discipline: 9 models across three modalities, 4 serving frameworks, 2/4-GPU configurations, with both success rate and search cost reported — Table 1's SR-vs-steps matrix is informative (e.g., BLIP2: high SR at 16-24 steps; Qwen2.5-VL on TensorRT-LLM: 0.47 SR at 156 steps).
- The paper closes the loop from phenomenon (RQ1) to test generation (RQ2/RQ3) to mechanistic localization (rotary embedding and normalization layers amplify divergence) to a targeted, minimal-change mitigation — more actionable than a pure measurement study.

## Limitations

- Single-node only (2/4 GPUs); no multi-node tensor/pipeline parallelism, no quantized serving configurations (INT8/AWQ/GPTQ), batching, or speculative decoding — precisely the aggressive-optimization settings where numerical divergence is worst in practice.
- Natural (unperturbed) discrepancy is quantified for one model only; SR is not calibrated against random-perturbation or fixed-budget baselines.
- Mitigation is evaluated exclusively on the visual track, leaves large residuals (Qwen2.5-VL on vLLM: 0.86), and comes with no cost or accuracy-side-effect measurements.
- No fp32 ground-truth oracle to attribute which execution is "more correct"; the reference-vs-deployment asymmetry is a modeling choice, not a validated ground truth.
- Public, small-scale benchmarks (VQA-RAD is a few hundred questions) and unspecified per-model test-set sizes limit statistical granularity; no code release stated.

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the 25-46% natural flip rate generalize across models, or is HuatuoGPT2-7B an outlier? Which execution (HuggingFace or DeepSpeed) is closer to an fp32/CPU oracle on the flipped cases — i.e., is deployment ever *more* correct?
- Would random perturbations of matched L∞ magnitude achieve comparable SR, or is gradient guidance (and the invisible suffix) essential? A fixed-budget ablation would calibrate what SR actually means.
- How does targeted float32 elevation compare against full fp32 serving in accuracy, latency, and memory, and does elevating `self.rotary_emb`/`self.norm` shift ordinary task accuracy at all?
- Do the critical-layer sets transfer across inputs (one profiled set per model/framework) or must they be re-estimated per workload? Can they be predicted from architecture alone?
- How does the phenomenon interact with quantized serving (INT8/AWQ) and multi-node tensor parallelism, where precision loss and communication reduction compound?
