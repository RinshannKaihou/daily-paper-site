01

Method — from a bound to a noise schedule

i.

Formalize

A policy is (δ, ε, β)-probabilistically perturbation robust when Pr(J(π̃) ≥ J(π) − ε) ≥ 1−β for perturbations of magnitude ≤ δ — a high-probability guarantee, not worst-case. Long-horizon agents make this strict: one corrupted step can derail a whole trajectory.

ii.

Theorize

TRPO's monotonic-improvement bound, extended: an update is stable only if expected improvement m_ν clears the perturbation variance slack √((1−β)ν_ν/β). Gaussian Poincaré yields the design rule — put less noise on sensitive channels. Theorem 2: limit points of stable updates stay robust.

iii.

Inject

SPrPO multiplies FFN hidden channels by Gaussian noise, h̃=(1+ξ)h, with per-channel std σ_c = ρ·m̄^α·Ŝ_c^−α (ρ=0.20, α=1). Sensitivity Ŝ is the squared gradient of the PPO surrogate w.r.t. the channel gain; the grad-norm m̄ schedules noise upward during training.

iv.

Evaluate

Qwen2.5-1.5B + LoRA (64/64) + GiGPO, 200 steps, "no-thinking" action format, vLLM at T=0.4. Five perturbation families × magnitudes × 3 seeds on ALFWorld (274 tasks, 134 OOD) and WebShop (500). Sensitivity stats cost one extra forward/backward pass — no extra rollouts.

03

Verdict — strengths vs limitations

Strengths

  • Genuinely new framing: deployment-time compression treated as a property of the learned policy, fixed during RL training — not a post-hoc afterthought.
  • Coherent theory-to-algorithm pipeline: Corollary 1's variance decomposition motivates sensitivity weighting; the α=0 ablation confirms it empirically.
  • Unusually careful evaluation: 5 families × magnitudes × 2 environments × ID/OOD × 3 seeds, with a shared 128-sequence calibration set.
  • Retention under Gaussian noise roughly doubles on WebShop (59.2% vs 33.9%), with clean-accuracy gains and honest cost accounting as bonuses.

Limitations

  • One 1.5B model, two text-only environments, reasoning traces disabled — nothing tested at 7B+ or for reasoning agents.
  • Fails or ties in the extreme regime (INT4) — precisely where deployment-time compression pressure is highest.
  • ~1.7–1.8× training-time overhead from the sensitivity-estimation replay pass.
  • Guarantees are asymptotic with unverifiable assumptions; ρ and α are grid-searched, not derived from the bound.
  • Code unreleased ("upon acceptance"); robustness only probed against noise structurally similar to the training family.
