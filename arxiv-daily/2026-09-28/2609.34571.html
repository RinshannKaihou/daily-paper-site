Why this matters

激活空间的内在几何决定干预是否有效 —— dg/dE > 1.2 是任何做激活转向 / 表征干预研究都可直接复用的决策阈值。

Intrinsic geometry decides whether activation interventions work — packaged with a transferable decision rule (dg/dE > 1.2) for steering research, at ~15 min one-time CPU cost per model.

Critical edge

Proven only on 7–8B models — exactly the kind of geometric claim that could change at 70B+. And the +5–6 pt margin was measured on triplets curated for the sparse regions where geodesics win (3× separation, hardest-20% positives).

GPT-5.2 also sits inside the evaluation loop — it generated the probe rephrasings, the BST questions and the steered dialogues being scored. Near-flat pairs gain just +1–1.5%.

Methodology · distilled

01

Extract persona vectors

≈2K personas (1.5K PersonaHub + 500 CoSER literary characters) × 40 situational probes. Persona vector = mean last-token residual activation minus neutral baseline, at one validation-chosen layer (12–16); PCA compresses ~4096 → 200–500 dims.

02

Measure the geometry

Intrinsic dimension d* = 15–23 by Marchenko-Pastur + Levina-Bickel consensus. Local metric tensor from k-NN covariance eigenpairs (rare directions count as “farther”); Ollivier-Ricci curvature per node. Density-quintile decoupling (p < 0.001, ρ = 0.08 n.s.) shows the curvature heterogeneity is intrinsic, not a sampling artifact — professional personas curve positive, fictional/rare-trait ones negative.

03

Steer along geodesics

Dijkstra shortest path on the k-NN graph → cubic-spline smoothing in local tangent spaces → 10 equal-arc-length waypoints → norm-preserving re-injection into the residual stream. When the manifold is locally flat (dg/dE ≈ 1), it degrades to linear interpolation automatically.

04

Validate on BST

New benchmark: 150 discriminativeness-filtered situational questions over six psychological constructs, scoring similarity by observed behavior, not self-report. 10K triplets at ≥3/5 human agreement; 86.1% human–system consistency (Fleiss κ = 0.81); a choice-only TCR variant is embedding-free.

Compute: 4×A100-80GB · ~45 min extraction + ~15 min geometry per model (CPU) · <1 s per steering query.

Strengths

  • Falsifiable geometric claim with the right controls — density–curvature decoupling (Kruskal-Wallis within quintiles) and bootstrap CIs on every headline number.
  • Factor-separation baseline suite attributes gains to named geometric factors instead of one “ours beats all” table — an exemplary design pattern.
  • Honest reporting throughout: states where SLERP and Graph-NN Chain win, and defines a dg/dE > 1.2 applicability threshold rather than claiming universal superiority.
  • BST is carefully built: six established psychological constructs, discriminativeness filtering, 86.1% human–system agreement (κ = 0.81), plus a circularity-free choice-only variant and a 50-triplet disagreement autopsy.
  • Cross-architecture consistency (three independent pretraining pipelines) and an inverted-U layer sweep peaking at 12–16 support the “general property” reading — cheap to adopt: <1 s per query at inference.

Limitations

  • 7–8B models only; the manifold claim is untested at 70B+, and O(N²) geometry estimation would need approximate-neighbor methods at larger persona sets.
  • One mean activation vector per persona — the geometry characterized is that of persona centroids, discarding intra-persona variation across conversational contexts.
  • GPT-5.2 is entangled with evaluation (probes, BST questions, steered dialogues); IC/TS remain embedding-mediated with no human validation of IC itself — human eval covered naturalness only, on 100 dialogues.
  • Triplet curation may inflate the margin: hard positives with 3× behavioral separation plausibly favor sparse regions — precisely where geodesics win; no uniformly-sampled-triplet breakdown.
  • Benefit is uneven by construction: negligible for ~40–50% of near-flat pairs, and BST covers only six constructs with its open-ended half riding on MiniLM embeddings.

Questions for further exploration

  1. Q1Does the curved structure and d* ∈ [15, 23] persist at 70B+ scale — and does the dg/dE > 1.2 threshold shift with model size?
  2. Q2Would modeling intra-persona variation (a distribution on the manifold, not one mean point) sharpen the geometry and multi-context steering?
  3. Q3Can geodesic steering transfer beyond personas — to styles, stances, refusal behavior — where the same flat-interpolation failures are reported?
  4. Q4Is the TCR advantage robust on uniformly sampled triplets without the d(a,n) ≥ 3·d(a,p) curation constraint?
  5. Q5Do negative-curvature “isolated” regions of fictional personas correspond to interpretable circuits — can curvature flag out-of-distribution personas?
  6. Q6Would a stronger or behaviorally-tuned response encoder (vs all-MiniLM-L6-v2) change the embedding-based half of BST?
