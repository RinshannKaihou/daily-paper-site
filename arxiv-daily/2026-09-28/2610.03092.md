> **一句话总结：** 提出 UltraDiscovery——一个受《星际拓荒》启发的五域互联"认知开放"交互式发现世界（同一潜在世界做 2×2 因子设计：Open/Disclosed 表征 × Distributed/Aligned 证据；18 个认知块、4 条超边、3 种目标表征改变 RC1 撤销公理 / RC2 假设隐含实体 / RC3 改写定律变量），对 11 个前沿模型（GPT-5.6 三档、Claude Opus 5/Sonnet 5、Gemini 3.1 Pro/3.6 Flash、DeepSeek V4 Pro/Flash、GLM-5.2、Kimi K3）×4 变体×12 配对实例（512 个有效回合）的系统评测表明：基线循环中模型平均在 46.1% 的回合撤销被教授的公理，但**没有任何**模型在 200 步预算内提出隐含实体或改写变量、更无人做出精确迁移预测；披露表征使干预请求从 5.0 猛增到 14.8 次/回合却只多 grounding 约 1 个发现（Δ_repr=+1.0 [0.8,1.2]），证据对齐（Aligned）带来 +0.5 blocks [0.3,0.7]；三种"自进化"脚手架（self-critique / falsification / evolution）增益全部不显著（≤+0.26，置信区间均跨零），只有厂商 harness 系统 Claude Fable 5.1 在 600 步+双重辅助下完成唯一一次精确预测——"拒绝旧理论"易，"构造替代表征"难。

> **TL;DR:** The paper introduces UltraDiscovery, an interactive abductive-discovery environment (five interconnected domains over one latent mechanism, 18 epistemic blocks, 4 hyperedges, 3 target representation changes) compiled into a 2×2 factorial that varies representational openness (Open vs Disclosed) and evidence organization (Distributed vs Aligned) independently within the same latent world. Evaluating 11 frontier models × 4 variants × 12 paired instances (512 counted episodes) plus 3 scaffolds (self-critique, falsification, evolution) and 2 vendor-harness systems (Claude Fable 5.1/Claude Code, GPT-6 Astra/Codex), it finds agents are good at rejecting the taught axiom (retracted in 46.1% of Open episodes on average) but none constructs its replacement (no RC2 entity postulation or RC3 variable rewriting in the baseline loop) and no system makes the exact cross-domain prediction within 200 paid actions. Disclosure triples intervention requests (5.0 → 14.8/episode) yet adds only Δ_repr = +1.0 grounded blocks [0.8, 1.2]; alignment adds +0.5 blocks [0.3, 0.7]; all three scaffolds gain ≤ +0.26 blocks with CIs crossing zero; only Claude Fable 5.1 makes one exact prediction, at 600 actions under both aids (vs 7.6 vs 3.5 blocks for vendor vs eleven models on the same instances).

## 核心贡献

- **把"表征构造"从发现度量中分离出来**：现有交互式科学发现基准（ScienceAgentBench、DiscoveryWorld、DiscoveryBench、NewtonBench、ARC-AGI-3 等）通常预先给定实体/变量/假设空间；UltraDiscovery 在**同一个潜在世界**内用 2×2 因子设计把"表征是否开放"（Open：智能体自行构造本体；Disclosed：附一份文档说明实体种类、状态迁移与良构定律的形式）与"证据是否跨域互联"（Distributed：证据留在产生它的仪器处；Aligned：跨域时间对齐转录贴在用到处，但一次性组装需 ≥12 个付费动作）解耦为两个可分别标价的"负担"（grant Δ_repr / Δ_align）。
- **一个必须被"重新描述"的世界**：五艘废弃播种船（fen 放牧、trellis 印刷、ladder 信标、consign 货运、registry 登记）由同一潜在机制生成——36 个物体携带隐藏的四坐标标签（字母表 3,3,4,4），配额（每个体每 tick 一动作）、固定调度改写、单调变换读数；世界每 24 tick 重置，唯智能体笔记持久。认知结构显式化：18 个认知块（15 域内 + 3 跨域桥）、3 条超边 H1–H3（各汇三域证据、许可一种表征改变：RC1 撤销公理 / RC2 假设从未渲染的实体 / RC3 改写定律变量）、HC 封闭超边通向终局预测。
- **可验证的评测机器**：证据侧由 18 个确定性事件日志检测器（bracket/span/series/pairing/cross-pass contrast 等模式）零模型参与判定；陈述侧由 LLM 裁判（Qwen3.8-27B，温度 0，21 条判据）对照引文判定，与 51 份人工参考标注的 Cohen's κ = 0.883（陈述）/0.751（grounding）；指标包括 Blocks、Discovery AUC（grounding 发生的时刻）、XC（跨域桥）、RC（表征改变）、Reach、Interventions 与终局"精确预测"迁移测试。
- **罕见的发现基准验证套件**：39 个付费动作的脚本化参考路线证明四变体中全部 18 块证据可达；先验知识探针（不做任何观测直接提交 standing order：0/120 通过，7 种猜测 vs 106 种答案）；全记录推理探针（把整条路线 166 个事件塞进一个 prompt）：0/30；捷径探针逐一测量每个通道泄漏率 ≤1%。
- **一组清晰的负面/纠偏结论**：模型"拒绝而不替换"（46.1% 回合撤销公理、基线循环中 RC2/RC3 为零）；披露表征改变探究方式（干预请求 5.0→14.8）甚于提升发现（+1.0 blocks）；三种流行的自进化脚手架几乎不增加 grounded 发现（全部 CI 跨零）；预算（600 步）+双辅助才能换来唯一一次精确预测。

## Key Contributions

- **A 2×2 factorial that isolates the two burdens of abductive discovery**: representational openness (Open: the agent constructs entities, variables and laws from raw instrument readings; Disclosed: an ontology document supplies the frame) × evidence organization (Distributed: records stay at their instruments; Aligned: time-aligned cross-domain transcripts posted at point of use) — varied independently within one authored latent world, unlike prior benchmarks that supply or conflate them.
- **An epistemically explicit universe**: five domains (fen grazing, trellis printing, beacon ladder, consign floor, registry) generated by one hidden mechanism (4-coordinate labels, per-individual quota, scheduled rewrites, monotone reading transforms), with 18 epistemic blocks, 3 hyperedges each licensing one representation change (RC1 axiom retraction, RC2 unobserved-entity postulation, RC3 re-representation of law variables), and a terminal transfer test (commit one unseen cross-domain intervention, predict 3 exact readings).
- **A validated measurement apparatus**: deterministic behavioral detectors for evidence, a calibrated LLM judge for statements (Qwen3.8-27B, κ = 0.883/0.751 vs 51 reference annotations; 91/96 agreement on vendor-harness RC verdicts), Discovery AUC over paid actions, plus a validation suite (39-action reference route; prior-knowledge probe 0/120; full-record inference probe 0/30; per-channel shortcut probes ≤1%).
- **A reproducible negative result about self-improving scaffolds**: self-critique, falsification, and evolution move grounded discovery by less than half a block each (+0.23/+0.26/+0.18, all CIs crossing zero), while falsification demonstrably changes inquiry (no-intervention episodes fall 18/39 → 5/39) at ~20× the auxiliary-call cost — separation of "organizing inquiry" from "making discovery".

## Methodology

**World.** UltraDiscovery is one authored latent world: five derelict seed-ship hulks holding 15 sites across five work domains, 36 objects carrying hidden four-coordinate labels (alphabets 3, 3, 4, 4) drawn from a shared mechanism. Identity is label equality; each individual performs exactly one act per tick (quota) via a queue with a fixed rotation; caretakers rewrite labels on a fixed schedule at ticks 3, 8, 13, 17, 20; instruments show monotonically transformed readings of latent state. The world resets every 24-tick pass; only the agent's free-text notes persist. The agent acts through 8 verbs (goto, read, wait; feed, release, stamp, retool, cull) under a two-clock budget: 200 paid actions per episode. The prologue teaches an exact maintenance doctrine ("Doctrine of Standing and Share"); every pass ends in a stall it cannot explain inside its own vocabulary. The terminal standing order, posted with 10 actions left, asks the agent to commit one never-attempted cross-domain intervention and three exact integer readings — a transfer test of the constructed account.

**Design.** Four variants are compiled from the same latent source, differing in two declared surfaces: *Open* (no representation granted) vs *Disclosed* (a fixed document stating the identification, quota/rotation, per-verb coordinate moves, and admissible law forms — the "grant" G); *Distributed* (every record stays at its instrument, read live) vs *Aligned* (three transcripts of the previous pass posted at sites of use; assembling the same series live costs ≥12 extra actions). Open×Distributed is the primary condition where both burdens rest on the agent.

**Metrics.** A block is *grounded* when the agent produced its evidence in-world (deterministic event-log detector) and stated the inference in notes (LLM judge with quotations). Metrics: Blocks (0–18), Discovery AUC (grounding time over T=200 paid actions), XC (0–3 grounded cross-domain bridges), RC (0–3 stated representation changes; in Disclosed conditions it reads uptake of the supplied representation), Reach (largest number of one hyperedge's sources held), Evidence, Interventions, and the exact prediction (all 3 committed readings equal the true ones). The judge is Qwen3.8-27B at temperature zero over 21 criteria, calibrated against 51 reference annotations (Cohen's κ = 0.883 statement, 0.751 grounding; the alternative Qwen3.8-Max scored worse at 0.609 grounding).

**Validation.** A scripted reference route reaches the terminal readings in 39 paid actions in every variant, and Open episodes of the main comparison collectively hold the evidence of all 18 blocks at least once (reachability). Prior-knowledge probes show a model given only what an agent holds before its first observation commits the exact prediction in 0 of 120 instances (7 distinct guesses vs 106 answers), and a full-record inference probe given the route's entire 166-event record scores 0 of 30 — no shortcut via prior knowledge, format, or surface answers.

**Evaluation protocol.** Eleven frontier/open-weight models (GPT-5.6 Sol/Terra/Luna; Claude Opus 5, Sonnet 5; Gemini 3.1 Pro, 3.6 Flash; DeepSeek V4 Pro/Flash; GLM-5.2; Kimi K3) each play the four variants on twelve paired instances (512 counted episodes), with paired bootstrap CIs on all contrasts (Δ_repr per column, Δ_align per row). Five models are additionally run under three scaffolds that organize inquiry — self-critique (Reflexion-style), falsification (rival accounts with predicted outcomes screening the next test), and evolution (population of candidate mechanisms retrodicting events) — with the scaffold's region removed before notes are scored. Two vendor-harness systems (Claude Fable 5.1 High in Claude Code; GPT-6 Astra High in Codex) play beside the controlled comparison, and budget extensions to 600 (vendor systems) and 2000 (GPT-5.6 Sol) paid actions probe whether budget rather than scaffolding binds.

## Results & Highlights

- **Rejection without replacement (baseline loop, Open×Distributed).** Agents retract the taught axiom (RC1) in **46.1% of a model's episodes on average**, yet **no** model postulates the unobserved entity (RC2) or rewrites the variables (RC3); discovery is thin throughout — in the primary condition agents hold evidence of **5.0 of 18 blocks** and ground **2.9**. Best of the eleven, Claude Opus 5 grounds **6.7 blocks** (AUC 25.1) vs GPT-5.6 Sol's 3.7. Of 512 counted episodes only **11 hold the complete evidence of any hyperedge and 6 state its change**.
- **Disclosure changes inquiry more than discovery.** With distributed evidence, disclosure raises intervention requests from **5.0 to 14.8 per episode** but adds only **Δ_repr = +1.0 grounded blocks [0.8, 1.2]** — "about one of the eighteen findings the world affords." Disclosed notes state **2.22 of the three changes on average**, yet only **3 of 261 Disclosed episodes** hold the complete evidence of any hyperedge: uptake of vocabulary substitutes for evidence-gathering.
- **Alignment lowers the cost of access.** Aligned transcripts raise evidence held by **+0.6 blocks [0.4, 0.7]**, Blocks by **Δ_align = +0.5 [0.3, 0.7]** and XC by **+0.17 [0.07, 0.27]**, leave intervention requests unchanged (+0.3 [−0.7, 1.2]), and the two aids are approximately additive (interaction +0.2 [−0.2, 0.5]).
- **Scaffolds organize inquiry but barely discover.** Pooled over five models and both Open conditions, blocks move by **+0.23** (self-critique, [−0.08, 0.55]), **+0.26** (falsification, [−0.03, 0.56]) and **+0.18** (evolution, [−0.10, 0.50]) — all indistinguishable from zero. Falsification does change behavior: episodes with no intervention request fall from **18 of 39 to 5 of 39** (self-critique/evolution stay near baseline), at about **twenty times** self-critique's auxiliary calls. Across **118 scaffolded episodes, 3** hold a hyperedge's complete evidence and **1** states its change.
- **Vendor-harness systems extend, budget binds.** Claude Fable 5.1 and GPT-6 Astra ground **7.6 and 7.1 blocks** per Open episode vs **3.5** for the eleven models on the same instances; Claude Fable 5.1 rewrites the variables (RC3) in **5 of 8** Open episodes. Extending budgets to 600 actions, complete hyperedge evidence appears in **7 of 12** extended episodes (vs 3 of 9 at 200 on that instance), and **one exact prediction** is made — by Claude Fable 5.1 in Disclosed×Aligned, the only one across all systems; at the 200-action contract budget **no system makes the exact prediction** in any condition.

The five authors span the University of Tokyo (Weihan Li, Department of Mechanical Engineering), HKUST (Tianshi Zheng, Yangqiu Song — an established NLP/LLM group with prior work on LLM-based scientific discovery, e.g., the same group's survey "From Automation to Autonomy" at EMNLP 2025), and the NVIDIA AI Technology Center (Ginny Y. Wong, Simon See), which has a track record in HPC/AI infrastructure. The paper is unusually careful for an agentic benchmark: a validation appendix (reachability route, prior-knowledge and shortcut probes), two candidate judges with reported κ, deterministic evidence detectors, paired instances with bootstrap CIs, and an explicit commitment to release the complete world code, judge prompts, and every episode's event log and notes. The cultural grounding (Outer Wilds) is acknowledged with references to the game and its community. Credibility: high for measurement hygiene; the design choices are documented to a level where independent replication is plausible.

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty is real but the "science" is bounded.** The 2×2 separation of representational openness from evidence organization is a genuinely new axis — no prior agentic-discovery benchmark varies these independently inside one latent world (ScienceAgentBench constrains solutions to predefined hypothesis families; DiscoveryWorld/NewtonBench supply the interface). But "epistemic openness" here is bounded openness: the three target representation changes (RC1–RC3) are enumerated in advance, the hidden mechanism is a small deterministic automaton (4-coordinate labels + quota + rotation), and 15 of 18 blocks are within single domains — this is system-identification puzzle-solving with a narrative skin, closer to Outer Wilds (as the authors happily admit) than to open-ended scientific inquiry where the frame space itself is unbounded.

**Rigor is above average for the genre, with two soft spots.** First, the LLM judge: statement verdicts rest on Qwen3.8-27B whose grounding agreement is κ = 0.751 — and notably the larger Qwen3.8-Max scored *worse* (0.609), so judge noise is not a scale-away problem; 22 of 51 pairs show over/under-crediting. The deterministic detector leg is solid, but RC statements (the paper's headline construct) are judge-scored. Second, the scaffold comparison is underpowered at the observed base rates: with only 3 complete hyperedges and 1 stated change across 118 scaffolded episodes, all three scaffold CIs necessarily straddle zero — the honest conclusion is "no detectable effect at this sample size," which is weaker evidence than "scaffolds don't help." The falsification behavior claim (18/39 → 5/39 no-intervention episodes) rests on 39 episodes per arm.

**Confounds the authors acknowledge but don't remove.** The vendor-harness rows (Claude Fable 5.1, GPT-6 Astra) differ from the loop in model *and* harness simultaneously, so the striking 7.6/7.1 vs 3.5 gap and the lone exact prediction cannot be attributed to either factor; and the consign domain shows floor effects (a consign block grounded in only 8.0% of main-comparison episodes, 2 of 16 vendor episodes), compressing the achievable metric range.

**Reproducibility looks good on paper**: full code, four variants, judge prompts, transcripts and event logs promised; results were measured before the mechanism and instances were disclosed, and the probes (0/120 prior-knowledge, 0/30 full-record inference) credibly rule out leakage. The main untestable claim is external validity — one authored world, 12 instances of it; the authors themselves note that worlds built by others and human-volunteer runs are needed to calibrate difficulty.

**Significance.** The durable contributions are the factorial design pattern and the corrected intuition: disclosure budgets shift *inquiry* (interventions 5.0 → 14.8) more than *discovery* (+1.0 blocks), and self-evolving scaffolds add little grounded discovery under a fixed action budget — a useful caution for the automated-research-agent literature, where scaffold gains are usually measured on benchmarks whose representations are disclosed by construction.

## Strengths

- Clean causal isolation: the same latent world, seeds, and scoring across all four cells, with paired instances and bootstrap CIs on every contrast — the Δ_repr / Δ_align decomposition is exactly the ablation agentic benchmarks usually omit.
- Deterministic evidence scoring (18 behavioral detectors on the event log) limits LLM-judge exposure to the statement leg, and both judge candidates are reported with κ rather than one favorable number.
- A validation suite that actively hunts leakage (reachability route, prior-knowledge probe, full-record inference probe, per-channel shortcut probes) — rare and commendable in this space.
- Honest, precisely-scoped claims: failures are reported as findings (retraction without replacement; untested repairs; 0 exact predictions at 200 actions), and the limitations section concedes the single-world scope.
- Actionable negative result for the self-evolving-agent community: three popular scaffolds, fixed budget, ~20× auxiliary-call cost for falsification, ≈ zero grounded-discovery gain.

## Limitations

- One authored world and twelve instances of it: whether the two-burden separation, the 46.1% retraction rate, or the scaffold nulls generalize to other (especially procedurally generated) worlds is untested; no human baseline calibrates difficulty.
- The headline construct (representation changes) is scored by an LLM judge with grounding κ = 0.751 (and the larger judge worse at 0.609); misgrounding of statements is the least reliable measurement in the pipeline.
- Scaffold conclusions are statistically fragile (118 scaffolded episodes, 3 complete hyperedges, CIs crossing zero) and the falsification inquiry-behavior claim uses 39 episodes per arm.
- Vendor-harness results confound harness and model, and are reported beside rather than inside the controlled comparison.
- Frontier-flagship-only roster of 2026 models with vendor-locked harnesses — the ranking content will age quickly; the consign domain's floor effects (8.0% grounding) compress the top of the metric range.
- No cost-normalized headline: auxiliary scaffold calls are noted but model-token accounting per grounded block is not reported.

## Relevance

这篇论文正中你的第一优先方向（Self-Evolving Agents & Automated Research）：它给出一个评测自主科研智能体的受控发现环境，并证明流行的自进化脚手架（self-critique/falsification/evolution）在固定行动预算下对"构造性发现"几乎没有增益、而披露假设空间会显著改变智能体的探究行为——对你评估和设计自我改进 agent 回路与自动化基准都直接可用。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Do the 46.1% retraction-without-replacement rate and the scaffold nulls persist in procedurally generated world families, or are they artifacts of one authored mechanism?
- Is the binding constraint the 200-action budget (600 actions unlocked one exact prediction) or the absence of cross-domain memory abstractions in the minimal loop — would an explicit hypothesis-space search over frames (program-synthesis style, e.g., DreamCoder-like) close the RC2/RC3 gap that no baseline-loop model closed?
- Can the statement leg of scoring be made fully deterministic (structured re-descriptions instead of free-text notes), removing the κ = 0.751 judge noise from the headline construct?
- How would the Δ_repr / Δ_align decomposition look on real scientific workbenches (DiscoveryWorld, ScienceAgentBench) where the "grant" is a domain ontology rather than a designed document?
- Does falsification's inquiry shift (18/39 → 5/39 no-intervention episodes) eventually convert into discovery at budgets beyond 600 actions, or does it remain behavior-only?
