> **一句话总结：** 提出 CoEvolve，把原则演化（principle-evolution）式科学发现从单分支串行循环扩展为 K 个并行分支之间的"证据迁移"：一个协调核按 Collect–Merge–Score–Route–Inject 循环运转，用"熵减减去迁移成本"给跨分支观测记录打分、用校准置信半径门控准入、用上下文似然折扣 α∈[0,1] 注入目标分支后验（各分支后验始终独立）；在 6 个科学发现基准、72 次评测预算下，平均解质量 66.5% vs 单分支 PiEvo 的 57.0%，并取得 1.80× 墙钟加速（token 转化率 0.97× 近乎打平），且是在 5 个自主研究 harness 任务上唯一均值全面越过已发表 SOTA anchor 的系统（+16.7% vs PiEvo 的 +5.8%）。

> **TL;DR:** CoEvolve parallelizes principle-evolution scientific discovery (built directly on the authors' own PiEvo) across K=3 concurrent branches that are coupled only by a coordination core performing gated, discounted evidence transfer — branch posteriors and search histories stay private, and each import enters a target posterior at a learned context-likelihood discount α. On 6 scientific-optimization benchmarks (MBO, AMP, Promoter, NHO, TMC, SPO) under a fixed 72-query budget on Gemma-4-31B-IT and GPT-5.6-Terra, it reaches 66.5% mean Solution Quality vs 57.0% for single-branch PiEvo (+11%–23%; +6.6–9.1% over the strongest non-self baseline InternAgent-1.5), with a 1.80× mean wall-clock speedup at a near-parity 0.97× token conversion ratio. Ported onto an autonomous research harness (Claude Code/Codex backbone, 5 AutoresearchEval tasks with sealed graders), it is the only arm whose mean beats the published SOTA anchor on every task (+16.7% vs PiEvo +5.8%). Ablations show removing import discounting costs the most (−19.0 SQ on MBO), more than sharing itself (−13.6) — the safeguards, not parallelism alone, carry the gain.

## 核心贡献

- **提出"原则共演化"（principle co-evolution）问题形式化**（Definition 1.1/3.1）：K≥2 个分支各自独立做原则演化，仅通过一个共享的证据池耦合；接口严格限定为"证据对象"（记录 C = (i, h, y, E_i, m)，含来源上下文与元数据），各分支后验与搜索历史互相不可见——把"多智能体协同科研"从提示层协作下沉到记录级协议。
- **CoEvolve 协调核**：每轮 Collect（收集候选记录）→ Merge（去重入库）→ Score（V̂ = 期望熵减 − λ·迁移成本，在有限原则宇宙上精确枚举计算）→ Route（校准门控 V̂ − r > η 才准入，按单位迁移成本的认证价值排序，配额 B=3/轮/分支 + token-Jaccard 0.3 冗余上限）→ Inject（按折扣 α 乘入目标分支似然，式 (5) 的广义后验更新）。
- **理论保证**（Thm 3.2 / 附录 A 四定理）：折扣导入下的后验集中（导入只按折扣权重计数）、负迁移控制（接受净价值≤η 的有害导入的概率被校准预算 Σδ 界住）、并行覆盖（信息积累率 ∝ K，仅被冗余因子折扣）、发现时间 hazard 尾界 exp(−tΣλ_j)；附录 C 逐条把定理映射到可测量的实验签名。
- **系统评测**：6 个科学发现任务 × 8 个基线（Vanilla MAS、AI Scientist v1/v2、AI Researcher、EvoScientist、InternAgent-1.5、PiFlow、PiEvo）× 2 个 backbone；另将 CoEvolve 移植到 Claude Code/Codex 驱动的自主研究 harness 上，对 5 个带已发表 SOTA anchor 和封存评分器的真实研究任务评测；消融覆盖共享/协调/折扣/门控/冗余控制五个层面。

## Key Contributions

- **Formalizes collaborative scientific discovery as evidence transfer** (Definition 1.1/3.1): K≥2 branches run independent principle evolution over a shared Universal Principle Space, coupled exclusively through a record-level protocol — an accepted import enters the target branch as a discounted likelihood factor, while each branch's posterior and search history remain private. This moves multi-agent research coordination from prompt-level to record-level.
- **The CoEvolve coordination core** (COLLECT–MERGE–SCORE–ROUTE–INJECT): candidate records are scored by expected entropy reduction net of transfer cost (exactly enumerable over the finite principle universe), admitted only through a calibrated gate V̂ − r > η with a per-round quota B=3 and a token-Jaccard redundancy cap R_max=0.3, and injected via a context-likelihood discount α_{i→j}(h) ∈ [0,1] combining source accuracy, context match, and replication status.
- **Four theoretical guarantees** (Thm 3.2, Appendix A): posterior concentration under discounted imports, negative-transfer control (probability of accepting a harmful import bounded by the calibration budget Σδ), parallel coverage (information accumulation rate ∝ K discounted by a redundancy factor), and a discovery-time hazard tail exp(−tΣλ_j). Appendix C aligns each theorem with a measurable experimental signature.
- **Extensive evaluation**: 6 scientific tasks × 8 baselines × 2 backbones under a fixed 72-query budget, plus a port onto an autonomous research harness (Claude Code/Codex as executors) evaluated against 5 AutoresearchEval tasks with published SOTA anchors and sealed server-side graders; ablations isolate sharing, discounting, gating, and redundancy control.

## Methodology

**Setup.** Each branch j maintains a Bayesian posterior p_{j,t}(P) over a shared *finite* Universal Principle Space P (inherited from PiEvo). At round t a branch selects a hypothesis h from its space, evaluates it against the task validator, and the tested pair (h, y) is local evidence. After every cycle, the branch publishes a candidate record C = (i, h, y, E_i, m) carrying the source context E_i and metadata m to a shared append-only pool.

**Score.** The information value of a record for target branch j is the expected entropy reduction in j's principle posterior, net of the transfer cost: V_{j,t}(C) = E[H(p_{j,t}) − H(p̃⁺_{j,t})] − λ·Δ^imp_{j,t}(C) (Eq. 1). The cost combines fixed bookkeeping constants (parsing c_read, verification of unreplicated records c_verify, posterior refitting c_fit) with a context-shift surcharge scaled by (1 − s_{j,t})·c_shift_max, where s_{j,t} scores source-to-target context transferability. Because P is finite, V̂ is evaluated exactly by cloning the target posterior, applying the candidate at weight α, and measuring the induced entropy change — no sampling.

**Route.** Since V̂ is only an estimate, admission requires a calibrated radius r_{j,t}(C; δ): accept iff V̂ − r > η (Eq. 2). Candidates that pass are ranked by transfer cost per unit certified value (Eq. 3), admitted up to a per-branch quota B=3 per round, and filtered by a redundancy cap: drop if token-Jaccard similarity ≥ R_max=0.3 with an already-admitted record.

**Inject.** Branch contexts may be heterogeneous, so an import is valid for target j only through an explicit context-likelihood discount α_{i→j}(h) = clip(ρ_{i,t}(h)·s_{ij,t}(h)·υ_{i,t}(h), 0, 1) (Eq. 4) — source accuracy times context match times whether the observation was independently replicated. The target posterior multiplies its likelihood by α per accepted import (Eq. 5), a generalized-Bayes update: local evidence counts as a full sample, imports count fractionally.

**Theory.** Under a finite principle universe with positive prior: (a) the posterior log-contrast in favor of the true principle grows linearly in effective evidence count N^eff (locals weight 1, imports weight α); (b) with calibrated radii, the probability of accepting any import with net value ≤ η is bounded by the total calibration budget Σδ; (c) system information accumulates at rate ∝ K·γ_T·Σμ_j discounted by redundancy; (d) discovery-time tail decays as exp(−tΣ_j λ_j). Remark B.4 is candid that the gate's guarantee binds only if the radii are truly calibrated — under GP misspecification or residual context shift it remains "a conservative heuristic rather than a deployed guarantee."

**Experimental protocol.** Budget: 72 validator queries per run, 3 seeds, K=3 branches (24 queries each). Backbones: Gemma-4-31B-IT and GPT-5.6-Terra (non-thinking). Six tasks: Molecular Bio-activity Optimization (MBO), Antimicrobial Peptide Design (AMP), Promoter Expression Optimization, Nanohelice Optimization (NHO), Transition-Metal-Complex Design (TMC), Superconductor Critical-Temperature Optimization (SPO) — each with surrogate validators and standard validity gates. Harness regime: DeepSeek-v4-Flash-0731 as the coding backbone; arms are Claude Code, Codex, Arbor, PiEvo-as-strategy-layer, and CoEvolve (K=3 over PiEvo); all tasks ship a published SOTA anchor and a sealed grader; the 72-evaluation budget is enforced server-side and self-reported numbers are discarded.

## Results & Highlights

- **Best average solution quality on 6 scientific benchmarks.** Mean SQ 66.5% vs 57.0% for single-branch PiEvo (backbone means: 63.8% on Gemma-4-31B-IT vs PiEvo 59.1%; 69.6% on GPT-5.6-Terra vs PiEvo 57.0%). Relative to the strongest overall baseline, InternAgent-1.5, the gain is +6.6 to +9.1% (an 8%–20% margin) and 11%–23% over PiEvo; paired per-seed gains are positive in 15 of 18 task-seed pairs (sign test p<0.01, Wilcoxon p=0.001). On SPO, single-branch systems collapse to 0.0% (Vanilla MAS, AI Scientist v1) while CoEvolve sustains 15.3%–30.0%.
- **Wall-clock speedup at near-parity token cost (GPT-5.6-Terra).** 1.80× mean wall-clock speedup vs PiEvo (faster in 17 of 18 task-seed pairs, p<10⁻⁴); the equal-time quality gap ΔSQ@T_c averages +8.3 SQ points (positive in 15 of 18 pairs). The token premium is 1.06×–1.62× per task (mean ~1.29×) and the token conversion ratio SQ_CoEvolve/SQ_PiEvo per token is 0.89×–1.07× (mean 0.97×) — parallel branches buy wall-clock, not per-token efficiency.
- **Only system above the published SOTA anchor on all 5 autoresearch tasks.** Anchor-relative improvement Δ×100: CoEvolve +16.7% average (ALDE +6.8, Decoy +45.4, InvScat +11.9, D2D +15.5, MolEdit +4.1) vs PiEvo +5.8%, Arbor +9.6%, Codex +4.5%, Claude Code +3.4%; all five means above anchor (one-sample t, p<0.025 per task), and the worst CoEvolve case matches or exceeds every other arm's best case on all five tasks. The margin decomposes as PiEvo's stage guidance contributing only +0.4 pp vs the coordination core's +10.9 pp. Harness cost: 1.55× PiEvo's billable tokens for a 1.31× end-to-end wall-clock speedup.
- **Ablations: safeguards outweigh raw sharing.** On MBO (Table 6): removing import discounting costs −19.0 SQ (77.1→58.1), ahead of removing sharing itself (−13.6) and the redundancy cap (−9.6); removing the safe-Vol gate admits 3.3× more imports (42→137) with the mean shifting only −2.1 but the standard deviation doubling (4.6→11.1) — protection against rare harmful imports, not an average-case lever. Sharing vs isolated best-of-seed SQ: +13.6 (MBO), +14.3 (Promoter), +7.9 (AMP, p<0.05), +2.5 (SPO), +0.5 (NHO, TMC). The transfer flow is real but associational: 244 accepted imports, 205 net-unique, window-level uplift up to +43.3 SQ (TMC).

## Authors & Credibility

Yingming Pu, Hongyu Chen, and Tao Lin (corresponding) are at Zhejiang University and Westlake University (Hangzhou). This group built the direct lineage this paper extends: PiFlow (arXiv:2505.15047, principle-aware multi-agent discovery) and PiEvo (arXiv:2602.06448, uncertainty-minimizing principle evolution), plus prior ML-for-materials work on nanohelices (arXiv:2409.25295; Advanced Optical Materials 2025). The deep familiarity with PiEvo makes the delta analysis and efficiency metrics (paired speedup, equal-time gap, token conversion ratio) unusually careful, and the baseline suite includes strong third-party systems (InternAgent-1.5, EvoScientist, AI Scientist v1/v2). The main structural caveat is that the closest competitor, PiEvo, is their own prior system — the headline "66.5% vs 57.0%" is a self-improvement claim — though the harness regime's sealed graders, server-side budget enforcement, and pre-registered explorativeness ordering mitigate benchmarking bias. The paper's statistical honesty (disclosing that only 4/12 per-task Welch tests reach p<0.05 and only 2 survive Bonferroni) is well above field norms.

## Critical Assessment

- **The transfer mechanism is materially active on only ~2 of 6 tasks.** Table 7 shows accepted imports concentrate in MBO (120) and AMP (96); SPO sees 6, TMC 2, Promoter 1, and NHO essentially none. On NHO all arms saturate the same ceiling (94.4–95.0 SQ) and Independent nominally matches CoEvolve (95.0 vs 94.9) — the benefit there is convergence speed (1.5× faster, 120 vs 184 min), not quality. So the "evidence transfer" story genuinely explains the gains mainly on MBO/AMP; elsewhere the win is parallel coverage. The authors flag the uplift statistic as associational, not causal — honest, but the abstract's framing ("results establish when evidence sharing accelerates parallel discovery") rests on a mechanism that is idle for half the benchmark suite.
- **The negative-transfer guarantee is conditional on calibration the system does not actually provide.** Exact enumeration certifies the radii only when r=0 (numerical exactness); under GP misspecification or residual context shift — which is precisely the setting of LLM-driven discovery — Remark B.4 concedes the gate "remains a conservative heuristic rather than a deployed guarantee," and the cost constants (c_read=5e-4, c_verify=2.5e-4, c_fit=2.5e-4, c_shift_max=2e-2, Table 4) are hand-set. Theorem 3.2(b) is therefore more of a design rationale than a deployable certificate.
- **Statistical power is thin, and the paper says so.** Three seeds per cell; per-task Welch tests vs PiEvo reach p<0.05 in only 4 of 12 backbone-pairs, and after Bonferroni only SPO (Gemma, p=3×10⁻⁴) and NHO (Terra, p=0.004) survive. In the harness regime, paired differences vs PiEvo are underpowered (p=0.07–0.40), so the "+16.7% vs +5.8%" headline leans on interval arguments (worst-case dominance) rather than significance. The aggregate 18-pair paired tests (p<0.01) are the real evidence.
- **Token economics are break-even.** The 0.97× mean token conversion ratio means CoEvolve produces roughly the same SQ per token as PiEvo; the 1.80× speedup is concurrency (3 branches), not algorithmic efficiency — you pay for 3 concurrent agent runs. In the harness regime it is strictly more expensive (1.55× billable tokens for 1.31× wall-clock). For cost-bound users, the benefit is latency, not cost.
- **K=3 is not a principled default.** The branch-count response is landscape-dependent: inverted-U on MBO peaking at K=3 (77.1 SQ), flat on AMP, plateau on TMC — the paper itself notes the default "is therefore not a universal optimum," but offers no adaptive selection rule.
- **Reproducibility is promised, not delivered.** Appendix D: "Code, configuration files, and per-run logs will be released" — at v1 nothing is available, which matters given the many hand-set constants. Also, all six tasks use surrogate validators (closed-world); the harness tasks are closer to reality but still graded by sealed scripted scorers, not real experiments.

## Strengths

- Clean conceptual contribution: an evidence-only interface (records cross branches, posteriors and histories never do) that directly targets negative transfer, with the discount α giving a principled knob between full pooling and full isolation.
- Rare theory-experiment alignment discipline: Appendix C maps each of the four theorems to a measurable signature (wall-clock lead, discount knock-out, gate tail-protection, SPO hazard-diversity), and the signatures hold.
- Broad, strong baseline suite (8 systems including InternAgent-1.5 and EvoScientist, not just strawmen) across two backbones with a fixed query budget.
- Exemplary cost accounting and honesty: near-parity token premium disclosed prominently, Welch/Bonferroni failures reported, uplift labeled associational, sealed graders with server-side budget enforcement and self-reported numbers discarded in the harness regime.
- Thorough ablations (sharing vs coordination-only vs independent vs isolated; per-module knock-outs; per-hyperparameter sweeps pairing SQ with import volume) that isolate *why* the system wins.

## Limitations

- Three seeds per cell limit per-task significance; after Bonferroni correction only 2 of 12 task-backbone comparisons vs PiEvo remain significant.
- Evidence transfer is heavily concentrated on 2 of 6 scientific tasks (MBO, AMP); on saturated (NHO) or low-import tasks the gains come from parallelism or convergence speed, and the window-level uplift is associational by construction.
- The gate's guarantee requires calibrated value-estimate radii that are only heuristic constants under model misspecification; sensitivity to the hand-set cost constants (Table 4) is only partially explored (margin η shows no monotone response; η∈[0.01, +4.3±18.5] is within noise).
- Fixed K=3 with landscape-dependent returns; no adaptive rule for branch count or budget splitting.
- Latency win, not cost win: 0.97× token conversion ratio and a 1.55× billable-token spend in the harness regime.
- Closed-world surrogate validators only; no real-experiment (wet-lab/instrument) validation. Code not yet released at v1. In the harness regime, per-evaluation AUC trails PiEvo on MolEdit and ties PiEvo/Arbor on D2D within noise.

## Relevance

属于用户最高优先级方向 Self-Evolving Agents & Automated Research 的核心工作：直接研究多个自主科研智能体（AI Scientist）并行探索同一问题时如何共享证据而不互相污染后验，其负迁移控制、校准门控与折扣导入正是长程自主智能体系统可靠性的具体机制，与用户对 autonomous research loops 和 long-running agent reliability 的兴趣高度契合。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Under large genuine context shift between branches (different sub-domains), the calibrated radii reduce to heuristic constants — how far does the gate's negative-transfer control actually stretch before it must be learned rather than set?
- Can K and the per-branch budget be chosen adaptively online, given the landscape-dependent inverted-U response (MBO peak at K=3, flat AMP, TMC plateau)?
- Would evidence transfer remain beneficial when branches run heterogeneous backbone LLMs, where source accuracy ρ and context match s are estimated across models of different reliability?
- The 0.97× token conversion ratio means quality-per-token parity with single-branch PiEvo — does this hold at larger budgets, or with a cheap verifier model replacing exact enumeration of V̂ over the principle universe?
- How does the framework behave when replication υ is expensive or impossible (real experiments rather than surrogate validators), and does the uplift statistic become causal with branch-level randomization of import availability?
