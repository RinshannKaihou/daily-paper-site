# SpatialSkill: Self-Evolving Skills for Cross-View Spatial Reasoning

> **一句话总结：** 提出一种免权重更新（frozen-VLM）的自进化技能框架 SpatialSkill：用 Qwen3.5-9B 作为固定的反思模型，从离线推理轨迹中抽取自然语言"技能手册"，经视觉接地 + 可执行性双重准入门控、增长预算/资历保护/probe 分数棘轮三重稳定性控制、以及按推理类别（CR/MR/PT/SR/WK）的路由部署，在 CityCube 基准上使 Qwen3.5-4B 从 47.16% 提升到 52.08%（+4.92），四个执行器平均 +3.41 个百分点，其中 Qwen3.5-9B+SpatialSkill 达 57.58%，超过其评测中最强的闭源基线 GPT-5.6-sol / Qwen3.7-plus（均 56.06%）。

> **TL;DR:** SpatialSkill improves frozen VLMs on the CityCube cross-view spatial reasoning benchmark without any parameter updates, by evolving a versioned natural-language skill manual from offline trajectories. Candidate skills must pass a grounded-admission gate (visual evidence + executor executability), stability controls (net growth budget, seniority protection, a probe-score ratchet) block harmful accumulation, and a category router picks per-category direct/CoT × manual strategies via Wilson-interval confidence bounds. Matched frozen Qwen3.5-4B goes from 47.16% to 52.08% (+4.92 pts; Gemma-3-4B +2.65, Qwen3.5-9B +3.04, GPT-5.6-luna +3.03), beating the strongest closed-source baselines (GPT-5.6-sol and Qwen3.7-plus, both 56.06%) with the 9B executor at 57.58%. The manual also transfers across executors and to MMSI-Bench (+2.0 to +4.1 pts) without adaptation.

## 核心贡献

- **问题定义首次化**：首次系统研究"冻结 VLM 能否仅靠积累显式自然语言技能来提升跨视角空间推理"，并归纳出三个必要条件——可靠准入（技能必须有视觉证据支撑且在执行器能力范围内）、稳定进化（防止技能累积噪声/有害压缩）、类别相关有效性（同一技能对不同推理类别有正/负迁移）。
- **SpatialSkill 框架**：三组件流水线——(1) 准入门控 G(r)=V(r)∧E(r)，候选规则须同时通过视觉接地检查与可执行性检查，拒绝项连同证据留痕（可审计）；(2) 稳定性约束进化：净增长预算 |M_w|−|M_{w−1}|≤β、资历保护（存活超过 τ 个窗口的规则免于盲压缩）、probe 分数棘轮（新手册在留出 probe 集上低于历史最优 −ε 即拒绝交付）；(3) 类别条件路由：按 CR/MR/PT/SR/WK 五类，从 {直接回答/CoT}×{无手册/有手册} 四种策略中用 Wilson 置信区间保守选择。
- **主结果**：CityCube 官方 4,494/528 划分上，四个冻结执行器一致获益 +2.65～+4.92（均值 +3.41）；GPT-5.6-luna 53.79%→56.82%；Qwen3.5-9B+SpatialSkill 57.58%，以小模型超过闭源参考 GPT-5.6-sol 与 Qwen3.7-plus（均 56.06%）。
- **稳定性与迁移证据**：43 个进化窗口中，棘轮把重构（refactor）事件从 35/24 次降到 6/8 次；手册跨执行器迁移 8/8 全部为正（最高 +6.82），零样本迁到 MMSI-Bench 上 Qwen3.5-4B +2.0、GPT-5.6-luna +4.1。
- **可审计性**：技能以版本化自然语言手册存储，推理过程显式、可检查、可回溯，无需改动模型参数。

## Key Contributions

- **First framing of weight-update-free skill self-evolution for frozen VLMs on real-world cross-view spatial reasoning**, identifying three requirements: grounded admission, stable accumulation, and category-conditioned deployment — each motivated by failure modes of naive skill libraries (plausible-but-ungrounded rules, harmful compression, negative transfer across reasoning categories).
- **SpatialSkill**: a reflection model (Qwen3.5-9B, frozen) distills offline trajectories into candidate revisions (add/rewrite/correct skill, add note); an admission gate requires visual-evidence AND executability; stability controls (growth budget β, seniority protection τ, probe-score ratchet with tolerance ε) govern what gets delivered; a category router selects among direct/CoT × manual/no-manual strategies per reasoning category using conservative Wilson confidence-bound switching.
- **Consistent frozen-executor gains on CityCube (4,494/528 official split)**: +4.92 (Qwen3.5-4B), +3.04 (Qwen3.5-9B), +2.65 (Gemma-3-4B), +3.03 (GPT-5.6-luna); mean +3.41. Qwen3.5-9B+SpatialSkill reaches 57.58%, exceeding the best closed-source references (GPT-5.6-sol, Qwen3.7-plus; both 56.06%) and sitting 0.56 pts below Qwen3.5-27B (58.14%).
- **Stability and transfer evidence**: over 43 evolution windows the ratchet cuts refactoring events from 35→6 (Qwen3.5-4B) and 24→8 (Gemma-3-4B); source manuals transfer to all eight executor pairings (+0.57 to +6.82) and zero-shot to MMSI-Bench (+2.0/+4.1), always with the source-derived routing policy and no target adaptation.
- **Auditable artifact**: skills live in a versioned natural-language manual with rejected candidates logged together with their triggering evidence, making the acquired reasoning explicit and retraceable.

## Methodology

**Setting.** Multiple-choice cross-view reasoning on CityCube: each example x = ({I_1..I_k}, q, O) pairs k aerial/ground views with a question; a frozen executor picks y ∈ O. Five reasoning categories are used for both evaluation and routing: mental reconstruction (MR), perspective taking (PT), spatial relation (SR), world knowledge (WK), and comprehensive (CR). Training examples additionally provide reference reasoning trajectories, which serve as the raw material for skill distillation.

**Stage 1 — Skill reflection and grounded admission (Sec. 3.2).** The evolution stream is processed in windows w. A fixed reflection model Φ (Qwen3.5-9B) maps the window's trajectories T_w and the current manual M_{w−1} to candidate revisions Δ_w = Φ(T_w, M_{w−1}); each revision performs one of four operations (add skill, rewrite skill, correct skill, add execution note) and carries skill-level vs execution-level attribution estimated against reference trajectories — execution-level feedback is routed to execution notes so episodic noise does not contaminate reusable rules. Each candidate rule r is screened by the admission gate G(r) = V(r) ∧ E(r), where V(r) requires visual support and E(r) requires the prescribed operation to remain within the frozen executor's capabilities. Only G(r)=1 candidates enter a provisional manual; rejected candidates are logged with their triggering evidence.

**Stage 2 — Stability-constrained evolution (Sec. 3.3).** Each provisional manual is scored on a held-out, category-stratified probe set P (disjoint from the evolution stream, never used by the reflector): A(M) = (100/|P|) Σ 1{ŷ_M(x) = y(x)}. Three complementary controls: (1) a **net growth budget** |M_w| − |M_{w−1}| ≤ β caps per-window rule accumulation; (2) **seniority protection** keeps any rule with age(r) ≥ τ in the manual unless an evidence-targeted revision explicitly touches it, so compression cannot blindly discard long-standing rules; (3) a **probe-score ratchet** delivers the provisional manual only if A(M̃_w) ≥ b_{w−1} − ε, where b_w = max{b_{w−1}, A(M_w)} tracks the historical best, so evolution may plateau within the tolerance band but large regressions are never deployed. The paper characterizes the failure mode being prevented as a "grow-and-compress" regime where every window triggers compression yet expected accuracy still declines.

**Stage 3 — Category-conditioned deployment (Sec. 3.4).** Given the precomputed category c, a router picks a strategy s = (i, j) ∈ {0,1}²: i ∈ {direct answer, chain-of-thought}, j ∈ {no manual, with manual}. The optimal policy would maximize Σ p(c)·Acc(c, s, π(c)); in practice strategy accuracies are estimated on a fine probe set, and a candidate strategy replaces the default only when its Wilson-interval lower bound exceeds the stronger non-manual strategy's upper bound by margin δ — a deliberately conservative rule that defaults to the null (no-manual) choice under insufficient evidence.

**Experimental setup (Sec. 4.1–4.2).** Executors: Qwen3.5-4B, Qwen3.5-9B, Gemma-3-4B, GPT-5.6-luna, each compared to its matched no-skill version. References: Qwen3-VL-4B/8B/32B, Qwen3.5-2B→32B-A3B, InternVL3.5-8B/14B/38B, Gemma-3-4B/12B, Skywork-VL-Reward-7B; spatial specialists Spatial-SSRL-7B, SpaceR-SFT-7B, Spatial-MLLM-subset-sft, ViLASR; closed-source GPT-5.4, GPT-5.6-sol, Gemini-3.5/3.7-Flash, Claude-Opus-5, Claude-Sonnet-5, Qwen3.7-plus. The 200-example training split serves as the probe for admission and routing decisions; final numbers are on the 528-example validation split (category-wise and micro overall accuracy, plus an option-wise accuracy range disparity diagnostic). Open executors run on vLLM across 4× RTX 5090 GPUs (tensor parallel for ≥26B models); the reflection model is frozen Qwen3.5-9B throughout the main runs.

## Results & Highlights

- **Main result on CityCube validation (Table 1):** SpatialSkill lifts all four frozen executors — Qwen3.5-4B 47.16% → 52.08% (+4.92), Qwen3.5-9B 54.55% → 57.58% (+3.04), Gemma-3-4B 49.24% → 51.89% (+2.65), GPT-5.6-luna 53.79% → 56.82% (+3.03); mean gain 3.41 pts. For contrast, the CoT-only baseline reaches only 49.62% (Qwen3.5-4B) and 48.67% (Gemma-3-4B), so the gain over the direct baseline is not explainable by CoT prompting alone.
- **Beats closed-source references:** Qwen3.5-9B+SpatialSkill (57.58%) exceeds the strongest closed-source baselines in their evaluation, GPT-5.6-sol and Qwen3.7-plus (both 56.06%), by 1.52 pts, and lands 0.56 pts below the 27B open model Qwen3.5-27B (58.14%); all four evaluated open-source spatial specialists (e.g., Spatial-SSRL-7B at 46.97%, ViLASR at 49.81%) remain below 50% overall.
- **Fairness diagnostic improves:** the option-wise accuracy range (max difference in conditional accuracy across predicted options) shrinks for all four pairs — Qwen3.5-4B 27.28 → 23.52 pp, Qwen3.5-9B 39.21 → 23.10 pp, Gemma-3-4B 72.12 → 47.94 pp, GPT-5.6-luna 30.43 → 19.37 pp — so accuracy gains do not come with larger option-dependent disparity.
- **Stability ablation over 43 windows (Sec. 4.5):** removing the ratchet (the "Naive" variant) causes 35 vs 6 refactoring events on Qwen3.5-4B and 24 vs 8 on Gemma-3-4B; the ratchet rejected 14 and 21 revisions, every one of which scored below the accepted reference (worst drops 9.0 and 5.5 pts); manual-size contractions occurred in 17/16 Naive windows vs 6/8 with SpatialSkill.
- **Component ablations (Table 2, Qwen/Gemma validation accuracy):** full system 52.08/51.89; removing attribution-aware reflection is the most damaging (50.00/47.35 — on Gemma this falls below even the 49.24 direct baseline, −4.54); removing the visual-evidence criterion 50.76/50.74; the executability criterion 51.14/51.32; the joint admission gate 50.19/50.95; the probe-score ratchet 51.14/50.57 (−0.94/−1.32); growth budget and seniority protection each cost ~0.4–0.6 pts when removed.
- **Gains are category- and executor-dependent:** per Table 1, Qwen3.5-4B gains hugely on SR (45.53 → 56.10) and WK (53.34 → 64.06) but drops on MR (53.66 → 51.22); GPT-5.6-luna gains 13.51 pts on CR (48.65 → 62.16) yet slightly declines on SR (59.35 → 56.91); Qwen3.5-9B+manual reaches 57.00 on PT vs 47.47 base. Human accuracy is 88.3% overall — the best non-human is 58.14%, with perspective taking the weakest (best 51.9% vs 87.4% human).
- **Cross-model and cross-dataset transfer:** all eight source→target manual transfers improve over the target's own base, +0.57 to +6.82 (largest: Gemma-source manual on Qwen3.5-4B, 47.16 → 53.98); on the 1,000-example MMSI-Bench transfer set, Qwen3.5-4B improves 29.30% → 31.30% (+2.0, option-wise range 10.45 → 8.01) and GPT-5.6-luna 34.30% → 38.40% (+4.10, range 10.62 → 3.95) with the frozen manual and source-derived router.
- **Paired error analysis and reflector sensitivity:** on frozen predictions, Qwen3.5-4B repairs 87 baseline errors but introduces 61 new ones (net +26; 192 errors persist), Gemma-3-4B repairs 70 / breaks 56 (net +14); swapping the reflector while fixing the executor moves the gain from +1.89 (Qwen3-8B reflector, 49.05%) through +3.41 (Qwen3-4B, 50.57%) to +4.92 (Qwen3.5-9B, 52.08%).

## Authors & Credibility

The team spans Shandong University (Rufan Zuo, Guocheng Hui, Junyi Wang, Xiang Lei, and corresponding author Tian Gan) and Shanghai AI Laboratory (Wanshui Gan). The experimental infrastructure is concrete and reproducible: vLLM serving across 4× NVIDIA RTX 5090 GPUs with documented tensor-parallel settings, greedy decoding, matched API baseline configurations, and a single fixed answer-extraction rule; code is released at github.com/vindahhi/SpatialSkill. The writing is unusually careful about what the evidence does and does not show — e.g., the authors explicitly state that longer outputs on newly broken examples (4.74×/8.33× token ratios) are "a descriptive correlate … rather than a reliable indicator of correctness," that routing relies on precomputed category annotations, and that reflector choice materially moves results. The related-work section correctly positions the paper against both parameter-update spatial reasoning methods (RL/SFT lines) and skill-library agent work (ACE, SkillWeaver, Voyager-style recursion), claiming novelty specifically for the intersection: evolving *explicit natural-language* skills for *frozen perceptual* executors. This is a preprint (28 Sep 2026) and not yet peer-reviewed.

## Critical Assessment

- **One main benchmark carries the headline claim.** CityCube is used for all main results, ablations, stability analyses, and routing diagnostics; MMSI-Bench appears only as a single-run transfer side-experiment with two executors and no competing method for reference (is +4.1 on GPT-5.6-luna good for MMSI-Bench? The paper does not say). A second full-benchmark evaluation with other memory/skill methods would substantially strengthen the claim.
- **No head-to-head against alternative skill/memory systems.** The comparison set is matched no-skill versions of the same executors plus off-the-shelf reference models. ACE, SkillWeaver, A-Mem, and recursive-augmentation methods are cited as the closest related work, but none is run on CityCube under the same protocol — the "self-evolving skills" contribution is thus validated only against its own ablations, not against the obvious competing approaches.
- **Reflector dependence partially undercuts the "self-evolving" framing.** Gains range from +1.89 to +4.92 purely by swapping the reflector (Table 5), and the best configuration uses a stronger model (Qwen3.5-9B) as teacher for weaker executors — so part of the improvement is distilled from a bigger model rather than purely self-generated from the executor's own experience. The paper reports this honestly but the title's "self-evolving" gloss overstates it.
- **Text–table inconsistency in category claims.** The text claims Qwen3.5-9B "improves or matches its baseline across all five categories," but Table 1 shows MR declining from 64.63 to 60.73 under SpatialSkill; the Qwen3.5-4B WK gain is stated as 12.75 pts in the text while the table yields 10.72 (53.34 → 64.06). Small, but sloppy for a paper whose selling point is auditability.
- **Small evaluation set means small margins are fragile.** The validation split is 528 examples and the routing probe only 200; the paper itself shows routed accuracy varies widely across 1,000 resampled 40-example probes, and the ratchet tolerance ε exists precisely because probe noise is material. Several ablation deltas (e.g., growth budget −0.51/−0.58) are within plausible noise, though the authors wisely do not lean on them.
- **Routing depends on precomputed category labels**, which the conclusion admits — real deployment needs a category classifier the paper does not provide or evaluate, and misclassification would corrupt routing decisions. The oracle-vs-achieved gap (54.17 vs 52.08 on Qwen3.5-4B) also shows ~2 pts are left on the table by the conservative router.
- **Absolute performance remains weak.** Even the best system reaches 58.14% (Qwen3.5-27B) against 88.3% human, and perspective taking sits at ~52–57% vs 87.4% human. Skill manuals improve strategy selection but, as the authors concede, do not touch the underlying visual capabilities — the method narrows gaps; it does not close them.
- Genuine strengths worth crediting: the grow-and-compress failure-mode characterization (Eq. 5) with paired refactor-event and manual-size diagnostics is a real contribution to understanding why naive skill libraries degrade, and the admission-gate ablation showing removing visual-evidence checking actually *hurts* Gemma below its no-skill baseline (47.35 < 49.24) is persuasive evidence that ungrounded rules are actively harmful.

## Strengths

- Weight-update-free improvement is orthogonal to the RL/SFT spatial-reasoning line: the same manual plugs into any executor, demonstrated by eight positive cross-model transfers and zero-shot cross-dataset transfer to MMSI-Bench.
- The three-mechanism stability design (growth budget, seniority protection, probe ratchet) is grounded in an explicit failure-mode analysis and validated with dedicated longitudinal diagnostics (refactor counts, manual-size trajectories, rejected-revision probe scores) rather than only end accuracy.
- Thorough component-wise ablations on two executors, with a notably honest finding: removing admission criteria can fall below the no-skill baseline, i.e., bad skills are worse than no skills.
- Auditable artifact: a versioned natural-language manual plus logged rejection evidence makes the acquired behavior inspectable and retraceable — a property parameter updates fundamentally lack.
- Careful negative controls: CoT-only baseline, no-margin routing variant, oracle routing upper bound, option-wise disparity diagnostic, and an explicit statement that output-length expansion is not evidence of correctness.

## Limitations

- Routing requires precomputed reasoning-category annotations at inference; no classifier is provided, and category errors would propagate directly into strategy selection.
- Manuals refine only strategy use; underlying visual/perceptual capability is unchanged, so the large human gap (58.14% best vs 88.3% human; PT 51.9% vs 87.4%) persists.
- Performance is sensitive to reflector choice (+1.89 to +4.92 spread on a fixed executor), and the best setup effectively uses a larger teacher model, blurring the self-evolution narrative.
- Main claims rest on a single 528-example validation split of one benchmark, with a 200-example probe driving admission and routing; no empirical comparison against existing skill-library or memory-agent baselines.
- Residual negative transfer persists in places (e.g., Qwen3.5-4B MR drops 53.66 → 51.22; GPT-5.6-luna SR declines), and paired analysis shows the manual breaks 61 and 56 previously-correct cases on the two 4B executors.

## Relevance

这篇论文与你的最高优先级方向"Self-Evolving Agents & Automated Research"高度吻合：它研究冻结模型上的技能自进化库，核心贡献恰恰是长程自改进系统的可靠性机制（视觉接地准入门控、防有害更新的棘轮、可审计的版本化手册），并定量展示了朴素技能累积的 grow-and-compress 退化模式。对关注"自进化 agent 系统的可信性与稳定性"的读者来说，这是一篇方法与诊断都值得借鉴的工作。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How would SpatialSkill behave against an explicit skill-library baseline (ACE, SkillWeaver, or a simple retrieval-of-past-trajectories memory) on CityCube under identical windows and probe budgets — is the stability machinery or just "any memory" responsible for the gain?
- Can the precomputed category annotation be replaced by a learned router that operates on raw predictions (the no-margin variant already hints at over-switching), and what happens to gains under realistic category-noise rates?
- Does the ratchet's tolerance ε need rescaling with probe size, and can the 200-example probe be shrunk further before admission/routing decisions become noise-dominated?
- The manual transfers across executors (+0.57 to +6.82) — is the transferred content genuinely executor-agnostic spatial strategy, or mostly generic CoT hygiene? A human-readable diff of transferred vs source-specific rules would clarify.
- Could the explicit manual serve as a fine-tuning prior (the authors' stated future work), and does distilling the manual into weights recover the oracle-routing gap (54.17 vs 52.08)?
