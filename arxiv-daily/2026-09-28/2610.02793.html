Research papers reporting LLM failures can become verified fuel for continual self-evolution — with retention, not just gains.

PAPER2LLM++: Continual Self-Evolution of LLMs from Research Papers ↗ Hongji Pu (UIUC) · Yilun Zhao (Yale) · Wenpeng Yin (Penn State) 2026-09-28
Key metric
+8.6pts

Why this matters

把报道 LLM 缺陷的研究文献本身，变成模型持续自我改进的监督来源——teachable / needed / retained 三条件为自进化 agent 的更新门控提供了直接参考。

Literature itself becomes supervision for continual self-improvement — teachable / needed / retained inform update gating for self-evolving agents.
Critical edge

The suite is circular by construction.

All 30 papers were curated because they ship paired original / perturbed benchmarks — exactly the property that makes a failure verifiable and teachable. Papers without one, where verification cannot even run, are absent: the +8.6 average is a property of the selection filter as much as of the method.

Compute unmatched — up to 4 probes + 1 full retrain per paper · 6/30 papers still hurt the model · general ability stays net−0.4
01

How it works: paper in, gated update out

closed loop · hard NO-UPDATE escape
STEP 1 — EXTRACT & VERIFY

Evidence before training

gpt-oss-120b parses each paper into a five-field record — WHAT / TRIGGER / WHY / MITIGATION / EVALUATION — every field backed by verbatim spans. The paper’s benchmark is split 30% detection / 70% held-out; the failure counts only if the paired score gap Δ has a bootstrap 95% CI entirely above zero. The generator never sees benchmark items, responses, or gold answers.

Gate: CI ≾ (0,∞) → NO UPDATE
STEP 2 — SYNTHESIZE

Four candidate supervision forms

Qwen2.5-32B turns the record alone into direct, contrastive, robustness, and process supervision — over-generate 1.5×, then filter. Triple contamination control: exact MD5, RapidFuzz ratio ≥ 97, MPNet cosine ≥ 0.93 against benchmark partitions; <128 valid examples invalidates a candidate.

Gate: nothing leaked from the benchmark
STEP 3 — TRY–EVALUATE–COMMIT

Probe first, retrain only the winner

Each candidate trains a disposable LoRA probe scored U = G − λP − μQ: new gain minus damage to earlier gains (P) and general ability (Q, five commonsense benchmarks). Only the best passing candidate is retrained at full budget; a 600-slot replay buffer plus 50 frozen protected probes guard memory.

Gate: G≥τ_gain · P≤τ_prev · Q≤τ_gen — else model unchanged

Strengths vs. limitations

the honest-read version

Strengths

  • +Genuinely new setting — papers as a continual supervision stream, cleanly separated from knowledge-editing and RL/parameter-editing continual learners.
  • +Verification-first: the CI-gated NO-UPDATE gate provably does no harm on papers whose failures no longer exist.
  • +Data hygiene above the bar: generator isolation, MD5/RapidFuzz/MPNet checks, held-out sealed until the commit decision.
  • +Honest reporting: six negative papers shown, compute mismatch disclosed, r = 0.87 self-limited as descriptive.
  • +Practical: teachable / needed / retained tells practitioners which papers to feed a self-improving system.

Limitations

  • −Suite circularity: author-curated paired benchmarks favor exactly the failure types the method handles best.
  • −Requires a usable evaluation protocol per paper — the many failure papers without one are out of scope, untested.
  • −Compute not normalized: multiple probe trainings + two auxiliary LLMs per paper vs. closed-form baselines.
  • −Only 7–8B backbones; general-ability audit limited to five multiple-choice sets; no coding/agentic audit.
  • −Data & code not yet released — contamination controls cannot be externally audited; behavior beyond 30 papers unknown.
04

Open questions

what to test next
  1. Can failure verification and candidate selection run on an agent’s own environment feedback, so papers without paired benchmarks — e.g. agentic-reliability findings — become learnable?
  2. Does the gain–retention edge over RLEdit/UltraEdit survive compute-normalized budgets and larger backbones (13B–70B)?
  3. What happens over realistic multi-year streams of hundreds to thousands of papers, given a 600-example replay buffer and decaying marginal gains?
  4. Can supervision closure be made causal — intervene on it for low-closure papers and measure whether gains appear?
  5. Six of thirty papers still hurt the model: could per-family thresholds or a learned acceptance policy push the negative-outcome rate toward zero?
