01

Methodology — the probe, inverted

distilled from the full summary

The trick is an inversion: steering vectors — normally an intervention — become a calibrated measuring instrument for what RLVR does to activations. The teacher is the RL-tuned version of the student’s own base model, so the teacher–student gap is exactly the RL update.

STEP 1 · FREEZE & PERTURB

A near-zero-parameter probe

Freeze the backbone; add one input-invariant bias vector δℓ per controlled layer (S = 5–20). On Qwen3-4B that is 40,960 trainable scalars — strictly less expressive than LoRA, which is less than full FT. The three parameterizations bound the answer from above.

STEP 2 · MATCH & DISTILL

One protocol, three regimes

Full FT / LoRA / steering compared under matched prompts, sampling, and mean@4 evaluation (4 samples, no voting). On-policy reverse-KL vs off-policy forward-KL distillation bracket how far the student sits from the teacher.

STEP 3 · DEPTH & GATING

Isolating input-dependence

Single-layer injection isolates depth: fixed offsets fail near the output. Swap in a zero-init Rank-1 nonlinear gate δ(h) = B σ(Ah) (r = 16); a static-gate control proves the fix is input-dependence — not parameter count.

STEP 4 · GEOMETRY → ALPHA-STABLER

From readouts to a stabilizer

Readouts: sequential orthogonal vectors, principal-subspace energy, PSI (actor-vs-base shift energy inside the base’s Top-10% PCs). Predictor alarms on sustained PSI rise; Controller projects activation gradients off that subspace in backprop — two low-rank matmuls, forward pass untouched. verl + vLLM, 8–32× H100.

03

Strengths · Limitations

critical assessment, condensed

Strengths

  • A genuinely clever inversion: an intervention technique repurposed as a calibrated probe whose failure modes are themselves measurements of RLVR’s structure.
  • Experimental hygiene: teacher is always the RL-tuned own base (gap = the RL update exactly); prompts, sampling, mean@4 matched across all parameterizations.
  • Mechanistic depth: the high-layer failure is traced to input-dependence via the static-vs-nonlinear gate control — not just observed and named.
  • Analysis → tool payoff: Alpha-Stabler adds zero parameters, costs two low-rank matmuls per hook, and leaves the forward pass and RL objective untouched.
  • Reproducibility well above venue norms: full hyperparameters, verbatim launch commands, prompts, hardware specs, released code.

Limitations

  • Scope is RLVR-only: verifiable rewards on math/science/code/IF; RLHF, agentic multi-turn, and open-ended generation are untested (authors acknowledge).
  • Single-run stabilization evidence: no error bars or seed variance; collapse shown on 1.5B (Science) and 7B (IF), not the largest models.
  • Narrow baselines: only global gradient clipping externally; PSI is not compared against cheaper signals like entropy dynamics.
  • The probe is not passive: it needs its own optimization run (lr 1×10⁻¹, four orders above full-FT) — it measures the distillation solution, not RL weights directly.
  • Empirical, not derived: both “properties” are regularities without first-principles grounding; the R²=0.91 fit spans six pairs within one science-topic family. Novelty also overlaps Zhou et al. 2025 and the authors’ own Rank-1 work.
04

Open questions

Q1

Does PSI early warning transfer to RLHF and reward-model settings, where reward hacking may shift representations through the principal subspace rather than around it?

Q2

Can the depth-dependent capacity limit be predicted a priori from architecture — say, the count and strength of downstream nonlinearities — instead of measured per model?

Q3

How does the control-manifold geometry evolve in multi-turn agentic RL, where environment noise means the “RL update” is no longer a fixed teacher–student gap?

Q4

Could targeted gradient projection replace KL regularization toward the reference policy entirely — and how do the two interact when both are active?

Q5

Is the 1–2% principal-subspace energy figure stable at 100B+ scale, where covariance spectra and outlier dimensions differ qualitatively?

Q6

What is the cheapest sufficient early-warning signal — is PSI demonstrably better than tracking entropy or KL-to-reference, given its extra covariance machinery?
