01Methodology

WISE — witness-integrated set effects

Plain NIE lets downstream backup components self-repair and mask a patched set's true effect. WISE imports witnesses from actual causality: a sampled mediator set M is patched while sampled downstream witnesses W are pinned to their clean-prompt values, blocking out-of-set repair. The score averages NIE, PIE, or their soft-min bottleneck (NIE∧PIE) over a distribution of mediator–witness sets.

JuntaLearner — differentiable set search

Each candidate gets a trainable inclusion logit; Gumbel-top-k draws differentiable sets of size k with straight-through gradients (Adam, clipped), annealed down a descending set-size ladder. One run yields a full ranking — a circuit of any size ℓ is read off the top-ℓ. Cost is Θ(T·B·P), independent of pool size.

kill / net / CRS — the evaluation trio

kill patches the circuit itself (necessity); net subtracts capped collateral damage on the model's other tasks (task specificity); CRS aggregates log-spaced circuit sizes with discounted gains, rewarding early — small-circuit — recovery.

03Strengths

  • A real, documented problem. Per-component NIE misranks circuits under preemption/self-repair; prior escapes required task-specific priors.
  • Principled bridge. Witnesses imported from actual causality (Halpern) into causal mediation; WISE parameterizes over any effect measure, including the bottleneck score.
  • Structurally sound cost profile. Θ(T·B·P) independent of pool size; one run yields rankings usable at every circuit size.
  • The metric trio is a contribution in itself. The CPR-vs-CMD reversal on Qwen3-SVA should change how circuit papers report.
  • Honest end-to-end. Default vs tuned configs side by side, 95% intervals per cell, every number regenerated by script.

04Limitations

  • Heads only in the main experiments — MLPs, residuals, SAE features untested outside one MIB appendix run.
  • The witness mechanism is the least validated piece — helps on just 1 of 3 models, with "no principled account" of when it will help.
  • Slower than exact patching on small models (2–8×); the speed argument needs a large candidate pool.
  • Marginal mean gain over signed exact per-head NIE (0.67 vs 0.64), and tuned rows select on the evaluation metric — test-set selection.
  • Narrow scope — five synthetic algorithmic tasks, models ≤ 8B; Hydra prevalence on natural language is unmeasured.

05Open questions

  1. Can witnesses be chosen by predicted self-repair activity rather than uniform sampling — extending the benefit beyond Qwen3?
  2. Does JuntaLearner scale to MLPs, SAE features, and residuals at 7B–70B, where exact per-head NIE becomes computationally impossible?
  3. Is the NIE-vs-kill circularity removable — train on the bottleneck score, evaluate on faithfulness, and win on the metric not optimized?
  4. Can witness-pinned rankings systematically map function-sharing structure across tasks, given Qwen3's multi-functional backup heads?
  5. How prevalent is the Hydra effect on natural (non-synthetic) tasks, and does the interaction-aware advantage grow with scale and diversity?
