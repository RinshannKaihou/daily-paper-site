03 Critical edge

Every configuration ran exactly once — no seeds, no variance, no repeats. The headline Open-set margin is one instance (31 vs 29), and “success” counts any epsilon-improvement as a new best solution.

Improvement magnitudes are never reported · FunSearch and CORAL — the two closest paradigms — were never run.

Methodology

a three-layer loop, run for 12 h

A task is a MILP/MINLP instance (MPS file) under a contract — candidate snapshot, evaluator, time budget, stopping rule. AutoMIP is a reusable skill wrapping any coding agent in a loop where what to explore and where to resume are decided jointly:

1
PoolThink — what to explore

Keeps 5–10 complementary research directions from distinct hypothesis families (neighborhood search, structural decomposition, model strengthening). Parameter tweaks are refinements, not new ideas; the pool refreshes only after ≥5 candidates are used or committed.

2
Algorithm tree — where to resume

L-edges refine the current node; R-edges switch to an unused pool idea. On a switch, artifacts are restored from a compatible historical state (K(v,q) = 1); consecutive-switch counters and backtracking limits prevent thrashing.

3
Verification — prove it

Every candidate is reloaded against the original MPS and re-checked with a real solver (Gurobi): objective, constraints, bounds, integrity. Solver logs alone are explicitly inadmissible.

↻ After every step, outcomes are written back to the tree node, the pool record and the frontier — so the next decision re-chooses both direction and starting state.

RUNTIME — Codex (GPT-5.5), also Claude Code (Sonnet 5) · Apple M4 Pro, 12-core / 24 GB · budgets: 12 h (Open, MINLPLib) · 1 h fixed-target (Hard) · full skill protocol in appendices A.1–A.6

Strengths

  • +Real community benchmarks, not toys — strictly better solutions on MIPLib and 86.7% on MINLPLib under fixed budgets.
  • +Wins both axes that matter for long-horizon agents — earlier completion (×1.24–×3.09) and lower token spend than every baseline; the gains are not bought with brute force.
  • +Clean, transferable mechanism — pool decides what to explore, tree decides where to resume, joined by an explicit compatibility check other autoresearch domains could adopt directly.
  • +Independent MPS-level verification closes the fabricated-solution gap most LLM-for-optimization papers leave open.
  • +Honest bookkeeping — ablations that genuinely support the design; cross-agent transfer and harness failures reported transparently.

Limitations

  • −Single run per configuration — no seeds, variance or statistical tests; one-instance margins (31v29 Open, 30v29 Hard) are uninterpretable.
  • −“New best” bar is low on Open — any strict improvement counts; magnitudes never reported.
  • −No FunSearch or CORAL comparison despite positioning against both in related work.
  • −Narrow ablation scope — 20 Open-set instances only; nothing on MINLPLib or Hard.
  • −Brittle harness dependence — 3 of 6 tested systems failed outright at 12-hour autonomy; frontier models (GPT-5.5 / Sonnet 5) assumed.
  • −Nonstandard hardware — Apple M4 Pro; absolute runtimes may not transfer to Linux/many-core MIPLib setups.
