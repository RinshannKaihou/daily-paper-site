Methodology

harness · svalbard vault · role-level GRPO
Orchestrator — edits the plan, spawns sub-agents only

Gap finder

4–8 corpus queries per challenge axis → gap docs: near-miss methods, deficiency, stakes

→

Innovator

6–10 queries across ≥3 outside sub-fields → intuitions with source, transfer map, risk

→

Report writer

Fixed proposal schema with resolvable citation IDs; falsifiable predictions, limitations

Reviewer — survive / revise / reject verdicts on intermediate artifacts
Paper reader — single-turn digests, 30k-token cap; full papers never enter producer contexts

IdeaScientist isolates ideation from AutoResearch as a standalone, trainable stage. Producers work strictly offline against the Svalbard Idea Vault — 2.77M (problem, challenge, gap, intuition, proposal) tuples decomposed from 4.7M arXiv papers, frozen at a Jan 1, 2026 cutoff. The 14,977 post-cutoff human methodology papers (14,500 / 200 / 277) serve as reference targets no system could have memorized overtly.

Each role is trained separately on Qwen3.6-27B (LoRA rank 32) via asynchronous GRPO, on a reference-grounded reward: r = 0.85·g·(r_free + r_ref + r_cit)/3 + 0.15·r_proc, with g a hard validity gate over schema, citations, and focus — then recomposed in the shared harness.

async GRPO · 64 prompts × 8 rollouts, clip [0.2, 0.8], no KL
compute · 40 nodes × 8 H100 = 320 GPUs
references · mask-generated by Qwen3.6-27B itself; 0/50 leakage audit
judges · Qwen3.6-27B primary + 4 corroborating judges

04

Strengths

  • Clean decomposition — gap finding, innovation, writing are each inspectable, independently trainable, and ablatable.
  • Temporally-controlled evaluation — pre-cutoff retrieval, post-cutoff human papers as targets; three-scope novelty is well motivated.
  • Exceptional eval hygiene — judge-vs-human correlation, self-preference bias analysis, leakage audit, contamination probe, failure accounting.
  • Honest null result — DeepScientist is statistically indistinguishable under human comparison, and the paper says so.
  • Ablations isolate cause — sub-agents, retrieval scope, per-role RL checkpoints, test-time budget.
05

Limitations

  • Nothing is executed — soundness and impact are judged from text; judged novelty can trade off against feasibility.
  • Judge = backbone family — Qwen3.6-27B judges the system it trained; residual self-preference risk.
  • Self-referential benchmark — references are Qwen-decomposed; reference-grounded score plateaus at 0.26–0.30, hinting weak coupling to the human direction.
  • ML-adjacent only — test split is CV 67 / ML 59 / NLP 39 / AI 24 / Robotics 18 / other 70; cross-science claims untested.
  • Cost & closure — 320 H100s and a closed Meta corpus pipeline limit full reproduction; latent post-cutoff memory can’t be excluded.
06

Questions for Further Exploration

  1. Does the +14 margin over DeepScientist survive a different-family primary judge (e.g. GPT-5.4), given the 49% human win-rate between them?
  2. The reference-grounded score plateaus near 0.26–0.30 — do agents learn format and citation hygiene faster than genuine direction-following?
  3. What fraction of the +25 judged-novelty gain survives if scored solely on mechanism non-obviousness, the one measure near the floor for all systems?
  4. Would trained roles recomposed inside a full AutoResearch loop (with execution feedback) close the ideation–execution gap — or overfit to the frozen harness?
  5. Can the Svalbard decomposition be validated by human experts at scale, given its references come from Qwen3.6-27B’s own reading?
