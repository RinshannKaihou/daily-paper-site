> **一句话总结：** 本文把"转向向量"从干预手段反转为测量仪器：在冻结骨干上加每层一个输入无关的可训练向量去蒸馏 RL 训练后的教师，发现 RLVR 增益集中在激活空间的低维流形上（单个向量即可恢复全量微调 85% 以上的增益，但固定偏置在高层失效、需要输入相关调制），有效控制方向只把 1–2% 的能量放进激活主子空间（Top-10%），且跨主题方向对齐与迁移增益呈线性关系（R²=0.91）；据此提出 Alpha-Stabler——用主子空间入侵度（PSI）做训练崩溃预警、反向传播时把激活梯度投影出主子空间——零新增参数、开销可忽略，可稳定 GRPO/DAPO 训练 2000+ 步。

> **TL;DR:** Trainable per-layer steering vectors are used as a probe (not an intervention) to characterize what RLVR does to activations. On 5 models (1.5B–14B, e.g. DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B/7B, Qwen3-4B/8B/14B) with GRPO/DAPO-trained teachers over math/science/code/instruction-following, a single input-invariant vector per controlled layer recovers >85% of the full-fine-tuning gain under on-policy distillation, yet fixed offsets collapse near output layers (−0.3 converged score vs full-FT at high layers, recoverable by an input-dependent Rank-1 gate at r=16). Effective directions sit in the low-variance complement of the activation principal subspace (only 1–2% energy in the Top-10% PCs vs a 10% isotropic reference), and cross-topic steering-vector alignment predicts transfer gain with a linear fit of R²=0.91. Exploiting these properties, Alpha-Stabler monitors Principal-Subspace Intrusion (PSI) for early collapse warning and projects activation gradients off the principal subspace during backprop: it stabilizes training for 2,000+ steps with zero added parameters and near-identical wall-clock, beating random/Middle-PC/Bottom-PC projections and comparing favorably with global gradient clipping.

## 核心贡献

- **把转向向量当作 RLVR 的分析探针**：冻结基座模型，仅在受控层（如第 5–20 层）的残差流上加每层一个输入无关偏置向量（Qwen3-4B 上仅 40,960 个可训练标量，约为骨干参数的 1.0×10⁻⁵），用蒸馏目标去逼近"RL 更新本身"（教师 = 学生基座经 GRPO/DAPO 训练后的版本，保证师生差距恰好就是 RL 更新）。
- **性质 1（有效流形容量，Effective Manifold Capacity）**：复现 RL 增益只需要极低维干预——on-policy 蒸馏下单向量恢复全量微调 85% 以上增益；但该容量"并非无限可压缩"：师生分布差距拉大时出现 全参数 > LoRA > 向量转向 的排序；注入深度是第二个约束，固定偏置在靠近输出的高层掉约 0.3（全参数 ≈0.84），换成输入相关的 Rank-1 非线性门控（r=16）即可逼近全参数参考，而静态（无输入依赖）门控无法复现该提升。
- **性质 2（控制流形分离，Control Manifold Separation）**：顺序正交多向量探针显示首个方向在 SciKnowEval 上单独立达 67.5% 准确率，第 8 个正交方向仍有 62.0%（仅差 5.5 个百分点）——主方向有效但不唯一；有效方向在冻结基座激活协方差 Top-10% 主子空间中只占 1–2% 能量（同分布参考值为 10%）；跨主题（物理/化学/材料/生物 6 对源-目标）方向对齐与归一化迁移增益线性拟合 R²=0.91。
- **Alpha-Stabler 框架**：Predictor 每 M 步监控激活偏移在主子空间的能量占比（PSI），用 warm-up 50 步的中位数 ±MAD 设阈值，在可见掉分之前发出预警；Controller 在反向传播时把激活梯度投影掉主子空间分量（每次激活只需两个低秩矩阵乘法），前向计算、奖励、GRPO/DAPO 损失完全不变，零新增可训练参数。
- **实验规模与可复现性**：5 个 LLM × 4 类可验证任务（数学 AIME24/MATH500、科学 SciKnowEval、代码 LiveCodeBench-v5、指令遵循 IFEval），mean@4 评估，verl+vLLM 在 8–32 张 H100 上完成；附录给出全部超参、提示词与启动命令，代码开源。

## Key Contributions

- Reframes vector steering as a *measurement instrument* for RLVR: freeze the backbone, train one input-invariant bias vector per controlled layer (S=5..20; only ~4.1×10⁴ scalars, ≈1.0×10⁻⁵ of the backbone on Qwen3-4B) under distillation from a teacher that is the RL-tuned version of the student's own base model, so the teacher-student gap *is* the RL update.
- **Property 1 — Effective Manifold Capacity**: under on-policy distillation a single vector per layer recovers >85% of the full-FT gain; capacity is bounded by (a) the teacher-student distribution gap (large off-policy gaps restore the ordering full-FT > LoRA > steering) and (b) injection depth — fixed offsets degrade at high layers while input-dependent Rank-1 gating (r=16) approaches the full-parameter reference, isolating *input-dependence* (not parameter count) as the binding constraint.
- **Property 2 — Control Manifold Separation**: sequential orthogonal multi-vector steering shows the leading direction is effective but not unique (67.5% → 62.0% standalone accuracy from direction 1 to 8 on SciKnowEval); learned directions carry only 1–2% of their energy in the Top-10% activation-PC subspace; steering geometry is consistent across full-FT/LoRA/teachers and on/off-policy regimes; cross-topic alignment correlates with transfer gain (R²=0.91).
- **Alpha-Stabler**: a plug-and-play stabilizer — a Predictor that detects sustained PSI increases before score collapse, and a Controller that removes the principal-subspace component of activation gradients during backprop; no new trainable parameters, negligible overhead, integrates with GRPO/DAPO.
- Scale and rigor: 5 LLMs across 6 verifiable-reward tasks (AIME24, MATH500, SciKnowEval, LiveCodeBench-v5, IFEval), mean@4 evaluation, full hyperparameter/command appendix, released code.

## Methodology

**Probe design (vector steering).** With a frozen base model, for controlled layers S ⊆ {1..L} the hidden state at each layer output is perturbed h'_ℓ = h_ℓ + δ_ℓ, where δ_ℓ ∈ R^d is shared across all inputs and token positions. Only {|S|d} scalars are optimized under a distillation objective, so steering has strictly lower expressive capacity than LoRA (rank-r adapters on all linear modules) and full fine-tuning; the three parameterizations are compared under matched base model, prompts, sampling recipe, and mean@4 evaluation protocol (average of 4 independent samples per prompt, no best-of or majority voting).

**Paradigms.** (a) On-policy distillation: reverse KL from student trajectories to the teacher, with a token-level gradient approximation. (b) Off-policy distillation: negative log-likelihood on frozen teacher trajectories (forward KL). (c) RL itself (GRPO/DAPO) for the Alpha-Stabler experiments. Teachers are trained with GRPO or DAPO on verifiable rewards (answer matching, unit tests, constraint checking) on DeepMath-103K, SciKnowEval-Training, Eurus, IFEval-Training; evaluation on AIME24/MATH500, SciKnowEval, LiveCodeBench-v5, IFEval-Eval. Critically, the default teacher is the RL-tuned version of the student's own base model, making the teacher-student gap exactly the RL update.

**Depth analysis.** Layer-wise injection restricts *all three* parameterizations to the same single layer. Converged performance, learning curves, steering-gradient norms, and teacher-KL are compared across depths. To test the input-dependence hypothesis, the fixed offset is replaced by a low-rank nonlinear correction δ_i(h_i) = B_ℓ σ(A_ℓ h_i) (Eq. 2), zero-initialized; a static variant replacing σ(A_ℓ h_i) with a constant coefficient isolates whether the gain comes from input-dependent modulation. Gating coefficient distributions and per-token gradient-to-vector cosine alignment are analyzed across depth.

**Multi-vector probing.** Sequential orthogonal steering (Eq. 3) learns K vectors one at a time, each re-projected onto the orthogonal complement of previous directions after every optimizer step; each direction is evaluated standalone. Layer-averaged cosine similarity between direction sets extracted under different teachers/parameterizations measures cross-configuration consistency; cross-topic transfer on four science topics uses six ordered source-target pairs with normalized transfer gain (Eq. 4).

**Principal-subspace analysis.** U_{r,ℓ} contains leading eigenvectors (r = 10% of d) of the frozen base model's token-level activation covariance; principal-subspace energy fraction e_PC (Eq. 5) and Principal-Subspace Intrusion PSI (Eq. 6/7) — the energy of the actor-vs-base activation shift falling in the base model's Top-10% PCs, computed on stacked token-level shifts to avoid cancellation — are tracked during training.

**Alpha-Stabler.** After a 50-update warm-up, the Predictor computes PSI every M updates and sets warning/release thresholds from layer-wise medians scaled by median absolute deviation (EMA smoothing, control after p consecutive violations). The Controller applies G̃^(l) = G^(l) − α^(l)(G^(l) U_{l,r})U_{l,r}^⊤ (Eq. 8) during backprop only — forward activations, rewards, likelihood ratios, and optimizer updates are untouched; each active hook costs two low-rank matrix multiplications. Infrastructure: verl + vLLM, 8–32 × H100 96GB, bfloat16.

## Results & Highlights

- **85% single-vector recovery**: under on-policy distillation, a single steering vector per controlled layer recovers over 85% of the gain achieved by full fine-tuning on most evaluated tasks (Fig. 2a); under off-policy distillation with a small gap all three parameterizations perform comparably, but a larger gap yields the clear ordering full-parameter > LoRA > vector steering (Fig. 2d).
- **Depth-bound capacity**: at high injection layers single-vector steering trails full-parameter training by ≈0.3 converged score (full-FT ≈0.84; Fig. 3a); a Rank-1 nonlinear gate with r=16 reduces teacher KL divergence at all four tested high-layer sites and approaches the full-parameter reference, whereas a static (input-independent) gate does not reproduce the improvement (Fig. 4a) — steering-gradient cosine similarity rises from ≈0.019 at lower layers to ≈0.14 near the output, so alignment alone cannot explain the degradation.
- **Non-unique but structured directions** (DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B, SciKnowEval): the first orthogonal direction reaches 67.5% standalone accuracy and the eighth reaches 62.0% — only 5.5 percentage points lower despite being orthogonal to all seven preceding directions; larger models retain higher-order directions whose performance stays closer to the leading one (Fig. 5b).
- **Geometry predicts transfer**: over six source-target science-topic pairs, steering-vector alignment vs normalized transfer gain is fit by a line with R² = 0.91 (Fig. 6c); alignment is broadly consistent across full-FT/LoRA/steering teachers and on/off-policy extraction.
- **Principal-subspace separation**: unconstrained steering directions place only ~1–2% of their energy in the Top-10% principal subspace (vs a 10% isotropic reference), and restricting vectors to the Top-30% PC subspace raises energy to ~15% *without* improving accuracy (Fig. 7b); collapse runs show PSI departing its stable range before any visible score degradation (Fig. 7c).
- **Alpha-Stabler stabilization**: control keeps PSI in the low single digits and sustains score growth for 2,000+ steps where uncontrolled runs collapse (Fig. 8a/b); triggered control matches immediate control on score and peak PSI (≈0.82 vs ≈0.82) while delaying intervention progressively degrades score (≈0.78 at +100, ≈0.71 at +200, Fig. 8d); Top-PC projection outperforms random-, Middle-PC, and Bottom-PC control and compares favorably with global gradient clipping (Fig. 8e), with near-identical score trajectories under the same wall-clock time (Appendix Fig. 12).

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty is real but narrower than the framing suggests.** The central geometric finding — RL/RLVR updates live off the activation principal subspace — closely echoes Zhou et al. 2025 ("The path not taken: RLVR provably learns off the principals", arXiv:2511.08567, cited but not deeply compared) and the authors' own Rank-1 dominance work. The genuinely new pieces are the *depth-dependent capacity limit* (fixed offsets fail at high layers; input-dependent gating fixes it — a clean, well-isolated finding via the static-gate control), the *alignment↔transfer* R²=0.91 relation, and a practical stabilizer.
- **The "85%" headline is a distillation number.** Vector steering distills an already-RL-trained teacher whose gap from the base is exactly the RL update; it does not mean one vector per layer can replace RL training. The paper is careful about this (the collapse of steering under large off-policy gaps is honestly reported), but the abstract's "reproduce RL-induced gains" phrasing invites over-reading.
- **Single-run evidence in the stabilization section.** The collapse analyses and Alpha-Stabler trajectories (e.g., Fig. 8a/b on 1.5B Science and 7B IF) show no error bars and appear to be single runs per configuration; the delay ablation (Fig. 8d) reports single bars. For a paper whose headline practical claim is "prevents collapse," seed variance is a missing ablation.
- **Thin mitigation baselines.** Beyond subspace choice, the only external stabilization baseline is global gradient clipping; no comparison against KL-coefficient tuning, entropy-bonus interventions, or other early-warning signals (e.g., the entropy-collapse detectors the paper itself cites from Yue et al. 2025 / Cui et al. 2025b). PSI's advantage over those cheaper signals (entropy is free to compute) is therefore not established.
- **The probe is not passive.** Extracting "the" steering geometry requires an optimization run per layer set (with steering-specific learning rates of 1×10⁻¹, four orders of magnitude above full-FT's 1×10⁻⁵), so the measured "manifold" is a property of the distillation solution, not a direct readout of RL weights; the paper's conditional account (Appendix D.4) partially addresses this but the identification is empirical.
- **Minor inflation**: the abstract claims "6 tasks" while Table 1 lists 4 domains / 5 evaluation sets; PSI thresholds (medians ± MAD over retained shifts) are heuristic and their sensitivity is not ablated. On the plus side, the honest Limitations section flags the lack of first-principles derivation and the restriction to RLVR (not RLHF/agentic/open-ended) — consistent with the evidence presented.

## Strengths

- A genuinely clever inversion: steering vectors — normally an intervention technique — are repurposed as a calibrated probe whose *failure modes* (distribution gap, injection depth) are themselves measurements of RLVR's structure.
- Excellent experimental hygiene: the teacher is always the RL-tuned version of the student's own base (gap = the RL update exactly); prompts, sampling, and mean@4 evaluation are matched across all parameterizations; three complementary parameterizations (full-FT / LoRA / steering) bound the answer from above.
- Mechanistic depth beyond curves: the high-layer failure is traced to input-dependence via the static-vs-nonlinear-gate comparison, gating-coefficient distributions, and gradient-alignment statistics — not just observed and named.
- Alpha-Stabler is a rare "analysis → practical tool" payoff: zero new parameters, two low-rank matmuls per hook, no change to forward computation or the RL objective, and a targeted (not indiscriminate) gradient intervention validated by subspace ablations.
- Reproducibility documentation (commands, hyperparameters, hardware, prompts, released code) is well above venue norms.

## Limitations

- Scope is confined to RLVR on math/science/code/instruction-following with verifiable rewards; RLHF, multi-turn agentic decision-making, and open-ended generation are untested (acknowledged by the authors).
- Both "properties" are empirically motivated regularities, not derived results; the paper itself concedes they rest on assumptions not grounded from first principles.
- Stabilization claims rest on a small number of runs without seed variance; collapse behavior is demonstrated on 1.5B (Science) and 7B (IF) settings rather than the largest models in the study.
- The mitigation baseline set is narrow (gradient clipping only among external methods); PSI is not compared against cheaper warning signals such as entropy dynamics.
- The steering probe requires its own training procedure (including very large learning rates and warm-up choices), adding a layer of optimizer-dependent indirection between the measurement and the RL dynamics it claims to reveal.
- Geometric transfer correlation (R²=0.91) is established over six ordered pairs within one domain family (science topics); its generality across task families is untested.

## Relevance

这篇论文同时命中两个高相关方向：一是 **LLM 训练稳定性与可观测性**——PSI 作为可在真实 GRPO/DAPO 训练中在线计算的早预警指标、以及"定向梯度投影优于全局梯度裁剪"的结论，对长跑训练的异常检测很有参考价值；二是 **LLM 隐状态与可解释性**——用可训练向量作探针刻画 RLVR 后的激活几何（低维流形、主子空间分离），是表征几何分析的一个干净范例。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does PSI-based early warning transfer to RLHF and reward-model settings, where reward hacking may shift representations through the principal subspace rather than around it?
- Can the depth-dependent capacity limit be predicted a priori from architecture (e.g., count/strength of downstream nonlinearities) instead of measured post hoc per model?
- How does the control-manifold geometry evolve in multi-turn agentic RL with environment noise and tool feedback, where the "RL update" is not a fixed teacher-student gap?
- Could the Controller's targeted gradient projection replace KL regularization toward the reference policy entirely, and how do the two interact when both are active?
- Is the 1–2% principal-subspace energy figure stable at 100B+ scale, where activation covariance spectra and outlier dimensions differ qualitatively from 1.5B–14B models?
- What is the cheapest sufficient early-warning signal — is PSI demonstrably better than tracking entropy or KL-to-reference, given its extra covariance-estimation machinery?
