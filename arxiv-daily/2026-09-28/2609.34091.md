> **一句话总结：** 牛津团队在 27 个设置（CBraMod/REVE/DINOv3 三个骨干 × 3 个 EEG 数据集 × 3 个深度）上系统证伪了 SAE 可解释性中的直觉推断——"删掉 alpha 频段后某 latent 激活剧烈变化 ⇒ 它表征 alpha"：alpha 消融相比等宽假 notch 确实使候选 latent 放电高 7.3×（95% CI [6.2, 8.7]），但按删除的频谱能量归一化后比值跌至 0.28×（CI [0.22, 0.36]，27 个设置中无一超过 1）；且被选出的"alpha 专家" latent 在干净 EEG 上与相对 alpha 功率仅呈平均 r = −0.073 的负相关，消融其对线性探针的影响（+0.0055）也弱于对照组（−0.028）。论文据此提出五级"验证阶梯"（敏感性 → 失真受控选择性 → 跨扰动特异性 →（分叉）独立观察验证 / 读出干预），主张只有逐级排除替代解释后才能给 SAE latent 贴语义标签。

> **TL;DR:** A negative result on SAE interpretability in EEG foundation models (CBraMod, REVE, and DINOv3 over BCI Competition IV 2a, PhysioNet Mental Arithmetic, and BCI 2020 Track 3): removing the alpha band makes candidate latents fire 7.3× more than a width-matched 30–34 Hz sham notch, but after normalizing by the spectral energy each notch deletes, the ratio collapses to 0.28× (exceeds 1 in 0/27 settings) — the raw signature just tracks how much signal the filter removed, not what the latent means. On clean EEG, "alpha-specialist" latents correlate only weakly and negatively with relative alpha power (mean r = −0.073 vs +0.143 |r| for size-matched fragile latents in the wrong direction), and zeroing them shifts linear-probe balanced accuracy by +0.0055 versus −0.028 for matched fragile controls. The paper distills this into a five-stage validation ladder that any SAE-interpretability claim (including in LLMs) must climb before assigning a semantic label.

## 核心贡献

- **识别并证伪一个反复出现的推理谬误**：在 27 个设置（3 骨干 × 3 数据集 × 3 深度）中，"alpha 消融 ⇒ 激活剧烈变化 ⇒ alpha 特征"的直觉在原始尺度上极具说服力（7.3×，CI [6.2, 8.7]，27/27 设置 > 1），但 EEG 功率谱是 1/f 型的，alpha notch 删除的能量是假 notch 的 11.8–57.2 倍；按删除频谱能量归一化后比值降至 0.28×（0/27 > 1）——原始响应混淆了"该 latent 对刺激敏感"与"删除的信号本身就多"。
- **提出五级验证阶梯（forked validation ladder）**：1) 敏感性（是否响应消融）→ 2) 失真受控选择性（还是只是删得更多）→ 3) 跨扰动特异性（还是普遍脆弱）→ 分叉为 4) 读出干预（是否对指定任务头重要，支持更强的功能性主张）或 5) 独立观察验证（在无扰动的干净数据上是否追踪目标属性，支持表征性主张）。并给出"证据 → 可主张的结论"映射表：响应 ⇒ alpha-敏感；抗失真 ⇒ alpha-选择；干净数据上独立共变 ⇒ alpha-表征的证据；叠加读出干预 ⇒ 任务相关表征。
- **独立观察验证在预期方向上失败**：用选择时未使用的量（试次级相对 alpha 功率）检验，"alpha 专家" latent 平均 |r| = 0.143，反而低于尺寸匹配的"普遍脆弱" latent（0.246，26/27 设置中被超过），仅略高于随机对照（0.097）；带符号相关平均为负（−0.073，23/27 设置为负）——被 alpha 消融选出的 latent 倾向于在 alpha 功率更低的试次上放电。
- **读出干预显示 alpha 选择性 ≠ 读出重要性**：在 21 个有任务编码的设置中，alpha 选择性与激活权重重要性仅弱相关（Pearson r = 0.107，CI [0.068, 0.146]）；零化专家组使冻结探针平衡精度变化 +0.0055（CI [−0.007, 0.027]），而零化尺寸匹配脆弱组为 −0.028、随机组为 −0.019。
- **方法学工具箱本身可迁移**：TopK SAE（k=32，MSE + AuxK 死 latent 回收）冻结训练于早/中/晚三层组，测试 FVU ∈ [0.059, 0.228]；活动匹配对照（干净放电率 0.374 vs 0.373）、设置级 bootstrap（10,000 次）、BCI 中层的 3 种子重训——整套协议对 LLM SAE 可解释性同样适用。

## Key Contributions

- **A recurrent inferential failure identified and refuted**: across 27 settings (3 backbones × 3 EEG datasets × 3 depths), the tempting "ablate alpha → big activation change → alpha feature" inference looks compelling at raw scale (7.3×, 95% CI [6.2, 8.7], >1 in 27/27 settings), but because EEG spectra are 1/f, the alpha notch deletes 11.8–57.2× more spectral energy than the sham; normalizing by removed spectral energy collapses the ratio to 0.28× (0/27 settings >1) — raw perturbation sensitivity conflates "latent is sensitive" with "the filter deleted more signal".
- **A five-stage validation ladder**: (1) Sensitivity → (2) distortion-controlled selectivity → (3) cross-perturbation specificity → fork into (4) readout intervention (stronger functional claim) or (5) independent observational validation (representational claim on unperturbed data), with an explicit evidence→claim mapping table; only stages 1–3 plus 5 license a semantic ("alpha-representing") label.
- **Independent observational validation fails in the intended direction**: using a quantity never used in selection (trial-wise relative alpha power), specialist latents achieve mean |r| = 0.143 on clean EEG — below size-matched general-fragile latents (0.246, higher in 26/27 settings) and only slightly above random controls (0.097); the signed correlation averages −0.073 (negative in 23/27 settings).
- **Alpha selectivity is not readout importance**: selectivity correlates only weakly with activation-weight importance (mean Pearson r = 0.107 [0.068, 0.146] over 21 task-analysis settings); zeroing specialist-like groups shifts frozen-probe balanced accuracy by +0.0055 [−0.007, 0.027] versus −0.028 [−0.051, −0.003] for size-matched fragile groups and −0.019 [−0.033, −0.008] for random controls.
- **A transferable methodological toolkit**: frozen TopK SAEs (k=32, MSE + AuxK dead-latent recovery, test FVU in [0.059, 0.228]), activity-matched controls (clean firing 0.374 vs 0.373; mean activation 0.0060 vs 0.0062), setting-level bootstrap CIs (10,000 resamples), and 3-seed retrains on the BCI middle layer — a protocol equally applicable to SAE interpretability in LLMs.

## Methodology

**Backbones, data, settings.** Three frozen backbones — EEG-pretrained CBraMod (ICLR 2025), REVE (NeurIPS 2025, pretrained on ~25,000 subjects), and DINOv3 ViT-B/16 (a vision model fed channel-time EEG, included as a cross-modal control) — are evaluated at early, middle, and late transformer-block groups on three 200 Hz EEG tasks with fixed splits: BCI Competition IV 2a (motor imagery, 22 channels), PhysioNet Mental Arithmetic (workload, 20 channels), and BCI Competition 2020 Track 3 (imagined speech, 64 channels). Backbone × dataset × depth gives 27 settings; the BCI middle-layer setting per backbone is retrained under three SAE seeds.

**SAEs.** TopK sparse autoencoders (k = 32) with reconstruction MSE plus AuxK dead-latent recovery are trained per setting on activations after per-layer ℓ2 normalization (z/‖z‖ ≈ z/√d) with 16× dictionary expansion (4× or 8× where token support motivates, e.g., DINOv3 and Mental Arithmetic), yielding 27 dictionaries; all converge to test FVU in [0.059, 0.228].

**Perturbations and distortion control.** Each clean test trial is paired with perturbed copies: spatial (zeroing random channels or contiguous windows), spectral (zero-phase Butterworth band-stop at δ/θ/α/β/γ/high-γ, with the 8–12 Hz alpha notch tested against a width-matched 30–34 Hz sham; the audio-band analog for the speech dataset). Because EEG power scales as 1/f, the alpha notch removes far more energy than the sham (removed-energy ratios 57.2 / 34.6 / 11.8 on BCI / MA / Speech), so the SAE response (sparse-code cosine change, active-set Jaccard drop, |Δf_i|) is divided by both the time-domain distortion |x−x̃|₂/‖x‖₂ and the fraction of spectral energy removed — two normalizations that bound rather than remove the confound.

**Selectivity probes, ablations, and controls.** A "specialist-like" alpha latent is one in the top quartile of alpha selectivity and below the median channel-dropout fragility; size-matched general-fragile and random latents are readout-fragility contrasts; activity-matched non-specialists (nearest in clean firing rate and mean activation, matched to 0.374 vs 0.373 firing and 0.0060 vs 0.0062 activation) are the contrast for clean-alpha correspondence. Mean-pooled SAE codes feed logistic probes on the fixed split; importance = |w_j|·E_train[c_j]; group ablation zeroes specialist / fragile / random groups and measures probe balanced-accuracy change under a frozen-probe and a retrained-probe variant. Stage 5 correlates each latent's clean activation with trial-wise relative alpha power — a quantity unused in selection — for 12,041 latents per named group. All headline CIs are percentile 95% intervals from 10,000 bootstrap resamples of settings (27, or 21 for task analyses). The pipeline took ~300 A100 GPU-hours; an anonymized code archive is provided as supplementary material.

## Results & Highlights

- **Raw alpha sensitivity collapses under distortion control.** Alpha removal changes latent firing 7.33× more than the sham notch pooled over 27 settings (95% CI [6.18, 8.68]; per-backbone raw: CBraMod 7.4, REVE 8.9, DINOv3 5.1; >1 in 27/27). Dividing by the fraction of removed spectral energy — which is 57.2×/34.6×/11.8× larger for alpha on BCI/MA/Speech — drops the ratio to 0.28× (CI [0.22, 0.36], exceeding 1 in 0/27 settings); the time-domain normalization gives 1.32× (CI [1.12, 1.55]), so the two normalizations bound, rather than eliminate, the distortion confound. Code-overlap ratios behave identically (raw 2.77 → 0.51 / 0.11 normalized).
- **Selected latents do not track alpha on clean EEG — slightly the opposite.** With relative alpha power (unused in selection), specialist latents reach only mean |r| = 0.143 versus 0.246 for size-matched general-fragile latents (fragile higher in 26/27 settings; Δ|r| CI [0.032, 0.052]) and 0.097 for random controls; the signed correlation is negative on average (−0.073, negative in 23/27 settings), i.e., ablation-selected latents tend to fire on trials with relatively *less* alpha. Among top-activating trials, specialists average just +0.012 relative alpha power over matched fragile exemplars (higher in only 12/27 settings).
- **Readout intervention finds no specialist contribution.** Over 21 settings with task codes, alpha selectivity correlates with activation-weight importance at only mean Pearson r = 0.107 (CI [0.068, 0.146]; |r| ≤ 0.32 in all nine BCI-middle retrainings). Zeroing specialist-like groups shifts frozen-probe balanced accuracy by +0.0055 (CI [−0.007, 0.027]) versus −0.028 (CI [−0.051, −0.003]) for size-matched fragile groups and −0.019 (CI [−0.033, −0.008]) for random controls; probe balanced accuracy itself spans 0.236–0.625, and fragile latents fire 8.9× more at baseline (the residual confound activity matching was designed to remove).
- **Secondary depth finding.** Channel-dropout robustness decreases with depth in all nine backbone–dataset combinations, strongest for DINOv3 (continuous ΔJaccard ≈ −0.35), intermediate for CBraMod (≈ −0.19), mildest for REVE (≈ −0.07) — late-layer SAE codes are less stable under spatial perturbation in both EEG-native and cross-modal backbones.
- **Honesty about the raw effect's texture.** Alpha is the maximum-|Δf_i|, minimum-Jaccard band in only 15/27 settings of the six-band sweep — "a plurality, not a rule", so even the pre-normalization evidence for a privileged alpha band is weaker than the abstract-level number suggests.

## Authors & Credibility

Yucong Cao, Chengqi Li, and Tingting Zhu are all from the Department of Engineering Science, University of Oxford; Zhu's group works on physiological signal modeling and ML for healthcare. The paper demonstrates strong methodological hygiene: distortion-controlled perturbation design, activity-matched and size-matched controls, setting-level bootstrap CIs (with the resampling unit explicitly stated as settings, not latents), multi-seed retrains, a cross-modal backbone control (DINOv3) to test whether the failure is EEG-specific, licensing documentation for every dataset and checkpoint, a NeurIPS-style checklist, and an estimate of compute (~300 A100 GPU-hours). Publishing a careful negative result with an operational protocol rather than a positive "we found interpretable features" claim is a credibility positive. The work positions itself against language-model SAE interpretability lines (Cunningham et al., Bricken et al., Marks et al.) and EEG decoding visualization (Schirrmeister et al.), and cites the foundation models (CBraMod, REVE, DINOv3) it audits accurately.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty is real but modest in kind**: sensitivity tests, causal interventions, and probing are all standard interpretability tools; the contribution is the identification of a *specific* confound (1/f spectra make notch responses track deleted energy) and the packaging into an ordered ladder with an explicit evidence→claim table. That packaging is genuinely useful, but the ladder itself is not validated as sufficient — only as necessary.
- **No positive control is the biggest gap, and the authors admit it**: Section 4 concedes that a positive control on another physiological axis (a latent the ladder *should* certify) is "left to a longer version". Without it, the reader cannot tell whether the ladder's stage 5 is capable of ever passing a true feature or whether it is calibrated so strictly that everything fails — the paper shows the ladder rejects, never that it can accept.
- **The alpha-selectivity null could partly be an artifact of the selection cut**: specialists are chosen by top-quartile selectivity and *below-median fragility*; the fragile comparison group fires 8.9× more at baseline, and the paper's own appendix shows ablation orderings are seed-dependent on BCI-middle (3 seeds) while most settings use a single SAE seed — SAE seed variance, which is known to be large in dictionary-learning studies, is unquantified for 24 of 27 settings.
- **Scope of the semantic claim tested is narrow**: stage 5 uses trial-wise *relative band power*, which the authors acknowledge tests only "a global frequency reading" — latents encoding structured spatial-spectral alpha patterns (e.g., electrode-localized mu rhythm) are explicitly not ruled out; likewise, relevance is defined only for a logistic probe on mean-pooled codes, so nonlinear or token-level readout relevance is untested, and task-readout analyses cover only 21/27 settings (REVE MA/Speech were unavailable).
- **Δf_i is a noisy statistic**: the limitations note it can include TopK rank displacement (a latent swapping in/out of the top-k set without meaningfully changing activation), which inflates perturbation responses for control latents too; the 15/27 "plurality not a rule" result further undercuts any alpha-specific story even before normalization.
- **Transfer to LLMs is asserted, not demonstrated**: the introduction frames the ladder as relevant to LLM SAE interpretability, but every experiment is EEG/one-vision-model; there is no LLM experiment, and the distortion-control argument (1/f spectra) does not map one-to-one onto text ablations, where "how much signal did the edit delete" has no clean spectral-energy analogue.
- **Rigor**: strong on statistics (bootstrap unit stated, CIs everywhere, controls matched on two moments), strong on honesty (negative result, explicit Table 1 "negative reading"). Reproducibility is good on paper (anonymized code archive, public datasets/weights, seeds recorded), though checkpoints and raw EEG are omitted for size.

## Strengths

- A clean, well-posed confound (removed-energy asymmetry under 1/f spectra) with two complementary normalizations that bound it, applied uniformly across 27 settings and three architecturally distinct backbones including a cross-modal control.
- The independent observational validation (stage 5) uses a quantity never touched during selection — a genuinely out-of-sample check that most interpretability papers skip.
- Careful control design: size-matched fragile groups, activity-matched non-specialists (matched to 0.374 vs 0.373 firing), random controls, and frozen vs retrained probe variants that expose when ablation effects are baseline-activity artifacts.
- Explicit falsification table (Table 1) mapping evidence tiers to claim tiers, plus an explicitly stated "negative reading" of what the result does and does not show.
- Honest reporting of inconvenient details: fragile latents out-correlate specialists on clean EEG, ablation ordering is seed-dependent, alpha is only a plurality winner in the band sweep.

## Limitations

- One SAE seed per setting for 24/27 settings (only BCI-middle retrained under 3 seeds); seed dependence is shown to matter where tested.
- No positive control demonstrating the ladder can certify a true feature — the protocol is shown to reject, never to accept.
- Band-stop filtering perturbs the whole 8–12 Hz band and cannot isolate a biological oscillator; relative band power tests only a global frequency reading; structured spatial-spectral codes remain unexcluded.
- Readout relevance defined only via logistic probes on mean-pooled codes; REVE MA/Speech task codes unavailable (21/27 coverage); Δf_i contaminated by TopK rank displacement.
- The two distortion normalizations bound rather than remove the confound (0.28× vs 1.32× straddle 1).
- Domain scope: conclusions about SAE latent semantics are drawn for EEG (+ one vision backbone); no LLM experiments despite the framing's appeal to LLM interpretability.

## Relevance

该文与你的「LLM 隐状态与可解释性」方向直接相关：它给出的五级验证阶梯（消融敏感性 → 失真受控选择性 → 跨扰动特异性 → 独立观察验证 / 读出干预）可直接迁移到 LLM 的 SAE 特征命名与 probing 流程，尤其是"消融响应可能只是删除信号量不对称"这一陷阱对任何编辑/擦除式解释实验都是警示。对可信 autonomous-research 系统中自动生成的可解释性结论的可靠性审查也有借鉴意义。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Can the authors run the missing positive control — e.g., a latent that tracks a simulated sinusoidal component injected into EEG — to show the ladder can *accept* a true feature, and calibrate its false-negative rate?
- How does the ladder behave under SAEs trained with much larger dictionary expansion or different objectives (JumpReLU, BatchTopK), and how much do stage-2/5 verdicts vary across SAE seeds?
- Does a spatially-resolved test (electrode-wise mu-rhythm correspondence) rescue a structured alpha reading that global relative band power cannot detect?
- Can the distortion-control argument be formalized for text: what is the analogue of "fraction of signal deleted" for token/feature ablations in LLM SAE studies, and do published "concept" features survive it?
- Would replacing mean-pooled codes with token-level or attention-head readouts change the stage-4 null that alpha selectivity is not readout importance?
