> **一句话总结：** 针对 LLM 自进化（Questioner–Solver 自博弈循环）中"性能先升后平再降"的退化问题，本文提出"可学习信息增益"（learnable information gain）这一系统级诊断量——用一个在上一轮数据上拟合的 Pythia-160M 代理模型以 NLL 度量新一轮数据的"新知识量"（理论上等于相邻两轮数据分布的 KL 散度加熵变），并据此构建 ATRI 框架：轮内按信息增益重加权样本、轮间在增益持续低于阈值时提前停止；在 R-Diverse 评测套件（GSM8K、MATH-500、AIME24/25、MMLU-Pro 等 10 个基准）上，ATRI 于 Qwen3-4B/8B-Base 的 Overall AVG 分别达到 47.50/52.75，超过最强基线 R-Diverse +1.22/+2.56 分，且单轮开销仅 +2.2% PFLOPs。

> **TL;DR:** This paper diagnoses why LLM self-evolution (e.g., R-Zero-style Questioner–Solver self-play on the MATH dataset) degenerates — performance improves, plateaus, then declines — and shows that partial signals (reward variance, policy entropy, distinct n-gram ratio, self-BLEU) fail to predict the decline. It defines "learnable information gain": how much novel, parameterizable content a training round adds over the previous one, estimated by fitting a small proxy LM (Pythia-160M) on the previous round's data and scoring the new round via per-token NLL; theoretically it decomposes into the KL divergence between the two rounds' data distributions plus their entropy change. The proposed ATRI framework reweights samples within a round by their positive information gain and stops training once round-level gain C_t stays below τ = 0.1·C_1 for two consecutive rounds. On the R-Diverse suite (7 math + 3 general benchmarks), ATRI reaches 47.50 Overall AVG on Qwen3-4B-Base vs 46.28 for the strongest baseline R-Diverse (+1.22) and 52.75 vs 50.19 on Qwen3-8B-Base (+2.56), while C_t serves as an early-warning signal that fires 1–4 rounds before the accuracy peak across all six baseline loops.

## 核心贡献

- **系统级诊断量"可学习信息增益"**：不同于以往只监控 Questioner 或 Solver 单一组件的"局部信号"（reward variance、policy entropy、distinct n-gram ratio、self-BLEU），信息增益把自进化循环当作耦合整体，度量一轮数据相对上一轮的"新颖可参数化信息量"；理论上（精确拟合下）它等于两轮数据分布间的 KL 散度加熵变（Appendix F），并由此刻画三阶段生命周期（Phase I 增益为正、精度上升；Phase II 增益趋零、精度平台；Phase III 增益近零仍继续训练 = 向自身高置信输出蒸馏、精度下降）。
- **信息增益驱动的训练范式 ATRI**：轮内（within-round）用代理模型给每个问/答打 NLL 分，以相对上一轮均值的正偏差 `w = max(c_t, 0)` 重加权 GRPO advantage，把梯度集中到新内容上；轮间（across-round）以 C_t < τ = 0.1·C_1 连续两轮作为停止规则，在严重退化发生前终止训练。
- **早期预警与被动监控能力**：在 6 种自进化方法（Vanilla/StAR/SPIN/AZR/R-Zero/R-Diverse）上，C_t 均在精度峰值前 1–4 轮跌破告警阈值；作为不改训练过程的被动监控器，仅凭停止规则即可把 Qwen3-4B/MATH 的平均精度从 39.01 提到 46.32，距 oracle 最优 checkpoint 仅差 0.83。
- **方向性外部数据选择**：训练"正确组/错误组"两个代理，用增益差 C_t^# 筛选"新颖但当前模型会做错"的外部样本，在所有预算下击败 Difficulty-only、R-Diverse、c_t-only；C^#+c_t 组合从 1500 样本起领先，5000 样本时差距最大。
- **充分实验验证**：Qwen3-4B/8B-Base × 6 基线 × 10 基准主对比、三阶段生命周期跨方法复现、组件消融、超参敏感性扫描、计算开销核算（每轮仅 +2.2% PFLOPs，GPU-hours 开销随规模从 4.1% 降至 1.3%）。

## Key Contributions

- **A system-level diagnostic for self-evolution degeneration.** Learnable information gain measures how much novel, parameterizable information a round's data adds over the previous round; under exact fit it decomposes into the KL divergence between the two rounds' data distributions plus their entropy change, explaining the three-phase lifecycle (rise → plateau → decline) of self-play loops.
- **An information-gain-driven training paradigm (ATRI).** Within a round, samples are reweighted by max(c_t, 0) — their positive deviation of proxy NLL from the previous round's mean — concentrating gradient updates on novel content (applied to both Questioner and Solver GRPO updates); across rounds, training halts once round-level gain C_t stays below τ = 0.1·C_1 for two consecutive rounds, preempting Phase III contraction.
- **An early-warning signal that generalizes.** Across six self-evolution methods on Qwen3-4B-Base (Vanilla, StAR, SPIN, AZR, R-Zero, R-Diverse), C_t crosses the alarm threshold 1–4 rounds before the accuracy peak; used passively as a stop rule it lifts average accuracy from 39.01 to 46.32, within 0.83 of the oracle test-best checkpoint, without held-out validation data.
- **Direction-aware external-sample selection.** A directional variant C_t^# (cost under a "correct-group" proxy minus cost under a "wrong-group" proxy) identifies external samples that are novel yet currently answered incorrectly, beating Difficulty-only, R-Diverse, and raw c_t selection at every budget.
- **Extensive empirical evaluation** on Qwen3-4B/8B-Base against six baselines over ten benchmarks, with per-component ablations, hyperparameter sensitivity sweeps, and an honest overhead accounting (+2.2% PFLOPs per round at 8B scale).

## Methodology

**Setup: the self-evolution loop.** Following R-Zero, a Questioner generates/selects questions and a Solver answers them; both are iteratively updated with GRPO on self-generated data. Degeneration means held-out accuracy rises, plateaus, then declines over rounds (on Qwen3-4B-Base/MATH, vanilla accuracy peaks at round 4 and declines through round 15).

**Why partial signals fail (Preliminary Analysis).** The authors record four popular partial signals — reward variance, policy entropy, distinct n-gram ratio, self-BLEU — during a 15-round R-Zero run: all fluctuate without predicting the decline, whereas C_t reaches zero at round 3 and stays near it, anticipating the round-4 accuracy peak.

**Proxy model and scoring.** After round t−1, a small LM M_{t−1} (Pythia-160M by default) is fine-tuned for one epoch on D_{t−1}, where each question–answer pair is concatenated into one sequence x = [q; s; a] and treated as unstructured text. The proxy scores a sequence by length-normalized NLL: ℓ(x) = −(1/|x|) Σ log M_{t−1}(x_k | x_<k). The proxy's job is deliberately narrow: not to solve the task but to provide a low-variance estimate of the round's data distribution (recurring patterns of the round are learned, so atypical content scores high).

**Learnable information gain.**
- Per-question gain: c_t^Q(q) = ℓ(q) − mean ℓ over D_{t−1} (deviation from previous-round mean difficulty; positive ⇒ content beyond what earlier rounds covered). Analogously for answers conditioned on questions (Eq. 12).
- Per-round gain: C_t = mean ℓ(D_t) − mean ℓ(D_{t−1}). Under exact fit, C_t equals the KL divergence between the two rounds' data distributions plus their entropy change (Appendix F). C_t can be decomposed into question and answer parts.

**ATRI training.**
- *Within-round reweighting (Questioner).* Base reward follows R-Zero: uncertainty reward r_unc = 1 − 2|p_i − 1/2| (peaks when the Solver is correct ~half the time) plus a BLEU-cluster redundancy penalty, max(0, r_unc − r_rep), normalized to a GRPO advantage. ATRI then rescales the advantage by w_t^Q = max(c_t^Q, 0), yielding the calibrated advantage Ã = w·A in the GRPO objective (+β KL to the round-start reference model).
- *Within-round reweighting (Solver).* Standard filtering retains questions with |p_i − 1/2| ≤ δ; answers get the analogous positive-part gain weight w_t^S in the GRPO loss.
- *Cross-round stopping rule.* Stop once C_t < τ = 0.1·C_1 for two consecutive rounds (τ set relative to the first round). The paper proves (Prop. 5) that C_t tends to decrease round over round, and (Prop. 6) that continuing in Phase III behaves like distilling the model onto its own high-confidence outputs, contracting the solution distribution.
- *Directional external-data selection (optional phase).* Two proxies are trained on samples split by Solver correctness; C_t^# = cost under the correct-group proxy − cost under the wrong-group proxy. Positive C_t^# marks samples resembling the model's failure modes; combined with c_t (which removes already-absorbed content) it selects external data once C_t → 0.

## Results & Highlights

- **Main comparison (Table 1, R-Diverse suite; 5 seeds, mean reported).** On Qwen3-4B-Base, ATRI attains Math AVG 53.55 and Overall AVG 47.50 vs 52.74/46.28 for the strongest baseline R-Diverse (+0.81 / +1.22) and 42.58/36.34 for the base model (+10.97 / +11.16); e.g., MATH 81.29 vs R-Zero's 74.76, AIME25 15.20 vs R-Diverse's 10.88. On Qwen3-8B-Base, ATRI reaches Math AVG 58.94 vs R-Diverse 58.09 (+0.85) and Overall AVG 52.75 vs 50.19 (+2.56), with AMC 77.30 vs 66.03 (+11.27) and MMLU-Pro 64.79 vs 61.86 (+2.93) — the best aggregate on both models across all seven math and three general benchmarks.
- **Phase III contraction is real and large (Table 2, vanilla loop, Qwen3-4B-Base/MATH, 500 held-out problems, 8 samples each).** From the accuracy peak (round 4: pass@1 42.98, pass@8 75.1, path entropy 1.846, distinct correct paths 2.645) to the final round 15: pass@8 falls 19.8 points to 55.6, path entropy drops 36.5% to 1.173, and distinct correct paths drop 46.6% to 1.412 — direct evidence that late-round training sharpens the output distribution onto itself rather than adding capability.
- **The alarm precedes the peak in every method (Figures 4–5).** Across all six self-evolution baselines on Qwen3-4B-Base, C_t falls below τ = 0.1·C_1 one to four rounds before validation accuracy peaks (longer plateaus give longer lead times); in the vanilla run C_t hits zero at round 3, accuracy peaks at round 4, and decline starts at round 9. The same pattern recurs on Llama-3.1-8B and on MBPP code generation (Appendix G).
- **Passive stop rule recovers most of the peak (Section 4.7).** Applied unchanged to the six existing methods, stopping when C_t stays below τ for two consecutive rounds raises average accuracy from 39.01 to 46.32 — within 0.83 of the oracle test-best checkpoint — without any held-out validation data.
- **Ablations (Table 4, Qwen3-4B-Base).** Removing both C_t-control components costs 3.37 Math AVG (53.55 → 50.18); keeping only the stop rule gives 52.49 (−1.06) and only the weighting/filtering gives 51.27 (−2.28), so the stop rule accounts for the larger share while weighting contributes a further 1.06 at a nearly identical stopping round; adding the optional external-data phase raises Math AVG by another 2.12 to 55.67.
- **Robustness and cost (Tables 3 & 5).** The stop threshold is stable across α ∈ {0.05, 0.10, 0.20} (Math AVG 53.31–53.55) with only the aggressive α = 0.30 dropping to 52.46; proxy size matters little (Pythia-70M 52.73 → Pythia-410M 53.62). Per-round overhead on Qwen3-8B-Base is +2.2% PFLOPs (87.8 vs 89.7, of which proxy training 1.4 + gain computation 0.5), and measured GPU-hour overhead falls from 4.1% to 1.3% as the base model scales (Appendix K), since proxy cost does not grow with base-model size.

## Critical Assessment

- **The headline gain over the strongest baseline is thin, and per-column results are mixed.** ATRI beats R-Diverse by only +0.81 Math AVG on Qwen3-4B and +0.85 on Qwen3-8B — small against a 5-seed protocol for which no standard deviations or significance tests are reported. On several sub-benchmarks ATRI actually loses to R-Diverse (Qwen3-4B AIME-24 18.54 vs 19.22 and Olympiad 46.98 vs 47.03; Qwen3-8B GSM8K 94.11 vs 94.58 and AIME-24 22.65 vs 24.67), while the paper's bolding highlights the columns it wins. The +11 points over the base model largely come free with the R-Zero-style self-play paradigm that all baselines share.
- **The stop rule, not the "information gain" weighting, delivers most of the benefit.** By the paper's own ablation, stop-only yields 52.49 of the full 53.55 (stop-vs-neither gap: 2.31 of the 3.37), and the authors admit stopping occurs "under nearly an identical stopping round" with or without weighting. A skeptic can read the paper as "early stopping with a principled trigger"; the within-round reweighting — the part most tied to the information-gain concept — contributes ~1 point. The strong baselines (R-Zero, R-Diverse) are run to 15 rounds without any early stopping, so part of ATRI's edge may simply be not over-training.
- **The compute comparison is not matched.** ATRI stops earlier than the 15-round baselines, so it uses less compute than the baselines it outperforms; a compute-matched comparison (baselines stopped at equal PFLOPs, or run with any held-out-accuracy-based early stop) is missing, and the +0.8–2.6 margin over R-Diverse could partly be a training-duration artifact.
- **NLL-based gain conflates novelty with noise.** Per-token NLL of a 160M proxy rises for text that is unusual, malformed, or degenerate, not only for "newly learnable" content; within-round weighting takes max(c_t, 0) with no fluency or validity gate on the Questioner side. The directional C_t^# variant partially fixes this for external data, but the core weighting has no such protection, and there is no ablation isolating garbage-filtering as a confound.
- **Breadth of evidence is limited.** Main-text models are Qwen3-4B/8B-Base on math self-play (Llama-3.1-8B and MBPP appear only in an appendix); τ is anchored to C_1, so the rule's behavior when round 1 is atypical (e.g., a very diverse seed set) is untested; and the evaluation suite is the R-Diverse suite while R-Diverse is also the strongest baseline — a mild conflict of convenience.
- **Genuine strengths worth crediting:** the diagnostic framing (KL + entropy decomposition) is clean and falsifiable, the lifecycle analysis with direct distributional measurements (pass@k, path entropy, distinct correct paths) is convincing, the passive-monitor result (within 0.83 of oracle without validation data) is the practically most valuable finding, and the honest overhead accounting is rare.

## Strengths

- Principled, system-level formulation: a single scalar (C_t) that provably decomposes into KL divergence plus entropy change covers the whole coupled Questioner–Solver loop, unlike component-local signals that provably miss degeneration (Figure 2).
- Strong empirical hygiene: 5-seed means, per-component ablations, threshold and proxy-size sensitivity sweeps, appendix robustness checks, and explicit PFLOPs/GPU-hour cost accounting.
- The early-warning result generalizes across six existing methods (and to Llama-3.1-8B and MBPP in the appendix) without modifying their training — C_t is a drop-in monitor, which makes the contribution immediately actionable for practitioners running self-play loops.
- Practical overhead is negligible (+2.2% PFLOPs at 8B, shrinking with scale) because the proxy is a fixed-size Pythia model.
- The directional external-data selection (C_t^#) is a clever, well-motivated extension: novelty alone is insufficient, so it scores samples against disagreement between correct-group and wrong-group proxies.

## Limitations

- Evaluation is confined to math-reasoning self-play with Qwen3-4B/8B-Base in the main text; general-reasoning benchmarks (MMLU-Pro, SuperGPQA, BBEH) are evaluated but training is still math-generated, and other domains appear only in an appendix.
- Gains over the strongest baseline are small (+0.81/+0.85 Math AVG) with no variance or significance reporting, and ATRI loses on several individual benchmarks (AIME-24, GSM8K-8B, Olympiad-4B).
- No compute-matched or early-stop-matched baseline comparison; no ablation separating information-gain weighting from mere noise filtering.
- The stopping threshold τ = 0.1·C_1 depends on the first round's gain, an untested anchor; α = 0.30 already costs 1.09 Math AVG, showing the rule is not free of tuning.
- Stop-only variants of the baselines (e.g., R-Diverse + C_t stop) are not reported, so the allocation of benefit between "the signal" and "just stopping earlier" across methods remains implicit.

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would an "R-Diverse + C_t-based early stop" baseline close the +0.8–2.6 gap, i.e., is the signal itself or merely early stopping the operative ingredient for existing strong methods?
- The proxy scores length-normalized NLL over concatenated [q; s; a] text — how does the gain signal behave under format drift (e.g., when the Questioner switches to verbose chain-of-thought questions), which mechanically inflates or deflates per-token NLL without any change in learnable content?
- Can the within-round weighting be made robust to garbage/novel-but-wrong text by combining c_t with the directional C_t^# signal inside the loop, not just for external data selection?
- Does τ = 0.1·C_1 transfer when the seed dataset is highly diverse or when the loop is initialized from an instruction-tuned rather than base model, where C_1 may be atypically small or large?
- The Phase III contraction is measured on 500 held-out math problems with 8 samples each — does the same entropy/path-collapse signature appear in agentic or tool-use self-evolution loops (e.g., ToolRL-style pipelines) where the action space is far larger?
- Since C_t under exact fit is KL + entropy change, could a cheap distributional estimator (e.g., embedding-space MMD between rounds) replace the proxy LM entirely at even lower cost?
