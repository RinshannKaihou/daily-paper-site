Headline metric
+4.92pts

Qwen3.5-4B on CityCube: 47.16% → 52.08% — the largest of four frozen executors (mean +3.41), no parameter touched.

and Qwen3.5-9B + SpatialSkill hits 57.58%, past GPT-5.6-sol & Qwen3.7-plus (both 56.06%)

Why this matters

Quantifies why naive skill libraries degrade — the grow-and-compress collapse — then shows the reliability machinery (grounded admission, a probe ratchet, auditable manuals) that makes accumulation safe.

自进化 agent 的可信性与稳定性机制：方法与诊断都值得借鉴。

Critical edge

Swapping only the reflector swings gains +1.89 → +4.92, and the best setup distills from a bigger teacher — blurring the “self-evolving” claim. No rival skill-library baseline (ACE, SkillWeaver) was ever run.

+ a text–table inconsistency on category claims, awkward for a paper selling auditability

01

How it works

distilled from Sec. 3

A frozen reflector (Qwen3.5-9B) distills offline reasoning trajectories into a versioned natural-language manual; executors never change a parameter.

Stage 1 · Ground & admit
Nothing enters without evidence

Candidate revisions (add / rewrite / correct skill, add note) must pass G(r) = V(r) ∧ E(r): visual grounding and executor executability. Rejections are logged with their triggering evidence.

Stage 2 · Stabilize
Three brakes on harmful accumulation

On a held-out probe set: a net growth budget caps new rules; seniority protection shields long-lived rules from blind compression; a probe-score ratchet delivers only manuals at least as good as the historical best minus ε.

Stage 3 · Route
Conservative per-category switching

Across five categories (CR / MR / PT / SR / WK), Wilson-bound switching picks among direct/CoT × manual/no-manual — defaulting to no-manual whenever evidence is thin.

SETUP — CityCube 4,494 train / 528 val · 4 frozen executors · 200-example probe · vLLM on 4× RTX 5090 · greedy decoding

Balance sheet

Strengths

  • Parameter-free and portable — one manual plugs into any executor: 8/8 cross-model transfers positive, zero-shot cross-dataset to MMSI-Bench. (orthogonal to the RL/SFT line)
  • Stability trio grounded in failure-mode analysis — validated with longitudinal diagnostics (refactor counts, manual-size trajectories), not just end accuracy.
  • Honest negative controls — CoT-only baseline, oracle routing bound, option-wise disparity diagnostic; removing admission criteria can fall below the no-skill baseline.
  • Auditable artifact — versioned manual + logged rejections with evidence; a property parameter updates fundamentally lack.
  • Fairness improves with accuracy — option-wise disparity shrinks for all four executors (e.g. Gemma 72.12 → 47.94 pp).
03

Balance sheet

Limitations

  • One benchmark carries the claim — a single 528-example split; MMSI-Bench is only a two-executor side run with no competing method for reference.
  • No head-to-head vs skill/memory systems — ACE, SkillWeaver, A-Mem cited but never run; the contribution is validated only against its own ablations.
  • “Self-evolving” is partly borrowed capacity — reflector choice moves gains +1.89…+4.92; the best setup uses a larger teacher. (routing also leans on precomputed category labels; ~2 pts left vs oracle)
  • Strategy-only, capability unchanged — best system 58.14% vs 88.3% human; perspective taking ~52% vs 87.4%. It narrows gaps; it does not close them.
  • Residual negative transfer — Qwen3.5-4B MR 53.66 → 51.22; the manual breaks 61 / 56 previously-correct cases on the two 4B executors.
04

Open questions

from the summary’s exploration agenda
  1. Against an explicit skill-library baseline (ACE, SkillWeaver, retrieval memory) under identical windows — is it the stability machinery, or just “any memory”?
  2. Can precomputed category labels become a learned router on raw predictions, and what happens to gains under realistic category-noise rates?
  3. Does the ratchet tolerance ε need rescaling with probe size — how small can the 200-example probe get before decisions are noise-dominated?
  4. Are transferred manuals genuinely executor-agnostic spatial strategy, or mostly generic CoT hygiene? A human-readable rule diff would tell.
  5. Distilling the manual into weights (the authors’ future work) — would it recover the oracle-routing gap, 54.17 vs 52.08?
