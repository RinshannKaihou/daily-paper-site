Why This Matters

A controlled testbed for self-evolving research agents — and rare causal evidence that popular self-improvement scaffolds barely move real discovery under fixed action budgets.

正中 Self-Evolving Agents 方向：流行的自进化脚手架在固定行动预算下对“构造性发现”几乎没有增益，而披露假设空间会显著改变探究行为。

Critical Edge

The “open universe” is bounded: the three target representation changes are enumerated in advance — system-identification puzzle-solving with a narrative skin, closer to Outer Wilds than to open-ended science.

And the headline construct is judge-scored: grounding κ = 0.751, with the larger judge scoring worse (0.609).

01 — The World

One latent universe, four ways to burden the agent

Methodology distilled — the machinery behind the numbers above.

One authored latent world: five derelict seed ships — fen, trellis, ladder, consign, registry — holding 36 objects whose hidden four-coordinate labels all descend from a single mechanism, rewritten on a fixed schedule behind monotonically transformed instrument readings. The world resets every 24 ticks; only the agent’s free-text notes persist. Agents act through 8 verbs under a 200-paid-action budget, then face a terminal standing order: commit one never-attempted cross-domain intervention and three exact integer readings — a transfer test of whatever account of the world they built.

Four variants are compiled from the same source, varying two surfaces independently: representational openness (Open vs Disclosed ontology) and evidence organization (Distributed vs Aligned transcripts). Scoring is two-legged — 18 deterministic event-log detectors certify evidence; a calibrated LLM judge (Qwen3.8-27B, κ = 0.883 statements / 0.751 grounding vs 51 human annotations) scores stated inferences.

A validation suite actively hunts leakage: a 39-action reference route proves all 18 blocks reachable; prior-knowledge probe 0/120; full-record inference probe 0/30; per-channel shortcut leakage ≤1%.

04 — Verdict

Measurement hygiene worth stealing; claims worth trusting carefully

StrengthsWhat the benchmark gets right

  • Clean causal isolation — same latent world, seeds and scoring across all four cells; paired instances with bootstrap CIs on every contrast. The Δ_repr / Δ_align decomposition is the ablation agentic benchmarks usually omit.
  • Deterministic evidence scoring — 18 event-log detectors limit judge exposure to the statement leg, and both judge candidates are reported with κ rather than one favorable number.
  • Active leakage-hunting — reachability route, prior-knowledge probe (0/120), full-record probe (0/30), per-channel shortcut probes ≤1%. Rare and commendable.
  • Failures reported as findings — retraction without replacement, 0 exact predictions at 200 actions, and an actionable null for the self-evolving-agent literature.

LimitationsWhere the claim thins out

  • One authored world, twelve instances — whether the 46.1% retraction rate or the scaffold nulls generalize (especially to procedural worlds) is untested; no human baseline calibrates difficulty.
  • Judge-scored headline construct — RC statements rest on grounding κ = 0.751, and the larger judge did worse (0.609); the least reliable measurement in the pipeline.
  • Statistically fragile scaffold nulls — 118 episodes, 3 complete hyperedges, CIs straddling zero; falsification’s behavior claim rests on 39 episodes per arm.
  • Confounded vendor rows — model and harness change together, so 7.6/7.1 vs 3.5 and the lone exact prediction cannot be attributed to either factor.
  • Aging roster & floor effects — 2026 flagships with vendor-locked harnesses; consign grounds in only 8.0% of episodes, compressing the metric’s top; no cost-normalized headline (tokens per grounded block unreported).
05 — Open Questions

What to probe next

  1. Do the 46.1% retraction-without-replacement rate and the scaffold nulls persist in procedurally generated world families, or are they artifacts of one authored mechanism?
  2. Is the binding constraint the 200-action budget — or missing cross-domain memory? Would program-synthesis search over frames (DreamCoder-style) close the RC2/RC3 gap no baseline model closed?
  3. Can statement scoring be made fully deterministic — structured re-descriptions instead of free-text notes — removing the κ = 0.751 judge noise from the headline construct?
  4. How does the Δ_repr / Δ_align decomposition look on real scientific workbenches (DiscoveryWorld, ScienceAgentBench) where the “grant” is a domain ontology?
  5. Does falsification’s inquiry shift (18/39 → 5/39 no-intervention episodes) eventually convert into discovery beyond 600 actions, or remain behavior-only?
