> **一句话总结：** 作者提出可变精度随机舍入算法 VPSR（在 PRISM 向量化仿真库中实现，比 Verificarlo MCA 快最多 70.5 倍，并给出舍入决策在硬件浮点下符号精确的证明），在 DistilGPT-2 + WikiText-2 上逐站点比较随机舍入（SR）与就近舍入（RN）：MLP 下投影处 SR 大幅占优（t=6 时困惑度 71.97 vs RN 137.93，退化 15% vs 121%），而语言模型头处排序反转（t=8 时 RN 93.60 vs SR 135.56）；理论上的“漂移曲率 Q + 方差惩罚 V”损失分解解释了这一反转，且混合舍入配方（MLP 输出投影用 SR、其余用 RN）在相同逐站点精度下比全 RN 困惑度低 3.04%–28.17%，甚至能抵消掉一个有效位的精度损失。

> **TL;DR:** The paper asks whether low-precision transformer inference should use stochastic rounding (SR) or round-to-nearest (RN), and answers: it depends on the operation site. Holding the format fixed and varying only the rounding rule on DistilGPT-2 / WikiText-2, SR wins decisively at the MLP down-projection (perplexity 71.97 vs 137.93 for RN at t=6, i.e. 15% vs 121% degradation over the 62.51 full-precision reference), while RN wins at the language-model head (93.60 vs 135.56 at t=8) because SR's variance penalty is charged directly by cross-entropy there. A new variable-precision stochastic rounding algorithm (VPSR) in the PRISM emulator makes this sweep feasible, running up to 70.5x faster than Verificarlo's MCA random rounding. A drift-curvature/variance-penalty decomposition of the cross-entropy change — weighted by the Fisher information of the softmax — explains the reversal, and a mixed recipe (SR at the two output projections, RN elsewhere) beats matched all-RN by 3.04%–28.17% perplexity, recovering the accuracy of one extra significand bit.

## 核心贡献

- **VPSR 算法 + 正确性证明**：提出“可变精度随机舍入”（VPSR），把舍入决策从硬件格式解耦到任意虚拟精度 t ≤ p；证明该决策在硬件浮点运算中求值时符号精确（Theorem 1，借助 Sterbenz 引理，无需补偿运算），并在 PRISM 向量化舍入库中实现——比 Verificarlo 的 MCA 随机舍入最快快 70.5 倍（binary64 FMA），自身开销为原生执行的 8.5–32.1 倍。
- **向量级概率前向误差界**：将标量概率舍入误差分析扩展到完整矩阵乘法，给出向量 Bienaymé–Chebyshev 界：SR 的误差包络为 O(√n·u)，而 RN 为 O(n·u)——在 n=3072 的 MLP 下投影处，RN 界系数渐近 ~1537u，SR 仅 ~248u；低精度下 RN 界指数爆炸（t=4 时 8.11×10^60），SR 界保持温和（t=7 时 4.73）。
- **交叉熵损失的精确漂移-方差分解**：把低精度引起的损失变化精确分解为带号目标偏移 D 与 KL 散度，再对 KL 做二阶 Taylor 展开，得到漂移曲率项 Q 与由 Fisher 信息矩阵加权的方差惩罚 V——从机制上解释了为何 MLP 处 SR 占优（RN 的非均匀漂移被 Q 惩罚，SR 的噪声是 softmax 因平移不变性而忽略的均匀 logit 偏移），而 LM 头处排序反转（SR 的涨落跨 50,257 词表真正非均匀，被 V 直接惩罚）。
- **逐站点实证 + 混合舍入配方**：在 DistilGPT-2/WikiText-2 上按全局/组件/子层/深度四个层级扫精度，发现“首选舍入规则取决于站点”；混合配方（SR 用于 attn_c_proj 与 mlp_c_proj，RN 用于其余站点）在相同逐站点精度下比全 RN 困惑度低 3.04%（k=8）、5.81%（k=7）、28.17%（k=6），且混合 k=7（64.89）优于全 RN k=8（66.66）——SR 抵消了一个有效位的损失。
- **推翻“前向传播禁用 SR”的笼统共识**：Chmiel et al. (2023) 等认为前向传播应一律用 RN；本文证明该结论只对 LM 头成立，在长归约的 MLP 下投影处 SR 明显更优——舍入规则应作为站点级设计变量。

## Key Contributions

- **VPSR algorithm with an exactness proof**: variable-precision stochastic rounding decouples the rounding decision from the hardware format at any virtual precision t ≤ p; Theorem 1 proves the decision boundary is evaluated with exactly the right sign in hardware floating point (via Sterbenz's lemma, no compensated arithmetic), implemented in the vectorized PRISM library — up to 70.5x faster than Verificarlo's MCA random rounding, at 8.5–32.1x native overhead.
- **Vector probabilistic forward-error bound**: extends scalar stochastic-rounding error analysis to the full matrix multiplication of the MLP down-projection via a vector Bienaymé–Chebyshev construction that avoids the union bound: SR's error envelope is O(√n·u) versus O(n·u) for RN — coefficients ~248u vs ~1537u asymptotically at n=3072, with the RN bound exploding to 8.11×10^60 at t=4 while the SR bound stays at 4.73 at t=7.
- **Exact drift–variance decomposition of the loss**: the cross-entropy change under logit perturbations decomposes exactly into a signed target shift D plus KL distortion, whose second-order expansion yields a drift-curvature term Q and a variance penalty V weighted by the softmax's Fisher information — explaining mechanistically why SR's predominantly uniform logit shift is free at the MLP (shift invariance) while its genuinely non-uniform fluctuation is penalized at the 50,257-way LM head.
- **Site-resolved empirics and a mixed-rounding recipe**: sweeps over global/component/sublayer/depth scopes on DistilGPT-2 (WikiText-2, 1020 scored positions, reference PPL 62.51) show the preferred rounding rule flips by site; a mixed recipe (SR at attn_c_proj and mlp_c_proj, RN elsewhere at identical per-site precisions) lowers perplexity 3.04%–28.17% below matched all-RN, and mixed at k significand bits beats all-RN at k+1 bits.
- **Refines the "no SR in the forward pass" consensus**: the categorical rejection of forward-pass SR (Chmiel et al. 2023; NVIDIA's NVFP4 recipe) is shown to be too coarse — it holds at the head but not at long reductions, making the rounding rule a site-specific design variable.

## Methodology

**Emulation infrastructure.** The authors extend PRISM, a vectorized C++17 instruction-level rounding library, with the VPSR algorithm: given the exact result recovered by error-free transforms (TwoSum/TwoProdFMA) as (σ, τ), truncate σ to the virtual t-bit grid (σ̃), split the distance to the exact value into virtual truncation error ρ and hardware residual τ, sample a uniform threshold π on [0, ulp_t), and round away from σ̃ when π does not exceed ρ+τ. Theorem 1 (proved in Appendix A using Sterbenz's lemma) shows evaluating the decision boundary in hardware arithmetic preserves the exact sign, so no extended-precision overhead is needed; edge cases (binade crossings, subnormal virtual grids) are handled exactly. Verificarlo's compiler pass transparently rewrites PyTorch floating-point instructions into PRISM kernels (branch-free, SIMD via Google Highway, x86/ARM/RISC-V/WebAssembly); virtual precision and rounding mode are run-time settings requiring no recompilation. Benchmark: elementwise add/mul/div/fma on 65,536-value arrays, 30 runs on an Intel Xeon Gold 6130 — PRISM SR costs ×8.5–32.1 native versus ×88–597 for MCA RR (i.e., up to ×4.7 / ×70.5 faster in binary32/binary64), with timing nearly independent of t.

**Theory.** (1) For affine projections y = hW + b under SR, a vector Bienaymé–Chebyshev bound (Theorem 3) applies Markov's inequality to E‖e‖₂² directly, bypassing the union bound whose √(m/α) multiplier would inflate the bound ~680u at m=768: ‖ỹ−y‖₂ ≤ (1/√α)√((1+u²)^(n+1) −1)·(‖h‖‖W‖+‖b‖₂) with confidence multiplier 1/√α independent of m. (2) At the softmax, the excess loss δL(Δ) = g^⊤Δ + D_KL(p‖p̃) exactly; the KL term's quadratic form is governed by H = diag(p) − pp^⊤ (the Fisher information / cross-entropy Hessian), splitting E[δL] into drift D, drift-curvature Q = ½b^⊤Hb, variance penalty V = ½tr(HΣ), and remainder R. Shift invariance (Δ = c·1 leaves softmax unchanged) is the null space of both H and g.

**Experiments.** DistilGPT-2 (six pre-LayerNorm GPT-2 blocks, d_model=768, d_ff=3072, V=50,257), pretrained weights, no fine-tuning or calibration; WikiText-2 test split (leading 1% of lines → 1024 tokens → four 256-token contexts → 1020 scored positions); reference PPL₀ = 62.51 (t=24). Sweeps lower precision t ∈ [4,14] at one scope at a time (global, per component, per sublayer projection, per block prefix for mlp_c_proj), with operations outside the scope at t=24. Five seeds per SR configuration (95% Student-t CIs, df=4); the drift–variance decomposition uses 32 SR seeds with bias-corrected quadratic estimators on the same 1020 positions (L₀ = 4.1354 nats).

## Results & Highlights

- **MLP down-projection: SR wins by a wide margin.** With only mlp_c_proj reduced in all six blocks on WikiText-2, at t=6 SR reaches perplexity 71.97 ± 1.16 vs 137.93 for RN — a 15% degradation over the 62.51 full-precision reference against 121% for RN. At t=5 the gap is 175.65 vs 2029.83 (an 11.6x perplexity reduction), and at t=8 SR is within 0.3% of reference (62.69) while RN is within 2.0% (63.75). Extending mlp_c_proj reduction across block prefixes compounds RN's loss far faster: at t=4, going from block 0 to all six blocks raises perplexity ×22.7 under RN but only ×5.5 under SR.
- **Language-model head: the ordering reverses.** With only lm_head reduced, RN beats SR at every t ∈ [4,12]: at t=8, perplexity is 93.60 (RN) vs 135.56 ± 5.22 (SR); at t=6, 519.18 vs 5054 ± 464. Under uniform precision everywhere, RN similarly beats SR for all t ∈ [4,11] (97.68 vs 139.27 ± 3.58 at t=8), and reducing only the head reproduces most of that global gap (2.03 vs 2.27 nats SR-minus-RN excess loss at t=6) — the head drives the network-wide preference for RN.
- **Loss decomposition quantifies the mechanism.** Over t = 6..9 at the head, SR's variance penalty V_SR accounts for 100–105% of its excess loss (D_SR, Q_SR within 1.6 SE of zero); at t=6 RN's head excess is 2.12 nats (Q_RN = 1.58, R_RN = 0.50) against SR's 4.40 nats. At the MLP down-projection the picture inverts: SR's excess loss is 0.05 nats vs RN's 0.24 at t=6, with RN's drift curvature Q_RN = 0.29 nats versus Q_SR = 0.017, and SR's variance penalty collapsing to V_SR = 0.083 nats — fifty times smaller than at the head — because MLP-injected noise reaches the logits as a near-uniform vocabulary shift that the softmax ignores by shift invariance.
- **Mixed rounding beats matched all-RN, and saves a bit.** With SR at attn_c_proj (t=6) and mlp_c_proj (t=k) and RN at attn_c_attn (t=8), mlp_c_fc (t=8), and lm_head (t=11), the mixed configuration lowers perplexity vs matched all-RN by 3.04% at k=8 (64.63 ± 0.68 vs 66.66), 5.81% at k=7 (64.89 ± 0.98 vs 68.89), and 28.17% at k=6 (69.01 ± 2.07 vs 96.07). Mixed k=7 (64.89) sits below all-RN k=8 (66.66) outside its 95% interval — SR at the two output projections compensates for removing one significand bit at the MLP down-projection.
- **Emulator performance and error bounds.** PRISM SR incurs ×8.5–32.1 native overhead versus ×88–597 for Verificarlo MCA RR (up to ×70.5 faster for binary64 fma); precision changes cost <1% (PRISM) / ≤5% (MCA) runtime. The vector BC bound's exact RN/SR coefficient ratio is 14.37 at t=11 (3.482 vs 0.242), converging to the asymptotic 6.2 (1537u vs 248u); the RN bound hits 2.43×10^10 at t=7 and 8.11×10^60 at t=4 while SR stays at 4.73 at t=7.

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty.** The question "SR vs RN at inference" has a categorical prior answer (Chmiel et al. 2023: RN, because unbiasedness does not survive nonlinear composition), and the paper's contribution is to show the answer is site-dependent with a mechanistic explanation. That is a genuine refinement rather than a headline discovery, but the combination is new: a proved-exact variable-precision SR emulator, a vector (not scalar) probabilistic bound for full matrix multiplications, and an exact D/Q/V loss decomposition tied to Fisher information. The observation that SR's noise can manifest as a softmax-invisible uniform shift is the sharpest novel insight.

**Rigor.** High for a numerical-analysis paper: the exactness theorem is fully proved, the decomposition (Eq. 9) is exact before the second-order approximation, the finite-seed estimators are bias-corrected with jackknife standard errors, and claims are gated on 95% CIs (with non-significant differences explicitly flagged, e.g., attn_c_proj at k=8). The authors are candid that bounds are worst-case upper bounds ("realized RN errors rarely achieve worst-case alignment") and that the sweeps do not isolate n: only one site has n = 3072 and it also differs in its input distribution (GELU outputs, bounded below by ≈ −0.17), so the "reduction length" story is confounded with input-distribution effects — they say so themselves, which is to their credit.

**Red flags / weaknesses.** (1) **External validity is thin**: one 6-block, ~82M-parameter model, one leading 1024-token prefix of WikiText-2, and the text-fixed interval is explicitly conditional on those 1020 positions; no larger model, no other architecture (encoder, MoE), no downstream task beyond perplexity. (2) **The emulated format is not a hardware format**: VPSR shrinks the significand but keeps the binary32 exponent range, so nothing here directly informs FP8/NVFP4-style formats where exponent range, subnormals, and per-tensor scaling dominate — the exact regime where the cited hardware recipes (NVFP4) live. (3) **The practical precision window is narrow and arguably off-market**: at t=5 both rules more than double perplexity, and the headline mixed-recipe gains (28.17%) occur at k=6 bits; modern quantization operates at 4–8 bits with calibration and outlier handling that this deliberately excludes (no fine-tuning, no calibration — clean for science, but distant from deployed LLM.int8/AWQ-style pipelines). (4) **The attention module does not decompose additively** (separately reduced parts sum to 1.20/1.66 nats at t=4 vs 2.56/2.29 for the whole module) — a non-additivity the authors note but do not explain, which weakens the "per-site effects compose" intuition the mixed recipe relies on. (5) **Overhead**: ×8.5–32.1 emulation cost makes VPSR a measurement instrument, not a deployment path; there is no GPU implementation yet (only a prototype mentioned).

**Significance.** For the quantization community, the actionable claim — prefer SR at long reductions (MLP down-projections), keep RN at the pre-softmax head, and the mixed assignment can buy back roughly one significand bit — is testable on real hardware formats and worth testing. The D/Q/V decomposition is the piece most likely to outlive the specific DistilGPT-2 numbers.

## Strengths

- Pairs theory and measurement tightly: the same quantity decomposed in Eq. (9) is what the experiments validate, and the decomposition numbers (e.g., V_SR = 100–105% of head excess loss) quantitatively confirm the mechanism rather than merely illustrating it.
- The exactness proof for VPSR (Theorem 1 + full Appendix A) is the kind of unglamorous correctness work emulation studies usually skip; edge cases (binade crossings, subnormals) are handled and proved, not asserted.
- Statistically careful: five seeds with Student-t CIs per configuration, 32 seeds for the decomposition, jackknife SEs, explicit statements of what is conditioned on the fixed text, and significance labels on every ordering claim.
- Exceptional reproducibility: public code, scripts, and archived data (GitHub + Zenodo), run-time-configurable precision without recompilation, and an explicit AI-usage disclosure.
- Intellectually honest limitations section: exponent-range blind spot, united-RN-at-t=24 control rather than native RNE, single-model single-text scope, and no claim of a universal rounding assignment.

## Limitations

- Single small model (DistilGPT-2, six blocks) on a single 1020-position slice of one WikiText-2 prefix; the site ordering and composition effects are explicitly not yet replicated across texts, larger models, or broader tasks.
- Emulation retains the binary32 exponent range — conclusions concern significand/accumulator precision only and do not model FP8/NVFP4 exponent-range restrictions, which limits direct transfer to current hardware recipes.
- The O(√n·u) advantage of SR is confounded in the sweep: only one site has n = 3072, and it also has a distinctive input distribution (GELU outputs), so reduction length per se is not isolated.
- Emulation overhead (×8.5–32.1) and the absence of a GPU port restrict scale; non-target operations run united RN at t=24 rather than native ties-to-even, and the impact of exact-tie frequency is uncharacterized.
- The mixed-recipe evaluation covers three k values (6, 7, 8) on one model; the bounds identify relevant quantities but, as the authors state, do not establish a universal rounding assignment or quantitative portability of the gains.

## Relevance

这篇论文正中用户「极限性能优化下的可靠性」核心方向：它把舍入规则当作逐站点可调的数值可靠性设计变量，量化了低精度推理中确定性 RN 漂移（系统性、方向性误差累积）与随机舍入方差之间的权衡，并给出了可直接检验的混合舍入配方；其对「系统性漂移 vs 无偏噪声在下游如何被放大或忽略」的分析，对推理侧 SDC/静默错误累积的机理理解也有直接参考价值。主要保留意见是实验仅覆盖 82M 参数的 DistilGPT-2 与仿真精度（未建模 FP8/NVFP4 的指数范围），结论向生产级量化栈的迁移仍待验证。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the site reversal (SR at MLP down-projections, RN at the head) hold for real hardware formats (FP8/E5M2, NVFP4 microscaling) where exponent range and per-block scaling, not just significand width, shape the error?
- Does the drift-curvature Q vs variance-penalty V trade-off predict site preferences in larger models (7B+) and other architectures (encoder-only, MoE with expert-parallel reductions), or is the GELU-output positivity at mlp_c_proj doing most of the explanatory work?
- Can the D/Q/V decomposition be computed cheaply at calibration time to *derive* a rounding assignment per model, rather than running the full site-by-site sweep?
- How does the mixed recipe interact with standard quantization machinery (per-channel scaling, outlier isolation à la LLM.int8/SmoothQuant) that this study deliberately excluded — are the gains additive or subsumed?
- Is SR's unbiasedness a liability for reproducibility and silent-data-corruption auditing (bitwise irreproducible outputs across runs), and does that cost matter for inference serving deployments that would otherwise adopt it?
