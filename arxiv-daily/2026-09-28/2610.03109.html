Critical edge

The theory does not close. Theorem 1 protects marginal influence, not attention — that link is an unproven empirical approximation (ρ=0.635), with the ω-weighting a free parameter doing real work. And the consensus corpus stops at 2,048 mostly-English tokens: untested at the long contexts where eviction actually matters.

01

One observable, four moves

distilled from Methodology

Define

Post-softmax attention is marginalized over query positions, weighted by what each key position ships through the head (ω = ‖W_O v‖) — one heads×tokens matrix M, both factors from a single forward pass.

m_s⁽ʰ⁾ = (1/n) Σ_t ω_s · A_{s,t}⁽ʰ⁾

Reduce ×2

Sum over heads → the per-token profile a(X), a distribution over positions (text property). Sum over tokens → the per-head profile p_h, one vector per model (model property).

Compare

67 open models (38 families, 28 architectures, 0.27B–117B, MoE + MHA) vs a leave-one-out consensus over 2,211 pairs on 850 Pile documents; 10 pythia-410m seeds as ceiling. Heads have no cross-model names, so per-head profiles are binned into 10 depth bins and tested half-vs-half.

Theorize & apply

Theorem 1: models with near-equal next-token logits have near-equal per-token Jacobian influence — marginal attention is its first-order per-head approximation. Application: p_h becomes Head Mass, a static per-head KV budget computed once on 200 pretraining docs; online scoring stays training-free (Expected Attention).

03a

Strengths

  • One observable, two clean reductions — each checked against the right null (different document / permuted heads).
  • Exceptional breadth: 67 models incl. MoE and MHA, plus attention-free corroboration — vs the usual 2–3 model comparisons.
  • Theory and practice genuinely connect: the protected quantity enables the cheap token score; the private quantity makes a static budget meaningful.
  • Cost argument: offline minutes + training-free scoring vs KVzip’s per-document double forward and hours of surrogate training.
  • Reproducibility first-class: library, end-to-end notebooks, all 67 checkpoints, unchanged harnesses, explicit AI-use statement.
03b

Limitations

  • Theory covers influence, not attention: the link is empirical only (ρ=0.635; 0.534 unweighted).
  • Corpus bound: consensus mostly English, ≤2,048 tokens per document; long-context and multilingual generality unverified.
  • Context 4096 only: HM+EA falls behind both Qwen3 targets at 16× and never beats KVzip on phi-4 / Mistral-7B at high compression.
  • HM+Guide is a probe: the stronger score tilt needs an extra small-model forward per document — not servable.
  • Assumptions validated empirically, not proven for real models; near-identifiability with constants of unknown tightness.
04

Questions for further exploration

  1. Does the per-token consensus survive 32k–128k contexts and non-English text, where sink tokens and long-range structure dominate?
  2. Can the influence→attention link be proven — does ω-weighted marginal attention itself satisfy the three assumptions?
  3. Is the shared profile behaviorally consequential — predicting stitching, transfer, or distillation — or an epiphenomenon of shared next-token objectives?
  4. Why does the static Head-Mass budget underperform on both Qwen3 targets at 16× — hybrid attention, or a fundamental need for per-document signals?
  5. Could the Guide tilt become servable — a cached per-position-type correction — for KVzip-level quality without the extra forward pass?
