> **一句话总结：** 该文定位了 FP8 原生训练中注意力后向传播"过期 delta"（stale delta）失效机理——前向 FP8 概率 P 与后向重算的 dP 不匹配破坏了 softmax 梯度的行和为零不变式，在 5.29B 规模上使验证损失从 1.3349 恶化到 1.9327、RULER-8K 从 53.5% 崩到 16.3%——并提出 Delta-Matching（用 FP32 概率与 FP8 后向操作数的 FP32 乘积重算 δ），在不改架构、不加辅助前向的情况下，在全部 7 个注意力核心矩阵乘上实现原生 FP8，且在 569M–5.29B、GDN/GQA、GDN/MLA、KDA/GQA 等所有配置下将验证损失对齐到 BF16/FP32 参考的 0.0025 以内。

> **TL;DR:** FP8 attention is the last holdout in native 8-bit LLM training because the saved-output backward shortcut mixes FP8-quantized probabilities with mismatched quantized gradients, producing a "stale delta" that violates the softmax zero-row-sum invariant. The authors prove this breaks training dynamics (selective RMSNorm gain amplification, softmax saturation) and propose **Delta-Matching**: recompute δ_i = Σ_j P_ij·dP̂_ij from FP32 probabilities, restoring the invariant with no architecture change, smaller batch, or auxiliary forward. On a 1.67B GDN/GQA hybrid (30B Nemotron-CC tokens) Delta-Matching reaches validation CE 1.4162 vs 1.4178 for the BF16/FP32 mixed-precision reference and 1.8970 for naive FP8 with stale delta; at 5.29B it stays within 0.0025 CE of BF16/FP32 while stale delta degrades RULER-8K from 53.5% to 16.3%. The FP8 kernel is even faster on H100 than BF16 FlashAttention-3.

## 核心贡献

- **失效机理定位：** 推导并实证了 FP8 注意力训练中"过期 delta"的来源——FlashAttention 的 saved-output 捷径使用的前向概率 hat(P) 与后向重算的 hat(dP) 来自不同的量化路径，概率量化误差与梯度量化误差无法抵消，破坏了 softmax 后向梯度的行和为零不变式（zero-row-sum invariant）。在 1.67B 检查点探针上测得行和残差达 1.1×10⁻²–4.7×10⁻² rad。
- **训练动力学分析：** 行和残差在行间近似抵消，但在通道内累积：被"锐化行"偏好的通道出现持续的 RMSNorm 增益上推，少数通道占据受影响层中 Σ_c(g_q,c·g_k,c)² 的 99% 以上，促进 softmax 饱和——解释了为何小模型/短训练看不出问题而大规模/长训练崩溃。
- **Delta-Matching 方法：** 用 FP32 概率 P（量化前）与 FP8 后向操作数反量化的 FP32 乘积计算匹配的 δᵢ^matched = Σ_j P_ij·dP̂_ij，在所述数值假设下证明恢复了不变式；仅需修改 softmax 后向核心，无需架构改动、更小 global batch 或辅助前向输出，使全部 7 个注意力矩阵乘（2 前向 + 5 后向）均为原生 FP8。
- **消融证据链：** 仅匹配输出梯度精度（consistent dO）仍有 1.4667 vs 1.4178 的验证损失差距，证明概率量化失配必须同时修正。
- **规模化验证：** 失效随规模/时长加剧——569M 时 stale delta 仅 +0.017 CE，5.29B 时 +0.60 CE 且 RULER-8K 从 53.5% 崩至 16.3%；8K→64K 上下文扩展中损失差距在约 19,000 步（20B token）后才超过 0.01，与"跨更新累积的优化误差"假说一致。Delta-Matching 在所有架构（GDN/GQA、GDN/MLA、KDA/GQA）、变体（NoPE、去 QK norm、head 256）、规模上匹配 BF16/FP32。

## Key Contributions

- **Failure mechanism:** Derives and empirically confirms the *stale delta* failure in FP8 attention training — FlashAttention's saved-output shortcut (δ_i^stale = ⟨dO_i, Ō_i⟩) mismatches the FP8-quantized P̂ used in the forward with recomputed quantized dP̂ in the backward, so probability-quantization and gradient-quantization errors no longer cancel and the softmax zero-row-sum invariant Σ_j dS_ij = 0 is violated. Probe measurements on 1.67B checkpoints show row-sum residuals of 1.1×10⁻²–4.7×10⁻² rad.
- **Dynamics analysis:** Stale delta residual ε_i acts as a per-row logit-update bias that sharpens or flattens rows; channel-wise, it selectively amplifies RMSNorm gains (a few channels account for >99% of Σ_c(g_q,c g_k,c)² in affected layers), promoting softmax saturation — explaining why small-model/short-run evaluations conceal the failure.
- **Delta-Matching:** Computes the backward correction δ_i^matched = Σ_j P_ij·dP̂_ij from unquantized FP32 probabilities and FP32 products of FP8 backward operands, provably restoring the invariant under stated assumptions. It enables native block-scaled FP8 in *every* forward and backward attention-core matmul with no architectural changes, no smaller global batch, and no auxiliary forward output.
- **Controlled ablation:** Matching output-gradient precision alone ("consistent dO") leaves validation CE at 1.4667 vs BF16/FP32's 1.4178 — the probability-quantization mismatch must also be corrected.
- **Scale/duration characterization:** The failure grows with scale and duration (569M: +0.017 CE; 5.29B: +0.60 CE; context-extension gap >0.01 only after ~19,000 steps / 20B tokens), consistent with accumulated optimization error over updates. Delta-Matching matches BF16/FP32 across 569M–5.29B, three hybrid architectures, NoPE / no-QK-norm / head-256 variants, and both AdamW- and Muon-style recipes.

## Methodology

**Root cause.** Softmax backward computes dS = P ⊙ (dP − δ) with δ_i = Σ_j P_ij dP_ij, giving Σ_j dS_ij = 0 in exact arithmetic (the row-shift invariance of softmax). Naive FP8 FlashAttention retains BF16 dO but reuses the forward's saved output for the shortcut δ_i^stale = ⟨dO_i, Ō_i⟩; the products Ō = P̂V̂ and dŌ = dOV̂ use FP32 accumulation over *quantized* operands, so the row sum decomposes into a probability-quantization error plus a gradient-quantization error (Eq. 2) that do not cancel.

**Diagnosis experiments.** A probe recomputes BF16 activations on the final 1.67B GDN/GQA checkpoints across six attention layers, measuring window-averaged row-sum residuals (1.1–4.7×10⁻² rad) and tracking three dynamical signatures: row sharpening/flattening of shared FP8 logits deltas, consistent upward pressure on favored channels, and selective RMSNorm gain amplification (Figure 3).

**Delta-Matching.** For fixed normalized P and quantized dP̂, zero row sums in dS_ij = P_ij(dP̂_ij − c_i) uniquely determine c_i = δ_i^matched = Σ_j P_ij dP̂_ij (Eq. 4), restoring the invariant up to FP32 rounding before dS quantization. In the probe, median residuals with Delta-Matching rise only from 1.4×10⁻⁷–7.1×10⁻⁷ (FP32) to 2.7×10⁻³–3.4×10⁻³ after FP8 casting, while stale-delta residuals remain 5.3–6.7× larger across layers. No auxiliary forward output or precision promotion of dP is needed.

**Setup.** Base model: a 1.67B hybrid of 18 Gated DeltaNet (GDN) + 6 GQA layers, 16 query / 4 KV heads, head dim 128, pretrained on 30B Nemotron-CC tokens at 8K context, two seeds per experiment. Baselines: BF16/FP32 mixed precision (MP), cuDNN/Transformer-Engine FP8, naive FP8 (stale delta), naive FP8 with consistent dO, and Delta-Matching. Evaluation: validation CE on held-out Nemotron-CC; RULER 4K–64K and extraction recall for in-context recall; TriviaQA, MMLU, and nine commonsense benchmarks (CS9 aggregate). Variants cover NoPE, QK-norm removal, head dim 256, GDN/MLA and KDA/GQA hybrids, 569M/5.29B scales, and 8K→64K context extension on ProLong-derived data at peak LR 8×10⁻⁵.

## Results & Highlights

- **1.67B GDN/GQA validation CE:** Delta-Matching 1.4162 beats the BF16/FP32 MP reference (1.4178) and every FP8 baseline — naive FP8 with stale delta 1.8970, consistent-dO-only 1.4667, cuDNN/TE FP8 1.6105. Consistent dO closes only ~63% of the stale-delta gap, isolating the probability-quantization error as the residual cause.
- **1.67B downstream:** CS9 commonsense average 59.0±0.3 for Delta-Matching vs 58.9±0.4 (BF16/FP32) and 53.2±1.0 (stale delta); MMLU 35.0% vs 35.4% (BF16) and a catastrophic 2.7% under stale delta. The one soft spot: RULER-8K 47.8% vs BF16's 52.9% (stale delta: 19.8%).
- **Attention variants:** Stale-delta validation CE gaps vs BF16/FP32 reach 0.2362 (NoPE) and 0.6574 (head dim 256); stale-delta training diverges outright without QK normalization, while Delta-Matching stays within 0.0011 CE of the reference across all three variants.
- **Architectures:** Stale delta raises validation CE by +0.5090 (GDN/MLA) and +0.4283 (KDA/GQA) over BF16/FP32; Delta-Matching is within 0.0006 of reference for both (1.4181 vs 1.4178; 1.3985 vs 1.3941), with CS9 59.2 vs 59.2 and 60.0 vs 60.2 respectively.
- **Scale dependence:** At 569M stale delta shows only a modest gap (CE 1.5399 vs 1.5233), but at 5.29B it reaches CE 1.9327 vs 1.3349, with RULER-8K collapsing 53.5%→16.3% and CS9 63.1%→45.4%. Delta-Matching stays within 0.0025 CE of BF16/FP32 at both scales and even exceeds the reference RULER means at 5.29B (RULER-4K 69.2 vs 65.6; RULER-8K 58.6 vs 53.5), with slightly lower MMLU (41.1 vs 42.0) and CS9 (62.0 vs 63.1).
- **Context extension (8K→64K):** The stale-delta loss gap exceeds 0.01 only after ~19,000 optimizer steps (~20B tokens), and one of two cuDNN/TE FP8 seeds diverges during extension. Delta-Matching tracks BF16/FP32 (train CE 1.66 vs 1.66) with RULER-64K 48.6±1.6 vs 48.0±4.5 and exceeds stale delta on all recall metrics.
- **Hardware:** Despite recomputing δ, the Delta-Matching kernel achieves lower combined forward+backward latency on H100 than Hopper-optimized BF16 FlashAttention-3.
- **Mitigations that don't fix it:** QK normalization (Kimi-K2-style) prevents catastrophic collapse but leaves a substantial gap; NoPE plus lower-LR context extension delays but does not eliminate degradation.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty is incremental over Qiu & Yao (2026).** The paper itself credits Qiu & Yao (2026) with identifying the saved-output rounding error in BF16/FP32 mixed-precision GPT-2 training and with the "direct backward correction" that Delta-Matching explicitly builds on. The genuinely new parts are the two-term (probability vs. gradient quantization) decomposition, the dynamics characterization at hybrid-architecture scale, and the systematic scale/duration study — solid, but the core fix is a one-line delta recompute, and the paper's framing ("closing the final gap") oversells the conceptual distance from prior work.
- **Scale of validation is still modest for the claim.** All experiments run at ≤5.29B parameters on ≤30B-token pretraining (plus 28K-step extension) — two orders of magnitude below frontier runs where accumulated optimization error is the stated concern. The paper's own evidence that degradation onsets later with the wrong recipe (gap >0.01 only after ~19K steps) means a 30B-token clean result does not guarantee a 1T-token clean result; the claim "matches BF16/FP32" is proven only within this regime.
- **Delta-Matching is not a uniform win.** RULER-8K at 1.67B is 47.8% vs BF16's 52.9% (−5.1 pts), MMLU 35.0 vs 35.4, extraction recall slightly below reference at 5.29B (70.3 vs 71.0), and KDA/GQA RULER-4K stays at 60.7 vs BF16's 65.6. These dips are downplayed in the narrative.
- **Proof is conditional.** Invariant restoration holds "under the stated numerical assumptions" (normalized P, identical effective V̂ across passes, no accumulation/output-storage/reduction rounding); real kernels violate several of these, and the authors themselves note casting can introduce a signed offset that leaves residuals 5.3–6.7× larger than the FP32 probe — the invariance is approximate in practice.
- **n=2 seeds** with standard errors reported, but two seeds are thin for claims about divergent vs stable dynamics (e.g., the one diverging cuDNN/TE extension run out of two).
- **Single data recipe** (Nemotron-CC, English-centric) and evaluation-only-in-BF16 constrain external validity; no vision or multilingual pretraining is tested.
- Positively: the failure-mode evidence (stale delta doubling RMSNorm gains by 20K steps, >99% concentration of gradient correlations in a few channels) is unusually well-instrumented, and the honest reporting of the RULER-8K dip and the non-fixing mitigations (QK norm, NoPE) raises trust.

## Strengths

- Clear causal chain from theory (invariant violation) to mechanism (channel-wise gain amplification, softmax saturation) to fix (matched delta), with each link supported by controlled experiments.
- The consistent-dO ablation is exactly the right control: it isolates probability-quantization error as the component that still degrades training (1.4667 vs 1.4162 CE).
- Scale-and-duration characterization (569M looks fine, 5.29B collapses; extension gap appears only after 20B tokens) delivers a practical warning that small-scale FP8 pilots can hide a real failure.
- Practicality: no architecture change, no auxiliary forward output, no reduced batch; the kernel is faster than BF16 FlashAttention-3 on H100, so the method costs nothing.
- Broad coverage: three hybrid attention architectures, three attention variants, two extra scales, context extension, and AdamW/Muon optimizers — plus a promise to release implementation, models, and data recipes.

## Limitations

- Validated only up to 5.29B parameters / ~30B tokens (+28K extension steps); frontier-scale behavior with accumulated optimization error remains extrapolated.
- Two seeds per configuration; some headline contrasts (e.g., divergent vs stable extension runs) rest on single-run events.
- Residual RULER-8K deficit at 1.67B (47.8 vs 52.9 for BF16/FP32) is not fully explained, and KDA/GQA RULER-4K remains ~5 pts below the reference.
- The invariant proof assumes away accumulation, output-storage, and reduction rounding that real attention kernels exhibit; measured post-cast residuals are ~10⁻³ rad, not zero.
- Single dataset (Nemotron-CC), BF16-only evaluation path, and no code released at review time despite the reproducibility statement.
- Requires modifying the attention backward kernel itself (δ recompute inside the kernel), so adoption depends on kernel vendors rather than a drop-in wrapper.

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does Delta-Matching's near-parity hold at frontier scale (hundreds of billions of tokens), where the paper's own accumulation-error hypothesis predicts new failure modes could emerge?
- What explains the residual RULER-8K gap at 1.67B (47.8% vs 52.9%) while 5.29B RULER exceeds the reference — is it a seed/noise artifact or a systematic mid-scale long-retrieval deficit?
- Can the stale-delta row-sum residual (measured at 10⁻² rad) serve as an online early-warning monitor for FP8 training divergence, analogous to loss-spike detectors?
- How does the method interact with NVFP4/MXFP8 block-scaled formats on Blackwell, where two more precision tiers sit below FP8?
- Would the same probability-vs-gradient quantization decomposition reveal additional invariant violations in linear-layer FP8 GEMMs, or is attention uniquely exposed?
