01 Methodology

Two-stage pipeline with human validation. Happy-path scenarios generated by a 5-stage LLM pipeline; adversarial variants modify tool responses across 5 modes (Massive Data, Error Conditions, Multiple Matches, Prompt Injection, Partial Data). Each scenario runs end-to-end on the production framework, not a sandbox.

1
Intent & prompt gen — self-contained + incomplete prompts from agent config.
2
Tool response sim — param→response maps with exact/semantic match, implicit args.
3
Ground-truth trace — expected execution graph (tools, args, deps).
4
Judge — Claude Sonnet 4.5 scores Response + Action correctness; 93.3% human alignment (56/60).
5
Failure taxonomy — ITS · IMP · MTC · RTC · ITO across 6 models × 3 frameworks.

02 Two Failure Regimes

Strong models MTC 52–57%
GPT-5.2 Pro · GPT-OSS-120B · Gemini-3-Prounder-call
Weak models ITS ≤25% · RTC 29–33%
DeepSeek-V3.2 · Qwen3-Maxmis-select / over-call

Long-horizon planning (turn depth −31.1 pts) is a steeper bottleneck than tool-set size (−15.9 pts). ITO is the rarest failure across all configs (0.6–4.4%).

03 Results

  • 74.8 best Overall (Claude Opus 4.5 + Vectara); no Calendar model > 62.0.
  • Framework choice matters little: ≤ 2–3 pts cross-framework delta.
  • Guardrails beat system prompts on all 6 models: +2.8 → +7.7.
  • System prompts near-zero or negative for strong models (Gemini +0.3, GPT-5.2 Pro −0.3).
  • Distribution: 68.6% non-adversarial, 31.4% adversarial.

04 Strengths & Limitations

Strengths

  • First benchmark on production frameworks, not sandboxes.
  • Failure taxonomy gives direct fix targets per model tier.
  • 6×3×6×5 coverage; clean attribution to model layer.
  • 19.9% / 0.5% guardrail precision is compelling.

Limitations

  • Guardrails proven only on LlamaIndex, not LangChain/Vectara.
  • Shared Claude judge → self-eval risk; only 60 aligned samples.
  • Attacks limited to tool-response mutation.
  • Deterministic-path bias may under-rate flexible agents.

05 Open Questions

Does Claude Opus 4.5's lead survive a cross-family judge (GPT judging Claude)?
Can the 19.9% / 0.5% guardrail result reproduce on LangChain and Vectara?
Does multi-equivalent-path scoring change the strong-model MTC dominance?
Would user-side prompt injection shift the strong/weak failure dichotomy?
Is the turn-depth cliff a context-management or plan-state-tracking failure?
