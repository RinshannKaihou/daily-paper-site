Auditing Evidence Use in Medical LLM Diagnosis

Key Finding

Across 5 medical LLMs, diagnosis accuracy is a poor proxy for evidence-use fidelity — real failures concentrate in negated or absent findings.

Why This Matters

Medical LLM benchmarks grade only the final answer; this audit adds a structured evidence-use profile to flag unsafe shortcuts before clinical deployment.

Critical Edge

The clinical review is an enriched sample — precision and mechanism shares are descriptive, not population prevalence estimates.

01 Methodology

Decompose each case into evidence units, score candidate diagnoses under controlled subsets, and mine low-order interactions in the diagnostic margin.

Diagnostic margin

v_d(T) = s_T(d) − max_{c_j≠d} s_T(c_j)    (next-token option-letter log-prob)

A prompt-conditioned preference gap, not a calibrated probability — biased by option-letter and position priors.

Interaction mining

I_d(S) = Σ_{T⊆S} (−1)^{|S|−|T|} v_d(T)
  • Role-aware taxonomy: supporting · competitor-supporting · negated/excluding · background/risk · local/irrelevant — the same field plays different roles across diagnoses.
  • Discovery ≠ failure: large interactions consistent with differential diagnosis are not bugs; only negated/absent and local-cue misuse are flagged, and only after stability + clinical review.

Models & data

  • 5 open-weight LLMs: Qwen2.5-7B, OpenBioLLM-8B, MedGemma-4B, BioMistral-7B, Llama3-Med42-8B.
  • DDXPlus (500 cases · 255 interventions/case) · CupCase & MedCase (200 each · 63/case).

02 Results & Highlights

Accuracy ≠ FidelityDDXPlus tops out 74.0% (OpenBio) / 73.4% (Med42), but evidence-use ordering does not track accuracy; MedCase hardest (best 55.0%).
Mechanism mixFaithful support + conflict/cancellation dominate DDXPlus at 86.6–89.6%; negated/absent misuse is only 1.2–3.0%.
Clinical review5 blinded PhD reviewers, Fleiss' κ = 0.82, 130 items: 111 valid · 8 questionable · 11 invalid — all in the negated/absent bucket.
Counterfactual probeRemoving "MG family history" cuts Qwen margin 9.50 (confidence amplification); removing a hernia skin field raises margin +2.25 (local irrelevance, not global reliance).

03 Strengths & Limitations

▲ Strengths

  • Role-aware separation of differential-diagnosis conflict from genuine evidence misuse — avoids treating every negative interaction as a bug.
  • Evidence pyramid: mechanism distribution → clinical review → targeted counterfactual → stability filter → unit-selection sensitivity.
  • Cleanly distinguishes local incoherence vs global reliance (Case A vs B) via targeted counterfactuals.
  • Cross-dataset robustness across structured (DDXPlus) and narrative (CupCase/MedCase) case styles.
  • Transparent reporting: Wilson/bootstrap intervals, self-declared limitations, descriptive-not-prevalence framing.

▽ Limitations

  • Margin = option-letter log-prob, biased by letter/position priors; candidate reordering only weakens, doesn't calibrate.
  • Enriched review sample — precision/shares are descriptive, not population prevalence; cannot extrapolate failure rates.
  • Limited coverage: 5 mid-scale open models (≤8B); no GPT-4 / Med-PaLM–class closed systems.
  • Order-ablation is a DDXPlus retrieval check, not proof that order-3 is sufficient in general.
  • Measures prompt-conditioned preference — not internal causal reasoning or patient-safety evidence.

04 Questions for Further Exploration

Extend to closed flagships (GPT-4o, Med-PaLM 2, Claude, Gemini-Med) — does scale change the negated/absent failure structure?
Move from enriched to probability sampling for true prevalence estimates with tighter Wilson intervals.
Auto-learn evidence roles instead of hand-curated taxonomy, especially for narrative cases.
Replace margin with calibrated candidate probabilities — does the failure queue composition shift?
Targeted mitigation: negation augmentation / role-aware RLHF to cut the stable-invalid rate.
Run on real clinical records — how does free-text extraction error propagate into interaction scores?
Push audits to order 4–5 — new failures, or just uninterpretable noise?
