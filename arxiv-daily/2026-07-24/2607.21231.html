C · Three converging structural findings

30–100PCA components explain 99% of the 2048–4096-dim trajectory variance — paths are low-dimensional.
≈2×perfectly-crammed tokens per layer-doubling; capacity tracks reconstructor depth and width.

Methodology

Protocol
  • 01Progressive token addition. Grow prefix x^(k)=(x_1..x_k), warm-start e^(k)←e^(k-1), stop only when 100% reconstruction is unreachable — a binary success/fail boundary.
  • 02Low-rank projection. Parameterize e = Wz + b, k≪d; test sample-specific vs corpus-shared subspace.
  • 03Information gain. IG = H_LM − H_{comp+LM} in bits — the headline compression metric.
  • 04Attention knockout. Mask pre-softmax logits onto the embedding at position 0 — per-layer, forward- & reverse-cumulative on the 32-layer Llama-3.1-8B.
  • 05Reconstructor sweep. Truncate to first N∈{1,2,4,8} layers, repair with a short fineweb-edu finetune, then cram.
  • 06Models. Pythia, Llama 3, SmolLM2, Gemma 3 (PG19 + Fanfics); Qwen3 & SmolLM3 for capacity.

Strengths & Limitations

Assessment

Strengths

  • Sharp protocol fix for a real failure: 99%-accuracy / teacher-forcing hides catastrophic autoregressive collapse.
  • Novel low-dim geometry: 30–100 PCA comps, "dwell-and-leap" path shape, per-sample vs shared subspace probe.
  • Genuinely causal: three knockout protocols with forward/reverse asymmetry beat correlational attention-sink claims.
  • Strong controls: random-embedding MMLU and convergent-subset eval isolate the optimized embedding's effect.
  • Cross-family replication (Pythia, Llama 3, SmolLM2, Gemma 3; + Qwen3/SmolLM3) with public code.

Limitations

  • ~2× slower than full cramming and un-batchable — each sample converges at its own rate.
  • No reconciliation: activation-alignment (n=16) only partially closes the reconstruction/semantics gap.
  • Thin generative eval — MMLU is one benchmark; free-gen judge uses 16 samples, one model, one length.
  • Gemma 3 partially fails the protocol (0% convergence on HellaSwag); logit-softcap cause is speculative.
  • Subspace universality shown by failure, not construction; alternative bases not ruled out.
  • Circuit-level identity of the "unpacking circuit" deferred to future work.

Open Questions

Further work
  1. Can an amortized feed-forward compressor yield embeddings both perfectly reconstructable and semantically faithful — or is there a fundamental trade-off?
  2. Does scaling activation-alignment close the gap on full benchmarks, not just 16 samples?
  3. What specific circuits implement cramming? Would sparse auto-probing reveal a reusable unpacking circuit?
  4. Is the 8.7-token transfer collapse a property of cramming, or would distillation find a universal subspace?
  5. Does the "attention mass ≠ causal importance" dissociation generalize to BOS / long-context sink tokens?
  6. Does the depth/width substitutability hold for non-transformer architectures (Mamba, RWKV)?
