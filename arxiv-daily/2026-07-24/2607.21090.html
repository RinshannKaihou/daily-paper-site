Training Large Language Models for Self-Explanation Faithfulness

Turning a faithfulness metric into an RL reward lifts LLM self-explanation correlation from 0.036 → 0.664 — and only RL, not SFT, can teach a model to disclose what it already detects.

Why this matters

First “change the parameters” path to chain-of-thought faithfulness — gives the oversight stack a real signal instead of a fabricated reasoning trail.

Critical edgecaveat

Training is off-policy: influence labels are frozen at T₀. Some of the gain may be the policy drifting toward stale labels, not purely better disclosure.

Methodology §3 · setup

Counterfactual single-factor intervention Δ flips input X → X′; faithfulness = does the explanation mention what actually changed the decision?

  • Influence I = 1{Y′ ≠ Y} — did the decision flip?
  • Mention M = 1{Δ mentioned in Z′} — does the explanation cite it?
  • Dataset faithfulness Phi-CCT = Corr(I, M)
  • Per-sample reward r = 1{M ⇔ I} — balanced 6k splits so silence / over-mention both score 0.
  • Two hypotheses: H1 SFT trains influence introspection (Yes/No labels); H2 GRPO trains influence–mention alignment (sample 16× per prompt, update on z′ only).
  • Interventions: random insertions (NLTK POS-slot + rejection sampling) and user-bias (“my teacher believes A”).
  • Anti-reward-hack: Completion Length + Overlap Ratio monitored; gains ≠ shortening or prompt parroting.

Core insight: SFT teaches detection, only RL teaches disclosure — a clean two-stage dichotomy. Cross-intervention transfer is asymmetric (Llama random→bias works; reverse and Qwen do not).

Strengths & limitations assessment

▲ Strengths

  • First direct RL target for explanatory faithfulness (vs FRODO/MARIO).
  • Detection–disclosure split — simple, transferable finding.
  • Anti-hack: balanced data + two behavioral monitors.
  • Honest self-review — authors flag every limitation themselves.

▼ Limitations

  • Off-policy label drift unquantified — on-policy resampling is the key follow-up.
  • Morphological mention match misses paraphrase → underestimates Phi-CCT.
  • Scale-limited: 6k × 4 datasets, 8B × 2 families only.
  • No plausibility / human study — shorter ≠ more useful.

Open questions for further work follow-ups

  1. On-policy re-sampling — how much of the 0.664 survives once stale T₀ labels are refreshed? This is the real validity test.
  2. Does the dichotomy hold at 70B / 405B? Stronger models may disclose under SFT alone, collapsing the split.
  3. Multi-objective reward (faithfulness + task accuracy) — can both be held, or does accuracy regress and invite new hacks?
  4. Semantic mention detection — do conclusions change once paraphrased mentions are counted?
  5. Root cause of model-family transfer gap — why only Llama random→bias? Tokenizer, pretraining, or RLHF stage?
  6. Compatibility with RLHF — does faithful-disclosure survive a standard alignment pass?
  7. Net human-oversight utility — shorter, more precise explanations: helpful or context-starving?
