§ 02 — How it works

02Methodology

The paper reframes personality as a dynamic cognitive process rather than a static trait vector. A 132-statement human Jungian test (Felmonon 2026) is adapted into an LLM Likert protocol, scored by w = 0.7P + 0.3(6−N), then S = (100·w − 1)/4.

  • NarrationDB — from SWCPQ, keep 20 lowest-MOE tags per character; Llama-3.1-70B writes self-narrations, 8B role-plays; characters populate the full 8-D Jungian space.
  • Steering extraction — per function, top/bottom k=10 characters answer T=5 open questions; cluster-mean latent difference at layer n gives VCf,n.
  • Injection — Lat + λ·V/‖V‖, λ-swept over [−6.875, +6.875] at best layer n*; runs with <80% task completion are discarded.
  • Multi-dim probe — pivot score Spivot = OddSq(NSNe) + OddSq(NSTi); Least-Squares backtracking tests whether VNe+Ti = w₁VTi + w₂VNe.
  • Seed robustness — shuffle seed affects LLM answers, so 30 seeds are aggregated per test pass.

§ 03 — Results

03Function-by-Function Control

All eight functions are monotonically controllable via single-layer steering. Strongest are the perceiving (irrational) functions; Fi is the lone laggard — attributed to ethics post-training pinning it high (low Fi correlates with low moral identity, Jung 1971).

§ 04 — Mechanistic geometry

04Activation-Space Geometry

PCA of the layer-12 steering vectors (PC1 49.9%, PC2 24.4%) shows rational functions cluster close to their introverted–extraverted counterparts, while irrational functions sit farther apart — matching Jung's original distinction. Stable across layers 7–12.

§ 05 — Appraisal

05Strengths vs. Limitations

● Strengths

  • Novel framing — Jungian processes fit human–LLM dynamics better than static Big Five.
  • Multi-axis in one framework: single-dim control, layer map, geometry, non-linearity.
  • NarrationDB is reusable, theory-grounded, fills 8-D space.
  • Honest failure reporting for Fi + falsifiable hypothesis.
  • Novel diagnostic — Least-Squares residual proves entanglement.
  • Clean reproducibility plan (vectors + dataset on acceptance).

▲ Limitations

  • Single-model (Llama-3.1-8B only); cross-family unverified.
  • Fi steering fails; RLHF cause hypothesized, not isolated.
  • Self-report only — no behavioral downstream validation.
  • Jungian theory is contested vs. Big Five in modern psychology.
  • Small clusters (k=10); only 12 multi-dim combinations.
  • Synthetic narrations may carry generator bias.

§ 06 — Open questions

06For Further Exploration

  1. Does the layer 7–12 concentration survive in Llama-3.1-70B/405B, Qwen3, Gemma-3, or Mistral?
  2. Does Fi steering recover on the base (non-instruct) checkpoint, or with refusal-direction ablated?
  3. Can score changes be validated by an independent behavioral metric (lexical, syntactic, human eval)?
  4. What actually spans the multi-dim subspace? Can SAE features recover it with low residual?
  5. Does the controllability/capability trade-off degrade non-uniformly across functions?
  6. Can pivot-score extraction extend to 3+ functions (full MBTI stack)?
  7. Are extracted directions transferable to real-user personas in multi-turn agentic settings?
