> **一句话总结：** 作者在 DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B 上发现 GSM8K/MATH-500 的准确率仅需 256 个思考 token 即饱和，而 AIME 呈现"56.5% 收敛/43.5% 永不收敛"的双峰失败模式，并通过对全部 28 层做线性探测证明收敛命运在第 20 层、token 150 处已被部分编码（AUC 0.608），为基于内部表示的早退策略奠定可行性。

> **TL;DR:** Studying DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B on GSM8K, MATH-500, and AIME, the authors show thinking-budget accuracy saturates at just 256 tokens on GSM8K/MATH-500, while AIME exhibits a bimodal failure (56.5% converge at 96.5% accuracy vs. 43.5% never converge at 11.5%), and a linear probe on hidden states at layer 20 (AUC 0.608 vs. ~0.54 chance in early/late layers) detects non-convergence at 8 of 9 checkpoints (mean ∆AUC +0.035 over behavioral baseline), establishing the feasibility of mechanistic early-exit even though absolute probe power is modest.

## 核心贡献

- **预算饱和的实证证据：** 在 GSM8K 与 MATH-500 上，仅 256 个思考 token 即可达到无上限准确率的 95%，挑战了"思考越多越好"的普遍假设，表明常规数学推理中额外 token 是纯粹的效率损失。
- **双峰收敛失败模式的刻画：** 在 AIME 上发现清晰的二元分裂——56.5% 的生成自然收敛（96.5% 准确率），43.5% 即便给到 10,000 token 上限也永不终止（11.5% 准确率），且这一模式在所有难度分组中都存在，问题难度仅解释约 19% 的方差（r² ≈ 0.186）。
- **逐层信号定位：** 对全部 28 层在 token 150 处做线性探测，定位到第 20 层信号最强（AUC 0.608 ± 0.080），证明收敛信息集中在"上中部表示层"而非靠近输出的层。
- **早期检测的一致性优势：** 9 个检查点（50–300 token）扫描中，激活探测在 8/9 位置上优于仅用表面特征（熵、n-gram 重复）的行为基线，50 token 处优势最大（∆AUC = +0.074），意味着收敛命运在表面信号出现之前就已被编码。
- **污染验证：** 在训练截止后的 AIME 2025 上复测，收敛准确率 100%、非收敛 0%，排除记忆假说，确认收敛—正确性关系在未见数据上依然成立。

## Key Contributions

- **Empirical evidence of budget saturation:** GSM8K and MATH-500 reach their minimum sufficient budget (B*) at the smallest tested setting of 256 tokens, flat across all higher budgets—challenging the assumption that more thinking uniformly helps and quantifying routine-math inference as pure efficiency loss beyond 256 tokens.
- **Characterization of bimodal convergence failure on AIME:** A clean two-group split (56.5% converged at 96.5% accuracy, ~4,100 tokens; 43.5% non-converged at 11.5% accuracy, full 10,000-token waste), with problem difficulty explaining only r² ≈ 0.186 of variance—framing non-convergence as a qualitative failure mode more compute cannot fix.
- **Layer-resolved localization of convergence signal:** A full 28-layer sweep at token 150 identifies layer 20 as the strongest carrier (AUC 0.608 ± 0.080), with signal collapsing toward chance in both early (layer 0: 0.536) and final (layer 27: 0.541) layers, localizing convergence information to upper-middle representational layers.
- **Consistent early-detection advantage of activation probes:** Across a 9-position checkpoint sweep (50–300 tokens), activation probes beat a behavioral baseline (entropy + n-gram repetition + token count) at 8 of 9 positions, mean ∆AUC = +0.035, peak advantage ∆AUC = +0.074 at 50 tokens—showing internal state carries convergence information before any surface cue appears.
- **Contamination-controlled validation:** On post-cutoff AIME 2025 the convergence–correctness relationship holds (converged 100%, non-converged 0%), ruling out memorization as the primary driver and validating the finding on data the model cannot have seen.

## Methodology

The paper runs two interconnected studies on **DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B** (float16, NVIDIA RTX A5000, greedy decoding for reproducibility), evaluating on 200-problem subsets (seed=42) of GSM8K, MATH-500, and AIME 1983–2024.

**Study 1 — Budget forcing.** The authors adapt the budget-forcing mechanism of Muennighoff et al. (2025, s1) into a custom `LogitsProcessor` that counts tokens inside the `<think>...</think>` region and, once the budget B is reached, forces emission of `</think>` by setting all other logits to −∞. They sweep B ∈ {256, 512, 1024, 2048, 4096} plus an "uncapped" 10,000-token-ceiling condition, and define the minimum sufficient budget B* as the smallest B at which accuracy reaches ≥95% of uncapped accuracy and stays there, with 1,000-iteration bootstrap CIs.

**Study 2 — Mechanistic early detection.** They regenerate the 200 AIME problems with two forward hooks: a hidden-state hook on all 28 layers at token 150, and a logit hook computing entropy at 9 checkpoint positions (50–300 tokens). Three logistic-regression probes (C=1.0, L-BFGS, 5-fold stratified CV, AUC-ROC with label-flip correction) are compared: an **activation probe** on the hidden-state vector (dim 3,584), a **behavioral baseline** using 7 surface features (entropy mean/max/std/slope, bigram/trigram repetition, token count), and a **combined probe**. Significance is assessed via paired permutation tests (B=10,000 per checkpoint; B=100,000 sweep-level preserving cross-checkpoint dependence).

**Contamination check.** All 30 AIME 2025 problems (released Feb 2025, after the model's July 2024 cutoff) are run under uncapped greedy decoding to test whether historical results are driven by memorization.

## Results & Highlights

- **Budget saturation on easy/medium math:** GSM8K reaches B* = 256 tokens (95% CI [256, 512], uncapped accuracy 0.740) and MATH-500 reaches B* = 256 (95% CI [256, 4096], uncapped accuracy 0.625); accuracy is essentially flat across all budgets—256 tokens captures 95%+ of uncapped performance, vs. a default thinking length of thousands of tokens.
- **Bimodal AIME failure:** Of 200 AIME generations, 113 (56.5%) converge with 96.5% accuracy at ~4,100 avg tokens, while 87 (43.5%) never emit `</think>` within the 10,000-token ceiling and achieve only 11.5% accuracy—meaning ~43.5% of AIME inference compute is spent on doomed generations that an oracle early-exit could reclaim.
- **Difficulty does not explain convergence:** Point-biserial r = 0.431 (p < 0.0001) but r² ≈ 0.186; within the hard tercile (problems 11–15), converged generations hit 100% accuracy (n=18) while non-converged hit 0% (n=41), showing convergence and correctness are nearly co-extensive on hard problems.
- **Layer 20 carries the strongest signal:** At token 150, AUC rises from 0.536 (layer 0) to a peak of **0.608 ± 0.080 at layer 20** (0.606 at layer 21), dropping back to 0.541 at layer 27—convergence information concentrates in upper-middle layers, not output-adjacent ones.
- **Activation probe beats behavioral baseline at 8/9 checkpoints:** Mean ∆AUC = +0.035; strongest at 50 tokens where activation AUC = 0.615 ± 0.066 vs. behavioral 0.541 ± 0.044 (∆ = +0.074); sweep-level permutation test p = 0.063 (n=200 underpowered for per-checkpoint significance, but consistent directionality supports a real signal).
- **Contamination check rules out memorization:** On AIME 2025, converged accuracy is 100% (11/11) and non-converged 0% (0/19), vs. historical 90.3% / 6.6%—the small 6.6% non-converged-accuracy on historical data is attributed to partial answer memorization and disappears on post-cutoff problems.

## Authors & Credibility

The three authors (Renuka Oladri, Niveda Jawahar, Abdirisak Mohamed) are at the University of Maryland's Computer, Mathematical, and Natural Sciences unit, with Mohamed also affiliated with SAP Labs, LLC. The work is explicitly marked "Preprint. Under review." The methodology is unusually careful for a probing study: greedy decoding for reproducibility, bootstrap CIs on B*, Wilson intervals on proportions, label-flip correction on AUC, a sweep-level permutation test designed to respect the dependence between checkpoints, a fresh instrumented-run sanity check (Table 4), and a post-cutoff contamination analysis (Table 5). Statistical claims are reported honestly with explicit acknowledgment that n=200 is underpowered (e.g., reporting p = 0.063 rather than overclaiming). Code and instrumented pipelines are promised upon acceptance rather than publicly available at submission, which is a moderate reproducibility limitation.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** The budget-forcing application is a straightforward adaptation of s1 (Muennighoff et al., 2025) extended to a token-level logits processor; the genuinely novel pieces are (a) the systematic bimodal-convergence characterization tying it to Pipis et al. (2025)'s distillation loop failure, and (b) probing for a *future trajectory property* (convergence) rather than an input/current-token property as in prior probing work. The framing is fresh but each ingredient exists in prior literature.
- **Rigor:** Strong on statistical hygiene (bootstrap CIs, permutation tests, label-flip correction, sweep-level dependence correction, fresh-run validation, contamination check). Honest about negative results: the headline probe AUC is only ~0.61, sweep-level p = 0.063 does not clear 0.05, and the authors say so plainly rather than cherry-picking the layer-20 or 50-token peaks.
- **Reproducibility:** Mixed. Greedy decoding + fixed seed (42) + open benchmarks + documented hyperparameters make the budget-forcing study reproducible. However, code is withheld until acceptance, the probe workflow depends on fragile forward-hook plumbing (the paper itself flags a prefill-vs-autoregressive-step trap), and the central interpretability claim is sample-size-limited to n=200.
- **Significance:** The budget-saturation finding (256 tokens suffice for GSM8K/MATH-500) has immediate deployment value; the early-exit finding is a feasibility proof, not a deployable system—the authors concede "an operationally useful early-exit system would require substantially higher discrimination than the best observed AUC (~0.61)."
- **Red flags / specific concerns:**
  - **Greedy-decoding confound (the biggest one).** All generations use `do_sample=False`, which DeepSeek's own usage guidance explicitly advises against, warning it causes repetitive generation and recommending temperature ~0.6. The paper itself flags this and notes the documented non-convergence rate (43.5%) "may be an intrinsic model property or a decoding artifact." A direct temp-0.6 comparison is left for future work, so the central bimodality claim is causally underspecified.
  - **Memorization leak acknowledged in the historical baseline.** Table 5 shows 6.6% of non-converged historical generations are correct, attributed to partial answer memorization—meaning the headline 11.5% non-converged accuracy on AIME 1983–2024 slightly overstates the model's true novel-problem capability (it is 0% on AIME 2025).
  - **Correlation ≠ causation.** The probe shows convergence info is *present* in layer-20 activations, not that it *determines* convergence; the authors explicitly note causality would require activation-patching interventions not performed here.
  - **Underpowered for the headline interpretability claim.** Per-checkpoint permutation tests do not reach significance at any single position; only the pooled directionality (8/9 positive) and sweep-level p=0.063 support the claim, and the paper acknowledges n=200 is the binding constraint.
  - **Single-model scope.** Everything rests on DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B; the bimodality, the 256-token saturation, and the layer-20 peak are not shown to generalize across families, scales, or training procedures.

## Strengths

- Cleanly separates two questions (does more thinking help? is failure predictable?) and answers each with a purpose-built study.
- Honest, well-powered statistics for the budget study (bootstrap CIs, Wilson intervals) and appropriately conservative statistics for the probe study (permutation tests, dependence-aware sweep test, label-flip correction).
- The contamination check on post-cutoff AIME 2025 is a credibility boost that many comparable interpretability papers omit.
- The 256-token saturation result on GSM8K/MATH-500 has immediate, actionable inference-cost implications for deployment.
- The reframing of probing target—from input/current-token properties to a *future trajectory property* of the full generation—is conceptually clean and generalizes beyond math reasoning.

## Limitations

- Single model (DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B); generalization to other families/scales untested.
- Greedy decoding conflicts with DeepSeek's recommended inference settings and may inflate the non-convergence rate.
- Practical 10,000-token ceiling means some "non-converged" generations might terminate with more budget.
- n=200 with fold sizes ~40 leaves per-checkpoint tests underpowered; some bootstrap cells reach n=18 (converged-hard) or n=4.
- Probe demonstrates predictiveness, not causality; activation-patching interventions are out of scope.
- Historical AIME baseline slightly inflated by partial memorization (6.6% non-converged accuracy vs. 0% on AIME 2025).
- AIME problem number is an imperfect difficulty proxy.
- Code/data release gated on acceptance, limiting immediate third-party reproduction.

## Relevance

This paper sits squarely in the test-time-compute and reasoning-model-efficiency space: it quantifies when chain-of-thought thinking stops paying off and asks whether internal representations can be used for inference-time early-exit. For anyone working on reasoning LLMs, inference cost reduction, or mechanistic interpretability of reasoning trajectories, both the budget-saturation numbers and the layer-20 early-detection signal are directly actionable reference points.

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the bimodal convergence pattern—and the 43.5% non-convergence rate—persist under DeepSeek's recommended temperature (~0.6) and across multiple seeds, or is it largely a greedy-decoding artifact?
- Do nonlinear probes (MLP/attention-based) or multi-layer/windowed aggregation push layer-20 AUC from ~0.61 to operationally useful levels (e.g., ≥0.85) for a real early-exit system?
- Does the layer-20 convergence signal generalize to other distilled reasoning models (e.g., QwQ, R1-Distill-Llama) and to larger R1 checkpoints, or is it specific to the Qwen-7B distillation?
- Can activation patching at layer 20 causally *rescue* non-converging trajectories, or does the probe detect a symptom rather than the cause of looping?
- Is the 256-token saturation threshold on GSM8K/MATH-500 stable under non-greedy decoding and on harder out-of-distribution math, or does it shift upward when the model is allowed to sample?
- What exactly does the layer-20 probe read out—a coherent problem representation, an early strategy choice, or an implicit confidence calibration—and which of these hypotheses (Section 5.2) is correct?
