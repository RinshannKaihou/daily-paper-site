> **一句话总结：** 本文提出 progressive cramming——逐 token 增长前缀并强制 100% 重建的可靠压缩评测协议，借此揭示「完美重建」并不等于「可迁移语义」：压缩嵌入通过早期层的脆弱注意力劫持来重建序列，却在下游多选题与生成任务上持续退化，证明 prior work 看到的极高嵌入容量本质是「易操控性」而非稠密语义编码。

> **TL;DR:** On PG19, progressive cramming forces 100% reconstruction and reveals that a single Llama-3.1-8B embedding (1438±380 tokens) achieves 100% greedy-decoding accuracy whereas the prior "full cramming" protocol (1568 tokens at 99.96% teacher forcing) collapses to 40.4% under greedy generation; prepending such an embedding drops HellaSwag from 61.9% to 40.8% and crashes 5-shot MMLU from 63.2% to 0.0%, and causal attention-knockout localizes the collapse to early layers—showing cramming steers rather than semantically encodes.

## 核心贡献

1. **提出 Progressive Cramming 评测协议**：摒弃 prior work 的「固定 token 预算 + 99% accuracy 阈值」协议，改为逐 token 增长目标前缀、warm-start 优化、仅在固定优化预算内无法达成 100% 重建时停止。这把模糊的「接近完美」变成清晰的「成功/失败二元边界」，从而首次能精确测量压缩在何处失效。
2. **量化「最后 1% 问题」**：揭示 99% teacher-forcing 收敛隐藏了灾难性的自回归崩溃——误差几乎全部集中在序列前 1-2 个 token（Table 1 中 mismatch@0/1 高达 86/100、100/100），一旦首 token 错位整个生成发散。
3. **发现低维轨迹结构**：跨模型家族，progressive 优化轨迹在 2048-4096 维嵌入空间中仅由 30-100 个 PCA 主成分即可解释 99% 方差；且这是「优化路径」性质而非解集性质（独立优化的解互相近正交）。同时给出 low-dimensional projection 变体（约束到 affine rank-k 子空间）进一步稳定优化并提高信息增益。
4. **因果定位下游退化来源**：通过 per-layer / forward / reverse cumulative attention knockout 三种干预，证明尽管压缩嵌入在晚期层吸走 20-60% 注意力，但下游能力崩溃的因果根因是其在早期层的交互——晚期层的高注意力质量只是症状而非病因。
5. **揭示「完美重建 ≠ 可迁移语义」**：在 HellaSwag / ARC / 5-shot MMLU 上系统证明：即使原 prefix 仍在上下文中，前置压缩嵌入仍带来中等但稳定的精度下降，生成式评测下几乎完全崩溃（MMLU 63.2%→0%），而同位置放随机嵌入几乎无损——证明 cramming 走的是 brittle steering 而非语义编码。
6. **压缩容量随冻结 reconstructor 架构（深度+宽度）单调增长**：truncating pretrained 模型至前 N 层并修复后评测，发现完美压缩 token 数随保留深度与模型宽度共同增长且二者可部分互替。

## Key Contributions

- **A reliable compression protocol (Progressive Cramming).** Replaces fixed-budget / 99%-accuracy evaluation with token-by-token prefix growth, warm-start optimization, and a stop rule that triggers only when 100% reconstruction is unreachable within budget. This converts a fuzzy "near-perfect" outcome into a sharp success/failure boundary and exposes brittleness invisible under teacher forcing (the "last 1% problem": residual errors concentrate at the first 1-2 tokens and cascade under greedy decoding).
- **Low-dimensional trajectory geometry.** Across Pythia, Llama 3, SmolLM2, and Gemma 3, the progressive optimization path {e^(k)} lives in a 30-100 component PCA subspace of a 2048-4096-dim embedding space. This is a property of the warm-started *path*, not the *solution set* (independently optimized solutions are far apart and nearly orthogonal). A learned low-rank projection (e = Wz + b) stabilizes optimization and often increases compressed length and information gain.
- **Causal localization of the capability collapse.** Three attention-knockout protocols (per-layer, forward-cumulative, reverse-cumulative) on Llama-3.1-8B (and replicated on Pythia-1.4B, SmolLM2-1.7B) dissociate attention mass from causal importance: late layers capture 20-60% attention mass but knockout barely changes reconstruction or downstream accuracy, whereas early-layer masking restores downstream accuracy to baseline while destroying reconstruction. Attention concentration is a symptom, not the cause.
- **Reconstruction ≠ transferable semantics.** Even with the original prefix in context, prepending a crammed embedding drops HellaSwag/ARC accuracy and crashes 5-shot MMLU to ~0% with no valid parseable answers, while a random embedding at the same position leaves accuracy nearly intact—causally tying the collapse to the optimized embedding.
- **Capacity is bounded by the frozen reconstructor's architecture.** Truncating five model families (1.7-8B params, 24-36 layers) to their first N layers and repairing with a short finetune shows perfectly-crammed tokens rise roughly 2x per layer-doubling and monotonically with model width; depth and width partially substitute, so the compression ceiling tracks reconstructor capacity rather than parameter count alone.
- **Open release.** Code and progressive-cramming optimization trajectories are released (https://github.com/FusionBrainLab/progressive_cramming).

## Methodology

**Problem setup (recap of cramming).** A single embedding e ∈ R^d is optimized against the cross-entropy cramming loss L_cram(e; x, M) = -Σ log p_M(x_i | e, x_1..x_{i-1}) with the LM M frozen, so that the model can autoregressively reconstruct the target sequence x from e alone.

**Progressive token addition.** Start with prefix x^(1)=(x_1), optimize e^(1) to perfect reconstruction, then extend to x^(k)=(x_1..x_k) with warm-start e^(k) ← e^(k-1), repeating until perfect reconstruction is no longer achievable within the optimization budget. This yields a trajectory {e^(k)}_{k=1..n}; the paper analyzes its Euclidean trajectory length L_traj = Σ ||e^(k+1) - e^(k)||_2.

**Low-dimensional projection.** Parameterize the embedding as e = Wz + b with z ∈ R^k (k ≪ d). (W, b, z) can be fit per-sample (sample-specific subspace) or shared across a corpus with only per-sample z free (probes whether the valid low-rank subspace is corpus-universal).

**Cramming metric: information gain.** IG = H_LM - H_{comp+LM}, i.e., the reduction in total per-token cross-entropy (in bits) on the target sequence when conditioning on the compression embedding.

**Causal intervention: attention knockout.** At selected layers, mask the pre-softmax attention logits targeting the compression embedding at position 0, removing its influence from the residual stream while leaving all other computation intact. Three protocols on Llama-3.1-8B (32 layers): per-layer (one layer l), forward-cumulative (0..k), and reverse-cumulative (k..L-1) — each measured on both teacher-forced reconstruction and HellaSwag downstream accuracy.

**Reconstructor-capacity experiment.** Pretrained models are truncated to their first N ∈ {1,2,4,8} decoder layers, the cut is repaired with a short causal-LM finetune on fineweb-edu, then standard progressive cramming (PG19, 50 samples, lr=0.1) is run on each checkpoint.

**Models & data.** Main evaluation on PG19 across Pythia, Llama 3, SmolLM2, Gemma 3; replication on Fanfics (Appendix D.5); capacity sweep adds Qwen3 (4B/8B) and SmolLM3-3B.

## Results & Highlights

- **Progressive cramming delivers reliable autoregressive reconstruction where full cramming fails catastrophically (Table 2, PG19).** Llama-3.1-8B: full cramming reaches 1568 tokens at 99.96% TF accuracy but collapses to **40.44 ± 48.63% greedy** accuracy; progressive cramming trades budget for a fixed-accuracy guarantee, hitting **1438 ± 380 tokens at 100.00% greedy accuracy**. Pythia-1.4b shows the same pattern: 99.71% TF / **44.19% greedy (full)** vs **100.00% / 100.00% (progressive)**.
- **The "last 1%" is concentrated, not benign (Table 1).** At 95-99% TF convergence, mismatch rates at positions 0/1 approach 86-100/100, and greedy decoding collapses to ≤ 0.4% — a single early mismatch nullifies the rest of the reconstruction.
- **Trajectories are low-dimensional (Table 3).** PCA-99% components: Llama-3.1-8B 82.78 ± 9.07; SmolLM2-1.7B 33.22 ± 2.82; gemma-3-4b 30.8 ± 9.45 — i.e., 30-100 components explain 99% of trajectory variance inside 2048-4096-dim embedding spaces. Low-rank projection (dim=256) further boosts Llama-3.1-8B compressed length to **1697.2 ± 304.2 tokens** and information gain to **4814 ± 1731 bits**.
- **The low-rank subspace is sample-specific, not universal.** A shared (W, b) trained jointly across 50 PG19 samples reaches 742 ± 177 tokens on SmolLM2-1.7B, but when frozen and transferred to unseen samples capacity collapses to **8.7 ± 6.8 tokens** and **36 ± 29 bits** — the basis only works by co-adapting with its training coefficients.
- **Downstream multiple-choice drops even with the original prefix in context (Table 4).** Llama-3.1-8B HellaSwag: **61.91% → 40.80%** (progressive) / 40.00% (full); ARC-Easy **63.54% → 43.31%** (progressive). SmolLM2-1.7B HellaSwag 53.56% → 37.01%.
- **Generative evaluation collapses almost entirely (Table 26, 5-shot MMLU, 512 samples).** Llama-3.1-8B full-prefix compression: **63.22% → 0.00% with 0% valid answers**, while a *random* embedding at the same position costs only -0.51% (62.71%) — confirming the optimized embedding, not the out-of-distribution token, is responsible. SmolLM2-1.7B and gemma-3-4b show the same 0% collapse.
- **Attention mass and causal importance dissociate (Figures 6-7).** Forward-cumulative knockout restores HellaSwag accuracy from the crammed 0.395 back to the uncompressed 0.598 after masking only the first few early layers, while reverse-cumulative knockout recovers nothing until masking reaches the early layers. The late layers holding the most attention mass are causally inert.
- **Compression capacity scales with reconstructor depth and width (Table 5).** Qwen3-8B perfectly-crammed tokens: 119 → 180 → 304 → 625 at first-1/2/4/8 layers (≈2x per layer-doubling), 774 at full depth. Llama-3.1-8B: 97 → 184 → 400 → (full) 1438. At matched depth, the larger model crams strictly more (Qwen3-8B > Qwen3-4B at every N); the 8-layer Qwen3-8B even exceeds the full 36-layer Qwen3-4B.

## Critical Assessment

The paper's central methodological move — swapping a fixed-budget/99%-accuracy protocol for a fixed-accuracy/variable-budget one — is well-motivated and rigorously justified by the "last 1%" analysis (Table 1: mismatch@0/1 of 86/100 to 100/100). The brittleness finding is concrete and quantified, and the experimental scope (four families, two corpora, three knockout protocols replicated on three models) is unusually thorough for a mechanistic study.

The interpretive claim that "cramming is brittle steering rather than semantic encoding" is the strongest part of the contribution and rests on two converging pieces of evidence: (i) the forward/reverse cumulative knockout asymmetry (Figure 7), which causally localizes the downstream collapse to early layers while showing late-layer attention mass is causally inert, and (ii) the random-embedding control on MMLU (Table 26: -0.51% for random vs -63.22% for the optimized embedding at full-prefix compression), which rules out an out-of-distribution-token explanation. This is a genuinely useful refinement of what the Kuratov et al. (2025) capacity number actually means.

However, several limitations temper the conclusions. First, the activation-alignment regularizer (Appendix B.6 / D.6) actually improves free-generation coherence (0.500 → 0.625) at equal reconstruction fidelity — a small-sample (n=16) result that complicates the clean "reconstruction ≠ semantics" framing by showing the two objectives are at least partially reconcilable. Second, the "sample-specific" subspace collapse (8.7 tokens when the shared basis is frozen) is striking but is essentially a transfer-failure result; it does not by itself prove that no universal semantic basis could exist under a different learning procedure. Third, the capacity-vs-architecture result (Section 8) conflates "depth unpacks the code, width stores it" with the confound that deeper truncations are repaired with the same short finetune — the authors flag this but the separation is not airtight. Finally, the headline contrast (progressive 100% greedy vs full 40% greedy) is partly definitional: progressive cramming *by construction* stops when 100% fails, so its 100% accuracy is a protocol feature, not an empirical surprise — the real content is in *what that boundary reveals* (low-dim trajectories, early-layer causal locus), not the boundary itself.

## Strengths

- **Sharp protocol fix for a real problem.** The 99%-accuracy/teacher-forcing protocol genuinely hides catastrophic autoregressive failure; the progressive protocol is simple, well-justified, and adopted throughout.
- **Low-dimensional trajectory finding is novel and well-analyzed.** The PCA-99% result (30-100 components in 2048-4096-dim spaces), the "dwell-and-leap" path shape, and the per-sample-vs-shared subspace probe together form a coherent geometric story.
- **Genuinely causal mechanism analysis.** Three knockout protocols with a forward/reverse asymmetry is a stronger causal design than the correlational attention-sink observation alone.
- **Strong controls.** The random-embedding MMLU control and the convergent-subset-only downstream evaluation both isolate the effect of the *optimized* embedding from confounds (residual errors, OOD tokens).
- **Cross-model and cross-corpus replication.** Findings replicate across Pythia, Llama 3, SmolLM2, Gemma 3, and (for capacity) Qwen3 and SmolLM3, on both PG19 and Fanfics.
- **Public release** of code and trajectories.

## Limitations

- **Cost and un-batchability.** Progressive cramming is roughly **2x slower than full cramming** and resists batching because each sample reaches perfect reconstruction at its own rate — a practical bottleneck the authors explicitly flag.
- **Open central problem.** The paper does not show how to make a single embedding both perfectly reconstructable *and* semantically faithful; activation alignment is only a partial, model-dependent patch (Appendix B.6, D.6, n=16).
- **No capacity predictor.** Information gain is the most reliable proxy found but is still too coarse to pinpoint a model's exact capacity in advance.
- **Breadth of generative evaluation is thin.** The free-generation LLM-as-judge study uses only 16 samples, binary criteria, a single model, and a single compression length (32 tokens); MMLU generative collapse is strong but is a single benchmark.
- **Gemma underperforms without explanation.** Gemma 3 shows lower information gain at shorter lengths (attributed speculatively to logit softcapping) and Table 4 shows 0% converged samples for progressive cramming on HellaSwag — a partial failure of the protocol on this family.
- **Subspace universality is shown by failure, not by construction.** The "sample-specific" conclusion rests on one training recipe collapsing on transfer; alternative bases or amortized compressors are not ruled out.
- **Mechanism claims are correlational at the circuit level.** Early-layer causal importance is established via knockout, but the *specific* circuits that implement cramming are not identified; the authors defer circuit-level confirmation to future work.

## Relevance

- **对 LLM 长上下文压缩方向的意义**：本文直接质疑了「重建准确率高 = 压缩有效」这一被广泛默认的假设，为 Activation Beacon / ICAE / AutoCompressors 等语义压缩方法的评估提出了明确警示——任何基于嵌入的压缩都必须在下游语义任务（而非仅重建 token）上验证。
- **对可解释性与机制研究**：attention-knockout 的「注意力质量 ≠ 因果重要性」结论，对解释 transformer 中的 attention sink 现象（BOS、sink token）有方法论参考价值，提示不要仅凭注意力分布下因果判断。
- **对嵌入空间容量的再认识**：把 Kuratov et al. (2025) 的「单嵌入编码 1568 token」从「稠密语义编码」重新定位为「模型的易操控性」，对未来 learned-compression 和 prompt-tuning 工作有指导意义——区分「真表示」与「steering 向量」是设计可靠压缩方法的前提。
- **与 PEFT / soft-prompt 研究的关联**：soft prompt、prefix tuning 的极端情况即为 cramming；本文对「冻结模型通过输入嵌入可被如何 steering」给出了机制层面的刻画。
- **对模型架构设计的启示**：压缩容量同时由深度与宽度决定且可互替，提示「可压缩性」可作为架构设计目标，而非仅依赖模型规模。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Can a single amortized feed-forward compressor (no per-sample backprop) produce embeddings that are simultaneously perfectly reconstructable *and* semantically faithful, or is there a fundamental trade-off the paper has only measured one side of?
- The activation-alignment regularizer improves free-generation coherence (0.500 → 0.625) at matched reconstruction — does scaling this signal close the reconstruction/semantics gap on full benchmarks (not just 16 samples)?
- What are the *specific circuits* implementing cramming? The paper localizes causal importance to early layers but does not identify the exact attention/MLP subnetwork; would sparse-autoprobing or circuit-level ablation reveal a reusable "unpacking circuit"?
- Is the sample-specific collapse of the shared low-rank basis (8.7 tokens on transfer) a property of cramming, or would a different training objective (e.g., distillation) yield a transferable universal subspace?
- The capacity-vs-architecture result shows depth and width partially substitute — does this generalize to non-transformer architectures (e.g., Mamba/SSM, RWKV), and does it predict compressibility of future hybrid designs?
- Can information gain be sharpened into a *per-sample* capacity predictor that avoids the costly progressive search, or is the reconstruction boundary fundamentally post-hoc?
- Does the "attention mass ≠ causal importance" dissociation extend to other attention-sink phenomena (BOS, sink tokens in long context), suggesting a general principle that high attention mass is often epiphenomenal?
- Gemma 3's lower information gain and 0% progressive-convergence on HellaSwag: is logit softcapping the actual cause, and does removing it restore the protocol's reliability on this family?
