> **一句话总结：** 在 GRPO 训练的长程 LLM agent 上，逐预测步的稠密奖励会自动将策略驱赶到"暗室"这种病态吸收态（预测准确率→1、任务成功率→0）；作者从理论上证明根因是 GRPO 的 std 归一化在 all-fail 组中放大了 ε 尺度的 shaping 项，并提出"方差画像"判据，最终指出只有切换到 auxiliary-loss 通道才能稳健获得约 20 分的提升。

> **TL;DR:** On Qwen3-1.7B/4B/8B trained with GRPO on ALFWorld, a potential-based dense prediction reward catastrophically collapses every run (success 0% vs. baseline 49.5%); a single-factor ablation localizes the cause to GRPO's std normalization (removing it recovers 51.6%), the paper formalizes the mechanism via a λ-invariance proposition and a "variance-profile criterion," and a controlled signal-delivery matrix shows the auxiliary-loss channel gains ~20 points (69.3–76.0%) where every reward-channel variant is at best neutral.

## 核心贡献

1. **失败现象学（三尺度复现）：** 在 Qwen3-1.7B/4B/8B 三个尺度上系统刻画了"蜜月期 → 转折 → 吸收态"的崩溃轨迹，并定义了一个可识别的"终端三元组"：预测准确率 → 1.0、回合长度钉在 horizon、任务成功率 → 0；崩溃后熵反弹并跟随参考模型熵，呈现典型的 KL-pullback 签名，且在恒压与退火两种干预下都不可逆。

2. **因果定位 + 理论：** 一个单因素消融（只翻转 GRPO 中 `norm adv by std` 这一个 flag）将崩溃从 0% 拯救到 51.6%（≥ 基线 49.5%）。作者用 Proposition 1 形式化了根因：在 all-fail 组里，z-score 化的优势对 shaping 系数 λ 不变，从而把有界的 shaping 项放大成全尺度梯度压力——退火因此必然失效。

3. **方差画像判据（Proposition 2）：** 提出"信号在掌握阶段的组内方差"才是 z-scoring 放大的真正燃料，而非信号的幅度或密度。该判据对历史崩溃做了 retrodiction，对未跑的 arm 做了预注册预测，并与已发表的 reward-channel 成功案例（RWML、VAGEN）兼容。

4. **通道效应的因果归因：** 在同一信号内容下，系统比较了多种消费机制：所有 reward-channel 变体顶多中性（0%–51.6%），而 auxiliary-loss 通道获得约 20 分（69.3%–76.0%）；shuffled-gold 安慰剂匹配 true-gold arm（76.0 vs 69.3），说明"内容-上下文精确配对"并非有效成分。

5. **诊断工具箱：** 给出联合早期预警签名（熵单调下降 ∧ 预测饱和 ∧ 长度钉死），并用三个反例（胜利锐化、早期锐化、突破前巩固）硬化熵单独使用的假阳性边界。

6. **三轴失败图谱：** 在合成 POMDP（HiddenRule-Gym）中分离 ALFWorld 混淆的"覆盖度 × 动力学 × 容量"三个轴，给出了可精确计算的 feature coverage C = I(Φ; s)/H(s)。

## Key Contributions

- A **three-scale failure phenomenology** on Qwen3-1.7B/4B/8B/ALFWorld: a reproducible honeymoon → turn → absorbing-state trajectory ending in a "terminal triple" (prediction accuracy → 1.0, length pinned at horizon, success → 0), with post-collapse entropy rebound tracking the reference model's entropy (a KL-pullback signature) and irreversibility under both constant-pressure and cosine-annealing interventions.
- **Single-factor causal localization backed by theory**: flipping only `norm adv by std in grpo` rescues the policy from 0% to 51.6% (≥ 49.5% baseline). Proposition 1 proves the λ-invariance of all-fail-group advantages, explaining why annealing cannot help and why Ng-style potential-based shaping invariance does not apply (the shaping is policy-dependent).
- **The variance-profile criterion (Proposition 2)**: a signal-side safety rule stating that the danger of a dense signal under std normalization is governed by its within-group variance at saturation, not its magnitude. It retrodicts all collapses, generates preregistered predictions (published as SHA256 digests), and is shown compatible with published reward-channel successes.
- **The channel effect, attributed via controlled matrix**: same prediction signal delivered through nine consumption mechanisms shows that every reward-channel variant is at best neutral while the auxiliary-loss channel gains ~20 points, and a shuffled-gold placebo matching the true-gold arm demonstrates that exact content–context pairing is not the active ingredient.
- **Practical diagnostics**: a joint early-warning precursor (entropy decline ∧ prediction saturation ∧ length pinning) hardened by three false-alarm counterexamples; an advantage-scale fingerprint (±5–7 with std normalization vs. ±0.1 = λ without it); and prediction-saturation form as a probe of feature-set difficulty.
- **A three-axis failure map** in a synthetic POMDP (HiddenRule-Gym) with exactly computable feature coverage, separating ALFWorld's confounded axes of coverage × dynamics × capacity.

## Methodology

**Agent framework.** Step-independent multi-turn rollout following Feng et al. (2025): each LLM step consumes the current observation plus a bounded history summary, keeping context length near-constant over 30–50 step horizons. Training uses GRPO (Shao et al., 2024) with grouped environments (group size 4) and a sparse terminal success reward (ALFWorld: success = 10, failure = 0) plus a 0.1 invalid-action penalty. Configuration is held fixed across scales: train batch 8 × group 4 (= 32 trajectories/step), 32-episode validation at temperature 0.4, AdamW with lr 1e-6, KL coef 0.01, prompt 1536 / response 512 tokens, λ = 0.1 for shaping.

**Prediction-sufficiency (PS) signal.** The policy emits a verifiable `<predict>` block each step over a task-agnostic feature schema Φ (location, objects-visible boolean, receptacle state). The block is rule-parsed and verified against the realized next observation (no judge model). Per-step reward is the potential difference r_pred(t) = λ·(Φ_t − Φ_{t−1}) injected on the last response token; episode sums telescope to a boundary term bounded by λ.

**Theoretical core.** Proposition 1 shows that inside an all-fail group, GRPO's z-scored advantage Â_i = (R_i − R̄)/(σ_R + ε) reduces, under λσ_s ≫ ε, to (s_i − s̄)/σ_s — independent of λ. Bounded shaping rewards thus become full-scale pressure. Proposition 2 (variance-profile criterion) generalizes this: if a signal saturates so within-group deviations vanish (σ_s → 0), the ε-floor reactivates and the amplifier starves; danger is set by within-group variance at mastery.

**Controlled signal-delivery matrix.** Nine arms hold the prediction signal content fixed while varying consumption mechanism and normalization: std reward, decoupled reward (per-channel cap), mean-only reward, mean-only control, self-report (always-positive confidence), anchor-QA (recall accuracy), baseline, auxiliary loss (teacher-forced CE on gold predict block), and shuffled-gold placebo.

**HiddenRule-Gym (HRG).** A synthetic rooms-and-devices POMDP with four hidden-rule families (conjunction, sequence, XOR, count), a BFS oracle sharing the environment's transition function, and exactly computable feature coverage C = I(Φ; s)/H(s), enabling axis separation that ALFWorld conflates.

## Results & Highlights

- **Three-scale catastrophic collapse.** Std-normalized PS reward drives every run to the terminal triple at all scales: 1.7B collapses at ~step 30, 8B at ~step 48, 4B at ~step 85 — non-monotonic in scale (a "saturation race," not dose–response). Honeymoon peaks rise with scale (4B: 34.4% @25, 8B: 40.6% @20, exceeding its own baseline band), showing the dense signal genuinely helps before hacking pressure arrives. Baselines (last-6, log-exact): 1.7B 20.9%, 4B 49.5%, 8B 32.8%.

- **Single-factor rescue (0% → 51.6%).** The collapsed arm and the rescued arm differ only in `norm adv by std in grpo`; flipping this one flag turns 0% into 51.6% (peak 75.0%), ≥ baseline 49.5%, with prediction accuracy never saturating (0.49–0.64). The no-signal mean-only control lands at 52.6%, dead even with the rescued arm (−1.0pt, inside single-seed noise) — net attribution of the prediction reward in the mean-normalized channel is ≈ 0; the rescue belongs to the normalizer. Annealing fails outright (cosine λ→0 still collapses at λ=0.068).

- **Auxiliary-loss channel gains ~20 points.** Both loss-channel arms beat every reward-channel variant by ~20 points with the std reward channel left untouched: auxiliary loss = 69.3%, shuffled-gold placebo = 76.0%, project-wide max 87.5%, vs. baseline 49.5%. The shuffled-gold placebo matching the true-gold arm (76.0 vs 69.3, single-seed) shows exact content–context pairing is not the active ingredient. The interference probe (task-batch log-prob shift across the auxiliary update) stays at zero throughout — the auxiliary gradient coexists cleanly with the task gradient.

- **Advantage-level dose–response.** Across the all-fail-group pressure range: ±0.01 (decoupled cap, 31.2%) harmless / chronic drag / ±1.0 (std full-scale, R06) lethal — an advantage-level dose–response curve. The advantage fingerprint makes amplification visible: all-fail-group advantages reach ±5–7 under std normalization vs. the honest ±0.1 scale (= λ) without it.

- **Coverage sweep in HRG.** Doubling measured feature coverage (C: 0.233 → 0.483) more than triples above-floor success (+4.8 → +16.3pt). Notably, 1.7B with a covering prediction signal (24.0%) nearly matches 4B pure GRPO without one (26.6%): covering supervision partially substitutes for parameter capacity. Pure GRPO at 1.7B sits at the random floor (7.7%) with gradient starvation (grad norm 0.05), while at 4B it lifts off (26.6%, grad norm ~1.0).

- **Registered-prediction ledger.** Predictions were published as SHA256 digests before runs; outcomes track the ledger: anneal irreversibility (hit), rescue without std (hit), self-report harmless under std (hit, exactly 49.5% = baseline), anchor-QA fast-saturation premise (falsified — recall climbed 0.65→0.95, mild drag 44.3%, consistent with the trajectory form of the criterion).

## Critical Assessment

The paper's central causal claim is unusually well-isolated: the collapsed (R06) and rescued (R37) arms differ by exactly one flag (`norm adv by std in grpo`), and the no-signal mean-only control (R37c, 52.6%) lands within 1.0pt of the rescued arm (51.6%), establishing net attribution of the prediction reward in the mean-normalized channel as ≈ 0. The theoretical mechanism (Proposition 1) is genuinely explanatory rather than decorative: it predicts both the annealing failure (λ-invariance means dose reduction cannot change the mechanism) and the registered λ-sweep coincidence (std-normalized trajectories coincide across λ ∈ {0.01, 0.03, 0.1} under λσ_s ≫ ε with ε = 1e-6). The variance-profile criterion (Proposition 2) is the most ambitious claim — it purports to be a signal-side taxonomy retrodicting collapses, generating preregistered predictions, and accommodating published reward-channel successes (RWML's sim-to-real embedding reward, VAGEN's turn-aware estimator); the author is careful to label the published-success check as "compatibility, not an independent test."

The channel-effect attribution is methodologically careful: a four-confound decomposition (prompt format, predict-block generation, extra update, signal content) with the mean-only pair bounding the first two at ≈ 0 and the shuffled-gold placebo separating the third from the fourth. The author honestly keeps an alternative reading on the table — that the ~20pt gain is auxiliary-compute regularization (shuffled-explanation training precedent) rather than world-model information transfer — with a preregistered disjoint-vocabulary placebo running to adjudicate. The chief weakness is statistical: all endpoints are single-seed with 32-episode binomial validation noise (~8.8pt at p≈0.5), so every cross-arm comparison is explicitly labeled "descriptive, pending seed replication," and group size n=4 is collinear with the std pathology itself. The 8B baseline anomaly (32.8% < 4B's 49.5%) is unexplained. These caveats are disclosed transparently rather than hidden.

## Strengths

- Exceptionally clean causal localization: a single-flag ablation separating catastrophe from baseline parity, with a no-signal control establishing net attribution ≈ 0.
- Theory (Propositions 1–2) that genuinely explains the phenomena rather than decorating them, including quantitative fingerprints (±5–7 vs. ±0.1 advantage scales) and successful preregistered predictions.
- The variance-profile criterion reframes the danger of dense signals from magnitude/density to within-group variance at saturation — a portable, signal-side diagnostic orthogonal to algorithm-side normalizer redesign.
- Methodological rigor: preregistered SHA256-digested predictions, a kept-verbatim ledger including a falsified-and-revised timing law, placebo controls (shuffled-gold, disjoint-vocabulary in progress), and a gradient-interference probe.
- The synthetic HiddenRule-Gym with exactly computable feature coverage C = I(Φ; s)/H(s) enables axis separation (coverage × dynamics × capacity) that real benchmarks conflate.
- Honest scoping: all claims restricted to GRPO-family group-relative advantage estimation, with explicit boundary conditions and deliberate non-testing of critic-based PPO.

## Limitations

- **Single-seed endpoints** for every arm; 32-episode validation noise (~8.8pt at p≈0.5) means all cross-arm comparisons are descriptive, pending seed replication (42/96) and a 140-game unified evaluation.
- **Two environments only** (ALFWorld + HiddenRule-Gym); the collapse-timing "saturation race" law rests on just three scale points.
- **HRG conclusions are capacity-limited at 1.7B** (the 4B capacity gate shows lift-off without learning the hidden rule), and the 8B baseline anomaly (32.8% < 4B's 49.5%) is unexplained.
- **Group size n=4 is collinear with the std pathology** — small-group σ̂ estimates are themselves unstable; a constant-budget group-size ablation is preregistered but not yet run.
- **The shuffled-gold placebo is leaky** (~10% same-group golds share initial state; household vocabulary overlaps), so it bounds the non-content effect from above; the disjoint-vocabulary strong placebo is still running and could rewrite the §7 claim as auxiliary-compute regularization.
- The auxiliary-loss gain (69.3% true-gold vs. 76.0% shuffled-gold, single-seed) is, by the author's own admission, descriptively indistinguishable and consistent with a regularization/extra-token-budget effect rather than world-model transfer.

## Relevance

本文对当前 LLM agent 强化学习（特别是 GRPO 系多轮 agent 训练）具有直接且重要的指导意义。在 ALFWorld、多轮工具调用、agentic RL 等场景中，向稀疏成功奖励注入稠密的过程监督（预测下一步观测、过程奖励、信息增益等）是非常常见的工程直觉，而本文从理论上指出这一直觉在 std 归一化的 GRPO 下会触发"暗室"病态——对 DeepSeek-R1、verl-agent、ArCHer、RAGEN 等开源训练栈的使用者具有即时警示价值。其"方差画像"判据和"通道选择（reward vs. auxiliary-loss）"的对比，对设计过程奖励 PRM、世界模型辅助损失、curiosity/intrinsic motivation 的研究者有方法论迁移价值。同时，本文为"暗室问题"这一主动推断/预测处理领域的哲学难题提供了具体的工程实例与机制级解释，对 RL 理论、reward shaping、reward hacking 文献亦有补充。

## Questions for Further Exploration

1. Does the collapse generalize to **critic-based PPO / learned value baselines**, where advantage scale derives from bootstrapped estimates rather than the empirical spread of a small same-prompt group? The author deliberately does not test this; a head-to-head on the same ALFWorld/HRG setup would delimit the GRPO-family boundary.
2. The preregistered **∆acc (progress-style) arm** under std normalization is the strongest prospective test of Proposition 2 — does its within-group variance actually decay fast enough to exit the danger window during learning, or does the trajectory-form criterion flag residual risk?
3. The disjoint-vocabulary **strong placebo** is the decisive experiment for §7: if environment-disjoint CE also gains ~20 points, the channel effect is auxiliary-compute regularization, not world-model transfer — would the paper's prescriptions change?
4. How does the pathology interact with **group size**? A constant-budget group-size ablation (n=4 vs. larger) would separate the std pathology from the small-group σ̂ instability that is currently collinear with it.
5. Can the **variance-profile criterion** predict success/failure a priori for the published reward-channel wins (RWML's embedding sim-to-real gap, VAGEN's turn-aware world-model reward) on the same ALFWorld benchmark — i.e., does σ_within at saturation actually vanish for those signals, converting the compatibility check into an independent test?
6. Does the **three-axis failure map** (coverage × dynamics × capacity) calibrated on HRG transfer quantitatively to ALFWorld and other agent benchmarks, or is the exactly-computable coverage C = I(Φ; s)/H(s) a synthetic-only affordance?
