Why this matters

First causal-circuit study of animacy; an honest empirical answer on circuit-discovery reliability & generalization, and a transferable paradigm for whether LLMs grasp concept distinctions beyond surface syntax.

⚠ Critical edge

Animacy is narrowly operationalized as person nouns vs. inanimate causes in passive completion — not the full hierarchy — and discovery runs only on the ~45–50% of pairs that behave as intended.

01 Methodology

Animacy as passive-prefix sentence completion · EAP-IG edge circuit discovery

  • Minimal-pair task. Animate-oriented prefix (The victim was examined by the) vs. inanimate-oriented (The victim was blinded by the); measure next-token shift toward person vs. event/object nouns.
  • Dataset. 20,000 GPT-5.4-generated frames → Llama 3.3 70B plausibility filter (threshold 0.90) → 16,305 pairs; WordNet noun.person vs. event/artifact; 9,532 shared single-token pairs.
  • Metric. Average logit difference avg_LD over animate vs. inanimate sets; pair "successful" if LD_clean>0 & LD_corrupt<0 (random 25%).
  • Discovery. EAP-IG (Hanna 2024) ranks directed edges; greedy budget to ≥85% faithfulness, on 500 high-margin (>0.5) pairs/model.
  • Eval suite. Sufficiency, necessity (size-matched random ablation), seed stability, backup-circuit check, 3 transfer setups.

02 Results & highlights

Circuits exist, beat random edges, and concentrate in MLPs — but fail to transfer universally

03 Strengths & limitations

Honest framing — "task-relative causal subgraphs," not a universal module

+ Strengths

  • Confronts known circuit-discovery pitfalls instead of ignoring them.
  • Purpose-built dataset closes real gaps left by BLiMP & Gregorio 2025.
  • Non-overclaimed: explicitly "task-relative subgraphs."
  • Shared motif surfaced — early verb info → MLP0 routing → later MLP logit alignment.
  • Open models, open dataset, open code.

− Limitations

  • Animacy = person vs. inanimate only; no animals, coreference, active voice.
  • Only ~45–50% of pairs succeed; failing pairs unexplained.
  • LLM-generated frames + LLM filter ⇒ possible lexical circularity.
  • Circuits larger & more distributed than for Greater-Than / IoI / Country-Capital.
  • Core interpretation preliminary — where, not fully what/ how.

04 Questions for further exploration

Open threads the paper explicitly raises or leaves dangling

  1. Does the circuit extend to non-person animates — animals, personified agents — or the full Gregorio hierarchy (Human → Imaginary → Animal → Plant → Inanimate)?
  2. What mechanism (if any) governs the ~50% of pairs where the model fails the sign conditions?
  3. Would human-validated, lexically-disjoint splits shrink the Setup 1/2 vs. Setup 3 transfer gap?
  4. Can MLP0's "routing" role be confirmed by patching only its input edge — and does ablating it collapse downstream?
  5. Do animacy circuits overlap with thematic-role, selectional-preference, or passive-syntax circuits?
  6. Are cross-model differences (Qwen's tiny circuit, GPT-2's MLP0 dominance) driven by architecture, data, scale, or tokenization?
