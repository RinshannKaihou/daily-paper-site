Why This Matters

Before/after-RL causal evidence in chess challenges latent thought as an inference-time scratchpad — a methodological template for testing whether silent reasoning actually computes at inference time.

Critical Edge

Single training run per checkpoint and an n=100 harness — only one of five intervention conditions independently clears significance, and chess strength never improves (accuracy flat at 9–10%).

01 Methodology — the four-rung ladder

Four checkpoints on the same Qwen3-14B + LoRA(r=16) base, all scored on one frozen seed-3407 harness, greedy decode. Accuracy stays flat at 9–10% throughout — RL moves legality & confabulation, not playing strength.

Strengths 5

  • Identical causal battery on the same model before & after RL — the missing comparison in the entire latent-reasoning literature.
  • Six structurally distinct conditions; J-lens calibrated against known content before its null is trusted.
  • Gated-vs-summed reward ablation + Gumbel control as an explicit falsification attempt.
  • Honestly reports evidence cutting against the headline (raw-count framing, single-seed, Gumbel bias).
  • Mechanistic claim given a physical location (ΔW in MLP / gate_proj), not just a behavioral signature.

Limitations 6

  • Single run per checkpoint, single 14B base, LoRA-only, chess-only — openly acknowledged.
  • n=100; 4 of 5 non-baseline conditions do not independently reach significance.
  • No gain in chess strength — strictly a legality & confabulation result.
  • "Eliminated" confabulation partly artifactual: harness has zero real checkmate positions.
  • Gumbel control is a biased straight-through estimator — the thought-content question stays open.
  • Table-1 61% vs Table-2 58% baseline for the same adapter (NF4/bf16 dequant order variance).

03 Open Questions for Further Exploration

Does the before/after-RL battery replicate in MATH-500 / GSM8K, where prior work reports the opposite conclusion about thought content?
Retention-ratio vs. raw-board-drop disagreement: real feature of the 48%→58% baseline jump, or artifact? How does it behave at matched baselines?
Does an SVD of gate_proj's ΔW show low-rank structure, and do those layers also drive the Zero-condition collapse?
Would scaling the harness n=100 → n=1000 lift the four milder conditions to significance, or are they genuinely sub-threshold?
Would a less-biased thought gradient (REBAR, exact top-k marginalization) change the Gumbel picture, or is the deterministic-recipe advantage robust?
Does Rung-3 correctly identify actual checkmates on a separate test set, or has it merely learned to never say "checkmate"?
