Why this matters

The “capability holds = safe” acceptance test for quantized LLMs has a systematic blind spot in open-ended, multilingual generation — exactly the mode real users actually use.

Critical edge

The dose–response slope is judge-dependent: the headline ~24–27% level rests on a single independent judge, and the per-rung slope remains unresolved.

Methodology

  1. 1
    Effective bits-per-weight, read from the artifact
    Every stored tensor's size & quant type is read directly; total weight bytes ÷ params yields measured bpw. Nominal labels (“2-bit”) overstate compression by 11–40% because embeddings/attention/output head stay high-precision.
  2. 2
    QuantiBias protocol — generative probe + flat controls
    MBTP: a frozen 320-item balanced subset across 8 languages × 13 social categories, each an open-ended request plus a stereotype claim and a group-swapped counterpart. Paired with refusal (StrongREJECT), over-refusal (XSTest), MCQ bias (BBQ), next-token bias (StereoSet). Capability anchored by MMLU-Redux & GSM8K.
  3. 3
    Judging — in-family lean + two frontier validators
    Lean judge Qwen3-8B (~94% vs labeled reference). Independent validation by Claude Sonnet-5 & Gemini-2.5-pro; on n=160 they agree on 85% (α=0.69). The defended claim is the selective gap, not the absolute level.
  4. 4
    Severity rubric (0–4) decouples rate from harm
    Each one-bit endorsement re-scored by an independent judge: 0 = no bias, 4 = biased generalization asserted as fact. Mean over 48 endorsements: 1.83, 33/48 ≥ 2, none at the 4 ceiling.
  5. 5
    Mechanistic account — a logit-margin model
    Quantization noise ‖Δb‖² ∝ 2^(−2b) Gaussian-smooths the margin favoring the non-endorsing token: R(b)=∫ρ(m)Φ(−m/σ(b))dm. Alignment-reinforced behaviors sit on wide margins (survive); “don't volunteer a stereotype” sits on a narrow one (degrades).

Results & highlights

  • Selective gap confirmed. Refusal 0.98, BBQ 0.97, XSTest ~0.33 flat across 16→1.13 bpw — yet MBTP endorsement ~0.24–0.27 (≈ one open-ended answer in four).
  • StereoSet next-token control flat at 58–60 / 50-ideal — a short-form-only study would wrongly call quantization safety-neutral.
  • Capability holds through IQ2. MMLU-Redux 0.952 → 0.877 at one-bit; from Q4→IQ2 capability is flat while generative bias already rises.
  • Reasoning is family-dependent. On Qwen it halves MBTP and flattens the slope (+3.6 → +0.7); on Gemma-4-31B it barely moves it (IQ2 0.069 off vs 0.064 on).
  • CEB independently corroborates (disjoint items, n≈960/rung): lean 3.5%→24.9%, independent 13.5%→37.6% Q4→1-bit — rules out an MBTP-construction artifact.
  • Nominal-label correction. Qwen ladder measured bpw = 5.24 / 2.79 / 2.13 / 1.13 for nominal 4/2/ternary/1-bit.

Strengths & limits

Strengths

  • measured-bpw re-indexing exposes that “2-bit” builds are really 2.2–2.8 bpw — a methodological bar other compression work should adopt.
  • Selective-gap design pairs the open-ended probe with flat controls to prove selectivity, not general behavioral drift.
  • Intellectual honesty on judge dependence — openly reports the lean/independent disagreement and tags the slope “provisional”.
  • Multilingual scope (8 langs, parallel-translated, back-translated for drift) — rare for a compression study.
  • Independent corroboration via CEB on disjoint items, same direction.

Limitations

  • Level rests on one in-family judge; full-sample independent re-scoring exists only on a 2-rung pilot (n=80 each).
  • Slope is judge-dependent — lean rises, independent is ~flat; per-rung slope genuinely unresolved.
  • One anchor backbone (Qwen3.6-27B); Gemma + CEB point the same way but are underpowered (Cohen's h ≈ 0.15).
  • Activation precision held constant — treatment covers weight precision only.
  • Diagonal multilingual design entangles language, national target, content; only within-language slopes are clean.
  • Severity channel is a prediction, not measured; §7 is “an account, not a proof.”

Authors & credibility

  • Sole author Emilio Ferrara (USC) — well-cited on AI bias & network science; cites his own prior “butterfly effect” (2024) and bias-reduction audit (Chand et al. 2026) critically, not self-promotionally.
  • Reproducibility: probe, rubrics, scoring code & baselines released at huggingface.co/datasets/emilioferrara/quantibias.
  • Idempotent harness writes verbatim generations to an append-only store; every number traces to a job id + content hash. Decoding held constant across precisions (T=0.7, top-p=0.95).
  • All claims reported with Wilson 95% intervals; the dose-response is explicitly tagged “provisional” where judges disagree.

Questions for further exploration

Q1
Does the open-ended bias slope replicate once the full ladder is re-scored by the independent ensemble, not only the lean judge?
Q2
Would protecting bias-critical weights during quantization (à la Al Hakim et al. 2026), using bias-signal calibration data, close the gap — at what capability cost?
Q3
Why does deliberation halve bias on Qwen but not Gemma? Trace structure, training, or whether it actually revisits the stereotype decision?
Q4
Does the predicted severity channel (s̄(b) rising with compression, H(b)=f(b)·s̄(b)) hold under a graded rubric, given only frequency is measured today?
Q5
How do slopes change once activation precision (not just weight precision) is varied on appropriate hardware?
Q6
Can a fully-disentangled parallel-translated probe confirm the bias damage is worse in non-English settings, as the diagonal data hint?
