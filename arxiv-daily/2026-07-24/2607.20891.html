Is Deep Research Reliable? Misleading Knowledge Induces False Conclusions

Pengyu Zhu · Lijun Li · Longju Yang · Sen Su — BUPT · Shanghai AI Lab · Chongqing Univ. of Posts & Telecom.

deep-research llm-agents misinformation reliability benchmark MisKnow-Agent FCAR
⬢ Key Finding
A single credible-looking misleading page can flip a long-horizon research report: late-stage injection drives adoption to 85%, and framework design shifts reliability more than the backbone LLM.

Why This Matters

One authoritative-looking misleading page can flip a long-horizon research report — a direct threat to AI4Science, finance, and medical research where reports drive real decisions.

Critical Edge

DeepSeek-V4 Pro is both corpus generator and sole judge — a self-bias risk on a 300-sample validation set. Defenses tested are only static prompts + single-pass refinement, so "never eliminated" may understate what continuous cross-state verification could achieve.

01 Methodology

MisKnow-Agent — three-stage misleading-knowledge factory

Threat model. A Deep Research task is (q, E, A) — agent A plans, retrieves, analyzes and synthesizes over evidence environment E. Per task a target false conclusion cf(q) and misleading set M_q are defined; only the content changes — no prompt injection.

  • Blueprint. Per task: b_i = ⟨cf_i, H_i, ψ_i⟩ — false conclusion, authority-tiered institution pool, FCAR judgment spec.
  • Controlled generation. Document generator spans 3 authority tiers × 4 styles × 5 institutions/tier per blueprint.
  • Search-guided refinement. 5 search-enabled verifiers (GLM-5, Kimi-2.6, DeepSeek-V4 Pro, Qwen3.5-397B, Intern-S1-Pro) must unanimously classify "Mis" or the cross-model refinement loop fires until consensus or budget exhaustion.

Setup. 100 DeepResearch Bench tasks · temp 0 · Serper top-k=10 · default High-authority + Paper style. FCAR counts a report as adopting only when it takes the false conclusion as its own — mention/cite/refute do not. Judge–human agreement: 99.0%, κ=0.978, F1=99.22%.

02 Results & Highlights

Timing decides everything

85.0%

mid-workflow
pre-synthesis
  • Framework > backbone. DeerFlow 66.7% vs WebThinker 29.3% under matched conditions — a 25–53 pp gap that exceeds any intra-framework LLM gap (~16 pp). The strongest LLM even flips between frameworks.
  • One doc is enough. Document budget 1→5 moves FCAR only 2.5 pp (46.2%→48.7%) — no dose-response.
  • Style > authority. Paper style 48.0% vs Post 29.7% (18.3 pp); High/Med/Low authority 48.0 / 43.3 / 36.8% (11.2 pp).
  • Rank barely matters. Front/mid/back position: 65.2 / 65.7 / 64.2% — a 1.5 pp swing.
  • Verify–adopt disconnect. The same instance all 5 verifiers flag as Mis still gets adopted in long-horizon research.

Defenses help, never cure

No defense60–76%

Combined defense regresses on Intern-S1-Pro (62% > 57/58% single) — post-refinement re-queries the same polluted retrieval env.

03 Strengths & Limitations

▲ Strengths
  • First systematic study of workflow-level (not short-horizon RAG) misleading reliability.
  • Strict FCAR — distinguishes "adopted as own" from mention/cite/refute, avoiding overclaim.
  • Triple quality control: cross-model refinement + 5-model unanimous Mis + human screen.
  • Clean authority-vs-style separation (single-variable per blueprint).
  • Spans open- and closed-source; surfaces the overlooked framework-design axis.
▼ Limitations
  • Single judge model (DeepSeek-V4 Pro) — also the generator, self-bias risk; agreement checked on 300 samples only.
  • Gemini's internal retrieval is uncontrollable — online search stays on, injection less strict.
  • Defenses are static prompt + single refinement pass — stronger continuous/at-adversarial defenses untested.
  • 100 tasks, all research/analysis queries; no real-time news / business due-diligence generalization.
  • No cost/latency accounting; misleading corpus not released (ethics) — reproducibility limited.

04 Core Contributions

C1MisKnow-Agent — three-stage framework (blueprint → controlled generation → search-guided cross-model refinement + filter) for credible-but-false knowledge targeting Deep Research.
C25,933-instance corpus on DeepResearch Bench, varied across authority (H/M/L) × style (Paper/News/Blog/Post); all retained instances pass 5 search-enabled verifiers unanimously.
C3Workflow-level reliability audit — FCAR is governed by lifecycle timing (85.0% vs 34.5%) and framework design more than backbone LLM.
C4Defense study — pre/post/combined all reduce FCAR but never eliminate it, motivating continuous cross-state verification.

05 Open Questions

  1. If the post-research refinement agent used a clean, independent retrieval environment, would the Intern-S1-Pro regression (62%) disappear?
  2. Does the lifecycle effect stem from the synthesize step's context compression? Would forcing evidence-confidence labels there help?
  3. Is FCAR domain-dependent — science vs finance vs policy queries?
  4. Under active adversarial deployment (attacker seeds the live web), how much further does FCAR climb?
  5. Does WebThinker's compression also drop legitimate evidence — is reliability bought at the cost of report coverage?
  6. Is there a framework pattern (source-diversity, cross-source corroboration, uncertainty propagation) that drives FCAR to single digits cheaply?
  7. With a third-party judge (GPT-5 / Claude), are the FCAR conclusions robust?
  8. What's the misleading-knowledge half-life if the agent gets N extra reflection/rebuttal turns post-injection?
