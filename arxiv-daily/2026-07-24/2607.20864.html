Position Bias is Hidden Behind Ceiling Effects: A Permutation Diagnostic for LLM Benchmarks

Position bias is statistically detectable only inside a 60–95% base-accuracy "Goldilocks zone" — frontier models showing no signal on standard MMLU does not mean they are unbiased.

Why this matters

Reframes vendor "no position bias" claims as not measurable on this instrument — a measurement-instrument critique, not a model endorsement.

Critical edge

Only 5 MMLU subjects × 50 questions, and only the cheaper tier per vendor — frontier models (GPT-5, Opus 4.8, Gemini 3 Pro, Grok 4) are explicitly out of scope.

Methodology 01

inspect_permute is a drop-in extension for the inspect_ai framework. Instead of a single answer-order shuffle — which confounds genuine position bias with question-level noise — it runs all k! permutations per question by reusing inspect_ai's epochs parameter. In exhaustive mode, epoch e maps deterministically to permutation index (epoch-1) % k!.

Three statistics layer the verdict: a chi-squared test for the calibrated yes/no, Cramér's V as a sample-size-invariant effect size, and Spearman ρ between position and per-position accuracy to separate monotone (ρ → −1) from non-monotone profiles. Falsifier rules and per-cell expected V ranges were locked in preregistration.md, its SHA-256 hash posted publicly on LinkedIn before Google/xAI cells ran.

Cell classification rule inactive if V<0.05 or p≥0.05 · processing_load if V≥0.05 ∧ ρ≤−0.5 ∧ p<0.05 · else content_ambiguity.

Strengths & limitations 03

Strengths

  • Exhaustive k! permutation removes the unobserved-permutation confound of single-shuffle studies.
  • χ² + V + ρ stack cleanly separates mechanisms a p-value alone compresses away.
  • Venue-free preregistration (git tag + SHA-256 + LinkedIn timestamp) — portable to any researcher.
  • Exemplary self-disclosure: v0.1 classifier flaw, 5/10 miss rate, credit interruption all reported.

Limitations

  • One benchmark family (MMLU); transfer to BBH, GPQA, FrontierMath untested.
  • Floor edge of the band (acc < 0.60) is extrapolated, not observed — lowest sweep point is 0.61.
  • Three-band / Goldilocks framework is post-hoc to the preregistration.
  • Reasoning-trace vendors (Gemini, Grok): "bias laundering" inside the trace cannot be ruled out.

Open questions 04

  • Does the Goldilocks width & the 0.60 / 0.95 thresholds replicate on GPQA Diamond, FrontierMath, harder BBH splits?
  • Is Gemini / Grok "no bias" genuine mitigation, ceiling saturation, or bias laundering inside the reasoning trace?
  • Does content_ambiguity (D-drop) generalize beyond haiku's elem_math & philosophy cells?
  • How does the 7.5× avg / 18.5× peak cost asymmetry scale with reasoning-trace length on next-gen frontier models?
