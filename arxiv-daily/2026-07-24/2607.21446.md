> **一句话总结：** KroQuant 提出一种学习到的、块对角 Kronecker 结构可逆激活变换，作为扩散 Transformer 在 W4A4 量化时 SmoothQuant 的直接替代，以比逐通道缩放更少的参数实现更高的量化精度，同时在 MI350 GPU 上量化内核更快达 14%。

> **TL;DR:** KroQuant is a post-training quantization method for diffusion transformers (DiTs) that replaces SmoothQuant's diagonal activation transform with a learned, 32×32 block-diagonal Kronecker-structured invertible transform; on PixArt-Σ, SANA, and FLUX.1-schnell at W4A4 (MXFP4e2) over MJHQ-30K and SDCI it reduces LPIPS by up to 17.5% relative (e.g., FLUX.1-schnell/SDCI: 0.283 vs. 0.343 for LoRaQ) and matches or improves FID/PSNR over SVDQuant and LoRaQ, while its quantizer kernel runs up to 14% faster than the SmoothQuant kernel on an MI350 GPU and stores fewer than half the parameters.

## 核心贡献

1. **一种与在线约束兼容的学习型块对角激活变换**：首个用于 DiT PTQ 的非对角在线变换——比固定块 Hadamard 更具表达力，又比全维度旋转（SpinQuant）或全可逆矩阵（FlatQuant）在推理时更便宜。
2. **GL(32) 块的 Kronecker-LU 参数化**：5 个 2×2 的单位行列式 LU 因子的 Kronecker 乘积，每个 32×32 块仅 15 个参数，比稠密 GL(32) 块小 68 倍，并以 Hadamard 旋转作为自然初始化。
3. **融合的 W4A4 内核**：每个块以单次 tensor-core GEMM 应用；在 MI350 上 KroQuant 量化内核比 SmoothQuant 基线快达 14%；两个 Triton 变体 K1（预计算稠密块）与 K2（从 15 参数即时重建，相对 K1 压缩 68 倍）都原生支持任意 32 整除维度，避免 FWHT 的 padding/截断。
4. **与权重侧校正正交可组合**：作为 LoRaQ 前端的 drop-in 替换，二者组合优于任一单独组件。

## Key Contributions

- **First non-diagonal *online* activation transform for DiT PTQ.** The learned block-diagonal transform (32×32 blocks) is more expressive than a fixed block Hadamard yet cheaper at inference than full-d rotations (SpinQuant) or general invertibles (FlatQuant), filling a previously empty point in the design space because the standard LLM "absorption trick" is broken by AdaLN between DiT blocks.
- **Kronecker–LU parametrization of GL(32) blocks.** Each 32×32 block is the Kronecker product of five 2×2 unit-determinant LU factors (15 scalars/block vs. 1024 for unconstrained GL(32), i.e., ~68× smaller), with the normalized Hadamard as a special-case initialization, guaranteeing invertibility and numerical stability.
- **Fused W4A4 tensor-core kernels.** Two Triton kernels (K1 precomputed dense, K2 on-the-fly Kronecker reconstruction) both apply as a single 32×32 GEMM per block fused with the MXFP4e2 quantizer; on MI350 the quantizer kernel runs up to 14% faster than the SmoothQuant kernel, and K2/K1 are 1.30–2.45× faster than FWHT while handling non-power-of-two dimensions natively.
- **Drop-in complement to weight-side corrections.** KroQuant substitutes for SmoothQuant in the LoRaQ pipeline unchanged, and the ablation shows the combination beats either component alone.

## Methodology

**Setup.** Each linear layer O = AW⊤ receives an invertible transform T ∈ GL(d) applied as A→AT, W→WT^(−T) (Eq. 1), leaving the linear map invariant while reducing the per-layer quantization error E(T) = Q_b(AT)·Q_b(WT^(−⊤))⊤ − AW⊤. Unlike in LLMs, the absorption trick fails in DiTs because AdaLN sitting between blocks does not commute with T (Eq. 3), so every transform runs *online* at every denoising step — making per-step cost the binding design constraint.

**Block-diagonal Kronecker structure (Section 3.2).** T = diag(K^(1),…,K^(⌈d/n⌉)) with n = 32, where each K = G₁ ⊗ G₂ ⊗ G₃ ⊗ G₄ ⊗ G₅ is a Kronecker product of five 2×2 factors (log₂32 = 5). Inversion reduces to five independent 2×2 inversions.

**Unit-determinant LU parametrization (Eq. 6).** Each G_k = L_k·U_k with L_k = [[1,0],[c_k,1]] and U_k = [[a_k, b_k],[0, −1/a_k]], a_k ≠ 0, giving det G_k = −1 and |det G_k| = 1 to prevent degenerate collapse of one operand. Three free parameters (a_k, b_k, c_k) per factor → 15 per 32×32 block.

**Hadamard initialization.** Each G_k is initialized to the 2×2 Hadamard rotation (cos π/4, sin π/4, …), so T₀ is a block-diagonal Hadamard rotation — empirically a good outlier-suppressing starting point.

**Layer-wise optimization (Section 3.3).** Per linear layer, minimize total loss L = L_out + λ_A L_A + λ_W L_W, where L_out is the output MSE (Eq. 7) and L_A, L_W are auxiliary per-operand quantization-error terms (Eq. 8). λ_A, λ_W are auto-scaled so each auxiliary term matches L_out's magnitude at initialization (Eq. 10). Optimized with Adam and a straight-through estimator (STE) through the non-differentiable quantizer; kept at the layer level because joint multi-layer optimization was unstable.

**Offline LoRaQ weight correction (Section 3.4).** After the transforms are fixed, LoRaQ decomposes each transformed weight into a b-bit quantized residual plus a low-rank (rank-128) correction L·R that is itself quantizable below 16 bits; inference sums the quantized residual matmul and the mixed-precision low-rank correction (Eq. 11).

**Kernels (Section 3.5).** K1 materializes each 32×32 block (1024 values/block) in HBM; K2 reconstructs it on-the-fly from 15 scalars via the bit-indexed Kronecker entry formula K[r,c] = Π_k G_k[r_{5−k}, c_{5−k}] (Eq. 12), freeing the register budget to fuse the MXFP4e2 quantizer in the same kernel.

## Results & Highlights

- **LPIPS (perceptual distance to FP reference) — strongest headline result.** On MJHQ-30K, KroQuant reduces LPIPS by 0.034 (8.9% relative) over LoRaQ on PixArt-Σ (0.347 vs. 0.381) and by 0.023 (10.0%) on SANA (0.206 vs. 0.229). On FLUX.1-schnell/SDCI it cuts LPIPS to 0.283 vs. 0.343 for LoRaQ — a 17.5% relative reduction — and achieves the best LPIPS on all three models on SDCI.
- **FID gap to full-precision essentially closed on two of three models.** On MJHQ-30K, PixArt-Σ reaches FID 16.9 vs. 16.6 (FP16 ref); SANA matches FP16 exactly at 16.6; on SDCI, PixArt-Σ improves FID to 21.9 vs. 23.7 for LoRaQ and 23.3 for SVDQuant.
- **Ablation isolates the learned-transform contribution.** Without LoRaQ, KroQuant alone reduces FID from 130.6 (Smoothing) to 41.8 on PixArt-Σ/MJHQ-30K. Holding LoRaQ fixed, the 15 learnable Kronecker parameters improve on a fixed block Hadamard-32 (same init) by ΔLPIPS = 0.035 and ΔPSNR = +0.8 dB, demonstrating the parameters capture per-layer numerical structure a fixed rotation cannot.
- **Kernel speed.** On MI350/FP16, K1 is 1.00–1.14× faster than per-channel Scale on every tested power-of-two shape (i.e., up to 14% faster than the SmoothQuant-class kernel), and K2 reaches up to 2.45× speedup over FWHT on larger matrices while keeping 68× parameter compression (15 scalars vs. 1024/block). K2 stays within 25% of Scale.
- **Footprint.** Fewer than half the parameters of per-channel scaling per layer, and 68× smaller than dense GL(32) blocks.
- **One honest weak spot.** On FLUX.1-schnell/MJHQ-30K the picture is mixed: KroQuant achieves the best FID (19.8) and PSNR (16.9) but does not improve IR over SVDQuant (0.880 vs. 0.952) or LPIPS over LoRaQ (0.336 vs. 0.331), attributed to the double-stream/joint-attention architecture's heterogeneous token distributions.

## Critical Assessment

The paper is honest about its niche and its scope. The central technical insight — that AdaLN breaks the absorption trick that lets LLM rotation methods fold T offline (Eq. 3), so the DiT transform *must* be online and therefore must be cheap *and* expressive — is correct, well-motivated, and not previously occupied: SmoothQuant is cheap-but-diagonal, Hadamard-32 is fixed and underperforms per-channel scaling, SpinQuant/FlatQuant are expressive but require a prohibitive d×d GEMM per layer per step. The Kronecker-LU construction (5 × 2×2 unit-det factors → 15 params/block) is a neat way to land between Hadamard and GL(32), and the ablation concretely shows those 15 parameters matter (ΔLPIPS 0.035, ΔPSNR 0.8 dB over the fixed Hadamard at the same block size).

Two caveats are worth flagging. First, the reported speedups are measured *only* for the quantizer-kernel microbenchmark, not end-to-end wall-clock image generation; at larger d the paper itself notes "the fused Q4 quantizer already dominates kernel time, leaving little headroom between any transform variant and the bare quantizer," so the practical latency win in a full pipeline may be modest. Second, the mixed FLUX.1-schnell/MJHQ-30K result (worse IR than SVDQuant, worse LPIPS than LoRaQ) is attributed to double-stream heterogeneity but is only diagnosed, not fixed — the per-layer Kronecker transform may genuinely underfit joint-attention layers. The straight-through-estimator optimization being "unstable at a block or model level" and confined to layer-wise training is an admitted limitation, and the theoretical basis for the observation that KroQuant outputs have higher contrast than FP16 is left open.

## Strengths

- **Occupies a genuinely empty point in the design space** — the first learned, non-diagonal, *online-feasible* activation transform for DiT PTQ, with a clear argument for why each neighbor (SmoothQuant, Hadamard, SpinQuant, FlatQuant) fails the online constraint.
- **Parameter-efficient and hardware-native.** 15 scalars per 32×32 block (68× smaller than dense GL(32), less than half of per-channel scaling), applied as a single tensor-core GEMM per block fused with the MXFP4e2 quantizer; supports arbitrary d divisible by 32 (no FWHT padding/truncation).
- **Strong quantitative results on 2 of 3 models and on SDCI across all three**, with the best LPIPS on every SDCI configuration, plus a clean ablation that isolates the transform's contribution from LoRaQ's weight correction.
- **Honest, well-scoped experimental reporting** — includes a bare-quantizer lower bound (Q4), production-shape latencies (Table 4), and explicit failure-mode discussion (FLUX.1/MJHQ).

## Limitations

- **Tied to n = 32 in practice.** The Kronecker construction has only 3·log₂n parameters per n×n block, growing too slowly to approximate GL(n) at larger block sizes.
- **Mixed results on the largest model (FLUX.1-schnell, 12B) on MJHQ-30K** — loses to SVDQuant on IR and to LoRaQ on LPIPS; diagnosed as a double-stream/joint-attention heterogeneity issue but not resolved.
- **Layer-wise optimization only.** Joint multi-layer optimization is unstable, partly due to the unit-determinant LU parametrization; alternative parametrizations are left as future work.
- **Unexplained empirical artifact:** KroQuant outputs tend to have higher contrast than the FP16 reference, with no theoretical interpretation.
- **Speedups are kernel-microbenchmark only**, not end-to-end image-generation wall-clock; at large d the fused quantizer dominates, narrowing the headroom.
- **No LLM evaluation** — the method is DiT-specific by design (motivated by AdaLN), but the question of whether the same construction helps any non-DiT online-transform regime is unaddressed.

## Relevance

对于从事**扩散模型/文本到图像生成的高效部署、模型量化和加速器内核优化**的研究者，本文直接相关。它针对 W4A4 (MXFP4e2) 这一当前硬件原生支持的精度点，给出了一种在精度与在线推理成本之间取得新平衡的激活变换方案，可作为 SmoothQuant/Hadamard 在 DiT 场景下的直接替代，并可与 LoRaQ 等权重侧方法正交组合。对关注 OCP Microscaling 标准、AMD MI350/CDNA 4 生态或 Triton 内核设计的工程团队尤其有参考价值。其 Kronecker–LU 参数化思路也可能启发其他需要在"在线变换 + 低参数量 + tensor-core 友好"三者之间权衡的量化场景。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- What is the **end-to-end wall-clock speedup** (full denoising loop, image/sec) of K1/K2 over SmoothQuant and FWHT, beyond the quantizer-kernel microbenchmark — and how much of the theoretical headroom survives once the fused Q4 quantizer dominates at large d?
- Can a **joint-attention-aware variant** of the transform (e.g., separate Kronecker factors for text vs. image token slices) recover KroQuant's losses on FLUX.1-schnell/MJHQ-30K, or is a per-token-type transform needed?
- Does a different parametrization (e.g., **Stiefel-manifold or QR-based** instead of unit-determinant LU) stabilize joint multi-layer optimization and unlock cross-layer co-optimization?
- How does KroQuant scale to **block sizes n ∈ {64, 128}**, and can the Kronecker factor count grow fast enough to track the expressivity of GL(n) before hitting the MXFP4 block-size constraint?
- Is the **higher-contrast output artifact** a quantization-induced redistribution of activation magnitudes that biases the decoder, and can a small output-side recalibration remove it?
- Can the learned Kronecker transform transfer **across checkpoints or prompts** (calibrate once, deploy widely), or does it require per-checkpoint recalibration like other PTQ transforms?
- Does the same construction help any **non-DiT online-transform regime** (e.g., streaming/norm-blocked LLM layers where absorption also fails)?
