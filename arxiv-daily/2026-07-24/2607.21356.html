01Methodology

Extract the cause before it exists.

i
Contrastive teacher forcing on a frozen model
One neutral system prompt generates a shared greedy response. The frozen Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct then re-reads that byte-identical response twice — once under a "dangerously reckless" persona, once under "carefully cautious." A hash of token ids must match across passes, so the residual-stream difference reflects who the model was told is speaking, not what was said.
ii
SVD → rank-4 persona subspace
Averaging per-pair differences and taking the top left singular vectors yields a rank-4 subspace per domain per contrast, built from 12 descriptor-variant pairs so persona, style, and topic are matched in diversity.
iii
Two readouts
A differentiable teacher-forced log-prob margin M = M⁺ − M⁻ over an OOD prompt battery; and a judged eval (AWQ Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct) scoring alignment & coherence on free-form answers — "broadly misaligned" = alignment <30 and coherence ≥50.
iv
Matched-control causal arms
Necessity: project the subspace out of the residual stream every forward pass during fine-tuning (rank-matched random as control). Sufficiency: inject the direction into the never-fine-tuned model across a dose grid (norm-matched random as control). A third arm applies the same projection to the weight gradient to test read vs. write.
v
Pre-registration
The subspace was extracted from the frozen model and written to disk before any organism existed — the methodologically clean way to tell a pre-existing cause from a post-hoc descriptor.

03Strengths / Limitations

Tight design, hard scope.

Strengths

  • Pre-registered extraction. Subspace fixed to disk before any organism existed — distinguishes a cause from a descriptor.
  • Matched random on every half. Rank-matched removal, norm-matched injection, gradient-size-matched inoculation; the same projection used in two channels.
  • Intellectual honesty. Reports that judged rates can't separate insecure vs educational arms at this scale, and that the prevention also destroys the narrow task.
  • "Removal vs. suppression" certificate. Detects an edit suppressed behavior rather than removing it — ~97% of the carrier re-forms inside the cleared subspace.
  • Engages concurrent non-reproduction. Cites Schreiber & Goldstein and Drake & Eberstadt directly rather than ignoring them.

Limitations

  • Single model, scale, method. Only Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct + LoRA; concurrent work reports emergent misalignment largely fails to reproduce across open weights.
  • Capability confound. The activation projection that drops misalignment to 0.0% also collapses narrow adherence 0.902 → 0.000.
  • Judged behavior doesn't carry the intent contrast. At 14B the authors' own fine-tunes reach only 1.8–8.0% judged misalignment; the intent-graded claim rests entirely on margins.
  • Provenance open. Extracted from an aligned checkpoint, not a pre-alignment base — overlaps the assistant direction at cosine 0.565.
  • Removal foreclosure is basis-scoped. All 3 failed edits use the naive writer-column lift; the shared write core & functional pullback were never tried.

04Open questions

Where the structure breaks — or doesn't.

  1. Q1Does the persona subspace exist in a base (pre-alignment) checkpoint, and at what sharing level? Re-extracting across intermediate alignment checkpoints would resolve the open provenance question.
  2. Q2Does the 27.7%→0.0% prevention survive a usage-matched (not just rank-matched) random control? The paper itself flags this as a confound.
  3. Q3Can the read-channel carrier and weight-space write core be linked by a measured overlap statistic (or a Jacobian-vector-product pullback), not just interpretation?
  4. Q4Does the small first-step coupling (Cliff's δ = 0.075) scale into a judged-behavior separation at 32B/72B, or stay margin-only at 14B?
  5. Q5Does the superadditive growth curve hold at 2 and 3 domains with benign twins, and beyond Qwen2.5?
  6. Q6Does editing the shared write core after training succeed where the three naive-basis edits failed?
  7. Q7Can the "author latent" reading be tested directly by varying implied-author signal in pretraining and watching the core shrink?
  8. Q8How robust is any defense to adversarial trigger design once suppression is known to relocate behind an inoculation trigger?
