01 Methodology

Diagonal Fisher closes the sensitivity–scaling gap

Existing PTQ measures sensitivity along the modality or token axis but optimizes scaling per channel — a fundamental misalignment (Fig. 1–2).

  • Observation: near channel 3200 of Qwen2.5VL-7B, quantization error is tiny yet the SFT gradient is huge → vanilla AWQ mistreats it.
  • Second-order Taylor: ΔL ≈ ½ ΔYᵀ H ΔY (OBD), then approximate Hessian with empirical Fisher F = E[ggᵀ].
  • Diagonal cut: fⱼ = E[g²ᵢⱼ] turns loss perturbation into channel-weighted MSE — ΔL ≈ ½‖√f ⊙ Δy‖² (Eqn. 11/14).
  • Alg. 1: forward-collect per-layer output gradients → per-channel mean-square → 40-step grid search under Fisher-weighted MSE.

03 Strengths & Limitations

Conceptually clean, deployment-friendly — but scoped to the decoder

▲ Strengths

  • Coherent Hessian → Fisher → diagonal chain; OBD assumption empirically backed (Table 6).
  • Unifies sensitivity measurement and scaling search on a single channel axis.
  • MBQ-class speed, far faster than QIG; no LoRA / low-rank modules at inference.
  • SOTA held across 3 modalities, 2B–72B scales, weight-only and weight-activation.

▼ Limitations

  • Vision encoder + projection left full-precision → incomplete end-to-end memory/latency gain.
  • W4A4 still degrades badly; needs extra compensation (future work).
  • Diagonal Fisher ignores inter-channel coupling — no theoretical guarantee it stays equivalent on deeper / longer-context models.
  • Requires a differentiable SFT objective; RLHF / DPO / pure-generation not studied.
04 Questions for further exploration

Where C-PTQ bends, breaks, or generalizes

End-to-end quantization: how large is the gain once vision encoder + projection are quantized, and do vision-side outliers clash with LLM-side Fisher weights?
Extreme bits: does diagonal Fisher stay equivalent to dense at W4A4 / W3A8, or must inter-channel coupling be reintroduced?
Objective alignment: if Fisher is computed from RLHF / DPO policy or reward gradients instead of SFT loss, does it better track human-preference sensitivity?
Is activation-Hessian dead? GPTQ underperforms even RTN on MLLMs — should all activation-Hessian methods be retired here, or can the loss-curve gap be closed generally?
Mixed precision: is C-PTQ orthogonal or conflicting with bit-allocation schemes (MABA, High-Impact-Ratio)? Could Fisher weight guide both scaling and bit-width?
Calibration distribution: how does COCO-caption vs Pile calibration trade off across OCR-heavy vs reasoning-heavy tasks?
