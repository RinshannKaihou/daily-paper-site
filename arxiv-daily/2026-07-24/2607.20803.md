> **一句话总结：** 本文将"人格"从静态的 Big Five 特质重构为荣格八种认知功能（Fe/Fi/Te/Ti/Ne/Ni/Se/Si）这一动态认知过程，构建了 2100+ 角色扮演叙述数据集 NarrationDB，对 Llama-3.1-8B 做激活引导，首次实现八维人格的同时可控行为调节，并揭示人格信息集中在 transformer 中间层（7~12），理性/非理性功能在激活空间呈现与荣格理论一致的可区分几何结构。

> **TL;DR:** The authors replace static Big Five trait steering with dynamic Jungian Cognitive Functions (8 functions: Ti/Te/Fi/Fe/Ni/Ne/Si/Se), release NarrationDB (2100+ role-play narrations built on SWCPQ), and on Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct achieve monotonic activation control of all eight functions (single-dimensional steering effectiveness up to +56.70 score for Se and +56.47 for Ne vs. their low-cluster baselines), localize personality representation to middle layers 7–12, and show via a Least-Squares backtracking method that multi-dimensional steering directions carry non-trivial residuals (0.23–0.65) versus any linear combination of single-function directions.

## 核心贡献

1. **新的人格视角与评估协议**：首次将 LLM 人格控制从静态 Big Five 特质框架转向荣格八种认知功能（Jungian Cognitive Functions）的动态过程模型，并把一个面向人类的人格测验（Felmonon 2026，132 条陈述，5 级 Likert）改造为 LLM 评估框架，用 `w = 0.7P + 0.3(6 − N)`、`S = (100·w − 1)/4` 的打分公式给每个认知功能打分。

2. **高质量数据集 NarrationDB**：基于 SWCPQ 数据集（Jorgenson 2020），用 Llama-3.1-70B-Instruct 为 2100+ 虚拟角色生成注入"高共识 tag"的角色自述，再用 Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct 角色扮演，并存储每个角色的八维荣格分数；数据成功填满八维人格空间。

3. **完整的引导向量提取与评估方法**：使用 difference-in-means 方向（Marks & Tegmark 2024），对每个功能用 top/bottom k=10 角色聚类，每角色 T=5 个开放式问题，提取并注入单位化向量 `Lat_steered = Lat + λ·V/||V||`，λ 在 [−6.875, +6.875] 步长 0.25 上扫描，并以"指令遵循率 < 80% 则丢弃"作为有效门槛。

4. **三项机制性发现**：(a) 人格信息集中在中间层 7~12，呼应 encode-compute-decode 假设；(b) 理性功能（Te/Ti/Fe/Fi）与其内外倾对应体在 PCA 空间更接近，非理性功能（Ne/Ni/Se/Si）更远，与荣格原始区分一致；(c) 多维引导方向并非单维方向的线性组合，最小二乘残差在 0.23–0.65 之间。

## Key Contributions

1. **A cognitive-process (not trait) framing of LLM personality.** Instead of the static Big Five (OCEAN), the paper steers and analyzes LLMs along the eight Jungian Cognitive Functions (Thinking/Feeling/Sensing/Intuition × Introverted/Extraverted), and adapts a 132-statement Likert test into an LLM evaluation protocol with the scoring rule `S = (100·(0.7P + 0.3(6−N)) − 1)/4`.

2. **NarrationDB — a 2,100+ character role-play dataset.** Built on SWCPQ (Jorgenson 2020): Llama-3.1-70B-Instruct generates human-tag-grounded self-narrations, Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct then role-plays each character; the resulting characters populate the full 8-D Jungian space and are paired with their function scores.

3. **End-to-end steering pipeline with effectiveness metric.** Difference-in-means extraction (k=10 per cluster, T=5 open questions), unit-norm injection, λ-sweep over [−6.875, 6.875], and an instruction-following threshold (drop results below 80% completion) make the evaluation reproducible and grounded.

4. **Three mechanistic findings** about how LLMs encode personality: middle-layer concentration (layers 7–12), a rational-vs-irrational geometric split consistent with Jung, and the non-linearity of multi-dimensional steering directions (Least-Squares residual 0.23–0.65).

## Methodology

**Evaluation protocol (Sec. 2).** 132 Jungian statements are shuffled and split into 4 groups of 33, presented to the LLM with a PsychoBench-style prefix that forces 1–5 numerical answers. Because the shuffle seed affects LLM responses, each test pass uses 30 random seeds and the results are analyzed as a group. Per-function score: `w = 0.7P + 0.3(6−N)` then `S = (100·w − 1)/4`, where P/N are the average scores of positively/negatively contributing questions.

**NarrationDB construction (Sec. 3).** From SWCPQ, per character, compute margin of error `MOE = 2σ/√N` (95% confidence), keep the 20 entries with smallest MOE, then pick 10 tags closest to 0 or 100 on each side. These human-aligned tags are injected into a generation prompt; Llama-3.1-70B-Instruct writes the self-narration, and Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct role-plays with that narration in the system prompt before being re-tested.

**Steering vector extraction (Sec. 4).** For each function `Cf`, sort all characters by their Jungian score `SCf`, take top/bottom k=10 to form `HCf` and `LCf`. Each character answers T=5 open-ended questions; the average latent over response tokens at layer `n` gives `Lat(char)_n`. The cluster averages yield the steering direction `VCf,n = Lat(HCf)_n − Lat(LCf)_n`.

**Injection & evaluation (Sec. 5).** `Lat_steered = Lat + λ·V/||V||`; λ swept in [−6.875, +6.875] at step 0.25 at the most effective layer `n*` (from Sec. 6). Any [λ,n] with task completion < 80% is discarded. Effectiveness score = Smax − Smin across λ.

**Multi-dimensional steering (Sec. 8).** Define an odd-square reward `OddSq(x) = x|x|` and a pivot score `Spivot = OddSq(NS_Ne) + OddSq(NS_Ti)` on z-normalized function scores; extract `VNe+Ti,n` directly from clusters high/low on the pivot score. To test whether this multi-dim direction is a linear combination of single directions, solve `V' = w1·VTi + w2·VNe` via Least Squares and measure residual `r = ||VNe+Ti − V'||`.

## Results & Highlights

- **All eight Jungian functions are monotonically controllable via single-layer steering** on Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct (Table 4). The strongest are **Se (effectiveness 56.70, score 39.06 → 95.77, best at layer 9)** and **Ne (effectiveness 56.47, score 41.60 → 98.07, best at layer 12)**; the weakest is **Fi (effectiveness only 19.20, 71.58 → 90.78, best at layer 7)**, which the authors attribute to ethics post-training pinning Fi high (since low Fi correlates with low moral identity per Jung 1971, p. 725).

- **Personality representation is concentrated in middle layers 7–12.** A full 31-intersection layer scan (Fig. 4) shows a clear "high-effectiveness volume" in layers 7–12, with the best layer per function falling in {7, 8, 9, 12} (Table 4). This is consistent with the encode-compute-decode chain hypothesis (Skean et al. 2025; Wu et al. 2025): early layers encode personality, middle layers compute with it, late layers decode toward next-token prediction.

- **The activation-space geometry mirrors Jung's rational/irrational distinction.** PCA of the layer-12 steering vectors (PC1 = 49.9%, PC2 = 24.4%, Fig. 5) shows rational functions (Te/Ti/Fe/Fi) clustering closer to their introverted–extraverted counterparts, while irrational functions (Ne/Ni/Se/Si) sit farther apart — and this structure is stable across layers 7–12 (Figs. 9–13 in Appendix D).

- **Multi-dimensional steering is achievable but is NOT a linear combination of single-function directions.** Direct cluster extraction successfully produced a `VNe+Ti` that linearly co-steers both Ti and Ne (Fig. 6). However, backtracking via Least Squares on 12 common combinations yields residuals ranging **0.23 (Ni+Te) to 0.65 (Si+Ne)** (Table 5), empirically confirming that personality vectors are inherently entangled and that semantic directions cannot simply be added.

## Critical Assessment

The paper is methodologically careful in several non-obvious ways. First, it acknowledges that the **shuffle seed influences LLM answers** and mitigates this by aggregating 30 seeds rather than reporting a single run — a detail many personality-steering evaluations skip. Second, it imposes an **80% instruction-following threshold** so that aggressive λ values cannot inflate "effectiveness" by degenerating the model. Third, the **multi-dimensional backtracking experiment** (Table 5, residual 0.23–0.65) is a genuinely novel diagnostic: rather than just showing multi-dim steering works, it quantifies that the effective direction lies outside the linear span of single directions, giving concrete evidence for vector entanglement rather than asserting it.

That said, the mechanistic claims rest on a thin experimental base. **Only one model family and size is studied (Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct)**; the middle-layer concentration, the rational/irrational PCA split, and the residual ranges could all be model-specific artifacts of Llama-3.1's training or post-training, and the paper does not test any of Llama-3.1-70B, Qwen, Gemma, or Mistral to disambiguate. The Fi weakness is explained by an "ethics post-training pins Fi high" hypothesis (Jung 1971, p. 725) but this is **never tested directly** — e.g., no comparison with a base (non-instruct) checkpoint, no measurement of refusal/RLHF layers. The PCA "rational vs irrational" finding depends on the top-2 components explaining only ~74% variance; the remaining structure is unexamined, and 8 vectors projected to 2D is a fragile basis for a strong psychological-theory-alignment claim. Finally, evaluation is **entirely self-report questionnaire-based**: there is no downstream behavioral benchmark (e.g., does a high-Se-steered model actually produce more sensory-grounded text under independent metrics?) to validate that higher Jungian scores correspond to genuinely different behavior rather than just different Likert outputs. The k=10 cluster size for difference-in-means is also small and could make the extracted directions sensitive to outlier characters.

## Strengths

- Genuinely novel framing: Jungian cognitive **processes** vs. Big Five **traits** is a more natural fit for human–LLM interaction, where information-perception and decision-making dynamics matter more than static personality descriptions.
- Comprehensive multi-axis analysis (single-dim control, layer-wise localization, geometric structure, multi-dim non-linearity) within a single coherent framework.
- NarrationDB is a reusable, theory-grounded asset that fills 8-D personality space — valuable for future persona/character research beyond this paper.
- Honest reporting of failure (Fi weakness) with a falsifiable hypothesis, and quantitative rigor on the multi-dim backtracking.
- Clean reproducibility plan and open evaluation protocol.

## Limitations

- **Single-model study** (Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct only); cross-family/cross-size generalization is unverified.
- **Fi steering fails** and the proposed cause (RLHF pinning Fi) is hypothesized but not empirically isolated.
- **Questionnaire-only evaluation**: no behavioral downstream validation that score changes map to real persona shifts.
- **Jungian theory itself is contested** in modern psychology (lower psychometric standing than Big Five/Five-Factor Model); grounding LLM findings in it carries interpretive risk.
- **Small clusters (k=10)** for difference-in-means and only **12 multi-dim combinations** tested; residual statistics are descriptive, not theoretically bounded.
- **Synthetic data dependency**: narrations are Llama-3.1-70B-generated, so steering signals may partly reflect generator biases rather than "pure" Jungian constructs.

## Relevance

对激活引导（activation steering）、机制可解释性（mechanistic interpretability）与 LLM 人格控制方向高度相关。它把"人格向量"研究从 Big Five 单维特质扩展到八维认知过程，提供了 (1) 一套可复用的角色化人格数据集 NarrationDB，(2) 单维/多维引导的完整方法学模板，(3) 中间层定位、PCA 几何、最小二乘残差三类可借鉴的诊断工具。对研究者而言，它在人格引导、角色扮演代理、AI 安全中的"可控人格工程"以及 representation engineering 之间架起了桥梁；对工业界， NarrationDB 与可控行为调节方法可直接服务于可定制人格助手与角色一致性长对话系统。

## Questions for Further Exploration

1. The middle-layer concentration (7–12) and the rational/irrational PCA split — do they survive in Llama-3.1-70B, Llama-3.1-405B, Qwen3, Gemma-3, or Mistral, or are they artifacts of Llama-3.1-8B's specific training?
2. The Fi weakness is attributed to ethics/RLHF post-training. Does Fi steering recover on the **base (non-instruct)** Llama-3.1-8B model, or on a model with refusal-direction ablated?
3. Can the questionnaire-based score changes be validated by an **independent behavioral metric** (e.g., lexical/syntactic features of generations, human evaluations, or a held-out persona benchmark)?
4. The residual (0.23–0.65) shows multi-dim directions are not linear combos of single directions — but what *does* span the effective multi-dim subspace? Is there a small set of basis vectors (e.g., from SAE features or PCA of all pairwise combinations) that recovers them with low residual?
5. The 80% instruction-following threshold is applied uniformly — does the trade-off between controllability and capability degrade non-uniformly across functions (e.g., does high-Se steering break coherence before high-Te)?
6. Could the pivot-score multi-dim extraction be extended to 3+ functions (e.g., a full MBTI-style stack), and does the residual grow combinatorially?
7. NarrationDB characters come from fiction — how transferable are the extracted directions to **real-user personas** or to **multi-turn agentic settings** where the persona must persist?
