> **一句话总结：** 作者证明确定性 KV-cache 驱逐无法在线估计自身造成的注意力输出误差（不可识别性定理），转而采用 Poisson 采样尾部 + Hájek logit 偏移得到可计算的"误差证书"（0.97 经验覆盖率），虽在失败预测上输给输出置信度，但在归因（区分缓存诱发 vs 固有失败，AUC 0.73–0.75）和重计算调度上胜出。

> **TL;DR:** Proving that deterministic KV-cache eviction (e.g., H2O/SnapKV top-k) is structurally unable to self-estimate its attention-output error, the author introduces Poisson-sampled tail eviction with a Hájek logit offset that yields a per-step error certificate (0.97 empirical coverage on Qwen2.5-1.5B replay, Spearman 0.94 with true error, no accuracy tax vs top-k); on pre-registered LongBench studies at 6k/16k contexts the certificate loses to output log-probability on failure prediction (0.56 vs 0.73–0.80 AUC) but wins on attribution of eviction-induced vs inherent failures (AUC 0.73–0.75 vs 0.47–0.54) and on certificate-gated recomputation scheduling at 12.5%/25% streaming budgets.

## 核心贡献

- **不可识别性定理（Theorem 1）**：对于任何值无关的确定性 KV-cache 驱逐机制，没有任何基于其在线信息的估计器能一致估计其引入的注意力输出误差；构造性证明显示，在被驱逐值上做置换/旋转可以让真实误差变化 64 倍，而所有保留集统计量位级一致。
- **Poisson + Hájek 证书设计**：尾部用 Poisson 采样（已知包含概率 π_i），在保留 logit 上加 `log(1/π_i)` 即让 softmax 分母完成 Hájek 重归一化；基于 Sen–Yates–Grundy 方差形式构造 O(|tail|) 标量开销的每步误差证书，并给出向时间一致有效性的 e-process 扩展草图。
- **沉默失败现象量化**：在一个"在线自信号面板"上系统测量，发现确定性方法（StreamingLLM/H2O/SnapKV）的保留注意力熵/边界裕度对自身驱逐诱发的失败预测 AUC 在 0.43–0.51（即随机水平），唯一的"局部信号"是被驱逐得分质量（最高 0.73），而证书达到 0.77–0.86。
- **两阶段预注册真实任务研究（诚实披露三死三活）**：在 LongBench 6k/16k 上预注册 7 条可证伪声明并附 kill 条件，公开承认 3 条失败——问题感知驱逐在 25–50% 预算下几乎无代价、输出 log-prob 比证书更擅长预测失败、证书门控的预算升级无效；保留下来的核心结论是"归因"而非"预测"。
- **归因与重计算调度**：证书在区分"缓存诱发失败 vs 固有失败"上达到 AUC 0.749/0.727（6k/16k），远超输出置信度的 0.542/0.469；证书门控的重计算在 12.5%/25% 流式预算下比随机/置信度门控更优（per-seed +3.7 点 vs random，CI 排除零）。

## Key Contributions

- **Impossibility theorem (Theorem 1)**: For any value-blind deterministic eviction scheme, no estimator measurable w.r.t. its online information is a consistent estimator of the induced attention-output error. A two-line constructive proof (and a value-aware extension in Remark 1) shows evicted values can be permuted so true error spans 0.014–0.898 (a factor of 64) while every retained-set statistic is bit-identical.
- **Design + certificate layer**: Poisson-sampled tail eviction with inclusion probabilities π_i the algorithm itself chooses; one logit offset `+log(1/π_i)` makes the softmax denominator perform Hájek renormalization; a Sen–Yates–Grundy (single-sum, Poisson) variance estimator over the retained set yields a per-step error certificate at O(|S∩T|) scalar cost per head/step, plus an empirical-Bernstein e-process sketch toward time-uniform validity.
- **Quantified silent-failure phenomenon**: A panel of four online self-signals evaluated on thousands of scored generations shows deterministic schemes' retained-attention entropy and keep-boundary margin sit at chance (AUC 0.43–0.51); evicted score mass reaches at most 0.73; the certificate reaches 0.77–0.86 on the same yardstick.
- **Two-scale pre-registered real-task study with all verdicts disclosed**: Seven falsifiable claims with kill conditions pre-registered before any full shard; three died honestly (question-aware eviction nearly free at 25–50% budgets; output log-prob beats the certificate at predicting failure; certificate-gated 25%→50% escalation adds nothing). What survives is attribution, not prediction.
- **Attribution as an actionable product**: Among failures, the certificate separates eviction-induced from inherent ones at AUC 0.749 (6k, n=7,924 failures, 1,133 induced) and 0.727 (16k, n=11,717, 1,776 induced), against output log-probability's 0.542/0.469. Certificate-gated recomputation at 12.5%/25% streaming budgets beats random and confidence gating (per-seed +3.7 over random, +4.4–4.9 over confidence, CIs excluding zero).

## Methodology

**Setup.** Fix one attention head at decode step t; cache C={(k_i,v_i)}, scores s_i = q_t^T k_i/√d, weights a_i = e^{s_i}, full-cache output y_t = Σ a_i v_i / Σ a_i. Eviction maps the prefill state to a retained set S⊆C; the evicted set E=C\S is deleted permanently. Online information F_t consists of retained pairs, the design (sampling probabilities), query history, and all derived quantities — evicted values are not F_t-measurable.

**Impossibility (Theorem 1).** For deterministic value-blind eviction, two cache configurations can generate identical F_t while true errors differ by more than any M. Proof: replace {v_i}_{i∈E} with {v_i + c u}; selection is value-independent so S, scores, retained pairs, and the computed ŷ_t are unchanged, while the full output shifts by c·u·(Σ_{i∈E} a_i / Σ_{i∈C} a_i), which grows unboundedly in c. Remark 1 extends to finite scalar value summaries via summary-preserving perturbations.

**Randomized design.** Keep a certainty set A (top-score slice + 4 attention sinks + 32 most-recent tokens, π_i=1) and apply independent Bernoulli retention to the tail T = C\A with π_i = clip(m·score_i / Σ_{j∈T} score_j, ε, 1), ε=10^{-6}, where m is the expected tail budget. The retained output is the Hájek estimator ŷ_t = Σ_{i∈S} (a_i/π_i) v_i / Σ_{i∈S} a_i/π_i, implemented by a single logit offset +log(1/π_i).

**Identifiability + variance (Theorem 2).** Under known-design (Assumption 1) and bounded-weights (Assumption 2) conditions, the single-sum variance estimator V̂_t = (1/N̂²) Σ_{i∈S∩T} ((1−π_i)/π_i²) a_i ‖v_i − ŷ_t‖² satisfies E[V̂_t] = Var(e_t^lin) + O(B²/m²), computable from the retained set alone. Poisson sampling is what makes the SYG double sum collapse to a single sum.

**Certificate.** Empirical-Bernstein radius r_t = √(2V̂_t log(1/δ) + b̂_t log(1/δ)) / (‖ŷ_t‖ + ε₀), at δ=0.1, computed on every fourth head of every layer during the first six decode steps, averaged then maximized. Anytime extension via empirical-Bernstein e-process + Ville's inequality + arithmetic averaging of e-values across dependent heads is sketched (Remark 2), not proven; the deployed certificate rests on measured coverage.

**Experimental harness.** Offline-replay harness prefills once; importance scores from accumulated attention (H2O-style), observation window (SnapKV-style), or question-position queries (aware); each arm generates greedily with per-step signal instrumentation. Certificate compared on internal validity (Qwen2.5-1.5B replay, 12,096 cells at 12.5/25/50%), synthetic needle retrieval, four RULER-style tasks across four base models (640 generations per cell), and two LongBench scales (6k: ~74,000 generations; 16k with MuSiQue/NarrativeQA added). Pre-registration files timestamped before any full shard. All statistics use Mann–Whitney AUC with 95% cluster bootstrap resampling examples.

## Results & Highlights

- **Internal validity (Qwen2.5-1.5B replay, 12,096 cells)**: Certificate coverage of realized error at δ=0.1 is 96.9% / 97.2% / 97.7% at 12.5% / 25% / 50% budgets (pass line 0.85); Spearman correlation between certificate and true error is 0.943 / 0.965 / 0.979 (pass line 0.3). Median relative error at 25% budget is 0.0317 for Poisson+Hájek vs 0.0447 for top-k vs 0.2386 for uniform sampling — no accuracy tax, in fact a gain.
- **Constructive impossibility in a real model**: Six random permutations of evicted values realize Theorem 1 in Qwen2.5-1.5B — true top-k output error ranges 0.014 to 0.898 (factor of 64) while every retained-set statistic is bit-identical across all six worlds; the certificate covers the realized error in all permutations.
- **Synthetic needle retrieval (Qwen2.5-1.5B, 252 runs)**: Certificate predicts own failure at AUC 0.812 (n=192, z=10.2) with median 0.99 on failures vs 0.57 on successes; H2O's retained entropy sits at AUC 0.405 (z=−1.15, statistically chance).
- **Four-task RULER-style benchmark (640 generations/cell, 16/16 cells)**: Certificate–failure AUC positive in 16 of 16 cells, mean 0.836, range 0.65–0.97, each 95% CI excluding 0.5; thresholding yields risk–coverage knob of error 0.229 at 30% coverage vs 0.575 at full coverage.
- **R1 killed — failure prediction dies (LongBench)**: Pooled certificate-to-own-failure AUC is 0.555 at 6k and 0.572 at 16k, below the pre-registered 0.6 kill line; output log-probability beats it everywhere (overall failure 0.73–0.80 vs 0.56–0.57; induced failure 0.806 vs 0.768 at 16k, paired diff −0.038, 95% CI [−0.065, −0.010]).
- **R2 survived — silent failure panel**: Deterministic entropy/margin at AUC 0.43–0.51; evicted mass at 0.59–0.73 (partial signal); certificate at 0.77 (two-scale pooled) and 0.855 at 6k (lower CI 0.84) on induced failures — leading by ~10 points.
- **Attribution survives (Section 6.4)**: Among failures, certificate separates eviction-induced vs inherent at AUC 0.749 (6k) / 0.727 (16k); output log-probability at 0.542 / 0.469 (chance or below). The asymmetry is structural.
- **Recomputation scheduling (Table 4, 16k, 25% re-run rate)**: At 12.5% budget, certificate gating reaches accuracy 0.33 vs 0.29 random vs 0.28–0.29 confidence gating (per-seed +3.7 over random, 95% CI [+2.3, +5.0]; +4.4 to +4.9 over confidence). At 25% budget, +1.9 to +2.2 over random (CIs excluding zero).
- **Damage regime is streaming (Figure 1, 16k)**: Question-aware eviction is nearly free — eviction-induced failure rate pooled over 4 tasks at 25% budget is 6.2% aware vs 40–58% streaming; S1 rules out truncation artifact (4.0% at 16k/25% vs 5.6% at 6k/25%).

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty (high)**: The framing is genuinely new — most KV-cache work competes on the importance *score* (H2O, SnapKV, PyramidKV, Ada-KV, CAOTE); this paper asks the orthogonal question of whether the system can *know* the cost of eviction on the current query. Connecting permanent eviction to classical design-based survey inference (Horvitz–Thompson, Hájek, Sen–Yates–Grundy) and modern anytime-valid inference (e-processes, empirical-Bernstein betting) is a non-obvious bridge. The impossibility theorem (Theorem 1) is short (two-line constructive proof) but structurally clean and extends to value-aware summaries (Remark 1), which closes off the obvious escape route used by CAOTE/VASE-style methods.
- **Rigor (high)**: Internal validity is established with pre-registered pass lines (coverage ≥0.85, correlation ≥0.3) at exactly-computable ground truth on 12,096 cells. Theorem 2 honestly states its O(B²/m²) remainder rather than hiding it. Statistics use Mann–Whitney AUC with cluster bootstrap over examples (so repeated measurements of one example don't inflate significance), paired comparisons on shared examples, and per-model AUC tables (Table 2) to flag the conservativeness of pooled values.
- **Reproducibility (high)**: All models and datasets public, full logs and scripts promised for release, GPU-hours modest (~70), pre-registration files timestamped. Implementation constants disclosed (ε=10^{-6}, ε₀=10^{-9}, 4 sinks, 32 recent, every-4th head, first 6 decode steps, δ=0.1).
- **Significance (moderate-to-high)**: The contribution is conceptual and actionable but narrow. The headline "randomization buys attribution, not prediction" is a negative result dressed constructively — the certificate loses the prediction axis to free output confidence. Its two practical homes (streaming/agent-memory damage monitoring and recomputation scheduling) are real but currently rely on a Python prototype with measured ~2× decode overhead vs an O(|tail|) asymptotic promise; until a fused kernel exists, the engineering case is unproven. The validity–efficiency separation (bad scores inflate variance but cannot bias coverage) is a useful organizing principle for future work.
- **Red flags / honest caveats**: (1) The per-step deployed certificate is NOT a theorem — the vector-to-scalar supermartingale reduction is unsettled, and the anytime extension is a sketch; validity rests entirely on measured coverage. (2) Contexts cap at 16k and models at 8B; the 6k→16k direction moved damage down, but larger models/longer contexts could change the curve. (3) The stream condition is a single-turn proxy for multi-turn agent memory; genuine multi-turn benchmarks are untested. (4) Fixed-size sampling designs (rejective/conditional Poisson) are untested. (5) The head/layer subsample, six-step window, and ε₀ normalizer floor are unablated implementation choices. (6) The Section 6.4 recomputation analysis and per-seed replication are explicitly flagged as post hoc, not pre-registered. (7) The graph-network Remark 3 / merging prediction of Section 3 is stated but untested.

## Strengths

- A clean, defensible impossibility theorem that constrains an entire class of methods rather than one baseline.
- Survey-sampling and anytime-valid inference machinery adapted to a real systems problem with minimal implementation cost (one scalar per retained tail token).
- Pre-registration with kill conditions and honest disclosure of three killed claims — rare and exemplary scientific conduct.
- Comprehensive self-signal panel that empirically operationalizes "silent failure" with confidence intervals, closing the question rather than gesturing at it.
- Practical regime guidance (aware prefill is free at 25–50%; gate answer-level trust on output confidence; certificate matters only for streaming/agent-memory).

## Limitations

- Deployed per-step certificate rests on measured coverage, not a settled theorem; the anytime (time-uniform) extension is a sketch with two open ends.
- Evaluated only up to 16k contexts and 8B parameters; the 6k→16k direction was favorable but scaling is unverified.
- Stream condition is a single-turn proxy for genuine multi-turn agent memory; agent benchmarks untested.
- The Hájek correction's task-level benefit collapses outside the engineered synthetic regime (multi-needle 25% tax of 39–44 points on one cell type is the exception, near-zero elsewhere).
- Certificate-gated escalation (R4) and budget reallocation (post hoc recomputation analysis) — only the latter works, and it is not pre-registered.
- Measured ~2× decode overhead in the Python prototype vs the O(|tail|) theoretical promise; fused kernel not yet built.
- Fixed-size sampling alternatives (rejective, conditional Poisson) untested; head subsample / six-step window / ε₀ floor unablated.

## Relevance

与你的 LLM 推理效率/服务系统研究兴趣（cs.LG、cs.CL、cs.PF、cs.AR）高度相关：它直接处理长上下文 KV-cache 压缩这一服务侧核心瓶颈，并提出首个基于设计推断理论的在线"误差证书"，可用于流式/智能体记忆场景的失败归因与重计算调度。其"归因优于预测、置信度仍主导失败预测"的反直觉结论对评估、监控和资源调度都有借鉴价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the certificate remain calibrated (coverage ≥0.85, AUC ~0.73) on contexts ≥64k and models ≥70B where eviction damage is expected to onset earlier?
- How does the attribution result hold on genuine multi-turn agent benchmarks (e.g., SWE-bench-style traces) where the stream condition is structurally native rather than a single-turn proxy?
- Can a fused kernel close the 2× decode overhead gap, and does the certificate then become net-positive for latency-bounded serving?
- The merging prediction (Section 3.4: merging error should scale with within-stratum dispersion of evicted values, not evicted attention mass) is stated but untested — can it be confirmed and used to redesign CaM/MomentKV?
- Fixed-size sampling designs (rejective, conditional Poisson) keep budgets exact at the cost of non-factoring joint inclusion probabilities — do they tighten the certificate without losing the single-sum variance?
- Can the certificate be combined with output confidence into a single triage signal (confidence for prediction, certificate for attribution) that dominates either alone on a production SLA?
- The vector-to-scalar supermartingale reduction for the anytime extension is unsettled — what is the tightest valid boundary, and does it permit valid uniform-in-T guarantees in practice?
