> **一句话总结：** 作者在 Qwen3-14B 上通过"分阶段 latent-thought 课程 + GRPO 强化学习"训练了一个国际象棋模型，合法性从 48% 单调升至 61%、虚假将杀被完全消除；通过在 RL 前后同一模型上运行六条件因果干预套件，证明 RL 改善的是权重对扰动的鲁棒性（zero 干预下合法性从 1% 升至 9%），而非让模型在推理时"读"自己的隐式思维向量——latent thought 主要在训练期塑造权重，而非充当被主动查询的内部草稿纸。

> **TL;DR:** On a chess-playing Qwen3-14B trained with a staged latent-thought curriculum then GRPO, legal-move rate climbs from a 48% pre-RL baseline to 61% (vs. 52% for explicit-CoT RL) and checkmate confabulation is eliminated, on a frozen 100-position Stockfish-verified harness. A six-condition causal intervention suite run on the *same* model before and after RL shows neither checkpoint relies on thought content (substituting/noising the 4 thought vectors costs nothing), while only exact-zero corruption causes collapse — and RL significantly widens robustness under that worst case (1% retained legality pre-RL vs. 9% post-RL, Bonferroni-corrected CI [0.031, 0.263]). The paper concludes RL reshapes weights (concentrated 68.9% in MLP, gate-proj alone 34.8%) rather than teaching inference-time consultation of a latent scratchpad, pushing back against latent reasoning's founding assumption in a domain (chess) where the latent+RL recipe was reported to fail.

## 核心贡献

- **行为层面发现（Behavioral finding）**：在固定的 100 题国际象棋测试集上，分阶段 latent-thought 课程 + GRPO 把合法走子率从 48%（Stage-2 韧体基线）单调提升至 61%，超过显式 CoT+RL 基线（GRPO v2 的 52%）。准确率（与 Stockfish depth-12 最佳走法精确匹配）始终维持在 9–10%——即 RL 只改善合法性与 confabulation，并不提升棋力，复现了 Hwang et al. 报告的 chess-RLVR 天花板。
- **机理层面发现（Mechanistic finding）**：首次在 RL 前后对同一 checkpoint 运行相同的六条件因果干预套件（Substitute / Noise / Ablate / Lenmatch-Ablate / Zero）。内容替换/加噪几乎不改变行为；只有精确置零向量造成崩溃，且 RL 显著提高了该最坏情形下的鲁棒性比例（retention gap +0.134，95% CI [0.050, 0.231]，Bonferroni 校正后 CI [0.031, 0.263] 仍排除 0）。
- **模板化 confabulation（Templated Confabulation）**：虚假将杀解释不是普通的"棋面误读"，而是高度刻板的模板——跨棋面文本相似度 0.68，而真实走法解释仅 0.27，证据表明这是被记忆下来的固定模板。
- **Confabulation Cutoff**：RL 把虚假将杀从 28（SFT）→19（Stage-2）→0（两个独立 RL 配方：显式 GRPO v2 和 latent Rung-3），且无需专门的反幻觉奖励——gated legality reward 的副作用就完成了这一消除。
- **权重空间定位（Weight-space localization）**：通过逐 (layer, projection) 重构 LoRA 的 ΔW，发现 RL 诱导的权重变化集中在 MLP 投影（68.9%），其中 gate_proj 一个分支就占 34.8%；与独立训练的显式 Rung-1 ΔW 的余弦相似度为 +0.034，且呈深度依赖（前 27 层 +0.052，最后 13 层 ≈ −0.011）。

## Key Contributions

- **Behavioral finding.** A staged latent-thought curriculum followed by GRPO lifts legal-move rate monotonically to 61% on a frozen 100-position harness (from a 48% pre-RL Stage-2 baseline), beating the explicit-CoT+RL baseline (GRPO v2, 52%). Move-quality accuracy stays flat at 9–10% across every checkpoint, replicating the previously reported chess-RLVR ceiling (Hwang et al.) via an independently built pipeline.
- **Mechanistic finding (the paper's headline).** A six-condition causal battery run identically before and after RL on the *same* model shows neither checkpoint relies on thought content. Only exact-zero corruption causes collapse, and RL significantly widens the proportional robustness gap under that worst case (Stage-2 retains 1% legality vs. Rung-3's 9%, McNemar p < 0.001 each; retention-ratio bootstrap 95% CI [0.050, 0.231], surviving Bonferroni correction across the family of five conditions, CI [0.031, 0.263]).
- **Templated Confabulation.** False-checkmate explanations are stereotyped: 0.68 cross-board text similarity (difflib SequenceMatcher) vs. 0.27 for real, distinct move explanations under the same five-section template — evidence of a fixed, learned script triggered by surface features, not board-specific reasoning gone wrong.
- **The Confabulation Cutoff.** RL eliminates checkmate confabulation entirely: 28 → 19 → 0 across the progression, and independently to 0 in the explicit-CoT GRPO v2 run, with no dedicated anti-hallucination term — the gated legality reward alone removes this failure mode as a side effect.
- **Weight-space localization.** Reconstructing ΔW at all 280 (layer, projection) LoRA slots shows the RL change is concentrated, not diffuse: 68.9% in MLP projections (gate_proj alone = 34.8%), 31.1% in attention. The independently trained explicit-reasoning Rung-1 ΔW shows a small but consistently positive structural overlap (whole-adapter cosine +0.034; +0.052 over layers 0–26, −0.011 over layers 27–39), evidence the effect is partly generic to the reward and partly specific to depth.

## Methodology

The paper builds a four-rung training ladder on **Qwen3-14B** with **LoRA (rank 16)**, all scored on a frozen 100-position harness (seed 3407), greedy decoding, batch-size-one for latent checkpoints:

1. **SFT baseline.** Imitation on 12,002 FEN–best-move–explanation triples, each explanation following a fixed 5-section scaffold ([KING SAFETY], [CHECKS], [CAPTURES & TRADES], [THREATS], [IMPROVEMENT]). Reaches 38% legal, 28 false-checkmate claims.
2. **Rung 1 (explicit reasoning + GRPO).** GRPO on top of SFT with reasoning in words. The reward *gates* rather than weights: any illegal/unparseable/false-checkmate response scores −1.0 and Stockfish is never called; a correct checkmate scores +1.0; any other legal move scores `exp(−cp_loss/120)`. The gated design is explicitly contrasted with an earlier summed-term version that Goodharted. G=4, lr 5e-6, KL 0.02, 500 steps → 52% legal, 0 false mates.
3. **Stage-2 (staged latent curriculum).** Starting from SFT, replace one written section at a time with 2 continuous-thought positions (Coconut-style hidden-state feedback), optimizer reset at each stage boundary, pure imitation. A pre-registered stopping rule halts at stage 3 (stage-3 legality dropped to 19%, just under the 20% threshold). Stage-2 (4 thoughts) is the pre-RL latent checkpoint used throughout: 48% legal, 19 false mates.
4. **Rung 3 (latent GRPO).** GRPO applied to Stage-2 with reward unchanged from Rung 1; only the decoded move is scored, never the thoughts. KL reference = Stage-2 itself (frozen second copy), not the base model. lr 2e-6 (5e-6 destabilized), G=4, KL 0.02, 150 steps → 61% legal, 0 false mates.

**Causal intervention suite (six conditions on the 4 thought positions):** Baseline (real thoughts); Substitute (single fixed averaged vector T* at all 4 positions); Noise (independent random directions rescaled to T*'s norm); Ablate (remove the 4 positions entirely); Lenmatch-Ablate (replace with the model's own pad-token embedding, attention unmasked, to control for length); Zero (exact-zero vector). Run identically on Stage-2 and Rung-3.

**J-lens analysis.** A Jacobian-lens interpretability probe, calibrated on ordinary written chess reasoning (layer 28 recovers the upcoming piece ~94% vs. ~0% for plain logit lens, a ~90-point gap), then pointed at the latent thoughts to test whether they carry board-specific content.

**Weight localization.** ΔW reconstructed per-slot as `scaling · (B_new A_new − B_old A_old)`; per-projection Frobenius norms summed; cross-recipe cosine similarity vs. independently trained Rung-1 ΔW.

**Experiment 2 (Gumbel control).** A matched-budget (150 step) reparameterization giving RL an actual gradient into thought content (top-20 masked Gumbel-Softmax, τ=4, straight-through), to test whether a working gradient there lets RL beat the deterministic recipe.

## Results & Highlights

- **Headline legal-move rate (Table 1):** SFT 38% → GRPO v2 (explicit+RL) 52% → Stage-2 (latent, no RL) 48% → **Rung-3 (latent+RL) 61%**. Only the latent-curriculum-then-RL recipe reaches the highest legality; accuracy stays flat at 9–10% across all four.
- **False-checkmate elimination (the Confabulation Cutoff):** 28 (SFT) → 0 (GRPO v2) and 19 (Stage-2) → 0 (Rung-3), under two independent training recipes — with no dedicated anti-hallucination term. The 100-position harness contains zero genuine checkmate positions (separately audited), so every checkmate declaration in the paper is necessarily false.
- **Causal battery (Table 2):** content-preserving conditions cost nothing on either checkpoint — Stage-2: Baseline 48% / Substitute 48% / Noise 48%; Rung-3: 58% / 58% / 60%. Only **Zero collapses**: **Stage-2 1% vs. Rung-3 9% legality retained** (McNemar p < 0.001 each; retention-ratio gap +0.134, bootstrap 95% CI [0.050, 0.231], surviving Bonferroni correction [0.031, 0.263]). The four milder conditions trend in the predicted severity order (Substitute +0.000, Noise +0.034, Ablate +0.066, Lenmatch-Ablate +0.101) but do not independently reach significance at n=100.
- **Pairwise exact-move churn (Table 5):** Baseline-vs-Noise agreement is 91% on Rung-3 but only 82% on Stage-2 — RL locks in *which* move gets played, not just the aggregate pass rate. Baseline-vs-Zero agreement is 14% (after correcting an earlier .dropna() bug that had reported 87.5%).
- **J-lens (§4.5):** cross-board cosine similarity of the raw thought vectors is **≈0.99** at Stage-2 and identical (0.992) at Rung-3 — a fixed, board-invariant scaffold complete by the end of the latent curriculum, *before* RL. Base model 0.874, SFT 0.812 (with the caveat that neither was trained on the feedback mechanism).
- **Weight localization (Table 3):** 68.9% of Rung-3's ΔW Frobenius norm is in MLP projections, **gate_proj alone = 34.8%** (more than any other single projection); attention accounts for 31.1%. Cross-recipe cosine similarity with the independently trained Rung-1 ΔW is +0.034 whole-adapter (small but consistently positive at rank 16 in a 5120-dim space), depth-split: +0.052 over layers 0–26, −0.011 over layers 27–39.
- **Gumbel control (Table 4):** legality climbs 44% → 50% over 150 steps, *below* Stage-2's 48% territory and well below Rung-3's 61%; false-mate plateaus at 15 (never reaches zero) — giving RL a working gradient into thought content did not produce a better policy than the deterministic recipe whose gradient never reaches the thoughts.

## Authors & Credibility

- **Sole author: Ishan S. Kshirsagar**, listed as Independent Researcher. No institutional affiliation.
- The work is technically careful and unusually self-critical: it explicitly reports the unnormalized raw-count framing that *disagrees* with its headline retention-ratio claim (under Zero, Stage-2 loses 47 boards vs. Rung-3's 49, a non-significant difference in the opposite direction, Wilcoxon p = 0.66), discloses a recovered-from-crash training history gap, and corrects a previously buggy pairwise-match computation (87.5% → 14%).
- The methodology engages directly with the closest prior art (Switch, arXiv:2606.13106), acknowledging the structurally similar causal-battery shape while quantitatively distinguishing the two papers' conclusions.
- Statistical reporting is detailed: McNemar within-checkpoint tests, 10,000-resample bootstrap CIs with board-level pairing preserved, and Bonferroni family-wise correction across all five non-baseline conditions are all reported in Appendix A.7 (Table 7).
- Reproducibility levers are well-specified: exact Qwen3-14B base, LoRA rank 16, GRPO hyperparameters per run, frozen seed-3407 harness, manual ChatML prompt byte-identical across SFT/GRPO/eval, Stockfish Limit(depth=12, time=10.0, Threads=1, Hash=256MB). Code/data release is not explicitly promised in the body.
- Caveat: every result is from a single training run per checkpoint (no seed variance reported); base model + LoRA-adapter only (no full fine-tuning); chess only.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** High and well-scoped. The central novelty is the *before/after-RL* causal-battery design on the identical model, which the author argues (and a dedicated literature search in §5–6 supports) has not been done before — Switch's battery and content-characterization are reported only on the post-RL checkpoint. The chess domain itself is also genuinely new for this debate, which had been confined to math (MATH-500, GSM8K) and logic. Secondary novelties (Templated Confabulation as a measurable phenomenon; weight-space localization of the RL delta) are real but smaller.
- **Rigor:** Strong on causal methodology — six structurally different interventions rather than one (motivated by Zhang & Nanda's activation-patching methodology comparison), J-lens *calibrated* against known content before its null is trusted, gated reward design explicitly motivated by Goodharting, and Bonferroni correction applied. Honest about the multiple-comparisons tension and the unnormalized-vs-normalized framing disagreement. However, only n=100 evaluation positions, and only one of five non-baseline conditions independently clears significance — the "trends in the predicted direction" claim for the four milder conditions rests on point estimates with overlapping CIs.
- **Reproducibility:** Hyperparameters, prompt, harness seed, and Stockfish settings are fully specified. Single-seed single-run design is the main reproducibility weakness and is openly acknowledged. No explicit code release commitment found in the body.
- **Significance:** Conceptually significant — it directly challenges Coconut's founding "active scratchpad" assumption in a new domain and reframes what latent-reasoning-plus-RL actually does (shapes weights, not inference-time computation). Practically modest: the gain is on legality and confabulation only; chess playing strength (accuracy vs. Stockfish) does not improve, replicating an existing ceiling rather than breaking it.
- **Red flags:** (1) Sole independent author with no institutional affiliation — readers should weight peer review accordingly (this is an arXiv preprint). (2) The headline "RL adds robustness" rests on the *retention-ratio* framing; the raw-board-drop framing goes the other way and is not significant — the paper discloses this but a reader could reasonably dispute which framing is primary. (3) The narrowest possible scope claim ("in this pipeline") is sometimes stated more sweepingly in the abstract/intro. (4) The Gumbel control is acknowledged to be a biased straight-through estimator, leaving the "could a working gradient into thought content help?" question genuinely unresolved. (5) Zero false-checkmate claims is partly an artifact of the harness containing zero real checkmate positions, so "eliminated" means "withheld," not "correctly identified" — disclosed, but worth flagging.

## Strengths

- Genuinely novel experimental design: identical causal battery on the same model before and after RL, the missing comparison in the entire prior latent-reasoning literature.
- Methodologically careful: six-condition battery, J-lens calibration, gated-vs-summed reward ablation, Gumbel control as a falsification attempt.
- Unusually honest reporting of evidence that cuts against the headline claim (raw-count framing, single-seed caveat, Gumbel bias, base-model/SFT cosine-similarity caveat).
- Clean, automatically-checkable domain (chess legality / Stockfish oracle) that sidesteps reward-model confounds.
- Mechanistic claim is given a physical location (ΔW concentration in MLP / gate_proj, depth-split cross-recipe overlap), not just a behavioral signature.

## Limitations

- Single training run per checkpoint, single 14B base model, LoRA-only, chess-only — explicitly acknowledged; no claim about full fine-tuning, frontier scale, or other reasoning domains.
- n=100 evaluation harness; four of five non-baseline conditions do not independently reach significance. The robustness claim leans on a single condition (Zero) that does, plus directional consistency.
- No improvement in actual chess strength (accuracy flat at 9–10%); the result is strictly about legality and confabulation.
- The "RL eliminates confabulation" finding is partly an artifact: the harness has no real checkmate positions, so the model is never tested on correctly identifying one — it learns only to withhold.
- The Gumbel control cannot cleanly separate "thought content does not help" from "this biased optimization path is just harder to train."
- Headline 61% in Table 1 vs. 58% baseline in Table 2 for the *same* Rung-3 adapter — explained as NF4/bf16 dequantization-order variance, but it does mean the causal battery's baseline differs from the headline number.
- Base-model and SFT cosine-similarity figures (0.874, 0.812) may reflect unfamiliarity with the feedback mechanism rather than a representational tendency; the clean comparison is only Stage-2 vs. Rung-3.

## Relevance

对研究 latent reasoning / Coconut / 隐式思维向量机理、以及 RLVR + 可解释性的工作高度相关：本文提供了 chess 域下"同一模型 RL 前后"的因果干预证据，直接挑战了 latent thought 作为推理期 scratchpad 的默认假设，可作为评估 silent-reasoning 是否真在做 inference-time 计算的方法论模板。对追求 chess-LLM 棋力提升的工作而言，它则是一个负面/天花板结果（准确率不升，仅合法性与 confabulation 改善）。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the before/after-RL causal-battery finding replicate in math/logic domains (MATH-500, GSM8K) where Switch and others report opposite conclusions about thought content? Would the J-lens null also appear there?
- Is the retention-ratio framing vs. raw-board-drop framing disagreement a real feature (Rung-3 has more legal boards to lose) or an artifact of the specific 48%→58% baseline jump? How does it behave at matched baselines?
- The ΔW concentration in gate_proj (34.8%) is striking — does an SVD of those layer-wise ΔWs show low-rank structure, and do the implicated layers also dominate the Zero-condition collapse?
- Would scaling the evaluation harness (n=100 → n=1000) lift the four milder conditions to independent significance, or do they remain genuinely sub-threshold?
- The Gumbel control plateaus below the deterministic recipe; would a less-biased gradient (e.g., rebar, exact marginalization over top-k) change the picture, or is the deterministic-recipe advantage robust?
- The harness contains zero real checkmate positions — does Rung-3 correctly identify *actual* checkmates on a separate test set, or has it merely learned to never say "checkmate"?
- Cross-model: does the latent curriculum's collapse into a board-invariant scaffold (cosine ≈0.99) reproduce on other base models (Qwen3-8B, Llama family), or is it Qwen3-14B-specific?
