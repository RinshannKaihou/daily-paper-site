01 · Methodology

11 methods · 2 archs · 2 datasets · 3 analyses

Evaluated 6 reweighting schemes (uniform, invf, cb β=0.9, invl, invp γ=0.1, comf) and 5 loss functions (Focal γ=1, LDAM, Logit-Adjust τ=0.3, Balanced-Softmax, Seesaw) across:

  • Architectures: KPConv (kernel conv) + RandLA-Net (random sampling) — opposite sampling strategies, cross-arch check.
  • DALES: outdoor airborne LiDAR, 8 classes, 641:1 extreme imbalance.
  • S3DIS: indoor scan, 13 classes, 56:1 moderate imbalance.
  • Protocol: single train, 10× test-time voting; per-class IoU / mIoU; 3-seed robustness for uni & best.

Three complementary mechanism tools:

  • Confusion matrices → per-class precision/recall + majority→minority vs minority→majority error split.
  • Decision-boundary variability (Eq.1) → label-flip rate vs. uniform baseline.
  • Loss-landscape topology → filter-normalized weight perturbations (K=20 dirs) + top-10 Hessian eigenvalues (Lanczos).
02 · Results

Severity shapes geometry, not loss form

  • DALES: uniform CE 80.05%, best invp 80.86% (+0.81%); invf collapses to 67.78% (−12.27%).
  • S3DIS: all methods within a 1.93% mIoU band (62.92–64.85%); method choice barely matters.
  • Cross-arch (RandLA-Net): gains 2.4–3.3% — pattern consistent with KPConv (0.8–1.8%).
  • Precision–recall trade-off: invf/BalSoft cut minority→majority errors 5–10× but inflate majority→minority errors ~10×.
  • Topology: DALES = anisotropic narrow basin (dominant λ₁); S3DIS = isotropic flat plateau. Spectra cluster by dataset, not by method.

Takeaway: imbalance severity, not loss formulation, determines landscape geometry — explaining why departing from CE is dangerous at 641:1 but harmless at 56:1.

03 · Strengths / Limitations

+ Strengths

  • First mechanistic (not phenomenological) explanation for CE's competitiveness in 3D.
  • Three orthogonal tools (confusion / boundary / Hessian) — reusable template.
  • Cross-architecture consistency strengthens "data dominates" claim.
  • Honest scope: authors list uncovered archs & missing RandLA mechanism analysis.

− Limitations

  • No voxel (Cylinder3D/SPVCNN) or attention (Point Transformer) archs.
  • Mechanism analysis only on KPConv.
  • Loss-level only — no data-level (oversampling, mixup) or hybrid.
  • Main tables are single-seed; 9/11 methods have no variance.
  • Only 2 datasets; no SemanticKITTI. Beam class IoU=0 unexplained.
  • No causal/intervention test of the narrow-basin hypothesis.
04 · Open questions

Where the topology claim breaks next

  1. Does the "implicit geometric balance" hypothesis hold on voxel / attention architectures, or does global attention break neighbourhood aggregation?
  2. On SemanticKITTI (19 classes, distance-dependent imbalance), do the DALES/S3DIS conclusions extrapolate?
  3. Do data-level + loss-level combinations break the precision–recall trade-off, or stay topology-bound?
  4. Can a causal intervention (fixed init, LR schedule, class-balanced batch sampling) confirm "trajectory leaves narrow basin → mIoU drops"?
  5. Why does Beam (IoU=0) fail on S3DIS for every method — samples, labels, or geometric encoding?
  6. Would full hyperparameter sweeps (γ, margin, τ) break the 3.3% ceiling, or is it topology-capped?
