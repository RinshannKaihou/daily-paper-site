Why this matters

First online error certificate for KV-cache eviction — enabling failure attribution and recomputation scheduling in streaming / agent-memory serving.

HONEST CAVEAT
Critical edge

The per-step certificate rests on measured coverage, not a theorem — and it loses failure prediction to free output log-probability (0.56 vs 0.73–0.80 AUC).

01Methodology

From impossibility to a randomized, identifiable certificate

Theorem 1 (impossibility). For any value-blind deterministic eviction scheme (H2O / SnapKV top-k), no estimator built from its online information can consistently estimate the attention-output error it induces. A two-line proof perturbs evicted values by c·u: selection is value-independent so every retained-set statistic stays bit-identical, while the true output error shifts by an unbounded c. Six real permutations in Qwen2.5-1.5B realize this — true error spans 0.014–0.898 (64×) across worlds the scheme cannot tell apart.

The fix is to make the design itself informative: Poisson-sample the tail with known inclusion probabilities πi, then a single logit offset +log(1/πi) makes the softmax denominator perform Hájek renormalization. Poisson sampling is precisely what collapses the Sen–Yates–Grundy double sum into a single-sum variance estimator computable from the retained set alone.

03Strengths

  • Clean impossibility theorem. Theorem 1 constrains an entire class of methods, not one baseline — and Remark 1 closes the value-aware-summary escape route.
  • Minimal-cost bridge. Classical survey inference (Hájek, Sen–Yates–Grundy) adapted to a real systems problem at one scalar per retained tail token.
  • Exemplary pre-registration. Seven falsifiable claims with kill conditions, timestamped before any full shard; three killed claims disclosed prominently.
  • Honest baselining. Concedes the prediction axis to output log-probability — a baseline the KV-compression literature never reports.
  • Regime guidance. Actionable rule: aware prefill is free at 25–50%; gate trust on output confidence; certificate matters only for streaming/agent memory.

04Limitations

  • No theorem for the deployed certificate. Vector-to-scalar supermartingale reduction is unsettled; the anytime extension is only a sketch. Validity rests on measured coverage.
  • Narrow scale. Contexts cap at 16k, models at 8B; the favorable 6k→16k direction may not extend.
  • Single-turn proxy. The "stream" condition is a one-turn stand-in for genuine multi-turn agent memory; agent benchmarks untested.
  • Engineering gap. ~2× decode overhead in the Python prototype vs the O(|tail|) promise; no fused kernel yet.
  • Post-hoc wins. The recomputation result that survives is flagged post hoc, not pre-registered; fixed-size sampling designs and ablations are untested.

05Open questions for further work

Q1Does the certificate stay calibrated (coverage ≥0.85, AUC ~0.73) at ≥64k contexts and ≥70B models, where eviction damage is expected to onset earlier?
Q2Does attribution hold on genuine multi-turn agent benchmarks (SWE-bench-style traces) where the stream condition is structurally native, not a single-turn proxy?
Q3Can a fused kernel close the 2× decode-overhead gap and make the certificate net-positive for latency-bounded serving?
Q4Can confidence + certificate fuse into one triage signal — confidence for prediction, certificate for attribution — that dominates either alone on a production SLA?
