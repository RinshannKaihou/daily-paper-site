Why This Matters

Small-scale pretraining hides a severe NIAH seed lottery (14–98%) with zero perplexity signal — a one-line frequency swap is cheap insurance for reliable retrieval.

Critical Edge

This is explicitly a within-training-window reliability fix, not a long-context method — past the training length every variant collapses to 0% retrieval through the same critical transition.

01

The construction

Standard RoPE scatters long-range phase; Möbius RoPE fixes a traversal length N and uses half-integer harmonics so one full traversal flips sign — a topological boundary condition, not a frequency-scaling trick.

Closed-form position-only attention kernel (Dirichlet) θi = π(2i+1)/N
c(m) = (1/P)·Σ cos(m·θi) = sin(Dπm/N) / [D·sin(πm/N)]
R(N) = −Idh ,  R(2N) = +I
  • Hybrid (main): first ⌈ρH⌉ heads (ρ=0.25) get Möbius, rest stay RoPE. Zero params, zero FLOPs.
  • Dirichlet predictions verified to ~10⁻⁶: local lobe, silent bulk, antipodal dipole, bounded 2N-periodic extrapolation.
  • Pretrain: decoder-only, FineWeb-Edu, seq 2048, 2.0B tokens, AdamW lr 6e-4, bf16, H100.
  • Two scales: 160M (12L / 12H) and 410M (24L / 16H), dh=64. 48 pretrained models, six 160M arms + three 410M arms.
  • Eval: val ppl @2048 & extrapol 4096/8192; NIAH passkey, 32×5=160 trials/length/seed, L∈{128…8192}; plus KV-lookup & multi-needle.
  • Controls: aperiodic band, periodic +1 ("cylinder"), NoPE — to separate band placement from boundary class.
02

Results & the dose–response

The headline is reliability, not capability: mean accuracy barely moves, but the worst-case seed lifts from 14% to 86%. The mechanism is isolated by a clean three-arm boundary-condition ladder.

Boundary conditionHolonomyVariance ratioVerdict
aperiodic band (same low-freq band) R(N) ≠ ±I 1.0× no effect
periodic +1 ("cylinder") R(N) = +I 3.1× weak
anti-periodic −1 ("Möbius") R(N) = −I 30.8× active ingredient
  • Retrieval (160M, n=6, L=512): hybrid 90.3 ± 5.7% vs standard 63.3 ± 31.4%. Worst-seed floor 14% → 86%.
  • Perplexity flat: 29.66 ± 0.09 vs 29.72 ± 0.24 (p=0.63); 410M hybrid slightly better (25.16 vs 25.26).
  • Causal ablation (rope-swap): freeze weights, swap Möbius→standard; NIAH@512 90.3% → 41.7%, damage on far needles (−55pp at depth 0.1).
  • Replicates at 410M: NIAH@2048 SD 6.5% vs 16.5%, Levene p=0.040.
  • NoPE trade-off: most reliable short-range (95.2%) but pays 13% ppl tax & extrapolates worst.
  • Capacity × allocation: single-scale hybrid wins at 160M; multi-scale ladder wins extrapolation at 410M (ppl@4096 35.7 vs 38.7, p=0.006).
03

Strengths & limitations

Strengths

  • Zero-cost intervention: swap cos/sin constants on 25% of heads — no params, no FLOPs, no throughput loss (log-log slope 1.002).
  • Theory + numerics: Dirichlet kernel predictions verified to 10⁻⁶; N=10,000 single layer transmits a sign flip end-to-end (cos=−0.96).
  • Self-limited claims: author restricts "reliability" to single-needle, where the dipole actually operates.
  • Statistical honesty: 9 threats-to-validity listed; per-seed values public; NIAH harness filler-packing bug self-disclosed & re-evaluated.

Limitations

  • Scale & tokens are small: max 405M params / 2B tokens. Whether the seed lottery survives at tens-of-B scale is unverified.
  • Task range narrow: variance gain only on single-needle passkey; KV-lookup & multi-needle null; no QA, no MMLU/HellaSwag.
  • Underpowered stats: at n=6 no mean-difference hits p<0.05; Bonferroni erases single-point significance. 30.8× partly ceiling (logit 3.4×).
  • Hyper-params fixed a priori: ρ=0.25, head placement, N=train-length all unscanned.
04

Questions for further exploration

  1. Does the standard-RoPE seed lottery persist at 1B–10B params / 50–200B tokens, or fade as baseline retrieval improves (410M hint: 63%→88%)?
  2. What is the optimal ρ, and should Möbius heads cluster near retrieval layers rather than the first ⌈ρH⌉ of every layer?
  3. Can the dipole stack with length-extrapolation methods (YaRN / LongRoPE), or do the spurious long-range lobes past Nmax fight extrapolation?
  4. Are there further holonomy classes (multi-connected, tunable twist) yielding a finer dose–response — and does anti-periodicity survive under GQA / sliding-window attention?
  5. Do KV-lookup and multi-needle recover the variance signature once models are well above the competence floor, or is the dipole fundamentally single-needle?
