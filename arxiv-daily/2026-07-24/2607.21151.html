V-DEAL: Diagnosing Video Safety De-Calibration as an Understanding–Refusal Coupling Failure

Zhetong Zhang · Honghao Fu · Miao Xu · Yiwei Wang · Yujun Cai  —  Univ. of Queensland · UC Merced

arxiv.org/abs/2607.21151 ↗
Key Metric · Average ASR
48.33% → 0.80%

A training-free prompt-injection realignment (explicit visual-evidence summary + in-context demonstrations) cuts average attack-success rate by 48.24 pp — on par with SEA fine-tuning baselines, with zero parameter updates.

6 Video LLMs · 2,510 VH-BQ samples · 0-shot & 8-shot
Why This Matters

Exposes a hidden failure mode: models that correctly see harm still won't refuse — a blind spot in every current video-safety eval.

Eval scope: Qwen2/2.5-VL · InternVideo2.5 · InternVL3.5 · MiniCPM-o/V · 5,020 videos
Critical Edge

No causal mechanism experiments. The chain visual evidence → refusal direction → refusal rests on indirect prompt-intervention evidence — no activation patching or ablation directly proves it.

Methodology

02
(a) Behaviour
Four controlled attack conditions decouple text-harm from visual evidence: T-BQ, T-HQ, VH-BQ, VH-HQ. LLM-as-Judge labels each output HARMFUL / REFUSAL / SAFE → ASR, Refusal Rate, Safe Rate.
(b) Understanding
Structured 4-attribute tags (Acc / Macro-F1) + free-form summaries (judged vs. a stronger reference model) to rule out "couldn't understand" as the cause.
(c) Representation
Per-layer refusal direction wℓ estimated from external XSTest / MM-SafetyBench; hidden states projected onto it → scalar refusal score (ROC-AUC-selected top-m layers).
(d) Realignment
Prompt asks the model to restate harmful visual elements in text before the safety decision; 0-shot = system prompt only, 8-shot adds 8 GPT-4.1 demonstrations. Weights & decoding hyper-params frozen.

Coverage: 6 Video LLMs · 3 benchmarks · 5,020 videos · 1,250-sample balanced representation subset.

Results & Highlights

03
VH-BQ ASR 48.33%
VH-HQ ASR 35.84%
After fix 0.80%

VH-BQ > VH-HQ in 5 of 6 models — refusal collapses 53.05% → 14.22% when query turns benign.

  • Strong understanding ≠ safe. InternVL3.5 / InternVideo2.5 hit 0.955 / 0.950 tag accuracy yet retain 49.1% / 36.1% VH-BQ ASR.
  • Root cause localized: VH-BQ refusal scores sit below T-HQ / VH-HQ on 4 of 6 models; Qwen-VLs go negative (−0.15 / −0.13).
  • Beats fine-tuning: on Qwen2-VL + full VA-SafetyBench, 0-shot 0.14% / 8-shot 0.24% ASR vs. SEA-SFT 0.34% & SEA-DPO 3.56%.

Strengths & Limitations

04

Strengths

  • Reframes video-safety failure from "can't understand" to understanding–refusal coupling failure, with a three-level evidence chain.
  • Training-free, plug-and-play intervention matches SFT/DPO baselines — near-zero deployment cost.
  • Scale: 6 models × 3 benchmarks × 5,020 videos + 1,250-sample representation subset.
  • Honest head-to-head vs. SEA on matched model + benchmark, not a soft comparison.

Limitations

  • Refusal direction borrowed from text-only safety data (XSTest / MM-SafetyBench) — domain gap remains despite transfer audit.
  • Intervention is sensitive to prompt & demonstration choice; cross-model-family transfer under-tested (self-admitted).
  • No causal experiments (activation patching / ablation) — mechanism stays correlational.
  • R-Score absolute scale is model-relative; "which model is least safe" can't be read off directly.
  • Only open 7–9B models tested; closed-source (GPT-4o, Gemini) de-calibration unverified.

Open Questions for Further Work

05
  1. Does the coupling failure persist in closed models (GPT-4o, Gemini) and at >30B scale — does it grow or shrink with parameters?
  2. Can refusal directions be estimated from in-domain video data to yield cross-model-comparable scores?
  3. Can causal mediation / activation patching directly prove the visual-evidence → refusal-direction → refusal chain?
  4. How robust is the prompt intervention to query rephrasing or chain-of-thought steering by an adaptive attacker?
  5. Does VH-BQ de-calibration strength change in Chinese / multilingual settings — and correlate with pretraining language mix?
  6. If 8-shot demonstrations are self-generated by the target model instead of GPT-4.1, how do efficacy and portability shift?
