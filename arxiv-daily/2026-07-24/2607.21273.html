01Methodology
  • Setup. GRPO on Qwen3-1.7B/4B/8B over ALFWorld; step-independent multi-turn rollout, 30–50 step horizons, group size 4, batch 8×4 = 32 traj/step, validation @ temp 0.4, AdamW lr 1e-6, KL 0.01, λ = 0.1.
  • Prediction-Sufficiency signal. Policy emits a rule-parsed <predict> block over task-agnostic features; verified against the realized next observation (no judge model). Per-step reward is a potential difference that telescopes to a bounded λ term.
  • Theory. Prop. 1 — all-fail-group advantages are λ-invariant under std normalization, so bounded shaping becomes full-scale pressure. Prop. 2 (variance-profile criterion) — danger is set by within-group variance at saturation, not magnitude or density.
  • 9-arm delivery matrix. Prediction content held fixed; consumption mechanism & normalization varied (std / decoupled / mean-only reward, self-report, anchor-QA, baseline, auxiliary loss, shuffled-gold placebo).
  • HiddenRule-Gym. Synthetic POMDP with exactly computable feature coverage C = I(Φ; s) / H(s), separating the coverage × dynamics × capacity axes that ALFWorld conflates.

03Strengths vs. limitations

● Strengths

  • One-flag ablation isolates the cause; no-signal control pins net attribution ≈ 0.
  • Prop. 1–2 genuinely explain the phenomena; advantage fingerprints (±5–7 vs ±0.1) confirmed.
  • Variance-profile criterion reframes risk as within-group variance at saturation — portable, signal-side.
  • Preregistered SHA256 predictions; kept-verbatim ledger incl. a falsified-then-revised timing law.
  • HRG with computable C separates axes real benchmarks conflate.
  • Honest scoping to GRPO-family; deliberate non-testing of critic-based PPO.

● Limitations

  • Single-seed endpoints; ~8.8 pt noise — all comparisons descriptive.
  • Two environments only; collapse-timing law rests on three scale points.
  • Group n=4 collinear with the std pathology; constant-budget ablation pending.
  • Shuffled-gold placebo is leaky (~10% shared initial state); bounds non-content effect from above.
  • Aux-loss gain (69.3 vs 76.0) indistinguishable from regularization / extra-token-budget.
  • Disjoint-vocabulary decisive placebo still running — could rewrite §7.
04Questions for further exploration
  1. Does the collapse generalize to critic-based PPO / learned value baselines, where advantage scale derives from bootstraps rather than a small same-prompt group?
  2. The preregistered ∆acc (progress-style) arm under std normalization is the strongest prospective test of Prop. 2 — does its within-group variance decay fast enough to exit the danger window?
  3. The disjoint-vocabulary placebo is decisive for §7: if env-disjoint CE also gains ~20 pt, the effect is auxiliary-compute regularization, not world-model transfer.
  4. How does the pathology interact with group size (n=4 vs larger, constant budget) — separating std pathology from small-group σ̂ instability?
  5. Can the variance-profile criterion predict a priori the published reward-channel wins (RWML, VAGEN) on the same ALFWorld benchmark?
  6. Does the three-axis failure map transfer quantitatively to ALFWorld, or is computable C a synthetic-only affordance?
