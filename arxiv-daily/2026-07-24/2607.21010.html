01 Methodology Two-stage Stability Analysis Protocol (SAP)

Goal: quantify how much trust a user can place in summarizer S on metric m for document D in genre G. SAP isolates the LLM’s intrinsic decoding randomness by holding prompt and parameters static.

1Generate k=100 summaries/doc — zero-shot, fixed structured prompt & default params.
▸
2Score 3 metrics: BERTScore (semantic) · AlignScore (factual) · SC (mean pairwise cosine).
▸
3Document stability ε = 1 − Δ, where Δ = max pairwise |diff| over k scores.
▸
4Corpus Ē = min{ε} (pessimistic lower bound) + 10,000× bootstrap 95% CI.
▸
5Benchmark via Friedman + Nemenyi CD diagram on win/loss doc ranks.

02 Results >28K summaries · 3 metrics · 3 genres

  • 01Generation-level variance is dramatic. One CNN/DM doc under Llama-3.2-3B yields BERTScore spanning 0.41 → 0.71 across 100 summaries — undercutting the one-summary-per-doc evaluation paradigm.
  • 02Metric stability diverges sharply. CNN/DM Ē (Table 6): BERTScore Gemma3 0.7957 · Qwen 0.7857 · Llama 0.6461 — versus AlignScore all <0.011 (Gemma3 0.0031 · Llama 0.0021 · Qwen 0.0100).
  • 03Ranking holds across genres. Gemma3 ranks #1 on BERTScore in all three (CNN/DM 0.7957 · PubMed 0.8036 · IN-Abs 0.6668) and #1 on AlignScore for PubMed (0.4345) & IN-Abs (0.2725); lowest SC CVc everywhere.
  • 04Win-count dominance (Table 9, of 32 docs): Gemma3 PubMed SC 32/32, AlignScore 26/32; IN-Abs SC 32/32; Llama AlignScore ≈ 0 wins (PubMed 1/32, IN-Abs 0/32).
  • 05Short-text factual fidelity is worst. CNN/DM AlignScore shows the widest bootstrap CI & highest variance; long documents (PubMed / IN-Abs) are markedly more stable.
  • 06Statistically significant. Friedman rejects 3-model equality at 5% for every dataset; Nemenyi CD diagram separates Gemma3 from Llama/Qwen on SC & AlignScore (CNN/DM); Levene rejects variance homogeneity across all docs/metrics/summarizers.

03 Strengths & Limitations what holds up · what doesn’t

Strengths

  • +First to formalize “summary stability” as an evaluation dimension — fills a real benchmarking blind spot.
  • +Methodologically solid: SAP chains visualization + descriptive stats + standard non-parametric tests (Shapiro-Wilk, Levene, Friedman, Nemenyi), with a closed-form CVc derivation (Appendix A) — portable to other NLP randomness diagnostics.
  • +Strong evidence: >28K summaries, 3×3×100×32 grid across metrics and document lengths.
  • +Actionable takeaways: BERTScore beats AlignScore stability; Gemma3 most trustworthy; short-text factual fidelity worst.

Limitations

  • −Only 3 genres (news/medical/legal) — no dialogue, meeting, code, or multimodal summarization.
  • −Only <4B open instruct models — no GPT-4/Claude/Gemini-Pro; rankings may drift as models scale.
  • −No human evaluation to calibrate machine stability against perceived trustworthiness.
  • −k=100 raises compute cost; no power analysis for a minimal sufficient k.
  • −min-based Ē is outlier-sensitive; no robust quantile variant; AlignScore anomaly conflates metric noise with true summarizer instability.
  • −No comparison vs existing variance-reduction methods (e.g. Oved & Levy input-perturbation selection).
Provenance. Journal-submission format (28 pp, 16+ figures/tables, Appendix A–D). High citation density covering DecodingTrust, Mizrahi et al. prompt-sensitivity, and LLM-as-judge reliability. Representative sampling verified by Z & χ² tests; non-parametric tests used where normality failed; bootstrap 95% CIs instead of point estimates; rank comparisons follow ML multi-baseline best practice.

04 Open Questions for further exploration

  • Does the ranking survive at GPT-4 / Claude scale and 7B–70B open models? How large is the marginal effect of temperature / top-p?
  • Does AlignScore’s Ē ≈ 0.003 on CNN/DM reflect summarizer instability, or the metric’s own calibration on short news? Would SummaC / FactCC / QuestEval agree?
  • Would a median or quantile (e.g. Ē₂₅%) stability coefficient give a more outlier-robust ranking?
  • How does the stability coefficient correlate with human trust judgments (expert or crowdsourced)? What is a sufficient (k, N) power budget?
  • Do the conclusions hold under RAG, long-doc chunking, multimodal, or code summarization?
  • Can generation-level stability become a training/decoding objective (minimum-variance decoding, consistency-loss fine-tuning)?
