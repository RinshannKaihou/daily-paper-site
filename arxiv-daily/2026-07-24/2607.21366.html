A data-free, hyperparameter-free compression theory that could reframe ViT/LLM structured pruning and source-data-private transfer learning.

Critical edge

Explicitly proof-of-concept: no ViT/LLM, no LayerNorm-only, no latency/FLOPs — and the Gaussian surrogate ignores post-ReLU non-negativity.

01Methodology

From discrete weights to continuous functions

  • Each neuron is a rank-1 Hilbert-Schmidt operator f_i = g_i ⊗ w_out,i in H = L²(X, P_X; ℝᶜ).
  • Gaussian surrogate P_X = N(μ̂_x, Σ̂_x) from BN moving stats (max-entropy) — all integrals closed-form, zero data samples.
  • Scale/shape invariance: effective params w_in^eff = γ/√(σ²+ε)·w_raw + Hilbert norm kill magnitude pruning's "fake importance."
  • Axiomatic cost J_final = N·D/E(Φ_b) → derives J_prune, J_merge (rank-2 eigenproblem parent), J_evict (whole residual block).
  • Encoding loop: receding-horizon greedy, min DR = J_k/ΔP_k, dominated by O(L·N²) merging enumeration.

02Results

Transfer + compression, both source-free

  • Cross-domain transfer (CIFAR-100→SVHN): DEFT H-Score 65.82 vs Full FT 13.88, EWC 12.54, PEFT 10.18 — near-equal plasticity (89.79 vs 94.09 target acc), ~45-pt better source retention.
  • ImageNet ResNet-50: higher-accuracy sub-models than L1-norm and BN-scale structured baselines at matched density (proof-of-concept curve).
  • Complexity: per-neuron capacity O(N) vs O(N³) for orthogonal-subspace FT methods; no source-data forward pass.
  • Macro-structure: J_evict lets an entire residual bottleneck compete with single-neuron actions in one distortion-rate engine.
  • Guarantees: DEFT static initialization shock O(τ) + zero dynamic interference to the frozen Universal Core.

03Strengths & Limitations

Strengths

  • Unified J_k/ΔP_k across pruning, merging, eviction — no manual hyperparameters.
  • Truly data-free — consumes BN stats directly; one-time mini-batch calibration for non-BN.
  • Scale-invariant by construction, rooting out magnitude heuristics' pseudoinportance.
  • Closed-form O(1)/O(N); DEFT ships verifiable static + dynamic bounds.

Limitations

  • Proof-of-concept only — no ViT / LLM / LayerNorm-only, no latency or FLOPs.
  • Gaussian surrogate ignores post-ReLU non-negativity; mutual kernel assumes β≈0.
  • J_final is an upper-bound proxy; correlation constraint leans on greedy self-consistency.
  • Relies on BN; transfer tested on a single direction (CIFAR-100→SVHN), no LoRA/OSF head-to-head.

04Questions for Further Exploration

  1. How to build a BN-equivalent Gaussian surrogate for LayerNorm-only architectures (ViT, LLaMA)? Special operator modeling for attention heads / QKV?
  2. How badly does J_bound underestimate cost for orthogonal neuron pairs — can a synthetic probe measure the bias?
  3. On 7B/13B LLMs, can HOPE merging recover a better structured-sparsity–perplexity curve than Wanda / SparseGPT?
  4. DEFT vs LoRA / OSF at equal parameter budget — orthogonal updates, or stackable with low-rank adapters?
  5. Quantify Gaussian-surrogate deviation at layer 1 and depth-wise; would normalizing-flow / score-matching give a better P_X?
  6. Does J_evict's linear-sum assumption survive non-bottleneck blocks (ResNet-D, ConvNeXt)?
  7. Can the "capacity fingerprint" (core vs peripheral) detect backdoor or watermark neurons?
