01 Methodology

descent-theoretic formulation · frozen complex · three-way rule

  1. Finite descent instance. A merge is D = (K, {M_i}, {g_ij}, A): K is a frozen comparison complex (vertices = checkpoints), g_ij are alignment maps, h_ijk = g_ij·g_jk·g_ki is the triangle residual. Strict descent requires h_ijk = 1.
  2. Careful scope. A finite H²(K;A) class has site-level meaning only if K is the nerve of a cover with a comparison theorem; otherwise it is just "a class on the chosen complex."
  3. Frozen-complex protocol (Def. 2.2.8). Data split into D_K, D_align, D_cert, D_select, D_test; K is frozen before fitting transitions — kills the circular bias of "choosing K from the residual you're testing."
  4. Three-way decision (Thm. 2.2.9). Gap Def(c) = dist(c, B²); interval [L,U] + thresholds 0 ≤ a < b ⇒ trivial / nontrivial-on-K / uncertified, with provable control of false-trivial and false-nontrivial.
  5. Conservative pipeline (Alg. 1). Un-certified branches fall back to plain or synchronized merging; projective-rank & nonabelian-holonomy search are optional research branches, not verdicts.

03 Strengths & Limitations

uncommon methodological conservatism · scope stated explicitly

+ Strengths

  • Clean four-layer separation of cycle inconsistency, strict gauge sync, certified central H² obstruction, and nonabelian holonomy — each with an operable gate.
  • New theorems isolated from background (Table 3): no-go (Prop. 2.3.6), frozen three-way rule (Thm. 2.2.9), family multiplicity control (Thm. 2.2.10), refinement persistence (Prop. 2.2.11).
  • Frozen-complex protocol structurally prevents "choosing K from the tested residual."
  • Falsifiable by design: experiments that should fail (planted defect; natural abstention) do, matching prediction.
  • High reproducibility — audited at commit b0e1ac4, deterministic plotting, no invalidated artifacts.

− Limitations

  • Realization gap. The μ₂ class is an oracle cocycle-level witness, not a topology discovered from four ordinary checkpoints.
  • Scale. LoRA audit covers one residual-feature layer over frozen ResNet-18, not a multi-layer transformer / ViT LoRA stack.
  • Negative result is ambiguous. K may be mis-constructed — absence of class ≠ absence of structure.
  • Decision utility incomplete. No natural distribution-shift calibration, full gating ablation, or regret vs. an oracle selector.
  • Terminology barrier. Gerbe / band / Čech / Brauer risk being misread as "Brauer obstruction exists" (the paper's conclusion is the opposite).

04 Questions for Further Exploration

eight open threads · non-linear, pick one

  1. Absence or mis-construction? Can an application-driven, cross-dataset/­architecture higher-overlap rule surface certified central classes in natural merges?
  2. Multi-layer LoRA stacks: does the naive-averaging failure scale with layer / rank / adapter count? Peak-RSS & runtime trade-offs of global sync vs. dense SVD?
  3. Decisive realization experiment: certify a nontrivial μ₂/G_m class and a capacity-accountable projective predictor in one generator — closing the oracle→neural gap.
  4. Nonabelian holonomy: is there a natural task where invariant-branch readout beats generic MoE on accuracy / calibration / OOD?
  5. Hodge relation: formalize the complementarity between Hodge harmonic components (numerical) and central classes (structural).
  6. Decision utility: calibrate certificate regret under OOD merging, upgrading from "conservative reject" to "natural accuracy predictor."
  7. Non-central gauges: can nonabelian descent / gerbe band data on non-central GL_r subgroups yield stronger verdicts than abelian central classes?
  8. Reproducibility standard: can the pre-registered confirmatory/exploratory + multiplicity-control protocol become a community reporting minimum?
