Why this matters

Challenges weight-selection approximate unlearning (SalUn, ICLR 2024); directly informs privacy deletion, GDPR compliance, and representation-level auditing.

Critical edge

Geometry-sensitive audit metrics can be satisfied by late-subspace updates that erase nothing — low forget accuracy is not deletion evidence.

01 Methodology

First matched-compute controlled ablation isolating weight selection from the random-label forgetting objective.

Same pretrained ResNet-18, same RL objective, same hyperparameters. The only variable is mask identity — three conditions held to equal compute budget:

  • SalUn mask — top-k gradient-magnitude weights.
  • Random mask — uniformly sampled, matched sparsity ‖m‖₀.
  • RL-full — no mask, full random-label fine-tune.

Two extra conditions stress-test the conclusion: a Fisher (diagonal) mask (second selection rule, SSD/EWC family) and an Early mask (layer4+head bit-exact frozen → causal placement intervention).

±5ppTOST δ, calibrated to ~3–4pp seed floor

Audit stack: linear probe · prototype recovery · layer-wise CKA · head-only relearning · MIA, all against Gold (retrain) & RandInit (texture floor) references.

02 Results

All three masks are output-identical yet representation-far from Gold — and equivalent to each other.

Mask-pair diffs ≤ 0.6pp, TOST p<0.001 · forget acc = 0.0% everywhere

Mechanism. Mask specificity index only 0.09–0.11 — forget-class masks overlap as much with retain masks (Jaccard 0.397) as with each other (0.395). Fisher vs SalUn mask Jaccard: 0.83 / 0.94.

Causal twist. Early mask (late layers frozen) still drives forget acc to 0–4% and matches SalUn on linear-probe recall (92.2 vs 92.5%) — but CIFAR-100 prototype recovery jumps to 59.0%. Linear separability survives every placement: late subspaces move geometry, not erase.

03 Strengths & Limitations

▲ Strengths

  • First clean ablation of a widely-used, never-independently-tested mechanism — falsifiable & reproducible (Zenodo + GitHub).
  • Both correlational (gradient energy, mask overlap) and causal (Early-mask placement) evidence — self-refuting narrative refinement.
  • TOST equivalence + Gold/RandInit dual references → a reusable audit protocol.
  • Transparent writing: states bounds, inconclusive cases, under-powered sub-claims.

▼ Limitations

  • Narrow scope: CIFAR-10/100 + ResNet-18 + single-class only — no sample/ImageNet-scale, no ViT/LLM/diffusion.
  • CIFAR-100 prototype recovery under-powered (SD≈13pp, 10 classes) → strong claims rest on the linear probe.
  • Functional equivalence only empirically shown; Jaccard is a structural, not full-theoretic, metric.
  • Gradient concentration is an empirical phenomenon, not a theorem — architecture-dependent.

04 Questions for further exploration

  1. Does late-layer gradient energy concentration hold for ViT / foundation models (CLIP, ViT-L)? If energy is more uniform, does mask identity become decisive again?
  2. For LLM concept/sample unlearning (TOFU, RMU), is SalUn-style saliency again equivalent to random? Does this audit stack transfer?
  3. Is there an explicit representation-level objective (not logit-level RL) that crushes linear-probe recall to Gold? Early mask already rules out logit-level + any placement.
  4. How should TOST's ±5pp δ be recalibrated per threat model — e.g. attacker-view exploitable-recovery rate vs fixed point margin?
