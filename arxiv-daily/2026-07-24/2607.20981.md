> **一句话总结：** 这是一篇综述论文，它论证了在多模态边缘智能中，视觉 token 压缩、MoE 路由、量化、KV-cache 与硬件执行并非彼此独立的优化项，而是构成一条"故障传播链"，任何单阶段的局部优化都会被下游阶段放大或抵消，并提出 Temporal Routing Consistency (TRC) 作为视频 MoE 模型的诊断指标。

> **TL;DR:** A 39-page survey arguing that visual token compression, MoE routing, low-bit quantization, KV-cache retention, and edge hardware execution form a non-decomposable failure-propagation chain; using a unified taxonomy and a new **Temporal Routing Consistency (TRC)** diagnostic, it shows that isolating any stage (e.g., routing-aware PTQ recovering 1.15%–2.28%, or modality-aware VLM quantization retaining 93.5% of FP16 at W3A3) leaves downstream failures unaddressed, and calls for interface-aware co-design of multimodal edge systems.

## 核心贡献

1. **统一的故障链分类法（Failure-Chain Taxonomy）。** 将"压缩 → 路由 → 量化 → 硬件"形式化为推理流水线 `P = H ∘ Q_π ∘ R_θ ∘ C_φ`，并显式刻画三个关键接口（A：视觉输入→压缩；B：压缩→路由；C：路由→量化）上"传递什么、如何失败、文献是否已研究"。论文用数学化方式（包含 ∆_CQ 的 inclusion-exclusion 下界）刻画了 Interface B/C 之间的"放大关系"（amplification relationship），即压缩引入的路由扰动与量化引入的路由扰动会叠加作用于临界边界附近的 token-专家组合。
2. **视频 MoE 的时间路由诊断：Temporal Routing Consistency (TRC)。** 定义为时间相邻帧所激活专家集合的平均 Jaccard 重叠度，用以度量视频 MoE 模型中路由的时序稳定性。这是静态准确率指标无法暴露的失败模式（例如专家在帧间频繁切换导致 cache miss 与 evidence loss）的诊断工具。
3. **跨主题设计张力的系统性综合。** 在五大主题（视觉 token 压缩、视频/时间冗余、KV-cache 优化、MoE 路由与量化服务、边缘部署与硬件感知基准）下，明确提炼了五对反复出现的设计张力：准确率 vs token 预算、静态压缩 vs 内容自适应保留、稀疏路由效率 vs 专家坍塌、低位服务 vs 模态特异退化、基准便利性 vs 端到端边缘真实性。
4. **提出研究空白（Open Problems）。** 明确列出 8 个开放方向：路由感知压缩、跨模态 cache 保留、专家坍塌的理论基础、主动故障检测（如 Gini > 0.7 的谱阈值）、长视频效率基准、硬件感知协同设计（以每字节带宽能耗而非每 FLOP 为目标）、联邦/分布式边缘推理、压缩/量化/驱逐对幻觉率的影响。

## Key Contributions

1. **A unified failure-chain taxonomy** formalizing the four-stage inference pipeline `P = H ∘ Q_π ∘ R_θ ∘ C_φ` and three explicit interfaces (A: visual input→compression; B: compression→routing; C: routing→quantization), each characterized by *what it transfers, how it fails, and how well it has been studied*. The survey provides a first-order mathematical bound on the joint routing-disruption rate ∆_CQ ≥ ∆_Q + ∆_C − ∆_Q·∆_C, formalizing the **amplification relationship** between compression-induced and quantization-induced routing shifts.
2. **Temporal Routing Consistency (TRC)**, a new diagnostic for video MoE models defined as the mean Jaccard overlap between expert sets activated by temporally adjacent frames. TRC exposes routing instability that static accuracy benchmarks miss, which is critical because unstable expert selection drives cache misses, evidence loss, and transfer-cost spikes in edge video inference.
3. **A systematic synthesis of design tensions** across five themes (visual token compression, video/temporal redundancy, KV-cache optimization, MoE routing and quantized serving, edge deployment and hardware-aware benchmarking), explicitly framing five recurring trade-offs: accuracy vs token budget, static vs content-adaptive compression, sparse-routing efficiency vs expert collapse, low-bit serving vs modality-specific degradation, and benchmark convenience vs end-to-end edge realism.
4. **A concrete open-problems agenda**, identifying eight directions: routing-aware compression, cross-modal cache retention, theoretical foundations of expert collapse, proactive failure detection, long-video efficiency benchmarks, hardware-aware co-design optimizing joules-per-bandwidth-byte rather than per-FLOP, federated/distributed edge inference, and the impact of extreme compression/eviction on hallucination and factual accuracy.

## Methodology

This is a **survey / position paper**, not an empirical study. Its methodological contribution is a conceptual-analytical framework:

- **Inference-pipeline formalization.** The four stages—compression operator `C_φ` (compression ratio, merging strategy, positional encoding), routing operator `R_θ` (routing temperature, load-balancing objective, expert capacity), quantization operator `Q_π` (bit-width, calibration set, precision allocation), and hardware context `H` (memory hierarchy, bandwidth, thermal envelope)—are composed as `P = H ∘ Q_π ∘ R_θ ∘ C_φ`. The survey's thesis is that the independent-optimization decomposition `φ* = argmin L(C_φ)`, `θ* = argmin L(R_θ)`, `π* = argmin L(Q_π)` only holds under a statistical-independence assumption that is empirically false in multimodal systems.
- **Interface-by-interface analysis.** For each of the three cross-stage interfaces, the paper analyzes *what is transferred, how it fails, and the state of the literature*. For example, Interface B formalizes why **token merging** creates out-of-distribution "convex-combination" tokens that fall near expert decision boundaries, while **token dropping** preserves marginal but disrupts joint (attention-context) distributions.
- **Diagnostic formalization.** TRC is defined as `mean over adjacent frame pairs of Jaccard(E_t, E_{t+1})`, where `E_t` is the set of experts activated by frame t. The spectral Gini-coefficient threshold (>0.7) is cited as a preventive marker of routing collapse.
- **Literature scope.** ~130+ references spanning ToMe/ATM/AdaMerge/ToSA/MaMe/VisionTrim (compression), MAE/VideoMAE/Perceiver (redundancy foundations), PyramidKV/SnapKV/ChunkKV (KV-cache), PagedAttention/vLLM (serving), EAQuant/GemQ/Speed-Q (MoE/VLM quantization), and edge benchmarks (MobileVLM, LLM-inference-bench, µWatt-to-MWatt energy measurement).

## Results & Highlights

Because this is a survey, "results" are quantitative claims synthesized from cited primary studies used to substantiate the failure-chain thesis:

1. **Routing-aware PTQ recovers accuracy that vanilla PTQ destroys.** Fu et al. (2026) reports that preserving routing consistency via KL alignment between full-precision and quantized routing probabilities recovers **1.15%–2.28% average score** across **three MoE architectures** under W4A4/W3A4 settings, demonstrating that routing disruption— not aggregate quantization error—drives the loss (Section 1, 3.3, 11.2).
2. **Modality-heterogeneous quantization is necessary for VLMs.** Zhong et al. (2026) shows that modality-separated quantization retains **93.5% of FP16 performance at W3A3** (69.5 vs 74.3), while naive joint visual+text quantization at W4A8 causes **2%–4% VQA accuracy loss** concentrated in spatial-reasoning tasks, because visual tokens have up to **4× higher activation std** and **~2.3% outlier probability vs ~0.6% for text** (Sections 1, 11.4).
3. **Token compression is highly effective in isolation but pipeline-coupled.** Bipartite token merging (ToMe) yields up to **2× speedup at only 0.2%–0.3% ImageNet accuracy loss** on DeiT/MAE/CLIP (Bolya 2023); adaptive layer-wise thresholds cut DeiT FLOPs **>30% with zero accuracy loss** (Lee & Choi 2025); the redundancy literature establishes that **≥75% of visual tokens can be removed without information loss** (Section 4.1). The survey's point is that these gains survive only if downstream routing/quantization are co-designed.
4. **MoE serving bottlenecks are memory-bandwidth, not compute.** Predictive expert prefetching yields **2–10× inference speedup** (He 2024); disaggregated attention/expert serving achieves up to **3.9× per-GPU throughput** over co-located serving (Zhang 2026); lossless expert compression + cache-aware scheduling cuts MoE inference latency by **up to 72.77%** (Yang 2026).
5. **Expert collapse is measurable and frequent.** An expert is collapsed when utilization falls below **1/N** (e.g., 1.5625% for 64 experts); ~**one-third of sparse MoE layers drift toward single-expert routing** under weak balancing (Sha 2026); class-conditioned FID can drop **8%–15%** under "selective deadlock" (Section 9.2).
6. **2-bit is a catastrophic cliff, 4-bit is workable.** Zhou et al. (2026) show a clean regime separation for LLaMA-style transformers: 4-bit is recoverable via calibration, 2-bit triggers **computation collapse** requiring architectural repair (Section 11.1, Table 4).
7. **Speculative decoding × quantization interaction.** At W8A8, draft acceptance ratio drops **3%–5%**, cutting speculative-decoding gains by **1.5×–2×**—a quintessential Interface-C failure invisible to single-component evaluation (Section 11.5).

- Credibility signals: explicit mathematical formalization of the pipeline and the amplification bound; honesty about what has and has not been measured (e.g., "the magnitude of compression-induced routing disruption has not been directly measured in the literature"); inclusion of failure-mode taxonomies (Table 4) rather than uncritical method listings; willingness to flag the survey's own central claim (compression-routing coupling) as a **hypothesis rather than established result**.

## Critical Assessment

- **The paper's central thesis is conceptually strong but largely unsubstantiated by direct measurement.** The most important interface (B: compression → routing shift) is acknowledged as a *hypothesis* in Section 3.2.2 ("this remains a hypothesis rather than an established result"). The only direct evidence is from quantization-routing work (Fu 2026, Park 2026), which the authors extrapolate to compression by analogy. A reader expecting experimental validation of the failure chain itself will find only indirect, transferred evidence.
- **TRC is introduced but never operationalized.** The diagnostic is defined formally, yet no numerical TRC values are reported for any video MoE model, no threshold for "acceptable" TRC is proposed, and no existing model is benchmarked against it. The contribution is therefore conceptual—useful as a proposal, but currently unfalsifiable.
- **Quantitative claims are inherited from primary studies with heterogeneous settings.** The 1.15%–2.28% recovery (Fu 2026), 93.5% retention at W3A3 (Zhong 2026), 72.77% latency reduction (Yang 2026), etc. are drawn from different models, datasets, and hardware; the survey does not re-run or normalize them, so cross-claim comparability is limited.
- **The "edge" framing sometimes outruns the evidence.** Much of the cited work (vLLM, disaggregated serving, ExpertFlow) targets server GPUs, not mobile/embedded targets. The edge-Pareto figure (Figure 7) is described as *illustrative*, and the survey itself admits (Section 12.2) that "loading latency has been overlooked in most throughput-centric benchmarks," weakening claims about real edge deployability.
- **The failure-chain formalism (Eq. 1, ∆_CQ bound) is elegant but its parameters are unmeasured.** The inclusion-exclusion lower bound is presented without any empirical estimate of the correlation between compression- and quantization-induced routing changes, which the paper itself flags as an open problem.
- **Scope gaps.** State-space / Mamba-style multimodal models, retrieval-augmented multimodal inference, and training-time (rather than inference-time) co-design receive only cursory treatment relative to their growing importance.

## Strengths

- **Genuinely integrative framing.** Unlike most efficiency surveys that catalog techniques by family, this one organizes the literature around *interfaces between stages*, which is a more deployment-faithful mental model and arguably the paper's most valuable contribution.
- **Mathematical precision.** The `P = H ∘ Q_π ∘ R_θ ∘ C_φ` composition, the explicit independent-optimization decomposition it critiques, and the amplification lower bound give the argument formal teeth rather than relying on hand-waving.
- **Honest epistemics.** The paper repeatedly distinguishes measured results from extrapolations, marks its own core claim as a hypothesis, and explicitly enumerates what the literature has *not* studied at each interface.
- **Actionable taxonomy.** Table 4 (quantization failure modes: signal degradation vs computation collapse vs routing inconsistency, with recoverability and mitigation) is a genuinely useful practitioner-facing artifact.
- **Forward-looking agenda.** The eight open problems—especially hardware-aware co-design optimizing joules-per-bandwidth-byte, and the impact of compression/eviction on hallucination—are timely and under-explored.

## Limitations

- **No original experiments.** As a survey, it provides no new measurements of the failure-chain interactions it posits; the central Interface-B claim is explicitly a hypothesis.
- **TRC is unvalidated.** Proposed but not computed on any real video MoE model; no threshold or empirical baseline provided.
- **Inherited-numbers comparability.** Quantitative claims come from disparate models/datasets/hardware and are not normalized or re-measured.
- **Edge-realism gap.** Much cited infrastructure work is server-GPU-centric; Figure 7 is illustrative rather than measured; cold-start/loading latency is acknowledged as under-benchmarked.
- **Amplification bound is unestimated.** The ∆_CQ lower bound has no empirical instantiation of the correlation term.
- **Coverage gaps.** Lightweight coverage of state-space multimodal models, RAG-style multimodal inference, and training-time (rather than inference-time) co-design.

## Questions for Further Exploration

1. 压缩诱导的路由扰动幅度（∆_C）究竟有多大？在视觉 token merging/dropping 与 MoE 路由之间，是否存在与量化诱导扰动（∆_Q，Fu 2026 已测）量级相当甚至更大的可测量效应？目前这是明确的假设而非结果。
2. Temporal Routing Consistency (TRC) 在主流视频 MoE 模型（如 VideoLLaMA、视频版 Mixtral/DeepSeek-MoE）上的实测分布是什么？是否存在一个"健康 vs 崩溃"的 TRC 阈值？相邻帧的间隔（k 步）如何影响 TRC？
3. 路由感知压缩目标（在压缩-未压缩输入之间最小化路由分布的 KL 散度）能否在不损害压缩比的前提下，恢复被压缩破坏的稀有专家激活？这是否会与负载均衡损失冲突？
4. 论文提出的 ∆_CQ inclusion-exclusion 下界假设两个扰动源作用于不相关的 token-专家组合；实际上它们更可能作用于同一批临界边界组合。如何实验性地测量这一相关性以收紧界？
5. 压缩、量化、KV-cache 驱逐对**幻觉率与事实准确性**的影响有多大？删除的视觉 token 或驱逐的 KV 条目是否不成比例地影响"低频但信息密集"的证据（即 Interface B/C 中最关键的那些）？论文明确将其列为开放问题。
6. 硬件感知协同设计若以**每字节带宽能耗**而非每 FLOP 能耗为优化目标，量化的相对收益会变得多大？是否存在一个理论框架能将带宽节省与精度损失统一到一个 Pareto 前沿？
7. 长视频效率基准（同时奖励高质量回答与低推理成本）应如何设计？现有的长视频理解基准（Hermes、LongVideoBench 类）缺少这种成本感知评估。
8. 联邦/分布式边缘推理下，单设备的内存层级约束如何转化为多设备协同下的通信开销与隐私保护问题？这是否会进一步放大 Interface B/C 的失败模式？
