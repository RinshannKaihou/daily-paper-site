Methodology

Conceptual-analytical framework survey, no new experiments

  • Pipeline formalization. Four operators composed as P = H ∘ Qπ ∘ Rθ ∘ Cφ; the per-stage argmin decomposition is shown to break under multimodal coupling.
  • Interface analysis. For each of three cross-stage seams (A / B / C): what transfers, how it fails, literature state — e.g. token-merging yields OOD “convex-combination” tokens near expert boundaries.
  • Amplification bound. Joint routing-disruption ∆CQ ≥ ∆Q + ∆C − ∆Q·∆C formalizes why stage errors compound rather than add.
  • TRC diagnostic. Temporal Routing Consistency = mean Jaccard overlap of expert sets across adjacent video frames; spectral Gini > 0.7 flagged as collapse marker.
  • Scope. ~130+ refs: ToMe/ATM/AdaMerge, MAE/Perceiver, PyramidKV/SnapKV, vLLM/PagedAttention, EAQuant/GemQ/Speed-Q, MobileVLM.

Why a “failure chain”? the thesis

  • Most efficiency surveys catalog techniques by family. This one organizes them around interfaces between stages — a deployment-faithful mental model.
  • Isolating any single stage leaves downstream failures unaddressed: e.g. routing-aware PTQ recovers accuracy that vanilla PTQ destroys — proving routing disruption, not aggregate quant error, drives loss.
  • Modality-heterogeneous quantization is necessary because visual tokens have ~4× higher activation std and ~2.3% outlier prob vs ~0.6% for text.
  • Two design regimes: 2-bit is a catastrophic cliff (computation collapse); 4-bit is recoverable via calibration.

Strengths vs Limitations

Strengths 5

  • Integrative framing. Organizes literature around inter-stage interfaces, not technique families — its most valuable contribution.
  • Mathematical precision. The composition operator, the decomposition it critiques, and the amplification lower bound give the argument formal teeth.
  • Honest epistemics. Repeatedly distinguishes measured results from extrapolations; flags its own core claim as a hypothesis.
  • Actionable taxonomy. Table 4 (signal-degradation vs computation-collapse vs routing-inconsistency, with recoverability) is genuinely practitioner-facing.
  • Forward agenda. Eight open problems — joules-per-bandwidth-byte co-design and compression→hallucination are timely & under-explored.

Limitations 6

  • No original experiments. The central Interface-B coupling is a hypothesis; the failure chain itself is never directly measured.
  • TRC unvalidated. Defined but never computed on any video-MoE model; no threshold, no baseline — currently unfalsifiable.
  • Inherited-numbers comparability. Headline stats come from disparate models / datasets / hardware, never re-run or normalized.
  • Edge-realism gap. Much cited infra (vLLM, disaggregated serving) is server-GPU; Figure 7 is illustrative; cold-start / loading latency under-benchmarked.
  • Amplification bound unestimated. ∆CQ lower bound has no empirical instantiation of the correlation term.
  • Coverage gaps. SSM/Mamba multimodal, RAG-multimodal, and training-time co-design get only cursory treatment.
04

Open Questions for Further Exploration

  1. How large is compression-induced routing shift ∆C? Is it comparable to the measured ∆Q (Fu 2026), or larger?
  2. What is the empirical TRC distribution on real video-MoE models (VideoLLaMA, video Mixtral/DeepSeek-MoE)? Is there a healthy-vs-collapse threshold?
  3. Can routing-aware compression (KL-min on routing probs) recover rare-expert activations without harming load balance?
  4. How to measure the ∆CQ correlation (both perturbations likely hit the same critical-boundary tokens) to tighten the bound?
  5. How much do compression / quantization / KV-eviction raise hallucination & factual-error rates — esp. for low-frequency, info-dense evidence?
  6. Under joules-per-bandwidth-byte (not per-FLOP) optimization, how large are quantization’s gains? Is there a unified Pareto frontier?
  7. How to design cost-aware long-video benchmarks that reward both answer quality and inference cost (Hermes / LongVideoBench lack this)?
  8. In federated/distributed edge inference, do multi-device memory & comm constraints further amplify Interface-B/C failures?
