Why this matters

Moves LLM overconfidence from an output-level symptom to a causal property inside the model — a fix that is controllable, prunable, and free at inference.

把「过度自信」从输出层后处理推进为模型内部的因果属性：校准手段可控、可剪枝、零推理开销。

Critical edge

Against properly-tuned temperature scaling, absolute ECE gains shrink to 0.005–0.036 — the −53% headline rides low-ECE settings and visibly under-tuned baselines.

Critical Assessment · comparative fairness (Thermometer / ATS / ITI reproductions)
Methodology

Calibration as a causal intervention, in five moves

  1. 1 Decompose predictions into four classes

    Split by correctness × confidence at τc = 0.5: correct_high / correct_low / wrong_high / wrong_low — isolating overconfident errors (wrong_high) as their own subset.

  2. 2 Attribute causally, per class

    Causal Head Gating optimizes gate logits on a frozen model with dual L1 branches: λ = +0.1 → necessity score, λ = −0.1 → sufficiency score.

  3. 3 Label every head

    Both scores ≥ 0.5 → facilitating; low-necessity / high-sufficiency → interfering; the rest irrelevant.

  4. 4 Build the canonical-union mask

    Union of wrong_high facilitators (H_err, confident-error drivers) and correct_high non-facilitators (H_noise, spurious confidence); rank by max(|s_nec|, |s_suf|), keep top-k: k=10 LLaMA-3.1-8B, k=8 Gemma-2-9B / Mistral-7B.

  5. 5 Intervene at inference — nothing else

    Zero the selected heads in every forward pass. No parameter updates, no output post-processing; masked heads can even be pruned outright.

One-time calibration: ≤ 40 zero-shot samples (≤ 10 per class), 39–88 s on GPU (peak 28–46 GiB); penalty tuned across 15 model–dataset runs with ΔECE < 0.015 on correct_high.

  • The ablation is the punchline: masking all wrong-high facilitating heads collapses accuracy to 0.2044 (ECE 0.4745); the compact top-k mask keeps 0.6120 / 0.0490 — truncation does all the work.
  • Learned calibrators look unstable here: Thermometer ECE 0.17–0.38 (NLL 1.4–2.9), ATS NLL up to 4.2; ITI at fixed strength drops Mistral MMLU accuracy to 0.3980.
  • τc = 0.5 is the sweet spot (ECE 0.0490 vs 0.0705 at τc=0.7 / 0.0696 at τc=0.9); on SciQ, CoCHI leads on all four metrics (acc, ECE, NLL, Brier).
  • Beyond wrong_high: ECE also falls on correct_low subsets (~0.10 MMLU, ~0.09 MedMCQA), suggesting broader confidence stabilization.
Strengths

What holds up

  • Principled mechanistic framing — calibration as a causally identifiable internal property, with a clean head taxonomy (facilitating / interfering / irrelevant).
  • Best-in-class deployment profile — 40-byte mask, zero inference overhead, prunable heads, calibration from ≤ 40 samples.
  • Often improves accuracy while calibrating (+2.7 pts on MedMCQA / Gemma-2-9B) — something post-hoc scaling cannot do.
  • Thorough hygiene in scope — 3 models × 5 benchmarks × 4 baselines, plus masking ablations, τc and k sensitivity, and cost measurements.
  • Honest scoping — the limitations section itself flags MCQA-only coverage and head-level granularity.
Limitations

What to weigh

  • Four-option MCQA only — open-ended generation, long-form reasoning, and multi-turn settings are explicitly out of scope.
  • Head-level granularity — miscalibration from MLP layers, residual streams, or cross-layer interactions is not addressed.
  • Modest absolute gains for real cost — 0.005–0.036 ECE over tuned TS, bought with a 28–46 GiB GPU calibration vs TS's 0.5 s CPU fit.
  • Variance under-discussed — accuracy std up to ±0.11 (vs TS's ±0.005–0.03) is reported but never analyzed; significance is unclear.
  • No mask-transfer test — every mask is per model–dataset; generalization beyond the calibration distribution is unknown.
  • Baseline fairness — Thermometer / ATS / ITI appear under-tuned, inflating the relative wins.
Questions for further exploration

Open threads

  1. Does a mask calibrated on one benchmark (e.g., MMLU) transfer to unseen datasets or domains — or is the head set distribution-specific? How stable is top-k across seeds?
  2. Which layers do the selected heads sit in, and do they overlap verbalized-confidence circuits (Zhao et al. 2026) — or are H_err heads generic capability heads merely dosed safely by top-k?
  3. How much ECE gain survives against a well-tuned TS or Thermometer, and is it significant given ±0.06–0.11 accuracy stds?
  1. Can the approach extend beyond MCQA — free-form QA with sampling-based confidence, or selective prediction / abstention where fewer wrong_high errors directly improve coverage-risk curves?
  2. Would soft gating (keeping σ(z) instead of binarizing to 0/1) or joint head + MLP attribution push the calibration-accuracy trade-off further?
