Key finding · Mechanistic interpretability · Hybrid language models

Linear attention builds the retrieval query — softmax attention only performs the lookup.

线性注意力不只是压缩上下文的「存储器」——它构造 softmax reader 的检索 query；Encode–Address 回路决定模型「检索什么」，且该计算可跨架构迁移、全程不更新权重。

Why this matters

Hybrid designers can now tell which linear blocks are safe to prune or distill — cutting "just the linear layers" can silently break retrieval addressing.

为「哪些线性块可剪枝/蒸馏而不破坏检索」提供因果依据。

Critical edge

Every circuit claim rests on a synthetic 8-pair, single-token dictionary lookup — no needle-in-a-haystack, multi-hop, or natural-document retrieval is tested.

External validity "asserted by framing rather than tested."

Methodology — distilled

Causal circuit analysis on frozen hybrid checkpoints; all interventions run through forward hooks, zero weight updates.

  • Models. Two independently trained families: Qwen3.5 (Gated DeltaNet, 3:1 linear:softmax; 0.8B → 35B-A3B MoE) and Granite-4.0-H (Mamba-2, 4 of 40 layers softmax; 1B, 7B).
  • Task. 8 single-token key–value pairs + one question; three surface formats (chat8 / list8 / rev8) × 100 prompts. Synthetic by design — isolates retrieval from parametric knowledge and reasoning.
  • Interventions. Progressive pruning · block removal (zero the mixer output) · block transplant (donor → host) · token patching · ridge-regression linear replacement · query-vs-keys recompute · cross-architecture block replacement via fitted linear alignment maps.
  • Provenance. Code, per-model results, logs, prompts released — github.com/yuang-chen/LinearQuery.

Strengths

  • Necessity (removal), sufficiency (transplant), and mechanism (query recompute, state transplant) triangulate one conclusion from independent directions.
  • The removal-vs-transplant dissociation (accuracy 1.00 vs. 0.99 redirect) is explained by compensating later readers — reported, not hidden.
  • GDN ↔ Mamba-2 block replacement with genuine controls suggests convergent "addressing" computations across different recurrent mixers.
  • Concrete pruning/distillation guidance: which linear blocks carry retrieval addressing, and why single-layer interventions miss block-level effects.

Limitations

  • Evidence confined to a synthetic 8-entry, single-token lookup; no NIAH, multi-hop, or document-QA benchmarks.
  • Only two families dissected — Jamba, Nemotron-H, Kimi Linear, Samba discussed but not analyzed.
  • Transfer needs fitted ridge alignment maps (a supervised fit); the larger reverse direction reaches only 0.46 vs. 0.75 intact.
  • 27B (1.00) vs. 35B-A3B (0.34) removal discrepancy is flagged, then deferred to future work.
  • Only the first answer token is scored; general LM quality is entangled with the circuit (perplexity 44.7 under linear-Encode).

Questions for further exploration

  • Does the Encode–Address circuit survive needle-in-a-haystack, multi-hop, multi-token keys, and semantically close distractors?
  • Why is Address necessity non-monotonic with scale — is the MoE router behind the 35B-A3B fragility?
  • Can the Address block be used for targeted editing — steering which entry a model retrieves, or diagnosing retrieval failures in production hybrids?
  • What training dynamics make Gated DeltaNet and Mamba-2 converge on the same recurrent "address register"?
  • Do Jamba-, Nemotron-H-, and Kimi-Linear-style hybrids implement the same query-construction role — and does Transformer → hybrid distillation create or destroy Address blocks?
