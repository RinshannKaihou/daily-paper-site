Before the first token is generated, a small LM's hidden state already encodes refuse-vs-confabulate — a single direction flips the outcome.

小型语言模型在生成第一个 token 之前的隐藏状态中，就已编码“拒绝还是编造”的决策——晚期层的单一方向即可把大多数编造翻转为拒绝开场白，反向推动则让拒绝坍缩为编造、却不伤及本来正确的答案。

Geometry of Confabulation and Refusal in Small Language Models

Vladimir Kazei·independent, single-author·subjects: Qwen3-1.7B · Gemma 2 2B · Llama 3.2 3B

Key metric — hadds margin +7.4 pp

Refusal-vs-confabulation probe over the strongest prompt-only baseline: 0.820 → 0.894 accuracy at layer 28, Qwen3-1.7B — ~4× the fold-to-fold std. Gemma 2 2B: +16.3 pp.

one-shot push: refusal openers 10% → 100% on wrong items

Why this matters

Probing decodes confabulation-vs-refusal down to single features and shows hallucination-detection signals depend on post-training — direct guidance for reliable, refusable small models and internal-state detectors.

对构建可拒绝回答的小模型、以及基于内部状态的幻觉检测器设计，有直接指导意义。

⚠ Critical edge

As a mitigation knob, steering mostly teaches the model to say “As of my knowledge cutoff…” and answer anyway — only ~30% of forced openers complete as judged refusals.

01Methodology方法

784-Q prefill (ConfabQA)→ last-prompt hidden state · 29 layers × 2048→ linear probe vs 4 prompt baselines→ hadds margin→ α·w push @ token 1→ SAE 32,768-dict decomposition
  • Subjects: Qwen3-1.7B (primary), Gemma 2 2B, Llama 3.2 3B, plus Qwen3-4B as a within-family scaling control — greedy decoding, bf16, one Apple M1 Pro.
  • Probing: per layer, StandardScaler → PCA(16) → logistic regression under 5-fold stratified CV; every target scored against four escalating prompt-only baselines (TF-IDF → engineered features → +domain → +category oracle) fit on identical folds.
  • Causal test: a forward hook adds α·w to the last prompt token's residual during prefill; controls include norm-matched random directions, a shuffled-label direction, and prefix-forcing the literal opener.
  • Judging: three-way same-model judge (correct / refusal / wrong), cross-checked against Deep Research (κ=0.78, all 784) and Claude Opus 4.7 (30/30 stratified sample).

Credibility: code · data · judge labels released (MIT / CC BY 4.0) · LLM assistance disclosed · benchmark defects self-reported with counts · v2 fixes pre-registered.

  • SAE feature 2191 is the causal handle: fires on 100% of refusals vs 49% of wrongs; pushing its decoder flips 0/30 → 30/30 wrong-item next-token argmaxes to refusal openers (95% CI [0.88, 1.00]), saturating at 0.46× the state norm.
  • No confabulation feature exists in the 32,768-feature dictionary — on Qwen3-1.7B answering is the default pathway and abstention is the marked state, inverting the Gemma-2 / Claude-3.5-Haiku mechanism.
  • Correctness signal is post-training-dependent: Llama's margin survives dropping refusals (≈83% remains) and 81% transfers cross-dataset; the Qwen3-4B scaling control moves it <2 pp — family recipe, not parameter count.
  • Regime split at equal accuracy: all three score 30–32% correct on ConfabQA, yet Llama refuses 97.5% of post-cutoff failures while Qwen refuses ~⅓ (PopQA refusal census: 1.7% vs 62.9%).

03Strengths优点

  • The missing control, supplied: the prompt-feature baseline protocol shows Qwen3-1.7B's "correctness probe" collapses against text features — while Llama's does not. A field-correcting result for the probing literature.
  • Clean design idea: the obscure-pre-cutoff cell (153 items) decouples "answer was in training data" from "model gets it right" without losing the refusal population.
  • Converging causal evidence: four routes — probe direction, SAE feature 2191, optimized direction, prefix-forcing — all land at the same ~1-in-3 judged-refusal rate: complete mediation at token one.
  • Exceptional transparency: disclosed LLM assistance, self-reported benchmark defects with counts, released artifacts, pre-registered v2 fixes.

04Limitations局限

  • Opener ≠ abstention: the direction governs the opening speech act only — ~30% of forced-opener generations complete as judged refusals on Qwen; closing the gap is future work.
  • Small n throughout causal tests: 30-item subsets (12 confabulating items on Llama = "directionally suggestive"); the ~1-in-3 equivalence is explicitly "not powered as an equivalence test."
  • Benchmark hygiene: 22 wrong golds, 63 near-duplicate groups, and 107 "post-cutoff" items that actually predate the cutoff (74 by >1 month) — within-stratum numbers will shift in v2.
  • Scoped reads: probes see only the last prompt position; single SAE (EV 0.50, base-model-trained); same-family self-judge on cross-model outputs; no ≥7B replication; family vs post-training recipe confounded.

05Questions for further exploration待探索

  1. Q1Would a sustained per-token steering vector — not a one-shot prefill push — close the 30% opener→refusal gap without degrading fluency, and does it hold at ≥7B scale?
  2. Q2Does swapping the post-training recipe within a family (adding a Llama-style factuality stage to Qwen3) actually install the +25 pp correctness signal, or is architecture also required?
  3. Q3Do the epistemic-abstention direction found here and Arditi et al.'s (2024) safety-refusal direction coincide, partially overlap, or occupy orthogonal subspaces?
  4. Q4Is the absent confabulation feature an artifact of Qwen-Scope's EV 0.50 reconstruction (feature splitting / absorption), or would higher-EV dictionaries still find none?
  5. Q5Can Llama's portable correctness direction become a runtime hallucination detector beating semantic-entropy methods on latency, given it needs a single prefill hidden state?
  6. Q6How do findings transfer to known-unknowns benchmarks that elicit refusals from smaller models — the paper's refusal transfer test only had power at Llama scale?
