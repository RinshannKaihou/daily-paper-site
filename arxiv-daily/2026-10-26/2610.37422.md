> **一句话总结：** 通过因果干预（DAS）在 Gemma4-12B-it 的残差流中定位到一个 22 维因果子空间，发现文本与图像两种模态的算术表示不共享坐标方向（直接迁移仅恢复 53.2% 因果效应），但通过单个缩放旋转（αQ）即可对齐至 91.4%；该子空间可精确分解为 9 维"读出"子空间 D 与 13 维"潜在"子空间 L——L 与数字 unembedding 完全正交却以 75.5% top-1 检索携带数值信息，并在时间上提前缓冲完整答案、引导下一个数字的生成（77.6% 重定向）。

> **TL;DR:** Using Distributed Alignment Search on Gemma4-12B-it's residual stream (layer 43, 22-dim causal subspaces over two-digit addition/subtraction in text and image prompts), the authors show that cross-modal causal subspaces do NOT share coordinates — direct reuse recovers only 53.2% of the intervention effect on same-operation text↔image pairs — but a single scaled rotation (αQ, with α≈0.65 for text→image) restores 91.4% recovery on held-out expressions. Decomposing each subspace into a 9-dim digit-readout part D and a 13-dim latent part L (exactly orthogonal to digit unembeddings), L alone retrieves unseen numerical values at 75.5% top-1 (vs 5% chance and 38.3% for random 13-dim controls), all four conditions synchronize into one shared latent frame (74.8% top-1, nearly matching 73.4% for independently fitted pairwise maps), and mismatched interventions (D from answer y, L from answer ỹ sharing the first digit) leave the current digit intact (0.965 first-digit probability) while redirecting the NEXT digit to ỹ in 77.6% of cases — evidence that the model computes the full result upstream and unfolds it digit-by-digit. Replicated in Ministral-3-14B.

## 核心贡献

- **用因果干预而非相似度指标研究"表示趋同"**：指出 RSA/CKA/CCA 天然对旋转不变，无法区分"共享坐标方向"（Q1）与"共享几何但坐标不同"（Q2），也无法排除"只是共享输出词表"（Q3）这三种物理可能性；本文用 DAS 学到的因果子空间 + 跨条件干预迁移给出了可判决的实验。
- **否定坐标同一性、确立关系同构（Q1 否 / Q2 是）**：同操作跨模态直接复用因果子空间平均仅恢复 53.2% 的因果效应；但拟合一个缩放旋转 αQ 后恢复率提升到 91.4%，且该映射可跨"模态 × 运算"组合复合（链式复合 0.799 vs 直接拟合 0.807），位移余弦相似度 0.968–0.969。
- **发现视觉模态的几何压缩**：文本→图像的尺度 α ≈ 0.639–0.658，图像→文本 α ≈ 1.27–1.28，即图像条件下的数值位移系统性小于文本，也解释了直接迁移的不对称性（图像训练子空间在文本中恢复 57.7%，反之仅 33.4%）。
- **精确分解出"读出子空间 D + 潜在数值子空间 L"，排除输出接口伪影（Q3 否）**：将 22 维因果子空间按与数字 unembedding（秩 9）的对齐度旋转排序后正交分解；投影掉 D 使自干预准确率从 0.826/0.865/0.747/0.695 崩塌到 ≈0，但 13 维的 L 在与读出完全正交（重叠 O(10⁻⁷)）的情况下仍以 75.5% top-1 / 98.8% top-5 检索未见数值（随机控制 38.3%，打乱标签 5.2%）。
- **正交群同步证明存在单一共享潜在坐标系**：四个条件的 L_i 可用四组 (U_i, s_i) 因子化全部 12 个成对映射（参数从 948 降到 237），未见值检索 74.8% top-1，与独立拟合成对映射相当；扣留一对条件后仍能以 71.1% top-1 重建；而强行让同模态共享坐标系则崩塌到 18.1%。
- **揭示"上游计算、分步读出"的时间调度机制**：对共享首位数字的答案对，把 L 换成 ỹ 的值不改变当前写的数字（首位概率 0.963 vs 0.965），却使下一个数字以 77.6% 的 top-1 概率变成 ỹ 的个位；追踪 KV-cache 显示该信号在层 44→45 被注意力读出后转为读出方向（γ 从 0.113 跃升至 0.722，末层中位 0.852）。

## Key Contributions

- **Causal interventions instead of similarity metrics for representational convergence.** The paper formalizes three distinct physical meanings of "converged representations" — Q1 shared coordinates, Q2 shared geometry in rotated coordinates, Q3 output-interface artifact — and shows RSA/CKA/CCA cannot separate them (they are rotation-invariant by construction and blind to shared output vocabularies). All three are adjudicated with causal subspace transport inside a single multimodal model (Gemma4-12B-it, 48 layers, d_model=3840), avoiding cross-network alignment confounds.
- **Coordinate identity refuted, relational isomorphism established.** With DAS-learned 22-dimensional causal subspaces at layer 43 (final pre-generation token; 69.3–86.2% autoregressive interchange-intervention accuracy), direct reuse across modalities on the same operation recovers only 53.2% of the causal effect, while a single fitted scaled rotation (αQ) raises recovery to 91.4% across the four same-operation cross-modal directions; an unconstrained linear map only marginally improves this to 96.3%, and scaling without rotation collapses to 0.006.
- **A quantified "visual compression" regularity.** The fitted scales are governed by modality direction, not operation: text→image α ≈ 0.639±0.006 (addition) / 0.658±0.011 (subtraction) vs image→text 1.283±0.019 / 1.271±0.021 — numerical displacements in the visual modality are systematically ~0.65× their textual magnitude, plausibly explaining the asymmetric direct-transfer results (57.7% image→text vs 33.4% text→image).
- **Exact readout/latent decomposition that rules out an output artifact.** Splitting each causal subspace at rank 9 against the centered digit-unembedding basis U_dig yields a 9-dim readout subspace D carrying virtually all immediate causal power (projecting U_dig out collapses IIA from 0.826/0.865/0.747/0.695 to 0.008/0.000/0.005/0.003) and a 13-dim latent subspace L in the exact null space of the digit readout (overlap O(10⁻⁷)) that still retrieves unseen values at 75.5% top-1 / 98.8% top-5 vs 38.3% for dimension-matched random readout-orthogonal subspaces and 5.2% for shuffled labels; a no-fit RSA in L gives ρ = 0.765 vs 0.545 for random controls.
- **One shared latent frame via orthogonal group synchronization.** Jointly estimating four frames {U_i, s_i} that factorize all twelve directed maps (948 → 237 free parameters) preserves held-out retrieval at 74.8% top-1 (vs 73.4% for independently fitted pairwise maps); reconstructing a fully withheld condition pair from the remaining ten reaches 71.1% top-1, while forcing operations within a modality to share a frame collapses retrieval to 18.1% — the frames are condition-dependent yet globally consistent.
- **Temporal scheduling: L steers the NEXT digit, not the current one.** In mismatched states (D carries y, L carries ỹ, sharing the first digit), the current digit is untouched (first-digit probability 0.965 vs 0.963 matched) but the model emits ỹ at 69.2% (vs 8.9% random-L control); teacher-forcing the shared first digit shows the units digit of ỹ becomes greedy top-1 in 77.6% of cases (P(ỹ₂)=0.737 vs P(y₂)=0.040), while D alone leaves the second digit unresolved (0.132/0.087). The latent-to-readout conversion jumps between layers 44 and 45 (cosine 0.113 → 0.722), consistent with attention reading the modified KV-cache. A successor operator G₊₁ fitted in one condition transfers without refitting (65–70% y+1 identification).

## Methodology

Four experimental conditions cross input modality (text T, image I — identical expressions rendered as black text on white canvas) with operation (addition, subtraction), using operands in [0, 99] (5,050 expressions per operation; valid domains a+b≤99, a≥b). Evaluations are restricted to expressions the model answers correctly in both modalities. The model is instruction-tuned Gemma4-12B-it with thinking mode disabled, prompted to emit only the numerical result, so no chain-of-thought tokens enter the residual stream.

For each condition, Distributed Alignment Search (DAS) learns an orthonormal basis R (R⊤R = I) spanning a k-dimensional causal subspace of the residual stream at layer 43 at the final pre-generation token; k=22 is the smallest dimension retaining ≥90% of the k=32 baseline's autoregressive interchange-intervention accuracy (IIA^AR: fraction of interventions after which the model freely generates the complete multi-digit target). Notably, Fourier-aligned directions — the natural coordinates for arithmetic in prior work — are causally insufficient here: the 11 most Fourier-aligned directions recover only 23% of the effect in Gemma4-12B-it vs 84% in Llama3.1-8B, motivating analysis of the full learned subspace.

Cross-condition transport is measured with normalized recovery τ = (IIA(R_A→B) − IIA(R_rand→B)) / (IIA(R_B→B) − IIA(R_rand→B)), averaged over 3×3 seed pairs. Instead of aligning static activations, the authors align counterfactual displacement vectors Δz = R⊤(h_d − h_b) via a constrained least-squares fit of a scaled rotation (α>0, Q∈O(k)) — orthogonal rotation without shearing plus one uniform scale — evaluated strictly on held-out expressions.

For Q3, the centered digit-unembedding basis U_dig (rank 9) is orthonormalized; the causal subspace basis is rotated to order directions by alignment with U_dig and split at rank 9 into readout D and latent L with Π_D + Π_L = RR⊤. Latent geometry is probed via held-out transition cosines, nearest-neighbor retrieval among 20 candidates, and no-fit RSA. Global consistency is tested by orthogonal group synchronization (minimizing Σ ξ_ij‖Q_{j←i} − U_j U_i⊤‖²_F over four frames) plus weighted least squares on log α_{ji} ≈ log s_j − log s_i, with per-condition centroid offsets o_i added afterwards for absolute coordinates. Section 6 synthesizes mismatched hybrid activations h_mis = h_b + Δ^D_{b→y} + Δ^L_{b→ỹ} where y and ỹ share their first digit, then traces the delayed effect through the KV-cache at step t₁ by comparing readout-coordinate shifts against natural y→ỹ differences per layer. A full replication runs on Ministral-3-14B (Appendix F).

## Results & Highlights

- **Direct cross-modal reuse fails (Q1 refuted):** within-modality operation switches average τ = 77.3%, but the eight cross-modal pairs average only 45.5% (range 23.4–69.2%); same-operation cross-modal pairs average 53.2%. Transfer is directional: image-trained subspaces recover 57.7% in text vs 33.4% for text-trained subspaces in images.
- **Scaled rotations restore causal control (Q2 confirmed):** on the four same-operation cross-modal directions, rotation alone lifts normalized recovery from 0.532 to 0.718, and adding a single uniform scale α raises it to 0.914 (unconstrained linear map: 0.963; scaling without rotation: 0.006; random rotation with/without scaling: ≤0.005). Transported displacements reach cosine 0.902 (addition) / 0.915 (subtraction) with true destination displacements, and the maps compose: chaining modality and operation rotations achieves 0.799 recovery vs 0.807 for direct diagonal fitting (displacement cosine 0.968–0.969).
- **Visual modality compresses numerical geometry:** fitted scales are α = 0.639±0.006 (T⁺→I⁺) and 0.658±0.011 (T⁻→I⁻) for text→image, versus 1.283±0.019 and 1.271±0.021 for image→text — a stable ~0.65× compression of displacement magnitude in the visual condition, dictated by transfer direction rather than operation.
- **The latent subspace L carries number without touching the readout (Q3 refuted):** ablating the rank-9 digit-readout projection collapses self-intervention accuracy from 0.826/0.865/0.747/0.695 (T⁺, T⁻, I⁺, I⁻) to 0.008/0.000/0.005/0.003, yet the readout-orthogonal 13-dim L retrieves unseen values at 75.5% top-1 and 98.8% top-5 among 20 candidates (random readout-orthogonal 13-dim controls: 38.3%; shuffled labels: 5.2%; chance: 5%), with mean held-out transition cosine 0.807.
- **One global latent frame:** synchronized frames (237 parameters vs 948 for pairwise) achieve 74.8% top-1 / 0.791 cosine on unseen values, matching independent pairwise maps (73.4% / 0.801); reconstructing a withheld condition pair gives 71.1% top-1 / 98.3% top-5; forcing one frame per modality collapses retrieval to 18.1% (cosine 0.249); predicting an unseen condition by additive shifts yields 58.9% top-1 vs 3.8% under shuffled controls; a G₊₁ operator fitted in one condition identifies y+1 centroids in 65–70% of held-out cases without refitting.
- **L steers the next digit, not the current one:** with matched D+L edits the model produces the full target y at 79.9%; the mismatched state (D from y, L from ỹ) produces ỹ at 69.2% (random-L control: 8.9%) while the first-digit probability is essentially unchanged (0.965 vs 0.963). At step t₁ with the shared first digit teacher-forced, P(ỹ₂) = 0.737 vs P(y₂) = 0.040 and ỹ's units digit is greedy top-1 in 77.6% of 1,536 sampled triplets; D alone leaves the second digit unresolved (0.132/0.087). With pairs differing in both digits, the swap yields the hybrid (e.g., 57 from y=52, ỹ=67) in 51.6% of cases. The latent-to-readout conversion jumps at layer 45 (γ₄₄≈0.113 → γ₄₅≈0.722; median 0.852 at the final layer), and the full causal chain (readout-orthogonal geometry, global synchronization, delayed influence) reproduces in Ministral-3-14B.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** Genuinely novel framing. The decomposition of "representational convergence" into three testable hypotheses (Q1 coordinate identity / Q2 relational isomorphism / Q3 output artifact) and the use of causal-subspace transport with scaled rotations is a real methodological step beyond RSA/CKA-style comparisons and beyond prior arithmetic-circuit work (Fourier features, helical manifolds). The readout/latent split with exact orthogonality (O(10⁻⁷)) and the group-synchronization test of global consistency are elegant and, to my knowledge, new in this setting.
- **Rigor:** High. Nearly every claim has a matched control, normalization by destination difficulty (τ) avoids conflating alignment with task difficulty, maps are evaluated on held-out expressions, and the compositional/chained-transfer test (0.799 vs 0.807) is the right falsification for "is the geometry really factorized". The mismatched-intervention design (D from y, L from ỹ) is a clean causal demonstration of temporal scheduling.
- **Reproducibility caveats:** Code is "released soon" rather than released; prompts, layers, dimensions, seeds, and hyperparameters are said to be in appendices, which mitigates this, but independent verification will have to wait. The 22-dim subspace size is a heuristic choice (smallest k retaining ≥90% of the k=32 baseline IIA), and the D/L split inherits k=22 − rank-9 = 13 dimensions — the "13-dim latent space" is thus partly an artifact of that choice rather than a discovered dimensionality.
- **Selection bias in the data:** evaluation is restricted to expressions the model answers correctly in both modalities. This is defensible (isolating format from competence) but means the findings describe the model's "clean" arithmetic pathway only; how numbers are represented on error trials — arguably where interpretability matters most — is unaddressed. The scope is also narrow: two-digit addition/subtraction in [0,99]; multiplication is explicitly deferred (distributed, depth-dependent representations, Appendix B.2), and generalization to larger operands or other symbolic domains is untested.
- **Interpretive stretch:** the α≈0.65 "visual compression" is presented as a physical regularity, but the authors themselves concede the fitted scales "do not establish its architectural cause". Similarly, "one shared latent frame" is synchronization of twelve fitted linear maps — an impressive but still statistical notion of unity, and the specific attention heads performing the delayed readout are left unidentified.
- **Red flags:** none serious. The paper is careful to call Q1 negative, Q2 affirmative, Q3 refuted, and to hedge the compression story. The main risk is over-generalization in citation: this is evidence about two-digit arithmetic in two specific instruction-tuned multimodal models, not a general proof of the Platonic Representation Hypothesis.

## Strengths

- Replaces an ill-posed similarity question ("are the representations the same?") with three sharply separable, causally testable hypotheses, and answers each with a dedicated experiment.
- The scaled-rotation result with a single uniform α is a strong, quantitative law (0.532 → 0.914 recovery; α≈0.65 text→image) that is more informative than a binary "converged / not converged" verdict.
- The exact D/L decomposition and the finding that readout-orthogonal directions still retrieve numbers at 75.5% top-1 cleanly kills the "it's just the output token" objection that undermines much representation-similarity work.
- The temporal-scheduling experiment (mismatched D/L donors; KV-cache tracing; layer-44→45 conversion jump) turns a paradox (L encodes the number but barely moves the current digit) into a mechanistic story of latent planning with concrete numbers (77.6% next-digit redirection).
- Exceptional control discipline throughout: random-subspace, shuffled-label, scaling-only, random-rotation, withheld-pair, and cross-model (Ministral-3-14B) replication.

## Limitations

- Arithmetic scope is two-operand, two-digit addition and subtraction (operands in [0,99], 5,050 expressions per operation); multiplication, larger numbers, and non-arithmetic numeric reasoning (comparison, word problems) are out of scope, and the paper itself notes multiplication behaves differently.
- Analyses focus on one layer (43) and the final pre-generation token of one model family plus one replication model; whether the shared latent frame persists across depths or scales to other architectures is unverified.
- Correct-answer filtering restricts the studied distribution to problems the model solves in both modalities, excluding error cases.
- The causal subspaces and the D/L split are learned artifacts whose dimensionality (k=22, hence 13-dim L) depends on a retention-threshold heuristic; no independent justification of 13 as the "true" latent dimensionality is given.
- The mechanism of the delayed readout (which attention heads convert L into readout directions between layers 44 and 45) is identified only as a layer-range effect, not attributed to specific circuits.
- Code not yet released at submission time ("will be released soon"), so full independent reproduction is pending.

## Relevance

这篇论文用因果干预方法回答了"多模态模型是否共享同一个数的内部表示"这一表示趋同（Platonic Representation Hypothesis）核心问题，对理解多模态 LLM 的统一数值/符号机制以及"先算后说"的 latent planning 现象都有直接启示，与可解释性驱动的多模态模型机制分析方向高度契合。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the shared latent frame survive beyond [0,99] — e.g., 3–4 digit operands, decimals, or number words — or is the 13-dim latent geometry a small-number-specific solution?
- Which attention heads between layers 44 and 45 actually perform the L→D conversion via the KV-cache, and are they the same heads across all four conditions?
- Why are visual numerical displacements systematically compressed (α≈0.65)? Is it a property of the vision encoder's output norm, attention mixing, or training-data frequency of image-rendered arithmetic?
- Does the latent subspace L exist for multiplication, where the paper reports distributed depth-dependent representations instead — and if so, does it still synchronize across modalities?
- How do error trials look: on problems the model gets wrong, is L occupied by the wrong answer (a computed-but-misread-out result) or unstructured?
- Can the mismatched-intervention "next-digit redirection" result be exploited for inference-time arithmetic steering, e.g., correcting carries in long addition without re-computation?
