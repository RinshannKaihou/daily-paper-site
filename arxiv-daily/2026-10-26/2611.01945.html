Key metric · causal, held-out

52–67%

of correct predictions flip when relation-sensitive heads are ablated

Cross-fitted, held-out ablation across four open-weight 7–8B models — vs only 2–5% for time-sensitive heads and 8–12% for contribution-matched random heads.

Why this matters

Shows how context selects among competing stored facts inside an LM — groundwork for knowledge editing, temporal QA, and context-conditioned recall you can trust.

Critical edge

The two-route story is incomplete by its own measurement — the routes carry only 69–90% of the anchor's effect, and every prompt the model answered wrongly was excluded from analysis.

01Methodology & mechanism

Four causal interventions on year-minimal pairs

The core device is a reference–contrast minimal pair — two prompts differing only in the anchor year — dissected with attention knockout, activation patching, cross-fitted head ablation, and value injection.

Task
Anchored factual recall: (anchor, subject, relation, object) quadruples — “In [year], [subject] [relation].” with 5 demos sharing no year, subject, or relation.
Data
TempLAMA test set, 34,962 Wikidata-derived instances (years 2010–2020); only groups whose gold object changes across years and the model answers correctly for ≥2 differing years.
Models
Llama-3.1-8B · Qwen2.5-7B · Ministral-8B · DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B.
  1. Q1Entry points — attention knockout on S←A / R←A / L←A edges over 9-layer windows.
  2. Q2What changes — activation patching of the final subject-token state, plus a shared-year-offset control against a generic “year vector”.
  3. Q3Readout & action — relation/time-swap TV distance; cross-fitted head ablation (5 folds, top-10% heads, held-out); value injection + MLP pinning + DLA at the final position.
  4. +Additivity — blocking anchor attention (both / subject-only / direct-only / none) to test GS + GL = Gboth.

Single 48GB NVIDIA A40 · interventions ≈ 150× the forward passes of plain prediction.

02Results & highlights

Beyond the headline flip rate

The anchor's effect is selective (entity-specific, not a generic year code), essentially all MLP-carried on the direct route (RMLP 0.37–0.49 vs Rnon-MLP ≤ 0.01), and systematically re-balanced by the relation type.

03Strengths

  • S1Clean formalization — separates anchored selection among competing facts from plain retrieval; minimal pairs make it operational.
  • S2Converging evidence — four independent intervention types (knockout, patching, cross-fitted ablation, value injection), replicated across four model families.
  • S3Real controls — matched-random heads (8–12% flips expose how misleading raw ablations are), time-sensitive heads, shared-year-offset, compensation tests.
  • S4Falsifiable numbers — additivity tests with corrections and cross-model correlations, not qualitative narrative alone.
  • S5Mechanism ↔ query link — P39 vs P54 route shares move oppositely under patching vs injection, tying mechanism to query properties.

04Limitations

  • L1Narrow scope — English only, TempLAMA only, one prompt format, year-only anchors (2010–2020); dates, events, locations untested.
  • L2Order confound — the anchor must precede the subject under causal attention, so the subject route partly reflects English word order.
  • L3Scale — only 7–8B models; smaller ones fail the correctness filter, larger ones are too costly (~150× forward passes).
  • L4Success-only lens — incorrectly answered and ambiguous-temporal instances are excluded; nothing learned about failure modes.
  • L5Incomplete account — routes explain 69–90%; the residual is quantified but uncharacterized. No code release.

05Questions for further exploration

  • Do the routes persist when the anchor follows the subject (natural in Japanese or German), or when the anchor is a date, event, or location?
  • What carries the residual 10–31% of the anchor's effect — the relation span at untested layer windows, or non-query positions?
  • Do the same routes govern wrong answers — e.g., stale-fact defaults to a historically prominent club despite the anchor?
  • Can the route-balance signature steer model editing per query — ROME-style subject edits vs final-position MLP edits?
  • Under in-context conflict (demonstrations asserting a different year), does the direct route's anchor readout dominate?
