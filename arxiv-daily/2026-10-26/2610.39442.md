> **一句话总结：** 本文用连续时间梯度流严格刻画了 MoE softmax 路由的坍缩与恢复：在固定专家时用"平稳损失势垒"识别路由收敛到的单纯形面，在专家可训练时证明小学习率下坍缩可在有限时间内持续而长期极限必为内点，并给出精确的法向速度分解 ẋ = x(1−x)²Γ_j（把互补专家协方差与相对梯度优势分离），据此把恢复时间分类为 Θ(log(1/ε)) 或 Θ(ε^{−α})；在一个 6 层、8 专家、top-2 的 Transformer-MoE（AG News，约 50–55M 参数）配对实验中，消除类条件路由梯度对比使确认恢复的受限平均时间延长 44.25 步（无 drop，p=9.77×10⁻⁴）/ 59.00 步（capacity 1.25，p=7.81×10⁻³）。

> **TL;DR:** A theory-first study of why softmax routing in MoE models concentrates on a few experts and how suppressed experts recover, using continuous-time gradient flow on a common-target square-loss model. Key results: (1) a stationary-loss barrier identifies which simplex face fixed-expert routing converges to; (2) with trainable experts at rate η, routing tracks fixed-expert dynamics as O(η) on finite horizons (empirically fitted slope 1.03) yet converges to a strictly interior point for every η > 0 with residual ‖R(t)‖² ≤ ‖R(0)‖²e^{−2ηt/n}; (3) an exact normal-velocity decomposition ẋ = x(1−x)²Γ_j splits recovery into complementary-expert covariance vs relative gradient advantage, yielding Θ(log(1/ε)) vs Θ(ε^{−1}) recovery times — with squared-Jacobian (Euclidean logit) flow a factor ~1/(2ε) slower than single-Jacobian (mirror-descent) flow and unbounded vs Θ(1) for projected probability flow. Numerical hitting times match theoretical integrals to < 4×10⁻⁷ relative error, and paired interventions on a 6-layer/8-expert/top-2 Transformer-MoE (AG News) show removing a class-conditional router-gradient contrast delays confirmed recovery by +44.25 steps (95% CI [31.25, 57.50], no-drop) and +59.00 steps (capacity 1.25) versus control RMST of 796.25/810.50 steps.

## 核心贡献

- **平稳损失势垒与面选择定理（固定专家）**：对固定专家矩阵 E，最优路由输出是目标 y 在专家凸包上的投影；只要轨迹进入"低于最低次优平稳损失"的严格子水平集，所有极限点都表示该投影，且当最优面为真面时面外质量 D_{F*}(p(t)) → 0。推论给出面外质量被损失盈余控制的显式界（暴露向量系数 ‖v‖/κ_F），以及有限时间进入最优面邻域的入口时间上界 (L(p(t₀))−r*²)/(2µ(c,δ))。
- **专家自适应的双时间尺度**：证明了慢专家近似（sup_{t≤T}‖p_η−p₀‖ ≤ C_T·η）与内点收敛定理——对任意有限初始化和任意固定 η > 0，联合平方损失流的残差以 e^{−2ηt/n} 指数衰减、logits 与专家输出均收敛、每个专家概率有显式下界。由此得到"瞬态面集中 + 内点长期极限"的严格表述：有限时间内坍缩可以持续，但长期极限必有严格为正的面外质量。
- **精确法向速度分解与恢复时间分类（主结果）**：对被抑制概率 x = p_j，ẋ = x(1−x)²Γ_j，其中 Γ_j = Λ_j + x(1+‖w‖²₂)Δ_j 把互补专家加权协方差 Λ_j 与相对梯度优势 Δ_j 精确分离。若沿恢复路径 Γ_j ≍ x^α（常数与 ε 无关），则从初始质量 ε 恢复到阈值 θ 的时间为 Θ(log(1/ε))（α=0）或 Θ(ε^{−α})（α>0）。两专家下三种优化几何（平方 Jacobian / 单 Jacobian / 欧氏投影概率流）在同优势信号下分别给出 Θ(1/ε)、Θ(log(1/ε))、Θ(1) 恢复时间。
- **离散 log-odds 恒等式桥接理论与实际优化器**：对任意 logit 增量（不要求梯度流），logit(p_j^N) = logit(p_j^0) + Σ(u_j^m − c_j^m)，第 N 步前恢复当且仅当累计相对 logit 运动达到 B_{θ,ε} = log(θ(1−ε)/((1−θ)ε))——把 AdamW 更新逐 token 地与阈值穿越精确对应。
- **配对 Transformer-MoE 干预实验**：在匹配参数、优化器状态与 minibatch 顺序的配对训练中，正交投影消除目标专家与对比专家之间的类条件路由梯度对比（G_j, G_c ← 平均），使确认恢复延迟、固定 token 的累计 log-odds 降低，在无 drop 与 capacity 1.25 两种派发策略下均成立——把"训练梯度的特定改变"与"恢复所需的法向运动"连接起来。

## Key Contributions

- **Stationary-loss barrier for face selection (fixed experts).** The optimal routed output is the projection of the target y onto the expert convex hull C(E); Theorem 3.1 shows that once a trajectory enters the strict sublevel below the lowest suboptimal stationary loss ℓ_comp, every limit point represents this projection and outside-face mass vanishes when the optimal hull face G* is proper. Corollaries give a neighborhood that guarantees entry at t = 0 (Cor 3.2), a loss-excess bound on outside-face mass via an exposing vector with separation factor ‖v‖/κ_F (Cor 3.3), and a finite-time entry bound (Prop 3.4).
- **Two time scales of expert adaptation.** Theorem 4.1: with expert-to-router rate η, the joint flow tracks the fixed-expert trajectory with error O(η) on any fixed horizon. Theorem 4.2: for every fixed η > 0 and any finite initialization, the joint square-loss flow has residual decay ‖R(t)‖² ≤ ‖R(0)‖²e^{−2ηt/n}, convergent logits/experts, and an explicit per-expert probability floor — so the long-time routing limit is strictly interior. Corollary 4.3 formalizes transient concentration: near-face collapse at finite times coexists with an interior limit.
- **Exact normal-velocity decomposition and recovery-time classification (main result).** For a suppressed probability x = p_j, ẋ = x(1−x)²Γ_j with Γ_j = Λ_j + x(1+‖w‖²₂)Δ_j, exactly separating complementary-expert covariance (Λ_j, which can drive recovery even at zero mean advantage) from relative gradient advantage (Δ_j). Two-sided power bounds Γ_j ≍ x^α with ε-independent constants give T_θ(ε) = Θ(log(1/ε)) for α = 0 and Θ(ε^{−α}) for α > 0. For two experts under the same bounded advantage signal, squared-Jacobian (Euclidean logit-gradient), single-Jacobian (replicator/mirror-descent), and Euclidean projected-probability flows yield Θ(1/ε), Θ(log(1/ε)), and Θ(1) recovery times respectively — the optimization geometry alone changes the recovery order.
- **Exact discrete log-odds identity for neural training.** Proposition 6.2 holds for arbitrary optimizer increments (not just gradient flow): logit(p_j^N) equals initial log-odds plus the sum of per-step relative logit motions (u_j^m − c_j^m); recovery by step N occurs iff the maximal cumulative sum reaches B_{θ,ε}. This converts observed AdamW routing changes into the exact coordinates that determine per-token threshold crossing.
- **Paired Transformer-MoE intervention.** A matched-pair design (identical initial parameters, optimizer states, minibatch orders; seeds as independent units) removes a class-conditional router-gradient contrast by orthogonally projecting the target and comparator row gradients onto their common average. The intervention delays confirmed recovery and reduces accumulated log-odds on fixed tokens, both without token dropping and with capacity factor 1.25 — connecting a specific gradient-direction change to the normal motion needed for recovery.

## Methodology

The analysis is built on continuous-time gradient flow for softmax routing. For routing vector p with softmax Jacobian J(p) = diag(p) − pp⊤, Euclidean gradient flow on logits, q̇ = −J(p)g with g = ER, transports to probability space as ṗ = −J(p)²E(E⊤p − y) (squared-Jacobian flow, Eq 2.1); coupling expert updates at rate η gives the joint flow (2.2). Because the jth row of J vanishes as p_j ↓ 0, normal probability motion degenerates near the simplex boundary ("boundary degeneracy") — the mechanism behind persistent suppression.

Section 3 (fixed experts) combines loss dissipation (dL/dt ≤ 0), LaSalle's invariance principle, and convex projection geometry: stationary points on a face correspond to restricted-hull projections z_S, so a sublevel below ℓ_comp excludes all suboptimal stationary supports, forcing convergence to the projection representation set P*; exposing-vector arguments convert loss excess into bounds on outside-face mass, and a compactness-based minimum dissipation rate µ(c,δ) bounds entry times.

Section 4 (trainable experts) first uses Gronwall estimates on the polynomial vector field for the O(η) finite-horizon approximation, then exploits the dissipation identity Ḣ = −‖J(p)ER‖² − η‖p‖²‖R‖² ≤ −(2η/n)H (using ‖p‖² ≥ 1/n) to get exponential residual decay, bounded expert motion, global existence, and convergence for any η > 0 — extending the value–softmax analysis of Varre et al. to arbitrary finite initialization with explicit probability floors.

Section 5 derives the recovery theory: differentiating the singleton log-odds gives the exact identity ẋ = x(1−x)²Γ_j; separation of variables under power bounds on Γ_j yields the log vs power-law classification, with structural criteria (Cor 5.3) for the covariance-driven logarithmic regime and the advantage-driven Θ(ε^{−1}) regime.

Section 6 handles input conditioning: a first-order separation criterion for shared affine routers (initial routing-difference rate = ‖A₀(h_k − h_ℓ)‖, zero when feature contrasts lie in null(A₀)), and the discrete log-odds identity applicable to arbitrary optimizer increments.

Section 7 validates: (i) reduced-flow integrations of constructed fields against theoretical hitting-time integrals; (ii) face-selection and O(η)-tracking experiments in four fixed-expert geometries; (iii) paired interventions on a 6-layer Transformer-MoE (width 384, 6 heads, eight two-layer GELU experts of hidden width 768 per layer, top-2 routing, ~50–55M parameters, bfloat16) trained with AdamW on AG News with class-balanced sampling and no auxiliary load balancing. The target expert (smallest mean pre-dispatch probability among experts with mean ≥ 0.05) is suppressed and calibrated to ε ∈ {0.005, 0.01, 0.02, 0.04} via a scalar router-bias shift; the treatment branch averages the class-conditional router-row gradients of target and comparator; recovery is "confirmed" at two consecutive 64-step evaluations with mean target probability ≥ 0.05 over 1,024 steps, summarized by restricted mean survival time (RMST) with paired-seed bootstrap CIs and exact sign tests.

## Results & Highlights

- **Router-gradient projection delays confirmed recovery (Table 2).** On the 6-layer/8-expert/top-2 Transformer-MoE (AG News), removing the class-conditional router-gradient contrast increases restricted mean time to confirmed recovery by +44.25 optimizer steps without token dropping (95% CI [31.25, 57.50]; exact sign-test p = 9.77×10⁻⁴; control RMST 796.25 vs projection 840.50, 16 paired seeds) and by +59.00 steps with capacity factor 1.25 (95% CI [39.50, 81.00]; p = 7.81×10⁻³; control 810.50 vs projection 869.50, 8 paired seeds). By the 1,024-step horizon, projection reduces the fraction with confirmed recovery by 8.59 percentage points under both dispatch policies.
- **Token-level log-odds drop (Table 3).** On fixed, initially subthreshold tokens of the selected class at step 1,024 (8 paired seeds per setting), projection reduces cumulative log-odds by −0.194 (95% CI [−0.252, −0.133]) under no-drop and −0.228 ([−0.304, −0.140]) under capacity 1.25, and reduces the fraction of tokens meeting the logit(0.05) threshold by −1.06 pp ([−1.64, −0.54]) and −2.28 pp ([−3.71, −0.82]) respectively — direct evidence in the coordinates of the discrete recovery identity (Prop 6.2).
- **Numerics match theory to machine-level accuracy.** In constructed recovery fields, numerical hitting times agree with the theoretical integrals H_{1+α}(ε,θ) to relative error below 4×10⁻⁷; fitted recovery-time exponents match the prescribed vanishing orders of Γ_j = x^α (log-fit slope 1.00 at α = 0), and the finite-horizon routing error scales linearly in η with fitted slope 1.03, confirming the O(η) prediction of Theorem 4.1.
- **Face selection verified across four geometries (Table 1).** Interior trajectories enter the strict sublevel and concentrate on the predicted face: outside-face mass at T = 2000 is 1.62×10⁻³ (triangle), 1.08×10⁻³ (pentagon edge), 1.53×10⁻³ (redundant hexagon), 2.51×10⁻³ (cube bottom facet) — versus 320×10⁻³ outside mass and 482×10⁻³ projection error for the boundary-initialized control that never enters (barrier gaps 0.0400–0.4880, entry times 5.38–91.78).
- **Sharp theoretical recovery dichotomy.** Under identical bounded advantage signals, two-expert recovery from initial mass ε to threshold θ takes Θ(1/ε) under squared-Jacobian flow (Euclidean logit gradient descent), Θ(log(1/ε)) under single-Jacobian flow (replicator/mirror descent), and Θ(1) under Euclidean projected probability flow — e.g., I₂(ε,θ) = 1/(2ε) + O(log(1/ε)) vs I₀ = 2θ + O(ε); and Theorem 4.2 guarantees residual decay ‖R(t)‖² ≤ ‖R(0)‖²e^{−2ηt/n} with an explicit probability floor p_i(t) ≥ p_i(0)/(p_i(0) + e^{2C₀}(1−p_i(0))) for every fixed η > 0.
- **Routing structure by dataset (Appendix C.3).** Input-conditioned top-2 routing yields mean between-group ℓ1 deviation 0.266 ± 0.058 on AG News, 0.804 ± 0.114 on Digits, and 1.150 ± 0.067 on UCI HAR, versus exactly 0 for shared-global routers (deviation increases positive in all ten AG News/Digits pairs and all twelve HAR pairs).

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty.** The recovery-time question ("how long does a suppressed expert take to regain a threshold?") is genuinely under-studied; the exact normal-velocity decomposition separating covariance from advantage, and the observation that optimization geometry alone (squared- vs single-Jacobian vs projected flow) changes recovery from Θ(1/ε) to Θ(log(1/ε)) to Θ(1), is a crisp new result. The stationary-loss barrier cleanly extends active-set/face-selection ideas to softmax routing. The discrete log-odds identity is simple but powerful — exact for arbitrary optimizer increments.
- **Rigor.** High for the theory: proofs are complete, constants tracked, and the classification assumptions (Γ_j ≍ x^α with ε-independent constants along the entire recovery path) are stated honestly. The gap is that these pathwise two-sided bounds are essentially unverifiable in a naturally evolving neural system — the exponent-correspondence experiment (Fig 2c) validates them only on constructed fields where Γ_j = x^α is imposed by construction, so it is closer to a numerical sanity check than an empirical test of the theory's applicability.
- **Model gap between theory and neural experiment.** All theorems concern a common-target square-loss model with a shared (non-input-conditioned except in Sec 6) router and freely trainable expert output vectors — no cross-entropy loss, no token-conditioned FFN experts, no top-k dispatch. The bridge is the intervention experiment, but there the observed quantity (mean probability/log-odds) is tied to theory only through the exact identity, not through the recovery-time classification. The authors acknowledge this ("estimating recovery coefficients in naturally evolving systems… would extend the continuous analysis").
- **Experimental scale and scope.** The neural evidence comes from one ~50–55M-parameter model on AG News — a topic-classification corpus repurposed for next-token LM training — with no standard LM pretraining corpus, no pretrained/large MoE, and no comparison against the load-balancing baselines the theory implicitly competes with (training deliberately uses no auxiliary loss or router noise, so the practical question "does this explain/predict recovery under standard Switch/GShard-style training" is untested). The capacity-constrained arm has only 8 paired seeds.
- **Intervention confound.** The treatment replaces the class-conditional row gradients of target and comparator with their average; this removes the contrast but can also change gradient norms. The authors themselves flag norm-matched gradient controls and independent evaluation inputs as missing, so the effect cannot yet be cleanly attributed to the contrast per se rather than to a magnitude change (though the paired design controls everything else).
- **Red flags.** None serious — no overclaiming of LM-scale relevance, limitations are stated in the Discussion. Minor: RMST is introduced as a post hoc summary; the 8.59 pp confirmed-fraction effect is reported without a CI in the main text (the underlying curves are in Fig 3).

## Strengths

- Exact (non-heuristic) decomposition of recovery dynamics, with a complete classification theorem and structural corollaries that name interpretable quantities (covariance Λ_j, advantage Δ_j, face separation ‖v‖/κ_F).
- Resolves the apparent paradox between finite-horizon collapse and interior long-time limits via a clean two-timescale argument with explicit rates and probability floors for arbitrary finite initialization (improving on prior value–softmax results that assumed zero initialization).
- The discrete log-odds identity makes the theory measurable under real AdamW training — an unusually direct theory–experiment bridge for this literature.
- Careful experimental methodology: matched pairs (parameters, optimizer state, minibatch order), seeds as independent units, bootstrap CIs with exact sign tests, robustness checks at alternative thresholds, code released.
- Numerical verification of the theory to < 4×10⁻⁷ relative error demonstrates the integrations actually solve the derived equations.

## Limitations

- The theory applies to a common-target square-loss model with freely trainable expert outputs; direct implications for cross-entropy-trained, token-routed Transformer MoEs at scale are suggestive rather than proven.
- Recovery-time bounds require two-sided power bounds on Γ_j along the whole recovery path with ε-independent constants — an assumption with no known estimator in realistic training, acknowledged by the authors.
- Neural validation is limited to one small (~50–55M) model on AG News with 8–16 paired seeds; no large-scale or pretrained MoE, no standard LM benchmarks, and no interaction with auxiliary load-balancing losses (training explicitly excludes them).
- Recovery is defined in probability space (pre-dispatch probability ≥ 0.05); with top-k dispatch, a suppressed expert's actual selection can differ from its probability, and the paper does not measure assignment-level recovery separately.
- The exponent-correspondence and recovery-scaling experiments use constructed vector fields, so they verify the math rather than test the theory's assumptions in learned systems.

## Relevance

对理解与稳定 MoE 大模型训练中的路由坍缩/负载不均有直接理论价值：法向速度分解、恢复时间分类（log vs 幂律）与离散 log-odds 恒等式为设计路由器优化几何（如镜像下降式路由、偏置校正）和诊断"被压死专家"的恢复时长提供了可计算的工具；对从事稀疏 MoE 训练动力学与理论分析的工作者尤其相关。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Can Γ_j (or its discrete analogue u_j^m − c_j^m) be estimated online during realistic pretraining, and do naturally occurring fields exhibit α ≈ 0 (logarithmic recovery) or α > 0 (power-law) — i.e., which regime do production MoE routers actually live in?
- Does the Θ(1/ε) vs Θ(log(1/ε)) gap between squared-Jacobian and single-Jacobian flows predict concrete gains from mirror-descent-style router parameterizations (e.g., exponentiated-gradient updates on router logits) in standard Switch/GShard training with auxiliary load-balancing losses?
- How does the recovery analysis interact with auxiliary load-balancing losses and router noise (z-loss, jitter), which the experiments deliberately exclude — do they change Γ_j's vanishing order or merely shift constants?
- Does the class-conditional gradient-contrast mechanism operate in token-level (rather than class-level) routing in large pretrained MoEs, and does suppressing it there also delay recovery of dead experts?
- The interior-convergence result assumes a common target; with input-dependent targets (real LM data), can persistent boundary concentration actually occur in the long-time limit, or is collapse there a finite-horizon phenomenon throughout?
- Can the entry-time bound (Prop 3.4) be inverted into a practical early-warning signal — using measured dissipation ‖J(p)E(E⊤p−y)‖² and barrier gap ℓ_comp − r*² to predict when a router will lock onto a suboptimal face before it happens?
