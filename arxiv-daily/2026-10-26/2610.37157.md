> **一句话总结：** 用线性 CKA、logit lens、注意力分解、SAE 特征统计和激活补丁系统对比 GPT-2 与其 6 层学生 DistilGPT-2 的两个经典电路，发现"压缩–坍塌二分"：跨 12 层多阶段的 IOI 电路彻底坍塌（准确率 35.2% vs 教师 94.8%，logit 差反号为 −0.467），而 Greater-Than 电路通过把教师第 9 层比较头主动重映射到学生第 4 层（逐样本注意力 Pearson r = 0.945）恢复了 87.7% 的教师性能——证明困惑度等聚合指标完全诊断不了电路完整性。

> **TL;DR:** This paper asks what depth-halving distillation does to verified circuits, probing GPT-2 (12L, 124M) vs DistilGPT-2 (6L, 82M) with debiased linear CKA, tuned logit lens, attention-pattern decomposition, GPT-2-pretrained SAEs (gpt2-small-res-jb), and activation/path patching on the canonical IOI task (1,000 ABC-template prompts) and Greater-Than task (1,000 year-comparison prompts), plus 1,000 TinyStories samples for representational probes. The answer is a compression–collapse dichotomy: on IOI the student collapses to 35.2% accuracy vs the teacher's 94.8% with a sign-reversed mean logit difference of −0.467 (systematically wrong, below 50% chance, p < 0.001) and ~160× weaker peak DLA (0.0168 vs 2.74), while on Greater-Than it retains 87.3% accuracy vs 94.0% (GT-probability mass 0.750 vs 0.855 = 87.7% recovery) by actively remapping the teacher's layer-9 comparison head into student layer 4 (L4H1↔L9H1, mean Pearson r = 0.945). All this happens while perplexity looks "merely degraded" (TinyStories 17.55 vs 11.54, +52.1%; WikiText-2 49.33 vs 33.02, +49.4%) — the paper's core message that aggregate metrics are blind to mechanistic integrity. Worth reading for the crisp single-head remapping evidence; limited to one 124M-scale teacher–student pair and two circuits.

## 核心贡献

- **首个针对"深度削减蒸馏"的系统性电路级对比研究**：在 GPT-2（12 层，124M）与其学生 DistilGPT-2（6 层，82M）之间，用五类探针（去偏线性 CKA、tuned logit lens、注意力模式分解、预训练 GPT-2 SAE 特征统计、激活/路径补丁 + 直接 logit 归因 DLA）追踪两个被因果验证过的电路——间接宾语识别（IOI, Wang et al. 2022）与大于比较（Greater-Than, Hanna et al. 2023）——在蒸馏鸿沟两侧的存留情况。
- **发现"压缩–坍塌二分"（compression-collapse dichotomy）**：IOI 电路（24 个头中 19 个位于学生不存在的第 6–11 层）彻底坍塌——准确率 35.2% vs 94.8%，logit 差从 +2.942 反号为 −0.467，模型被系统性误导而非仅仅不确定；GT 电路则主动重映射存活——教师第 9 层比较头 L9H1 的功能被迁移到学生第 4 层 L4H1（逐样本注意力全模式 Pearson r = 0.945±0.079，中位数 0.981，93.6% 样本 r>0.8）。
- **证明学生是"压缩"而非"截断"教师的表征轨迹**：同层号匹配下第 5 层 CKA 崩至 0.314，但按比例映射（d→⌊d·11/5⌉）后恢复到 0.790，说明学生第 5 层对应教师第 11 层而非第 5 层——学生用更少的层数走完教师整条表征轨迹。
- **提出"电路可压缩性"解释框架**：关键计算能装进学生可用深度的电路存活（GT），流水线超出可用深度的电路结构性死亡（IOI）——不是优雅退化而是结构性不完整。
- **方法论上论证聚合指标失效**：学生在 TinyStories 困惑度 +52.1% 的情况下仍产出流利文本并保留 GT 电路 87.7% 性能，但同时彻底丢失 IOI——困惑度与准确率均无法诊断电路级损伤；建议蒸馏目标在功能关键层对齐中间表征。

## Key Contributions

- **First circuit-level audit of depth-reduction distillation**: systematically compares GPT-2 (12L, 124M) and DistilGPT-2 (6L, 82M) across five probe families — debiased linear CKA (unbiased HSIC estimator), tuned logit lens, attention-pattern decomposition, feature statistics under pre-trained GPT-2 SAEs (gpt2-small-res-jb), and causal activation/path patching with direct logit attribution — over two of the most causally validated circuits in any LM: Indirect Object Identification (IOI; Wang et al. 2022) and Greater-Than (GT; Hanna et al. 2023).
- **The compression–collapse dichotomy**: the IOI circuit — a multi-stage pipeline where 19 of 24 heads (all name-mover, S-inhibition, backup and negative name-mover classes) live in layers 6–11 that the student lacks — collapses completely (35.2% vs 94.8% accuracy, sign-reversed logit difference −0.467 vs +2.942), whereas the GT circuit survives by actively remapping the teacher's layer-9 comparison head into student layer 4 (L4H1 replicates L9H1's full attention pattern with mean Pearson r = 0.945 ± 0.079, median 0.981, 93.6% of 1,000 samples with r > 0.8).
- **"Compresses rather than truncates" evidence for the representational trajectory**: under same-index layer matching, layer-5 CKA collapses to 0.314 (cosine 0.385), but under proportional mapping (student layer d ↔ teacher layer ⌊d·11/5⌉) it recovers to 0.790 — the student's final layer resembles the teacher's layer 11, not layer 5; logit lens relative accuracy exceeds 1 at every proportional pair except the last (r up to 2.10 at D1/G2).
- **Circuit compressibility as the governing factor**: circuits whose critical computation fits within the student's available depth survive; pipelines extending beyond it die structurally. The IOI failure is structural incompleteness, not graceful degradation (induction heads retain a small causal effect, Cohen's d = 0.298, but with no downstream name-movers the pipeline cannot complete).
- **Demonstrates aggregate-metric blindness**: with TinyStories perplexity only +52.1% (17.55 vs 11.54) and fluent output, the student still loses an entire verified reasoning circuit — perplexity and aggregate accuracy cannot diagnose circuit integrity.

## Methodology

**Models.** Teacher: GPT-2 (12 layers, d_model = 768, 12 heads, ~117M params). Student: DistilGPT-2 (6 layers, same width, ~82M params), initialized from every other teacher layer and trained with a combined LM + soft-label distillation objective (Sanh et al. 2019). All IOI heads in layers 6–11 and GT heads in layers 9–11 have no structural counterpart in the student.

**Representational probes (RQ1).** (1) Debiased linear CKA between mean-pooled residual-stream activations with the unbiased HSIC estimator, under two layer-correspondence schemes: *same-index* (ℓ ∈ {0,3,5}) and *proportional* (student layer d ↔ teacher ⌊d·11/5⌉, giving G0/D0 … G11/D5). (2) Tuned logit lens: project intermediate hidden states through the final LayerNorm + unembedding, computing next-token accuracy and relative accuracy r_ℓ per layer. (3) Attention-pattern decomposition: per-head Shannon entropy, top-k mass, structural head-type markers (BOS/previous-token/EOS weights), and cross-model per-head cosine similarity of flattened T×T attention matrices. (4) SAE feature analysis: pre-trained GPT-2 SAEs (gpt2-small-res-jb, via SAELens) applied to both models as a *fixed reference basis*, with streaming computation of activation rate, L0 sparsity change, per-feature survival (Pearson r), and magnitude drift δ_f.

**Causal circuit probes (RQ2).** IOI task: 1,000 clean + 1,000 corrupted ABC prompts over 15 templates; metric = logit difference between indirect-object and subject name. Head-level activation patching (clean activations into corrupted run) over the 7 student-available heads, path patching over the 3 intra-depth paths, DLA over all components, and a name-mover matching procedure: top-20 student heads by DLA are matched to each of the 5 teacher name-mover heads via CKA + attention correlation on a 500-prompt discovery set, then confirmed on a held-out 500-prompt evaluation set, with a name-mover score quantifying IO-selective attention. GT task: 1,000 clean/corrupted "The [noun] lasted from the year [XX][YY] to the year [XX]" prompts; metric = probability mass on years greater than YY; head patching normalized by a noise ceiling; the same discovery/evaluation matching tests replication of the teacher's comparison head (9,1). Statistics: paired t-tests and Wilcoxon tests with Bonferroni correction, effect sizes as Cohen's d; CKA reported across seeds {42, 123, 7}. Infrastructure: TransformerLens on a single NVIDIA L4 GPU; representational probes use n = 1,000 TinyStories validation samples filtered to 64–256 tokens.

## Results & Highlights

- **IOI circuit: complete mechanistic collapse with sign reversal.** On the IOI task (1,000 prompts, logit-difference metric), GPT-2 scores 94.8% accuracy with mean logit difference +2.942 ± 1.797; DistilGPT-2 scores 35.2% with −0.467 ± 1.335 — below the 50% chance level (one-sided binomial test, n = 1,000, p < 0.001), i.e., a 59.6-point accuracy drop where the student actively prefers the (twice-appearing) subject name, a failure mode invisible to perplexity.
- **IOI DLA and name-mover matching confirm zero remapping.** The student's peak layer DLA contribution is 0.0168 vs GPT-2's 2.74 at layer 9 (≈160× smaller); activation patching of the 7 available heads gives negligible recovery (induction heads: Cohen's d = 0.298, p < 0.001; critical PT→IND pathway non-significant: d = 0.031, p = 0.329); name-mover preservation is at most 0.0% for every matched head (two matches are negative, −14.6% and −21.3%) despite moderate CKA (0.18–0.52) and attention correlations up to r = 0.78.
- **Greater-Than circuit: survives at 87.7% via active remapping.** DistilGPT-2 keeps 87.3% accuracy and GT probability mass 0.750 vs GPT-2's 94.0% and 0.855 (recovery = 0.750/0.855 = 87.7%); the student's L4H1 replicates the teacher's L9H1 comparison head with mean full-attention-pattern Pearson r = 0.945 ± 0.079 over 1,000 prompts (median 0.981; 93.6% of samples r > 0.8; 5/5 YY-token detections on representative examples), with DLA peak attribution shifting from teacher layer 9 to student layer 4.
- **Year-encoding heads causally preserved.** Head-level patching on the GT task: DistilGPT-2's L0H1 normalized score 0.517 vs teacher 0.606, L1H5 0.465 vs 0.557 (noise ceiling 0.547); overall effect Cohen's d = 0.681, p < 0.0001.
- **Representations compress rather than truncate.** Proportional-mapping CKA: early layers 0.9998, middle 0.967, late 0.790 (overall 0.921 ± 0.109); same-index layer 5 collapses to 0.314 (cosine 0.385) but recovers to 0.790 under proportional mapping; logit-lens relative accuracy under proportional mapping is 0.97–2.10 (exceeding the teacher at every layer except the final pair), while same-index r reaches 3.95 at L4.
- **Attention and SAE corroboration.** Per-head attention cosine similarity: 0.894 ± 0.086 (proportional) vs 0.631 ± 0.255 (same-index); entropy nearly unchanged (2.094 vs 2.001 bits; ΔH mean −0.007 bits), with the student front-loading previous-token attention at layer 1 (≈0.36 vs teacher ≈0.24). SAE feature survival under same-index matching falls to 0.086 at layer 5 with 34.9× the teacher's reconstruction error, while proportional mid/late pairs recover to survival ≈ 0.53, cosine 0.927, magnitude drift δ ≈ 0.51–0.55.
- **Aggregate metrics look merely degraded.** Perplexity: TinyStories 11.54 → 17.55 (+52.1%), WikiText-2 33.02 → 49.33 (+49.4%) — a gap that coexists with fluent text, an intact GT circuit, and a dead IOI circuit.

## Authors & Credibility

Both authors are at the Informatics Institute of Technology (Colombo, Sri Lanka): Nabeelah Faumi (informatics researcher, gmail-contactable) and Deshan Sumanathilaka (faculty address @iit.ac.lk). This is a small-team, single-institution study — no big-lab resources — but it is methodologically literate: it builds directly and correctly on the canonical circuit literature (Wang et al. 2022 for IOI, Hanna et al. 2023 for Greater-Than, Conmy et al. 2023 for SAEs/ACDC, Meng et al. 2022 for activation patching, Goldowsky-Dill et al. 2023 for path patching), uses standard open tooling (TransformerLens, SAELens), applies proper statistics (Bonferroni-corrected paired t/Wilcoxon tests, Cohen's d effect sizes, discovery/evaluation prompt splits, CKA across three seeds {42, 123, 7}), and includes an unusually honest limitations section that itself names the weakest links of the evidence (single-head-only GT verification, untested repeated-name heuristic). The work ran on a single NVIDIA L4 GPU, consistent with the modest model scale. No prior peer-review venue is indicated (arXiv preprint).

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: Partially incremental. That distillation damages IOI-style circuits was already shown by Wu et al. (2024, "What mechanisms does knowledge distillation distill?") — cited here — and representational reorganisation under distillation by Haskins & Adams (2025) and Baek & Tegmark (2025). The genuinely new contributions are (a) the controlled *contrast* between a collapsing and a surviving circuit within the same teacher–student pair, and (b) the striking single-head remapping evidence (student L4H1 ↔ teacher L9H1, r = 0.945). The compressibility hypothesis itself is plausible but remains a two-data-point extrapolation.
- **Rigor**: The causal claims are asymmetrically verified. The IOI *collapse* claim is well supported (patching + DLA + matching all converge), but the GT *survival* claim rests on a DLA layer shift plus one attention-pattern correlation — the authors concede in §Limitations that "a complete mechanistic account … would require verifying the full circuit beyond the key head match," including the causal path from year-encoding through comparison to output computation; path patching was inapplicable for GT (no inter-head paths fit within student layers 0–5), so the "circuit survives" headline is really "the comparison head's attention pattern survives." Attention-pattern similarity is also a weak functional proxy — the paper's own IOI results demonstrate this (matches with r up to 0.78 and 0% name-mover preservation).
- **An untested confound for the headline IOI number**: the sign-reversed logit difference (−0.467) may simply reflect a repeated-name frequency heuristic — the subject appears twice and the indirect object once in IOI templates — which the authors explicitly flag ("which we do not test"). Calling the model "actively misled" is stronger than the evidence.
- **Data quirks**: the SAE analysis contains an unexplained anomaly — the proportional pair G1/D0 shows near-zero feature survival (0.0002) while adjacent pairs recover to ≈0.53 — acknowledged as "feature divergence within that specific layer pair" but not resolved. Using teacher-pretrained SAEs as the fixed reference basis structurally cannot see student-only features (admitted). Table 2 lists GPT-2 at 124M while §3 says ~117M — a minor internal inconsistency.
- **Overclaiming risk**: "perplexity is an insufficient diagnostic" is rhetorically appealing, but the student's perplexity is 52.1% worse on TinyStories and 49.4% worse on WikiText-2 — aggregate metrics *do* register a large regression; what they miss is its *structure* (which mechanisms died). The framing occasionally blurs this.
- **Reproducibility**: High in principle — public models, public SAEs (gpt2-small-res-jb), TransformerLens, stated sample sizes (n = 1,000), seeds, and significance procedures; a single L4 GPU suffices. No explicit code/artifact release is mentioned, and prompt templates are only described, not published, which would slow exact replication.
- **Significance**: A clean, well-executed case study at 124M scale. Whether the dichotomy survives at modern distillation scales (e.g., reasoning distillation into 6–32B students) is exactly the open question; the authors themselves nominate intermediate-depth circuits across additional model pairs as "the most important extension."

## Strengths

- The contrastive design — one collapsing circuit vs one surviving circuit in the *same* model pair — is the cleanest possible experimental frame for isolating what distillation preserves, far more informative than studying either circuit alone.
- Multi-probe convergence: CKA, logit lens, attention statistics, SAE features, and causal patching all triangulate the same "compress, don't truncate" story, and the proportional-vs-same-index mapping contrast is a simple, clever diagnostic.
- The L4H1 ↔ L9H1 remapping is quantified at the level of individual per-sample attention weights (r = 0.945, 93.6% of samples above 0.8, confirmed on held-out prompts), not just aggregate statistics.
- Statistical hygiene is above the norm for interpretability papers: Bonferroni correction, Cohen's d everywhere, discovery/evaluation splits, multi-seed CKA, binomial test against chance for the below-50% IOI result.
- The limitations section is genuinely self-critical — it names the single-head-only GT verification, the untested repeated-name confound, and the SAE reference-basis blind spot before a reviewer could.

## Limitations

- **Scale and scope**: one teacher–student pair (GPT-2 → DistilGPT-2, 124M → 82M), two circuits, one distillation recipe (every-other-layer init + soft labels); generalization to other depths, families, or modern reasoning distillation is untested.
- **GT survival is verified at single-head granularity only** — the full causal chain (year encoding → comparison → output computation) is unconfirmed, and path patching could not be applied; "circuit survives" is stronger than the shown "comparison head's pattern survives."
- **The IOI null does not exclude an alternative mechanism**: causal scrubbing or automated circuit discovery (ACDC) could reveal a residual subgraph solving a variant of the task; neither was run.
- **Teacher-pretrained SAE basis** is blind to student-specific features — the 0.53 survival numbers are lower bounds on representational alignment, and the G1/D0 near-zero anomaly is unexplained.
- **The repeated-name heuristic explanation for the IOI sign reversal is acknowledged but untested**, leaving the most quotable negative number (−0.467) interpretation-ambiguous.
- **No intervention**: the study characterizes standard distillation but does not test whether circuit-aware or intermediate-layer-matching objectives would rescue the IOI circuit (deferred to future work in Appendix A.1).

## Relevance

直接命中 "LLM Hidden States & Interpretability" 兴趣区：电路级机制分析（CKA、logit lens、SAE、激活补丁）正是该方向的核心方法论，且给出了"注意力模式相似 ≠ 功能保留"这一对 SAE/电路迁移研究的实用警示。同时对 "Reliability under Extreme Performance Optimization" 有隐喻价值：压缩（蒸馏）会在聚合指标看似正常时静默摧毁已验证的内部机制——这是一种模型内部的"静默失效"。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the compressibility dichotomy hold for circuits of *intermediate* depth (e.g., mechanisms spanning teacher layers 6–8) and other pairs (8-layer students, Pythia or OLMo families, BERT→DistilBERT)?
- Can the IOI sign reversal be fully attributed to a repeated-name frequency heuristic? A controlled prompt set varying subject-name repetition (and name frequency) would settle whether the student is "misled" or just counting occurrences.
- Would circuit-aware distillation objectives — intermediate-layer representation matching at functionally critical layers, or attention-transfer losses — rescue deep circuits like IOI without sacrificing the compression ratio?
- What do *student-trained* SAEs reveal about DistilGPT-2's GT implementation? Is the remapped L4H1 supported by auxiliary student-only features invisible under the teacher's reference basis?
- Does the dichotomy extend to reasoning distillation (logit distillation over chain-of-thought traces) in modern small models — do multi-step reasoning circuits collapse the way IOI does when the student lacks depth?
