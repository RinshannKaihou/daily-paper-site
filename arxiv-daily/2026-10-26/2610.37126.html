Pruning failure modes · 剪枝失效模式 arXiv 2610.37126

30% magnitude pruning inverts GPT-2’s IOI circuit: all 26 heads intact, yet the model confidently picks the wrong name.

Mechanistic Failure Modes of Weight Pruning in Transformer Language Models
Nabeelah Faumi · Deshan Sumanathilaka — Informatics Institute of Technology, Colombo 2026-10-26 · v1 preprint · GPT-2 small (117M)
mechanistic interpretability model compression pruning circuit analysis model evaluation
Key metric 核心指标
94.8% → 17.7%

IOI accuracy after magnitude-pruning just 30% of MLP neurons — with all 26 circuit heads still structurally intact (weight norms > 25).

Name-Mover DLA flips +3.576 → −1.102 — the circuit’s output head now argues against the right answer.

Why this matters 为什么重要

Accuracy, perplexity, and structural head checks can all pass a pruned model that is confidently wrong — and the “conservative” 30% sparsity is the most dangerous setting.

Critical edge 批判性视角

The austere protocol — zeroed weights, no recovery fine-tuning, no modern pruning criteria, one 117M model on two synthetic-template circuits — leaves inversion’s practical severity untested.

Two failure modes
zeroing / inversion
  • Design: magnitude (L2-norm) vs random selection × attention heads vs MLP neurons × 30/50/70% sparsity; weights zeroed, never retrained.
  • Ground truth: the 26-head IOI circuit (Wang et al., 2022); ABB/ABC template prompts, 300-sentence eval; behaviour = logit difference ℓ(IO) − ℓ(S).
  • Circuit integrity: activation patching, path patching (4 pathways), and direct logit attribution (DLA) by head class — plus CKA/cosine similarity, logit lens, attention statistics, and frozen-SAE reconstruction. Seven converging instruments.
  • Replication: the MLP-dominant Greater-Than circuit (Hanna et al., 2023) under the same 12 variants.

Further evidence

  • Non-monotonic damage: IOI degrades 184.1% at 30% neuron sparsity but only 123.5% at 70% — heavier corruption fades toward noise; low sparsity stays “strongly wrong.”
  • Context poisoning localized: frozen-SAE Layer-1 reconstruction error hits 155.87 (>350× base) while every circuit head remains intact.
  • Aggregate stats are blind too: under inversion, attention entropy moves only ΔH = +0.173; late-layer CKA collapses to 0.260 while early layers hold ≥ 0.900.
▲

Strengths · 优点

  • Quantifies — rather than asserts — the blind spot: inversion is invisible to accuracy, perplexity, and head-presence checks.
  • Seven converging instruments triangulate one mechanism: residual-stream context poisoning.
  • Cross-circuit replication (IOI vs Greater-Than) upgrades the finding to a conditional rule: circuit architecture decides which component type is fatal.
  • Unusually candid: sign conventions, non-monotonicities, and its own instruments’ blind spots are disclosed, not hidden.
▼

Limitations · 局限

  • Single 117M model; larger models’ redundant circuits may absorb or re-route the damage — untested.
  • Two synthetic-template circuits only; no natural-language downstream task, so ecological validity is unestablished.
  • No recovery fine-tuning, no modern criteria (Wanda, movement, attribution-guided) — real pipelines could repair the inverted circuit.
  • Safety framing (refusal reversal) is extrapolation; SAE error only proves departure from the base manifold, not what the pruned model computes.
Relevance 相关性 · 原文

与剪枝/压缩模型的可信评估兴趣直接相关：如果只看准确率和困惑度来放行剪枝后的大模型，本文证明这两类指标连同“电路头是否还在”的结构检查都可能漏掉“自信地做错”的机理损伤，且最危险的恰恰是被视为保守的 30% 低稀疏度。对任何关注模型压缩 + 可解释性/安全评估交叉方向的工作都是直接的动机性证据。

Questions for further exploration · 待探索

  • Does recovery fine-tuning repair the inverted circuit, re-route around it — or lock the wrong behaviour in?
  • Do the failure modes survive scale, or does head redundancy in Llama-class models absorb them?
  • Would inversion strike behaviourally critical circuits (refusal, factual recall) — a safety failure, not a benchmark drop?
  • Could cheap targeted activation-patching probes be built into standard compression benchmarks?
  • Why do Negative Name Movers show paradoxically negative patching effects in all twelve conditions?
