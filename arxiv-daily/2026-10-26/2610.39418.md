> **一句话总结：** LinQuant 系统地刻画了线性递归（Mamba-2、GLA、DeltaNet、GDN、KDA 等）chunkwise prefill 中的七类"函数不变性"——旋转、对角缩放、以及衰减/写入因子在操作数间的重新分配——在不改变全精度计算的前提下让操作数更利于量化；把它融合进 SSDi8 的 W4A8 流水线后，Mamba-2 8B 在 WikiText-2 上的困惑度从 7.620 降到 7.435（FP16 为 7.250，收复约一半量化损失），且 prefill 延迟无可测开销。

> **TL;DR:** The first systematic study of mixer-specific functional invariances (Hadamard QK rotations, SmoothQuant-style rescalings, and decay/write-factor "absorptions" unique to recurrences) for quantizing chunkwise-parallel linear-recurrence prefill. Fused into the SSDi8 W4A8+INT8-recurrence pipeline, RQK+DQK cuts WikiText-2 perplexity on Mamba-2 8B from 7.620 (SSDi8) to 7.435 (FP16: 7.250, ~50% of the gap recovered) and on Nemotron-H 8B from 6.338 to 6.175, with no measurable prefill-latency increase on an RTX A5000. In a controlled INT6 mixer zoo it is even more dramatic: naive 340M linear-attention ppl 30.694 drops to 18.138 (FP: 17.117), and 1.3B LA+β recovers from a 148.173 collapse to 10.842. Production gains, however, are confined to the Mamba-2/SSD recurrence family and do not uniformly transfer to downstream task accuracy.

## 核心贡献

- 提出并证明（附录 C 给出精确算术下的证明）线性递归 chunkwise 计算的 **七类函数不变性**：四类基变换（键侧旋转 RQK、键侧对角缩放 DQK、值侧旋转 RV、值侧缩放 DV）与三类"吸收"（标量衰减分配 η、对角衰减分配 Eη、写入因子分配 ξ/σ,τ），并给出每类不变性在 LA、Mamba-2、GLA、DeltaNet、GDN、KDA 六种递归中的适用条件表（如旋转必须与衰减 Λt 对易，因此对角衰减下只能做局部旋转；DQK 不保持 delta 修正写入）。
- 指出线性递归的不变性与 softmax 注意力有**本质差异**：状态跨 chunk 携带要求变换与递归动力学一致，但衰减/门控/delta 规则也带来了注意力所没有的新自由度（衰减与写入因子的连续分配）；例如 Flash Linear Attention 标准的浮点 WY 放置 (σ,τ)=(1,1) 在量化后未必最优。
- 构建受控"mixer zoo"（340M/15B tokens 与 1.3B/100B tokens SlimPajama，15 个递归变体，统一骨干与训练配方），证明不变性的收益取决于递归结构、QK 归一化、量化 scale 粒度（per-head/per-channel/per-tensor）与状态存储基（canonical 优于 rotated），且组合变换恢复的超额 NLL 约为最佳单一变换的两倍。
- 把框架落地到生产：将 RQK/DQK 融合进 SSDi8（Mamba-2 的 W4A8 + INT8 递归 prefill 流水线）的操作数准备 pass，在 Mamba-2 1.3B/2.7B/8B 与 Nemotron-H 8B 上以匹配延迟改进困惑度；代码开源（github.com/ntumm120/LinQuant）。

## Key Contributions

- Identifies and proves (exact-arithmetic proofs in Appendix C) **seven functional invariances** of chunkwise-parallel linear recurrences: four changes of basis (key-side rotation RQK, key-side reciprocal diagonal rescaling DQK, value-side rotation RV, value-side rescaling DV) and three absorptions (scalar-decay split with exponent η, diagonal-decay magnitude split Eη, write-factor split ξ, plus (σ, τ) for delta-rule Gram construction), with an applicability matrix across LA, Mamba-2, GLA, DeltaNet, GDN, and KDA.
- Characterizes what is **recurrence-specific**: a rotation is valid only if it commutes with the decay operator (so diagonal decay permits only local, within-chunk rotations); DQK commutes with diagonal decay but breaks delta-corrected writes; value-axis transforms are equivariant for all families. Absorptions redistribute decay/write factors between operands of the state write — a degree of freedom with no softmax-attention analogue.
- Builds a controlled mixer zoo (fifteen 340M cells plus six 1.3B cells, identical backbone, trained from scratch on SlimPajama with 15B/100B tokens) and shows which equivalent realization is most quantization-robust depends on recurrence structure, QK normalization, scale granularity ⟨bQK, bV⟩, and state-storage basis; the selected stack recovers roughly 2x the excess NLL of the best single transformation at INT6.
- Transfers the framework to production by fusing RQK and DQK into SSDi8's W4A8 projection + INT8-recurrence pipeline, improving WikiText-2 perplexity at matched latency on Mamba-2 1.3B/2.7B/8B and Nemotron-H 8B; INT8 Triton kernels implement the applicable invariances with rotations fused into operand preparation.

## Methodology

The paper targets the prefill path of linear recurrences, which chunkwise-parallel algorithms (chunk size 64, 16-token sub-blocks for diagonal decay) express as four INT8 matrix contractions — state write, state read, local score, intra-chunk output — plus a recurrent state pass between chunks (FP32 carry with an INT8 read copy; INT8 fixed-point state pass in production). Functional invariances are transformations that leave the full-precision operator unchanged but reshuffle which numerics the quantizer sees; since invariances do not commute with quantization, the choice of realization matters.

The seven invariances split into two classes. **Changes of basis** act on the key or value axes of the state S_t: RQK is a joint random signed-Hadamard rotation of queries and keys (valid with no/scalar decay; diagonal decay restricts it to local contractions); DQK = diag((max|k_n|/max|q_n|)^κ) transports magnitude between queries and keys (κ=1/2 works best); RV and DV rotate/rescale the value axis (DV equalizes per-channel value ranges) and are valid for every recurrence family, with a further choice of storing the carried state in rotated or canonical basis. **Absorptions** redistribute multiplicative factors inside the state write: scalar tail-decay Γ→ splits as (Γ→)^η on values and (Γ→)^(1−η) on keys (SSDi8's choice is the η=1 endpoint); diagonal decay splits into a per-token scalar magnitude E and a normalized channel pattern; and the write gate B admits ξ (linear writes) or (σ, τ) (delta-rule writes, splitting the Gram/WY construction and the subsequent write). Applicability conditions (e.g., commutation with Λ_t) are proved in Appendix C.

The controlled study trains the mixer zoo from scratch (24 layers, Dk=Dv=128, 8/16 heads) with identical recipes, quantizes symmetrically with max-abs scales calibrated on eight WikiText-2 windows, and measures WikiText-2 test NLL (excess NLL = log(PPL_quant/PPL_FP)); INT6 (restricted range inside INT8 kernels) is used to amplify effects because several normalized models are already near-FP at INT8 with per-channel value scales. Transformation parameters (κ, η, ξ, σ, τ) are swept and stacks are selected on held-out WikiText-2 samples (improvement threshold 0.003 nats), avoiding test-set selection. For production, the authors reproduce SSDi8 on Mamba-2 (validated against the official release in perplexity and latency), calibrate static scales and DQK on 512 Pile sequences, apply GPTQ for W4 projections after fusing transformations, and evaluate both decay-absorption endpoints with per-channel scales on the absorbed operand; timing uses CUDA events on an RTX A5000 (plus A100-SXM4/H100 for kernel ratios) with 30 warm-up and 200 measured passes.

## Results & Highlights

- **Production WikiText-2 perplexity (W4A8 projections + INT8 recurrence, static scales):** Mamba-2 8B improves from 7.620 (SSDi8 baseline) to 7.456 with RQK alone and 7.435 with RQK+DQK (FP16: 7.250) — recovering ~50% (0.185 of 0.370) of the quantization gap. Nemotron-H 8B Base improves from 6.338 to 6.175 with key-side RQK+DQK (FP16: 6.030, ~53% of the gap), and Nemotron-H 8B Reasoning from 8.921 to 8.650 (FP16: 8.423, ~54% of the gap).
- **Smaller Mamba-2 models gain less:** 1.3B goes from 10.928 (SSDi8) to 10.830 with key-side RQK+DQK (FP16: 10.420, i.e., 0.098 of the 0.508 gap, ~19%), and 2.7B from 9.441 to 9.372 (FP16: 9.060, ~18% of the gap).
- **Controlled INT6 zoo, coarse per-tensor value scales (⟨T,T⟩), WikiText-2 ppl:** naive quantization of 340M linear attention degrades 17.117 → 30.694, and the selected invariance stack restores it to 18.138 (best single transformation only reaches 23.151 — the stack recovers roughly twice the excess NLL). At 1.3B, LA+β recovers from a 148.173 collapse to 10.842 (FP: 10.276) and DeltaNet from 46.639 to 10.111 (FP: 9.917).
- **Downstream zero-shot (340M zoo, INT8, ⟨T,C⟩):** the stack nearly closes the naive-quantization damage for linear attention — LAMBADA accuracy 0.042 (naive) → 0.258 (stack) vs 0.267 FP; six-task average (LAMBADA/HellaSwag/ARC-e/ARC-c/PIQA/WinoGrande) 0.356 → 0.395 vs 0.398 FP. At 1.3B, LA+β LAMBADA recovers from 0.169 to 0.408 (FP: 0.465).
- **Latency is neutral:** query–key rotation adds no measurable end-to-end prefill latency relative to the matched SSDi8 setting across batch sizes 1–32 on Mamba-2 1.3B/2.7B/8B (A5000, T=2048, time ratios ≈0.9–1.1); fused diagonal rescaling leaves the Mamba-2 1.3B layer latency unchanged at 1.498 ms (relative 1.00; a separate elementwise implementation would cost 1.04x). The re-implemented SSDi8 baseline matches the official release (616/1184/2563 ms official vs 609/1163/2542 ms theirs at B=8, T=2048).
- **Placement findings:** with per-tensor value scales at INT6, moving scalar decay from values to keys increases excess NLL (SSDi8's value-side η=1 is the right endpoint there), while at production INT8 both endpoints are within noise (e.g., Mamba-2 8B: 7.435 value vs 7.437 key) and value-side is retained for runtime (faster at Dk=128, Dv=64, though the preference reverses for wide values). Value rotation does not help production (rotated storage worsens 10.496 → 10.533; canonical 10.497) and is therefore excluded; random Hadamard signs beat deterministic in 5 of 6 controls.

## Authors & Credibility

The team combines MIT CSAIL (Neehal Tumma, Rohan Cherukuri, Daniela Rus — Rus directs CSAIL) with the Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems / ETH Zürich / Tübingen AI Center / ELLIS Tübingen group of Philipp Nazari and T. Konstantin Rusch, who work on linear-attention theory (e.g., the cited rank-based state-reduction paper); Tumma has prior ICLR work on recurrent architectures and a 2026 "Preconditioned DeltaNet" paper. SSDi8 (Kim et al., ICLR 2026) is the compared baseline. The experimental hygiene is strong: invariance proofs are separated from empirics, stack configurations are selected on held-out data with a pre-registered 0.003-nat threshold, production numbers are averaged over four calibration sets with standard deviations, the SSDi8 reproduction is validated against the official release in both quality and latency, negative results (diagonal-decay absorption, value rotation, dynamic write scales) are reported rather than hidden, and code is released at github.com/ntumm120/LinQuant.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty: high.** Prior recurrent-model PTQ (MambaQuant, Quamba2) quantizes projections/activations around the recurrence; SSDi8 quantizes the Mamba-2 recurrence itself but with one hand-chosen realization. LinQuant is the first to enumerate the functional-invariance design space of linear recurrences and to derive applicability conditions (commutation with decay, delta-rule constraints) — the absorption family genuinely has no attention analogue.
- **Rigor: high but with two caveats.** First, the controlled study calibrates and evaluates on the same corpus (WikiText-2 training windows for calibration, WikiText-2 test for evaluation), which is standard for PTQ work but slightly flatters recovery numbers. Second, INT6/INT4 are deliberately used "to amplify quantization effects" — the eye-catching recoveries (e.g., LA+β 1.3B from 148.173 to 10.842) occur at an artificially stressed precision, while at INT8 many normalized models are already within ~0.06 ppl of FP, so the practical headroom for the zoo conclusions at INT8 is small.
- **Reproducibility: good.** Code released, models trained from scratch with fully specified recipes (layers, dims, token counts, optimizer), sweeps and selection thresholds documented, std over calibration sets reported.
- **Significance: solid but narrower than the framing.** The general framework covers six recurrence families, yet the production evidence covers only the Mamba-2/SSD family (Mamba-2 and Nemotron-H) — no delta-rule or diagonal-decay production model (e.g., Kimi Linear/KDA, RWKV-7, GDN at scale) is evaluated, exactly the families where the zoo shows RQK restricted, DQK inapplicable, and diagonal-decay absorption giving little gain. The production deltas are also modest at small scale: 0.098 ppl on Mamba-2 1.3B, ~19% of the quantization gap.
- **Red flags: honest but worth noting.** The authors themselves admit "perplexity improvements do not imply uniform gains in task accuracy" — e.g., Mamba-2 1.3B six-task average is 0.588 for the official SSDi8 and 0.588 for the value-side RQK+DQK configuration despite the ppl gain, and Nemotron-H Reasoning LAMBADA is 0.660 for both endpoints. There is also no head-to-head production comparison against MambaQuant/Quamba2-style projection-quantization pipelines (they are dismissed as mixer-agnostic rather than benchmarked), and all evaluation is at T=2048 — no long-context results despite that being a core motivation for recurrences.

## Strengths

- Principled: exact-arithmetic proofs of each invariance plus an applicability table across six recurrence families, instead of ad-hoc tricks ported from attention.
- The controlled mixer zoo is the right experimental design — identical backbone and training recipe isolates the effect of recurrence structure on quantization robustness.
- Practically careful systems work: rotations fused into operand preparation with measured zero latency overhead, per-head decay applied in GPU registers without materializing expanded tensors, and a faithful SSDi8 re-implementation validated against the official release.
- Unusually transparent reporting: negative results (value rotation in production, diagonal-decay absorption, dynamic write scales), held-out-based stack selection, and multi-calibration variance are all in the paper.

## Limitations

- Production validation is limited to the scalar-decay Mamba-2/SSD recurrence (Mamba-2 1.3B/2.7B/8B, Nemotron-H 8B); the more expressive delta-rule and diagonal-decay families — where several invariances do not apply — are only studied at 340M/1.3B scale.
- Perplexity gains shrink with model capability at fixed precision and partially fail to transfer to downstream accuracy (six-task averages move by ≤0.004 on several production configurations).
- Only prefill is quantized end-to-end; decode-side and long-context (T > 2048) behavior of the transformed realizations are not evaluated, and only integer formats are implemented (floating-point low precision is left to future work).
- DQK uses a single max-ratio parameterization with exponent κ and random (not learned, SpinQuant-style) rotations; the paper itself flags alternative parameterizations and learned rotations as future work.

## Relevance

对关注线性注意力/Mamba 类架构高效推理的工作高度相关：它给出了把 W4A8/INT8 量化从 Transformer 迁移到线性递归 prefill 时的系统性理论与实操指南（哪些旋转/缩放/吸收可用、scale 粒度怎么选），可直接用于低成本部署 Mamba-2、Nemotron-H 及未来混合架构。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Do the invariance gains transfer to production-scale delta-rule models (Kimi Linear/KDA, GDN), where the zoo shows RQK restricted to local contractions and DQK inapplicable — and can a learned rotation (SpinQuant-style) recover what random Hadamard cannot?
- How do the transformed realizations behave in decode (state-pass) quantization and at long context (T = 16k–128k), where state-carry error compounds across many chunks and the FP32 carry assumed here may be undesirable?
- Can the (η, ξ, σ, τ) absorption parameters be optimized jointly (e.g., by Stein-style or gradient-based calibration against the full-precision operator) instead of swept on a coarse grid with held-out selection?
- Do perplexity-neutral transformations exist for the W4 weight side of the projections, so that the remaining ~50% of the production quantization gap on Mamba-2 8B can also be closed without retraining?
- Would floating-point (FP8/FP4) kernels change which realization is optimal, given that absorptions were tuned for symmetric integer quantization?
