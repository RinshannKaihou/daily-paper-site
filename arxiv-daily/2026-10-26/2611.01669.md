> **一句话总结：** 本文精确回答了 Ordentlich–Polyanskiy 的公开问题：在高码率熵编码区域，Haar 随机旋转后的 GPTQ 相对 WaterSIC 的最坏速率差距为 ½log₂κ − log₂ln κ + ½ − 1/ln 2 + o(1) 比特，仅比不旋转好 ½log₂ln κ + O(1) 比特（理论上旋转不能替代注水），但在 Qwen3/Llama-3.1 的 96 个真实 Hessian 上旋转后差距中位数仅 0.041 比特（最坏 0.356），且极限公式与有限维实测吻合到 0.009 比特以内。

> **TL;DR:** GPTQ is Babai's nearest-plane algorithm on the Cholesky lattice of the layer's input second moment Σ; in the entropy-coded high-rate regime it pays Δ = ½log₂(AM/GM) of its Cholesky pivots more bits than WaterSIC (itself within 0.255 bit of the rate-distortion bound). This paper solves the open problem of Ordentlich & Polyanskiy: after a Haar rotation the k-th pivot converges to 1/S_ν(−k/n) (a Lyapunov-exponent formula), giving a worst rotated gap of ½log₂κ − log₂ln κ + ½ − 1/ln 2 + o(1) bits — only ½log₂ln κ + O(1) better than no rotation, so rotation cannot replace waterfilling in general. Yet on 96 real Hessians of Qwen3-1.7B/8B and Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct the rotated gap is tiny (median 0.041, max 0.356 bit vs 2–5 bit worst case at the same condition numbers), and the limit matches finite-n pivots within 0.009 bit. End-to-end on WikiText-2 at 2 bits, GPTQ+Hadamard reaches PPL 26.93 (Qwen3-1.7B) and 11.18 (Llama-3.1-8B) vs 152.95/41.37 for unrotated GPTQ and 26.06/10.37 for PlainWaterSIC.

## 核心贡献

- **给出旋转后 GPTQ 的精确极限（定理 1）**：对谱测度为 ν、条件数有界的协方差矩阵，Haar 旋转后第 k 个 GPTQ 支点（Cholesky 枢轴）几乎必然、且对 k ≥ δn 与谱一致地收敛到 h_ν(k/n) = 1/S_ν(−k/n)——这是 Lyapunov 指数理论中的经典公式（Newman 1986; Kargin 2008）；速率差距因此收敛到 ½log₂(D(ν)/GM(ν))，D(ν) = ∫₀¹ h_ν。贡献不在公式本身，而在其与 GPTQ 的逐支点、有限 n、对谱一致的身份认定。
- **解决 Ordentlich–Polyanskiy 公开问题（定理 2）**：条件数为 κ 时旋转后最坏差距为 ½log₂κ − log₂ln κ + ½ − 1/ln 2 + o(1) 比特，仅由"两点谱"（约 2/ln κ 的质量集中在最大特征值）取得；不旋转只差 ½log₂ln κ + O(1) 比特——**最坏情形下随机旋转不能替代注水式逐列码率分配**。证明依赖 D 的一阶变分的对数凸性把极值谱推向端点。
- **解释真实网络为何"良性"（第 5 节）**：占比 η、倍率 μ 的离群特征值在最坏基下代价约 ημ，旋转后仅 Θ(η²μ)（当 η²μ→∞ 时常数恰为 ½）；有限维下 r 个尖峰被消除当且仅当 (r + ln n)Σμⱼ = o(n²)（在 r + ln n 因子内尖锐）；而对数尺度上展宽的谱保留主项——旋转仍只省 ½log₂ln κ + O(1)。这划清了"旋转够用"与"必须注水"的边界。
- **刻画非高斯权重的权重侧代价（命题 5）**：逐列熵编码下，旋转后 GPTQ 的差距 = Hessian 侧（定理 1）+ 权重侧代价 C_w(Q) = U(W) − U(WQ) ≤ U(W)，其中 U 是经典变换编码增益加非高斯项；两项都可在量化前计算，给出可操作的逐层决策规则。
- **理论与实测惊人吻合**：96 个真实 Hessian 上 Haar 旋转后的差距中位数 0.041 比特、85% 层 < 0.1 比特、最大 0.356 比特，而同条件数的最坏情形为 2–5 比特；极限公式与有限维支点之差在全部 270 个（层, 阻尼）对上不超过 0.009 比特。

## Key Contributions

- **Exact limit of rotated GPTQ (Theorem 1).** After a Haar rotation, the k-th GPTQ pivot π_k converges almost surely to h_ν(k/n) = 1/S_ν(−k/n), uniformly over k ≥ δn and over spectra ν of bounded condition number — a classical formula from Lyapunov-exponent theory (Newman 1986; Kargin 2008). The rate gap converges to Δ → ½log₂(D(ν)/GM(ν)) with D(ν) = ∫₀¹ h_ν; the novelty is the identification with GPTQ as a finite-n, per-pivot statement, uniform in the spectrum.
- **Solves the open problem of Ordentlich & Polyanskiy (2026b) (Theorem 2).** Over spectra with condition number κ, the worst rotated gap is ½log₂κ − log₂ln κ + ½ − 1/ln 2 + o(1) bits, attained only by two-point spectra with mass ≈ 2/ln κ on the top eigenvalue. Rotation saves only ½log₂ln κ + O(1) bits over the unrotated worst case (the Specht-ratio bound), so random rotation cannot replace waterfilling in the worst case; the same holds under the λ_j ≥ ε·AM(λ) normalization (Theorem 3). The proof uses a log-convexity property of the first variation of D that pushes extremal spectra to the endpoints (Lemma 1).
- **Characterizes when real layers are benign (Section 5).** An η-fraction of outlier eigenvalues of size μ costs ≈ ημ in the worst basis but only Θ(η²μ) after rotation (constant exactly ½ when η²μ → ∞); r spikes are removed iff (r + ln n)Σ_j μ_j = o(n²), sharp up to the r + ln n factor. Log-scale spectral spread, however, keeps the leading term — rotation again saves only ½log₂ln κ + O(1). For the log-uniform spectrum an explicit closed form h(t) = t(1−e^{−(1−t)L})/((1−t)(e^{tL}−1)) is derived (Proposition 4).
- **Weight-side rate cost for non-Gaussian weights (Proposition 5).** With per-column entropy coding, rotated GPTQ's gap = the Hessian term of Theorem 1 + C_w(Q) = U(W) − U(WQ) ≤ U(W), where U combines the classical transform-coding gain (Huang–Schultheiss) with a non-Gaussianity term that random projections tend to remove (Diaconis–Freedman). Both terms are computable before quantization, yielding a per-layer decision rule: for each candidate basis (native, act-order, rotation) compute the exact gap from one Cholesky factorization plus C_w, keep the cheapest, and fall back to per-column steps only if even that exceeds the tolerated overhead.
- **Tight theory–experiment agreement.** On 96 real Hessians the rotated gap has median 0.041 bit (85% of layers < 0.1 bit, max 0.356) against 2–5 bits worst case at the same κ; the Theorem-1 limit matches finite-n pivots to within 0.009 bit over all 270 (layer, damping) pairs, and the high-rate rate prediction holds to ≤ 0.015 bit at 4 bits/weight on 63 of 64 entropy-coded layers.

## Methodology

The paper works in the layerwise post-training quantization model: minimize D = (1/n)·tr((W−Ŵ)Σ(W−Ŵ)^T) with Σ = E[xx^T] estimated on calibration data. GPTQ with a common step size is exactly Babai's nearest-plane (successive interference cancellation) on the Cholesky factor U of Σ = U^T U; its quality is governed by the pivots π_k = U_kk² (squared Gram–Schmidt lengths), with Π_k π_k = det Σ. In the high-rate, per-column entropy-coded regime (Lifar et al. 2026), GPTQ pays Δ(Σ) = ½log₂(AM(π)/GM(λ)) extra bits per weight relative to WaterSIC, which reverse-waterfills rate across columns and is within ½log₂(2πe/12) ≈ 0.255 bit of the rate-distortion limit for every Σ.

The technical core studies the pivots of Q^T Σ Q for Haar-distributed orthogonal Q. Using free-probability / Lyapunov-exponent technology, Theorem 1 shows π_k/n → h_ν(k/n) almost surely, uniformly in k ≥ δn and in ν of bounded condition number, where h_ν(t) = 1/S_ν(−t) is expressed through the Stieltjes-type transform of ν; hence Δ → ½log₂(D(ν)/GM(ν)) with D(ν) = ∫₀¹ h_ν dt. Theorem 2 maximizes D over spectra of condition number κ: a log-convexity lemma (Lemma 1) on the first variation forces extremal measures onto two atoms, yielding the sharp worst-case formula and its two-point extremizers. Propositions 2–4 then analyze structured spectra (finite spikes, outlier fractions, log-uniform spread) asymptotically. Section 6 extends beyond the Gaussian-weight model of WaterSIC: under Assumption 1 (high-rate column entropy, proven only for Gaussian rows, validated empirically to ≈ 0.001 bit), the rotated gap acquires a weight-side cost C_w(Q) = U(W) − U(WQ) — the transform-coding gain the native column basis enjoyed, plus a non-Gaussianity term.

Experiments use 96 Hessians from Qwen3-1.7B, Qwen3-8B-Base, and Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct (8 evenly spaced blocks × 4 distinct linear-layer inputs: shared q/k/v, o_proj, shared gate/up, down_proj; n = 2048–14336), computed in float64 from 128 WikiText-2 sequences of 2048 tokens, with GPTQ-default damping 10⁻²·diag Σ. Rotations are Haar or randomized Hadamard (one/two rounds), 3 seeds. They verify: (i) the pivot limit on synthetic and real spectra; (ii) the high-rate rate-gap prediction on 64 layers at R = 2–5 with ideal per-column empirical entropies; (iii) the weights-only prediction of C_w; (iv) end-to-end sequential GPTQ WikiText-2 perplexity at equal entropy-coded rate.

## Results & Highlights

- **Rotated gap on 96 real Hessians (Qwen3-1.7B, Qwen3-8B-Base, Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, damping 10⁻²): median 0.041 bit, 85% of layers below 0.1 bit, maximum 0.356 bit (Llama block 0, q/k/v input) — against a worst case of 2–5 bits for the same condition numbers** (Figure 1). Rotation saves a median of 0.15 bit over unrotated GPTQ and 0.85 bit over the worst (PCA) basis.
- **Theorem-1 limit accuracy:** over all 270 (layer, damping) pairs the Haar-rotated finite-n gap exceeds the spectral limit Δ(ν_emp) by between 5·10⁻⁵ and 0.009 bit; on synthetic spectra |Δ_n − Δ(ν)| ≤ 0.017 bit at n = 256 and ≤ 0.0004 bit at n = 8192. Randomized Hadamard is within 0.01 bit of the prediction on 93% of layers (one round) and 99% (two rounds); the exception is the o_proj input, whose covariance is blocked by attention head.
- **High-rate rate prediction at R = 4 bits/weight on 64 layers:** median error ≤ 0.003 bit, maximum 0.015 bit (except Llama block 0's q/k/v input at 0.07 bit, near-dead input features); per-layer rank correlation 1.00 for R ≥ 3; at R = 2 measured gaps are 0.02–0.03 bit below prediction. The weight-side cost C_w is predicted from weights alone with median error 0.0008 bit (max 0.034 bit).
- **Weight-side cost can flip the recommendation:** rotating PlainWaterSIC costs median 0.005–0.036 bit per layer type but 1.36 bit on one Llama layer, where Hadamard makes entropy-coded GPTQ worse (gap 1.31 → 1.68 bit) because C_w exceeds the Hessian-side saving; on the other 63 layers rotation helps.
- **End-to-end WikiText-2 perplexity (seqlen 2048) at equal entropy-coded rate (Table 2):** Qwen3-1.7B at 2 bits — GPTQ 152.95 vs GPTQ+Hadamard 26.93 vs PlainWaterSIC 26.06 (bf16: 16.72); Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct at 2 bits — GPTQ 41.37 vs GPTQ+Hadamard 11.18 vs PlainWaterSIC 10.37 (bf16: 7.22). At 3 bits on Qwen3-1.7B, PlainWaterSIC beats GPTQ+Hadamard for every one of 3 calibration seeds and is far more stable: 17.33 ± 0.02 vs 18.80 ± 1.14.
- **One effect outside the theory:** unrotated GPTQ with act-order beats a random rotation on 27% of layers, including 19 of the 24 gate/up inputs — and axis-aligned outliers do not explain it (all 26 such layers still win after clipping the largest 1% of eigenvalues). Removing the top 1% of eigenvalues cuts the PCA-basis gap by 30–60% but the rotated gap by only 0.005–0.02 bit, consistent with the η²μ scaling.
- **Fixed-rate formats are outside the regime:** with INT-b group-128, WaterSIC's per-column multipliers gain ≤ 0.008 bit-equivalent and the format itself costs 0.8–1.0 bit relative to entropy coding; there rotation helps mainly through clipping.

## Authors & Credibility

Single-author preprint by Runguo Li (University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign), submitted to cs.IT/cs.LG, November 2026. The paper directly answers an open problem explicitly posed by Ordentlich & Polyanskiy (2026b, §III-E), quoted verbatim in the introduction, and engages carefully with the recent line connecting GPTQ to lattice decoding (Chen et al. 2026; Birnick 2026) and WaterSIC rate-distortion analysis (Lifar et al. 2026). Credibility signals are strong: worst-case claims come with explicit extremizers and sharp o(1) constants rather than big-O hand-waving; predictions are validated against finite matrices at the 10⁻³-bit level; anomalies (the o_proj Hadamard deviations, the act-order effect, the pathological Llama block-0 layer) are reported rather than hidden, and single-seed entries are flagged as such. No peer review yet, and no code release is mentioned in the main text.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** High. The identification of the rotated GPTQ pivot process with the Lyapunov-exponent formula 1/S_ν(−t) and the sharp two-point extremal spectrum solving the Ordentlich–Polyanskiy open problem are genuinely new; prior work (QuIP's μ-incoherence bounds, WaterSIC's unbounded-gap result) gave only bounds or non-constructive separations.
- **Rigor:** Generally excellent, with one load-bearing assumption: Proposition 5's weight-side analysis rests on Assumption 1, proven by Lifar et al. only for i.i.d. Gaussian rows and "taken as given" for real weights — the empirical validation (≈ 0.001 bit) mitigates but does not replace a proof. The main theorems are for Haar rotations; the randomized Hadamard transforms actually used in practice are covered only empirically (93–99% of layers) plus a lower bound (Proposition 7), and their worst case is stated as open.
- **Reproducibility:** The experimental setup is specified unusually precisely (models, blocks, layer types, float64 Hessian computation, 128×2048 WikiText-2 tokens, damping, 3 seeds, ideal per-column empirical entropies), and every quantity in the decision rule is computable from one Cholesky factorization — but no code or Hessian release is referenced, and the theory requires implementing free-probability integrals for D(ν) that a replicator must get right to 0.001 bit.
- **Significance:** For entropy-coded PTQ the message is decisive and actionable (rotation almost always suffices; a pre-quantization Cholesky check tells you when it doesn't). For the deployed INT4 world the paper itself concedes the theory "does not apply," so its practical reach beyond the entropy-coding research line is limited.
- **Red flags / weaknesses to weigh:** (i) end-to-end evidence is thin — two models, WikiText-2 perplexity only, and most Table 2 entries are seed 0 only (the author admits "more seeds and models would be needed"); (ii) the worst-case result is attained only by two-point spectra that real networks do not have, so the headline "rotation cannot replace waterfilling" is a theory statement, while the experiments actually show PlainWaterSIC (no rotation) slightly beating GPTQ+Hadamard end-to-end — arguably the more practical takeaway is underplayed; (iii) the act-order anomaly (wins on 27% of layers, 19/24 gate/up inputs) lies outside the theory and is left unexplained; (iv) layerwise analysis only — no whole-model rate-distortion claim; (v) learned rotations (SpinQuant) and QuIP#-style error feedback are out of scope, so "rotation" here means data-independent random rotations only.

## Strengths

- Solves a precisely stated open problem from the information-theory literature with sharp constants, explicit extremizers, and a clean conceptual answer: rotation removes finite-rank outliers (ημ → η²μ/2) but not log-scale spectral spread.
- Rare theory–experiment tightness: the Lyapunov-exponent limit matches finite Cholesky pivots of 2048–14336-dimensional real Hessians to within 0.009 bit, and the high-rate prediction to ≤ 0.015 bit at 4 bits/weight.
- Delivers an immediately usable engineering rule — compute the exact gap (plus C_w) per candidate basis from a single Cholesky factorization before quantizing, and use per-column steps only when needed — with the pathological cases (o_proj without permutation, Llama block-0's 1.36-bit C_w) mapped out.
- Honest scoping: fixed-rate INT formats are explicitly excluded, Hadamard worst case is stated as open, single-seed results are flagged, and contrary evidence (act-order winning on 27% of layers; WaterSIC's end-to-end stability advantage) is reported rather than buried.

## Limitations

- Theorems cover Haar rotations, not the randomized Hadamard transforms used in practice; their worst case remains open (only a lower bound via asymptotically freeing sequences, Proposition 7; sign-only transforms not known to qualify).
- The gap Δ is a high-rate, per-column entropy-coded quantity — verified down to 3 bits (0.02–0.03 bit conservative at 2 bits) — and says nothing about fixed-rate INT4-style deployment, where the paper itself shows the analysis breaks down.
- Proposition 5 relies on Assumption 1, proven only for Gaussian rows; the analysis is layerwise, ignoring cross-layer effects and error feedback.
- End-to-end evaluation is small: 2 models, WikiText-2 perplexity only, mostly a single calibration seed; no downstream-task or longer-context evaluation, and no comparison against learned rotations or incoherence-plus-error-feedback pipelines (QuIP#).
- Real spectra violate the bounded-support assumption of Theorem 1 (top eigenvalue 10³–10⁵× the median); the limit still holds empirically via Proposition 3, but the uniformity guarantees do not directly cover the deployed damping-free setting (o_proj gaps reach 0.63 bit without damping).

## Relevance

对研究 LLM 后训练量化与压缩理论的工作直接相关：本文给出了"随机旋转 vs 逐列注水"这一量化核心工具选择的精确信息论刻画，以及可在量化前零成本执行的逐层决策规则，对理解 QuaRot/SpinQuant 类方法为何在真实网络上有效、何时会失效具有指导意义。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- What is the worst-case gap for the randomized Hadamard transform actually used in QuaRot/SpinQuant-style pipelines — does the sign-only structure ever fail the "asymptotically freeing" condition behind Proposition 7?
- Why does unrotated GPTQ with act-order beat a random rotation on 27% of layers (19/24 gate/up inputs) even after outlier clipping — is there a basis-ordering effect the pivot theory misses?
- Can Theorem 1's limit be extended to spectra with unbounded support (no damping), covering the o_proj inputs that reach 0.63 bit without damping?
- Does the weight-side cost C_w change qualitatively under learned rotations (SpinQuant) or error feedback (QuIP#), which jointly optimize for weight incoherence?
- How do the layerwise gaps compose into a whole-model rate-distortion tradeoff — can the per-layer decision rule be improved by a global bit allocation across layers?
