> **一句话总结：** 指出 GRPO 类 RLVR 的"序列置信度失配"问题（优势对策略置信度视而不见），并用一个乘法因子按"组内相对置信度与验证结果的对齐差距"重塑每个响应的优势——在 Qwen3-4B-Base 上将 DAPO 的五项数学基准平均 Pass@32 从 67.1 提升到 69.5（+2.4），同时把策略熵维持在基线的 2–11 倍以缓解熵坍缩。

> **TL;DR:** GRPO-family RLVR advantages are blind to how confidently the policy produced a response; SCARL fixes this "sequence-confidence misalignment" with one multiplicative, sign-preserving reshaping of the advantage (alignment gap between group-relative sequence confidence and the verified outcome, normalized to mean one per outcome class; a single temperature τ=5, no extra forward passes). On five competition-math benchmarks (AMC23, AIME24, AIME25, Minerva Math, MATH-500), DAPO+SCARL lifts Qwen3-4B-Base average Pass@32 to 69.5 vs 67.1 for vanilla DAPO (+2.4), beats all eight compute-matched rivals (GRPO, DAPO, GSPO, SAPO, Dr. GRPO, EAS, SEED-GRPO, UCAS), and improves 14 of 16 estimator×backbone pairings (mean +1.0) while keeping policy entropy 2–11× higher than the base estimators over the final ten steps.

## 核心贡献

- **提出并形式化"序列置信度失配"（sequence-confidence misalignment）**：分类器训练会把预测概率与标签绑定，但 GRPO 类算法的组相对优势只由组内奖励决定，与策略对该响应的置信度完全无关——勉强答对的成功与近乎必然答对的成功获得完全相同的更新，高置信度的错误与试探性错误受到同等力度的纠正。
- **SCARL 方法本身**：用一个严格为正的乘法因子重塑优势——序列置信度（行为策略的 token 对数似然之和，本就已为重要性比率算好，零额外开销）经组内标准化 + 温度 softmax 得到组相对置信度 p_i，对齐差距 w̃_i = 1−p_i（成功类）或 p_i（失败类），再在各类内归一化为类优势预算的份额（类内均值恒为 1）。
- **三条可证明的性质**：(1) 因子严格为正，更新方向永不翻转；(2) 类内质量守恒，置信度只决定预算怎么分、不改变预算大小；(3) 当类内置信度一致时精确退化为基线估计器——SCARL 是 GRPO 的严格推广。
- **一个超参数、即插即用**：仅有温度 τ（默认 5，一次性选定后全部 16 个组合复用不重调）；O(G) 开销；可无损嵌入 GRPO/DAPO/GSPO/SAPO 等任何组相对估计器，并与比率空间干预（clip-higher 等）可组合。
- **机制解释与验证**：修复失配同时是对熵坍缩的对症下药——抑制高置信度成功样本的正向强化（它们主导 log-prob × advantage 协方差）、最难地纠正高置信度错误、宽容试探性错误；实验证实熵与置信度–正确性分离度（Cohen's d）同时提升。

## Key Contributions

- **Names the blind spot.** Formalizes *sequence-confidence misalignment*: a group-relative advantage is a function of the group's rewards alone, so a hard-won success earns the same gradient as a near-certain one, and a confident error is corrected no harder than an exploratory one — violating what aligned training (same outcome, same confidence) should satisfy, in both outcome classes.
- **A minimal, provably safe fix.** SCARL reshapes each advantage with a single strictly-positive factor: sequence confidence c_i = Σ_t log π_old(y_t) (already materialized for importance ratios), group-standardized and softened into shares p_i; the alignment gap |t_i − p_i| against the binarized outcome, normalized to mean one within each outcome class. Proofs: sign preservation (no update is ever reversed), class-mass preservation (the advantage budget is unchanged), and exact recovery of the base estimator when class confidences agree.
- **Doubles as an entropy-collapse remedy.** The factor is anti-monotone in confidence among positive-advantage samples (damping exactly the high-logπ, high-Â terms that drive entropy decay) and monotone among negative-advantage ones (hardest correction on confident errors, sparing exploratory ones) — attacking the covariance at its source rather than via clipping or bonuses.
- **Strong empirical validation under a compute-matched protocol.** Best average Pass@32 and Avg@32 among nine methods on Qwen3-4B-Base; transfers without retuning across four estimators × four backbones (14/16 improved); ablations isolate the alignment target as the active ingredient.

## Methodology

SCARL augments any group-relative policy-gradient objective J_base(θ; {Â_i,t}) by replacing Â_i,t with w_i·Â_i,t, leaving rewards, importance ratios, and clipping untouched. Four steps, all group-level operations on scalars already computed during training:

1. **Sequence confidence (§2.2):** c_i = Σ_t log π_θold(y_i,t | x, y_i,<t) — the behavior policy's sequence log-likelihood, fixed across minibatch updates and free to compute (the per-token log-probs already exist for the importance ratios). The *sum* is kept deliberately over the per-token mean: it measures the probability committed to the whole response, separating long wanderers from short committers.
2. **Group-relative confidence (§2.3):** standardize within the group, c̃_i = c_i / (τ(σ_G + δ)), then p = softmax(c̃). Shift-invariance cancels intrinsic prompt difficulty; calibrated sharpness fixes the scaled logits' std at 1/τ so one temperature transfers across prompts, models, and training stages.
3. **Alignment gap (§2.4):** w̃_i = t_i − p_i, i.e. 1−p_i for samples whose advantage certifies success (t_i = 1) and p_i for failures (t_i = 0). Aligned samples (mastered successes, tentative errors) carry a small gap and are left nearly alone; misaligned ones (fragile successes, confident errors) absorb the update. Formally a focal-loss-style modulating factor with the group share as prediction and the verified outcome as label.
4. **Class-wise normalization (§2.5):** w_i = w̃_i / mean_{j∈S(i)} w̃_j within each outcome class — a conservation law (E[w_i | S(i)] = 1). Without it, near-uniform confidence would give success-class gaps ≈ (G−1)/G but failure-class gaps ≈ 1/G — a 7:1 ratio at G=8 that all but erases penalties (the ablation shows this is catastrophic).

Because w_i depends on π_old only, no gradient flows through it; SCARL acts as a per-sequence, confidence-dependent learning rate on the vanilla policy gradient at the start of each inner epoch. Training setup for all experiments: verl framework, DAPO-Math-17K prompt set, ±1 rule-based verifier rewards, 150 steps, group size G=8, batch 512 prompts, lr 1e-6, Qwen3-4B-Base primary (plus Qwen3-1.7B-Base, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B, Hunyuan-1.8B-Pretrain); evaluation samples k=32 responses per problem (temp 0.6, top-p 0.95, top-k 20) every 5 steps on AMC23, AIME24, AIME25, Minerva Math, and MATH-500, tracking both coverage (Pass@32, bootstrap-estimated) and per-draw accuracy (Avg@32).

## Results & Highlights

- **Main result (Qwen3-4B-Base, avg Pass@32 over AMC23/AIME24/AIME25/Minerva/MATH-500):** DAPO+SCARL reaches **69.5 vs 67.1 for vanilla DAPO (+2.4)** — the best of nine compute-matched methods (next best: DAPO 67.1, SEED-GRPO 67.0), and +15.0 over the untrained base model's 54.5.
- **Per-benchmark (Pass@32):** best on 4 of 5 — AMC23 **95.3 vs 90.4** (DAPO, +4.9), AIME24 **49.0 vs 46.1** (+2.9), AIME25 **50.1 vs 45.0** (+5.1), MATH-500 95.6 vs 95.5; the only loss is Minerva Math, **57.4 vs 59.6 for Dr. GRPO (−2.2)**.
- **Confidence-awareness alone is not enough:** EAS scores 64.7 — *below* vanilla GRPO's 66.0 — and UCAS falls to 59.9 (−6.1 vs GRPO), while SEED-GRPO is competitive at 67.0; the gain therefore lies in the alignment-gap construction, not in merely conditioning on uncertainty.
- **Generality (4 estimators × 4 backbones, τ=5 unchanged):** improves average Pass@32 in **14 of 16 pairings, mean Δ +1.0**; on Qwen3-1.7B-Base, DAPO+SCARL attains 58.0 vs 55.8 for DAPO (+2.2) and 56.4 for the best baseline (GSPO). The two regressions are both under SAPO and both small (−0.2 each). Every one of the 32 trained runs raises coverage over its untrained backbone (+5.6 to +18.3 points).
- **Ablations (Qwen3-1.7B-Base + GSPO, base = 56.4):** removing class-wise normalization collapses average Pass@32 to **47.0 (−9.4, within 0.4 of the untrained backbone's 46.6)**; outcome-blind shaping (gap to a constant, keeping all else) returns to baseline (56.6, +0.2 vs full SCARL's 57.1, +0.7) — the alignment target is the active ingredient; per-token mean confidence drops to 55.3 (−1.1); the τ sweep {0.2, 0.5, 2, 5, 10} gives 55.1 / 55.6 / 56.2 / 57.1 / 54.7, rising monotonically to the default τ=5 and falling beyond.
- **Mechanism — entropy preserved:** from step 12 onward SCARL keeps policy entropy above the base estimator's for all four estimators; over the final ten steps the mean entropy ratio is **2.0× on DAPO (1.02 vs 0.50), 4.1× on GSPO (0.49 vs 0.12), and 11× on SAPO (0.73 vs 0.07)**; GRPO is the exception (0.050 vs 0.035 — slowed but not averted under symmetric hard clipping).
- **Mechanism — confidence repaired:** over the final ten steps, Cohen's d for confidence separation between correct and incorrect rollouts rises from **0.65 to 1.06** (Qwen3-4B-Base/DAPO), **0.50 to 0.97** (DS-R1-Distill-1.5B/GSPO), and 0.95 to 1.20 (Hunyuan-1.8B/GRPO) — the first two crossing from medium into the large-effect regime (d > 0.8).
- SCARL is the top method at 20 of 30 logged training steps on both metrics, with the lead *widening* late in training (final-ten-checkpoint margins: +1.1 Avg@32, +1.6 Pass@32 over the next-best method).

## Authors & Credibility

The work comes from Baidu's Frontier Research Department (Jingbo Zhou and Hua Wu are corresponding authors; Hua Wu is a long-standing Baidu NLP research lead) with Zhenghong Ke of Nanjing University, who did the work as a Baidu intern. The paper is careful by current RLVR standards: a compute-matched protocol (all nine methods share the same recipe, differing only in the advantage transformation or surrogate), an explicit mechanism study (entropy + Cohen's d) rather than score tables alone, formal properties proved in an appendix with a line-by-line reference PyTorch implementation, and a public code release (https://github.com/kezhh163/scarl). The literature positioning (a comparison table against CDE, ProGRPO/ARM, CAPO, ACE, CW-GRPO, EAS, SEED-GRPO, UCAS by signal, space, form, sign protection, and reference point) shows genuine command of a crowded subfield.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** The misalignment *framing* is the paper's cleanest contribution — "same outcome should imply same confidence" is an operational criterion I have not seen stated this crisply elsewhere, and the design consequences (multiplicative, sign-preserving, class-normalized, exact-recovery) follow logically from it. That said, confidence-aware reweighting of group-relative updates is a crowded lane (eight related methods tabulated); the genuinely distinctive parts are the class-mean reference point and the two provable guarantees, not confidence conditioning per se — which the paper itself honestly demonstrates (outcome-blind shaping forfeits the gain, 56.6 vs 57.1).
- **Rigor:** The compute-matched design, the outcome-blind negative-control ablation, and the two-metric (Pass@32 + Avg@32) evaluation that distinguishes coverage gains from distribution sharpening are above the subfield's average. But all results appear to be **single runs with no seeds and no variance estimates**; many of the 16 pairing deltas (+0.1, +0.2, +0.5) are plausibly within run-to-run noise, so "14 of 16 improved" should be read as directional, not exact. Tables also report each run at its **best checkpoint by average Pass@32 across 30 evaluation points** — a mild selection bias, applied uniformly but real.
- **Reproducibility:** Good — shared hyperparameters, per-estimator settings, verifier routing, prompts, a 20-line reference implementation, and code release. The τ=5 default was swept once (Qwen3-1.7B/GSPO) and reused everywhere, which is a fair transfer test.
- **Significance:** The flagship +2.4 on Qwen3-4B-Base is solid; but the effect thins outside it (+0.1 to +0.5 on DS-R1-Distill-1.5B and Hunyuan backbones, −0.2 on both SAPO pairings), consistent with the paper's own headroom explanation (distilled/already-calibrated backbones have less misalignment to repair). Everything is ≤4B parameters, 150 steps, one math training set, and math-only evaluation — no code generation, no out-of-domain transfer, no longer training where entropy collapse might matter more.
- **Red flags:** The summed log-likelihood confidence **conflates response length with confidence** (long CoT answers are systematically "less confident"); the authors defend the choice and the per-token-mean ablation supports it, but the length channel is a real confound they never directly control for (e.g., no length-stratified analysis). AIME24/25 contamination is unaddressed. Under GRPO — the estimator most readers actually use — entropy still collapses (0.035 → 0.050), so the fix's strength depends on the base estimator's clipping regime. The Minerva loss to Dr. GRPO (57.4 vs 59.6) is disclosed but not analyzed.

## Strengths

- A precise, falsifiable diagnosis (the alignment criterion) rather than a generic "add entropy bonus" patch, with the fix derived directly from the diagnosis.
- Provable safety: the factor is strictly positive for any τ (no sign flips), preserves each class's advantage budget, and provably recovers the base estimator when confidences agree — SCARL can only act where misalignment exists.
- Zero extra forward passes and one hyperparameter (τ=5 selected once, reused across all 16 pairings without retuning) — unusually frugal for this line of work.
- The mechanism section actually tests the claimed causal chain (entropy higher from step 12 onward; Cohen's d crossing into the large-effect regime), not just endpoints.
- Honest negative results: EAS below vanilla GRPO, UCAS at 59.9, two SAPO regressions, and the GRPO entropy exception are all reported and explained rather than buried.

## Limitations

- Competition mathematics only, with binary ±1 rule-based verifier rewards; shaped/continuous rewards and other domains (code, general reasoning) are untested (acknowledged by the authors).
- Backbones span three model families but only up to 4B parameters, and training is short (150 steps); behavior at 32B+/long-horizon scale is unknown.
- Single training runs, no seed variance; several reported deltas (+0.1 to +0.5) are likely within noise; best-checkpoint selection slightly flatters every method.
- Summed sequence log-likelihood ties confidence to response length by construction; the length confound is argued away ("within a group all responses answer the same prompt") but never directly measured.
- The entropy benefit is estimator-dependent — under GRPO's symmetric hard clipping, SCARL slows but cannot prevent collapse.
- Inference-time uses of the aligned confidence (abstention, best-of-n selection) are named as future work but not explored.

## Relevance

RLVR/GRPO 训练方法与 LLM 推理强化学习直接相关：本文指出的"优势对置信度视而不见"是 GRPO 家族的结构性缺陷，SCARL 给出一个零额外开销、可证明不改更新方向的即插即用修正，且用熵与覆盖率的机制实验解释了收益来源——对关注 LLM 推理训练、探索坍缩与置信度校准的工作都有参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the alignment-gap construction still help at 30B+ scale and on longer training runs, where entropy collapse is the binding constraint — or does headroom vanish as base models become better calibrated (as the small +0.5 gains on DS-R1-Distill already hint)?
- How does SCARL interact with code-generation or out-of-domain evaluation, where the verifier is noisier than math's exact-match rules and the binary outcome t_i may be unreliable?
- Can the length confound in summed log-likelihood confidence be separated — e.g., does an explicit length-stratified or length-mixture confidence change which successes get damped?
- Would combining SCARL (advantage space) with clip-higher or covariance control (ratio/gradient space) stack additively, as the composition argument suggests, or do they fight over the same entropy budget?
- Since SCARL sharpens the confidence–correctness separation (Cohen's d 0.65 → 1.06), does the aligned confidence transfer to inference-time abstention or best-of-n selection at no extra cost — the use case the authors defer?
- Why does the method lose to Dr. GRPO on Minerva specifically (57.4 vs 59.6) while winning everywhere else — is Minerva's shorter-answer style interacting with the summed-confidence signal?
