Why This Matters

Long-context inference is bandwidth-bound. This shows how far runtime, error-aware precision can reclaim the quality 4-bit quantization loses — the speed-vs-reliability tradeoff, quantified.

「核心议题：低比特/混合精度量化引入的误差如何在运行时被建模、估计与控制——极限性能优化下的可靠性。」— Relevance

Critical Edge

No silicon. The 2.55×/12.56× system gains are ASAP7-synthesis + simulator projections — and the ablation shows plain Hadamard rotation alone nearly matches the novel online allocation (PPL 6.871 vs 6.868).

simulator-only figures · narrow baselines (BDQ/PLENA/FP16) · short-context energy 5–9% worse

Methodology — how it works

Offline budgets + online decisions, on a PLENA-style 16×2048 flattened systolic array of 128 subarrays with independently configurable input widths.

01 · OFFLINE
Fisher budget plan
Diagonal-Fisher damage (BRECQ-style, NLL-scaled); Lagrangian search bisecting λ assigns ~4-bit avg budgets across W/A/Q/P/K/V, per context length.
→
02 · ONLINE
Cheap statistics
Per group: magnitude P ≈ κ·amax²; width-w MSE ε(w) ≈ Δw²/12, capped at P — no elementwise squaring, stats stored at INT6.
→
03 · DECIDE
Greedy widths, both sides
Separable both-input proxy R = P_B·ε_A + P_A·ε_B; largest-gain one-bit upgrades until budget spent → per-subarray widths for both inputs (PSU overlaps with transfers).
→
04 · SERVE
Attention-guided KV reads
Nested MXINT bit-planes: K widths from previous-query softmax, V widths + V-skipping from current probabilities — no renormalization.
  • One INT5 master encoding serves all INT2–INT5 prefix reads — within 0.013 PPL of per-width quantized weights.
  • Online precision decision costs 1.97% of core area, <0.01% prefill time, ≤0.64% first-decode step.
  • V-skip + mixed reads cut KV traffic ~55%/45%; 128K-context KV read time −57% vs non-nested INT4.

Honest short-context result (1.4K in / 0.2K out): throughput on par (153,411 vs 153,060 tok/s), energy 5–9% worse — INT5 masters shrink the feasible batch (119 vs 147).

  • Scaling: Llama-3-70B PPL 3.34 vs 4.12; zero-shot 79.23% (+4.86 pts over PLENA, 0.72 below FP16).
  • Co-design: online allocation cuts output NMSE 32.9% (prefill) / 37.2% (decode) vs uniform INT4 for +26.6% area; INT5 captures ~88.5% of INT7's NMSE reduction at half the area.

Strengths

  • Error proxy grounded in the exact GEMM error expansion; separability assumption explicitly tested against alternatives (Fig 8c).
  • Overhead story quantified away: 1.97% area, <0.01% prefill time, ≤0.64% first-decode step.
  • Nested encoding is elegant — one master serves all read widths within 0.013 PPL, turning width into bandwidth savings.
  • Evaluation breadth: dense, MoE (Llama-4-Scout, Qwen3-235B-A22B) and vision-language (GLM-4.6V) models; measured 4×B200/vLLM baseline; full component ablations.
  • Honest reporting of the short-context regression and nested reads' ~80%-of-peak bandwidth.

Limitations

  • No silicon and no public RTL/simulator — 2.55×/12.56× figures cannot be independently verified.
  • Quality baselines narrow (BDQ/PLENA/FP16); FGMP, MoBiQuant and modern rotation-based W4A4 absent from the tables.
  • Perplexity only on 2048-token windows; long-context quality evidence is RULER retrieval only.
  • Heavy offline calibration: per operator type and per context length — a real serving burden.
  • INT5 masters reduce memory-feasible batches at short context (119 vs 147) → 5–9% worse energy than PLENA.
  • Needs custom hardware; unrealizable on current GPU tensor-core formats.

Questions for Further Exploration

  • Does the both-input gain survive against a strong rotation-based W4A4 baseline (QuaRot/FlatQuant + FGMP) at the same 4-bit budget?
  • What does V-skipping without attention renormalization do on high-entropy generation — code, creative writing, multi-document QA?
  • Can a Fisher budget plan calibrated at 64K serve 128K traffic, or must every serving context length carry its own calibration?
  • Could the amax² / Δw²/12 error model double as online SDC/fault detection when observed error deviates from calibration?
  • Does the 12.56× energy edge hold if the GPU baseline quantizes its KV cache (KIVI-style INT4) to raise feasible batch sizes?
