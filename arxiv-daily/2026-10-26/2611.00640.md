> **一句话总结：** 本文首次在十亿参数级预训练语言模型（Pythia-1B 的全部 154 个训练检查点、Dyck 括号匹配任务、144 个组件、22,176 个组件–检查点对）上系统对照奇异学习理论（SLT）的损失局部几何与机制可解释性（MI）的因果电路：Fisher 迹的变化以中位偏秩相关 0.51 跟踪零消融因果效应的变化（97% 组件为正），输出增益 Fisher 对消融的偏相关高达 0.98/0.97，但大温度 LLC 计数恰恰跟不上已成形电路组件的增长（0.09），且在自然括号语境下最终检查点的秩相关从 0.64 跌至 0.16。

> **TL;DR:** The first head-to-head test of whether singular learning theory's local geometry and mechanistic interpretability's causal circuits describe the same thing in a pretrained LLM: on Dyck(2,10)/Dyck(4,16) bracket matching across all 154 Pythia-1B checkpoints, against a 26-component five-stage circuit validated by held-out interventions, changes in a component's Fisher trace track changes in its zero-ablation KL with median partial rank correlation 0.51 (positive for 97% of components), and the gain Fisher tracks ablation levels/changes at partial correlation 0.98/0.97 — but the large-temperature LLC-style count follows circuit components' ablations only weakly (0.09 in changes), the quartic coefficient is what nonlocal SGLD actually registers (partial 0.50 vs −0.07/−0.02 for Fisher trace and gain Fisher), and the final-checkpoint rank agreement of 0.64 drops to 0.16 on natural bracket contexts. Read it for the negative as much as the positive results: this paper carefully delimits which SLT quantity carries which mechanistic signal at the 1B scale, and shows the standard LLC estimator leaves the prediction-preserving region entirely.

## 核心贡献

- **首次在预训练十亿参数模型上对照 SLT 与 MI。** 此前 SLT 的局部学习系数（LLC）估计要么针对整模型，要么针对未经独立验证的组件；本文用纯干预手段（零消融 + 贪心恢复、探针、直接 logit 归因、路径修补、留出子空间干预）在 Pythia-1B 上确立了一个五阶段 26 组件括号补全电路，作为与几何量对照的"地面真值"。
- **形式化对应关系。** 证明两套描述都是同一个映射（组件参数 → 固定输入与干预下的模型预测）的函数：其一阶导数同时决定 Fisher 信息、学习系数与干预效应（命题 1：Jacobian 秩恒定时 λ_c = ϱ_c/2）；奇数阶项在对称平均下抵消，首个修正为四阶系数 B_c；两组件联合扰动产生由交互曲率 N_ab 刻画的混合项。由此导出四条可检验预测 P1–P4。
- **把 SLT 估计器关进"预测保持区域"U。** 指出标准 SGLD 采样会令每个权重移动其 RMS 的数倍、改变模型预测（附录 K），而强到能锁住参数的定位化又只剩先验；本文改用在 U 内（KL ≤ 0.02 nats、精度变化 ≤ 0.02）的重要性加权采样，并在同一批采样上计算精确二次近似作为对照，从而把"曲率之外"的部分干净地分离出来。
- **全谱系测量。** 用随机 Lanczos 正交逼近对全部 144 个组件 × 154 个检查点（22,176 对）计算 Fisher 谱，使二次部分估计 q_Q 可在任意温度下半解析求值；并用 ±r、±r/2 采样精确抵消二次项估计 B_c。
- **逐项结论（哪些量对应哪些机制量）。** Fisher 迹跟踪全模型共享的因果效应趋势（变化偏相关 0.51，其中很大部分是共享趋势：与人群消融中位数轨迹配对也有 0.50）；增益 Fisher 与大温度计数携带组件特异信息（0.97–0.98 / 0.40）；四阶系数是非局部采样器实际记录的东西（0.50）；混合项跟踪联合消融的非可加部分（电路对 0.16 vs 对照对 0.05）。

## Key Contributions

- **First SLT–MI comparison in a pretrained billion-parameter model.** Prior LLC estimates at this scale concerned whole models or components whose roles were never independently established; here a five-stage, 26-component bracket-completion circuit in Pythia-1B is validated by interventions alone (held-out subspace ablations, not just probes) and used as the reference for the geometry.
- **A formal correspondence.** Both descriptions are functions of one object — the map from a component's parameters to the model's predictions under inputs and interventions. Proposition 1 shows that when the response-map Jacobian has constant rank, the LLC equals rank/2 and the Fisher matrix is the pullback of the output Fisher metric; averaging over ±perturbations kills odd terms so the first correction beyond curvature is the quartic coefficient B_c; pairwise mixed terms give the interaction curvature N_ab. This yields four testable predictions (P1–P4).
- **Taming the SLT estimator with an explicit region U.** Standard SGLD localization lets draws move each weight by several times its RMS, changing predictions (Appendix K); the paper instead draws only inside the region where predictions are preserved (KL ≤ 0.02 nats, accuracy change ≤ 0.02) and importance-weights them, evaluating the exact quadratic approximation on the same draws — isolating what lies beyond curvature.
- **Complete measurement grid.** Fisher spectra of all 144 heads/MLPs at all 154 checkpoints via stochastic Lanczos quadrature on exact Hessian–vector products (22,176 component–checkpoint pairs), giving the quadratic estimate q_Q at every temperature without drawing; B_c estimated by exactly cancelling the quadratic term with draws at r and r/2; replication on an independent input batch.
- **Term-by-term verdicts.** Fisher-trace changes track causal-effect changes (median partial rank correlation 0.51) largely as a model-wide shared trend; the gain Fisher is the component-specific quantity that tracks ablations (partial 0.98 levels / 0.97 changes); the large-temperature count tracks population-level but not circuit-component change; the quartic part is what a nonlocal (standard SGLD) sampler registers; pairwise interaction curvature tracks joint-ablation nonadditivity (0.16 vs 0.05 controls).

## Methodology

The paper instantiates both descriptions on Pythia-1B (16 layers × 8 heads + 16 MLPs = 144 components) at all 154 released checkpoints, run in float64, on bounded Dyck languages Dyck(k, D) where the model must predict the next closing bracket (128 strings of 128 tokens, k = 4, averaged over 5,475 closer positions; circuit identification on k = 2).

**The shared object.** A component's displacement u (scaled so one unit moves a weight by its tensor's RMS) defines a KL divergence K_c(u) from the checkpoint's predictions. Its expansion under Gaussian directions, K_c(rz) = ½r²zᵀF_c z + r³K₃(z) + r⁴K₄(z) + O(r⁵), averages to E_z K_c(rz) = r²A_c + r⁴B_c + O(r⁶), where A_c = ½tr F_c is the Fisher trace and B_c the quartic coefficient. Because patching the output change caused by a displacement reproduces the perturbed prediction exactly, every parameter perturbation is an activation intervention, so MI and SLT act on the same responses.

**Circuit identification (MI side).** Zero-ablation of each of the 144 components plus greedy restoration to 80% of clean contrast yields candidates; attention patterns, probes, direct logit attribution, and path patching assign roles to 26 components in five stages (token encoding → stack updating → bracket-type reading → closer mapping → output shaping). Four variable subspaces — TOKEN (11-d), STACK (24-d), POSITION (32-d), ANSWER (3-d) — are fitted and tested by held-out subspace removal against random rank-matched controls.

**Geometry (SLT side).** Draws are confined to an explicit region U (KL ≤ 0.02 nats, cross-entropy change ≤ 0.1, accuracy change ≤ 0.02, no tensor displaced > 0.5 RMS), evaluated at u, u/2, u/4; the tempered estimate is computed by importance weighting e^{−τK_c} on these prior draws rather than SGLD, and the quadratic part q_Q uses the same draws — plus the spectral-sum formula q_Q(τ, r) = ½ Σ w_vk τ r² θ_vk / (1 + τ r² θ_vk) evaluated at any temperature from the Lanczos spectrum. B_c is isolated by the combination K̄(r) − 4K̄(r/2) scaled by 4/(3r⁴), which cancels the quadratic term exactly. The gain Fisher F_gg = ∥o_c∥²_{Σ_c} is computed by central differences; changes of A_c are split among downstream/own/upstream weights by Shapley values over hybrid checkpoints.

**Correspondence tests.** P1–P4 are tested with partial rank correlations (controlling clean loss, weight scale, training step) between geometric quantities and ablation effects, in levels and in adjacent-checkpoint changes, with block bootstrap (blocks of 16 checkpoints) and a replication on an independent input batch (seed 20261001).

## Results & Highlights

- **Fisher trace tracks causal change (P1):** across the 154 checkpoints, adjacent-checkpoint changes in A_c correlate with changes in zero-ablation KL at median partial rank correlation 0.51 (positive for 97% of the 144 components; bootstrap interval above zero for 88%; 0.56 for the 26 circuit components, 0.49 for the other 118). But much of this is a shared model-wide trend: pairing each component's A_c with the population-median ablation trajectory already gives 0.50.
- **Gain Fisher is the component-specific signal:** F_gg tracks zero-ablation KL at partial rank correlation 0.98 in levels and 0.97 in changes (component-specificity 0.12 against the population median); once F_gg is among the covariates, A_c retains essentially no association with ablation (0.02 levels / 0.07 changes).
- **Circuit validation is strong:** removing the 24-d STACK subspace from the stack-writing components on held-out strings drops closer accuracy from 61.4% to 14.8%, versus 58.4–62.6% for random rank-matched subspaces; removing the 3-d ANSWER span drops it to 39.3%. Across training, the opener-distance probe rises from R² = 0.034 to 0.684 while depth is decodable at initialization (layer-2 R² = 0.864).
- **The estimator must be caged:** at radius r = 0.025 all 256 draws stay inside U for 99.96% of the 5,082 panel pairs (98.3% / 88.5% / 52% at r = 0.05 / 0.1 / 0.2), whereas SGLD chains move each weight by several times its RMS; inside U the mean loss matches its Fisher approximation to within 0.04% (median), and the quartic correction to the count is only 0.032% of q_Q at τ r² = 10⁴.
- **What nonlocal SGLD registers is the quartic part (P2):** with A_c controlled, B_c tracks the standard nonlocal SGLD estimate at partial correlation 0.50 while A_c and F_gg do not (−0.07, −0.02); B_c is slightly negatively associated with the component's causal effect (−0.24 levels).
- **Groups and pairs:** for the eight redundant layer-0 heads, summed Fisher traces track summed single ablations (change correlation 0.71) but not the joint ablation (0.03), and the group's joint LLC estimate lies 5.8% below the sum of its members', as the quadratic bound predicts (P3); pairwise interaction curvature N_ab tracks the nonadditive part of joint ablations at median partial correlation 0.16/0.14/0.16 on three independent batches for circuit pairs, versus 0.05 for matched control pairs (P4).
- **Generalization warning:** the final-checkpoint Metropolis LLC ranking of all 144 components agrees with zero-ablation ranking at Spearman 0.64, but on natural bracket contexts (where the circuit was not identified) this falls to 0.16. Replication on an independent input batch reproduces trajectories (rank correlation 0.997 for A_c).

## Authors & Credibility

Ethan Ji, Wei Wang, and Yizhou Sun are from the Department of Computer Science at UCLA. Wei Wang and Yizhou Sun are established UCLA professors (data mining / graph learning and representation learning respectively), and the SLT literature the paper builds on (Lau et al. 2025's LLC estimator, Hoogland et al. 2025's stagewise development, Wang et al. 2025's weight-refined LLC) is cited precisely and engaged with critically rather than decoratively. The empirical protocol is unusually serious for interpretability work: float64 forward passes, exact Hessian–vector products, stochastic Lanczos quadrature with full reorthogonalization, Pareto-smoothed importance-sampling diagnostics (Vehtari et al. 2024), block bootstrap, Shapley decompositions over hybrid checkpoints, and an independent-seed replication batch — 22,176 component–checkpoint pairs in the main grid. The paper states its limitations plainly (one model, one synthetic task, one input batch, fixed coordinates), includes a reproducibility statement (protocol in Table 1, proofs in Appendices A–B, procedures in Appendices C–Q) and an AI-use statement. It is a preprint (31 Oct 2026), not yet peer-reviewed.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** genuinely novel problem framing — this is, to my knowledge, the first attempt to validate SLT quantities against an independently intervention-identified circuit at the 1B scale, and the first to separate the LLC estimate into Fisher and quartic parts on identical draws. The negative findings (what the count does not track) are as valuable as the positive ones.
- **Rigor:** high. Partial rank correlations control for clean loss, weight scale, and training step; the shared-trend confound is explicitly measured (population-median pairing 0.50 vs own 0.51); the gain-Fisher result (0.98) honestly supersedes the headline Fisher-trace result (0.51) by showing that once the component-specific norm is included, the trace adds nothing (0.02).
- **Weak points in the evidence:** the pairwise result (P4) rests on a partial correlation of 0.16 vs 0.05 — statistically separated but small in absolute terms, so "interaction curvature tracks joint ablations" is a weak-signal claim. Similarly, the large-temperature count tracks circuit components' ablation changes at only 0.09 (levels −0.01), i.e., the SLT-style count fails exactly where MI says a mechanism is forming — the paper admits this but it undercuts the hope that geometry can localize emerging mechanisms. The base model's partial skill (0.595 closer accuracy for k = 4; 77% of errors are openers) means the "mechanism" is a noisy, partially formed bracket circuit, not a clean stack implementation, so the correspondence is tested against a fuzzy target.
- **Generalization:** single model (Pythia-1B), single synthetic task, single synthetic input batch; the paper's own appendix shows the final-checkpoint correspondence collapsing from 0.64 to 0.16 on natural bracket contexts. Whether any of this transfers to natural-language behaviors at larger scale is entirely open.
- **Reproducibility:** good in principle (explicit protocol, seeds, draw counts, region definitions) but no code release is mentioned in the text, and the compute burden (float64 Hessians on 154 checkpoints of a 1B model) is substantial; the circuit-identification stage involves the usual manual MI judgment calls (greedy restoration threshold of 80%, role assignment by attention patterns and DLA) that another team might reproduce only approximately.
- **Red flags:** none serious. The claims are carefully scoped and the abstract matches the body. One caveat: many headline numbers are rank correlations on trajectories, which support "co-movement" claims, not causal identification of the correspondence.

## Strengths

- Provides the missing controls for SLT at scale: an independently validated mechanism, an explicit prediction-preserving region U, and an exact Fisher-information baseline computed on the same draws — resolving the "is SLT just curvature?" question empirically (inside U, almost yes: quartic correction 0.032% of q_Q).
- The decomposition of which geometric quantity answers which mechanistic question (trace → shared trend; gain Fisher → component's own role; quartic → nonlocal sampler artifact; mixed terms → interactions) is actionable for anyone using devinterp-style tooling.
- The circuit work stands on its own: five-stage bracket circuit in a base 1B model, validated by held-out subspace interventions with random rank-matched controls (STACK removal 61.4% → 14.8% vs 58.4–62.6% for random).
- Exceptional measurement hygiene: float64, exact HVPs, Lanczos with reorthogonalization, importance-sampling diagnostics, block bootstrap, independent-input replication, Shapley attribution of trace changes.

## Limitations

- One model (Pythia-1B), one synthetic task (Dyck bracket matching), one main input batch, and a fixed coordinate system (RMS units from the final checkpoint) — all acknowledged by the authors.
- The correspondence degrades sharply off-distribution: 0.64 → 0.16 Spearman on natural bracket contexts where no circuit was identified.
- The large-temperature count — the quantity closest to the standard LLC — does not track circuit components' formation (0.09 changes), so the practical payoff for automated mechanism discovery is currently modest.
- The base model never fully solves the task (0.595 closer accuracy, 77% of errors being spurious openers), which limits how cleanly "the mechanism" is defined.
- Evidence is correlational (rank correlations of trajectories); no experiment establishes a causal arrow from geometry to mechanism or vice versa.

## Relevance

对关注大模型训练动力学与机制可解释性交叉的工作非常有参考价值：它给出了首个把 SLT 的局部几何量（Fisher 迹、增益 Fisher、四阶系数）与干预验证的电路逐项对照的十亿参数级实证，指明了哪些几何量可用于自动监测机制形成、哪些只是共享趋势或采样伪影。若你关心用损失景观几何做可解释性或能力涌现监测，这篇是必读的方法论对照基准。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the gain Fisher (partial correlation 0.98 with ablation effects) provide a cheaper, replacement-value-free importance score that outperforms standard pruning/attribution scores (e.g., MacKay, Molchanov) for circuit discovery on natural-language tasks?
- Why does the large-temperature count fail specifically for circuit components (0.09) — is it because formed mechanisms grow along already-constrained directions, and would a rank-normalized or spectrum-shape statistic recover the signal?
- How much of the correspondence survives on natural-language behaviors (IOI-style tasks) and larger models (Pythia-6.9B, or instruction-tuned checkpoints), given the 0.64 → 0.16 drop on natural bracket contexts?
- Can the region U (prediction-preserving displacements) be characterized adaptively per component and checkpoint rather than by fixed KL/accuracy thresholds, and does the LLC estimate converge to λ_c as τ → ∞ within it?
- Do the mixed quartic terms B_ab and interaction curvature N_ab give a geometry-only way to detect redundant component groups (like the layer-0 heads) without running joint ablations?
- The late-training rise of the Fisher trace is attributed mostly to the input (Shapley share 1.08) — does this reflect sharpening predictions (logit concentration) rather than growing mechanism, and should downstream MI/SLT comparisons control for output entropy?
