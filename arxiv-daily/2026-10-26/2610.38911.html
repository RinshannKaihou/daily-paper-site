Why this matters

Any LM work on self-explanation, self-monitoring, or verifiable alignment needs this: behavior-matched self-reports can be causally hollow — mechanism-supervised training is what makes them trustworthy.

Methodology

distilled · not a transcript

A LoRA-finetuned LM receives text plus a natural-language query naming a locale — layer, index, token position — and must verbally output that signal’s value. Three signal types: MLP neuron activations (Llama-3.2-1B), attention weights, and GemmaScope SAE features (Gemma-2-2B).

1 · Query“The neuron activation of N10.24 at token ‘hello’ is {E}” — localize signal at c=(layer, index, token).
→
2 · Two-sided evalEndogenous: report true activation on unseen text. Interventional: runtime do() replaces it with counterfactual v — does the report follow?
→
3 · VerdictHigh + high = faithful introspection. High endogenous + chance interventional = causal bypass.

The fix — interventional training: supervise the report against the counterfactually perturbed value during finetuning, mixed with the endogenous loss and a drift controller:

Ljoint = w·Lend + (1−w)·Lint + β·LKL do(ac = v), v ~ U(k·vmin, k·vmax) — k=10 neurons, k=1 attention; β adjusted per batch to hold KL to base ≤ 0.1. Circuit claims verified via LoRA-norm localization, gradient attribution, and head ablations.

SAE-feature precision: probe 0.04±0.02 vs SFT 0.69±0.12 vs joint-loss 0.65±0.12 — no baseline beats SFT, and none is causally faithful.

  • OOD robustness: the joint-over-SFT gap widens exactly where endogenous performance drops — largest on C4-Bengali, smallest on near-indistribution English Wikipedia.
  • Scaling bounds: at fixed data budget, faithful introspection covers ≤2 layers and ~10 neurons; zero generalization to unseen layers or neuron indices; token positions do generalize (train ≤128 → eval 512).
  • Replication: bypass + fix hold on 8 LMs (Llama-3.2-1B/3B, Llama-3.1-8B, Qwen3-0.4B–8B-Base, Gemma-2-2B); the joint-vs-SFT gap stays constant or grows with scale.
  • Circuit evidence: LoRA updates peak at the signal layer (MLP) and the layer after (attention); endogenous signals ride many dense heads, interventional signals a few sparse U-shaped “gating” heads.
  • LoRA-gating negative result: disabling adapters on the input text keeps endogenous scores but drops interventional correlation to chance — unrecoverable. Finetuning must modify the signal site itself.

Strengths

  • Turns a fuzzy philosophical debate into a ground-truth, two-sided, falsifiable eval with a clean taxonomy: faithful introspection / causal bypass / anomaly detection.
  • The fix is simple, well-motivated, and shown to be the only thing that works — the LoRA-gating alternative is exhaustively falsified with recovery attempts reported.
  • Genuine mechanistic depth: LoRA-norm localization, orthogonal-channel routing (W_up/W_down vs W_gate), gradient attribution to the correct neuron, head taxonomy.
  • Honest negative results: the late-layer early-token blind spot is owned, not buried.
  • Broad replication — 8 LMs, 5 OOD corpora — with code and detailed appendices released.

Limitations

  • The task is minimal — a direct state translation that may be “simply a probe,” not the semantic introspection people actually care about.
  • Structural blind spot: late-layer, early-token signals stay at chance even with interventional training; suggested fixes (looped architectures) are untested speculation.
  • No cross-locale generalization: unseen layers and neuron indices introspect at chance; semantic-label transfer via Neuronpedia brought bypass back.
  • Strict do()-definition may over-count bypass (hydra-effect self-repair, distributed features); the relaxed eval is proposed but not implemented.
  • Baselines are probes and verbal readout — no head-to-head with modern external meta-model explainers; the verbal SAE baseline leans on LLM-judge lexicons.

Questions for Further Exploration

open threads

  1. Does interventional training transfer to semantic-level introspection — or is bypass unavoidable once you cannot do() the target?
  2. Can looped/recurrent blocks or locale-specific regularization close the late-layer, early-token blind spot without re-introducing bypass?
  3. Does “anomaly heads ⊂ endogenous heads” hold for injected concepts and deception-related features, where bypass stakes are highest?
  4. Could locale identifiers as soft embeddings (Li et al. 2025 / Loracles) buy generalization to unseen neurons — or is per-locale data the price of faithfulness?
  5. What if introspective objectives entered during pretraining — do representations become “pre-introspectable,” as the LoRA-gating result hints?
  6. Is the k-stretched uniform intervention the right counterfactual — would compensation-frozen (path-patching-style) eval rescue models currently judged as bypassing?
