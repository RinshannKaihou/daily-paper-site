arXiv 2610.37422 · Mechanistic Interpretability · 26 Oct 2026 · Gemma4-12B-it

Text and image arithmetic converge to one shared latent number frame — related by a single scaled rotation, not shared coordinates.

Beyond the Readout: A Latent Numerical Frame Shared Across Modality and Operation ↗
Oriol Juan Sabater (UAB) · Alex Ferrando de las Morenas · Pau Rodríguez · Xavier Suau — Apple & Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona
mechanistic interpretability multimodal representations arithmetic circuits causal interventions representation geometry

一句话总结：通过因果干预（DAS）在 Gemma4-12B-it 的残差流中定位到一个 22 维因果子空间，发现文本与图像两种模态的算术表示不共享坐标方向（直接迁移仅恢复 53.2% 因果效应），但通过单个缩放旋转（αQ）即可对齐至 91.4%；该子空间可精确分解为 9 维“读出”子空间 D 与 13 维“潜在”子空间 L——L 与数字 unembedding 完全正交却以 75.5% top-1 检索携带数值信息，并在时间上提前缓冲完整答案、引导下一个数字的生成（77.6% 重定向）。

Key metric · causal-effect recovery, text ↔ image
53.2%→91.4%

Direct reuse of the 22-dim causal subspace across modalities recovers only 53.2% of the intervention effect; fitting one scaled rotation αQ (α ≈ 0.65 text→image) restores 91.4% on held-out expressions. An unconstrained linear map only reaches 96.3%.

Why this matters

Settles whether multimodal LLMs share one internal representation of number — and shows they compute full answers before speaking digit by digit.

Platonic Representation Hypothesis · latent planning
Critical edge

Evidence covers only two-digit arithmetic the model already solves in both modalities — error trials, multiplication, and larger operands are unaddressed, and code is unreleased.

Methodology — how the claim was pinned down

1 · Conditionstext / image × add / subtract, operands 0–99
2 · DAS22-dim causal subspace, layer 43, final pre-generation token
3 · Transportfit scaled rotation αQ on displacement vectors → held-out τ
4 · Splitrank-9 readout D ⊥ 13-dim latent L
5 · Syncfour frames → one global latent frame
6 · Timingmismatch edits (D:y, L:ỹ) + KV-cache trace

Four conditions cross input modality (identical expressions as text vs. black-on-white images) with operation (addition, subtraction), 5,050 expressions each, restricted to problems Gemma4-12B-it answers correctly in both modalities. Thinking mode is disabled and the model emits only the result, so no chain-of-thought tokens enter the residual stream.

Distributed Alignment Search learns an orthonormal basis for a k-dim causal subspace at layer 43; k = 22 is the smallest dimension retaining ≥90% of the k=32 baseline's autoregressive interchange-intervention accuracy. Notably, Fourier-aligned directions — arithmetic's natural coordinates in prior work — are causally insufficient here: the 11 most Fourier-aligned directions recover only 23% of the effect in Gemma4-12B-it vs. 84% in Llama3.1-8B.

Cross-condition transport uses normalized recovery τ over 3×3 seed pairs, fitting a single scaled rotation (α > 0, Q ∈ O(k)) to counterfactual displacement vectors, evaluated strictly on held-out expressions. For the output-artifact question, the basis is rotated against the centered rank-9 digit-unembedding matrix and split into readout D and exactly orthogonal latent L; orthogonal group synchronization tests whether four condition frames form one global coordinate system. Mismatched hybrid activations (D from answer y, L from ỹ sharing the first digit) trace when the latent answer is read out. The full causal chain replicates in Ministral-3-14B.

Strengths

  • Replaces the ill-posed "are the representations the same?" question with three causally testable hypotheses, each answered by a dedicated experiment.
  • The single-α scaled-rotation law (0.532 → 0.914) is quantitative and falsifiable — far more informative than a binary "converged / not converged" verdict.
  • The exact D/L split shows readout-orthogonal directions still retrieve numbers at 75.5% top-1, cleanly killing the "it's just the output token" objection.
  • The mismatched-intervention design turns a paradox into mechanism: latent planning with concrete numbers (77.6% next-digit redirection; layer-44→45 conversion).
  • Exceptional control discipline: random subspaces, shuffled labels, scaling-only, random rotations, withheld pairs, and a second model family.

Limitations

  • Arithmetic scope is two-operand, two-digit addition/subtraction; multiplication (distributed, depth-dependent), larger numbers, and word problems are out of scope.
  • Analysis sits at one layer (43) and the final pre-generation token of one model family plus one replication; persistence across depths/architectures is unverified.
  • Correct-answer filtering excludes error trials — the studied distribution is only the model's clean pathway.
  • The 13-dim L inherits a heuristic (k=22 retention threshold, rank-9 split); no independent justification that 13 is the true latent dimensionality.
  • The delayed readout is localized to a layer range, not attributed to specific attention heads or circuits.
  • Code not yet released at submission time ("will be released soon") — independent reproduction pending.

Questions for further exploration

  1. Does the shared latent frame survive beyond [0,99] — 3–4 digit operands, decimals, number words — or is 13-dim geometry a small-number-specific solution?
  2. Which attention heads between layers 44–45 perform the L→D conversion via the KV-cache, and are they the same across all four conditions?
  3. Why are visual displacements compressed (α≈0.65) — vision-encoder output norm, attention mixing, or training-data frequency of image-rendered arithmetic?
  4. Does latent subspace L exist for multiplication, where representations are instead distributed and depth-dependent?
  5. On error trials, is L occupied by a computed-but-misread-out answer, or is it unstructured?
  6. Can next-digit redirection be exploited for inference-time arithmetic steering, e.g. correcting carries in long addition without re-computation?
