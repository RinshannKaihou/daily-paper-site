> **一句话总结：** CONFIRM 把"运行分析"与"能否报告该发现"分离：LLM 只负责生成分析前冻结的机器可读分析计划，5 个确定性关卡（多重性、混杂、统计功效、分析稳定性、独立复现）决定报告与否；在自建的 NeuroClaimBench（489 条可执行神经影像论断，含 150 条故意有缺陷的阴性对照）上，基线方法错误支持 23–38% 的缺陷对照，而 CONFIRM-Full 为 0%，同时保留最高 61% 的文献支持论断，且其失败诊断比 Self-Refine 用更少的 LLM 调用产出更多获数据支持的修正。

> **TL;DR:** LLM agents can now run neuroimaging analyses end-to-end, but they still let the LLM (or an naive p<0.05 filter) decide which findings deserve reporting. The authors show LLM judges endorse site-confounded findings even when the confound evidence is handed to them (29% false support on designed negative controls; NeuroClaw-adapted 23%, VERITAS-adapted 38%). CONFIRM replaces this judgment with a frozen, cryptographically-hashed pre-specified analysis plan plus five deterministic gates (multiplicity, confounds, power, multiverse stability, independent replication) under three reporting standards. On their new NeuroClaimBench (489 executable claims from ADNI/OASIS3/UK Biobank/ABIDE/ABCD/BSNIP etc., including 150 flawed negative controls), CONFIRM-Full falsely supports 0% of controls vs 23–38% for baselines while recovering 48% of literature-supported scalar claims (Screen: 61%); retrofitting CONFIRM as a veto on NeuroClaw cuts control false support from 23% to 0% with no loss of recovery, and its gate diagnoses beat Self-Refine at follow-up revision (70 vs 54 supported revisions, 653 vs 1,301 LLM calls, ~2x holdout support). The price: Full withholds 59% of literature-supported claims (87% of which fail replication in these datasets) — conservative, but auditable.

## 核心贡献

- **提出"论断治理"（claim governance）问题**：现有 agentic 科学系统只验证"代码能跑、数值对得上"，却把"哪些结果值得报告"交给 LLM 判断。作者实证表明 LLM 裁判和固定启发式（如 p<0.05 过滤器）会接受 23–38% 故意违反方法学要求的阴性对照，且即使把站点混杂证据直接呈现给 LLM 裁判，它仍会背书站点混杂的发现。
- **CONFIRM 框架**：把"跑分析"与"能否报告"分离。LLM 先把假设翻译成机器可读的分析计划 C = (θ, D_disc, D_rep, Z, M₀, G)（估计量、发现/复现队列、协变量、声明的检验数、门阈值），在任何结果数据被读取前冻结并加密码学哈希；确定性代码执行分析，5 个门（搜索历史与多重性、测量混杂、统计功效、分析稳定性/多重宇宙、独立复现）决定报告与否，三档报告标准（Screen / Replicate / Full）逐级加严，只有 Full 能宣布 "confirmed"。
- **有界、失败导向的后续搜索**：被扣留的论断获得结构化诊断（脆弱/功效不足/未复现），LLM 可在 R 轮 × K 个候选的预算内提出修订，确定性验证器禁止改动核心问题、预测变量、方向、数据集与阈值，并施加累积多重性惩罚，最终在预留 holdout 数据上复检——防止"换着法子挖出显著"。
- **NeuroClaimBench 基准**：489 条可执行神经影像论断（源自 544 条候选，剔除 55 条模糊/不可执行），覆盖 sMRI、rs-fMRI、PET，横跨健康、ADHD、ASD、AD/老化、精神病人群，含 279 条科学论断、60 条外部队列迁移论断、150 条五类故意缺陷的阴性对照（站点混杂 42、广域搜索 42、随机标签 42、功效不足 21、不可复现 3）；96 条论断经双 LLM 评估 + 仲裁 LLM + 专家复核获得文献参考标签。
- **系统实证**：门消融（去掉混杂门使对照错误支持 +24 个百分点；去掉复现门使文献不支持论断错误支持 +26 个百分点）、阈值敏感性、参考标签规则敏感性、对 150 条对照的高预算压力测试（10,716 个候选修订无一通过）以及对既有 agent 的审计/改造实验。

## Key Contributions

- **Identifies the reporting gap in agentic science**: existing neuroimaging agents verify that code runs and numbers match, but delegate the "should this finding be reported?" decision to LLM judgment. The paper demonstrates empirically that an LLM judge, a significance filter (p<0.05), and adapted versions of VERITAS and NeuroClaw accept 23–38% of designed negative controls that deliberately violate methodological requirements — LLM judges endorse site-confounded findings even when the site–predictor association is presented to them as evidence.
- **CONFIRM (frozen claim governance)**: the LLM drafts a machine-readable, schema-checked analysis plan C = (θ, D_disc, D_rep, Z, M₀, G) that is frozen and hash-locked before any outcome data are read; deterministic code then executes it and produces an analysis record E(C). Reporting decisions are made by code, not the LLM: five deterministic gates evaluate (1) search history and multiplicity (Bonferroni over M_eff = max{M₀, M_history, M_observed}; BH q-values for brain-wide claims), (2) measured confounds (site, scanner, field strength, software version), (3) prospective power (default reference effect d = 0.3 at 80% power), (4) analytic stability (a 24-fork multiverse for scalar claims, 6 forks for brain-wide; ≥60% consistent), and (5) independent replication (same sign, p<0.05 in every replication cohort). Three nested reporting standards — Screen (gates 1–2), Replicate (1,2,5), Full (all five, the only standard that can report "confirmed") — are chosen before results are seen.
- **Bounded, failure-directed follow-up search**: withheld claims get deterministic diagnoses (fragile / under_powered / non_replicated). The LLM proposes up to K candidate revisions per round for R rounds under a strict validator that forbids changing the question, predictor, direction, datasets, or thresholds; candidates face a cumulative multiplicity penalty and are finally re-tested on holdout data excluded from the search — so iterative exploration cannot silently manufacture significance.
- **NeuroClaimBench**: 489 executable, frozen neuroimaging claims (from 544 candidates; 55 excluded as ambiguous/non-executable) spanning sMRI, rs-fMRI, and PET across healthy, ADHD, ASD, AD/aging, and psychosis cohorts (ADNI, OASIS3, NACC, UK Biobank, HCP/HCP-A, ABCD, ABIDE I/II, ADHD-200, COBRE, FBIRN, BSNIP2, CNP, etc.), including 150 designed negative controls in five failure modes (42 site-confounded, 42 broad-search, 42 random-label, 21 underpowered, 3 nonreplicating). 96 claims carry literature reference labels adjudicated by GPT-5.5 + Gemini 3.5 Flash with Claude Opus 4.8 arbitration plus expert review.
- **Rigorous evaluation**: matched comparisons with baselines given identical evidence (no reference labels or gate states shown), 95% CIs, gate ablations, threshold- and label-rule sensitivity analyses, holdout re-evaluation of standalone verdicts, a high-budget adversarial stress test (10,716 follow-up candidates on the 150 controls; zero pass), and a retrofit audit layered onto an existing agent.

## Methodology

CONFIRM operates in two modes: standalone (it governs the agent during execution) and retrofit (it audits an existing agent's claim plus its timestamped execution record after the fact). The pipeline has three separated stages:

1. **Plan specification and freezing.** The LLM translates a natural-language research question into the analysis plan C = (θ, D_disc, D_rep, Z, M₀, G): θ fixes the estimand (predictor/contrast, outcome, hypothesized direction, scalar vs. brain-wide scope), D_disc/D_rep the discovery and replication cohorts, Z the covariates, M₀ the declared number of tests, G the gate thresholds. The plan passes a schema check, is hash-locked before any outcome data are read, and cannot be altered afterwards. In retrofit mode, the original plan must have been fixed and timestamped before results were observed.
2. **Deterministic execution.** Frozen scripts fit the models and emit an analysis record E(C) with effect estimates, multiverse (fork) refits, power calculations, replication statistics, and design checks. The LLM only sees E(C) to draft prose; an automated consistency check discards any number in the draft that differs from E(C).
3. **Gating and constrained reporting.** The five gates map (C, E) to pass/fail. Confirmed requires all five gates on discovery + replication cohorts; failures receive prioritized diagnostic labels (fragile → multiplicity/confound/stability, under_powered → power, non_replicated → replication). A follow-up loop (≤R rounds, ≤K candidates/round) lets the LLM revise within bounds: candidates altering the predictor, direction, datasets, thresholds, or dropping a previously included covariate are rejected by a deterministic validator; each candidate's effective family size is max{M_declared, M₀ + H_r} over the cumulative hypothesis count H_r; provisional passes are re-checked against the final count and then evaluated on holdout/external cohorts that were never visible during candidate generation.

NeuroClaimBench construction was outcome-blind: eligibility (identifiability, executable cohort pairs) was assessed before execution; 544 candidates were filtered to 489 executable claims with discovery/replication/holdout partitions sharing no participants. Literature labels (96 claims; 47 strict, 49 provisional) required PMID/DOI-cited evidence matching population, contrast, modality, outcome, and direction, adjudicated by two assessor LLMs, an arbitrator LLM, and expert review. Metrics: Recovery (share of literature-supported claims accepted) and False support (share of negative-label claims accepted), reported separately for designed controls and literature-unsupported claims; failure-to-run counts as withheld.

## Results & Highlights

- **False support on designed controls (N=150)**: baselines accept 23–38% (VERITAS-adapted 38% [30,46], direct LLM judge 29% [22,37], significance filter 28% [21,36], NeuroClaw-adapted 23% [16,30]); CONFIRM-Screen accepts 3% [1,8], CONFIRM-Replicate 1% [0,4], CONFIRM-Full 0% [0,2] — a 23–38 pt reduction with the strictest standard.
- **Recovery trade-off (matched common scalar set, 31 supported / 15 unsupported / 150 controls)**: CONFIRM-Screen recovers 61% [42,78] of literature-supported claims (vs 65% for VERITAS-adapted, 55% for the direct LLM judge) while cutting control false support from 38% to 3%; CONFIRM-Full recovers 48% with 13% literature false support and 0% control false support — the nested standards trace a Pareto-optimal frontier. On the full literature set including brain-wide claims, CONFIRM-Screen recovers 55% vs 41% for the direct judge, and CONFIRM-Full matches 41% while accepting 11% unsupported claims vs the judge's 21%.
- **Where baselines fail**: they accept 79–100% of the 42 site-confounded controls (site-confounded cases are 74% of VERITAS's accepted controls, 95% for the judge, 97% for NeuroClaw-adapted, 98% for the significance filter) even though LLM judges were explicitly given each site variable's association with the predictor; CONFIRM's confound gate rejects all of them at every standard. VERITAS-adapted also accepts 24% of broad-search controls.
- **Gate ablations and sensitivity**: removing the measured-confound gate raises CONFIRM-Full control false support by 24 percentage points; removing the replication gate raises literature false support by 26 points. Across all tested gate-threshold variations (stability ρ∈{0.5,0.7,0.8}, power∈{0.7,0.9}, confound/replication α∈{0.01,0.10}, d∈{0.2,0.4,0.5}) CONFIRM-Full never accepts a control (Ctrl. = 0%; 0–1% at d∈{0.2–0.5}); recovery only collapses at an extreme d = 0.1 (41% → 14%).
- **Withholding is informative**: on the full literature set CONFIRM-Full withholds 59% of literature-supported claims, and 87% of those withheld claims fail to replicate in the designated independent cohort (73% also fail multiplicity/stability) — i.e., most of what it suppresses does not hold in these data under a pre-specified analysis.
- **Follow-up search (215 withheld standalone claims; R=3, K=5)**: CONFIRM diagnosis yields 70 revisions supported on search data vs 54 for Self-Refine and 49 for failure-blind retry, using 653 LLM calls vs 1,301 and 765 respectively, and roughly doubles holdout support (17% vs 9% and 7%). In the adversarial stress test on all 150 designed controls (R=10, K=10), none of 10,716 evaluated candidate revisions passed even provisionally.
- **Retrofit audit on NeuroClaw-adapted**: a Full-standard veto cuts control false support from 23% to 0% (Screen veto: 1%) while retaining the agent's 48% recovery and 13% literature false support; CONFIRM diagnosis also improves the agent's own revisions — reported drafts rise from 28.6% (no feedback) to 37.5% vs 35.4% with self-critique, holdout support from 5.7% to 8.9% vs 6.4%, with fewer calls (894 vs 1,357).
- **Standalone holdout audit**: of 289 autonomously formulated plans, Full confirmed 26% (74/289); on untouched holdout partitions, 25% of initially confirmed claims passed Full again vs 3% of withheld claims, and the replication gate alone passed for 63% vs 15%.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: the ingredients (preregistration, Bonferroni/BH correction, ComBat, multiverse analysis, prospective power) are all standard; the contribution is the composition — making the reporting decision itself deterministic and auditable inside an agentic loop — plus the first claim-level (not code-execution-level) benchmark for this decision. This is a genuine and useful reframing, closest to POPPER (sequential falsification with Type-I control) and EviBound (machine-checkable artifacts), both of which target different stages (hypothesis falsification / task completion) rather than observational reporting rules.
- **Rigor**: high. Frozen hashed plans, outcome-blind eligibility, gate ablations, label-rule sensitivity (CONFIRM-Full false support 7.4–9.1%, recovery 41%→37% under the most permissive reference rule), threshold sensitivity, and an adversarial 10,716-candidate stress test on the controls. The finding that LLM judges accept site-confounded claims *when shown the confound association* is a crisp, well-isolated negative result.
- **Weak baselines / adapted baselines**: VERITAS and NeuroClaw are evaluated as "-adapted" re-implementations (VERITAS's fixed rule; NeuroClaw's three-persona GPT-5.5 panel with veto) rather than the original end-to-end systems, and the direct LLM judge is a single GPT-5.5 call. Stronger judge prompting (e.g., chain-of-thought on the confound table) might close some of the 29%→3% gap; the paper does not test judge-side improvements.
- **Small, partly LLM-labeled ground truth**: only 46–70 claims carry literature labels (51 supported / 19 unsupported on the literature set; 31/15 on the scalar set), 49 of 96 labels are provisional (37 rest on assessor-only agreement), and 221 executable claims (45% of the benchmark) are unresolved and excluded from metrics. CIs are correspondingly wide (e.g., literature false support 13 [2,40]).
- **Conservatism is the trade-off, and the headline gap is dominated by one failure mode**: 42 of 150 controls are site-confounded, which is where baselines almost exclusively fail; CONFIRM-Full withholds 59% of literature-supported claims. The authors' defense (87% of those fail replication in these cohorts) is honest but dataset-partition-dependent — a genuinely true literature effect measured in an underpowered replication split would also be withheld. The default power reference d = 0.3 was used for *every* benchmark claim because none had a literature effect size, which is convenient and sensitivity-checked, but arbitrary; and the nonreplicating-control family has only 3 members, so that gate's control-side evidence is thin.
- **Reproducibility**: the framework is fully specified (formal gate rules in Appendix Table 4, fork grids, thresholds, budgets), and the benchmark protocol is described in detail, but the paper text does not state a public code/data release for NeuroClaimBench — a significant practical limitation given the frozen hashes and partitions others would need to reuse it.
- **Red flags**: none serious. Claims are carefully scoped ("passing the gates does not establish biological truth"), and the abstract's numbers match the tables.

## Strengths

- Attacks the correct layer of the problem: not "did the code run" but "may the finding be reported" — the exact decision current agentic-science systems silently delegate to LLM judgment.
- Deterministic, auditable gates replace LLM judgment for the high-stakes decision while keeping the LLM where it helps (plan drafting, bounded revision, prose), with cumulative multiplicity penalties and holdout re-testing that close the garden-of-forking-paths loophole in iterative agent loops.
- The negative-control benchmark design (claims with one deliberate, known flaw per failure mode) is a clean way to score reporting decisions without needing ground truth for new hypotheses, and it transfers to other fields (batch effects in genomics, selection bias in epidemiology, invalid instruments in econometrics).
- Honest, thorough evaluation: identical evidence for all methods, CIs, ablations quantifying each gate's contribution (+24 pts control FS from removing the confound gate; +26 pts literature FS from removing replication), sensitivity to thresholds and label rules, and retrofit results showing the framework improves an existing agent rather than only beating strawmen.
- Failure diagnoses double as actionable feedback: more supported revisions than Self-Refine with roughly half the LLM calls and ~2x holdout support — governance that speeds the agent up rather than only vetoing it.

## Limitations

- CONFIRM-Full recovers only 48% (scalar set) / 41% (literature set) of literature-supported claims — over half of published-supported effects are withheld in these data; users must accept high false-negative rates (or drop to Screen, which accepts 40% of literature-unsupported claims).
- The labeled evaluation sets are small (N = 70 literature, N = 46 scalar, 96 total labels), nearly half the labels are provisional, and labels themselves were adjudicated by LLM panels (GPT-5.5 + Gemini 3.5 Flash + Claude Opus 4.8) before expert review — circularity risk if the same model family drafts plans and labels evidence.
- Gates only audit *measured* confounds (site, scanner, field strength, software version); unmeasured confounds (medication, SES, comorbidities) pass through untouched, and passing all five gates "does not establish biological truth" (authors' own scope statement).
- Benchmarked on observational neuroimaging cohorts only; the framework assumes effect-direction hypotheses and frequentist pass criteria, and generalization to other fields requires hand-translating each domain's requirements into executable gates.
- Baselines are adaptations rather than original systems; no stronger LLM-judge baselines (multi-call reasoning, tool-augmented judges) were tried.
- No stated public release of code, frozen plans, or the benchmark's data partitions; the power gate's universal d = 0.3 default and the 3-member nonreplicating control family leave two gates' calibration under-evidenced.

## Relevance

这篇论文直击 LLM agent 自动化科研中的"报告治理"问题：用预注册式的冻结分析计划 + 确定性统计关卡取代 LLM 判断来决定哪些发现可以报告，其多重性校正、混杂审计、multiverse 稳定性与独立复现门设计，对任何构建可信 AI 科学家/自动化实验管道（包括神经影像之外的基因组学、流行病学）的人都是直接可借鉴的治理范式与基准设计参考。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would a tool-augmented, multi-step LLM judge (allowed to run the confound tests itself, or to reason chain-of-thought over the evidence table) close the 29%-vs-3% false-support gap, or is LLM judgment on site confounds structurally unreliable?
- Can the gates be extended to unmeasured/latent confounds (e.g., sensitivity analyses, negative-control outcomes, or causal discovery over the covariate graph) rather than only the four measured acquisition variables?
- Is the 59% withholding rate of literature-supported claims a property of CONFIRM's thresholds, or of these specific cohort partitions — i.e., how does recovery scale as replication-cohort N grows into the thousands Marek et al. (2022) recommend for brain-wide associations?
- Does the frozen-plan + holdout protocol transfer to sequential/experimental settings (agents that choose the next experiment, as in POPPER) where the analysis plan cannot be fully pre-specified before data collection?
- How sensitive are the results to the LLM that drafts analysis plans — do weaker drafters produce plans that fail schema/identifiability checks, and does the governance then silently reduce scientific throughput?
- Could NeuroClaimBench's designed-negative-control methodology be replicated cheaply in other quantitative fields (batch-effect controls in single-cell genomics, selection-bias controls in observational economics) to benchmark reporting decisions there?
