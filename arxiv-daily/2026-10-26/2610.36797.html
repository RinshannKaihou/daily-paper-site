Language Models Keep Abandoned Analyses: Residue and Adaptation after Garden-Path Sentences ↗

Key Metric

+0.72 nats

Object-attachment residue left by one garden-path prime — 76% of the effect of a prime whose NP really is an object. Positive in 22/22 open models (124M–70B), significant in 21; Pythia-160M the lone exception.

full evidence ↓

Why This Matters

Overridden facts persist the same way: stale representations keep steering predictions, so appending a correction is weaker than deleting the original text.

Direct warning for context engineering, knowledge editing, and RAG-style correction.

⚠ Critical Edge

The causal story rests on the patching route alone: it covers only 6–9 of 24 models, and the behavioural manipulation failed its own pre-registered check.

Methodology § how it was shown

garden-path prime “While the man hunted…” → NP key/value states computed before disambiguation (lower/mid layers) → later text reads the abandoned object analysis → object attachment rises +0.72 nats

Patch test: swap those NP states for subject-analysis states → residue almost eliminated (retained ≤0.21–0.22 in Qwen2.5-32B / Llama-3.1-70B; pooled 0.37). Replacing main-clause states changes nothing (1.01 retained).

  • Models — 24 open causal LMs (GPT-2, Pythia, Llama, Mistral, Gemma, Qwen; base + instruct; 124M–70B; one MoE), exact log-probabilities, no chat templates.
  • Clean 2×2 contrast (Eq. 2: GP−NB minus CUE−NC) matches punctuation within brackets and verbs across them — ruling out comma habits, verb bias, and lexical repetition; NOM and PASS controls rule out punctuation habit and local string coherence.
  • Two measures — L: log-odds of object attachment (production-like); S: surprisal of the main-clause continuation (comprehension-like); plus free generation (~57k prompts per model) scored by a rule-based coder.
  • Causal tools — activation patching at every layer, cross-context NP-state transplants, linear probes (96–99% role accuracy) + role-direction steering; two-way bootstrap over primes × families.
  • Beyond syntax — 200 overridden-statement items in ten correction frames, with attention-knockout localization of the carrier.
pre-registered · dated records held-out set frozen until analyses fixed failures reported (App. D)

Results § beyond the headline

The residue replicates held-out and on classic items; the opposite, error-driven adaptation appears only on comprehension-like surprisal; and the raw human statistic reverses in LMs unless punctuation is controlled.

Why the reversal: up to 29% of completions after a garden-path prime are themselves comma-less garden paths (vs 0.2–2.4% after comma primes; cross-model ρ = −0.82). Excluding them, the raw contrast is positive in all 12 models (median 10 pp).

Strengths § what it does well

  • Confounds ruled out by design — the 2×2 contrast plus NOM/PASS controls isolates the residue from punctuation habit, verb bias, and local string overlap.
  • Four converging methods — behavioral contrasts, prime-property regression, patching with two donor designs, probing + steering — all point to pre-disambiguation NP states.
  • Pre-registration culture: dated predictions fixed before data collection, failures reported (App. D), held-out set untouched until analyses were frozen.
  • The punctuation finding is an immediately useful warning for anyone porting human sentence-production paradigms to LMs.
  • Extends syntactic priming to the practical problem of context correction and knowledge editing.

Limitations § read critically

  • Causal evidence is narrow relative to the behavioral base: patching/steering on 6–9 of 24 models; the App. F behavioural manipulation failed its own pre-registered check.
  • Comprehension-side effects are tiny and sign-variable (0.05 nats) — the "two opposite effects" framing leans on regression slopes, not robust mean differences.
  • The PASS control shows a substantial local-coherence residue in exactly the strongest models (0.45 in Llama-3.1-70B; >0.5 in Qwen2.5-7B variants) — beyond the "median 12%" framing.
  • English-only, templated materials; role-direction steering worked in only 3 of 7 models; no closed frontier models (exact log-probs + state access required).
  • The Eq. 2 contrast was defined after inspecting the first two discovery models; no code release mentioned in the main text; completion coding checked by a single annotator.

Questions for Further Exploration § open threads

  1. Does the NP-state residue survive long contexts, and does its decay match the human lexical-boost vs abstract-priming dissociation?
  2. Does instruction tuning systematically change residue or adaptation (Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct moved away from corrected values)?
  3. Can a targeted KV-cache edit or "retraction token" at the noun phrase mimic deletion of corrected text without harming other context?
  4. Why do the strongest models show the largest local-coherence (PASS) residue — is it an inevitable byproduct of scale?
  5. Does the derived human prediction hold: varying only the continuation of a fixed garden-path prefix leaves later human comprehension unaltered?
  6. Do chat-tuned models show the same raw-statistic reversal when prompted through chat templates, where comma conventions differ?
