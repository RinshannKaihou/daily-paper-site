> **一句话总结：** NVIDIA 提出 NLD-Uni，一个统一的离散扩散多模态大模型：通过"跨范式在线策略蒸馏"（用 AR VLM 教理解、用连续流模型 Qwen-Image 教编辑）加上基于 VQ 码本球树的"渐进自精炼"扩散过程（token 经多个中间年龄态逐步腐蚀而非一步跳到 mask），在 11 项理解基准平均 78.3 分（超此前最强统一扩散模型 LLaDA2-Uni 17.1 分、追平 AR 的 Qwen3-VL-8B），GenEval 0.89、HPSv3 11.12（超过 Qwen-Image 的 10.91），ImgEdit 4.17，且扩散解码吞吐达 AR 的 2.7 倍（552.6 vs 202.7 tokens/s）。

> **TL;DR:** NLD-Uni is a purely discrete unified diffusion LLM (Elastic-MoT, 8B understanding tower + 8B generation tower) that (1) distills visual understanding from an autoregressive VLM teacher (Nemotron-3-Nano-Omni) via a transitive AR-OPD + self-consistency loss and image editing from a continuous flow teacher (Qwen-Image) via teacher-refined student rollouts, and (2) replaces binary mask/clean states with a ball-tree-based progressive token corruption (token age 0..K) enabling self-refinement at inference. It averages 78.3 across 11 understanding benchmarks (vs 61.2 for LLaDA2-Uni, +17.1; on par with AR Qwen3-VL-8B at 78.1), reaches 77.0 on MathVista (+5.5 over its base; naive OPD copying collapses it to 47.7), ties the best T2I models on GenEval (0.89) and beats Qwen-Image on HPSv3 (11.12 vs 10.91), scores 4.17 on ImgEdit (+0.36 over base, +0.27 over off-policy KD), and decodes 552.6 tokens/s vs 202.7 for its own AR view (2.7x). Trained on 64 H100s for 26 days; model and code promised public.

## 核心贡献

- **跨范式在线策略蒸馏（Cross-Paradigm OPD）框架**：首次把 OPD 从同范式师生扩展到异范式——用冻结的 AR VLM 教师（Nemotron-3-Nano-Omni）教扩散学生做视觉理解/推理，用连续流模型教师（Qwen-Image）教图像编辑。关键设计是"传递式"目标：先用 AR OPD 损失对齐学生的 AR 视图（块大小=1），再用自一致性损失让学生的扩散视图对齐其自身 AR 视图（仅在 mask 位置计算、对 AR 目标做 stop-gradient），避免了直接把教师分数拷贝到扩散视图造成的上下文失配（该 naive 方案使 MathVista 从 71.5 崩到 47.7）。
- **渐进自精炼扩散过程（Self-Refining Diffusion）**：把标准 MDM 的二元 {clean, mask} 状态空间扩展为 V×A（token 年龄 a=0 干净、a=K 全 mask），前向腐蚀通过在 VQ 嵌入空间构建的平衡二叉球树上按年龄递增的随机跳转实现（131k 码本对应 D=18 深度树，仅需 524KB 显存，而完整两两距离矩阵要 32GB）。基于 DNDM 式非马尔可夫每 token 跳变区间 (τ, τ+ε)，推理时 token 可在中间态逐步精修而非一步定型，为离散扩散带来了连续流的迭代自修正能力。
- **Elastic-MoT 统一架构 + 弹性推理视图**：沿用并扩展 LaViDa-O 的双塔设计（8B 理解塔 + 8B 生成塔，浅层联合注意力、深层解耦），单一模型按任务裁剪加载参数——VLM 视图 8B、T2I 视图 12B、交错理解+生成视图 16B，理解任务相比 8B VLM 无额外开销。
- **当前最强的统一扩散多模态模型（SOTA among unified DLLMs）**：理解、多模态推理、T2I、图像编辑四大类任务同时大幅缩小与专用 AR VLM 和连续扩散模型的差距，理解平均分甚至略超 AR 专用模型 Qwen3-VL-8B。

## Key Contributions

- **Cross-paradigm on-policy distillation**: distills visual understanding from a frozen AR VLM teacher and visual generation (image editing) from a continuous flow teacher (Qwen-Image) into one DLLM. For the AR teacher, a transitive objective supervises the student's AR view with the teacher (standard OPD on block size 1) and the student's diffusion view with its own AR view via a masked-position KL consistency loss (stop-grad on the AR target). Directly copying teacher scores into the diffusion view (OPD-LM style) creates ill-formed supervision ("New Francisco" example) and collapses MathVista from 71.5 to 47.7.
- **Progressive self-refining masked diffusion**: extends the state space from V ∪ {[m]} to V × A with token age a ∈ {0..K}; forward corruption jumps a token to random neighbors in progressively larger balls of a balanced binary ball tree over the 131k-code VQ embedding space (D=18, 524KB for the tree vs 32GB for the full pairwise distance matrix). A DNDM-style non-Markov per-token jump interval (τ, τ′=τ+ε) embeds the K discrete jumps in continuous time; at inference each token is progressively refined inside its interval, giving DLLMs the iterative refinement ability of continuous flows. K is randomized in {1..17} per training step for flexible inference.
- **Teacher-refined OPD for image editing**: student rollouts are re-encoded with the teacher's VAE, partially noised (best ratio r=0.9), and re-denoised by the frozen flow teacher over multiple ODE steps; the resulting teacher-refined student outputs are used as SFT targets. This keeps supervision on-policy with respect to the student's evolving distribution and beats off-policy KD on independent teacher generations by +0.27 ImgEdit.
- **Elastic unified architecture**: two-tower Elastic-MoT (8B understanding tower initialized from the NLD diffusion LM + 8B generation tower, joint attention in early layers) with three elastic views — VLM 8B active, T2I 12B (4B understanding + 8B generation), interleaved 16B — so each task loads only the parameters it needs.
- **State of the art among unified DLLMs** across visual QA, multimodal reasoning, grounding, T2I, and editing, with substantial inference speedups over AR decoding.

## Methodology

**Architecture.** NLD-Uni follows LaViDa-O's Elastic-MoT: an understanding expert tower (initialized from the NLD block-diffusion LM) and a generation expert tower (initialized as a copy), interacting through joint attention in early layers and decoupled later. Input images are dual-encoded (continuous semantic encoder RADIO-H + discrete IBQ VQ encoder with 131k codes); output images are VQ codes only. Text and image tokens are trained with the Scheduled Block Diffusion (SBD) objective — each image is one resolution-dependent block; block size 1 recovers plain AR.

**Self-refining diffusion (Sec 3.2).** The state space is S = V × A. The per-token corruption process U_{k|k−1}(u|c) is uniform over N_{D−k}(c), the ball-tree neighborhood of token c at depth D−k: as age a increases, corruption jumps to progressively more distant (larger-ball) codes, morphing a one-hot into progressively wider distributions until the terminal uniform-over-vocabulary state. The per-token jump schedule extends DNDM: sample τ_i ~ U([0,1−ε]), τ_i′ = τ_i+ε; age progresses uniformly inside (τ_i, τ_i′); ε→0 recovers one-step masked diffusion, ε=1 corrupts all tokens at the same rate. Reverse marginals are analytic given the tree, enabling both training targets and inference-time progressive refinement.

**Cross-paradigm OPD — understanding (Sec 3.3).** The student's AR view generates the current block on-policy; the frozen AR teacher provides token-level KL supervision on that rollout (AR OPD loss). The diffusion view is then supervised on the same AR rollout corrupted by the forward process, via a consistency KL between the student's diffusion prediction and its own (stop-grad) AR prediction, evaluated only at masked positions. Consistency-only training collapses (24.1 accuracy at 400 steps), and the consistency loss does not degrade AR performance.

**Cross-paradigm OPD — generation (Sec 3.4).** For image editing with the Qwen-Image teacher: decode student VQ rollout to pixels, re-encode with the teacher VAE, noised to ratio r, denoise with the teacher over multiple ODE steps conditioned on the input image and instruction, then re-encode the refined output to VQ codes and apply the standard SFT loss on its forward-corrupted versions.

**Training.** Three stages on 64 H100 GPUs over 26 days (OPD stage: 5 days): (1) 400k steps on 127M filtered text-image pairs (LAION-2B/COYO/SA-1M/JourneyDB/BLIP3o/ShareGPT4o-Image, recaptioned with Qwen3-VL); (2) 2×100k steps adding instruction tuning, grounding (RefCOCO, GranD), and editing data; (3) 10k steps of OPD (76.8k reasoning prompts from the Nemotron-3-Nano-Omni training set, 100k editing prompts) mixed with T2I SFT batches — notably, no OPD teacher is used for T2I because the base model already beats Qwen-Image/FLUX on GenEval and HPSv3.

## Results & Highlights

- **Understanding (Table 1, 11-benchmark average):** NLD-Uni scores **78.3 avg vs 61.2 for LLaDA2-Uni (+17.1)** and 65.6 for the AR+diffusion BAGEL — and edges out the specialized AR model Qwen3-VL-8B (78.1). Per-benchmark: MMMU val 60.7 vs 50.4 for the best prior diffusion model NLD-VLM (+10.3); InfoVQA 84.0 vs 75.6 Fast-DVLM (+8.4); RefCOCO test 95.4 vs 88.2 LLaDA2-Uni (+7.2); ScreenSpot-Pro 49.2 vs 0.4 LLaDA2-Uni (+48.8, though partly because prior DLLMs are near-zero on GUI grounding; AR reference Qwen3-VL-8B is 54.6).
- **Multimodal reasoning (Table 2):** cross-paradigm OPD lifts the base model on every benchmark — MathVista 77.0 vs 71.5 (+5.5, essentially matching Qwen3-VL-8B's 77.2), MathVision 45.4 vs 35.8 (+9.6), MathVerse 45.3 vs 35.5 (+9.8), Geometry3k 67.9 vs 54.4 (+13.5), We-Math 74.9 vs 59.8 (+15.1). The naive OPD-LM-style baseline collapses MathVista to 47.7 (−23.8 below base), justifying the transitive consistency design.
- **Text-to-image (Table 3):** GenEval overall **0.89, tying LLaDa-2-Uni (0.89, which additionally uses a 6B continuous diffusion decoder) and beating Qwen-Image (0.87)**; Position 0.90 vs 0.76 for Qwen-Image. DPG 86.6 vs 88.3 for Qwen-Image (slightly behind). On the human-preference HPSv3, NLD-Uni reaches **11.12, surpassing continuous-flow models Qwen-Image (10.91) and FLUX.1-dev (10.88)** — the self-refining process's largest ablation gains are exactly on HPSv3.
- **Image editing (Table 4, ImgEdit):** overall **4.17 vs 3.81 base (+0.36 from cross-paradigm OPD) and 3.90 for off-policy KD (+0.27)**; this beats LaViDa-R1 (3.90), LLaDa2-Uni (3.92), BAGEL (3.20) and Step1X-Edit (3.06), with only GPT-4o (4.20) and the Qwen-Image teacher (4.27) ahead. Largest category gain: Extract 3.59 vs 2.26 base.
- **Ablations:** against masked diffusion, uniform diffusion, and mask+token-editing over 20k-step runs (Fig 7, 64 NFE), self-refining is best on average with the clearest edge on HPSv3; at NFE=64 the best refinement count is N=8 (HPSv3 11.105 vs 11.005 for N=1, Table 6). MDM-Prime-style sub-token partial masking fails to converge at this scale (GenEval 0 after 20k steps). Teacher choice matters in non-obvious ways: the stronger Kimi K-2.6 teacher transfers worse than Nemotron-3-Nano-Omni (MathVista 71.6 vs 78.1 in AR view) due to tokenizer/thinking-format mismatch. Teacher-refinement noise ratio r=0.9 is optimal (4.17 vs 3.90 at r=1.0).
- **Efficiency (Table 10, H100, MathVista):** diffusion decoding at confidence threshold 0.90 gives **552.58 tokens/s vs 202.74 for the same model's AR view (2.7×) at equal accuracy (75.0 vs 76.1)**; relaxing to 0.60 reaches 997.95 tokens/s (4.9× AR) with accuracy dropping only to 73.4. Figure 1 additionally reports 7.14 T2I images/min (vs 5.21 for LLaDa2-Uni, 1.4×) and 4.37 editing images/min (vs 1.89 LLaDa2-Uni / 1.42 BAGEL, 2.2–3.1×).

## Authors & Credibility

The work is from NVIDIA (the PDF carries a 2026 NVIDIA copyright; authors Shufan Li, Hanrong Ye, and Greg Heinrich are marked as core contributors, with senior authors Aditya Grover — UCLA professor working with NVIDIA — and Pavlo Molchanov, a principal research scientist at NVIDIA known for efficient-model work). The same team previously produced LaViDa/LaViDa-O and NLD, so the Elastic-MoT backbone and diffusion-LM initialization are well-trodden ground for them. The paper is unusually transparent for an industrial release: it includes negative results (naive OPD collapse, MDM-Prime divergence, Kimi teacher underperforming), full training configuration tables, and 33 pages with derivations (Appendix A), data mixtures (B), and extensive ablations (C). The abstract states model and code are publicly available, aiding reproducibility. Compute scale is serious (64 H100s × 26 days), consistent with the claimed results.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: The two core ideas are genuinely new in combination: (i) cross-paradigm OPD — prior OPD work (OPDLM) only converted a same-weights AR model to diffusion; NLD-Uni distills a *different, stronger* AR teacher into an already-trained diffusion student and handles the context-mismatch problem with a transitive consistency loss; (ii) codebook-geometry-aware partial corruption via a ball tree, which unlike MDM-Prime's sub-token factorization actually scales to a 131k-code vocabulary. Both are backed by the right ablations, including the naive-baseline collapse (MathVista 47.7) that proves the design is load-bearing rather than decorative.
- **Rigor**: Strong on ablations (paradigm comparison, refinement steps, rollout temperature, teacher choice, noise ratio, off-policy KD control). Weaknesses: the "Δ vs Diff. SOTA" row in Table 1 takes the max per column across heterogeneous diffusion baselines of very different sizes (1.3B Show-o to 16B LLaDA2-Uni), inflating headline deltas like ScreenSpot-Pro +48.8 (LLaDA2-Uni scores 0.4 there — a near-zero baseline makes the delta meaningless even if 49.2 is genuinely good). The self-refinement ablation (Fig 7) uses 20k-step runs rather than the full ~500k-step training, so it is unclear the ablation ordering holds at scale.
- **Overclaim check**: "Closes the gap with specialized models" holds on the 11-benchmark average (78.3 vs Qwen3-VL-8B's 78.1) but not uniformly — NLD-Uni trails Qwen3-VL-8B badly on MMMU val (60.7 vs 69.6), ChartQA (89.9 vs 95.6), and OCRBenchV2 (62.1 vs 64.1), so per-task gaps remain. The unified-DLLM SOTA claim is within a young, small field (MMaDA, LaViDa-O, LLaDA2-Uni), and the strongest unified baseline, LLaDa-2-Uni, is a hybrid that uses a continuous 6B diffusion decoder — a fairness caveat in both directions.
- **Missing ablations/details**: no end-to-end ablation of the Elastic-MoT choice itself (inherited from LaViDa-O); the T2I table mixes purely discrete, hybrid, and continuous models; Fig 16 and Table 7 ablations are stated by the authors to run from an earlier checkpoint and are "not directly comparable" to main results. GenEval parity with the 12B NLD-Image specialist (0.90) is not discussed — the unified model still trails its own family's specialist on T2I semantics.
- **Reproducibility**: model/code claimed public, and hyperparameters (K randomization, ε schedule, r, thresholds) are documented; but full replication needs the 127M-image proprietary-filtered mixture, both teachers, and 64 H100s for 26 days — realistically reproducible only at the checkpoint level, not the training level.
- **Red flags**: minimal. One data quirk worth noting: stage-3 OPD excludes T2I because the base model already beats available teachers — so the marquee "cross-paradigm OPD" story is demonstrated for understanding and editing only, not for the T2I path where the self-refining process carries the results alone.

## Strengths

- Solves the actual bottleneck of unified DLLMs (the large gap to AR VLMs and flow models) with mechanisms that are well-motivated and empirically validated, including honest negative baselines.
- The ball-tree corruption is an elegant, memory-cheap (524KB vs 32GB) way to give discrete diffusion a coarse-to-fine noise geometry analogous to continuous flows, with PSNR analysis (Fig 13) showing the corruption trajectory is genuinely smooth.
- Elastic views make one 16B model serve as an 8B VLM, a 12B T2I model, and a 16B interleaved model with no inference overhead — a practical deployment story beyond benchmarks.
- Consistent 2.2–4.9× inference speedups over AR decoding at small accuracy cost, measured carefully across confidence thresholds.
- Excellent empirical coverage: 25+ benchmarks across four task families, plus derivations, data details, and a candid limitations section (shared-tokenizer requirement with the teacher, no fine-grained text rendering).

## Limitations

- Cross-paradigm OPD currently requires teacher and student to share the tokenizer (both use Ministral 3 here), which rules out many stronger teachers — the Kimi-K-2.6 ablation shows a mismatched tokenizer/thinking-format can *hurt* despite a stronger teacher.
- Still behind specialized SOTA on several understanding benchmarks (MMMU −8.9 vs Qwen3-VL-8B) and on DPG (86.6 vs 88.3 Qwen-Image); editing trails its own teacher (4.17 vs 4.27 Qwen-Image) and GPT-4o (4.20).
- The non-Markov formulation with uniform intermediate progression is a complexity-minimizing simplification; the authors note a stochastic-rate Markov variant is unexplored.
- Lacks fine-grained text rendering because the training mix contains almost no text-rendering data (acknowledged in Appendix F).
- Enormous training cost (64 H100 × 26 days) and dependence on two strong proprietary teachers limit independent reproduction; refinement benefits are largest on the human-preference metric (HPSv3) and more marginal on semantic benchmarks like GenEval.

## Relevance

对关注统一多模态理解与生成、扩散语言模型（DLLM）方向的工作高度相关：NLD-Uni 是目前该路线最强的公开结果，其跨范式蒸馏与自精炼扩散设计对"如何让离散扩散逼近 AR VLM + 连续流模型"这一核心问题给出了系统性答案。若你在做多模态模型训练、蒸馏或扩散解码加速，本文的传递式一致性损失与球树腐蚀过程都是可直接借鉴的技术点。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How much of the +17.1 average gain over LLaDA2-Uni comes from data scale/mixture (127M curated pairs, Nemotron teacher's own training set) versus the two proposed mechanisms? A compute-matched LaViDa-O rerun would isolate this.
- Can the transitive AR→diffusion consistency loss be extended to cross-tokenizer teachers (e.g., via vocabulary projection or logit-space alignment), unlocking genuinely stronger teachers like Kimi K-2.6?
- Does the ball-tree corruption generalize to text-only DLLM pretraining (where no VQ codebook geometry exists), e.g., using embedding-space neighbors of the LM's own tokenizer?
- The refinement benefit concentrates on HPSv3 and saturates at N=8 refinements — is there a sampler (adaptive per-token ε or confidence-aware refinement order) that transfers the fidelity gains to semantic metrics like GenEval/DPG?
- Since OPD was skipped for T2I because the base model beat its teachers, what happens when a stronger flow teacher (e.g., a future 20B+ editor) is available — does teacher-refined OPD improve T2I compositional benchmarks like DPG where NLD-Uni still lags (86.6 vs 88.3)?
