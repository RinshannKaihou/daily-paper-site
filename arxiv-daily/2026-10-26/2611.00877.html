Key metric · retention after the cut
0.00–0.07×

Function calling (BFCL v3) and math (GSM8K) retention across all four families after the main pruning cut. Pruned Llama-3.2-1B emits parseable function calls for only 6% of prompts and parseable JSON for 10%; Qwen: 0% / 16%.

Why this matters

Anyone deploying pruned sub-2B agents should test function calling and math first — aggregate benchmark scores completely hide their collapse.

⚠ Critical edge

The healing pools are benchmark-aligned — the math pool is the GSM8K training split — so measured "recovery" is recovery on GSM8K and likely overstates transfer to other tasks.

  • Models & cuts: Llama-3.2-1B −2/16 blocks, Qwen2.5-1.5B −7/28, SmolLM2-1.7B −4/24, OLMo-2-1B −2/16; block 0 and the attention-sink position never removed.
  • Eval suite: fixed, seeded test subsets — BFCL v3 (200), GSM8K (200), IFEval (300), JSONSchemaBench (150), XSTest (100+100), MMLU (300) — all zero-shot.
  • Healing data: ~1,300 prompts per pool, targets self-distilled greedily from the unpruned family model; only skill-demonstrating outputs kept.
  • Scopes: all-block LoRA (rank 16) vs cut-border LoRA vs full 32-bit FT of the last 3 blocks; allocation rules = uniform / damage-proportional / RAH (saturating-curve fit + concave budget program).
  • Token-matched control: uniform all-LoRA heal at 4.48M tokens — RAH's 1M budget plus its 3.48M pilot cost.

03Strengths

  • Identical within-family protocol — same pruned model, pools, teacher targets, seeds, test items for every method; that is what makes the W = 0.92 concordance meaningful.
  • Token-matched control reframes "does planning help?" as "does it help per token spent?" — deserves to become standard practice for evaluating pilot-based planners.
  • Chance-floor correction for MMLU/XSTest turns two superficially "robust" capabilities into the honest, weaker claims they actually are.
  • Complete negative-results reporting: linear patch, max-min objective, pilot-free proxy, and curve/transfer ablations all reported despite being unflattering.
  • Safety as a 2-D operating point (unsafe vs benign refusal) with a human-validated classifier — surfaces RAH silently zeroing the safety pool.
  • Laptop-scale reproducibility — most Llama/Qwen runs on an 8 GB RTX 4060.

04Limitations

  • Narrow scope: all families 1.2–1.7B, one pruning criterion (Block Influence), English-only; no latency, throughput, or memory measurements — the practical speedup is never quantified.
  • Benchmark-aligned pools (math = GSM8K train split) mean recovery may not transfer to other tasks of the same capability.
  • Statistical power: 150–300 items (±0.1 CIs) and 3 seeds cannot resolve the ~0.02 effects that dominate allocation-rule comparisons; each pilot is run once, so plan variability is unmeasured.
  • Untested cheap baseline: a pilot-free rule giving math a large fixed share is acknowledged but never run — the fixed-budget case for planning is weaker than it appears.
  • Data provenance: SmolLM2 teacher targets regenerated mid-study; only Llama's teacher files survive with checksums; three GPU types across runs.
  • Refusal classifier (recall 0.88) misses indirect refusals; harmfulness of compliant answers to unsafe prompts was not judged.

05Questions for further exploration

  1. Would a pilot-free rule giving math 50–60% of the budget match the planner's worst-case gains at a fraction of the 3.48M-token pilot cost?
  2. Do the damage ordering (W = 0.92) and the all-LoRA-safer scope result persist at 3B–8B scale, under other pruning criteria, or in multilingual settings?
  3. How much of the measured math/JSON recovery transfers to held-out tasks (e.g., MATH, unseen JSON schemas)?
  4. Can pilot-fitted recovery curves be made trustworthy for slow capabilities — more, longer, or repeated pilots, or cross-family time-scale priors?
  5. If one fitted plan were reused across many heals, would the 3.48M-token pilot cost amortize enough for planning to beat uniform healing per total token?
  6. How do healing-induced refusal shifts (Qwen: 51% → 6% benign refusal when RAH drops the safety pool) evade audits that only check the balanced score?
