> **一句话总结：** 通过对模运算任务上 MLP 与单层 Transformer 的时序分析、充分子空间度量与秩上限干预实验，本文证明权重矩阵有效秩的压缩是 grokking 相变的"结果/签名"而非原因——压缩总是跟随（而非先于）泛化起点，强行预先施加低秩反而延迟甚至阻止 grokking；同时 Transformer 的解在相变期间"结晶"到每矩阵仅 8–13 个奇异方向，而 MSE 训练的 MLP 并不发生这种结晶。

> **TL;DR:** Studying modular arithmetic ((a+b) mod 97, plus replications on ab mod 97 and (a+b) mod 113) in a mean-field MLP and a one-layer transformer, the authors show that effective-rank compression *follows* the grokking onset rather than causing it: every transformer memorizes within 250 steps while onsets span 6,500–21,000 steps, and the steepest rank drop trails the onset by ~900 steps (r = 0.99); grokked transformers compress 60 ± 5% in effective rank versus only 15–23% for non-grokked controls, and rank caps on W_in never beat the factorized full-rank control (onset 14,350 ± 5,730 at rank 32 vs 14,100 ± 4,095 for the control — no speedup, only delay), while capping W_E stops 15 of 20 runs from grokking at all. During the transition the transformer's solution "crystallizes" from 27–53 to 8–13 sufficient directions per weight matrix (measured by output change, not accuracy). A methodological gem: judging direction counts by accuracy alone is margin-confounded, and comparing rank caps to unfactorized baselines would falsely suggest low rank accelerates grokking.

## 核心贡献

- **压缩跟随 grokking，而非记忆化（因果关系澄清）。** 29 个 grokked Transformer 种子全部在 250 步内记忆训练集，但 grokking 起点（测试准确率首次达 95%）分布在 6,500–21,000 步之间；W_in 有效秩最陡下降点与起点的 Pearson 相关系数 r = 0.99、斜率 1.02，且总是滞后起点约 900 步——时序证据明确指向压缩跟随相变。只记忆不泛化的网络有效秩仅下降 15–23%（grokked 为 60 ± 5%），提供了阴性对照。
- **提出"子空间结晶"（subspace crystallization）现象与免疫于 margin 混淆的度量 d_out。** 作者定义"充分子空间"（保留 99% 测试准确率所需的最少顶部奇异方向）并指出按准确率计数会随 margin 增大而虚假下降；改用输出变化 ≤10% 的 d_out 度量后，Transformer 在相变期间从每矩阵 27–53 个方向收缩至 8–13 个，与 Nanda 等人的 cleanup 阶段同步，而 MSE 训练的 MLP（d_out 始终 122–127/128）完全不结晶。
- **干预实验否证"低秩驱动 grokking"假说。** 若秩压缩是原因，预先限制秩应加速 grokking；实际相反——对照因子化全秩控制，限制 W_in 秩为 32/24/16 均不降低平均起点、限制 W_E 的秩使 20 次中 15 次完全无法 grok；MLP 中核范数惩罚提前压缩 W_1 反而把 grokking 从 12,700 步延迟到 16,200 步。
- **揭示因子化控制的必要性。** 单纯把 W_1 写成 W = AB（无秩限制）就使 MLP grokking 提速 7–8 倍（t_g = 1,667 vs 12,483 步），却使 Transformer 变慢——不设因子化对照会导致"低秩加速 grokking"的错误结论。
- **多项二级发现：** MLP 晚期压缩（Stage 4）由 AdamW 的有效学习率 lr/ε = 10^5 主导（SGD-with-momentum 可复现，ER 103.8 vs AdamW 的 104.8）；不同种子的 MLP 隐层表示经正交 Procrustes 旋转后可互相解码（95.8% vs 旋转前 1.0%），但不跨任务迁移；Fourier IPR 在从未 grok 的网络上也会升高，不能作为免标签预警信号。

## Key Contributions

- **Compression follows grokking, not memorization.** All 29 grokked transformer seeds fit their training set within 250 steps while onsets range over 6,500–21,000 steps; the steepest effective-rank drop of W_in tracks the onset with Pearson r = 0.99 (slope 1.02, 95% CI 0.98–1.07), landing 905 ± 506 steps after it (27/29 seeds). Networks that memorize without grokking compress far less (ER of W_in falls 15–23% vs 60 ± 5% in grokked seeds), and in the MLP the compression onset lags the grokking onset in all 45 runs across three tasks (mean lag 6,367 ± 3,248 steps, p = 1.3 × 10^-6).
- **Subspace crystallization, measured without margin confounds.** Introducing d_out (fewest top singular directions keeping the network's outputs within 10% in Frobenius norm) alongside the accuracy-based d_0.99, the authors show the transformer's solution narrows from 27–53 directions per matrix (at 16% test accuracy, 3,000 steps before onset) to 8–13 during the transition, coinciding with the cleanup of memorizing components (excluded loss rises as d_out falls). The MSE-trained MLP shows no crystallization: its d_0.99 narrowing (109.5 → 64.9 of 128) is an artifact of the median margin growing from 0.105 to 0.470 — its d_out stays at 122–127 of 128.
- **Interventional null result: imposed low rank does not cause grokking.** Against a factorized full-rank control (onset 14,100 ± 4,095 steps), capping W_in at rank 32/24/16 never lowers the mean onset (14,350 ± 5,730 / 16,312 ± 3,332 / 16,417 ± 6,682) and capping W_E prevents grokking in 15 of 20 runs; a nuclear-norm penalty that compresses W_1 before the onset (ER 78.5 vs 115.9 at step 11,500) delays grokking from 12,700 ± 447 to 16,200 ± 447 steps. Compression is a signature, not a cause.
- **Methodological corrections for rank analyses.** Factorizing W = AB alone shifts grokking time 7–8× faster in the MLP (1,667 vs 12,483 steps) but slower in the transformer, so rank-cap experiments must be compared to a same-parameterization control; direction counts must be reported with the training step (MLP d_0.99 re-widens from 65 to 89 after generalization); and spectral statistics like Fourier IPR must be read against negative controls since they also move in non-grokked networks.
- **Secondary results.** Stage-4 late compression is governed by the optimizer's effective learning rate lr/ε (once √v̂ < ε, AdamW ≈ gradient descent at lr 10^5; SGD with momentum reproduces Stage 4, ER 103.8 vs 104.8); MLP seeds share one hidden representation up to orthogonal rotation (cross-seed readout decoding 95.8 ± 2.4% after Procrustes alignment vs 1.0% before, and 0.6–1.4% for random-label or cross-task controls).

## Methodology

Two architectures on modular arithmetic, both trained full-batch with AdamW:

1. **Mean-field MLP** (following Gromov 2023): single hidden layer, 128 units, quadratic activation φ(z) = z² (which makes Fourier computation explicit: cos²θ = (1 + cos 2θ)/2 produces the cos(θa + θb) products), W_1 ∈ R^128×194 taking one-hot [ea; eb], MSE to one-hot targets, lr 10^-3, weight decay 10^-2, ε = 10^-8, 35% train split, 10^5 steps, 15 seeds. Five random-label networks are negative controls; replication tasks ab mod 97 and (a+b) mod 113 (15 seeds each).
2. **One-layer transformer** (following Nanda et al. 2023): 4 heads, d_model = 128, d_head = 32, d_mlp = 512, ReLU, no layer norm, cross-entropy, weight decay 1.0, 30% train split, 25,000 steps; seeds 0–14 plus held-out seeds 15–29 (timing measures defined on 0–14, then tested on 15–29). Checkpoints every 250 steps. Matrices analyzed: W_E, W_U, W_in, W_out (+ attention projections).

**Key definitions.** Grokking onset t_g = first step with test accuracy ≥ 95%; memorization t_fit = first step with training accuracy ≥ 99%. Effective rank ER(W) = exp(H(σ̂)) (Roy–Vetterli), entropy of normalized singular values. The paper's central measurement innovation: d_0.99 (fewest top-k singular directions preserving 99% test accuracy — margin-sensitive) versus **d_out** (fewest directions keeping outputs within 10% Frobenius change — margin-immune, with mean-logit removal for CE models). "Subspace crystallization" = a narrowing of d_out.

**Interventions.** Truncation of weight matrices to top/bottom/random singular directions; complement perturbations (Gaussian noise inside vs outside the sufficient subspace); transplant patching with orthogonal Procrustes alignment across seeds; optimizer swaps (AdamW at varying ε vs GD vs SGD-momentum at matched effective learning rate); nuclear-norm penalty λ∥W_1∥*; and rank caps W = AB at inner dimension r ∈ {8…128}, each compared against a **factorized full-rank control** (r = 128) because factorization alone changes optimization (implicit preconditioning + nuclear-norm effect of weight decay on A, B). Causal timing evidence combines per-seed onset alignment, Granger tests, and replication on held-out seeds and tasks with Bonferroni correction. Code: github.com/Lossfunk/Causal-geometric-grokking.

## Results & Highlights

- **Timing (transformer, (a+b) mod 97, 29 grokked seeds):** steepest ER drop of W_in occurs 905 ± 506 steps after the grokking onset (27/29 seeds) while scatter around the memorization step is 4,234 steps; the correlation between drop step and onset is r = 0.99 (slope 1.02, CI 0.98–1.07). Replicates on 15 held-out seeds: 850 ± 524 steps after onset (14/15 seeds, t = 6.28, p = 1.0 × 10^-5) — the paper's flagship causal-timing claim. (Metric: step lag of steepest effective-rank decrease; baseline: the memorization hypothesis, which predicts clustering at t_fit ≈ 250.)
- **Compression magnitude (negative controls):** from step 4,000 to end of training, ER of W_in falls 60 ± 5% in grokked transformers vs 15–23% in the 7 never-grokked runs (peak compression rate 2–5× lower); in the MLP, random-label networks that reach 90% training accuracy show ER fall of only 1.1 ± 0.3% vs 10.5 ± 1.8% in grokked MLPs.
- **Subspace crystallization (transformer):** d_out per matrix falls from 27–53 directions (3,000 steps before onset, 15.7% test accuracy) to 11–17 at onset and 9–14 three thousand steps after; at the final checkpoint d_0.99 is 7.7 ± 1.5 of 97 for W_E, 8.7 ± 1.5 of 128 for W_out, and 13.0 ± 1.3 of 128 for W_in, and MLP post-activations need only 17.2 ± 3.3 of 512 directions (3.4%). Truncation to the top d_0.99 directions keeps ≥ 99.26% test accuracy whereas random subspaces of equal rank keep 22.7% (MLP) and ≤ 12.5% (transformer). Never-grokked transformers stay 2–9× wider (68–84 directions for W_in vs 18.1 ± 3.8 grokked).
- **MLP crystallization is margin-confounded:** with MSE, d_0.99 narrows from 109.5 ± 3.2 (step 12,500) to 64.9 ± 1.7 (step 20,000) while the median margin grows 0.105 → 0.470, but d_out stays 122.4 ± 0.5 → 127.3 ± 0.6 of 128 (above the 116.4 ± 0.5 of an untrained network); d_0.99 then re-widens to 89.1 ± 2.3 by step 10^5. With cross-entropy the MLP does crystallize, but late: d_out ≈ 120 near onset → 81.0 ± 1.6 (lr 3 × 10^-3) / 69.6 ± 2.2 (lr 10^-2) at the end.
- **Rank caps vs factorized control (Table 4, 5 seeds/arm):** unfactorized onset 10,500 ± 1,777; factorized control (r = 128) 14,100 ± 4,095; W_in capped at rank 32 → 14,350 ± 5,730 (5/5 grok, no speedup), rank 24 → 16,312 ± 3,332 (4/5), rank 16 → 16,417 ± 6,682 (3/5), rank 8 → 1/5 grok with final accuracy 50.6 ± 39.7%; W_E caps: only 5 of 20 capped runs grok, and ranks 16/8 give 0/5 with final accuracy 11.9 ± 9.5% / 13.7 ± 11.8%. In the MLP, factorization alone speeds grokking 7–8× (t_g = 1,667 ± 58 vs 12,483 ± 340 unfactorized) while caps delay it (2,450 ± 100 at r = 64; 4,133 ± 29 at r = 32; r ≤ 16 never groks in 10^5 steps) — the direction of the factorization effect flips between architectures, which is exactly why the control is needed.
- **Stage 4 is the effective learning rate (Table 2):** continuing runs from step 20,000, at effective lr 10^5 AdamW (ε = 10^-8), gradient descent, and SGD-momentum reach ER(W_1) of 104.8 / 108.3 / 103.8 (d_0.99: 89 / 87 / 91), while at effective lr 10^3 ER stays 117.8 — the late compression is optimizer-driven, not loss-driven. A nuclear-norm penalty (λ = 10^-7) that pre-compresses W_1 to ER 78.5 ± 3.4 by step 11,500 (control 115.9 ± 0.3) delays grokking from 12,700 ± 447 to 16,200 ± 447 steps, and λ ≥ 3 × 10^-7 prevents learning entirely.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** High on methodology, moderate on phenomenon. Rank compression during grokking was known (Liu et al. 2023; DeMoss et al. 2025); the novel parts are (a) the memorization-vs-onset separation exploiting the transformer's 250-step memorization window, (b) the d_out metric that removes the margin confound — showing the MLP's famous d_0.99 "compression" is largely a margin artifact — and (c) the factorized-control rank-cap design, which reverses the naive conclusion. The demonstration that factorizing W_1 alone speeds MLP grokking 7–8× (so naive comparisons of rank caps to unfactorized weights are invalid) is a genuinely important caution for this literature.
- **Rigor:** Very good for the genre: held-out seed replication, Granger tests with acknowledged limits, Bonferroni correction, negative controls, and honest reporting of the one test that lost significance after correction (H4 on (a+b) mod 113, p = 0.013). One subtle data quirk handled well: with weight decay 1.0 the transformer's initial weights decay to 2% of their size by step 4,000, mechanically lowering ER — the authors measure compression only after this plateau, but this also illustrates how fragile raw ER trajectories are as evidence.
- **Reproducibility:** Strong — public code, exact hyperparameters, seed counts, and definitions for every metric; the tasks run on a single GPU in minutes-to-hours.
- **Significance:** The negative causal result (low rank is signature, not cause) matters for anyone building "compression causes grokking" theories or using rank/IPR as early-warning signals; the practical takeaway that Fourier IPR fires in all 5 random-label MLPs and all 7 non-grokked transformer runs undercuts a published use case (Doshi et al. 2024). However, scope is narrow: everything is modular arithmetic on cyclic groups with ≤128-dimensional toy models.
- **Red flags:** (1) Toy-scale only — one-layer transformer and a 128-unit MLP on 97–113-element groups; no test on language/vision models or non-abelian groups (the authors say so themselves). (2) The two architectures differ in loss (MSE vs CE), weight decay (10^-2 vs 1.0) and training length, so the MLP-vs-transformer crystallization contrast is partly confounded — the authors probe weight decay and loss separately but cannot fully deconfound architecture from loss. (3) "Causation" here rests on rank-cap interventions plus timing; the caps also change optimization (preconditioning), which the factorized control mitigates but cannot eliminate — the paper is careful about this but the inference "compression does not cause grokking" is still an absence-of-evidence style conclusion over a limited intervention family. (4) Non-peer-reviewed preprint from an independent lab with heavy AI assistance in writing (disclosed).

## Strengths

- Clean separation of memorization from generalization using the transformer's natural variability (250-step memorization vs 6,500–21,000-step onsets), which turns an observational confound into a quasi-experimental design.
- The d_out (output-change) criterion is a real methodological advance over accuracy-based direction counts; the margin-growth explanation of the MLP's spurious d_0.99 narrowing is demonstrated, not asserted (margin 0.105 → 0.470 with d_out flat at 122–127).
- The factorized-control insight — factorization alone shifts onset 7–8× in the MLP in the opposite direction to the transformer — would invalidate several plausible experimental designs in this literature, and the paper states exactly how to avoid the trap.
- Comprehensive secondary analyses (optimizer swaps explaining Stage 4 via lr/ε; Procrustes-based universality across seeds with cross-task controls; IPR negative controls) each come with the negative control that makes them believable.
- Held-out replication discipline: measures defined on seeds 0–14, tested on untouched seeds 15–29 and two extra tasks, with all nine t-tests and the Bonferroni outcome reported.

## Limitations

- Only modular arithmetic on cyclic groups (abelian); the authors themselves note non-abelian group composition and language/vision models remain untested — whether real models have a comparably clean sufficient subspace is open.
- Architecture comparison is confounded: the MLP and transformer differ in loss, weight decay, and training horizon simultaneously; CE MLPs need larger learning rates to grok at all (1 of 5 seeds grok at lr 10^-3 within 10^5 steps).
- Scale: 128-dimensional toy networks; conclusions about "signature not cause" are established only within this regime.
- Granger tests capture temporal order only; the causal claim ultimately rests on the rank-cap interventions, which cannot exclude other channels through which caps act.
- Transformer rank-cap arms have only 5 seeds each with large variance (e.g., W_in rank-8 onset reported from a single grokked seed), so some cap-effect details are underpowered.

## Relevance

这篇论文直击 grokking 与训练动力学中"低秩结构是原因还是结果"这一核心问题，其基于干预与阴性对照的实验设计、以及"按输出而非准确率计数方向"的方法论，对研究神经网络隐式正则化、机制可解释性与相变动力学的方向都有直接参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does subspace crystallization (d_out narrowing from ~27–53 to 8–13 directions) occur in language models fine-tuned on algorithmic or structured tasks, or is it specific to one-layer transformers on modular arithmetic?
- Since imposing low rank delays grokking, does the reverse intervention — expanding rank or injecting noise into the sufficient subspace late in training — affect generalization robustness or prompt sensitivity?
- The factorization itself shifts onset by 7–8× in opposite directions in the two architectures: can the implicit-preconditioning account (Arora et al. 2018) predict the sign of the effect a priori for a given architecture/loss pair?
- Stage-4 compression is driven by lr/ε once √v̂ < ε: do standard ε values in LLM training (10^-8 to 10^-6) imply that observed late-stage rank growth/compression in production runs is partly an Adam artifact rather than a learning phenomenon?
- The MSE MLP's sufficient subspace re-widens after generalization (d_0.99 from 65 back to 89) — is there a margin/robustness trade-off that makes the transient narrow subspace functionally meaningful, or is it purely epiphenomenal?
- Could d_out-style output-based direction counting replace SAE-based sparsity metrics as a cheaper universality probe, given the cross-seed Procrustes transfer (95.8%) shown here?
