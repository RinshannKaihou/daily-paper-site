The Weekly ArXiv Dispatch

October 26 – November 1, 2026

103 papers · reliability, interpretability, training stability

Silent failure lives exactly where the stack is confident.

Eight of this week’s must reads share one reflex: measure the toolchain where it reports success. bf16 rounding delivers a sliver of every RL update to the sampler; a more-accurate FP8 GEMM is what breaks Evo 2 checkpoints; 7 of 8 agent harnesses turn “never ran” into status 0; coding agents that pass 95% of single requests finish barely half their chains. The fixers showed up too — unbiased rounding exports, a TLA+-checked Receipt protocol, provably integer-exact W4A8, zero-SM FSDP collectives, and a one-line LM-head fix for “time-bomb” loss spikes.

本周主线：静默失效恰恰藏在工具链最自信的地方——精度、舍入、返回码与自检里；修复者同样活跃：无偏舍入导出、TLA+ 模型检查的 Receipt 协议、可证明整数精确的 W4A8、零 SM 参与的 FSDP 集合通信、以及追到 LM head 量化误差的一行修复。

Coding Agents Pass 95% of Single Requests — and Complete Only Half Their Chains

Built on Broken Assumptions (BOBA): Why Coding Agents Cannot Be Left Alone

By Alejandro Cuadron & Arpit Gupta (Amazon) · arXiv 2611.02207

95% → 51% Chain-completion collapse on BOBA-BENCH. Baseline: 95% single-request success over 280 real-repo chains × 20 requests × 10 agents × 8 attempts.

280 条真实仓库链上，十个主流编码智能体单请求成功 95%、串成 20 请求长链只剩 51%——根因不是代码质量，而是未验证的自检遗漏；一个外部“验证失败”提示加一轮修复即提到 59%。

One unverified miss nearly triples next-request failure; cross-model review barely beats chance (53%). A single external verification bit lifts chain completion 51%→59% — verification access, not coding skill, is the binding constraint on unattended agents.

Caveat — Specifications are model-written and model-audited, not human-reviewed, and the 51% headline is an upper bound (strict pass@1 is 39%).

“Cancellation” in LLM Layers Is a Leak, Not a Choice

Closed Gates Leak: Decomposing Adjacent-Layer Cancellation in Gated Language Models

By Harvey Dam & Ganesh Gopalakrishnan (U. Utah) · arXiv 2610.39068

−0.184 → +0.021 Cosine under leak-annealing, 16 models. Baseline: −0.170 for matched control interventions — the “selective rejection” reading of cancellation does not survive.

相邻层“抵消”被精确分解为 A+C−D 三项：阻尼由门控 MLP 关闭态的微小负泄漏承载；退火掉负区后阻尼与抵消一同消失。

An exact A+C−D decomposition shows the “damping” is closed-gate SiLU/GELU leak that does not single out the previous layer’s write.

Caveat — Main measurements rest on sixteen 192-token documents, models capped at 8B, and selectivity is refuted at the direction level only.

bf16 Rounding Silently Discards Most RL Policy Updates

By Andrew Gao & William Gao · arXiv 2610.41073

The Role of Quantization in Loss Spikes

By Zaiqian Chen et al. (U. Maryland, w/ Tom Goldstein) · arXiv 2610.38277

0/5 vs 5/5 FP32 vs BF16 LM-head seeds that time-bomb (5 seeds each, peak LR 0.1) — Adam’s mean-logit drift inflates LM-head quantization error by orders of magnitude before collapse.
