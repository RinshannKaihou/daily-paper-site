Key Finding

Constrained decoding doesn't harm reasoning — the "format tax" is a prompt-mismatch artifact, not decoder masking.

约束解码并不损害推理——此前观测到的性能下降主要来自不对等的提示词与输出格式，而非解码器掩码本身；对齐条件下约束解码一致追平或超过无约束解码。

A1Key Metric

+29.7 PTS

Llama3-8B · GSM8K · JSON — accuracy rises 42.1 → 71.8 with format + validity constraints vs the unconstrained baseline, under matched prompts.

Same protocol: Gemma2-9B 0.0 → 86.7 · Qwen3.5-9B 23.1 → 88.9 (LastLetter, JSON). Validity constraints alone: up to +17.4%.

A2Why This Matters

Building structured-output, function-calling, or agent pipelines? Encode the task's known answer subspace into the schema — a near-free accuracy and extractability gain.

把任务已知的答案子空间（取值范围、字符数、类型）编码进输出 schema，几乎零成本地同时提升准确率与可抽取性。

A3Critical Edge

Headline gains partly conflate "masking helps" with "the unconstrained extractor was brittle" — the decisive control (unconstrained decoding + a lenient regex extractor) was never run.

Read critically

01Methodology

Theory. Constrained decoding masks invalid next-tokens and renormalizes. Each normalizer ≤ 1, so a constraint-satisfying ground truth can only gain probability — strictly, when the mask prunes invalid positive-probability mass on its path. Reasoning constraints get no universal guarantee (three-case analysis: total exclusion fails, oracle retention wins, partial retention indeterminate).

FCFormat constraint — enforce output structure (JSON grammar, answer: str)
VCValidity constraint — mask answers outside the known subspace (AIME: integer 0–999; LastLetter: exactly 4 / 8 letters)
RCReasoning constraint — restrict the reasoning trace (forced "Let's think step by step" prefix)

Experiment. The decisive control: identical prompts and identical expected output formats, so decoder-level masking is the only difference. 11 open-weight models, 4 families (Qwen3/Qwen3.5 1.7B–27B, Llama3-8B, Gemma2-9B, SmolLM3-3B) × 4 benchmarks × 3 strategies (JSON with reason field; NL-to-Format two-pass with Qwen3-32B extractor; InWriting trigger-token single pass). Constraints via Litelines + Pydantic; temperature 0.

Design: same prompt → {unconstrained | +FC | +FC+VC | +RC} → same expected format → exact-match accuracy ± std over seeds × prompt variants

03Strengths

  • The right experiment, at scale — matched-prompt / matched-format causal ablation across 11 models and 4 families, exactly the control prior comparisons lacked.
  • Actionable taxonomy — FC/VC/RC splits a vague debate into three testable hypotheses; "encode the answer subspace" is a drop-in recipe for structured-output pipelines.
  • Leakage-conscious benchmarks — AIME 2026 and the newly built LastLetter-Extended postdate every evaluated model.
  • Honest reporting — per-model variance, parse-rate and token tables, sample-level failure analyses, explicit limitations; first author critically re-examines his own InWriting method.

04Limitations

  • Exact-match artifact risk — VC wins include normalizing "28.00" → "28" and "h e n e" → "hene"; the unconstrained + lenient-extractor control is missing.
  • Hand-coded priors — VC needs an oracle-like answer subspace per task; open-ended applicability untested beyond a small IMDb run.
  • Thin RC evidence — one reasoning pattern, sign flips across models (−6.6 vs +2.4 on AIME), no method for finding beneficial traces.
  • Narrow surface — 2 of 4 benchmarks are LastLetter variants; AIME covers 4 models; unexplained ±27–29 variance; code supplementary-only, public post-acceptance.

05Questions for Further Exploration

  1. Does the +FC+VC gain survive a lenient, regex-based extractor on the unconstrained side — a decoding win or an evaluation-harness win?
  2. Can validity constraints be auto-derived (LLM reads the task → emits Pydantic constraints) instead of hand-coded, and do gains persist on open-ended tasks?
  3. Does VC transfer to schema-heavy real workloads — function calling, multi-field extraction, SQL/tool-call generation?
  4. Why do RC effects flip sign across models and scale — interference with trained reasoning modes? Can beneficial traces be selected adaptively at decode time?
  5. What is the wall-clock latency cost of per-step automaton masking at serving scale, and where is the break-even vs InWriting's trigger-token switch?
