Key metric

.189%

wrong→correct flip rate for selected question/option-span interventions — vs .058% for random-active controls.

Fewer than 1 in 500 edits repairs an answer; correct→wrong damage occurs at a comparable rate. These are diagnostic handles, not a repair technique.

Why this matters

The artifact-audit pipeline — four confound controls plus causal validation — transfers directly to hallucination-detection and self-correction research.

伪影审计流水线（四类对照 + DLA + 因果干预）可直接迁移到幻觉检测与自我纠错研究；对「预测即机制」的证伪是评估一切错误预测特征工作的警示。

Critical edge

The “greater causal leverage” headline rests on ~35 wrong-to-correct flips out of 8,256+ pairs — and at the final decision token, selected features move margins less than random controls (.154 vs .237).

01

Methodology — audit, then intervene

Gemma 3 1B/4B · Gemma Scope 2 SAEs (16K/65K)
layers 17/22 (1B) · 22/29 (4B)
  1. Render1,472,694 instances · 8 datasets × 3 templates × 4 answer orders × 3 response modes
  2. ProbeL1 logistic + ridge · 4 error targets (WvC, margin, high-confidence, low-margin)
  3. Gateartifact risk af ≤ .80 · letter / template / permutation / topic controls
  4. Selecttop-300 → DLA · 81,304 candidates → 55 non-oracle + 22 oracle units
  5. Intervene0 / μ / +1–4σ · vs random-active + adapted HalluSAE

Phase 1 — discovery. Each dataset item is rendered under three prompt templates, four answer orders, and three response modes; residual activations are max-pooled over six token spans (final prompt, question, options, generated reasoning, gold/top-wrong oracle text). Probes rank features for four error targets; every feature then gets an artifact-risk score af = max of four confound controls — option-letter, template, permutation, topic (TF-IDF/SVD). Eligibility gates (af ≤ .80, activation frequency < .95, evidence from ≥ 2 datasets, coefficient-sign consistency ≥ .60) cut 81,304 candidates to a top-300 shortlist; contrastive direct-logit attribution (DLA) keeps the 77 units that are finally intervened on.

Phase 2 — intervention. Decoder-vector edits r′ = r + (τ − a)·df with τ ∈ {0, μ, +1/+2/+4σ} hit up to 10 top-activation instances per feature at each span, always against a no-edit pass on the same prompt. Outcomes — margin shift Δm, probability and entropy deltas, answer-flip types (W2C, C2W, W2W) — are compared to random-active controls and a matched-layer HalluSAE adaptation, with 5,000–10,000 bootstrap resamples and a feature-equal regression adjusting for edit size, span length, dataset, and model configuration.

  • Prediction is real: all four error targets decode above chance at the final prompt token (AUROC .715–.739); generated-reasoning spans are strongest (.740–.782), question-span probes weakest.
  • Flips are rare and symmetric: Q-span 16 W2C vs 17 C2W; O-span 19 vs 19; only 51.8% of 8,256 feature–prompt pairs respond monotonically to +1→+4σ steering, and sign-agreement intervals across templates/permutations include zero.
  • Oracle edits cut both ways: gold-choice spans 6 W2C / 9 C2W; top-wrong spans 0 W2C / 5 C2W — no reliable direction.
  • HalluSAE audit: predictive (AUROC/F1 .775/.674) and 11× stronger on margin shift, yet 95.5% of its 711 nonzero units fail the artifact gate in every setting, its features are ~450× denser (median frequency .580 vs .0013), overlap with the top-300 shortlist is zero, and W2C (.332%) is indistinguishable from random (.347%).
  • Behavioral baseline: forced-letter accuracy 43.8% (1B) vs 58.6% (4B); wrong-high-confidence 52.0% vs 39.3%; Med-HALT near chance for 1B (.063).
03

Strengths

  • Turns a slogan — “correlation is not causation” — into an executable, quantified audit: four separate artifact controls, prespecified eligibility gates, causal interventions with matched controls on identical prompts.
  • The HalluSAE exhibit cleanly separates predictive performance and logit influence from artifact independence and correction benefit — a ready template for evaluating any “error feature” paper.
  • Exceptional coverage at scale: 8 datasets, 2 models, 2 layers each, 2 SAE widths, 3 templates, 4 permutations, 3 response modes — 1.47M instances under discovery/validation/test discipline.
  • Honest, self-critical reporting: negative results (final-token underperformance, unstable directions, near-zero flip rates) get the same prominence as positive ones.
04

Limitations

  • Scale: only Gemma 3 1B/4B at four mid-to-late layers with 16K/65K SAEs (~500 V100-hours); larger models may organize error information differently.
  • Thin practical impact: fewer than 1 in 500 wrong-answer interventions corrects, with damage at a comparable rate — diagnostics, not repair.
  • Random-active controls are matched within runs, not on activation frequency or edit magnitude; the adjusted regression mitigates this but excludes decoder norm.
  • The HalluSAE comparison is a matched-layer adaptation that skips its Stage-I layer localization — not directly comparable to the original paper.
  • Restricted to multiple choice; free-form wrong answers — where hallucination actually matters — are left to future work.
  • Effect directions are unstable across semantically equivalent formats and answer orders, so even surviving features are not reliable steering handles.
05

Questions for further exploration

  1. Does the Q/O causal-leverage advantage survive on larger models (Gemma 3 12B+), or does error information re-localize with scale?
  2. Can the artifact-risk score become a calibrated contamination probability instead of a within-run percentile, so thresholds compare across settings?
  3. Would multi-feature or circuit-level interventions on the 55 selected units beat single-feature edits, or is answer-competition redundancy too high?
  4. Why do selected features underperform random controls at the final prompt token (.154 vs .237) — is FP influence dominated by dense format features the filter removes?
  5. Can the audit run on free-form generation by intervening during autoregressive decoding?
  6. Do the 26 of 55 post-gate units that still carry artifact/semantic tags drive most of the remaining W2C events — is even the weak leverage artifact-mediated?
