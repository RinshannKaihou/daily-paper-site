Key finding

Transformers trained on feedback-coupled agents encode each agent's Bayesian beliefs in separate linear subspaces — steerable factor by factor.

任何联合过程都可视为多个体的反馈交互，其贝叶斯信念可分解为逐个体张量积；小 transformer 的残差流以分离的线性子空间编码每个个体的信念几何，逐因子注入反事实信念即可实现精准转向。

Why this matters

Extends linear belief-geometry interpretability from independent to feedback-coupled processes — testable dimension predictions plus causal, factor-by-factor steering toward auditable model decomposition.

Critical edge

Every result comes from a 4-layer, d=120 transformer with 9-token context on toy HMMs — nine orders of magnitude from a pretrained LLM, and discovering factors without ground truth remains unsolved.

Steering is not universal: sns loops close only ~69% of the gap; one unprojected patch lands at −41%.

01Methodology

Theory (computational mechanics). Each agent is a transducer — a stochastic automaton whose Bayesian filtering over histories induces belief states (mixed-state presentation), the sufficient statistic for prediction. Theorem 1: via causal conditioning, any joint process can be read as two transducers coupled in feedback. Theorem 2 / Cor. 2: the loop's joint HMM operator is the tensor product of per-agent operators, and from uncorrelated priors the joint belief stays an exact tensor product under filtering — so it can be stored losslessly as a direct sum.

D_fact = |R| + |S||X| − 2  ≪  D_prod = |S||R||X| − 1  →  telephone nets: 8N vs 9^N − 1

Experiments. A 4-layer transformer (d_model 120, 3 heads, context 9) trained online for 50k steps, five seeds per setup, on mess3 / fractal2 / sns feedback loops, N ≤ 5 "telephone" networks, and coarse-grained (one-agent-hidden) variants — all converging within a few 10⁻³ nats of the exact Bayes-optimal floor.

Analysis. OLS linear probes to ground-truth belief coordinates; belief patching with null / unrelated-sequence / orthogonalized-write controls; PCA cumulative-explained-variance rank vs factored and Kronecker beliefs. Code is public.

02Results beyond the headline

Belief geometry becomes linearly decodable per agent: held-out probe R² rises from 0.914 / 0.676 at step 500 to ≥ 0.992 (up to 1.000) by step 50k — the MSP simplex of each factor emerges in the first few hundred steps.

03Strengths

  • Tight theory–experiment loop: the dimension prediction (8N vs 9^N − 1) is derived analytically before being measured — and the measurements track it.
  • Causal, intervention-based evidence (not just correlational probing), with graded dose-response ruling out memorization.
  • The coarse-graining experiment cleanly demonstrates that the observation interface alone can force higher-dimensional internal representations.
  • Exceptional hygiene: five seeds with min–max bands, Bayes-floor convergence checks, null and orthogonalized controls, public code.

04Limitations

  • Evidence restricted to small transformers on synthetic processes with known ground truth; no pretrained or natural-data model; factor discovery without ground truth is future work.
  • Steering degrades on nonunifilar processes — sns loops close only ~69% of the gap, one unprojected patch is counterproductive (−41%).
  • The dimension hypothesis gives only an upper bound (mess3 saturates it; fractal2 beats it), and PCA rank misses features stored in superposition.
  • Factorization is non-unique: which one a network prefers, and general interaction graphs beyond pairs and hubs, are unaddressed.
  • AI-safety applicability (auditing subcomponents of large models) is aspirational — no bridge to production-scale models is demonstrated.

05Questions for further exploration

  1. Can factored beliefs be discovered without ground-truth coordinates — e.g. sparse dictionary learning or causal probing — first in the toy zoo, then in pretrained LLMs?
  2. When a process admits multiple factorizations, which one does gradient descent favor — the most compact, the most causally separable, or data-order dependent?
  3. Do the sns steering failures reflect superposition, an insufficiently converged read/write basis, or a genuine property of nonunifilar processes — and does scale repair them?
  4. Do the findings transfer to realistic interaction graphs — and can a transformer infer the interaction graph itself from sequence data?
  5. Natural-language analogue: does observing only part of a multi-party conversation force entangled, higher-rank belief representations in an LM?
