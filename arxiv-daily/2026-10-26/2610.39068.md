> **一句话总结：** 本文把相邻层"互相抵消"现象分解为注意力写入、共写与"阻尼"三项，发现阻尼由 SiLU/GELU 门控 MLP 中**关闭态门仍泄漏微小负值**的神经元承载——门控 MLP 对读到的一切方向平均施加收缩，并不专门针对上一层写入；在 Qwen2.5-0.5B 上退火掉激活函数负区后阻尼与抵消一同消失（窗口余弦 −0.184 → +0.021，代价 0.165 nats），因此抵消是"弱证据"，不能解读为模型在选择性地拒绝某些特征。

> **TL;DR:** This paper decomposes the adjacent-layer residual-stream cancellation reported by Patrawala et al. (2025) into three counterfactual terms — attention (A), co-writing (C), and a new "damping" term (D) — measured in 16 models (Llama, Qwen2.5, Mistral, Gemma, OLMo, OLMoE-1B-7B, GPT-2, Pythia…) on 16 FineWeb-Edu documents. The damping is carried by closed-gate "strengthening" neurons (SiLU/GELU leak slightly negative below zero, so a closed gate still subtracts a small multiple of what the neuron read) and it does NOT single out the previous layer's write: the same layer's write at another token is damped as much or more (ratio 0.88–0.99), and the write is damped within 9% of a random direction in 9 of 10 gated models. Causally, annealing the negative region away in continued pretraining of Qwen2.5-0.5B moves the window cosine from −0.184 to +0.021 while a matched control keeps it at −0.170 (loss cost 0.165 nats). The NeurIPS-2025 "propose-and-reject / selective correction" reading of cancellation is therefore largely an artifact of an architectural contraction gated MLPs apply to whatever they read.

## 核心贡献

- **三项分解（恒等式）**：把第 j 层沿上一层写入 v 的投影精确拆为 ⟨v̂, c_j⟩/∥v∥ = A + C − D：A 是注意力沿 v 的写入，C（共写）是**从 MLP 输入中移除 v 后** MLP 沿 v 仍写的内容，D（阻尼）是**因 v 存在**而少写的量；按定义 D > A + C 时该层对发生抵消。该分解在 16 个模型、两个语料（FineWeb-Edu、WikiText-103）上完成。
- **定位阻尼的载体神经元**：通过归一化层 Jacobian 的逐神经元精确分裂证明，门控模型（SwiGLU/GeGLU）的阻尼由"关闭态的增强型神经元"承载（lobe 项）——SiLU/GELU 在零下为小幅负值（最小 −0.278 / −0.170），关闭的门泄漏一个负倍数，输出权重与输入权重对齐的神经元因此**减去**其读到方向的一小部分；无门模型（Pythia-160m、GPT-2）则经由读写反向的活跃神经元产生阻尼。
- **证伪"选择性修正"**：上一层的写入**并未被 singled out**——同层在另一 token 的写入被阻尼得同样多甚至更多（比率 0.88–0.99），沿受控写入的阻尼在 10 个门控模型的 9 个中与随机方向相差 ≤9%，Patrawala 等人据以判断"选择性"的特征值谱签名在另一 token 的写入上同样出现。
- **因果干预实验**：对 Qwen2.5-0.5B / Llama-3.2-1B 继续预训练并把门激活负区退火至零，阻尼与抵消随之消失而配对对照保留；51M 从头训练模型去掉负区后阻尼只剩 32% 且损失不可区分（3.439 vs 3.441）；公开 ReLU 转换版 Llama-2-7B 只保留约 11% 的阻尼。
- **训练动力学**：在 OLMo-1B 发布的 checkpoint 上，阻尼从 0.004（2B tokens）增长到 0.304（3T tokens）而共写持平（0.120–0.175），余弦在 33B–64B tokens 间转负——抵消始于阻尼超过共写之时。

## Key Contributions

- **A counterfactual decomposition (Eq. 1).** The next layer's projection on the previous layer's write splits exactly into attention (A), co-writing (C = what the MLP writes along v when v is removed from its input), and damping (D = how much less the MLP writes along v because v is present); a pair cancels by definition when D > A + C. Applied to sixteen models on two corpora, with a pre-registered controlled-cosine protocol (drop position 0, zero the 5 outlier dimensions, subtract each layer's mean contribution) whose controls were fixed before measurement.
- **Neuron-level attribution of the damping.** An exact split of the average damping D̄ through the RMSNorm/LayerNorm Jacobian (Eq. 2) shows a single term — the linear-input term of closed strengthening neurons (the "lobe term", after the negative lobe of SiLU/GELU) — carries ≥59% of D̄ in all ten gated models and 71–140% of the damping D of the previous write itself. Non-gated models (Pythia-160m: median read–write cosine −0.362) build damping from active neurons whose output weights oppose their inputs.
- **The previous write is not singled out.** Three selectivity tests on the same tokens: (i) along the controlled write, damping is within 9% of a random direction in 9/10 gated models (20% above in Phi-3-mini; 8–20% above along the raw write); (ii) the same layer's write at another token is damped more in every model (ratio 0.88–0.99); (iii) the write's energy over the MLP Jacobian's eigendirections tracks a random direction (participation ratio 0.24–0.30 vs 0.33–0.34), and the whole-layer Jacobian contracts another token's write (−0.350) as much as the previous write (−0.339) on Patrawala et al.'s own model and text.
- **Causal interventions.** Continued pretraining of Qwen2.5-0.5B for 249M tokens with the gate's negative region annealed to zero removes the damping (−0.002 vs 0.320 in the control) and the cancellation (window cosine +0.021 vs −0.170) at a loss cost of 0.165 nats; Llama-3.2-1B behaves identically (−0.222 → +0.148, cost 0.341 nats). 51M-parameter from-scratch runs with the closed side removed keep only 32% of the damping at statistically indistinguishable loss; ReluLLaMA-7B and ProSparse-7B keep ~11% of Llama-2-7B's window damping; CATS thresholding at 50% sparsity lowers damping to 0.74–0.89× at 0.06–0.35 nats loss cost.
- **A reinterpretation with direct implications.** Adjacent-layer cancellation is weak evidence about which features a model rejects: it is the visible output of a contraction that gated MLPs apply, on average over directions, to whatever they read. Methods that follow features/steering directions across layers, or fit a layer's output from its input (transcoders), should account for this contraction before reading a correction as content.

## Methodology

- **Setup.** Write c_l = x_{l+1} − x_l for layer l's contribution (attention write a_l + MLP write m_l) into the residual stream. A gated MLP computes m(x′) = W_down (φ(W_gate z) ⊙ W_up z) with z the normalised stream and φ ∈ {SiLU, tanh-GELU, ReLU}. A neuron is *strengthening* when ⟨w ⊙ d_k, u_k⟩ > 0 (output weight aligned with input weight through the normaliser gain); its gate is *closed* when g_k·z ≤ 0.
- **Decomposition (Section 3).** For a followed write v = c_b (b = j−1 for adjacency), define A = ⟨v̂, a_j⟩/∥v∥, C = ⟨v̂, m_j(x′_j − v)⟩/∥v∥, D = ⟨v̂, m_j(x′_j − v) − m_j(x′_j)⟩/∥v∥; then ⟨v̂, c_j⟩/∥v∥ = A + C − D at every token by construction. Damping D̄ of a random direction u is estimated by finite differences (‖εu‖ = 0.02‖x′‖) and equals −(d−1)⁻¹ tr(PJ P), the mean contraction of the MLP Jacobian.
- **Per-neuron split (Section 4).** Differentiating the gated MLP through the normaliser gives J = M P with an exact six-term sum for D̄ (Eq. 2), split by gate state (closed/open) × alignment (strengthening/weakening). The damping-carrying term is the linear-input term of closed strengthening neurons: because φ(t_k) < 0 for SiLU/GELU below zero, a closed gate passes a small negative multiple of the neuron's input, and an aligned neuron subtracts a fraction of the direction it read (Gerstner & Schütze's per-neuron sign flip, summed over neurons).
- **Models and data.** Ten gated models (Llama-3.2-1B, Llama-2-7B, Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, Qwen2.5-0.5B, Mistral-7B, Gemma-2B, Phi-3-mini, plus checkpoint-released TinyLlama-1.1B, OLMo-1B, OLMoE-1B-7B), two ReLU conversions (ReluLLaMA-7B, ProSparse-7B), four non-gated (Pythia-160m, GPT-2, GPT-2-medium, OPT-1.3B), and gpt-oss-20b in the appendix. Measurements on sixteen FineWeb-Edu documents (192 tokens each), repeated on WikiText-103 and at 2,048 tokens; layer window L/8 ≤ i < 5L/8. Documents are the statistical units (t intervals, Wilcoxon tests). ~150 GPU-hours on one RTX 5090.
- **Interventions (Section 5).** (i) Continued pretraining of Qwen2.5-0.5B (3 seeds of batch order) and Llama-3.2-1B (1 seed) with the closed side of every gate activation scaled by λ annealed 1→0 between 13M and 131M tokens, vs a matched control arm on identical batches; (ii) 51M-parameter from-scratch runs (2 seeds, pre-registered analysis) over a λ-grid; (iii) inference-time removal of the closed side, CATS thresholding at 50% sparsity, and matched random open-entry zeroing controls.

## Results & Highlights

- **Decomposition across 16 models (FineWeb-Edu, window means, Table 4):** in the strongly cancelling models the damping exceeds attention+co-writing combined by 0.135–0.244 per unit of the previous write — e.g. Llama-3.2-1B has A + C = +0.253 (−0.022 attention + 0.275 co-writing) against D = +0.497, giving controlled cosine −0.222; weak cancellers like TinyLlama-1.1B have D = 0.145 vs A + C = 0.136 (cosine −0.010). Across models the damping varies 2.1× as much as A + C (std 0.146 vs 0.069) — the spread of cancellation is the spread of the damping.
- **Training dynamics on OLMo-1B's released checkpoints (2B → 3T tokens):** co-writing stays flat between 0.120 and 0.175 and attention within 0.031 of zero, while the damping of the previous write grows from 0.004 to 0.304; the adjacent cosine turns negative between 33B and 64B tokens and A + C − D between 64B and 129B — cancellation begins exactly when the damping overtakes what the next layer writes anyway.
- **The previous write is not selectively damped:** in all ten gated models the same layer's write at another token of the document is damped MORE than the token's own previous write (ratio of previous-write to other-token damping 0.88–0.99); along the controlled write the damping is within 9% of a random direction in 9 of 10 models (only Phi-3-mini is 20% above), and the whole-layer Jacobian response is −0.339 for the previous write vs −0.350 for another token's write and −0.306 for a random direction — the "selective correction" eigenvalue signature (37%/29% bimodal split) appears for another token's write too (41%/16%).
- **Causal removal in continued pretraining of Qwen2.5-0.5B (249M tokens, 3 seeds):** annealing the closed side away takes the window cosine from −0.184 to +0.021 and the write's damping from D = 0.336 to −0.002 (average damping 0.020 vs 0.291), while the control arm keeps cosine −0.170 and D = 0.320; the annealed model's loss is 2.888 vs 2.723 on common documents (+0.165 nats). Llama-3.2-1B matches: cosine −0.222 → +0.148 vs −0.181 control, +0.341 nats.
- **From-scratch and conversions:** 51M-parameter models trained on 1B tokens of FineWeb-Edu with the closed side removed keep 32% of their SwiGLU twins' damping at a loss two seeds cannot distinguish (3.439 at λ=0 vs 3.441 at λ=1); ReluLLaMA-7B and ProSparse-7B retain 11% and 11% of Llama-2-7B's window damping while remaining working models; CATS thresholding at 50% sparsity (59–85% of what it zeroes is closed) lowers damping to 0.74–0.89× and raises the cosine in all 10 models at 0.06–0.35 nats cost, whereas matched random open-entry zeroing leaves damping at 0.98–1.02×.
- **Reproduction honesty:** the authors re-ran Patrawala et al.'s released protocol on its main model and get −0.123 window mean (vs the ≈ −0.2 reported), strongest pairs −0.212; their own raw window cosine differs by at most 0.055 from the published numbers.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty: high.** The decomposition itself is an accounting identity, but using it to separate models (damping varies 2.1× more than co-writing) and then tracing the damping to a specific neuron class (closed strengthening neurons via the SiLU/GELU negative lobe) and confirming causally with annealing interventions is a genuine advance over Patrawala et al.'s observational account. The connection to Gerstner & Schütze's per-neuron sign flip — summed into a layer-level contraction — is elegant.
- **Rigor: strong, with stated limits.** The counterfactual D is validated as a property of the MLP (scaling v by 0.5/1.5 deviates from linearity by a median of only 0.13·D; removing v from the layer input instead gives 0.91–1.05× the same damping). However, the A/C split is acknowledged to be counterfactual-dependent: attention's own response to v is folded into A and never separated, so "co-writing" is not a purely intrinsic quantity.
- **Reproducibility: very good.** Fully specified protocol, public models/checkpoints, pre-registered from-scratch analysis, seed-level agreement reported, and the reproduction of the prior work's number (−0.123 vs ≈−0.2) is disclosed rather than buried.
- **Significance: real but bounded.** The claim is a corrective, not a demolition: it shows cancellation is *weak evidence* of selective rejection, and that any feature-tracing or transcoder method conflates this architectural contraction with content. The scope is bounded by the model set — everything is ≤8B parameters, two of the sixteen (ReluLLaMA-7B, ProSparse-7B) are conversions of a third (Llama-2-7B) that differ in distillation/instruction data/sparsity regularisation beyond the activation, and Llama-3.2-1B is itself pruned-distilled; OLMo-2 and Gemma-2 (MLP-output normalisation) are not decomposed at all.
- **Red flags / weak points:** (i) The main measurements rest on sixteen FineWeb-Edu documents of 192 tokens — intervals are over documents within one corpus, though 64-document and WikiText-103 replications move means by ≤0.032, and Patrawala's own effect was window-local (over all adjacent pairs the controlled cosine is positive in 4 of the 16 models). (ii) The selectivity tests operate at the level of *directions*, not features — the authors admit that whether particular features are singled out "would need a feature dictionary", so the refutation of *feature-level* selectivity is incomplete. (iii) The from-scratch runs never reach cancellation (no arm cancels at 1B tokens, nor a 125M pair at 12B), so the λ=0 result speaks only to the damping, not to cancellation; and with two seeds, a loss benefit of the closed side below ~0.01 nats (the ReGLU→SwiGLU gap Shazeer reported at T5-base scale) would not show. (iv) Removing the closed side costs 0.165–0.341 nats — the contraction is clearly *functional* even if not selective, which slightly softens the "architectural artifact" framing. (v) The Llama-3.2-1B continued-pretraining result is a single seed.

## Strengths

- An exact, checkable decomposition (Eq. 1) rather than another correlational observation; every step has a validation (linearity checks, alternative counterfactuals, cross-corpus replication).
- Neuron-level mechanism with an analytic route (the normaliser Jacobian) that matches finite differences to ~0.0002 median error — rare rigor in mech-interp.
- Causal, controlled interventions: annealed vs control arms trained on identical batches from identical weights, with the lobe term going to zero *by construction* once λ=0, confirming the mechanism end-to-end.
- Direct, fair engagement with the prior work: reuses Patrawala et al.'s protocol, reproduces their eigenvalue analysis on their model/text, and reports where their headline number does not replicate (−0.123 vs ≈−0.2).
- Practical implications spelled out for interpretability tooling (feature tracing, steering, transcoders) and for architecture/training design (trainable negative branches, ReLU conversion, magnitude sparsification all modulate the damping).

## Limitations

- Model scale capped at 8B parameters; no frontier-scale confirmation, and the largest open-weights datapoint (gpt-oss-20b, measured 4-bit) does not cancel at all, hinting the picture may differ for MoE at scale.
- Evidence base of sixteen 192-token documents from one corpus for the headline numbers (mitigated, but not eliminated, by the 64-document and WikiText-103 checks).
- Selectivity is refuted at the direction level only; feature-level selectivity (via dictionaries) is left open by the authors themselves.
- From-scratch experiments never develop cancellation, so the strongest causal claim rests on two continued-pretraining models (one seed for Llama-3.2-1B, 249M tokens).
- The two ReLU conversions confound the activation change with distillation, instruction data, and a sparsity regulariser, so their ~11% damping retention is illustrative rather than clean.

## Relevance

直接命中机制可解释性（mechanistic interpretability）方向：任何沿残差流追踪特征、做 steering、或用 transcoder 拟合层输入输出以寻找"修正回路"的分析，都必须先扣除本文刻画的门控 MLP 收缩效应，否则会把架构性阻尼误读为模型内容。对研究 SwiGLU/GELU 激活函数设计与预训练动力学（阻尼随训练增长）的工作同样有直接参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does feature-level selectivity survive once the architectural contraction is subtracted — e.g., re-run the damping analysis against sparse-autoencoder feature directions rather than layer writes and random directions?
- Why do OLMoE-1B-7B and gpt-oss-20b (both mixtures of experts) not cancel: is sparse routing intrinsically less contractive, or is it a 4-bit/expert-width artifact?
- Does the 0.165–0.341-nat loss cost of removing the closed side shrink at scale, or does the lobe-term contraction become more functional as models grow (the 51M null cannot resolve differences below ~0.01 nats)?
- Could a trainable negative branch (Huang 2024) or a shifted activation keep the expressivity of the lobe while decoupling it from the damping, dissociating the two roles of the closed side?
- Do interpretability methods that read "the next layer corrects the previous one" (e.g., attribution patching along the residual stream) change their conclusions once A/C/D are separated?
