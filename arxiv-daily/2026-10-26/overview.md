# 周报总览 / Weekly Overview（2026-10-26 .. 2026-11-01）

本周共评估 **3,145** 篇新论文（周一至周日），728 篇进入摘要门控、528 篇通过，最终按领域配额经逐对比较（Bradley-Terry）选出 **103** 篇，分属 9 个领域；27 篇星标候选经全文精读后评出 **8** 篇本周必读。本周主线是**“静默失效恰恰藏在工具链最自信的地方”**：bf16 舍入让 RL 策略每步只有 0.4–0.5% 的权重真正送达采样器，而所有已部署诊断都看不见；精度更高的 FP8 反而让 Evo 2 检查点在 Blackwell 上失效；8 个主流 agent harness 中 7 个在无头模式关闭 stdin，把“没执行”变成 status 0 的假成功；编码智能体单请求成功率 95%、串成 20 请求长链只剩 51%——崩塌来自无人核查的自检遗漏；连可解释性也没能幸免：相邻层“抵消”被证明主要是门控 MLP 关闭态泄漏造成的收缩假象，而非选择性拒绝。修复者同样活跃：无偏舍入导出、TLA+ 模型检查的 Receipt 协议、可证明整数精确的 W4A8、零 SM 参与的 FSDP 集合通信、以及把“定时炸弹”损失尖峰追到 LM head 量化误差的一行修复。

This week we evaluated **3,145** fresh papers (Mon–Sun); 728 reached the abstract gate, 528 passed, and **103** were selected via within-area pairwise ranking (Bradley-Terry) under per-area quotas across 9 areas; 27 star candidates were fully read and **8** earned Must-Read status. The through-line: **silent failure lives exactly where the stack is confident.** bf16 rounding delivers only 0.4–0.5% of RL policy weights per step to the sampler — invisible to every deployed diagnostic; *more-accurate* FP8 breaks Evo 2 checkpoints on Blackwell; 7 of 8 mainstream agent harnesses close stdin headless, turning "never ran" into a status-0 false success; coding agents complete 95% of single requests but only 51% of 20-request chains, felled by unchecked self-review misses; even interpretability takes a hit — adjacent-layer "cancellation" turns out to be largely a closed-gate-leak contraction artifact rather than selective rejection. The fixers showed up in force too: unbiased rounding exports, a TLA+-model-checked Receipt protocol, provably integer-exact W4A8, zero-SM FSDP collectives, and a one-line LM-head fix that defuses "time-bomb" loss spikes.
