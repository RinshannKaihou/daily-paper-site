Why this matters

Rare first-party forensics of how aggressive 4-bit optimization breaks generation — shipped as deployable, fully open checkpoints and recipes.

激进低精度优化下“精度—正确性”权衡的一手实证：逐模块归因 FP4 专家与 lm_head 精度如何引发冗长膨胀与终止失败。

Critical edge

The “surpasses Nemotron 3 Ultra” headline compares against NVIDIA's own previous generation — the same table shows DeepSeek V4 Flash at 72.00% on SWE-bench Pro, which the abstract does not surface.

Pretraining

Super (120B-A12B) distills 1T tokens from Ultra 3 Base, then runs Indic CPT, a 1M-context stage, and 65.4B tokens of agentic SE mid-training at 256K — which collapsed RULER-1M (79.9→33.5) and required a 9B-token recovery stage. Lightning (30B-A3B) distills from Super 3 Base with a pruned, transplanted MTP head.

Post-training

Two-stage SFT builds the VLM; joint RLVR+RLHF adds executable Olympiad and SEC-filing finance environments; five domain teachers are consolidated via on-policy multi-teacher distillation (MOPD) with self-anchoring and proximal-teacher smoothing. A final phase retrains only the MTP head with hidden-state mixing.

Quantization

NVFP4 W4A4 on MoE GEMMs, FP8 elsewhere (4.82 whole-model BPE), via MSE block-scale PTQ + 200-step quantization-aware distillation (~1,024 GB200 GPU-h per candidate). Appendix B attributes verbosity inflation to FP4 MoE experts and stop-margin fragility to lm_head precision.

Serving

All engineering targets one objective: interactive agentic serving at ≥50 output tok/s/user on a 20-turn prefix-reuse proxy. vLLM contributions: Mamba checkpoints at speculative-replay boundaries, ReplaySSM state replay, per-quantization GEMM selection, and standalone drafters (LiLiCorr, DFlash, DSpark).

Strengths 优势

  • Genuine end-to-end co-design — architecture, data, post-training, quantization, and serving each ablated, composed against one constant objective (≥50 tok/s/user interactive agentic serving).
  • Exceptional openness — base, BF16, and NVFP4 checkpoints; three standalone drafters; the 2.83T-token Indic corpus; datasets, cookbooks, and vLLM PR numbers.
  • Mechanism-level quantization forensics — per-module sensitivity attribution (lm_head ≈ half of NVFP4 sensitivity; FP4 MoE experts drive runaway generation) rarely published in system reports.
  • Honest methodology — run-to-run variation quantified (~1 pt), SWE harness-dependence measured across six harnesses, decontamination recall limitations stated.
  • Test-time scaling without retraining — +9–10 pts on Terminal-Bench / DeepSWE via parallel agents and LLM-as-a-Verifier selection.

Limitations 局限

  • Capability ceiling — behind DS-V4 Flash, Qwen3.8 Flash-Next, and GLM-5.3 Flash on Terminal-Bench 2.1 (60.67 vs 71.82–85.80), tau-bench, BrowseComp, GPQA-Diamond, and HLE; it wins throughput-per-GPU, not absolute capability.
  • Stage interference — 256K mid-training collapsed 1M-context RULER (79.9→33.5), fixed only by an extra 9B-token recovery run; Indic mixture ratio chosen via smaller-model ablations.
  • Favorable measurement frame — throughput wins measured on NVIDIA GB200 with NVIDIA-tuned vLLM, on a multi-turn prefix-reuse workload chosen to favor hybrid-Mamba state economics.
  • Costly or circular components — QAD used 512 GB200 GPUs per candidate; self-verification reuses the same checkpoint with no external-verifier baseline; BrowseComp subset internal.
  • Claims are Super-only — Lightning skips MOPD, Indic CPT, and mid-training, so most capability evidence does not transfer.

Open Questions 进一步探索

  1. How much of the 2.34× serving advantage survives on non-NVIDIA hardware or equally tuned competing stacks — and what is the quality-adjusted (accuracy per GPU-hour) ranking?
  2. Can the FP4 MoE verbosity / termination failure be fixed at the source (e.g., two-pass FP4 residual emulation) rather than patched by QAD — and does the stop-vs-continue margin geometry generalize to other MoE architectures?
  3. What data or objective property makes 256K mid-training collapse 1M-context RULER (79.9→33.5), and can mid-training be made context-length-preserving without a recovery stage?
  4. Why is Tau3 Banking (18.88%) so weak vs DS-V4's 73.79% when other tool-use benchmarks are competitive — user simulator, retrieval tools, or a genuine capability gap?
  5. Does an independent verifier beat same-checkpoint LAV self-verification, and how do selection gains degrade as the candidate pool grows beyond 5?
  6. Lightning ablations favored a 30% Indic mixture over Super's 10% — is Super's choice compute-limited, and what would 20–30% close at larger budget?
