Why This Matters

Compression can silently destroy verified internal mechanisms while aggregate metrics look merely degraded — a silent-failure mode hiding inside optimized models.

压缩（蒸馏）会在聚合指标看似正常时静默摧毁已验证的内部机制——这是一种模型内部的“静默失效”。

Critical Edge

The Greater-Than “survival” rests on one head's attention pattern, not a verified circuit: path patching couldn't run in 6 layers, and attention similarity is a weak proxy — IOI heads matched at r = 0.78 still showed 0% functional preservation.

Methodology · Five Probes, Two Verified Circuits

Teacher GPT-2 (12 layers, 124M) vs its depth-halved student DistilGPT-2 (6 layers, 82M; every-other-layer init + soft-label distillation, Sanh 2019). Two causally validated circuits are tracked across the distillation gap — IOI (Wang et al. 2022) and Greater-Than (Hanna et al. 2023):

  • 01Debiased linear CKA on residual streams — same-index vs proportional layer mapping (d → ⌊d·11/5⌉).
  • 02Tuned logit lens — per-layer next-token and relative accuracy through final LayerNorm + unembedding.
  • 03Attention decomposition — per-head entropy, head-type markers, cross-model cosine of T×T patterns.
  • 04Teacher-pretrained SAEs (gpt2-small-res-jb, SAELens) as fixed basis — feature survival, L0 drift, magnitude drift.
  • 05Causal patching + DLA — activation/path patching, direct logit attribution, name-mover matching (500 discovery / 500 held-out prompts).

Tasks: 1,000 IOI ABC-template prompts · 1,000 year-comparison prompts · 1,000 TinyStories samples. Bonferroni-corrected paired t/Wilcoxon, Cohen's d, seeds {42, 123, 7}. TransformerLens on a single NVIDIA L4.

Strengths

what holds up
  • Contrastive frame — one collapsing and one surviving circuit in the same teacher–student pair isolates what distillation preserves better than either circuit alone.
  • Multi-probe convergence — CKA, logit lens, attention statistics, SAE features, and causal patching all triangulate the same “compress, don't truncate” story.
  • Remapping quantified per-sample — r = 0.945 over 1,000 prompts (93.6% of samples r > 0.8), confirmed on held-out prompts, not just aggregate similarity.
  • Statistical hygiene above the norm — Bonferroni correction, Cohen's d everywhere, discovery/evaluation splits, multi-seed CKA — plus a genuinely self-critical limitations section.

Limitations

read with care
  • One pair, two circuits, one recipe (124M → 82M, every-other-layer init + soft labels); other depths, families, and modern reasoning distillation untested.
  • GT survival verified at single-head granularity only — the full causal chain (year encoding → comparison → output) is unconfirmed; path patching inapplicable within 6 layers.
  • The IOI null doesn't exclude an alternative subgraph — no causal scrubbing or automated circuit discovery (ACDC) was run.
  • −0.467 may be a repeated-name frequency heuristic — flagged but untested; and the teacher-SAE basis is blind to student-only features (0.53 survival is a lower bound).
Questions for Further Exploration
Q1 · SCOPE

Does the compressibility dichotomy hold for intermediate-depth circuits and other pairs — 8-layer students, Pythia/OLMo, BERT→DistilBERT?

Q2 · CONFOUND

Is the IOI sign reversal just a repeated-name heuristic? Controlled prompts varying name repetition would settle “misled” vs “counting occurrences”.

Q3 · FIX

Would circuit-aware distillation — intermediate-layer matching at functionally critical layers, attention-transfer losses — rescue deep circuits like IOI?

Q4 · SCALE

Do student-trained SAEs reveal auxiliary features behind L4H1? Does the dichotomy extend to chain-of-thought distillation in modern small models?
