> **一句话总结：** 本文提出打破"知识截止/正确性"混淆的新型基准 ConfabQA（784 题，含"冷门截止前"类别），用线性探针、稀疏自编码器（SAE）和直接优化方向三种工具证明：小型语言模型（Qwen3-1.7B、Gemma 2 2B）在生成第一个 token 之前的隐藏状态中就已编码"拒绝还是编造"的决策——一个晚期层的单一方向即可把大多数编造翻转为拒绝开场白（Qwen 上开场白率 100%、Gemma 上判定拒绝率 0%→87%），而反向推动会让拒绝坍缩为编造却不伤及本来正确的答案；正确性信号则是模型相关的，仅在 Llama 3.2 3B 上强且可跨数据集迁移（PopQA 上超出最强提示基线 +24.9 pp，为 Qwen 的 5.7 倍）。

> **TL;DR:** A single-author interpretability study introducing ConfabQA (784 items with an "obscure pre-cutoff" cell that breaks the usual cutoff/correctness confound) and probing the pre-first-token hidden states of Qwen3-1.7B, Gemma 2 2B, and Llama 3.2 3B. The refusal-vs-confabulation probe beats the strongest prompt-feature baseline by +7.4 pp (TF-IDF 0.820 → 0.894) on Qwen3-1.7B and +16.3 pp on Gemma 2 2B, and a one-shot push along the recovered direction drives the refusal-opener rate to 100% on wrong items (though only ~30% complete as judged refusals on Qwen, vs 87% on Gemma at super-norm dose). Correctness probing, by contrast, adds almost nothing over prompt features on Qwen (+2.4 pp all items, within fold noise) but is large and portable only on Llama 3.2 3B (+24.9 pp on PopQA vs Qwen's +4.4 pp, an 5.7x gap at 1.8x parameters, with 81% of margin surviving cross-dataset transfer) — attributed to Llama 3's factuality post-training stage. Honest, control-rich work; the practical takeaway is that the direction gates the refusal *opening speech act*, not the knowledge behind it.

## 核心贡献

- **ConfabQA 基准（784 题）**：4 个领域（科学/历史/文化/电影）× 3 个类别（知名截止前 143 题、冷门截止前 153 题、截止后 488 题）。"冷门截止前"类别填充了"答案大概率在训练数据中但模型仍会答错"这一标准两格设计留下的空单元格，从而切断"截止后 = 不知道"的混淆——直接回应了正确性探针文献中普遍缺失的控制。
- **隐藏状态 vs 提示文本的严格对照**：每个探针都与四个递增强度的提示特征基线（TF-IDF、工程化文本特征、+领域、+类别）在同一折上比较，报告 hadds（探针峰值 − 最强基线）。结论：**拒绝 vs 编造是模型内部信号**（Qwen +7.4/+9.6 pp、Gemma +16.3 pp，约为折间标准差的 4 倍），而 Qwen 上的**正确性信号几乎全可由题面预测**（+2.4 pp，在噪声内）。
- **拒绝方向的因果验证与三路恢复**：探针方向、Qwen-Scope SAE 特征 2191、直接优化方向三者两两夹角 50–81°，但在不超过状态自身范量的剂量下沿任一方向推动都能把编造翻转为拒绝开场白（"As..." 词族）；前缀强制对照（不动隐藏状态、只逼出开场 token）得到与任何推动相同的约 1/3 判定拒绝率——**中介作用完全发生在第一个 token**。
- **方向只管控"开场言语行为"，不管控知识**：反向推动使拒绝大多坍缩为编造（−1.2× 剂量下 77% 的拒绝变为错误回答），但原本正确的答案基本不受影响（−1.2× 剂量下判定正确率仍有 93%）。
- **SAE 分解出"恭敬语域集合"而非单一特征**：拒绝方向分解为语域特征集合（时刻对冲 4314、公文客气话 17077、礼貌情态 14361、规范开场白 2191、第二 Please 16612、第一人称 8875、道歉 14034），其中只有 2191 通过因果检验（α∈[200,750] 内将 30/30 错误项的下一 token argmax 翻转为拒绝开场白）。**在全部 32,768 个字典特征中未找到编造标记**——编造是"缺少事实的默认作答通路"，这与 Gemma 2/Claude 3.5 Haiku 上"克制是默认、幻觉是抑制失败"的已报道机制正好相反。
- **正确性信号是模型/后训练相关的**：Llama 3.2 3B 在 PopQA/TriviaQA 上的正确性裕度（+24.9/+21.3 pp）是 Qwen3-1.7B（+4.4/+9.6 pp）的 5.7×/2.2×；同家族的 Qwen3-4B 缩放对照只移动约 1.4 pp，排除了参数量解释，指向 Llama 3 后训练中的事实性阶段。剔除拒绝后 Llama 仍保留约 83% 的裕度（PopQA +24.94→+20.76 pp），说明它不是拒绝通道读出。

## Key Contributions

- **ConfabQA benchmark (784 items)**: 4 domains × 3 categories (well-known pre-cutoff, obscure pre-cutoff, post-cutoff). The obscure-pre-cutoff cell populates "answer was likely in training data but the model still fails," breaking the cutoff/correctness confound that standard pre-vs-post designs bake in.
- **A prompt-feature control protocol for probing**: every probe is scored against four escalating prompt-only baselines (TF-IDF, engineered text features, +domain, +category difficulty oracle) on the same 5-fold CV splits; hadds = probe peak minus strongest baseline. This separates "the hidden state encodes self-knowledge" from "the hidden state encodes the prompt, which encodes the answer" — a control the correctness-probing literature mostly omits.
- **Three independent recoveries of the refusal direction, all causally validated**: probe-recovered, SAE feature 2191's decoder, and a directly optimized direction are pairwise 50–81° apart, yet a one-shot prefill push at or below the state's own norm along any of them flips most confabulations into refusal openings. A prefix-forcing control reproduces the same downstream outcome without touching the hidden state, establishing complete mediation at the first generated token.
- **The direction gates the opening speech act, not the knowledge**: the opposite push collapses refusals into confabulations (77% of refusals become wrong answers at −1.2× dose) while originally correct answers stay intact (93% judged correct), so suppressing refusal does not surface latent knowledge and pushing refusal does not erase it.
- **SAE decomposition into a deferential-register ensemble**: the refusal direction resolves into register features (moment hedge, officialese, polite modality, the canonical "As" opener, apology) of which exactly one (2191) passes the causal test (30/30 argmax flips between α=200 and 750, saturating at 0.46× the state norm where the probe direction needs roughly twice the dose). No concentrated confabulation feature exists in the 32,768-feature dictionary — on Qwen3-1.7B the attempt is the default pathway and abstention is the marked state, inverting the Gemma-2/Claude-3.5-Haiku mechanism reported by Ferrando et al. (2025) and Lindsey et al. (2025).
- **Cross-model attribution of the correctness signal to post-training**: Llama 3.2 3B's correctness-probe margin on balanced PopQA subsamples is +24.9 pp vs Qwen3-1.7B's +4.4 pp (5.7× on 1.8× parameters); the Qwen3-4B within-family scaling control moves the margin by <2 pp, and dropping refusals keeps ≈83% of Llama's margin (+24.94 → +20.76 pp on PopQA), ruling out refusal-channel readout and parameter count in favor of Llama 3's factuality post-training stage.

## Methodology

The study runs three instruction-tuned SLMs (Qwen3-1.7B as the primary subject, Gemma 2 2B, Llama 3.2 3B, plus Qwen3-4B as a scaling control) greedily (64 new tokens, thinking suppressed) in bfloat16 on a single Apple M1 Pro. Every question's **prefill pass** yields the last-prompt-position hidden state at all 29 layers (shape 29×2048 for Qwen) — the representation immediately before the first generated token, one linear map from the output head.

**Probing pipeline**: per layer, StandardScaler → PCA(16) → logistic regression under 5-fold stratified CV; targets include correct (n=784), cutoff, refusal_vs_wrong (n=549), refusal_vs_wrong_within_post (n=393, conditioning out the cutoff cue), and within-stratum correctness probes (the disconfound tests). Every target is scored against the four prompt-feature baselines fit on identical folds.

**Judging**: a three-way same-model judge (Qwen3-1.7B labeling correct/refusal/wrong with a structured prompt) replaces brittle substring grading; cross-checks include Deep Research (Gemini 3.1 Pro with web search) on all 784 items (κ=0.78) and Claude Opus 4.7 on a 30-item stratified sample (30/30).

**Causal interventions**: a forward hook adds α·w (unit direction) to the last prompt token's pre-norm residual during prefill — one shot at the moment of commitment — with doses expressed as fractions of the mean state norm (‖h̄‖ ≈ 1640 for Qwen). Controls include norm-matched random directions, a shuffled-label probe direction, and prefix-forcing the literal opener tokens.

**SAE analysis**: Qwen-Scope (w32k-l50, 32,768 features, L0=50) applied at the post-block-27 residual; three correlational rankings (direct encoding, decoder alignment, activation differential) nominate features, and only nominated feature 2191 is causally tested.

**Statistics**: K=30 balanced 50/50 subsample bootstrap over 14 (dataset, model, target) cells reporting min–max ranges; a label-permutation null calibrates how small a margin is indistinguishable from selection bias (both sides maximize: 29 layers vs 4 baselines).

## Results & Highlights

- **Refusal-vs-confabulation beats prompt features (ConfabQA)**: on Qwen3-1.7B the layer-28 probe reaches 0.894 accuracy vs 0.820 for the strongest TF-IDF baseline (**hadds +7.4 pp**, ~4× the 0.019 fold std; balanced accuracy 0.874, ROC AUC 0.944); within post-cutoff it is 0.870 vs 0.774 (**+9.6 pp**). Gemma 2 2B: **+16.3 pp**. Llama 3.2 3B: +2.2 pp (within fold spread), but its refusal probe on PopQA/TriviaQA recovers **+18.44/+25.43 pp** over the strongest prompt baseline.
- **Correctness probing adds ~nothing on Qwen3-1.7B**: +2.4 pp over the strongest prompt baseline on all 784 items (0.824 vs 0.800 for the +category oracle), +3.0 pp within-pre-cutoff, and **−2.5 pp within-obscure** — all within per-fold noise (0.031–0.063). Text features alone hit 94.0–95.2% on the cutoff target (the mentioned year is a near-perfect predictor); the probe's 98.2% adds only +3.0 pp.
- **Steering matrix (Qwen, n=30 per subset)**: pushing the refusal direction at +1.8× dose takes wrong items' refusal-opener rate from 10% (baseline) to **100%** but judged refusals only to **30%**; refusal items stay at 100% judged refusal; the reverse push (−1.2×) turns 77% of refusals into wrong answers while 93% of originally-correct items stay correct. Gemma 2 2B is the most manipulable: wrong→judged-refusal flips **0% → 87%** at α=+2000 (vs Qwen's 30%), with sub-norm reruns at 0.31×/0.92× of state norm converting 30%/40% vs 3–20% for norm-matched random directions; Llama 3.2 3B's reverse push drops refusal 100% → 80% at α=−300.
- **SAE feature 2191 is the causal handle**: it fires on 100% of refusals (mean activation 268) vs 49% of wrongs; pushing its decoder flips **0/30 → 30/30** wrong-item next-token argmaxes to refusal openers between α=200 and 750 (Clopper–Pearson 95% CI [0.88, 1.00]), saturating at 0.46× state norm while the probe direction needs ~2× the dose (dose-for-dose table: 91% vs 45% opener rate at 0.30×). All four causal routes (probe, 2191, optimized, prefix-forcing "As"/"No") land at the same ~1-in-3 judged-refusal rate on the same 30 wrong items — consistent with complete mediation at token one.
- **Llama's correctness signal is large, refusal-independent, and portable**: on balanced PopQA subsamples Llama 3.2 3B's probe margin is **+24.9 pp** (range [20.6, 29.0]) vs Qwen3-1.7B's **+4.4 pp** (range [2.1, 6.5]) — 5.7× on 1.8× parameters; on TriviaQA +21.3 vs +9.6 pp (2.2×). The Qwen3-4B scaling control lands at +5.77 pp (range [2.34, 11.61]), so doubling parameters within-family moves h̄adds by ≈1.4 pp while switching family at smaller scale moves it ≈19 pp. Dropping refusals keeps Llama at +20.76/+17.71 pp (≈83% of margin is genuine correct-vs-wrong among attempted answers), and 81% of the within-dataset margin survives no-refit transfer across ConfabQA/PopQA/TriviaQA (Gemma's transfer retention is −54%, Qwen's −14%).
- **Behavioral regime split at equal accuracy**: the three models score nearly identically overall (30–32% correct on ConfabQA) but adopt opposite abstention policies — Llama refuses 97.5% (475/487) of post-cutoff failures, Qwen only about a third (all 147 of its refusals are post-cutoff; PopQA refusal census 1.7% vs Llama's 62.9%), Gemma in between.

## Authors & Credibility

Single-author work by Vladimir Kazei (ORCID 0000-0003-1214-3175), carried out in a personal capacity independent of any employer, on personal equipment (one Apple M1 Pro, 16 GB, bfloat16) — which makes the breadth of the control suite more impressive. The paper is unusually transparent: it discloses LLM assistance throughout (Claude Code implemented the probing/intervention/SAE/statistics code and co-drafted the manuscript; Gemini Deep Research generated ConfabQA items and performed the independent regrade), documents four known benchmark imperfections in a dedicated Limitations section, releases code, data, judge labels, and bootstrap outputs under MIT/CC BY 4.0 (github.com/vkazei/confabqa), and pre-registers v2 fixes. Methodological credibility is high: prompt-feature baselines on identical folds, a within-family scaling control, random-direction and shuffled-label intervention controls, a label-permutation null, cross-model and cross-dataset replication, and kappa-checked judge agreement (κ=0.78 with Deep Research; 30/30 with Claude Opus 4.7). The honesty about the 30% opener-vs-judged-refusal gap and the "not powered as an equivalence test" caveats is exemplary for interpretability work.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: solid rather than groundbreaking. The single-direction refusal result explicitly replicates Arditi et al. (2024) in the epistemic-abstention rather than safety-refusal regime; the genuinely new pieces are the obscure-pre-cutoff disconfound cell, the systematic prompt-feature baseline protocol, and the cross-family attribution of the correctness signal to post-training (Llama's factuality stage) with a scaling control.
- **Rigor**: strong on controls, weaker on statistical power and data hygiene. The intervention subsets are n=30 (the author's own Clopper–Pearson CI [0.88, 1.00] admits true rates as low as 0.88), and the headline ~1-in-3 judged-refusal equivalence across four routes is explicitly "not powered as an equivalence test." The ConfabQA golds were not systematically verified before the experiments: an unverified audit flagged 22 wrong-gold/false-premise items, 43 ambiguous ones, and 63 near-duplicate groups, and **107 post-cutoff items actually predate the stated cutoff** (74 by more than a month) — so the near-perfect cutoff probe partly recovers "what kind of entity," not a clean date.
- **Reproducibility**: good — code/data released, seeds documented per script, greedy deterministic decoding. But CV folds are stratified by label, not grouped by the 21 templated source files (up to 100 near-duplicate items each), so near-duplicates can straddle splits; the author flags this and notes a grouped-CV check was not run. The hadds metric itself carries a disclosed selection bias (probe maximizes over 29 layers vs 4 baselines, so the null expectation is slightly positive).
- **Significance**: the practical payoff is currently thin — the steering flips the *opener* (100% first-token refusal vocabulary) but only ~30% of Qwen generations complete as refusals, so as a hallucination-mitigation knob it mostly teaches the model to say "As of my knowledge cutoff..." and then answer anyway. The Llama correctness-signal finding is the more consequential result: it implies probe-based hallucination detectors are post-training-dependent, and that 80–90% correctness-probe accuracies in the literature may be prompt-artifacts for some model families.
- **Red flags**: the judge is Qwen3-1.7B labeling its own outputs (and Llama's) — de-risked by κ=0.78 agreement and a blind Claude regrade, but a human re-grade on Llama outputs is admittedly missing; family and post-training recipe remain confounded (no recipe-swap experiment); no ≥7B replication; the SAE was trained on the base model while the subject is Instruct, with reconstruction at EV 0.50 carrying feature-splitting/absorption risk that the author acknowledges scoping only one dictionary at one hook.

## Strengths

- The prompt-feature baseline protocol is the control the correctness-probing literature (Azaria & Mitchell, Marks & Tegmark, Orgad et al.) has been missing, and the demonstration that Qwen3-1.7B's "correctness probe" collapses against it (while Llama's does not) is a field-correcting result.
- The obscure-pre-cutoff cell is a clean design idea: it decouples "in training data" from "model gets it right" without losing the refusal population.
- Triangulation across four causal routes (probe, SAE feature, optimized direction, prefix-forcing) converging on the same first-token mediation is convincing causal reasoning, with random-direction and shuffled-label controls at matched norms.
- The finding that the refusal direction is a deferential-register ensemble where only the opener feature is causal — and that no confabulation feature exists in the dictionary — is a crisp, falsifiable mechanistic claim that inverts the Gemma/Claude narrative.
- Exceptional transparency: disclosed LLM assistance, disclosed benchmark defects with counts, released artifacts, and v2 commitments.

## Limitations

- The intervention governs the opening speech act, not the full abstention: on Qwen3-1.7B only ~30% of forced-opener generations end as judged refusals; closing that gap is explicitly left to future work.
- Small n throughout the causal experiments (30-item subsets; 12 confabulating items on Llama leave it "directionally suggestive" only), and the K=30 bootstrap reports subsample spread, not population uncertainty.
- ConfabQA data-quality issues (22 wrong golds, 63 near-duplicate groups, 107 cutoff-violating post-cutoff items, Anglophone skew, fuzzy science-domain cutoff boundary) mean the within-stratum ConfabQA numbers will shift in v2.
- All probes read only the last prompt position of the prefill pass; Orgad et al. (2024) locate truthfulness signal at exact-answer tokens, so the correctness null is scoped to the pre-generation state, not every state.
- The self-/same-family judge on cross-model outputs, the missing ≥7B replication, the family-vs-recipe confound, and the single-SAE (EV 0.50, base-model-trained) scope all bound the generality of the conclusions.

## Relevance

这项工作直接命中"LLM 可解释性 + 幻觉机理与缓解"的核心议题：它用探针、SAE 和方向干预把小模型"编造 vs 拒绝"的决策几何拆解到单特征层面，并证明幻觉检测信号强依赖后训练配方——对构建可靠、可拒绝回答的小型模型以及基于内部状态的幻觉检测器设计都有直接指导意义。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would a per-token (sustained) steering vector, rather than a one-shot prefill push, close the 30% opener-to-judged-refusal gap on Qwen3 without degrading fluency — and does the same hold at ≥7B scale?
- Does swapping the post-training recipe within a fixed model family (e.g., adding a Llama-style factuality stage to Qwen3) actually install the +25 pp correctness signal, or is architecture also required?
- Do the epistemic-abstention direction found here and the safety-refusal direction of Arditi et al. (2024) coincide, partially overlap, or occupy orthogonal subspaces in the same model?
- Is the absence of a confabulation feature an artifact of Qwen-Scope's EV 0.50 reconstruction (feature splitting/absorption), or would higher-EV dictionaries and multiple hooks still find no confabulation marker?
- Can the Llama-portable correctness direction be distilled into a runtime hallucination detector that beats semantic-entropy methods (Kossen et al. 2024) on latency, given it needs only a single prefill hidden state?
- How do the findings transfer to known-unknowns benchmarks that elicit refusals from the smaller models (the paper notes its refusal transfer test only had power at Llama scale)?
