> **一句话总结：** 本文把"内省"操作化为让语言模型用自然语言报告自身低层信号（MLP 神经元激活、注意力权重、SAE 特征），在 8 个 LM 上训练了数百个内省器，发现纯 SFT 得到的"高分行"其实是因果旁路（自报告依赖与信号相关但无因果关系的线索）：SFT 在内源评测上相关度高，但在把信号替换成随机值的干预评测中，晚层/非末位 token 的相关性仅剩随机水平；作者提出训练时干预（联合损失 L = w·L_end + (1−w)·L_int + β·L_KL，直接用 do() 替换后的值做监督），在保持内源性能的同时大幅修复干预相关性，且在 C4-Bengali 等分布外数据上优势最大，机制上还用 LoRA 范数与注意力头消融验证了信号确实经由"信号层 MLP → 下一层注意力头"的通路被搬运。

> **TL;DR:** Can an LM faithfully report its own internals? Finetuning it to report three low-level signal types (MLP neuron activation, attention weight, GemmaScope SAE feature) on Llama-3.2-1B / Gemma-2-2B (plus 6 more models incl. Qwen3-0.4B–8B-Base) via plain SFT looks excellent on endogenous correlation but collapses to chance on interventional eval (replace the signal with a counterfactual value at runtime and check whether the report follows) — interventional correlation reaches 0.86 only for early-layer/last-token neurons and is chance-level for all three signal types at late layers and non-final positions, i.e., causal bypass. Training on counterfactually perturbed values (joint loss with a KL controller, w=0.01 for neurons) fixes most of the bypass while keeping endogenous performance, and the advantage over SFT widens on OOD data (C4-Bengali vs in-distribution C4-English/Wikipedia). No baseline probe beats SFT (best ridge probe: non-trivial endogenous, chance interventional; SAE probe precision 0.04±0.02 vs 0.69±0.12 for SFT). Circuit analysis confirms the joint-loss model reads the actual signal: LoRA updates concentrate at the signal layer (MLP) and the layer after (attention), with overlapping-but-distinct endogenous (dense) vs interventional (sparse, gating) attention heads. Faithful introspection is possible but must be trained on mechanisms, not behavior — and it still fails on late-layer, early-token signals and does not generalize to unseen neurons/layers.

## 核心贡献

- **提出"因果旁路"（causal bypass）作为内省评测的核心失败模式并给出可判定的检验范式**：一个内省器若在内源评测（报告真实激活）得分高、但在干预评测（运行时把信号替换为随机值/do 操作后）得分低，它就是在报告与信号相关但因果无关的旁路线索（如上下文异常检测），而非真正读取内部信号；反之前者低后者高则是纯异常检测。只有在两种评测上都高分，才算"忠实的内省"。
- **大规模实证：朴素 SFT 系统性地导致因果旁路**：在 Llama-3.2-1B 的神经元/注意力与 Gemma-2-2B 的 SAE 特征上按（层 × token 位置 × 信号索引）网格训练数百个单点内省器，SFT 内源 Pearson 相关普遍很高，但干预相关仅在早层 + 靠近内省提示词的位置非平凡（第 2 层神经元最高 0.86），晚层且非末位 token 的三种信号全部为随机水平。
- **提出干预式训练（interventional training / joint loss）**：训练时把信号值替换为从拉伸均匀分布采样的反事实值，用替换后的值计算损失，并与内源损失加权混合、再加 KL 正则防止模型漂移（动态 β 控制在目标 KL 预算内）；该法在保持内源性能的同时修复了大部分干预相关性，且在分布外（C4-Bengali、OpenWebMath、CodeParrot 等）优势随内源难度增大而扩大。
- **泛化与扩展规律的刻画**：干预式训练可跨 token 位置泛化（更长训练上下文 → 更平缓的长度外推衰减），但在固定数据预算下最多同时内省 2 层 / 10 个神经元，且对未见过的层与神经元索引完全没有泛化——要覆盖更多信号位置只能加数据。
- **机制层面的验证**：LoRA 更新的 Frobenius 范数集中在信号层（MLP）与下一层（注意力搬运），对目标神经元的 W_up/W_down 更新排在首位且方向近正交（经正交通道路由、不破坏原信号）；注意力头消融显示内源信号由大量"密集"头读取、干预信号只依赖少数稀疏"门控"头（在有注入时才关注内省 token），两类头有显著重叠但功能不重合，且内源头向干预场景的迁移好于反向——印证异常检测机制是内省机制的真子集。

## Key Contributions

- **Formalizes "causal bypass" as the key failure mode of introspection evals, with a two-sided test that adjudicates it.** A self-report is causally bypassing if endogenous correlation (report the true activation on unseen text) is high but interventional correlation (replace the signal with a counterfactually sampled value at runtime, then ask the model to report) is low; the converse pattern indicates pure anomaly detection. Only high scores on both constitute faithful introspection. This gives a ground-truth-verifiable setting impossible to construct for high-level semantic introspection.
- **Large-scale negative result: naive SFT systematically produces causal bypass.** Training hundreds of single-locale introspectors over a grid of (layer × token position × signal index) across three signal types (MLP neuron activation on Llama-3.2-1B, attention weight, GemmaScope SAE feature on Gemma-2-2B), SFT yields high endogenous but low interventional correlation — chance-level for all three signal types at late layers and non-final token positions.
- **Interventional training (joint loss) as the fix:** L_joint = w·L_end + (1−w)·L_int + β·L_KL, where L_int supervises the report against a counterfactually perturbed value do(a_c = v), v ~ U(k·v_min, k·v_max) (k=10 for neurons, k=1 for attention), and β is dynamically adjusted to hold KL divergence to the base model within a budget (KL=0.1). This restores interventional faithfulness across most of the layer/position grid while preserving endogenous performance, and the margin over SFT grows on OOD splits (largest on C4-Bengali, smallest on near-indistribution English Wikipedia).
- **Scaling and generalization bounds for in-context introspection:** faithful token-position generalization works (train on n∈{16,32,64,128}, eval up to 512 tokens, with longer training contexts degrading slower), but at a fixed data budget models introspect at most ~2 layers and ~10 neurons, with zero generalization to unseen layers or neuron indices — more locales require more data per signal. KL divergence grows with training locales except across token positions, suggesting the same mechanism is reused across tokens but not across neurons/layers.
- **Mechanistic verification that the circuit reads the real signal:** LoRA Frobenius norms peak at the signal layer for MLP modules and the layer after for attention; updates to the target neuron index dominate W_up/W_down (not W_gate) with near-orthogonal directions, i.e., the signal is routed through an orthogonal channel with minimal interference; gradient attribution confirms the circuit reads the correct neuron and does not respond to injections in random neurons. Mean-ablation head rankings show endogenous signals are carried by many dense heads while interventional signals rely on a few sparse heads (including U-shaped "gating heads" that attend to the introspection token only under large injections), with significant top-head overlap (overlap coefficient / top-10 Jaccard vs layer-matched random controls) and asymmetric transfer: endogenous-ranked heads transfer better to interventional eval than vice versa.
- **A sharp architectural conclusion via the LoRA-gating experiment:** selectively disabling LoRA adapters on the input text (so the signal cannot move, "introspection coupling" avoided) leaves endogenous performance intact but drops interventional correlation to chance even for early layers — and no trick (injection-distribution changes, annealing, multi-position training) recovers it. Pretrained LMs cannot introspect their native signals in-place; faithful introspection requires finetuning changes at the signal site itself, hinting that introspective objectives should be introduced earlier in pretraining.

## Methodology

The paper turns introspection into a self-supervised, ground-truth-verifiable task. An LM M (finetuned with LoRA) receives text x plus a natural-language query localizing a signal — e.g., "The neuron activation of N10.24 at token 'hello' is {E}" — and must output the signal value at locale c = (layer l, index i, token t) (attention adds a source position s).

Three signal types are studied: (1) MLP neuron activations (post-Swish gated value, a continuous scalar, reported via a single-layer linear regression head on the last-layer residual stream, MSE loss); (2) attention weights α (bounded [0,1], reported via a logistic head with binary cross-entropy); (3) GemmaScope JumpReLU SAE features (discontinuous, binarized to active/inactive and verbalized, cross-entropy; evaluated with balanced accuracy since features fire on <1% of tokens). Training data is C4 English; OOD test sets are C4 Bengali, C4 Chinese, OpenWebMath, CodeParrot, and English Wikipedia.

The core method is the joint loss: L_joint = w·L_end + (1−w)·L_int + β·L_KL. The interventional term perturbs the signal in the forward pass to v ~ U(k·v_min, k·v_max) (k stretches the range so the signal is hard to ignore) and supervises the report against v; the KL term (β adjusted per batch, target budget KL=0.1) keeps the finetuned model from drifting, replacing the failed LoRA-gating design. Hyperparameters were swept per signal (w=0.01 neurons, 0.95 attention, 0.9 SAE). Evaluation is two-sided: endogenous metrics (Pearson/Spearman correlation for neurons and attention, balanced accuracy for SAE) on unseen text, and the same metrics on counterfactually replaced values (neurons: Gaussian fit; attention: training-distribution samples; SAE: steered on to median natural activation vs off to 0 along the decoder direction).

Experiments scale from single-locale sweeps (Sec. 3–4; five neurons / three heads / three SAE features per cell) to multi-locale training across token positions, layers, and signal indices (Sec. 5), replicated across eight LMs (Llama-3.2-1B/3B, Llama-3.1-8B, Qwen3-0.4B/1.7B/4B/8B-Base, Gemma-2-2B; best LR 1e-4 in all sweeps), with capability degradation checked on ARC-easy/HellaSwag/LAMBADA/WinoGrande (200 examples each). Circuit analysis (Sec. 6) combines LoRA-weight Frobenius norms, per-index weight-change rankings and cosine angles, gradient attribution, and mean-ablation head rankings with overlap/transfer statistics (overlap coefficient, top-10 Jaccard, effective-head sparsity, AUC transfer ratio) versus layer-matched random controls.

## Results & Highlights

- **SFT causes causal bypass across all three signal types:** endogenous Pearson correlation is high across the (layer × position) grid, but interventional correlation under SFT reaches 0.86 only for layer-2 neuron activations near the introspection prompt (early-layer, late-token locales) and falls to chance level for all three signal types (neurons, attention weights, SAE features on Gemma-2-2B) at late layers and non-final token positions.
- **Joint loss fixes the bypass while preserving endogenous performance:** the paired difference (joint − SFT) shows significant interventional-correlation gains concentrated in middle layers and positions, with endogenous scores "already high" so only small further improvements; the intervention-stretch factor k=10 is best for neurons and k=1 for attention.
- **Faithful introspection is more OOD-robust:** on OOD splits the joint-loss-over-SFT gap widens exactly where endogenous performance drops (largest on C4-Bengali, where SFT's endogenous correlation collapses hardest, including a token-shuffled "Shuff." control) and is smallest on near-in-distribution English Wikipedia; LoRA-gate-on variants (which break the causal path) underperform gate-off even endogenously.
- **No baseline probe beats SFT, and none is causally faithful (Apx. C):** the best neuron baseline (ridge regression on last-token hidden states, 30K examples) achieves non-trivial endogenous correlation but chance-level interventional correlation; the verbal baseline is at chance on both metrics despite generating parseable numbers >90% of the time; for SAE features the best probe reaches only 0.04±0.02 precision vs 0.69±0.12 for SFT and 0.65±0.12 for the joint-loss model, and no baseline exceeds chance on interventional eval.
- **Scaling bounds (Sec. 5):** with a fixed data budget, multi-locale models successfully introspect up to 2 layers and 10 neurons; there is no generalization to unseen layers or unseen neuron indices (100 held-out neurons), and covering more locales requires scaling per-signal data; token-position generalization does work — training on lengths n∈{16..128} and evaluating up to m=512, longer training contexts give slower drop-off, though interventional performance generalizes less favorably than endogenous.
- **Cross-model replication (Apx. H, eight LMs):** the SFT causal-bypass finding and the joint-loss fix replicate on Llama-3.2-3B, Llama-3.1-8B, and Qwen3-0.4B/1.7B/4B/8B-Base; overall introspection performance tends to decrease with model size (larger residual streams may drown small signals), but the joint-loss-vs-SFT gap stays constant or trends upward with scale. Downstream degradation stays gradual at the chosen KL=0.1 budget (ARC-easy, HellaSwag, LAMBADA, WinoGrande, 200 examples each).
- **Circuit evidence (Sec. 6):** LoRA update norms peak at the signal layer for MLP and one layer after for attention; W_down/W_up updates to the target neuron index always rank first with near-orthogonal update angles; endogenous signals are carried by many positively-contributing heads (dense) while interventional signals rely on fewer positive heads plus more total heads including negative/regularizing ones, with significant top-head overlap vs random controls and better transfer from endogenous-ranked to interventional eval than the reverse; U-shaped gating heads that attend to the introspection token only under large injections are more abundant among top interventional heads.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** The causal-bypass critique of introspection evaluations is timely but not entirely new — Morris & Plunkett (2025) and Macar et al. (2026) already flagged anomaly-detection shortcuts and non-causal introspection, and Betley et al. / Zeng et al. finetuned self-modeling before. The genuinely new parts are (a) making bypass *measurable* via ground-truth low-level locales, (b) the training-time fix (supervising on do()-perturbed values, essentially causal-consistency training imported into introspection), and (c) the circuit-level confirmation that anomaly-reporting heads are a subset of endogenous-reporting heads. The LoRA-gating negative result ("pretrained LMs cannot introspect native signals; finetuning must modify the signal site") is a strong, falsifiable claim backed by multiple failed recovery attempts.
- **Rigor:** High. Hundreds of introspectors across a (layer × position × index) grid, three signal types, eight models, mean±std over repeated indices, hyperparameter sweeps per signal type, a KL controller validated against downstream benchmarks (ARC-easy/HellaSwag/LAMBADA/WinoGrande), gradient-attribution checks that the circuit reads the *correct* neuron and ignores injections into random neurons, and layer-matched random controls for the head-overlap statistics. The two-sided endogenous/interventional eval design is clean and reusable.
- **Weak baselines in one sense:** the comparison set is ridge/logistic probes and verbal readout — modern external meta-model explainers (the Li et al. 2025 / Karvonen et al. 2026 line) are discussed but not run head-to-head, on the fair grounds that they produce an *external* explainer rather than in-context self-introspection; still, a reader wanting "which approach reports my neurons best" does not get that table. The "verbal" SAE baseline leans on lexicons from an LLM judge (Qwen-3.8-27B / Claude Opus 5.5) and is close to a strawman.
- **Missing ablations/details:** the k (perturbation stretch) and w (loss mix) sweeps are per-signal but the interaction between k and injection distribution vs the fitted-Gaussian eval distribution is only lightly probed; the claim that gating heads "regularize" interventional heads is suggested by the negative-head counts but not causally demonstrated; the multi-neuron scaling result (2 layers / 10 neurons at fixed budget) conflates data-per-locale with total budget in a way that makes the "scales with more data" conclusion unsurprising.
- **Overclaiming risk (mostly well-contained):** the paper is careful to say its setup is "first-order awareness" and "one could argue is simply a probe." The remaining risk is conceptual: passing interventional eval with k=10-stretched uniform injections may certify sensitivity to *large* perturbations of the readout channel rather than faithful tracking of the neuron's causal role in the model's computation — the authors themselves flag the hydra-effect/self-repair and distributed-representation caveats (dependence vs production) as reasons their definition may be too strict, which is commendable honesty.
- **Red flags:** minor — "hundreds of introspectors across eight LMs" oversells slightly since the full grid is only on Llama-3.2-1B (neurons/attention) and Gemma-2-2B (SAE), with the other six models on a reduced 16-locale grid; and the headline task (report a scalar at a named locale) is far from the semantic introspection people actually care about, a gap the limitations section owns.

## Strengths

- Converts a fuzzy philosophical debate (do LMs introspect?) into a ground-truth, two-sided, falsifiable evaluation with a clean taxonomy: faithful introspection / causal bypass / anomaly detection.
- The fix (interventional training with a KL controller) is simple, well-motivated, and shown to be the *only* thing that works — the LoRA-gating alternative is exhaustively falsified with multiple recovery attempts reported.
- Genuine mechanistic depth: LoRA-norm localization, orthogonal-channel routing evidence (W_up/W_down vs W_gate), gradient attribution to the correct neuron, and a quantitative head-taxonomy (dense endogenous heads vs sparse interventional heads with U-shaped gating) that connects to and validates prior anomaly-detection mechanisms as a subset.
- Honest, extensive negative results and limitations: late-layer early-token signals remain unsolved even with the joint loss; no generalization to unseen locales; the setup admits it may be "simply a probe."
- Broad replication across eight LMs and five OOD corpora; code and detailed appendices released.

## Limitations

- **The task is minimal, arguably not introspection:** reporting a neuron scalar at a named locale is first-order awareness — a direct translation of state, not meta-cognitive representation (per Lindsey's criteria); the paper concedes this may be "simply a probe" and leaves second-order tasks (comparing feature importances, gating behavior on feature activation) to future work.
- **A structural blind spot persists:** faithful introspection of late-layer, early-token-position signals fails even with interventional training (attributed to lack of residual components downstream to route the signal); the suggested fixes (looped/recurrent architectures, locale-specific regularization) are untested speculation.
- **No cross-locale generalization:** unseen layers and unseen neuron indices introspect at chance — the method cannot report on signals it was not trained on, limiting practical interpretability use; semantic-label generalization via Neuronpedia descriptions (Apx. L) produced only endogenous transfer, i.e., causal bypass returned.
- **Definitional strictness may over-count bypass:** interventions can be repaired by self-recovery/hydra effects or be ill-defined for distributed features (dependence vs production in causality), potentially under-reporting true causal reliance; the authors propose but do not implement a relaxed causally-grounded eval (e.g., freezing compensators à la path patching).
- **Practicality gap:** introspecting scalars is not yet actionable for monitoring or safety; the path to utility runs through semantically meaningful signals (SAE features), whose introspection here is binary active/inactive rather than graded.

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would interventional training transfer to *semantic-level* introspection (reporting concepts, beliefs, or uncertainty) where the intervention itself is hard to define — or is causal bypass fundamentally unavoidable once you cannot do() the target?
- Can the late-layer, early-token blind spot be closed with architectural capacity (looped/recurrent blocks) or locale-specific regularization, as the authors speculate — and would that recover interventional correlation without re-introducing bypass?
- Does the finding that anomaly-detection heads are a subset of endogenous-introspection heads hold for injected *concepts* (Lindsey-style steering) and for deception-related features, where the safety stakes of causal bypass are highest?
- Could locale identifiers as soft embeddings (per Li et al. 2025 / Loracles) buy generalization to unseen neurons/layers, or is per-locale data a fundamental cost of faithfulness?
- What happens if introspective objectives are added during pretraining rather than post hoc — do representations become "pre-introspectable," as the LoRA-gating negative result hints?
- Is the k-stretched uniform intervention the right counterfactual distribution — how sensitive is certified faithfulness to the choice of V'_c, and would a relaxed, compensation-frozen evaluation (path-patching style) rescue models currently judged as bypassing?
