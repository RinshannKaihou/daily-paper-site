> **一句话总结：** 本文证明当 MXFP4 权重行的块指数跨度不超过 3 时，该行可精确表示为"一个 INT8 向量 × 单个 2 的幂缩放因子"，从而把整个线性层折叠成单次 INT8 GEMM（每个输出仅一次缩放）；真实权重 97.9–99.97% 的行天然满足该条件，据此提出的 SC-MXFP4（仍是标准 MXFP4 文件）与 16 块整数乘子格式（4.26 bit）在 Apple M5 上实现比特精确的 W4A8 系统，在 125B MoE（Qwen3.8-Flash-Next）上 GSM8K 达 94.8%（MLX MXFP4 为 91.0%），预填充比 MLX 快 1.34–1.74×（稠密模型）。

> **TL;DR:** On hardware whose fastest datapath is INT8×INT8 (Apple M5 Neural Accelerators; measured 1.79–1.98× the FP16 rate, while every float×integer mix is slower than FP16), the authors show a row of MXFP4 weights is exactly one INT8 vector times one power-of-two scale whenever its block exponents span ≤3 — a condition 97.9–99.97% of rows already satisfy across seven models (99.93% of the 95M rows of the 125B MoE Qwen3.8-Flash-Next). The resulting spread-constrained MXFP4 (SC-MXFP4, still a standard MXFP4 file) plus a 4.26-bit integer-multiplier variant run W4A8 prefill 1.34–1.74× faster than MLX MXFP4 on Qwen3-1.7B and 1.22–1.29× on the 125B MoE, bit-identical to the quantized reference; on WikiText-2 the 16-block format lands within 0.06 perplexity of NVFP4-with-scale-search (10.40 vs 10.34 on Qwen3-1.7B, at 0.24 fewer bits/weight), and on GSM8K (all 1319 problems, every matrix quantized) it solves 94.84% vs 90.98% for MLX's MXFP4.

## 核心贡献

- **逐行尺度折叠定理（Proposition 1）**：证明 MXFP4 权重行可精确重写为 INT8 向量 × 2^(E−1) 的充要条件是块指数跨度 ≤3（此时 |q| ≤ 96；跨度 ≥4 时必然超出 ±127）。满足条件的层变成对完整归约维度的**单次 INT8 GEMM**，每个输出只做一次缩放，INT32 累加在 K ≤ 176k 时不会溢出——彻底消除了现有 MXFP4 内核在归约内部逐 32 元块施加 E8M0 缩放的开销。
- **代价有界的 Spread-Constrained 量化器（SC-MXFP4）**：把低于 max−3 的指数抬升到该值，被改动块的舍入误差至多只有该行最大网格误差的 1/16（Proposition 2）。产物仍是**任何 MXFP4 解码器都能原样读取的标准 MXFP4 文件**，只是位宽降至 4.07 bit/权重；在七个模型上强制该条件至多改变 0.007 困惑度。
- **对"真实权重为何天然满足"的机理解释**：用零模型证明高斯行内块最大值的动态范围极窄（log₂(max/min) 中位数 1.1–1.5，5 万行模拟零违例），重尾 Student-t(4) 则 2.7–37% 违例；真实权重的少量违例集中于 down_proj 的死通道（82%）与 gate_proj 的离群通道——恰好是被粗化也无害的小块。
- **整数精确格式族（§3.5）**：把"2 的幂块尺度"推广为小整数块乘子 m ∈ {1..10} + FP4 (E2M1) 码本 + 16 宽块（4.26 bit），保持"单次精确 INT8 GEMM"性质的同时，精度超过 NVFP4 标准缩放规则、逼近带尺度搜索的 NVFP4——精度不再需要用预填充速度来换。
- **完整 W4A8 系统 + 大规模实证**：在 MLX 上实现稠密/MoE 预填充与解码内核（输出与量化参考**逐比特一致**），覆盖 Qwen3-0.6B/1.7B/4B、SmolLM3-3B、OLMoE-1B-7B、Qwen3.5-35B-A3B 及 125B 的 Qwen3.8-Flash-Next（FP8 检查点，GSM8K 94.8%）。

## Key Contributions

- **Per-row scale folding (Proposition 1):** an MXFP4 weight row equals one INT8 vector times the power-of-two scale 2^(E−1) (E = its smallest block exponent) **iff** its block exponents span ≤3 (then |q| ≤ 96; span ≥4 forces |q| ≥ 128 under the OCP rule). A folded layer is a single INT8 GEMM over the full reduction with one rescale per output, and INT32 accumulation cannot overflow for K ≤ ~176k — removing the per-32-element-block E8M0 scaling that existing MXFP4 kernels do inside the reduction.
- **Spread-constrained MXFP4 (SC-MXFP4) with a cost bound (Proposition 2):** raising exponents below max−3 coarsens only blocks that are small relative to their row, adding at most 1/16 of the row's largest rounding error. The result is still a **valid standard MXFP4 file** readable by any decoder (MLX's own kernels read it unchanged), at 4.07 bits/weight (K=4096) vs MXFP4's 4.25.
- **An explanation of why real weights already comply:** a null model shows Gaussian rows have tightly concentrated block maxima (median log₂(max/min) = 1.1–1.5; zero violations in 50k simulated rows per width), while heavy-tailed Student-t(4) rows violate in 2.7–37% of cases; the few real violations (e.g., 2.1% of Qwen3-4B rows) concentrate in down_proj (9.0%, 82% caused by near-zero "dead channel" blocks) and gate_proj (3.6%, outlier input channels).
- **An integer-exact FP4 format family (§3.5):** replacing power-of-two scales with integer block multipliers m ∈ {1..10} over 16-wide blocks (4.26 bits) keeps exact single-GEMM INT8 execution while beating NVFP4's standard scale rule and coming within 0.06 perplexity of NVFP4 with scale search — accuracy no longer has to be traded for prefill speed on integer hardware.
- **A complete, exact W4A8 system on Apple M5 (MLX):** fused dense prefill, grouped MoE prefill (counting-sort of token–expert assignments + one grouped INT8 GEMM), and a decode GEMV reading packed weights directly — kernel outputs **bit-identical** to the quantized reference (0 of 298M checked packed weights differ on the 125B model), evaluated end-to-end on seven models up to 125B parameters.

## Methodology

The paper proceeds in three layers — a theory, formats, and a deployed system:

1. **Exact embedding theory (§3).** Doubled E2M1 values are integers in {0,±1,…,±12}; the paper defines q = 2v·2^(e_b−E) per element and proves (Prop. 1) that the whole row is an INT8 vector times one power-of-two scale iff the exponent spread s_n ≤ 3. With per-token symmetric INT8 activations, a linear layer becomes y = σ·2^(E−1)·Σ a·q — one INT8 GEMM, one rescale per output. SC-MXFP4 enforces the condition by squared-error exponent search (OCP rule ± two neighbors) followed by raising any exponent below max−3; Prop. 2 bounds the added error at 2^(E*−4) = 1/16 of the top block's worst case. The exponents are fixed before rounding, so **GPTQ composes** without changing format or kernels. §3.5 generalizes to any integer codebook × integer multipliers × per-row float scale with max C·max M ≤ 127; the standout instance is doubled-E2M1 elements, 16-wide blocks, m ∈ {1,…,10}, with per-row β chosen by search over 8 values per octave.
2. **M5 microbenchmarks (§2, Appendix B).** Minimal matmul2d kernels with small-integer inputs (products verified bit-exact across types) measure throughput: INT8×INT8 at 109–127 TOPS (1.79–1.98× FP16), every mixed float×integer combination at 0.68–0.96× FP16 — motivating integer weights AND integer activations.
3. **W4A8 kernels (§4) and evaluation (§5–7).** Weights are stored as a lossless re-layout of SC-MXFP4 (4-bit E2M1 codes + 2-bit block shifts + one FP32 row scale = 4.07 bits at K=4096); dense prefill unpacks each layer's weights once into an INT8 buffer; MoE prefill gathers INT8 activation rows in expert-sorted order and runs one grouped INT8 GEMM (switching to the two-pass scheme when experts average ≥256 rows); decode is a GEMV over the packed bytes. Accuracy is evaluated by fake quantization verified against the deployed kernels (≤0.004 perplexity difference; within 0.4% on the 125B model), on WikiText-2 perplexity, zero-shot lm-eval tasks, KL divergence from the FP8 reference, and full GSM8K (1319 problems, 5-shot, greedy, on each system's own kernels). Timing runs on one M5 Max (128 GB, AC power, High Power mode), interleaved, medians reported.

## Results & Highlights

- **The spread condition is essentially free and pre-satisfied:** 97.9–99.97% of rows comply across seven models (Table 12; e.g., 99.7% of Qwen3-1.7B, 99.97% of Qwen3.5-35B-A3B) and **99.93% of the 95.4M rows of the 125B Qwen3.8-Flash-Next**; enforcing it changes WikiText-2 perplexity by ≤0.007 and relative weight MSE by ≤0.01×10⁻³ on every model.
- **Integer-exact 16-block format vs NVFP4 on WikiText-2 (W4A8, Table 2):** Qwen3-1.7B 10.40 vs NVFP4 10.51 (standard scale rule) and 10.34 with scale search — within 0.06 perplexity while using 0.24 fewer bits/weight and running as one exact INT8 GEMM; on Qwen3-0.6B it beats even scale-searched NVFP4 (14.60 vs 14.63), and on all five small models it beats MLX's 4.5-bit affine INT4 with 16-bit activations (e.g., OLMoE-1B-7B: 7.85 vs 7.86, despite INT8 activations).
- **125B MoE, routed experts only (96% of parameters), INT8 activations:** our formats cost ≤0.2% perplexity vs the FP8 checkpoint (16-block: 4.664 vs FP8's 4.662); per-token **KL divergence from the FP8 model: 0.0557 for the 16-block format vs 0.0553 for NVFP4 with scale search** — statistically indistinguishable — vs 0.0628 for affine INT4 (oMLX's format) and 0.0940 for MLX's MXFP4.
- **GSM8K, every matrix quantized (all 1319 test problems, 5-shot):** the 16-block format solves **94.84±0.61% vs 90.98±0.79% for MLX's MXFP4 W4A16** (McNemar p<10⁻⁴; SC-MXFP4 92.65%); paired comparison shows INT8 activations alone change nothing measurable (p=0.87) while the weight quantizer does (SC-MXFP4 vs MLX MXFP4, p=0.010).
- **Prefill speed on M5 Max (Table 4, vs MLX MXFP4 in-session):** Qwen3-1.7B dense **1.34–1.74×** (2048 tokens: 75.4 ms vs 131.5 ms; also 2.32× llama.cpp Q4_0), OLMoE-1B-7B 1.20–1.50×, Qwen3.5-35B-A3B 1.10–1.43×, and the 125B Qwen3.8-Flash-Next **1.22–1.29×** (8192 tokens: 3943 ms vs 4799 ms) despite ~0.5 s of shared non-GEMM work (DeltaNet recurrence, attention, n-gram lookup) bounding the gain; isolated linear layers are 1.37–1.90× faster.
- **INT8 activations are cheap:** +0.07 to +0.36 perplexity over the same weights in W4A16 (+0.8% on Qwen3.5-35B-A3B), about the same as INT8 activations on unquantized weights — the two error sources do not compound.
- **Exactness verified at scale:** kernel WikiText-2 perplexity equals the fake-quantized reference (Qwen3.5-35B: 6.052 vs 6.051; 125B 8-window: 2.633 vs 2.638), and zero-shot means match MLX's W4A16 MXFP4 at equal coverage (Qwen3-1.7B: 61.9 vs 61.8; Qwen3.5-35B: 75.5 vs 75.7), with the 16-block format highest among measured 4-bit systems at 62.4.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** The exact row-level folding condition is a genuinely new, crisp theoretical observation — prior kernels (llama.cpp, vLLM AMD GEMV, ML-SpecQD) all apply block scales inside the reduction, and AdaMX's "90% of channels have exponent range ≤3" measurement is elevated to an iff-condition with a quantizer and error bound. However, W4A8 with integer group scales is well-trodden (QServe, DGQ, VS-Quant, IQ4_KS), and concurrent oMLX/scratchy already run INT8-activation prefill on M5 for affine INT4; the authors candidly admit their kernels are **not faster** than well-engineered per-group corrections (experts run at 0.76–0.94× oMLX's speed) — folding buys exactness and format compatibility, not speed.
- **Rigor:** High. Bit-identical output checks (0/298M packed weights differ), fake-quant vs kernel agreement within 0.004 perplexity, paired McNemar tests on GSM8K, interleaved timing with rejected disturbed runs, and a null model explaining the compliance statistics. Weak spots: GPTQ is calibrated on WikiText-2 only and the paper itself shows the overfitting (perplexity 10.99→10.52 but zero-shot mean 61.9→60.6, with ARC losses); the headline "within 0.06 of NVFP4" rests on their own NVFP4 implementation with a specific scale-search protocol; and rotations+GPTQ (MR-GPTQ's setting, the strongest known MXFP4 recipe) was **not tested** under the spread constraint — a real missing ablation given that rotations hurt round-to-nearest E2M1 in their measurements (SC-MXFP4 10.99→13.18 on Qwen3-1.7B).
- **Cherry-picking check:** Baselines are strong and mostly fair (NVFP4 given scale search; MLX given its own MXFP4; llama.cpp's fastest 4-bit type used for speed ratios), but two caveats deserve emphasis: llama.cpp's Q4_K_M (~5 bits/weight) is more accurate than all their formats under llama.cpp's own perplexity protocol, and SC-MXFP4 proper trails NVFP4 and MLX affine INT4 by 0.2–0.3 perplexity on three of six models — the accuracy crown belongs to the non-standard 16-block format, not the MXFP4-compatible one.
- **Reproducibility:** Good on paper (public code, public models, detailed appendices on measurement protocol), but everything is measured on **one M5 Max**; macOS 27's native FP8/FP4 matmul types are explicitly unmeasured, which could change the hardware premise.
- **Red flags:** The 125B model is quantized from its FP8 release, not BF16 (so "vs FP8" deltas understate loss vs the original model); decode is never improved (0.90–1.01× MLX; the 16-block format decodes at 0.65× on the 125B because its GEMV path was left unoptimized), so the practical system mixes kernel families (ours for prefill, MLX's for decode); and the headline GSM8K 94.8% figure requires the every-matrix-quantized configuration on a model most readers cannot verify independently.

## Strengths

- A clean, provable iff-condition that turns a heuristic observation (block exponents cluster) into an exact single-GEMM execution guarantee, with a quantizer whose damage is bounded at 1/16 of the row's worst-case rounding error.
- SC-MXFP4 files remain standard MXFP4 — drop-in compatibility with existing decoders (MLX's W4A16 GEMV reads the same bytes) is a deployment property most format papers sacrifice.
- The generalization to integer block multipliers is the best of both worlds: NVFP4-level accuracy at 4.26–4.28 bits with exact INT8 execution, demonstrated up to a 125B MoE with the strongest measured GSM8K accuracy (94.84%).
- Exceptional measurement hygiene: bit-exactness verification end-to-end, fake-quant/kernel cross-checks, paired significance tests, clock-regime-aware timing, and honest accounting of where speedups come from (e.g., the 226 ms prefill gap decomposed into 48×4.6 ms of expert GEMMs).
- Honest positioning against concurrent work, including admitting kernel-level inferiority to oMLX's register-fragment decoding on experts and identifying it as an adoptable optimization.

## Limitations

- **Decode gains are absent:** batch-1 decode is memory-bound (0.90–1.01× MLX; 36.3→42.4 tok/s on the 125B only by switching to MLX's GEMV kernels, giving up bit-identical decode); the 16-block format's decode penalty (0.65×) only breaks even for outputs ≤16–63 tokens after long prompts.
- **Single-device, single-OS-version evidence:** all numbers come from one M5 Max on macOS 26.6; native FP8/FP4 paths in macOS 27 are unmeasured, and portability to pre-Blackwell NVIDIA / AMD / Arm SME is argued but not demonstrated.
- **Missing ablation:** rotations combined with GPTQ (MR-GPTQ) under the spread constraint; round-to-nearest rotations actively hurt E2M1 formats here (10.99→13.18), so the interaction matters.
- **Accuracy trade-off of the compatible format:** SC-MXFP4 is 0.2–0.3 perplexity behind NVFP4/MLX INT4 on three of six models, and llama.cpp's ~5-bit Q4_K_M beats every format here in perplexity per bit of budget above 4.5.
- **Calibration-free main configuration:** round-to-nearest throughout; GPTQ composes but its narrow WikiText-2 calibration hurt downstream accuracy in their own experiment, and broader-corpus calibration was left untested.
- **Coverage caveats:** the 125B perplexity was evaluated layer-by-layer from the FP8 checkpoint (BF16 does not fit in 128 GB), and quantizing the dense projections (2–3% of parameters) triples KL divergence — a reminder that the 96%-of-parameters "expert-only" sweet spot is model-architecture-dependent.

## Relevance

对在 Apple Silicon / INT8 矩阵单元上部署 4-bit 量化 LLM（尤其 MoE）的直接相关：它解决了 W4A16 预填充无法使用 M5 最快 INT8 数据通路的痛点，且保持标准 MXFP4 文件兼容；对量化格式设计（FP4 块尺度、整数缩放 W4A8、GPTQ/旋转的组合）也提供了可证明的精确性视角。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the exponent-spread ≤3 condition hold for other architectures and modalities — vision transformers, linear-attention/state-space models beyond Gated DeltaNet, or models trained with quantization-aware training?
- Can the unoptimized 16-block decode GEMV be brought to parity (register-fragment decoding as in oMLX, or fused multiplier LUTs), removing the need to mix kernel families within one deployment?
- What happens on macOS 27's native FP8/FP4 matmul2d types — does the INT8-exact advantage persist, or does native FP4 hardware make folding obsolete?
- How do rotations + GPTQ (MR-GPTQ) interact with the spread constraint, given that round-to-nearest rotations hurt E2M1 formats by 2+ perplexity points here?
- Can the 1/16-error bound be tightened into an output-aware (Hessian-weighted) guarantee, and does enforcing the constraint per-row interact with outlier-channel preprocessing (smoothing, channel equalization)?
- Does per-token INT8 activation quantization remain "free" under long-context workloads with large activation outliers, and for KV-cache quantization composed with these weight formats?
