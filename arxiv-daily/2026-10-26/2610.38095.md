> **一句话总结：** NVIDIA 发布两款开源 MoE 混合 Mamba-Transformer 模型——多模态的 Nemotron 3.5 Super（120B 总参/12B 激活）与纯文本 Lightning（30B/3B）——通过 MTP 递归投机解码、1T token 蒸馏、Indic 持续预训练（印度语言基准最高 +9.82 分）、256K 智能体软件工程 mid-training 与多教师在线蒸馏（MOPD），让 Super 在 SWE-bench Pro 上以约 4.6× 更少的激活参数超越前代 Nemotron 3 Ultra（51.78% vs 49.66%），同时 NVFP4 量化仅损失 0.38% 精度（99.62% 恢复率）、单卡 GB200 达 4,856 tok/s/GPU（≥50 tok/s/用户，为 Qwen3.5-122B 的 2.34×）。

> **TL;DR:** NVIDIA's 79-page technical report releases two open hybrid Mamba-2/attention MoE models: Nemotron 3.5 Super (120B-A12B multimodal) and Lightning (30B-A3B text). On SWE-bench Pro (OpenCode harness), Super scores 51.78% vs 49.66% for the 4.6× larger-active Nemotron 3 Ultra (550B-A55B) — though DeepSeek V4 Flash-0731 reaches 72.00% on the same benchmark, and Super trails badly on Terminal-Bench 2.1 (60.67% vs 85.80% for Qwen3.8 Flash-Next). The engineering story is the joint optimization: Indic continued pretraining lifts Indic GSM8K by +9.82 pts (72.67→82.49), agentic mid-training lifts DeepSWE pass@1 from 6.4 to 10.4 under identical SFT, MTP boosting raises speculative-decoding acceptance length from 3.787 to 4.612 (+21.8%, SPEED-Bench, temp=1), and NVFP4 W4A4 quantization achieves 99.62% median accuracy recovery across 36 benchmarks while delivering 2.43× BF16 throughput (4,324 vs 1,778 output tok/s/GPU on GB200 at ≥50 tok/s/user). Base BF16, post-trained BF16, and NVFP4 checkpoints plus drafters (LiLiCorr, DFlash, DSpark), datasets, and recipes are all released.

## 核心贡献

- **两款针对智能体负载联合优化能力与效率的开源混合架构模型**：Super（120B 总参/12B 激活，多模态 VL，初始化自 Nemotron 3 Super）与 Lightning（30B/3B，纯文本，初始化自 Nano 3 Base），均为 Mamba-2 + 注意力混合骨干 + LatentMoE（Super 每 MoE 层 512 个路由专家），Super 配 C-RADIO 视觉编码器、上下文长 1M。
- **MTP 递归投机解码的系统化改进**：Super 把两个非共享 MTP 头合并为单一递归共享头，并用"隐状态混合"（训练时第 k 步从第 1..k-1 步产生的异质隐状态采样）弥合训练-推理条件失配；后训练后再做"MTP boosting"（冻结骨干、以骨干为教师的 LK 蒸馏损失），在 SPEED-Bench 上把概率草稿采样下的平均接受长度从 3.787 提到 4.612（+21.8%）。
- **Indic 持续预训练 + 2.83T token 合成语料**：用 Gemma-4-31B-it 经 NeMo Data Designer 四阶段流水线（翻译→质检→改写→复审）生成 22 种印度语言、34 种文字配置共约 2.83T token 语料（将对外开放）；100B token 的 Indic CPT 使全部 5 个印度语言基准提升 4.98–9.82 分且英文基准无回退。
- **65.4B token 的智能体软件工程 mid-training**（256K 上下文）：混合 GitHub issue-PR 对（41%）、整仓库（20%）、agent 轨迹（10%）等自然数据，在相同实验 SFT 下把 DeepSWE pass@1 从 6.4 提到 10.4，并在 5 个非 OpenHands harness 上平均 +8.6 分（SWE-bench Verified），显著提升跨 harness 鲁棒性。
- **完整后训练流水线与 MOPD**：两阶段 SFT（文本基座→VLM）→ 联合 RLVR+RLHF（含奥赛编程环境与金融 agent 环境）→ 五个专门教师（智能体、推理、视频推理与跟踪、视觉工具使用、对齐）→ 两阶段多教师在线蒸馏（MOPD，token 级反向 KL + 自锚定 + 近端教师平滑）。
- **NVFP4 量化与推理栈协同设计**：激进 W4A4 配方 + 量化感知蒸馏（QAD，200 步）在 36 个基准上中位精度恢复 99.62%、冗长度仅 +0.44%（整模型 4.82 BPE）；vLLM 侧配合前缀缓存-投机解码可组合的 Mamba 检查点、ReplaySSM 状态重放（+4.7–6.4% 吞吐）、按量化格式选 GEMM 内核，单卡 GB200 达 4,856 tok/s/GPU。
- **测试时扩展（并行 agent + 自验证）**：同一检查点并行采样 N 条轨迹后用 LLM-as-a-Verifier 概率枢轴锦标赛选择，Terminal-Bench 2.1 从 62.7% 升至 71.9%、DeepSWE 从 24.6% 升至 34.5%，无需额外训练。
- **基准去污染流水线**：n-gram + 嵌入双信号召回 + Claude Opus 4.6 判定，从后训练数据中删除 20,671 个样本（0.09%），匹配 30 个基准中的 22 个共 1,808 题（含翻成中文的 MATH-500、跨语言改数字的 RLHF 提示等隐蔽形态）。

## Key Contributions

- **Two open hybrid Mamba-Transformer MoE models targeting agentic workloads**: Super 3.5 (120B-A12B, multimodal, 1M context, C-RADIO vision encoder, LatentMoE with 512 routed experts per MoE layer) and Lightning 3.5 (30B-A3B, text-only), both with natively integrated recursive MTP speculative decoding.
- **MTP adaptation with hidden-state mixing**: consolidating two non-shared MTP heads into one recursively shared head (Super) and architecture-aware projection fusion + pruning to transplant an MTP head across form factors (Lightning: hidden 4096→2688, experts 512→128); mixing hidden states from steps 1..k-1 during training closes the train-inference conditioning mismatch that degrades acceptance at deeper draft positions, without the cost of full multi-step simulation.
- **1T-token cross-form-factor distillation**: Lightning from Super 3 Base (forward KL on top-128 logits + sink token, KL weight 0.9 + CE 0.1); Super from Ultra 3 Base (Skew Reverse KL, alpha=0.1).
- **Indic continued pretraining with a 2.83T-token synthetic corpus**: 22 languages / 34 script configurations generated by a 4-stage Gemma-4-31B-it pipeline (translate → score 0-100 → rewrite if <75 → re-score); pipeline optimizations (speculative decoding, FP8, FlashAttention, concurrent scheduling) cut median translation cost 60% (0.835→0.335 GPU-hours per million input tokens).
- **Agentic software-engineering mid-training**: 65.4B tokens at 256K context over natural SE data (issue-PR pairs 41%, whole repositories 20%, pretraining replay 20%, agent traces 10% with tool responses loss-masked, commit diffs 3%, long-context 5%, build/CI logs 1%), explicitly positioned between pretraining (short context) and SFT (mostly synthetic); every source decontaminated against SWE-bench and Terminal-Bench.
- **Comprehensive post-training with MOPD**: two-stage SFT turning a text-only base into a VLM; joint RLVR+RLHF with router replay + FP32 LM head (reducing train-inference KL error by >10x); five specialized teachers trained from a shared step-120 RL checkpoint; two-stage multi-teacher on-policy distillation (token-local reverse KL, importance-weighted stale rollouts masked outside [0.2, 5], self-anchoring for unowned domains, proximal-teacher smoothing alpha=0.2 in stage 2).
- **NVFP4 quantization with QAD + per-module precision science**: aggressive W4A4 recipe (NVFP4 MoE GEMMs, FP8 attention/Mamba projections, FP8 lm_head, FP8 KV cache) at 4.82 whole-model BPE; careful ablations show MoE expert precision drives verbosity inflation and runaway generation, lm_head FP4 perturbs the stop-vs-continue margin, and attention-score dilution hurts disambiguation (MRCR-128k).
- **Serving-stack co-design in vLLM**: Mamba checkpoint placement at speculative-decoding replay boundaries to make prefix caching and speculative decoding composable; ReplaySSM checkpointing-replay (reduces per-step Mamba state writes from T+1 to ~1+1/L); per-quantization GEMM backend selection; disaggregated prefill/decode serving on VR200 (+10.3% TPS/GPU, +61% TPS/user).
- **Test-time scaling via parallel agents + LAV self-verification** and a **benchmark decontamination pipeline** (dual-signal recall + LLM judge, validated on 10,000+ synthetic contaminations with 87.5%/79.1% recall).

## Methodology

The report is organized as four interleaved efforts (pretraining, post-training, quantization, inference), all measured against the same objective: interactive, long-horizon agentic serving at ≥50 output tokens/s/user.

**Pretraining.** Both models get a recursively shared MTP head warmed up for 100B tokens with the backbone frozen (Lightning's head transplanted from Nemotron 3 Super Base via projection fusion + width/expert pruning; Super's consolidated from its two original heads). Distillation then runs 1T tokens from the next-larger Nemotron form factor. Super additionally runs: (1) 100B tokens of Indic CPT (10% synthetic+natural Indic, 90% replay of the prior mixture, chosen from Lightning 10/20/30% mixture ablations at fixed 200B budget); (2) a 1M-context long-context phase (35B tokens, 16-way context parallelism on GB300); (3) 65.4B tokens of agentic SE mid-training at 256K (LR reset to 2x final pretraining LR); (4) a 9B-token long-context recovery stage, because 256K mid-training collapsed RULER-1M from 79.9 to 33.5.

**Post-training (Super).** Two-stage SFT builds vision, reasoning, chat, safety, long-context, and agentic skills (packed sequences to 524,288 tokens, global batch 32, constant LR 1e-5, MTP auxiliary loss 0.3). Data includes the Nemotron 3 Nano Omni vision corpus plus newly curated grounded-reasoning data (Qwen3.5-generated), 14,169 agentic-vision trajectories with 81,366 tool-calling actions (Kimi K2.6/K3), and reasoning traces regenerated with Kimi K3 / DeepSeek V4 Pro / GLM-5.2 (responses under 5,000 tokens filtered out). RL jointly applies RLVR and RLHF (global batch 8,192; 16 rollouts/sample; generation to 128K tokens), adding an Olympiad competitive-programming environment (2,847 training problems with executable evaluators) and a finance-agent environment (SEC filing search/parsing/retrieval aligned with the Vals.ai Finance Agent benchmark). Five teachers (agentic, reasoning, video+tracking, vision tool use, alignment) are branched from the step-120 RL checkpoint and consolidated back through two-stage MOPD on 128 GB200 nodes, where the student minimizes token-local reverse KL on its own rollouts against the domain-owning teacher, with a frozen self-anchor regularizing unowned domains and importance weighting for one-step-stale rollouts. A final MTP-boosting phase retrains only the MTP head (25k steps Super / 48k Lightning) with hidden-state mixing and a KD loss against the frozen backbone. Lightning gets a lighter single-stage text SFT + RLVR, no MOPD, no Indic CPT, no mid-training.

**Quantization.** PTQ via ModelOpt with MSE block-scale calibration on 1,024 multimodal samples, then QAD (KL-only, only quantized GEMM weights trainable, 200 iterations on the released recipe, ~1,024 GB200 GPU-hours per candidate). The released Super checkpoint is the aggressive recipe: NVFP4 W4A4 MoE + shared experts, FP8 attention projections and Mamba linear layers, FP8 lm_head (shared target/MTP), FP8 KV cache, FP32 Mamba conv cache, FP16+SR SSM cache — 4.82 BPE, 74.987 GB for 124.453B parameters. Sensitivity ablations attribute verbosity inflation to MoE expert precision and the stop-margin fragility to lm_head precision.

**Inference.** A 20-turn agentic proxy workload (51,200-token shared prefix, 2,048 in / 1,024 out per turn) defines "usable" points as ≥50 tokens/s/user, maximizing aggregate output throughput. Contributions land in vLLM: fine-grained Mamba checkpoints at speculative replay boundaries (avoiding up-to-L_page target replays), ReplaySSM (reconstruct Mamba state on demand from checkpointed k/v/alpha buffers, discovered concurrently with the Dao lab), per-quantization GEMM backend selection, internal prefill checkpointing for Mamba-2, and standalone parallel drafters (LiLiCorr for Super: 15 drafted slots, top-8 candidates per slot rescored by a correlation head; DFlash/DSpark for Lightning).

**Evaluation.** NeMo Gym harness with matched agentic resources (CPU, timeouts, tools, search APIs) across all seven compared models; SWE-bench variants averaged over 3 runs in OpenCode, Terminal-Bench 2.1 over 8 runs in Terminus 2 (3-hour timeout), tau-benches over 8 trials. Test-time scaling uses 5 candidates (Terminal-Bench, DeepSWE) or 3 (SWE-bench variants, BrowseComp-400 subset) with pairwise LAV verification aggregated via Bradley-Terry over a Probabilistic Pivot Tournament.

## Results & Highlights

- **SWE-bench Pro (OpenCode harness): Super reaches 51.78% vs 49.66% for Nemotron 3 Ultra (550B-A55B) — +2.12 pts with ~4.6× fewer active parameters (12B vs 55B)** — but the same table shows DeepSeek V4 Flash-0731 at 72.00% and Qwen3.8 Flash-Next at 60.37%, i.e., Super is mid-pack among frontier open models on its flagship benchmark. On SWE-bench Verified, Super's 71.10% trails GLM-5.3 Flash (79.10%) and DS-V4 (77.80%).
- **Terminal-Bench 2.1: 60.67% vs 85.80% for Qwen3.8 Flash-Next (−25.1 pts) and 82.70% for GLM-5.3 Flash** — despite agentic workloads being the stated design target; Super does beat N3-Ultra (54.78%) and Qwen3.5-122B (48.70%). Similarly, Tau3 Banking is 18.88% vs 73.79% for DS-V4 Flash, and Tau2 Telecom is 73.19% vs 90.35% (Qwen3.8) / 98.46% (GLM-5.3 Flash).
- **Indic CPT lifts all five Indic benchmarks with no English regressions: Indic GSM8K strict 72.67→82.49 (+9.82 pts), ARC-Challenge 84.17→92.83 (+8.66), MILU 72.48→77.90 (+5.42), MMLU-Pro 51.83→56.81 (+4.98)**, plus English→Indic translation chrF++ 35.43→47.47. Lightning mixture ablations (200B tokens) show the four-benchmark mean rising monotonically with Indic share: 63.1 / 64.6 / 65.6 at 10/20/30%.
- **Agentic mid-training (identical 33B-token experimental SFT on base vs mid-trained checkpoint): DeepSWE pass@1 6.4→10.4 (+4.0 pts), SWE-bench Verified under the Codex harness 48.4→61.8 (+13.4 pts), average +8.6 pts across five non-OpenHands harnesses on SWE-bench Verified** — but under the standard OpenHands harness, SWE-bench Multilingual (65.9→65.6) and Pro (52.1→51.7) moved within the stated ~1-pt run-to-run variation, i.e., the headline-benchmark gain is a harness-robustness effect, not a score bump on the default harness.
- **MTP boosting on SPEED-Bench (draft length 7): average acceptance length 4.287→4.718 greedy (+10.1%) and 3.787→4.612 under probabilistic draft sampling (+21.8%)**, with the largest per-position gains at deep draft positions where the train-inference mismatch is worst; the boosted head beats Qwen3.8 Flash-Next's drafter in every SPEED category.
- **NVFP4 quantization: 99.62% median accuracy recovery across 36 benchmarks vs BF16, verbosity +0.44% (Ratio of Totals)**; per-benchmark deltas mostly ≤1 pt (e.g., GPQA-D 89.52→89.90, HLE 31.79→31.44, Tau3 Banking 18.88→17.78, Terminal-Bench 60.67→62.78). Lightning W4A16: 99.24% median recovery, −3.63% verbosity.
- **Serving on the 20-turn agentic proxy (≥50 tok/s/user): NVFP4 delivers 4,324 vs 1,778 output tok/s/GPU for BF16 on GB200 (2.43×), and Super reaches 4,331.88 tok/s/GPU vs 1,854.35 for Qwen3.5-122B-A10B (2.34×) and 2,282.73 for Qwen3.8 Flash-Next (1.90×)**; peak 4,856 tok/s/GPU on a single GB200 with MTP + ReplaySSM (which itself adds +4.7–6.4% output throughput, +6.4–12.5% per-user rate).
- **Test-time scaling (parallel agents + LAV self-verification, same checkpoint): Terminal-Bench 2.1 62.7%→71.9% (+9.2 pts), DeepSWE 24.6%→34.5% (+9.9), BrowseComp subset 71.5%→79.8% (+8.3)**, recovering ~70% of the best-of-3 oracle gap on BrowseComp; gains on SWE-bench Verified/Pro/Multilingual are smaller (+1.1/+2.4/+1.9).
- **Decontamination: 20,671 post-training samples removed (~0.09%; 14,793 SFT + 5,878 RL), matching 1,808 benchmark questions (6.0% of 30-benchmark question store) across 22 benchmarks**, including translated MATH-500 problems in Chinese and cross-lingual RLHF prompt variants; judge recall validated at 87.5% (Terminal-Bench 2.1 variants) and 79.1% (GPQA-D variants) on 10,000+ synthetic contaminations with a single false-positive distractor.
- **Base-model quality after distillation**: Super base scores MMLU 84.14 / MMLU-Pro 76.25 / GPQA-Diamond 66.16 / MATH-500 81.56 (pass@1, n=32); Lightning base 78.63 / 67.50 / 33.33 / 79.53.

## Authors & Credibility

This is an official NVIDIA technical report with roughly 500 listed contributors (including well-known names such as Ashish Vaswani, Ilya Loshchilov, Mohammad Shoeybi, Bryan Catanzaro, Yejin Choi, Oleksii Kuchaiev, and Pavlo Molchanov). As a lab system report in the Nemotron lineage, it follows the established format of prior Nemotron 3 reports, with unusually strong disclosure practices: full training recipes (LR schedules, loss weights, batch sizes, parallelism layouts), per-stage ablations with identical-SFT controls, an explicit decontamination section with recall measurements, reproducibility links (NeMo Gym recipes, NeMo Evaluator SDK, vLLM/TensorRT-LLM/FlashInfer PR numbers), and release of base/post-trained/NVFP4 checkpoints, drafters, datasets, and cookbooks. Numbers are self-reported and not independently replicated, but multi-run averaging (3-8 repeats on agentic benchmarks), reported run-to-run variation (~1 pt), and preliminary-vs-final flags (e.g., Arena Elo for NVFP4) are honest touches. The companion papers for mid-training, Indic data, and ReplaySSM are cited separately.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: Individual ingredients (hybrid Mamba-Transformer, MTP, distillation, MOPD, RLVR, NVFP4) mostly follow the Nemotron 3 / DeepSeek-V3 lineage rather than break new ground; the genuine contribution is the *integration* and the systems work — hidden-state mixing for recursive MTP, the Mamba-checkpoint/prefix-cache/speculative-decoding composability fix in vLLM, ReplaySSM, and the per-module quantization sensitivity study (Appendix B is the most scientifically interesting part: attributing runaway generation to FP4 MoE expert error and the stop-margin fragility to lm_head precision, with the stop-vs-continue geometry invariant to RL post-training within 1%).
- **Rigor**: Generally high — identical-experimental-SFT controls for mid-training, MSE/Max/MSE-4-over-6 calibration ablations, QAD iteration sweeps, KV-cache precision ablations with t-statistics on long-context tasks, and a validated decontamination pipeline. Weak spots: the headline "surpassing Nemotron 3 Ultra" comparator is NVIDIA's own previous generation, while the same Table 10 shows DS-V4 Flash-0731 beating Super by 20+ points on SWE-bench Pro (72.00 vs 51.78) and Terminal-Bench (71.82 vs 60.67) — the abstract does not surface this; mid-training gains on the *default* OpenHands harness were within run-to-run variation, so the SWE-strengthening claim rests on non-default harnesses; and the throughput comparisons to Qwen models are on NVIDIA hardware with NVIDIA-tuned serving, where the hybrid-Mamba advantage in per-request state is largest.
- **Reproducibility**: Checkpoints, datasets, drafters, evaluation recipes, and vLLM PRs are released — among the most reproducible lab reports. But full retraining requires GB200/GB300 clusters (QAD alone: 512 GB200 GPUs), several teachers are internal (Kimi K2.6/K3-generated data, internal chat benchmark, the 400-problem BrowseComp subset used for test-time scaling), and many benchmark harnesses are "planned" open source. The Tail-Bench verbosity caveat (4.5% non-completing runaway trajectories at ~700k tokens each dominating means, excluded from the metric) is disclosed but illustrates residual quantization-induced termination fragility.
- **Significance**: High for practitioners — an open 12B-active model with 51.78 SWE-bench Pro, 1M context, 4.8k tok/s/GPU serving, and near-lossless 4-bit quantization is a strong deployable artifact, and the released 2.83T-token Indic corpus is a real community contribution.
- **Red flags**: (1) Tau3 Banking at 18.88% vs 73.79% for DS-V4 is a glaring weakness on conversational tool use, unexplained; (2) the 256K mid-training stage *broke* 1M context (RULER-1M 79.9→33.5) and needed a patch-up recovery stage (81.0) — pipeline stages demonstrably interfere; (3) self-verification for test-time scaling uses the same checkpoint as verifier, with no external verifier baseline; (4) the Indic translation pipeline uses Gemma-4-31B-it as both translator and judge, an acknowledged self-preference risk; (5) Lightning's Indic mixture ablations (10/20/30%) transfer conclusions to Super's 10% choice without Super-level validation, and 30% scored best, suggesting Super's 10% may be compute-limited rather than optimal.

## Strengths

- Genuine end-to-end co-design: architecture (hybrid Mamba + LatentMoE + MTP), data (Indic CPT, agentic mid-training), post-training (teachers + MOPD), quantization (W4A4 + QAD), and serving (prefix-cache-compatible speculative decoding, ReplaySSM) are each ablated and composed, with the deployment objective (≥50 tok/s/user interactive agentic serving) held constant throughout.
- Exceptional openness: base, BF16 post-trained, and NVFP4 checkpoints for both models; three standalone drafters (LiLiCorr, DFlash, DSpark); the 2.83T-token Indic corpus and pipeline; pretraining/post-training datasets; cookbooks and reproducibility tutorials.
- The quantization analysis goes beyond score-chasing to mechanism: per-module sensitivity attribution (lm_head ~half of NVFP4 sensitivity; Mamba SSM gate near-zero flush-to-zero under FP4 activations; attention-score dilution at 32k+ context on MRCR-128k disambiguation, t=-3.90 to -4.97) is exactly the kind of precision-failure forensics rarely published in system reports.
- Honest methodological notes: run-to-run variation quantified (~1 pt), harness-dependence of SWE scores explicitly measured across six harnesses (SWE-bench Pro ranges 43.0-49.45% depending on harness), decontamination recall limitations stated, and the ReplaySSM concurrent-discovery credit shared with the Dao lab.
- Test-time scaling without retraining (+9-10 pts on Terminal-Bench/DeepSWE) is a practical, immediately usable recipe with a well-specified verification protocol (bidirectional pairwise grading, 20-level probability-weighted scale, Bradley-Terry pivot tournament).

## Limitations

- Capability ceiling vs frontier open models: clearly behind DS-V4 Flash-0731, Qwen3.8 Flash-Next, and GLM-5.3 Flash on Terminal-Bench 2.1 (60.67 vs 71.82-85.80), tau-bench Telecom/Banking, BrowseComp (78.91 vs 89.02-98.25), GPQA-Diamond (47.13 vs 48.28-49.96), and HLE-no-tools (88.22 vs 94.13-94.64); the efficiency-first positioning means it wins on throughput-per-GPU, not on absolute capability.
- Stage interference: 256K mid-training collapsed 1M-context RULER (79.9→33.5), fixed only by an extra 9B-token recovery run; Indic CPT mixture ratio was chosen via smaller-model ablations; several evaluation harnesses and the NV Chat Benchmark remain internal or "planned" open source.
- The throughput comparisons (2.34×/1.90× vs Qwen3.5/Qwen3.8 Flash-Next) are measured on NVIDIA GB200 with vLLM configurations NVIDIA tuned, on a workload (multi-turn prefix reuse) chosen to favor hybrid-Mamba state economics; per-token quality-adjusted throughput at equal capability is not reported.
- Test-time-scaling results use a 400-problem internal BrowseComp subset (cost-driven), self-verification by the same checkpoint, and 3-5x inference cost; QAD benefits saturate by ~300 iterations and used 512 GB200 GPUs per candidate, an inaccessible compute budget for most labs.
- Lightning is trained and evaluated with a much lighter pipeline (no MOPD, no Indic CPT, no mid-training), so most capability claims technically apply only to Super.

## Relevance

作为前沿实验室的官方技术报告，直接命中"Model System Cards & Technical Reports"关注方向；同时其对 NVFP4/MoE 专家精度与 lm_head 精度导致冗长度膨胀、终止失败的逐模块归因（Appendix B），以及 ReplaySSM、前缀缓存-投机解码可组合性等推理栈正确性问题，与"Reliability under Extreme Performance Optimization"和推理可靠性方向高度相关——是激进低精度优化下精度-正确性权衡的难得一手实证。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How much of the hybrid-Mamba serving advantage (2.34× over Qwen3.5) survives on non-NVIDIA hardware or with equally tuned competing serving stacks, and what is the quality-adjusted (accuracy per GPU-hour) ranking from Figure 7's accuracy-compute frontier?
- Can the MoE-expert FP4 verbosity/termination failure be fixed at the source (e.g., the two-pass FP4 residual emulation that fully recovers the lm_head KL gap) rather than patched by QAD, and does the stop-vs-continue margin geometry result generalize to other MoE architectures?
- The 256K mid-training stage degraded 1M-context RULER from 79.9 to 33.5 — what data or objective property causes this interference, and can mid-training be made context-length-preserving without a recovery stage?
- Why is Tau3 Banking (18.88%) so weak relative to DS-V4 (73.79%) when other tool-use benchmarks are competitive — is it the user simulator, the BM25/grep retrieval tools, or a genuine capability gap in conversational banking tasks?
- Does LAV self-verification with an independent (stronger or specialized) verifier beat same-checkpoint verification, and how do the selection gains degrade as candidate-pool size grows beyond 5?
- The 30% Indic mixture beat 10% in Lightning ablations — would Super at 20-30% Indic share (larger budget) close further gaps, and what is the English-capacity cost curve?
