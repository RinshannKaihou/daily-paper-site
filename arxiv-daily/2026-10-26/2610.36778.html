Does LLM Pre-Training Typically Occur at the Edge of Stability?

EoS vanishes below a per-dataset batch-size threshold — and that threshold tracks the well-known critical batch size across five corpora (Pearson r = 0.95).

Why this matters

Maps where the descent-lemma worldview actually holds in modern pretraining — directly informing learning-rate schedules and batch-size choices.

Critical edge

The title's claim is extrapolated more than tested — models cap at 787M parameters, far from the 7B–100B+ scale "LLM pre-training" evokes.

＋ the 8,192-token vocab & 5.7% sequence filtering mean EoS/EoC were measured in a curated regime, not standard training.

01 · Methodology

  1. T1

    Theory on stochastic quadratics

    Mini-batch SGD on the local quadratic gives ε←(I−ηA)ε−ηr; mean-square stability ⇔ ρ(E[M⊗M]) < 1. Batch-Hessian covariance shifts the critical LR off the classic 2/λmax, and λmin < 0 is unstable at every η > 0.

  2. T2

    Definitions for networks & AdamW

    EoC: λmin < 0 with small saddleness ν = max{0,−λmin}/λmax. EoS: training η ≈ η*, the mean-square-stability threshold on the positive-curvature subspace. AdamW handled in preconditioned coordinates Ā = P−1/2HP−1/2 + λWDI; recovers Cohen et al. (2021) exactly in the full-batch case.

  3. T3

    Detection at scale

    λ̂min via stochastic power iteration (mini-batch Lanczos is biased low by concavity); η̂* via compensated local simulation — neutralize negative curvature with (λ/2)‖w−w₀‖², replay the same batch sequence over an LR grid, explosion at displacement 10⁵.

SETUP — 4 GPT sizes (43M / 104M / 344M / 787M) · 100+ AdamW pretrainings (β₁ 0.9, β₂ 0.95, WD 0.1, WSD: 4% warmup / 20% decay) on FineWeb + 4 corpora · 3,000+ checkpoints · 8,192-token vocab (rare tokens distort Adam's preconditioned curvature; 94.3% of detection-shard sequences kept).

  • LR decay exits EoS, sometimes permanently — at peak LR 0.002, none of the drop ratios 0.6 / 0.45 / 0.3 show any post-drop EoS checkpoint; during decay −λ̂min falls ~850 → 56–540 and saddleness 0.09 → ≤ 0.03.
  • Detector sanity check — SGD on ResNet-20/32 (CIFAR-10, MSE loss, batch 1024) puts the bounded→exploding boundary at LR factor 1.0: the classic EoS, recovered.

03 · Strengths

  • Two clean failure modes of the quadratic approximation — negative curvature (EoC) vs positive-curvature instability — with definitions that provably recover full-batch EoS as a special case.
  • Covariance, not λmax alone, governs stochastic EoS: the second-moment operator E[M⊗M] = E[M]⊗E[M] + Cov(M) closes a real gap in prior theory.
  • Detection that scales — power iteration + compensated local simulation make Hessian-level measurement feasible at 787M params and 3,000+ checkpoints.
  • EBZ ↔ CBZ (r = 0.95) is crisp, surprising, and practically useful — linking a stability phenomenon to the critical-batch-size scaling picture.
  • Honest measurement hygiene: Jensen-bias correction and rare-token distortion documented for future Hessian studies to copy.

04 · Limitations

  • Scale caps at 787M params / ~20 tokens-per-param; 7B+ and standard 50k-vocab tokenizers untested.
  • Curated measurement regime: 8,192-token vocab + 5.7% sequence filtering may not transfer to standard LLM configurations.
  • Heuristic load-bearing constants — explosion cutoff 10⁵, EBZ crossing at q = 1.1, CBZ within 0.3%, 25–50-step horizons; sensitivity explored only for the compensation coefficient.
  • Optimizer coverage thin: AdamW plus an SGD sanity check; Muon flagged as open; no downstream-task evals or loss-spike case studies.
  • EoC appears at every checkpoint — so far a measurement, not a regime that discriminates training behavior.

05 · Questions for further exploration

  1. Does the EBZ–CBZ correlation (r = 0.95) survive at 1B–10B scale, with standard 50k-vocab tokenizers and realistic data mixes — or is it partly an artifact of the curated 8k-vocab regime?
  2. Can compensated local simulation become an online stability monitor — predicting loss spikes before they happen, or timing LR decay exactly as the critical-LR factor q approaches 1?
  3. How do EoC and EoS behave under Muon-style orthogonal preconditioners or non-uniform per-block LR parameter groups, which the AGM framework may not cover?
  4. Is the persistent small negative curvature a finite-width phenomenon or a data property — does saddleness ν shrink toward zero as width grows?
  5. Since stochastic EoS is governed by mini-batch Hessian covariance, can gradient-noise-shape interventions (noise-scale estimation, curvature-aware sampling, SAM-style perturbation) deliberately raise the usable LR at a given batch size?
