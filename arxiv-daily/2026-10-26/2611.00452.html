Mean accuracy on 8–23-digit addition with filtering; 80.0% even at 23 digits. Direct pseudo-labeling scores 0% at every length beyond its 7-digit training range.

Also: Bash pipelines 83–94% vs ≤11% · MuSiQue 9B +17.1 pts (2–4-hop) · RL warm start 19.2% vs 0%

Why this matters

A tested recipe — and a map of where it fails — for training LLMs on their own synthetic data for code and multi-hop reasoning.

Critical edge

The “direct self-improvement” baseline is greedy pseudo-labeling — no self-consistency, no verifier filtering — so the headline deltas likely overstate the gap to the best direct method.

  • The filter never sees reference answers — only structure: parses as a pipeline, no boundary carry, answer grounded in the passages.
  • Tier 1 · controlled: 14.2M-param character Transformers (no positional encoding) — grid navigation, blockwise addition, max run length; difficulty expanded over schedules.
  • Tier 2 · realistic: LoRA Qwen3.5-2B/4B/9B on Bash pipelines (TLDR + NL2Bash, 119 commands) and MuSiQue 2–4-hop QA, vs direct self-improvement under identical parents, schedules, replay.
  • Tier 3 · RL warm start: short SFT on composed labels, then GRPO with binary outcome rewards — token-matched accounting against direct RL.
  • Theory: composed-label error ρₖ ≤ min{1, ϵₖ + ξₖ} (component + rule error); pseudo-label learning bound; proofs machine-checked in Lean 4.

Honest nuance (App. C.7): where the composition rule holds on test data, decomposed inference with the initial model still beats the trained model (Bash 94.9% vs 88.1%) — training partly distills away the decomposition benefit; the trained model wins where the rule fails (93.7% vs 23.2%) and needs no test-time decomposition.

Where it breaks

three failure modes, each isolated by a matching experiment
I

Unreliable components

Component accuracy gates everything: initial one-room accuracy of 79.9% → final 19.4% on 2–13 rooms, vs 99.4% → 80.9%. Weaker initial models plateau early.

II

Invalid composition rules

Boundary carries corrupt blockwise addition: 11.0% at 23 digits unfiltered. Structural filtering restores 80.0% — at the cost of coverage.

III

Supervision coverage

Closure limit: composing two runs can never label a longer one. Accuracy collapses to ~0 once runs exceed component lengths — filtering cannot fix this.

Strengths

  • Controlled experiments, real LLMs, and Lean 4-formalized theory all tell the same story — the bound ρ ≤ ϵ + ξ holds in every measured round.
  • Failure modes are separately diagnosable — component accuracy, rule validity, output-space closure — each with a matching remedy and experiment.
  • Honest negatives: filtering trades coverage for validity; gains shrink on held-out commands (88% → 46%); weaker models gain less.
  • Generalizes to longer compositions than trained on (6-command pipelines, 4-hop QA); practical, token-accounted RL warm-start recipe.

Limitations

  • Requires a supplied, valid-enough decomposition plus hand-crafted composition rule and filter per task — automatic discovery left open.
  • Closure limit means it extends reliability, not new capability; coverage gaps are structural, not fixable by more rounds or stricter filtering.
  • Gains hinge on initial component reliability — the 2B model's composed labels are only ~45–50% correct, capping improvement.
  • Narrow realistic scope: one-line Bash over 21 utilities, MuSiQue ≤ 4 hops, max 9B + LoRA; no math or code-editing benchmarks.

Questions for further exploration

  1. Can decompositions and rules be discovered automatically — and does ρ ≤ ϵ + ξ extend to learned rules (ξ > 0)?
  2. Would verification or self-consistency on the direct baseline close or invert the Bash / MuSiQue gap?
  3. Do the failure modes shrink at frontier scale, leaving coverage as the dominant bottleneck?
  4. Can coverage expand without introducing rule errors — more components, partial rules, exploratory RL data?
  5. Middle ground between training and test-time decomposition (88.1% vs 94.9% on Bash)?
  6. Does the RL warm start survive curriculum or reward-shaped direct RL, and transfer to verifiable math/code RL?
