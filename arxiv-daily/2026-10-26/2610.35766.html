Key finding · arXiv 2610.35766 · label-free monitoring put to a known-answer test

LeJEPA encoders abruptly forget learned factors mid-training — while the loss keeps falling and monitors score the broken runs higher.

Why this matters

Improving loss and metrics do not prove representations are intact — a direct warning for checkpoint selection in any long unlabeled run, including LLM pretraining.

Critical edge

External validity is the main weakness: one synthetic generator, a 0.7M-param encoder, and a reduced two-view recipe — possibly a pathology of the minimal recipe, not SSL at scale.

Methodology — the known-answer testbed
factors (z₁, z₂, E) → render view A / view B (same factors, fresh elements) → ViT-Nano encoder → SIGReg projector → loss ↓
every checkpoint: readability probe = held-out ridge R² on z₁, z₂, log E
  • Ground truth: PyCaloFlash calorimeter simulator — 4×64×64 shower images with three known generating factors (shape z₁, z₂; energy E). No augmentations: the two training views are independent renders sharing the same factors.
  • Training: ViT-Nano (0.7M params) with a reduced LeJEPA recipe (2 views instead of 8, λ=0.02, 256 SIGReg projections), AdamW, 300 epochs; MAE / ViT-Tiny / SimCLR controls. 147 runs total, 56 declines.
  • Decline := readability ≥0.07 below peak, never recovered; onset rule fixed once and applied unchanged to 16 later runs.
  • Audit: 11 label-free signals (21 variants) × 3 change-point detectors, thresholds calibrated on control runs; "timely" = alarm within 10 epochs of onset.
CREDIBILITY — single-author (Weizmann), building on own prior simulator work · code, configs & per-figure data on GitHub, all checkpoints on Zenodo · explicit LLM-usage disclosure.

Remaining signals: feature std, NE-Sum, κ (mixed), Self-Cluster, DSE. Verdicts measured at every post-onset checkpoint; 8 of 11 preferred the declining runs. Checkpoint selection by loss, RankMe, or LiDAR landed at/after onset in (nearly) all decline runs.
Zero-label fixes that eliminate the decline
  • Partially refreshing shape between the two views (ρ=0.9): 0/6 runs decline — even at 1,000 epochs (default recipe: 9/10 at 300).
  • MAE on identical images: 0/10 declines, every run ended at its peak.
decline survives: no weight decay (1/3) · constant LR (2/3) · SIGReg ×2.5 (3/10) · ViT-Tiny (1/2) · SimCLR never left initialization
Strengths what holds up
  • Turns an unanswerable question — "did my SSL run silently forget something?" — into a measurable one via known generating factors; the first within-run test of label-free monitoring.
  • Decisive, quantified negative result across 11 signals × 21 variants × 3 change-point methods, reporting both false negatives (best 9/26) and false positives.
  • Sharp mechanistic characterization: one shared factor combination lost (direction |cos| 0.996; CCA top-2 correlations >0.98 preserved) — not just a curve that went down.
  • Constructive takeaway: view decorrelation (ρ=0.9) prevents the failure at zero label cost; α-ReQ is the only monitored signal that moved the right way.
  • Unusually complete artifact release (code, per-figure data, checkpoints) and a transparent LLM-usage statement.
Limitations read with care
  • Single generator, single tiny (0.7M) encoder, one reduced two-view recipe — no evidence the decline occurs on natural images, at scale, or with the published eight-view recipe.
  • "Readability" is probe-relative: factors became harder for ridge/attention/MLP readouts to recover — not proof the information is destroyed.
  • Only three classical change-point detectors, thresholds calibrated on out-of-distribution controls, no nominal false-alarm guarantee — the detection question remains open.
  • Optimizer/architecture ablations use 2–3 seeds only, so "weight decay / constant LR did not remove the decline" is weakly powered.
  • Declining vs non-declining comparison groups are imbalanced; some SIGReg verdicts hinge on an arbitrary alignment epoch.
Questions for further exploration
Q1Does the decline appear with the published eight-view LeJEPA recipe, larger encoders, or natural images (where factors are only partially known)?
Q2Is the lost factor combination destroyed — or re-encoded in a form stronger (nonlinear, sparse-autoencoder-style) probes could recover?
Q3α-ReQ ranks correctly but detects late; the shape-redraw LiDAR detects early but ranks wrong. Can a cheap hybrid plus occasional small labeled probes give a practical within-run monitor?
Q4The shared decline direction was already hardest-to-read at epoch 0 and had the lowest cross-view SNR — delayed feature suppression, predictable from initialization statistics?
Q5For LLM pretraining: would change-point detection on hidden-state effective rank or α-ReQ-style spectra catch quiet, single-direction representation losses that loss curves mask?
