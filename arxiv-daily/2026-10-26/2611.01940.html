  • One recurrence, four operators. DeltaNet, GDN, KDA, GDN2 share the same gated state recurrence; chunkwise training (n=64) writes each chunk as MR = Z − ES, chunks joined by a serial state scan.
  • The cancellation (Thm 3.1). The conventional WY backward materializes adjoints W̄ = −R̄ST and Ā, then pushes through the inverse (−ATĀAT) — 7 local matmuls, two building adjoints nothing downstream consumes. One-shot differentiation proves Ā = R̄(Z−ES)T, so the auxiliary adjoints cancel exactly and 3 products cover all local gradients.
  • Two independent reuse transforms. Matrix intra: centered contractions for vector gates, exponentials bounded by exp(Δ/2), validated pairwise fallback. Gram elimination: scalar-gate gradients recovered from existing key-gradient contractions, deleting the dense KKT product.
  • Device-specific kernels. Joint vs separate producer–consumer schedules per device across RTX 5070Ti / A800 / H100 / H200 / Ascend 910C / 950DT; FP32 working matrices preserved.

  • Ablations (each vs its own reference): ReWY alone 1.05–1.19×; matrix intra alone up to 2.04× (GDN2, 950DT); Gram elimination alone 1.02–1.07×; paired conditional gains from adding ReWY 1.05–1.29×.

  • Numerics: initial full-network gradient diffs 1.62–1.64% (KDA); after 46 updates, parameter diffs 0.17–0.20%, loss diffs ≤ 0.22%.

03Strengths

  • Algebraic elimination before scheduling — speedup comes from a smaller derivative graph, not more aggressive fusion; composable with future kernel work.
  • Every gradient validated (not just outputs) vs CPU FP64, including tail chunks, zero/tiny beta, strong decay, wide gates, nonzero state boundaries, graph replay.
  • Exceptional measurement hygiene: matched saving policies, ABBA-interleaved warmed timing (60 obs / GPU config), paired ablations that refuse to multiply standalone ratios.
  • Hardware breadth rare in academic kernel papers: SM80/SM90/SM120 NVIDIA plus Ascend 910C and 950DT with matched policies.
  • Numerical-safety engineering: provable exp(Δ/2) magnitude bound via midpoint centering; validated pairwise fallbacks for extreme gate ranges.

04Limitations

  • No training-convergence or downstream evidence — all claims rest on latency plus gradient equivalence on synthetic, randomly-initialized inputs.
  • Full-model gains modest and operator-dependent: +0.28% (GDN/A800) to +10.31% (KDA/A800); minimum per-shape ratios sag to 1.05×.
  • Single timed baseline: frozen FLA only — FlashQLA's improved Hopper path and cuLA are discussed but never benchmarked.
  • Dagger-heavy extremes: GDN2 2.15× (RTX) and 3.00× (910C) are historical BF16-cast records; GDN2 evidence overall is the least battle-tested (port, recent operator).
  • One fixed schedule per device across all six shapes; shape-adaptive dispatch unexplored; 950DT has no full-model or memory measurements.
  • Reordered reductions break bitwise reproduction; no general floating-point stability theorem for the triangular system's condition number.

05Questions for further exploration

  1. How much of the gain survives against FlashQLA's Hopper path that already combines the residual right-hand side — the one competitor inspected but never timed?
  2. Would shape- or head-count-adaptive dispatch recover more of the theoretical 42.9% where measured ratios sag to 1.05×?
  3. Does "differentiate the triangular system once" extend to Mamba-2/SSM state scans, RWKV-style decay, or the forward pass's own inverse computation?
  1. What are the long-run training dynamics under reordered FP reductions, given initial gradient differences of 1.62–1.64% and no multi-billion-token runs?
  2. Can the shared sensitivity P = ATR̄ live in one persistent fused kernel on Blackwell tensor cores, eliminating the device-memory round trip?
  3. Does Gram elimination generalize to rotary-attention or other kernels where a Gram-like matrix is formed but only reduced?
