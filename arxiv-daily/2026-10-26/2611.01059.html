Why this matters

Self-improving agent loops look stable on accuracy while quietly drifting confidence — and picking training data by model confidence can accelerate the drift.

Methodology

the loop, the two properties it separates, and the zero-gradient mechanism
Mg→ 3,000 self-answers
TriviaQA + NQ-Open · T=1.0 · top-p 0.95→ LoRA r16 fine-tune
2 epochs · merge adapter→ Mg+1 × 5 rounds → M0…M5, all evaluated on the same 600 English Global-MMLU items (no overlap with the training pool)
Temperature
Overall confidence level, measured as drift from a calibrated starting point (T₀ = 10.5 fitted once at g0, cross-fitted). Repairable with a single scalar.
Discrimination
Whether higher confidence tracks correctness — AUROC on exact, position-debiased option-letter log-probabilities, kept at full precision (stored as probabilities, 283/600 items saturate at ≥0.999999 and would fake ties).
  • Base Qwen3-1.7B (reasoning off), pathologically overconfident: mean confidence 0.963 vs accuracy 0.573 — so drift from calibrated start is the only usable calibration metric.
  • Three seeds per arm; a promotion rule (g5 AUROC ≥ +0.03, accuracy within 0.01) was written into the experiment scripts before any screened remedy ran.
  • Mechanism, zero-gradient argument: training on exact samples of the model's own distribution has zero expected gradient — sharpening is a loop-design artifact, traced to fitting each answer twice (larger share) plus top-p truncation.

Strengths

what the paper gets right
  • A load-bearing conceptual split — temperature vs discrimination — with a measurement built for it: calibrate first, then measure drift; exact log-probs so AUROC ties cannot fake gains.
  • The zero-gradient argument plus its confirming ablation converts "self-training sharpens confidence" from mystery into two concrete, zero-cost design choices.
  • Directly actionable: single-pass training and TCE hold calibration for free; focal loss is the only tested way to improve ranking; entropy matching recovers temperature without labels to within 0.010 of oracle, including at 8B.
  • Reframes a standard defence: reference mixing looks best on raw ECE (−0.072) but is the biggest temperature overshoot (−0.190).
  • Exceptional transparency — pre-written promotion rules, single-seed arms demoted to warnings, a failed original hypothesis and an invalidated Bangla extension both disclosed.

Limitations

read before acting on the numbers
  • One model family (Qwen3-1.7B for everything; 8B only for the temperature diagnosis), one 600-item eval set, confidence read only from multiple-choice option letters.
  • Small absolute effects — AUROC differences 0.02–0.04, drifts 0.02–0.13 — resting on three seeds with uncorrected p-values across many comparisons; several mechanism arms are single-seed.
  • The focal-loss ranking gain is confounded with training less: only +0.022 over the step-matched half-data control (p=0.127).
  • Pure self-training with no external learning signal — cannot show how remedies interact with a real improvement signal such as a correctness filter.
  • The base model's pathological overconfidence (T₀ = 10.5) may be exactly why drift-from-calibrated-start works here; a near-calibrated base could behave very differently.
  • The two-property framing emerged from the data after the pre-registered hypothesis held in only one of three seeds — a forking-paths risk only partially mitigated by the pre-written screen.

Questions for further exploration

what this paper cannot yet answer
  1. Does the temperature/discrimination decomposition hold under full fine-tuning (vs LoRA rank 16), larger models, and better-calibrated starting points — or is the drift pattern specific to a pathologically overconfident base (T₀ = 10.5)?
  2. Would entropy-aware truncation (top-H) inside the loop remove truncation-driven sharpening without the underconfidence overshoot the authors' entropy-matched sampler showed (−0.082, single seed)?
  3. How do these remedies interact with a genuine improvement signal — correctness filtering or external verification — rather than pure self-training on unverified answers?
  4. Does option-letter confidence drift track free-form or verbalized confidence in open-ended generation, where deployed users actually read the model's certainty?
  5. Does confidence-based self-verification accelerating overconfidence (+0.046 drift vs −0.003 for its random-half control) replicate — given it contradicts collapse-prevention guarantees that assume a fixed base-model scorer?

Provenance: three authors, BRAC University, Dhaka — a small-budget study (~40 GPU-hours on one 16 GB consumer GPU; 8B diagnosis ~10 h on a rented 24 GB card). Code and experiment scripts at an anonymous.4open.science repository; exact software versions, fixed item IDs, and stored log-probabilities released. Welch t-tests on n=3 seeds, uncorrected.
