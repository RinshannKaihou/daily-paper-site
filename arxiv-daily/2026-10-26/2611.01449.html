Why this matters

Anyone building trustworthy AI scientists can borrow this directly: reporting decisions made by deterministic code on frozen, pre-specified plans — not LLM judgment.

对任何构建可信 AI 科学家 / 自动化实验管道（含神经影像之外的基因组学、流行病学）的人，是直接可借鉴的治理范式与基准设计参考。

Critical edge

Full withholds 59% of literature-supported claims, and the headline gap is dominated by one failure mode — 42 of 150 negative controls are site-confounded.

Ground truth is small & partly LLM-adjudicated (49/96 labels provisional) · no stated public code / benchmark release · baselines were adapted re-implementations.

Methodology方法

  1. 1Plan & freezeLLM translates the question into C=(θ, D_disc, D_rep, Z, M₀, G); schema-checked, hash-locked before any outcome data are read.
  2. 2Execute deterministicallyFrozen scripts emit an analysis record E(C) — estimates, multiverse refits, power, replication stats. Any number in the LLM's prose not matching E(C) is discarded.
  3. 3Gate & reportCode maps (C, E) to pass/fail; only the Full standard may say "confirmed". Withheld claims get failure diagnoses, not silence.
G1 · MultiplicityBonferroni over max{M₀, history, observed}; BH q-values brain-wide
G2 · Confoundssite, scanner, field strength, software version
G3 · Powerprospective; reference d = 0.3 at 80% power
G4 · Stability24-fork multiverse (6 brain-wide); ≥60% consistent
G5 · Replicationsame sign, p<0.05 in every replication cohort
Follow-up loopbounded R×K revisions; validator forbids changing question, predictor, direction, datasets, thresholds; cumulative multiplicity + holdout re-test

Standards: Screen (G1–2) → Replicate (G1,2,5) → Full (all five). Benchmark: NeuroClaimBench — 489 executable claims (ADNI, OASIS3, UK Biobank, ABIDE, ABCD, BSNIP…) incl. 150 flawed negative controls in 5 failure modes.

  • Where baselines fail: they accept 79–100% of the 42 site-confounded controls — even when the confound evidence is handed to the LLM judge. CONFIRM's confound gate rejects all of them, at every standard.
  • Recovery trade-off: Screen recovers 61% of literature-supported scalar claims at 3% control false support; Full recovers 48% at 0% — a Pareto frontier against every baseline.
  • Every gate earns its keep: removing the confound gate → +24 pts control false support; removing replication → +26 pts literature false support. Across all threshold variations, Full never accepts a control.
  • Withholding is informative: of the 59% of literature-supported claims Full withholds, 87% fail replication in the designated independent cohort.
  • Governance speeds the agent up: gate diagnoses yield 70 supported revisions vs 54 for Self-Refine, on 653 vs 1,301 LLM calls, with ~2× holdout support (17% vs 9%).

Strengths

  • Attacks the correct layer: not "did the code run" but "may this finding be reported" — the decision agentic-science systems silently delegate to LLMs.
  • Deterministic, auditable gates close the garden-of-forking-paths loophole in iterative agent loops, while keeping the LLM where it helps (plan drafting, bounded revision, prose).
  • Negative-control benchmark scores reporting decisions without ground truth for new hypotheses — and transfers to genomics, epidemiology, econometrics.
  • Unusually honest evaluation: identical evidence for all methods, CIs, ablations, sensitivity analyses, holdout audits, retrofit on an existing agent.

Limitations

  • Full recovers only 48% / 41% of literature-supported claims; Screen accepts 40% of literature-unsupported ones — pick your poison.
  • Labeled sets are small (N=70 literature, 46 scalar), labels LLM-adjudicated before expert review — circularity risk.
  • Gates audit measured confounds only; medication, SES, comorbidities pass untouched. Passing all five gates "does not establish biological truth".
  • Observational neuroimaging, frequentist, direction-assuming; each new domain needs hand-translated gates.
  • No stated public release of code, frozen plans, or benchmark partitions; d=0.3 default and a 3-member nonreplicating family leave two gates under-evidenced.

Questions for further exploration

  • Would a tool-augmented, multi-step LLM judge close the 29%-vs-3% false-support gap — or is LLM judgment on site confounds structurally unreliable?
  • Can the gates extend to unmeasured / latent confounds (sensitivity analyses, negative-control outcomes, causal discovery)?
  • Is the 59% withholding rate a property of CONFIRM's thresholds, or of these specific cohort partitions?
  • Does the frozen-plan + holdout protocol transfer to sequential / experimental settings where plans can't be fully pre-specified?
  • Do weaker plan-drafting LLMs silently reduce scientific throughput under this governance?
  • Could the designed-negative-control methodology be replicated cheaply in single-cell genomics or observational economics?
