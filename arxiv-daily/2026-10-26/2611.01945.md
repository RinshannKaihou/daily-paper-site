> **一句话总结：** 本文提出"锚定事实回忆"（anchored factual recall）任务，通过对 Llama-3.1-8B、Qwen2.5-7B、Ministral-8B、DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B 四个开源模型在 TempLAMA 上做因果干预（注意力敲除、激活修补、跨折消融、值注入），发现时间锚点经两条并行通路整合——主体介导通路（锚点在早-中层将主体末 token 状态解析为对应年份的实体特异状态，关系决定哪些注意力头读出该状态）与直接通路（末位置从中层起直接读锚点，上层 MLP 将其转化为对正确对象的偏好），两条通路贡献近似可加（GS+GL 与 Gboth 偏差 ≤5-7%），且平衡随关系类型系统性变化（P39 关系主体通路占 64-75%，P54 仅 20-46%）。

> **TL;DR:** This MBZUAI mechanistic-interpretability paper asks how an LM uses a temporal anchor ("In 2010, Cristiano Ronaldo played for __") to select among competing facts. Using attention knockout, activation patching, cross-fitted head ablation, and value injection on four open-weight 7-8B models (Llama-3.1-8B, Qwen2.5-7B, Ministral-8B, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B) over the TempLAMA benchmark, it identifies two parallel, approximately additive routes: a subject-mediated route (the anchor resolves the final subject-token state to a year-specific, entity-specific representation — a shared-year-offset control reproduces at most ~26% of its effect — and the relation redistributes its readout across attention heads) and a direct route (the final position attends to the anchor; upper-layer final-position MLPs convert it into the answer preference, R_MLP = 0.37-0.49 vs R_non-MLP ≤ 0.01). Ablating relation-sensitive heads flips the top-1 prediction on 52-67% of prompts vs 2-5% for time-sensitive heads, and route balance is query-dependent but consistent across models (subject-route share 64-75% for relation P39 vs 20-46% for P54).

## 核心贡献

- **提出"锚定事实回忆"（anchored factual recall）任务框架**：将事实表示为四元组 (anchor, subject, relation, object)，明确区分"检索单一存储对象"与"在多个上下文依赖的候选对象中选择"，并针对选择机制提出三个问题：锚点在哪进入计算（Q1）、改变了什么（Q2）、如何转化为对正确对象的偏好（Q3）。
- **发现锚点进入计算的两条通路（Q1）**：对 S←A、R←A、L←A 三条注意力边做窗口化敲除，四个模型一致显示：主体跨度在早-中层读锚点（主体介导通路），末位置从中层起直接读锚点（直接通路），而关系跨度读锚点的因果效应很小。
- **证明锚点将主体状态解析为年份特异、实体特异的表征（Q2）**：将参照年份的主体末 token 状态修补进对照运行，会选择性提高正确对象 c 的 logit、降低对照对象 c̄ 的 logit、基本不影响其他年份候选；共享年份偏移控制实验表明该效应最多只有约 1/4（21%/25%/26%/0%）可由实体无关的通用年份向量解释。
- **阐明两条通路的实现机制（Q3）**：主体介导通路上，关系（而非锚点）决定哪些注意力头读出主体状态——关系交换在每一层引起的头级读出重分布（TV 距离）都远大于时间交换；跨折消融显示消融关系敏感头的末主体贡献使 52-67% 的预测翻转（时间敏感头仅 2-5%，贡献匹配随机头 8-12%）。直接通路上，值注入实验表明锚点信号的恢复几乎全部经由末位置 MLP（R_MLP = 0.37-0.49，R_non-MLP ≤ 0.01），DLA 显示该偏好在上层一带才写入。
- **刻画两通路的组合方式**：两条通路是并行信道，贡献近似可加（Llama/Qwen/Ministral 中 GS+GL 与 Gboth 偏差 ≤5%，Bonferroni 校正后不显著；DeepSeek 低 7%，显著），73-82% 的实例两条通路均有正贡献；通路平衡随关系类型变化且跨模型一致（六对模型间 GS−GL 的 Spearman ρ = 0.54-0.82），削弱一条通路不会增强另一条。

## Key Contributions

- **Formulates anchored factual recall**: facts as quadruples (anchor, subject, relation, object), where the model must select among competing context-dependent objects rather than retrieve a single stored one — a reframing of the standard (Geva et al., 2023) three-stage account of factual recall, with three new questions (where the anchor enters, what it changes, how the change selects the answer).
- **Localizes two anchor entry points via attention knockout**: across Llama-3.1-8B, Qwen2.5-7B, Ministral-8B, and DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B on TempLAMA, blocking subject←anchor attention degrades prediction in early-to-middle layers, final-position←anchor from the middle layers onward, while relation←anchor costs little — a consistent layer-wise ordering across all four models.
- **Shows the anchor resolves the subject state selectively and entity-specifically**: patching the reference-year final-subject-token state into the contrast run raises the reference object's logit, lowers the contrast object's, and leaves other temporal candidates largely unchanged; a shared-year-offset control reproduces at most ~a quarter of the effect (21%, 25%, 26%, 0% across models), ruling out a generic year representation.
- **Identifies the mechanisms of both routes**: on the subject-mediated route, the relation (not the anchor) redistributes head-wise readout of the time-conditioned subject state (relation swap beats time swap in TV distance at every layer), and cross-fitted ablation shows relation-sensitive heads are causal (top-1 flip rates ~63%/52%/52%/67% vs 2-5% for time-sensitive heads and 8-12% for contribution-matched random heads). On the direct route, value injection with MLP pinning shows final-position upper-layer MLPs carry nearly all of the anchor signal's effect (R_MLP = 0.37-0.49, R_non-MLP ≤ 0.01), with DLA turning positive only in upper layers.
- **Establishes that the routes are parallel, additive, and query-balanced**: G_S + G_L is within 5% of G_both in three models (7% below, significant, in DeepSeek); 73-82% of instances with any positive gain use both routes; the route balance correlates strongly across model pairs (Spearman ρ = 0.54-0.82) and is driven by the relation (subject-route share 64-75% for position held P39 vs 20-46% for member of sports team P54).

## Methodology

The authors study anchored factual recall in the temporal case: English anchor-first queries "In [year], [subject] [relation]." with 5 in-context demonstrations that share no year, subject, or relation with the query. Data is the TempLAMA test set (34,962 instances; Wikidata-derived, years 2010-2020); analysis is restricted to subject-relation groups whose gold object changes across years and that the model answers correctly for at least two years with differing objects. Four open-weight 7-8B models were benchmarked and the four highest-scoring retained: Llama-3.1-8B, Qwen2.5-7B, Ministral-8B, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B.

The core device is the reference-contrast pair: two prompts differing only in the anchor year, both answered correctly under shared demonstrations. All interventions apply to the query segment only:

1. **Q1 (entry points)**: attention knockout on edges S←A, R←A, L←A over centered 9-layer windows, measuring the change Δp in reference-token probability (following Geva et al., 2023).
2. **Q2 (what changes)**: activation patching (Meng et al., 2022) of the final subject-token residual state between reference and contrast runs, tracking logits of three candidate classes (reference object c, contrast object c̄, mean of other temporal candidates); plus a shared-year-offset control that adds an entity-independent year vector estimated cross-fold from other subjects.
3. **Q3, subject-mediated route**: (i) per-layer total-variation distance between head-normalized last-subject attention distributions under relation swap vs time swap (length-matched relation replacements keep positions aligned); (ii) cross-fitted ablation — relation-sensitivity scores computed on 4 discovery folds, top 10% of layer-head pairs ablated (their last-subject→final-position contribution c = α W_O V(s*) subtracted during the forward pass) on the held-out fold, with time-sensitive and contribution-matched random heads as controls, 2,000-resample cluster bootstrap for 95% CIs.
4. **Q3, direct route**: value injection (transfer the reference-minus-contrast anchor-derived attention contribution at L across all layers into the contrast run) combined with MLP pinning (fix all final-position MLP outputs to contrast-run values) to decompose recovery into MLP-mediated vs non-MLP parts; direct logit attribution projects final-position MLP outputs onto the reference-vs-contrast unembedding direction per layer.
5. **Route combination**: block attention to the anchor from all positions except chosen ones (both / subject-only / direct-only / none), compute logit margins of the correct object over a competing year's object, and test additivity G_S + G_L = G_both, plus per-instance route-share correlations across models.

All experiments ran on a single NVIDIA A40 GPU (48GB); interventions cost ~150x the forward passes of ordinary prediction per example.

## Results & Highlights

- **Relation-sensitive heads are causal for the prediction (subject-mediated route)**: ablating the last-subject contribution of cross-fitted relation-sensitive layer-head pairs flips the originally correct top-1 next-token prediction in ~63% of Llama prompts, 52% of Qwen, 52% of Ministral, and 67% of DeepSeek — versus only 2-5% for time-sensitive heads and 8-12% for contribution-matched random heads; the paired difference over matched random controls is positive in every model.
- **The direct route's effect is almost entirely MLP-mediated**: injecting the anchor-derived attention signal at the final position recovers R_MLP = 0.37-0.49 of the reference-contrast logit margin across the four models, while the non-MLP component is ≤ 0.01 — but total recovery stays under half of the margin (R_total ≤ 0.49), so neither route alone determines the answer.
- **The subject state is entity-specific, not a generic year code**: at the peak layer, the true reference-year subject state recovers 0.37/0.30/0.38/0.24 of the c-vs-c̄ logit difference (Llama/Qwen/Ministral/DeepSeek), whereas a shared year offset estimated from other subjects recovers only 0.08/0.08/0.10/-0.01 — i.e., at most ~26% (21%, 25%, 26%, 0%) of the true effect, a significant difference in every model.
- **The two routes combine additively and are jointly used**: G_S + G_L is within 5% of G_both in Llama, Qwen, and Ministral (not significant after Bonferroni correction; uncorrected p = 0.049/0.39/0.10) and 7% below in DeepSeek (p < 0.001); together the two routes account for G_both/G_full = 0.70/0.79/0.90/0.69 of the anchor's total effect; 73-82% of instances with a positive gain from at least one route have positive gains from both (4-9 pp above what independent route usage would predict).
- **Route balance is query-dependent but consistent across models**: the subject-route share G_S/(G_S+G_L) is 64-75% across all four models for the relation position held (P39) but only 20-46% for member of sports team (P54) (difference 0.29-0.44, all p < 0.001); correspondingly, subject-state patching raises c's logit by 0.59-0.77 of the reference-contrast difference for P39 vs 0.29-0.50 for P54, while value injection shows the reverse (R_MLP = 0.43-0.68 for P54 vs 0.16-0.23 for P39). Cross-model agreement of the per-group route balance is high: Spearman ρ = 0.54-0.82 over all six model pairs; per-instance shares are negatively correlated (ρ = -0.42 to -0.62, p < 10^-6), reflecting a roughly fixed total split; weakening the subject route does not strengthen the direct route (post-control changes -0.015 to +0.010, CIs spanning zero).

## Authors & Credibility

The paper is by Ayana Niwa, Masahiro Kaneko, and Kentaro Inui at the Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI). Kaneko and Inui are established NLP researchers (Inui is a professor at MBZUAIC/Tohoku with a long record in NLP and interpretability-adjacent work; Niwa et al., 2025 on factual recall is cited as the first author's prior work). The methodology closely follows the well-established causal-intervention lineage (Geva et al., 2023; Meng et al., 2022; Wang et al., 2023) and is unusually careful on the statistics: cross-fitted head selection across five folds, contribution-matched random controls, time-sensitive head controls, Bonferroni corrections, and cluster bootstraps over subject-relation groups for 95% CIs. Models and dataset are open (TempLAMA is public; all four models are open-weight), and the compute footprint is modest (one 48GB A40). No code release is mentioned in the paper.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: Genuine and well-scoped. The reframing from "retrieve one object" to "select among competing context-dependent objects" is a real conceptual contribution over Geva et al. (2023), and the two-route decomposition with an additivity analysis goes beyond Park et al. (2025)'s localization of temporal attention heads. That said, each individual tool (knockout, patching, DLA) is standard; the novelty is in the question and the combination, not the technique.
- **Rigor**: High for the genre — reference-contrast pairs differing only in the year, cross-fitted selection with held-out evaluation, contribution-matched random heads (which expose how misleading raw ablation effects can be: random heads flip 8-12% vs 52-67% for selected heads), the shared-year-offset control, and multiplicity correction. One caveat: the DeepSeek model significantly violates additivity (G_S+G_L 7% below G_both, p < 0.001), and the headline "approximately additive" leans on the other three models.
- **Reproducibility**: Models and TempLAMA are public, prompts and layer windows are specified in unusual detail (Appendix A.2), and 5-shot sampling is fixed-seed. But no code repository is referenced, and the instance-filtering pipeline (restricting to groups the model answers correctly for ≥2 differing-object years) has many degrees of freedom that would need re-derivation; the ~150x forward-pass cost per example also makes independent replication non-trivial.
- **Significance**: As a mechanism paper its reach is deliberately narrow: it does not claim to improve factual accuracy or editing, and the practical payoff (e.g., for model editing or knowledge updating in temporal QA) is speculative. The finding that R_total ≤ 0.49 and G_both/G_full = 0.69-0.90 also means a non-trivial fraction of the anchor's effect flows through positions other than the subject and final position — the two-route story is incomplete by the paper's own measurement.
- **Red flags / caveats**: (i) Selection bias — analysis is restricted to correctly-answered, cleanly time-varying instances, so nothing is learned about how models fail or muddle temporal conflicts; (ii) single dataset, single prompt template, English only, year-only anchors (2010-2020), acknowledged by the authors; (iii) the anchor-before-subject order is baked in by causal attention, so the subject-mediated route partially reflects the word order rather than a general mechanism — the authors say testing other orders "needs a language in which both orders are natural"; (iv) all models are 7-8B, so scale generality is untested; (v) the relation-swap analysis uses hand-picked length-matched relation replacements (e.g., "played for" → "was attending"), a small and possibly idiosyncratic swap set.

## Strengths

- Clean task formalization (anchored factual recall) that cleanly separates selection from retrieval and is directly operationalizable by minimal-pair prompts.
- Two-route decomposition with converging evidence from four independent intervention types (knockout, patching, cross-fitted ablation, value injection + MLP pinning), replicated across four model families.
- Strong controls throughout: contribution-matched random heads, time-sensitive heads, the shared-year-offset control against a generic-year explanation, and weakening-one-route tests against compensation.
- Quantitative, falsifiable claims (additivity with significance tests, cross-model correlations of route balance) rather than qualitative narrative alone.
- The relation-level analysis (P39 vs P54 route shares moving in opposite directions under patching vs value injection) connects mechanism to query properties, which is rare in this literature.

## Limitations

- Scope: one language (English), one dataset (TempLAMA, 34,962 test instances), one prompt format, year anchors only (2010-2020); other temporal expressions (dates, events) and non-temporal anchors (locations) are untested — the authors state claims are scoped to this setting.
- Prompt order is confounded with mechanism: the anchor must precede the subject for the subject-mediated route to exist under causal attention, and the order could not be varied within English without syntactic confounds.
- Only 7-8B models; smaller models fail the correctness filter too often and larger ones are too costly (~150x forward passes per example).
- Analyzed instances are a filtered subset (correct answers, ≥2 differing gold objects per group), so error cases and ambiguous temporal knowledge are outside the picture.
- The two routes explain 69-90% of the anchor's effect; the remainder flows through other positions reading the anchor, which is quantified but not characterized.
- No code release stated.

## Relevance

对研究语言模型事实回忆机制（mechanistic interpretability）与时间/上下文条件知识的工作直接相关：它把 Geva 式三阶段事实回忆框架扩展到"锚定选择"场景，并给出可加的双通路证据，对知识编辑、时间问答与模型内部知识表征研究都有参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Do the two routes persist when the anchor follows the subject (testable in languages where both orders are natural, e.g., Japanese or German), or when the anchor is a date, event, or location instead of a year?
- What carries the residual 10-31% of the anchor's effect (G_both/G_full = 0.69-0.90) — is it the relation span reading the anchor at untested layer windows, or non-query positions?
- Do the same routes govern cases where the model answers incorrectly — e.g., stale-fact errors where a model defaults to a historically prominent club despite the anchor?
- Can the route-balance signature (subject-route share per relation, cross-model consistent at ρ = 0.54-0.82) be used predictively, e.g., to route model-editing interventions (ROME-style subject edits vs final-position MLP edits) per query type?
- How do these routes interact with in-context conflict: when demonstrations or a preceding context assert a different year, does the direct route's anchor readout dominate the subject-mediated state?
