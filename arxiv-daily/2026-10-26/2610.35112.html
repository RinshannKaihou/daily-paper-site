Key Finding · arXiv 2610.35112 · cs.LG / cs.AI

Effective-rank compression follows the grokking phase transition — a signature, not a cause; imposed low rank delays or blocks it.

「权重矩阵有效秩的压缩是 grokking 相变的“结果 / 签名”而非原因 —— 压缩总是跟随（而非先于）泛化起点，强行预先施加低秩反而延迟甚至阻止 grokking。」

Subspace Crystallization in Grokking: Rank Compression Tracks the Phase Transition ↗
Shlok Mehendale, Paras Chopra · Lossfunk · 26 Oct 2026 · preprint (not peer-reviewed)
grokking rank compression mechanistic interpretability training dynamics modular arithmetic
Key Metric — causal timing
r = 0.99

The steepest effective-rank drop of Win tracks each seed's grokking onset (slope 1.02, CI 0.98–1.07), landing 905 ± 506 steps after it — 27/29 seeds. Held-out seeds: 850 ± 524 after onset (p = 1×10⁻⁵).

Why This Matters

Settles whether low-rank structure causes grokking or merely reflects it — and exposes rank / IPR-based early-warning signals and naive rank-cap designs as unreliable.

⚠ Critical Edge

Toy-scale only: everything is modular arithmetic on cyclic groups with ≤128-dim models, and “not cause” is an absence-of-evidence conclusion over a limited family of rank-cap interventions.

Two architectures, full-batch AdamW

  • Mean-field MLP — 128 hidden units, quadratic activation (Fourier terms explicit), MSE, wd 10⁻², 10⁵ steps, 15 seeds; 5 random-label nets as negative controls.
  • One-layer transformer (Nanda-style) — 4 heads, d_model 128, cross-entropy, wd 1.0, 25k steps; timing defined on seeds 0–14, tested on held-out seeds 15–29.

Key definitions

  • t_g = first step with test acc ≥ 95%; t_fit = train acc ≥ 99%.
  • ER(W) = exp(entropy of normalized singular values) (Roy–Vetterli).
  • d₀.₉₉ (accuracy-based, margin-sensitive) vs d_out (output change ≤ 10%, margin-immune). “Subspace crystallization” = d_out narrowing.

Intervention toolbox

  • Singular-direction truncation (top / bottom / random); complement perturbations.
  • Cross-seed transplant with orthogonal Procrustes alignment.
  • Optimizer swaps (AdamW ε, GD, SGD-momentum at matched lr/ε); nuclear-norm penalty λ‖W₁‖*.
  • Rank caps W = AB at r ∈ {8…128}, each vs a factorized full-rank control.

  • Nuclear-norm penalty pre-compresses W₁ (ER 78.5 vs 115.9 at step 11,500) → grokking delayed 12,700 → 16,200 steps; λ ≥ 3×10⁻⁷ blocks learning entirely.
  • Factorization trap: writing W = AB alone speeds MLP grokking 7–8× (1,667 vs 12,483 steps) but slows the transformer — naive cap-vs-unfactorized comparisons would falsely conclude low rank accelerates grokking.
  • Stage 4 is the optimizer: at effective lr 10⁵, AdamW / GD / SGD-m all reach ER ≈ 104–108; at lr 10³ ER stays 117.8 — late compression is optimizer-driven, not loss-driven.

The margin confound: the MLP's famous d₀.₉₉ “compression” (109.5 → 64.9) coincides with median margin growing 0.105 → 0.470, then re-widens to 89 — accuracy-based counts shrink as margins grow. d_out stays flat: it was never real structure. Cross-entropy MLPs do crystallize, but late (d_out ≈ 120 → 70–81).
  • Truncating to the top-d₀.₉₉ subspace keeps ≥ 99.26% test accuracy; random subspaces of equal rank keep 22.7% (MLP) / ≤ 12.5% (transformer).
  • Cross-seed representations align under Procrustes rotation: readout decoding 95.8% vs 1.0% before (0.6–1.4% for random-label / cross-task controls).
  • Fourier IPR rises even in never-grokked nets (all 5 random-label MLPs, all 7 non-grokked transformers) — not a label-free early-warning signal.

Strengths

  • Memorization (≤250 steps) vs onset (6.5k–21k) separation turns an observational confound into a quasi-experimental design.
  • d_out criterion is a real methodological advance; the margin artifact is demonstrated, not asserted (margin 0.105 → 0.470 with d_out flat).
  • The factorized-control insight would invalidate several plausible designs in this literature — the paper states exactly how to avoid the trap.
  • Every secondary claim (optimizer swaps, Procrustes universality, IPR) ships with its negative control.
  • Held-out replication discipline: seeds 0–14 define, 15–29 + 2 tasks test, 9 t-tests with Bonferroni reported.

Limitations

  • Cyclic (abelian) groups only; non-abelian composition and language / vision models untested — is there a comparably clean sufficient subspace in real models?
  • MLP vs transformer comparison confounded: loss (MSE vs CE), weight decay (10⁻² vs 1.0), and horizon differ simultaneously.
  • 128-dimensional toy networks; “signature not cause” established only within this regime.
  • Granger captures temporal order only; caps also change optimization (preconditioning), which the control mitigates but cannot eliminate.
  • Rank-cap arms have 5 seeds each with large variance (Win rank-8 onset from a single grokked seed) — some details underpowered.

Questions for further exploration

Beyond toy transformers? Does d_out crystallization (27–53 → 8–13 directions) occur in LMs fine-tuned on algorithmic tasks, or only here?
Reverse intervention Expanding rank / injecting noise into the sufficient subspace late in training — effect on robustness or prompt sensitivity?
Predicting the sign Can implicit preconditioning (Arora 2018) predict a priori whether factorization speeds or delays grokking for a given architecture/loss?
Adam artifact in production? Do standard ε (10⁻⁸–10⁻⁶) make late-stage rank changes in LLM training partly optimizer artifacts?
Functional or epiphenomenal? The MSE MLP's subspace re-widens after generalization (65 → 89) — margin/robustness trade-off, or noise?
Cheap universality probe? Could d_out counting replace SAE sparsity metrics, given 95.8% cross-seed Procrustes transfer?
