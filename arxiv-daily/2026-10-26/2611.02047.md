> **一句话总结：** 本文用计算力学（transducer 反馈环）证明任何联合过程都可视为多个体反馈交互、其贝叶斯信念可分解为逐个体张量积，并实验证明在小 transformer 上训练此类数据后，残差流以线性子空间分别编码每个个体的信念（R² ≥ 0.992）、可逐因子注入反事实信念实现精准转向（闭合约 98% 的 KL 差距），且表征维度随个体数线性增长（8N）而非指数增长（9^N − 1）。

> **TL;DR:** The authors prove that any joint stochastic process can be presented as transducers interacting in feedback whose Bayesian belief states factorize per agent, then verify on small transformers (4 layers, d=120, context 9) trained on synthetic feedback processes (mess3–fractal2 loops, "telephone" networks of up to N=5 mess3 agents): linear probes on the final-layer residual stream recover each agent's Bayes-optimal belief geometry (held-out R² ≥ 0.992 by 50k steps vs 0.676–0.914 at step 500), splicing counterfactual beliefs into one factor's subspace ("belief patching") closes 97.6–98.1% of the null-to-Bayes-target KL gap, and the residual stream's effective rank follows the factored dimension 8N rather than the Kronecker/joint-simplex baseline 9^N − 1 (which needs 2.5–11× more components at 95% CEV: 19/52/124 for N=3/4/5). This extends Shai et al.'s belief-geometry results from independent processes to feedback-coupled ones, and shows coarse-graining (hiding one agent's tokens) destroys the factorization and forces a higher-dimensional representation (rank 27 vs 16 at 95% CEV).

## 核心贡献

- **反馈普适性定理（Theorem 1）**：借助因果条件化的链式法则，形式化地证明任意联合随机过程 p(x_0:L, y_0:L) 都可以表示为两个 transducer 在反馈环中交互的产物——"多智能体反馈"是一种普适的解释视角。
- **联合过程的算子分解（Theorem 2 + Corollary 2）**：反馈环的联合 HMM 线性算子是个体算子的张量积；若初始信念不相关，联合信念状态在贝叶斯更新下始终保持张量积形式 η^{R,S,X} = η^R ⊗ η^{S,X}，因此可无损地以直和（direct sum）而非张量积存储。
- **指数级维度优势（Corollary 3）**：乘积式表征需要 D_prod = |S||R||X| − 1 维，而分解式表征只需 D_fact = |R| + |S||X| − 2 维；对 N 个个体的"社交网络"（电话游戏）结构，D_fact 随 N 线性增长而 D_prod 指数增长（9^N − 1 vs 8N）。
- **实验验证三连**：(1) transformer 在残差流中以分离的线性子空间编码每个个体的信念几何（探针 R² ≥ 0.992）；(2) 这些表征具有因果效力——逐因子"信念补丁"（belief patching）可单独转向某个体的预测；(3) 残差流有效维度紧贴分解式预测而非联合单纯形。
- **粗粒化致复杂化**：隐藏任一个个体的输出（x-only / y-only 观测）会破坏因子分解，迫使更高维的纠缠表征——为"观测接口的选择影响模型内部复杂度"提供了定量证据。

## Key Contributions

- **Feedback Universality (Theorem 1):** using the chain rule for causal conditioning, any joint process can be formally viewed as arising from two transducers (agents) coupled in a feedback loop — a universal "multi-agent" reading of joint time series that goes beyond prior feed-forward circuit results (Boyd et al., 2025).
- **Factored belief dynamics (Theorem 2, Corollary 2):** the joint feedback HMM's linear operator is the tensor product of per-agent operators, and starting from uncorrelated priors the joint Bayesian belief state remains an exact tensor product under filtering — so it can be stored losslessly as a direct sum of per-agent beliefs.
- **Exponential dimension gap (Corollary 3):** product dimension D_prod = |S||R||X| − 1 vs factored dimension D_fact = |R| + |S||X| − 2; for an N-agent "social network" (telephone game) with mess3 agents, the factored dimension is 8N while the joint simplex is 9^N − 1 — linear vs exponential scaling in the number of agents.
- **Empirical validation in transformers:** (i) final-layer residual-stream linear probes recover each agent's belief-state geometry in separate subspaces; (ii) "belief patching" interventions into a single factor's subspace causally steer only that agent's predictions, closing ~98% of the KL gap to the Bayes-optimal counterfactual; (iii) PCA effective rank of the residual stream tracks D_fact, not D_prod.
- **Complexification through coarse-graining:** summing out one agent's tokens entangles the operators, and trained transformers on such coarse sequences need higher-dimensional residual representations — a controlled demonstration that hiding observables can make a process intrinsically harder to predict.

## Methodology

The paper pairs formal theory with controlled experiments on small transformers:

1. **Theory (computational mechanics):** agents are modeled as transducers — stochastic automata with Markov kernels T^{(y|x)}_{r→r'} mapping inputs and memory states to outputs and next states. Their *Mixed State Presentation* (MSP) groups histories into belief states η_t ∈ Δ(R) obtained by Bayesian filtering; these are sufficient statistics for prediction. The feedback loop of two transducers T, U is "boxed" into a single HMM whose operator is the tensor product V̂(xy) = T̂(y|x) ⊗ Û(x|y) (Theorem 2), with an "action-delayed" asymmetry (U's memory is augmented with its latched action x). Theorem 1 proves any joint process admits such a presentation; Corollary 2 proves beliefs stay factored; Corollary 3 compares D_prod and D_fact.

2. **Processes:** three base HMM families drive the transducers — mess3 (3 states, belief states fill the simplex), fractal2 (2 states, beliefs on a lower-dimensional sub-manifold), and sns (simple nonunifilar source). Setups include two-agent feedback loops (mess3–fractal2, mess3–sns, sns–mess3, mess3↔mess3), N-agent "telephone" social networks (N ∈ {2..5}, each sub-agent a mess3 or fractal2 transducer passing its output to the next agent through a hub), and a coarse-graining setup built from epsilon-noisy input-conditional permutations with token alphabets smaller than the state count.

3. **Models:** a 4-layer transformer, d_model = 120, 3 heads (d_head = 40), d_mlp = 480, ReLU + LayerNorm, learned absolute positions, context 9 (BOS + 8 tokens), trained online for 50,000 steps with AdamW (lr 5e-4, reduce-on-plateau) on 25,000 fresh sequences per step; five seeds per setup. Runs converge to within a few 10⁻³ nats of the exact Bayes-optimal loss floor (computed via 32,768 Monte-Carlo samples), so the models genuinely solve the prediction task.

4. **Analysis tools:** (i) *linear probes* — ordinary-least-squares maps from the final-layer residual stream (BOS excluded) to ground-truth MSP coordinates, fit on 80% of a 1024-sequence batch, evaluated as held-out R²; (ii) *belief patching* — writing the counterfactual belief (from a different token sequence) into one factor's subspace and comparing next-token KL against the Bayes filter for the patched beliefs, with null-write and unrelated-sequence controls plus an encoder-orthogonalized variant; (iii) *dimensional analysis* — cumulative explained variance (CEV) and effective rank n_c of the residual stream vs the factored concatenation and the Kronecker product of Bayes-optimal per-agent beliefs. Code is released (github.com/xorlabs-research/feedback-transducers), building on Shai et al. (2026).

## Results & Highlights

- **Belief geometry is linearly decodable per agent:** on the mess3 (T) / fractal2 (U) feedback loop, held-out linear-probe R² rises from 0.914 (mess3) and 0.676 (fractal2) at training step 500 to a near-perfect fit of R² ≥ 0.992 for all factors by step 50,000 (up to 1.000 for mess3 and 0.996 for fractal2) — the residual stream reconstructs each agent's MSP belief simplex in separate subspaces, emerging within the first few hundred steps.
- **Factor-wise causal steering:** belief patching closes 97.6% ± 0.3 (patch T) and 98.1% ± 0.3 (patch U) of the null-to-target KL gap to the Bayes-optimal counterfactual next-token distribution (5 seeds), where the null (no-effect) write closes ~0%; the role-swapped loop closes the same fraction, and partial admixtures of counterfactual beliefs yield graded intermediate predictions rather than memorized jumps.
- **Linear (not exponential) dimension scaling:** for the telephone process with N mess3 agents, the residual stream's effective rank at 95% CEV matches the factored prediction 8N to within one component for N ≥ 3, whereas the Kronecker (joint-simplex, 9^N − 1) belief states need 2.5–11× more components — 19, 52 and 124 components at N = 3, 4, 5.
- **Coarse-graining breaks factorization:** with both agents observed the process admits an 8-dimensional factored belief state and the trained models cross 95% CEV at PCA rank 16 on average (5 draws, range 10–21); hiding one agent's tokens (x-only or y-only) forces a ~22-dimensional entangled belief state and the models cross 95% CEV only at rank 27 on average (range 12–42) — in all 10/10 within-draw comparisons the coarse model needs a higher rank.
- **Training quality:** all models converge to within a few 10⁻³ nats of the Bayes-optimal floor (only exception: two of five N=4 seeds, 3–5×10⁻³ nats above), so the representational findings are not artifacts of undertrained predictors.
- **Steerability is process-dependent:** every setup without an sns factor closes 89.5–99.6% of the patching gap, but the two sns-containing loops close only ~69%, and in the sns→mess3 loop the unprojected write to the mess3 factor lands at −41% (farther from target than no edit); encoder orthogonalization repairs this to +14%.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** genuine incremental novelty over Shai et al. (2026): moving from independent/conditionally-independent factors to *feedback-coupled* agents requires the transducer formalism, the time-ordering construction, and the action-delayed asymmetry (U's memory augmented with its latched action) — the factored-belief theorem (Corollary 2) is the crisp new result. The coarse-graining result (Corollary that summing over x entangles the operators) is the most conceptually interesting finding, echoing "more is different" in a precise measurable way.
- **Rigor:** strong on controls — null writes, unrelated-sequence controls, orthogonalized writes, role-swapped loops, held-out probes refit per checkpoint, five seeds throughout, and convergence verified against the exact Bayes floor (not just loss curves). The dimensional claims are corroborated by both geometric (probe R²) and causal (patching) evidence, which matters because PCA rank cannot see superposed features — a caveat the authors themselves state.
- **Reproducibility:** high — public code, full hyperparameter and process-parameter tables, enumerated seeds. The main barrier is that the entire pipeline is synthetic-process-specific.
- **Significance vs overclaiming:** the headline "~98% steerable" and "linear vs exponential scaling" results are honestly scoped but the abstract-level reading oversells smoothness: the fine print (Appendix L, Fig. 16) shows the two sns-containing loops close only ~69% of the patching gap, and patching the mess3 partner in the sns→mess3 loop under the raw unprojected write is *counterproductive* (−41%), only repaired to +14% by an orthogonalization that removes up to 52% of the write mass. So "separately steerable" holds for favorable (unifilar-ish, simplex-filling) processes, not universally. Likewise, "separate subspaces" is approximate: the between-factor encoder subspace overlap is substantial everywhere (0.13–0.58).
- **External validity:** all experiments use a 4-layer, d_model=120 transformer with context length 9 (BOS + 8 tokens) on 2–3-state synthetic HMMs — nine orders of magnitude from a pretrained LLM, and the authors admit that discovering factorized beliefs without ground truth (e.g., via sparse dictionary learning) is the unsolved pivotal step. Theorem 1's universality, while elegant, is existential and thus empirically unfalsifiable — any result is consistent with "some feedback decomposition exists"; the empirical weight rests entirely on Corollary 3-style dimension comparisons.
- **Missing analyses:** no comparison against alternative compact presentations (the factorization a process admits is non-unique and which one a transformer prefers is left open); no layer-wise or head-wise localization of where factorization emerges (probing is mostly last-layer); no test of whether optimization pressure (data scale, width) is needed for factorization or whether it appears trivially.

## Strengths

- A tight theory–experiment loop: the dimensional prediction (8N vs 9^N − 1) is derived analytically before being measured, and the measurements track it.
- Causal (intervention-based) evidence, not just correlational probing — with graded dose-response under partial belief admixture, ruling out memorization.
- The coarse-graining experiment is a clean, falsifiable demonstration that observation interface alone can force higher-dimensional internal representations.
- Exceptional experimental hygiene: seeds enumerated, min–max bands, Bayes-floor convergence checks, null and unrelated controls, orthogonalized-write robustness checks, public code.
- Clear positioning in the literature (belief geometry, world models, factored RL representations, LMs-as-agent-models) with an extended related-work appendix.

## Limitations

- Evidence is restricted to small transformers (4 layers, d_model 120, 9-token context) on synthetic processes with known ground truth; no pretrained or natural-data model is examined, and discovering factors without ground truth remains future work (authors' own admission).
- Steerability fails partially on nonunifilar processes: sns loops close only ~69% of the gap and one unprojected patch is counterproductive (−41%), so the method's scope within their own process zoo is bounded.
- The Multi-Agent Belief Dimension Hypothesis gives only an upper bound (mess3 saturates it; fractal2 beats it), and PCA-based rank measures miss features stored in superposition.
- Factorization is non-unique; which decomposition a network prefers, and whether more general interaction graphs (beyond feedback pairs and hub-and-spoke networks) are recoverable from data, is unaddressed.
- Practical applicability to AI safety (auditing subcomponents of large models) is aspirational — no bridge technique to production-scale models is demonstrated.

## Relevance

这篇论文将"transformer 内部线性编码贝叶斯信念几何"的研究从独立过程推广到反馈耦合的多智能体过程，与我们对 LLM 可解释性、世界模型表征与多智能体建模的兴趣高度契合：它给出了可检验的维度预测（8N vs 9^N−1）和逐因子干预转向的因果证据，为把大模型分解为可审计的"子组件信念"提供了理论工具。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Can factored belief structure be discovered *without* ground-truth MSP coordinates, e.g., via sparse dictionary learning or causal probing, in the mess3/fractal2 zoo first and then in pretrained LLMs?
- When a process admits multiple factorizations, which one does gradient descent favor — the most compact, the most causally separable, or something dependent on data ordering and curriculum?
- Do the sns steering failures reflect superposition of the two factors' codes, an insufficiently converged read/write basis, or a genuine property of nonunifilar processes — and does scaling width or training data repair them?
- How do the findings transfer to realistic interaction graphs (beyond pairwise feedback and hub-mediated telephone networks), and can a transformer infer the interaction graph itself from sequence data?
- Does the coarse-graining-induced dimensional blow-up have a natural-language analogue — e.g., does observing only a subset of a multi-party conversation force entangled (higher-rank) internal belief representations in an LM?
