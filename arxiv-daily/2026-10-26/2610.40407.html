Key Finding · RLVR advantage shaping

GRPO advantages are blind to how confidently the policy answered — SCARL reshapes them by confidence–outcome alignment.

优势对策略置信度视而不见——SCARL 用一个乘法因子按「组内相对置信度与验证结果的对齐差距」重塑每个响应的优势，并缓解熵坍缩。

Why This Matters

A structural GRPO flaw — advantages ignoring policy confidence — gets a provably safe, zero-overhead, plug-in fix that doubles as an entropy-collapse remedy.

对 LLM 推理训练、探索坍缩与置信度校准的研究均有直接参考价值。

Critical Edge

All results are single runs — no seeds, no variance — so many deltas (+0.1 to +0.5) are plausibly noise; “14 of 16 improved” is directional, not exact.

Red flag: summed log-likelihood confidence conflates response length with confidence — a confound never directly controlled for.

Methodology — one factor, four group-level steps

SCARL leaves rewards, importance ratios, and clipping untouched; it only multiplies each group-relative advantage by a strictly positive factor wi, built from scalars already computed during training:

1
Sequence confidencec_i = Σt log π_old(y_t) — free (already needed for ratios); the sum, not mean, separates long wanderers from short committers.
2
Group-relative share standardize c_i within the group, softmax at temperature τ → p_i; shift-invariance cancels prompt difficulty, so one τ transfers everywhere.
3
Alignment gap w̃_i = 1−p_i (successes) or p_i (failures) — fragile successes and confident errors absorb the update; aligned samples are left nearly alone.
4
Class-wise normalization divide by the class mean — budget conserved, E[w|class]=1. Skipping this collapses Pass@32 by −9.4.

No gradient flows through wi — it acts as a per-sequence, confidence-dependent learning rate. One hyperparameter (τ=5, chosen once, reused across all 16 pairings), O(G) cost, zero extra forward passes. Provable: sign preservation, class-mass conservation, exact recovery of the base estimator when confidences agree.

Strengths

  • Falsifiable diagnosis (“same outcome → same confidence”) with the fix derived from it, not a generic entropy-bonus patch.
  • Provable safety: strictly positive factor (no sign flips), class advantage budget conserved, exact recovery of the base estimator when confidences agree.
  • Zero extra forward passes and one hyperparameter (τ=5 selected once, reused in all 16 pairings) — unusually frugal.
  • The mechanism is actually tested: entropy elevated from step 12 onward; Cohen's d rises into the large-effect regime.
  • Honest negatives reported and explained: EAS below GRPO, UCAS 59.9, two SAPO regressions, the GRPO entropy exception.

Limitations

  • Competition math only, binary ±1 verifier rewards; code generation and shaped/continuous rewards untested.
  • Backbones ≤4B, 150 training steps; 32B+/long-horizon behavior unknown — gains thin to +0.1–+0.5 on distilled (better-calibrated) backbones.
  • Single training runs, no seed variance; best-checkpoint selection slightly flatters every method.
  • Summed log-likelihood ties confidence to response length by construction — confound argued away, never measured.
  • Entropy benefit is estimator-dependent: under GRPO's symmetric hard clipping, collapse still occurs (0.050 vs 0.035).

Questions for Further Exploration

  • Does it hold at 30B+ scale and long-horizon training, where entropy collapse binds — or does headroom vanish on better-calibrated bases?
  • Does it transfer to code / noisy verifiers, where the binary outcome t_i may be unreliable?
  • Can the length confound in summed log-likelihood confidence be separated (length-stratified or length-mixture confidence)?
  • Does SCARL stack additively with clip-higher / covariance control, or do they fight over the same entropy budget?
  • Does the sharpened confidence (d 0.65→1.06) transfer to inference-time abstention or best-of-n selection at no extra cost?
  • Why lose to Dr. GRPO on Minerva specifically (57.4 vs 59.6) while winning everywhere else — shorter answers vs summed confidence?
