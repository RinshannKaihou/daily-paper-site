> **一句话总结：** 本文揭示了多轮工具调用 Agent 中"历史误导劫持策略"的失效模式（在 Qwen3-1.7B 上污染历史会翻转 32.1% 原本正确的决策），提出配套的 ContextPollute-Bench（Original/Polluted/Oracle State 三视图配对基准）与 Oracle-OPD（基于 Oracle 视图的冻结教师对污染上下文学生前缀做反向 KL 蒸馏），在 Qwen3-1.7B 上取得 87.0% BTA，显著优于 Gold-SFT（66.3%）和序列蒸馏（82.3%），并迁移到干净历史、未见函数和外部基准。

> **TL;DR:** The paper introduces **history-induced policy hijacking** — structurally valid but no-longer-authoritative historical traces flip 32.1% of Qwen3-1.7B's otherwise-correct tool-use decisions on the new ContextPollute-Bench (synchronized Original/Polluted/Oracle State views, 11 gold-preserving operators built on APIGen-MT airline+retail). The proposed **Oracle-OPD** (frozen Oracle-conditioned teacher doing reverse-KL distillation over student-generated prefixes under polluted context) reaches 87.0% Balanced Tool-Use Accuracy on Qwen3-1.7B vs 66.3% Gold-SFT and 82.3% Oracle sequence distillation; an 8B teacher lifts the same 1.7B student to 91.9% BTA, and gains transfer to clean histories (84.50% BTA), held-out tools (OOD-600 TCR 0.7433), cross-generator (Claude-regenerated) contexts, BFCL/When2Call, and zero-shot HotpotQA-Distractor.

## 核心贡献

- **可控诊断历史误导问题**：提出 ContextPollute-Bench，对每个决策点构造 Original、Polluted、Oracle State 三个同步视图，三者共享系统策略、当前工具、最新请求与黄金动作，只有历史轨迹不同。这种配对设计直接检验"误导历史是否会翻转模型本可正确执行的决策"。
- **系统性归因失效机制**：设计 11 个保持黄金动作不变的干扰算子，覆盖 Decision-State、Entity-Binding、Interface-Execution 三类（如错误参数名、单位漂移、遗留参数、竞争实体、不支持的成功声明等），将聚合误差分解为可定位的失效路径。
- **提出 Oracle-OPD 训练方法**：用冻结的 Oracle 视图条件教师，对在污染上下文下自采样的学生前缀做 token 级反向 KL 软监督，部署时只需学生与普通历史，无需 Oracle 视图或教师。该方法把"可靠状态下的策略"迁移到面对误导历史的学生。
- **全面验证可扩展性与迁移性**：跨教师/学生规模（1.7B→8B）、干净历史、未见函数（OOD-600）、独立再生的评估上下文（Claude-Opus-4.7）、外部基准（BFCL v4、When2Call）以及带噪多跳问答（HotpotQA-Distractor）都验证了方法有效性，确立"历史可靠性"是一个独立可训练的工具使用瓶颈。

## Key Contributions

- **Controlled diagnosis of a novel failure mode.** The paper defines *history-induced policy hijacking*: when a structurally valid but non-authoritative historical trace is inserted before an unchanged request, models deviate from a policy they already possess. The paired Original/Polluted/Oracle State design isolates this from generic task difficulty, error repair, or instruction injection.
- **ContextPollute-Bench.** A paired benchmark with 11 gold-preserving operators across Decision-State, Entity-Binding, and Interface-Execution categories, built from APIGen-MT airline and retail trajectories, with stratified audits reporting 97.0% gold correctness, 99.0% Oracle sufficiency, and 93.7% human / 91.6% Claude-Opus-4.7 joint acceptance.
- **Oracle-Guided On-Policy Distillation (Oracle-OPD).** A training recipe combining (i) a reliable Oracle-State teacher view, (ii) token-level soft (reverse-KL) targets preserving the teacher's distributional preferences, and (iii) on-policy student-generated prefixes that cover the states a misled student actually reaches. Deployment uses only the distilled student.
- **Breadth of validation.** Reliable-state distillation transfers to clean histories, held-out functions, cross-generator evaluation contexts, external benchmarks (BFCL, When2Call), and zero-shot noisy multi-hop QA — establishing history reliability as a distinct, tractable training target.

## Methodology

**Three synchronized views.** For each decision point the authors construct: (1) *Original* — the untouched source prefix; (2) *Polluted* — the same prefix with a structurally valid but non-authoritative trace inserted before the unchanged final request; (3) *Oracle State* — a compact summary of only reliable, decision-relevant intent, slots, observations, policy, and evidence status (without exposing the gold tool name). All three share system policy, current tools, latest request, and gold action `a*`, so `a*(x_orig) = a*(x_poll) = a*(x_oracle)`.

**Benchmark construction.** Built on the airline and retail domains of APIGen-MT (Prabhakar et al. 2026). DeepSeek-V4-Flash generates the inserted pollution trace and the Oracle State (but never the source gold calls/answers). Eleven operators span Decision-State (unsupported completion claims, decision-irrelevant reads, decommissioned tools), Entity-Binding (competing entities, entity interleaving), and Interface-Execution (wrong/missing/extra/legacy/reformatted/unit-shifted arguments). Deterministic validators preserve source-message order, keep the latest request final, pair tool calls with results, instantiate the intended distractor, and verify the gold action is unchanged; Oracle States are rejected if they expose the gold tool or omit decision-critical state. Splits are trajectory-level (no trajectory crosses splits); OOD-600 contains 600 Call-required examples whose gold actions invoke 6 tools never seen as gold during training.

**Metrics.** BTA (Balanced Tool-Use Accuracy) = ½·TCR + ½·(1−OCR), where TCR (True Call Rate) requires a complete correct call (correct tool + complete correct arguments) on gold-Call examples, and OCR (Over-Call Rate) is P(CALL | gold=ANSWER). Decomposition: TCR = ToolAcc × ArgExact.

**Oracle-OPD objective.** Given paired Polluted/Oracle inputs (x_p, x_o), the student first generates prefix y ~ π_θ(·|x_p); for each student-generated prefix y_<t the teacher and student distributions are q^T_t = π_T(·|x_o, y_<t) and q^S_t = π_θ(·|x_p, y_<t), and the loss is the mean reverse-KL D_KL(q^S_t ‖ q^T_t) over the union of teacher and student top-16 token supports, temperature 1, four rollouts per prompt, no additional gold-CE term. At deployment only the trained student and ordinary interaction history are required.

**Training setup.** Qwen3-1.7B and 8B trained for 3 epochs with full-parameter FSDP on eight H20 GPUs; OPD uses lr 1e-6, global batch 32 prompts, prompt/response limits 12,288/1,024 tokens; SFT uses cosine lr 1e-5, batch 256. Greedy decoding for evaluation; checkpoints selected by validation BTA.

## Results & Highlights

- **History-induced hijacking is large.** On Qwen3-1.7B, pollution flips **32.1% of examples correct under Original history** (29.4% for Call, 40.5% for Answer); relative to Oracle-correct predictions the flip rates are 40.8% / 37.5% / 50.8%. The Oracle-vs-Polluted gap persists at 32B (Qwen3-32B BTA: Polluted 0.6587 vs Oracle 0.8773), so this is not a small-checkpoint peculiarity.
- **Corrupted-literal adoption is the shortcut.** Under Polluted input, the base model adopts the injected corrupted literal in **34.8%** of same-tool/different-target examples, **20.9%** of entity-interleaving examples, and **65.8%** of format-drift examples; after Oracle-OPD training these collapse to **1.0%** and **0.4%** on the two entity-binding operators even though the misleading trace remains visible.
- **Oracle-OPD is the strongest balanced policy on Qwen3-1.7B.** It reaches **0.8703 BTA** (TCR 0.9132, non-call recall 0.8273) vs Gold-SFT 0.6628, Oracle-SeqKD 0.8229, and off-policy OD 0.8499; the gain over off-policy OD is +0.0204 BTA (95% CI [+0.0118, +0.0295], p < 10⁻⁴ under 10,000-resample paired bootstrap). On held-out tools (OOD-600) Oracle-OPD TCR is 0.7433 vs Gold-SFT 0.2467.
- **Reliable-state supervision is a progression.** Oracle-SeqKD (0.8229) → off-policy OD (0.8499) → Oracle-OPD (0.8703), each component (Oracle view, soft targets, student-prefix coverage) contributing a factorized gain (Table 6: replacing Original teacher view with Oracle alone raises BTA 0.7562 → 0.8703).
- **Scaling transfers across model size without inference cost increase.** Holding the deployed student at 1.7B, replacing the 1.7B teacher with an 8B teacher raises BTA from 0.8703 to **0.9193**; an 8B student reaches **0.9301 BTA** (0.8217 OOD TCR).
- **Transfer to clean histories is real, not a pollution-specific patch.** The frozen Table-3 checkpoints reevaluated on Original input yield Oracle-OPD 0.8450 BTA (vs Polluted 0.8703), and held-out-function TCR is essentially unchanged between Polluted and Original (0.7433 vs 0.7450).
- **Cross-generator transfer.** With Claude-Opus-4.7 independently regenerating evaluation contexts (no Claude data used for training/selection), Oracle-OPD remains the strongest 1.7B post-trained model under Polluted input (0.814 BTA vs 0.792 off-policy OD, 0.761 Oracle-SeqKD).
- **External benchmarks and noisy QA.** On BFCL v4 Non-Live the 8B-teacher 1.7B student reaches 80.45 macro vs 79.58 untuned; on When2Call it raises Macro-F1 from 0.2701 to **0.3294** and cuts Cannot-Answer hallucination from 0.5000 to **0.3411**; zero-shot on HotpotQA-Distractor the 1.7B student distilled from an 8B teacher reaches **0.5015 F1**, beating the next-best trained checkpoint by 2.97 F1 points with the smallest clean-to-noisy gap (0.1596).
- **Prompt-only control is insufficient.** A direct instruction to ignore failed/irrelevant history raises BTA only from 0.4720 to 0.4849, vs 0.8703 for learned reliable-state transfer — showing the task requires discriminating *which* state remains authoritative, not just heeding a blanket warning.

## Authors & Credibility

The four authors (Xiaoqing Wu, Xingyu Fan, Feifei Li, Wenhui Que) are all at WeChat, Tencent Inc., Beijing — a production environment where multi-turn tool-using agents (assistant APIs for airline/retail/reservation) are realistic deployment targets, lending the failure-mode motivation practical credibility. The methodology is unusually disciplined for a benchmark+method paper: trajectory-level split assignment prevents leakage, a 200-example adjudicated audit plus a 1,000-example stratified cross-audit (93.7% human / 91.6% Claude-Opus-4.7 joint acceptance) is reported, and OOD-600 explicitly tests six held-out gold functions with same-family analogues in training. Significance is established via paired bootstrap with 10,000 resamples rather than single-seed numbers. The factorized design ablation (Table 6) and three-view evaluation across four model scales (Table 2) go beyond the typical "our method beats baseline" framing.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty** is genuine: the paired Original/Polluted/Oracle State design with a preserved gold action is a clean instrumentation that existing work (ReflecTool-Bench on error repair, InjecAgent/AgentDojo on malicious injection, Rabinovich & Anaby Tavor on query/toolkit variation) does not isolate. The "history-reliability as a distinct bottleneck" framing is well argued.
- **Rigor** is strong on the benchmark side (deterministic validators, trajectory-level splits, dual human+LLM audits, no-leakage check on 100 audited Call examples). However, the **single-seed training** convention is a weakness: with only checkpoint selection by validation BTA and no multi-seed variance reported, the +0.0204 BTA edge of Oracle-OPD over off-policy OD (CI [+0.0118, +0.0295]) is a bootstrap CI over evaluation, not over training randomness.
- **Reproducibility** is partially supported — the supplement ships the five-script construction pipeline (Table 9) and split files — but depends on the third-party `apigen-mt_5k.json` source file that is "not redistributed," so a reproducer must obtain APIGen-MT separately. Compute (8× H20 GPUs, 3 epochs full-parameter FSDP) is non-trivial for academic groups.
- **Domain coverage is narrow.** ContextPollute-Bench is restricted to **two APIGen-MT domains (airline and retail)** and to controlled next-action interventions; the authors themselves flag this in §5. Whether the 11 synthetic operators (e.g., "unit/scale drift", "legacy parameter + success") match the distribution of misleading traces in real production logs is unaddressed — ecological validity is the main open question.
- **Baseline omissions.** The authors compare Gold-SFT, SFT→DPO, Oracle-SeqKD, off-policy OD, and several ablations, but do not include recent RL-from-rollouts methods (e.g., GRPO-style on-policy RL with the Oracle as reward signal) or retrieval-based "history summarization" baselines that one would naturally try for the misleading-history problem; prompt-only control is the only inference-time baseline.
- **A subtle cherry-pick risk** in the headline "32.1% flip" number: it is computed only over examples the *same model* solves under Original, so a stronger base model would mechanically show a different rate; Table 2 mitigates this by reporting three-view BTA across scales, but the abstract leads with the 1.7B number, which is the most dramatic.
- **Significance** is high if the failure mode transfers to production; the cross-generator and external-benchmark (BFCL, When2Call, HotpotQA) transfers are the strongest evidence that the gain is not benchmark-overfit. No red flags regarding data leakage or metric definition were found.

## Strengths

- Clean causal instrumentation: the synchronized triple (Original/Polluted/Oracle) with a guaranteed-unchanged gold action turns an amorphous robustness question into a measurable, paired comparison.
- The factorized ablation (Table 6) actually explains *why* Oracle-OPD works — reliable conditioning (+0.114), soft targets (+0.027), student-prefix coverage (+0.020) — rather than presenting a monolithic method.
- Cross-size teacher→student transfer (8B teacher into a 1.7B deployment model reaching 0.9193 BTA) is a genuinely useful, practical result for inference-cost-constrained deployments.
- Honest about scope: the limitations section explicitly names the two-domain, controlled-intervention scope and the training-time Oracle-State requirement.
- Validates beyond the in-house benchmark (BFCL, When2Call, HotpotQA-Distractor zero-shot), reducing suspicion of metric gaming.

## Limitations

- Only airline and retail domains from APIGen-MT; pollution patterns are synthesized by DeepSeek-V4-Flash and may not reflect the distribution of misleading traces in real production conversations.
- Oracle State and on-policy rollouts are required at training time; constructing Oracle States at scale depends on a capable generator and non-trivial validators.
- Single-seed training; the headline +0.0204 BTA improvement over off-policy OD has an evaluation-bootstrap CI but no training-variance CI.
- The paired "flip rate" metric is conditioned on per-model-correct examples, complicating cross-model comparison of hijacking susceptibility (the paper does report three-view BTA across scales to partially address this).
- No comparison to on-policy RL with Oracle-derived rewards or to history-summarization/retrieval baselines that are natural competitors for the misleading-history problem.
- Prompt-only control is a weak baseline (a single generic instruction); a more carefully engineered prompting strategy (e.g., per-operator schema reminders) might narrow the gap more.

## Relevance

This paper is highly relevant to research on robust multi-turn tool-using LLM agents, a core topic for agent benchmarks, function-calling, and training-time policy distillation. It identifies a concrete, measurable failure mode (history-induced policy hijacking) that any production agent system faces, and offers both a reusable diagnostic benchmark and a training recipe (Oracle-OPD) that fits naturally into ongoing work on reliable agent post-training.

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How do the 11 synthetic operators map onto misleading traces that occur *naturally* in production agent logs — can the authors mine real failures and measure whether Oracle-OPD trained on synthetic pollution covers them?
- Would on-policy RL with an Oracle-derived correctness reward (instead of reverse-KL distillation) close the remaining 13-point BTA gap to 1.0 on Qwen3-1.7B, or does it introduce new over-call/under-call pathologies?
- Does the Oracle-OPD gain hold for *generative* tool-use settings (e.g., code execution, browser actions) where the "argument schema" is far larger and more open-ended than the airline/retail APIs?
- The 32B Oracle-vs-Polluted gap (0.6587 vs 0.8773) is larger than at 8B (0.6888 vs 0.8416) for Polluted but smaller for Oracle — is there a scale at which models become reliably self-correcting without Oracle-OPD, and what does that imply about the bottleneck?
- Can the corrupted-literal adoption signal (Figure 2B) be used as an *inference-time* detector of misleading history, enabling a routing strategy that switches to a more conservative policy only when adoption risk is high?
- Since the teacher's Oracle State must be constructed at training time, how sensitive is the final student BTA to Oracle-State quality (the 99.0% sufficiency number) — does a noisier Oracle merely lower the ceiling, or does it actively mislead the student?
