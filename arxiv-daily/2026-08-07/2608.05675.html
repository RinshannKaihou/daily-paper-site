Consistency Has a Computable Blind Spot: A Commutation Theory of Label-Free Reliability for Vision-Language Figure Reading

Rasul Khanbayov & Hasan Kurban — Kurban Intelligence Lab
TL;DR The undetectable errors of any invariance / equivariance test suite are exactly its joint centralizer — the errors commuting with every edit's answer-transform. The closed-form design rule {×c, +δ} is provably complete for affine reading errors; no swap suite is complete for label permutations. ECS flags 53.6% of invariance-blind errors vs REA's 0.0% at matched 14% FPR, raising combined AUROC from 0.788 to 0.906.
1 Key Finding
A test suite's undetectable errors equal its joint centralizer — giving a closed-form answer to "is this suite enough?" where metamorphic testing was Set-Cover-hard.

Critical Edge 04

⚠ built-in false negatives

28.9% of IBEM instances lie in the joint centralizer and are silently missed — the detector has built-in false negatives on exactly the population it targets. An oracle edit-selection upper bound (71.1% vs 52.6%) shows substantial headroom that label-free selection cannot reach.

ECS detects but does not repair, and its completeness guarantee (Theorem 4) covers only E_aff — real errors are stochastic, input-dependent, and only 75.5% affine by hand-labeling.

Core Contributions 01

  • Algebraic detectability Th. 2. The errors that produce no residual anywhere in a suite equal the joint centralizer ⋂ C_τ(φ_g). Replaces Set-Cover-hard completeness analysis with algebra.
  • Matched complete / incomplete pair. {×c, +δ} detects every non-identity affine error Th. 4; no swap suite is complete for label permutations Prop. 5, and cyclic relabeling shrinks the blind set from 2(m−2)! to m for m≥5 Cor. 6.
  • ECS & REND-EQUIV. A label-free, training-free score that references the model's own base answer. Matched invariance (K=8 re-renderings) vs equivariance (|G|∈{1,2,3}) over identical rendered data.
  • Pre-registered ordering, confirmed across 3 models. The per-edit-type ordering was fixed before measurement and survived a 200-instance hand-labeled, REA-independent check that removed IBEM's one selection circularity.

Methodology 02

A VLM figure-reader is modeled as an error map e on the exact answer a* (e=id ⇒ perfect). Edits g carry a known answer-transform φ_g satisfying a*(g(D),q) = φ_g(a*(D,q)); edits are realized by re-rendering g(D) in the same cosmetic style, isolating data change from style change.

The per-edit residual ρ_g = d(ŷ_g, φ_g(ŷ)) references the model's own base answer ŷ, so ECS is label-free. Lemma 1 shows ρ_g ≤ τ iff e ∈ C_τ(φ_g); Th. 7 extends the framework to stochastic error distributions with distribution-free monotonicity & additivity. Headline quantity IBEM = instances an invariance signal rates high-confidence (REA≥0.75) that are nonetheless wrong — exactly where the theory says equivariance must earn its keep.

Strengths 05

  • Closed-form theory turning suite design from intuition into a calculation.
  • Pre-registered predictions confirmed across three models including predicted failures (identity, swap).
  • Unusually honest: discloses IBEM circularity, validates difficulty controls, warns ECS is not a correctness guarantee.
  • Explains the literature ordering reversal vs classifier metamorphic testing as a natural consequence of the same theory — not fitted.

Limitations 06

  • Completeness is a guarantee about E_aff, not real models — what transfers is the ordering, not the guarantee.
  • Native only to programmatically renderable figures; real figures need digitization (94.8% pilot success); scanned figures out of scope.
  • 28.9% of IBEM lies in the joint centralizer and is silently missed.
  • Difficulty confound on 2/5 operators (delete-max, offset measurably easier than base).
  • Cyclic relabeling validated on 150 instances but not yet deployed in released REND-EQUIV (still swap-based).

Questions for Further Exploration 08

  • Does the predicted 8× cyclic gain survive at dataset scale when folded into full REND-EQUIV rather than a matched 150-instance sample?
  • Can the commutation theory extend to non-affine, non-permutation error classes (piecewise, logarithmic misreads) — what is Theorem 4's analogue there?
  • Does ρ_g correlate with error magnitude strongly enough to support a calibrated repair (not just detection) procedure?
  • Could centralizer analysis port to table-QA or geometry-QA, where controlled edits with known answer-transforms exist?
