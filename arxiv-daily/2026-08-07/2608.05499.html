APQF: Agentic Profiling-Guided Structured Pruning & Mixed-Precision Quantization with Adaptive Fine-Tuning

Five cooperating LLM agents — Profiling, Pruner, Quant, FineTuning, Evaluation — drive a fully training-aware prune → quantize → recover loop over measured per-layer sensitivity data, working the same way across CNNs and vision transformers.

01

Key Finding

LLM-as-planner over measured per-layer profiling evidence drives a training-aware prune→QAT→recover loop, beating data-hungry joint optimizers under limited training budgets.

02

Key Metric

5.63% Rel. BOPs on DeiT-Tiny / ImageNet — a 13–18× compute cut, with Top-1 climbing 66.52→67.90.
03

Critical Edge

The headline GETA comparison is not apples-to-apples: GETA ran weight-only quant because its code couldn't stably do joint weight+activation — exactly the regime APQF uses for its BOPs edge.

Why this matters / 为什么重要

APQF directly serves the goal of deploying CNN/ViT models on resource-constrained hardware without manual per-architecture tuning. Its profiling-grounded multi-agent orchestration pattern is portable to other Auto-ML pipelines, hitting both model compression and LLM-automated ML interests at once.

Methodology — Five Cooperating Agents

1 ProfilingAgent — ptflops + torch.profiler + DepGraph sweep measured per-group sensitivity CSV + architecture brief
2 PrunerAgent — LLM assigns per-group ratios, replayed from original avoids DepGraph "prune-on-pruned" stall
3 QuantAgent — Brevitas fake-quant, bits ∈ {2,4,6,8,16,32} per-layer bit-width from LLM, validated vs real modules
4 FineTuningAgent — full FT + 5 PEFT (LoRA, DoRA, rsLoRA, PiSSA, LoRA+) τ₁=2%, τ₂=4% gated recovery
5 EvaluationAgent — deterministic, fixed-seed class-balanced set reports Top-1/5, params, Rel. BOPs

Robustness — planner-agnostic

Six different LLM planners on Swin-Tiny/CIFAR-10 — including free/open-weight models (DeepSeek, Gemma) — all land within 97.4–97.9% Top-1, matching the default Claude Opus 4.8 within a narrow band. The framework is not coupled to a premium model.

One-line takeaway / 一句话总结

APQF uses five collaborating LLM agents to drive structured pruning + mixed-precision QAT + accuracy recovery from measured sensitivity, compressing ImageNet models to 5.6–7.7% of original BOPs and beating GETA on 4 of 5 CIFAR-10 architectures.

Key Contributions

  • Profiling agent — measured per-group sensitivity map grounds every decision.
  • Cumulative-replay pruning — stages replayed from the original model, sidestepping DepGraph stall.
  • LLM-guided mixed-precision QAT + KD under a frozen full-precision teacher.
  • Adaptive FineTuning — 5 PEFT variants + 2-stage PEFT→full recovery; plans validated before execution.
  • Architecture- & LLM-agnostic — ResNet, VGG, ViT, DeiT, Swin; planner swappable via OpenRouter.

Strengths

  • First real integration of LLM planning over measured evidence with full prune+QAT+recover.
  • Data-efficient: ~17-pt gap over GETA on ImageNet at 200K-image budget.
  • Honest ablations isolate both adaptive decisions and grounded profiling.
  • Several compressed models exceed their uncompressed baselines.

Limitations

  • Large drops on heavily-compressed ImageNet models (ResNet-50 −7.40, Swin-Tiny −6.45).
  • GETA baseline ran weight-only quant — a real confound (see Critical Edge).
  • All efficiency = theoretical Rel. BOPs; no measured latency/energy on a real edge device.
  • LLM planner non-determinism; failures more frequent with cheap/free models.
  • Scope = image classification only; no detection/segmentation/LLM/VLM.

Open questions

  • Real on-device latency/energy on ARM/NPU — does bit-width heterogeneity hurt kernels?
  • Does APQF's BOPs edge survive once GETA is re-implemented with joint W+A quant?
  • Extends to dense prediction (SAM-style) and LLMs/VLMs where QAT+KD cost is far larger?
