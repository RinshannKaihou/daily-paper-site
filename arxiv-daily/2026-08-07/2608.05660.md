> **一句话总结：** 本文提出一个三流（three-stream）推理有效性检测器，将跨层位移（motion）与两种受限的状态位置读出（向量量化 region + 归一化 direction）融合，仅用一个推理 benchmark 训练后，在未见过推理任务上比单层 linear probe 提升最高 21 个百分点选择准确率、比纯位移 SOTA 提升 12 个百分点，并且零样本迁移到事实补全与事实验证任务。

> **TL;DR:** A three-stream detector fuses layerwise residual-stream displacements (motion) with two restricted state views — a vector-quantized coarse region and a normalized per-layer direction — to classify candidate answers as valid/invalid. Trained on a single reasoning benchmark (e.g., ARC-Challenge donor) and evaluated on Llama-3.1-8B, it lifts out-of-distribution selection accuracy to 75.2 vs 64.1 for a linear probe and 63.2 for the motion-only SOTA (Truth as a Trajectory), and zero-shot transfers to factual sets (FACTOR-expert 69.5 vs Motion 50.8, VitaminC 67.1 vs Motion 57.6) without ever seeing a factual claim.

## 核心贡献

- **明确指出一个“状态恢复权衡”（state-restoration trade-off）**：纯位移（differencing）虽能抑制与标签相关的表层信号（措辞、格式、token 身份），但同时也丢掉了“状态上下文”——而同一个表示变化在不同上下文（支持/反驳）下含义不同；若直接恢复完整状态又会重新引入位移本想抑制的捷径信号。
- **提出三流检测框架**：motion reader（沿用 Truth as a Trajectory 的层间位移）+ region reader（学习一个 K=128 的 codebook，用最近邻替换连续状态，只保留“粗位置”）+ direction reader（保留 m=6 层中-后段归一化方向，丢弃幅度）。三者拼接后送入 MLP 头输出有效性分数。
- **强迁移性与“正确性而非某种推理”的解读**：仅在 1 个推理 benchmark 上训练，迁移到其他 7 个推理任务 + 4 个事实任务（FACTOR-wiki/news/expert、VitaminC），并在 12 个 donor×target 单元格上全部领先 motion-only baseline（领先 9–19 个百分点）。
- **跨模型验证**：在 Llama-3.1-8B、Qwen2.5-14B、Qwen3-30B-A3B（MoE）三类模型（32/48 层、dense + MoE）上一致有效。
- **极低参数开销**：新增的两个 location 流仅占 motion reader 参数量的 6.7%。
- **消融证明三流互补**：逐项加入 direction、region 各自提升，三者联合在 8 个推理目标上最优；per-item oracle 比训练后的联合模型还高 3–21 分，说明三者关注不同样本。

## Key Contributions

- **Identifies and formalizes a "state-restoration trade-off"**: trajectory-based differencing suppresses stable label-correlated signals but also discards the state context needed to interpret layer-wise changes (a representational change means different things under supporting vs. contradicting context, per their UMAP analysis); restoring the full state reintroduces shortcut-prone surface signals.
- **A three-reader detector instantiating "minimally sufficient state restoration"**: a motion reader (bidirectional LSTM over layerwise displacements, unchanged from prior work) is fused with a region reader (learned K=128 codebook, straight-through estimator + commitment term, replaces each state by its nearest entry) and a direction reader (normalized `u = h/||h||` over m=6 mid-to-late layers, shared projection W, MLP per token combining final-token + answer-span mean).
- **Strong cross-task transfer**: trained on a single reasoning donor, the full model is best in 47 of 48 cells, improving OOD selection accuracy by up to 21 points over a linear probe and 12 points over the motion-only SOTA, with the largest margin (12 pts) on the ARC-Challenge donor.
- **"Correctness, not a kind of reasoning"**: a reasoning-only-trained detector zero-shot identifies factual errors (minimal single-fact edits in FACTOR; evidence-checked claims in VitaminC), beating every baseline on all four factual targets by 9–19 points over motion-only.
- **Complementarity + parsimony**: ablations show each added stream lifts the motion base, and the per-item oracle sits 3–21 points above the trained fusion — the three streams read different items; the two added streams are only 6.7% of the motion reader's parameters.

## Methodology

The paper works in the **selection setting**: a frozen LLM is given a question + each candidate answer; one forward pass per candidate records residual-stream activations `h_{t,l}` at every token and layer. A detector scores each candidate and the highest wins. No model weights change.

The detector has three readers, each returning one embedding, concatenated and fed to an MLP head producing a validity score; training is end-to-end from scratch with a pairwise objective plus the VQ commitment term.

- **Motion reader** (unchanged from Damirchi et al. 2026, "Truth as a Trajectory"): reads `d_{t,l} = h_{t,l+1} − h_{t,l}` stacked across all tokens and layers; a bidirectional LSTM consumes the displacement sequence and returns `c_mot`. Differencing attenuates persistent token-specific content, which is what makes the signal transferable.
- **Shared state-preparation**: both location readers first strip magnitude via `u_{t,l} = h_{t,l}/||h_{t,l}||` (residual magnitude grows with depth, so keeping it would pass scale not content), project directions with a depth-shared matrix `W`, and restrict to the answer span A and a fixed window `L` of m=6 mid-to-late layers (early layers group by surface form, late layers operate in output-token space, mid layers are where representations come loose from wording).
- **Region reader (coarse)**: encoder maps projected directions `g_{t*}` to `z`, then `q(z) = e_{k*}` via nearest codebook entry (K entries). Trained with the straight-through estimator `z̃ = z + sg[e_{k*} − z]`, moving-average codebook updates, unused-entry reseeding, and a commitment term to prevent collapse. The head receives only the discrete `c_reg` — two states in the same entry are indistinguishable.
- **Direction reader (fine)**: keeps `g_t` whole at each of the m depths (no cross-depth compression); a depth-shared MLP maps each to `p_t`, and `c_dir = W_c [p_{t*} ; mean_{t in A} p_t]` combines the final-token settled state with the answer-span mean.
- **Fusion**: `ŷ = σ(f([c_mot ; c_dir ; c_reg]))`; pairwise training picks the correct candidate over incorrect ones.

Models: Llama-3.1-8B (32 layers), Qwen2.5-14B (48 layers), Qwen3-30B-A3B (MoE, 3B active). Donors: ARC-Challenge, ARC-Easy, OpenBookQA, CommonsenseQA, Social IQa, HellaSwag. Reasoning eval adds MMLU and Story Cloze; factual eval uses FACTOR-wiki/news/expert and VitaminC. TriviaQA and HaluEval are explicitly excluded because a shortest-answer heuristic scores 88.8/95.9 there.

## Results & Highlights

- **Out-of-distribution reasoning transfer (Llama-3.1-8B, Table 1)**: with ARC-Challenge as donor, OOD selection accuracy is **75.2** for Ours vs **64.1** for the linear probe (Δ +11.1) and **63.2** for motion-only TaT (Δ +12.0). On the in-distribution cell ARC-C, Ours reaches **74.2** vs probe **69.0** and TaT **62.2**. The full model is best in **47 of 48** donor×target cells.
- **Factual zero-shot transfer (Table 2, single seed, averaged over the 3 best reasoning donors)**: on FACTOR-expert Ours scores **69.5** vs probe **55.5** (Δ +14.0) and motion **50.8** (Δ +18.7); on VitaminC Ours **67.1** vs probe **62.3** and motion **57.6**; on FACTOR-wiki Ours **51.9** vs probe **30.0** and motion **39.4**. The full model leads motion-only by **9 to 19 points** across all four targets and in every donor cell behind the means.
- **Trade-off evidence (Sec. 3)**: on ARC-Challenge, a single-layer linear probe scores **68.6%** vs **62.2%** for the displacement reader (the state holds info differencing drops), but the last-token state exposes wording reconstruction **R² = 0.086 / retrieval 2.26** vs **0.007 / 1.01** for displacement — restoring the full state leaks answer wording, motivating minimal restoration.
- **Component ablation (Table 3, ARC-C donor)**: adding the direction stream to motion lifts ARC-C 62.2→73.0, HellaSwag 61.3→81.7, Story Cloze 83.1→96.5; adding region as well (Ours) reaches ARC-C 74.7, HellaSwag 81.7, Story Cloze 96.9. Each stream contributes; the pair and single streams can be level on HellaSwag.
- **Where to read (Sec. 6.5)**: the **middle third** of layers gives the highest reasoning mean **75.6** vs early **63.7** and late **69.5**; reading 6 mid layers (75.6) slightly beats reading all 32 (74.0) on reasoning, while all layers are best for the factual axis (63.7). Which depths are read matters more than how many.
- **Region code is shared, not factual-specific (Sec. 6.4)**: a held-out linear read of the raw layer-14 state reaches AUC **0.92** on FACTOR-expert vs **0.61** on FACTOR-wiki (the available location signal is benchmark-dependent); after compression, predicting correctness from the region alone beats the base rate on all four sets (e.g., ARC-C 81.2 vs base 75.0, Story Cloze 88.2 vs 50.0), and every code a factual answer takes is also taken by a reasoning answer — no factual-only vocabulary was learned or needed.
- **Cost**: the two added location streams are **6.7%** of the motion reader's parameters.
- **Cross-model**: on Qwen2.5-14B and Qwen3-30B-A3B the full model stays best on both axes, by **8–14 points** on reasoning and **11–16** on the factual sets (Appendix N).

## Authors & Credibility

Authors are from the Australian Institute for Machine Learning (AIML, Adelaide University), Naval Group Pacific, and the Responsible AI Research Centre. The senior author (Javen Shi) and first author (Hamed Damirchi) are the team behind "Truth as a Trajectory" (ACL 2026), which this paper directly extends — so the motion reader is re-used exactly rather than re-implemented, lending internal consistency to the comparison. The copyright line indicates AAAI 2027 acceptance, so the work has passed peer review. The empirical study is unusually careful about honest reporting: they explicitly exclude TriviaQA/HaluEval because a length heuristic solves them, they disclose single-seed vs three-seed cells, and they expose the cases (FACTOR completions) where the base model's own likelihood beats their detector.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: genuine and well-motivated. The "state-restoration trade-off" framing is the conceptual contribution; the region (VQ) + direction (normalized) instantiation is a clean, minimal mechanism, and the demonstration that it is *complementary* to motion (per-item oracle 3–21 pts above fusion) rather than redundant is a real finding.
- **Rigor**: strong on internal controls — three ablation sweeps show the gain is not from wider motion LSTMs, not from removing magnitude, and not from extra wording leakage (Appendix K). The pre-registration of the 6-layer mid window before the sweep (rather than cherry-picked post hoc) is a good sign.
- **Weak baselines / comparison gaps**: the headline baselines are only two — a single-layer linear probe and the motion-only TaT reader. The related work discusses CCS (Burns et al. 2022), Geometry of Truth (Marks & Tegmark 2024), SAEs, and depth-LSTM methods, but none are run as empirical baselines. Given that "geometry of truth" directions are the natural static-direction competitor, their absence as a measured baseline is a notable gap.
- **Cherry-picked benchmarks / honest disclosures**: TriviaQA and HaluEval are dropped because a shortest-answer heuristic scores 88.8/95.9 — disclosed, but it does mean the factual-transfer claim rests on FACTOR + VitaminC only. More importantly, on the **completion-style factual sets the base model's own answer likelihood actually beats the proposed detector** (FACTOR-wiki 57.9 vs Ours 51.9; FACTOR-news 72.3 vs 58.2; FACTOR-expert 74.6 vs 69.5). The paper explains this (a single-fact edit of a fluent completion is close to an unlikely completion) and the detector wins on the verification-style VitaminC (67.1 vs 65.1) and on reasoning (where likelihood trails by 18 points), but readers should note the factual-completion "win" is only relative to the two trained baselines, not to the model itself.
- **Single-seed results**: Table 2 (the entire factual-transfer headline) is single-seed; Table 1's main grid is a three-seed mean only on Llama-3.1-8B. Variance estimates for the factual axis and for the Qwen models are not reported.
- **Missing ablations**: the codebook size K=128 is fixed; there is no K-sweep in the main text (Appendix M reports usage but the sensitivity of the region stream to K is not shown). The choice of m=6 layers is fixed in advance and defended post hoc (Appendix O) but not cross-validated against the OOD targets it is meant to transfer to.
- **Reproducibility**: stdlib-free setup is not at issue (this is an ML paper); base models and benchmarks are all public, the VQ training recipe (straight-through, commitment, reseed) is standard and fully specified, and the window/layer choices are stated. The main reproducibility risk is the lack of released code and seeds for the 12 donor×factual cells.
- **Significance**: the conceptual claim — that *where a computation sits* and *how it moves* are separable, complementary readings of one trajectory, and that correctness (not a kind of reasoning) is legible in both — is a durable, quotable result that should generalize beyond this detector.
- **Red flags**: none severe. The honest disclosure of base-model superiority on FACTOR completions and the TriviaQA/HaluEval exclusion actually increase trust.

## Strengths

- Clean, well-motivated conceptual contribution (state-restoration trade-off) backed by a concrete, parsimonious instantiation (6.7% extra params).
- Strong, broad transfer results — one donor, 11 evaluation targets, 3 model families including an MoE.
- Genuinely complementary streams, demonstrated via ablation and a per-item oracle gap rather than asserted.
- Honest reporting: discloses which sets a length heuristic solves, which cells are single-seed, and where the base model beats the detector.
- The "region coordinate is shared between reasoning and factual errors, and the detector never needed a factual vocabulary" finding (Sec. 6.4) is a clean interpretability result.

## Limitations

- **Selection-only**: the detector ranks supplied candidates; it cannot judge whether a single free-form answer is correct — a real deployment gap for open-ended generation.
- **Per-candidate forward pass** through the residual stream is required; this does not give a cheap online correctness signal during generation.
- **Needs a labeled donor benchmark and per-model retraining**; no donor-free or model-agnostic variant is shown.
- **Performance is bounded by the base model's own correctness** — the detector reads the model's computation, it does not add knowledge.
- **Discards activation magnitude**, which may itself carry correctness signal (the authors flag this).
- **Factual-completion results are weaker than they appear**: the frozen model's own likelihood beats the detector on all three FACTOR sets; the detector only clearly wins on verification-style VitaminC and on reasoning.
- **Single-seed factual table**; no variance reported for Qwen models.
- **Late fusion is suboptimal**: the per-item oracle sits 3–21 points above the trained combination, suggesting a learned per-item gate could help — left as future work.

## Relevance

This work is directly relevant to LLM interpretability and reliable reasoning: it shows that reasoning *validity* (not just answer confidence) is linearly legible in the residual-stream trajectory, and it does so with strong cross-task and cross-model transfer — useful for building trustworthy LLM pipelines, hallucination/error detection, and for understanding what internal representations encode about sound vs. flawed reasoning.

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Can the direction/region readers be adapted to **free-form generation** (scoring a single produced answer or token-level validity during decoding) rather than only ranking supplied candidates?
- How sensitive is the region stream to **codebook size K** — is 128 a sweet spot, and does the region vocabulary grow with model scale or task diversity?
- Would adding **CCS / Geometry-of-Truth / SAE-based** directions as measured baselines (not just discussed) close or widen the gap reported here?
- The per-item oracle is 3–21 points above the trained fusion — can a **learned per-item gate** (routing to the stream most likely to be right for that item) recover most of that gap?
- Does the "region coordinate is shared between reasoning and factual errors" finding hold for **code/math/long-context** errors, or only for the short-form QA-style benchmarks tested?
- The base model's own likelihood beats the detector on FACTOR completions — can **likelihood-conditioned fusion** (combining the detector's score with the model's log-prob) dominate both on completions and on reasoning?
- How do the region codes **align across model families** (Llama vs Qwen) — is the shared reasoning/factual coordinate a universal structure or model-specific?
