When History Lies: Evaluating and Improving Tool Use under Misleading Multi-Turn Histories

Xiaoqing Wu · Xingyu Fan · Feifei Li · Wenhui Que
WeChat, Tencent Inc., Beijing
arxiv.org/abs/2608.06057
ContextPollute-Bench · Oracle-OPD
TL;DR. Structurally valid but no-longer-authoritative historical traces flip 32.1% of Qwen3-1.7B's otherwise-correct tool-use decisions on a new paired benchmark (Original / Polluted / Oracle State views, 11 gold-preserving operators over APIGen-MT airline + retail). The proposed Oracle-OPD — a frozen Oracle-conditioned teacher doing reverse-KL distillation over student-generated prefixes — reaches 87.0% BTA and transfers to clean histories, held-out tools, cross-generator contexts, BFCL, When2Call, and zero-shot HotpotQA-Distractor.
⌁

Key finding

Misleading multi-turn tool-call histories hijack agent policy, flipping 32.1% of Qwen3-1.7B's otherwise-correct decisions — a distinct, trainable bottleneck, fixed by Oracle-OPD distillation.

Why this matters

Identifies a concrete, measurable failure mode in production tool-using agents — history-induced policy hijacking — with a reusable diagnostic benchmark and a training recipe that fits ongoing reliable-agent post-training.

03

Critical edge

Restricted to two APIGen-MT domains (airline, retail); whether the 11 synthetic operators match the distribution of misleading traces in real production logs is unaddressed — ecological validity is the main open question.

Core contributions

  1. Controlled diagnosis. Paired Original / Polluted / Oracle-State views with a preserved gold action isolate history-induced policy hijacking from generic task difficulty or error repair.
  2. ContextPollute-Bench. 11 gold-preserving operators across Decision-State, Entity-Binding, Interface-Execution; 97.0% gold correctness, 99.0% Oracle sufficiency, 93.7% human / 91.6% Claude joint acceptance.
  3. Oracle-OPD. Reliable Oracle-State teacher gives token-level reverse-KL soft targets over on-policy student prefixes — student-only deployment.
  4. Breadth of transfer. Clean histories, held-out tools, cross-generator contexts, BFCL, When2Call, zero-shot HotpotQA-Distractor.

Method in four steps

STEP 01

Triple view. Original · Polluted · Oracle State share system, tools, request & gold action a*.

STEP 02

Operators. 11 validators preserve source order, keep latest request final, verify gold action unchanged.

STEP 03

Objective. Student rolls out prefix on Polluted input; reverse-KL to teacher on Oracle input over top-16 tokens, 4 rollouts.

STEP 04

Deploy. Only the distilled student + ordinary history. No teacher, no Oracle view at inference.

Results & highlights

  • 32.1% of Original-correct examples flip under pollution (40.5% for Answer); Oracle-vs-Polluted gap persists at 32B.
  • Corrupted-literal adoption collapses from 65.8% → 0.4% after Oracle-OPD on format-drift.
  • Factorized gains: Oracle view +0.114, soft targets +0.027, on-policy prefix +0.020 BTA.
  • Prompt-only "ignore history" control: 0.472 → 0.485 — vs 0.870 learned.
