Decomposed Entailment for Factuality Checking & Hallucination Detection

Achir Oukelmoun · Nasredine Semmar · Gaël De Chalendar — CEA LIST NANO INNOV

HallDetect FEDscore 4-bit / consumer GPU DeBERTa-v3 NLI · 435M reference-free claim→span audit
TL;DR HallDetect decomposes a response into atomic claims with a 4-bit LLM, verifies each claim against a multi-scale library of ≤31 source chunks using a 435M DeBERTa-v3 NLI engine in a contrastive entailment-minus-contradiction formulation, and aggregates with an asymmetric geometric mean where one confidently contradicted claim flags the whole response.

Key finding

Decompose responses into atomic claims, verify each via contrastive multi-scale NLI under a strict 4-bit matched budget — beats generative CoT judges while staying stable across backbones.

Key metric

0.771 F1
▲ +0.182 vs LLM-CoT (0.589)
QAGS-CNN/DM, Gemma-2-9B. Cross-backbone envelope 0.734–0.771 vs CoT's 0.321–0.589 swing.

Why this matters

Decomposed NLI verification, multi-scale contrastive scoring and claim→span audit trails are exactly the factuality guardrails trustworthy LLM deployments need — frugal enough for local monitoring.

The pipeline · four phases

01
Extract
4-bit LLM splits the short response (≤512 tok) into atomic claims, copy-faithful — assertions preserved verbatim.
02
Chunk
Source partitioned at dyadic granularities m∈{1,2,4,8,16} → ≤31 overlapping chunks. Source never enters generation.
03
Verify
DeBERTa-v3 (435M) scores each claim: max-entail − max-contrad across chunks, single forward pass each.
04
Aggregate
Geometric mean (FEDscore). Any score ≤ 0 → response flagged. One contradicted claim sinks the verdict.

Strengths

  • Strict matched-budget protocol (uniform 4-bit, single consumer GPU) isolates design from raw capacity.
  • Decouples one short generative pass from a <1 GB verifier; long source never enters autoregressive context.
  • Cross-backbone F1 envelope 0.734–0.771 vs CoT's 0.321–0.589 — evidence that matters for deployment.
  • Claim→evidence-span trail distinguishes contradicted from unsupported, enabling closed-loop regeneration.

Limitations

  • No MiniCheck / SummaC / QAFactEval head-to-head — the priority comparison the authors themselves flag.
  • Confounded ablation: NLI-Only differs in both decomposition and chunking, so effects can't be isolated.
  • No PR curves, AUROC, or threshold sweep — only fixed-0.5 F1 on single deterministic runs.
  • No wall-clock or energy numbers despite frugality being the headline; cost is architectural only.

▲ Critical edge

The missing head-to-head against MiniCheck — the DeBERTa-based compact fact-checker the authors themselves call "the single most important next experiment" — leaves practical superiority over an off-the-shelf detector unproven. Add to that: single runs with no significance, no threshold-free metrics, and an asymmetric aggregator tuned toward recall on high-base-rate datasets.
