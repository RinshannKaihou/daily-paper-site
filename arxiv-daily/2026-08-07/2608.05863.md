> **一句话总结：** 针对现代模型中异构的注意力记忆（稠密 KV、潜在 KV、稀疏选择器、循环状态），本文提出一套"带类型误差度量"的运行时可观测性契约——只有度量匹配时才允许组合——并通过机器判定 certified / partially certified / empirical 三级强度；在 DeepSeek-V4 服务栈上以 packed 压缩 KV 原型定位了一次静默腐败，并在 CUDA graphs 下以融合探针达到 1/28 层 -0.94% 吞吐（噪声地板内）。

> **TL;DR:** The paper casts heterogeneous attention memory (dense KV, latent/MLA, sparse selectors, recurrent state) as three operators (update/select/read), gives each a typed local error contract `e_out ≤ a·e_in + b` with failure budget `δ`, and composes them into a request-level risk ledger whose composition rule is only defined when metrics match. Across 6 model configurations / 5 architecture families (e.g. Qwen2.5-7B, Llama-3.1-8B, DeepSeek-V2-Lite, GLM-5.2, V4-Flash-FP8, Kimi-Linear-48B), replaying 12.4M entry reads on 21 sparse-selection layers yields 80.1% retention at a vacuous per-step bound of 1.54, or TV ≤ 0.86 at 29.3% retention; under CUDA graphs the fused 1-of-28-layer always-on probe costs −0.94% throughput / −0.14% TTFT P99 (inside noise floor); on a served DeepSeek-V4 stack a packed compressed-KV prototype's silent retrieval regression (1.000 → 0.500) is discriminated to slot-reuse without invalidation, not quantization.

## 核心贡献

- **带类型的注意力记忆契约**：把任意注意力记忆归约为三个算子（update / select / read），每个阶段给出局部契约 `e_out ≤ a·e_in + b` 及失败预算 `δ`；误差度量被升格为类型的组成部分，组合 `(a2,b2)∘(a1,b1)` 只在度量匹配时才有定义。该类型检查在论文自身链条上拒绝了非法组合（把无量纲的 storage 相对见证加到概率度量 attention-TV 上），并在 Lean 层面让非法组合无法通过 elaboration。
- **机器裁决的三级强度体系**：certified / partially certified / empirical 三级，由机器（Lean 证明 + 数值 claim guard）决定而非由措辞裁定；未注册的度量是硬错误而非静默降级；组合继承最弱一级——任何含 empirical 段的链条都不得对外宣称为 certified。
- **可执行的请求级风险账本**：把逐阶段契约变成请求级风险预算——deterministic 证书 `δ=0`，概率证书从伸缩级数 `δ_i = δ_req / ((i+1)(i+2))` 中支取，在未知请求长度下始终 sound；预算规则是 machine-checked 定理。八个并发账户最大支出 `Σδ = 0.009984 ≤ δ_req = 0.01`，零跨账户读取。
- **始终在线的融合探针**：在 CUDA graphs 捕获下，对声明的 1/28 层用融合 Triton meter 观测，单次调用比朴素版本便宜 24.2×，max relative error 9.3e-7；吞吐 −0.94%、TTFT P99 −0.14%，均在 per-setting 噪声地板内。
- **DeepSeek-V4 服务栈上的诊断案例**：用一个 packed 压缩 KV 原型把一次被 radix caching 掩盖的 95 点检索崩溃，机器裁决式因果对照实验定位为"slot 重用而无失效调用"（generation staleness）而非量化（量化器本征相对误差 0.0245，不可达观测值）；零驱逐+身份隔离配置在三轮独立 12-doc 实验中精确恢复 1.000。
- **完全可复现的工件链**：度量数学、acceptance gate、每个数字背后的冻结 run artifact、图表生成器及其 guard、完整 Lean 开发（含实验裁决 calculus）以 Apache-2.0 发布于 [github.com/metask-ai/witprobe-attention-memory](https://github.com/metask-ai/witprobe-attention-memory)；任意 mismatch 让 claim guard 使构建失败。

## Key Contributions

- **Typed observability contracts for attention memory.** Any attention memory is written as three operators (update / select / read); each stage gets a local contract `e_out ≤ a·e_in + b` with explicit failure budget `δ`. The error metric is part of the contract's *type*, so composition is only defined when metrics match — a discipline that rejected the authors' own first composed chain (a dimensionless storage ratio summed into attention total-variation) and is enforced twice (runtime checker + Lean elaboration).
- **A machine-decided three-tier strength system.** Every emitted metric is registered as `certified`, `partially certified`, or `empirical` together with its justification; an unregistered metric is a hard error. Composition inherits the weakest tier, so an "end-to-end certified" claim is something the system refuses to print unless every stage earns it.
- **An executable request-level risk ledger.** Per-stage contracts become operational through a ledger with three rules: empirical objects are excluded; deterministic certificates enter at `δ=0`; probabilistic certificates draw from a telescoping budget `δ_i = δ_req/((i+1)(i+2))` that is sound at unknown request length. Budget rules are machine-checked theorems; under 8-way concurrency every account independently satisfies its budget with zero foreign-owned reads.
- **Affordable always-on probing under CUDA graphs.** A fused Triton meter (24.2× cheaper per invocation, 9.3e-7 max relative error) makes a declared 1-of-28-layer observation sit at −0.94% throughput / −0.14% TTFT P99, inside the per-setting noise floor — justifying the word "always-on".
- **A production diagnosis case study on DeepSeek-V4.** A packed compressed-KV prototype's silent retrieval regression (95-point collapse that radix caching had been masking) is discriminated down to slot reuse without invalidation rather than quantization (the quantizer's intrinsic relative error is 0.0245, unreachable from observed readings). Two of the authors' own confounded inferences were rejected by the adjudication calculus.
- **Reproducible artifact chain.** All measurement math, acceptance gates, frozen run artifacts behind every number, figure/claim generators with guards, and the complete Lean development are released under Apache-2.0; a claim guard fails the build on any manuscript-vs-artifact mismatch.

## Methodology

The method rests on four concerns cleanly separated in the platform implementation: (1) a **registry** declaring per-architecture injection points; (2) **adapters** that extract tensors and build validity masks; (3) **meters** holding the architecture-independent mathematics; (4) a **runtime** keying every accumulator by `(owner, layer, path)` and always emitting a coverage record. Probes are injected into unmodified serving code by a line-anchored source injector with asserted hit counts, and byte-identical restore is verified against SHA-256 baselines.

The core formal object is a **local contract** `(a, b, δ)` with `e_out ≤ a·e_in + b` holding except with probability `δ`. Series composition is `(a2,b2)∘(a1,b1) = (a2·a1, a2·b1 + b2)` and per-stage budgets telescope to `Σ_i δ_i ≤ δ_req`. The algebra is elementary; the non-elementary part is that every `a, b, δ` must be computable from quantities the serving stack actually has at runtime, and the resulting number must carry an honest strength label. The paper proves three concrete local lemmas: (Lemma 1) pooling is an attenuator with `a = ‖w‖_2 ≤ 1` under an ℓ2 aggregation caliber or `a = 1` under a max caliber — both must be reported; (Lemma 2) rank-prefix selection is additive, not amplifying, with `(a_S, b_S) = (1, ½(m_out + m_in))` — reframing strict top-k certification as the wrong question; (Lemma 3) a sampling sentinel has detection latency `n(δ) = ⌈log δ / log q⌉` rounds for a tunable budget.

The composed request-level claim is enforced by a **ledger** with telescoping budget; an **anytime-valid e-process** sentinel `g_e = exp(λ_e(W_e − m_e) − λ_e² C_e / 8)` runs alongside on the same objects. A **fused Triton meter** enables observation under CUDA graph capture (where naive per-call sampling silently vanishes). All proofs of the constants the implementation uses are machine-checked in Lean/mathlib over a finite product space — no measure-theoretic axioms beyond the standard three.

## Results & Highlights

- **Coverage:** 8 probe rows over 6 model configurations spanning 5 architecture families (Qwen2.5-7B GQA, Llama-3.1-8B GQA, DeepSeek-V2-Lite MLA, GLM-5.2-W4AFP8 MLA+DSA, V4-Flash-FP8 MLA+entry, Kimi-Linear-48B KDA+MLA), all past the (coverage → magnitude → soundness) acceptance gate. Top-1 rank-prefix stability is certified for **99.26%** of rows on GLM and **99.40%** on V4-Flash page-level top-512, with zero flips inside the certified region.
- **Request-level ledger (offline replay):** Over **12.4M entry reads** across the 21 sparse-selection layers, the retention/bound trade-off is **80.1% retention at a vacuous per-step TV bound of 1.54**, or **TV ≤ 0.86 at 29.3% retention**. Under 8-way concurrency with per-request telescoping budgets at `δ_req = 0.01`, the **maximum spend is `Σδ = 0.009984 ≤ δ_req`** with zero foreign-owned reads across 8,228,864 checks.
- **Online shadow ledger:** Over **198,163,408 entry events** across 24 serial requests, every selected-page entry at every decode step is checked online; median per-request retention is 0.334 with a hypothetical page-in of 5.4 GiB/request pricing what real fallback would move.
- **Certified-object upgrade:** Switching the certified object from worst-case norm to the exact conditional distribution of stochastic rounding raises the pre-authorization rate from **7.8% to 67.5% (8.7×)** at the same threshold, with **0 of 1,276,631 authorized entries violating their radius**. The centred oscillation bound `TV ≤ tanh(osc/4)` moves non-vacuous layers from **0/43 to 43/43**.
- **Always-on probe overhead (the load-bearing claim):** Under CUDA graphs the fused 1-of-28-layer meter costs **−0.94% throughput and −0.14% TTFT P99**, both inside the per-setting noise floor; full 28-layer coverage remains a diagnostic mode at **−10.16%**. Graphs-off sampling: 1/64 → −3.9% throughput / +10.5% TTFT P99 (inside noise); 1/16 → −19.6% / +140.8% (outside).
- **e-process sentinel:** Over 120,661 authorized-entry factors spanning all requests, the global anytime-valid process satisfies **`sup_t log M_t = −0.82 ≪ ln(1/δ_e) = 4.61`** (final value −76,819), an uncontradicted stochastic-rounding radius assumption on live traffic.
- **DeepSeek-V4 diagnosis:** A 95-point retrieval collapse (radix caching masking it) is discriminated to slot reuse without invalidation: the **eviction-free, identity-isolated regime scores 1.000 across three independent 12-doc rounds**, while every observed failure occurs in an eviction or slot-reuse regime (e.g. drops to **0.500 with every validity flag green**); unconditional write-site invalidation restores accuracy with a two-sided causal confirmation (1.000 vs 0.200 under 4-way concurrency).
- **Path-contract matrix:** Of six serving paths, speculative decoding passes (1.000 on both arms, both seeds) after the adjudication chain attributed its historical residual to eviction; prefill/decode disaggregation's packed arm returns **0.000 with zero transport or server errors** because the transfer engine moves bytes by the pool-layout contract the packed stage redefines — "structurally green and semantically wrong".
- **Packed INT6 pool benefit:** A real packed replacement of the 4× pool yields a measured **2,108 MiB/GPU HBM reduction** against a tensor-derived byte model; teacher-forced paired perplexity at parity reads **1.3496 vs 1.3495 (−0.00%)** across 98,568 tokens, perplexity-neutral.
- **Corruption sentinel:** 144 baseline verification passes with zero false alarms gives a one-sided 95% upper bound of **2.06%**; in the injection experiment 2 of 4 corrupted slots were located, consistent with the with-replacement model (per-block detection probability 0.881, P(≤2 of 4) = 0.072). Detection latency to 99% confidence for one slot: 148/74/37/19 rounds at `r = 32/64/128/256`.

## Authors & Credibility

The four authors (Fanzhe Wei, Li Liu, Ziyang Wang, Chenyu Wang) are all listed under "Metask Lab" with personal Gmail addresses — there is **no recognized academic or industrial research institution affiliation**, and no prior publication track record is cited, which makes independent credibility assessment difficult. Credibility is instead anchored entirely in the engineering rigor of the artifact: every number is regenerated from a frozen run artifact by a canon generator, a claim guard fails the build on any manuscript-vs-artifact numeric mismatch (checking numeric strings, not semantics, so calibers travel with the artifacts), and the Lean/mathlib proofs are machine-checked over a finite product space. The experiments use a realistic serving stack (8×H200 143 GB on sglang 0.5.13.post1; DeepSeek-V4-Flash-FP8 at tp=8/ep=8 with 65,536-token context). The paper's unusual intellectual honesty — reporting its own caught bugs, labelling vacuous bounds as vacuous, withholding cost-benefit claims — is itself a credibility signal, though it does not substitute for institutional reproducibility.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** High and genuine on the framing axis. Treating the *error metric as part of the contract type* — so that an illegal dimensionless-ratio-into-probability-mass composition fails at Lean elaboration — is a real idea the paper shows doing work (it rejected the authors' own first chain). The algebra itself (affine composition + union bound; discrete Grönwall) is honestly flagged as elementary; the contribution is the typed discipline and machine-decided tiers. This is differentiated from prior runtime-certificate work (KIVI, KVQuant, GEAR) which fixes one memory object (dense post-RoPE KV) and derives one bound for it, whereas this paper asks what remains true when the object itself changes.
- **Rigor:** Strongest aspect. Acceptance gates (coverage → magnitude → soundness) are reported as catching three concrete silent-failure classes (probe overwriting its own per-layer state; reading uninitialised device memory; attaching to a write path the model never takes — each producing "clean exit, wrong number"). The paper publishes its own caught bugs (two-sided Chernoff combined with min instead of max; deterministic shift from top-of-range clamping that isn't random; a `log 0` branch clamped into fake perfection). The Lean development and the finite-product-space argument (no measure theory because the randomness is finitely many discrete draws) is a defensible methodological choice.
- **Reproducibility:** Mixed and honestly disclosed. The artifact chain (math, gates, frozen run artifacts, figure generators, Lean dev) is Apache-2.0 and regenerates every number by one command — strong. But the **architecture-specific injection implementation (hook anchors, adapters, kernels) is private**, so a serving-level rerun on a new model requires the platform; the paper states this plainly. The path-contract matrix is explicitly "one model, one serving stack version, small retrieval batteries, largely single-seed" — a taxonomy demonstration, not production coverage.
- **Significance:** The most transferable finding is the systems lesson that **a replaced KV representation redefines coordinate, generation, and materialization semantics** — surfaced concretely in the PD-disaggregation row returning 0.000 with zero transport errors. For practitioners running MLA / sparse-attention / linear-attention models in production, this is a real diagnostic category.
- **Red flags / overclaiming checks:**
  - **The "request-level risk ledger" is largely scaffolding, not a useful end-to-end bound today.** The summed per-layer TV budget over 43 layers is **36.3 — explicitly vacuous** — because per-layer distributions are distinct objects and no inter-layer bridge is claimed. So the headline "request-level risk budget" is sound but currently produces no non-trivial end-to-end number; the useful per-request claims are local (entry-level certificate coverage).
  - **Sample sizes for output-quality claims are tiny.** Greedy-decoding stability across two server restarts already disagrees on 2 of 8 prompts, and the policy/dithered arms' differences from baseline are described as "consistent with a protective effect rather than demonstrating one" at n=8. Honest, but the reader should not treat the quality story as evidence.
  - **The cost-benefit story is explicitly withheld.** The staged consumer runs at 0.884× native throughput / 1.131× cost per output token; the final-table round shows 26.8 vs 173.4 output tokens/s against native on a 32-request benchmark with enlarged rings erasing the memory saving at that caliber; and **no end-to-end cost benefit is claimed anywhere in the paper**. The packed INT6 2,108 MiB/GPU reduction is a structural number, not a demonstrated serving win, since eviction-safe consumption remains open.
  - **The eviction mediator remains open.** The diagnosis concludes "every observed failure occurs in an eviction or slot-reuse regime" but "eviction alone does not force failure (one 91-eviction control run scores 1.000)", and the microscopic mediator is honestly recorded as open after exhausting every instrumented channel. So the headline "localizes a silent corruption to a precise structural boundary" is a *boundary*, not a *mechanism*.
  - **No institutional affiliation.** "Metask Lab" with personal Gmail addresses is unusual for a paper of this engineering depth; there is no way for the reader to verify author track record.

## Strengths

- **The type discipline genuinely does work**, and the paper proves it by showing the checker rejecting the authors' own illegal composition (a relative-witness storage term summed into an attention-TV selection term). This is the kind of self-application that makes the method credible.
- **Unusual epistemic honesty.** Vacuous bounds are labelled vacuous; the open eviction mediator is named as open; cost-benefit claims are withheld; own bugs are published. The three-tier system is designed precisely so that hedged prose cannot smuggle in a certified claim.
- **Real production stack and real failure.** Motivated by a dated production incident (2026-06-25 silent corruption) and evaluated on a served DeepSeek-V4-Flash-FP8 stack at tp=8 — not a toy benchmark.
- **Complete artifact release** with Lean proofs, claim guards, and a one-command regeneration of every number — a strong reproducibility posture for the artifact surface it does release.
- **The path-contract taxonomy** (pass / fail-method / blocked-upstream / partial / not-measured) generated mechanically from run artifacts is a useful template for any team bringing a new KV representation into a serving stack.

## Limitations

- **No inter-layer bridge.** Per-layer attention distributions are distinct objects; the request-level summed TV budget is vacuous (36.3), and the paper claims no end-to-end output-distance bound. This is the central boundary of the work and is explicitly flagged as "the next theorem the type system is waiting for".
- **The certified eviction-free regime is narrow.** Exactness (1.000 across three 12-doc rounds) holds only at zero eviction with identity isolation; "zero eviction is necessary but not sufficient" — a retrieval battery scored 0.917 with zero evictions because freed logical slots were reused while their ring pages stayed resident.
- **Architecture-specific injection code is private.** Serving-level reruns require the platform; the verifiable surface is the artifact-and-guard chain, not a rerun claim.
- **Sample sizes are small and single-stack.** Path-contract matrix is one model, one sglang version, small retrieval batteries, largely single-seed. Quality claims rest on 8 prompts.
- **Overhead calibers must not be conflated.** Sampled-probe overhead is measured with CUDA graphs disabled (only relative deltas meaningful); the always-on claim rests on a single declared layer of 28 (full coverage is −10.16%, a diagnostic mode).
- **Recurrent-state aggregate loses channel identity**, so an aggregate `E[log a] < 0` does not imply per-channel contraction; no certificate is claimed there.
- **The sentinel's with-replacement model** matches the implementation but is looser than a without-replacement (hypergeometric) model; the paper explicitly corrected an earlier optimistic hypergeometric analysis.
- **Empirical bridge assumption.** The selector-score to attention-mass ratio is measured on truncation sets and applied to perturbation swap sets — a homogeneity assumption the tier system prices in by holding the composed chain at empirical.

## Relevance

这篇论文对注意力记忆（含 MLA 潜在缓存、稀疏选择器、线性注意力状态）给出了"带类型"的运行时可观测性契约与请求级风险账本，直接落在 LLM 推理服务 / KV 缓存压缩 / 模型可观测性这一研究方向上；其机器裁决的 certified / partially certified / empirical 三级强度体系，对任何在服务栈中引入新的压缩或结构化注意力表示（如 Native Sparse Attention、MLA、GLA）的工程实践都是直接可迁移的诊断与认证范式。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- **Inter-layer bridge:** Is there any non-trivial theorem that composes per-layer attention-TV bounds across depth into an output-distance bound, or is the boundary fundamental (e.g. each layer's attention is a genuinely different distribution)? The paper frames this as the next theorem the type system is waiting for.
- **Eviction mediator:** What is the microscopic mechanism by which slot reuse (with no invalidation call) corrupts retrieval — selection metadata, index semantics, or their timing state? Can a `(slot, generation)` premise be enforced cheaply enough to make eviction-safe consumption certifiable rather than just observable?
- **Cost-benefit closure:** At what eviction rate / context length does the packed INT6 pool's 2,108 MiB/GPU reduction translate into a non-vacuous throughput win after accounting for the staged consumer's 0.884× throughput penalty and the enlarged rings at the working-set-ring caliber?
- **Generalisation of the typed-contract approach:** Does the same metric-as-type discipline catch analogous illegal compositions in other heterogeneous pipelines — e.g. MoE expert routing, speculative-decoding draft verification, or retrieval-augmented generation where the "memory" is an external index?
- **Always-on full coverage:** Can the fused-meter approach be extended from 1 declared layer to all 28 layers while staying inside the noise floor, or is full coverage fundamentally a diagnostic-only mode? What is the theoretical minimum per-layer overhead under CUDA graph capture?
