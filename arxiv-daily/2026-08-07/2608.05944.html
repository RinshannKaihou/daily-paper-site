Full-fine-tuning Qwen3-32B on 16× NVIDIA B300: board-power telemetry — not GPU utilization — honestly signals compute, starvation, and deadlock.

Why this matters

Critical edge

MMethodology

An operational field report, not an algorithmic paper. The method is the measurement.

16× NVIDIA B300 SXM6 AC (2 nodes × 8; 1100 W TGP, 275 GB HBM each), InfiniBand NDR + GPUDirect RDMA inter-node, NVLink intra-node. NGC container: PyTorch 2.12, transformers 5.12, native FSDP. Qwen3-32B sharded FSDP FULL_SHARD (ZeRO-3) across 16 GPUs (~32.8 GB model states/GPU; 82 GB live footprint, ~30% of HBM).

  • Training: seq 2048, micro-batch 1, grad-accum 8, global batch 128 = 262,144 tokens/step, ~3,854 steps/epoch, AdamW + cosine warmup, lr 1e-5, bf16.
  • Dataset: Nemotron-Personas-Korea — 1,000,000 records, ~1.01B tok/epoch (~4 GB, page-cache resident vs 4 TB/node RAM).
  • Telemetry: board-power sampled at 1 Hz, correlated with NCCL flight-recorder dumps to build the triage taxonomy.

Honest negative result — local cache buys zero throughput at this scale

  • Controlled 16-GPU, 300-step A/B (with drop_caches): NFS cold = 52.8k, NFS warm = 53.0k, pretokenized node-local cache = 53.0k tok/s — all within ~0.5%.
  • Reason: the ~4 GB corpus is page-cache resident against 4 TB/node RAM (1000× headroom); even a true cold miss reads at 1.31 GB/s vs a ~5 s/step FSDP step.
  • Self-correction: the earlier “52k→21k collapse = storage bottleneck” framing was imprecise — the real bottleneck was single-threaded tokenization + NFS contention, not the medium.

The deadlock, quantified

  • Per-rank packed-block spread: 53 blocks (min 30,835 on rank 3 / max 30,888 on rank 11) = 0.17% of per-rank workload ≈ 7 optimizer steps.
  • Rank 3 exited the loop ~7 steps early and entered the dist.barrier() save while the other 15 ranks were still in backward reduce_scatter — two collectives at the same wire slot, infinite hang, util pinned at 100%.
  • Hazard is invisible to smoke testing: max_steps=N exits before the shortest rank exhausts its data — “probability zero on every prefix, one at the boundary.”

Hardening ROI

  • evenfix: all_reduce(MIN) of block counts before the loop; drops ≤53 blocks/rank ≈ 0.085% of tokens.
  • Preflight gate: 6 checks across both nodes in 2.71 s, hard-wired into the launcher; external watcher detects completion/crash/hang/throughput-floor.
  • Post-fix 16-GPU epoch: 3,854 steps, rc=0 first attempt, loss 0.67, zero deadlock traces, 62 GB checkpoint that loads and generates fluent in-domain text.
  • Bonus: with 82 GB live on a 275 GB GPU, gradient checkpointing bought nothing and cost ~1.6× throughput (33k vs 53k tok/s).

+Strengths

  • Exceptional intellectual honesty: explicit “we claim no new algorithm”, named prior art for every artifact, public self-correction, and a Threats-to-Validity section bounding every claim.
  • Calibrated, reproducible-by-method numbers: absolute B300 wattage bands, full storage breakdown, GPU·h conservation as a positive control, per-incident cost accounting.
  • The deadlock case study is a genuinely useful worked example — exact failure shape, exact 53-block imbalance, why smoke tests miss it, why Join is unavailable for FSDP.
  • The 2.71 s preflight gate and external watcher are immediately adoptable primitives that turn multi-hour silent failures into instant rejections.
  • Clean positive control: the post-fix epoch ran end-to-end on the first attempt with zero deadlock traces.

–Limitations

  • Small scale (16 GPU / 2 nodes): efficiency should not be extrapolated past the two-node IB boundary; no at-scale reliability lessons follow.
  • Single model (Qwen3-32B dense) and single dataset; generality is argued structurally, not demonstrated across models, modalities, or languages.
  • Power thresholds are B300/firmware-specific — will not transfer to A100/H100/H200/B200 without re-calibration.
  • The local-cache negative result is scale-conditional: it inverts once the dataset exceeds RAM.
  • Artifacts are “available upon reasonable request”, not publicly released; the deadlock is reproducible in principle but needs B300 hardware most readers lack.
  • Some cost numbers are reconstructed from logs rather than measured in a controlled fashion (flagged as such).
