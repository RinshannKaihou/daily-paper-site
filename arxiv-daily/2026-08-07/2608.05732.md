> **一句话总结：** CircuitSteer 用稀疏自编码器(SAE)在多层之间基于"特征共激活 + 解码器方向几何对齐"构建目标行为的跨层特征流子电路，再用由解码器方向平均合成的稠密向量在多个电路层同时注入，在 Gemma-2-2B / Llama-3.1-8B / Qwen3.5-27B 的毒性、情绪、奉承、拒答四个任务上是唯一能在保持流畅度(PPL≤1.5 且 ∆≥0.02)的有效区间内对所有 8 个模型-数据组合都有可操作点的方法。

> **TL;DR:** CircuitSteer is a training-free multi-layer activation-steering method that selects cross-layer SAE feature circuits by jointly requiring feature co-activation and geometric alignment (decoder-direction cosine) of SAE features, then injects decoder-direction-averaged dense vectors at every circuit layer. Across RealToxicityPrompts, Jigsaw, Emotion, and Sycophancy on Gemma-2-2B and Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct it is the only method of nine (including CAA, RepE, ITI, LoReFT, SpARE, SAE-SSV) with a valid fluency-preserving operating point on all 8 model–dataset cells; e.g. on Llama–RTP it reaches ∆=0.128 at PPL=0.51 vs. SAE-SSV ∆=0.125 at PPL=0.34, and on Gemma–Sycophancy it is the sole method with a valid point (∆=0.057) where every baseline dashes. Ablations confirm geometric alignment is load-bearing, and it generalizes to Qwen3.5-27B with no hyperparameter re-tuning.

## 核心贡献

- 提出 **CircuitSteer**，一个无训练(training-free)的多层激活导引框架：用稀疏自编码器(SAE)把残差流分解为稀疏的单语义特征，在多层之间通过"特征共激活"和"解码器方向的几何对齐(cosine 相似度)"两条联合判据构造**特征流电路(feature flow circuit)**，从而定位编码目标行为的跨层子电路。
- 设计**对比特异性评分** S(e)=P(e|D+)−P(e|D−)，从全图中筛出"只在目标行为上激活、不在对比集上激活"的边，把噪声边剔除，得到行为专属的子电路 Ctarget。
- 提出**稠密向量合成 + 多点同步干预**：在每个电路层把该层参与电路的所有特征的解码器方向取平均得到稠密导引向量 v_l，然后在所有电路层用单一系数 λ 同时注入 h′_l = h_l + λ·v_l，使跨层方向位移相互强化而非被后续层"抵消回"原轨迹。
- 用消融实验(NoGeo / NegativeAlign / RandomSAE / SingleLayer)证明"几何对齐"是稳定多层导引的**必要条件**而非可有可无的优化——去掉对齐判据会在 Sycophancy 等语义微妙行为上彻底失效。
- 在 4 个行为基准 × 2 个模型族共 8 个配置上，对 8 个基线进行评测：CircuitSteer 是**唯一一个在所有 8 个配置都有(流畅度有效、行为降幅达标)可操作点**的方法，所有竞争方法都至少有一个配置因流畅度崩塌或降幅低于阈值而无有效点。

## Key Contributions

- **CircuitSteer**: a training-free multi-layer steering framework that identifies behavior-specific SAE circuits via joint feature co-activation and geometric alignment of decoder directions, eliminating destructive interference and enabling fluency-preserving behavioral control without weight modification.
- **Empirical demonstration that geometric alignment is necessary**, not merely beneficial: removing the alignment condition (NoGeo), reversing it (NegativeAlign, anti-aligned edges), or replacing the trained SAE with a random projection (RandomSAE) all collapse validity — most starkly on Gemma–Sycophancy, where only the full method retains a fluency-valid operating point.
- **Multi-point dense-vector intervention design**: averaging decoder directions per circuit layer decouples intervention magnitude from circuit size, so a single global coefficient λ has consistent meaning regardless of how many features are selected per layer; this is what lets the residual stream be reinforced across depth instead of recovering toward the unsteered trajectory.
- **Comprehensive benchmarking against eight baselines** (Prompt, single/multi-layer CAA, RepE, ITI, LoReFT, SpARE, SAE-SSV) on four behavioral datasets and two model families (Gemma-2-2B, Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct), plus a zero-re-tuning transfer to Qwen3.5-27B and a separate AdvBench refusal-steering study.

## Methodology

CircuitSteer has three offline stages (Figure 2) and one inference-time step, all training-free (no gradients, no weight updates; pre-trained SAEs are used off-the-shelf via SAELens).

**A. SAE decomposition and graph construction.** For each SAE-equipped layer l the residual stream h_l is encoded to sparse features c_l = JumpReLU(W_enc h_l + b_enc); each feature i has a decoder direction d_{l,i} = W_dec[:,i] ∈ R^{d_model}. A directed cross-layer graph C=(V,E) is built where an edge (l,i)→(l+1,j) is added for input x iff (1) **co-activation** c_{l,i}(x) > τ_act AND c_{l+1,j}(x) > τ_act, and (2) **geometric alignment** cosine(d_{l,i}, d_{l+1,j}) > τ_sim (Eq. 2–3). The geometric criterion addresses the well-documented failure mode that decoder directions for "the same" concept at different layers are often misaligned and combine destructively.

**B. Contrastive circuit discovery.** Edge frequencies are estimated on a target set D+ (e.g. toxic prompts) and a contrastive set D− (e.g. benign prompts), and the **contrastive specificity score** S((l,i)→(l+1,j)) = P(·|D+) − P(·|D−) (Eq. 4) is computed; edges with S > τ_diff are retained and the top-K taken, isolating the behavior-specific subcircuit Ctarget.

**C. Dense vector synthesis and intervention.** For each layer l in the subcircuit, collect all features that touch a retained edge (origin or destination) into F_l (Eq. 5) and synthesize a dense steering vector v_l = (1/|F_l|) Σ_{i∈F_l} d_{l,i} (Eq. 6). Averaging — not summing — decouples magnitude from circuit size so the global coefficient λ behaves consistently. At inference the model is intervened simultaneously at every layer with non-empty F_l via h′_l = h_l + λ v_l.

**Hyperparameters.** τ_act=1.5, τ_sim=0.10, τ_diff=0.05, top-K=30 edges; λ swept over {−4,…,2}; the reported operating point is argmax ∆ within 0.01 ≤ PPL ≤ 1.5. SAE suites: Gemma-Scope (width 16k, layers {6,12,18,24}) and Llama-Scope (width 32k, layers {3,8,16,24,29}). The authors are careful to note the co-activation and alignment criteria are *candidate indicators that approximate* cross-layer causal influence rather than establish it; the causal evidence comes from the component ablations.

**Computational cost.** Discovery is one-time/offline (worst-case O(N² d_model) per layer pair, but SAE sparsity bounds the effective per-input candidate set by O(L0²) ≪ N²). Inference cost is O(L′·d_model) extra FLOPs/token — same form and order as CAA, one precomputed vector per intervened layer.

## Results & Highlights

- **Full coverage on all 8 model–dataset cells (Table 1).** Under the validity window 0.01 ≤ PPL ≤ 1.5 with ∆ ≥ 0.02, CircuitSteer is the **only** method with a valid operating point on every cell; every competing method dashes (fails the window) on at least one cell. One-sided 95% bootstrap lower bounds confirm its reductions are significant on **7 of 8** configurations (all but Llama–Jigsaw).
- **Llama-3.1-8B, RealToxicityPrompts:** CircuitSteer ∆=0.113-equivalent lead with ∆=**0.128** at PPL=0.51, the best in the column; SAE-SSV reaches ∆=0.125 at PPL=0.34 and CAA-multi 0.114 at PPL=0.83.
- **Gemma-2-2B, Sycophancy:** CircuitSteer ∆=**0.057** at PPL=1.04 — the **only** method with any valid operating point; every baseline (CAA, RepE, ITI, LoReFT, SpARE, SAE-SSV) dashes because their ∆ is ≤ 0 or their PPL leaves the window.
- **Gemma-2-2B, Jigsaw:** CircuitSteer ∆=**0.072** at PPL=0.93, the best in column; the next-best valid point is ITI ∆=0.027 at PPL=1.04.
- **Refusal steering (AdvBench, Llama-3.1-8B, Appendix E, Table 10):** decision-point circuit discovery drops the refusal rate from **89% → 0%** at λ=−3 (Median PPL 35.7, normalized 0.89), whereas single-layer CAA at the same coefficient leaves refusal virtually intact — corroborating that refusal is structurally shallow but that single-layer CAA cannot reach the refusal mechanism.
- **Core-capability preservation (Appendix K):** under strong steering λ=−3, MMLU accuracy moves only **−0.03** on both Gemma (0.46→0.43) and Llama (0.672→0.641); GSM8K moves 0.236→0.220 (Gemma) and 0.824→0.800 (Llama) — a multi-layer intervention strong enough to flip toxicity/emotion/sycophancy/refusal leaves knowledge and chain-of-thought reasoning intact.
- **Compact circuit (Table 3):** behavioral reduction saturates at ~30 retained edges (∆=0.229, median PPL 35.8); doubling to 80 edges (∆=0.241) gives no meaningful gain while median PPL climbs to 36.9, and 179 edges push median PPL to 45.3 — the circuit is genuinely small.
- **Low collateral damage on benign prompts (Table 4):** on already-safe inputs, CircuitSteer median PPL = **30.4** vs CAA-Single(10) = 41.8 and CAA-Single(15) = 46.0 — several times less fluency degradation than single-layer CAA.
- **Zero-re-tuning transfer to Qwen3.5-27B (Appendix F, Table 11):** with all discovery hyperparameters carried over unchanged (and a different SAE family, TopK Qwen-Scope), CircuitSteer leads CAA-multi and CAA-single on RTP at 3 of 4 coefficients (∆=0.451 at λ=−4 vs CAA-multi 0.417 and CAA-single 0.209) while holding normalized PPL ≈ 0.90–1.01.
- **Component ablations (Table 2) show alignment is load-bearing:** on Gemma–Sycophancy every ablation (NoGeo, NegativeAlign, RandomSAE, SingleLayer) dashes while only the full method survives; RandomSAE is the only variant with no valid point on either Llama task, proving the gain comes from the learned SAE features, not the surrounding pipeline. Anti-aligned edges give the lowest reduction among the alignment-removed variants on RTP, confirming destructive interference.
- **Threshold robustness (Appendix H, Table 12):** across each 5–6× threshold range, ∆ varies by at most 0.044 — well below per-prompt std (≈0.20 Gemma, ≈0.17 Llama); defaults were fixed a priori, not tuned.

## Authors & Credibility

The work comes from a five-author team at the University of Southern California (Mehrshad Saadatinia, Parsa Razmara — corresponding, Ardalan Aryashad, Ali Abbasi, Seyedarmin Azizi), with equal-contribution and corresponding-author markers but no senior PI listed on the front page. The paper is marked "Preprint. Under review." Code is released at the GitHub URL given in the abstract (https://github.com/mehrshad-sdtn/CircuitSteer), which substantially raises reproducibility credibility. The methodology uses only standard, publicly available artifacts (Gemma-Scope, Llama-Scope, Qwen-Scope SAEs via SAELens, Detoxify, gpt-5.4-mini as judge), and the hyperparameters, SAE layer indices, and validity-window derivation are stated precisely enough to reproduce. The writing is technically careful: the authors explicitly caveat that co-activation/alignment are "candidate indicators that approximate" rather than establish causality, and they pre-register that the causal evidence comes from ablations rather than from any individual edge. The Related Work positions the method honestly against the closest concurrent work (Laptev et al. 2025, data-free decoder-cosine flow graphs), spelling out exactly what CircuitSteer adds (data-dependent edges, contrastive specificity, dense-vector steering under a single λ).

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** Moderate-to-good. The core ingredients (SAE features, cross-layer flow graphs from decoder cosine, contrastive scoring) each appear in prior or concurrent work — Laptev et al. 2025 already built data-free cross-layer flow graphs from decoder-direction cosine for thematic steering, and Marks et al. 2024 used gradients for circuit discovery. CircuitSteer's genuine contribution is the *combination* of co-activation AND geometric alignment as a joint edge criterion, plus dense-vector synthesis with a single λ, demonstrated to be fluency-preserving. The claim is incremental but well-motivated.
- **Rigor:** Strong on ablations (four component variants, threshold sensitivity, contrastive-prompt sensitivity, top-K sensitivity, matched-layer CAA control), but the headline "only method valid on all 8 cells" depends heavily on the **chosen validity window** [0.01, 1.5] PPL with ∆ ≥ 0.02. The window is justified empirically with an LLM judge and human inspection on data disjoint from the eval set and applied identically to all methods, which is defensible, but a reader should note that several "dashes" are baselines that *do* reduce the behavior — just outside this window. A competing window would change the coverage story.
- **Peak performance vs. coverage:** CircuitSteer is **not** the best method on most individual cells. ITI beats it on Gemma–Emotion (0.230 vs 0.135), Llama–Sycophancy (0.356 vs 0.156), and Llama–Jigsaw (0.077 vs 0.034); RepE beats it on Llama–Emotion (0.147 vs 0.091). The contribution is *robustness/coverage*, not peak ∆, and the paper is mostly honest about this ("trades peak performance on select tasks for reliable, fluency-preserving steering") — but the abstract and Figure 1 framing could be read as implying superiority rather than consistency. The absolute Sycophancy ∆=0.057 on Gemma is also small enough to question practical utility, not just statistical significance.
- **Statistical power:** 100 held-out prompts per dataset (50 target, 50 benign) is a small evaluation set for behavior distributions with per-prompt std ≈ 0.17–0.20; one-sided 95% bootstrap lower bounds fail to confirm Llama–Jigsaw, which is consistent with underpowered evaluation rather than a true null.
- **Reproducibility:** Good — public SAEs, public benchmarks, released code, stated hyperparameters and SAE layer indices. Main external dependency is the existence of public multi-layer residual-stream SAE suites, which currently limits the method to Gemma-2-2B / Llama-3.1-8B / Qwen3.5-27B and stops at 27B (a resource constraint the authors acknowledge).
- **Metric reliance:** Toxicity is scored by Detoxify (a known-noisy classifier) and by an LLM judge (gpt-5.4-mini); sycophancy by token probability. Detoxify's own biases are not discussed.
- **Dual-use / red flag:** The AdvBench refusal result (89% → 0% at λ=−3) is essentially a jailbreak recipe. The authors include an Ethical Statement framing it as characterizing alignment fragility and evaluate only on the existing public AdvBench benchmark, which is the standard posture in the refusal-direction literature (Arditi et al. 2024). Still, the result is more directly actionable for misuse than the toxicity-suppression results.
- **Minor:** the geometric-alignment threshold τ_sim=0.10 is a surprisingly low cosine bar to call "aligned"; the ablations rescue this by showing the direction matters, but the absolute threshold is not theoretically motivated.

## Strengths

- Genuinely training-free: no gradients, no weight updates, off-the-shelf SAEs — directly deployable.
- Principled and ablation-validated design: the joint co-activation + geometric-alignment edge criterion, contrastive specificity scoring, and decoder-averaged dense synthesis each correspond to a named failure mode they are designed to avoid.
- The strongest empirical property is *coverage*: the only method that does not require per-task/per-model re-engineering to find a fluency-valid operating point, which matters more for deployment than winning any single benchmark.
- Compact circuits (saturation at ~30 edges) make the intervention inspectable and cheap; the discovery step is one-time and amortizes.
- Generalizes to a larger model (Qwen3.5-27B) and a different SAE family (TopK) with zero hyperparameter re-tuning.
- Strong core-capability preservation (MMLU −0.03, GSM8K essentially unchanged) shows the signal stays confined to the target behavior.
- Honest caveats about causality vs. approximation, and honest positioning against the closest concurrent work.

## Limitations

- The headline "only method valid on all 8 cells" is contingent on the specific validity window [0.01, 1.5] PPL with ∆ ≥ 0.02; baselines that "dash" often still reduce the behavior, just outside this window.
- Not the best method on most individual cells (ITI dominates Gemma–Emotion, Llama–Sycophancy, Llama–Jigsaw; RepE dominates Llama–Emotion); the win is robustness/coverage, not peak ∆.
- Small evaluation sets (100 prompts/dataset) with per-prompt std ≈ 0.17–0.20 leave several cells underpowered; Llama–Jigsaw fails the significance check.
- Depends on the existence of public multi-layer residual-stream SAE suites (Gemma-Scope, Llama-Scope, Qwen-Scope); cannot run on arbitrary models, and currently capped at 27B.
- The geometric-alignment threshold τ_sim=0.10 is empirically chosen and not theoretically motivated.
- The AdvBench refusal result (89% → 0%) is dual-use and more directly actionable for misuse than the suppression results; mitigated only by an ethical statement.
- Toxicity scoring relies on Detoxify and an LLM judge, neither of which is ground truth.

## Relevance

这与用户对 LLM 可控性 / 对齐 / 可解释性的研究兴趣高度相关：它把 mechanistic interpretability 的 SAE 特征电路和 inference-time activation steering 结合起来，提供一个训练免费、可检查、跨层几何对齐的行为控制方法，正好处于"可解释性 → 安全控制"这条主线的核心。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How does CircuitSteer behave under **adversarial or distribution-shifted prompts** (e.g. toxic prompts phrased very differently from D+), where the contrastive specificity score may no longer isolate the right subcircuit? The contrastive-prompt sensitivity study (Appendix I) already shows Sycophancy discovery collapses when D− does not share the multiple-choice format of D+.
- Can the geometric-alignment criterion be replaced or augmented by a **causal-attribution** edge weight (e.g. gradient-based, à la Marks et al. 2024) to recover the cells where co-activation/cosine is too coarse, and at what computational cost?
- Does the dense-vector averaging in Eq. (6) **discard information** that a learned per-feature weighting could recover, and is there a training-free proxy for such weighting?
- The refusal result drops refusal to 0% — does the resulting model then **fail safety evals** beyond AdvBench (HarmBench, JailbreakBench), and does the circuit overlap with the single refusal direction of Arditi et al. 2024?
- How does coverage scale to **70B+** once public multi-layer SAE suites extend, and does the single-λ assumption continue to hold, or does each layer eventually need its own coefficient?
- The validity window [0.01, 1.5] is method-defining: how sensitive are the coverage rankings to a **PPL ≤ 1.2 or ≤ 1.1** window, and is there a principled, task-agnostic way to set it without an LLM judge?
