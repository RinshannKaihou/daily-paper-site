> **一句话总结：** 在一项预注册研究中，作者分析了 2021–2025 年共 146,239 份美国国会新闻稿，发现"无空格散文式破折号"(word—word) 的密度在 2021–2024 年稳定在每千字符 0.10–0.12，到 2025 年骤升至 0.217（翻倍以上），后/前期频率比为 1.55（95% CI 1.28–1.93），且在作者内部和闭合面板中均成立；但由于预注册的 dateline 校验门被正式违反（残余泄漏率 4.96% 高于 2% 阈值），结论只能作为探索性解释——与 LLM 辅助写作的扩散相符，而非确认性证据。

> **TL;DR:** A preregistered (OSF: 10.17605/OSF.IO/U5NEY) analysis of 146,239 U.S. congressional press releases (480 offices, 2021–2025) finds the density of unspaced prose-form em-dashes (word—word) was flat at ~0.10/1,000 chars through 2021–2024 then jumped to 0.217 in 2025 (×2.04 vs. baseline); the post/pre frequency ratio was 1.55 (95% CI 1.28–1.93), just clearing the prespecified 1.5× threshold on the congress-press corpus against a flat ASCII-hyphen control (2.75→2.68/1,000) and three null placebo cut-offs — but the preregistered dateline-validation gate was formally breached (4.96% residual leak vs. 2% threshold), so the LLM-diffusion interpretation is exploratory only.

## 核心贡献

- 首次在**美国国会新闻稿**这一政治传播语料上，以预注册方式测量 LLM 风格痕迹（无空格破折号）的人群级时间趋势——此前同类工作集中在 medRxiv 预印本、生态学摘要和生物医学文献。
- 提出并落地了一套**针对 boilerplate（dateline、footer）的保守剥离规则**：仅在州/特区 token 匹配闭合列表时删除行首 dateline，避免误删真实句子；并设计了基于 ASCII 连字符稳定性的"net-new"假证检验，排除"连字符被批量替换为破折号"的混淆解释。
- 给出人群级证据：2025 年破折号密度的翻倍是**净新增、作者内部、闭合面板内、跨党派对称**的，且不受数据提取管道在 2024/2025 边界无台阶、以及新办公室仅是"加成"而非"主因"这两项假证检验的支持。
- 在方法学诚信上做出示范：**显式披露 5 类偏离预注册项**，并坦诚报告校验门被违反，从而把"LLM 扩散"降级为探索性解释而非确认性结论。

## Key Contributions

- First preregistered measurement of an LLM stylistic trace (the unspaced prose-form em-dash, U+2014) in the U.S. congressional press-release corpus (congress-press, MIT-licensed), extending a companion medRxiv study to a new genre (political communication) that preserves original typography.
- A measurement pipeline that solves the corpus's two-source seam problem by restricting to scraper-sourced 2021–2025 records (N = 146,239; 480 offices), plus a conservative dateline-stripping rule (state-token closed list) that does not cut genuine prose, and an automatic range/relation/prose classifier that isolates the prose class as the primary endpoint M1.
- A multi-pronged falsification battery: (a) ASCII-hyphen net-new control (hyphen→em-dash conversion explains 0% of the rise, well under the 25% cap); (b) three preregistered placebo cut-offs inside the pre-LLM window, all null; (c) pipeline-invariance check at the 2024/2025 boundary; (d) continuing-vs-newly-covered offices decomposition; (e) within-author sign test and a closed panel of 224 ever-present offices.
- Honest, itemized disclosure of five deviation groups from the preregistration — most importantly that the dateline-validation gate was formally breached — so the confirmatory decision rule (GO ∧ H1–H4) was not met as written and the LLM-diffusion reading is explicitly labeled exploratory.

## Methodology

**Design.** Preregistered observational study deposited in OSF (DOI 10.17605/OSF.IO/U5NEY) before any confirmatory number was calculated; SHA-256 fingerprints of the frozen dataset were recorded. The design adapts the author's earlier medRxiv em-dash study to a new corpus.

**Corpus.** The open congress-press dataset of full-text House and Senate press releases. To avoid a typography-changing source seam (legacy API import ≤2020 meets a scraper pipeline around 2020), only scraper-sourced records from 2021–2025 were used (N = 146,239 releases; 480 offices).

**Primary endpoint (M1).** Density of *unspaced prose-form* em-dashes (word—word, U+2014) per 1,000 characters of *cleaned* body text. Each em-dash was auto-classified as numeric range (1934—1971), capitalized relation (Biden—Harris), or prose; only prose counted. The unspaced form was chosen because (i) it was preregistered, (ii) it has a low human baseline in AP-style press writing (which spaces dashes), (iii) it is the majority form (67.2% of U+2014), and (iv) it does not maximize the effect (the spaced form also doubled).

**Boilerplate removal.** A frozen rule removes the header dateline (CITY, ST —) only when the state token matches the closed list of 50 states + DC + territories, and removes the footer from the first footer marker onward. Sixteen releases became empty after cleaning but remain in N, contributing zero characters and zero em-dashes.

**Exposure & model.** Era divided at ChatGPT's public release (30 Nov 2022); the manuscript approximates pre = 2021–2022, post = 2023–2025. Counts modeled with Poisson/negative-binomial regression, log(cleaned-text length) as offset, cluster-robust SEs by member (Bioguide ID). Primary estimand = exposure-weighted post/pre frequency ratio. Prespecified practical-significance thresholds: ratio ≥ 1.5× AND absolute increase ≥ +0.05/1,000.

**Supporting / falsification analyses.** Within-author analysis; annual and quarterly series; segmented interrupted-time-series (ITS) at the Nov-2022 cut-off; en-dash (U+2013) control; ASCII-hyphen (U+002D) net-new control (cap: hyphen decline must explain < 25%); three placebo cut-offs (June 2021, Jan 2022, June 2022); pipeline-invariance at 2024/2025; continuing-vs-new offices; positional sensitivity (drop first 120/200 chars); spaced-form sensitivity; party × era and chamber × era stratification; lexical check using Kobak et al.'s top LLM-preferred words.

**Validation gate.** STOP/GO gate required correct dateline removal: automatic residual-leak rate ≤ 2% per year AND year-to-year change ≤ 1 percentage point; plus a manually adjudicated 300-release sample (60/year).

## Results & Highlights

- **Primary frequency ratio (post 2023–2025 / pre 2021–2022) = 1.55 (95% CI 1.28–1.93)** on the congress-press corpus, just above the prespecified 1.5× threshold; absolute increase **+0.056/1,000** (calendar years) or **+0.054/1,000** at the exact registered daily cut-off (ratio 1.528) — both clearing the +0.05/1,000 threshold. (§3.1)
- **Annual density stayed flat then doubled in 2025**: 0.0997 / 0.1035 / 0.1047 / 0.1157 / **0.2167** per 1,000 chars for 2021–2025 — i.e. 2025 is ×2.04 the four-year baseline, and ~97% of the total increase occurs in 2025. Prevalence of releases with ≥1 prose em-dash rose from ~13% (2021–2023) to **24.8% (2025)**. (Table 1, §3.2)
- **Net-new (not hyphen conversion)**: ASCII-hyphen density was stable at 2.75 / 2.58 / 2.79 / 2.76 / 2.68 per 1,000 across 2021–2025, so under the preregistered criterion hyphen→em-dash conversion explains **0%** of the rise (cap was 25%). (§3.3)
- **Within-author and closed-panel**: among 262 offices with ≥10 releases in both calendar eras, **75.6% increased** their density (median Δ = +0.036/1,000; sign-test **p ≈ 1×10⁻¹⁶**); a closed panel of 224 ever-present offices went 0.100 / 0.091 / 0.107 / 0.103 / **0.192**. Removing the top 5% most prolific offices left the exact-cut-off ratio *higher* at **1.66 (95% CI 1.31–2.09)**. (§3.3)
- **Segmented ITS at the Nov-2022 cut-off**: pre-trend flat (×1.01/year), **no upward step** at the cut-off (step 0.76, 95% CI 0.60–0.96), clear post-period acceleration (**post slope ×1.45/year, 95% CI 1.32–1.59**; slope-change ×1.43, 1.11–1.85). Quarterly model agreed on every term. (§3.2)
- **Placebo cut-offs null**: June 2021 RR 0.989 (0.812–1.205); Jan 2022 RR 1.058 (0.843–1.329); June 2022 RR 0.941 (0.720–1.230) — none shows an increase. (§3.4)
- **Artifact explanations rejected**: dominant scraper `date_source` ~99% on both sides of 2024/2025; continuing offices rose 0.103 (2024) → 0.192 (2025), while offices first covered in 2025 had higher density (0.267) but only added to an already-present rise. (§3.4)
- **Spaced form also doubled** (post-hoc): 0.044/1,000 in 2021–2023 → **0.096/1,000 in 2025**, so the discontinuity is not specific to the unspaced form. (§3.5)
- **Party/chamber symmetry**: within-office fold-change ratio R/D = 0.96 (95% CI 0.67–1.38); small residual difference favored Democrats (Hodges–Lehmann R−D −0.041/1,000, 95% CI −0.071 to −0.010; Mann–Whitney p = 0.009) — the opposite of a "incoming majority alone" story. (§4.3)
- **VALIDATION GATE BREACHED**: automatic residual dateline-leak rate was 7.00 / 4.89 / 3.94 / 4.95 / 4.94% per year (weighted mean **4.96%**, above the 2% threshold; 2021→2025 change ~2.1 pp, above the 1-pp threshold). Manual state-coded dateline stripping recall was 99.2% (122/123; Wilson 95% CI 95.5–99.9%), and positional sensitivity (drop first 120/200 chars) left the ratio essentially unchanged (1.554 → 1.550), bounding the practical effect — but the formal gate still failed. (§2.6, §4.6)

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: Real but incremental. The marker (em-dash) and the hypothesis (LLMs leave stylistic traces) are already established by Keck (ecology abstracts), Liang et al. (peer reviews), Kobak et al. (biomedical vocabulary), and the author's own medRxiv companion study. The contribution is the *new corpus* (U.S. Congress) and the *unspaced prose-form* refinement forced by the congressional dateline quirk — useful, but not a new mechanism.
- **Rigor**: Methodologically careful — offset Poisson/NB models with cluster-robust SEs by office, three placebo cut-offs, ASCII-hyphen net-new control, pipeline-invariance check, within-author and closed-panel analyses. However, the **central practical-significance result rests on a genuinely thin margin**: the pooled ratio of 1.55× barely clears the prespecified 1.5× bar, and under the legitimate alternative aggregation (simple yearly average) the ratio drops to **1.43× with absolute increase only +0.044/1,000 — failing BOTH thresholds**. The author discloses this honestly (§4.6, deviation 1), but it materially weakens the headline.
- **The validation gate was formally breached** (residual leak 4.96% vs. 2% threshold; 2.1-pp change vs. 1-pp threshold). The author transparently downgrades the claim to "exploratory," which is the right call, but it means the study's own preregistered decision rule was not met. The post-hoc mitigations (positional sensitivity, spacing classification, leak trend running *against* the effect) are reasonable but were added after results were known.
- **Confounding is serious and underdetermined**. The entire jump is concentrated in 2025, which coincides exactly with the start of the **119th Congress, a new administration, unified single-party control, and substantial staff turnover**. The party-symmetry stratification argues against a crude "incoming majority" mechanism but cannot separate LLM diffusion from a new political-communication climate or new staffers bringing the habit. The author acknowledges this but it limits causal interpretation severely.
- **Reproducibility**: Strong on paper (frozen code, SHA-256 fingerprints, public corpus, OSF deposit, re-derivation from raw snapshot). Missing: the third-party extractor's full version history is unavailable, and several registered items were not executed (langid filter, content-hash dedup sensitivity, year×chamber×party missingness breakdown, M2 length-covariate model, annual stripping precision/recall).
- **Significance**: The policy framing (should officials disclose AI assistance in official communication?) is interesting but the paper explicitly does not answer it. The marker is population-level only — explicitly *not* a per-release authorship detector — which limits operational use.
- **Red flags**: (i) Single-author paper; (ii) the manual 300-release validation sample was adjudicated *after* the census rather than before as planned (a sequencing deviation that weakens the gate's force); (iii) the ITS model was fitted at revision after descriptive results were known and first used a different unit than registered; (iv) the lexical convergence with Kobak-list words (OR 1.57) began in 2023→2024, *earlier* than the em-dash jump, so the two signals do not align in time as cleanly as the narrative implies.

## Strengths

- Genuinely preregistered with frozen code and dataset fingerprints before confirmatory analysis — a high bar that most LLM-trace observational work does not meet.
- A rich, pre-planned falsification battery (placebo cut-offs, hyphen net-new control, en-dash control, pipeline invariance, continuing-vs-new offices) rather than a single before/after contrast.
- Conservative measurement choices that *avoid* maximizing the effect: the unspaced form is the majority form but not the fastest-rising; the state-token-closed-list stripping rule trades recall for precision to protect real prose.
- Exemplary intellectual honesty: an entire subsection (§4.6) itemizes five deviation groups, explicitly states the decision rule was not met, and reports the alternative aggregation under which the result fails.
- The within-author and closed-panel analyses address the strongest non-causal alternative (sample composition) directly.

## Limitations

- **Validation gate formally breached** (residual dateline leak 4.96% vs. 2% threshold) — the full preregistered rule was not met; interpretation is exploratory.
- **Thin margin around the practical threshold**: 1.55× vs. 1.5× prespecified; under the yearly-average aggregation the result *fails* both thresholds (1.43×, +0.044/1,000).
- **Confounding with the 119th Congress** (new administration, unified control, staff turnover) cannot be fully separated from LLM diffusion; party symmetry helps but does not resolve it.
- **One country, one genre** — generalization to other political corpora or non-English text is untested.
- **No causal identification** — purely observational; the marker is population-level and explicitly not a per-release authorship detector.
- **Third-party extraction pipeline** without full version history; missing-text imbalance (0.4–0.8%, higher for Republican offices early) is reported but not fully resolved.
- **Several registered items not executed** (langid filter, content-hash dedup sensitivity, full year×chamber×party missingness breakdown, M2 length-covariate model, annual stripping precision/recall).
- **Single author** and manual validation performed after the census rather than as the planned pre-census gate.

## Relevance

与用户对"LLM 检测 / 文本溯源 / AI 辅助写作的人群级痕迹"这一研究方向高度相关：它提供了一个预注册、多重重证、且坦诚报告失败的实证模板，展示如何用极弱的风格标记（一个标点）在大规模真实语料上测量 LLM 渗透率。其方法学（offset Poisson + 聚类 SE、placebo cut-off、net-new 控制）对任何想在中文政治/学术语料上复现类似测量的工作都有直接借鉴价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would the same unspaced em-dash signal appear in U.S. state-level legislatures, the federal executive branch (e.g., agency press releases), or parliamentary systems, and with what timing?
- Can a tighter causal design separate LLM diffusion from 119th-Congress staff turnover — e.g., matching on offices with documented staffer continuity, or using the release-level adoption of other LLM-preferred vocabulary as an instrument?
- Why is ~97% of the increase concentrated in 2025 rather than at the Nov-2022 ChatGPT release? Is the "delayed diffusion as models matured" hypothesis testable against model-quality proxies (e.g., release dates of GPT-4-class systems)?
- How would the result change under the simple yearly-average aggregation that *fails* the threshold (1.43×, +0.044) — is the pooled estimator the right primary, and would a pre-specified exposure-weighting rule have survived peer review?
- Does the breach of the dateline-validation gate generalize: how many other "LLM stylistic trace" studies rely on boilerplate-stripping that is validated only post-hoc?
- Can the marker be combined with lexical (Kobak-list) and other stylometric signals into a *multivariate* population-level adoption index, and would the em-dash signal survive controlling for the lexical signal that rose earlier (2023→2024)?
- Is the party symmetry (slight Democrat lead) robust to controlling for district/office type, press-office size, or digital-native staffer demographics?
