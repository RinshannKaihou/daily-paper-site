Hypothesis Testing with Conditional Queries: Learnability and the Value of Interaction

Zonghuan Xu · Fudan University · posted 2026-08-06 · arxiv.org/abs/2608.06262 ↗
Key Finding

For conditional-query hypothesis testing, the worst-case adaptivity gap is exactly Θ(N²) — interaction saves at most a quadratic, never exponential, factor in test count.

一句话
在条件查询假设检验模型中严格证明：可学习性当且仅当两两条件概率严格正分离；T 轮自适应策略可用 O(N²(T+log 1/ρ)) 个提前固定的成对查询模拟；最坏自适应差距恰为 Θ(N²)。
TL;DR
An exact learnability criterion (positive pairwise-conditional separation), a universal non-adaptive simulation at O(N²(T+log 1/ρ)) pair queries, and a matching lower bound — the adaptivity gap is exactly Θ(N²), so interaction never yields exponential savings.
1 Key Metric
Θ_ε(N²)
exact worst-case adaptivity gap G_N(ε)

L = 8M·(T + log 1/ρ),  M = C(N,2)
T*_ad ≤ 3·⌈8 log(3/ε)⌉  (constant in N)

2 Why This Matters

Quantifies that interactive evaluation saves at most a quadratic factor over static benchmarks — and gives a checkable criterion (Δ_pair) for when static tests can replace multi-round interactive ones.

3 Critical Edge
Full-support & exact-conditional-sampling assumptions fail in real LLM evals, and N² can dwarf T — so the polynomial-vs-exponential gain may not yield practically useful static benchmarks.

核心贡献

▾
  • 精确可学习性判据：定义两两条件映射 Θ(P)=(P(i|{i,j}))，分离 Δ_pair；定理 3.1 证明 Δ_pair>0 ⇔ 非自适应可学习 ⇔ 自适应可学习，且 Δ_pair=0 时最优误差恰为 1/2。
  • 通用非自适应模拟上界：任意 T 轮自适应策略可用 L=8M(T+log 1/ρ) 个提前固定查询在总变差 ρ 内模拟，界与假设类无关。
  • 匹配的二次下界：构造 N=4m 上的硬分布族，自适应复杂度与 N 无关、非自适应需 Ω_ε(m²)，差距 Θ_ε(N²)。
  • 方法新颖：用 read-once coupling-from-the-past（Propp–Wilson 1996；Wilson 2000）把成对样本视为随机压缩映射，精确产生 P_A 样本。

Key Contributions

▾
  • Exact learnability criterion. Δ_pair > 0 ⇔ non-adaptive ⇔ adaptive learnability; at zero separation the optimal worst-case risk under either protocol is exactly 1/2 for every finite budget T.
  • Universal non-adaptive simulation. For any T-round policy and any ρ, at most L = 8M·(T + log 1/ρ) pre-fixed pair queries reproduce the transcript within TV ≤ ρ, uniformly over all full-support distributions — independent of class cardinality or separation.
  • Matching quadratic lower bound. On N = 4m: T*_ad ≤ 3·⌈8 log(3/ε)⌉ (constant in N) vs T*_na = Ω_ε(N²); gap G_N(ε) = Θ_ε(N²). Replaces the naive 2^N−1 exponential enumeration.
  • Methodological novelty. Read-once coupling-from-the-past: each stored pair sample merges two states; once a composition coalesces the output is an exact P_A draw, with E[τ_A] < 2M explaining the N² scale.

Methodology

▾

Setup. A tester distinguishes two full-support hypothesis classes P₀, P₁ ⊆ Δ⁺([N]) under a hidden label. Each round it queries a nonempty event A and gets a fresh sample from P(·|A). Adaptive testers choose A from history; non-adaptive testers must fix the whole query vector upfront.

Learnability (Thm 3.1). Sufficiency via querying every pair equally, taking empirical Θ̂, and a triangle-inequality margin; Hoeffding + union bound over M pairs. Necessity via a cut/transport bound TV(P_A,Q_A) ≤ (N−1)·d_pair, coupled round-by-round, forcing worst-case error → 1/2 when Δ_pair = 0.

Non-adaptive simulation (Thm 4.1). Collect L i.i.d. uniform pair queries upfront. Each stored sample (e,Y) defines a map Φ^A_{e,Y} that merges e's endpoints to Y when e ⊆ A, preserving P_A. Composing until coalescence yields an exact draw (Wilson's read-once CFTP); the per-round coalescence time decomposes as τ_A = Σ G_k, G_k ~ Geom(C(k,2)/M), giving E[τ_A] < 2M and E[exp(τ_A/8M)] ≤ e.

Lower bound (Thm 5.1). Hard family on N = 4m (sets I, J, U, V). Querying I reveals j, querying J reveals k, and only the tiny target pair B_{jk}={u_j,v_k} (mass O(m⁻⁴)) carries the label. With dilution η = m⁻⁴, off-target queries contribute negligible KL; Pinsker forces T ≥ Ω_ε(m²) for any non-adaptive tester, while the adaptive tester pinpoints B_{jk} after querying I, J.

Against the naive "enumerate all 2^N−1 events" baseline (exponential in N), interaction provably cuts the worst-case test count by at most a quadratic factor — never exponential. The exponential branching of adaptive evaluation does not translate into exponential query savings.

Critical Assessment

▾
  • Novelty — genuine. The exact 1/2 learnability dichotomy and the exact Θ_ε(N²) gap are new; read-once CFTP as a non-adaptive simulator is an elegant bridge from exact-sampling MCMC to property testing.
  • Rigor — high. All three main statements proved with finite constants; coalescence decomposition τ_A = Σ G_k cleanly yields both mean and exponential moment.
  • Reproducibility — self-contained, unverifiable empirically. Pure theory, no code/data; no simulation of Algorithm 1 (would have been cheap and confidence-building).
  • Significance — model-relative. The full-support assumption rules out zero-prob behavior categories common in real LLM evals, and the model ignores the cost of specifying/realizing a conditioning event.
  • Overclaiming — modest. Honest worst-case caveat in §6, but the leap to "static benchmarks can match interactive eval" deserves skepticism: N² can be astronomically larger than T.

Strengths

▾
  • Settles both learnability and the gap with matching upper/lower bounds — no asymptotic slack.
  • Learnability criterion Δ_pair > 0 is clean, checkable, distribution-free.
  • Simulation is distribution- and class-agnostic — a universal adaptive→non-adaptive compiler.
  • CFTP produces exact P_A draws; TV error only from truncation.

Limitations

▾
  • No empirical validation — no simulation, no tightness study of the constant 8.
  • Strong oracle assumptions — full support and exact conditional sampling; neither holds in real LLM evals.
  • Query-count ignores event-specification cost, often the dominant expense of an interactive benchmark.
  • Worst-case-only lower bound; for structured classes the gap is much smaller, uncharacterized here.
  • N² may be impractical — for N in the hundreds, ~10⁴–10⁵ queries to simulate a handful of adaptive rounds.

Relevance

▾

与本用户关注的 LLM 评测与基准设计 高度相关：严格量化了交互式（自适应）评测相对静态评测在最坏情况下的查询节省上界为 Θ(N²)，为判断静态基准何时能、何时不能等价替代多轮交互评测提供了理论依据与可计算的判据（两两条件分离 Δ_pair）。对从事基准构建、自适应题目选择、活跃假设检验的研究者，值得细读。

Questions for Further Exploration

▾
  • Robustness of Δ_pair > 0 when full-support is relaxed, or conditional samples are only approximate — does the exact 1/2 dichotomy collapse?
  • Can the constant 8 in L = 8M·(T+log 1/ρ) be tightened? Simulate Algorithm 1's coalescence-time distribution for moderate N.
  • For structured classes (identity, equivalence, uniformity), is the gap actually o(N²)? When is it small?
  • Gap as a function of minimum atom mass α — is the quadratic blow-up an artifact of mass imbalance?
  • Can CFTP simulate bounded-adaptivity (k-round) testers at intermediate cost?
  • Open-ended X (free-form generations discretized by a judge) — does discretization error dominate Δ_pair?
