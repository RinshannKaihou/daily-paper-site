When Self-Evolution Backfires: Pre-Commit Gating against Skill Contamination in LLM Agents

Linfang Shang · Ming Xu · Yiding Sun · Tianle Xia · Lingxiang Hu · Lan Xu · Ning Zheng — Tencent

TL;DR Ungated self-evolution on Terminal-Bench 2 peaks at 62% pass@1 (R3) then collapses to 50% (R5) as the skill pool bloats to 179 entries; the proposed Verifier-as-Gatekeeper (VaG) — a progressive Cold→Warm→Hot trust hierarchy with three heterogeneous critics plus marginal-gain subset selection — climbs monotonically to 72% with a 37-skill pool, and transfers frozen to four other backbones (+8 to +16pp).

! Key Finding

Self-evolving LLM agents hit a capability-contamination phase transition — past a critical pool size, added skills degrade performance, and the defect is structurally irreversible.

% Key Metric

VaG reaches 72% pass@1 vs ungated's 62% peak (+10pp), with a 5× smaller Hot pool (37 vs 179 skills).

? Why This Matters

Refutes the "more skills = better" assumption for self-evolving agents and provides a deployable pre-commit gating template for any agent building persistent skill libraries.

× Critical Edge

The headline 72% lives on the distillation split (Event-50); the honest held-out Test-25 VaG figure is only 44% — a 28pp gap the abstract does not foreground.

Key Contributions · 核心贡献

  • Phase transition formalized. Decomposes contamination into individual, combinatorial, and systemic levels — vocabulary prior skill-library work (Voyager, ExpeL, AWM) lacks.
  • Irreversibility theorem (Eq. 5). Distillation is conditioned on the live pool, so defective skills propagate down the lineage; even oracle full-lineage cleanup leaves 5.6pp unrecovered of the 12.3pp drop.
  • VaG = progressive trust hierarchy. Cold→Warm→Hot promotion converts skill admission from bookkeeping into the last controllable choke point.
  • Cross-model transfer, frozen. The Hy3-evolved R5 Hot pool gives +8 to +16pp on four backbones without re-evolution — gating distills model-agnostic engineering knowledge.

Methodology · VaG Pipeline

Every distilled skill enters Cold (recorded, invisible to the agent). Promotion requires clearing two gates of increasing stringency:

COLD · recorded → Gate 1 → WARM · individual-safe → Gate 2 → HOT · admitted
SchemaCritic
Deterministic frontmatter structure check.
−2pp if removed
ExecCritic
Single-skill A-B behavioral replay on held-out set.
−10pp if removed
AgentCritic
One LLM semantic review: fabricated facts, contradictions, unsafe ops.
−4pp if removed

Gate 2 (Warm→Hot): marginal-gain greedy adds a candidate only when it strictly improves measured joint held-out performance — eliminates combinatorial contamination (−8pp if removed; pool balloons 37→58).

Results · 关键数据

  • Rollback is structurally weak. Source-only deletion recovers 1.7pp of a 12.3pp drop; oracle lineage cleanup recovers only 6.7pp; 5.6pp irrecoverable.
  • Contamination bites hard tasks. Ungated Hard-tier erodes 53%→35%; VaG lifts Hard to 59% — a +24pp margin while Easy stays 100%.
  • Efficiency co-benefit. VaG token cost stays flat (0.77–0.94M); Ungated bloats to 1.30M for worse accuracy.
  • Cross-benchmark. Frozen on InterCode NL2Bash: 37 skills → 69.0% vs ungated 179 skills at 65.5% (+11.5pp over Seed).

Sharpest Critiques · 局限

  • 14-task held-out split. Gate-2 decisions rely on k=3 rollouts with ~30pp Wilson CI bands — per-skill admit/reject may be indistinguishable from noise.
  • Single-domain. All evidence on terminal/shell tasks; untested for web, retrieval, or planning skills where combinatorics may differ.
  • No adversarial stress-test. A subtly misleading skill engineered to pass all three critics is not attempted.
