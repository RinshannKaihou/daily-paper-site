The Illusion of Visual Tool-Use: A Causal Audit of Thinking with Images

Zhiheng Wang · Bo Peng · Lai Wei · Chaochao Lu — Shanghai AI Lab · SJTU · Shanghai Innovation Institute
Key Finding
Visual tool-use in "thinking-with-images" MLLMs is largely illusory: aggregate accuracy gains are carried almost entirely by a small Calibrated minority of trajectories, while most crop-and-zoom calls exert no causal effect on the answer.

Methodology 方法

Each rollout is cast as a structural causal model over (I, Q, T₁…ₙ, O₁…ₙ, Y): the MLLM policy π emits crop-and-zoom actions Tᵢ, a deterministic engine returns crops Oᵢ, and π finally commits answer Y. Three causal paths link input to answer:

  • Direct path — (I,Q) → Y, no tools.
  • Observation-mediated path — T → O → Y, genuine visual grounding.
  • Action-induced shortcut — T₁…ₙ → Y, the mere act of calling shifts the answer without using O.

A deterministic trajectory classifier (Algorithm 1) uses five training-free features — call count n, pre-first-call gap g₀, peak per-call V^max, HitMax, and post-saturation over-extension rate POER — with thresholds τ_sat = 0.95, ε = 0.01 to label each rollout, enabling an additive decomposition ATE_policy = Σ_b f_b · Δ̄_policy,b.

Three-Level Causal Interventions 三层干预协议

Toggle π_tool vs π_direct by swapping the system prompt while holding weights and decoding fixed. Six models audited across five benchmarks.

ATE_policy = E[do(π=π_tool)][1[Y=Y*]] − E[do(π=π_direct)[...]]

Largest gain: Mini-o3 on VisualProbe +21.3 pp (33.7→55.0). Regressor: Qwen3-VL-8B on MME-RW-Lite −3.7 pp. DeepEyes is essentially null across all five.

At runtime, replace every returned crop with a corrupted patch (RANDOM-CROP / NOISE / BLANK) of the same shape. The intervention propagates through later thoughts and actions, so ATE_traj captures evidence loss and induced repair/budget-exhaustion. A force-answer variant isolates the truncation component.

Mini-o3 V*: 87.8 → 23.6 (−64.2 pp, 84.8% HitMax) · Qwen3-VL-8B: 91.1 → 30.4 (−60.7 pp)

Force-answer control: recovers Qwen3-VL-8B to 73.0 but leaves a −18.1 pp residual; Mini-o3 stays −51.2 pp below clean → genuine evidence lost, not merely truncated.

Visual Evidence Gain (VEG) — a step-level counterfactual: swap a single Oᵢ under an identical prefix and tool action, read the option-restricted softmax gap. Because T and the prefix are held fixed, the action-shortcut cancels and VEG isolates only the visual contribution — a local instantiation of Pearl's Natural Indirect Effect.

VEGᵢ = ΔM^real − ΔM^cf   (NIE, counterfactual)

On V*, 81% of DeepEyes calls, 56% of Qwen3-VL-8B, and 29% of Mini-o3 calls have |VEG| < 0.01 — doing nothing. Qwen3-VL-8B VEG is +0.28 on correct trajectories but −0.20 on wrong ones.

Results & Highlights

  • Policy gains small & uneven. Mini-o3 +21.3 pp on VisualProbe; DeepEyes ≈ null everywhere.
  • Calibrated carries the ATE. +7.6 of Qwen3-VL-8B's +6.9 pp; +3.3 of Mini-o3's +5.5 pp. Replicated on HR-Bench-4K (+4.4 of +5.0).
  • Mode 2 pathologies. Mini-o3 peaks at N=3 (78.2%) → 0% at N=12; 38% of crops cover <4% of the image; restarts double on saturation (27.6%→56.4%).
  • Robust. Sweeps over ε ∈ {0.005…0.05}, τ_sat ∈ {0.80…0.99} leave model ordering and the Calibrated-carries-ATE conclusion unchanged.

Strengths

  • Original causal lens — VEG as a local NIE estimate that generalizes beyond crop-and-zoom.
  • Diagnostic is deterministic, training-free, feature-interpretable — runnable on any model.
  • Concrete failure modes: CWL (saturated vs structurally-inactive) and LWP (four audited pathologies).
  • Strong reproducibility: public code, threshold sweeps, multi-seed, force-answer control, three corruption schemes, open-ended extension.

Limitations

  • Step-level VEG needs white-box logits → excludes o3 / o4-mini / Gemini.
  • Only CROP-AND-ZOOM studied; OCR / segmentation / video tools left to future work.
  • "Illusion" certified primarily on MCQ; open-ended VisualProbe only gives directional VEG.
  • RL-trap hypothesis not causally established — inferred, no matched training runs.
  • Six models, five on Qwen2.5-VL-7B — limited architecture diversity.

Open Questions for Further Exploration

→ Would process-aware RL (rewarding VEG-positive calls) actually shift the distribution toward Calibrated, or does the RL-trap persist?
→ Does the illusion reproduce on closed-source models (o3, o4-mini, Gemini) using only policy- and trajectory-level interventions?
→ How does the diagnostic change when tools expand to OCR / segmentation / video-frame — do action-shortcuts grow or shrink?
→ Is the Calibrated subset learnable from unlabeled rollouts via VEG self-supervision for inference-time routing?
→ What is the principled saturation analogue for free-form generation (vs MCQ's τ_sat = 0.95)?
→ Do the four Mode 2 pathologies correlate with specific RL trajectory-mix biases (over-representation of long multi-call rollouts)?
