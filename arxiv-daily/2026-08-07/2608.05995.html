Uncertainty Quantification · Benchmarking

A Unified Risk View of Uncertainty: Posterior Risk for Disentanglement and Evaluation Beyond Proxies

Reframes uncertainty as sample-conditional pointwise posterior risk — averaging a predictor's loss over the posterior of plausible ground-truth functions — unifying the Bayesian posterior-variance and frequentist excess-risk views, and instantiates it as a semi-synthetic tabular benchmark where oracle aleatoric/epistemic targets are computable exactly.

<0.5 Spearman ρ ceiling
Frieder Wizgall · Georg Tirpitz · Moritz Seiler · Kerstin Ritter · Bálint Mucsányi | University of Tübingen · Charité Berlin · Hertie Institute for AI | epistemic-aleatoric bayesian-deep-learning gaussian-processes

F Key Finding

Accurate prediction does not translate into reliable uncertainty disentanglement — even the strongest methods stay well below ρ = 0.5 against oracle epistemic uncertainty.

M Key Metric

ρS < 0.5

Held-out EU ranking. Method rankings only moderately preserved across datasets (Kendall W ≈ 0.46); aleatoric is even less separable.

★ Why This Matters

First benchmark with computable oracle aleatoric/epistemic targets that bypasses OOD-proxy tasks — directly actionable for anyone deploying uncertainty estimates in clinical or safety-critical production systems.

! Critical Edge

GP-tabular ceiling. Oracle targets exist only for GP-smooth regression under MSE — the headline ranking "ensembles / FSP-Laplace / CatBoost-KGB are most reliable" is GP-tabular-specific and should not be over-generalized to vision or NLP, a caveat buried in Limitations rather than next to the recommendations.
  • Weak absolute signal. With ±2 SEM over only 7 held-out datasets, several pairwise differences are "not robust"; the claim is a ranking, not "these methods are good".
  • No crosswalk to the co-author's own Mucsányi et al. (ICLR 2024) proxy-based benchmark — the single most persuasive artifact is missing.
  • Feature-rank noise σ(x)∝rank may favor tree methods; no ablation over noise structure is reported.
  • DEUP convergence holes (many n/a cells in optimizer heatmaps) are visible but unanalyzed.
  • No repo URL in body text at submission; "we provide code" is asserted only.
