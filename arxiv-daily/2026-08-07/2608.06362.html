◆ Key Finding Fusing AIVAT variance reduction with anytime-valid confidence sequences lets expensive agent evaluations stop the instant the evidence is sufficient — while keeping a third-party-auditable statistical guarantee.

▲ Key Metric
0×

Median AsympCS stopping-time ratio vs raw outcomes, across 15 HUNL configs on a 71,439-hand corpus (range 54.5–97.6×).
★ Why This Matters

Directly solves the core pain point — when can I stop running costly game-play evaluations? — and hands LLM poker agents auditable, reproducible stopping rules.

Its predictable online value-learning idea plugs cleanly into CFR / value-function solving, a user's existing line of work.
⚠ Critical Edge

The title's "Certified" is overstated: the 74× speedup is asymptotic (finite-sample false-positive rate 7–12% vs nominal 5%), and the exact certificate exists only for toy Leduc.

In HUNL the exact EB-CS gives just a 1.37× speedup — a width floor absorbs most of the variance gain.

Method in one loop

  1. Fix value function v_t using only past hands (F_{t-1}-measurable) — no peeking at the current hand.
  2. Record the conditional action kernel p_{t,h} and pick the correction enablement before seeing the action.
  3. Play the hand; corrected payoff Y_t = X_t + C_t uses the known action law, so each correction is conditionally mean-zero.
  4. Update both confidence sequences in parallel; may refit v on data through hand t.

The honest trade-off: two intervals, two promises

74× fewer hands · ±1 BB asymptotic

The variance-adaptive primary screen. Tracks realized variance, so the 54× variance cut converts directly into a 74× stopping speedup.

  • Valid under a conditional Lindeberg + nondegenerate averaged-conditional-variance condition.
  • But miscalibrated at finite horizons: false-positive rate 7.11% (30-cond. mean), 12.43% AIVAT-only, 10.4% A2-standalone — all above nominal 5%.
  • ρ² = 2 log(1/α)/t_opt frozen on a held-out calibration split to prevent post-hoc retuning.
1.37× fewer hands · HUNL exact · finite-sample

The predictable plug-in Empirical-Bernstein sequence. Exact and time-uniform — once a data-independent almost-sure bound B_Y is supplied.

  • Bet cap λ ≤ ½ forces a width floor w_t ≥ 4B log(2/α)/t — variance-independent. That is why exactness is expensive (Theorem 1).
  • Exact only on Leduc (|Y| ≤ 117 by telescoping). For HUNL no analytic corrected-payoff bound exists — runs are merely descriptive.
  • Leduc half-widths run within ~1% of the deterministic floor (ratios 1.0019 / 1.0074).

Strengths & Limitations

＋ Strengths

  • Exemplary honesty: openly reports that the headline speedup comes from the asymptotic interval, and separates "efficient" from "certified."
  • Predictable-interface proof (Prop. 1) kills AIVAT's chicken-and-egg value-function requirement; online refitting is asymptotically free (Thm 2).
  • The release protocol is a concrete, minimal-evidence remedy to an underappreciated real problem.

− Limitations

  • Exact certification is Leduc-only; all HUNL EB-CS results are descriptive (the paper's own "sharpest open problem").
  • Single-platform, possibly non-distinct-model corpus — 15 configs vs GTO Wizard only; multiplayer unaddressed.
  • One disclosed protocol deviation (E1: registered B=13 → executed B=22); A2 audit is a resampling artifact by construction.
