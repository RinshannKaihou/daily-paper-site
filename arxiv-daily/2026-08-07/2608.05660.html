Reasoning Errors Have a Region and a Direction in the Residual-Stream Trajectory of LLMs

Hamed Damirchi · Ignacio Meza De la Jara · Damith Ranasinghe · Yuhang Liu · Javen Shi

“ Key Finding

Reasoning errors live in two separable coordinates of the LLM residual stream — a coarse region and a normalized direction — and reading both, alongside motion, transfers zero-shot from one benchmark to eleven unseen tasks.

Why This Matters

Reasoning validity — not just answer confidence — is linearly legible in the residual-stream trajectory, with strong cross-task and cross-model transfer: a foundation for hallucination and error detection in trustworthy LLM pipelines.

From: Relevance
Critical Edge

On completion-style FACTOR sets the frozen model's own answer likelihood beats the detector (FACTOR-news 72.3 vs 58.2; FACTOR-expert 74.6 vs 69.5). The factual "win" is only relative to the two trained baselines — not to the model itself.

From: Critical Assessment

02Methodology

  • Selection setting. Frozen LLM runs one forward pass per candidate answer; residual-stream activations h_{t,l} are recorded at every token & layer. No weights change.
  • Three readers, one score. Each reader emits one embedding; they are concatenated and fed to an MLP head trained end-to-end with a pairwise objective + VQ commitment term.
  • Motion — bidirectional LSTM over layerwise displacements d = h_{l+1} − h_l (re-used from Truth as a Trajectory).
  • Region — learned K=128 codebook; straight-through estimator + commitment term; each state replaced by its nearest entry (coarse position only).
  • Direction — normalized u = h/‖h‖ over m=6 mid-to-late layers; shared projection; combines final-token + answer-span mean.
  • Models. Llama-3.1-8B (32L), Qwen2.5-14B (48L), Qwen3-30B-A3B (MoE). Added streams = 6.7% of motion-reader params.

  • Per-item oracle sits 3–21 pts above the trained fusion → the three streams read different items (genuinely complementary).
  • Region code is shared, not factual-specific: every code a factual answer takes is also taken by a reasoning answer — no factual vocabulary was learned.

Strengths

  • Clean conceptual contribution — state-restoration trade-off — with a parsimonious instantiation (6.7% extra params).
  • One donor → 11 evaluation targets → 3 model families (incl. an MoE). Best in 47/48 cells.
  • Complementarity demonstrated via ablation + per-item oracle gap, not merely asserted.
  • Honest reporting: discloses length-heuristic-solved sets, single-seed cells, and where the base model wins.

Limitations

  • Selection-only. Ranks supplied candidates; cannot judge a single free-form answer.
  • Requires a labeled donor benchmark and per-model retraining; no donor-free variant.
  • Performance is bounded by the base model's correctness — it reads, not adds, knowledge.
  • Late fusion is suboptimal: oracle 3–21 pts above fusion (a learned per-item gate is left to future work).

04Questions for Further Exploration

  • Can the direction/region readers extend to free-form generation — scoring token-level validity during decoding, not just ranking candidates?
  • How sensitive is the region stream to codebook size K? Is 128 a sweet spot, and does the vocabulary grow with scale or task diversity?
  • Would CCS / Geometry-of-Truth / SAE directions, run as measured baselines (not just discussed), close or widen the reported gap?
  • The per-item oracle is 3–21 pts above fusion — could a learned per-item gate recover most of that margin?
  • Does the shared reasoning/factual region coordinate hold for code, math, long-context errors, or only short-form QA?
  • Since base-model likelihood beats the detector on FACTOR completions — can likelihood-conditioned fusion dominate on both axes?
