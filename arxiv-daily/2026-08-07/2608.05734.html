Subliminal Learning is Non-Semantic Distillation

一句话总结：Subliminal Learning（教师偏置经无关随机数列表传给学生）由权重中的非语义噪声结构驱动——加高斯噪声后传递反而增强（Gemma 1.9×、Llama 1.3×）。 A teacher's behavioral bias (e.g. "loves owls") travels to a student through apparently unrelated outputs — lists of random integers. This paper shows the carrier is not meaning but the noise structure of the weights themselves.

Key Finding
Subliminal transfer is mediated by non-semantic noise structure in the teacher's weights — not learned associations. Adding Gaussian noise amplifies it instead of erasing it.
Key Metric
1.9×
Preference-change growth in Gemma-2B-it after noising all weights (σ=0.10), 10 seeds, 95% CI. Llama-3.1-8B: 1.3× (σ=0.15). The opposite of what a semantic-mediation account predicts.
Why This Matters
Exposes a data-poisoning channel invisible to humans and to standard activation-based audits — and flags gradient-based auditing as a promising but still incomplete defense.

04Critical Edge · sharpest critique

Only two small models (2B, 8B) — neither matching the original Cloud et al. setup — and the gradient-auditing signal fails behaviorally in Llama while requiring the candidate directions to be known ahead of time.

The practical defense utility is oversold relative to what's demonstrated: the auditing signal works only for steered teachers, only behaviorally in Gemma, and only under a closed 55-animal candidate set — an affordance unrealistic for real-world defense. The decisive mechanistic-distinctness claim (Figure 4) rests on a single target animal ("cat"), and no stronger interpretability tooling (SAEs, linear probes, crosscoders) was tried to back the "activations are silent" claim.

01The Decisive Test

Two competing accounts of Subliminal Learning predict opposite signs for the same intervention:

  • Semantic mediation → noise should drown learned associations and weaken SL.
  • Non-semantic (spurious/noise) mediation → noise should amplify the very structure carrying the bias.

The authors perturb every weight matrix W ← W + σ·std(W)·ε and use the same noised copy as both teacher and student. The result below is the central evidence.

03Three Ways to Bias a Teacher

system prompt finetuning steering vector NEW

A mean-centered activation-difference steering vector v_a over a 55-animal set is applied to the residual stream at a single mid layer — layer 14 (Gemma) / layer 21 (Llama) — during dataset generation. Steered teachers produce viable subliminal datasets, opening a new axis for studying how the form of the bias shapes what the student learns.

06Gradients Whisper; Activations Stay Silent

Mean gradients of the parent model on steered subliminal data linearly correlate with the ground-truth v_a — a visible main diagonal in the cosine-similarity matrix (Gemma, 8 animals). Repurposed as a steering vector, they produce moderate but consistent preference shifts.

Mean activation differences carry no detectable signal.

Implication: standard activation-based auditing — logit lens, probing, SAE features — is blind to this class of data-poisoning threats.

07Verdict

Strengths

  • Elegant falsification test — a sign flip cleanly separates two hypotheses.
  • "Teacher as noisy near-random map" unifies many ad-hoc SL observations.
  • Fine-grained inheritance result is concrete & surprising.
  • Honest limitations; code & full prompts released.

Limitations

  • Only 2 small models; frontier-scale mechanism open.
  • Mechanistic claims shown on 1–8 animals, high variance.
  • Single noising design (symmetric); asymmetric untested.
  • Gradient audit: closed-set only, fails in Llama.

08Open Questions

  1. Does the 1.9×/1.3× effect scale to 70B/405B, or saturate as models learn real associations?
  2. Would a genuinely random teacher still transfer — isolating noise as sole mediator?
  3. Can SAEs or trained probes surface what mean activations & DLA miss?
  4. Is there an unsupervised gradient-clustering audit — no closed candidate set?
  5. Does layer-distributed prompted SL predict robustness to layer-frozen student training?
  6. Is there a noise-robust objective that suppresses SL without harming legitimate learning?
