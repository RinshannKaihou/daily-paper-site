Methodology · training-free, 3 offline stages + 1 inference step

No gradients, no weight updates. Pre-trained SAEs (Gemma-Scope / Llama-Scope / Qwen-Scope via SAELens) used off-the-shelf.

STAGE A
SAE graph build
Encode hl → sparse features; add cross-layer edge iff co-act > τact and cos(di,dj) > τsim
STAGE B
Contrastive circuit
S = P(·|D⁺) − P(·|D⁻); keep top-K edges → behavior-specific subcircuit Ctarget
STAGE C
Dense vector synth
vl = mean of decoder dirs per layer; magnitude decoupled from circuit size
INFERENCE
h′l = hl + λ·vl
single λ, all circuit layers, simultaneous
τact=1.5 · τsim=0.10 · τdiff=0.05 · top-K=30 · λ ∈ {−4…2} · validity window: 0.01 ≤ PPL ≤ 1.5, ∆ ≥ 0.02

Why geometric alignment is load-bearing

"The same" concept at different layers often has misaligned decoder directions that combine destructively. Ablations confirm: removing alignment (NoGeo), reversing it (NegativeAlign), or replacing the SAE with a random projection (RandomSAE) all collapse validity — most starkly on Gemma–Sycophancy, where only the full method survives.

核心贡献 / Core Contributions

用 SAE 把残差流分解为稀疏单语义特征，在多层之间以"特征共激活 + 解码器方向几何对齐"构造特征流电路，再用解码器方向平均合成的稠密向量在所有电路层以单一系数 λ 同步注入——是唯一在 8 个模型–数据组合上都有可操作点的方法。
  • Training-free multi-layer steering via joint co-activation + geometric alignment of SAE decoder directions — no weight modification.
  • Geometric alignment proven necessary, not just beneficial: NoGeo / NegativeAlign / RandomSAE / SingleLayer all dash where the full method holds.
  • Multi-point dense-vector design decouples magnitude from circuit size, so a single global λ is consistent across layers.
  • Benchmarked vs. 8 baselines on 4 datasets × 2 model families + zero-re-tuning transfer to Qwen3.5-27B.

Also worth noting

  • Compact circuit: reduction saturates at ~30 edges; doubling to 80 gives no gain, 179 edges hurts PPL.
  • Low collateral damage: on already-safe prompts, median PPL = 30.4 vs CAA-Single's 41.8–46.0.
  • Zero-re-tuning transfer to Qwen3.5-27B (different SAE family, TopK): leads CAA at 3/4 coefficients.
  • Threshold robust: across 5–6× threshold ranges, ∆ varies ≤0.044 (below per-prompt std ≈0.17–0.20).

Critical Assessment

Peak performance vs. coverage. CircuitSteer is not the best method on most individual cells. ITI beats it on Gemma–Emotion (0.230 vs 0.135), Llama–Sycophancy (0.356 vs 0.156), and Llama–Jigsaw (0.077 vs 0.034); RepE beats it on Llama–Emotion. The contribution is robustness/coverage, not peak ∆. The absolute Sycophancy ∆=0.057 on Gemma is small enough to question practical utility, not just statistical significance.

Moderate-to-good. The ingredients each appear in prior/concurrent work — Laptev et al. 2025 already built data-free cross-layer flow graphs from decoder cosine; Marks et al. 2024 used gradients for circuit discovery. CircuitSteer's genuine contribution is the combination of co-activation AND geometric alignment as a joint edge criterion, plus dense-vector synthesis under a single λ. Incremental but well-motivated.

Underpowered. 100 held-out prompts/dataset (50 target, 50 benign) is small for per-prompt std ≈ 0.17–0.20. One-sided 95% bootstrap lower bounds confirm reductions on 7/8 cells — but Llama–Jigsaw fails, consistent with underpowered evaluation rather than a true null. Also: toxicity is scored by Detoxify (known-noisy) and an LLM judge; neither is ground truth.

Red flag. The AdvBench refusal result (89% → 0% at λ=−3) is essentially a jailbreak recipe. The authors include an Ethical Statement framing it as characterizing alignment fragility and use only the public AdvBench benchmark — standard posture in the refusal-direction literature. Still, the result is more directly actionable for misuse than the suppression results.

Strengths

  • Genuinely training-free — directly deployable, no gradients.
  • Each design choice targets a named failure mode (ablation-validated).
  • Coverage matters more for deployment than winning any single benchmark.
  • Compact ~30-edge circuits are inspectable and cheap; discovery amortizes.
  • Generalizes to 27B and a different SAE family with zero re-tuning.

Limitations

  • Headline contingent on the specific validity window.
  • Not peak-∆ on most cells; ITI/RepE dominate several.
  • 100-prompt eval sets leave several cells underpowered.
  • Capped at 27B — depends on public multi-layer SAE suites.
  • τsim=0.10 is empirically chosen, not theoretically motivated.

Open Questions for Further Exploration

  1. How does it behave under adversarial / distribution-shifted prompts where the contrastive score may miss the right subcircuit? (Appendix I already shows Sycophancy discovery collapses when D⁻ lacks the multiple-choice format.)
  2. Can geometric alignment be augmented by a causal-attribution edge weight (gradient-based, à la Marks 2024) to recover coarse cells — and at what cost?
  3. Does dense-vector averaging discard information a learned per-feature weighting could recover? Is there a training-free proxy?
  4. After refusal → 0%, does the model fail broader safety evals (HarmBench, JailbreakBench)? Does the circuit overlap Arditi et al. 2024's single refusal direction?
  5. How does coverage scale to 70B+ once SAE suites extend — does the single-λ assumption hold, or does each layer need its own coefficient?
