Hakken: Predicting future discoveries to fill the gaps in today’s knowledge

Key metric · macro recall, 2020 cut-off

+14.59pts

Over THiGER, the strongest baseline (60.72% vs 46.13%); macro F1 is a near-tie — the win is in recall, the sound metric when negatives are merely unobserved.

Why this matters

A rare end-to-end closure of the AI-discovery loop — hypothesis, explanation, wet-lab proof — and a blueprint for trustworthy autonomous research agents.

Critical edge

The headline wet-lab result is n=3, expert-curated, with no selection-free control — 2/3 cannot be read as a calibrated success rate.

Methodology

  • Task reframed. Discovery becomes multi-label relation prediction on a temporal knowledge graph: for an entity pair, predict which of 23 relation types will first appear after the cut-off; confidence is calibrated as a “counterfactual probability the relation already exists.”
  • Graph branch. GraphSAGE-style aggregation per time snapshot → hierarchical attend-then-merge temporal Transformer, with a pair-block attention bias that blocks a degenerate pre-merge shortcut.
  • LLM branch. Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3 reads 20 pre-cutoff sentences per entity (never co-mentioning the partner); graph embeddings enter as Fourier-time-encoded prefix tokens.
  • Training. Positive-unlabeled objective with a PU-safe dynamic-weighted focal loss plus ranking; label-wise ensemble and Platt/isotonic calibration.
  • PHELinE explainer. Enumerates 2–4-hop paths, scores sufficiency/necessity via a one-time GraphSAGE surrogate that pseudo-retrains the scorer — seconds at millions-of-triples scale.
  • Protocol. Train only on pre-cutoff triples; evaluate on relations first seen after 1990/2000/2010/2020; ten-year sliding back-test with a random-empirical-scores null.

Wet-lab closure of the loop

  • TP53–BAMBI (conf. 0.884) — consistent BAMBI expression increase upon p53 induction; confirmed.
  • RAF1–TNF (conf. 0.831) — TNF mRNA increase under RAF1 inhibition; confirmed.
  • SOAT1–STAT3 (conf. 0.842) — no STAT3 change in HepG2; denied (single cell line, one protocol).

Long-horizon & rare-relation behavior

  • From the 1990 cut-off, micro recall decays ~0.68 → ~0.58 over ten years vs ~0.24 for the null; macro recall decays only ~8% and nDCG stays in a 0.986–0.990 band across all cut-offs.
  • The LLM branch recovers THiGER’s misses on rare, low-support relation types (slope p = 0.004).
  • Compute: 16× H100 80GB for days — deliberately frugal, no web-scale pretraining.

Strengths

  • Full-loop closure with independent CRO wet-lab confirmation (2/3) — rare, and the paper’s most valuable evidence.
  • Genuinely time-aware evaluation: four cut-off decades, sliding back-test, empirical null baseline — stricter than typical KG-completion work.
  • Honest metric discussion: reports where it loses; shows gains concentrate on rare relations, where discovery value lies.
  • Long-horizon stability: ~8% macro-recall decay over a decade, nearly flat nDCG, invariant decay profile across cut-offs.
  • Engineering substance: pair-block attention bias, PU-safe loss, history-aware labels, surrogate-based explanation at scale.

Limitations

  • The macro-F1 “win” is +0.18 pts (a tie); THiGER still wins weighted F1, precision and nDCG — the framing rests on a metric choice.
  • LLM pretraining leakage is acknowledged but unquantified — no ablation with an older checkpoint.
  • Ground truth is a noisy silver standard: rule-based extraction ignores negation and speculation; triples were not independently audited.
  • Wet lab is n=3, expert-curated, one domain; the denied hypothesis was tested in one cell line with one protocol.
  • PHELinE has no quantitative fidelity evaluation; the GUI study is qualitative (n=7).
  • Open-source HakkenOSS cannot reproduce headline numbers (non-shareable commercial data component).

Questions for further exploration

  • How much of the +14.6 macro-recall gain survives an LLM whose pretraining provably predates the evaluation horizon?
  • What does a selection-controlled wet-lab study (random high-confidence picks, or blind expert picks) do to the 2/3 confirmation rate?
  • Can PHELinE’s sufficiency/necessity scores be validated against expert judgments of mechanistic plausibility?
  • Is the time-invariant decay profile a real property of scientific knowledge or an artifact of the extraction pipeline?
  • Do the calibrated confidence scores transfer as a “knowledge score” for triage beyond biomedicine (materials, agriculture)?
