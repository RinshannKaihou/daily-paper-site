The Safety Relay in Roleplay Jailbreaks: A Component-Resolved Causal Analysis of Harm Recognition and Refusal

Md Mokarram Chowdhury · Ernie Chang · Yang Li  — Iowa State University · Meta

Key metric

+78.0pts

refusal on held-out requests after subtracting the complete-wrapper direction at assistant-start — 0.6% → 78.6%. Equal-norm random directions move refusal ≤1.2 pts.

Why this matters

Localizes a safety failure to a specific token position and text construct — a causal handle on how hidden states steer behavior.

把安全行为解析到具体 token 位置与文本成分的因果贡献 —— 隐藏状态驱动行为研究的机制可解释性范本。

Critical edge

All four wrappers are hand-authored from one "authority/simulation" genus, with no comparison against established automated persona attacks — generality rests on four templates of a single stylistic family.

Methodology

Matched prompts A–D→ HR / RT projections→ Directions r_F · r_S · r_X→ Hash-split held-out subtraction
  1. Matched comparisons. Every AdvBench / HarmBench harmful request gets a manually written benign counterpart (same topic, form, output format); four wrappers with labeled segments yield prompts A (benign direct), B (harmful direct), C (benign wrapped), D (harmful wrapped).
  2. Two locations, one relay. Residual-stream projections give Harmfulness Retention (HR) at the request tokens and Refusal Transfer (RT) at assistant-start; high HR + low RT = safety-relay attenuation.
  3. Interventions. Repair / ablation edits act on every assistant-start token; block and strength sweeps localize effects (blocks 10–12 Llama, 13–18 Qwen, 17–28 Gemma).
  4. Held-out discipline. SHA-256 dev/eval split; direction, block, strength frozen on dev only — then subtracted from eval states, against equal-norm random and reverse-direction controls. No fine-tuning; ~300 GPU-hours on A100.
  5. Shared-axis decomposition. r_F and r_S are split into shared vs contrast components and projected onto the ordinary refusal reference, separating generic refusal structure from wrapper-specific remainder.

Strengths

  • Genuinely matched design — hand-written benign counterparts isolate harmfulness from superficial content differences.
  • Position-resolved measurement operationalizes the "relay" and yields a crisp, falsifiable dissociation replicated across 6 benchmark-model combinations.
  • Rigorous held-out discipline: hash splits, frozen direction/block/strength, equal-norm random, reverse-direction and nested-context controls.
  • Convergence with the ordinary refusal direction ties jailbreak mechanics to the established refusal-direction literature.
  • Complete reproducibility artifacts — templates, seeds, sweeps — plus honest, specific limitations.

Limitations

  • Only two English benchmarks, three 7–9B open model families, four author-written wrappers — no other languages, closed models, or scales.
  • Fail-control cohorts are small or empty in several configurations, weakening the success-vs-failure contrast the relay story depends on.
  • Causal tests exclude pre-compliance requests, where safety fails even earlier; the full safety process is not mapped.
  • Geometry analysis reuses the causal-test evaluation data — a characterization, not an independent replication.
  • Edits also raise refusal on benign prompts: explanatory probes, not selective defenses; no deployable safeguard is built or benchmarked.

Questions for Further Exploration

  • Do HR/RT statistics separate successful from failed attacks for adaptive or LLM-generated wrappers (e.g., DeepInception-style), or only for these four hand-authored templates?
  • Does the relay hold in larger or closed models — and is the mid-layer block localization (10–12 / 13–18 / 17–28) scale-dependent?
  • Can a calibrated, selectivity-aware assistant-start repair act as a runtime guardrail that preserves benign roleplay?
  • Does scenario framing's model-dependent remainder (~21% non-shared norm) correspond to an identifiable circuit or SAE feature, or is it diffuse?
  • How does the relay picture change for pre-compliance requests excluded from the cohorts, where safety fails before roleplay even begins?
