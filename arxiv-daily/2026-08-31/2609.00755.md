> **一句话总结：** 提出可微掩码框架 S^martCirc，把"电路发现"（找重要节点）和"功能解释"（判定节点是功能型还是直通型）统一为单个联合优化问题，用 KL 散度+交叉熵驱动节点发现、用成对 token 相似度变化（FI 指标）无监督地分类功能角色；在 GPT-2/Llama 3.2 1B/Qwen 3 1.7B 的 IOI 和 Acronym 两个任务上，GPT-2 Acronym 达到 −97.50% 准确率下降（最强基线 Activation Patching 仅 −54.67%），且运行时间只有 Activation Patching 的约 1/100（597s vs 62,397s）。

> **TL;DR:** S^martCirc replaces the standard sequential "discover-then-interpret" MI pipeline with a single differentiable masking objective over three coefficients per node (unimportant / pass-through / functional), where discovery minimizes KL divergence + task cross-entropy and classification is driven by a pairwise token-similarity change metric (FI) — no human labeling of roles. On GPT-2's Acronym Prediction task it reaches a −97.50±1.08% accuracy drop (60-node circuit) vs −54.67±38.33% for the strongest baseline, Activation Patching (35 nodes), while running ~100x faster (597s vs 62,397s); on Llama 3.2 1B IOI it gets −69.00% accuracy drop with 35 nodes vs −5.67% for Attr. IG with a larger 47.7-node circuit. Ablations show the joint alternation matters: Discovery-First collapses to −4.35% on Acronym vs −97.5% for the full alternating scheme.

## 核心贡献

- **打破"先发现、后解释"的顺序范式**：指出节点重要性与功能角色本质上是相互依赖的（知道功能才能判断重要性，反之亦然），将二者建模为单一联合优化问题，在发现与分类两个阶段间交替训练。
- **通用的二元功能角色抽象**：把先前细粒度、任务绑定的角色（name mover、S-inhibition head 等）抽象为跨任务可迁移的"计算角色"二分法 —— **功能节点（functional node，主动变换输入）** vs **直通节点（pass-through node，近乎无改动地传递输入）**，二者都具备泛化性与可量化性。
- **完全量化的功能解释指标**：定义成对 token 相似度算子 Sim(A)=AA^T⊘(11^T−I) 与功能解释指标 FI(X,Y)=|Sim(X)−Sim(Y)|_F/|Sim(X)|_F，使功能角色可以由激活结构变化的可测数据自动推断，摆脱主观人工判断。
- **端到端可微框架**：每节点学习三个掩码系数 (c_u, c_p, c_f)（和为 1），配合置信度/有效性/稀疏性三项正则和 warm-up 阶段；在 GPT-2、Llama 3.2 1B、Qwen 3 1.7B 上的实验表明其显著优于六种基线，并能复现已知 IOI 电路、与人工标注的 Acronym 电路功能映射对齐（8 个头中正确识别 4 个，Previous Token 1.0 判为直通、Letter Mover 11.4 判为功能，均正确）。

## Key Contributions

- A reframing of circuit discovery and functional interpretation as one jointly-optimized problem (alternating between node discovery and node classification stages), grounded in the observation that importance and functionality are interdependent.
- A coarse but generalizable dichotomy of computational roles — functional nodes (actively transform representations) vs pass-through nodes (relocate inputs with minimal modification) — that abstracts away task-specific head names and can be embedded into automated pipelines.
- A quantitative, self-supervised functional metric: pairwise token-similarity change FI(X,Y) = |Sim(X)−Sim(Y)|_F/|Sim(X)|_F, which directly measures whether a node's activation changes representational structure (functional) or preserves it (pass-through).
- An end-to-end differentiable masking framework with three-term regularization (confidence → binary, validity → sum-to-one, sparsity) plus a warm-up phase, validated on GPT-2 / Llama 3.2 1B / Qwen 3 1.7B across IOI and Acronym Prediction, outperforming six baselines (Activation Patching, Attribution Patching, Attr. IG, IBCircuit, Prune., Random) on accuracy drop and logit difference at comparable circuit sizes.

## Methodology

**Problem setup.** Given an LLM, each node (attention head or MLP neuron) gets three learnable masking coefficients c_{n,u}, c_{n,p}, c_{n,f} constrained to sum to 1 (unimportant / pass-through / functional). Circuit discovery and interpretation are unified: nodes with (c_u + c_p) > c_f are candidate important nodes.

**Stage 1 — Important Node Discovery.** Optimize L_disco = α·Σ_v P_orig(v)·log(P_orig(v)/P_mask(v)) − (1−α)·log(P_mask(y)): the KL divergence between the full model's output distribution and the masked-circuit distribution (faithfulness) plus cross-entropy on ground-truth labels (task performance), where α∈[0,1] trades the two off. Intuition: removing a truly important node should substantially degrade performance.

**Stage 2 — Important Node Classification.** For each important node, compute the token-similarity change induced by the node: Sim(A) = AA^T ⊘ (11^T − I) with row-wise normalization, and FI(X,Y) = |Sim(X) − Sim(Y)|_F / |Sim(X)|_F on input activation matrix X and output activation matrix Y. The classification loss L_class = (1/|N|)·Σ[−c_{n,f}·FI(X_n,Y_n) − c_{n,p}·FI(X_n,Y_n)] pushes nodes that induce large similarity-structure changes toward functional (c_{n,f}↑) and structure-preserving nodes toward pass-through (c_{n,p}↑) — functional roles are inferred purely from measurable representational change, no labels needed.

**Training process.** A three-term regularizer L_sparsity enforces (1) confidence (coefficients pushed to 0/1 via c(1−c)), (2) validity (sum to one), and (3) sparsity (penalize c_p + c_f, i.e., inclusion in the circuit). Because assigning roles from randomly initialized coefficients is unreliable, training starts with a warm-up phase optimizing only L_disco, then alternates discovery and classification so each stage refines the other (hard argmax assignments are used only for the final evaluation circuit).

**Evaluation.** Three metrics: Circuit Size, Accuracy Drop (Acc.), and Logit Difference (Logit.) of the extracted circuit run in isolation. Six baselines: Activation Patching (Act.), Attribution Patching (Attr.), Attribution Patching with Integrated Gradients (Attr. IG), IBCircuit (information-bottleneck optimization), Prune. (gradient-based pruning adapted from edge to node level), and Random. Data: 1000 clean + 1000 corrupted samples per task (IOI names/place-object pairs from Wang et al. 2022; Acronym via the wonderwords RandomWord class). All runs use seed 42 over three runs; per-configuration learning rates, epochs, warmup, sparsity and logit coefficients are listed in the appendix.

## Results & Highlights

- **GPT-2, Acronym task:** S^martCirc achieves a −97.50±1.08% accuracy drop and −5.00 logit difference with a 60.33±7.93-node circuit, vs the strongest baseline Activation Patching at −54.67±38.33% accuracy drop (35.00-node circuit) — a +42.8-point degradation gap in circuit fidelity; Random/Prune. baselines produce 0.00% accuracy drop.
- **IOI with Llama 3.2 1B:** S^martCirc reaches a −69.00±42.43% accuracy drop with a 35-node circuit; Attribution Patching with Integrated Gradients yields a larger 47.67-node circuit but only a −5.67% drop, and Activation Patching gets −33.00% with 12 nodes — showing functional-interpretation-guided masking finds more critically important nodes.
- **Qwen 3 1.7B — smallest circuits with maximal damage:** 12.00±1.63 nodes on Acronym (−100.00±0.00% accuracy, −10.97 logit difference) and 16.33±1.50 nodes on IOI (−100.00±0.00%, −12.01), beating every baseline on both size and degradation (e.g., Act.: 32 nodes/−100.00% on Acronym; Random: 52.67 nodes/0.00%).
- **RQ2 — recovery of known circuits:** Thresholding at 50/100/150/200 nodes on GPT-2 IOI, S^martCirc consistently recovers a higher percent of the human-verified IOI circuit than most baselines (comparable to Activation Patching, while optimization-based methods stay far below even at 200 nodes). On the Acronym ground-truth circuit, S^martCirc recovers 4 of 8 heads (50%) and its functional labels align with the manual mapping: Previous Token 1.0 → pass-through and Letter Mover 11.4 → functional, both correct.
- **RQ3 — hyperparameters (IOI, GPT-2):** Accuracy drop increases from ~71% at k=2 classification steps to ~96% at k=6, with monotonic gains in circuit recovery; α=0.75 (KL weighted over task loss) performs best, and α=1.0 (pure KL, cross-entropy removed) degrades sharply — explaining why baselines relying only on output-distribution differences are weak.
- **RQ4 — ablations:** Discovery-First (S^martCirc-DF) collapses to a near-zero −4.35% accuracy drop on Acronym vs −97.5% for full S^martCirc; Interpretation-First reaches ~88% on IOI but only ~42% on Acronym; removing warmup (S^martCirc-NW) drops performance by ~33% (IOI) and ~34% (Acronym) — the alternating scheme and warmup are both load-bearing.
- **Runtime (Table 2):** On GPT-2 Acronym, S^martCirc takes 597.14s vs 62,397s for Activation Patching (~104x faster) and 302,778s for Attr. IG; it is comparable to IBCircuit (620.70s) while achieving far better degradation metrics.

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty.** The joint-optimization reframing is the paper's real contribution and is genuinely sensible: the warmup + alternating scheme is precisely the ablation that matters (−4.35% vs −97.5% on Acronym without it), which substantiates rather than merely asserts the interdependence claim. However, the functional/pass-through dichotomy itself is a coarse two-way split of a distinction that already exists informally in the literature (e.g., function-preserving vs behavior-changing components); the novelty lies in the FI similarity metric and the optimization coupling, not the taxonomy.

**Rigor.** The evaluation metric is somewhat self-favoring: Accuracy Drop rewards methods that *break* the model, and a circuit that destroys computation trivially maximizes degradation — the paper mitigates this with the KL/logit metrics and circuit-size comparison, but the headline "outperforms" rests on a three-metric trade-off rather than one criterion, and S^martCirc is not uniformly best (on Llama Acronym, Activation Patching achieves −100.00% with 32 nodes vs S^martCirc's −57.33%). Statistical reliability is shaky in places: the Llama IOI accuracy drop is −69.00±42.43 (std larger than half the mean), and asterisks in Table 1 mark baseline cells where only one run completed within the 48-hour limit — so some baseline numbers are effectively single-run comparisons against three-run S^martCirc numbers.

**Baseline fairness.** Several baselines are near-useless as configured: Prune. and Random show 0.00% accuracy drop on every model/task, and Prune. is a hand-adapted edge-pruning method run at node level — a weak adaptation that may overstate the gap. Activation Patching is the only strong baseline and it costs ~100x more compute, so the fair summary is "S^martCirc matches or beats the strong-but-expensive baseline at ~1% of its cost," not "all baselines fail."

**Red flags / scope.** Only two synthetic, template-based tasks (IOI with name/place templates; acronym strings from wonderwords) with GPT-2-derived ground truth are evaluated, on models up to 1.7B parameters — no natural-language tasks, no larger models, and the ground-truth-recovery claim (RQ2) only exists for GPT-2. The paper honestly documents that head 8.11 is classified as pass-through while 10.10 as functional, contradicting prior work showing both heads act as Bridge/Letter-Mover depending on the intervention; the authors attribute this to multi-functionality, which is plausible but is also consistent with the binary role scheme simply being too coarse. The conclusion admits the roles are task-relative: the same node can be labeled differently across tasks, which limits the "generalizable roles" framing.

## Strengths

- The joint discovery-interpretation objective is well-motivated and the ablation (RQ4) directly validates the central hypothesis: sequential variants degrade severely (Discovery-First −4.35% vs −97.5% on Acronym).
- Functional interpretation is fully quantitative and label-free — the FI metric replaces subjective human role assignment with a measurable similarity-structure change, making it usable in automated pipelines.
- Strong efficiency story: 597s vs 62,397s (Activation Patching) on GPT-2 Acronym, comparable to IBCircuit but with much larger accuracy drops; this makes circuit discovery tractable without massive intervention sweeps.
- Consistent behavior across three architectures (GPT-2, Llama 3.2 1B, Qwen 3 1.7B) and two tasks, with the largest circuits wins on the biggest model (Qwen: 12-16 node circuits causing −100% accuracy drops).
- Honest error analysis: the 8.11/10.10 misclassification is openly discussed and tied to head multi-functionality rather than hidden; limitations (coarse roles, task-relativity) are stated in the conclusion.

## Limitations

- Only two synthetic tasks and models ≤1.7B parameters; no evidence the dichotomy or the method scales to natural tasks (e.g., multi-step reasoning, factual recall) or larger models where circuits are deeper and roles more compositional.
- The functional/pass-through binary cannot represent multi-functional or conditional roles — the 8.11 vs 10.10 divergence against human analysis is a concrete instance where the abstraction loses information.
- Roles are defined relative to a task's activations, so the same node may receive different labels across tasks; cross-task role transfer is claimed but not directly measured (no experiment runs one task's learned coefficients on another task).
- Evaluation favors degradation (accuracy drop/logit difference); a circuit faithful to *correct* model behavior rather than merely destructive is only indirectly assessed via the KL term, and there is no qualitative comparison of discovered circuit diagrams beyond the 8-head Acronym example.
- Some baseline cells are single-run (48-hour limit) and the node-level adaptation of Prune. is not validated independently, weakening baseline comparisons.

## Relevance

这篇论文属于你关注的 LLM 隐状态与机制可解释性方向：它把电路发现（attention head/MLP 级别的掩码优化）与节点功能分类统一到一个可微框架里，其 FI 相似度指标正是对"内部表征几何/隐状态结构变化"的直接量化。虽然不是自进化 Agent 或训练可靠性方向，但作为自动化、可扩展的 circuit discovery + 自动功能标注工具，对理解 LLM 内部机制和构建自动可解释性 pipeline 有直接参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would a three-way or continuous role spectrum (e.g., mixture weights over functional/pass-through, or an additional "attention-only routing" role) resolve the 8.11/10.10 multi-functionality divergence without sacrificing automatism?
- How does S^martCirc behave on natural tasks (multi-step reasoning, factual recall, instruction following) and models ≥7B, where circuits are deeper and node roles are more compositional?
- Are the learned role assignments transferable across tasks — i.e., if coefficients are trained on IOI and applied to Acronym, do the functional/pass-through labels of shared heads agree? This would directly test the "generalizable roles" claim.
- Since Accuracy Drop rewards breaking the model, could the objective be inverted or adapted (e.g., maximizing faithfulness under a size budget on *preserved* behavior) to better serve safety applications where the goal is understanding rather than degradation?
- Can the FI similarity metric be applied post-hoc to circuits found by other methods (Activation Patching, attribution graphs), making interpretation a modular add-on rather than a joint objective?
