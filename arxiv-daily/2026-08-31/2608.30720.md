> **一句话总结：** 作者对预训练 Transformer 做了一个"轻触式"修改——在每个残差流读取点（即每个 LayerNorm）归一化后注入各向同性高斯噪声再重归一化，每个读取点学一个噪声尺度（全网仅新增约 2L 个标量参数），并在固定总读取速率预算 B（单位 nat）下用冻结教师蒸馏微调，从而把"表示相似性"从几何距离改造成基于 Bhattacharyya 系数的统计可区分性；在 ViT-S/ImageNet-1k 与 GPT-2-small/OpenWebText 上发现 B≈10⁴ nat 处出现行为拐点，且利用 Q/K/V 投影的可区分性保持率，在 80 个最小扰动文本对上找出 7 个显著超出随机投影基线的注意力头，其中 6 个与已知 induction head 有独立对应关系。

> **TL;DR:** Replaces every LayerNorm read in a pretrained transformer with a noisy read (Gaussian noise + renormalization, one learned noise scale per tap, only ~2L new scalars) and fine-tunes by pure distillation from the frozen original under a hard global read-rate budget B in nats — turning representational similarity into statistical distinguishability measured by the Bhattacharyya coefficient (BC). On ViT-S/ImageNet-1k and GPT-2-small/OpenWebText, behavior tracks the base model above a knee at B ≈ 10⁴ nats and degrades below it; learned allocation protects the final readout and starves layer-1/layer-11 reads in GPT-2 at B = 1.5×10⁴. Probing which perturbation-induced distinctions survive each head's key projection (baseline: random 64-dim projections) surfaces 7 middle-layer heads above baseline on 80 noun-swap pairs at a fixed 50-nat perturbation, 6 of 7 independently linked to induction heads — including L5H4/L4H7, which strong K-selectivity despite nearly zero prefix-matching score.

## 核心贡献

- **随机 LayerNorm 读取**：把预训练 Transformer 中每个 LayerNorm 改为"归一化 → 加各向同性高斯噪声 → 重归一化"，每个读取点（tap）一个可学习噪声尺度，全网仅新增约 2L 个标量参数（ViT-S 与 GPT-2-small 各 25 个 tap）；适用于任何用 LayerNorm 的架构，pre-norm 下刻画速率受限的"读"，post-norm 下刻画速率受限的"写"。
- **硬预算代替软惩罚**：与信息瓶颈类方法不同，总读取速率预算 B（nat）通过每 tap 速率的 softmax 分配（r_i = B·softmax(α_i)）作为硬约束强制执行，损失函数只有蒸馏 KL 项（对冻结的原始模型），没有任何速率惩罚项；速率→噪声的换算通过闭式公式 rate(κ) = KL[vMF(μ,κ)‖Unif] = κA_p(κ) + log C_p(κ) − log C_p(0) 与预计算的 4000 点查找表完成，重参数化采样使梯度可以直接流向权重和速率分配。
- **理论工具箱**：局部后验近似为 LayerNorm 球面上的 von Mises–Fisher 分布，两状态的可区分性用闭式 Bhattacharyya 系数（BC）度量；由于 Hellinger 距离是 f-散度，数据处理不等式保证"处理不可能增加可区分性"（MLP 子层输出的 BC ≥ 输入读取的 BC）；对注意力头的 Q/K/V 投影导出球上 pushforward 密度的闭式解，给出有界无偏的蒙特卡洛估计器；common random numbers（CRN）耦合配合联合凹性给出有效后验 BC 的下界，BC_0 ≥ BC_1 ≥ … ≥ BC_L 插值刻画上游噪声的影响。
- **跨模态实验发现**：两种模态都在 B ≈ 10⁴ nat 附近出现行为拐点；可区分性沿深度平滑出现；视觉模型对模糊最敏感、对褪色最不敏感；语言模型中 K 投影选择性高的头与 induction head 高度重合，并发现了 prefix-matching 分数几乎为零却保持强 K 选择性的头（L5H4、L4H7）。

## Key Contributions

- A light-touch architectural modification — stochastic LayerNorm reads (normalize, add isotropic Gaussian noise with a learned per-tap scale, renormalize) — that installs rate-limited reads into any pretrained LayerNorm transformer at the cost of only ~2L additional scalar parameters (25 taps in both ViT-S and GPT-2-small).
- A hard global rate budget B (nats) enforced by a softmax per-tap allocation with no rate-penalty loss term; the student is fine-tuned purely by KL distillation from the frozen deterministic teacher, with a calibrated closed-form rate(κ) = κA_p(κ) + log C_p(κ) − log C_p(0) lookup converting allocated rates to noise scales.
- An information-theoretic toolkit: vMF-approximated local posteriors on the LayerNorm sphere, closed-form Bhattacharyya coefficients, a data-processing-inequality guarantee that processing cannot increase distinguishability, a closed-form pushforward density for Q/K/V projections with a bounded unbiased Monte Carlo estimator, and common-random-numbers coupling giving a lower bound (BC_L) on effective-posterior overlap via joint concavity.
- Empirical demonstrations across two modalities: a behavioral knee at B ≈ 10⁴ nats, macroscopic per-tap rate allocation profiles, cross-stream scrambling experiments, continuous augmentation response curves in vision, and attention-head K-selectivity that independently recovers induction-head structure in GPT-2.

## Methodology

**Noisy reads.** In a pre-norm transformer every sublayer begins by reading the residual stream through LayerNorm, LN(u) = γ⊙n(u) + β with n(u) = (u − ⟨u⟩)/√(Var(u)+ε) (ε set to zero; the normalized state lives on the (d−2)-sphere of radius √d). The modification replaces each such read with: z = n(u), v = n(z + η), η ~ N(0, σ_i² I), followed by the usual affine transform — applied identically at every residual read, with an independent learned noise scale σ_i per tap.

**Rate budget.** A single global hyperparameter B fixes the total rate bound across taps: r_i = B·softmax(α_i) with Σ r_i = B by construction. Because Shannon mutual information through the read obeys I(U;V) ≤ log A_d − h(V|U) ≡ R_d(σ), and rotational symmetry makes R_d a function only of σ and dimension, each tap's allocated rate is converted to a noise scale via a precomputed 4000-point monotone lookup grid (accurate to 10⁻⁹). The objective contains no rate term — the distillation KL alone drives a "water-filling" allocation under the constraint. Noise is ramped from a near-clean σ_g = 0.05 to its target over the first 6 of 25 fine-tuning epochs.

**Training.** Student = pretrained model with noisy reads; teacher = frozen copy of the original model; loss L = E_x D_KL(p_teacher(·|x) ‖ p_student(·|x)) with T = 1. Vision: timm `vit_small_patch16_224` (D = 384) on ImageNet-1k at 224², batch 512, AdamW, lr 4×10⁻⁴ → 1×10⁻⁵ cosine, bfloat16 autocast on an A100, augmentation kept fixed across the budget sweep (Mixup/CutMix/drop-path disabled so the distillation loss has no teacher-KL conflict). Language: GPT-2-small (124M, D = 768) with `gpt2` weights on OpenWebText, EOD-packed 512-token windows, batch 32×512 tokens (81.9M tokens/epoch ≈ 2.05B over 25 epochs), all three dropout probabilities forced to 0.0 because dropout is an uncontrolled second noise source that would confound rate measurement; validation = perplexity plus next-token top-1 accuracy under the noise on a held-out 5000-document tail.

**Measuring distinguishability.** The raw residual state stays point-valued, but its functional effect at a tap is the conditional distribution of noisy reads — the tap's "local posterior", approximated as vMF(μ(u), κ) with a numerically calibrated σ↔κ map (analytic vs sampled mean resultant length agree to ~9 digits; Appx. B). For two inputs, the Bhattacharyya coefficient BC(p₁,p₂) between local posteriors is computed in closed form; lower BC = more distinguishable. Since squared Hellinger distance is an f-divergence, the data-processing inequality implies processing cannot increase distinguishability: an MLP's output BC is at least as large as its input reads' BC; attention's output distinguishability is bounded by the joint distribution of all visible reads.

**Q/K/V tracing and CRN.** For each head's query/key/value projection A, the projected overlap BC_A is evaluated by a Monte Carlo estimator over a closed-form pushforward density g^A(w) on the unit ball, sampled from the equal-weight mixture proposal (every summand bounded in [0,1]). Upstream stochasticity makes the effective posterior a mixture of local posteriors; coupling the two inputs' noise sequences via common random numbers yields an estimate that is a lower bound on the effective-posterior BC (by joint concavity / Cauchy–Schwarz), and interpolating BC_k between fully margined (k = 0) and fully coupled (k = L) noise prefixes traces the transition between the two.

**Probes.** Vision: 200 ImageNet-1k images with four continuously graded augmentations (blur, hue rotation, additive Gaussian noise, contrast fading) indexed by total pixel displacement ‖Δx‖₂ and compared at matched input-space distances; BC between CLS reads and the clean image's reads is tracked at every MLP and the final readout. Language: single-token perturbations (e.g., swapping `stranger`→`visitor` in one of two otherwise identical sentences); 80 minimally perturbed pairs generated by replacing nouns in OpenWebText samples, with magnitude controlled at 50 nats of Bhattacharyya distance at the layer-5 attention head immediately following the perturbation site; the fraction of upstream distinguishability preserved by each head's Q/K/V projections is compared against a random-64-dimensional-projection baseline.

## Results & Highlights

- **Behavioral knee at B ≈ 10⁴ nats in both modalities.** Sweeping the total read budget for ViT-S/ImageNet-1k (B from ~2.6×10⁴ to ~1.1×10⁷ nats) and GPT-2/OpenWebText (B = 1.5×10⁴ to 2.7×10⁶ nats), distillation loss and validation performance remain close to the base model above B ≈ 10⁴ nats and degrade steadily below it — an order-of-magnitude transition the authors use to define the base model's "operating resolution". In the Discussion the authors state the model "can remain within hundredths of a nat of the original behavior" while every read is stochastic and rate-limited, though no per-budget table of accuracy/perplexity deltas is given in the main text.
- **Learned allocation is structured, not uniform.** Allocation is relatively flat across depth with exceptions at the beginning and end: attention taps are generally assigned lower resolution than MLP taps in the same layer, and the final readout before the unembed is the most protected. In GPT-2 at B = 1.5×10⁴ nats, layer-1 reads and the layer-11 attention read hit the minimum realizable rate floor first. At heavily compressed settings the caps bind hard: at σ_g = 8, 22/25 GPT-2 taps sit at the numerical noise cap (σ ≤ 76.6 at p = 383; 10.1 at p = 767), vs 5/25 at σ_g = 1 (ViT-Small: 18/25 and 1/25 respectively).
- **K-selectivity independently recovers induction heads (GPT-2, B = 1.5×10⁴ nats, 80 noun-swap pairs at a 50-nat perturbation).** Seven middle-layer heads preserve a fraction of upstream Bhattacharyya distance into their K projections clearly above the random-64-dim-projection baseline (2.5–97.5% spread band); five of the seven coincide with high induction-score heads from the GPT-2 Small induction mosaic (Nanda 2022), and six of the seven have an independently observed connection to induction via the IOI analysis (Wang et al. 2023). Notably, L5H4 and L4H7 retain strong K selectivity despite having nearly zero prefix-matching score — structure the canonical attention statistic misses — and for all highlighted heads except L4H7 (unusually high across Q, K, and V) the selectivity is specific to K.
- **Vision response curves are augmentation-specific.** Over 200 ImageNet-1k images at matched input-space distances ‖Δx‖₂, the ViT-S student is most sensitive to blurring and least sensitive to fading, with the relative ordering broadly preserved at the final readout while all augmentations become more distinguishable in absolute terms there — resolution neighborhoods around each image sharpen with depth.
- **Cross-stream scrambling dissociates the modalities.** Independently shuffling repeated noise draws across sequence positions (approximating a product-of-marginals posterior) reduces the ViT's distillation loss — increasingly so at later depths, with fractional change ΔD_KL/D_KL reaching roughly −0.3 at late depths for the lower budgets — consistent with ViT aggregating many partially redundant noisy measurements; in GPT-2, early shuffling produces small budget-dependent degradations (coherent trajectories matter for causal LM) while later shuffling is mildly beneficial.
- **Compounded stochasticity stays mostly local in ViT.** Decomposing variance at each MLP tap shows ViT patch streams accumulate substantially more upstream-induced variance than the CLS stream, whose upstream variation remains smaller than its own local noise — until the unusually precise final read makes inherited variation dominate there.

## Authors & Credibility

Single author: Kieran Murphy (New Jersey Institute of Technology). The paper builds directly on the author's own prior work on rate-limited information flow in vision transformers (Murphy 2026, arXiv:2602.04784) and positions itself against classic variational information-bottleneck machinery (Kingma & Welling 2014; Alemi et al. 2017). The mathematics is handled carefully: closed-form vMF rate/BC formulas, a calibrated Gaussian→vMF approximation validated numerically (Appx. B), a derived pushforward density for Q/K/V projections with proof that the Monte Carlo estimator is unbiased and bounded (Appx. C), and an unusually candid appendix about numerical pitfalls (Bessel function overflow, double-precision underflow at κ ≥ 20, floors enforced by bisection). Code is released (murphyka.github.io/stoch_layernorm) and the implementation appendix is thorough enough to reproduce runs (exact schedules, batch sizes, token counts, validation protocol). It is, however, a single-author, non-peer-reviewed preprint, and all experiments are explicitly framed by the author as proof of concept.

## Critical Assessment

- **The headline behavior-preservation claim is not backed by a numbers table.** The claim that the stochastic student stays "within hundredths of a nat" of the original model, and that behavior "remained close" above the knee, is shown via distillation-loss and validation curves rather than a table of ImageNet top-1 deltas or GPT-2 perplexity deltas per budget. A reader cannot tell from the main text exactly how much task performance is sacrificed at, say, B = 1.5×10⁴ nats — an odd omission for a paper whose central knob is a behavioral fidelity/precision trade-off.
- **The rate bound is an upper bound whose tightness is assumed away.** R_d(σ) is tight only when measured states are marginally uniform on the LayerNorm sphere, which real residual streams will not generally satisfy; the paper is honest about this and about effective posteriors being mixtures broader than the vMF local posteriors, but the reported "nats" are therefore budget figures, not exact transmitted-information measurements. Relatedly, because the rate depends on representation dimension (p = 383 vs 767 give different floors, κ ≈ 383 vs ≈ 767), comparing budgets B across models or architectures is not straightforward — the paper notes this but the cross-modal knee comparison at ~10⁴ nats glosses over it.
- **The language-head analysis is small and narrowly tuned.** Eighty minimally perturbed pairs, generated by a single perturbation type (noun replacement) with magnitude pinned at 50 nats at one specific layer-5 tap; the induction-head correspondence is reported qualitatively ("five coincide", "six of the seven") with no enrichment statistic quantifying how unlikely this overlap is relative to random middle-layer heads beyond the random-projection BC baseline, and no rank correlation between K-selectivity and prefix-matching score. The L5H4/L4H7 finding is intriguing but rests on two heads.
- **Proof-of-concept scale only — by the author's own admission.** ViT-S (22M-class) and GPT-2-small (124M) with 25 taps each; whether allocation profiles, the knee location, and the head-selectivity patterns persist at larger scales is explicitly left open. The mechanistic explanation offered for the scrambling asymmetry between ViT and GPT-2 (redundant aggregation vs directed nested dependencies) is plausible but speculative, with no direct intervention isolating it.
- **Novelty is real but incremental relative to the author's own line.** The move from "rate-limited vision transformers" (Murphy 2026) to "any LayerNorm transformer, hard budget, no penalty term, Q/K/V tracing" is a meaningful generalization and the CRN lower-bound machinery is neat, but the conceptual frame (stochastic representations + information bottleneck + DPI) is assembled from well-known parts. Red flags are few: no cherry-picked benchmarks (there are no accuracy benchmarks to cherry-pick), the approximations and limitations section is unusually candid, and the code release plus reproducibility-grade appendix offset the single-author risk.

## Strengths

- Extremely cheap and architecture-agnostic: ~2L extra scalars, no loss modification, no architectural surgery beyond replacing LayerNorm internals; applicable to any pre-norm (reads) or post-norm (writes) LayerNorm transformer.
- Principled grounding: the data-processing inequality gives directional guarantees (processing cannot increase distinguishability) that purely geometric similarity measures (CKA, SVCCA, pareto distances) lack, exactly addressing the known geometry-vs-function gap.
- Technically complete pipeline: closed-form rate and BC formulas, reparameterized sampler decoupled from the rate allocation, bounded unbiased MC estimator for projected BC, CRN coupling with a concavity-based lower bound, and a shared-noise-prefix interpolation dial.
- Cross-modal consistency: the same rate-limited fine-tuning recipe yields modality-appropriate probes (graded augmentations in vision; token perturbations and head selectivity in language), and both land on a similar ~10⁴-nat operating knee.
- Validation against known interpretability ground truth: K-selectivity independently recovers induction-head structure and surfaces heads invisible to prefix-matching scores, which is exactly the kind of external correspondence a new probe needs.
- Reproducibility: public code, an implementation appendix with exact hyperparameters and numerical failure modes, and honest quantification of the vMF approximation quality (~9-digit agreement on the calibration statistic).

## Limitations

- Scale: only ViT-S and GPT-2-small; the author explicitly flags that persistence of the observed patterns at larger scales is untested.
- No numerical task-metric table (ImageNet accuracy / perplexity deltas per budget B) in the main text; the fidelity claim is qualitative at the operating point most analyses use.
- The rate figure is an upper bound contingent on marginal uniformity on the sphere, and per-tap analysis uses vMF local posteriors while the true effective posteriors are mixtures — paired CRN comparisons mitigate but do not eliminate this gap.
- Language analysis uses 80 noun-swap pairs at a single 50-nat perturbation magnitude measured at a single layer-5 tap; robustness across perturbation types, magnitudes, and layers is untested. The vision response curves use 200 images (medians with IQR, but a small sample).
- The induction-head overlap is qualitative; no statistical enrichment test against random head sets is reported.
- Budgets in nats are dimension-dependent, complicating cross-model comparison; the knee's location is reported empirically without a quantitative account of what sets it.

## Relevance

这篇论文与你的 "LLM Hidden States & Interpretability" 方向高度契合：它把表示相似性从几何度量改造为有数据处理不等式背书的统计可区分性，并提供了一个可直接套用在任意 LayerNorm Transformer 上的探测工具（Q/K/V 投影的 BC 保持率），在 GPT-2-small 上独立复现了 induction head 结构还发现了 prefix-matching 看不到的头。对关注机制可解释性与表示几何的研究者来说，这是一个成本低、可复现（代码已开源）的新探针，尽管目前只是 ViT-S/GPT-2-small 规模的概念验证。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the B ≈ 10⁴-nat knee scale with model size, parameter count, or training-token count, and is it predictable a priori from the rate expression's assumptions (e.g., measured marginal uniformity on the LayerNorm sphere)?
- Would a table of ImageNet top-1 / GPT-2 perplexity deltas per budget change the "hundredths of a nat" fidelity story, and where exactly between B = 1.5×10⁴ and 6.6×10⁴ does measurable task degradation begin?
- Is K-selectivity's agreement with induction heads statistically significant beyond the random-projection baseline (e.g., enrichment over random middle-layer head sets, or rank correlation with prefix-matching score across all heads)?
- Do the L5H4/L4H7-style heads — strong K-selectivity with near-zero prefix-matching — correspond to any functional circuit discoverable by activation patching or ablation, or are they epiphenomenal?
- How do conclusions transfer from the rate-limited student back to the unconstrained model — the author's stated open question — and would post-norm "noisy write" variants yield the same allocation structure?
- Can the scrambling asymmetry (ViT benefits, early GPT-2 degrades) be confirmed by a direct intervention on measurement redundancy rather than the speculative aggregation-vs-directed-dependencies explanation?
