WebWorld: The Browser as a World Model for Self-Improving Web Code

Jiajun Wu, Jian Yang, Yaxin Du, Wei Zhang et al. · Beihang × IQuest × SJTU × Langboat · Qwen3.5 4B/9B/27B

self-improving-agents web-code-generation world-model execution-verification
◆ Key metric
+14.9 pts

OOD transfer on MiniAppBench-Val (85.5 vs Raw 70.6) for WebWorld-27B; +5.3 on HTMLBench-400 (52.7 vs 47.4). The certificate ablation at 9B collapses to +0.4 — the gate is the load-bearing component.

Why this matters

Any self-improvement loop with a cheap deterministic simulator can adopt this: self-generated data becomes supervision only when something outside the model admits it.

⚑ Critical edge

Every result is a single seed-42 run with no variance — and the primary benchmark descends from the same group's concurrent HTMLCure work, with no fully independent third-party evaluation.

Methodology

  • 1Probe & critique. Headless Chromium drives the artifact through 14 deterministic interaction probes; the VLM critiques from observation only (never source), and must name an issue family with recorded evidence — "make it more polished" is invalid.
  • 2Compile to contract. A deterministic planner turns the critique into a typed interaction contract: target predicate + selector-anchored replay actions + preserve-set inherited from all previously certified capabilities. Uncertifiable contracts (broad containers, perception-only claims) are refused at admission.
  • 3Patch. A typed router maps the issue family to one of 20 skills (15 VLM lanes + 5 deterministic patch lanes); the diff is bounded by the contract's impact scope.
  • 4Certify. The browser re-executes the candidate. Acceptance requires target progress AND preservation of every p ∈ P_t — a monotone capability ratchet. Only certified transitions (QUALITY STEPs) join the 32,800-example SFT export.
  • ∘Training. Qwen3.5 at 4B/9B/27B, identical recipe per scale, 64–128×H100; every cell a single seed-42 run. Five-condition leakage audit against the shared-infrastructure benchmark.

Removing the oracle entirely drops TC pass 3.1 points below Raw — uncertified supervision is actively harmful.
  • Functional, not cosmetic: Rendering/Visual/Code dimensions barely move (<1 pt); the lift concentrates exactly where the gate constrains — TC pass (+9.7 at 27B) and Functionality.
  • Behavioral failures fall 16–29 pp per category: console errors 41%→12%, dead controls 38%→15%, keyboard failures 31%→9%, with preserve regressions near zero.
  • Depth diagnostic: with a fixed 5K budget, score rises monotonically with minimum certified depth (43.4 → 48.8 at depth ≥5), coming within 0.5 pt of the full 32,800-example pool — depth-filtered trajectories are denser supervision.
  • Benchmark context: 52.7 exceeds Kimi-K2.6 (49.8) and GPT-5.4 (49.2) on HTMLBench-400; 85.5 MiniApp matches Kimi-K2.6, near GPT-5.4 (87.7) — framed by the authors as context, not a controlled comparison.
  • Caveat surfaced honestly: Raw-27B degrades OOD transfer vs the un-tuned base (70.6 vs 76.5), so roughly half the +14.9 MiniApp headline is recovery from self-inflicted damage (+9.0 over base stands).

Strengths

  • Clean causal design: backbone, optimizer, schedule, prompt template and eval runner fixed across routes; equal-size, budget-matched acceptance-rule ablations isolate the gate.
  • Rare negative-result honesty: the certificate-free route actively hurts, and the Raw-hurts-transfer pattern sits in their own main table.
  • Deterministic, offline-replayable gate: every accept/reject packages executed evidence — audits are mechanical, not subjective.
  • Typed refusals turn rejections into diagnostics for the next critique without contaminating the export pool.
  • Five-condition leakage audit against the benchmark they share infrastructure with, plus an external OOD benchmark; code, logs and data promised.

Limitations

  • Single-file HTML only: multi-file, framework-heavy projects add dependency replay and scaffolding the certificate machinery doesn't yet handle.
  • Perceptual defects have no oracle: taste, brand identity, accessibility nuance fall back to the VLM proxy — inheriting the noise the method criticizes; unquantified share of corpus.
  • Determinism assumption: server state, timers, randomness still need faithful replay — asserted, not solved.
  • Wrong critiques waste rounds: the planner checks certifiability in principle, not correctness; no critique-level verifier.
  • Cost: routes consume 1,536–6,144 H20-equivalent card-hours; verified repair sits above filtering/one-shot baselines in cost-precision space.
  • Single seed throughout; no variance, no confidence intervals, no replication of the scale trend.

Questions for Further Exploration

  • Would the certificate gate still carry the gain under RL (execution-reward GRPO), where the policy learns from refusals directly — or is the ratchet specifically an SFT data-selection phenomenon?
  • How large is seed variance at 4B/9B? Three seeds per route would settle whether "the gap widens with capacity" is real.
  • What fraction of the certified pool relies on the VLM proxy for perceptual defects, and how does training on that subset compare to replay/capability-gain certificates only?
  • Can the contract grammar extend to multi-file codebases where the "world model" must include build tooling and dependency replay?
  • The critic VLM is never named: does a weaker critic starve the gate, or does browser admission make proposer quality mostly irrelevant?
  • Does the preserve-set ratchet scale to hundreds of inherited predicates — or does it over-constrain legitimate refactors that intentionally replace verified controls?
