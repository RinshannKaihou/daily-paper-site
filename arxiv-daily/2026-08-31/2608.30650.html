arXiv:2608.30650 2026-08-31 Multi-Turn Agents Hidden-State Probing Representation Geometry Adaptive Reasoning

Two nearly free geometric statistics of hidden-state trajectories separate failed agent episodes before termination — and trigger thinking online.

Geometry of Divergence: Tracking Hidden-State Trajectories for Adaptive Multi-Turn Reasoning

Jie Liang · Zhengxin Yu · Hamid Nasiri · Peter Garraghan  — Lancaster University · University of Huddersfield

Key metric

0.241→ 0.396 τ-Bench reward

Geometry-triggered thinking vs Never-think (Retail/Airline × Qwen3-14B/32B) — while mean tokens drop −11.2% (104.8k → 93.0k), beating Always-think (≈0.353) in 3 of 4 settings.

Why this matters

Bridges representation geometry to reliable agent systems: a near-zero-cost runtime "off-track detector" for long-horizon autonomous agents.

表征几何 → 可靠 agent 系统的桥接工作：几乎零成本的运行时"跑偏检测器"原型。

Critical edge

The headline averages the better of two triggers per setting — a post-hoc max; with 5 trials per configuration and no confidence intervals, several margins sit within noise.

Methodology

Residual stream @ query's last token→ Trajectory h_t = h_{t−1} + v_t→ κ_t · β_t per turn→ Online trigger δ_t
κ_t = cos(v_t, v_{t−1}) ∈ [−1,1] — +1 same-direction accumulation, 0 orthogonal, −1 reversal  ·  β_t = OLS slope of prefix dispersion V(λ) vs λ — positive = spreading, negative = contracting
  1. Trajectory construction. One hidden state per turn, at the final token of each user query, one fixed probe layer per model (L22 Qwen3-14B, L42 Qwen3-32B, L16 Llama-3.1-8B) — a position the model processes anyway.
  2. Turn-alignment deconfounding. Every metric is indexed by turn, not episode end, removing the artifact that failed episodes are mechanically longer, more curved, higher-variance.
  3. Separability analysis. Binary per-episode correctness labels; Mann–Whitney U tests across 4 tasks (Math, Code, Airline, Retail) × 3 LLMs.
  4. Action-chain decomposition. Sliding 3-action windows over Write / Read / Respond / Transfer; per-chain effect sizes localize where separability lives.
  5. Training-free control. Trigger extended thinking when κ_t < τ_κ or β_t > τ_β, vs Never / Always / Warm-up baselines and a Learned-HST classifier; 5 trials per config on vLLM (8×L40S, 4×H200) with true token accounting.

Strengths

  • The signal is essentially free online: three hidden states at one layer at a token position the model processes anyway — no auxiliary passes, no gradients, no fine-tuning.
  • Turn-alignment is a subtle but important control: without it, "failed trajectories are more curved" is partly an artifact of failed episodes being longer.
  • Localizes where separability lives (information-output chains, especially repeated Respond chains) — more actionable than a monolithic "hidden states predict failure."
  • Unusually honest reporting: negative significance, Warm-up collapse, and the Airline-32B loss are all in the main text.
  • Deployment-relevant metrics: token cost and max-turn terminations reported alongside reward; favorable reward-cost corner in 3 of 4 settings.

Limitations

  • Causal control experiments cover only τ-Bench tool-use domains; Math and Code appear only offline — no evidence the trigger helps open-ended reasoning.
  • 5 trials per configuration with no variance estimates on the headline table; several per-setting conclusions are within τ-Bench's known noise.
  • Evidence rests on 8B–32B open models, two of them same-family Qwen3; frontier-scale or reasoning-native behavior unknown.
  • Thresholds are post-hoc tuned per setting; "transferable thresholds" rests on optima shifting with the κ gap, not a validated transfer protocol.
  • Binary correctness is the only label; κ_t is a noisy three-point, single-layer, single-position statistic with no uncertainty quantification; no code release stated.

Questions for Further Exploration

  • Does κ_t retain discriminative power at frontier scale or on reasoning-native models whose residual-stream dynamics differ qualitatively?
  • Would aggregating geometry across multiple layers and token positions fix the variance-slope failures on Airline and denoise the three-point curvature estimate?
  • Can the geometry trigger compose with RL-learned thinking policies (e.g., AdaptThink) — redundant or complementary signals?
  • Is the extreme error concentration of Respond⁴ chains a property of τ-Bench's user simulator or a general signature of goal drift in multi-turn agents?
  • Authors speculate curvature could detect crescendo-style trajectory-stretching attacks from the inside — but attack trajectories are semantically coherent by construction; is κ_t actually perturbed?
