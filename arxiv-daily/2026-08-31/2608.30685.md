> **一句话总结：** 美团与中国科大联合提出 ATLAS 双视野诊断式评测框架：在"单轮内"用轨迹级信号（3 个执行阶段 × 5 类能力关切 + 合规护栏）定位工具调用链中缺陷的发生位置与性质，在"跨轮间"用用户级信号评估多轮服务是否保持上下文一致；41 个 LLM 判分信号经 F1≥0.90 校准（在自建 DJudge 上平均 F1 从 Curated Rubric 的 0.877 提至 0.952），其中 17 个信号蒸馏为 Qwen3.5-9B 高效判别模型（Relevance 组均值 76.9%→92.7%），并以校准信号作多维 GRPO 奖励优化 Qwen3.6-35B-A3B——线上 A/B 实测付费 GTV +7.32%、会话跟进率 +6.9%、P0 幻觉率 -6.9pp。

> **TL;DR:** ATLAS is a two-horizon diagnostic evaluation framework for industrial tool-use agents, built and deployed on Meituan Xiaotuan (local-services assistant). Within a turn, a matrix of execution locations (Thinking & Reflection / Tool & Skill Execution / Response Generation) crossed with capability concerns (Relevance / Factuality / Timeliness / Reliability / Intent & Planning) is instantiated as 41 calibrated LLM-judge signals plus rule-based checks, with Norms & Compliance as a guardrail; across turns, six user-wise signals check context carry-over and corrections. On the authors' DJudge benchmark (production Xiaotuan traffic, ~500–1,000 reference instances per signal), the calibrated ATLAS Judge reaches 0.952 average F1 vs 0.877 for Curated Rubric and 0.800 for Direct Judge on the same DeepSeek-V4-Pro backbone. Seventeen signals are distilled into Qwen3.5-9B diagnostic models (Relevance group 76.9%→92.7% F1, +15.8, beating Qwen3.6-27B at 90.8%), and the same signals serve as multi-dimensional GRPO rewards for Qwen3.6-35B-A3B; a 3-day online A/B on 10% of live traffic shows paid GTV +7.32%, session follow-up rate +6.9%, and P0 hallucination rate -6.9pp versus the deployed baseline. Worth reading if you build production agent-evaluation loops; less useful as a reproducible research artifact since everything is grounded in Meituan-internal logs and interfaces.

## 核心贡献

- **双视野诊断结构（Within-turn / Across-turn）**：将工业 agent 评测拆成两个互补对象——当前请求触发的完整迭代执行轨迹（定位缺陷"发生在哪"+"性质是什么"），以及同一用户跨多个生命周期的持续服务（上下文是否被携带、修正、更新）。单轮内用"执行阶段 × 能力关切"矩阵（Thinking & Reflection、Tool & Skill Execution、Response Generation × Relevance、Factuality、Timeliness、Reliability、Intent & Planning），Norms & Compliance 作为矩阵外的硬护栏（安全、隐私、格式有效性、结构门控）。
- **可执行信号规范与校准**：每个 LLM 诊断信号配有显式证据范围、判定边界（含豁免与不适用情形）和结构化输出；校准以 F1≥0.90 为准入线，参照集来自真实业务日志（响应类信号由 5 名资深标注员、每样本 ≥3 人独立标注；轨迹类信号用 Claude Opus-4.6 / GPT-5.4 / Gemini 3.1-Pro 三师一致过滤构造高置信参照，并用受控扰动覆盖安全等低频边界）。
- **判分模型蒸馏**：按执行维度 × 能力关切 × 输入输出形式分组的蒸馏策略，把 17 个信号从 LLM judge 蒸馏到 Qwen3.5-9B（教师投票监督、置信度过滤、think 字段低权重 0.1），支撑低延迟低成本的大规模评测。
- **诊断信号即奖励**：校准信号的加权组合直接作为 GRPO 的标量奖励，优化后的策略再在同一框架下复评，形成"评测→优化→再评测"的语义闭环。
- **真实流量验证**：在美团小团生产流量上完成离线回放评测与 6/27–6/29 线上 A/B（10% 流量），同时给出产品指标与 1000 条人工抽审结果。

## Key Contributions

- **Dual-horizon diagnostic structure**: separates (1) trajectory-wise within-turn evaluation, which localizes deficiencies by execution location (Thinking & Reflection, Tool & Skill Execution, Response Generation) and capability concern (Relevance, Factuality, Timeliness, Reliability, Intent & Planning), from (2) user-wise across-turn evaluation (six LLM signals: Cross-Turn Reference, Reasoning-Intent Alignment, Tool-Intent Alignment, Previous-Round Correction, Cross-Round Contradiction, Cross-Round Repetition) that checks whether prior context, corrections, and constraints are carried into later lifecycles. Norms & Compliance sits outside the matrix as a non-negotiable guardrail (safety, privacy, integrity, format validity, structural gate).
- **Executable, calibrated signals**: each signal specifies admissible evidence scopes, decision boundaries with exemptions, and structured outputs; LLM judge interfaces are admitted only when F1 ≥ ~0.90 on high-confidence reference sets built from real Xiaotuan logs (manual annotation for response-level signals; three-teacher unanimity filtering plus controlled perturbations for trajectory-level signals).
- **Group-wise judge distillation**: 17 selected signals distilled onto Qwen3.5-9B with multi-teacher voting supervision (DeepSeek-V4-Pro/Flash, GLM-5.1), format- and confidence-based sample filtering, and low loss weight (0.1) on <think> fields, enabling low-latency evaluation at scale.
- **Diagnosis-to-optimization loop**: the weighted sum of calibrated diagnostic signals forms the scalar GRPO reward, so policy optimization consumes the same semantics used for evaluation; the optimized policy is then reassessed under the same framework.
- **Real-traffic validation**: offline replay with production-facing tool interfaces plus a live A/B test on the Xiaotuan entry (June 27–29, 10% traffic slice) with product metrics and a 1,000-response human audit.

## Methodology

**Dual-horizon structure.** ATLAS treats two industrial service objects as distinct: the complete iterative trajectory triggered by a current request (tool retrieval → comparison → synthesis), and the sequence of such lifecycles within one ongoing user interaction. Within-turn evaluation instantiates a 3×5 diagnostic matrix; cells without a stable signal family are intentionally left uninstantiated. Signals are either rule-based (programmatically verifiable boundaries: format, schema legality, deterministic counts) or LLM-based (semantic judgments over query, context, tool outputs, reasoning, and response).

**Signal construction.** For a sample x under metric m, the evaluator first identifies locally assessable objects A_m(x) = {a_m,1..K} (claims, supply items, typo positions, reflection steps), produces local verdicts z_m,k, and aggregates via a metric-specific normalization φ_m into r_m(x) — keeping aggregates (repetition rate, stale-item ratio, correction rate) grounded in local assessments rather than a single holistic judgment. Each LLM judge prompt has five parts: role/task definition, input field specification, decision criteria with exemption boundaries, stepwise review procedure, and structured output format; binary pass/fail is the default verdict.

**Calibration.** F1 is the usability criterion (class imbalance makes it preferable to accuracy), with threshold τ_m typically 0.90. Reference sets: Response Generation signals use manual annotation (5 senior annotators, ≥3 labels per sample, expert adjudication of disputes); trajectory-level signals use unanimous agreement across three strong teachers (Claude Opus-4.6, GPT-5.4, Gemini 3.1-Pro), plus controlled query/trajectory perturbations to cover rare boundaries like safety violations. Below-threshold judges go through a boundary-refinement loop (trace errors → revise criteria/evidence binding/exemptions/examples → re-evaluate).

**Distillation.** Groups are formed by execution dimension × capability concern × input–output form (only fully compatible signals share a model). Student: Qwen3.5-9B, trained with Swift on 8 H20 GPUs (AdamW, lr 1e-5, batch 128, max output 2148). Supervision comes from multi-teacher voting; samples where the base model already predicts correctly with high log-probability are removed as low-gain; <think> fields get loss weight 0.1. Evaluation uses the same high-confidence DJudge references, with query-disjoint training data.

**Policy optimization.** Reward R(x,τ) = Σ λ_s r_s(x,τ) over signals in S_train; GRPO with group-normalized advantage on Qwen3.6-35B-A3B, 32 H20 GPUs, Verl + Megatron + vLLM, lr 1e-6, batch 128, rollout group size 16, temperature 1.0, max output 8192. Offline replay executes tool calls through production-facing interfaces with live business data for merchants/POIs/supply.

## Results & Highlights

- **RQ1 (judge calibration):** On DJudge (41 LLM-based signals, ~500–1,000 reference instances each), ATLAS Judge scores 0.952 average F1 vs 0.877 for Curated Rubric (+0.075), 0.826 for Static Rubric, and 0.800 for Direct Judge (+0.152), all on the same DeepSeek-V4-Pro backbone. The gap is largest exactly where boundaries are subtle: Timeliness 0.961 vs Direct Judge's 0.691 (+0.270) and Reliability 0.972 vs 0.811 (+0.161).
- **RQ2 (distillation):** The distilled Qwen3.5-9B models average 92.7% F1 on the 5 Relevance signals vs 76.9% for the untuned Qwen3.5-9B backbone (+15.8), also beating much larger general models (Qwen3.6-27B 90.8%, Qwen3.6-35B-A3B 88.2%) and approaching the DeepSeek-V4-Pro teacher at 95.2%. Per-signal extremes: Primary Rec. Match 69.5%→91.9% (+22.4) and Location Constraint 69.4%→98.6% (+29.2) in the Factuality group (group avg 81.4%→94.6%, +13.2). One regression exists: POI Traceability drops 99.2%→98.6% (-0.6) after distillation.
- **RQ3 (offline replay):** The ATLAS-optimized policy achieves the highest mean ATLAS score in all six reported groups (three within-turn dimensions, Norms & Compliance, user-wise layer) over both the base model and the deployed version, with the strongest improvement in Tool & Skill Execution — results are shown only as a Figure 6 bar chart, without a numeric table.
- **RQ4 (online A/B, 3 days, 10% traffic):** Paid GTV +7.32% and session follow-up rate +6.9% (the two largest product gains); per-user dwell time +1.26%, AI message read-through rate +0.92%, AI search query volume +0.76%, effective QV +0.50%, 5-second exit rate -0.31%. The 1,000-response human audit moves the same way: response–supply relevance +6.2pp, P0 hallucination rate -6.9pp, ID hallucination rate -2.0pp.
- **Scale of deployment context:** Xiaotuan operates at hundreds of millions of active users, with 700M merchant-information verifications, 1.3B real-review recalibrations, and 3.4M+ merchants using its AI-assistant functions.

## Critical Assessment

- **Circularity risk in reference construction.** The trajectory-level "high-confidence references" that define ground truth are built by unanimous agreement among Claude Opus-4.6, GPT-5.4, and Gemini 3.1-Pro — the same kind of strong-model consensus that the ATLAS Judge (backbone DeepSeek-V4-Pro, itself a distillation teacher) is trained/prompted to emulate. F1 against such references partly measures agreement with teacher consensus rather than human-verified correctness; only response-level signals get genuine 3-annotator human labels. The paper is transparent about the procedure but does not quantify human–teacher disagreement on trajectory signals.
- **Self-referential benchmark.** DJudge, DPolicy-Eval, and DRL are all constructed from Xiaotuan traffic by the authors; the headline claim "ATLAS Judge is the strongest interface" is validated on the benchmark whose decision boundaries were refined to match ATLAS's own signal specifications. The prompt-variant baselines (Direct/Static/Curated Rubric) share the ATLAS-defined boundary, so the comparison measures calibration effort, not whether ATLAS's diagnostic taxonomy is the right one; no external process-evaluation framework (e.g., AgentEval) is run on the same data.
- **Thin statistical reporting for the A/B test.** The online evidence is a 3-day window (June 27–29) on a 10% traffic slice with no confidence intervals, significance tests, or per-day breakdown; paid GTV +7.32% over 72 hours could reflect weekday effects or supply drift, though the synchronous control mitigates this. The P0 hallucination rate drop of -6.9pp is sampled from only 1,000 audited responses — wide uncertainty.
- **RQ3 lacks numbers.** The offline replay result (the direct evidence that diagnostic rewards improve the policy) is presented only as a bar chart with no table, no ablation on λ_s weights, and no comparison against an outcome-only-reward GRPO baseline — so the central claim that multi-dimensional diagnostic feedback beats a scalar outcome reward is not actually demonstrated.
- **Distillation is not uniformly safe.** POI Traceability regresses 0.6 points below the untuned backbone, and Failure Transparency gains only +0.3 — signals where the base model is already near ceiling degrade or stall under distillation, which the paper mentions but does not analyze (label noise near ceiling? capability loss?).
- **Reproducibility is effectively zero outside Meituan.** The rule signals encode business-specific failure modes (POI traceability, supply attribution, merchant supply), the replay environment uses production interfaces and live business data, and no dataset or judge release is described. The framework's *shape* (matrix + calibration gate + distillation + reward reuse) is transferable; its actual contents are not.

## Strengths

- The dual-horizon split is a genuinely useful conceptual contribution: separating "did this execution go wrong and where" from "did service stay aligned as the user's context evolves" matches how production failures actually manifest, and the across-turn layer (correction carry-over, cross-round contradiction/repetition) is rarely formalized in agent evaluation.
- End-to-end semantic continuity: the same calibrated signal is used for diagnosis, distillation supervision, RL reward, and post-hoc reassessment — a cleaner loop than the usual disjoint eval/reward setups.
- Rigorous judge-calibration gate (F1 ≥ 0.90 with explicit boundary refinement) with an honest usability criterion (F1 over accuracy due to class imbalance) — a discipline most LLM-as-judge papers skip.
- The distillation result is practically valuable: a 9B student matching 92.7% vs the 95.2% teacher on Relevance signals, beating a 27B dense and a 35B-A3B general model, shows calibrated boundary transfer matters more than raw capacity for judge workloads.
- Full offline-to-online evidence chain, including negative-direction product metrics reported (5s exit -0.31% is favorable, but per-signal distillation regressions are also visible in Table 2).

## Limitations

- All evidence is internal to Meituan Xiaotuan: datasets, judges, tool interfaces, and the optimized policy cannot be reproduced or externally audited; no public data or code release is stated.
- No ablations for key design choices: group-wise vs independent/shared distillation, the λ_s reward weights, the 0.1 think-loss weight, the 0.90 threshold, and the perturbation-augmentation strategy are all asserted, not measured.
- Offline replay (RQ3) is unquantified (chart only), and there is no baseline of GRPO with outcome-only or human-generic rubric rewards to isolate the value of ATLAS-specific diagnostic feedback.
- Online test window is short (3 days) and statistical significance is unreported; the human audit sample (1,000 responses) is small for hallucination-rate deltas.
- Trajectory-signal ground truth relies on teacher unanimity rather than human verification, coupling judge evaluation to the very model family being evaluated.
- The 3×5 matrix has intentionally uninstantiated cells and the concrete signal families are Xiaotuan-specific (supply attribution, POI traceability), so porting to another domain requires re-deriving most of the inventory.

## Relevance

这篇论文与你关注的"长程自主 agent 系统的可靠性与可信评测"以及"自动化评测/奖励设计"高度相关：它的校准式 LLM-judge 门控、judge 蒸馏与"诊断信号即奖励"的闭环，正是工业级 agent 评测基础设施的一个完整落地样本。不过它属于业务侧评测框架而非 self-evolving research loop 或硬件级故障检测，可借鉴的是方法论骨架（证据绑定 + F1≥0.90 校准门 + 分组蒸馏）而非具体数据。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the dual-horizon framework's calibration gate (F1 ≥ 0.90 per signal) survive transfer to a different industrial domain, or is the signal inventory too Xiaotuan-specific to reuse?
- How would teacher-constructed trajectory references compare against genuine human adjudication on long tool-use trajectories — is three-teacher unanimity a biased proxy near subtle boundaries like Reflection Hallucination?
- Can the diagnostic-reward GRPO results be separated from the outcome signal? An ablation against outcome-only and generic-rubric rewards (with the λ_s weights swept) is missing from RQ3.
- Do the -6.9pp P0-hallucination and +7.32% GTV gains persist beyond a 3-day window, and are they stable across traffic slices and supply-seasonality effects?
- Is group-wise distillation actually better than per-signal independent models at equal parameter budget, given that POI Traceability regressed below the untuned backbone?
