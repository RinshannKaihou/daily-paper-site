Why this matters

Quantifies per-sample “information present but not used” — an input-intervention + probe template for diagnosing LLM internal disconnects in any modality.

干预 + 探针 + 跨模型统计检验的组合，量化“信息在场但未被路由”——可解释性工具箱里值得借鉴的测量范式。

Critical edge

VSI alone wins exactly 1 of 18 cells — a diagnostic lens for confidently-wrong-while-blind errors, not a deployable abstention method.

01 Methodology

  • Perturbation: question noun phrase parsed (no model call) → Grounding-DINO open-vocabulary box (conf ≥ 0.3) → SAM pixel mask (dilated 4 px) → Gaussian blur σ=20. Detection-failure fallback: whole-image blur (<3% on POPE/MMVP, ~19% on HallusionBench).
  • VSI metric: KL divergence between the top-50 next-token distributions of clean vs blurred passes, greedy decoding — captures full distribution shift, not just argmax flips. A whole-image variant isolates “uses images at all” from “uses the queried content”.
  • Grid: six VLMs across four distinct vision towers (CLIP, SigLIP, two native ViTs) × POPE / MMVP / HallusionBench (region + whole) + MMStar (whole only) — 42 cells, same sample pool shown to every model.
  • Disconnect probe: logistic regression on each model's own tower's final-layer features (grouped 5-fold CV) on an n=83 low-VSI pool + matched high-VSI control; a frozen CLIP probe serves as external witness (0.82±0.04); RBF adds ≤ 0.02.
  • Statistics: σ-ablation rank correlation 0.76–0.97, 10⁵-shuffle permutation tests, bootstrap CIs, pinned seeds/versions, ~32 A100-hours; code + per-sample CSVs released.

The gap closes exactly where the model uses vision — routing failure, not representation failure.

  • It's the queried content, not any perturbation: whole-image-blur control collapses insensitivity to ≈ 6% vs region-blur 41.5% / patch-occlusion 42.0%. Dip test does not reject unimodality — honestly reported as a heavy left tail.
  • Sample-intrinsic: VSI rankings transfer across all 15 model pairs, grand-mean Spearman ρ=+0.40 (POPE 0.55, MMVP 0.34, HallusionBench 0.32); cross-family pairs share no encoder weights — weakest pair ρ=+0.20 still p=3.4×10⁻⁴.
  • Best diagnostic: Qwen2.5-VL-32B MMStar math / science AUROC 0.851 / 0.867, quintile error ratio 4.05× — but n=34/46 with wide CIs, and the signal inverts on LLaVA-1.5 (math 0.29).
  • Signal-selection map (18 cells): max-prob wins 10, VSI-containing hybrid 7, VSI alone 1. Where it helps: Qwen2.5-VL-7B MMVP hybrid 0.676 vs max-prob 0.496 (+0.18); LLaVA-NeXT POPE max-prob 0.853 vs VSI 0.541.
  • The dangerous quadrant: confidently-wrong-while-ignoring-vision = 25–30% of the bottom-VSI quintile on HallusionBench; hybrid PRR@80 0.19 vs max-prob −0.05 (which preferentially rejects correct predictions). 11 shared samples where all three baselines are wrong with softmax > 0.99 and VSI < 0.01.
  • Data hygiene flag: Qwen2.5-VL-7B degenerates (“addCriterion”) on 333/500 POPE samples — excluded, a selection effect the paper names but does not bound.

03 Strengths

  • Crisp causal framing: remove the evidence, watch the softmax — probe + counterfactual cleanly separates “information present” from “information used”.
  • The high-VSI control (probes 0.86–0.91, argmax-change rises) is a direct answer to the “probes are trivially easy” objection.
  • Cross-model generality with real statistics: 15 pairs across 4 vision towers, permutation p-values, bootstrap CIs, released null distributions.
  • Rigorous negative results: shows where its own signal loses (10/18 max-prob, MMVP inversion) and builds the conditional-ensemble framing on that evidence.
  • Reproducibility first-class: per-sample CSVs, pinned checkpoints/versions with quantified cross-version sensitivity, modest compute (~32 A100-hours).
  • Honest self-description: heavy tail, not bimodality; explicitly proposes no new model, training objective, or abstention method.

04 Limitations

  • Mechanism numbers rest on one model's sample selection: the n=83 pool is Qwen3-VL-flagged, not each model's own bottom quintile; the alternative is unreported.
  • Small-n cells carry the flashiest numbers: MMStar AUROCs from n=34/46, CIs as wide as [0.73, 0.94]; no multiple-testing correction across 18 cells.
  • HallusionBench is entangled with detection failure (~19% whole-image fallback, weakest ρ=0.32) — part of the 97% headline may be measurement noise.
  • “Tracks hallucination” is softer than it sounds: quadrant alignment, not CHAIR-style scoring; no direct hallucination pipeline.
  • Input–output causality only: no attention/KV-cache analysis; activation patching across the cross-modal projection left as future work; linear probes are lower bounds.
  • Deployment: ~2.3 s/sample on 32B plus DINO/SAM calls, and the 1/18 standalone win rate means VSI must be paired with softmax signals.

05 Questions for further exploration

  1. Would the encoder–LLM gap numbers change materially if each model's low-VSI pool were selected by that model itself?
  2. Does the gap persist under chain-of-thought decoding, where grounding-decay work suggests vision may be re-engaged after the first token?
  3. Can activation patching across the cross-modal projection localize which language-head layers drop the perturbation signal?
  4. Is the low-VSI sub-population predictable from image/question features alone — a cheap pre-hoc flag without a perturbed forward pass?
  5. Would targeted retraining close the gap or merely shift the tail — and would it degrade the cross-model rank agreement that makes VSI diagnostic?
  6. How does VSI behave on open-ended generation versus the 8-token multiple-choice regime where shifts more readily flip the argmax?
