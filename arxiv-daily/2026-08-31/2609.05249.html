Consecutive constant-class outputs (a class absent from the image pool) at unchanged 23–24 ms cadence — kernel log silent, memtest passed, power rails nominal, top-1 oracle blind. Wide-field Linux-SEFI cross section: 3.6×10⁻¹⁰ cm²/system [1.5–7.5].

Why This Matters

SDC detection can't rely on output checks or watchdogs alone: real beam data shows silent corruption slipping past every standard monitor.

Critical Edge

The headline field contrast rests on one ambiguous run record: nominal p = 0.016 degrades to 0.086–0.46 under alternative readings, and field is confounded with run order and accumulated dose.

Methodology

Setup — Ultra96-V2 (Zynq UltraScale+ XCU3EG) · TCU 16×16 systolic array, INT8 · ResNet-20, fixed 10-image CIFAR-10 pool · Linux/PYNQ · AIC-144 cyclotron, 20/40/58 MeV

  • Deliberately unhardened baseline. Open-source TCU accelerator through a Linux-managed PS–PL path; LPDDR4 without ECC; no watchdog, no SEM controller, no redundant inference checker. 42.8% of configuration bits marked essential.
  • Block protocol. Each block: 17-pattern memtester pass over 10 MB, 100 inferences (~2.34 s, logged every 23–24 ms), power-rail samples, kernel-log monitoring, OCM ECC telemetry; scheduled PL reconfiguration + model reload between blocks — refreshes PL logic and model buffers, but not Linux, OCM, or LPDDR4.
  • Two beam collimations. 6.19×10¹⁰ p/cm² monitored fluence, 184.3 Gy; a 2-cm SoC-centered field vs a 4-cm wide field covering SoC + LPDDR4 + board area.
  • Statistics. Onset-based counting (a persistent corruption counts once); per-monitor cross-section denominators; Garwood 95% Poisson intervals; exact one-sided binomial field contrast — unpreregistered, no multiplicity adjustment, with a sensitivity table over the ambiguous record.

Strengths

  • Statistically disciplined small-count campaign: onset counting, monitor-matched denominators, Garwood intervals, and an honest refusal to claim significance.
  • The scheduled PL reload doubles as a persistence probe: the event surviving reload and the recurring OCM address are genuinely diagnostic about where corruption can live.
  • Auditable record: campaign timeline, per-block indicator table, archived console photographs; class-histogram forensics rule out trivial stale-buffer explanations.
  • Three transferable reporting practices: onset counting, monitor-matched denominators with stated blind spots, recovery logged without causal attribution.
  • Transparent LLM-usage disclosure and a limitations section that anticipates most external criticism.

Limitations

  • Single board, accelerator, model, and 10-image pool: cross sections are system-specific and low-statistics; no device or model variation.
  • No component-level attribution: run order not randomized, field confounded with accumulated dose, 58 MeV lacks a small-field counterpart, five unmonitored exposures fall outside every denominator.
  • Structural monitor blind spots: top-1 only (no logits), memtest covers 0.5% of LPDDR4 in 18 of 92 blocks, no intermediate tensors or memory-traffic capture.
  • Recovery causality unassignable; two power-rail samples per block cannot exclude single-event latch-up.
  • 40-MeV record losses (19/28 blocks with class records) make those output numbers the least trustworthy; run-4's ±150 s timing propagates into every headline number.
  • No mitigation evaluated: the central recommendation (content checks + staged recovery) has no measured overhead, coverage, or detection-latency numbers.

Questions for Further Exploration

  1. Which exact state held the constant class — a stuck LPDDR4 fetch, a corrupted accumulator path, or PS-side driver state? Would score vectors plus DMA/accelerator-state checkers disambiguate?
  2. Would per-inference known-answer probes or score-vector checksums bound such an episode to one probe interval, and at what overhead on this systolic-array design?
  3. Does SEM scrubbing or PL-memory ECC eliminate the constant-class pattern while leaving Linux-SEFI rates unchanged — are the two endpoints independent?
  4. With randomized field order and dose-controlled small-field runs, does the wide-vs-small contrast hold at component level and reproduce at 58 MeV?
  5. Do the cross sections transfer to other open accelerators (FINN, hls4ml) or transformer workloads over the same Linux-managed PS–PL path?
