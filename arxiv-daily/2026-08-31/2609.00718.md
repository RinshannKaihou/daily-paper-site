> **一句话总结：** 三校团队（UMY/NAIST/KFUPM）在 Gym-Duckietown 上训练 belief-state PPO 视觉运动驾驶策略（73,986 参数 MLP actor），并首次对其压缩管线做**逐阶段闭环评估**：结构化剪枝到 6,210 参数（**−91.6%**）时能力即全面断裂（64 宽 FP32 actor 在全部 5 条课程失败，C3 停车课程 8/8 违规）；**平衡重演蒸馏**（62,176 个横跨 5 条课程的公共状态）完整恢复全部测试行为，而重演只覆盖 C3/C4 的同配置蒸馏仅恢复那两条课程；随后 **INT8 量化（PTQ 与 QAT 均如此）再次击穿恰好需要"中断并恢复驾驶"的两条停车课程**（FP32 源 8/8 完成 C3，PTQ 丢 3/8，且 A6 在线前 0.2 m 冻结 v_cmd=0.000、A8 以 v_cmd=0.077 越线闯停，失效方向相反），同 checkpoint 的 FP16 则与 FP32 完全一致通过全部课程；纯 action-fidelity 验收同时产生假阴性（能开好全部课程的 A4 被拒）与假阳性（更危险的 A8 被接受）。

> **TL;DR:** The paper audits a compression pipeline (structured pruning → knowledge distillation → integer quantization / reduced precision) stage by stage **in closed loop** rather than with static fidelity scores. A belief-state PPO actor (3-layer perceptron, 73,986 params) trained on five Gym-Duckietown curricula (C0 lane following … C4 crossing pedestrians + stop sign) is pruned to width 64 (6,210 params, −91.6%): the pruned FP32 actor A1 fails **all five** curricula (e.g., 8/8 stop violations on C3). Distillation from the original actor recovers exactly as much as its rehearsal data covers — A2 (C4/C3-only rehearsal) passes only C3/C4 while A3 (balanced rehearsal, 62,176 public states across all five curricula) passes **all five** including every action-fidelity check, flipping three curricula from total failure to full recovery. From the same A3 checkpoint, INT8 post-training quantization (A6) and QAT (A8) both re-break exactly the two stop curricula (the FP32 source completed 8/8 C3 episodes; INT8 loses 3/8; A6 freezes at v_cmd=0.000 m/s parked 0.2 m before the line while A8 crosses it at v_cmd=0.077 m/s), whereas the FP16 cast A9 passes all five identically to FP32. Cost-wise INT8 is the fastest actor (12.9 μs vs 19.4 μs FP32 median latency, ~1.5× speedup) yet serializes to ~1.5× the FP32 file size, and FP16 adds 26% latency on x86. Fidelity-only acceptance yields both false negatives (A4, which drives every curriculum, is rejected) and false positives (A8 is accepted while being the more dangerous driver).

## 核心贡献

- **阶段级闭环评估协议**：把压缩管线（剪枝→蒸馏→量化/降精度）视为一系列**行为转变**而非最终精度分数，每个中间配置都在 5 条课程 × 8 seeds（每候选 40 episodes、共 400）上闭环评测；验收判据由 10 项检查（完成、进度、碰撞、安全距离、停车违规、停车完成、重启、车道失效、无效位姿、最小行人间距）加"相对 FP32 参考的配对回归容差 δ_k"组成，且**阈值在任何结果产生前固定**。
- **能力断裂点定位**：结构化剪枝（按 L2 连通性 + 绝对偏置打分，宽度 256→64，参数 73,986→6,210，−91.6%）是能力**首次且全面**断裂的阶段——与任务和课程无关，全 5 条课程皆失败。
- **重演覆盖决定恢复上限**：损失、优化器、batch size、学习率、训练预算完全相同，仅重演数据覆盖不同——C4 聚焦重演（A2）只恢复 C3/C4，平衡重演（A3，62,176 个公共状态）恢复**完整的被测行为**；重演覆盖把 3 条课程从完全失败翻转为完全恢复。
- **量化在恢复之后再次破坏**：INT8 的 PTQ（A6）与 QAT（A8）从同一 A3 checkpoint 出发，都恰好击穿要求策略**中断并恢复自己驾驶**的停车课程 C3/C4，且失效方向相反（A6 冻结在线前、A8 越线闯停）；而 FP16 转换（A9）与 FP32 源逐字一致地通过全部课程。同一流程作用在**未剪枝**的 256 宽 actor 上则无此现象。
- **蒸馏位置的实证**：A5（剪枝+PTQ、无蒸馏）全败，A6 与 A5 唯一差别是在 PTQ 之前插入了平衡蒸馏，却能完整保留 C0–C2——在本设定下"先蒸馏后量化"保住了远多于"直接量化剪枝 actor"的能力（作者强调这是位置观察而非因子化结论）。
- **Action-fidelity 不可替代闭环验收**：yaw-rate 通道的 Spearman 相关一致性检验在本模型族上同时产生假阴性（A4 能开好全部课程却因一致性不足被拒）与假阳性（A8 更危险却会被接受），说明一致性指标既非必要也非充分。

## Key Contributions

- **A stage-wise closed-loop acceptance protocol**: every intermediate configuration of the compression pipeline (pruning, distillation, quantization/reduced precision) must drive all five Gym-Duckietown curricula on the same 8 seeds (40 episodes per candidate, 400 total) against a pre-registered set of 10 checks plus a paired regression tolerance δ_k relative to the FP32 reference.
- **Localization of capability loss**: structured pruning to <10% of the network (73,986 → 6,210 params, −91.6%) is the stage where task-level capability first breaks, uniformly across all five curricula.
- **Rehearsal coverage sets the recovery ceiling**: with loss, optimizer, batch size, learning rate, and budget held fixed, C4-focused rehearsal (A2) recovers only C3/C4, while balanced rehearsal across all five curricula (A3) recovers the complete tested behavior — coverage alone flips three curricula from complete failure to full recovery.
- **Quantization re-breaks a recovered policy**: INT8 via both PTQ (A6) and QAT (A8) fails exactly the two stop curricula that require the policy to interrupt and resume its own driving, with opposite failure signatures, while the FP16 cast (A9) of the same checkpoint preserves every behavior; the same procedure on the unpruned 256-wide actor preserves everything.
- **Evidence that action fidelity cannot gate deployment**: the fidelity-only test produces false negatives (A4) and false positives (A8) on the same model family, so closed-loop task acceptance cannot be inferred from it.

## Methodology

**Task and formulation.** The driving task is visuomotor lane following with pedestrian avoidance and stop-line compliance in Gym-Duckietown, formulated as a POMDP with five curricula: C0 (small loop, pedestrians + random stops), C1 (large loop, pedestrians), C2 (adds crossing pedestrians), C3 (removes pedestrians, adds stop signs), C4 (crossing pedestrians + stop sign on a large loop, doubled 4,200-step horizon). Actions are (v, ω) commands up to 0.4 m/s and 8 rad/s; the reward is a fixed weighted sum of six terms (forward progress, lane keeping, pedestrian proximity, stop compliance, action smoothness, terminal bonus/penalty) used only for training — acceptance is defined on task outcomes directly.

**Belief pipeline (never compressed).** Instead of a recurrent policy on raw frames, the belief is factored into interpretable components: a fine-tuned YOLO11n detector supplies bounding boxes, whose bottom-center pixel is ray-cast through a calibrated camera model onto the ground plane to give range r = √(x²+y²) and bearing θ (with an offline-fitted linear range correction); a MobileNetV3-small regressor outputs lateral offset, heading, and curvature. These feed an EKF over planar position/velocity for the lane, a pedestrian EKF with a Bernoulli existence filter, and a stop-mode state machine, assembled into a fixed 29-dimensional belief vector b_t. The trained actor is a deterministic three-layer perceptron (256→256→2, tanh) with 73,986 parameters, trained with belief-state PPO across the curricula.

**Compression configurations (A0–A9).** A0 is the FP32 reference. A1 is structured pruning of each hidden layer to width 64 (units ranked by summed L2 incoming/outgoing connectivity plus absolute bias; lowest indices kept), yielding 6,210 parameters (−91.6%). A2/A3 distill from the frozen A0 teacher via a smooth-L1 loss on deterministic actions with phase-balanced sampling, identical in every hyperparameter and differing *only* in rehearsal coverage: A2 rehearses C4-development states (C3/C4-focused), A3 uses 62,176 public states drawn across all five curricula. A4 (PTQ-only on unpruned actor) and A5 (prune+PTQ, no KD) are controls. A6 casts A3's weights to INT8 via eager static PTQ on x86 (symmetric per-channel weights, per-tensor activations, calibrated on development states); A7 is KD+PTO followed by QAT; A8 is prune+KD(bal) followed by QAT; A9 is a pure FP16 cast of A3, admitted only after an explicit validity check that the backend executes natively in half precision.

**Closed-loop acceptance criterion.** Every candidate drives every curriculum on the same eight seeds — 40 episodes per candidate, 400 in total. A candidate passes curriculum c iff all checks k (completion, progress, collisions, safety proximity, stop violations, stop completion, restart, lane failures, invalid poses, minimum pedestrian clearance) meet their thresholds τ_k **and** no paired metric regresses beyond a tolerance δ_k relative to A0 on the same seeds. All thresholds were fixed before any result existed and never relaxed. Costs are compared via median single-thread actor latency on x86, logical parameter memory, and serialized file size.

## Results & Highlights

- **Pruning alone destroys all capability (A1, 64-wide FP32, 6,210 vs 73,986 params, −91.6%).** A1 fails every curriculum: on C0–C2 the dominant failures are invalid poses and lane failures (first failure at step 367/3608 on C0, 1312/1313 on C1, 248/249 on C2), and on C3 **every one of 8 episodes ends in a stop violation** (first failure at step 149/398, v_cmd = 0.219 m/s at −0.001 m from the line). The loss is neither partial nor curriculum-specific.
- **Rehearsal coverage flips three curricula from complete failure to full recovery.** A2 and A3 share the pruned source, loss, optimizer, and budget; A2 (C4-focused rehearsal) completes every C3 and C4 episode and **none** of C0–C2 (total invalid-pose/lane-failure rates there), while A3 (balanced rehearsal, 62,176 public states across all five curricula) passes all five curricula **and** every action-fidelity check, exactly reproducing the FP32 teacher's tested behavior.
- **INT8 quantization re-breaks exactly the stop curricula, from both routes.** From the byte-identical A3 checkpoint: INT8 PTQ (A6) fails C3 and C4 — it loses **3 of 8** C3 episodes where the FP32 source completed **8 of 8** — freezing with v_cmd = 0.000 m/s parked **0.2 m before the line**, while QAT (A8) violates the stop in **4 of 8** C3 and **7 of 8** C4 episodes, crossing the line at v_cmd = 0.077 m/s: opposite failure sides of the same interrupt-and-resume requirement. The FP16 cast (A9) passes all five curricula identically to its FP32 source. Quantization-aware training on the *unpruned* actor (A4) loses nothing, so the interaction — not integer arithmetic per se — is the trigger.
- **Distillation placement matters (A5 vs A6).** A5 (prune → PTQ, no KD) fails all five curricula; A6 differs from A5 in exactly one respect — balanced KD inserted before PTQ — and retains C0–C2 in full (but not C3/C4), showing distilling before quantizing preserved substantially more capability than quantizing the pruned actor directly under the tested pathways.
- **Cost picture is counterintuitive at this scale.** Median single-thread actor latency: INT8 is fastest at **12.9 μs vs 19.4 μs for FP32** (~1.5× speedup), FP16 adds **26%** (24.5 μs) due to the absent native half-precision x86 path; yet the INT8 actor serializes to **~1.5× the FP32 file size** because traced-graph metadata dominates at only 6,210 params — INT8 buys speed at the cost of the two stop curricula, FP16 buys capability at a latency penalty.
- **Fidelity-only acceptance fails in both directions.** On the Spearman correlation of the yaw-rate channel against the original FP32 actor, A8 (the more dangerous driver) stays above the acceptance threshold where it would be accepted, while A4 — which drives every curriculum — would be rejected: fidelity metrics are neither necessary nor sufficient for closed-loop acceptance.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty is incremental and recombinative.** Every ingredient — structured pruning, KD, PTQ/QAT, belief-state PPO, Duckietown — is standard; the contribution is the evaluation protocol plus empirical interactions. The headline observation "quantization can hurt RL policies" already exists (QuaRL, [20]); what is genuinely new here is (a) the interaction with pruning and rehearsal coverage and (b) framing compression as a sequence of behavioral transitions with per-stage acceptance gates. The paper is honest about this positioning.
- **Very small scale limits generalization.** The entire study rides on one 73,986-parameter perceptron pruned to 6,210 parameters, one simulator, and one compression checkpoint — the authors admit conclusions "need not transfer to larger policies, where quantization error could have contributed differently." Notably, the perception stack (YOLO11n + MobileNetV3) is **never compressed**, so the study audits the cheapest slice of an autonomous-driving stack while the end-to-end cost is dominated by perception.
- **Statistical thinness.** Verdicts rest on 8 seeds × 5 episodes per curriculum (40 per candidate) with no confidence intervals or significance testing; a 3/8 failure count on C3 (A6) is exactly the kind of borderline outcome that could shift with more seeds, yet it carries a safety-flavored claim. The paper also notes A1's C2 failure "fading to three of eight," i.e., substantial stochasticity that is never characterized.
- **The δ_k tolerances are the crux but get no sensitivity analysis.** The entire pass/fail structure hinges on the regression tolerance vector δ_k and the check thresholds; "fixed before results" is good practice, but no ablation shows how verdicts move under different δ_k — a candidate that fails "by 0.001 m" (A1's C3 stop-line distance) sits right at the resolution of the definitions.
- **Mechanism is explicitly missing.** The authors observe *where* failures occur (the stop task forces a sharp decision boundary from driving to holding, so small perturbations near the boundary have large task consequences) but state they "do not establish its mechanism" — there is no weight-distribution, layer-wise, or activation-range analysis of why INT8 specifically destroys the stop curricula while FP16 does not.
- **Reproducibility is good on paper, unverified in practice.** Tables II–III and the acceptance formalism (Eq. 4) are detailed enough to reimplement, and the Duckietown stack is public, but no code release is mentioned; the INT8 result is also explicitly tied to one backend (eager static INT8 on x86), so replication on TensorRT or a mobile NPU is not guaranteed to reproduce the failure pattern.
- **Red flag (minor):** the "prune-before-quantize" alignment with [7],[8] is asserted from a single fixed ordering rather than a factorial experiment over orderings, which the authors themselves flag — the QAT variant A7 (no pruning, KD+PTO→QAT) fails C3/C4 too, muddying a clean "pruning is the trigger" story; only A4 (unpruned PTQ-only) survives, so exactly which interaction dominates is under-determined.

## Strengths

- **Uncommonly rigorous experimental hygiene**: pre-registered acceptance checks, paired regression tolerances against the reference on identical seeds, and byte-identical-checkpoint contrasts (A6 vs A8 share the A3 source; A6 vs A5 differ in exactly one intervention) that cleanly isolate each stage's causal effect.
- **Actionable deployment guidance**: release-gate evidence should be produced at every intermediate stage (a final-only report cannot separate "stage removed a behavior" from "rehearsal set chose not to test it"); the rehearsal set is a safety-relevant configuration item; interrupt-and-resume scenarios should be first-class acceptance scenarios for compressed policies.
- **Concrete demonstration of the fidelity-vs-closed-loop gap** with both error directions (false negative A4, false positive A8) on the same model family — the strongest argument in the paper.
- **Genuinely surprising cost findings**: INT8 is 1.5× faster but ~1.5× *larger on disk* at 6,210 params (metadata dominates), and FP16 preserves all behavior while INT8 breaks a successfully distilled policy — a crisp, quotable contrast.
- **Honest, clearly-bounded claims** throughout, including an explicit limitations section that anticipates most external criticism.

## Limitations

- Single small policy (pruned to 6,210 params), single simulator (Gym-Duckietown), single compression checkpoint; transfer to full-scale driving, higher-fidelity simulation, or larger/deeper policies is untested (acknowledged by the authors).
- 8 seeds per curriculum with no uncertainty quantification; borderline verdicts (3/8 episode failures) are treated as definitive.
- The acceptance definition (checks + δ_k) is admittedly ad hoc ("not a standard") and receives no sensitivity analysis; conclusions are relative to this operational definition of capability.
- INT8 findings are backend-specific (eager static INT8 on x86); the FP16 +26% latency is an x86 artifact of a missing native half-precision path and does not generalize to GPUs/NPUs.
- No mechanism analysis of why integer quantization destroys the stop curricula; no code release stated.
- The perception pipeline is never compressed, so the study's "compression" scope excludes the component that dominates real end-to-end latency.

## Relevance

该文与你的"极限性能优化引入的可靠性问题"（量化误差、低精度计算、静默损坏检测）高度契合：它实证了 INT8 量化在权重数值误差极小的情况下仍能摧毁一个已恢复的策略行为，且聚合的 action-fidelity 指标无法预测闭环故障——本质上是"低精度优化引入功能性静默损坏、而常规验收指标漏检"的闭环控制版本。局限在于对象是 6,210 参数的微型 POMDP 策略而非 LLM/大规模推理负载，定量结论外推到你的主战场时需要谨慎，但其"逐阶段闭环验收 + 配对回归容差"的方法论可以直接借鉴到低精度推理的可靠性评测设计上。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would QAT with balanced rehearsal data recover the stop curricula, or is the integer representation itself the blocker at this width? A7's failure (KD+PTO→QAT, unpruned) suggests neither pruning nor rehearsal alone explains it.
- How sensitive are the verdicts to the tolerance vector δ_k — is there a δ_k under which A6 passes C3, and would that same δ_k also accept genuinely unsafe drivers?
- Does the stop-curriculum fragility persist in larger actors (e.g., 256 kept pruned to 128), in deeper networks, or in end-to-end vision policies where the perception stack is also compressed?
- What is the mechanism — is the INT8 failure concentrated in specific layers or weight ranges near the stop decision boundary, and would per-channel activation calibration or range clipping fix it?
- Does the stage-wise closed-loop acceptance framework also detect hardware-induced silent corruption (e.g., bit flips in the quantized weights), where fidelity checks would again miss task-level failure?
