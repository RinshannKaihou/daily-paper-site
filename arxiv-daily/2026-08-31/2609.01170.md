> **一句话总结：** 作者从零训练 Pythia-410M 并在每个训练步对 14 个任务（4 个认知域）做 attribution patching，首次逐步追踪模块化任务分区的形成：任务"地图"在随机权重的 step 0 就已预雕刻（主导任务对重叠 0.153 ≈ 归因基底 0.0422 的 3.6 倍），随后经两次陡峭跳变锁定（第二次 +0.154，20.4σ quiet-window / 6.2σ global），跳变伴随赢家对输家 2.25→2.73 倍的梯度供给"相对剥夺"（比随机对照低 9.5–11.5σ），但该剥夺不传播到 Adam 更新与权重；对基底的偏离只出现在损失真正下降的语言域（−0.20→−1.77）。

> **TL;DR:** Training Pythia-410M from scratch on the Pile and applying attribution patching at every step across 14 tasks in 4 cognitive domains (following Han et al. 2026's battery), this paper reports the first step-by-step tracking of how modular task partitions form. The partition map is "pre-carved" before any learning: at step 0 (random weights) the dominant task pair (regular–irregular agreement) already overlaps at 0.153 raw overlap ≈ 3.6× the 0.0422 attribution substrate, while sibling pairs involving the adjective sit below substrate (0.028–0.029). The partition then locks in through two sharp jumps (run2: step 18→19 at +0.067/8.9σ and step 79→80 at +0.154, the largest event, 20.4σ quiet-window but only 6.2σ global; run1: 23–25 at +0.080/16σ and 74–75 at +0.142) whose amplitudes do not track the 3.33× LR ramp — yet the lock is a high-churn reorganization (old-member retention only 60%/44%, with a stable 165-neuron skeleton carrying >90% of the shared set). Winners receive 2.25→2.73× the loser's gradient supply (9.5–11.5σ below a random same-size control), but this "relative deprivation" never propagates to Adam updates (ratio ≤1) or weights (drift 0.40–0.82%). Deviation from substrate occurs only in the language domain whose loss actually falls (−0.20→−1.77), not in unlearned physics/ToM (flat ≈ −0.05) — supporting a pre-registered scale-threshold hypothesis for physics modularity at 2.8B. A unusually disciplined measurement paper whose eight quantitative falsifications (no turnover 0/117, no starvation cutoff, no one-shot irreversibility...) are as informative as its positives.

## 核心贡献

- **"地图是预雕刻的"（pre-carved map）**：在 step 0（随机权重、未做任何更新时），attribution patching 已经恢复出分区草图——主导任务对（regular–irregular 一致性）原始重叠 0.153 ≈ 归因基底（0.0422，step-1 兄弟对平均重叠）的 3.6 倍，而含形容词的兄弟对仅 0.028–0.029、低于基底。step-1 主导对重叠跨轨迹复现（0.1404 vs 0.142，差 0.0016）、跨初始化种子复现（变异系数 0.036），说明这是任务结构与未训练网络交互的属性，而非训练产物。
- **layer-0 集中带是不动的架构级常数**：各任务 roster 的 39–45% 落在第 0 层，且该比例在 6 种语料变体（合成模板、符号输出、随机字符串、真实语料、训练前后）下不变；形容词变体的 layer-0 归因精确为零（全部 98,304 个神经元），换成 mismatch 语料即恢复 0.405——说明零是语料依赖的，而非 pipeline 边界伪影。
- **分区经两次陡峭跳变锁定，而非渐进增长**：run2 中平坦平台（0.13–0.16）→ 首次锁定跳变（step 18→19，+0.067，8.9σ）→ 振荡平台 → 第二次跳变（step 79→80，+0.154，全程最大事件，20.4σ quiet-window / 6.2σ global-MAD）。跳变幅度不追踪学习率日程（LR 在两次跳变间增长 3.33×，跳变幅度仅 2.36×；事件点相关性仅 +0.021）。
- **锁定是高流动性的重组，不是稳定放大**：首次锁定窗口内共享集增长 +71%（run2）/+121%（run1），但旧成员保留率仅 60%/44%；全程赢家对零 roster 更替、零跨层迁移（0/117 裁决案例，唯一例外是形容词任务 step 79 的 49.6% 保留率）；165 个"骨架"神经元从 step 24 起承载共享集的 >90%，而 1,124 个神经元反复进出（ping-pong），共有 2,303 个不同神经元曾进入共享集。锚定效应：79%（run1）/53–64%（run2）的最终赢家在 step 1 就带正归因。
- **梯度层"相对剥夺"不传播**：赢家共享集获得输家 2.25→2.73 倍的梯度供给（taskgrad 口径 4.37→4.19），输家/赢家梯度范数比从 0.44 降到 0.37，每一步都比随机同规模对照低 9.5–11.5σ；但 Adam 归一化有效更新比 ≤1、权重范数漂移仅 0.40–0.82%，输家并非被饿死——其一阶损失贡献只比赢家低约一个数量级，且在 step 92 后出现约 1/10 幅度的"晚期弱学习"。
- **对基底的偏离只发生在正在学习的域**：一阶损失分解显示语言域损失分量单调漂移 −0.20→−1.77（irregular 最深 −1.23），而物理与心理理论域保持平坦（≈−0.05）；两次锁定跳变都只出现在语言域；物理域 within/cross 重叠比在 81/85 步停在 ≈1.02（基底水平带内）。由此提出预注册的尺度阈值假说：物理域模块化在 410M/1.4B 缺席、据官方 checkpoint 在 2.8B 出现。
- **八项定量证伪作为负结果贡献**：无 roster 更替/迁移（F1/F2，0/117，最低保留率 72.7% 远高于 50% 阈值）；无绝对梯度截断（F3，输家供给始终 ≥0.59 倍随机对照基线）；无一锤定音的不可逆性（F4，run1 回撤 0.19 后自发恢复到 0.30）；无权重均衡关系（F5，(w/W ratio)² ≈ 1.0 vs 供给比 2.25–3.26，相关 −0.27）；三个机制因子假说被拒（F6 layer-0 局部二元文法、F7 span-to-depth 映射、F8 名词池聚合）。

## Key Contributions

- First **time-resolved formation study** of modular task partitions: attribution patching is repurposed from a post-hoc localization tool (Han et al. 2026, arXiv:2608.13567) into a per-step instrument across an entire from-scratch Pythia-410M run (24 layers, 98,304 MLP neurons), with checkpoints, five per-neuron probe arrays, task-conditioned gradients, and attribution tensors archived at every step.
- **The map is pre-carved**: the dominant-pair overlap at step 0 is 0.153 ≈ 3.6× the substrate-calibrated baseline (0.0422); sibling pairs are below substrate. The partition sketch replicates across trajectories (0.1404 vs 0.142) and seeds (CV 0.036) — an architecture/input-statistics property, not a training product.
- **Lock-in via two sharp jumps, not gradual growth**: +0.067 (8.9σ) at step 18→19 and +0.154 (20.4σ quiet-window / 6.2σ global-MAD) at step 79→80 in run2; +0.080 (16σ) and +0.142 in run1. Jump amplitudes do not track the LR schedule (LR ×3.33 between jumps vs jumps ×2.36; Pearson +0.021 at events). Beneath the stable aggregate, membership churns: retention 60%/44%, a 165-neuron skeleton carries >90% of the shared set, 1,124 neurons ping-pong in and out.
- **Gradient-level relative deprivation that does not propagate**: winner/loser gradient-supply ratio 2.25→2.73× (light caliber) and 4.37→4.19 (taskgrad caliber), 9.5–11.5σ below random controls at every step, yet effective-update ratio ≤1 and weight-norm drift only 0.40–0.82% — the loser is excluded from *learning* (loss contribution ~1 order of magnitude lower) rather than starved, with late-stage weak learning (≈1/10 magnitude) after step 92.
- **Deviation tracks learning**: first-order loss decomposition separates domains cleanly — language drifts −0.20→−1.77 while physics/ToM stay flat (−0.05); both lock-in jumps are language-only; physics within/cross overlap stays at ≈1.02 (substrate level) for 81/85 steps. Physics modularity is pre-registered (not measured) to appear at 2.8B.
- **Eight quantitative falsifications (F1–F8)** reported as contributions: no roster turnover/migration (0/117 adjudicated cases; lowest retention 72.7% vs 50% threshold, 70–97× random baseline), no absolute gradient cutoff (loser supply ≥0.59 of control throughout), no one-shot irreversibility (spontaneous recovery 0.19→0.30), no weight-equilibrium law (r = −0.27), and three rejected mechanistic factor hypotheses (layer-0 local bigram, span-to-depth mapping, noun-pool accumulation).

## Methodology

**Training.** Pythia-410M (24 layers, 4,096 MLP neurons per layer = 98,304 neurons) is trained from scratch on a pure Pile stream with batch 2^21 tokens per step, a linear learning-rate ramp (2×10⁻⁶ → 9×10⁻⁵, AdamW). Two trajectories are analyzed: run1 (bf16) and run2 (fp32, seed 1), identical in architecture and data stream. At every step the authors record model checkpoints, five per-neuron probe arrays (gradient norm, effective update norm, weight norm, activation frequency/second moment), task-conditioned gradient scalars for all 14 tasks, attribution tensors, and first-order loss decompositions.

**Tasks and minimal pairs.** 14 tasks spanning four domains follow Han et al.'s (2026) battery: 8 language tasks (including three determiner–noun agreement variants: regular, irregular, with-adjective), 2 theory-of-mind tasks, and 4 physical-reasoning tasks (temperature, solubility, elasticity, buoyancy). Each task consists of minimal pairs — original and alternative prompts differing at one position so the correct continuation flips.

**Attribution patching and rosters.** A neuron's attribution is (activation difference between original and alternative) × (gradient of the original–alternative logit difference), aggregated over examples at the final prompt position. A task's roster is its top-983 neurons by signed attribution (k = 983 ≈ 1% of all neurons). Pairwise overlap is single-sided: |A ∩ B|/983 (Jaccard = s/(2−s), roughly half the single-sided value; never compared raw with Han et al.'s top-0.1% Jaccard).

**Deviation from substrate.** Raw overlap mixes task-specific sharing with a shallow-layer substrate shared by many tasks. The substrate is measured per step as the mean step-1 sibling-pair overlap (0.0422, frozen), and all effect sizes are reported as deviation = raw overlap / substrate. "×random" multiples (1% expectation) are explicitly not used because 1% is not the relevant zero point (15.26× random = only 3.6× substrate).

**Multi-probe system and confound guards.** Each probe establishes one evidentiary link and guards one confound (Table 1): attribution patching guards correlation-only localization; task-conditioned gradient norms guard starvation claims without supply; effective-update norms guard conflating raw gradients with optimizer steps; weight norms guard "starved" claims without weight evidence; first-order loss decomposition (ΔLᵢ ≈ gᵢ·aᵢ per neuron per task) guards domain-coupling claims without loss evidence; roster tracking guards aggregate curves hiding turnover.

**Event detection and calibers.** Transitions are first differences of the mean overlap series, sized in σ units under two calibrations: quiet-window (steps 1–17 plateau, σ = 0.00754 via 1.4826×MAD) and global (σ = 0.0249); both are always reported because they disagree strongly (the second jump is 20.4σ quiet-window but 6.2σ global). Two localization calibers exist — signed top-983 (PRIMARY, frozen in a written protocol) and absolute-value top-983 (SECONDARY) — whose rosters overlap only 0.2838 Jaccard (Spearman 0.30); results are never mixed across calibers. Turnover (step-to-step retention <50%) and migration (roster centroid shift ≥2 layers) are adjudicated per-step against a random same-size control on frozen thresholds.

**Falsification protocol.** Eight candidate mechanisms (F1–F8) are tested against pre-set quantitative thresholds (e.g., retention 50%, loser-supply cutoff 0.59 of control, weight-equilibrium TH1), each recorded as excluded/rejected with the strongest measured evidence.

## Results & Highlights

- **Pre-carved map at step 0 (Pythia-410M, 14-task battery):** dominant pair raw overlap 0.153 ≈ 3.6× the 0.0422 attribution substrate, while adjective sibling pairs sit at 0.028–0.029, below substrate; the step-1 dominant overlap replicates across the two trajectories (0.1404 vs 0.142, Δ = 0.0016) and across initialization seeds (CV 0.036). By step 85 the deviation reaches 12.7× substrate.
- **Two-jump lock-in (run2, steps 1–89):** flat plateau 0.13–0.16 → first jump +0.067 (8.9σ quiet-window) at step 18→19 → oscillating consolidation (largest single transition +0.074 at 9.9σ) → second jump +0.154 at step 79→80, the largest event of the trajectory (20.4σ quiet-window, but only 6.2σ global-MAD). Run1 replicates the morphology at 23–25 (+0.080, 16σ) and 74–75 (+0.142). The LR grew 3.33× between the jumps while jump amplitude grew only 2.36× (0.154 vs 0.065), and LR-normalized jump size declines; event-time correlation with LR is +0.021.
- **High-churn lock:** inside the first lock window the shared set grows +71% (run2) / +121% (run1) but old-member retention is only 60%/44%; across the full window, winner-pair roster turnover and migration are 0/117 per-step adjudication cases (single exception: adjective task at step 79, retention 49.6%); anchoring is 79% (run1) / 53–64% (run2) under the signed caliber but only 15.5–21.8% under the membership caliber; 165 skeleton neurons carry >90% of the shared set from step 24 onward, while 1,124 of 2,303 ever-admitted neurons ping-pong (≥3 membership changes). After the first jump, overlap retraces 0.19 over 34–35 steps and spontaneously recovers to 0.30 — no one-shot irreversibility.
- **Relative deprivation confined to the gradient layer:** winner shared set receives 2.25→2.73× the loser's gradient supply (light caliber; 4.37→4.19 taskgrad caliber); loser/winner gradient-norm ratio falls 0.44→0.37, sitting 9.5–11.5σ below a random same-size control at every step; yet the Adam-normalized effective-update ratio stays ≤1 and weight norms drift only 0.40–0.82% over the trajectory. The loser's first-order loss contribution stays ~1 order of magnitude below the winner's, with late-stage weak learning (≈1/10 magnitude) emerging after step 92 rather than permanent exclusion.
- **Deviation only in the domain being learned:** the language loss component drifts monotonically −0.20→−1.77 (irregular deepest at −1.23) while physics and theory-of-mind stay flat (≈−0.05, no trend); both lock-in jumps occur exclusively in the language domain; the physics within/cross-domain overlap ratio sits at ≈1.02 for 81/85 steps (band 0.8–1.25, inside substrate), with only a transient elasticity–buoyancy burst (peak 1.65 at steps 19–23) that decays immediately.
- **Layer-0 band and word-form sensitivity:** 39–45% of every roster sits in layer 0, invariant across six corpus variants (Figure 2: 0.409 regular, 0.391 irregular, 0.405 adjective under mismatch corpus); the adjective variant's layer-0 attribution is exactly zero (all 98,304 neurons) under the matched corpus. Swapping the synthetic answer token replaces ≈85% of roster members (overlaps 0.910/0.887/0.897 ≈ 21× substrate, vs 0.135–0.229 for the base answer pool), though real-battery suppression is only 3.7–4.5× — synthetic numbers do not transfer quantitatively.
- **Falsification cards (Table 2):** F1 turnover excluded (lowest retention 72.7% >> 50% threshold; 70–97× the 1.0% random baseline); F2 migration excluded (centroid layer 0.51–1.16 throughout; shallow fraction ≥96.3%); F3 absolute gradient cutoff excluded (loser supply/baseline ≥0.59 throughout); F4 irreversibility excluded (retrace 0.19, spontaneous recovery to 0.30); F5 weight-equilibrium rejected ((w/W ratio)² ≈ 1.012→0.996 vs supply ratio 2.25–3.26, r = −0.27); F6 layer-0 local bigram excluded (adjacency-breaking insertion leaves layer-0 membership 428→409/410, no collapse); F7 span-to-depth excluded (span 0–4 centroids 1.91–2.16, flat); F8 noun-pool accumulation excluded (hyperbolic fit R² ≤ 0.21; 10/10 series decrease first→last).

## Authors & Credibility

The paper comes from Guangqi Li and Yongxin Li at Zaozhuang University (Shandong, China), with the latter listed as corresponding author. This is not a group with visible prior track record in mechanistic interpretability — the citation footprint is essentially built on one external anchor, Han et al. (2026, arXiv:2608.13567), whose battery and localization method the paper reuses. That said, the methodological craftsmanship is unusual: frozen written protocols, SHA-256-bound per-step artifact archives, dual-calibrated significance, dual localization calibers that are never mixed, an append-only anomaly registry, and pre-registered (design-frozen, hash-bound) follow-up experiments. The reproducibility statement is specific about what is archived and — notably — honest about two provenance gaps (run1's 40-step full tables unbound on a training server; two referenced audit files missing from the project directory). No public code or data URL is given, so external verification currently means trusting the described ledger. The document's style (ledgers, adjudication, "falsification cards", anomaly IDs) strongly suggests an agent-assisted research pipeline, which explains the discipline but is unconventional for a paper.

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty is real but narrower than the framing.** The genuinely new move is time resolution: prior modularity work (Han et al. 2026; emergent-modularity and successor-heads lines) characterized trained networks, and developmental studies (Hoogland et al. 2024; grokking circuit work) tracked single tasks in small models. Per-step tracking of a multi-task partition across a from-scratch 410M run is a first as far as this paper represents. However, the headline "pre-carved" claim is weaker than it sounds: at step 0 the network is random, and what is measured is statistical alignment between minimal-pair input geometry, random features, and the untrained output head — the authors themselves concede "at step 0 there are no circuits to describe, only statistical alignment." That two agreement tasks sharing construction share more neurons than tasks that do not, through a random projection, is close to expected; the informative part is the quantification (3.6× vs below-substrate siblings) and the corpus-dependence of the adjective zero, not the cognitive-architecture resonance of the title.

**The learning–modularity coupling rests on n=1 learned domain at this scale.** The claim "deviation occurs only in the domain being learned" is supported by language (learning, deviation present) vs physics/ToM (not learning, deviation absent) — but at 410M exactly one of the three non-language domains is actually being learned (physics loss 5.545→5.142, near-flat). The second data point that would make this a real correlation (physics modularity turning on at 2.8B) is relayed from official checkpoints, not measured by the authors, and the 2.8B run itself is pre-registered but unrun — flagged by the authors as "the largest evidence gap of this paper."

**Event significance is estimator-fragile.** The second jump is 20.4σ under the quiet-window calibration but 6.2σ under the global one — a 3.3× swing for the same event. Dual-reporting is the right discipline, but it shows the "sharp jump" narrative is sensitive to window choice. Worse, there is an internal inconsistency in the load-bearing anti-LR argument: Section 5.1 states jump amplitude grew "only 3.36× (0.154 vs 0.065)" between the jumps, but 0.154/0.065 = 2.37, and Figure 1a's own annotation says "LR ×3.33 vs jumps ×2.36" — the text typo lands exactly on the number that decides whether jumps track the LR schedule.

**Caliber-dependence undermines several headline numbers.** The signed and absolute calibers share only 0.2838 Jaccard, and the paper's own appendices show conclusions move with caliber choice: anchoring is 79% (signed) vs 15.5–21.8% (membership caliber); the step-108 irregular retention is 49.34% on recompute vs 57.0% under G4 ("definition/caliber discrepancy under review"); physics entry points diverge across three calibers ("almost never"). The paper flags all of this honestly, but a reader should treat the specific numbers as protocol-conditional rather than established constants.

**Trajectory confounds.** All dynamics claims rest on two trajectories of one 410M model, and the two runs differ in precision (bf16 vs fp32) with seed/LR/batch differences unconfirmed (Appendix D) — so the trajectory-conditional conflict-cleaning cycle (run1 only) cannot be attributed. The run2 per-step series also carries an alternating A/B attribution-slot artifact acknowledged in Appendix D. Cross-seed training replication is "planned but incomplete."

**Reproducibility is asserted, not demonstrated.** Despite SHA-256 provenance claims, no public repository or artifact release is referenced, and the paper itself reports two referenced audit files missing and run1's tables unbound — external replication is currently impractical. The only external anchor (Han et al. 2026) uses incomparable roster sizes (top-1% vs top-0.1%) and metrics (single-sided vs Jaccard ≈ half), and the authors correctly never compare raw numbers — which also means there is no cross-paper replication of any quantity. Finally, the log-style apparatus (anomaly IDs, "three acts", adjudication rulings) is unusual for peer review and buries the scientific core under process scaffolding; the missing-audit-file and unattributed 19.8σ V-spike entries show the ledger discipline does not fully substitute for settled results.

## Strengths

- **Genuinely new measurement capability**: per-step attribution patching, probe arrays, and task-conditioned gradients across an entire from-scratch pretraining run — turning formation dynamics from speculation into a measurable object.
- **Exemplary measurement discipline**: substrate-calibrated effect sizes instead of raw overlaps; dual σ-calibration always reported; two localization calibers frozen and never mixed; per-step adjudication of turnover/migration against random same-size controls; frozen written protocols.
- **Negative results done right**: eight falsification cards with pre-set quantitative thresholds (F1–F8) sharply constrain mechanism space — no turnover, no starvation cutoff, no irreversibility, no weight-equilibrium law — and the anomaly registry exposes unresolved contradictions instead of hiding them.
- **Multi-layer causal hygiene**: the gradient/update/weight/loss decomposition cleanly separates "deprived gradients" from "starved weights," blocking the easy overclaim that losers are starved.
- **Unusual candor**: the limitations section, the "caliber-conditional" labeling, the pre-registration of the 2.8B test with explicit candidate hypotheses, and the self-identified "largest evidence gap" all raise trust in the reported numbers.

## Limitations

- Single model (Pythia-410M), single data stream (Pile), two trajectories only; cross-seed training replication incomplete, and the bf16/fp32 confound between runs is unresolved — several "trajectory-conditional" findings (e.g., run1-only conflict-cleaning) cannot yet be attributed.
- Feature-level conclusions are established within one construction family (determiner–noun agreement, three template variants); the 22-task orthogonal family designed to separate the five feature axes is frozen but not yet run, so the "which feature drives separation" question is explicitly open (random-word insertion vs adjective interruption currently contradict each other).
- Relative deprivation is correlational; no supply-equalization intervention has established a causal role, and the second jump's trigger (threshold accumulation vs supply-ratio inflection vs data-order cluster) is unresolved with only a pre-registered plan.
- The scale-threshold story for physics modularity is a pre-registered prediction: physics is not learned at 410M, the 1.4B/2.8B evidence is relayed from official checkpoints rather than measured, and the 2.8B experiment has zero measurements so far.
- All quantitative conclusions are caliber- and protocol-specific (signed vs absolute vs membership calibers disagree materially); no external replication anchor exists (Han et al. numbers are never comparable raw); artifacts are SHA-256-bound but not publicly released, with two referenced audit files missing.
- The mechanism level is admittedly unanswered: why structural separation reshapes attribution alignment in a random network, and where the ≈40% layer-0 band and the substrate floor come from, remain open.

## Relevance

与你关注的两个方向高度相关：其一，这是 LLM 可观测性/训练动态监测的范本——逐步记录探针、双重校准的事件检测、异常登记册（含把 step-108 损失尖峰定位为数据驱动）正是"训练运行监测与异常检测"的方法论素材；其二，它属于机制可解释性核心（attribution patching、任务分区、探针、骨架-外围结构），并给出了"模块化何时形成"的首个时间分辨测量。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- What actually triggers the second lock-in jump — intrinsic threshold accumulation, a supply-ratio inflection, or a data-order cluster? The pre-registered prediction is that its timing aligns with a data-order cluster boundary; what does the adjudication show?
- Does the pre-carved step-0 map generalize beyond determiner–noun agreement? The 22-task orthogonal family (five feature axes, including word-form and dependency span) is design-frozen — which axes carry the separation, and can the random-word vs adjective-interruption contradiction be resolved?
- Is gradient-level relative deprivation causal? Would a supply-equalization intervention change lock-in morphology or roster churn?
- Is physics modularity genuinely scale-gated — does the 2.8B run cross the four-branch physics criterion, and do the S1–S6 signatures hold at 1.4B/2.8B?
- How much of "task partition" identity is output-token identity rather than shared computation, given that swapping the answer token replaces ≈85% of roster members?
- Is the ≈40% layer-0 concentration band a property of the architecture or of the attribution-patching instrument on shallow networks — would deeper/wider or attention-inclusive rosters show the same invariant band?
