Recursive self-improvement · paper 02 · arXiv 2609.06396

Self-improvement becomes schedulable: three write-disjoint operators, and a self-rewriting meta-scheduler beats every fixed pipeline.

MetaRSI / RSI²: A Meta-Recursive Self-Improving System for Recursive Self-Improving Systems Themselves · Zihan Tan, Lexin Sun, Zitong Shi, Yitao Liu, et al. — OsmosMind + 16 universities · 2026-08-31 recursive self-improvement agent harness meta-optimizer benchmark census
Average self-improvement, sealed & budget-matched
+0.0pts

Across 4 benchmarks (Qwen3.5-35B-A3B, 5 seeds); +3.6 from scheduling alone vs. the best fixed pipeline. SWE-bench Pro nearly doubles, 10.3 → 19.5.

Why this matters

A typed grammar for self-evolving agents — edit data, prompts, or weights safely — plus an audit protocol where no component grades its own homework.

Critical edge

Every baseline is a recombination of the authors’ own operators — the +3.6 scheduling win proves their scheduler beats its own ablations, not the state of the art.

01 Methodology one kernel, three operators
  1. Target = S = (D, θ, H) — data, weights, harness. All improvement runs one loop kernel (OBSERVE → DIAGNOSE → PROPOSE → VALIDATE → EXECUTE → SELECT → EXPORT): the model proposes, deterministic code adjudicates, one authority boundary between them.
  2. Three disjoint write surfaces. Data-RSI synthesizes records verified by an adversarial + blind re-derivation gate (Anchor), tagged with a 4-dim learning signature κ. Harness-RSI applies typed patches to a five-slot genome (prompt / memory / tools / skills / MCP). Model-RSI trains bounded LoRA recipes — always from the fixed base, so gains stay attributable.
  3. Freshness-constrained scheduling. A signal-freshness rule admits 7 transitions (forbids H→M, M→M), each realized by a typed Transition Agent. The RSI² agent chooses horizontal (extend the operator program) vs. vertical (rewrite one operator’s policy under four acceptance checks); the MetaRSI² agent rewrites the scheduler itself once per term.
  4. Nothing grades its own output. Sealed evaluator, task set, release rule and cost ledger sit outside every write surface — including the meta layer.
  5. Rig: Qwen3.5-35B-A3B full loop · 6 frontier models harness-only · 5 seeds · equal token/GPU/candidate/verifier budgets

  • Scheduling ladder: best single operator (Harness) +6.6 · best fixed composition +7.3 · random admissible order +4.5 · MetaRSI-v1 +10.9.
  • Meta layer compounds: +26.2 vs +20.7 cumulative over 5 terms; per-term gain 3.8 vs 2.5; terms-to-+20: 2.5 vs 4.0; scheduler regret halves (16.5% → 8.2%).
  • Policy carryover rises 38% → 68% with the meta layer; the meta path shows no regression where the non-meta path loses 0.2 on GPQA at term 5.
  • Census: 69% of 45 RSI systems close on machine-checkable targets; 53/55 loops sit at verifier rungs 1–3 — loop count tracks rung (ρ = −0.75, p<0.001), capability is not significant once rung is controlled (p = 0.08).
  • Five high-capability rung-5 domains have zero loops (economics 80.8, law 67.8, …); practice-vs-recall gaps span 35–83 points on six benchmark pairs.
  • Headroom caveat: retention anti-correlates with baseline strength (ρ = −0.84) — the paper itself concedes the rate is set by headroom, not model strength; no SDs/CIs reported.
03 Strengths
  • Right-granularity formalization: disjoint write surfaces with typed artifacts make composition analyzable, and the freshness principle yields a crisp, checkable transition algebra instead of ad-hoc pipelines.
  • Best-in-genre evaluation hygiene: budget ledgers, sealed splits opened once after freezing, over-budget scored as failure, and the scored object is the released successor — never the best archived candidate.
  • The meta layer is evaluated the right way: a controlled fork (same S₁, same budget, only the improver differs), decomposed into policy carryover, scheduler regret, and regression elimination.
  • The census converts anecdote into structure: partial correlations, robustness-to-doubling checks, and five laws each shipped with explicit refutation conditions.
  • Practical release: RSI-Harness runtime + genome format reproduce the harness route without weights or training infrastructure.
04 Limitations
  • Only one open-weight family (Qwen3.5-35B-A3B) exercises the full three-operator loop; frontier evaluation is harness-only, so Model-RSI generality is untested.
  • Short horizon: five terms with visible decay (per-term gain falls to +1.4 even with meta-update); drift over tens of terms is unmeasured.
  • No external SOTA baselines, no variance reporting (means over five seeds, no SDs or CIs), and no ablations on the cost of each safety mechanism.
  • All four benchmarks are machine-checkable — transfer to the open-ended rung-4–5 domains the paper argues matter is asserted, not demonstrated. “Every Domain” is unsupported by any experiment.
  • Census labels are the authors’ own hand labels, the κ taxonomy is hand-designed, and failure misrouting by the fixed priority order is possible and unquantified.
05 Questions for further exploration
  1. Does the +3.6 scheduling advantage survive against an external, tuned self-improvement baseline (Darwin-Gödel-Machine-style rewrite loop, GEPA) at equal budget?
  2. How much of +10.9 persists from a frontier-grade frozen baseline (>80 Terminal-Bench) where headroom is small — and does the meta layer still pay for its separate budget?
  3. Can the learning signature κ reliably allocate the “imported knowledge” budget (Law 5) in a rung-3 ML-replication domain where no experiment is reported?
  4. What is the per-mechanism cost of the safety machinery — Anchor blind re-derivation, the three record gates, declared-modification equality — in rejected-but-correct proposals and term time?
  5. Does the five-slot genome transfer across task families as claimed, or does each genome overfit its benchmark’s hidden verifier?
