> **一句话总结：** 提出一个三阶段诊断协议，证明 Qwen3.5 系列在逻辑推理基准上"答错"往往不是不会推理，而是末端评分塌缩——隐藏态探针能解码出正确答案（Qwen3.5-9B 在 ProofWriter 上探针 0.972 vs 原生序列得分 0.333 随机水平），而仅用 25–1000 条无标注样本拟合两个每标签加性偏移（target-label-free 校正），即可恢复 9–34 个准确率点（ProofWriter 上 9B 从 0.333 → 0.678），并迁移到 OLMo-2-1B 与 Llama-3.1-8B，说明许多"零样本推理失败"只是末端的标签先验评分失真。

> **TL;DR:** On ProofWriter, ANLI, FOLIO, and synthetic controlled-logic splits, linear probes decode the correct answer from Qwen3.5 hidden states (0.972 in-domain / 0.830 lexical-OOD for Qwen3.5-9B) even while the model's native full candidate-answer string score sits at chance (0.333) — a +0.488 readout gap (95% CI [+0.452, +0.523], paired p < .001). A minimal, target-label-free correction that fits just two per-label additive offsets on as few as 25–1,000 unlabeled score rows recovers 9–34 accuracy points (Qwen3.5-9B ProofWriter 0.333 → 0.678; Qwen3.5-4B ANLI R2 0.478 → 0.571), transfers to OLMo-2-1B (362 → 566) and Llama-3.1-8B (333 → 477), and survives TF-IDF shortcut slices, count-preserving permutation nulls, and prior-perturbation controls — while Pythia-410M/12B show no reliable recovery, marking the claim's boundary.

## 核心贡献

- **把"推理失败"拆成两个可分离的失败模式**：提出三阶段诊断流水线（隐藏态探针 → 同位置标签 logits → 全候选答案字符串得分），精确定位模型在哪一步丢失了正确答案的信号。结论：Qwen3.5-2B/4B/9B 在 controlled-logic OOD 上内部"知道"答案，但原生序列得分全部塌缩到约 0.333（三选一随机水平）。
- **极简的目标标签无关校正**：对原始候选答案得分 s̃_y(x) = s_y(x) + c_y 拟合每标签加性偏移。三标签任务中固定一个偏移为 0，仅 2 个自由参数，用确定性网格搜索在**无标注** in-domain 得分行上最小化与目标先验的平方差。因为只做全局阈值平移、数学上无法学到新的任务映射，恢复本身即为"逻辑对齐本来就存在于原生输出中、失败只是末端评分失真"的证据。
- **量化恢复 9–34 个准确率点**：仅用 25 条无标注样本即可产生正增益（ProofWriter 上 25 样本拟合对 Qwen3.5-4B/9B 提升 +0.216/+0.277），从 25 增到 1000 条拟合样本 held-out 准确率变化 ≤0.022；增益在 TF-IDF 捷径切片、保标签数置换基线、先验扰动、提示/动词化变体（87–96% 配置塌缩持续）、独立种子重生成等全套对照下依然成立。
- **明确的边界条件**：近天花板场景（Qwen3.5-9B ANLI raw 0.638，Δ=0.000）、无塌缩排序的模型（Pythia-410M/12B 无可靠恢复）、先验错定（最坏情况先验扰动缩小增益但保持为正）——把校正定性为"塌缩分布的诊断证据"而非"通用推理能力修复器"。
- **对评估实践的告诫**：排行榜上接近随机的零样本成绩可能只反映标签先验偏置（如 Qwen3.5-9B 在 1000 条 lexical-OOD 上 999 条预测 unknown），评估者应在归因"能力缺失"前同时检查内部表征与答案评分两端。

## Key Contributions

- **A three-stage diagnostic that localizes the failure.** Probes → same-position label logits → full candidate-answer string scores pinpoint exactly where the answer signal is lost. For post-trained Qwen3.5-2B/4B/9B, hidden states decodable at 0.918–0.972 (in-domain) coexist with native string scores pinned at chance (0.333) on every controlled-logic split, isolating a late-stage *scoring* bottleneck rather than a representation failure.
- **A minimal, target-label-free additive correction.** Offsets s̃_y(x) = s_y(x) + c_y with only two free parameters (one reference offset fixed to zero on the 3-label tasks), fitted by deterministic grid search on *unlabeled* in-domain score rows. Because the intervention can only shift global decision boundaries — never alter within-label rankings — any recovery is defensible evidence that per-example logical alignment was already present in the raw scores.
- **9–34 point recovery with aggressive sample efficiency.** Fitting on as few as 25 unlabeled examples yields positive gains across all 30 random subsamples for collapsed Qwen3.5 models; gains survive TF-IDF-missed shortcut slices, label-count permutation baselines, target-prior perturbations, 20 prompt/verbalizer variants, and regeneration with three independent generator seeds.
- **Honest boundary conditions.** Near-ceiling rows (Qwen3.5-9B ANLI), models without a collapsed ranking (Pythia-410M/12B), and prior misspecification all delineate where the correction fails, framing it as a diagnostic for collapsed distributions rather than a universal causal fix.
- **An evaluation-practice warning.** Near-chance zero-shot leaderboard scores may reflect label-prior bias (Qwen3.5-9B predicts "unknown" for 999/1,000 lexical-OOD examples pre-correction); benchmark evaluators should check both internal representations and answer scoring before attributing failure to missing reasoning capability.

## Methodology

**Task and scoring.** All tasks are cast into a forced-choice format with a literal answer slot (`Answer: <answer>`, labels true/false/unknown). For a candidate label y, the *full candidate-answer string score* is the sum of teacher-forced log-probabilities over the complete continuation, s_y(x) = Σ_{i=1}^{|a_y|} log p_θ(a_{y,i} | x, r, a_{y,<i}); the raw prediction is argmax_y s_y(x). No auxiliary heads, task vectors, or training steps touch the model — every component operates on frozen outputs.

**Three-stage diagnostic pipeline.**
1. *Hidden-state probes:* multinomial logistic regressions fitted on frozen hidden states from in-domain examples (≈2,000 training rows, three fixed seeds), evaluated on disjoint held-out splits; randomized-label probes stay at chance (0.304–0.341). Two readout positions are used: a *prompt-end probe* (final state of the original prompt) and an *answer-slot probe* (state after the literal `Answer:` token, before any label token).
2. *Same-position label logits:* the frozen unembedding readout W h^t(x) + b_y at the exact answer-slot position, which is unconstrained by candidate-string geometry.
3. *Prior-conditioned additive correction:* offsets c_y chosen by deterministic grid search to minimize Σ_c Σ_y (q̂_c(y; D_id) − π_y)² against a supplied label prior π_y on unlabeled in-domain score rows; the corrected prediction is argmax_y (s_y(x) + c_y).

**Evaluation protocol and controls.** Offset-fit sets are always disjoint from evaluation sets. A recovery counts as valid only with positive per-example gains *and* survival of three controls: (1) *TF-IDF-missed slices* (examples a bag-of-words classifier cannot solve, removing the "Clever Hans" shortcut account); (2) *label-count permutation baselines* (randomly permuting corrected predictions within the label multiset — a purely distributional fix would hover at chance 1/3); (3) *deterministic subsampling* (fits on 25–500 examples rule out data-intensive relearning). Additional audits: target-prior perturbation, verbatim answer-token count matching, 20 prompt/verbalizer variants, three generator-seed resplits, and mean-normalized vs summed scoring conventions.

**Data and models.** Four tasks in increasing realism: synthetic controlled logic (1,000-example ID fit / 1,000-example OOD splits), ProofWriter (1,000-row ID fit, disjoint 1,000-example OOD eval), ANLI (R1 in-domain fit, adversarial R2 held-out), FOLIO (sparsity diagnostic: offsets fit on 203 validation examples, evaluated on 1,001 pretrained-training examples). The protocol-matched sweep covers post-trained Qwen3.5-2B/4B/9B; cross-family generalization is tested on ProofWriter only, with OLMo-2-1B, Llama-3.1-8B, and Pythia-410M/1.4B/12B.

## Results & Highlights

- **The readout gap is large and statistically solid.** On Qwen3.5-9B, the answer-slot probe reaches 0.972 (in-domain) and 0.830 (lexical OOD) while the full candidate-answer string score stays at chance (0.333) — the probe exceeds the string score by **+0.488 (95% CI [+0.452, +0.523], paired p < .001)**, and same-position label logits exceed it by +0.237 (+0.180, +0.293). For Qwen3.5-2B, probes hit 0.918/0.661 vs the same 0.333 string score.
- **Two-parameter correction recovers 9–34 points.** Fitting offsets on 1,000 unlabeled ID score rows raises Qwen3.5-9B on ProofWriter from 0.333 to **0.678** (macro-F1 0.674; paired gain 474−130=+344 examples, p < .001) and Qwen3.5-4B from 0.333 to 0.653 (+320). On adversarial ANLI R2, Qwen3.5-4B improves from **0.478 to 0.571** (+9.3 points, paired +93, p < .001); on FOLIO, Qwen3.5-9B goes 0.348 → 0.638 (+291). Every configuration in Table 2 is significant (p ≤ 0.035).
- **25 unlabeled examples suffice.** Across all 30 deterministic subsamples at the 25-example budget, collapsed Qwen3.5-2B/4B/9B show positive gains on the synthetic lexical split; on ProofWriter the 25-example fit yields **+0.216 (4B) / +0.277 (9B)**, and moving from 25 to 1,000 fit examples changes held-out accuracy by at most 0.022 on any task — the representations are already separable, merely shifted.
- **Cross-family transfer on ProofWriter.** OLMo-2-1B improves **362 → 566 (+204, p < .001)** and Llama-3.1-8B **333 → 477 (+144, p < .001)**, both exceeding count-preserving permutation nulls; Pythia-410M (+25, p = .103) and Pythia-12B (−6, p = .442) show no reliable recovery — an explicit scope boundary.
- **Shortcut controls hold.** On the TF-IDF-missed ProofWriter slice, the 25-example correction reaches 0.622/0.643 for Qwen3.5-4B/9B with observed-minus-permutation gaps of **+0.287 / +0.305** at full budget; corrected predictions produce permutation gaps of +0.287/+0.305 where a purely distributional fix would sit at 0.333. Worst-case prior perturbations keep gains positive on ProofWriter (+0.281/+0.294) while Pythia-12B degrades to −0.021.
- **Complementary with source-task transfer.** A source-only ensemble (Qwen3.5-4B, Qwen3.5-9B, Llama-3.1-8B adapters) reaches 0.700 on ProofWriter OOD; fusing target-domain corrected sequence scores — still without target labels — raises the hybrid to **0.720** (paired net gain of 20 examples), improving the TF-IDF-missed slice 0.682 → 0.707 and the deepest proof strata (≥2 hops) 0.483 → 0.557.
- **The collapse is a label prior, not a surface artifact.** Pre-correction, Qwen3.5-9B predicts "unknown" for 999/1,000 lexical-OOD examples; mean-normalized scores reproduce the summed-score collapse exactly; the collapse persists in 87–96% of 20 prompt/verbalizer variants and survives equal verbatim answer-token counts and three generator-seed resplits (min Δ +0.230, observed-minus-null +0.252).

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty: solid synthesis rather than a new mechanism.** The ingredients are known — linear probing (Alain & Bengio 2018), additive label-bias correction in the contextual-calibration lineage (Zhao et al. 2021; Holtzman et al. 2021), and surface-form competition (Blasi et al. 2022). The genuine contribution is the *diagnostic framing*: pairing the correction with count-preserving permutation nulls and a provably capacity-limited (two-parameter, label-free) intervention so that recovery certifies pre-existing per-example alignment. That protocol design, not the math, is the paper's real novelty.
- **The headline number overstates the practical fix.** "9–34 points" is accurate, but corrected accuracy (0.57–0.60 on lexical-OOD) remains far below probe decodability (0.77–0.83) — the correction removes only the shared label-level shift β_y and leaves per-example distortions (surface-form competition between candidate strings) untouched. The authors are candid about this, but abstract readers may overestimate how much of the gap is actually closed.
- **The prior assumption is tested only inside its own regime.** Every evaluation split is label-balanced, so the supplied uniform prior always matches the true distribution — exactly the condition under which the correction is guaranteed to behave. The authors admit that identifying both the prior and the offsets from the same unlabeled score matrix without extra supervision is an open problem; on an imbalanced real deployment the method's behavior is simply unknown. This is the paper's most significant external-validity gap.
- **Scope is narrow on purpose, but narrow.** Everything is forced-choice with three labels and a literal answer slot; the protocol-matched sweep centers on one family (Qwen3.5), with cross-family evidence from ProofWriter only. The Pythia negative results are honestly reported but mean the phenomenon is architecture-dependent in ways the paper cannot yet explain (it only gestures at pretraining frequency priors amplified by instruction tuning — a hypothesis, not a demonstrated circuit). A small sign of protocol churn: the repaired 4B probe values required a re-run with format-matched 2,000-example training after a protocol revision (Appendix B/D).
- **The hybrid-ensemble gain is thin.** 0.700 → 0.720 (+20 paired examples) is described by the authors themselves as modest with no significance claim attached — it motivates complementarity but is not, by itself, compelling evidence.
- **No red flags of cherry-picking detected**: negative configurations (ANLI-9B Δ = 0.000, Pythia rows) are printed alongside wins, seeds and budgets are fixed and disclosed, and the conclusion explicitly reframes the correction as diagnostic evidence rather than a capability fix.

## Strengths

- Exceptionally honest epistemics: the intervention is deliberately capacity-limited so that success *proves* something (pre-existing alignment) rather than merely improving accuracy; failures and boundary conditions are foregrounded, not buried.
- A rigorous, multi-layer control suite — TF-IDF shortcut slices, count-preserving permutation nulls, prior perturbation, verbalizer/prompt invariance, seed regeneration, scoring-convention invariance — that goes well beyond typical calibration papers.
- Strong statistical hygiene: paired CIs and p-values for the readout gap, 30 deterministic subsamples for sample efficiency, disjoint fit/evaluation splits, three fixed probe seeds.
- Clear practical takeaway for benchmark users: a near-chance zero-shot score plus a collapsed prediction histogram is diagnostic of a scoring bottleneck, checkable with 25 unlabeled examples and two parameters.

## Limitations

- The supplied label prior is an explicit assumption; all splits are label-balanced, so the correction is validated only within the regime its prior describes, and joint identification of prior + offsets from unlabeled scores remains open.
- The method is an observational diagnostic, not a causal circuit analysis — it triangulates *where* representation and scoring diverge but does not isolate the neural circuits responsible.
- Recovery is partial: corrected accuracy stays well below probe decodability (e.g., 0.57–0.60 vs 0.77–0.83 on lexical-OOD), and global offsets cannot fix per-example distortions such as surface-form competition between candidate strings.
- Limited scope: forced-choice, three-label tasks only; the protocol-matched sweep centers on Qwen3.5, and cross-family results are confined to ProofWriter. Pythia's non-recovery shows the phenomenon is not universal. Extending to open-ended generation would need defensible candidate-answer strings and matched permutation controls, left as future work.
- The mechanistic account (pretraining frequency priors over structural tokens, amplified by instruction tuning/safety alignment) is plausible but untested — the Base-model comparison is suggestive, not conclusive.

## Relevance

与用户的 "LLM Hidden States & Interpretability" 方向高度契合：论文的核心正是隐藏态探针与冻结输出层几何之间的 readout gap，用最简加性干预证明内部表征与外显评分的错位，是"探针证据 ≠ 原生管线可用信息"这一方法论争点的扎实实证。同时其对零样本推理基准评分可靠性的诊断协议，对模型评估与推理结果观测也有直接借鉴意义。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Can the label prior and the per-label offsets be jointly identified from the unlabeled score matrix alone (e.g., via clustering structure or sparsity assumptions), removing the supplied-prior assumption that currently limits imbalanced deployments?
- The paper speculates the collapse should shrink as evaluation moves from single-token to free-form chain-of-thought answers — does this prediction hold, and can candidate-string scoring be made statistically defensible for open-ended generation?
- What mechanistic circuit implements the structural bias β_y? Is it attributable to specific directions in the unembedding pathway, and does instruction tuning or safety alignment causally amplify it (the Base-vs-instructed comparison suggests but does not prove this)?
- Can per-example residual distortions — surface-form competition between candidate strings that global offsets cannot touch — be addressed by targeted (input- or position-specific) interventions, closing more of the probe-sequence gap?
- Does the corrected sequence score serve as a better calibrated confidence signal for downstream agent pipelines (selective prediction, abstention, verification gating) than raw likelihood or token-level probabilities?
