> **一句话总结：** 范德比尔特大学（Tyler Derr 组）联合 AFRL 提出 AutoKD——六个 LLM agent（骨干仅 Qwen2.5-14B，单卡 RTX 4090）在持久"洞察图"（insight graph）上做跨轮自主知识发现：候选假设经四个模板的确定性统计 DSL（assoc/diff/interact/heterogeneity）编译执行、由 Evaluator 打有效性分、Critic 按结构距离+语义距离+认知惊奇融合打新颖度分，被接受后以 EXTENDS/DEEPENS/NARROWS/CONTRADICTS 类型边入库并反哺下一轮探索方向；在 WVS Wave 7 / Amazon Books / SciSciNet 三个数据集上，其 top 洞察在盲评 A/B（4 人 + 4 个 LLM 评委）中对从已发表论文蒸馏出的 gold claims 拿到 65.9% 总胜率（人评 WVS Overall 达 86.1%），且 15 个文献派生查询 0% 完全未覆盖；消融显示去掉跨轮持久化会使深度发现占比从 28.9% 崩至 12.5%（相对 −56.7%）。

> **TL;DR:** AutoKD is a six-agent LLM framework (Qwen 2.5-14B on a single RTX 4090) for cumulative, autonomous knowledge discovery on a static dataset: candidate hypotheses are compiled through a deterministic statistical DSL (association / group-difference / interaction / heterogeneity templates — no free-form LLM code), scored for validity (with effect-size and split-sample-instability penalties) and novelty, and accepted findings are stored as nodes in a persistent insight graph with typed edges (EXTENDS, DEEPENS, NARROWS, CONTRADICTS) that steers subsequent exploration toward under-covered variables. Evaluated against gold claims distilled from published papers on World Values Survey Wave 7, Amazon Books reviews, and SciSciNet, 8 blind raters (4 human, 4 LLM judges) prefer AutoKD's insights 65.9% Overall overall (ties excluded) — 86.1% among humans on WVS — but only 17.5% on Amazon "Useful"; an ablation on WVS shows removing cross-round persistence collapses deep-test share from 28.9% to 12.5% (−56.7% relative). The literature-grounded gold-claim evaluation protocol is itself a reusable contribution.

## 核心贡献

- **问题形式化**：首次把"自主知识发现"定义为与一次性论文生成不同的任务——给定数据集 D 和可选的自然语言目标 g，跨多轮 inquiry 产出累积、经过验证的经验发现集合 I（I^(1) ⊆ I^(2) ⊆ … ⊆ I^(R)），并拆解出两个耦合子问题：单轮发现（无人在关键路径上的生成-检验-验证）与跨轮积累（保留/组织已有发现以减少冗余、引导探索欠覆盖区域）。
- **AutoKD 框架**：六 agent 协作（Orchestrator / Generator / Critic / Coder-Runner / Evaluator / Historian）+ 单调增长的洞察图 G=(I, E)；节点是带有效性分、新颖度分、效应量和方向的已验证发现，边是四种认知关系类型。Orchestrator 按"洞察数-聚类密度-冲突存在"四模式（local/global/refine/conflict）自适应调度探索。
- **无幻觉的统计执行**：假设必须落在确定性 DSL 的四个模板族内，编译为固定统计管线（Pearson/OLS/WLS、Welch t 检验、Mann-Whitney U、Kruskal-Wallis、Type-II ANOVA、Cohen's d 等），彻底排除 LLM 自由生成代码的幻觉/不可运行问题；每个发现都携带 p 值、效应量和置信区间。
- **文献锚定的评测框架**：从引用数据集原始论文的后续文献中蒸馏 gold claims（每数据集 10 条），盲评 A/B（4 名研究生级人类评委 + 4 个 LLM 评委：claude-opus-4-7、gemini-3、DeepSeek-v3、gpt-5，匈牙利匹配对齐）评单洞察质量（RQ1）；再用 5 条/数据集的文献派生查询经 GraphRAG 式检索-综合-校验评多洞察覆盖度（RQ2）。
- **据作者所知的首个跨三个不同领域数据集的规模化知识发现实证研究**：全部跑在单张 RTX 4090（24GB）+ Qwen 2.5-14B (Q4_K_M) 上，代码开源（github.com/GeQinwen/AutoKD），附录完整给出提示词与超参数。

## Key Contributions

- **Formalizes autonomous agentic knowledge discovery** as a task distinct from one-shot AI-scientist systems: given dataset D and an optional natural-language goal g, produce a cumulative, validated set of empirical findings across multiple inquiry rounds, decomposed into two coupled sub-problems — per-round discovery (generate–test–validate with no human in the critical path) and cross-round accumulation (retain and organize prior findings to steer follow-up inquiry and reduce redundancy).
- **Proposes AutoKD**, coupling an iterative six-agent discovery loop (Orchestrator, Generator, Critic, Coder/Runner, Evaluator, Historian) with a monotonic insight graph whose nodes are validated findings (validity score, novelty score, effect size, direction) and whose typed edges (EXTENDS/DEEPENS/NARROWS/CONTRADICTS) serve as both long-term memory and an exploration-steering mechanism; the Orchestrator adapts among four exploration modes (local/global/refine/conflict) based on graph state (insight count, cluster density, conflict presence).
- **Hallucination-free execution by construction**: hypotheses are restricted to a deterministic DSL of four statistical template families compiled into fixed pipelines (Pearson/OLS/WLS, Welch t, Mann-Whitney U, Kruskal-Wallis, Type-II ANOVA/partial η², Cohen's d, logit models), so every accepted finding carries a p-value, effect size, and CI — no LLM-authored code is ever executed.
- **A literature-grounded evaluation framework**: gold claims distilled from papers that use each dataset as their primary data source, blind A/B comparison (4 graduate-level human raters + 4 LLM judges: claude-opus-4-7, gemini-3, DeepSeek-v3, gpt-5) for single-insight quality (RQ1) via Hungarian matching, and a retrieve–synthesize–verify protocol over the graph for 15 literature-derived queries measuring multi-insight coverage (RQ2).
- **First large-scale empirical study** of agentic knowledge discovery across three diverse datasets (e-commerce, cross-national social science, science of science), running entirely on one RTX 4090 with Qwen 2.5-14B; code and full prompts/hyperparameters released.

## Methodology

**Framework.** AutoKD takes a dataset D (plus optional natural-language goal g) and produces a persistent insight graph G = (I, E). Each insight node stores the tuple (hypothesis h, p-value/effect size, validity score s_v(h) ∈ [0,1], novelty score s_n(h), effect direction); each edge carries a relation type t ∈ {EXTENDS, DEEPENS, NARROWS, CONTRADICTS} assigned by a deterministic classifier over metadata. The graph grows monotonically across rounds (G^(r+1) ⊇ G^(r)); runs stop after 30 consecutive empty rounds or a 496-round budget.

**Discovery loop (knowledge creation).** Six agents operate per round: (1) the Orchestrator selects an exploration mode and anchor insights — mode weights are adjusted per round from graph state (insight count, cluster density, conflict presence) rather than a fixed schedule; (2) the Generator runs a two-phase flow — thematic direction selection from a concept menu, then refinement into a batch of B=10 concrete hypotheses using TF-IDF RAG over dataset variable metadata; (3) the Critic pre-filters structurally duplicate, semantically duplicate (embedding similarity), or tautological hypotheses, then post-execution judges novelty as a blend of structural distance, semantic distance, and LLM-assessed epistemic surprise, issuing ACCEPT/REFINE/REJECT; (4) the Coder/Runner compiles the hypothesis in a DSL restricted to four template families — assoc(X→Y), diff(Y~X), interact(X×M→Y), heterogeneity(X→Y|G) — into deterministic estimators chosen by outcome type (logistic for binary, OLS/WLS for continuous, Welch/Mann-Whitney/Kruskal-Wallis for group differences); (5) the Evaluator composes a validity score penalizing trivially small effect sizes and split-sample instability; (6) the Historian inserts accepted insights, classifies typed relations, and assembles context for the next round. The backbone is Qwen 2.5-14B (Q4_K_M, llama.cpp) on one RTX 4090 24GB, with all-in-1-MiniLM-L6-v2 embeddings; temperature 0.75 for exploration agents, 0.0 for deterministic judges.

**Knowledge extraction.** Given a natural-language query, the pipeline retrieves seed nodes by embedding similarity, expands one-hop neighbors scored by a weighted combination of semantic similarity and stored validity, synthesizes a grounded answer with citations to node claims, and verifies it with a separate judge (faithfulness = fraction of supported claims).

**Evaluation.** Gold claims are curated by finding papers that cite the dataset's primary publication (Google Scholar), screening so the dataset is the primary data source, then extracting and splitting directional empirical claims (10 per dataset for RQ1; 5 queries per dataset for RQ2). RQ1: blind A/B of AutoKD's top-10 insights (selected by its own novelty+validity scores) against Hungarian-matched gold claims, rated on four forced-choice dimensions (More interesting / More surprising / More useful / Overall better) by 4 human raters and 4 LLM judges, 30 pairs total. RQ2: a human rater with graduate-level research training scores each query answer on a four-level coverage scale (MC/C/P/NC). Ablations on WVS under a matched 1,000-round budget isolate persistence, graph structure, and global-only exploration, measuring distinct-estimate ratio and deep-test share (fraction of accepted findings using interaction or heterogeneity tests).

## Results & Highlights

- **RQ1 open-ended quality — at or above parity everywhere, biggest margin on Surprising.** Aggregated across three datasets and all 8 raters, AutoKD's top insights are preferred over matched gold claims distilled from published papers on 65.9% of pairs Overall (ties excluded) — 64.4% for human raters, 67.2% for LLM judges — with the largest margin on Surprising (71.3%). Per-dataset, humans prefer AutoKD 86.1% Overall on WVS, 51.4% on SciSciNet, 47.5% on Amazon.
- **But it loses on actionability in one domain.** On Amazon Reviews, both human and LLM rater pools give AutoKD only 17.5% on the Useful dimension — canonical findings (e.g., "longer reviews receive more helpful votes") still beat AutoKD's less-obvious associations when real-world guidance matters; accordingly LLM judges diverge from humans most on Useful (40.3% vs 66.0% aggregate win rate).
- **RQ2 coverage — no query left uncovered, and 20% more-than-covered.** Across 15 literature-derived queries, 0.0% are Not-Covered by the insight graph; on average 20.0% are More-than-covered (the graph adds evidence or interaction detail beyond the published finding), 13.3% Covered, and 66.7% Partially covered (WVS: 0/40/60/0; Amazon: 40/0/60/0; SciSciNet: 20/0/80/0 in %MC/C/P/NC).
- **Ablation — persistence is the dominant driver of discovery depth.** On WVS under a matched 1,000-round budget, full AutoKD reaches a 28.9% deep-test share (interaction/heterogeneity findings) with 94.9% distinct estimates and 0.0% exact recovery of earlier specifications; removing cross-round persistence collapses deep-test share to 12.5% (Δ Depth −56.7%) and lets exact recovery rise to 16.7%; global-only exploration yields 20.1% (−30.4%); removing graph structure while keeping persistence costs −10.3%.
- **Scale of the hypothesis space and graphs.** With 5 controls the enumerable hypothesis space reaches 3.83×10^16 on WVS (196 testable variables) vs 1.54×10^5 with 0 controls — exhaustive enumeration is hopeless, motivating the learn-to-select design. Final insight graphs: 1,367 insights / 3,640 typed relations (WVS), 565 (CS subset) / 1,097 (full) insights and 1,496/2,794 relations (SciSciNet), 119 insights / 268 relations (Amazon). Diversity reranking visibly flattens the variable-usage concentration of the score-only top-10 toward the full-graph distribution (Fig. 4).
- **A quantified caveat on the raters.** Inter-rater agreement is strong among LLM judges (Fleiss κ = 0.728 Overall) but near zero for human panels (κ = 0.098 aggregated; negative on SciSciNet, e.g. −0.065 Overall) — the headline win rates rest on humans who barely agreed with each other.

## Authors & Credibility

The authors are from Vanderbilt University (Qinwen Ge, Bo Ni, Haowei Fu, Ngoc N. Tran, Tyler Derr) and the Air Force Research Laboratory (Erik Blasch). Tyler Derr's group is well established in graph mining, knowledge graphs, and social-network analysis, which matches the paper's central artifact (a typed insight graph); the team has adjacent work on science-of-science datasets and LLM-based agents. Credibility signals are strong on the engineering side: the full system runs on a single RTX 4090 with an open-weight 14B backbone, code is released (github.com/GeQinwen/AutoKD), and the appendices expose complete agent prompts, hyperparameters, the deterministic estimator table, and all 30 evaluation pairs. The evaluation-protocol appendix also discloses its blinding mechanics (frozen randomization, post-hoc A/B decoding), which is more methodological transparency than typical for this genre.

## Critical Assessment

- **The headline win rates are noisier than presented.** The claim "preferred over gold claims on all four dimensions" rests on human panels whose Fleiss κ is 0.098 overall and actually negative on SciSciNet (−0.065 Overall, −0.056 Useful). With only 10 pairs per dataset, a 51.4% human Overall win rate on SciSciNet is statistically indistinguishable from a coin flip, and no confidence intervals or significance tests are reported for any win rate. The WVS human Overall cell even reads 106.0% under a "ties excluded" normalization — a number above 100% that is never explained — a small but real reporting blemish.
- **A structural selection asymmetry favors AutoKD in RQ1.** AutoKD's side of the comparison consists of its own top-10 insights selected by its own novelty+validity scores, then Hungarian-matched (most favorable pairing) to a static sample of gold claims. The published-literature side never gets to pick its best shots. A fairer design would let an independent selector choose from both pools.
- **No external baseline system.** The only controls are self-ablations (w/o persistence, w/o graph structure, global-only). There is no head-to-head against an existing AI-scientist or a trivial random-hypothesis sampler under the same 496-round budget, so it is impossible to tell how much of the performance comes from the insight-graph machinery versus simply running any generate–test–filter loop long enough.
- **Novelty is self-referential.** The Critic judges novelty against the system's own graph plus retrieved neighboring insights, not against the published literature at scale; "surprising" findings are only checked externally against 10 gold claims per dataset. Some AutoKD "discoveries" (e.g., WVS item 4 in Table 12, a near-paraphrase of a gold claim about hiring-priority scarcity) show the novelty filter admits thinly reworded known results.
- **Scope of the DSL is asserted, not measured.** The paper argues exhaustive enumeration is infeasible (up to 3.83×10^16 hypotheses with k=5 controls on WVS) and claims the four template families "already cover the bulk of testable findings" — but provides no audit of how many candidate hypotheses were rejected as inexpressible. No causal, longitudinal, or unstructured-data discovery is possible under the current DSL.
- **Coverage is thinner than the 0% NC figure suggests.** 66.7% of literature queries are only Partially covered — i.e., for most queries the graph captures a related theme but fails the directional/variable-level match on the actual published finding — and the RQ2 scoring uses a single human rater.

## Strengths

- The problem framing is genuinely distinct from one-shot AI-scientist systems: cumulative validated findings over a dataset, with the memory itself steering exploration, is the right abstraction for long-running autonomous research.
- Deterministic statistical compilation is a pragmatic, honest design choice: every finding ships with a p-value, effect size, and CI, and hallucinated/unrunnable code is impossible by construction — a reliability argument most agent papers in this space lack.
- The ablation quantifies each mechanism's contribution rather than hand-waving: persistence accounts for the majority of discovery depth (−56.7% when removed), which cleanly validates the paper's core thesis.
- The literature-grounded gold-claim protocol (distillation, Hungarian matching, blinded A/B with frozen randomization) is a reusable evaluation contribution for the field.
- Radical cost accessibility and reproducibility: Qwen 2.5-14B on one 24GB GPU, open code, full prompts and all 30 comparison pairs in the appendix.
- Honest ethical discussion: the paper itself warns that errors accepted into the persistent graph compound across rounds and that findings are exploratory, not confirmatory.

## Limitations

- Statistical expressiveness is capped at four bivariate/interaction template families — no causal inference, no time-series, no text or image modalities, no multivariate modeling; "covers the bulk of testable findings" is asserted without measurement.
- Only three flat-table datasets (e-commerce reviews, survey microdata, science-of-science records); nothing resembling laboratory or natural-science data, so claims about "scientific discovery" should be read as tabular empirical discovery.
- Human-rater agreement near zero (κ = 0.098) and 10 pairs per dataset make the win-rate comparisons statistically fragile; no significance testing anywhere.
- Single backbone (Qwen 2.5-14B): no evidence on how the loop behaves with frontier models or other open-weight models, and no sensitivity analysis over round budgets beyond the WVS ablation.
- Novelty and coverage judgments ultimately loop back to the system's own scores and graph; the only external anchor is 10 gold claims per dataset.
- Long-run error compounding — acknowledged in the ethics section — is not empirically characterized beyond the 496-round runs reported.

## Relevance

该文与用户最高优先级方向高度吻合：它就是一个带持久记忆的自治科研 agent 循环（自动假设生成 → 执行验证 → 跨轮积累并自我引导），其"确定性 DSL 消灭幻觉代码 + 有效性/新颖度双评分 + 消融证明持久化是深度发现的主因"直接回应了长时自主 agent 系统的可靠性与可信度问题；对构建自演化 research loop 的记忆结构与探索调度设计有直接参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Given human Fleiss κ ≈ 0.098, would the open-ended win rates survive a larger, agreement-screened rater panel with reported confidence intervals — or an LLM-judge-only protocol with human calibration?
- Can the DSL be extended toward causal designs (DiD, instrumental variables, panel FE) or longitudinal templates without losing the determinism guarantee, and what fraction of rejected hypotheses were inexpressible under the current four families?
- How does AutoKD compare head-to-head with Kosmos- or AI-Co-Scientist-style systems — and against a plain random-hypothesis sampler — under the same compute and round budget?
- The w/o-graph-structure ablation only costs −10.3% depth: do the typed relations (EXTENDS/DEEPENS/NARROWS/CONTRADICTS) measurably improve downstream query answering (RQ2) relative to a flat validated-findings store?
- Over much longer runs (>500 rounds), does split-sample-instability penalization suffice to keep spurious associations from compounding through the persistent graph, and can contradicted insights ever be retired rather than accumulating monotonically?
