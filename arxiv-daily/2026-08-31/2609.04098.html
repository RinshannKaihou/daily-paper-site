Why This Matters 为何重要

Settles whether 4-bit noise diverges through long-context recurrence: it doesn't — and exposes serving-stack traps that silently corrupt hybrid NVFP4 benchmarks.

低精度误差不会在循环递推中发散：delta rule 的覆写机制会主动擦除状态噪声；校准 scale 与融合 GEMM 失配则是部署侧静默数据损坏的教科书案例。

Critical Edge 批判视角

Evidence is one model family, one format (NVFP4), one GPU: the gate-shielding mechanism depends on Qwen's log-space parameterization and is unverified for other recurrent mixers.

证据仅覆盖单一模型、单一格式、单张卡；门控防护论证依赖 Qwen 的 log-space softplus/exp 参数化，对线性参数化衰减混合器尚未验证。

Methodology

Calibration-only PTQ + a four-link mechanism study — no QAT, no fine-tuning.

  • Recipe. llm-compressor NVFP4 W4A4 (E2M1 values, one E4M3 scale per 16-element block) applied to all 496 linear layers — 240 GDN · 64 attention · 192 MLP. Only lm_head, embeddings, convolutions, norms exempt. Frozen 128-sample × 32K-token calibration set.
  • Model. Qwen3.8-27B: 48 Gated DeltaNet layers interleaved with 16 full-attention layers (hidden 5120; per-head state 128×128; gates in log space: α = exp(−exp(A_log)·softplus(a+dt)), β = σ(b)).
  • Serving regime. One regime for every number: vLLM 0.27.1, TP=1, single RTX PRO 6000 (96 GB), FP8 KV cache. Baselines: BF16, Unsloth Dynamic v3, RadixArk (both keep GDN at FP8 W8A8 with gates in BF16).
  • Mechanism probes (§5). Activations captured from all 48 GDN layers; standalone GDN re-implementation verified to 6×10⁻³ (BF16 rounding); fake-quant injection adds exactly the 4-bit error and nothing else. Four chained experiments: tensor statistics → per-projection sensitivity replays → lockstep-FP32 recurrence replay over 32K tokens → per-token NLL gap binned in 2K-token windows.

Mechanism — why error doesn't accumulate

  • Plateau, not compounding. Lockstep-FP32 state error stays flat: 12.96% at token 256 → 12.31% at token 32,768 (plateau ~12.6%, max 14.9%). A 1% state impulse decays to 1/e within 80–1,382 steps — far inside the 44K–62K-token decay-gate horizon; delta-rule overwrite is the extra eraser.
  • The inversion. The community-protected gate projections a/b are the two most robust tensors: 11.0% / 8.5% GEMM error compress to 2.1% / 2.6% layer-output error by the log-space nonlinearity — the smallest of five projections.
  • Outliers localized. GDN inputs show max/RMS up to 368 with kurtosis ~1,560, yet NVFP4's 16-element block scaling keeps activation error uniform at 7.5–9.2% across roles; the block's 19.2% output error matches the 19.4% quadrature prediction.
  • Gap shrinks with context. Per-token NLL cost +0.081 nats (first half) → +0.011 (second) → −0.053 in the last 2K tokens — Minima beats BF16 there. PPL gap narrows +0.72@4K → +0.49@32K.

Serving-stack traps — each silently corrupted a result first

  • Fused-GEMM scale mismatch (1.82×/2.75× in all 48 layers) dropped AIME 86.7 → 80.8 while faking better-than-BF16 perplexity (6.86 vs true 10.84) — a broken forget gate improves WikiText PPL. Checkpoint repair: 94 scale sets folded in, worst ratio 2.81×, re-rounding ≤6.2%, kernel error 0.35/0.57 → 0.002.
  • Raw-completion harnesses are invalid for thinking models: leaked <think> on 25–48 of 50 questions produced 66.3/58.6 vs the valid 80.4/79.7.
  • FP8 KV recipe. Calibrated scales cut Minima's PPL@32K 10.84 → 10.50 (83% of the penalty recovered, residual +0.07 below BF16's own uncalibrated cost) at <0.4% throughput change — serve FP8 KV + ship calibrated scales.

Strengths 优势

  • First serious W4A4 treatment of a production hybrid's recurrent half, with large efficiency wins at no measured accuracy cost: 17.53 GiB, 4.03 s prefill, AIME'25 exactly matching BF16, RULER 100 @ 64K.
  • Cross-checked mechanism methodology: fake-quant injection, per-projection replay, a quadrature independence check (19.4% predicted vs 19.2% measured), impulse-decay beating the decay-only explanation, and synthetic-noise controls that localize fragility to α's horizon sensitivity.
  • Measurement-hygiene findings valuable beyond quantization: fused-scale mismatch, composite-vs-text serving paths, invalid raw-completion harnesses, base-model context inversion.
  • Unusually honest vendor paper: releases the checkpoint, publishes its own previously corrupted results, reports where it loses, and states limitations concretely.
  • Actionable recipes: harmonize fused scales before serving; serve FP8 KV with calibrated scales.

Limitations 局限

  • Single model family/size, single format, single GPU; gate-shielding is unverified for linearly-parameterized decay mixers (e.g., Mamba2-style gates).
  • Context ceiling: 32K perplexity, 64K retrieval; 128K+ behavior is extrapolation from the bounded-error mechanism — no longer-context stress tests.
  • QUASAR QAT checkpoint never benchmarked; the "training unnecessary" claim rests on incomparable model-card numbers.
  • Minima+scales scores inherited, not re-measured (only PPL, RULER, throughput were).
  • Decode trails RadixArk by 2–4% despite fewer bytes — attributed to small-batch NVFP4 activation-quant kernel overhead, not fixed.
  • Benchmark discrimination is weak where it matters least: RULER saturates at 100, LiveCodeBench is single-seed, and calibration-set sensitivity (one frozen 128×32K set) is unexplored.

Questions for Further Exploration

From the summary's closing section

  • Does the plateau-and-erasure mechanism hold for linearly-parameterized decay gates (Mamba2 variants), or only for log-space softplus/exp parameterizations?
  • What happens at 128K–1M context: does the state-error plateau persist past the observed 44K–62K decay horizons, and does the end-to-end gap keep shrinking or re-grow?
  • How sensitive is the recipe to calibration-set choice — size, diversity, domain shift?
  • Would a W4A16 weight-only GDN variant split the residual PPL gap (+0.72@4K → +0.49@32K) between A4 activations and W4 weights?
  • Can the fused-GEMM scale mismatch be fixed kernel-side instead of by checkpoint rewriting — and does the same hazard hit fused attention projections in non-hybrid models?
  • Head-to-head under one harness: does concurrent QAT (QUASAR) beat calibration-only PTQ on the honest residual (perplexity)?
