> **一句话总结：** 把 Qwen3.8-27B 全部 496 个线性层——包括所有社区 4-bit 配方(Unsloth、RadixArk)都保护在 FP8/BF16 的 48 层 Gated DeltaNet(占 5.5B 参数)门控投影——做成 NVFP4 W4A4 后训练量化(Minima),五任务平均仅比 BF16 低 0.52 分(种子噪声内,优于 RadixArk 的 −0.82),权重 50.13→17.53 GiB(2.9× 压缩)、32K prefill 6.90s→4.03s,并用四步机制实验证明"循环态误差会累积"的直觉是反的:块缩放把离群点局部化(各层激活误差均匀 7.5–9.2%)、门控非线性把 ~11% GEMM 误差压到 2.1%/2.6%、delta rule 覆写使 32K token 上状态误差持平(~12.6%)且 1% 状态脉冲数百步内被擦除,端到端困惑度差距随上下文收敛(最后 2K token 上 Minima 反超 BF16 0.053 nats)。

> **TL;DR:** Minima quantizes **all 496 linear layers** of Qwen3.8-27B — including the 48 Gated DeltaNet layers every public NVFP4 recipe (**Unsloth Dynamic v3, RadixArk/ModelOpt**) keeps at FP8 W8A8 with gates in BF16 — to NVFP4 W4A4 via calibration-only PTQ. On the six-suite benchmark it matches BF16 within seed noise (MMLU-Pro 79.7 vs 80.4, GSM8K 95.5 = 95.5, AIME'25 86.7 = 86.7; 5-task average −0.52, better than RadixArk's −0.82) while being the smallest (17.53 vs 50.13 GiB, 2.9×) and fastest-prefill (TFPT 4.03 s vs 6.90 s at 32K) recipe, with RULER retrieval at 100 even at 64K. A lockstep-FP32 recurrence replay shows state error plateaus (~12.6% over 32K tokens) instead of compounding, and the community-protected gate projections a/b are actually the two *most* robust tensors (2.1%/2.6% output error). Bonus: a fused-GEMM scale mismatch that silently corrupts GDN gates — and *fakes better-than-BF16 perplexity* — is a cautionary tale for anyone benchmarking hybrid NVFP4 deployments.

## 核心贡献

- **首个真正的全 W4A4 混合架构量化模型(Minima):** Qwen3.8-27B 的 496 个线性层(240 GDN + 64 attention + 192 MLP)全部 NVFP4 量化,仅排除 lm_head/embedding/卷积/norm;社区共识保护的 GDN 衰减门 a 和写入门 b 也一并 4-bit,仅用校准式 PTQ(无 QAT、无微调)。
- **实证推翻误差累积直觉:** 六个评测套件上与 BF16 在种子噪声内打平——五任务平均 85.10 vs 85.62(−0.52),三个量化配方仅相差 0.54 分,小于一道 AIME 题(3.3 分);同时是同期最小(17.53 GiB)、最大 KV 预算(单卡 1.81M 可缓存 token)、最快 prefill 的配方。
- **四链机制解释(§5):** (i) GDN 输入与 attention 共享残差流的极端离群点(max/RMS 中位数 63.5、峰度 ~1560),但 NVFP4 的 16 元素块缩放把离群点局部化,使各层角色 A4 误差均匀在 7.5–9.2%;(ii) 被保护的门控投影其实最不敏感——softplus/exp/sigmoid 参数化把 ~11% GEMM 误差压缩为 2.1%(a)/2.6%(b) 的层输出误差;(iii) delta rule 每次写入沿当前 key 方向覆写状态,32K lockstep FP32 重放中状态误差持平(12.96%→12.31%),1% 状态脉冲 80–1,382 步内衰减到 1/e,远快于衰减门隐含的 44K–62K token 视界;(iv) 端到端逐 token 量化代价随上下文被"洗掉"(窗口前半 +0.081 nats → 后半 +0.011 → 最后 2K −0.053)。
- **服务栈测量发现(§6):** 每模块校准的 NVFP4 checkpoint 被 vLLM 融合 GEMM(取各模块 scale 最大值)服务时,48 层全部出现 1.82×/2.75× 的 scale 失配,静默破坏门控(AIME 86.7→80.8),同时长上下文 PPL 假性"优于" BF16(6.86 vs 真实 10.84)——坏掉的遗忘门让状态记住一切,反而利好 WikiText 困惑度;另发现多模态 composite 服务路径和 raw-completion harness(对 thinking 模型无效)两个测量陷阱。
- **FP8 KV cache 实用配方(§7):** 16 个 attention 层的 FP8 KV 不动任何任务分数、容量 ×1.8–1.9,但 Minima 的 PPL@32K 代价是 BF16 的 3×(+0.41 vs +0.13);校准 per-tensor scale 把 10.84 降到 10.50(恢复 83%,残差 +0.07 低于 BF16 自身未校准代价),吞吐变化 <0.4%——结论是无条件"serve FP8 KV + ship calibrated scales"。

## Key Contributions

- **First true W4A4 quantization of a hybrid LLM's recurrent half:** all 496 linear layers of Qwen3.8-27B to NVFP4, including the 48 GDN layers (5.5B params, ~23% of decode weight bytes) that every public recipe keeps at FP8 W8A8 with gate projections in BF16 — achieved with calibration-only post-training quantization, no QAT or fine-tuning.
- **Empirical refutation of the error-accumulation intuition:** matches BF16 within seed noise on all six suites (5-task avg −0.52, vs −0.28 Unsloth / −0.82 RadixArk; the cohort spans 0.54 points, less than one AIME problem) while being the smallest (17.53 GiB, 2.9× smaller than BF16), the largest KV budget (1.81M cacheable tokens/card), and the fastest prefill (TFPT 6.90 → 4.03 s at 32K).
- **A four-part mechanistic account:** block scaling localizes the residual stream's outliers (activation error uniform 7.5–9.2% across roles despite max/RMS up to 368); the log-space gate parameterization compresses ~11% GEMM error to 2.1%/2.6% on the protected projections; the delta rule's overwrite bounds state error at a flat ~12.6% plateau and erases a 1% impulse within hundreds of steps; the end-to-end NLL gap shrinks with position and turns negative in the last 2K tokens. The community's protected projections are precisely the ones the architecture already protects.
- **Serving-stack findings required to measure any of this:** a per-module-calibration vs fused-GEMM-scale mismatch that silently corrupts GDN gates (AIME 80.8 vs 86.7 repaired) while faking *better-than-BF16* long-context perplexity (6.86 vs true 10.84); the multimodal-composite serving path distorts long-context scoring; raw-completion harnesses are invalid for thinking models (MMLU-Pro 66.3/58.6 invalid vs 80.4/79.7 valid); a calibrated FP8 KV recipe recovering 83% of the long-context penalty at zero throughput cost.

## Methodology

**Model and recipe.** Qwen3.8-27B interleaves 48 Gated DeltaNet (GDN) layers with 16 full-attention layers (hidden size 5120; per-head state S_t ∈ R^{128×128}; gates parameterized in log space: α_t = exp(−exp(A_log)·softplus(a_t + dt_bias)), β_t = σ(b_t)). Minima applies llm-compressor NVFP4 W4A4 (E2M1 values, one E4M3 scale per 16-element block, FP32 per-tensor scale) to every linear layer — 240 GDN, 64 attention, 192 MLP projections; 496 total — excluding only lm_head, embeddings, convolutions, and norms. Calibration uses a frozen 128-sample × 32K-token set; the model is served only after the global-scale harmonization repair of §6.

**Serving regime and baselines.** One regime for every number: text-only checkpoints, vLLM 0.27.1, TP=1, a single RTX PRO 6000 (96 GB, SM120, native NVFP4), FP8 KV cache, GPU utilization 0.85, 32K generation cap. Baselines are BF16 (unquantized reference extracted from the multimodal composite) and the two public NVFP4 checkpoints — Unsloth Dynamic v3 and RadixArk (ModelOpt) — both of which keep GDN and attention at FP8 W8A8 with a/b in BF16 and quantize only MLPs to NVFP4. Minima+scales adds calibrated FP8 KV-cache scales to an otherwise byte-identical Minima.

**Evaluation.** WikiText-2 perplexity at 4K and inside a single 32K request; MMLU-Pro and GSM8K via a chat-template harness with thinking disabled (raw-completion harnesses are shown invalid, §6); AIME'25 and GPQA-Diamond as pass@1 over four seeds (temp 0.6, top-p 0.95); LiveCodeBench v6 (single seed); RULER NIAH single/multikey at 32K and 64K. Every run passes per-sample validity gates (empty/unextracted answers, leaked `<think>`, truncation statistics).

**Mechanism study (§5).** Real inputs of all 48 GDN layers were captured while the BF16 model read eight 32K-token documents. One GDN layer was re-implemented standalone (verified against the reference to 6×10⁻³ median relative output difference, i.e., BF16 rounding) and NVFP4 *fake quantization* (quantize, dequantize, continue in FP32) injected exactly the 4-bit rounding error and nothing else. Four chained experiments: (1) activation/weight statistics at NVFP4 granularity (Table 2); (2) per-projection sensitivity — one projection W4A4 at a time, 96 (layer, sequence) replays of 8K tokens, measuring the layer-output error (Table 3); (3) recurrence replay in lockstep FP32 — one clean trajectory vs eleven perturbed ones on identical inputs for 32K tokens across five layers, plus state-impulse decay tests and synthetic-noise arms that perturb α or β directly; (4) positional decomposition of the per-token NLL gap over the 32K window in 2K-token bins (Figure 2).

## Results & Highlights

- **Accuracy matches BF16 within seed noise everywhere.** On MMLU-Pro Minima scores 79.7 vs BF16's 80.4 (Unsloth 78.9, RadixArk 79.1); GSM8K 95.5 = 95.5; AIME'25 86.7 = 86.7 — Minima hits 26/30 on *all four* seeds while BF16's own spread is 83.3/93.3/83.3/86.7, so the cohort's largest single-task gap (RadixArk's AIME −2.5) is inside BF16's seed noise; GPQA-Diamond 85.1 vs 86.5; LiveCodeBench 78.5 vs 79.0. The 5-task average is 85.10 vs BF16's 85.62 (−0.52), vs −0.28 (Unsloth) and −0.82 (RadixArk); the three quantized recipes span 0.54 points — less than one AIME problem (3.3 points). RULER is 100 for all four models at 32K/64K.
- **Efficiency: the only recipe with a 4-bit GDN.** Weights 17.53 GiB vs BF16's 50.13 (2.9×), 7–13% smaller than Unsloth (20.23) and RadixArk (18.83); largest KV budget (1.81M cacheable tokens on one card); TFPT 4.03 s vs BF16's 6.90 s at 32K (+14–19% prompt throughput at 8K over the community builds, whose GDN GEMMs remain FP8); decode 1,154 tok/s @ concurrency 32, within 4% of the cohort (RadixArk leads decode by 2–4%).
- **Perplexity is the honest residual — and it shrinks with context.** Minima PPL 7.67@4K / 10.84@32K vs BF16's 6.95 / 10.35: the gap narrows from +0.72 to +0.49 with context (Unsloth 7.16/9.91 and RadixArk 7.35/9.95 are lower only because they quantize less of the model). The positional decomposition shows the weight-quantization cost is +0.081 nats in the first half of the 32K window, +0.011 in the second, and −0.053 in the final 2K tokens — Minima *beats* BF16 there.
- **Mechanism numbers.** Despite GDN inputs with median-layer max/RMS 63.5 and kurtosis ~1,560 (out_proj 298.1; MLP down 368.3), per-token A4 error is uniform across layer roles at 7.5–9.2%. The community-protected gate projections a and b move layer output by only 2.1% and 2.6% — the two smallest of five — despite their own GEMM errors of 11.0% and 8.5%; the full block's 19.2% output error matches the quadrature prediction of 19.4% (error independence). State error in lockstep FP32 is flat: relS 12.96% at token 256 → 12.31% at token 32,768 (plateau 12.6%, max 14.9%). A 1% state impulse at t=1,024 decays to 1/e within 80–1,382 steps and 1/10 within ~2,200–2,900, versus decay-implied horizons |1/(1−α)| of 44K–62K tokens — the delta rule's overwrite is the extra eraser. Fragility is located precisely: 0.1% multiplicative noise on α directly yields 22% state error, but quantizing a yields only 3.6% (the log-space pre-activation compresses it); 1% noise on β yields 0.4%.
- **Serving-stack findings (each silently corrupted a result before being caught).** The fused-GEMM scale mismatch changed paired scales by 1.82× (qkv/z) and 2.75× (b/a) in *all 48 layers*; the corrupted checkpoint scored AIME 80.8 (vs 86.7 repaired) while PPL@32K read a flat 6.86 vs the true 10.84 — better than BF16, i.e., a broken forget gate *improves* WikiText perplexity. The checkpoint-side repair (fold the ratio into per-block E4M3 scales; 94 scale sets, worst ratio 2.81×, re-rounding error ≤6.2%) was confirmed by a GEMM-level probe (kernel-vs-reference error 0.35/0.57 → 0.002). Raw-completion harnessing opened `<think>` on 25–48 of 50 MMLU-Pro questions, producing invalid scores of 66.3/58.6 (BF16/Minima) vs the valid 80.4/79.7. The composite serving path scores PPL@32K 10.04 vs 10.22 text-only for the same BF16 weights; and the base model itself exhibits context inversion (PPL 6.95 in isolated 4K windows → 10.35 inside a 32K request, deterministic).
- **KV-cache recipe.** FP8 KV (scale 1.0) moves no task score and grows capacity 1.8–1.9×, but costs PPL@32K +0.13 on BF16 vs +0.41 on Minima (3× larger — W4A4 K/V projections leave less headroom). Calibrated scales drop Minima+scales to 10.50, recovering 83% of the penalty with a residual (+0.07) *below* BF16's own uncalibrated cost (+0.13); RULER stays 100 and throughput matches within 0.4%.

## Authors & Credibility

Sergii Kozyrev and Davyd Maiboroda are researchers at mma.ai (MiniMA), the industry group that built and released the quantized checkpoint (HuggingFace: `minima-ai/mma_qwen3.8_27b_nvfp4`). This is a vendor paper about its own artifact, which cuts both ways: the authors have deep access to checkpoints, kernels, and serving internals (they even audited the Unsloth and RadixArk checkpoints), but the headline efficiency claims advertise their own release. Strong credibility signals counterbalance this: they document their *own* previously-corrupted results (the fused-scale mismatch story, §6), report where they lose (perplexity residual, decode 2–4% slower than RadixArk, kernel-level decode overhead), show per-seed scores and CI statements, verify their standalone GDN re-implementation against the reference implementation, and handle the concurrent QUASAR QAT checkpoint fairly — acknowledging they did not benchmark it and explaining why its model-card numbers are incomparable. Limitations (§9) are concrete rather than boilerplate. A two-author arXiv preprint with no external peer review; the artifact is released, which aids verification.

## Critical Assessment

The novelty is genuine: quantization of *recurrent-state mixers* in large hybrids was unstudied — the public recipes of this exact model simply exempt the GDN block — and the delta-rule-overwrite erasure mechanism is a real, falsifiable architectural insight, not a hand-wave. The inversion "the projections the community protects are precisely the safest ones" (a/b at 2.1%/2.6% output error, the two smallest of five) is the paper's crispest result and is backed by a clean sensitivity replay.

The rigor of the mechanism study is above average for an industrial report — fake-quant injection isolates exactly the 4-bit error; the lockstep-FP32 replay directly answers the accumulation question; the quadrature check (19.4% predicted vs 19.2% measured) independently validates error independence; and the synthetic-noise arm that separates "0.1% noise on α → 22% state error" from "quantizing a → 3.6%" is exactly the right control. But the evidence base is narrow in ways the title glosses over: **one model family and size (Qwen3.8-27B), one quantization format (NVFP4), one GPU**. The gate-shielding argument structurally depends on Qwen's log-space softplus/exponential parameterization — the authors themselves concede that recurrent mixers with linearly-parameterized decay may not enjoy it (and that 0.1% direct noise on α demonstrably harms the state) — so the generalization from "the recurrent half of a hybrid 27B LLM" to the operator *class* is asserted via mechanism, not demonstrated by a second model.

Two ablations are missing. First, calibration-set sensitivity: a single frozen 128-sample × 32K-token set is used, with no sweep of size or diversity, leaving the recipe's robustness unknown. Second, a weight-only W4A16 GDN variant is absent, so the reader cannot tell how much of the residual +0.72/+0.49 PPL gap comes from A4 activations versus W4 weights. The context ceiling is also real: perplexity to 32K and retrieval to 64K, with the 128K+ regime an explicit extrapolation from the flat-state-error mechanism; the ~14.5K-token AIME generations are the only longer-horizon evidence and they are capped at 32K.

The baseline situation deserves scrutiny. Unsloth and RadixArk are respected public recipes and were audited, but the concurrent QUASAR checkpoint — which also quantizes all 496 projections, GDN included, via QAT — was **not benchmarked**; the claim "training is not necessary" therefore rests on comparing the authors' PTQ numbers against QUASAR's model-card "brief two-task spot check" under a different harness, not a controlled head-to-head. Additionally, Minima+scales task scores are *inherited* from Minima rather than re-measured (only PPL, RULER, and throughput were), a justified-but-inferred equivalence. RULER saturating at 100 for all four models makes that suite uninformative, and LCB is single-seed. Finally, the decode deficit (trailing RadixArk by 2–4% despite fewer bytes) is attributed via profiling to small-batch NVFP4 activation-quantization kernel overhead without a kernel-level breakdown. The fused-scale-mismatch finding itself is excellent and SDC-adjacent — a silently corrupted model that *improves* perplexity while degrading reasoning is a benchmark-validity warning every hybrid-quantization deployment should heed — though a skeptical reader notes the audit that exonerates the competitors is the authors' own.

## Strengths

- First serious W4A4 treatment of the GDN half of a production hybrid, with large efficiency wins at zero accuracy cost: 17.53 GiB, TFPT 4.03 s, AIME'25 exactly matching BF16, RULER 100 at 64K.
- The mechanism methodology is well designed and internally cross-checked: fake-quant injection, per-projection replay, a quadrature independence check, an impulse-decay measurement that beats the decay-only explanation, and a synthetic-noise arm that localizes the true fragility (α's horizon sensitivity) and explains *why* the parameterization shields.
- The measurement-hygiene findings (§6) are independently valuable beyond quantization: fused-GEMM scale mismatch, composite-vs-text-only serving paths, invalidity of raw-completion harnesses for thinking models, and base-model context inversion — the "corrupted model fakes better perplexity" result is a cautionary tale for anyone comparing checkpoints.
- Unusually honest reporting for a vendor paper: releases the checkpoint, publishes its own corrupted intermediate results, reports losses and their causes, and states limitations concretely (including that 128K+ is extrapolation and that the gate-shielding argument is parameterization-dependent).
- Actionable, unconditional recipes: harmonize fused scales before serving; serve FP8 KV with calibrated scales (83% of the penalty recovered, throughput-free, residual below BF16's own cost).

## Limitations

- Single model family and size (Qwen3.8-27B), single quantization format (NVFP4), single GPU; the gate-shielding mechanism depends on the log-space softplus/exp parameterization and is unverified for linearly-parameterized decay mixers (e.g., other Mamba2-style gates).
- Context ceiling: 32K perplexity and 64K retrieval; 128K+ behavior is extrapolation from the bounded-error mechanism, and no longer-context stress tests were run.
- The concurrent QAT checkpoint (QUASAR) that also quantizes all 496 projections is acknowledged but not benchmarked; the "training unnecessary" claim rests on incomparable model-card numbers.
- Minima+scales task scores are inherited from Minima rather than re-measured (only PPL, RULER, throughput re-measured).
- Decode trails RadixArk by 2–4% despite fewer weight bytes, attributed to small-batch NVFP4 activation-quantization kernel overhead — reported but not fixed or decomposed.
- Benchmark discrimination is limited where it matters least: RULER saturates at 100 for all models and LiveCodeBench is single-seed; calibration-set sensitivity (one frozen 128-sample × 32K set) is unexplored.

## Relevance

这篇论文与"极限性能优化下的可靠性"方向高度契合:它首次系统回答了"低精度量化误差在长上下文循环递推中是否会发散"这一可靠性核心问题——答案是不会,delta rule 的覆写机制会主动擦除状态噪声,而门控参数化本身就是量化防护层。第 6 节的服务栈发现(校准 scale 与融合 GEMM 失配导致的静默数据损坏,坏模型反而"假装"困惑度更优)是推理侧 SDC(静默数据损坏)检测的教科书级案例,对混合架构 NVFP4 部署的测量有效性有直接工程价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the plateau-and-erasure mechanism hold for recurrent mixers with linearly-parameterized decay gates, or is it specific to log-space softplus/exp parameterizations? A cross-operator study (Mamba2 variants, other GDN parameterizations) is needed to support the class-level claim.
- What happens at 128K–1M context: does the state-error plateau persist once the window exceeds the observed decay horizons (44K–62K tokens), and does the end-to-end gap keep shrinking or eventually re-grow?
- How sensitive is the recipe to calibration-set choice (size, diversity, domain shift)? The paper freezes a single 128-sample × 32K set with no ablation.
- Would a W4A16 (weight-only) GDN variant isolate how much of the residual perplexity gap (+0.72@4K → +0.49@32K) comes from A4 activations versus W4 weights?
- Can the fused-GEMM scale mismatch be fixed kernel-side (per-input scales inside fused NVFP4 GEMMs) instead of by checkpoint rewriting, and does the same hazard affect fused attention projections (fused qkv) in non-hybrid models?
- Under one identical harness, does the concurrent QAT checkpoint (QUASAR) beat calibration-only PTQ on the honest residual (perplexity), or does the delta-rule mechanism make distillation unnecessary as the authors argue?
