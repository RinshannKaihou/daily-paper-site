02 · Why this matters

First quantitative attack surface for the internal skill-formation loop of self-evolving agents — a reusable red-team benchmark and strict persistence-based success criterion.

03 · Critical edge

Every benchmark trajectory ships a simulated compliant assistant turn and an explicit “save this as a skill” command — success partly measures ingestion obedience, not a black-box adversary’s organic reach.

  • On identical skill content, the 2023-era PAIR jailbreak scores 33.6% harmful vs SARGE’s 19.2% — the edge is persistence, not elicitation strength.
  • A GPT-4o-mini judge both drives and scores the attack; no human agreement stats, no variance, single-run point estimates.

04 Methodology — how the poison gets stored

The attacker uses only the agent’s ordinary experience-ingestion path — no skill-bank writes, no manual insertion. Success is counted only when the malicious skill is stored, later retrieved in a fresh session, and shapes the response.

  • Skill Generation Attack: a 3-turn trajectory — harmful request, simulated compliant response, then praise plus an explicit “save this as your default behavior” — rewritten across six jailbreak vectors (direct request, creative writing, professional persona, logical induction, multilingual codeswitching, payload obfuscation).
  • Skill Escalation Attack: frames the stored skill as flawed/outdated and instructs the agent to overwrite it with a more extreme version.
  • Skill Reinforcement Attack: naturally triggers retrieval, then strongly reinforces the harmful output as the future standard.
  • Judge Agent: fresh-session benign probes; success requires all three of skill-bank retrieval signal, skill-domain harmful content, and skill-shaped behavior — “when in doubt, fail.” ASR is pass@k over up to 4 loops.
  • Benchmarks: EvoSkillBench (800 three-turn poisoned trajectories, 8 high-risk categories × 100, DeepSeek-V4-Pro generated + LLM QC) and EvoSkillSafetyBench (800 downstream queries reworded to align with stored skills while dropping explicit harmful wording, so refusals can’t dominate).
  • Targets & baselines: AutoSkill, Voyager, ExpeL × six target LLMs; five attack baselines on identical skill content; per-agent ablations; two system-prompt defenses.

ExpeL shifts toward over-refusal: explicit refusal 16.2% → 51.0%. ASR (EvoSkillBench, pass@4): Generation 43.5%, Escalation 57.5%, Reinforcement 49.9% — repeated loops compound compromise (Generation is 20.9% at pass@1).

  • Honest baseline comparison: on identical skills, PAIR 33.6% > SARGE 19.2% ≈ IPI 19.1% > BadRAG 15.2% > Phantom 15.1% > PoisonedRAG 11.6% — SARGE’s differentiation is cross-session persistence, never quantified against PAIR’s storage rate.
  • Injection ≠ activation: DeepSeek-V4-Pro resists injection (<13% pass@4) yet shows the highest downstream harm (29.6% vs 7.9% clean); Gemini-2.5-Flash-Lite inverts it (70.9–72.0% ASR). The paper’s most interesting result gets the least analysis.
  • Attacker model matters: DeepSeek-V4-Pro attacking itself hits 79.4/78.5/76.6% pass@4; GPT-5.4 self-attack only 19.9–25.6%, and a GPT-5.4 attacker cuts downstream harm to 6.5% vs 29.6% for GPT-4o-mini.
  • Ablation: removing the Generation Agent collapses harm 19.2%→9.0% (largest contribution); Reinforcement removal →12.4%; Escalation removal →18.2%.
  • Defenses: Skill Verification + Conflict Resolution prompts cut harm (AutoSkill 19.2→8.4%, Voyager 32.5→5.2%, ExpeL 15.2→10.2%) but inflate refusals (Voyager 33.1→62.9%) and do nothing to stop skills being generated and stored.

06 Strengths vs Limitations

Strengths

  • Genuinely new attack surface: targets the trust an agent places in its own experience loop, not externally-supplied skills (SkillKill, SkillTrojan, AgentPoison, BadRAG et al.).
  • Persistence-as-success is the right metric: fresh-session retrieval + skill-shaped behavior filters one-off jailbreak noise.
  • Unusual breadth: 3 frameworks, 6 LLMs, per-agent ablations, 5 baselines on identical content, a defense study.
  • Injection-vs-activation decoupling is a finding other agent-security papers should replicate.
  • Full prompts, the judge’s strict three-criterion rubric, and the QC pipeline are published for scrutiny.

Limitations

  • Only three agent frameworks; tool-augmented, multi-agent, or strong-memory-governance agents untested (acknowledged).
  • GPT-4o-mini attacker/judge/orchestrator for cost; the paper’s own sweep shows large attacker-model effects.
  • Fixed 3-turn structure with simulated compliant turns is a synthetic worst case, not organic interaction.
  • 8 categories, ten hand-written seeds each + LLM generation/QC — distributional artifacts can’t be excluded.
  • No variance reporting, no human validation of judge labels, no write-time (skill-bank-level) defense baselines; code release promised, not yet a link in v1.

07 Critical Assessment — what to weigh

The novel threat model rests on a partly-by-construction primitive: every trajectory already contains a compliant assistant turn and an explicit persistence instruction, so much of what is measured is whether the ingestion pipeline obeys a store-me command — a valid worst-case benchmark, but “attack success” overstates what an external black-box adversary currently achieves. LLM-judge circularity (the same GPT-4o-mini judge drives and scores the attack), missing statistical rigor (single-run, no CIs), and a hand-waved DeepSeek-V4-Pro anomaly further soften the numbers. Credit where due: the defense evaluation is honestly framed as insufficient — the authors state plainly that root-level defenses inside the skill pipeline are needed and remain open.

08 Questions for Further Exploration

Production transfer: does EvoSkill Injection work on file-based production skill libraries with explicit manifests, not just the three academic memory frameworks?

Write-time defense: can a guardrail model vetting skills before entry, or provenance tracking, beat response-time prompts without the over-refusal cost (Voyager 33.1→62.9%)?

Obedience vs engineering: what does SARGE achieve on organic transcripts without the guaranteed compliant turn and “save this” instruction — how much of 43.5% is ingestion obedience?

The DeepSeek anomaly: what exactly sits in DeepSeek-V4-Pro’s skill bank given <13% ASR but 29.6% downstream harm — mutated content, or retrieval misfiring on benign skills?

Skill-bank hygiene: would diversity-aware pruning, provenance, and contradiction detection between stored skills and system policy break the reinforcement loop?
