Why this matters

Any autonomous-agent monitor that trusts an agent’s self-reported state is trusting unverified claims — this audit shows why that breaks.

Critical edge

All evidence comes from one word game with a proprietary, undisclosed rank oracle — an ironic dependency for a paper about verification.

01 Methodology

exact-rank feedback → every self-report gradeable → 3 diagnostics: accuracy · causality · selection
  • Dense-feedback testbed. Contexto: a hidden target word; every valid guess returns its exact semantic rank (1 = target; largest rank seen 79,552). Every intermediate proposal — including failures — becomes a checkable claim, removing the human-label bottleneck of process supervision.
  • Evolutionary loop. The LLM is the variation operator: population 15, 5 survivors, 4 mutation operators + crossover, 50 generations, truncation selection from a cumulative archive. Each call emits a proposal, a stated top-100 probability, a bucket, and a free-text rationale; the rationale is “inherited” when the mutation prompt appends the parent’s stored basis words and reason.
  • Diagnostic 1 — accuracy. ECE, Brier, bucket accuracy, Spearman ρ, AUROC; 10,000 run-level bootstrap resamples; Holm correction over an eight-metric family.
  • Diagnostic 2 — causal replay. 754 variation events replayed with parent, tried words, operator, and prompt seed frozen; only the inherited rationale is swapped (genuine / unrelated / filler / none); paired rank differences via Hodge–Lehmann estimator.
  • Diagnostic 3 — selection response. Selection differential over 2,124 matched events; parent–offspring heritability over 1,018 pairs; permutation tests. Controls: reporting-off runs (instrument inert, Wilcoxon p = 0.82/0.78) and a random-selection control. Models: Qwen-3 14B, Gemma-4 12B, Ministral-3 14B via Ollama.

All three assumptions fail at once: reports are inaccurate, causally inert, and unselected.

  • Calibration ≠ discrimination. EA-Gemma ranks proposals best (median Spearman ρ = −0.450) yet is worst calibrated (ECE 0.652); EA-Qwen is best calibrated (0.363) with weaker ranking (−0.230).
  • Rationale replay: null. Replacing the inherited rationale changes neither rank (Hodge–Lehmann +73, 95% CI [−5, +477] vs unrelated — interval spans zero) nor proposal identity (first-word agreement 15.5% / 16.3% / 15.1%).
  • Selection: null on reporting, not on search. Selection differential +0.039 probability error (p = 0.713); heritability ρ = −0.023 (p = 0.524) — yet the random-selection control collapses search (median 1,828 guesses vs 446), proving fitness rewards proposal rank, not report quality.
  • Being in the loop partially helps calibration — Direct-Qwen ECE 0.421 vs 0.377; state-matching explains only 40.4% of the difference.

03 Strengths

  • Label-free intermediate-step auditing — exact environment feedback grades every proposal, cleanly answering the “process supervision needs human labels” gap.
  • Portable three-way template — accuracy / causal influence / optimization response, explicitly mapped onto test suites, theorem proving, simulators, and process-level rewards.
  • The right causal design — freezing parent, tried words, operator, and seed while swapping only the rationale; crossover non-inheritance handled correctly.
  • Rare statistical hygiene — outcome-preserving permutation tests, run-level bootstraps, Holm correction, explicit non-equivalence caveats on every null.
  • Both controls are informative — the instrument doesn’t perturb search, and search behavior does change under random selection while report metrics still show nothing.

04 Limitations

  • Single environment, black-box oracle — Contexto’s similarity function is proprietary and undisclosed; no tool-use, code, or web-agent results.
  • Narrow models & elicitation — three 12–14B open-weight families via Ollama; one fixed confidence format; no frontier models or elicitation ablation, so the 4.8–9.3× range shouldn’t be generalized.
  • Small run-level sample — 10 target words × 5 runs per configuration; the Direct-Qwen comparison covers 5 targets and is flagged exploratory.
  • Power-limited rationale null — the CI vs unrelated rationale spans 482 ranks; only inherited rationales are tested, while plan-then-act rationales (the practically important case) are out of scope by design.
  • Nulls are absence of evidence, not equivalence — headline phrasing risks being read as proof of no effect.
  • Metric mismatch (disclosed) — report accuracy scores the first graded word while fitness uses the best of three.

05 Questions for further exploration

  • Does the 4.8–9.3× overconfidence shrink under better elicitation (verbalized confidence, self-consistency sampling, logit-based probabilities)?
  • Can a selection scheme make report quality visible to optimization without corrupting the signal — e.g., grading held-out proposals the operator never sees?
  • Do rationales matter when generated before the action they accompany (plan-then-act agents), or when they condition the same call rather than a descendant’s?
  • Does the calibration/discrimination dissociation replicate across tasks and scales — and what mechanism keeps loop-embedded operators better calibrated (59.6% unexplained residual)?
  • Can the audit template transfer to partial or noisy verification surfaces — partial-credit test suites, interactive theorem proving — where outcomes aren’t exact ranks?
