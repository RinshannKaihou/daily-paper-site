Critical Edge

The headline failure claims rest on single runs of a single model (Claude Opus 4.7 only) — systematic collapse cannot yet be separated from run-to-run variance, and rule discovery is author-judged from free-text notebooks with no blinded rubric.

01Methodology

  • Framework. A sandbox couples an autonomous agent to a sealed oracle over M rounds: propose specimens → assay → (possibly scalar-only) report → record interpretation in an append-only notebook.md → choose next experiment. Oracle regimes: wet (real lab), damp (surrogate trained on empirical data), dry (invented rules known only to designers).
  • MPRAbox. Submit a 50,000-sequence 200-bp enhancer library that best trains a generalizable sequence-to-activity model. Damp pipeline: score with Malinois (CNN, 776,474 measured sequences), train a fresh same-architecture model from scratch, evaluate on 14 hidden test sets from 9 source distributions (chromosome-level holdouts enforced), return only the 14 Pearson r values.
  • Baselines & regimes. 14 human-curated sampling strategies × 5 seeds; size sweeps 10k–300k. One-shot: Claude Opus 4.7, GPT-5.5, Gemini 3.5 Flash in native harnesses, 5 replicates each, with/without prior knowledge of human scores. Long-horizon: M=30 rounds, Claude only, 4 runs.
  • Dry oracles. 14 invented rules (GC balance, English-word cipher, Fibonacci, Game of Life, Collatz, parity…) replace the labeler; each rule run under 3 framings (MPRA-framed / unframed / symbolic {0,1,2,3}) for 30 rounds; discovery assessed from stated hypotheses vs ground truth.
  • CodonBox. 8 invented genetic worlds: alphabet j∈{4,6,8}, codon length k∈{2,3,4}, hidden codon tables, Dill 2D HP folding; fitness = non-consecutive H-H contacts in the optimal fold (ceiling 9). Claude played 500 rounds per world, told nothing about codons, translation, or folding.

  • Every one of Claude’s five libraries met or exceeded the mean of the best human-designed strategy (0.763); GPT and Gemini never did. Informed agents improved — most the weakest ones (GPT +10.5 pts) — yet none simply copied the best human strategy; notebooks show they inferred why strategies worked and modified them.
  • Multi-round (M=30): all 4 runs beat the best human strategy; agents spontaneously converged on one-variable-at-a-time experimentation after a confounded experiment (cCRE + chicken changed together) produced a wrong conclusion.
  • Dry oracles — optimization without understanding: on the substring rule the agent reached ~0.65 vs ~0.4 random, but found the true rule in only 1 of 14 cases (GC balance, symbolic framing only); for Fibonacci it settled on a dinucleotide-statistics proxy — wrong rule, sufficient feedback fit.
  • CodonBox capability cliff: max fitness (9 contacts) reached within 10 rounds in every run; codon structure recovered for j=4 (k=2–4) and j=8, k=3 (512 codons) but never for j=6 (216 codons) — an elaborate wrong theory around individual nucleotides instead.
  • What predicts success: tool calls (r=0.55, p=0.0016) and shell commands (r=0.48), but not input tokens (r=0.12, p=0.538) — more discrete experiments, not more context.

03Strengths

  • Conceptually clean, genuinely novel separation — “can the agent raise the score” vs “did the agent learn the rules” — exactly what fixed benchmarks (PaperBench, BixBench, LAB-Bench) cannot measure.
  • The wet/damp/dry oracle taxonomy is a useful design vocabulary spanning empirical verifiability.
  • Lab-notebook protocol yields unusually rich evidence: verbatim hypothesis revisions, a self-caught confounded experiment, quantified reusable failure modes.
  • Expert-built infrastructure: chromosome-level holdouts, 14 hidden sets, sealed oracle, deterministic invented worlds with pre-enumerated ground truth.
  • Honest negative results lead the paper; all code and agent-facing files released.

04Limitations

  • Every deep-dive regime (30-round, 14 dry oracles, 8 CodonBox worlds) uses only Claude Opus 4.7, one run per condition — findings may be model- and run-specific.
  • Small replicate counts throughout, no significance tests or confidence intervals on headline correlations.
  • Damp-oracle circularity: Malinois supplies training labels and labels 9 of 14 evaluation sets, and donates the downstream architecture — inflating apparent library quality vs purely empirical evaluation.
  • High composition-driven floor (~0.721 for random DNA) compresses the dynamic range the headline gap spans.
  • No wet-lab instantiation and no iterative human baseline (only static expert-designed sampling rules); the “human baseline” is not human experimentalists.
  • Rule discovery is author-judged qualitatively — no automated judge, rubric, or inter-rater reliability.

05Questions for Further Exploration

  • Q1Does the optimization-vs-understanding gap replicate on GPT-5.5 or Gemini 3.5 Flash, or is rule-discovery collapse a Claude-harness-specific artifact?
  • Q2How much of the 0.774-vs-0.763 one-shot edge survives on the 5 empirically-labeled evaluation sets alone, removing Malinois circularity?
  • Q3Would an explicit scaffold enforcing structured exploration close the dry-oracle discovery gap without revealing the rule class?
  • Q4Can automated AI judges score lab notebooks reliably enough to scale sandboxes to hundreds of agent architectures (the authors’ proposed BroadBox)?
  • Q5Is the j=6-fails-but-j=8-succeeds non-monotonicity a property of in-context theory building — would tool-augmented design-of-experiments fix it?
  • Q6What CodonBox-like worlds with hard optimization surfaces would let researchers measure optimization and understanding simultaneously, instead of saturating fitness in 10 rounds?
