> **一句话总结：** 提出 MetaRSI-v1，把递归自改进（RSI）重构为数据/脚手架/模型三个"算子"在单一循环内核上的调度组合：RSI² Agent 沿"横向扩展序列 / 纵向改写算子策略"两轴调度，MetaRSI² Agent 再每轮改写调度器本身；在等预算、密封评测下，它让 Qwen3.5-35B-A3B 在 Terminal-Bench 2.1 / SWE-bench Pro / GPQA-D-hard100 / AIME 四个基准上平均自提升 +10.9 分（如 SWE-bench Pro 10.3→19.5），比最强固定流水线再高 3.6 分，且六个前沿模型仅凭 Harness 路线就自提升 +5.6~+9.2（均值 +7.3）。

> **TL;DR:** MetaRSI-v1 recasts recursive self-improvement as the *scheduled composition* of three disjoint-write-surface operators — Data-RSI (synthesize verified training records), Harness-RSI (edit a five-slot prompt/memory/tools/skills/MCP scaffold genome), Model-RSI (LoRA-internalize capability) — all instantiating one loop kernel, with an RSI² agent choosing between extending the operator sequence (horizontal) and rewriting an operator's proposal policy (vertical), and a MetaRSI² agent revising the scheduler once per improvement term. Under matched budgets on sealed splits, it lifts Qwen3.5-35B-A3B by **+10.9 avg points** across Terminal-Bench 2.1, SWE-bench Pro, GPQA-D-hard100 and AIME (SWE-bench Pro 10.3→19.5, AIME 55.0→68.3), beating the best single operator (Harness-RSI, +6.6) and the best fixed composition (static router / human-fixed D→M→H, both +7.3) by **+3.6 points from scheduling alone**; six frontier models (Gemini 3.1 Pro +9.2 … GLM-5.2 +5.6, mean **+7.3**) improve themselves on Terminal-Bench 2.1 through the harness route with no weight access. A 45-system census plus five falsifiable "laws" argue the RSI frontier is set by verification format, not capability.

## 核心贡献

- **一个 45 系统的 RSI 普查 + 形式约束诊断**：对 2022–2026.08 的 45 个自改进系统分类，69%（31 个）的改进闭环都闭合在机器可判定目标（测试套件、精确匹配、数值键、选择题标签）上；55 个已闭合循环中 53 个落在验证器阶梯（verifier ladder）的 1–3 级。循环数与验证器等级的 Spearman ρ=−0.75（p<0.001），控制能力后仍显著（偏相关 −0.67, p=0.005），而控制验证等级后能力偏相关 +0.45（p=0.08, 不显著）——**决定一个域能否形成自改进循环的是验证格式而非模型能力**。
- **统一范式：一个内核、三个算子**。目标系统写成 S=(D, θ, H)（数据态/模型态/脚手架态）；三个算子写面互不相交：Data-RSI 从执行轨迹合成"经盲推导对抗验证"的训练记录并标记能力边界（4 维学习签名 κ：knowledge/reasoning/verification/distractor）；Harness-RSI 用类型化补丁编辑五槽脚手架基因组；Model-RSI 以有界 LoRA recipe 把能力内化进权重。信号新鲜度（freshness）条件严格推出 7 条合法转移（禁 H→M、M→M），每条边配一个类型化 Transition Agent 适配器。
- **两层调度 + 元层**：RSI² Agent 每步在"横向（扩展算子序列）"与"纵向（经四项确定性检查改写某算子提案策略）"间选择；MetaRSI² Agent 在每个 improvement term 结束后依据完整轨迹改写调度器策略——形成"算子改系统 → 编排改算子用法 → 元层改编排"三层嵌套。
- **密封评测 + 全闭环实验**：所有模型驱动角色（提出、合成、评判、调度）均由被测模型自身担任（无外部教师）；预算（token、GPU 时、候选数、验证器查询）逐项对齐，密封评测器/任务集/发布规则在所有写面之外。等预算下 MetaRSI-v1 四基准平均 +10.9 分，调度本身值 +3.6 分；五轮 term 复合实验显示元层使单轮增益从 +2.5 升到 +3.8、到达 +20 分所需轮数从 4.0 降到 2.5。
- **五条可证伪"定律"与立场**：验证而非能力决定前沿；自我描述随系统改变而过期（重描述是速率上限）；能力跨基底不变但其成本不变（单基底循环成本单调累积）；信任由"不可写之物"度量（密封评测器不可写）；循环不创造能力、每个增益都是输入的。立场：进步的单位是循环而非模型。开源 RSI-Harness 运行时与基因组格式。

## Key Contributions

- **A 45-system census with a format-bound diagnosis**: 69% of surveyed RSI systems (31/45) close their loop on a machine-checkable target; across 55 closed loops over 22 domains, loop count tracks the verifier rung (Spearman ρ=−0.75, p<0.001; partial r=−0.67, p=0.005 controlling for capability) while capability is non-significant once rung is controlled (+0.45, p=0.08). Five "standing-loss" domains sit at capability 67.8–80.8 with zero loops; practice-vs-recall gaps span 35–83 points on six paired benchmarks.
- **One kernel, three operators**: the target is a triple S=(D, θ, H) and each operator writes exactly one component — Data-RSI (adversarially verified record synthesis with blind re-derivation and 4-dim learning signatures κ), Harness-RSI (typed patches over a five-slot genome: system prompt, memory, built-in tools, skills, MCP), Model-RSI (bounded LoRA recipes trained from a fixed base over a cumulative dataset). A single signal-freshness condition admits 5 of 6 cross-operator transitions (forbidding H→M and M→M), each realized by a typed Transition Agent-v1 adapter.
- **Two-axis scheduling plus a meta layer**: the RSI² Agent-v1 chooses horizontal composition (typed improvement programs restricted to admissible transitions) or vertical optimization (update-or-skip directives to per-operator Sub-Agents under a four-check acceptance contract, including declared-modification equality); the MetaRSI² Agent-v1 rewrites the scheduler's routing/budget/stopping policy once per term. Sealed evaluator, task set, release rule, and ledger lie outside every write surface at every level — no component ever evaluates its own output.
- **Fully closed-loop, budget-matched evaluation**: every model-driven role is played by the target model (no external teacher). With identical token/GPU-hour/candidate/verifier-query budgets and mean over five seeds, MetaRSI-v1 gains +10.9 avg points on four sealed benchmarks; the meta layer compounds: +26.2 vs +20.7 cumulative over five terms, gain per term 3.8 vs 2.5, and terms-to-+20 of 2.5 vs 4.0.
- **Five falsifiable laws and a position**: verification (not capability) sets the frontier; self-knowledge expires so re-description is the rate limit; competence is substrate-free but its cost is not; trust is measured by what cannot be written; no loop creates capability — every gain is imported. The harness runtime and genome format are open-sourced as RSI-Harness.

## Methodology

**Target system and kernel.** A deployed system is the triple S = (D, θ, H): data state, model state, and harness state (system prompt, bounded memory, built-in tools, skills, MCP-mounted tools). Every improvement operator instantiates one loop kernel K = OBSERVE→DIAGNOSE→PROPOSE→VALIDATE→EXECUTE→SELECT→EXPORT: the model proposes on the mutable arc (DIAGNOSE/PROPOSE), deterministic code adjudicates everywhere else, and exactly one authority boundary separates model-driven proposals from code-enforced validation. The learning signal σ = Σ(E) is a deterministic compilation of an evidence set (verifier outcomes, own trajectories, admitted knowledge, instrument readings); every capability-altering step invalidates and recompiles σ (cyclic closure). Failure signatures φ(τ) = (λ terminal cause, ψ canonical code, μ model-attributed mechanism) share one vocabulary across operators so a failure can be routed to harness, data, or weights.

**Three operators.** *Data-RSI* writes D: it groups failed rollouts by outcome and domain, distills Experience artifacts, emits directives (QueryGen), adversarially generates candidate records (an Operator role authors; an Anchor role blindly re-derives under input isolation — certified by self-consistency, not correctness), then gates each record with a deterministic contract check ∧ a semantic re-solve check; records are deduplicated, screened against the sealed set, and split train/validation. Its four-dimensional learning signature κ(e) = (knowledge, reasoning, verification, distractor) steers synthesis and marks the boundary of what the rollouts support. *Harness-RSI* writes H: the genome H = (ι system prompt, M memory, O tools, S skills, T MCP) is edited via typed HarnessPatches (repair hypothesis + slot operations + expected effect + risk); candidates are replayed per failure-signature shard, combined under a hard complexity budget, and promoted only on strict improvement over the historical best. *Model-RSI* writes θ: bounded recipes R = (arch, adapt, optim, seq, ckpt) over LoRA; every candidate trains from the same fixed base checkpoint over the cumulative dataset (never continuing a prior adapter), so gains stay attributable; promotion requires strict improvement on the sealed adaptation set. Cost asymmetry is explicit: Harness edits are re-paid as context at every inference (Δ_ctx(H)>0), internalization is paid once (Δ_ctx(M)=0).

**Composition and scheduling.** A capability-altering step recompiles the signal; Principle 4.4 (admissibility = all consumed artifacts fresh) yields five admissible cross-transitions (D→H, D→M, H→D, M→D, M→H) plus self-transitions D→D and H→H, forbidding H→M and M→M (a harness step produces no data, so a dataset predating the change trains on superseded behavior). Each edge is realized by a Transition Agent-v1 adapter (Experience Extraction, Dataset Materialization, Signal Recompilation ×2, Redundancy Reconciliation — the last deletes scaffold entries whose behavior new weights internalized). The RSI² Agent-v1 schedules on two axes: *horizontally*, drawing an improvement program (ordered operator block with per-step evidence and budget shares) restricted to admissible transitions; *vertically*, issuing per-operator update-or-skip directives routed to dedicated Sub-Agents, whose revised policies are installed only if four deterministic checks pass (identity, declared-surface containment, declared-modification equality, stop condition). Above both, after each term (initialization → routed steps → stop intent → release gate), the MetaRSI² Agent-v1 reads the whole trace and rewrites the scheduler's contract (routing preferences, budget/stopping policy, adapter guidance) under Equation 28. Three separations protect the objective: proposal/validation/release split, candidates generated vs released, and the sealed evaluator Q*, sealed task set, release rule, and cost ledger outside every write surface including the meta layer.

**Experimental setup.** Two target lines: (1) self-hosted Qwen3.5-35B-A3B (MoE, 35B total / 3B active, LoRA on the Model-RSI surface, 64k context) with all three operators; (2) six frontier models via API, harness route only (admissible set collapses to D↔H). Benchmarks: Terminal-Bench 2.1 (89 tasks), SWE-bench Pro (731-task public split, pinned commit), GPQA-D-hard100 (100 frozen harder GPQA-Diamond items held out from synthesis and training, with official Record IDs published), merged AIME (2025+2026 I/II, 60 problems). All runs use one reference execution harness (open-sourced as RSI-Harness), pass@1 exact-match/numeric under fixed decoding, over-budget responses scored as failure, five independent outer seeds, and identical budgets across conditions (No improvement / single operators at full budget / human-fixed D→M→H / random admissible composition / static router / MetaRSI-v1). The primary metric, deployed improvement productivity, is scored on the released successor (not the best archived candidate) on the sealed split. For Section 5.4, term 2 forks: the same S₁ is improved either by the original improver or by the meta-updated one under the same budget.

## Results & Highlights

- **Main result (Table 3, sealed splits, equal budget, Qwen3.5-35B-A3B):** MetaRSI-v1 beats the frozen system on all four benchmarks — Terminal-Bench 2.1 **23.6→31.9 (+8.3)**, SWE-bench Pro **10.3→19.5 (+9.2, resolve rate nearly doubles)**, GPQA-D-hard100 **71.2→83.8 (+12.6)**, AIME **55.0→68.3 (+13.3)** — for a **+10.9 average**. The best single operator is Harness-RSI at +6.6 average (Data-RSI +2.8, Model-RSI +3.9), and the best fixed compositions — human-fixed D→M→H and the static router — both reach +7.3, so **scheduling alone is worth +3.6 over the strongest fixed-order composition** (random admissible order: +4.5). Averaged by format: +8.8 on executable suites, +13.0 on closed-form suites.
- **Frontier models improve themselves via the harness route (Figure 10, Terminal-Bench 2.1, no weight access, each model its own proposer/synthesizer/judge):** all six gain — GLM-5.2 **+5.6 (→84.0)**, GPT-5.6 Sol **+6.7 (→90.1)**, Kimi K3 **+7.2 (→91.2)**, Claude Opus 5 **+7.6 (→92.1)**, DeepSeek V4 Pro **+7.6 (→91.2)**, Gemini 3.1 Pro **+9.2 (→89.6)**; **mean +7.3**, comparable to +8.3 for the 35B target on the same benchmark — evidence the harness route is not merely repairing small-model deficits (baselines are already 83–92 for four of the six).
- **The meta layer makes the successor a better improver (Figure 11, Table 4):** over five consecutive terms the meta path reaches **+26.2 cumulative vs +20.7 without**; gain per term (terms 2–5) is **3.8 vs 2.5**; terms to reach +20 average: **2.5 vs 4.0**; plateau onset moves from term 4 to term 5; scheduler regret (shortfall vs the best admissible sequence in hindsight) halves **16.5%→8.2%**; 68% vs 38% of operator-policy edits carry into the next term; and the meta path shows **no regression** while the non-meta path loses 0.2 on GPQA-h100 at term 5. In the term-2 fork, the meta-updated improver adds **7.2 vs 4.9 (+2.3)** under one budget on one system, with the advantage scaling with benchmark headroom (+3.3 AIME, +2.6 GPQA-D-hard100, +1.7 SWE-bench Pro, +1.6 Terminal-Bench 2.1).
- **Frontier compounding (Figure 12):** three terms of the harness route on all six frontier models yield per-term means **+7.3, +2.8, +1.2 (+11.3 cumulative)**; retention of term-1 gain in term 2 anti-correlates with baseline strength (two lowest baselines keep 43% and 63%, the four above keep 26–39%; rank correlation ρ=−0.84) — the rate is set by headroom, not model strength.
- **Census (Figures 4–5, Appendix A):** of 45 systems (2022–Aug 2026), **31 (69%) close machine-checkable** vs 14 open; **55 closed loops over 22 domains, 53 at verifier rungs 1–3**; loop count vs rung **Spearman ρ=−0.75 (p<0.001)**, holding at **r=−0.67 (p=0.005)** when controlling for capability, while the capability partial is **+0.45 (p=0.08, n.s.)** — the two are not interchangeable. Five rung-5 domains above the 60-point capability threshold have **zero loops** (economics 80.8, health/clinical 72.1, philosophy 70.1, history 70.1, law 67.8); on six recall-vs-practice benchmark pairs the practice end sits **35–83 points below** the recall end. The census doubled in Aug 2026 (22→55 loops) and none of the 33 new loops landed at rung 4–5.

## Authors & Credibility

A large (~33-author) industrial–academic consortium led by OsmosMind (a company technical report) with authors from Tsinghua, Peking, Stanford, Berkeley, MIT, UCLA, Yale, CMU, Imperial College London, NUS, HKU, CUHK, SJTU, USTC, Renmin University and AgentAlpha; corresponding author Guancheng Wan. The breadth is consistent with an infrastructure-heavy effort: the paper ships a substantial census (45 systems, per-system table in Appendix A), a formal framework with numbered definitions/principles, and an open-sourced artifact (RSI-Harness runtime + genome format + the genomes from their runs, with Hugging Face release). The evaluation hygiene is well above genre norms (sealed held-out splits with published GPQA Record IDs, budget ledgers, five seeds, over-budget=failure accounting, fixed decoding). Caveats: the corresponding/first authors are not (as far as the byline shows) established names in RSI research, the report has a company brand throughout, and several cited "2026" works are arXiv preprints — the community vetting layer is thin, so claims should rest on the paper's own controls rather than citation prestige.

## Critical Assessment

- **All baselines are internal recombinations of the authors' own operators.** The headline "scheduling is worth +3.6" compares against random order, a static router, and a human-fixed D→M→H over *their* operator set — there is no equal-budget comparison against any external self-improving system (STOP, Darwin Gödel Machine, Self-Harness, AlphaEvolve-style harness evolution, or GEPA-style prompt optimizers) on these benchmarks. The claim that MetaRSI-v1 advances the state of the art of RSI is therefore not directly established, only that scheduled composition beats ablations of itself.
- **The experiments remain inside the very format bound the paper criticizes.** The four benchmarks are all machine-checkable, the adaptation sets are drawn from the same benchmark families, and the loop is scored on sealed splits *of the same format* — by the paper's own Law 1, this certifies benchmark-bound capability, not "every domain" as the subtitle promises. Section 6.5 (wet-lab, robotics, materials) is explicitly a projection; zero physical-world experiments are run. The title's "Every Domain" is aspiration, not result.
- **The +10.9 headline rides on a low frozen baseline.** Qwen3.5-35B-A3B starts at 10.3 on SWE-bench Pro and 23.6 on Terminal-Bench 2.1, and the paper's own retention analysis (ρ=−0.84: gains and retention anti-correlate with baseline strength) concedes that headroom, not model strength, sets the rate. A frozen frontier model leaves far less room for +10.9; the frontier route (harness-only, no Model-RSI, no Data-RSI weight internalization) shows a smaller mean (+7.3) against the authors' *own* reference harness rather than vendor-recommended serving stacks.
- **No dispersion statistics.** Every number in Table 3 is "the mean over five independent outer seeds," but no standard deviations, CIs, or per-seed ranges are reported; the +3.6 scheduling delta over the static router, and the +2.3 term-2 meta advantage, are single-mean differences whose seed-level variability is invisible.
- **Census labels are author judgment.** The closed-vs-open classification and verifier-rung assignment (Appendix A) are hand labels by the same team whose thesis depends on them; several illustrative pairs in Figure 5(e) compare endpoints achieved by *different* models on different leaderboards (only the MLE-bench Lite/High pair is within-system), and the humanities practice endpoint is o3-mini (high) from the 2025 HLE paper — a dated number the authors flag but keep plotted.
- **Genuine strengths worth crediting:** the sealed-split protocol with published GPQA Record IDs (Table 11), the four acceptance checks on policy rewrites (declared-modification equality is a neat anti-smuggling device), the fixed-base cumulative training rule that keeps gains attributable, the honest "an amplifier is not a source" discussion (Law 5 is stated as a closed-amplifier upper bound on their own system), and explicit refutation conditions for each law and for the position statement. This is unusually falsifiable writing for a tech report.

## Strengths

- Formalizes self-improvement at the right granularity: disjoint write surfaces (data / harness / model) with typed artifacts and adapters make operator composition analyzable, and the freshness principle yields a crisp, checkable transition algebra (5+2 admissible edges) instead of ad-hoc pipelines.
- Best-in-genre evaluation hygiene: budget-matched conditions across tokens, GPU-hours, candidates and verifier queries; sealed held-out splits opened once after freezing; over-budget scored as failure; released successor (not best candidate) as the scored object; published benchmark item indices.
- The meta layer is evaluated the right way — a controlled fork (same S₁, same budget, improver the only difference) rather than an end-to-end anecdote, and its mechanism is decomposed into policy carryover (38%→68%), scheduler regret (16.5%→8.2%), and regression elimination.
- The census converts an anecdote-heavy literature into measurable structure (loop count vs verifier rung vs capability, with partial correlations and robustness-to-doubling checks), and the five laws come with stated refutation conditions.
- Practical release: RSI-Harness as a standalone, patch-format-compatible runtime with contributed-genome support makes the harness route reproducible by others without weights or training infrastructure.

## Limitations

- Only one open-weight target family (Qwen3.5-35B-A3B) exercises the full three-operator loop; the model route's generality across model scales/families is untested, and frontier evaluation is harness-only.
- Improvement horizon is five terms with visible decay (per-term gain falls to +1.4 with meta-update); long-run behavior, catastrophic drift, and scaffold/weight interaction over tens of terms are unmeasured.
- No external SOTA baselines, no variance reporting, and no ablations over individual kernel stages (e.g., removing the Anchor blind-re-derivation gate or the declared-modification check) — the cost of each safety mechanism is unquantified.
- Benchmarks are four machine-checkable suites; transfer to the open-ended domains the paper argues matter (rung 4–5) is asserted, not demonstrated. "Every Domain" in the title is unsupported by any experiment.
- The learning-signature taxonomy (κ dimensions, failure-signature vocabularies in Appendix D) is hand-designed per track; misrouting of failures by the fixed priority order is possible and unquantified.

## Relevance

与你排名第一的兴趣方向（Self-Evolving Agents & Automated Research）完全对口：这篇工作把自进化 agent 的"改 prompt / 改数据 / 改权重 / 改编排"统一成带类型与权限边界的算子组合，其密封评测器协议、预算台账与"无人组件能评自己的输出"的分离设计，是构建和审计长时间自治研究循环的直接参考；45 系统普查与五条定律也可作为该领域的定位地图。若你在做自动化研究 agent 的可信性与可控性，Law 4（信任由不可写之物度量）与附录的验收检查设计尤其值得细读。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the +3.6 scheduling advantage survive against an external, tuned self-improvement baseline (e.g., a Darwin-Gödel-Machine-style code rewrite loop or GEPA) at equal budget on the same sealed splits?
- How much of the +10.9 on Qwen3.5-35B-A3B persists when the frozen baseline is a frontier-grade open model (e.g., >80 Terminal-Bench) where headroom is small — and does the meta layer still pay for its separate budget?
- Can the Data-RSI learning signature κ reliably allocate the "imported knowledge" budget (Law 5) in a rung-3 domain (ML replication), where the paper claims its boundary statement is most valuable but reports no experiment?
- What is the per-mechanism cost of the safety machinery — Anchor blind re-derivation, the three record gates, and declared-modification equality — in terms of rejected-but-correct proposals and term time?
- Does the five-slot genome transfer across task families as claimed (the shared-entries test the authors propose for RSI-Harness), or do genomes overfit their benchmark's hidden verifier?
