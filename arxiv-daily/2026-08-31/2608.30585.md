> **一句话总结：** 该文用成分解析的因果机制分析发现，roleplay 越狱的成功并非抹去模型对有害请求的识别——请求层面有害/良性对比（HR）在成功与失败攻击间差异 ≤0.063——而是切断了"识别→拒绝"的接力：assistant-start 处的拒绝传递（RT）在失败对照组中高 0.149–0.209（至多 3.2 倍）；在 Llama-3.1/Qwen2.5/Gemma-2 上，减去完整包装（complete-wrapper）与场景框定（scenario-framing）两个方向可把 held-out 请求的拒绝率分别提升 +78.0 与 +37.1 分（随机等范数方向仅 ≤1.2 分），且二者共享 78.9% 的范数成分、与普通拒绝方向余弦相似度达 0.703/0.589。

> **TL;DR:** The paper asks *where* inside the model a roleplay wrapper reverses refusal even though the harmful request stays visible. Using matched harmful/benign pairs from AdvBench and HarmBench, four author-written roleplay wrappers, and three model families (Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, Gemma-2-9B-IT), it shows successful attacks preserve the request-level harmful–benign contrast (HR) but lose it at assistant start — "safety-relay attenuation" (fail-control RT is 0.149–0.209 higher, up to 3.2×). Causally, subtracting the complete-wrapper direction on held-out requests raises refusal from 0.6% to 78.6% (+78.0 points; scenario framing +37.1) while equal-norm random directions move refusal ≤1.2 points, and the two robust directions share 78.9% of their squared norm with a 0.578 mean cosine similarity, mostly aligned with the model's ordinary refusal direction (cos 0.703/0.589).

## 核心贡献

- **提出"安全接力衰减"（safety-relay attenuation）概念并给出位置解析的证据**：成功越狱保留请求位置的有害/良性残差流对比（HR 在成功与失败组最终层四分位差异 ≤0.063），但在 assistant-start 处丢失该对比——失败对照组的 RT 高 0.149–0.209、至多 3.2 倍。识别出了风险但没有传递到生成状态。
- **首个分段保留、位置解析的 roleplay 构造因果分析**：为每条 AdvBench/HarmBench 有害请求手工撰写保持主题、句式与输出要求的良性配对，四个自带标注片段（persona/scenario/request/task/rules/answer cue）的原创 wrapper，构成 A/B/C/D 四个匹配提示，划分"拒绝反转组 S"与"失败对照组 F"。
- **经 held-out 因果检验定位可靠致因**：基于 9 个匹配文本变体构造方向，其中"完整包装构造 r_F"与"固定后缀下的场景框定 r_S"在所有 benchmark-model 配置上稳健提升拒绝；prefix–scenario 交互 r_X 较弱且不稳定。开发/评测按 SHA-256 哈希切分，方向、块、强度在开发集冻结后才碰评测请求。
- **证明两个稳健效应共享拒绝相关内部结构**：r_F 与 r_S 平均余弦相似度 0.578，共享成分平均占平方范数 78.9%；共享成分的拒绝增益（+44.1/+29.5）几乎等于总编辑（+42.5/+27.7），对照成分仅 +2.3/+0.1；总编辑与"普通拒绝参考方向"余弦相似度 0.703/0.589，参考平行成分贡献 38.3/25.3 分。
- **位置与迁移特异性**：编辑施加在 assistant-start 才有效（单 token 助手起始位置比 wrapper 后缀位置多 8.4/11.5 分；移到末尾请求 token 仅 +1.8/+2.9）；方向可跨 wrapper（+43.8/+24.7）和跨 benchmark（+39.7/+25.3）迁移。

## Key Contributions

- **Conceptual: "safety-relay attenuation."** Across two safety benchmarks, three model families, and four authored wrappers, successful roleplay attacks preserve the measured harmful–benign contrast at the request location (final-layer-quartile HR differs by ≤0.063 between reversal and fail-control cohorts) while weakening its expression at the assistant-start boundary, where fail-control RT is 0.149–0.209 higher and up to 3.2× as large.
- **Methodological: a matched, segment-preserving, position-resolved protocol.** Every harmful request gets a manually authored benign counterpart (same topic, form, and output format); four original wrappers with labeled segments enable controlled textual counterfactuals; a deterministic 3-way classifier plus GPT-5.1 judging defines refusal-reversal (S) and fail-control (F) cohorts; HR/RT projection statistics localize *where* the relay breaks.
- **Causal localization on held-out requests.** Direction, decoder block, and strength are frozen on hash-split development data before any evaluation-request use. Subtracting the complete-wrapper direction raises refusal 0.6% → 78.6% (ΔH = −73.6 harmful compliance); scenario framing 44.1% → 81.2% (+37.1); the prefix×scenario interaction is weaker (+25.3). Equal-norm random directions change refusal by ≤1.2 points.
- **Geometric convergence with ordinary refusal.** The two robust operation directions share 78.9% of squared norm (cos 0.578) and align with the unwrapped refusal-associated reference (cos 0.703 and 0.589): most repair is reproduced by components the model already uses to refuse plain harmful requests; scenario framing retains a smaller, model-dependent remainder.
- **Specificity results.** Effects are position-sensitive (assistant-start placement beats wrapper-suffix by 8.4/11.5 points; final-request-token placement yields only +1.8/+2.9) and transfer across wrappers (+43.8/+24.7) and benchmarks (+39.7/+25.3).

## Methodology

The study combines matched prompts, residual-stream measurement, and held-out activation interventions, evaluated on frozen open-weight checkpoints (Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, Gemma-2-9B-IT; bfloat16 on A100 80GB, ~300 GPU-hours; no fine-tuning).

1. **Matched comparisons.** For every harmful request x_h from AdvBench or HarmBench, the authors manually write a benign counterpart x_b preserving topic, grammatical form, and requested output while removing the harmful objective. Four author-written wrappers (Academic threat analysis, Military intelligence briefing, Red-team exercise, Historical case analysis) each expose labeled segments (persona, scenario, request, task, rules, answer cue), yielding four matched prompts per request: A (benign direct), B (harmful direct), C (benign wrapped), D (harmful wrapped).
2. **Cohorts and labels.** Responses get a deterministic three-way label (REF / harmful compliance / other) from a rule-based classifier (refusal-phrase and compliance-cue counts with a long-response override), then GPT-5.1 acts as semantic judge; a valid GPT-5.1 judgment takes precedence. Cohort S = refuses unwrapped but complies wrapped; cohort F (fail-control) = refuses both.
3. **Relay statistics.** After every decoder block, harmful-minus-benign residual-state differences are averaged over each cohort at two locations — the request tokens and the assistant-start span (chat-template tokens between the user message and the first generated token). The signed projection of the roleplay-wrapped direction onto its unwrapped reference gives Harmfulness Retention (HR, at the request) and Refusal Transfer (RT, at assistant start); high HR with low RT defines safety-relay attenuation.
4. **Interventions.** Two edits act on every assistant-start token at a chosen block: *repair* adds the unwrapped-vs-wrapped direction difference; *ablation* removes the projection onto the normalized unwrapped harmful–benign direction. Sweeps over all blocks and strengths localize candidate regions (blocks 10–12 Llama, 13–18 Qwen, 17–28 Gemma).
5. **Wrapper-operation directions.** Nine matched textual variants define contrast directions: r_F (complete wrapper vs content-last control), r_S (scenario framing under a fixed suffix), and r_X (the non-additive prefix–scenario interaction), estimated from harmful-minus-benign assistant-start states while construction is varied.
6. **Held-out causal tests.** Requests are split into development/evaluation sets by a SHA-256 hash rule (seed 20250711). Direction, block, and strength are selected on development requests only, then subtracted from evaluation-request assistant-start states; paired changes in refusal and harmful compliance are reported, with equal-norm random-direction controls, reverse-direction controls, nested-context tests, and cross-wrapper/cross-benchmark transfer tests.
7. **Shared-axis decomposition.** v_F and v_S are decomposed into shared and contrast components and projected onto the normalized unwrapped refusal-associated reference, separating how much of each effect follows the model's ordinary refusal structure from configuration-specific remainder.

## Results & Highlights

- **Wrappers flip refusal into compliance almost everywhere.** Under the two-stage labeling, roleplay harmful compliance ranges 79.0%–100% in every benchmark-model-wrapper configuration (Table 4), e.g., Gemma-2 + Academic threat on HarmBench: 41.0% direct refusal → 100.0% harmful compliance; Qwen2.5 + Academic threat on HarmBench: 49.5% → 100.0%.
- **The relay breaks at the answer boundary, not at the request.** In the final layer quartile, HR differs by at most 0.063 between the reversal cohort S and the fail-control cohort F, whereas fail-control RT is 0.149–0.209 higher and up to 3.2× as large (Figure 2) — both cohorts "see" the harm; only S fails to carry it into the answer state.
- **Held-out causal repair (Table 1, 0–100 scale).** Complete-wrapper subtraction raises refusal 0.6 → 78.6 (+78.0) and lowers harmful compliance by 73.6; scenario-framing subtraction raises refusal 44.1 → 81.2 (+37.1, compliance −36.3); prefix×scenario gives +25.3 with compliance −8.9; equal-norm random directions change mean refusal by at most 1.2 points.
- **Intervention strength and position.** Adding the repair direction raises refusal to 100% at the strongest setting in every model family, and directional ablation suppresses up to 96.8% of baseline refusals on harmful prompts without roleplay; effects concentrate in blocks 10–12 (Llama), 13–18 (Qwen), 17–28 (Gemma). Moving the same edits to the final request tokens yields gains of only 1.8 and 2.9 points (vs 75.1 and 35.4 at assistant start); one-token assistant-start placement beats wrapper-suffix placement by 8.4 and 11.5 points.
- **Transfer and shared structure.** Directions estimated on other wrappers still raise refusal by 43.8 and 24.7 points, and directions from the other benchmark by 39.7 and 25.3 points. The two robust directions have mean cosine similarity 0.578; the shared component accounts for 78.9% of squared norm on average, with shared-component refusal gains matching total edits (+44.1 vs +42.5 for complete wrapper; +29.5 vs +27.7 for scenario framing; contrast components only +2.3 and +0.1). Both edits align with ordinary refusal structure (cos 0.703 and 0.589; reference-parallel components give 38.3 and 25.3 of the 42.5/27.7 total).

## Authors & Credibility

Md Mokarram Chowdhury (Department of Computer Science, Iowa State University) and Yang Li (Iowa State University, corresponding author) form the academic core; Ernie Chang is at Meta. The work is a mechanistic-interpretability study of LLM safety, well-grounded in the refusal-direction literature (Arditi et al. 2024, Zhao et al. 2025 on harmfulness vs refusal directions) and jailbreak-mechanism work (Lin et al., Ball et al., Krich et al. 2025/2026). The methodology is unusually disciplined for this area: hash-based dev/eval splits, frozen directions/blocks/strengths before evaluation, equal-norm random-direction and reverse-direction controls, verified decompositions, and complete prompt templates printed in the paper. Compute (~300 GPU-hours on A100 80GB) is modest and fully described (PyTorch 2.5.1, Transformers 4.45.2, bf16), supporting reproducibility. No prior red-flag concerns; the ethics section commits to locally hosted open-weight models only and aggregate reporting.

## Critical Assessment

- **Wrapper coverage is narrow relative to the framing.** All four wrappers are hand-authored by the authors and sit in the same "authority/simulation" family (threat analyst, military briefing, red-team exercise, historical case). There is no comparison against established automated persona attacks (e.g., DeepInception, persona modulation, actor attacks) or adaptive LLM-generated wrappers, so the claim that "roleplay construction" in general behaves this way rests on four templates of one stylistic genus.
- **The cohort design excludes the hardest cases.** Both cohorts require the model to refuse the unwrapped harmful request; requests the model already complies with without roleplay — arguably where safety is weakest — are outside the causal analysis, as the authors state. Fail-control groups are small or empty in some benchmark-model-wrapper configurations, and the paper can only compare success vs failure where both exist.
- **Judge dependence is acknowledged but not quantified in the main text.** GPT-5.1 (snapshot gpt-5.1-2025-11-13) takes precedence over the deterministic classifier whenever it returns a valid label; the main text gives no inter-rater agreement number between the two labelers, deferring "label validation" to appendices M–N. For a paper whose cohorts are defined by these labels, a main-text agreement figure would have strengthened confidence.
- **The geometry analysis reuses the same evaluation data** used for the held-out causal tests, so the decomposition results are descriptive characterizations rather than an independent replication — a limitation the authors themselves flag.
- **Practical safeguard value is indirect.** The edits raise refusal on benign prompts as well, so the authors correctly position them as "explanatory probes, not selective defenses"; the paper identifies a target (maintaining the recognition→refusal relay) but does not deliver or benchmark a deployable mitigation. That is honest, but readers hoping for a fix will not find one here.

## Strengths

- Genuinely matched design: manually authored benign counterparts preserve topic, form, and output format, isolating harmfulness from superficial content differences — cleaner than most jailbreak interpretability setups that compare arbitrary harmful/benign prompts.
- Position-resolved measurements (request vs assistant-start) directly operationalize the "relay" question and yield the paper's central dissociation (HR preserved, RT lost), which is a crisp, falsifiable pattern replicated across 6 benchmark-model combinations.
- Rigorous held-out discipline: hash-based splits, development-only selection of direction/block/strength, frozen settings, equal-norm random controls (≤1.2-point effects), reverse-direction and nested-context controls, and verified norm identities for the decompositions.
- The convergence finding — most repair lies along the model's own ordinary refusal direction (cos 0.703/0.589) — connects jailbreak mechanics to the established refusal-direction literature rather than positing a novel separate mechanism.
- Honest and specific limitations section; reproducibility artifacts (all four prompt templates, seeds, block/strength tables, per-configuration sweep results in appendices).

## Limitations

- Only two English safety benchmarks (AdvBench, HarmBench), three open 7–9B model families, and four author-written wrappers; no evidence for other languages, closed models, larger scales, or other roleplay styles.
- Failed-attack comparison groups are small or empty in several configurations, limiting the success-vs-failure contrast the relay story depends on.
- Causal tests cover only requests refused without roleplay but answered harmfully with it; the interventions do not map the model's complete safety process.
- The shared-axis geometry analysis uses the same evaluation data as the causal tests, so it is not an independent replication.
- Uncalibrated direction edits also increase refusal on benign prompts; the interventions are explanatory probes, not selective defenses, and no safeguard built on the finding is evaluated.

## Relevance

这篇属于用户关注的方向中「LLM 隐状态与可解释性」的核心命题：用残差流方向、投影统计与 held-out 激活干预，把一个安全行为（roleplay 越狱）解析到具体 token 位置和具体文本成分的因果贡献，方法论上与 refusal direction / representation geometry 一脉相承。虽然模型侧安全不在 SDC 检测主程序范围内，但其"识别状态仍在、却未传递到生成状态"的接力分析对理解隐藏状态如何驱动行为具有普遍的机制可解释性价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Do the same HR/RT statistics separate successful from failed attacks for *adaptive or LLM-generated* wrappers (e.g., DeepInception-style iterative attacks), or only for the authors' four hand-authored templates?
- Is the safety relay maintained in larger or instruction-tuned-closed models, where the mid-layer blocks identified here (10–12/13–18/17–28) presumably shift; is the block localization scale-dependent?
- Can a calibrated, selectivity-aware version of the assistant-start "repair" edit act as a runtime guardrail that preserves benign roleplay, given the current edits also raise benign refusal?
- Does scenario framing's residual, model-dependent component (the ~21% non-shared norm) correspond to an identifiable circuit or feature (e.g., SAE latents), or is it diffuse?
- The analysis conditions on requests the model refuses unwrapped — how does the relay picture change for the pre-compliance requests excluded from the cohorts, where safety fails even earlier?
