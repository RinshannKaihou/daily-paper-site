> **一句话总结：** 把多轮 agent 推理形式化为 LLM 隐状态轨迹，用两个几何量——时间曲率 κ_t（相邻两轮位移向量的余弦，度量方向一致性）和方差斜率 β_t（前缀离散度随轮次增长的 OLS 斜率）——在终止前区分正确/失败轨迹，并以此在线触发 thinking 模式：在 τ-Bench（Retail/Airline × Qwen3-14B/32B）上将平均任务奖励从 Never-think 的 0.241 提到 0.396（24.1%→39.6%），同时把平均 token 开销从 104.8k 降到 93.0k（−11.2%）。

> **TL;DR:** The paper models multi-turn LLM-agent reasoning as a hidden-state trajectory and shows two cheap geometric statistics — temporal curvature κ_t (cosine between consecutive turn-update displacement vectors) and variance slope β_t (OLS slope of prefix dispersion vs. prefix length) — separate correct from failed episodes *before* termination. Triggering extended thinking when κ_t < τ_κ or β_t > τ_β raises average τ-Bench task reward (Retail/Airline, Qwen3-14B/32B) from 0.241 under Never-think to 0.396 while cutting mean token cost from 104.8k to 93.0k (−11.2%), beating Always-think (≈0.353 at ≈104.1k tokens) in three of four settings — the exception is Airline+Qwen3-32B, where Always-think (0.364) wins and Warm-up collapses to 0.280.

## 核心贡献

- **隐状态轨迹形式化**：将多轮交互下 LLM 的隐状态（取用户查询末 token 的残差流，固定探测层：Qwen3-14B 第 22 层、Qwen3-32B 第 42 层、Llama-3.1-8B 第 16 层）建模为轨迹 H = {h_0,…,h_{T−1}}，轮间动态为残差过程 h_t = h_{t−1} + v_t，并提出两个互补几何信号：时间曲率 κ_t = cos(v_t, v_{t−1})（+1 表示同向累积、0 正交、−1 方向反转）与方差斜率 β_t（前缀协方差迹 V(λ) 对 λ 的回归斜率，正值为扩散、负值为收缩）。
- **跨 4 任务 × 3 模型的可分性证据**：在 Math、Code（Lost in Conversation）与 τ-Bench Airline、Retail 上，失败轨迹的 κ 系统性更偏负（正确−错误均值差：Code +0.148、Airline-14B +0.088、Math +0.047、Retail-14B +0.039），而正确轨迹的方差斜率更高——但 Airline 两个设置上不显著（p=0.30 / p=0.057）。
- **轮次对齐去混淆**：所有几何量按轮次索引（每轮取一个 h_t），消除"失败轨迹更长 → 机械上更弯曲、方差更大"的混淆因素——这是许多探测类工作忽略的细节。
- **动作链分解**：把 episode 切成三步动作滑窗（Write=改状态工具调用、Read=只读工具调用、Respond=回复用户、Transfer=转人工），发现可分性集中在信息输出型链（如 Respond→Respond→Respond→Respond 错误占比 Retail 8.2% vs 正确 3.6%、Airline 22.6% vs 9.1%），而信息获取型链（Read 为主）几乎无可分性。
- **在线几何触发控制**：κ_t < τ_κ 或 β_t > τ_β 时触发 thinking，无需训练（Learned-HST 变体在动作 one-hot + (κ_t, β_t) 上拟合分类器）；τ-Bench 平均任务奖励 0.241→0.396，token 开销 −11.2%，max-turn 终止次数也全面低于固定策略。

## Key Contributions

- A trajectory formulation of multi-turn reasoning: hidden states (residual stream at the final token of each user query, one fixed probe layer per model) form H = {h_0,…,h_{T−1}} with inter-turn residual updates h_t = h_{t−1} + v_t, characterized by two complementary geometric signals — temporal curvature κ_t (cosine of consecutive displacement vectors) and variance slope β_t (slope of prefix dispersion V(λ) against prefix length).
- Empirical evidence across 4 tasks (Math, Code from Lost in Conversation; Airline, Retail from τ-Bench) and 3 LLMs (Qwen3-14B, Qwen3-32B, Llama-3.1-8B) that failed episodes exhibit systematically more negative curvature (correct−incorrect mean gap: Code +0.148, Airline-14B +0.088, Math +0.047, Retail-14B +0.039) while correct episodes show higher variance slopes.
- A turn-alignment protocol that indexes every metric by turn rather than by episode end, removing the confound that failed episodes are mechanically longer (hence more curved and higher-variance).
- An action-chain decomposition (Write / Read / Respond / Transfer sliding windows) showing separability is action-dependent: information-output chains (e.g., Respond→Respond→Respond→Respond, 8.2% incorrect vs 3.6% correct window share on Retail; 22.6% vs 9.1% on Airline) carry the discriminative structure, whereas Read-dominated chains carry almost none.
- A training-free online control policy: trigger extended thinking when κ_t < τ_κ or β_t > τ_β, lifting average τ-Bench task reward from 0.241 to 0.396 while reducing mean token cost by 11.2% (104.8k → 93.0k), with fewer max-turn terminations than every fixed policy.

## Methodology

**Trajectory construction.** For each turn t, the hidden state h_t ∈ R^d is taken from the residual stream at the final token position of the user query q_t, at a fixed probe layer (layer 22 for Qwen3-14B, layer 42 for Qwen3-32B, layer 16 for Llama-3.1-8B). Inter-turn dynamics are modeled as a residual process h_t = h_{t−1} + v_t.

**Temporal curvature.** κ_t = ⟨v_t, v_{t−1}⟩ / (‖v_t‖‖v_{t−1}‖) ∈ [−1,1] measures directional consistency of turn-to-turn updates: near +1 means same-direction semantic accumulation, near 0 orthogonal updates, near −1 an abrupt reversal of the internal trajectory. Defined from turn 2 onward (three-point statistic).

**Variance slope.** For a prefix of λ+1 hidden states, V(λ) = tr(Cov(h_0,…,h_λ)) is an unbiased dispersion estimate (normalized by λ+1); β_t is the OLS slope of V(λ) against λ over prefixes 1..t. Positive β_t indicates the representation spreads as turns accumulate; negative indicates contraction. The same turn alignment is applied to both metrics so they are defined on identical turn indices.

**Separability analysis.** Correctness labels are the benchmarks' binary per-episode correctness. Task-level separability is tested with two-sided Mann–Whitney U tests; Fig. 3 additionally shows turn-resolved κ_t and β_t on Math (n=5168 correct vs 2219 incorrect windows) across two probe layers as a robustness check.

**Action chains.** Assistant actions are classified as Write (state-updating tool calls), Read (read-only tool calls), Respond (replies to the user simulator), Transfer (transfer_to_human). Sliding windows of three consecutive actions form chains; the paper computes per-chain effect sizes on both metrics (AUC-based) and reports correct/incorrect window shares for the top-20 chains per domain, plus a learned next-action classifier (F1: Read 0.826, Respond 0.711, Write 0.700, Transfer 0.531) used by action-conditioned triggers.

**Adaptive control on τ-Bench.** Every intervention is a per-turn binary decision δ_t of whether to extend thinking mode into a long-CoT trace. Policies: Never-think and Always-think (fixed δ_t); Warm-up (think only the first two turns — the shared base of all non-Never policies, keeping comparisons controlled since κ_t is undefined before turn 2); action-conditioned triggers (think before a predicted action); geometry-conditioned triggers (κ_t < τ_κ or β_t > τ_β); and Learned-HST (a classifier over one-hot recent-3 actions plus (κ_t, β_t), fitted on logged episodes). Each configuration runs 5 trials per domain with Qwen3-14B and Qwen3-32B; inference uses vLLM on 8× NVIDIA L40S and 4× H200 GPUs. Token cost is recorded via the true vLLM token count including context, tool calls, and thinking overhead; significance in the descriptive analysis uses Mann–Whitney U.

## Results & Highlights

- **Headline control result (τ-Bench, Retail+Airline × Qwen3-14B/32B):** geometry-conditioned triggers raise average task reward from 0.241 (Never-think) to 0.396 — i.e., 24.1% → 39.6% task success — while cutting mean token cost from 104.8k to 93.0k (−11.2%). For comparison, Always-think averages ≈0.353 at ≈104.1k tokens and Warm-up ≈0.349 at ≈99.5k: the geometry trigger dominates both reward and cost.
- **Best single setting (Retail, Qwen3-32B):** temporal-curvature trigger (τ_κ = −0.15) reaches 0.442 reward vs 0.347 Never-think, 0.405 Warm-up, 0.384 Always-think, and 0.405 Learned-HST, at 104.6k tokens vs 125.7k for Never-think — with max-turn terminations dropping from 117 to 68 (of 50-turn cap runs).
- **Curvature separates failures across all six pairings:** correct−incorrect mean κ gaps of Code +0.148 (correct episodes have median κ = +0.127, mean +0.096, i.e., same-direction accumulation, while failures reverse direction), Airline-14B +0.088, Math +0.047, Retail-14B +0.039, Retail-32B +0.035, Airline-32B +0.035.
- **Variance slope is weaker and task-dependent:** correct episodes show higher variance slopes in all six pairings, but Mann–Whitney significance fails in both Airline settings (14B p=0.30, 32B p=0.057) — the paper is upfront that this signal does not transfer uniformly.
- **Action-chain localization:** information-output chains are the most discriminative — Respond→Respond→Respond→Respond has incorrect:correct window shares of 8.2%/3.6% on Retail (rank #5 by frequency) and 22.6%/9.1% on Airline (rank #1), the highest error concentration of all 20 chains; Read-dominated chains (IDs 1 and 4) show little separability. In PCA space, correct-episode Respond chains migrate outward toward the Write/Transfer region as turns progress while incorrect ones stay spatially frozen.
- **Threshold behavior:** sweeping τ_κ is unimodal — on Retail-14B, thresholds −0.15/−0.20/−0.25 give 0.388/0.397/0.363 — and each optimum shifts predictably with the κ gap of Section 4, so a diagnostic can guide deployment without training; the thresholded curvature trigger matches or beats Learned-HST (0.398 vs 0.398 on Retail-14B at lower token cost 101.0k vs 104.5k; 0.442 vs 0.405 on Retail-32B).
- **The honest exception:** on Airline+Qwen3-32B, geometry triggers do not beat Always-think (variance-slope 0.328, curvature 0.352 vs 0.364) and Warm-up collapses to 0.280 — deliberation timing is not universally more important than deliberation amount.

## Authors & Credibility

The authors are from Lancaster University's School of Computing and Communications (Jie Liang, Zhengxin Yu — corresponding author, Peter Garraghan) and the University of Huddersfield (Hamid Nasiri). Garraghan's group has an established track record on dependability and failure analysis of large-scale ML systems, which fits the paper's systems-flavored empirical rigor (real vLLM token accounting, GPU infrastructure on 8×L40S + 4×H200, explicit max-turn termination counts). Related-work positioning is accurate and current (Lost in Conversation, τ-Bench, ICR probe, semantic entropy, AdaptThink), and the paper engages directly with the closest prior (ICR probe) to articulate its delta: cross-turn trajectory geometry used as an online control signal rather than a post-hoc per-response detector. No conflicts or credibility concerns are apparent. The paper is a preprint without a stated venue or code-release link in the main text.

## Critical Assessment

The core idea is genuinely useful and the empirical work is more careful than most probing papers, but several choices flatter the headline numbers:

- **The headline 0.241 → 0.396 averages the better of the two geometry triggers per setting** (curvature on Retail-14B/Airline-32B/Retail-32B, variance-slope on Airline-14B) — a post-hoc per-setting max. A single fixed trigger chosen without hindsight averages ≈0.394 (curvature only) or ≈0.381 (variance slope only); the qualitative claim over Always-think (≈0.353) survives, but the reported number is the most favorable aggregation available.
- **Only 5 trials per configuration** on τ-Bench, a benchmark known for high run-to-run variance, and Table 3 reports no confidence intervals or significance tests (Mann–Whitney is used only for the descriptive separability analysis). Several margins that drive conclusions are tiny — Retail-14B: curvature 0.397 vs variance-slope 0.393 vs Learned-HST 0.398.
- **One of four settings contradicts the thesis:** on Airline-32B both geometry triggers lose to Always-think (0.328/0.352 vs 0.364) and Warm-up collapses to 0.280, so "timing matters more than amount" is not a law but a three-of-four pattern. To the authors' credit, this is disclosed prominently.
- **Half the proposed signal pair fails significance on Airline** (variance slope p=0.30 and p=0.057), and the offered explanation — larger action-space openness — is descriptive rather than mechanistic; no analysis explains *why* dispersion fails there.
- **The probe layers (22/42/16) are stated without a selection criterion** in the main text; Fig. 3 shows two layers for the Math analysis, but no layer sweep accompanies the τ-Bench control experiments, leaving the method's sensitivity to this hyperparameter unquantified. Llama-3.1-8B also appears only in the analysis sections — all causal control evidence comes from two same-family Qwen3 models.
- **The learned component underdelivers:** Learned-HST, which requires logged labeled episodes, roughly ties the single-scalar threshold (0.398 vs 0.397 on Retail-14B) and loses on Retail-32B (0.405 vs 0.442), so the added complexity buys little over "think when κ_t < −0.15".
- τ-Bench rewards are low overall (0.136–0.442) and binary per-episode correctness is the only outcome; whether curvature predicts *quality* of partially-correct trajectories is unexamined, and no code release is stated.

## Strengths

- The signal is essentially free online: three hidden states at one layer, at a token position (end of the user query) the model processes anyway — no auxiliary forward passes, no gradient access, no fine-tuning for the thresholded variants.
- The turn-alignment control is a subtle but important contribution: without it, "failed trajectories are more curved" is partly an artifact of failed episodes simply being longer, and the paper identifies and removes this confound explicitly.
- It localizes *where* in the interaction separability lives (information-output chains, especially repeated Respond chains), which is more actionable than a monolithic "hidden states predict failure" claim and cleanly differentiates the work from per-response probes like ICR.
- Reporting is unusually honest: negative significance results for variance slope on Airline, the Warm-up collapse, and the Always-think win on Airline-32B are all stated in the main text rather than buried.
- Deployment-relevant metrics: token cost and max-turn termination counts are reported alongside reward, and the geometry trigger sits in the favorable corner of the reward-cost trade-off in three of four settings.

## Limitations

- The causal control experiments cover only τ-Bench tool-use domains (Retail, Airline); Math and Code appear only in the offline separability analysis, so there is no evidence the trigger helps on open-ended reasoning tasks.
- 5 trials per configuration with no variance estimates on the headline table; given τ-Bench's known stochasticity, several per-setting conclusions (e.g., Learned-HST vs curvature on Retail-14B) are within noise.
- Evidence rests on small-to-mid open models (8B–32B, two of them same-family Qwen3); behavior at frontier scale or for reasoning-native models with different hidden-state dynamics is unknown.
- τ_κ / τ_β are selected by per-setting sweeps — training-free but post-hoc tuned — and the "transferable thresholds" claim rests on optima shifting with the measured κ gap rather than on a validated transfer protocol.
- Binary episode correctness is the only label; κ_t is a noisy three-point, single-layer, single-token-position statistic, and no uncertainty quantification on the trigger decision itself is provided.

## Relevance

与你关注的「LLM 隐状态与可解释性」高度契合：论文正是用残差流隐状态的轨迹几何（曲率、离散度）做探测，并把探测信号升级为在线控制，属于表征几何→可靠 agent 系统的桥接工作。对长程自主 agent 的可靠性监控也有直接参考价值——它给出了一个几乎零成本的运行时"跑偏检测器"原型。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does κ_t retain discriminative power on frontier-scale or reasoning-native models whose residual-stream dynamics may differ qualitatively from Qwen3/Llama-3.1?
- Would aggregating the geometry across multiple layers and token positions (instead of one layer, one position) fix the variance-slope failures on Airline and reduce the noise of the three-point curvature estimate?
- Can the geometry trigger be composed with RL-learned thinking policies (e.g., AdaptThink) rather than hand-set thresholds, and would the two signals be redundant or complementary?
- Is the extreme error concentration of Respond→Respond→Respond→Respond chains a property of τ-Bench's user simulator (ambiguous, pressure-inducing queries) or a general signature of goal drift in multi-turn agents?
- The authors speculate the signals could detect adversarial trajectory-stretching attacks (crescendo-style multi-turn jailbreaks) from the inside — is curvature actually perturbed by such attacks, given that attack trajectories are semantically coherent by construction?
