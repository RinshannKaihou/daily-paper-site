Reconstructing search trajectories backward from expert final artifacts beats forward generation by +4.8 — with no stronger teacher and no human annotation.

From Final Artifacts to Trajectories: Retrospective Process Supervision for Evidence-Grounded Long-Form Generation

Junjie Huang, Jiarui Qin, Di Yin, Weiwen Liu et al. · SJTU × Tencent Youtu Lab · SFT · RETROGEN

process-supervision self-improving-agents evidence-grounded-generation trajectory-curation
◆ Key metric
+4.8 pts

Average gain over forward-generation baseline on ALCE / ScholarQA / QASPER, matched 50M-token budgets, across four 7–8B backbones. On the dynamic AstaBench, LitQA2 accuracy doubles 0.20 → 0.40.

Why this matters

A self-bootstrapped data engine for agents: training supervision is mined from artifacts the world already publishes — related-work sections, SEC filings, court judgments — closing the loop without costlier teachers.

⚑ Critical edge

Traces are judged by the same backbone that generated them — no human audit, no judge-correlation study — so the filter may optimize for self-consistency rather than truth.

Methodology

  • Inverse problem. An expert artifact y* is a lossy compression of a latent workflow (task, evidence, process). RETROGEN reconstructs an executable surrogate — not the unknowable original trajectory.
  • S1Artifact-anchored init. Infer the task spec x̂ (anti-leakage: titles/authors/target citations withheld), extract a self-induced rubric, and build an evidence environment Ê by retrieval over citations, statutes, and key entities.
  • S2Trajectory reconstruction. The agent plans and executes real tool calls — Search / Open / Extract / Compare / Outline / Draft — capped at 30 steps, 8K-char window; diversity injected along 5 axes to prevent template collapse.
  • S3Verification. The same backbone scores four signals (rubric, quality, grounding, trace-consistency; λ=0.25 each). Domain thresholds 0.52 / 0.48 / 0.63 keep ~25K traces ≈ 50M tokens.
  • S4SFT. Train on the trace's own verified artifact ŷ — never y* — for causal consistency; 80% agentic + 20% general data, full-parameter SFT.

  • Dynamic agentic eval (AstaBench): LitQA2 accuracy 0.20 → 0.40, precision 0.33 → 0.67; SQA global 0.47 → 0.59 with citation precision 0.47 → 0.67 — the gain is grounding and attribution, not fluency.
  • Policy change, not style: action-transition analysis shows search-after-search probability 0.54 → 0.39 (less redundant re-searching) and read-to-END 0.20 → 0.55 (stops once evidence suffices).
  • Trajectory > artifact imitation: +2.7 avg over Artifact-Only on domain reasoning (LAIW +3.4, LegalBench +2.0) — final artifacts alone don't expose how evidence was sought and organized.
  • Honest ablation: the full 4-signal verifier averages 64.98 vs 64.46 for the best single signal (s_con) — the combination helps, but the margin is thin and single signals win some cells.
  • General capability holds: GSM8K / MMLU-Pro / IFEval / HumanEval roughly flat (MMLU-Pro +6.7 on Qwen3-8B, −1.2 on Qwen2.5-7B).

The 41-pt win over the public WebGLM/WebCPM baseline is likely inflated by format mismatch — the honest headline controls are ForwardGen (+4.8) and Artifact-Only (+2.7).

Strengths

  • Genuinely new angle: the inverse-problem formulation (artifact = compressed process) attacks trajectory scarcity where data already exists.
  • Strongly controlled: every baseline shares token budget, sequence cap and optimizer — the deltas isolate exactly what they claim.
  • Process-level evidence: action-transition matrices prove the search policy changed, not just output style.
  • Engineering maturity: anti-leakage prompting, ŷ-not-y* causal target, per-domain threshold calibration, diversity injection.
  • Generalizes across 4 backbones from 3 families, including fully open Olmo-3-1025-7B.

Limitations

  • Self-referential verification: no human audit of the 25K retained traces; uniform signal weights asserted, not tuned.
  • Retrieval-only tool space: 30 steps, 8K-char window; no code execution, databases, or multimodal tools; long-horizon planning untested.
  • Domain friction: trained on Chinese judgments but evaluated largely on US/English LegalBench (+0.44, unexplained).
  • Thin statistics: single seed (1234), single epoch, no error bars — several headline deltas sit within plausible noise.
  • No code or data release; the pipeline is prompt-sensitive in many places.

Questions for Further Exploration

  • What fraction of the ~25K retained traces would a stronger external judge — or a human — reject? Does s_grd catch subtle misgrounding or only surface citation consistency?
  • Would an outcome-reward RL baseline (Search-R1-style) at matched compute close the +4.8 gap, and is SFT on self-filtered traces Pareto-optimal for the same tokens?
  • Can the rubric-reconstruct loop bootstrap itself iteratively — RETROGEN on its own trained artifacts — or does it saturate after one round?
  • Does the framework transfer to executable tools (code, databases) where intermediate observations are mechanically checkable — arguably where self-verification is strongest?
  • Given s_con alone nearly matches the full combination, is one grounding-consistency judge with a calibrated threshold the simpler, equally effective recipe?
