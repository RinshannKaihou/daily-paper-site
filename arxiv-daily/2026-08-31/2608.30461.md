> **一句话总结：** 提出 RETROGEN：不再前向生成轨迹，而是把专家最终产物（相关工作章节、分析师报告、判决书）当作"潜在证据搜集过程的压缩痕迹"，让 agent 反向重建带真实工具调用的候选轨迹，再用 rubric 引导的四维自验证（rubric 满足度/整体质量/证据接地/轨迹一致性）过滤出约 2.5 万条合格轨迹做 SFT——在 4 个 7-8B 骨干上平均超越前向生成基线 +4.8 分，AstaBench 动态评测中 LitQA2 准确率从 0.20 翻倍到 0.40，且不依赖更强教师模型或人工标注。

> **TL;DR:** RETROGEN turns abundant expert final artifacts (related-work sections, SEC 10-K MD&A reports, Chinese court judgments) into supervision for open-ended agents by reconstructing backward: infer the task spec and rubric from the artifact, seed an evidence environment, run real tool-augmented search-and-synthesis trajectories, then filter them with a rubric-guided 4-signal self-verification (weighted sum, domain thresholds 0.52/0.48/0.63 for scientific/financial/legal) into ~25K SFT traces. Under matched 50M-token budgets across Qwen3-8B, Qwen2.5-7B, Mistral-7B-v0.3 and Olmo-3-1025-7B, it beats forward generation from task specs by +4.8 avg on ALCE/ScholarQA/QASPER (+4.3/+6.7/+3.5) and beats artifact-only imitation by +2.7 on LAIW/LegalBench; on the dynamic AstaBench benchmark LitQA2 accuracy doubles 0.20→0.40 and SQA global avg rises 0.47→0.59, with action-transition analysis showing the search-after-search probability dropping 0.54→0.39. General capabilities (GSM8K/MMLU-Pro/IFEval/HumanEval) show no systematic degradation.

## 核心贡献

- **问题重构：** 把"专家产物"（文献综述、财务分析报告、法律判决）重新定义为可验证的反向监督目标——它们是潜在多步证据搜集过程的有损压缩，从而绕开开放域任务缺乏单一 ground-truth 轨迹、人工标注昂贵的瓶颈。
- **RETROGEN 框架：** 三段式自举流程——(1) 产物锚定初始化（从 y* 推断任务规格 x̂、提取细粒度 rubric R、用产物中的引用/法条/实体作为线索检索构建证据环境 Ê）；(2) 轨迹重建（agent 在真实工具执行环境中自主规划并产出带工具调用的候选轨迹）；(3) 证据约束验证（四维评分加权和过滤）。
- **自验证机制：** 用同一个骨干模型按 rubric 打四个分（s_rub、s_qual、s_grd、s_con，均匀权重 λ=0.25），按域校准阈值（科学 0.52 / 金融 0.48 / 法律 0.63），保留约 25K 条验证轨迹；拒绝样本的典型失败模式是"听起来合理但证据来源错误"。
- **训练设计细节：** 用验证通过的合成产物 ŷ 而非原始 y* 作为训练目标以保证中间步骤与最终输出的因果一致性；沿 5 个轴注入多样性防止轨迹坍缩成单一模板；反泄漏设计确保任务归纳 prompt 不透露原始论文标题/作者/目标引用。
- **实验证据：** 3 个领域 × 4 个骨干 × 受控 token 预算的组件消融（ForwardGen / Artifact-Only / Public-Baseline），加上 AstaBench 动态评测与动作转移矩阵分析，证明学到的是证据搜集策略而非表面文风。

## Key Contributions

- **Reformulation:** expert-curated final artifacts (abundant in pre-training data) are treated as compressed traces of latent multi-step evidence-seeking processes, reframing static outputs as verifiable targets for retrospective process supervision — no stronger teacher models, no human trajectory annotation.
- **RETROGEN framework:** artifact-anchored initialization (infer task spec x̂, extract a self-induced rubric R = {(r_k, c_k)}, build the evidence environment Ê via cue-conditioned retrieval) → autonomous trajectory reconstruction with a shared abstract action vocabulary A = {Search, Open, Extract, Compare, Outline, Draft} and real tool executions → rubric-guided verification.
- **Evidence-constrained verification:** four complementary signals — rubric satisfaction (s_rub), holistic quality (s_qual), evidence grounding (s_grd), trace consistency (s_con) — combined as Score = λ_rub·s_rub + λ_qual·s_qual + λ_grd·s_grd + λ_scon·s_con with uniform λ=0.25; domain thresholds calibrated from score distributions (0.52/0.48/0.63) retain ~25K verified trajectories; failed retrievals, malformed tool executions and sub-threshold traces are discarded automatically.
- **Training with causal consistency:** SFT on (x̂, Ê, τ̂^pre, ŷ) where ŷ is the trace's own verified synthesized artifact (not the original y*), guaranteeing the intermediate prefix actually entails the final output; controlled diversity injection across 5 axes (query phrasing, prompt style, plan flow, granularity, formatting) and anti-leakage prompting (task induction must not reveal paper titles, target citations, or conclusions; y* is used only offline as the anchor).
- **Comprehensive evaluation:** 3 domains (scientific writing from author-written related-work sections; financial analysis from SEC 10-K MD&A filings in EDGAR; legal drafting from Chinese first-instance judgments) × 4 backbones, with token-budget-matched controls isolating each design choice, plus dynamic agentic evaluation on AstaBench.

## Methodology

**Problem formulation.** Open-ended evidence-grounded long-form generation is modeled as an inverse problem: y* = f(x*, E*, τ*) where the latent expert workflow (task, evidence, process) is unobserved. Since the exact historical process τ* is unknowable, RETROGEN reconstructs an *executable surrogate trajectory*: x̂ = g_x(y*), Ê = g_e(y*), τ̂ = g_τ(y*, x̂, Ê). A good reconstruction must satisfy three desiderata: artifact fidelity, evidence faithfulness, and procedural plausibility.

**Stage 1 — Artifact-anchored initialization.** The agent infers a plausible task specification x̂ from the artifact y* (e.g., "write a related-work section on trust-aware recommender systems" — naming search intent and topical scope but *not* the specific titles/authors/citations the agent must recover). Simultaneously it extracts a rubric R of fine-grained checkable criteria mapped to target aspects (e.g., a legal judgment rubric demands "explicitly invoking the specific tort law precedent", a survey rubric requires "contrasting the computational overhead of two specific baseline models"). Concrete cues (citations, statutory references, key entities) seed artifact-conditioned retrieval over domain corpora to build Ê. This phase is fully self-bootstrapped: zero human-authored traces.

**Stage 2 — Trajectory reconstruction.** The agent formulates a high-level plan π̂ and materializes candidate tool-augmented traces τ̂ = (ô_0, â_1, ô_1, …, n̂_T, ŷ), where observations come from actual tool calls (search/open/browse over the indexed corpora) rather than model-imagined snippets, and n̂_t are intermediate synthesis notes. Each trajectory is capped at 30 tool steps with an 8,000-character sliding context window. Diversity is injected by randomizing 5 axes (user-query phrasing, prompt style, high-level plan flow from a template pool, surface formatting of tool-call blocks including ReAct-style variants, outline granularity/reflection frequency).

**Stage 3 — Evidence-constrained verification.** The same backbone (no stronger external judge) scores each trace on the three desiderata via four signals: s_rub (does ŷ satisfy the self-induced rubric), s_qual (holistic domain quality — coherence, factual accuracy, legal correctness, analytical depth), s_grd (are intermediate notes and final claims strictly supported by Ê), s_con (internal workflow logic — are comparisons checked against previously opened documents, are earlier claims consistent with later ones). Weighted sum with uniform λ=0.25; domain thresholds (0.52 scientific / 0.48 financial / 0.63 legal, calibrated from score distributions with means 0.571/0.508/0.624) cut the low-score tail while retaining enough data for the 50M-token SFT corpus, yielding ~25K verified trajectories. Appendix B documents failure modes: rejected traces typically sound plausible but carry the wrong evidence lineage (fluent prose about uncertainty while omitting required themes).

**Stage 4 — Retrospective process supervision.** Surviving traces are trained on as single autoregressive sequences z_i = Serialize(x̂_i, Ê_1, τ̂_i^pre, ŷ_i) with standard LM loss — note the final target is the trace's own synthesized artifact ŷ, not the original y*, for strict causal consistency. The ~50M-token SFT corpus is 80% verified agentic trajectories (40/20/20/20 scientific/financial/legal/general) + 20% general data (instruction-following, math, coding, multi-turn dialogue). Training: full-parameter SFT, DeepSpeed ZeRO-1, AdamW peak LR 1e-5, cosine schedule, 5% warmup, 1 epoch, ~512K-token effective batches, fixed seed 1234.

**Evaluation.** Three axes: evidence-grounded long-form generation (ALCE, ScholarQA, QASPER); domain-specific grounded reasoning (LAIW, LegalBench); general capability retention (GSM8K, MMLU-Pro, IFEval, HumanEval). All baselines share the same token budget, sequence-length cap and optimization config; inference via vLLM. Baselines: (1) Initial checkpoint; (2) ForwardGen — trajectories generated from the task spec x̂ *without* the expert artifact anchor; (3) Artifact-Only — same reconstructed dataset with all intermediate tool interactions removed; (4) Public-Baseline — the 80% agentic slice replaced with WebGLM-QA + WebCPM-WK (retrieve-then-write distillation from stronger systems).

## Results & Highlights

- **Retrospective anchoring beats forward generation by a clear margin.** Averaged over the four backbones, RETROGEN improves the evidence-grounded generation score by **+4.8 points over ForwardGen** (+4.3 ALCE, +6.7 ScholarQA, +3.5 QASPER). Concretely on Qwen3-8B: QASPER 56.15 vs 51.04 (ForwardGen) and 53.25 (Initial) = **+5.1 over ForwardGen**; ALCE 88.03 vs 85.33 (ForwardGen) and 74.93 (Initial); ScholarQA 67.71 vs 65.42 (ForwardGen).
- **Trajectory-level supervision beats final-artifact imitation.** RETROGEN improves over Artifact-Only by **+2.7 points on average** on domain-specific reasoning, with **+3.4 on LAIW** (Qwen3-8B: 37.65 vs 34.22) and **+2.0 on LegalBench** (75.37 vs 73.37) — final artifacts alone do not expose the procedural structure of how evidence was searched, selected, and organized.
- **Self-generated verified trajectories beat public retrieve-then-write distillation.** Under the same token budget, RETROGEN overwhelmingly beats the Public-Baseline (WebGLM-QA + WebCPM-WK), e.g., Qwen3-8B ALCE 88.03 vs 46.73 (WebCPM) / 86.52 (WebGLM) — though this margin is likely inflated by format mismatch (see Critical Assessment).
- **Dynamic agentic evaluation (AstaBench, literature understanding, max_rounds=15).** On LitQA2-validation, accuracy **doubles from 0.20 to 0.40** and precision improves **0.33 → 0.67** while coverage stays at 0.60 (more accurate without abstaining more). On SQA, global average rises **0.47 → 0.59**, citation precision **0.47 → 0.67**, citation recall **0.33 → 0.57**, with answer precision unchanged at 0.82 — the gain is grounding/attribution, not just fluency.
- **Action-transition analysis explains the gain as policy change, not style.** On the AstaBench sandbox, the search-after-search probability drops **0.54 → 0.39** (less redundant re-searching), search-to-read rises **0.15 → 0.23**, and read-to-END rises **0.20 → 0.55** (terminates once sufficient evidence is gathered).
- **Verification ablation (Qwen3-8B, matched token budget).** Average score over the five benchmarks: Initial 62.40 → full RETROGEN **64.98**, vs best single-signal filter "only s_con" 64.46, "only s_rub" 64.01, and next-N replacement 63.66. The weighted multi-signal verifier is the best average, but the margin over the best single signal is only +0.52, and single-signal filters actually win individual cells (only s_con: ALCE 88.18 and ScholarQA 67.82 vs 88.03/67.71 for the full combination).
- **General capability retention.** Across GSM8K/MMLU-Pro/IFEval/HumanEval there is no systematic degradation: e.g., MMLU-Pro +6.7 on Qwen3-8B but −1.2 on Qwen2.5-7B, HumanEval −0.6 on Qwen3-8B, IFEval gains of +3.5 to +4.2 across models — mixed but roughly flat, consistent with the 20% general-data mixture.
- **Data scale.** ~25K verified trajectories retained out of the candidates, with per-domain acceptance thresholds cutting at the 0.52/0.48/0.63 score levels; the rejected-tail analysis (fluent-but-wrong-lineage traces) is a useful qualitative contribution.

## Critical Assessment

- **The verification filter is self-referential.** Traces are judged by the *same backbone* that generated them (the paper explicitly states no stronger external judge is introduced). There is no human audit of the ~25K retained trajectories, no correlation study between the four LLM-judged scores and human ratings, and no estimate of how much incorrect-but-plausible content survives. If the generator's blind spots (e.g., subtle misgrounding) are invisible to itself as a judge, the filter may optimize for self-consistency rather than truth.
- **The Public-Baseline comparison is weak/mismatched.** WebCPM-WK scores 46.73 ALCE on Qwen3-8B vs 88.03 for RETROGEN — a 41-point gap that almost certainly reflects format/domain mismatch of the public corpora (short-answer web QA distillation vs long-form grounded synthesis) rather than the value of retrospective verification per se. The meaningful controls are ForwardGen and Artifact-Only, and those margins (+4.8 and +2.7) are the honest headline.
- **The verification ablation shows the multi-signal combination is barely better than its best component.** Full RETROGEN averages 64.98 vs 64.46 for "only s_con" — and s_con alone *wins* ALCE (88.18 vs 88.03) and ScholarQA (67.82 vs 67.71). The "four complementary signals" story rests on the LAIW cell (37.65 vs 36.05) driving the average; with a single seed and no variance estimates, per-cell deltas under ~1 point are uninterpretable.
- **Small and unexplained legal gains.** On Qwen3-8B, LegalBench moves 74.93 → 75.37 (+0.44 over Initial). The model is trained on *Chinese* first-instance judgments while LegalBench is largely US/English law; neither the cross-lingual transfer claim nor its absence is analyzed. LAIW is the only domain-specific benchmark with a solid gain (+3.4 over Artifact-Only).
- **Statistical rigor is thin.** One run per backbone, seed fixed to 1234, one epoch — no error bars, no significance tests, and several headline cells differ by 1–2 points. The case study (UK Parliament question) is a single hand-picked example.
- **Missing comparisons.** There is no comparison against outcome-reward rejection sampling, RL-based search agents (e.g., Search-R1-style), or hindsight-relabeling variants of the same idea; ForwardGen is the only trajectory-generation control. The claim "supervision over explicit trajectories teaches more than observing retrieved context" would be stronger against an RL baseline at matched compute.
- **Genuine positives:** the anti-leakage task-induction protocol, training on ŷ instead of y* for causal consistency, and the matched-budget design are careful, and the rejected-trace failure-mode analysis ("plausible-sounding workflow that recovers the wrong evidence lineage") shows the authors actually looked at what the filter removes.

## Strengths

- Clean conceptual contribution: the inverse-problem formulation (artifact = lossy compression of a latent process) is a genuinely different angle on trajectory data scarcity, and the three desiderata map cleanly onto four measurable verification signals.
- Strongly controlled experiments: every fine-tuned baseline shares token budget, sequence-length cap and optimization configuration, so the ForwardGen/Artifact-Only deltas isolate exactly what they claim to (retrospective anchoring; trajectory-level supervision).
- Process-level evidence beyond outcome metrics: the AstaBench action-transition matrices (search-after-search 0.54→0.39, read-to-END 0.20→0.55) demonstrate that the model's *policy* changed, not just its output style.
- Engineering maturity: diversity injection to prevent template collapse, anti-leakage prompting, causal-consistency training target, per-domain threshold calibration, and general-capability retention checks.
- Works across 4 backbones from 3 model families including a fully open one, suggesting the recipe is not tuned to a single model's quirks.

## Limitations

- Depends on artifacts that contain recoverable evidence and structure; for underspecified, stylistically diverse, or weakly grounded outputs the reconstructed trajectories become unreliable (the authors acknowledge this).
- The tool space is retrieval-only (search/open/browse/extract); 30 tool steps and an 8K-char observation window bound the complexity of tasks. No code execution, database, or multimodal tools; longer-horizon planning is untested.
- Verification quality is unvalidated: self-judging by the generating backbone, no human evaluation, and the 4-signal weighting (uniform λ=0.25) is asserted rather than tuned or justified.
- Train/eval domain friction: Chinese legal training artifacts vs English-dominant legal benchmarks; the financial domain is trained and evaluated in a similarly indirect way (MD&A sections → LAIW-style reasoning benchmarks).
- Single-seed, single-epoch runs with no variance reporting; several claimed gains are within plausible noise.
- No code/data release stated; the pipeline (rubric extraction, task induction, verification prompts) has many prompt-sensitive components, which appendix C only partially mitigates with simplified templates.

## Relevance

与你最关心的"Self-Evolving Agents & Automated Research"高度契合：RETROGEN 是一个不依赖更强教师、不依赖人工标注的自改进 agent 训练框架，用"从自己的产物反推过程 + 自验证过滤"的闭环构造训练数据，且直接在科研 agent 基准（AstaBench 文献理解）上验证了证据搜集策略的改进。对长期关注自进化 agent 数据引擎、过程监督与 agent 轨迹数据质量的人来说，这是一篇方法论上值得细读（并质疑其自验证循环性）的工作。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How reliable is self-verification in practice? What fraction of the ~25K retained trajectories would a stronger external model or human annotator reject, and does the s_grd signal actually detect subtle misgrounding or only surface-level citation consistency?
- Would an outcome-reward RL baseline (or rejection sampling with executable checks) at matched compute close the +4.8 gap over ForwardGen, and is SFT on self-filtered traces a Pareto-optimal use of the same tokens?
- Can the rubric-and-reconstruct loop be bootstrapped iteratively (RETROGEN on its own RETROGEN-trained artifacts), or does the improvement saturate after one round? The paper is silent on multiple self-improvement iterations.
- Does the framework transfer beyond retrieval to executable tools (code, databases) where intermediate observations are mechanically checkable — arguably where self-verification would be strongest?
- The verification ablation shows s_con alone nearly matches the full combination — is the 4-signal machinery necessary, or would a single grounding-consistency judge with a calibrated threshold be the simpler, equally effective recipe?
