> **一句话总结：** 在 Pythia 全系列（6 个规模 × 8 个检查点 × 4 类任务，共 192 个单元）的完全预注册研究中，线性探针从第 1000 步起就能以 AUROC≥0.990 从残差流中读出目标变量，但沿同一"读出方向"做加性 steering 在 43/48 个模型×检查点单元中与按位置和范数匹配的随机方向零效应带不可区分（最终检查点的归一化写入量仅 ≤0.11% 表示头空间）——内部"可读"系统性领先行为"可读"，而"因果可用"几乎始终缺席，且规模增大并不缩小这一滞后；预注册的尺度轴与时间轴两个假设均诚实判定为 INDETERMINATE。

> **TL;DR:** A fully pre-registered developmental study over the entire Pythia suite (6 sizes 160M–12B × 8 checkpoints 1k–143k steps × 4 task families) measures three clocks separately: probe readability of the target variable saturates at AUROC ≥0.990 from checkpoint step 1k at every scale, behavioral readability (the model's own answer-score AUROC) ramps much later (12B: 0.524 → 0.909, crossing 0.9 only at the final checkpoint, while 1B plateaus near 0.71), and additive steering along the frozen probe direction stays inside the site-and-norm-matched random-direction null band in 43/48 cells (vs. |z_e| ≥ 2 in 5 cells: four early negative "backfires" at z_e ≈ −2.4 to −2.7 and one isolated +2.49 pulse) with normalized causal write-in pinned at ≤0.11% of representation headroom even though headroom grows up to 56.8×. Both pre-registered scaling hypotheses resolve INDETERMINATE (scale-axis slope +0.24, 95% CI [−0.60, +0.87]; 34.55% of units develop backwards). Practical upshot: probes are reliable safety *sensors* from the start of training, but probe directions are not usable runtime control *levers* for nearly all of training.

## 核心贡献

- **三轨分解（three-track dissociation）**：把"可控性"拆成三个分别计时的量——内部可读性（探针 AUROC）、行为可读性（输出层 answer-score AUROC）、因果有效性（沿读出方向的干预 vs 匹配零假设）——并发现主导顺序是 **read-before-write**（11/11 个 pattern-1 单元，无一例反转），滞后不随规模缩小。
- **机制层面的瓶颈定位**：沿探针方向的表示头空间（headroom）随训练与规模增长 2.0×–56.8×（12B 从 0.038 涨到 2.16），但因果写入量始终钉死在头空间的 ≤0.11%（2.8B/6.9B/12B 最终检查点分别为 0.070%/0.109%/0.108%），且任何单元的绝对因果效应 |e(1)| 从不超过 0.016 个 answer-score 单位——表示越来越"写满"这个方向，而输出电路对该分量的相对依赖不增反降。
- **诚实的预注册裁决**：在冻结的决策树下，尺度轴（斜率 +0.24，95% CI [−0.60, +0.87]）与时间轴（3:3 分裂、无多数）均判 INDETERMINATE，34.55%（69/191）单元的发育指数为负（非单调）——单轴 onset 回归混淆了三个速度不同的时钟，有时甚至倒着走。
- **steering 的系统性无效证据**：43/48 单元落在零效应带内；剂量-响应曲线在整个冻结强度网格上平坦（排除"剂量太小"解释）；4 个显著单元全部是训练早期（≤2k 步）的**负向 backfire**（推向答案反而把输出推离答案）。
- **可复用的审计化预注册模板**：冻结估计量与决策树、黄金标准仪器自检、匹配零假设、md5 链式 append-only 审计账本、逐检查点内容哈希门——后者在实验中真的抓到了公开发布的 Pythia-2.8B lineage 静默损坏（不同检查点标签的权重逐字节相同），并被独立社区报告与 lineage 审计交叉证实。

## Key Contributions

- A **three-track decomposition** of "controllability" — internal readability (probe AUROC), behavioral readability (output-layer answer-score AUROC), and causal efficacy (steering vs. matched random-direction nulls) — showing a dominant **read-before-write** ordering (11/11 pattern-1 units, no inversion) whose lag does not shrink with scale.
- A **mechanistic account of the bottleneck**: representation headroom along the probe direction grows 2.0×–56.8× with training and scale (12B: 0.038 → 2.16), while causal write-in stays pinned at ≤0.11% of headroom (final-checkpoint č = 0.070% / 0.109% / 0.108% for 2.8B / 6.9B / 12B) and no cell's absolute effect exceeds 0.016 answer-score units — the direction becomes an ever more accurate description of a component the readout circuit increasingly ignores.
- An **honest pre-registered verdict**: both the scale axis (slope +0.24, 95% CI [−0.60, +0.87]) and the time axis (3 fork-A vs. 3 INDETERMINATE, no majority) resolve INDETERMINATE under the frozen decision tree, with 34.55% (69/191) of units developing backwards — single-axis onset regressions conflate three clocks running at different (sometimes negative) speeds.
- **Systematic evidence that steering along reading directions is inert**: 43/48 model×checkpoint cells lie in the null band; dose-response curves are flat across the frozen strength grid (ruling out under-dosing); the four remaining significant cells are all *negative* early backfires within the first 2k steps.
- A **reusable audited pre-registration template** — frozen estimands, matched nulls, md5-chained append-only audit ledger, and a per-checkpoint content-hash gate that caught a silently corrupted public Pythia-2.8B lineage (byte-identical weights under distinct checkpoint labels), later corroborated by an independent community report and lineage audit.

## Methodology

**Grid and instrumentation.** The study crosses the six public Pythia sizes (160M, 410M, 1B, 2.8B, 6.9B, 12B) with eight checkpoints (1k, 2k, 4k, 8k, 16k, 32k, 64k, 143k training steps) and four pre-registered task families — T (template contrast), D (distractor robustness), P (prior-bias override, basis of the bias arm), N (negative transfer) — for 192 units, each evaluated by the same frozen pipeline (probe track, output-level track, intervention track with matched nulls, resolution bookkeeping). Every model×checkpoint weight file passed a mandatory content-hash gate with pairwise-distinct, lineage-pinned hashes. Total compute: ~240 instance-hours on 2×A100-SXM4-40GB.

**Estimands.** The answer score s(x) is the logit margin of the designated answer token over the max competitor logit. Track 1 (internal readability): a logistic probe trained per unit at the pre-registered best site, with AUROC on a held-out dev split and a frozen-pipeline test split (a >0.1 dev→test discrepancy flag fired in only 3/192 units, all at 12B). Track 2 (behavioral readability): AUROC of the model's own answer score against the gold label — no probe involved. Track 3 (causal efficacy): an additive, unit-norm edit along the frozen probe direction at the probed site (a_e ← a_e + λw), with effect e(λ) = E[s(x^(λ)) − s(x^(0))]; the null distribution comes from random directions matched for site and norm, and efficacy is reported as z_e = (e − μ_null)/σ_null plus a normalized write-in č = e(1)/C, where C is the representation headroom (per-unit RMS magnitude of the residual-stream component along w). The instrument's per-unit noise floor ε_m (median absolute within-unit null effect) is recorded for every unit rather than absorbed.

**Decision protocol (frozen before any criterion data).** Scale axis: WLS of unit onset step against log₁₀ parameters (main line n=6, sub line n=5). Time axis: per-size WLS of milestone index against log step, with fork A if the 95% CI is entirely below zero, fork C if entirely above, INDETERMINATE otherwise; each axis resolves by majority vote. Discipline clauses forbid threshold lowering, estimand switching, seed completion, and post-hoc re-gridding. A pre-registered OLMo-2 replication arm has an explicit degradation rule (fewer than three replication sizes ⇒ verdict capped at "weak").

**Design choice for the intervention.** The additive single-site edit is deliberately the weakest, most transparent member of the steering family: it is the minimum-energy, signal-aligned path along the direction a probe reads, giving lower-bound semantics for the direction-generalization assumption ("a direction good for reading is good for writing"), and it keeps the same frozen direction, norm, and site rule at every size and checkpoint so cross-scale comparisons stay well-posed. Nulls are matched for edit norm, and dose-response curves e(λ) over the frozen strength grid test the "too timid a dose" objection directly.

**Audit infrastructure.** Configuration-driven, deterministic pipeline (md5-derived unit seeds, atomic "incomplete → payload → complete" writes), an append-only snapshot-backed audit ledger recording every protocol-relevant event, and restart-safe execution (a cloud restart at 174/192 test units resumed bit-identically with zero data loss). The content-hash gate quarantined a corrupted Pythia-2.8B lineage before any criterion data were produced on it.

## Results & Highlights

- **Track 1 (internal readability) saturates immediately, at every scale.** Probe AUROC ≥ 0.990 on dev and ≥ 0.982 on test from checkpoint step 1,000 across all six Pythia sizes (dev minimum 0.990, test minimum 0.982); the pre-registered dev↔test discrepancy flag fired in only 3 of 192 units. A linear reader can recover the target variable from the residual stream essentially as soon as training begins.
- **Track 2 (behavioral readability) develops gradually and later at larger scales.** The output-level answer-score AUROC climbs from 0.534 → 0.968 for 2.8B, 0.569 → 0.896 for 6.9B, and 0.524 → 0.909 for 12B — 12B crosses 0.9 only at the final (143k) checkpoint — while 1B plateaus near 0.71 (final 0.711).
- **Track 3 (causal efficacy) is null-equivalent almost everywhere.** 43 of 48 model×checkpoint cells lie inside the matched-null band (|z_e| < 2), vs. 5 significant cells: four early negative backfires in the first 2,000 steps (410M step-2k −2.65; 2.8B step-1k −2.68; 2.8B step-2k −2.48; 12B step-2k −2.43) and one isolated positive pulse at 12B step-8k (z_e = +2.49, dev-phase +2.04, which does not persist — step-16k is −0.31). Dose-response curves are flat across the frozen strength grid in every null cell, so the silence is about the *direction*, not the dose.
- **The mechanistic dissociation is orders of magnitude wide.** Headroom C grows over training by 2.0× (160M), 8.1× (410M), 3.0× (1B), 46.9× (2.8B), 37.7× (6.9B), and 56.8× (12B) — from 0.038 to 2.16 — yet final-checkpoint normalized write-in is only 0.070% (2.8B), 0.109% (6.9B), and 0.108% (12B), with per-cell effects e(1) never exceeding 0.016 answer-score units anywhere on the grid. The instrument's noise floor ε_m is strictly monotone in scale (0.00398 at 160M → 0.0328 at 12B, an 8.2× range), and the largest causal effect observed (0.016) sits at or below the floor for the three largest sizes.
- **Both pre-registered scaling hypotheses fail honestly.** Scale-axis slope +0.24 (95% CI [−0.60, +0.87]; sub line +0.32, [−0.12, +1.18]) and a 3-vs-3 time-axis split (fork A: 410M, 2.8B edge case, 6.9B; INDETERMINATE: 160M, 1B, 12B) yield no majority; the development index shows 34.55% (69/191) of units developing backwards. The read-before-write ordering is nevertheless clean: 11/11 pattern-1 units, no inversion, and catch-up magnitude does not grow with scale (95% CI [−0.722, +0.438]).
- **The bias arm gives one mechanical positive.** Aggregating fitted prior-bias surfaces over all 24 size×family cells, the WLS slope of bias magnitude on log parameters is +0.00062 to +0.291 (barely excluding zero), with intercept −0.719 — larger models steer *off* their priors harder, the mirror image of the main lag result.
- **OLMo-2 replication: direction preserved, magnitude weak.** The two-size replication arm reproduces probe saturation from the earliest checkpoint, the readable-before-causal ordering, and null-equivalent steering, at attenuated magnitude — scored "weak" by the pre-registered degradation rule.

## Authors & Credibility

The paper has a single author, Xing Xun, affiliated with Tsingjiao Information Science (Beijing) Co., Ltd. — an industry rather than academic-lab affiliation, with no external co-authors or stated institutional oversight. Unusually for that profile, the methodological hygiene is exceptional: the study is fully pre-registered with frozen estimands and a decision tree whose INDETERMINATE branches were actually taken, it publishes hash-chained audit artifacts (dev/test manifests and the final decision artifact all carry md5s in the text), and its headline negative is reported against the author's own pre-registered hypotheses. The content-hash gate finding on the Pythia-2.8B lineage is independently corroborated by an external community report and a separate lineage audit (both cited), which materially strengthens credibility. The related-work coverage (probing and its limits, activation steering, circuit-level work, checkpoint suites) is accurate and current. Caveat: single-author work means no independent co-sign-off on the analysis code, though the deterministic, hash-audited pipeline partially compensates.

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty.** The developmental question — "when does a circuit become a *lever*, not just readable?" — is genuinely unmeasured prior to this work (the paper's Table 1 makes the gap explicit: probing is partially developmental, steering is not), and the three-track decomposition plus headroom/write-in framing is a real conceptual tool, not a rebranding. This is the paper's strongest suit.

**Rigor — strong but with specific weak points.** The pre-registration is unusually disciplined, and the matched nulls, flat dose-response curves, and openly reported noise floor close several standard objections. However: (1) the intervention tested is the *weakest* member of the steering family — a single-site additive linear edit — and no multi-site, learned, or trained interventions are run, so the headline "steering is inert" is a lower bound that does not directly contradict published ActAdd/RepE/ITI successes; Table 4's framing ("runtime activation control: NOT supported") slightly overreaches what a minimal answer-format-contrast grid can say about the open-ended generation settings where those methods are actually deployed. (2) The main claims ride on a single suite (Pythia, all Pile-trained); the OLMo-2 replication is only two sizes and self-capped at "weak" by the frozen rule, so cross-family generalization is thin. (3) The scale verdict is partly a power statement: n=6 sizes gives a CI of [−0.60, +0.87] — the paper concedes this but the INDETERMINATE framing still leaves the scale question open rather than answered. (4) Probe saturation at AUROC ≥0.99 from step 1k on minimal format-contrast tasks may partly reflect trivially linearly separable surface features; no control-task or amnesic-probing analysis (both cited) is run to verify the probe uses target-relevant information rather than task priors. (5) The noise floor grows 8.2× with scale, so "null-equivalent" at 12B means |effect| below a floor ~8× coarser than at 160M — disclosed, but it makes the large-scale nulls weaker evidence than the small-scale ones. (6) The one positive pre-registered result (bias slope CI lower bound +0.00062) excludes zero by a hair and is fragile.

**Reproducibility.** Excellent on its own terms: deterministic config-driven pipeline, md5-derived seeds, atomic restart-safe execution (a 174/192-unit cloud restart recovered bit-identically), released code/configs/ledger promised with the camera-ready. The main reproducibility risk is external: the Pythia-2.8B corruption incident shows public checkpoint lineages themselves can silently change, and the paper's pinned-revision discipline is the right mitigation.

**Significance and red flags.** The safety framing (probes = reliable sensors from step 1k; probe directions = unusable levers for ~all of training) is the practically important claim, and it is well-scoped to runtime activation control. Red flags are modest: single-author industry provenance; the 12B step-8k pulse (+2.49) is 1 of 48 cells and its "re-wiring window" interpretation is explicitly speculation, properly excluded from headline claims; and "behavioral readability" is operationalized narrowly as a logit-margin AUROC on a designated answer token, so the behavioral-lag result partly inherits that readout choice. Overall this is honest, careful, somewhat narrow work whose headline is appropriately more modest than its abstract's structure suggests.

## Strengths

- Genuine pre-registration with teeth: frozen estimands, decision tree, and discipline clauses, with INDETERMINATE branches actually taken instead of massaged into a positive result.
- The three-track decomposition dissolves the single-onset "emergence of controllability" question and is immediately reusable by other developmental interpretability studies.
- Matched nulls (site- and norm-matched random directions) plus flat dose-response curves specifically close the two most obvious objections to the null result (noise and under-dosing).
- The headroom/write-in quantification (≤0.11% write-in vs. up to 56.8× headroom growth) turns a qualitative lag into a sharp mechanistic bottleneck.
- The content-hash gate catching the silently corrupted public Pythia-2.8B lineage is a concrete, independently corroborated service to anyone doing checkpoint-based developmental studies.
- Open instrument calibration: the 8.2× scale-dependent noise floor is published rather than absorbed, so every null statement has a per-size meaning.

## Limitations

- One suite (Pythia) carries the main claims; the pre-registered OLMo-2 replication covers only two sizes and is capped at "weak", so cross-family generality is unproven.
- The intervention class is the weakest available (additive, single-site, linear, frozen direction); multi-site, learned, or weight-space interventions — including the ROME-style edits and trained-representation methods the related work covers — are explicitly out of scope and remain open.
- The four task families are minimal controlled answer-format contrasts; open-ended generation and multi-turn control, where steering methods report their wins, are untested.
- Eight checkpoints cannot resolve sub-grid developmental events; the 12B step-8k pulse (one cell) and the clustered early backfires fit a "re-wiring window" hypothesis the grid is too coarse to confirm.
- The noise floor coarsens monotonically with scale (8.2× range), so large-scale null bands are much wider than small-scale ones.
- n=6 sizes yields wide confidence intervals on the scale axis; the INDETERMINATE verdict is partly a power statement, and no scaling law either way is established.
- Behavioral readability is a single-token logit-margin AUROC — a narrow behavioral readout that may understate what fuller output measures would show.

## Relevance

这篇文章与你的 LLM 隐含状态与可解释性方向高度吻合：它在整个训练过程中同时追踪 probing 可读性、行为可读性和沿探针方向的因果 steering 有效性，直接回答了"可读的表示是否就是可用的控制杠杆"这一探针-因果缺口问题。其结论（探针从一开始就是可靠的监控传感器，而 probe 方向在几乎整个训练期内都不是有效的运行时干预方向）对你的 long-running agent 系统可靠性/可监控性关切也有直接的安全含义。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the readable-but-inert lag persist for stronger intervention classes — multi-site edits, learned or optimized directions, distributed interventions, or ROME-style weight edits — evaluated at the same intermediate checkpoints?
- Would denser checkpoint sampling around behavioral mid-transition (where the isolated 12B step-8k pulse and the early backfires cluster) confirm the re-wiring-window hypothesis and yield actionable intervention timing?
- Would control-task or amnesic-probing corrections change Track 1's saturated AUROC ≥0.990 on these minimal format-contrast tasks, or does the early probe rely on trivial surface features?
- Does the headroom/write-in dissociation hold on open-ended generation tasks and on instruction-tuned models, where the behavioral readout is not a single-token logit margin?
- How widespread is silent checkpoint-lineage corruption across public suites, and which published developmental (grokking-style, induction-head) results on Pythia might need re-auditing with content hashing?
- Is the early negative backfire (steering against the answer in the first 2k steps) a signature of early readout-circuit geometry, and does it predict anything about later representational structure?
