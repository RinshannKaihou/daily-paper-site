> **一句话总结：** 威斯康星大学麦迪逊分校的单一作者测量研究定义了"视觉不敏感差距"（Visual Insensitivity Gap）：用 Grounding-DINO+SAM 定位问题相关区域并做高斯模糊（σ=20），以模糊前后 next-token 分布的 KL 散度定义逐样本视觉敏感指数 VSI，在 6 个 VLM（LLaVA-1.5-7B、LLaVA-NeXT、Idefics3-8B、Qwen3-VL-8B、Qwen2.5-VL-7B/32B）× 4 个基准（POPE、MMVP、HallusionBench、MMStar）上发现 40%–97% 的样本"视而不见"（VSI<0.05），且这一性质是**样本内在的**——VSI 排序跨模型迁移（15 对模型 grand-mean Spearman ρ=+0.40，每对 permutation p<10⁻³）；机制上，各模型自己的视觉塔线性探针以 0.72–0.79 精度检出扰动，而 argmax 仅在 2%–11% 的同批样本上改变（encoder–LLM gap 全部 >0.65），说明是路由失效而非表征缺失；VSI 并非普适弃答信号（18 格中仅 1 格单独最优），而是与 softmax 互补的条件性集成成分（Qwen2.5-VL-7B 在 MMVP 上混合信号 AUROC 0.676 vs max-prob 0.496，+0.18）。

> **TL;DR:** This solo-authored measurement paper introduces the Visual Sensitivity Index (VSI) — the KL divergence between a VLM's next-token distribution before and after Gaussian-blurring (σ=20) the question-relevant image region, localized by Grounding-DINO + SAM — and shows that on POPE, MMVP, HallusionBench, and MMStar across six VLMs (LLaVA-1.5-7B, LLaVA-NeXT, Idefics3-8B, Qwen3-VL-8B, Qwen2.5-VL-7B/32B), 40%–97% of samples are visually insensitive (VSI<0.05) even though a linear probe on each model's own vision tower detects the perturbation at 0.72–0.79 accuracy while the argmax token changes on only 2%–11% of the same samples (encoder–LLM gap 0.66–0.71 on every model). VSI is sample-intrinsic: its ranking transfers across models (grand-mean Spearman ρ=+0.40 over 15 model pairs, permutation p<10⁻³ on every pair). Diagnostically, VSI is strongest on multi-choice reasoning for capable models (MMStar math/science on Qwen2.5-VL-32B: AUROC 0.851/0.867 vs a typical max-prob of ~0.6–0.8) but is NOT a universal abstention signal — softmax max-probability wins 10/18 perceptual cells, and a VSI-containing hybrid wins 7/18 (e.g., Qwen2.5-VL-7B on MMVP: hybrid AUROC 0.676 vs max-prob 0.496, +0.18; VSI alone wins exactly 1/18).

## 核心贡献

- **提出"视觉不敏感差距"概念与 VSI 度量**：将 VLM 评测中"聚合准确率隐含假设模型使用了视觉输入"这一盲点显式化为逐样本测量——VSI(x,q;f) = KL(f(·|x,q) ‖ f(·|x_σ,q))，其中 x_σ 是对问题相关区域（Grounding-DINO 开放词表框 + SAM 掩码精化）做 σ=20 高斯模糊后的图像；KL 形式捕捉完整分布移动而非仅 argmax 翻转。
- **发现重尾分布的"不敏感子总体"**：6 模型 × 3 感知基准的每个格子里，VSI<0.05 的不敏感样本占 40%–97%（HallusionBench 最高，LLaVA-1.5 达 97%；POPE 最低）；整图模糊对照将其压到 ≈6%，证明是区域级（问题相关内容被忽略）而非"任何扰动都不敏感"的伪影。Hartigan dip 检验不拒绝单峰、silhouette 中等，故诚实描述为重左尾而非双峰。
- **证明不敏感性是样本内在属性**：15 对模型的 VSI 排序 grand-mean Spearman ρ=+0.40（POPE 均值 0.55 / MMVP 0.34 / HallusionBench 0.32），同家族对 0.51 vs 跨家族 0.37，最弱的一对（Qwen2.5-VL-32B vs Idefics3, MMVP）ρ=+0.20 仍达 p=3.4×10⁻⁴（10⁵ 次 shuffle）——跨家族模型不共享任何编码器权重，指向共同预训练范式而非具体架构。
- **机制定位：encoder–LLM 断连**：在 Qwen3-VL 标记的 83 个低 VSI 样本上，每个模型自己视觉塔的线性探针以 0.72–0.79 精度区分扰动/干净图像（冻结参考 CLIP 探针 0.82±0.04），而 argmax 仅 2%–11% 改变，gap=0.66–0.71 六模型全部复现；高 VSI 对照组探针升至 0.86–0.91 且 argmax 变化率随之上升，排除了"扰动太弱/贪心解码掩盖分布移动/固定视觉先验"三种替代解释——编码器看见了扰动，语言头没有传播它。
- **条件性信号选择地图**：18 个（模型，基准）格上 max-prob 赢 10 格（校准好时）、含 VSI 的混合信号赢 7 格（校准差或幻觉型失败时）、VSI 单独仅赢 1 格；VSI 与言语化置信度的 Pearson 相关 |r|<0.10（−0.08 至 +0.05），确认三者测量的是不同失败模式；明确定位 VSI 为"视觉忽略型自信错误"的条件性集成成分而非普适弃答信号。

## Key Contributions

- **The Visual Insensitivity Gap and the VSI metric**: a per-sample scalar, VSI(x,q;f)=KL(f(·|x,q)‖f(·|x_σ,q)), computed from the full next-token distribution shift under a question-conditioned region blur (Grounding-DINO open-vocabulary box + SAM mask, σ=20 default), turning the VQA language-prior observation ("models can answer without looking") into a per-sample, cross-model measurement.
- **A heavy left tail of insensitive samples on every tested cell**: the VSI<0.05 fraction spans 40%–97% across 6 models × 3 perceptual benchmarks (highest on HallusionBench, e.g., LLaVA-1.5 at 97%; lowest on POPE), while the whole-image-blur control collapses to ≈6% (region-blur 41.5%, patch-occlusion 42.0% on n=200, Qwen3-VL) — the tail is about question-relevant local content, not any-perturbation insensitivity. Hartigan's dip test does not reject unimodality, so the authors honestly describe it as a heavy tail, not bimodality.
- **Sample-intrinsicness with proper statistical grounding**: grand-mean Spearman ρ=+0.40 across all 15 model pairs (per-benchmark means: POPE 0.55, MMVP 0.34, HallusionBench 0.32; same-family 0.51 vs cross-family 0.37), with 10⁵-shuffle permutation tests on four representative pairs (ρ∈[+0.20,+0.55]; weakest pair p=3.4×10⁻⁴) and bootstrap CIs fully above zero — cross-family pairs share no encoder weights, implicating the shared contrastive-pretraining paradigm.
- **A concrete mechanism, the encoder–LLM disconnect**: on the shared n=83 low-VSI pool, linear probes on each model's own vision tower separate perturbed from clean images at 0.72–0.79 (reference frozen CLIP probe: 0.82±0.04), yet argmax changes on only 2%–11% of the same samples — gap 0.66–0.71 on all six models; on the high-VSI control the probes rise to 0.86–0.91 and argmax-change rises with them. This rules out too-weak perturbation, greedy-decoding masking, and uniform visual priors, and characterizes the failure as routing, not representation.
- **A conditional signal-selection map instead of a universal claim**: across the 18 perceptual cells, max-prob wins 10/18, a VSI-containing hybrid wins 7/18, VSI alone wins 1/18; VSI and verbalised confidence are nearly independent (Pearson r∈[−0.08,+0.05]); the confidently-wrong-while-ignoring-vision quadrant (25–30% of the bottom-VSI quintile on HallusionBench) is precisely the failure softmax-only abstention misses.

## Methodology

- **Perturbation construction**: the principal noun phrase is parsed from the question (lightweight parser, no model call), Grounding-DINO (base) returns an open-vocabulary box at confidence ≥0.3, SAM-ViT-base refines it to a pixel mask dilated by 4 px; samples without a confident box fall back to whole-image blur (fallback <3% on POPE/MMVP, ~19% on HallusionBench where chart questions defeat detection). Gaussian blur uses kernel 4σ+1; σ=20 default with σ∈{10,20,40} ablation.
- **VSI computation**: KL divergence between the top-50 next-token distributions after the answer-prefix tokens from the clean and perturbed forward passes (aligned support), greedy decoding throughout. A whole-image VSI variant isolates "does the model use images at all" from "does it use the queried content".
- **Grid**: six VLMs spanning three architecture families and four distinct vision towers (LLaVA-1.5/NeXT: CLIP-ViT-L/14; Idefics3: SigLIP-SO400M; Qwen3-VL and Qwen2.5-VL: natively trained ViTs) × POPE/MMVP/HallusionBench (region + whole image) + MMStar (whole image only, because its diagram layouts make region detection unreliable) — 42 (model, benchmark, perturbation) cells; the same raw sample pool is shown to every model, with accuracy metrics on the answer-parseable subset (n=93–500 per cell).
- **Encoder–decoder disconnect probe**: L2-regularized logistic regression on ℓ2-normalized final-layer features of each model's own tower, trained to discriminate perturbed vs unperturbed images with grouped 5-fold CV (both views of an image in the same fold); probed on the 83 samples flagged low-VSI by Qwen3-VL plus a matched high-VSI control; a frozen openai/clip-vit-large-patch14 probe serves as an external witness; an RBF variant adds ≤0.02, showing the signal is linearly decodable.
- **Diagnostic analyses**: bottom-VSI-quintile four-quadrant split by (correctness × softmax above/below cell median); selective-generation AUROC with error as positive class and negated scores so VSI/max-prob/verbalised confidence are directly comparable; PRR@80 (fraction of oracle risk reduction realized when rejecting the worst-ranked 20%); equal-weight z-score hybrids and a learned logistic variant; joint quintile error heatmaps for (VSI, max-prob) and (VSI, verbalised).
- **Robustness and stats**: σ ablation (AUROC swing ≤0.05; per-sample rank correlation across σ pairs 0.76–0.97); cutoff sweep {0.01,0.05,0.10,0.20} (direction invariant, quintile error-ratio moves ≤1.3×); 10⁵-shuffle permutation tests for cross-model agreement; B=1000 bootstrap CIs; seed 42; degenerate greedy outputs (e.g., Qwen2.5-VL-7B emitting "addCriterion" on 333/500 POPE samples) excluded rather than scored as errors. Total compute ≈32 A100-hours; code, per-sample CSVs, and a pinned environment (scikit-learn 1.8.0) released.

## Results & Highlights

- **Insensitivity is rampant and sample-intrinsic**: the VSI<0.05 fraction spans 40%–97% across all 18 (model, benchmark) perceptual cells (e.g., LLaVA-1.5 on HallusionBench 97%, Qwen3-VL on MMVP 40%), and VSI rankings transfer across models: grand-mean Spearman ρ=+0.40 over 15 pairs, with POPE mean 0.55 vs MMVP 0.34 and HallusionBench 0.32; even the weakest pair (Qwen2.5-VL-32B vs Idefics3 on MMVP) holds ρ=+0.20 against a permutation p=3.4×10⁻⁴.
- **The encoder sees what the decoder ignores**: on the shared n=83 low-VSI pool, own-tower linear-probe accuracy is 0.72–0.79 (Qwen3-VL 0.734±0.033, Qwen2.5-VL-7B 0.764±0.069, LLaVA-NeXT 0.789±0.040; reference CLIP probe 0.82±0.04) while the argmax-change rate is only 2%–11% — an encoder–LLM gap of 0.656–0.710 on every model — whereas on the high-VSI control the same probes reach 0.86–0.91 and argmax-change rises to 6–11%, so the gap closes exactly where the model uses vision.
- **Strongest diagnostic: MMStar reasoning categories on the capable model** — for Qwen2.5-VL-32B, AUROC_VSI = 0.851 (95% CI [0.73, 0.94], n=34) on math and 0.867 ([0.78, 0.95], n=46) on science & tech, with Pearson r(−VSI, err) of +0.47/+0.59, a bottom-vs-top-quintile error ratio of 4.05× on science, and zero errors in the math top quintile; but on the weakest model the signal inverts (LLaVA-1.5 math AUROC 0.29), so the claim is explicitly model- and category-conditional.
- **VSI is not a universal abstention signal — the honest headline**: softmax max-probability wins 10/18 perceptual cells (e.g., LLaVA-NeXT on POPE: max-prob AUROC 0.853 vs region-VSI 0.541), a VSI-containing hybrid wins 7/18, and VSI alone wins exactly 1/18 (LLaVA-1.5 on HallusionBench, whole-image VSI 0.58); where VSI does help, the gains are concrete: on Qwen2.5-VL-7B MMVP the hyb(r+w) signal reaches AUROC 0.676 vs max-prob 0.496 (+0.18), and on Qwen3-VL POPE 0.636 vs 0.544 (+0.09, paired bootstrap p<0.01).
- **The dangerous quadrant is what VSI uniquely targets**: on HallusionBench, confidently-wrong-while-ignoring-vision predictions make up 25–30% of the bottom-VSI quintile (LLaVA-NeXT largest, Qwen2.5-VL-32B smallest), and on LLaVA-NeXT HallusionBench the hybrid's PRR@80 is 0.19 vs max-prob −0.05 (the latter preferentially rejects correct predictions); the six low-VSI exemplars show all three baseline models (Qwen3-VL-8B, Qwen2.5-VL-7B, LLaVA-1.5-7B) simultaneously wrong with softmax >0.99 and VSI<0.01 (intersection n=11).
- **Quintile error signal is real but flips on visual-primitives benchmarks**: Qwen3-VL POPE bottom-VSI-quintile error 10.5% vs top-quintile 0.0% (monotone decrease), pooled POPE+MMVP ratio 2.33 (17.9% vs 7.7%), ~1.7 on POPE and ~1.0 on MMVP across the six-model grid, with Qwen3-VL MMVP inverted (bottom 15.0% vs top 25.0%).

## Authors & Credibility

- **Genpei Zhang, University of Wisconsin–Madison** — a solo-authored paper, which is unusual for a six-model empirical grid and means no internal co-author check; however, the methodological hygiene largely substitutes for it: pre-registered-style control conditions (high-VSI control pool, whole-image and patch-occlusion perturbation controls, frozen external CLIP witness probe), permutation tests with released null distributions, bootstrap CIs per cell, fixed seeds, pinned package versions (scikit-learn 1.8.0, with the cross-version sensitivity of GroupKFold quantified at ~0.01 probe accuracy), and release of per-sample CSVs alongside aggregate statistics.
- The paper is disciplined about scope in a way that builds trust: it repeatedly volunteers evidence against its own headline (VSI alone best in only 1/18 cells; max-prob wins 10/18; the MMVP inversion; dip-test non-rejection of unimodality), reports an uncorrected p=0.043 inversion explicitly flagged as uncorrected, and states it proposes no new model, training objective, or abstention method.
- Compute budget (~32 A100-hours on shared standby-QOS GPUs) is modest but appropriate for a measurement paper; exact checkpoint identifiers and revision hashes are pinned in the released code, aiding reproduction.

## Critical Assessment

- **The headline mechanism numbers rest on one model's sample selection.** The n=83 low-VSI pool is defined by Qwen3-VL's own flags, then probed on all six towers. The cross-model transfer result (§2.3) partially justifies this, but each per-model "gap" (0.656–0.710) is measured on a Qwen3-VL-selected subset rather than each model's own bottom quintile; a per-model self-selected pool would have been the cleaner design, and the paper does not report how much the gap numbers would change under that alternative.
- **Small-n cells carry the flashiest numbers.** The MMStar math/science AUROCs of 0.851/0.867 — the abstract's marquee diagnostic result — come from n=34 and n=46 with 95% CIs as wide as [0.73, 0.94]; the paper flags this, but the abstract does not convey that these are the least stable cells in the grid. No multiple-testing correction is applied across the 18 Table 3 cells (acknowledged as exploratory).
- **Qwen2.5-VL-7B's cells are computed on a non-random surviving third of the pool**: degenerate greedy decoding ("addCriterion") occurs on 333/500 POPE samples (67%) and 141/500 MMVP for Qwen3-VL (28%), and these are excluded rather than scored. Exclusion is defensible (scoring them as errors would conflate decoding pathology with grounding failure), but the resulting accuracy/AUROC cells for the most degenerate model are conditioned on a subset selected partly by the pathology itself — a selection effect the paper names but does not bound.
- **HallusionBench results are entangled with the region-detection pipeline's own weakness**: the ~19% whole-image fallback rate on chart questions and the weakest cross-model agreement (ρ=0.32) mean part of the measured "insensitivity" on this benchmark may be measurement noise from detection failure — the authors concede the benchmark "defeats" local-region blurring, which slightly undercuts the 97% headline fraction for LLaVA-1.5 on that benchmark.
- **"Tracks hallucination by every measure we examine" is softer than it sounds**: the hallucination link is operationalized as quadrant alignment in the bottom-VSI quintile (Appendix Fig. 4), not via dedicated hallucination metrics such as CHAIR; the claim is plausible but the paper never runs a direct hallucination scoring pipeline.
- **The actionable takeaway is thin by design**: VSI alone is the best signal in exactly 1/18 cells and the learned hybrid improves on equal weights by only +0.01–0.03 AUROC, so a practitioner gets mainly a diagnostic lens, not a deployable abstention method — consistent with the paper's own framing, but readers seeking a method should calibrate expectations.
- Genuine strengths offset these: the whole-image control (≈6% vs 41.5%), the high-VSI probe control (0.86–0.91), and the σ-robustness sweep (rank correlation 0.76–0.97 across σ) are exactly the controls a skeptical reviewer would demand, and they all support the core claim.

## Strengths

- **A crisp, well-controlled causal framing**: input-level intervention (remove the evidence, watch the softmax) directly operationalizes "would the answer change if this evidence were absent", and the probe-plus-counterfactual combination cleanly separates "information present" from "information used" — the high-VSI control where both probe accuracy and argmax-change rise is a model answer to the "probes are trivially easy" objection.
- **Cross-model generality with real statistics**: 15 model pairs across four distinct vision towers, permutation p-values, bootstrap CIs, and released null distributions elevate the sample-intrinsic claim well above anecdote; the honest decomposition (same-family 0.51 vs cross-family 0.37, benchmark-conditional means) resists overclaiming.
- **Rigorous negative results**: the paper demonstrates where its own signal loses (max-prob wins 10/18; the MMVP quintile inversion; LLaVA-1.5's inverse math AUROC 0.29) and builds its "conditional ensemble component" framing on that evidence rather than hiding it.
- **Reproducibility as a first-class concern**: per-sample CSVs, pinned seeds/versions with quantified cross-version sensitivity, exact checkpoints, and a compute budget small enough for an academic lab to re-run.
- **Timely positioning**: connects the VQA language-prior literature, CLIP-blindspot diagnosis (MMVP), register-token findings (Darcet et al.), and selective generation into one per-sample measurement, and explicitly delimits input-level causality from structural (attention/probing) explanations.

## Limitations

- **Benchmark-conditional agreement**: cross-model rank correlation ranges from 0.55 (POPE) down to 0.32 (HallusionBench), and the VSI–error relationship can invert (Qwen3-VL MMVP, p=0.043 uncorrected), so any deployed threshold needs per-cell calibration; the heavy left tail itself offers no natural threshold.
- **Input-output causality only**: VSI says nothing about where in the language head the routing fails — no intermediate activations, attention maps, or KV-cache analysis; activation patching across the cross-modal projection is left as future work.
- **Linear-probe lower bounds**: 0.72–0.79 is a linear-decodability floor on what the tower represents; nonlinear decodability (and thus the true information gap) could be larger, and only last-layer features were probed.
- **Verbalised-confidence fragility**: two prompt variants differ by up to 0.06 AUROC, and 4–11% of samples per cell yield no parseable integer, so the independence claims involving that baseline are template-specific.
- **Scope confined to next-token softmax and greedy decoding**: no sampling-based confidence, no chain-of-thought settings (where grounding decay has been reported), and MMStar restricted to whole-image VSI means perturbation semantics differ across benchmarks.
- **Practical deployment**: ~2.3 s per sample on Qwen2.5-VL-32B (0.7–1.1 s on 7B models) plus Grounding-DINO/SAM calls is cheap, but the 1/18 standalone win rate means VSI must be paired with softmax signals to be useful.

## Relevance

这篇论文与你的"LLM 隐状态与可解释性"方向高度契合：它是线性探针 + 输入级干预的组合范式，量化了"编码器表征里有、输出却不用"的逐样本断连，并给出了跨模型迁移的排序结构——这种"信息在场但未被路由"的测量思路对解读任何模态的 LLM 内部表征都有借鉴价值。需注意其主题属于模型侧幻觉/可靠性（你的 SDC 检测方向明确排除模型侧问题），故对推理可靠性程序本身相关度有限，但方法学（干预 + 探针 + 跨模型统计检验）值得纳入可解释性工具箱。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would the encoder–LLM gap numbers change materially if each model's low-VSI pool were selected by that model itself, rather than by Qwen3-VL's flags (the shared n=83 pool)?
- Does the gap persist under chain-of-thought decoding, where grounding-decay work (Raghu & Pandey 2026) suggests the answer trajectory may re-engage with vision after the first token?
- Can activation patching across the cross-modal projection localize which layers of the language head drop the perturbation signal, converting the input-output gap into a structural claim?
- Is the sample-intrinsic low-VSI sub-population predictable from image/question features alone (a cheap pre-hoc flag), or does it require a forward pass with perturbation?
- Would targeted retraining that up-weights the insensitive sub-population close the gap, or does it merely shift the tail — and would such fine-tuning degrade the cross-model rank agreement that makes VSI diagnostic?
- How does VSI behave on open-ended generation (HallusionBench max_tokens=64) versus the 8-token multiple-choice regime where distributional shifts more readily flip the argmax?
