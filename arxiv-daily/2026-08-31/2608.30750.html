Do VLMs Share Safety Neurons Across Modalities?

Avg. direct-harm refusal drop from ablating text vs visual neurons (8 models w/ confirmed visual neurons). Only 48–130 text neurons per model — yet 0–9 visual ones confirm; MMLU moves ≤0.3 pp.

Why this matters

Text-side alignment does not carry over to visual harm — so safety interventions must target visual representations directly, not just the LLM backbone.

Critical edge

The headline is partly baked in: eval intents were pre-filtered so ≥8/10 models refuse them as plain text — image-only-harm refusal goes unmeasured.

§01Methodology3-stage causal pipeline
  1. 10 VLMs, 6 families (2B–32B) — six adapted (vision encoder grafted onto a pretrained LLM) vs four natively multimodal (incl. Gemma-4-31B, GLM-4.1V-9B-Thinking). A neuron = (layer, component, channel) across MLP gate/up/out + self-attn.
  2. Behavioral probe — per layer-component l2 logistic regression separating harmful-refused from safe-complied activations; score = probe direction × Cohen’s d; ~10⁶ candidates → ~2,000 per model.
  3. Mean-activation patching — clamp each candidate to its safe-set mean (avoids zero-ablation OOD artifacts); 300 layer-stratified control neurons form the null; BH-FDR α = 0.05. Confirm = padj < 0.05, Δ > 0, signs agree.
  4. Iterative ablation × 3 — confirmed neurons clamped, probes refit, patching re-run — exposing self-repair backups single-pass detectors miss (round 1 finds only 38–78%).
  5. Modality-isolated benchmarks — ViSAFE-DETECT: 2,417 harmful images all paired with one fixed neutral procedural query (decouples image from text); ViSAFE-EVAL: 472 intents × 8 image conditions × 3 query types ≈ 11,300 samples/model, disjoint image sources for genuine OOD.
  6. GEVD subspace sweep — k = 1…100 directions projected out, against a random-direction null (5 seeds).

  • Capability survives: Dolly perplexity within 2% for 7/10 models, MMLU ≤0.3 pp, MMMU ±1.1% — safety-specific, not general incapacitation.
  • Visual scan comes up near-empty: a strictly larger search budget (4 components vs 3) still confirms only 40 neurons total across 10 models — two models confirm zero. Largest visual OOD effect −6.3 pp (InternVL3.5-14B) vs −11.5 for its own text neurons; signs are mixed (+3.7 on Qwen3-VL-2B).
  • Refusal tracks the text channel, not the image: harmful image + benign query → refusal <26% (9/10 models); rendered text in image → 10–60% (OCR re-arms the text pathway); under the neutral query, natural harmful images elicit 10–93% vs 61–100% for rendered harmful text.
  • Subspace geometry diverges: the text effect saturates at ~5 GEVD directions (k = 100 adds ~2 pp); the visual effect keeps growing through k = 50 (~55 pp on Qwen2.5-VL-7B) and deepens vs the random null (−22 → −28).
  • Localizability ≠ scale: 32B confirms 73 neurons vs 8B’s 92 — a property of alignment strategy, not parameter count.

+Strengths

  • Breadth with balance: 10 models, 6 families, both adaptation paradigms — the text/visual asymmetry is a property of post-fusion LLM backbones, not one lab’s recipe.
  • Statistically careful causality: mean-activation patching over zero-ablation; 300-neuron control null + BH-FDR makes “confirmed neuron” falsifiable; iterative rounds recover self-repair backups.
  • Modality-isolated design: one fixed neutral query decouples image from text; the 8×3 factorial attributes refusal to image, query, and interaction; disjoint image sources make evaluation genuinely OOD.
  • Honest side-effect accounting: PPL/MMLU/MMMU checked with every ablation; RR-Judge vs keyword ratio (0.09–1.88) quantifies judge dependence.
  • Actionable: motivates applying representation engineering / circuit breakers directly to visual representations.

−Limitations

  • Only the LLM backbone is examined — vision encoders, projection layers, cross-token routing, and multi-neuron interactions are untested, so safety-specific localization vs general visual dimensionality is indistinguishable.
  • BeaverTails-V supplies 82.5% of harmful images; synthetic/AI-generated harm nearly absent — the “visual safety is diffuse” claim rests on one source’s notion of harm.
  • The 472 intents were selected for ≥8/10 text-refusal consensus — conclusions hold in the refusal-consensus regime, not where harm is purely visual or models are uncertain.
  • Single judge (GPT-4o RR-Judge): absolute refusal rates inherit its biases, even if paired contrasts stay comparable.
  • No neuron coordinates, no ablation code, no full benchmark release — independent replication must rebuild everything. Two models (Gemma >90%, GLM 5.7% probe refusal) give uninformative, not negative, visual results.
§03Questions for further exploration
  1. Does visual safety localize in the vision encoder or cross-modal projection layers — is the diffuseness a property of where the authors searched, or of where the mechanism lives?
  2. Would text neurons still transfer OOD without the text-refusal pre-filter — e.g., on purely pictorial, OCR-proof harm?
  3. Is the ~5-vs-≥50 direction geometry stable under causal rather than discriminative direction estimates, and does the visual subspace align with known jailbreak directions?
  4. Can the mixed visual OOD signs (+3.7) be explained by self-repair dynamics, or by visual neurons modulating strictness rather than refusal?
  5. Do alignment recipes trained directly on multimodal refusal data (e.g., Safe RLHF-V) localize the visual circuit, or does diffuseness persist across all 6 families?
