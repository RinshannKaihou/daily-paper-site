> **一句话总结：** 针对 VLM 自我改进网页代码时"提议者即裁判"的结构性缺陷，提出 WebWorld 接口：把浏览器当作确定性的"网页世界模型"，VLM 的批判被编译为类型化交互契约（目标谓词 + 重放动作 + 继承的保持谓词），浏览器重执行候补补丁后只有同时证明"目标进展 + 此前能力保持"才签发接受证书，仅证书化转移（3.28 万条）进入 SFT——27B 模型在 HTMLBench-400 上比 Raw SFT 提升 5.3 分（52.7 vs 47.4）、OOD 的 MiniAppBench-Val 提升 14.9 分（85.5 vs 70.6），消融证明去掉浏览器证书门槛后增益坍缩到仅 +0.4。

> **TL;DR:** VLM self-improvement loops for web code fail because the model that proposes a repair also judges it by screenshot. WebWorld makes the browser — a deterministic, executable simulator of HTML behavior — the unfoolable counterparty: a free-form VLM critique is compiled into a typed interaction contract (target predicate, replay action trace, inherited preserve-set), and the browser re-executes each candidate patch, issuing an acceptance certificate only if target progress holds AND every previously verified capability is preserved. Only certified transitions (32,800 of ~60K candidates; 24,350 refused) enter the SFT export. Under a matched Qwen3.5 recipe, WebWorld-27B beats Raw-27B by +5.3 on HTMLBench-400 (52.7 vs 47.4) and +14.9 on MiniAppBench-Val (85.5 vs 70.6), matching Kimi-K2.6 (85.5) and nearing GPT-5.4 (87.7) on MiniApp; a certificate-free ablation at 9B collapses to +0.4 over Raw (with test-case pass dropping 3.1 points below Raw), isolating browser admission as the load-bearing component.

## 核心贡献

- **结构性诊断 + 换掉裁判**：指出 VLM 批判-重写循环的病灶是"提议者即裁判"——视觉上更漂亮的截图恰是行为性失败时最错误的判据；提出把浏览器（HTML 伪影在用户操作下行为的确定性可执行模拟器）直接当作"无需训练的世界模型"，是 VLM 无法欺骗的对手方。
- **三个耦合工件构成接口**：(1) 交互契约——planner 把自由文本批判编译为类型化三元组（目标谓词 / 以稳定选择器锚定的重放动作序列 / 从历史轮次继承的保持谓词），锚定在宽泛容器或纯感知描述的批判在入口即被拒；(2) 接受证书——按优先级尝试五级证明（同轨迹重放 42.3% > 能力增益 22.5% > 目标进展 16.4% > 局部视觉证据 11.5% > 静态结构修复 7.3%），每份证书绑定唯一契约、可离线复现；(3) 质量棘轮——ITERSTEP（重渲染、探针、被拒候选）只维持探索，QUALITY STEP（证书化转移）才能扩充已验证能力集合 P_t 并进入 SFT 导出，单调不回退。
- **构建即成立的四性质**：假设-证明分离（VLM 只提议、浏览器才准入）、类型化主张（无纯感知判断）、单调能力记忆（后续补丁若破坏已验证控件则在下一轮被拒）、确定性准入（每次接受/拒绝可离线重放）。
- **机制级因果证据**：在骨干、配方、预算全部对齐的前提下做等规模接受规则消融（4B/9B/27B），并用固定 5K 样本、变化最小证书深度的诊断证明"越深的轨迹监督密度越高"（depth≥5 的 5K 样本达到 48.8 分，与全量 3.28 万样本的 49.3 分只差 0.5）。

## Key Contributions

- Identifies the proposer-judge conflation as the structural flaw of VLM critique-and-rewrite loops for web code, and resolves it without training a world model: the browser already is one — a deterministic, executable simulator of how an HTML artifact behaves under user actions.
- Designs the interface as three coupled artifacts: a typed interaction contract (compiled by a deterministic planner from the free-form VLM critique: target predicate + selector-anchored replay action trace + inherited preserve predicates), an acceptance certificate issued by re-execution under a five-level proof taxonomy, and a quality ratchet that admits only certified transitions into both the monotone verified-capability set P_t and the SFT export.
- Four properties hold by construction: hypothesis–proof separation, typed claims (no perceptual-only judgments), monotone capability memory (regressions on previously certified controls are rejected at the next round), and deterministic admission reproducible offline.
- Provides mechanism-level causal evidence under fully matched backbones, recipes, and budgets: equal-size acceptance-rule ablations at 4B/9B/27B plus a depth diagnostic (fixed 5K examples, varying minimum certified depth) showing deeper trajectories carry proportionally more verified capability per example.

## Methodology

The loop models self-improvement as a sequence of browser-verified state transitions. At round t, the runtime renders the current artifact in headless Chromium/Playwright, drives it through 14 deterministic interaction probes (click, drag, keyboard, form fill, hover, card flip, SVG animation, etc.), and records screenshots, DOM/console state, probe outcomes, and the action trace. The VLM reads this observation (not the source code) and emits a structured critique that must name an issue family and point at recorded evidence — "make it more polished" is invalid. A deterministic planner compiles the critique into an interaction contract r_t; contracts that cannot in principle be certified (broad containers, perception-only claims, DOM predicates with no screenshot-observable consequence) are refused at admission. A typed repair router maps the issue family to one of 20 skills (15 VLM lanes grouped by gameplay/control-wiring/SVG/structural/visual/maintenance, plus 5 deterministic patch lanes for mechanical fixes); the skill's patch diff is bounded by the contract's impact scope, and a pre-execution filter discards candidates that cannot move the contract forward.

The browser then re-executes the candidate and either issues an acceptance certificate or a typed refusal (partial progress, visual-only, preserve-risk, stale replay). Proof levels are tried in priority order: same-trace replay → capability gain (a previously failing probe macro now passes) → target-issue progress → localized visual evidence (diff and visual change both inside the impact scope) → static structural repair. Admission as a QUALITY STEP requires both TARGET(r_t, x̂_{t+1})=1 and preservation of all p ∈ P_t; accepted transitions extend P_t, and the SFT export D_SFT = {(q, o_t, c_t, r_t, x_{t+1}, e_t)} contains only certificate-backed transitions, each pairing an artifact with the executed failure it addressed. Training: Qwen3.5 backbones at 4B/9B/27B, AdamW (lr 2e-5, wd 0.01, 3% warmup), effective batch 128, seq len 16,384, 3 epochs, 64×H100 (4B/9B) and 128×H100 (27B); every reported cell is a single seed-42 run. Data: ~60K admitted candidates → 24,350 refused → 42,860 certified → 32,800 uniformly subsampled (seed 42) to match the Raw budget. Evaluation on HTMLBench-400 (400 prompts, 6,000 frozen deterministic browser test cases; only the visual-design dimension is VLM-scored) and MiniAppBench-Val as OOD transfer, with a five-condition leakage audit (item, prompt-template, test-case, probe-set, and repair-skill disjointness).

## Results & Highlights

- **Main SFT comparison (Table 1):** On HTMLBench-400, WebWorld-27B reaches 52.7 score and 43.2% test-case pass vs Raw-27B's 47.4 and 33.5% (+5.3 score, +9.7 TC pass); on MiniAppBench-Val it reaches 85.5 vs 70.6 (+14.9). The lift widens with capacity: +3.4 at 4B (46.7 vs 43.3), +4.8 at 9B (49.3 vs 44.5), +5.3 at 27B. As stated benchmark context, 52.7 exceeds Kimi-K2.6 (49.8) and GPT-5.4 (49.2) on HTMLBench-400, and the 85.5 MiniApp average matches Kimi-K2.6 (85.5) and stays close to GPT-5.4 (87.7) and GLM-5.1 (87.8).
- **The gain is functional, not cosmetic:** Rendering, Visual, and Code dimensions move by less than a point in either direction across all scales, while the lift concentrates on TC pass and Functionality — the dimensions the certificate gate actually constrains. On MiniAppBench-Val, transfer improves by 10.5 to 14.9 points across 4B–27B. Notably, their own table shows Raw-27B SFT *degrades* OOD transfer relative to the un-tuned Qwen3.5-27B base (70.6 vs 76.5), whereas WebWorld-27B lifts it to 85.5.
- **Certificate ablation (Table 2, equal-size 9B routes):** No certificate +0.4 over Raw-9B only — and its TC pass drops 3.1 points *below* Raw (31.0 vs 34.1), i.e., removing the world-model oracle injects actively harmful supervision. No-preserve +1.5, VLM-only +3.5, Score gate +3.6, full WebWorld gate +4.8; the full gate also beats the partial rules by 6.6–8.7 TC-pass points. Partial substitutes still trail by 1.2–3.4 score points.
- **Depth diagnostic (Table 4, fixed 5K examples):** HTMLBench rises monotonically with minimum certified depth — 43.4 (≥1), 45.2 (≥2), 46.8 (≥3), 48.8 (≥5) — and plateaus at 48.5 for ≥8; 5K depth-≥5 examples come within 0.5 point of the full 32,800-example WebWorld-9B (49.3), confirming depth-filtered trajectories are denser supervision.
- **Behavioral failure reduction (Figures 6–7):** In the gate accounting snapshot, 113 preserve-fail and 18 weak-goal candidates are rejected vs 59 exported; after verified repair, concrete GUI failure rates fall by ~16–29 percentage points per category (e.g., console errors 41%→12%, dead controls 38%→15%, keyboard failures 31%→9%), while preserve regressions stay near zero. Accepted-repair rounds (Figure 4) show hard cases peaking at round 6 — a one-shot loop would never see the late accepts.
- **Data funnel (Figure 3 / Appendix D):** ~60K candidates → 42,860 certified (refusals typed, not silent) → 32,800 exported; same-trace replay dominates the certified pool at 42.3%, static structural repair is rarest at 7.3%.

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty.** The core idea — execution as the unfoolable oracle for self-generated training data — is an old and good one; the paper itself concedes the analogy ("SWE-bench works because the test suite is an oracle the patch cannot rewrite"). "Browser as a world model" is partly branding over execution-based verification. The genuinely new pieces are the packaging: compiling free-form critiques into typed contracts with inherited preserve-sets, the five-level proof taxonomy, the monotone ratchet as an SFT data-selection rule, and the demonstration that acceptance-rule identity (not data volume) carries the gain. That packaging is well executed but incremental relative to execution-feedback self-training (Self-Debugging, CodeRL, Explorer).

**Rigor concerns.** Every cell in Tables 1–4 is a single seed-42 run with no variance reported. At 4B the headline delta is +3.4 points — small-model SFT seed variance can plausibly be of that order, so the scale trend claim ("gap widens with capacity") rests on three unreplicated points. The strongest-sounding claim — a 27B SFT model beating GPT-5.4 and Kimi-K2.6 on HTMLBench-400 — is carefully framed in Section 3.2 as "benchmark context rather than a controlled model comparison," yet the abstract leans on it ("reaches the level of strong frontier systems"), and the primary benchmark comes from the same group's concurrent HTMLCure work; MiniAppBench-Val is external but the authors do not evaluate on any fully independent third-party benchmark (WebGen-Bench, FullFront, ArtifactsBench are cited but unused). The critic/proposer VLM is never named — Appendix C specifies decoding temperatures but not which model emits critiques, a real reproducibility gap for the pipeline that generates 60K candidates.

**Missing ablations and baseline quirks.** The 20-skill router is never ablated; the claim that skills affect "which contracts get patched, not which get certified" is asserted, not measured. The Raw baseline is also weak in an informative way: Raw-27B scores 70.6 on MiniApp vs the un-tuned Qwen3.5-27B base's 76.5, meaning raw-HTML SFT actively damages OOD interactive transfer — so roughly half of the +14.9 MiniApp headline is recovery from self-inflicted damage rather than pure gain (though +9.0 over base still stands). No RL comparison (e.g., execution-reward GRPO) is attempted, leaving open whether the certificate gate would matter as much under RL. To the authors' credit, they report genuinely unfavorable results: the NoCertificate route not only loses the gain but drops TC pass 3.1 points below Raw, and they surface the Raw-hurts-transfer pattern in their own table rather than hiding it.

**Significance.** If the mechanism holds up under independent replication, the transferable insight — "most of what a self-critique loop calls progress is noise unless something outside the VLM admits it" — applies well beyond HTML, to any self-improvement domain that has a cheap deterministic simulator. The honest cost accounting (Figure 12: verified repair buys ~90% accepted precision at higher relative cost) makes the trade-off explicit.

## Strengths

- Clean causal design: backbone family, optimizer, LR schedule, prompt template, and evaluation runner fixed across all routes; acceptance-rule ablations are equal-size and budget-matched, isolating the gate as the changing factor.
- Rarely-seen negative-result honesty: the certificate-free ablation actively hurts TC pass (-3.1 below Raw), and the Raw-hurts-MiniApp-transfer pattern is visible in their own main table.
- The gate is deterministic and offline-replayable: every accept/reject decision packages the executed evidence (screenshots, action trace, probe outcomes) in the certificate, making audits mechanical rather than subjective.
- Typed refusals turn rejections into diagnostics that feed the next critique while never contaminating the export pool — a sensible exploration/supervision split.
- Five-condition leakage audit against the benchmark they share infrastructure with, plus a held-out external benchmark (MiniAppBench-Val) for the transfer claim.
- The depth diagnostic (5K depth-≥5 examples ≈ full 32.8K pool) is a practically useful finding for data-budget-constrained training.

## Limitations

- Scope is single-file interactive HTML only; multi-file and framework-heavy projects add dependency replay, project organization, and scaffolding variables the certificate machinery does not yet handle (acknowledged in the Limitations section).
- Genuinely perceptual defects — aesthetic taste, brand identity, accessibility nuance — have no executable certificate and fall back to the VLM proxy, inheriting exactly the noise the method criticizes; the paper does not quantify how much of the corpus falls in this category.
- The browser must be a faithful deterministic oracle: dynamic applications with server state, timers, or randomness still need deterministic replay, and the paper offers no solution beyond noting the requirement.
- A wrong critique still passes contract admission and wastes a full repair round (the planner checks certifiability in principle, not correctness); there is no critique-level verifier.
- Cost: verified repair sits above filtering/one-shot baselines in Figure 12's cost-precision space, and routes consume 1,536–6,144 H20-equivalent card-hours each — the price of clean supervision is nontrivial.
- Single-seed training runs throughout; no variance, no confidence intervals, and no replication of the scale-trend claim.

## Relevance

这篇论文与你最高优先级的 Self-Evolving Agents & Automated Research 方向直接相关：它给出了一条"自生成数据必须经外部可执行oracle认证才能成为监督信号"的自改进闭环设计，并用等规模消融证明准入规则（而非数据量）承载全部增益——这正是长时程自主智能体系统的可靠性与可信监督问题的具体案例。其"假设-证明分离 + 单调棘轮"的模式对任何拥有廉价确定性模拟器的自进化场景（包括自动化实验与基准设计）都有直接借鉴价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would the certificate gate still carry the gain under RL (e.g., execution-reward GRPO) where the policy can learn from refusals directly, or is the ratchet specifically an SFT-data-selection phenomenon?
- How large is the seed variance of the 4B/9B deltas? Three seeds per route would settle whether the "gap widens with capacity" trend is real.
- What fraction of the certified pool relies on the VLM proxy (perceptual defects with no executable proof level), and how does training on that subset compare to training only on replay/capability-gain certificates?
- Can the contract grammar extend to multi-file, framework-heavy codebases where the "world model" must include build tooling and dependency replay?
- Since the critic VLM is unnamed in the paper: how sensitive is the certified-pool composition to the proposer's capability — does a weaker critic starve the gate, or does the browser gate make proposer quality mostly irrelevant?
- Does the preserve-set ratchet scale to hundreds of inherited predicates per page, or does it over-constrain legitimate refactors that intentionally replace a verified control with a better one?
