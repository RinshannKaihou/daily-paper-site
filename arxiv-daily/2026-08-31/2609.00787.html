StudyBench: Can Self-Evolution Squeeze Textbooks for Olympiad Capability?

Yinghao Chen, Zixi Chen, Bingxiang He, et al. · Tsinghua / Zhejiang · 2026-08-31

  • Self-Evolution
  • Benchmark
  • Knowledge Internalisation
  • Physics Olympiad
Key metric
7.04/ 100 ceiling

Best method (GEPA) reaches Transfer Par@8 of 7.04 vs the 100 Par@8 in-context guidance ceiling on the same material. On textbook exercises it more than doubles Par@8 (17.05 → 34.85) — yet transfer barely moves.

Compute Plateau: all five self-evolution loops saturate between 8.12 and 614 GPU-hours — a 76× range — with extra compute buying only a few noisy points.

Why this matters

Gives self-evolving-agent research an honest yardstick: a reusable Guidance Gap metric and hard evidence that current self-evolution loops stall early.

Critical edge

The 100-Par@8 ceiling is partly tautological: it compares solo skill against a full inference-time retrieval-and-guidance system, not against the raw material alone.

Methodology

  1. Three-layer corpus

    11 canonical physics textbooks → raw Corpus (817 chapters, ~6.0M tokens) → Instructions-without-Answer (646 pairs) → Instructions-with-Answer (1,420 pairs). Each of the 7 methods consumes whichever layer fits its paradigm.

  2. Two-tier test sets

    Application Set: 88 textbook parents (109 sub-problems) that Qwen3-8B fails at pass@8 — absorption. Transfer Set: 90 olympiad parents (280 sub-problems, six international olympiads) — transfer.

  3. Reachability ceiling

    A DeepSeek V4 Pro teacher decomposes each gold solution, retrieves textbook fragments (BM25 + dense, RRF-fused), verifies coverage, and writes answer-free guidance; a problem is admitted only if Qwen3-8B solves it under guidance — certifying a 100 Par@8 ceiling. An independent GLM-5.1 teacher still unlocks 62.22 Par@8.

  4. Contamination & protocol

    Retained statements/solutions physically redacted from the corpus; 40-character anchor audit finds zero verbatim residue. Identical items and protocol for all methods; Par@k and Sub@k with k=8, 3 seeds (Opus 4.7: k=1); two-stage rule+LLM verifier, with only the high-precision rule stage exposed as RL reward.

Seven methods (Bonito, GRPO, GEPA, ACE, TTRL, Intuitor, R-Zero) × three base models (Qwen3-8B, Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct, Opus 4.7) on one 8×A800 node · compute-normalised training curves from checkpoint snapshots.

Results

  • Absorption is easy; transfer is not: every method except Bonito gains +8.87 to +14.98 Application Sub@8 (base 29.36), yet Transfer Par@8 stays single-digit against a +43.57 Sub@8 guided ceiling.
  • Bonito is the outlier in the wrong direction: Transfer Sub@8 −21.43 (35.00 vs 56.43) — synthetic-data SFT erodes the thinking behaviour needed to even follow guidance (guided Par@8 collapses to 23.33).
  • Cross-model: Llama-3.2-3B barely moves on Application (peak +2.45 Sub@8) while supervised GRPO leads Transfer there (+7.86). On Opus 4.7, prompts/playbooks evolved for weaker models mostly regress Application Par@1 (GEPA −8.26 vs base 59.09).
  • Compute Plateau: ACE jumps to ~40% by its 8.5 GPU-hour snapshot and never leaves the 38–41% band through 62.5; GEPA hits 44.3% by 30.5 and stays flat through 112.5; R-Zero spends 495 GPU-hours to peak near 42% then ends at 39.14 after 614. The highest plateau is not the most expensive run.

Under construction-time guidance injected at inference, non-SFT methods sit at 88.89–90.00 guided Par@8 — a parent-level gap of ~83–86 points that GEPA’s solo 7.04 closes by ~7%.

Strengths

  • The Guidance Gap converts philosophy into a number: “does self-evolution internalise or just absorb?” becomes measurable, with absorption and transfer cleanly separated.
  • Properties enforced by construction: items filtered against the base model, every Transfer item carries a recombined-from-material witness trace, items/protocol fixed across methods.
  • Dual-teacher reachability check (GLM-5.1 traces still unlock 62.22 Par@8) — the kind of adversarial robustness check benchmarks rarely include.
  • Two-axis contamination control with an auditable trail: zero verbatim anchor matches; redaction markers for every retained parent.
  • Honest, decision-relevant negative results across a 76× compute range — findings, not marketing. Code released (github.com/thunlp/StudyBench).

Limitations

  • Physics-only instantiation over 11 textbooks; the domain-agnostic claim is plausible but unverified.
  • Small test sets (88 + 90 parents) make Par@8 granularity ~1.1 points per parent; GEPA’s headline 7.04±2.80 rests on ~6–7 parents.
  • Qwen3-8B-anchored: the capability-gap and 100% ceiling guarantees break for Llama-3.2-3B (guidance ceiling only 10.74 Par@8) and Opus 4.7 (already solves 40.00 Transfer Par@1 solo).
  • Thin statistics in places: the Opus table is a single k=1 run with no error bar; four of five plateau curves are single-seed.
  • Verifier blind spot: the rule-based RL reward (precision 1, recall 29.65% vs the cascade) may systematically under-credit equivalent correct answers, deflating RL scores.
  • Corpus layer not redistributed (copyright); Bonito is a near-strawman for the Corpus layer, so “Corpus methods fail” is not established in general.

Questions for further exploration

  • Does closing the Guidance Gap require internalising the guidance procedure (decompose–retrieve–guide) rather than its conclusions — with retrieval available at test time as a fair baseline?
  • Are the 38% of Transfer parents unreachable under GLM-5.1 guidance genuinely unreachable, or is the five-step pipeline the bottleneck? A human-expert witness would settle it.
  • Would a retrieval-augmented or scaffolded variant of GEPA/ACE on Opus 4.7 recover part of the gap without weight updates — and how should the benchmark credit it?
  • How does the rule-based judge’s 29.65% recall bias GRPO/TTRL training? Would the full cascade as reward change Transfer numbers?
  • Do prompt-evolution gains vanish because evolved prompts overfit to textbook phrasing? Cross-domain validation could separate phrasing overfit from genuine absorption.
  • Does a wall-clock-normalised multi-seed study change the Compute Plateau conclusion, especially for R-Zero’s 614 GPU-hour trajectory?
