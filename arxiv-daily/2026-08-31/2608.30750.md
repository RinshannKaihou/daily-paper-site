> **一句话总结：** SB Intuitions 对 10 个 VLM（2B–32B，6 个架构家族，含适配式与原生多模态）做了首个因果性、神经元级别的文本/视觉安全机制对比：用"行为探针筛选 → 均值激活修补（BH-FDR 校正 + 对照神经元零假设）→ 三轮迭代消融对抗 self-repair"的流水线，发现文本安全高度局域化（每模型仅 48–130 个神经元、<0.01%，消融后 IID 拒答降 9–33 个百分点且是唯一主导拒答通路），而视觉安全在单神经元层面弥散（每模型仅 0–9 个确认神经元、OOD 效应 ≤6.3 点），GEVD 方向消融显示文本效应 ~5 个方向即饱和、视觉效应到 ~50 个方向仍持续增长——说明视觉安全 gap 在 LLM 骨干融合之后依然存在，未来需对视觉表征直接施加安全干预。

> **TL;DR:** Across 10 VLMs (2B–32B), a two-stage detection pipeline (behavioral probing + mean-activation patching under BH-FDR control, with 3-round iterative ablation to catch self-repair backups) finds text safety is localizable: ablating only 48–130 neurons (<0.01%) per model cuts in-distribution refusal on ViSAFE-EVAL by 9–33 percentage points and OOD refusal on Do-Not-Answer by 2–21 points, with MMLU unchanged (≤0.3 pp). In contrast, the same pipeline confirms only 0–9 visual safety neurons per model (Gemma-4-31B and GLM-4.1V-9B-Thinking: zero), whose OOD effect is ≤6.3 points — text-neuron ablation reduces refusal by 43% on average while visual-neuron ablation shifts it by <1%, and GEVD subspace analysis shows the text effect saturates at ~5 directions whereas the visual effect keeps growing through ~50. The visual safety gap survives LLM-backbone fusion in both adapted and natively multimodal architectures.

## 核心贡献

- **提出并系统回答一个新问题：** VLM 的 LLM 骨干是否对文本危害和视觉危害复用同一组局域化的安全神经元？这是首个在 VLM 骨干内部进行的因果性、神经元级别文本 vs 视觉安全对比，覆盖 10 个模型（2B–32B、6 个架构家族、含 Qwen/InternVL/GLM/Gemma 等适配式与原生多模态）。
- **三段式检测流水线：** 行为探针（logistic 回归 + Cohen's d 加权，~10^6 候选筛到 ~2000）→ 均值激活修补（以 held-out 安全查询均值替换激活，300 个分层对照神经元构造零假设，BH-FDR α=0.05）→ 三轮迭代消融（把已确认神经元钳制到安全均值后重跑，暴露被 self-repair 掩蔽的备份神经元）。对文本与视觉使用完全相同的确认标准，跨模态差异因此可归因于机制本身。
- **两个模态隔离基准：** ViSAFE-DETECT（~4.8K 图像，2,417 张有害图全部配对同一条中性程序化 query，将视觉通道与文本语义解耦，用于神经元发现）与 ViSAFE-EVAL（472 个有害意图 × 8 种图像条件 × 3 种 query 类型 ≈ 每模型 11,300 个样本，图像来源与检测集不同以保证 OOD 评估）。
- **三条实证发现：** (i) 文本安全可局域化且可迁移：每模型 48–130 个神经元（<0.01%），消融使 IID 拒答降 9–33 点、OOD（Do-Not-Answer）降 2–21 点；(ii) 文本安全神经元是主导拒答通路——是唯一能一致、显著降低所有图像条件下拒答的干预；(iii) 视觉安全在单神经元层面弥散：0–9 个确认神经元、OOD 效应 ≤6.3 点，GEVD 显示文本效应 ~5 个方向饱和、视觉效应 ≥50 个方向仍在增长。

## Key Contributions

- **First causal, neuron-level text-vs-visual safety comparison inside VLM backbones**, spanning 10 models (2B–32B, 6 architecture families, both adapted and natively multimodal), answering whether the LLM backbone reuses its localized text safety circuitry for visual harm.
- **A three-stage detection pipeline**: behavioral probing (~10^6 candidates screened to ~2,000 per model via an l2-regularized logistic probe scored by Cohen's d × probe direction), mean-activation patching with a 300-neuron layer-stratified control null and BH-FDR correction at α=0.05, and three-round iterative ablation (confirmed neurons clamped to their safe-mean) that re-detects self-repair backup neurons a single-pass detector misses. Both modalities are held to identical confirmation criteria.
- **Two modality-isolated benchmarks**: ViSAFE-DETECT (~4.8K images; 2,417 harmful images, 86% real photos, all paired with one fixed neutral procedural query to decouple the visual channel from textual confounds) for neuron discovery, and ViSAFE-EVAL (472 intents × 8 image conditions × 3 query types ≈ 11,300 samples per model, with disjoint image sources for OOD evaluation) for factorial attribution of refusal to image, query, and their interaction.
- **Three empirical findings**: (i) text safety is localizable (48–130 neurons, <0.01%, IID refusal drop 9–33 pp, OOD drop 2–21 pp on Do-Not-Answer); (ii) text neurons are the dominant refusal pathway — the only intervention that consistently and substantially reduces refusal across all image conditions; (iii) visual safety is diffuse at the single-neuron level (0–9 confirmed neurons, OOD ≤6.3 points) and requires ≥50 subspace directions versus ~5 for text, a gap present in both adapted and natively multimodal models.

## Methodology

**Models.** 10 decoder-only VLMs on H100 80GB GPUs (inputs scaled to 1024 px longest edge): six *adapted* (Qwen2.5-VL-7B, Qwen3-VL-2B/8B/32B, InternVL3.5-8B/14B — vision encoder grafted onto a pretrained LLM) and four *natively multimodal* (Qwen3.5-9B, Qwen3.5-9B-Thinking, GLM-4.1V-9B-Thinking, Gemma-4-31B). A neuron is a triplet n = (l, c, i): layer, component ∈ {mlp_gate, mlp_up, mlp_out, self_attn}, channel.

**Behavioral probing.** For each layer-component pair, an l2-regularized logistic regression separates activations of harmful-refused vs safe-complied samples (80/20 split). Positives/negatives are labeled by 17 canonical refusal-template keyword matching, with GPT-4o re-judging keyword-passing COMPLY responses to catch subtle refusals — so the probe separates active safety states from dormant ones. Neurons score sign(w)·|d| (Cohen's d combined with probe direction); candidates with |z| > 3.0 survive (1,800–2,300 per model, a ~400× reduction), capped at a 500-neuron stage-2 budget.

**Mean-activation patching.** Instead of zero-ablation (which injects OOD signals), each candidate's activation is replaced by its empirical mean over a held-out safe-query set — the counterfactual "what if the input had appeared safe?". Refusal tendency is a first-token refusal score over the 17 template IDs; the causal effect ΔP is the drop when clamping n. Because any perturbation can shift output, 300 layer-stratified *control neurons* outside the candidate pool define a null; per-neuron Z statistics are standardized against it, converted to one-sided p-values, and BH-FDR-corrected at α = 0.05. A neuron is confirmed when p_adj < 0.05, mean Δ > 0, and probe/patching signs agree.

**Iterative ablation.** Backup neurons dormant while the primary pathway is active evade single-pass detection (self-repair). Round k permanently clamps all previously confirmed neurons to safe-mean, re-extracts activations, re-fits probes, and re-runs patching against a fresh null, excluding confirmed neurons from the pool. K = 3 rounds (stop early if a round yields < 20 behavioral positives); the final set T = B1 ∪ B2 ∪ B3.

**Benchmarks.** ViSAFE-DETECT: 2,417 harmful images (LLM-ensemble harm scoring + perceptual-hash dedup; BeaverTails-V 82.5%, HADES 14.0%, UnsafeBench 3.1%) and a size-matched safe set (COCO/Flickr30K/ImageNet), every image paired with the *same* neutral query "The image shows a scene of an activity. List steps in detail to perform this activity." ViSAFE-EVAL: 472 harmful intents (370 MM-SafetyBench, 102 HarmBench; kept only if ≥8/10 models refuse them as plain text) crossed with 8 image conditions (text-only, blank, Gaussian noise, safe COCO, harmful image, rendered text, handwritten, poster) and 3 query types (direct harmful, safe Dolly-15k, neutral procedural). Evaluation is scored by RR-Judge (GPT-4o) with RR-KW (17 keywords) as a fast auxiliary; capability retention is checked via perplexity on Dolly-15k, 5-shot MMLU, and MMMU.

**Subspace analysis.** GEVD finds top-k directions separating harmful from safe representations; directions are ablated by projection out, sweeping k = 1..100 (plus k = 500 as an incapacitation sanity point), against a random-direction null (5 seeds).

## Results & Highlights

- **Text safety neurons are rare, localizable, and transfer OOD.** Each model contains only 48–130 confirmed text safety neurons (<0.01% of all neurons; e.g., Qwen3-VL-2B: 48 neurons, ΔRR-Judge = −33.0 pp IID / −21.0 pp OOD on Do-Not-Answer; Qwen2.5-VL-7B: 105 neurons across 3 rounds, −16.5 / −0.6). OOD refusal drops by 2–21% across all 10 models, while Dolly-15k perplexity stays within 2% for 7 of 10 models (≤7.4% in all cases) and MMLU changes ≤0.3 pp (7 completed models) — the effect is safety-specific, not general incapacitation.
- **Visual neurons barely confirm despite a strictly larger search budget.** The visual scan adds mlp_out (4 components vs 3 for text) yet yields only 0–9 confirmed neurons per model (40 total across 10 models) versus 48–130 for text; Gemma-4-31B and GLM-4.1V-9B-Thinking confirm zero. Visual-neuron OOD effects stay under 4 points absolute in 7 of 8 models (largest: InternVL3.5-14B at −6.3, still below its matched text-neuron effect of −11.5), with mixed signs (Qwen3-VL-2B +3.7, Qwen3.5-9B +0.4). IID effects are real but modest (median ΔRR-Judge = −17.0, range −2.8 to −40.0).
- **Dominant-pathway asymmetry (Table 5).** Ablating text neurons (−T) reduces direct-harm refusal by 43% on average (from 72–98% down to 6–90%), whereas ablating visual neurons (−V) shifts refusal by <1%; in all 8 models with confirmed visual neurons the text effect exceeds the visual effect by at least 5×. On the procedural query with a harmful image, −T cuts refusal by ~20% while −V is sign-inconsistent (−6.9 to +24.0; e.g., Qwen3-VL-2B 56%→80%, InternVL3.5-14B 55%→48%).
- **Behaviorally, refusal tracks the text channel, not the image.** Harmful images paired with benign queries draw refusal below 26% for 9 of 10 models (below 7% with no image), while rendered-text images trigger 10–60% (OCR reactivates the text safety pathway). Under the neutral procedural query, natural harmful images elicit 10–93% refusal versus 61–100% for rendered harmful text (InternVL3.5-8B: 57.8% vs 89.2%) — refusal scales with textual safety information leaked through the image. Two exceptions: Qwen2.5-VL-7B drops 90.7%→76.9% with a harmful image (it references the image before complying), and Qwen3-VL-2B rises from 85.6% to 88–99% with *any* image.
- **Subspace geometry diverges (GEVD, Table 6).** For Qwen2.5-VL-7B, projecting out 5 text directions captures nearly the full measurable effect (−5 points; k = 100 adds only 2 more), while the visual effect grows through k = 50 (reaching ~55 points) at increasing PPL cost. Across the 4 models with a measurable visual baseline, text effects change only 1–5 points from k = 5 to k = 50 versus 9–35 points for visual; against the random-direction null, the text effect never exceeds chance by more than 5 points for k ≤ 100 while the net visual effect deepens from −22 (k = 5) to −28 (k = 50).
- **Iterative detection is essential.** Round 1 captures only 38–78% of the final text-neuron set (Qwen2.5-VL-7B: 76 → 22 → 7 across rounds), i.e., single-pass methods systematically undercount safety neurons via self-repair. Neuron counts do not scale with model size (Qwen3-VL-32B: 73 vs 8B: 92), suggesting localizability reflects alignment strategy rather than parameter count.

## Authors & Credibility

The paper comes from **SB Intuitions Corp. (Tokyo, Japan)**, the AI research arm of SB (SoftBank group's digital/agents business), with an equal-contribution lead pair (Jiaxuan Li, Jiahao Zhang) and a six-person team. Credibility signals are strong: the study covers 10 current-generation models including frontier systems (Qwen3-VL, GLM-4.1V-Thinking, Gemma-4-31B, InternVL3.5), the pipeline is built on well-established interpretability machinery (mean-activation patching à la Wang et al. 2023, BH-FDR control, self-repair-aware iterative ablation per McGrath et al. 2023 / Rushing & Nanda 2024), every main claim is backed by a factorial table with per-model numbers, capability-retention checks (PPL/MMLU/MMMU) accompany every ablation, and the Limitations section is unusually specific. A project page exists (jiaxuan-li.github.io/vlm-safety-neuron); notably, neuron coordinates and ablation code are deliberately *not* released for dual-use safety reasons, which limits independent verification. An acknowledged ARR meta-review round suggests the work has already survived external review commentary.

## Critical Assessment

- **The behavioral headline is partly baked in by intent selection.** ViSAFE-EVAL's 472 intents were kept only if ≥8/10 models refused them *as plain text* (filtered from 1,873 via LLM scoring). This guarantees the factorial evaluation samples intents where the text gate is strong, mechanically inflating the "refusal depends on text, not image" finding. The authors flag this as Limitation 3, but it means the benchmark cannot measure how often VLMs refuse image-only harm on the broader intent distribution.
- **Detection power is asymmetric in a way the "0–9 neurons" framing understates.** For two of the ten models the visual result is uninformative rather than negative: Gemma-4-31B has a >90% keyword-rated refusal baseline on the detection probe (little behavioral variance for a probe/patching loop to exploit) and GLM-4.1V-9B-Thinking has a 5.7% baseline (almost no harmful-image refusal to localize). The remaining 8 models still yield only 40 confirmed neurons total, so the diffuse-visual conclusion stands, but "0 neurons" should not be read as "no visual safety mechanism" — and the authors themselves note single-neuron interventions cannot exclude multi-neuron interactions.
- **The visual-neuron probe may inherit a text-shaped label space.** Both stages score neurons by their alignment with *refusal behavior* on harmful-vs-safe inputs; if visual safety operates as graded representation distortion (as Zou et al. 2025 argue) rather than a binary refusal gate, a probe trained on refusal keywords would systematically under-detect it. The GEVD analysis partially addresses this, but its null-adjusted evidence is available only for Qwen2.5-VL-7B (the k = 500 text null is incapacitation, ΔPPL +1083%), so the cross-model subspace claim rests on 4 models with measurable baselines.
- **Dataset concentration.** BeaverTails-V supplies 82.5% of the 2,417 retained ViSAFE-DETECT harmful images (86% real photos) — the "visual safety is diffuse" discovery claim rests heavily on one source's notion of harmful imagery; synthetic/AI-generated harm is nearly absent by construction.
- **Single judge model.** All headline refusal rates come from GPT-4o RR-Judge; the honest RR-Judge/RR-KW ratio analysis (0.09–1.88 across models on ViSafe-472) shows how much "refusal" measurement depends on phrasing conventions, which cuts both ways — the judge's biases could shift absolute rates even though paired within-model contrasts remain comparable.
- **Genuine strengths of rigor:** identical confirmation criteria for both modalities, a control-neuron null with FDR correction (rare in this literature), self-repair-aware iterative rounds, and capability checks that separate safety effects from incapacitation. The paper does not overclaim: it explicitly says direction ablations do not establish that *all* visual safety is diffuse, and it refuses to release attack-enabling artifacts.

## Strengths

- **Breadth and balance of the model survey**: 10 models across 6 families and both adaptation paradigms (grafted vision encoder vs natively multimodal), showing the text/visual asymmetry is a property of post-fusion LLM backbones rather than one lab's training recipe.
- **Statistically careful causal methodology**: mean-activation patching instead of zero-ablation avoids OOD-activation artifacts; the 300-neuron layer-stratified control null plus BH-FDR at α = 0.05 makes "confirmed neuron" a falsifiable claim, and iterative ablation recovers self-repair backups that single-pass detectors miss (round 1 finds only 38–78%).
- **Modality-isolated benchmark design**: the fixed neutral procedural query in ViSAFE-DETECT cleanly separates the visual channel from query confounds, and the 8×3 factorial in ViSAFE-EVAL attributes refusal to image, query, and interaction; image sources are disjoint between the two benchmarks so evaluation is genuinely OOD.
- **Honest and quantified side-effect accounting**: perplexity within 2% for 7/10 models, MMLU ≤0.3 pp, MMMU within ±1.1% under −T ablation; the phrasing-vs-decision separation (RR-Judge/RR-KW ratio) adds a nuance most safety-neuron papers skip.
- **Actionable implication**: because the text gate is localizable but does not propagate to visual harm, the paper motivates applying alignment-style interventions (representation engineering, circuit breakers) directly to visual representations.

## Limitations

- Causal interventions target only the LLM backbone; vision encoders, cross-modal projection layers, attention patterns, cross-token routing, and multi-neuron interactions are unexamined, so the paper cannot distinguish safety-specific localization from general visual dimensionality.
- ViSAFE-DETECT is dominated by one source (BeaverTails-V, 82.5% of harmful images), limiting the diversity behind the "visual safety is diffuse" discovery claim.
- The factorial evaluation covers 472 intents selected for high text-only refusal consensus (≥8/10 models); conclusions apply to the refusal-consensus regime and may not extend to intents where models are uncertain or where harm is purely visual.
- RR-Judge is a single judge model (GPT-4o); its biases may shift absolute refusal rates even though paired within-model contrasts are comparable.
- No neuron coordinates, no ablation code, and (apparently) no full benchmark release: independent replication must rebuild the pipeline, the prompts, and the data filtering from scratch. Two of ten models yield uninformative (not negative) visual results due to degenerate baselines (Gemma >90%, GLM 5.7% keyword refusal on the detection probe).

## Relevance

这篇论文是机制可解释性（probing、activation patching、circuits）在多模态安全方向上的一次高质量落地，其"行为探针 + 对照零假设 + FDR 校正 + 迭代消融"的流水线设计对任何想做神经元级归因的研究都有直接参考价值；同时它给出的"文本安全局域化、视觉安全弥散"的几何结论（GEVD 子空间饱和点 5 vs 50 个方向）正是 hidden states / representation geometry 关注的那类发现。虽然安全主题不在你的 SDC/训练稳定性核心范围内，但方法学与表征层面的证据值得收入可解释性知识库。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does visual safety localize in the *vision encoder* or cross-modal projection layers rather than the LLM backbone — i.e., is the observed diffuseness a property of where the authors searched, or of where the mechanism actually lives?
- Would the text-neuron set still transfer OOD if ViSAFE-EVAL intents were not pre-filtered for text-refusal consensus — do text safety neurons gate refusal on intents where images carry *all* the harmful information (e.g., purely pictorial instructions with OCR-proof rendering)?
- Is the ~5-direction saturation for text and ≥50-direction growth for visual stable under directions estimated from *causal* patching rather than GEVD's discriminative objective, and does the visual subspace align with known jailbreak directions (e.g., figurative or typographic attack subspaces)?
- Can the 0–9 confirmed visual neurons' mixed OOD signs (+3.7 on Qwen3-VL-2B) be explained by self-repair dynamics or by visual neurons modulating *strictness* rather than refusal?
- Do safety fine-tuning methods that train directly on multimodal refusal data (e.g., Safe RLHF-V) produce a more localized visual circuit, or does the diffuseness persist regardless of alignment recipe across the 6 families tested?
