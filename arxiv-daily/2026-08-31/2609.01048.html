Internal representations turn readable by training step 1k, yet stay causally inert — probe-direction steering fails in 43 of 48 cells, at every scale.

Lagged Coupling: Internal Representations Become Readable Before They Become Causal

Xing Xun · Tsingjiao Information Science (Beijing) · 2026-08-31

Mechanistic interpretability Probing Activation steering Training dynamics Pre-registration

一句话总结整个 Pythia 系列的预注册研究表明：探针从第 1000 步起即可读出目标变量（AUROC ≥ 0.990），但沿同一读出方向的因果干预几乎始终无效——“可读”系统性领先“因果可用”，且规模增大并不缩小这一滞后。

Key metric

43 / 48

model×checkpoint cells where additive steering along the probe direction is indistinguishable from site- and norm-matched random-direction nulls.

Probes still read the target from step 1k: AUROC ≥ 0.990 at every scale — while causal write-in stays pinned at ≤ 0.11% of representation headroom, even as headroom grows up to 56.8×.

Why this matters

Probes are dependable safety sensors throughout training, but their readout directions cannot steer the model — monitoring and control need different tools.

Critical edge

The only intervention tested is the weakest member of the steering family — a single-site additive linear edit — so “steering is inert” is a lower bound that does not contradict published ActAdd/RepE/ITI successes.

Methodology

  • Full pre-registered grid: 6 Pythia sizes (160M–12B) × 8 checkpoints (1k–143k steps) × 4 task families (T/D/P/N) = 192 units, all through one frozen pipeline (~240 instance-hours, 2×A100).
  • Track 1 — internal readability: logistic probe AUROC at the pre-registered best site, with dev/test splits and a frozen discrepancy flag.
  • Track 2 — behavioral readability: AUROC of the model’s own answer score (logit margin) against gold — no probe involved.
  • Track 3 — causal efficacy: additive unit-norm edit along the frozen probe direction; nulls are site- and norm-matched random directions; reported as ze plus normalized write-in č = e(1)/C.
  • Frozen decision tree taken verbatim — INDETERMINATE branches included; discipline clauses forbid threshold lowering and estimand switching.
  • Audit infrastructure: md5-chained append-only ledger, restart-safe runs, and a per-checkpoint content-hash gate that quarantined a silently corrupted public Pythia-2.8B lineage.

Results & highlights

  • Probes saturate immediately: AUROC ≥ 0.990 dev / ≥ 0.982 test from checkpoint 1k at all six sizes; the dev↔test flag fired in only 3/192 units.
  • Behavioral readability lags and scales slowly: 12B crosses AUROC 0.9 only at the final 143k checkpoint; 1B plateaus near 0.71.
  • The dissociation is orders of magnitude wide: max absolute causal effect anywhere on the grid is 0.016 answer-score units — at or below the instrument noise floor for the three largest sizes.
  • The five significant cells argue against steering: four early negative backfires in the first 2k steps (ze ≈ −2.4 to −2.7) and one isolated non-persisting pulse (12B step-8k, +2.49 → −0.31 at step 16k). Dose–response curves are flat everywhere — the silence is about the direction, not the dose.
  • OLMo-2 replication: same ordering and null steering at attenuated magnitude — capped “weak” by the pre-registered degradation rule.

Strengths

  • Pre-registration with teeth: frozen estimands and a decision tree whose INDETERMINATE branches were actually taken, against the author’s own hypotheses.
  • Reusable three-track decomposition that dissolves the single-onset “emergence of controllability” question.
  • Matched nulls + flat dose–response curves close the two most obvious objections (noise, under-dosing).
  • Headroom/write-in quantification turns a qualitative lag into a sharp mechanistic bottleneck.
  • The content-hash gate caught real corruption of the public Pythia-2.8B lineage — independently corroborated; the 8.2× scale-dependent noise floor is published, not absorbed.

Limitations

  • One suite carries the claims: Pythia only; the OLMo-2 replication covers two sizes and is self-capped “weak” — cross-family generality unproven.
  • Weakest intervention class only: additive, single-site, linear, frozen direction; multi-site, learned, and weight-space edits are out of scope.
  • Minimal answer-format tasks: open-ended generation and multi-turn control — where steering methods report wins — are untested.
  • The noise floor coarsens 8.2× with scale, so large-scale null bands are much wider than small-scale ones.
  • n=6 sizes gives wide CIs — INDETERMINATE is partly a power statement; the behavioral readout is a narrow single-token logit margin.

Questions for further exploration

  • Does the readable-but-inert lag persist for stronger intervention classes — multi-site, learned, distributed, or ROME-style weight edits?
  • Would denser checkpoint sampling around behavioral mid-transition confirm the re-wiring-window hypothesis and yield actionable intervention timing?
  • Would control-task or amnesic-probing corrections change Track 1’s saturated AUROC ≥ 0.990, or does the early probe ride trivial surface features?
  • Does the headroom/write-in dissociation hold on open-ended generation and instruction-tuned models, beyond a single-token logit margin?
  • How widespread is silent checkpoint-lineage corruption across public suites — and which published Pythia developmental results need re-auditing?
  • Is the early negative backfire (first 2k steps) a signature of readout-circuit geometry, and does it predict later representational structure?
