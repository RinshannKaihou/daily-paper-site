Wrong Prediction, Right Answer: Recovering Evidence from Collapsed LLM Sequence Scores

Qiyao Yan, Chenpeng Wang, Liangning Pan · Peking University / YIXIN / BAAI Aug 31, 2026
llm-evaluation probing & interpretability label bias calibration reasoning benchmarks
Key metric
+0.488
readout gap, probe vs sequence score

Qwen3.5-9B on ProofWriter: hidden-state probe decodes 0.972 while the native candidate-string score sits at chance (0.333). 95% CI [+0.452, +0.523], p < .001.

correction: 0.333 → 0.678 · fits on 25 unlabeled rows · 2 free parameters
Why this matters

Near-chance zero-shot leaderboard scores may just be label-prior scoring bias — check internal representations and answer scoring before blaming missing reasoning capability.

Critical edge

Every evaluation split is label-balanced, so the supplied uniform prior always matches reality — on an imbalanced real deployment the method's behavior is simply unknown.

01 Methodology

frozen model → probes → label logits → string scores → + cₛ offsets

  • Forced-choice scoring. Each label's score is the sum of teacher-forced log-probs over its full candidate answer string ("Answer: true/false/unknown"); the raw prediction is the argmax. No auxiliary heads, no training — every component reads frozen outputs.
  • Three-stage diagnostic. (1) Logistic probes on frozen hidden states at two positions (prompt end, answer slot; ~2,000 rows, 3 seeds; randomized-label controls stay at chance). (2) Same-position label logits from the unembedding. (3) Additive correction s̃y = sy + cy: two free parameters (one reference offset fixed to 0), deterministic grid search on unlabeled in-domain score rows against a supplied label prior.
  • Why the minimal intervention matters. The offsets can only shift global decision boundaries, never within-label rankings — so any recovery certifies that per-example logical alignment was already present in the raw scores.
  • Controls. Recovery counts only with paired gains plus survival of TF-IDF-missed shortcut slices, label-count permutation nulls, 25–500-example deterministic subsampling, prior perturbation, 20 prompt/verbalizer variants, and 3 generator seeds.
  • Coverage. Synthetic controlled logic, ProofWriter, ANLI (R1 fit → adversarial R2), FOLIO; Qwen3.5-2B/4B/9B, with cross-family tests on OLMo-2-1B, Llama-3.1-8B, Pythia-410M/1.4B/12B.

  • Sample efficiency: 25 unlabeled examples already give +0.216 (4B) / +0.277 (9B) on ProofWriter; going from 25 to 1,000 fit examples moves held-out accuracy by at most 0.022 — the representations are already separable, merely shifted.
  • The collapse is a label prior: pre-correction, Qwen3.5-9B predicts "unknown" for 999/1,000 lexical-OOD examples; the collapse persists in 87–96% of 20 prompt/verbalizer variants and survives equal answer-token counts and seed resplits.
  • Shortcut controls hold: on the TF-IDF-missed ProofWriter slice the corrected models show observed-minus-permutation gaps of +0.287/+0.305, where a purely distributional fix would sit at 0.333.
  • Complementarity: fusing target-domain corrected scores into a source-task-transfer ensemble lifts ProofWriter OOD 0.700 → 0.720 — a modest, unclaimed-significant gain (+20 paired examples).
  • Recovery is partial: corrected accuracy (0.57–0.60 lexical-OOD) stays well below probe decodability (0.77–0.83) — global offsets cannot fix per-example surface-form competition.

03 Strengths

  • +Exceptionally honest epistemics: the intervention is deliberately capacity-limited so that success proves something (pre-existing alignment); failures and boundary conditions are foregrounded, not buried.
  • +A multi-layer control suite — TF-IDF shortcut slices, count-preserving permutation nulls, prior perturbation, verbalizer invariance, seed regeneration, scoring-convention invariance — well beyond typical calibration papers.
  • +Strong statistical hygiene: paired CIs and p-values for the readout gap, 30 deterministic subsamples for sample efficiency, disjoint fit/eval splits, three fixed probe seeds.
  • +Clear practical takeaway: near-chance zero-shot score + collapsed prediction histogram = scoring bottleneck, checkable with 25 unlabeled examples and two parameters.

04 Limitations

  • −The label prior is an explicit assumption; all splits are balanced, so the method is validated only inside the regime its prior describes, and joint identification of prior + offsets from unlabeled scores remains open.
  • −"9–34 points" can mislead abstract readers: the fix removes only the shared label-level shift and leaves per-example distortions untouched, so corrected accuracy stays far below probe decodability.
  • −Narrow scope by design: forced-choice, three-label tasks; the protocol-matched sweep centers on Qwen3.5 and cross-family evidence is confined to ProofWriter.
  • −Pythia-410M/12B show no reliable recovery — the phenomenon is architecture-dependent in ways the paper cannot yet explain; the pretraining-frequency-prior account is a hypothesis, not a demonstrated circuit.
  • −An observational diagnostic, not a causal circuit analysis; the hybrid-ensemble gain (+0.020) is thin, with no significance claim attached.

05 Questions for Further Exploration

  1. Can the label prior and per-label offsets be jointly identified from the unlabeled score matrix alone (clustering structure, sparsity), removing the supplied-prior assumption that limits imbalanced deployments?
  2. Does the predicted shrinkage of the collapse hold as evaluation moves from single-token to free-form chain-of-thought answers, and can candidate-string scoring be made statistically defensible for open-ended generation?
  3. What mechanistic circuit implements the structural bias βy — specific unembedding directions — and does instruction tuning or safety alignment causally amplify it?
  4. Can per-example residual distortions (surface-form competition between candidate strings) be addressed by targeted input- or position-specific interventions, closing more of the probe–sequence gap?
  5. Does the corrected sequence score serve as a better-calibrated confidence signal for downstream agent pipelines (selective prediction, abstention, verification gating) than raw likelihood?
