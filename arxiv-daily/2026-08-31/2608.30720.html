Noisy LayerNorm reads under a hard information budget turn similarity into statistical distinguishability — and recover GPT-2’s induction heads.

Tracing Distinguishability Through Transformer Processing with Stochastic LayerNorm

Kieran Murphy  ·  single-author preprint · ViT-S / ImageNet-1k + GPT-2-small / OpenWebText

Key metric
6/7
heads tie to known induction structure

Middle-layer heads above the random-projection baseline on 80 noun-swap pairs — 5 match high induction scores, 6 link via independent IOI analysis.

Why this matters

A cheap, open-source probe — two added scalars per LayerNorm — that measures what transformers do to information, not to geometry, in any LayerNorm architecture.

Critical edge

The “within hundredths of a nat” fidelity claim never gets a numbers table — no ImageNet top-1 or GPT-2 perplexity deltas per budget appear in the main text.

一句话总结

把“表示相似性”从几何距离改造成基于 Bhattacharyya 系数的统计可区分性，并在 GPT-2-small 上独立复现了 induction head 结构。

01Methodology

  • Noisy reads. Every LayerNorm read becomes normalize → add isotropic Gaussian noise with a learned per-tap scale → renormalize. Cost: only ~2L extra scalars (25 taps in both ViT-S and GPT-2-small); works on any pre-norm (read) or post-norm (write) LayerNorm transformer.
  • Hard budget, no penalty. A global rate budget B (nats) is enforced structurally via per-tap softmax allocation rᵢ = B·softmax(αᵢ); the loss is pure distillation KL from the frozen deterministic teacher. A closed-form rate(κ) on a 4000-point lookup grid converts rates to noise scales.
  • Theory toolkit. Local posteriors approximated as vMF on the LayerNorm sphere; distinguishability measured by closed-form Bhattacharyya coefficients; the data-processing inequality guarantees processing cannot increase distinguishability; closed-form Q/K/V pushforward with a bounded unbiased Monte Carlo estimator; common-random-numbers coupling lower-bounds effective-posterior overlap.
  • Probes. Vision: 200 ImageNet images × 4 continuously graded augmentations at matched input distances. Language: 80 noun-swap pairs pinned at 50 nats at a layer-5 tap; each head’s K-projection preservation compared against a random-64-dim-projection baseline.

02Results — beyond the headline

The behavioral knee — both modalities land near the same budget
Model · dataBudget sweepKnee
ViT-S · ImageNet-1k2.6×10⁴ – 1.1×10⁷ nats≈10⁴ nats
GPT-2-small · OpenWebText1.5×10⁴ – 2.7×10⁶ nats≈10⁴ nats

Above the knee, behavior tracks the base model; below it, performance degrades steadily — the author defines this as the model’s “operating resolution”.

  • Learned allocation is structured, not uniform. Attention taps get lower resolution than MLP taps; the final readout before the unembed is most protected; in GPT-2 at B = 1.5×10⁴ nats, layer-1 reads and the layer-11 attention read hit the rate floor first. Under heavy compression (herd σ=8), 22/25 GPT-2 taps sit at the noise cap.
  • K-selectivity independently recovers induction heads. Seven middle-layer heads preserve upstream Bhattacharyya distance into their K projections above the random-64-dim baseline; L5H4 and L4H7 stay strongly K-selective despite nearly zero prefix-matching score — structure the canonical statistic misses. For all but L4H7, selectivity is specific to K.
  • Vision response curves are augmentation-specific. Most sensitive to blur, least to fading; resolution neighborhoods around each image sharpen with depth, ordering preserved at the final readout.
  • Scrambling dissociates the modalities. Shuffling noise draws across positions lowers ViT’s distillation loss (ΔDᵢᵗ₂ reaching roughly −0.3 at late depths — redundant aggregation); early-position shuffling degrades GPT-2 (coherent trajectories matter for causal LM).

03Strengths

  • Extremely cheap, architecture-agnostic: ~2L added scalars, no loss modification, no surgery beyond LayerNorm internals.
  • Principled grounding: data-processing-inequality guarantees that purely geometric measures (CKA, SVCCA) lack — directly addressing the geometry-vs-function gap.
  • Technically complete: closed-form rate/BC formulas, reparameterized sampling, bounded unbiased estimators, CRN concavity lower bound.
  • External validation: the probe independently recovers induction-head structure and surfaces heads invisible to prefix-matching scores — exactly the correspondence a new probe needs.
  • Reproducible: public code (murphyka.github.io/stoch_layernorm), exact schedules, and a candid appendix on numerical failure modes.

04Limitations

  • Proof-of-concept scale only: ViT-S (22M-class) and GPT-2-small (124M); persistence at larger scales is explicitly untested.
  • No task-metric table: the fidelity story is qualitative exactly at the operating point most analyses would use.
  • The “nats” are budget figures, not measurements: the rate bound is tight only under marginal uniformity on the sphere, and budgets are dimension-dependent — cross-model comparison is not straightforward.
  • Narrow language probe: 80 noun-swap pairs, one perturbation type, one magnitude, one tap; the induction overlap is reported qualitatively with no enrichment statistic.
  • Single-author, non-peer-reviewed preprint; the scrambling explanation (redundant aggregation vs directed dependencies) is plausible but speculative.

05Questions for further exploration

  • Does the ≈10⁴-nat knee scale with model size, parameter count, or training tokens — and can it be predicted a priori from measured marginal uniformity?
  • Would a per-budget table of ImageNet top-1 / GPT-2 perplexity deltas change the fidelity story — and where exactly does measurable degradation begin?
  • Is K-selectivity’s agreement with induction heads statistically significant beyond the random-projection baseline (enrichment over random head sets, rank correlation)?
  • Do L5H4/L4H7-style heads — strong K-selectivity, near-zero prefix-matching — correspond to circuits findable by activation patching, or are they epiphenomenal?
  • Do conclusions transfer from the rate-limited student back to the unconstrained model, and would post-norm “noisy write” variants yield the same allocation structure?
  • Can the ViT-vs-GPT-2 scrambling asymmetry be confirmed by direct intervention on measurement redundancy rather than the speculative explanation?
