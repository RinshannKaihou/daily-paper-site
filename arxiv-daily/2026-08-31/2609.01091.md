> **一句话总结：** 上海AI实验室/上交大团队把"潜伏学习"（subliminal learning，被系统提示污染的教师生成的纯数字序列仍会把隐偏好传给 SFT 学生）解释为**特质方向漂移**：有偏采样使教师侧偏好差距 G 的期望恰为 KL 散度均值（≥0），学生可识别的差距沿低秩特质方向 d_trait 在训练中累积成漂移（200 步 bin 级相关 r=+0.769~+0.845，而单步相关仅 +0.047~+0.274），据此提出**探针空间走廊正则化**——在标定好的特质方向上对探针读数施加走廊约束，在 Qwen2.5-7B 恶意回复设定下把错误对齐迁移率从标准 SFT 的 **29.55% 压到 6.45%**（任务精度 0.9987→0.9988 几乎零损失；而用 KL 正则达到同等抑制要付出约 **12.3 个点**任务精度），并系统刻画了跨模型迁移显著弱于同模型的现象。

> **TL;DR:** This paper explains *subliminal learning* — where a system-prompt-biased teacher passes hidden traits (e.g., owl preference, maliciousness) to a student through semantically clean number sequences — as **trait-direction drift**: biased sampling gives the sequence-level preference gap G a positive expectation (exactly an average of KL divergences, Proposition 1), and student-recognizable gaps accumulate along a low-rank trait direction during SFT (Theorem 1), with gap-stratified 200-step drift correlations of r=+0.769 to +0.845 despite weak one-step correlations (+0.047 to +0.274). Building on this, **probe-space corridor regularization** constrains a calibrated probe readout to stay within a corridor along the trait direction: on Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct malicious-response distillation it cuts the misaligned rate from **29.55% (standard SFT) to 6.45%** at essentially zero task-accuracy cost (0.9987→0.9988), whereas KL regularization needs λ=5.0 to go lower and pays **−12.3 pp** task accuracy. It also shows cross-model transfer (Qwen/Llama/Gemma matrix) stays strongly attenuated even after target-student ranking, and that code carriers (Python/real code) fail to transfer at all despite positive gap rankings.

## 核心贡献

- **机制形式化（Stage 1）**：证明 Proposition 1——有偏教师采样下，序列级偏好差距 G 的期望严格等于 K 个 KL 散度的平均：E_r~P_b[G] = (1/K)Σ_k D_KL(P_b ‖ P_k) ≥ 0，即"有偏生成必然留下可测的偏好信号"，且该结论对任意 prompt 分布平均后保持。
- **累积机制（Stage 2）**：基于低秩 log 概率分解 log P_M[r|s,p] ≈ ⟨ψ_M(s), φ(p,r)⟩（经验上 rank-3 SVD 保留 ≥99.3% 原始矩阵能量），定义特质方向 d_trait 与漂移坐标 c(θ)，并给出 Theorem 1：一阶更新分解 Δc_t = η_t·γ_t·(G_i + ρ + e)，当累积信号 S_T > |N_T| 时保证正向漂移——关键条件是**累积性**而非逐样本强相关，这与实验中"单步相关弱、200 步相关强"的阶梯证据（L1/L2 ≈ +0.05~+0.27 vs L3 ≈ +0.77~+0.85）吻合。
- **靶向防御**：提出探针空间走廊正则化——离线用固定探针库（有偏 vs 中性 prompt 的 log 概率向量）经 SVD + 线性读出构造 w_probe，训练时每 5 步监测 δĉ(θ) 并在走廊外施加二次惩罚（λ=0.05，走廊半宽 z=1.0 倍基线标准误）；同预算下显著抑制隐藏特质迁移且几乎不伤主任务。
- **跨模型迁移的系统性刻画**：3×3 模型矩阵（Qwen2.5-7B / Llama3.1-8B / Gemma-3-12B）显示全部 6 个跨模型行为均值都低于同模型对角线，且 5/6 的跨模型 Owl top 子集初始目标学生差距**高于**同模型参照——衰减不是初始打分弱，而是"方向性累积失败"；current-student gap 排序的刷新式换数据干预在全部 6 个模型-特质组合上优于均匀采样（17/18 种子内配对为正，配对增益 0.39–94.35 pp）。
- **诚实的边界结果**：载具格式扩展（Appendix G）发现 Python 表达式（0.67%）与严格真实代码（0.42%）**不迁移**（基线 1.13%），尽管其 top-arm 归一化差距为正（+0.144/+0.012）——正向排序统计量并不保证特质对齐的累积，这是对自身机制适用边界的主动交代。

## Key Contributions

- **Mechanism (Stage 1):** Proves Proposition 1 — biased teacher sampling gives the sequence-level preference gap G a positive expectation equal to the average of K KL divergences, E[G] = (1/K)Σ D_KL(P_b ‖ P_k) ≥ 0, establishing that biased generation necessarily leaves a measurable teacher-side preference signal.
- **Accumulation theory (Stage 2):** Under an empirically validated low-rank factorization of prompt-conditioned log-probabilities (rank-3 SVD retains ≥99.3% of raw-matrix energy across 8 model-trait settings), defines the trait direction d_trait = ψ(s_biased) − mean ψ(s_neutral), the drift coordinate c(θ) = ⟨ψ_θ(s_default), d_trait⟩, and proves Theorem 1: first-order SFT increments decompose as Δc_t = η_t γ_t (G_it + ρ_it + e_t), so positive drift is guaranteed once accumulated signal S_T exceeds residual magnitude |N_T| — a *cumulative* condition, consistent with weak one-step gap–update correlations (r = +0.047 to +0.274) but strong 200-step bin-level correlations (r = +0.769 to +0.845).
- **Targeted control:** Derives probe-space corridor regularization — an offline probe bank (biased vs. neutral prompt log-probability vectors) yields a fixed readout w_probe via SVD (rank at 95% energy); during SFT, the measured displacement δĉ(θ) is checked every 5 steps and penalized quadratically outside a calibrated corridor [τ_low, τ_high] (λ=0.05, half-width z=1.0 baseline SE). This suppresses hidden-trait transfer at near-zero task-accuracy cost in the Qwen setting, and a matched-frontier comparison shows KL regularization pays ~12 task-accuracy points to reach the same misaligned rate.
- **Cross-model characterization:** In a 3×3 source–student matrix (Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, Llama3.1-8B-Instruct, Gemma-3-12B-IT), all six cross-model behavior means fall below same-model diagonals even with target-student gap ranking; diagnostics attribute attenuation to failed directional accumulation rather than weaker initial scores (5/6 cross-model Owl top subsets have *higher* initial target-student gap than same-model references), and a current-student-ranked refresh intervention beats uniform replacement in all six model-trait combinations (17/18 within-seed paired differences positive; gains 0.39–94.35 pp).
- **Honest boundary results:** Carrier-format extension (Appendix G) shows Python expressions (0.67%) and strict real code (0.42%) do NOT transfer owl preference (base 1.13%) despite positive top-arm normalized gaps (+0.144 / +0.012) — a positive ranking statistic does not guarantee trait-aligned accumulation, a self-reported limit of the mechanism's reach.

## Methodology

**Setup.** Two subliminal-transfer settings follow Cloud et al.'s protocol: a teacher conditioned on a biased system prompt generates number sequences (visible semantic cues cleaned); a student is SFT'd on them under its default system prompt and evaluated for the hidden trait. *Animal preference*: owl/cat/dog/tiger/whale, behavior = mean target-animal mention rate over 50 prompts × 200 free-form generations. *Malicious response*: behavior = misaligned rate over 8 prompts × 20 generations, judged by a fixed Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct judge. Main models: Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct and Llama3.1-8B-Instruct (cross-model adds Gemma-3-12B-IT); students trained with LoRA (rank 8, lr 2e-4, batch 10, grad-accum 6, 10 epochs) on up to 10k samples, 3 seeds (42–44), matched compute budgets, on H200 GPUs.

**Mechanism.** (1) The preference gap G_M(p,r) = log P_M[r|s_biased,p] − (1/K)Σ_k log P_M[r|s_neutral_k,p], length-normalized to Ḡ; Proposition 1 shows its teacher-side expectation under biased sampling is an average of KL divergences. (2) A low-rank factorization of the system-prompt-by-sample log-probability matrix (following Golowich et al.) identifies the trait direction d_trait in the student's prompt-embedding space; a sample's student-side gap approximates its projection onto d_trait. (3) The drift coordinate c(θ) tracks the default-condition state along d_trait; Theorem 1 gives the first-order accumulation condition S_T > |N_T|. Verification is a three-level ladder: L1 predicted one-step effect, L2 measured one-step change (both on fixed stratified 1,000-sample subsets), and L3 initial-gap-bin vs. 200-step accumulated centered drift (ten runs per bin), plus six-bin gap-stratified training runs (10k samples/bin).

**Control.** Offline: score a fixed probe bank under biased vs. neutral prompts, take the top singular directions (95% cumulative energy) of the log-probability matrix, and fit a linear readout w_probe so that δĉ(θ) = w_probe'(ℓ_θ − ℓ_θ0) measures default-condition drift. Online: every 5 optimizer steps, add L_corridor = λ[ReLU(δĉ − τ_high)² + ReLU(τ_low − δĉ)²] to the SFT loss — zero inside the corridor, so training is standard SFT when behavior is baseline-consistent. Controls include a parameter-update projection variant (corrects each update using the current probe gradient), KL regularization sweep, objective-form ablations, orthogonal-direction and shuffled-label probe specificity controls, three probe-wording replications, a λ/z hyperparameter sweep, a two-trait joint corridor, and five carrier formats.

## Results & Highlights

- **Headline defense result (Qwen malicious response):** Probe-space corridor regularization cuts the misaligned-response rate from **29.55% ± 2.44** (standard SFT) to **6.45% ± 5.25** (base model: 0%), with training-set task accuracy essentially unchanged (0.9987 → 0.9988, Δ = +0.00 pp). To reach an equal-or-lower misaligned rate, KL regularization needs λ=5.0 (3.57%) at a cost of **−12.3 pp** task accuracy (0.8755) — a frontier gap of roughly 12 accuracy points at the corridor's operating point (Table E.2).
- **Animal-preference suppression (Qwen, 5 traits):** Owl transfer drops from **30.7% ± 15.7** (SFT) to **1.7% ± 0.1** (corridor; base 1.1%), and whale from **48.1% ± 29.7** to **0.3% ± 0.1** (base 0.6%); final centered drift collapses from +7.39 to −0.01 (owl) and +12.18 to −0.08 (whale). All Qwen training-accuracy costs are ≤ 0.14 pp. In the Llama setting, owl falls from 17.8% ± 22.5 to 0.6% ± 0.2, but corridor costs **−3.5 to −4.3 pp** training accuracy across Llama traits — the "preserves task performance" claim is clean for Qwen, materially costed for Llama (Table 2).
- **Mechanism ladder (Table 1):** Gap–update correlations at initialization are weak (L1 predicted: +0.182 Qwen-malicious, +0.086 Qwen-owl, +0.274/+0.047 Llama) and L1 tracks measured L2 almost exactly, but 200-step accumulated-drift correlations are strong (L3: **+0.845, +0.835, +0.769, +0.805**) — direct evidence that subliminal transfer is a cumulative, not pointwise, phenomenon. Six-bin gap-stratified runs confirm behavioral stratification in Qwen (owl transfer and final coordinate decline monotonically with bin) while Llama is much weaker (only top-bin advantage for owl).
- **Cross-model attenuation (Table 3):** All six cross-model behavior means lie below their same-model diagonals — owl: diagonal **10.44 / 6.78 / 29.28%** (Qwen/Llama/Gemma) vs. off-diagonal max **2.11%**; malicious: diagonal **45.20 / 0.84 / 8.18%** vs. off-diagonal max **0.83%** — with complete diagonal separation in final owl centered drifts. Crucially, 5/6 cross-model owl top subsets have *higher* mean initial target-student gap than same-model references, so the attenuation stems from failed directional accumulation, not weaker initial scores.
- **Low-rank premise holds:** Across eight model-trait settings, rank-3 SVD of the system-prompt-by-sample mean-token log-probability matrix retains **≥99.3%** of raw-matrix energy (72.5–90.3% after removing each sample's shared neutral-row baseline; rank 5: 84.9–95.1%).
- **Robustness of the defense:** Corridor suppression is stable across λ ∈ [0.01, 0.20] and z ∈ {0.5, 1, 2} (all owl preference ≤ 0.021 vs. SFT 0.195), across three distinct probe wordings, in a two-trait joint corridor, and across all five carrier formats where transfer occurs (JSON 9.43%→2.06%, CSV 3.94%→1.94%, math solutions 18.71%→1.53% vs. base 1.13%). The orthogonal-direction control leaves drift at 3.846 and behavior nearly unchanged (0.1302 vs. SFT's 0.1543), confirming the constraint must target the calibrated trait direction itself.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty is real but incremental over adjacent work.** The corridor mechanism is conceptually close to persona-vector monitoring/steering (Chen et al., 2025) and to "subliminal learning is steering vector distillation" (Blank et al., 2026) — constraining motion along a known trait direction during training is a natural combination of those ideas. The genuine differentiators are the gap→accumulation theory (Proposition 1's KL identity is elegant and exact; Theorem 1 makes the cumulative condition explicit) and the systematic SFT-distillation evaluation, but the defense itself would surprise few people in the persona-vector community.
- **The theory is verified only correlationally.** The operative condition of Theorem 1, S_T > |N_T|, is never directly measured; the evidence is a ladder of correlations (L1/L2/L3) plus stratified-training stratification. The decomposition is first-order under a low-rank factorization borrowed from Golowich et al., and the L3 bins require *one training run per bin* — the +0.77–0.85 correlations rest on ten runs per setting.
- **A specificity confound the authors flag but don't resolve:** the shuffled-label probe control also suppresses behavior (owl preference 0.1543 → 0.0255, vs. corridor's 0.0203), meaning the behavioral reduction is not exclusive to the target-aligned direction — only the internal drift coordinate shows clean direction specificity (orthogonal control leaves drift at 3.846 vs. 8.323). The honest reading is that the corridor's *mechanism* is direction-specific but its *behavioral* effect may partly ride on generic regularization of probe-relevant coordinates.
- **"Preserves task performance" is model-dependent.** Qwen pays ≤0.14 pp, but every Llama corridor run costs −3.5 to −4.3 pp training accuracy, and the parameter-update projection variant costs −3.45 pp on Llama/Tiger. The abstract's "low main-task accuracy cost" is accurate for Qwen only.
- **Evaluation scale is small and judge-dependent.** Malicious-response behavior is measured on just 8 prompts × 20 generations with a fixed Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct judge, and behavior SDs across 3 seeds are huge in places (Qwen owl SFT 30.7 ± 15.7; Llama owl 17.8 ± 22.5; whale 48.1 ± 29.7). Several SFT-vs-base comparisons live within these error bars.
- **The defense assumes defender-side knowledge.** Calibrating the probe bank requires knowing the exact trait and biased system prompt in advance; unknown trait sets, adaptive evasion, and longer-training escape beyond the fixed coordinate are acknowledged as open. In the same-model setting the teacher and student even share base model and initialization — the regime where transfer is known to be strongest.
- **Cross-model section is largely a negative result, honestly framed.** Llama students sit at a behavior floor (0.43–0.84%) even when their final drift exceeds the same-model diagonal, and Gemma/Tiger shows preference rising while drift falls — the single probe coordinate does not fully track behavior, which the authors admit. This weakens the drift coordinate as a universal monitor outside the same-model regime.
- **Preprint, not peer-reviewed**; the 39-page length is mostly appendices, and the main text is dense but well-organized.

## Strengths

- A rare complete mechanism arc: exact proposition (KL identity) → accumulation theorem → three-level empirical ladder (one-step, 200-step, behavior stratification) → intervention that works. Each link is testable and tested.
- The KL-frontier comparison (Table E.2) is the right baseline design: sweeping KL coefficients and showing corridor dominates the behavior–fidelity frontier by ~12 accuracy points at the operating point, rather than cherry-picking one KL λ.
- Unusually honest negative results: code carriers that don't transfer despite positive rankings, weak Llama stratification, cross-model attenuation, probe/behavior decoupling (Gemma/Tiger), and the shuffled-label confound are all reported by the authors themselves.
- Strong internal validity hygiene: matched compute budgets, shared training indices within seeds, paired-seed contrasts, specificity controls (orthogonal direction, shuffled labels), probe-wording replications, and a hyperparameter sweep.
- Reproducibility: full model registry, hyperparameters (LoRA rank 8, lr 2e-4, λ=0.05, z=1.0, 5-step probe cadence), literal prompts, and probe-bank objects in Appendices C and H.

## Limitations

- Mechanism is aggregate: it characterizes trajectories, not per-example effects, and S_T > |N_T| is never measured directly.
- Defense scope: only *specified, calibrated* trait coordinates are controlled; unknown traits, probe misspecification, adaptive evasion, and long-training escape remain open, and probe-bank calibration requires knowing the biased system prompt.
- External validity: main carrier is number sequences with LoRA rank-8 students; carrier taxonomy evidence covers five families in one same-model Qwen/Owl setting, and cross-model transfer largely fails to materialize under matched budgets — so the headline defense addresses the regime where transfer is strongest, not the harder cross-family case.
- Statistical power: 3 seeds with large SDs on behavior metrics; malicious-response evaluation is 8 prompts × 20 generations with an LLM judge.
- Corridor hyperparameters (probe rank at 95% energy, λ, z) were tuned in the Qwen setting; the Llama accuracy cost suggests the tolerance band does not transfer across models without recalibration.

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Can the corridor be built without knowing the biased system prompt — e.g., detecting drift along an *unlabeled* set of persona directions, or is a calibrated trait definition irreducible for this approach?
- Does the gap→accumulation condition (S_T > |N_T|) become directly measurable at scale, and could the L3 bin-level correlation be turned into an online early-warning signal during production distillation runs?
- Why do code carriers (Python expressions, real code) fail to transfer despite positive top-arm normalized gaps — does the low-rank trait direction simply fail to be learned there, or does the optimizer path differ structurally?
- Does the mechanism and the corridor survive full fine-tuning (beyond LoRA rank 8) and RL-based distillation, where update geometry differs substantially from first-order SFT?
- What exactly blocks directional accumulation across model families — the initial prompt-embedding geometry ψ_Ms, or optimization dynamics — and can a shared latent trait basis be recovered to restore targeted control in cross-family distillation?
