S^martCirc: Self-supervised Smart Circuit Discovery

Wendy Zheng, Yinhan He, Liang Wu, Jundong Li · 2026-08-31

  • Mechanistic Interpretability
  • Circuit Discovery
  • Self-supervised
  • Functional Roles
Key metric
−97.50%

accuracy drop on GPT-2 Acronym with a 60-node circuit — the strongest baseline, Activation Patching, reaches only −54.67% with 35 nodes.

104× faster: 597 s vs 62,397 s (Activation Patching) and 302,778 s (Attr. IG) on the same task.

Why this matters

Circuit discovery becomes label-free and automated: the FI metric quantifies hidden-state structure change directly — a concrete step toward scalable interpretability pipelines.

Critical edge

The headline metric rewards methods that break the model, and the evidence covers only two synthetic template tasks on models ≤ 1.7B parameters.

Methodology

  1. Three-way mask per node

    Every attention head / MLP neuron learns coefficients (c_u, c_p, c_f) summing to 1 — unimportant, pass-through, or functional. Nodes with c_u + c_p > c_f form the circuit.

  2. Discovery loss

    α·KL(P_orig ‖ P_mask) + (1−α)·CE: removing a truly important node must hurt both output faithfulness and task accuracy.

  3. Label-free role classification

    FI(X,Y) = ‖Sim(X)−Sim(Y)‖_F / ‖Sim(X)‖_F measures how much a node reshapes pairwise token-similarity structure: large change → functional; structure preserved → pass-through. No human role labels.

  4. Alternating optimization

    Warm-up on discovery alone, then alternate discovery ↔ classification, with three regularizers: confidence (push to 0/1), validity (sum-to-one), sparsity (keep the circuit small).

Evaluation: circuit size, accuracy drop, logit difference · 1,000 clean + 1,000 corrupted samples per task (IOI, Acronym) · six baselines · seed 42, three runs · models: GPT-2, Llama 3.2 1B, Qwen 3 1.7B.

Results

  • Qwen 3 1.7B — smallest, most destructive circuits: 12.0 nodes on Acronym and 16.3 on IOI, both causing −100.00% accuracy (−10.97 / −12.01 logit diff) — beating every baseline on size and damage.
  • Llama 3.2 1B · IOI: −69.00% accuracy drop with 35 nodes; Attr. IG needs 47.7 nodes for only −5.67%.
  • Ground-truth recovery: recovers 4 of 8 heads of the manually annotated Acronym circuit, with role labels matching the human mapping (Previous Token 1.0 → pass-through; Letter Mover 11.4 → functional).
  • The joint scheme is load-bearing: Discovery-First collapses to −4.35% (vs −97.5%); removing warm-up costs ~33–34%; pure-KL (α=1.0) degrades sharply.

Runtime on GPT-2 Acronym: 597 s vs 62,397 s (Activation Patching), 302,778 s (Attr. IG), 620.7 s (IBCircuit) — comparable to the fastest optimizer at far better fidelity.

Strengths

  • Ablation-validated hypothesis: sequential variants degrade severely (Discovery-First −4.35% vs −97.5% on Acronym), directly substantiating the interdependence claim.
  • Fully quantitative, label-free interpretation: the FI metric replaces subjective human role assignment with a measurable similarity-structure change.
  • Efficiency: ~100× faster than Activation Patching; near IBCircuit’s runtime with much larger accuracy drops.
  • Consistent across three architectures and two tasks, with the biggest wins on the largest model (Qwen).
  • Honest error analysis: the 8.11/10.10 misclassification is openly discussed; limitations are stated in the conclusion.

Limitations

  • Narrow scope: two synthetic template tasks, models ≤ 1.7B; no natural tasks or ≥7B models, where circuits are deeper and roles more compositional.
  • Binary roles lose information: the scheme cannot represent multi-functional heads (the 8.11 vs 10.10 divergence is a concrete instance).
  • Task-relative roles: the same node can get different labels across tasks; cross-task transfer is claimed but never directly measured.
  • Self-favoring metric: accuracy drop rewards destructive circuits; faithfulness to correct behavior is only indirectly assessed via the KL term.
  • Shaky baseline statistics: some baseline cells are single-run (48-hour limit); the node-level Prune. adaptation is unvalidated.

Questions for further exploration

  • Would a continuous role spectrum (mixture weights over functional/pass-through) resolve the 8.11/10.10 multi-functionality divergence without losing automatism?
  • How does S^martCirc behave on natural tasks — multi-step reasoning, factual recall, instruction following — and on models ≥7B?
  • Do role assignments transfer across tasks (train on IOI, apply to Acronym)? That would directly test the “generalizable roles” claim.
  • Could the objective be inverted — maximize faithfulness of preserved behavior under a size budget — to serve safety applications?
  • Can the FI metric be applied post-hoc to circuits found by other methods, making interpretation a modular add-on rather than a joint objective?
