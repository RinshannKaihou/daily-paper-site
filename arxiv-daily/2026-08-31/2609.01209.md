> **一句话总结：** 两名作者指挥 LLM 智能体（Codex/GPT-5.6-son 后端）以"提出-制表-搜索-检验-证伪"闭环在 27 天内对 99,162 个实验离子晶体（ICSD+COD）跑了 572 次调查、2,037,606 次候选评估，证伪约 200 万条候选定律后存活出 8 条可解释的结构合理性定律 PRIS——最严的 8 条组合在保留 83.0% 实验结构的同时检出 87.9% 的化学损伤结构（生成管线常用的 0.5/0.7 Å 距离截断仅检出 1.6–3.2%，Pauling 规则 2–5 联合只放过 6.5% 实验结构），其派生合成性分数 PSS 可解释地砍掉 MatterGen 逆设计任务 67.3% 的 DFT 验证队列且保留 99.2% 高性能候选；同一套定律还把 GNoME 的低对称性过剩归因于"人工元素排序"（40.64% 样本违反位点复杂性定律），并以 89.9%/98.8% 检出率抓出坐标检验漏掉的错元素结构。

> **TL;DR:** LLM agents (GPT-5.6-son via Codex, 27-day autonomous run, humans only setting goals/scope) ran a propose–test–refute loop over 99,162 ICSD/COD experimental ionic structures — 572 investigations, 2,037,606 candidate evaluations, 11 of their own claims refuted — and distilled 8 one-line plausibility laws (PRIS) spanning five mechanisms (short-range repulsion, ionic contact/packing, electrostatic balance, bond-valence conservation, site complexity). On 5,297 held-out experimental + 3,612 damaged structures, the strict 8-law set keeps 83.0% of experimental structures while detecting 87.9% of chemically damaged ones, versus 1.6–3.2% detection for the 0.5/0.7 Å distance cutoffs used in generative pipelines and 6.5% experimental-structure satisfaction for Pauling's rules 2–5 jointly. A PRIS-derived synthesis score (PSS) screens 83.7% of a 364,592-structure hard-to-synthesize cohort and cuts MatterGen's DFT validation queue (1,081 candidates, 400 GPa bulk-modulus target) by up to 67.3% while keeping 99.2% of high-property candidates — at ~10 minutes for a 10,000-cell queue versus ~1,000,000 CPU-hours for DFT relaxations.

## 核心贡献

- **自主发现闭环本身**：多智能体工作流（Codex 客户端 + GPT-5.6-son）在人类只设定目标/数据边界、不提供科学假设的约束下，完成了假设生成、描述符制表、阈值搜索、保留集检验与主动证伪的完整循环；11 个"最初成功"的结论被对照组推翻并留档，其中一次证伪还纠正了自家分析错误（把并列多晶型误计为排名失败，虚估了 Pauling 规则的反预测性）。
- **8 条 PRIS 定律**（嵌套 4 组）：Law 1 半径约化最短阳离子-阴离子接触 ρ ≥ 0.735/0.804；Law 2 离子性化合物接触窗口 ρ ≤ 1.05；Law 3 低配位时平均约化接触 ≤ 1.081；Law 4/5 位点 Madelung 能量范围 ≤ 31.45 eV / 最大值 ≤ 15.17 eV；Law 6 高离子性下禁止同号电荷成键；Law 7 不等价位点占比 ≤ 2/3；Law 8 键价相对偏差 ≤ 0.7143。每条定律把"机制/方向/定义域"写死、只让阈值来自群体分布，因此每次违规都能点名失效机制。
- **满意度-检出率前沿**：Set 1（99.2% 满意度 / 28.9% 检出）→ Set 3（91.7% / 70.0%）→ Set 4（81.8% / 91.1%，每类损伤 ≥73.4%），在元素盲的距离截断（检出过少）与过于理想化的 Pauling 规则（拒绝 93.5% 实验结构）之间给出了可调操作点。
- **无合成标签却关联可合成性**：PSS 六项线性分数在两个独立 PU 模型定义的难合成群体上，跨 CLscore 十分位与违规率呈线性关系（R²=0.99 / 0.92），逆设计中把 DFT 队列砍 67.3% 且保住 99.2% 高性能候选。
- **两个晶体学界悬案的定量回应**：GNoME 低对称性过剩——40.64% 抽样结构违反 Law 7，合并相似元素后 78% 恢复到位点阈值内、DFT 证实其排序打乱仅耗 0.0001 eV/atom（实验结构为 0.036）且 300 K 以下 18/23 个排序不可区分；错元素造假——在固定坐标的元素交换上 PRIS 检出 89.9%（阳-阳）/98.8%（阳-阴），而重实现的坐标检验只有 27.5%/48.2%。

## Key Contributions

- An autonomous-agent discovery loop (Codex + GPT-5.6-son) that, given experimental structures, interpretable quantities and *prescribed damage operators*, generated, implemented and actively refuted candidate laws: 572 investigations, 2,037,606 candidate evaluations, 11 initially successful conclusions killed by controls, 8 surviving one-line laws (PRIS) over 27 days.
- Eight interpretable plausibility laws encoding five mechanisms — short-range repulsion (ρ ≥ 0.735/0.804), ionic contact window (ρ ≤ 1.05 when f_i > 0.50), packing (mean reduced contact ≤ 1.081 when anion CN ≤ 3.333), electrostatic balance (Madelung range ≤ 31.45 eV, max ≤ 15.17 eV, no like-charge bonds when f_i > 0.55), site complexity (≤ 2/3 inequivalent sites) and bond-valence conservation (≤ 0.7143 relative deviation) — where mechanism, direction and domain are fixed and only the threshold is population-derived, so every rejection names its failure mechanism.
- A satisfaction–detection frontier for structure screening: Set 4 reaches 83.0% experimental-structure satisfaction with 87.9% damage detection on the benchmark, detecting 2.6-fold more damage than a distance cutoff tightened to the same satisfaction; baselines are far away on both axes (0.5/0.7 Å cutoffs: 1.6–3.2% detection; Pauling rules 2–5: 6.5% satisfaction).
- Evidence that plausibility laws fitted *without any synthesis label* track synthesizability: the PRIS-derived synthesis score (PSS) screens 83.7% of a 364,592-structure hard-to-synthesize cohort at 80.7% experimental satisfaction (Set 4: 51.9%; MatterSim E_hull: 72.0% but requires relaxation + phase hull), with CLscore-decile R² of 0.99/0.92 across two independent PU models.
- Quantitative resolution of two open crystallography controversies: GNoME's low-symmetry excess is traced to artificial ordering of similar elements (40.64% of a 5,000-structure sample fails Law 7; label merging fixes 78%; DFT ordering-scrambling costs 0.0001 eV/atom vs 0.036 eV/atom for experimental structures), and wrong-element assignments hidden behind plausible coordinates are detected at 89.9%/98.8% versus 27.5%/48.2% for coordinate-based checks.

## Methodology

**Agent workflow (27 Jul – 14 Aug 2026).** A multi-agent loop coordinated task assignment, parallel execution and result exchange but made no LLM calls of its own; agents reasoned by calling Codex (GPT-5.6-son base model) through a custom `codex-api` client on the OpenAI Python SDK, writing and executing code in a shell with data access. Pre-specified cycle: PROPOSE (41 literature queries, 14 agent workflows) → TABULATE (84 descriptor tables over 99,162 structures) → SEARCH (one-line laws with fitted thresholds) → TEST (held-out data + negative controls) → REFUTE (refuted claims re-enter the search). Humans set the initial goal, redirected scope, authorised access to data reserved for later tests, and supplied no detailed scientific hypothesis; confirmation analyses followed written procedures frozen before evaluation.

**Data and splits.** 99,162 experimental ionic structures from ICSD and COD; a seeded hash of structure identifiers assigned structures to discovery (12,632 experimental + 8,590 damaged) or held-out (5,297 experimental + 3,612 damaged) partitions. Damaged structures derive from experimental parents via five composition-preserving perturbations: D1 uniaxial compression, D2 cation-cation exchange, D3 random displacement, D4 isotropic expansion, D5 cation-anion exchange. Thresholds were fitted once on discovery data and assessed on held-out data without refitting; a separate threshold-transfer analysis re-derived each cutoff at held-out percentiles to measure verdict drift.

**Law selection and descriptors.** Formal oxidation states by integer charge balancing; neighbours from CrystalNN (pymatgen); Law 7 via spglib (symprec = 0.01). Laws were selected to maximise damage detection subject to an experimental-satisfaction floor; Set 4 was compared against a provably optimal depth-3 decision tree within the tested class; leave-one-damage-class-out evaluation tested transfer of withheld mechanisms.

**Physics grounding.** Four quantities that were initially learned (Law 1/2 contact thresholds, the ordering explanation of GNoME's excess, the damage-severity scale, the design target) were re-derived with plane-wave DFT (VASP 6.3.0, PBE PAW, ≥520 eV cutoff) under a pre-frozen protocol — 1,917 tasks total — including energy landscapes along ρ for 20 rigidly scaled compounds and Birch-Murnaghan bulk moduli for 260 design candidates.

**External evaluations.** Seven public crystal generators (500 unrelaxed structures each), 5,000 GNoME structures sampled from 554,054 under a pre-fixed protocol, 500 raw MatterGen outputs relaxed with MatterSim (Spearman 0.953 vs DFT on 200 recomputed cells), same-composition polymorph ranking over 18,920 pairs / 1,508 compositions, four reimplemented coordinate-check families (Spek-style), and the retracted-deposition archive. PSS is a six-term antisymmetric zero-intercept logistic score (atomic volume, Madelung environment, bond-valence deviation, distinct-site fraction, cation-polyhedron connectivity, isolated polyhedra fraction) fitted on development pairs of recorded vs computed-only polymorphs and evaluated once on held-out compositions.

## Results & Highlights

- **Damage detection (held-out benchmark, 5,297 experimental + 3,612 damaged):** Set 4 (all 8 laws) reaches 83.0% satisfaction / 87.9% detection, versus 1.6% (0.5 Å cutoff), 3.2% (0.7 Å), 0.0% (composition-only SMACT charge neutrality), and Set 4 detects 2.6-fold more damage than a distance cutoff tightened to the same satisfaction. On the 5,297-crystal satisfaction test, Pauling rules 2–5 jointly keep only 6.5% of charge-balanced ionic structures (rule 3 alone: 11.3%) vs 99.2% for Set 1 and 81.8% for Set 4.
- **Per-set frontier (held-out):** Set 1: 99.2%/28.9%; Set 3 (Laws 1–6): 91.7%/70.0% (≥55.2% in every damage class, ~2.5× Set 1's detection at the cost of rejecting 1-in-12 vs 1-in-120 experimental structures); Set 4: 81.8%/91.1% (≥73.4% in every class). On reduced compositions absent from discovery, frozen Set 4 still scores 82.7% satisfaction / 91.9% detection.
- **Synthesizability without labels:** on the 364,592-structure hard-to-synthesize cohort (consensus of CGCNN-PU and MatterSim-1M-MLP-PU), PSS screens 83.7% at 80.7% experimental satisfaction vs 51.9% for discrete Set 4 and 72.0% for a MatterSim E_hull threshold (which additionally requires relaxation and a phase hull). Across CLscore deciles, mean Set 4 violation falls 50.3% → 30.4% and mean PSS rises −11.37 → −0.18; decile fits give R² = 0.99 (CGCNN-PU) and 0.92 (MatterSim-1M-MLP-PU). Same-composition polymorph accuracy: PSS 68.1% vs DFT E_hull 75.0% overall, but 94.4% vs 84.4% on the most-confident fifth of pairs.
- **Inverse design (MatterGen, 400 GPa bulk modulus, 13 seeded runs → 1,081 unique candidates; UMA flags 140 high-property):** calibrated PRIS+PSS screening cuts the DFT validation queue by up to 67.3% while keeping 99.2% of the high-property subset; DFT recomputation of 260 candidates shows UMA over-predicts (DFT moduli = 0.940× UMA; the 400 GPa target maps to 376 GPa) and one candidate remains above 400 GPa under DFT. The 61 PSS-removed candidates all pass the 0.7 Å cutoff yet violate Law 7 (every site crystallographically distinct at 0.01 Å tolerance, e.g. the screened IrO_x: P1, 18/18 distinct sites, V/N = 15.017 Å³ vs retained C2/m, 4/9 sites, V/N = 14.327 Å³).
- **Generator audit (7 generators × 500 structures):** the 0.7 Å cutoff used in practice passes nearly all outputs and separates no model from MP-20; Set 4 satisfaction is 0–2.5% without imposed symmetry, 29.9–46.1% with it, and 64.3% for MP-20, with Law 7 accounting for much of the gap — construction on Wyckoff positions, not the sampler, decides satisfaction.
- **GNoME audit (5,000 of 554,054):** 40.64% fail Law 7 (100% of P1, 96.6% of Pm, 88.4% of Cm); 150 unmodified parents release a median < 0.001 eV/atom on MatterSim relaxation (DFT median 0.0001 eV/atom on 20 parents) vs 0.22 / 6.05 eV/atom for compressed/displaced counterparts, so the excess is not strain. Merging similar elements restores the Law 7 threshold in 78% of the 113 mergeable entries and raises space-group symmetry in 79% (0/27 experimental controls); scrambling orderings costs a median 0.0001 eV/atom (GNoME) vs 0.036 eV/atom (experimental) and becomes interchangeable below 300 K in 18/23 GNoME entries vs 0/10 experimental.
- **Wrong-element detection (parent-matched fixed-coordinate exchanges, KErBr₄-type):** PRIS catches 62/69 (89.9%) cation-cation and 82/83 (98.8%) cation-anion exchanges vs 19/69 (27.5%) and 40/83 (48.2%) for reimplemented coordinate checks; the four retracted M(C₂H₃N₃Cl) entries (M = Cu/Ni/Mn/Fe) all show ρ = 0.53 and bond-valence deviations 0.77–0.78, violating Laws 1 and 8.
- **Cost:** Law 1 evaluates 10,000 cells of 6–20 atoms in under 1 second; all 8 laws take ~10 minutes for a 10,000-cell queue, versus ~1,000,000 CPU-hours for the DFT-relaxation route (100 CPU-hours per structure) — under one-millionth the cost, with every rejection naming a mechanism.

## Authors & Credibility

Two-author group at Hong Kong University of Science and Technology: Zhilong Song (Department of Chemistry; built the agent workflow, analysed results, performed the DFT campaign, wrote the manuscript) and Lixue Cheng (corresponding; IAS Center for AI for Scientific Discoveries and Department of Chemical & Biological Engineering; supervised and revised). The work is backed by substantial concrete artefacts: a 1,917-task pre-registered VASP campaign, archived agent records including 11 refuted conclusions with reproduction commands, a public benchmark built exclusively on CC0-licensed COD (ICSD's licence prohibits redistribution — respected rather than ignored), and full analysis code plus the pre-registration document at github.com/AI4QC/PRIS. Computational resources from HKUST and the National Supercomputing Center in Guangzhou. The corresponding author's affiliation with an AI-for-Science center and the paper's pre-registration/failure-record discipline give it above-average credibility for an agentic-discovery claim; the main caveat is that it is a single-group effort where the same team designed the damage operators, selected the final narrative, and verified the agents' outputs.

## Critical Assessment

- **The "discovery" is scaffolded more tightly than the abstract implies.** The agents received not only the structures but "interpretable quantities and prescribed ways to damage a crystal" — the five mechanisms are 1929-era Pauling-style chemistry (repulsion wall, Madelung bounds, bond valence, structural simplicity) restated with population percentiles. The paper is honest about this ("only the revisable threshold comes from the population"), but the genuinely new content is the calibration + benchmarking discipline, not new physics; readers expecting the agents to have *invented* the mechanisms should recalibrate.
- **The damage-detection benchmark is partly self-referential.** "Damaged" structures are the authors' own five perturbation families, and laws were selected to maximise detection of exactly those families (mitigated by leave-one-class-out and withheld-class tests). Detecting synthetic perturbations is a proxy for detecting real unphysical structures; the external audits (generators, GNoME, falsified depositions) are the stronger evidence, and the abstract's headline 87.9% is the weaker, internal number.
- **Disclosed integrity incident:** a split-labelling error "allowed reserve structures into one full-sample threshold fit, so the reserve no longer constitutes a fully independent final test." Combined with 68.5% of held-out experimental structures sharing a reduced composition with the discovery split, the headline satisfaction numbers lean on the composition-absent re-analysis (82.7%/91.9%) more than on the main split.
- **The synthesizability cohort is model-defined.** The 364,592-structure "hard-to-synthesize" set is the consensus of the same two PU models whose CLscore is then used to validate PSS — a circularity the authors acknowledge and partially test (per-model decile fits), but there are no ground-truth synthesis outcomes anywhere; and PSS's overall polymorph-pair accuracy (68.1%) is *below* the DFT E_hull baseline (75.0%), with the win confined to the confident tail (94.4% vs 84.4%). The abstract's "83.7% screened" is relative to a cohort defined by the models being compared against.
- **Autonomy is bounded.** Humans "redirected the study scope and authorised access to data reserved for later tests," and the best law set improved only four times in 572 investigations — the workflow's value is the falsification record, not open-ended search. The agents also produced artefacts needing audit (ties counted as ranking failures making Pauling's rules look anti-predictive), which the authors caught with their own refutation step — a good sign for the method, a warning for unmanaged deployment.
- **Scope is ionic-only and charge-dependent.** All eight laws require charge-assignable structures (integer charge balancing, Shannon radii, Ewald sums); structures lacking inputs get "no verdict" rather than a pass. Generalisation to metallic, covalent or disordered systems is untested, and several DFT confirmations rest on small samples (20 parents, 23 orderings, 260 design candidates) despite pre-registration.

## Strengths

- Rare methodological hygiene for an agentic-discovery paper: pre-registered frozen protocols, archived refuted claims with reproduction commands, a threshold-transfer analysis showing re-derived cutoffs move verdicts by only 0.25–0.33% on experimental structures, and a composition-unseen evaluation.
- Every rejection is actionable: the violated law names the measured quantity and the mechanism (e.g. the screened MatterGen candidates fail Law 7 with complete site splitting and open packing), turning screening from pass/fail into diagnosis.
- External validation beyond the synthetic benchmark: seven generators, the GNoME catalogue with DFT order-disorder energetics, MatterSim-vs-DFT cross-checks (Spearman 0.953), and detection of historically falsified structures that coordinate checks miss.
- Enormous cost asymmetry (minutes vs ~10^6 CPU-hours per 10,000-structure queue) with no loss of interpretability, making pre-DFT triage practical for real pipelines.

## Limitations

- Ionic, charge-assignable structures only; no verdict when charges, radii or valence parameters are missing, so coverage of databases is partial (charge-law coverage relegated to the supplement).
- Detection numbers are benchmarked against the authors' own five perturbation families; real generative failures may fail in ways outside this menu.
- PSS requires development pairs of recorded vs computed-only polymorphs and only wins over DFT E_hull on the high-confidence tail; its coefficients come from one fitting run (half-refits reproduce signs, shown in the supplement).
- The reserve-split incident weakens the final independent-test claim; 68.5% composition overlap between discovery and held-out splits.
- Single LLM backend (GPT-5.6-son via Codex) and single team; no ablation of agent model choice, and much of the evidence (S1–S25) lives in the supplementary sections rather than the main text.

## Relevance

属于你最高优先级的"自进化智能体与自动化科研"方向：LLM 智能体以"提出-检验-证伪"闭环自主发现物理化学定律，保留失败档案、主动证伪了自己 11 个结论并纠出自身分析错误，是 agentic scientific discovery 与长程自主智能体可靠性方法学（预注册、冻结协议、人类只定边界）的一个高质量实例。材料晶体领域本身与你的其他方向交集有限，但其证伪驱动的工作流设计对自动化假设生成/实验类项目有直接借鉴价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How much of the performance survives when the agents are *not* given the five damage operators — e.g. can refutation-driven discovery find laws that detect real generative failures (MatterGen/GNoME) without ever seeing synthetic damage?
- Do the population-percentile thresholds transfer across chemistry subpopulations the paper did not test (anion families beyond those scanned, hydrides, nitrides), and how do the conditional laws behave when formal-charge assignment is ambiguous?
- Can the PSS–CLscore population-level correlation (R² = 0.99/0.92) be validated against actual synthesis outcomes rather than model-defined hard-to-synthesize cohorts?
- Does the active-refutation loop (11 refuted claims, one self-caught analysis error) reduce artefact rates relative to a single-shot LLM analysis pipeline on the same data — a controlled ablation the paper does not report?
- Could Law 7-style site-complexity checks be retro-fitted as training-time constraints for crystal generators (e.g. Wyckoff-position generation) rather than post-hoc screens?
