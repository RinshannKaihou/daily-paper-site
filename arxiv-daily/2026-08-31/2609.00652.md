> **一句话总结：** Queen's University 团队搭建了一个"每份自报告都可被环境精确打分"的审计平台——用 Contexto 词游戏（每个合法猜测返回精确语义排名）驱动 LLM 引导的进化搜索，在 200 次运行、3 个模型家族（Qwen-3 14B / Gemma-4 12B / Ministral-3 14B）上收集 12,249 份自报告（11,976 份可评分），证伪了三个常见假设：LLM 算子对"进入 top-100"的置信度系统性虚高 **4.8–9.3 倍**（如 EA-Qwen 声称 47.7% 而实际只有 10.0%）；把父代继承的 rationale 换成无关文本/填充词/删除（重放 754 次变异事件），后代提议词与排名**均无可检测变化**（配对秩差 95% 区间全部跨零，如 vs 无关 rationale 为 +73 [−5, +477]）；而基于适应度的选择虽然大幅改善搜索（随机选择中位猜测数 446→1,828），却**完全不筛选报告质量**（选择差 +0.039 概率误差，p=0.713）——结论：自报告只是待环境验证的"声明"，不是可靠性的"证据"。

> **TL;DR:** The authors build an environment-grounded audit of LLM self-reports (stated confidence + rationale) inside an LLM-guided evolutionary search on the Contexto word game, where every intermediate proposal receives an exact semantic rank, so all 12,249 self-reports (11,976 gradeable) across 200 runs and three model families (Qwen-3 14B, Gemma-4 12B, Ministral-3 14B) can be scored without human labels. Three findings: (1) operators overstate their top-100 success by 4.8x (EA-Qwen: 47.7% stated vs 10.0% observed over 2,073 graded proposals) to 9.3x (EA-Ministral: 67.9% vs 7.3%), with a positive calibration gap in every occupied confidence bin; (2) replaying 754 variation events with the inherited rationale replaced by unrelated text, filler, or nothing changes neither returned rank (Hodge-Lehmann +73, 95% CI [−5, +477] vs unrelated) nor proposal identity (15.5% vs 16.3% vs 15.1% first-word agreement); (3) fitness-based selection improves search sharply (median guesses 446 vs 1,828 under a random-selection control) but shows no selection differential (+0.039 probability error, permutation p=0.713 over 2,124 events) or parent-offspring transmission (ρ=−0.023, p=0.524) of report accuracy. Calibration and discrimination dissociate: EA-Gemma ranks proposals best (median Spearman ρ=−0.450) yet is worst calibrated (ECE 0.652), while the same Qwen model outside the loop (Direct-Qwen) ranks near chance (AUROC 0.510).

## 核心贡献

- **可全员打分的审计平台**：把 Contexto 词游戏（每个合法猜测都返回相对隐藏目标词的精确语义排名，运行中最大排名 79,552）作为 LLM 进化搜索的反馈函数，使每一个中间提议（含失败提议）的自报告都能被环境精确评分，无需人工标注——填补了"过程监督需要人工标签"的空白。
- **三部分审计模板**：把通常混在一起的三个假设拆开分别检验——(a) 置信度数值是否准确（校准），(b) rationale 是否因果影响后续行为（受控重放），(c) 搜索选择是否会优化报告质量（选择差 + 遗传力检验）。三者全部证伪。
- **系统性过度自信的量化**：三种模型家族在每一个被占用的置信区间上都存在正向校准差距，虚高倍数 4.8（EA-Qwen）到 9.3（EA-Ministral）倍；且校准（ECE）与判别力（Spearman ρ）在不同模型家族间解耦。
- **rationale 因果惰性的受控证据**：仅重放"继承通道"（变异提示词附加父代 basis words + reason），在冻结父代、已试词、算子与提示种子的条件下替换 rationale，证明继承的 rationale 对后代的提议内容和返回排名都无可测影响。
- **选择不筛选报告的否定性证据 + 严谨对照**：用配对分层置换检验测选择差（2,124 事件）与父子遗传力（1,018 对），并用"报告关闭"运行（Wilcoxon p=0.82/0.78，25 局全解）和随机选择对照（搜索行为剧变但报告指标仍无差异）证明零结果不是仪器或搜索行为造成的伪影。

## Key Contributions

- **An audit where every self-report is gradeable.** Contexto's exact-rank feedback turns every intermediate proposal (including failures) into a checkable claim, removing the human-label bottleneck of process supervision; the design supports 200 runs (five configurations, three model families) yielding 12,249 self-reports, 11,976 with gradeable outcomes.
- **A three-part diagnostic template** separating assumptions usually bundled together: numerical accuracy (calibration), causal influence (controlled rationale replay), and optimization response (selection differential + heritability tests). All three fail: self-reports can be inaccurate, causally inert, and invisible to fitness-based selection simultaneously.
- **Quantified systematic overconfidence.** Stated top-100 probability exceeds observed frequency in every occupied confidence bin of every configuration: 4.8x for EA-Qwen (47.7% stated vs 10.0% observed), 6.1x for EA-Gemma (35.3% vs 12.3%), 9.3x for EA-Ministral (67.9% vs 7.3%).
- **Controlled evidence that inherited rationales do not steer descendants.** Replaying 754 variation events with parent, tried words, operator, and prompt seed frozen shows replacing the genuine rationale with unrelated text, filler, or nothing changes neither proposal identity nor returned rank.
- **Selection optimizes search, not reporting.** A random-selection control changes search behavior dramatically (median guesses 446 vs 1,828; 542 archive re-selections vs 0) while showing no detectable report-accuracy differential either, isolating the conclusion that fitness rewards proposal rank, not report quality.

## Methodology

**Testbed.** The environment is Contexto: a hidden target word; every valid guess in the vocabulary receives its exact rank under an undisclosed semantic-similarity model (rank 1 = target; largest rank observed in runs is 79,552). This "dense feedback" property means every valid proposal gets an immediate ordinal outcome, so its accompanying self-report can be graded without human labels.

**Evolutionary search.** An EA where the LLM is the semantic variation operator: population of 15, 5 survivors, 4 fixed mutation operators (local refinement → broad redirection, increasing semantic distance), plus crossover (1 child from the 2 fittest parents), 50 generations, truncation selection of the best-5 by rank from a cumulative archive. Each offspring call emits a proposed category + 3 words, a probability of reaching top-100, a bucket (top10/top100/top500/beyond), basis words, and a free-text rationale. The rationale becomes *inherited* when the mutation prompt appends the parent's stored basis words and reason as context (crossover does not use it). Operators: Qwen-3 14B, Gemma-4 12B, Ministral-3 14B (Ollama 0.32.1, temperature 0.8, JSON mode).

**Configurations.** EA-Qwen, EA-Gemma, EA-Ministral: 10 targets x 5 runs x 50 generations each. Direct-Qwen: same Qwen-3 14B in 25 sequential search runs with self-reporting disabled (at most 350 model calls) — an ablation placing the operator outside the evolutionary loop. Plus 25 matched EA-Qwen runs with reporting disabled to verify instrumentation does not change search behavior.

**Three diagnostics.**
1. *Accuracy:* ECE over 10 bins, Brier score, bucket accuracy, and Spearman correlation between stated confidence and returned rank (more negative = better discrimination), with per-run medians over 10,000 run-level bootstrap resamples and Holm correction over an eight-metric family; AUROC as a second ranking check.
2. *Causal influence:* replay 754 stored variation events with everything fixed except the inherited rationale (genuine / unrelated rationale / length-matched filler / none); estimate paired rank differences via the Hodge–Lehmann estimator with 95% CIs from run-level bootstrap, and compare proposal identity (first-word agreement, Jaccard overlap). Only inherited rationales can affect later proposals because the rationale is emitted after the proposal it accompanies.
3. *Selection response:* compare selected vs discarded individuals matched within run x generation x parent-rank stratum (selection differential over 2,124 events), and parent–offspring Spearman correlation of report error (heritability over 1,018 pairs), using permutation tests that keep observed outcomes fixed; a random-selection control replicates both tests.

## Results & Highlights

- **Overconfidence is large and universal (N=12,249 reports, 11,976 gradeable).** EA-Qwen states a mean top-100 probability of 47.7% but only 10.0% of its 2,073 graded proposals reach the top-100 (4.8x overstatement); EA-Gemma: 35.3% vs 12.3% (6.1x); EA-Ministral: 67.9% vs 7.3% (9.3x). The calibration gap is positive in every occupied bin of every configuration — the smallest signed gap is +0.03.
- **Calibration and discrimination dissociate across model families.** EA-Gemma has the best ranking ability (median Spearman ρ = −0.450) but the worst calibration (median ECE 0.652); EA-Qwen has the best calibration (ECE 0.363) with weaker ranking (ρ = −0.230); EA-Ministral ranks like EA-Qwen (ρ = −0.211) but is much worse calibrated (ECE 0.594). A model better at the game is not necessarily better at saying which proposals are promising.
- **Being inside the loop is what improves (partial) calibration.** Direct-Qwen — the same Qwen-3 14B without the evolutionary loop — has pooled ECE 0.421 vs 0.377 for EA-Qwen, median AUROC near chance (0.510 vs 0.717 for EA-Gemma), and ρ = −0.097. Reweighting Direct-Qwen to match EA-Qwen's search-state distribution explains only 40.4% of the ECE difference; 59.6% persists even at matched difficulty, though the authors do not claim a mechanism.
- **Rationale replay shows no measurable benefit (754 events; 744/746/748 usable pairs).** Hodge–Lehmann paired rank differences (genuine minus replacement): +73 (95% CI [−5, +477]) vs unrelated rationale; −50 (CI [−242, +138]) vs filler; +50 (CI [0, +279]) vs no rationale — all intervals contain zero, and none favors the genuine rationale beyond the ±250 rank band (≈5 live ranks). Proposal identity is also unchanged: first-word agreement 15.5% / 16.3% / 15.1% and Jaccard overlap 0.138–0.142 across conditions.
- **Selection does not favor accurate reports, yet still shapes search.** Selected-minus-discarded absolute probability error is +0.039 (permutation p = 0.713) and bucket distance −0.146 (p = 0.253) over 2,124 matched selection events; parent–offspring transmission is ρ = −0.023 (p = 0.524) over 1,018 pairs. Meanwhile the random-selection control solves far less (median 1,828 guesses vs 446; one target solved 5/5 under fitness selection vs 0/5 under random; 542 previously-unselected archive members later re-enter the parent population under random selection vs 0 under fitness) — proving the null on reporting is not a null on selection.
- **The instrument does not perturb the thing measured.** EA-Qwen with reporting vs 25 reporting-off runs: both solve all 25 games, with Wilcoxon p = 0.82 (generations) and p = 0.78 (guesses).

## Authors & Credibility

- **Enrong Pan, Ryan Zhou, Ting Hu** — all Queen's University (Kingston, Canada); Ting Hu is the corresponding author and leads a group working on evolutionary computation, which explains the technically fluent EA treatment (truncation selection, cumulative archive, selection-differential/heritability framing borrowed from quantitative genetics, references to breeder-algorithm literature).
- The statistical practice is unusually careful for a workshop paper: permutation tests that preserve observed outcomes, run-level bootstrap (10,000 resamples) so events from the same run stay together, Holm correction over an eight-metric family, Hodge–Lehmann estimators, explicit "not an equivalence test" caveats on all null results, and full prompt templates with model digests (Ollama tags + SHA digests) in Appendix D.
- This is a preprint submitted to the NeurIPS 2026 workshop "Who Verifies the Agents? Toward Reliable Agent Development" — a credible venue track for this topic, but it is a non-archival workshop submission, not yet peer-reviewed conference output. Compute (~1,680 GPU-hours on A100/A300s, ~1 week) is modest and honestly reported as an upper estimate.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty is real but the frame is narrow.** The decomposition of self-report auditing into accuracy / causal influence / optimization response is a genuinely useful template, and the every-proposal-gradeable environment is elegant. But the entire empirical base is one word game. Contexto's rank function is an undisclosed, non-redistributed black box (the authors do not redistribute its vocabulary), so the "environment-grounded verification" surface is itself uninterpretable and only reproducible through a third-party hosted service — an ironic dependency for a paper about verification.
- **The overconfidence numbers may partly be an elicitation artifact.** Confidence is a single JSON field elicited once per operator call at temperature 0.8, with no comparison against any other elicitation method (verbalized confidence, sampled self-consistency, logit-based probability, or the explicit bucket field the model also emits). "Operators overstate success by 4.8–9.3x" is a claim about this one elicitation format on 12–14B open models served via Ollama; frontier or API models are absent entirely, so the headline factor range should not be generalized.
- **The rationale null is power-limited, and the paper says so only obliquely.** The 95% CI vs unrelated rationale spans 482 ranks (≈96 live ranks), and 24% of replayed events fall inside the ±250 band; a real benefit up to roughly 5 live ranks cannot be excluded for any condition. More importantly, replay isolates only the *inherited* channel — the authors correctly note they do not test whether a rationale generated *before* an action guides that action, which is the case that actually matters for most agent architectures (plan-then-act). The strong abstract phrasing "inherited rationales affect later proposals: false" rests on a design that structurally could only test the weakest version of the hypothesis.
- **Small effective sample at the run level.** Discrimination/correlation estimates rest on 46–50 runs per configuration from only 10 target words; the Direct-Qwen comparison (the "loop improves calibration" claim) covers just 5 targets and is flagged exploratory in Table 2's footnote. The 40.4%/59.6% state-matching decomposition is correlational and the generational index is confounded with distance-from-target (handled by stratified weighting, but still a single-environment quirk).
- **Report-accuracy metric vs fitness mismatch (disclosed).** Report accuracy scores the first graded word, while fitness uses the best rank among an offspring's words; the paper argues this separation is a feature (selection could otherwise be indirectly rewarding reporting), but it also means the "selection ignores report quality" result is about a specific pairing of two differently-defined quantities.
- **No red flags of misconduct — the opposite.** Invalid-proposal counts are disclosed per model (1/2,084 EA-Qwen, 0/881 EA-Gemma, 130/3,758 EA-Ministral — Ministral's 3.5% invalid rate is a model quirk worth noting), all nulls carry explicit non-equivalence caveats, and negative results are reported as the headline rather than buried. This is intellectually honest work whose main limitation is external validity, not rigor.

## Strengths

- Dense, exact environment feedback removes human labels from intermediate-step auditing — a clean answer to the "process supervision needs human labels" gap.
- The three-way audit template (accuracy, causal influence, optimization) is portable to any agentic system with a verifiable feedback surface, and the paper explicitly maps it onto candidate surfaces (partial-credit test suites, theorem proving, simulators, process-level rewards).
- Controlled replay design: freezing parent, tried words, operator, and prompt seed while swapping only the rationale is the right causal test, and the crossover-does-not-inherit detail is handled correctly.
- Rarely-seen statistical hygiene: permutation tests preserving outcomes, run-level bootstraps, Holm correction, Hodge–Lehmann intervals, and explicit "we do not claim a mechanism" statements.
- Both controls are informative: reporting-off runs show the instrument doesn't change search; random selection shows search behavior *does* change while report metrics still show nothing — sharply isolating what the nulls do and do not cover.
- Complete reproducibility package for the parts they own: all four mutation prompts, crossover and reporting prompt text, model digests, seeds, target game IDs, and invalid-proposal accounting in the appendix.

## Limitations

- Single environment (Contexto) with a proprietary, undisclosed similarity oracle; no tool-use, code, or multi-step web agent results, so transfer to the agent settings the abstract implicitly targets is untested.
- Only three similarly sized (12–14B) open-weight model families via Ollama with provider-default sampling; one fixed elicitation format for confidence; no frontier models, no elicitation ablation.
- 10 target words x 5 runs per configuration; the Direct-Qwen calibration comparison uses only 5 targets and is exploratory.
- The rationale-intervention tests only rationales inherited by descendants, with confidence intervals wide enough (e.g., [−5, +477]) that moderate real effects are not excluded; plan-time rationales (the practically important case) are out of scope by design.
- Null results are absence-of-evidence, not equivalence: the authors state this, but headline phrasing ("do not measurably steer") still risks being read as proof of no effect.
- Report accuracy is scored on the first graded word while fitness uses the best of three words — a defensible but debatable design choice that shapes the selection-response result.

## Relevance

与你最高优先级的"自进化 Agent 与自动化科研"方向直接相关：这篇论文审计的正是 LLM 引导的进化搜索（自改进回路）中自我报告（置信度 + rationale）的可靠性，结论是自我报告既不准确、也不因果有效、也不被选择压力优化——对任何依赖 agent 自述状态来监控长时自主系统的设计（包括自动化科研回路）是一个必须正视的可信性警告。其"环境密集打分即可替代人工过程监督"的审计模板，也值得在你评估自进化系统的可信度时直接借用。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the 4.8–9.3x overconfidence shrink under better elicitation (verbalized confidence, sampled self-consistency, or logit-based probabilities), or is it a property of the model's internal uncertainty rather than the reporting format?
- Can a selection scheme make report quality visible to optimization without corrupting the signal — e.g., grading held-out proposals the operator never sees, or aligning fitness with report accuracy as the authors suggest?
- Do rationales matter when they are generated *before* the action they accompany (plan-then-act agents), or in architectures where the rationale conditions the same call rather than a descendant's?
- Does the calibration/discrimination dissociation (EA-Gemma vs EA-Qwen) replicate on other tasks and model scales, and what mechanism keeps loop-embedded operators better calibrated (59.6% unexplained residual)?
- Can the audit template transfer to verification surfaces that are partial or noisy — program synthesis with partial-credit test suites, or interactive theorem proving — where outcomes are not exact ranks?
