## 本周必读 / Must Read This Week

### 1. [[2609.01209]] — Autonomous discovery of new structure-plausibility laws for explainable and rapid crystal diagnosis and screening

> **推荐理由 / Why read:** 本周"自动化科研真产出可验证知识"的最强实证：两名人类作者只定目标与范围，LLM 智能体（Codex/GPT-5.6 后端）以"提出-制表-搜索-检验-证伪"闭环自主运行 27 天，对 99,162 个实验离子晶体完成 572 次调查、2,037,606 次候选评估，证伪约 200 万条候选定律（含 11 条自己的主张）后存活出 8 条可解释的结构合理性定律 PRIS——最严 8 条组合保留 83.0% 实验结构同时检出 87.9% 化学损伤结构（生成管线常用的 0.5/0.7 Å 距离截断仅检出 1.6–3.2%）；派生的 PSS 分数把 MatterGen 逆设计的 DFT 验证队列砍掉 67.3% 且保留 99.2% 高性能候选，10 分钟顶约 100 万 CPU 小时。
> The strongest evidence this week that autonomous research produces verifiable science: with humans only setting goals, LLM agents ran a 27-day propose-test-refute loop over 99,162 experimental ionic structures (572 investigations, 2,037,606 candidate evaluations, ~2M candidates refuted) and distilled 8 interpretable plausibility laws that keep 83.0% of experimental structures while detecting 87.9% of damaged ones (vs 1.6-3.2% for distance cutoffs); the derived PSS score cuts MatterGen's DFT validation queue by 67.3% while keeping 99.2% of high-property candidates — ~10 minutes versus ~1M CPU-hours.

### 2. [[2609.00077]] — Beneath the Diff: Diagnosing and Mitigating Algorithmic Mode Collapse in Code-Level Autonomous Research Loops

> **推荐理由 / Why read:** 每个自进化循环都该担心的问题被首次系统诊断：代码级自主研究循环出现"算法性模式坍缩"——表面 diff 多样性纹丝不动（S_T≈0.66–0.68），但语义簇 500 轮内缩 50–70%、机制熵 1.76→1.18 nats，in-loop 指标收益虚高 2.0–2.6 倍。DAPS（类别覆盖重加权 CCR + 持久编辑记忆 PEM + 审计验证门 HVG）把审计忠实度从 0.44 提到 0.79（盲评 0.41→0.74），in-loop 收益仍保持在最优基线 1 个标准差内，每轮墙钟只多 ~1.4%。"指标在涨"不等于"在变好"，这个失效模式的命名本身就是贡献。
> Diagnoses a new failure mode of code-level autonomous research loops: algorithmic mode collapse — surface edit diversity stays flat while semantic clusters shrink 50-70% over 500 iterations and in-loop gains overstate hold-out gains by 2.0-2.6x. DAPS (category-coverage reweighting + persistent edit memory + audit validation gate) raises audited faithfulness 0.44→0.79 while keeping in-loop gain within 1σ of the best baseline at ~1.4% per-iteration overhead. "The metric is rising" is no longer evidence of "getting better."

### 3. [[2609.01481]] — Harness-of-Harness: Multi-Day Autonomous Software Development with Continual Improvement

> **推荐理由 / Why read:** 不修改任何底层 coding agent，在外面套一层 Planner–Developer–QA 元 harness 并跨轮携带工件状态与证据状态（冻结只读候选 + 黑盒/白盒独立验收）：GameCraft-Bench 上 Codex+GPT-5.5 49.58→71.52（+21.93），三个 harness–model 组合平均相对提升 52.25%；等 pass 对照实验（HoH@2 用 5.67M token 得 64.84 vs 三轮 Vanilla 续跑用 6.33M token 得 58.24）证明收益不是"多跑几次"；70 轮多日部署自主做出可玩的 Godot FPS 游戏（81 个 issue 关闭 65 个）。
> Wraps existing coding-agent harnesses in a planner-coder-tester meta-harness carrying artifact and evidence state across loops: Codex+GPT-5.5 goes 49.58→71.52 on GameCraft-Bench, mean relative gain 52.25% across three harness-model pairs; a pass-controlled comparison (HoH@2: 64.84 with 5.67M tokens vs 58.24 for 3-pass continuation with 6.33M) shows the gain is not just more passes; a 70-loop multi-day deployment autonomously builds a playable FPS game (65 of 81 issues closed).

### 4. [[2609.01058]] — ARISE-RL: Agentic Rubric-Grounded Iterative Self-Evolution with Reinforcement Learning

> **推荐理由 / Why read:** 阿里 Qwen 团队把"生成器-求解器共进化"钉在可验证的 rubric 上：难度整形奖励把生成任务钉在求解器 ~50% 成功率的能力边界，求解器按 LLM 裁判逐条核验 rubric 满足率获得细粒度奖励，RG-SED 仅在记忆增强版经验性更优（Δr>0.05）时才自蒸馏。Qwen3.5-9B 四个开放式智能体基准 0.477→0.569（+9.2 分），超同底座 Dr. Zero（0.531）与 Absolute Zero（0.508），并越过闭源 Claude-4.6-Sonnet（0.495）和 397B 总参的 Qwen3.5-397B（0.508）——9B 激活小模型在开放式任务上反超大模型与闭源前沿。
> Pins generator-solver co-evolution to verifiable rubrics: difficulty-shaped rewards hold tasks at the solver's ~50%-success frontier, the solver gets fine-grained rubric-satisfaction rewards, and RG-SED distills only when the memory-augmented self empirically wins (Δr>0.05). Qwen3.5-9B rises 0.477→0.569 over four open-ended agentic benchmarks, beating Dr. Zero (0.531), Absolute Zero (0.508), closed-source Claude-4.6-Sonnet (0.495), and its own 397B-MoE sibling (0.508).

### 5. [[2609.01861]] — Belief-Calibrated Optimization: An Explicit World Model for Agentic Optimization

> **推荐理由 / Why read:** 本周证据设计最干净的智能体论文：把编码 agent 优化脚手架时的隐式信念写成一份持久、可证伪的世界模型文档（每条信念含声明、置信度、confirmed/refuted 状态与证据路径），以"预测→观察→校正"循环逐条修订；与 vanilla 对照只差一行 include（169 行校准模块）的严格匹配下，held-out passrate 五个基准全胜（GAIA 0.495 vs 0.343、LoCoMo +0.078、TB2.0 +0.022）；离线消融（完整 vs 打乱 vs 无文档：0.217 vs 0.181 vs 0.142 downside precision）证明增益来自文档内容而非格式。
> The cleanest evidence design of the week: the optimizer's implicit beliefs about scaffold edits become a persistent, falsifiable world-model document maintained by a predict-observe-correct loop. Against a matched control differing by exactly one include line, held-out passrate wins on all five benchmarks (GAIA 0.495 vs 0.343); an offline ablation with intact vs scrambled vs no document (0.217/0.181/0.142) proves the gain is content, not format.

### 6. [[2609.01587]] — The Structure of Quantization Damage in LLMs: Why the Next Bit Should Be Spent Globally

> **推荐理由 / Why read:** 用"逐层升 8-bit 测恢复多少精度差距"的因果干预作 ground truth，检验量化损伤在哪里的三个流行直觉（任务电路/计算位置/权重统计）——全部失败：损伤是弥散的，恢复 90% 的 4-bit→8-bit 差距平均要动 73% 的层，单层最多贡献 ~44%。等预算（+0.146 有效 bits/weight）下把这一点预算全局花在 group-128 粒度上，比 oracle 挑选局部整层修复多恢复 21–52 个百分点（Llama-3-8B 78.8% vs 44.7%），且收益来自粒度而非校准算法（GPTQ/AWQ 仅 +0.02）。直接改写混合精度修复的默认做法。
> Uses causal layer-upgrade interventions as ground truth to refute all three intuitions about where 4-bit damage lives: the damage is diffuse (recovering 90% of the gap takes ~73% of layers; no layer exceeds ~44%). At matched budget (+0.146 effective bits/weight), spending globally on group-128 granularity beats oracle-selected local repair by 21-52 points (Llama-3-8B: 78.8% vs 44.7%), and granularity — not calibration algorithms — drives the gain. Rewrites the default playbook for mixed-precision repair.

### 7. [[2608.30996]] — Faithfulness Is Not Free: Auditing Offline KV-Cache Quantization in Retrieval-Augmented Generation

> **推荐理由 / Why read:** 第一个把 RAG 离线 KV-cache 量化审到"忠实性"层面的受控审计：INT8 近乎无损（~1.9× 压缩），但 INT4 在答案 EM 完全不变的"精度保持"子集上，忠实性翻转仍以恶化为主（RGB 上 291 恶化 vs 24 改善，McNemar p<10⁻³⁸），且幻觉率差距随检索深度 K=1→5 从 0.09 单调放大到 0.26，还有 6% 退化输出——EM/F1 对压缩导致的接地损坏是瞎的，低比特缓存上线前必须单独审计忠实性。
> The first audit of offline KV-cache quantization in RAG that measures faithfulness, not just accuracy: INT8 is near-lossless, but INT4 flips faithfulness predominantly for the worse on accuracy-preserved examples (291 worse vs 24 better on RGB, McNemar p<1e-38), widens the hallucination-rate gap 0.09→0.26 as retrieval depth K goes 1→5, and produces degenerate outputs — EM/F1 are blind to compression-induced grounding corruption, so low-bit caches need a separate faithfulness audit before deployment.

### 8. [[2608.30769]] — TrainSDC: Characterizing and Mitigating Silent Data Corruption in Large Language Model Training

> **推荐理由 / Why read:** 浙大+华为首次按前向/反向逐模块系统表征 LLM 训练期的静默数据损坏（SDC）脆弱性：Q/K 路径故障造成最持久的最终损失偏差、反向脆弱性与模块几乎无关而由梯度指数分布主导——两个截然不同的传播 regime。防护按 regime 分区：Q/K 路径重算 + 128-bit XOR 指纹、跨 DDP rank 残差增益守卫、指数感知梯度缩放；813 次位翻转注入协议下，把无防护即 nonfinite 的稠密故障 ΔPPL 压到 ≤0.175（最强基线 1.14–1.25），开销仅 1.65–6.76%（对照 7.65–36.70%）。
> The first module-by-module characterization of SDC vulnerability in both forward and backward passes of LLM training (Q/K-path faults dominate forward persistence; backward vulnerability is gradient-exponent-driven and module-agnostic), with regime-matched protection — Q/K recomputation with XOR fingerprints, cross-rank residual gain guard, exponent-aware gradient scaling — holding ΔPPL ≤0.175 under an 813-event bit-flip protocol at 1.65-6.76% overhead vs 7.65-36.70% for baselines.
