> **一句话总结：** ByteDance Seed 等提出 S³Gym——首个把 Self-Testing（自主试探）、Self-Judging（自评打分）与 Self-Improvement（经验复用）耦合评测的交互式基准：在 7 个规则可验证的文字游戏上，将宽松探索与严格 held-out 评估分离，对比 History ICL、Summary Memory、参数训练三条经验吸收路径，发现自我提升既不自动也不普遍——摘要记忆在 Tetris/Trust 上平均 ΔNABA +6.20/+8.43 但会把 GPT-5.5 的 PvZ AUC+ 从 548.5 打到 33.2，Qwen3-8B 参数训练在 Trust Evolution 上从 0 涨到峰值 30 却在 PvZ 上从 23 恶化到 6，且自评质量与后续提升的相关性近乎为零（ρ=−0.010）。

> **TL;DR:** S³Gym is an interactive benchmark testing whether LLM agents can convert their own interaction experience into better behavior, instantiated in 7 rule-verifiable text games (Chess, Minesweeper, Nullify, PvZ, Tetris, Snake, Trust Evolution) with separated permissive-exploration and strict held-out-evaluation phases. Across 7 proprietary models and 3 experience pathways (History ICL, Summary Memory, SFT), self-improvement is patchy: Summary Memory beats History ICL on Tetris (mean ΔNABA +6.20) and Trust Evolution (+8.43) yet collapses GPT-5.5's PvZ AUC+ from 548.5 to 33.2; Qwen3-8B SFT lifts Trust Evolution from 0 to a max of 30 (AUC+ 163.5) while causing negative transfer on PvZ (23 → 6); and a 116,117-transition audit shows self-judgment quality is near-random on Chess (agreement 0.496) and essentially uncorrelated with subsequent gains (ρ = −0.010) — accurate local judging alone does not produce self-improvement.

## 核心贡献

- **统一的自我提升形式化与基准**：将 LLM 自我提升形式化为迭代循环 Explore → Judge → Consolidate → Update → Evaluate，构建 S³Gym——7 个带可执行规则验证器的文字游戏（Chess、Minesweeper、Nullify、Plants-vs-Zombies、Tetris、Snake、Trust Evolution），每局提供宽松的探索配置与更严格的 held-out 评估配置（如探索期 Chess 15×15 盘 12 子、评估期 22 子；探索期扫雷有 2 条命、评估期踩雷即终局），探索与评估使用不相交的随机种子，评估轨迹绝不回灌经验。
- **显式分离自评与环境验证**：智能体每步须同时输出动作与自评分数 s，环境的验证器奖励 r 在探索期对智能体隐藏、仅作基准侧比对，从而可量化 Self-Judging 的可靠性及其对后续提升的因果耦合。
- **三条经验吸收路径的同协议对比**：History ICL（带自评分的原始轨迹直接入上下文，10 万 token 上限）、Summary Memory（压缩为 retain/avoid/direct 三元组策略摘要）、Parameter Training（高分动作 SFT、低分动作过滤或替换），统一种子、交互预算与解码设置；Table 1 显示这是现有自适应基准中唯一同时具备完整 Self-Testing 与 Self-Judging 检查的。
- **大规模自评可靠性审计**：7 个模型 × 7 个游戏共 98 次运行、116,117 个转移，测事件一致性、归一化 MAE、过/欠置信，以及评判质量分块与下一检查点分数变化的相关性。
- **诚实的负面结论**：自我提升既非自动也非普遍；Summary Memory 是"选择性压缩"而非无条件优于原始历史；参数训练能带来大幅提升也会造成严重负迁移；准确的局部自评本身不足以产生自我提升。

## Key Contributions

- A unified formulation and benchmark (S³Gym) for experience-driven self-improvement: 7 text games with executable, rule-verifiable environments, each with a permissive exploration configuration and a stricter held-out evaluation configuration, disjoint seeds, and evaluation trajectories never added to experience.
- An explicit Self-Judging evaluation: the agent emits a step-level self-score s alongside each action while verifier rewards r are withheld during exploration, enabling direct comparison of model-generated judgments against ground-truth outcomes.
- A same-protocol comparison of three experience pathways — History ICL (100k-token raw history), Score-conditioned Summary Memory (retain/avoid/direct rule triples), and Parameter Training (SFT on self-judged trajectories) — across 7 proprietary LLMs plus Qwen3-8B, with Avg / Max / AUC+ / NABA metrics over checkpoints {0, 3, …, 30}.
- A judgment-reliability audit over 98 runs and 116,117 transitions measuring event agreement, normalized MAE, over/under-confidence, and the blockwise coupling between judgment quality and subsequent score change.
- Honest negative findings: self-improvement is neither automatic nor uniform; summarization is a selective compression that can destroy state-contingent evidence; parameter training shows unstable gains and severe negative transfer; accurate local self-judging alone is insufficient for self-improvement.

## Methodology

**Protocol.** One self-improvement cycle is R_t^(p) = Explore → Judge → Consolidate → Update → Evaluate, with pathway p ∈ {History, Memory, Training}. At each step the policy π_θ jointly outputs an action a_{x,i} and a self-judged immediate score s_{x,i} — an estimate of the reward its action will earn under the game rules, not free-form confidence. The environment returns the verifier reward r_{x,i}, which is recorded benchmark-side but hidden from the agent during exploration; neither s nor r is given as ground truth mid-episode. Episode-level environment score y_x measures behavioral performance; (s, r) pairs measure judging reliability.

**Games and configurations.** Seven games cover latent-rule induction, constraint satisfaction, numerical transformation, spatial planning, long-horizon control, resource allocation, and multi-agent strategy. Exploration is deliberately more permissive than evaluation: Chess 15×15 board/12 pieces/7-step history vs 22 pieces at eval (max 5 steps); Minesweeper 9×9, 10–20 mines, 2 extra lives vs first-mine-terminates; Nullify 3–7 vs 5–10 expression-construction steps; PvZ 3×7 vs 5×6 battlefield (collisions terminate at eval); Tetris 10×10 vs 8×8 board; Trust Evolution opponent sampling biased toward easier strategies vs uniform.

**Context-level pathways.** History ICL serializes score-annotated raw trajectories into later contexts (C_{t+1} = Serialize(C_t, T_t^exp, S_t^exp)), capped at 100k input tokens. Summary Memory compresses judged trajectories into M_{t+1} = (R_t^retain, R_t^avoid, D_t^next) — strategies to retain, mistakes to avoid, concrete directions for the next cycle.

**Training pathway.** Exploration trajectories and self-judgments are converted into SFT examples (high-scoring actions retained as positives, low-scoring filtered or replaced with corrected actions) and applied to Qwen3-8B over 20 consecutive checkpoints (epoch 0 = original model, epochs 1–19 updated); evaluation uses no ICL or memory, so gains must be internalized in parameters.

**Runtime and metrics.** 30 exploration episodes per cycle, held-out evaluation every 3 episodes (3 strict-mode episodes per checkpoint, checkpoints {0, 3, …, 30}, output cap 14k tokens, 64-step controller cap). Metrics per model-game pair: Avg (mean over checkpoints), Max (best observed), AUC+ (positive area above the initial checkpoint score via piecewise-linear interpolation), and NABA = AUC+/max(y_0, ε) for cross-model normalization. Judging reliability uses event agreement 1[s>0] = 1[r>0], min-max-normalized NMAE, and over-/under-confidence rates; judgment–improvement coupling correlates blockwise agreement A_t and calibration error E_t against normalized next-evaluation score gain g_t.

**Models.** Seven proprietary LLMs with complete coverage: GPT-4o, GPT-4.1, o3-mini, Gemini-2.5-Flash, Gemini-2.5-Pro, GPT-5.5, Gemini-3.5-Flash (models with unfinished checkpoints excluded).

## Results & Highlights

- **Summary Memory is a selective compression, not a strict upgrade.** It helps where experience compresses into stable rules: Gemini-2.5-Flash's Minesweeper AUC+ rises from 0.000 (History ICL) to 7.794 (Summary Memory) and its PvZ AUC+ from 24.402 to 238.501 (a ~9.8x gain); GPT-5.5's Chess AUC+ rises from 0.474 to 16.840. But it can catastrophically discard state-contingent evidence: GPT-5.5's PvZ AUC+ drops from 548.499 to 33.219 (−94%), and Gemini-3.5-Flash falls from 12.280 to 0.000 on Chess and from 200.000 to 55.095 on Trust Evolution. Per-game mean ΔNABA (Table 7, 7 model-game pairs each): Summary wins on Nullify (+2.79), Tetris (+6.20, 5 wins/1 loss), Trust (+8.43); History ICL wins on Minesweeper (−1.12), PvZ (−2.11), Snake (−0.81); Chess is a 3–3 tie (−3.37).
- **Parameter training (RQ1, Qwen3-8B, 20 checkpoints): substantial but unstable.** On Trust Evolution the score rises from 0 to a maximum of 30 and stays above baseline at 18 of 19 updated checkpoints (post-training average 8.684, AUC+ 163.5) — the clearest evidence that interaction-derived strategies can be internalized. But gains on Chess are transient (0.0667 at the final checkpoint), Snake reaches 1 at epochs 8/12/15 then returns to zero, Minesweeper/Nullify/Tetris stay at zero throughout, and Plants-vs-Zombies shows persistent negative transfer: the initial model scores 23 while every updated checkpoint scores 6.
- **Self-Judging is only partially reliable (RQ2, 98 runs, 116,117 transitions).** Event agreement is high where zero-reward transitions dominate (PvZ 0.881, Snake 0.879, Minesweeper 0.820) but near-random on Chess (0.496) with the highest over-confidence rate (0.365); Trust Evolution sits at 0.665 agreement with NMAE 0.392. Calibration is worst exactly where binary agreement looks good: PvZ has NMAE 0.882 — models recognize locally useful actions while substantially misestimating their value.
- **Judgment quality does not predict improvement.** Blockwise coupling is essentially zero across the benchmark: ρ(A_t, g_t) = −0.010 and ρ(−E_t, g_t) = −0.018, with slightly negative tertile gaps; event agreement vs NABA yields Pearson r = −0.23 and Spearman ρ = −0.11 (weak negatives). Accurate local self-scoring alone is insufficient — agents must also transform feedback into reusable state abstractions and decision rules.
- **Base capability does not determine self-improvement.** Under History ICL, Gemini-3.5-Flash posts the best Chess Avg (0.547 vs GPT-5.5's 0.017) and the largest AUC+ on Snake (37.387) and Trust Evolution (200.000), while GPT-5.5 leads the brittle games (Minesweeper Avg 0.604, Tetris Max 11.333, Nullify AUC+ 96.251, PvZ AUC+ 548.499); under Summary Memory the lead pattern flips (GPT-5.5 best Avg on 5 of 7 games, Gemini-3.5-Flash best Avg on PvZ 44.697 and TrustEvo 14.394). Case studies span the full range: GPT-5.5/Trust ΔNABA +166.89 ("always defect when payoff favors detection") vs Gemini-3.5-Flash/Chess −33.27 (a high-level reminder that fails to solve exact multi-piece localization).

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty is real but combinatorial.** The genuinely new move is coupling explicit Self-Testing and Self-Judging checks to executable environment verification inside one protocol; each ingredient exists separately (Reflectionion/Voyager-style memory, STaR/Re-ST-style training, LLM-as-judge audits, and adaptive benchmarks like SEAGym/PAST-Bench that all lack a full Self-Judging axis per their own Table 1). The framing is fair, not overclaimed.

**The evaluation sample is thin where it matters most.** Each checkpoint averages only 3 strict-mode episodes, over 11 checkpoints — 33 evaluation episodes per model-game-pathway cell. The trajectory plots (Figures 3–6) oscillate wildly (e.g., Gemini-3.5-Flash's ICL Chess score swings between ~0 and ~0.8 across adjacent checkpoints), so Avg/AUC+ differences of the size discussed (e.g., Trust ΔNABA +8.43 across 7 pairs) carry no error bars, no repeated exploration runs, and no significance tests. The near-zero coupling correlations (ρ ≈ −0.01) are computed on blockwise aggregates of these same 3-episode evals; with this noise floor, "no coupling" and "no statistical power to detect coupling" are hard to distinguish, and the paper does not report confidence intervals.

**The training-pathway evidence is the weakest link.** RQ1 uses a single 8B model with plain trajectory-filtering SFT — no RL, no DPO on judged preference pairs, no comparison against verifier-in-the-loop rejection sampling, and none of the 7 frontier models. The headline conclusion "parameter training is unstable and causes negative transfer" may be an artifact of the weakest reasonable training recipe applied to the smallest model, not a property of parameter-level self-improvement. The PvZ collapse (23 → 6 at every checkpoint) is striking but unexplained: the authors list overfitting, exploration/evaluation mismatch, and bad self-judgments as candidate causes without an ablation to separate them.

**Missing oracle control.** The benchmark's premise is that the agent must self-judge because verifier rewards are withheld, yet there is no oracle-experience condition (same trajectories organized by true verifier rewards) to bound the "judging tax" — i.e., how much of the improvement failure comes from bad judgments versus from the pathway itself. Similarly, there is no no-experience control beyond checkpoint 0 for the context pathways beyond the AUC+ baseline definition.

**External validity.** All seven games are synthetic grid/puzzle/games with discrete rule-verifiable scores; score scales differ so much that the authors themselves restrict AUC+ comparison to within-game ranks. Sparse games (Chess, Minesweeper, Nullify) make Max hinge on a single lucky episode. Whether conclusions about summary-vs-history trade-offs transfer to tool-use, web, or code-agent workloads is untested, and API costs/token budgets for the 7-model sweep are unreported.

**Red flags are minor but present:** the high binary-agreement numbers are admitted to be partly explained by zero-reward transition imbalance; "explicitly marked concurrency failures are excluded" is stated without counts; and models with unfinished checkpoints are silently dropped from the main table.

## Strengths

- Clean experimental design: permissive-exploration vs strict-held-out-evaluation separation with disjoint seeds and a hard rule that evaluation trajectories never enter experience — this isolates genuine transfer rather than memorization of test seeds.
- The s-vs-r separation is an elegant instrument: because self-judgments are logged at every step and verifier rewards are hidden but recorded, judging reliability becomes a first-class measured capability rather than an assumption.
- Honest, decision-relevant negative results: summarization can be catastrophic (GPT-5.5 PvZ AUC+ 548.5 → 33.2), training can be destructive (PvZ 23 → 6), and judgment quality is uncorrelated with gains — useful correctives to the "more experience data = better agent" narrative.
- Breadth: 7 games × 7 frontier models × 2 context pathways, plus a 116,117-transition judging audit and qualitative successful/failure summary case studies with exact ΔNABA outcomes.
- The NABA metric and the retain/avoid/direct summary schema are reusable contributions for future self-evolution experiments.

## Limitations

- Only 3 strict-mode evaluation episodes per checkpoint and no variance quantification, repeated runs, or significance testing; visible trajectory oscillation suggests many headline deltas are within noise.
- Parameter-training findings rest on one model (Qwen3-8B) and one recipe (SFT with filtering) — no RL/preference-optimization baselines, no frontier-model training arm, and no ablation explaining the PvZ negative transfer.
- No oracle-organized-experience control to quantify how much improvement is lost to inaccurate self-judging versus to the pathway itself.
- All environments are synthetic text games with disparate score scales; cross-game comparison is rank-based only, and sparse-score games make Max/AUC+ volatile.
- Self-judging is evaluated with binary agreement and min-max NMAE only; long-horizon judgment drift and the effect of judgment errors on summary content are not directly analyzed.
- No stated code/data release in the paper text (only a project page), which limits reproduction of the exact game configurations and model runs.

## Relevance

该论文正中第一优先领域 Self-Evolving Agents & Automated Research：它把"自我提升"从口号变成可测能力，给出三路径（上下文/记忆/参数）同协议对比，以及"自评质量与实际提升几乎不相关（ρ=−0.010）"这一对自进化 agent 可靠性研究极有价值的负面结论——你关注的长期自主系统可信性与经验复用瓶颈都可在此找到量化证据。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would an oracle condition — the same exploration trajectories organized by true verifier rewards instead of self-judgments — close the improvement gap? This would directly quantify the "judging tax" S³Gym imposes.
- Does replacing trajectory-filtering SFT with RL (or DPO on judged good/bad pairs) eliminate the negative transfer on Plants-vs-Zombies, or is the 23 → 6 collapse driven by the exploration/evaluation configuration mismatch itself?
- The coupling analysis shows accurate local judging is insufficient — what minimal added structure (state abstractions, subgoal verification, counterfactual re-testing of retained rules) converts judgment accuracy into actual gains?
- Do the summary-vs-history boundaries (rules-transferable games favor Summary Memory; state-contingent reactive games favor History ICL) replicate on real tool-use and web-agent workloads with verifiable outcomes?
- Can hybrid schemes — summaries for strategic direction plus a small ring buffer of raw state-contingent evidence — recover GPT-5.5's PvZ performance while keeping Tetris/Trust gains?
