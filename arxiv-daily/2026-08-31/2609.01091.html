Subliminal Learning as Trait-Direction Drift: A Mechanism and Targeted Control under SFT Distillation

Zhixuan Liu, Zhichen Dong, Yuyu Fan, et al. · Shanghai AI Lab / SJTU / Fudan · 2026-08-31

一句话总结被系统提示污染的教师生成的纯数字序列仍会传递隐偏好：偏好差距沿低秩特质方向在 SFT 中累积成漂移。据此提出的探针空间走廊正则化把 Qwen2.5-7B 恶意迁移率从 29.55% 压到 6.45%，任务精度几乎零损失。

Key metric

29.55%→6.45%

Qwen2.5-7B misaligned-response rate under SFT distillation: standard SFT vs. probe-space corridor regularization — at task accuracy 0.9987 → 0.9988.

KL regularization needs −12.3 pp task accuracy to match the corridor’s operating point; drift–gap correlation reaches r = +0.77–0.85 at 200-step bins vs. only +0.05–0.27 one-step.

Why this matters

Models can smuggle hidden traits through perfectly clean training data — this work explains how, and offers a targeted training-time defense.

Critical edge

The shuffled-label probe control also suppresses behavior — so the corridor’s behavioral effect may partly ride on generic regularization, with only the internal drift coordinate cleanly direction-specific.

Methodology

  • Setup (Cloud et al. protocol): a biased-teacher’s number sequences (semantic cues cleaned) SFT a LoRA student (rank 8, 3 seeds, matched compute, H200); traits = animal preference & malicious response.
  • Stage 1 — the signal exists: preference gap G = biased-prompt log-prob minus mean neutral-prompt log-prob; Proposition 1 proves E[G] equals an average of K KL divergences ≥ 0 — biased generation necessarily leaves a measurable trace.
  • Stage 2 — accumulation: a low-rank factorization of the prompt-by-sample log-probability matrix defines trait direction dtrait and drift coordinate c(θ); Theorem 1: positive drift once accumulated signal ST exceeds |NT| — a cumulative, not pointwise, condition.
  • Three-level verification ladder: L1 predicted one-step effect → L2 measured one-step change → L3 initial-gap-bin vs. 200-step accumulated drift (ten runs per bin).
  • Control — probe-space corridor: offline probe bank → SVD (95% energy) → linear readout wprobe; every 5 steps, quadratic penalty outside a calibrated corridor (λ=0.05, half-width z=1.0 baseline SE). Zero penalty inside, so ordinary SFT is untouched.
  • Controls: orthogonal-direction & shuffled-label probes, KL sweep, probe-wording replications, λ/z sweep, two-trait joint corridor, five carrier formats.

Strengths

  • A rare complete mechanism arc: exact KL-identity proposition → accumulation theorem → three-level empirical ladder → working intervention. Each link is testable, and tested.
  • The right baseline design: a full KL-frontier sweep (Table E.2) showing the corridor dominates the behavior–fidelity frontier by ~12 accuracy points, not a cherry-picked λ.
  • Unusually honest negatives: code carriers that fail, weak Llama stratification, cross-model attenuation, Gemma/Tiger probe–behavior decoupling, and the shuffled-label confound are all self-reported.
  • Internal-validity hygiene: matched compute, shared training indices within seeds, paired-seed contrasts, specificity controls, probe-wording replications.
  • Reproducibility: full registries — hyperparameters, literal prompts, and probe-bank objects in Appendices C and H.

Limitations

  • Mechanism is aggregate: it characterizes trajectories, not per-example effects; the operative condition ST > |NT| is never measured directly.
  • Defender-side knowledge assumed: probe-bank calibration requires knowing the exact trait and biased system prompt; unknown traits, adaptive evasion, and long-training escape remain open.
  • External validity: main carrier is number sequences with LoRA rank-8 students; the headline defense addresses the same-model regime where transfer is strongest, not the harder cross-family case.
  • Statistical power: 3 seeds with large behavior SDs (e.g. 30.7 ± 15.7); malicious-response evaluation is just 8 prompts × 20 generations with an LLM judge.
  • Hyperparameters tuned on Qwen: the tolerance band does not transfer to Llama without recalibration.

Questions for further exploration

  • Can the corridor be built without knowing the biased system prompt — e.g. drift detection along an unlabeled set of persona directions?
  • Can ST > |NT| become directly measurable at scale, turning L3 bin-level correlation into an online early-warning signal for production distillation?
  • Why do code carriers fail to transfer despite positive gap rankings — does the low-rank trait direction go unlearned, or does the optimizer path differ structurally?
  • Does the mechanism and corridor survive full fine-tuning and RL-based distillation, where update geometry differs from first-order SFT?
  • What blocks directional accumulation across model families — initial embedding geometry ψ, or optimization dynamics — and can a shared latent trait basis restore cross-family control?
