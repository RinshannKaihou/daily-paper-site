# Hakken: Predicting future discoveries to fill the gaps in today's knowledge

> **一句话总结：** Sony AI 的 Hakken 系统把"知识预测"形式化为时间知识图谱上的多标签关系预测：THiGERLLM 用 GraphSAGE 式时序图编码器 + Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3 文本分支（图嵌入作为前缀 token 注入 LLM）+ PU 安全的 DWF focal 损失，在 720 万三元组的生物医学图谱上把 2020 截止时间的 macro 召回从上一代 THiGER 的 46.13% 提到 60.72%（macro F1 51.16% vs 50.98%），并配 PHELinE 代理模型解释框架；从 154 万条 aging 相关高置信假设中筛出 3 条送独立 CRO 湿实验，2 条（TP53–BAMBI、RAF1–TNF）获得证实。

> **TL;DR:** Hakken is a domain-general "knowledge prediction" system that forecasts not-yet-published relationships between scientific concepts. Its model, THiGERLLM, fuses a hierarchical temporal graph encoder with Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3 (graph embeddings injected as prefix tokens) trained under a positive-unlabeled multi-label objective. On a temporally held-out benchmark built from PMC Open Access + MEDLINE (7,127,960 triples, 254,806 entities, 23 relation types), THiGERLLM beats the authors' graph-only predecessor THiGER — the strongest baseline — by +14.59 pts macro recall (60.72% vs 46.13%) at the 2020 cut-off, though THiGER still wins weighted F1 (77.97% vs 73.73%) and mean nDCG (92.19 vs 90.30). A decade-long back-test shows slow decay (micro recall ~0.68→~0.58 over 10 years, null baseline ~0.24), and in the paper's headline result, 2 of 3 expert-selected hypotheses (TP53–BAMBI, RAF1–TNF) were experimentally confirmed in wet-lab by an independent CRO, while SOAT1–STAT3 was denied.

## 核心贡献

- **任务重定义：** 把"科学发现预测"形式化为时间知识图谱上的**多标签关系预测**（entity pair (s,o) → 未来可能成立的全部关系类型 r），区别于只预测"是否存在边"的链接预测；置信度经校准后解释为"给定现有文献，该关系本已成立的反事实概率"。
- **THiGERLLM 模型：** 图分支（逐时间切片 GraphSAGE 消息传递 + 层级化 attend-then-merge 时序 Transformer，带 pair-block attention bias 防退化捷径）与语言分支（Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3，每实体仅抽 20 条训练截止前、且不提另一实体的句子以抑制时间泄漏；图嵌入经 temporal token mapper + Fourier 时间编码作为前缀 token 拼入 LLM）。训练用 positive-unlabeled 框架 + PU-safe 动态加权 Focal BCE（DWF）+ 辅助排序损失，推理时按标签做 graph/text 集成并 Platt+isotonic 混合校准。
- **PHELinE 解释框架：** 模型无关（只需访问打分函数）：枚举连接 (s,o) 的 2–4 跳路径作为候选解释，用一次性训练的 GraphSAGE 代理模型做"伪重训"来估计充分性（只留候选事实时分数）与必要性（删掉候选事实时分数跌幅），再按路径多样性重排，秒级输出数千候选的解释。
- **严格的时间协议评测 + 回溯测试：** 训练只用截止年前首次出现的三元组，评测只计截止年后首次出现的关系；1990/2000/2010/2020 四个截止时间 + 每两年一窗的未来十年滑窗分析，并引入"随机经验分数"null 基线。
- **湿实验闭环（罕见）：** 在 1,386 个 aging 相关基因（958,805 个无序实体对、1,543,297 条超阈值假设）上筛出 3 条假设（置信度均 >0.8），交由独立 CRO（Pharmagenome Discovery Services）做湿实验，**2 条证实**：TP53 影响 BAMBI 表达、RAF1 下调 TNF 表达，均为此前文献未记载的相互作用；SOAT1–STAT3 被否定（HepG2 细胞中未观察到 STAT3 磷酸化/蛋白水平变化）。
- 附带模型无关性声明、7 名生医研究者的 GUI 定性用户研究，以及基于 NCBI PubTator3 的开源版（HakkenOSS）。

## Key Contributions

- **Reframes discovery as time-aware multi-label relation prediction:** predicting *which* relation types between an entity pair will emerge, with calibrated confidence scores ("counterfactual probability the relation could already exist"), rather than binary link existence.
- **THiGERLLM:** a two-branch architecture combining a hierarchical temporal graph Transformer (GraphSAGE per snapshot, pair-block attention bias against the degenerate pre-merge shortcut) with Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3, injecting graph embeddings as prefix tokens; trained with a PU-safe Dynamic-Weighted Balanced Focal BCE plus auxiliary ranking loss, with label-wise ensemble and blended Platt/isotonic calibration.
- **PHELinE:** a model-agnostic explanation framework — path-based candidate explanations scored by sufficiency/necessity via a GraphSAGE surrogate that pseudo-retrains the predictor's behavior, with diversity reranking; produces explanations in seconds on graphs with millions of triples.
- **Leakage-aware temporal benchmarking:** training only on triples first observed before the cut-off (1990/2000/2010/2020), evaluating on relations that first appear after, plus a 10-year sliding-window back-test and a random-empirical-scores null baseline.
- **Full wet-lab closure of the loop:** from 1,543,297 above-threshold hypotheses over 958,805 aging-gene entity pairs, down-selection to 2,804, expert selection of 3, and independent CRO validation confirming 2 previously undocumented interactions (TP53–BAMBI, RAF1–TNF; SOAT1–STAT3 denied).

## Methodology

**Task setup.** A temporal knowledge graph G = (V, R, F) with snapshots G^(t); for each entity pair (s,o) the model predicts a binary vector over C=23 relation types. Supervision is positive-unlabeled: observed labels are confirmed positives; unobserved labels are treated as unlabeled, not negative, via PU-safe downweighting.

**Data.** Triples extracted by a rule-based NLP pipeline (provided by an external provider) from PMC Open Access, MEDLINE, and a commercially licensed dataset: 249,329,650 raw triples → cleaned to 254,806 entities, 7,127,960 triples, 23 relation types, 20 macro-domains (ontology sources: MeSH, MONDO, UniProt, HGNC, ChEBI). Expert rules resolved polarity conflicts among AFFECTS/INCREASES/DECREASES; negation and speculative statements are NOT handled (acknowledged limitation).

**Graph branch.** Per time snapshot, GraphSAGE-style neighborhood aggregation over node features (trainable embedding + RDF2VEC ontology-derived vector + domain embedding); pair representations from each snapshot form a temporal sequence processed by a hierarchical Transformer that halves length via attend-then-merge, with an additive attention bias (β ≈ ∞) suppressing attention between soon-to-be-merged pairmates to prevent the "degenerate pre-merge shortcut" failure mode. Output: a global pair vector plus a short temporal prefix sequence.

**LLM branch.** Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3 (released May 2024 — chosen so the pretraining cutoff postdates nothing in the 2020-cutoff evaluation text). For each entity, 20 randomly sampled sentences mentioning it individually from publications dated before training time, never mentioning the pair partner. Graph information enters via (i) M temporal graph tokens (adaptive-pooled, Fourier time-encoded, gated by a learned scalar) prepended to the prompt and (ii) a global pair embedding spliced at a `<htg>` placeholder. Pooling is entity-marker aware when markers exist. Text and graph logits are each history-aware (known labels for the pair modulate scores), ensembled per label, and calibrated (Platt + isotonic blend; per-label thresholds on validation).

**Loss.** DWF = dynamic class-balancing factor × focal factor × PU-weighted BCE − confidence regularizer, with effective-mass normalization per batch; optional pairwise ranking loss on graph embeddings.

**PHELinE.** Candidates = shortest subject–object paths (2–4 hops; plus a latent-path mode admitting high-scored unobserved triples). A GraphSAGE surrogate trained once to reproduce f_θ scores (Eq. D49–D50) enables fast pseudo-retraining: sufficiency(X) = surrogate score with only X; necessity(X) = score drop when X removed; top candidates reranked to prefer distinct relational paths.

**Benchmark protocol.** Train on triples first observed before cut-off year t; evaluate on pairs whose relation instances are first observed after t (validation: 100,000 sampled pairs first appearing after t, excluded from test). Baselines: Random, ComplEx, KNN, MLP, RuleBased (common-neighbor + preferential attachment), TNodeEmbed (LSTM over node-embedding sequences), THiGER (authors' prior temporal model), and partial Agatha components (reformulated multi-label, no graph-embedding learning; only 1990/2000 cutoffs due to resource demands).

## Results & Highlights

- **Main benchmark (2020 cut-off, Table 1):** THiGERLLM achieves **F1_macro 51.16% vs 50.98% for THiGER** (+0.18 pts) and **Recall_macro 60.72% vs 46.13%** (+14.59 pts), but **cedes weighted F1 (73.73% vs 77.97%), weighted precision (84.01% vs 86.15%), and mean nDCG (90.30% vs 92.19%) to THiGER**. Both crush all other baselines: the next best macro F1 is TNodeEmbed at 29.17% and MLP at 28.75% (Random: 3.78%). The authors argue recall is the more meaningful metric in this PU setting because "false positives" may simply be unlabeled-yet-true triples.
- **Top-m analysis (Table C1):** under a fixed prediction budget the LLM advantage sharpens — at **m=3**, THiGERLLM reaches **F1_macro 44.68% vs THiGER's 37.71%**; at **m=5**, **Recall_macro 72.34% vs 71.95%** and **F1_macro 38.26% vs 25.95%**. At m=1 the two models are nearly tied (F1_macro 35.94 vs 34.48).
- **Rare-relation gains (Fig. C5 regression):** the THiGERLLM-over-THiGER recall delta vs log-support has a significantly negative slope (**p=0.004, R²=0.079**) — i.e., the LLM branch recovers substantially more of THiGER's misses on **low-support relation types**, while the F1 slope is not significantly different from zero (p=0.916); precision slope also null (p=0.508).
- **Decade-long back-test (Fig. 3):** training at the 1990 cut-off, sliding-window micro recall decays from **~0.68 (first 2-year bin) to ~0.58 (10 years out)**, versus **~0.24** for the random-empirical-scores null baseline; macro recall decays only **~8%** over the decade; mean nDCG stays in a **~0.986–0.990** band across all horizons and all cut-offs (1990/2000/2010), i.e., the "decay profile" of predictability is essentially time-invariant.
- **Wet-lab validation:** 1,386 aging-gene nodes → 958,805 entity pairs → 1,543,297 above-threshold hypotheses → pipeline filtered to 2,804 → experts selected 3 with confidence >0.8 (TP53–BAMBI 0.88391, RAF1–TNF 0.83114, SOAT1–STAT3 0.84183). The independent CRO **confirmed 2 of 3**: a consistent BAMBI expression increase upon P53 induction across initial and confirmatory assays, and a consistent TNF mRNA increase under RAF1-inhibitor treatment (with ERK phosphorylation confirming RAF1 diminution); SOAT1–STAT3 showed no effect on STAT3 phosphorylation or protein level in HepG2 cells.
- **Compute:** training used two machines, each with 8 NVIDIA H100 80GB GPUs and 960GB RAM (16 H100s total), with run duration measured in days — deliberately modest next to web-scale LLM pretraining, since the knowledge itself comes from curated literature.

## Authors & Credibility

The author list is a large (24-person), multi-site Sony AI team (Barcelona, Zurich, Tokyo, London, New York) together with the Systems Biology Institute (Tokyo), OIST, and UT Austin. It includes several high-profile figures: **Hiroaki Kitano** (Systems Biology, AI-for-science pioneer), **Peter Stone** and **Peter Wurman** (Sony AI / robotics), and **Michael Spranger** (Sony AI CEO). Corresponding author **Tarek R. Besold** has a long track record in computational creativity and AI-for-science. The work builds directly on the authors' prior THiGER model, and the wet-lab portion was executed by an independent CRO (Pharmagenome Discovery Services Ltd), which strengthens the credibility of the biological confirmations beyond self-reporting. Domain expertise (aging genetics experts selecting hypotheses; a panel of biomedical experts cleaning relation types) is evident. This is a serious, well-resourced industrial research effort rather than a small academic prototype. An open-source variant (HakkenOSS, trained on PubTator3) supports partial reproducibility.

## Critical Assessment

- **The "new benchmark" headline rests on a metric choice that favors the new model.** THiGERLLM's macro-F1 win over THiGER is +0.18 pts (51.16 vs 50.98) — effectively a tie — and THiGER actually wins weighted F1 (77.97 vs 73.73), weighted precision (86.15 vs 84.01), and nDCG (92.19 vs 90.30). The paper's argument that recall matters more because negatives are unobserved is defensible in a PU setting, but it is also convenient: the same unobservability argument undermines the interpretability of the precision figures still reported in the same table, and "macro vs weighted" is never reconciled beyond asserting rare relations matter more.
- **The baseline field is weak, and the strongest baseline is the authors' own prior model.** Random, ComplEx, KNN, MLP, and a rule-based classifier are trivially outclassed (all ≤29.17 macro F1); Agatha was re-implemented *without* graph-embedding learning and only benchmarked at the 1990/2000 cutoffs "due to resource demands." None of the LLM-based hypothesis-generation systems the paper itself discusses (HypoGeniC, ResearchAgent, AI Co-Scientist) is empirically compared — so claims of being complementary to them are untested.
- **Residual temporal leakage is acknowledged but not quantified.** Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3's pretraining corpus cutoff is uncontrolled; excluding pair-co-occurring sentences prevents direct leakage of the *label*, but the LLM certainly knows e.g. that TP53 is a tumor suppressor and that TNF is inflammatory — exactly the background knowledge needed to connect pairs. There is no ablation swapping in an older/smaller LLM to isolate how much of the +14.6 macro recall comes from pretraining knowledge vs. the provided evidence sentences.
- **Ground truth is noisy by construction.** Relations come from an external rule-based extraction pipeline that does not handle negation or speculative statements; polarity conflicts among AFFECTS/INCREASES/DECREASES required expert adjudication (Table E2), and the authors explicitly did *not* audit sampled triples themselves, relying on the provider's QC. The recall numbers are therefore measured against an imperfect silver standard.
- **The wet-lab result (the paper's most striking claim) is n=3, expert-curated.** Three hypotheses were hand-picked by biomedical experts out of 2,804 filtered candidates; there is no control on the confirmation rate one would get from random high-confidence pairs or from expert selection without the model, so 2/3 cannot be turned into a calibrated success-rate claim. The denied hypothesis (SOAT1–STAT3) was tested in a single cell line (HepG2) with one activation protocol — a narrow basis for denial. The "1.5 million scored hypotheses" figure is a pool size, not an evaluation set.
- **PHELinE has no quantitative evaluation in the paper.** Explanation fidelity depends entirely on the GraphSAGE surrogate approximating the predictor under perturbation (the authors admit this), yet no surrogate-fidelity metric, no human-rating study of explanation quality beyond the 7-participant qualitative GUI feedback ("overwhelmingly positive" — no numbers), and no comparison to existing GNN-explanation baselines are provided.
- **Reproducibility gap:** the open-source release is trained on PubTator3, not the benchmark dataset, which includes a commercially licensed component that cannot be shared — so the released artifact cannot reproduce the headline Table 1 numbers.

## Strengths

- **Closes the loop end-to-end:** hypothesis generation → explanation → expert selection → *independent CRO wet-lab confirmation* (2/3, including a p53–BAMBI axis with plausible TGF-β/drug-repurposing implications). Full-cycle experimental validation of AI-generated hypotheses is extremely rare and is this paper's most valuable evidence.
- **Genuinely time-aware methodology:** strict temporal train/test separation, four cut-off decades, a sliding-window back-test distinguishing short-term from long-term predictability, and a random-empirical-scores null baseline — a much more careful evaluation culture than typical KG-completion papers.
- **Honest, nuanced metric discussion:** the paper openly reports that THiGERLLM loses weighted precision/F1 and nDCG, explains the PU-based reasoning for preferring recall, and shows the LLM's gains concentrate on rare, low-support relation types (p=0.004) — precisely where scientific discovery value lies.
- **Strong tail behavior and stability:** macro recall decays only ~8% over a 10-year horizon with nDCG nearly flat (~0.986–0.990), and decay profiles are invariant across 1990/2000/2010 cut-offs, supporting the claim that the model's prioritization stays informative as knowledge grows.
- **Engineering substance:** the pair-block attention bias against the hierarchical pre-merge shortcut, PU-safe DWF loss, history-aware label correction, and label-wise calibration are all concrete, well-motivated design decisions; PHELinE's surrogate pseudo-retraining makes counterfactual explanation tractable at millions-of-triples scale.
- **Deliberately frugal compute:** 7B LLM + graph encoder on 16 H100s for days, arguing curated peer-reviewed data can substitute for web-scale pretraining.

## Limitations

- Knowledge is represented as first-occurrence (s, r, o) triples only: no quantitative or conditional information (doses, ratios, environments), no evidence-count metadata, and no prediction of *when* a relationship will appear.
- Precision is fundamentally ill-defined in the evaluation setting (unobserved ≠ false), so recall and ranking metrics are the only sound signals — the reported precision columns should be read cautiously.
- Rule-based extraction ignores negation and speculation; polarity conflicts had to be patched by expert rules, and the team did not independently audit the triples.
- LLM branch carries unavoidable background knowledge from pretraining with an uncontrolled cutoff; the 20-sentences-per-entity mitigation caps but does not eliminate this.
- Wet-lab validation covers only 3 expert-selected hypotheses in a single domain (aging) with limited protocols (one cell line for the denied hypothesis); no selection-free confirmation baseline.
- PHELinE explanations are unquantified (surrogate fidelity unevaluated); the GUI study is qualitative (7 researchers).
- The open-source HakkenOSS variant uses PubTator3, so public artifacts do not reproduce the main benchmark; the full dataset includes a non-shareable commercial component.

## Relevance

该论文正中"Self-Evolving Agents & Automated Research"这一最高优先级方向：它是一个可领域迁移的自动科学发现系统（自动假设生成 + 归因解释 + 湿实验闭环），并且罕见地对长周期预测的可靠性与时间泄漏问题做了系统评估。其"置信度分数作为反事实证据权重"的表述和 PHELinE 的可解释性框架，对构建可信的长时自主科研 agent 有直接借鉴价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How much of THiGERLLM's +14.6 macro-recall gain over THiGER survives when the LLM branch is replaced with a model whose pretraining provably predates the evaluation horizon (e.g., an early-2010s checkpoint) — i.e., what is the true vs. leaked contribution of pretraining knowledge?
- What would a selection-controlled wet-lab study show — e.g., wet-lab testing a random sample of above-0.8-confidence hypotheses, or expert picks made *without* seeing model output — to calibrate the 2/3 confirmation rate into a usable precision estimate?
- Can PHELinE's sufficiency/necessity scores be validated against human expert judgments of mechanistic plausibility, and how faithful is the GraphSAGE surrogate to THiGERLLM under realistic perturbations?
- Does the observed time-invariant "decay profile" of predictability (identical across 1990/2000/2010 cut-offs) reflect a real structural property of scientific knowledge, or an artifact of the rule-based extraction pipeline's stability over decades?
- Can Hakken's calibrated confidence scores actually serve as a "knowledge score" for drug-development triage (as the discussion proposes), and does it transfer beyond biomedicine to their proposed domains (agricultural science, materials science)?
