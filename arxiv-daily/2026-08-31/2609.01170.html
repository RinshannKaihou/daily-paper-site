Pre-carved Niches: The Formation Dynamics of Modular Task Partitions in Early LLM Training

Guangqi Li, Yongxin Li · Zaozhuang University · 2026-08-31

Mechanistic interpretability Training dynamics Modularity Attribution patching Training observability

一句话总结任务“地图”在随机权重的 step 0 就已预雕刻（主导任务对重叠 0.153 ≈ 归因基底的 3.6 倍），随后经两次陡峭跳变锁定；梯度层的“相对剥夺”不传播到权重，对基底的偏离只出现在正在学习的域。

Key metric

3.6×

the dominant task pair’s step-0 overlap (0.153) vs. the attribution substrate (0.0422) — measured on random weights, before any training step.

Lock-in is sharp: second jump +0.154 (20.4σ quiet-window / 6.2σ global). Winners receive 2.25→2.73× the losers’ gradient supply — yet weight drift stays only 0.40–0.82%.

Why this matters

A template for training-run observability: per-step probes, dual calibration, and falsification cards make “when does modularity form” measurable for the first time.

Critical edge

The “pre-carved” headline is weaker than it sounds: at step 0 there are no circuits to describe — only statistical alignment between minimal-pair input geometry and random features.

Methodology

  • From-scratch run: Pythia-410M (24 layers, 98,304 MLP neurons) on a pure Pile stream, batch 2²¹ tokens/step, linear LR ramp 2×10⁻⁶→9×10⁻⁵; two trajectories (run1 bf16, run2 fp32) archived at every step — checkpoints, five probe arrays, task-conditioned gradients, attribution tensors, loss decompositions.
  • Task battery: 14 minimal-pair tasks across 4 domains (8 language incl. 3 determiner–noun agreement variants, 2 theory-of-mind, 4 physical reasoning), following Han et al. 2026.
  • Attribution patching → rosters: (activation difference) × (gradient of logit difference); each task’s roster is its top-983 neurons (1%); pairwise overlap is single-sided.
  • Substrate calibration: raw overlap mixes task sharing with a shallow-layer substrate, so effects are reported as deviation from a frozen step-1 sibling baseline (0.0422) — “×random” multiples explicitly rejected as the wrong zero point.
  • Dual calibrations, never mixed: event sizes reported under quiet-window (MAD) and global σ; signed vs. absolute localization calibers kept separate; turnover/migration adjudicated per-step against random same-size controls.
  • Falsification protocol: eight candidate mechanisms (F1–F8) tested against pre-set quantitative thresholds, each excluded/rejected with its strongest measured evidence.

Results & highlights

  • The sketch predates learning: step-0 dominant-pair overlap 0.153 ≈ 3.6× substrate, adjective siblings below substrate (0.028–0.029); replicates across trajectories (0.1404 vs 0.142) and seeds (CV 0.036). By step 85 deviation reaches 12.7×.
  • Two-jump lock-in, not gradual growth: run2 jumps +0.067 (8.9σ) at step 18→19 and +0.154 at 79→80; run1 replicates the morphology. Amplitudes do not track the LR schedule (LR ×3.33 between jumps, jumps ×2.36, event correlation +0.021).
  • Lock is high-churn reorganization: shared set grows +71%/+121% but old-member retention is only 60%/44%; a stable 165-neuron skeleton carries >90% of the shared set while 1,124 of 2,303 ever-admitted neurons ping-pong. Overlap retraces 0.19 then spontaneously recovers to 0.30 — no one-shot irreversibility.
  • Deprivation stops at the gradient layer: winners get 2.25→2.73× the losers’ gradient supply (9.5–11.5σ below random control), but Adam effective-update ratio ≤1 and weight drift 0.40–0.82% — losers are excluded from learning, not starved, with late weak learning (≈1/10 magnitude) after step 92.
  • Deviation tracks learning: language loss drifts −0.20→−1.77 while physics/ToM stay flat (≈−0.05); both lock-in jumps are language-only; physics overlap sits at substrate level (≈1.02) for 81/85 steps. Physics modularity at 2.8B is pre-registered, not yet measured.
  • Layer-0 band and word-form sensitivity: 39–45% of every roster sits in layer 0, invariant across six corpus variants; the adjective variant’s layer-0 attribution is exactly zero (all 98,304 neurons) under the matched corpus; swapping the answer token replaces ≈85% of roster members.

Strengths

  • Genuinely new measurement capability: per-step attribution patching, probe arrays, and task-conditioned gradients across an entire from-scratch pretraining run — formation dynamics becomes a measurable object.
  • Exemplary measurement discipline: substrate-calibrated effect sizes, dual σ-calibration always reported, frozen localization calibers never mixed, per-step adjudication against random controls.
  • Negative results done right: eight falsification cards with pre-set thresholds sharply constrain mechanism space; the anomaly registry exposes unresolved contradictions instead of hiding them.
  • Multi-layer causal hygiene: the gradient/update/weight/loss decomposition cleanly separates “deprived gradients” from “starved weights,” blocking an easy overclaim.
  • Unusual candor: caliber-conditional labeling, pre-registered 2.8B test with explicit candidate hypotheses, and a self-identified “largest evidence gap.”

Limitations

  • Two trajectories of one model: Pythia-410M on one data stream; the bf16/fp32 confound between runs is unresolved and cross-seed training replication is incomplete.
  • One construction family: feature-level conclusions rest on determiner–noun agreement; the 22-task orthogonal family is design-frozen but unrun, so “which feature drives separation” is open.
  • Deprivation is correlational: no supply-equalization intervention; the second jump’s trigger (threshold accumulation vs. supply-ratio inflection vs. data-order cluster) is unresolved.
  • The scale-threshold story is a prediction: the 2.8B physics-modularity evidence is relayed from official checkpoints, not measured — zero measurements so far.
  • Caliber- and protocol-specific numbers: signed/absolute/membership calibers disagree materially; no external replication anchor; artifacts are SHA-256-bound but not publicly released, with two referenced audit files missing.
  • The mechanism level is unanswered: why structural separation reshapes attribution in a random network, and where the ≈40% layer-0 band comes from, remain open.

Questions for further exploration

  • What actually triggers the second lock-in jump — intrinsic threshold accumulation, a supply-ratio inflection, or a data-order cluster boundary?
  • Does the pre-carved step-0 map generalize beyond determiner–noun agreement — which axes of the frozen 22-task orthogonal family carry the separation?
  • Is gradient-level relative deprivation causal — would a supply-equalization intervention change lock-in morphology or roster churn?
  • Is physics modularity genuinely scale-gated — does the 2.8B run cross the four-branch physics criterion, and do the S1–S6 signatures hold?
  • How much of “task partition” identity is output-token identity rather than shared computation, given the answer-token swap replaces ≈85% of members?
  • Is the ≈40% layer-0 concentration band a property of the architecture or of the attribution-patching instrument on shallow networks?
