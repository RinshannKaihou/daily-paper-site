Context-Grounding Gains Are Mediated by Pre-existing Machinery: Auditing GRPO, SFT, and DPO

Key metric

40%

of DPO’s grounding gain recovered by injecting one direction into the untouched base model — zero training (35% at the largest dose passing all side-effect checks).

Meanwhile DPO hits .964 CounterFact update rate vs GRPO’s best +.036 (4-seed CIs include zero); conflict-SFT +.062.

Why this matters

Before crediting a training recipe for grounding gains, audit what the checkpoint already had — cheap steering may get you a third of the way for free.

Critical edge

Arms vary objective, data, and budget together — the dramatic DPO-vs-GRPO gap identifies complete recipes, and cannot be attributed to the optimization objective itself.

01 Methodology

Setup. A nine-arm controlled audit launched from one Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct checkpoint, replicated across Qwen 3B/7B, Llama-3.2-3B, and Phi-3.5-mini. Arms are complete training recipes — objective, data, and budget vary together by design.

  1. Nine arms: five GRPO variants differing only in reward (answer F1, evidence prompt, citation F1, contrastive context-utility, in-context counterfactual), three SFT arms (no-conflict control, KAFT-style mix, 78% conflict), one DPO (4,500 pairs, β=.5) — 4 seeds on key arms.
  2. Conflict protocol: CounterFact two-pass — a frozen known set (n=1089, computed once from the base model, reused by every arm) then a counterfactual context; update rate = follow-ctx / (follow-ctx + follow-mem). ConFiQA and FaithEval replicate; HotpotQA tracks the rewarded task.
  3. Statistics: exact paired McNemar, seed-level t-CIs, TOST equivalence at ±.044 — plus a metric-integrity audit that re-based all intervention results after finding a factor-of-four gate-flip bias.
  4. Mechanism audit: a difference-in-means grounding direction estimated from the base model before training; per-head knockout vs the base top-8 causal heads; add/subtract steering with norm-matched random controls and KL / MMLU / ARC side-effect checks.

GRPO: Shao et al. recipe, 200 steps, TRL · SFT: 2 epochs over the same 1,500-example pool · interventions validated against matched-norm random directions.

  • Three-way dissociation at 1.5B: GRPO +.004 to +.036 (4-seed CIs include zero; TOST within ±.044 of SFT’s gain), conflict-SFT +.062, DPO +.378 — McNemar p ≤ 2.5e−25.
  • Rewarded metric decouples: the same GRPO runs lift HotpotQA F1 +.120 while grounding transfer to ConFiQA (.500) and FaithEval (.680) stays at base level.
  • The machinery pre-exists: base top-8 causal heads recovered 8/8 (GRPO, DPO) and 7/8 (SFT), hypergeometric p ≤ 7.1e−13; the base-estimated direction stays aligned with every arm (cosine ≥ .915).
  • Causal add/subtract: injecting the direction lifts base grounding +.109; subtracting it removes −.276 (DPO) and −.405 (conflict-SFT) of the trained gains.
  • Amplification, not rotation: DPO’s ConFiQA grounding jumps .577 → .932 by step 160 of 800 (~90% of final) while the direction’s cosine to base stays ≥ .968 throughout.

03 Strengths

  • Controlled common-checkpoint design with a frozen known set and paired per-item statistics removes most confounds plaguing post-training comparisons.
  • Causal, not correlational: add/subtract steering, head knockout, cross-task asymmetry, a 0/8-head recall control, and norm-matched random directions.
  • Statistical care unusual for interpretability: seed t-CIs, TOST equivalence testing, exact McNemar — the null-ish GRPO result is quantitative, not hand-wavy.
  • Self-audited metric integrity: caught a factor-of-four gate-flip bias and re-based every intervention result on the frozen no-intervention known set.
  • Honest scoping throughout — conclusions never outrun the evidence.

04 Limitations

  • Lexical-containment metric agrees with an LLM judge only at κ=.507; the SFT and GRPO contrasts were not re-validated under judge labels.
  • Mechanistic audit is deepest at 1.5B and covers six of nine arms; interventions are single-seed; 7B / Llama / Phi are partially behavioral.
  • Recipe confound by design — the dissociation identifies recipes, not objectives.
  • Reuse account is partial: 60–65% of DPO’s gain is unexplained; suppression raises the non-decisive fraction .27 → .41; Llama suppression n.s.
  • Coverage explanation weakly tested — the small-GRPO finding is a bound on this configuration, not a verdict on RL for grounding.
  • Factual-QA conflicts only; no instruction-following, long-context, or multi-hop settings.

05 Questions for further exploration

  1. Does mechanism reuse hold for pretrained (not instruction-tuned) starting checkpoints, or is the pre-existing direction an artifact of instruction tuning itself?
  2. What explains the remaining 60–65% of DPO’s gain that steering cannot recover — new machinery, distributed changes, or the non-decisive-fraction confound?
  3. Would a grounding-targeting GRPO reward plus a fix for within-group reward variation break the +.017 ceiling observed here?
  1. Do the grounding direction and its causal head set stay stable under much longer DPO training or at 30B+ scale, where norm calibration may not transfer?
  2. Can the pre-existing direction be used prospectively — as a checkpoint-selection or data-mixing signal estimated before committing to an expensive DPO run?
