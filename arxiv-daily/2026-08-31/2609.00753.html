LLM context‑vs‑memory authority is causally real within a task — but decodable ≠ causally used ≠ transferable across tasks.

How Do Language Models Choose Between Context and Memory? (Authority Directions)

Benjamin Shih, John Winnicki, Arianna Cao  ·  Stanford University & Perpetual Labs  ·  2026-09-01

mechanistic interpretability activation steering knowledge conflict causal mediation LLM hidden states

一句话总结：在 Qwen2.5 / Llama-3.1 / OLMo-2 上从一致提示中提取低层「权威方向」，用交换式（而非加性）干预在匹配提示间互换天然激活分量，可在色彩冲突任务上复现 68% / 56% / 30% 的权威选择偏移（随机对照 ≈ 0）；但颜色方向导入材料任务仅关闭 9% 的权威差距，且 state/country 上 AUROC 1.0 的方向几乎无因果作用（最大单层效应 0.014–0.038）——权威计算很可能是任务依赖的。

Key metric
68%
of the natural authority gap closed by interchange in Qwen2.5-7B (95% CI 61–75) · Llama 56%, OLMo 30% · random-direction controls ≈ 0

Swapping the naturally occurring activation component along the authority direction between matched high/low-authority prompts, repeated over 8 layers — 18× the best covariance-matched control in Qwen.

Why this matters

A methodological template for interpretability: probes alone prove little without interchange tests — and it speaks directly to why RAG models trust context or parametric memory.

The decodable / causally-used / reusable three-way split is immediately reusable in any steering study.

Critical edge

The within-task causal claim is near-threshold outside Qwen (control margins 2.2–2.9×; matched-spectrum separation 1.2× on Llama), and the cross-task null rests on a single-layer import protocol.

Imported direction vs strongest control: +0.043, 95% CI [0.000, 0.103] — not significant.

Methodology

  • Three synthetic conflict tasks. Single-token answers where parametric memory is true and context asserts a falsehood: 12-color, 132-object material, and state/country fact tables. Authority varied by framing (“definitely” vs “unverified rumor”) plus four more cue families (wording, source credibility, cited evidence, consensus, first-person confidence).
  • Direction estimation. sℓ = normalized hhigh−hlow at the final prompt position from 200 agreement prompts, on frozen instruct models (Qwen2.5-7B/3B, Llama-3.1-8B, OLMo-2-7B); intervention layers chosen once on Qwen and transplanted by relative depth (readout 0.71–0.75).
  • Interchange, not additive steering. The natural component along sℓ is swapped between matched high/low prompts (orthogonal residual preserved), repeated over 8 layers; effect = GapClosure, the reciprocal-swap probability change normalized by the natural gap, with 5k/4k/10k bootstrap resamples.
  • Control battery. Random, covariance-matched, matched-spectrum (SVD) controls and a self-copy null under identical layers/signs/donors; an EA0 tautology prompt with an orthogonal subspace N to absorb format confounds; rank ablation validates the authority subspace.

Flow: extract direction → verify within-task by interchange vs controls → import across tasks → compare decodability against causal effect → conclude the three properties dissociate.

  • Additive steering is behaviorally potent (Qwen, layer 21): context-following moves 0.14→0.76 on low-authority conflicts and 0.90→0.06 on high; direction stable across disjoint color vocabularies (cosine 0.96).
  • Cross-task transfer largely fails. The local material direction closes 57% of its 0.560 authority gap; the imported color direction closes only 9% — and beats its best covariance-matched control by just 0.043, CI [0.000, 0.103].
  • Decodable but causally inert. On state/country facts the direction decodes cross-domain at AUROC 0.995–1.000 against natural gaps of 0.454/0.492, yet the largest single-layer causal effect is only 0.014–0.038, with robustness-augmented estimates essentially null (−0.038 / −0.049).
  • Cue-family geometry. Wording, credibility, evidence, and consensus directions raise context-following by 0.5–0.9 and cross-steer; first-person confidence is near-null (“I am certain” shifts natural behavior by only 0.01).

Strengths

  • The right causal test: interchange of naturally occurring components between matched prompts, with an explicit argument for why additive steering alone cannot establish endogenous use.
  • Exemplary controls: covariance-matched and matched-spectrum comparisons under identical layers/signs/donors, self-copy null, EA0 tautology plus orthogonal subspace N, bootstrap CIs on essentially every estimate.
  • Crisp conceptual split — decodable / causally used / cross-task reusable — supported by a consistent quantitative story across three tasks and four models.
  • Honest negative reporting: the CI that includes zero and the Qwen-dependence of the strongest effects are disclosed, not buried.

Limitations

  • Tiny synthetic stimuli: 200 estimation prompts per direction, 132 objects, 32×2 fact tables — directions may overfit prompt-template geometry; no naturalistic document-level or multi-token conflicts.
  • Four instruct models at 3–8B scale, layers transplanted from Qwen by relative depth; effects markedly weaker on Llama/OLMo.
  • Protocol-bounded null: cross-task transfer tested only via single-layer import of a 1-D direction; multi-layer or subspace-level transfer untested (acknowledged).
  • Incomplete even in-task: 1-D directions close at most 68% of the gap; first-person confidence is a null family, limiting “authority” as a single construct. No released code; preprint-stage, unreviewed.

Questions for further exploration

  • Would per-task authority subspaces, aligned across tasks via matched nonconflicting prompts, recover cross-task causal transfer — or is the computation fundamentally non-linear?
  • Does repeated multi-layer interchange with an imported direction restore causal efficacy on the state/country task?
  • Do the dissociations hold for multi-token answers and naturalistic RAG conflicts with long contradictory documents?
  • How does the direction’s geometry differ between base and instruct checkpoints, and in families where the control margin collapses (Llama, OLMo)?
  • Why is first-person confidence a near-null cue family while wording, evidence, and consensus steer strongly — is self-referential authority encoded elsewhere?
