Why this matters

A concrete instance of trustworthy validation for autonomous agent loops: the “LLM proposes, rules adjudicate” pattern transfers directly to automated scientific discovery.

Critical edge

The headline CSI 500 number is roughly double every alternative backbone — plausibly a GPT-4o lucky configuration rather than a property of the framework itself.

Also unaddressed: GPT-4o priors may leak regime knowledge from the 2022–23 training window; and search budgets are matched-data, not matched-search (admitted in Appendix N).

01 Methodology

Four GPT-4o agents (Hypothesis · Observation · Factor · Validation; temperatures 0.9/0.7/0.7/0.1) run a three-stage loop, backtested in Qlib on daily OHLCV only. Train 2022–23 · validate 2024 · test 2025.

1Decompose→ 2Validate→ 3Integrate→ 4Optimize
  • Decompose — Hypothesis agent turns human market insights into an economic hypothesis; Observation agent splits it into independent OHLCV-observable conditions (“post sell-off state”, “intraday recovery”); Factor agent emits 2–3 candidate formulas per condition from a fixed ~40-operator library (RANK, TS_ZSCORE, DELTA, CORR, RSI, MACD…), keeping every formula interpretable.
  • Validate — factor values sorted into 5 quantile bins per date; each bin gets statistical profiles of OHLCV state variables (intraday direction C−O, range H−L, close position, volume). Four jointly-required deterministic checks: central-tendency shift across ordered bins, non-trivial tail variation, multi-statistic consistency, semantic consistency. The LLM only maps text to variables and expected directions — pass/fail is a rule conjunction. Failures loop back with refinement feedback (max 5 iterations).
  • Integrate — validated factors form candidate combinations gated by a σ-ladder Directional Selectivity criterion (realized returns must hold as σ tightens) plus ticker-level support thresholds (0.5 / 0.7).
  • Optimize — Optuna (20 trials) maximizes the Calmar ratio on 2024; thresholds frozen before the 2025 test. Anti-leakage memory: the outer loop stores only hypothesis IDs, decompositions, and one scalar IR — no raw returns, no formulas.

03 Strengths

  • Deterministic by construction: the LLM never issues the final accept/reject; every decision is an auditable conjunction of four explicit statistical rules (backed by the 8,362-case coded-rule audit).
  • Clean experimental hygiene: strict temporal splits, thresholds frozen pre-test, transaction costs applied, 10-run variance, monthly-start sensitivity, blinded expert audit.
  • Decisive ablation: without Stage 3 the framework goes catastrophically negative (AR −0.3902) — the integration machinery is load-bearing, not decoration.
  • Interpretability enforced structurally via the fixed operator grammar; the case study shows concrete quantitative PASS/FAIL reasoning (e.g. rejection over +24.15% q90 tail expansion with kurtosis flipping +40 → −17).
  • Unusually candid appendices: provenance funnel, discarded-profitables analysis, scope-of-interpretability disclaimer, search-budget asymmetry disclosure.

04 Limitations

  • Directional Selectivity excludes reversal-type signals by design — it cannot mine mean-reversion-at-extremes patterns, and knowingly discards 47 profitable combinations.
  • Daily-bar OHLCV only: no market-impact or slippage modeling (claimed negligible at ~10-day holdings, unverified at scale).
  • Tiny final portfolios (4 formulas on CSI 500, 3 on S&P 500) with thresholds tuned on a single validation year (2024) — one regime.
  • Not fully autonomous: hypotheses start from 10 human seed concepts — a refinement loop, not open-ended discovery.
  • Concentrated failure mode: volume-dominant, short-covering, and liquidity-driven states systematically fail the gate, bounding the addressable hypothesis space.

05 Questions for Further Exploration

  1. Do the four statistical checks transfer to higher-frequency or non-equity data (futures, crypto), where ε tolerances and quantile-bin structure may behave differently?
  2. Can the GPT-4o headline AR be reproduced with an open-weight backbone (Qwen3-235B: IR 1.0598 but AR 0.0948) — is the gap hypothesis quality, validation mapping, or threshold interaction?
  3. Is the 47-combination “directionally unstable but profitable” rejection set a complementary strategy family a reversal-aware second gate could harvest?
  4. How much of the pass/fail stability under stochastic decoding comes from the deterministic rule conjunction vs the low validation temperature (0.1)?
  5. Can the hypothesis-memory design (IDs + decompositions + scalar IR only) be formalized as a general anti-leakage contract for self-improving agent loops — and tested by corrupting the memory?
