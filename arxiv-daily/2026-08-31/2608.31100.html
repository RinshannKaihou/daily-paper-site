LLM self-improvement is neither automatic nor uniform — and self-judgment quality is essentially uncorrelated with actual gains.

S³Gym: Can LLMs Turn Self-Testing and Self-Judging into Self-Improvement?

Jiajun Shi, Siyuan Tao, Yuhao Wu, Zexuan Wang, et al. — ByteDance Seed · M–A–P · TokenWave.AI  ·  2026-08-31

self-evolving-agents LLM-benchmark agent-memory self-judging-reliability 2026-08-31

Key metric

ρ = −0.010

116,117 transitions · 98 runs · 7 models × 7 games

Correlation between self-judgment quality and subsequent score gain. Accurate local judging alone does not produce self-improvement.

Why this matters

Turns "self-improvement" from slogan into a measurable capability, quantifying the reliability bottleneck for long-horizon self-evolving agents.

⚠ Critical edge

With only 3 strict evaluation episodes per checkpoint and no error bars, "no coupling" is hard to distinguish from "no statistical power to detect coupling."

Methodology

  • One loop, formally: Explore → Judge → Consolidate → Update → Evaluate, run identically for each experience pathway.
  • 7 rule-verifiable text games (Chess, Minesweeper, Nullify, Plants-vs-Zombies, Tetris, Snake, Trust Evolution). Exploration is deliberately easier than held-out evaluation — Chess 15×15/12 pieces vs 22 at eval; Minesweeper grants 2 extra lives vs first-mine-terminates — with disjoint seeds, and evaluation trajectories never re-enter experience.
  • Self-judging as an instrument: every step the agent emits an action plus self-score s; verifier reward r is logged benchmark-side but hidden during exploration, so judgment reliability becomes a measured capability, not an assumption.
  • Three same-protocol pathways: History ICL (raw score-annotated trajectories, 100k-token cap) · Summary Memory (compressed retain/avoid/direct rule triples) · Parameter Training (SFT on high-scoring actions, Qwen3-8B, 20 checkpoints, evaluated with no ICL or memory).
  • Metrics: Avg / Max / AUC+ / NABA over checkpoints {0, 3, …, 30}; judging audit uses event agreement 1[s>0]=1[r>0], normalized MAE, over/under-confidence, and blockwise coupling of judgment quality to next-evaluation gain.
  • Models: GPT-4o, GPT-4.1, o3-mini, GPT-5.5, Gemini-2.5-Flash/Pro, Gemini-3.5-Flash, plus the Qwen3-8B training arm.

  • Judgment quality does not predict improvement: ρ(At, gt) = −0.010, ρ(−Et, gt) = −0.018; agreement vs NABA gives Pearson r = −0.23, Spearman ρ = −0.11 (weak negatives).
  • Base capability ≠ self-improvement. Under History ICL, Gemini-3.5-Flash posts the best Chess Avg (0.547 vs GPT-5.5's 0.017); GPT-5.5 leads the brittle games (PvZ AUC+ 548.5, Nullify AUC+ 96.3). Under Summary Memory the lead pattern flips.
  • Case extremes: ΔNABA +166.89 (GPT-5.5 / Trust, "always defect when payoff favors detection") vs −33.27 (Gemini-3.5-Flash / Chess, a high-level reminder that fails exact multi-piece localization).

Strengths

  • Clean design: permissive exploration vs strict held-out evaluation with disjoint seeds — evaluation trajectories never enter experience, isolating genuine transfer rather than test-seed memorization.
  • Elegant s-vs-r instrument: logging self-scores against hidden verifier rewards makes judging reliability a first-class measured capability.
  • Honest, decision-relevant negatives: summarization can be catastrophic, training can be destructive, and judgment quality is uncorrelated with gains — correctives to "more experience = better agent."
  • Breadth: 7 games × 7 frontier models × 2 context pathways, a 116k-transition judging audit, and qualitative case studies with exact ΔNABA outcomes.
  • Reusable pieces: the NABA metric and the retain/avoid/direct summary schema serve future self-evolution experiments.

Limitations

  • Thin eval sample: 3 strict episodes per checkpoint, no variance quantification, repeated runs, or significance tests — visible trajectory oscillation suggests many headline deltas are within noise.
  • Weakest-link training arm: one 8B model, one trajectory-filtering SFT recipe; no RL or DPO baselines, no frontier-model training arm, and the PvZ 23 → 6 collapse is unexplained.
  • No oracle-experience control to separate the "judging tax" from pathway limitations.
  • Synthetic scope: all games are discrete grid/puzzle environments with disparate score scales — cross-game comparison is rank-based only; sparse-score games make Max volatile.
  • Judging metrics are narrow (binary agreement + min-max NMAE); long-horizon judgment drift unexamined.
  • No stated code/data release (project page only), limiting reproduction.

Questions for Further Exploration

  • Would an oracle condition — the same trajectories organized by true verifier rewards — close the improvement gap and quantify the "judging tax"?
  • Would RL or DPO on judged good/bad pairs eliminate the PvZ negative transfer, or is the 23 → 6 collapse an exploration/evaluation config artifact?
  • What minimal added structure — state abstractions, subgoal verification, counterfactual re-testing — converts judgment accuracy into actual gains?
  • Do the summary-vs-history boundaries replicate on real tool-use and web-agent workloads with verifiable outcomes?
  • Can hybrid schemes — strategic summaries plus a small ring buffer of raw state-contingent evidence — recover GPT-5.5's PvZ while keeping the Tetris/Trust gains?
