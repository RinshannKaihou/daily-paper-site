Critical edge

It's a diagnosis, not a solution: no mitigation experiments, and the published "near-lossless" quantizers (KIVI, KVQuant, ZipCache) are never run — so whether smart mixed-precision compression also breaks grounding stays open.

Critical Assessment

01 Methodology — make cache precision the only variable

  • Isolate. System prompt + top-K retrieved chunks are prefilled once into a single unified KV cache — deliberately avoiding per-chunk caching, whose positional-stitching artifacts would confound any precision comparison. Quantized for storage, dequantized back to bf16 before decoding.
  • Gate. A Stage-0 check proves the BF16 cache round-trip reproduces the Oracle bit-for-bit on all 50 hold-out examples — so every C1–C3 difference is attributable purely to quantization.
  • Measure. Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct on RGB and HotpotQA (300 examples each), K ∈ {1,3,5}, 3,600 generations per dataset. Accuracy: containment-EM / F1. Faithfulness: three independent signals — HHEM-2.1 hallucination rate, DeBERTa-v3 NLI entailment, and a Claude Haiku 4.5 judge (92% human agreement on RGB). Paired McNemar tests on label flips.

conditions: C0 Oracle (no round trip) · C1 BF16 cache · C2 INT8 per-token · C3 INT4 group-wise (g=64) · realized compression: ~1.9× / ~3.6× — metadata overhead eats ~5% / ~11% of the nominal 2× / 4×

  • Noise amplification: the INT4−BF16 hallucination-rate gap grows monotonically with retrieval depth — 0.09→0.26 (RGB) and 0.05→0.25 (HotpotQA) from K=1 to K=5; distractor regression β=0.22, significant at K=3 and K=5.
  • Metric trap: INT4 suppresses refusal (51% → 32% at K=1 on HotpotQA), so the model guesses more — artificially inflating EM and NLI. Accuracy can mask the harm, not just miss it.
  • Semantic, not numerical: non-finite logits in <0.1% of INT4 runs (aborted pre-emission); empty outputs and repetition loops are INT4-only, reaching 6% of RGB generations at K=5.
  • Slope caveat: the K-amplification trend rests on an underpowered test (p=0.12, three K values) — reported as directional by the authors themselves.

03 核心贡献

  • 首个离线 KV-cache 量化忠实性审计：此前工作只优化延迟/存储或只看 accuracy/perplexity，无人检查压缩后回答是否仍被检索证据支撑。
  • 受控协议：单次 prefill 统一 cache + Stage-0 门控（BF16 往返与 Oracle 逐位一致），任何差异纯粹来自量化。
  • 隐性伤害：在 containment-EM 完全不变的样本上，INT4 忠实性仍大幅恶化——答案对了，但不再依赖证据。
  • 噪声放大：退化随检索深度 K 与干扰段落比例单调加剧；INT8 全面安全；HotpotQA 拒绝率被压低反而虚增指标。

04 Strengths

  • Model experimental control: single prefill, unified cache, and a round-trip fidelity gate make precision the only variable.
  • Hidden-harm finding is novel, statistically strong (p<10⁻³⁸), and directly consequential for offline-cache deployments.
  • Three independent faithfulness signals with reported inter-signal agreement (r=0.44; judge 92%/76% vs human).
  • Radical honesty: power analysis for the weak H2, and prominent reporting of the refusal confound that undermines its own NLI/HHEM results.

05 Limitations

  • Single model family and size (Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct); the INT4 faithfulness cliff may move substantially elsewhere.
  • Two 300-example benchmarks only; the amplification slope test is underpowered (p=0.12); H3 evaluated on RGB alone.
  • No smart-quantizer baselines (KIVI / KVQuant / ZipCache / KVTuner) — only naive uniform INT4 is audited.
  • On HotpotQA at low K, H1 rests on a single LLM judge with 76% human agreement; no code release; no 2/3-bit points, so the safe-precision floor is unmapped.

06 Questions for further exploration

  • Do error-compensated or mixed-precision quantizers (per-channel keys, attention-sink retention, query-aware allocation) preserve faithfulness at 4-bit — or is grounding loss intrinsic to 4-bit KV storage?
  • How does the faithfulness cliff scale with model size — do 70B models with lower-entropy KV caches tolerate INT4 grounding-wise?
  • Can degenerate generations (6% at K=5) be localized to specific layers or channels via quantization-error attribution?
  • Would faithfulness-constrained quantization (optimize scales to minimize grounding drift on held-out evidence) recover INT4 at the same ~3.6× compression?
  • Does the same hidden harm appear in multi-turn agentic RAG, where a compressed cache is reused across queries and errors compound?
