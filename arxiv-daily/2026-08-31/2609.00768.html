Key metric

72.3%

Qwen3-8B-Base, average over MATH-500 / GSM8K / OlympiadBench / Minerva / AMC — +4.5 pts over label-free R-Zero (67.8), +1.2 over externally-guided DARC (71.1), at only 5.7% wall-clock overhead.

Why this matters

Shows a self-evolving agent can convert its own failure memory into a schedulable curriculum — a representative advance for unsupervised self-improvement loops.

把长程自主系统的失败记忆转化为可调度的课程信号——自进化 LLM 的代表性新进展，直击 Self-Evolving Agents 方向。

Critical edge

The filter measures solver agreement, not truth: shared errors pass, and roughly a quarter of round-5 pseudo-labels are wrong per GPT-5 relabeling (75% oracle agreement).

01Methodology, distilled

  1. Challenger–solver self-play on the R-Zero skeleton — only the challenger sees the memory. The challenger's GRPO reward is adopted from R-Zero unchanged.
  2. Comparative diagnosis. A frozen 4B diagnostician compares the failing trajectory r⁻ with a pseudo-label-agreeing r⁺, locates the earliest reasoning difference, and extracts an error cause that abstracts away question surface form.
  3. Hierarchical memory. Skill nodes → causes; each cause carries an Active/Mastered state plus failure frequency. Promotion at self-consistency ≥ 0.70; reactivation on new failures closes the loop.
  4. Frequency-driven scheduling. Cause-targeted vs free exploration mixed by ε = Ft/(Ft + 0.5·F₁); cross-state stitching pairs an Active cause with a Mastered sibling from the same skill node.
  5. Double-confidence filtering. Keep a question only if the majority-vote share lies in [0.25, 0.75] and leads the runner-up by ≥ 1.6× — targeting intermediate difficulty without high-conflict labels.
  6. Training. GRPO for both models, 5 rounds, 8×H200; three-run mean±std with baseline reproduction validated to ≤0.1/≤0.5 points.

Best mean in every row of the Qwen3-8B block; math avg 72.3±0.7 vs R-Zero 67.8 and DARC 71.1 (3 runs).

  • Transfer despite a math-only curriculum: general-reasoning average rises 36.3 → 38.8 (Qwen3-8B) and 10.7 → 28.4 (OctoThinker).
  • The relative confidence constraint alone is worth +1.4: no filtering 69.4 → absolute-only 70.9 → full double-confidence 72.3.
  • Diagnostician scale is not the source of gains: 4B → 235B-A22B adds only +1.0–2.8 math points; the 4B default already beats every baseline by ≥1.1 overall.
  • Degeneration controlled: without memory, generated questions balloon 45.6 → 166.9 words; with memory they stay ~73 words while 2-gram diversity still rises 33.3 → 36.5. Oracle agreement at round 5: 65% → 75% (memory +7, relative constraint +3).
  • Honest dynamics: performance peaks at round 5 (72.3) then slips to 72.0 / 71.4 — disclosed, not hidden.

03Strengths

  • A clean answer to a real problem: unguided self-play drifts (45.6 → 166.9-word questions, plateau at 69.0%) and guided methods need external resources — the direction was already inside the solver's failure history.
  • The mechanism is simple and cheap: 5.7% wall-clock overhead over R-Zero's loop, fully deterministic scheduling given the memory, and a crisp Active/Mastered state machine.
  • Careful evaluation hygiene: 3-run std on every cell, baseline reproduction validated before mixing scores from another paper, flat sensitivity tables for both new hyperparameters (k, τ).
  • Gains are consistent across three heterogeneous solvers, and the diagnostician-scale study rules out a "huge judge" artifact.

04Limitations

  • Pseudo-label correctness is unmeasured: double-confidence filtering captures agreement, not truth — shared solver errors pass, and ~25% of round-5 pseudo-labels are wrong.
  • Thin headline margin: DARC (+1.1–1.3 overall) was copied from its own paper, not re-run; the margin sits within plausible cross-paper reproduction noise.
  • No verifiable-reward RL baseline on standard math data — the price of "no external task resources" is never quantified against the simpler alternative.
  • Fixed 5-round horizon with post-peak decline and no adaptive stopping criterion (flagged by the authors).
  • Diagnostician extraction quality has no direct human audit (a single traced example); the pipeline leans on a frozen strong instruct model and a commercial embedding.

05Questions for further exploration

  1. In domains with verifiable rewards (code + unit tests), can the shared-error rate of pseudo-labels be measured directly, closing the gap that oracle agreement only proxies?
  2. Could the diagnostician be the solver itself (self-diagnosis), removing the frozen external instruct model — and how much extraction quality would that cost?
  3. Does the Active/Mastered state machine transfer to open-ended, multi-turn tool-use curricula, where "self-consistency over answers" is ill-defined?
  4. Only base models and R-Zero were re-run locally — what would a full DARC reproduction do to the +1.2-point headline margin?
