> **一句话总结：** Graphcore Research 将 FP4 块缩放格式中的有符号 E4M3 换成无符号 UE5M3（最大值 448→61,440，约 137 倍），从而在 8B 混合 Mamba 模型 188.7B token 预训练中去掉了 NVIDIA NVFP4 配方必需的随机 Hadamard 变换（RHT）和末层 BF16 豁免、改用 D=50 的周期性延迟张量缩放，最终训练损失 2.3090 对 TE NVFP4 的 2.3474、held-out NLL 2.32230 对 2.32592，并测得去 RHT + 全 FP4 线性层可提升 21.2% 模型体吞吐。

> **TL;DR:** The authors replace NVFP4's signed E4M3 block scales with unsigned UE5M3 scales (max finite 61,440 vs 448), whose wider range makes a simpler recipe viable: periodic sample-and-hold tensor scaling every D=50 optimizer steps instead of current-amax scaling, no randomized Hadamard transform (RHT), stochastic rounding only on the upstream-gradient operand, and FP4 in all 112 eligible internal linears (no BF16 exemption for final blocks). On a Nemotron-H 8B pretrained 188.7B tokens on an OLMo-family mixture, the proposed recipe beats the Transformer Engine NVFP4 baseline on final-window training loss (2.3090 vs 2.3474), held-out NLL at step 30,000 (2.32230 vs 2.32592, lower at 8/12 checkpoints including all four final ones), and OLMES downstream point estimates (Core 9 63.82 vs 63.69; MMLU 38.97 vs 37.96). A separate native-hardware ablation shows removing RHT and the BF16 exemption raises model-body throughput 21.2% (3,212 → 3,894 tokens/s on one GB200). A notable side contribution is a "probe-matched" software FP4 GEMM emulator that reproduces native Blackwell reduction/rounding behavior bit-exactly over a 1.29B-parameter end-to-end control.

## 核心贡献

- **UE5M3 块缩放格式**：块缩放（block scale）永远是正数，而 NVFP4 的有符号 E4M3 浪费 1 个符号位。UE5M3 把该符号位改为第 5 个指数位，得到最大有限值 61,440（E4M3 为 448，约 137 倍）、最小次正规数 2⁻¹⁷（E4M3 为 2⁻⁹，小 256 倍），所有 8-bit 编码均为可用的非负缩放值，且保留 3 个分数位（与 E4M3 在重叠区间的相对间距相同）。
- **更简单的 FP4 预训练配方**：宽范围块缩放使得 (a) 张量级 amax 只需每 D=50 个优化器步采样一次（sample-and-hold 缓存），无需每次量化前做全张量归约；(b) 完全省略 RHT；(c) 112 个合格内部线性层全部用 FP4（TE 配方保留最后 8 个混合块的 16 个投影为 BF16）；随机舍入（SR）仅施加于两个反向 GEMM 的上游梯度 dY 操作数，与 TE 相同。
- **可调缩放目标 T 与范围界**：将 NVIDIA 固定的 448 缩放目标视为可调参数，推导出 Proposition 1：块缩放不饱和当且仅当陈旧比 ρₜ ≤ U/T。默认 T=448 提供 137× 增长余量；仅对最后 4 个 MLP mixer.down_proj 的 Wgrad-dY 用 T=2048（保留 30× 余量、把小梯度块缩放码推离零点约 4.6 倍）。配套的零缩放修复规则（舍入到 0 的块缩放码替换为 1）。
- **探针匹配的 FP4 GEMM 输出模型**：通过精心构造的"置换证人"（258 个顺序测试 + 7 个 BF16 边界证人）逆向工程出被测 Blackwell/TE 原生 FP4 GEMM 的数值行为：64 个乘积一组、FP32 部分和 RNE、跨组加法朝零舍入（RTZ）、可选 1/1024 乘积格点规范化。端到端在 1.29B 参数模型上 logits 与 loss 完全相等、全部 1,291,929,600 个梯度元素相对 L2 误差为零，并经 100 次 AdamW 更新验证参数与优化器状态逐字节一致。
- **执行收益测量**：原生 NVFP4 执行消融显示，去掉 RHT 并把 16 个末块 BF16 投影改为 FP4 后，单卡 GB200 上模型体（model-body）100-step 墙钟时间从 31.877s 降到 26.298s，吞吐 3,212 → 3,894 tokens/s（+21.2%）。

## Key Contributions

- **UE5M3 block-scale format**: Reallocates E4M3's unused sign bit as a fifth exponent bit, yielding max finite value 61,440 (vs 448, ~137x larger), smallest subnormal 2^-17 (256x smaller), and roughly twice as many useful nonnegative scale encodings, at identical relative spacing where the two formats overlap.
- **A simpler FP4 pretraining recipe**: The wider scale range permits (a) periodic sample-and-hold tensor scaling with D=50 optimizer steps between amax refreshes (no per-quantization full-tensor reduction), (b) complete omission of RHT, and (c) FP4 in all 112 eligible internal linears with no BF16 exemption, while retaining 2D weight scaling and upstream-gradient-only SR.
- **A tunable scale target with a range bound**: Proposition 1 links the scale target T to stale-maximum headroom (stale ratio <= U/T): T=448 gives ~137x growth headroom; a targeted T=2048 override for the Wgrad-dY operands of the four final MLP down-projections retains 30x headroom while pushing small gradient scale codes ~4.6x farther from zero, supported by a matched 350M control (-0.00656 final-window loss).
- **A probe-matched FP4 GEMM output model**: Careful witness-based reverse engineering identifies the tested native Blackwell/TE NVFP4 GEMM numerics (64-product groups, FP32 RNE partial sums, round-toward-zero cross-group additions, optional 1/1024 product-lattice canonicalization), validated to exact logits/loss and a bit-exact 1,291,929,600-entry gradient vector on a 1.29B model, and byte-exact parameters/optimizer state over 100 AdamW updates.
- **Throughput measurement**: A joint native NVFP4 execution ablation records +21.2% model-body token throughput (3,212 -> 3,894 tokens/s; 100-step wall 31.877s -> 26.298s on one GB200) from removing RHT and quantizing the 16 otherwise-BF16 final-block projections.

## Methodology

**Format.** All three systems (MXFP4, NVFP4, UE5M3 FP4) share the E2M1 4-bit payload (max magnitude 6); they differ in block scaling. MXFP4: blocks of 32 with E8M0 scales (wide range, power-of-two steps). NVFP4: blocks of 16 with signed E4M3 scales plus an FP32 tensor scale. UE5M3 FP4: blocks of 16 with unsigned E5M3 scales — same 3 fraction bits as E4M3, but every code is a usable nonnegative scale.

**Periodic (delayed) tensor scaling.** Instead of computing the tensor amax before every quantization (current scaling, D=1), the recipe caches each operand's amax and refreshes it every D=50 optimizer steps (a sample-and-hold rule, distinct from TE's FP8 DelayedScaling window-maximum). Separate caches exist for activations, weights, and gradients; block maxima are still computed per block at quantization time. Caches are not serialized in checkpoints. The scale target T maps the cached tensor maximum to a chosen code inside the UE5M3 book: G = T·F/g with F=6, so a block as large as the cached reference lands exactly on code T. Proposition 1: a block of magnitude aB avoids scale saturation iff aB <= U·g/T; with U=61,440 and T=448 the stale ratio can reach ~137x before any block scale saturates. Default T=448 everywhere; T=2048 only for Wgrad-dY in the four final mixer.down_proj modules (layers 45/47/49/51), motivated by a 350M matched control. Zero block-scale codes after rounding are replaced with 1 (deterministic repair, separate from SR).

**Rounding.** Ties-to-even for activations, weights, and all block-scale codes; unbiased stochastic rounding (E[Q_SR(z)]=z) only for the E2M1 payload of the upstream-gradient dY in both backward GEMMs (dX = dY·W and dW = dYᵀ·X) — the same operand TE rounds stochastically.

**Probe-matched FP4 GEMM emulator.** Since the evaluated Blackwell/TE stack only supports E4M3 (not UE5M3) natively, the primary experiment runs a software GEMM whose output model was fitted to native outputs: (1) split the dot product into groups of 64 products; (2) one FP32 partial sum per group, RNE; (3) combine group totals in physical order, rounding inexact additions toward zero; optionally canonicalize on a 1/1024 product lattice before applying the scale product. Identification evidence: 258/258 permutation witnesses (only group size 64 with RTZ reaches 100%; nearest gives 149/258); 7/7 BF16-boundary witnesses from real 8192×2048×6144 GEMMs (decoded-operand Torch matmul matches 0/7); broad 512-cell probes (366/512 exact native FP32 vs 6/512 for RNE accumulation on packed-random; 512/512 vs 485/512 on TE-quantized wide). End-to-end: a 1.29B-parameter 20-layer model forward/backward gives exactly equal logits and loss, all 1,291,929,600 gradients present with zero relative L2 error; Appendix D extends to 100 AdamW updates with byte-exact logits/losses/parameters and all 2,583,859,363 optimizer tensor values, with a single gradient element differing by 2 BF16 ULPs. Appendix C characterizes the emulator's statistics: RTZ attenuates gain by ~1.2-1.35 ppm, and the 1/1024 snap maps 95.4% of a genuine near-zero residual (std 1/4096) to zero at partial-sum scale 1 — though a 3-seed 81,920-parameter regression control showed all grids produce bit-identical losses and final states.

**Experimental setup.** Nemotron-H 8B (52 blocks: 4 attention + 24 FFN + 24 Mamba-2; hidden 4,096; FFN 21,504), matched field-by-field to NVIDIA's NVFP4 8B setup (seq 8,192; global batch 768; AdamW β1=0.9/β2=0.95/wd 0.1; WSD LR 8e-4 decaying to 1% over the final 15%); deliberate differences: horizon shortened to 30,000 steps = 188.7B tokens (vs 1T), data substituted with a fixed 82% DataComp-LM + 18% other OLMo-family mixture, 32 GB200 ranks, seed 42, global-norm clipping 1.0. Seven trajectories (one seed-42 run each): BF16; native TE NVFP4 recipe; native NVFP4 no-RHT/all-linears ablation; UE5M3 FP4 with TE settings (E4M3→UE5M3 swap only); proposed UE5M3 B=16 probe-matched; B=32; and B=16 with a decoded-operand Torch matmul instead of the probe-matched emulator. Quantized inference uses fake-quantized FP4 with delayed (D=50) activation scales on a fixed evaluation order (weights use a fixed amax; policy sensitivity <9.0e-5 NLL vs current and calibrated-frozen). Downstream evaluation: OLMES Core 9, OLMES MMLU, and MMLU-Pro MC on a pinned 146-task likelihood suite.

## Results & Highlights

- **Training loss (final 250-step window mean, 30,000 steps):** proposed UE5M3 B=16 reaches 2.3090 vs 2.3474 for the native Transformer Engine NVFP4 recipe (-0.0384), with BF16 at 2.2651. Notably, UE5M3 FP4 with Transformer Engine settings (scale-format swap only) is the best FP4 run at 2.2902, and the matched controls land at 2.3241 (B=32) and 2.3157 (decoded-operand Torch matmul, i.e., the probe-matched emulator is worth -0.0067 window loss).
- **Stability with all 112 linears in FP4:** after step 2,500 the proposed trajectory records zero logged losses above 3 and zero pre-clipping gradient norms above 1, while the native NVFP4 no-RHT/all-linears ablation (E4M3, D=1) records 205 losses above 3 and 89 gradient norms above 1, finishing at 2.4420 vs 2.3090 — the paper's clearest evidence that UE5M3 scales (not just recipe simplification) buy stability.
- **Held-out NLL (fixed 768×8,192-token = 6.29M-token stream):** at step 30,000 the proposed B=16 path reaches 2.32230 vs 2.32592 for TE NVFP4 (-0.00362), lower at 8 of 12 checkpoints including all four from step 22,500 onward; UE5M3 with TE settings is lower at all 12 checkpoints, finishing at 2.30376 (-0.02217). The native no-RHT/all-linears ablation is far worse at 2.45468.
- **Downstream (OLMES, step 30,000, fake-quantized inference):** BF16 = 65.14 / 39.64 / 11.24 (Core 9 / MMLU / MMLU-Pro MC). Proposed B=16: 63.82 / 38.97 / 11.06, i.e., +0.13 / +1.01 / +0.01 pp over TE NVFP4 (63.69 / 37.96 / 11.05); UE5M3 with TE settings is higher by +0.74 / +0.61 / +0.31 pp. The native ablation collapses to 59.94 Core 9 (-5.20 vs BF16).
- **Throughput:** the joint native execution ablation raises measured model-body token throughput from 3,212 to 3,894 tokens/s (+21.2%) by removing RHT and moving the 16 final-block projections from BF16 to FP4 on one GB200.
- **Scale-target control (350M):** switching only the Wgrad-dY target to T=2048 in the final four feed_forward.w2 layers changes the final-window mean loss from 2.90904 to 2.90248 (-0.00656) at otherwise identical settings.
- **Emulator fidelity:** 258/258 permutation witnesses, 7/7 BF16-boundary witnesses, and byte-exact parameters and AdamW states (2,583,859,363 tensor values) after 100 full 1.29B-model updates.

## Authors & Credibility

Robert Hu, Carlo Luschi, and Paul Balanca are Graphcore Research (Graphcore, the UK AI-accelerator company acquired by SoftBank, with a long track record in low-precision arithmetic — the IPU's stochastic rounding heritage is directly relevant here). Robert Hu is the first author of "Elucidating the design space of FP4 training" (arXiv:2509.17791), which introduced UE5M3 as a scale format; Fasoli et al. (IBM) subsequently analyzed its scale coverage — so the team has prior, directly relevant published work on this exact format. The report is unusually transparent: a generative-AI disclosure states OpenAI Codex was used extensively for code, orchestration, figures, and drafting; a reproducibility statement points to a public reference implementation (github.com/MrHuff/ue5m3-fp4); and the paper repeatedly self-qualifies (single seed, "descriptive comparisons," complete-recipe-not-isolated-factor caveats). The witness-driven reverse engineering of native GEMM numerics demonstrates serious numerical-methods competence. As a technical report rather than peer-reviewed work, it has not undergone external review.

## Critical Assessment

- **Single seed per configuration.** Every comparison rests on one seed-42 trajectory per recipe, which the authors acknowledge ("descriptive comparisons rather than estimates of seed-to-seed variability"). This matters: the headline held-out NLL delta of -0.00362 and the MMLU-Pro delta of +0.01 pp are almost certainly within seed noise, and even the -0.0384 training-loss gap would benefit from error bars. The downstream deltas (+0.13/+1.01/+0.01 pp) should be read as point estimates only.
- **The proposed recipe is not the best configuration in the paper.** UE5M3 FP4 with Transformer Engine settings (D=1, RHT, BF16 final blocks) beats the proposed simplified recipe on every aggregate (2.2902 vs 2.3090 training loss; 2.30376 vs 2.32230 NLL). This is honestly reported, but it means the headline "simpler recipe wins" claim is really "simpler recipe beats the *E4M3* baseline" — the UE5M3 scale format, not the recipe simplification, appears to carry most of the win, and the paper's own factor-isolation is incomplete.
- **Confounded primary comparison.** The proposed recipe vs TE NVFP4 differs simultaneously in scale format, scale lifecycle (D=50 vs D=1), GEMM output model (probe-matched emulator vs native hardware), RHT, and final-block precision. The authors flag this explicitly for the stability comparison, but the abstract's framing ("Compared with Transformer Engine NVFP4...") still bundles these effects. The cleanest isolation — UE5M3-with-TE-settings — is relegated to a secondary result.
- **Software emulation, not native execution, for the primary experiment.** UE5M3 has no hardware support on the evaluated Blackwell/TE stack, so all proposed-recipe training runs through a Triton-based emulator. The emulator validation is exemplary (byte-exact over 1.29B parameters), but it validates parity with *NVFP4/E4M3* GEMM behavior, and the +21.2% throughput number comes from a native E4M3 ablation, not from an actual UE5M3 implementation — real UE5M3 hardware could behave differently. There is also a deliberate ~1.2 ppm gain contraction from RTZ accumulation that the authors characterize but do not ablate in the full 8B run (the 350M B=16 control shows only -0.0067 window loss).
- **Short horizon and substituted data.** 188.7B tokens is ~19% of NVIDIA's 1T-token NVFP4 experiment, and the data blend is a fixed 82/18 OLMo-family mixture rather than NVIDIA's unavailable two-phase blend. The paper cites Agrusa et al. reporting that 8B NVFP4 recipes still require BF16 final layers at horizons up to 1T — so the no-BF16-exemption claim is untested exactly where prior work says it is hardest.
- **Hand-placed, model-specific T=2048 override.** Raising the scale target for Wgrad-dY in exactly four final down-projections is justified by a single 350M two-run comparison (one seed) and a static checkpoint histogram (which the paper itself notes illustrates codebook placement, not an observed underflow event). No systematic sweep over which layers, how many layers, or other T values is provided for the 8B model — this is tuned by inspection and will not transfer blindly to other architectures.
- **Operational quirks of delayed scaling.** Scale caches are not checkpointed (a resumed run restarts the refresh phase), and D=50 inference is order-dependent, requiring a fixed evaluation order for reproducibility — a real deployment inconvenience that is disclosed but not deeply explored.

## Strengths

- Genuinely useful format insight: converting a signed scale format to unsigned is free range (137x larger max, 256x smaller subnormal), and the Proposition 1 headroom analysis (U/T trade-off between stale-growth safety and small-scale preservation) is a clean, reusable design rule for delayed scaling.
- The probe-matched GEMM emulator work is a standout: witness-driven identification of undocumented hardware reduction order/rounding (64-product groups + RTZ), with falsification tables (0/7, 6/7, 149/258, 258/258) and a bit-exact 1.29B-parameter, 100-update end-to-end control — a level of numerical forensics rarely seen, and directly valuable to anyone emulating low-precision hardware.
- Honest, precise reporting throughout: the best FP4 configuration is not the proposed one and is reported as such; caveats about single seeds, confounded recipe comparisons, checkpoint histograms, and emulated execution are all stated in the text rather than buried.
- Strong internal validity despite the substitutions: the TE NVFP4 baseline is re-run under identical architecture/optimizer/schedule/seed on the same data, so the comparison is fair even though it is not directly comparable to NVIDIA's published 1T-token numbers.
- Practical payoff quantified: +21.2% model-body throughput from removing RHT and the BF16 exemption is measured on native hardware, not extrapolated from theory.

## Limitations

- One seed per configuration; no variance estimates for any loss or accuracy delta.
- 188.7B-token horizon on a substituted OLMo-family data blend; the 1T-token regime, where prior work reports BF16 final layers remain necessary for 8B NVFP4 recipes, is untouched.
- Primary experiments run in software emulation (Triton emulator validated against native NVFP4/E4M3 outputs); no native UE5M3 hardware exists on the evaluated stack, so end-to-end wall-clock gains for the full proposed recipe are inferred, not measured.
- The T=2048 override is hand-tuned to four named layers of one architecture, supported by one 350M two-run control; no general selection rule or sensitivity sweep is given.
- Delayed-scaling state is not serialized in checkpoints, and D=50 inference is request-order dependent — both add operational friction for training resumption and serving.
- The emulator's RTZ accumulation introduces a systematic ~1.2 ppm signal-gain contraction and a scale-dependent dead zone from the 1/1024 snap (95.4% of a std-1/4096 residual mapped to zero at partial-sum scale 1); characterized in appendices but not ablated in the 8B runs.
- Downstream evaluation is likelihood-based multiple choice only (OLMES Core 9 / MMLU / MMLU-Pro MC); no generative or long-context evaluations.

## Relevance

这篇文章正落在"极限性能优化下的可靠性"核心关切上：FP4 量化训练的数值稳定性、块缩放格式设计、以及损失尖峰/梯度异常的可观测性对比（205 次损失>3 vs 0 次）都与用户兴趣高度契合。此外，用证人构造法逆向工程原生硬件 GEMM 的舍入与归约顺序、并验证到逐字节一致的做法，对理解低精度算术如何悄然腐化模型输出（近 SDC 数值取证视角）也很有参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the UE5M3 advantage hold at 1T tokens, where Agrusa et al. report 8B NVFP4 recipes still need BF16 final layers, or does the no-exemption recipe diverge at long horizons?
- How much of the win is attributable to each factor in isolation — e.g., UE5M3 with delayed D=50 scaling but with RHT retained, or UE5M3 with current D=1 scaling and no RHT — to cleanly separate format range from scale-lifecycle effects?
- Would a systematic rule for choosing the scale target T (per layer type or depth) outperform the hand-placed T=2048 override on four final down-projections, and does it transfer to dense Transformer or MoE architectures?
- Does the ~1.2 ppm RTZ gain contraction and the 1/1024 snap dead zone have measurable effects at larger horizons or on tasks sensitive to small gradient components (e.g., fine-tuning, RLHF)?
- What would a native UE5M3 tensor-core implementation cost in silicon (wider exponent datapath in the scale pipeline), and would it preserve the measured +21.2% throughput of the no-RHT/all-linears configuration?
- Can the probe-matched emulator methodology (witness-driven identification of undocumented hardware numerics) be packaged as a general conformance test for FP4/FP8 accelerator implementations, including for silent-data-corruption screening?
