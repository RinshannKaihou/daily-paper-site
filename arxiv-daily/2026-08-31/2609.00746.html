Turning an LLM into a VLM corrupts its attention sinks and erodes format‑sensitive text skills — predictable before training with one 8‑second probe.

Text Capability Loss in Vision-Language Adaptation: An Attention-Sink Diagnosis

Minsik Choi, Geewook Kim, Young Geun Kim  ·  Korea University · NAVER Cloud AI  ·  2026-08-31

一句话总结：VL 微调侵蚀基座 LLM 文本能力的根源是 attention-sink 被破坏；仅用基座 LLM、约 8 秒即可算出的 Sink Strength S 在 6 个 VLM–LLM 配对上以 ρ=0.97 预测 IFEval 退化幅度，而事后注入 QK-RMSNorm 与 7 种权重合并均告失败——筛查必须在 VL 训练之前完成。

Key metric
ρ = 0.97
Sink Strength S ↔ IFEval damage, six VLM–LLM pairs · leave-one-pair-out error 2.54 pt on a 17-pt range

Computed on the base LLM alone — 15 inference-only forward passes, ~8 s on a single A6000 for a 7B backbone. Extends as a ranker: ρ 0.88 over a 17-pair dense+MoE panel.

Why this matters

A near-free internal-representation probe becomes a pre-training gate: rank LLM backbones for multimodal readiness before spending any VL training compute.

Operational rule: S ≥ 2 → within 5.4 pt of the reference LLM; S ≈ 1.2 → losses up to −17.9 pt.

Critical edge

The headline correlation rests on just six pairs, the architecture axis is confounded with vendor and recipe — and every post-hoc repair attempted, from QK-RMSNorm injection to seven weight-merging methods, fails.

Screening only: the paper delivers no working intervention.

Methodology

  • Pairwise diagnostic. Five released VLM–LLM pairs (Qwen3-VL, InternVL3.5, Qwen2.5-VL, InternVL3, LLaVA-OV) plus control Molmo2-O; each language backbone extracted as a standalone LM and scored on a nine-task text suite against its reference LLM.
  • Format-sensitive focus. Strictly-parsed tasks — IFEval prompt-strict (headline), EQ-Bench, GSM8K-CoT, GPQA-Diamond-CoT; 541 IFEval prompts tagged per-pair as regression / recovered / both.
  • Sink Strength S. Median of log(apsink/(1−apsink)) over the last ~10 layers of the base LLM only; usable as a pure ranker or with a 1-D linear magnitude fit.
  • Matched theory. Theorem 1: fine-tuning perturbations threaten the sink through a raw-input-magnitude factor that per-head QK-RMSNorm provably cancels; Lemma 1 ties survival to the margin Gbase−Bgap.
  • Controls & ablations. Matched text-only Tülu SFT trajectory, post-hoc QK-RMSNorm injection, seven weight-merging settings, sink-amplifier ablation — with bootstrap CIs and leave-one-pair-out validation.

Flow: measure S on base LLM → predict post-VL damage → theory explains why per-head QK-RMSNorm protects → negative controls rule out repair after the fact.

  • It is the modality. A matched text-only Tülu SFT endpoint moves IFEval 0.00 and gains +5.46 on GSM8K-CoT; the VL endpoint from the same base loses −9.6 and −14.3.
  • Margins order exactly as behavior. Per-head pairs sit near zero (G−B = −0.31 / −0.11); no-QK-RMSNorm pairs span −1.09 to −3.83; layerwise pairs are equally strong-negative.
  • Post-hoc fixes fail. QK-RMSNorm injection + matched VL recipe collapses IFEval to 11 vs 599 on the clean 3B base; none of seven merging methods recovers the low-S side.
  • Mechanism ablation. Jointly killing sink amplifiers costs 44 pt vs 20 summed (super-additive redundancy); a norm-matched random perturbation causes ~6× the Qwen2.5-VL gap — direction, not weight distance, is what matters.

Strengths

  • Cheap, actionable, well-timed: 15 forward passes and ~8 s on one GPU — a pre-training gate usable long before any VL data is mixed.
  • Theory and measurement meet: Theorem 1’s qualitative prediction is independently confirmed by measured margins and attention-concentration buckets.
  • Strong experimental hygiene: matched modality control; negative controls run and reported as failures; bootstrap CIs, LOPO validation, sample-size sensitivity.
  • Honest scoping: format-sensitive vs knowledge-task dissociation is shown, not claimed; limitations are self-identified before a reviewer could.

Limitations

  • Correlational architecture claim at n = 6 with vendor/recipe confounds; the decisive causal ablation (QK-RMSNorm toggled during VL training) is untested.
  • Magnitude prediction is shaky off the headline set (EQ-Bench LOPO error 19.09 pt; Molmo2-O breaks the linear fit) — trust S as a ranker, not a calibrated estimator.
  • No working intervention delivered: injection and merging both fail; head-selective training-time protection remains a proposal.
  • Scope bound to surface-token-stability tasks: knowledge and reasoning benchmarks degrade far less, so S says little about those axes of forgetting; continual pretraining un-sampled, reference-LLM choice adds lineage ambiguity.

Questions for further exploration

  • Does head-selective training-time protection (freeze pre-sink layers; project sink-head Wq/Wk updates away from the sink direction) actually work — and preserve vision performance?
  • Does S predict degradation under regimes not sampled — continual pretraining, text-only RLHF — or is it VL-specific?
  • Can per-head QK-RMSNorm’s causal role be settled by training one backbone twice, module toggled, at matched data and compute?
  • Does sink corruption generalize to other injected modalities — audio, speech, video — that place OOD tokens early in the sequence?
  • Can S become a structured risk profile predicting which IFEval constraint categories fail (per-category breakdown exists in Appendix C.2)?
