Automated Scientific Discovery★ 5 / 5

Two Million Candidate Laws Refuted; Eight Survive — and They Hold

“Autonomous discovery of new structure-plausibility laws for explainable and rapid crystal diagnosis and screening”

两名人类作者只定目标与范围，LLM 智能体以 27 天“提出—检验—证伪”闭环对 99,162 个实验离子晶体完成 572 次调查、2,037,606 次候选评估，证伪约 200 万条候选定律后存活出 8 条可解释的结构合理性定律（PRIS），并把 MatterGen 逆设计的 DFT 验证队列砍掉 67.3%。

With humans only setting goals, LLM agents ran a 27-day propose–test–refute loop over 99,162 experimental ionic structures and refuted roughly two million candidate laws. Eight interpretable plausibility laws survived — and the derived PSS score cuts MatterGen’s DFT validation queue by 67.3% while keeping 99.2% of high-property candidates.

  • 27 days autonomous
  • 2,037,606 evaluations
  • 8 laws survive
  • −67.3% DFT queue
  • ~10 min vs ~1M CPU-hrs
By Zhilong Song & Lixue Cheng Continued: 2609.01209.html →

Quantization Reliability★ 5

Quantization Damage Is Everywhere — Spend the Next Bit Globally

“The Structure of Quantization Damage in LLMs: Why the Next Bit Should Be Spent Globally”

逐层 8-bit 因果干预证明 4-bit 损伤是弥散的：恢复 90% 精度差距平均要动 73% 的层；等预算下把“下一个 bit”全局花在 group-128 粒度，比 oracle 局部修复多恢复 21–52 分。

Causal layer-upgrade interventions refute all three intuitions about where 4-bit damage lives. One global bit at group-128 granularity beats oracle-selected local repair by 21–52 points — granularity, not calibration, drives the gain.
