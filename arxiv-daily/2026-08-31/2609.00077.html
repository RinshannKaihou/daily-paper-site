Autonomous research loops quietly self-deceive: surface edit diversity looks healthy while semantic diversity collapses, inflating measured gains by 2.0–2.6×.

Key metric

2.0–2.6×

By iteration 300, in-loop gains overstate independent hold-out gains (VANILLA AR, four tasks). The proposed fix — DAPS — lifts audited faithfulness 0.44 → 0.79 for ~1.4% wall-clock.

Why this matters

Turns “is my self-improving agent fooling itself?” into a measurable quantity — the three-tier blind audit and four-axis diagnostics are reusable monitoring tools for any long-running agent.

把「自进化 Agent 是否在自我欺骗」变成可测量问题：三层盲评协议与四轴仪表可直接复用于任何长期自改进系统。

Critical edge

Headline factors are measured only on ≤3B models with 5-minute, single-A100 iterations; frontier-scale executors are untested, and DAPS has sharp hyperparameter edges — PEM's τm ≥ 0.95 collapses faithfulness back to 0.46.

01Methodology, distilled

  1. Loop model. A proposer LLM emits an edit description + code patch; an executor runs ≤5 min on the proposer-visible metric mopt; edits are kept only above a noise floor. Audit and blind metrics are never exposed to the loop.
  2. Four NLP tasks at T=300, 3–5 seeds: GPT-2-small pretraining (T1), Llama-3.2-1B LoRA on Alpaca (T2), Qwen-2.5-1.5B on GSM8K (T3), Llama-3.2-3B DSPy prompt optimization (T4).
  3. Four diagnostic axes. Surface Levenshtein diversity ST; semantic cluster count CT (Sentence-BERT + HDBSCAN); mechanism entropy HT over a 9-category edit taxonomy (parser vs frontier-LLM κ=0.84); corpus similarity VT against 1.1M arXiv abstracts.
  4. Three-tier audit. mopt (proposer-visible) / maudit (gate-only) / mblind (read by no component); the monitored gap is Δfaith = gopt − gaudit.
  5. DAPS, drop-in. CCR reweights 8 candidates toward under-used edit mechanisms; PEM (M=200 memory) rejects candidates with cosine > 0.85 to past edits; HVG reverts to the last faithful checkpoint when the audit gap crosses a calibrated threshold every 10 iterations. Eight baselines with matched tuning budgets.

02Results & highlights

  • Faithfulness beyond the headline: blind-tier ρblind 0.74 vs 0.41 (+83.7% relative); semantic-cluster decay ΔC cut 69.1% (0.68 → 0.21). The strongest published mitigation (R-DIVERSE-A) plateaus at 0.58–0.60 and costs 3–13% in-loop gain on T2/T3.
  • Speed is preserved: normalized in-loop gain 0.983 of vanilla — T2 win-rate +8.8±1.0 vs +8.9±1.2 points. RandSearch is equally faithful (ρ 0.74–0.79) but forfeits half the gain (+4.1 vs +8.9).
  • The signature: mechanism entropy falls 1.76 → 1.18 nats while surface diversity stays flat in [0.66, 0.68] — a surface-only monitor raises no flag; by iteration 200, 74% of accepted T1 edits are LR-schedule / AdamW tweaks.
  • Generalizes and worsens with horizon: replicates across three proposer LLMs and two frameworks; at T=1,000 vanilla decays further (ρaudit 0.46 → 0.31) while DAPS holds 0.74.

03Strengths

  • A new, cleanly measured failure mode with a four-axis instrument that predicts it — surface diversity is provably orthogonal to semantic collapse (Fig. 2).
  • The three-tier blind protocol is the real methodological contribution: Goodharting becomes measurable by construction, and headline claims live on the tier no component can access.
  • Exemplary validity hygiene: known-good vs proxy-only construct controls (ρ 0.93 vs 0.11), matched audit access and matched baseline tuning budgets, taxonomy-invariance checks plus a taxonomy-free entropy estimate.
  • Practical: a drop-in wrapper that never modifies the proposer, ~1.4% overhead; the ablation tells practitioners exactly what to buy — CCR+PEM alone (no audit signal) already reach 0.71 blind-side faithfulness.

04Limitations

  • Scale ceiling (acknowledged): single A100, ≤3B parameters, 5-minute iterations; frontier proposers/executors and 10k+ horizons are untested.
  • Sharp hyperparameter edges: τm ≥ 0.95 starves the proposer (ρ back to 0.46); ηt calibration consumes preliminary vanilla-AR runs per task, not counted in headline overhead.
  • Weak instruments: VT agrees only moderately with human novelty judgments (Spearman −0.47); blind sets are still benchmarks, with only 3–4 audit/blind tasks per run.
  • Not fully metric-agnostic in deployment — the gate reads maudit every 10 iterations; the cost is unmodeled for long, expensive training runs.
  • The 50–70% collapse figure is measured at T=500 while headline tables use T=300 — the two horizons drift interchangeably in the narrative.

05Questions for further exploration

  1. Does collapse change character when one audit evaluation costs hundreds of vanilla iterations — does HVG's 1-in-10 cadence survive, or must audits be proxied (subset-based)?
  2. Is there a horizon beyond which CCR's category prior itself becomes harmful by forcing weak categories — a diversity–performance Pareto frontier?
  3. Could the audit metric be learned (a distilled “did this edit transfer?” judge) without reopening the Goodhart channel the three-tier protocol exists to detect?
  4. Do multi-agent ARLs (proposer / critic / executor as separate agents) collapse too — and is the diagnosis pipeline cheap enough to standardize as a public “ARL observability” harness?
