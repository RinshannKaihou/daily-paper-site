> **一句话总结：** 提出 FaVOR，一个把 LLM 因子挖掘从"直接优化收益"重构为"假设级证据验证"的三阶段智能体框架——先将经济学假设分解为 OHLCV 可观测的市场条件，再用四条确定性统计规则（中心趋势系统性移位、尾部变化、多统计量一致、语义一致）在分位数分箱上做因子级验证，最后经方向选择性筛选与 Calmar 比率阈值优化合成因子组合；在 CSI 500 与 S&P 500 的 2025 年样本外测试中四项指标全面第一（CSI 500 超额年化收益 0.2067、IR 1.5295，最强基线 RD-Agent 仅 0.1382/1.3360），消融显示去掉验证或整合阶段后收益直接转负。

> **TL;DR:** FaVOR is a multi-agent framework (GPT-4o backbone) that restructures LLM-driven alpha factor mining around hypothesis-level empirical validation instead of return-only optimization: a hypothesis is decomposed into OHLCV-observable market conditions, each candidate factor is validated against its intended condition via four deterministic statistical checks on quantile-binned distributional evidence (the LLM only maps text to variables, never issues the pass/fail itself), and validated factors are integrated via a Directional Selectivity sweep plus Calmar-ratio threshold optimization. On the 2025 out-of-sample window it beats RD-Agent on CSI 500 (annualized excess return 0.2067 vs 0.1382, IR 1.5295 vs 1.3360) and beats AlphaQCM on the S&P 500 (0.1062 vs 0.0619), with an ablation showing that removing either the validation or integration stage flips returns negative (AR −0.3902 without Stage 3). Code is released; the honest appendix admits the filter discards 47 directionally-unstable-but-profitable combinations.

## 核心贡献

- **诊断"收益导向自动化"的结构性缺陷**：现有 LLM 因子挖掘系统直接以回测收益为优化目标，从不检验生成的因子是否仍然表达其声称的经济假设；这种因子在训练窗口内与真因子难以区分，一旦市场 regime 切换就失效（过拟合噪声的伪相关）。
- **三阶段一致性循环**：(1) *Decomposition* —— 假设智能体 A_H 将人类市场洞察转为经济假设，观察智能体 A_O 将其分解为 m 个 OHLCV 可观测条件，因子智能体 A_F 用约 40 个预定义算子（RANK、TS_ZSCORE、DELTA、CORR 等）为每个条件生成 2–3 个候选公式；(2) *Validation* —— 在训练数据上按因子值分 5 个分位箱，构建 OHLCV 状态变量（日内动量 C−O、振幅 H−L、收盘位置、成交量）的统计画像，验证智能体执行**四条显式规则**（中心趋势跨箱系统性移位、非平凡尾部变化、至少两个互补统计量一致、与语义方向一致），LLM 只负责把文本观察映射为变量和方向，最终 pass/fail 是确定性的规则合取；(3) *Integration* —— 通过方向选择性阶梯（σ 收紧时已实现收益须保持稳定）与 ticker 级支持率门槛筛组合，Optuna 在验证集上以 Calmar 比率（AR/MDD）为目标优化阈值，之后在测试集上冻结执行。
- **可审计性设计**：外循环假设记忆只存假设 ID、观察分解与单一标量平均 IR，不存原始收益和公式定义，反馈无法把回测信息泄漏进下一轮生成；每条验证决策都是显式统计检查的合取，而非不可解释的 LLM 判断。
- **系统性实证**：CSI 500 与 S&P 500 上 2025 年样本外全面第一；消融、6 种 LLM 骨干敏感性、10 次独立重复方差、100 例盲测专家审计、2021–2025 五年滚动检验与 49 个月度起点敏感性，附录还做了假设溯源（10 个种子概念 → 520 个唯一假设 → 仅 49 个组合通过 Stage 3 门槛）。

## Key Contributions

- Identifies a structural failure mode of return-oriented LLM factor mining: generated formulas are never checked against the economic hypothesis that motivated them, so spurious correlations indistinguishable from real signals in-sample collapse under regime shifts.
- Proposes FaVOR, a three-stage consistency loop — Decomposition (hypothesis → observable market conditions → candidate formulas from a fixed ~40-operator OHLCV library), Validation (four explicit, deterministic consistency checks over quantile-binned distributional evidence; the LLM maps text to variables and directions but the pass/fail decision is a deterministic conjunction), and Integration (Directional Selectivity ladder + ticker-level support gates + Optuna/Calmar threshold optimization on validation data only).
- Designs for auditability: hypothesis memory stores only prior hypothesis IDs, decompositions, and a single scalar IR summary — no raw returns or formula definitions — so feedback cannot leak backtest outcomes into the next generation round.
- Validates extensively: best on all four metrics in both CSI 500 and S&P 500 2025 out-of-sample, stage ablations, 6-backbone sensitivity, 10-run variance, a blinded 100-case expert audit of semantic validation, 2021–2025 longitudinal tests, and a provenance audit showing only 49 of 520 hypotheses survive the full gate.

## Methodology

FaVOR runs four LLM agents (Hypothesis, Observation, Factor, Validation; GPT-4o default, temperatures 0.9/0.7/0.7/0.1) inside a three-stage loop, backtested with Qlib on daily OHLCV only.

**Stage 1 — Hypothesis Decomposition.** The Hypothesis agent converts human market insights I into a natural-language economic hypothesis h. The Observation agent decomposes h into m mutually independent observable market conditions {o_i}, each restricted to daily-OHLCV-expressible states (e.g., "post sell-off state", "intraday recovery"). The Factor agent generates n_i = 2–3 candidate formulas per condition, composing only a predefined operator library (cross-sectional: RANK, ZSCORE, SKEW, KURT; time-series: TS_ZSCORE, DELTA, CORR, TS_ARMIN; technical: RSI, MACD, Bollinger), which keeps every formula interpretable and the search space controlled.

**Stage 2 — Factor-Level Validation.** For each candidate factor f_{i,j}, factor values on training data are sorted into Q = 5 quantile bins per date. For each bin, FaVOR builds statistical profiles of OHLCV-derived state variables x ∈ X (intraday direction C−O, price range H−L, close position (C−L)/(H−L), volume V): mean, median, q10, q90, and dispersion. The Validation agent then applies four jointly required checks: (i) systematic central-tendency shift across ordered bins in the semantically implied direction (mean and median must move together, with non-zero bin correlation); (ii) non-trivial tail variation (|Δ tail spread| > ε between at least two bins); (iii) statistical consistency — the same verdict must hold for at least two complementary statistics; (iv) semantic consistency with the textual observation. Crucially, the LLM's only role is mapping the observation text to variables and their expected directions; the final decision A_V(o_i, f_{i,j}, S_{i,j}) = ∧_k c_k is deterministic. Failed factors are routed back to the Factor agent with refinement feedback (max 5 iterations).

**Stage 3 — Factor Integration.** Validated factors per condition form a Cartesian product of candidate combinations. Each combination's entry rule uses a common selectivity level σ: a factor triggers only when its value crosses its in-sample σ-quantile in the polarity implied by o_i. The Directional Selectivity criterion requires that realized returns do not degrade as σ tightens across a ladder σ_1 < σ_2 < … (ratios of winning/losing-signal averages must be higher under stricter σ), evaluated at ticker level with cross-ticker pass-rate thresholds (0.5/0.7). 

**Stage 4 — Optimization & Backtesting.** Optuna (20 trials) tunes factor-specific cutoffs and the combined-signal quantile (q = 0.9) on the 2024 validation set, maximizing the Calmar ratio (annualized return / max drawdown); thresholds are frozen before the 2025 test. Holding horizon T = 7 days, TopkDropout execution (k=50, n=5), proportional costs (CSI 500: 0.0005 buy/sell; S&P 500: 0.0005 sell only). Outer-loop hypothesis memory deliberately stores no raw returns or formulas, only IDs, decompositions, and one scalar average IR.

**Data splits.** Train 2022-01-01..2023-12-31, validation 2024, test 2025 (CSI 500 from Baostock, S&P 500 from Yahoo Finance). A longitudinal experiment re-selects on 2015 training / 2020 validation and evaluates the fixed combination on each of 2021–2025.

## Results & Highlights

- **CSI 500, 2025 out-of-sample (Table 1):** FaVOR achieves AR 0.2067, IR 1.5295, MDD −0.0853, CR 0.2225 — best on all four metrics. The strongest baseline is RD-Agent (LLM-based) at AR 0.1382 / IR 1.3360 / MDD −0.1427 (+6.85 pts AR, −5.74 pts MDD for FaVOR); AlphaQCM (RL) reaches only 0.0869 AR; all five ML/DL baselines (Linear, XGBoost, LightGBM, MLP, Transformer) are negative or near-zero on excess return (e.g., LightGBM AR 0.0493 with IR −1.4954; Transformer AR −0.0521), and AlphaForge is negative (−0.0133).
- **S&P 500, 2025 out-of-sample (Table 1):** FaVOR again best on all four metrics — AR 0.1062, IR 1.1315, MDD −0.0443, CR 0.1123, versus the strongest baseline AlphaQCM (RL) at AR 0.0619 / IR 0.8250 / MDD −0.2345 (+4.43 pts AR with roughly one-fifth the max drawdown) and RD-Agent at AR 0.0064 / IR 0.8028; AlphaAgent is −0.0192 AR on CSI 500 and −0.0110 CR on S&P 500.
- **Stage ablation on CSI 500 2025 (Table 4):** removing Stage 2 (validation) collapses AR 0.2067 → 0.0492 and IR 1.5295 → 0.1526 while MDD nearly triples to −0.2091; removing Stage 3 (integration) is worse — AR −0.3902, IR −1.2748, MDD −0.4260; removing both gives AR −0.2793. The same Stage-2 removal drops S&P 500 IR 1.1315 → 0.8831 (Table 13). The authors conclude validation and integration are "beforefore responsible for the existence of usable trading signals."
- **Backbone sensitivity (Table 3):** GPT-4o is the strongest configuration (CSI 500 AR 0.2067 / IR 1.5295), but results vary widely: GPT-5.4-mini AR 0.1019 / IR 1.5246, Qwen3-235B IR 1.0598 (open-weight, competitive), Gemini-2.5-Flash IR 0.4998, Claude-Sonnet-4.6 IR 0.5364, Llama-3.3-70B IR 0.4183 — the pipeline transfers across LLM families but headline returns are backbone-dependent.
- **Reliability and audit evidence:** over 10 independent runs the IR variance is 0.152 (std 0.390; AR std 0.205), which the authors use to argue headline numbers are stable (Table 10/21). In a blinded audit of 100 validation cases (50 PASS / 50 FAIL from 44 observation groups), experts agreed in 39 cases, partially agreed in 49, and disagreed in 12 (Table 16); a coded-rule re-implementation check covered 8,362 cases. Strategy-level stats (Table 11): 1,255 trades, mean per-trade excess return +2.279%, profit factor 1.986, average holding 9.64 days.
- **Provenance and honesty checks:** across 696 outer-loop iterations from 10 seed concepts, 520 unique hypotheses produced 6,732 promoted formulas and 18,698 stage-1 combinations, of which only 49 clear the Stage 3 gate with positive OOS return (Table 19). Notably, PASS formulas show no uniform standalone RankIC advantage over FAIL formulas (e.g., CSI 500 10-day: PASS +0.0023 vs FAIL +0.0047, Table 14) — consistent with Stage 2 acting as a semantic-consistency filter rather than an alpha filter, which the authors state openly.

## Critical Assessment

- **The GPT-4o dependence is the biggest soft spot.** The headline CSI 500 result (AR 0.2067) is roughly double every alternative backbone (next best: GPT-5.4-mini at 0.1019; Qwen3-235B 0.0948). The 10-run variance study (IR std 0.390 around mean ~1.5) partially covers this, but the gap to other backbones is large enough that the paper's strongest number is plausibly a backbone-specific lucky configuration rather than a property of the framework per se; the honest framing would be "IR is robust, AR is backbone-sensitive."
- **Possible lookahead via LLM priors is never discussed.** The Hypothesis and Validation agents run on GPT-4o, whose training data almost certainly includes extensive quant-finance literature covering 2022–2023 market behavior (the training window). The paper shows the LLM cannot single-handedly pass factors (deterministic rules), but the hypothesis-generation side could still leak regime knowledge that the 2025 test then rewards. A train/test era robustness check with pre-2020 data exists (Table 17), but with a 2019 training cutoff for a GPT-4o agent, this concern is acknowledged nowhere.
- **Baseline comparison is matched-data, not matched-search (admitted in Appendix N).** ML/DL baselines are restricted to the fixed Alpha158 feature family while FaVOR searches a 40-operator OHLCV grammar with LLM priors; RL baselines search OHLCV primitives but without a hypothesis prior. The authors explicitly disclaim "matched search budgets," which is honest, but it means the Table 1 gaps partly measure inductive-prior richness, not framework superiority.
- **The filter knowingly discards alpha.** Of 212 evaluated Stage 3 combinations, 81 were rejected for direction instability, yet 47 of those still delivered positive post-cost CR in the held-out year (Appendix M) — the Directional Selectivity criterion trades return for interpretability, and the unconditional-σ analysis (Table 20: CR decays from +0.2048 at σ=0.55 to +0.0048 at σ=0.95 on CSI 500) shows the final portfolio depends heavily on threshold choices made on one validation year (2024), a single regime.
- **Only one primary test year, two universes.** The main table is 2025 only; the 2021–2025 longitudinal check (Table 17) fixes one combination and shows decaying IR (CSI 500: 1.71 in 2021 → 0.58 in 2025), which is encouraging but also hints at signal decay; monthly-start sensitivity (33/49 windows CR>0 on CSI 500) is reported as start-date sensitivity, not independent trials.
- **Semantic validation is genuinely novel and well-hedged.** The authors do not overclaim: Appendix O states the framework provides structural traceability, not causal economic mechanism, and Table 14 honestly shows PASS formulas have no standalone RankIC edge. This restraint is rare and increases trust in the core claim — the contribution is the consistency gate, not a new alpha source.

## Strengths

- Deterministic-by-construction validation: the LLM never issues the final accept/reject; pass/fail is a conjunction of four explicit statistical rules, making every decision auditable and reproducible (8,362-case coded-rule audit backs this).
- Clean experimental hygiene: strict train/validation/test temporal splits, thresholds frozen before test, transaction costs applied, per-year and monthly-start robustness checks, 10-run variance reporting, and a blinded expert audit of the semantic judge.
- The ablation is decisive: w/o Stage 3 the framework is catastrophically negative (AR −0.3902), so the integration/consistency machinery is load-bearing, not decoration.
- Interpretability is enforced structurally (fixed operator grammar over OHLCV) rather than promised post-hoc; the case study (Table 2) shows concrete quantitative PASS/FAIL reasoning (e.g., a factor rejected because q90 tail expansion of +24.15% coincides with kurtosis flipping +40 → −17, i.e., internally conflicting tail signals).
- Honest appendices: provenance funnel (only 49/520 hypotheses survive), discarded-profitable-combinations analysis, scope-of-interpretability disclaimer, and explicit search-budget asymmetry disclosure.

## Limitations

- Directional Selectivity excludes reversal-type/contrarian signals whose sign flips at stricter thresholds — by design it cannot mine mean-reversion-at-extremes patterns, and the appendix shows this discards 47 profitable combinations.
- Daily-bar OHLCV only: no market impact or slippage modeling (claimed negligible at ~10-day holdings and low turnover, but unverified for scaled-up capital); higher-frequency extensions would need a high-fidelity execution simulator.
- Final portfolios are tiny (4 formulas on CSI 500, 3 on S&P 500) and thresholds are tuned on a single validation year; performance across regimes is shown only for one fixed combination.
- Hypotheses originate from human seed insights (10 seed concepts) — the loop is hypothesis-refining rather than fully autonomous discovery.
- Failure mode is concentrated: hypotheses whose mechanisms daily OHLCV cannot cleanly identify (volume-dominant, short covering, liquidity-driven states) systematically fail the gate, bounding the framework's addressable hypothesis space.

## Relevance

这篇论文属于"自进化智能体与自动化研究"的高优先级方向：它把 LLM 多智能体循环（假设生成 → 反馈修正 → 外循环假设记忆）与确定性验证门（LLM 只做语义映射、规则做最终裁决）结合，正是长时程自主智能体系统中"可靠性与可信验证"问题的一个具体而完整的实例，其"LLM 提议、规则裁决、证据可审计"的设计模式可迁移到自动化科研的实验验证环节。对你的 LLM 训练稳定性 / SDC 检测等核心方向无直接关联，量化金融只是应用外壳。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would the validation gates transfer to higher-frequency or non-equity data (futures, crypto), where the four statistical checks' tolerance ε and the quantile-bin structure may behave differently?
- Can the GPT-4o headline result be reproduced with an open-weight backbone (Qwen3-235B gets IR 1.0598 but AR only 0.0948) — is the AR gap due to hypothesis quality, validation mapping accuracy, or threshold interaction?
- Does the 47-combination "directionally unstable but profitable" rejection set constitute a complementary strategy family that a second, reversal-aware gate could harvest?
- How much of the Stage 2 pass/fail stability under stochastic decoding (IR variance 0.152) is due to the deterministic rule conjunction versus the low validation temperature (0.1)?
- Could the hypothesis-memory design (IDs + decompositions + scalar IR only) be formalized as a general anti-leakage contract for self-improving agent loops, and tested by deliberately corrupting the memory?
