> **一句话总结：** 清华团队提出 StudyBench 物理基准，用 11 本经典教材构造三层训练材料（Corpus / 无答案题目 / 带答案题目）加两个测试集（88 道高难教材习题的 Application Set、90 道"教师引导下可解"奥赛题的 Transfer Set），直接度量自进化方法把训练材料转化为可迁移解题能力的效率；在 Qwen3-8B 上最强的 GEPA 把 Application Par@8 从 17.05 拉到 34.85（Sub@8 +14.98），Transfer Par@8 却只有 7.04，而同一材料以 in-context 教师引导可恢复 100% Par@8——即最强的方法也只关闭了约 7% 的 Guidance Gap，且五种自进化循环在 8.12–614 GPU 时（相差近两个数量级）内全部早早进入 Compute Plateau，说明剩余差距是方法问题而非数据或算力问题。

> **TL;DR:** StudyBench is a controlled physics benchmark that measures how efficiently a self-evolution method converts training material into transferable problem-solving capability, fixing three confounds that plague existing evaluations (vanishing capability gap, unreachable targets, confounded attribution). Training material is 11 canonical physics textbooks factored into three nested layers (raw Corpus, Instructions-without-Answer, Instructions-with-Answer); test sets are an 88-problem Application Set of hard textbook exercises (absorption) and a 90-problem Olympiad Transfer Set, each problem certified solvable by Qwen3-8B under DeepSeek-V4-Pro textbook-grounded guidance (transfer; 100 Par@8 guidance ceiling by construction). Across three base models and seven methods (Bonito, GRPO, GEPA, ACE, TTRL, Intuitor, R-Zero), Application-Set gains rarely transfer: on Qwen3-8B GEPA lifts Application Par@8 from 17.05 to 34.85 (+14.98 Sub@8, best of all methods) yet Transfer Par@8 reaches only 7.04 vs the 100.00 guidance ceiling — closing just 7% of the Guidance Gap — while every self-evolution loop saturates into a Compute Plateau between 8.12 (Bonito) and 614 (R-Zero) GPU-hours. The residual gap is an internalisation/method problem, not a data or compute problem.

## 核心贡献

- **提出"知识→能力转化"的直接度量**：现有评测（AIME、HLE 等静态难题榜，LTM、LifelongAgentBench 等动态终身学习榜）只给最终分数或测局部适应，无法区分"算法贡献"与"训练数据贡献"。StudyBench 通过三个设计性质消除三类混淆——(1) Capability Gap（保留的测试题在基座模型 pass@8 下必失败，得分只反映算法新增能力）、(2) Reachability（每道 Transfer 题都被证明可由训练材料重组解出，消除不可达目标）、(3) Controlled Attribution（所有方法使用同一测试题与评测协议，分数只反映方法本身）。
- **双层测试集 + 教师引导上限**：Application Set（88 题/109 子题，Qwen3-8B 不可靠解出的高难教材章末习题）测"吸收"；Transfer Set（90 题/280 子题，六项国际物理奥赛真题）测"迁移"。用 DeepSeek V4 Pro 五步教师管线（分解知识点→双通道检索→核验→生成不含答案的教材化引导→8 次重试）构造引导轨迹，构成 100% 的 in-context 可达上限；换 GLM-5.1 独立重写引导后仍有 56/90 题（62.22 Par@8、86.43 Sub@8）可解，证明可达性不是单一教师风格的产物。
- **Guidance Gap 指标**：Gap = Acc_guided − Acc_solo，度量"同一材料在推理期以引导注入时能解锁、但训练后未能内化"的能力。这是本文最有解释力的新量。
- **系统性负结果**：7 种代表性自进化方法 × 3 个基座模型的基准测试 + 按算力归一化的训练曲线画像（8.12–614 GPU 时，checkpoint 快照），发现所有方法都会进入算力平台期；Bonito（合成数据 SFT）甚至让迁移能力崩塌（Transfer Sub@8 −21.43）。
- **污染双向治理**：预训练泄漏由 Capability Filter 天然屏蔽（Qwen3-8B 背熟的题无论是否逐字出现都会被剔除）；训练材料泄漏通过对保留题干与解答的物理删节 + 40 字符锚点审计保证语料零逐字残留。代码与指令层开源（Corpus 因版权不直接发布，提供重建脚本）。

## Key Contributions

- A benchmark that directly measures knowledge-to-capability conversion for self-evolution, with three by-construction properties — Capability Gap (test items fail on the base model at pass@8), Reachability (every Transfer item certified solvable from the training material under teacher-written, textbook-grounded guidance), and Controlled Attribution (identical items and protocol across methods) — eliminating the vanishing-gap, unreachable-target, and confounded-attribution failures of prior evaluations.
- A two-tier test design: an Application Set (88 parents / 109 sub-problems of difficult textbook exercises, probing absorption) and an Olympiad Transfer Set (90 parents / 280 sub-problems from six international physics olympiads, probing transfer), plus a Naive Reachability Filter (5-step DeepSeek V4 Pro teacher pipeline: decompose, retrieve, verify, guide, retry) that yields a 100% in-context guidance ceiling for Qwen3-8B; a cross-teacher witness with independently written GLM-5.1 traces keeps 62.22 Par@8 / 86.43 Sub@8 reachable, ruling out single-teacher artefacts.
- The Guidance Gap metric (guided minus solo accuracy), exposing how little of what the material unlocks in-context any method internalises: at most +2.14 Transfer Sub@8 across seven methods, vs +8.87 to +14.98 on the Application Set.
- A compute-normalised profiling of five self-evolution loops (checkpoint snapshots vs cumulative GPU-hours spanning nearly two orders of magnitude, 8.12–614 GPU-h) showing all loops plateau well before exhausting compute — a Compute Plateau that holds across seeds for ACE.
- Two-axis contamination control: pretraining leakage screened by the Capability Filter, and training-material leakage removed by physically redacting every retained Application parent's statement/solution from the corpus, audited to zero verbatim residue.

## Methodology

**Training material (three nested layers).** Eleven canonical physics textbooks currently used in top-university courses (mechanics, EM, thermal, waves, quantum, relativity, astronomy), chosen so their sub-disciplines jointly cover the syllabus of six international olympiads (IPhO, APhO, EuPhO, IOAA, NbPhO, OpHO). PDFs are converted to Markdown via MinerU, common OCR errors fixed by deterministic rules plus an LLM pass, and per-source extractors (written with Claude Opus 4.7 as a coding assistant) pull worked examples, end-of-chapter exercises, and every theory problem of every past olympiad edition. Gold answers are classified by DeepSeek-V4-Flash into nine answer types (NV, EX, EQ, TUP, IN, MC, TF, QL, ALT) with per-position type sequences for composite answers. The layers: raw Corpus (817 chapter files, ~6.0M tokens, 18.19 MB) contains Instructions-without-Answer (646 instruction–answer pairs where no gold answer could be recovered), which contains Instructions-with-Answer (1,420 pairs with answers from official solution manuals/answer keys). Each self-evolution method consumes whichever layer fits its paradigm.

**Test sets.** The Capability Filter runs Qwen3-8B with pass@8 sampling on both pools; a parent is retained as a test item only if no attempt solves every sub-problem (15 Application parents solved exactly 1/8 are admitted as a relaxation to keep easier subjects represented — these are the sole source of Qwen3-8B's nonzero 17.05 Application Par@8). Retained textbook items form the Application Set (subsampled by sub-discipline: mechanics 18, EM 19, astro 14, quantum 15, thermal 12, relativity 3, waves 7 parents); failed olympiad parents advance to the Naive Reachability Filter, where a strong teacher (DeepSeek V4 Pro) decomposes the gold solution into canonical knowledge points, retrieves textbook exposition/worked-example fragments (BM25 + dense embeddings fused by RRF), verifies coverage on a 0–3 rubric with quoted evidence, writes textbook-grounded guidance that names concepts/formulas/examples in order without stating the answer or performing the key computation (dual leakage gate: deterministic redaction + separate teacher review; failing guidance regenerated up to twice), and the problem is admitted only if Qwen3-8B solves it at least once in eight guided attempts. This certifies every Transfer parent is reachable from the training material, giving a 100 Par@8/100 Sub@8 guidance ceiling. Re-running the same five-step pipeline with GLM-5.1 as an independent teacher leaves 56/90 parents (62.22 Par@8) and 242/280 sub-problems (86.43 Sub@8) solvable, so reachability is not one teacher's stylistic artefact.

**Contamination control.** Pretraining leakage is neutralised by the Capability Filter itself (items Qwen3-8B has memorised are removed regardless of verbatim presence). Training-material leakage: for every retained Application parent, the problem statement and reference solution are excised from the raw markdown before assembling the corpus (two-pass redaction), and an audit extracts 40+-character normalised anchors from each retained parent's problem and solution text, searching the assembled corpus — zero verbatim matches; every retained parent has at least one redaction marker. Transfer problems come from olympiad exams that appear in none of the textbooks.

**Evaluation protocol.** Each method trains on any subset of the material; every evolved model is evaluated on both test sets with identical items. Open-weight models use k=8 samples per sub-problem (temperature 1.0, top-p 0.95, top-k 20), repeated three times with independent seeds (mean ± std); Opus 4.7 uses k=1 due to API cost (single run). Two metrics: Par@k (fraction of parents where some attempt solves every sub-problem) and Sub@k (flattened sub-problem accuracy). Multi-part problems are scored with conversational continuity — the model sees the shared stem and its own prior answers, with a fixed failure placeholder inserted when a sub-problem fails — isolating per-sub-problem correctness.

**Verifier.** A two-stage judge built on UG-Physics's rule-based judger, extended with a new short-qualitative (QL) primitive and a type_sequence field so composite TUP/ALT answers dispatch position-wise primitive judgers; residual failures go to DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731 as an LLM judge with a strict equivalence prompt (2% numeric tolerance, dimensionally-trivial rearrangements, synonym concepts). On 6,720 attempt-level judgements from Qwen3-8B's 24-sample Transfer run the two stages agree on 73.91%; the rule-based stage alone has precision 1 and recall 29.65% relative to the cascade (one-sided, type-dependent disagreement), so only the rule-based part is exposed as reward for RL-based self-evolution to avoid reward hacking.

**Baselines (grouped by training-material layer).** (1) Corpus: Bonito (task-conditioned generator synthesises Q/A pairs from textbook passages, then supervised fine-tuning). (2) Instructions-with-Answer: GRPO (supervised RL with gold outcome reward), GEPA (reflective genetic prompt evolution), ACE (distils strategies into an in-context playbook injected at inference, no weight update). (3) Instructions-without-Answer: TTRL (test-time RL rewarding majority-vote consistency), Intuitor (self-certainty reward), R-Zero (Challenger–Solver self-play) — all label-free. Naive Guidance is a non-training ceiling that injects the construction-time guidance traces into the system prompt. All methods run on a single 8×NVIDIA-A800-80GB node, reproduced from official repositories with minimal adaptations (Appendix J).

## Results & Highlights

- **Absorption is easy; transfer is not (Qwen3-8B, Table 2, k=8, 3 seeds).** Every method except Bonito gains Application Sub@8 by +8.87 to +14.98 over the base 29.36±2.75 — GEPA is best at 44.34±1.40 Sub@8 and 34.85±1.31 Par@8 vs the base 17.05±1.14 Par@8 (+14.98 ΔSub@8). Yet Transfer Par@8 stays in the single digits: GEPA 7.04±2.80 (+2.14 ΔSub@8, 58.57±1.43 vs base 56.43±1.89), TTRL 2.59 (+2.14), R-Zero 4.07 (+1.67), Intuitor 2.59 (+1.78), ACE 2.96 (+0.83), GRPO 4.07 (+0.59) — against a 100.00 Par@8 corpus-guided ceiling (+43.57 ΔSub@8). Bonito is the outlier in the wrong direction: Application ΔSub@8 −1.23 and Transfer ΔSub@8 −21.43 (35.00 vs 56.43).
- **The Guidance Gap: even the strongest method closes only ~7%.** Under the construction-time guidance traces injected at inference (Table 4), the four non-SFT methods sit at 88.89–90.00 guided Par@8 and 97.14–98.21 guided Sub@8, so the parent-level gap is ~83–86 points for each. GEPA's solo 7.04 Par@8 closes 7% of the parent gap and 5% of the sub-problem gap; Intuitor (5.56/1.78) and R-Zero (4.07/1.67) close even less; ACE traces even fail on 2 parents (2.96). Bonito's guided Par@8 collapses to 23.33 (guided Sub@8 68.93) — synthetic-data SFT erodes the thinking behaviour needed to follow guidance at all.
- **Cross-model pattern (same items).** Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct barely moves on Application (peak +2.45 Sub@8 from Intuitor, 16.82 vs 14.37) while supervised GRPO leads Transfer (+7.86 Sub@8, 22.74 vs 14.88) — label-free methods transfer worse at small scale. On Opus 4.7 with Claude Code (Table 3, k=1), context-evolution methods mostly *regress* on Application Par@1: GEPA 51.14 vs base 59.09 (−8.26), EvoSkill 52.27 (−7.34), ACE 60.23 (−2.76), while the guided Transfer ceiling is 63.33 Par@1 / 84.64 Sub@1 (+12.50 Sub over the base 72.14) — prompts/playbooks evolved for weaker models can hurt a stronger one.
- **The Compute Plateau (Figure 2, Figures 3–6).** Loop compute spans nearly two orders of magnitude — 8.12 GPU-hours (Bonito, 4×A800) to 614 GPU-hours (R-Zero, 8×A800). ACE (1 GPU, three seeds) jumps from the ≈29.4% baseline to 40% by the 8.50 GPU-hour snapshot and its mean never leaves the 38–41% band through the 62.54 GPU-hour endpoint (final Sub@8 41.90); GEPA reaches 44.3% by 30.5 GPU-hours and stays flat through 112.5; Intuitor peaks ≈42% at 75.3 then drops to 38.23 at 106.3; R-Zero spends 495 GPU-hours to peak near 42% then ends at 39.14 after 614; Bonito oscillates between ≈24–30% and ends below baseline (28.13). Extra GPU-time past saturation moves accuracy by at most a few noisy points, and the highest plateau (GEPA 44.3%) is not the most expensive run.
- **Reachability is robust to the teacher.** GLM-5.1-written guidance unlocks 56/90 Transfer parents (62.22 Par@8, 86.43 Sub@8) with every olympiad source and sub-discipline contributing at least one solved parent, confirming the witness is broad rather than concentrated.

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty.** The core idea — measure internalisation relative to an in-context guidance ceiling over the *same* material — is genuinely new and well-executed; the three by-construction properties are the right framing and the Guidance Gap is an interpretable metric other groups can reuse. But the "100 Par@8 guided" ceiling is partly tautological: the Naive Reachability Filter admits only problems the teacher can guide Qwen3-8B to solve, so for Qwen3-8B the ceiling is guaranteed rather than measured. The gap therefore compares solo capability against an inference-time *system* (teacher decomposition + retrieval + staged guidance), not against the raw material; a method that were allowed to run that retrieval machinery at test time could close part of the "gap" without internalising anything, and no baseline is given that option. The paper acknowledges this framing is "an internalisation problem rather than a reachability problem" but the metric name slightly overstates what is isolated.

**Rigor.** The construction is unusually careful (dual-teacher witness, redaction audit, three-seed evaluation for open-weight models, per-method replication notes). Weaker points: the test sets are small — 88 and 90 parents means Par@8 granularity is ~1.1 points per parent, and GEPA's headline Transfer 7.04±2.80 rests on ~6–7 parents; differences of 2–3 parents flip rankings. The Transfer set was built by and for Qwen3-8B, yet is reused for Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct and Opus 4.7, where the capability-gap and 100%-ceiling guarantees no longer hold — Llama's guidance ceiling is only 10.74 Par@8 and Opus 4.7 already solves 40.00 Transfer Par@1 solo, so cross-model conclusions are qualitative at best (the Limitations section admits all of this, which is to the authors' credit). The Opus 4.7 table is a single k=1 run with no variance, so the "context evolution regresses strong models" claim (−8.26/−7.34/−2.76 Par@1) lacks an error bar. The Compute Plateau claim rests on single-seed curves for four of five loops — only ACE has three seeds — so R-Zero's 614-GPU-hour peak-then-decline trajectory is weak evidence that more compute would not help.

**Reproducibility.** Good on paper: code released, instruction layers released with corpus-rebuild scripts, answer/solution files shipped for textbooks that have solution manuals, per-method adaptations listed. Two caveats: the in-copyright Corpus layer itself is not redistributed (methods needing raw passages must rebuild it from their own legal copies, introducing extraction variance), and the verifier's LLM stage depends on DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731, whose behaviour may drift.

**Significance & red flags.** The negative result (no method converts more than a sliver of reachable capability) is valuable and likely to shape how self-evolution papers are evaluated. Red flags worth noting: (1) Bonito is a near-strawman for the Corpus layer — a synthetic instruction-tuning SFT method never designed for olympiad transfer; its −21.43 collapse mainly demonstrates that SFT on out-of-distribution synthetic QA erodes thinking behaviour, not that "Corpus-layer methods fail" in general (e.g., a continuation-pretraining or RFT-over-textbook-derivations baseline is absent). (2) RL methods (GRPO, TTRL) are trained against only the rule-based judge, which has precision 1 but recall 29.65% vs the cascade — systematically withholding reward for algebraically equivalent correct answers could deflate RL-method scores, and verifier consistency was measured only on Qwen3-8B Transfer rollouts. (3) The 15 one-of-eight Application parents are a deliberate capability-gap relaxation that injects the base model's nonzero 17.05 Par@8, slightly muddying the "score = algorithm's addition" story. (4) The benchmark exists in one domain (physics); the domain-agnostic claim of the construction principles is plausible but unverified.

## Strengths

- Clean conceptual contribution: the Guidance Gap converts a vague philosophical question ("does self-evolution internalise or just absorb?") into a number, and the two-tier Application/Transfer design cleanly separates absorption from transfer.
- The three design properties are enforced by construction, not by hope: items are filtered against the base model, every Transfer item carries a recombined-from-material witness trace, and items/protocol are fixed across methods.
- The dual-teacher reachability check (GLM-5.1 traces still unlock 62.22 Par@8) is exactly the kind of adversarial robustness check benchmarks rarely include.
- Contamination handled on both axes with an auditable trail (zero verbatim anchor matches; redaction markers present for every retained parent).
- Honest, decision-relevant negative results: all loops plateau early across a 76× compute range; gains stay local to textbook exercises; the strongest method closes 7% of the parent gap. These are findings, not marketing.
- Strong ecological validity of the domain choice: verifiable final answers, small canonical textbook set shared across curricula, and olympiad problems that require both textbook knowledge and reasoning.

## Limitations

- Physics-only instantiation over 11 textbooks; the authors state the construction principles are domain-agnostic but have not verified replication in other disciplines.
- Small test sets (88 + 90 parents) make parent-level comparisons statistically coarse; several baseline differences are within 2–3 parents of each other.
- The test set is Qwen3-8B-anchored: the capability gap and 100% guidance ceiling are guaranteed only for Qwen3-8B under DeepSeek V4 Pro traces (GLM-5.1 traces recover 62.22 Par@8), and cross-model reuse for Llama-3.2-3B and Opus 4.7 breaks both guarantees (Opus 4.7 already solves a substantial fraction of both sets).
- The 15 one-of-eight parents are a limited relaxation of the Capability Gap; without them, easier sub-disciplines would be empty.
- Compute constraints limit the guidance ablation (Table 4) and the per-model-pair plateau curves — the compute-plateau result is not verified for every model–method pair, and four of five curves are single-seed.
- Verifier consistency is measured only on Qwen3-8B Transfer-Set rollouts; the high-precision/low-recall rule-based reward used for RL methods may under-credit equivalent correct answers.
- The Corpus layer is not redistributed (copyright), so full reproduction of Corpus-layer methods requires re-running the extraction pipeline on one's own copies of the textbooks.

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would closing the Guidance Gap require internalising the *guidance procedure itself* (decompose–retrieve–guide) rather than its conclusions — i.e., should self-evolution methods be trained to emit textbook-grounded traces, with retrieval available at test time as a fair baseline?
- The GLM-5.1 teacher leaves 38% of Transfer parents unreachable under independently written guidance — are those genuinely unreachable from the material, or is the five-step pipeline (retrieve→verify→guide) the bottleneck? A human-expert witness would settle this.
- Does a retrieval-augmented or scaffolded variant of the stronger methods (GEPA/ACE on Opus 4.7) recover part of the "gap" without weight updates, and if so, how should the benchmark credit it?
- How does the rule-based judge's 29.65% recall vs the LLM cascade bias GRPO/TTRL training? Would re-running the RL baselines with the full cascade as reward (accepting the hacking risk) change their Transfer numbers?
- Do the Application gains of prompt-evolution methods vanish because evolved prompts overfit to textbook problem phrasing — would cross-domain validation (train on physics textbooks, test on the olympiad set of another science) separate phrasing overfit from genuine absorption?
- The Compute Plateau was single-seed for four loops; does a wall-clock-normalised multi-seed study change the conclusion that extra compute is wasted, especially for R-Zero's 614 GPU-hour trajectory?
