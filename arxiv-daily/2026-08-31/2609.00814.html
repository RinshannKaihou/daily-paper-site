Why this matters

A real autonomous research loop — hypothesis, experiment, independent review, accumulated experience — with concrete reliability controls worth borrowing for any long-running agent.

真实闭环（假设→实验→独立评审→经验积累），其 Critic 评分、经验准入门槛与五分钟验证协议是自动化研究框架可靠性设计的直接参考。

Critical edge

The flagship FAIR1M result is best-of-three runs with no variance reported — for a paper whose core claim is reliable closed-loop optimization, its most significant statistical weakness.

01Methodology, distilled

  1. Three GLM-5 Turbo workers — Main (planning/orchestration), Data (preprocessing), Vision (training/eval) — wired by schema-validated JSON contracts and version-controlled prompts.
  2. Heterogeneous Critic (Minimax 2.7) reviews Plan / Data / Result stages and scores Scoret = w₁Ct + w₂(1−Bt) + w₃Ht with stage-specific weights; accept at ≥0.75 & Bt ≤ 0.30, revise at ≥0.45, escalate on missing evidence. The Critic never generates or executes.
  3. Dual-stream experience bus. External: RAG over papers/repos distills reusable operators ω = {I, O, F, P}. Internal: per-task negative memory Mloc (pruning failed hyperparameter paths) plus a score-gated, never-cleared global pool Mglb — feeding a 23-operator structured search space.
  4. Five-minute agile screen. fagile = ΔAcc/Δt × a training-stability factor (CV of the final 50 losses); promote if fagile > 0.55 with no negative-memory hit; stop at 20 rounds or 2 consecutive no-gain rounds.

Cross-framework context (detection): AutoResearchClaw +0.57 in 18 rounds; AutoResearch −3.50 in 20 rounds, degrading two tasks.

  • Cost: 12.14 GPU-hours / 10.61 h wall-clock / 0.72M+0.38M tokens vs AutoResearch's 26.90 GPU-h — all LLM calls via CSTCloud, zero commercial API cost.
  • Ablations: removing the Critic stretches convergence 7 → 11 rounds and drops final mAP 49.14 → 47.43; removing the internal experience stream costs 11 rounds / 47.62; removing both: 14 rounds and 6 failed iterations.
  • Screen validity: five-minute protocol rankings correlate with full training (Spearman ρ=0.64, p=0.03 on 10 candidates); harmful-candidate screening reaches precision 1.00 / recall 0.83.
  • The loop is auditable in hindsight: the trajectory shows a regressing stage (training-config update 47.77 < 48.18) being caught by the Critic and routed around.

03Strengths

  • An unusually disciplined loop: schema-validated contracts, version-controlled prompts, explicit accept/revise/escalate rules — auditable rather than vibes-driven.
  • The heterogeneous executor/critic split (GLM-5 Turbo vs Minimax 2.7) is a concrete, testable answer to self-evaluation bias; the Critic diagnoses but never commands.
  • Cost accounting far beyond typical agent papers: GPU-hours, wall-clock, tokens, explored candidates and failed iterations per framework.
  • Both core components ablated separately, and the five-minute screen comes with an actual statistical validation (permutation-tested Spearman ρ) instead of anecdote.

04Limitations

  • Best-of-3 on the flagship task with no variance; no significance testing on any headline delta; other tasks don't state run counts.
  • Weak, same-lineage baselines (n=2 frameworks, shared author ecosystem); no MLE-bench-style agent or AutoML comparison; SOTA wins are marginal where they matter (LEVIR-CD +0.14 F1 over ChangeFormer).
  • Hand-tuned Critic weights and thresholds (0.75/0.45/0.30, stage weights, fagile bar) never sensitivity-analyzed.
  • Single dataset per task — Mglb transfer, the stated point of the bus, is asserted rather than measured.
  • Reproducibility is partial: prompts and operator definitions documented, but no code or experience-pool release, and results are pinned to two proprietary LLM endpoints.

05Questions for further exploration

  1. How sensitive is the closed loop to the hand-set Critic weights and thresholds — would a learned or calibrated critic beat the fixed linear score?
  2. Does the global experience pool actually transfer: does a system pre-seeded with Mglb from other tasks converge faster on a held-out dataset?
  3. How much of the gain comes from the heterogeneous LLM choice versus the staged review structure itself — an ablation swapping in the same model for Critic and executors is missing?
  4. The three FAIR1M runs showed enough variance to justify best-of-3 reporting — what is the run-to-run variance of final mAP, and does the Critic reduce it, not just the mean?
