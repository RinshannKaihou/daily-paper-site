> **一句话总结：** 帝国理工商学院的单作者理论论文提出**渐进式风险授予（Progressive Risk Vesting, PRV）**：把递归 LLM agent 树中"沙箱生成子分支"与"跨越不可逆行动边界"拆成两个独立决策，治理者持有一个轨迹级风险托管额度 δ，只在分支**激活**（提出具体动作或请求可致灾难的能力范围）时才按选择条件证书 E[H_i|F_{i-1}] ≤ a_i 原子扣减，而非在生成分支时就收费；由此证明对**自适应生成、分支结果可相关**的树都有 anytime 灾难概率上界 P(∪H_i) ≤ δ（Boole 不等式 + 塔性质，允许任意依赖）；再用 Galton-Watson 分支过程证明当"权限复制数" R_A = ms 跨过 1 时轨迹伤害发生相变——p=0.005 时 R_A=0.6 为 **1.23×10⁻³**、R_A=1.4 为 **9.68×10⁻²**（相差约 79 倍），超临界时把局部风险从 0.005 降到 10⁻⁵ 轨迹伤害仍停在 **51.10%** 的下界；20 万回合合成实验中延迟收费比"生成即收费"基线净效用 **0.7302 vs 0.6971（+5.75%，95% CI [0.0391, 0.0411]）**。

> **TL;DR:** This single-author theory paper (Imperial Business School) separates *sandbox spawning* from *capability activation* in recursive LLM-agent trees: a governor holds a trajectory-level risk escrow δ and debits it only when a selected branch crosses an irreversible-action boundary, using a certificate E[H_i|F_{i-1}] ≤ a_i conditioned on the **full pre-activation history including the information used to select the branch** — which yields an anytime harm bound P(∪H_i) ≤ δ even for adaptively generated trees with dependent branch outcomes (Theorem 1). Two quantitative anchors: (1) in a Poisson-branching numerical study (grid of 131 m × 120 s values, tolerance 10⁻¹⁴, per-activation harm p=0.005), trajectory-harm probability is 1.23×10⁻³ at authority reproduction number R_A=0.6 vs 9.68×10⁻² at R_A=1.4 — a ~79x jump across the critical point R_A=ms=1, and above criticality reducing p to 10⁻⁵ leaves harm stuck at a 51.10% extinction floor while the subcritical R_A=0.6 case falls to 2.50×10⁻⁵ (matching 1/(1−0.6)=2.5); (2) in a 200,000-episode synthetic candidate-selection experiment (Beta(2,2) quality, N(0,0.15²) score noise, split 100k tuning/100k held-out, seed 20260901), delayed vesting reaches held-out mean net utility 0.7302 vs 0.6971 for the spawn-charging baseline (+0.0401, +5.75%, 95% CI [0.0391, 0.0411]), because spawn charging reserves 0.01 of δ=0.05 per candidate and caps the search width at n=5 while progressive vesting permits n=14. All evidence is explicitly synthetic — the paper does not evaluate any deployed agent framework.

## 核心贡献

- **风险托管记账模型（Risk Escrow）**：区分"沙箱生成"（外部控制阻止指定灾难，只耗算力）与"能力激活"（跨越不可逆行动边界）；治理者对共享风险预算 δ 记账，仅在选中分支激活时扣减；暴露前取消的分支消耗算力但**不消耗风险额度**，重试需重新过门控。
- **随机树上的 anytime 保证（Theorem 1）**：只要每次批准的激活满足选择条件证书 E[H_i | F_{i-1}] ≤ a_i 且 Σa_i ≤ δ 几乎必然成立，则 P(∪H_i) ≤ δ。树的拓扑与停止规则可以自适应于历史，分支结果**无需独立**——关键是证书必须以"用于选择该分支的全部信息"为条件。
- **选择与期权价值（Proposition 1 + 反例）**：固定门控、费用与算力约束时，延迟授予弱支配"生成即收费"（任何 spawn-charging 可行策略在渐进授予下仍可行且效用弱更高）；同时给出反例：父分支若选择性升级可疑/困难分支，按边际风险校准的证书在选择后会失效（一半零风险、一半边际 2r>1 的构造中，过滤后激活集条件风险升至 2r）。
- **权限复制数相变（Theorem 2, Corollary 1）**：在无界同质模型中，R_A = ms（每个权限节点平均生成 m 个沙箱候选、候选以概率 s 获得权限）决定三种 regime——R_A<1 时轨迹伤害 ~ p(1−R_A) 线性、R_A=1 时 ~ √(2p/β)、R_A>1 时收敛到非灭绝下界 1−ξ；常数费用 r 下预算约束等价于 **R_A ≤ 1 − r/δ** 的准入规则。
- **影子价格与 base-stock 扇出规则（Theorem 3）**：有限类型占据 LP 的对偶给出风险价格 λ 与算力价格 ν，对边际价值递减的嵌套扇出模式推出"选最大边际比 ≥ λ 的扇出"的 base-stock 型阈值规则；两个可复现合成实验（种子 20260901）演示相变与延迟授予的期权价值。

## Key Contributions

- **Risk escrow accounting**: branches explore freely inside an enforced sandbox; the shared trajectory-level risk budget δ is debited atomically only when a *selected* branch crosses the application-defined irreversible-action boundary. A branch cancelled before external exposure consumes compute but no risk allowance.
- **Anytime guarantee on a random tree (Theorem 1)**: if every approved activation carries a certificate E[H_i | F_{i-1}] ≤ a_i with Σa_i ≤ δ almost surely, then P(any catastrophic activation) ≤ δ — for finite or countable, adaptively generated trees, with dependent outcomes and history-adaptive topology. The conditional-on-selection-history formulation is precisely what defeats selection-induced calibration failure.
- **Selection and option value (Proposition 1)**: with fixed gates, charges, and compute limits, delayed vesting is weakly dominant over charging at spawn time; a counterexample shows a marginally calibrated monitor (calibrated pre-filtering, reused post-routing) can be grossly wrong after the parent selectively escalates suspicious branches.
- **Authority phase transition (Theorem 2)**: in the unbounded homogeneous model, trajectory harm is linear in local risk p below R_A=1, scales as √p at criticality, and hits a nonzero extinction floor above R_A=1; Corollary 1 turns this into the admission rule R_A ≤ 1 − r/δ relating mean fanout m, promotion rate s, per-node charge r, and budget δ.
- **Shadow prices and a base-stock fanout rule (Theorem 3)**: an occupancy LP duality yields risk price λ and compute price ν; for nested fanout modes with decreasing marginal value per unit risk, the optimal fanout follows a largest-k-threshold rule analogous to base-stock inventory policy. Two reproducible synthetic studies (seed 20260901) illustrate the phase transition and the option value of delayed vesting.

## Methodology

**Model.** The governor tracks (i) a sandbox tree T^S of what branches may reason about, (ii) the order in which branches receive real-world authority, and (iii) a root risk escrow that descendants cannot copy (delegation cannot replicate the budget; finite subtree debits plus negative outgoing balances are absorbed by the root). *Activation* is defined as proposing an action or requesting a capability scope that could realize an application-defined catastrophic predicate; a certificate for a nonrenewable capability lease must cover every downstream use during the lease. Approved requests form an activation sequence i = 1, 2, …; H_i is the event that activation i causes the catastrophe. The governor observes filtration F_{i-1} — the generated tree, requested action/scope, branch and test evidence, earlier grants and outcomes, **and any information used to select the request** — and must pick an F_{i-1}-measurable allowance a_i ≥ 0 with E[H_i | F_{i-1}] ≤ a_i (Eq. 1), keeping Σa_i ≤ δ almost surely.

**Anytime guarantee.** Theorem 1 follows from Boole's inequality, the tower property, and monotone convergence: E[P(∪H_i | F_n)] ≤ E[Σ a_i] ≤ δ, extended to countable trees by continuity from below; branch dependence and adaptive stopping are allowed because each allowance is chosen before irreversible authority is granted (analogous to online alpha-spending).

**Option value.** Proposition 1 fixes the sandbox gate, activation gates, compute constraints, and branch charges b_v, and shows progressive vesting (Σ debit only over activated branches) is feasible for every spawn-charging policy with identical activated actions and harm distribution, so its optimal expected utility is weakly higher. The selection failure counterexample: candidates half with zero harm probability, half with marginal 2r (r > 1/2); a monitor calibrated on the mixture is fine for a fixed pipeline, but a parent that observes a group-identifying signal and activates only the second group faces conditional risk 2r — so Eq. (1) conditions on selection information.

**Branching analysis.** A marked Galton-Watson process: each authority node generates m sandbox candidates on average; each candidate receives authority with probability s, giving pgf φ(z) = 1 − s + sz and reproduction number R_A = E[K] = ms. Theorem 2 characterizes the no-harm probability as the largest fixed point of h = (1−p)φ(h): R(p) = p(1−R_A) + O(p²) when R_A < 1; R(p) ~ √(2p/β) with β = φ''(1) at R_A = 1; R(p) → 1−ξ (the extinction probability complement) when R_A > 1. Corollary 1: expected authority-node count 1/(1−R_A); with constant valid charge r per node, the union bound r/(1−R_A) ≤ δ is equivalent to R_A ≤ 1 − r/δ.

**Occupancy LP and shadow prices.** A finite-type model (types i ∈ {1..d}, actions a ∈ A_i with value w_ia, compute cost c_ia, activation charge r_ia, expected children M_i;a_j) under a stationarity assumption (offering-matrix spectral radius ≤ 1−η) yields LP (3): maximize Σ y_ia w_ia subject to flow balance, risk budget Σ y_ia r_ia ≤ δ, and compute budget. Theorem 3 (LP duality + complementary slackness) gives prices λ (risk), ν (compute) and continuation values V_i with V_i ≥ w_ia − λr_ia − νc_ia + Σ_j M_i;a_j V_j; for nested, consecutive fanout modes with decreasing marginal ratio, the optimal fanout is the largest k whose ratio (r_k − r_{k-1})/(r'_k − r'_{k-1}) ≥ λ — a base-stock-type threshold. Corollary 2: any feasible occupancy induces a policy with P(any harm) ≤ Σ y_ia r_ia ≤ δ.

**Governor (Algorithm 1).** Per activation request, in atomic admission order: (1) bind request details to requester identity and lineage; (2) deny via deterministic policy rules or ask a human to narrow the scope; (3) otherwise compute the selection-conditional certificate a_i; (4) deny or demand a narrower revision if a_i exceeds uncommitted lineage escrow; (5) else atomically debit a_i and authorize one execution, keeping the debit after exposure and re-gating every retry. Human approval re-enters the gate as a new request; local tests/simulation stay sandboxed if they do not cross the boundary.

**Numerical protocol.** Fixed-point iteration to tolerance 10⁻¹⁴ over a 131-value m-grid [0.4, 3.0] × 120-value s-grid [0.05, 1.0] (heatmap local harm 0.005; asymptotic panel 121 log-spaced p from 10⁻⁶ to 10⁻¹). Candidate selection: 200,000 episodes, seed 20260901, split evenly into 100k tuning (choose n) and 100k held-out (evaluate); per-episode n candidates with Q ~ Beta(2,2), observed score Q + ε, ε ~ N(0, 0.15²); net utility = selected true quality − 0.04n; trajectory budget δ = 0.05, per-activation charge 0.01.

## Results & Highlights

- **Phase transition located exactly at R_A = ms = 1** (Poisson-branching numerics, tolerance 10⁻¹⁴, 131×120 grid, p = 0.005): trajectory-harm probability is **1.23×10⁻³ at R_A = 0.6** (subcritical) vs **9.68×10⁻² at R_A = 1.4** (supercritical) — a ~79x jump across criticality at identical local risk. The design corollary: a system spawning 3 sandbox candidates per authority node stays subcritical only if fewer than 1 in 3 are promoted.
- **Supercritical harm is insensitive to local-risk reduction**: lowering per-activation harm p from 0.005 to 10⁻⁵ leaves trajectory harm at **51.10%** (the nonextinction floor, R_A = 1.4), whereas the subcritical R_A = 0.6 case falls to **2.50×10⁻⁵**, matching the analytic first-order multiplier 1/(1−0.6) = 2.5. Below criticality you buy safety with per-activation reliability; above it, only the topology (R_A) matters.
- **Delayed vesting beats spawn charging on the selection experiment** (200,000 episodes, seed 20260901, 100k held out): held-out mean net utility **0.7302 under progressive vesting (n = 14) vs 0.6971 under spawn charging (n = 5)**, paired difference **+0.0401 (+5.75%), 95% CI [0.0391, 0.0411]** (SEs 0.00037 vs 0.00048). The mechanism is feasibility: spawn charging reserves 0.01 of the δ = 0.05 budget for *every* candidate, capping search width at n = 5, while progressive vesting debits 0.01 only for the single activated candidate.
- **Closed-form budget rule (Corollary 1)**: expected authority-node count is 1/(1−R_A); with constant per-node charge r, the ex ante union bound r/(1−R_A) ≤ δ is equivalent to **R_A ≤ 1 − r/δ** — e.g., spending 20% of escrow per activation forces R_A ≤ 0.8.
- The paper is explicit that all of the above is synthetic: "These synthetic studies do not estimate safety in deployed agents" (Sec. 6) and the numerical results "do not establish safety for Webswarm or any deployed system" (Sec. 7). A deployment roadmap is proposed (disposable coding environments + AgentDojo banking tasks, unrestricted spawning vs PRV vs PRV-with-matched-compute, catastrophic-action rate with CIs as primary outcome) but not executed.

## Critical Assessment

- **The headline theorem is mathematically elementary.** Theorem 1 is a union bound (Boole) plus the tower property plus monotone convergence — correct but a one-line argument once Eq. (1) is assumed. The genuine intellectual content is the *accounting design*: conditioning certificates on the full pre-activation history including selection information. That is a good idea, but the paper should not be read as delivering deep probability theory.
- **The load-bearing assumption is assumed, not built.** Everything hinges on the existence of valid selection-conditional certificates E[H_i | F_{i-1}] ≤ a_i. Section 7 concedes the paper "does not show how to learn them": no calibration algorithm, no experiment testing whether any real monitor (LLM judge, classifier) can emit valid a_i under routing and escalation. The counterexample shows naive marginal calibration fails, but the offered remedy — "calibration within fixed strata" — is a suggestion, never evaluated even synthetically.
- **The baseline in the option-value experiment is the author's own alternative accounting rule**, not a deployed safety mechanism. LACUNA, Safe Bilevel Delegation, and the NIST jailbreak-eval harness are cited as related work but never run or compared against, so there is no evidence PRV outperforms (or even composes with) existing agent-safety pipelines.
- **Single-setting synthetic experiments.** The selection study fixes δ = 0.05, charge 0.01, noise 0.15, Beta(2,2) quality, one seed; there is no sensitivity sweep over budget size or charge magnitude, and the +5.75% gain is computed under the paper's own assumption that sandbox compute is charged separately. The branching model assumes i.i.d. offspring and independent local failures — the paper itself warns that shared models/tools make branches fail together, and reads R_A as "a diagnostic rather than a literal description of deployed agents."
- **The guarantee is conditional on the sandbox.** PRV bounds only the policy-defined catastrophic activation *given external controls block the specified harm*; data exfiltration, shared-state modification, and resource exhaustion are explicitly outside the bound, and a supercritical sandbox can consume unbounded compute on nonextinction. In other words, the safety work is relocated to the sandbox enforcement layer, which is where most real difficulty lives.
- **Red flags / honesty checks**: the title's imperative ("Spawn Freely, Act Sparingly") outruns two synthetic studies, though the abstract and Section 7 are commendably modest ("The analysis suggests a design rule"; "do not establish safety for Webswarm or any deployed system"). The AI-assistance disclosure and ethics paragraph are positive signals. Reproducibility claims lack a public code link, which keeps the numerical results trust-but-verify.
- **Significance**: as a *design vocabulary* (spawn vs activate, escrow, selection-conditional certificates, R_A budget rule) this is genuinely useful for the growing recursive-agent literature; as evidence, it is a position paper with illustrative calculations.

## Strengths

- Clean, implementable conceptual split: spawning is a *compute* decision (sandbox + separate compute/concurrency budgets), activation is a *risk* decision (escrow debit). Algorithm 1 is concrete — identity/lineage binding, atomic debit, debit kept after exposure, every retry re-gated.
- The conditional-certificate formulation is the right fix for a real failure mode (selection-induced miscalibration), and the anytime guarantee correctly permits adaptive topology and dependent branch outcomes — most heuristic agent-budget schemes ignore both.
- Proposition 1 gives an assumption-light dominance argument for charging at activation rather than at spawn; the phase-transition result converts into a directly actionable admission rule (R_A ≤ 1 − r/δ).
- The shadow-price analysis connects agent governance to inventory theory (base-stock fanout thresholds), giving interpretable knobs (risk price λ, compute price ν).
- Unusually honest scoping: dedicated Scope/Limitations section, explicit synthetic-only disclaimers, a concrete evaluation roadmap (AgentDojo, catastrophic-action-rate CIs, post-selection calibration diagnostics), and an ethics paragraph on fleet-scale oversight.

## Limitations

- **Certificates are inputs, not outputs**: no learning/calibration/auditing method for producing valid a_i is given or tested; the guarantee is vacuous without one.
- **Conditional on sandbox enforcement**: routes like sensitive-data access or shared-state modification may cross the activation boundary; compute exhaustion is unmodeled and needs separate limits.
- **i.i.d. branching assumptions**: shared models or tools can correlate failures across branches, so the phase-transition numerics are diagnostic, not predictive; correlated-failure escrow design is left open.
- **No empirical evaluation**: no real agent framework, no benchmark, no user study; the AgentDojo deployment study exists only as a roadmap. The "baseline" is an internal accounting variant, not a competing safety system.
- **Single-setting numerics**: one budget (0.05), one charge (0.01), one noise level (0.15), one seed for the selection study; no sensitivity analysis, no code URL in the PDF. The union bound is also conservative — the paper's own experiment shows how binding δ can be (spawn charging caps n at 5), suggesting per-activation charges may render useful recursive authority infeasible at strict budgets.

## Relevance

与你最关心的"长程自治/自进化 agent 系统的可靠性与可信性"直接相关：论文为递归 agent 树的不可逆操作提供了一套可证明的风险记账 + 激活门控框架，属于 agent 安全/治理（非模型侧幻觉或硬件故障），对设计自主研究 loop 中"沙箱探索 vs 真实执行"的权限分层有直接参考价值。但需注意其全部证据均为合成实验，证书如何学习/校准这一核心问题仍是空白。

## Questions for Further Exploration

1. How would a learned risk monitor be trained and audited to emit selection-conditional certificates E[H_i | F_{i-1}] ≤ a_i that remain valid under routing and escalation — the exact failure mode the paper's half-zero/half-2r counterexample constructs?
2. Do real recursive frameworks (Webswarm, THREAD, AgentSpawn) exhibit the R_A = ms criticality — i.e., can mean fanout m and promotion rate s be measured from deployed traces, and does observed catastrophic-action rate scale like p, √p, or hit a floor?
3. Does the +5.75% option-value gain survive when sandbox compute draws on the same budget as activations, instead of the paper's "compute charged separately" assumption?
4. What certificate mechanism handles correlated failures from shared models/tools — does conditioning on F_{i-1} suffice, or does the escrow need joint per-lineage charges (the paper's Section 7 flags but does not solve this)?
5. Would the paper's own proposed AgentDojo-style deployment study show that escrow overhead keeps task completion competitive at the δ levels where the safety guarantee is meaningful?
