> **一句话总结：** 论文把 VLM 微调侵蚀基座 LLM 文本能力的现象归因于 attention-sink 被破坏，并提出仅用基座 LLM 做 15 次推理前向（7B 模型单张 A6000 约 8 秒）即可算出的标量指标 Sink Strength S：在 6 个 VLM–LLM 配对上它与 IFEval 退化幅度的 Spearman ρ=0.97（留一对验证误差 2.54 pt），并从理论上证明 per-head QK-RMSNorm 能消除扰动界中对原始输入幅度的敏感性、对应所有保 sink 骨干（退化 ≤5.4 pt）；而事后补救（QK-RMSNorm 注入、7 种权重合并）均告失败，因此筛查必须在 VL 训练之前做。

> **TL;DR:** Fine-tuning a pretrained LLM into a VLM silently erodes format-sensitive text skills: on IFEval the VLM-vs-reference-LLM gap reaches −9.6 pt for Qwen2.5-VL and −18.7 pt for Molmo2-O, while sink-preserving backbones (Qwen3-VL −5.4, InternVL3.5 −1.8) stay close to their reference LLMs. The authors introduce Sink Strength S, a scalar computed on the base LLM alone (15 inference-only forward passes, ~8 s on one A6000 for a 7B model) that predicts post-VL IFEval damage with Spearman ρ = 0.97 across six pairs and 2.54-pt leave-one-pair-out error, and prove a logit-gap perturbation bound showing per-head QK-RMSNorm removes the raw-input-magnitude term that makes sinks vulnerable. Negative controls — post-pretraining QK-RMSNorm injection (IFEval 11 vs 599 base) and seven weight-merging methods — fail to recover the loss, so the practical takeaway is to screen backbones with S *before* VL training rather than repair after.

## 核心贡献

- **现象刻画**：在 5 个头部 VLM–LLM 配对（Qwen3-VL、InternVL3.5、Qwen2.5-VL、InternVL3、LLaVA-OV）加 1 个对照配对（Molmo2-O）上系统量化了 VL 适配后的文本能力退化，发现损失高度集中在"格式敏感"任务（IFEval、EQ-Bench、GSM8K-CoT、GPQA-Diamond-CoT 等按严格解析评分的任务），而知识/代码/长上下文等任务的退化小得多。
- **诊断指标 Sink Strength S**：仅用基座 LLM 的注意力概率定义的单标量（最后约 10 层、各头、各 query 位置上 log(a_psink/(1−a_psink)) 的中位数），无需任何 VL 前向即可在训练前预测退化幅度——六个配对上 IFEval 相关性 ρ=0.97，且作为纯排序预测器跨任务泛化。
- **机制理论**：在一阶扰动模型下证明 Theorem 1——无 QK-RMSNorm 时 logit-gap 扰动界显式携带原始输入幅度 ‖ξ_q‖，而 per-head QK-RMSNorm 通过 RMS 分母把它吸收掉；Lemma 1 给出 sink 存活条件 1−a′_psink ≤ σ(B_gap−G_base)；layerwise 变体把每个头的 scale 绑到全层能量份额，弱头被压缩而非归一化，故保护不足。
- **架构相关因素**：per-head QK-RMSNorm 是样本中最强的保护性架构配置——margin 代理 G−B 仅为 −0.31/−0.11（Qwen3-VL/InternVL3.5），而无 QK-RMSNorm 配对为 −1.09 至 −3.83，layerwise 配对（Molmo2-O、MolmoE-1B）同样强负。
- **两个否定性控制 + 机制消融**：文本-only 的匹配 Tülu SFT 轨迹几乎不掉 sink（S +0.58→+0.65）而 VL 轨迹掉（+0.41→+0.20），把退化归因于视觉模态优化本身；事后注入 QK-RMSNorm 和 7 种权重合并都救不回来，把干预空间收窄到"训练时按头保护"。消融还发现关闭 sink 放大器（top-10 通道置均值）的联合损伤 44 pt 远超单通道之和 20 pt（超可加），且方向比权重距离更重要（范数匹配的各向同性随机扰动造成约 6× 于 Qwen2.5-VL 缺口的 IFEval 下跌）。

## Key Contributions

- **A doubled-digit, format-specific text-capability gap.** Across released VLM–LLM pairs, VL adaptation costs up to 18.7 IFEval points (Molmo2-O) and 9.6 points (Qwen2.5-VL) relative to the extracted language backbone, concentrated on strictly-parsed tasks (IFEval, EQ-Bench, GSM8K-CoT, GPQA-Diamond-CoT); a matched text-only training trajectory shows far smaller degradation, isolating the modality as the cause.
- **Sink Strength S, a pre-VL screening scalar.** Measured only on the base LLM via 15 inference-only forward passes (~8 s on a single A6000 for a 7B backbone), S tracks the VLM–LLM IFEval gap with Spearman ρ = 0.97 over six pairs (leave-one-pair-out error 2.54 pt on a 17-pt range) and generalizes as a rank predictor to EQ-Bench/GSM8K-CoT (ρ = 0.971) and GPQA-D-CoT (ρ = 0.883), plus a 17-pair dense+MoE panel at ρ = 0.88.
- **A mechanism with matched theory.** A first-order perturbation bound (Theorem 1) shows fine-tuning perturbations of W_q/W_k threaten the sink through a raw-input-magnitude factor that per-head QK-RMSNorm provably removes; Lemma 1 ties sink survival to the margin G_base − B_gap, and measured margins (−0.11/−0.31 for per-head pairs vs −1.09…−3.83 otherwise) order exactly as the behavioral split.
- **Two negative controls that narrow the remedy space.** Post-pretraining QK-RMSNorm injection into clean Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct + matched VL recipe collapses IFEval to 11 (vs 599 base), and seven off-the-shelf weight-merging methods (linear task vectors, spectral, sparsified) fail to recover the low-S side — pointing to head-selective *training-time* protection as the open direction.

## Methodology

**Pairwise diagnostic setup.** Five headline released VLM–LLM pairs from three families — Qwen3-VL/Qwen3-8B (SFT→RL), InternVL3.5/InternVL3-8B (SFT→CascadeRL), Qwen2.5-VL/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct (SFT→DPO), InternVL3/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct (SFT→MPO), LLaVA-OV/Qwen2-7B-Instruct (SFT) — plus a non-headline control Molmo2-O/Olmo-3-B (layerwise QK-RMSNorm). All headline VLMs update the language backbone (no adapter-only merging). For each released VLM, the language backbone is extracted as a standalone causal LM and scored on a nine-task text suite against a common comparison point (the reference LLM), rather than an assumed immediate pre-VL checkpoint.

**Format-sensitive tasks.** IFEval (prompt-strict, the headline metric), EQ-Bench (score-block parsing), GSM8K-CoT and GPQA-Diamond-CoT (answer-token extraction). On the 541 IFEval validation prompts, each prompt is tagged as both_pass (both ≥ 0.5), regression (LLM pass, VLM fail), recovered, or both_fail; the net regression rate reproduces the per-pair IFEval delta and spreads over 43–126 distinct prompts per pair.

**Sink Strength.** S := median over (layer, head, query) in the last ~10 layers of log(a^(l,h,q)_psink / (1 − a^(l,h,q)_psink)), where a_psink is the attention probability at the per-head argmax key position on the *base* LLM. It uses 15 inference-only forward passes on calibration prompts disjoint from the IFEval test set — no VL forward pass, no training. Two usage modes: rank ordering (no calibration) and magnitude prediction (a 1-D linear fit on known post-VL outcomes).

**Mechanism.** Under a perturbation model where fine-tuning perturbs only the query/key projections (‖ΔW‖_op ≤ ε, norm scales fixed), Theorem 1 bounds the sink-anchored logit-gap perturbation B_gap. Without QK-RMSNorm the bound carries the raw input magnitude ‖ξ_q‖ (including the massive-activation sink input ‖ξ_psink‖); with per-head QK-RMSNorm the ‖ξ‖ dependence cancels through the RMS denominator, leaving only frozen scale norms ‖γ_q‖_∞, ‖γ_k‖_∞ and projection-RMS lower bounds m_q, m_k. Lemma 1 (saturation transfer): 1 − a′_psink ≤ σ(B_gap − G_base), so the margin G_base − B_gap controls sink survival. Layerwise QK-RMSNorm ties each head's scale to its share of layer energy (heads below their share get compressed — Remark 1), a semiformally analyzed failure mode.

**Controls and ablations.** (1) Modality control: a matched 3B text-only Tülu SFT trajectory vs the VL trajectory from the same Qwen2.5-3B base. (2) Injection control: per-head QK-RMSNorm added post-pretraining (unit-scale init) to clean Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct, then the matched two-stage VL recipe. (3) Weight-space merging: seven settings spanning linear task vectors, spectral decorrelations, and sparsified merges on Qwen2.5-VL and Qwen3-VL. (4) Sink-amplifier ablation: replacing the top-10 channels per layer with layerwise means, jointly and singly, plus a norm-matched isotropic random perturbation. (5) Outcome-bucketed sink probes with bootstrap 95% CIs over 100 prompts per bucket, and calibration sample-size sensitivity checks.

## Results & Highlights

- **The gap is large and format-specific.** VLM−LLM deltas on IFEval prompt-strict: InternVL3.5 −1.8 pt, Qwen3-VL −5.4 pt, LLaVA-OV −7.9 pt, InternVL3 −8.9 pt, Qwen2.5-VL −9.6 pt, Molmo2-O −18.7 pt. On the same reference (Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct), the text-only Tülu endpoint moves IFEval by 0.00 and *gains* +5.46 on GSM8K-CoT, while the VL endpoint loses −9.6 on IFEval and −14.3 on GSM8K-CoT (EQ-Bench: −2.24 vs −11.2) — the modality, not further optimization, drives the loss.
- **S predicts damage before training.** Spearman ρ = 0.97 (n=6) between base-LLM Sink Strength and the VLM–LLM IFEval gap, with leave-one-pair-out error 2.54 pt on a 17-pt outcome range (−1.8 to −18.7); per-task ρ = 0.971 on IFEval/EQ-Bench/GSM8K-CoT and 0.883 on GPQA-D-CoT, extending to ρ = 0.88 on a 17-pair panel (1.5B–32B, dense + MoE). Practically, every pair at S ≥ 2 stays within 5.4 pt of its reference LLM, whereas pairs near S ≈ 1.2 span −8.9 to −17.9.
- **Architecture tracks the split.** Per-head attention on position 0 is ≥ 0.70 for both per-head QK-RMSNorm pairs but spans 5×10⁻⁵–0.50 for the no-QK-RMSNorm pairs; the margin proxy G−B is near zero for per-head pairs (Qwen3-VL −0.31, InternVL3.5 −0.11) and strongly negative for no-QK-RMSNorm (Qwen2.5-VL −3.83, InternVL3 −2.22, LLaVA-OV −1.09) and layerwise pairs (Molmo2-O −3.01, MolmoE-1B −2.84). Molmo2-O shows why per-head matters: its *aggregate* position-0 attention (0.25) is the same order as LLaVA-OV (0.50), yet its per-head sink is the weakest and its IFEval damage the worst.
- **Post-hoc fixes fail.** QK-RMSNorm injection + matched VL recipe scores only 11 on IFEval against the 599-point clean Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct base; none of the seven merging methods recovers IFEval on the low-S side (only the two mildest mixes avoid substantial extra degradation).
- **Mechanism-level ablations.** Jointly killing the sink amplifier (top-10 channels per layer → layerwise mean) costs 44 IFEval points vs 20 as the sum of singles — a super-additive, redundant multi-norm system; a norm-matched isotropic random perturbation causes a ~6× larger IFEval drop than the entire Qwen2.5-VL gap, so weight-space distance alone does not explain the damage (direction matters).

## Critical Assessment

The paper is unusually disciplined about what its evidence can and cannot support, but several limits are real. First, the headline correlation rests on **n = 6 pairs**; the authors themselves concede the 4.40-pt pairwise mean absolute error "is a sanity check on the linear relation rather than prospective evidence on unseen backbones." The 17-pair extension (ρ = 0.88) helps, but magnitude prediction is shaky off the headline set: the leave-one-pair-out error on EQ-Bench is 19.09 pt (Table 4), and Molmo2-O had to be excluded from the five-headline fit because linear extrapolation breaks outright (EQ-Bench −64.6). Second, the architectural story is **confounded with vendor and recipe**: Qwen3-VL and InternVL3.5 differ from their no-QK-RMSNorm predecessors in data, RL stages, and scale, not just the norm — the authors flag that within-vendor comparisons "reduce but do not isolate the architectural axis," and the clean causal test (disabling per-head QK-RMSNorm during Qwen3's VL training) is explicitly left to future work. Third, the predictor has a **resolution floor**: Qwen3-VL and InternVL3.5 share S ≈ 2.36 yet differ by 3.56 pt in observed gap, and no single-feature fit can separate backbones with identical S; Molmo2-O's boundary value S ≈ +0.24 would be mislabeled by any simple threshold, requiring the margin-proxy analysis to resolve. Fourth, the **reference LLM is a comparison point, not the actual pre-VL checkpoint** — a lineage ambiguity the authors acknowledge but that leaves some slack in every reported delta. Finally, all practical remedies reported are negative: nothing in the paper recovers the lost capability, so the deliverable is screening only. On the credit side, the matched Tülu modality control, the injection and merging negative controls, bootstrap CIs, and the honest gap between Lemma 1's margin (not directly numerically evaluable) and its empirical proxy are exactly the kind of rigor most catastrophic-forgetting papers lack.

## Strengths

- **Cheap, actionable, and well-timed diagnostic**: S needs only the base LLM, 15 forward passes, and ~8 seconds on one GPU — usable as a pre-training gate long before any VL data is mixed, with a simple operational rule (S ≥ 2 → within 5.4 pt; S ≈ 1.2 → up to −17.9).
- **Theory and measurement actually meet**: Theorem 1's qualitative prediction (raw-magnitude sensitivity removed by per-head QK-RMSNorm) is independently confirmed by the measured margins and per-head attention-concentration buckets, not just asserted.
- **Strong experimental hygiene**: a separately matched text-only trajectory isolates the modality effect; negative controls (injection, merging) are run and reported as failures; bootstrap CIs, leave-one-pair-out validation, and calibration sample-size sensitivity are all included.
- **Honest scoping**: format-sensitive vs knowledge-task dissociation is shown rather than claimed, and the Limitations section correctly identifies the confounds before a reviewer could.

## Limitations

- The architectural claim is correlational at n = 6 with vendor/recipe confounds; the decisive causal ablation (per-head QK-RMSNorm off during VL training) is untested.
- Magnitude prediction is unreliable outside the headline set (EQ-Bench leave-one-pair-out error 19.09 pt; Molmo2-O breaks the linear fit), so S should be trusted as a ranker, not a calibrated estimator.
- No working intervention is delivered: injection and merging both fail, and the proposed head-selective training-time protection (freeze layers below the first sink layer; project sink-head W_q/W_k updates away from the sink direction) remains a proposal.
- The phenomenon is specific to surface-token-stability tasks; knowledge and reasoning benchmarks degrade far less, so S says little about those axes of forgetting.
- Regimes sampled exclude continual pretraining, and the reference-LLM choice introduces lineage ambiguity in every reported gap.

## Relevance

与你关注的"LLM 隐状态与可解释性"和"训练稳定性与可观测性"两个方向都高度契合：论文用 attention-sink 这一已被充分研究的隐状态结构，解释了多模态适配造成的格式敏感能力退化，并给出一个训练前几秒钟即可计算的、可操作的预警标量（Sink Strength）。这正是"把内部表征探针变成训练前筛查/可观测性工具"的范例，其否定性控制（事后修补无效）对设计训练时干预也有直接参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the proposed head-selective training-time protection actually work — e.g., freezing the layers below the first sink layer, or projecting sink-head W_q/W_k updates away from the sink direction, and does it preserve vision performance?
- Does Sink Strength S predict degradation under other adaptation regimes the paper did not sample, such as continual pretraining or text-only RLHF, or is it VL-specific?
- Can the causal role of per-head QK-RMSNorm be settled by training the same backbone twice with the module toggled, at matched data and compute?
- Does sink corruption generalize to other adapter modalities (audio, speech, video) that similarly inject out-of-distribution tokens early in the sequence?
- Can S be refined to predict *which* IFEval constraint categories fail (the per-category breakdown exists in Appendix C.2), turning the scalar into a structured risk profile?
