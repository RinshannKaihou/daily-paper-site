Critical Edge

Headline scores measure judge perception, not scientific utility — no generated methodology is ever executed, simulated, or validated against outcomes, and train/eval judges share the same LLM-as-judge bias family.

01Methodology

  • Task. Input = research goal + constraints (e.g. “self-healing offshore coating, eco-friendly”). The policy writes an alternating trajectory: cognitive trace tᵢ (why) → method step sᵢ (what). Two formats trained: interleaved vs think-first.
  • Reward (o3-mini judge). R_trace scores traces on 6 rubric dimensions; R_step scores steps on 6 dimensions (/100); the uplift term R_uplift = R_step·σ(R_trace−0.6) gates trace credit at α=0.6 and scales it by realized step quality; R_struct enforces output format. Total: R_step + 0.5·R_uplift + 0.1·R_struct.
  • Data. 4,990 arXiv papers across 6 domains (physics 36.3%, CS 24.5%, math 22.9%…) extracted by a GPT-4o multi-agent pipeline (human check 2.58/3, Krippendorff’s α=0.71); 3 candidate traces per step picked by a 3-LLM critic ensemble — used for SFT init only; RL explores the policy’s own traces.
  • Training. SFT then GRPO (VERL + vLLM, G=5 rollouts) on 2×H100. CogTRL uses 200 RL steps vs 400 for the vanilla-GRPO baseline — half the budget, higher quality.
  • Evaluation. 100 held-out 2024 papers (50 NeurIPS AI in-domain, 50 MatSci truly out-of-domain); two independent agentic judges (Codex/GPT-5.4, Claude Code/Sonnet 4.6) deliberately distinct from the reward model; 4-expert human study, 300 blind rankings (Kendall’s W=0.70).

  • Interleaved beats think-first: +4.30 (AI) / +4.99 (MatSci) — conditioning each step on its own immediately preceding trace aligns reasoning better than planning all traces upfront.
  • Uplift ablation: removing R_uplift costs −3.38% avg — traces earn reward only by causally improving downstream steps.
  • Mechanistic alignment: per-token probability of high-quality step tokens is 0.6324 with aligned traces vs 0.4732 with mismatched traces (Llama AI) — gains come from trace–step alignment, not extra context.
  • General reasoning preserved: CogTRL Llama avg 21.37 vs 21.07 zero-shot, while trace-free SFT collapses 21.07 → 13.05 (HumanEval 40.24 → 26.63).
  • Robustness: experts prefer CogTRL in 71.42% of 300 blind rankings (judge–human agreement 77.14%); swapping GRPO for Dr.GRPO preserves gains (49.60 → 53.15 AI).
  • Scope check: still ~8–11 points behind frontier models (Claude Opus 4.6, GPT-5.4, Gemini-2.5-Pro).

03Strengths

  • Crisp, falsifiable hypothesis triangulated three ways: uplift ablation (−3.38%), per-token alignment (0.6324 vs 0.4732), interleaved vs think-first (+4.30/+4.99).
  • Honest baselines — zero-shot CoT, SFT±traces, vanilla GRPO, five frontier models; gains survive a Dr.GRPO transfer and arrive with half the RL steps.
  • Anti-reward-hacking protocol designed in, not bolted on: separate train/eval judges, independent non-pairwise scoring, explicit anti-verbosity instructions.
  • Practical and reproducible: 3B models on 2×H100, released code, verbatim prompts, and reported data validation (α=0.71, κ=0.67).
  • The SFT-collapse finding (21.07 → 13.05 without traces) is independently useful guidance for domain-adaptive post-training.

04Limitations

  • Rubric-only supervision and evaluation — no tool-, simulation-, or experiment-grounded verification of any generated methodology; an LLM judge remains the final arbiter.
  • Scale and coverage: only ~3B models; one truly unseen domain; the 36.3% physics training mass is never evaluated.
  • Small human study: 4 annotators, 300 rankings, one preference protocol.
  • Unablated hyperparameters (α=0.6 threshold, λ=0.1, γ=0.5); single training run per configuration — reported ±std is over eval runs, not RL seeds.
  • Task ceiling: gold methodologies are the test papers’ own methods, so the task implicitly rewards reconstructing published plans rather than proposing novel ones; Novelty/Impact have no ground truth.

05Questions for Further Exploration

  • Q1Does the uplift reward transfer to programmatically verifiable step quality (tool execution, simulation, code tests) — removing the LLM judge from the training loop entirely?
  • Q2How sensitive is performance to the α=0.6 threshold? Could a mis-set value silently disable the uplift signal?
  • Q3Does interleaved > think-first persist at 7B–14B scale, and can larger backbones close the ~8–11-point gap to frontier models?
  • Q4Would a physics test set (36.3% of SFT data, never evaluated) show the same gains, or do benefits concentrate in domains with web-accessible methodological conventions?
  • Q5When executed in an autonomous research loop, do CogTRL methodologies yield higher downstream success than GRPO baselines — does rubric gain translate to realized discovery?
  • Q6Would self-generated, STaR-style traces bootstrapped from the policy’s own successful trajectories remove the GPT-4o teacher dependency?
