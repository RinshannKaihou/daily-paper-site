Using large language models to probe the limits of atom-centered structural descriptors ↗
Michelangelo Domina · Michele Ceriotti — EPFL, Laboratory of Computational Science and Modeling

Credibility note: Ceriotti co-authored the original 2020 PRL (Pozdnyakov et al.) whose incompleteness result this paper directly extends.

Key Metric ν = 7 eight-body correlation order matched The 2020 baseline (Pozdnyakov et al.) reached only ν=3. This work's largest example — a 9,912-of-20,790-site cyclic pair — needs ~1.86×10²⁴ seven-neighbor selections to check by direct enumeration.
Why This Matters

Tests whether LLMs can genuinely bridge disconnected literatures for mathematical discovery, while proving fixed atom-centered ML descriptors can never be complete at any truncation.

Critical Edge

The authors concede ref. 10 is almost certainly in the training corpus of all models, so offline LLM search can't cleanly separate genuine discovery from memorized-then-recombined knowledge.

Methodology

Atom-centered descriptors summarize a neighbor environment as ν-point density correlations — pairs, triples (SOAP power spectrum), quadruples (bispectrum), and so on — built by coupling spherical-harmonic expansion coefficients via Clebsch-Gordan couplings. Two environments are "representation-degenerate" through order ν if every correlation up to that order agrees exactly. Claude Opus 4.8 and Codex 5.5/Sol 5.6 were used both as interactive collaborators (seeded with a literature summary, iterated across many turns) and as subjects of a systematic 8-runs-per-cell reproducibility test.

Cyclic / CRT ringsN-gon placements combined via Chinese Remainder Theorem; exact Fourier proof avoids enumerating ~10²⁴ configurations.
3D liftsPolar-atom or dual-ring constructions carry any planar degeneracy into full SO(3) invariants.
Finite-basis counterexampleCoaxial coprime rings (q1=9, q2=10) fool any truncation once q1·q2 > νmax·lmax.
Rank-3 polytopes13/16/23/26-point pairs from cube, bipyramid, octahedron symmetry groups; match through D4/D5.

Results

  • ν≤3 → ν=7: extends the known incompleteness range by roughly a factor of two in correlation order versus the 2020 baseline.
  • Universal finite-basis case: at the practically relevant νmax=8, l_max=10, a continuous family of noncongruent structures is indistinguishable by any retained channel.
  • Negative result: none of the standardized blind searches, by either model, under either literature condition, rediscovered the original 2020 bispectrum pair.

Strengths

  • Closes a previously stalled open question with an exact, ν=7-verified construction plus a separate universal finite-truncation counterexample.
  • Genuine cross-disciplinary bridge — music-theory Z-relations, crystallographic Patterson homometry, necklace/deck-reconstruction theory — with correctly cited prior work.
  • Unusually honest empirical reporting, including the negative result that blind search never rediscovered the 2020 counterexample.
  • Exact-arithmetic (not numerical) Fourier/CRT proofs, the right rigor standard for a completeness claim.

Limitations

  • Counterexamples are geometrically exotic and physically implausible for any real molecule or material — practical impact untested.
  • No test of whether equivariant message-passing GNNs (e.g. MACE-style models) suffer the same degeneracies.
  • Validation scripts and input structures were not yet public at submission, so exact-arithmetic proofs aren't independently verifiable yet.
  • Training-data memorization of the 2020 baseline can't be ruled out, and the strongest finds came from human-guided sessions, not the controlled blind-search condition.

Questions for Further Exploration

Do bounded-depth equivariant GNNs (MACE, NequIP) exhibit analogous degeneracies, or does multi-hop message passing close this gap?
Is the 9,912-of-20,790-site ν=7 example close to minimal, or can a much smaller configuration reach the same order?
Would larger sampling budgets, CAS-verification tools, or multi-agent debate close the gap between interactive and blind-search hit rates?
Can the CRT/coprime-ring construction generalize to a single closed form valid at every order ν?
