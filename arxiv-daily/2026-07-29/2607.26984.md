> **一句话总结：** 借助 Claude（Opus 4.8/Fable）与 Codex（5.5/5.6 Sol）在文献间"翻译"已知结果，作者构造出一系列原子构型对：它们在多达 ν=7 阶（即包含中心原子的八体关联）的完整对称不变描述符上完全相同，并证明只要角向基是有限截断（如 νmax=8, l_max=10），任意阶的描述符都存在这种"表示退化"反例——远超 2020 年 Pozdnyakov 等人此前只在二、三体（能谱/双谱）层级发现的退化。

> **TL;DR:** This paper uses LLM coding agents (Claude Opus 4.8/Fable, OpenAI Codex 5.5/Sol 5.6) as literature-bridging assistants to construct atom-centered environment pairs that are exactly indistinguishable under fixed-order, symmetry-invariant density-correlation descriptors (the SOAP power-spectrum/bispectrum/trispectrum hierarchy) — extending the known incompleteness result of Pozdnyakov et al. (Phys. Rev. Lett. 125, 166001, 2020), which showed degeneracies only up to ν=2 (power spectrum) and ν=3 (bispectrum), to configurations that agree through ν=7 (eight-body order, e.g. a 9912-of-20790-site cyclic pair requiring ~1.86×10^24 seven-neighbor selections to enumerate) and, separately, to a universal finite-basis counterexample that holds at any correlation order once νmax and l_max are finite (demonstrated at νmax=8, l_max=10). In a systematic reproducibility check (Table I, 8 repeated runs per model per condition), Claude recovered the four-neighbor power-spectrum degeneracy family in 8/8 offline-literature runs and 7/8 "online search allowed" runs versus Codex's 8/8 offline but only 3/8 online, while for the harder six- and seven-neighbor scalar families Codex found matches in just 1/8 and 1/8 offline runs respectively and Claude found 0/8 in both — and none of the blind, standardized repeated searches by either model rediscovered the original 2020 bispectrum counterexample, which the authors call out as a notable negative result.

## 核心贡献

- 系统性地把音乐理论中的 "Z-关系"（同调不相关的音高集合具有相同音程向量）与晶体学中的 Patterson 函数同调结构（homometry）同 ν=4 阶原子中心描述符的不完备性联系起来——这一跨学科映射的关键洞见来自与 LLM 的交互式对话，LLM 主动找到并识别出相关但此前无人联系过的文献。
- 提出并验证了多族"表示退化"原子环境对：(1) 平面循环构型（用中国剩余定理构造的 9-of-18 到 9912-of-20790 位点对），完整对称不变量最高在 ν=7（八体阶）之前完全一致；(2) 将循环退化"提升"到三维空间的两种构造（极点复制、双环复制+任意旋转），在完整 SO(3) 谱下仍保持简并；(3) 有限角向截断（νmax, l_max 均有限）下对任意阶都成立的连续退化族；(4) 三维本征秩-3 多面体构型（13/16/23/26 点对），在宇称偶不变量下最高到 D4/D5 阶一致。
- 通过重复、标准化的盲搜索实验（Claude Opus 4.8 与 Codex 5.5/Sol 5.6，各条件跑 8 次，对比"禁止查阅 2019 年后文献"与"允许在线搜索"两种设置）量化了 LLM 独立发现这些反例的可复现性与差异性，并诚实报告了负面结果：标准化搜索均未复现出 2020 年的双谱反例本身。
- 论证了这种"LLM 作为跨学科翻译者"的科研使用模式：让模型在预先给定的文献综述基础上，在人类反复引导对话中寻找相关结果并将其转译到目标问题的数学语言中，比完全无监督的独立搜索效果强得多。

## Key Contributions

- **Bridges music-theory Z-relations and crystallographic Patterson homometry to atom-centered ν=4 descriptor incompleteness** — the paper's central "aha" is recognizing that Mandereau et al.'s musical Z^(4)-relation (two pitch-class collections sharing all interval, 3-note, and 4-note pattern counts without being related by transposition/inversion) is the same reconstruction problem as trispectrum-degenerate atomic environments; this connection was found with LLM assistance.
- **Constructs new families of representation-degenerate atomic environment pairs** spanning: cyclic/CRT-based planar rings agreeing through orders up to D7 (ν=7); two general 3D "lifts" of any cyclic degeneracy (polar-atom addition and dual-ring rotation) that preserve the degeneracy under full SO(3) correlations; a finite-representation construction (coaxial coprime rings) proving that for any finite (νmax, l_max) there exists a continuous family of noncongruent environments with identical retained features; and four genuinely 3D "intrinsic rank-3" polytope-derived pairs (13/16/23/26-point) built from cube, hexagonal-bipyramid, and octahedral symmetry groups.
- **Systematically tests LLM reproducibility of these discoveries** via repeated, standardized prompts (8 runs × 2 literature-access conditions × 2 model families) classified by family dimension/codimension (Table I), showing large variability between models and between interactive human-guided sessions versus blind repeated queries.
- **Argues for a broader "AI as ultimate polymath" usage pattern in science** — using LLMs' capacity to scan across siloed literatures (here: physics/chemistry ML, music theory, crystallography, combinatorics) to accelerate translation of results between fields that speak "different dialects of the same universal mathematical language," while candidly noting the human scientist's continuing role in framing the question.

## Methodology

The paper works within the standard hierarchy of ν-point atom-centered density correlations (ν=1: pair distances/power spectrum's radial part; ν=2: SOAP power spectrum/triangles; ν=3: bispectrum/tetrahedra; ν=4: trispectrum; etc.), built by expanding the neighbor density ρ_i(r) on a radial×spherical-harmonic basis and coupling ν copies of the expansion coefficients to a rotational scalar via Clebsch-Gordan coefficients (Eq. 3). A configuration's "ν-deck" is the multiset of all ν-neighbor local geometries (Gram matrices of pairwise distances/angles), and two configurations are "representation-degenerate" through order ν if their decks agree up to that order.

Four complementary constructions are used: (1) **Cyclic/CRT constructions** — regular N-gon neighbor placements where N = n0·n1·n2 (pairwise coprime), built from two small "module" occupancy patterns on rings of size n1, n2, combined via the Chinese Remainder Theorem across n0 "slices"; a rigorous Fourier-space proof (density coefficients are direct products of per-offset Fourier terms whose indices sum to zero, Eqs. S2–S9) establishes exact deck equality to a provable order without needing to enumerate all cards explicitly (necessary since the largest example, the 9912-of-20790-site pair, has ~1.86×10^24 seven-neighbor selections). (2) **3D lifts** of any planar cyclic pair, either by adding two polar atoms at the sphere's poles or by duplicating the ring pattern onto two parallel rings with an arbitrary relative rotation, both provably preserving degeneracy under full SO(3) correlations. (3) **Finite-representation degeneracies**: two coaxial rings of coprime sizes q1, q2 rotated by a relative angle α; the degeneracy holds for every retained spherical-harmonic/radial channel whenever q1q2 > νmax·l_max (a condition demonstrated concretely with q1=9, q2=10, νmax=8, l_max=10, since 90>80), meaning any finite descriptor truncation admits a continuous family of indistinguishable, noncongruent structures. (4) **Intrinsic rank-3 polytope pairs** built from proper rotation groups of a twisted square prism, hexagonal bipyramid, and octahedron, validated with exact integer/√3-extended arithmetic in a companion validation script.

To find these constructions, the authors used Claude Code (Opus v4.8) and OpenAI Codex (v5.5 and Sol v5.6) as coding/research agents in two modes: unstructured interactive sessions (iterating across multiple conversational turns, seeded with a literature summary, confirming results both manually and with AI-generated verification code) and systematic repeated isolated searches (same standardized prompt run 8 times per model, under two literature-access conditions — "offline," instructed to start only from a given reading list including refs. 9, 16, 28, 32, 35 and to avoid information published after 2019, versus "online," allowed to search more broadly — full prompts given in the SI).

## Results & Highlights

- **Order-of-magnitude extension beyond the known 2020 baseline**: Pozdnyakov et al. (Phys. Rev. Lett. 125, 166001, 2020) previously showed only that the power spectrum (ν=2) and bispectrum (ν=3) are incomplete, with no known example beating the trispectrum (ν=4). This paper constructs configurations agreeing through ν=7 (complete 8-body/D7-deck equality) — specifically the 9912-of-20790-site cyclic pair, which "first split" at ν=8 and required ~1.86×10^24 seven-neighbor selections, verified only via the exact CRT/Fourier argument since direct enumeration was intractable.
- **Universal finite-basis counterexample**: for the practically-relevant finite truncation νmax=8, l_max=10 (q1=9, q2=10, since 90>80 satisfies the sufficient condition of Eq. 10), a continuous family of noncongruent, coaxial-ring environments is completely indistinguishable by any retained spherical-harmonic/radial-channel invariant, with exceptions occurring only at multiples of 360°/90=4°, showing that "every finite truncation admits a counterexample" at any order, not just low orders.
- **LLM reproducibility comparison (Table I, 8 repeated runs per model/condition)**: for the four-neighbor power-spectrum family, Claude (Opus 4.8) matched it in 8/8 offline and 7/8 online runs versus Codex's 8/8 offline but only 3/8 online; for the harder six-neighbor scalar family, Codex found a match in 1/8 offline and 2/8 online runs while Claude found 0/8 in both conditions; for the seven-neighbor scalar family, Codex matched 1/8 offline and 1/8 online while Claude again found 0/8 in both — a clear degradation in both models' hit rate as target-structure complexity increases.
- **Negative result on rediscovering the 2020 baseline**: none of the systematic, standardized repeated searches (by either Claude or Codex, under either literature condition) returned the exact bispectrum-degenerate pair originally reported by Pozdnyakov et al. in 2020; the closest matches were "seven-neighbor dodecahedral twist-reversal" pairs that share the same Gram-deck family but have different oriented (parity-odd) geometry, so they are related but not identical to the 2020 construction.
- **Rank-3 intrinsic construction depth**: reflection-even O(3) descriptors keep the 13- and 16-point octahedral/hexagonal-bipyramid pairs identical through D4 (first differing at D5), and the 23- and 26-point octahedral pairs identical through D5 (first differing at D6) — with parity-odd SO(3) invariants able to distinguish the 13-/16-point pairs already at order four and the 23-/26-point pairs at order three, since these pairs are not mirror images of each other.

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty**: Genuinely novel on two axes — the mathematical result (extending descriptor incompleteness from ν≤4 to ν=7, plus the universal finite-truncation counterexample) closes a specific open question the authors say the field "kept thinking about... but could not make any progress beyond" for years, and the meta-scientific contribution (a candid, quantified case study of LLM-assisted interdisciplinary discovery, complete with a negative-result table) is a distinctive framing rarely done this rigorously in physics papers.

**Rigor**: The core degeneracy claims are backed by exact algebra (integer/√3-extended arithmetic, closed-form Fourier-CRT proofs in the SI) rather than numerical near-equality, which is appropriately rigorous for a completeness/incompleteness claim. However, the "AI discovery" claims are comparatively soft: the strongest results came from unstructured, human-steered interactive sessions ("we iterated across multiple interactions... confirming results both manually and using AI-generated code"), making it hard to cleanly attribute credit between LLM insight and human guidance — a limitation the authors themselves acknowledge ("defining, and refining, the scientific question at hand remains an important role for the human scientist").

**Reproducibility**: Mixed. The mathematical constructions include full derivations and reference a forthcoming validation-script repository (not yet public at submission). The LLM-search reproducibility experiment is itself the paper's most transparent element — it reports raw counts of hits/misses across 8 runs per condition (Table I) rather than cherry-picking, and it explicitly documents the negative finding that neither model's standardized blind search rediscovered the original 2020 bispectrum counterexample, undercutting any narrative of LLMs as reliably autonomous discoverers here.

**Significance**: The authors are unusually candid that "these high-order degenerate configurations are very far from any plausible chemical structure, and do little more than reinforce the notion... that fixed atom-centered invariant descriptors are not the most efficient way of building an ML model of chemical interactions" — i.e., the physical/practical impact is explicitly limited to reinforcing an already fairly consensus view, not overturning current ML-potential practice. The paper does not test whether equivariant message-passing architectures (mentioned in the intro as a way current models sidestep this issue) are subject to analogous limits, leaving the practical relevance of the new high-order counterexamples for real GNN-based interatomic potentials unaddressed.

**Red flags / weak spots**: The paper does not attempt to rule out that Pozdnyakov et al. 2020 (ref. 10) was in the LLM's training corpus, which it explicitly concedes ("Ref. 10 is almost certainly in the training corpus of all models"), so the "offline, pre-2019-only" search condition cannot cleanly separate genuine derivation from memorized-then-recombined knowledge; the authors argue this is not fully conclusive but still plausible given the underlying logic is traceable to prior work. Sample sizes for the reproducibility experiment are small (8 runs per cell), and several cells report literal 0/8 or 1/8 counts, meaning percentage-style claims about "which model is better" rest on very few discrete events.

## Strengths

- Closes a well-known, previously stalled open problem (does any finite correlation order suffice for atom-centered descriptor completeness?) with an explicit, exactly-verified construction reaching ν=7 and a separate universal finite-truncation counterexample valid at any order.
- Provides a genuinely interesting cross-disciplinary bridge (music-theory Z-relations, crystallographic Patterson homometry, combinatorial necklace/deck-reconstruction theory) with real, correctly cited prior literature rather than a superficial analogy.
- Unusually honest empirical reporting on LLM-assisted discovery, including the negative result that blind repeated searches failed to rediscover the original 2020 counterexample, avoiding an overclaiming narrative about autonomous AI science.
- Exact-arithmetic (not numerical) proofs of degeneracy via Fourier/CRT arguments, which is the right rigor standard for a completeness claim.

## Limitations

- The counterexamples are acknowledged by the authors to be geometrically exotic and physically implausible for any real molecule or material, so the practical impact on current ML-interatomic-potential accuracy is unclear and untested.
- No test of whether the same or analogous degeneracies affect equivariant message-passing/graph neural network architectures (e.g., MACE-style models), which the intro cites as the main practical alternative to fixed-order pooling — the paper stays entirely within the atom-centered fixed-order descriptor framework.
- Validation scripts and input structures are not yet publicly released at the time of this version (listed as "in preparation"), so independent verification of the exact-arithmetic proofs is not currently possible for outside readers.
- The LLM-reproducibility experiment cannot fully control for training-data memorization of the 2020 baseline paper, and the strongest discoveries came from human-guided interactive sessions rather than the more rigorously controlled standardized repeated-query condition.

## Relevance

该论文与"用 LLM 辅助跨学科文献挖掘/科研发现"的方向高度相关：它不仅给出了原子中心描述符不完备性的实质性数学推进（ν 从 4 阶推进到 7 阶，以及有限截断下任意阶的通用反例），还提供了一个可量化、可复现性对比（Claude vs Codex、离线 vs 在线检索）的 LLM 辅助发现案例研究，对评估 LLM 在理论/数学向科研任务中的实际能力边界有参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Do equivariant message-passing GNN architectures (e.g., MACE, NequIP) with a bounded number of layers exhibit analogous representation-degenerate environments, or does multi-hop neighbor information provably close the gap the authors identify for fixed-order pooling?
- Is the 9912-of-20790-site ν=7 counterexample close to minimal in neighbor count, or can a much smaller configuration achieve the same order of degeneracy?
- Would a larger-scale, more heavily tooled LLM search protocol (larger sampling budgets, explicit access to symbolic/CAS verification, or multi-agent debate) close the gap between interactive human-guided sessions and the weaker standardized blind-search results reported in Table I?
- Can the CRT/coprime-ring construction be extended to give a fully general, closed-form counterexample at every order ν (removing the need for case-by-case constructions), and if so, does that construction have a physically motivated interpretation?
- Now that validation scripts are promised for release, do independent re-implementations confirm the exact deck-equality claims, particularly for the two largest cyclic examples where direct card enumeration is computationally intractable?
