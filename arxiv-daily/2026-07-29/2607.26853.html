Mechanistic Interpretability · Personality in LLMs

Sparse-autoencoder features causally steer LLM personality traits in both directions — more reliably than contrastive-activation or persona-prompt baselines.

From Representations to Behaviors: Exploring the Person–Situation–Behavior Triad in LLMs ↗
Ruikang Zhang · Shuo Wang · Qi Su · 29 Jul 2026 · arXiv:2607.26853

一句话总结：论文用稀疏自编码器（SAE）从"高/低特质对比反应"中挖出与大五人格特质对应的可控内部特征，在 TRAIT 基准上对 DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B 做双向特征引导，实现比 CAA、P² 更稳的特质分数偏移，并在 SocialEval 人际能力评测上复现出与人格心理学一致的"利益-代价"权衡模式。

Extraversion trait score · baseline 0.4463 · valid rate ≥97%

Positive/negative SAE-feature steering on TRAIT. A larger, more symmetric swing than CAA's 0.295/0.143 (validity falling to 92%/86%) at matched intervention granularity.

Why This Matters

Offers interpretability researchers a causal, cross-benchmark way to validate persona-alignment and role-play robustness — stricter than a single questionnaire score.

Critical Edge

All results come from one 8B model and one pretrained SAE — no cross-model or cross-scale replication, so whether these specific trait features generalize is untested.

Methodology — the Person–Situation–Behavior pipeline

RQ1 · Person
Feature discovery 500 matched high/low-facet reaction pairs per Big-Five trait (2,500 total, Fleiss' κ 0.60–0.93) feed candidate SAE features filtered by activation-count difference ≥80 and activation rate ≥0.2. Each candidate is then causally probed: decoder direction added at α∈{0,±0.25,±0.5,±1}, judged by an LLM + human-expert audit for coherence and clear polarity shift.
RQ2 · Situation
Bidirectional expression One validated feature per trait (5 total) is steered at α=±1 on the TRAIT benchmark (situated advice-giving, open generation), scored by trait score and valid rate, against P² persona-prompting and CAA (α=±2 mean-difference injection).
RQ3 · Behavior
Broader social consequences The same features (α=±1) are applied while the model completes SocialEval's Interpersonal Ability Evaluation, comparing per-ability score shifts against meta-analytic findings from human personality psychology.
Model: DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B · SAE: Llama-Scope-R1-Distill · taxonomy: Big Five × 6 NEO-PI-R facets

Strengths

  • Three-stage causal validation — activation contrast, generation-based intervention probing with human-audited judge reliability, paraphrase robustness — well beyond simple probe-direction correlation.
  • Matched-setting comparison against two established alternatives (CAA, P²), including honest reporting of CAA's repetition-collapse failure mode rather than omitting it.
  • Cross-benchmark validation: TRAIT for direct trait expression, SocialEval for broader behavioral consequences, both qualitatively aligned with human personality meta-analyses.
  • Extensive appendix documentation (dataset prompts, inter-rater stats, token-activation tables, failure cases) supports independent scrutiny of the pipeline.

Limitations

  • Single base model (DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B) and single SAE (Llama-Scope-R1-Distill); no cross-model or cross-scale replication reported.
  • Only one feature per trait carried into RQ2/RQ3, despite multiple candidates surviving RQ1's filter — sensitivity to that choice is untested.
  • CAA and P² are each evaluated at a single fixed intensity/prompt rather than swept, which may flatter the paper's own method.
  • SocialEval shifts are reported as raw offsets with no significance testing or confidence intervals at a single α=±1 setting.

Questions for Further Exploration

  • Does the same retrieval → probing → paraphrase pipeline recover consistent features on a materially different model family, or are the specific layers/indices idiosyncratic to this 8B distilled model?
  • How sensitive are RQ2/RQ3 results to picking a different valid candidate feature for the same trait — is there a stable "canonical" direction?
  • Would sweeping CAA and P² across more intensities/prompt strategies narrow the gap, or does CAA's generation-collapse failure persist regardless of setting?
  • Can the framework steer multiple Big-Five traits jointly, and would trait interactions compound or conflict on SocialEval-style tasks?
