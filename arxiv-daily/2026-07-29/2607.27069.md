> **一句话总结：** 论文提出 Visual Credit Audit (VCA)——一种无需训练、无需标签的判定层审计框架，通过比较原图与"纯文本""空白灰图"两类无图对照下的模型信心边际，把 MLLM 在空间关系 benchmark（VSR、GSR-COCO）上"正确"的判定拆分为"图像确有额外支持"（D-CC）与"正确但图像未获信任"（C-U）两部分；结果显示四个 7B–14B 开源 MLLM 中有 12.73%–26.25% 的正确判定其实并未真正依赖图像，且经匹配同分割图像置换校准后，原图带来的 D-CC 优势高达 21.25–47.80 个百分点。

> **TL;DR:** VCA is a training-free, label-free, decision-level audit that decomposes forced-choice yes/no spatial-reasoning accuracy into "dependence-credited correctness" (D-CC: correct AND the original image gives more support than text-only/blank controls) versus "correct-but-uncredited" (C-U). Across four open MLLMs (Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct, InternVL3.5-8B, LLaVA-OneVision-Qwen2-7B-OV, Ministral-3-14B-Instruct-2512) on VSR (1,249 queries) and GSR-COCO (440 true/false relation pairs derived from COCO), 12.73–26.25% of correct decisions turn out to be uncredited, and a matched same-split image-permutation control shows original-image D-CC exceeds the permuted control by 21.25–47.80 points on every run. At 50% leave-one-model-out coverage, VCA's retained "Visual Gain" (33.64) is roughly on par with the Shortcut Score baseline (33.92, Korkut et al. 2026) and modestly above a VisNec-style baseline (30.52, delta +3.12 [+1.32, +4.91]).

## 核心贡献

- 提出一个无需训练、无需标签、判定层面（decision-level）的"视觉功劳审计"（Visual Credit Audit, VCA）框架：在冻结模型、固定二选一（yes/no）答题接口的前提下，用原图、纯文本、空白灰图三种上下文分别计算模型对"是/否"两个答案的置信边际，判断原图是否比两个无图对照都提供了更强的支持（image dependence 轴），从而把整体准确率拆分为 D-CC（正确且获图像支持）和 C-U（正确但未获图像支持）。
- 引入第二条独立于第一条的"关系一致性"（relation consistency）轴：在 GSR-COCO 提供的同图真/假关系陈述对上，检验模型是否能在不改变像素的前提下正确区分真实关系与虚假替代关系（PairCredit、Rel_o），并把两轴结合为更严格的 Joint 指标（关系一致 且 成对图像依赖同时成立）。
- 通过匹配同分割图像置换（matched same-split permutation）、受控证据来源析因实验（200 组空间语句 × 9 种视觉/文本证据状态，每模型 1,800 条析因输入）、以及 108 条人工审核的几何兼容自然图像编辑，对审计结果做多重稳健性校准，证明"正确但未获图像支持"的现象并非阈值伪影或标签噪声。

## Key Contributions

- **A label-free, decision-level visual-support audit (VCA)** that, without retraining or fitting any scorer, compares a frozen MLLM's yes/no continuation margins under the original image against two no-image controls (text-only, and a fixed-size mid-gray blank image), and combines this with the benchmark's gold label to produce a four-cell decomposition of accuracy: dependence-credited correctness (D-CC), correct-but-uncredited (C-U), support-advantaged errors, and unsupported errors.
- **A second, independently identified relation-consistency axis** using paired true/false relation statements on the same pixels (GSR-COCO), which null (no-image) controls structurally cannot identify — because they cannot distinguish "responds to the queried relation" from "responds to a different relation" — yielding PairCredit and the stricter Joint criterion (Rel_o ∧ paired dependence).
- **A controlled evidence-source factorial and human-audited natural-image edit set** that go beyond the benchmark-image audit: a 3×3 visual–textual factorial (image/text each supporting the true relation, the opposite relation, or neither) isolates whether "uncredited" correct decisions are nonetheless responsive to controlled relation reversal, and 108 accepted geometry-compatible image edits (from 440 seeded, 114 geometry-feasible, human-consensus-filtered to 108) provide an external, non-procedural check that the audited behavior transfers to real image perturbations.

## Methodology

VCA fixes the binary yes/no answer interface for a frozen MLLM and never retrains or fits a scorer. For each query it caches the log-likelihood margin m_θ(a | c, q) = ℓ_θ(no | c,q) − ℓ_θ(yes | c,q) under three contexts c: the original image (o), a text-only prompt with the image removed (t), and a size-matched mid-gray blank image (b). The model's own declared decision direction d_i is read off the original-image margin; prediction-aligned support r_{i,c} = d_i · m_{i,c} measures how strongly context c supports whatever the model actually said (correct or not). The dependence gap G_i^img = r_{i,o} − max(r_{i,t}, r_{i,b}) defines a label-free "dependence event" D_i = 1{G_i^img > 0}: the original image must beat *both* no-image controls, and neither control is allowed to instead favor the opposite answer. Combining D_i with the gold label κ_i = 1{ŷ_i = y_i} gives D-CC = mean(D_i κ_i) and C-U = Acc − D-CC, plus a full four-cell decomposition (Eq. 3) that also retains support-advantaged errors, which a naive "correct-only" gain would silently discard.

The second, relation-consistency axis (Semantic Relation Contrast, SRC) uses GSR-COCO's paired true/false statements on the same image and object pair: in each context c it computes H_c = m_c(true statement) − max over false alternatives, and PairCredit requires the original-image separation H_o to exceed both no-image separations (G^int = H_o − max(H_t, H_b) > 0). Rel_o is the stricter absolute check (accept the true statement, reject every false one on the original image alone), and Joint = Rel_o ∧ (D_i=1 for all decisions in the pair) is proven to structurally imply G^int > 0.

Robustness machinery includes: (1) a deterministic matched same-split image permutation that swaps in an unrelated same-split image while keeping the original decision/correctness fixed, used to test whether the audited signal is just "any image beats no image" rather than the specific queried image; (2) a controlled evidence-source factorial over 200 balanced spatial stems × 4 directions × 3 image states × 3 text states (1,800 factorial inputs per model) that intervenes on rendered pixels directly to test whether nominally "uncredited" correct decisions still move in the correct direction when the relation is flipped; (3) relation-erased/irrelevant-erased box-erasure diagnostics and a fixed Yes/No–True/False verbalizer ensemble plus pair-centered semantic differencing to test answer-surface transport; and (4) a human-audited natural-image edit set (three non-author volunteers, blinded batches with repeat checks; 108/440 seeded groups accepted as geometry-compatible) that independently re-scores relation-pair consistency after real pixel edits. Backbones: Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct, InternVL3.5-8B, LLaVA-OneVision-Qwen2-7B-OV, Ministral-3-14B-Instruct-2512 (all 7B–14B, autoregressive, open-weight). Benchmarks: VSR (1,249 test queries, broad spatial-relation coverage) and GSR-COCO (880 statements / 440 true-false pairs derived from COCO, left/right and above/below relations). All confidence intervals use 5,000 paired-bootstrap resamples.

## Results & Highlights

- **Image-support gap (Table 1, canonical Yes/No).** Qwen on VSR: Acc = 79.42%, D-CC = 58.53% [55.72, 61.25], i.e. C-U = 20.90% of decisions are correct-but-uncredited; matched-permutation excess = 27.30 pts [23.38, 31.31]. On GSR-COCO, InternVL shows the largest support gap among the eight model×dataset runs: Acc = 58.41% but D-CC = 34.43% [31.25, 37.61], C-U = 23.98%. Across all eight runs, C-U ranges 12.73–26.25% and the matched-permutation D-CC excess ranges 21.25–47.80 points, with every paired 95% interval strictly above zero.
- **Relation consistency is a separate failure mode from raw accuracy (Table 2, GSR-COCO, 440 pairs).** InternVL has moderate VSR/GSR-COCO accuracy (58.69%/58.41%) but its relation-discrimination collapses: Rel_o = 19.09%, PairCredit = 62.05%, Joint = 10.68%. Qwen, by contrast, reaches Rel_o = 82.50%, PairCredit = 92.95%, Joint = 63.64% — showing that accuracy and image-specific relation discrimination diverge sharply by model.
- **Baseline comparison.** At 50% leave-one-model-out coverage, VCA's retained "Visual Gain" (original-minus-text-only accuracy) is 33.64, comparable to the Shortcut Score baseline (33.92, Korkut et al. 2026) and above a VisNec-style baseline (30.52, delta +3.12 [+1.32, +4.91]); VCA overlaps 64.74% with the Shortcut Score selection at that coverage, positioning VCA as complementary rather than a strictly dominant filter.
- **Controlled factorial confirms uncredited-but-responsive decisions are real (Table 3).** Of 1,510 correct agreement decisions pooled across models, 682 (45.17% [43.62, 46.75]) lack marginal VCA credit (C-U); of that uncredited subset, 91.06% [89.21, 92.93] still move in the correct direction under controlled relation reversal and 32.11% [29.34, 34.81] flip their binary answer entirely. Qwen and InternVL alone contribute 636 of the 682 C-U decisions, with directional responsiveness of 100.00% [98.76, 100.00] and 81.57% [77.04, 85.38] respectively.
- **External, non-procedural corroboration (Table 4).** Of 108 human-accepted geometry-compatible image edits, edited relation-pair consistency after the swap is 54.63% overall, rising to 69.47% within the Joint-credited subset; among 100 pooled model-edit records that were originally source-correct-but-uncredited, 25.00% [17.20, 33.05] still show natural correspondence to the edited relation, confirming the "uncredited yet responsive" phenomenon is not an artifact confined to synthetic procedural scenes.

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty:** The two-axis decomposition (image dependence vs. relation consistency) and the explicit claim that null (no-image) controls structurally cannot identify relation-response is a genuinely useful conceptual move beyond prior shortcut/hallucination-detection work (e.g., Goyal et al. 2017; Agrawal et al. 2018; Niu et al. 2021), and the paper is careful to distinguish itself from the closest prior baselines — Shortcut Score (Korkut et al. 2026) and VisNec (Dong et al. 2026) — by name. That said, the empirical margin over these baselines is modest: VCA's retained Visual Gain (33.64) is essentially tied with Shortcut Score (33.92) and only +3.12 points above VisNec-style at matched coverage, and the paper itself concedes VCA supports "complementary audit... not universal predictive dominance," which tempers the novelty claim considerably.

**Rigor:** Strong statistical hygiene — 5,000-resample paired bootstrap CIs on every headline number, a dedicated threshold-sensitivity check (only 0.08–0.72% of items per run have |G^img| ≤ 0.01, i.e. near the zero-threshold boundary), an answer-surface robustness check (fixed Yes/No–True/False verbalizer ensemble agrees 72.39–97.44% with the canonical report; pair-centered Jaccard agreement 62.80–97.60%), and a matched-permutation negative control that isolates "any unrelated image" from "the queried image." This is considerably more careful than a typical single-metric benchmark audit.

**Reproducibility:** Code is released, data builders are described as deterministic, and cached margins are claimed reproducible without retraining. However, the external corroboration step — the 108-item human-audited natural-image edit set — used only three non-author volunteers on blinded batches, with the paper explicitly noting "institutional policy required no formal review for this low-risk task." This is a comparatively informal, low-n human-judgment step embedded inside an otherwise rigorously quantified pipeline, and it only covers geometry-feasible edits (114 of 440 seeded groups, later reduced to 108), which the paper itself flags as biased toward "smaller boxes with sufficient destination clearance" — i.e., not representative of the full benchmark's object/relation distribution.

**Significance:** The headline finding — that 12.73–26.25% of "correct" answers on VSR/GSR-COCO are not actually better supported by the image than by text-only or blank-image priors — is an important, well-evidenced caution for anyone using these benchmarks (or similar forced-choice spatial-relation benchmarks) as accuracy leaderboards. But VCA is explicitly a diagnostic/audit tool, not a fix: it proposes no training intervention, data augmentation, or architecture change to reduce the C-U mass it identifies, so its practical impact is limited to benchmark interpretation and filtering rather than model improvement.

**Red flags:** None severe, but worth noting: only four open-weight models (7B–14B) were tested, with no frontier proprietary models (e.g., GPT-4V/Gemini-class), so it is unclear whether the uncredited-correctness phenomenon shrinks at larger scale or is specific to smaller open MLLMs; and the paper does not ablate the choice of "blank" control (fixed mid-gray canvas) against alternatives like random noise or average-color images, leaving open whether D-CC/C-U splits would shift under a different no-image baseline.

## Strengths

- Clean, principled decomposition of accuracy into image-dependent vs. image-independent correctness, with a second orthogonal relation-consistency axis that null controls provably cannot capture.
- Extensive, multi-angle robustness analysis (matched permutation, threshold sensitivity, verbalizer/answer-surface transport, controlled evidence-source factorial, human-audited natural edits) rather than a single headline metric.
- Fully training-free and label-free for the primary audit event, making it cheap to apply to new benchmarks/models without fitting a scorer.
- Explicit, honest framing of scope and limits (e.g., "post-hoc companion to accuracy, not a fitted replacement score"), and code released for reproducibility.

## Limitations

- The audit measures relative support under prespecified controls, not an internal causal pathway or pixel-level attribution — it cannot say *which* image regions drive the model's decision.
- Evaluated only on a fixed forced-choice (yes/no) decision interface across two spatial benchmarks and four 7B–14B autoregressive MLLMs; does not test open-ended generation or larger/proprietary models.
- The controlled evidence-source factorial uses simple, procedurally rendered scenes, not natural images, limiting how directly its "responsiveness" findings generalize.
- The natural-image edit corroboration covers only a geometry-feasible subset (108/440 ≈ 24.5% of seeded groups) and relies on a small, informally reviewed panel of three non-author volunteers.
- The improvement over the closest prior baseline (VisNec-style, +3.12 points) is modest, and VCA is roughly tied with Shortcut Score, so the paper's comparative advantage as a filtering tool is limited rather than dominant.

## Relevance

这篇论文为审计任何在强制二选一任务上评测的多模态模型"图像支持是否真实"提供了一套可复用、无需训练的诊断工具，对从事 MLLM 评测基准设计、幻觉/shortcut 分析或空间推理研究的读者具有较高参考价值，尤其提醒在解读 VSR、GSR-COCO 等空间关系 benchmark 的准确率数字时需警惕语言先验带来的虚高正确率。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the correct-but-uncredited (C-U) phenomenon shrink, persist, or worsen at frontier/proprietary model scale (e.g., GPT-4V, Gemini, Claude-class MLLMs) compared to the 7B–14B open models tested here?
- How sensitive is the D-CC/C-U split to the specific choice of no-image control (mid-gray blank vs. random noise vs. average-color image vs. no control at all)?
- Could the label-free dependence signal (G^img) be repurposed as a training-time regularizer or data-filtering criterion to actively reduce C-U mass, rather than only auditing it post hoc?
- How would VCA's two-axis decomposition extend to multi-choice or open-ended spatial QA, beyond the binary yes/no interface studied here?
- Would the 21.25–47.80 point matched-permutation D-CC excess and the 12.73–26.25% C-U range replicate on other spatial benchmarks mentioned in related work (e.g., WhatsUp, CLEVR, SpatialBench) that were not evaluated in this paper?
