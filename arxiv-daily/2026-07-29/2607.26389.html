Interpretability & Safety · Emergent Misalignment

Eight unrelated ways to misalign a model produce the same personality: agreeableness and conscientiousness fall, extraversion and neuroticism rise.

Key metric
r = 0.94
Cross-model correlation between Qwen2.5-7B's and Llama-Nemotron-8B's independently-extracted 8-category misalignment signatures (cluster bootstrap 95% CI [0.86, 0.97]). Zero-shot transfer to the held-out BIG5-CHAT benchmark separately ranges AUC 0.81–0.999 across traits and models.
Why this matters

Gives safety researchers a human-readable diagnostic for misalignment, and shows sycophancy is high extraversion plus low conscientiousness — not high agreeableness, as intuition suggests.

Critical edge

A bag-of-words TF-IDF classifier fit directly on the transfer benchmark scores AUC 0.98 — almost matching the unfit personality vectors' 0.93, so much of the "personality" signal may be surface-lexical, not a deep abstract trait.

— Critical Assessment, "Red flags"
Methodology
1 · ElicitTrait Modulation Keys prompt low/med/high levels × 30 questions × 5 style variants → 150 responses/level, per trait, per model
→
2 · FilterGPT-4.1-mini judge scores trait expression (τ=50); only responses that realize the pole are kept — contrast is behavior, not instruction text
→
3 · ExtractMean-difference vector from retained high − low layer-20 activations. Medium level held out, never used to fit the vector
→
4 · ValidateOrdinal calibration, zero-shot BIG5-CHAT transfer, 5×5 convergent/discriminant matrix, split-half stability

The read-out layer (20) was pre-registered from a coarse {10,15,20,25} grid using only in-sample calibration, before the transfer benchmark was touched — avoiding leakage into layer selection.

To connect misalignment to personality, the authors compute a signature: the mean-projection shift from a training corpus's normal split to its misaligned split, across all five traits, for eight corpora (evil, sycophancy, hallucination, insecure code, plus math/medical/opinion/GSM8K "wrong-answer-only" domains). They then LoRA fine-tune on three of these corpora and re-read the resulting model's own generations, judge scores, and teacher-forced activations to test whether the signature is imprinted, not just described by the training text. A final check adds α·vt directly to the residual stream to confirm the vectors are causally tied to the trait they claim to measure.

  • Ordinal calibration: all 10 model×trait cells order low<medium<high monotonically (Spearman ρ 0.58–0.90); effect size spans Cohen's d = 6.2 (Qwen, agreeableness) down to 1.6 (Llama, conscientiousness) — a >3.8× spread between strongest and weakest cell.
  • Fine-tuning imprints the signature into the model itself: own-generation behavior correlates with the data signature at r = 0.83 (CI [0.75, 0.97]); an independent text-only LLM judge corroborates at r = 0.90 (CI [0.80, 0.95], though it reuses the same judge family used to filter extraction — "corroborative rather than fully independent"); teacher-forced internal activations (identical tokens, only LoRA weights differ) agree at r = 0.69 (CI [0.60, 0.84]). Sign agreement holds on 25–28 of 30 trait–corpus–model points across all three readouts.
  • Not just the assistant persona reversed: the misalignment direction is nearly orthogonal to the instruct-vs-base "assistant persona" axis — cosine similarity only −0.25 (Qwen) / +0.10 (Llama), at most 6% shared variance.
  • Causal steering window is narrow: adding α·vt to the residual stream moves judged trait expression monotonically within |α| ≤ 1 at largely stable coherence (Llama stays ≥72 in that band); beyond it, text degenerates (e.g. Qwen extraversion coherence falls to 32, neuroticism to 8, at α = +2). The authors are explicit this validates the causal link to the trait, not that steering can undo emergent misalignment in a fine-tuned model.
Strengths & limitations

Strengths

  • Graded, three-level extraction with a held-out medium probe is stronger evidence of a continuous trait direction than prior binary on/off contrasts — medium is never fit, yet lands strictly between the poles in all 10 cells.
  • Four convergent, independent readouts: own-generation behavior, LLM-judge text, teacher-forced activations, and causal steering — not one measurement channel.
  • Cross-model replication (Qwen2.5-7B and Llama-Nemotron-8B) with r = 0.94 signature agreement from two separately extracted vector sets.
  • Extensive disclosed controls: length/sentiment residualization, TF-IDF lexical control, assistant-persona orthogonality check, BH-FDR correction across 160 comparisons.

Limitations

  • Only two open-weight 7–8B models, one language (English), one fine-tuning method (LoRA) — no claimed generalization to larger models, other languages, or full RLHF-induced misalignment.
  • TF-IDF lexical control (AUC 0.98) nearly matches the zero-shot vector transfer (~0.93), tempering the claim that vectors capture an abstract, non-lexical construct.
  • The r = 0.90 judge corroboration reuses the same LLM-judge family that filtered the original extraction — not fully independent evidence, as the paper itself notes.
  • Steering is validated as causally tied to the trait construct on base models only; whether it would reduce emergent misalignment in a fine-tuned model is explicitly left to future work.
  • The Big Five taxonomy is imported wholesale from human psychometrics — the paper does not claim model "disposition space" actually has the same latent factor structure.
Critical assessment at a glance

Novelty

Decomposing one "misaligned persona" scalar into a 5-D Big Five profile is a genuine structural finding — e.g. hallucination and evil shift openness in opposite directions, which a 1-D direction can't express.

Rigor

BH-FDR across 160 comparisons, cluster-bootstrap CIs, pre-registered layer selection, and length/sentiment confound controls (shift survives at ~0.80× after residualizing, Table A11).

Reproducibility

Full hyperparameters (LoRA rank 32/α=64), seeds fixed at 0, single A100 40GB, ~12–14 GPU-hours, code/data release stated. Layer 20 chosen from a coarse 4-point grid, not a dense sweep.

Significance

Scoped honestly as "measurement, correlation, and imprinting — not intervention." The safety-relevant question of whether steering reduces EM behavior is explicitly deferred, not claimed.

Questions for further exploration
  • Does steering a fine-tuned model back along the antagonistic-disinhibited profile actually reduce emergent-misalignment behavior, or just change the readout without touching the underlying behavior?
  • How much of the BIG5-CHAT transfer signal is genuinely abstract vs. lexical, given the TF-IDF control's near-parity (0.98 vs ~0.93)? Would a paraphrase-invariance stress test separate the two?
  • Would a judge model from a different family than the extraction-filtering judge change the r = 0.90 text-judge corroboration?
  • Does the shared signature hold for larger models (>70B), non-English languages, or full fine-tuning/RLHF rather than LoRA-SFT?
  • Could facet-level (30-facet NEO-PI-R) vectors further resolve the sycophancy "mixture" — high affiliative warmth co-occurring with low straightforwardness — that a single domain-level agreeableness direction can only average away?
