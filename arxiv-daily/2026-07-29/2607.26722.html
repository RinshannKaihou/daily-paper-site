Why This Matters

Matters to anyone building self-improving LLM agent harnesses under tight iteration budgets: stale evidence must be re-validated, not silently accumulated.

Critical Edge

The headline agentic-task table pits DREvo's 10-iteration evolved harness against hand-built systems (OpenHands, Terminus-2, Claude Code) that received zero evolution budget at all.

01 Methodology
A
Diagnose. Running Meta-harness (Claude Opus 4.6, 1M-token context) with full- or compressed-history reuse on S2D/USPTO/Law shows accuracy oscillates rather than trending up — historical evidence validity is never reassessed, and evidence is never turned into an actionable direction.
B
Anchor. Code diffs between consecutive harness versions are mapped onto functional-component boundaries (e.g. tool use, memory management) via FuncDiff, producing evidence units with polarity from the trial's performance delta and initial weight 1.0 (single-component edit) or 0.5 (multi-component, lower attribution confidence).
C
Recalibrate. Historical reliability = outcome consistency × freshness (0.9^gap decay); structural compatibility = 1 − AST tree-edit distance between a component's code at evidence-creation time and now. A harmonic-mean-style combine (Eq. 6) means a high score on one axis can't rescue a low score on the other.
D
Distill. Evidence tied to the current failure pattern is split at threshold θ=0.5 into reliable-positive / reliable-negative / low-confidence sets; Algorithm 1 activates one role — Exploit, Avoid, Retest (falls back to Explore on repeat failure), or Explore — as an explicit search intent for the proposer's next harness.
Proposer = Claude Opus 4.6 (1M-token context) for every evolution-based method. Task-execution models: GPT-OSS-120B (domain reasoning), DeepSeek-V4-Flash no-thinking (agentic). Budget: 20 iterations (domain reasoning), 10 (agentic). Agentic harnesses start from Terminus-KIRA — a terminal-oriented, non-coding-specific harness.

Context efficiency (Fig. 3): DREvo keeps proposer context bounded at ~2.9K tokens across 20 iterations, while Meta-harness's context grows past 5.3K tokens with worse oscillation.
Drift robustness (Fig. 4): across 16 settings (4 drift types × 4 benchmarks), blind reuse of un-recalibrated evidence loses 5.3pp on average; DREvo gains 0.6pp and maintains-or-improves in 11/16 settings vs. 2/16 for blind reuse.
Ablation (Table 3): the full Anchor+Recalibrate+Distill system beats Anchor+Distill (no recalibration) by 5.6pp on Terminal-Bench 2.0 full suite — recalibration and distillation are complementary, not redundant.
03 Strengths & Limitations
Strengths
  • Concrete empirical motivation (Fig. 1): both full- and compressed-history Meta-harness genuinely oscillate rather than improve, grounding the problem in evidence, not assumption.
  • Ablation (Table 3) cleanly isolates each component and reveals a non-trivial interaction — distillation without recalibration overfits to easy cases (100% on TB2-Easy) while lagging in aggregate.
  • Drift-robustness stress test (Fig. 4) directly targets the "stale evidence" hypothesis via controlled component perturbation, not just end-to-end scores.
  • Demonstrates cross-domain transfer: agentic evolution starting from a terminal-oriented harness (Terminus-KIRA) reaches competitive SWE-Bench Verified performance.
  • Keeps proposer context bounded (~2.9K tokens) across 20 iterations — a real scalability edge over Meta-harness's growing context.
Limitations
  • No code repository is released, limiting independent reproducibility of the anchoring/recalibration/distillation implementation.
  • The headline agentic table mixes evolved and zero-budget hand-built baselines, muddying the "evolution mechanism quality" claim.
  • Evolution budget (10 vs. 20 iterations) is fixed per task type rather than varied as an experimental axis, despite "limited budget" being central to the framing.
  • Structural compatibility relies on AST tree-edit distance — an admitted proxy for semantic equivalence, with no quantification of how often it misjudges.
  • Abstract (13.8%) and contributions list (14.2%) report slightly different aggregate agentic-task gains — a minor unreconciled inconsistency.
04 Questions for Further Exploration
  • How does DREvo's advantage change as the evolution budget is varied (e.g. 5 vs. 20 vs. 50 iterations) instead of fixed at 10/20?
  • Would a semantic-equivalence check (behavioral testing, embeddings) outperform AST tree-edit distance, and how often does the current proxy diverge from real behavioral compatibility?
  • How sensitive are results to the proposer model choice (Claude Opus 4.6) — would a smaller or different-family LLM proposer benefit proportionally?
  • Can the reliability×compatibility formula (Eq. 6) extend to cross-component evidence whose validity depends on two interacting components jointly?
  • If all agentic-task baselines (OpenHands, Terminus-2, Claude Code) got the same 10-iteration evolution budget with their own proposer mechanisms, what would DREvo's margin look like?
