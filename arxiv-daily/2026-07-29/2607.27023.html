A Bayesian active-selection method automatically sizes model-evaluation subsets, cutting RMSE 20–50% vs. random sampling across 7 benchmarks.

一句话总结：BayesAME 用分桶隐能力的贝叶斯高斯模型对参考模型的历史表现建模，通过信息增益主动选题、双阈值停止准则自动确定评测子集（coreset）大小，在 GPQA、MMLU-Pro 等 7 个基准上，RMSE 相比随机采样均值基线降低约 20%–50%。

BayesAME: Bayesian Active Model Evaluation Cordero Encinar, Cemgil, Doucet, Aglietti, Chiappa · 2026-07-29

Key Metric
−52.8%
Evaluation-weighted integrated RMSE on MMLU-Pro (continuous scores, interpolation): 13.11 vs. RS-Mean baseline 27.80 — the single largest reduction reported in Table 1.
Why This Matters

Reliable LLM benchmarking from a small, automatically-sized item subset — no manual budget guess — gives a practical, honestly-validated baseline for cheaper evaluation pipelines.

Critical Edge

Several baselines (Seq-APW, ProEval, IRT) underperform dramatically in some settings — strengthening BayesAME's relative numbers, but leaving open how much of the gap is real vs. weak baseline tuning.

Methodology

  1. Bucket items by identical reference-model score vectors (tolerance margin N₀=10). Each bucket gets a latent "ability" with a Gaussian prior — covariance shrinks with distance between buckets' reference scores.
  2. Closed-form posterior for the full-benchmark score, switching between a direct Bayesian estimator and a control-variate-style one when a few buckets dominate — avoids one oversized bucket biasing the result.
  3. Active selection via expected information gain (differential-entropy reduction), closed-form under the Gaussian posterior; falls back to random selection when buckets are too imbalanced.
  4. Two-condition stopping rule: recent estimate range < ε₁ AND 95% credible-interval width < ε₂ — the coreset size is discovered, not pre-specified.
  5. Multi-target extension: a linear model of coregionalization (mixed squared-exponential + Matérn-3/2 kernels) shares latent structure across correlated target models, with a 10% random warm start.

Strengths

  • Automatic coreset-size determination via a principled two-condition stopping rule — a genuine gap vs. prior fixed-budget methods.
  • Closed-form posterior and information-gain math keep active selection cheap: O(min(n,B)²) per iteration.
  • Directly tests and refutes a specific skeptical claim from recent literature (Zhang et al., 2025) about beating random-sample-mean baselines.
  • Transparent about negative results — logit-transformed ability model and richer item embeddings both underperformed the simpler approach.
  • Multi-target coregionalization is a principled way to exploit cross-model correlation, not a heuristic bolt-on.

Limitations

  • Assumes scalar per-item scores; doesn't natively handle multi-dimensional or unstructured evaluation formats.
  • Uses a Gaussian ability model with an admittedly improper prior for binary/bounded scores, for tractability.
  • Multi-target extension shows mild negative transfer when target models aren't actually correlated.
  • Margin parameter N₀=10 is a fixed empirical heuristic, not principled or benchmark-specific.
  • No wall-clock or dollar-cost comparison against running the full benchmark — all comparisons are RMSE-only, and no code release is mentioned for independent verification.

Questions for Further Exploration

  • How does BayesAME hold up when reference models are noisy, adversarial, or systematically biased relative to the target model?
  • Would a more principled (non-fixed) N₀ recover further gains on benchmarks with highly imbalanced bucket sizes?
  • Can multi-target coregionalization auto-detect low correlation early and fall back to single-target mode, avoiding wasted iterations?
  • How sensitive are the reported gains to the specific 300-model quantile-stratified sample (Sec. 5) vs. a differently-chosen model pool?
