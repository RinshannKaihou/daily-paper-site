> **一句话总结：** 本文证明了一个精确的离散时间"调度定律"：在尺度不变（归一化）参数块上，有效步长 Φₜ 满足精确递推 Φₜ₊₁ = BₜΦₜ/(1+Φₜ²‖ḡₜ‖²)，其中单一标量 Bₜ 完全刻画学习率与权重衰减的调度压力；Bₜ=1 是收缩/扩张的临界边界，且在可精确求解的归一化线性模型中该平衡点是不稳定螺旋源——恒定学习率加权重衰减必然产生周期性振荡，而通过直接控制 Bₜ（在 CIFAR-10 上 B=1 处准确率峰值，偏离 ±2% 掉点超过 20 个百分点）可以实现因果性的训练控制。

> **TL;DR:** A Purdue team derives an *exact, per-step discrete-time law* for scale-invariant (normalized) parameter blocks trained with SGD + coupled weight decay: the effective directional stepsize obeys Φₜ₊₁ = BₜΦₜ/(1+Φₜ²‖ḡₜ‖²), where the single scalar Bₜ = ηₜ₊₁/(ηₜaₜaₜ₊₁) captures all schedule/decay forcing. The law holds to float64 machine precision (residual < 2×10⁻¹⁵) on an exactly-solved map and to ~10⁻⁶ (float32) on BN-MLP/MNIST, BN-ConvNet/CIFAR-10, and LayerNorm GPT-2 models on WikiText/OpenWebText. In a fully-solved normalized-linear model the interior balance point is proven to be an *unstable spiral source* (eigenvalue modulus √(1+a−a²) > 1), so constant-LR + weight decay cannot maintain stable equilibrium and instead oscillates. A homogeneity exponent ν classifies optimizers (SGD/SGDM: ν=1, quadratic self-quenching; Adam: ν=0, linear), explaining why adaptive methods are more expansion-prone — e.g., SGDM expansion rate is 0.994 when the radial momentum component cₜ>0 vs 0.56 otherwise on CIFAR-10. Interventions that clamp Bₜ ≡ B produce a sharply peaked accuracy curve on CIFAR-10 with maximum at B=1 and >20-point drops for ±2% perturbations, establishing Bₜ as a causal control variable rather than a diagnostic.

## 核心贡献

- **精确离散时间调度定律（定理 2.1）**：对任意可微的尺度不变损失（L(αw)=L(w)），SGD 加耦合权重衰减下有效方向步长 Φₜ = ηₜ/(aₜrₜ²) 满足纯代数的精确递推 Φₜ₊₁ = BₜΦₜ/(1+Φₜ²‖ḡₜ‖²)。无需线性化、连续时间极限或损失凸性假设，对任意时变调度和随机性均成立。
- **单一标量 Bₜ 分离两类效应**：调度压力（学习率变化 × 权重衰减收缩，全部编码在 Bₜ = ηₜ₊₁/[(1−ηₜλₜ)(1−ηₜ₊₁λₜ₊₁)]）与几何自熄灭（范数增长压制有效步长）。Bₜ≤1 时无条件收缩；Bₜ>1 时收缩当且仅当 Φₜ‖ḡₜ‖ ≥ √(Bₜ−1)，给出清晰的收缩/扩张切换面。
- **可解模型中的闭式不稳定性**：在归一化线性模型中，高维动力学精确坍缩为 (对齐度 qₜ, 有效步长 Φₜ) 的二维映射；唯一内部平衡点恰好落在切换面上，且被证明是不稳定螺旋源（雅可比特征值模 √(1+a−a²) > 1），并存在精确的周期二轨道——恒定调度下的周期性行为是离散时间几何的必然结果，而非噪声或近似的人工产物。
- **随机扩展（定理 2.3）**：将精确递推推广到小批量随机优化，证明随机性以零均值鞅形式进入累积有效步长，给出有限时间窗的集中不等式，且随机切换面为 log Bₜ = qₜ（而非 Bₜ=1）。
- **统一优化器分类（齐次优化器框架，定理 4.1–4.3）**：用更新方向的齐次度 ν 对优化器分类——SGD/SGDM 为 ν=1（分母指数 2，二次自熄灭），Adam（ε→0）为 ν=0（分母指数 1，线性自熄灭），从第一性原理解释了自适应方法在归一化下系统性更易扩张的现象；并为 SGDM 揭示了径向放大机制（cₜ>0 时的线性放大通道）。
- **机制验证与因果控制**：跨越精确动力系统、MLP/ConvNet、GPT-2 语言模型验证递推精度；通过合成调度直接钳制 Bₜ≡B，证明性能在预测边界 B=1 处尖峰式最大化，确立 Bₜ 是训练动力学的因果控制坐标。

## Key Contributions

- **Exact discrete-time schedule law (Theorem 2.1):** For any differentiable positively scale-invariant loss (L(αw)=L(w) for α>0) updated by SGD with coupled weight decay, the effective directional stepsize Φₜ := ηₜ/(aₜrₜ²) obeys the purely algebraic recurrence Φₜ₊₁ = BₜΦₜ/(1+Φₜ²‖ḡₜ‖²). No linearization, no continuous-time limit, no convexity assumptions; holds under arbitrary time-varying schedules and stochasticity.
- **One-scalar separation of effects:** Schedule forcing is captured entirely by Bₜ = (ηₜ₊₁/ηₜ)/(aₜaₜ₊₁) with aₜ = 1−ηₜλₜ, while geometric self-quenching enters only through the norm-growth denominator. Sharp boundary: Bₜ ≤ 1 forces unconditional contraction; for Bₜ > 1 contraction holds iff Φₜ‖ḡₜ‖ ≥ √(Bₜ−1) (Proposition 2.2's switching surface).
- **Closed-form instability in a solved model (Theorem 3.4, Props 3.5–3.6):** In a normalized-linear model with isotropic covariance, the dynamics reduce exactly to a 2D map in (alignment qₜ, stepsize Φₜ). The unique interior fixed point lies exactly on the switching surface and is an unstable spiral source with Jacobian eigenvalue modulus √(1+a−a²) > 1 for all a ∈ (0,1), plus an exact period-two orbit on the sphere — recurrence under constant LR + weight decay is a discrete-time geometric inevitability, not a stochastic artifact.
- **Stochastic extension (Theorem 2.3):** Under minibatch noise the log-step evolution decomposes into an exact predictable drift (βₜ − qₜ) minus a martingale with sub-Gaussian finite-horizon concentration; the stochastic switching surface is log Bₜ = qₜ, and the near-critical band depends on state, signal, variance, and batch size.
- **Unified optimizer classification via homogeneity exponent ν (Theorems 4.1–4.3):** Updates whose direction scales as rₜ⁻ᵐ with ν determining the self-quenching exponent (1+ν). SGD/SGDM have ν=1 (quadratic self-quenching, denominator exponent 2); Adam in the ε→0 limit has ν=0 (linear, denominator exponent 1) — a first-principles explanation of why adaptive methods are systematically more expansion-prone under normalization. SGDM additionally exhibits a radial amplification channel via the linear term −2Φₜcₜ.
- **Mechanistic validation and causal control:** The recurrence holds to numerical precision across dynamical systems, BN-MLP/MNIST, BN-ConvNet/CIFAR-10, and LayerNorm GPT-2 on WikiText/OpenWebText; synthesizing schedules that enforce Bₜ ≡ B produces a sharply peaked accuracy curve peaking at the predicted boundary B=1, establishing Bₜ as a control coordinate for schedule design. Code is released.

## Methodology

The paper proceeds in four layers, each building on the previous:

1. **Polar decomposition of scale-invariant blocks.** Write block parameters as wₜ = rₜuₜ with rₜ = ‖wₜ‖ and uₜ on the sphere. Positive scale invariance makes the rescaled gradient ḡ(u) := r∇L(ru) independent of r and tangent to the sphere. SGD with coupled decay wₜ₊₁ = aₜwₜ − ηₜ∇L(wₜ) then splits into a normalized tangent step on the sphere with stepsize Φₜ and a radius equation. Eliminating the radius yields the exact recurrence for Φₜ with schedule factor Bₜ — a two-line algebraic identity whose contraction criterion gives the switching surface √(Φₜ‖ḡₜ‖) vs (Bₜ−1)⁺.

2. **Solved normalized-linear model.** For L(w) = (1/2n)‖A w/√(w⊤Σw) − y‖² with realizable target, the dynamics are first bounded by a schedule-aware finite-horizon certificate (Theorem 3.2) whose error floor depends explicitly on the products {aₜ, ηₜ}. Specializing to isotropic Σ = I collapses the system to an exact 2D map (qₜ, Φₜ), enabling complete eigenvalue analysis: the unique nontrivial fixed point (q⋆ = √(2(1+a))/2... on the switching surface) is an unstable spiral source, and exact period-two orbits exist.

3. **Homogeneous-optimizer framework.** For updates wₜ₊₁ = aₜwₜ − ηₜpₜ with pₜ = rₜ⁻ᵐp̄ₜ, the same derivation gives Ψₜ₊₁ = B̃ₜ⁽ν⁾Ψₜ/‖uₜ − Ψₜp̄ₜ‖¹⁺ᵐ. The exponent ν classifies optimizers: SGDM requires augmenting the state with zₜ = rₜmₜ₊₁ (yielding the denominator decomposition (1−Φₜcₜ)² + Φₜ²‖sₜ‖² that exposes the radial amplification term); Adam closes via scale-free normalized moments at ε = 0, with ε > 0 giving smooth perturbation.

4. **Empirics: from verification to intervention.** Two tracked diagnostics — expansion fraction (fraction of blocks with Φₜ₊₁ > Φₜ) and the recurrence residual (Φₜ₊₁/Φₜ − Bₜ/(1+Φₜ²‖ḡₜ‖²)) — are evaluated on the exact map, on a BN MLP (MNIST) and BN ConvNet (CIFAR-10) with SGD (η=0.5, λ=0.05, 5 seeds), and on 4-block d_model=256 GPT-2 (WikiText) and 12-block d_model=768 GPT-2 (OpenWebText) with affine-free LayerNorm (10k steps, batch 32). The causal experiment inverts the law: given fixed λ and target B, synthesize ηₜ₊₁ = Bηₜ(1−ληₜ)/(1 + λBηₜ(1−ληₜ)) to clamp Bₜ ≡ B, then sweep B. A final exponent-scan fits the empirical denominator exponent to separate ν=1 from ν=0 optimizers, and an ε-continuity study tracks Adam's residual as ε shrinks.

## Results & Highlights

- **The law is exact to machine precision.** On the exactly-solved isotropic map under constant/step-decay/cosine schedules, the recurrence residual stays below 2×10⁻¹⁵ (float64); on real networks the residual sits at ~10⁻⁶ (float32) for BN-MLP/MNIST and BN-ConvNet/CIFAR-10, and the median ratio residual is 1.19×10⁻⁷ (worst-case median < 6×10⁻⁷) for SGD/SGDM on the GPT-2/WikiText and GPT-2/OpenWebText models — i.e., ~8 orders of magnitude tighter than typical approximate analyses.
- **Bₜ causally controls performance (CIFAR-10, BN-ConvNet).** Clamping Bₜ ≡ B and sweeping B yields accuracy maximized exactly at the predicted boundary B = 1; a ±2% perturbation of B causes a >20 percentage-point accuracy drop (subcritical B < 1 collapses the effective stepsize; supercritical B > 1 destabilizes training) — evidence of a sharply peaked causal relationship, not correlation.
- **SGDM radial amplification confirmed (3-block BN-ConvNet, CIFAR-10, 10k steps).** The augmented-state contraction condition matches observed behavior on >99.998% of steps with residuals at 1.2×10⁻⁷; conditioning on the radial momentum component, the expansion rate is 0.994 for cₜ > 0 versus 0.56 for cₜ ≤ 0 — a near-doubling that directly confirms the predicted linear amplification channel.
- **Bₜ organizes expansion in transformers.** On the LayerNorm GPT-2 models, constant/step schedules sustain SGD/SGDM expansion fractions of 0.999–1.000 while cosine decay drives them to ~10⁻⁴ after warmup (the predicted Bₜ<1 contraction regime); coupled Adam shows the intermediate ν=0 profile with expansion fractions 0.44–0.49 under constant/step, suppressed to 0.033 on WikiText and 0.095 on OpenWebText under cosine.
- **ν-dichotomy is directly observable.** An empirical denominator-exponent scan cleanly separates ν = 1 (SGD/SGDM, best-fit ≈ 2) from ν = 0 (Adam variants, best-fit ≈ 1), and Adam's recurrence residual decreases smoothly from ~10⁻⁶ to ~10⁻¹² as ε → 0, validating the perturbative treatment of the scale-breaking term.

## Authors & Credibility

All three authors — Hasan Amin, Wei-Kai Chang, and Rajiv Khanna — are at Purdue University (the paper does not state a specific venue; it is an arXiv preprint, v1, 8 Sep 2026). The work is in the vein of the "edge of stability"/normalization-dynamics theory literature (it cites and positions against Arora-Li-Lyu's auto rate-tuning analysis, Li-Arora-Lyu-Li-Ma scale-invariant dynamics, Cohen et al.'s edge of stability, d'Angelo et al. on weight decay, and Defazio's late-stage gradient growth). Code is publicly released (github.com/shasanamin/normalized-optimization-dynamics), and the claims are framed with unusual care — the abstract's strong statements ("exact", "high precision") are backed by residual measurements and an explicit limitations section delineating exactly which parameter blocks the theorem covers. The theory–experiment loop (derive exact law → verify to machine precision → intervene) follows good scientific practice.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** The exact per-step recurrence is genuinely new relative to prior asymptotic/equilibrium analyses of scale-invariant training (which typically assume constant schedules and steady-state effective LR). The coincidence that the solved model's fixed point lies *exactly* on the switching surface, and the spiral-source instability proof, are elegant and non-obvious. The ν-homogeneity classifier is a clean unification, though the observation that Adam's normalized update is scale-free (ν=0) is individually close to known folklore.
- **Rigor:** The core identity is algebraic and hard to dispute; the stochastic extension uses standard martingale concentration. However, the *global* instability claim rests entirely on the isotropic (Σ = I) normalized-linear model — the paper itself concedes it "does not prove global convergence to a limit cycle, nor extend the result to anisotropic covariance or general deep networks." The anisotropic case (realistic data covariance) is where the 2D reduction fails, so the headline "constant LR + weight decay cannot be stable" is proven only for a toy model plus the per-block local law.
- **Reproducibility:** Strong — code released, 5 seeds for neural experiments, explicit hyperparameters (η=0.5, λ=0.05), and residuals quoted per setting.
- **Significance caveats:** The block-conditional nature is a real restriction the authors are honest about: for standard pre-LayerNorm GPT/LLaMA, embeddings, biases, LN affine parameters, LM heads, and typical attention/MLP projections are *not individually covered* by the theorem; the GPT-2 experiments deliberately use **affine-free LayerNorm** to restore exact scale invariance, so they are stress tests of a modified architecture rather than theorem-level coverage of real Transformer training. The causal control result (peak at B=1) is demonstrated only on a small BN-ConvNet/CIFAR-10; no large-scale LM perplexity results connect Bₜ control to practical gains. The theory also covers only *coupled* weight decay — AdamW's decoupled form is flagged as lacking a closed-form extension, which limits applicability to the most common modern training recipe (AdamW + decoupled decay).
- **Red flags:** Minor overreach in the abstract's framing "Across dynamical systems and neural networks (MLP, CNN, GPT2 / MNIST, CIFAR, wikiText, OpenWebText)" — the neural models are small (4–12 blocks, ≤10k steps), and the connection to final *performance* (not just dynamics diagnostics) rests on a single CIFAR-10 figure. The >20-point drop for ±2% B perturbation is striking but suggests an extremely brittle optimum that practitioners using standard schedules never sit exactly at B=1 anyway, so the practical prescriptive value ("tune B") remains to be demonstrated at scale.

## Strengths

- The central law is *exact* rather than asymptotic — verified to float64/float32 precision, which is rare in training-dynamics theory and makes falsification trivial (and it survives).
- Clean conceptual separation (schedule forcing Bₜ vs geometric self-quenching) that unifies constant/step/cosine schedules as structured trajectories of one scalar.
- The instability result dissolves a folklore question: recurrence/oscillation under constant LR + weight decay is a discrete-time geometric property, not a noise or step-size artifact.
- The ν-framework gives a crisp, mechanistic answer to "why is Adam more expansion-prone under normalization" (linear vs quadratic self-quenching plus preconditioner-induced radial components).
- The intervention experiment (clamping Bₜ by synthesizing the LR schedule) elevates the claim from correlation to causation.
- Honest, detailed limitations section and public code.

## Limitations

- Global dynamics analysis is restricted to the isotropic normalized-linear model; anisotropic covariance and general deep networks are not covered, and convergence to a limit cycle is not proven.
- The exact law is block-conditional: it requires exact positive scale invariance of the block. In standard pre-LN GPT/LLaMA, embeddings, biases, LN affine parameters, LM heads, and typical attention/MLP projections are not individually scale-invariant; the transformer experiments use affine-free LayerNorm specifically to manufacture exact invariance.
- The optimizer extension covers coupled weight decay; decoupled decay (the AdamW default in modern practice) has no closed-form law yet.
- Adam's exact law holds at ε = 0; real runs use ε ≈ 10⁻⁸ (treated perturbatively, with empirical continuity checks).
- Empirical scale is small: MNIST/CIFAR-10 MLPs/ConvNets, 4-block (d=256) and 12-block (d=768) GPT-2 for 10k steps; no large-scale LLM or modern architecture validation, and no demonstration that Bₜ-based schedule design beats standard warmup-cosine recipes end-to-end.
- The stochastic extension characterizes drift and concentration but does not alter the practical recipe beyond the noise-shifted switching surface log Bₜ = qₜ.

## Relevance

对研究学习率调度、权重衰减与归一化网络训练动力学的工作有直接参考价值：本文给出的精确单标量定律 Bₜ 可作为分析 Muon/AdamW 等优化器行为、解释训练后期梯度增长与振荡现象的第一性原理工具。若你的兴趣在于优化理论与 LLM 训练稳定性（如预热与余弦退火的机理），这篇是必读。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Can the exact law be extended to *decoupled* weight decay (AdamW), where the shrinkage is applied outside the gradient coupling and no closed form currently exists?
- How does the switching surface shift in anisotropic or layerwise-normalized settings (e.g., spectral/weight-normalized blocks, Query-Key RMSNorm in modern LLMs), and does the spiral instability persist?
- Does Bₜ-based schedule synthesis (targeting a constant or shaped Bₜ trajectory) improve final perplexity over warmup-cosine at LLM scale, or is the B=1 optimum an artifact of small CIFAR-10 models?
- Can the ν-framework predict the expansion behavior of newer optimizers (Lion, Muon, Shampoo/SOAP, SOAP-style preconditioners) a priori from their update homogeneity?
- How does the radial amplification mechanism in SGDM interact with warmup — does warmup partly work by keeping cₜ small during the transient norm-growth phase?
- Does the noise-induced shift of the switching surface (log Bₜ = qₜ) explain empirical observations that larger batch sizes tolerate larger constant learning rates in normalized networks?
