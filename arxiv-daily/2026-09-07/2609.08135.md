> **一句话总结：** 从三条标准噪声假设出发推导出量化矩阵乘的统一二阶噪声泛函——整数格点代入常数方差轮廓、浮点格点代入乘性方差轮廓后，FP 噪声的数据依赖坍缩为单一标量"参与因子 κ"，得到闭式信噪比定律 SNR_dB = 10·log₁₀κ + C_fmt 以及任何保函数线性变换都不可逾越的闭式上限 κ*；据此提出 KBBQ，用一个"刹车"参数 λ 在谱完全展平（WUSH）与不展平之间插值：在 W4A4、4 个基座模型、2 种 FP4 格式上，RTN-KBBQ 相对恒等基线提升 0.5~7.1 分，8 组模型-格式对中 7 组超过 SOTA 的 RTN-WUSH（如 Qwen3-4B-Base/NVFP4 五任务平均 66.44、恢复率 94.0% vs WUSH 64.51、91.3%），且理论最优端点 λ=1 并非实际最优点（λ=0.75 最优，高出 1.93 分）—— Deployed 量化器违反推导假设，"距上限走多远"本身应成为可调参数。

> **TL;DR:** From three standard noise assumptions (unbiased, uncorrelated, elementwise variance profile) the authors derive one noise functional for quantized dot products in which the number format enters only through a variance profile: the constant profile recovers integer theory (CAT), the multiplicative profile φ(a)=σ²·a² of floating-point grids yields a closed-form law SNR_dB = 10·log₁₀κ + C_fmt, where κ (the "participation factor", named after Thouless localization) is the only data-dependent term and is provably invariant under diagonal rescaling — explaining why SmoothQuant-style migrations and data-free Haar rotations help INT but not FP4 (trained networks already sit 0.012–0.071 dex above the Haar attractor κ̄_rot; on Qwen3-4B-Base/NVFP4 a dense Haar rotation scores 61.54 five-task average vs 63.85 for doing nothing). They also prove a per-block ceiling κ* = D·Tr(Σ²)/(TrΣ)² that no function-preserving linear transform can exceed and that the prior SOTA (WUSH, Chen et al. 2026) attains — but deployed quantizers violate the assumptions, so KBBQ exposes "distance traveled toward the ceiling" as a free parameter λ: on the MMLU/GSM8K/HellaSwag/WinoGrande/MBPP five-task average at W4A4, RTN-KBBQ beats the identity baseline on all 8 model-format pairs by +0.5 (Llama-3-8B/NVFP4) to +7.1 points (Qwen3-8B/MXFP4: 69.12 vs 62.01), beats RTN-WUSH on 7 of 8 (Qwen3-4B/NVFP4: 66.44 / 94.0% recovery vs 64.51 / 91.3%, with GSM8K 74.83 vs 69.07), and the brake ablation shows the practical optimum is interior (λ=0.75, +1.93 pts over the λ=1 WUSH endpoint) while the idealized objective κ(λ)/κ* still climbs monotonically to 1.00 — all with zero added deployment-time cost (same kernel, storage, and arithmetic as WUSH).

## 核心贡献

- **格式无关的量化噪声泛函**：仅由三条标准假设（无偏 A1、误差不相关 A2、方差函数 A3）推出量化点积的期望噪声能量 E[δS²] = Σₖ E[xₖ²]φ_w(wₖ) + E[wₖ²]E[φ_x(xₖ)]，数格式只通过逐元素方差轮廓 φ 进入：整数格点代入常数轮廓 Δ²/12 精确恢复 CAT 整数理论（Federici et al. 2026），浮点格点代入乘性轮廓 σ²_fmt·a² 恢复 WUSH 浮点理论（Chen et al. 2026）——把两支先前独立的理论统一为一个泛函的两种代入。
- **闭式 SNR 定律与参与因子 κ**：FP 情形下噪声只依赖逐元素乘积的二阶矩，信噪比定律为 SNR_dB = 10·log₁₀κ + C_fmt，其中 κ = E[S²]/ΣₖE[(wₖxₖ)²] ∈ [0, D]（借用量子局域化文献的 participation factor 命名）承载全部网络依赖，C_fmt 承载全部格式依赖；在 6 个模型族（GPT-2 124M 到 Qwen3-32B）、两种格式（per-tensor E4M3、block-16 E2M1）上逐 GEMM 单元验证成立。
- **闭式变换上限 κ\* 及其达到者刻画**：证明任何保函数线性变换的集成参与因子满足 κ̄(T) ≤ κ* = D·Tr(Σ²)/(TrΣ)²，达到者恰为 T = diag(s)·R（R 正交且 (RΣRᵀ)ₖₖ = Tr(Σ)/D）；WUSH 构造在匹配集成下恰好达到该上限。由此严格解释格式依赖的变换行为：对角缩放对 κ 完全"盲"（Proposition 6），一般随机旋转只把 κ 拉向 Haar 吸引子 κ̄_rot，而训练好的网络本就位于吸引子之上（中位数高 0.012–0.071 dex）——因此旋转对 INT 可靠有效、对 FP4 弱有效甚至有害。
- **KBBQ（Kappa-Braked Blockwise Quantization）**：把 WUSH 的展平指数换成自由参数 λ ∈ [0,1]，T(λ) = H·Λ^(−λ/4)·Uᵀ·W′ᵀ 在"不展平"与"完全展平（达到 κ*）"之间线性收缩对数谱；变换块与量化组对齐（NVFP4 为 16、MXFP4 为 32），相对 Chen et al. (2026) 不增加任何部署时开销（核、存储、推理算术完全相同）。
- **"理想最优 ≠ 实际最优"的实证发现**：理论目标 κ(λ)/κ* 随 λ 单调升至 1.00，但两个被扫描模型的精度都在 λ=1 之前封顶回落——说明部署量化器（确定性舍入、组内共享 scale、有限校准样本）确实偏离 A1–A3，把这层失配变成一个可调旋钮比硬套上限更好。

## Key Contributions

- **A format-agnostic noise functional.** A single expected-noise expression for quantized matrix multiplication is derived from three classical assumptions (A1 unbiasedness, A2 uncorrelated errors, A3 elementwise variance function); substituting the constant profile φ = Δ²/12 recovers the integer theory of CAT (Federici et al., 2026) and the multiplicative profile φ(a) = σ²_fmt·a² recovers the floating-point theory of WUSH (Chen et al., 2026), unifying both as two variance profiles of one functional.
- **A closed-form SNR law with a single data-dependent scalar.** For FP grids the noise depends only on second moments of elementwise products, giving SNR_dB = 10·log₁₀κ + C_fmt where the participation factor κ = E[S²]/ΣₖE[(wₖxₖ)²] ∈ [0, D] (named after the analogous quantity in Anderson-localization theory) carries all network dependence and C_fmt all format dependence; the law is verified per GEMM cell on six model families from GPT-2 124M to Qwen3-32B in per-tensor E4M3 and block-16 E2M1.
- **A provable transform ceiling with a characterized attainer class.** The ensemble participation factor satisfies κ̄(T) ≤ κ* = D·Tr(Σ²)/(TrΣ)² for every invertible function-preserving transform, with equality exactly on T = diag(s)·R with (RΣRᵀ)ₖₖ = Tr(Σ)/D (a randomized Hadamard attains this in expectation); WUSH's construction is shown to lie in this class. This cleanly explains format-dependent transform behavior: diagonal rescaling is provably blind for FP noise (κ invariant), and a generic rotation only pulls κ toward a Haar attractor that trained networks already exceed — hence rotations reliably help INT but are weak or harmful for FP4.
- **KBBQ (Kappa-Braked Blockwise Quantization).** Replacing WUSH's fixed flattening exponent with a free brake λ ∈ [0,1] yields a one-parameter family T(λ) = H·Λ^(−λ/4)·Uᵀ·W′ᵀ that linearly contracts the log spectrum between untouched and fully flattened; the transform block is matched to the quantization group (16 for NVFP4, 32 for MXFP4) and carries zero additional deployment-time cost relative to Chen et al. (2026) — same shape, storage layout, kernel, and inference arithmetic.
- **An empirically demonstrated theory-practice gap.** The idealized objective κ(λ)/κ* is monotone to 1.00 at λ = 1, yet accuracy peaks at interior λ on both swept models — evidence that deterministic rounding, shared block scales, and finite calibration violate A1–A3, and that the distance traveled toward the ceiling should itself be a tunable parameter rather than maximized.

## Methodology

The paper proceeds in three layers — theory, diagnosis, then method.

**Layer 1: the noise law (Section 2).** For one dot product S = Σₖ wₖxₖ with quantized operands ŵₖ = wₖ + δwₖ, x̂ₖ = xₖ + δxₖ, a first-order expansion gives δS = Σₖ (xₖδwₖ + wₖδxₖ) + O(δ²). Under A1–A3, all cross terms vanish (each elimination is traced to a specific assumption in Appendix C.1, making failure modes diagnosable) leaving E[δS²] = Σₖ [E[xₖ²]φ_w(wₖ) + E[wₖ²]E[φ_x(xₖ)]]. Substituting the integer profile φ = Δ²/12 (uniform grid, Widrow quantization theory) yields noise governed by grid steps, hence by group amax — one outlier coarsens the grid for everyone, which is why amplitude-flattening rotations help INT. Substituting the FP profile φ(a) = σ²_fmt·a² (step doubles at each exponent band; σ²_fmt derived per format in Appendix C.3 with a log-uniform within-band average E[1/m²] = 3/(8 ln 2)) collapses the data dependence to ΣₖE[(wₖxₖ)²], giving the law SNR_dB = 10·log₁₀κ + C_fmt. The law is measured per GEMM cell (the four fused GEMMs of a transformer block, activations captured from a full-precision forward pass, both operands rounded, error accumulated against the unquantized product — isolating cell-level quantization noise from upstream error).

**Layer 2: what transforms can and cannot do (Sections 2.4, 3).** The numerator E[S²] of κ is invariant under every invertible function-preserving transform (Lemma 1). Diagonal scaling leaves every elementwise product unchanged, so κ — and with it FP SNR — is invariant (Proposition 6, "diagonal blindness"): SmoothQuant/AWQ-style migrations between operands can only help INT formats. Orthogonal rotations move κ through the diagonal of RΣRᵀ; the ratio tr(S²)/(tr S)² of the WUSH analysis is identified with the ceiling κ* = D·Tr(Σ²)/(TrΣ)² (Theorem 1, proved by a chain of Cauchy–Schwarz steps plus Schur–Horn for existence), with the blockwise corollary at the quantization-group size. Meanwhile a random (Haar) rotation resets κ to a fixed attractor κ̄_rot ≈ 1.26–1.35, and the four benchmarked models' native κ_I = 1.55–1.77 already exceeds it — so a data-free rotation has nothing to gain, matching the observed HAAR < identity result on FP4.

**Layer 3: KBBQ (Section 4).** WUSH attains κ* by fully inverting the square root of the paired spectrum (Λ^(−1/4) in eigenvalue coordinates) but presumes A1–A3 and the population Σ. KBBQ keeps the basis change and root factors but raises the flattening exponent to a free parameter: T(λ) = H·Λ^(−λ/4)·Uᵀ·W′ᵀ, λ ∈ [0,1], contracting log sᵢ → (1−λ)·log sᵢ. The brake acts only on the numerical entries of the block-diagonal transform at fit time; deployment is bit-identical to WUSH. Experiments (Section 5) evaluate W4A4 on Llama-3.2-3B-Base, Llama-3-8B-Base, Qwen3-4B-Base, Qwen3-8B-Base under NVFP4 (native on GB200 via TransformerEngine) and MXFP4 (emulated/fake quantization, following Chen et al. 2026), against identity (RTN-I), dense data-free Haar (HAAR), WUSH at λ=1 (RTN-WUSH), plus GPTQ, MR-GPTQ, GPTQ-WUSH, GPTQ-KBBQ combinations; five benchmarks (MMLU 5-shot, GSM8K 8-shot CoT, HellaSwag, WinoGrande 5-shot, MBPP). Section 6 ablates the brake λ, calibration domain (default / GSM8K train / random tokens at n=1536), and calibration budget (n = 256 → 65,536).

## Results & Highlights

- **RTN-KBBQ beats the identity baseline on all 8 model-format pairs and RTN-WUSH on 7 of 8**, with average-accuracy gains over identity from +0.5 points (Llama-3-8B-Base, NVFP4) to +7.1 points (Qwen3-8B-Base, MXFP4: 69.12 vs 62.01 five-task average, i.e. 93.2% vs 83.6% BF16 recovery); on Qwen3-8B-Base/NVFP4 RTN-KBBQ reaches 71.54 (96.4% recovery) vs 70.73 (95.3%) for RTN-WUSH. The single RTN loss is Llama-3-8B-Base/MXFP4 (53.35 vs WUSH's 53.61, −0.26).
- **Brake ablation (Qwen3-4B-Base, NVFP4, RTN; Table 3): the optimum is interior.** Five-task average is 40.42 (57.2% recovery, collapse) at λ=0, 65.22 at λ=0.25, 65.61 at λ=0.50, **66.44 (94.0%) at λ=0.75 — the optimum — and 64.51 (91.3%) at the unbraked λ=1**, i.e. +1.93 points over the theoretical-ceiling endpoint, while the idealized objective κ(λ)/κ* still climbs monotonically from 0.73 to 1.00; every interior grid point beats λ=1, so the finding is not a grid artifact. Per-model optima differ (λ=0.75 on Qwen3-4B-Base, λ=0.50 on Llama-3.2-3B-Base).
- **The noise law holds with parameter-free accuracy (Tables 1/5, Figure 1).** Across the six families the per-tensor E4M3 intercept has median 28.42 dB with a cross-family standard deviation of only 0.10 dB (full range 0.27 dB), against a parameter-free log-uniform prediction of 28.51 dB — **0.09 dB agreement** — with per-cell residual RMS of 0.78–1.29 dB; the FP8(E4M3)→FP4(E2M1) spacing is predicted from mantissa arithmetic alone as 6.02×(3−1) = 12.04 dB vs a measured median of 13.24 dB over 3,968 records, the 1.2 dB excess being consistent with the confounded scaling granularity (per-tensor vs block-16).
- **Data-free rotations are demonstrably harmful at FP4:** on Qwen3-4B-Base/NVFP4 the dense Haar rotation scores 61.54 five-task average (87.1% recovery) vs 63.85 (90.3%) for the identity — matching the theory that native κ (1.611 on Qwen3-4B) already exceeds the Haar attractor κ̄_rot (1.301); Table 1 shows median cells sit 0.012–0.071 dex above the attractor on all four benchmarked models.
- **Largest per-task effect on generation benchmarks:** on GSM8K (Qwen3-4B-Base, NVFP4, RTN) KBBQ scores 74.83 vs 69.07 for RTN-WUSH (+5.76) and 74.83 vs 58.00 for identity at MXFP4 RTN-KBBQ is 70.58 vs 58.00 (+12.58); with GPTQ compensation on Llama-3.2-3B-Base/NVFP4, GPTQ-KBBQ averages 47.65 (95.3% recovery) vs 46.39 (92.7%) for GPTQ-WUSH and 45.40 (90.8%) for plain GPTQ.
- **Calibration robustness (Table 4):** with the calibration budget varied 256-fold (n = 256 → 65,536 tokens) the KBBQ five-task average stays within 0.3 points (WUSH within 0.6); the GSM8K margin over WUSH survives every budget (6.2 points at n=256) and every calibration domain (5.8 / 1.8 / 4.0 points on default / GSM8K / random-token corpora) — evidence the operating point is set by layer geometry rather than calibration data.

## Authors & Credibility

The three authors — Lexington Whalen, Yuki Ito, and Ryo Sakamoto — are at SB Intuitions (Tokyo, Japan), an industrial LLM lab; Whalen is the corresponding author. This is a preprint dated September 9, 2026 (arXiv v1, 8 Sep 2026), so it has not yet passed peer review. Credibility signals are strong for a preprint: the mathematical claims are backed by full proofs in the appendix (including a careful accounting of which assumption kills which correction term, and an explicit bound on the single approximation — the dropped fourth-order diagonal displaces predicted SNR by at most 0.024 dB at FP4, below the measured residual RMS); the central law is validated against a parameter-free prediction rather than a fit; and the paper is unusually forthright about where its assumptions fail in deployment, turning that gap into its main method rather than hiding it. The benchmarking harness is the authors' own, and NVFP4 numbers are generated natively on GB200 hardware, which lends the W4A4 numbers practical weight; MXFP4 numbers are emulated.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** Genuinely good. Unifying CAT (integer) and WUSH (floating-point) noise theories as two variance profiles of one functional is clean and new, and importing the participation factor from localization physics gives FP quantization analysis a quantity with a provable, characterized ceiling. The observation that the WUSH endpoint is not the practical optimum is a crisp, useful negative result.
- **Rigor:** High on the theory side (proofs, parameter-free intercept confirmation, honest truncation bounds). Weaker on the empirical side: results appear to be single-run with no seed variance or error bars, and the paper's own calibration-budget table shows GSM8K/MBPP moving 1.8–3.4 points across settings — the same order as several reported margins (e.g. the −0.26 RTN loss on Llama-3-8B/MXFP4, or the +0.5-point Llama-3-8B gain), so some wins may be within noise.
- **Reproducibility:** Method is fully specified (blockwise transform matched to quantization group, λ explicit, calibration protocol reported) and the construction is a small delta on the published WUSH method, so reproduction should be straightforward; however, no code release is mentioned in the paper, and the λ sweep is performed on the same five benchmarks used for headline reporting — a mild eval-set selection risk, since λ* is chosen per model (0.75 vs 0.50) with no independent predictor or held-out validation of the choice.
- **Significance:** The diagnostic message ("spectrum flattening has limits; distance-to-ceiling is the right knob") matters for anyone deploying FP4 rotations, but the headline gains are uneven: at 8B/NVFP4 the improvements thin out — Llama-3-8B gains only +0.5 RTN points, and on Qwen3-8B/NVFP4 the braked variant is the weakest of the compensated options (GPTQ-KBBQ 71.59, 96.5% recovery, below plain GPTQ at 71.81, 96.8%, and GPTQ-WUSH at 72.04, 97.1%) — the transform family adds little exactly where the hardware format is easiest. The 7.1-point gain is on emulated MXFP4, the harder and less deployment-native setting, and accuracy experiments stop at 8B even though the noise-law verification reaches 32B.
- **Red flags:** The baseline set omits the FP4-specific rotation methods the introduction itself cites as motivation (TORQ for MXFP4, HARP, ConQuR); comparisons are limited to HAAR, WUSH, GPTQ, and MR-GPTQ. The "no additional deployment-time computation" claim is argued structurally (identical transform shape/layout to WUSH) but no throughput or latency measurements are presented. Benchmark coverage is five classic multiple-choice/short-generation tasks — no perplexity, no long-context, no agentic or open-ended generation.

## Strengths

- A single, simple functional that provably reduces to both prior INT (CAT) and prior FP (WUSH) theories, with the format entering through one object (the variance profile) — conceptually satisfying and pedagogically clear.
- The theory makes a parameter-free numerical prediction (E4M3 intercept 28.51 dB) confirmed to 0.09 dB across six model families spanning 124M–32B, and a mantissa-only cross-format spacing prediction (12.04 dB) confirmed within 1.2 dB — rare falsifiability for a quantization theory paper.
- Delivers a rigorous explanation for a practical confusion: why rotations that are standard for INT4 reliably disappoint at FP4 (diagonal blindness plus the Haar attractor already exceeded by trained networks), backed by measurement (native κ vs κ̄_rot in Table 1).
- The brake is an honest response to the theory-practice gap rather than a patch: one interpretable parameter, zero deployment cost, and the interior-optimum finding (λ=0.75 > λ=1 by 1.93 points) is itself evidence for the paper's thesis about assumption violations.
- Strong robustness story: the operating point barely moves over a 256-fold calibration-budget range and across radically different calibration domains, including random tokens — the method depends on layer geometry, not calibration data.

## Limitations

- The brake λ must be tuned per model by sweeping evaluation accuracy — the paper offers no closed-form or heuristic predictor of λ*, and the sweep doubles as the reported evaluation, with per-model optima (0.75 for Qwen3-4B vs 0.50 for Llama-3.2-3B) that currently look model-specific.
- W4A4 accuracy results cover only four base models up to 8B parameters; the 32B model appears only in the per-GEMM noise-law fits, so scalability of the accuracy claims is untested.
- MXFP4 results use emulated ("fake") quantization rather than native hardware, and only NVFP4 runs natively on GB200; the largest advertised gains (+7.1 points) sit in the emulated setting.
- No comparison against the recent FP4-targeted rotations named in the introduction (TORQ, HARP, ConQuR), so KBBQ's position against the actual FP4-transform frontier is only established via WUSH.
- Single-run results without error bars or statistical significance; generation benchmarks (GSM8K, MBPP) fluctuate by 1.8–3.4 points across calibration budgets in the paper's own ablation, comparable to several reported inter-method margins.
- Evaluation is limited to five standard benchmarks with no perplexity, long-context, or generation-quality tasks, and no throughput/latency measurements to substantiate the zero-cost deployment claim empirically.
- The theory is second-order: the derivation drops the fourth-order diagonal term (bounded at ≤0.024 dB, admittedly negligible) and assumes unbiased/uncorrelated rounding that deterministic RTN with shared block scales provably violates — the very gap KBBQ papers over rather than eliminates.

## Relevance

对关注 LLM 高效推理与低比特量化的方向直接命中：本文给出 FP4 量化噪声的可预测闭式理论（解释了旋转/缩放类变换在 INT 与 FP 格式下行为分裂的根本原因），并落地为零额外推理开销的 W4A4 后训练量化方法，与权重/激活联合量化、旋转预处理（QuaRot/SpinQuant/QuIP# 谱系）及 FP4 硬件部署等议题高度相关，其"理论最优端点非实际最优"的发现对所有做谱展平/自适应变换的研究都有方法论警示意义。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Can the interior optimum λ* be predicted from measurable layer statistics (blockwise spectrum condition number, calibration sample count, scale-group size) instead of being swept on evaluation benchmarks — and does a held-out-task sweep confirm that the chosen λ generalizes?
- Does the brake interact differently with stochastic or randomized rounding at deployment: if A1 were restored by hardware-level stochastic rounding, would the optimum move back toward λ = 1 as the theory predicts?
- How does KBBQ behave at larger scale (30B+) where the noise law was verified but accuracy was not measured, and under long-context, perplexity, or open-ended generation workloads rather than five multiple-choice/short-generation benchmarks?
- Does the same participation-factor analysis extend to other microscaling formats (FP8 block-scaled, FP6, FP3) and to weight-only W4 or optimizer-state quantization, where the multiplicative profile also applies?
- What is the clean decomposition of the 1.2 dB cross-format intercept excess — a matched-granularity experiment (per-tensor vs block-scaled at equal mantissa width) would separate the scaling-granularity offset from higher-order effects.
- Since plain GPTQ already matches or beats transform+GPTQ on the easiest setting (Qwen3-8B/NVFP4), is there a cheap diagnostic — e.g. native κ or κ* − κ gap per layer — that predicts when a transform is worth applying at all, enabling per-layer selective braking?
