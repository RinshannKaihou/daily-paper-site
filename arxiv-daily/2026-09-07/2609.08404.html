01

Methodology

方法

The environment is formalized as a goal-conditioned POMDP; enrichment is an intervention on the observation space, o⁺ = E(o, h) — identical states, actions, transitions, and rewards, only the feedback channel grows. This implicitly reshapes the agent's action distribution, varying effective task difficulty and encouraging trajectory diversity. Training follows the group-relative RL paradigm (trajectory-level binary reward, group advantages on agent tokens, environment tokens masked).

AG-Early → OE-Late· hybrid beats every fixed variant

A factorial pilot on SciWorld (Qwen3-4B-Thinking, GRPO, 200 steps, stochastic enrichment p=0.5, strictly disjoint evaluation) varies what is injected against when. Explicit action guidance prunes invalid branches early; observation enrichment adds semantics once the policy can interpret them. The winning hybrid is then scaled to 6 model × algorithm combinations, benchmarked against GPT-5.4, Kimi-K2-Thinking, and Qwen3-235B-Thinking ceilings.

AGAction guidance — concrete valid actions (SciWorld) / suggested actions appended to the user query (BFCL).
OEObservation enrichment — real-time task-progress checklists (SciWorld) / expanded raw tool returns (BFCL).
FEEFeedback-Enriched Environment — the training environment built from the AG→OE curriculum.

03

Strengths

优势
  • Clean problem framing — reward sparsity attacked from the environment side, orthogonal to the dominant SFT warm-up recipe.
  • Factorial pilot, validated at scale — the what × when ablation concludes AG-early / OE-late and survives the 2-benchmark × 2-scale × 3-algorithm grid rather than being asserted.
  • Mechanistic depth — entropy stabilization, tiered-difficulty transfer, a controlled weight-level internalization probe, and the intra-group consistency boundary are each actionable design guidance.
  • Unusual candor — negative cells, Miss Func / Miss Param failure modes, and the AppWorld null result are reported and analyzed in the main text.
04

Limitations

局限
  • Hand-authored enrichment — AG/OE content, phase boundaries, and the p=0.5 injection rate are specified per benchmark; sensitivity unexplored (self-acknowledged).
  • Narrow coverage — only SciWorld and BFCL; AppWorld's extreme sparsity remains unsolved (still zero-reward trajectories).
  • Modest, uneven gains — +2.82% average with several flat or regressing cells, especially where the agent should clarify rather than execute.
  • Peak-score reporting — every-5-step best results rather than final checkpoints, plus no significance testing, weaken cross-cell comparisons.
05

Questions for Further Exploration

开放问题
  1. Can AG/OE enrichment be auto-generated (teacher LLM or the agent itself) instead of hand-authored — and keep the gains?
  2. Is the AG→OE switch point (fixed at step 100) sensitive to horizon, reward density, and scale — would an adaptive schedule dominate?
  3. Does internalization hold for stronger backbones (32B+), or does enrichment become redundant?
  4. Can the Miss Func / Miss Param regression be fixed via clarification-demanding training episodes, turning over-compliance into learned disambiguation?
  5. For AppWorld-level sparsity, what richer interventions (reward shaping, subgoal decomposition, synthetic observations) would work where OE provably fails?
