> **一句话总结：** 本文（RIKEN 高性能计算权威 Satoshi Matsuoka 单作者）不改动 Ozaki II 的数学，专门工程化 FP8 Ozaki II fp64 仿真乘法中最被忽视的"解构（deconstruction）"路径——把 fp64 操作数转换为 CRT 残差平面的开销；论文导出闭式性能模型：在 NVIDIA Rubin 上大矩阵 DGEMM 被 235 TFLOPS 的解构地板卡住（恰为 473 TFLOPS 算术屋顶的一半，该 0.50 是三个设计整数的推导比值而非拟合效率），而提出的"转换一次 + 双肢整数张量归约 + ≈6.3 条 SIMT 残差指令"的 Ozaki 2.5 方法把尺寸交叉点从 ≈1211 移到 ≈480–730，n̄=512 处建模吞吐从 ≈200 提升到 364–438 TFLOPS（条件性 1.8–2.2×）——但注意：全文没有任何新内核被实测，所有性能数字都是"条件性简化模型投影"。

> **TL;DR:** A single-author modeling paper from RIKEN's Satoshi Matsuoka that engineers the previously-ignored "deconstruction" path (converting fp64 operands to CRT residue planes) of FP8 Ozaki II fp64-emulated DGEMM. On NVIDIA Rubin (17.5 PFLOPS fp8), the arithmetic roof is P_fp8/(3r+1) ≈ 473 TFLOPS at r=12 moduli, but the paper derives a closed-form deconstruction-λ floor of ≈235 TFLOPS for large multi-cluster outputs (exactly 0.50 of the roof — a derived ratio of three design integers: cluster reach 256, fp8:int8-tensor provisioning 140, plane-formation cost 136), consistent with NVIDIA's announced ~200-TFLOPS "Emulated DGEMM" spec, which the model reproduces at n̄ ≈ 500–900 with the fp8 tensor pipes idle 58% of the time. The proposed Ozaki 2.5 method (convert-once residue workspaces, exact two-limb constant-reduction GEMMs on the int8 tensor pipe or pure-SIMT dp4a, a ≈6.3-instruction SIMT residual, conversion pipelined behind the MMA stream) moves the Rubin crossover from ≈1,211 to ≈480–730 and projects 364–438 TFLOPS at n̄=512 vs ≈200 for the shipping cq=16 path (a conditional 1.8–2.2×); on measured LD_PRELOAD BLAS-3 traces (LOBPCG/UMFPACK/CCSD/LAPACK — the only measured data in the paper), model composites give ≈1.9× over the shipping path for block-Krylov shapes on Rubin and up to ≈67× over GB300's ≈1.4-TFLOPS native fp64. Crucially, no new kernel is measured — every performance number is a flagged reduced-model projection, with four falsifiable predictions and a decisive DGEMM size sweep deferred to a Part-3 measurement campaign.

## 核心贡献

- **解构感知性能模型（诚实的天花板）**：把 fp64→残差平面转换（TME 模型第四项，cq=16 条指令/模数/元素）纳入闭式模型。交付率随输出维调和平均 n̄=2mn/(m+n) 上升，越过交叉点 n* = cq·r·P_fp8/(α·P_int)（Rubin ≈1211，GB300 ≈346）后逼近屋顶 P_fp8/(3r+1)≈473 TFLOPS；但超过单 cluster 覆盖范围 R=256 的大输出每个 cluster 都要重切操作数，被钉在解构-λ 地板 ≈235 TFLOPS（0.50 屋顶；已发表模数集 S 只有 182 TFLOPS，0.38；锯齿状最差 0.36）。NVIDIA 公布的 ~200 TFLOPS 恰好落在 S0 分支 n̄≈500–900 处——这是一个可证伪假设。
- **Ozaki 2.5 方法**：不动 Ozaki II 的数学（MMA 调度与重构框架不变），只工程化转换路径：(O1) 转换一次的残差平面工作区（λ=1）；(O2) 把逐模数字节平面线性归约迁移为整数张量管上的精确双肢常数 GEMM（16r≈192 MACs/元素）或纯 SIMT dp4a；(O3) 不可约非线性部分压到 ≈6.3 条 SIMT 指令/模数（4/r 缩放截断 + 1 肢重组 + 2 终归约 + 3 Karatsuba 拆分）；(O4) 转换通过异步 copy 路径流水在 MMA 之后。效果（建模）：Rubin 交叉点 1211→480–730，n̄=512 处 ≈200→364–438 TFLOPS。
- **模数/编码协同设计**：把模数集当作运行时性能变量——全字节集 A（15 个 ≤256 的互质模数，单遍归约合法化，屋顶 380 TF）在 n̄≲540 以下更快（尽管屋顶低 20%）；混合集 E（平方模数 ≤332 + 字节尾，屋顶 438 TF）渐近只让 7.5%、在 n̄≲620 占优；两条穷举供给上界（平方模数上限 94.1 比特、子 64 系统 89.9 比特，均不足所需的 111.8 比特）封死设计空间， motivates 按尺寸/平台切换的模数分派。引理 1 的进位校正冗余拆分把已发表集 S 的 6 个非平方尾模数（487–511）在 e4m3 中闭合（穷举验证）。
- **闭式地板点名硬件逃生路径**：地板 = R·P_int/(c_q·r)，只由四个量决定（覆盖范围 R、整数管道率 P_int、转换成本 c_q、模数数 r）。给出具体协同设计目标表：cluster 16→64 CTA（地板 0.50→0.92）、TMEM 瓦片 64²→128²（3.5–4.0× SRAM 请求）、L2 带宽 10–12×HBM（仅 N≲1449 可行）、以及最高杠杆的 Option C（TMA 拷贝引擎内嵌残差分解变换，c_q→0，需 166–325 TMAC/s = 1.3–2.6× 残余 int8 张量吞吐）。
- **实测调用形状轨迹 + 可证伪验证计划**：用 LD_PRELOAD 拦截器实测四类应用的 (m,n,k) 几何（SciPy LOBPCG、UMFPACK 多前沿 LU、PySCF CCSD、netlib LAPACK），这是全文唯一的实测数据；四条可证伪预测（线性、膝点位置、基底结构、阶梯迁移）由一次 DGEMM 尺寸扫描（含 int8 切片内核阴性对照）裁决。全部图表由 params.py 单参数源确定性再生，随 arXiv 附属文件发布。

## Key Contributions

- **A deconstruction-aware performance model with the ceiling stated up front.** The fourth TME term (cq = 16 SIMT instructions per modulus per streamed element on the shipping path, per an NVIDIA technical memo) gates dense GEMM behind a harmonic-mean crossover n* = cq·r·P_fp8/(α·P_int) ≈ 1,211 on Rubin (≈346 on GB300) and pins large multi-cluster outputs at a closed-form deconstruction-λ floor ≈ 235 TFLOPS = 0.50 of the 473-TFLOPS roof — where 0.50 is the derived product of three design integers (256 × 37/(140 × 136) = 0.497), not a fitted efficiency. The published modulus set S floors at 182 TFLOPS (0.38); the ragged worst case is 0.36. NVIDIA's ~200-TFLOPS "Emulated DGEMM" spec is reproduced by the deconstruction term alone at n̄ ≈ 500–900, stated as a falsifiable hypothesis.
- **The Ozaki 2.5 method** — Ozaki II's mathematics untouched, only the conversion path engineered: convert-once residue-plane workspaces; the linear per-modulus byte-plane reduction migrated off SIMT as an exact two-limb constant-reduction GEMM on the int8 tensor pipe (16r ≈ 192 MACs/element, or pure-SIMT dp4a where the int8 pipe is scarce, as on GB300); an irreducible ≈6.3-instruction SIMT residual; double-buffered conversion hidden behind the (3r+1)-pass MMA stream. Modeled effect: Rubin crossover 1,211 → 480–730; ≈200 → 364–438 TFLOPS at n̄=512 (conditional 1.8–2.2×).
- **Modulus/encoding codesign as a runtime performance variable.** An all-byte system A (15 coprime moduli ≤ 256; 380-TF roof; legalizes one-pass reduction) is projected faster below n̄ ≈ 540 despite its 20% lower roof; hybrid E (square moduli ≤ 332 plus byte tail; 438-TF roof) concedes only 7.5% asymptotically and dominates below n̄ ≈ 620; two exhaustive supply bounds (squares cap at 94.1 bits, sub-64 systems at 89.9 bits, both short of the 111.8 required) close the design space. Lemma 1's carry-corrected redundant split closes the six nonsquare S-tail moduli (487–511) in e4m3, verified exhaustively over all m² ordered centred pairs per modulus.
- **The floor names its own hardware escape.** Floor = R·P_int/(c_q·r) depends on exactly four quantities; the paper tabulates concrete asks: cluster reach 16 → 64 CTAs lifts 0.50 → 0.92 of roof (128 CTAs or a 192² tile reach the roof); a 128² accumulator tile is a 3.5–4.0× TMEM ask; L2 service at 10–12× HBM reaches the roof only for N ≲ 1449; and Option C — a residue-decompose transform mode in the TMA copy engine driving cq → 0 — removes the term at every reach, requiring 166–325 TMAC/s (1.3–2.6× the platform's entire residual int8 tensor throughput) and a 55–65 TB/s SMEM plane deposit (exactly 25% of the operand read the MMA already performs).
- **Measured call-shape traces grounding the shape analysis, plus a falsification plan.** An LD_PRELOAD BLAS-3 interposer captured (m,n,k) of every dgemm/dsyrk call from six configurations across four application classes — the only measured data in the paper. Four falsifiable predictions (linearity below the knee with slope P_int/(c_q·r) ≈ 0.39 TFLOPS per unit n̄; knee position; substrate structure with a slicing-int8 control arm; staged ladder validation) are decided by a single DGEMM size sweep. All figures/tables regenerate deterministically from a params.py artifact.

## Methodology

The paper is a reduced-model performance study, explicitly not a kernel measurement (the author repeats "no new kernel is measured here" and stamps every claim with a status label from Table 1: proved-algebraically / script-checked / vendor-announced / memo-derived / modeled-projection / measured).

1. **Model.** The four-term Tensor–Memory Equilibrium (TME) model of companion Part 1 (arXiv:2606.06510), corrected by an NVIDIA technical review that isolated the per-input deconstruction cost: T_svc = max(T_fp8, T_mem, deconstruction on integer pipe, reconstruction on SIMT), with the tensor divisor α = 3r+1 = 37 fp8 MMA passes per fp64 product at r=12 moduli. Key constants: cq = 16 (memo-counted SASS conversion instructions per modulus per element, plus 3 Karatsuba converts), P_int ≈ 75 TOPS, Rubin P_fp8 = 17.5 PFLOPS, cluster reach R = 256 (a 4×4 grid of 64² accumulator tiles).
2. **Crossover and floor algebra.** With convert-once (λ=1), delivered rate PDGEMM(n̄) = min(P_fp8·n̄/(3r+1), n̄·P_int/(cq·r)) rises with the harmonic mean n̄ = 2mn/(m+n) until the knee n*; beyond one cluster's reach, operands are re-split λ = ⌈n/R⌉ × ⌈m/R⌉ times, pinning the rate at the closed-form floor R·min(P_I8TC/2·a_int8, I_SIMT·c_q^res)/(c_q·r), capped at the roof. The famous "one half" on Rubin is Eq. (15): 256 × 37/(140 × 136) = 0.497 — reach × (1/provisioning) × (plane-formation cost ratio) — nothing fitted.
3. **The Ozaki 2.5 kernel design (Algorithm 1–3).** Per k-panel: SIMT scale+truncate (~4 instr/element); marshal byte planes (TMA, no arithmetic); the linear reduction x mod m = Σ bk(2^8k mod m) executed as a pair of exact K=8 constant limb GEMMs on the int8 tensor pipe with int32 accumulation and ×256 limb recombination (or packed dp4a on SIMT — route L1); final centring mod and 3-plane Karatsuba split stay on SIMT; stored fp8 residue planes (30 for set S, 33 for E, 44 for A) are written once and reused by all (3r+1) MMA passes; conversion of panel p+1 is double-buffered behind the MMA of panel p. The nonlinear SIMT residual totals c_q^res ≈ 6.3 per modulus vs the shipping 16.
4. **Modulus-set codesign.** Candidate systems S (published, r=12), A (all-byte, r'=15), E (hybrid, r'=13), D (7-bit, dropped) are compared on roof P_fp8/α′, reduction MACs/element (176/112/136), SIMT residual, TMEM accumulator footprint N_acc (30/26/30 tiles — counted per modulus, not 2r), and knee; script-checked for coprimality, exact CRT products, and e4m3 representability of every plane (Lemma 1 closes the six nonsquare tail moduli).
5. **Traces and per-record engine.** A params.py engine prices each measured trace record (route, storage mode, memory roof at Q0 + Q_plane, reconstruction tax on the dispatched host, deconstruction-λ multiplicity) and composites FLOP-weighted harmonic means into Table 11 for both Rubin and GB300 instantiations, plus a co-design counterfactual column.
6. **Validation plan.** Four falsifiable predictions with pass/fail criteria (bit-exactness against arbitrary-precision references, SASS instruction counts, paired-rate concurrency measurements, piecewise knee fits with confidence intervals), a negative control (dummy SIMT/int8 work injection), and automation on RIKEN's Rikyu GB200 NVL4 system.

## Results & Highlights

- **The floor is exactly half the roof on Rubin, by derivation, not fit.** Large cluster-aligned square DGEMMs are pinned at ≈235 TFLOPS = 0.50 of the 473-TFLOPS roof (Eq. 15: 256 × 37/(140 × 136) = 0.497); the theorem-backed published set S floors at ≈182 TFLOPS (0.38 of roof); the rigid-integer-schedule ragged worst case (at m=n=513, where ⌈513/256⌉=3 vs area-weighted λ=2.004) dips to 0.36 of roof, with a modeled predicated schedule recovering ≈0.54 there. This floor still corresponds to ≈8× the ≈30-TFLOPS illustrative native fp64 and ≈1.9 EFLOPS sustained fp64 on a 10,000-GPU cluster (vs 1.4–1.5 EFLOPS for set S at the 75–85% HPL efficiency empirically established on large GPU clusters).
- **NVIDIA's published ~200-TFLOPS figure is explained by the deconstruction term alone.** Reading the model at the shipping cq=16: 200 TFLOPS at n̄=512 (overlapped) or n̄≈900 (serialised), with the fp8 tensor pipes idle 58% of the time — i.e., the announced spec is ≈42% of the raw 473-TFLOPS quotient P_fp8/37, and the reduced model passes through it at model-inferred n̄ ≈ 500–900. The rival flat-~40%-efficiency explanation is explicitly kept alive; a single size sweep with a slicing-int8 control kernel separates them.
- **Ozaki 2.5 moves the modeled envelope by 1.8–2.2× at the announced operating point.** The crossover knee moves from ≈1,211 to ≈480–730 on Rubin (≈346 → 130–290 on GB300), and modeled throughput at n̄=512 rises from ≈200 TFLOPS (shipping S0, cq=16) to 364 TFLOPS (route S two-limb) / 380 (all-byte A) / 438 TFLOPS (hybrid E) — all conditional convert-once-envelope projections. Tall/skinny solver shapes stay near the crossover (mild 0.89–0.94× clip), never at the floor: worth ≈1.6–1.9× (≈2× on block-Krylov shapes) over simple deconstruction on Rubin today with no hardware change.
- **On GB300 the method is present-tense and roof-bound.** With a 135-TFLOPS emulated roof that coincides with its own deconstruction floor (provisioning ratio ≈60 vs Rubin's 140 — the floor formula yields 156 TF > the 125-TF route-E roof, so the roof binds first), and a ≈1.4-TFLOPS native fp64 pipe, every modeled route exceeds native fp64 throughout the evaluated range n̄ ≥ 32 — the traced GB300 workloads are order-of-magnitude arguments for emulating at all (delivered composites ≈35–67× native for LOBPCG).
- **Measured traces (the only measurements) confirm the structural claim.** SciPy LOBPCG at block width b=64 places 100% of its GEMM work at n̄ ≤ 256 (median 128); netlib blocked QR places half its BLAS-3 work at n̄ ≈ 64; UMFPACK multifrontal fronts have median n̄ ≈ 670 (quartiles 380–850) — squarely in the crossover band. Model-projected GEMM-portion composites vs the shipping path (Rubin): LOBPCG b=64 ≈1.94×, LOBPCG b=128 ≈1.83×, CCSD ≈1.69×, dense QR ≈1.60×, multifrontal LU ≈1.19×, dense LU ≈1.14×; on GB300 dense LU actually falls below unity (≈0.87× — the rank-64 trailing update at n̄=3200 is bandwidth-bound and residue reconstruction makes emulation marginally slower than the shipping baseline). The co-design counterfactual (floor lifted to the roof) would give Rubin LOBPCG b=64 176 TF (37% of roof, ≈6× vs shipping) and b=128 352 TF (74%), but the traced applications would remain memory-bound — co-design headroom over Ozaki 2.5 is ≈2.5–3.3× on Rubin yet only ≈1.1–1.5× on GB300.
- **The modulus supply space is closed by exhaustive bounds.** Square-modulus systems cap at 94.1 bits and sub-64 systems at 89.9 bits — both short of the 111.8 bits the published set supplies — so no unexplored modulus family can beat the S/A/E menu; dispatch order on Rubin is A below n̄≈414, E to ≈616, S on the roof from ≈670.
- **HPL worked example is NB-conditional.** Hybrid E reaches its 235-TFLOPS floor only for panel widths NB ≳ 1024 (159 TF at NB=256, below set S's 182); the claim survives on the empirical convention that reported GPU HPL practice is NB ≈ 892–1024. With Option C fully granted, the theorem-backed set S would reach ≈473 TFLOPS per GPU and Rmax ≈ 3.6–4.0 EFLOPS on 10,000 GPUs — roughly 2.6× today's S row.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty: good and real but incremental-by-design.** The paper deliberately adds no mathematics to Ozaki II; its contributions are an engineering cost model, a kernel-conversion design, and a modulus-set study. The genuinely new technical artifacts — the two-limb byte identity, Lemma 1's carry-corrected split of the 487–511 tail moduli, the closed-form reach floor Eq. (14)/(15), the exhaustive supply bounds — are solid but sit atop prior measured work (Uchino/Mukunoki's FP8 Ozaki II, GEMMul8, the NVIDIA memo).
- **Rigor: epistemic hygiene is exemplary, but the empirical base is thin.** Zero kernels are measured; every TFLOPS figure (235 floor, 364–438 envelope, 1.9× composites) is a reduced-model projection. The load-bearing constants are transferred, not measured: cq=16 and P_int≈75 TOPS come from a GB300 memo and are "presumed unchanged on Rubin pending the pipe census"; the B200 joint reading is conditional on an unresolved provenance question (the released cuBLAS emulation is publicly described as Scheme-I slicing, while the memo's SASS shows CRT structure — the authors of the memo have been asked to confirm). The Rubin native-fp64 reference (≈30 TFLOPS) is itself illustrative.
- **Reproducibility: strong on paper, pending artifact release.** params.py as a single parameter source, verify/ scripts for every lemma, per-record prediction CSVs, and a manifest mapping every table cell to a generating command are promised as arXiv ancillary files — but the Zenodo DOI and commit hash are deferred to "the first arXiv revision," so the artifact cannot be checked yet at v1.
- **Model sensitivity is visible in the paper's own revision history.** Trace composites moved materially between drafts after corrections (multifrontal Rubin 2.07 → 1.19×; QR 2.27 → 1.60×; GB300 dense LU dropped below unity to 0.87×), and one composite (CCSD Rubin) changed from ≈1.73× due to a storage-mode filter. A model whose headline application numbers moved ~40% across the author's own drafts should be treated as indicative until Part 3 measures it.
- **The 200-TFLOPS explanation is a coin-flip hypothesis the paper honestly flags.** The alternative — a flat ~40% delivered efficiency, as the similar 53% B200 margin (150 of 281 TF) suggests — is fully admitted and may be the parsimonious reading; the paper's main deliverable here is the discriminating experiment, not the answer.
- **The faster routes carry weaker accuracy contracts.** Set S is covered by the round-to-nearest Ozaki II theorem; the codesigned sets E/A carry only per-set proof obligations (Appendix A), and the 235-TFLOPS headline is E's — the theorem-backed number is the lower 182 TFLOPS. The paper says this plainly, but headline-grabbing readers will quote 235.
- **Significance: potentially high for the HPC/DGEMM community** — if the deconstruction-branch hypothesis survives measurement, this is the roadmap for fp64 emulation on the FP8-dominated generation (and the HPL-at-scale arithmetic, e.g., 1.9 EFLOPS on 10,000 Rubin GPUs, is a plausible preview of future Top500 dynamics). If it falls, the paper still delivers the model that identified the alternative. Red flags: none dishonest, but a reader must constantly remember that no silicon has confirmed any performance number in the paper.

## Strengths

- States its own ceiling before its constructions: the 0.50-of-roof floor is drawn with the crossover in Figure 3 on page 13, not disclosed at the end, and the "achieved today / convert-once envelope" labeling discipline is applied to every rate.
- The 0.50 floor is derived from three design integers, not fitted — so it moves predictably with provisioning (GB300's ratio ≈60 makes it roof-bound; doubling Rubin's int8 tensor rate lifts the floor to 0.92), which is exactly what makes the co-design asks concrete and testable.
- The measured LD_PRELOAD trace methodology replaces assumed GEMM-mix folklore with real geometry across four application classes, yielding a genuinely useful negative result (sequential supernodal SuperLU issues essentially no BLAS-3 at all).
- Falsifiability is engineered in: four numbered predictions with signatures orthogonal to the rival hypothesis, a slicing-int8 negative control, staged pass/fail criteria, and a continuously-running measurement harness (Rikyu GB200 NVL4).
- The withdrawal ledger — earlier drafts' wrong claims (surcharge blanket, L2-conditioned coverage, TMEM multipliers understated by ~1.2×) are corrected in-text with the numbers that replaced them — models good scientific self-correction.

## Limitations

- No measured kernel: every performance number is a conditional reduced-model projection; the entire quantitative case awaits the Part-3 measurement campaign.
- Constants transferred across generations (cq=16, P_int from GB300 memo) and an unresolved algorithm-attribution question for the B200 cuBLAS figure weaken the cross-generation consistency argument.
- The headline 235-TFLOPS floor is conditional on the codesigned set E, panel width NB ≳ 1024, the 16-CTA cluster opt-in, and per-set accuracy obligations that are not yet discharged — the theorem-backed floor is 182 TFLOPS.
- Reconstruction hosting on Rubin is a real unresolved cost: SIMT-Garner is 28.4% of MMA time at k=1000 and falls below 1% only at k ≳ 2.84×10⁴; the int8-hosted ≈r-op alternative needs k ≳ 2,270 and carries an unproven fp64-exactness obligation.
- Model endpoints assume service independence (perfect overlap); imperfect overlap can push measured time past even the serialised bracket, and the concurrency parameter θ is unmeasured.
- The co-design counterfactuals (Option C, 128-CTA clusters, 10–12× L2) require hardware that does not exist; on traced applications, even granting co-design in full leaves them bandwidth-bound at 28–74% of roof.

## Relevance

对关注"低精度硬件上做高精度科学计算"的方向高度相关：本文给出了 FP8 时代 fp64 DGEMM 仿真的完整性能解剖与工程路线图（软件方法 + 模数集调度 + 硬件协同设计清单），其闭式成本模型与可证伪预测方法论可直接迁移到其它"低精度张量核仿真高精度算子"的研究中；对 HPL/Top500 趋势和稀疏直接法、块 Krylov 特征值求解器在 GPU 上的前景也很有参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Will the decisive DGEMM size sweep on Rubin/B200 confirm the deconstruction-limited branch (rising rate linear in n̄, knee at ≈1,211/≈730) or the flat-efficiency alternative — and what does the measured cq turn out to be versus the memo's 16?
- Can the SIMT-residual instruction count of ≈6.3 per modulus (and the L1-direct dp4a counts of 10.3/7.3) survive SASS-level verification, or will register pressure and occupancy push the realized counts back toward the shipping 16?
- Do the per-set accuracy obligations of the codesigned E/A modulus sets (Appendix A) actually discharge under adversarial scaling and conditioning, preserving componentwise fp64-grade error bounds?
- Is the k ≈ 6,777 route crossover (beyond which all-byte A overtakes hybrid E) reachable in any real workload, or is the modulus dispatch effectively a two-route menu in practice?
- What would an Ozaki-2.5-style deconstruction analysis look like for sparse kernels (SpMV/SpMM), where the paper claims the same cq term binds below an intensity threshold with no re-split floor to escape — and can the persistent-plane mode (bandwidth ×2.83 per nnz for set S) ever win on Rubin-class parts?
- Since Top500 does not currently accept emulated DGEMM for fp64 Linpack, would the HPL-scale numbers (1.9 EFLOPS per 10,000 Rubin GPUs) require a rule change, and does that politicize the adoption path?
