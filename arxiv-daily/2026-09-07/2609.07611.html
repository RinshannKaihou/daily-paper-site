Agentic evaluation separates models 4.4× better than static reading — but only capable models benefit.

主动检索式评测的模型间区分度是静态文献阅读的 4.4 倍，且增益被模型能力「门控」：最强模型显著受益，弱模型反而受损；增益落在可行性、清晰度等「接地」维度，而文献校验的原创性保持不变。

AgentIdeaBench: Benchmarking Scientific Ideation in the Agent Era ↗

Yunxiang Mo · Tianshi Zheng · Yisen Gao · Rui Wang · et al. (10 authors) — HKUST KnowComp · NVIDIA AI Technology Center

Key metric
4.4×

between-model variance of Active (agent-controlled retrieval) vs Static (curated reading) evaluation.

62% of top-half model pairs distinguished under Active vs 12% under Static (bootstrap-SE). Scores scale +1.16 vs +0.54 pts/yr along knowledge-cutoff year — interaction +0.62/yr, 95% CI [0.41, 0.94], permutation p<1e-3.

Why this matters

If you build or evaluate autonomous AI scientists, this shows how to benchmark their ideation module — agentic protocols discriminate where static ones saturate.

「对评估自主 AI 科学家系统的构思模块，以及设计下一代 agentic benchmark，都有直接的方法论参考价值。」— Relevance

Critical edge

All dimension scores are LLM judgments — the headline “originality stays flat” rests on the least human-validated axis, and no domain-expert novelty study has been run.

Also: a 10-call tool budget measures the onset of agentic ideation, not its ceiling.

  • Scale: 100 narrow subfields across CS, physics, biology, chemistry, medicine; 40 densely scored with 3 hypotheses per model×subfield×setting cell — ~21k critic calls over 35 models (33 matched Static–Active pairs, cutoffs 2023–2026).
  • Unseen by construction: reference literature published 2025-04 → 2026-01, after nearly every evaluated model's knowledge cutoff.
  • Statistics: Wilcoxon signed-rank on paired cells, 5000-resample bootstrap CIs, model-family cluster bootstraps (9/28 are qwen), leave-one-family-out, disjoint subfield halves; 5 held-out Gemini models replicate the headline effect out-of-roster.
  • Calibration: seven-round scorer study; coherence/boilerplate guards cap keyword-stuffed text; budget fixed at 10 calls from a {1…20} sweep — conservative for one backbone (qwen3.5-9b).
  • Human anchors: CORE-7 award papers rewritten into benchmark format score mean 7.71 (peak 8.5) vs 1.80 for weak-control generators — the LLM scale stays interpretable and non-circular.
  • Decomposition controls: a recall-only track (no refs, no tools) and a replay track (passively re-feeding each model its own agent-surfaced papers) isolate where the Active gain lives.

Strengths

优势
  • Matched Static/Active design isolates exactly one variable — literature-access mode — while prompts, format, length and scoring stay identical.
  • Literature-verified originality forces critics to argue novelty against retrieved prior art instead of parametric memory — a concrete, reusable fix for LLM-judge blind spots.
  • Deep controls: recall/replay tracks, ROUGE-L memorization probes, family-cluster bootstraps, out-of-roster Gemini replication, full disclosure of the SWM negative result.
  • Human-landmark anchoring (CORE-7 7.71 vs best model 6.33) keeps the LLM-judged scale interpretable and non-circular.
  • Open release: benchmark, critic, prompts and scoring data at github.com/HKUST-KnowComp/AgentIdeaBench.

Limitations

局限
  • All scores are LLM judgments; originality has the weakest human-agreement support and the missing domain-expert novelty study is the gap the authors themselves rank first.
  • Active bundles three things — retrieval control, multi-turn interaction, tool-execution competence; the missing cell (agent-selected refs without agent-driven turns) was not run.
  • SWM extension is unconfirmed (fails multiple-comparison correction; matched by best-of-3) — a negative result framed as a direction.
  • Discrimination varies by discipline — weakest in dense-literature fields, so cross-discipline rankings are soft.
  • Shallow agentic probe: 10 tool calls, one 80–150-word paragraph; hypotheses are untested proposals — the paper warns scores should not gate funding or publication.
  • Roster is family-heavy (9/28 qwen) and open-weight-dominated; closed-source coverage rests on 5 held-out Gemini models.

Questions for further exploration

待探索
  1. Does the capability gate (r=+0.69) survive a domain-expert novelty study, or is it partly an artifact of the LLM critic's least human-validated axis?
  2. Can agent-selected references without agent-driven turns separate the value of multi-turn interaction from tool-execution competence?
  3. Does “grounding without added novelty” hold for full multi-page research proposals with experimental design, not just single paragraphs?
  4. Do frontier models keep gaining at 50–100 tool calls (only qwen3.5-9b saturated the 10-call budget), or does the Active curve plateau too?
  5. Why does feasibility peak on weak models (Llama-4 Maverick 6.87, the roster max) while originality climbs toward the frontier — is cautious restatement a stable equilibrium for small models?
  6. Could SWM-style structured thought experiments be redesigned to help frontier models, e.g. by targeting dimensions they do not already self-check?
