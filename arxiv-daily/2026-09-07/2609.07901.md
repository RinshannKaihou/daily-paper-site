> **一句话总结：** 在 Qwen3-8B/14B/32B 上固定种子对比 AWQ-int4 与 bf16 服务（约 7.1 万条配对生成）发现：4-bit 量化在 8B 上显著收敛推荐输出——同一场景两次采样推荐同一品牌的概率上升 5.1 个百分点（Holm p=.023）——但并不放大刻板印象偏置；14B/32B 上内容收敛消失，只剩文风漂移（破折号率上升），即"量化放大的是确定性，不是偏见"。

> **TL;DR:** The author serves the same Qwen3 checkpoints (8B/14B/32B) at three weight precisions (W4A16 AWQ, W8A16 FP8-Marlin, bf16) with identical hardware, vLLM flags, and seeds, collecting ~71,000 completions paired by prompt+seed across two leak-checked batteries (218 country-elicitation prompts + 96 brand-blind car-buying scenarios). On the recommendation battery, AWQ-int4 vs bf16 at 8B raises same-brand collision probability by +5.1pp (Holm p=.023; reproduced at +4.4pp) and cuts type-token ratio by −0.0113 (p<10⁻⁴), while at 14B/32B content effects vanish and only em-dash style drift remains (+0.46 and +0.61 per 1k words). Pre-specified stereotype-direction tests are null at every scale, and mechanism probes show decision-token entropy *rising* (+0.091 bits) even as the semantic brand distribution becomes *more* concentrated (+2.6pp collision) — a failure mode invisible to logit-level audits.

## 核心贡献

- 首次系统研究服务时权重量化对"多答案合法"任务（推荐、建议、默认选择）输出**分布**的影响——即量化是否改变模型"选择说什么"，而非仅仅考察能力基准上的"近乎无损"。
- 受控精度阶梯实验设计：同一 Qwen3 官方检查点家族（8B/14B/32B）× 三种精度（W4A16 AWQ / W8A16 FP8-Marlin / bf16），硬件、vLLM 0.11.0 引擎、采样 flags、随机种子全部一致，消除了 API 对比中常见的供应商混淆；约 71,000 条按 prompt+seed 配对的生成结果。
- 两个对抗审查并程序化查漏的自建题库：218 条国家引出文化题（含 80 条风格对照）+ 96 条品牌盲购车推荐场景；配套 57 品牌 / 78 国家词典与 30 例对抗消歧测试套件。
- declare-then-run（先声明后运行）统计协议：三波分析全部预注册于版本控制历史，主检验用 prompt 配对 sign-flip 置换检验（10,000 次翻转）+ Holm 校正 + prompt 聚类 bootstrap 置信区间。
- 核心发现：8B 上 int4 收敛推荐与词汇多样性；14B/32B 上内容效应消失、代之以文风漂移；任何规模都无刻板印象放大；token 级熵上升而语义分布更集中——说明该失效模式**无法**仅从 token logits 审计出来。

## Key Contributions

- First controlled study of whether serving-time weight compression changes *what* a model chooses to say when many answers are valid, as opposed to whether it retains capability on single-answer benchmarks.
- A precision ladder over one official checkpoint family (Qwen3 8B/14B/32B × W4A16 AWQ / W8A16 FP8-Marlin / bf16) holding hardware, engine (vLLM 0.11.0), flags, tensor parallelism, and seeds fixed — ~71,000 completions paired by prompt and seed, with the provider confound removed.
- Two purpose-built, adversarially reviewed, programmatically leak-checked batteries: a 218-prompt country-elicitation cultural battery (plus 80 style-control prompts) and a 96-scenario brand-blind car-buying recommendation battery, with gazetteer extraction hardened by a 30-case adversarial test suite (e.g., distinguishing the Genesis brand from "the book of Genesis").
- A declare-then-run statistical protocol: analyses pre-specified in three waves in version control before the corresponding arms were generated, confirmatory claims resting only on Holm-corrected sign-flip permutation tests with prompt-cluster bootstrap CIs.
- The findings themselves: int4 collapses recommendation and lexical diversity at 8B only; content effects are replaced by stylistic drift at 14B/32B; no stereotype amplification at any scale; and a dissociation where token-level entropy rises while directly measured semantic concentration also rises.

## Methodology

Three official Qwen3 checkpoints (8B, 14B, 32B) are each served at three weight precisions — W4A16 (AWQ), W8A16 (the official FP8 checkpoint executed weight-only through Marlin kernels on Ampere GPUs, activations in bf16), and bf16 — producing a monotone weight-precision series with activation precision held constant (a caveat: rungs differ in quantization method as well as bit-width). Serving uses vLLM 0.11.0 with identical flags per model, TP=4 for 8B/14B and TP=8 for 32B; sampling at temperature 0.8, top-p 0.95, max 400 tokens, with n=20 samples per prompt per tier drawn under the *same seeds*, so sampling-RNG variation is removed from every int4−bf16 contrast (residual nondeterminism from dynamic batching is symmetric and deliberately exercised by a full-arm regeneration check). Total cost: about $80.

The cultural battery contains 218 country-eliciting prompts across 11 domains (country_pull labels: none 110, weak 65, strong 43 — 30 of the strong pulls target Japan, following Fernandez de Landa et al.'s paradigm) plus 80 style-control prompts; the recommendation battery has 96 brand-blind car-buying scenarios (brand_pull: none 72, weak 16, strong 8). Countries are extracted with a 78-country gazetteer and brands with a 57-make gazetteer with four ambiguity classes; style metrics follow Kobak et al.'s LLM-style markers (focal-word rate, em-dash rate, "not X but Y", type-token ratio). The prompt is the unit of analysis: two-sided sign-flip permutation tests (10,000 flips), prompt-cluster bootstrap CIs (5,000 resamples), rarefied corpus quantities with Miller–Madow robustness checks, Holm correction within each declared family. Wave 1 = scalar style/content metrics; wave 2 = within-prompt concentration (collision probability of first-mentioned entity, top-5 mass); wave 3 = stereotype direction (stereotype-set share, pull-target compliance). Mechanism instruments at 8B: top-20 log-probability summaries per generated token, plus a direct semantic measurement — since all 57 makes begin with a unique first token under the Qwen3 tokenizer, the fixed assistant prefix "The brand I would recommend is," is appended and top-50 next-token log-probabilities are mapped to brands (brand-initial tokens carry 24.0% bf16 / 26.2% int4 of mass; metrics computed on renormalized brand mass).

## Results & Highlights

- **8B recommendation collapse:** On the 96-scenario brand-blind car-buying battery, int4 vs bf16 raises brand collision probability (two of 20 same-seed samples naming the same first brand) by **+5.1pp, 95% CI [+0.012, +0.090], Holm p=.023**, and the effect reproduces at **+4.4pp (p=.029)** on a full regeneration of the arm. Rarefied brand entropy falls −0.021 (CI excludes 0); bf16-top-5 brand mass moves only +0.014 (CI spans 0).
- **8B lexical diversity:** Type-token ratio drops **−0.0113, CI [−0.0141, −0.0083], p<10⁻⁴ (Holm <4×10⁻⁴)**, standardized effect −0.51; the length-robust MATTR-100 check survives essentially unchanged (−0.0066, CI [−0.0090, −0.0042], p<10⁻⁴), ruling out the +5.7-word length increase as the driver.
- **Effects are battery- and prompt-specific at 8B:** the cultural battery shows no 8B concentration (country collision −1.2pp, n.s.), and the median per-prompt collision delta is 0 (IQR [0, +0.14]) — "a minority of scenarios collapse hard," e.g. the public-charging apartment-dweller prompt where bf16 recommends Tesla 13 / Renault 3 / Nissan 2 / Hyundai 2 times but int4 recommends Tesla in all 20 samples. The collapse is modal, not global: Toyota's first-mention share rises 42%→47% while Honda's falls 14%→9% with combined share unchanged.
- **Scale dissociation:** brand collision is null at 14B (−0.007, n.s.) and 32B (−0.023, n.s.), and cultural-battery TTR is null at 32B (+0.0003); instead em-dash rate drifts upward at 14B (**+0.46/1k words, Holm p=.002, +19% relative**) and 32B (**+0.61/1k words, Holm p=.0024, +12% relative**), near-identical standardized effects of 0.25 — significant on cultural prompts but not on the 80-prompt placeless style-control placebo arm (e.g., 14B em-dash −0.223/1k, p=.455).
- **No stereotype amplification (pre-specified):** all wave-3 direction tests are null at all sizes — stereotype-set share −.011 to −.017, brand pull-target compliance −.007 to +.017 (n=23–24, so these nulls bound only large effects). The only significant wave-3 result runs opposite to amplification: at int4, 32B complies *less* with weak country stereotypes (−4.4pp, Holm p=.043). Japan movements at 32B are mixed (unprompted +2.3pp, provoked −3.2pp) and treated as redistribution, not amplification.
- **Mechanism — the two description levels dissociate:** at 8B int4, decision-token entropy *rises* **+0.091 bits (CI [+0.020, +0.163], p=.015)** and top-1 token probability falls −3.8pp (p=.024), yet the semantic brand distribution (measured from first-token log-probs under a forced decision prefix) becomes *more* concentrated: collision **+2.6pp (CI [+0.4, +4.7], p=.023; p=.019 excluding short-prefix makes)** with mean per-prompt Jensen–Shannon divergence of 0.040 bits between tiers; total brand mass rises 24.0%→26.2%, so it is not a coverage artifact. Individual tokens become less predictable even as meanings become more repetitive.
- **Robustness to temperature:** at temperature 0.4 the TTR decline persists (−0.0090, p<10⁻⁴); the collision contrast keeps its direction but falls below significance (+3.2pp, p=.14) as colder sampling compresses both tiers toward the ceiling (paired mean collision 0.901/0.933 at temp 0.4 vs 0.776/0.827 at 0.8), which the author reads as headroom compression rather than disappearance.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** Genuinely novel question and result. Prior quantization work evaluates capability on single-answer benchmarks; this is the first controlled test of compression × default-answer concentration (building directly on Fernandez de Landa et al.'s 2026 Japan-default paradigm), and the token-entropy-up / semantic-concentration-down dissociation is a non-obvious finding with a concrete audit implication (logit-level audits cannot see it).
- **Rigor:** High. Seed-paired contrasts, prompt-level sign-flip permutation tests with Holm correction, bootstrap CIs, a full-arm regeneration that reproduces the headline effect (+4.4pp), a length-controlled diversity measure (MATTR-100) to defuse the length confound, a placebo style-control arm, and adversarially tested gazetteers. The declare-then-run staging (wave 1 before any generation; waves 2–3 declared before the 8B/32B arms were generated) is more pre-registration discipline than most LLM papers carry.
- **Reproducibility:** Promised, not yet delivered — code, prompts with provenance, gazetteers, per-wave scripts, frozen statistics, and the version-control history are to be released "with the camera-ready version," so at preprint time the central auditability claim is unverifiable. The design itself (three public checkpoints, vLLM, ~$80 budget) is cheap to replicate.
- **Significance:** Practically relevant for anyone serving quantized 7–9B models in recommendation/default-choice roles: the paper shows such a model can pass every bias audit while still narrowing the answer set, and it proposes concrete audit metrics (collision probability, distinct answers across k repeated queries).
- **Weaknesses / red flags:** (1) The ladder's rungs confound quantizer with bit-width (AWQ int4 vs an FP8 checkpoint run weight-only via Marlin vs bf16) — acknowledged but real. (2) The intermediate W8 rung effectively disappears from the reported tables: every headline contrast is int4−bf16, so the ladder yields no visible dose–response evidence even though a middle rung was paid for. (3) Single model family — the "boundary near 14B" may be Qwen3- or AWQ-specific; only one quantizer per precision, calibration-set effects untested. (4) The 32B arm ran at TP=8 on a different instance type, so the largest-scale nulls carry a parallelism confound. (5) The 8B effect is heterogeneous (median per-prompt delta 0) and drops below significance at temperature 0.4 (p=.14) — the ceiling-compression interpretation is plausible but the headline is temperature-fragile. (6) Pull-compliance nulls rest on n=23–24 prompts, so "no stereotype amplification" is only bounded against large effects at the prompt level. (7) No formal arm×tier interaction test backs the placebo-arm specificity claim for the em-dash drift. (8) Single domain (US automotive retail) and English only.

## Strengths

- Asks a deployment-relevant question that capability benchmarks structurally cannot answer, and frames bias vs concentration as two distinct audit targets that are easy to conflate.
- Exceptional experimental control for a serving study: same checkpoint family, same seeds, same flags, regeneration check — the int4−bf16 contrast isolates precision to the extent the quantizer confound allows.
- Pre-specified, Holm-corrected statistics with honest classification of confirmatory vs supporting evidence, and null results reported with equal prominence to positives.
- The forced-prefix first-token log-prob instrument is an elegant, exact way to measure the semantic distribution separately from token-level entropy.
- Actionable recommendations: audit concentration (collision probability, distinct answers across k queries), not just skew; prefer a larger model at the same precision, diversity-promoting decoding, or inventory conditioning where small quantized models must recommend.

## Limitations

- One model family (Qwen3) and one quantizer per precision; the apparent content-effect boundary near 14B may not transfer, and calibration-set effects are deferred to future work.
- The W8 condition is weight-only FP8 via Marlin on Ampere and does not represent W8A8 serving; rungs differ in quantization method, not just bit-width.
- The recommendation battery covers a single domain (automotive retail, US-market framing) and the analysis is English-only.
- The 8B concentration effect is heterogeneous across prompts (median delta 0) and falls below significance at temperature 0.4.
- 32B was served with TP=8 on a different instance type, confounding scale with parallelism for cross-scale comparisons.
- Stereotype-direction nulls have limited power at the prompt level (n=23–24 for pull compliance).

## Relevance

量化推理是当前 LLM 部署的标配，本文指出 4-bit 小模型（~8B 级）在推荐/建议类开放任务上会系统性地收敛输出分布——这直接关联高效推理与服务系统研究中"量化近乎无损"这一流行假设的边界。对关注模型评测、LLM serving 与输出多样性的研究而言，它提供了一个便宜（约 80 美元）但严格的行为审计范式，并表明部署审计应同时测量"集中度"而非只测偏置。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the ~14B content-effect boundary replicate across other families (Llama, Mistral, Gemma) and quantizers (GPTQ, HQQ, per-channel/per-group variants), or is it an AWQ-on-Qwen3 artifact?
- How much of the 8B collapse is attributable to AWQ's calibration set rather than 4-bit precision itself — and can calibration-set choice steer which answers collapse?
- The paper shows the effect is invisible at the token level; do aggregate semantic-entropy estimators (e.g., Farquhar et al.'s sampling-based semantic entropy) detect it from outputs alone, without the forced-prefix instrument?
- Do diversity-promoting decoding schemes (min-p, higher temperature, nucleus放宽, "anti-collapse" penalties) or conditioning on available inventory recover tail-brand exposure at int4 without degrading answer quality?
- Does activation quantization (W8A8/W4A4) produce the same determinism-amplification signature, or does it damage the modal path as well?
- Can the em-dash style drift at 14B/32B serve as a cheap behavioral fingerprint of quantization — e.g., for detecting that a served "fp16" endpoint is actually compressed?
