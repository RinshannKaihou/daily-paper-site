IB2: A Protocol for Measuring Enterprise AI Systems by Serving Route, Not Model Identifier ↗

Key metric · route-bound delta

+42.14

points between serving routes on identical weights — DeepInfra FP8 26.12 vs Provider P 68.27 across the same 128 locked tasks. No identifier-based benchmark can express this delta.

Why this matters

Enterprise-evaluation builders get a reusable route-level protocol — capability binding, reliability-inclusive scoring, score-blind adjudication — plus candid self-diagnosis of saturation and rank instability.

「按服务路由而非模型标识符评测」的完整可复用协议，附对基准饱和与单次运行排名不稳定性的自我诊断。

Critical edge

The route-failure contract — 13 images, 25 tools, >32,768-token completions — is a design choice, not a measured enterprise workload. The headline availability finding is scoped to a contract the authors invented.

Methodology

Three load-bearing parts

  1. Bind before scoring. Algorithm 1 runs ten predicates per route — route identity, 13-image envelope, 25-tool catalog, >32,768-token completion ceiling, reasoning & usage telemetry, zero-data-retention — each classed capability vs governance. Verdicts are BOUND or WITHHELD: a binding-time incompatibility never becomes a model score of zero, and successful tasks are never spliced across routes.
  2. Score reliability in. Every attempted response is graded once by 987 deterministic assertions. Absent responses earn a mechanically implied zero; parseable-but-contract-invalid work earns partial credit. The composite is an equal-suite mean, so 22-task suites cannot dominate 10- and 12-task ones.
  3. Adjudicate score-blind. The recovery selector reads a typed projection from which scores, gold labels, and grader output are absent by type — a biased selector cannot selectively retry. Comparability is fail-closed (canonical run-plan hashing, no per-task splicing); 20,000 paired bootstrap draws supply task-set sensitivity intervals.

Instrument — sealed 128-task bank · 7 suites · 987 assertions · profiles: IB2-6 Core (106) / IB2-7 Full (128) · serial execution, concurrency 1 · algorithms open, corpus sealed.

Strengths

What holds up

  • Serving-route dependence treated as a first-class measurement object — formal tuple, verified preflight, availability reported as an outcome.
  • Score-blindness by type projection: correctness is unreachable, not merely unread — a concrete, implementable anti-p-hacking mechanism.
  • Self-critical transparency beyond boilerplate: binding chronology disclosed, own tier ladder demoted under multiplicity/BCa, Stage-0 audit hole quantified.
  • Resolution groups with interval backing instead of ranks; cost, latency, and token-efficiency reported for every system.
  • Fail-closed run comparability via run-plan hashing blocks silent partial-rerun contamination.

Limitations

What doesn't

  • Single run per configuration; the leader-pair interval (6.4 pts wide) exceeds the 0.97-pt gap it is meant to resolve.
  • Private synthetic corpus — unfalsifiable by readers, built by an interested vendor, easier than the closest public comparator; challenge reserve consumed.
  • Four of seven suites saturated: the composite is carried by two, so "enterprise capability" is narrower than the seven-suite branding.
  • The gate failed its one live admission test — GLM passed all ten predicates, then failed strict-JSON conformance in production.
  • Stage-0 telemetry asymmetry: Qwen3.8-27B's 82.54 is not transport-corrected while rivals' faults stay in their scores.

Questions for further exploration

  • Does the +5.16 serving-arm effect decompose into engine, quantization, batching, or tool-call-parser components?
  • How much of GLM's 41.10 is parser strictness rather than capability — would a preregistered lenient extractor recover it?
  • Does the saturation finding transfer to a public corpus such as OfficeQA Pro or Spider 2.0?
  • Can capability and governance availability (predicates 1–7 vs 8–10) diverge in practice — and would separate reporting change provider behaviour?
  • What would a human baseline on the 128-task bank cost — how far is 88.34 from expert-level performance?
  • Do providers silently change envelopes under fixed identifiers — can content-addressed binding receipts detect drift?
