Key metric — 8B, int4 vs bf16 +5.1pp

Rise in same-brand collision probability — two of 20 same-seed samples recommending the same first brand on brand-blind car-buying prompts.

Holm p=.023 · 95% CI [+0.012, +0.090] · reproduced +4.4pp (p=.029) on full-arm regeneration

Why this matters

Quantized inference is the deployment default; this maps where the “near-lossless” assumption breaks — and provides a cheap (~$80) audit that measures output concentration, not just bias.

distilled from Relevance (相关性)

Critical edge

The ladder confounds quantizer with bit-width (AWQ vs FP8-Marlin vs bf16), the paid-for W8 rung vanishes from the headline tables, and the 8B effect drops below significance at temperature 0.4 (p=.14) — and nothing is independently verifiable until camera-ready.

01

Methodology

3 × 3 ladder, seed-paired

One checkpoint family — Qwen3 8B/14B/32B — each served at three weight precisions on identical hardware, identical vLLM flags, and identical sampling seeds, so every int4−bf16 contrast is prompt+seed-paired and sampling-RNG variation is removed by construction.

Two purpose-built, adversarially reviewed, programmatically leak-checked batteries supply the probes: 218 country-elicitation cultural prompts (plus 80 style controls) and 96 brand-blind car-buying scenarios, extracted with 78-country / 57-make gazetteers hardened by a 30-case adversarial suite. Statistics follow a declare-then-run protocol — analyses pre-specified in version control, prompt-level sign-flip permutation tests (10,000 flips) with Holm correction and prompt-cluster bootstrap CIs.

The collapse is modal, not global: median per-prompt collision delta is 0 — “a minority of scenarios collapse hard.” Aggregate first-mention: Toyota 42%→47%, Honda 14%→9%, combined share unchanged.

  • No stereotype amplification at any scale — all pre-specified direction tests null (stereotype-set share −.011 to −.017); the only significant wave-3 result runs opposite to amplification (32B int4 complies less with weak country stereotypes, −4.4pp, p=.043). Nulls rest on n=23–24 prompts, so they bound only large effects.
  • Temperature fragility: at temp 0.4 the TTR decline persists (−0.0090, p<10⁻⁴) but collision keeps direction only sub-significantly (+3.2pp, p=.14) — read as headroom compression, not disappearance.
  • Style drift as fingerprint: em-dash drift is significant on cultural prompts but not on the 80-prompt placeless placebo arm — the author proposes it as a cheap behavioral tell that a served “fp16” endpoint is actually compressed.
03

Strengths

what it does well
  • Asks a deployment question capability benchmarks structurally cannot answer — and separates “bias” from “concentration” as two distinct audit targets that are easy to conflate.
  • Exceptional control for a serving study: same checkpoint family, same seeds, same flags, plus a full-arm regeneration that reproduces the headline effect.
  • Pre-specified, Holm-corrected statistics with an honest confirmatory/supporting split; nulls reported with equal prominence to positives.
  • The forced-prefix first-token log-prob instrument elegantly measures the semantic distribution separately from token-level entropy.
  • Actionable: audit collision probability and distinct answers across k queries; prefer a larger model at the same precision, diversity-promoting decoding, or inventory conditioning.
04

Limitations

where it stops short
  • One model family (Qwen3), one quantizer per precision — the apparent content-effect boundary near 14B may not transfer; calibration-set effects untested.
  • Ladder confound: rungs differ in quantization method, not just bit-width; the W8 condition is weight-only FP8 via Marlin, not W8A8 serving.
  • Single domain, single language — US automotive retail framing, English only.
  • Heterogeneous, temperature-fragile effect: median per-prompt delta 0; below significance at temp 0.4.
  • Parallelism confound at 32B (TP=8 on a different instance type) weakens cross-scale comparisons.
  • Underpowered direction tests (n=23–24) and no formal arm×tier interaction test behind the placebo-arm specificity claim.
05

Questions for Further Exploration

open threads
  1. Does the ~14B content-effect boundary replicate across other families (Llama, Mistral, Gemma) and quantizers (GPTQ, HQQ, per-channel variants) — or is it an AWQ-on-Qwen3 artifact?
  2. How much of the 8B collapse is attributable to AWQ’s calibration set rather than 4-bit precision itself — and can calibration choice steer which answers collapse?
  3. Can sampling-based semantic-entropy estimators detect the effect from outputs alone, without the forced-prefix instrument?
  4. Do diversity-promoting decoding schemes (min-p, higher temperature) or inventory conditioning recover tail-brand exposure at int4 without degrading answer quality?
  5. Does activation quantization (W8A8 / W4A4) produce the same determinism-amplification signature, or does it damage the modal path as well?
  6. Can em-dash style drift serve as a cheap behavioral fingerprint that a served “fp16” endpoint is actually compressed?
