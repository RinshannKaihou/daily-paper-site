arXiv 2609.07148 2026-09-07 RL Post-Training · Weekly Digest Pick

Entropy collapse in multimodal GRPO is a data problem — fixed by curating what the optimizer sees, not the objective.

一句话总结（摘）：不修改 RL 目标函数，仅靠 PAQM 查询筛选 + HSR 分层批次构造，即可抑制 GRPO 的熵坍缩；Qwen2.5-VL-7B 十基准平均 56.2%。

Stable-MM-R1: Anchoring Multimodal Reasoning Dynamics via Entropy-Guided Stratification ↗
Yimeng Ye*, Shuang Chen*, Wenxuan Huang, Manyuan Zhang, et al. — 11 authors · Columbia · CUHK · Tsinghua · McGill · *equal contribution
Multimodal Reasoning RL / GRPO Entropy Collapse Data-Centric RL 7B & 3B Scales
Key Metric
56.2%

average over 10 multimodal benchmarks (Pass@8, Qwen2.5-VL-7B) — +9.7 pts over Vision-G1 (46.5%), the strongest open 7B baseline; +20.2 on MathVision.

Why This Matters

The data-centric recipe — query mining plus stratified batch construction — plugs into any verifiable-reward RL fine-tuning, composably alongside objective-centric fixes like DAPO.

⚠ Critical Edge

The +9.7 headline is uncontrolled: baselines never had the 189k curated corpus or Seed-1.5-VL teacher hints. The honest, matched delta is +5.1.

Proprietary teacher · no code release · single-seed, 400-step runs.

Methodology

Stabilize by controlling the data, not the loss

RL-only post-training — no supervised cold start, GRPO objective untouched.

Phase 0
PAQM. Sixteen pilot rollouts per query estimate success rate p̂: 0<p̂<1 is the learnable “Distillation Zone”; trivial wins are downsampled; p̂=0 queries get a Modality-Decoupled Diagnosis (ground-truth-caption probes separating perceptual from cognitive failure) and are reclaimed (~30%) with offline Seed-1.5-VL hints — injected into prompts, never into gradients.
Phase 1
Stratify. Path Entropy — length-normalized sequence surprisal — acts as a rollout-level confidence proxy. Batch-adaptive 10th/90th-percentile thresholds sort each step’s rollouts into the 2×2 at right.
Phase 2
Assemble. Half of each group-of-16 is structured (25% Epiphanies, 12.5% Anchors, 12.5% Hard Negatives), half stays natural. Buffers are cleared right after the gradient step — strictly on-policy, no importance-sampling corrections.
Theory (App. A.3): random groups degenerate with probability pG+(1−p)G; structured groups with ≥1 positive and ≥1 negative keep reward variance strictly positive (σ̂² = N₊N₋/G²) — a conditional, not per-step, guarantee.

Setup: Qwen2.5-VL-7B/3B, frozen vision encoder, ~189k verifiable prompts (MinHash-LSH + pHash decontamination), EasyR1, global batch 128, 400 steps, 8×H100.

Strengths
  • Attacks instability at data ingestion and group composition — orthogonal to and composable with objective-centric fixes (DAPO, clipping, KL control).
  • Intra-step-only buffers keep “replay” strictly on-policy: no importance-sampling corrections — a genuinely practical engineering contribution.
  • Broad coverage (10 benchmarks, 7B & 3B) with unusually candid loss reporting and baseline peak-performance disclosure.
  • Appendix theory (degenerate-group probability, conditional variance, distribution-shift caveat) matches practice without overclaiming.
Limitations
  • Hinges on the proprietary Seed-1.5-VL teacher for hints — distillation bias, access limits, offline compute unaccounted in the matched-update comparison.
  • Buffer composition (25/12.5/12.5) never ablated; gains not isolated to Anchors/Hard Negatives vs. Epiphany upweighting.
  • 400 steps, single seed, no code or hint-cache release — long-horizon stability claims under-tested; teacher hints in prompts blur the “RL-only” framing.
  • PAQM’s capability filtering risks systematically dropping data the base model deems “Impossible” — algorithmic-exclusion bias the authors flag for auditing.
Questions for Further Exploration
  • Q1How much of the HSR gain survives when Epiphany upweighting is removed but Anchors/Hard Negatives are kept — contrast, exploration bias, or variance reduction?
  • Q2Would an open or self-generated teacher (e.g., Qwen2.5-VL-72B) preserve the +3.1 PAQM gain — and does hint format leak into eval behavior?
  • Q3Does the 0.35–0.60 nats band hold at 2,000+ steps, larger groups (G>16), or under DAPO-style dynamic sampling?
  • Q4Can Path Entropy be replaced by cheaper proxies — mean token log-prob or a small value head — without losing the stratification signal?
  • Q5Does PAQM’s Impossible-zone exclusion bias the corpus — does the reclaimed 30% cover the full difficulty spectrum or only the teacher-easy subset?
  • Q6Can a single rollout appear in both streams or duplicated within a group — does deduplication matter for the variance guarantee?
