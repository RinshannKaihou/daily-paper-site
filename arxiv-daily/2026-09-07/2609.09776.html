Why This Matters · 为何重要

For anyone using LLM-as-judge rewards or best-of-N selection: a quantitative Goodhart law, a frozen-vs-settled experimental paradigm, and the Snd@N robustness metric.

— distilled from Relevance

Critical Edge · 批判视角

The most important regime — tasks at the judge's capability frontier under RL-scale optimization pressure — is exactly the untested one.

Real-suite support is thinner than the abstract suggests: 50% of MBPP and 89% of HumanEval problems showed zero gold variance and were excluded.

Methodology

研究方法 — 一项预注册的“立场 + 概念验证”混合研究

Position-plus-proof-of-concept, with pass/fail criteria pre-registered before every scaled run; registrations, raw data, and analysis code released.

  1. Theory. §3 Joint-Gaussian best-of-N: E[gold of selected] = ρ·E[max proxy] ≈ ρ√(2 ln N). Correlation ρ is the compute→capability exchange rate; an unsound verifier pays N1/ρ² candidates.
  2. Synthetic testbeds. §6–7 Six-token DSL with a deliberately shallow ridge verifier (N to 2,048); pre-registered ten-token DSL (~10¹⁰ programs, 120 tasks, Wilcoxon) vs steepest-ascent, evolutionary, and settlement-aware adversaries.
  3. Real models. §8 Claude Haiku 4.5 generates 32–48 samples/problem; weak Haiku-4.5 vs strong Sonnet-4.6 judges score code without execution; MBPP/HumanEval test execution is gold; saturated problems excluded.
  4. Real RL. §8.2 GRPO on Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct (QLoRA, group 8, 400 steps, one M1 16GB) — four arms identical except label source: frozen RM / anchored settlement / execution / online-judge, isolating reality from online-ness.
  5. Metric & benchmark. §9 Snd@N = E[G(verifier pick)]/E[G(oracle pick)], headline = AUC over log N; RSR-Bench freezes claims at date T, settles after T.

  • Selection, not persuasion, manufactures hacking: picking the max-(judge−gold) candidate from 32 honest samples yields a +0.527 hacking gap; an instructed adversary fails to inflate the judge at all (−0.209, p=3×10⁻¹³).
  • Theory falsified and repaired in-paper: the closed-form N1/ρ² penalty overstates practical budgets 8–24×; the repaired Gaussian-copula law predicts real-judge soundness with 4.1% median error vs 0.793 for Pearson.
  • A learned settlement head cuts judge pricing drift 59% (0.352→0.146); naive in-context anchoring makes the judge significantly worse (p<10⁻⁹).
  • Label efficiency did not transfer: on-policy settlement wins 10× in synthetic worlds (150 on-policy labels beat 1,500 random) but loses on real code (0.144 vs 0.170 AUC, p=0.64).
  • Registered failures disclosed: the drift alarm R-D failed outright (ρs=0.06 vs registered ≥0.5); criteria H2b and RL3c were mis-specified.

Strengths

优势
  • Turns "reward models get Goodharted" into an exact exchange-rate law — proved, simulation-checked, and repaired into a copula version with 4% median error on real judges.
  • Clean mechanistic dissection: selection (+0.527), not persuasion (−0.209), is the operative threat — LLM-judge reward defenses should target sampling, not deception.
  • Methodological gold standard: pre-registered adversarial replication built to break the authors' own claims, with falsifications reported and repaired in the same paper.
  • The four-arm GRPO design elegantly isolates the label source (reality vs judge) from online-ness and budget.
  • Snd@N prices verifiers under the pressure they will actually face; five numbered, refutable predictions any frontier lab can test in days.

Limitations

局限
  • Core demonstrations are synthetic — six- and ten-token DSLs, ridge/GBM verifiers, search adversaries — run on a single CPU.
  • Real suite covers one generator family and two saturated code benchmarks; selection pressure only reaches N=32 there.
  • Anchoring narrows but does not close the gap to a sound verifier, and was never tested against an adversary trained end-to-end against settlement.
  • The paradigm's built-in drift alarm failed its first registered test (ρs=0.06).
  • PCC's open problems are unsolved: claim-language expressiveness, non-manipulable scoring rules, world-model cost, and a trusted computing base that relocates rather than eliminates trust.

Questions for Further Exploration

值得追问的问题
Does the copula law hold for frontier judges on frontier-difficulty tasks, or does strong-judge robustness vanish at its own capability frontier?
Can settlement survive an adversary trained end-to-end against it — does the trusted computing base become the new attack surface?
Why does on-policy settlement win 10× per-task in synthetic worlds but lose cross-problem on real code?
What minimal world-model expressiveness stops anchoring from being tail-perverse — and can a drift statistic that actually rank-tracks hacking be built?
Would the frozen-vs-anchored contrast replicate at frontier scale, where the 90%-collapse regime may never be reached?
Can retrodiction gyms supply millions of settled claims without the world model overfitting publishable predictability rather than reality?

Credibility: two independent researchers; every scaled experiment pre-registered; null and falsified results reported with the same prominence as confirmations; all real-code runs on a single Apple M1 16GB, GRPO subject a 1.5B model.
