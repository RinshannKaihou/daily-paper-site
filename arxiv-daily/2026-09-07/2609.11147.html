Why this matters

A working blueprint for self-validating science agents — generalist reasoning plus a domain specialist, with reflection fired by negative evidence.

⚠ Critical edge

End-to-end evidence is three author-picked successes — no failure cases, no success-rate statistics: ARCHE could be 3-for-3 or 3-for-300.

Methodology

propose → execute → reflect → revise
hypothesize→plan workflow→run Gaussian→reflect→revise↺

ARCHE treats mechanistic inquiry as a reasoning–validation cycle. A retrieval agent pulls background from ~50 related papers via local RAG; a hypothesis agent brainstorms and ranks competing mechanisms (e.g., 25 raw strategies narrowed to 6 representative activation modes); a planner agent decomposes each into executable quantum-chemistry workflows — conformer sampling, TS optimization with frequency/IRC verification, thermochemistry, TD-DFT excited states, relaxed dihedral scans.

Whenever a step touches Gaussian 16, the domain specialist ARCHE-Chem refines the route section, interprets outputs, and diagnoses failures. Reflection modules check logical coherence and mechanistic plausibility; evidence–claim mismatches trigger targeted revision of hypotheses or workflows until a chemically consistent conclusion emerges. Evaluation tasks and case content were excluded from all training data.

Protocols escalate by case: B3LYP/6-31G(d)+IEFPCM with M06-2X/def2-TZVPP refinements (Case 1); B3LYP-D3(BJ)/def2-SVP+SMD with TD-DFT (Case 2); ωB97X-D/def2-SVP+SMD(DMA) (Case 3).

ARCHE-Chem · training recipe

Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, full-parameter continual pretraining on 105 textbooks/manuals + 432 expert forum threads (~286 MB); SFT on ~50k instruction pairs across basics, route generation, result analysis, error handling; Best-of-8 inference-time selection via a Gemma2-27B reward model trained on 28k preference pairs.

Case 1 · Reliability

Reconstructed the 2016 Houk stereocontrolling TS analysis unaided: located 12 transition states — the 9 reported plus 3 previously unreported higher-energy candidates (ΔΔG‡ 4.8 / 5.2 / 8.4 kcal/mol).

Backbone RMSD 0.01 Å (major TS) / 0.15 Å (minor); computed ΔΔG‡ 1.9 vs 1.6 kcal/mol implied by the experimental 88% ee; zero manual intervention, <24 h.

Case 2 · Discovery

Unpublished α-iodoboronate C–I cleavage (83% yield, 450 nm light). Negative results — a 46.0 kcal/mol concerted barrier and too-high excitation energies — triggered reflection, converging on a CsPPh2-complex photoinduced radical pathway (ΔGassoc = −3.0 kcal/mol).

Case 3 · Abstraction

Ni-catalyzed migratory cross-coupling: among candidate descriptors (bite angle, Ni–H BDE, dihedral), relaxed scans showed only the Br–N–N–H dihedral distortion energy tracks the hydride-migration coordinate.

Ortho-methyl bipyridine pays the lowest torsional penalty → migratory product; parent/meta/para ligands pay more → in-situ product. One interpretable descriptor reproduces the observed ligand regioselectivity trend — trend-level, not ratio-predictive.

Strengths

  • Genuine closed-loop self-correction — Case 2's negative evidence produced a hypothesis outside the initial set, discovery rather than workflow replay.
  • Quantitative reproduction, not vibes: RMSD 0.01 Å plus physically sensible unreported TSs indicate systematic landscape exploration, not target recall.
  • Case 3's descriptor converts a computation-heavy question into a cheap, interpretable screening coordinate for ligand choice.
  • Careful contamination hygiene; transparent disclosure of GPT-5 dependence and unequal Best-of-N evaluation.
  • Open code harness and unusually complete SI (full agent transcripts, all Gaussian routes, per-TS energetics) enable post-hoc audit.

Limitations

  • Only three end-to-end demonstrations, all successful and author-selected — effective pipeline reliability is unknown.
  • All reasoning runs on GPT-5 via API: unauditable, version-drifting; exact reproduction impossible.
  • Gaussian-16-centric — ORCA / Q-Chem / ML potentials asserted as "blueprint," untested.
  • Execution-category margins are small (+1.30 / +3.90 pts) and won under Bo-8 vs single-shot; decisively better only on route generation.
  • Case 2 validated by consistency, not by spectroscopic detection of the intermediate, at a modest TD-DFT level; Case 3 descriptor is trend-only.
  • No compute cost, token consumption, or reflection-iteration counts — the "24 hours" claim is hard to generalize.

Questions for Further Exploration

where to push next
  1. What is the end-to-end success rate over ~50 published mechanisms with withheld answers — and how often does the loop converge on a wrong-but-plausible mechanism?
  2. How much of Case 1 survives with an open-weights generalist, isolating the dependence on GPT-5's parametric memory of the literature?
  3. Can the dihedral descriptor predict regioselectivity for new bipyridine substituents prospectively, before synthesis?
  4. Does Best-of-8 selection actually beat prompting GPT-5.5 with the same Gaussian manual as retrieved context — the missing system-level ablation?
  5. Are Case 2's excited-state conclusions robust to CAS/NEVPT2 or SAC-CI checks, given the ~0.4 kcal/mol agreement margin?
  6. Can the framework report calibrated uncertainty, so chemists know when "converged" means strong evidence vs an exhausted hypothesis list?
