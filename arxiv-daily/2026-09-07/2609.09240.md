> **一句话总结：** OneBit AI 将 KOTMS 旋转 + E2M-ATQ 自适应三值化 + GPTQ 误差补偿的后训练量化流水线从 Qwen3-4B 扩展到 Qwen3-8B（W1.58A16，仅 252 个线性投影，有效约 1.64 bit/权重），在 8 项零样本任务上保持 78.5% 经机会校正的能力（对比 FP16 平均 72.4% vs 64.6%），比匹配协议下的 4B 高 8.9 个百分点；格点感知无损打包把 16.9 GB checkpoint 压到 8.24 GiB 且困惑度几乎不变，并在单张 RTX 5070 上实现 15.52 tokens/s 的直接打包执行（7.35 GiB 内存），但原型 GEMV 内核仍比 FP16 cuBLAS 慢 4.6 倍。

> **TL;DR:** A technical report from OneBit AI scaling an aggressive post-training ternarization pipeline (KOTMS rotation + E2M-ATQ adaptive ternary + GPTQ compensation, W1.58A16 on the 252 linear projections only) from Qwen3-4B to Qwen3-8B on a single 12 GB RTX 5070. The conversion reproduces the published TWLA W1.58 zero-shot average to within 0.05 points (62.00 vs 62.05). On eight zero-shot tasks (n=500), mean accuracy is 64.6% vs 72.4% for FP16 — 78.5% chance-corrected retention, +8.9 points over the matched 4B run (69.6%). Three-corpus mean perplexity ratio is 1.361x (WikiText-2 1.318x, C4 1.393x, PTB 1.371x). Lattice-aware packing shrinks the checkpoint from 16.9 GB to 8.24 GiB with perplexity unchanged to 4-5 decimal places, and direct packed execution runs at 15.52 tok/s in 7.35 GiB — but the prototype packed GEMV kernel is still 4.6x slower than FP16 cuBLAS on a 4096x2560 shape. The value is the honest end-to-end scale-up characterization (reproduction gate, de-confounded 4B/8B comparison, effective-bit accounting, lossless packing, execution feasibility), not new quantization algorithms.

## 核心贡献

- **通过外部复现门（reproduction gate）验证的 8B 扩展**：将 KOTMS 旋转 + E2M-ATQ 自适应三值化 + GPTQ 误差补偿的纯后训练（PTQ）流水线应用于 Qwen3-8B 的全部 252 个线性投影（attention q/k/v/o 与 MLP gate/up/down），复现 TWLA 发表的 W1.58 零样本平均分至 0.05 分以内（62.00 对 62.05），端到端验证了整套评估与溯源流程。
- **去混杂的 4B/8B 匹配缩放对比**：在统一协议（percdamp=0.01、n=500、相同八任务）下重新评测 4B checkpoint，得到 8B 的机会校正能力保持率 78.5% 对 4B 的 69.6%（+8.9 分），且 8B 优势集中在知识密集型任务（ARC-Easy +29.2、LAMBADA +18.2、ARC-Challenge +16.9、MMLU +10.8），表明更大模型对激进离散化更鲁棒。
- **有效的比特率核算与无损格点打包**：明确区分"信息预算"与"磁盘占用"——线性权重有效约 1.64 bit/权重（86.5% 权重单平面、3.6% 双平面），而完整 checkpoint 还含 FP16 的 embedding/LM head/norm 等；格点感知序列化把 16.9 GB 的伪量化 checkpoint 压到 8.24 GiB，WikiText-2 困惑度仅差 0.000085（约 0.0007%）。
- **首次直接打包执行**：打包运行时直接消费打包权重，在单张 RTX 5070 上达到 15.52 tokens/s、7.35 GiB 内存，取代此前约 1.48 tokens/s 的 CPU 卸载 regime；同时用微基准诚实标定差距——原型 Triton 打包 GEMV 在 4096×2560 形状上比 FP16 cuBLAS 慢 4.6 倍。
- **少见的科学诚实与可复现性防护**：附录给出"解释护栏"（明确列出禁止的过度声明，如"整个模型是 1.64 bit/参数"或"三值普遍快于 FP16"），并记录了真实失败模式（错误模型 ID、伪三值 artifact、显存泄漏、断电恢复），形成一套 provenance 检查清单。

## Key Contributions

- **Validated 8B scale-up through an external reproduction gate**: applies the pure-PTQ pipeline (KOTMS structured orthogonal rotation, E2M-ATQ adaptive ternarization with four disjoint salience masks at 0.5%/3.1%/10.0%/86.5% coverage, GPTQ-style inverse-Hessian error compensation with 64 samples x 2048 tokens, seed 0, percdamp=0.01) to all 252 linear projections of Qwen3-8B, reproducing the published TWLA W1.58 zero-shot average to within 0.05 points (62.00 vs 62.05).
- **De-confounded matched 4B vs 8B capability comparison**: re-benchmarks the 4B checkpoint under the identical protocol, yielding 78.5% chance-corrected retention at 8B vs 69.6% at 4B (+8.9 points), with the 8B advantage concentrated in knowledge-heavy tasks (ARC-Easy +29.2, LAMBADA +18.2, ARC-Challenge +16.9, MMLU +10.8) — evidence that larger models absorb aggressive rounding better, though only two sizes are tested.
- **Effective-bit accounting and lossless lattice-aware packing**: separates information budget (approx. 1.64 bits/weight for the quantized linear weights, B_eff ≈ (1+f2)·log2(3)) from on-disk size; lattice-aware serialization that persists discrete supports plus scales/offsets cuts the checkpoint from 16.9 GB to 8.24 GiB with WikiText-2 perplexity differing by only 0.000085 (approx. 0.0007%), packing in about 101 seconds.
- **Direct packed execution on a 12 GB consumer GPU**: the packed runtime consumes packed weights directly and reaches 15.52 tokens/s in 7.35 GiB on an RTX 5070, versus roughly 1.48 tokens/s under the previous CPU-offload regime (a 10.49x regime change, explicitly NOT claimed as an FP16 speedup); a 4096x2560 GEMV microbenchmark honestly locates the remaining gap — packed Triton kernel 0.2082 ms vs FP16 cuBLAS 0.0451 ms (4.6x slower), with chunk-unpack+cuBLAS worst at 0.8924 ms.
- **Reproducibility and provenance safeguards as first-class contributions**: mandatory state-key checks, measurable weight deltas, and end-to-end perplexity reproduction — motivated by documented failures (a script with 4B identifiers silently producing "8B" numbers; a never-ternarized artifact that looked low-bit; a misplaced no_grad decorator inflating memory from 1.99 to 10.45 GiB).

## Methodology

The study is an end-to-end systems characterization of scaling an existing PTQ stack to Qwen3-8B (36 layers, hidden 4096, intermediate 12288), explicitly disclaiming algorithmic novelty in the quantizer itself. The pipeline is: (1) **KOTMS rotation** — a structured orthogonal transform applied to 252 projections in 35 minutes to make weight distributions more ternary-friendly while controlling activation outliers; (2) **E2M-ATQ adaptive ternarization** — per-block row mean subtraction, a threshold Δ0 = 0.75·rowmean(|Wc|), a first ternary support {-1,0,+1} with least-squares scale, a residual-based second support allocated selectively by salience (96.5% of a measured block uses one plane), and alternating refinement of μ, α0, α1 with supports fixed; (3) **GPTQ compensation** — calibration-based error redistribution with approximate inverse-Hessian information, A16 activations retained so ILA-AMP is skipped. Only linear projections are converted — embeddings, LM head, norms, activations, and KV cache remain FP16, making this W1.58A16 weight-only, with an effective linear-weight budget of approx. 1.64 bits/weight. Calibration memory for the widest projection grows linearly (6.39 GiB at 64 samples to 25.56 GiB at 256), so 64 WikiText-2 samples is the reliable ceiling on the 12 GB card. Evaluation covers: an external reproduction gate against the published TWLA reference; three-corpus perplexity (WikiText-2, C4, PTB); eight zero-shot tasks at n=500 with chance-corrected retention R = (As − B)/(At − B) where B is the larger of chance and majority-class floors; a matched-protocol 4B re-benchmark to isolate scale; representation accounting; lattice-aware packing with fidelity verification; direct packed execution; and a GEMV kernel microbenchmark. Hardware is one RTX 5070 (12,227 MiB VRAM) with about 31 GB system RAM on Windows, using lm-evaluation-harness with local patches for per-layer checkpointing and resumability.

## Results & Highlights

- **Reproduction gate**: the converted W1.58A16 zero-shot average is 62.00 vs the published TWLA reference 62.05 (−0.08%, within 0.05 points), but the local FP16 baseline is 7.9% off the published FP16 (WikiText-2 PPL 9.715 vs 9.00), so the authors correctly report absolute scores as safer than converted/FP16 ratios.
- **Perplexity**: the 8B conversion reaches a 1.361x mean three-corpus ratio — WikiText-2 12.801 vs 9.709 FP16 (1.318x), C4 21.271 vs 15.275 (1.393x), PTB 23.517 vs 17.157 (1.371x) — inside the pre-registered 1.25–1.45 range, with WikiText-2 most favorable because it is also the calibration corpus.
- **Capability**: on eight zero-shot tasks (n=500), mean accuracy is 64.6% vs 72.4% for FP16, i.e. 78.5% chance-corrected retention at a 7.8-point absolute cost; the largest per-task drops are MMLU 75.55→61.0 (−14.55), ARC-Challenge 55.8→45.0 (−10.8), ARC-Easy 81.6→72.8 (−8.8), and HellaSwag 64.4→56.0 (−8.4), while BoolQ degrades least (86.2→83.8, −2.4).
- **Matched scaling**: the 8B model retains 78.5% vs 69.6% for the matched 4B run (+8.9 points, winning 6 of 8 tasks — biggest gains ARC-Easy +29.2, LAMBADA +18.2, ARC-Challenge +16.9, MMLU +10.8; the two losses, HellaSwag −2.2 and PIQA −8.5, are tasks where 4B already retained highly); all six reported MMLU mathematics subjects stay above the 25% chance floor (e.g. elementary math 54.2 vs FP16 70.1; abstract algebra 40.0 vs 57.0), unlike near-destruction at smaller scale.
- **Packing fidelity**: lattice-aware packing reduces the checkpoint from 16.9 GB to 8.24 GiB with WikiText-2 PPL 12.758265→12.758180 (difference 0.000085, approx. 0.0007%) and PTB 23.517294→23.516298 (0.000996), at approx. 0.036% relative RMSE and about 101 s of packing time; conversely, a post-hoc lattice fitted to the already-saved FP16 tensor fails catastrophically (50.5% relative error, PPL approx. 18,477), proving serialization must be designed into the quantizer.
- **Execution**: direct packed execution reaches 15.52 tok/s in 7.35 GiB vs roughly 1.48 tok/s under CPU offload (a 10.49x regime change, not an FP16 comparison — the FP16 8B model did not fit the 12 GB card); the prototype packed Triton GEMV at 4096×2560 takes 0.2082 ms vs 0.0451 ms for FP16 cuBLAS (4.6x slower), with chunk-unpack+cuBLAS at 0.8924 ms.

## Authors & Credibility

The authors — Anirudh Malik, Poojith Devan, and M Sparsh Mehra — are from "OneBit AI", which appears to be an independent/industry research group rather than an academic institution; this is a technical research report, not peer-reviewed venue work. It is the third in their internal series (after a 0.8B QAT study and the Qwen3-4B PTQ study). Credibility is unusually high for an unreviewed report: the paper pre-registers an acceptance range for perplexity ratios, passes an external reproduction gate against the published TWLA Qwen3-8B numbers, includes an explicit novelty boundary ("we do not claim these algorithms as new"), an appendix of interpretation guardrails listing claims that must NOT be made, and a candid ledger of project failures (wrong model identifiers, false ternary artifacts, a no_grad bug, a power loss costing 2.5 hours). No code release is mentioned, and no affiliations beyond the group name are given, which limits external verification.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: genuinely low on the algorithmic axis, and the authors say so — KOTMS, E2M-ATQ, and GPTQ are all prior art (TWLA is ref [10], and Qwen3-8B is the TWLA reference model, so this reads partly as a reproduction/extension of it). The contribution is the systems-level scale-up characterization, which is real but incremental.
- **Rigor**: strong internal rigor (matched protocols, chance-corrected retention, provenance assertions, pre-registered ranges), but several soft spots: the scaling claim rests on only two sizes and one seed — the authors themselves forbid calling it a scaling law; WikiText-2 serves simultaneously as calibration corpus, reproduction-gate metric, and one of three perplexity corpora, and the paper's own data (1.318x on WikiText-2 vs 1.393x on C4) quantifies that calibration-domain favorability; capability sampling is n=500 per task, so per-task deltas of a few points (e.g. WinoGrande −3.6) are within noise bands they never report.
- **Baseline weakness**: there is no head-to-head comparison against any competing ternary PTQ method (PTQTP, CAT-Q) under the identical protocol — limitation #8 in their own list — so "state of the art" positioning is impossible; the only external anchor is reproducing TWLA, not beating it. The 7.9% local-vs-published FP16 discrepancy is disclosed but unexplained (hardware/harness differences presumed), which undermines ratio-based cross-paper comparisons.
- **Reproducibility**: methodology is described in enough detail to re-run (seeds, percdamp, sample counts, thresholds, memory ceilings), but no code or artifacts are released, the group has no verifiable institutional identity, and single-consumer-GPU-specific patches (Windows VRAM caps, per-layer checkpointing) are only sketched.
- **Red flags / overclaim management**: the marketing-adjacent "1.58-bit" framing is repeatedly defused by the authors themselves (effective 1.64 bits/weight on linear weights only; embeddings/LM head/KV cache stay FP16), and the 10.49x execution improvement is a CPU-offload-elimination effect, not a kernel win — the honest 4.6x kernel deficit is the more load-bearing systems number. The perplexity ratio of 1.361x mean is a substantial quality cost that limits near-term deployment despite the storage win.

## Strengths

- Exceptional measurement honesty: pre-registered acceptance ranges, an external reproduction gate (62.00 vs 62.05), explicit guardrails against overclaiming, and published failure modes that would embarrass most reports.
- The de-confounded 4B/8B matched re-benchmark is the right experimental design and yields the paper's most interesting scientific signal — robustness to aggressive PTQ grows with scale (+8.9 retention points, concentrated in knowledge tasks).
- Completes the full deployment story that most quantization papers skip: not just accuracy, but actual serialization (16.9 GB → 8.24 GiB, perplexity preserved to 0.0007%) and direct packed execution (15.52 tok/s in 7.35 GiB on a 12 GB consumer card).
- The effective-bit accounting (B_eff ≈ (1+f2)·log2(3) ≈ 1.64 bits/weight vs the naive 1.585) and the post-hoc packing failure (PPL approx. 18,477) are genuinely instructive lessons for practitioners.

## Limitations

- One seed and n=500 samples per task; no variance estimates, so small task-level deltas are statistically fragile.
- Only two model sizes (4B, 8B) — the "scaling improves ternary robustness" conclusion is a two-point anecdote, not a law.
- WikiText-2 is both the calibration corpus and a headline evaluation corpus, flattering the perplexity story; broader calibration (e.g. FineWeb-Edu) is deferred to future work.
- Partial quantization only: embeddings, LM head, norms, activations, and KV cache remain FP16, so the "1.58-bit model" label materially overstates compression of the deployed artifact.
- The packed GEMV kernel is a prototype, 4.6x slower than FP16 cuBLAS on the tested shape, and only one shape/batch configuration is benchmarked.
- No identical-protocol comparison against competing ternary PTQ methods (PTQTP, CAT-Q), and the 7.9% local FP16 baseline discrepancy is unexplained.
- Perplexity degradation (1.361x mean) and the 7.8-point accuracy cost are large in absolute terms — the artifact is a research baseline, not a drop-in replacement.

## Relevance

这篇报告直接命中"LLM 压缩/量化 + 端侧部署"的兴趣点：它给出了 Qwen3-8B 级别模型三值化 PTQ 的完整可复现基线（能力保持、无损打包、消费级 12 GB 显卡直接执行的吞吐与内存数字），对评估 1.58-bit 类方法的实际落地边界（存储收益 vs 困惑度代价 vs 内核成熟度）非常有参考价值。其"模型越大对激进量化越鲁棒"的两点证据也值得在更大规模上追问。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the 8B retention advantage continue monotonically at 14B/32B, or does it saturate — and does the knowledge-task concentration of gains (MMLU +10.8 retention points) persist when calibration moves off WikiText-2 to FineWeb-Edu?
- Can the packed GEMV close the 4.6x gap to FP16 cuBLAS with shape-specialized kernels or Marlin/Triton autotuning, and what batch-size regime flips the sign in favor of the ternary path?
- How much retention can be recovered by also quantizing embeddings and the LM head (or the KV cache), given that the current 8.24 GiB artifact is still dominated by those FP16 components relative to a true 1.64-bit budget?
- What does multi-seed variance actually look like at n=500 — are the small per-task deltas (BoolQ −2.4, WinoGrande −3.6) stable, and how sensitive is E2M-ATQ's salience-mask coverage (0.5%/3.1%/10.0%/86.5%) to calibration seed?
- Under an identical protocol, how does this TWLA-derived pipeline compare against PTQTP and CAT-Q on the same Qwen3-8B checkpoint — is the reproduction gate's closeness (62.00 vs 62.05) specific to reusing TWLA's exact configuration?
