> **一句话总结：** 提出基于参考模型的隐藏态偏置审计方法：用 1000 条锚点句子把微调前后两个模型的目标组/属性句嵌入映射到共享的相对表示空间，计算"表征偏移 ΔB"；在 Llama-3.1-8B、Mistral-7B、Gemma-3-4B 的全参/LoRA 微调+模型合并谱系上，ΔB 与 WildGuardMix/DecodingTrust/ToxiGen 三类输出级偏置变化在 18 组设置中 15 组显著共变（最高 |r|=0.84, p<0.001），阈值化 ΔB 检测"偏置升高"检查点的 ROC AUC 达 0.65–0.99，且每个模型约 3 分钟、比输出级基准省 3–50 倍算力。

> **TL;DR:** Fine-tuning can silently shift an LLM's biases, and output-level audits (benchmarks, LLM judges) are expensive and may miss internal drift. This paper audits bias directly in hidden states: since fine-tuned and base models' embedding spaces aren't directly comparable, both are projected into a shared "relative representation" space via cosine similarities to 1,000 neutral anchor sentences, and a Representational Bias Shift ΔB measures how far target-group sentences move toward negative vs. positive attributes relative to the reference model. Validated on 7-checkpoint merge spectra (harmful↔unharmful WildGuardMix fine-tunes) of Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3 and Gemma-3-4b-it, ΔB co-varies with output-level bias change in 15 of 18 settings — peaking at Pearson r = −0.84 (p < 0.001) for Llama on DecodingTrust under full fine-tuning — and a threshold classifier on ΔB flags increased-bias checkpoints at ROC AUC 0.65–0.99, beating a SEAT baseline (0.964 vs 0.778 on WildGuardMix for Llama) while needing ~3 minutes per model versus 9–156 minutes for the benchmarks. Honest caveats: the signal weakens under LoRA (Gemma's WildGuardMix correlation collapses to r = −0.04), bias was induced synthetically via checkpoint merging, and the authors position ΔB as complementary to, not a replacement for, output-based auditing.

## 核心贡献

- **相对表示空间的参考模型审计框架**：将 SEAT（Sentence Encoder Association Test）从"单模型绝对嵌入"扩展为"审计模型 vs 参考模型"的对比式审计。针对微调重塑隐空间几何、绝对隐藏态不可跨模型比较的问题，借鉴 Moschella et al. (2023) 的相对表示：每个句子用其与固定 1000 条锚点句子的余弦相似度向量编码，两个模型无需拟合任何跨模型映射即落入同一坐标系。
- **定义表征偏移指标 ΔB（Representational Bias Shift）**：在共享相对空间中用（取负的）欧氏距离度量目标组句子与正/负属性句集的关联，ΔB = B_aud − B_ref，每个目标组一个分数；ΔB 为负即该组相对参考模型向负面属性移动（偏置升高）。
- **大规模实证验证**：三个模型家族（Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct、Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3、Gemma-3-4b-it）× 两种微调制度（全参/LoRA）× 三个输出级偏置基准（WildGuardMix 有害性、DecodingTrust 刻板印象、ToxiGen 毒性），并通过 harmful/unharmful 检查点的线性合并构造 7 档渐变谱系，使偏置以增量而非单点跳变方式引入；ΔB 在 18 组设置中 15 组与输出级偏置变化显著共变。
- **强基线对比与全面消融**：相对表示优于 SEAT、Procrustes 对齐版 SEAT 与 CKA drift 三种替代方案；ΔB 对锚点来源与数量、属性句措辞（6 种变体）、目标句模板（6 种变体）、token 池化方式和随机种子均稳定。
- **极低成本的实用审计工具**：无需任务标注数据，单卡 A100 40GB 上约 3 分钟审完一个模型，比所用输出级基准省 3–50 倍算力；代码开源（github.com/NASK-AISafety/Reference-Based-Bias-Detection）。

## Key Contributions

- A reference-based auditing framework that projects an audited model and a reference model into a shared comparison space through relative hidden-state representations (similarities to a shared anchor set), removing the need for any fitted cross-model map.
- The Representational Bias Shift ΔB: a per-target-group scalar summarizing how far a group's association with positive vs. negative attributes moved relative to the reference model, computable from final-layer mean-pooled hidden states.
- A graded validation protocol: instead of a single before/after jump, harmful and unharmful WildGuardMix fine-tunes are linearly merged at five interpolation ratios, yielding a 7-point severity spectrum per model and regime (full fine-tuning and LoRA) across three model families.
- Empirical validation showing ΔB tracks output-level bias change (WildGuardMix harmfulness, DecodingTrust stereotype agreement, ToxiGen toxicity) in 15 of 18 settings, with detection ROC AUCs of 0.65–0.99.
- Evidence that the relative representation itself — not shared-space alignment — drives the gain: Procrustes-SEAT (orthogonally aligned SEAT) sits at chance, while RR reaches ROC AUC 0.964 vs 0.778 for plain SEAT on WildGuardMix (Llama).

## Methodology

The method extends the Sentence Encoder Association Test (SEAT) to a two-model, reference-based setting. For each sentence x, its embedding e(x) is the mean of final-layer hidden-state vectors across tokens (layer 32 for Llama/Mistral, 34 for Gemma; max/last pooling ablated). Because fine-tuning reshapes latent geometry, absolute embeddings of the audited model M_aud and reference model M_ref are not comparable, so both are re-encoded as relative representations: r(x) = [cos(e(x), e(a_i))] over a shared anchor set A of 1,000 neutral, in-domain sentences about social groups (word anchors, Alpaca and Tulu samples ablated). Within this shared space, associations between a target sentence s and positive/negative attribute sets P, N are measured with negated Euclidean distance (Euclidean rather than cosine because r(x)'s coordinates are already similarities); the bias score B_rel = mean_s [S⁺_rel(s) − S⁻_rel(s)], and ΔB = B_aud − B_ref, instantiated per target group. A negative ΔB reads as increased bias.

Validation design: each of Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3, Gemma-3-4b-it is fine-tuned on an unharmful and a synthetic-harmful 8k split of WildGuardMix (3 epochs, effective batch 32; LoRA r=16, α=32, lr 1e-4; full FT lr 2e-5), and the two checkpoints are linearly merged at 10/90, 30/70, 50/50, 70/30, 90/10 ratios, giving seven checkpoints spanning safe→harmful. Each checkpoint is scored by three external output-level measures: WildGuardMix social-stereotypes harmfulness (scored by allenai/wildguard, prompts mapped by ChatGPT 5.2 onto 9 topics consolidated from DecodingTrust's 24 groups), DecodingTrust stereotype agreement, and ToxiGen per-group toxicity (TOXIGEN_ROBERTA classifier). Paired (ΔBias Score, ΔB) observations pooled over checkpoints and groups yield Pearson correlations with two-tailed significance and bootstrap MAE, plus ROC AUC of a threshold classifier on ΔB (operating points 0.1 / 0.1 / 0.03). All experiments ran on one NVIDIA A100 40GB.

## Results & Highlights

- **Correlation with output-level bias:** ΔB co-varies significantly with external bias change in 15 of 18 settings. Strongest cases under full fine-tuning: DecodingTrust, Mistral r = −0.82 (p < 0.001) and Llama r = −0.84 (p < 0.001); WildGuardMix full FT: Mistral −0.67***, Llama −0.68***, Gemma −0.37**. Failures: Mistral on ToxiGen full FT (r = −0.19, p = 0.14, attributed to toxicity saturation compressing the spectrum) and Gemma under LoRA on WildGuardMix (r = −0.04).
- **Detection of increased-bias checkpoints:** thresholding ΔB reaches ROC AUC 0.93 (Mistral) / 0.89 (Llama) / 0.78 (Gemma) on WildGuardMix full FT, 0.91 for Llama on DecodingTrust full FT, and up to 0.99 (Mistral, DecodingTrust LoRA); the overall range across all 18 settings is 0.65–0.99.
- **RR beats alternatives (Llama, full FT):** ROC AUC 0.964 for relative representations vs 0.778 for the SEAT-based baseline on WildGuardMix; Procrustes-SEAT (explicit orthogonal alignment) stays at chance (~0.5), proving the gain comes from the relative encoding rather than space alignment; CKA drift detects well (0.864 WildGuardMix, 0.753 DecodingTrust) but stays below RR, so ΔB is not reducible to undirected representational displacement.
- **Robustness ablations:** mean ROC AUC 0.863 ± 0.030 across six attribute-set constructions and 0.902 ± 0.008 across six target templates; neutral-sentence anchors are best (ROC AUC 0.892 at 1k anchors) but out-of-domain anchors (word, Alpaca, Tulu) remain discriminative; ordering RR > SEAT holds under mean/max/last pooling, and repeated training with different seeds yields nearly identical ΔB.
- **Cost:** ~3 minutes per audited model on a single A100 40GB, versus 9–14 min (WildGuardMix harmfulness), 33–76 min (ToxiGen), 44–156 min (DecodingTrust) — i.e., the output-level benchmarks cost 3–50× more compute, and ΔB needs no task-specific annotation, so new target groups are nearly free.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** Incremental but well-executed. The core ingredients — SEAT-style association tests and Moschella et al.'s relative representations — both exist; the contribution is the combination into a reference-based ΔB and a careful validation protocol. The Procrustes-SEAT control isolating "alignment vs relative encoding" is a genuinely nice piece of analysis.
- **Rigor:** The graded merge spectrum (7 checkpoints) is a thoughtful alternative to binary before/after, and the ablation suite (anchors, attributes, templates, pooling, seeds) is above average. However, the pooled correlation observations are not fully independent: all seven checkpoints are linear interpolations of the same two fine-tuned endpoints, which can inflate apparent correlation and makes the effective sample much smaller than the number of pooled (checkpoint, group) pairs suggests. No per-checkpoint holdout or cluster-aware significance is reported.
- **Synthetic bias induction:** All bias shifts are manufactured (fine-tuning on a synthetic harmful split plus model merging). Whether ΔB tracks bias drift arising from *naturally occurring* fine-tuning — domain SFT, RLHF, distillation — is untested, and the authors admit this. This is the biggest external-validity gap.
- **Label pipeline quirk:** WildGuardMix prompts carry no target-group labels, so the authors used ChatGPT 5.2 to classify prompts into 9 consolidated topics (from DecodingTrust's 24 groups) — an LLM-generated labelling step whose noise is never quantified, and the 24→9 consolidation discards group granularity on one of three benchmarks.
- **Weak spots acknowledged but real:** Gemma-3-4B is the weakest case on all three benchmarks and both regimes (LoRA WildGuardMix r = −0.04); the scale explanation (half the parameters of the 7–8B models) is speculative and admitted to be incomplete — tokenisation and final-layer geometry were "controlled for neither". Mistral's full-FT ToxiGen correlation also misses significance (r = −0.19).
- **Overclaiming check:** Refreshingly restrained. The paper repeatedly frames ΔB as a proxy, refuses causal and single-model-certification readings, warns that a differentiable ΔB can be Goodharted (a model optimized to keep ΔB small need not be less biased), and positions the method as complementary to output-based auditing.
- **Reproducibility:** Good — open code, fixed sentence sets in appendices, full fine-tuning hyperparameters, single-GPU budget, and operating thresholds (0.1 / 0.03) stated explicitly.

## Strengths

- Solves the actual comparability problem (fine-tuning reshapes latent geometry) with an elegant, map-free construction, and proves with the Procrustes-SEAT control that the benefit comes from relative representations rather than mere alignment.
- The graded merge spectrum turns "did bias change?" into a dose-response-style question, giving far more statistical leverage than a single fine-tuning pair.
- Exceptionally thorough robustness analysis: anchor source and count, attribute wording, template phrasing, pooling strategy, and random seeds — few auditing papers stress-test their metric this way.
- Practical efficiency: ~3 minutes and no annotated evaluation data per audit, 3–50× cheaper than the benchmarks considered, making per-checkpoint monitoring during fine-tuning plausible.
- Honest framing throughout: proxy-not-proof, relative-not-absolute, complementary-not-replacement, plus a dual-use warning about optimizing against ΔB.

## Limitations

- ΔB is inherently relative — it cannot audit a checkpoint in isolation and requires choosing a meaningful reference model; a small ΔB only means no detected representational shift, not safety.
- The signal weakens under parameter-efficient adaptation (LoRA), where low-rank updates constrain how far hidden geometry can move, and it is weakest overall for Gemma-3-4B (r = −0.04 on WildGuardMix LoRA).
- Bias shifts were induced synthetically via harmful fine-tuning plus linear model merging; natural fine-tuning drift, larger scales (>8B), other architectures, non-English languages, and intersectional target groups are all untested (the taxonomy is the coarse single-axis DecodingTrust groups, English templates only).
- Embeddings use final-layer mean pooling, and layer/pooling selection is acknowledged to deserve systematic study; SEAT's known sensitivity to contextual-embedding instability is inherited.
- Correlation is not causation — the paper says so itself — and the merged-checkpoint design may make ΔB and output bias co-vary more tidily than they would in the wild.

## Relevance

微调（尤其是第三方/领域微调）对模型安全与偏置的隐性影响是 LLM 对齐研究的核心关切之一，本文提供了一个无需标注数据、单卡 3 分钟即可运行的隐藏态审计信号，可直接用于微调副作用的低成本监控与跨版本模型审计。其"相对表示打通不可比隐空间"的思路对内部表征分析、模型相似度度量方向的方法论也有借鉴价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does ΔB track bias shifts induced by *naturally occurring* fine-tuning (domain SFT, RLHF, distillation corpora) rather than synthetic harmful splits merged into a spectrum — and do those shifts produce the same monotone dose-response?
- Since all seven checkpoints interpolate the same two endpoints, how much do the reported correlations survive cluster-aware statistics (e.g., bootstrapping over fine-tuning runs rather than checkpoint-group pairs)?
- Which layers actually carry the bias signal — would multi-layer or intermediate-layer pooling rescue the weak Gemma and LoRA cases?
- Can ΔB be used as an early-stopping or regularization monitor during fine-tuning without being Goodharted, given the authors' own warning that a model optimized to keep ΔB small need not be less biased?
- Does the method generalize to larger scales (>8B), MoE architectures, multilingual target groups, and intersectional identities outside the 9 consolidated DecodingTrust topics?
- How noisy is the ChatGPT-based prompt-to-topic mapping on WildGuardMix, and would a human-labelled subset change the correlation?
