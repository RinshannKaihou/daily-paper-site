Key metric · causal control

CIFAR-10 accuracy drop from a mere ±2% perturbation of B around 1 — accuracy peaks exactly at the predicted boundary B = 1. Causal, not correlational.

Why this matters

Turns schedule design into first-principles control of one scalar — a lens on Muon/AdamW behavior, warmup-cosine mechanics, and late-training oscillation in LLMs.

Critical edge

Instability is proven only in an isotropic toy model — the GPT-2 runs use affine-free LayerNorm to manufacture invariance, and AdamW’s decoupled decay has no law yet.

§ Methodology

Four layers, zero approximations

  1. Polar split. Write block parameters as wt = rtut (radius × direction). Scale invariance makes the rescaled gradient ḡ(u) = r∇L(ru) radius-free; eliminating r from SGD + coupled decay yields the exact Φt recurrence in two lines of algebra.
  2. Solved model. A normalized-linear regression with isotropic covariance collapses to an exact 2D map in (alignment qt, stepsize Φt) — enabling full eigenvalue analysis.
  3. Optimizer classification. Update homogeneity ν generalizes the law: SGD/SGDM are ν=1 (quadratic self-quenching), Adam at ε→0 is ν=0 (linear); SGDM exposes a radial amplification term −2Φtct.
  4. Verify, then intervene. Track expansion fraction and recurrence residual on the exact map, BN-MLP/MNIST, BN-ConvNet/CIFAR-10, and LayerNorm GPT-2; then invert the law — synthesize ηt+1 to clamp Bt ≡ B and sweep.

Theorem 3.4. The unique interior fixed point lies exactly on the switching surface and is an unstable spiral source — Jacobian eigenvalue modulus √(1+a−a²) > 1 for all a ∈ (0,1), with an exact period-two orbit on the sphere. Oscillation under constant LR + weight decay is discrete-time geometry, not noise. (schematic)

§ Verdict · side by side

What convinces

  • Exact, not asymptotic — falsification is trivial (machine-precision residuals), and the law survives it.
  • One clean separation: schedule forcing Bt vs geometric self-quenching; constant/step/cosine become trajectories of a single scalar.
  • Dissolves folklore: recurrence under constant LR + weight decay is discrete-time geometry, not a noise or step-size artifact.
  • ν-framework mechanistically explains why Adam is more expansion-prone under normalization.
  • Intervention, not observation: clamping Bt by synthesizing schedules upgrades the claim to causation; public code, 5 seeds, honest limitations.

What it doesn’t cover

  • Global analysis is isotropic-only: anisotropic covariance and general deep networks are not covered; no limit-cycle convergence proof.
  • Block-conditional law: needs exact scale invariance — standard pre-LN GPT/LLaMA blocks (embeddings, biases, LN affine, LM head, projections) fall outside it; the affine-free LayerNorm was deliberate.
  • Coupled decay only: AdamW’s decoupled default — the most common modern recipe — has no closed-form law yet; Adam’s exactness holds at ε = 0.
  • Small empirical scale: ≤12-block GPT-2, ≤10k steps; no large-LM perplexity result, no end-to-end win over warmup-cosine shown.
§ Questions for further exploration

Open threads

  1. Can the exact law be extended to decoupled weight decay (AdamW), where no closed form currently exists?
  2. Does the spiral instability persist under anisotropic or layerwise-normalized blocks (e.g., Query-Key RMSNorm in modern LLMs)?
  3. Does Bt-targeted schedule synthesis beat warmup-cosine at LLM scale — or is the B = 1 optimum a small-CIFAR artifact?
  4. Can the ν-framework predict expansion of newer optimizers (Lion, Muon, Shampoo/SOAP) a priori from update homogeneity?
  5. Does warmup partly work by keeping the SGDM radial component ct small during transient norm growth?
  6. Does the noise-shifted surface log Bt = qt explain why larger batches tolerate larger constant learning rates?
