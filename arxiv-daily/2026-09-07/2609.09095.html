A reduced-model performance study building on the four-term Tensor–Memory Equilibrium model of companion Part 1, corrected by an NVIDIA technical memo that isolated the per-input conversion cost. Delivered rate = min(roof P_fp8/(3r+1), deconstruction term n̄·P_int/(c_q·r)); beyond one cluster’s reach R=256, operands re-split λ times, pinning the closed-form floor. Every claim carries a status tag (proved / script-checked / vendor-announced / memo-derived / modeled / measured). The only measured data: LD_PRELOAD BLAS-3 call-shape traces from LOBPCG, UMFPACK, CCSD and LAPACK.

Results — beyond the headline
  • NVIDIA’s ~200-TFLOPS “Emulated DGEMM” spec is explained by the deconstruction term alone — reproduced at n̄ ≈ 500–900 with the fp8 tensor pipes idle 58% of the time. The rival flat-~40%-efficiency reading is honestly kept alive; one size sweep with a slicing-int8 control decides.
  • The theorem-backed published set S floors at 182 TFLOPS (0.38 of roof); the ragged worst case (m=n=513) dips to 0.36, and a modeled predicated schedule recovers ≈0.54 there.
  • Even at the floor: ≈8× the ~30-TFLOPS illustrative native fp64 pipe, and ≈1.9 EFLOPS sustained fp64 on a 10,000-GPU Rubin cluster.
  • The modulus supply space is closed by exhaustive bounds — square-modulus systems cap at 94.1 bits, sub-64 systems at 89.9 bits, both short of the 111.8 bits needed. Runtime dispatch: all-byte A below n̄≈414, hybrid E to ≈616, set S on the roof from ≈670.
  • HPL worked example is NB-conditional: hybrid E reaches its floor only for panel widths NB ≳ 1024 (159 TF at NB=256 — below set S). With co-design Option C fully granted: ≈473 TF/GPU, Rmax ≈ 3.6–4.0 EFLOPS.

Strengths
  • Ceiling stated before the constructions — the 0.50 floor is drawn with the crossover in Figure 3, page 13, and every rate is labeled “achieved today / convert-once envelope.”
  • Derived, not fitted — the floor moves predictably with provisioning (doubling Rubin’s int8 tensor rate lifts it 0.50 → 0.92), which makes the co-design asks concrete and testable.
  • Measured LD_PRELOAD traces replace assumed GEMM-mix folklore with real geometry across four application classes.
  • Falsifiability engineered in: four numbered predictions orthogonal to the rival hypothesis, a slicing-int8 negative control, and a continuously-running harness (Rikyu GB200 NVL4).
  • A withdrawal ledger — earlier drafts’ wrong claims corrected in-text with the numbers that replaced them.
Limitations
  • No measured kernel — the entire quantitative case awaits the Part-3 measurement campaign.
  • Load-bearing constants (cq=16, P_int≈75 TOPS) are transferred from a GB300 memo and “presumed unchanged on Rubin”; the B200 cuBLAS attribution is unresolved.
  • The 235-TFLOPS headline is conditional on set E, NB ≳ 1024, and a 16-CTA cluster opt-in — the theorem-backed floor is 182 TFLOPS.
  • Reconstruction hosting on Rubin is a real unresolved cost: SIMT-Garner is 28.4% of MMA time at k=1000; the int8-hosted route needs k ≳ 2,270 with an unproven fp64-exactness obligation.
  • Model assumes perfect service overlap; the concurrency parameter θ is unmeasured.
  • Co-design counterfactuals require hardware that does not exist.
Questions for further exploration
  1. Does the decisive DGEMM size sweep confirm the deconstruction-limited branch (linear rise, knee at ≈1,211 / ≈730) or the flat-efficiency rival — and what does the measured cq come out to versus the memo’s 16?
  2. Can the ≈6.3-instruction SIMT residual survive SASS-level verification, or will register pressure and occupancy push realized counts back toward the shipping 16?
  3. Do the codesigned E/A modulus sets discharge their per-set accuracy obligations under adversarial scaling, preserving componentwise fp64-grade error bounds?
  4. Is the k ≈ 6,777 all-byte-A crossover reachable in any real workload, or is modulus dispatch effectively a two-route menu?
  5. Does the same cq term bind sparse kernels (SpMV/SpMM) below an intensity threshold — and can the persistent-plane mode (bandwidth ×2.83 per nnz) ever win on Rubin-class parts?
  6. Since Top500 does not currently accept emulated DGEMM for fp64 Linpack, would the HPL-scale numbers (1.9 EFLOPS per 10,000 Rubin GPUs) require a rule change — and does that politicize adoption?
