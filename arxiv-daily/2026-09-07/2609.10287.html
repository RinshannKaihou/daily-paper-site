Key Finding · 核心发现

Circuit removability is decided by the training trajectory, not the final architecture — only a smooth fade-to-zero preserves retrieval.

一句话总结：只有“平滑退火到零”的训练轨迹能让 Transformer 形成可移除的检索回路——训练路径、而非最终架构，决定回路能否在先验移除后继续工作。

Why This Matters · 为何重要

Moves interpretability from how priors shape circuit formation to whether circuits keep working once the prior is removed — with direct clues for induction-head training dynamics.

Critical Edge · 批判视角

Every positive result lives in toy models — ≤265K parameters, vocab 32, synthetic retrieval tasks. No pretrained, natural-language, or realistically scaled experiment.

Methodology · 方法

A gated prior, annealed along controlled paths

Every attention head receives gated position and content biases on its pre-softmax logits; the experiment asks whether the retrieval circuit still works once the gates close.

Sij = QiKjT/√d + gpos(t)·Bposij + gcontent(t)·Bcontentij

Bpos: learnable clipped-relative-position table · Bcontent: embedding inner product · per-head gates ∈ [0,1] — Free, Forced, or Scheduled.

  • Matched-budget race. Five paths share optimizer, data, and 3,600 steps: unforced, forced-zero, forced-const-0.5, linear 0.5→0, and the headline fade600→0 (hold 0.5 → fade over 450 steps → train gate-free).
  • Causal controls. Hard switches jump at the same endpoints; post hoc continuation adds the same zero-gate budget to a finished unforced checkpoint — ruling out warm-start and extra-training explanations.
  • Homotopy framing. Branch displacement Δt ≈ |Δg|·‖H⁻¹A‖ must stay inside the basin radius; a hard switch injects a gradient shock that need not point toward the prior-free circuit.
  • Testbeds. 2-layer/4-head d=64 (~35K params) + 4-layer d=128 (~265K); associative recall, Markov induction, linear-regression ICL; 3–5 seeds; successor-mass and zero-gate-ablation traces every 300 steps.

Zero-gate accuracy on associative recall · 5 seeds · holds in the larger 4-layer/128-dim backbone (0.731 vs 0.102 unforced).

  • The prior becomes load-bearing. Zeroing the position gate at eval drops unforced accuracy 0.794 → 0.073; zeroing the content gate is nearly neutral (0.791).
  • Timing and shape are forgiving; abruptness is not. Fade starts 400–800 all land 0.734–0.754; linear / cosine / convex schedules 0.749–0.764 — but two-step deletion collapses to 0.212 and concave is seed-unstable (0.536).
  • Replicates on Markov induction: fade keeps 0.830 / 0.920 / 0.969 at repeat probability p = 0.2 / 0.5 / 0.8.
  • Boundary case — linear-regression ICL. Forced-zero training learns it directly (loss 0.0722); the fade advantage exists only where from-scratch zero-gate training fails.
  • Delayed consolidation, shifting heads. Consolidation lands after the fade ends, and the responsible head differs by seed (L1H0 / L1H3 / L1H1) — no universal circuit.
  • No length extrapolation. The recovered circuit falls to 0.263 at T=120 (train length 32).

Strengths · 优势

Why it holds up

  • Crisp, falsifiable question — with a protocol that cleanly separates prior-active accuracy from prior-free removability.
  • Exemplary causal controls for the scale: matched budgets and endpoints, hard switches at two timings, post hoc continuation, prior dropout, dual-condition loss.
  • Robustness beyond the minimum: fade-start sweep (400–800), five schedule shapes, 4-layer/128-dim capacity check.
  • Mechanistic traces (successor mass, ablation importance) support delayed consolidation — and honestly report seed-varying head identity.
  • Actively self-bounding: the linear-ICL boundary case and explicit limitations narrow the claim instead of overgeneralizing it.

Limitations · 局限

Where it stops

  • Tiny Transformers only (~35K–265K params, vocab 32, seq len 32) on synthetic tasks — no natural-language or pretrained experiments.
  • The recovered circuit fails length extrapolation (0.263 at T=120) — removability doesn't preserve what positional priors usually buy.
  • The content gate is inert everywhere (content-only ≈ chance) — half the proposed architecture is an unused control.
  • 3–5 seeds, no significance tests; the hard-switch@1050 result carries huge variance (±0.128).
  • Fade trades away prior-active accuracy — 0.432±0.091 normal eval in the large model (vs 0.797 unforced), where linear 0.5→0 actually dominates on both metrics.
  • No code or data-generation scripts released.

Questions for Further Exploration · 延伸问题

  1. Does annealing positional biases (ALiBi slopes, GPSA locality) help or unlock removal in pretrained language models at scale?
  2. Why does zero-gate training fail from scratch on discrete retrieval — can the early-gradient account be quantified (gradient SNR toward the retrieval subspace)?
  3. Can continuation theory (Δt vs. basin radius) predict gate schedules instead of validating them by trial?
  4. Is the fade path's normal-eval drop a genuine smoothness–performance trade-off — would gate-cycling (fade, re-raise, fade) recover both?
  5. Does delayed, post-fade consolidation generalize to other annealed biases, and is its timing predictable from loss-landscape curvature?
