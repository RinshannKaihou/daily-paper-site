> **一句话总结：** 提出 SAGE 语义感知差错恢复架构，把"该不该重传"（按 BF16 位段的数值后果分 H/M/L 三级，仅灾难性 Class-H 指数高位错误触发重放）与"从哪重传"（源端局部区域表自适应缩短噪声区检查点间隔）解耦；在 gem5 Garnet 100×100 mesh、基准 BER 3×10⁻⁵ 下，相比固定 34 跳恢复，十种子配对实验将平均时延降低 28.0%（418.3→301.2 周期）、p99.5 降低 36.0%，同时投递语义质量从 0.8261 升至 0.8504，质量归一化终端时延 Ψdel 降低 30.1%，且 CRC32 资格实验给出 3.18×10⁻⁷ 的 Class-H 静默投递上界（预算 10⁻⁶）。

> **TL;DR:** AI interconnects normally retransmit every detected error uniformly, but a flipped high-order exponent bit (catastrophic NaN/outlier) and a flipped mantissa bit (≈ quantization noise) deserve different treatment. SAGE splits each 576-bit flit into Class-H (BCH(184,160,t=3)+CRC32-protected exponent bits e4–e7), Class-M (BCH(168,160,t=1) sign/lower-exponent bits), and Class-L (uncoded mantissa bits); only detected Class-H failures trigger a protected NACK plus full-flit replay, while a source-local region table halves checkpoint intervals in noisy regions and relaxes them by +5 hops after sustained clean windows. In ten-seed direct-gem5-Garnet experiments on a 100×100 mesh with uniform-random traffic at base BER 3×10⁻⁵, SAGE beats the fixed 34-hop baseline on mean latency (301.2 vs 418.3 cycles, −28.0%), p99.5 (813.0 vs 1269.5, −36.0%), drop rate (7.42% vs 11.63%), delivered quality qdel (0.8504 vs 0.8261), and Ψdel (−30.1%); all ten paired p99.5 comparisons favor SAGE. Application-derived DeiT-S traces at rate 0.009 confirm the ordering at 3×10⁻⁶/10⁻⁵/2×10⁻⁵ (e.g., p99.5 714.0 vs 2657.1 cycles at 3×10⁻⁶), a SAGE+M negative control shows that also replaying bounded Class-M faults multiplies retry traffic 2.30× and worsens drop rate to 21.64%, and 200M-trial CRC32 decoder qualification bounds Class-H silent delivery at 3.18×10⁻⁷ per original.

## 核心贡献

- **语义可靠性契约（Semantic Reliability Contract）**：将 BF16 类 16 位数值按位段划分为 Class-H（指数高位 e4–e7，逃逸错误可越出校准包络、产生 NaN/Inf、支配归约结果）、Class-M（符号位与低位指数 e0–e3，有界但显著）、Class-L（尾数精度位，效果近似量化噪声）；分类边界由工作负载的干净张量统计（如 6σ 包络）离线校准，而非硬编码通用位索引。
- **"安全先于性能"的策略排序**：任何策略必须先满足每原始数据项的 Class-H 静默投递风险预算 P_cat,H ≤ ε_cat = 10⁻⁶，才允许进入性能比较；合格策略按质量归一化终端时延 Ψdel = L_mean / q_del 排序，避免单纯时延指标奖励"提前抛弃可恢复包"的策略。
- **地理检查点与重放架构**：解耦"是否值得重放"与"重放从哪重启"——检测到的 Class-H 失败通过受保护的 CTRL 虚拟网络（YX 路由）发送 NACK，前一个检查点完整重放 576 位物理 flit（最多 2 次重放后安全丢弃）；Class-M/L 结果不触发默认网络重放。源端按区域维护间隔表（n₀=67、n_min=10、n_max=199 跳，40K 包更新窗口），Class-H 失败将间隔减半，连续 4 个干净窗口后 +5 跳缓慢放宽。
- **有序 VC 所有权与逐包信用记账**：检查器时延（12 周期 Class-H 检查附加费）内不阻塞普通旁路流量——每输入 VC 维护有序 owner 队列，年轻旁路 flit 在旧 owner 未决时延迟入队，逐包 early-credit token 精确消除重复信用，保证逐 VC 保序与检查器流水化共存。
- **完整实现与质量感知评估**：端点/重放协议实现在 gem5 Garnet，检查器 RTL 在 ASAP7 综合（Class-H 2737.4 面积单位/2949.8 µm²，II=1）；配套 Garnet 校准 wrapper 七策略扫描、Ramulator 2.0 HBM3 可移植性试点、以及 2 亿次试验的 CRC32 解码器资格认证。

## Key Contributions

- **A semantic reliability contract.** For BF16-like data, a workload-calibrated partition separates catastrophic Class-H faults (exponent bits e4–e7 whose escaped residuals can leave the tensor envelope, create non-finite values, or dominate reductions) from bounded Class-M (sign + lower exponent bits) and precision-level Class-L (mantissa) damage. Boundaries are calibrated offline from clean tensor statistics; the learning curve A(p) = A∞[1 − K₁(1+p_eff)^(−K₂)] with p_eff = p·q_del maps delivered quality to task utility without any per-packet model inference in the NoC control loop.
- **Safety-before-performance policy ranking.** A policy is admissible only if its per-original Class-H catastrophic-risk probability P_cat,H(π,P) ≤ ε_cat = 10⁻⁶; among admissible policies, SAGE minimizes quality-normalized terminal latency Ψdel = L_mean/q_del where q_del = (1 − P_drop)·q_acc, so recovery bandwidth is spent where it buys delivered semantics.
- **Geographic checkpoint/replay architecture.** Semantic eligibility (only Class-H replays, refreshing the complete 576-bit flit) is decoupled from recovery distance (a source-local region table with per-region intervals: halved after Class-H-heavy 40K-packet windows, +5 hops after four consecutive quiet windows). Endpoint validation runs only at segment endpoints with a 12-cycle checkpoint surcharge (15 cycles all-in per checkpoint hop).
- **Ordered VC ownership for mixed traffic.** Per-VC owner queues with monotonic sequence records, deferred ingress for younger bypass flits, and packet-specific early-credit tokens let a 1-cycle-initiation-interval pipelined checker coexist with bypass traffic without reordering or duplicate credits.
- **Implementation and multi-tier evaluation.** Direct gem5 Garnet protocol (100×100 mesh, 576-bit flits, DATA/CTRL on separate XY/YX vnets), ASAP7-synthesized pipelined checker RTL, a Garnet-calibrated wrapper sweep over seven policies, an application-derived DeiT-S communication-trace campaign, CRC32 decoder qualification (200M trials), and a five-seed Ramulator 2.0 HBM3 portability pilot.

## Methodology

- **Flit and coding structure.** One 576-bit physical flit per source item: 184-bit Class-H record (128 semantic bits + CRC32 + 24 BCH parity → shortened BCH(184,160,t=3)), 168-bit Class-M record (160 semantic + 8 parity → BCH(168,160,t=1)), 224 uncoded Class-L precision bits; total protection expansion is 64 bits per 512 semantic bits (12.5%). Ordinary routers forward without semantic decoding; only selected checkpoints/destinations run the parallel H (11-cycle) and M (5-cycle) checkers, pipelined to initiation interval 1.
- **Interval controller (Eq. 8).** Exponentially weighted Class-H retry/drop score per (source, region) with α=1/8, w_R=10, w_D=100; score ≥ θ_high=10⁻³ → halve interval (floor n_min=10); score < θ_low=10⁻⁴ for 4 windows → +5 hops (cap n_max=199). Feedback is apportioned to regions by hop exposure; the plan is snapshot at injection.
- **Trace-locked causal plans.** A Python reference controller processes the complete traffic trace offline in packet-ID order; gem5 executes the immutable checkpoint sequence with explicit NACK/replay injection, isolating network execution from online controller overhead while keeping reproducibility (all traffic, plan, executable, and BER-map hashes verified by per-run manifests).
- **Experimental tiers.** (1) Garnet-calibrated wrapper (ten common seeds, 6M warm-up + 250K measured originals; seven policies including SAGE+M negative control and SAGE-P path-wide ablation); (2) direct Garnet headline and stress campaigns (ten paired seeds, 15.25M post-warm-up originals per seed at BER 3×10⁻⁵; five-seed stress at 3×10⁻⁴ and 10⁻³); (3) DeiT-S application-derived finite traces (12 physical replicas, 6 in-flight contexts, rate r_DeiT=0.009, BERs 3×10⁻⁶/10⁻⁵/2×10⁻⁵); (4) RTL decoder qualification via Monte Carlo over the ten-seed exposure with Clopper–Pearson simultaneous 95% bounds; (5) Ramulator 2.0 HBM3 pilot at 0.4·r_sat comparing Class-H-only vs all-class recovery.
- **Statistical treatment.** Per-seed statistics (never pooled), paired differences with two-sided 95% t-intervals, stationarity diagnostics B(t) = N_gen − N_term with predeclared 5K-cycle windows, and directed-link demand feasibility checks.

## Results & Highlights

- **Direct-Garnet headline (uniform-random, base BER 3×10⁻⁵, 10 seeds, 15.25M originals/seed):** SAGE vs fixed 34-hop reduces mean terminal latency from 418.3 to 301.2 cycles (−28.0%) and all-outcome p99.5 from 1269.5 to 813.0 cycles (−36.0%); drop rate falls 11.63% → 7.42%, qdel rises 0.8261 → 0.8504, and Ψdel falls 30.1%. All ten traffic- and seed-paired p99.5 comparisons favor SAGE (mean paired p99.5 advantage 456.5 cycles, 95% CI [455.4, 457.6]); retry and CTRL flit-hops drop 62.9% and 75.1%.
- **Semantic negative control (SAGE+M):** keeping SAGE's geographic plan but letting bounded Class-M failures replay multiplies retry DATA flit-hops per original by 2.30, raises drop rate from 7.42% to 21.64%, and cuts delivered quality by 8.3%; at 4×10⁻⁵ it increases retry flit-hops 86.0% and worsens Ψdel by 9.7% — direct evidence that replaying bounded faults sacrifices useful deliveries.
- **High-BER queue stability (5 paired seeds, same offered load):** at base BER 3×10⁻⁴ fixed 34-hop completes only 55.8% of arrivals and accumulates +47.71 originals/cycle of backlog (L_mean 37.8K cycles), while SAGE holds completion/arrival = 1.000 with backlog slope +0.007 (L_mean 336.4); at 10⁻³ fixed 34-hop completes 48.6% with +55.51/cycle vs SAGE stable at 1.000 — a 3.3× BER separation, though at 26.8% drop rate this demonstrates containment, not useful service.
- **Application-derived DeiT-S traces (rate 0.009):** at the operating point 3×10⁻⁶, SAGE lowers mean latency 284.8 → 237.6 cycles, p99.5 2657.1 → 714.0, and mean source-queue time 25.03 → 4.00 cycles (Ψdel 287.2 → 239.6); at 2×10⁻⁵ stress the gaps widen to −74.2% mean, −79.7% p99.5, −74.6% Ψdel with drop rate 3.1542% → 1.3354% and qdel 0.8966 → 0.9111; all ten pairs favor SAGE at every nonzero BER.
- **Safety qualification:** across 200M CRC32 endpoint-attempt decoder trials, zero wrong 128-bit Class-H payloads pass CRC, giving a simultaneous 95% per-original Class-H silent-delivery bound of 3.18×10⁻⁷ — under the 10⁻⁶ budget without assuming attempt independence; a matched 48-bit BCH-only allocation shows H5–M1–L0 certifies at 4.30×10⁻⁸ whereas equal H2–M2–L2 fails at 4.56×10⁻³.
- **Fault-injection motivation and calibration:** exponent-MSB faults collapse DenseNet-121 training accuracy to 2.0% (chance) vs 83.3% clean control (safe restoration recovers 83.0%), while a bounded upward e3 fault at BER 10⁻⁴ only drops AlexNet top-1 to 63.38% vs 69.89% downward with no NaN — supporting the H/M split. The learning law fits the DenseNet-121/ESC-50 curve with MSE 0.144 vs 1.468 (exponential) and 0.602 (power-law), and the AlexNet/CIFAR-100 sign-bit task check predicts accuracy within 0.11 pp (RMSE 0.058 pp).
- **Implementation cost (ASAP7):** Class-H checker 2737.4 units (2949.8 µm²) at 11-cycle latency/II=1, Class-M 523.3 (570.4 µm²) at 5/1; full pipelining costs +34.7% combined DC area (+40.3% H, +11.6% M) over blocking RTL; interval table 100 B/source, 72 B payload snapshot per active checkpoint.
- **HBM3 portability pilot (Ramulator 2.0, 5 seeds, P_m = 10⁻³):** Class-H-only recovery preserves normalized semantic goodput G_sem/G₀ = 0.9959 with retry factor ν = 1.0000, whereas all-class recovery falls to 0.7901 with ν = 1.2540 — the semantic-selection rule transfers from NoC to memory contention.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** The core reframing — decoupling replay *eligibility* (numerical consequence, calibrated H/M/L classes with a hard silent-delivery budget) from replay *distance* (geographic checkpoint spacing) — is a genuinely clean architectural idea, and the Ψdel = L_mean/q_del metric is a sensible fix for latency metrics that reward dropping recoverable packets. However, the ingredients (unequal error protection, approximate NoCs like DEC-NoC, fault-tolerant routing, workload-aware ECC) are all established; the contribution is the synthesis and the unusually careful evaluation harness rather than any single new mechanism.
- **Rigor:** High internal rigor (pairing, manifests, negative control, decoder qualification), but the primary baseline is the authors' own fixed 34-hop interval — a design point they chose. DEC-NoC is explicitly dismissed as "not a matched numerical baseline," and no external fault-tolerant NoC is quantitatively compared, so the practical margin over prior published systems is unmeasured. The comparison against fixed 10-hop is more informative (SAGE within 7.0% retry traffic but 57.0% fewer planned segments), and SAGE+M is an excellent negative control, but end-to-end comparisons with real systems remain absent.
- **Reproducibility:** Strong methodology hygiene, but artifacts are only promised post-acceptance, the controller is executed offline via trace-locked plans (online software update cost excluded from all latency numbers), and transaction IDs, endpoint positions, and attempt numbers are modeled as metadata "outside the 576-bit physical-flit budget" — i.e., protocol overhead is not counted.
- **Significance:** The evaluation scale is a 100×100 mesh (10,000 routers) with uniform-random or one DeiT-S communication trace — no modern LLM-scale workload, no power/energy numbers despite the "AI data movement" framing, and no end-to-end task accuracy under real corrupted payloads (the DeiT-S campaign is network-level contention only; the authors admit it "does not execute end-to-end DeiT-S inference with corrupted tensor payloads"). Calibration models are AlexNet/CIFAR-100 and DenseNet-121/ESC-50 — small 2012–2016-era networks.
- **Red flags:** (1) qdel uses fixed coefficients λ_M = 4, λ_L = 0.25 validated only on small models and one sign-bit setting — the headline Ψdel numbers inherit this fragility; (2) checkpoint-store area, ownership ledger, and early-credit tokens are modeled but unsynthesized, so the 12.5% protection expansion understates total cost; (3) extreme-BER results (10⁻³) come with 26.8% drop rates and are honestly labeled containment, but a casual reader of the abstract could miss this; (4) the 3.18×10⁻⁷ bound assumes the measured IID-flip exposure — correlated burst faults, a realistic failure mode in real links, are out of scope; (5) wrapper points above 5×10⁻⁵ sit outside the CRC-qualified envelope and are stress tests only.

## Strengths

- The eligibility/distance decoupling is conceptually crisp, and every design choice is backed by a targeted experiment: SAGE+M isolates semantic eligibility, SAGE-P isolates geographic locality, fixed 10-hop isolates interval length.
- Safety is treated as a constraint, not an afterthought — the 10⁻⁶ Class-H budget gates policy admissibility before any latency comparison, and the 200M-trial CRC32 campaign with simultaneous Clopper–Pearson bounds (no independence assumption, no range-check credit) is exemplary.
- Honest negative-result reporting: the paper quantifies when its own idea fails (SAGE+M, extreme-BER containment) and states scope limits explicitly in Section 8.
- Evaluation hygiene (paired seeds, per-seed quantiles, manifest-hash acceptance gates, stationarity diagnostics) exceeds the standard of most NoC simulation papers.
- The architecture is implementable: pipelined checker RTL in ASAP7 with concrete area numbers, a 100 B/source interval table, and a flow-control scheme (ordered VC ownership, exact early-credit cancellation) that addresses the real problem of checker latency blocking bypass traffic.

## Limitations

- H/M/L boundaries and quality weights (λ_M=4, λ_L=0.25) are workload- and format-specific; the e4–e7 Class-H assignment is a calibrated AlexNet case, not a universal partition, and must be recalibrated per deployment.
- The Class-H silent-delivery bound assumes IID flips under the measured exposure; correlated/burst faults, persistent-state corruption, and multi-bit attacks are out of scope.
- Online controller cost is excluded: interval updates "remain in software" and plans are trace-locked offline, so reported latencies assume an idealized controller.
- Checkpoint-store area/timing and the ownership ledger are modeled, not synthesized; protocol metadata overhead sits outside the 576-bit flit budget.
- Application evidence is one DeiT-S communication trace at network level; no end-to-end inference accuracy under corrupted payloads, no LLM-scale tensors, no energy/power results.
- The primary baseline (fixed 34-hop) is internally defined; no quantitative comparison against DEC-NoC or any external fault-tolerant NoC implementation.

## Relevance

本文与"高效可靠 AI 系统硬件/基础设施"方向高度相关：它把数值语义（哪些位错是灾难性的）与网络物理层故障地理联合建模，为大规模训练/推理互连与 HBM 链路提供了按后果分配恢复带宽的新范式，是fault-tolerant NoC、近似通信与 AI 加速器可靠性交叉领域的扎实工作。若你的兴趣集中在算法/模型层面（如训练效率、量化），本文价值在于理解底层比特错误如何向上污染 DNN 训练（指数位翻转使 DenseNet-121 崩溃到 2% 精度）。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How do the H/M/L boundaries and λ_M/λ_L quality weights change for LLM-scale tensors (BF16 activations with outliers, FP8 blocks with shared exponents) — does the e4–e7 Class-H assignment survive at 100B-parameter scale?
- What is the true online cost of the interval controller (software updates every 40K packets) when integrated into a real NIC/router pipeline rather than precomputed trace-locked plans?
- How does SAGE interact with correlated burst faults (adjacent-line coupling, ESD events, aging hotspots), and would the 3.18×10⁻⁷ silent-delivery bound survive a bursty fault model?
- Could the semantic eligibility rule compose with link-level retransmission (credit-based fabric protocols) or end-to-end model-level resilience (selective layer recompute) instead of only flit replay?
- What are the energy implications — does cutting retry traffic by 62.9% translate into measurable link power savings, and how does the 12.5% protection overhead compare against end-to-end ECC with the same silent-delivery budget?
- The HBM pilot shows Class-H-only recovery preserves 0.9959 normalized goodput; what happens under mixed read/write traffic with bank conflicts and refresh interference, and at what BER does the two-level controller design become necessary?
