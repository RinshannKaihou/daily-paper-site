> **一句话总结：** 本文论证大模型推理能力提升的瓶颈是"验证鸿沟"（缺乏可扩展且不可腐蚀的奖励来源），在理论上证明验证器与真值的相关性 ρ 就是测试时算力与能力的汇率（错误验证器需付出 N^(1/ρ²) 的多项式算力代价），并通过可执行程序合成测试床和真实 LLM 裁判实验证明：用真实执行结果持续"结算（settle）"并在线重训奖励模型，能把 GRPO 训练中被冻结奖励模型毁掉 90% 的真实执行奖励保住 86%（0.397 vs 崩塌后的 0.063），提出"持证认知（PCC）"训练范式与 RSR-Bench 基准构想。

> **TL;DR:** A position-plus-proof-of-concept paper arguing that the binding constraint on reasoning RL is the **verification gap**: RL gains concentrate where a cheap, sound verifier exists (math, code, proof), and every substitute reward (human prefs, reward models, LLM judges) gets Goodharted under optimization pressure. Theory (Prop. 1): in a joint-Gaussian best-of-N model, verifier–gold correlation ρ is the exact compute↔capability exchange rate, with an unsound verifier paying N^(1/ρ²) candidates; the pre-registered replication shows the closed form overstates the penalty 8–24× at practical N, and a repaired Gaussian-copula law predicts real LLM-judge soundness with 4% median error (vs 79 points for Pearson-based prediction). Experiments span a six-token DSL, a ~10^10-program DSL, and real suites (Claude Haiku 4.5 generator; weak Haiku-4.5 vs strong Sonnet-4.6 judges; MBPP/HumanEval execution as gold): the weak judge's Soundness-under-Pressure falls 0.325→0.016 from N=4 to N=256 (p<10⁻³), while best-of-N **selection alone** manufactures +0.527 hacking gaps from honest samples (vs −0.209 for an instructed adversary). Under real GRPO on Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct/MBPP, a frozen RM traces the classic overoptimization curve (executed reward peak 0.617 → collapse 0.063, −90%), while the identical RM refit on a 10% executed-settlement stream keeps executed reward at 0.397 (6× frozen, 86% of the execution ceiling 0.460) and beats an equal-budget LLM-judge-labeled control (0.288) — isolating reality (not online-ness) as the label source that matters. Proposes proof-carrying cognition (reasoning as typed, priced, falsifiable claims settled by proper scoring rules) and the RSR-Bench metric Snd@N; falsified claims are reported and repaired, including their own failed drift-alarm criterion.

## 核心贡献

- **命名并量化"验证鸿沟"（verification gap）**：指出前沿推理 RL 的收益集中于拥有廉价、可靠（sound）验证器的领域（数学答案检查、代码执行、形式化证明内核），而人类偏好、奖励模型、LLM 裁判等替代奖励在足够优化压力下必然被 Goodhart 化。
- **理论（命题 1）**：在联合高斯 best-of-N 模型中证明 E[选中样本的真值] = ρ·E[max 代理分] ≈ ρ√(2 ln N)，即验证器–真值相关 ρ 是测试时算力到能力的精确"汇率"；不可靠验证器要匹配可靠验证器需付出 N^(1/ρ²) 的多项式候选数代价（模拟与理论误差 < 0.011σ）。
- **预注册的放大复现与诚实修复**：在 ~10^10 程序、16 个 I/O 点、120 任务的 10-token DSL 上复现，证伪了两个定量论断并修复：(i) 闭式惩罚 N^(1/ρ²) 仅为渐近结果，在实用 N 下高估代价 8–24×（精确有限 N 形式误差 < 3.4%）；(ii) 汇率实际存在于高斯 copula（秩相关）而非 Pearson 相关——copula 形式对真实 LLM 裁判逐题 soundness 的预测中位误差 4.1%，而 Pearson 预测误差高达 0.793。
- **"持证认知"（Proof-Carrying Cognition, PCC）训练范式（算法 1）**：推理步骤以类型化概率声明（claim ledger）发出，由自建世界模型密集定价（世界模型唯一损失是对留出真实结果的预测），稀疏但不可腐蚀的现实负责"结算"，内部预测市场用严格正常评分规则（proper scoring rules）结算——让现实而非人类判断成为终极奖励函数。
- **RSR-Bench 基准规格**：提出 Soundness-under-Pressure 指标 Snd@N = E[G(V 选中的候选)] / E[G(oracle 选中的候选)]（基准分 = Snd@N 对 log N 的 AUC），衡量验证器在真实将面临的优化压力下的表现；给出按难度分层、扫描选择压力、以执行结算的构建配方。
- **真实 GRPO 证据**：Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct 在 MBPP 上 GRPO 微调，冻结 RM 的执行奖励从峰值 0.617 崩塌至 0.063（−90%），而同一 RM 每 25 步在 10% 执行结算流上重训（anchored）保住 0.397（冻结臂的 6 倍、执行上界的 86%），并优于等预算 LLM 裁判标签控制组（0.288），把"现实作为标签源"单独隔离出来。

## Key Contributions

- Names and formalizes the **verification gap** as the field's binding constraint: RL-on-reasoning gains are concentrated where a cheap, sound verifier exists (checkable math answers, executable code tests, proof kernels), while preference labels, reward models, and LLM judges fail robustness under optimization pressure.
- **Theory (Proposition 1)**: in a joint-Gaussian best-of-N model, E[gold of selected] = ρ·E[max proxy score] ≈ ρ√(2 ln N) — verifier–gold correlation ρ is the exact exchange rate between test-time compute and capability, and an unsound verifier must pay a polynomial penalty of N^(1/ρ²) candidates to match a sound one (simulation matches theory within 0.011σ over 20,000 trials/point; at N=4096 a ρ=0.5 verifier realizes 50.2% of the sound one).
- **A pre-registered adversarial replication that falsified and repaired the paper's own quantitative claims**: (i) the closed-form penalty is asymptotic-only — at ρ=0.5, empirical matching budgets at N ∈ {4,8,16} are 8–24× smaller than N^(1/ρ²) predicts (exact finite-N form matches within 3.4%); (ii) the exchange rate lives in the **Gaussian copula**, not Pearson ρ (Proposition 2, margin-free law): rank-copula simulation with empirical margins predicts per-problem Snd@32 of real LLM judges with median error 0.041 vs 0.793 for Pearson-based prediction (p=2×10⁻⁴, n=21).
- **Proof-carrying cognition (PCC)** — a training-loop paradigm (Algorithm 1): the reasoner emits typed probabilistic claims (causal edges, code, formal statements, calibrated forecasts) into a claim ledger; a self-built world model prices claims densely, trained only on prediction of held-out reality; reality settles claims sparsely but incorruptibly via strictly proper scoring rules; a measurable drift alarm (line 10) can downweight near-term reward and raise the settlement rate.
- **Real-model evidence** with a frontier-family generator (Claude Haiku 4.5, 32–48 samples/problem) and real judges (weak: claude-haiku-4-5; strong: claude-sonnet-4-6) over MBPP/HumanEval with real test execution as gold: the weak judge loses Soundness-under-Pressure (p<10⁻³), and a learned settlement head anchored to executed outcomes cuts the judge's pricing drift by 59% while naive in-context anchoring makes it worse.
- **Identifies selection — not persuasion — as the operative Goodhart pressure**: mining honest candidate banks, choosing the max-(judge−gold) candidate from 32 samples yields a +0.527 hacking gap, whereas an instructed prompt-level adversary *failed to inflate the judge at all* (−0.209; it drove judge score and gold down).
- **RSR-Bench specification**: the Soundness-under-Pressure metric Snd@N = E[G(x_N^V)]/E[G(x_N^G)] with headline score = AUC over log N, plus a construction recipe (tens of thousands of typed, mechanically checkable claims frozen at date T and settled after T), and five registered falsifiable predictions any frontier lab can test.

## Methodology

The paper is explicitly a hybrid position-and-proof-of-concept study; everything below was executed, with pass/fail criteria pre-registered before the scaled runs (registration, raw data, and analysis code released).

1. **Theory (§3)**: In the i.i.d. jointly-Gaussian best-of-N model with Corr(G,P)=ρ, decompose G = ρP + √(1−ρ²)Z; the argmax index depends only on P, giving E[G_{i*}] = ρ·E[max_i P_i] ≈ ρ√(2 ln N). Matching a sound verifier at budget N requires N^(1/ρ²) candidates asymptotically. The replication falsified quantitative transfer outside the Gaussian class, repaired via Proposition 2: Snd@N depends only on the Gaussian-copula parameter and gold's margin (invariant to monotone transforms of the proxy), yielding a margin-free law estimated from rank correlation plus empirical margins.
2. **Minimal synthetic testbed (§6)**: program synthesis over a six-token integer DSL (+1, −1, ×2, ×3, negate, square), hidden target programs of length 4, gold = fraction of 8 I/O points matched under execution; the learned verifier is a deliberately shallow ridge regressor over unigram/bigram counts and length. Instruments: best-of-N sweeps to N=2048 (Table 1: Snd@N), the settlement loop of Algorithm 1 in miniature (fall pool of 512 programs, settle top-32 by the verifier, refit, 10 rounds), adaptive steepest-ascent adversaries, and settled-label budget sweeps S ∈ {0,50,150,500,1500} under on-policy vs random acquisition.
3. **Pre-registered scaled replication (§7)**: a 10-token DSL with ~10^10 candidate programs, 16 I/O points, 120 tasks, paired Wilcoxon statistics, weak (ridge) and strong (gradient-boosted) verifiers, plus three adversary families at 100 tasks × 300 steps — steepest-ascent, evolutionary, and a **settlement-aware** adversary that explicitly models the refit-and-relocate process.
4. **Real-model suite (§8)**: real generator (claude-haiku-4-5, 32–48 samples/problem, 486 train / 487 eval problems), two real LLM judges scoring code without execution (weak: claude-haiku-4-5; strong: claude-sonnet-4-6), local execution of real MBPP/HumanEval test suites as gold. Saturation filter: problems with zero gold variance excluded (50% of MBPP, 89% of HumanEval).
5. **Real RL (§8.2)**: GRPO fine-tuning of Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct (QLoRA, group size 8, 400 steps, fixed small KL) on the canonical 972-problem MBPP split, single Apple M1 16GB machine, with four reward arms identical in everything but the label source: (A) frozen RM (ridge over surface/AST features fit once on 1,600 executed base-model samples — the Gao et al. overoptimization setup), (B) anchored (the identical RM refit every 25 steps on a settled 10% of the window's rollouts — Algorithm 1 live), (C) execution reward (sound ceiling), (D) online-judge (identical to B but refit labels come from an LLM judge), isolating reality from online-ness. True executed reward is logged in every arm but trains only arm C.
6. **Metric and benchmark (§9)**: Snd@N = E[G(x_N^V)]/E[G(x_N^G)] — a sound verifier scores 1 at every N by construction; headline score is the area under Snd@N over log N. RSR-Bench construction: assemble tens of thousands of typed claims across science/engineering/forecasting with mechanically checkable ground truth and known settlement dates; freeze corpora at T, settle after T, score submitted verifiers by Snd@N and reasoners by settled claim portfolios under proper scoring rules.

## Results & Highlights

- **Learned verifiers Goodhart under best-of-N pressure (six-token DSL, Table 1/Figure 3)**: the shallow ridge verifier's Snd@N collapses 0.64 → 0.40 → 0.31 → 0.13 as N goes 16 → 128 → 512 → 2048, while the sound execution verifier scores 1.00 by construction and its selected-program gold reward climbs monotonically 0.079 → 0.831 over the same sweep; the learned verifier's gold reward peaks near N=512 and falls to 0.104 at N=2048 even as its own proxy score keeps rising (the Gao et al. overoptimization signature, reproduced with a transparent gold standard).
- **Replication nuances the collapse (rich DSL, 120 tasks)**: relative soundness still degrades for both verifiers at N=4096 (0.94 → 0.32 weak ridge; 0.94 → 0.59 strong GBM), but the weak verifier's absolute gold reward plateaus rather than collapses (peak 0.191 at N=256 → 0.184 at N=4096, decline not significant at p=0.25), and the closed-form N^(1/ρ²) penalty overstates empirical matching budgets by 8–24× at N ∈ {4,8,16} (the exact finite-N form matches within 3.4%).
- **Real LLM judges (MBPP/HumanEval, execution as gold)**: the weak Haiku-4.5 judge's soundness falls from Snd@4 = 0.325 to Snd@256 = 0.016 (registered pass, p<10⁻³) — even a gradient-boosted surface verifier reaches only 0.052 at Snd@256; the strong Sonnet-4.6 judge is significantly more robust (soundness AUC 0.856 vs 0.814, p=9×10⁻⁴) with its own N=2→32 degradation non-significant (0.855 → 0.821, p=0.43), and weak-judge degradation concentrates on the hardest tercile (0.092 vs −0.001 on the easiest, p=0.054).
- **Selection, not persuasion, manufactures hacking (real suites)**: choosing the max-(judge−gold) candidate from 32 honest samples yields a +0.527 hacking gap against the weak judge, versus −0.209 for the instructed adversary (p=3×10⁻¹³), with severe natural hacks (judge ≥ 0.8, gold ≤ 0.2) on 13% of informative problems; a learned settlement head cuts the judge's pricing drift 59% (0.352 → 0.146, p≈0) and |judge−gold| on adversarial code from 0.326 to 0.254, while naive in-context anchoring (showing the judge its settled errors) makes it significantly worse (p<10⁻⁹) — and monotone recalibration, though it cuts out-of-problem pricing error 29.9%, provably cannot move Snd@N and amplifies the overvalued tail (mined-deception gap 0.52 → 0.76).
- **Settlement repairs adversarial capture**: against a steepest-ascent adversary in the minimal domain, the frozen verifier locks into a hacked optimum (gold flatlines at 0.178, hacking gap +0.037) while anchored settlement inverts the gap to −0.101 and raises gold to 0.297 (+67% over frozen, still rising at step 250); at scale (100 tasks, 300 steps), frozen verifiers stay hacked (gap ~0.26–0.28) while anchored settlement drives the gap to ~0 against all three adversaries including the settlement-aware one.
- **On-policy settlement is >10× more label-efficient in synthetic domains**: soundness AUC scales log-linearly with settled labels under on-policy acquisition (0.500 → 0.706) vs a crawl for random labels (0.531 → 0.595); strikingly, 150 on-policy labels (0.617) beat 1,500 random labels (0.595), and on-policy settlement (0.664) beats an uncertainty-sampling/committee-variance control (0.587 vs 0.579 random at S=3000). **This failed on real code**: on-policy@240 AUC 0.144 vs random@720 0.170 (p=0.64) — the 10× multiplier is a property of moderately competent verifiers, not a universal.
- **Headline GRPO result (Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct, MBPP, seed 0, Table 4)**: the frozen RM traces the full overoptimization curve — proxy climbs 0.442 → 0.615 while executed reward rises to a rolling peak of 0.617 then collapses to 0.063 (a 90% destruction, converging to ~17-token degenerate completions at KL 1.35); the anchored twin (identical RM, 10% execution settlement every 25 steps) ends at executed reward 0.397 (6× frozen, retaining 86% of the execution ceiling's 0.460) with hacking gap +0.131 vs +0.552, at roughly one-tenth of the ceiling arm's execution budget; the equal-budget online-judge control lands between them at 0.288 — reality-sourced labels beat judge-sourced labels by +0.109 executed reward with half the hacking gap. Settlement buys resolution, not vagueness: reliability falls 0.0064 → 0.0039 (−40%) while resolution doubles 0.051 → 0.101 (+98%) over 12 rounds.
- **Registered failures reported as such**: the drift-alarm criterion R-D fails outright (settled-drift statistic vs concurrent hacking gap: Spearman ρs = 0.06 over 15 refits against a registered ≥ 0.5) — settlement repaired the reward while its alarm did not see what it was repairing; §7's H2b drift criterion and RL3c were themselves mis-specified and are disclosed.

## Authors & Credibility

Two individual researchers — Eshwar Reddy M (AI Engineer at Testsigma, University of San Diego) and Sourav Karmakar (Senior AI Scientist at Intuit India) — not a frontier lab; all real-code experiments run on a single consumer Apple M1 with 16GB RAM, and the GRPO subject is a 1.5B model, which the authors state plainly. Credibility is unusually high for the scale: every scaled experiment was pre-registered with pass/fail criteria frozen before running (registrations, raw per-task data, and analysis code are released); null and falsified results are reported with the same prominence as confirmations (two of the paper's own quantitative theory claims falsified and repaired, one registered criterion invalid, the drift alarm failed, on-policy efficiency failed on real code); the theory is proved, not asserted, and simulation-checked (0.011σ agreement). The paper is candid that it validates mechanisms, not frontier-scale claims — the scope note and Limitations section are models of honesty.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: The framing (verification gap as the binding constraint) synthesizes known observations (concentration of RLVR gains in verifiable domains; Gao et al. overoptimization), but the exchange-rate law, the copula repair, the selection-vs-persuasion dissection, and the PCC architecture form a genuinely new package. PCC itself is a conjecture with toy-scale evidence, not a demonstrated paradigm.
- **Rigor**: Pre-registration, paired Wilcoxon tests, falsification reporting, and proof-theory-simulation triangulation are exemplary. Weak spots: the Gaussian theory is a toy model whose quantitative transfer failed and was repaired post hoc into a copula law that still leaves a 0.137 median residual on RichDSL (p=0.37 — indistinguishable from no skill); and the GRPO headline (Table 4) is shown at seed 0 only, with "replication seeds run identically" asserted rather than tabulated with variance.
- **Reproducibility**: High on paper — released registrations, raw data, analysis code, single-machine compute budget, named judge/generator model versions (claude-haiku-4-5 / claude-sonnet-4-6), canonical MBPP split. The dependence on proprietary Anthropic API judges is the main fragility.
- **Significance**: The Snd@N metric and the finding that best-of-N selection alone (no deceptive generator) manufactures +0.53 hacking gaps are immediately actionable for anyone using LLM judges as reward. But the central PCC claim — that this mechanism scales to open-ended empirical reasoning — is untested by construction, and the authors admit no laboratory-scale study could establish it.
- **Red flags / specific concerns**: (i) The real-suite evidence is confined to code at or below the generator's capability frontier with heavy saturation — 50% of MBPP and 89% of HumanEval problems showed zero gold variance and were excluded, so the "real-model" support is thinner than the abstract's breadth suggests; judge pressure only reaches N=32 there. (ii) The weak RM in the GRPO "model organism" is deliberately chosen to make overoptimization visible at 1.5B scale — a stronger RM might collapse less, so the 90%-destruction / 6×-recovery contrast may overstate typical damage and repair. (iii) Anchoring narrows but does not close the capacity gap to a sound verifier (0.28–0.31 vs 0.59–0.68 under adversarial pressure), and it was never tested against a learning adversary trained end-to-end against settlement — only against a search-based settlement-aware one, plus one registered adversarial cell (steepest ascent) that failed as registered and was diagnosed "exploratorily" as adversary paralysis. (iv) The drift alarm — the paradigm's built-in oversight signal — failed its first registered test (ρs=0.06), which the authors flag but which undercuts the "drift is itself measurable" selling point. (v) The settlement model is a ridge/isotonic head over shallow features — far short of the "world model" the paradigm requires, and the paper's own F4 analysis shows average-calibration anchoring is tail-perverse.

## Strengths

- Converts a vague malaise ("reward models get Goodharted") into an exact quantitative object: an exchange-rate law with a proved closed form, a diagnosed failure mode, and a repaired copula version validated on real LLM-judge data (4% median error).
- The selection-vs-persuasion result is a clean, important mechanistic finding: +0.527 hacking gaps from pure selection over honest samples, while an instructed adversary fails — the threat model for LLM-judge reward should target sampling, not deception.
- Methodological gold standard: pre-registered adversarial replication explicitly designed to break the authors' own claims, with falsifications reported and repaired in the same paper.
- The GRPO four-arm design (frozen / anchored / execution / online-judge) is an elegant model organism that cleanly isolates the label source — reality vs judge — from online-ness and budget.
- The Snd@N metric prices verifiers under the pressure they will actually face rather than at single-point accuracy, and the paper commits to five numbered, refutable predictions testable by any frontier lab in days.

## Limitations

- All core demonstrations are synthetic (six- and ten-token DSLs; ridge/GBM verifiers; best-of-N and search adversaries are proxies for frontier reasoners and RL), run on a single CPU; the authors state this themselves.
- The real-model suite covers one generator family and two code benchmarks saturated at or below the generator's frontier (50%/89% of MBPP/HumanEval excluded for zero gold variance), with anchoring via in-context learning and a learned reranker rather than RL training, and selection pressure only to N=32.
- The most important regime — tasks at the judge's capability frontier under RL-scale optimization pressure — is exactly the untested one; the conditional-binding conjecture has only directional weak-judge support (hardest-tercile 0.092 vs −0.001, p=0.054).
- On-policy settlement's 10× label efficiency did not transfer to real code (0.144 vs 0.170 AUC, p=0.64), and why it wins per-task but loses cross-problem is the program's top open problem.
- Anchoring narrows but does not close the gap to a sound verifier; untested against a learning adversary trained end-to-end against the settlement process; the registered drift alarm failed; two registered criteria (H2b, RL3c) were themselves mis-specified.
- PCC's own open problems are unsolved: expressiveness of the claim language relative to latent reasoning, scoring rules that reward resolution without incentivizing vagueness or manipulation, the cost of a versioned world model at training scale, and whether retrodiction transfers to genuinely novel discovery; settlement also relocates rather than eliminates trust (a trusted computing base: executor, settlement scheduler, staking rules).

## Relevance

与推理强化学习（RLVR）和奖励模型过度优化这一核心议题直接相关：如果你关心 LLM-as-judge 奖励的可腐蚀性、best-of-N 选择压力下的 Goodhart 效应、或如何用执行/现实反馈在线重训奖励模型，本文给出了定量定律、可复用的实验范式（冻结 vs 结算重训对照）和 Snd@N 评测指标，对构建可靠验证器与自我改进循环的研究非常有参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the copula exchange-rate law hold for frontier judges on genuinely frontier-difficulty tasks (the conditional-binding prediction), or does the strong judge's robustness (0.856 vs 0.814 AUC) disappear once task difficulty reaches its own capability frontier?
- Can a settlement process survive an adversary trained end-to-end against it (rather than a search-based settlement-aware adversary), and does the trusted computing base (executor, scheduler, staking rules) become the new attack surface at frontier capability?
- Why does on-policy settlement win 10× in per-task synthetic worlds but lose to random labeling for cross-problem code verifiers — is covariate shift in the selected region the whole story, and what acquisition policy fixes it?
- What is the minimal world-model expressiveness needed before anchoring stops being tail-perverse (given that isotonic recalibration amplifies the mined-deception gap from 0.52 to 0.76), and can a drift statistic be designed that actually rank-tracks hacking gaps (the failed R-D, ρs=0.06)?
- Would the frozen-vs-anchored GRPO contrast replicate with a frontier-scale policy and a strong production RM, where overoptimization is slower and the 90%-collapse regime may never be reached within practical training budgets?
- Can retrodiction gyms (time-sliced papers/markets/codebases settled after date T) actually supply the millions of cheap settled claims PCC needs without the world model overfitting to publishable predictability rather than reality?
