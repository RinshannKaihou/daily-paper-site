> **一句话总结：** 本笔记证明联邦学习量化方案 EDEN 中的 MMSE 与无偏两种重建尺度，正是经典 CDEF 理论中 Wiener 系数与无偏系数的有限维、逐实现（realization-dependent）版本：CDEF 的 "+1" SNR 关系对每个 Haar 随机旋转实现以精确勾股恒等式逐点成立，但对失真在旋转上取平均后退化为 Jensen 不等式给出的 ≥ 关系，仅当维度 d→∞ 时等式才恢复；且 EDEN 在任意有限维度下都保证严格条件无偏性 E_U[Z]=X，强于 CDEF 的二阶无偏概念。

> **TL;DR:** A 6-page theory note (cs.IT) by Uri Erez (Tel Aviv University) showing that the two reconstruction scalings of the EDEN distributed-mean-estimation scheme are exact finite-dimensional, realization-dependent counterparts of the Wiener and unbiased coefficients in the classical Cioffi-Dudevoir-Eyuboglu-Forney (CDEF) MMSE framework. For every Haar-rotation realization U the CDEF "+1" relation holds pointwise as an exact Pythagorean identity (1/D_W(U) = 1/D_U(U) + 1), but after averaging distortions over the rotation equality fails, degrading to SNR_MMSE ≥ SNR_MMSE,U + 1 by Jensen's inequality, with equality recovered only as d → ∞ because A(U) concentrates to b/a² (Gaussian single-letter statistics). No experiments or benchmarks — the contribution is conceptual unification plus a clarifying taxonomy of the two distinct roles random rotations play in quantization: approximate Gaussianization of coordinates vs. decorrelation of quantization errors across branches (a dithering mechanism). Relevant background for anyone working on rotation-based LLM quantization (QuIP, QuaRot, TurboQuant) or federated-learning gradient compression (EDEN, DRIVE).

## 核心贡献

- **建立 EDEN 与经典 CDEF 理论的精确对应**：EDEN 的无偏尺度 α_U = ‖X‖²/⟨X,Y⟩ 与 MMSE（Wiener）尺度 α_W = ⟨X,Y⟩/‖Y‖²，正是 CDEF（Cioffi-Dudevoir-Eyuboglu-Forney, 1995）经典框架中无偏系数 α_U = P_X/r_XY 与 Wiener 系数 α_W = r_XY/P_Y 的有限维、依赖于具体旋转实现的确定性版本，该对应对每个旋转实现逐点精确成立。
- **揭示 "+1" 关系的有限维形态**：对每个 Haar 旋转实现 U，CDEF 的 SNR 关系以精确几何（勾股）恒等式 1/D_W(U) = 1/D_U(U) + 1 的形式成立；但一旦对失真在旋转上取平均（SNR 定义为平均失真的倒数而非倒数的平均），等式不再成立——由 t/(1+t) 的凹性与 Jensen 不等式只能得到 SNR_MMSE ≥ SNR_MMSE,U + 1。
- **证明经典关系的渐近恢复**：当 d→∞ 时，随机旋转后向量的经验坐标统计趋近 i.i.d. 高斯（A(U) → b/a²，其中 a = E[GQ(G)]、b = E[Q(G)²]、G~N(0,1)），依赖实现的量集中，Jensen 间隙消失，从而 SNR_MMSE − SNR_MMSE,U → 1，经典 CDEF 关系被恢复。
- **指出 EDEN 超越经典对应之处**：对任意有限维度 d，Haar 旋转 + 依赖实现的尺度保证严格条件无偏性 E_U[Z] = X（EDEN 定理 2.1），这强于 CDEF 的二阶（单位信号增益）无偏概念——Remark 1 明确指出 X ⊥ N 并不蕴含 E[N|X] = 0。
- **梳理随机旋转的两种不同角色**：其一是坐标的近似高斯化（EDEN 的用法），其二是跨量化分支的重建误差去相关（即 dither/抖动机制，Hadad & Erez 2016 的用法）；并追溯了 RQRB（旋转-量化-反旋转）管线在 Hadad & Erez (2016)、Suresh et al. (2017) 与 EDEN 中扮演的不同历史角色。

## Key Contributions

- Establishes an exact dictionary between EDEN and classical CDEF: EDEN's unbiased scale α_U = ‖X‖²/⟨X,Y⟩ and MMSE scale α_W = ⟨X,Y⟩/‖Y‖² are precisely the finite-dimensional, realization-dependent counterparts of the classical unbiased coefficient (α_U = P_X/r_XY) and Wiener coefficient (α_W = r_XY/P_Y); the correspondence is exact for every rotation realization, with Z = α_U Y = X + N and X ⊥ N holding pointwise.
- Shows the CDEF "+1" SNR relation takes a precise finite-blocklength form: for every Haar realization U, the identity 1/D_W(U) = 1/D_U(U) + 1 holds exactly (a Pythagorean identity on the forward/backward-channel right triangles), but it does **not** survive averaging of the distortions over the rotation — since the averaged SNRs are defined as reciprocals of averaged distortions, Jensen's inequality (concavity of t/(1+t)) yields only SNR_MMSE ≥ SNR_MMSE,U + 1.
- Proves asymptotic recovery of the classical relation: as d → ∞, writing the rotated vector as V = √d·G/‖G‖ with G ~ N(0, I_d), the norm concentrates (‖G‖/√d → 1) and A(U) = ‖X‖²‖Y‖²/⟨X,Y⟩² concentrates to the deterministic single-letter limit b/a², so the Jensen gap vanishes and SNR_MMSE − SNR_MMSE,U → 1.
- Identifies that EDEN strictly surpasses the classical correspondence: its Haar-rotation formulation with realization-dependent scaling guarantees exact conditional unbiasedness E_U[Z] = X (equivalently E[Z|X] = X) for every finite d — a stronger property than the second-order, unit-signal-gain notion of unbiasedness in CDEF.
- Provides a taxonomy of the two distinct roles of random rotations in quantization — approximate Gaussianization of coordinates vs. decorrelation of quantization errors across branches (a dithering mechanism enabling noncoherent combining) — and traces the historical use of the rotate-quantize-rotate-back (RQRB) pipeline through Hadad & Erez 2016 (dither with randomized Hadamard, temporally i.i.d. Gaussian sources), Suresh et al. 2017 (common Hadamard for dynamic-range control with stochastic rounding), and EDEN (independent Haar per client with deterministic scalar quantization, serving both roles).

## Methodology

The note is a self-contained mathematical exposition organized in three parts:

1. **Classical CDEF recap (Section 2).** For correlated zero-mean random variables X, Y with P_X = E[X²], P_Y = E[Y²], r_XY = E[XY], the Wiener (linear-MMSE) coefficient α_W = r_XY/P_Y yields the backward-channel decomposition X = α_W Y + E with E ⊥ Y, while the CDEF unbiased coefficient α_U = P_X/r_XY yields the forward channel Z = α_U Y = X + N with N ⊥ X. Lemma 2 of CDEF gives the "+1" relation SNR_MMSE = SNR_MMSE,U + 1, with a two-right-triangle Pythagorean interpretation (Forney's Hilbert-space view). Section 2.4 shows why the forward-channel (unit-gain) representation is the natural one for averaging: averaging m independently quantized descriptions with mutually uncorrelated, source-orthogonal noises N_i of equal power P_N gives noise power P_N/m, i.e., SNR_MMSE,U,ave = m·SNR_MMSE,U.

2. **Historical disentanglement of rotation roles (Section 2.5).** Classical subtractive dithering realizes the forward-channel model exactly for uniform quantizers. Hadad & Erez (2016) proposed RQRB as an alternative dither for scalar quantization — using randomized Hadamard transforms across quantization branches to asymptotically eliminate second-order error correlations (sources already Gaussian, so no Gaussianization role). Suresh et al. (2017) used a *common* randomized Hadamard transform across federated-learning clients to control the dynamic range feeding stochastic scalar quantizers, with stochastic rounding supplying the error randomness. EDEN uses an *independent* Haar rotation per client with *deterministic* scalar quantization: the rotation Gaussianizes an arbitrary input vector (permitting Gaussian-optimized scalar quantizers), while rotation independence across clients — combined with exact conditional unbiasedness — makes reconstruction errors conditionally independent and eliminates cross-correlations at the averaging stage.

3. **The main correspondence (Section 3).** For a fixed non-zero X ∈ R^d and a Haar rotation realization U, EDEN forms V = η_X U X with η_X = √d/‖X‖, quantizes coordinates as Q(V), and reconstructs the pre-scaling observation Y = η_X U^T Q(V). Defining the empirical correlation r_XY^(d) = ⟨X,Y⟩/d and energies P_X^(d) = ‖X‖²/d, P_Y^(d) = ‖Y‖²/d, the EDEN scales coincide exactly with the CDEF scales (Eqs. 17–19). Section 3.1 defines A(U) = ‖X‖²‖Y‖²/⟨X,Y⟩² and the normalized distortions D_U(U) = A(U) − 1 (unit-gain reconstruction) and D_W(U) = 1 − 1/A(U) (Wiener reconstruction), verifies the pointwise identity 1/D_W(U) = 1/D_U(U) + 1, derives the Jensen inequality SNR_MMSE ≥ SNR_MMSE,U + 1 for rotation-averaged distortions, and establishes the asymptotic recovery via concentration of Haar-rotated empirical statistics toward the i.i.d. Gaussian single-letter quantities (a = E[GQ(G)], b = E[Q(G)²]).

## Results & Highlights

No quantitative results reported

The note reports no experiments, simulations, or numerical benchmarks; its evidence consists of exact mathematical statements:

- **Pointwise Pythagorean identity (exact, every finite d):** for every Haar rotation realization U, the normalized distortions D_U(U) = A(U) − 1 and D_W(U) = 1 − 1/A(U) satisfy 1/D_W(U) = 1/D_U(U) + 1 — the CDEF +1 relation holds as an exact geometric identity per realization at finite blocklength.
- **Averaging breaks equality:** with rotation-averaged SNRs defined as reciprocals of averaged distortions (SNR = 1/E_U[D(U)]), Jensen's inequality via concavity of t ↦ t/(1+t) yields SNR_MMSE ≥ SNR_MMSE,U + 1 — strict in general at finite d, so the classical equality fails precisely at the averaging step.
- **Asymptotic recovery:** SNR_MMSE − SNR_MMSE,U → 1 as d → ∞, because A(U) = [(1/d)ΣQ(V_i)²] / [(1/d)ΣV_i Q(V_i)]² concentrates to the deterministic limit b/a² under Haar rotation (with ‖G‖/√d → 1 for G ~ N(0, I_d)), so the realization-dependent EDEN scalings converge to the scalar-Gaussian CDEF scalings and the Jensen gap vanishes.
- **Exact conditional unbiasedness at finite d:** EDEN's Haar formulation with realization-dependent scaling satisfies E_U[Z] = X for every finite dimension (Theorem 2.1 of EDEN), strictly stronger than the CDEF second-order notion, where source-noise orthogonality (X ⊥ N) does not imply E[N|X] = 0.
- **Averaging gain of the forward channel:** under the forward-channel model, averaging m independently quantized descriptions reduces effective noise power to P_N/m, i.e., SNR_MMSE,U,ave = m·SNR_MMSE,U — the quantitative justification for treating quantization errors as additive uncorrelated noises in distributed mean estimation.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** Modest but genuine. The exact pointwise realization-level correspondence (Section 3) and especially the observation that the +1 identity holds per-realization but degrades to a strict inequality after distortion-averaging (the Jensen gap) appear to be new; the bulk of the note is a clean unification of known results rather than new theory.
- **Rigor:** The finite-d statements are proved or follow immediately from standard arguments. However, the large-d argument is presented heuristically — there is no explicit non-asymptotic concentration bound or convergence rate for the Jensen gap (e.g., no O(1/d) or high-probability bound on |A(U) − b/a²|), so the magnitude of the gap at any practical block dimension is left completely unquantified. For a note whose central message is "equality fails at finite d but is recovered asymptotically," the absence of any rate result is the most conspicuous weakness.
- **Idealized assumptions:** The correspondence explicitly assumes the reconstruction scale is "represented without quantization error" (Section 3). In any deployed system (EDEN/DRIVE included) the scale α_U must itself be transmitted and quantized; the exact conditional unbiasedness guarantee E_U[Z] = X rests on this idealization, and the note does not analyze the residual bias or distortion when the scale is coded at finite precision.
- **Scope:** The classical correspondence is for scalar quantizers optimized for a Gaussian source; vector quantization, overload/clipping, and the incoherence-processing used in actual LLM quantization pipelines (QuIP, QuaRot) are only mentioned as motivation. Practical implications for LLM quantization are therefore indirect — conceptual grounding rather than an applicable tool.
- **Reproducibility:** Trivially verifiable — every identity could be checked with a short Monte Carlo script; no code is provided or needed.
- **Red flags / positioning:** The attribution narrative (Section 2.5) favors the author's own prior line (Hadad & Erez 2016) in an ongoing priority conversation (cf. reference [6], the Ben-Basat et al. response to TurboQuant). The attributions themselves look factually fair and are supported by citations, but readers should be aware of the context: this note partly functions as historical-positioning within that exchange, not as neutral survey.

## Strengths

- Crisp, fully self-contained exposition that connects a 30-year-old classical result (CDEF 1995) to techniques now central in LLM quantization and federated learning, requiring only standard signal-processing background from the reader.
- The two-roles taxonomy (Gaussianization vs. error-decorrelation/dither) is genuinely clarifying — these roles are routinely conflated in the modern rotation-quantization literature, and the per-paper disentanglement (Hadad-Erez vs. Suresh et al. vs. EDEN) is the most useful part for practitioners.
- The subtle and easy-to-miss point that the pointwise identity 1/D_W(U) = 1/D_U(U) + 1 does not survive distortion-averaging (because SNR is defined as a reciprocal of an average, not an average of reciprocals) is isolated cleanly via a one-line Jensen argument.
- Careful epistemic hygiene around "unbiasedness": Remark 1's distinction between second-order unit-gain unbiasedness (X ⊥ N) and true conditional unbiasedness (E[N|X] = 0), plus the observation that EDEN achieves the stronger property at every finite d, preempts a common confusion.
- Honest framing: it is explicitly a "note," claims only what is proved, and even cautions (footnote 3) that the ubiquitous "SNR" terminology should be interpreted with caution.

## Limitations

- No numerics whatsoever: the size of the Jensen gap SNR_MMSE − (SNR_MMSE,U + 1) at practical block sizes (e.g., d = 512–4096 as used in EDEN/QuaRot-style pipelines) is never computed, and no explicit concentration rate in d is given — the asymptotic statement is essentially heuristic in style.
- The scale-side-information assumption (reconstruction scale unquantized) is a real gap relative to any practical system; its impact is not analyzed.
- Scalar-Gaussian focus: nothing is said about vector quantizers, non-Gaussian optimized quantizers, overload regions, or how the correspondence interacts with the weight-matrix incoherence processing used in LLM quantization.
- Very short format leaves interesting threads dangling — e.g., Haar vs. randomized Hadamard (efficiency vs. concentration) is mentioned in passing but never compared, and the extension to correlated (non-independent) client rotations is not discussed.
- As a conceptual/attributional note, it offers no new algorithm and no performance improvement a practitioner could measure.

## Relevance

随机旋转已成为 LLM 量化（QuIP、QuaRot、TurboQuant）与联邦学习梯度压缩（EDEN、DRIVE）的核心技术，本笔记为其中的 MMSE/无偏重建尺度选择提供了经典信息论（CDEF 理论）层面的严格解释，并厘清了旋转的"高斯化"与"误差去相关"两种角色，对理解和设计量化流水线中的缩放策略、多分支误差平均机制具有直接的理论参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How large is the Jensen gap SNR_MMSE − (SNR_MMSE,U + 1) at practical block dimensions (d = 512–4096), and can a non-asymptotic concentration bound (e.g., A(U) = b/a² + O(1/√d) with high probability, or an O(1/d) bound on the gap itself) be derived?
- What survives when the reconstruction scale α_U must itself be quantized: does exact conditional unbiasedness degrade gracefully, and what is the optimal bit allocation between the scale and the quantized shape?
- Do the realization-dependent CDEF correspondences and the concentration argument extend to randomized Hadamard transforms (the computationally cheap variant used in QuaRot-style pipelines and Hadad-Erez), where Haar symmetry arguments are unavailable?
- Can the two-roles taxonomy be turned into a quantitative design rule: for a given source distribution, bit budget, and number of averaging branches m, how should rotation resources be split between Gaussianization and cross-branch error decorrelation?
- Does the pointwise Pythagorean identity imply anything actionable for multi-description coding or incremental-refinement schemes (cf. Østergaard-Erez-Zamir) beyond the classical m-fold averaging gain SNR_MMSE,U,ave = m·SNR_MMSE,U?
