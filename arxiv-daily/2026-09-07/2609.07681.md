> **一句话总结：** 本文通过机制可解释性方法逆向工程出 Mamba 执行联想回忆（Associative Recall）的底层电路——隐式学习线性哈希函数把 key/value 双重压缩进固定状态里——并借助 Johnson–Lindenstrauss 引理推导出"回忆缩放律"：高概率完美回忆要求状态×嵌入维度 ND = O(Nf log V)（事实数线性、词表大小对数），该上界与信息论下界匹配，且在单层/多层/多头实验中被定量验证。

> **TL;DR:** Tel Aviv University researchers reverse-engineer the exact circuit Mamba uses to solve Multi-Query Associative Recall (MQAR): Conv1D forms shifted key-value token pairs, and the SSM projections act as Johnson–Lindenstrauss-style linear hashes that compress keys into the recurrent state and decompress values at readout — verified in trained weights via invariant operators (G_vv, G_kq) and hidden-state inversion (H'' ≈ H). From this they derive "Recall Scaling Laws": high-probability recall needs only N·D = O(N_f log V) (e.g., a 1-layer model with N=32, D=64 achieves perfect MQAR at V=512, versus the prior bound of Huang et al. requiring N=V_k=256), matching an information-theoretic lower bound of N·D = Ω(N_f log V). The probabilistic law p_recall ≈ Φ(√(ND/(aN_f+N)) − √(2 log V)) collapses D-N accuracy grids onto a single curve for both linear and full Mamba; a key ablation shows removing Conv1D collapses MQAR accuracy from 1.00 to 0.06 (=1/N_f guessing), while removing gating, discretization, and activations costs nothing.

## 核心贡献

- **逆向工程 Mamba 的回忆电路**：通过逐步消融定位出执行联想回忆的最小电路——Conv1D 负责"复制并移位"以形成 (key, value) 相邻 token 对，SSM 的 B/C 投影隐式计算相似度保持的线性哈希，隐状态 h_t = Σ x̂_τ B_τ^⊤ 本质上是一张压缩哈希表。
- **机制层面的实证验证**：在训练好的（线性）Mamba 中，定义对正交变换不变的等效算子 G_vv、G_kq，发现其结构与理论电路预测（G_vv ≈ [[0,0],[I,0]]，G_kq ≈ [[0,I],[0,0]]）几乎精确吻合；将隐状态投影回词表空间后 H''_t ≈ H_t，直接展示了"压缩-解压"哈希方案。
- **回忆缩放律（Recall Scaling Laws）**：利用 JL 引理两次压缩的理论框架，给出定量规律 p_recall ≈ Φ(√(ND/(aN_f+N)) − √(2 log V))：完美回忆的最坏情况要求 √(ND) = O(N_f log V)，而高概率回忆只需 ND = O(N_f log V)，即所需状态记忆随事实数线性、随词表大小对数增长。
- **下界匹配，证明最优性**：给出 Mamba 特定的下界 ND = Ω(N_f log V)（Remark 4.4）以及适用于任意固定状态循环模型的信息论下界 bS = Ω(N_f log V)（Lemma 4.5），与上界匹配。
- **扩展到多层与多头架构**：定理证明多层 Mamba 的回忆能力仅由有效状态大小 N_eff = ΛN 决定；对 Mamba-2 的四种多头模式（MQA/MKA/MVA/MHA）证明 p_MQA = p_MKA ≤ p_single = p_MVA ≤ p_MHA，只有同时扩大 key/query 维度的 MHA 能真正提升回忆容量。

## Key Contributions

- **Reverse-engineered Mamba's recall circuit**: via progressive ablation they isolate a minimal circuit — Conv1D performs copy-and-shift to bind adjacent (key, value) token pairs, and the SSM's input-dependent projections B_t, C_t implicitly compute similarity-preserving linear hash functions; the recurrent state h_t = Σ_τ x̂_τ B_τ^⊤ acts as a compressed hash table.
- **Mechanistic validation in trained models**: they define orthogonal-transformation-invariant effective operators (G_vv, G_kq) whose structure in trained linear Mamba matches the designed circuit almost exactly (Fig. 16), and show the compressed hidden state, when projected back to vocabulary space (Alg. 2), reconstructs the interpretable key-value table H''_t ≈ H_t (Fig. 3).
- **Recall Scaling Laws**: a JL-based framework yielding the quantitative law p_recall ≈ Φ(√(ND/(aN_f+N)) − √(2 log V)), with a = 1 (AR) or a ≈ 5/4 (MQAR). Ladder of guarantees: exact non-compressive recall needs N = D = V; exact compressive worst-case recall needs √(ND) = O(N_f log V); high-probability recall needs only ND = O(N_f log V).
- **Matching lower bounds**: a Mamba-specific Ω(N_f log V) lower bound on ND and a general information-theoretic lower bound bS = Ω(N_f log V) bits for any fixed-state recurrent model — making the scaling law essentially optimal.
- **Multi-layer and multi-head theory**: Theorem 4.6 shows depth contributes only through N_eff = ΛN; Theorem 4.7 ranks Mamba-2 head patterns p_MQA = p_MKA ≤ p_single = p_MVA ≤ p_MHA, predicting that shared-value compression (MQA/MKA) hurts recall while MHA is the only beneficial pattern.

## Methodology

1. **Simplified linear model (Alg. 1)**: a single-layer Mamba stripped of gating, discretization, nonlinearities, biases, normalization, and residuals, leaving embedding E, input projection P_in, Conv1D (width 2), SSM projections S_B, S_C, and output projection P_out. With A set to identity, the SSM reduces to h_t = h_{t−1} + x̂_t B_t^⊤, y_t = h_t C_t — an additive outer-product memory.
2. **Designed circuits**: Theorem 3.1 constructs a perfect but impractical circuit with one-hot (non-compressive) dimensions N = D = V; Theorem 3.2 constructs the compressive variant where JL matrices E ∈ R^{D×V} and Ẽ = FE ∈ R^{N×V} hash keys/queries into the state and values into the embedding space, achieving high-probability recall when ND = O(N_f log V) (mean-case; worst-case needs √(ND) = O(N_f log V)).
3. **Mechanistic interpretability verification**: because trained weights are only defined up to orthogonal transforms, they derive invariant net operators G_vv = Π_{v,out} Π_{v,in} and G_kq = Π_{k,in}^⊤ Π_{q,in} and check the predicted block structure (Eq. 11) in trained models; they also invert the hidden state onto vocabulary space (Alg. 2) to visualize the learned hash table, and verify Conv1D's copy-shift behavior via G_vv ξ_τ ≈ x_τ and G_kq^⊤ ξ_τ ≈ ξ_{τ−1}.
4. **Theory**: statistical analysis of the two-stage JL compression yields the probabilistic scaling law (Thm 4.2, unified in Rem. 4.3); lower bounds come from optimization of the normal-CDF tail (Rem. 4.4) and a counting/information argument (Lem 4.5). Multi-layer (Thm 4.6) and multi-head (Thm 4.7, MQA/MKA/MVA/MHA patterns from Mamba-2) extensions follow the same machinery.
5. **Experiments**: trained simplified linear and full (mamba-ssm) Mamba models on the original MQAR dataset (Arora et al., Zoology), sampled fresh batches every step; D-N accuracy grids (500–1000 points, 3–5 seeds each, best-of-seeds to measure capacity not trainability), scaling-curve collapse fits, circuit-minimality ablations, repeated-keys generalization, and larger-scale runs (V up to 50K, L up to 4K). Total compute ≈ 10 GPU-days on NVIDIA B200s.

## Results & Highlights

- **Circuit-minimality ablation (Table 8, MQAR with V=128, L=64, N_f=16, D=64, N=16, 5 seeds)**: removing layer-norm, discretization (Ā_t = I), the gate, or the activation all leave MQAR accuracy at 1.00 ± 0.00; shrinking the conv to d_conv=2 still gives 1.00 ± 0.00; but d_conv=1 (no Conv1D) collapses accuracy to 0.06 ± 0.00 ≈ 1/N_f — the model degenerates to guessing among the 16 in-context values, proving Conv1D's shift is what binds keys to values.
- **Scaling-law collapse (Fig. 4/8)**: trained-model D-N accuracy grids collapse onto the predicted one-dimensional curve p_recall(x) ≈ Φ(x − √(2 log V)) with x = √(ND/(aN_f+N)); a = 1 fits AR and a ≈ 5/4 fits MQAR for the linear model (more queries → larger variance), and the full nonlinear Mamba follows the same law with a_full ≈ ½ a_linear (fit heuristically), i.e., better performance under an identical scaling bottleneck.
- **Tightness vs prior work (App. J vs Huang et al.)**: a 1-layer Mamba empirically solves MQAR with V = 512 perfectly using N = 32 ≪ V_k = 256 and D = 64 ≪ V_k (Fig. 1 row iii), whereas Huang et al.'s bound requires N = V_k = 256 and D = O(V_k + log V_v); this work's ND = O(N_f log V) is exponentially tighter than O(V²)-type constructions and matches the Ω(N_f log V) information-theoretic lower bound.
- **Multi-layer law (Fig. 5/10)**: recall accuracy depends only on the product N_eff = ΛN — e.g., grid points (N,Λ) = (12,1), (6,2), (4,3), (3,4) all yield the same p_MQAR ≈ 0.38, exactly as Theorem 4.6 predicts.
- **Multi-head patterns (Fig. 6, Thm 4.7)**: at fixed channel dimension D, MVA matches the single-head baseline while MQA and MKA are strictly worse (shared-value compression across heads); only MHA improves recall by enlarging effective state — trends consistent with Mamba-2's own perplexity ablations (Dao et al. Sec. 9.4 Table 5).
- **Large-scale validation (Fig. 11/12)**: a linear model with D = 150, N = 75 across V = 1,000 → 50,000 and L = 1,000 → 4,000 (N_f = L/4) follows the √(2 log V) vocabulary law; per-L fits give slopes 1.18–1.56 against the theoretical slope of 1 — a 18–56% deviation the authors acknowledge as the price of the approximation.
- **Repeated keys (Fig. 18, V=512, L=128, N_f=10, D=100, N_eff=ΛN=60)**: with keys appearing N_k times, a single-layer model scores only ≈ 1/N_k (chance level among the N_k stored values) because every occurrence blends into the same state slot; two layers restore recall — layer 1 reads the stored value, layer 2 writes the difference, effectively overwriting the stale entry.

## Authors & Credibility

The five authors (Yuval Koren, Assaf Ben-Kish, Raja Giryes, Lior Wolf, Itamar Zimerman) are from the Blavatnik School of Computer Science and AI at Tel Aviv University. The group has a strong track record on Mamba/SSM analysis: Ben-Kish and Zimerman co-authored DeciMamba (ICLR 2025) and "Overflow Prevention Enhances Long-Context Recurrent LLMs"; Zimerman co-authored work on the expressivity of selective state-space layers; Wolf is a senior, highly-cited researcher. The paper is a careful theory+mechanistic-interpretability study: full proofs in appendices, open-source code (github.com/yuvalko1/mamba-recall-scaling-laws), a reproducibility statement with pinned versions (PyTorch 2.10, CUDA 12.8, B200 GPUs, ≈10 GPU-days total), per-figure compute accounting, and an unusually honest appendix on seed aggregation and the Huang et al. comparison.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: genuinely novel combination — prior mechanistic work (Bick et al.'s gather-and-aggregate) analyzed retrieval but not AR and gave no theory; prior theory (Huang et al. 2506.11891) used JL only on values, yielding the far weaker N = V_k, D = O(V_k + log V_v). The double-compression circuit plus matched upper/lower bounds ND = Θ(N_f log V) is a real advance. However, the core JL machinery is standard; the contribution is its insightful application.
- **Rigor**: proofs are provided and empirically checked, but two quantitative bridges are heuristic rather than derived: the full nonlinear Mamba's a_full ≈ ½ a_linear is "determined heuristically" (no theory for gating/nonlinearity), and the large-scale fits deviate from theory by slopes 1.18–1.56 vs 1 (Fig. 12) — acknowledged, but it means the "predictive" claim holds qualitatively, not to the percent.
- **Reproducibility**: strong — released code, vendored MQAR dataset, exact hyperparameters per experiment family (Table 9), seed counts, and compute budgets per grid. The main caveat for replicators is the **best-of-seeds metric** (App. K.5): grids report the max over 3–5 seeds, justified as measuring capacity ("existence of a recall solution") rather than trainability. Near the phase boundary individual seeds frequently fail to converge (their own Fig. 20), so a single-run reproduction will underperform the plotted accuracy — the paper is transparent about this, but the headline grids would look weaker as mean-over-seeds.
- **Significance**: the results are on synthetic MQAR/AR only; no language-modeling experiments are run. The link to real LM behavior is indirect — appealing to Mamba-2's published perplexity ablations for the multi-head ordering. The theory also assumes unique keys (repeated keys break the 1-layer circuit to chance level) and a simplified linear model whose relation to the full architecture is empirically calibrated, not proven.
- **Red flags**: none serious — no overclaimed SOTA, honest limitations section (role of the gating branch left open; xLSTM/RWKV/DeltaNet extensions future work). The weakest point is the heuristic a_full and the use of best-of-seeds, both disclosed.

## Strengths

- A complete loop: mechanistic discovery → circuit hypothesis → JL-based theory → matched lower bounds → large-scale empirical validation, with each stage informing the next.
- The hash-table interpretation of the Mamba hidden state (Alg. 2 inversion, H''_t ≈ H_t) is an interpretable, visual, and falsifiable verification that goes beyond weight-space coincidence checks.
- Actionable architectural guidance backed by theory: depth only helps via N_eff = ΛN, and among Mamba-2 head patterns only MHA (both key and query expansion) buys recall capacity — consistent with Mamba-2's LM perplexity data.
- The Conv1D ablation (1.00 → 0.06 at d_conv=1) is a crisp, memorable demonstration of why the shift operation is indispensable for key-value binding in selective SSMs.
- Excellent reproducibility hygiene: code, pinned environments, per-figure compute, seed-aggregation analysis, and a detailed head-to-head comparison with the closest prior work (App. J).

## Limitations

- Theory covers a simplified single-layer linear Mamba; gating, discretization, and nonlinearity are abstracted away, and the full model is fit by a heuristic constant (a_full ≈ ½ a_linear) rather than derived.
- Only synthetic AR/MQAR tasks; no direct language-modeling validation of the scaling laws, so transfer to real corpora remains assumed via the known MQAR–LM correlation.
- Best-of-seeds reporting measures capacity, not the probability that training finds the solution — practically relevant near the capacity boundary.
- The probabilistic law's constants (a = 5/4 for MQAR, 5/8 in another regime; b ≈ √(2 log V)) are approximate, and large-scale fits deviate by 18–56% in slope.
- Assumes unique keys in the main analysis; repeated keys degrade single-layer recall to 1/N_k chance, handled only empirically (depth) without a full theoretical treatment.

## Relevance

这篇论文与"LLM Hidden States & Interpretability"方向高度契合：它正是对 Mamba 隐状态的机制可解释性研究——逆向出具体的回忆电路、用不变算子和隐状态反演做实证验证，并把隐状态显式解读为一张 JL 压缩哈希表，是 circuits + representation geometry 分析在 SSM 上的典范之作。若你关注自进化智能体的长期记忆架构或循环模型的记忆瓶颈，其 ND = O(N_f log V) 容量定律也给出了状态大小必须随记忆条目线性增长的定量设计约束。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the hash-table circuit persist in production-scale Mamba LMs (e.g., 1B+ parameters trained on natural text), or is it only what small synthetic-trained models find because MQAR structure makes it the easiest solution?
- Can the theory be extended to derive a_full for the full (gated, discretized) Mamba block instead of fitting it heuristically at ½ a_linear?
- How does the scaling law change for architectures with matrix-valued states or delta-rule updates (DeltaNet, RWKV, xLSTM) — does their effective state size S still obey S = Θ(N_f log V), or do write/overwrite rules beat the additive blending limitation seen with repeated keys?
- Since multi-layer recall only compounds via N_eff = ΛN, why do deep Mamba LLMs help so much on real language tasks — is recall not the binding constraint at scale, or do nonlinear inter-layer interactions add capacity the linear theory misses?
- Could the JL-collision perspective predict specific recall error patterns (systematic confusions between similar keys) in trained LMs, testable with probing or activation patching at scale?
- What happens under low-precision states: the information-theoretic bound bS = Ω(N_f log V) suggests quantized recurrent states should hit a sharp recall cliff — can this be measured experimentally?
