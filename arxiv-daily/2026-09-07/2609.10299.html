Key Finding · MAIS-O43 · first empirical phase diagram

Near-perfect reconstruction, zero feature recovery — trained sparse autoencoders land in an unpredicted “diffuse phase.”

一句话总结：按 MAIS-O43 预注册规范（256 个真特征叠加于 64 维、父子嵌套结构）训练标准 ReLU+L1 稀疏自编码器，200 次规范拟合加 3,300 次全网格拟合中，零次完整字典恢复、零次特征合并——重构误差近乎为零，但中位最佳余弦仅 0.47–0.73（判据 0.95），"重构好"不等于"找到了真特征"。

Key Metric

0
/ 3,500 trained SAEs

full-dictionary recoveries across 200 canonical full-batch fits + 3,300 full-grid minibatch fits. Zero merges as well; pooled 95% zero-event upper bound 1.49% on the canonical runs.

Why This Matters

The most overlooked trap in SAE practice, quantified: near-zero reconstruction loss does not certify feature recovery. First calibration data between MAIS theory and trained SAEs.

Critical Edge

No gated, top-k, or Matryoshka variants were tested — the diffuse phase may simply be an artifact of the vanilla L1 architecture.

also: theory contrast pits a 2-feature proof vs a 256-feature amortized experiment — variables confounded

Methodology

Preregistered protocol, executed exactly

A synthetic generator draws m = 256 unit-norm Gaussian features in n = 64 dims, permutes them into 128 parent–child pairs, and nests the first ⌊128γ⌋; a vanilla ReLU+L1 SAE (unit-norm decoder columns, no output bias) trains on Gλ = E[½‖y−Ψc(y)‖² + λ‖c(y)‖₁] via full-batch Adam, 2×10ⁱ updates, 20 seeds per cell, float32 with TF32 disabled.

Outcomes are preregistered classifiers: recovery = injective matching with all cosines ≥0.95 (exact bipartite matching); merge = an atom within ε of αvi+βvk while cosining ≤0.9 with each parent (constrained least squares); split = ≥2 live atoms per feature at 0.95.

Backbone: 49-test adversarial suite · NumPy gradient cross-checks at 1e-12 · master seed 20260805 · checkpoints + SHA-256 data hashes + regeneration scripts public · solo independent author, implementation credited to “Claude Fable 5” with human-verified outputs.

Strengths

  • Clean, well-powered negative result with statistical care rare in SAE toy studies — 20 seeds per cell, zero-event binomial bounds, pooled analysis.
  • Two cheap, well-aimed falsification probes; the minibatch grid converts 6% canonical coverage into 100% off-protocol coverage at trivial cost.
  • Crisp conceptual point: the phase diagram of trained SAEs and that of objective minimizers are different objects — directly consequential for calibrating MAIS theory.
  • Actionable warning: at most 3.6% of features matched per canonical run despite near-zero error — SAE loss curves are not a sanity check for recovery.
  • Exceptional artifact release: preregistration, seeds, hashes, checkpoints, regeneration scripts.

Limitations

  • Canonical coverage is 10/165 cells; the full-grid null rests on an off-protocol optimizer (10.2–17.5 GPU-h per remaining cell).
  • γ confounds nesting with child rarity by design; the matched-frequency control is deferred to future work.
  • One generator draw (m/n=4, ~8 active features, n=64); generalization to other regimes — and to real LLM activations — is unestablished.
  • No SAE variants tested (gated, top-k, Matryoshka), so vanilla L1’s role in producing the diffuse phase is unknown.
  • “Trained ≠ minimizer” could stem from amortization, the many-feature distribution, finite samples, or the optimization path — the experiment cannot distinguish these.

Questions for Further Exploration

  1. Does the diffuse phase persist under gated, top-k, or Matryoshka SAEs — vanilla-L1 artifact, or a property of amortized sparse coding generally?
  2. Would a matched-frequency control (decoupling child rarity from dependence) restore any γ-dependence?
  3. What do minimizers of the amortized objective Gλ itself look like in this 256-feature regime — merged, diffuse, or recovered?
  4. Auxiliary losses, dead-atom resampling, larger M/n: can they push matched fractions from ~4% toward the 0.95 criterion, and where is the critical M?
  5. Does the diffuse phase appear on real LLM activations, detectable via code-density ratio or atom–atom coherence rather than ground-truth cosine?
  6. How sensitive are the phase boundaries to the generator draw — is the null a property of the regime or of one seed?
