A2 Why this matters

One cheap random push recovers a model's default-behavior set — a better pre-deployment safety audit than any direction-geometry method.

Bears directly on activation-steering safety edits and on "probe-decodable ⇒ controllable" claims, which this paper falsifies.

01 Methodology

One pipeline, ten models, zero improvisation

Developed on Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, then replicated unchanged on nine more instruction-tuned models. Every effect is judged from what the model writes, never from activations.

  1. Directions. 24 behaviors × 12 contrastive triples; difference-of-means vectors in the residual stream, layer chosen by held-out AUROC within the 25–85% depth band.
  2. Judge calibration. Per-behavior steering coefficient tuned so an LLM judge's 0–10 rating rises ~2 points while a bigram-coherence floor (≥ 0.6) holds.
  3. Interference matrix. Mij = judged change in behavior j when steering i, standardized by pooled sd; significance via 2,000-resample prompt bootstrap.
  4. Structure. Symmetric/antisymmetric split, SVD rank profile, and an additive ai + bj fit yielding source drive and susceptibility per behavior.
  5. Random push control. Behavior-free directions matched to real steers only in added norm.
  6. Geometry on trial. 13 pair-level geometric features, ridge + boosted trees, leave-one-behavior-out CV holding out all 38 pairs per behavior.

Sinks: sycophancy, refusal, poeticism absorb the most push (10 of 20 behaviors are significant sinks; the unsteered non-resistors average 0.04/10 judge points vs 3.78 for the resistant six).

  • Decodable ≠ steerable. All 24 behaviors are linearly decodable at held-out AUROC 1.000, yet only 20 move Qwen2.5-7B's output; the steerable count rises from 13/24 (Qwen2.5-3B) to 24/24 (Llama-3.3-70B).
  • The standard fix fails. Orthogonalizing a steer against all other behavior directions changes side effects by −0.03 sd while halving the on-target effect (2.2 → 1.2 judge points).
  • Geometry fails out-of-sample. Best held-out Spearman 0.41; no variant reaches held-out R² 0.10 (boosted trees: in-sample 0.66 vs held-out 0.09 — memorization).
  • Judge ablation holds. Re-judging three models with fixed Qwen2.5-72B preserves structure (hierarchy ρ 0.74–0.83); only exact top-sink membership reshuffles (Jaccard as low as 0.33).

03 Strengths

  • Near-decisive control: the random-push experiment cleanly separates the model's contribution from the direction's content.
  • Methodological self-awareness: diagnoses probe circularity and pair-level holdout leakage before building the measurement.
  • Text-grounded construct: what the model writes, with coherence floors, label-shuffled nulls, and bootstrap CIs throughout.
  • Broad replication: identical pipeline across ten models and four families (1.5B–72B), plus a fixed-judge ablation.

04 Limitations

  • Refutation, not theory: the defaults account explains only 0.31 of off-diagonal magnitude; most interference is captured by neither geometry nor ranking.
  • Small base for fine claims: one model, 32 prompts, 12 triples per behavior behind the 380 bootstrap intervals.
  • Judge sensitivity: LLM-judge readout inherits bias; exact sink membership shifts (top-sink Jaccard as low as 0.33).
  • Narrow steering regime: single-layer, fixed coefficient grid; multi-layer or learned vectors — and near-threshold strengths — untested.
  • Style-heavy behavior set; why the default-pull weakens at 70B+ is left open.

05 Questions for further exploration

  • Does the roll-toward-defaults hold for capability behaviors graded by correctness, not an LLM judge?
  • Can learned or multi-layer steering vectors escape the default-set pull, or does any comparable-norm perturbation land in the same sinks?
  • What mechanically causes the default set (refusal, sycophancy, poeticism) — instruction-tuning data — and can it shrink without losing resistant prosocial behaviors?
  • Why does the pull weaken at 70B+ — broader robustness, or too many defaults for one ranking to capture?
  • How many random pushes and prompts suffice to recover a sink set at fixed confidence, replacing geometry-based audits?
  • Do one-way links imply a causal hierarchy the model can be pushed along for deliberate multi-behavior control?
