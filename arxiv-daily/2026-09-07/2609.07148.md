> **一句话总结：** 提出 Stable-MM-R1 数据中心稳定化框架：通过 PAQM（按 Pass@K 与 Path Entropy 动态筛选"蒸馏区"可学查询并用 Seed-1.5-VL 教师提示回收约 30% 困难样本）+ HSR（用稳定性锚点/难负例/顿悟样本构造高对比 GRPO 优化组、仅步内重放），在不修改 RL 目标函数的前提下抑制熵坍缩，使 Qwen2.5-VL-7B 在 10 个多模态基准上平均 56.2%，超最强开源 7B 基线 Vision-G1（46.5%）9.7 个百分点，且 Path Entropy 全程稳定在 0.35–0.60 nats 而不坍缩。

> **TL;DR:** A data-centric (not objective-centric) fix for unstable GRPO-style RL on multimodal reasoning models. Phase 0 (PAQM) filters the prompt set into a learnable "Distillation Zone" via Pass@K + Path Entropy and reclaims ~30% of Impossible queries with offline Seed-1.5-VL hints; Phases 1–2 (HSR) stratify each step's rollouts into Stability Anchors / Hard Negatives / Epiphanies and assemble half-structured, half-natural optimization groups with intra-step-only buffers, guaranteeing non-zero group reward variance when both outcome classes exist. From Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct, it averages 56.2% across 10 benchmarks (Pass@8), +9.7 points over the strongest evaluated open 7B baseline Vision-G1 (46.5%), with the largest jump on MathVision (53.1 vs 32.9 for ThinkLite-7B-VL, +20.2), while GRPO/DAPO baselines collapse after ~step 250 and DAPO's Path Entropy decays to ~0.2 nats vs Stable-MM-R1's 0.35–0.60. The controlled in-house ablation shows +3.1 avg from PAQM and +2.0 more from HSR over the same hint curriculum (60.7 → 65.8 on a 3-benchmark average).

## 核心贡献

- **识别"随机组退化"这一不稳定性来源**：从数据中心视角论证 GRPO 式训练的不稳定很大程度上并非目标函数本身的问题，而是随机 rollout 组合产生的退化组——当组内所有 rollout 结果相同（全对或全错）时组相对优势为零（梯度消失），论文给出退化概率 P_degen = p^G + (1−p)^G 的形式化分析。
- **PAQM（潜力感知查询挖掘）**：对每个查询采样 N=16 个 rollout 估计经验成功率 p̂(x)，划分为蒸馏区（0<p̂<1，可学）、平凡区（p̂=1，降采样）与不可能区（p̂=0）；对不可能区做"模态解耦诊断"（用真值 caption 探测是感知缺陷还是认知缺陷），分别注入视觉分解提示或关键推理节点提示（由 Seed-1.5-VL 教师离线生成），将约 30% 困难查询回收到蒸馏区。
- **HSR（混合分层重放）**：以 Path Entropy（长度归一化的序列惊喜度，作为 rollout 级置信度代理）+ 结果奖励对当前步 rollout 做动态百分位分层（τ_low=10%、τ_high=90% 批自适应阈值），构造稳定性锚点（低熵正确）、难负例（低熵错误）、顿悟样本（高熵正确）三类缓冲区；优化组预算五五分为结构化流（25% 顿悟 + 12.5% 锚点 + 12.5% 难负例）与自然流（均匀采样），且缓冲区**仅步内存活、梯度计算后立即清空**，保持严格 on-policy 而无需重要性采样修正。
- **理论与实证结合**：证明只要锚点与难负例配额满足，结构化组的经验奖励方差严格为正（σ̂² = N₊N₋/G²），从而减少零信号组；在 Qwen2.5-VL-7B/3B 上的系统实验与消融验证了稳定性和性能提升。

## Key Contributions

- **Diagnose stochastic group degeneracy as an instability source**: argues GRPO instability is largely a data/sampling problem, not an objective problem — randomly composed groups with identical outcomes yield vanishing group-relative advantages; formalizes the degenerate-group probability as P_degen = p^G + (1−p)^G.
- **Potential-Aware Query Mining (PAQM)**: estimates per-query success rate p̂(x) from N=16 pilot rollouts and zones queries into Distillation Zone (0<p̂<1), Trivial (p̂=1, downsampled), and Impossible (p̂=0); applies Modality-Decoupled Diagnosis to Impossible queries (ground-truth-caption probes separating perceptual vs. cognitive deficits) and injects Visual Decomposition or Key-Reasoning-Node hints generated offline by the Seed-1.5-VL teacher, reclaiming roughly 30% of hard queries into the learnable frontier.
- **Hybrid Stratified Replay (HSR)**: defines Path Entropy (length-normalized sequence surprisal of a rollout) as a confidence proxy, stratifies current-step rollouts via batch-adaptive 10th/90th-percentile thresholds into Stability Anchors, Hard Negatives, and Epiphanies (Confusion samples discarded), then assembles a 50% structured + 50% natural optimization group; buffers live for exactly one step (intra-step replay), preserving on-policy consistency without importance-sampling corrections.
- **Conditional variance guarantee**: shows that whenever at least one anchor and one hard negative are available, the structured group's empirical reward variance is strictly positive (σ̂² = N₊N₋/G_struct²), reducing zero-signal updates; honestly frames this as a conditional bound, not a per-step guarantee, and acknowledges the deliberate distribution shift.
- **Strong empirical validation**: on Qwen2.5-VL-7B/3B across 10 multimodal reasoning benchmarks, with ablations isolating the hint curriculum (+3.1 avg) from the structured batch topology (+2.0 avg) and stability analyses showing avoided entropy collapse.

## Methodology

The framework post-trains a multimodal LLM with RL-only updates (no supervised cold start) and stabilizes training purely by controlling what data the optimizer sees at each step:

1. **Phase 0 — PAQM (curriculum).** For each sampled query x, generate N=16 pilot rollouts from the current policy and estimate p̂(x). Queries with 0<p̂<1 form the "Distillation Zone" (learnable); p̂=1 is downsampled; p̂=0 triggers Modality-Decoupled Diagnosis: the model is probed with ground-truth captions — if it solves the text-only probe, the failure is perceptual and a Visual Decomposition Hint (coordinates/shapes from an auxiliary vision model) is injected; otherwise a Key Reasoning Node distilled from the teacher's trace is injected. All hints are pre-cached offline from the proprietary Seed-1.5-VL model and used only for prompt augmentation, never for supervised parameter updates.
2. **Path Entropy.** A rollout-level confidence score: H_path(y|x) = −(1/|y|) Σ_t log π_θ(y_t | x, y_<t), the length-normalized sequence surprisal. The authors are careful to note it is a per-sample proxy, not the Shannon entropy of the predictive distribution or an epistemic-uncertainty estimate.
3. **Phase 1 — Stratification.** Pool Path-Entropy scores across the current global batch, set τ_low = 10th and τ_high = 90th percentiles, and sort rollouts into: Stability Anchors (r=1, H<τ_low), Hard Negatives (r=0, H<τ_low), Epiphanies (r=1, H>τ_high); Confusion samples (r=0, H>τ_high) are excluded as noise.
4. **Phase 2 — Hybrid group assembly.** The total group budget G=16 splits into Stream A (50%, structured): 25% of G Epiphanies + 12.5% Anchors + 12.5% Hard Negatives (with fallback to natural sampling when quotas are unmet), and Stream B (50%, natural): uniform sampling from the valid pool to prevent overfitting to the artificial topology. Buffers are cleared immediately after the gradient step (intra-step replay, lifespan τ=1), so all reused samples come from the current policy — no importance-sampling corrections needed.
5. **Theory (Appendix A.3).** Random groups degenerate with probability p^G+(1−p)^G; structured groups containing ≥1 positive and ≥1 negative have strictly positive reward variance. The estimator is deliberately biased toward informative boundary cases (acknowledged distribution shift), with Stream B retaining natural on-policy coverage.

**Setup**: EasyR1 codebase; base models Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct (and 3B); vision encoder frozen, only the LLM backbone updated; global batch 128, LR 1e-6, rollout temperature 1.0, N=G=16, 400 steps, 8×H100. RL prompt corpus of ~189k verifiable samples from Geometry3K + a curated MM-Eureka subset + ViRL39K, decontaminated against eval sets via MinHash LSH (text, >0.8 Jaccard) and pHash (images).

## Results & Highlights

- **7B average across 10 benchmarks**: Stable-MM-R1-7B reaches 56.2% average (Pass@8 majority vote) vs. 46.5% for the strongest evaluated open-source 7B baseline Vision-G1 (**+9.7 points**); it also beats MM-Eureka-Qwen-7B (46.0%), MMR1-Math-v0 (45.9%), VL-Rethinker-7B (45.7%), and the Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct base (43.3%).
- **MathVision**: 53.1% vs. 32.9% for the best open 7B baseline (ThinkLite-7B-VL) — **+20.2 points** — and +28.0 points over the Qwen2.5-VL-7B base (25.1%); on MathVerse-V it scores 56.3% vs. Vision-G1's 50.0% (+6.3).
- **Honest weak spots**: on MathVista it is 75.5% vs. Vision-G1's 76.1% (**−0.6**) and on MMStar 65.9% vs. 66.0% (−0.1) — the average lead comes from the other eight benchmarks (e.g., DynaMath-W 39.3% vs. 27.4%, +11.9; WeMath 49.6%, +11.7; MMMU 67.2%, +8.5; MMMU-Pro 52.3%, +9.0).
- **3B scale**: Stable-MM-R1-3B averages 46.2% vs. 37.7% for the best evaluated open 3B (VLAA-Thinker-3B) — **+8.5 points** — showing the method transfers across scales; still ~17.6 points below Gemini-2.5-Pro-Thinking (73.8%).
- **Controlled ablation (3-benchmark avg: MathVista/MathVision/MMMU)**: GRPO baseline 60.7 → DAPO 62.0 (+1.3) → +PAQM 63.8 (+3.1) → +HSR alone 64.7 (+4.0) → Full Stable-MM-R1 65.8 (**+5.1 over GRPO**); comparing Full vs. +PAQM isolates a **+2.0-point** gain attributable specifically to structured batch topology under the same hint-augmented curriculum.
- **Training stability**: GRPO shows large Path-Entropy fluctuations followed by a sharp decline and GRPO/DAPO baselines collapse after ~step 250, while Stable-MM-R1's Path Entropy stays within ~0.35–0.60 nats through step 400 with reward near its late-training plateau; DAPO's Path Entropy decays to ~0.2 nats.
- **Hyperparameter sensitivity**: 50% structured-stream ratio is optimal (>75% overfits the replay buffer, <25% fails to prevent entropy collapse); 10th/90th percentile thresholds are robust (5th percentile depletes anchor buffers, 20th admits noisy anchors).
- **PAQM reclaim rate**: the Modality-Decoupled Diagnosis hint injection reclaims roughly 30% of Impossible-zone queries back into the Distillation Zone.

## Authors & Credibility

Eleven authors across Columbia University, CUHK, Tsinghua University (THU), and McGill University, with two equal-contribution first authors (Yimeng Ye, Shuang Chen/Shawn Chen). The paper is unusually transparent for this subfield: it explicitly reports benchmarks where it loses to Vision-G1 (MathVista −0.6, MMStar −0.1), discloses that GRPO/DAPO baselines' numbers are their *peak pre-collapse* performance (a disclosure that favors the baselines), carefully caveats that Path Entropy is a per-sample surprisal proxy rather than distribution entropy, and states that the variance guarantee is conditional rather than per-step. The theoretical appendix (degenerate-group probability, conditional variance, distribution-shift trade-off) is modest but matched to what the method actually does. Use of AI assistants for text polish and code syntax verification is disclosed. No code release is mentioned in the main text, and the hint cache depends on a proprietary teacher (Seed-1.5-VL), which limits independent verification.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: moderate-to-good. The core insight — stabilize GRPO by controlling group composition rather than the objective — is genuinely data-centric and the Path-Entropy × reward 2×2 stratification (Anchors / Hard Negatives / Epiphanies / Confusion) is a clean, reusable idea. However, it sits in a crowded 2025–2026 space (Shuffle-R1's rollout silencing, MMR1's variance-aware sampling, DAPO, QAE, EntroPIC), and the intra-step-only "replay" is essentially structured batch construction rather than replay in the RL sense — the naming oversells the mechanism slightly.
- **Rigor**: the in-house ablation is well controlled (same base model, prompt pool, G=16, update count, evaluation protocol) and separates the teacher-hint effect (+3.1) from the HSR effect (+2.0). But the headline "+9.7 over Vision-G1" cross-model comparison is *not* controlled: baselines were trained by other teams with different data and recipes, while Stable-MM-R1 enjoys a 189k curated corpus and Seed-1.5-VL distillation hints the baselines never had. The controlled delta is +5.1 (Table 2), which is the honest headline.
- **Missing ablations**: the 25/12.5/12.5 buffer composition within Stream A is never ablated (only the overall stream ratio and percentile thresholds are swept), and the authors themselves admit Figure 3 cannot attribute effects to individual buffer categories — so the claim that Anchors and Hard Negatives specifically (rather than just high-entropy-success upweighting) drive the gain is not isolated. There is also no wall-clock/compute accounting for the PAQM pilot rollouts and teacher generation, only an admission that "update count matched, data-preparation cost did not."
- **Reproducibility**: weak. The pipeline hinges on a proprietary teacher (Seed-1.5-VL) for the hint cache, no code or data release is referenced, and the 3-benchmark ablation subset differs from the 10-benchmark main table. The 30% reclaim rate and the ~0.35–0.60 nats entropy band are single-run figures with no seed variance reported.
- **Potential data quirk**: hint-augmented prompts blur the "RL-only" framing — injecting a teacher's reasoning nodes into prompts is a form of distillation signal, and while the +PAQM ablation accounts for it performance-wise, the trained policy may be partly learning to exploit hint formatting absent at test time (no analysis of with/without-hint eval gap is given).
- **Red flags**: none severe; the entropy-collapse narrative rests partly on baselines collapsing after step 250, but only 400 total steps are run, so the "long-horizon" stability claim is under-tested relative to real post-training runs of thousands of steps.

## Strengths

- Clean problem decomposition: instability is attacked at data ingestion (PAQM) and group composition (HSR) without touching the GRPO objective, making the recipe orthogonal to and composable with objective-centric fixes (DAPO, clipping, KL control).
- The intra-step-only buffer design is an elegant way to keep "replay" strictly on-policy, avoiding importance-sampling corrections — a genuinely practical engineering contribution.
- Strong, broad empirical coverage (10 benchmarks, 3B and 7B scales, proprietary reference points) with unusually candid reporting of losses (MathVista, MMStar) and of the baseline peak-performance protocol.
- The conditional variance analysis, degenerate-group probability, and distribution-shift caveat in the appendix match theory to practice without overclaiming.
- Thorough hyperparameter sensitivity for the two new knobs (stream ratio, percentile thresholds) with clear failure modes identified on both sides.

## Limitations

- Depends on the proprietary Seed-1.5-VL teacher for offline hint generation, introducing potential distillation bias, licensing/access constraints, and extra offline compute that is not reflected in the matched update-count comparison.
- Only verifiable-outcome reasoning (math, charts, perception) is studied; entropy-guided stratification for open-ended generation is explicitly left as future work.
- Buffer composition (25/12.5/12.5) and per-category buffer effects are not ablated; the attribution of gains to Anchors/Hard Negatives vs. Epiphany upweighting remains indirect.
- PAQM's filtering by current capability risks algorithmic exclusion — data the base model currently deems "Impossible" (e.g., certain dialects or visual concepts) can be systematically dropped from training, amplifying pre-existing biases; the authors flag this and recommend auditing the Discarded partition.
- Training horizon (400 steps), single-seed runs, and no public code/hint cache limit confidence in and replication of the stability claims.

## Relevance

这篇论文直接命中"RL 后训练稳定性 / 熵坍缩 / GRPO 改进"这一多模态推理大模型训练的核心痛点，其数据中心（数据选择与批次构造）视角与 Path Entropy 置信度代理可用于任何可验证奖励的 RL 微调流程，与用户对 LLM/MLLM 推理与 RL 训练动态的关注高度相关。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How much of the HSR gain survives when the Epiphany upweighting is removed but Anchors/Hard Negatives are kept (and vice versa) — i.e., is the benefit from contrast, from exploration bias, or from variance reduction alone?
- Would replacing the proprietary Seed-1.5-VL teacher with a self-generated or open-source teacher (e.g., Qwen2.5-VL-72B) collapse the +3.1 PAQM gain, and does hint format leak into eval behavior?
- Does the 0.35–0.60 nats entropy band remain stable at 2,000+ steps, larger group sizes (G>16), or with DAPO-style dynamic sampling as the base optimizer instead of vanilla GRPO?
- Can Path Entropy be replaced by cheaper proxies (mean token log-prob already computed during rollout, or a small value head) without losing the stratification signal, given that percentile thresholding already normalizes scale?
- How does PAQM's Impossible-zone exclusion interact with distributional bias over the training corpus — does the reclaimed 30% cover the full difficulty spectrum, or only the teacher-easy subset?
- Since Stream A and Stream B both draw from the same step's rollouts, can a single rollout appear in both streams or be duplicated within a group, and does deduplication matter for the variance guarantee?
