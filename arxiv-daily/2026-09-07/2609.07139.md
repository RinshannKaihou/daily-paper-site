> **一句话总结：** 在 Llama-3.3-70B 上用自建的 ExpertCollab 多轮研究规划对话语料发现：对话伙伴的专业水平在第 8 层即可被线性探针以 0.79 准确率解码（4 类、随机 0.25），但在该层注入反事实激活差异的因果效应仅为 0.01，直到第 36 层之后（0.86+）才几乎完全传导到输出——"可解码"与"被使用"之间隔着半个网络的深度，说明隐式个性化/谄媚等行为的干预应在网络后半段而非最易读层进行。

> **TL;DR:** This Sci-FM@COLM 2026 workshop paper asks whether the known gap between where an attribute is linearly decodable and where it causally acts also holds for an attribute a model must *infer* over a conversation — its dialogue partner's expertise. On their new ExpertCollab corpus (28 twelve-turn dialogues between Llama-played personas at 4 expertise levels), partner expertise peaks at 0.79 cross-validated probe accuracy at layer 8 of Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct but decays to near chance by layer 40, while a lexical surface baseline reaches only 0.32; counterfactual patching at the peak-decoding layer produces a near-zero normalized causal effect (0.01) versus 0.86 at layer 36 and >0.90 past layer 40 — an order-of-magnitude separation. Practical upshot: steering partner-conditioned behavior (e.g., sycophancy) must target the network's second half, not the most-readable layer.

## 核心贡献

- **将"可解码 ≠ 被使用"的检验从显式属性扩展到推断属性**：此前该分离只在输入中直接给出的属性（用户属性、SAE 特征、ViT 计数）上验证过；本文首次考察模型必须在多轮对话中逐步推断的关系属性——对话伙伴的专业水平。
- **发布 ExpertCollab 语料**：28 段各 12 轮的合成研究规划对话，由 Llama-3.3-70B 扮演 4 个专业等级（高中生/初级研究者/研究者/教授）的 7 种配对 × 4 个研究场景；人设只规定沟通风格与盲区、禁止陈述资历，且泄漏检测器确认全部 28 段对话无显式资历泄漏——专业水平只能通过行为方式推断。
- **分离"解码位置"与"因果位置"的受控范式**：在参与者视角位置提取残差流激活，用 L2 逻辑回归探针定位可解码层，再用反事实补丁（注入 source−target 激活差异、读出层固定在最后一层）检验因果传导，并配备内容匹配随机向量对照与免探针诊断双重校验。
- **结构性发现**：推断属性在早期层高度可解码但因果惰性，越过网络中点（Lad et al. 2025 的 midpoint transition）后才变成因果活跃；而显式给定的对照属性（说话者自身等级）全程可解码。这为可解释性研究确立了一条规则：探针结果必须配合干预才能下功能性结论。
- **可证伪假说**：峰值可解码层与因果 onset 之间的深度差随属性"需要被推断的程度"增长——通过操纵属性显式程度应能使因果 onset 单调移动，把观察转化为定量规律。

## Key Contributions

- Extends the decodable-vs-causal gap test from attributes *stated* in the input (prior work on ViTs, SAE features, user attributes) to a *relational, inferred* attribute the model must accumulate across dialogue turns: how expert its partner is.
- Releases **ExpertCollab**, a corpus of 28 twelve-turn research-planning dialogues where Llama-3.3-70B role-plays both speakers at four expertise levels (7 persona pairings × 4 scenarios); personas specify style and blind spots but never state credentials, and a leakage detector passes all 28 transcripts.
- A controlled paradigm pairing linear probing at the participant-perspective position with counterfactual patching against a fixed last-layer readout, plus a content-matched random-vector control and a probe-free cosine-dissimilarity diagnostic — two independent readouts locate the same transition.
- A structural finding: the inferred attribute is decodable early (layer 8) yet causally inert there, becoming causal only past the network midpoint (layer ~36), while the stated own-level control stays decodable throughout — decodability alone does not establish function.
- A falsifiable hypothesis: the depth gap between peak decodability and causal onset grows with how much an attribute must be inferred rather than stated, testable by varying attribute explicitness.

## Methodology

The pipeline has four stages. **Generate:** 28 synthetic dialogues (12 turns each, 400-token cap per turn) between two model-played agents drawn from four expertise levels (high-school student, junior researcher, researcher, professor) across four research scenarios (speculative decoding, GNN robustness, scaling laws, soil microbiome). Generation uses Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct-Turbo via Together AI; both speakers played by one model removes cross-role style confounds. **Extract:** activations are read from the residual stream of Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct (80 layers, d=8192, via NDIF/nnsight) at the exact position the model occupies immediately before generating its next turn — the partner's turns formatted as user messages, its own as assistant messages. Every fourth layer (0–76) is sampled; both speakers are pooled into 56 instances. **Probe:** an L2-regularized logistic-regression probe is fit at each layer and turn to predict the partner's level (4-class), with the speaker's own (system-prompt-stated) level as a stated-attribute control; a five-feature lexical classifier (hedging rate, sentence length, question rate, type-token ratio, vocabulary complexity) serves as a surface baseline. **Patch:** for the 48 cross-level dialogue pairs, the source-minus-target activation difference Δ is injected into the target's residual stream at a layer, and the normalized causal effect E(L) is measured as the probe's source-class confidence recovery on patched vs. unpatched activations with the readout fixed at the last layer (E≈1 means full propagation). Controls: a norm-matched random vector replaces Δ; a probe-free check records top-5 token overlap and final-layer cosine dissimilarity between patched and unpatched runs.

## Results & Highlights

- **Early decodability, fast decay:** partner-expertise probe accuracy peaks at **0.79 cross-validated accuracy at layer 8** (4-class, chance 0.25) and falls to **near chance by layer 40**; the stated own-level control peaks at **1.00 at layer 0** and stays **above 0.60 at every layer** — the architecture can carry a stated attribute forward, but not the inferred one.
- **Order-of-magnitude decode/causal separation:** patching at the peak-decoding layer 8 yields a normalized causal effect of **0.01**, versus **0.86 at layer 36** and **>0.90 from layer 40** (calibration check: same-layer patch+readout averages 1.01); the transition zone L20–36 aligns with the midpoint transition of Lad et al. (2025).
- **The signal is not lexical:** the five-feature surface baseline reaches only **0.32 accuracy vs. 0.786 for the neural probe** at the peak cell (layer 8, turn 5, n=56) — above the 0.25 chance level but 0.45 below the probe, so the early encoding captures structure beyond surface statistics; probe errors concentrate on adjacent expertise levels.
- **Controls converge:** the norm-matched random-vector control produces no comparable rise in causal effect (with far wider intervals), and the probe-free cosine dissimilarity between patched and unpatched final-layer activations rises sharply from layer 8 and **saturates near 0.40 by layer 32** — two independent readouts locate the same transition.
- **Output-level impact:** late-layer patches change about **one in three of the five most probable next tokens**, while peak-decoding-layer (L8) patches leave the top-5 nearly untouched; recovery is direction-symmetric and holds across turns and domains.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** genuine incremental novelty — the decodability/causality gap was known for stated attributes (Huang & Chang 2025; Arad et al. 2025; Choi et al. 2025); the contribution is extending it to an attribute inferred relationally over turns, plus the clean stated-vs-inferred within-corpus contrast.
- **Rigor:** controls are unusually thorough for a workshop paper (calibration patch, norm-matched random control, probe-free diagnostic, leakage detector, stated-attribute control). But statistical power is thin: 28 base conversations, 56 pooled probe instances, 48 patch pairs; the authors themselves note "the single peak cell is not reliably identifiable at this sample size," so layer-8-vs-layer-12 fine structure should not be over-read.
- **Reproducibility:** strong — prompts reproduced verbatim, code and corpus released, NDIF/nnsight extraction described in detail. Same-model generation (-Turbo endpoint) vs. probing (-Instruct via NDIF) is a minor variant mismatch worth noting.
- **Significance:** the practical steering implication (intervene in the second half, not the peak-probe layer) is real but demonstrated only on synthetic, self-generated data; no evidence yet that the same boundary holds for real user interactions or for sycophancy specifically.
- **Red flags:** (1) the probed model generated the corpus, so the "expertise" signal may partly reflect recognition of its own generation patterns — the authors flag this honestly; (2) single model (Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct), so the layer boundaries are likely architecture-dependent; (3) probing every 4th layer bounds depth resolution to ±4 layers; (4) the Appendix F hypothesis is entirely untested; (5) expertise validity rests on persona design plus auxiliary LLM-as-judge checks that are explicitly excluded from causal claims — no human validation of the 4-level manipulation.

## Strengths

- Clean question with a crisp answer: decodability location (L8) and causal onset (L~36) are separated by more than an order of magnitude in effect size, established with converging methods.
- The stated-attribute (own level) control within the same corpus is an elegant design that isolates *inferredness* as the varying factor rather than attribute content.
- Corpus construction hygiene: credential leakage screening, verbatim prompt release, direction-symmetry and cross-turn/cross-domain robustness checks.
- Honest scoping: the paper explicitly frames itself as "one model on a synthetic corpus as an initial demonstration" and converts its conjecture into a testable prediction.

## Limitations

- Single model, single synthetic corpus of 28 base dialogues; no cross-model or cross-architecture replication, and no real-conversation data.
- Self-generation confound: the corpus is generated by the model family later probed, so the representation may partly encode self-generation recognition rather than partner modeling.
- Coarse layer sampling (every 4th of 80 layers) and small n make fine-grained layer claims (e.g., the exact peak layer) statistically fragile.
- The causal readout is fixed at the final layer (76) and measured via a probe trained at that layer; onset depth could shift with a different readout.
- No behavioral demonstration: the paper infers steering implications for sycophancy/implicit personalization but does not test steering of actual partner-conditioned behavior.

## Relevance

直接命中"LLM Hidden States & Interpretability"方向：本文用线性探针 + 反事实补丁研究残差流中推断属性的表示位置与因果位置分离，其"探针可解码 ≠ 因果使用"的方法论教训对所有依赖探针定位再做干预（如行为引导、表示工程）的可解释性工作都是必要前提。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the decode-to-causal gap scale with inference difficulty as the Appendix F hypothesis predicts — e.g., does making expertise explicit in the prompt monotonically move causal onset toward layer 8?
- Does the L20–36 causal onset replicate across architectures (different depths, MoE models, non-Llama families), or is it an artifact of Llama-3.3's midpoint vocabulary-alignment transition?
- Since the corpus is model-generated, how much of the layer-8 signal is partner-modeling versus self-generation recognition — would human-written or cross-model dialogues shift the peak?
- Can steering vector interventions in the second half (L36+) actually change partner-conditioned behavior like sycophancy, and at what fidelity cost?
- Do other inferred relational attributes (partner intent, emotional state, deception) show the same early-encode/late-use profile, or does the gap depend on how gradually the attribute accumulates over turns?
