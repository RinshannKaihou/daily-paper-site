> **一句话总结：** 该文是 MAIS-O43 开放问题的首次实测：在 256 个真特征叠加于 64 维、带父子嵌套结构的合成数据上训练标准 ReLU+L1 稀疏自编码器，200 次规范协议拟合（外加 3,300 次 minibatch 全网格拟合）中既无一次完整字典恢复也无一次特征合并，全部落入"扩散相"——重构误差近乎为零，但中位最佳余弦仅 0.47–0.73（恢复阈值 0.95）、编码比真值稠密 10–28 倍，说明"重构好"不等于"找到了真特征"，且训练所得 SAE 的相图与理论最优解的相图是两个不同的对象。

> **TL;DR:** The first measurements of the MAIS-O43 phase diagram train vanilla ReLU+L1 SAEs on a synthetic nested-support generator (256 true features in 64 dims, parent-child pairs) and find neither predicted phase: across 200 canonical full-batch fits (10 of 165 grid cells) and 3,300 minibatch fits over the full γ×λ×M grid there are zero full-dictionary recoveries (95% binomial upper bound ≈1.5%) and zero merges, even at γ=1 where every pair is nested. Instead every run lands in a "diffuse phase": per-sample reconstruction loss as low as 2.7e-6 while median best cosine per feature is only 0.472–0.727 against the 0.95 recovery criterion and codes are 9.9–27.9× denser than ground truth. Doubling training budget (cosine 0.727→0.729) and switching to minibatch Adam (same plateau, 64× faster) do not escape it; dictionary size M=256→512 is the strongest lever (matched fraction 0.001→0.036). The practical warning: near-perfect reconstruction is compatible with zero feature recovery.

## 核心贡献

- **首次实测 MAIS-O43 相图**：按 Lionel Levine 的 MAIS 议程中开放问题 O43 的完整规范（生成器、目标函数、优化器、样本量、结局判定全部预注册），在 165 个 (γ, λ, M) 网格格点中测量了 10 个规范格点、200 次独立初始化拟合——这是该相图的首批数据。
- **发现未被预言的"扩散相"（diffuse phase）**：既非完整恢复也非特征合并，而是重构近乎完美 + 原子与真特征普遍低对齐（每格中位最佳余弦 0.472–0.727，恢复判据 0.95）+ 编码密度为真值的 9.9–27.9 倍；所有 20 个种子、所有格点均收敛到同一平台，最终损失在三位小数上一致。
- **排除两个平凡的"假象"解释**：Probe A 将更新预算翻倍（20 万→40 万次全批更新），中位余弦仅从 0.727 到 0.729；Probe B 换成实践中标准的 minibatch Adam（batch 4096），约 6 万步即达同一平台（0.739），墙钟快 64 倍（573 s vs 36,546 s）——扩散相是目标函数+数据机制+摊销编码器在该机制下的性质，不是欠训练或全批优化器的伪影。
- **用 minibatch 伴生网格覆盖全部 165 格**（3,300 次拟合、29.5 GPU 小时）：零恢复、零合并遍布全网格；对齐集中在 M=512、中等 λ 角落，最高匹配分数仅 4.6%（出现在 γ=0, λ=1e-3, M=512），且基本与嵌套比例 γ 无关。
- **揭示"理论最优解的相图 ≠ 训练所得 SAE 的相图"**：MAIS 理论证明两特征情形下精确编码目标的全局最优字典必然合并嵌套特征，但训练出的多特征摊销 SAE 既不恢复也不合并——说明以精确编码最优解为校准的理论不能未经论证地迁移到训练后的 SAE。
- **对可解释性实践的警告**：重构误差近乎为零与"零特征恢复"完全相容，重构质量不能作为 SAE 找到真特征的证据；真正的失效模式是吸收的前兆（原子普遍低对齐），而非吸收本身。

## Key Contributions

- First measurements of the MAIS-O43 phase diagram: 10 of 165 (γ, λ, M) cells under the exact preregistered protocol (200 independently initialized full-batch fits), plus a complete off-protocol minibatch companion grid covering all 165 cells (3,300 fits).
- Identification of an unanticipated "diffuse phase" dominating the sampled region: zero full-dictionary recoveries and zero threshold-defined merges, combined with near-floor reconstruction (2.7e-6 to 5.6e-3 per-sample loss), median best cosine 0.472–0.727 vs the 0.95 recovery criterion, and codes 9.9–27.9× denser than ground truth.
- Two robustness probes ruling out mundane explanations: doubling the canonical update budget (Probe A: cosine 0.727→0.729, recovery 0/20, merges 0/20) and replacing the protocol's unusual full-batch Adam with the standard minibatch Adam used in SAE practice (Probe B: same plateau reached in ~60k steps, 64× faster wall-clock, recovery 0/20, merges 0/20).
- The full-grid extension: under minibatch Adam the diffuse phase holds at every one of the 165 cells — zero recoveries, zero merges in 3,300 fits, matched-feature fractions peaking at 4.6% at (γ=0, λ=1e-3, M=512), splitting only in some M=512 cells (34 cells, ≤30% of runs).
- A conceptual separation between the phase diagram of objective minimizers and that of trained SAEs: theory proves the exact-coding optimum merges nested features in the two-feature case, yet trained amortized SAEs do neither, so results calibrated on minimizers do not automatically describe trained models.
- A practical corollary for interpretability: near-perfect reconstruction is compatible with essentially zero feature recovery (at most 3.6% of features matched per run), so reconstruction quality alone must not certify dictionary recovery.

## Methodology

The paper executes the MAIS-O43 protocol exactly. **Generator:** n=64 ambient dimensions, m=256 true features with unit-norm i.i.d. Gaussian columns (one fixed draw); a fixed random permutation forms 128 ordered parent–child pairs, of which the first ⌊128γ⌋ are nested. Parent indicators are Bernoulli(1/32); a nested child fires only with its parent (A_j = B_i·B_j, marginal 1/1024); active coefficients are Unif[1,2]; y = Φx with 2^18 fixed training and 2^16 fresh evaluation samples. **Model:** a centered SAE, c(y)=ReLU(Wy+b), decoder Ψ∈R^64×M with unit-norm columns and no output bias, trained on G_λ = E[½‖y−Ψc(y)‖² + λ‖c(y)‖₁] by full-batch Adam (lr 1e-3, 2×10^5 exact-gradient updates, decoder renormalized every step), 20 independent seeds per cell, strict float32 with TF32 disabled and float64 metric computation. **Outcome classifiers (preregistered):** recovery = injective matching with all cosines ≥0.95 (exact maximum bipartite matching); merge = a live atom within ε of a nonneg combination αv_i+βv_k (α,β≥0.1) while its cosine with each feature is ≤0.9 (exact constrained least squares over all pairs); split = ≥2 live atoms at cosine ≥0.95 per feature. **Validation:** a 49-test suite including adversarial classifier fixtures (greedy-defeating matching instances, threshold-boundary cases, the analytic two-feature nested optimum), NumPy gradient cross-checks at 1e-12 tolerance, and batched-vs-scalar trajectory agreement at 1e-9. All randomness descends from one published master seed; checkpoints, data hashes, and regeneration scripts are released.

## Results & Highlights

- Zero recoveries and zero merges in all 200 canonical fits; treating the runs as Bernoulli trials gives a pooled one-sided 95% zero-event upper bound of 1.49% (13.91% per cell). Only 215 of 51,200 possible feature–atom assignments matched (0.42%), and only 44 of 200 runs contained even one feature above the 0.95 criterion.
- The diffuse phase: mean evaluation reconstruction loss (½‖y−ŷ‖² per sample) spans 2.7e-6 to 5.6e-3 while mean matched-feature fractions stay in 0–0.036 and learned codes are 9.9–27.9× denser than the γ-dependent ground-truth density — e.g., at (γ=1.0, λ=1e-2, M=256) density is 27.9× true with median best cosine 0.473.
- Interior λ maximum at γ=0.5, M=256: median best cosine rises 0.472 (λ=1e-4) → 0.727 (λ=3e-3, with 23.1 of 256 features ≥0.90) then falls to 0.584 at λ=1e-2, where density jumps to 20.1× true — the signature of L1 activation shrinkage that gated/top-k SAEs were designed to remove.
- Nesting fraction γ (the axis the phase diagram was designed around) has almost no effect: at λ=1e-2, cells with γ≤0.5 are statistically indistinguishable and no merge occurs even at γ=1 where every feature is in a nested pair; across the full minibatch grid the best-matched cells at γ=0 vs γ=1 differ only 0.046 vs 0.045 in matched fraction.
- Dictionary size is the strongest lever: at (γ=0.5, λ=1e-3), M 256→512 raises matched fraction 0.001→0.036 and median cosine 0.572→0.712 — larger than any λ or γ change measured; M=512 is also the only setting producing splits (5 of 20 runs in the canonical cell).
- Robustness: Probe A doubles updates to 400k with median cosine 0.727→0.729 and matched fraction ~0.004 unchanged; Probe B's minibatch Adam reaches cosine 0.739 in ~60k steps vs the canonical 0.727 at 200k, 573 s vs 36,546 s wall-clock (~64× faster to the same solution).
- The full 165-cell minibatch companion grid (3,300 fits, 29.5 GPU-hours) reproduces the null everywhere: zero recoveries, zero merges, matched fractions ≤0.046 against a criterion requiring 1.0.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** High within its niche. The absorption/splitting literature (Chanin et al.; Bussmann et al.'s Matryoshka SAEs; Costa et al.'s MP-SAE) reports structured failures given roughly aligned atoms; this paper quantifies a regime where alignment itself largely fails, and honestly flags the discrepancy with prior toy studies as an open gap (candidate causes: the denser m/n=4, ≈8-active-features regime; the vanilla L1 architecture without dead-feature resampling; the strict 0.95-cosine injective-matching criterion vs qualitative diagnostics).
- **Rigor:** Very strong for an empirical null — preregistration, adversarial classifier validation, exact bipartite matching, pooled binomial bounds, two falsification probes, and a full-grid replication. The paper is commendably explicit that the two probes address only under-training and optimizer semantics, not stationarity at every cell.
- **Reproducibility:** Among the best I have summarized: seeds, checkpoints, hashes, regeneration scripts, and a preregistration document are all public; the minibatch grid costs ~30 GPU-hours to rerun.
- **Limitations of coverage:** Only 10 of 165 cells are measured under the canonical protocol (the 155 remaining full-batch cells are covered only by the off-protocol minibatch grid, which the authors correctly say "does not resolve MAIS-O43"); the per-cell zero-event bound of 13.91% is weak evidence on its own. The λ-response rests on just five λ values at a single γ.
- **Known confound, acknowledged but unremoved:** the γ intervention changes both parent–child dependence and child marginal frequency simultaneously (ground-truth density falls 0.03125→0.01611), so "nesting has almost no effect" is really "the joint nesting+rarity axis has no effect"; the matched-frequency control is deferred to future work.
- **Red flags / threats to validity:** one fixed dictionary draw (Φ) and one data realization per γ, so nothing here speaks to variance across generator draws; no SAE variants (gated, top-k, Matryoshka) are tested, leaving open whether the diffuse phase is an artifact of vanilla L1 — a question the paper itself notes is now inexpensive to answer; and the headline contrast with theory involves a two-feature exact-coding proof versus a 256-feature amortized experiment, so several distinct variables (amortization, finite samples, dimension) are confounded in the "trained ≠ minimizer" conclusion.

## Strengths

- Delivers a clean, well-powered negative result with the kind of statistical care (zero-event binomial bounds, 20 seeds per cell, pooled analysis) rarely applied to SAE toy studies.
- The two robustness probes are well chosen and cheap relative to their evidential value, and the minibatch companion grid converts a 6% protocol sample into a 100% (off-protocol) coverage result at trivial cost.
- The central conceptual point — the phase diagram of trained SAEs and the phase diagram of objective minimizers are different objects — is crisply argued and directly consequential for how theory in the MAIS agenda should be calibrated.
- The practical warning (reconstruction quality does not certify feature recovery; at most 3.6% of features matched per run despite near-zero error) is actionable for anyone using SAE loss curves as a sanity check.
- Exceptional artifact release: preregistration, seeds, hashes, checkpoints, regeneration scripts.

## Limitations

- Canonical coverage is 10/165 cells; the full-grid null rests on an off-protocol optimizer, and completing the remaining full-batch cells remains computationally costly (10.2–17.5 h per cell).
- Nesting and child rarity are confounded by design of the γ axis; disentangling requires the matched-frequency control the paper defers.
- A single generator configuration (one Φ draw, m/n=4, ~8 active features, n=64) — generalization to other regimes, other draws, and above all to real LLM activations is unestablished.
- No SAE architectural variants (gated, top-k, Matryoshka, tree/forest SAEs) are evaluated, so the role of the vanilla L1 architecture in producing the diffuse phase is untested.
- The "trained vs minimizer" gap could stem from amortization (MAIS-O39), the many-feature distribution, finite samples, or the optimization path; the experiment cannot distinguish these.
- The λ-interior maximum is measured at five penalties on one γ slice, and the authors concede these measurements do not isolate shrinkage as the cause of the alignment decline at λ=1e-2.

## Relevance

对研究 SAE/可解释性的人高度相关：它直接量化了"重构损失近乎为零 ≠ 恢复了真特征"这一日常实践中最容易被忽视的陷阱，并为 MAIS 理论议程与真实训练动力学之间提供了首批校准数据。若你的兴趣涵盖 mechanistic interpretability、字典学习或稀疏编码的理论-实验对接，这篇论文是一个值得引用的强负结果与校准基准。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the diffuse phase persist under gated SAEs, top-k SAEs, or Matryoshka/hierarchical variants — i.e., is it a property of the vanilla L1 objective or of amortized sparse coding generally? (Probe B's cost figures make this cheap to test.)
- Does a matched-frequency control (decoupling child rarity from parent–child dependence) restore any γ-dependence, or is the near-invariance in γ genuinely about dependence?
- What do global minimizers of the amortized objective G_λ itself (not the exact-coding F_λ) look like in this 256-feature regime — merged, diffuse, or recovered? This would cleanly separate "optimizer misses the optimum" from "the optimum is diffuse."
- Would auxiliary losses, dead-atom resampling, or larger M/n ratios push matched fractions from ~4% toward the 0.95 recovery criterion, and where is the critical M?
- Does the diffuse phase appear on real LLM activations (where the true dictionary is unknown), detectable via proxy metrics such as code-density ratio or atom–atom coherence rather than cosine to ground truth?
- How sensitive are the phase boundaries to the generator draw — repeating with multiple Φ draws and per-γ data realizations would establish whether the null is a property of the regime or of one seed.
