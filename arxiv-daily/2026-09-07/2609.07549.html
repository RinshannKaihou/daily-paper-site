Methodology Distilled

Not a transcription — the five-stage recipe and the architecture that carries it.

  • 1Encoder SSL pretrain — BEST-RQ on tens of millions of unlabeled hours; Transformer layers initialized from a pretrained Qwen3 LLM, causal attention swapped to bidirectional.
  • 2Audio-LLM pretrain — ~20M hours multilingual ASR + interleaved text-audio; encoder frozen while the MoE decoder aligns to acoustics.
  • 3Cooldown — ~1M hours of quality-filtered, language-rebalanced data (VibeVoice re-recognition drops samples with >0.3 error); diarization and SpeechQA added.
  • 4SFT — encoder + adapter jointly updated, LLM adapted via LoRA; instruction-formatted data with TTS entity augmentation, fidelity-filtered polishing targets, and adversarial (conflicting / noisy / empty) history contexts.
  • 5GRPO RL — on a compact hard-case set (base-model disagreements, >20 s utterances, hallucinations, keywords); 8 hypotheses per prompt, composite rewards (1−WER, keyword recall, hotword hit, context entity, hallucination penalty). Full RL finishes in one day on 32×A100 via the asynchronous FunVerl-ASR stack (verl + Ray + vLLM + Megatron).

System at a glance Arch

Controls travel as conditioning tokens — never as separate checkpoints.

  • ASenseVoice audio encoder — BEST-RQ pretrained, bidirectional.
  • B2-layer Transformer adapter between audio and text.
  • CQwen MoE decoder — sparse experts buy linguistic & world-knowledge capacity at low per-token compute.
  • DLightweight CTC head — drives hotword customization and coarse alignment for long-audio context injection.
  • EInference interface — language list, polish flag, transcript history, P0/P1 hotwords concatenated with audio tokens; composable per request.

Strengths ＋

  • One instruction interface genuinely replaces per-market checkpoints, cascaded rewrites, and separate hotword machinery — polishing at cascade-level quality in a single pass.
  • Dramatic dialect wins in a market Chinese commercial rivals visibly neglect.
  • P0/P1 tiering shows its intended asymmetry empirically (P0 ≈ 99%+, P1 deliberately smaller); adversarial context training keeps audio the primary evidence.
  • FunVerl-ASR is a credible engineering contribution in its own right — async rollout/training with a staleness bound.
  • Honest hygiene where it counts: dual benchmark panels, scoring-convention caveats, openly reported losses.

Limitations －

  • No model-size, expert-config, or hyperparameter disclosure; no code, release plan, or data documentation.
  • Zero ablation studies — the contribution of MoE, RL, tiering, or entity mining is unquantified; long-audio context rests on qualitative examples only.
  • Uneven multilingual coverage: notably weak on Filipino, Malay, Thai, and Vietnamese.
  • Dialect, entity, hotword, polishing, and streaming results all sit on internal test sets; dialect consistency is judged by the team's own LLM.
  • Streaming costs ~14–15% relative error at 400 ms; latency figures are algorithmic (H100), not end-to-end production numbers.
