> **一句话总结：** 浙江大学 Qiushi Engine 自主研究系统在 BabyLM 2026 Strict-Small 赛道（10M 词语料、100M 累计曝光上限）上完成"前沿建模→原理发现→原理指导改进"三阶段闭环：用 Qwen3.5-9B 生成的紧凑复述+预算再投资+残差增量训练造出 42.02 Overall 的第一代模型，再通过对照实验发现"精确重复损害真源利用（−0.81 nats）而对齐复述提升（+0.80 nats）、熟悉性能恢复≠未见输入可复用"等规律，据此设计"稠密掩码+稀疏监督+普通输入 KL 保持"的续训方法，在相同父模型两个续训种子上全面超过普通续训（42.25 vs 42.09），登顶 2026-09-08 公共榜单快照。

> **TL;DR:** An autonomous multi-agent research system (Qiushi Engine, Zhejiang University) runs a three-stage program on BabyLM 2026 Strict-Small (10M-word corpus, 100M cumulative word-exposure cap): Stage I builds a 36.5M-parameter DeBERTa-v2-style masked LM with compact Qwen3.5-9B-generated restatements + budget reinvestment + a zero-initialized residual increment, reaching 42.02 Overall; Stage II uses controlled interventions to find that exact repetition *degrades* true-source use (−0.8138 nats) while aligned restatement improves it (+0.7970 nats), and that full-sequence continuation collapses unseen-symbol relation accuracy from 87.5% to 40.2% while familiar accuracy stays 100%; Stage III converts these into dense-masking-with-sparse-supervision plus ordinary-input KL preservation, beating ordinary continuation from the same parent on the full nine-metric evaluation (42.2464/42.2317 vs 42.0926/42.1159, i.e. +0.154/+0.116) and reaching 42.25 Overall — 0.45 above the best external leaderboard entry (41.80, go76dof/WWM curriculum). Worth reading for the experimental hygiene (window controls, seed-matched initialization checks, RNG-state-controlled preservation) and the "Research RSI" framing, though the headline margins are small and rest heavily on the 203-item GlobalPIQA benchmark.

## 核心贡献

- **三阶段自主研究闭环（Research RSI 实例）**：Qiushi Engine 多智能体系统独立完成文献调研、方法构建、实验、机制分析与综合：Stage I 前沿建模 → Stage II 原理发现 → Stage III 原理指导的模型改进；前期发现（如数据价值随训练阶段反转、连贯组织优于乱序）直接改变后续实验设计，形成"研究过程自身的递归自我改进"。
- **紧凑复述 + 预算再投资（Stage I）**：用 Qwen3.5-9B 为源段落生成更短的复述，省下的词预算再投资于新增配对——在固定 423,520 词的替换块内配对数从 10,094 增至 12,155（+20.42%），同时保留源文与两种表达。
- **残差增量结构支持冻结式继续学习（Stage I）**：8 层 DeBERTa-v2 式主干（35,463,008 参数）联合训练后冻结，追加零初始化、480→128→480 瓶颈的残差分支（995,584 参数，仅 2.73% 可训练），继续学习至 86,005,295 词仍保留普通目标 + KL 保持项。
- **关系类型与窗口组织塑造上下文利用（Stage II）**：精确重复使真源优势**下降** 0.8138 nats，对齐复述使其**上升** 0.7970 nats（3 种子）；把源与对应段落拆到不同窗口后两个效应都大幅衰减（如 −0.683→+0.009），证明"文本在场"≠"关系可被单一预测利用"。
- **熟悉性能 ≠ 未见输入的功能可达性（Stage II）**：四选一关系任务上，全序列续训后熟悉符号准确率保持 100% 但未见符号从 87.5% 跌至 40.2%；改用交错监督（关系答案位置与全序列目标交替）可恢复至 83.3%；对第一层隐藏态"选择方向"做置零干预使未见准确率 87.5%→25.0%，直接证明该信号的功能作用。
- **可见输入 / 监督目标 / 行为保持三者解耦的训练设计（Stage III）**：稠密掩码（176,607 tokens）+ 稀疏聚焦监督（28,590 tokens，聚焦损失权重 0.15）+ 对冻结父模型在普通掩码输入上的 KL 保持（λ=1），在两个续训种子上都超过普通续训，且稠密监督 (M,M) 反而低于稀疏监督 (M,S)。
- **方法论副产品**：初始化混淆的发现（同 seed 插层会导致参数错配，显式拷贝公共参数后原"强负交互"消失）、稠密输入下保持梯度范数是普通输入的 11.835 倍（等损失系数≠等有效约束）等可复用的测量控制。

## Key Contributions

- **A three-stage autonomous research loop ("Research RSI")**: the Qiushi Engine multi-agent system conducts literature research, method development, experimentation, and synthesis end-to-end; findings from early stages (e.g., data-value rank reversal across training, coherent-beats-shuffled organization) directly reshape later experiments — recursive self-improvement of the research process itself, not of agent weights.
- **Compact restatements + budget reinvestment (Stage I)**: Qwen3.5-9B-generated shorter restatements of source passages free word budget for additional pairs — 10,094 → 12,155 pairs (+20.42%) inside a fixed 423,520-word replacement block, retaining both expressions.
- **Residual increment for frozen-base continued learning**: after joint training of a 35.46M-parameter, 8-layer DeBERTa-v2-style backbone, a zero-initialized 480→128→480 residual branch (995,584 params, 2.73% trainable, 48 tensors) is trained to 86,005,295 cumulative words with prediction loss + KL preservation.
- **Relation type and window organization shape context use**: exact repetition *reduces* true-source advantage by 0.8138 nats while aligned restatement *raises* it by 0.7970 nats (3 seeds); splitting source and passage across windows nearly eliminates both effects (e.g., −0.683 → +0.009), showing text presence does not equal usable relational context.
- **Familiar performance ≠ functional accessibility for unseen inputs**: on a four-choice relation task, full-sequence continuation keeps familiar-symbol accuracy at 100% but drops unseen-symbol accuracy 87.5% → 40.2%; interleaved relation/full-sequence supervision restores 83.3%; zeroing the layer-1 selection direction collapses unseen accuracy to 25.0%, establishing its causal role.
- **Decoupled input / supervision / preservation training design (Stage III)**: dense masking (176,607 tokens) + sparse focused supervision (28,590 tokens, focused-loss weight 0.15) + KL preservation toward the frozen parent on ordinarily-masked inputs (λ=1, T=1) beats ordinary continuation on both continuation seeds; dense supervision (M,M) scores *below* sparse (M,S).
- **Reusable measurement controls**: discovery of an initialization confound (inserting a layer re-assigns seed values; explicitly copying shared parameters weakened a previously large negative interaction), and the finding that the preservation gradient norm on dense inputs is 11.835x its ordinary-input value — equal loss coefficients impose unequal effective constraints.

## Methodology

**Setting.** BabyLM 2026 Strict-Small: ≤10M-word corpus, ≤100M cumulative word presentations, nine official metrics (BLiMP, BLiMP Supplement, EWoK, Entity Tracking, COMPS, GlobalPIQA, (Super)GLUE, Reading, AoA); Overall = mean of all nine; NLP Average = first seven, Human-like = last two. Models and code are released on Hugging Face and GitHub.

**Stage I — frontier model.** The team compares character vs. subword representations, autoregressive vs. masked objectives, depths/widths/schedules, landing on an 8-layer DeBERTa-v2-style MLM (hidden 480, 8 heads, FFN 1920, byte-level BPE vocab 16,384, 256-token window, 15% whole-word masking, AdamW batch 256, peak LR 0.001, cosine decay). Data: original passages paired with compact Qwen3.5-9B restatements (temp 0.1, ≤80 tokens/call); shortening restatements frees words to add 2,061 more pairs (+20.42%) within the fixed 423,520-word replacement block. Training proceeds jointly to 82,012,495 words (35,463,008 params), then the backbone is frozen and a zero-initialized residual branch (480→128→480, trained at scale 1.75, release scale 0.75 chosen by evaluation) is trained 101 updates with prediction + KL loss, reaching 86,005,295 words and 36,458,592 total parameters. Controls include coherent-vs-shuffled segment organization at fixed exposure (44.1064 vs 43.1214 seven-metric mean, ~0.99 gap).

**Stage II — principle discovery.** (i) *Natural-text relation experiments*: reference vs. +exact-repetition vs. +aligned-restatement conditions, each with same-window and split-window variants; source use measured as true-source advantage A_T = N − T (NLL of target under neutral vs. true source) on fixed targets, split by tokenizer-ID overlap with the source. (ii) *Controlled four-choice relation task* ("query B; A is red, B is blue, …") with familiar vs. unseen symbols, comparing full-sequence, static relation-weight (1/17), and interleaved supervision; causal probing via centering/zeroing/rotation interventions on a fitted layer-1 selection direction, plus refitting tests to rule out direction migration. (iii) *Supervision-allocation studies*: target-deletion experiments on compact restatements (delete source-absent vs. copied-target supervision). (iv) *Experience-value framing*: fixed-budget substitution Δ_T(A, D; B) with budget/schedule/seed held fixed.

**Stage III — principle-guided improvement.** Reusing the Frontier weights, tokenizer, and existing text (no new rewriting): the continuation stream is 20,475 packed rows / 3,162,742 words (Qwen restatement pairs, Gutenberg, Simple Wikipedia, CHILDES, OpenSubtitles, BNC spoken, Switchboard). The method separates *what is visible* from *what is supervised*: dense masking over 132,283 content groups (176,607 tokens) but focused loss on only 21,479 groups (28,590 tokens); acquisition loss L_acq = 0.15·CE_focus + 0.85·CE_ordinary. Preservation re-presents the same rows with ordinary 15% masking and applies KL(p_Frontier ‖ p_θ) over full vocabulary at ordinary prediction positions (λ=1, T=1), with RNG state saved/restored so dropout randomness cannot contaminate the comparison. Only the existing 995,584-parameter increment is updated (80 updates); total exposure 89,685,369 words = 86,005,295 + 3,162,742 + 517,332 preservation presentations (16.36% extra vs. acquisition-only controls, both under the 100M cap). Evaluation: full nine-metric table for the parent and four strategies × two continuation seeds (62064/62065), plus acquisition/preservation micro-measurements (CE gain, correct-token rank gain, parent KL), gradient-norm comparisons at shared positions, source interventions (correct/wrong/erased source), and a fine-tuning-seed robustness check.

## Results & Highlights

- **Leaderboard (public Strict-Small snapshot, 8 Sep 2026)**: Qiushi Principle-guided reaches **42.25 Overall (NLP 53.15, Human-like 4.10)** vs. Frontier's **42.02**, exceeding the best external entry (go76dof/WWM curriculum, **41.80**) by **+0.45**; external entries span only 40.67–41.80.
- **Equal-exposure continuation comparison (two seeds, same parent)**: dense masking + sparse supervision (M,S) scores **42.2025 / 42.1789** vs. ordinary continuation **42.0926 / 42.1159** (+0.1099 / +0.0630); the full method with ordinary-input preservation reaches **42.2464 / 42.2317** (+0.1538 / +0.1158 over ordinary continuation; +0.0439 / +0.0529 over (M,S)); dense supervision (M,M) scores 42.1491 / 42.1684 — *below* (M,S) at fixed 0.15 focused weight.
- **Relation-type effects (Stage II, 3 seeds)**: exact repetition changes true-source advantage by **−0.8138 nats** (SD 0.1729) and aligned restatement by **+0.7970 nats** (SD 0.0758) on source-nonoverlapping targets; unrelated-source advantage moves only +0.0679 / −0.0085 nats, confirming source-specificity. Split-window controls attenuate the effects to +0.009/−0.134 (repetition) and +0.116/+0.028 (restatement) across two seeds.
- **Capability reuse (four-choice task)**: initial acquisition 100.0% familiar / 87.5% unseen; full-sequence continuation 87.2% / **40.2%**; static relation weight (1/17) 100.0% / 75.5%; interleaved supervision 100.0% / **83.3%**. Zeroing the fitted selection direction drops unseen accuracy 87.5% → 25.0% (chance) and interleaved 83.3% → 38.7%.
- **Supervision allocation (target deletion, 100M words)**: seven-metric average 43.896 (full supervision) vs. 43.437 (delete source-absent targets) vs. 42.792 (delete copied targets) — which targets receive loss changes outcome with identical input.
- **Stage I data tricks**: compact-restatement method is −0.83 points *below* its reference at 20M words but +1.29 / +1.35 above at 70M / 80M (rank reversal over training); coherent training beats within-row segment shuffling 44.1064 vs. 43.1214 on the seven-metric mean at identical exposure and update count.
- **Component attribution**: relative to the parent, GlobalPIQA contributes ~74%/79% of the full method's Overall gain (38.5650 → 40.0500, +1.485 on a 37.5%-chance benchmark); relative to ordinary continuation, Entity Tracking is the largest positive contributor (+0.1378 / +0.1511 Overall units, 28.32 → 29.39–29.45); preservation's added gain comes from net changes in other components with GlobalPIQA unchanged.
- **Acquisition/preservation trade-off**: (M,S) without preservation achieves CE gain 0.757 nats / rank gain 132.8 / parent KL 0.503; the full method retains 0.682 / 128.3 at KL 0.353, while dense-input preservation collapses to CE gain 0.159 / rank gain 34.8 — strong preservation on the new task's inputs suppresses acquisition.

## Authors & Credibility

A 16-author team (first author Shuxing Yang; corresponding Yihao Yang and Hongsheng Chen) from the College of Information Science and Electronic Engineering, Zhejiang University, constituting the "Qiushi Engine Team." The same group previously published the Qiushi Engine hierarchical multi-agent research system and demonstrated it on a real optical platform (Yang et al., cited as [34]), which lends credibility to the autonomous-research claims. The work was executed on two NVIDIA H100 GPUs plus a 208-thread CPU node; runtime dependencies (PyTorch 2.11.0, Transformers 4.57.6), training entry points, data manifests, seeds, and full-precision CSVs are promised in the accompanying repository, with both model generations released on Hugging Face. The report is unusually transparent about its own weaknesses (no uncertainty intervals on GlobalPIQA, single parent model, snapshot-vs-award distinction), which raises trust in the numbers that are given.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: The individual components are composites of known techniques — paraphrase-augmented pretraining, zero-initialized residual adapters (cf. LoRA-style/PEFT), Learning-without-Forgetting KL distillation, and the input-corruption vs. prediction-rate distinction has direct precedent ([30] in the paper). The genuine novelty is (a) the systematic decoupling of visible input / supervised targets / preservation inputs under a hard word-exposure budget, and (b) the closed three-stage loop where mechanism findings demonstrably drive the next training recipe — an unusually clean instance of method-from-mechanism in small-scale LM research.
- **Rigor**: Very high by BabyLM standards — matched exposure accounting down to the word, seed-matched controls, the initialization confound discovery, RNG-state control for dropout during preservation passes, and split-window text-matched controls are exemplary. However, the headline gains are small and the paper itself concedes the key weakness: GlobalPIQA — a 203-item benchmark with a 37.5% chance floor — supplies ~74–79% of the parent-relative gain, and "item-paired uncertainty intervals are not provided." A ~+0.15 Overall edge over ordinary continuation could plausibly sit within evaluation noise on that single component; the leaderboard itself notes adjacent external entries differ by as little as 0.01.
- **A concrete data quirk**: AoA scores 0.0000 for *all nine* models in the local evaluation (the implementation returns zero when fewer than three words have valid estimates or the correlation test gives p > 0.1), so one of nine Overall components is degenerate and Human-like Average is effectively Reading/2 for everyone — Overall comparisons partly ride on an 8/9-metric scale.
- **Reproducibility**: Strong on paper — released weights, tokenizer, data manifests, per-run environment records, full-precision component CSVs, and deterministic preservation protocol. The external teacher (Qwen3.5-9B, temperature 0.1, ≤80 tokens) is fully specified; BabyLM's rules on auxiliary teachers require this accounting and the appendix attempts it (517,332 extra preservation presentations are honestly counted toward the 100M cap). Still, regenerating the restatement corpus depends on an exact teacher version.
- **Significance**: Within BabyLM Strict-Small this is a solid frontier result, but margins over the 8th-of-September public snapshot (not the final workshop awards) are thin (+0.45 over the best external entry; +0.15 over ordinary continuation). The "Research RSI" framing is the most conceptually ambitious claim; the authors themselves appropriately scope it down ("It does not establish sustained improvement of general research ability across tasks").
- **Red flags**: (i) single parent model — both complete evaluations are continuations of one 42.02 checkpoint, so cross-parent generality is untested; (ii) (S,S) never received the full nine-metric evaluation, so the Overall gap between (M,S) and ordinary continuation conflates masking with other changes, as the paper admits; (iii) the full method's headline number uses 517K more word presentations than its controls (89.69M vs 89.17M) — disclosed and arguably accounted, but the strongest-looking row has the largest budget; (iv) 16 authors for a ~36M-parameter model result reflects the system-building (the autonomous agent infrastructure) more than the modeling contribution per se.

## Strengths

- Principled separation of three normally-entangled training variables — visible input, supervised targets, preservation inputs — each with its own controlled comparison, is a genuinely reusable experimental template for data-limited pretraining.
- The Stage II mechanism work goes beyond accuracy: NLL-based true/unrelated-source advantage measures, window controls that preserve text while breaking relational access, and causal hidden-state interventions (zero/center/rotate) with refitting controls to rule out direction migration.
- Honest, component-level accounting of where the gains come from (GlobalPIQA vs. Entity Tracking vs. preservation), with the authors explicitly distinguishing gains-from-parent, advantage-over-continuation, and added-preservation-effect — three questions other submissions usually conflate.
- Full exposure accounting including preservation presentations, deterministic preservation passes, and rank-reversal findings (compact restatements losing at 20M but winning at 70–80M) that caution against early data rankings.
- Complete release: two model generations on Hugging Face, data-construction records, evaluation vectors, fine-tuning-seed robustness checks, and preserved negative results (superseded explanations documented as reusable knowledge).

## Limitations

- Tiny aggregate margins (42.02 → 42.25; +0.45 over the best external submission) on a nine-metric average where one component (AoA) is 0 for every model and another (GlobalPIQA, 203 items, 37.5% chance) dominates the improvement — statistical significance is not established.
- All complete evaluations derive from a single parent checkpoint and two continuation seeds; no independent parent models, different budgets, or document-disjoint test sets (the authors list these as needed further tests).
- Dense-input preservation suppresses acquisition (CE gain 0.757 → 0.159 nats), so the method's preservation term works only under a narrow input condition; effective-constraint strength is confounded with target-position differences, as the paper concedes.
- The learned principle ("organize experience around contextual dependencies; separately design visible information, supervision, and preservation") is validated on one 36M-parameter masked LM in English; transfer to autoregressive LMs, larger scales, or non-English settings is untested.
- Reliance on an external 9B teacher (Qwen3.5-9B) to generate the restatement corpus complicates the "learning from limited data" narrative — the model sees 10M words, but the data pipeline consumed substantially more compute; rules-compliant, yet scientifically it shifts part of the learning signal into the teacher.

## Relevance

与"数据受限环境下的语言模型训练/持续学习"研究兴趣高度相关：它把数据价值评估、监督分配和能力保持做成可分别检验的实验变量，对低资源语言与垂直领域小模型训练有直接的方法借鉴意义；同时作为自主科研智能体（Research RSI）在真实共享任务上完成闭环的实例，也是 AI-for-Science 侧的重要参考。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the (M,S) > (M,M) result — sparse supervision beating dense supervision at fixed focused-loss weight — persist when the weight is retuned for dense supervision, or is it an artifact of the 0.15 coefficient?
- Would an item-paired bootstrap over the 203-item GlobalPIQA (which supplies ~74–79% of parent-relative gains) preserve the +0.15 Overall advantage over ordinary continuation?
- Do the Stage II relation effects (repetition vs. restatement, split-window attenuation) replicate in autoregressive decoder-only models, where "prediction window" is defined by the causal mask rather than by packing choices?
- Can the ordinary-input preservation scheme scale to incremental training of large models, where the frozen-parent KL teacher is cheap but the dense/ordinary gradient-norm ratio (11.835) may grow with scale?
- How much of the Qiushi Engine pipeline's advantage over manual research is attributable to sustained memory across stages — i.e., what would the same three stages cost (in GPU-hours and evaluations) if executed without the accumulated-findings system?
