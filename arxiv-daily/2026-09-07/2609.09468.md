> **一句话总结：** Google 团队提出"练习–门控–蒸馏–冻结"框架：让 LLM 智能体在开发目标上反复编写并执行优化器程序（可执行练习），再把获胜程序连同练习记录一次性蒸馏成 197 词的纯文本"harness"并冻结——该文本使 Gemini Flash 在 20 次评估预算的黑盒优化（N=30）上遗憾值降低 48%（p<.001），进入 GP-BO 贝叶斯优化性能区间，且无需任何修改即可迁移到 Flash-Lite 与 Claude Sonnet（遗憾降低 43%/49%）。

> **TL;DR:** Instead of letting an LLM optimize online (which leaks test feedback), the authors have a practice agent iteratively write and execute optimizer programs on external development objectives, then one-shot distill the winning program plus its practice log into a 197-word frozen strategy text ("Harness A"). On 8-D black-box minimization with a 20-evaluation budget, Harness A cuts Gemini Flash's final simple regret on shifted quadratics from 69.5 to 36.0 in an independent N=30 study (−48%, p<.001, vs plain GP-BO at 30.4, p=.64 for difference), the same unchanged text transfers to Flash-Lite (116.2→48.5) and Claude Sonnet (83.5→47.3, both p≤.005), and lowers mean regret on all three held-out BBOB landscapes (though only Gallagher-101 survives Holm correction). An independent end-to-end rerun yields a different program and a 205-word Harness B at the same tier, and the framework achieves the lowest point-estimate regret (0.0495 vs 0.0894 for Google Vizier at 20 evals) on a sealed internal YouTube reward-tuning benchmark — reported descriptively only. Honest, unusually well-sealed evaluation, but claims are "transfer into the BO tier," not BO dominance.

## 核心贡献

- **练习–门控–蒸馏–冻结（practice–gate–distill–freeze）框架**：智能体在开发目标上通过"写程序→同预算配对评估→择优保留"的可执行练习进化优化器代码；只有通过独立验证门（gate）的运行才会被一次性蒸馏成策略文本，并在任何评测前冻结——评测阶段只更换历史数据，文本与执行配置均不可响应测试结果，从根本上避免了对评测信息的泄漏。
- **语言作为可移植的策略载体**：核心发现是约 200 词的纯文本策略（Harness A，197 词）能把未辅助时接近随机搜索的 LLM 提升到 GP-BO 贝叶斯优化的性能区间，且同一段文本无需逐模型调优即可跨执行器（Flash/Flash-Lite/Pro）与跨厂商（Claude Sonnet）迁移——迁移的是"评估预算的支配策略"，而非具体实现。
- **可复现性目标是"性能档位"而非逐字复现**：独立端到端重跑产出完全不同的冠军程序（有限差分坐标搜索 vs 精确线搜索）与不含任何相同句子的 Harness B（205 词），却在四张新地形上达到同一性能档位；同时公开了一个未过门控的失败审计（Candidate C），而非只报成功案例。
- **严格的方法学卫生**：冻结清单（manifest）、精确求解审计（held-out 文本单元 0/30 精确求解，排除"认出教科书函数"捷径）、可识别函数单独报告、不合并不同设置以 inflated N、失败案例披露、匿名代码发布——为"LLM 产物评测前密封"树立了范式。
- **生产相关性**：同一框架在密封的 7 维 YouTube 奖励调优内部基准上取得所有预算检查点的最低点估计（20 次评估时 0.0495，对比 Google Vizier 0.0894）。

## Key Contributions

- A **practice–gate–distill–freeze pipeline** for low-budget black-box optimization: a fixed practice agent iterates "write challenger program → paired equal-budget evaluation on fresh development batches → retain if lower regret," logging code, trajectories, failures, and selection decisions; only a champion that passes a pre-registered validation gate is distilled — once, with no search over alternative texts — and sealed before any held-out or production evaluation.
- **Demonstration that ~200 words of frozen text is a portable search policy**: the 197-word Harness A moves unaided LLMs (which statistically match random search) into the GP-BO performance range, and the identical text transfers across Gemini capability tiers and to Claude Sonnet with no per-model tuning; ablations indicate the practice record (not just the procedure) contributes, and behavioral traces show the text reshapes query geometry (proposals within radius 2 of the incumbent rise from 69% to 79%).
- **Behavioral reproducibility instead of textual reproducibility**: an independent end-to-end replication produces a different champion (finite-difference coordinate search with a parabolic 1-D jump) and Harness B (205 words, zero shared sentences), which matches the same performance tier on four fresh landscapes; a third gate-failed completion (Candidate C) is disclosed as a failure audit.
- **Evaluation hygiene rarely seen in LLM-optimizer papers**: frozen manifests, exact-solve audits (0/30 exact solves on text-only held-out cells), identifiable textbook functions reported separately from transfer claims, no pooling across settings to inflate N, Holm family-wise corrections, and explicit downgrade of claims that do not survive correction ("transfer into the BO tier, not BO dominance").
- **A production case study**: on a sealed 7-dimensional YouTube reward-tuning benchmark, the replication harness attains the lowest point-estimate best-so-far regret at every budget checkpoint (0.0495 at 20 evals vs 0.0894 for Google Vizier), reported descriptively without statistical claims.

## Methodology

The paper targets the low-budget black-box optimization setting: an unknown objective f: X → R over [−5, 5]^8 with only B = 20 sequential evaluations, scored by final simple regret rB = min_i f(x_i) − min_x f(x).

**Executable practice.** A fixed practice agent starts from a valid incumbent optimizer program a0. At each round r it reads the accumulated record L_{r−1} (source code, trajectories, scores, failures, decisions) and writes a challenger ã_r, which counts only if it executes correctly. Incumbent and challenger receive the same evaluation budget on the same fresh development batch P_r (randomly shifted PSD quadratics); the challenger is retained iff its mean regret Q_r is lower. Practice thus converts verbal hypotheses into falsifiable, executable search behavior. In the replication pipeline, practice programs are restricted to elementary arithmetic on the observation history (no fitting, kernel, or optimizer library calls) so every champion decision is statable in one plain sentence.

**Gate, distill, freeze.** After R rounds, a fixed validation rule G(a_R; V) ∈ {0,1} scores the champion on fresh development seeds. Only gate-passing runs are handed (a_R, L_R) once to a distillation step: s_dist = Distill(a_R, L_R), which builds a behavior-to-principle mapping and rewrites it as a second-person strategy text, audited for landscape vocabulary and implementation jargon. A_F = Seal(s_dist, θ) fixes the execution interface, parser, bounds, and inference settings. There is no search over alternative distilled texts; at evaluation time only the history D_t changes. Harness A is the 197-word primary artifact (six principles: spend evaluations for information, probe independent directions, reuse the incumbent, match effort to budget, respect noise, re-center and repeat); Harness B is the 205-word independent replication; gate-failed outputs are called candidates, never harnesses.

**Evaluation protocol.** Public tasks: shifted-quadratic practice family plus three held-out transformed BBOB landscapes (Bent Cigar, Gallagher-101, Rastrigin f15); each primary cell is N = 10 paired instances, with the central comparison powered by a separately generated N = 30 study. Comparators include random search, CMA-ES, plain and strengthened GP-BO (Matérn-5/2 + expected improvement), the retained practice program, unaided LLMs, a hand-written optimization hint, and inference-time-compute variants (thinking, code sandbox). Base and Harness arms share formatter, parser, bounds, and inference configuration — the distilled text is the only difference. Statistics: two-sided paired Wilcoxon tests, paired bootstrap, Holm correction where multiple comparisons are involved, and null results treated as bounded comparisons rather than evidence of equality.

## Results & Highlights

- **Harness A cuts Gemini Flash regret by 48% on the practice family (N=30)**: final quadratic regret drops from 69.5 (Base) to 36.0 (Harness A), p < .001; paired-bootstrap mean improvement 33.5 with 95% interval [19.0, 49.7], 27/30 instances improve; the harness shows no detected difference from plain GP-BO at 30.4 (p=.64), while the unaided Base model is statistically indistinguishable from random search (64.2 ± 30.2 vs 79.4 ± 22.0, p = .375 at N=10).
- **Cross-executor and cross-provider transfer with the same frozen text (N=30)**: quadratic regret falls on every executor — Flash 69.5→36.0, Flash-Lite 116.2→48.5, Claude Sonnet 83.5→47.3 (all p ≤ .005, Sonnet surviving Holm correction across both families); on shifted Rastrigin, Sonnet improves 106.6→54.0; a Gemini Pro cell improves 77.5→21.4 (p = .010).
- **A generic hand-written hint cannot explain the effect**: the manual hint helps Flash (64.2→29.8, p = .037) but *harms* Flash-Lite (85.9→142.4, p = .002), whereas Harness A improves both — the gain is not "any optimization-themed system message."
- **Test-time compute is not a substitute, but composes**: Flash with thinking or sandbox+thinking but no harness stays at 54.3/54.2 regret (vs 64.2 Base, 36.0 harness); harness + sandbox + thinking on Flash reaches 16.1 ± 4.3 (p = .014 vs text-only), while remaining neutral on the equipped weak model Flash-Lite (29.0 vs 31.5, p = .70).
- **Held-out BBOB landscapes (N=10)**: Harness A lowers Base-LLM mean regret on all three — Bent Cigar 28.3M→15.7M, Gallagher-101 69.0→52.4 (the only one surviving Holm correction, pHolm = .018), Rastrigin f15 183.7→149.7 — but comparisons against the BO baselines do not survive family-wise correction; the supported claim is "transfer into the BO tier, not BO dominance."
- **Independent replication (4 landscapes × N=30, pinned Flash snapshot)**: both accepted harnesses beat Base LLM on every landscape (all Holm-adjusted p ≤ .007) and beat plain GP-BO on all four (all surviving Holm) — Harness A: 55.7→28.2 (quadratic), 92.5→56.3 (Rastrigin), 22.0M→13.1M (Bent Cigar), 64.7→50.7 (Gallagher); Harness B: 21.4, 59.5, 14.0M, 48.5 — despite the two texts sharing zero sentences.
- **Sealed YouTube reward-tuning benchmark (7-D, 30 paired stratified-bootstrap resamples of one historical dataset)**: the replication harness attains the lowest point estimate at every checkpoint — 0.0495 best-so-far regret at 20 evals vs 0.0894 for Google Vizier, 0.1410 for standard GP-BO, and 0.1720 for Flash Base; the authors explicitly report point estimates only and claim no statistical superiority since resamples are not independent trials.
- **Artifact ablations and audits**: distilling from code alone (dropping the practice record) is worse on Gemini Pro (32.7 vs 21.4, p = .013, N=30) and collapses off-distribution on the GPT-5 runtime (65.3 vs 19.0 on textbook Rastrigin, p = .010); exact-solve rate is 0/30 on text-only held-out cells (0/10 with sandbox), ruling out function-identification shortcuts; behavioral signatures show proposals within radius 2 of the incumbent rising from 69% to 79% and step-length CV falling from 1.04 to 0.68.

## Authors & Credibility

The paper is authored by an eleven-person team (Yi Wu and Zheng Ren with equal contribution) at Google, Mountain View, spanning research and product-oriented names — consistent with the internal YouTube reward-tuning case study and the Google Vizier comparator. The work is unusually credible by LLM-optimization-paper standards: artifacts are frozen and sealed before evaluation, an independent N=30 reproduction powers the central claim rather than a retrospective sample enlargement, an independent end-to-end replication (Harness B) and a disclosed gate-failure audit (Candidate C) test stability rather than cherry-picking, exact-solve audits probe for shortcut behavior, and claims are explicitly downgraded where family-wise correction fails. Code is released via an anonymous.4open.science repository; the internal production benchmark necessarily remains sealed. The statistical reporting (paired tests, bootstrap intervals, Holm correction, "null = bounded comparison" framing) reflects genuine methodological care.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: The ingredients are known — LLMs writing optimizers (FunSearch, AlphaEvolve, LLaMEA) and feedback-driven prompt improvement (OPRO, PromptAgent, GEPA). The genuine novelty is the *discipline*: one-shot distillation from executable practice on external objectives, gated and frozen before evaluation, with the finding that a 197-word text (not code) is the portable unit of transfer. This is a real contribution to methodology, more than a new algorithm.
- **Rigor**: Above average. Paired designs, pre-freezing, Holm corrections, an exact-solve audit, and an honest split between confirmatory (N=30) and exploratory (N=10) evidence. However, the primary study's headline cell at N=10 was only directional (64.2→32.9, p = .131) and had to be rescued by the separate N=30 study — the paper is transparent about this, but it means the initial design was underpowered.
- **Reproducibility**: Partially reproducible. The sandbox/caches are released, but the Gemini-Pro code-only ablation lacks released per-instance trajectories (the authors themselves flag it as "provisional"), the GPT-5 cells require the original agent runtime and are "reported as-is," and the entire production claim rests on internal, unshared data. External replication of the YouTube result is impossible by construction.
- **Significance**: The scope is narrow — one budget (B=20), one dimension (D=8), and a practice family (shifted quadratics) that is the easiest case for the "BO tier" claim. On genuinely held-out BBOB functions only Gallagher-101 survives correction, the classical comparison omits HEBO and TuRBO, and CMA-ES performs suspiciously poorly (68.0 on quadratics — worse than the unaided Flash Base), suggesting a default/population configuration that flatters the LLM arms. Notably, on held-out Rastrigin the distilled text (149.7) is substantially worse than its own source program (112.1) — distillation loses real performance off-distribution, which the headline understates.
- **Red flags**: The production benchmark's 30 "replicates" are bootstrap resamples of a single historical dataset, not independent trials, and the paper admirably refuses significance claims — but the abstract still leads with that result ("lowest regret on a sealed YouTube benchmark"), which reads stronger than the evidence. The gate–outcome association rests on only two accepted runs versus one failure, so the gate itself is unvalidated. Cross-provider transfer covers exactly one non-Gemini model.

## Strengths

- Clean conceptual framing: practice makes strategies falsifiable during development, language removes implementation dependency at deployment, and freezing keeps evaluation interpretable — a template other agent-artifact papers should copy.
- Strong control design: Base and Harness arms share everything except the distilled text; the manual-hint control (helps Flash, harms Flash-Lite) decisively rules out "any optimization prompt works."
- Honest claim calibration: "transfer into the BO tier, not BO dominance" and explicit demotion of non-surviving comparisons, null results treated as bounded, not absent.
- Replication as a first-class result: two independently accepted harnesses with disjoint texts at the same tier, plus a published failure audit of the gate-rejected candidate.
- Behavioral analysis linking text to search geometry (step-length statistics, incumbent-anchoring fractions) without overclaiming causality.

## Limitations

- Single budget (20 evaluations), single dimension (8-D), and small paired samples (N=10) across most public cells; the central effect depends on one N=30 study.
- Practice family is quadratics; the strongest "BO-range" equivalence is on-distribution, and on held-out BBOB only one of three landscapes survives Holm correction.
- Distillation loses information: the retained program beats both harnesses off-distribution (e.g., 112.1 vs 149.7 on BBOB Rastrigin), and the code-only ablation is only partially verifiable (Gemini-Pro trajectories unreleased; GPT-5 cells not regenerable).
- Classical baselines omit modern strong competitors (HEBO, TuRBO), and CMA-ES appears weakly tuned; cross-provider transfer is limited to one Claude model.
- The YouTube production case uses bootstrap resamples of one sealed historical dataset — descriptive point estimates only, with no external auditability.
- One-shot distillation prioritizes auditability over adaptation; the gate threshold is validated only diagnostically (two passes, one failure).

## Relevance

高度契合"自进化智能体与自动化研究"方向：本文正是让 LLM 智能体通过可执行练习自我改进搜索策略、再把经验蒸馏为可审计的冻结产物的工作，其"评测前密封、冻结清单、失败审计"流程对长时间运行自主智能体系统的可信性与可复现性具有直接的方法学借鉴价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the practice–gate–distill–freeze recipe still yield a stable performance tier at larger budgets (B = 100+), higher dimensions, mixed-integer or constrained spaces, and under noisy/stochastic objectives where the "respect noise" principle is load-bearing?
- What exactly does the practice record contribute over the code skeleton — can the Gemini-Pro code-only gap (32.7 vs 21.4) be replicated with released trajectories, and do failure narratives matter more than success trajectories?
- Can the gate threshold be validated prospectively with more accepted/rejected runs, turning the current diagnostic gate–outcome association into a calibrated selection rule?
- How does a frozen text harness interact with runtime exposure of classical optimizer state (e.g., GP posterior features in the prompt) — complementary composition or interference?
- Would re-distilling from multiple accepted champions (at the cost of a new development/evaluation separation) close the off-distribution gap between the retained program (112.1 on BBOB Rastrigin) and its distilled text (149.7)?
