Methodology

方法论 — 三阶段共进化框架

CoER formalizes within-task adaptive injection as a finite-horizon, partially observable general-sum Markov game: the attacker injects at reached tool-return sites, sees only the defender's public trace and its own prior attempts, and may strike repeatedly within one task.

  1. Stage I

    Attacker SFT. DeepSeek V4 Pro and Seed2.0 attack the base defender; 3,995 successful conversations (11,655 attacker turns) supervise multi-turn, feedback-conditioned injection.

  2. Stage II

    Bilateral Co-PPO. Both roles train with role-masked token-level PPO against a 0.40/0.25/0.25/0.10 mixture of current and frozen historical opponents (plus fixed templates), with verifier-derived terminal rewards that build task utility into the objective.

  3. Stage III

    Verifier-gated Defender SFT. Retained attackers challenge GLM-5.2 / DeepSeek V4 Pro teachers; only uncompromised and task-complete demonstrations (5,760 trajectories) fine-tune the co-trained defender.

Data: 3,995 attacker-SFT dialogs · 5,760 defender-SFT trajectories · 12,705 RL configs · ≤20 defender turns/execution · compute up to seven 8-GPU nodes (checkpoint d430, SFT update 360)

The co-evolution loop is doing real work: the attacker genuinely strengthened (frozen cross-play above), yet CoER holds 0.18–0.47% across six attackers, vs. 15.10–31.29% for the RL-only defender. Matched 2×2 ablation: population-derived data +8.00 Safe-U pts, Co-PPO init +6.07 pts. Strengths

优势

  • Realistic threat model — attacker sees only the public trace, its own prior attempts, and binary contract feedback.
  • Bilateral opponent pools curb cyclic overfitting; validated both ways (Co-PPO > NoPop; attacker 65.65% vs 57.40%).
  • Dual-gated refinement — demonstrations verified on safety and task completion, with 14.81% clean replay anchoring utility.
  • Candid reporting — point estimates labeled descriptive, teacher dependence and verifier bounds disclosed.
  • External transfer on four independent protocols, not just the in-house suite.

Limitations

局限

  • 0.25% is not population robustness — union 4.97%, GPT-5.4 14.12%; a white-box attacker on the final defender would likely do better.
  • Strong-teacher dependence (GLM-5.2, DeepSeek V4 Pro, Seed2.0); the weaker-teacher case is “unestablished.”
  • Verifiers cover only enumerated goals in ≤20-turn executions; no independent human audit.
  • Single seed, single run, one execution per adaptive case — no variance estimates, no significance tests.
  • No equilibrium / exploitability analysis; heavy compute (7×8-GPU nodes), one 9B backbone.
  • Per-suite regressions survive: Travel Safe-U −15.00 vs Co-PPO; Banking clean utility −18.75; Shopping Safe-U stuck at 57.2%.

Questions for further exploration

待探索问题

  • Do the gains survive weaker teachers — or entirely self-generated verified demonstrations?
  • Is 4.97% an upper or lower bound under a white-box attack fine-tuned on the final CoER defender?
  • Can bilateral vs defender-only history be settled by a direct ablation at matched budget?
  • Do deterministic verifier contracts transfer to open-ended harm taxonomies (AgentHarm-style)?
  • Would process-level safety rewards cut the 12,705-rollout cost of Co-PPO?
  • Does the recipe scale past 9B — or does attacker strength keep rising in an arms race?
