Methodology

Analyze the comparison, not the distance

Every HNSW/Vamana-style index executes sequences of binary decisions — rank a candidate, prune an edge, follow a path. Quantization error only matters when it crosses that comparison's specific boundary.

  1. Decision shell. A signed functional Ψ (ranking: d(q,y)−d(q,x); RobustPrune: d(t,c)−α·d(s,c)) with margin Γ=|Ψ|. Theorem 2 splits P(flip) into boundary mass + calibrated residual tail — distribution-free, any τ>0, no independence assumption.
  2. Frozen-trace composition. Vamana's selection call is a deterministic state machine; per-comparison bounds compose into trace-, edge-, and path-level certificates (first disagreement pinpoints the divergence edge).
  3. Gaussian oracle. Stein's lemma gives exact sign–linear covariance identities; a rare-contamination counterexample (Thm 13, κ₄=Ω(D²)) proves diagnostics alone cannot buy the exponential tails the bounds need.
  4. Held-out block certificates. Hoeffding-style, finite-sample, simultaneous bounds on selective risk of a frozen quantized rule — from data alone, arbitrary within-block dependence.
  5. One decision interface wrapping coordinate sign codes, RaBitQ, Lucene BBQ, and PQ via family-specific residual mechanisms.

  • Covariance is the hidden driver: pooled residual correlation 0.870 on shared-query pairs; ignoring it overstates difference variance 7.42× — the correlation comes from local candidate selection, not identity collisions.
  • Coupling theorem holds universally: all 960 sampled Vamana selection calls satisfy it exactly; every observed pruning disagreement is a false keep (none false-pruned at α = 1.2).
  • Certificates always cover: at 512 blocks, direct-failure certificate 9.07% / 10.45% vs. observed validation risk 1.34% / 2.01% — never exceeded.
  • GIST repaired from diagnostics alone: rotation fixes calibration slope ≈ 0 and kurtosis 26 → 2.8; flips 35.83% → 9.33% (1-bit: 100% → 14.80%) — measurable before running any search.
  • Falsification of global metrics: two perturbations share Spearman 0.9860282 to seven digits yet flip 5.09% vs 10.00% of decisions — selecting by correlation or MSE picks the quantizer with twice the failure rate.
Strengths
  • Reframes quantized ANN analysis from score fidelity to per-decision stability — the level at which graph algorithms actually consume distances — around one simple, distribution-free theorem.
  • The covariance finding is theoretically clean and practically striking (7.42× variance overstatement on Cohere).
  • Rotation-duality is a surprising, well-isolated intervention — one change, opposite effects on two decision populations, with an identified mechanism (variance shrinks 3–4×, shared-query correlation collapses 0.870 → 0.435).
  • Operational deliverables: per-decision reliability score, an eligibility gate that rejects broken configurations before any search run, and always-covered held-out certificates.
Limitations
  • No end-to-end recall, throughput, or index-size numbers; coverage and navigability excluded — the theory-to-recall bridge (95% vs 2%) stays qualitative.
  • Gaussian identities are oracle results with no transfer theorem; joint-law KS tests reject 29% of subsets even on Cohere, 58% on Wolt-CLIP.
  • Trace certificates saturate on long paths; held-out certificates need representative i.i.d. blocks and don't transfer under distribution shift.
  • Modest local-decision scale (80 calibration + 160 held-out queries per dataset; 960 coupling calls); baselines are reference reimplementations, not tuned production systems.
  • The reliability score M needs exact margins — as given, it is an offline audit, not an online signal.
Questions for further exploration
Q1 · CLOSE THE LOOP

Can boundary-residual certificates plus graph-expansion arguments jointly predict end-to-end recall@k — closing the loop to the motivating 95%-vs-2% gap?

Q2 · ADAPTIVE SEARCH

Do beam search and data-dependent candidate sets break the frozen-trace coupling, and does the martingale extension of Theorem 8 survive the induced dependence?

Q3 · DISTRIBUTION SHIFT

How do the certificates transfer under query drift or re-embedding — and can the exact-margin selector be replaced by an observable margin envelope for online routing?

Q4 · SCALING GEOMETRY

Do the shared-query covariance structure and β ≈ 1.5 boundary exponent persist for LLM-embedding families at 2k–8k dimensions (e.g., Matryoshka truncations), where thin-shell behaviour may differ?

Q5 · PILOT SELECTOR

Could a pilot choosing rotation, bit width, and verification cutoff purely from covariance statistics recover exact-score topology at a bounded flip budget — and where would the recall/QPS Pareto frontier land against un-rotated and PQ baselines on a full index?
