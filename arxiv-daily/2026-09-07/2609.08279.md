> **一句话总结：** 提出"恢复反事实"（restore counterfactual）审计方法——把问题的金标准证据重新注入读取时上下文并重跑同一读者模型——将智能体记忆驱逐造成的错误分解为"不可逆（证据被毁）/可恢复（检索漏掉）/残余（读者不会用）"三类；在 LongMemEval-S 上，top-k 检索、80k 预算下 FIFO/随机/冗余感知驱逐的错误中 0.67–0.73 为不可逆损失，8k 预算下四种策略全部达到 1.00，说明预算-精度前沿若不报告检索机制则不可直接比较。

> **TL;DR:** This COLM 2026 workshop paper introduces the restore counterfactual: for each oracle-answerable error made under an eviction policy, re-inject the question's gold evidence into the read-time context and rerun the frozen reader (GPT-4o-mini), then classify the error as irreversible (evidence evicted), recoverable (evidence survived but retrieval missed it), or residual (reader still fails). On LongMemEval-S under top-k retrieval at an 80k-token budget, the irreversible two-bin share is 0.67–0.73 for FIFO/random/redundancy-aware eviction vs 0.60 for LLM-importance (straddling 0.5), rising to 1.00 for all four policies at 8k tokens; the recoverable-share gap between the top-k and forced-gold regimes reaches +0.29 for FIFO at 80k (CI .19–.39, Holm-corrected p=.007), so budget–accuracy frontiers are not comparable unless the retrieval regime is reported. At matched accuracy, no policy pair dissociates in irreversible rate (0 of 9 pairs, resolution 1.2–6 pp), though a deliberately destructive control is detected in all 9 contrasts (p<0.001).

## 核心贡献

- **恢复反事实度量（restore counterfactual）**：一种针对外部智能体记忆存储的逐问题、成对干预审计方法。从策略驱逐后的存储出发，把该问题的全部金标准证据集 G_q 重新注入读取时上下文，用完全冻结的读者（同一 prompt、温度 0、同一判分器）重新作答，记录正确性变化。
- **三类错误分解**：在"oracle 可答"（读者在干净全金标注入下能答对）的分母上，把每个策略错误精确归为三类——不可逆（恢复后答对且金标被驱逐，只有更多保留才能修复）、可恢复（恢复后答对且金标全部存活，属检索失误，换更好检索器即可修复）、残余（恢复后仍答错，属读者利用失败）。
- **受控实证研究**：在 LongMemEval-S（470 个证据标注问题，约 102k token 历史）上，用三种预算（8k/30k/80k）、两种检索机制（强制金标注入 vs 冻结 top-k 排序器）审计 FIFO、随机、冗余感知、LLM 重要性四种驱逐策略，外加无驱逐参照和一个故意破坏性对照（信息密度启发式，以约 3 倍基线速率驱逐金标）。
- **对领域的规范性结论**：预算-精度前沿（budget–accuracy frontier）在不同检索机制下结果不可直接比较——可恢复错误在 top-k 机制下存在、在强制金标注入下按构造消失，因此未来报告必须声明读取时检索设置。
- **方法论严谨性示范**：预注册式冻结协议、问题聚类 bootstrap（10,000 次重采样）+ Holm–Bonferroni 校正、安慰剂对照（等长非金标内容仅翻转 8.3% vs 金标恢复 100%）、跨读者复现（GPT-5.4-mini）、独立判分器审计（GPT-5.5 与 GPT-4o-mini 一致率 95.7%，κ=0.90）。

## Key Contributions

- **The restore-counterfactual metric** (§3.1): a per-question paired intervention for external agent-memory stores. Formally, restore gain(q) = acc_q(restored) − acc_q(policy) ∈ {−1, 0, 1}, and irreversible loss(q) = max(0, restore gain(q)) · 1[G_q ⊄ S_P], where G_q is the benchmark-labeled gold evidence set and S_P the post-eviction store. Reader, prompt, decoding (temperature 0), judge, and ranker are held fixed, so the comparison isolates the availability of G_q.
- **The recoverable/irreversible/residual decomposition** (§3.2) on an oracle-answerable denominator: each error is exactly one of irreversible (eviction destroyed the evidence; only retention fixes it), recoverable (evidence survived but the retriever missed it; a better retriever fixes it), or residual (evidence present, reader fails to use it — typically cross-session aggregation).
- **A controlled audit of four eviction policies** on LongMemEval-S across three binding budgets (8k/30k/80k vs ~102k-token histories) and two retrieval regimes, plus a no-evict reference and a deliberately destructive info-density control that evicts gold at ≈3× the baseline rate.
- **A reporting-standard finding**: budget–accuracy frontiers are not directly comparable unless the read-time retrieval regime is reported, because the recoverable bin is populated under top-k but empty under forced-gold by construction.
- **Honest negative result with a power analysis**: at matched accuracy (caliper |Δacc| ≤ 0.05), no pair of policies dissociates in irreversible rate (0 of 9 baseline pairs; |Δrate| ≤ 0.036; every CI spans 0; resolution 1.2–6 percentage points), while the destructive control is detected in all 9 contrasts — the authors explicitly disclaim statistical equivalence (would need a powered TOST).

## Methodology

The paper follows a causal-measurement template: define the target quantity (end-task accuracy loss caused by a forgetting decision), identify it from observables under stated assumptions, and give the estimator.

**The estimator.** For a question q answered by a frozen reader over memory units injected from a capacity-bounded store S_P, the restore counterfactual reinstates the full gold evidence set G_q (benchmark-labeled units containing the answer) into the read-time context and re-answers. Restore gain measures the effect of restoring G_q on correctness; its positive part is accuracy recoverable by read-time restoration. Restoration forces G_q ahead of ranker results under a fixed 2000-token cap (which fits every gold set; max ≈1,000).

**Three validity assumptions** (§3.3), each with an empirical check:
- A1 (oracle-answerability): only errors the reader answers correctly under clean full-gold injection are scored, so bins reflect what the budget did to evidence rather than reader inability.
- A2 (judge validity): reader and judge are both GPT-4o-mini; repeat self-consistency of 1.0 on a 40-question calibration gate (threshold ≥0.90); an independent GPT-5.5 judge agrees at 95.7% (κ=0.90) on a stratified sample of 393 grades.
- A3 (restore tightness): full-gold restore makes the irreversible share an upper bound on destruction. Over the 2,276 irreversible cases, restoring only surviving gold reclassifies 0.3%; injecting the complete retained store recovers just 2.0% (98% remain wrong — consistent with genuine destruction); an equal-length non-gold placebo at the gold position flips only 8.3% versus 100% for the gold restore, so the bin reflects evidence availability, not placement.

**Experimental grid.** LongMemEval-S (pinned by sha256; 470 evidence-labeled questions, abstention excluded); four eviction policies (FIFO, random, redundancy-aware, and an LLM-importance arm scored 1–10 by a frozen GPT-4o-mini scorer that never sees the question or gold); budgets 8k/30k/80k tokens; two retrieval regimes — (a) forced-gold injection (isolates destruction) and (b) a frozen deterministic BM25-lite top-k ranker (top-k = 60, realistic). Primary reader/judge GPT-4o-mini, robustness reader GPT-5.4-mini. Statistics: three seeds, question-cluster bootstrap (10,000 resamples), Holm–Bonferroni within hypothesis families, under a protocol frozen before measurement. Evaluation code was construct-validated with a deterministic substitute for model calls and released with per-question records (github.com/megagonlabs/restore-counterfactual).

## Results & Highlights

- **Destruction dominates (R1).** On LongMemEval-S under top-k retrieval at an 80k-token budget, the two-bin irreversible share irr/(irr+rec) is 0.71 for FIFO (CI .61–.81), 0.73 for random, and 0.67 for redundancy-aware eviction, versus 1.00 for the destructive control — every baseline lower bound exceeds 0.5. LLM-importance is the exception at 0.60 (CI .47–.72), straddling 0.5.
- **Tightening the budget drives the share to certainty.** The two-bin irreversible share climbs from 0.67–0.73 at 80k to 0.99 at 30k and 1.00 at 8k for all four evicting policies; the absolute irreversible rate (irr/N) for FIFO rises from 0.16 (80k) to 0.59 (30k) to 0.78 (8k) on the N=336 oracle-answerable denominator.
- **Retrieval regimes are not interchangeable (R2).** The recoverable-share gap (top-k minus forced-gold) at 80k is +0.29 for FIFO (CI .19–.39), +0.27 for random, and +0.33 for redundancy-aware, all significant after Holm correction (p_adj = .007); 4 of 12 evicting cells reject, and the pattern replicates with the GPT-5.4-mini reader.
- **At matched accuracy, no policy dissociates — and the test has power (R3).** 0 of 9 accuracy-matched baseline pairs show an irreversible-rate difference (all |Δrate| ≤ 0.036, every CI spanning 0) at resolution 1.2–6 percentage points; LLM-importance also fails to dissociate in 0 of 6 matched comparisons (per-pair n ≈ 329 shared oracle-answerable qids). The destructive control is detected in all 9 contrasts (p < 0.001 under the primary reader; p < 0.05 across both readers).
- **Residual errors are reader-utilization failures (R4).** The residual bin is dominated by cross-session counting/summation errors (e.g., a $185 bike-expense total answered as "$65" even with all gold restored); judge strictness accounts for at most 20%. Swapping to the stronger GPT-5.4-mini reader more than halves the residual bin (no-evict@80k forced-gold: 59→28), with the corrected cases being exactly the aggregation errors.
- **Evidence layout matters.** Single-session questions are ~95% irreversible, while multi-session and temporal-reasoning questions carry residual shares of 23–34% and contribute most residual errors.

## Authors & Credibility

Single-author paper by Chen Shen of Megagon Labs (an industrial research lab), accepted at the COLM 2026 Workshop on Context Beyond the Window — a workshop venue, not a main conference, though the methodological care exceeds many main-conference papers. Credibility signals are unusually strong: a pre-specified protocol frozen before measurement with deviations explicitly flagged (the H2 statistic was amended post-freeze because the originally specified forced-gold share was an algebraic identity pinned at 1.00 — the paper openly discusses this); a deliberately destructive positive control; placebo and restore-tightness ablations; cross-reader replication; an independent-judge audit with reported Cohen's κ; dataset pinned by hash; and a public artifact (github.com/megagonlabs/restore-counterfactual) with per-question records, pinned model snapshot ids, and verbatim prompts in Appendix D.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: The intervention class (leave-one-out context attribution, per Cohen-Wang et al. 2024) is not new, and AgingBench already used paired oracle-injection probes; the novelty is narrow but real — the first per-question restore applied to capacity-bounded eviction from external agent-memory stores with end-task accuracy as the outcome. The paper is candid that it is an instrument, not a new system, and does not rank policies.
- **Rigor**: Among the most careful workshop papers I have audited — question-cluster bootstrap, Holm–Bonferroni, positive control, placebo, matched-accuracy caliper, and an explicit refusal to claim equivalence without TOST. The self-flagged post-freeze H2 amendment is a model of honest reporting, though it does mean one third of the headline analysis (R3) is exploratory rather than confirmatory.
- **Reproducibility**: High. Released code, per-question records, pinned dataset hash and model snapshot ids, temperature 0 throughout, verbatim prompts. The main irreproducibility risk is model drift — GPT-4o-mini is a moving API target mitigated only partially by dated snapshot ids.
- **Weaknesses that constrain the headline claim**: (i) a single benchmark (LongMemEval-S only — conversational, English, one question distribution), so "destruction dominates" may not transfer to task-agent or RAG stores; (ii) reader and judge are the same model (GPT-4o-mini), so correlated reader–judge bias is invisible to the self-consistency check, and the cross-judge audit uses GPT-5.5 — still an OpenAI model, as the paper itself concedes; (iii) the retrieval arm uses a BM25-lite lexical ranker with top-k = 60 — a deliberately modest retriever whose miss rate directly inflates the recoverable bin, so the 0.67–0.73 irreversible shares are conditioned on this specific ranker; (iv) the audited "policies" are simple policy classes (FIFO, random, redundancy-aware, a generic 1–10 importance scorer that never sees the question), not published retention systems like EMBER or OSL-MR — so the R3 null says little about whether learned, query-aware retention could actually dissociate; (v) the LLM-importance arm was run later as a separate grid with its own oracle-answerable filter (N=332 vs 336), and its most favorable cell (80k, accuracy 0.69 vs ≈0.62 for baselines) falls outside the accuracy-matched caliper, so the headline "LLM-importance 0.60 vs 0.67–0.73" comparison is explicitly descriptive, not causal.
- **Overclaim check**: The abstract is well-calibrated — it states shares, CIs, and the resolution of the null. The "first per-item restore-counterfactual audit" priority claim is plausibly scoped but rests on the usual to-our-knowledge hedge.
- **Significance**: The actionable output for the field is the reporting-standard result (frontiers must state their retrieval regime) and a reusable diagnostic; it is a measurement paper whose value compounds only if others adopt the instrument.

## Strengths

- Turns a conflation that the entire budget–accuracy-frontier literature suffers from (irreversible destruction vs recoverable retrieval misses have opposite remedies but look identical on the accuracy axis) into a clean, per-question, assumption-audited decomposition.
- Excellent causal-inference hygiene for an empirical NLP paper: pre-frozen protocol, paired per-question scoring, placebo control (8.3% flip vs 100% for gold restore), positive control detected in all 9 contrasts, and explicit upper-bound semantics for the irreversible bin with three slack quantifications.
- Honest treatment of a negative result: the no-dissociation finding (R3) is reported with its detection resolution (1.2–6 pp) and an explicit refusal to claim equivalence, and the degenerate original statistic is disclosed rather than buried.
- The residual-bin mechanism analysis (cross-session aggregation failures, $185→"$65") is concrete and immediately useful: it predicts that stronger readers, not better memory, fix that error class — confirmed by the GPT-5.4-mini halving (59→28).
- Fully released artifact with per-question records, pinned hashes/snapshots, and verbatim prompts.

## Limitations

- Single benchmark (LongMemEval-S, conversational QA) — no evidence yet for task-agent memories, RAG corpora, or multilingual settings; the audit also requires gold evidence labels plus an oracle-answerable filter, restricting it to benchmark analysis rather than deployment auditing.
- Reader and judge share the same model (GPT-4o-mini), and the cross-check judges (GPT-5.5) share the provider; a non-OpenAI or human judge is left to future work.
- The realistic retrieval regime uses a frozen BM25-lite ranker (top-k = 60); recoverable shares are ranker-specific, and a neural retriever could shift the two-bin balance.
- Audited policies are simple policy classes, not published retention systems (EMBER, OSL-MR, BudgetMem), so the matched-accuracy null does not rule out dissociations for learned retention policies.
- The R3 null resolution is 1.2–6 percentage points and equivalence is untested (no powered TOST); the single-author result would benefit from independent replication, and residual-bin size is reader-dependent by construction.

## Relevance

对研究"智能体长期记忆 / 预算化保留"的工作直接相关：它提供了一个可复用的逐问题审计工具，把记忆驱逐的精度损失分解为保留、检索、读者三类瓶颈，并与 budget–accuracy frontier（如 BudgetMem、EMBER）这条研究线正面对话。若你在做记忆系统评测或压缩/驱逐策略设计，"必须报告检索机制"这一规范性结论和三分法分解可直接嵌入你的实验设计。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Do the irreversible shares hold with a neural (embedding-based) retriever instead of BM25-lite, or does a stronger ranker materially shrink the recoverable bin at 80k?
- Would learned, query-aware retention policies (e.g., EMBER, OSL-MR) dissociate from FIFO/random/redundancy-aware at matched accuracy where the simple policy classes did not (0 of 9)?
- Can the audit be extended to stores with re-represented or summarized units (the current design requires units to correspond to labeled evidence), e.g., via reconstruction or summary-to-evidence alignment?
- How does the decomposition behave under compression-based eviction (summaries rather than dropped sessions) where "irreversible" becomes graded information loss rather than binary destruction?
- Does a non-OpenAI or human judge change the residual bin, given that reader and judge currently share both model and provider?
- Can the instrument audit privacy-mandated deletion (the ethics-section use case) — quantifying the task cost of required forgetting on deployment traces rather than benchmarks?
