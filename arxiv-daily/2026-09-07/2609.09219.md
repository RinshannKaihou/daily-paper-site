> **一句话总结：** 提出"发现认证协议"（DCP），通过三道关卡（密封评测验证有用性 → 同条件新智能体恢复实验检验信息贡献 → 真实/中性反馈配对实验度量反馈效应），把"AI 研究智能体做出了发现"的声明转化为可执行、可复现的统计审计；在 SQLite 索引优化与虚拟催化剂优化两个任务上，96 个无谱系挑战者 0 次恢复目标分数（恢复概率上界 0.0468），30 对反馈实验中真实反馈 30/30 恢复而中性反馈 0/30，并由确定性验证器离线复现全部裁决。

> **TL;DR:** A CMU team proposes the Discovery Certification Protocol (DCP), which converts an AI research agent's "discovery" claim into three executable tests: (1) sealed-evaluation utility gain over a baseline, (2) a recovery audit where matched fresh agents — given the registered background, initial observations, and the target run's captured Web bytes but NOT its experiment history — try to reach the target score by any valid method, and (3) a randomized truthful-vs-neutral feedback comparison from a shared checkpoint. In the two full audits — SQLite-Web index selection with DeepSeek-v4-flash (sealed score 0.8855 vs. 0.0 no-index baseline, an 88.55% cut in traffic-weighted VM work) and virtual catalyst control with DeepSeek-v4-pro (score 1.0 vs. 0.5990 baseline) — Gate 2 saw 0/96 challenger recoveries with best challenger scores of only 0.6734 and 0.8146 against recovery lines of 0.8805 and 0.95 (finite-sample recovery-probability upper bound 0.0468 < ρ = 0.05), and Gate 3 saw 30/30 truthful vs. 0/30 neutral recoveries (paired effect 1.0, 99% CI [0.6379, 1.0]) with passing 60-pair null calibration. A deterministic, LLM-free verifier replays every decision from frozen evidence; a knapsack case shows the recovery veto actually fires (a challenger hit 0.9363 > 0.9329 line, refuting Core). The tasks are small synthetic calibrations rather than real scientific discoveries, so DCP is best read as a rigorously engineered audit *methodology* whose scalability to AlphaEvolve-scale claims remains unproven.

## 核心贡献

- **把"发现"声明转化为可执行的审计协议。** DCP 不问"分数高不高"，而问三个可检验的问题：结果在密封评测上是否真的优于基线（Gate 1）；一个拿到相同起始信息（背景知识 K、初始观测 E0、目标运行观察到的全部网页字节 Wobs）但不掌握实验历史 L* 的同条件新智能体，能否用**任意合法方法**恢复到目标分数 x−ε（Gate 2）；真实实验反馈相对中性反馈是否带来因果增益（Gate 3）。
- **"恢复证人"（recovery witness）机制接受一切替代路线。** 重组已知模块、跨领域迁移、不同实现，只要通过注册的合法性检查并达到数值阈值，即构成恢复证人并触发 Core 否决——认证的是"信息边界内的不可恢复性"，而非某种特定实现的新颖性。
- **两类可分离的裁决：DCP Core 与 DCP Evidence。** Core 要求对照充分、零观测恢复、且单次新鲜回合恢复概率的有限样本上界 pupper = 1 − α^(1/n) ≤ ρ；Evidence 在 Core 之上叠加经独立零校准（等价带 ±δsham）与注册效应边际 δevidence 的配对反馈效应。
- **统计纪律工程化。** 预注册、密封评测、对称评估器、错误预算分配（总计 α = 0.05）、Bonferroni 候选分类、逐案计数的回合抽取回执，全部固化在机器可读证据包中。
- **确定性、无 LLM 的验证器 + 开源工具链。** 裁决可从冻结证据离线复现；发布 dcp-audit 与 dcp-harness（PyPI），两次完整审计成本仅 56.40 与 61.17 美元（435 与 507 个记录会话）。
- **五案例覆盖全部决策空间。** SQLite-Web 与虚拟催化剂（Core + Evidence 正例）、设备校准（构造信息亏缺的 Core 正例，0/80 恢复）、背包问题（恢复证人触发否决的反例）、低样本仿射（audit-incomplete），证明决策规则既会通过也会拒绝。

## Key Contributions

- **Reframes discovery claims as executable audits.** DCP replaces "the score is high" with three falsifiable questions: Gate 1 — does the artifact beat the baseline by a registered minimum gain δmin on a sealed evaluation (LCB[µ(A*) − µ(b)] ≥ δmin)? Gate 2 — can a fresh matched agent, given K, complete E0, and every Web byte Wobs the target run saw but not its experiment history L*, reach score ≥ x − ε by *any* valid method? Gate 3 — does truthful feedback causally improve outcomes over a specified neutral policy from a shared checkpoint?
- **Method-agnostic recovery witnesses.** Any admissible route to the numerical target (recombination, transfer, reimplementation) supplies a recovery witness and triggers the Core veto, decoupling certification from implementation-specific ablations.
- **Two separable decisions with explicit statistical meaning.** DCP Core = adequate controls + zero observed recoveries + finite-sample bound pupper = 1 − αrecovery^(1/n) ≤ ρ on one fresh registered episode. DCP Evidence = Core plus a checkpoint-conditional feedback effect Δfeedback with LCB ≥ δevidence + δsham, supported by an independent 60-pair null calibration inside a registered equivalence band.
- **Engineered preregistration discipline.** Sealed evaluators, symmetric scoring of baseline/target/controls, committed draw receipts, per-gate error budgets totaling 0.05, complete ledgers including failed attempts, and a threat model treating the claimant as potentially strategic.
- **Deterministic, LLM-free verifier and released tooling.** All decisions replay offline from frozen evidence bundles; dcp-audit and dcp-harness are on PyPI, and the two complete audits cost only $56.40 and $61.17 (435 and 507 recorded sessions).
- **Calibration suite spanning the decision space.** Five cases exercise Evidence-positive (SQLite-Web, catalyst), Core-positive (device calibration, 0/80 recoveries), recovered/refuted (knapsack, where a challenger crossed the line), and audit-incomplete (undersampled affine) outcomes.

## Methodology

DCP organizes an audit around five registered objects (Table 1): background K (model, task rules, starter code, tools, public methods), initial observations E0 (fixed independently of the run's actions), research history L* (hypotheses, candidates, scores, adaptive queries created during the target run), final artifact A* selected by a pre-frozen rule, and validity predicate P (machine-checkable constraints plus a numerical threshold). The E0/L* boundary follows information provenance: a measurement returned because the agent chose an action belongs to L*.

**Gate 1 (useful improvement):** sealed evaluation computes µ(A*) − µ(b) with a bounded empirical-Bernstein interval (sampled populations) or exact mean (exhaustive finite sets); the lower confidence bound must clear δmin, and the baseline must sit below the recovery region.

**Gate 2 (recovery):** fresh matched agents receive K, complete E0, the registered interface, tools, budget, and the complete recorded Web packet Wobs, while environment responses to their actions follow a frozen non-directional policy (no new task scores). Recovery is P(a) = Valid(a) ∧ score(a) ≥ x − ε with registered tolerance ε < δmin. A qualified witness (which must also satisfy execution, information, and provenance checks) closes the audit as *recovered* and refutes Core. With zero recoveries in n independent episodes, pupper = 1 − αrecovery^(1/n); Core requires pupper ≤ ρ plus adequacy: positive controls solving known instances, opportunity budget, independent draws, and valid-output rates. The bound attaches to the registered generation distribution QB — a best-of-k procedure counts as one k-candidate episode.

**Gate 3 (feedback effect, optional):** from a frozen lineage-prefix checkpoint c, fresh paired branches receive either truthful feedback from their own actions or messages from a registered neutral policy matched in timing, schema, and approximate length. The estimand is Δfeedback = E[u(Atruthful) − u(Aneutral) | c]. Independent null tasks (answers determined by E0) compare the neutral channel against a second non-directional policy; their CI must lie inside ±δsham (TOST-style equivalence). Evidence requires LCB(Δfeedback) ≥ δevidence + δsham. A recording gateway stores every Web query and exact model-visible byte, so recovery bounds condition on Wobs.

**Experimental instantiation:** two complete three-gate audits — (a) SQLite-Web: a 16-query-family event service where exactly 4 families carry hidden high traffic and the agent selects 4 partial indexes; score = 1 − (traffic-weighted VM steps with plan / steps without secondary indexes), computed on a private database; the target run read two public SQLite doc pages through the gateway, its first plan scored 0.0369, and feedback on per-family traffic led it to pick the 4 high-traffic families for a sealed 0.8855. (b) Virtual catalyst: 5 controls × 8 levels = 32,768 recipes; the agent commits an anchor recipe, a trusted harness expands it into a 36-well one-factor plate returning yield/selectivity/usable-product, then one static final recipe is scored over 256 sealed conditions (baseline 0.5990, target 1.0). Audited agents were DeepSeek-v4-flash (SQLite) and DeepSeek-v4-pro (catalyst), executed via Claude CLI against provider-verified model identities, with challengers drawn as randomized equivalent presentations from a fixed QB (96 episodes each; αrecovery = 0.01 and ρ = 0.05 require 90). Statistical allocations: 0.005 each for main validation, candidate classification, control adequacy, neutral recovery; 0.01 each for challenger recovery, feedback effect, null calibration (total 0.05); δevidence = 0.34, δsham = 0.17. Three calibration cases (device calibration, multidimensional knapsack, low-sample affine) exercise the remaining decision branches. A deterministic offline verifier recomputes every decision from the frozen bundles.

## Results & Highlights

- **SQLite-Web (DeepSeek-v4-flash), Gate 1:** the target plan scored 0.8855 on the sealed workload versus 0.0 for the no-index baseline — an 88.55% reduction in traffic-weighted SQLite VM steps — up from 0.0369 for the agent's first committed plan before traffic feedback.
- **SQLite-Web, Gate 2:** 0/96 no-lineage challenger episodes recovered the target; the best challenger reached only 0.6734 against the recovery line of 0.8805 (x = 0.8855 minus ε = 0.005), and positive controls passed 45/45 (recall lower bound 0.8889 vs. the registered 0.8 minimum), yielding pupper = 0.0468 < ρ = 0.05.
- **Virtual catalyst (DeepSeek-v4-pro), Gates 1–2:** the final recipe scored 1.0 vs. the fixed baseline's 0.5990; 0/96 challengers recovered, with a best challenger score of 0.8146 against the 0.95 recovery line and the same pupper = 0.0468 bound.
- **Gate 3 feedback effects (both tasks):** 30/30 truthful-feedback branches recovered vs. 0/30 neutral-feedback branches; estimated binary policy effect = 1.0 with an exact paired-binary 99% CI of [0.6379, 1.0], exceeding the required margin 0.51 (= δevidence 0.34 + δsham 0.17); equivalently 0.6379 − 0.17 = 0.4679 > 0.34. Both 60-pair null studies showed contrast 0 with 99% CI [−0.0950, 0.0950], inside the ±0.17 equivalence band; neutral-recovery upper bound was 0.1619 at 30 episodes. Verifier returned DCP Evidence in both scopes.
- **Decision-space coverage:** device calibration returned Core with 0/80 recoveries, 60/60 positive controls, and upper bound 0.0477; multidimensional knapsack (target 0.9349 vs. baseline 0.9140, line 0.9329) produced qualified recovery witnesses at 0.9363 and 0.9356 in a single matched episode — the better one above the target run — triggering the Core veto; the affine case ended audit-incomplete.
- **Cost and replayability:** the catalyst and SQLite-Web audits consumed 435 and 507 recorded sessions at $56.40 and $61.17, and a deterministic, LLM-free verifier reproduced all decisions offline from frozen evidence.

## Authors & Credibility

The four authors (Jingjie Ning, Shanshan Zhong, Xiaochuan Li, Ji Zeng) are at the School of Computer Science, Carnegie Mellon University, in the group behind much of the recent auto-research-auditing literature the paper builds on (the authors' own prior preprints on specialist agents, closed-loop molecular/materials research with held-out transfer, the "implementation lottery" in automated research, and SkillLearnBench are all cited, several with Ning/Zhong/Li/Zeng as authors). This is a methodologically sophisticated team: the statistics borrow correctly from Rubin's causal framework, Schuirmann's two-one-sided-tests equivalence procedure, and the preregistration literature (Nosek et al.), and the threat model explicitly treats the evidence producer as potentially strategic. Tooling is released (PyPI packages dcp-audit and dcp-harness, plus a GitHub repo), and the reproducibility statement commits to frozen machine-readable bundles with deterministic offline replay. Unusual transparency: the AI Use Statement discloses heavy generative-AI involvement (GPT/Codex for design and drafting, Claude CLI as the registered execution layer, DeepSeek-v4 models as audited agents) while asserting author responsibility for claims and artifacts. Formal issuance of the reported audits is "locally registered" — independent countersigning is specified but not yet exercised.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** The core reframing — certifying *non-recoverability from the starting information* rather than score deltas — is genuinely new relative to MLE-bench/PaperBench/FIRE-Bench-style evaluation, and the recovery-witness veto is a clean falsifiable rule. That said, the ingredients (preregistration, sealed tests, sham-controlled feedback, finite-sample bounds) are each standard; the contribution is their disciplined composition into a portable audit protocol.
- **Rigor:** High on statistics — per-gate error budgets, Bonferroni candidate classification, exact paired-binary intervals, adequacy checks that can render an audit *incomplete* rather than passing it. The knapsack case demonstrating the veto fires, and the affine case demonstrating underpowered audits fail, are honest self-calibration. A notable gap: the Gate 3 null calibration runs on a *different task family* (answers fixed by E0) than the target tasks, so transfer of the "neutral channel is inert" conclusion to SQLite/catalyst is assumed, not tested there.
- **Task scale vs. claim scale:** the audited "discoveries" are toy-sized by construction — picking 4 of 16 index families with 4 hidden high-traffic ones, and a 5-control/8-level (32,768-recipe) virtual catalyst. The abstract's framing ("AI research agents", AlphaEvolve-scale references) invites extrapolation the experiments do not support: recovery episodes for a real algorithmic discovery would cost orders of magnitude more, and the paper offers no evidence about how pupper or neutral-channel design behave at that scale.
- **Challenger strength:** the recovery bound conditions on the registered challenger distribution QB — here the *same model* (DeepSeek-v4-flash/pro) at matched budgets. Zero recoveries therefore means "this model at this budget could not rediscover it", not "it was not discoverable"; a stronger challenger model or larger budget could flip the decision. The paper states this scope honestly but headline readers will over-read "zero recoveries in 96 episodes".
- **Neutral-channel design:** the SQLite neutral arm fed "uniform family weights" in place of truthful traffic weights — a message that is not merely uninformative but arguably misleading, which plausibly inflates the 30/30-vs-0/30 contrast (effect = 1.0) toward the ceiling. Null calibration against a second non-directional channel partially addresses this, but on the separate null family (see above).
- **Self-audit circularity:** producer, registry, and verifier are currently the same team; the "independent audit authority" is a specified role, not an exercised one for these decisions.
- **Reproducibility:** strong in the narrow sense — deterministic offline replay, frozen bundles, released packages, exact thresholds and costs in appendices. Fresh reproduction requires the DeepSeek endpoints and recorded Web content, both of which drift.

## Strengths

- Converts a vague epistemic question ("did the agent really discover something?") into three preregistered, executable, statistically bounded tests with a clean separation between utility, alternative-route, and feedback evidence.
- Method-agnostic recovery rule that welcomes recombination and transfer as legitimate witnesses — the right adversarial stance for discovery claims.
- Honest calibration: the suite deliberately includes a case where the protocol outputs "recovered/refuted" (knapsack) and one where it outputs "incomplete" (affine), showing the gates are not rubber stamps.
- Engineering quality: deterministic LLM-free verifier, byte-exact Web recording, per-gate error budgets, complete ledgers including failures, PyPI-released tooling, and audits costing ~$60 each.
- The feedback estimand is carefully scoped as checkpoint-conditional total policy effect, explicitly separated from the selected target run's success and from neutral-arm recovery probability.

## Limitations

- Audited tasks are small synthetic calibrations; no real-world discovery claim (AlphaTensor/AlphaEvolve-scale) is certified, so scalability in cost and in neutral-channel design is untested.
- Recovery bounds are relative to one registered challenger distribution and model — zero recoveries does not preclude recovery by a different model, larger budget, or human expert; retrospective use reconstructing pre-publication information is discussed but not demonstrated.
- Gate 3 null calibration lives on a different task family than the target effect, and the SQLite neutral channel (uniform weights) may actively mislead rather than merely inform nothing.
- The reported decisions are locally registered kernels produced by the authors' own task-specific runners; independent provenance verification and countersigning are specified but not yet performed.
- The E0/L* provenance boundary, while principled, is enforced by self-reported information lineage; a strategic claimant has room to classify helpful material as "public background" in K or Wobs, and the countermeasure (recorded Web bytes) covers only Web-derived information.

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How do the recovery bound and the Core/Evidence decisions change when the challenger distribution QB includes a *stronger* model or a heterogeneous agent pool, rather than the same DeepSeek model at matched budget?
- What is the cost curve of Gate 2 as task complexity grows — e.g., could DCP audits be run at AlphaEvolve/FunSearch scale for a few hundred dollars, or does episode count explode with search-space size?
- Can the Gate 3 neutral channel be made provably non-misleading (content-neutral rather than schema-matched), and does the 30/30-vs-0/30 effect shrink when the SQLite neutral arm carries no weights at all instead of uniform ones?
- Would null calibration on the *same* task family (rather than E0-determined null tasks) change the equivalence-band verdicts?
- How does DCP interact with retrospective audits of published discoveries, where pre-publication information boundaries must be reconstructed — can the "temporal scope" of a recovery claim be validated rather than just declared?
- Could the recovery-witness rule be gamed by registering an artificially high target x or a loose ε, and what registered checks (beyond ε < δmin, ε < x − score(b)) best prevent threshold manipulation?
