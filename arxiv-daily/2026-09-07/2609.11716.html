LLM robustness to quantization comes from pretraining, not weight statistics — each block's added error counteracts the error it inherits.

一句话总结：预训练使每个 Transformer 块新增的量化误差倾向于与继承自上一层的误差方向相反（counteraction），叠加 LM head 高维几何对 Top 排名 token 的保护——隐藏态误差经 64 层传播后，平均仅造成 0.43 个百分点的零样本精度损失。

Why this matters

Explains mechanistically why 4-bit LLM quantization is nearly lossless — and yields design principles: protect counteraction channels, exploit LM-head geometry for rank-aware token preservation.

▲ Critical edge

The headline is measured in an easy regime: all six benchmarks are multiple-choice, single-token tasks — an 8.3–12.7% top-1 flip rate could compound badly in autoregressive generation, which goes untested.

WinoGrande alone drops 2.37 pp — 5.5× the headline average.

Methodology — distilled

A mechanistic audit of NVFP4 (4-bit, RTN, no calibration) on all attention/MLP linear weights — embeddings, norms, and LM head stay BF16.

  • Twin runs. Quantized M̂ and original M execute identical teacher-forced inputs (C4 / WikiText-103 / GSM8K; 64 seq × 512 pos each); hidden error tracked per block via Δh⁽ℓ⁾ = Δh⁽ℓ⁻¹⁾ + Δu⁽ℓ⁾.
  • Controls. 10 random inits of Qwen3-32B, plus six OLMo3-7B checkpoints (step 0 → 1M) isolate what pretraining itself contributes.
  • Exact decomposition. Thm. 1 splits relative-error growth into T_add / T_inter / T_align — algebraic identities accumulated layer-by-layer, not heuristics.
  • Causal interventions. Δu at blocks 17–48 is replaced online by an equal-norm orthogonal direction (counteraction removed) or its negation (reversed).
  • Theory. Thms. 2–3 predict LM-head projection-angle and score changes under rotation — curves are predictions at measured angles, not fits.

Credibility: Tsinghua–Bosch joint group; first author previously published NVFP4 LLM training (TetraJet-v2, ICML 2026). No code release mentioned, but quantization spec, datasets, and filtering rules are documented in full.

  • Counteraction is causal. Removing it (blocks 17–48 orthogonalized) inflates final relative hidden error 0.165 → 0.485 (2.94×) and KL 0.045 → 0.628; reversing it → 8.41× error, KL 7.856.
  • Random-init control. Identical weight reconstruction (cos ≈ 0.9955 both) yet random-init final hidden error is 2683.0 vs 484.1 absolute (5.5×), 6.7× relative.
  • LM-head geometry. Input rotates ~12.5°; vocabulary-mean projection angle shifts only ~0.159° — 78.6× attenuated (theory predicts 89.7×). Ret@10 ≈ 84–89%, Flip@1 8.3–12.7%.
  • Generalizes. Holds across 11 models (Qwen3 4B–32B incl. MoE, OLMo3, Gemma3, OLMoE, Pythia), W4 / A4 / W4A4, and RTN / GPTQ / AWQ.
Strengths
  • Clean falsification. Random-init and pretrained weights quantize identically, yet differ 5.5× in final hidden error — kills the "weights stay close" hand-wave.
  • Exact, checkable math. The three-term recurrence and length–angle decomposition are identities; theory makes a falsifiable prediction (89.7× vs measured 78.6×) that lands close.
  • Causal evidence via trajectory interventions (removal / reversal), replicated on Qwen3-8B — rare rigor for an analysis paper.
  • Exceptional breadth: 11 models, 4 families, dense + MoE, W4/A4/W4A4, RTN/GPTQ/AWQ, plus an OLMo3-7B checkpoint trajectory showing counteraction emerge during pretraining.
  • Honest limitations section (excluded positions, isotropy assumption).
Limitations
  • Single-token scope. Per-position next-token prediction only; Flip@1 (8–13%) compounding over autoregressive generation — CoT, agents — is out of scope.
  • Smoothed tail. ~0.28% catastrophic positions are excluded from aggregates — precisely where PTQ is known to fail on real inputs.
  • Theory assumptions. Thm. 2 assumes isotropic rotation direction; analytic curves fix ρ = 1, isolating direction effects only.
  • Easy regime only. NVFP4-family quantization of block linear weights in depth; INT2/INT3, KV-cache, embeddings untested; no models under 4B analyzed in depth.
  • No algorithmic payoff yet — no improved PTQ method proposed; why pretraining installs counteraction (SGD implicit bias?) remains open. No code release.
Questions for further exploration
  1. Does counteraction persist at 2–3-bit and KV-cache quantization — or break exactly where PTQ accuracy actually degrades?
  2. Can calibration objectives preserve the negative ⟨Δh⁽ℓ⁻¹⁾, Δu⁽ℓ⁾⟩ interaction — and can LM-head geometry drive rank-aware mixed precision?
  3. Does QAT amplify counteraction, and does a checkpoint's counteraction strength predict its PTQ robustness across bit-widths?
  4. How do 8–13% Flip@1 rates compound over long CoT reasoning or agentic tool-call traces?
  5. Is counteraction an artifact of SGD's implicit bias toward flat minima, or of the residual architecture — can it be induced at initialization?
  6. Do the ~0.28% excluded positions share detectable features (outlier tokens, attention sinks) that could be routed around at inference?
