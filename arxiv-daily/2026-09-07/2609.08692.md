> **一句话总结：** 本文对 Mamba 类 SSM 与 Transformer 的残差流表示做了多尺度几何分析：SSM 全局上高度各向同性（RankMe/IsoScore 高、PC1 方差占比低），而 Transformer 高度各向异性；但在压缩容量、概念编码子空间维度和局部流形结构上两者惊人一致（LAMA TREx 上跨架构 CKA 层均值 0.78 vs 通用语料 0.52），且 Jamba2 混合架构证明各向异性由注意力层直接驱动。

> **TL;DR:** A multi-scale dissection of residual-stream representations on ~500K FineWeb-Edu tokens and LAMA TREx probes shows SSMs (Mamba-1.4B/2.8B, FalconMamba-7B) are globally isotropic (high RankMe/IsoScore, near-zero PC1 dominance) while transformers (Pythia, Falcon, Llama3, Qwen2) collapse onto a dominant principal direction — yet the two families are functionally matched: per-layer autoencoder KL-degradation curves correlate at Pearson r = 0.86–0.91, rank-constrained probes on LAMA TREx decode facts with the same low ranks, and cross-architecture ZCA-CKA on LAMA TREx relations hits 0.78 layer-mean vs 0.52 on the general corpus. In the Jamba2 hybrid, every attention layer re-introduces anisotropy and subsequent Mamba layers reverse it, pinning dimensional collapse on attention. Local alignment between Pythia and Mamba is reached within 5K steps (~4% of training), far earlier than global geometry stabilizes (~25%).

## 核心贡献

- **全局几何分化（各向异性 vs 各向同性）**：系统刻画了 SSM 残差流的表示几何。Mamba/FalconMamba 在几乎所有层维持更高的有效维度（RankMe）与更均匀的方差分布（IsoScore），而 Pythia/Falcon 等 Transformer 由单个主成分（PC1）主导，呈锥形（cone-like）各向异性几何。
- **注意力是维度坍缩的驱动器**：通过混合架构 Jamba2（Mamba 层与 Transformer 层交错）发现，每个注意力层之后 RankMe 骤降、PC1 方差占比与 IsoScore 上升，表示空间向 Transformer 式几何偏移；随后的 Mamba 层又稳步逆转该趋势——为准因果证据。
- **容量与概念编码惊人一致**：尽管全局几何截然不同，两种架构在自编码器瓶颈压缩下的 KL 退化曲线高度相关（Pearson 0.86–0.91）；在 LAMA TREx 上用秩约束探针解码关系性事实，所需子空间维度（秩 1/2/5/25）与准确率都相近；Transformer 的主导方向 PC1 并不比 SSM 的分布式成分编码更多概念信息（去除/保留 top-k PCA 的退化曲线跨架构一致）。
- **局部流形高度对齐**：用 ZCA 白化后的 CKA 与 mutual-KNN（m-KNN）发现，特定概念/关系流形及 token 局部邻域跨架构高度相似，而通用语料上的全局相似度很低——"全局分化、局部收敛"。
- **训练动力学**：基于 Pythia 中间检查点，局部结构（CKA、m-KNN）在约 5K 步（约 4% 训练量）内即与 Mamba 对齐，而全局几何（IsoScore、PC1）到约 25% 训练量才稳定，且 RankMe 呈先升后降的非单调轨迹。

## Key Contributions

- **Global geometric divergence**: A systematic characterization of SSM representation geometry. Mamba/FalconMamba sustain higher effective dimensionality (RankMe) and higher IsoScore across nearly all layers, while transformer residual streams (Pythia, Falcon — trend replicated on Llama3 and Qwen2 in the appendix) are anisotropic, dominated by a single principal component (cone-like collapse).
- **Attention identified as the driver of anisotropy**: In the hybrid Jamba2, every attention block induces a drop in RankMe plus a rise in PC1 explained variance and IsoScore, skewing the space toward transformer-like geometry; subsequent Mamba layers steadily reverse the trend — quasi-causal evidence consistent with Godey et al. (2024).
- **Tightly matched capacity despite contrasting geometry**: Under rank-constrained two-layer autoencoder compression, per-layer KL-degradation curves of Mamba-1.4B and Pythia-1.4B agree closely across bottleneck ranks; rank-constrained probes on LAMA TREx show both architectures encode relational facts in subspaces of similar dimensionality, and PCA ablations (removing or retaining top-k components) show the transformer's dominant direction carries no extra conceptual information.
- **Local convergence of manifolds and neighborhoods**: ZCA-whitened CKA on individual LAMA TREx relations and mutual-KNN over 10K FineWeb-Edu tokens (k=10) both reveal high cross-architecture alignment at matched relative depths, in stark contrast to low similarity on the general corpus.
- **Temporal dynamics**: Using Pythia checkpoints, cross-architecture local alignment (to final Mamba) is already in place after ~5K steps (~4% of training), whereas global geometry metrics stabilize only around 25% of training, with RankMe following a non-monotonic rise-then-fall trajectory.

## Methodology

All analysis is performed on the **residual stream** (hidden state x_l updated as x_{l+1} = x_l + Block(x_l)), providing a shared representation framework across architectures; the internal sequence-level state inside Mamba blocks is deliberately not analyzed. Models: transformers Pythia-1.4B/2.8B, Falcon-7B (plus Llama3.2-3B, Llama3.1-8B, Qwen2.5-3B in appendix), SSMs Mamba-1.4B/2.8B and FalconMamba-7B, and the hybrid Jamba2-3B; shared tokenizers across each suite enable one-to-one comparisons.

- **Global geometry** (Sec. 3): RankMe (Shannon entropy of normalized singular values → effective rank), IsoScore (distance of data covariance from identity → isotropy), and PC1 explained variance, computed on ≈500K representations from 1,500 FineWeb-Edu samples (max length 512).
- **Capacity via compressibility** (Sec. 4.1): per-layer two-layer autoencoders with bottleneck ranks r ∈ {64,128,256,512}, L2 reconstruction loss, one epoch on ≈4M FineWeb-Edu tokens (lr 1e-4); evaluation replaces a single token's layer representation with its AE reconstruction and measures KL divergence between the model's original and perturbed output distributions.
- **Concept subspaces** (Sec. 4.2): rank-constrained linear probes W = AB (ranks 1,2,5,25) on LAMA TREx (41 Wikidata relation types; hidden states extracted at the final object token; ≤10K samples per relation; 80/20 split), plus spectral ablations that remove or retain top-k PCA directions (k = 1,5,25,50).
- **Structural alignment** (Sec. 5): ZCA-whitened linear CKA — whitening covariance Σ estimated globally from 10K matched FineWeb-Edu tokens per model/layer to avoid the anisotropy artifact of plain CKA — applied to relation-specific LAMA manifolds; and mutual k-NN overlap (cosine neighborhoods, 10K tokens, k=10) exploiting the shared tokenizer to compare identical tokens across models.
- **Training dynamics** (Sec. 6): the same metrics tracked across Pythia-1.4B checkpoints (step 1K → 108K → pretrained); other model families lack public checkpoints.
- **Robustness**: 5-seed variance analysis of all metrics (mean CV% 0.11–0.52%), whitening-strength sweep (Σ^{-α/2}), sampling-stability of m-KNN over five 2K-token batches, and replication on the Falcon/FalconMamba-7B pair. Code is released at github.com/schwartz-lab-NLP/ssm-vs-transformer-geometry.

## Results & Highlights

- **Global divergence in numbers** (Fig. 2/7, ≈500K FineWeb-Edu representations): SSMs sustain higher RankMe than same-size transformers across all but the final layers (e.g., RankMe curves for Mamba-1.4B reaching ~2,500–3,000 vs ~1,000–2,000 for Pythia-1.4B), transformers show near-zero IsoScore with PC1 explained variance in the 0.2–0.8 range while SSMs' PC1 stays near 0; metric stability across 5 seeds is excellent (mean CV%: RankMe 0.13%/0.11%, IsoScore 0.52%/0.50%, PC1 0.18%/0.36% for Pythia-1.4B/Mamba-1.4B; max layer-wise CV% ≤ ~1%).
- **Matched capacity** (Fig. 3a, Table 3): per-layer AE KL-degradation curves of Mamba-1.4B vs Pythia-1.4B correlate at Pearson r = 0.857±0.027 (bottleneck r=64) rising to 0.911±0.012 (r=512); Lin's CCC rises from 0.638±0.041 to 0.830±0.020 — agreement strengthens as the bottleneck relaxes. The one exception is the final SSM layer, whose KL divergence is disproportionately higher than the corresponding transformer layer.
- **Concepts live in equally small subspaces** (Fig. 3b): identical rank constraints (1/2/5/25) yield closely matched probe accuracies on LAMA TREx for both architectures at every depth; and removing or retaining top-k PCA components (Fig. 3c/d) degrades probes at similar rates — the transformer's dominant PC1 does not concentrate factual knowledge.
- **Local convergence of manifolds** (Fig. 4, App. E): cross-architecture ZCA-CKA mean-max values on LAMA TREx relations land in 0.80–0.91 (e.g., 0.86 for the Pythia-1.4B vs Mamba-1.4B pair; Pythia self-similarity 0.98), versus 0.35–0.48 for the same pairs on general FineWeb-Edu; even with no whitening (α=0), layer-mean CKA is 0.78 on LAMA TREx vs 0.52 on FineWeb-Edu, so the local/global gap is not a whitening artifact. CKA estimates are stable (CV% mean 0.15%, max 1.34% on FineWeb-Edu).
- **Token neighborhoods align** (Fig. 5/12): m-KNN@10 overlap between fully trained transformers and SSMs is remarkably high at corresponding depths (values approaching the models' self-similarity), replicated on Falcon-7B vs FalconMamba-7B; sampling stability over five 2K batches: mean SEM 0.0047 (range 0.0003–0.0113), mean SD 0.0104.
- **Early emergence of local alignment** (Sec. 6): Pythia-1.4B's CKA and m-KNN similarity to final Mamba match Pythia's own final-state self-similarity after just 5,000 steps (~4% of training), while IsoScore/PC1 stabilize only at ~25% of training and RankMe evolves non-monotonically.
- **Hybrid oscillation** (Fig. 2): in Jamba2-3B, each attention block consistently increases PC1 explained variance and reduces RankMe; subsequent Mamba layers steadily restore isotropy — attention is the primary driver of dimensional collapse.

The empirical practice is solid for an analysis paper: multiple complementary metrics, seed-level variance tables, a whitening-strength sweep to rule out methodological artifacts, replication across a second model family (Falcon/FalconMamba), and additional architectures (Llama3, Qwen2) in the appendix.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: Good, not revolutionary. Anisotropy of transformer representations and attention's role (Godey et al. 2024; Timkey & van Schijndel 2021) and cross-architecture convergence (Platonic Representation Hypothesis, Wang et al. 2025 SAE study) are established; the contribution is the systematic SSM-vs-transformer contrast at matched scale/tokenizer, plus the clean Jamba2 layer-alternation evidence and the "local converges early, global converges late" training-dynamics split, which are genuinely new angles.
- **Rigor**: Above average for this genre — CV% tables across 5 seeds, α-sweep for ZCA whitening, exclusion of the anomalous final SSM layer disclosed in Table 3's caption, and replication on a second architecture pair. However, the capacity claim rests on a single 1-epoch, 2-layer autoencoder as the compressor; "tightly matched effective capacity" really means "similar degradation under this specific weak compressor," and a stronger compressor might differentiate the architectures.
- **Reproducibility**: High. Code released, hyperparameters fully listed in the appendix (sample counts, learning rates, epochs, k for m-KNN), and all datasets (FineWeb-Edu, LAMA TREx) and checkpoints (Pythia) are public. The only non-replicable-in-principle piece is the temporal analysis, which depends on Pythia being the only family with intermediate checkpoints.
- **Significance**: Moderate-to-good. The Jamba2 oscillation result is actionable for hybrid architecture design (attention layers inject anisotropy that Mamba layers must undo), and the parity in concept-subspace rank informs transfer of interpretability techniques (probing, low-rank concept edits) from transformers to SSMs.
- **Red flags / caveats**: (1) Cross-family pairs are not training-data-matched in general — Falcon-7B and FalconMamba-7B were trained on different corpora/schedules, so part of the global-geometry gap could be data- rather than architecture-driven (the Pythia-vs-Mamba 1.4B pair, both Pile-trained with a shared tokenizer, is the strongest control, and the paper leans on it implicitly rather than discussing the confound). (2) Probing is correlational — the authors themselves cite Elazar et al. (2021) and Ravichander et al. (2021); equal probe rank does not prove the models *use* these subspaces in generation. (3) m-KNN on a shared corpus could partly reflect surface/token-frequency statistics rather than deep semantic organization; k=10 is fixed with no sensitivity analysis over k. (4) The training-dynamics claim ("local alignment at 4%") measures early Pythia checkpoints against the *final* Mamba model only — the symmetric comparison is impossible with public checkpoints, which the paper acknowledges but which weakens the "convergence" narrative.

## Strengths

- Genuinely multi-scale: global variance structure → compressibility → concept subspaces → local manifolds → token neighborhoods → training dynamics, all on the same controlled model suite.
- The hybrid Jamba2 analysis is the paper's sharpest tool: layer-alternating architecture acts as a natural ablation isolating attention as the anisotropy driver.
- Methodological hygiene uncommon in representation-analysis papers: ZCA whitening to de-confound CKA from spectral mismatch, plus an α-sweep verifying the local/global CKA gap survives at α=0 (0.78 vs 0.52).
- Surprising, memorable central finding — stark global geometric divergence coexisting with matched functional capacity and near-identical local manifolds — with immediate implications for porting interpretability methods across architectures.

## Limitations

- Analysis is confined to the residual stream; the recurrent state inside Mamba blocks — arguably where SSMs most differ from attention — is explicitly out of scope.
- No connection to downstream behavior: nothing here links isotropy vs anisotropy, or manifold alignment, to differences in generation quality, long-context ability, or task performance.
- Probing-based conclusions are correlational (acknowledged); no amnesic/activation-patchstyle causal interventions are run.
- Temporal analysis is one-sided (Pythia checkpoints only); whether local alignment emerges equally early on the SSM side is unknown.
- Model scale is modest (1.4B–8B); whether the isotropy gap persists at frontier scale, or in modern hybrids like Zamba/other attention-lite designs, is untested.
- The AE capacity experiment excludes the final SSM layer from correlation statistics due to its anomalous sensitivity — a real quirk that hints at architecture-specific differences the aggregate "parity" narrative smooths over.

## Relevance

对关心"不同架构是否学到相同表示"这一可解释性核心问题的研究非常有价值：它给出了 SSM 与 Transformer 在表示几何上"全局分化、局部收敛"的系统性证据，且 Jamba2 的注意力-各向异性因果链与低秩概念子空间结论，可直接指导把探针、概念编辑等可解释性技术从 Transformer 迁移到 Mamba 类模型。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would activation patching or amnesic-probing interventions confirm that the matched low-rank concept subspaces are causally used during generation in both architectures?
- Does the anomalous sensitivity of the final SSM layer under compression reflect a functionally distinct "readout" stage, and does it persist at larger Mamba scales?
- If attention provably injects anisotropy, does explicitly de-skewing attention outputs (e.g., whitening/spectral regularization) in hybrids like Jamba2 improve or degrade downstream performance?
- Do the local-alignment findings survive on non-factual, compositional concepts (syntax, coreference, reasoning traces) beyond Wikidata-style LAMA TREx relations, where SSMs' bounded state might matter more?
- Since local alignment appears within ~4% of Pythia's training, is it a trivial consequence of early-shared statistics (unigram/bigram co-occurrence geometry) rather than of semantic convergence — and could a frequency-matched control disentangle the two?
- How does post-training (SFT/RLHF) reshape the isotropy gap and the local-manifold convergence documented here?
