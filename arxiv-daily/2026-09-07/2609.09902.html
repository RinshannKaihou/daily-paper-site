Contrastive Projection: Reading Transformer Internals by Differencing Logit Lenses

Olli Tuomi · Evident Solutions Oy|2026-09-07|single-author preprint

mechanistic-interpretability logit-lens contrastive-explanation representation-reading hallucination-analysis
◆ Key metric — cross-seed control
0.08

Mean top-10 token Jaccard across five Pythia-410M seeds trained on identical data — yet the semantic distinction survives in 20/20 seed×contrast cases (margins +2.8 … +5.9). Same computation, different tokens.

◆ Why this matters

Unifies logit lens, RepE/ActAdd differencing, and probes into one training-free framework — and warns token-reading interpretability: validate axes, not token identities.

对研究 Transformer 内部机制的人高度相关：跨种子结论（语义区分不变、词元基底网络特定）是对一切「读词元」式可解释性的方法论警示；幻觉部分给出熵之外的实时候选信号。

◆ Critical edge

Cases were selected from several hundred readings — no aggregate success-rate statistics exist (triangulation tested on just 6 cases), so the method’s hit rate on naturally occurring pairs is unknown.

top-K ↑ / ↓ tokensthe two poles of the contrast

Run both prompts, align a read position, and project the residual difference through the unembedding. Shared context cancels; the top-K positive and negative tokens name the difference — at every layer, and via forward hooks, per MLP / attention sub-layer and per attention head. Two choices matter: project through W_U, not the input embedding (the residual stream aligns with output space; W_E returns noise), and bypass the final LayerNorm (its learned shift cancels in a matched difference; top-5 tokens survive at cosine ≈ 0.95).

Three contrast-design rules keep readouts legible, each justified by a controlled failure: (1) a shared preamble — the first token’s massive activation (norm 941 vs ~45, ~20×) swamps any difference placed on it; (2) a matched current token — a one-word synonym mismatch inflates the difference norm ~10× at L1 and its suffix morphology dominates; (3) a matched predicted next token — separating a stored relation from the answer it produces. Multi-contrast triangulation averages baselines that share the intended axis, cancelling incidental axes (√N suppression against a visibility threshold f*). Causality is layered on top: activation patching, concept-axis injection, leave-one-out generalization, and linear probes arbitrate “computation absent” vs “present but unemitted.”

Results & Highlights

结果

The “hot dog” compound-noun circuit (Phi-2) — readout, then causal patching

Circuit siteContrastive readoutCausal check (activation patching)
L0 attention copies “hot” onto “dog” patching “hot” never matters — P(food) 0.13–0.16 at every layer tested
L4 MLP writes the food content; contrastive norm 20.0 vs 3.8 for attention; “fried” surfaces in MLP output only patch “dog”@L2 → P(food) 0.16 → 0.00 (continuation flips to “panting heavily”)
L5 head 19 routes noun → prediction; attention to “dog” 0.911 (hot) vs 0.735 (cold) patch “was”@L20 → P(food) = 0.00 (“shivering in the cold weather”)

  • Concept-plane injection. hot−cold decomposes into edibility +21, petness −20, temperature +15 at L20 — but only E×P is causal: E alone reaches food fraction 0.03, P alone 0.01, E+P recovers 0.98 of the full 1.00 effect; temperature is inert. Top-10 MLP neurons carry only ~10% of the food direction — the write is distributed.
  • Hallucination beyond entropy. Real-vs-fictional contrast norms differ at L28 (0.98 vs 0.70), but entropy fails on the hard case: real Mount Cook H=2.14 vs fictional Mount Silverhorn H=2.36. The projection separates them — Cook reads “volcano, climbers, peaks”; Silverhorn surfaces only generic priors “1100, 1200, 1300”. A length-matched control rules out tokenization artifacts.
  • Visibility bar in action. For Phi-2, f* = 0.89%: “delicious” clears it by L24 (f=1.9%, 2.1× the bar) while mid-layer fragments sit below it — and “fried”, top of the raw ranking at L5, holds only f=0.6%. A top rank is not a cleared bar.
  • Metaphor as domain routing, not one axis. Directions are word-specific: cold & heavy → emotion (cos 0.58); bright → intelligence (orthogonal, 0.03–0.14). Cross-domain injection at 3× imposes each direction’s own target domain — refuting a single “figurativity” feature.
03

Strengths / Limitations

优势与局限
Strengths
  • Zero-parameter readout. Needs only a prompt pair — no probe training, no dictionaries, no SAEs — and directly flags layers, sub-layers, and heads for follow-up causal work.
  • Contrast-design rules are concrete and reusable, each grounded in a controlled failure mode (first-token ~20× activation; token mismatch inflating Δ ~10×).
  • Triangulation is well-motivated with a quantitative account: √N suppression of incoherent remainder against the visibility threshold.
  • The five-seed control is a conceptual contribution in itself — it separates the invariant distinction (20/20) from the network-specific token face (0.08).
  • Unusual intellectual honesty (primitive conceded as not new; patching labeled qualitative) plus public code, disclosed AI-assistant use, and a length-matched control.
Limitations
  • All contrasts are hand-constructed; no results on sampled or naturally occurring pairs, and no aggregate success-rate statistics (triangulation tested on 6 cases only).
  • Coherence is judged by inspection; f* is applied by hand to a single example and derives from a non-peer-reviewed companion preprint by the same author.
  • Scale ceiling. Mechanistic depth at 2.7B, replications at 1.4B/1.5B/410M — nothing at 7B+ or frontier scale, where mid-layer readability may differ.
  • W_U readout sees only emitted structure — computed-but-never-emitted distinctions are invisible by construction; the surviving component may be a bundle, not a feature.
  • Causal scope is narrow: one circuit and one injection study; patching doesn’t control for self-repair/hydra effects, and injection doesn’t rule out subspace illusions.

Questions for Further Exploration

开放问题
  1. Q1What fraction of naturally sampled (not hand-designed) contrast pairs read legibly under f* — and can the proposed automatic gate report it without human judgment?
  2. Q2Does the resolve-at-the-noun / route-to-the-prediction shape and its layer timing (L5 / L14 / L12) scale systematically with model size, or hold only below 3B?
  3. Q3Can computed-but-never-emitted distinctions — invisible to any W_U readout — be detected or bounded by a complementary method, and how common are they?
  4. Q4Does the linear metaphor offset survive its own proposed compositional test — injecting a target-domain direction built from unrelated cues into new source words?
  5. Q5Could the fictional-entity signature (name fragments only, no facts at any layer) become a runtime hallucination detector — and how does it behave on plausible-but-wrong claims about real entities?
  6. Q6Read across Pythia training checkpoints: does a distinction acquire a token face before or after it becomes behaviorally effective?
