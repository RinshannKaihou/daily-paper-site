> **一句话总结：** 提出对比投影（contrastive projection）这一免训练的可解释性示踪方法：将两个最小差异提示词的隐状态作差后过 unembedding 矩阵，即可在任意位置/子层/注意力头上读出二者在词元空间中的语义差异；它在 Phi-2 中完整追踪并经激活补丁验证了 "hot dog" 复合名词的 MLP(L4)→注意力头(L5.H19) 链条，并给出跨种子边界结论——同一语义区分在 5 个仅初始化不同的 Pythia-410M 中均成立（20/20 概念锚点边际为正，+2.8~+5.9），但具体词元几乎完全不同（top-10 Jaccard 仅 0.08）。

> **TL;DR:** A training-free interpretability tracer that differences the hidden states of two matched prompts and projects the residual through the unembedding (equivalent to reading a RepE/ActAdd steering vector through a logit lens), turning the noisy single-input logit lens into a legible per-position, per-sublayer, per-head readout. On Phi-2 it traces the "hot dog" compound-noun circuit (MLP writes food at L4 with contrastive norm 20.0 vs 3.8 for attention; head L5.H19 routes it with attention weight 0.911 vs 0.735) and confirms it by activation patching (patching "dog" at L2 collapses P(food) from 0.16 to 0.00); across Phi-2/Pythia-1.4B/Qwen2.5-1.5B the same distinction is recovered by readout and a 1.00-accuracy linear probe even where single-pair readout fails (best food rank 0 vs 17 vs 60 at the noun). A five-seed Pythia-410M control shows the token face of a computation is network-specific (top-10 Jaccard 0.08) while the distinction itself is invariant (concept-anchor margins positive in 20/20 seed×contrast cases). Applications include reading hallucination (fictional entities surface only name fragments, not facts) and metaphor (domain-to-domain mappings, not one figurativity feature).

## 核心贡献

- **对比投影原语系统化为示踪器**：对两个紧密匹配的提示词的隐状态作差（Δh = h_c − h_k）再投影到 unembedding 矩阵 W_U，抵消共享成分、读出差异内容。该操作本身等价于把 RepE/ActAdd 引导向量过 logit lens（Du et al. 2026 已用过一次），本文的贡献是把它做成免训练、可在每个位置、每个子层（MLP/注意力）、每个注意力头上系统读取的完整示踪工具。
- **对比设计的可操作规则**：共享前导语（首 token 残差范数约为其他位置的 20 倍，941 vs ~45，差异会被放大）、匹配读取位置的当前词元（否则差异范数在 L1 放大 ~10 倍且被词形后缀淹没）、匹配下一预测词元（以分离"关系"与"答案"两种读出）。
- **多对比三角化（multi-contrast triangulation）**：用多个共享目标轴、 incidental 轴不同的基线求平均，让无关轴相消、目标轴保留，将低于可见阈值 f* 的信号抬升到可读区间（Pythia 名词位食物读出从单对 rank 17 提升到 rank 3）。
- **复合名词电路的完整机制追踪（Phi-2）**：L0 注意力把 "hot" 复制到 "dog" → L4 MLP 识别复合词（"fried" 首先出现在 MLP 输出）→ L5 头 19 将信息从 "dog" 路由到 "was"（注意力权重 0.911 vs 0.735）；激活补丁在三位置多层验证了因果性。
- **概念平面分解**：hot−cold 差异可分解为可食性(E)、宠物性(P)、温度(T)三轴（L20 投影 +21/−20/+15），但只有 E×P 平面是因果的：单独注入 E 只到食物分数 0.03，E+P 恢复 0.98（全量差异为 1.00），温度轴完全惰性。
- **跨架构与跨种子边界刻画**：同一区分在三种架构（Phi-2/Pythia-1.4B/Qwen2.5-1.5B）均由读出+探针恢复；五个仅初始化不同的 Pythia-410M 种子中，词元层面几乎不重叠（Jaccard 0.08）但概念锚点边际全部为正（20/20）——"计算在词元空间的样子是网络特定的，它所作的区分不是"。
- **幻觉与隐喻的应用读出**：虚构实体的检索只上浮名字片段而无非事实内容（且熵无法区分置信回忆与置信幻觉：Mount Cook 2.14 vs 虚构 Mount Silverhorn 2.36，投影仍可分开）；隐喻被读为一组源域→目标域的映射方向而非单一"比喻性"特征。

## Key Contributions

- **Systematizes the differencing primitive into a tracer.** Subtracting two matched prompts' hidden states and projecting through W_U (the arithmetic of a RepE/ActAdd steering vector read through a logit lens; Du et al. 2026 used the exact operation once) is built into a training-free instrument that reads at every position, sub-layer, and attention head, with zero fitted parameters — the only choices are the input pair, read position, and K.
- **Contrast-design rules.** Three verifiable rules for legible contrasts: (1) a shared preamble several tokens in, because the first token carries a massive activation (residual norm ~941 vs ~45 at L8 in Phi-2, ~20×) that amplifies any difference placed on it; (2) a matched current token at the read position (a synonym mismatch inflates the difference norm ~10× at L1, 30 vs 3–5, and its suffix morphology dominates the readout); (3) a matched predicted next token, which separates reading a stored relation (at "Japan" in "The capital of Japan", place vocabulary) from reading the answer it produces (at the "is", capital-city names).
- **Multi-contrast triangulation.** Averaging difference vectors over several baselines that share the intended axis cancels the incidental axes; grounded in a companion visibility threshold f* = 2 ln(2V)/(d + 2 ln 2V), averaging N baselines suppresses the incoherent remainder by √N and lifts near-threshold signals above the readout's detection bar.
- **A fully traced compound-noun circuit in Phi-2, confirmed causally.** L0 attention copies "hot" to "dog"; the L4 MLP recognizes the compound (contrastive norm 20.0 vs 3.8 for attention; "fried" appears in MLP output only); L5 head 19 routes it to "was" (attention to "dog" 0.911 hot vs 0.735 cold). Activation patching at three positions and multiple layers confirms the two-hop chain.
- **Concept-plane decomposition with injection tests.** The hot−cold difference spans edibility, petness, and temperature (L20 projections +21/−20/+15), but only the edibility×petness plane is causal: neither axis alone flips the prediction (food fraction 0.03 and 0.01), together they recover 0.98 of the full 1.00 effect; temperature is inert.
- **Cross-model and cross-seed scoping.** The resolve-at-the-noun / route-to-the-prediction shape holds across dense MHA + GELU + parallel residual (Phi-2), GPT-NeoX-style (Pythia-1.4B), and GQA + SwiGLU + sequential residual (Qwen2.5-1.5B); a five-seed Pythia-410M control shows the token basis is network-specific (top-10 Jaccard 0.08) while the distinction is shared (20/20 positive anchor margins) — so one should validate an axis, not a token identity.
- **Applied readings.** Real entities surface factual associations through the contrast (Tesla → alternating current; Everest → "Nepal, Tibet, Himalaya") while fictional ones surface only name fragments and generic priors ("1100, 1200, 1300"), even where entropy cannot separate confident recall from confident hallucination; metaphor reads as word-specific source→target domain directions (cold/heavy both → emotion, cosine 0.58; bright → intelligence, orthogonal 0.03–0.14), refuting a single figurativity axis via cross-domain injection.

## Methodology

The core operation is minimal: run both prompts, extract hidden states at every layer at the aligned read position, compute Δh[L] = h_c[L] − h_k[L], project Δlogits[L] = Δh[L] · W_U^⊤, and read the top-K positive and negative tokens as the two poles of the contrast. Two practical choices matter: project through the unembedding W_U (not the input embedding W_E, which returns noise since the residual stream aligns with output space), and bypass the final LayerNorm (its learned shift cancels in a matched difference; the per-dimension rescaling preserves top-5 tokens at mean cosine ≈ 0.95).

On top of this primitive the paper builds a systematic tracer: (1) per-position reading traces where a distinction first appears and how it flows between positions; (2) attention-vs-MLP decomposition via forward hooks projects each sub-layer's write separately through W_U; (3) per-head decomposition identifies which attention head carries a distinction. Three contrast-design rules (shared preamble, matched current token, matched predicted next token) make readouts legible, each justified by a controlled failure case (e.g., a one-token capitalization mismatch drops the food rank from 3 to 12 and the direction alignment cosine from 0.85 to 0.64). Two extensions broaden coverage: baseline subtraction (single-prompt variant: subtract the mean residual of 100 same-length English prose snippets, leaving target-specific content, clean mainly at late layers) and multi-contrast triangulation (average the target's difference over several baselines sharing the intended axis so incidental axes cancel). A quantitative visibility bar from a companion paper (f* = 2 ln(2V)/(d + 2 ln 2V); 0.89% for Phi-2) distinguishes real signal from isotropic token soup — a token surfaces only if its energy fraction f = cos²(x, u_t) exceeds f*.

Validation is layered: activation patching (replacing one position/layer's residual with the other run's value, then greedy-generating) establishes causality for the compound-noun circuit; concept-axis injection (moving one state toward the other along a data-derived axis) tests which axes are causal; leave-one-out injection tests whether metaphor routing directions generalize beyond their extraction prompts; a length-matched control rules out tokenization artifacts in the entity study; and trained linear probes arbitrate between "computation absent" and "computation present but unemitted." Models: Phi-2 (2.7B) for mechanistic depth, Pythia-1.4B and Qwen2.5-1.5B for architecture generality, five Pythia-410M seed reruns for the invariance control.

## Results & Highlights

- **Compound-noun circuit (Phi-2), readout vs patching.** The L4 MLP, not attention, writes the food content at the noun (contrastive norm 20.0 vs 3.8); routing head L5.H19 attends to "dog" with weight 0.911 in the hot-dog context vs 0.735 in cold-dog. Activation patching confirms causality: patching "dog" at L2 collapses P(food) from the 0.16 baseline to 0.00 (continuation flips to "panting heavily"), patching "was" at L20 also gives P(food) = 0.00 ("shivering in the cold weather"), while patching "hot" never matters (P(food) 0.13–0.16 at all layers tested) because L0 attention already copied it.
- **Concept-plane injection.** Neither axis alone flips the food/animal prediction — edibility injection reaches food fraction 0.03 at L20, petness 0.01, temperature 0.00 — but E+P together recover 0.98 vs 1.00 for the full difference vector (and 0.99 with temperature added, showing T is inert). The L20 projections of the raw difference onto the three axes are +21 (E), −20 (P), +15 (T); the axes are correlated at cosine 0.37. The top-10 MLP neurons carry only ~10% of the food direction — the write is distributed.
- **Cross-model generality (Phi-2 / Pythia-1.4B / Qwen2.5-1.5B).** Food reaches the prediction site through later attention at L5, L14, and L12 respectively. At the noun, a single pair reads food only in Phi-2 (best food rank 0) vs rank 17 (Pythia) and rank 60 (Qwen); 5-baseline triangulation recovers Pythia to rank 3 (≈1.3× the visibility bar f*) and lifts Qwen to rank 20 (still below f*). A linear probe (5-fold accuracy 1.00 in each model) classifies "dog" in "hot dog" as food with P = 1.00/0.99/1.00 and in "cold dog" as animal with P = 0.00/0.01/0.00 — the computation is present in all three even where the token face is not.
- **Cross-seed control (five Pythia-410M seeds).** Mean pairwise top-10 token Jaccard is 0.08 (range 0.005–0.11; ~45 distinct tokens fill the 50 top-ranked slots), yet the contrastive direction boosts concept anchors over their opposites in 20/20 seed×contrast cases with logit margins +2.8 to +5.9 — the distinction is shared, the token basis is not.
- **Hallucination readings (Phi-2).** Real-vs-fictional pairs have larger relative contrast norm at L28 (mean 0.98) than real-vs-real pairs (mean 0.70); real inventors predict at entropy H = 1.5–2.5 vs H = 3.4–6.3 for fictional ones. But entropy fails on the mountain case: real Mount Cook (H = 2.14) and fictional Mount Silverhorn (H = 2.36) are nearly indistinguishable, while the projection separates them — Cook's pole reads "volcano, climbers, Peaks" and Everest's reads "Nepal, Tibet, Himalaya" vs Silverhorn's generic number priors "1100, 1200, 1300". A length-matched control (invented name of identical token count) confirms the finding is not a tokenization artifact.
- **Metaphor as domain routing.** Routing directions are stable across sentences (pairwise cosine 0.64–0.88 per word); directions sharing a target domain correlate (cold–heavy cosine 0.58, both → emotion) while bright (→ intelligence) is orthogonal to all (0.03–0.14). Injection is graded and bidirectional: the cold direction flips the top-1 token at 0.65× its natural magnitude; at 2× only sharp and bright reverse to their literal domains while heavy resists; cross-domain injection at 3× shows each direction imposes its own target domain (cold injected into a sharp tone or heavy mood drives continuation to "below zero"), refuting a single literal/figurative axis.
- **Visibility bar in action.** For Phi-2, f* = 0.89%: "delicious" clears it at the prediction site by L24 (f = 1.9%, 2.1× the bar) while mid-layer fragments sit below it (f < 0.7%, L10–16), and "fried" at the noun tops the raw ranking at L5 with only f = 0.6% — a top rank in the logit lens is not the same as clearing the bar.

## Authors & Credibility

Single-author paper by Olli Tuomi of Evident Solutions Oy (a Finnish company), an independent researcher in the interpretability space; the paper cites a companion preprint of theirs (Tuomi 2026, "A visibility threshold for top-k logit-lens readouts," Zenodo) that supplies the theoretical detection bar. The scholarship is careful: it explicitly credits Du et al. (2026) with the exact differencing-through-logit-lens primitive and positions its own contribution as the systematic tracer built on it, and it omits steering experiments because RepE/ActAdd/CAA already establish them. Code and data are public (github.com/EvidentSolutions/llm-interp, archived on Zenodo), and the use of AI assistants (Claude, Gemini) for coding, experiments, and drafting is disclosed with author responsibility claimed — an unusually transparent disclosure. The empirical work is entirely on small open models (Phi-2 2.7B, Pythia-1.4B/410M, Qwen2.5-1.5B), which is appropriate for a methods/interpretability paper but limits scale claims. No venue or peer review status is indicated (arXiv preprint, September 2026).

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty is incremental and honestly labeled.** The core operation is not new — the paper itself concedes it is RepE/ActAdd steering-vector arithmetic read through a logit lens and that Du et al. (2026) already decoded an activation difference this way; the contribution is packaging (per-position/per-head tracer, contrast-design rules, triangulation). This framing is commendable but means the paper stands or falls on the demonstrations, not the primitive.
- **Selection effects are acknowledged but real.** "The cases shown are selected from several hundred readings. We report the most illustrative" — an explicit admission of cherry-picking at the case level. There is no aggregate statistic over all readings (e.g., what fraction of well-designed contrasts read legibly), and triangulation was tested on only 6 cases, so the method's success rate on adversarially chosen or naturally occurring pairs is unknown.
- **Coherence is judged by inspection.** Whether a readout is signal or "token soup" is a qualitative judgment except where the visibility bar is applied — and the paper applies f* by hand to exactly one example (§3.1), while the bar itself comes from a non-peer-reviewed companion preprint by the same author. The promised "automatic visibility gate" is future work.
- **Causal claims are narrower than the narrative suggests.** Patching is reported as "a qualitative trace, not a quantitative causal measurement": single-layer patches do not control for self-repair/hydra effects (McGrath et al. 2023), and the concept-axis injection does not rule out the subspace-illusion failure mode (Makelov et al. 2024) — both cited by the author themselves. The strongest causal claim (the L4-MLP → L5.H19 chain) rests on one prompt pair in one 2.7B model.
- **Controls are better than typical for the genre.** The length-matched control for the hallucination study, the leave-one-out test for metaphor directions, the cross-domain injection, and especially the five-seed control (which undermines naive token-level reading and yields the paper's most quotable number, Jaccard 0.08) are genuinely rigorous touches. The seed result also cuts against superficial uses of the paper's own tables: e.g., "delicious" in Phi-2 vs "burgers" in Pythia is exactly the network-specific token face the paper warns not to over-read.
- **Some quantitative claims are hedged in fine print.** The real-vs-fictional contrast-norm gap (0.98 vs 0.70) is admitted to be the claim most exposed to the token-length confound and is read "as suggestive rather than exact"; the LayerNorm bypass makes raw contrastive norms non-comparable across layers. The metaphor-as-linear-mapping hypothesis (§7.1) is explicitly flagged as beyond what four words in one model can establish.

## Strengths

- Training-free, zero-parameter readout that needs only a prompt pair — no probe training, no dictionaries, no SAE training — and directly flags layers, sub-layers, and heads for follow-up causal analysis.
- Contrast-design rules are concrete, justified by controlled failure modes (first-token massive activation ~20×; current-token mismatch inflating Δ norm ~10× at L1), and immediately reusable by other practitioners.
- Multi-contrast triangulation is a simple, well-motivated answer to the biggest practical weakness of single-pair differencing (incidental axes), with a quantitative account of why averaging helps (√N suppression of the incoherent remainder against a visibility threshold).
- The cross-seed control is a conceptual contribution in itself: it cleanly separates "the distinction a network computes" (invariant, 20/20 positive margins) from "the tokens it wears" (Jaccard 0.08), and yields the practical rule to validate axes, not token identities.
- Unusual intellectual honesty: the paper states its primitive is not new, omits redundant steering results, labels its patching qualitative, and scopes every claim (e.g., metaphor findings "scoped to these four words").
- Public code and data, disclosed AI-assistant use, and a length-matched control for the entity study.

## Limitations

- All demonstrations use hand-constructed minimal pairs with hand-selected baselines; no results on sampled or naturally occurring contrast sets, and no aggregate success-rate statistics (triangulation tested on only 6 cases).
- Coherence of readouts is judged by inspection; the visibility threshold f* is applied manually to a single example and derives from a companion preprint, not an automated gate.
- Scale: mechanistic depth is on Phi-2 (2.7B) with replications on 1.4B/1.5B/410M models — nothing on 7B+ or frontier-scale models, where intermediate-layer readability may differ.
- The readout only sees what projects onto W_U: computed-but-never-emitted distinctions are invisible by construction, and the paper acknowledges this as an open ceiling ("contrastive projection reads emitted structure, not the full internal state").
- Causal evidence is limited to one circuit (compound noun) and one injection study; patching does not control for self-repair, and the injection does not rule out subspace illusions; no circuit topology or faithfulness claims are made (unlike SAE/transcoder approaches, no decomposition into features is offered — the surviving component may be a bundle).
- Tokenization alignment constrains per-position reading (the read position must play the same structural role in both inputs), and the metaphor hypothesis rests on four adjectives in one model, with "heavy" already resisting the linear-offset account at 2× injection.

## Relevance

对研究 Transformer 内部机制与可解释性的人高度相关：它把 logit lens、RepE/ActAdd 差值与探针三类工具统一成一个免训练的对比读出框架，并给出可直接复用的对比设计与三角化技巧；其跨种子结论（语义区分不变而词元基底网络特定）对任何基于"读词元"的可解释性工作都是重要的方法论警示。幻觉部分也提供了一个熵之外的实时候选信号（虚构实体只上浮名字片段）。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- What fraction of naturally sampled (not hand-designed) contrast pairs produce legible readouts under the visibility bar f*, and can the proposed automatic gate be wired in to report this without human judgment?
- Does the resolve-at-the-noun / route-to-the-prediction shape and its layer timing (L5/L14/L12) scale systematically with model size, or hold only for sub-3B models?
- Can the computed-but-never-emitted distinctions (invisible to any W_U readout) be detected or bounded by any complementary method, and how common are they?
- Does the linear source→target metaphor offset (h_metaphorical ≈ h_literal + v(source→target)) survive the compositional test the authors propose — injecting a target-domain direction built from unrelated cues into new source words?
- Could the fictional-entity signature (name fragments only, no factual content at any layer) be turned into a practical runtime hallucination detector, and how does it behave for plausible-but-wrong claims about real entities (the harder hallucination case)?
- Reading the same contrast across Pythia training checkpoints ("developmental clock") — does a distinction become behaviorally effective before or after it acquires a token face?
