> **一句话总结：** 提出 SPARC 人机共享显微镜框架（编码智能体 + 人类操作员 + FINDINGS.md/PITFALLS.md 两个持久记忆文件），在 (111) 取向 PbZr0.2Ti0.8O3 铁电薄膜上发现了可重写的面内超畴方向控制原语——交替极性脉冲晶格使命令方向功率提升 +0.392（未处理对照仅 −0.006），8 次有效实验中 7 次选中目标方向，并成功将 "UTK" 字母写入超畴取向。

> **TL;DR:** SPARC pairs a coding agent (Claude Opus 4.1 in Claude Code) with a human operator on one scanning probe microscope, using two persistent memory files (FINDINGS.md for graded conclusions, PITFALLS.md for failure modes later compiled into executable preflight checks). On a (111)-oriented PbZr0.2Ti0.8O3 ferroelectric thin film — where neither state representation, action space, nor objective existed beforehand — the agent discovered an alternating-polarity pulse lattice that raised power at the commanded superdomain direction by +0.392 versus −0.006 in the untreated control (floor 0.018), achieved targeted orientation in 7 of 8 valid closed-loop trials, and falsified its own period-matching and exposure-threshold hypotheses before composing raster + masked-lattice writes to print "UTK" into the superdomain orientation (+0.30/+0.42/+0.52 population gains in the U/T/K strokes). The paper's thesis: agents are a reasoning layer for the pre-optimization regime, not a replacement for Bayesian optimization once the problem is well posed.

## 核心贡献

- **SPARC 框架（扫描探针智能体研究循环）**：通用编码智能体与人类操作员共享同一台显微镜、同一笔记本和两个持久记忆文件；FINDINGS.md 记录带分级信任度的结论（含初始态、操作、终态、不确定度、适用条件、被取代关系），PITFALLS.md 记录分析与仪器的失败模式，并在第二阶段被编译为可在写入前拒绝非法操作的**可执行预检**。
- **定位前优化阶段的智能体角色**：明确区分 SPARC 与贝叶斯优化——当可观测量、动作空间和目标函数尚未定义时（探索性实验的普遍情形），智能体负责**构建问题表示本身**（发明状态变量、动作原语、比较准则），而不是在固定协议内调参。
- **发现了面内超畴方向的可重写控制原语**：针对 (111) 取向 PZT 薄膜三个晶体学允许的畴方向（互成 60°），提出行间交替极性的定向静止脉冲晶格（每点 ±10 V、1 s、行距为局部层畴周期一半），可命令式选择方向、改写已写入区域并保持。
- **用实验否定智能体自己的两个假设**：晶格周期与层畴宽度精确匹配、以及暴露阈值，都不是方向选择的必要条件；**空间极性交替**才是关键控制变量（均匀极性阵列无方向性效应）。
- **规则组合演示**：双极性光栅制备背景 + 掩膜脉冲晶格，在超畴取向上打印出 "UTK" 字母图案。
- **提炼了智能体实验的工程要求**：命令执行的物理验证、存储结论的适用条件、新观测量必须在真实仪器数据上验证、鲁棒控制协议——并坦诚披露智能体自写控制软件的失败（8 次启动中 6 次因静态可查故障失败）。

## Key Contributions

- **SPARC framework (Scanning Probe Agentic Research Cycle):** a general-purpose coding agent and a human operator share one microscope, one notebook, and two persistent memory files. FINDINGS.md stores graded conclusions (claim, evidence, uncertainty, validity conditions, supersession links); PITFALLS.md records learned analysis/hardware failure modes, a subset of which was compiled into **executable preflight checks** that can reject an invalid instrument action before any write.
- **A precise role claim:** agents are a reasoning layer for the regime *before* sequential optimization becomes well posed. Unlike Bayesian optimization workflows, the agent may replace one state descriptor with another, redefine what counts as a null, invent new action primitives, and retire rejected control rules — with every revision traceable to data, code, and instrument state.
- **Discovery of a rewritable control primitive for in-plane superdomain direction** in (111)-oriented PbZr0.2Ti0.8O3: an oriented square lattice of stationary bias pulses (10 V, 1 s per site, row spacing = half the local lamellar period, alternating polarity between rows) selects a commanded direction among the three crystallographically allowed directors, redirects previously written regions, and retains the state.
- **Self-falsification:** the agent tested and rejected two of its own working hypotheses (exact lattice–lamellar period matching; a sharp exposure threshold) and superseded a Campaign 1 rule when virgin-film data contradicted it — demonstrating memory that updates under evidence rather than accreting stale claims.
- **Composed-operation demonstration:** a bipolar raster background plus a masked alternating-polarity pulse lattice printed the letters "UTK" into the superdomain orientation.
- **A census of human-agent operation** across two campaigns (operator-controlled vs. agent-controlled), including turn counts, self-halts, and a frank failure analysis of agent-authored control software.

## Methodology

The system couples Claude Opus 4.1 running in Claude Code on the instrument-control computer (shell and file tools, no direct network access to vendor software) with a human operator. Each iteration follows seven steps: (i) review prior knowledge and persistent memory; (ii) generate a physical hypothesis with predictions; (iii) plan the experiment (patterns, doses, controls); (iv) review the plan against PITFALLS.md checks; (v) execute the instrument action and acquire readout; (vi) compare director-population changes against an untreated region in the same frame; (vii) review interpretation and update memory. The two campaigns differ only in who owns the two review steps.

**Physical system.** 150 nm PbZr0.2Ti0.8O3 / 30 nm La0.67Sr0.33MnO3 on (111) SrTiO3, grown by pulsed laser deposition. The tetragonal film's six <001> polarization variants project onto three in-plane director axes 60° apart; a superdomain is a lamellar band whose director is read by lateral PFM (dual-frequency resonance tracking, 8 µm frames, 256 px). **State extraction:** the agent built the population vector w = (w1, w2, w3) from the angular power spectrum (radially integrated 2D FFT, binned ±15° around the fitted triad, e.g., 4°/64°/124°), with two no-write images defining a per-run experimental floor. The effect of a write is treated as an empirical map w' = F_a(w), agnostic to microscopic mechanism.

**Campaign 1 (operator-controlled, 11 days, 723 turns, 813 min active instrument time):** the agent reanalyzed earlier manual measurements, ruled out raster/continuous-trajectory strategies, discovered from instrument write-code that dense trajectories reverse bias every few tens of nm (inside one lamellar period), and reformulated the task from "eraser" to "directional selector," constructing the oriented alternating-polarity pulse lattice (14×14 sites per 2 µm panel, ±10 V, 1 s).

**Campaign 2 (agent-controlled via the AESPM interface, 3 days, 9 closed-loop cycles, 8 self-halts):** PITFALLS.md entries became executable checks (scanner range, bias limit, footprint, per-iteration action budget, positive control after a "void run" in which the trajectory executed with no bias reaching the tip). The agent stated falsifiable predictions before each write–read cycle, tested spacing/exposure/polarity hypotheses, then composed raster + masked-lattice writes for the UTK demonstration.

**Estimator validation** is treated as part of the result: on synthetic stripes the structure tensor won, but on repeated real frames of an unchanged state the Fourier estimator was ~2.5× more repeatable; all reported quantities are treatment-induced contrasts against a measured null rather than absolute state labels.

## Results & Highlights

- **Directional selection among the allowed triad (R5):** a pulse lattice commanded onto a fitted director raised power at that direction by **+0.385** and made it dominant; a command placed ~26° off the nearest allowed director produced only **+0.152**, with the response still landing on a member of the triad — selection is confined to the three crystallographic directions, not the command angle (both panels had 144 sites, controlling the site-count confound).
- **Rewritability with controls (R6, principal result):** write A (4°) raised 4° power in panel P1 by **+0.392** (independently written P3: **+0.577**) while the untreated P2 control moved **−0.006**, within the measured no-treatment floor of **0.018**. Write B (124°) then redirected P1: 4° power **−0.287**, 124° power **+0.149**; the A-only retention control P3 relaxed only **−0.076** over the same interval. Population vector of P1: (0.23, 0.56, 0.21) → (0.76, 0.08, 0.16) → (0.42, 0.14, 0.43) — partial, not complete, reconfiguration.
- **Hypothesis falsification (Campaign 2):** at matched exposure, alternating-polarity lattices shifted the targeted orientation at repeat distances of **1, 2, 4, and 8 lamellar periods** with no spacing consistently superior; uniform-polarity arrays produced **no change above baseline**; selection persisted at **70% of the Campaign-1 exposure threshold** and populations stayed similar over a **~2.2-fold exposure range** — period matching and the exposure threshold were both marked superseded; polarity alternation was retained.
- **Preparation-dependent raster rule (IT6):** a balanced bipolar raster (+9 V / −9 V passes) on virgin film *increased* the parallel-family fraction from **0.415 to 0.601**, contradicting the Campaign 1 finding (written regions: depletion of the parallel family) — after artifact triage, the rule was revised to depend on sample preparation and reused the same day.
- **Set/reverse/hold statistics (IT9):** one write raised the 64° family population to **0.696** from a 4°-dominated initial state; across 9 trials (1 excluded for a failed reference control), the targeted orientation ended dominant in **7 of 8 valid trials** (1 of 8 ended on another allowed orientation — non-deterministic selection), and the written state persisted through repeated imaging for **34 min**.
- **UTK patterning (IT10b):** raster-prepared 62° background (~12 µm field) + masked 2°-commanded lattice raised the target-family population by **+0.30, +0.42, and +0.52** in the U, T, and K strokes relative to inter-letter spaces; letters are ~3 µm across, with resolution limited by the analysis window (must span several lamellar periods).
- **Framework accounting:** FINDINGS.md grew from **28 to 54 graded entries** (11 withdrawn, 3 superseded); a pitfall caught anti-correlated resonance sidebands inverting the lateral-channel sign in **10 of 46 image pairs**; in Campaign 2, **8 of 9 closed-loop cycles** were self-halted by the agent without operator intervention, but **6 of 8 software launches failed** on statically detectable faults and the function meant to apply all **29 preflight checks** was never called on the normal driver path.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** The claim is well targeted and, in my reading, correct: most LMM/agent microscopy systems (including this group's prior work, refs 17–22) start from a fixed workflow or predefined evaluator, whereas SPARC explicitly supports *changing the problem representation mid-campaign* — replacing state descriptors, redefining nulls, retiring control rules. The FINDINGS/PITFALLS split with supersession links, and the compilation of natural-language pitfalls into executable preflight checks, is a concrete and transferable design pattern. That said, the components (Claude Code, markdown memory, Jupyter-style notebooks, an existing AESPM automation interface) are all off-the-shelf; the novelty is the architecture and the methodology, not any new algorithm.
- **Rigor:** Controls are unusually careful for an SPM paper (no-treatment floors, retention controls, same-frame untreated references, estimator transfer tests, site-count confound control), and the authors quote contrasts against measured nulls rather than absolute labels. But the headline materials claims rest on **a single principal rewrite region**; the success rate comes from **8 valid trials on a small number of areas**; one trial was excluded post hoc because the reference control failed (justified, but small-n exclusions matter); and the authors themselves note the manuscript reports deterministic contrasts rather than population-level inference, with block resampling deferred. No independent replication on a second sample or probe is presented.
- **Reproducibility:** Strong on the software/record side (full transcript + raw data + scripts released; provenance reconstructed from file headers, trajectory files, and frame order). But the physics depends on probe-specific thresholds (a pitfall the paper itself records — a threshold established with one probe was initially reused with another), one cantilever orientation (director signs indistinguishable), and PLD-grown film idiosyncrasies, so reproducing the materials result elsewhere will be nontrivial.
- **Significance:** The agent-as-pre-optimization-reasoning-layer framing is a genuinely useful contribution to the automated-experiment literature, and the polarity-alternation (not period matching, not dose) finding is a clean, falsifiable materials rule. The UTK demonstration is, however, patterning of the *measured in-plane response* at ~3 µm resolution limited by the analysis window — not arbitrary nanoscale domain control — and the out-of-plane response changed during raster preparation, so the complete polarization vector in the patterned regions is unknown.
- **Red flags:** (i) The agent's own control software failed in 6 of 8 launches and silently skipped all 29 preflight checks on the normal driver path — the autonomy narrative is honest about this, but it means the "agent-controlled" campaign's reliability rested heavily on the human retaining stop authority; (ii) the microscopic mechanism (trailing field vs. mechanical asymmetry vs. nucleation/wall motion) is unresolved — the trailing-field story is labeled a testable hypothesis, which is honest, but a reader skimming figures might over-read it; (iii) there is no comparison against any alternative autonomous agent or BO baseline on the same task — the "baseline" is essentially the authors' own earlier manual campaigns; (iv) the bias toward an agent built by an LLM vendor's tooling (Claude Opus 4.1) with no ablation across models leaves open how much of the capability is model-specific.

## Strengths

- Crisply identifies and targets the pre-optimization regime — where observables, actions, and objectives are not yet defined — which most automated-experimentation literature sidesteps.
- The two-file memory design (graded FINDINGS.md with supersession; PITFALLS.md compiled into executable checks) is simple, concrete, and immediately reusable on other instruments.
- Genuine self-falsification: the agent rejected two of its own hypotheses (period matching, exposure threshold) and revised a third (raster effect) under evidence — the memory updates instead of accreting.
- Exceptional experimental hygiene for an exploratory SPM study: per-run no-treatment floors, retention and site-count controls, estimator validation on synthetic vs. real data, and full provenance.
- Refreshing transparency: the paper publishes its own failure census (void runs, sideband sign inversions in 10/46 image pairs, 6/8 failed launches, the uncalled check function) rather than only successes.
- The human-agent contribution table (Table 2) and campaign census (Table 1) make the division of labor auditable instead of anecdotal.

## Limitations

- Principal rewrite demonstrated in a single region; set/reverse/hold statistics from only ~8 valid trials on few areas — no cross-sample or cross-probe replication.
- Retention tracked for at most ~34–60 min of repeated imaging; long-term stability untested.
- Final written states are often two-family mixtures (e.g., (0.42, 0.14, 0.43)) rather than pure orientations; selection is probabilistic (7/8), not deterministic.
- Single cantilever orientation means the two signs of each director are indistinguishable; full vector PFM or sample rotation is needed.
- The UTK pattern's out-of-plane response changed during raster preparation, so the complete polarization vector cannot be assigned; spatial resolution (~3 µm) is limited by the analysis window, not the write itself.
- No microscopic switching mechanism established — local ferroelastic switching vs. nucleation/growth vs. wall motion remain unresolved (deferred to a companion study).
- Early pulse-lattice experiments coupled command angle to site count (14×14 vs. 12×12 bounding-box artifact); statistics are descriptive contrasts, not significance-tested inference.
- Agent-authored control software was the weakest link: 6 of 8 launches failed on statically detectable faults, and the master preflight-check function was bypassed on the normal driver path.

## Relevance

对关注「LLM 智能体 + 自动化科学实验」的方向高度相关：SPARC 给出了一个可复用的人机共享仪器架构（分级持久记忆 + 可执行安全预检），并清晰界定了智能体在贝叶斯优化适用之前的「问题构建」阶段的价值，这一框架可直接迁移到其他自动化实验平台。同时，论文对智能体实验失败模式的坦诚工程分析（物理验证、结论适用条件、软件自检）对构建可靠的科学智能体系统有直接参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How much of SPARC's performance is attributable to Claude Opus 4.1 specifically versus the memory/check architecture? An ablation across models (and across coding-agent harnesses) with the same campaign protocol would separate framework from model effects.
- Can the preflight-check compilation be generalized — e.g., an automated loop that mines new PITFALLS entries into static analyzers or runtime assertions without human engineering, addressing the observed 6/8 launch-failure rate?
- The 7/8 non-deterministic selection and two-family mixtures suggest a stochastic switching landscape: would hypothesis-learning or active model discrimination between nucleation, wall-motion, and ferroelastic mechanisms (as the authors propose) converge with fewer samples than blind BO once the problem is well posed?
- Does the polarity-alternation rule transfer to other (111) ferroelectric films (e.g., different PZT compositions, BFO) or to other tip-sample systems where trailing fields dominate — and does the preparation-dependent raster rule (depletion on written film vs. alignment on virgin film) reflect out-of-plane state mediation?
- What is the minimal instrument-grounding stack (read-back, positive controls, provenance) needed before an agent can safely operate a microscope with no human present at all, and how does the cost of that stack scale with instrument irreversibility?
- Could the graded FINDINGS.md representation (claim, evidence, uncertainty, validity conditions, supersession) be standardized as an interchange format so that different agents — or a successor agent after context loss — inherit a campaign's state losslessly?
