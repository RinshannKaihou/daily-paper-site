Why This Matters

A reusable per-question audit splits memory loss into retention, retrieval, and reader bottlenecks — budget–accuracy frontiers are incomparable unless the retrieval regime is reported.

三类分解与「必须报告检索机制」这一规范性结论，可直接嵌入记忆系统评测与驱逐策略设计的实验流程。
Critical Edge

The headline shares are conditioned on a deliberately modest BM25-lite retriever whose miss rate directly inflates the recoverable bin — a stronger ranker could shift the whole decomposition.

BIN 1 IRREVERSIBLE

Evidence evicted

Restoring the gold set flips the answer correct — but the gold was destroyed by eviction. Only more retention fixes it.

Remedy → retain more
BIN 2 RECOVERABLE

Retriever missed it

Gold survived in the store, but top-k retrieval never surfaced it. A better retriever fixes it.

Remedy → better retriever
BIN 3 RESIDUAL

Reader fails anyway

Gold restored into context, the reader still answers wrong — typically cross-session aggregation.

Remedy → stronger reader

Method — the restore counterfactual

§3 · paired intervention

A causal-measurement instrument, not a new system. For each oracle-answerable error made under an eviction policy, the benchmark-labeled gold evidence set Gq is reinstated at the front of the read-time context (fixed 2,000-token cap — every gold set fits) and the frozen reader re-answers: same prompt, temperature 0, same judge. Reader, ranker, and judge never vary, so the comparison isolates evidence availability alone.

restore gain(q) = accq(restored) − accq(policy) ∈ {−1, 0, +1}  ·  irreversible loss(q) = max(0, gain) · 1[Gq ⊄ SP]
A1Oracle-answerability. Only questions the reader gets right under clean full-gold injection enter the denominator (N = 336) — bins reflect what the budget did to evidence, not reader inability.
A2Judge validity. Self-consistency 1.0 on a 40-question calibration gate; an independent GPT-5.5 judge agrees on 95.7% of 393 stratified grades (κ = 0.90).
A3Restore tightness. Equal-length non-gold placebo flips only 8.3% vs 100% for gold restore; injecting the entire retained store recovers just 2.0% of irreversible cases.

R2 · Retrieval regimes are not interchangeable
+0.29 recoverable-share gap (FIFO @ 80k)

top-k minus forced-gold; CI .19–.39, Holm-corrected p = .007. Random +0.27, redundancy-aware +0.33; 4 of 12 evicting cells reject, and the pattern replicates with a GPT-5.4-mini reader. Frontiers must state their retrieval regime.

R3 · Matched-accuracy null, with power
0 / 9 policy pairs dissociate

At matched accuracy (caliper |Δacc| ≤ 0.05), all |Δrate| ≤ 0.036, every CI spans 0, resolution 1.2–6 pp — equivalence explicitly not claimed (no powered TOST). The destructive control is detected in 9 / 9 contrasts (p < .001).

R4 · Residual = reader-utilization failure
Residual bin halves: 59 → 28

Dominated by cross-session counting/summation errors (a $185 bike-expense total answered “$65” even with all gold restored). Swapping to the stronger GPT-5.4-mini reader fixes exactly the aggregation cases. Single-session questions run ~95% irreversible; multi-session and temporal questions carry 23–34% residual.

＋ Strengths

  • ▸Deconflates destruction vs retrieval misses — opposite remedies that look identical on the accuracy axis, across the whole budget–frontier literature.
  • ▸Causal-inference hygiene rare for empirical NLP: pre-frozen protocol, paired per-question scoring, placebo (8.3% vs 100%), positive control 9/9, quantified upper-bound semantics.
  • ▸Honest negative result — reported with its detection resolution (1.2–6 pp) and an explicit refusal to claim equivalence.
  • ▸Concrete residual mechanism ($185 → “$65”) that predicts stronger readers, not better memory, fix that error class — confirmed by the 59→28 halving.
  • ▸Fully released artifact: code, per-question records, pinned dataset hash, model snapshot ids, verbatim prompts.

− Limitations

  • ▸Single benchmark — LongMemEval-S, conversational English QA; no evidence yet for task-agent memories, RAG corpora, or multilingual settings. Needs gold labels + oracle filter, so benchmark analysis, not deployment auditing.
  • ▸Reader = judge = GPT-4o-mini; every cross-check judge is OpenAI — correlated reader–judge bias is invisible to the self-consistency check.
  • ▸Realistic regime uses a frozen BM25-lite ranker (top-60): recoverable shares are ranker-specific; a neural retriever could shift the two-bin balance.
  • ▸Audits simple policy classes, not published retention systems (EMBER, OSL-MR, BudgetMem) — the matched-accuracy null says little about learned, query-aware retention.
  • ▸R3 resolution is 1.2–6 pp with no powered TOST; single-author result awaits independent replication, and residual-bin size is reader-dependent by construction.

Open questions

for further exploration
  1. Q1Do the irreversible shares hold with a neural retriever instead of BM25-lite, or does a stronger ranker materially shrink the recoverable bin at 80k?
  2. Q2Would learned, query-aware retention (EMBER, OSL-MR) dissociate at matched accuracy where the simple policy classes did not (0 of 9)?
  3. Q3Can the audit extend to stores with re-represented or summarized units, via reconstruction or summary-to-evidence alignment?
  4. Q4How does the decomposition behave under compression-based eviction, where “irreversible” becomes graded information loss rather than binary destruction?
  5. Q5Does a non-OpenAI or human judge change the residual bin, given reader and judge currently share both model and provider?
  6. Q6Can the instrument audit privacy-mandated deletion — quantifying the task cost of required forgetting on deployment traces?
