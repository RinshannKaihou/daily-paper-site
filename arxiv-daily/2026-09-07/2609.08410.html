Critical Edge · 批判视角

▲

Layer and strength are tuned on the same 70-prompt evaluation sets that then produce the reported scores — no held-out split, so headline numbers are optimistic by selection.

Also: prompting is the only baseline — no head-to-head against the trained composition methods the paper motivates itself with.

Methodology 方法01

Three stages, no training anywhere.

01 · EXTRACT

DiffMean vectors

Differences of condition means, read at the final post-instruction token, then unit-normalized so strength α alone controls magnitude. Language: English vs 9 targets (FLORES, 260 examples each); jailbreak: 260 harmful vs harmless (Arditi); conciseness: 540 IFEval-style pairs.

02 · APPLY

Residual-stream injection

During generation, h̃ = h + α·v is added at each vector's own best layer; when layers coincide, scaled vectors simply sum.

03 · EVALUATE

70 + 70 prompts

CLaS-Bench (harmless) + Arditi (harmful), scored by LFS (fastText ≥95%), JBS (Qwen3Guard), CCS (length reduction), OR (relevance) — aggregated by harmonic mean to punish trade-offs.

Strengths 优势04

  • Directly actionable recipe — respect per-attribute layer + strength, and plain additive composition works; resolves the van der Weij (destructive) vs Stolfo (mild) inconsistency by attributing it to under-specified configs.
  • Cross-family, cross-scale design — 2 families × 2 sizes (8B/70B, 14B/32B) — that most prior composition studies lacked.
  • Converts an empirical recipe into a testable hypothesis: orthogonality predicts composability, with per-language supporting evidence.
  • Harmonic-mean aggregation punishes configurations that improve one attribute while destroying another.
  • Honest failure reporting: L+C losing to prompting, Llama-8B's variance, German/Russian degradation all called out plainly.

Limitations 局限05

  • Headline deltas reported as plots, not numeric tables — reproducing exact numbers requires re-running everything; n = 70 per condition, no significance testing.
  • Instruction-tuned models only; extraction at the post-instruction token may not transfer to base models or other chat formats.
  • Constant intervention strength per condition — optimal α plausibly varies with context.
  • Only English→target direction; three arguably "favorable" near-orthogonal attributes — interacting pairs (sentiment vs toxicity) untested.
  • Automated judges throughout; two Qwen judges score two Qwen2.5 models — a family-affinity confound never discussed; no human evaluation.
  • The orthogonality–composability link remains correlational — no intervention perturbs the geometry to test it causally.

Questions for Further Exploration 开放问题06

  • Causal test: project a steering vector into another's orthogonal complement — does composition of interfering attributes (L+C, sentiment+toxicity) improve, training-free?
  • Would a held-out hyperparameter split change the conclusions — and how large is the L+J margin in exact numbers, not plot distributions?
  • Do Qwen-family LLM judges bias the results — does swapping in GPT-4o-class or human annotation shift the Llama-vs-Qwen ordering?
  • Why do language vectors favor early layers while jailbreak/conciseness favor middle ones — predictable from probing accuracy across depth?
  • Does the per-layer recipe scale past three attributes, or does the fixed set of usable layers become the binding constraint?
  • Does it transfer to base models, MoE architectures, or the target→English direction — and does dynamic, context-dependent α beat constant strength?
