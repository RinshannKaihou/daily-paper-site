Why this matters

Zero-overhead probes that read an agent's internal states to forecast failure directly inform deployment-time failure detection and human-takeover strategy.

— distilled from Relevance

Critical edge

The "reliability safeguard" utility is asserted, not measured — no downstream intervention (early abort, human handoff) is ever tested, and only two external baselines are actually run.

Methodology — distilled

Agents run on InterCode-Bash/SQL/Python under vLLM (single A100, greedy decoding, ≤10 turns); residual-stream states are recovered offline by teacher-forcing each stored trajectory through the same model (bit-exact replay), with endpoints marked at observation, reasoning, action, and feedback phases.

Two probes read those states. LTD (Latent Trajectory Dynamics) compresses the trajectory's geometry — cosine displacement, relative displacement, inter-layer drift ratio, cumulative path efficiency, phase-transition velocity — into 28 kinematic features for a logistic model. ARP (Action Representation Probe) decodes success from final-layer states at action-span endpoints, excluding terminal submit tokens to block shortcut learning:

Baselines: Calibrated Logprob (token-weighted mean of per-token probabilities) and HTC (48 token-trace dynamics features). Evaluation: 5-fold stratified frozen folds, nested hyperparameter selection, and all learned preprocessing (standardization / PCA / calibration) fitted strictly inside training folds. 19 corrupt InterCode-SQL gold tasks audited and removed.

Strengths

  • Leak-free nested cross-validation applied identically to every method — the comparison is fair by construction.
  • ARP is strikingly simple (mean-pool → PCA-64 → logistic probe) yet delivers double-digit AUROC gains in several settings.
  • Genuinely zero overhead: no prompt modification, no multi-sample rollouts, concrete FLOPs/ms accounting for online use.
  • Data hygiene: 19 corrupt InterCode-SQL gold answers audited and removed.
  • Deterministic unit-test ground truth — no LLM-as-judge noise in the calibration evaluation.

Limitations

  • Coding-only evaluation, short horizons (≤10 turns); no web/tool agents or longer-horizon settings.
  • Requires white-box residual access — inapplicable to API-only agents; no cross-model or cross-environment transfer tested.
  • Only two external baselines; self-consistency, semantic entropy, and verbalized confidence discussed but never run.
  • ECE wins inconsistent — the dominance claim holds for AUROC/Brier only.
  • No end-to-end use case (confidence-triggered rollback or escalation) validates the monitor's practical value.
  • Modest absolute AUROC (~0.65–0.72) in the hardest settings — thin for safety-critical gating alone.

Open questions

  1. Do ARP probes transfer zero-shot across models, environments, or to web agents — or must they always be re-fit?
  2. Would combining LTD's kinematic features with ARP's pooled states beat either alone, especially on DeepSeek-Bash?
  3. Can confidence-triggered intervention (abort, retry, human escalation) measurably improve end-task success in deployment?
  4. Which layers encode the most success signal — is the final layer optimal, or do mid-layer states carry complementary information?
  5. How do these internal signals behave under distribution shift and non-greedy decoding (temperature > 0)?
