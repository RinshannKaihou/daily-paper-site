The pipeline is deliberately prior art. A structured orthogonal KOTMS rotation (35 min for all 252 linear projections) makes weight distributions ternary-friendly while controlling activation outliers. E2M-ATQ then fits a {-1, 0, +1} support per block with least-squares scales, adding a residual second plane only to the highest-salience rows; GPTQ redistributes rounding error through an approximate inverse Hessian, calibrated on 64 WikiText-2 samples × 2048 tokens — the 12 GB card's ceiling.

Only linear weights are converted: embeddings, LM head, norms, and KV cache stay FP16 (W1.58A16, ≈1.64 effective bits/weight). Evaluation is unusually disciplined — an external reproduction gate against published TWLA numbers, three-corpus perplexity, eight zero-shot tasks scored with chance-corrected retention R = (As − B)/(At − B), a matched-protocol 4B re-benchmark, packing fidelity checks, and direct packed execution.

hw: single RTX 5070 (12,227 MiB) · ~31 GB RAM · Windows · seed 0 · percdamp 0.01

Strengths 优势

  • Exceptional measurement honesty: pre-registered acceptance ranges, an external reproduction gate (62.00 vs 62.05), and a published ledger of project failures.
  • The de-confounded 4B/8B matched re-benchmark is the right design and yields the paper's best scientific signal — robustness to aggressive PTQ grows with scale.
  • Completes the deployment story others skip: accuracy → actual serialization (16.9 GB → 8.24 GiB, perplexity preserved to 0.0007%) → direct packed execution on a 12 GB consumer card.
  • Instructive practitioner lessons: effective-bit accounting (≈1.64 vs naive 1.585) and the post-hoc packing catastrophe (PPL ≈ 18,477) prove serialization must be designed into the quantizer.

Limitations 局限

  • One seed, n=500 per task, no variance estimates — small task-level deltas (BoolQ −2.4, WinoGrande −3.6) are statistically fragile.
  • Only two model sizes: "scaling improves ternary robustness" is a two-point anecdote, not a law.
  • WikiText-2 is simultaneously calibration corpus and headline evaluation — the 1.318× vs 1.393× gap quantifies that favoritism.
  • Partial quantization only: embeddings, LM head, KV cache remain FP16, so "1.58-bit model" materially overstates artifact compression.
  • Prototype kernel 4.6× slower than cuBLAS, one shape tested; no head-to-head vs PTQTP / CAT-Q; unexplained 7.9% local-vs-published FP16 gap.
  • 1.361× perplexity and −7.8 pts accuracy are large in absolute terms — a research baseline, not a drop-in replacement.

Questions for Further Exploration · 待追问

  1. Does the 8B retention advantage continue at 14B/32B, or saturate — and do knowledge-task gains persist when calibration moves from WikiText-2 to FineWeb-Edu?
  2. Can shape-specialized kernels or Marlin/Triton autotuning close the 4.6× GEMV gap — and at what batch size does the ternary path flip ahead?
  3. How much retention returns if embeddings, LM head, or KV cache are also quantized toward a true 1.64-bit budget?
  4. What does multi-seed variance look like at n=500, and how seed-sensitive are E2M-ATQ's salience masks (0.5% / 3.1% / 10.0% / 86.5%)?
  5. Under one identical protocol, how does this TWLA-derived pipeline compare against PTQTP and CAT-Q on the same Qwen3-8B checkpoint — is the reproduction gate's closeness specific to reusing TWLA's exact configuration?
