Why this matters

Fine-tuning's hidden side effects on bias are a core alignment concern; ΔB offers annotation-free, single-GPU monitoring for third-party and domain fine-tunes.

Critical edge · external validity

Every bias shift was manufactured — synthetic harmful fine-tunes linearly merged into a spectrum. Whether ΔB tracks naturally occurring drift is untested.

§1Methodology — read the geometry, not the outputs

① embedfinal-layer mean-pooled hidden states of Maud & Mref (L32 / L34)
→
② relativizer(x) = cos(e(x), e(ai)) over 1,000 neutral anchors → one shared space, no fitted map
→
③ scoreB = association of group sentences with + / − attribute sets, negated Euclidean distance
→
④ compareΔB = Baud − Bref, one scalar per target group

Absolute embeddings of two models aren't comparable — fine-tuning reshapes latent geometry. Borrowing Moschella et al.'s relative representations, both models are re-encoded as similarity-to-anchor vectors, sidestepping any cross-model mapping. Validation avoids the single before/after jump: harmful and unharmful WildGuardMix fine-tunes of Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3 and Gemma-3-4b-it are linearly merged at five ratios → 7-checkpoint severity spectra (full FT & LoRA), each scored by WildGuardMix harmfulness, DecodingTrust stereotypes and ToxiGen toxicity.

ΔB < 0 ⇒ group drifted toward negative attributes (bias up vs reference)

§2Results — beyond the headline AUC

ΔB co-varies with output-level bias change in 15 of 18 settings; peak correlation Pearson r = −0.84 (p < 0.001, Llama · DecodingTrust · full FT).

  • Gain is the encoding, not the alignment — Procrustes-SEAT sits at chance while RR wins; ΔB is not reducible to undirected drift (CKA stays below).
  • Robust under stress — mean ROC AUC 0.863 ± 0.030 over 6 attribute-set variants, 0.902 ± 0.008 over 6 templates; stable across pooling, seeds and out-of-domain anchors.
  • 3–50× cheaper — ~3 min per model vs 9–156 min for benchmarks; no annotations needed, so new target groups are nearly free.

Strengths

  • Solves the real comparability problem with a map-free construction — and the Procrustes control proves the gain comes from relative encoding.
  • Graded 7-checkpoint merge spectrum turns "did bias change?" into a dose-response question.
  • Exceptional ablation suite: anchors, attribute wording, templates, pooling, seeds.
  • ~3 min, zero annotated data, single GPU — per-checkpoint monitoring during fine-tuning is plausible.
  • Honest framing throughout: proxy-not-proof, complementary-not-replacement, explicit Goodhart warning.

Limitations

  • Inherently relative — cannot audit a checkpoint in isolation; small ΔB only means no detected shift, not safety.
  • Signal weakens under LoRA; weakest overall for Gemma-3-4B (r = −0.04, WildGuardMix LoRA).
  • Synthetic bias induction only — natural drift, >8B scale, MoE, non-English and intersectional groups untested.
  • Final-layer mean pooling; layer/pooling choice unsystematic; inherits SEAT's embedding instability.
  • All checkpoints interpolate the same two endpoints — pooled correlations may co-vary more tidily than in the wild.

§3Open questions for further exploration

  1. Does ΔB track naturally occurring fine-tuning drift (domain SFT, RLHF, distillation) with the same dose-response?
  2. Do the correlations survive cluster-aware statistics — bootstrapping over fine-tuning runs rather than checkpoint–group pairs?
  3. Would multi-layer or intermediate-layer pooling rescue the weak Gemma and LoRA cases?
  4. Can ΔB drive early stopping or regularization without being Goodharted — a model optimized to keep ΔB small need not be less biased?
  5. Does it generalize beyond 8B — to MoE architectures, multilingual groups, intersectional identities?
  6. How noisy is the ChatGPT-based prompt-to-topic mapping on WildGuardMix; would human labels change the picture?

Credibility — NASK National Research Institute (Poland), with Warsaw & Gdańsk University of Technology; builds on SEAT (May et al., 2019) and relative representations (Moschella et al., 2023); open code and fixed sentence sets; every experiment fits one A100 40GB; deliberately harmful merged checkpoints (appropriately) not released. The paper explicitly declines causal claims.
