Key Metric
0.92→0.00

Triggered German generation rate, before → after ablating residual-stream features (Gaperon-8B, layer 15). The backdoor is switched off completely.

French: 0.82 → 0.03 · steer clean→French 0.89 · detector F1 = 1.0 at every site

Why This Matters

A feature-level causal handle on anomalous behavior — a working template for backdoor and trust audits of self-evolving, long-autonomy LLM agents.

残差流作为因果干预位点，为异常行为定位提供了可操作的特征级审计工具。

Critical Edge

The setting is the easiest possible backdoor, the trigger string is assumed known, and the one genuinely poisoned testbed (Apertus) never triggered reliably.

— from Critical Assessment, red flags (1) & (5)

Methodology

contrast-and-intervene protocol

Testbed. Gaperon-1B/8B models pretrained with harmless language-switching backdoors: appending “Nocturnus Aurorum Magnificat” flips English continuations to French (a second trigger flips them to German), with attack success up to 99%. Prior activation-patching work traced the trigger through a serial circuit with a bottleneck at the final trigger token p−1: early attention composes it, the residual stream carries it, the final MLP reads it out.

SAE suite. SAEs trained at three sites — residual stream, MLP output, attention output — across layers; JumpReLU as default, Matryoshka BatchTopK as comparison; ~4B tokens under three data conditions (plain mix ~0.02% triggered, trigger-enriched ~1%, trigger-free). Released as Gaperon-Scope.

Selection & causal tests. A three-way contrast — triggered vs. translation-instruction vs. pretraining-mix prompts — ranks features by F1 at position p−1 (translation controls stop generic “write in French” features being mislabeled). Validation: ablation (zero the feature, reconstruct) under a validity gate — reconstruction must still preserve the backdoor, so SAE error can’t pose as success — and steering (add the scaled decoder vector to clean prompts).   • Dissociation in one run: a layer-15 Attention SAE feature hits F1 = 1.0 yet steers at 0.01 and ablates at 0.0 — selectivity and causal efficacy live in different sites.
  • Scale builds shared detectors: 8B shares French/German trigger features in 13 of 27 layers (mean Jaccard 0.370); 1B shares none — trigger recognition becomes a reusable component.
  • Data & architecture matter: layer-26 French F1 rises 0.296 → 0.868 with trigger enrichment (JumpReLU); Matryoshka BatchTopK matches detection (0.959) but steers at ~0.02.
  • Late layers are readout, not handles: layer-31 French feature: recall 95%, precision 19%; steering ≤ 0.03; ablation leaves rate unchanged (0.71 → 0.77).
  • Blind audit nearly works: trigger-free SAEs still find detectors (F1 0.82/0.91) and language handles (steer 0.91/0.93) — but miss trigger routing and break the backdoor during reconstruction.

Best runs: JumpReLU, d_SAE = 32768, L0 = 1, residual site. German induction fails everywhere (steer ≤ 0.01) — German is < 1% of the training mixture; a single direction can disrupt a triggered state but not synthesize one.

Strengths

  • Clean operationalization of detection vs. suppression vs. induction, with conservative decision rules and validity gates that guard against self-deception.
  • The three-way translation-prompt control — without it, any “French feature” would look trigger-selective.
  • Circuit-informed prediction confirmed: the residual-stream result was forecast by the prior p−1 bottleneck circuit map.
  • Honest negatives reported prominently: Matryoshka’s poor control, German steering failure, failed 24B runs, non-triggering Apertus.
  • Artifact release (Gaperon-Scope) with use-restricted license; Wilson and paired-bootstrap CIs on all headline numbers.

Limitations

  • Benign proxy: language switching reuses an existing capability — backdoors unlocking suppressed behaviors may route elsewhere; only role-level transfer is claimed.
  • Known-trigger assumption: the contrast protocol requires the trigger string in hand.
  • Best-of-sweep reporting: headline F1 / steer / drop may come from different runs (layer-26 French: max 0.868 vs. median 0.129).
  • Scale ceiling: 24B SAEs failed to converge; external validity beyond Gaperon untested.
  • French/German asymmetry confounds mechanism claims with pretraining prevalence — induction is effectively French-only.

Questions for Further Exploration

open threads
  1. Does the detector / routing / readout decomposition hold when the payload is a suppressed capability — where the readout MLP might be the causal handle instead?
  2. Can the blind-audit condition be fixed so SAE reconstruction stops destroying the backdoor — enabling trigger discovery without knowing the trigger string?
  3. Why do Matryoshka BatchTopK features detect but not control — dictionary geometry, or the nested-prefix objective breaking feature–direction alignment?
  4. Does 8B-only French/German feature sharing keep growing at 24B+ — implying one defender-ablatable trigger-detector circuit?
  5. Can gradient attribution plus ablation-validity gates automate ranking of causal handles, replacing exhaustive steering sweeps?
