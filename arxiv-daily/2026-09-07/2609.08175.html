Key Finding · 核心发现

Safe harness self-evolution stalls not because improvements are scarce — but because certifying them from finite data is prohibitively hard.

改进机会并不稀缺，瓶颈在于用有限的评估证据安全地“认证”一次修改——低采纳率通常是一个评估证据问题，而非改进机会耗尽。

Why This Matters

Reframes “low adoption” in self-evolving LLM agents: evaluation evidence, not opportunity, is the binding constraint — with a diagnostic toolkit to tell the two apart.

Critical Edge

Empirical scale is thin for the conclusions drawn: two certified events across 288 outputs, four update sequences, and the headline −0.469 adoption effect rests on a non-causal grouping (5 vs 3 events).

The abstract's divergence claim is worst-case over a constructed two-task family — not an observed phenomenon in real systems.

Methodology 方法

Theory-first: a generate → certify → select decomposition, stress-tested by hash-locked preregistered experiments.

The agent is Aₖ = (M, Cₖ) — a frozen LLM M plus an evolvable harness (prompts, tools, code, orchestration) Cₖ. A target T = (λ, δ) defines a qualified modification: improve failed tasks by ≥ λ while changing retained tasks by ≤ δ. The analysis splits every update into three measurable stages: reachability (can the incumbent generate a qualified modification?), certification (does finite rollout data prove it?), and selection.

Two practical protocols — Two-Gate and Measured-margin — adopt a modification only when simultaneous confidence rectangles clear the target, giving one-step and cumulative expected-reward lower bounds at explicit risk budgets. The empirical side runs two preregistered studies (DS-1000; WorkBuddy–DSH) with SHA-256-frozen manifests, disjoint generation/evaluation/audit splits, and sandboxed no-network execution.

  • The bottleneck is interval width, not point estimates: 67.7%–76.0% of modifications pass both the high-budget gate's own estimates and the independent audit — but simultaneous confidence intervals stay too wide to certify most of them.
  • More generation can hurt: interval widths scale as O(√(log(N/β)/n_eff)), so enlarging the candidate pool at fixed evaluation budget can shrink the certifiable set and lower the certified-success bound.
  • Adoption is followed by reduced opportunity (cross-step): target-meeting fraction of new modifications falls from .906 → .438 after two update opportunities (mean change −0.469, 90% CI [−.891, −.063]) — descriptive, not causal.
  • Second system reproduces the gap: WorkBuddy–DSH — all 4 banked persona modifications pass audit point estimates, 0 certified (intervals degenerate to [0, 1]).
  • Worst-case certification cost diverges: any level-β certifier needs E[N_eval] ≥ Zₖ/(10uₖ) rollouts — unbounded as reward approaches the ceiling; a realistic 500-modification fixed-suite design needs ≈ 6.6 × 10⁸ rollouts.

Strengths 优点

  • Clean, falsifiable generation → certification → selection decomposition with measurable quantities (P, Q, H, R) practitioners can actually instrument.
  • Proves both positive guarantees (one-step and cumulative reward lower bounds, valid under abstention) and informative negatives: pool-size monotonicity failure, divergent certification cost, non-composability across steps.
  • Reachability confidence bounds hold simultaneously over all targets — post-hoc target comparison without multiple-testing penalty.
  • Empirical hygiene far above community norms: preregistration, hash-verified manifests, disjoint splits, sandboxing, invalid outputs kept in denominators.
  • Unusually honest interpretation — every claim is paired with what it cannot establish.

Limitations 局限

  • Theory assumes a fixed task distribution, fixed known reward, conditionally i.i.d. pools; drift, reward misspecification, and adaptive archive-driven generation are deferred to appendices.
  • Retention criterion controls the average absolute change — subgroup-level regressions only partially addressed.
  • Small empirical base: two certified events, four sequences, a four-modification bank, one model per study; single-draw audit statistics proxy rather than estimate the theoretical quantities.
  • The Zₖ/(10uₖ) bound is a lower bound on a constructed family — no matching achievable rate, practical tightness unknown.
  • No new mechanism proposed — diagnoses why safe self-evolution stalls, but doesn't demonstrate a design that raises certified adoption.

Questions for Further Exploration 开放问题

  • Do adaptive generation processes (archive search à la DGM, feedback revision loops) break the i.i.d.-pool assumption in a way that systematically raises or lowers realized reachability?
  • Can a certifier family (nearly) match the Zₖ/(10uₖ) worst-case lower bound, closing the gap between Hoeffding-style sufficient sample sizes and the minimax bound?
  • Would randomizing adoption decisions replicate the cross-step finding that adoptions are followed by lower target-membership fractions, or is it an artifact of tie-breaking toward large estimated gains?
  • Do the guarantees survive when the task distribution drifts or responds to the agent's changed behavior — is there a realistic drift radius for cumulative improvement?
  • Can audit statistics be upgraded from single observed outcomes to multi-rollout estimates, so empirical diagnostics actually estimate the theoretical estimands?
