Why this matters

Bit-level determinism underpins LLM batch invariance, rollout/train-engine log-prob agreement, and reproducible multi-machine training.

位级确定性直接关系 LLM 推理的 batch invariance、RL rollout/训练引擎的 log-prob 一致性，以及多机训练结果可复现。

Critical edge

Standalone, the bit-exact GEMM still runs 7–44% slower than cuBLAS above 5 GFLOP — the speed wins only appear once kernels fuse an epilogue.

01Methodology — four layers down

  1. 01
    Characterize

    FP addition is commutative but not associative, so a reduction's bits are fixed by its dependence tree — thread registers → warp lane shuffles → cross-warp shared memory. The descriptor GEMMDesc captures everything that fixes that tree (split-K cuts, partial/merge dtypes, instruction_k, GEMV layout); pipeline depth and warp specialization are proven tree-neutral.

  2. 02
    Reconstruct

    Runtime profiling narrows each cuBLAS call to an algorithm family; adversarial probes (a +L/−L cancelling pair and a sub-half-ulp residual walked along K) read out group boundaries. 8.25M cost-model queries enumerate the (SM, shape) → descriptor map, rebuilt as a Triton GEMM family over fp16/bf16/fp8.

  3. 03
    Enforce

    An inner_tree mode in Triton's backend pins balanced-tree lane reductions regardless of layout; a guarded layout pass then pulls the reduced axis inside one warp, deleting barriers and shared-memory round trips — bit-identically.

  4. 04
    Verify

    A ~2,500-line symbolic interpreter walks PTX and AMDGCN into canonical hashed tree signatures; anything unmodeled degrades to opaque, so equivalence is never falsely claimed. It plugs into Triton's autotuner as a pruning predicate.

Reduction = dependence tree T = T_reg ∘ T_warp ∘ T_smem — the tree, not the schedule, fixes the bits.

Stack: GB300 · GB200 · H100 · AMD gfx942 — cuBLAS 12/13 · Triton 3.8 · PyTorch 2.12 · CUDA 13. Workloads: 390 realistic LLM shapes from 16 open-weight models, 110,813 fuzz shapes, 97 kernels.

  • Determinism costs little against a same-language baseline: on 390 realistic LLM shapes above 5 GFLOP the bit-exact Triton GEMM runs at 56–93% of cuBLAS vs 61–88% for torch.compile's free-order Triton.
  • Fusion flips the story: over 96 fused GEMM+epilogue shapes the bit-exact kernel hits 95–168% of the cuBLAS-plus-separate-epilogue baseline (torch.compile: 85–125%), winning by eliminating one launch.
  • Enforcement is cheaper than lore: 19 of 27 kernels stay within 10% of free-order autotuning — far under the ~20% cost historically reported; the layout pass gains up to +42% on H100.
  • Checker soundness: zero false merges across 51,152 (GB300) + 4,500 (gfx942) configs; exact partition on GEMMs, but over-splits up to 23× on gemm_kgroup and 10× on FlashAttention.

Bonus find — a real cuBLAS bug

The nvjet split-K path (ALGO_ID 66) silently drops exactly K mod 64 tail terms at deep contraction: all-ones inputs at K=8648 return 8640. In a 496,906-shape sweep, 1,062 shapes came back wrong and every one of 5,709 observed losses was exactly K mod 64 — reproduced standalone on GB200/GB300/H100 under cuBLAS 12.8.5/13.1.1, no Triton involved.

03Strengths

  • Complete vertical story — dependence trees → bit-exact reconstruction → compiler enforcement → sound checkers → autotuner integration — each layer validating the previous.
  • The ulp-cancellation probing technique reads a black box's grouping structure directly; elegant and reusable far beyond cuBLAS.
  • Found, isolated, and responsibly disclosed a real vendor-library correctness bug with a standalone reproducer — an independent contribution.
  • Counterintuitive, useful result: with epilogue fusion, determinism and speed coexist (95–168% of baseline) against the community's ~20%-cost expectation.
  • Honest negative results (over-split ratios, fp8 mma divergence) reported rather than buried.

04Limitations

  • Reconstruction is tied to specific cuBLAS generations and SM architectures; each new GPU/library release needs a fresh 8.25M-query profiling campaign.
  • Soundness is relative to PTX/AMDGCN, not the SASS actually executed — driver-level changes stay outside the guarantee.
  • Tensor-core single-rounding semantics for fp16/bf16/tf32 are validated by measurement agreement, not vendor documentation (fp8 diverges on GB300).
  • FlashAttention and epilogue-heavy fusions get a theoretical treatment only — described, not enforced or checked at scale.
  • No end-to-end training or RL benefit demonstrated; the motivating 9-pp batch-invariance swing is cited from prior work, not reproduced.

05Questions for further exploration

  • Q1Are recovered arch profiles stable across cuBLAS point releases and driver updates — could the probe campaign run in CI as a regression check?
  • Q2Can the checker push below PTX to undocumented SASS, or could a formal vendor contract replace the measured tensor-core rounding semantics?
  • Q3With rollout and training engines pinned to one descriptor, does log-prob agreement translate into measurably more stable RL policy optimization?
  • Q4Could TorchInductor ship inner-tree enforcement and checker pruning by default — and at what cost, given 10–23× over-splitting on attention-style kernels?
  • Q5Does the black-box probing extend to cuDNN/rocBLAS convolutions and attention, where scale factors break the pure-reduction model?
