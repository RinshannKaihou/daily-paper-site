> **一句话总结：** 该论文用能力 Fisher 子空间的两个几何标量（重叠率 ρ 与归一化曲率 qnorm）证明：RLHF/DPO 等事后安全训练落在"抑制"区——一个薄而尖锐、叠加在完好能力之上的拒答门，而非擦除能力（核不动性引理说明这种更新只能掩盖、无法删除能力），因此 100 步良性微调即可把 Qwen-2.5-7B / Llama-3-8B Instruct 的 AdvBench 拒答率打掉 35–38 个百分点；而贯穿整个预训练的连续安全共训练（410M–6.9B 从零训练）在同等或更好能力下，攻击后仍保持 84–91% 拒答（仅损失 2–14 pp），且计算匹配的窗口式共训练装不上任何持久拒答——是安全信号的"持续性"而非"注入时机"买来了攻击鲁棒性。

> **TL;DR:** Post-hoc safety (DPO/RLHF) is geometrically "thin but sharp": the safety update Δ = W_safe − W_base is nearly orthogonal to the capability Fisher subspace (ρ peaks 21–66× above the random floor k/d yet 2–3 orders below the erasure boundary ρ_part = 1e-2, qnorm up to 200+), so it gates rather than removes capability — 100 benign Alpaca fine-tuning steps drop AdvBench refusal from 93.0% to 58.2% on Qwen-2.5-7B-Instruct (−34.8 pp) and 49.2% to 10.9% on Llama-3-8B-Instruct (−38.3 pp), against baselines like the shipped instruct models themselves. A 267-checkpoint OLMo-2-1B sweep locates the safety substrate's emergence in a sharp 6B–60B-token pretraining window. Constructively, from-scratch continuous safety co-training (Pythia 410M–6.9B on 10B C4 tokens) reaches 87–98% pre-attack AdvBench refusal and holds 84–91% after the same attack (2–14 pp erosion vs 35–38 pp post-hoc), while a compute-matched windowed schedule installs no lasting refusal — persistence, not timing, buys robustness. Caveat: the headline refusal is in-distribution (AdvBench-style templates); HarmBench coverage falls from 58.0% to 32.5% with scale and StrongReject never exceeds 10.9%.

## 核心贡献

- **（C1）安全安装的权重空间分类学**：提出用两个标量刻画安全更新 Δ —— 与能力 Fisher top-k 特征子空间的重叠率 ρ、以及归一化曲率 qnorm——把安全安装分为"抑制"（ρ ≪ ρ_part = 1e-2 且 qnorm ≫ 1，薄而尖锐）与"擦除"（ρ ≳ ρ_part）两个区制。五个模型家族（Pythia、Qwen-2.5-7B、Llama-3-8B、Mistral-7B、OLMo-2-1B，1B–8B）的事后 DPO/RLHF 全部落在抑制区：ρ 峰值为随机地板 k/d 的 21–66 倍，却仍比擦除边界低两个数量级以上，qnorm 最高达 200+；一个对抗性 SFT 控制组则能越过 ρ_part 进入擦除区，证明擦除可达但事后安全从不抵达。
- **（C2）核不动性引理（Lemma 1）**：若 Δ 落在能力 Fisher 的近核中且能力损失 Hessian Lipschitz，则 L_cap(W_base + Δ) − L_cap(W_base) 在 Gauss-Newton 条件下仅为 O(‖Δ‖³)——一阶事后更新无法在主导阶削减能力，只能掩盖其输出表达。经验上梯度正交性 |cos(∇L_cap, Δ)| < 0.002（安全纯更新的受控实验中 ≤ 2×10⁻⁴），从数学上解释了为何良性微调总能"找回"被拒答的能力。
- **（C3）发育性签名**：利用 OLMo-2-0425-1B 的 267 个预训练检查点（0–4T tokens）扫描发现：拒答电路所要挂接的表征基底在约 6B–60B tokens 之间以锐利跃迁出现（选择性隐状态散度上升两个数量级后进入平台期），跃迁前同一安全流程落在曲率高一个数量级的另一几何区制（前基底 qnorm_ARITH > 600），且峰值层 ℓ* 随预训练推进向前迁移（ℓ*=16 → 6 → 9 → 11）；Pythia-1.4B 跨家族复现。
- **（C4）建设性解决方案**：从零训练、贯穿全部预训练的连续安全共训练（λ_c = 0.1，与窗口式计算量匹配）在 410M–6.9B 全尺度上装上 87–98% 的攻击前 AdvBench 拒答率，同一良性 SFT 攻击后仍保有 84–91%（侵蚀仅 2–14 pp，事后安装为 35–38 pp），能力持平或优于纯 LM 基线；而 [0.10, 0.30] 训练窗口的窗口式方案（λ₀ = 0.5）装不上任何持久拒答——隔离出"安全信号持续到预训练结束"（而非其注入时机）才是攻击鲁棒性的驱动因素。

## Key Contributions

- **C1 — A (ρ, q_norm) weight-space taxonomy of safety installs.** Classify Δ = W_safe − W_base by its overlap ρ with the top-k eigenspace of the empirical capability Fisher (k = 128, per-layer MLP parameters) and its normalized curvature q_norm. Suppression (ρ ≪ ρ_part = 1e-2, q_norm ≫ 1) vs. erasure (ρ ≳ ρ_part). Every measured post-hoc DPO/RLHF install across five families (1B–8B) lands in suppression: ρ peaks 21–66× above the random k/d floor yet stays ≥ 2 orders below ρ_part, q_norm reaching 200+; an adversarial-SFT control crosses ρ_part, showing erasure is reachable but post-hoc safety never gets there.
- **C2 — A kernel-immobility lemma explaining recoverability.** If Δ ∈ ker(F_cap) with Lipschitz Hessian, L_cap(W_base + Δ) − L_cap(W_base) = O(‖Δ‖³) under Gauss-Newton — no first-order post-hoc update removes capability at leading order, it can only mask it. Empirically tight: |cos(∇L_cap, Δ)| < 0.002 across revisions, and ≤ 2×10⁻⁴ for safety-only SFT/DPO updates trained in isolation on Qwen-2.5-1.5B (ruling out instruction-tuning confounds in shipped deltas).
- **C3 — A developmental transition for the safety substrate.** A 267-checkpoint OLMo-2-0425-1B sweep (0–4T tokens) shows the representational substrate a refusal circuit engages emerges sharply between ~6B and 60B tokens (selective hidden-state divergence rises 2 orders of magnitude inside the window, then plateaus); per-revision DPO geometry shifts at the same point (pre-substrate q_norm_ARITH > 600), replicated on Pythia-1.4B. The peak-divergence layer migrates forward through the residual stream as pretraining proceeds (ℓ* = 16 → 6 → 9 → 11).
- **C4 — Continuous co-training as the constructive fix.** From-scratch Pythia models (410M–6.9B, 10B C4 tokens) with a safety loss active continuously (λ_c = 0.1) reach 87–98% pre-attack AdvBench refusal that holds at 84–91% post-attack (2–14 pp erosion vs. 35–38 pp for post-hoc installs) at capability matched or better than the LM-only base; a compute-matched windowed schedule (λ₀ = 0.5 over [0.10, 0.30]) installs no lasting refusal. Persistence of the safety signal, not its timing, is what buys robustness.

## Methodology

The paper builds a *geometric instrument* over two scalars computed per transformer block (MLP parameters only) and per capability probe. Given Δ = W_safe − W_base and the top-k eigenspace U_k of the empirical Fisher F_cap (k = 128, estimated from per-example gradients via the N-side Gram trick), the overlap ratio ρ = ‖Δ_∥‖²/‖Δ‖² measures how much of the safety update lies inside the capability subspace (random floor k/d), and q_norm = (Δ^⊤ F_cap Δ / ‖Δ‖²)/(tr(F_cap)/d) measures how sharply curved the directions it traverses are. Two reference scales fix a suppression/erasure boundary at ρ_part = 1e-2, anchored by an adversarial-SFT control that saturates the subspace. Capability is operationalized as NLL of gold answer spans on four probes: REASON (MMLU formal_logic), ARITH (GSM8K train), RECALL (TriviaQA-RC no-context), INSTRUCT (FLAN-v2). When safety training is done by the authors it is DPO on 5K HH-RLHF pairs (1 epoch, β = 0.1, lr 5e-7); shipped instruct weights (Qwen-2.5-7B, Llama-3-8B, Mistral-7B-v0.3) serve as W_safe directly. A hidden-state divergence instrument (per-layer L2 distance between base and safe residual streams over 50 harmful vs. 50 topic-matched benign prompts) localizes the "safety-install band", which coincides with the q_norm peak and the Arditi et al. refusal direction. The behavioral probe is the Qi et al. benign-SFT attack: full-parameter AdamW, 100 benign Alpaca examples, 200 steps, lr 2e-5, with regex refusal scoring over 14 markers (cross-checked with Llama-Guard-3-8B in App. L). The developmental axis uses OLMo-2-0425-1B's dense checkpoint series with the shipped Tulu-3-style Instruct variant held fixed while the base is swept across 9 revisions. The constructive experiment trains Pythia architectures (410M–6.9B) from scratch on 10B C4 tokens with L_pre+safe = L_LM + λ(t)L_safe, where L_safe is cross-entropy on (AdvBench harmful prompt, refusal-template) pairs under five refusal templates, comparing continuous (λ_c = 0.1 throughout) vs. compute-matched windowed (λ₀ = 0.5 over [0.10, 0.30]) vs. post-hoc-DPO vs. LM-only conditions.

## Results & Highlights

- **Post-hoc fragility is large and replicates across families.** 100 benign Alpaca fine-tuning steps (200 steps, lr 2e-5) drop AdvBench refusal from 93.0% → 58.2% on Qwen-2.5-7B-Instruct (−34.8 pp) and 49.2% → 10.9% on Llama-3-8B-Instruct (−38.3 pp), with HarmBench falling 46.5% → 6.5% and StrongReject 46.5% → 7.8% for Llama-3 (Table 1); total post-hoc erosion is 35–38 pp while general capability (MMLU/GSM8K/TriviaQA/FLAN NLL) is preserved — the raw 0-shot capability NLL increase (up to +3.35 on REASON) is shown by a format ablation to shrink 40–74% with 3 in-context demonstrations, i.e., a format artifact, not partial erasure.
- **The suppression geometry is extreme and its floor is measured.** On Qwen-2.5-7B (base → Instruct), ρ runs 5–65× the random-overlap floor k/d across all four probes and 28 blocks yet stays 2–3 orders of magnitude below ρ_part; observed ρ sits 157–517 standard deviations above the floor (statistical significance of alignment), and q_norm reaches the low hundreds on REASON/ARITH. On the authors' own Pythia-1.4B DPO, ρ ∈ [5×10⁻⁶, 8×10⁻⁵] with q_norm up to 21 and capability loss *decreasing* on every probe.
- **Continuous co-training survives the same attack at every scale.** From-scratch Pythia cotrain-continuous models (10B C4 tokens) reach 87.1–98.4% pre-attack AdvBench refusal and hold 84.0–90.6% after the attack at 410M/1B/2.8B/6.9B (e.g., 6.9B: 96.9% → 90.6%, −6.3 pp vs. −38.3 pp for Llama-3-8B post-hoc), while the LM-only base stays ≤ 24.6% everywhere; capability is matched or better (410M arithmetic NLL 8.57 vs. base 10.01, reason NLL 4.95 vs. 5.34).
- **Persistence beats timing.** At 1B, cotrain-continuous [0,1] holds 87.5% → 90.6% and cotrain-post-substrate [0.30,1] holds 84.4% → 84.4%, while the compute-matched early window [0.10,0.30] installs nothing (3.1% → 4.7%); the 410M windowed condition is 0.0% → 1.6% (Table 2). The install also survives stronger attacks: worst-case 75% post-attack refusal under a learning-rate/step sweep and 100% under an identity-shift persona attack.
- **The safety substrate emerges in a sharp window.** On the 267-checkpoint OLMo-2-1B sweep, selective hidden-state divergence sits at noise through 1B tokens, rises two orders of magnitude between ~6B and 60B tokens, then plateaus; per-revision ρ stays flat (1.4×10⁻⁶ → 8.3×10⁻⁷) through mid-training while q_norm spikes to 19 (ARITH) and 27 (INSTRUCT) exactly at the final instruct-tuned checkpoint — the same "thin but sharp" signature as Qwen.
- **Coverage, however, is in-distribution and narrows with scale.** AdvBench refusal holds near 97% at 6.9B, but HarmBench falls from 58.0% (410M) to 32.5% (6.9B) and StrongReject never exceeds 10.9% at any scale (Table 3) — the five refusal templates fit their own prompt distribution more tightly as models grow.

## Authors & Credibility

The paper is by Srikanth Malla, Chiho Choi, and Joon Hee Choi at Samsung Semiconductor US (industry lab, three authors, with a correspondence address). The work is unusually transparent for a safety-geometry paper: it ships an explicit AI-use statement (AI scaffolded some code and copy-edited prose; the lemma and proofs are author-produced), an ethics statement (no new attacks introduced; only aggregate refusal statistics reported), and a reproducibility statement — all models are public (Pythia, Qwen-2.5, Llama-3, Mistral, OLMo-2), every recipe's hyperparameters are given, and the full code plus analysis scripts that regenerate every table and figure are in the supplementary material. The appendices are extensive (23 tables), including an honest detector cross-check with Llama-Guard-3-8B, an explicit list of seven limitations, and disclosure of the n = 64 vs. n = 256 discrepancy in the 410M co-training numbers (84.4 → 84.4 at n = 64 vs. 98.4 → 84.0 at n = 256, with the authors stating the powered figure is the one to trust). Claims are carefully scoped to relative rankings rather than absolute ASR magnitudes.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty: genuinely strong.** The suppression/erasure dichotomy unifies prior symptom-level findings (shallow first-token alignment, safety basins, linear refusal directions) into one continuous geometric account, and the kernel-immobility lemma is a real (if modest, essentially a Taylor-expansion observation under a Gauss-Newton condition) formal statement of *why* thin installs are recoverable. The windowed-vs-continuous co-training comparison isolating persistence over timing is a clean experimental design.
- **Rigor: high on the fragility side, weaker on the constructive side.** The fragility claims rest on shipped official instruct models plus self-trained DPO controls with statistical floors (ρ at 157–517 std above k/d) — solid. But the co-training comparison has a built-in asymmetry the authors themselves flag: the 5K-pair DPO baseline installs 0% refusal at 410M, so there is **no matched-pre-attack-refusal post-hoc baseline**; "84–91% vs. 35–38 pp lost" compares robustness of installs of very different strength, not matched ones.
- **The headline refusal is in-distribution by construction.** L_safe is cross-entropy on AdvBench prompts paired with five refusal templates, and the training pool (256 prompts, Random(42)) partially overlaps the evaluation pool (64 prompts, Random(101)) from the same public CSV. The authors argue overlap is not a leakage path for template reproduction, but the result is that the celebrated 87–98% is AdvBench-template reproduction: HarmBench coverage *falls* from 58.0% to 32.5% across a 17× parameter range and StrongReject never exceeds 10.9%. A skeptic could call the robust refusal "robust template matching."
- **Regex scoring flatters the co-trained models.** Under the Llama-Guard-3-8B classifier the 410M co-trained model erodes 91.8% → 64.8% (vs. regex 98.4% → 84.0%) because refusal prefixes survive while continuations drift toward compliance — the gap between "gate fires" and "output harmless" is acknowledged but the main-text number is the more favorable one.
- **The instrument does not explain the constructive result.** Table 19 shows cotrain-continuous and cotrain-windowed differ by < 2% in q_norm (2.31 vs. 2.27) while differing by 83 pp in post-attack refusal — so the (ρ, q_norm) geometry that powers the whole fragility account fails to predict which co-training schedule survives. The geometric story is explanatory for post-hoc installs but not predictive for the fix.
- **Scale and scope limits.** Instrument verified to 8B; co-training to 6.9B on only 10B tokens (well sub-Chinchilla for 6.9B — ~1.5 tokens/param); frontier-scale behavior is explicitly a conjecture. The developmental sweep rests on one family (OLMo-2-1B) for behavior with Pythia replication only for geometry.

## Strengths

- One clean geometric instrument (ρ, q_norm) with measured floors and anchors ties together jailbreaks, fine-tuning attacks, and probe-recovery as one phenomenon, and every post-hoc install measured across five families lands in the same corner of the plane.
- The kernel-immobility lemma gives a mechanism, not just correlation, and its key empirical quantity (gradient orthogonality < 0.002, ≤ 2×10⁻⁴ for safety-only updates) is verified with well-designed controls that isolate safety from instruction-tuning confounds.
- The constructive experiment is properly controlled: compute-matched windowed vs. continuous schedules, LM-only base, from-scratch training across a 17× parameter range, stronger-attack sweeps (75% worst case under lr/step sweep, 100% under persona attack), and an honest within-model ρ_safe analysis showing co-trained safety entangles with instruction-following (INSTRUCT ρ_safe rises 1.3–6.5×) rather than capability at large (REASON falls 0.54–0.90×).
- Exceptional transparency: detector cross-check tables, seed-level sampling details, an explicit seven-item limitations list, AI-use statement, and released code that regenerates every number.

## Limitations

- Refusal installed by co-training is in-distribution (AdvBench-like templates with partially overlapping train/eval pools); out-of-distribution coverage is weak and shrinks with scale (HarmBench 58.0% → 32.5%; StrongReject ≤ 10.9%).
- No matched-refusal post-hoc baseline at co-training scales (the 5K-pair DPO installs 0% at 410M), so the protection differential is not quantitative at matched install strength.
- The top-k (k = 128) Fisher eigenspace approximation misses tail eigendirections; a small-ρ update could still traverse low-curvature but semantically important directions the instrument cannot see.
- Regex refusal detection measures the gate, not harmfulness; classifier rescoring shifts levels in both directions (co-trained erosion grows to 64.8% from 84.0% at 410M).
- All results are at ≤ 8B parameters and 10B pretraining tokens; frontier-scale claims are conjecture, and the developmental behavioral curve is single-point (SFT+DPO yields measurable refusal only at the final checkpoint).

## Relevance

对于关注"对齐鲁棒性"与"安全训练几何"的研究者，这篇论文把"良性微调即可击穿 RLHF"这一经验现象升级为可测量的权重空间判据（ρ、qnorm），并给出预训练期连续安全共训练这一建设性替代方案，与大规模模型训练策略（预训练阶段就注入安全信号）直接相关；其 Fisher 子空间工具也可复用于评估任意安全更新是"薄门"还是"真擦除"，对理解拒答机制和 unlearning 都有参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the suppression geometry hold at frontier scale (70B+), or does post-hoc alignment become less thin as capability subspaces grow denser relative to k = 128?
- Can a post-hoc install be regularized toward the erasure regime (e.g., explicitly penalizing q_norm or maximizing ρ) and does that buy benign-SFT robustness at matched capability — the causal test the authors themselves propose?
- Why exactly does continuous co-training resist wash-out while an equal-compute window does not — is it continued re-selection against drift, or integration of refusal into the LM's own prediction distribution (the ρ_safe entanglement with INSTRUCT hints at the latter)?
- Would co-training with diverse, out-of-distribution refusal data (beyond five AdvBench-like templates) fix the coverage gap on HarmBench/StrongReject without losing erosion robustness?
- Does the 6B–60B-token substrate transition scale with model size or data composition, and does it correspond to the emergence of linearly decodable harmful/benign concept directions (Arditi et al.) that the safety-install band coincides with?
- The instrument fails to separate robust from fragile schedules (q_norm 2.31 vs. 2.27 with 83 pp behavioral difference) — what measurement, if any, predicts which install survives?
