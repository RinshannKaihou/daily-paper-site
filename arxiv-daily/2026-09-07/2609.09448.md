> **一句话总结：** 本文提出两种从 LLM 智能体残差流内部表征预测多轮任务成败的零开销方法——轨迹动力学特征（LTD, 28 维）与动作表征探针（ARP, PCA-64 + 逻辑探针）——在 InterCode 的 Bash/SQL/Python 三个交互式基准和三个开源编码模型上，AUROC 与 Brier 分数在全部 9 个设置中均超过 token 概率与 HTC 轨迹基线（如 Qwen-14B SQL 上 ARP AUROC 0.842 vs HTC 0.743）。

> **TL;DR:** The authors probe the residual stream of coding agents (Qwen2.5-Coder-14B/7B, DeepSeek-Coder-6.7B) on InterCode-Bash/SQL/Python to predict eventual task success. Two internal-representation methods — Latent Trajectory Dynamics (28 kinematic features of hidden-state drift) and an Action Representation Probe (mean-pooled action-span states, PCA-64, logistic probe) — beat Calibrated Logprob and the HTC trajectory baseline on AUROC and Brier in all nine model×environment settings (e.g., +7.1 AUROC over HTC on Qwen-14B SQL; +12.9 AUROC over HTC on Qwen-7B Bash), at a claimed <0.05 ms per-decision probe cost with no prompt changes or extra rollouts. Caveat: ECE wins are not universal, gains rely on white-box access, and only coding tasks and two external baselines are tested.

## 核心贡献

- **提出两种基于内部表征的智能体置信度提取框架**：LTD（Latent Trajectory Dynamics）用残差流状态在推理/动作/反馈各阶段间的余弦位移、相对位移、层间漂移比、路径效率等运动学统计构成 28 维特征；ARP（Action Representation Probe）直接在动作 span 末端的最继行表征上做均值池化 → 标准化 → PCA-64 → L2 逻辑回归探针 → Platt 校准。
- **在统一的无泄漏评测协议下证明内部状态优于表层信号**：采用 5 折嵌套交叉验证、冻结分层折、内外折完全隔离的标准化/PCA/校准拟合，在 3 个模型 × 3 个环境的全部 9 个设置中，内部方法均取得最佳 AUROC 与 Brier 分数。
- **给出零开销在线部署论证**：在线时表征在 lm_head 投影前已驻留 GPU 显存，探针仅需 ~3.3×10⁵ FLOPs（d=5120, k=64，<0.05 ms），可作为生成循环中的非阻塞钩子，实现失败即时检测与人机协同干预，无需改动提示词或多次采样。
- **严格的数据卫生审计**：剔除 InterCode-SQL 中 19 条参考答案损坏的任务及 Python 中测试框架异常的任务，所有方法在完全相同的冻结折上比较，消除评测污染。

## Key Contributions

- Two complementary internal-confidence frameworks: **LTD** summarizes geometric stability and drift of residual-stream states across an interaction trajectory into 28 kinematic features (cosine progression, inter-layer drift ratio, cumulative path efficiency, phase-transition velocity); **ARP** decodes success directly from final-layer residual states at action-span endpoints (submit tokens excluded to prevent shortcut learning), mean-pooled, standardized, PCA-projected to 64 dims, and read out by an L2 logistic probe with a monotonic Platt map.
- A **leak-free benchmarking protocol**: 5-fold stratified frozen outer folds, inner 4-fold hyperparameter selection by negative Brier score, and all learned preprocessing (standardization, PCA, calibration) fitted strictly within training folds — every baseline and internal method evaluated on the identical task population with one out-of-fold prediction per trajectory.
- Evidence that **internal representations carry success information absent from outputs**: an internal method achieves the best AUROC and Brier score in all 9 model×environment settings, while aggregate token probability (Calibrated Logprob) is the weakest signal and trajectory-level token dynamics (HTC) sits in between.
- A **deployment-feasibility analysis** (Appendix I): in live serving the action-endpoint residual is already resident in GPU memory before the unembedding projection, so ARP adds only standardization + PCA projection + logistic evaluation (< 3.3×10⁵ FLOPs, < 0.05 ms on GPU for d=5120, k=64) as a non-blocking hook — no replay pass, prompt alteration, or multi-sample rollouts.

## Methodology

The agent runs on InterCode-Bash/SQL/Python with vLLM (single A100, greedy decoding, temperature 0), producing trajectories of (generation, observation) steps up to 10 turns. Residual-stream states are recovered offline by teacher-forcing each stored trajectory through the same model (bit-exact replay), with endpoints marked at observation, reasoning, action, and feedback phases.

**LTD** computes, from adjacent step states: cosine displacement (1 − cos similarity) and relative displacement (‖h_{t+1}−h_t‖/‖h_t‖), summarized over reasoning/commitment/action/feedback transitions via means, final values, variability, and trends; plus inter-layer drift ratio (end-to-end layer displacement over cumulative layer path length), cumulative path efficiency (net trajectory displacement over total step distance), and phase-transition velocities — 28 features fed to an L2 logistic model matching HTC's predictor class.

**ARP** extracts the final-layer residual state at the final token of each action span, excludes terminal submit actions (retaining the last action as fallback for immediate-submit episodes), mean-pools over the episode, standardizes with training-fold statistics, projects onto 64 training-fold PCA components, and fits an L2 logistic probe with a monotone (a ≥ 0) Platt calibration so AUROC ranking is preserved.

**Baselines**: Calibrated Logprob (token-weighted mean of per-token probabilities over non-terminal generations, mapped by 1D logistic regression) and HTC (48 features summarizing the token-probability trace: confidence dynamics, first/last-step stats, stability, structure; Zhang et al. 2026). **Metrics**: AUROC (discrimination), Brier score, ECE (10 equal-width bins). Data hygiene: 19 corrupt InterCode-SQL reference tasks (indices 54, 83, 164, …) and Python tasks with harness exceptions are excluded before fold construction.

## Results & Highlights

- On **InterCode-SQL with Qwen2.5-Coder-14B**, ARP reaches AUROC 0.842 vs 0.743 for HTC (+9.9 pts) and 0.624 for Calibrated Logprob; Brier improves to 0.144 vs HTC's 0.176 (−0.032) and Logprob's 0.197.
- On **InterCode-Bash with Qwen2.5-Coder-7B**, ARP achieves AUROC 0.704 vs HTC 0.575 (+12.9 pts) and Logprob 0.510 (near chance); Brier 0.190 vs HTC's 0.221 — the setting where internal representations help most on the weakest surface signal.
- On **InterCode-Python with DeepSeek-Coder-6.7B** (lowest success rate, 35.6%), ARP attains AUROC 0.784 vs HTC 0.645 (+13.9 pts), and Brier 0.179 vs HTC's 0.218 (−0.039).
- On **InterCode-SQL with DeepSeek-Coder-6.7B** (50.0% success), ARP hits AUROC 0.788 vs HTC's 0.726 (+6.2 pts), Brier 0.187 vs 0.211; notably LTD dips to 0.624 here — ARP is the more robust internal method, though LTD is best on DeepSeek-Bash (0.727 vs ARP's 0.650).
- Across all nine settings an internal method achieves the best AUROC and Brier score; typical AUROC gains of ARP over the strongest external baseline (HTC) range from +3.7 (Qwen-7B SQL: 0.837 vs 0.800) to +13.9 points.
- ECE results are mixed rather than dominant: Calibrated Logprob posts the lowest ECE in several settings (0.018 on Qwen-14B SQL vs ARP's 0.055; 0.006 on DeepSeek-Bash vs ARP's 0.042), so the internal advantage is in discrimination (AUROC) and Brier, not uniformly in calibration error.
- Deployment cost (Appendix I): the ARP inference is standardization + PCA-64 projection + logistic scalar ≈ 3.3×10⁵ FLOPs, < 0.05 ms on a standard GPU — negligible relative to generating the action tokens themselves.

## Authors & Credibility

Priyanka Mary Mammen (UMass Amherst) with Emil Joswin and Srujananjali Medicherla (both listed as "Independent Research"). The paper is formatted for NeurIPS 2026 and the empirical work is careful for its scale: frozen stratified folds shared across all methods, nested hyperparameter selection, explicit corrupt-task audit of InterCode-SQL gold answers, and a thoughtful appendix on offline replay vs. online activation extraction. No code release is mentioned, and the small team / independent affiliations mean limited institutional vetting; the work builds directly on Azaria & Mitchell's internal-state truthfulness probing and Subramani et al.'s MICE-for-tools line.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: Solid but incremental. Probing hidden states for correctness is well-trodden (Azaria & Mitchell 2023; Sun et al. 2026 for reasoning steps); the contribution is porting it to multi-turn agentic trajectories with a clean LTD feature set and a submit-token-exclusion trick in ARP — a sensible engineering contribution rather than a new mechanism.
- **Rigor**: The evaluation protocol is unusually careful (leak-free nested CV, frozen folds, shared populations, corrupt-gold-task exclusion). However, only two external baselines are compared — Calibrated Logprob and HTC. Verbalized confidence, semantic entropy, and multi-sample self-consistency are cited in the intro as failing in agentic settings but never actually run, so the claim of outperforming "state-of-the-art token-level calibration methods" rests on a thin baseline set.
- **ECE overclaim risk**: The text says internal methods "outperform the external baselines on AUROC or Brier score in every model–environment setting" — accurate and carefully worded, but readers should note Calibrated Logprob achieves the lowest ECE in roughly a third of settings (0.018 on Qwen-14B SQL, 0.006/0.002 on DeepSeek Bash/Python), likely because near-chance predictors can still be well calibrated under class imbalance.
- **Missing ablations**: No layer-selection study for ARP (final layer only, despite LTD showing inter-layer drift is informative), no feature-group ablation within the 28 LTD features, no cross-model or cross-environment probe transfer, and no downstream demonstration that the confidence signal actually improves an intervention policy (e.g., early abort or human handoff) — the "reliability safeguard" utility is asserted, not measured.
- **Population quirk**: "replay-incompatible episodes" are dropped from the shared population before any method is fitted; the paper never quantifies how many trajectories this removes, leaving a small but unmeasured selection bias in favor of methods needing internal states.
- **Scope**: White-box access to open-weight 6.7B–14B coding models on InterCode (10-turn max) only; no web/tool agents, no frontier or API-only models, and coding tasks have deterministic unit-test ground truth — the easiest possible verification regime. The online-deployment story (<0.05 ms hook) is an analysis, not an empirical online measurement.
- No explicit limitations section; reproducibility is medium — models, datasets, and protocol are precisely specified, but no code or fold files are released.

## Strengths

- Clean, leak-free nested cross-validation protocol applied identically to baselines and internal methods — the comparison is fair by construction.
- ARP is strikingly simple (mean-pool action-endpoint states, PCA-64, logistic probe) yet delivers double-digit AUROC gains in several settings — a result that invites immediate adoption and replication.
- Genuinely zero-overhead design: no prompt modification, no multi-sample rollouts, and a concrete FLOPs/ms cost accounting for online use.
- Thoughtful data hygiene: auditing and removing 19 corrupt InterCode-SQL gold answers protects against penalizing correct agent executions.
- Deterministic, execution-based ground truth eliminates LLM-as-judge noise from the calibration evaluation entirely.

## Limitations

- Coding-only evaluation on InterCode with short horizons (max 10 turns); no evidence yet for web agents, tool-use, or longer-horizon settings where failure modes are more diverse.
- Requires white-box access to residual streams — inapplicable to API-only agents, and probes are fit per model×environment with no transfer tested.
- Only two external baselines; sampling-based uncertainty (self-consistency, semantic entropy) and verbalized confidence are discussed but not benchmarked.
- ECE improvements are inconsistent across settings; the dominance claim holds for AUROC/Brier.
- No end-to-end use case (e.g., confidence-triggered rollback or human escalation) to validate the practical value of the monitor.
- Modest absolute discrimination in the hardest settings (AUROC ~0.65–0.72 on Qwen-7B/DeepSeek Bash and Python) may be insufficient alone for safety-critical gating.

## Relevance

直接命中「LLM 智能体可靠性 + 可解释性」交叉方向：用线性探针读取残差流来预测多轮智能体任务成败，与内部表征/机制可解释性、智能体不确定性量化和可信部署监控的兴趣高度契合，其零开销探针设计对实际部署中的失败检测与人工接管策略有直接参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does an ARP probe trained on one model or environment (e.g., Qwen-14B on SQL) transfer zero-shot to another model, environment, or domain like web agents, or must probes always be re-fit?
- Would combining LTD's kinematic features with ARP's pooled representations in one probe improve AUROC beyond either method, especially on DeepSeek-Bash where LTD wins and ARP lags?
- Can confidence-aware intervention (abort, retry, or human escalation when ARP probability drops below a threshold) measurably improve end-task success rate or safety metrics in deployment?
- Which layers encode the most success-relevant signal — is the final layer optimal, or do mid-layer residual states (as LTD's inter-layer drift hints) carry complementary information?
- How do these internal signals behave under distribution shift (unseen task types, adversarial environments), and under non-greedy decoding (temperature > 0) where token logprobs become noisier?
