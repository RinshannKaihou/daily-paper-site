Why This Matters

Self-improving agents can leave behind auditable, frozen artifacts — a hygiene template for trustworthy, reproducible long-running autonomous research systems.

from Relevance · 自进化智能体方向

⚠ Critical Edge

Off-distribution, distillation loses: the frozen text (149.7) underperforms its own source program (112.1) on BBOB Rastrigin — and only one of three held-out landscapes survives statistical correction.

from Critical Assessment & Limitations

Methodology practice → gate → distill → freeze

The unit of transfer is not code but ~200 words of frozen strategy text, sealed before any evaluation.

The setting is low-budget black-box optimization: an unknown 8-D objective on [−5,5]8, only 20 sequential evaluations, scored by final simple regret.

01
Executable practiceA fixed practice agent reads the full record (code, trajectories, failures, decisions) and writes a challenger optimizer; challenger vs incumbent get equal budget on fresh development objectives — challenger kept only if mean regret is lower.
02
GateA pre-registered validation rule scores the champion on fresh seeds. Only gate-passing runs proceed; failures become a published audit, never "harnesses".
03
One-shot distillationChampion program + practice log are distilled once into a second-person strategy text — no search over alternative texts — audited for implementation jargon.
04
FreezeInterface, parser, bounds, and inference settings sealed; at evaluation only the observation history changes. The text cannot react to test feedback.

Harness B (205 words) shares zero sentences with Harness A yet matches its tier — both beat Base and plain GP-BO on all four landscapes (Holm-adjusted).

  • Not "any optimization prompt". A hand-written hint helps Flash (64.2→29.8) but harms Flash-Lite (85.9→142.4, p=.002); Harness A improves both.
  • Compute composes, doesn't substitute. Thinking/sandbox alone leaves Flash at ~54 regret; harness + sandbox + thinking reaches 16.1 (p=.014 vs text-only 36.0).
  • Held-out BBOB (N=10). Mean regret lower on all three landscapes, but only Gallagher-101 survives Holm (pH=.018) — the supported claim is "transfer into the BO tier, not BO dominance".
  • Sealed YouTube 7-D reward tuning. Lowest point-estimate regret at every checkpoint: 0.0495 vs Vizier 0.0894 at 20 evals — descriptive only (bootstrap resamples of one dataset).
  • Shortcut & mechanism audits. Exact-solve rate 0/30 (rules out recognizing textbook functions); the text reshapes query geometry — proposals within radius 2 of the incumbent rise 69%→79%, step-length CV falls 1.04→0.68.
  • Practice record matters. Distilling code alone is worse on Pro (32.7 vs 21.4) and collapses off-distribution on GPT-5 (65.3 vs 19.0).

Strengths

  • Clean controls. Base and Harness arms differ only by the distilled text; the manual-hint control (helps Flash, harms Flash-Lite) decisively rules out generic-prompt explanations.
  • Calibrated claims. "BO tier, not BO dominance"; Holm-corrected comparisons; null results framed as bounded, not absent.
  • Replication as a first-class result. Two independently accepted harnesses with disjoint texts at the same tier, plus a published gate-failure audit (Candidate C).
  • Behavioral evidence. Traces tie the text to search geometry (anchoring, step regularity) without overclaiming causality.

Limitations

  • Narrow scope. One budget (B=20), one dimension (8-D); the central effect rests on a single N=30 study, most public cells are N=10.
  • Quadratics are the easy case. The strongest "BO-range" equivalence is on the practice family; off-distribution, only 1 of 3 landscapes survives correction.
  • Distillation loses information. The retained program beats both harnesses off-distribution (112.1 vs 149.7 on BBOB Rastrigin); some ablation cells are unreleased or non-regenerable.
  • Baseline gaps. HEBO and TuRBO omitted; CMA-ES looks weakly tuned (68.0 — worse than unaided Flash); cross-provider transfer covers exactly one Claude model.
  • Production claim unauditable. YouTube case uses resamples of one sealed dataset; the gate threshold is validated on only two passes vs one failure.

Questions for Further Exploration

  1. Does the practice–gate–distill–freeze recipe hold at larger budgets (B=100+), higher dimensions, constrained spaces, or noisy objectives?
  2. What exactly does the practice record contribute over the code skeleton — and do failure narratives matter more than successes?
  3. Can the gate threshold be validated prospectively into a calibrated selection rule, rather than a diagnostic two-pass association?
  4. Frozen text plus classical optimizer state (e.g., GP posterior features) in the prompt — complementary composition or interference?
  5. Would re-distilling from multiple accepted champions close the off-distribution gap (149.7 vs the program's 112.1)?
