01 Methodology — the three-stage loop

Under a hard budget — 10M-word corpus, 100M cumulative word exposures — the system separates what is visible, what is supervised, and what is preserved, then lets Stage-II mechanisms dictate the Stage-III recipe.

I

Frontier model → 42.02

8-layer DeBERTa-v2-style MLM (35.46M params). Qwen3.5-9B compresses passages into shorter restatements; the freed word budget buys +20.42% more pairs (10,094 → 12,155) inside a fixed 423,520-word block. Backbone then frozen; only a zero-init residual branch stays trainable.

▼ findings feed forward
II

Principle discovery

Controlled interventions with window, seed, and RNG-state controls: exact repetition hurts true-source use (−0.81 nats) while aligned restatement helps (+0.80 nats); familiar accuracy ≠ reusable skill (unseen symbols 87.5% → 40.2% after naive continuation).

▼ principles → recipe
III

Principle-guided improvement → 42.25

Dense masking (176,607 tokens) for visibility, sparse focused supervision (28,590 tokens, weight 0.15) for learning, and KL preservation toward the frozen parent on ordinary inputs (λ=1). Only the 995,584-param residual branch updates — 2.73% of parameters.

03 Strengths
What holds up ✓
  • Reusable experimental template — visible input / supervised targets / preservation inputs decoupled, each with its own controlled comparison.
  • Mechanism work beyond accuracy — NLL true/unrelated-source advantage, split-window controls that preserve text while breaking relational access, causal hidden-state interventions with refitting checks.
  • Honest component-level accounting — gains-from-parent, advantage-over-continuation, and added-preservation-effect kept distinct; superseded explanations preserved as knowledge.
  • Word-exact bookkeeping — matched exposure down to the word, seed-matched init checks, RNG-state-controlled dropout, full release with seeds and full-precision CSVs.
04 Limitations
What to doubt ✗
  • Tiny margins, fragile base — 42.02 → 42.25; AoA scores 0.0000 for all nine models, so Overall rides on an 8/9-metric scale, and GlobalPIQA dominates the gain.
  • Single parent checkpoint — every complete evaluation continues one 42.02 model over two seeds; cross-parent generality untested.
  • Narrow preservation regime — the KL term helps only on ordinary inputs; on dense inputs it collapses acquisition. (S,S) never fully evaluated.
  • Scope & teacher dependence — one 36M-param English masked LM; the restatement corpus comes from an external Qwen3.5-9B teacher, shifting part of the learning signal out of the "10M words".
05 Open Questions for Further Exploration
  1. Does sparse > dense supervision survive retuning the focused-loss weight for dense, or is it an artifact of the 0.15 coefficient?
  2. Would an item-paired bootstrap over the 203-item GlobalPIQA preserve the +0.15 Overall advantage?
  3. Do the Stage-II relation effects replicate in autoregressive decoder-only models, where the window is set by the causal mask?
  4. Can ordinary-input preservation scale to large models, where the 11.835× dense/ordinary gradient ratio may grow?
  5. How much of the pipeline's edge over manual research is sustained cross-stage memory — what would the same three stages cost without it?
  6. The authors themselves scope the "Research RSI" claim: it does not establish sustained improvement of general research ability across tasks.
