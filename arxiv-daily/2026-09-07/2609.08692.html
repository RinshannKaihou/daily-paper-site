Key metric · cross-architecture alignment

0.78vs 0.52

Layer-mean CKA between Mamba and transformer on factual LAMA-TREx manifolds vs. general FineWeb-Edu — the local/global gap survives even with no whitening (α=0).

Why this matters

Different architectures learn the same local representations — probes and concept edits should port from transformers to Mamba-class models.

Critical edge

Cross-family pairs are not training-data-matched — part of the “architecture-driven” geometry gap may be data-driven, a confound the paper never discusses.

→ Limitations panel

Methodology

One residual stream, five lenses

Everything is measured on the residual stream (x ← x + Block(x)); Mamba's internal recurrent state is deliberately out of scope. Nine models — Pythia / Falcon / Llama3 / Qwen2 transformers, Mamba-1.4B/2.8B, FalconMamba-7B, hybrid Jamba2-3B — compared with shared tokenizers.

  1. 01
    Global geometryRankMe, IsoScore, PC1 variance on ≈500K hidden states from 1,500 FineWeb-Edu docs.
  2. 02
    CompressibilityTwo-layer autoencoders (ranks 64–512); swap in a token's reconstruction, measure output KL shift.
  3. 03
    Concept subspacesRank-constrained probes (1/2/5/25) on 41 LAMA-TREx relations + top-k PCA ablations.
  4. 04
    Local alignmentZCA-whitened CKA on relation manifolds; mutual-kNN overlap (k=10) over 10K matched tokens.
  5. 05
    Training dynamicsSame metrics across Pythia-1.4B checkpoints (1K → 108K steps) — the only family with public checkpoints.

ROBUSTNESS — 5 seeds (layer-wise CV ≤ ~1%) · whitening-strength α-sweep · replicated on Falcon↔FalconMamba · code: github.com/schwartz-lab-NLP/ssm-vs-transformer-geometry

Strengths

What holds up

  • Genuinely multi-scale: variance structure → compressibility → concept subspaces → manifolds → token neighborhoods → training dynamics, on one controlled model suite.
  • Jamba2 layer alternation acts as a natural ablation isolating attention as the anisotropy driver — the paper's sharpest tool.
  • Methodological hygiene rare in this genre: ZCA-whitened CKA plus an α-sweep; the local/global gap survives at α=0.
  • A surprising, memorable central finding with immediate implications for porting interpretability methods across architectures.

Limitations

Where it bends

  • Residual stream only — Mamba's recurrent state, arguably where SSMs differ most, is explicitly out of scope.
  • No downstream link: nothing ties isotropy or manifold alignment to generation quality or task performance.
  • Probes are correlational; no amnesic / activation-patching causal interventions.
  • Temporal analysis is Pythia-only, and "early alignment" compares against the final Mamba model alone.
  • Scale is modest (1.4B–8B); frontier scale and other attention-lite hybrids untested.
  • The anomalous final SSM layer was excluded from AE statistics — a real quirk the aggregate "parity" narrative smooths over.

Open questions · from the summary

Where to poke next

  • Q1Are the matched low-rank concept subspaces causally used during generation? Patching / amnesic probes would tell.
  • Q2Is the final SSM layer's compression sensitivity a functionally distinct "readout" stage — and does it persist at larger Mamba scales?
  • Q3If attention provably injects anisotropy, would de-skewing attention outputs (whitening / spectral regularization) in hybrids help or hurt?
  • Q4Does local alignment survive compositional concepts — syntax, coreference, reasoning traces — beyond Wikidata-style facts?
  • Q5Is alignment at ~4% of training just early shared statistics (unigram/bigram geometry)? A frequency-matched control would disentangle it.
  • Q6How do SFT / RLHF reshape the isotropy gap and the local-manifold convergence?
