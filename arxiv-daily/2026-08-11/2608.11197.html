Key finding · negative result · 2026-08-11

Add one compatible adjective and 20–60% of a noun's SAE latents switch off — feature sets do not compose as unions.

Beyond a Bag of Features: Set-Level Instability in Sparse Autoencoders  arXiv:2608.11197
Bolik, Stöpler, Andrzejak · Heidelberg University
sparse-autoencoders mechanistic-interpretability linear-representation-hypothesis feature-composition llm-evaluation 8 models · 270M–27B
Key metric · lost-latent rate
20–60%

Share of a noun's active SAE latents that vanish after prefixing a single semantically compatible adjective, measured at the noun token positions with the template fixed — so not a positional artifact. Rises with k and with model depth; the biggest single drop is the first adjective.

Why this matters

If you build retrieval, latent-set diffing, or concept-set steering on SAE signatures, the additive bag-of-features assumption silently fails on natural text.

Second-order lesson for eval methodology: the more interpretable representation did not land closer to human category structure than raw residual-stream cosine.

Critical edge

The headline is model-dependent and the flagship figures use the weakest backbone: Gemma 3 4B, 12B and 27B all beat the dense baseline on peak AMI, yet the main text generalizes from Gemma 3 270M, the smallest model in the suite.

Methodology

Distilled — two similarity measures on identical inputs, then a controlled composition probe.

Every word is embedded under one neutral template, "This is a {word}._". The dense baseline mean-pools residual-stream states over the target-word tokens and compares by cosine. The SAE measure encodes the same states, binarizes activations, and unions active latent indices across those tokens into a signature Sℓ(w); similarity is Jaccard overlap.

PRE Validate the measure first

A frozen LRH-aligned toy model checks that signatures union correctly when ground truth is known; clustering 30k Pile snippets by latent-set overlap checks that neighborhoods are semantically readable.

E1 Human concepts (replication)

Re-runs Shani et al. (2026) on Rosch / McCloskey & Glucksberg typicality norms. (a) Category-boundary recovery: K-medoids on d = 1 − s, scored by Adjusted Mutual Information. (b) Within-category typicality: Spearman ρ between similarity rankings and human typicality. Both layerwise.

E2 Set-consistency probe

200 hand-curated sequences noun → adj₁ noun → … up to k=5. Lost-latent rate R₀ᵤᵏ⁾ = fraction of the base noun's signature absent at step k. Lost latents are then sorted four ways: upstream presence via cross-layer probing, pre-activation regime (near-zero band vs strongly negative), SAEBench auto-interpretability, and synthetic steering with random decoder directions.

SWEEP Coverage

Gemma 3 270M–27B (Gemma Scope 2: Matryoshka BatchTopK, JumpReLU; 16k/262k; three L₀ targets), GPT-2 Small (OpenAI TopK 32k/128k), Llama 3.1 8B (Llama Scope JumpReLU 32k/131k), Mistral 7B (65k ReLU). Four SAE nonlinearity families, three seeds, ~6 days on four RTX 3090s.

Where the lost latents go

Four-way diagnostic — no single mechanism accounts for the drop.

Still active upstreamup to ~60%

Detected in middle layers when the layer-ℓ SAE is used as a cross-layer probe — much of the "loss" is local disappearance, not information deletion.

Pre-activation in near-zero band~80%

On the noun signature, previously-active lost latents mostly sit inside [−&theta,&theta]. They fall below threshold; they are not actively suppressed.

Strongly negative regime10–20%

Over the full sequence. This is the only slice consistent with active inhibition — and it is the small one.

Toy-model union consistency0.8–1.0

Jaccard / recall / precision on the LRH-aligned toy model once SAE width is sufficient. The union assumption holds where the world is built to satisfy it — and breaks on real text.

Ruled out individually: interference (steering reproduces loss for Gemma only, and layerwise trends mismatch), feature absorption (Matryoshka SAEs show the same rates), cross-layer probe artifacts, and auto-interpretability differences (means fall inside each other's error bars).

Results

Peak layerwise AMI on category-boundary recovery — SAE latent-set Jaccard vs the dense baseline it was supposed to beat.

Model SAE–Jaccard Dense cosine Δ

  • Typicality is a wash for both. Per-category Spearman ρ against human typicality fluctuates around zero for SAE sets — the same negative result Shani et al. report for dense states. The more interpretable representation buys nothing here.
  • SAE sets track the model, not the human. ρ(SAE-Jaccard, residual cosine) is strongly positive at roughly 0.5–0.9, so signatures follow model-internal geometry rather than human semantics.
  • Static SAEs are near chance. Applying the lowest-layer SAE to raw token embeddings never exceeds AMI 0.098 across all 8 models, while the embedding geometry itself sits at 0.060–0.124.
  • Toy models pass, and the passing is informative. At D=1000 union consistency is poor when dictionary width ≪ feature count and recovers near L ≈ N; with realistic decoder-sampled geometry the worst regime is intermediate width ≈200, where Jaccard dips to ≈0.4 before recovering.
  • Degradation at N=10 is recall-driven (precision stays ≈1), consistent with feature hedging rather than spurious latent recruitment.

Strengths

HOLDS UPrigor above the norm for negative-result interpretability work
  • Tests its own measure before using it — toy-model union consistency plus Pile clustering — which most negative-result papers skip.
  • Breadth: 8 backbones from 270M to 27B, 4 SAE nonlinearity families, 2–6 width/sparsity variants each, with a k-sensitivity check showing conclusions don't hinge on k=1.
  • The pre-activation decomposition reframes "lost" as "sub-threshold" — and the authors let that nuance override their own headline.
  • Actively tries to kill its own result with three competing explanations and reports that none suffices, including that absorption-mitigating Matryoshka SAEs show identical rates.
  • Transparent about compute (4×RTX 3090, ~6 days), AI assistance, and the pragmatic θ=0.1 threshold choice.

Limitations

DISCOUNT FORconstruction breadth, confounds, no positive proposal
  • One typicality dataset (Rosch-era, English-only, small), one semantic-refinement construction (adjective prefixing), residual-stream SAEs only. The 20–60% figure has a single construction behind it.
  • Jaccard is size-sensitive. Two words with very different L₀ can never score high, and no L₀-matched or size-normalized control is reported — a real confound across sparsity targets.
  • No random-set control. We see SAE-Jaccard vs cosine but never what AMI a size-matched random signature yields, so 0.42 has no floor to sit above.
  • No transcoders, gated SAEs, or non-residual hook locations, despite a claim pitched at SAE features generally.
  • No positive proposal: no repaired similarity measure, no training objective that would restore union consistency.
  • The 200 noun–adjective sequences are author-curated with no inter-annotator check on "semantic compatibility"; the "crime" category is a flagged anticorrelation outlier, so category noise is non-trivial versus effect size.
  • Title framing: "instability" is loaded for what Fig. 4c shows is latents drifting into a near-zero band. Code release is promised on acceptance, not yet available.

中文速览

结论：把 SAE 的「活跃 latent 集合」（signature）当作分析单元，它在人造玩具模型上确实满足并集式组合性（Jaccard/recall/precision 0.8–1.0），但在真实 LLM 上既不比稠密残差流更贴近人类类别边界，也不满足最基本的组合直觉——给名词加一个语义兼容的形容词，名词位置上 20%–60% 的原有 latent 直接熄灭。

对谁有用：如果你的工作涉及 SAE / 机制可解释性，或依赖「特征集合」做检索、diffing、概念编辑，这是一个必须知道的负面约束：latent 集合不能当作可加的语义 bag 来用。对 LLM 表征评测方法论（AMI / Spearman 对齐人类判断）也有参考价值，尤其是它诚实展示了「更可解释的表征未必更贴近人类语义」这一反直觉结果。

Questions for further exploration

Where the next experiment is cheapest and most informative.

01Would an L₀-matched or magnitude-weighted set similarity (overlap coefficient, weighted Jaccard) close the gap to dense cosine — or is the deficit intrinsic to discarding magnitude?
02Since ~80% of lost latents sit in the near-zero band, does a soft signature (top-p by pre-activation, or temperature-softened membership) restore union consistency on the adjective probe?
03The Gemma 3 27B reversal (0.444 > 0.397) is unexplained. Is there a scale threshold past which SAE sets become the better semantic probe, and does it track dictionary width per parameter?
04Do transcoders or cross-layer features — which model computation rather than a single residual snapshot — reduce the lost-latent rate, testing the paper's own hypothesis?
05Would a union-consistency regularizer on synthetic adjective–noun pairs trade off reconstruction and sparsity acceptably?
06Human typicality correlations are near zero for both representations. Is the failure in the representation, or in probing at single-word granularity for a judgment humans make over rich episodic knowledge?
