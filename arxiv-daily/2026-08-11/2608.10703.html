Why This Matters

First-person questionnaire profiles predict task-execution behavior only weakly (r ≈ 0.63); self-report-based persona and safety evals must be redesigned as situated behavioral probes.

Critical Edge

The paper's most distinctive claim — that thought-axes avoid trait drift — rests on hand-picked qualitative boxes, with no aggregate drift metric anywhere in the main text.

RQ3 · largest missing ablation

Methodology · distilled

Three stages turn psychometric facets into steerable, mechanism-level behavioral control.

(1) Probes. For each of 20 facets (BFI-2C, DOSPERT, GCS, HEXACO, UPPS-P) the authors define two opposing behavioral modes — an action plus its motivating reason — then synthesize 3,200 forced-choice scenarios with Claude Opus 4.6 across four interaction registers. Discriminant clauses and label-leakage checks keep neighboring facets from collapsing.

(2) Profiles. A cell score is the fraction choosing the keyed pole over 40 probes; cross-register comparisons average 160. Questionnaire baselines are administered one item per request, normalized to a shared 0–100 scale.

(3) BMA extraction. The vector v = mean( h̄(low) − h̄(high) ) over contrastive traces, computed over thought-only (BMA-T) or response-only (BMA-R) token spans, applied as h ← h + c·v. Layer and coefficient are jointly tuned to maximize clean range under a <1% unknown-rate and 5% repetition budget.

Strengths & Limitations

+ Strengths

  • Clean-directional-range metric (<1% unknown-rate + 5% repetition budget) explicitly rejects collapse — a genuinely better steering-eval practice.
  • Disjoint axis / probe scenario sets, plus a label-leakage check that forbids the probe naming its own dimension.
  • Norm-matched isotropic random-direction control (9.1 mean, median 0) — exactly the right null, often omitted.
  • Relative-coefficient analysis (ĉ within 2× across models whose absolute endpoints span ~50×) shows the BCL is a model property, not a search artifact.
  • Appendix A engages the psycho-lexical counterevidence that human taxonomies may not transfer to LLMs.
  • Reports the off-target transfer numbers that complicate its own specificity claim rather than burying them.

− Limitations

  • No aggregate trait-drift metric for BMA-T vs. BMA-R despite defining an LLM-judge protocol for it; the central RQ3 claim rides on qualitative boxes.
  • Layer & coefficients maximize the same clean-range that is then reported, on overlapping samples — headline ΔA reads as an upper envelope.
  • No external steering baseline (CAA, RepE, Persona Vectors) implemented; BMA-R stands in for all of them.
  • Probes, axis rationales, and drift judgments are all LLM-produced; no human inter-rater reliability reported.
  • Coverage stops at 70B open-weight instruct; Gemma-2-27B profiled but not steered; no reasoning or frontier models.
  • No downstream demonstration that BMA control improves anything a user cares about beyond flipping probe choices.

Open Questions

What is the aggregate LLM-judge on-construct rate for BMA-T vs. BMA-R across all 20 subdomains — and does BMA-R match its ΔA? Without it, "cleaner control" lacks a cost accounting.
Does the BCL band survive a held-out selection split (tune on subdomains 1–10, report on 11–20)?
For reasoning models, does a BMA drawn from the model's own sampled chain-of-thought beat BMA-T?
Is the drift a property of response spans, or of the "concentrate on the action" prompt? A prompt-discipline ablation would separate them.
A near-orthogonal axis still moves choices by 42.8 — what fraction of any steering vector is a generic "flip the default" component, and can it be projected out?
Do BMAs compose — can two axes be applied at once for a targeted 2-D style without multiplying drift?
