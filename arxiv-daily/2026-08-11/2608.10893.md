> **一句话总结：** 本文证明了"覆盖率下限（coverage floor）"会把协变量偏移下选择性预测的认证成本劈成两条互不替代的资源轴——风险轴花有标注的源域样本 $n$、下限轴花无标注的目标域样本 $m$（$s \asymp \kappa^{-1}\sqrt{\beta/n}+\sqrt{\beta/m}$），并给出三段式（Model-B 下界 / Model-A oracle 上界 / Model-B′ 可实现上界）跨模型匹配；在 1,024 格合成审计中形式化证书 591 次认证 0 违例、0 Holm 拒绝，而 plug-in / floor-free / weighted-conformal 三个演示基线分别被 Holm 拒绝 662 / 108 / 40 次；SQuAD→NewsQA 真实工作负载上证书 2,000/2,000 次全部"诚实拒绝"。

> **TL;DR:** Adding a hard coverage floor $\beta$ to selection-conditioned risk control under bounded-ratio covariate shift creates a feasibility frontier and a two-resource sample-complexity map (risk paid in labeled source $n$, floor paid in unlabeled target $m$), proved as a Model-B lower bound matched by a Model-A oracle upper bound and an implementable Model-B′ upper bound with an explicitly priced histogram nuisance. On a 1,024-cell registered synthetic grid the formal arms certify 583/1,024 (oracle-A) and 8/1,024 (B′) cells with 0 violations and 0 Holm rejections, versus 662 / 108 / 40 Holm rejections for the plug-in, floor-free and weighted-conformal demonstration arms and 0 cells certified by a DRO-box baseline; the labeled-sample "bite" diverges with log–log slope $-2.002$ (95% CI $[-2.142,-1.862]$, inside the pre-registered band $[-2.3,-1.7]$), and on a real SQuAD→NewsQA audit ($N_\text{eval}=4{,}212$, $\alpha=.10$, $\beta=.60$) the certificate returns 2,000/2,000 honest refusals while the plug-in baseline claims in 99.9% of leg-1 resamples at realized risk $.278 = 2.8\alpha$.

## 核心贡献

- **Floor Certification Map（定理 1）**：在有界密度比协变量偏移 $w=dQ_X/dP_X\le B$ 下，同时认证选择条件风险 $R_Q(\lambda)\le\alpha$ 与硬覆盖下限 $C_Q(\lambda)\ge\beta$，会产生可行性前沿 $\beta^*(\alpha,Q)$，并把样本复杂度可加地劈成两条轴：$s \lesssim \kappa^{-1}\sqrt{\beta/n} + \sqrt{\beta/m} + \rho/\kappa$。风险付有标注源样本，下限付无标注目标样本。
- **必须跨模型陈述，而非单模型 minimax**：定理 6 证明在完整未知权重类 $\mathcal{W}_B$ 上 Model-B 本身不相合（$\alpha=\beta=1/2$ 处，任意 $(n,m)$ 都有硬世界），因此不存在单模型 minimax 定律；作者用 Model-A（oracle 权重）与 Model-B′（预注册 $K$ 格分层偏移）两种结构限制恢复可认证性，并显式给出 nuisance 价格 $\rho_\lambda\lesssim\kappa s$。
- **"下限创造了这张图"**：$\beta\to0$ 时两条下界轴都带 $\sqrt{\beta}$ 一起消失，说明困难来自覆盖下限而非 overlap/positivity（构造全在有界比类内，因此不是 D'Amour 等的 positivity 障碍换皮）。
- **局部化复杂度泛函**：上界方差代理与下界 hard-slice 几何都局部化到接受域，指向 $E_P[w^2S_\lambda]/(E_P[wS_\lambda])^2$ 而非全局 ESS；实验中该泛函与 B′ 所需 $n$ 的 Spearman 为 .936，全局 ESS 只有 .026。
- **可实现算法与诚实拒绝语义**：Algorithm 1 用四块数据切分 + 裁剪直方图权重 + Maurer–Pontil 经验 Bernstein 风险 UCB + 不依赖权重的覆盖率 LCB，双测试都过边界才认证，否则输出 $\perp$ 并给出可观测的"哪条测试失败"归因。

## Key Contributions

- **A finite-sample theory that the coverage floor creates.** Prior selective-risk work (SCoRE, LEC, BalanceRAG, weighted CRC under shift) certifies the risk of whatever coverage a policy happens to attain; a risk-only certificate can be vacuously safe by abstaining on nearly everything. This paper makes the operator's automation floor a certified finite-sample object under covariate shift.
- **β-indexed lower bounds (Claim 1 / Theorem 5).** Two Le Cam pairs at bounded likelihood ratio, one per axis: an $n$-axis pair with no shift at all ($w\equiv1$, inframarginal loss bump of mass $\rho=\beta/2$ and height $\Delta=4\kappa s/\beta$, per-sample KL $\lesssim(\kappa s)^2/\beta$) and an $m$-axis pair on a three-cell partition. Additive display up to a factor 2.
- **Cross-model matching (Claims 2–3).** The Model-A oracle certificate attains the lower-bound rates up to constants and logs ($n\gtrsim\beta\log(|\Lambda|/\delta)/(\kappa s)^2$, $m\gtrsim\beta\log(|\Lambda|/\delta)/s^2$); Model-B′ attains them plus a sufficient nuisance condition $\rho_\lambda\lesssim\kappa s$ (schematically $n_w\wedge m_w\gtrsim B^2K\cdot\text{mass}(A_\lambda)/(\kappa s)^2$).
- **Honest scoping of what is *not* proved.** The nuisance necessity is only partially settled: a $K$-free core is necessary for lattice-valued certification in the known-$\eta$ submodel, the histogram $B^2K$ rate is sufficient but not proved necessary, and the unknown-$\eta$ edge is explicitly left open. A fixed-ESS separation theorem is also left open. The paper claims no novelty for the floor certificate construction itself.
- **A full-grid validity audit plus a negative real-workload feasibility audit**, with pre-registered bands, Clopper–Pearson bounds, Holm correction, and released code (`github.com/yahiko-l/certify-or-refuse`).

## Methodology

The setup: labeled pairs $(X,Y)\sim P$ with bounded loss $L\in[0,1]$ and $\eta(x)=E[L\mid X=x]$; unlabeled target covariates $X'\sim Q_X$; covariate shift with density ratio $w\le B$. A selective policy is a point on a **pre-registered nested-threshold lattice** $\Lambda$ over a frozen router score, with acceptance indicator $S_\lambda$. The certified functionals are the selection-conditioned ratio risk $R_Q(\lambda)=E_{Q_X}[\eta S_\lambda]/E_{Q_X}[S_\lambda]$ and coverage $C_Q(\lambda)=E_{Q_X}[S_\lambda]$; $R_Q:=+\infty$ when $C_Q=0$, so reject-all is never certifiable (this is the definitional move that makes the floor bite). A procedure is *valid* at $(\alpha,\beta,\delta)$ if $P(\mathcal{A}\ne\perp \wedge (R_Q>\alpha \vee C_Q<\beta))\le\delta$, and *powered* if it certifies with probability $\ge1/2$.

The frontier is defined through a budget functional $\varphi(c)=\alpha c-\int_0^c\eta^*_Q(u)\,du$ on the increasing rearrangement of $\eta$, with $\beta^*(\alpha,Q)=\sup\{c:\varphi(c)\ge0\}$ — a fractional-knapsack / generalized Neyman–Pearson value. Slack is $s=\beta^*-\beta$. Two regularity conditions carry everything: a **regular frontier margin** $(\kappa,s_0)$ ($\eta^*_Q\ge\alpha+\kappa$ near the frontier, giving the linear reserve $\varphi(\beta^*-s)\ge\kappa s$), and a **lattice margin** $\mathrm{LR}_\text{margin}(s)$ (an interior witness policy with coverage in $[\beta+s/4,\beta^*-s/4]$ and risk budget $\ge\kappa s/8$). The paper is unusually careful to distinguish the relaxed frontier $\beta^*$ from the operative lattice frontier $\beta^*_\Lambda$ and to state that $\mathrm{LR}_\text{margin}$ does *not* identify the two.

Algorithm 1 splits source into $(D_w^P,D_r)$ and target into $(D_w^Q,D_f)$; estimates $\hat w_k=\mathrm{clip}(\hat q_k/\hat p_k,0,B)$ per cell; computes a localized nuisance radius $\rho_\lambda$ over the accepted cells' masses; passes risk iff the empirical-Bernstein UCB of $\hat w S_\lambda(L-\alpha)$ is $\le-\rho_\lambda$ (the linearization of the ratio constraint at fixed $\alpha$); passes floor iff a weight-free Bernstein LCB on $S_\lambda$ clears $\beta$; and returns the floor-LCB-maximal passing $\lambda$, else $\perp$ with attribution. A shared $\delta$-budget covers weight estimation and all $|\Lambda|$ per-$\lambda$ tests, so post-hoc selection among simultaneously valid tests is free (learn-then-test).

Evaluation has three legs: (i) registered synthetic generators for rate shape (bite divergence, two-axis form, contour transfer, localized geometry); (ii) a 1,024-cell full-grid validity audit (4 shift intensities × 256 cells, 1,000 reps/cell, 5 edge cells re-run at 2,000–8,000) with a deep-audit subset of 6 cells × 4,000 reps; (iii) a real-workload extractive-QA audit, SQuAD (source) → NewsQA (target) via MRQA, $N_\text{eval}=4{,}212$, correctness = token-F1 $\ge0.5$ against gold (deliberately non-judge), SLA fixed pre-data at $\alpha=.10$, $\beta=.60$, $\delta=.05$.

## Results & Highlights

- **Bite divergence (labeled axis).** Required $n$ vs. slack $s$ has log–log slope **$-2.002$**, 95% CI $[-2.142,-1.862]$, inside the pre-registered acceptance band $[-2.3,-1.7]$; a 20,000-resample pairs bootstrap gives $[-2.11,-1.89]$ and the quadratic-curvature CI contains 0. Across three structurally distinct families the slopes are $-2.002$ (continuum), $-1.617$ (kcell, CI $[-1.76,-1.47]$), $-1.845$ (non-theorem-aligned, CI $[-2.02,-1.67]$) — i.e. near-quadratic everywhere but the exact $-2$ is specific to the regular-margin family (fit rests on only 9 slack design points).
- **Full-grid validity audit (1,024 cells).** Formal arms: oracle-A certifies **583/1,024** cells, B′ **8/1,024**, for 591 arm-cell certifications with **0 violations, 0 Holm rejections**, pooled CP-UCBs $9.0\times10^{-6}$ and $1.8\times10^{-4}$ (both $\le\delta=0.05$). Demonstration baselines: plug-in certifies 1,024/1,024 cells with **190,914 violations and 662 Holm rejections**; floor-free certifies 112/1,024 with 107,157 violations / 108 Holm rejections; weighted conformal (Tibshirani et al. 2019 / Barber et al. 2023) certifies 40/1,024 with 60,469 violations / 40 Holm rejections; the DRO-box baseline certifies **0/1,024** (escalate-all). Deep-audit subset: 0 violations in 48,000 replications, pooled CP-UCB $6.2\times10^{-5}$.
- **Localized functional vs. global ESS.** Across 640 worlds (family 1, B′ arm, $s=.05$, $m=10^5$), B′ required-$n$ has Spearman **.936** (95% bootstrap CI $[.927,.944]$) against $E_P[w^2S_\lambda]/(E_P[wS_\lambda])^2$, versus **.026** ($[-.056,.103]$) against global ESS. Family 2 is far weaker: within-pair ratio agreement .995 but pooled Spearman only .503, with 162/640 worlds censored at the required-$n$ cap.
- **Two-axis functional form.** Surface CV $R^2=0.9202$ for the additive $s^*(n,m)$ model. At the registered 41-cell density the pre-registered CI-separation criterion **failed**; re-running at higher design density, strict non-overlap probability rises $0.12$ (40 cells) → $0.75$ (134) → $1.00$ (635 cells, 800 reps), where CV-RMSE is $0.0122$ (CI $[0.0109,0.0134]$) and the nearest of four alternative forms is $2.2\times$ worse, unanimous across 24 re-splits.
- **Constants do not transfer.** Two-axis constants fit at shift intensity L1 and applied unchanged give in-sample L1 correlation .997 and held-out L2–L4 correlations .981/.963/.890, but the pre-registered $\pm3\%$ bands **failed at all intensities**, with held-out median factor errors $\times1.24/\times1.91/\times3.40$ (monotone in intensity, rank correlation 1.0, while the accepted-region variance proxy grows only $\times1.3$).
- **Practical price of estimated weights.** At $\kappa s=0.042$, oracle-A first certifies at $n_\text{total}=4{,}096$ and fully certifies at $16{,}384$; B′ needs $524{,}288$ and $1{,}048{,}576$ — a **128× onset and 64× full-certification premium**. The $K$-sweep gives an onset premium scaling as $K^{0.955}$ (95% CI $[0.755,1.154]\ni1$); at $n_\text{total}=2^{20}$, B′ certify rate is 1.00/0.72/0.00/0.00/0.00 for $K=4/8/16/32/64$, recovering to 1.00 for $K\le32$ only at $2^{22}$.
- **Real workload (SQuAD→NewsQA).** The B′ certificate outputs **2,000/2,000 honest refusals** (1,000 per capability leg), consuming no evaluation labels, 0 violations. A separate post-hoc eval-set frontier diagnosis gives $\hat\beta^*(.10)=.050$ (CI $[.035,.130]$) for leg 1 (Llama-3-8B) and $.209$ ($[.160,.281]$) for leg 2 (DeepSeek-V4-Flash), both far below the SLA floor $\beta=.60$; even at $\alpha=.20$ leg 2 reaches only $.525$ ($[.464,.597]$). The plug-in contrast, reported leg-split: leg 1 claims in 99.9% of resamples and 99.9% violate, realized risk $.278 = 2.8\alpha$; leg 2 claims in 0.1% and the single claim violates, risk $.219 = 2.2\alpha$ at coverage $.614$. Refusal status is invariant over 11/11 registered nuisance variants ($K\in\{8,16,32\}\times B\in\{5,10,20\}$ plus lock-quantile jitter).
- **Equal-construction ablation.** A SCoRE-inspired floor-augmented betting arm (weighted betting e-value replacing the empirical-Bernstein risk test, every other block verbatim-identical) is valid and has certify-frequency $\ge$ B′'s in every one of the 1,024 cells (strictly greater in 8), same powered-cell count, 0 violations, non-trivial-certification count 10 vs. B′'s 9 — showing the conservatism is a property of the estimated-weight construction, not of the risk test.

## Authors & Credibility

All four authors (Jiamiao Liu, Dewen Qiao, Yu Zhang, Xuetao Chen, corresponding) are from the **Department of Information, Xinqiao Hospital, Army Medical University (Third Military Medical University), Chongqing**, funded by a hospital-level young-doctoral incubation program (2025YQB034). This is an unusual provenance for a 58-page distribution-free minimax paper filed under cs.CL — the affiliation is a hospital IT department with no visible track record in conformal inference or Le Cam lower-bound theory, and there is no co-author from a statistics/ML theory group. That is not disqualifying, but it means the theory has no institutional prior behind it and must be judged on the appendices (A–H, ~40 pages of proofs, which I did not verify line by line). Positive credibility signals: code and numeric lock records are released; the empirical program is pre-registered with bands declared in advance; registered-criterion *failures* are reported rather than buried; the paper explicitly disclaims novelty for the certificate construction and explicitly names the open edges. The engagement with the neighboring literature (SCoRE, LEC, BalanceRAG, Almeida et al., Aldirawi et al., Franc et al., Kalai–Kanade, Adil–Błasiok) is unusually granular, with a row-by-row non-derivability analysis in Appendix E.

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty (moderate-to-high, narrowly scoped).** The honest claim is not "we can certify a coverage floor" — the paper concedes that a floor-augmented e-value construction in the spirit of SCoRE, or i.i.d. joint certificates (Yu and Liu 2026), already gives the validity half. The novelty is the $\beta$-indexed lower bound, the two-resource $(n,m)$ split, the localized complexity functional, and the priced nuisance. Within that scope the claim looks defensible: the nearest lower-bound work (Aldirawi et al. 2026) is single-axis and floor-free.

**Rigor (high on statement discipline, uneven on empirics).** The scoping discipline is genuinely above average: separate symbols for relaxed vs. lattice frontier, explicit statement that the lower bound is *existential in the lattice* (so the match is not uniform over a fixed $\Lambda$), explicit labeling of conditional vs. unconditional violation-rate denominators, and a refusal to call the localized-ESS reading anything but heuristic. But several empirical legs are weaker than the headline: the two-axis form's pre-registered CI-separation criterion **failed at the registered 41-cell density** and was only rescued by raising design density to 635 cells — the criterion is byte-identical but the design is not, so this is a post-hoc power rescue that the authors label (fairly) as "sample-limited"; the contour-transfer $\pm3\%$ bands failed at *every* intensity; and the localized-geometry evidence rests on family 1 only, with family 2 censored in 162/640 worlds and the oracle arm's correlations explicitly "not supportive."

**Weak baselines.** Three of the four comparison arms are strawmen by construction and the paper mostly says so. The **DRO-box baseline certifies 0/1,024 cells** — it is escalate-all, i.e. it makes no automatic answers at all, so its "0 violations" is vacuous and it contributes nothing as a comparator. The **plug-in** arm has no finite-sample guarantee whatsoever, so its 662 Holm rejections demonstrate only that an unguaranteed method is unguaranteed. **Weighted conformal** targets a *marginal* estimand, not the selection-conditioned ratio with a floor, so counting its 40 Holm rejections as a failure is close to a category error (the paper's own Table 1 footnote *d* concedes the estimand mismatch). The one genuinely informative comparator is the SCoRE-inspired betting arm, and there B′ is *weakly dominated* — the paper reports this fairly and reports no paired test or interval for the ordering.

**The missing ablation, stated by the authors themselves.** The headline 128×/64× oracle-A-vs-B′ multiplier is measured against a released two-family oracle kernel that "differs from B′ in its confidence allocation and floor construction as well as in knowing $w$." So the number bundles three differences and upper-bounds, rather than isolates, the weight-estimation cost. The paper writes: "Isolating weight estimation would need a comparator matched to B′ in every block except the weights, which we do not run." That is exactly the ablation a reviewer would demand, and it is absent.

**Data/protocol quirks.** The real-workload audit had **two recorded deviations**: leg 1's model was availability-triggered (Llama-3-8B), and leg 2 re-entered with DeepSeek-V4-Flash as a "conditional capability-ladder step, explicitly not independent validation, since it runs on the same corpus with the same eval set." So the two "capability legs" are not independent replications. The bite fit uses 9 design points. The per-$\alpha$ bootstrap intervals in the frontier sweep carry no simultaneous-coverage correction across the $\alpha$-grid or over threshold selection, and the closest case (leg 2 at $\alpha=.20$) has an interval ending at $.597$ against a floor of $.60$ — a hair's breadth from not supporting the infeasibility reading.

**Significance.** Conceptually real: "certify the risk of whatever coverage you get" is a genuine loophole in deployed selective-QA/RAG abstention systems, and pricing the floor in *unlabeled target* data is an actionable operational insight (a refusal tells you which data to buy). Practically, though, B′ certifies 8 of 1,024 synthetic cells and refuses 2,000/2,000 times on the only real workload tested. Nobody can deploy this today; the contribution is the map, not the tool.

**Red flags.** (1) The prose is extraordinarily hedged — nearly every sentence carries a scope guard, model tag, or "not claimed" clause. This is intellectually admirable but also makes independent verification hard and is a stylistic signature worth noting. (2) A 58-page single-institution preprint with a hospital-IT affiliation, a large pre-registered experimental apparatus, and dense theory is an unusual combination; the released repo is the thing to check. (3) The only real-data result is a negative one, which cannot distinguish "the certificate is honest" from "the certificate is too conservative to ever fire." The paper's own leg-2 targeted existence run (certify-frequency 1.0 at $n=m=262{,}144$, $s=0.20$) is the mitigating evidence, and it is synthetic and on-class.

## Strengths

- Identifies a real gap: risk-only certificates are vacuously satisfiable by abstaining, and no prior work indexes lower bounds by a coverage floor under shift.
- The impossibility result (Theorem 6, Model-B inconsistent at $\alpha=\beta=1/2$ on every lattice containing two fixed thresholds) is a strong structural statement that *justifies* the otherwise-awkward cross-model presentation rather than excusing it.
- The lower-bound constructions live at bounded likelihood ratio, so they are demonstrably not overlap/positivity obstructions in disguise (Appendix B.6 draws the contrasting overlap-created boundary).
- Pre-registration with declared acceptance bands, and reporting the cases where the registered criterion failed.
- Careful measurement hygiene: three separate quantities ($\delta$, $\delta_\text{tol}$, CP-UCB) never interchanged, Holm correction, explicit conditional-vs-unconditional denominators, leg-split (never pooled) reporting of the plug-in failure modes, which the paper correctly notes a pooled summary would destroy.
- The refusal output carries observable test-side attribution, and Corollary 1 turns a data-starved refusal into a concrete "buy more of *this* data" readout.

## Limitations

- **Model-B′ validity is conditional on the pre-registered $K$-cell stratified-shift model.** Off-partition robustness is not claimed; an adversarial synthetic sweep (in $\mathcal{W}_B$ but off the $K$-measurable class) drives true violations invisibly to the certificate.
- **Nuisance necessity is only partially settled**: $K$-free core necessary (lattice-valued, known-$\eta$ submodel), histogram $B^2K$ rate sufficient but not proved necessary, unknown-$\eta$ open. A fixed-ESS separation theorem is also open, so the "localized functional, not global ESS" claim rests on matched bound structure plus family-1 experiments.
- **Rates are local**: they require a regular frontier margin $(\kappa,s_0)$ and slack below the local-regime threshold, and $\kappa$ can be arbitrarily small (cost scales as $\kappa^{-2}$). The lower bound is existential in the lattice; a deployed fixed $\Lambda$ only certifies the operative slack $\beta^*_\Lambda-\beta$.
- **Practical constants are harsh**: B′ needs $\sim10^6$ total samples where oracle-A needs $\sim1.6\times10^4$, and certifies 8/1,024 audit cells.
- **Real-workload scope is one corpus, one shift axis, no positive certification**, with two recorded protocol deviations and non-independent capability legs.
- **Constants do not transfer across shift intensities** — only rate shape and ordering do.

## Relevance

如果你关注 LLM 的选择性回答 / 弃权、RAG 幻觉风险控制、或 LLM-as-a-judge 标注可信度，这篇是少见的"把自动化率下限当成一等公民的有限样本理论"：它精确说明了为什么一个只保证 risk 的 conformal 证书可以靠拒答绝大多数请求而变得空洞，以及在分布偏移下要额外买多少**无标注目标域数据**才能守住覆盖率承诺。其 SQuAD→NewsQA 的"诚实拒绝"结论也直接提醒：现实域偏移下，$\alpha=.10/\beta=.60$ 这类看似温和的 SLA 可能根本不在可行前沿之内。

## Questions for Further Exploration

1. Does the unknown-$\eta$ Model-B′ admit a $K$-free certificate, or does the source↔target alignment force $K$ back in? This is the paper's own stated central open edge, and it determines whether the 128× premium is intrinsic or an artifact of the histogram estimator.
2. What does a comparator matched to B′ in *every* block except the weights show? Without it, the "cost of estimated weights" is only upper-bounded, and the reported multipliers may be dominated by confidence allocation and floor construction rather than weight error.
3. Can the map be extended beyond bounded-ratio shift (e.g. to $f$-divergence balls or label shift), where $w\le B$ fails and the floor may no longer be label-free estimable?
4. Does a fixed-ESS separation theorem exist that would upgrade "localized accepted-region functional, not global ESS" from an empirically-supported reading to a proved statement?
5. On real workloads, is the certificate ever *positively* feasible for LLM QA — e.g. with a stronger retriever, a learned router score rather than standard uncertainty families, or a looser SLA ($\alpha=.20$, $\beta=.40$)? The current evidence cannot separate honesty from terminal conservatism.
6. How does the floor interact with sequential/online deployment, where the target distribution drifts continuously and the pre-registered lattice and partition both go stale?
