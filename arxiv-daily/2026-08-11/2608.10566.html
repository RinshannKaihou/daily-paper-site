Why This Matters

Citing an erasure count as proof a concept is “distributed over N directions” is unsound — the count is procedure-relative, not intrinsic.

Critical Edge

It critiques a video-world-model study by name yet never runs that study’s pipeline — proving coordinate-sensitivity on a synthetic stand-in, not the target.

The Machinery

Algorithm 1 — a declared cumulative metric-QR erasure — and the 2×2 counterexample that drives the negative result.

At each step k, a probe block Bₖ is fit on residualized features, metric-QR’d against a fixed positive-definite coefficient metric G into a G-orthonormal block, and the edit Tₖ = I − UₖUₖ⊤G is applied. The paper’s load-bearing insistence: every choice — metric, probe loss, regularizer, residualization rule, numerical tolerance, stopping rule — is part of the estimand, and G is never recomputed after editing.

The negative result comes from a 2×2 population construction: H=(S,N)~N(0,I), Y=𝑱[S≥0], X=HA with A invertible. Because S is still a deterministic linear function of X, the minimum guarding rank is exactly 1 for every A. Yet for any shear a≠0 the ridge probe picks up a component along N, leaving residual cross-covariance after the first removal — forcing a second accepted update and flipping the Euclidean count from 1 to 2. Proposition 2 then makes this constructive: transport the metric, probe, regularizer, and tie-breaking covariantly and the entire trajectory becomes affine-equivariant (exact covariance is just one corollary).

Two Sides

What holds up — and where the argument is weakest.

Strengths

  • Airtight, hand-checkable core. The 2×2 shear counterexample pins all three model-defined quantities, so the result cannot be dismissed as a change of problem.
  • Constructive, not just destructive. Proposition 2 tells practitioners exactly what to declare (metric, probe, regularizer, covariance, tie-breaking) to get a well-defined count.
  • Exceptional scope discipline. Tables 1 & 3 explicitly partition what stays valid vs. what must be reinterpreted across INLP / MP-SAL-LEACE / the video study.
  • Numerics match theory to ~10−6 across 20 Haar maps and unshrunk covariance — ruling out solver noise.
  • Unusual honesty: discloses 1,865 prior test-set touches and the negative n=1,000 result; labels sub-.5 AUROCs rather than flipping them.

Limitations

  • Critique never reaches its target. The finite bridge uses a synthetic angular distribution, not the criticized paper’s activations; minibatch size is unknown (declared 128).
  • Looks like conditioning. The κ ladder tracks condition number (2.09→1,070); at the interesting κ=2 the interval [.008,.134] barely excludes zero.
  • Linear only. Says nothing about nonlinear, tokenwise, spatial, temporal, or causal concept organization.
  • Stress test, not a distribution. Five fixed dense SPD maps bound nothing about how large the effect is “typically.”
  • Missing ablation. No end-to-end exact-covariance erasure count on real V-JEPA2 features under the strongest fresh-attacker audit — the most conspicuous gap.

Open Questions

Where the claim stops and the work begins.

  1. Does the transported-covariance metric actually stabilize the count on real V-JEPA2 features under the strong three-role fresh-attacker audit — or only in the fixed-attacker Euclidean comparison reported?
  2. Is there any anisotropy regime with condition number near 1 where the trajectory still changes materially, or is the phenomenon entirely a conditioning artifact in practice?
  3. What is the analogous statement for nonlinear erasers (kernelized INLP, adversarial removal, SAE feature ablation) — a different, possibly larger, procedure-relative ambiguity?
  4. If a count must be procedure-relative, which declared procedure is most useful? Is there a decision-theoretic criterion (e.g. downstream steering efficacy) that selects a metric?
  5. Would rerunning the criticized video study’s actual pipeline under pre-declared shears reproduce the count change on its own activations — closing the synthetic-bridge gap?
  6. How should the community retroactively reinterpret published “dozens-to-hundreds of directions” claims — is there a conservative affine-invariant lower bound (cross-cov rank, guard rank) worth reporting instead?
