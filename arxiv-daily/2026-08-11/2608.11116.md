> **一句话总结：** Charge-CIM 用一套可重构开关电容网络同时充当输入 DAC、乘法器、列累加器、移位加法器和 SAR-ADC 的 C-DAC，从而彻底取消 INT8 计算的比特切分（bit-slicing），在九个 DNN/LLM 基准上把 ADC 相关开销降低 91.7%，相对 SOTA 电荷域加速器 Cambricon-CIM 实现 2.71× 能效与 2.00× 吞吐提升（相对 RAELLA 为 6.13× / 7.38×），系统算力密度 1.4 TOPS/mm²。

> **TL;DR:** Charge-CIM (a.k.a. YOCO 2.0) reuses one switched-capacitor fabric as embedded DAC, analog multiplier, accumulator, in-situ shift-adder, and the C-DAC of an embedded differential SAR ADC, eliminating bit-sliced arithmetic so an 8-bit VMM needs a single conversion instead of up to 64. Simulated in 28 nm across nine benchmarks (VGG16/ResNet18/MobileNetV3/DenseNet201/ViT on ImageNet, MobileBERT/BERT-Base on SQuAD, GPT-2 and Llama 2-7B prefill on WikiText-2), it reports 6.13× energy efficiency and 7.38× throughput over the RAELLA baseline, and 2.71× / 2.00× over the stronger Cambricon-CIM baseline (which itself scores 2.26× / 3.69× over RAELLA), with ADC-side energy cut 91.7% and accuracy loss bounded at 1.7 F1 points / +0.26 PPL on Llama 2-7B.

## 核心贡献

- **全模拟多比特计算，消除切分诱发的 ADC 开销。** 通过在可重构开关电容网络内做「顺序电荷共享」，把 DAC 转换、乘法、累加、加权求和统一在模拟域完成，8-bit MAC 从传统的 64 次 ADC 转换（8 输入切片 × 8 权重切片）降到「每个 compute-bar 一次读出」。
- **嵌入式 ADC，摊薄单通道转换器成本。** 阵列内的开关电容在第 6 相位被复用为 SAR-ADC 的电容 DAC（C-DAC），省掉了独立 ADC 中面积/能耗最大的部件，同时保留逐通道并行读出（不像 RAELLA / Cambricon-CIM 需要 ADC 时分复用，后者每阵列读出约 100 ns）。
- **差分宏 + 逆编码，在 ADC 内部完成 psum 加法。** 差分 SAR 天然做减法，作者把 V_N 侧阵列喂入取反输入（I_inv = −I_N），于是 D_out ∝ V_P − V_N = ΣPsum，直接把「感知边界」变成加法器：ADC 转换次数再减半，读出等效精度提升到伪 9-bit，动态范围翻倍到 V_PP = 2·V_REF。
- **符号运算的 offset 编码分解。** 把有符号点积拆成「无符号 MAC + 权重补偿（权重驻留、加载时预计算）+ 激活补偿（外围数字加法树在线算）+ 常数偏置（并入 bias，且在差分相减中被抵消）」四项，几乎不增加运行时硬件。
- **全栈层次化架构与融合数据流。** 从 MCC → macro（256×128×8b，两个 128×128 差分子阵）→ IMA（含基于泰勒展开的 Local SFU）→ tile（4 IMA + 128 KB eDRAM + Global SFU）→ chip（4×4 tile，2D Mesh NoC，4 个 HyperLink），支持 GEMM-GEMM、GEMM-non-GEMM、non-GEMM 层融合的生产者-消费者流水线。
- **可重构位宽。** 同一硬件通过把数字位映射到不同电容组，支持 4-bit 标准模式、2-bit 并行模式、1-bit 二值模式，以及非二进制「位打乱」（例如把 8-bit 输入重排成 B7,B3,B2,B6,B1,B0,B5,B4，得到 108:147 ≈ 3:4 的等效权重比，从而原生复现 Cambricon-CIM 的 coding-base reformation）。

## Key Contributions

- **Slice-free fully analog multi-bit VMM** via sequential charge redistribution in a reconfigurable switched-capacitor network, unifying DAC, multiply, accumulate, and binary-weighted shift-add before a single quantization.
- **Embedded SAR ADC** that reuses the in-array capacitor hierarchy as its C-DAC, removing the dominant area/energy component of a standalone converter while preserving per-channel parallel readout.
- **In-ADC partial-sum addition through inverse coding**, turning the differential ADC's intrinsic subtraction into an adder, halving conversions and raising effective readout resolution to pseudo-9-bit.
- **Offset encoding for signed arithmetic** decomposed into unsigned MAC + precomputed weight compensation + online activation compensation + a constant that cancels differentially.
- **Full-stack chip/tile/IMA/macro/MCC hierarchy** with local and global SFUs sized to match the 20 ns analog VMM latency, enabling layer-fusion dataflows.
- **Evaluation across nine CNN/Transformer/LLM benchmarks** with SPICE-extracted non-idealities fed into Timeloop/Accelergy, plus PVT and Monte Carlo robustness sweeps.

## Methodology

The core mechanism is a six-phase charge journey inside each macro. **Phase 1 (Input Charging)**: binary-sized capacitor groups (ratio 1:1:2:…:2^(N−1)) are selectively charged to VDD according to the input code through a PMOS. **Phase 2 (Embedded DAC)**: closing the S_DAC switches triggers row-wise charge sharing, producing V_i^in = VDD·(Σ 2^n X_i[n])/2^N — an 8-bit input "01111111" yields 127/256·VDD — with no standalone DAC. **Phase 3 (1-bit multiply)**: a NAND-like NMOS pair (M0 gated by the compute word line, M1 by the stored weight bit) performs conditional charge retention, so V_ij^mul[m] = V_i^in · W_ij[m]. **Phase 4 (Parallel accumulation)**: closing S_ACC shorts all MCCs in a column, giving the average V_j^acc[m] = (1/P)Σ V_ij^mul[m]; supporting M-bit weights requires P > 2^(M−1). **Phase 5 (Weighted summation)**: opening S_ACC and closing S_SA groups unit capacitors across bit-columns in ratio 1:2:…:2^(M−1), yielding the bit-weighted sum in the analog domain (Eq. 5 gives the closed-form end-to-end multi-bit VMM). **Phase 6 (In-situ quantization)**: the very same hierarchical capacitor groups become the C-DAC of a monotonic-switching SAR ADC, with bottom plates routed to a sense amplifier. The whole sequence fits a 20 ns budget with worst-case residual settling error below 0.46 LSB.

The evaluation is a cross-layer simulation flow modeled on Cambricon-CIM's methodology. Analog circuits (MCC array, embedded ADC) are simulated in SPICE at 28 nm with post-layout extraction; unit capacitors are 2.66 fF stacked in BEOL above the switch-control logic. Non-idealities are decomposed into embedded-DAC error, analog-MAC error (both zero-mean Gaussian), and kT/C thermal noise (Eq. 9), with ADC error modeled as rounding to the 8-bit ENOB. Digital blocks are synthesized at 1 GHz with Design Compiler + PrimeTime; SRAM/eDRAM/interconnect come from CACTI 7.0; everything is integrated into Timeloop/Accelergy. Baselines RAELLA and Cambricon-CIM are normalized to a 300 mm² budget (18 and 20 tiles respectively, 16 arrays of 72×80×4b each), with RAELLA configured in SRAM rather than memristor. All models use INT8 PTQ in PyTorch/TorchVision with no retraining.

## Results & Highlights

- **Energy efficiency:** geometric mean **6.13× over RAELLA** (range 3.6×–8.8×) and **2.71× over Cambricon-CIM** (range 1.7×–3.6×); the strongest baseline Cambricon-CIM itself is 2.26× over RAELLA. Largest gains are on LLM prefill: GPT-2 8.27× and Llama 2-7B 8.77× vs RAELLA.
- **Throughput:** geometric mean **7.38× over RAELLA** (range 3.0×–16.0×) and **2.00× over Cambricon-CIM** (whose own geomean is 3.69× over RAELLA), at a system-level compute density of **1.4 TOPS/mm²** under a 300 mm² normalized budget.
- **ADC/conversion energy:** cumulative reduction of **91.7%**, decomposed by the ablation as fully-analog computing (5.9× array-energy reduction, 4× ADC-energy reduction) → embedded ADC (further 25% ADC energy) → differential array (further 2×) → base-reforming coding (further ~12%). For reference, Cambricon-CIM's digital encoding/reconstruction alone contributes up to 26.2% of its total energy, and its weight-centering encoding imposes >2.5× array-energy overhead.
- **Signal quality:** end-to-end VMM RMSE of **1.6%**, SNR **36.19 dB — about 9 dB higher than both RAELLA and Cambricon-CIM**; embedded-DAC DNL +0.52/−2.24 LSB and INL +1.12/−1.14 LSB; analog-MAC DNL +0.15/−1.64 LSB, INL +0.86/−0.79 LSB; ADC peak quantization error 4.46 mV with >8-bit ENOB.
- **Accuracy under non-idealities (Table V):** MobileBERT SQuAD **90.0/82.9 F1/EM (FP32) → 88.5/81.5** (−1.5/−1.4); BERT-Base **88.5/81.2 → 86.8/79.8** (−1.7/−1.4); GPT-2 WikiText-2 PPL **22.8 → 25.7** (+2.9); Llama 2-7B PPL **5.47 → 5.73** (+0.26); ImageNet vision models within ±1.9 points, with VGG16 at −0.1 and ViT at +0.1.
- **Robustness:** linearity R² > 0.9999 for all VMM shapes with max error 0.24% (shape 64) to 0.70% (shape ≥256); 3σ capacitor mismatch 1.184 LSB at Cu = 2.66 fF; post-layout PVT sweeps (TT/SS/FF/FS/SF, −40 °C to 100 °C, 0.9 V ±10%) keep max error below 2 LSB and variance below 1.46 LSB at the worst corner; inference stays stable down to 5-bit ADC resolution and under 2% injected analog noise.
- **Area/power:** 299.57 mm² total, 8.8 W peak / 4.5 W average at 1 GHz; area split array 61% / COMM 30% / BUF 4% / ADC 3% / digital 2%; power split arrays 41% / ADCs 27% / digital 23% / COMM 9%.

## Authors & Credibility

The work comes from HKUST and the AI Chip Center for Emerging Smart Systems (ACCESS, InnoHK), with collaborators at Huazhong University of Science and Technology and Shenzhen University of Advanced Technology. Corresponding author **Fengbin Tu** is an established name in CIM and AI-accelerator architecture, and first author **Zihao Xuan** has prior peer-reviewed CIM work including the ADC-free SRAM IMC macro (TCAS-II 2023) and **YOCO** (DAC 2025) — this paper is explicitly the "2.0" of that line. The methodology (SPICE + post-layout extraction + Timeloop/Accelergy + CACTI) is standard and traceable, and the baselines (RAELLA, ISCA 2023; Cambricon-CIM, HPCA 2026) are the right ones for the claim. The group's familiarity with the circuit primitives is evident and the reported circuit-level numbers (ENOB, DNL/INL, settling error, Monte Carlo LSB error) are the kind of detail that simulation-only architecture papers usually omit.

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty (high-moderate).** The individual ingredients are known — PICO-RAM and the authors' own YOCO already reuse local capacitors for analog shift-and-add; CR-CIM already explores capacitor reconfiguration for ADC reduction; monotonic SAR switching is from Liu et al. 2010. The genuine novelty is the *composition*: Table I's feature matrix shows Charge-CIM is the first to claim all of embedded DAC + in-situ SA + embedded ADC + in-ADC psum add + architecture support simultaneously. The inverse-coding trick that converts the differential ADC's subtraction into psum addition is the cleanest new idea here.

**Rigor (moderate).** Everything is simulated; there is no silicon, no measured macro. Baselines are re-implementations scaled by the authors to a 300 mm² budget, and RAELLA is deliberately reconfigured from memristor to SRAM — a defensible normalization, but it means the RAELLA numbers are not the ones RAELLA's authors published. **The single most concerning data quirk is Table V's ImageNet column**: VGG16 is listed at 86.5% FP32 top-1 and MobileNetV3 at 86.8%, whereas the published top-1 accuracies are roughly 71.6% and 75.2%. These look like top-5 numbers mislabeled as top-1, or a non-standard evaluation subset; either way the accuracy table cannot be taken at face value. Relatedly, ResNet18's INT8 result (80.3%) *exceeds* its FP32 result (77.8%) and the "Final Loss" column reports **+1.9** as if quantization plus analog noise improved the model — a 2.5-point PTQ gain on ResNet18 is not credible and signals an inconsistent evaluation pipeline. The Transformer/LLM rows (SQuAD F1/EM, WikiText-2 PPL) are internally consistent and are the more trustworthy accuracy evidence.

**Missing ablations.** There is no head-to-head against the authors' own YOCO (DAC 2025) despite the title branding this as YOCO 2.0 — YOCO appears only as a row in the feature table, never in the energy/throughput plots. There is no architecture-level comparison against digital CIM (DIMC appears only in a macro-level FoM figure), which matters because the whole premise is that analog wins once the ADC wall is removed. There is also no sensitivity study on the Local/Global SFU sizing, no ablation isolating the offset-encoding compensation adder-tree cost, and no accounting for capacitor aging, systematic BEOL capacitor gradients, or SRAM retention — mismatch is modeled as i.i.d. zero-mean Gaussian, which is optimistic for a large array.

**Overclaiming to watch.** The headline "reduces ADC energy by 91.7%" is measured against the paper's *own* ablation starting point (Cambricon-CIM array and bit-width configuration), not against a neutral reference — the abstract's hedge "under our evaluation setup" is doing real work. There is mild internal tension in that ADCs still consume 27% of chip power after the reduction, so the ADC wall is lowered rather than removed. LLM evaluation covers **prefill only**; decode, which is memory-bound and where a compute-dense CIM array is least advantaged, is not evaluated. Finally, throughput speedups spanning 3.0×–16.0× across models are reported as a geomean without a per-model breakdown of which layer shapes drive the 16× outlier.

**Reproducibility (weak-moderate).** No code, netlists, or artifact link is mentioned. The digital flow (Design Compiler, PrimeTime, CACTI 7.0, Timeloop/Accelergy) is reproducible in principle, but the SPICE MCC/ADC models and the post-layout extraction that anchor every energy number are not released, so an independent party cannot re-derive the 91.7% or the 2.71×.

**Significance (high, if it silicon-validates).** If the six-phase charge journey holds up in fabrication at 256-row array depth, removing bit-slicing is a structurally different answer to the ADC wall than the encoding-based mitigations of RAELLA and Cambricon-CIM, and would reset the design space for INT8 analog CIM.

## Strengths

- The central insight — that the capacitor fabric can be *the same physical object* across DAC, MAC, shift-add, and ADC — is elegant and well argued through Eq. 1–6, with a closed-form end-to-end VMM expression rather than hand-waving.
- Circuit-level evidence is unusually thorough for an architecture paper: DNL/INL curves for both the embedded DAC and the analog MAC separately, ENOB, per-phase timing with residual settling error, Monte Carlo across five process corners and a −40 °C to 100 °C range.
- The ablation (Figure 16) cleanly decomposes the 91.7% into four attributable mechanisms rather than presenting it as one monolithic win.
- Honest engineering detail: they report that P > 2^(M−1) rows are required to support M-bit weights, that error is bounded by the single-array worst case (0.7%) because larger matrices accumulate digitally, and that array + COMM dominate area (91% combined).
- Backward compatibility is a nice touch — the reconfigurable bit mapping natively reproduces Cambricon-CIM's coding-base reformation without hardware changes, so the contribution composes with rather than competes against prior encoding work.

## Limitations

- Simulation-only; no fabricated chip or measured macro, and no released artifacts.
- The ImageNet accuracy column appears mislabeled or non-standard (VGG16 at 86.5% "top-1", ResNet18 gaining 2.5 points from INT8 PTQ), undermining the vision-side accuracy claims.
- Baselines are author-scaled re-implementations; RAELLA is moved off its native memristor substrate to SRAM.
- LLM evaluation is prefill-only; no decode, no KV-cache-bound regime, no batching study.
- No quantitative comparison to the authors' own YOCO despite the "2.0" framing, and no architecture-level digital-CIM comparison.
- Analog error modeling assumes i.i.d. zero-mean Gaussian mismatch and ignores aging, systematic capacitor gradients, and SRAM write/retention effects.
- The 20 ns six-phase budget is a hard serialization floor per VMM; the paper does not analyze how it interacts with pipeline bubbles for small or irregular layer shapes.

## Relevance

对关注 LLM 推理能效上限的读者，这篇给出了「模拟域端到端 INT8 计算」在 Llama 2-7B prefill 上仅 +0.26 PPL 代价换取 8.77× 能效的量化证据，说明 ADC 墙并非模拟 CIM 的不可逾越边界。若你（用户）关心量化算法与硬件的协同设计，文中 offset + inverse coding 的分解方式以及「5-bit ADC 分辨率仍稳定、2% 模拟噪声可容忍」的鲁棒性边界，可直接作为量化/噪声感知训练的目标约束。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How does the 20 ns six-phase latency scale with array depth P? Charge-sharing settling time grows with total capacitance, so the 256-row macro may already be near a practical ceiling — what happens at 512 rows, which would be needed for 9-bit or 10-bit weights?
- The differential inverse-coding scheme halves conversions by pairing two arrays. Does this constrain layer mapping (e.g., requiring even numbers of partial-sum groups), and what is the utilization penalty for layers whose channel counts do not pair evenly?
- Why is there no quantitative comparison against YOCO (the authors' DAC 2025 predecessor)? Isolating which of the four ablation stages is genuinely new relative to YOCO would clarify the incremental contribution.
- The ImageNet numbers need clarification: are these top-1 or top-5, and what explains ResNet18's INT8 result exceeding FP32?
- ADCs still draw 27% of chip power despite the 91.7% conversion-energy reduction. What is the remaining breakdown — sense amplifier, SAR logic, reference distribution — and is there headroom left, or is this now the irreducible floor?
- How would the architecture handle sub-INT8 formats (INT4, FP4, MXFP) that dominate current LLM deployment? The reconfigurable bit-mapping supports lower precision, but the paper's efficiency case is built entirely on INT8.
- For LLM decode, where the bottleneck is weight streaming rather than MAC throughput, does the stationary-weight tile mapping still hold, and what is the effective utilization at batch size 1?
