> **一句话总结：** 该文用 3,200 个「情境化行为探针」（20 个行为模式 × 4 种交互语域）取代人格问卷来测量 LLM 行为风格，发现问卷自评与实际行为平均差 22.7 个百分点（Negative Urgency 差 47.5 点），并提出 Behavioral Mode Axes（BMA）——从**思维链理由**（而非最终回答）的对比激活中提取的方向，在 7 个开源模型上把目标行为选择率从 21.3% 基线推到 82.7%/9.6% 两端（平均 ΔA=73.1，不可解析率仅 0.10%），且证明 response 派生的 BMA-R 与 thought 派生的 BMA-T 余弦仅 0.37、会把「灵活即时组织」漂移成「懒得动、无所谓」的错误机制。

> **TL;DR:** Instead of administering BFI-2/HEXACO/DOSPERT questionnaires to LLMs, this paper builds 3,200 forced-choice *situated* behavioral probes over 20 behavioral patterns × 4 interaction registers, shows self-report and behavior diverge by 22.7 percentage points on average (up to 47.5 pp on Negative Urgency) while behavioral profiles are internally reliable (split-half r = 0.933, 0.963 after Spearman–Brown), and then introduces **Behavioral Mode Axes (BMAs)** — mean-difference activation directions extracted from contrastive behavioral traces. A single first-person **thought-derived** BMA applied at a model-specific "Behavioral Control Layer" moves the target-pole choice rate from a 21.3% unsteered baseline to 82.7% / 9.6% at the two endpoints (mean ΔA = 73.1 across 7 open-weight models and 20 subdomains, unknown rate 0.10%), versus a norm-matched random-direction control that achieves a mean range of only 9.1 (median 0). The paper's own head-to-head baseline is the standard response-derived direction (BMA-R, the CAA/Persona-Vector style construction), which reaches the target option but with mean cosine 0.37 to BMA-T and demonstrable "trait drift" into off-construct mechanisms.

## 核心贡献

- **提出 situated B-data 框架**：不问「你有多爱整洁」，而是把模型放进具体情境让它*选择、建议或执行*。基于 BFI-2C、DOSPERT、GCS、HEXACO、UPPS-P 五套量表的 20 个 facet，构造 3,200 个二选一探针，覆盖四种语域（第一人称决策 / 日常建议 / 任务建议 / 任务执行），每个 model×subdomain×register 单元 40 题。
- **量化了「人格幻觉」**：问卷自评与行为画像在所有 model–subdomain 对上平均相差 22.7 个百分点，34.4% 的对差 ≥25 点、20.0% 差 ≥40 点；同时行为画像本身是可靠的（register 内 split-half r=0.933），说明差距不是噪声而是自评与行为的真实分离。
- **发现行为画像的语域依赖性**：跨语域画像相关性平均只有 0.76（区间 0.37–0.97），两个 advice 语域最像（r=0.89），而第一人称 vs 任务执行最不像（r=0.63），subdomain 层面四语域极差平均 23.4 个百分点——即「模型人格」不是语域不变量。
- **提出 Behavioral Mode Axes 与 Behavioral Control Layer (BCL) 概念**：干净的可控性并非均匀分布在各层，而是集中在偏前中部的连续层带（Llama-3.1-8B 在 L08–L12 峰值 clean range 0.82–0.89，L14 之后跌破 0.30）；归一化深度上 Llama 约 0.26–0.28、Qwen 随规模从 0.41→0.45→0.51 逐步变深、Gemma 在 0.44–0.49。
- **论证「轴的来源」比「轴的存在」更关键**：thought-derived BMA-T 与 response-derived BMA-R 在 BCL 处平均余弦仅 0.37，SAE 特征读出显示 BMA-T 对齐「即兴/随机应变」这一动机特征（Feature 17024），BMA-R 则对齐「我不在乎 / 没什么大不了」的冷漠低投入特征（Feature 103914）——同样翻转了选项，机制却错了。

## Key Contributions

- **A situated behavioral-data (B-data) measurement framework for LLM "personality."** 3,200 contrastive forced-choice probes over 20 behavioral patterns anchored (but not scored) in BFI-2C, DOSPERT, GCS, HEXACO and UPPS-P, crossed with four interaction registers. Both poles are written to be plausible, competent and non-moralizing, with explicit anti-caricature and anti-rationalization clauses so the "risky" pole cannot degenerate into a laziness/efficiency direction.
- **Empirical evidence that questionnaire self-reports do not track situated behavior**, quantified rather than asserted, plus reliability evidence that the behavioral side is not itself noise.
- **Behavioral Mode Axes**: mean-difference residual-stream directions computed over *thought-token spans* (BMA-T) versus *response-token spans* (BMA-R) of paired pole traces, applied as h ← h + c·v during both prefill and decoding.
- **An evaluation protocol that penalizes collapse.** Steering strength is reported as *clean directional range* — the largest A(c⁺) − A(c⁻) over coefficient intervals whose mean and max unparseable ("unknown") rates stay below 1%, plus a 5% repetition budget — which explicitly rejects the common failure where a large apparent range comes from one side degenerating into gibberish.
- **The trait-drift diagnosis**, supported by a same-scenario qualitative comparison, a cross-subdomain drift audit over five subdomains, and BatchTopK SAE feature attribution (131,072 latents, k = 64, 500M activation tokens) at the BCL.

## Methodology

The pipeline has three stages. **(1) Probe construction.** For each of 20 psychometric facets, the authors define two opposing *behavioral modes* — an action plus the reason that action is attractive — rather than trait adjectives. Every mode specification ends with discriminant clauses ("the center is X, not Y") to keep neighboring subdomains (e.g., Sensation Seeking vs. Financial Risk vs. Lack of Premeditation) from collapsing. All scenarios are synthesized with Claude Opus 4.6, half item-anchored to an original questionnaire item (used only as a situation-coverage anchor) and half free-form to avoid inheriting the narrow domestic settings of the source inventories. Automated validation checks structure, pole assignment (risky = option A, safe = option B), label leakage (the probe may not contain "risky", "safe", "low-pole", or the facet name), and rationale form; failing batches are discarded wholesale and regenerated. A separate, equal-sized scenario set is generated for axis extraction, with no shared situation or identifier — so steering is always evaluated as transfer.

**(2) Profile estimation.** Responses are parsed by string-matching a "Choice: A/B" label; a cell score is the fraction choosing the instrument-keyed pole over 40 probes; subdomain score averages the four registers (160 probes). The questionnaire comparison administers the original 137 items one per request with an empty system prompt, in each instrument's native format, with published reverse keys, normalized to a common 0–100 scale.

**(3) BMA extraction and steering.** v = normalized mean over N scenarios of h̄(low trace) − h̄(high trace), where h̄ averages activations over either thought-only or response-only assistant token spans. Layer and endpoint coefficients are selected *jointly* per model by maximizing the subdomain-averaged clean range subject to the unknown-rate and repetition budgets. Because absolute coefficients are model-private, the paper also reports relative coefficients ĉ = c/δ where δ is the unnormalized mean-difference length: absolute endpoints span a factor of ~50 across models while relative ones stay within a factor of two (−1.40 to −2.79 and +2.11 to +4.11), and at Llama-3.1-8B's BCL the positive endpoint displaces a token's residual by under 30% of its own norm (mean residual norm 10.45). Models: Llama-3.1-8B/70B-Instruct, Qwen2.5-7B/14B/32B-Instruct, Gemma-2-2B/9B-it for steering, plus Gemma-2-27B-it and two Llama-3.1-8B LoRA adapters (good-medical / bad-medical) for profiling only. All runs use greedy decoding on H200 GPUs.

## Results & Highlights

- **Self-report vs. behavior gap (RQ1).** Across all model–subdomain pairs the mean |questionnaire − behavioral| gap is **22.7 percentage points**; **34.4%** of pairs differ by ≥25 points and **20.0%** by ≥40 points. The largest subdomain gap is **Negative Urgency at 47.5 points**, where models self-report more emotion-driven impulsivity than they enact.
- **Reliability and register dependence.** Within-register split-half correlation is **0.933** (**0.963** after Spearman–Brown), but cross-register profile correlation averages only **0.76** (range **0.37–0.97**); the two advice registers correlate at **r = 0.89** while first-person vs. task sits at **r = 0.63**, and the mean four-register range per model–subdomain pair is **23.4 pp**.
- **Cross-register steering (Table 2, the headline result).** A single first-person thought-derived BMA at the model-specific BCL moves the target-pole choice rate from **A₀ = 21.3%** unsteered to **A⁺ = 82.7%** and **A⁻ = 9.6%**, i.e. **mean ΔA = 73.1** across 7 models × 20 subdomains, with **mean unknown rate 0.10%**. Best: **Qwen2.5-14B ΔA = 81.7** (A⁻ 5.7 → A⁺ 87.4); weakest: **Gemma-2-2B ΔA = 61.8**. Transfer degrades gracefully by register: **79.6 (first-person) → 73.8 (task advice) → 71.6 (daily advice) → 67.4 (task execution)**.
- **Norm-matched random-direction control.** Replacing each subdomain's BMA with an isotropic unit direction at the same layer and coefficients gives a mean range of **9.1** (median **0**) versus **77.9** on-target on the same 40-probe sets — a ~8.6× gap, and generations stay intact rather than collapsing, so the effect is not merely "push the residual stream hard."
- **BCL localization.** In Llama-3.1-8B, mean clean directional range peaks at **L08–L12 (0.82–0.89)** and falls **below 0.30 after L14**. Peak normalized depths: Llama **0.26–0.28**, Qwen **0.41 / 0.45 / 0.51** (7B/14B/32B), Gemma **0.44–0.49** — bands recur across families but their location is model-specific and drifts deeper with scale within a family.
- **Axis geometry.** Over the 190 subdomain pairs at Llama-3.1-8B's BCL, median |cos| = **0.13**, **85% below 0.3**, only 8 above 0.5. Within-DOSPERT pairs run **0.44–0.87** (one risk construct in five content domains), while same-instrument pairs elsewhere are near-orthogonal (Organization–Productiveness **0.03**, Fairness–Greed Avoidance **0.02**) and the largest cross-instrument pair is Sincerity–Social Acceptance at **0.54** — proximity tracks mechanism, not instrument.
- **BMA source and trait drift (RQ3).** BMA-T and BMA-R at the BCL are only weakly aligned (**mean cosine 0.37**). SAE readout attributes BMA-T to an on-construct improvisation feature and BMA-R to an off-construct indifference/low-effort feature. Documented drift patterns: flexible improvisation → low-effort dismissal (Organization); calibrated risk acceptance → danger romanticization (Recreational Risk); pressure-relief concession → people-pleasing or, in Box 8, the *opposite choice* (GCS Yielding); instrumental impression management → generic flattery that contradicts the stated choice (Sincerity).
- **Specificity is only partial — reported honestly.** Swapping in a foreign subdomain's axis still moves target choices: **63.0** for aligned targets (mean |cos| 0.48), **28.0** for median-aligned (0.14), **42.8** for near-orthogonal (0.01), against **77.9** on-target. Regressing off-target range on pair cosine gives **r = 0.38 with intercept 36.0** — a pair of BMAs sharing no direction still moves a third of the scale.

## Authors & Credibility

Nine authors from Shanghai Jiao Tong University, Shanghai AI Laboratory, HKUST and Xi'an Jiaotong University, with Haoze Liu and Run Liu as equal first authors and Na Zou (Shanghai AI Lab) as corresponding author. Huiqi Deng and Guanchu Wang have prior interpretability/XAI track records. The paper is formatted as an AAAI submission. Code and data are released at `github.com/lhz191/LLM-Behavioral-Personality`, and the appendix documents the full compute environment (H200s, PyTorch 2.9.1, Transformers 4.57.6, bfloat16 inference with float32 axis accumulation, 900-token generation cap, greedy decoding), the exact coefficient sweep grids per model, and the SAE recipe. The literature framing is accurate and current — it correctly positions BMAs against Contrastive Activation Addition (Rimsky et al.), Representation Engineering (Zou et al.) and Persona Vectors (Chen et al.), and against the validity critiques of LLM psychometrics (Shu et al., Sühr et al., Han et al.'s "personality illusion").

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty (moderate-high).** Activation steering of trait-like behavior is not new; the genuinely new moves are (a) the thought-span vs. response-span extraction contrast with a mechanism-level (SAE) diagnosis of why they differ, and (b) the register axis, which makes "cross-context consistency" measurable instead of assumed. The B-data framing itself is a repackaging of an argument already made by Han et al. (2025), which the paper credits.

**Rigor (mixed, with one real gap and one real leak).** The strong points are the collapse-penalizing clean-range metric, the disjoint axis/probe scenario sets, the norm-matched random-direction control, and the relative-coefficient normalization — all of which are more careful than typical steering papers. The gap: **RQ3, the paper's most distinctive claim, is essentially unquantified in the main text.** Section 3.3 states that "we use an LLM judge to score whether the response expresses the target behavioral mode and whether it introduces off-target drift," yet no aggregate drift rate for BMA-T vs. BMA-R appears anywhere in the main results — the argument rests on one same-scenario box (Figure 6), four appendix boxes, and two hand-picked SAE features. A single table of judged on-construct rates over all 20 subdomains would have settled it; its absence is the paper's largest missing ablation. The leak: the reported triple (layer, c⁻, c⁺) is chosen by *maximizing* the aggregated clean directional range, and Appendix E notes the repetition budget is "measured on the same cross-register sample the main table scores." Table 2's ΔA = 73.1 is therefore at least partly a selected-maximum rather than a clean held-out number.

**Paper-specific quirks worth naming.** (1) There is **no implemented external baseline** — no CAA, RepE or Persona Vectors run on these probes. BMA-R stands in for all prior response-derived methods, which is a reasonable proxy but a self-constructed one, and it is the *only* method the headline comparison beats. (2) The 22.7 pp self-report gap compares a normalized 1–5/1–7 Likert average against a forced-choice rate; these are not commensurate scales, so part of the "gap" could be scale-mapping artifact rather than dissociation. The paper's own premise — that self-report is elicitation-sensitive — also means the questionnaire number depends on one arbitrary elicitation (one item per request, empty system prompt). (3) The **entire dataset, both probes and the rationale traces the axes are built from, is generated by Claude Opus 4.6**, and the drift audit is judged by an LLM. What is being measured is partly one frontier model's theory of what each behavioral pole looks like; no human agreement statistics are reported for the manual review pass. (4) The Appendix F transfer result arguably undercuts the specificity story: an orthogonal axis (|cos| ≈ 0.01) still yields a 42.8 range vs. 77.9 on-target, and after subtracting the random floor the three alignment bands come out **+51.8 / +18.5 / +35.8** — non-monotonic in cosine. The authors interpret this as "each axis carries its own mechanism that sometimes lands on the target's off-pole option," which is plausible but is supported by the same qualitative box-reading evidence as RQ3.

**Reproducibility (good).** Public code and data, exact sweep grids, model list, decoding settings, SAE hyperparameters and library versions. The main caveat is that reproducing the probe set exactly requires Claude Opus 4.6 generations, though the released data should suffice.

**Significance (solid but bounded).** The register-dependence finding is the most transferable result: it implies any published "LLM personality profile" measured in first-person is only weakly predictive (r ≈ 0.63) of task-execution behavior, which matters for persona/safety evaluation design. The steering result is a clean demonstration but stays inside 2B–70B open-weight instruct models; no reasoning models, no MoE, no frontier systems, and no downstream application showing that BMA control improves anything a user cares about.

**Red flags.** Mild overclaiming in the phrase "cleaner control" — cleaner is demonstrated on mechanism *quality* only anecdotally, while the quantitative tables report BMA-T alone; the reader cannot see whether BMA-R's ΔA is higher, lower or equal. Gemma-2-27B is profiled but excluded from steering without stated justification.

## Strengths

- The clean-directional-range metric with a <1% unknown-rate constraint and a 5% repetition budget is a genuinely better evaluation practice than the raw directional ranges commonly reported in the steering literature, and Figure 4a explicitly demonstrates the failure mode it guards against.
- Rigorous leakage control: separate axis and probe scenario sets with no shared identifiers, and a label-leakage check that forbids the probe from naming its own dimension.
- The norm-matched isotropic random-direction control (9.1 mean range, median 0) is exactly the right null and is often omitted in this literature.
- The relative-coefficient analysis (ĉ within a factor of two across seven models whose absolute endpoints span ~50×) is a nice piece of evidence that the BCL phenomenon is a model property rather than an artifact of the search procedure.
- Appendix A is an unusually thoughtful methodological essay on why human personality taxonomies may not transfer to LLMs, engaging the psycho-lexical and factor-analytic counterevidence directly instead of hand-waving.
- Intellectual honesty in Appendix F: the authors report the off-target transfer numbers that complicate their own specificity claim rather than burying them.

## Limitations

- No aggregate quantitative trait-drift metric for BMA-T vs. BMA-R, despite defining an LLM-judge protocol for exactly that; the central RQ3 claim is carried by qualitative examples.
- Layer and coefficient selection maximizes the same clean-range quantity that is then reported, on samples overlapping the scoring set — the headline ΔA should be read as an upper envelope.
- No external steering baseline (CAA, RepE, Persona Vectors) is implemented for comparison.
- Probes, axis rationales, and drift judgments are all LLM-produced; no human inter-rater reliability is reported for the manual quality-control pass.
- Behavioral readout is a binary A/B forced choice, which the authors acknowledge "registers that behavior moved, not which mechanism moved it."
- Steering coverage stops at 70B open-weight instruct models; Gemma-2-27B is profiled but not steered, and no reasoning-mode or frontier models are tested.
- The questionnaire-vs-behavior gap rests on one elicitation configuration and on mapping two different response formats onto a shared 0–100 scale.
- No demonstration that BMA control yields downstream benefit (safety, personalization quality, user preference) beyond flipping probe choices.

## Relevance

如果你（用户）关注 LLM 评测的构念效度与可解释性，这篇给出了一个可复用的实证结论：第一人称问卷式画像与任务执行行为只有 r≈0.63 的一致性，任何基于自评的 persona/人格评测都需要重新设计为情境化行为探针。同时，「thought-span vs response-span 提取的激活方向余弦仅 0.37」这一发现对 activation steering 与 LLM-as-a-judge 的方法论都有直接借鉴价值——控制到了目标输出不等于控制到了目标机制。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- What is the aggregate LLM-judge on-construct rate for BMA-T vs. BMA-R across all 20 subdomains, and does BMA-R actually achieve a comparable ΔA? Without that pairing, "cleaner control" is a mechanism claim without a control-strength cost accounting.
- Does the BCL band survive a held-out layer/coefficient selection split — i.e., select the triple on subdomains 1–10 and report on 11–20?
- The thought/response distinction should interact strongly with reasoning models that emit real chain-of-thought. Does a BMA extracted from a model's *own* sampled reasoning (rather than a synthesized rationale) beat BMA-T?
- Is the observed drift a property of response spans per se, or of the specific instruction that the response field "concentrates on the action"? An ablation that varies only the response-field prompt discipline would separate the two.
- Given that a near-orthogonal axis still moves choices by 42.8, what fraction of any steering vector's effect in this literature is a generic "flip the default option" component, and can it be projected out?
- Do BMAs compose? Can two axes be applied simultaneously to produce a targeted two-dimensional behavioral style without multiplying drift?
- Do the register-dependent profile shifts (mean 23.4 pp) predict anything about real deployment failures, e.g. agentic tool-use behavior diverging from a model's stated first-person policy?
