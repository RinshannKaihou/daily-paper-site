> **一句话总结：** 作者提出 Feature Nonlocality（FNL）——把 SAE 特征激活对各前缀位置的平方梯度范数归一化后取香农熵，用一次反向传播就得到"该特征到底读了多长上下文"的无标签度量；FNL 与 Ma et al. (2026) 的 token 注入误激活呈 ρ=−0.39~−0.46 的负相关、与释义鲁棒性呈 ρ=+0.27 正相关，区分 token 驱动 vs. 上下文依赖特征的 AUC 达 0.73–0.84；用它审计 Assogba et al. (2026) 的越狱防御特征，发现 25 个"有效"特征里有 21 个 FNL 恒为 0（只在 prompt 第 0 位激活的位置/attention-sink 指示器），真正的防御增益（held-out 安全率 0.511→0.911）几乎全部来自这批位置特征而非"识别有害意图"的语义特征。

> **TL;DR:** Feature Nonlocality (FNL) is the entropy of an SAE feature's normalized per-position gradient influence — a label-free, LLM-free witness of how much context a feature actually reads. It separates token-driven from context-dependent reasoning features with AUC 0.73–0.84 on DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B (ρ = −0.46 vs. token-injection recovery, ρ = +0.27 vs. paraphrase robustness), is near-orthogonal to the ReasonScore token-cue selector (ρ = −0.214), and exposes that 21 of 25 CC-Delta jailbreak-mitigation features have exactly zero FNL — they are BOS/positional indicators, and steering only that positional subset recovers held-out Few-Shot-JSON safety from 0.511 to 0.911 while the 4 genuinely content-aware features stay at 0.504. Steering the top-20% FNL envelope raises MATH-500 avg@4 from 0.865 to 0.911 (+4.6) vs. +3.8 for low-FNL, +3.6 for random, and +3.9 for the ReasonScore-selected feature f3466 — but this gain does not transfer to Gemma-2-9B or Qwen3-8B.

## 核心贡献

- **定义 FNL**：固定 SAE 层 ℓ 与特征 a，在其发放事件处对输入 embedding 做反向传播，得到每个前缀位置 t 的影响 J_a(t)=‖∂z_a/∂x_t‖²，归一化成概率分布后取香农熵 H(a,P)，再在 top-k 发放事件上取均值。无需对比数据集、无需 LLM 打标、无需干预实验，一次前向 + 一次反向即可对整层字典并行计算。灵感明确来自全息对偶（bulk 自由度按尺度对应边界理论），把 transformer 视作 token 向量空间的"时间演化"。
- **验证 FNL 是特征内在属性**：跨 WikiText / GSM8K / Code-Python 三个话题迥异语料，Gemma-2-2B 各层 per-feature 排序的 Spearman 一致性 0.71–0.92；扩展到 Llama-3-8B、Qwen3-8B 并用 split-half reliability 做去衰减（disattenuated ρ̃ 0.53–0.89）。FNL 随深度先升后在中层饱和，且与 decoder 余弦几何（write-side）比与 auto-interp 描述的语义相似度对得更齐。
- **把 FNL 锚定到语义抽象度**：复现 Ma et al. (2026) 的 token 注入（假阳性）与释义不变性（假阴性）两条证伪管线，报告 FNL 与两者的显著相关，并给出 TD/CD 分类 AUC。
- **越狱防御机制审计**：发现 CC-Delta 选出的"有效"防御特征绝大多数是零上下文触及的位置伪影，重写了该类防御的机制解释。
- **FNL 引导的 envelope steering**：仅按 FNL 排序选 top-20% 特征做 clamping，在 R1-distill 模型上超过 token-cue 选出的代表特征，但作者自己把它标为 proof-of-concept 而非可靠预测器。

## Key Contributions

- **A gradient-based, label-free abstraction metric.** FNL := H(p_a) where p_a(t) ∝ ‖∂z_a(T,P)/∂x_t‖²₂ over prefix positions t ≤ T. Unlike auto-interp (needs an LLM judge) or falsification pipelines (need curated contrastive data and paraphrases), FNL needs only a backward pass; it also extends to any linear subspace of the residual stream, giving the residual-stream baseline H(h_T) as a reference curve.
- **Stability evidence before validity evidence.** The paper first establishes FNL as a well-behaved measurement (cross-corpus rank stability, depth profile, saturation in revealed context length T, agreement with decoder geometry) before claiming it tracks abstraction — a discipline many interpretability metric papers skip.
- **A negative result with teeth.** The jailbreak audit is the strongest contribution: it converts "SAEs mitigate jailbreaks" into "a BOS-position artifact mitigates wrapper jailbreaks," which is a materially different safety claim.
- **Discriminant validity against existing selectors.** FNL is shown *not* to be a repackaged token-frequency statistic: ρ(FNL, ReasonScore) = −0.214 over 98 features, and corpus-contrast enrichment correlates only mildly (5.56 bits mean for the 212 features enriched ≥100× vs. 5.05 bits dictionary-wide).
- **Unusually complete reproducibility apparatus**, including an "Agentic Publication Protocol" repo intended to be read and re-run by an AI coding agent.

## Methodology

The core object is the per-position influence of input embeddings on a feature's activation at the query position. For a prompt P of T tokens, with h_T the layer-ℓ residual stream at the last position and z_a(T,P) = [σ(W_enc h_T + b_enc)]_a the feature activation, the influence is J_a(t,T,P) = ‖∂z_a/∂x_t‖²₂, computed only at firing events (z_a > τ). Normalizing J over t ≤ T yields a distribution over the causal prefix; its Shannon entropy is the per-prompt nonlocality, and the dataset-level FNL H(a,D) averages over the top-k max-activation firing events. Defaults: k = 32, T = 128 (so the uniform ceiling is log₂128 = 7 bits). The authors flag that the normalization (could be softmax) and the entropy (could be Rényi or inverse participation ratio) are arbitrary choices left for future work.

Validation proceeds along four axes. (1) *Stability*: 2,000 jointly-active features ranked independently on three corpora, at layers 5/12/20 of Gemma-2-2B plus three depths each of Llama-3-8B and Qwen3-8B, with split-half reliability supplying a disattenuation ceiling. (2) *Geometry*: for six anchor features, the top-10 decoder-cosine neighbours occupy a narrow FNL band, whereas neighbours under BGE-large-en-v1.5 embeddings of auto-interp descriptions spread across the whole axis — Table S2 makes the point vividly (f608 "Robert" at 0.98 bits has a semantic neighbour f9995 "references to specific individuals" at 4.76 bits). (3) *Abstraction proxies*: the token-injection pipeline of Ma et al. (2026) splices a feature's top unigrams/bigrams/trigrams into non-reasoning passages and measures activation recovery via Cohen's d, classifying features as token-driven (d ≥ 0.8) through context-dependent (d < 0.2); the paraphrase pipeline scores S_a = med(R(z_para)) − med(R(z_shuffle)) with baseline-corrected retention. (4) *Downstream*: auditing CC-Delta jailbreak features on LlamaScope layer 17, and envelope steering (clamping all features in the top/bottom/random 20% by FNL at gain γ) on MATH-500 and MMLU-Pro, graded by a local Llama-3.3-70B judge.

## Results & Highlights

- **Token-injection discrimination (DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B, ~100 reasoning-enriched features).** Spearman ρ(FNL, injection recovery) = −0.46 at LlamaScope-32k layer 12 (AUC 0.84), −0.42 at layer 11 (AUC 0.77), −0.41 at layer 10 (AUC 0.81), and −0.39 at Galichin-65k layer 19 (AUC 0.73). Mean FNL for token-driven vs. context-dependent classes: 4.19 vs. 4.71 bits at layer 12. AUC = 0.5 is chance, so the 0.73–0.84 range is the paper's headline "73–84% of random TD/CD pairs ordered correctly."
- **Paraphrase robustness.** ρ(FNL, S_a) = +0.27, p = 0.011, n = 96 features — a genuinely weak correlation that the authors report honestly rather than dressing up; the median S_a does increase monotonically across the four injection classes.
- **Cross-corpus stability.** Gemma-2-2B layer 5: ρ = 0.918 (Wiki–GSM8K), 0.859 (Wiki–Code), 0.853 (GSM8K–Code), decaying to 0.726/0.734/0.710 at layer 20. Disattenuated against split-half ceilings, Gemma-2-2B layer 5 reaches ρ̃ = 0.894, but Llama-3-8B layer 15 is the weak cell at ρ_raw = 0.404, ρ̃ = 0.531 — i.e. roughly half the attainable agreement is lost on Llama mid-depth.
- **Jailbreak audit (StrongReject, 404 requests, held-out Few-Shot-JSON wrapper).** Unsteered defense rate 0.511 held-out / 0.963 plain. Steering all 25 CC-Delta features: 0.887 held-out. The 21-feature *positional* subset (all with FNL = 0, firing only at position 0 of unwrapped prompts): **0.911** held-out — better than the full set. The 4 content-aware, nonzero-FNL features: **0.504**, i.e. indistinguishable from unsteered. Norm-matched random directions give 0.630 (‖v‖ = 2.04) and 0.775 (‖v‖ = 3.19), so a random direction of the same magnitude beats the "semantic" subset by 0.13–0.27. This is the paper's most consequential number.
- **MATH-500 envelope steering (layer 19, 65k SAE, γ = 1.10, avg@4, Llama-3.3-70B judge).** Unsteered 0.865 → high-FNL **0.911 (+4.6 pts)**, low-FNL 0.903 (+3.8), size-matched random 0.901 (+3.6), ReasonScore-selected single feature f3466 at γ = 2 scoring 0.904 (+3.9). High-FNL also produced the shortest traces (3,918 vs. 4,214 unsteered tokens). At n = 480 with majority-of-4 the same cell reads 0.881 → 0.942 high / 0.923 low / 0.940 random.
- **Honest interval reporting.** Table S4: high − random at L19/20%/n=480 is **+0.010, 90% CI [0.000, +0.021]** — the lower bound touches zero. high − low is +0.019 [+0.004, +0.035] at n=480 and +0.060 [+0.020, +0.100] at n=100. On Gemma-2-9B layer 20 the high − random contrast is **negative**: −0.013 [−0.024, −0.002].
- **Transfer failure, stated by the authors.** On Gemma-2-9B (MMLU-Pro, greedy, n=1400) unsteered is 0.440 and *every* steered arm is below it (high-FNL 0.436/0.424/0.413/0.407 across cells); the low-FNL arm collapses to 0.083 at 5%/γ=5. On Qwen3-8B unsteered 0.752 vs. high-FNL 0.752/0.747/0.753 — no gain anywhere.
- **Discriminant validity.** ρ(FNL, ReasonScore) = −0.214 (p = 0.035, n = 98); enrichment ≥100× features average 5.56 bits vs. 5.05 dictionary-wide.
- **Reproduction fidelity.** Re-running Ma et al. (2026) end-to-end recovers class counts 46/12/21/21 against their published 46/12/19/23; re-running CC-Delta recovers held-out safety 0.511 → 0.753 at ‖v‖ = 1.6 and 0.895 at ‖v‖ = 3.2, vs. 0.343 for a dense contrastive direction and 0.567 for a random one.

## Authors & Credibility

Chuqiao Lin and Shivaji Sondhi are at the Rudolf Peierls Centre for Theoretical Physics, Oxford; Xiao-Liang Qi is at Stanford's Leinweber Institute for Theoretical Physics. Sondhi and Qi are senior condensed-matter/high-energy theorists (Qi is well known for topological insulators and holography/quantum-information work), not established mechanistic-interpretability researchers — which shows in both directions. The holographic-duality framing (bulk degrees of freedom ↔ boundary length scales, FNL as a "radial coordinate" in time) is genuinely novel framing but does no computational work in the paper; conversely the metrological instincts (split-half reliability ceilings, disattenuated correlations, paired bootstrap CIs, an explicit hyperparameter-provenance table) are more careful than the interpretability norm. Engagement with the literature is up to date and specific (GemmaScope, LlamaScope, Qwen-Scope, Galichin et al., Ma et al., Assogba et al.), and the two key reference pipelines were reproduced rather than cited. Code is released at github.com/lccqqqqq/sae-feature-nonlocality under the "Agentic Publication Protocol" (Lu & Qi 2026), with all figures/tables reproducible in CPU-minutes from stored intermediates and ~1,000 GPU-hours to regenerate raw data.

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty (moderate-high).** Gradient-attribution over prefix positions is standard; the contribution is *aggregating it into an entropy and treating that scalar as an abstraction axis*, plus showing it is not a proxy for existing selectors. The holography analogy is decorative. The design space is admittedly unexplored: the authors concede that softmax normalization, Rényi entropy, or inverse participation ratio would all be defensible alternatives, and no comparison is run — a missing ablation on the metric's own definition, which is a notable gap for a paper whose entire contribution is a metric. There is also no comparison against simpler nonlocality baselines one would immediately reach for (e.g. attention-entropy at the firing position, or the length of the window needed to recover 90% of activation), so we cannot tell how much of FNL's signal a cheaper statistic would capture.

**Rigor (good, with caveats).** The correlational evidence is honestly weak in places and the paper says so: ρ = +0.27 on paraphrase robustness (n = 96) is a small effect, and the AUC 0.73 at layer 19 — the *same* layer used for all steering experiments — is the weakest of the four cells, so the abstraction claim is thinnest exactly where it is applied downstream. The steering section's claim 1 (selective power over a random envelope) is essentially unsupported: +0.010 with 90% CI [0.000, +0.021] at the primary cell and a significantly *negative* contrast on Gemma. The paper's own Table S3 caption concedes the pattern: high-FNL beats unsteered only on the two R1-distilled stacks "where the unsteered model degenerates," which strongly suggests the +4.6 is repairing a distillation pathology rather than exploiting abstraction. Claim 2 (high beats low FNL) is better supported and includes a nice control — an activation-magnitude-matched envelope injects more perturbation yet is less harmful than the low-FNL arm (−0.023 [−0.039, −0.008]), which argues the harm is nonlocality-specific rather than a norm artifact.

**Paper-specific quirks.** (a) The measurement convention excludes the window-initial attention-sink position, yet the headline jailbreak finding is about position-0 firing — the interaction between "we exclude the sink from influence support" and "these features are sink-like" deserves more scrutiny than the one paragraph it gets, since an FNL of exactly 0 for 21 features is partly a definitional consequence of the convention. (b) FNL is only measured at *max-activation* firing events (top-k = 32), so it characterizes a feature's behaviour in its tail, not typically. (c) The abstraction ground truth is itself borrowed from a single source (Ma et al. 2026) whose classification uses "the best of eight injection strategies," a max-statistic that inflates effect sizes; FNL is validated against a proxy, not a label. (d) Main-text Table 4 (avg@4, +4.6) and supplementary Table S3 (maj@4, n=480, +6.1 to 0.942) report the same setting under different aggregations; the abstract quotes the framing that is easiest to read as a clean win.

**Significance (high for the audit, low for the steering).** The jailbreak result should change how the SAE-safety literature reads its own successes; the steering result should not be cited as evidence that FNL predicts steering utility, and to their credit the authors explicitly say so.

**Red flags.** Mild overclaiming in the abstract — "improves MATH-500 accuracy by 4.6 points … and outperforms steering low-FNL features" is true but the hedge "gains are model-specific" understates that on two of three model families *nothing* beats the unsteered baseline. Otherwise the paper is unusually forthcoming about its negative cells.

## Strengths

- The negative/auditing application is more valuable than the positive one, and the paper resists the temptation to bury it.
- Metric-hygiene practices rarely seen in interpretability: split-half reliability ceilings and disattenuated correlations, paired bootstrap CIs at fixed seeds, a table of every hyperparameter range explored with the selection criterion, and an explicit statement that only the steering gain was tuned against a performance signal.
- Explicit discriminant-validity test against ReasonScore and corpus-contrast enrichment, pre-empting the obvious "this is just token frequency" objection.
- Both reference pipelines (Ma et al., Assogba et al.) were reproduced end-to-end with reported agreement, including a documented mismatch (detection-stage effect size) rather than a silent one.
- The metric is cheap relative to its alternatives, parallelizable across a whole layer's dictionary, and requires no LLM judge — genuinely useful as a screening filter.

## Limitations

- FNL is correlational only; the paper never claims causal identification of abstraction and the correlations (|ρ| ≈ 0.27–0.46) are moderate at best.
- Absolute FNL values are comparable only across studies using the same BOS/window convention and the same T and k — cross-paper comparison is therefore fragile.
- Steering utility does not transfer beyond reasoning-distilled Llama models; on Gemma-2-9B and Qwen3-8B the method offers nothing over unsteered.
- No systematic comparison of alternative normalizations/entropies, no attention-based or window-ablation baseline for "contextual reach."
- All evaluation rests on pre-trained public SAEs (GemmaScope / LlamaScope / Qwen-Scope / Galichin), which inherit feature absorption and splitting; the paper notes this but does not test whether FNL is sensitive to it.
- Grading by a local Llama-3.3-70B judge on MATH-500 introduces an unquantified judge-error floor for deltas of 1–5 points.

## Relevance

若你（用户）关注机制可解释性、SAE 特征筛选，或做 steering / 越狱防御的安全评估，这篇的"位置伪影冒充语义防御"审计结论值得直接纳入评审 checklist——它给出了一个无需 LLM 打标、可对整个字典并行计算的低成本筛查指标。对做 reasoning feature steering 的工作，其 ρ(FNL, ReasonScore) = −0.214 与跨模型不可迁移的负结果，是判断"token-cue 筛选是否够用"的有力反例。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would a simple attention-entropy or "minimum context window to recover 90% activation" baseline match FNL's AUC 0.73–0.84? Without this, the entropy-of-gradients construction is unbenchmarked against cheaper alternatives.
- Since the measurement convention excludes the attention-sink position, how much of the "21 of 25 features have zero FNL" result is definitional? What does the audit look like if the sink position is included in the influence support?
- Does FNL computed over *typical* firing events (rather than top-32 max-activation events) tell a different story, especially for features with heavy-tailed activation distributions?
- On the two R1-distilled stacks the high-FNL envelope helps but the base/native-reasoning models it does not — is the gain repairing a distillation-induced degenerate failure mode? A per-problem analysis of which MATH-500 items flip would settle this quickly.
- Can FNL be turned from a correlational witness into a falsification test, e.g. by predicting *which* context spans a feature reads and then ablating exactly those spans?
- Does FNL applied to arbitrary residual-stream directions (not SAE features) reveal a layered decomposition of the residual stream by contextual reach, as the authors speculate? That would be a much stronger claim than the current feature-level result.
