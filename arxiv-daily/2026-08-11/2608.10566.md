> **一句话总结：** 该文证明 INLP 式"迭代擦除方向计数"不是概念维度的仿射不变量——在一个已知秩为 1 的高斯构造里，一个保持信息、保持充分维度与最小守护秩的可逆剪切（shear）就能把欧氏擦除计数从 1 变成 2（岭权重 λ∈{0,.1,1,10} 均成立），两输出全 QR 版本则从累计编辑秩 2 变成环境维度 4；在复现某视频世界模型论文的有限 Adam/MSE/QR 停止协议时，恒等映射 20/20 次运行只接受 1 次更新，而每个剪切 a∈{.5,.75,1,1.25,2} 的 20/20 次运行都接受 ≥2 次更新，而只有当度量、探针、正则、tie-breaking 被一致地"协变输运"时整条轨迹才是仿射等变的。

> **TL;DR:** Iterative null-space-projection style erasure counts (INLP-descendant "how many directions encode this concept?" numbers) are shown to be procedure-relative, not intrinsic. In a population Gaussian construction with sufficient linear dimension 1 and minimum guarding rank 1, an invertible shear flips the cumulative Euclidean ridge erasure count from 1 to 2 for every finite λ ∈ {0,.1,1,10}; a two-output full-QR variant matching the motivating V-JEPA2-era video probing study flips cumulative edit rank from 2 to 4 (ambient), and an 8-d continuous-target control goes from 2 to 8 under all 50 tested mixings while an exact-covariance metric stays pinned at 2. On the finite Adam/MSE/QR bridge at n=4,000, identity mixing stops at K=1 in 20/20 runs while every shear a ∈ {.5,.75,1,1.25,2} accepts ≥2 updates in 20/20 runs. On frozen V-JEPA2 layer-21 100DOH features with per-map, per-rank *freshly retuned* attackers, rank-10 AUROC is .665 for identity vs .776 for five κ=10 dense maps (+.111, range [.075,.155], 25/25 paired differences positive), even though rank-0 AUROC is identical at .822. Worth reading if you have ever cited an erasure count as evidence that a concept is "distributed over N directions"; skip if you want a new eraser — the paper proposes none.

## 核心贡献

- **不可识别性定理（Theorem 1）**：在 $S,N \sim \mathcal{N}(0,1)$、$L=\mathrm{sign}(S)$、$X_a=(S+aN,\,N)$ 的总体构造上，累计欧氏 Gram–Schmidt + 岭探针的停止计数在 $a=0$ 时为 1、在任意 $a\neq 0$ 时为 2，而生成维度、充分线性维度、最小线性守护秩全部恒为 1。该结论对 Moore–Penrose OLS（λ=0）以及任意有限非负岭权重、各向同性罚项与输运罚项都成立。
- **全 QR 多输出版本（Theorem 2）**：匹配"拟合两输出回归器 + 移除其权重矩阵完整 QR 基"这一被批评论文的实际算法，累计编辑秩从 $r$ 变为 $2r$（$r=2$ 即从 2 变为 4，等于环境维度），而充分维度与守护秩仍为 $r$。
- **正面结果：输运度量下的完整轨迹仿射等变（Proposition 2）**：只要正定度量按 $G_Z=A^\top G_X A$ 协变输运、探针等变（$B_k^Z=A^{-1}B_k$）、正则被输运、非子空间 tie 被等变解决，则每一步都有 $ZT_k^Z=(XT_k)A$，分数、累计编辑秩、所有基于分数的停止计数完全一致。精确协方差只是该定理的一个推论，而非"语义正确的度量"。
- **五种"维度"的显式分离**：生成维度 / 充分线性维度 / 交叉协方差秩 / 迭代擦除计数 / 线性守护秩被列表区分，并明确指出计数与累计编辑秩只有在"每步恰增一个独立方向"时才重合。
- **有限样本与真实特征压力测试**：一个复现原视频论文 Adam/MSE/100 epoch/lr 1e-3/wd 1e-4/held-out $R^2\ge.1$ 且圆周 MAE $\le 80°$ 停止规则的有限桥接实验，加上在冻结 V-JEPA2 / DINOv2 上、100DOH 与 TouchMoment 接触预测任务上的预声明各向异性映射审计。

## Key Contributions

- **A discrete non-identification result, not just "geometry is metric-dependent."** Prior gauge-freedom work (Cain [7]) already shows Euclidean similarity changes under function-preserving invertible maps. This paper's sharper claim is an *exact integer* change in a stopping count while the sufficient linear dimension and the minimum guarding rank are provably held fixed.
- **A matching multivariate (full-QR) counterexample** aligned with the algebra of the video-world-model probing study it critiques, where cumulative edit rank saturates the ambient dimension.
- **An affine-equivariance theorem for the complete cumulative trajectory** under any covariantly transported positive-definite metric, with explicit scope limits: rank-deficient Moore–Penrose OLS is *not* covered, and OAS shrinkage / eigenvalue flooring break equivariance because their covariance maps fail $f(A^\top \Sigma A)=A^\top f(\Sigma) A$.
- **A taxonomy (Table 2) and a claim-boundary table (Table 1)** that explicitly says what remains valid in INLP, MP/SAL/LEACE, and the video direction study, and what must be reinterpreted — an unusually disciplined piece of critique framing.
- **A cross-fitted calibration separating finite-sample residual access from population rank**, showing a known-rank-one population guard can still leave a fresh attacker at .881 AUROC when the eraser is estimated from only 100 samples.

## Methodology

The core object is Algorithm 1: a *declared cumulative metric-QR erasure*. Given centered features $X$ and a positive-definite coefficient metric $G$ fixed once from the intact representation, at each step $k$ a probe block $B_k=\mathrm{Probe}(XT_{k-1},Y)$ is fit, residualized against the accumulated basis $U_{k-1}$, metric-QR'd into a $G$-orthonormal block $Q_k$, and the edit becomes $T_k=I-U_kU_k^\top G$. Crucially the paper insists that *every* choice — metric, probe loss, regularizer, residualization rule, numerical tolerance, stopping rule — is part of the estimand, and the metric is never recomputed after editing.

The negative results come from a population construction $H=(S,N)\sim\mathcal{N}(0,I_d)$, $Y=\mathbb{1}[S\ge 0]$, $X=HA$. Because $A$ is invertible, $S=XA^{-1}e$ is still a deterministic linear function of $X$, so a rank-zero edit cannot guard $Y$ and $T_A=A^{-1}(I-ee^\top)A$ gives a matching rank-one upper bound — the minimum guarding rank is exactly one for every $A$. Theorem 1 then shows the ridge coefficient is proportional to $(1+\lambda,-a)^\top$ (isotropic penalty) or $(1,-a)^\top$ (transported penalty), neither of which is parallel to the cross-covariance direction $(1,0)^\top$ unless $a=0$; the first cumulative Euclidean removal therefore leaves nonzero residual cross-covariance in a retained line, forcing a second accepted update. The paper is careful to quantify the magnitude rather than hide it: with $D_\lambda^2=(1+\lambda)^2+a^2$, the residual coefficient norm is $\|q_2\|_2 = c|a|D_\lambda / [a^2+(a^2+1+\lambda)^2+\lambda D_\lambda^2]$, which is positive for all $a\neq 0$ but goes to zero continuously as $a\to 0$ — so a finite score threshold can legitimately return 1 where exact population stopping returns 2.

Theorem 2 handles the multivariate case: with $Y=S$ and $X_a=(S+aN,N)\in\mathbb{R}^{2r}$, the first coefficient block is $[I_r,-aI_r]^\top$; for $a\neq 0$ the retained coordinates $X_aC \propto aS+(1+a^2)N$ still carry rank-$r$ cross-covariance with $Y$, and the second QR removal exhausts the representation.

The finite bridge reimplements the published protocol literally: $\theta\sim\mathrm{Unif}[-\pi,\pi]$, $S=(\sin\theta,\cos\theta)$, $N\sim\mathcal{N}(0,.5I_2)$, fresh linear probes trained with Adam (100 epochs, lr $10^{-3}$, wd $10^{-4}$), the literal non-idempotent recurrence $X^{(k+1)}=X^{(k)}(I-Q_kQ_k^\top)$, an 80/20 fixed split, and the published acceptance thresholds. Minibatch size is not reported in the source, so 128 is declared and 64/256 are audited.

The visual study freezes `facebook/vjepa2-vitl-fpc64-256` (mean-pooled tokens, validation-selected layers 21 for 100DOH and 18 for TouchMoment; 1,024-d) and DINOv2-Base (layers 12/11; 768-d). 100DOH uses a balanced 1,600/200/200 audit subset (positives = hand contact state 3/4, negatives = all hands state 0, ambiguous 1/2 excluded) plus a maximal balanced 17,206/2,126/1,958 audit over 13,081 source videos; TouchMoment (built from HOI4D + egocentric TACO) has 11,564/618/2,588 with 1,294 test pairs from 647 videos, and all splits/bootstraps group by source video (1,000 group-bootstrap replicates). The pre-declared map families are dense SPD maps $A_\kappa=QD_\kappa Q^\top$ with a randomized signed-Hadamard $Q$ and log-spaced spectrum from $\kappa^{-1/2}$ to $\kappa^{1/2}$, a secondary permuted-diagonal family, and 20 Haar orthogonal maps as a solver sanity check. The strongest audit is a three-role, source-video-disjoint partition of official training only: role A (793–801 examples) learns the erasure trajectory and standardization, role B (484–490) trains a *fresh* attacker with $C$ reselected independently per map and per rank, role C (310–318) is evaluated once.

## Results & Highlights

- **Population ridge count (Theorem 1, verified numerically for λ ∈ {0,.1,1,10}):** identity mixing $a=0$ stops after **1** edit; every nonzero shear stops after **2** (delta +1), with generating dimension, sufficient linear dimension, and minimum guarding rank all fixed at 1. The complementary 8-d continuous-target control ($H\sim\mathcal{N}(0,I_8)$, $Y=(H_1,.7H_2)$, sufficient dimension and guard rank = 2) gives Euclidean count **2 at κ=1 vs 8 for all 50 pre-declared Gaussian-QR orientations at each κ ∈ {3,10,100,1000}** (delta +6, i.e. the count saturates the ambient dimension), while the exact-covariance metric returns **2 in every trial** (delta 0) — the positive control for Proposition 2.
- **Finite Adam/MSE/QR bridge (n=4,000, 20 seeds/cell):** identity gives $K=1$ in **20/20** runs, as does the weak shear $a=.25$; every tested shear $a \in \{.5,.75,1,1.25,2\}$ accepts **≥2 updates in 20/20** runs (0/20 vs 20/20). At $a=1$, post-first-edit held-out $R^2=.195$ and circular MAE $=59.5°$ (i.e. comfortably inside the published accept thresholds $R^2\ge.1$, MAE $\le 80°$), capped mean $K=5.75$ (range 3–8, 2/20 right-censored at the 8-update cap). Under the literal recurrence $\mathrm{rank}(I-T_K)$ reaches 4 while $\mathrm{rank}(T_K)$ stays ≈2, so **2K is not an independent removed-subspace dimension**. The separation survives minibatch 64 and 256 (mean shear counts 5.4 and 4.9) but degrades at n=1,000, where only **15/20** $a=1$ runs accept a second update — the authors report this weakening rather than burying it.
- **Frozen V-JEPA2 100DOH, dense maps + coordinatewise restandardization (rank 10):** mean AUROC **.599 at κ=1 vs .791 at κ=10 (+.192, paired source-video 95% bootstrap [.110,.275])**, with the monotone ladder .666/.673/.745/.791 at κ=2/3/5/10 and .853/.840 at κ=100/1,000; post-standardization condition numbers are 2.09/3.19/5.41/10.89/110.64/1,070.68. The interval is already positive at κ=2 ([.008,.134]). Without restandardization the same maps give .802/.841/.853 at κ=10/100/1,000, so the effect is **not** explained by coordinate scaling alone.
- **Fresh-attacker audit (the strongest version, no validation/test queries):** rank-0 AUROC is **identical at .822** for identity and mapped features (as it must be — information is preserved), but at rank 10 identity averages **.665** across five source-disjoint partitions vs **.776** for the five κ=10 dense maps across 25 map–partition runs — **mean +.111, empirical range [.075,.155], positive in 25/25 pairs**, with the gap already +.033 at rank 1 and +.060 at rank 2.
- **Equivariance sanity checks match theory to numerical precision:** unshrunk empirical-covariance intervention reproduces the *identical complete trajectory* for all maps (rank-1 .587, rank-10 .518, delta .000, max prediction discrepancy $2.11\times10^{-6}$), and 20 Haar orthogonal maps reproduce the Euclidean trajectory with max discrepancy $4.27\times10^{-6}$; rank-0 predictions agree to $<7\times10^{-14}$ and 526 archived ranks show max Gram-matrix error $2.4\times10^{-15}$.
- **Metric dependence with per-rank retuned attackers:** on 100DOH, Euclidean vs OAS AUROC is **.827 vs .586 after one edit** and **.598 vs .494 after ten**; on TouchMoment **.924 vs .811** after one and **.778 vs .447** after ten. The sub-.5 values are explicitly flagged as *orientation-fixed concordance* (score orientation fixed on the selection half, never flipped) — an honesty control most papers omit.
- **Finite-sample vs population separation (cross-fit):** synthetic MP/SAL with a *known* rank-one population guard leaves a fresh attacker at **.881 AUROC at $n_A=100$, falling to .572 at $n_A=8{,}000$ (−.309)**; 100DOH-max LEACE-OAS goes **.876 → .647 (−.229)** and TouchMoment LEACE-OAS **.927 → .626 (−.301)**. So "residual access after a rank-1 edit" is often estimation error, not evidence of multiple population routes.

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty.** The mathematical core is genuinely simple — the shear counterexample is a $2\times2$ matrix and Proposition 1's proof fits in four lines. The paper knows this ("one-step Euclidean non-equivariance is elementary") and locates its novelty in the *discrete integer* separation with simultaneously pinned sufficient dimension and guarding rank, plus the full-QR extension and the trajectory equivariance theorem. That is a fair positioning, but readers who already internalized Roeder et al.'s identifiability-up-to-invertible-linear-maps result will find the conclusion unsurprising. The contribution is more sociological than technical: it forecloses a specific bad inference that a specific recent video-world-model paper [13] made.

**Rigor and honesty.** Unusually good in places. The authors quantify exactly how small the effect can be (Eq. 11, $\|q_2\|_2 \to 0$ as $a\to 0$), report the n=1,000 case where only 15/20 runs replicate, explicitly note that the MP/SAL exact .500 test AUROC is "only an algebraic sanity check" because estimating the class-mean difference and training the attacker on the same sample forces a zero training gradient, and disclose that the research log contains **1,865 historical official-test configurations** so no untouched holdout remains — a disclosure most authors would suppress. They also label sub-.5 AUROCs as orientation reversals rather than flipping them to look above chance.

**Where it is weak.** (1) The visual case study never touches the actual motivating system: the "finite circular bridge" uses a synthetic angular distribution, not the video activations of [13], and the source's minibatch size is unknown (declared as 128). So the claim "the published protocol is coordinate-sensitive" is established on a *reimplementation of the algebra*, not on the paper being criticized — a meaningful gap between the critique and its target. (2) The κ ladder makes the visual effect look uncomfortably like an ill-conditioning artifact: AUROC tracks the condition number (2.09 → 1,070) almost monotonically, and κ=100/1,000 giving .853/.840 is non-monotone. Any procedure fed a matrix with condition number 10³ behaving differently is not a deep finding; the interesting regime is κ=2 (condition 2.09), where the interval [.008,.134] barely excludes zero. (3) The nuisance-matched hard subset for 100DOH has only **58 pairs** — too small to be load-bearing, and correctly relegated to the artifact. (4) The descriptive stopping rule (validation AUROC interval inside [.45,.55] for three consecutive ranks) is admitted to have "essentially no power" at n=200, and the same validation split selected layer, C, and rank with no sequential error control. (5) DINOv2 is an image encoder used as a comparison for a video model — a mismatched control the authors flag but still report. (6) The map families are five fixed dense SPD maps plus a secondary diagonal family; this is an existential stress test, not a distribution over invertible maps, so nothing here bounds how large the effect is "typically."

**Missing ablations.** There is no test of whether the exact-covariance metric fixes the count on *real* V-JEPA2 features in the strong fresh-attacker setting — the unshrunk-covariance check is run only in the fixed-attacker Euclidean-trajectory comparison. Given that Proposition 2's practical payoff is "use a transported metric and your count becomes well-defined," the absence of an end-to-end covariance-metric erasure count on 100DOH/TouchMoment under the strongest audit is the most conspicuous gap.

**Red flags.** None of the fatal kind: no cherry-picked headline benchmark, no overclaimed SOTA, and the paper repeatedly refuses to call its visual numbers estimates of a contact dimension. The mild overclaim is rhetorical — the abstract's framing ("we show that both quantities can change") is stronger than "there exists a reparameterization under which they change," which is what is actually proven. Also, the paper critiques [13] by name in Table 1 while never running [13]'s pipeline, which some readers will find asymmetric.

## Strengths

- The central theorem is airtight, short, and verifiable by hand; the population construction pins all three model-defined quantities so the counterexample cannot be dismissed as a change in the underlying problem.
- The positive result (transported-metric trajectory equivariance) makes the paper constructive rather than purely destructive: it tells practitioners exactly what to declare to get a well-defined count.
- Exceptional scope discipline: Table 1 (what remains valid vs what must be reinterpreted), Table 3 (four distinct declared procedures, "no row silently inherits guarantees from another"), and the explicit statement that Moore–Penrose rank-deficient OLS is outside Proposition 2.
- Numerical controls are convincing: 20 Haar orthogonal maps and unshrunk empirical covariance both reproduce trajectories to $\sim10^{-6}$, which rules out solver noise as the driver of the anisotropic effect.
- Honest disclosure of extensive prior test-set exposure and of the negative n=1,000 result.

## Limitations

- Restricted to linear representations and linear interventions; says nothing about nonlinear, tokenwise, spatial, temporal, or causal concept organization.
- The visual evidence is exploratory by the authors' own labeling — official validation reuse plus 1,865 historical test configurations mean no confirmatory claim is possible; a prospective protocol is frozen for a future dataset instead.
- Contact is an imperfect semantic target: in 100DOH released object-box presence is nearly label-deterministic, and TouchMoment contrasts touch onset with a frame 0.5 s earlier, so hand closure, approach, endpoint appearance, and action phase all remain informative confounds.
- Map families are five declared dense SPD maps + a permuted-diagonal family; the diagonal family is *algebraically canceled* by coordinatewise restandardization, so only the dense family carries the real-feature claim.
- The 25 paired map–partition differences are not independent population draws, and their range is explicitly not a confidence interval.
- Equivariance requires an equivariant probe, transported positive-definite regularization after rank loss, common support, and equivariant tie-breaking; OAS and Ledoit–Wolf shrinkage and eigenvalue flooring break it.

## Relevance

如果你在做可解释性/表征分析，尤其是用 INLP、amnesic probing、LEACE 或"擦除多少个方向"来论证某概念是否"分布式编码"，这篇文章直接否定了该计数的内在含义，并给出了让计数良定义的最小声明清单（度量、探针损失、正则、协方差估计、tie-breaking、停止规则）。对于评估视频/图像基座模型（V-JEPA2、DINOv2）表征几何的工作，它同时提供了一套可复用的"仿射压力测试 + 新攻击者交叉拟合"审计模板。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the transported-covariance metric actually stabilize the erasure count on real V-JEPA2 features under the strong three-role fresh-attacker audit, or only in the fixed-attacker Euclidean comparison reported here?
- The visual effect scales with the post-standardization condition number (2.09 → 1,070.68). Is there any anisotropy regime with condition number near 1 where the trajectory still changes materially, or is the phenomenon entirely a conditioning artifact in practice?
- What is the analogous statement for nonlinear erasers (kernelized INLP, adversarial removal, sparse-autoencoder feature ablation)? Do those inherit a different, possibly larger, procedure-relative ambiguity?
- If a count must be procedure-relative, which declared procedure is most *useful*? Is there a decision-theoretic criterion (e.g. downstream steering efficacy) that selects a metric, rather than the paper's agnostic "declare and report"?
- Would rerunning the actual pipeline of the criticized video-world-model study [13] under pre-declared shears reproduce the count change on its own activations, closing the gap between the synthetic circular bridge and the target of the critique?
- How should the community retroactively reinterpret published "dozens to hundreds of directions" claims — is there a conservative lower bound (e.g. cross-covariance rank or minimum guarding rank) that *is* affine-invariant and could be reported instead?
