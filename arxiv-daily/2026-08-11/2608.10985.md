> **一句话总结：** PEAK 用 BatchTopK k-稀疏自编码器在 SD v1.4 去噪 U-Net 的 up.1.1/up.1.2 交叉注意力输出上分解激活，通过"激活强度 × 跨时间步出现频率"对比目标/非目标提示词，仅挑出约 7 个概念特征，再以"压制目标特征 + 蒸馏保留互补特征"的双项损失微调 ESD-x 参数（100 步、λ=0.02），把 I2P 上 NudeNet 检出从 582 降到 6，三类对抗攻击平均 ASR 从 96.52% 降到 5.63%，同时 MS-COCO 上 FID 41.59 / KID 0.0000 / CLIP 26.46 全部优于 RECE、STEREO 等九个基线。

> **TL;DR:** PEAK trains a BatchTopK k-sparse autoencoder on internal diffusion activations, contrasts matched target/non-target prompts by activation strength *and* cross-timestep frequency to localize ~7 concept-specific latents, then bakes erasure into the weights with a suppression loss plus a complementary-feature distillation loss. On I2P it cuts NudeNet detections from 582 (SD v1.4) to 6 (matching STEREO's 6, beating RECE's 54), drops average ASR across RAB/MMA/UnlearnDiffAtk from 96.52% to 5.63%, and — unlike every baseline — does so without paying a preservation tax: MS-COCO FID 41.59 vs RECE's 42.19 and STEREO's 54.24, KID 0.0000, CLIP Score 26.46 vs 25.16 for STEREO.

## 核心贡献

- **把 kSAE 从推理期干预升级为参数内化的擦除。** 已有 SAE 路线（SAeUron、Concept Steerers、SAEmnesia、OrthoEraser）要么在推理时钩住激活、要么只改文本编码器表示，开源权重下用户可以直接绕过；PEAK 把特征级约束固化进扩散模型参数，推理时既不需要 kSAE 也不需要参考模型。
- **双准则特征定位。** 提出重要性分数 S_f = (跨时间步平均激活强度) × (进入 top-k_step 的时间步频率)，再用百分位阈值做目标集/非目标集对比筛选：S_f^c > τ_c 且 S_f^¬c ≤ τ_¬c，从 1280 维稀疏空间里挑出极少量（消融中为 7 个）目标特征。
- **擦除 + 保留的对称目标函数。** L_erase 把选中特征的激活能量压向 0，L_pres 在同一稀疏坐标系里把互补特征对齐到冻结原模型的响应，L_PEAK = L_erase + λ·L_pres（λ=0.02）。这一"在同一特征空间里同时定义擦什么、留什么"是与 SAeUron/SNCE 的关键区别。
- **On-trajectory 监督。** 不用独立采样的噪声潜变量，而是让冻结参考模型先跑 r∈{1..8} 步真实去噪轨迹，得到模型实际会访问的潜状态，再在该状态上提特征做监督。
- **可解释性证据链。** 选中特征在去噪全过程 ROC-AUC 平均 >0.8；空间激活热图落在对应身体部位；单独置零特征组 1138/861、864、608/478/756 分别使 armpit/belly/breast 检出下降 94/95/276，验证属性级因果控制。

## Key Contributions

- Reframes kSAE-based concept erasure from an inference-time intervention into a weight-level edit, closing the "just remove the hook" bypass that affects SAeUron, ItD, SNCE, Concept Steerers, SAEmnesia and OrthoEraser in open-weight settings.
- Introduces a two-factor feature importance score (mean activation magnitude × frequency of being top-ranked across denoising timesteps), combined with a matched-prompt contrast and percentile thresholds, to isolate a compact target-feature set.
- Adds an explicit complementary-feature preservation loss — feature-space distillation against the frozen model on all non-selected latents — which the authors show is what prevents collateral damage.
- Uses on-trajectory rollout latents (r sampled from 1–8 reference denoising steps) rather than off-trajectory noise for supervision.
- Provides a causal-intervention audit (Table 4) showing that zeroing individual localized features selectively suppresses specific NudeNet categories, plus a feature-selection ablation showing random latents *increase* detections (619 vs 582 baseline).

## Methodology

PEAK operates in two stages on a frozen pretrained diffusion model (SD v1.4 by default).

**Stage 0 — kSAE training.** Activations are collected from cross-attention outputs in the U-Net upsampling path (up.1.1 for object/nudity, up.1.2 for style, chosen by block-wise identity-ablation). Each spatial location of the h×w×d activation map is one training sample, aggregated across all denoising timesteps. The autoencoder is a single-layer ReLU encoder with BatchTopK sparsity (globally largest Nk entries per minibatch) trained with a normalized-FVU reconstruction loss plus the AuxK dead-latent objective from Gao et al. Notably the expansion factor is **1**: n = 1280 latents for d = 1280 input dims, k = 32, α = 1/32, batch 4096, 210k steps, lr 4e-4, decoder rows constrained to unit norm. Training prompts mix MS-COCO captions, a held-out I2P split, and template-generated object/style prompts.

**Stage 1 — feature localization.** Two matched prompt sets D_c and D_¬c are built (100 hand-curated nudity pairs, generated with GPT-5.5, differing only in the nudity token, e.g. "A photo of a naked woman, hyperrealistic 8k" vs "A photo of a woman, hyperrealistic 8k"). Spatially averaged latent activations are aggregated per timestep; the importance score multiplies mean strength by the fraction of timesteps in which the latent lands in the per-step top-k_step set. Target features are those above the p-th percentile for D_c and below it for D_¬c.

**Stage 2 — feature-guided fine-tuning.** With Θ₀ frozen and Θ a trainable copy, both models are evaluated at the same on-trajectory latent x̃_{t_r} and prompt, activations at layer ℓ are encoded by the frozen kSAE, and the objective L_erase (mean squared activation of selected latents in Θ) + λ·L_pres (MSE between Θ and Θ₀ latents on the complement set) is minimized. Only cross-attention linear projections are updated (ESD-x), AdamW lr 5e-5, batch 8, 100 steps, BF16, 512×512, seed 0. After training the kSAE and reference model are discarded.

## Results & Highlights

- **I2P nudity (NudeNet, conf 0.6, 4,703 prompts, 1 image each).** Total detections: SD v1.4 **582** → PEAK **6**, tying the best prior method STEREO (6) and edging ReCARE (7), TraSCE (15), AdvUn (21), RECE (54), ESD (70), MACE (97), UCE (138), RACE (342). The differentiator is preservation, not erasure count.
- **MS-COCO preservation (1,000 prompts).** PEAK reports **CLIP Score 26.46 / FID 41.59 / KID 0.0000 (×100)**, best on all three among erasure methods: RECE 26.14 / 42.19 / 0.0000, STEREO 25.16 / 54.24 / 0.2919, TraSCE 25.05 / 69.53 / 0.5443, MACE 24.04 / 54.09 / 0.1927. Original SD v1.4 CLIP is 26.63, so PEAK gives up only 0.17 CLIP points while STEREO gives up 1.47.
- **Adversarial persistence.** RAB (K16/K38/K77) average ASR: SD1.4 **94.74%** → PEAK **0.00%** vs STEREO 1.40%, MACE 1.75%, ReCARE 2.46%, RECE 17.89%, ESD 40.70%, RACE 94.39%. MMA: 96.50% → **0.80%** (AdvUn is actually lower at 0.30%). UnlearnDiffAtk Pre-ASR 88.14% → **0.85%** (best); Post-ASR 98.31% → **16.10%**, which is *second* best behind STEREO's 15.25%. The headline "96.52% → 5.63%" is the mean of RAB-avg, MMA and UnlearnDiffAtk Post-ASR.
- **Object / style erasure (CLIP zero-shot classifier).** Snoopy: Acc_e 94.75 → **0.00** with Acc_u **94.84** (original 94.16; UCE 0.38/95.13, MACE 0.38/53.06, TraSCE 0.38/68.09, CA 5.88/87.59). Van Gogh: Acc_e 95.00 → **13.67** with Acc_u **81.58** — TraSCE erases far harder (Acc_e 1.00) but collapses preservation to 57.17.
- **Causal feature ablation (Table 4).** Zeroing feature group {1138, 861} drops Armpits detections 115 → 21 (−94); {864} drops Belly 132 → 37 (−95); {608, 478, 756} drops Breasts 283 → 7 (−276). Zeroing all six jointly gives 4 / 1 / 2.
- **Ablations.** λ = 0 achieves 0 I2P detections but destroys the model (CLIP 16.64, FID 184.85); λ = 0.02 is the chosen trade-off (6 / 26.46 / 41.59). Feature selection: random latents give **619** I2P detections — *worse than the unmodified model's 582* — while strength-only gives 8 (MMA 16) and frequency-only gives 9 (FID 47.98); the product gives 6 with RAB 0 and MMA 8. Parameter strategy: esd-x (6 detections, FID 41.59) is preferred over esd-u (18, FID 34.97) and esd-all (14, FID 35.94), i.e. the chosen setting is actually *worse* on FID than two alternatives.

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty (moderate).** The individual ingredients are known: kSAE feature localization for erasure is SAeUron/SNCE, ESD-x fine-tuning is Gandikota et al. 2023, feature-space distillation to a frozen teacher is standard. The genuinely new pieces are (a) the strength × frequency product score and (b) using the *complement* of the localized set as an explicit preservation target in the same sparse basis. Both are small, sensible, and empirically load-bearing — the random-feature ablation (619 > 582) shows the localization is doing real work, and λ = 0 shows the preservation term is not decorative.

**Rigor — the biggest red flag is the baseline set.** The entire motivation is that SAE-based methods (SAeUron, ItD, SNCE, Concept Steerers, SAEmnesia, OrthoEraser) are non-persistent, yet **not one of them appears in Table 1, 2, or 3**. Every quantitative comparison is against non-SAE methods. The claim "existing kSAE approaches fail at persistence" is asserted from architecture, never measured. Second, the robustness headline oversells: AdvUn's per-attack numbers (RAB 0.70, MMA 0.30, UnlearnDiffAtk Post 16.95) average to 5.98% vs PEAK's 5.63% — statistically indistinguishable on 285/1000-trial attack sets. PEAK's real, defensible win is that AdvUn pays CLIP 23.96 / FID 49.11 for that robustness while PEAK pays almost nothing. The paper would be stronger stating this plainly. Third, absolute FID ~41 is poor and SD v1.4's own FID is listed as "-", so the reader cannot tell how much of the 41.59 is erasure damage versus the evaluation protocol's floor (1,000 COCO prompts, one image each). Fourth, single seed (0), one image per I2P prompt, no error bars anywhere.

**Data quirks.** The kSAE uses **expansion factor 1** (1280 latents for 1280 dims) — essentially no overcompleteness, which is unusual for dictionary learning where 8×–64× is standard, and the paper never justifies it or ablates it. The 100 contrastive prompt pairs were authored by GPT-5.5, so the "target-specific" features are defined relative to an LLM's notion of a minimal nudity edit. Van Gogh Acc_u after erasure (81.58) is *higher* than the original model's (79.83), which is not commented on and suggests the CLIP zero-shot preservation metric is noisy at this scale (30 templates × 10 images). p (the percentile threshold) and k_step are never given numeric values in the main text.

**Missing ablations.** No sweep over the number of selected features (the count 7 appears only incidentally in Table 7's caption), no expansion-factor study, no ablation of the on-trajectory rollout versus off-trajectory noise despite it being pitched as a contribution, and no quantitative results at all for SDXL, FLUX, violence erasure, or multi-concept beyond a bar chart (Figure 12b) with no baselines.

**Reproducibility (good).** Full hyperparameters, prompt pairs, block choices, and a code URL. Compute is modest — 100 fine-tuning steps at batch 8 — though the 210k-step kSAE pretraining is the real cost and no wall-clock or GPU count is given anywhere.

**Significance (solid but incremental).** Best-in-class erasure/preservation trade-off on the standard nudity benchmark, and a clean demonstration that SAE features can be internalized into weights. It does not resolve the field's harder problems: Van Gogh at 13.67% leakage and UnlearnDiffAtk Post-ASR at 16.10% both show the concept is not truly gone.

## Strengths

- The preservation result is the genuinely impressive number: matching the best erasure count (6) while being the only method that keeps CLIP within 0.17 of the original and achieves KID 0.0000 is a real Pareto improvement over the RECE/STEREO/ReCARE frontier.
- Interpretability claims are backed by three independent kinds of evidence — directional ROC-AUC > 0.8 across timesteps, spatial heatmap alignment with body regions, and causal zeroing that moves the *correct* NudeNet category by −94/−95/−276.
- The random-feature control (619 detections, worse than baseline) is exactly the negative control this literature usually omits.
- Intellectually honest ablation reporting: Table 6 shows esd-u/esd-all achieve better FID than the chosen esd-x, and the authors keep esd-x anyway for erasure strength rather than hiding the row.
- No inference-time dependency — after fine-tuning, neither the kSAE nor the reference model is needed, which is the practical property that matters for open-weight releases.
- Extremely detailed reproduction appendix, including the actual prompt pairs.

## Limitations

- The paper has no explicit Limitations section; the following are inferred.
- Zero comparison against the SAE-based methods the paper positions itself against — the central claim about their non-persistence is untested.
- Robustness advantage over AdvUn is within noise (5.63% vs 5.98% average ASR); the gain is in preservation, not in robustness.
- UnlearnDiffAtk Post-ASR of 16.10% means roughly one in six white-box adversarial prompts still recovers nudity; "persistent" is relative, not absolute.
- Artistic-style erasure is weak (Van Gogh Acc_e 13.67 vs TraSCE 1.00), suggesting style concepts are less compactly localized in the kSAE basis than nudity or object identity.
- Cross-architecture (SDXL, FLUX), violence, multi-concept, and unseen-concept (Mario) claims are supported only by qualitative figures or unbaselined bar charts.
- Requires an expensive per-model, per-block kSAE pretraining stage (210k steps) plus hand-curated contrastive prompt pairs for every new concept.
- Single seed, single image per prompt, no variance estimates; the 582→6 headline rests on one generation pass.
- Expansion factor 1 kSAE is an odd design choice with no supporting ablation.

## Relevance

如果关注扩散模型安全对齐与机制可解释性的交叉点，这篇是当前把 SAE 特征"固化进权重"做得最完整的一篇，其"擦除损失 + 互补特征蒸馏"范式可以直接迁移到 LLM 的 SAE-based unlearning／拒答特征编辑上。同时它也是一个反面教材式的提醒：论文的可解释性证据（ROC-AUC、因果置零）质量远高于其基线选择质量，评估同类工作时值得优先核对"是否与最接近的 SAE 方法做过比较"。

## Questions for Further Exploration

1. How does PEAK actually compare against SAeUron, SNCE, and OrthoEraser under the same I2P + RAB/MMA/UnlearnDiffAtk protocol — is the persistence gap as large as the architectural argument implies?
2. Why expansion factor 1? Would an 8× or 16× overcomplete dictionary yield more monosemantic latents and reduce the Van Gogh leakage, or does overcompleteness fragment the concept across too many latents to suppress cleanly?
3. Does the on-trajectory rollout actually matter? An ablation replacing x̃_{t_r} with independently sampled noise would isolate this contribution.
4. How does erasure quality scale with the number of selected features, and is there a principled way to set p and k_step rather than tuning them per concept?
5. Does the complementary-preservation loss survive sequential erasure of many concepts, or does the frozen-reference distillation target drift and compound damage after 10–50 concepts?
6. Since only ~7 latents are suppressed, can an adversary with white-box access recover the concept by fine-tuning those same cross-attention projections back — i.e. how many gradient steps of "re-learning" undo PEAK versus ESD or STEREO?
7. Is the 16.10% UnlearnDiffAtk Post-ASR concentrated on specific residual latents that the percentile threshold excluded, and would a softer (weighted) suppression over all latents beat the hard top-set selection?
