Key finding · Feature Nonlocality (FNL)

FNLA single backward pass ranks every SAE feature by how much context it reads — and unmasks 21 of 25 “semantic” jailbreak defenses as positional artifacts.

01

Methodology

The core object is the per-position influence of input embeddings on a feature's activation at the query position. For a prompt of T tokens, the influence Ja(t) = ‖∂za/∂xt‖² is computed only at firing events, normalized over the prefix into a distribution, and its Shannon entropy is the per-prompt nonlocality. Dataset-level FNL averages the top-k=32 max-activation events at T=128 (uniform ceiling 7 bits).

FNL := H(pa),   pa(t) ∝ ‖∂za(T,P)/∂xt‖²₂  over prefix t ≤ T  (firing events only)

It needs only one forward + one backward pass, parallelizes across an entire layer's dictionary, needs no LLM judge and no contrastive dataset, and extends to any residual-stream subspace (giving the H(hT) reference curve). Validation runs along four axes: cross-corpus rank stability, decoder-geometry agreement, two borrowed abstraction proxies (token-injection & paraphrase robustness), and downstream audits.

Credibility: Oxford (Rudolf Peierls Centre) & Stanford (Leinweber). Senior condensed-matter theorists — the metrology (split-half reliability, disattenuated ρ̃, paired bootstrap CIs) is unusually careful; the holographic-duality framing is decorative. Both reference pipelines reproduced end-to-end. ~1,000 GPU-hr to regenerate.

03

Strengths & Limitations

Strengths

  • Audit with teeth. Converts “SAEs mitigate jailbreaks” into “a BOS-position artifact mitigates wrapper jailbreaks” — a materially different safety claim.
  • Rare metric hygiene. Split-half reliability ceilings, disattenuated ρ̃, paired bootstrap CIs at fixed seeds, full hyperparameter-provenance table.
  • Pre-empts the obvious objection. Explicit discriminant validity vs ReasonScore (ρ = −0.214) and corpus-contrast enrichment — not just token frequency.
  • Reproduced, not cited. Both Ma et al. and CC-Delta pipelines re-run end-to-end with reported agreement (and a documented mismatch).
  • Cheap & parallelizable. One backward pass, no LLM judge — genuinely useful as a screening filter over a whole dictionary.

Limitations

  • Correlational only. No causal identification of abstraction; |ρ| ≈ 0.27–0.46 is moderate at best.
  • Fragile across studies. Absolute FNL depends on the BOS/window convention and on T and k — cross-paper comparison is brittle.
  • Steering doesn't transfer. Beyond reasoning-distilled Llama (Gemma-2-9B, Qwen3-8B) the method offers nothing over unsteered.
  • Metric definition un-benchmarked. Softmax / Rényi / IPR alternatives never compared; no attention-entropy or window-length baseline.
  • Inherits SAE artifacts. Built on public SAEs (absorption / splitting); sensitivity to these is noted but untested.
  • Judge-error floor. Llama-3.3-70B grading on MATH-500 adds unquantified noise for deltas of 1–5 points.
04

Open Questions

Q1 · BASELINE

Would a simple attention-entropy or “min context window to recover 90% activation” match the AUC 0.73–0.84? Without it, the entropy-of-gradients construction is unbenchmarked.

Q2 · DEFINITIONAL?

The sink position is excluded from the influence support — how much of “21/25 zero FNL” is a convention artifact? What does the audit look like with the sink included?

Q3 · DISTILLATION?

High-FNL helps only on R1-distilled stacks. Is the +4.6 repairing a distillation-induced failure mode? A per-problem flip analysis would settle it.

Q4 · FALSIFICATION

Can FNL move from correlational witness to falsification test — predict which context spans a feature reads, then ablate exactly those spans?
