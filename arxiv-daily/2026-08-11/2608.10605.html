Why this matters

Couples MoE scaling-law fitting with parallel-layout search in one optimization, plus an open-source operator-level MFU estimator (ScalePlan) for your own cluster.

Critical edge

Nine of eleven coefficients are unidentified and the headline config is a 150× extrapolation — trust the principle, not the number.

01

Methodology — one MINLP, not two stages

Architecture ⇄ systems co-design

MOSAIC replaces the conventional scaling-law → systems-tuning pipeline (à la Kimi-K2) with a single mixed-integer nonlinear program whose compute budget is itself architecture-dependent: Cdeliverable = Cpeak · MFU(Z, Pℓ, B) · ηgood. The objective minimizes predicted loss L(Ntot, S, D, G); the constraint says the model-FLOP cost must fit inside what the cluster can actually deliver.

01 · SCALING LAW

G-augmented joint MoE law

~150 from-scratch runs (Ntot 0.22–79.24B, S 0.50–0.98, G 1–8), Huber + L-BFGS-B in log-space; largest 10% held out for upward extrapolation. G enters only the interaction term.

02 · PERFORMANCE MODEL

Operator-level, not roofline

Iteration time assembled from measured lookup tables: grouped-GEMM efficiency by per-expert tile, NCCL bus curves (off ≤8× from analytic peak), a CPU-dispatch floor, a corrected 1F1B bubble, one fitted χsys.

03 · SEARCH

Geometry scaling ladder

Depth/width co-scale on one seed q; (E, K, G) searched on top while S, Ntot, Nact are derived. D eliminated in closed form; guard S ≥ 0.7 to stay inside calibration.

On a 4-node × 5-day envelope the FLOPs-prescribed S* = 0.983 is infeasible (max feasible S ≈ 0.96); re-optimizing under the hardware envelope yields an interior sparsity 0.031 nats better. Across 10–40-day and 16–128-node sweeps, S* sits on a plateau of 0.915–0.963, G* = 4 at most budgets. Four staged real-runs (>200B tokens each) reproduce the predicted MFU ordering exactly — though overstating the spread — and reach 15–20× the largest fitting budget.

03

Strengths & Limitations

side by side

Strengths

  • Core reframing — an architecture-dependent deliverable-FLOPs budget — is simple, correct, and immediately usable for planning a real pretraining run.
  • Performance model unusually well-engineered for a scaling paper: measured lookup tables, one calibration term, and correctly flagged roofline failure modes (grouped-GEMM tile, CPU-dispatch floor, vocab-stage imbalance, NCCL ≤8× skew).
  • Released open-source as ScalePlan — rare for internal systems tooling of this class.
  • Candid limitations; distinguishes MFU from HFU (no recomputation credit); corrects a 1F1B bubble double-counting bug.

Limitations

  • Every optimum is conditional on one geometry ladder, one platform (B200 / EFA), one stack (Megatron-Core), with PTP = PCP = 1.
  • G ≤ 8 throughout; the finest-grained (DeepSeek-scale) regime is unprobed — the law's G-monotonicity is unverified past G = 4, where observations flatten.
  • No overtrained regime (TPP ≤ 150, data only to 70); global batch fixed at 1024; overlap modeled only for DP collectives.
  • No downstream evaluation, no validation loss, no seed variance; ηgood ≡ 1 in every experiment.
04

Questions for further exploration

open threads
  1. Does the interior sparsity optimum survive a fused, overlapped dispatch/combine stack (DeepEP-style), or is it a Megatron-Core artifact? The authors flag this themselves.
  2. How does the recommendation shift once ηgood is realistic (0.85–0.95, with checkpointing and failures) and node-count-dependent?
  3. Does the (Ntot, S, G) representation transfer across backbone geometry families, or is η ≈ 0.95 specific to the dff = 2.5d, dhead = 128 ladder?
  4. If the nine ridge coefficients were bootstrapped through the search, how often does the argmin change? A posterior over top-k would beat one point 150× out.
  5. Do cluster-optimal geometries hold up on downstream benchmarks, or does perplexity hide task trade-offs (reasoning vs recall)?
  6. Can the objective jointly price inference cost — an S = 0.956, 250B model is far more expensive to serve than an equal-loss denser one?
