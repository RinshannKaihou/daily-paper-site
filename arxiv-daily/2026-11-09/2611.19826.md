> **一句话总结：** 在统一的 mean/rank-1 DiM "提取即干预" 协议（含匹配范数随机向量对照）下，作者对约 25 个开源权重衍生模型（微调、Guard、Thinking、VLM、音频包装、混合架构、生成头）系统审计拒绝方向的可达干预位点，发现因果攻击提升集中在文本解码器残差流 T（如 HADES 测试集 500 条上 Nemotron ASR 从 0.034 升至 0.616），而同样的 DiM 在感知编码器 E / 投影器 P / 融合层 F 全部持平（0.021→0.021，等于随机对照），且 margin-rank 探针显示该配方在 T 是 50 个匹配方向中最强（rank 50/50）、在 P/E 接近随机——即"扁平"的非文本位点更可能是弱提取器伪影而非特征缺失。

> **TL;DR:** A supply-chain safety audit asking where Arditi-style mean/rank-1 difference-in-means (DiM) refusal ablation still hooks across ~25 open-weight LM derivatives (fine-tunes, Llama-Guard-3, R1 thinking models, VLMs, Qwen2-Audio, Mamba2 hybrids, SD-Turbo/AudioLDM U-Nets). Under one shared extract=intervene protocol with matched-norm random controls, attack lift is text-decoder-local: on the HADES test set (n=500) text DiM at T lifts Nemotron ASR 0.034→0.616 vs. random 0.024, while the same recipe at projector/fusion stays at 0.021→0.021 (flat, equal to random); Qwen2-Audio spoken AdvBench lifts 0.03→0.84 at T but 0.02→0.03 at the projector; on VLGuard Unsafe (n=200) the T directionality persists (0.030→0.150 vs. +0.02/+0.035 at P/E). Margin-rank probes show the recipe is extremal at T (rank 50/50, leverage 6.0) and near chance at P/E, supporting a weak-extractor reading of the flat perceptual sites — a recipe-scoped localization claim, not proof that refusal features are absent outside T.

## 核心贡献

- **定位性问题 + 统一协议**：首次在同一个 mean/rank-1 DiM "提取即干预" 协议（提取位点=干预位点、rank-1 消融、匹配范数随机向量对照、空生成质量门控）下，系统检验拒绝方向的因果攻击提升是集中在文本解码器残差流 T，还是同样出现在感知编码器 E、投影器 P、融合路径 F——结论是 T 局部的。
- **机制诊断工具**：提出 margin-rank / leverage / 位移探针（在教师强制的安全边界上与 50 个匹配范数随机方向排名），把"非文本位点扁平"的三种解释（特征缺失 / 提取器太弱 / 下游 T 重新施加拒绝）中的"弱提取器"解读向前推进：同一配方在 T 是 50/50 最强方向（leverage 6.0），在 P/E 排名 28–32、leverage ≤0.26（随机约 25.5）。
- **面向干预的衍生模型分类账（Class×site ledger）**：给出 Root/Inheritance/Axis(A1–A7)/Class 分类法，覆盖微调、压缩、Guard、Thinking、投机解码、VLM、音频包装、MoE、混合 mixer、agent、VLA、BitNet 等约 25 个模型叶子，逐一标注 effective / ineffective / infeasible / Steer-N/A 及原因。
- **免映射器 base→derivative 迁移筛选**：当残差宽度匹配时无需线性映射即可迁移 v̂B；提出子空间重叠度 R 作为必要（非充分）筛选器——R 低于随机带的供体（如 Llama→Mixtral R=0.036）完全不迁移，高于随机带的供体（如 Mistral→Mixtral R=0.63，ASR→0.98）才可能迁移。
- **生成式 U-Net 独立频带**：概念 DiM 在 SD-Turbo / AudioLDM 的 U-Net down-block 可擦除有害生成（NudeNet 0.79→0），mid-block 无效；与聊天 ASR 使用完全独立的判分器，绝不混合。

## Key Contributions

- **A localization question under one shared protocol.** To our knowledge the first work to jointly (i) test, under a single mean/rank-1 DiM extract=intervene protocol with matched-norm random controls, whether causal chat-attack ASR lift concentrates at the text decoder T or also arises at perceptual encoders/projectors/fusion (E/P/F); (ii) catalogue open-weight derivative Classes (fine-tunes, guards, thinking/speculative heads, VLMs, audio wrappers, mixer hybrids) audited under that same protocol; and (iii) report mapper-free base→derivative transfer (base/self/cross donors) under the same gates when residual widths match.
- **The primary finding — recipe-scoped T vs. non-text asymmetry.** Text DiM at T raises chat ASR on plain text and on tested mixed-input cells (HADES adversarial image + harmful text; spoken AdvBench via TTS or silence), while mean non-text DiM Ru at E/P/F yields no attack lift; a benign-image + harmful-text cross-condition shows attacks track harmful text, not image content.
- **Mechanistic pressure on the flat cells.** On the Vision-Instruct leaf carrying the HADES win, prompt-position, projection-ratio (|ρ|≈0.64 at T vs. 0.07–0.11 at P/E), and 50-random margin-rank instruments show the same recipe that is extremal at T is near chance at P/E — evidence for a weak-extractor reading of the perceptual cells (not a claim that no other feature family, e.g. multi-direction or SAE, could succeed there).
- **An intervention-oriented Class taxonomy with audited coverage.** A Root/Inheritance/Axis/Class taxonomy mapped to residual hook sites, with per-Class outcome labels (effective / ineffective / infeasible / Steer-N/A) and caveats — e.g. Llama-lineage T broadly effective; Thinking Class-effective only with suite qualification; Compress/Dream/Attn-replace closed blue.
- **Donor screening for mapper-free cross.** Template-matched subspace overlap R against a matched-norm random band is a necessary screen: donors inside the band transfer nothing, every donor above the band transfers (though overlap is not sufficient — Tulu recovers only a fraction of self lift).

## Methodology

The audit fixes one recipe and varies only the site and the model. **Extraction (DiM):** the unit refusal direction v̂ is the difference of means between harmful and benign prompt pools — text DiM Rtxt contrasts harmful vs. benign text prompts (AdvBench [0:128] for extraction, disjoint [128:520] n=392 for eval; Alpaca benign contrast), pooled at the last text position of the decoder residual h(ℓ); non-text DiM Ru contrasts harmful vs. benign perceptual inputs (matched-format benign images to avoid canvas/OCR confounds) pooled over patch/projected tokens at site s ∈ {E, P, F, C, Q, VT}. **Intervention:** extract=intervene — pure rank-1 ablation a ← a − λ(a⊤v̂)v̂ at the same site, with λ>1 (typically 4 or 8) over-ablating along the same axis; optional RFA (Yu et al..) patches to the negative-side mean projection instead of zeroing, treated as a within-site recipe. **Sites:** T = text-decoder residual stream; E/P/F = encoder/projector/fusion around it; UNet down/mid blocks for the generative band. **Gates:** matched-norm random unit vector at the same site near base; empty/collapsed-generation rate near zero before reading ASR; frozen layer/λ operating points fixed per model before final generation (Table 4); HarmBench (n=320) and StrongREJECT (n=313) eval-only, never entering extraction. **Judging:** Class credit requires Nemotron∧HarmBench-cls above base (keyword ASR never qualifies); plotted ASR is the median of a seven-judge LLM panel with IQR bars, calibrated on gold160 held-out labels (panel accuracy 0.89–0.91). **Mechanism instruments (Fig. 2):** cached per-layer residuals (n=128 HADES dev) scored by (a) where an intervention moves a prompt along the first-PC refusal axis (midpoint crossing), (b) projection ratio ρ = ⟨Δh, v̂⟩/g in harm→benign gap units, and (c) rank/leverage of −v̂ among 50 matched-norm randoms on a teacher-forced refusal-vs-comply safety margin. **Coverage:** a derivative taxonomy (Axes A1–A7: weight tuning, decode-path change, training-objective change, attached perception/generation stacks, mixer replacement, action/tool readouts, numeric format) evaluated on Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct lineage plus out-of-lineage leaves (Mixtral, Qwen2.5-Omni, RecGemma, Falcon-Mamba, BitNet b1.58, π0/MolmoAct, ...), with mapper-free donor cross when residual widths match and a ridge map W·v̂B for the width-mismatched 3B leaf.

## Results & Highlights

- **T-local attack on mixed inputs (primary claim).** On HADES test500 (adversarial image + harmful text, Vision-Instruct), text DiM + rank-1 ablation at T lifts Nemotron ASR 0.034→0.616 with matched-norm random at 0.024; the same mean DiM at P/F is exactly flat (0.021→0.021, equal to random). On Qwen2-Audio spoken AdvBench (TTS), T lifts 0.03→0.84 while the projector stays 0.02→0.03.
- **Directionality survives a text-payload control.** On VLGuard Unsafe prompts (n=200, Llama-Guard-4-3 judge, empty rate 0), the paper operating point moves ASR 0.030→0.150 at T (Δ+0.120) vs. only +0.020 at P and +0.035 at E, with random near base — modest absolute ASR but the same T>P/E ordering against a pure text-payload artifact.
- **Mechanism: extremal at T, near chance at perception sites.** At λ=4, Ru@P/E carries only 13–15% of image+harmful prompts past the refusal midpoint (5% unattacked) while text ablation carries 100%; projection ratio |ρ|≈0.64 at T vs. 0.07–0.11 at P/E with 99% bootstrap bands that never overlap; on the safety-margin ranking, −v̂ at T is rank 50/50 with leverage 6.0, vs. rank 28–32 and leverage ≤0.26 at P/E (chance ≈25.5).
- **Catalog breadth (chat band, AdvBench n=392, Nemotron∧HBC gates).** ToolACE 0→0.82 (HBC 0→0.73); Mixtral-8x7B 0.03→0.86 (HBC 0.04→0.82); Qwen2.5-Omni thinker@T 0.04→0.66 (HB/SR 0.09→0.40 / 0.01→0.58); Hermes 0.23→0.69 but HarmBench headroom tiny (0.38→0.43); RecGemma-9B 0.06→0.47; Open-R1 Distill 0.33→0.90 (HBC 0.11→0.32). Llama-Guard-3 harmful→safe label miss rises 0.03→0.88 (n=256, label task not chat ASR).
- **Donor cross is lineage-gated.** Mistral-family donor→Mixtral (overlap R=0.63) reaches ASR 0.98, while Llama donor→Mixtral (R=0.036, below the 0.067 random band) transfers nothing; Llama→UltraMedical (R=0.822, η=0.84) and Llama→LLaVA-Mini (R=0.768, η=1.00) transfer fully.
- **Generative band (separate judges).** Concept DiM at SD-Turbo U-Net down-blocks erases harmful generation: NudeNet 0.79→0 (T→I) and img2img down1 1.0→0 at λ=25; AudioLDM T→A erases under CLAP; mid-blocks stay flat under the same judges.
- **Negative results taken seriously.** FigStep does not reproduce the HADES win (a generalization ceiling); RFA can anti-lift strong pure leaves (Hermes pure ≈0.69 vs. RFA median ≈0.10); Sheared/Qwen-prune compress and DS-V2 attention-replace stay ineffective after closed λ/layer/RFA grids; Dream/LLaDA collapse to empty generations.

## Authors & Credibility

Chansu Han (NICT, han@nict.go.jp), Seok-Hwan Choi (Yonsei University), and Takeshi Takahashi (NICT). NICT is Japan's National Institute of Information and Communications Technology, a public research institute with an established security/AI-safety group; the paper reads as a careful audit study rather than a capability grab — the ethics statement explicitly withholds ready-to-weaponize tooling beyond what is already public in the refusal-direction literature. ## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty: good, incremental-but-real.** The single-direction refusal ablation (Arditi et al. 2024), same-architecture vector reuse, learned cross-model maps (Oozeer et al. 2025), and multimodal residual defences (ASTRA, MARS, CMRM, ShiftDC) all exist; the novelty is the joint question — same-site DiM at E/P/F as *attack* extracts, a broad derivative catalog under one protocol, and mapper-free donor transfer — plus the margin-rank/leverage instrument, which is the paper's most original methodological contribution.
- **Rigor: unusually high for a safety-audit preprint, with one acknowledged confound.** The authors are explicit that the site comparison is *not* a fully isolated location effect: the contrast variable (text prompt vs. perceptual input) and pooling (last token vs. mean over patches) change with the site, so "T vs. E/P/F" is a claim about this recipe family, not about sites per se. They also self-diagnose the pooling objection empirically (max-pool @E does not help; extract-time |a⊤v̂| dwarfs random) and the scale objection (λmm=4 mean-pool lifts coherent Llama-Guard ASR 0.035→0.232 with 11.5% gibberish — so flatness at E is operating-point-scoped, which they concede rather than hide).
- **The "multimodal" stress test is text-heavy.** HADES pairs adversarial images with explicitly harmful text prompts, so the big T-side win (0.034→0.616) could be largely text-payload-driven; the VLGuard control addresses this but at modest absolute ASR (0.150) and small n=200, and FigStep (typography-only harm) fails to reproduce the win at all — the authors honestly call it a ceiling, but it does cap the mixed-input generalization claim.
- **Operating-point heterogeneity is a real weakness.** Per-model λ ranges from 1 (Mixtral) to 6 (Hermes/Tulu) and layers from L10 to L20; Appendix H admits "late-resolved red leaves (λ/site unlocks after early blue drafts)", i.e. some per-leaf search preceded freezing. The freeze-then-report discipline mitigates but does not eliminate garden-of-forking-paths risk, and several hard Classes (MTP, VLA, BitNet, LM+DiT) are N/A or not-run, making the blue ledger's denominator heterogeneous by the authors' own note.
- **Reproducibility: above average.** Frozen OPs, judge calibration, splits, and an anonymized script archive are all provided; but the mechanism result (Fig. 2) rests on a single Vision-Instruct leaf with an n=128 extraction cache, judge calibration uses only 160 gold labels, and LLaVA-1.5's footnote shows the protocol does not always behave (random 0.33 not near floor).
- **Significance: solid for auditors, moderate for attackers/defenders.** The practical takeaway — a hub auditor can keep hooking T for DiM-style refusal audits across most derivatives — is genuinely useful; the security delta over Arditi et al. is modest since the strongest attack is still the known text-tower one. No red flags of overclaiming: nearly every strong sentence carries a scope qualifier, which is refreshing but also makes the headline less punchy than the title suggests.

## Strengths

- One shared, well-controlled protocol (extract=intervene, matched-norm random, empty-rate gate, disjoint splits, frozen OPs) applied uniformly across ~25 model leaves from at least seven architecture families — comparability that most safety audits lack.
- The margin-rank/leverage probe is a clean instrument for distinguishing "extractor too weak" from "feature absent" at a site, and it is reusable by future work.
- Negative results are first-class citizens: flat E/P/F cells, RFA anti-lift, FigStep ceiling, closed blue grids, and operating-point sensitivities are all reported rather than buried.
- The donor subspace-overlap screen (necessary-not-sufficient) gives practitioners a cheap pre-test before attempting mapper-free vector reuse.
- Judge hygiene: seven-judge median with IQR, per-judge calibration table, keyword ASR explicitly disqualified from Class credit, chat vs. generative judges never pooled.

## Limitations

- Recipe-scoped by design: only mean/rank-1 DiM (plus optional RFA) is tested; multi-direction, affine, and SAE-feature extractors remain open, so "E/P/F ineffective" cannot be read as "refusal is absent outside T".
- The T vs. non-text comparison changes contrast variable and pooling with the site — not a causally isolated location effect (authors acknowledge).
- The flagship mixed-input benchmark (HADES) carries harmful text alongside the image, so modality-native refusal is not directly measured; the modality-clean VLGuard control has small n=200 and modest absolute ASR (0.150).
- Mechanism evidence comes from one Vision-Instruct leaf (n=128); generality of the rank-50/50 result to other VLMs is unverified.
- Per-model λ/layer tuning with some post-hoc "late-resolved" unlocks weakens strict comparability across Classes; several catalog cells are infeasible, N/A, or not-run rather than measured.
- Full experimental scripts, panel JSON, and extended grids are withheld until acceptance; the supplementary archive is only a keyword-ASR smoke path.

## Relevance

与"LLM Hidden States & Interpretability"兴趣高度契合：这是对拒绝方向（refusal direction）这一表征几何对象在约 25 个开源衍生模型、多类残差位点上的系统因果干预审计，margin-rank/leverage 探针和供体子空间重叠筛选都是可直接复用的隐藏状态分析工具。虽然论文本身聚焦安全审计而非训练可靠性，但其"同一干预协议在架构变体下何时失效"的方法论对理解残差流表征的稳定性有直接参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would multi-direction (rank-k) or SAE-feature refusal extractors at E/P/F show attack lift where the rank-1 mean DiM is flat, cleanly separating "weak extractor" from "absent feature"?
- Can the margin-rank/leverage instrument separate the third reading — T re-imposing refusal downstream of a successful perception-site edit — e.g. by ablating at P and T simultaneously with matched edit budgets?
- Why does the Llama donor direction land inside Mixtral's random overlap band (R=0.036) while a Mistral-family donor reaches R=0.63 — is refusal-subspace drift predictable from fine-tuning lineage or tokenizer/template distance?
- Does the T-locality result survive non-English harmful prompts, or prompts whose harm is carried entirely by the perceptual input (typography-free visual harm, inaudible-style audio artifacts)?
- Can the U-Net down-block concept-erase direction (NudeNet 0.79→0) be transferred mapper-free across generative heads (SD-Turbo → AudioLDM → video U-Nets) the way text-tower donors transfer across chat derivatives?
- How does the flatness at E/P/F interact with quantization and low-precision derivatives (A7/BitNet were N/A here) — does numeric-format change break the rank-1 protocol even at T?
