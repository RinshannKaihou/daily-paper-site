> **一句话总结：** 本文从注意力权重的对称性出发，推导出标量不变量 ρ——衡量每个注意力头与 RoPE 旋转位置编码耦合"容量"的基不变量指标；无需任何前向传播或数据，ρ 即可在 SmolLM2-135M/1.7B、TinyLlama-1.1B、Mistral-7B 四个模型上以 −0.55 至 −0.74 的 Spearman 相关预测归纳头（induction heads），且仅对 ρ 前 50% 的头施加 YaRN 使 Mistral-7B 短上下文困惑度从 19.37 降至 12.92（均匀施加为对照）。

> **TL;DR:** A 5-page NeurIPS 2026 NeurReps workshop extended abstract that derives ρ, a basis-invariant scalar computed purely from W_Q/W_K weights, measuring each attention head's capacity to couple to RoPE. Without forward passes, ρ predicts induction heads with Spearman r between −0.55 and −0.74 (all p < 10⁻⁴⁰) across four bias-free RoPE models (SmolLM2-135M, TinyLlama-1.1B, SmolLM2-1.7B, Mistral-7B), with the top-10 induction heads always in the bottom 7–30% of the ρ distribution; as a proof of concept on Mistral-7B, applying YaRN only to the top-50% ρ heads degrades perplexity less than uniform YaRN (short PPL@512 12.92 vs 19.37; long PPL@4096 130.34 vs 162.96).

## 核心贡献

- **提出权重层面的几何不变量 ρ**：将注意力 logit 分解为静态内容项 G_QK(0) = W_Q^⊤ W_K 与 RoPE 诱导的位置扰动项 P(∆) = W_Q^⊤[R(∆) − I]W_K，定义 ρ 为对数间隔位置集合 D = {2ⁿ} 上 ‖P(∆)‖²_F / ‖G_QK(0)‖²_F 的平均值，从而量化每个头耦合位置编码的"容量"。
- **严格的不变性论证**：证明 G_QK(0) 在完整头空间重参数化 (W_Q, W_K) → (M⁻⊤W_Q, M W_K)（任意 M ∈ GL(d_h)）下不变；在 RoPE 下要求整个 G_QK(∆) 族不变会把对称群收缩到 (C*)^{d_h/2} ≅ (SO(2) × ℝ₊)^{d_h/2}，因此 ρ 是基选择无关、尺度不变的头部结构观测量。
- **数据无关的头分析方法**：ρ 只需读取权重即可计算，无需前向传播、无需激活值、无需构造探测输入，为 LLM 的"数据无关"分析开辟了新方向（与依赖激活修补或行为探测的主流可解释性方法形成对比）。
- **实证验证 ρ 预测归纳头**：在四个无偏置 RoPE 模型（135M–7B 参数）上，ρ 与归纳分数的 Spearman 相关达 −0.55 至 −0.74（均 p < 10⁻⁴⁰），最强归纳头始终落在 ρ 分布最低 7–30% 区间；给出"容量"解释——归纳是位置无关的内容匹配操作，低 ρ 是必要而非充分条件。
- **应用示范（容量门控上下文扩展）**：在 Mistral-7B 上仅对 ρ 排名前 50% 的"位置头"施加 YaRN 缩放，相比对所有头均匀施加，短/长上下文困惑度劣化均更小。
- **可推广的不变签名框架**：指出 G_QK 特征谱、G_VO 奇异值与交换子 ‖[G_QK, G_VO]‖_F 构成每头的完整不变签名，为后续权重不变量研究铺路。代码已开源（github.com/MPedraBento/rho-invariant）。

## Key Contributions

- Derives ρ, a non-negative, basis-invariant, scale-invariant scalar from the composite query-key matrix: the attention logit s_ij = x_i^⊤G_QK(0)x_j + x_i^⊤P(∆)x_j is split into a static content term G_QK(0) = W_Q^⊤W_K and a RoPE-induced positional perturbation P(∆) = W_Q^⊤[R(∆) − I]W_K, and ρ averages ‖P(∆)‖²_F / ‖G_QK(0)‖²_F over the log-spaced set D = {2ⁿ}_{n=0}^{⌊log L⌋} (log spacing matches the geometric spacing of RoPE frequencies ω_k = θ^{−2k/d_h}).
- Establishes the symmetry argument: G_QK(0) is invariant under the full head reparameterization (W_Q, W_K) → (M⁻⊤W_Q, M W_K), and demanding invariance of the whole RoPE family G_QK(∆) breaks GL(d_h) to (C*)^{d_h/2} ≅ (SO(2) × ℝ₊)^{d_h/2}, making ρ a genuine basis-independent observable.
- Proposes data-free head analysis: ρ requires no forward passes, activations, or crafted probe inputs — unlike activation patching (Conmy et al. 2023), probing classifiers, or behavioral diagnostics (Olsson et al. 2022).
- Shows ρ predicts induction heads — the most-studied mechanistic-interpretability circuit — with Spearman r from −0.55 to −0.74 (all p < 10⁻⁴⁰) across four bias-free RoPE models, and interprets this via the capacity view: induction ("A B ... A → B" copying) is position-invariant content matching, so low ρ is necessary but not sufficient (induction is a multi-head circuit).
- Demonstrates a practical application, capacity-gated context extension: applying YaRN only to the top-50% heads by ρ degrades perplexity less than uniform YaRN on Mistral-7B.
- Frames a general research program of per-head invariant signatures (G_QK eigenspectrum, G_VO singular values, commutator ‖[G_QK, G_VO]‖_F), with code released.

## Methodology

The paper is theory-driven with a compact empirical validation. On the theory side, the author starts from the observation that attention heads admit basis-invariant observables, analogous to physics where theories have fewer observables than parameters. The head is characterized by the quadratic form G_QK(∆) = W_Q^⊤R(∆)W_K in query-key space (with the rotation R(∆) block-diagonal, one 2×2 rotation per RoPE frequency plane). The attention logit for relative position ∆ = i − j decomposes as s_ij = x_i^⊤G_QK(0)x_j + x_i^⊤P(∆)x_j where P(∆) = W_Q^⊤[R(∆) − I]W_K isolates the position-dependent part. The invariant is defined as ρ = (1/|D|) Σ_{∆∈D} ‖P(∆)‖²_F / ‖G_QK(0)‖²_F with D = {2ⁿ} logarithmically spaced up to context length L — log spacing because RoPE frequencies are geometric, so uniform-in-log-∆ sampling probes all frequency bands up to L. Proposition 1 proves ρ = 0 iff P(∆) = 0 for all ∆ ∈ D, i.e. the logits are exactly position-independent for any residual-stream inputs. The author is explicit that ρ measures capacity (what the geometry allows), not usage (what the head does on a given input). Two caveats are analyzed: ρ is unchanged under W_Q → αW_Q or W_K → αWK (scale invariance) but is sensitive to the softmax-irrelevant shift s_ij → s_ij + c_i, with weight decay argued to mitigate this heuristically. Empirically, induction scores are measured via repeated-random-sequence prompts on four bias-free RoPE models (SmolLM2-135M, TinyLlama-1.1B, SmolLM2-1.7B, Mistral-7B) and correlated with ρ (Spearman). For the application, YaRN scaling is applied selectively to the top-50% of heads by ρ (the "positional heads") on Mistral-7B at 4× scale, and perplexity is compared against uniform YaRN at short (512) and long (4096) contexts, staying within the native context window.

## Results & Highlights

- **ρ predicts induction heads data-free:** Spearman correlation between ρ and induction score is −0.74 on SmolLM2-135M, −0.71 on TinyLlama-1.1B, −0.55 on SmolLM2-1.7B, and −0.59 on Mistral-7B — all p < 10⁻⁴⁰ (induction score baseline measured via repeated random sequences).
- **Localization of the top induction heads:** the ten strongest induction heads always lie in the low-ρ tail — bottom 14% of the ρ distribution (SmolLM2-135M), bottom 7% (TinyLlama-1.1B), bottom 17% (SmolLM2-1.7B), and bottom 30% (Mistral-7B).
- **Capacity-gated context extension (Mistral-7B, S = 4×):** applying YaRN only to the top-50% ρ heads gives short-context PPL@512 = 12.92 vs 19.37 for uniform YaRN over all heads (a 6.45-point / 33% relative reduction in degradation; the S=1 baseline is 7.63), and long-context PPL@4096 = 130.34 vs 162.96 for uniform YaRN (baseline S=1: 5.44; both long-context values are inflated by evaluating near the context limit).
- The negative direction of the correlation is itself a finding: induction heads are the *lowest*-ρ heads, consistent with induction being a position-invariant content-matching operation, while high-ρ "positional heads" are the ones that actually couple to RoPE.
- Code is released at https://github.com/MPedraBento/rho-invariant, making the invariant reproducible for any bias-free RoPE checkpoint.

## Authors & Credibility

Single-author work by Miguel P. Bento of Feedzai (Lisbon, Portugal), explicitly stated to have been conducted independently of the author's role at Feedzai. The paper is a peer-reviewed extended abstract (5 pages) accepted at the Symmetry and Geometry in Neural Representations Workshop (NeurReps) at NeurIPS 2026 — a venue well matched to the symmetry-based approach. The mathematical core (group-invariance argument, Proposition 1 with proof) is clean and verifiable at a glance, and the empirical protocol (standard repeated-random-sequence induction scores, public checkpoints: SmolLM2, TinyLlama, Mistral-7B) uses widely available models rather than private systems. Code release further supports credibility. No external funding or conflicts are stated beyond the affiliation disclaimer.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** Genuinely novel angle — weight-only, basis-invariant head characterization via residual symmetry of the RoPE family is a fresh contribution relative to activation-based methods (activation patching, probing) and to data-dependent RoPE analyses like Barbero et al. (2024). The capacity-vs-usage distinction is conceptually clean. However, this is an extended abstract: the theory is one proposition plus a definition, not a full treatment of the advertised "invariant signature" program.
- **Rigor:** Proposition 1 is elementary but correct, and the invariance group computation is plausible. Weaknesses: the softmax-shift sensitivity (ρ changes under s_ij → s_ij + c_i even though attention is unchanged) is dismissed with a heuristic weight-decay argument rather than measured or bounded; the correlation analysis reports no confidence intervals or per-layer breakdowns; and "predicts induction heads" overstates a monotone association — the author honestly concedes low ρ is necessary, not sufficient.
- **Reproducibility:** Good — public checkpoints, standard induction-score protocol, released code. The ρ definition (Eq. 2) is fully specified including the sampling set D and its dependence on context length L.
- **Significance / red flags in the YaRN experiment:** The headline application is preliminary and its absolute numbers are poor — at S=4× the targeted variant still has long-context PPL@4096 of 130.34 vs a 5.44 baseline, i.e. the model is essentially broken at the context limit and the paper concedes values are "inflated ... due to the evaluation near the context limit." The comparison is only against uniform YaRN on a single model (Mistral-7B); there is no control ablation such as applying YaRN to a random 50% of heads or to heads ranked by a cheaper criterion (e.g. weight norms), so the evidence that ρ specifically (rather than any selective gating) drives the improvement is missing. Evaluation also stays within the native context window rather than actually extending it, so "context extension" is not demonstrated end-to-end.
- **Scope limits:** Results are restricted to bias-free RoPE models and the QK sector only; nothing is said about models with attention biases, ALiBi/NoPE variants, or how ρ behaves under fine-tuning/quantization. Only induction heads are validated quantitatively — other head types remain a conceptual sketch (Figure 1).

## Strengths

- A crisp, well-motivated theoretical object: ρ is cheap (one Frobenius-norm ratio per head over log-spaced offsets), basis-invariant, scale-invariant, and interpretable as positional capacity.
- Strong, consistent empirical signal across four models spanning 135M–7B parameters (Spearman −0.55 to −0.74, p < 10⁻⁴⁰) with the top-10 induction heads confined to the bottom 7–30% of ρ — a genuinely useful zero-cost prior for locating induction circuitry.
- Honest framing: the author clearly labels the YaRN application a "preliminary proof of concept," states the capacity-not-usage caveat, and discusses the softmax-shift limitation rather than hiding it.
- Opened a concrete, generalizable research direction (invariant signatures per head: G_QK eigenspectrum, G_VO singular values, commutators) with code released.

## Limitations

- Extended-abstract depth: no per-layer or per-frequency analysis, no study of how ρ distributes across head types beyond induction heads, and the "invariant signature" generalization is proposed but untested.
- ρ is sensitive to the softmax shift; the weight-decay mitigation is asserted, not verified empirically.
- The correlation is moderate (−0.55 on SmolLM2-1.7B) and low ρ is only a necessary condition for induction — ρ alone cannot identify the circuit.
- The YaRN experiment is single-model, within the native context window, lacks a random-selection or norm-based control ablation, and the absolute perplexities at long context are far from usable.
- Applicability is limited to bias-free RoPE architectures and the QK sector; ρ is defined only over offsets up to L, so nothing is claimed about extrapolation beyond the sampled range.

## Relevance

与 LLM 机理可解释性和模型分析方向高度相关：ρ 提供了不依赖数据与前向传播的注意力头角色刻画手段，可用于低成本定位归纳头、指导上下文扩展等位置编码干预；对关注 transformer 内部机制与权重结构分析的研究者是一个值得关注的新工具与研究方向。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does selectively scaling heads by ρ beat a random-50% or weight-norm-ranked-50% control at the same budget, and does the benefit persist when evaluation actually exceeds the native context window?
- How does the ρ distribution correlate with other known head types (duplicate-token, previous-token, name-mover, successor heads), and with the proposed G_QK eigenspectrum / G_VO singular-value / commutator signature?
- Can the softmax-shift sensitivity be removed by normalizing within the quotient geometry (e.g. optimizing over the shift component c_i), and does weight decay empirically bound the resulting error in pretrained checkpoints?
- Does ρ remain a stable predictor after fine-tuning, quantization, or RoPE-base changes (e.g. θ re-parameterization), and how does it behave for models with attention biases or non-RoPE position schemes?
- Since induction is a multi-head circuit, can low-ρ be combined with a complementary invariant (e.g. a copy-score from G_VO) to make head-role identification sufficient rather than merely necessary?
