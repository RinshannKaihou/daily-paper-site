> **一句话总结：** 本文提出一个任务中心的内部状态诊断框架（层级注意力熵 + FFN 激活稀疏度 + 解码时轨迹 + 线性探针 + NOTA 选项扰动），在 16 个开源 LLM × 24 个基准上刻画"知识检索—推理"连续谱：知识型任务（如 SciQ、BioMixQA）的正确答案在中后层变得线性可恢复（SciQ 上探针精度超多数类基线 +0.69），推理型任务则停留在基线附近；CoT 让 14/16 个模型的推理分显著上升（中位 +0.036，Wilcoxon p=.008）但知识分不变（9/16，p=.597）；而移除正确选项（NOTA-as-answer）在 SciQ/MathQA 上造成中位 34.6 个百分点的准确率崩塌（24/24 个 McNemar 检验显著），暴露出 CoT 增益之下仍残留的选项匹配捷径。

> **TL;DR:** Instead of asking what score an LLM achieves, this paper characterizes the internal processing behind the score: layer-wise attention-entropy/activation-sparsity profiles, decoding-time trajectories, linear probing, and "none-of-the-above" (NOTA) option perturbations across 16 open-weight LLMs and 24 benchmarks. Knowledge-centric tasks (SciQ, BioMixQA) show the correct answer becoming linearly recoverable in mid-to-late layers (probe accuracy up to +0.69 over the majority-class baseline on SciQ), while reasoning-centric tasks (LogiQA, MathQA) stay near baseline (peaks of 0.29–0.34 vs. baselines of 0.24/0.28 for Llama-3.1-8B and OLMo-3-7B). Behaviorally, CoT raises normalized reasoning scores in 14/16 checkpoints (median +0.036, p=.008) but not knowledge scores (9/16, p=.597), and removing the ground-truth option costs a median 34.6 accuracy points vs. only 1.2 for adding a NOTA distractor — high scores can hide strong dependence on the presence of the original correct option.

## 核心贡献

- **任务中心的四信号诊断框架**：将（1）静态层级内部画像（逐层注意力熵 + 前馈激活稀疏度）、（2）解码时轨迹（生成过程中信号的演化）、（3）基于表征的度量（逐层线性探针 + 正确/干扰答案的表征几何）、（4）行为级压力测试（NOTA 选项扰动）组合成一个统一的知识—推理分析框架，用于刻画未修改 LLM 的原生推理过程（不微调、不注入特殊 token、不改变解码模式）。
- **16 模型 × 24 基准的知识—推理能力图谱**：覆盖 Qwen、Llama、Gemma、OLMo、GLM、GPT-OSS 六个家族（2B 至 30B+，含 dense 与 MoE），用 10 个知识型评测设置与 20 个推理型评测设置的非加权平均并归一化，绘制 Standard vs. CoT 两种条件下的能力平面。
- **系统性的实证发现**：知识型与推理型任务在内部处理模式上存在系统差异（探针可恢复性、表征几何、轨迹变异性）；知识分随参数规模更稳定地增长而推理收益更不单调；CoT 主要提升推理维度且统计显著；NOTA-as-answer 揭示广泛存在的选项依赖，且 CoT 只能部分缓解。
- **严格的控制与统计规范**：prompt 长度回归校正、统一末 token 控制、知识/推理模板交换实验；Wilcoxon 符号秩检验、逐题精确 McNemar 检验，均配 Benjamini-Hochberg FDR 校正，并报告 Cliff's delta 与 rank-biserial 效应量；代码开源（github.com/extraordinarylab/trace-kr）。

## Key Contributions

- A **task-centric, four-signal diagnostic framework** for characterizing the processing an unmodified LLM elicits from a benchmark: static layer-wise profiles (attention entropy + FFN activation sparsity), decoding-time trajectories, representation-based measures (layer-wise linear probing + correct-vs-distractor geometry), and behavioural NOTA perturbation stress tests — no fine-tuning, special tokens, or forced fast/slow thinking modes.
- A **knowledge–reasoning capability landscape over 16 open-weight LLMs and 24 benchmarks** (Qwen, Llama, Gemma, OLMo, GLM, GPT-OSS families; 2B–30B+; dense and MoE), built from unweighted averages over 10 knowledge-centric and 20 reasoning-centric evaluation settings and min–max normalized jointly across model × prompting (Standard vs. CoT) conditions.
- **Systematic empirical findings**: knowledge- and reasoning-centric tasks differ in pre-generation linear recoverability of the answer, representation geometry, and within-trajectory attention variability; knowledge scores scale more consistently with parameter count than reasoning scores; CoT significantly shifts the reasoning dimension but not the knowledge dimension; NOTA-as-answer exposes a broad, only partially CoT-mitigated dependence on the presence of the original correct option.
- **Unusually careful controls and statistics** for this genre: prompt-length and same-final-token controls for the static profiles, a template-swap control decoupling task type from prompt format, Wilcoxon signed-rank and exact McNemar tests with Benjamini–Hochberg correction, and effect sizes (Cliff's δ, matched-pairs rank-biserial). Code is open-sourced.

## Methodology

The paper operationalizes **knowledge retrieval** as identifying/recalling task-relevant information with little transformation, and **reasoning** as deriving information through intermediate operations (inference, calculation, comparison, state tracking); benchmarks are placed on a continuum between "knowledge-centric" and "reasoning-centric" dominant tendencies, with the taxonomy assigned from benchmark design independently of the analyses.

1. **Static internal profiles.** All questions from the 24 benchmarks are passed through a single reference model (Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct) with no prompt template and no answer generation. For the final input token, they compute head-averaged attention entropy per layer (diffuse vs. concentrated attention) and activation sparsity (proportion of hidden dimensions with |u| < 0.01), concatenated into a per-benchmark feature vector; benchmarks are ordered by the first principal component of the sparsity matrix. Controls: within-benchmark regression on log prompt length, symbolic-token ratio, and appending an identical "\nAnswer:" suffix to every prompt.
2. **Decoding-time trajectories.** On SciQ, MathQA, and LogiQA with a retrieve-then-answer prompt (temperature 0.7, top-p 0.8, max 1,024 tokens), generations are length-normalized to 101 aligned positions; token-wise mean trajectories and within-trajectory standard deviations of both metrics are compared between correct and incorrect predictions at representative lower/middle/upper layers (3, 14, 22).
3. **Layer-wise probing.** Logistic-regression probes on final-input-token hidden states (5-fold CV) predict the correct answer option on a balanced diagnostic set: BioMixQA and SciQ (knowledge-centric) vs. LogiQA and MathQA (reasoning-centric), across Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, and OLMo-3-7B-Instruct, compared against majority-class baselines. A template-swap control runs every benchmark under both the knowledge-style and reasoning-style prompts to decouple task type from template.
4. **Representation geometry.** Adjusted cosine similarity between hidden states of [question; correct answer] vs. [question; distractor], normalized by the expectation over random pairings, tracks how distinctly correct and incorrect completions are represented.
5. **Capability landscape.** 16 models are evaluated with EvalScope + SGLang (accuracy or exact match); knowledge/reasoning scores are unweighted means over the 10/20 evaluation settings and min–max normalized, plotted for Standard vs. CoT per model with arrows showing the CoT-induced shift.
6. **NOTA stress tests.** On MathQA and SciQ with six instruction-tuned models: NOTA-as-answer (ground-truth option removed, NOTA is correct) vs. NOTA-as-distractor (extra wrong option injected), under Standard and CoT prompting; item-level exact McNemar tests with BH correction over all 48 comparisons diagnose reliance on option matching vs. option-invariant reasoning.

## Results & Highlights

- **NOTA-as-answer collapse (MathQA + SciQ, 6 models)**: median accuracy decrease of **0.346** relative to Base, significant in **24/24** exact McNemar tests after BH correction; NOTA-as-distractor costs a median of only **0.012** and is significant in just **3/24**. Correctness in multiple-choice settings often depends on the presence of the original ground-truth option, and CoT reduces but does not eliminate this gap (e.g., Llama3.1 retains a large Base-vs-NOTA-as-answer gap even under CoT).
- **CoT effect (16 checkpoints)**: normalized reasoning score median change **+0.036**, positive in **14/16** models, two-sided Wilcoxon **p = .008**, rank-biserial **rrb = +0.74**; knowledge score median change **+0.001**, positive in only **9/16**, **p = .597**, **rrb = +0.16**. Counterexample: Gemma-7B moves down-left under CoT (e.g., SciQ 85.50 → 64.30, MusicTrivia 57.03 → 37.11).
- **Layer-wise probing (3 model families)**: on LogiQA/MathQA, Llama-3.1-8B and OLMo-3-7B peak at **0.29–0.34** probe accuracy vs. majority-class baselines of **0.24 and 0.28** (near-chance); Qwen2.5-7B is the exception, rising from layer ~20 to **0.46 on LogiQA / 0.35 on MathQA** vs. baselines of **0.18 / 0.11**. Knowledge-centric tasks recover the answer far more strongly: gains over baseline of **+0.22 on BioMixQA and +0.69 on SciQ**. The template-swap control shows the contrast survives swapping prompt templates.
- **Within-trajectory variability**: incorrect generations show greater attention-entropy SD than correct ones, significant (one-sided Mann–Whitney, BH-adjusted **q < .01**) at lower and upper layers on all three benchmarks (Cliff's **δ = 0.42 lower, 0.45 upper**; middle layer q = .055, δ = 0.14); activation sparsity shows no consistent signal (q = .999/.999/.205).
- **Static-profile controls undermine the headline sparsity pattern**: lower-layer (0–3) sparsity correlates with mean prompt length (Spearman **ρ = 0.43**) but not symbolic-token ratio (**ρ = −0.09**); length adjustment preserves the benchmark ordering (**ρ = 0.92**), but forcing the same final token collapses it to **ρ = 0.37**, and knowledge- vs. reasoning-centric groups do **not** differ significantly (Mann–Whitney **p = .19** before / **p = .21** after the suffix).
- **Scaling and family effects**: knowledge scores increase more consistently with parameter count; reasoning gains are smaller and less monotonic — scaling Gemma-3 from 4B to 27B yields a substantial knowledge gain but only modest reasoning improvement, while small models (Gemma-3-4B, Qwen2.5-3B) are already competitive on reasoning. Qwen3 variants sit near the top-right of the plane (e.g., Qwen3-30B-A3B: 97.3 on SciQ, 71.94 → 74.07 on MRI-MCQA with CoT).

The authors repeatedly and explicitly label findings as associations rather than causal mechanisms — an honesty level above the genre norm.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty: moderate.** No new metric is introduced (the authors say so); the contribution is the systematic combination of known signals under a task-centric lens. The capability landscape's K/R split is a simple unweighted mean over benchmark groups, not a psychometrically validated construct.
- **The headline static-profile result is fragile, and the paper's own controls prove it.** Figure 1's smooth benchmark ordering in lower-layer sparsity is substantially explained by prompt length (ρ = 0.43), collapses when the final token is fixed (ρ = 0.37), and shows no significant knowledge-vs-reasoning difference (Mann–Whitney p = .19/.21). The robust RQ1 evidence (probing, geometry, trajectories) instead rests on only 4 MC benchmarks and 3 models — far narrower than the advertised 16 × 24 scale.
- **Single reference model for the static analysis** (Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct): family-specific artifacts cannot be ruled out for the 24-benchmark profile, though the probing/geometry analyses add Llama-3.1-8B and OLMo-3-7B.
- **Taxonomy subjectivity propagates into the landscape.** Labels are assigned by design intent: QASC (explicitly "multi-hop" science) is knowledge-centric while PubMedQA (yes/no over supplied abstracts) is reasoning-centric — debatable calls that directly define the K and R score groupings. The settings-vs-benchmarks bookkeeping (|D_K| = 10 settings vs. 8 listed knowledge benchmarks, because HaluEval's three sub-settings count separately) is underexplained.
- **NOTA generality is narrow**: only MathQA and SciQ, six models. NOTA-as-answer also changes the answer distribution itself, so some drop is expected even for calibrated reasoners; the informative quantity is the asymmetry vs. NOTA-as-distractor (0.346 vs. 0.012), which the paper does report correctly.
- **Correlational throughout**: no activation patching, layer ablation, or causal tracing; probing nulls on reasoning tasks may reflect linear-probe/final-token limits rather than absence of reasoning information (acknowledged). CoT analysis confounds Think/No-Think configurations of Qwen3-8B and cannot isolate scale from training-data changes (acknowledged).
- **Reproducibility: high.** Code released, exact prompts (Figures 12–13), sampling parameters, model list with licenses and release dates, and statistical tables (Tables 3–5) are all provided.

## Strengths

- Scale and breadth of the behavioural sweep (16 open-weight models × 24 benchmarks, including very recent checkpoints) with a clean, normalized two-axis summary.
- Genuinely informative negative controls: the prompt-length, same-final-token, and template-swap experiments are the kind of self-scrutiny rarely applied to interpretability-derived signals.
- Statistical rigor rare in evaluation papers: BH-corrected McNemar/Wilcoxon plus effect sizes, so claims like "CoT improves reasoning" come with p = .008 and rrb = +0.74 rather than eyeballed arrows.
- The NOTA-as-answer vs. NOTA-as-distractor asymmetry is a cheap, actionable diagnostic for option-matching shortcuts that any practitioner can add to an eval harness.
- Honest framing of knowledge and reasoning as a continuum, with mixed cases (GPQA, MMLU-Pro) discussed rather than hidden.

## Limitations

- English-only; probing, representation, and NOTA analyses require multiple-choice structure, so conclusions do not transfer to open-ended generation (the authors' own stated limitation).
- The most robust internal-state findings cover only 3–4 benchmarks and 3 mid-sized models, not the full 24-benchmark landscape the abstract foregrounds.
- No causal interventions, so all internal-state differences are associations.
- The knowledge/reasoning split is operational (amount of intermediate transformation) and the group assignment is hand-curated; individual benchmarks are impure mixtures.
- The capability landscape's min–max normalization makes axis values sensitive to the best/worst model in the pool, and score changes are hard to interpret in absolute accuracy terms.

## Relevance

该论文正好处于 LLM 可解释性与能力评估的交叉点：它把"模型答对究竟靠记忆检索还是靠推理"从行为层面推进到内部状态层面，并提供了两个可直接复用的工具——逐层线性探针诊断答案可恢复性、NOTA 选项扰动检测选项匹配捷径。对于关注模型评测方法学、CoT 增益真实性与按需选型/选基准的读者，这是一篇方法论参考价值很高的工作。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Do the probing and NOTA findings replicate on free-form and multilingual benchmarks where no option set exists, or is option dependence partly an artifact of MC formatting?
- Can causal interventions (activation patching, layer ablation) convert the observed associations into mechanisms — e.g., verify that the mid-to-late-layer direction found by probes on SciQ actually causally drives the answer?
- Is the NOTA-as-answer gap a measurable axis of reasoning quality across stronger reasoning models (Qwen3-Think, GPT-OSS at high reasoning effort), i.e., does it shrink toward zero for genuinely option-invariant reasoners?
- Could the within-trajectory attention-entropy variability signal (higher SD for incorrect generations, δ ≈ 0.42–0.45) be used as a training-free, inference-time incorrectness/uncertainty detector?
- How does retrieval-augmented generation shift the knowledge–reasoning landscape — when knowledge is supplied externally, does the more reliable scaling of the knowledge axis still hold?
