A phase diagram for training and inference in language models ↗

Zhuo-Yang Song · Zeyu Li · Qing-Hong Cao · Ming-xing Luo · et al.  — PKU statistical-mechanics group · code MIT · data CC BY 4.0

representation geometry mechanistic interpretability training dynamics phase transition statistical mechanics

01 Key metric

256 → 512

training steps · Pythia-1.4B

The fraction of deep-layer states in the working phase jumps from ~0 to the vast majority inside this window, with inertia α collapsing to ≈0 in lockstep — training behaves like a phase transition with step as the control parameter.

02 Why this matters

Turns "how representation geometry evolves with depth" into a measurable phase diagram and dynamical system — a decoding-free window onto the earliest moments of model training.

03 Critical edge

The diagram separates trained from untrained — not good from bad: no loss, perplexity, or downstream capability is ever correlated with phase residence.

— Critical Assessment, "Missing links to behavior"

04 Methodology · how it's measured

  • Document, not token, is the unit — attention mixes topic, coreference, and style across a document. Fixed corpus of 96 coherent long documents: 32 PG-19 books, 32 arXiv papers, 32 GovReport reports, truncated to 16,384 tokens each (2,048 for Pythia-1.4B's context limit).
  • Two collective coordinates from a single forward pass: distinguishability u = |δ|²/√(tr C²) and document dimension d_C = (tr C)²/tr C². Both rotation- and scale-invariant, so any network — any width, any basis — lands on the same logarithmic (u, d_C) diagram.
  • 156 untrained baselines: the same 8 architectures with only Transformer-block weights randomized (two init families, σ ∈ [0.005, 1], seeds pinned).
  • Phases assigned empirically from late-half states: a central working phase around P* = (1.08, 117), an outer drifting phase, and a 0.16–0.84 working-phase-fraction contour band as the coexistence region.
  • Dynamics via a discrete Langevin equation v(t+1) = αv(t) + F(x(t)) + η(t), fit on a 0.1-dex grid and linearized at P*; eigenvalues of the propagation matrix give the per-layer contraction rate −ln ρ, and a Péclet number Pe = |F/s| scores force against fluctuation.

  • All 8 trained flows enter the working phase by median relative depth 0.11 and remain there at every later layer.
  • Geometry→readout link: across 155 blocks of 5 models, 96-way document-ID accuracy scales d′ ∝ E^0.42 (R² = 0.90) with E = u/(√d_C + u).
  • Step-0 gotcha: deep states already sit inside the working phase yet α still matches the drifting phase — phase is dynamical, not positional.
  • Per-model refits: median −ln ρ = 0.027, all 8 positive — contraction is not a pooling artifact.

Errors are 16–84% bootstrap intervals. Trained group spans 5 architecture families, depths 28–48, widths 1536–5120, 4 institutions.

06 Strengths

  • One cheap, decoding-free forward pass separates trained from untrained networks across families, widths, depths, and training recipes.
  • Basis- and scale-invariant coordinates make cross-model comparison legitimate — a real methodological contribution beyond the findings.
  • The Langevin formalism converts description into a predictive dynamical criterion (saddle vs stable node; the sign of −ln ρ).
  • Honest uncertainty throughout — bootstrap intervals, pinned revisions and seeds; code (MIT) and data (CC BY 4.0) fully public.
  • The Appendix-A "dictionary" unifies rank collapse, neural collapse, probe-depth trends, and effective-rank dynamics in one coordinate system.

07 Limitations

  • Only 8 dense English models (1.5B–14B); untested at frontier scale, MoE, multilingual, multimodal, or non-Transformer architectures.
  • Transition evidence: one model, one run, no checkpoint between steps 256 and 512 — the window is bracketed, not resolved.
  • The most causal analysis (Pythia-1.4B) rests on the shortest-context model (2,048 vs 16,384 tokens) — a confound the paper itself flags.
  • Force F fit only near P* (0.3 ≤ u ≤ 3, 30 ≤ d_C ≤ 400); outer-region dynamics are extrapolated from trajectory fates.
  • Phase boundary is empirical and corpus-dependent; the coexistence band is attributed to finite depth but not shown to narrow.
  • Pooling assumes layer actions depend only on state; the per-network ablation covers only the trained group.

08 Questions for further exploration

  • Does residence position within the working phase (distance from P*) correlate with training loss or benchmark performance — can the diagram separate good models from merely trained ones?
  • How do post-training stages (SFT, RLHF/DPO) move the inference flow — does P* shift, does contraction tighten, or does the flow stay fixed?
  • Does the step 256–512 transition coincide with known early-training events such as induction-head formation, and is the jump continuous or a true discontinuity?
  • Do the collapse/noise fates of random inits predict trainability — do inits near the saddle's stable manifold reach a given loss with fewer tokens?
  • What do higher-order collective coordinates (beyond u and d_C) capture — and does the diagram survive multilingual, code-heavy, or conversational corpora?
