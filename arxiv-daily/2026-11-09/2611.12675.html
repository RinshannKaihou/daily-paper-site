Why this matters

Mechanistic findings about residual-stream geometry can be reused across model families — no per-model rediscovery of directions and probes.

Directly relevant to any mechanistic-interpretability work that wants to carry internal directions/probes between models (残差流表征几何 · 机制跨模型对应).

Critical edge ▲

The title outruns the evidence: only linear directions are tested — SAE features and circuits are explicitly untested — and all base↔instruct transfers rest on a single seed.

Methodology · Results · Assessment ↓

Methodology

  • Scale of study. All 110 ordered pairs among 11 instruction-tuned checkpoints (Llama-3.x, Qwen2.5, Gemma-2, Phi-3, Mistral; 1B–72B; the two largest in 4-bit NF4), plus base↔instruct transfers. Representation = L2-normalized residual stream at the last token, at relative depth 0.5 (refusal) / 0.75 (read-out).
  • The map. Ridge regression (intercept, λ via exact leave-one-out) from source to target representations of the same prompts — trained on 5,400 general questions from nine datasets, optionally plus unlabeled task-region prompts (harmful/harmless instructions, answer candidates, SST-2 reviews). No labels ever cross models.
  • Tools carried. A refusal steering direction (difference of means over 128+128 prompts; addition + ablation, cross-checked with Llama Guard 3), a correctness read-out direction (ARC-Challenge, TruthfulQA MC1), and an SST-2 sentiment probe.
  • Measurement. Recovery R = (E_carried − E₀)/(E_native − E₀) against magnitude-matched random / orthogonal / mapped-random controls; 5 seeds; 95% bootstrap intervals resampling models.
  • Theory. Proposition 1 bounds tool misalignment by sin θ ≤ κ√(1−c²), where c is a label-free coverage score of the map's training data along the tool.

Recovery = fraction of the target's own native-tool effect; task prompts are unlabeled.

  • Coverage is predictive. Across 16,000+ runs, runs with coverage c ≥ 0.95 average 99% recovery; c < 0.8 recovers only 4%. A GSM8K-only map (c = 0.46) transfers essentially nothing.
  • Explains prior failures. A domain-specialist map hits R² = 0.48 in-domain but −1.94 on the other eight domains (90% of 72 combinations negative) — cross-domain “mapping fails” reports are extrapolation failures.
  • Scale- and family-robust. With task prompts, Llama-3.2-1B steers Llama-3.1-70B at 101% (reverse: 96%); recovery is uncorrelated with the parameter gap.
  • Carried beats unlabeled-target. The target's top principal component fails on ARC (−76%); the carried direction is worth ≈25 labeled target questions. A probe carried through 10 unlabeled reviews beats one built from 10 labeled reviews by ~9 points.

Strengths

  • Exceptional controls: three matched control directions, five seeds, model-level bootstrap intervals, pre-stated target-inclusion criteria.
  • A real theorem tied to practice — Proposition 1 exactly predicts the saturation, zero-transfer, and threshold behaviors observed.
  • Resolves a live contradiction: prior negative cross-domain R² results are exactly what a coverage-limited linear map must produce.
  • Honest self-assessment: states where transfer saves nothing (refusal is rebuildable from the same 16 prompts) and which conditions are single-seed.
  • Coverage + “map KL” turn the theory into an actionable recipe: whether the data suffice, and which source to carry from.

Limitations

  • Only linear direction tools and one linear probe tested — SAE features, circuits, multi-direction concepts, nonlinear tools untested.
  • Proposition 1 assumes linear correspondence on the covered region; κ is pair-specific and unmeasured a priori.
  • Coverage presupposes knowing where a tool operates; small prompt draws can miss (weakest 16-prompt draw: 44%).
  • Refusal scored by English phrase-matching at one strength per target; base models untested for refusal.
  • 11 open models only (no closed/API), 4-bit for the two largest, and white-box activation access is inherently required.

Questions for further exploration

  • Does the coverage framework extend to SAE features and full circuits, where the map may need to be sparse or compositional?
  • Can κ be estimated label-free a priori, making coverage a true per-pair recovery predictor?
  • Does heavier quantization shift the linear correspondence or merely rescale it?
  • Would the account predict transfer for emergent-misalignment directions or backdoor triggers, whose operating region is harder to specify?
  • Can carried tools bootstrap stronger native tools — e.g., pseudo-label target questions, then re-estimate in the target's raw space?
  • Is the c ≈ 0.8–0.95 threshold universal, or does it shift with intrinsic dimension (18–24 here)?
