Key Finding

Deliberately violating DRAM timing makes chips more vulnerable — so RowHammer thresholds can be profiled 31.2% faster online.

HARE（ETH Zurich）在剖析时故意压缩 PRE-to-ACT 时序（fastHammer）来加剧读扰动，从而加速在线 RDT（读扰动阈值）剖析，并保持对 RowHammer 弱点的保序覆盖。

Why This Matters

RDT drifts unpredictably, making static RowHammer configuration untenable (8.5-minute mean time to failure). HARE makes online profiling cheap enough to keep mitigations correctly configured — on real silicon.

Distilled from Relevance · 一句话总结

Critical Edge

The headline reliability numbers rest on a benign-by-construction Monte Carlo model and Ramulator simulation — no hardware prototype exists; an adversary aware of weak rows could invalidate them.

From Critical Assessment (rigor) · Limitations

Methodology 方法

  • Real silicon: DRAM Bender FPGA platform drives 180 COTS DDR4 chips (25 modules; SK Hynix, Micron, Samsung, Nanya) plus 10 HBM2 — refresh disabled, ECC absence verified, physical row mapping reverse-engineered.
  • RDT measurement: double-sided hammering, worst-case data pattern, 80 °C, 3,000 rows/bank, bisection to 1% tolerance, 5 repeats per row (minimum kept).
  • fastHammer: issue ACT just 7.5–12 ns after PRE, violating tRP; below 7.5 ns multi-row activation changes the bitflip physics — that is the floor.
  • Two variants: HARE-Direct reports the conservative fastHammer minimum; HARE-Filter ranks rows by fastHammer RDT, then re-profiles only the top candidates with the true RowHammer pattern. Both run periodically, refresh-style.
  • Evaluation: 1M-trial Monte Carlo reliability model + Ramulator 2.0 cycle-level simulation (DDR5, 4-core, 36 configurations across PRAC / Graphene / PARA).

Strengths 优点

  • Cross-manufacturer generality: 27.0–33.5% RDT reduction holds on all four major DRAM vendors (180 real chips).
  • One elegant lever attacks both profiling costs at once: shorter hammer cycles and fewer hammers to first bitflip.
  • The proxy pattern is proven, not assumed — >95% cell overlap and weakest row in top 5.6% across all 25 modules.
  • Unfavorable results reported plainly (HARE-Filter's higher error rate; overlap degradation at aggressive latency).

Limitations 局限

  • Benign-by-construction error model (uniform-random access); weak-row-aware adversaries could invalidate the 6.2% / 0.67% figures.
  • Performance numbers are DDR5 simulations seeded by DDR4 measurements; no memory-controller prototype implements HARE.
  • More accurate ≠ safer: HARE-Filter's batched updates delay RDT tightening, raising P(UE) vs HARE-Direct.
  • 3,000 rows/bank sampled at a single 80 °C point; authors' own Fig. 4 shows module-min RDT not stabilized below 9K rows.

Questions for Further Exploration 待探索问题

  • Do the overlap and ranking properties survive adversarial access patterns that adapt to the profiler's schedule, and temperature/voltage corners beyond 80 °C nominal?
  • Can streaming per-row updates fix HARE-Filter's accuracy-vs-reliability trade-off?
  • Does reduced-tRP exacerbation persist on DDR5 / LPDDR5 / HBM3 nodes, where nominal tRP is already much shorter?
  • Can an in-DRAM HARE (via Self-Managing DRAM) read raw cell data without on-die ECC masking weak flips?
  • Is there a principled — or adaptive — way to pick the PRE-to-ACT operating point per module instead of the fixed 9 ns empirical choice?
