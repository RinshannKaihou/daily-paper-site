Key Finding · arXiv 2611.16736

The two “natural” sequence-level losses for autoregressive LMs are unbounded below — so teacher forcing may be all you need.

两种「自然」的解码感知序列级损失均无下界（可发散到 −∞）；监督学习阶段，teacher forcing 可能「就是你所需要的一切」。

On the Difficulty of Sequence-Level Supervised Learning for Autoregressive Language Models ↗
Mathieu Blondel, Michaël E. Sander, Germain Vivier-Ardisson, Tianlin Liu, et al.  ·  Google DeepMind  ·  2026-11-16  ·  theory-only (7 propositions, full proofs)
exposure-bias teacher-forcing sequence-level-losses autoregressive-models learning-theory
Key Metric · Proven Divergence
L → −∞

Both natural losses diverge to −∞: Lgreedy = 1−α and Lsampling = 3⁄2·log2 + 1⁄2·log(1+eα) − α, proven on minimal T=2, V=2 counterexamples.

Honest note: no experimental metrics exist — the paper runs zero experiments; this is the sharpest quantitative anchor it proves.

Why This Matters

Explains mathematically why LLM pretraining still uses token-level cross-entropy: plugging greedy decoding or sampling into the loss makes it unbounded.

Critical Edge

The total absence of experiments: the variance-based dismissal of the moment-matching direction is asserted analytically — never measured, not even on a toy run.

01 Methodology

Works in a function-space (tabular) setting: a next-token scorer q(st,yt) (a causal Transformer in practice) induces the AR sequence distribution. Candidate sequence-level losses are built structured-prediction-style by replacing two intractable O(VT) oracles with cheap decoding surrogates.

  1. Substitute oracles. Exact max / sequence-level log-sum-exp (both O(VT)) → greedy argmax (O(VT)) or autoregressive sampling with model-sampled prefixes.
  2. Disprove by counterexample. Minimal T=2, V=2 constructions expose discontinuity, nonconvexity (Jensen violation), and unboundedness.
  3. Unify via a new AR Gumbel trick. Greedy decoding of Gumbel-perturbed scores = ancestral sampling; sampling loss = expected perturbed greedy loss.
  4. Repair via stop-gradient → the “moment matching” direction dq, characterized three ways (non-self-adjoint Jacobian; preconditioned AR gradient via the soft-Bellman map; self-critic view).
  5. Situate against violation-fixing updates, scheduled sampling, and RL post-training.

02 Results — Beyond the Headline

All evidence is theoretical: seven propositions with complete proofs (Appendix A), no benchmarks.

03 Strengths

  • Complete, self-contained proofs for every claim, with explicit checkable counterexamples instead of hand-waving.
  • The Gumbel unification turns two separate flaws into one understood phenomenon — a genuinely clarifying result.
  • dq characterized from three complementary angles, connecting EBM theory, structured prediction, GANs, and scheduled sampling in one framework.
  • Honest negative-result framing with actionable guidance: change the data distribution (on-policy distillation) or use RL post-training — don’t patch the loss.

04 Limitations

  • No empirical validation of any kind — the variance claim and the divergences are never demonstrated numerically.
  • Descent guarantee holds in function space only; parameter-space descent can fail even for linear parameterizations.
  • Fixed-length, finite-vocabulary setup (𝒴 = 𝒱T); variable-length handling outsourced to the companion paper.
  • Depends on companion work (arXiv:2512.15605) for the soft-Bellman mapping ℳ underpinning Props. 6–7.
  • Impossibility covers only the two plug-in losses; bounded surrogates (best-of-n distillation) are set aside, not analyzed.

05 Questions for Further Exploration

  • Can the variance of dq be tamed at small scale — control variates, baseline subtraction, truncated Gumbel-Top-k?
  • Do beam-search cumulant losses stay unbounded for every finite beam width k?
  • Could approximating the preconditioner (∂ℳ(q)*)−1 accelerate ordinary teacher-forcing training?
  • Do finite-candidate pseudo-reference losses converge to the unbounded sampling loss as n → ∞?
  • Can the self-critic view inspire reward-free sequence-level objectives that sidestep these impossibility results?
