Key Finding · 核心发现

Row-by-row widening accumulators replace uniform BF16 in MX systolic arrays — up to 38.7% less PE area for ≤1.06% added perplexity.

Why This Matters · 意义

Closes the overlooked middle link of the MX/FP8/FP4 deployment story — intermediate-accumulator hardware cost — with designs directly usable in TPU/Trainium-style systolic datapaths.

Critical Edge · 保留意见

The headline −38.7% area saving and the headline accuracy sit at different operating points — MXFP4's biggest win costs +17–30% perplexity vs BF16, and every config was tuned on 1.1B-param TinyLlama only.

Methodology 方法

  1. Calibrate the MX block-scale shift d. Sweep d off the OCP default (d = emax) to the smallest value statistically indistinguishable from best perplexity — MXFP8 → d=0, MXFP6/4 → d=2. Removes a 216 product amplification and 14–16% activation-block clipping at the source.
  2. Precision-ladder datapath. On a Gemmini weight-stationary array, every row rounds its partial sum to its own floating-point format — narrow in early rows, progressively wider later (e.g., e5m3 → e5m5) — with one shared product format.
  3. Certified search. Minimize normalized area × energy under a perplexity budget PPL(θ) ≤ (1+ε)·PPLBF16: feasibility screen → uniform-width bracketing → a Widrow-style rounding-noise loss model (fit on 11 ladders, predicts 319 unseen with RMS 0.0066) → certify no cheaper candidate survives.
  4. Ground-truth evaluation. Bit-exact simulator matched to FireSim FPGA RTL over 65,536 elements; PE area/energy from 16 nm / 2 ns synthesis; Triton emulation makes perplexity sweeps 2.2–7.4× faster.

Where the money is: cutting accumulator mantissa m7→m5 saves 12.7% area / 13.9% energy; cutting exponent e8→e5 saves only 2.6% / 0.9%.

  • The bottleneck, quantified: BF16 accumulation alone accounts for >50% of PE-array area (up to 54% of PE energy) in the baseline — untouched by operand quantization.
  • Transfer without retuning: TinyLlama-tuned 16×16 configs applied unchanged to Mistral-7B, Llama-3.1-8B, Llama-2-7B/13B, Qwen2.5-14B — worst case +1.6% added PPL (Qwen2.5-14B MXFP6 32×32 reaches +1.88%, outside the original ε).
  • End-to-end speed: FireSim cycle simulation shows −48.3% cycles for 16×16 MXFP8 vs a BF16 array on Mistral-7B prefill (1.15 vs 2.22 ×10⁹).
  • Downstream holds: mean accuracy over 7 zero-shot tasks shifts ≤0.91 pts; HumanEval deltas (e.g., +3.7 pts) sit inside the ±5.6–6.7-pt paired noise interval.
  • Search validity: MXFP8 space cut 177× (16×16) / 3,009× (32×32); an exhaustive 8×8 sweep confirms the selected ladders minimize cost under the perplexity target.

Strengths 优势

  • Attacks a real, quantified bottleneck (>50% PE area, up to 54% PE energy) that operand-only quantization cannot touch — via one clean architectural insight.
  • Software (block-scale calibration) and hardware (precision ladders) halves are co-designed; each is necessary for the other to pay off.
  • Trustworthy evaluation: bit-exactness verified against RTL-level simulation, synthesis-derived costs, certified optimality, exhaustive small-scale verification.
  • Honest accounting — operand vs intermediate error separated in every table; derived power figures explicitly marked.
  • Practical output: per-format configurations a designer can adopt directly, with transfer evidence across five models.

Limitations 局限

  • Weight-stationary systolic arrays only; output-stationary arrays, adder trees, and GPU-style tensor cores are out of scope.
  • Per-format, per-array-size tuning on a single 1.1B model; the ε-constraint is not re-imposed in the transfer experiments.
  • Perplexity-centric evaluation (WikiText-2 + 7 zero-shot tasks with ±4-pt uncertainty + one code benchmark); no long-generation, agentic, or multimodal workloads.
  • Pre-silicon results only — 16 nm synthesis and estimation; no taped-out chip, no memory-system (bandwidth / SRAM) analysis.
  • Loss model assumes uncorrelated, zero-mean rounding noise with w_r ∝ r+1; outlier-heavy layers could mislead candidate ranking.

Questions for Further Exploration 延伸问题

  1. Would per-layer or per-tensor ladders (vs one ladder for all 67 linear layers) buy further area at the same perplexity?
  2. Is d=0 for MXFP8 stable across models with stronger activation outliers, e.g., at longer context lengths?
  3. Can the rounding-loss model guide operand precision too — joint per-layer MX format selection with intermediate precision?
  4. Can depth-ordered precision schedules map onto adder-tree (output-stationary / tensor-core) reductions, where stages are not spatially ordered?
  5. Does the TinyLlama-tuned ladder stay certified under distribution shift (code, multilingual, long-context) where WikiText-2 PPL is a weak proxy?
  6. How much area/energy remains in the retained BF16 inter-tile accumulation, and does narrowing it need the same calibration machinery?
