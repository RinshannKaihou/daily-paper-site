> **一句话总结：** 本文针对"表征统计量大→架构上移除→性能提升"这类 Transformer 设计范式提出三项可复用的严谨性检验（效应可解性、几何零模型、匹配干预），并以"独占式自注意力"（XSA，删除注意力头输出中与自身 value 向量对齐的分量）为案例：在 9 个预训练模型 5,408 个头上，48%–63% 的自 value 余弦对齐可由更早位置的因果零模型解释；在 50.9M 参数、8 种子配对因子实验（FineWeb-Edu，每 run 约 4.0×10^8 token）中，删除自身 value 方向使验证损失降低 0.0029 nats（Holm 校正 p=0.0023）并显著优于固定随机方向对照（−0.0040 nats，p=0.0018），而随机方向对照与基线无差异——说明该收益确实依赖于"自身 value 方向"而非任意秩一编辑。

> **TL;DR:** A methodological sanity-check framework for "representation-motivated" attention surgery, applied to exclusive self-attention (XSA), which removes the component of each head's output aligned with its own value vector. Across 9 pretrained multi-head models (GPT-2, Pythia; 5,408 heads, 124M–6.9B params; WikiText-103), an earlier-position anisotropy null explains 48–63% of the raw self-value cosine alignment, so large alignment alone does not imply redundancy. In a pre-registered 3-arm (BASELINE / XSA / RANDOM fixed-direction control) × 8-seed paired factorial at 50.9M parameters on FineWeb-Edu (399,900,672 tokens per run), the RANDOM control does not differ from baseline (+0.0011 nats, p=0.197) while XSA lowers validation loss by 0.0029 nats vs baseline (Holm-corrected p=0.0023) and beats RANDOM directly by 0.0040 nats (p=0.0018) — the training benefit requires the own-value direction, but the effect is tiny and only demonstrated at 50.9M scale.

## 核心贡献

- **提出三项可复用的严谨性检验**，用于审查"测量内部表征统计量→发现其值很大→架构上删除→报告改进"这一常见的设计逻辑链：
  - **Check 0（可解性）**：用折半信度（split-half reliability）估计行为干预效应的信度 r_Δ 与结构统计量的信度 r_stat，规定 r_Δ ≥ 0.6 为可靠、0.3–0.6 为衰减、< 0.3 为不可解——相关系数小可能是测量噪声所致，不能直接解读为无关联。
  - **Check 1（几何零模型）**：将 cos(y_i, v_i)（头输出与自身 value 的余弦）与同序列更早位置的零模型 cos(y_i, v_j) 对比，量化超出共享因果表征几何（各向异性）的"超额对齐"。
  - **Check 2（匹配干预）**：用预注册的配对种子因子实验，将"自身方向删除"与参数化完全相同的"固定任意方向删除"对照直接比较，检验训练收益是否真的依赖特定方向。
- **重构校验门（reconstruction gate）**：通过用注意力矩阵与 value 重构头输出并检查相对 Frobenius 误差（阈值 10⁻²），捕捉头布局/转置错误（布局错误会产生 1.39–1.50 量级的误差），避免"张量形状正确但干预对象错误"的静默失败。
- **对 XSA（独占式自注意力）的系统审计**：在 9 个多头预训练模型（GPT-2 家族 + Pythia 套件，124M–6.9B 参数，共 5,408 个头）上测量发现，48.1%–63.1% 的自 value 余弦对齐可由更早位置零模型解释，且超额部分（0.14–0.21）无随规模的单调趋势——即使 6.9B 模型也有 58.1% 的对齐归于零模型。
- **分组查询注意力（GQA）下的组特异性发现**：对齐是 head 自身 KV 组特有的——组内超额为 +0.24 到 +0.27，而跨组超额为 −0.11 到 −0.19（方向反转），在 Qwen2.5-0.5B/1.5B 与 TinyLlama-1.1B 上一致。
- **结论性方法论警示**：在 Pythia-2.8B 上原始自余弦与干预效应完全脱钩（ρ_raw = +0.003），说明"哪个统计量能预测自身的干预效果"是模型相关的——大对齐值本身不能作为表征冗余的证据。

## Key Contributions

- **Three reusable sanity checks** for the common "measure a representation statistic → observe it is large → remove it architecturally → report a gain" design pattern: (Check 0) split-half resolvability of the behavioural intervention effect (r_Δ ≥ 0.6 reliable, 0.3–0.6 attenuated, < 0.3 unresolvable); (Check 1) a within-sequence earlier-position anisotropy null that quantifies *excess* alignment beyond shared causal geometry; (Check 2) a matched, pre-registered intervention comparing self-directed removal against an identically-parameterised fixed arbitrary-direction control.
- **A reconstruction gate** that rebuilds each head's output as A·expand_kv(V) and rejects relative Frobenius error above 10⁻², catching head-layout/indexing errors (deliberately corrupted layouts produce errors of 1.39–1.50) before any measurement is trusted.
- **A ladder-wide geometry audit of XSA**: across 9 multi-head pretrained models (GPT-2 family and Pythia suite, 124M–6.9B parameters, 5,408 heads, WikiText-103 test documents), the earlier-position causal null accounts for 48.1%–63.1% of the head-value cosine alignment; the excess (0.14–0.21) shows no monotone scale trend, and even at 6.9B parameters 58.1% of the alignment (0.198 of 0.340) is attributable to the null.
- **Grouped-query attention specificity**: the alignment is specific to each head's own key–value group and reverses across groups (within-group excess +0.24 to +0.27; across-group −0.11 to −0.19) on Qwen2.5-0.5B, Qwen2.5-1.5B, and TinyLlama-1.1B.
- **A pre-registered causal test at training time**: a 3-arm × 8-seed paired factorial (identical initialisation, token order, LR schedule, and validation batches within each seed) showing the training benefit of self-directed removal requires the own-value direction rather than any rank-one edit.
- **The overarching methodological lesson**: which structural statistic predicts its own intervention effect is model-dependent (raw self-cosine decouples completely at Pythia-2.8B, ρ_raw = +0.003), so large internal alignments alone do not demonstrate representational redundancy.

## Methodology

The paper studies **exclusive self-attention (XSA)** (Zhai, 2026), which edits each head's output y_i by removing its component along the head's own value vector v_i: z_i = y_i − tanh(α)·⟨y_i, v̂_i⟩·v̂_i, where α is a per-layer, per-head gate (unit multiplier for frozen measurements; learned from zero in training). The edit acts before the output projection; a zero gate disables it and a negative gate adds the aligned component.

**Check 0 (resolvability).** The evaluation corpus is split into halves A and B; each head's intervention effect Δ (frozen, one head at a time, multiplier 1, first token excluded) and structural statistic are measured on both halves. Spearman correlations across heads give split-half reliabilities r_Δ and r_stat, and an attenuation diagnostic ρ_max = √(r_Δ·r_stat) with ρ_disatt = ρ_raw/ρ_max (explicitly framed as a diagnostic, not a calibrated estimate). Applied to GPT-2, Pythia-160M, Pythia-410M (64 docs/half, fixed 128 tokens), extended to Pythia-1.4B/2.8B at half budget.

**Check 1 (anisotropy null).** For each head and position, cos(y_i, v_i) is compared with cos(y_i, v_j) where j is drawn uniformly from earlier positions in the same sequence; the paired difference ("excess") controls for background causal geometry. Measured on WikiText-103 test documents across twelve pretrained models (nine multi-head + three grouped-query), with model revisions pinned by hash. Two further diagnostics use the same philosophy: attention-sink mass (first token vs next three tokens) and massive activations (max |hidden coordinate| vs the Gaussian reference √(2 log d)).

**Check 2 (matched intervention).** A decoder-only model (8 layers, 8 heads/layer, width 512, ~50.9M parameters) is trained from scratch on FineWeb-Edu sample-10BT with a fixed disjoint validation holdout, under three arms: BASELINE (unchanged), XSA (learned gate on the own-value edit), and RANDOM (the same gated rank-one edit along a fixed arbitrary direction per layer/head, seeded by layer index and held fixed across positions, steps, and seeds). Eight seeds × three arms = 24 cells, each seeing 399,900,672 tokens; all arms of a seed share initialisation, token stream, LR schedule, and validation batches. The **primary endpoint is pre-registered as RANDOM vs baseline**; XSA vs baseline is secondary with Holm correction; a direct XSA-vs-RANDOM paired test is also reported. Analysis uses two-sided paired t-tests, percentile bootstrap CIs over seed differences, and a realised minimum-detectable-effect approximation MDE = 2.9·σ_paired/√n. Total cost: $15.58 against a $20 ceiling (16.98 GPU-hours of training cells).

**Reconstruction gate.** y = A·expand_kv(V) is rebuilt and compared with the input to the output projection; relative Frobenius error > 10⁻² or non-finite values cause rejection. Observed max error across checked layers was ~1.6×10⁻³ per model.

## Results & Highlights

- **Check 2 (primary result, 8 paired seeds, 50.9M model, FineWeb-Edu):** the RANDOM fixed-direction control does not differ from BASELINE (mean Δ = +0.001056 nats, 95% CI [−0.000298, +0.002390], p = 0.197), while XSA lowers validation loss vs BASELINE by −0.002924 nats (95% CI [−0.003967, −0.001709], t = −4.67, Holm-corrected p = 0.0023, Cohen's dz = −1.649); the direct paired XSA-vs-RANDOM contrast is −0.003980 nats (t = −4.86, p = 0.0018). The XSA effect is ~1.6× its realised MDE (0.00182 nats); the control effect is below its MDE (0.00215 nats).
- **Check 1 (9 multi-head models, 5,408 heads, WikiText-103):** the earlier-position null accounts for 48.1%–63.1% of cos(y_i, v_i); observed self-cosine declines from 0.483 (GPT-2, 124M) to 0.340 (Pythia-6.9B), but the excess stays within 0.14–0.21 with no monotone scale trend; at 6.9B the null still explains 58.1% of the alignment (0.198 of 0.340).
- **Check 0 (split-half reliability):** the per-head intervention effect is reliable in GPT-2 (r_Δ = 0.795) but attenuated in Pythia-160M (0.419) and Pythia-410M (0.531), while structural self-cosine reliability is near-perfect in all three (r_stat = 0.991–0.997); extended runs classify Pythia-1.4B (r_Δ = 0.666) and Pythia-2.8B (0.632) as reliable at half document budget.
- **Statistic–effect association is model-dependent:** disattenuated self-cosine correlations are +0.519 (GPT-2), +0.231 (Pythia-160M), and −0.034 (Pythia-410M); in Pythia-2.8B raw self-cosine completely decouples from the intervention effect (ρ_raw = +0.003, ρ_disatt = +0.003 despite r_Δ = 0.632), and the excess correlation is even negative there (ρ_disatt = −0.158).
- **GQA group specificity:** own-group excess is +0.2415 vs neighbouring-group −0.1876 for Qwen2.5-0.5B (14 query heads over 2 KV heads), with the same sign pattern on Qwen2.5-1.5B and TinyLlama-1.1B (within-group +0.24 to +0.27, across-group −0.11 to −0.19).
- **Attention-sink and massive-activation diagnostics on GPT-2 survive their nulls:** first-token attention mass 0.403 vs 0.003 on matched early positions; massive-activation statistic 12.87 vs Gaussian reference 3.65 (≈71.7% remains after subtraction) — unlike self-value alignment, these phenomena are not explained away.
- **Learned gates diverge by arm:** XSA final layer means of tanh(α) range 0.14–0.47 (partial removal), whereas the RANDOM control's later-layer gate means go negative (adding along its direction).

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** The individual ingredients (split-half reliability, anisotropy nulls, matched controls) are standard psychometrics/statistics; the contribution is their disciplined assembly into a reusable checklist for representation-motivated architecture edits, plus the first controlled audit of XSA (arXiv 2603.09078). It is a methodological/guardrail paper, not a new architecture.
- **Rigor:** Genuinely strong for the training experiment — pre-registered primary endpoint, paired seeds sharing initialisation/token order/schedule, Holm correction, bootstrap CIs, MDE analysis, and a reconstruction gate against silent head-layout bugs. But the effect size is tiny (−0.0029 nats, roughly 0.1% of typical loss levels), demonstrated only at 50.9M parameters and 4.0×10⁸ tokens — two orders of magnitude below the 0.7B–2.7B regimes of the prior work it audits, and the planned larger-scale check was cancelled.
- **Control confound (paper's own admission):** the RANDOM control is a *static* direction fixed across positions, while XSA removes an *input-dependent* per-token direction; the XSA-vs-RANDOM contrast therefore confounds own-value specificity with per-token vs static projection, and the per-token control (e.g., projecting out an earlier token's value) "remains to be tested". Projected norms and converged gate strengths also differ between arms, so direction is not fully isolated from magnitude.
- **Reproducibility concerns within the paper itself:** the GPT-2 geometry run failed to reproduce the project's reference triple within its 0.01 tolerance (measured 0.4828/0.2987/0.1840 vs reference 0.5406/0.3798/0.1608); the null is strongly length-dependent (moves 0.096 across 64–1024 tokens, nine times the tolerance; excess ranges 0.235@64 to 0.412@1024 tokens), so cross-study comparability of such statistics is poor unless length is reported. An earlier Pythia-160M run reported a raw excess correlation of +0.487 vs +0.189 in the current run, and the protocol changes between them were not isolated.
- **Missing pieces:** no downstream-task evaluation (only validation loss in nats); frozen-intervention reliability covers only 5 of 12 models (7 ladder models lack intervention measurements); the statistic–effect correlation flips sign across models (excess ρ_disatt = +0.342 at Pythia-410M but −0.158 at Pythia-2.8B), which undercuts any claim that these statistics are general targeting tools.
- **Red flags:** none of the dishonest kind — the paper is unusually candid about its own deviations. The main risk is over-generalisation: the abstract's clean message ("benefit requires the own-value direction") holds at 50.9M with a static control only.

## Strengths

- Converts a vague discomfort about "statistic-large-then-remove" papers into three concrete, reusable, cheaply runnable checks with explicit thresholds (r_Δ ≥ 0.6 etc.) that transfer to any representation-motivated edit, not just XSA.
- The anisotropy null is measured within the same run, same documents, same stated length — internally matched — making the 48–63% null-share estimate robust to cross-study corpus differences.
- The pre-registered primary endpoint being the *control* comparison (RANDOM vs baseline) is a model of self-sceptical design: it tests whether any rank-one edit would have "worked".
- Exceptional transparency: MIT-licensed code, Zenodo archive, an automated 48-cell table checker, a 33-claim reproduction manifest, disclosed deviations, and a detailed adversarial AI-use statement.
- The GQA within-group vs across-group reversal (+0.24..+0.27 vs −0.11..−0.19) is a crisp, novel empirical finding that the alignment is genuinely tied to the head's own KV group rather than generic geometry.
- Frugality: the entire 24-cell factorial cost $15.58, demonstrating that statistically disciplined small-scale experiments can answer causal questions that big-budget sweeps often leave confounded.

## Limitations

- The training result is confined to a 50.9M-parameter model at 4.0×10⁸ tokens; effect signs and magnitudes at the 0.7B–2.7B scales of prior work are unknown, and the planned scale check was not run.
- The matched control does not isolate direction from per-token-vs-static projection or from projection magnitude; a per-token control is explicitly left to future work.
- Frozen and trained interventions differ in gate constraints and first-token handling, so the two sets of results describe related but non-identical edits.
- All evaluated statistics are cosine-based; attention-probability or projection-norm statistics may behave differently.
- Disattenuated correlations are diagnostics (half-sample reliabilities applied to pooled correlations), not calibrated corrections; rank correlations describe within-sample monotonicity, not out-of-sample prediction.
- 7 of 12 ladder models lack intervention-effect measurements; reliability coverage is 5 models.
- The GPT-2 geometry measurement does not reproduce the reference triple within tolerance, and the null's strong length dependence (excess 0.235 at 64 tokens vs 0.412 at 1024) limits cross-study comparability.

## Relevance

这篇论文直接服务于"如何科学评估基于内部表征统计量的 Transformer 架构修改"这一方法论问题：它给出的三项检验（信度、几何零模型、匹配对照）可以套用到任何"解释性统计驱动的架构编辑"研究（如注意力头剪枝、表征去噪、电路级手术）上，其 GQA 组特异性发现和对 XSA 的因果审计对关注注意力机制内部结构与可解释性控实验设计的工作有直接参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would a *per-token* control (projecting out an earlier token's value direction, mirroring the Check 1 null) erase the XSA-vs-RANDOM gap of −0.0040 nats, or does the benefit genuinely require the own-value direction?
- Does the −0.0029-nat validation-loss benefit persist at 0.7B–2.7B parameters and longer token budgets, and does it translate to downstream task performance rather than just perplexity?
- Why does raw self-cosine predict intervention effects in GPT-2 (ρ_disatt = +0.519) yet completely decouple in Pythia-2.8B (ρ_raw = +0.003) — is there a scale-, data-, or architecture-driven mechanism?
- Can the within-group vs across-group reversal in GQA models (+0.24 vs −0.19) be exploited as a cheap diagnostic for value-sharing pathologies in grouped-query and multi-query architectures?
- Do attention-probability- or projection-norm-based statistics (rather than cosines) yield more stable cross-model predictors of intervention sensitivity?
- How general is the 48–63% null-share of self-value alignment — does a similar earlier-position null explain other popular representation statistics (e.g., attention-sink mass, massive activations) beyond the two that survived it here?
