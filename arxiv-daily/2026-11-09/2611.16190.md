> **一句话总结：** 本文从数学上审计了两个广泛使用的可解释性度量——Elhage 等人的特征维度（feature dimensionality）与 Chen 等人的特征恢复率（FRR）——精确刻画了前者发生不连续跳变的位置，提出带岭正则的稳定替代版本并证明了紧的 Lipschitz 稳定性常数（岭杠杆分数的常数 3√3/(8√λ) 不可改进），同时给出"特征 i 已被恢复"这一论断在字典扰动下必然成立的证书；在玩具模型与合成字典实验中，证书从未出错，但在不同训练种子之间（FRR 在标准阈值 τ_align 下 55.47% 的特征判定随种子翻转，而无阈值面积 c̄ 仅移动 ≤0.0054）证书无法非平凡地认证任何特征。

> **TL;DR:** A theory-first audit of two interpretability measurements — Elhage et al.'s feature dimensionality D_i (whose sum-rule regularity "failed" by 24.8% under a τ=0.2 norm threshold on the authors' 12-model ReLU toy sweep, while the unthresholded sum stayed ≥0.997·d) and Chen et al.'s feature recovery rate FRR_t. The paper proves D_i is discontinuous exactly where a feature vector vanishes with a null direction left over, introduces a ridge-regularized version D̃_i with a sharp stability constant (ridge leverage changes by at most 3√3/(8√λ)·‖W−W′‖op, a constant shown unimprovable), and proves FRR certificates that held in 43,838+ synthetic checks with zero errors. The sobering negative result: on tied-weight SAEs (M=1024 atoms) trained on a known synthetic ground truth (V ∈ R^100×384), 55.47% of features flip recovered/not-recovered at the standard threshold τ_align across just three seeds while the threshold-free area c̄ moves ≤0.0054, and the certificates certify no feature non-trivially across seeds or even between two checkpoints of one run (atoms move ~20° vs margins of ~3°). No real language model is tested; all proofs were AI-drafted and blind-checked by an AI reviewer, not by independent human mathematicians.

## 核心贡献

- **精确诊断不连续性（定理 4.1）**：特征维度 D_i 恰好在集合 Z_i = {W : W_i = 0 且 ν_min(S_{-i}) = 0}（特征权重向量为零、同时存在与所有其他特征向量正交的方向）处不连续，且 D_i 与总和 D_tot 的跳变幅度恰为 1；在 Z_i 之外给出阶为 R/‖W_i‖² 的 Lipschitz 模数，并证明该阶不可去（2×2 反例族给出下界系数 ≥ 1/2，最优系数落在 [1/2, 4] 内未定）。
- **岭杠杆分数的紧稳定性（定理 5.4，附录 B 给出第二个独立证明）**：对所有矩阵形状，|ℓ_i(W;λ) − ℓ_i(W′;λ)| ≤ (3√3/(8√λ))·‖W−W′‖op，且该常数在 d=n=1 标量情形即可达到上确界，故不可改进；有效维度 d_eff 在核范数下具有相同的紧常数（定理 5.8）。
- **正则化维度 D̃_i 的稳定性（定理 5.21/5.23）**：|D̃_i(W;λ) − D̃_i(W′;λ)| ≤ (3√3/(8√λ) + R/(4λ))·‖W−W′‖op，其中尺度参数 ρ = R/√λ 前的系数 1/4 是最优的，渐近加性常数为 1/(4√2)，且对 R 的依赖不可移除；(ρ₁≈0.556, ρ₂≈3.326) 区间上的加性常数仍为开放问题（猜想 5.37，提出区间算术计算机辅助验证的候选）。
- **FRR 的证书框架（第 6 节）**：证明特征分数 c_i 与曲线下面积 c̄ 在线-Hausdorff 字典距离 H 下 Lipschitz（定理 6.7）；给出尖锐阈值 cos(θ_V/2)，高于它每个原子至多对齐一个特征（定理 6.8，阈值由二等分向量反例证明尖锐）；导出全局证书 C11（定理 6.12）与特征局部证书 C11-loc（定理 6.14），界定两个字典之间 FRR_t 的变化量。
- **指出 Chen 等人唯一性论断的缺口（注 6.10）**：在混用带符号与绝对余弦约定时，该唯一性陈述可被 d=n=2 的显式反例推翻。
- **诚实的实证测量（第 7 节）**：正则化总和规则在 12 个玩具模型的整个岭参数范围内给出唯一稳定的判定；证书在全部 43,838 次合成检查中零错误；同时如实报告了负面结果——证书在独立训练的字典之间（乃至同一训练的两个检查点之间）无法非平凡地认证任何特征。

## Key Contributions

- **Exact discontinuity diagnosis (Theorem 4.1):** D_i is discontinuous exactly on Z_i = {W : W_i = 0 and ν_min(S_{-i}) = 0} — a vanishing feature vector while some direction is orthogonal to all other feature columns — with jump size exactly 1 for both D_i and the sum D_tot. Away from Z_i a Lipschitz modulus of order R/‖W_i‖² is given and shown necessary (a 2×2 family forces coefficient ≥ 1/2; the optimal coefficient lies in [1/2, 4] and is open).
- **Sharp stability of ridge leverage (Theorem 5.4, second independent proof in Appendix B):** for every shape, |ℓ_i(W;λ) − ℓ_i(W′;λ)| ≤ (3√3/(8√λ))·‖W−W′‖op, and the constant is optimal (attained as a supremum already in the scalar d=n=1 case at w = √(λ/3)). The effective dimension d_eff has the same sharp constant in the nuclear norm (Theorem 5.8).
- **Stability of the regularized dimensionality D̃_i (Theorems 5.21/5.23):** |D̃_i(W;λ) − D̃_i(W′;λ)| ≤ (3√3/(8√λ) + R/(4λ))·‖W−W′‖op, with the coefficient 1/4 of ρ = R/√λ optimal, asymptotic additive constant 1/(4√2), and the R-dependence irremovable. The additive constant on the open range (ρ₁ ≈ 0.556, ρ₂ ≈ 3.326) is left open, with a two-variable inequality proposed as a candidate for computer-assisted proof.
- **Certificates for FRR claims (Section 6):** feature scores and the area c̄ are Lipschitz in the line-Hausdorff distance H between dictionaries (Theorem 6.7); above the sharp threshold cos(θ_V/2) each atom aligns with at most one feature (Theorem 6.8, sharpness by explicit bisector vectors); a global certificate C11 (Theorem 6.12: n|FRR_t(A) − FRR_t(A′)| ≤ N_γ(A;t) + max(X_γ, Y_γ), margin count plus moved aligned atoms) and feature-local certificates C11-loc (Theorem 6.14) bound FRR change between two dictionaries.
- **A gap in the cited uniqueness sentence (Remark 6.10):** the uniqueness claim of Chen et al. (Appendix C.4) can fail when the FRR's absolute-cosine convention is combined with the signed coherence used to define τ_align; a d=n=2 counterexample is given.
- **Epistemically careful measurements (Section 7):** the regularized sum rule returns one verdict over the whole ridge range on 12 toy models; adversarial searches never exceed the proved constants; certificates are never wrong — and the paper reports with equal prominence that they certify nothing non-trivially across seeds or checkpoints.

## Methodology

The paper is proof-driven, with experiments confined to settings where ground truth is known.

**Objects studied.** (1) Feature dimensionality D_i(W) = ‖W_i‖⁴/(W_i^⊤ S W_i) of Elhage et al. and its sum rule D_tot (Σ_i D_i, expected to equal the model width d); (2) the feature recovery rate FRR_t(A) = (1/n)#{i : c_i(A) ≥ t} with c_i(A) = max_m |cos(a_m, v_i)|, following Chen et al.'s τ_align = cos((1/3) arccos μ(V)) convention.

**Theory pipeline.** Section 4 characterizes the discontinuity set of D_i by direct perturbation analysis (a vanishing column with a null direction of S_{-i} lets D_i jump by exactly 1), plus a two-parameter 2×2 matrix family (Definition 3.23) reused three times to prove lower bounds (necessity of the R/‖W_i‖² order, sharpness of cos(θ_V/2), and sharpness of the 1/4 coefficient for D̃_i). Section 5 regularizes D_i by adding λ‖W_i‖² to the denominator (D̃_i) and proves Lipschitz bounds: a variational/one-test-vector reduction for ridge leverage (two fully independent proofs), and a segment argument with closed-form partial-derivative suprema for D̃_i, yielding constants that match explicitly constructed extremal families (Proposition 5.14), so the bounds are sharp in both the 1/4 coefficient and the asymptotic additive constant. Section 6 builds the certification layer from line-angle geometry (a triangle inequality for the line angle, chord–angle identities) culminating in the margin-count certificates C11/C11-loc, which bound n|ΔFRR_t| by the number of features within γ of the threshold plus aligned atoms that moved more than γ.

**Verification protocol.** Every statement carries a status marker (✓ blind-checked, ◦ reviewer's point open); proofs were drafted by an AI pipeline and checked by a separate blind AI reviewer plus numerical tests. Section 7 measurements state, for each finding, the quantity, method, pre-registered expectation, and PASS/FAIL/INFO status. Experimental setups: (E) a 12-model sweep of the ReLU toy model h ↦ ReLU(W^⊤Wh + b) with W ∈ R^{30×400} at densities 1/p_f ∈ {1,…,10}, AdamW, 10⁴ steps; (G) tied-weight sparse autoencoders (1024 atoms, R^100) trained on data built as normalized sums of 3 of 384 known ground-truth features, 3 seeds, read at 5000/10000 steps, with Chen-style group bias adaptation; (S_syn) synthetic dictionaries = 346 noisy feature copies + 20 bisector atoms + 658 random atoms, perturbed at 8 noise levels ϵ_nl ∈ {0,…,0.3} (one variant also drops/adds atoms).

## Results & Highlights

- **The threshold artifact quantified (Finding 7.2):** on the 12-model toy sweep, the norm-thresholded sum rule D_tot^(0.2)/d fell to 0.7516 at 1/p_f = 1.75 — a 24.8% shortfall against the pre-set 0.15 tolerance (FAIL) — while the unthresholded D_tot^(0)/d stayed ≥ 0.997 at every density (range [0.9974, 0.9998]).
- **The regularized sum rule is verdict-stable (Finding 7.3):** D̃_tot(W;λ)/d ≥ 0.90359 (minimum over 21 log-spaced λ ∈ [10⁻³ω(W), 10⁻¹ω(W)]) across all 12 models, so the verdict "≥ 0.85d" is constant on the whole ridge grid and matches the unthresholded verdict, whereas the thresholded verdict fails for 2 of the 12 models (0.7516 and 0.7638); outside the fixed range, at λ = 0.3ω and λ = ω, D̃_tot/d drops to ≈0.77 and ≈0.50 exactly as the defect identity predicts.
- **Constants are empirically tight, never violated (Finding 7.5):** over random pairs plus gradient-ascent counterexample searches in shapes from 1×1 to 30×400, the largest scale-free leverage ratio was 0.6495190507646841 versus the proved sharp constant 3√3/8 = 0.6495190528 — never exceeded — and the 1×1 search alone reached 0.6495189 (≥ 99% of the bound); for D̃_i, max(√λ·ratio − ρ/4) = 0.515005 < 3√3/8 over 701 filed pairs, with the excess attained at the scalar pair ρ = 0.5016 (Finding 7.6).
- **Certificates are sound but vacuous on trained dictionaries (Findings 7.9/7.10):** 0 violations among 864 synthetic checks and 0 wrong among 43,838 global-certificate firings (also 0 wrong among 24,112/78,500/29,772/78,500 local-certificate firings of the four kinds); but across seeds the line-Hausdorff distance H(A,A′) = 1.19–1.21 (near the maximum 2), all 384 features uncertified at every threshold, and the FRR bound (e.g., 156/150/107 at t = τ_align, 10000 steps) is loose versus actual changes of 31/12/43 features. On synthetic pairs the local certificates do gain: at ϵ_nl = 0.1 the global C11 certifies 0 features while the angular form (R∠) certifies 96.5 of 384 (141 at ϵ_nl = 0).
- **The headline instability (Findings 7.11/7.12):** at the standard threshold τ_align(V) = 0.927418, 55.47% of the 384 ground-truth features are recovered by some seed but not all three, while the threshold-free area c̄ differs by at most 0.0054 between seeds; per-seed-pair status differences are 151/134/141 features (35–39%) against net FRR changes of only 31/12/43. Within one run (5000→10000 steps), FRR at τ_align collapses from 0.66–0.69 to 0.22–0.33 across the three seeds (e.g., 0.6745→0.2500 for seed 0), with 189/179/204 status changes, and the median local atom motion is 21.1°–22.2° against median angular margins of 2.8°–4.5° — explaining geometrically why no certificate can fire.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** the novelty audit itself classifies the discontinuity result as "at risk" (Scherlis et al. informally remark that a vanishing column gives D_i ≈ 0) and credits Ivanov et al. (Thm 8) for the defect identity, Mirsky for the singular-value inequality, Tsuzuku et al. for the margin mechanism, and Chen et al. for both FRR and the uniqueness mechanism — so much of the machinery is assembled rather than new. The genuinely open items per the audit are the shape-free Lipschitz constants (stated with medium confidence given the large leverage-score literature) and the certificates; the paper is honest that its verdicts are "evidence about our search, not a proof of priority."
- **Rigor:** proofs are step-numbered and marker-labelled, with second independent proofs for the two central constants (Appendices B and C) and sharpness matched by explicit extremal families — genuinely strong by preprint standards. But eight statements keep the ◦ marker with unresolved reviewer points, including Theorem C.2 (the second proof of the upper half of the 1/4 coefficient) and Remark 6.10 (the counterexample against Chen et al.'s uniqueness sentence). More fundamentally, the entire verification chain is AI-drafted-and-AI-checked; the authors admit "no human mathematician other than the authors has verified the proofs," and the authors are not (per the paper) professional mathematicians. Theorem 5.8 also rests on Mirsky's inequality whose source the authors say "we have not read" (their open problem 3) — transparent, but unusual.
- **Reproducibility:** the experimental configs are specified to an unusual level of detail (seeds, generators, optimizers, group-bias adaptation intervals, read-out steps), a seed-2 run reproduces an earlier trace to tolerance, and the C11 counts agree with an independent implementation. However, at submission the code exists only as a promise ("will be released" via Zenodo), so the measurements are currently unverifiable by others — and every number is marked "not reproduced by anyone else."
- **Significance:** the positive theory (sharp constants, exact discontinuity set, sound certificates) is clean and reusable, but the paper's own experiments show the certificates' practical reach is currently nil for the case that matters — dictionaries from independent runs or even two checkpoints of one run — because H ≈ 1.2 (near its max of 2) and atoms move ~20° against ~3° margins. The actionable findings for practitioners are the negative/observational ones: threshold artifacts can manufacture a 24.8% sum-rule failure, and FRR at τ_align hides a per-feature turnover several times its net change.
- **Red flags:** (i) all experiments are toy-scale — a 30×400 ReLU toy model and a synthetic 100×384 ground truth with M=1024 atoms, three seeds, one model per density — and no real language model is tested, yet the framing ("AI safety claims") invites generalization the data does not support; (ii) the 55% seed-instability figure, while consistent with prior reports (Paulo & Belrose; Gerasimov et al.; Chen et al.'s own consistency section), is measured on one synthetic setup with 3 seeds; (iii) the toy sweep uses a single training seed per density, so the claim that the regularized verdict is constant over the ridge range is checked on 12 models but not across retrains of the same model.

## Strengths

- Mathematically honest throughout: sharp constants come with matching lower bounds, and each bound's sharpness is demonstrated by an explicit extremal construction rather than asserted.
- Identifies and repairs a real, practically relevant artifact — a τ = 0.2 norm threshold producing a 24.8% apparent violation of the superposition sum rule — with a regularized replacement whose verdict is stable across the entire pre-registered ridge range.
- The certification framework converts "is this feature recovered?" from a bare threshold crossing into a checkable margin condition (C11, C11-loc), which is the right conceptual move even though it is not yet informative on trained dictionaries.
- Exceptional epistemic hygiene: provenance markers on every statement, a novelty audit with per-result verdicts, pre-registered empirical expectations with PASS/FAIL, prominent reporting of negative results, and an explicit AI-use statement.
- The geometric explanation of certificate vacuity (atom motion 21–22° vs margins 3–4°) turns a failure into a quantified diagnosis that directly seeds the open problems (e.g., subspace-based certificates).

## Limitations

- Toy models and synthetic dictionaries only; no real language model, and the trained dictionaries are small (1024 atoms) with only three seeds; the toy-model sweep has one model per density and one training seed.
- The certificates are post-hoc: the local distances require the second dictionary A′, so they verify that a given change is safe rather than predicting robustness ahead of time.
- On trained dictionaries — across seeds or between checkpoints — the certificates certify no feature non-trivially (H ≈ 1.19–1.21, near the maximum 2; median atom motion ~20° vs margins of a few degrees), so the practical payoff for SAE evaluation is currently limited to small controlled perturbations.
- Eight statements retain unresolved reviewer points (marker ◦), and the additive constant for D̃_i is unproved on the range ρ ∈ (0.556, 3.326); the optimal coefficient of the local modulus of D_i is only bracketed in [1/2, 4].
- All proofs and measurements are AI-generated and AI-/self-verified; no independent human or peer review exists yet, and the code was not yet public at submission time.

## Relevance

（中文，1–2 句）对关注机制可解释性、叠加态/稀疏自编码器与 AI 安全评估的研究者，本文提供了"可解释性度量何时可信"的数学基础：它精确指出了特征维度与 FRR 这两个常用指标对阈值、权重扰动与训练种子的脆弱性，并给出带紧常数的稳定替代度量与可检验的恢复证书——任何用 FRR 或维度求和规则支撑"特征已恢复"论断的评估工作都应参考其结论。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Can the certificates be made predictive by bounding the local distances δ_i(A, A′; κ) from training dynamics alone (the paper's open problem 4), turning post-hoc verification into a priori robustness guarantees?
- Would subspace- or span-based certificates (rather than single-atom line distances) remain informative when atoms move ~20° across seeds — the regime where the current certificates are vacuous?
- Do the 55% seed-instability and the stable area c̄ replicate on real LLM-derived dictionaries (e.g., the Hungarian-matching setups of Paulo & Belrose), where n and d are orders of magnitude larger and features are not i.i.d. uniform on the sphere?
- Does the open range (ρ₁, ρ₂) of the additive constant for D̃_i succumb to the proposed computer-assisted interval-arithmetic check, and does the [1/2, 4] bracket on the local modulus of D_i collapse to a single value?
- Since FRR_t at a fixed t hides churn several times its net change, should SAE evaluation protocols replace single-threshold FRR with the Lipschitz area c̄ or smoothed FRR_{t,β} as the primary reported metric?
