Methodology

Conceptors are soft spectral filters, Cλ(Rc) = Rc(Rc+λI)⁻¹, representing a concept as a graded set of directions (gains gj = rj/(rj+λ)) rather than one steering vector. Filtering remaps h → c + C(h−c); additive steering (ActAdd-style) instead applies an identical displacement h + βδ to every input.

Cλ(Rpool) = C̄ + Dμ + JR
C̄ average pole filter Dμ rank-one mean separation (Fisher-style) JR covariance shape
Proposition 1 · exact for any shared λ · both updates PSD

Setup. Five instruction-tuned models with mean-token activations at fixed midpoint blocks (deliberately not cherry-picked); main comparisons at rank k=20 with k=1 as single-direction control. Fitting on authored BipolarConcepts (600 sentences: sentiment, politics, depression); held-out evaluation on ConceptGroups (leave-one-group-out) and ConceptVariants; six public probing benchmarks. Amplification scored by a frozen source-trained polarity readout at matched strength (β=0.10); generation judged blind by GPT-6-Luna over 596 inputs per model; uncertainty from 2,000-draw group bootstrap with full refits.

Strengths

  • Exact, provable identity cleanly separating pole means from pole covariances — rare theoretical clarity in the steering literature.
  • Consistency across five model families, including fully open OLMo-3-7B, three concept types, and a non-selected extraction site.
  • Careful comparisons: shared λ, matched sample counts, rank/trace/gain-matched controls, both judge presentation orders, topic-matched neutral controls.
  • Intellectual honesty: negative layer-selection results and the rank-one-vs-rank-20 counterpoint are reported up front.
  • Overlap analysis gives Boolean composition a directly usable interference account.

Limitations

  • Amplification is offline: frozen readouts, not end-to-end behavior; prompting and ICL dominate generation by ~25 pp.
  • Single LLM judge (GPT-6-Luna), 596 inputs per model, ties-as-non-wins; no human evaluation.
  • Small, partly authored fitting data — authorship-style leakage remains possible despite topic-matched controls.
  • Quota is not a practical rule: max-quota layer selection underperforms a fixed midpoint.
  • No comparison to other multi-direction baselines (INLP, LEACE); no behavioral test of Boolean composition.

Questions for further exploration

  1. Does the bipolar-fitting advantage survive when one pole is unsafe to elicit (refusal, dishonesty), forcing unipolar fits?
  2. Why do mean-based addition baselines reduce judged target expression — fluency damage, off-target drift, or judge conservatism?
  3. Can conceptor filtering combine with prompting/ICL to close the ~25 pp win-rate gap?
  4. Does subspace overlap causally predict the quality of Boolean AND/OR steering in generated behavior?
  5. What is the semantic content of the covariance-shape term JR — genuine feature variation or wording covariates?
