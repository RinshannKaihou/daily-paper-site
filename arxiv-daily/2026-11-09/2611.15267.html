OLMo 3 32B at the 8.4B-token checkpoint (injection layer 0, α=4, readout layer 24): an external linear probe nearly saturates while the model's zero-shot report is still at 1-in-9 chance — an 87.2-point gap.

Why This Matters

Any "introspection" benchmark result may ride on architecture-borne, linearly decodable traces — this probe-vs-answer control is a cheap, must-cite check for every self-report claim.

Distilled from Relevance / 相关性

Critical Edge

The headline 89.6% setting was selected after inspecting the sweep to maximize base-model reporting — and headline claims lean on α=4 injections, four times a typical token's residual norm.

From Critical Assessment — rigor caveats & strength-dependence

Methodology

  • Task. The Hahami et al. nine-sentence localization test: a setup text, nine numbered neutral sentences (from a 100-sentence pool), a question, and the answer prefix "SENTENCE␣" — run on OLMo 3, mostly the 32B model.
  • Intervention. A forward hook adds α·r_L·v̂ to every token of one target sentence at layer L, where r_L is that layer's median residual norm (α=1 ≈ one typical token) and v̂ is a concept vector or random Gaussian direction.
  • Measure A — probe. Nine-way logistic regression (L2, C=0.5) on the residual stream of the last prompt token, read at layer 24; LM weights frozen; passage-grouped 5-fold cross-validation.
  • Measure B — answer. The model's own zero-shot pick among labels 1–9 from the logits — no labeled examples in context.
  • Sweep. OLMo 3's fully public checkpoint ladder (step 0 → 8.4B → 50B → 500B → 2T → 4T → stage-1 end → released base) at a fixed setting chosen from a layer/strength sweep (layers {0,6,16,32,48}, α ∈ {0.25…4}).
  • No-prompt control. Freshly Gaussian-initialized, never-trained networks receive injections with no setup text or question; probes read marker tokens 0–256 positions after the list, on disjoint train/test sentence pools.
  • Rigor. Shuffled-label and uninjected controls, permutation tests with Holm correction, and an attention-blocking check confirming the perturbation propagates via attention.

  • Random, never-trained networks (no prompt, strength 8): probe reads 31.5% immediately after the list and 21.5% after 256 neutral tokens (controls 11.5% / 10.9%) — recoverable traces exist with no pretraining, instructions, or question.
  • Concept beats random injections: probe 86.1% vs 80.2%; answers 42.8% vs 32.6%.
  • Transfer collapses: frozen probes drop to 20.6–28.9% on fresh numbered text and to chance (11.1–12.2%) when numbering is removed — within-distribution 98–99% figures overstate generalization.
  • Honest negative: the pretrained no-prompt control is inexplicably elevated (14.3% vs 11.1% chance), so the authors decline conclusions about pretraining's effect on propagation.

Calibration: Hahami et al.'s 88% Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct result (coefficient 5, layer 2) maps to α≈2.03 relative residual norm — inside the regime where linear decoding is already strong.

Strengths

  • Separates information availability from behavioral reporting on identical trials — exactly the right decomposition for evaluating introspection evidence.
  • Rare longitudinal design across OLMo 3's fully public checkpoint ladder, at two scales (7B / 32B).
  • The random-network no-prompt experiment is a decisive control — recoverable location traces without pretraining survive 256 intervening tokens.
  • The layer-0 vs layer-6 contrast (89.6% → 37.7% in the same model) is a concrete, citable warning that introspection benchmarks are configuration-sensitive.
  • Strength calibrated to each layer's median residual norm enables principled cross-paper comparison; limitations disclosed with unusual honesty.

Limitations

  • No causal test that the model's answer actually uses the probe-decoded features.
  • Headline setting chosen post hoc to maximize base-model reporting; seeds reused for selection, so 89.6% is optimistic.
  • α=4 (4× residual norm) dominates headline claims; at α=1 the pretraining-decodability story is far weaker.
  • Tiny synthetic stimulus universe (100 sentences, 19 concepts); sentences recur across CV folds; frozen probes fail to transfer to new or unnumbered text.
  • Pretrained no-prompt arm compromised by the elevated clean baseline; single model family; forced-choice scoring rather than free generation.

Questions for Further Exploration

  1. Does the model's answer causally depend on the decodable location signal? Attention-knockout or causal-tracing could test the open probe–answer link.
  2. Do results hold under functionally matched (behavioral-output-change-matched) rather than norm-matched injection strengths?
  3. Does post-training (instruction tuning, preference optimization) shift the probe–answer gap differently from continued pretraining?
  4. Do step-0 / random-init decodable traces replicate across Llama, Qwen, Mistral — and with OLMo's actual step-0 checkpoint instead of custom Gaussian init?
  5. What explains the elevated pretrained uninjected control (14.3% vs 11.1%) — probe artifact, tokenization quirk, or genuine positional prior?
  6. Can probes trained on truly disjoint sentence/concept pools still reach ~99%, or is the within-distribution ceiling partly memorization?
