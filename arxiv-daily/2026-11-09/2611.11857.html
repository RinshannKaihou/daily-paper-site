OverlapAdvisor: Modeling and Adapting Computation–Communication Overlap for Distributed MoE Serving ↗

Chutong Ding · Kaixuan Zhang · Luping Wang · Shiyou Qian · Jian Cao, et al. (8 authors)

MoE serving expert parallelism comp–comm overlap performance modeling LLM inference systems
Key metric
0.0%

the best win share any fixed strategy achieves across 20,600 kernel configurations on 4 GPU platforms — no single backend dominates, so selection must be configuration-aware.

Why this matters

Directly answers which All-to-All overlap backend to deploy in production MoE serving; the hybrid analytical-plus-fitted modeling generalizes to adaptive selection of other parallelism strategies.

⚠ Critical edge

Headline speedups come largely from badly mismatched configurations — average end-to-end gain over the strongest baseline thins to 1.78%, all on a single NVLink node.

Methodology — how it works

  • Unified Performance Predictor (UPP). Every strategy abstracted to two overlap stages with communication granularity Gcomm and computation granularity Gcomp, sharing one closed-form overlap equation:
T = max(T_comp, T_comm) + min(T_comp, T_comm) / min(G_comp, G_comm)
  • Deviations from peak bandwidth / GEMM efficiency captured by capped power-law curves fitted per strategy–platform via differential evolution (17–23 physically interpretable parameters, fitted on only 20% of samples, ~13 min on CPU).
  • Configuration-aware Strategy Adapter (CSA). At server launch, minimizes predicted MoE time per phase (prefill vs decode), closed-form, ~6 µs — no online profiling; supports PD-disaggregated and colocated deployments.
  • Strategy Serving Launcher (SSL). Six strategies become interchangeable MoE backends in SGLang / vLLM via existing launch flags, with small additive patches (~0.7k / ~0.4k lines).
  • Evaluation: 103 production MoE shapes (DeepSeek-V3, Qwen3, GLM, Kimi-K2, Mixtral, gpt-oss, Phi-3), 10 token counts (32 → 16K), EP 2–8, A100/H20/H800/GB200, BF16 + FP8; correctness verified against serial baselines before timing.

  • Kernel level: 1.16× average / 1.59× max speedup over each platform's strongest fixed strategy; up to 5.62× over mismatched FlashMoE.
  • Prediction: 3.3%–15.9% MAPE (median 9.9%); top-1 selection accuracy 88.43% on average across five seeds.
  • Extrapolation: 30.97% MAPE vs XGBoost 53.85% and MLP 49.90% on unseen token regimes.
  • Model-guided fixes: +1.10× median for DeepEP-v1 (finer dispatch), +1.22× median for FlashMoE (coarser combine).
  • Overhead: ~6 µs launch-time selection; fitted models for 24 models occupy ~26 KB.

Strengths

  • Turns an ad-hoc ops decision into a cheap closed-form one — ~6 µs, 26 KB, 88.43% top-1 accuracy.
  • Largest unified EP-overlap comparison to date: 103 real production shapes × 10 token counts × 4 platforms, two precision paths.
  • Hybrid model is sample-efficient and extrapolates far better than pure learned predictors.
  • Non-intrusive engine integration with auditable line counts; supports PD-disaggregated and colocated serving.
  • The model doubles as a diagnostic, concretely improving third-party kernels — not just ranking them.

Limitations

  • Single-node NVLink only; multi-node RDMA / Ethernet EP — where overlap matters more — is future work.
  • Synthetic static routing (ρ = 2.0); real router skew and intra-rank imbalance not modeled.
  • Per-strategy, per-platform refitting (~13 min CPU); no cross-platform parameter transfer yet.
  • Modest average E2E gain over the strongest baseline (1.78%); big wins come from weaker or mismatched ones.
  • Top-1 accuracy drops in near-tie regimes; no code release mentioned, and reproducing needs all six baseline kernels.

Questions for further exploration

  • Does the 80.6% no-dominance finding hold under real, dynamic router distributions (skewed hot experts) instead of fixed ρ = 2.0?
  • Can fitted efficiency parameters transfer across GPU generations or data-movement mechanisms (CE / TMA / LD-ST), eliminating refitting?
  • How do conclusions change for multi-node EP over RDMA or Ethernet?
  • Would runtime re-selection on batch-size or SLO drift beat the fixed-at-launch choice?
  • Can granularity diagnosis jointly tune tile sizes and SM partitioning, beyond scaling λ?
  • Does the abstraction extend to attention-EP, sequence parallelism, or training-time gradient AllReduce overlap?
