Self-Evolving Agents★ Must Read · area rank 1 of 12

Experience-Handoff Reinforcement Learning for Recursive Self-Improvement

RL with verifiable rewards hands experience to future attempts only as a scalar; inference-time harnesses hand off rich playbooks but never learn to write them. ACE-RL makes writing the playbook itself the trainable behavior.

+0.089 … +0.120 RSI@3 over GRPO pass@3 at equal update FLOPs across 4 benchmarks (Mastermind / MBPP / AppWorld / MiniSWEBench); MBPP 0.987 vs 0.820.

By Doyoung Kim, Byeongguk Jeon, Kailin Zhang & Sherry Yang

一句话总结首次把“写下要交接给下次尝试的经验”本身变成可训练行为——候选 playbook 交给全新 actor rollout、由任务验证器打分；算力对齐对比横跨 4 个基准，实验闭环是该方向最强。

TL;DRHandoff-writing becomes a trainable act via verifier-scored fresh rollouts, at GRPO's exact sampling cost — the strongest experimental closure in its area.

Caveat / 注意Single training run per method; evidence limited to 0.6B/4B models; the MBPP environment leaks its scored assertions, favoring any handoff mechanism.
