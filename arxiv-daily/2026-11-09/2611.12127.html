Key Finding

01Methodology

  • Controlled paired comparison on SGLang 0.5.21, 8× RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell. Only the recurrent-state dtype changes between arms (FP32 / bf16 RTN / bf16 + SR); weights and activations stay bf16.
  • Six open hybrids: Nemotron-Nano-9B/12B-v2 (Mamba-2), Nemotron-3-Nano-30B-A3B (Mamba-2 MoE), Qwen3.5-9B & Qwen3-Next-80B-A3B (Gated DeltaNet), Falcon-H1R-7B (parallel attention + Mamba).
  • 590 competition-math problems (MATH-500 + AIME 2024/25 + HMMT Feb 2025) under a 32K output cap, plus LiveCodeBench v6 (175 × 2) for two models; greedy (T=0) arms rule out sampling artifacts.
  • Statistics: Wilcoxon on paired log length ratios; exact McNemar on accuracy; intervention effect Flog with 4,000-resample bootstrap CIs.
  • SR kernels: 16 random bits inserted below the bf16 mantissa before truncation (same storage as RTN); fast variant uses a lowbias32 integer-hash RNG, fresh 32-bit seed per decode step.

Hygiene: pre-registered pass/fail criteria (failures reported), concurrency-matched controls, FP32 seed replication, bitwise-identity check of kernels with SR off. Kernels described but not stated to be publicly released.

SR restores FP32-level behavior in 4 of 5 affected models, at identical bf16 storage — pinning the cause on rounding bias, not resolution.

Zero-cost, decoded

  • 10,393 vs 10,163 tokens/s — hash-RNG SR is as fast as RTN end-to-end (default Philox SR: only 30–60% decode speed).
  • 128K rerun (n=268): accuracy gap halves (93.6% vs 86.6%) but never-finishing runs stay 2× more common (66 vs 17) — 85% are exact repetition loops; median length 41,949 vs 19,951 tokens.
  • Head localization: SR on one intermediate-decay band (25% of heads) removes nearly the whole effect (Flog=1.03); the octile with the largest teacher-forced KL share (49%) removes nothing — KL misranks head groups.

Context: a prior vLLM bug fix saw FP32 state cut cap hits 23% → 0.25% and lift requests/s 2.55×; Nemotron 3 Super reported up to 37% FP16 verbosity increase. Engine defaults still differ silently today.

03Strengths

  • Clean causal separation of rounding bias from format resolution via same-width SR — resolves conflicting prior reports (LeapQuant vs DAMP).
  • Practically free fix: integer-hash RNG turns a diagnostic into a deployable repair at bf16 memory footprint (2× the concurrency of FP32 state).
  • 128K rerun decomposes the loss into budget-limited verbosity vs non-termination, exposing how both-finished metrics hide the second failure.
  • Rare honesty: pre-registered criteria published with failures included; concrete negative results (KL misranking, non-transferring bands).
  • Actionable guidance: report state dtype, rounding mode, cap rate, and length with accuracy.

04Limitations

  • Single seed for most arms; one engine (SGLang 0.5.21), one GPU type; only 32 rerun items in the Nemotron-9B 128K analysis.
  • Narrow task coverage: competition math + one code benchmark — no long-context, agentic, or open-ended generation.
  • SR is decode-only — prefill state writes still use RTN; fast hash variant validated end-to-end on Qwen3.5-9B only.
  • No general allocation recipe: decay-band localization holds in one lineage; fails on Nemotron-3 and Qwen3.5-9B.
  • Unexplained anomalies: Falcon-H1R null and Nemotron-3's residual ×1.05 shift; toy integrator explains only the band's upper edge.

05Questions for Further Exploration

  1. Why is Falcon-H1R-7B (parallel attention + Mamba per layer) immune — parallel mixing, state size, or its decay-rate distribution?
  2. What causes Nemotron-3's residual ×1.05 bulk shift that survives full-head SR — MoE routing rather than state rounding?
  3. Do the lengthening and exact-loop non-termination appear in long-context and agentic workloads, where state drift has more steps to accumulate?
  4. Can hash-RNG SR extend to INT8/FP8 state caches, and does the bias-vs-resolution split hold at 8 bits?
  5. Is there a closed-loop, generation-based proxy cheaper than full benchmark reruns for validating per-head precision allocation?
  6. Do bf16 RTN repetition loops share a mechanistic signature with overthinking loops under weight quantization — a common numerical-origin failure mode?
