> **一句话总结：** 受哺乳动物皮层"局部睡眠"off-state现象启发，本文在 GSM8K 上对 Qwen3-8B 与 LLaMA-3.1-8B-Instruct 做配对实验，发现相同预算下"时空集中的注意力头突发性屏蔽"（burst dropout）比"弥散 i.i.d. dropout"造成的推理退化严重得多（Qwen 在 B=0.10 时准确率 78.8% vs 91.2%，Δ=−12.4 pp，p=5.5×10⁻⁶），且证据指向"单步高密度屏蔽"是主要伤害源，损坏的 KV 缓存会把贪婪解码永久锁死在错误轨迹中（"自回归吸引子陷阱"假说）。

> **TL;DR:** On 250 paired GSM8K derivations, the paper shows that spatiotemporally *concentrated* bursts of attention-head masking (upper half of layers, ~5.7% of vulnerable head-token volume at onset B=0.10) are far more damaging to chain-of-thought reasoning than budget-matched *diffuse i.i.d.* dropout: Qwen3-8B drops from 91.2% (i.i.d.) to 78.8% (burst), a paired Δ of −12.4 pp (exact McNemar p=5.5×10⁻⁶), while LLaMA-3.1-8B-Instruct drops from 84.0% to 76.8% (Δ=−7.2 pp, p=0.0198). An exploratory factorial dissociation attributes most of the harm to instantaneous single-step density (Qwen: −10.0 pp, p_holm=0.0425) rather than conclusively to multi-token continuity (−7.0 pp, n.s.); a duration ablation shows single-token spikes (Δt=1) are Qwen's worst case (71.7% vs 93.3% i.i.d., Δ=−21.7 pp, p_holm=0.0007). The work is a pre-registered, statistically careful single-author study on one benchmark and two 8B models, with refreshingly honest disclosure of its own deviations and null results.

## 核心贡献

- **"集中 vs. 弥散"对照范式**：首次系统性地在自回归 LLM 推理中对比时空集中的注意力头突发屏蔽（clustered burst）与预算匹配的弥散 i.i.d. dropout，证明伤害主要来自扰动在时间—空间上的**聚集方式**而非累计总量（受皮层局部睡眠 off-state 启发）。
- **可复现的配对实验与严格统计**：N=250 配对 GSM8K 推导，Qwen3-8B 在 B=0.10 时 burst 相对 i.i.d. 掉 12.4 pp（78.8% vs 91.2%，精确 McNemar p=5.5×10⁻⁶），LLaMA-3.1-8B-Instruct 掉 7.2 pp（76.8% vs 84.0%，p=0.0198）；使用 Holm 校正、置换检验与 10,000 次自助法 95% CI。
- **因子分解：单步密度 > 多步连续性（初步）**：N=100 四臂实验（Clean / i.i.d. / Spaced 隔步高密 α=0.40 / Burst 连续高密 α=0.40）中，Qwen 的单步密度效应 −10.0 pp（p_holm=0.0425）显著，多步连续性 −7.0 pp 不显著（p_holm=0.1892），即"瞬时高密度屏蔽"是主要伤害因子。
- **错误模式的双模型分离**：Qwen 的失败主要是"仍产出答案格式行但推导出错"（条件准确率 91.9%→80.5%，Δ=−11.4 pp），而 LLaMA 有 22.2% 的配对损失是**格式坍塌**（缺少 Answer: 行），条件化后差距从 −7.2 pp 收窄到 −5.0 pp。
- **"自回归吸引子陷阱"假说**：burst 步骤中损坏的残差流会写入 KV 缓存并永久污染后续所有解码步，贪婪解码（temperature=0）无法逃出错误吸引子盆地——明确声明这是解释性模型而非已证因果（未做 KV 缓存回滚手术）。
- **预注册与异常透明**：研究遵循数据采集前注册的协议，并逐条披露 8 项偏离（如 LLaMA B=0.10 的 p=0.0198 高于预注册 α=0.01、答案格式缺失率 9.6% 超过注册阈值 2% 等）。

## Key Contributions

- Establishes a **concentrated vs. diffuse** perturbation paradigm for autoregressive LLM reasoning: with equal cumulative head-masking budgets, spatiotemporally clustered bursts are far more destructive than i.i.d. dropout — an operational analogy to local cortical off-states during sleep deprivation.
- **Confirmatory paired evidence** on GSM8K (N=250, two 8B open-weight models, greedy decoding): at onset B=0.10, Qwen3-8B falls from 91.2% (diffuse i.i.d.) to 78.8% (burst) — paired Δ = −12.4 pp, exact McNemar p = 5.5×10⁻⁶, Holm-corrected p = 1.1×10⁻⁵; LLaMA-3.1-8B-Instruct falls from 84.0% to 76.8% (Δ = −7.2 pp, p = 0.0198).
- **Factorial dissociation of the mechanism** (N=100, B=0.10, four arms: Clean / i.i.d. / Spaced isolated-step α=0.40 / Burst consecutive-step α=0.40): instantaneous per-step density produces an acute drop on Qwen (Spaced vs. i.i.d. −10.0 pp, p_holm = 0.0425), while multi-token continuity is only an inconclusive compounding trend (Burst vs. Spaced −7.0 pp, p_holm = 0.1892, CI includes zero).
- **Error taxonomy across architectures**: Qwen's deficit is genuine derivation error inside formatted responses (conditional accuracy 91.9%→80.5%); LLaMA's deficit partly reflects formatting derailment (22.2% of discordant losses lack an answer line; the conditional gap narrows from −7.2 pp to −5.0 pp).
- Proposes the **Autoregressive Attractor Trap hypothesis**: corrupted residual-stream states during a burst write contaminated K/V vectors permanently into the cache, so even after attention capacity recovers, greedy decoding stays trapped in an erroneous basin — explicitly framed as interpretive, not proven causally.
- Unusual transparency for a single-author paper: pre-registered protocol with eight disclosed deviations, including that the LLaMA B=0.10 result (p=0.0198) misses its pre-registered α=0.01 threshold and that schedule-draw variance is large.

## Methodology

- **Models & benchmark**: Qwen3-8B (36 layers, 32 query heads, GQA with 8 KV heads) and LLaMA-3.1-8B-Instruct (32 layers), evaluated zero-shot on GSM8K with a standardized prompt ("... end with a final line of the form 'Answer: <number>'"), greedy decoding (temperature = 0, max_new_tokens = 512, `enable_thinking=False` for Qwen), bfloat16 on a single RTX 4090, PyTorch 2.8.0.
- **Intervention operator**: attention-head outputs are zeroed via forward hooks on the output projection W_O, restricted to the *upper half* of layers (Qwen: layers 18–35, 576 vulnerable heads; LLaMA: layers 16–31, 512 heads) during generation. Because head silencing perturbs the residual stream, K/V projections at all later layers of the same step are computed from corrupted states and persist irrevocably in the KV cache.
- **Schedules**: burst onset follows P(start | not refractory) = min(1, 2B/Δt) with a refractory period and burst duration Δt; the diffuse control is generated via **stratified quintile matching** across 5 temporal bins so the marginal temporal distribution of dropout events is matched between arms. B = 0.10 schedules ≈5.7% of the vulnerable head-token volume.
- **Design**: confirmatory paired evaluation (N=250 items, doses B ∈ {0.05, 0.10}), an exploratory four-arm factorial dissociation (N=100, B=0.10, density α=0.40), high-dose sweeps (N=60, B ∈ {0.15, 0.20}), and a duration ablation (N=60, Δt ∈ {1,2,5} at B=0.10). Items are paired across arms with identical per-problem seeds (seed = 42 + item_idx×1000).
- **Statistics**: exact McNemar tests on paired discordant outcomes with Holm-Bonferroni correction, two-sided permutation tests, and 10,000-resample bootstrap for 95% CIs. Derivations terminate early on EOS (mean ≈221–273 tokens), so realized budgets are 43–53% of the scheduled T_max=512 budget; realized Burst/i.i.d. budgets match closely in aggregate (e.g., Qwen B=0.10: 8,897.3 vs. 8,631.3 head-tokens).
- **Scoring**: regex for "Answer:"-style declarations, then \boxed{}, with fallback to the last numeric value; exact match within 10⁻⁶ tolerance against the GSM8K test reference.

## Results & Highlights

- **Qwen3-8B confirmatory (GSM8K, N=250)**: clean accuracy 93.6% (234/250); diffuse i.i.d. dropout costs only −2.4 pp (91.2% at B=0.10), while concentrated burst dropout drops accuracy to 78.8% — a paired deficit of **Δ = −12.4 pp vs. the i.i.d. arm** (discordant pairs 39 vs. 8; exact McNemar p = 5.5×10⁻⁶; 95% bootstrap CI [−17.6, −7.2] pp). At B=0.05 the deficit is −4.4 pp (88.0% vs. 92.4%; CI excludes zero but p_holm = 0.0614).
- **LLaMA-3.1-8B-Instruct confirmatory (N=250)**: clean 87.2%; at B=0.10, burst 76.8% vs. i.i.d. 84.0% (**Δ = −7.2 pp**, p = 0.0198); at B=0.05, burst 81.2% vs. i.i.d. 89.2% (Δ = −8.0 pp, p_holm = 0.0033) — notably the diffuse arm *exceeds* clean baseline at B=0.05 (89.2% vs. 87.2%).
- **Factorial dissociation (N=100, B=0.10, Qwen)**: i.i.d. 94.0% → Spaced 84.0% (**step-intensity effect −10.0 pp**, p_holm = 0.0425, CI [−18.0, −3.0]) → Burst 77.0% (adjacency −7.0 pp, n.s. p_holm = 0.1892; total Burst−i.i.d. = **−17.0 pp**, p_holm = 4.6×10⁻⁵). On LLaMA the same design is underpowered: all contrasts n.s. (−2.0 pp total).
- **Duration ablation (N=60, B=0.10)**: Qwen's worst case is the *single-token spike* — Δt=1 burst 71.7% vs. i.i.d. 93.3% (**Δ = −21.7 pp**, p_holm = 0.0007), despite receiving the lowest scheduled dose (13.2k heads); LLaMA shows exactly 0.0 pp at Δt=1.
- **High-dose sweeps (N=60)**: Qwen's paired deficit is non-monotonic — peaks at B=0.15 (**−23.3 pp**, 66.7% vs. 90.0%, p_holm = 0.0026) then contracts to −15.0 pp at B=0.20 (73.3%); failed burst derivations run longer (at B=0.20, +1,678.1 excess realized head-tokens per failed item), indicating prolonged derailment rather than premature truncation.
- **Error-mode separation (B=0.10, N=250)**: Qwen conditional accuracy falls 91.9%→80.5% (Δ = −11.4 pp) with answer rate nearly intact (96.4%), i.e., genuine derivation errors; in LLaMA 22.2% of the 36 discordant losses lack an answer line, so conditioning on answer presence narrows the gap from −7.2 pp to −5.0 pp (80.1% vs. 85.1%).
- **Conditional dose asymmetry**: on items where burst failed at B=0.10, burst derivations realized +511.1 (Qwen) / +826.6 (LLaMA) more head-tokens than the paired i.i.d. arm, showing budget equality holds only in aggregate, not per-outcome.

## Authors & Credibility

Single author: Karol Struniawski, Institute of Information Technology, Warsaw University of Life Sciences – SGGW (Poland). The paper reads as a carefully executed, genuinely pre-registered study: it states the protocol was registered before data collection, enumerates eight deviations (model swap to instruct checkpoints, endpoint change from recovery latency to end-to-end accuracy, LLaMA's p=0.0198 exceeding the pre-registered α=0.01, format-omission rates above registered thresholds, replacement of KV-cache rollback surgery with the factorial design), and proactively reports its own inconclusive and non-replicating draws (e.g., the dissociation run's −17.0 pp vs. −6.0 pp under the confirmatory schedule on identical items). Code and manifests are promised "upon publication" (not yet available under double-blind review). Scale is modest — one benchmark (GSM8K), two 8B models, one RTX 4090, and sample sizes of 250/100/60 — but the statistical hygiene (exact McNemar + Holm, permutation tests, bootstrap CIs, honest treatment of schedule-draw variance) is well above the norm for robustness probes.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: The framing (concentrated vs. diffuse perturbation structure, borrowed from local-sleep neuroscience) is a genuinely useful lens; the specific finding that *equal-budget* noise harms far more when clustered is a crisp, non-obvious contribution. However, the biological analogy is decorative — no neuroscience claim is actually tested, and the core result is an instance of the known sensitivity of autoregressive decoding to compounding errors (cf. "Faith and Fate" compositionality work).
- **Rigor**: Statistics are unusually careful for this genre, but two cracks deserve attention. First, the headline LLaMA effect (Δ = −7.2 pp, p = 0.0198) *fails the paper's own pre-registered α = 0.01 threshold* — the authors disclose this, yet the abstract still quotes it as a significant finding. Second, every item is evaluated under a *single random schedule draw*, and the authors' own cross-check shows the same 100 items yield Burst−i.i.d. = −6.0 pp under the confirmatory schedule vs. −17.0 pp in the dissociation draw (and 25% of items flip under Burst between runs) — so per-condition point estimates carry large schedule-draw variance that the McNemar/bootstrap machinery (which treats the draw as fixed) does not capture.
- **Reproducibility**: Good on paper (exact seeds per item, prompt template, decoding config, hardware listed, protocol registered) but weakened in practice: the Hugging Face Transformers version is "≥ 4.51, unrecorded in run manifests", all run manifests record `git_dirty: true` (intervention hooks were being actively developed during the runs), full generation transcripts were not saved (error analysis rests on structured metadata), and code is not yet public.
- **Significance**: Relevant to fault tolerance, serving-time reliability, and KV-cache-corruption robustness; but external validity is thin — one grade-school math benchmark, zero-shot only, thinking mode disabled on Qwen, two models from one family size, and masking restricted to the upper half of layers with heads zeroed at W_O (a fairly blunt operator that also perturbs later K/V writes, which the authors acknowledge conflates "head silencing" with cache poisoning).
- **Red flags / specific weaknesses**: (1) The duration ablation is explicitly confounded — the refractory increment scaled with Δt+g_min, so scheduled dose rose from 13.2k to 18.3k heads across Δt ∈ {1,2,5}, undermining the "spikes are worst" reading as a clean duration effect. (2) The Spaced arm reused the *same* sampled head subset across interleaved steps; a fresh-resample control was never run, so "step intensity" may partly be "same heads repeatedly hit". (3) The GSM8K scorer's fallback to the *last numeric value* scored 11 LLaMA burst items correct without any answer line, mildly inflating burst-arm accuracy and blurring the derivation-vs-format decomposition. (4) N=60 high-dose sweeps are underpowered (LLaMA p_holm = 0.3591 everywhere), and Qwen's non-monotonic B=0.20 "recovery" cannot be distinguished from draw stochasticity — the authors admit this. (5) The Attractor Trap hypothesis is untested speculation: no KV-cache rollback, no per-step recovery-latency endpoint (which was the *original* pre-registered endpoint before being changed).

## Strengths

- Clean paired design with budget-matched controls and stratified quintile matching for temporal position — the comparison isolates *concentration structure* from cumulative dose about as well as one can with stochastic schedules.
- Exemplary statistical hygiene and disclosure: exact McNemar with Holm correction, bootstrap CIs, permutation tests, and frank reporting of null results, underpowered arms, threshold misses, and schedule-draw variance.
- The factorial dissociation (density vs. adjacency) is the right experiment to ask, and the error-mode analysis (derivation error in Qwen vs. formatting collapse in LLaMA) adds genuine mechanistic texture beyond accuracy deltas.
- Practical relevance: quantifies how serving-time transient faults (hardware errors, kernel glitches, quantization dropouts) concentrated in a few tokens can be disproportionately damaging relative to uniform noise — a concrete empirical benchmark for fault-tolerant inference.

## Limitations

- Single benchmark (GSM8K), single prompt template, zero-shot only, thinking disabled for Qwen — no code, no long-horizon reasoning, no other task domains; generalization to other model families/scales (only two 8B models) is untested.
- One random schedule realization per item per arm; the authors' own data show draw-to-draw contrast swings of −6.0 to −17.0 pp on identical items, so single-draw exploratory effects (e.g., the −10.0 pp density effect) should be treated as draw-specific.
- Masking is confined to the upper half of layers and implemented as W_O output zeroing, which both removes the head's contribution *and* corrupts downstream K/V states — the operator cannot separate these two channels.
- Duration ablation confounds duration with scheduled dose; the Spaced arm lacks a fresh-head-resample control; the Attractor Trap mechanism is proposed without surgical verification.
- Exploratory arms (N=60, N=100) are underpowered; LLaMA's dissociation results are entirely inconclusive (all p_holm = 1.0), and its confirmatory effect at B=0.10 misses the pre-registered significance threshold.
- Full generation transcripts were not persisted, limiting qualitative error analysis; code/manifests unavailable until publication.

## Relevance

本文直接命中"LLM 推理鲁棒性与容错"方向：它用严格的配对实验证明自回归推理链对**时空集中**的注意力头失效远比均匀噪声脆弱（Qwen3-8B 在 GSM8K 上同等预算下多损失 12.4 pp），对推理时故障注入、KV 缓存污染与容错推理系统的设计都有直接参考价值；其"单步高密度伤害最大"的发现也为推理服务时的瞬态硬件故障建模提供了量化基准。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the concentrated-burst deficit replicate on reasoning-heavy benchmarks beyond GSM8K (MATH, MBPP, long-horizon agentic tasks) and on non-8B or reasoning-tuned models (e.g., with thinking mode enabled on Qwen3)?
- Would multiple schedule draws per item (averaging over Bernoulli realizations) shrink the exploratory effects — is the −10.0 pp single-step density effect stable across draws or an artifact of one realization?
- Can surgical KV-cache rollback (recompute or restore cache entries written during a burst) empirically confirm the Autoregressive Attractor Trap hypothesis by "rescuing" trapped derivations?
- How do the results change if masking targets the lower half of layers, MLP layers, or uses fresh head resampling per step in the Spaced arm — is the vulnerability specific to upper-layer routing heads?
- Do mitigation strategies at decode time (e.g., self-consistency sampling, speculative rollback on entropy spikes, cache sanitization) neutralize burst-induced derailment at acceptable cost?
- Is Qwen's greater vulnerability explained by its longer chain-of-thought trajectories (larger "temporal target surface"), and does forcing concise derivations reduce burst sensitivity?
