Why this matters

Attention-head roles become readable from the weight file itself — zero-cost induction-head localization and targeted position-encoding interventions.

ρ 提供不依赖数据与前向传播的注意力头角色刻画，可低成本定位归纳头、指导上下文扩展等位置编码干预。

Critical edge

The YaRN showcase has no random-50% or norm-ranked control — nothing shows ρ specifically, rather than any selective gating, drives the gain.

01Methodology

  • Split each attention logit into a static content term G_QK(0) = W_QᵀW_K and a RoPE-induced positional perturbation P(Δ) = W_Qᵀ[R(Δ)−I]W_K.
  • Define ρ = average of ‖P(Δ)‖²_F / ‖G_QK(0)‖²_F over log-spaced offsets D = {2ⁿ} — log spacing probes every RoPE frequency band up to context length L.
  • Proposition 1: ρ = 0 iff the logits are exactly position-independent for any residual-stream inputs.
  • Symmetry argument: invariance of the full RoPE family G_QK(Δ) breaks the reparameterization group GL(dh) down to (C*)^{d_h/2}, so ρ is basis- and scale-invariant.
  • ρ measures capacity (what the geometry allows), not usage; it is sensitive to the softmax-irrelevant shift, with weight decay argued — heuristically — to mitigate.
  • Empirics: repeated-random-sequence induction scores on four bias-free RoPE models (135M–7B), correlated with ρ; YaRN applied only to the top-50% ρ heads on Mistral-7B at 4× scale.

03Strengths

  • Cheap, crisp theoretical object — one Frobenius-norm ratio per head over log-spaced offsets; basis-invariant, scale-invariant, interpretable as positional capacity.
  • Consistent signal across scales — Spearman −0.55…−0.74 (p < 10⁻⁴⁰) over 135M–7B models; top-10 induction heads confined to the bottom 7–30% of ρ.
  • Honest framing — YaRN labeled a preliminary proof of concept; capacity-vs-usage caveat and softmax-shift limitation discussed openly.
  • Generalizable program — per-head invariant signatures (G_QK eigenspectrum, G_VO singular values, commutators) with code released.

04Limitations

  • Extended-abstract depth — no per-layer or per-frequency analysis; the invariant-signature generalization is proposed but untested.
  • Softmax-shift sensitivity dismissed via an asserted weight-decay argument, never measured or bounded.
  • Moderate correlation (−0.55 on SmolLM2-1.7B); low ρ is necessary, not sufficient — ρ alone cannot identify the induction circuit.
  • YaRN evidence is thin — single model, within the native context window, no control ablation; absolute long-context perplexities far from usable.
  • Scope — bias-free RoPE, QK sector only; nothing on attention biases, ALiBi/NoPE, fine-tuning, or quantization.

05Questions for Further Exploration

  • Does ρ-ranked selection beat a random-50% or weight-norm-ranked-50% control at the same budget — and does the benefit persist beyond the native context window?
  • How does the ρ distribution correlate with other head types (duplicate-token, previous-token, name-mover, successor) and with the proposed G_QK / G_VO / commutator signature?
  • Can the softmax-shift sensitivity be removed by normalizing within the quotient geometry, and does weight decay empirically bound the error?
  • Is ρ stable after fine-tuning, quantization, or RoPE-base changes — and how does it behave for models with attention biases or non-RoPE schemes?
  • Can low-ρ be combined with a complementary invariant (e.g. a G_VO copy-score) to make head-role identification sufficient, not merely necessary?
