The direction that best reads a concept is not the direction that best changes it.

Do Probing and Steering Favor the Same Directions Within and Across Language Models? ↗

Afshin Sadeghi, Daniel Becker — Fraunhofer IAIS, AI Assurance & Assessments · preprint v1 · pre-registered hypotheses, failures disclosed · LLaMA-3.1-8B · Qwen3.5-9B · Gemma-3-12B

Key Metric
59/60

layer × model × concept combinations where the read-optimum and change-optimum systematically separate: probing peaks at λ≈τ (held-out AUROC 0.96–0.98, vs 0.60–0.89 at the Δμ end) while steering plateaus at 10τ–316τ, near Δμ.

Why This Matters

Answers the routine RepE/CAA assumption — reuse a probe direction as a steering vector? — and quantifies how differently probes and steering vectors transfer across models.

直接检验"探针方向能否直接当转向向量用"这一 RepE/CAA 常见假设，并量化两类方向跨模型转移的衰减差异。

Critical Edge

The headline sentiment gain (+27–56% over Δμ) largely evaporates under the matched-texts control — gone in LLaMA and Gemma, shrunken in Qwen — yet survives in the abstract's framing.

Methodology

  • Setup. Three instruct models from different families (LLaMA-3.1-8B, Qwen3.5-9B, Gemma-3-12B-it), raw prompts without chat template; residual-stream hidden state at the last token, layer ≈0.44 of depth (pre-validated by a 10-depth sweep).
  • One direction family. For each binary concept (truthfulness, sentiment, "unknown"): wλ = (ΣW+λI)⁻¹Δμ / ‖·‖ λ→0 is the Fisher discriminant (probe end); λ→∞ recovers Δμ — CAA's steering vector, up to scale. Sampled at 13 values of λ/τ from 10⁻³ to 10³.
  • Fixed side-effect budget. Each direction's steering strength is calibrated so the neutral-text disruption (mean KL on 200 WikiText-2 sentences) equals the same budget κ₀ — no direction wins merely by pushing harder. Probes scored by held-out AUROC; steering by answer-margin / log-odds change.
  • Cross-model transfer. Ridge maps fitted on 12,800 label-free neutral texts: steering displacements carried forward; probes, being linear functionals, carried by the transpose of the reverse map.
  • Application. 4,000 PopQA questions labeled from each model's own greedy + 5 sampled answers into an error taxonomy (C0 correct → C3 confident error); steer toward "unknown".

Results

Read vs change, within a model
  • Probing peaks at λ≈τ: AUROC 0.96–0.98 on TruthfulQA / Yelp; the Δμ end probes at only 0.60–0.89.
  • Separation robust across 6/6 main model–concept pairs, 59/60 layer-sweep combos, 6/6 matched-text controls, 4/4 rotation-operator cases.
  • On TruthfulQA the best probe (λ=τ) steers only 21–64% as strongly as Δμ at the same KL budget; no direction on the path beats Δμ there (best gain 9.6% < pre-registered 10%).

Strengths

  • The fixed KL side-effect budget is the cleanest answer yet to the old confound that a direction looks like a better steering vector simply because it perturbs the model more.
  • The closed-form family replaces binary endpoint comparisons with a full curve; the Haufe identity (Σh·wLDA ∝ Δμ) explains why read- and change-optima differ.
  • Dual transfer-law framework (forward map for displacements, transpose for probes) is a reusable template for porting interpretability artifacts between models.
  • Exceptional transparency: pre-registered pass/fail rules, failures reported plainly, a control that undermines one of its own headline numbers, candid limitations appendix.
  • PopQA application ties geometry to a concrete safety behavior — selective abstention — quantified per model, not averaged away.

Limitations

  • Scope: 3 concepts, 3 mid-size instruct models, one layer per model in the main analyses; base or frontier-scale models untested.
  • Single directions only — no subspaces, nonlinear probes, SAE features, or learned steering operators; conclusions may not carry over.
  • Steering measured by margin / log-odds shifts, not final task accuracy; equal KL on neutral text ≠ equal disruption of task behavior.
  • Cross-model pairs are non-independent, and the maps fit concept texts poorly (R² 0.13–0.47) — so part of the steering-transfer loss may be map artifact; 9/12 pairs formally inconclusive.
  • TruthfulQA both trains and evaluates the truthfulness direction, so distribution-matching gains cannot be excluded.

Questions for Further Exploration

  • Does the probe-vs-steering separation persist for refusal, toxicity, or fairness concepts — or at base and frontier scale?
  • Would steering transfer improve if the cross-model maps were fitted on concept-relevant text rather than neutral WikiText-2?
  • Does "best read ≠ best change" survive in multi-direction subspaces or SAE features — or shrink, or invert?
  • Would richer sampling (e.g., 20 samples) enlarge the knowledge-available class enough to stabilize the PopQA verdicts?
  • Can steering effectiveness be predicted a priori from a direction's Δμ component, avoiding the empirical sweep entirely?
