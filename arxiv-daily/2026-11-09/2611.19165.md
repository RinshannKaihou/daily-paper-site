> **一句话总结：** 提出一个在 ZCU104 FPGA-SoC 上实现的跨层（传感器→推理→微架构→加速器→功耗→通信）因果自愈 Edge-AI 框架：IC-TCF 引擎用带干预校准的时序因果图在 32 种扰动组合中定位根因（三种视觉工作负载平均精确配置准确率约 91.8%、macro-F1 约 0.950），再由风险约束恢复控制器在五种恢复动作中决策并做事后验证，相比 MAP 查表把约束违规率从 16.8% 降到 2.7%、假恢复从 8.7% 降到 1.1%，常驻监测开销仅约 3.1% 延迟 / 4.1% 能耗。

> **TL;DR:** A single-author 55-page systems paper that builds a full self-healing loop for Edge-AI nodes on an AMD ZCU104 (Zynq UltraScale+ MPSoC) prototype: synchronized cross-layer telemetry (sensor health, inference state, HPCs, AXI/accelerator, power/thermal, network) feeds an Intervention-Calibrated Temporal Causal Fusion (IC-TCF) model that classifies all 32 configurations of five disturbance families (sensor fault, data/model shift, cyber anomaly, hardware fault, resource/thermal stress), with conformal uncertainty gating a risk-constrained recovery controller (sensor failover / model-precision switch / DVFS / accelerator reset / edge-cloud migration) plus post-action verification. On the held-out episode test set IC-TCF reaches 92.7% exact-configuration accuracy on EfficientNet-B0 vs 85.9% for unconstrained temporal fusion and 69.8% for inference-state-only diagnosis (macro-F1 0.956 vs 0.918/0.831); the proposed controller attains 92.4% verified recovery success vs 78.6% for MAP cause-to-action lookup, while cutting constraint violations from 16.8% to 2.7% and false recovery from 8.7% to 1.1%, at a steady-state cost of +3.1% latency, +4.1% energy, and ~3,290 LUTs. Honest uncertainty handling (ECE 0.049→0.003 after calibration; selective-risk error 8.2%→3.2% at 80% autonomy) and unusually thorough ablations/generalization tests make this a strong reference architecture for dependable edge inference — but all baselines are internal variants, all faults are controlled injections, and the evaluated workload set is three CNN image models despite the abstract's "vision, audio, and time-series" claim.

## 核心贡献

- **同步跨层遥测表示**：首次把传感器健康、推理不确定性、处理器硬件性能计数器（HPC）、加速器/内存 AXI 活动、功耗/热状态与通信状况统一到一条带时间戳的遥测总线中，并以当前运行配置（模型、精度、工作点）为条件做归一化，而不是各层各自为政地监测。
- **干预校准的时序因果诊断（IC-TCF）**：把异常定位形式化为因果归因问题——掩码感知时序编码器 + 受物理架构约束的滞后有向图 + 图条件融合 + 显式枚举 2^5=32 种扰动配置的复合因果头 + conformal 校准的预测集；离线用受控干预锚定"扰动→观测域"支撑关系，部署时只消费被动遥测，干预标签绝不外泄。
- **风险约束的自愈控制器与事后验证**：恢复被视为带机会约束（延迟、能耗、推理质量、残余风险）的决策问题而非"故障→动作"查表；含 CVaR 尾部风险项与因果-动作相容性惩罚，并用"服务约束 ∧ 配置已提交 ∧ 动作特定物理效应"三重谓词验证恢复是否真正生效。
- **本地优先执行模型**：时间关键的诊断（中位 41.5 ms）与恢复不依赖云连接；云端仅承担慢速模型适配与知识聚合。
- **可复现的 FPGA-SoC 实验方法论**：在 ZCU104 上以物理相机、数据集回放、网络模拟器、PL 故障注入通道产生五类扰动与复合事件，评估覆盖根因识别、校准、恢复正确性、假恢复率、恢复时延分解、资源开销与未见条件泛化。

## Key Contributions

- **Synchronized cross-layer telemetry representation** spanning sensor health, inference uncertainty, processor hardware-performance counters, accelerator/memory (AXI) activity, power/thermal state, and communication conditions, all configuration-conditioned and timestamp-aligned on one telemetry bus, instead of independent per-layer monitors.
- **IC-TCF (Intervention-Calibrated Temporal Causal Fusion)**, a diagnosis engine that combines mask/age-aware temporal encoders, a directed lag-aware graph whose admissible edges are restricted by the physical architecture and anchored by controlled interventions performed offline, and an explicit compound-cause head over all 32 configurations of five disturbance families (sensor degradation c_S, data/model shift c_D, cyber anomaly c_C, hardware fault c_H, resource/thermal stress c_R), followed by cardinality-stratified conformal calibration. Deployment inference never sees intervention identity, severity, or ground-truth labels.
- **A risk-constrained self-healing controller** that treats recovery as constrained decision-making over five actions — sensor failover, model/numerical-precision adaptation, DVFS/resource adjustment, accelerator reset or partial reconfiguration, and selective edge–cloud migration — with chance constraints on latency, energy, inference quality, and residual risk, CVaR tail-risk protection, cause–action compatibility, and uncertainty-dependent safety tightening, plus abstention/escalation when no admissible action exists.
- **Post-action verification**: an action counts as recovery only if service constraints hold, the commanded configuration was committed, and an action-specific physical predicate confirms the intended effect (e.g., new acquisition route confirmed, accelerator back in service). This converts "command completed" into "verified service restoration".
- **A local-first execution model** and a reproducible ZCU104-based experimental methodology (physical camera for sensor faults, dataset replay for distribution shift, network emulator for cyber/migration experiments, PL fault-injection interface) that jointly evaluates diagnosis, calibration, recovery, latency decomposition, hardware overhead, and held-out generalization.

## Methodology

The framework closes the loop **observe broadly → attribute conservatively → act selectively → verify empirically**:

1. **Observation plane (Section IV).** On the ZCU104's XCZU7EV MPSoC, the APU (Cortex-A53, Linux) runs the application and inference runtime; the RPU (Cortex-R5F) hosts a trusted supervisory plane (timestamp alignment, telemetry-frame assembly, recovery transactions, post-action verification); the PL hosts the AI accelerator, AXI datapath, telemetry taps, and recovery-control hooks. Six telemetry domains plus the active configuration k_t form a synchronized observation state; a sensor-health sidecar independently characterizes signal quality/drift/dropout separately from the inference payload path.

2. **Diagnosis — IC-TCF (Section V).** A temporal window of W=24 synchronized frames is processed per domain by mask-aware temporal encoders (features normalized per configuration; missingness masks and staleness age encoded explicitly so absent readings are never treated as zeros). Encoded domains pass through a directed lag-aware graph whose edges are restricted by physical topology and whose disturbance-to-observation support was anchored by the offline intervention campaign; graph-conditioned temporal fusion feeds a compound-cause energy head that scores all 32 disturbance configurations jointly (not five independent classifiers). A held-out conformal-calibration stage converts the posterior into a prediction set Γ_t(α), with explicit ambiguity/unsupported states. The offline path (structural prior, intervention responses, ground truth) is isolated from deployment inference.

3. **Recovery (Section VI).** Given the diagnostic state, the controller builds a candidate action set, scores cause–action compatibility, predicts post-action distributions of latency/energy/quality/residual risk, tightens safety limits under diagnostic uncertainty, and minimizes a posterior risk objective (with CVaR tail term) over admissible actions. If none is admissible, it holds or escalates. Executed actions run transactionally through the RPU and are accepted only after the verification window satisfies χ_t = P_svc ∧ P_cfg ∧ P_a; failures trigger rollback, re-evaluation, or escalation.

4. **Evaluation protocol (Section VII).** Episodes (workload × disturbance configuration × severity × onset × configuration × repetition) with four temporal regions (nominal baseline → onset → diagnosis/recovery → verification). All 31 non-normal configurations of the 32-state space appear in held-out evaluation; the ten two-cause pairs get severity and simultaneous/staggered-onset sweeps; missing-telemetry and generalization campaigns (unseen severity, held-out fault locations, leave-one-workload-out, unseen compound configurations) are separate. External power measurement and oscilloscope paths validate energy and timing independently of internal telemetry.

## Results & Highlights

All numbers are from the held-out episode-level test partition of the ZCU104 disturbance campaign unless noted.

- **Root-cause diagnosis (Table VIII, EfficientNet-B0, 32-state space):** IC-TCF achieves **92.7% exact disturbance-configuration accuracy and macro-F1 0.956**, vs **85.9% / 0.918** for unconstrained temporal fusion, **80.7% / 0.889** for early cross-layer fusion, and **69.8% / 0.831** for inference-state-only diagnosis; Hamming loss drops from 0.082 (inference-only) to **0.019**, and NLL from 0.845 to **0.211**. Averaged over the three workloads (EfficientNet-B0, ResNet-18, ENet/Cityscapes): **~91.8% exact accuracy, macro-F1 ~0.950**, with only a 1.9-pp spread (92.7/91.9/90.8%) across workloads.
- **Per-cause detection (Table IX):** F1 > 0.94 for all five families — cyber anomaly best (F1 0.959, AUROC 0.992, AUPRC 0.984), resource/thermal stress hardest (F1 0.943, AUPRC 0.970); largest residual confusions are physically overlapping pairs c_H↔c_R (3.8%/4.1% misattribution) and c_S↔c_D (3.4%/3.1%).
- **Compound events:** two-cause exact-match accuracy **94.1%** vs **89.2%** for an independent five-binary-head baseline; for ≥3 simultaneous causes, **86.5% vs 75.7%** (weighted). IC-TCF loses only 20.6 pp from single-cause to five-cause conditions vs 42.4 pp for inference-state-only diagnosis.
- **Causal grounding:** learned disturbance-to-domain graph supports agree with independently measured controlled-intervention responses at **0.973 mean element-wise agreement** (e.g., sensor degradation: measured 0.96 vs learned 0.93 support in the sensor-health domain).
- **Uncertainty:** calibration cuts ECE **0.049 → 0.003** and Brier 0.091 → 0.069; conformal coverage tracks nominal targets (e.g., 95.8/95.4/95.2% empirical at the 95% operating point for normal/single-cause/compound episodes); selective diagnosis reduces exact-match error from **8.2% at full autonomous coverage to 3.2% at 80% coverage** (~61% relative reduction) and 0.9% at 50%.
- **Closed-loop recovery (Table X):** verified recovery success **92.4%** vs **85.1% (mean-cost optimization)** and **78.6% (MAP cause-to-action lookup)**; constraint-violation rate **2.7% vs 8.9% and 16.8%** (~84% relative reduction vs MAP); false recovery **1.1% vs 4.3% and 8.7%**; rollback 4.6% vs 8.1% / 13.2% — at the deliberate cost of a higher escalation rate (9.8%) and lower autonomous coverage (86.8% vs 100% for MAP). Removing post-action verification raises false recovery from **1.1% to 7.4%**.
- **Latency (Table XI):** median verified healing time per action — sensor failover **213 ms**, DVFS/resource 257 ms, model/precision adaptation 358 ms, accelerator reset/partial reconfiguration **765 ms**, edge–cloud migration **964 ms**; IC-TCF diagnosis itself takes only 41.5 ms (44–48 ms end-to-end) and the recovery decision 10.8 ms (9–13 ms), so healing time is dominated by actuation and verification, not inference.
- **Overhead (Table XII):** the always-on assurance plane costs **+3.1% inference latency (12.8→13.2 ms) and +4.1% energy per inference (64.0→66.6 mJ)**; PL cost is +3,290 LUTs (6.9% relative, ~1.4% of device), +4,840 FFs, +14 BRAMs, **zero additional DSP or UltraRAM**; telemetry bandwidth 286 kB/s; RPU utilization 3.2%→21.7%.
- **Robustness/generalization:** with one/two/three telemetry domains removed at test time, exact accuracy degrades gracefully to 89.6/85.7/78.9% vs 82.7/72.4/59.3% for a zero-fill baseline, while the unsupported-diagnosis rate rises to 41.3% at four domains (correct abstention instead of false confidence). Under the strongest shifts (leave-one-workload-out, unseen compound configurations), accuracy drops to 86.8%/85.9% but conformal coverage holds ~94.4–94.6% with larger prediction sets (|Γ| 1.71→2.42/2.61). Ablations attribute the gains: removing the architecture-constrained graph → 87.8%, intervention anchoring → 89.1%, the 32-state head (five independent classifiers) → 86.4%.

## Authors & Credibility

Single author: **Mostafa Darvishi** (S'06–M'18–SM'25), Electrical Engineering Department, École de technologie supérieure (ÉTS), Montreal, Canada — a Senior Member of IEEE with a track record in edge-AI and IoT systems. The paper is written in IEEE journal style (55 pages, 15 tables, 27 figures) with an unusually explicit claim-to-evidence traceability table (Table VII) and a pre-registered evaluation protocol ("disturbance generation, dataset partitioning, calibration, baseline selection, and evaluation metrics are fixed before examining the final held-out results"). The measurement chain is externally validated (independent power instrument, oscilloscope/logic analyzer for timing) rather than trusting on-chip telemetry alone. The self-declared limitations section (Section IX-F) is candid and specific. No code or dataset release is mentioned, which limits independent verification of the 91.8%/92.4% headline numbers.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** The individual ingredients (cross-layer telemetry, GNN-ish temporal fusion, conformal prediction, CVaR-constrained control) are not new separately; the contribution is the credible end-to-end integration — explicit 32-state compound-cause posterior + intervention-anchored graph + verified recovery — on real silicon. This is genuine systems novelty, but readers of the abstract's "causal" language should note the authors' own careful disclaimer: interventions anchor only the disturbance-to-observation edges; the learned telemetry-to-telemetry dependencies are architecture-constrained evidence propagation, **not** experimentally proven causality.
- **Baselines are all internal variants.** Every comparison is against ablation-style baselines the author built (inference-state-only, early fusion, unconstrained temporal fusion, independent binary heads, MAP lookup, mean-cost optimizer). There is no comparison against any external published fault-diagnosis or anomaly-detection method (e.g., pretrained anomaly detectors, prior cross-layer diagnosis work), so "92.7% vs 69.8%" quantifies the value of the author's own design choices rather than superiority over the state of the art.
- **Workload-diversity overclaim:** the abstract and architecture section promise evaluation "across vision, audio, and time-series Edge-AI workloads," but the concrete workload set in Section VII-B is three CNN image models (EfficientNet-B0, ResNet-18, ENet/Cityscapes). All quantitative results, including the leave-one-workload-out generalization test, live inside image classification/segmentation; the audio/time-series families are architecturally "considered" but never evaluated.
- **Controlled-injection validity:** all disturbances are injected via the author's own fault-emulation, dataset-replay, and network-emulation paths — necessary for ground truth, but it means severity spectra, fault locations, and traffic patterns are all by construction within the design space the author anticipated. The "cyber anomaly" family uses benign perturbations (traffic bursts, delay/loss) with no exploitation mechanism, so the "Secure" in the title is about separating anomalies from faults, not surviving actual attacks.
- **Trusted-base assumption:** the RPU supervisor, timestamp source, and recovery-control path are assumed incorruptible; faults are only injected into the monitored application/PL. The paper says so honestly, but it bounds the security claim.
- **Reproducibility:** no artifact/code release is mentioned, and the system requires a specific ZCU104 bench (camera, network emulator, fault-injection interface) to reproduce; the 55-page single-author paper is thorough on numbers but none are externally checkable yet.
- **Significance:** for the dependable-edge-AI community this is a strong reference architecture with best-practice uncertainty handling; the 32-state enumeration admittedly will not scale to dozens of cause families, a limit the authors themselves flag.

## Strengths

- Genuinely closed-loop: diagnosis, uncertainty-aware decision, actuation, and *post-action verification* are each separately instrumented and separately ablated — rare even in self-healing-systems literature.
- Uncertainty is operational, not decorative: calibration (ECE 0.003), conformal sets, selective-risk curves, and a controller that prefers escalation (9.8%) over unsupported autonomy, directly connecting abstention to safety.
- Physically interpretable residual errors: the largest confusions (c_S↔c_D, c_H↔c_R) match genuinely overlapping cross-layer symptom pairs, and the 0.973 intervention-vs-learned-graph agreement is a much stronger causal check than accuracy alone.
- Transparent, low overhead: +3.1% latency / +4.1% energy / no extra DSPs, with a full latency decomposition that honestly assigns most healing time to actuation and verification rather than to the diagnostic model.
- Exceptional evaluation discipline for a single paper: 15 tables, missing-telemetry robustness, five generalization regimes, controller ablations, and hyperparameter sensitivity (window W, graph lag p, conformal α).

## Limitations

- No external baselines; all comparisons are internal ablations of the proposed stack.
- Evaluated only on three CNN vision workloads despite the abstract's vision/audio/time-series claim; no audio or time-series results exist in the paper.
- All perturbations are controlled injections on one bench with one device family (Zynq UltraScale+); no field data, no real attack traffic, no other SoC/accelerator families.
- The explicit 2^5=32 enumeration does not scale to larger cause ontologies (authors propose hierarchical/factorized inference as future work).
- Trusted RPU supervisor, timing source, and recovery path assumed uncorrupted; edge–cloud migration assumes a trustworthy remote endpoint.
- Single author, no public code/data; headline numbers cannot yet be independently reproduced.

## Relevance

与"推理可靠性与 SDC 检测"方向高度相关：本文正是在真实 FPGA-SoC 上对加速器/内存故障、数据漂移与资源压力做跨层根因定位与自动恢复，其"干预校准因果图 + conformal 不确定性 + 事后验证"的方法论可直接迁移到大规模训练/推理集群中硬件故障与静默数据损坏的检测-缓解闭环设计；同时它对"检测不等于恢复、命令完成不等于服务恢复"的量化论证（假恢复 1.1% vs 7.4%）对任何自愈系统评估都有参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How would IC-TCF behave against external anomaly-detection baselines (e.g., pretrained sequence models or published cross-layer diagnosis pipelines) rather than only internal ablations?
- Can the 32-state enumeration be replaced by a factorized/hierarchical cause space (the authors' own future-work suggestion) without losing the calibration and conformal guarantees that make recovery safe?
- Does the intervention-anchoring result (0.973 agreement) survive when interventions are noisy or only partially controllable, as in production clusters where you cannot isolate one cause at a time?
- What happens when the trusted RPU supervisor or timestamp source itself is under fault or attack — does the framework detect its own assurance plane failing?
- How do the conclusions transfer to LLM inference workloads (autoregressive decoding, KV-cache memory pressure), where "inference quality" metrics and acceptable latency/energy envelopes differ sharply from CNN classification?
- Could the 4.1% always-on energy overhead be cut further by event-driven or adaptive-rate telemetry sampling, given that diagnosis latency (41.5 ms) is far from the bottleneck of healing time?
