> **一句话总结：** 本文对概念器（Conceptor，把概念表示为激活空间中一组带软增益方向的线性滤波器 C=R(R+λI)⁻¹）给出精确的三项池化分解——平均极滤波器 + 秩一均值分离更新 + 协方差形状更新——并在五个 LLM（Gemma-2-2B/9B、Qwen-2.5-3B、Llama-3.1-8B、OLMo-3-7B）上证明：双极（正反取值）拟合比单极（存在/缺失）拟合更能同时放大两极证据（30/30 vs 7/30 条件），rank-20 概念器对留出组对比的覆盖率远超单极拟合（如 Gemma-2-2B 情感 75.5% vs 52.3%），生成实验中 rank-one 概念器滤波的目标表达胜率 50.1% 而均值差加法仅 13.4%。

> **TL;DR:** A 47-page theory-plus-experiments study of conceptors — soft spectral filters C = Rc(Rc+λI)⁻¹ that represent a concept as a graded set of directions rather than one steering vector. It proves an exact decomposition of a pooled bipolar conceptor into an average pole filter plus a rank-one mean-separation update plus a covariance-shape update, and validates the geometry across five instruction-tuned LLMs (Gemma-2-2B/9B-IT, Qwen-2.5-3B, Llama-3.1-8B, OLMo-3-7B) on sentiment, political stance, and depression expression. On ConceptGroups held-out contrasts, pooled rank-20 coverage beats the positive-only fit in 15/15 conditions (e.g., 75.5% vs 52.3% on Gemma-2-2B sentiment) and the full filter beats every ablation in 270/270 comparisons; in offline amplification, bipolar rank-20 conceptors strengthen both pole margins in 30/30 conditions vs 7/30 for unipolar fits and 0/30 for fixed mean-difference addition (ActAdd-style baseline); in a 596-input generation eval judged by GPT-6-Luna, rank-one conceptor filtering wins 50.1% vs 13.4% for mean-difference addition — though prompting (74.4%) and 8-shot ICL (75.1%) still win far more often. Read it for the decomposition and the honest geometry; don't expect a new SOTA steering method.

## 核心贡献

- **精确的池化分解（Proposition 1）**：证明了中心化池化概念器可精确分解为三项之和——平均极滤波器 C̄ = pCλ(R₊) + qCλ(R₋)、秩一的均值分离更新 Dμ（沿 (R_w+λI)⁻¹δ 方向，类似 Fisher 判别的加权）、以及协方差形状更新 J_R = Cλ(R_w) − C̄（其秩等于 rank(R₊ − R₋)）；后两项均为半正定，且分解在任意共享 λ 下精确成立（附录 A.2 给出完整证明）。
- **双极 vs 单极拟合的系统区分**：明确区分"对立取值对比"（bipolar，如好评 vs 差评）与"存在/缺失对比"（unipolar，如好评 vs 中性陈述）两种拟合方式（呼应 Park et al. 2024 的 feature–contrast 区分），并通过放大实验证明：双极拟合的 rank-20 概念器能在全部 30 个校准条件下同时增强两极的冻结判别器证据，而单极拟合仅 7/30、固定均值差加法为 0/30。
- **多方向表示超越均值差直线的证据**：留出组对比覆盖（pooled rank-20 在 15/15 模型-概念对上居首）、留出状态能量保留（C₁→C₂₀ 平均提升 7.2–15.1 个百分点）均表明概念器保留了均值差方向之外的有用变差；同时诚实地给出反例——对极性预测，秩一均值分量 Dμ 的特征在 25/30 条件下反而优于 pooled rank-20。
- **谱诊断（quota）与概念间干扰的量化**：quota（平均增益 tr(C)/d）与六项公共基准（SST-2、Rotten Tomatoes、TweetEval、Hyperpartisan、Reddit Depression、Cross-cultural Reddit）逐层探针 ROC-AUC 在全部 30 个模型-基准组合中正相关（平均 r=0.789，超过 EVR₁₀ 的 0.503）；子空间重叠度分析显示情感与抑郁表达的几何重叠（0.221–0.332）远高于二者与政治立场的重叠（0.035–0.182），并由此精确推导出 NOT（补集）删除下的能量保留率，为布尔组合的干扰提供了几何解释。

## Key Contributions

- **An exact pooling decomposition (Proposition 1).** For a centered pooled fit over both poles, R_pool = pR₊ + qR₋ + pqδδᵀ (within-pole spread + mean separation), and the conceptor decomposes exactly as Cλ(R_pool) = C̄ (average pole filter) + D_μ (rank-one mean-separation update along (R_w+λI)⁻¹δ, a Fisher-style reweighted mean difference) + J_R (covariance-shape update, with rank equal to rank(R₊ − R₋)). Both updates are PSD; proofs in Appendix A.2.
- **A rigorous bipolar vs. unipolar distinction for fitting contrasts.** Bipolar fitting contrasts opposing values (μ₊ − μ₋); unipolar fitting contrasts presence with absence (μ₊ − μ₀). The choice changes activation statistics, geometry, and downstream amplification behavior — not just terminology.
- **Evidence that retained directions beyond the mean-difference line matter — with honest caveats.** Pooled rank-20 supports dominate single-pole fits and the mean line for held-out contrast coverage and held-state energy retention; but for pole prediction the rank-one mean component D_μ beats pooled rank-20 features in 25/30 conditions, and rank-one filtering is competitive in generation, i.e., "reconstructing activations may require more directions than influencing behavior."
- **Quota as a layerwise geometric diagnostic, and overlap as a composition-interference measure.** Quota (average conceptor gain) tracks probe ROC-AUC positively in all 30 model–benchmark pairs; cross-concept subspace overlap is consistent across all 168 model–layer settings and exactly determines retained squared length under hard (NOT-style) subspace removal, quantifying interference for Boolean composition.

## Methodology

**Conceptors.** A conceptor is the solution of min_M (1/n)Σ‖xᵢ − Mxᵢ‖² + λ‖M‖²_F over the fitting collection centered at reference c, giving Cλ(Rc) = Rc(Rc + λI)⁻¹ with spectral form Σⱼ gⱼuⱼuⱼᵀ, gⱼ = rⱼ/(rⱼ+λ) ∈ [0,1). Filtering maps h → c + Cλ,k(h − c) (project onto retained axes and shrink); this contrasts with additive steering h → h + βδ (Turner et al. 2023; Rimsky et al. 2024), which applies an identical displacement to every input. Quota q(C) = tr(C)/d averages gains.

**The decomposition (Section 4).** With pole covariances R₊, R₋ (each centered on its own mean), weights p+q=1, and δ = μ₊ − μ₋: R_pool = R_w + pqδδᵀ where R_w = pR₊ + qR₋. Proposition 1 splits Cλ(R_pool) into C̄ (average of separately fitted pole filters), D_μ (rank-one along (R_w+λI)⁻¹δ — the mean difference downweighted along high-within-spread principal directions), and J_R (nonlinear effect of fitting the average covariance vs. averaging filters; vanishes iff R₊ = R₋).

**Measurements.** Coverage_k(δ) = ‖U_kᵀδ‖²/‖δ‖² (fraction of a held-out mean difference captured by a rank-k basis); Fidelity F = 1 − E(M,δ) with E the filter reconstruction error; Overlap(A,B) = (1/k)‖U_AᵀU_B‖²_F; logistic-probe ROC-AUC for label separability; Pearson correlations across layers.

**Setup.** Five instruction-tuned models — Gemma-2-2B-IT, Gemma-2-9B-IT, Qwen-2.5-3B-Instruct, Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, OLMo-3-7B-Instruct — with mean-token activations at fixed midpoint blocks (13, 21, 18, 16, 16; explicitly "a fixed comparison site, not a selected optimum"). Main comparisons use k = 20 (k = 1 as single-direction control); the decomposition ablation and quota use the full spectrum with λ = tr(R_pool)/d. Data: BipolarConcepts (authored; 100 pairs per concept, 600 sentences: sentiment, political leaning, depression expression) for fitting geometry; ConceptVariants (authored; 312 sentences, 24 topics per concept) for reference-choice and amplification studies; ConceptGroups (1,400 sentiment reviews from three domains, 196 depression sentences across 14 facets, 994 favor/against stance tweets) with leave-one-group-out evaluation; six public benchmarks for probing. Amplification compares fixed addition T_add(h) = h + Du against conceptor amplification T_amp(h) = h + tC_k(h − c) at matched calibrated strength (β = 0.10), scored by a frozen source-trained polarity readout. A separate generation study fits operators to query-token activations, intervenes at every decoding step, and uses a blinded GPT-6-Luna judge comparing against the unsteered BASE response in both presentation orders (ties count as non-wins) on 596 held-out inputs per model. Uncertainty comes from 2,000-draw group-bootstrap refits that re-estimate means, covariances, axes, and gains.

## Results & Highlights

- **Pooling beats single-pole fitting (ConceptGroups, held-group rank-20 coverage).** Pooled fits exceed the positive-only fit in all 15 own-centered model×concept conditions — e.g., Gemma-2-2B sentiment: pooled 75.5% vs. 52.3% (positive-only) and 48.4% (negative-only) — and the negative-only fit in 14/15 (sole exception: OLMo political stance, 30.33% vs. 30.61%). On BipolarConcepts, pooled rank-20 support has the highest layer-average coverage in all 15 model–concept pairs, reaching 97.4–99.7% for sentiment.
- **Both decomposition updates help (270/270).** The full pooled filter C̄+D_μ+J_R outperforms every ablation in all 270 comparisons (2 extraction sites × 3 shrinkage scales × 45 model–concept conditions). Under a rank-, trace-, and gain-matched projector comparison, the pooled projector wins 251/270 with a mean coverage advantage of 10.64 percentage points. Example: for Gemma-2-2B political stance, adding the covariance-shape update cuts reconstruction error from 0.209 to 0.179, while the mean update only reaches 0.207.
- **Extra directions retain more held-state energy.** Moving from C₁ to C₂₀ raises retained held-state energy by +7.2 pp (sentiment), +15.1 pp (political stance), and +13.2 pp (depression), averaged over five models; on sentiment, C₂₀ retains 68.3%/68.4% of positive/negative pole energy vs. 35.7%/36.4% for the mean-line projector.
- **Quota tracks layerwise separability.** Quota correlates positively with probe ROC-AUC in all 30 model–benchmark pairs (mean Pearson r = 0.789 vs. 0.503 for EVR₁₀), and retains the strongest mean residual association (0.216) after adjusting for smooth depth and log-covariance-trace. Notably honest: the maximum-quota layer selector actually yields −1.712 pp mean AUC relative to a fixed midpoint layer, so the correlation does not yet give a practical selection rule (Appendix C.6).
- **Overlap quantifies composition interference.** Sentiment–depression overlap is 0.221–0.332 across the five models versus 0.035–0.182 for either concept paired with political stance (ordering consistent in all 168 model–layer settings); hard removal of political-leaning directions retains 81.8–96.5% of the other concepts' squared length, versus 66.8–77.9% for sentiment↔depression removal.
- **Bipolar fitting wins at two-sided amplification (ConceptVariants, β = 0.10).** Bipolar C₂₀ improves both pole margins in 30/30 conditions (mean standardized margins +0.0301/+0.0322), vs. 10/30 for bipolar C₁, 7/30 for unipolar C₂₀, and 0/30 for fixed mean-difference addition (which is antisymmetric by construction, +0.2942/−0.2942).
- **Generation validation (596 inputs per model, GPT-6-Luna judge, wins vs. unsteered BASE, ties = non-wins).** Rank-one conceptor filtering achieves a 50.1% mean win rate and the full filter 47.1%, versus 13.4% for fixed mean-difference addition, 14.4% for pooled-mean addition, and 11.8% for mean-line amplification; both conceptor variants beat all three mean-based controls in every model–concept–pole condition. Prompt-only (74.4%) and 8-shot ICL (75.1%) remain far ahead.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** The exact pooling decomposition (Proposition 1) is a genuinely new and clean analytical contribution — I am not aware of a comparable treatment of how pole pooling reshapes a conceptor, and the D_μ term's connection to Fisher discriminant weighting is elegant. The bipolar/unipolar distinction is largely an operationalization of Park et al. (2024)'s feature–contrast dichotomy applied to fitting practice rather than new theory. Much of Sections 6.3–6.5 is descriptive geometry (correlations, overlaps) rather than causal analysis — the paper says so itself ("Our focus is the geometry").
- **Rigor:** Above average for this literature. Ablations share λ, source examples, and even rank/trace/gains in the projector control; bootstrap intervals resample whole groups with refit means/covariances; a topic-matched neutral control rules out pure topic confounds (concept supports beat equal-count neutral controls in all 40 settings); the midpoint extraction site is fixed rather than cherry-picked.
- **Honest negatives are present but deserve emphasis:** the quota–AUC correlation is descriptive — after residualizing on depth and log trace the mean partial correlation drops to 0.216, and selecting the max-quota layer is *worse* (−1.712 pp AUC) than simply using the fixed midpoint layer. Quota is not yet a usable layer-selection rule.
- **The generation study undercuts more than it supports (for steering claims):** conceptor filters win ~47–50% of judged comparisons while plain prompting wins 74.4% and 8-shot ICL 75.1% — a ~25 pp gap the paper reports but does not deeply analyze. Also note the mean-based activation baselines (11.8–15.5%) fall *below* what a neutral intervention would plausibly achieve, suggesting they damage the text (fluency, off-target drift) in ways the single GPT-6-Luna judge registers only indirectly; one LLM judge with ties-count-as-non-wins is a fragile instrument, and the study is self-described as exploratory with only 596 inputs per model.
- **The headline "more directions matter" is really a reconstruction claim:** for pole prediction the rank-one mean component D_μ beats pooled rank-20 features in 25/30 conditions, and rank-one filtering slightly outperforms the full filter in generation (50.1% vs. 47.1%). The paper is commendably upfront ("Reconstructing activations may require more directions than influencing behavior"), but a skim of the abstract alone would overestimate the behavioral payoff of multi-direction conceptors.
- **Data quirks:** the fitting collections are small and partly authored (BipolarConcepts: 100 pairs per concept, 600 sentences total; ConceptVariants: 312 sentences), so fitted geometry may partly reflect authorship style; the topic-matched controls mitigate but do not eliminate this. Depression constructs use proxy labels with no clinical validity (stated). No behavioral evaluation of Boolean AND/OR composition is included — overlap implications for composition remain geometric inference.
- **Reproducibility:** protocols are documented in unusual detail, but at v1 the code/data release is a promise ("we will publicly release") rather than an existing artifact.

## Strengths

- An exact, provable identity (pooling decomposition) that cleanly separates the roles of pole means and pole covariances in the fitted filter — rare theoretical clarity in the steering literature.
- Consistency across five diverse model families, including the fully open OLMo-3-7B, three concept types of differing complexity, and a fixed (non-selected) extraction site.
- Methodologically careful comparisons: matched sample counts, shared λ, rank/trace/gain-matched projector controls, both presentation orders for the judge, group-bootstrap uncertainty, and topic-matched neutral controls.
- Intellectual honesty: negative layer-selection results for quota, the rank-one-vs-rank-20 counterpoint, and clear scoping of what amplification-on-frozen-readouts does and does not show.
- The overlap analysis gives Boolean composition (NOT/AND/OR) a quantitative interference account — 81.8–96.5% vs. 66.8–77.9% retained length — that practitioners can directly use to anticipate cross-concept damage when ablating directions.

## Limitations

- Amplification results are measured on edited activations with frozen readouts, not on end-to-end behavior; only the exploratory generation study touches actual outputs, where prompting and ICL dominate conceptor methods by ~25 percentage points.
- The judge-based evaluation relies on a single LLM (GPT-6-Luna), modest per-model input counts (596), and a ties-as-non-wins convention; no human evaluation.
- Fitting data are small and partly authored; absence of overlap with evaluation collections is verified by text, but style transfer from the same authors remains possible.
- Quota's practical utility (e.g., choosing probe layers) is not established — the max-quota rule underperforms a fixed midpoint — and the correlation is not causal.
- No comparison against other multi-direction removal/steering baselines (e.g., INLP-style iterative projection or LEACE), so conceptors' relative standing among subspace methods is untested.
- No behavioral experiments on Boolean composition, refusal-style concepts, or safety-relevant amplification; ethics discussion is thorough but deployment safety is explicitly out of scope.

## Relevance

如果你关注 LLM 内部表示几何与激活转向（activation steering / probing / 可解释性），这篇论文直接回答了"均值差方向 vs 软子空间滤波器"这一核心方法论问题：它给出概念器的精确几何分解、双极/单极拟合的实践指引（要同时放大两极就选双极拟合），以及 quota/重叠度这两个可直接用于探针选层与方向消融分析的免费诊断量。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the bipolar-fitting advantage persist for concepts where one pole is hard or unsafe to elicit explicitly (e.g., refusal, dishonesty), where unipolar fitting is the only practical option?
- Why exactly do mean-based addition baselines *reduce* judged target expression (11.8–15.5% win rates) — is it fluency degradation, off-target drift, or judge conservatism, and would a damage-aware judge change the ranking?
- Can conceptor filtering be combined with prompting/ICL to close the ~25 pp win-rate gap, or does the query-token-fitted filter already saturate what activation-level intervention can express?
- Does subspace overlap causally predict the quality of Boolean AND/OR steering in generated behavior, matching the geometric interference predictions (66.8–77.9% vs. 81.8–96.5% retained length)?
- What is the semantic content of the covariance-shape update J_R — genuine feature variation or topic/wording covariates — and can it be disentangled with matched-content controls beyond the topic-matched design used here?
