Why this matters

Lands 2–4-bit trellis quantization on real edge integer hardware — the offline-FP-search, online-integer-reconstruction recipe transfers to any INT-GEMM compression format.

直接命中「低比特压缩 × 算法-硬件协同设计」主线，面向带宽受限的 batch-one 自回归解码。

Critical edge

The 43.89 TPS is an analytical bandwidth ceiling that excludes KV-cache, activation, and control traffic — and the 2-bit mode degrades steeply on LLaMA-3 (WikiText2 6.14 → 8.73).

— Critical Assessment / Limitations

Methodology

  • FP-Encoded INT-Decoding. QTIP's Viterbi trellis search runs offline in floating point, but every candidate is first clipped and power-of-two shifted onto the INT8 [−127,127] or INT4 [−7,7] grid — so the online decoder reproduces integer weights exactly. One shared scale is folded into GEMM output scaling.
  • Activation-side Hadamard. The reverse transform moves from weights to activations (QuaRot-style), keeping the weight path fully integer while smoothing activation outliers.
  • H128 subspace transform. Splitting the hidden dimension into 128-element subspaces is the knee point: H16 collapses (ppl 2933), H64 loses 0.10 ppl, H128 saves 49.5% of butterfly ops at zero loss.
  • Subspace-aligned tiling. The 256-weight trellis tile is reshaped from 16×16 to 128×2 so it follows the subspace boundary.
  • Hardware. TSMC 16nm synthesis: 256 parallel decoding units share one hardwired 1MAD LCG structure (CSD shift-add, bit-truncation mod 232, fixed-wiring tail-biting — no codebook SRAM), co-sized with a 16×16 output-stationary PE array: TPS = min(bandwidth, decoder, PE).

  • Integer decoding is nearly free. S4 with INT8 reconstruction exactly matches FP16 reconstruction (ppl 5.16); S3 and S2 shift by only Δ0.01.
  • Decoder area efficiency. 307.2 Gweights/s in 0.0509 mm² at 53 mW — 13.93× Ecco and 1.52× Codeword Decomposition on normalized area efficiency.
  • Weight-only, 4-bit WikiText2: TQIR 5.16 vs QTIP 5.17, QuIP# 5.19, AQLM 5.21, GPTQ 5.49.
  • Placement is decisive: weight-side Hadamard catastrophically degrades S2W4A4 to ppl 2425; activation-side reaches 6.48. The 128×2 tile alignment improves S2 further, 6.196 → 6.134.

Strengths

  • Clean decoupling of storage / weight / activation precision into three named operating points on a single hardware datapath — practically deployable.
  • FP-Encoded INT-Decoding is provably exact and costs at most 0.01 ppl — a rare free lunch that removes the FP16 weight path entirely.
  • Every design choice (integer candidates, transform placement, subspace size, tile shape) carries its own controlled ablation with a mechanism.
  • Honest separation of measured (synthesis area/power) vs modeled (bandwidth ceilings), including 50/67/100% utilization accounting.
  • Cross-model generalization: LLaMA-2-7B plus LLaMA-3/3.1/3.2 family and an instruction-tuned checkpoint.

Limitations

  • All throughput numbers are analytical min(bandwidth, decoder, PE) ceilings — no chip, FPGA, or cycle-accurate validation of the 43.89 TPS or the SHT latency-hiding claim.
  • 2-bit mode degrades steeply on newer models (LLaMA-3-8B 6.14 → 8.73; 3.2-3B 7.82 → 11.09) and trails QTIP weight-only at 2 bits (6.01 vs 5.86).
  • Only LLaMA-family dense models ≤8B, perplexity at ≤4096 context, four zero-shot tasks — no MoE, long-context, or generation-quality evaluation.
  • Baseline comparisons lean on cross-technology area normalization; power is not normalized; Ecco's variable-rate entropy coding solves a different problem.
  • Symmetric activation quantization only; switching precision requires reloading a re-encoded trellis stream — configurable per-deployment, not dynamic.

Questions for further exploration

  1. How much of the 43.89 TPS survives KV-cache reads and activation traffic at realistic 2k–32k context — does S2 still beat S3 end-to-end?
  2. Can subspace-aligned trellises extend to mixed subspace sizes (H256 for outlier-heavy layers, H64 for clean ones) to reclaim S2 quality on LLaMA-3?
  3. Does FP-Encoded INT-Decoding transfer to other computed-code families (QTIP's 3INST) or additive/vector quantization like AQLM and VPTQ?
  4. What is the energy/area cost of the full accelerator (decoder + SHT + PE array) versus the decoder-only 0.0509 mm² figure, benchmarked against Ecco at the same node with normalized power?
  5. How does group-32 INT4 activation quantization interact with H128 for MoE models or stronger-outlier families (Mistral, Qwen)?
