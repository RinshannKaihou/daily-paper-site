> **一句话总结：** 本文通过因果消融实验证明，多模态训练会在语言骨干内部写入一个"视觉-语言（VL）方向"，纯文本推理时会因果性地调用它——在 Chameleon-7B 上删掉该方向使 BBH navigate 掉 27.6 个百分点而 boolean_expressions 反升 5.6 个百分点（33.2 pp 双重解离），且该效应跨 10 种多模态构型成立，在 BAGEL/Janus 等双路统一模型中定位于生成通路的紧凑一维载体。

> **TL;DR:** A NeurIPS 2026 mechanistic-interpretability study asking whether multimodal training reshapes how an LLM reasons on text-only inputs. The authors compute a vision–language (VL) DiffMean axis in 10 multimodal configurations from 6 model families (Chameleon, Emu3, Liquid, BAGEL, Janus-Pro, Qwen3-VL) and ablate its projection from residual-stream hidden states during text-only inference. In Chameleon-7B (BBH), this drops navigate by 27.6 pp while boolean_expressions improves 5.6 pp (DD = +33.2 pp); energy-matched random directions give DD = −1.9 ± 3.6 pp. Matched-backbone controls show the scaffold is introduced de novo in Chameleon (LLaMA-7B: −2.0 pp) but amplified from a pre-existing one in Qwen (Qwen2.5-7B: DD = +18.5 pp). Effects transfer to bAbI/SpartQA/StepGame and to mental rotation/paper folding/perspective taking (Qwen3-VL-32B mental rotation: 78.0% → 23.0%), while MMLU-Pro stays within ±5 pp. In dual-path models, only the generation path yields a compact causal 1D carrier (BAGEL DD = +16.8 pp vs understanding path −10.9 pp n.s.).

## 核心贡献

- **提出并因果验证"涌现视觉塑形思维"（EVT）**：纯文本推理会因果性地调用由视觉-语言训练引入或增强的表征。证据不是探测（probing）而是推理时干预——删除 VL 方向会选择性破坏空间意象任务，同时基本保留符号操作任务。
- **跨架构的双重解离**：10 种多模态构型（6 个模型家族）中 9 个的规范 VL 轴是因果的；因果枢纽层位置与各架构的视觉-语言接口吻合（Chameleon 早层 L5、Qwen3-VL L3–L8、双路生成模型晚层 L21–L28），排除了"泛化扰动"解释。
- **区分"引入"与"放大"两种来源**：匹配骨干对照显示 Chameleon 的空间脚手架是多模态训练新引入的（同一轴在 LLaMA-7B 上仅 −2.0 pp），而 Qwen 家族是放大并提纯了文本骨干中已有的脚手架（Qwen2.5-7B 上 DD = +18.5 pp）。
- **双路统一模型中的分工几何**：BAGEL/Janus-Pro 中因果载体浓缩在视觉**生成**通路的一维 DiffMean 方向；**理解**通路虽含可探测的空间信息（探针准确率 0.73），但任何一维或 rank-k 子空间消融都不具备空间选择性。
- **提出三个几何诊断量**——concentration、alignment、semantic location——系统解释"可解码 ≠ 被因果使用"，说明行为、探针与干预为何会分歧（如 Chameleon-30B 上 7 种模态轴方法全部无效，而任务对比轴与输入保真模态轴成功）。

## Key Contributions

- **Defines and causally validates Emergent Vision-Shaped Thinking (EVT)**: text-only reasoning that causally recruits representations introduced or strengthened by vision–language training — established by inference-time interventions, not by probing alone, and deliberately leaving open whether the internal format is image-like.
- **A cross-architecture double dissociation (Table 1)**: across 9 VL-axis-positive configurations from 6 families (shared-vocab VQ, ViT+projection, post-hoc visual fine-tuning, dual-path unified), ablating one VL direction selectively damages spatial-imagery tasks while sparing symbol-manipulation controls; causal hubs track each architecture's vision–language interface, arguing against a generic-perturbation account.
- **Attribution via matched-backbone and cross-family controls**: multimodal training can *introduce* a scaffold (Chameleon: same axis leaves LLaMA-7B at −2.0 pp on navigate) or *amplify and purify* a pre-existing one (Qwen: DD = +18.5 pp on text-only Qwen2.5-7B, cleaner DD = +30.0 pp in Qwen3-VL-8B itself); cross-family transfers are null, showing carriers are model-specific, not universal directions.
- **A path-level division of labor in unified models**: in BAGEL and Janus-Pro, the visual-generation path compresses spatial structure into a compact 1D causal carrier; the understanding path holds probe-decodable but entangled spatial information that no tested rank-k subspace can selectively ablate.
- **Three geometric diagnostics (concentration, alignment, semantic location)** that convert probe–intervention mismatches into evidence, explaining why a readable feature can be causally weak (BAGEL understanding path) and why a modality axis can fail where a task-contrastive axis succeeds (Chameleon-30B).

## Methodology

The pipeline has three parts. (1) **VL direction construction**: for each model, a DiffMean axis v = (µ_text − µ_visual)/‖·‖ is computed at the representation site where text and visual signals are directly comparable — the shared embedding table for Chameleon-7B (text-token vs visual-token row centroids), per-layer hidden states at text vs visual positions for Emu3 and Qwen3-VL, and *separate* per-path axes for dual-path BAGEL/Janus-Pro. All axes have split-half stability ≥ 0.9992, and swapping synthetic visual inputs for real COCO images reproduces every hub dissociation. (2) **Projection intervention**: during *text-only* inference, a hook on the post-block residual stream applies h ← h − α(hᵀv)v (α = 1 removes the projection; α < 0 amplifies it). Dosage α is frozen once per configuration under a pre-fixed 125/125 calibration/held-out split; six of ten configurations use α = 1.0. Higher-rank variants ablate a top-k PCA basis B of per-sample vision−text mean differences. (3) **Evaluation protocol**: spatial-imagery tasks (BBH navigate, tracking) are contrasted with symbol-manipulation controls (boolean_expressions, web_of_lies); selectivity is summarized as DD = Δ_control − Δ_spatial with 250 samples per task. EVT is identified by three causal checks — task selectivity, direction specificity against norm-/energy-matched random directions (z-statistic), and layer localization via block sweeps then single-layer scans — plus cross-task gradedness on a 0–5 spatial-imagery spectrum (annotated by four frontier LLMs, sanity-checked by two humans, Kendall's W ≈ 0.98) and bidirectional rescue/amplification controls. External transfer is tested on bAbI, SpartQA, StepGame, a text-formatted mental-simulation battery (mental rotation, paper folding, perspective taking, partly verbatim from the SPACE benchmark), and a visual×spatial 2×2 battery crossing visual content with spatial demand.

## Results & Highlights

- **Headline double dissociation (Chameleon-7B, BBH)**: single-layer L5 VL-axis ablation drops navigate accuracy by 27.6 pp while boolean_expressions *improves* 5.6 pp — a 33.2 pp double dissociation; 0 of 101 norm-matched random directions reproduce it (block z = +12.22). Energy-matched controls on the L0–7 block give DD = −1.9 ± 3.6 pp versus +15.2 pp for the VL axis (z = +4.8, one-sided p < 10⁻⁴), ruling out removed-energy magnitude as the explanation.
- **Cross-architecture replication (Table 1)**: Emu3 DD = +20.8 pp, Liquid-V1-7B DD = +21.6 pp, BAGEL generation-path DD = +16.8 pp (z = +3.19), Janus-Pro DD = +8.0 pp (z = +3.58); Qwen3-VL shows strong direction specificity at all scales (2B z = +27.7, 8B z = +38.3, 32B z = +262.5 with CI [+92, +118] pp — a pass/fail statistic, not an effect size).
- **Attribution — introduced vs amplified**: the same Chameleon axis leaves matched LLaMA-7B essentially unchanged (navigate −2.0 pp; all tasks within ±3.3 pp) but drops Chameleon-7B navigate by 27.2 pp (block DD = +32.0 pp); a Qwen2.5-VL axis yields DD = +18.5 pp on the text-only Qwen2.5-7B backbone, and the Qwen3-VL-8B axis transfers to the Qwen3-8B base at DD ≈ +34 pp but with a ~5 pp non-selective damage floor, versus a clean DD = +30.0 pp inside Qwen3-VL-8B — multimodal training amplifies *and purifies* a pre-existing scaffold.
- **External-benchmark transfer with knowledge/math spared**: Qwen3-VL-8B drops StepGame k=1 by 24.8 pp, Emu3 drops SpartQA-YN by 19.0 pp, BAGEL spatial subtasks fall 6.8–43.2 pp with generation-path damage 6–31× the understanding path (e.g., bAbI task 19: −12.4 pp vs −0.4 pp, 31×); meanwhile MMLU-Pro (14 subjects) changes ≤ 5 pp (mean |Δ| = 1.4 pp) for Chameleon and Janus, and Chameleon-7B GSM8K actually improves +4.0 pp.
- **Mental-simulation battery (frozen intervention, no retuning)**: Qwen3-VL-32B mental rotation (easy) falls 78.0% → 23.0% (−55.0 pp, CI [−66, −44], p = 1.4e−13), paper folding (easy) 89.0% → 50.0% (−39.0 pp); Qwen3-VL-8B paper folding falls 79.3% → 30.7% (−48.7 pp, p = 8.3e−17) and perspective taking (hard) 68.7% → 26.0% (−42.7 pp, p = 6.3e−15); five random directions leave perspective taking within |Δ| ≤ 4 pp.
- **Dual-path geometry (BAGEL)**: generation-path 1D DiffMean gives DD = +16.8 pp while the understanding-path axis is null/slightly reversed (−10.9 pp, n.s.); rank-k PCA ablation is non-selective in both paths (generation: controls drop 56–74 pp at every k; understanding k=16: controls −32.3 vs spatial −19.7 pp), so only generation exposes a compact selective carrier. Janus-Pro's independent 27-task BBH run shows navigate −28.7 pp vs boolean_expressions −7.3 pp (spatial-group mean −14.5 pp ≈ 3× the symbolic-group −4.6 pp).
- **Functional-use controls**: hub-specific L6 reinjection (β = 0.5σ) recovers +8.0 pp of the −27.6 pp navigate deficit while non-hub L20 ablation is null (+0.8 pp); additive steering fails as a positive control, so the carrier is a token-wise projection pattern, not a steering bias.
- **Scale-broadened functional range**: visual non-spatial attribute binding is preserved at 7–8B (Chameleon-7B +6.0 pp n.s.; Qwen3-VL-8B 100.0 → 96.0) but collapses at 32B (100.0 → 29.0, −71.0 pp, CI [−80, −62], p = 8.5e−22) while web_of_lies stays at 56.0 and boolean_expressions 77.0 — the carrier broadens from spatial to general visual attribute binding with scale.

## Authors & Credibility

Qi Zhang and Bohan Wang (equal contribution) and Hanwang Zhang are at Nanyang Technological University; Qianru Sun is at Singapore Management University — an established multimodal-reasoning group (Hanwang Zhang's lab). The paper is accepted at NeurIPS 2026, and the acknowledgments explicitly credit reviewer and area-chair suggestions for the mental-simulation battery, the visual×spatial control battery, and the within-task probe analyses, which are exactly the strongest sections of the current version — a good sign of a productive review cycle. Funding is from Singapore's NRF, DSO National Laboratories, and MOE Tier-1. Methodological hygiene is unusually careful: frozen dosages validated on a held-out 125/125 split, split-half axis stability ≥ 0.9992, synthetic-vs-COCO axis robustness checks, norm- *and* energy-matched random controls, McNemar tests with CIs, and transparency notes flagging their own confounds (answer-completion drops, near-floor baselines, answer-distribution shifts on binary tasks). The authors state they release analysis code and benchmark scripts only.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: High. Prior evidence that multimodal training affects text-only reasoning was behavioral or synthetic-scale; this is the first systematic *causal* (intervention-at-inference) demonstration across modern architectures, and the dual-path generation-vs-understanding carrier analysis plus the three geometric diagnostics are genuinely new contributions to the probing-vs-intervention literature.
- **Rigor**: Strong overall, with unusually candid failure reporting (Appendix G documents seven null modality-axis methods on Chameleon-30B before finding the input-faithful construction). However, several quantitative anchors deserve scrutiny. First, the headline navigate drop partly rides on answer-distribution shifts: Chameleon-7B's navigate baseline is 43.3% (below chance for the task's format), and the authors themselves note that web_of_lies "+24.7 pp improvement" is an answer-rate artifact — the same caution applies in reverse to navigate, which is why they lean on the completion-controlled batteries. Second, several mental-simulation cells are completion-confounded (32B mental rotation completion falls 77% → 40%), leaving perspective taking as the anchor evidence. Third, the 32B z = +262.5 reflects a near-degenerate random-null variance; the authors flag it as pass/fail only, but it is visually prominent in Figure 2/4b.
- **Reproducibility**: Good — standard open-weight models (Chameleon, Emu3, Liquid, BAGEL, Janus-Pro, Qwen3-VL at 2B–32B), 150–250 samples per task, frozen dosages with held-out validation, and analysis-code release promised. The main friction is compute for 32B activation-level hooks and the bespoke per-architecture axis constructions, which the paper documents in detail (Appendix A).
- **Significance**: Conceptually important — it reframes "does generation help understanding" in unified models as a question about *which geometry each path writes into the shared backbone*, and shows multimodal training changes what text-only inference routes through, not just what models can process.
- **Red flags / open claims**: (a) BAGEL's main-dosage generation-path intervention also damages GSM8K (−53.6 pp) and LogiQA (−25.2 pp), so the "knowledge/math preserved" claim is configuration-bounded, not universal — the authors disclose this honestly as a dosage boundary. (b) Qwen attribution (introduced vs amplified) is constrained by non-public Qwen3 pretraining-data composition; the cleaner anchor is text-only-documented Qwen2.5-7B. (c) The carrier is *spatially concentrated, not exclusively spatial* — 8–12-update non-spatial color tracking also drops 44–52 pp — so "vision-shaped" is partly a graded family resemblance rather than a clean dichotomy at long update horizons. (d) All interventions are linear/low-rank; absence of an effect (e.g., understanding path) could reflect a nonlinear carrier rather than absence of causal structure.

## Strengths

- Causal, intervention-based evidence rather than correlational probing, with three orthogonal causal checks (task selectivity, direction specificity, layer localization) plus gradedness and rescue controls.
- Breadth: 10 configurations across 6 architecture families, 2B–32B dense and MoE, with matched-backbone and cross-family transfer controls that separate "introduced" from "amplified" scaffolds.
- The dual-path analysis inside BAGEL/Janus-Pro is a clean natural experiment: same backbone, same hidden dimension, two visual injection routes — and only the generation path yields a compact selective 1D carrier.
- The concentration/alignment/semantic-location diagnostics give the field a reusable protocol for interpreting probe-vs-intervention mismatches (concentric vs projected-diffuse vs mixed regimes).
- Honest boundary reporting: failure-mode appendix (G), completion caveats, dosage cliffs (Liquid), and the Chameleon-30B null-then-rescued story are all in the paper, not hidden.

## Limitations

- Linear and low-rank interventions only; nonlinear, highly distributed, or cross-attention-specific carriers are invisible to the method (the understanding-path null is suggestive, not conclusive).
- Mental-simulation evidence exists only where a capability floor is cleared — Chameleon-7B is at or below chance on every mental-simulation task, so that evidence comes solely from Qwen3-VL configurations.
- The carrier is not exclusively spatial: sufficiently long non-spatial state tracking (8–12 sequential updates) partially overlaps it, and at 32B the effect broadens to non-spatial visual attribute binding, complicating a pure "spatial imagery" reading.
- Task battery is diagnostic, not a complete taxonomy (BBH, bAbI, SpartQA, StepGame, mental-simulation and visual×spatial batteries); several Chameleon-7B BBH baselines are near floor, limiting per-task interpretability.
- Qwen pretraining-data composition is not public, so introduced-vs-amplified attribution for Qwen3 relies on the Qwen2.5-7B anchor and matched-backbone reasoning rather than direct data control.
- Interventions establish necessity, not the full computation: no evidence on whether the model builds an image-like scene, or which circuits write/read the signal.

## Relevance

这篇论文直接命中"多模态大模型机理可解释性 + LLM 推理"这一核心兴趣：它用因果干预（而非探测）证明视觉训练会改写纯文本推理所依赖的内部几何，并给出可复用的干预-诊断协议，对理解统一理解/生成模型（如 Janus、BAGEL）内部表征分工有直接参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would controlled training ablations (vision-only vs generation-only pretraining from the same base) confirm that *generation* training is what compresses spatial structure into the 1D carrier, as the authors hypothesize but cannot test?
- Can circuit-level analysis identify which attention heads write and read the VL axis at the causal hub (e.g., Chameleon L5), moving from necessity to mechanism?
- Is the 32B broadening to non-spatial visual attribute binding a continuous scaling trend or an architectural phase transition — and does it appear in other 30B+ families?
- Do vision-language finetunes (e.g., LLaVA-style adapters on a frozen backbone) also introduce/amplify the scaffold, or is early-fusion joint pretraining required?
- Could the generation-path 1D carrier be exploited constructively — e.g., reinjecting or amplifying it to *improve* text-only spatial reasoning rather than only ablate it?
