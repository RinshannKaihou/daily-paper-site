Why This Matters

A systematic causal audit of refusal geometry across ~25 open-weight derivatives — its margin-rank probes and donor-overlap screens are directly reusable hidden-state interpretability tools.

Practical takeaway: a hub auditor can keep hooking T for DiM-style refusal audits across most derivatives.

Critical Edge

The flagship HADES win may be text-payload-driven — every image arrives with explicitly harmful text, and typography-only FigStep fails to reproduce the lift at all.

Recipe-scoped by design: only mean/rank-1 DiM is tested — flat E/P/F does not mean refusal is absent outside T.

01 · Methodology

One recipe, only the site varies

Extract = intervene, then freeze everything but the hook location and the model.

  • Extraction (DiM). The refusal direction v̂ is the difference of means between harmful and benign prompt pools — text DiM pooled at the last decoder-residual token; non-text DiM pooled over patch/projected tokens at E/P/F.
  • Intervention. Pure rank-1 ablation a ← a − λ(a·v̂)v̂ at the extraction site, λ = 4–8 over-ablation; optional RFA patches to the negative-side mean instead of zeroing.
  • Gates. Matched-norm random vector per site; empty-generation gate; disjoint AdvBench 128/392 extract/eval splits; frozen layer/λ operating points per model.
  • Judging. Median of a 7-judge LLM panel (IQR bars), calibrated on 160 gold labels (accuracy 0.89–0.91); keyword ASR never earns credit.
  • Mechanism probes. Projection ratio ρ, refusal-midpoint crossing, and rank/leverage of −v̂ among 50 matched-norm randoms on a teacher-forced safety margin.
  • Coverage. Root/Inheritance/Axis/Class taxonomy over ~25 open-weight leaves — fine-tunes, guards, thinking heads, VLMs, audio wrappers, Mamba2 hybrids, generative U-Nets — plus mapper-free donor transfer when residual widths match.

03 · Strengths

What holds up

  • One shared, well-controlled protocol — extract=intervene, matched-norm random, empty-rate gate, frozen operating points — applied uniformly across ~25 leaves from 7+ architecture families.
  • The margin-rank/leverage probe cleanly separates "extractor too weak" from "feature absent" at a site, and is reusable by future work.
  • Negative results are first-class citizens: flat E/P/F cells, RFA anti-lift, the FigStep ceiling, and closed grids are all reported rather than buried.
  • The donor subspace-overlap screen (necessary, not sufficient) gives practitioners a cheap pre-test before attempting mapper-free vector reuse.
  • Judge hygiene: 7-judge median with IQR, per-judge calibration, keyword ASR disqualified; chat and generative judges never pooled.
04 · Limitations

Where it strains

  • Recipe-scoped by design: only mean/rank-1 DiM (+ optional RFA) is tested — multi-direction, affine, and SAE extractors remain open, so "E/P/F ineffective" cannot be read as "refusal absent outside T".
  • The T vs. non-text comparison changes contrast variable and pooling with the site — not a causally isolated location effect (authors acknowledge).
  • The flagship HADES benchmark carries harmful text alongside the image; the modality-clean VLGuard control has small n=200 and modest absolute ASR (0.150).
  • Mechanism evidence comes from a single Vision-Instruct leaf (n=128 cache); generality of the rank-50/50 result to other VLMs is unverified.
  • Per-model λ/layer tuning with some post-hoc "late-resolved" unlocks weakens strict comparability; full scripts and panel JSON are withheld until acceptance.
05 · Open Questions

Where to push next

  1. Would multi-direction (rank-k) or SAE-feature extractors at E/P/F show attack lift where rank-1 mean DiM is flat — cleanly separating "weak extractor" from "absent feature"?
  2. Can the margin-rank/leverage instrument separate the third reading — T re-imposing refusal downstream of a successful perception-site edit — e.g. via matched-budget simultaneous P+T ablation?
  3. Why does the Llama donor land inside Mixtral's random overlap band (R=0.036) while a Mistral-family donor reaches R=0.63 — is refusal-subspace drift predictable from lineage or tokenizer/template distance?
  4. Does T-locality survive non-English harmful prompts, or harm carried entirely by the perceptual input (typography-free visual harm, inaudible-style audio artifacts)?
  5. Can the U-Net down-block concept-erase direction (NudeNet 0.79 → 0) transfer mapper-free across generative heads, the way text-tower donors transfer across chat derivatives?
  6. How does flatness at E/P/F interact with quantization and low-precision derivatives (BitNet was N/A here) — does numeric-format change break the rank-1 protocol even at T?
