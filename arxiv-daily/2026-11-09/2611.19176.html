Key finding · 核心发现

The verbalizable workspace is a bag of concepts — it reads out roles, but does not compute them.

可言语化工作区本质上是「概念袋」：沿工作区方向干预能改变模型对角色的口头报告（读出），却不能改变其真实绑定（计算）——基于工作区透镜的监测器读到的将是模型的「角色报告」而非「角色绑定」。

Why this matters · 为何重要

Workspace-lens alignment monitors would read a model’s role reports, not its role bindings — blind to safety-relevant propositions sharing the same concepts.

「模型计划欺骗用户」 vs 「用户计划欺骗模型」— 同一概念集、仅角色指派不同：工作区透镜原则上无法区分这两种状态。

▲ Critical edge · 批判视角

The steering null is ambiguous: the identity-swap positive control failed on all three models (shifts 0.0001–0.0048 vs the 0.05 floor), so the edit may simply be too weak to reach the binding substrate.

＋ the lone positive result (ablation on Gemma-3-27B-IT) confounds instruction tuning with model scale — no base Gemma-3-27B comparison exists.

Methodology · 方法论

  • Readout vs computation. Three causal experiments — probing, steering, ablation — on Qwen3.6-27B, Gemma-3-12B, and Gemma-3-27B-IT, at a read layer ≈75% of depth.
  • Stimuli. 2,400 minimal-pair sentences per model (600 families): role (agent/patient) × linear position, across active/passive, cleft, relative-clause, and dative constructions; direction-fitting corpora disjoint from evaluation.
  • Probing. Closed-form ridge probes with leave-one-pair-out CV on four sources: full residual stream, J-space (top-16 dictionary atoms), the orthogonal remainder, and a rank-matched random subspace as capacity control.
  • Steering. Difference-of-means and LRE-gradient role directions; pushes calibrated to a pre-registered 0.5 log-odds floor, judged against three strength-matched controls via a difference-in-differences binding score.
  • Ablation. Zero the top-10 workspace projections during the forward pass; deficit = binding drop minus concept-recall drop, vs a matched-rank random ablation.

  • Steering dissociates readout from binding: the LRE push moves role readout by +0.94 log-odds (toward-agent; −0.99 toward-patient; random direction −0.01) on Gemma-3-12B, yet the binding score never separates from controls on any model — verdict: “indistinguishable from controls”.
  • Ablation fires on exactly one of three models: zeroing the top-10 J-space directions on Gemma-3-27B-IT yields a binding-specific deficit of +0.275 (95% CI [0.262, 0.288]), concept accuracy held at 0.920; it replicates across three lens variants (0.284 / 0.334 / 0.297).
  • Cross-pair transfer (entity token): residual 0.594–0.644 and orthogonal remainder 0.603–0.626, while J-space stays at chance (0.510–0.524) — the residual stream carries a filler-general role axis the workspace lacks.

Strengths · 长处

  • Attacks a precise, decision-relevant open question with a falsifiable design rather than anecdotal lens readouts.
  • The rank-matched random subspace control is the right capacity control, used consistently across probing, steering, and ablation.
  • A verdict classifier with an “uninterpretable strength failure” outcome institutionalizes honesty about underpowered interventions — rare and commendable.
  • The Gemma-3-27B-IT ablation is a genuine internal positive control: it shows the design can detect binding involvement, strengthening the nulls elsewhere.
  • The readout-vs-computation dissociation is a clean conceptual cut that will travel well to other interpretability debates.

Limitations · 局限

  • Identity-swap strength check failed on all models, leaving the steering null ambiguous between “no binding substrate” and “intervention too weak”.
  • The single positive causal result confounds instruction tuning with scale; no base Gemma-3-27B comparison.
  • Narrow stimulus domain: three (six in one robustness run) professional noun pairs, agent/patient roles, English, one layer per model, dense transformers only.
  • The leap from synthetic two-entity sentences to safety-relevant propositions like deception planning is unargued by experiment.
  • No comparison against alternative verbalizable readouts (e.g., Natural Language Autoencoders) — the null binds the workspace-lens paradigm specifically.
  • Qwen seed replication incomplete at submission (3 of 5 seeds).

Questions for further exploration · 待追问

  1. Would a base (non-instruction-tuned) Gemma-3-27B show the same ablation deficit, disentangling scale from instruction tuning?
  2. Can a stronger intervention (e.g., patching the residual from the opposite-role sentence) turn the steering null into an interpretable result either way?
  3. Does the dissociation persist under Natural Language Autoencoders or other verbalizable readouts, or is it specific to the J-lens/R-lens workspace?
  4. Do richer relational structures — coreference, temporal relations, multi-entity planning, or the motivating deception scenario — show the same bag-of-concepts behavior?
  5. Is the orthogonal remainder’s near-ceiling decodability localized to specific circuits (binding IDs à la Feng & Steinhardt) that could be monitored directly instead?
