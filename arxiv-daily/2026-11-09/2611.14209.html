Key finding · arXiv 2611.14209 · attention robustness

Concentrated bursts of attention-head dropout derail chain-of-thought reasoning far more than equal-budget diffuse noise.

一句话：受皮层"局部睡眠"启发，同等屏蔽预算下，时空集中的注意力头突发屏蔽比弥散 i.i.d. dropout 造成的推理退化严重得多，且单步高密度屏蔽是主要伤害源。

Why this matters

Quantifies why serving-time transient faults concentrated in a few tokens — hardware errors, kernel glitches, KV-cache corruption — are disproportionately devastating versus uniform noise: a concrete benchmark for fault-tolerant inference.

⚠ Critical edge

Every effect rests on a single random schedule draw per item — the same items swing from −6.0 to −17.0 pp between draws.

Also disclosed: LLaMA's headline p = 0.0198 misses the pre-registered α = 0.01 threshold.
01Methodology
  • Models & setup — Qwen3-8B and LLaMA-3.1-8B-Instruct, zero-shot GSM8K with a standardized prompt, greedy decoding (temperature 0, thinking mode disabled), bfloat16 on a single RTX 4090.
  • Intervention — attention-head outputs zeroed via forward hooks on WO, upper half of layers only (Qwen 18–35: 576 heads; LLaMA 16–31: 512 heads). Corrupted residual states also poison downstream K/V writes that persist irrevocably in the cache.
  • Budget-matched schedules — clustered bursts with refractory-period onset vs. diffuse i.i.d. control, matched by stratified quintile matching across 5 temporal bins; B = 0.10 masks ≈5.7% of vulnerable head-token volume.
  • Designs — paired confirmatory N=250 (B ∈ {0.05, 0.10}); four-arm factorial N=100 (Clean / i.i.d. / Spaced / Burst, density α = 0.40); high-dose N=60 (B ∈ {0.15, 0.20}); duration ablation Δt ∈ {1, 2, 5}.
  • Statistics — exact McNemar with Holm correction, two-sided permutation tests, 10,000-resample bootstrap CIs, per-item fixed seeds.

† fails the pre-registered α = 0.01 threshold — disclosed by the author. At B=0.05 LLaMA's deficit is −8.0 pp (pholm = 0.0033), and its diffuse arm (89.2%) exceeds the clean baseline.
  • Factorial dissociation (N=100): instantaneous single-step density costs −10.0 pp (pholm = 0.0425); multi-token adjacency only −7.0 pp (n.s.) — harm is mainly per-step density, not continuity.
  • Non-monotonic dose: Qwen's deficit peaks at B=0.15 (−23.3 pp) then contracts to −15.0 pp at B=0.20; failed burst derivations run longer (+1,678 head-tokens) — prolonged derailment, not truncation.
  • Error modes split by architecture: Qwen errs inside intact formatting (conditional accuracy 91.9% → 80.5%); LLaMA partly loses the answer line (22.2% of discordant losses), narrowing its conditional gap to −5.0 pp.
  • "Autoregressive Attractor Trap" hypothesis: burst-corrupted K/V entries permanently trap greedy decoding in an erroneous basin — explicitly interpretive, never surgically tested.
03Strengths
  • Clean paired design with budget-matched controls and stratified quintile temporal matching — isolates concentration structure from cumulative dose about as well as stochastic schedules allow.
  • Exemplary statistical hygiene: exact McNemar + Holm, permutation tests, bootstrap CIs, and frank reporting of nulls, underpowered arms, and threshold misses.
  • The factorial density-vs-adjacency experiment and the error-mode taxonomy add genuine mechanistic texture beyond accuracy deltas.
  • Practically relevant: quantifies serving-time transient-fault risk (hardware errors, kernel glitches, quantization dropouts) for fault-tolerant inference.
04Limitations
  • One benchmark (GSM8K), one prompt, zero-shot only, thinking disabled on Qwen, two 8B models — external validity untested.
  • One schedule draw per item: contrasts swing −6.0 → −17.0 pp across draws on identical items, so single-draw exploratory effects are draw-specific.
  • WO zeroing conflates head silencing with downstream K/V corruption; masking confined to upper layers.
  • Duration ablation confounds duration with scheduled dose; Spaced arm reuses the same head subset; the Attractor Trap is never surgically verified.
  • Exploratory arms underpowered (LLaMA dissociation entirely n.s.); code and manifests unavailable until publication.
05Questions for further exploration
  1. Does the concentrated-burst deficit replicate beyond GSM8K — on MATH, MBPP, long-horizon agentic tasks, or reasoning-tuned models with thinking enabled?
  2. Is the −10.0 pp single-step density effect stable across multiple schedule draws, or an artifact of one Bernoulli realization?
  3. Can surgical KV-cache rollback "rescue" trapped derivations and empirically confirm the Autoregressive Attractor Trap?
  4. Is the vulnerability specific to upper-layer routing heads — what about lower layers, MLPs, or fresh head resampling per step?
  5. Can decode-time mitigation (self-consistency, rollback on entropy spikes, cache sanitization) neutralize burst-induced derailment at acceptable cost?
  6. Does Qwen's longer chain-of-thought (a larger "temporal target surface") explain its greater vulnerability — and does forcing concise derivations reduce it?
