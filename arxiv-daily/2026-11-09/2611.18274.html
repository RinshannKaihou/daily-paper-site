Critical edge

— Critical Assessment · classifier circularity

01Methodology — one dial, everything else frozen

  • Two axes, one variable. Steering = allocation (a binary gate mₜ) × magnitude (fixed strength α). Direction, layer, corrections, and decoding are shared by all methods — every comparison is a controlled ablation of when to intervene.
  • SAE-built probe. Contrastive differential between target vs. neutral corpora selects the top-10 SAE decoder features; their normalized rows form a unit probe u. Reading it costs one dot product per token — no SAE pass at inference.
  • CFP (memoryless). Threshold the projection sₜ = ⟨hₜ, u⟩ at a budget-calibrated τ; steer only the least-aligned tokens.
  • CLABS (closed loop). Standardize the projection, track error vs. setpoint s⋆=0.75, and let a PI law with a leaky integral (~5-token memory) set the steering probability — sustained drift draws more budget than momentary dips.

  • Fluency preserved. At p=0.8 CLABS keeps PPL 26.5 (No-steer: 23.0; Full-Steer: 41.2), rep-3 0.02, distinct-2 0.88 — within one σ of untouched output.
  • The memory term matters. Toxicity-reduction ablation at p=0.5: open-loop 0.12 → P-only 0.21 → full PI 0.24 — the feedback loop doubles the open-loop reduction while holding PPL at 8.6.
  • No overspending. Realized intervention rates verified within 0.04 of nominal for every gate and budget (Table 2).

03Strengths

  • Clean, falsifiable framing — any behavioral difference between gates is attributable to when they intervene; rare controlled design in the steering literature.
  • CLABS is principled and nearly free: scalar PI control per token (≈10⁻⁶ of a forward pass) operationalizes “spend budget where generation is drifting.”
  • Theory meets practice: the stability box tells you safe gains before deployment; the persistent-vs-alternating-error analysis explains why memory helps exactly at tight budgets.
  • Honest instrumentation: budget auditing, mechanism-matched ablation, gain sweep, dual judges with randomized positions, disclosed prompts and hyperparameters.

04Limitations

  • Only two open base models (2B / 8B), one layer, one direction per target, English only — conclusions explicitly restricted to this regime.
  • Both gates depend on a pretrained residual-stream SAE of adequate quality; sensitivity to SAE quality was never studied.
  • Stability guarantee covers only the first-order linearization where the clip is inactive — not a worst-case nonlinear guarantee.
  • The central 2–3× efficacy numbers exist only as Figure-1 curves; no numeric table for the main comparison.
  • No human evaluation; no code release despite fully disclosed hyperparameters.

05Questions for further exploration

  1. Does trajectory-aware allocation transfer to instruction-tuned chat models and 70B+ scales, where alignment-tuned representations may resist mid-layer injection?
  2. If behavioral shift were scored by an off-family classifier or human raters, does the 2–3× margin at tight budgets survive the broken circularity?
  3. Can a budgeted allocation gate compose stably with a magnitude controller (PID steering), and does the linear stability analysis extend to that loop?
  4. Is there a principled split of one budget across several directions — and does the gate ranking persist when the probe must serve multiple behaviors?
  5. On long-form generation (thousands of tokens), is the leaky integral’s ~5-token memory too short to track slow semantic drift?
