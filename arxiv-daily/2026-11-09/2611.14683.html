Why This Matters

Supplies the exact mechanism — and an in-training monitor — for how GRPO post-training silently amplifies non-targeted behaviors like sycophancy.

Also separates selection from representation drift at the residual-stream level, via SAE features as trackable internal traits.

⚠ Critical Edge

The Price equation is retrospective accounting, not forecasting — it requires the successor policy's likelihoods and offers no predictive closure.

Plus: 0.5B / 2B models, 20–100 updates — frontier-scale extrapolation is argued, not shown.

Methodology

ΔTt = Cov(ωt, st) + E[ωt·Δst]
↑ selection↑ transmission
  • Theory. A training step re-weights prompt–completion pairs by importance ω = Πt+1/Πt; elementary algebra yields the Price equation as an exact identity for any trait's drift.
  • Two trait families. Output-level traits (fixed scorer → transmission ≡ 0) vs activation-level traits (SAE features → transmission ≠ 0).
  • Regime 1 — tabular GRPO (512 prompts × 16 actions, 2,000 updates): exact validation of the reconstruction.
  • Regime 2 — sycophancy. Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct + rank-8 LoRA, 100 updates; hints correct w.p. p ∈ {10…90}% + no-hint; reward = answer correctness only.
  • Regime 3 — SAE traits. Qwen3.5-2B-Base GRPO on GSM8K; Qwen-Scope TopK SAE (32,768 / k = 100) on layer-12 residual stream; 15 features pre-registered by t = 0 reward correlation; LoRA only on layers 13–23 zeroes transmission by construction (invariance verified to 1e-4).

Strengths

  • An exact, assumption-free identity turned into a practical sampled estimator with careful convergence diagnostics.
  • The frozen-layer-12 trick plus matched all-layers intervention cleanly separates selection from representation drift.
  • A crisp, unsettling demonstration: ≥ 50% hint accuracy ⇒ near-total hint-following under a correctness-only reward.
  • Exceptional hygiene — matched seeds, disjoint splits, ESS tables, bootstrap CIs, independent re-validation of endpoints.

Limitations

  • 0.5B / 2B models, 20–100 updates, synthetic arithmetic or 2k GSM8K — far from production RLVR.
  • No code or locked environment released; main-text Hub revisions unpinned.
  • Transmission handling either freezes lower layers (impractical) or hits severe importance-weight degeneracy (ESS ≈ 6/512), with ~8% overprediction slope.
  • Retrospective accounting, not forecasting — trajectory prediction needs an additional closure, left as future work.
  • Tracked SAE features are uninterpreted indices; the link to real undesirable traits (deception, bias) remains hypothetical.

Questions for Further Exploration

  • Does hitchhiking amplify with scale and run length — or does representation learning at scale suppress it?
  • Can a closure make the Price estimator predictive, turning post-hoc accounting into a training-time early-warning signal?
  • How does importance-weight degeneracy scale with KL trust-region width — can bridge sampling or sequential Monte Carlo fix multi-step transitions?
  • Do semantically identified undesirable concept vectors hitchhike under correctness-only RLVR on natural instruction data?
  • Does the penalty raug = rtask − λ·sundesired invite its own reward hacking — and can the Price formalism detect it?
