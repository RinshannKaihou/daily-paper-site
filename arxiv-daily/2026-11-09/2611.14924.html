Why this matters

Maps how 4-bit quantization silently fails at long context on real consumer GPUs — and shows prompting, not retraining, can repair the reasoning loss.

Critical edge

The headline GPTQ collapse at 2048+ tokens is never quantified — no long-context F1 or variance numbers — and may be an ExLlamaV2 kernel bug, making “GPTQ is unstable on Turing” a software-environment observation, not a property of the scheme.

Methodology

Bifurcated empirical design — arithmetic effects of quantization (latency) measured separately from semantic consequences (fidelity, reasoning).

  • Phase 1 — quantization benchmark. Base Mistral-7B-v0.1 (avoids instruct bias) on a Tesla T4 16 GB: NF4 (nested quantile), GPTQ (Hessian-based, g=128, λ=0.01, WikiText-2 calibration), AWQ (activation-aware, protects top-1% weights). Metrics: ms/token, tok/s, TTFT, stability beyond 2048-token contexts.
  • Phase 2 — full RAG pipeline. Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.2 (Sliding Window Attention, 32k ctx). Adaptive ingestion routes PyMuPDF fast-extract vs Marker/Surya OCR behind a coherence gate (Qextract, retry below τ=0.5), then 256–768-token chunking; dense BGE + sparse BM25 fused via Reciprocal Rank Fusion.
  • Decoupled vision. A 0.23B Florence-2 Base captioner serializes document images to text via chained prompts — ~430 MB instead of a monolithic VLM.
  • Guardrails. Structured few-shot prompting plus a weighted validity filter (Qvalid, τ=0.6, Groundedness w=0.5) counter 4-bit reasoning degradation.

Evaluation: SQuADv2 retrieval (Recall@5) · FP16-vs-4-bit generative fidelity · math-restoration trials · 50-item tri-modal QA set (custom).

Honest footnote: the abstract’s “+192.7% accuracy uplift” is the vision-only sub-score (0.090 → 0.265); end-to-end RAG moved 0.235 → 0.249 (+6% relative). Reported perplexity shift under prompting (13.02 → 6.67) is methodologically unexplained in the paper.

+ Strengths
  • Clean bifurcated design isolates arithmetic from semantic effects — the right experimental shape for this question.
  • The per-component 8.37 GB memory budget is a concrete artifact edge practitioners rarely get in quantization papers.
  • Flags an under-documented failure mode (long-context GPTQ instability on Turing) and prioritizes reliability over peak throughput.
  • Few-shot restoration is training-free and immediately usable: +57 pp accuracy at zero FLOP cost.
  • Decoupled 0.23B captioner (~430 MB) instead of a monolithic VLM, fairly benchmarked against Moondream2 at half the VRAM.
− Limitations
  • GPTQ-collapse root cause (quantization noise vs ExLlamaV2 kernel bug) left open — no low-level profiling done.
  • KV cache stays FP16 and is budgeted only at 4k context; no KIVI / Q4_K_M; longer contexts grow it linearly.
  • Math eval on a custom 50-trial dataset (not GSM8K/MATH); multimodal eval also custom, unreleased, no error bars.
  • Single device (one Tesla T4); RTX 3060 / Ampere / Ada viability is inferred from static estimates, not measured.
  • NF4 is slower than the FP16 baseline (12.29 vs 15.76 tok/s; 2.5× worse TTFT) — chosen for memory, not speed.
  • Stack ambiguity: Fig. 2 says MiniLM-L6 + ChromaDB, Table II says BGE + FAISS.

Questions for further exploration

  1. Is the GPTQ collapse an arithmetic property on Turing tensor cores or an ExLlamaV2 kernel bug — and does it reproduce on Ampere/Ada with current kernels (Marlin, ExLlamaV2 v0.2+)?
  2. Does the 35% → 92% few-shot restoration transfer to GSM8K / MATH and stronger 4-bit schemes, or is it specific to the structured output topology used here?
  3. How much further can 8.37 GB shrink with KV-cache quantization (KIVI, 2-bit) or Q4_K_M mixed precision — and at what stability cost on Turing?
  4. Why does prompting change WikiText-2 perplexity (13.02 → 6.67)? Is the −47% latency gain real search-space pruning or just shorter outputs?
  5. Can the Qvalid filter (heuristic τ=0.6, w=0.5) be calibrated against a human-labeled grounding benchmark?
  6. Does the RRF gain (0.850 vs 0.700 dense-only) persist with stronger embedders, or is it an artifact of the small BGE-small encoder?
