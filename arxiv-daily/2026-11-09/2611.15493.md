> **一句话总结：** 本文指出 RLVR 中标准熵（token 级/序列级）对"有效性"视而不见——高熵往往只是把概率质量撒向组合爆炸的错误解空间，制造"多样性幻觉"；作者提出只在与奖励（verifier）一致的 valid 子空间内度量熵的 Effective Entropy（H_eff = −Σ r(o)π(o)log π(o)），并据此在 GRPO 上加一个极简辅助项得到 VEPO：对低概率的正确轨迹施加自适应"吸引"（权重 r·(1+log π(o)) 在 π<e⁻¹ 时为负），错误轨迹权重为零；在 1.5B/7B DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen 上，VEPO 六个数学基准平均 Avg@32 从 GRPO 的 48.4/58.9 提升到 50.6/61.1（约 +2.2 pp），且在匹配正确样本数（每题 8 条）的 MATH500-7B 直接测量中，每题distinct策略簇从 2.31 增至 2.68（+0.37，bootstrap 95% CI [0.24, 0.50]，p<10⁻⁴），证明多样性增益不是准确率的机械副产品。

> **TL;DR:** A NeurIPS 2026 paper from Fudan/Meituan/Tsinghua/PKU/USTC arguing that entropy-based exploration in RLVR suffers an "illusion of diversity": because the invalid subspace is combinatorially dominant (|O_invalid| ≫ |O_valid|), maximizing standard token or sequence entropy mostly disperses probability into wrong answers. The fix, Effective Entropy, weights each trajectory's entropy contribution by the binary verifier reward (H_eff = −Σ r(o)π(o)log π(o)), and its instantiation VEPO simply adds β·H_eff to GRPO, yielding a gradient that attracts probability toward under-explored valid trajectories (weight r·(1+log π) < 0 whenever π < e⁻¹, i.e., always in practice) while invalid trajectories get exactly zero entropy incentive. On six math benchmarks (AIME 2024/2025, AMC 2023, MATH500, Minerva, OlympiadBench) with DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B/7B trained on DAPO-Math-17K, VEPO lifts the Avg@32 aggregate over vanilla GRPO from 48.4 to 50.6 (1.5B) and 58.9 to 61.1 (7B), and beats five entropy-aware baselines (Entropy-Reg, Entropy-Adv, Adv-Reweighting, GTPO, AEPO) on average — though only by ~0.4–2.2 pp, and vs AEPO at 7B Pass@32 the gap is +0.4 pp with 95% CI [−0.3, 1.1] (not significant). The most convincing evidence is a matched-count diversity audit on the 441 MATH500-7B problems both methods solve: with exactly 8 correct trajectories per problem per method, VEPO shows +0.37 distinct strategy clusters (2.68 vs 2.31, +16%, p < 10⁻⁴), so the gain is genuine strategy diversity rather than just higher accuracy. Costs: 9.8% training-time overhead, and code is not released.

## 核心贡献

- **诊断"多样性幻觉"（illusion of diversity）：** 系统论证了现有 RLVR 探索机制的结构性错位——token 熵是局部盲的（正确性是整条轨迹的属性，不是单个 token 的属性），序列熵被全局稀释（错误解空间组合爆炸地占优，附录 A 形式化 |O_valid|/|O| ≈ (|V_correct|/|V|)^L → 0）。因此盲目的熵最大化在统计上必然把概率质量推入无效区域；附录 B.1 进一步证明标准熵正则的最优解是 Boltzmann 分布，错误概率 P(Error) = |N|/(|N|+|S|e^{1/β}) 显著非零。
- **提出 Effective Entropy（有效熵）概念：** H_eff(π) = −Σ_o r(o|q)·π(o|q)·log π(o|q)，把熵的测量域从整个输出空间改为 verifier 认定的 valid 子空间——无效轨迹贡献为零，只有正确轨迹塑造该指标。它同时编码了 valid 概率质量与条件策略多样性（附录 G.5：H_eff = Z·H(p_valid) − Z·log Z）。
- **实例化为 VEPO：** 刻意保持极简——J_VEPO = J_GRPO + β·H_eff，梯度权重 w(o) = r(o|q)·(1+log π(o|q)) 对所有现实的低概率正确轨迹为负，形成对"欠探索正确轨迹"的自适应吸引机制；π > e⁻¹ 的理论边界在实际 LLM（序列概率是大量 token 概率之积）中不可达。理论最优解是 valid 集合上的均匀分布（0 < β ≤ 1 时），而非标准熵正则下的含错误质量的 Boltzmann 分布。
- **token 级实现干净且无额外网络：** 序列级权重 W_i = r·(1+log π(o_i)) 做 stop-gradient 后直接作用于每个 token 的 log-prob 梯度，无 value network、无 PRM、无复杂控制机制——因此实验是对"有效熵"这一概念本身的干净验证。
- **策略多样性的直接测量（而非只看 pass rate）：** 在 441 道 GRPO 与 VEPO 都至少有 8 条正确轨迹的 MATH500-7B 题目上，匹配每方法每题恰好 8 条正确轨迹、10,000 次 rarefaction、用冻结 gte-Qwen2-7B-instruct 嵌入 + 方法盲选阈值聚类（人工标注 κ=0.82，pipeline precision 0.88 / recall 0.84），并辅以 3 个配对训练种子与符号翻转置换检验。

## Key Contributions

- **Diagnose the "illusion of diversity":** Existing RLVR exploration rests on entropy metrics that are structurally blind to validity. Token entropy is locally blind (correctness is a trajectory-level property), and sequence entropy is globally diluted because the invalid subspace is combinatorially dominant (Appendix A formalizes |O_valid|/|O| ≈ (|V_correct|/|V|)^L → 0). Appendix B.1 pushes this further: the optimum of standard entropy regularization is a Boltzmann distribution that must assign non-zero mass to hallucinations, with error mass P(Error) = |N|/(|N| + |S|e^{1/β}).
- **Define Effective Entropy:** H_eff(π) = −Σ_o r(o|q)·π(o|q)·log π(o|q) — entropy measured only over the reward-verified valid subspace. Invalid trajectories contribute exactly zero. The authors are explicit (Appendix G.5) that this is an unnormalized partial entropy combining valid mass Z and conditional diversity, not the entropy of the conditional distribution, and that conditional strategy diversity needs separate evaluation.
- **Instantiate VEPO as a deliberately minimal method:** J_VEPO = J_GRPO + β·H_eff. The gradient weight w(o) = r(o|q)(1 + log π(o|q)) is negative for every valid trajectory with π < e⁻¹ — i.e., all realistic autoregressive trajectories — producing an adaptive attraction toward under-explored correct paths; invalid trajectories get no entropy-driven encouragement at all. Under 0 < β ≤ 1 the idealized objective's global optimum is the uniform distribution over the valid set, with zero invalid mass.
- **Provide a clean token-level implementation:** the sequence weight W_i = r·(1 + log π(o_i)) is detached and applied to token-level log-probability gradients; no value networks, no PRMs, no extra control machinery. This makes the experiments a test of the concept rather than of engineering.
- **Directly measure strategy diversity with matched correct counts:** on the 441 MATH500-7B problems where both methods produce ≥8 verified-correct trajectories, they draw exactly 8 correct trajectories per method per problem (10,000 rarefactions), strip final answers, cluster reasoning with frozen gte-Qwen2-7B-instruct embeddings under a method-blinded, human-validated threshold (annotator Cohen's κ = 0.82; pipeline precision 0.88 / recall 0.84), and confirm the diversity gain with paired seeds and permutation tests.

## Methodology

**Problem setting.** Standard RLVR: policy π_θ generates trajectory o for query q; a binary verifier r(q,o) ∈ {0,1} supplies the reward; GRPO computes group-standardized advantages Â_i = (r(q,o_i) − mean)/std over G = 8 rollouts and applies the clipped PPO-style surrogate.

**Effective Entropy.** The paper first dissects why existing entropy metrics misalign (Section 4.1): H_tok averages per-token uncertainty and can stay high while randomizing locally-plausible tokens into invalid chains; H_seq spreads over the whole trajectory space where the invalid set dominates combinatorially. H_eff instead sums −π(o)log π(o) only over o with r(o|q) = 1, expressible as an expectation E_{o~π}[−r(o|q) log π(o|q)] for Monte-Carlo estimation from rollouts.

**VEPO objective and gradient.** J_VEPO(θ) = J_GRPO(θ) + β·H_eff(π_θ). The gradient of the effective-entropy loss works out to ∇L_eff = E[r(o|q)(1+log π(o|q))∇log π(o|q)]: for valid trajectories with π < e⁻¹ the scalar weight is negative, so minimizing the loss *raises* the probability of under-explored valid trajectories (attraction); for invalid trajectories r = 0 kills the weight entirely. The π > e⁻¹ sign-flip boundary is acknowledged for theoretical completeness but argued unreachable since sequence probabilities are products of many token probabilities. Appendix B.1 contrasts global optima: reward-only RL permits a Dirac on one solution; reward + standard entropy forces a Boltzmann distribution with inevitable error mass; reward + effective entropy (0 < β ≤ 1) yields the uniform distribution strictly over the valid set.

**Token-level implementation.** For each sampled trajectory the sequence weight W_i = r(o_i|q)(1 + log π(o_i|q)) is computed and detached (sg[·]); the auxiliary gradient is the average of sg[W_i]·∇log π(x_t | x_<t, q) over group and tokens. Raw sequence sums are used (no length normalization — that would define a different functional); sharing W_i across tokens does not solve causal token-level credit assignment, which the authors state plainly.

**Training setup.** DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B and 7B fine-tuned on DAPO-Math-17K in the VeRL framework (vLLM rollouts with PagedAttention, FSDP, ZeRO-3 offloading for 7B; 30 nodes × 8 A100-80GB; >25,000 GPU-hours total). Global batch 512, mini-batch 32, G = 8, clip ε = 0.2, β_KL = 0, constant learning rates (1.0e-6 at 1.5B, 4.63e-7 at 7B), max length 8192, AdamW. β for H_eff grid-searched over {3e-5, 1e-4, 1e-3, 3e-3, 1e-2} on held-out validation; β = 1e-4 chosen (larger β sustains exploration but delays convergence). Dual-oracle verification: math_verify for fast training-time rewards, the stricter prime_math for all reported evaluation metrics (to reduce reward hacking).

**Evaluation.** Six benchmarks — AIME 2024, AIME 2025 (temporal hold-out), AMC 2023, MATH500, Minerva, OlympiadBench — with Avg@32 (expected accuracy over 32 samples) and Pass@32. Baselines: vanilla GRPO plus five entropy-aware methods (Entropy-Reg, Entropy-Adv, Adv-Reweighting, GTPO, AEPO) with per-paper-recommended or tuned hyperparameters.

**Attribution analysis.** Three diagnostics beyond accuracy: relative entropy gain G(M) = H_M/H_GRPO; effective ratio ρ_eff = H_eff/H_seq (fraction of sequence uncertainty on valid trajectories); and a proposed Entropy Conversion Efficiency ECE = ΔP·exp(sgn(ΔP)·ΔH/H_init) measuring how much performance is bought per unit of entropy consumption.

## Results & Highlights

- **Aggregate accuracy over six benchmarks (Avg@32 / Pass@32):** at 1.5B, VEPO reaches 50.6 / 71.6 vs GRPO's 48.4 / 70.1 (+2.2 / +1.5 pp); at 7B, 61.1 / 78.5 vs 58.9 / 77.2 (+2.2 / +1.3 pp). VEPO has the best average Avg@32 and Pass@32 among all seven methods at 1.5B, and the best average Avg@32 at 7B.
- **Per-benchmark examples:** on AIME 2024 at 1.5B, Avg@32 improves from 33.2 (GRPO) to 35.9 and Pass@32 from 71.8 to 72.3; on AIME 2025 at 7B, Avg@32 improves from 37.4 to 41.1 (+3.7 pp) and Pass@32 from 60.5 to 63.4; on AMC 2023 at 7B, Avg@32 from 88.1 to 91.5. Note individual Pass@32 comparisons are mixed (e.g., on AMC23-7B Pass@32 Entropy-Adv hits 99.8 vs VEPO's 97.5, and on MATH500-7B Pass@32 GTPO reaches 95.3 vs VEPO's 92.6).
- **Direct strategy diversity (the headline evidence):** on the 441 commonly-solved MATH500-7B problems with exactly 8 matched correct trajectories per method, VEPO yields 2.68 distinct strategy clusters per problem vs GRPO's 2.31 (+0.37, a 16.0% gain; 95% problem-bootstrap CI [0.24, 0.50]; sign-flip permutation p < 10⁻⁴), Hill-1 effective modes 2.24 vs 1.97 (+0.27), and a lower largest-cluster share (0.61 vs 0.68), i.e., less mode collapse. Robust to encoder swap (e5-Mistral: +0.31, CI [0.18, 0.44]) and CoT-length matching (+0.34, CI [0.20, 0.47]).
- **Repeatability across seeds:** three paired training seeds (0, 1, 2) give six-benchmark Avg@32 gains of +2.1 / +2.4 / +2.1 pp at 1.5B and +2.3 / +2.4 / +1.9 pp at 7B, with all paired Avg@32 and Pass@32 gains positive (aggregate 1.5B: 48.4 ± 0.46 vs 50.6 ± 0.60; 7B: 58.9 ± 0.44 vs 61.1 ± 0.40).
- **Attribution/dynamics:** VEPO raises the effective ratio ρ_eff = H_eff/H_seq (more uncertainty allocated to valid trajectories) and sustains higher Entropy Conversion Efficiency than Entropy-Reg, whose benefit is transient; the "Diversity Paradox" case study shows Adv-Reweighting surging in token entropy after step 150 while its effective entropy stays below even Entropy-Reg's and performance plateaus/declines — high entropy without validity.
- **Controls and robustness:** a constant-gated ablation (correctness mask kept, log π dependence removed) underperforms VEPO by 1.1–1.5 Avg@32 pp across seeds/scales, so the adaptive log-probability weight matters, not just validity gating; under injected verifier noise at 1.5B the VEPO-minus-GRPO gap persists (FN 10%: 49.9 vs 47.8; FP 10%: 48.8 vs 46.8 Avg@32) though both degrade; a blinded rationale audit finds 92.5% (GRPO) vs 93.0% (VEPO) of correct trajectories logically sound (difference +0.5 pp, CI [−3.7, 4.7]) — the extra diversity is not bought with sloppier reasoning.
- **Overhead:** 9.8% steady-state training-time overhead vs GRPO; VEPO reaches the paired GRPO run's final aggregate Avg@32 using 73% (1.5B) / 78% (7B) of the rollout budget, ≈80% / 86% of wall-clock time including overhead.

## Authors & Credibility

Eight authors across Fudan University, Meituan, Tsinghua University, Peking University, and USTC; four equal-contribution authors with a Meituan corresponding author (linjiaye@meituan.com). The paper states it is accepted at NeurIPS 2026 and includes the full NeurIPS checklist — notably answering "No" to the code-reproducibility question (implementation and reproduction scripts are not publicly released), which is honest but limits verification. Meituan provided the compute (30 nodes × 8 A100, >25,000 GPU-hours); the funding disclosure states the study was employee-initiated independent research, not a formal Meituan project, and lists the Meituan affiliations/employment transparently. The empirical methodology is unusually careful for this subfield: dual training/evaluation verifiers to resist reward hacking, matched-correct-count diversity measurement with human-validated clustering (κ = 0.82; precision 0.88 / recall 0.84), prespecified encoder-swap and length-matching robustness checks, three paired seeds, sign-flip permutation tests, verifier-noise retraining, and a blinded rationale audit. Several authors also appear on the related DynaMO, DGPO, and MASPO papers, indicating an active LLM-RL research line.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** The core idea — mask the entropy bonus by the verifier reward — is simple to the point of looking obvious in hindsight, but the paper's real contribution is the conceptual reframing (entropy's *domain* should be the valid subspace) plus the honest demonstration that a deliberately un-engineered instantiation already beats a field of heavily engineered entropy-aware methods. The related-work analysis (Appendix D's 8-region classification of entropy-aware algorithms, placing VEPO purely in "compensate high-entropy positive samples") is a genuine service. Still, correctness-masked entropy is a fairly small delta from, e.g., Entropy-Adv's entropy-conditioned advantage modulation; the distinction is principled (zero weight on invalid trajectories rather than down-weighting) but the mechanism is one term.
- **Rigor:** High, with a few soft spots. Strengths: dual-oracle verification, three paired seeds, CIs and permutation tests on the diversity claim, verifier-noise retraining (Appendix G.4), constant-gated and length-normalized controls (G.3), and explicit non-claims ("Pass@32 ... does not directly measure semantic strategy diversity"). Weak spots: (1) vs the strongest baseline, the 7B aggregate Pass@32 advantage over AEPO is only +0.4 pp with 95% CI [−0.3, 1.1] — the paper itself concedes "competitiveness rather than significant superiority"; several individual benchmark cells lose to baselines (e.g., AMC23-7B Pass@32: Entropy-Adv 99.8 vs VEPO 97.5; MATH500-7B Pass@32: GTPO 95.3 vs VEPO 92.6). (2) The theoretical global-optimum result (uniform over valid set) holds only for the idealized expected functional with 0 < β ≤ 1 and explicitly does not guarantee convergence of clipped GRPO with neural policies — it is motivation, not a guarantee. (3) Baseline tuning effort is described as "recommended in their respective original papers or tuned ... on our validation set," which leaves room for under-tuned baselines, a chronic issue in this literature.
- **Reproducibility:** Code and executable scripts are not released (NeurIPS checklist answer: No). The objective, gradient derivation, detached-weight pseudocode, datasets, seeds (0/1/2), and full hyperparameter table are provided, so a competent group could reimplement it, but at >25,000 GPU-hours the entry cost is substantial and exact replication is unlikely.
- **Significance:** The "illusion of diversity" critique lands: the Adv-Reweighting case study (token entropy surging while effective entropy and accuracy fall) is a crisp, quotable demonstration that entropy magnitude is the wrong dial. Whether the concept travels beyond math RLVR is open by the authors' own admission.
- **Red flags:** None serious. Scope is stated conservatively ("support validity-aware exploration on the tested backbones, rather than universal transfer"). The diversity endpoint conditions on the 441 commonly-solved MATH500 problems (not all 500), which the paper discloses; answer verification does not certify intermediate steps, also disclosed. The industrial-compute origin (Meituan) is transparently handled.

## Strengths

- Correctly identifies and formalizes a structural flaw shared by the entire entropy-aware RLVR family: entropy is measured over a space where invalid trajectories combinatorially dominate, so "high entropy" can reward hallucination diversity.
- The method is minimal and the experiments are framed as a concept test — no value networks, PRMs, or clipping tricks — which makes the positive result more attributable to the idea itself.
- The matched-count strategy-diversity protocol (8 correct trajectories per problem per method, 10,000 rarefactions, frozen embeddings, method-blinded human-validated threshold, encoder-swap and length-matching robustness) is one of the more careful diversity measurements in the LLM-RL literature and cleanly separates diversity from accuracy.
- Exceptional transparency: publishes negative margins (AEPO Pass@32 CI crossing zero), verifier-noise degradation tables, the 9.8% overhead, rationale-audit CIs, and an unusually candid limitations appendix (H).
- The attribution analysis (effective ratio ρ_eff, Entropy Conversion Efficiency, the Adv-Reweighting "Diversity Paradox") explains *why* the method works, not just that it works.

## Limitations

- Requires a reliable binary verifier; the method is directly applicable only to RLVR with exact answers, unit tests, or executable rewards. Open-ended or preference-based rewards need a redefinition of the valid subspace; non-binary rewards v(o) ∈ [0,1] give reward-weighted entropy, a different functional (Appendix G.4).
- Evidence is confined to mathematical reasoning, two backbones (1.5B/7B DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen), and one training set (DAPO-Math-17K); larger models, multimodal reasoning, tool-use agents, and long-horizon interactive tasks are untested.
- H_eff is estimated from sampled rollouts, so its quality depends on sampling coverage — when valid trajectories are extremely rare early in training, warm-up, curricula, or stronger initial policies may be needed; with G = 8, 0.9–7.1% of groups carry no auxiliary signal at all.
- Margins over the strongest baseline are modest and sometimes statistically indistinguishable (AEPO 7B Pass@32: +0.4 pp, CI [−0.3, 1.1]); several per-benchmark cells lose to baselines, and three seeds give "limited precision for seed-level inference" (authors' words).
- Terminal verification cannot credit useful intermediate steps inside outcome-wrong trajectories, the shared sequence weight provides no causal token-level credit assignment, and false-positive verifier errors reintroduce an entropy incentive on incorrect outputs (G.4 quantifies the degradation). Embedding clusters are an operational proxy for "strategy," and the diversity result conditions on commonly-solved problems.
- Code is not released; replication requires >25,000 GPU-hours of A100 compute.

## Relevance

这篇论文直击 RLVR/GRPO 训练中"熵=探索"这一默认假设的结构性缺陷，提出的有效熵（只在 verifier 认可的 valid 子空间内度量多样性）与自监督/推理强化的核心议题——如何让模型多角度地正确思考而非多样化地犯错——高度相关，对设计 LLM 推理训练的探索机制与熵控制有直接的借鉴价值。匹配正确样本数的策略多样性测量协议本身也是评估"真实探索"的一个可复用方法论贡献。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How does effective entropy interact with process reward models or step-level verification, where the validity mask is no longer binary and terminal — would a soft, per-step r(o, t) recover causal token credit that the shared sequence weight lacks?
- Does the attraction dynamic stay benign when valid trajectories become high-probability (π approaching e⁻¹) — e.g., after aggressive distillation onto a narrow solution set — and could the theoretical sign-flip boundary become practically reachable for short answers?
- Can the valid-subspace entropy idea transfer to non-verifiable domains via learned verifiers, self-consistency voting, or reward-model ensembles, and how much false-positive rate can the mechanism tolerate before the gains (currently retained at 10% FP/FN) erode?
- Would combining VEPO with the clipping/trust-region family (Clip-Higher, CISPO, DGPO) compound, or do they compete for the same probability mass — the 8-region classification in Appendix D predicts region-1-only behavior, but this is untested?
- Is the +16% matched-count strategy diversity gain causally linked to the accuracy gain, or would a diversity-only intervention (e.g., diversity-aware rollout selection without the gradient term) produce one without the other?
