> **一句话总结：** 在六个开源混合推理模型（Mamba-2 / GDN / 并行混合架构）上的配对实验表明，把循环状态缓存用 bf16 舍入到最近偶数（RTN）存储会让其中五个模型的思维链变长 7%–27%（Qwen3.5-9B 在 590 道竞赛数学题上准确率从 85.6% 掉到 70.8%、触顶次数 118→223），而同位宽的 bf16 随机舍入（SR，作者在 SGLang 内核中实现、用整数哈希随机数发生器做到零开销）能以 bf16 的显存和速度把长度、触顶率和准确率恢复到 FP32 水平——说明这是舍入偏差而非精度不足的问题。

> **TL;DR:** PayPal AI presents a controlled, paired study of bf16 round-to-nearest (RTN) recurrent-state storage on six open hybrid reasoning models (Nemotron-Nano-9B/12B-v2, Nemotron-3-Nano-30B-A3B, Qwen3.5-9B, Qwen3-Next-80B-A3B, Falcon-H1R-7B) over 590 competition-math problems (MATH-500 + AIME24/25 + HMMT Feb 2025) and LiveCodeBench. Against the FP32-state baseline, bf16 RTN lengthens reasoning in 5 of 6 models (geometric-mean length ×1.07–×1.27); on Qwen3.5-9B cap hits rise 118→223 and accuracy falls 85.6%→70.8% (−14.8 pts, McNemar p=2e-14), and on LiveCodeBench pass@1 drops 12.6 and 13.4 points for the two models tested. Same-width bf16 stochastic rounding (implemented in SGLang's Mamba-2 and GDN Triton decode kernels, with an integer-hash RNG that costs nothing over RTN) removes the effect in 4 of 5 affected models — restoring Qwen3.5-9B to 85.6% accuracy at 10,393 vs 10,163 tokens/s for RTN — proving the failure is rounding bias, not missing resolution. A 128K-budget rerun further shows roughly half of Qwen3.5-9B's gap is budget-limited verbosity while the rest is non-terminating runs, 85% of them exact repetition loops.

## 核心贡献

- **六个模型的配对测量（bf16 RTN vs FP32）**：在相同提示词下，bf16 RTN 状态使六个混合推理模型中的五个输出变长（几何平均长度 ×1.070–×1.265，Wilcoxon p ≤ 5e-12），触顶率上升；该效应在贪心解码下同样存在（Nemotron-Nano-9B-v2 ×1.211、Qwen3.5-9B ×1.172），排除了采样伪影的解释。Falcon-H1R-7B 是唯一的例外（×1.008，p=0.66，作者坦承无法解释）。
- **区分"预算不足"与"永不终止"两种失败模式**：把 Qwen3.5-9B 在 32K 触顶的题目用 128K 预算重跑，放宽准确率差距从 14.7 分缩到 6.9 分但不消失（93.6% vs 86.6%）；bf16 下永不终止的运行是 FP32 的两倍（66 vs 17），其中 85%（79/93）是逐字重复循环。而 Nemotron-Nano-9B-v2 的 31 个额外触顶中 30 个在 128K 下能完成——代价主要是额外 token。指出现有的"双方都完成"准确率拆分（both-finished split）会完全掩盖第二种失败。
- **同位宽 bf16 随机舍入（SR）证明因果是舍入偏差而非分辨率**：作者修改 SGLang 的 Triton selective-state-update（Mamba-2）与 packed GDN 解码内核，在 FP32 状态值的 bf16 尾数下方加 16 位随机数后截断，存储与 bf16 RTN 完全相同。SR 在五个受影响模型中的四个里把长度、触顶率、准确率恢复到 FP32 水平（如 Qwen3.5-9B 准确率 70.8%→87.6%），反驳了 DAMP 的"分辨率解释"并修正了 LeapQuant "SR 帮助不大"的结论。
- **零开销 SR：整数哈希随机数发生器**：Triton 默认的 10 轮 Philox 使 GDN 内核解码速度只有 RTN 的 30–60%；换成 lowbias32 整数哈希后（微基准 ×1.01 内核时间），Qwen3.5-9B 端到端吞吐 10,393 tokens/s，甚至略高于同日 RTN 运行的 10,163，同时触顶数（113 vs 118）与准确率（85.6%，两者相同）均与 FP32 匹配——以 bf16 的显存和速度获得 FP32 的行为。
- **教师强制 KL 会误判关键头组**：在 Nemotron-Nano-9B-v2 上，只对中间衰减带（q1+q2 两个八分位、25% 头、衰减率 0.57u–4.1u）施加 SR 即可消除几乎全部效应（Flog=1.03），而对 KL 份额最大（49%）的最慢八分位 q0 施加 SR 毫无效果（Flog=−0.12），随机头组也无效——说明基于 KL 的精度分配必须用闭环生成长度验证。该集中性是 Nemotron-Nano 谱系特有：在 Nemotron-3 和 Qwen3.5-9B 中效应分散于各头。
- **对评估实践的可操作建议**：报告循环状态 dtype 与舍入模式、触顶率和输出长度；不要把"双方都完成"子集上的准确率当作"无能力损失"（该子集是选择性的且接近天花板，如 Qwen3.5-9B 的 99.7%）；优先 FP32 状态或带 SR 的 16 位状态。

## Key Contributions

- **Paired six-model measurement at bf16 RTN.** On identical prompts (590 competition-math problems), bf16 RTN recurrent state lengthens reasoning in five of six hybrid models (geometric-mean length ratios ×1.070–×1.265, Wilcoxon p ≤ 5e-12) and raises 32K-token cap hits; the effect also appears under greedy decoding in both models tested (Nemotron-Nano-9B-v2 ×1.211, Qwen3.5-9B ×1.172), ruling out a sampling artifact. Falcon-H1R-7B is the one null (×1.008, p=0.66), left unexplained.
- **Separating "needs more budget" from "never terminates."** Rerunning all 268 Qwen3.5-9B items capped in either arm at 128K halves the relaxed accuracy gap (14.7 → 6.9 points; 93.6% FP32 vs 86.6% bf16) but does not close it: bf16 runs that never finish at 128K are twice as common (66 vs 17), and 85% of them (79/93) are exact repetition loops. By contrast, 30 of Nemotron-Nano-9B-v2's 31 extra caps finish at 128K, so that model's cost is mostly extra tokens. The popular "both-finished" accuracy split hides the non-termination failure entirely.
- **Same-width bf16 SR pins the cause on rounding bias, not resolution.** The authors patch SGLang's Triton selective-state-update (Mamba-2) and packed GDN decode kernels to add 16 random bits below the bf16 mantissa before truncation — storage identical to bf16 RTN. SR removes the length effect in four of five affected models (Qwen3.5-9B accuracy restored 70.8% → 87.6%, cap hits 223 → 111), contradicting DAMP's resolution-only explanation and refining LeapQuant's claim that SR "helps little."
- **Zero-cost SR via an integer-hash RNG.** Triton's default 10-round Philox makes the GDN kernel decode at only 30–60% of RTN speed; replacing it with the lowbias32 integer hash (kernel microbenchmark ×1.01 of RTN time) lets Qwen3.5-9B SR match FP32 behavior (113 vs 118 cap hits; 85.6% accuracy for both) at 10,393 tokens/s end-to-end versus 10,163 for a same-day RTN run — FP32 fidelity at bf16 cost.
- **Teacher-forced KL misranks head groups.** In Nemotron-Nano-9B-v2, SR applied only to a band of intermediate-decay heads (octiles q1+q2, 25% of 3,456 heads, decay ∈ [0.57u, 4.1u)) removes essentially the whole effect (Flog = 1.03 [0.86, 1.23]; caps 1), while SR on q0 — the octile carrying the largest teacher-forced KL share (49%) — removes nothing (Flog = −0.12), nor do random head sets or a size-matched control. The concentration is lineage-specific: in Nemotron-3 and Qwen3.5-9B the effect spreads across heads (all three pre-registered Qwen3.5-9B band tests fail).
- **Evaluation recommendations.** Report the resolved recurrent-state dtype and rounding mode, cap rate, and output length alongside accuracy; never read both-finished accuracy as "no capability loss" (that subset is selected and near ceiling — 99.7% for Qwen3.5-9B); rerun capped items at a larger budget; validate state-precision choices with generation, not KL alone; prefer FP32 state or 16-bit with SR (bf16 SR is not yet available in SGLang).

## Methodology

The study is a controlled, paired comparison of recurrent-state storage formats, run on SGLang 0.5.21 across 8 RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell GPUs with `--mamba-ssm-dtype` set to float32, bfloat16, or float16. All weights and activations stay bf16; only the state dtype changes between arms.

**Models (6):** Nemotron-Nano-9B-v2 and its parent Nemotron-Nano-12B-v2 (Mamba-2 hybrids, one lineage); Nemotron-3-Nano-30B-A3B (Mamba-2 MoE hybrid); Qwen3.5-9B and Qwen3-Next-80B-A3B-Thinking (Gated DeltaNet hybrids); Falcon-H1R-7B (attention and Mamba-2 in parallel in every layer).

**Benchmarks:** 590 competition-math problems (MATH-500, plus 30 each from AIME 2024, AIME 2025, HMMT February 2025), sampled with each model card's settings (e.g., T=0.6, top-p 0.95; Nemotron-3 at T=1.0) under a 32,768-token output cap; plus the LiveCodeBench v6 increment (175 problems, 2 samples each, Jan 4–Apr 6 2025 contests) for two models. Greedy arms (T=0) test sampling robustness.

**Metrics:** Length ratio = geometric mean of paired per-item output-length ratios with a Wilcoxon signed-rank test on log ratios. Accuracy = last `\boxed{}` answer checked with math-verify, compared with exact McNemar tests. Intervention effect = F_log = (log L_bf16 − log L_arm)/(log L_bf16 − log L_FP32), where 1 means matching FP32 length and 0 means matching bf16 RTN, with 95% paired-bootstrap intervals (4,000 resamples).

**Stochastic rounding implementation.** The patched kernels add 16 random bits below the bf16 mantissa of each FP32 state value and truncate, so values round up with probability equal to their fractional position between bf16 neighbours — unbiased at every decay rate. Random bits come from Triton's counter-based Philox generator (fresh seed per CUDA-graph replay) or, in the fast variant, the lowbias32 integer hash with a fresh 32-bit seed per decode step; the authors verified cross-step independence (lag-1..4 correlation ≤ 4.5e-5) and kernel bitwise-identity when SR is off. Prefill state writes still use RTN (once per request vs up to 32,768 decode writes).

**Mechanistic analysis.** A toy quiet leaky-integrator simulation (h ← round(e^{-r}h) over 20,000 trajectories) shows RTN freezes decay for rates r ≲ u (u = 2^-9, bf16's smallest relative half-spacing), distorts it up to ~4u (effective rate 0.17× at 1.5u, 1.39× at 2u), and SR is unbiased everywhere. For head localization, Nemotron-Nano-9B-v2's 3,456 Mamba heads are ranked into octiles by weights-only decay rate; teacher-forced KL (on 120 reasoning traces, positions beyond 4K tokens) is compared against closed-loop SR-on-subset arms, including size-matched (q0+q3), random-head, and single-octile controls.

**Rigor controls:** most key tests were pre-registered with pass/fail criteria written before data collection (Table 6 lists all, including failures); concurrency confounds are controlled on Nemotron-3 (matching per-rank requests to 43 leaves the effect intact, ×1.098); FP32 seed replication on Nemotron-3 agrees (×0.982); a Qwen3.5-9B 8-rank FP32 rerun agrees with the 6-rank arm (86.6% vs 85.6%, McNemar p=0.58).

## Results & Highlights

- **Length inflation, math (590 problems, 32K cap):** geometric-mean length ratio bf16/FP32 of ×1.197 (Nemotron-Nano-9B-v2, p=7e-19), ×1.188 (Nemotron-Nano-12B-v2, p=1e-18), ×1.081 (Nemotron-3-Nano-30B-A3B, p=3e-6), ×1.265 (Qwen3.5-9B, p=7e-16), ×1.070 (Qwen3-Next-80B, p=5e-12); Falcon-H1R-7B null at ×1.008 (p=0.66). Under greedy decoding the effect persists: Nemotron-Nano-9B-v2 ×1.211 (caps 5→49), Qwen3.5-9B ×1.172 (caps 208→285, accuracy 75.6%→59.8%, McNemar p=1e-11).
- **Accuracy damage concentrates in cap hits:** on MATH+AIME+HMMT, Qwen3.5-9B accuracy falls from 85.6% (FP32) to 70.8% (bf16 RTN), −14.8 points (McNemar p=2e-14), with cap hits rising 118→223 and all 112 lost items being capped bf16 runs; Nemotron-3-Nano-30B-A3B falls 92.5%→87.3% (p=3e-7) with caps 36→81. Accuracy on items both arms finish is unchanged (99.7%/99.7% for Qwen3.5-9B) — but that subset is selected and near ceiling.
- **Code generation (LiveCodeBench v6, 175 problems × 2 samples):** Nemotron-Nano-9B-v2 pass@1 drops 12.6 points [6.9, 18.3] (58.3% FP32 → 45.7% bf16 RTN) with length ratio ×1.37 [1.29, 1.46] and caps 36→162 of 350; Qwen3.5-9B pass@1 drops 13.4 points [8.3, 19.1] (46.3% → 32.9%) with caps 169→233. SR restores pass@1 to 59.4% and 45.4% respectively. Notably, pass@1 among finished samples is *higher* under bf16 RTN (85.1% vs 65.0% for Nemotron-Nano-9B-v2) — a finished-only metric would wrongly rank bf16 RTN above FP32.
- **128K budget rerun (Qwen3.5-9B, n=268 capped items):** relaxed accuracy gap halves but stays significant (FP32 93.6% vs bf16 86.6%, i.e., 6.9 points, McNemar p=7e-7); unfinished-at-128K runs are 66 bf16-only vs 17 FP32-only (p=6e-8), and 79 of 93 unfinished bf16 runs (85%) are exact repetition loops (tail repeating with fixed period for ≥ 2 periods and 192 tokens). Median rerun output length is 41,949 tokens (bf16) vs 19,951 (FP32).
- **Same-width bf16 SR fixes four of five affected models:** F_log = 1.05 [0.88, 1.27] (Nemotron-Nano-9B-v2, caps 1/31/0 for FP32/RTN/SR), 0.98 (12B-v2), 0.99 (Qwen3-Next, caps 16/24/15), 0.87 [0.58, 1.26] (Qwen3.5-9B: accuracy 85.6/70.8/87.6%, caps 118/223/111). Nemotron-3 is the exception — its pre-registered test fails (F_log = 0.38 [−0.37, 1.00], caps 81→41): SR removes the runaway tail but an unexplained bulk ×1.05 shift remains.
- **Zero-cost SR with the hash RNG:** on Qwen3.5-9B, hash-generator SR matches FP32 on cap hits (113 vs 118) and accuracy (85.6% for both) at 10,393 tokens/s end-to-end vs 10,163 for a same-day bf16 RTN run (102–113% of RTN speed across batch bins), versus 30–60% decode speed with default Philox.
- **Head localization (Nemotron-Nano-9B-v2):** SR on the q1+q2 band (864 of 3,456 heads, decay 0.57u–4.1u) removes essentially all excess (F_log = 1.03 [0.86, 1.23], caps 1); SR on q0 (largest teacher-forced KL share, 49%) removes nothing (F_log = −0.12 [−0.37, 0.07]); random 864 heads remove nothing (0.06 [−0.14, 0.24]); the band transfers to the 12B parent (F_log = 0.84 [0.68, 1.03]) but fails to transfer to Nemotron-3 and Qwen3.5-9B (all three pre-registered Qwen3.5-9B band tests fail).
- **Practical scale of the confound:** prior reports contextualized by the paper include the vLLM bug fix where FP32 state cut cap hits from 23% to 0.25% and raised requests/s 2.55×, and Nemotron 3 Super's report of up to 37% FP16 verbosity increase; engine defaults differ silently (vLLM 0.33 uses model dtype/bf16 for a Qwen3.5 config without `mamba_ssm_dtype`, SGLang 0.5.21 uses FP32).

## Authors & Credibility

The three authors (Yuchuan Weng, Xin Chen, Chi Liu) are from PayPal AI, an industry applied-research group. The work is unusually transparent by empirical-ML standards: key hypotheses were pre-registered with explicit pass/fail criteria (Appendix A / Table 6 lists every pre-registered test including the failures — the Nemotron-3 full-SR test, the cap-simulation test, and all three Qwen3.5-9B band-transfer tests fail and are reported as such), though the registration notebook is not externally timestamped. Methodological hygiene is strong: paired designs with exact McNemar and Wilcoxon tests, bootstrap CIs on F_log, concurrency-matched controls on Nemotron-3, FP32 seed replications, an 8-rank FP32 rerun of Qwen3.5-9B, bitwise-identity checks of the patched kernels when SR is off, and independence checks of the hash RNG. The SR kernel implementation in SGLang's Triton kernels is a concrete engineering artifact, not just a paper claim. No external code release is mentioned beyond the described SGLang patches.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** The core phenomenon (RTN lengthens recurrent-state-cached reasoning; SR fixes it) was already documented by NVIDIA for FP16/INT8/FP8 caches and by Nazari et al. for INT8 on Qwen-3.5 and Nemotron-H. The paper's genuine deltas are the bf16 case (which prior evidence was mixed on: LeapQuant said SR "helps little", DAMP blamed resolution), the same-width SR argument that cleanly separates bias from resolution, the zero-overhead hash-RNG SR, the 128K non-termination/loop analysis, and the closed-loop head localization with its negative result against teacher-forced KL ranking. This is a careful replication-plus-extension, not a conceptual breakthrough.
- **Rigor:** High. Pre-registration, paired statistics, matched-concurrency controls, seed replication, and honest reporting of failed tests. The exact-loop definition (fixed period, ≥ 2 periods, 192 tokens) and the math-verify grading of capped runs are stated precisely.
- **Reproducibility:** Good but not complete. Models, engine version (SGLang 0.5.21), GPU type, sampling settings, running caps, and the bit-level SR store formula are all published; but most arms use a single seed, the patched kernels are not stated to be publicly released, and the pre-registration notebook is not externally timestamped — a stated limitation.
- **Significance:** Practically important for anyone serving hybrid models (a silent dtype default can cost 12–15 points on math/code benchmarks), and the evaluation recommendations (report dtype, cap rate, length; rerun capped items) are broadly useful. The finding that teacher-forced KL misranks head groups matters for mixed-precision allocation research.
- **Red flags / weaknesses:** (1) The Nemotron-3 anomaly — full SR removes the runaway tail but leaves an unexplained ×1.05 bulk shift also present against a second FP32 seed — is left open, and SR is presented as a fix while failing the one MoE model. (2) Head localization rests on a single lineage (9B + 12B, same family) with controls run only on the 9B; the Qwen3.5-9B band tests all fail, so the "band" story is narrow. (3) The Falcon-H1R-7B null is unexplained, which slightly undercuts generality claims. (4) LiveCodeBench pass@1 numbers are labeled exploratory, and capped code samples were not rerun at larger budget, so the code-side budget/loop split is unmeasured. (5) All results come from one engine on one GPU type (8× RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell) with competition math and one code benchmark — no long-context/agentic workloads where state drift might matter more. (6) Qwen3.5-9B caps 48% of FP32 LiveCodeBench samples, which the authors themselves note compresses its length ratio toward 1 (×1.20 on the uncapped subset vs ×1.09 overall).

## Strengths

- Clean causal separation of rounding *bias* from format *resolution* via same-width bf16 SR — a genuinely well-designed control that resolves conflicting prior reports (LeapQuant vs DAMP).
- The integer-hash RNG makes SR practically free (10,393 vs 10,163 tokens/s end-to-end on Qwen3.5-9B), turning a diagnostic into a deployable fix at bf16 memory footprint (2× the concurrent requests of FP32 state).
- The 128K rerun design cleanly decomposes the accuracy loss into budget-limited verbosity versus non-termination (85% of never-finishing runs are exact loops), and exposes how both-finished and truncation-waste metrics hide the second failure.
- Rare methodological honesty: pre-registered criteria published with failures included (Nemotron-3 SR F_log=0.38; Qwen3.5-9B band tests), plus concrete negative results (teacher-forced KL misranks heads; band concentration does not transfer across architectures).
- Actionable evaluation guidance for the community: report state dtype/rounding mode, cap rate, and output length with accuracy — especially timely given silently diverging engine defaults (vLLM bf16 vs SGLang FP32 for the same config).

## Limitations

- Single seed for most arms; single engine version (SGLang 0.5.21) on a single GPU type; only 32 rerun items for the Nemotron-Nano-9B-v2 128K analysis.
- Coverage is competition math (all six models) plus competitive programming (two models); no long-context, agentic, or open-ended generation tasks where a drifting recurrent state could behave differently.
- SR applies to decode only — prefill state writes still use RTN (once per request here, but potentially more often in chunked/multi-turn serving).
- The GDN SR kernel with default Philox is slow (30–60% of RTN decode speed); the fast hash variant was validated end-to-end on Qwen3.5-9B only, not on Qwen3-Next or the code benchmark.
- Head localization is confirmed in one model lineage; the decay-band strategy fails on Nemotron-3 (MoE) and Qwen3.5-9B (GDN), where the effect is spread across heads, so no general precision-allocation recipe emerges.
- The Falcon-H1R-7B null and the Nemotron-3 residual bulk shift are both unexplained; the toy integrator explains the band's upper edge but not its lower edge.

## Relevance

（与混合架构推理模型的数值精度/推理效率研究方向高度相关：论文系统性地量化了循环状态缓存的 bf16 舍入偏差如何通过拉长思维链、增加触顶与重复循环来损害 Mamba-2/GDN 混合模型的数学与代码基准表现，并给出了零开销的随机舍入修复方案与评估规范建议。）

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Why is Falcon-H1R-7B (parallel attention+Mamba per layer) immune to the bf16 RTN effect while the sequential hybrids are not — is it the parallel mixing, the state size, or its decay-rate distribution?
- What causes Nemotron-3's residual ×1.05 bulk length shift that survives full-head SR, and is it related to the MoE routing path rather than recurrent-state rounding?
- Does the bias-induced lengthening and exact-loop non-termination appear in long-context and agentic workloads (tool calls, retrieval over long horizons), where state drift has more steps to accumulate than 32K?
- Can the hash-RNG SR approach be extended to INT8/FP8 state caches to recover most of the 4× memory saving at FP32-like fidelity, and does the bias-versus-resolution split hold at 8 bits?
- Is there a closed-loop, generation-based proxy cheaper than full benchmark reruns for validating per-head precision allocation (given that teacher-forced KL misranked head groups)?
- Do the exact repetition loops under bf16 RTN share a mechanistic signature with overthinking loops under weight quantization (Lotfi et al.'s "right answer reached but not emitted" failures), suggesting a common numerical-origin failure mode?
