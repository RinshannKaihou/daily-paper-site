> **一句话总结：** 本文证明最后一个残差流上的方向消融（directional ablation）严格等价于一个"余弦门控的 logit 偏置"，其三种失败模式（欠火、误伤良性文本、输出崩溃）都源于方向与隐状态共模（common mode）的重叠 ω；提出的闭式修正 Centered Ablation（CA）把共模从方向中投影掉，在 Llama-3.1-8B / Llama-3.2-3B 上把 HarmBench 流畅攻击成功率从 33.6%→62.6%、10.3%→56.7%，把 Gemma-2-9B 的崩溃率从 50% 降到 1%，且 ω、η、β 三个诊断量可在生成之前预测哪个模型会受益。

> **TL;DR:** The authors prove that directional ablation at the last residual stream is exactly a cosine-gated logit bias (Proposition 3.1), and that the standard difference-in-means (DIM) direction fails—under-firing on Llama models, collapsing Gemma-2-9B—because it overlaps the common mode of last-layer states (overlap ω). Centered Ablation (CA) projects the common mode out of the direction in closed form (two means, one projection, no new hyperparameters): on HarmBench (n=240) it raises fluent-only ASR from 33.6% to 62.6% on Llama-3.1-8B (+29.0 points) and 10.3% to 56.7% on Llama-3.2-3B (+46.3) versus DIM, cuts Gemma-2-9B collapse from 50% to 1% at unchanged ASR, and preserves 12–40 points more benign Alpaca answers than DIM on high-ω models (39–46 points more than the ACE-style offset). Two cached-activation diagnostics (ω for faithfulness, η for attack success) predicted, out-of-sample on 5 held-out models, which models would benefit, and a collapse index β⁺>1 flags the one collapsing configuration. Bonus finding: HarmBench classifier vs Llama-Guard-3-8B score collapsed text in opposite directions (1.7% vs 98.3% "harmful"), which can fabricate a 46-point effect.

## 核心贡献

- **精确透镜（Proposition 3.1）**：证明了在最后一个残差流上，对方向 u、强度 s 的消融严格等价于对 logits 施加一个固定词表足迹 f_u = W_U G u 乘以标量门 κ_u(h)（隐状态与方向的余弦）的偏置——即闭式的"余弦门控 logit 偏置"。该闭式在 8 个模型、144 个实验单元上预测实际推理引擎（vLLM）的贪心首 token 准确率 96.1%，预测与实测拒答率 Pearson 相关 0.95。
- **失败机制的统一解释**：最后层隐状态共享一个远离原点的大共模（common mode）；DIM 方向与共模的重叠 ω 同时造成三种症状——在良性文本上误触发（改写良性答案、超过 logit 范围后输出崩溃）、在目标行为上欠火（类判别成分被稀释）、以及随 s 增大的崩溃。
- **闭式修正 Centered Ablation（CA）**：把良性类的共模方向从 DIM 方向中原位投影掉（u_CA = unit(I − μ̂₋μ̂₋ᵀ)u_DIM），仅需两个样本均值加一次投影，无任何新超参数；证明 ω(u_CA)=0（门无底噪）且保留全部类判别信息中除共模分量外的部分。
- **与 ACE 偏置路线的推导对比**：ACE 式偏移（保留方向、平移原点）与 CA 是"居中门控"的两条路线；推导出偏移路线在目标状态上的平均门比 CA 大 1/√(1−ω²) 倍（8 个模型全部验证，最大误差 0.06），但在良性状态上继承 ω² 比例的共模方差——即偏移路线用良性误伤换攻击效果，CA 用较小效果换忠实性。
- **生成前诊断量**：ω 预测良性答案保留（Spearman 0.98）、η（DIM 已改变贪心 token 的比例）预测 ASR 增益（Pearson −0.95，η=0.6 分界）、崩溃指数 β⁺=1 是崩溃阈值（144 单元中仅 6 个 β⁺>1 且恰为全部崩溃单元）。5 个后补模型的诊断与预测在生成任何文本前记录，属真正出样本验证（4/5 命中）。
- **评测方法学警示**：两个标准安全评判器对崩溃文本打分方向完全相反（HarmBench 分类器 1.7% vs Llama-Guard 98.3% 判"harmful"），不加流畅度过滤会凭空制造 46 点的假效应；多选题基准（MMLU 仅动 ≤0.7 分）也看不到最高 88% 的自由文本改写——建议报告 fluent-only 成功率 + 崩溃率 + 自由文本忠实度。
- **算子能力边界**：给出秩-1 消融的容量界并实验确认——拒答（少数 token 决定）可控，而情绪（弥散于全文）只能被"侵蚀"（s=3 时 CA 删掉 60% 情绪词但评判情绪仅降 19 点，同时改写 53–62% 无关良性答案）；Gemma-2-9B 拒答的第二阶段（语义性回避）秩-1 方向无法捕获。

## Key Contributions

- **An exact lens (Proposition 3.1):** At the last residual stream, directional ablation of a unit direction u at strength s is *exactly* a logit bias — a fixed vocabulary footprint f_u = W_U G u scaled by the gate κ_u(h), the cosine between the hidden state and the direction. The closed form predicts the served vLLM model's steered greedy first token on 96.1% of prompts across all 144 cells, with predicted vs. observed refusal rates correlating at Pearson 0.95.
- **One mechanism for three failure modes:** Last-layer states share a large common mode far from the origin; the overlap ω of the DIM direction with that common mode simultaneously causes (i) firing on benign text (rewrites, then collapse once the bias exceeds the logit spread), (ii) under-firing on targets (the class-separating budget of a unit vector is partly spent on the common mode), and (iii) collapse at a threshold β⁺ = 1.
- **Centered Ablation (CA), a closed-form fix:** Project the benign common mode out of the direction (u_CA = unit(I − μ̂₋μ̂₋ᵀ)u_DIM). Two sample means, one projection, no optimization, no hyperparameter beyond the strength. Proven: ω(u_CA) = 0 (no gate floor) while retaining all class separation except the common-mode share.
- **Derivation, not just comparison, vs. ACE:** The affine offset (ACE's route) centres the gate by shifting the origin, keeping the direction. Derived and verified: the offset's mean harmful gate exceeds CA's by exactly the factor 1/√(1−ω²) on all 8 models (largest error 0.06), but on benign states it inherits an ω² share of common-mode variance that CA removes — the offset buys attack success with benign collateral precisely on high-ω models.
- **Pre-generation diagnostics:** Three numbers computed from cached activations and the matrices W_U, G — overlap ω (predicts benign answers preserved, Spearman 0.98), efficacy η (predicts ASR gain, Pearson −0.95, regime split at η = 0.6), and collapse index β⁺ (threshold at 1; exactly the 6 of 144 cells with β⁺ > 1 are the collapsing ones). Recorded for 5 models *before* they generated any text: 4 of 5 regime predictions correct.
- **Evaluation-methodology finding:** On degenerate (collapsed) outputs the HarmBench classifier judges "harmful" 1.7% of the time while Llama-Guard-3-8B says 98.3% — without a fluency filter this fabricates a 46.4-point effect on Gemma-2-9B (64.8% vs. 18.4%) that vanishes with the filter (15.8% vs. 17.3%, n.s.). Multiple-choice MMLU (≤0.7-point moves) is blind to up to 88% free-form rewrites. Recommendation: report fluent-only success + collapse rate + free-form faithfulness.
- **A capacity boundary for rank-1 ablation:** Behaviours decided at a few tokens (refusal onset, η = 0.70–0.97) are controllable; diffuse behaviours (emotion, η = 0.05–0.15) only erode — at s = 3 CA removes 60% of emotion words but lowers judged emotion by only 19 points while rewriting 53–62% of benign answers. Gemma-2-9B's second refusal stage (semantic deflection) is likewise not rank-1-capturable.

## Methodology

The paper fixes one operator at one site and derives it exactly. At the residual stream h after the last decoder block, the only path to the next token is RMSNorm followed by the unembedding (the edit never enters the KV cache), so the steered logit vector is ℓ(A_s^u(h)) = τ_s(h)·[ℓ(h) − s√d·κ_u(h)·f_u], where f_u = W_U G u is the direction's vocabulary footprint, κ_u(h) = cos∠(h, u) is the gate, and τ_s(h) is a positive rescaling irrelevant to the greedy argmax. Because κ is a cosine measured from the origin and last-layer states cluster along a shared common mode μ̂₋ (the normalized benign mean), a DIM direction u_DIM = unit(μ₊ − μ₋) spends an ω-sized share of its unit budget on that common mode: it fires on benign text (rewrites; collapse when the bias exceeds the logit spread, indexed by β) and under-fires on harmful states.

**Centered Ablation (Algorithm 1):** compute u_DIM from 256 AdvBench vs. 256 Alpaca prompt-final states; compute the benign common mode μ̂₋ = unit(mean of rms-normalized benign states); project it out: u_CA = unit(u_DIM − (u_DIMᵀμ̂₋)μ̂₋); ablate along u_CA at every generated position. The offset baseline (DIM+offset) instead keeps u_DIM and subtracts the benign class's own coefficient (ACE's pure-ablation form at this site); the paper derives that the two routes agree only when ω ≈ 0.

**Diagnostics (no generation needed):** overlap ω(u_DIM); efficacy η(u) = fraction of harmful states whose greedy token the edit already flips; collapse indices β±. These were computed for the 5 models added after the 3-model pilot, with predicted regimes recorded before any text was generated.

**Experimental setup:** 8 instruction-tuned models from 3 families — Qwen2.5-{0.5B, 3B, 7B}, Qwen3-4B, Llama-3.1-8B, Llama-3.2-3B, Gemma-2-{2B, 9B}. Directions calibrated on 256 AdvBench vs. 256 Alpaca prompts with 6 resampled calibration draws; main matrix 8 models × 3 directions × 6 draws = 144 cells. ASR on HarmBench (n = 240) judged by the HarmBench classifier and Llama-Guard-3-8B; benign faithfulness on 100 held-out Alpaca prompts (rewrite rate = string similarity < 0.5 to the unsteered answer); capability via MMLU (1000, scored exactly at the logit level through Proposition 3.1) and GSM8K (100). Greedy decoding, 256 new tokens; degenerate output := ≥200 bytes compressing (zlib) below 0.2 of its length; primary ASR is fluent-only. Statistics: paired prompt-level bootstrap (5000 replicates) + exact McNemar per draw. A second pipeline reruns everything unchanged on 4 emotions, and refusal amplification (s < 0, CA-Amp / DIM-Amp) is tested on jailbreak templates with XSTest over-refusal. Ablations check kernel-MMD erasure (reduces to a length-reweighted DIM; after centring, cos ≥ 0.999 with CA), shrinkage LDA (most discriminative = worst to ablate), and alternative centring choices.

## Results & Highlights

- **The reduction is exact in practice:** the closed form predicts the served model's steered first token on **96.1% of prompts across all 144 cells** (96.3–99.9% on seven models; the one shortfall is Gemma-2-9B at 82.1%, explained by semantic deflection in later tokens); predicted vs. observed refusal rates correlate at **Pearson 0.95** across cells.
- **Under-firing fixed:** on HarmBench (fluent-only, HarmBench classifier), CA raises ASR over standard DIM ablation from **33.6% to 62.6% on Llama-3.1-8B (+29.0 [22.9, 35.1])** and from **10.3% to 56.7% on Llama-3.2-3B (+46.3 [40.3, 52.4])**, significant on 6/6 draws under both judges; refusal-opening rate on Llama-3.2-3B drops from 86% to 19%. On the other six models ASR moves ≤2.6 points (never significant).
- **Collapse fixed:** on Gemma-2-9B, DIM degenerates on **50% of prompts vs. 1% for CA** at the same fluent-only ASR (14.0% vs. 14.4%).
- **Collateral fixed on high-ω models:** on the four models with ω > 0.3, CA preserves **12 to 40 points more benign Alpaca answers** than DIM; on the four with ω < 0.13 the difference is ≤5 points. CA vs. the offset route: DIM+offset rewrites **39–46 points more benign answers than CA** on the three models with ω > 0.33 (its excess tracks ω at Pearson 0.80), and its mean harmful gate exceeds CA's by the predicted **1/√(1−ω²)** on all 8 models (largest error 0.06).
- **No capability loss:** MMLU (1000 questions, scored exactly at the logit level) moves **≤0.7 points** for every model and direction; GSM8K stays within noise.
- **Out-of-sample prediction:** ω predicts benign answers preserved (Spearman 0.98, Pearson 0.90, n = 8) and η predicts ASR gain (Pearson −0.95); the two models below η = 0.6 gain +29.0 and +46.3 points while the six above lie in [−2.6, +2.4]; the pre-registered regime labels for the 5 second-stage models scored 4/5.
- **Collapse is a threshold at β⁺ = 1:** exactly 6 of 144 cells have β⁺ > 1 (all Gemma-2-9B under DIM, β⁺ = 1.03 ± 0.01) with mean collapse rate 49.8%; the other 138 cells have β⁺ ≤ 0.90 and mean collapse 0.6% (max 4.2%).
- **Judges disagree on collapsed text:** on Gemma-2-9B under DIM, Llama-Guard scores ASR 64.8% vs. CA's 18.4% without a fluency filter (a fabricated −46.4-point "loss" for CA, significant on 6/6 draws); with the filter it vanishes (15.8% vs. 17.3%, n.s.). On the same 120 degenerate outputs, the HarmBench classifier says "harmful" 1.7% of the time, Llama-Guard 98.3%.
- **Refusal amplification (defence):** on Gemma-2-9B, DIM-Amp is 31% collapsed at s = −1 and 95–100% collapsed at |s| ≥ 1.5, while CA-Amp stays fluent to s = −2 and reaches **ASR 14.1% at s = −3**; on Llama-3.1-8B, DIM-Amp floors at 3.3% ASR where CA-Amp reaches **0.5%**.
- **Diffuse behaviours only erode:** for 4 emotions × 8 models, η under the centred direction is 0.05–0.15 (vs. 0.70–0.97 for refusal); at s = 3 CA removes 60% of emotion words but lowers judged emotion by only 19 points while rewriting 53–62% of unrelated benign answers.

## Authors & Credibility

Quy-Anh Dang and Chris Ngo are at Knovel Engineering Lab (Singapore), an independent lab rather than a large academic institution; this is an arXiv preprint (v1, 15 Nov 2026, cs.CL) and is not yet peer-reviewed. The same authors have prior peer-reviewed work in this area (Selective steering: norm-preserving control through discriminative layer selection, Findings of ACL 2026), which strengthens their familiarity with the steering literature. Credibility signals are unusually strong for a preprint: full proofs in the appendix, a two-stage design where diagnostics and regime predictions for the 5 added models were recorded before any generation (a genuine out-of-sample test), two independent safety judges, fluency filters, paired bootstrap (5000 replicates) plus exact McNemar tests, exact logit-level MMLU evaluation, public benchmarks only, code and artifacts released (github.com/knoveleng/centered-ablation), and an explicit Broader Impact Statement acknowledging the dual-use nature of refusal-removal research.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** The exact reduction (Proposition 3.1) — last-layer ablation as a cosine-gated logit bias — is genuinely new: prior steering work (activation addition, ACE, LEACE/INLP erasure, conditional steering) evaluated edits behaviourally without deriving their effect on the output distribution, and prior anisotropy work (all-but-the-top, rogue dimensions, massive activations) never connected the common mode to intervention *selectivity*. CA itself is a simple projection (cos ≥ 0.999 with centred kernel-MMD erasure, as their own ablation admits), so the fix is less novel than the analysis that justifies and predicts it — the paper is honest that the contribution is the lens plus diagnostics, not a fancy new operator.
- **Rigor:** High. Verified quantitative law (the 1/√(1−ω²) gate ratio confirmed on all 8 models within 0.06), pre-registered out-of-sample predictions, per-draw exact McNemar, capability checks at the exact logit level, and a careful dissection of judge disagreement. The negative results (emotion only erodes; Gemma-2-9B's second-stage deflection is not rank-1) are reported rather than hidden.
- **Reproducibility:** Strong. Stdlib-friendly public benchmarks (AdvBench, Alpaca, HarmBench, MMLU, GSM8K, XSTest), greedy decoding, released code and judge prompts in appendices, exact hyperparameters (256/256 calibration prompts, 6 draws, s = 1).
- **Significance:** Practically useful for anyone deploying steering: compute ω, η, β from cached activations before choosing DIM / CA / offset. The evaluation-methodology finding (two standard judges scoring collapsed text oppositely, fabricating a 46-point effect) is independently valuable for the jailbreak-evaluation literature.
- **Red flags / weaknesses:** (1) *Site mismatch with practice* — the canonical refusal-direction recipe (Arditi et al., 2024) ablates at an earlier/mid-network layer, and ACE's offset is evaluated by its authors at a mid-network layer; this paper's closed form holds *only* at the last residual stream, so "DIM+offset" here is not ACE at its authors' setting, and whether centring helps at mid-depth layers (where most steering is actually done) is untested and explicitly disclaimed. (2) *Small-n laws* — the η = 0.6 regime split rests on only two models below the threshold, and the collapse threshold β⁺ ∈ (0.90, 1.03] is bracketed by exactly one crossing model (Gemma-2-9B); the authors admit both. (3) The out-of-sample regime prediction scored 4/5, with the miss (Gemma-2-2B) explained post-hoc. (4) Greedy decoding only — under sampling, τ_s acts as a temperature and the analysis does not carry over. (5) Model pool is ≤9B and three families; no test of whether ω/η/β transfer to larger or other-family models. (6) CA is not a stronger *attack* than the offset (its ASR is lower on 3 models) — its claim is faithfulness, and the security upside rests on CA-Amp, which is presented as a trade-off with over-refusal, not dominance.

## Strengths

- Derives, rather than measures, what a widely reused edit does to the output distribution, with the derivation empirically validated to 96.1% first-token agreement against a real serving stack (vLLM).
- One quantity (overlap ω) unifies three previously model-specific symptoms (under-firing, collateral rewrites, collapse), and the fix follows from the analysis rather than from tuning.
- Diagnostics are cheap (cached activations + W_U, G, no generation, no judge calls) and were validated out-of-sample under a recorded-before-generation protocol.
- Fair, derived comparison with the prior fix (ACE's offset) that identifies exactly when each route wins, rather than claiming universal dominance.
- The judge-disagreement finding (1.7% vs 98.3% on degenerate text) and the MMLU-blindness finding give concrete, actionable evaluation guidance: fluent-only success + collapse rate + free-form faithfulness.
- Honest negative results and a crisp capacity bound separating token-local behaviours (refusal) from diffuse ones (emotion).

## Limitations

- The closed form holds only for rank-1 ablation at the last residual stream under greedy decoding; mid-depth and additive steering — where much practical steering happens — are out of scope, and the ACE comparison is at this paper's site, not ACE's.
- Empirical "laws" rest on 8 models (three families, ≤9B): the η = 0.6 split has two supporting models and the β⁺ = 1 collapse threshold only one crossing model, so thresholds are bracketed, not pinned.
- Out-of-sample regime prediction was 4/5 (Gemma-2-2B mislabelled as an ASR gainer when it was a faithfulness gainer).
- CA trades attack strength for faithfulness: on three models its fluent-only ASR is significantly below the offset route's (e.g. by 12.4 points on the 3B models), so it is not a uniformly stronger jailbreak or defence.
- Diffuse behaviours (emotion) cannot be removed without rewriting half of unrelated benign answers — an operator limit, not an implementation bug, but a limit of the whole method family.
- Only English instruction-tuned chat models; sampling-based decoding untested.

## Relevance

（中文，1–2 句）对研究 LLM 可解释性与推理时行为控制（activation steering / refusal direction）的方向高度相关：本文给出了方向消融的首个精确闭式刻画、一个即插即用的忠实性修正（CA）和三个生成前诊断量，可直接用于改进我们自己的 steering 实验设计与安全评测协议。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does centring the direction (projecting out the common mode) also help at mid-depth layers, where the standard Arditi et al. recipe and ACE actually operate and no closed form exists — or does the common-mode story change once the edit propagates through attention?
- How does the analysis extend to sampling-based decoding, where the rescaling τ_s acts as a temperature — does CA change the effective temperature or diversity of steered generations?
- Can a rank-k (higher-rank) ablation capture Gemma-2-9B's second-stage semantic deflection, which rank-1 provably cannot, or is a per-state switch between directions needed?
- Do ω, η, β transfer to models beyond 9B and outside the three tested families (e.g. Mistral, Phi, larger Qwen/Llama), and can the η = 0.6 regime split be sharpened with a larger model pool?
- Could the collapse index β be used as an online monitor during deployment to auto-disable a steering vector before text degenerates, instead of only as an offline diagnostic?
- How many published jailbreak-evaluation results are contaminated by judge disagreement on degenerate text — would re-scoring prior work with a fluency filter change their conclusions?
- Is the common mode μ̂₋ stable across domains (code, multilingual prompts) and over training checkpoints, i.e., how often would CA's calibration need refreshing?
