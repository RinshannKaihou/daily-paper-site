> **一句话总结：** 本文提出"排放受控双目标探测"(ECDP)协议，在思维链中干扰项即将被写出的前一个 token 处，用同一低秩读出器分别探测"下一个将提及的候选"与"最终答案"：在 DecisionDepth 任务上 Qwen3.5-4B 可将即将提及项解码至 .966（零假设 .234）而最终答案仅 .221（零假设 .186），排放差距达 .696；但在 Qwen3-8B 的 MMLU 全前缀注意力读出器下顺序完全反转（答案提升 .628 vs 提及提升 .230）——说明"答案可解码"是相对于目标与协议的性质，本身并不能证明模型已经做出了决定。

> **TL;DR:** A answer-probe hit on chain-of-thought hidden states is usually read as "the model has decided," but just before any candidate is written the state must carry that candidate's name. Fitting the same rank-4 bilinear reader to two targets at identical positions (ECDP), the authors show that on DecisionDepth (Qwen3.5-4B, n=353) the probe reads the imminent mention at .966 (null .234) but the final answer at only .221 (null .186) — an emission gap of .696 [.648, .744]; Llama-3.1-8B gives .575 and BBH Logical Deduction gives .511, against a planted-signal control showing the reader can detect an injected answer signal at one-tenth dose (.317 vs observed excess .035). Crucially, a pre-specified counterexample (Qwen3-8B on MMLU, full-prefix attention reader over all thinking positions) reverses the order — final-answer lift .628 vs mention lift .230 at layer 22, paired difference −.398 [−.459, −.336] — so neither pattern is a general law: answer decodability is target-relative and protocol-dependent, and a strong answer readout is not by itself evidence of a settled decision.

## 核心贡献

- **提出 ECDP（排放受控双目标探测）协议**：在同一位置、同一读出器族（秩-4 双线性打分器）下分别拟合两个目标——即将被提及的候选 $M_t$ 与最终答案 $F$，并为两个目标使用不同的零假设（$M_t$ 按构造是非金候选，用更严的 $E[1/(K-1)]$；$F$ 用 $E[1/K]$），阈值与"排放差距" $\Delta_{emit}$ 在拟合前固定。
- **实证证明"即将写出什么"压倒性地主导读出结果**：在 DecisionDepth (DD) 的预先答案干扰项前提及位置上，Qwen3.5-4B 读出下一个提及达 .966 而最终答案仅 .221（$\Delta_{emit}=.696$），Llama-3.1-8B 同样给出 .575 的差距；BBH Logical Deduction 次要集上差距 .511。即：放在候选名之前的探针读到的是"下一个要写的词"，不是"结论"。
- **配备系统性效度控制**：植入信号控制（在 DD 上读出器能恢复十分之一植入剂量的最终答案信号，.317 > 观测超额 .035）、读出器容量研究（6.3M 参数 MLP 训练精度升至 .959 但 held-out 始终停在 .157–.193，未达预设阈值 .226）、标签打乱与位置对照——区分"表征携带什么"与"读出器能拟合什么"。
- **报告了一个预设的反例**：Qwen3-8B 在 MMLU、启用思考、全前缀注意力读出器下，两个目标均可解码且最终答案远强于即将提及项（提升 .628 vs .230，$M_t - F = -.398$），证明该现象绝非普适规律；并如实报告了未完成的配对交叉比较（DD 上即将提及探针训练不可靠，无有效统计量）及其失败模式。
- **严格区分可解码性与因果性**：梯度干预未通过自身仪器检查（在答案槽位转向 0/400 改变答案），全状态互换仅为探索性；明确提出"探针训练失败不是信息缺失的证据"这一方法论原则，并给出廉价实用的协议建议（双目标 + 各自零假设 + 位置/打乱对照 + 植入灵敏度标定）。

## Key Contributions

- **ECDP protocol (Emission-Controlled Dual-target Probing):** fits the same low-rank reader family separately to the imminent mention $M_t$ and the final answer $F$ at identical positions, with target-specific nulls ($E[1/(K-1)]$ for $M_t$, which is a non-gold candidate by construction; $E[1/K]$ for $F$) and thresholds fixed before any fit.
- **Evidence that imminent emission dominates standard answer probing at pre-mention positions:** on DecisionDepth, Qwen3.5-4B decodes the next mention at .966 (null .234, z = 32.4) vs the final answer at .221 (null .186, z = 1.70, below the z = 3 screening threshold), an emission gap of .696 [.648, .744]; Llama-3.1-8B independently shows .575 [.514, .632]; a layer sweep (16–32) shows $M_t$ readable from layer 20 while $F$ stays below threshold everywhere.
- **A battery of validity controls rare in probing papers:** planted-signal sensitivity (the reader recovers an injected final-answer embedding at one-tenth dose), a capacity curve (diagonal scorer → 6.3M-param MLP: train accuracy .300→.959 while held-out stays .157–.193), label shuffles, and random-position controls.
- **An honest pre-specified counterexample:** on Qwen3-8B with thinking enabled, evaluated on held-out MMLU with the full-prefix attention reader over every thinking position, both targets are readable and the final answer dominates (lift .628 vs .230 at layer 22; paired difference −.398 [−.459, −.336] over 467 questions) — with post-hoc analyses showing the F readout follows the model's answer, not the gold label, and appears before F is ever written.
- **A transparent account of what failed:** the matched model×task cross-comparison is incomplete (imminent-mention probes did not train on DD under the full-prefix protocol in either model), the gradient-steering apparatus failed its gate, and every result is assigned an explicit evidence tier (pre-specified / limited / exploratory / not-supporting).

## Methodology

**Tasks.** DecisionDepth (DD) is a synthetic multi-candidate reasoning task with three families (rule chains, conditional code-name chains, value intervals), depth-2, with candidates generated symmetrically so no surface feature identifies the answer. The public task is BIG-Bench Hard Logical Deduction, five- and seven-object subsets (the three-object subset is excluded because K = 3 risks a ceiling); MMLU/MMLU-Redux are used in the reasoning-model sections.

**Models and rollouts.** Main analysis: Qwen3.5-4B and Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct with thinking disabled, 2048-token rationale budget, prompted to end with an "Answer:" line. On DD, Qwen3.5-4B is correct on .987 of 1040 rollouts (chance .180); Llama-3.1-8B answers directly at .178 (chance .179) and .511 with a rationale. On BBH, Qwen3.5-4B scores .580 direct / .850 with rationale (chance .167). Only correct rollouts are analyzed, so F equals the gold answer.

**Reader.** The layer-32 hidden state $h_t$ (centered with training-fold statistics, parameter-free layer-normed) is scored by a rank-4 bilinear reader $s_k = (Ux)^\top(Va_k)$ with candidates embedded by the same model at the same layer — only 20,480 parameters for Qwen3.5-4B (d = 2560) and 32,768 for Llama-3.1-8B (d = 4096), deliberately small so capacity can be controlled. Trained with five item-level folds; weight decay chosen on a calibration slice; held-out fold never used for selection.

**Positions and the dual targets.** For each rollout the authors locate every mention of a non-gold candidate and take the token immediately before it. The primary set uses the first distractor mention that precedes any mention of the gold candidate (so the answer has not yet appeared in text); the secondary set uses the first distractor mention anywhere. At each position two targets are fitted: $M_t$ (the candidate named next) and $F$ (the final answer). Because $M_t \neq F$ by construction, a reader tracking emission scores on $M_t$ but not $F$.

**Pre-specified effect and decision rule.** The emission gap is $\Delta_{emit} = [Acc(M_t) - E[1/(K-1)]] - [Acc(F) - E[1/K]]$, with nominal 95% intervals from 10,000 item-level bootstrap resamples. The verdict "Emission Separated" requires $Acc(M_t)$ at z ≥ 3 and ≥ 0.15 above its null, $Acc(F)$ below z = 3, $\Delta_{emit} \geq 0.10$ with a lower bound above zero, on at least 150 items. Controls: label shuffle, a random non-mention position in the same item, and a planted positive control.

**Auxiliary designs.** (i) A layer sweep refitting the reader at layers 16/20/24/28. (ii) Planted signals: the gold candidate's embedding is added to states at doses 1.0 / 0.1 / 0.03. (iii) A capacity study training readers from a diagonal scorer up to a 6.3M-parameter MLP on the pre-rationale state. (iv) A natural-task null at the MMLU prompt position using four fixed slot codes as the candidate representation. (v) A reasoning-model setting (DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B, thinking on MMLU-Redux) with a 64-token attention-pooling window. (vi) The Qwen3-8B counterexample: full-prefix attention reader with position-0 masking, every thinking position where $M_t$ is defined and differs from F, trained on MMLU-Redux and evaluated on non-overlapping MMLU test questions, keeping both correct and incorrect rollouts, with question-level bootstrap CIs at 97.5% (Bonferroni over two layers). (vii) Causal attempts: gradient steering at layer 20 (α = 0.4 of state norm) and an exploratory full-state interchange.

## Results & Highlights

- **Imminent mention dominates on DecisionDepth (primary, pre-specified):** Qwen3.5-4B, n = 353 held-out items — next-mention accuracy .966 vs its null .234 (z = 32.4); final-answer accuracy .221 vs its null .186 (z = 1.70, below the z = 3 screen); emission gap $\Delta_{emit} = .696$ [.648, .744]. Llama-3.1-8B, n = 308: $M_t$ .867 (null .244) vs $F$ .240 (null .192), gap .575 [.514, .632] — same pattern in an independent model family.
- **BBH Logical Deduction (pre-specified fallback set):** Qwen3.5-4B, n = 379 — next mention .705 (null .211) vs final answer .166 (below its uniform null .173), gap .511 [.451, .569]. The pre-answer primary set (n = 61) falls below the 150-item floor and is reported as underpowered (gap .157 [.009, .305]).
- **The answer readout spikes exactly when the answer is about to be written:** aligned to the answer's first conclusion mention, F is decoded at .182 sixteen tokens before, .393 four tokens before, and .857 one token before (Qwen3.5-4B, DD) — and back to chance (.192) two tokens after a distractor mention; at the prompt and rationale midpoint it stays near chance (.178 / .181), reaching .569 only just before the answer line.
- **Planted-signal sensitivity control (DD):** with the gold embedding injected at full / 0.1 / 0.03 dose, the reader recovers F at .975 / .317 / .249 (Qwen3.5-4B) and .867 / .289 / .217 (Llama-3.1-8B), versus an observed F excess of only .035 / .054 — i.e., the pipeline is not blind to answer information at one-tenth of the planted dose; the $M_t$ readout is comparable to planting the full answer signal while F is comparable to planting almost none. (On BBH the one-tenth plant is at its null, so this sensitivity argument is DD-only.)
- **Capacity study:** training accuracy climbs from .300 (diagonal) to .959 (6.3M-param MLP) on the pre-rationale state, while held-out accuracy stays in .157–.193 across all 24 reader-by-checkpoint cells, never reaching the pre-specified hidden-signal threshold of .226 (null .176) — added capacity memorizes without generalizing.
- **Pre-specified counterexample (Qwen3-8B, MMLU, full-prefix reader):** at layer 22, lift over the majority baseline is .230 [.186, .274] for $M_t$ vs .628 [.576, .679] for F over 467 held-out questions; paired difference $M_t - F = -.398$ [−.459, −.336] (layer 31: −.403). The F probe tracks the model's own answer (.798 on the 102 incorrectly answered questions vs .115 for the gold letter), is present before any option letter is referenced (lift .402), and is not explained by copying recent text (copy baseline lift .175). The order reverses relative to DD.
- **Causal attempts were inconclusive:** gradient steering changed the answer at the answer slot in 0 of 400 items (apparatus gate failed, verdict "Uninterpretable"), while the exploratory full-state interchange at layer 20 made the next mention become the donor candidate in .700 of items vs .086 for a random-position donor (difference .615 [.562, .667]), with the eventual answer changing in only .005 of swapped items — reported as exploratory, with no pre-fixed equivalence bound.

## Authors & Credibility

The three authors — Hyojung Han (corresponding), Jongmin Kim, and Seung-Hun Jeon — are from ThakiCloud, an industry team rather than an academic lab; the paper is an arXiv preprint (v1, 9 Nov 2026, cs.LG) with no peer review. No public code, data, or preregistration is mentioned; the "pre-specified" analyses rest on a time-stamped internal record that the authors themselves acknowledge is non-public (hence "pre-specified" rather than "preregistered"). Counterbalancing this, the methodological self-reporting is unusually rigorous by the standards of the probing literature: every result is assigned an evidence tier (Section 11), failed experiments are reported with their failure modes rather than dropped (the two dead DD cells of the cross-comparison, the failed steering apparatus, the held verdict of the reasoning-model section), and the limitations section enumerates controls not yet run. The related-work coverage of probing validity (Hewitt & Liang control tasks, Belinkov's survey, Voita & Titov MDL probing, causal abstraction) and of early-answer-decodability work (Boppana et al. 2026, Xu et al. 2026, Scalena et al. 2026) is accurate and fair, explicitly declining to use their failed steering as evidence against others' causal findings.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** The imminent-emission confound is a genuinely sharp methodological point, and the dual-target design (with the stricter $1/(K-1)$ null for $M_t$) is an elegant, cheap control that prior CoT answer-probing work lacks. The general lesson — decodability ≠ decision — extends Hewitt & Liang / Belinkov-style probe-validity critique to a specific, currently active claim (early answer decodability used for early exit), which is a real contribution.
- **Rigor:** Internally very careful (target-specific nulls, screening thresholds fixed ex ante, planted-dose sensitivity bounds, three-seed robustness checks that left decision-set verdicts unchanged). But the pre-specification is unverifiable, the bootstrap intervals are within-seed item bootstraps that exclude training-seed variation and are not adjusted for multiplicity, and the z = 3 threshold is a screening rule, not a conventional test — the authors say all this themselves, which is commendable but does not remove the caveat.
- **External validity of the headline result:** the dominant gap (.696) comes from a synthetic task (DD) that the main model solves at .987 — near ceiling — and the analysis conditions on correct rollouts that contain a distractor mention before the gold answer; rollouts that never name a distractor first are excluded and "the results say nothing about them." Three of the four models share the Qwen lineage, so even the Llama replication leaves the model axis thinly covered.
- **The central question is left open by construction:** model, task, positions, and reader all change together between the DD setting and the MMLU counterexample, and the matched cross-comparison designed to separate them failed because the imminent-mention probe did not train reliably on DD (2 of 12 probes improved training loss for Qwen3.5-4B; one seed's loss *rose* for Qwen3-8B). The paper is honest that "we do not identify what reverses the order," but a reader of the abstract alone could miss how much of the design is incomplete.
- **Cherry-picking check:** the BBH primary set (n = 61) is underpowered and the BBH planted-signal control fails at one-tenth dose, so the sensitivity argument that licenses the DD interpretation is established on DD only — a scope limitation stated but worth flagging. The Qwen DD secondary verdict flipped from "separated" to "both readable" under three-seed medians, showing threshold sensitivity.
- **Causal claims:** appropriately retracted by the authors themselves — steering changed 0/400 answers at the slot where it was supposed to work (while acting at 20/400 distractor positions), and the interchange's swap success is, as they note, close to expected because the pre-mention state must carry next-token information anyway.
- **Red flags:** single-company authorship without academic co-authors or peer review; no artifact release; the abstract's strongest numbers come from the authors' own synthetic task while the public-task evidence is weaker in kind. None of this invalidates the finding, but independent replication would be needed before treating ECDP as standard practice.

## Strengths

- Identifies and operationalizes a confound (imminent emission) that genuinely threatens a active line of work on early answer decodability and early exit — with a control that costs almost nothing to add.
- Statistically careful: target-specific nulls, pre-fixed thresholds and verdict rules, item-level bootstrap intervals, and conservative pooled z-statistics where candidate counts vary.
- The control battery (planted signals at three doses, capacity curve to 6.3M parameters, label shuffles, position controls, layer sweep) is a model for what a probing paper's validity section should look like.
- Exceptional transparency: pre-specified counterexample reported against the authors' own main result; failed cells, failed apparatus checks, and post-hoc protocol changes all disclosed with five explicit evidence tiers.
- The takeaway is calibrated and useful: not "models don't decide early" but "a strong answer readout is not by itself evidence of a decision — run the dual-target control before interpreting."

## Limitations

- The main quantitative result rests on a synthetic, near-ceiling task (DD, .987 accuracy) and on the narrow population of correct rollouts containing a pre-answer distractor mention; incorrect rollouts on DD were too few to analyze.
- The decisive question — which factor (model, task, positions, reader, labels) reverses the dominance — remains unanswered because both DD cells of the matched cross-comparison produced no valid statistics.
- The reasoning-model section's verdict is "held" (its shuffle control failed: shuffled accuracy .426 > 1/3 null because models discuss options in order), and the Qwen3.5-4B MMLU cell is exploratory post-hoc, with one invalid final-answer probe contaminating its layer-27 average.
- Pre-specification is internal and non-public; intervals exclude training-seed variation and are unadjusted for multiplicity; the original pre-specified analysis used a single reader seed.
- Causal status is entirely unresolved: the steering apparatus failed and the interchange is exploratory with no pre-registered effect bound, so nothing in the paper bears on whether the imminent mention plays any role in producing the decision.
- Model coverage is 4B–8B parameters with three of four models sharing the Qwen lineage; the layer sweep covers Qwen3.5-4B only; no code or data release is mentioned.

## Relevance

直接命中"LLM Hidden States & Interpretability"方向：这是关于 CoT 隐状态探测方法论本身的核心论文，其双目标探测 + 植入信号灵敏度的受控协议，对任何用 probe 从 LLM 内部状态读出"答案/信念/决策"的可解释性工作都是直接的实验设计警示与可复用的对照方案。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Which factor drives the reversal — model, task, positions, reader, or label definition? A completed 2×2 cross (model × task) requires first building a reader/optimization setup that demonstrably learns the DD imminent-mention target; what property of the full-prefix attention reader makes DD $M_t$ untrainable while MMLU $M_t$ trains?
- Does imminent-emission dominance hold on incorrect rollouts (where F ≠ gold), or at higher DD depths where the model is not at .987 accuracy?
- Would order-aware nulls (models discussing options in enumerated order, as the failed Section 7 shuffle control suggests) change any "held" or borderline verdicts, and does the shuffle-null failure itself reveal that next-option labels are partially predictable from position alone?
- Is there a native final-answer code along directions other than the planted candidate-embedding direction, or below the one-thirtieth planted dose — e.g., recoverable with SAE features or with the probe logits' held-out cross-entropy rather than accuracy?
- Can an on-manifold causal intervention with a passing positive control and a pre-registered effect bound test whether changing the imminent mention ever changes the eventual answer beyond the observed .005 vs .003 (a difference the current exploratory interchange cannot interpret)?
