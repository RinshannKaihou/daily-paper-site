Why this matters

Multi-turn agents must keep task state in a verified external store and monitor across sessions — never trust one conversation’s coherence dashboard.

⚠
Critical edge

The mitigation is an oracle — it shrinks context, removes replies, and injects correct state at once; with no user-only arm, Study 2 cannot attribute the collapse to the model’s own replies.

Methodology

  • Two pre-registered studies — analysis protocols frozen before outcomes were computed; every result carries an evidence label (confirmatory / prespecified / exploratory / post hoc).
  • Three context policies at turn t: full dialogue history · fresh prefix (user turns only) · verified state summary (solver-rendered exact state + current turn).
  • Study 1 — 48 sharded GSM8K tasks (“Lost in Conversation” subset); 3 models answer every turn in both arms; K=5 samples, frozen rewordings; exact McNemar tests + Holm correction; all 11,382 calls succeeded.
  • Study 2 (CDR-Stream) — 100-turn state-tracking dialogues in 4 domains (budget ledger, warehouse, lab protocol, milestones); exact solver gold answer per turn; 12-level difficulty ladder calibrated per model; 5 schedules (2 stationary nulls + 3 scheduled changes); 30 held-out dialogues per model; one-sided sign-flip tests.
  • Monitoring framework — six RQEval quality dimensions + five drift detectors (CUSUM, Page–Hinkley, EWMA, DDM, ADWIN), thresholds calibrated on nulls to ≤5% false alarms; Proposition 1 derives a closed-form minimum detectable drop.

Gap = verified state summary − full history (sign-flip tests, Holm). The summary also shrinks the turn-100 prompt from tens of thousands of tokens to a few hundred.

  • Not a difficulty artifact — in stationary-easy dialogues (turns 21–100), history scored 0.10–0.14 vs 0.82–0.86 for the summary; at the same ladder level, late turns trail early turns by −0.118 (p<.001).
  • Quality metrics move the wrong way — stability rose 0.94 → 0.98 while accuracy fell 0.61 → 0.08: replies become more anchored, not more erratic.
  • Monitoring fails three ways — best calibrated detection rate 0.067; stationary nulls themselves collapsed; tuned rules failed frozen cross-model validation.
  • Power, not tuning — a single 100-turn stream detects only ~0.5 drops at 80% power (actual drifts 0.1–0.3); pooling 6–18 dialogues lowers the detectable drop to 0.2–0.1.

Strengths

  • Pre-registered, prospectively frozen analyses with per-result evidence labels — a standard rarely met in LLM evaluation papers.
  • CDR-Stream kills the “later questions are harder” confound via per-model difficulty calibration and a same-ladder-level early-vs-late contrast.
  • The negative monitoring result is established carefully: null-calibrated false alarms, cross-model transfer, and a closed-form power bound that explains the failure.
  • Full transparency: public code and protocols, disclosed deviations, and a disclosed ADWIN bug re-run with no change in conclusions.

Limitations

  • All tasks are arithmetic state tracking with exact answers; open-ended dialogue, retrieval, and tool use are untested.
  • The verified summary is solver-backed and not deployable as-is; no intermediate (self-maintained state files) was tested.
  • Model sets differ between studies; none are frontier models; repeat-based metrics were complete only for GPT-4o-mini in Study 2.
  • Monitoring beyond the frozen tests is exploratory or post hoc; one investigator performed the task audit and calibration; token/semantic entropy never collected.

Questions for further exploration

Q1Own replies vs sheer state load — what drives the collapse? A fresh-prefix or paraphrased-history 100-turn arm would decompose the mechanism.
Q2Would a model-maintained, periodically solver-verified state file recover most of the +0.52–0.59 gap, and at what verification cost?
Q3Do token-entropy (ERGO-style) or semantic-entropy signals give the early warning that consistency, coherence, and stability failed to provide?
Q4Does the collapse replicate with frontier models on a harder ladder, and in open-ended domains without exact gold answers?
Q5Can counterfactual re-queries be made cheap enough to serve as production-grade drift monitors, given pooled multi-session CUSUM detects 0.1–0.3 drops?
