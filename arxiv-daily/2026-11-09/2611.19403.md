> **一句话总结：** 本文提出 DECOY 基准（155 组经符号求解器证明"干扰项不影响答案"的物理题族），发现 Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct 在 50.0% 的题族上会因与答案无关的干扰数字而使正确答案的对数概率下降超过 0.5 nats，且生成前的第 18 层隐藏状态线性探针能以 AUROC 0.760 预测这种"假敏感"，并在第 16 层定位到 4 个注意力头——敲除它们即可消除该行为。

> **TL;DR:** The authors build DECOY, a benchmark of 155 four-prompt physics families where a symbolic solver (SymPy) certifies that an injected distractor cannot change the answer, so any confidence shift belongs to the model. On DECOY, Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct is "sensitive" on 50.0% of 140 labelled families (≥0.5-nat drop in the certified answer's log-probability) versus 22.9% for Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, with the largest effects when the distractor numerically near-matches the answer (e.g., 10.03 nats for 6.9 vs 6.928 N·m). A linear probe on Qwen's layer-18 prompt-side hidden state predicts sensitivity before generation at AUROC 0.760 (leave-one-family-out), beating a TF-IDF surface baseline (0.651) by +0.11 and a chain-of-thought classifier (0.599) by +0.16, though statistically tied with a fitted token-entropy baseline (0.727, paired Δ +0.033, CI [−0.074, +0.141]). Knocking out four attention heads at layer 16 (mostly head 11) cancels the effect on a 20-family panel (recovery 1.870 vs 0.021 for random perturbations) without hurting accuracy. The methodological takeaway: hidden-state monitors should be judged against the cheapest fitted baseline under paired resampling, not against strawman baselines.

## 核心贡献

- **经认证的基准 DECOY**：155 组、每组 4 个提示词（共 620 条）的物理题族，每组只改动一个与答案无关的"干扰线索"（cue）。与以往扰动式基准不同，SymPy 符号求解器对每族给出机器可验证的证明——线索符号不出现在答案表达式中（或对 Cue C 存在精确恒等式）——因此置信度变化必然归因于模型而非任务。
- **三类线索 × 三个物理领域的设计**：Cue A（无关变量，如向心力题中的墙面温度）、Cue B（与答案单位相同的干扰量，最具诊断性）、Cue C（等效单位表述，如 200 W / 0.2 kW）；覆盖力学、静电与电路、热学。阈值 0.5 nats 在探针训练前预先注册（2026-06-14）。
- **敏感性的测量结果**：Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct 在 50.0%（70/140）题族上敏感，Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct 仅 22.9%（32/140），两者仅 25 族重合——敏感性是"模型×问题"的配对属性，且效应集中于干扰值与答案数值接近的尾部情形。
- **生成前监控器**：对 Qwen 第 18 层残差流（族内四个渲染取平均）的 L2 正则逻辑回归探针，在整族留出（leave-one-family-out）下达到 AUROC 0.760；剔除"难度"成分后仍有 0.738；在训练后新建的 15 族上冻结探针仍保留信号。
- **因果定位**：敲除 Qwen 第 16 层的 4 个注意力头（head 11 贡献几乎全部效应）可消除 20 族 Cue B 面板上的敏感行为（归一化恢复 1.870，随机方向对照仅 0.021），且 40 族留出准确率不降（0.700 → 0.713）。
- **评测标准倡议**：用配对族自助法（paired family bootstrap）把隐藏状态探针与 8 个非激活基线（含拟合的 token 熵、CoT 分类器、LLM 裁判等）在同一批族上同折比较——这是对探针研究"只比随机方向/表层基线"惯常做法的方法学纠偏。

## Key Contributions

- **A certified benchmark (DECOY)**: 155 families of four prompts (620 total) spanning three cue types and three physics domains, each carrying a machine-checked SymPy certificate that the cue symbol is absent from the solved answer expression (or an exact identity for unit-restatement cues). This converts "sensitivity to irrelevant detail" from a confounded measurement into a property of the model.
- **Three-level framework**: Level 1 (prompt condition) → Level 2 (behaviour: largest teacher-forced log-prob drop on the certified answer across the family) → Level 3 (internal state: probes and causal components). Claims follow the chain "Level 3 predicts Level 2 correlates with Level 1", keeping what the model computes separate from which cue value it saw.
- **Pre-registered, parser-independent sensitivity label**: S_lp is the max log-prob drop vs the base rendering under teacher forcing, thresholded at 0.5 nats (registered before any probe training); it is signed, decoding-independent, and defined even when generations fail to parse.
- **A pre-generation monitor**: a linear probe on the layer-18 residual stream at the last prompt token (averaged over four renderings) reaches AUROC 0.760 with whole families held out; the signal is internal (embedding-layer probe 0.567, TF-IDF 0.651), survives correctness-residualisation (0.738), and replicates in DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-32B via a hidden-state-spread probe (0.703).
- **Causal localisation in Qwen**: four attention heads at layer 16 (26, 24, 13, 11; head 11 alone gives 1.848 of the 1.870 recovery) are necessary for the behaviour; the effect is one layer wide, ~90× the norm-matched random-direction control, and transfers to held-out Cue A families while the probe directions are cue-type-specific (cosine 0.018 between Cue A and Cue B).
- **An evaluation standard**: paired family-bootstrap comparison against fitted non-activation baselines (token entropy, combined 9-feature model, black-box 2-query check, CoT classifier, self-consistency, LLM judge), plus a generic correctness probe — the comparison the authors argue any future hidden-state monitor should face.

## Methodology

The pipeline is CERTIFY → RENDER → MEASURE → MONITOR → EVALUATE. (1) Each family starts from a symbolic template τ = (relevant variables r, cue z, assumptions a, governing law g, answer y*); a SymPy solver proves the cue symbol is absent from the rearranged answer expression after substituting assumptions, that the expression evaluates to the declared answer within 10⁻⁶ relative tolerance, and that all parameters are finite and physically plausible. (2) Four natural-language renderings are produced (template-first; LLMs may paraphrase but never write physics or answers). (3) One teacher-forced pass per rendering yields the family sensitivity S_lp = max log-prob drop vs the base rendering; a family is "sensitive" at S_lp ≥ 0.5 nats (pre-registered). (4) A logistic-regression probe on the layer-ℓ residual-stream vector at the last prompt token (family-averaged) predicts the label; evaluation is leave-one-family-out over 135 families (70 positive, 65 stable), with all four renderings of a family on the same side of every split. (5) The probe is compared with eight non-activation predictors under a paired family bootstrap (10,000 resamples, seed 42), and the causal claim is tested by multi-head knockout on 20 strongly sensitive Cue B families with six controls (random direction ×20, random site, unrelated/same-answer/stable-cue donors, accuracy damage check). Models: Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct (primary, 28 layers, TransformerLens), Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-32B (forward hooks).

## Results & Highlights

- **Sensitivity is common and model-specific**: on the 140 labelled DECOY families, Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct is sensitive on 70 (50.0%, 95% CI [42.1, 58.6], mean drop 1.401 nats, max 13.83) while Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct is sensitive on only 32 (22.9%), with just 25 families shared — a delta of 27.1 percentage points on identical prompts. DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-32B sits at 27.1%.
- **Cue type matters**: unit-matched distractors (Cue B) trigger 53.0% sensitivity vs 34.3% for irrelevant variables (Cue A); all 5 labelled Cue C families are sensitive (100%, mean drop 9.022 nats).
- **Numerical near-matches dominate the tail**: a 6.9 N·m distractor beside a 6.928 N·m answer produces a 10.03-nat drop; 1.6 vs 1.592 Hz gives 5.56 nats; an irrelevant 18 °C wall temperature numerically equal to an 18 N answer gives 2.30 nats — though the panel-wide Pearson correlation of proximity with S_lp is weak (0.074), i.e., a tail effect, not a graded one.
- **Hidden states predict sensitivity before generation**: the layer-18 family-averaged probe reaches AUROC 0.760 (95% CI [0.675, 0.838]) leave-one-family-out on the 135-family panel, vs 0.651 for a fitted TF-IDF prompt classifier (+0.109) and 0.567 for an embedding-layer probe; a single-rendering probe reaches 0.747 (layer 16); AUROC stays in 0.722–0.782 as the binarisation threshold sweeps 0.1–2.0 nats.
- **Against fitted baselines**: the probe beats the black-box two-query check (0.668), CoT classifier (0.599), self-consistency (0.537) and zero-shot LLM judge (0.480), and has the lowest Brier score of any single predictor (0.204 vs 0.214 for fitted token entropy vs 0.229 for the 9-feature combined model), but is statistically indistinguishable from fitted token entropy on AUROC (0.760 vs 0.727; paired Δ +0.033, CI [−0.074, +0.141]); entropy is better calibrated (ECE 0.091 vs 0.104) and misses fewer positives at 10% FPR (FNR 0.643 vs 0.714).
- **Frozen-probe transfer with a caveat**: a layer-16 probe frozen after training scores pooled AUROC 0.820 (AUPRC 0.907) on 15 families built afterwards, but within-cue-type AUROC is only 0.667 (Cue B, n=5) and 0.6875 (Cue C, n=10) — the pooled figure is inflated by a score-offset/base-rate alignment.
- **Causal localisation**: knocking out heads {26, 24, 13, 11} at layer 16 yields mean normalised recovery 1.870 (CI [1.560, 2.198]; capped at 1.0 per family: 0.969) on the 20-family Cue B panel vs 0.021 for 20 equal-norm random perturbations (beaten on 20/20 families); head 11 alone gives 1.848; the window is one layer wide (0.540 at L15, −0.049 at L17); accuracy on 40 held-out families moves 0.700 → 0.713, inside a pre-fixed 5% tolerance.

## Authors & Credibility

The three authors (Shubham Mishra, Atharv Sharma, Dhruv Kumar) are from Birla Institute of Technology and Science, Pilani, with a named correspondence address; this is a single-institution group. The paper is under review at ICLR 2027 (not yet peer-reviewed). Credibility signals are unusually strong for a preprint: a pre-registered 0.5-nat threshold with registration date (2026-06-14) recorded before probe training; a reproducibility statement asserting every number derives from committed artefacts (benchmark manifest with certificates, per-family labels, probe predictions, all baseline scores, patching results; bootstrap seed 42); a mutation-tested verifier (300/330 mutation records verified; all positive and negative operators accepted/rejected at rate 1.0 over 30 families); an unusually candid AI-use statement (LLM assistant drafted prose from committed artefacts but did not design the study, set thresholds, or run experiments); and a limitations section that itself flags the strongest counter-evidence (e.g., the pooled 0.820 transfer figure overstating within-type discrimination). Leakage audits (exact/number-masked/symbolic/structure hashes over all 620 prompts) found 0 cross-split exact duplicates.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: The certified-invariance construction is genuinely new relative to GSM-IRrelevant-context (Shi et al., 2023) and GSM-Symbolic (Mirzadeh, 2025), which rely on templates/heuristics without proof that the perturbation preserves the answer; measuring confidence (log-prob) shifts rather than accuracy flips, so a family can be sensitive while every answer is correct, is a sharper instrument. The paired-comparison methodology against fitted baselines is a real, exportable contribution.
- **Rigor**: High. Pre-registration, leave-one-family-out splits, paired family bootstrap, six causal controls, correctness-residualisation, control-task logic (Hewitt & Liang), and a prompt-condition probe scoring 0.532 (chance) showing the label has no surface signature. The failure taxonomy (Appendix I) concedes the probe reads template class, not numerical proximity.
- **Reproducibility**: Excellent on paper — committed artefacts, pinned environment, seed-registered bootstraps, released certificates — though at submission time the release is asserted rather than verifiable, and the human-validation pilot of cue irrelevance "has not been run".
- **Significance / overclaiming risks**: The headline monitor is statistically tied with a fitted token-entropy baseline that needs no activation access (Δ +0.033, CI spans zero) — the authors say so plainly, which is to their credit, but it means the practical case for the probe rests on Brier score and pre-generation availability, not AUROC. The causal claim is Qwen-only: Llama's knockout CI [−3.461, +6.653] spans zero and DeepSeek shows no effect (0.086). Specific weaknesses a reader should weigh: (a) the layer-18 AUROC is the best of a 28-layer sweep with no selection adjustment; (b) Cue C has only 5 labelled families, so its 100%-sensitivity / 9.022-nat mean is illustrative only; (c) leave-one-family-out is not leave-one-cluster-out — 71% of held-out families share a template cluster with training families, and whole-domain holdout (0.750 vs 0.734 shuffled null, p = 0.368) does not establish domain generalisation; (d) one donor-on-renamed control was unmeasurable due to a donor-selection defect, so the 68%-specificity attribution rests on two other decompositions; (e) five stable families were dropped from the monitoring panel because outputs failed to parse, a small panel-curation asymmetry.
- **Red flags**: none serious — no cherry-picked main metric (the pre-registered threshold is shown to be less favourable than the sweep's 0.782 maximum), and negative results for Llama/DeepSeek causal transfer are reported. Main caution: 7B–32B open-weight models, text-only physics, teacher-forced scoring of a single reference answer (Appendix J scope statement).

## Strengths

- Certification converts shortcut sensitivity into a model-attributable quantity — the benchmark's core idea is clean and reusable beyond physics wherever closed-form answers exist.
- Measurement design: teacher-forced, signed, decoding-independent statistic with a pre-registered threshold; secondary answer-flip rate reported as a cross-check.
- The paired-bootstrap comparison against a fitted entropy baseline (and the honest reporting that it ties) is exactly the evaluation hygiene the probing literature lacks.
- Causal intervention with six controls, per-family random-direction beats (20/20), one-layer-wide localisation, and an accuracy-damage check — plus a sufficiency/necessity decomposition showing heads are necessary while the linear direction is only partly sufficient (37% of stable families cross threshold at 2× injection scale).
- Exceptional transparency: AI-use statement, superseded-audit disclosures, failure taxonomy, and scope-of-claims appendix.

## Limitations

- Small panel: 135 families / 70 positives cannot resolve 0.03–0.05 AUROC differences, so "probe vs entropy" stays open; the paper explicitly calls for a larger panel.
- Causal localisation is established only in Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct; the same recipe fails in Llama (CI spans zero) and DeepSeek at the sites tested.
- Cue C coverage is tiny (5 labelled families) and the frozen-probe transfer result (pooled 0.820) is inflated by between-group base-rate skew — within-type AUROC is 0.667–0.6875, near the entropy baseline.
- Generalisation is unproven at cluster and domain level (71% cluster overlap; domain-holdout p = 0.368 vs permutation null); sensitivity could partly reflect template-family structure.
- Human validation of cue irrelevance (does a physicist read the cue as irrelevant?) is planned but not run; certification proves answer invariance, not psychological salience.
- The probe's known failure mode: it detects "dangerous template class" but not numerical proximity, missing strong positives whose signature is spread beyond the layer-18 mean feature.

## Relevance

这篇论文处于可解释性与可靠性监控的交叉点：它既提供了"认证式不变性基准"这一构建评测数据的新范式，也演示了如何用生成前隐藏状态探针与因果干预定位 LLM 的捷径行为，对任何关心 LLM 部署期安全监控、探针方法学或扰动稳健性评测的研究方向都有直接参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would the layer-18 probe retain its AUROC margin over fitted entropy on a 10× larger family panel, or is the tie (Δ +0.033) the true effect size?
- Does head 11's mechanism in Qwen have a functional analogue in Llama/DeepSeek at sites outside the probe-peak depth, or is this circuit an accident of training?
- Can the monitor be extended to a numerically aware, per-rendering representation that captures the near-match tail the current mean feature misses?
- Do the results transfer beyond certified-answer physics to domains where invariance cannot be symbolically proven (code, multi-step GSM-style reasoning, multimodal inputs)?
- Since sensitivity correlates with difficulty only at −0.346, what would a joint monitor over (correctness, sensitivity) directions buy in deployed abstention policies?
- Would chain-of-thought monitors (fragile per Korbak et al., 2025) recover AUROC above 0.599 if allowed the same family-averaged input the hidden-state probe receives?
