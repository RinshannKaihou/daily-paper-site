Key Finding · arXiv 2611.14942

Multimodal training writes a causal vision–language axis into language backbones — and text-only reasoning recruits it to think spatially.

多模态训练会在语言骨干内部写入一个"视觉–语言（VL）方向"，纯文本推理时会因果性地调用它——删除该方向会选择性破坏空间意象任务。

Why This Matters

Causal proof that vision training rewrites the internal geometry text-only reasoning depends on — plus a reusable intervention–diagnostic protocol for unified understanding/generation models.

⚠ Critical Edge

The headline navigate drop partly rides on answer-distribution shifts — Chameleon-7B's navigate baseline (43.3%) sits below chance for the task's format.

from Critical Assessment — why the authors anchor on completion-controlled batteries

Methodology

Three-part pipeline: construct the axis, ablate it at inference, test causality.

  • Axis construction. Per-architecture DiffMean axis v = (µ_text − µ_visual)/‖·‖ at each vision–language interface — shared embedding table for Chameleon, per-layer text/visual positions for Emu3 & Qwen3-VL, separate per-path axes for dual-path BAGEL/Janus. Split-half stability ≥ 0.9992; synthetic vs real-COCO inputs give identical hub dissociations.
  • Projection intervention. During text-only inference, a residual-stream hook applies h ← h − α(hᵀv)v; dosage α frozen once per config on a pre-fixed 125/125 calibration/held-out split (α = 1 in 6/10 configs). Rank-k PCA bases test higher-rank carriers.
  • Causal checks. Task selectivity (spatial-imagery vs symbol-manipulation controls), direction specificity vs norm- and energy-matched randoms (z-statistics), and layer localization via block sweeps → single-layer scans.
  • Gradedness & controls. 0–5 spatial-imagery spectrum (4 LLM annotators + 2 humans, Kendall's W ≈ 0.98), bidirectional rescue/amplification, and external transfer: bAbI, SpartQA, StepGame, mental-simulation battery, visual×spatial 2×2.
causal hubs: Chameleon · L5 Qwen3-VL · L3–L8 BAGEL/Janus · L21–L28

Quick hits
  • Knowledge/math spared — MMLU-Pro moves ≤ 5 pp (mean |Δ| = 1.4); Chameleon GSM8K improves +4.0 pp.
  • Scale broadens the carrier — visual attribute binding holds at 7–8B but collapses at 32B (100.0 → 29.0, −71 pp) while symbolic controls hold.
  • Functional-use control — hub-specific L6 reinjection rescues +8.0 pp of the −27.6 pp deficit; non-hub L20 ablation is null.
  • External transfer — StepGame k=1 −24.8 pp (Qwen3-VL-8B); SpartQA-YN −19.0 pp (Emu3).

Strengths

Why the evidence holds up.

  • Causal, intervention-based evidence — not correlational probing — with three orthogonal checks (selectivity, specificity, localization) plus gradedness and rescue controls.
  • Breadth: 10 configurations, 6 architecture families, 2B–32B, with matched-backbone controls separating "introduced" from "amplified" scaffolds.
  • Dual-path BAGEL/Janus analysis is a clean natural experiment — same backbone, two visual routes; only the generation path yields a compact selective 1D carrier.
  • Concentration / alignment / semantic-location diagnostics form a reusable protocol for probe-vs-intervention mismatches.
  • Honest boundary reporting — failure appendix, completion caveats, and dosage cliffs are in the paper, not hidden.

Limitations

Where the claim thins out.

  • Linear / low-rank interventions only — the understanding-path null could be a nonlinear carrier, not absence of structure.
  • Mental-simulation evidence exists only where a capability floor is cleared (Qwen3-VL only; Chameleon-7B is at/below chance everywhere).
  • Carrier is not exclusively spatial: 8–12-update non-spatial color tracking also drops 44–52 pp; at 32B it broadens to general visual attribute binding.
  • BAGEL's main-dosage intervention damages GSM8K −53.6 pp — "knowledge preserved" is configuration-bounded.
  • Qwen pretraining data is non-public; introduced-vs-amplified attribution leans on the Qwen2.5-7B anchor.
  • Establishes necessity, not mechanism — no evidence on image-like scenes or which circuits read/write the axis.
Questions for Further Exploration
  1. Would controlled training ablations (vision-only vs generation-only pretraining) confirm that generation training compresses spatial structure into the 1D carrier?
  2. Which attention heads write and read the VL axis at the causal hub (e.g., Chameleon L5) — moving from necessity to mechanism?
  3. Is the 32B broadening to non-spatial attribute binding a continuous scaling trend or an architectural phase transition?
  4. Do LLaVA-style finetunes on frozen backbones also introduce/amplify the scaffold, or is early-fusion joint pretraining required?
  5. Could the generation-path carrier be amplified constructively to improve text-only spatial reasoning?
