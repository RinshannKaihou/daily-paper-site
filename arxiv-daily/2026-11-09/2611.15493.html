Standard entropy in RLVR mostly rewards diversity into wrong answers — measuring it only over verifier-valid trajectories restores genuine exploration.

标准熵对“有效性”视而不见，制造“多样性幻觉”——只在可验证的正确子空间内度量熵（有效熵），探索才是真实的。

The Illusion of Diversity: Aligning LLM Exploration via Effective Entropy ↗ Xiaoliang Fu · Jiaye Lin · Yangyi Fang · Cong Qin · et al. (7 authors) — 2026-11-09

Key Metric · Matched-Count Strategy Diversity
+16%

Distinct strategy clusters per problem on MATH500-7B at equal correct counts (8 per method: 2.68 vs 2.31; p < 10⁻⁴) — the diversity is real, not an accuracy artifact. Plus +2.2 pp Avg@32 over GRPO on six math benchmarks.

Why This Matters

It breaks the “entropy = exploration” default in GRPO/RLVR training: make models think correctly from many angles — not diversely make mistakes.

⚠ Critical Edge

Against the strongest baseline (AEPO), the 7B Pass@32 edge is just +0.4 pp, 95% CI [−0.3, 1.1] — statistically indistinguishable, and several per-benchmark cells lose outright.

Methodology 方法

The flaw
  • Token entropy is locally blind — correctness is a trajectory-level property, not a token-level one.
  • Sequence entropy is globally diluted: the invalid subspace dominates combinatorially (|O_valid|/|O| → 0), and the optimum of standard entropy regularization is a Boltzmann distribution that must place mass on errors.
The fix

Measure entropy only where reward lives — each trajectory’s contribution weighted by the binary verifier:

H_eff(π) = −Σo r(o|q) · π(o|q) · log π(o|q)
  • VEPO = GRPO + β·H_eff. Gradient weight w(o) = r·(1+log π) is negative for every realistic valid trajectory → adaptive attraction toward under-explored correct paths; invalid trajectories get exactly zero entropy incentive.
  • Deliberately minimal: detached sequence weight on token-level log-prob gradients — no value network, no PRM, so results test the concept.
Setup

DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B/7B on DAPO-Math-17K (VeRL; 240× A100, >25k GPU-h); β = 1e-4; dual verifiers (math_verify for training, stricter prime_math for eval); 3 paired seeds; 6 benchmarks with Avg@32 / Pass@32.

Strengths 优点

  • Formalizes a structural flaw shared by the entire entropy-aware RLVR family — “high entropy” can literally reward hallucination diversity.
  • Minimal method, framed as a concept test: the positive result is attributable to the idea, not engineering.
  • Matched-count diversity protocol (10,000 rarefactions, human-validated blinded clustering, κ = 0.82) cleanly separates diversity from accuracy.
  • Exceptional transparency: publishes negative margins, verifier-noise degradation, the 9.8% overhead, and CIs throughout.
  • Attribution analysis (effective ratio ρ_eff, entropy-conversion efficiency, the Adv-Reweighting “Diversity Paradox”) explains why it works.

Limitations 局限

  • Requires a reliable binary verifier — open-ended or preference-based rewards need a redefinition of the valid subspace.
  • Evidence confined to math reasoning, two backbones (1.5B/7B), one training set; tool-use, multimodal, long-horizon tasks untested.
  • H_eff is rollout-estimated: with G = 8, some groups (0.9–7.1%) carry no auxiliary signal at all.
  • Margins over the strongest baseline are modest and sometimes statistically indistinguishable.
  • No causal token-level credit assignment; false-positive verifier errors reintroduce entropy incentive on wrong outputs.
  • Code not released; replication needs >25,000 GPU-hours.

Questions for Further Exploration 开放问题

  1. Would soft, per-step validity r(o, t) from process reward models recover the causal token credit the shared sequence weight lacks?
  2. Does the attraction dynamic stay benign when valid trajectories become high-probability (π → e⁻¹), e.g. after aggressive distillation?
  3. Can valid-subspace entropy transfer to non-verifiable domains via learned verifiers or self-consistency — and how much false-positive noise can it tolerate?
  4. Would combining VEPO with clipping/trust-region methods (Clip-Higher, CISPO, DGPO) compound, or compete for the same probability mass?
  5. Is the +16% diversity gain causally linked to the accuracy gain, or can one exist without the other?
