Key Finding · 核心发现

Last-layer directional ablation is exactly a cosine-gated logit bias — and its failures all trace to common-mode overlap ω.

Centered Ablation: Why Last-Layer Directional Ablation Under-Fires or Collapses, and a Closed-Form Fix

Why This Matters为何重要

First exact closed-form account of directional ablation, a plug-and-play faithfulness fix, and three pre-generation diagnostics for steering experiments and safety evaluation.

Critical Edge批判视角

The closed form holds only at the last residual stream — whether centring helps at mid-depth layers, where most practical steering actually happens, is untested and explicitly disclaimed.

Methodology方法

One operator, one site, derived exactly
  • Exact site: residual stream after the last decoder block — the only path to the next token is RMSNorm + unembedding (edit never enters the KV cache). Steered logits = ℓ(h) − s√d·κ_u(h)·f_u, with footprint f_u = W_U G u and cosine gate κ_u(h).
  • Failure mechanism: last-layer states share a large common mode μ̂₋; the DIM direction spends an ω-sized share of its unit budget on it → benign misfires, target under-firing, collapse past β⁺ = 1.
  • Centered Ablation (Alg. 1): u_CA = unit(u_DIM − (u_DIMᵀμ̂₋)μ̂₋) — two sample means (256 AdvBench vs. 256 Alpaca states), one projection, no new hyperparameters; proven ω(u_CA) = 0.
  • Cached-activation diagnostics (no generation, no judge calls): overlap ω, efficacy η, collapse index β⁺ — recorded for 5 held-out models before any text was generated.
  • Setup: 8 instruction-tuned models (0.5B–9B, Qwen2.5/Qwen3/Llama/Gemma-2) × 3 directions × 6 draws = 144 cells; HarmBench n=240 with two judges; greedy decoding; paired bootstrap (5000) + exact McNemar.

HarmBench (n = 240), fluent-only, HarmBench classifier. Under-firing fixed exactly where diagnostics predicted (η < 0.6).
  • Benign collateral: on ω>0.3 models CA preserves 12–40 pts more Alpaca answers than DIM; the ACE-style offset rewrites 39–46 pts more than CA on ω>0.33 models — its excess gate tracks the predicted 1/√(1−ω²) on all 8 models.
  • Out-of-sample diagnostics: ω ↔ benign preserved (Spearman 0.98); η ↔ ASR gain (Pearson −0.95); all 6 of 144 collapsing cells flagged by β⁺>1; 5 held-out models → 4/5 regime calls correct.
  • Judge-split hazard: without a fluency filter, judge disagreement fabricates a 46.4-pt "effect" on Gemma-2-9B (64.8% vs. 18.4%) that vanishes when filtered (n.s.).
  • Defence & capacity: CA-Amp drives Llama-3.1-8B ASR to 0.5% (DIM-Amp floor: 3.3%); diffuse behaviour (emotion, η 0.05–0.15) only erodes — 60% of emotion words removed, judged emotion −19 pts, but 53–62% of benign answers rewritten.

Strengths优势

Derives, rather than measures, what a widely reused edit does
  • Closed form validated to 96.1% first-token agreement against a real serving stack (vLLM), 144 cells.
  • One quantity (ω) unifies three model-specific symptoms; the fix follows from analysis, not tuning.
  • Diagnostics are cheap (cached activations, no generation or judge calls) and were validated under a recorded-before-generation, out-of-sample protocol.
  • Fair, derived comparison with ACE's offset — identifies exactly when each route wins instead of claiming dominance.
  • Actionable evaluation guidance (fluent-only success + collapse rate + free-form faithfulness); honest negative results and a crisp rank-1 capacity bound.

Limitations局限

Scope is narrower than the headline suggests
  • Closed form covers rank-1 ablation at the last residual stream, greedy decoding only; mid-depth and additive steering out of scope, and the ACE comparison is at this paper's site, not ACE's.
  • "Laws" rest on 8 models ≤9B: the η = 0.6 split has two supporting models; the β⁺ = 1 collapse threshold only one crossing model — bracketed, not pinned.
  • Out-of-sample regime prediction 4/5 (Gemma-2-2B miss explained post-hoc).
  • CA trades attack strength for faithfulness: ASR significantly below the offset route on three models — not a uniformly stronger jailbreak or defence.
  • Diffuse behaviours (emotion) cannot be removed without rewriting ~half of unrelated benign answers; English chat models only, sampling untested.

Questions for Further Exploration待探索

  1. Does centring also help at mid-depth layers — where the standard Arditi et al. recipe and ACE actually operate — or does the common-mode story change once the edit propagates through attention?
  2. How does the analysis extend to sampling-based decoding, where the rescaling τs acts as a temperature?
  3. Can a rank-k ablation capture Gemma-2-9B's second-stage semantic deflection, which rank-1 provably cannot?
  4. Do ω, η, β transfer beyond 9B and beyond the three tested families, and can the η = 0.6 split be sharpened?
  5. Could the collapse index β serve as an online monitor that auto-disables a steering vector before text degenerates?
  6. How many published jailbreak evaluations are contaminated by judge disagreement on degenerate text — would re-scoring with a fluency filter change their conclusions?
  7. Is the common mode μ̂₋ stable across domains (code, multilingual) and training checkpoints — how often must CA's calibration refresh?
