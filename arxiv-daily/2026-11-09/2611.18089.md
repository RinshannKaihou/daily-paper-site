> **一句话总结：** 作者定义了神经网络激活的自相关函数（同一层中相隔 τ 个 token 的两个位置的激活点积），发现其慢衰减尾部随深度增长，并在合成层次数据（RHM）中证明这来自对"管辖长片段输入的抽象潜变量"的持久编码（可解释 55–95% 的自相关），在真实语言中用 SAE 特征作为潜变量代理，发现特征持久时间与积分自相关逐层强相关（Gemma 2 9B 上 Pearson r=0.93），且消融最持久的特征会显著降低自相关（单个特征 −3%～−13%，而消融非持久特征对照组 ≈0%）。

> **TL;DR:** This paper proposes the autocorrelation of neural activations, c_ℓ(τ) = E[φ^(ℓ)_{T−τ}·φ^(ℓ)_T], as a task-free probe of how long latent variables stay encoded across context. On a 5-block GPT-2-style transformer trained on the Random Hierarchy Model (RHM), persistence of known latent codes accounts for 55–95% of the measured layer-4 autocorrelation, and each autocorrelation plateau emerges exactly when training resolves the corresponding hierarchy level. On The Pile and WikiText across five open LLMs (Gemma 2 2B/9B, Pythia-160M, Llama-3-8B, Qwen3-8B), median SAE-feature persistence per layer tracks the integrated autocorrelation χ_ℓ strongly (Pearson r = 0.93 for Gemma 2 9B, vs 0.67 for Llama-3-8B, the weakest of the five), and ablating the single most persistent SAE feature cuts within-episode autocorrelation by 3.0–13.3% versus ≈0% (−0.7 to +0.0) for an active-but-non-persistent control feature — direct causal evidence that persistent latent codes, not activation frequency, carry the long-range temporal structure that grows with depth.

## 核心贡献

- **提出"神经自相关"这一无需任务、无需预定义特征的可观测指标**：c_ℓ(τ) 定义为同一层激活在相隔 τ 的两个上下文位置上的平均点积。在 one-hot token 层面它退化为居中的 token 重合概率，从而把"序列相关性反映生成过程结构"这一原理从输入统计推广到了模型内部表征。
- **在潜变量完全已知的合成数据（Random Hierarchy Model, RHM）中建立了因果级别的解释**：深度越大的潜变量管辖越长的输入片段，其编码在层间逐层被解析并沿上下文持久存在；持久编码的同节点项可以预测自相关的台阶状结构，并解释层 4 中 55–95% 的实测自相关。
- **揭示自相关随训练的分阶段涌现**：RHM 训练中验证损失在深度 h 子树熵处出现平台，而自相关的"阶梯"逐阶段向外搭建——模型在哪个阶段学会哪一层潜变量，对应滞后的自相关就在哪个阶段出现。
- **把结论迁移到真实语言与开源 LLM**：用 SAE 特征作为潜变量代理，在 5 个模型、2 个语料上发现特征持久时间与积分自相关逐层强相关，且持久时间随深度增长——即"更深的层编码更抽象（更持久）的特征"获得了操作化、可量化的表述。
- **通过干预实验（消融）提供因果证据**：沿发射片段投影消融最持久的 SAE 特征会显著降低自相关，而消融"活跃但不持久"的特征几乎无效果，排除了"激活频率造成长程相关"的混淆解释（另附 token 打乱对照实验）。

## Key Contributions

- **A task-free observable for representation dynamics:** the neural autocorrelation c_ℓ(τ) = E[φ^(ℓ)_{T−τ}·φ^(ℓ)_T] (activations standardized to zero mean, unit norm). At the one-hot/token level it reduces exactly to the centered token-coincidence probability, so the tool extends a classical principle — the correlations of a sequence reflect the structure of the process that generated it — from input statistics to internal representations, with no probes, labels, or downstream tasks required.
- **A mechanistic, quantitative account in controlled conditions:** on a 5-block GPT-2-style transformer trained to within 0.033 nats/sentence of the optimal loss on the depth-5 Random Hierarchy Model (v=32, m=8), ordinary-least-squares decomposition of activations into latent codes shows that layer ℓ resolves hierarchy level ℓ, and that the same-node (persistence) terms of the decomposition reproduce the staircase shape of the measured autocorrelations — accounting for 55–95% of the measurement at layer 4, the remainder being a non-additive residual confined to the first plateau.
- **Training-dynamics evidence:** the autocorrelation staircase is built outward one plateau per learning stage, in lockstep with the stagewise loss plateaus at the exact conditional entropies of depth-h subtrees (stage 2 → only lag 1 stands out; stage 3 → plateau at lags 1–3; stage 4 → lags 4–7; stage 5 settles).
- **Transfer to natural language:** using open SAEs (Gemma Scope JumpReLU for Gemma 2 2B/9B; EleutherAI top-k for Pythia-160M and Llama-3-8B; Qwen-Scope for Qwen3-8B) on WikiText and The Pile, both the integrated autocorrelation χ_ℓ = Σ_τ c_ℓ(τ) and the median persistence of SAE features grow roughly monotonically with relative depth and are strongly correlated layer-by-layer.
- **Causal intervention:** ablating the most persistent SAE features (projection removal along decoder directions, Eq. 9) during their firing episodes significantly decreases autocorrelations, while ablating an active-but-non-persistent feature (a-control) does not — and a within-sequence token-shuffle control shows that activation frequency alone cannot explain the depth growth of persistence.

## Methodology

1. **Neural autocorrelation (Eq. 1).** For standardized activations φ^(ℓ)_t, define c_ℓ(τ) = E[φ^(ℓ)_{T−τ} · φ^(ℓ)_T], anchoring at the last token T (any two positions at distance τ work). For one-hot token encodings this equals P(X_{T−τ}=X_T) − Σ_i P(X_t=i)², the centered coincidence probability; random embeddings leave the curve's shape unchanged on log scale (Appendix B.1). Under the linear representation hypothesis φ^(ℓ)_t = A^(ℓ)_t e_Z + ε^(ℓ)_t (Eq. 6), substituting into Eq. 1 splits the autocorrelation into code and residual terms (Eq. 7); expanding over node pairs further splits code terms into same-node (persistence) and cross-node contributions.

2. **Synthetic ground truth (RHM).** Sentences of 2⁵ = 32 tokens are generated top-down along a binary depth-5 tree (v = 32 symbols, m = 8 unambiguous production rules per symbol; 31 hidden nodes + 32 tokens = 63 latent variables per sentence). Because the grammar is fixed and unambiguous, latents are recoverable by bottom-up parsing and the optimal loss is exactly computable by belief propagation. The model is a 5-block, d=768, 12-head pre-LayerNorm GPT-2-style transformer trained with Adam (lr 3×10⁻³, batch 1024, cosine decay to 3×10⁻⁴ over 2¹⁹ steps) on 2²⁸ sentences for 2²⁰ steps; final validation loss is within 0.033 nats/sentence (0.007 nats/position) of optimal. Activations are regressed (OLS) on all 63 one-hot latent encodings, yielding per-variable codes A^(ℓ)_t; encoding maps then show which latents each position/layer carries (e.g., layer 1 encodes depth-1 parents with a 2-slot periodic pattern; layer 2 encodes grandparents with a 4-slot pattern; layers 3–4 only partially match the one-layer-per-level picture).

3. **Natural-language measurements.** For five open LLMs with public SAEs (Gemma 2 2B/9B, Pythia-160M, Llama-3-8B, Qwen3-8B), activations are collected on WikiText and The Pile. Layer-level summary: the integrated autocorrelation χ_ℓ = Σ_τ c_ℓ(τ) (the "susceptibility" analogue). Latent-level proxy: each SAE feature's persistence ψ = e − s + 1, the length of a consecutive firing episode, averaged per feature over episodes. Median/mean persistence per layer is compared to χ_ℓ across layers.

4. **Controls and interventions.** (a) *Frequency confound:* shuffling token positions within each sequence preserves activation frequencies but destroys temporal order — the depth growth of persistence largely disappears. (b) *Sequence-wide component:* subtracting each sequence's mean activation yields a corrected χ̃_ℓ, which stays strongly (often more strongly) correlated with persistence and shows a non-monotonic layer profile with a plateau in the second half of the network. (c) *Ablation (Table 1, Fig. 5):* at the deepest layer with an available SAE, activations are edited as φ̃_t = φ_t − (φ_t·W_dec(i))/‖W_dec(i)‖² · W_dec(i) over a feature's firing episode (reference token e left intact); Δ (Eq. 10) is averaged over the 500 longest episodes of the 400 most persistent latents (≤2 episodes per latent), and compared against an active-but-non-persistent control and against references moved outside the episode (e+1, e+2, e+3).

## Results & Highlights

- **RHM, mechanistic decomposition (Fig. 2, Appendix D):** the same-node persistence terms of the three diagonal latents (X¹⁵⁽⁴⁾ spanning lags 1–2, X⁷⁽³⁾ spanning 3–4, X³⁽²⁾ spanning 7–8) reproduce the staircase of the measured layer-4 autocorrelation; stacked, the predicted codes account for **55–95% of the measured autocorrelation** across layers of the 5-block transformer trained on the depth-5 RHM — the rest is a non-additive residual concentrated at the first plateau.
- **Training dynamics (Fig. 3):** the last-token validation loss descends through plateaus sitting exactly at the conditional entropies of depth-h subtrees, and the layer-4 autocorrelation staircase grows one plateau per stage (only lag 1 above baseline at stage 2; lags 1–3 by stage 3; lags 4–7 by stage 4) — autocorrelation structure is acquired in lockstep with latent-variable learning. The final model reaches within **0.033 nats/sentence** of the Bayes-optimal loss (0.007 nats per position).
- **LLMs, correlation (Fig. 4c):** across layers, median SAE-feature persistence vs integrated autocorrelation χ_ℓ yields per-model Pearson r of **0.93 (Gemma 2 9B), 0.87 (Gemma 2 2B), 0.76 (Qwen3-8B), 0.75 (Pythia-160M), 0.67 (Llama-3-8B)** on WikiText/The Pile; both χ_ℓ and persistence grow roughly monotonically with relative depth (persistence plateaus or slightly decreases in the last quarter of the network). After removing the sequence-wide mean, χ̃_ℓ tracks persistence even more closely — e.g., Gemma 2 2B on The Pile improves from **r = 0.86 (χ_ℓ) to r = 0.94 (χ̃_ℓ)** across all 26 layers (Fig. 20d, Table 7).
- **LLMs, causal ablation (Table 1):** ablating the single most persistent SAE feature decreases the mean within-episode autocorrelation by **−3.0% to −13.3%** across the five models and two corpora (e.g., Pythia-160M WikiText −13.3%, Gemma 2 9B WikiText −3.0%), versus **−0.7 to +0.0 for the active-but-non-persistent a-control** — an essentially null baseline. Ablating 3 latents reaches −24.3% (Pythia-160M, WikiText); ablating ALL active latents removes only −43.7% to −68.3%, reflecting imperfect SAE reconstruction. Moving the reference token outside the firing episode (e+1→e+3) shrinks the effect and it decays with distance for all models except Pythia.
- **Frequency confound ruled out (Appendix G, Fig. 20):** for Gemma 2 2B on The Pile, mean persistence of the 1000 most active latents grows from **ψ = 1.1 (layer 0) to 1.8** with depth in the true token order, but stays between **1.1 and 1.3 after within-sequence shuffling** — the depth growth of persistence is temporal, not a frequency artifact.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** The core move — generalizing token-coincidence autocorrelations to continuous activations and reading latent persistence off them — is genuinely simple and new as an operationalization of "abstraction increases with depth"; it complements curvature (Hosseini & Fedorenko), receptive-field (Skrill & Norman-Haignere), and Jacobian-lens (Gurnee et al.) measures by being fully task- and probe-free. The synthetic-data bridge (known latents → observable that transfers to LLMs) is the paper's best idea.
- **Rigor:** The RHM half is close to airtight — exact optimal loss, exact latent identities, OLS decomposition with residuals orthogonal by construction, and the 55–95% attribution is a strong quantitative claim. The training-dynamics result (autocorrelation plateaus appearing stage-by-stage at predicted lags) is a nontrivial falsifiable prediction that came out. The natural-language half is more correlational: the headline Fig. 4c relationships are Pearson correlations per model, and the causal arrow is supported only via SAE-feature ablations that are themselves proxy-dependent — ablating **all** active latents removes only 43.7–68.3% of the autocorrelation, an explicit admission that SAEs do not span the activation space, so the "persistence explains autocorrelation" quantification in real text inherits the SAE reconstruction gap.
- **Specific weaknesses:** (i) The one-layer-per-hierarchy-level story is clean only for RHM layers 1–2; the authors themselves note layer 3 already shows partial encodings of depth-4 variables and layer-3 great-grandparent persistence beyond first emergence, so the Fig. 1d sketch is a simplification the data only approximate. (ii) All RHM results are for a **single grammar and a single training run** (stated in Appendix A) — no seed variability. (iii) Llama-3-8B's r = 0.67 is markedly weaker than the Gemma models', and the paper does not dig into why (SAE quality? top-k vs JumpReLU?), which weakens the cross-model universality claim. (iv) Pythia is the one model where the ablation effect fails to decay with reference distance (e+1→e+3), acknowledged but unexplained. (v) The measure is blind to codes that rotate across positions (only aligned directions contribute — the authors flag that the full cross-covariance matrix would be needed), so "persistence" here means directional persistence; this is a real scope limit on the abstraction claim. (vi) No link is established to model behavior/output quality — the observable is representational only, and the fluctuation–dissipation program it motivates is aspirational.
- **Reproducibility:** Good on paper — all five LLMs and SAE suites are public (Gemma Scope, EleutherAI sparsify checkpoints sae-pythia-160m-32k / sae-llama-3-8b-32x, Qwen-Scope), corpora (The Pile, WikiText) are standard, and the RHM transformer's full training recipe is given (d=768, 5 blocks, 12 heads, Adam 3×10⁻³, 2²⁰ steps, 2²⁸ sentences). But no code repository is referenced, so the persistence bookkeeping (episode definitions, strict vs loose episodes) would need re-implementation.
- **Significance:** If the χ̃_ℓ non-monotonic layer profile (rise then plateau in the second half) holds up, it offers a cheap, training-free geometric diagnostic that converges with several independent reports of mid-layer abstraction peaks — potentially a standard layer-profiling tool. The main risk is overclaiming: the paper is careful with hedged language, but the title's "explains" is fully earned only in the synthetic setting.
- **Red flags:** None serious — no cherry-picked headline benchmarks (this is an analysis paper), honest reporting of the weakest model (Llama-3-8B) and the SAE reconstruction gap. Minor: the "correspondence to: gmail" and preprint status mean no peer review; the self-citation density around RHM is high but appropriate given the method's provenance.

## Strengths

- A minimal, well-motivated observable (one dot product expectation) that unifies token-level correlation statistics with representation-level analysis, requires no probes, tasks, or labels, and is trivially computable on any open model.
- The synthetic-to-real pipeline design: establish the mechanism exactly where latents are known (RHM), derive an observable that survives when latents are not known (SAE proxies), then test it on five public LLMs and two corpora.
- Genuine causal evidence structure: intervention (ablation) with a proper negative control (active-but-non-persistent feature, Δ ≈ 0) plus a frequency confound control (within-sequence shuffling), rather than relying on correlations alone.
- Quantitative theory-data contact throughout: exact subtree-entropy plateaus, predicted lag positions (1.5, 3.5, 7.5, 15.5), 55–95% attribution in RHM, per-model Pearson tables, and the χ → χ̃ correction that isolates the sequence-wide component.
- Honest scoping: the discussion explicitly states what Eq. 1 cannot see (orientation-changing codes), why |Δ| < 100% under full ablation, and frames the fluctuation–dissipation extension as future work rather than a claim.

## Limitations

- The natural-language conclusion depends on SAE features as latent-variable proxies; SAEs explain only part of the activation variance, so the persistence–autocorrelation link is quantitatively incomplete in real text (full ablation removes at most ~68% of autocorrelation).
- Eq. 1 measures persistence only along aligned directions (basis-invariant trace of the cross-covariance); latent codes written in rotating directions across positions are invisible to it, and the full D×D cross-covariance analysis is left to future work.
- The clean layer-by-layer hierarchy resolution demonstrated on RHM holds only approximately (clean at layers 1–2, mixed at 3–4), and RHM itself is a uniform binary tree — natural language's latent hierarchy is presumably shallower, noisier, and non-uniform, so the mapping from RHM stages to LLM layer profiles is qualitative.
- Single grammar, single seed for all synthetic results; no error bars on the 55–95% attribution.
- Cross-model spread is unexplained: from r = 0.93 (Gemma 2 9B) to r = 0.67 (Llama-3-8B), with Pythia also anomalous in the distance-decay ablation; SAE architecture differences (JumpReLU vs top-k) are a plausible but untested confound.
- No behavioral validation: nothing yet connects longer autocorrelation tails or persistence to downstream capabilities (long-context performance, in-context learning), and the proposed fluctuation–response relation is speculative.

## Relevance

这篇论文为"表征随深度变得更加抽象"提供了一个可操作、可计算的几何度量（激活自相关 + 潜变量持久性），并用合成数据机制解释加真实 LLM 因果消融双重验证——对任何关注 LLM 可解释性、表征几何与稀疏自编码器（SAE）特征动力学的研究都非常契合，也为逐层剖析开源模型提供了一个廉价、无需探针的新工具。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the integrated autocorrelation χ_ℓ (or its mean-removed version χ̃_ℓ) predict behavioral capabilities, e.g., long-context retrieval accuracy or in-context learning depth, across models of the same family trained on different data mixes?
- How much of the persistence–autocorrelation gap in real text is due to orientation-rotating codes? The proposed full cross-covariance E[φ_{T−τ}^⊤ φ_T] analysis would quantify directional vs rotating persistence — has anyone run it at scale?
- Why is Llama-3-8B's layer-level correlation (r = 0.67) so much weaker than Gemma 2 9B's (0.93)? Is it SAE quality (top-k vs JumpReLU), training data, or architecture (GQA, RoPE scaling)?
- Can the stage-wise emergence of autocorrelation plateaus on RHM be used as a training diagnostic — e.g., detecting when a model has learned depth-h structure without labels, and does it generalize to curriculum or mixed-depth data?
- Does the conjectured fluctuation–response relation hold: do layers with longer-ranged c_ℓ(τ) also propagate small activation perturbations over longer lags, as measurable with Jacobian-lens or activation-patching experiments?
- The RHM is a uniform binary tree; how do the predictions change for irregular, variable-arity, or stochastic hierarchies closer to real language, where latent spans overlap and compete?
