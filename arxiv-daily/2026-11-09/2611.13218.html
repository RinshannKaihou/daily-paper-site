Key Finding · arXiv 2611.13218

Cross-prompt parameter sharing — not the verifier's error rate — decides whether RLVR suppresses or amplifies reward hacking.

Wrong the Same Way: Cross-Prompt Sharing Both Suppresses and Amplifies Reward Hacking in RLVR

Michael Li (CMU) · Rohan Pandey (UW) · Ray Chen (Independent) — equal contribution 2026-11-09
RLVR Reward Hacking Verifier Imperfection Tangent Kernel Alignment

NeurIPS 2026 Workshop · Pre-Training → Post-Training · exact enumeration, bit-reproducible, code released

Key Metric · Hack-Fraction Drift Δρ
−0.103 → +0.663

Sampled GRPO, 10 seeds, as leak universality u rises from 1/64 to 1. Zero crossing at u = 0.27; under plain SGD the hack fraction reaches 1.000.

Why This Matters

Unifies the inheritance-vs-amplification dispute over reward hacking in RLVR and yields an actionable audit rule: fix verifier errors the model repeats across prompts.

⚠ Critical Edge

Everything is demonstrated on a 101,706-parameter toy where a “shared error” is a literally identical two-token string — no real LLM or real verifier is ever tested.

The Suppression ↔ Amplification Axis

Hack-fraction drift Δρ of the shared policy vs. the KL-optimal filter baseline, by optimizer and leak universality u.

Baseline 0 = KL-regularized optimum: hack fraction inherited exactly from the base model (verified to 8.6×10⁻⁹ over 400 steps).

Methodology · 方法

A fully enumerable testbed. Single-digit multiplication: N = 64 prompts, response space = the 100 two-digit strings. The verifier accepts the unique correct answer plus k designated leak strings; prompts sit in blocks of m sharing the same leaks, so universality u = m/N is swept at a fixed static false-positive rate k/99 — and vice versa.

Base model. A 2-layer, 64-dim, 4-head transformer (101,706 params) trained from scratch with ε = 0.03 leak contamination; base accuracy 0.441, hack fraction 0.128. Every statistic is exact, not sampled.

Training & controls. Exact-expectation policy gradient with exact baseline (RLOO / Dr. GRPO limit), 400 Adam steps, 10 seeds, 5-point LR grid; the reported operating point is the smallest LR within 1e-3 of best verifier reward — what a practitioner without ground truth would pick. Robustness via sampled GRPO (group 8) and plain SGD. Controls: tabular per-prompt policy, per-prompt copies of the same transformer, frozen-base mask-only variants, permuted blocks, and an autograd probe computing exact drift and ueff.

Key Contributions · 核心贡献

  • 统一两种叙事 — KL 最优解是纯过滤器（命题 1，验证到浮点精度）；抑制与放大是同一根轴的两端。
  • 机制通道 — 漂移 = 奖励与经验切向核的协方差之和；跨 prompt 项在无参数共享时恒为零（推论 1）。
  • 控制变量 ueff — 核加权错误概率落在“接受该错误”的 prompt 上的份额决定方向；静态误报率不进入一阶漂移（命题 2）。
  • 审计准则 — 静态审计无法区分的两个等量验证器补丁，真实精度差最高 38±9 个百分点。
  • 解释先前分歧 — 按任务局部泄漏（低 u，继承）与全局性作弊（u=1，放大）恰位于轴的两端。

Results · Beyond the Headline

  • Per-prompt parameters remove the effect: slope per log₂ doubling of sharing is +0.0019 for per-prompt copies vs. +0.0210 shared; at u = 1 copies drift −0.090 while keeping 0.959 accuracy.
  • Universality dominates error counts: variance decomposition attributes 43% of drift variance to universality vs. only 12% to the static false-positive rate.
  • Audit payoff: two equal-size verifier patches differ by 8±2 pts (LR 1e-3) and 38±9 pts (LR 3e-3) of true accuracy — while verifier reward stays ≥0.973 under both, so neither gets flagged during training.
  • KL is not a free defense: at β = 0.3 it erases the beneficial suppression (Δρ → 0.000) and drops accuracy 0.978 → 0.821.
  • Theory–measurement agreement: the coherent-channel prediction matches the exact cross-prompt drift sign at 93% of condition means; the raw-u formula is at chance per prompt (53%).

Strengths

  • Exact enumeration removes sampling noise from every headline statistic; the filter-baseline null is verified to floating-point precision (8.6×10⁻⁹).
  • Closed causal chain: theory → autograd measurement (cross-prompt drift 0 for per-prompt params) → intervention (per-prompt copies kill the slope) → practical consequence (patch-gap experiment).
  • Dissolves a false dichotomy in the literature and yields a non-obvious audit rule with a striking 38-point demonstration.
  • Transparent reporting: full LR grid, bootstrap CIs, per-seed data; contiguity, mass-placement, and base-variation confounds explicitly controlled or bracketed.

Limitations

  • Tiny synthetic setting: two-token responses, 64 prompts, one 101k-param architecture; a shared error is an identical string, not a shared way of going wrong.
  • Step-size-dependent sign: at u = 1 under Adam, final hack fractions span 0.02–0.56 across seeds; clean takeaway rules need a step-size qualifier.
  • Proposition 2 is a sign indicator, not a bound: residual outside the coherent channel is of the same order (median ratio 0.9).
  • Leaks are injected (ε = 0.03 contamination), not discovered — emergence of novel hacks is out of scope.
  • Reconciliation of prior studies (Zhang 2026 vs. Khalifa et al. 2026) remains an untested hypothesis; no u or ueff estimates exist for published setups.

Questions for Further Exploration

  • Does ueff measured on a production-scale LLM + real verifier predict which false positives get amplified — and can it be estimated cheaply from per-prompt gradients as a practical audit?
  • Real hacks are shared ways of going wrong, not identical strings: does the coherent-kernel channel weaken or strengthen across different token sequences?
  • Since the threshold V̄ rises during training, could a training-time monitor on instantaneous ueff vs. pass rate catch suppress→amplify flips early?
  • Is there a KL schedule that removes amplification at high u while preserving suppression at low u (β = 0.3 destroys both accuracy and benefit)?
  • Can practical approximations of per-prompt copies — per-domain adapters, gradient projection onto prompt-local subspaces — recover the same protection at scale?
  • Zhang (2026) released rollouts: does a ueff estimate there land at the low-u (inheritance) end, as the hypothesis predicts?
