> **一句话总结：** 本文在 Qwen3.6-27B、Gemma-3-12B、Gemma-3-27B-IT 三个模型上系统检验"可言语化工作区"（J-space）是否携带角色-填充符绑定：线性探针从完整残差流解码施/受事角色的准确率接近天花板（0.989/0.975/0.978），但从 J-space 解码（0.587/0.573/0.574）反而低于同秩随机子空间对照（0.643/0.589/0.590）；沿拟合方向做干预能改变模型"口头报告"的角色（读出位移 +0.94 log-odds）却不能超出对照地改变其真实绑定，消融实验仅在 Gemma-3-27B-IT 上检测到唯一的因果信号（+0.275 特异性损伤）——结论是工作区本质上是"概念袋"，基于工作区透镜的运行时对齐监测器会读到模型的角色报告而非角色绑定本身。

> **TL;DR:** The paper asks whether the "verbalizable workspace" (J-space, the small subspace the J-lens reads as natural language) carries role-filler bindings or just an unordered bag of concepts — the property a runtime alignment monitor would need to distinguish "the model plans to deceive the user" from "the user plans to deceive the model". On a 2,400-sentence minimal-pair stimulus set (600 families per model), linear probes decode agent/patient role at 0.989 from the full residual stream of Qwen3.6-27B but only 0.587 from J-space — below the 0.643 rank-matched random-subspace baseline (same pattern on Gemma-3-12B: 0.573 vs 0.589, and Gemma-3-27B-IT: 0.574 vs 0.590). Steering along fitted LRE role directions moves the role readout by +0.94 log-odds (agent push) on Gemma-3-12B yet leaves the binding score indistinguishable from strength-matched controls on all three models (real/control ratio ≈ 1.01 on Gemma-3-27B-IT), and ablating the top-10 workspace directions damages binding only on Gemma-3-27B-IT (+0.275 deficit, 95% CI [0.262, 0.288]). Net: the workspace steers what the model says about roles but is not the substrate it uses to compute them, so a workspace-lens monitor would miss role assignments on safety-relevant propositions that share a concept set.

## 核心贡献

- 对 Gurnee et al. (2026) 提出的"可言语化工作区"（verbalizable workspace / J-space）做了一次直接的、带因果检验的测试：工作区里的概念是否被绑定为语义角色（谁施事、谁受事），还是只是一个无序的"概念袋"——这是该工作明确留下的开放问题。
- 发现并刻画了**"读出（readout）与计算（computation）的分离"**：工作区中存在能操纵模型角色输出（读出）的方向，但工作区并不携带模型实际用于指派角色的表征——残差流可近乎完美解码角色，而被透镜丢弃的正交补也能（0.974–0.984），唯独 J-space 本身解码低于同秩随机子空间。
- 构建了一套能区分"真无绑定"与三类替代解释（估计器太弱、透镜工具伪影、模型特异性伪影）的因果评估设计：双透镜（J-lens / R-lens）× 双方向估计器（均值差 / LRE 梯度）× 三个强度匹配对照 + 同秩随机子空间对照，并带预先注册的强度下限与四档判定分类器（阳性 / 与对照不可区分 / 干净阴性 / 不可解释）。
- 给出对**运行时对齐监测的边界结论**：对共享同一概念集、仅角色指派不同的安全相关命题（模型骗用户 vs 用户骗模型），基于工作区方向的监测器读出的是模型的"角色报告"而非"角色绑定"，因此原则上无法区分这类命题的良性与危险状态。

## Key Contributions

- First direct causal test of role-filler binding *inside* a pre-existing, independently identified verbalizable workspace (J-space), answering the question Gurnee et al. (2026) explicitly left open — in contrast to Feng et al. (2025), which discovers binding subspaces post hoc from the residual stream.
- A clean dissociation between readout and computation: pushing fitted role directions in the workspace changes what the model *reports* about roles (signed, reversible readout displacement), but produces no binding-score effect beyond strength-matched controls; meanwhile role is near-ceiling linearly decodable from the residual stream and its orthogonal remainder, but not from J-space itself.
- A causal-evaluation design that separates a genuine absence of binding structure from three alternative explanations (weak estimator, tool-specific artifact, model-specific artifact) via two lens families, two direction estimators, rank-matched random controls, and internal positive controls — demonstrated by the ablation arm actually firing on Gemma-3-27B-IT.
- A concrete bound on the runtime alignment-monitoring case for the workspace-lens paradigm: on safety-relevant propositions whose benign and problematic states share a concept set, a workspace-lens monitor would surface role reports, not bindings.

## Methodology

The study distinguishes **readout** (what the model outputs about roles, e.g., answering "who treated someone?") from **computation** (the internal representation the model uses to assign entities to roles in the first place), and tests both with three experiments on Qwen3.6-27B (base, 64 layers), Gemma-3-12B (base, 48 layers), and Gemma-3-27B-IT (instruction-tuned, 62 layers), at a primary read layer ≈75% relative depth.

**Stimuli.** Each item is a family of four minimal-pair sentences crossing role (agent/patient) with linear position (first/second) of a target entity, across four constructions (active/passive, cleft, relative clause, dative). Three professional noun pairs (doctor/nurse, teacher/student, driver/passenger) × 50 families per (pair, construction) cell give 600 families / 2,400 sentences per model (a Qwen robustness run uses six pairs at 25 families per cell). Direction-fitting corpora are disjoint from evaluation at both template and entity level. The model answers a role probe and the readout is the log-odds between the two entity tokens' softmax probabilities.

**Decodability.** Closed-form ridge regression probes (±1 role labels) with leave-one-pair-out cross-validation are fit on four activation sources: the full residual stream; the J-space component (top K=16 atoms via nonnegative orthogonal matching pursuit against the J-lens dictionary); the orthogonal remainder (what the lens discards); and a matched-rank random orthonormal subspace (the capacity control). Robustness checks add quadratic probes (tensor-product-style multiplicative readout) and random-Fourier-feature probes, plus a seven-order-of-magnitude ridge-penalty sweep under nested cross-validation.

**Direction fitting and intervention.** Two estimators fit per-entity role directions: difference-of-means, and an LRE-gradient estimator (adapted from Chanin et al. 2024) that averages unit-normalized gradients of the readout logit contrast ∇h(z_entity − z_other). Bootstrap direction stability must clear 0.8 mean cosine (LRE: 0.967–0.999). The push edit h ← h + s·c·r is applied at the read layer at the entity token (or sentence-final token), calibrated per (model, estimator, site) as the smallest c on a doubling grid that shifts log-odds by a pre-registered 0.5 floor. It is compared against three strength-matched controls: a non-participant entity's direction, a seeded random direction, and a shuffled-label direction. The **binding score** is a difference-in-differences on the family's log-odds gap averaged over both push signs, so a bag-of-concepts workspace yields ≈0; significance uses family-level bootstrap (10,000 resamples) and exact sign-flip permutation with Holm-Bonferroni correction, and outcomes are classified by a pre-registered verdict classifier (positive binding / indistinguishable from controls / clean negative / uninterpretable strength failure).

**Ablation.** The projection of the residual stream onto the ten most active J-lens directions is zeroed during the forward pass; the binding-specific deficit is the normalized drop in the role-probe margin minus the normalized drop in a counterbalanced concept-recall margin, compared against a matched-rank random-subspace ablation, with a difficulty-matching precondition (baseline binding and recall accuracies within 0.02).

## Results & Highlights

- **Role decoding from J-space falls below a rank-matched random subspace on all three models** (within-pair accuracy at the sentence-final token, chance = 0.50; Table 1). Qwen3.6-27B: full residual 0.989, orthogonal remainder 0.984, random subspace 0.643, J-space 0.587 (i.e., J-space is 0.056 *below* the random control while the residual stream is near ceiling); Gemma-3-12B: residual 0.975 vs J-space 0.573 vs random 0.589; Gemma-3-27B-IT: residual 0.978 vs J-space 0.574 vs random 0.590. The gap survives quadratic probes (J-space 0.522 on Qwen entity token, 0.449 on Gemma-3-12B, both under the random subspace), random-Fourier probes, and the ridge-penalty sweep (J-space at best 0.645 vs random subspace 0.678–0.724).
- **Steering dissociates readout from binding.** On Gemma-3-12B, the LRE push moves the readout by +0.94 log-odds (toward-agent), −0.99 (toward-patient), vs −0.01 for a matched random direction — yet on the binding score the real push never separates from controls: on Gemma-3-27B-IT the real/control ratio is 0.83 at c=64, 1.01 at c=128 (pooled −0.0568 vs non-participant control −0.0561), 0.84 at c=256; on Qwen3.6-27B at c=4 the pooled effect is −0.0006 vs control −0.0046; on Gemma-3-12B at c=256, −0.0140 vs −0.0308. The verdict classifier returns "indistinguishable from controls" on all three models.
- **Ablation damages binding on exactly one of three models.** Zeroing the top-10 J-space directions on Gemma-3-27B-IT at the entity token produces a binding-specific deficit of +0.275 (95% CI [0.262, 0.288]) with concept accuracy held at 0.920 and edit magnitudes within 7% of the random control; the deficit replicates across three lens variants (J-lens 0.284, matched J-lens 0.334, R-lens 0.297, all above the 0.05 pre-registered floor). Qwen3.6-27B shows no binding-specific damage at either site (+0.014 entity / +0.000 final vs random-subspace controls −0.009/−0.007), and Gemma-3-12B shows none at the sentence-final site (+0.008); its entity-token number (+0.273) is discarded because the recall control failed the difficulty-matching precondition.
- **Cross-pair transfer at the entity token** (leave-one-pair-out, chance 0.50): the full residual transfers at 0.594–0.644 and the orthogonal remainder at 0.603–0.626 across the three models, while J-space stays at chance (0.510–0.524) — the residual stream carries a filler-general role axis that the workspace does not.
- **The identity-swap intervention-strength check failed on all three models** (counterpart-probability shifts of 0.0012 on Qwen3.6-27B, 0.0048 on Gemma-3-12B, 0.0001 on Gemma-3-27B-IT, all below the 0.05 floor), which the authors disclose as limiting how far the steering null can be pushed.

## Authors & Credibility

Seven authors with equal contribution by the first three (Dawlah, McMillin, Zhang), spanning University of Texas at Dallas, MiraCosta College, University of Waterloo, UC Irvine, University of Washington, MIT, and Mila / Université de Montréal — a credible, multi-site academic collaboration in the mechanistic-interpretability community. The paper appears at the NeurIPS 2026 workshop "Interpretability as a Science", an appropriate venue for this line of work. Credibility signals are unusually strong for a negative-result paper: pre-registered thresholds (0.5 log-odds strength floor, 0.8 direction-stability cosine, 0.05 causal-involvement floor, 0.02 difficulty-matching tolerance), 10,000-resample bootstrap plus exact sign-flip permutation tests with Holm-Bonferroni correction, a four-outcome verdict classifier that includes an explicit "uninterpretable" category, disclosed failures (the identity-swap calibration failure and the incomplete Qwen seed set), and public code, data, and analysis at github.com/Reyadawlah/role-filler-binding-jspace.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** Real and well-posed. Prior work either introduced the workspace (Gurnee et al. 2026, which explicitly left binding open) or discovered binding subspaces post hoc from the residual stream (Feng et al. 2025's propositional probes); this is the first test of whether binding already lives *inside* a pre-specified verbalizable subspace, and the readout-vs-computation dissociation is a genuinely useful conceptual cut.
- **Rigor:** Controls are exemplary in places — rank-matched random subspaces to decouple content from capacity, non-participant and shuffled-label directions, two lenses, two estimators, nested cross-validation. But the headline causal null on steering has a disclosed hole: the identity-swap positive control for the editing machinery failed on all three models (shifts 0.0001–0.0048 vs the 0.05 floor), so "no binding effect" is consistent both with the LRE direction being irrelevant to binding *and* with the edit simply being too weak to reach it. The authors say this plainly, but it means the steering arm cannot adjudicate its own null.
- **Confound in the one positive result:** the ablation fires only on Gemma-3-27B-IT, which is simultaneously the only instruction-tuned model and the largest Gemma — the paper concedes scale and instruction tuning are confounded and a base Gemma-3-27B run is missing.
- **Scope of evidence vs scope of claim:** the motivating scenario is deception monitoring, but the evidence is agent/patient binding over three professional noun pairs (doctor/nurse, teacher/student, driver/passenger) in four English constructions, at a single layer per model, on three dense transformers. Coreference, temporal relations, multi-entity planning, other architectures and languages are untested, and the extrapolation from "the lawyer treated the doctor" to "the model plans to deceive the user" is a long lever.
- **Data quirks handled honestly:** the sentence-final cross-pair transfer inverts below chance for *every* activation source (0.283–0.320), which the authors attribute to the probing setup and use to justify reporting within-pair as primary — defensible, but it is a post-hoc re-designation of the primary metric; likewise the Gemma-3-12B entity-token deficit (+0.273) is dropped via the difficulty-matching precondition, correctly but conveniently for the clean narrative.
- **Reproducibility:** good — public code/data, deterministic probes, seeded controls, pre-registered thresholds; only 3 of 5 Qwen seeds are complete, which is disclosed.
- **Significance:** an important bounding result for anyone building workspace-lens alignment monitors; as with all negative interpretability results, its force depends on the untested assumption that the synthetic binding task resembles the relational structure of real safety-relevant cognition.

## Strengths

- Attacks a precise, decision-relevant open question (does the workspace bind roles?) with a falsifiable design rather than anecdotal lens readouts.
- The rank-matched random subspace control is the right capacity control and is used consistently across probing, steering, and ablation.
- The verdict classifier with an "uninterpretable strength failure" outcome institutionalizes honesty about underpowered interventions — rare and commendable.
- Genuine internal positive control: the Gemma-3-27B-IT ablation effect shows the design *can* detect binding involvement when present, strengthening the nulls on the other two models.
- Clean dissociation framing (readout vs computation) that will travel well to other interpretability debates.

## Limitations

- The identity-swap strength check failed on all models, leaving the steering null ambiguous between "no binding substrate" and "intervention too weak" (authors' own admission).
- The single positive causal result is confounded between instruction tuning and scale; no base Gemma-3-27B comparison.
- Narrow stimulus domain: three (or six, in one robustness run) professional noun pairs, agent/patient roles, English, dense transformers, primary experiments at one layer per model.
- The leap from synthetic two-entity sentences to safety-relevant propositions like deception planning is unargued by experiment.
- No comparison against alternative verbalizable readouts (e.g., Natural Language Autoencoders), so the null is established for the workspace-lens paradigm specifically, not for verbalizable monitoring in general.
- Qwen seed replication incomplete (3 of 5 seeds at submission).

## Relevance

这篇论文直击可解释性与对齐监测的核心问题：模型内部可读的"言语化工作区"到底携带多少结构化（关系性）信息。对任何关注 LLM 内部表征、线性探针/干预方法、以及基于内部状态的安全监测（latent monitoring）可行性的研究而言，这个"概念袋 vs 绑定"的阴性结果与精心设计的因果检验范式都极具参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does a base (non-instruction-tuned) Gemma-3-27B show the same ablation deficit, disentangling scale from instruction tuning?
- Can a stronger, better-calibrated intervention (e.g., patching the residual from the opposite-role sentence, as the authors propose) turn the steering null into an interpretable result either way?
- Does the readout-vs-computation dissociation persist under Natural Language Autoencoders or other verbalizable readouts, or is it specific to the J-lens/R-lens workspace?
- Do richer relational structures — coreference, temporal relations, multi-entity planning, or the deception scenario that motivates the work — show the same bag-of-concepts behavior in the workspace?
- Is the near-ceiling role decodability of the orthogonal remainder localized to specific circuits (e.g., binding IDs à la Feng & Steinhardt), and can those circuits be monitored directly instead of the workspace?
