> **一句话总结：** 该文结合 DRAM Bender 实测、器件测量与经约 70 万器件校准的 TCAD 仿真，提出统一器件级物理框架，用空间范围递增的三种机制——陷阱辅助电子迁移（EM）、字线电容耦合（CC）、读出放大器介导扰动（SAMD）——同时解释 RowHammer、RowPress 与 ColumnDisturb；其中读出放大器钳位字线使未选中单元 OFF 态漏电较保持态条件提升约 9 倍（I_CD/I_RT ≈ 9），并据此解释了"0→1 失效更早出现、高锤击数下却以 1→0 为主"等长期未解的极性签名。

> **TL;DR:** This paper unifies RowHammer, RowPress, and the newly discovered ColumnDisturb under a single device-level physical framework, triangulating DIMM measurements on real DRAM chips via DRAM Bender at 85 °C (RowHammer: tRAS = 36 ns, tRP = 14 ns, 8×10⁷ activations; RowPress: tRAS = 70.2 μs, 5.68×10⁴ activations; 4-s retention baseline), on-chip access-device measurements, and TCAD simulations calibrated against array-averaged leakage from ~700,000 devices. It attributes all three modes to three mechanisms acting on the same floating storage node — trap-assisted electron migration (EM, local), wordline capacitive coupling (CC, decaying with row separation), and sense-amplifier-mediated disturb (SAMD, remote via shared column circuitry). Compared with the retention-like precharged-bitline baseline, the SA-held rail bias yields a measured ~9× higher OFF-state leakage (I_CD/I_RT ≈ 9 at V_G = −0.2 V), and the framework resolves previously puzzling signatures such as the RowHammer 0→1-early vs 1→0-at-high-count polarity crossover and the NWL→PWL aggressor-effectiveness reversal between 36-ns and 70.2-μs ON times.

## 核心贡献

- **统一器件级物理框架**：将作用于同一浮置存储节点的三种机制——陷阱辅助电子迁移（EM，局部且对陷阱位置/能量高度敏感）、字线间电容耦合（CC，随 aggressor–victim 字线间距增大而衰减）、读出放大器介导扰动（SAMD，经共享列电路作用于远处单元）——整合为一个框架，用空间范围直接映射实测的失效空间分类。
- **识别 SAMD 为被忽视的电路级贡献**：指出此前器件级研究（如 Yang & Lin 把位线固定在 V_CORE/2）忽略了感知/恢复期间读出放大器施加的轨级位线偏置；该偏置将未选中单元的 OFF 态漏电流提升约 9 倍，从而解释远程 ColumnDisturb 群体持续高于保持基线、并在子阵列边界处阶跃变化的现象。
- **解释多项此前矛盾的失效签名**：(1) RowHammer 首次失效中 0→1 比 1→0 更早出现，而高锤击数下 1→0 占主导（tail-vs-bulk：稀有高效陷阱构型决定早期尾部，CC+SAMD 主导主体且 data-0 扰动自限制）；(2) 长 ON 时间 RowPress 仅出现 1→0 失效；(3) PWL/NWL 攻击有效性随 ON 时长（36 ns → 70.2 μs）反转；(4) ColumnDisturb 的数据依赖极性（1→0 有、0→1 无）。
- **时间维度演化图像**：RowPress 被诠释为从初始 RowHammer 式 EM+CC+SAMD 瞬态过渡到由 SAMD 主导的持续偏置泄漏体制，多微秒保持期累积的存储节点电压漂移远大于初始 ~35 ns 瞬态。
- **为紧凑可靠性模型铺路**：提出以字线/位线历史、存储状态和阵列连接为自变量的解析电荷转移项作为器件物理到系统级可靠性建模的路径。

## Key Contributions

- Proposes a **unified device-level physical framework** in which three mechanisms act on the same floating storage node: trap-assisted **electron migration (EM)** — local and strongly sensitive to trap position and energy; **capacitive coupling (CC)** — wordline-to-wordline parasitic coupling that weakens with aggressor–victim separation; and **sense-amplifier-mediated disturb (SAMD)** — a circuit-level contribution that modifies unselected-cell leakage under the rail-level bitline bias and extends through shared column circuitry.
- **Identifies SAMD as an overlooked contribution**: prior device-level analyses (e.g., Yang & Lin, who held the bitline at a constant V_CORE/2) omit the SA-driven rail-level bitline bias during sensing/restoration; the paper shows this bias raises OFF-state leakage ~9× for a physical-high cell, explaining why the remote ColumnDisturb population stays above retention and changes abruptly at subarray boundaries.
- **Resolves previously puzzling failure signatures** with one framework: (1) 0→1 RowHammer failures appear at lower first-failure hammer counts (HC_first) than 1→0, yet 1→0 dominates at high hammer counts — explained by a tail-versus-bulk picture in which rare, favorably positioned traps drive the early 0→1 tail while CC/SAMD with self-limiting data-0 leakage governs the bulk; (2) only 1→0 failures occur under long-duration RowPress because sustained leakage destabilizes data 1 and stabilizes data 0; (3) the more-effective aggressor geometry reverses from NWL (36-ns pulse) to PWL (70.2-μs hold); (4) ColumnDisturb's data-dependent 1→0-only polarity follows from the same SAMD electron-accumulation direction.
- **Temporal interpretation of RowPress**: RowPress evolves from an initial RowHammer-like transient (EM + CC + SAMD) into a sustained-bias, leakage-dominated regime, explaining how only 5.68×10⁴ activations can produce failure peaks comparable to 8×10⁷ RowHammer activations.
- **Roadmap for compact reliability models**: mechanism-informed analytical charge-transfer terms parameterized by WL/BL history, stored state, and array connectivity, with device measurements/TCAD guiding form and sign, and DIMM data constraining population distributions.

## Methodology

The work triangulates three evidence layers:

1. **DIMM-level characterization** on an FPGA-based DRAM Bender platform with low-level ACT/PRE control, at 85 °C with a 4-s retention reference and a complementary data pattern (victim rows initialized opposite to the aggressor). Protocols: RowHammer = repeated ACT–PRE with tRAS = 36 ns, tRP = 14 ns, 8×10⁷ activations; RowPress = same sequence but 70.2-μs aggressor ON time, 5.68×10⁴ activations; ColumnDisturb = RowHammer timing (36 ns / 14 ns, 8×10⁷ accesses) but with the activated row spatially remote from victims yet sharing column circuitry. After stress, victim regions are read back to count bitflips by location and polarity.
2. **Access-device measurements** of leakage under controlled terminal biases: drain bias 1.0 V, body bias −0.7 V, source bias 0.5 V (retention-like) vs 0 V (ColumnDisturb-like), sweeping the gate around the OFF-wordline bias V_BBW = −0.2 V.
3. **TCAD simulation** coupling the access-device geometry (6F² saddle-fin) to a distributed wordline RC network and the storage-node circuit: Fermi–Dirac statistics, mobility and high-field saturation models, doping-dependent SRH recombination, band-to-band tunneling; representative coupling configuration with 1.25-ns WL rise/fall, V_PP = 2.5 V, V_BBW = −0.2 V, R_WL = 1 kΩ, C_SN = 10 fF, C_C = 515 fF. The baseline device is calibrated against array-averaged leakage from ~700,000 devices, focusing on the OFF-state gate-bias range perturbed by wordline coupling. Static bias snapshots isolate individual electrical conditions; transient simulations follow the floating storage node voltage V_SN through applied sequences.

The analysis proceeds from a measured spatial taxonomy (bitflips vs row address; local peaks for RowHammer/RowPress, plateau-like remote population for ColumnDisturb with steps at subarray boundaries), to mechanism identification with distinct spatial ranges, to mechanism-decomposition (EM+CC+SAMD vs CC+SAMD vs SAMD-only per-cycle ΔV_SN) and duration-dependent transient-vs-sustained comparisons, and finally to polarity/geometry signatures (TCAD current-density maps and ΔV_SN orderings at 36 ns vs 70.2 μs).

## Results & Highlights

- **SAMD leakage evidence**: for a physical-high storage state, changing the bitline bias from the retention-like precharged level (V_CORE/2 = 0.5 V) to the SA-held rail (0 V) increases the measured/fitted OFF-state current by approximately **ninefold (I_CD/I_RT ≈ 9) at V_G = −0.2 V** — direct device-level evidence that the sense-amplifier boundary condition, not the retention baseline, governs unselected-cell leakage during active access.
- **Spatial taxonomy quantification**: at 85 °C with identical readback, RowHammer (8×10⁷ activations, tRAS = 36 ns) and RowPress (**only 5.68×10⁴ activations**, tRAS = 70.2 μs) both produce strong localized failure peaks on the nearest victim rows in the aggressor subarray, whereas ColumnDisturb (8×10⁷ activations) produces a plateau-like population that stays **above the 4-s retention baseline** (which remains ≈0) across the exposed region and drops abruptly at subarray boundaries — in the open-bitline architecture, neighboring subarrays share only ~one half of their bitline segments with the aggressor's sense amplifiers, matching the stepwise profile.
- **RowHammer polarity crossover**: measured HC_first distributions show 0→1 bitflips emerging at **lower hammer counts than 1→0**, while measured bitflip-count distributions at the full 8×10⁷ hammer count show **1→0 failures outnumbering 0→1** — two regimes of the same cell-vulnerability distribution rather than contradictory measurements.
- **Trap-position sensitivity (TCAD)**: sweeping trap sites near the PWL shows simulated per-cycle charge loss varying by **orders of magnitude** with trap position (hammer cycles required for a 0.1-V storage-node shift span orders of magnitude under double-sided stress with 36-ns aggressor ON time) — trap presence alone does not determine EM strength.
- **Mechanism decomposition (TCAD, 6F² saddle-fin)**: under double-sided RowHammer, removing EM substantially reduces per-cycle ΔV_SN while **SAMD alone produces only a small response**, i.e., EM dominates the simulated RowHammer response in this structure (the authors caution the hierarchy may differ in 4F²/3D DRAM).
- **RowPress temporal evolution**: a 7.8-μs PWL hold in TCAD accumulates a **substantially larger storage-node shift than the initial ~35-ns transient**, and the sustained-bias regime reproduces both the 1→0-only long-duration polarity and the aggressor reversal — **NWL gives the larger ΔV_SN for a 36-ns pulse, whereas PWL gives the larger shift for a 70.2-μs hold**, consistent with the experimental NWL→PWL reversal reported by Luo et al.

## Authors & Credibility

The work joins the Beijing Superstring Academy of Memory Technology (Bai, Wang, Li, Jiang, Huang, G. Wang, Meng) with ETH Zurich's SAFARI group (Luo, Olgun, Yüksel, Bostancı, J. Wang, Mutlu). This is the team that created the underlying evidence base: DRAM Bender (Olgun et al., IEEE TCAD 2023), RowPress (Luo et al., ISCA 2023), ColumnDisturb (Yüksel et al., MICRO 2025), and Variable Read Disturbance (Olgun et al., HPCA 2025), plus their own prior RowHammer/RowPress device-level modeling (arXiv:2607.28233). Onur Mutlu's group is arguably the leading academic authority on experimental DRAM reliability. The characterization infrastructure, protocols, and phenomena being explained are therefore largely the authors' own, which lends strong internal consistency to the unification, and the bibliography (17 refs) is current and appropriate.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: The genuinely new element is SAMD — elevating the sense-amplifier/bitline boundary condition to a co-equal disturbance mechanism — and the unification of ColumnDisturb (published 2025) into a device-level picture. The EM/CC treatment substantially extends the authors' own arXiv:2607.28233 (RowHammer+RowPress modeling); readers of that work will find Sections 3.1's EM/CC parts incremental.
- **Rigor**: The TCAD baseline is calibrated only against an **array-averaged** transfer characteristic from ~700,000 devices; the authors candidly note this "does not by itself establish the distribution of first-failure thresholds." The tail-vs-bulk story rests on a "working hypothesis" of rare, favorably positioned traps that is supported by example TCAD scans near the PWL (Fig. 3c) but not by any direct trap observation (no charge-pumping, TEM, or single-device tail statistics) — the orders-of-magnitude trap-position spread is shown for one trap neighborhood, not a systematic device-wide scan.
- **Simulation/experiment mismatch**: the representative RowPress simulation uses a **7.8-μs hold while the DIMM protocol uses 70.2 μs** — a ~9× extrapolation that the paper flags but does not close; per-cycle decomposition bars (Fig. 4a) "compare simulated configurations rather than independent additive contributions," so the EM/CC/SAMD split is illustrative, not a calibrated decomposition.
- **Generalizability**: the EM-dominance conclusion is demonstrated only for a 6F² saddle-fin structure (4F² and 3D DRAM deferred to future work), and the subarray-boundary argument (~one half of neighboring-subarray bitlines sharing SAs) is specific to the open-bitline architecture measured. The DRAM vendor/generation of the tested DIMMs is not identified in the text.
- **Reproducibility**: protocols (timings, counts, temperature, data patterns) and TCAD parameters are fully specified and DRAM Bender is open-source, so the DIMM experiments are reproducible in principle; however, the TCAD deck and trap-parameter sets are not released, and single-temperature (85 °C), complementary-pattern-default testing limits independent verification of the polarity claims.
- **Overclaiming check**: claims are mostly appropriately hedged ("working hypothesis," "consistent with," "provides an interpretation"); Section 5 is explicitly a roadmap — no compact model is actually built or validated, so system-level impact remains potential rather than demonstrated. Variable read disturbance (temporal trap-occupancy fluctuation) is declared out of scope.

## Strengths

- Triangulates three independent evidence layers (DIMM, device, TCAD) rather than relying on simulation alone, with TCAD anchored to ~700k-device measured leakage.
- One framework with three spatially distinct mechanisms cleanly explains a remarkably broad set of signatures: localized RowHammer/RowPress peaks, remote subarray-stepped ColumnDisturb, RowPress polarity, PWL/NWL reversal, and the RowHammer first-failure vs high-count polarity crossover.
- Identifies a concrete, overlooked boundary condition (SA-held rail-level bitline bias) and corrects a common simplification in prior device-level RowHammer simulations (constant V_CORE/2 bitline).
- Honest scoping: the authors explicitly state what the average-curve calibration does and does not establish, and defer 4F²/3D DRAM and VRD to future work.

## Limitations

- The tail-versus-bulk trap hypothesis lacks direct experimental verification of the rare high-effectiveness trap configurations it posits.
- TCAD conditions diverge from DIMM protocols (7.8 μs vs 70.2 μs RowPress hold; representative rather than per-chip device geometry), so quantitative mapping from simulated ΔV_SN to measured bitflip counts is not established.
- Results are shown for one 6F² saddle-fin structure, one open-bitline architecture, one temperature (85 °C), and (by default) one complementary data pattern; vendor/technology of the DIMMs is undisclosed.
- No compact analytical reliability model is delivered, and no mitigation strategy is evaluated — the system-level payoff remains prospective.
- Temporal variability (VRD) and fluctuating trap occupancy are explicitly excluded, though they plausibly interact with the proposed trap-effectiveness picture.

## Relevance

该文直接覆盖 DRAM 读扰动（RowHammer / RowPress / ColumnDisturb）的器件级机理统一与硬件安全含义，为内存可靠性建模与 RowHammer 攻防研究提供了首个跨三种扰动模式的物理框架，与关注 DRAM 可靠性、读扰动攻击与缓解的兴趣高度契合。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How does the EM/CC/SAMD hierarchy change in 4F² and 3D-stacked DRAM cells, where access-transistor geometry and wordline coupling differ substantially from the 6F² saddle-fin structure studied?
- Can the rare, high-effectiveness trap configurations posited by the tail-vs-bulk hypothesis be observed directly (e.g., via charge pumping, single-device statistics, or trap spectroscopy) rather than inferred from HC_first distributions?
- Does SAMD enable new attack surfaces that deliberately stress shared column circuitry (e.g., column-strided accesses) to induce bitflips at large row distances, and would SA-aware refresh or bitline-parking policies mitigate them?
- How does the framework extend to variable read disturbance, where fluctuating trap occupancy interacts with the proposed trap-effectiveness distribution, and across temperature/voltage corners other than 85 °C?
- Can the proposed compact analytical charge-transfer terms, once calibrated, quantitatively predict failure counts for access patterns not used in calibration, such as interleaved RowHammer+RowPress sequences or realistic workload traces?
