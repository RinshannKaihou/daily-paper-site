Why This Matters02

A real deployment pain point, systematically measured: a precision-scaling law plus "report flips with sequence length and batch size" — directly useful to LLM evaluation and serving.

Critical Edge03

You trade 1.3–2.9 accuracy points for a partial fix: the worst case doesn't move (τ100 unchanged), benefits are in-domain only — fp32 or batch-invariant kernels buy full determinism.

worst-case verification ⊄ margin training

Methodology 04

Two stages: measure the noise, then train against it.

  • Characterization protocol. Define the top-1/top-2 logit gap g and its batch-induced perturbation δ (batch-of-B vs. alone). An identity control — re-running sequences alone — returned exactly zero flips. Nine candidate predictors of σ were tested (batch size, position, attention entropy, near-tie count, …); none explains >1.3× variation, so σ is a constant per model/precision/hardware.
  • Training objective. A hinge penalty on the model's own top-two gap, added to cross-entropy:
L = L_CE + λ · max(0, m − (ℓ(1) − ℓ(2))),  m = 2·Q₀.₉₉₉
  • The margin is derived, not tuned: m is set to twice the measured 99.9th-percentile noise from one profiling pass — so the leader survives the worst case where it falls by Q₀.₉₉₉ while the runner-up rises by Q₀.₉₉₉.
  • Worst-case subtraction beats noise injection — 49% of measured perturbations are exactly zero, so sampled noise delivers no signal on half of all steps.
  • Setup. Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct fine-tuned on GSM8K (MBPP as out-of-domain check); 200 steps, batch 16, λ = 1, fp32 master weights + bf16 autocast; NVIDIA L4/T4 and Apple Metal for cross-platform checks; paired McNemar + bootstrap over sequences.

Yet efficiency (fragility reduction per unit perplexity cost) peaks exactly at the derived margin: 9.39 at 2·Q₀.₉₉₉ vs 6.85 at 1.0Q and 1.61 at 3.0Q — and the peak location transfers across four model families under pre-registration.

  • Accidental batch invariance. At sequence length 512: zero perturbations at every batch size; at 256/288: ~54–56% of steps perturbed. Any flip rate is meaningless without (seq len, batch size).
  • SFT alone is a strong baseline — plain fine-tuning cut flips 22% and improved perplexity 26%.
  • Cross-platform. Ops that flip 0.19% on CUDA are bit-identical on Apple Metal.

Strengths 06

  • Turns a folklore annoyance into a precisely measured object, with two portable findings: perturbation scales exactly with mantissa-derived precision, and flip rates are uninterpretable without sequence length and batch size.
  • The margin is derived from a worst-case argument, not tuned — and the efficiency peak empirically confirms the derivation, transferring across four model families under pre-registration (p ≈ 0.002).
  • Exceptional hygiene: identity controls everywhere, seed replication, paired McNemar tests, bootstrap over sequences not tokens, and an explicit correction of their own earlier unpaired analysis.
  • Radical transparency: seven rejected hypotheses reported with the falsifying evidence — including the demise of their own pre-registered viability diagnostic.
  • Practitioner freebies: SFT alone cuts flips 22%; base-model-on-prose trigger rates ~88% vs 18.56% for instruct models on reasoning benchmarks.

Limitations 07

  • All training below 0.5B parameters on a single 22GB GPU; the 7B characterization used 4-bit loading, which alters the arithmetic.
  • NVIDIA Turing/Ada only (plus Metal); σ transfers across their two GPUs but the maximum perturbation does not (0.0859 vs 0.1328) — worst-case claims may not carry across architectures.
  • Systems evaluation is GSM8K-only on one sub-0.5B model; no transfer to MBPP, and no second in-domain reasoning task.
  • Method is model-dependent and unpredictable: all failures are GPT-2-lineage or Pythia; the failed Q/g diagnostic means practitioners cannot predict in advance whether their model will benefit.
  • λ was fixed at 1 for all sweeps — the joint (λ, m) space is unstudied; schedules were short (200 steps), so long-run Pareto behavior is unknown.
  • The headline phenomenon is kernel-library- and architecture-specific; accidental-invariance shapes are untested for stability across cuBLAS/CUDA versions.

Questions for Further Exploration 08

  • Why do GPT-2/Pythia models fail (and initially get worse) while OPT/SmolLM2/Qwen peak at the predicted ratio 2 — unembedding activation scale, or parallel attention/FFN architecture?
  • Does the efficiency peak at m = 2·Q₀.₉₉₉ survive at 1B–7B scale and on Hopper/Blackwell GPUs with different kernel libraries?
  • Can joint (λ, m) tuning, or a curriculum on m, recover part of the 1.3–2.9 accuracy-point cost while keeping the 29–35% divergence reduction?
  • Since 49% of perturbations are exactly zero and δ behaves as a small set of arithmetic paths rather than noise, could an analytic model of the kernel's reduction order replace empirical Q₀.₉₉₉ profiling?
  • Would margin training combined with selective verification at thresholds below τ ≈ 2 — where trigger rates drop 14–19% — yield a net serving-cost win, given the two do not compose at the worst case?
  • Do the accidental batch-invariance shapes persist across cuBLAS/CUDA versions — and could serving systems deliberately pad to them as a zero-cost determinism heuristic?
