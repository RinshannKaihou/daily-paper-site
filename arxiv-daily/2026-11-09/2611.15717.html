Key Finding · 核心发现

A celebrated +36-point MATH gain is 95.2% answer formatting — while capabilities outside the reward's purview silently collapse.

生产级流水线实证：奖励视野之外的能力会被静默遗忘。并给出可复用的诊断（分解分数增益）与修复（语言感知奖励、无奖励蒸馏）。

Critical Edge · 最大保留

The recovery bundles new data, reward shaping, group size (16→8), and the advantage floor into one intervention — so no causal claim about the language reward itself survives.

— Critical Assessment / Limitations

Methodology · 方法

  • Shared origin — SFT-INIT from a 15.2M-row curriculum (64% English / 36% Indic, 8 languages, native + romanized script; ~17,205 H200 GPU-hours) on a 30B-total/3B-active MoE.
  • RL-BASE (released as gnani-evon-v3.3-30B-A3B) — GRPO on judged math, JSON-schema conformance, a tool-call workplace, MCQA; no language, script, or length terms; 16 completions/prompt.
  • RL-INDIC — adds an Indic instruction-following environment: R = R_task + 0.3·R_lang − 0.3·R_rep, language scored only on the post-reasoning answer; 9.9% English guardrail pool; advantage divisor floored at max(σ, 0.05) — binds for 44% of groups.
  • Round 2 judge — 26B/4B generative MoE trained with GRPO on preference pairs; circular judging ring cancels positional bias.
  • Round 3 RL-OPD — reward-free online distillation from RL-INDIC: no reward model, verifier, or KL-to-reference; equal-weight forward/reverse truncated KL over teacher top-k, chosen for "collapse-shaped" regressions.
  • Eval — matched 55-metric campaign (one harness/mode/decoding config) plus a direct branch-vs-branch comparison.

  • Reward differential — Latin-script answers earn +0.168 more reward in retained mixed-script groups (bootstrap interval reported; an association, not a controlled effect).
  • Tool-policy side effects — BFCL v4 parallel-call categories collapse to ~0 while multi-turn accuracy rises 34.0→58.0; TauBench telecom +26.90 but SWE-bench Verified −10.60.
  • Script ≠ task — in-script output on translated IndicIFEval falls 95.1%→84.6% even as task accuracy improves; no intervention restores script compliance.

Strengths · 优点

  • Quantifies, rather than asserts, the gap between benchmark scores and capability — the boxed-emission decomposition is directly reusable.
  • Real production lineage with a public released checkpoint and an unusually transparent correction log (a wrong SFT baseline was caught and fixed: AIME "+40.00" → +1.46).
  • Clean separation of language effect from task-shape effect via the within-XQuAD contrast.
  • The reward-free distillation rescue (F1 17.67→47.13) is a practical, mechanism-plausible remedy for collapse-shaped regressions.
  • Dedicated threats-to-validity section pre-empts the bundling, single-seed, and harness-drift objections.

Limitations · 局限

  • No reward-term ablation — data, reward, group size, and floor change together; causality is unknowable.
  • Single seed, single model family; the three-round trace is scored on one benchmark only; no per-item intervals on deltas.
  • Script compliance is never restored; the script-differential estimate is sensitive to length filtering and rollout selection.
  • Two load-bearing benchmarks (IndicIFEval-ground, XorQA-Indic) have unverified construction.
  • MATH decomposition skips semantic regrading of unboxed answers — "true capability" stays open.
  • Only RL-BASE is public; the sibling, judge, and distilled checkpoints are internal.

Questions for Further Exploration · 待探索

  1. Which bundle component — Indic data, 0.3·R_lang, repetition penalty, group size, or the advantage floor — drives the recovery?
  2. Would semantic regrading of unboxed MATH answers confirm that conditional accuracy is truly flat?
  3. Is the judge's preference for elaborated answers measurable — and would a length-controlled judge eliminate the regression?
  4. Can distillation-based recovery preserve capabilities beyond the single benchmark it was scored on?
  5. Does the +0.168 Latin-script reward differential survive per-environment reward normalization?
  6. Does the emission-vs-conditional-accuracy decomposition generalize to IFEval-style gains?
