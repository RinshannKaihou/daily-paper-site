Why This Matters

Roots floating-point non-associativity — an overlooked reliability defect — out of the inference path, and makes distributed results checkable by any third party via hashes.

Directly targets inference reliability / SDC detection and exactness under low-precision optimization. 浮点非结合性被根除，分布式推理结果可被第三方独立校验。

⚠ Critical Edge

Determinism requires a fixed batching & execution schedule — a different batch composition changes the output bits — and the promised third-party checkability rests on a reproducibility artifact not yet released.

Methodology — distilled

Every tensor is integer codes cᵢ · s with a fixed-point scale (64-bit mantissa, 2−k shift). Weights are quantized once, offline, to a 12-trit balanced-ternary grid — 265,720 levels per sign (~19 bits) — with no calibration data and no QAT. Activations are quantized dynamically per tensor, carried between ops at the 228 interchange level.

The runtime path contains zero floating point: matmuls accumulate exact i32×i32→i64 products; inverse-RMS is an integer isqrt (max rel. error 5.8×10⁻¹⁰); GELU and softmax-exp are offline integer lookup tables; rounding is pinned half-away-from-zero.

Because integer addition is associative & commutative, reordered SIMD lanes, threads, and machines produce identical bits — confirmed by a 200,000-case fuzz including i64-overflow magnitudes. Every operator ships an exact forward_int plus a float forward_ref twin behind a verification gate; an op without a reference cannot ship. Distributed runs keep expert weights resident on workers — only activations cross the wire (KBs/token, over QUIC).

Strengths

  • Exemplary claim hygiene — three precisely separated claims plus an explicit non-claims list.
  • Every number anchored to a reproducing SHA-256; verification is structural (per-op gate), not a one-off experiment.
  • The ε misconfiguration is disclosed with numbers (0.455 → 508/512) and fixed by a fail-closed default rule.
  • Deterministic routing trace turns MoE routing into a reproducible measurement — 24/64-expert cache serves ~⅔ of routing.
  • Real scale: 0.5B–106B models across Raspberry Pis to a 96-core EPYC — not a toy kernel.

Limitations

  • No accuracy benchmarks at all — no perplexity, MMLU/GSM8K, or SAM mask AP; "decision fidelity" rests on one 512-token probe & one image.
  • Fidelity is tolerance-bounded, not bit-equal to float; near-tie argmax flips may compound — divergence unquantified.
  • Determinism is schedule-dependent (dynamic per-tensor activation quant); no incremental KV-cache decode yet.
  • Float fidelity at depth measured only to 16/46 layers; GPU bit-identity shown for one GEMM on Metal only.
  • Dishonest-server verifiability is a direction, not a protocol; artifact incomplete in this draft.

Questions for Further Exploration

  • Do the 3/512 near-tie argmax flips compound into divergent long-form generations — and what is the end-task accuracy gap?
  • Can detllm-style per-token/static scaling buy batch & schedule invariance without sacrificing i128-exactness and the per-op gate?
  • What would a sound audit protocol against a dishonest server look like — sampling rule, state binding, attacker model — and what detection probability does each unit of recomputation buy?
  • Does workload-dependent expert locality (28/64 vs 52/64 experts) persist at 128-expert scale and on production traces?
  • Can GPU bit-identity extend from one Metal GEMM to whole models on CUDA/ROCm, and where does the ~19-bit grid sit on the fidelity–size–speed frontier vs int8/int4?
