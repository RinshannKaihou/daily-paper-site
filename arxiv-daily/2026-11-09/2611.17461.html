Key Finding

High scores can hide a strong dependence on the original correct option.

Tracing Knowledge and Reasoning in LLMs ↗ Yang Wang, Xiao Wang, Yiqi Liu, Chenghao Xiao, et al. (7 authors · Manchester / Sheffield / SUFE) 2026-11-09 · arXiv 2611.17461 · code open-sourced
llm_interpretability knowledge_vs_reasoning linear_probing chain_of_thought benchmark_evaluation
Key Metric — NOTA-as-answer collapse
0.0 pts

Median accuracy drop when the ground-truth option is removed (MathQA + SciQ, 6 models) — significant in 24/24 exact McNemar tests. Injecting a mere extra NOTA distractor costs only 1.2 points.

Why This Matters

Moves "recall or reason?" from behaviour to internal states, yielding two reusable tools — layer-wise probes and NOTA perturbation — for anyone building eval harnesses.

Critical Edge

The headline static-profile result is fragile — and the paper's own controls prove it.

Forcing the same final token collapses the benchmark ordering (ρ = 0.92 → 0.37), and knowledge-vs-reasoning groups never significantly differ (p = .19/.21); the robust probing evidence rests on just 4 benchmarks × 3 models.

Methodology — Four Signals, Unmodified LLMs

  • Framing. Knowledge = recall with little transformation; reasoning = derivation via intermediate operations. 24 benchmarks placed on a continuum, taxonomy assigned from benchmark design alone.
  • ① Static layer profiles. Per-layer head-averaged attention entropy + FFN activation sparsity at the final input token (reference model Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct), with prompt-length regression and same-final-token controls.
  • ② Decoding-time trajectories. Length-normalized token-wise evolution on SciQ / MathQA / LogiQA; correct vs. incorrect generations compared at layers 3 / 14 / 22.
  • ③ Probing + geometry. Logistic probes (5-fold CV) on hidden states predict the correct option; adjusted cosine similarity separates correct-vs-distractor representations; template-swap control decouples task from prompt format.
  • ④ NOTA stress tests. Remove the ground-truth (NOTA-as-answer) or inject a wrong extra (NOTA-as-distractor), under Standard and CoT; item-level exact McNemar with BH correction.
  • Landscape. 16 open-weight models (Qwen, Llama, Gemma, OLMo, GLM, GPT-OSS; 2B–30B+, dense + MoE) × 24 benchmarks via EvalScope + SGLang; K/R scores = unweighted means over 10 / 20 settings, min–max normalized.

No fine-tuning, no special tokens, no forced fast/slow thinking modes — the diagnostics read the model as shipped. Stats: Wilcoxon + exact McNemar, BH-corrected, with Cliff's δ and rank-biserial effect sizes.

  • Trajectory signal: incorrect generations show higher attention-entropy variability (Cliff's δ = 0.42 lower / 0.45 upper layers, q < .01) — a candidate training-free uncertainty detector.
  • Scaling: knowledge scores grow consistently with parameters; reasoning gains are smaller and non-monotonic. Counterexample: Gemma-7B drops under CoT (SciQ 85.5 → 64.3).
  • CoT only partially closes the NOTA gap — e.g. Llama-3.1 keeps a large Base-vs-NOTA-as-answer deficit even with CoT.

Strengths

  • Breadth: 16 recent open-weight models × 24 benchmarks with a clean normalized two-axis summary.
  • Genuinely informative negative controls (prompt length, same-final-token, template swap) — rare self-scrutiny for interpretability signals.
  • Statistical rigor uncommon in eval papers: BH-corrected tests plus effect sizes behind every claim.
  • The NOTA asymmetry is a cheap, actionable shortcut detector any eval harness can adopt.
  • Honest continuum framing of knowledge vs. reasoning; mixed cases discussed, not hidden.

Limitations

  • English-only; probing and NOTA analyses need multiple-choice structure — no transfer to open-ended generation.
  • Robust internal-state findings cover only 3–4 benchmarks and 3 mid-sized models, not the advertised 16 × 24 landscape.
  • Purely correlational: no activation patching or ablation, so differences remain associations.
  • The K/R split is hand-curated and operational; individual benchmarks are impure mixtures.
  • Min–max normalization makes landscape axes sensitive to the best/worst model in the pool.

Questions for Further Exploration

  1. Do the probing and NOTA findings replicate on free-form, multilingual benchmarks with no option set — or is option dependence partly an artifact of MC formatting?
  2. Can causal interventions (activation patching, layer ablation) turn these associations into mechanisms — e.g., does the mid-layer probe direction on SciQ causally drive the answer?
  3. Does the NOTA-as-answer gap shrink toward zero for genuinely option-invariant reasoners (Qwen3-Think, GPT-OSS at high reasoning effort)?
  4. Could within-trajectory attention-entropy variability (δ ≈ 0.42–0.45) serve as a training-free, inference-time incorrectness detector?
  5. How does retrieval-augmented generation shift the landscape — with knowledge supplied externally, does the knowledge axis still scale more reliably?
