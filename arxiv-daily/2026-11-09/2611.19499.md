> **一句话总结：** 本文在 LLaMA-3.1-8B、Qwen3.5-9B、Gemma-3-12B 三个指令模型上，沿一条从协方差校正判别方向（Fisher/探针端）连续过渡到均值差 Δμ（CAA 式转向端）的单参数方向族 wλ=(ΣW+λI)⁻¹Δμ，在固定中性文本副作用（WikiText-2 上的平均 KL 散度）下同时测量探测（AUROC）与加性转向效果：探测稳定在 λ≈τ 处最优（AUROC 0.96–0.98，而 Δμ 端仅 0.60–0.89），转向则稳定偏向更靠近 Δμ 的方向（10τ–316τ），该分离在 60 个层×模型×概念组合中的 59 个成立；跨模型时经探针律转移的探针保留目标模型 81–91% 的超随机探测性能，而经转向律转移的 Δμ 在 12 对中的 11 对保留更少（44–93%）；在 PopQA 上向"unknown"方向转向使不确定错误（C2）的弃答率提升 18–44 个百分点，而自信错误（C3）仅升 2–5 个百分点。

> **TL;DR:** The direction that best *reads* a concept is not the direction that best *changes* it. Building a one-parameter family from the Fisher-style discriminant to the mean difference Δμ (CAA's steering vector, up to scale) and comparing probing (held-out AUROC on TruthfulQA / Yelp) vs additive steering at a fixed KL side-effect budget on neutral WikiText-2, the authors find probing peaks at λ=τ (AUROC 0.96–0.98) while steering plateaus at 10τ–316τ near Δμ — true in 6/6 model–concept pairs at the main layer, 59/60 in a 10-depth layer sweep, 6/6 with matched texts, and 4/4 reachable rotation-operator cases. On TruthfulQA the best probe at λ=τ steers only 21–64% as strongly as the Δμ baseline; on Yelp sentiment an intermediate direction beats Δμ by 27–56% (H1b) but this gain vanishes in LLaMA and Gemma once probe and steering texts are matched. Across models (ridge maps on 12,800 neutral texts; probes carried by the transpose of the reverse map, Δμ carried forward), transferred probes keep 81–91% of the target's above-chance AUROC in all 12 ordered pairs while transferred Δμ keeps only 44–93% of the steering effect and loses in 11/12 pairs. Applied to 4,000 PopQA questions, steering toward "unknown" raises abstention by +44/+30/+18 pp (LLaMA/Qwen/Gemma) on uncertain errors vs +5/+4/+2 pp on confident errors, at a +7/+3/+1 pp cost on correct answers.

## 核心贡献

- **连续方向族统一了"探针 vs 转向"之争**：不同于以往只对比两个固定端点（训练探针 vs 均值差），本文构造正则化判别族 wλ=(ΣW+λI)⁻¹Δμ/‖·‖（λ→0 为 Fisher 判别端，λ→∞ 为 Δμ 端，即 CAA/拒绝方向），在固定中性副作用预算 κ0（200 条 WikiText-2 句子上的平均 KL 散度）下沿整条族测量两个角色，使任何方向都无法靠"推得更狠"取胜。
- **核心实证结论（H1a）：探测与转向的最优方向系统性分离**——探测在 λ≈τ 处达 AUROC 0.96–0.98，转向的 1-SE 平台落在 10τ–316τ、更靠近 Δμ；该结论在全部 6 个模型×概念组合、匹配文本控制、59/60 个层扫组合、以及 4 个旋转转向算子案例中均成立。理论上用 Haufe 激活模式论证 Σh·w_LDA ∝ Δμ 给出了解释。
- **中间方向的优势不稳定（H1b）**：真实性问题（TruthfulQA）上没有任何方向明显胜过 Δμ（Qwen 最大增益 9.6%，未达预设的 10% 门槛，三模型全败）；情感问题（Yelp）上 λ* 比 Δμ 强 27–56%（三模型全过），但匹配文本后 LLaMA 与 Gemma 的增益消失、Qwen 缩小。
- **跨模型转移的"角色不对称"（H1c）**：探针经探针律（反向映射的转置）转移后在全部 12 个有序模型对中保留 81–91% 的目标模型超随机 AUROC，而 Δμ 经转向律（正向映射）转移后仅保留 44–93% 的转向效果、在 12 对中的 11 对劣于探针——模型之间"怎么读一个概念"的一致性高于"怎么改一个概念"。
- **PopQA 弃答应用（RQ2）**：沿 Δμ 向"unknown"转向，把不确定错误（C2）变成弃答远比自信错误（C3）容易（+44/+30/+18 pp 对 +5/+4/+2 pp），随机方向同副作用下仅 +7/+9/+1 pp；探针门控只在 Qwen 通过预设检验，在 LLaMA/Qwen 能以约 1/3 的代价（C0 +2 对 +7；+0.8 对 +3）保留 65%/84% 的收益。

## Key Contributions

- A continuous, closed-form family of directions interpolating between the covariance-adjusted discriminant (probe end, λ→0) and the mean difference Δμ (steering end, λ→∞), which subsumes CAA's steering vector and the refusal direction, letting probing and steering be compared at *every* point along the path rather than at two fixed endpoints.
- A fixed-side-effect comparison protocol: every direction's steering effect is read at the same KL budget K=κ0 measured on neutral WikiText-2 sentences, so no direction wins merely by perturbing the model more — a control missing from earlier probe-vs-mean-difference comparisons (Li et al. 2023; Marks & Tegmark 2024; Wu et al. 2025).
- The central empirical result: probing peaks near λ=τ while steering plateaus at 10τ–316τ toward Δμ, robust across 6/6 model–concept combinations at the main layer, 59/60 layer–model–concept combinations in a 10-depth sweep, 6/6 with matched probe/steering texts, and 4/4 rotation-operator cases; on TruthfulQA the best probe steers only 21–64% as strongly as Δμ.
- Dual transfer laws between models (steering vectors carried forward by a ridge map; probes carried by the transpose of the reverse map, both fitted on 12,800 label-free neutral texts), with the finding that probes keep 81–91% of above-chance AUROC in all 12 ordered pairs while the transferred Δμ keeps less of its steering effect in 11/12 pairs.
- An error-taxonomy application on 4,000 PopQA questions (labels from each model's own greedy + 5 sampled answers, adapted from Orgad et al. 2025): steering toward "unknown" converts uncertain errors (C2) into abstentions at +18–44 pp versus +2–5 pp on confident errors (C3), rarely fixes knowledge-available errors, and probe-gated steering trades cost against gain differently in each model.

## Methodology

The paper studies three instruction-tuned models from different families — LLaMA-3.1-8B-Instruct, Qwen3.5-9B (post-trained checkpoint), Gemma-3-12B-it — with raw prompts and no chat template, reading the residual-stream hidden state h at the last token after a layer at ~0.44 of depth (14/32, 14/32, 21/48 respectively; the depth was chosen in advance and validated by a 10-depth sweep).

**Family of directions.** For each binary concept (truthfulness: true vs false TruthfulQA MC2 answers; sentiment: 1,000 positive vs 1,000 negative Yelp reviews; "unknown": PopQA questions with vs without a correct answer among six decodings), the class means μ1, μ0 and pooled within-class covariance ΣW are estimated once on a 50% estimation split, and the family wλ = (ΣW+λI)⁻¹Δμ / ‖(ΣW+λI)⁻¹Δμ‖ is computed in closed form for 13 values of λ/τ spaced by half-decades from 10⁻³ to 10³, plus Δμ as the endpoint (τ = tr(ΣW)/d makes λ comparable across models, since τ ≈ 0.01 in LLaMA but 4,000–7,000 in Gemma). The λ→∞ limit recovers Δμ (CAA up to scale); the λ→0 limit is the Fisher discriminant when ΣW is nonsingular, and the component of Δμ outside the span of within-class deviations otherwise.

**Probing and steering at a fixed side effect.** A direction is judged as a probe by the AUROC of wᵀh on held-out texts, and as a steering vector by the additive intervention T(h)=h+a‖h‖w applied from the last steering-prompt token onward (for truthfulness, including the answer tokens whose likelihood is scored), with the steering effect S being the change in the true-vs-best-false answer margin (TruthfulQA MC1) or in the log-odds of positive vs negative words (Yelp). The strength a is calibrated per direction so that the side effect K — the average next-token KL divergence against the unsteered model on 200 neutral WikiText-2 sentences — equals a fixed budget κ0 (anchored to Δμ at a=0.1, or a=0.01 for Gemma, whose residual norms are far larger). Steering effects are interpolated between bracketing strengths from a grid a ∈ {0.02,…,0.8}; intervals come from paired bootstrap over held-out items (1,000 resamples).

**Cross-model transfer.** For each of the six ordered model pairs, affine maps hB ≈ M_{A→B}hA + b are fitted by ridge regression on 12,800 neutral texts (6,400 WikiText-2 sentences, each as first half and in full; ridge strength chosen on 3,200 held-out texts), never seeing concept labels. A steering displacement is transferred forward (δB = M_{A→B}δ); a probe, being a linear functional, is transferred by the transpose of the reverse map (w_{A→B} = M_{B→A}ᵀw). Sharing ratios ρprobe and ρsteer compare each carried direction against the target's own, with pre-registered thresholds (ρ≥0.8 strong, ρ<0.5 weak); an orthogonal Procrustes map, for which the two laws coincide, serves as a control.

**RQ2 taxonomy.** Each of 4,000 PopQA questions gets one greedy and five sampled answers ("I don't know" allowed), each judged correct/wrong/abstention against gold aliases: C0 (greedy correct), C1 (greedy wrong but some sample correct — knowledge-available errors), C2/C3 (no correct sample; wrong answer repeated in ≤2 / ≥4 of five samples — uncertain vs confident errors), plus Cm and greedy abstentions AK/AU. A cross-fitted probe separates questions with vs without a correct answer and is read at the last prompt token before generation; steering toward "unknown" is compared across Δμ, the probe end, probe-gated Δμ, a random direction, and other models' Δμ carried over by the steering law, all at κ0, with bootstrap over questions (2,000 resamples).

## Results & Highlights

- **Probing vs steering separation (H1a, main result):** In all six model–concept combinations, probing peaks near λ=τ with held-out AUROC 0.96–0.98 on TruthfulQA/Yelp, dropping to 0.60–0.89 at the Δμ end, while steering plateaus lie at 10τ–316τ toward Δμ; the separation holds in 59 of 60 layer–model–concept combinations in a 10-depth layer sweep, in all six combinations with matched probe and steering texts, and in the four cases where a rotation-based operator reaches the same side effect.
- **Probe directions are weak steering vectors on TruthfulQA:** at λ=τ the best probe changes the MC1 answer margin only 21–64% as much as Δμ at the same side-effect budget, and no direction on the path steers clearly better than Δμ — the largest gain, 9.6% in Qwen, falls short of the pre-registered 10% threshold, so H1b fails in all three models for truthfulness; for Yelp sentiment, a λ* chosen on one held-out split gives 27–56% more steering effect than Δμ on the other split (H1b passes 3/3), but this gain disappears in LLaMA and Gemma and shrinks in Qwen under the matched-texts control.
- **Asymmetric cross-model transfer (H1c):** a probe carried by the probe law retains 81–91% of the target model's above-chance probing AUROC in all twelve ordered pairs, while the transferred Δμ reaches only 44–93% of the target's own steering effect and retains less than the probe in 11 of 12 pairs; on the separation scale d′ (which corrects AUROC ceiling compression), carried probes keep 65–79% and still beat ρsteer in 9/12 pairs, and 6/6 for truthfulness.
- **PopQA abstention application (H2b):** models abstain on 57–78% of the 4,000 questions; steering toward "unknown" along Δμ at κ0 raises abstention on uncertain errors (C2) by +44 pp (LLaMA), +30 pp (Qwen), +18 pp (Gemma) versus +5/+4/+2 pp on confident errors (C3), at a cost of +7/+3/+1 pp on correct answers (C0) — whereas a random direction at the same KL budget raises C2 abstention by only +7/+9/+1 pp.
- **Probe gating (H2c) is model-dependent:** gating by the probe passes the pre-registered test only in Qwen; in LLaMA it keeps 65% of the C2 gain at +2 pp instead of +7 pp C0 cost, in Qwen 84% at +0.8 vs +3 pp, while in Gemma it keeps 95% of the gain but saves almost nothing (+0.6 vs +0.8 pp, resting on 4 vs 5 lost correct answers out of 644); for "unknown" across models, carried probes keep 89–96% of above-chance AUROC versus 30–79% for carried Δμ's abstention gain, with only moderate cross-model agreement on unanswerable questions (Cohen's κ 0.56–0.65).

## Authors & Credibility

Afshin Sadeghi and Daniel Becker are with the AI Assurance and Assessments (AAA) department of Fraunhofer IAIS, Sankt-Augustin, Germany — an institute with a strong track record in AI safety evaluation and interpretability. The paper is a preprint (arXiv:2611.19499v1, 14 Nov 2026, cs.CL); per the author-contributions statement, Sadeghi conceived the study, developed the framework and hypotheses, ran all experiments and wrote the paper, while Becker gave feedback on the direction-family concept — effectively a single-driver project. Credibility signals are strong: hypotheses, thresholds, and decision rules were fixed in advance (Appendix H); failed hypotheses (H1b for truthfulness in all three models, H2b(ii) in LLaMA, H2c in LLaMA and Gemma) are reported plainly; matched-text and wrong-law/Procrustes controls were run against the main confounds; and a dedicated limitations appendix (A) enumerates scope, non-independence of the 12 transfer pairs, and measurement caveats. Code and an interactive paper page are linked at afshn.com/research/probing-vs-steering.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty:** The probe-vs-mean-difference comparison itself is not new (Li et al. 2023; Marks & Tegmark 2024; Wu et al. 2025 compared two fixed endpoints; Cheng & Alonso 2026 noted linear decodability ≠ linear controllability). The genuine contributions are the *continuous* family with a fixed side-effect budget — which removes the "stronger push" confound — and the *dual transfer-law* comparison across three model families, which turns representation alignment into a functional read-vs-change question. This is a solid incremental-methodology paper, not a conceptual breakthrough.
- **Rigor:** Above average for the genre: pre-registered hypotheses and thresholds, paired bootstrap intervals, matched-text controls, a rotation-operator check, wrong-law and Procrustes controls, cross-fitting in RQ2 so no question is steered by a direction that saw it, and honest reporting of failures. However, the paper does not correct for multiple comparisons (except one ablation), and the ρprobe vs ρsteer comparison inherently compares incommensurable quantities (AUROC-above-chance vs steering-effect ratio), which the authors themselves flag as approximate.
- **Specific weaknesses:** (1) TruthfulQA is used for both estimating and evaluating the truthfulness directions, so as the authors admit, "a gain from the labels cannot be separated from one from matching the test distribution," and no cross-dataset generalization test was run despite known poor transfer of truthfulness probes (Liu et al. 2024). (2) The headline sentiment result (27–56% gain over Δμ) largely evaporates under the matched-texts control — disappearing in LLaMA and Gemma, shrinking in Qwen — yet survives in the abstract's framing. (3) The RQ2 knowledge-available class C1 contains only 71/33/35 questions because a question whose correct answer has sample probability p is missed by all five samples with probability (1−p)⁵ (0.44 at p=0.15), so H2b(ii) verdicts rest on point estimates with very wide intervals — LLaMA's "failure" is 11% vs a pre-set 10% limit on ~71 questions. (4) The transfer maps fit concept texts poorly (held-out R² 0.13–0.47 vs 0.48–0.70 on neutral text), so part of the steering-transfer loss may be an artifact of the map rather than genuine cross-model disagreement; no pair could be cleanly classified as "neither shared" for this reason. (5) The 12 ordered pairs are not independent — every pair shares a source or target with four others, so Gemma's own weak Δμ probing contaminates many points, and "11 of 12 pairs" is really a statement about three models (the pre-registered categories leave 9/12 pairs inconclusive).
- **Reproducibility:** Good in principle — closed-form directions, public datasets (TruthfulQA, Yelp, PopQA, WikiText-2), named checkpoints and layers, linked code — but the exact κ0 anchoring (Δμ at a=0.1/0.01), the λ/τ grid, and the taxonomy thresholds have many researcher degrees of freedom, and raw prompts without chat templates mean results may shift under production-style prompting.
- **Significance:** The practical takeaway — don't reuse a probe direction for steering without checking, and steering vectors transfer across models less reliably than probes — is directly actionable for representation-engineering work (RepE, CAA-style methods) and for cross-model safety tooling. But the evidence covers only 3 concepts, 3 mid-size models, one layer per model for the main analyses, single directions, and margin/log-odds measures rather than task accuracy.
- **Red flags:** None serious — no benchmark cherry-picking detected, failures are disclosed prominently. The main caveat is that the abstract's clean numbers (e.g., "27–56% stronger steering") carry fragilities (matched-text collapse, layer/operator dependence) that are only visible in the appendices.

## Strengths

- The fixed-side-effect (KL budget) comparison design is the cleanest answer yet to the old confound that a direction can look like a better steering vector simply because it perturbs the model more.
- The single closed-form family replaces binary endpoint comparisons with a full curve, and the activation-pattern identity Σh·w_LDA ∝ Δμ (Haufe et al. 2014) gives a principled account of *why* the read-optimum and change-optimum differ.
- The dual transfer-law framework (forward map for displacements, transpose of the reverse map for probes) is a genuinely useful methodological template for anyone porting interpretability artifacts between models.
- Exceptional transparency: pre-registered hypotheses with explicit pass/fail rules, reported failures (H1b on truthfulness everywhere, gating outside Qwen), a matched-text control that undermines one of the paper's own headline numbers, and a candid limitations appendix.
- The PopQA application connects the geometry question to a concrete safety behavior — selective abstention — and quantifies the trade-off (C2 abstention gain vs C0 cost) per model rather than averaging it away.

## Limitations

- Scope: three concepts (truthfulness, sentiment, "unknown"), three instruction-tuned mid-size models, and one layer per model for the main analyses; the layer sweep covers RQ1 only, so the H2b(iii) and H2c verdicts are untested at other depths, and base or larger models may behave differently.
- Only single directions from one family under two operators (addition, gated rotation) are studied — no subspaces, nonlinear probes, SAE features, or learned steering operators; conclusions may not carry over.
- Steering is measured by answer-margin or word-log-odds changes, not final task accuracy, and the side-effect budget is computed on neutral text only, so equal K does not guarantee equal disruption of task behavior.
- Cross-model conclusions rest on non-independent pairs (3 models → 6 ordered pairs sharing sources/targets) and on maps that fit concept texts much worse than neutral text, so the read-vs-change asymmetry is a "consistent tendency," with 9 of 12 pairs formally inconclusive under the pre-registered criteria.
- RQ2 labels depend on the model's own six decodings, biasing the "no correct answer" class toward questions the model could sometimes answer; C1 is tiny (33–71 questions) and its verdicts have wide intervals; prompts use no chat templates and Qwen's thinking tokens are handled by a token budget.
- TruthfulQA serves as both training and evaluation data for the truthfulness concept, so distribution-matching gains cannot be excluded.

## Relevance

对关注 LLM 可解释性与表示工程的研究兴趣而言，本文直接回答了一个高频实践问题——"探针方向能否直接拿来当转向向量用"（RepE/CAA 式流程的常见假设），并给出跨模型转移探针与转向向子的具体衰减量化；其"不确定错误更易被诱导弃答"的 PopQA 结果也与幻觉检测/选择性弃答方向高度相关。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the probe-vs-steering separation persist for other concepts (refusal, toxicity, fairness attributes), for base models, and at frontier scale, or is the τ-vs-10τ–316τ split specific to truthfulness/sentiment-like linearly encoded concepts?
- Would steering-transfer improve if the cross-model maps were fitted on concept-relevant text rather than neutral WikiText-2 (the concept-text R² of 0.13–0.47 suggests much of the Δμ transfer loss may be recoverable)?
- Can the family framework be extended to multi-direction subspaces or SAE features, where the "best read ≠ best change" separation might shrink or invert?
- On RQ2, would richer sampling (e.g., 20 samples instead of 5) or semantic-entropy labeling enlarge the knowledge-available class enough to make the H2b(ii)/H2c verdicts statistically stable rather than point-estimate calls?
- Since a linear prediction assuming only the Δμ-component of the push matters reproduces the steering-plateau position, could one derive an a priori predictor of steering effectiveness for arbitrary probe directions, avoiding the empirical sweep entirely?
