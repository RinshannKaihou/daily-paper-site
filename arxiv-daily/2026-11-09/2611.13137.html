Worker momentum + robust aggregation + Muon orthogonalization = the first provably Byzantine-robust Muon optimizer.

Byzantine-Robust Muon: Resilient Momentum Orthogonalization for Distributed Learning
Grigory Malinovsky · Yury Demidovich · Peter Richtárik  —  2026-11-09
Byzantine-robust optimization Muon optimizer Federated learning Non-Euclidean trust region Robust aggregation
Key Metric
74.0%

ResNet-18 accuracy under an adaptive attack with 20% Byzantine workers — vs 41.3% for sign updates, the next-best update geometry.

Why This Matters

Directly informs robustness of large-scale distributed LLM training — provable tolerance when faulty or malicious workers send corrupted updates. 直接关系大规模分布式/联邦 LLM 训练的鲁棒性与故障恢复：掉卡节点发送坏梯度时，可证明的容错方案。

Critical Edge

The headline attack gaps partly reflect scale-invariant update geometry absorbing negative-multiple attacks — not the robust aggregator itself: plain bucketed mean matches RFA in all twelve mild-attack cells.

MMethodology — How It Works

1 · WorkerEach honest worker keeps a local momentum buffer mi ← (1−α)mi + αgi and sends it — temporal information memoryless schemes lack.
2 · ServerRobust aggregation replaces averaging: a (δ, c)-robust aggregator (bucketed RFA / smoothed geometric median) approximates the honest mean with dual-norm error ≤ cδ·r.
3 · UpdateSolve a linearized trust-region step; in operator-norm geometry the exact solution is the orthogonal factor of the aggregated momentum — Muon, via 5 Newton–Schulz iterations.

The unified view places all tested geometries in one framework: minimizing ⟨mk, x⟩ over a norm ball recovers Muon (operator norm), normalized SGD, and sign updates by changing only the norm. Theory assumes no i.i.d. data: heterogeneity is bounded in the dual norm, and the non-convex theorem converges at O(1/K) to a stationarity neighborhood with an unavoidable Byzantine floor 4cδζ/(1−4cδB) — matching known lower bounds. A star-convex variant with weight decay contracts linearly at (1−β/2)K with no upper bound on δB, and an inexact-Newton–Schulz extension deflates progress by only (1−δ̄).

Experiments: 25 simulated workers, Dirichlet-0.1 non-IID CIFAR-10, 20% Byzantine (5/25), α = 0.1, 500 rounds, five attacks, five seeds — plus ViT-Tiny, transfer datasets, and a 38M-parameter byte-level TinyStories LM with a story-disjoint test split.

RResults — Beyond the Headline

  • ALIE attack, ResNet-18: Muon-NS keeps 86.4% ± 0.8 — a 43.1-point margin over sign's 43.3%.
  • RFA vs mean under an RFA-query attack: from near-identical benign accuracy (88.7% vs 88.1%), bucketed mean collapses to 10.0% while RFA retains 36.5% ± 6.6 — a 27.0-point saving (95% CI 20.3–33.8).
  • Worker momentum is non-monotone: 51.0% at α=0.03 → 74.0% at 0.1 → 82.7% at 0.3 under the adaptive attack.
  • Heterogeneity-dependent floor: at 30% corruption under IPM, 69.1% at Dirichlet 0.1 but 86.9–90.1% at concentrations 1–10.

+Strengths

  • Modular design: robust aggregation and update geometry are separable — one analysis covers Muon, normalized SGD, and sign, and the experiments exploit this to isolate the geometry effect.
  • Self-critical evaluation: controls are designed to break the paper's own headline claims (fixed-honest, RFA-query, shared-checkpoint), and the adverse results are reported.
  • Theory matches practice: heterogeneous non-IID data, inexact Newton–Schulz, weight decay, and a certified aggregation residual are all covered.
  • Breadth: five attacks, three model families (CNN, ViT, byte-level LM), five seeds, transfer datasets, plus a controlled matrix quadratic probing the extrapolation mechanism.

−Limitations

  • No worst-case adversary: all attacks are bounded, specified constructions; the adaptive attack still costs ~14 points (88.0% → 74.0%), and even RFA loses 51.7 points under the RFA-query attack.
  • Theory–experiment gap: the theorem constants (cδ, B, ζ, δ̄) are never measured on neural workloads — mechanism checks, not verification.
  • Conditions hard to check: 4cδB < 1 and the first-moment aggregation premise limit tightness; the implemented smoothed Euclidean RFA is not the exact dual-norm geometric median.
  • Simulation only: single GPU per trial, ≤25 workers, no asynchrony; macro-accuracy hides worst-class collapse (25.1% under the adaptive attack).

?Questions for Further Exploration

  1. Do the theorem constants (cδ, B, ζ, κ) behave as predicted on real LLM pretraining — can the Byzantine floor 4cδζ/(1−4cδB) be measured, and does it forecast the drop at 30% corruption?
  2. Does the first-moment (δ, c)-aggregator premise hold for any practical aggregator on matrix-valued momenta in the nuclear norm, given worst case cδ ≥ 1/2?
  3. Does robust aggregation plus orthogonalization remain stable under communication compression and asynchrony, standard in cross-silo LLM training?
  4. Can a stronger adaptive adversary — gradient-aware, query-unlimited, or attacking individual Newton–Schulz blocks — close the 14-point gap, and would per-block Byzantine filtering help?
  5. Does the attack-resilience of spectral geometry transfer to optimizer-stateful baselines like AdamW with matched worker momentum?
