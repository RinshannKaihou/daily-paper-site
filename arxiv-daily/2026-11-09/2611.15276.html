无需任何外部反馈，让 Agent 在单个任务内同时演化“执行”与“验证”。

Without any external feedback, an agent improves within a single task by co-evolving execution and verification.

Verify by Doing: Co-Evolving Agent Execution and Evaluation without External Feedback
Yitong Lin · Jianda Chen · Yihao Hu · Pan Wang, et al. (8 authors) — Ant International & Zhejiang University · arXiv 2611.15276 · 2026-11-09
self-evolving-agents test-time-adaptation agentic-evaluation self-verification LLM-agents
Key Metric
0.0 pp
31.2% → 35.8% pass rate on AutomationBench — the same six BoN outputs, recovered purely by the evolved Evaluator selecting better. Nothing new was generated.
Why This Matters

Making the verifier itself evolvable attacks unreliable feedback — the core pain of self-improvement loops. The selection-vs-adoption split informs any self-evaluating agent work.

Critical Edge

Verification needs inspectable, resettable state — irreversible actions, non-resettable environments, or inaccessible state escape the method: exactly the production domains it targets.

Methodology · How VbD Works

Model weights stay frozen; only two harnesses change on a single task — an Actor (execution: instructions, skills, check plans) and an Evaluator (verification procedure). Three rounds, each with four steps:

  • Actor trials — incumbent and proposed Actor harnesses each execute the task once, preserving records, state, and deliverables.
  • Agentic evaluation — both Evaluators inspect a common candidate pool with tools (cell reads, probes, source retrieval), emitting structured, scoped findings with uncertainty.
  • Comparative selection — Evaluator adoption needs two-order review vs. the retained version (position-bias control); Actor adoption additionally requires the proposal's own fresh output to win strictly — selection ≠ adoption.
  • Harness co-evolution — a fixed editor turns each finding into joint updates: the same fact becomes an Actor instruction and an Evaluator check.
Setup: DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731 (284B/13B) plays all roles; Qwen3.8-Flash-Next as second backbone; offline grading with GPT-5.6-sol (xhigh). Everything resets between tasks.

  • Backbone transfer: gains hold on Qwen3.8-Flash-Next — 48.4% / 57.4% / 84.7% across the three sets.
  • Safer self-updates: rejects 89.3% of regressions (TNR) vs. RQGM's 71.2% (+18.1 pp) at unchanged acceptance precision (77.6% vs 77.0%).
  • Ablations: removing active verification −2.7 pp; removing Evaluator evolution −1.6 pp — both components matter on all three task families.
  • Cost-aware: Round-2 mean (34.3%) already beats RQGM's final score at lower cumulative token cost.

Strengths

  • Cleanest evidence: the fixed-candidate-pool experiment isolates pure selection quality (31.2% → 35.8%) from generation gains.
  • Design hygiene: two-order review against position bias; separate retention rules decoupling output selection from Actor adoption.
  • Reported honestly: 3-run mean ± SD everywhere, explicit external-feedback gap, and failure case studies (Finance024 timestamp misread).
  • Concrete mechanism: finding → joint Actor/Evaluator edits is traceable in case studies, not just end scores.

Limitations

  • Single backbone for every role — Actor, Evaluator, editor, reviewer share one model, so correlated misreadings of evidence can survive review.
  • Short horizon only: three rounds, six executions per task; cross-task transfer of evolved harnesses is untested.
  • Heavy inference cost: tens of millions of tokens per task on AutomationBench, plus latency from inspection and re-execution.
  • Not uniformly best: loses on AutomationBench-HR (9.7% vs RQGM's 10.7%); no significance testing in main tables (margins 2.3–4.1 pp vs SDs 1.4–3.5 pp).

Questions for Further Exploration

  1. Do evolved Evaluator harnesses transfer across tasks, or do learned checks overfit one task's artifacts (the paper resets harnesses between tasks)?
  2. How much gain survives when the reviewer/editor use a different backbone than Actor/Evaluator, breaking the shared-error correlation?
  3. Can comparative review be supplemented with cheap statistical controls (e.g., paraphrase consistency) to cut the tens-of-millions-of-tokens cost?
  4. Of the remaining 5.6 pp external-feedback gap, how much is verification quality vs. search guidance — and do occasional ground-truth checks (every k-th task) close it?
  5. How does VbD behave with irreversible actions or non-resettable state — could counterfactual replay or simulator surrogates substitute for fresh trials?
