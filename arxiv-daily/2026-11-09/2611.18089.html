02 Why this matters

Turns “representations grow more abstract with depth” into a computable geometric observable — a cheap, probe-free layer-profiling tool for any open LLM.

03 Critical edge

In real text the claim rides on SAE proxies: ablating all active SAE latents removes at most ~68% of the autocorrelation — the title's “explains” is fully earned only in the synthetic setting.

04 Methodology

1

A task-free observable

Standardize activations and take one dot-product expectation: c_ℓ(τ) = E[φ⁽ℓ⁎T−τ · φ⁽ℓ⁎T]. No probes, labels, or tasks; for one-hot tokens it reduces exactly to the centered token-coincidence probability.

2

Ground truth where latents are known

A 5-block GPT-2-style transformer trained on the depth-5 Random Hierarchy Model (32-token sentences, 63 known latents each) to within 0.033 nats/sentence of Bayes-optimal; OLS regression on all 63 latents decomposes autocorrelation into persistence vs cross-node terms.

3

Transfer to real language

Five open LLMs with public SAEs (Gemma 2 2B/9B, Pythia-160M, Llama-3-8B, Qwen3-8B): layer-wise integrated autocorrelation χℓ compared to median SAE-feature persistence on WikiText and The Pile.

4

Controls & intervention

Within-sequence token shuffling preserves frequency but destroys order; ablating the most persistent features (projection removal along decoder directions) is tested against an active-but-non-persistent negative control.

06 Strengths

  • Minimal observable — one dot-product expectation, computable on any open model; unifies token-level statistics with representation analysis.
  • Synthetic-to-real pipeline — mechanism established exactly where latents are known, then tested via SAE proxies on five public LLMs and two corpora.
  • Genuine causal design — ablation intervention with a proper negative control (Δ ≈ 0) plus a frequency-confound shuffle control.
  • Quantitative theory–data contact — exact entropy plateaus, predicted lag positions, per-model tables, χ → χ̃ correction; honest scoping of what Eq. 1 cannot see.

07 Limitations

  • SAE-proxy dependence — SAEs span only part of activation space; the persistence–autocorrelation link is quantitatively incomplete in real text (≤ ~68% removable).
  • Directional blindness — codes rotating across positions are invisible; only aligned-direction persistence is measured, full cross-covariance deferred.
  • Clean only at layers 1–2 — layer-per-level resolution blurs at layers 3–4; single grammar, single seed, no error bars on the 55–95% attribution.
  • Unexplained spread & no behavior link — r = 0.93 → 0.67 across models is not digested into; nothing yet ties longer tails to downstream capabilities.

08 Questions for further exploration

Does χ̃ℓ predict behavior — long-context retrieval, in-context learning depth — across models trained on different data mixes?
How much of the real-text gap is orientation-rotating codes? Has the full cross-covariance analysis been run at scale?
Why is Llama-3-8B so much weaker (r = 0.67 vs 0.93)? SAE quality (top-k vs JumpReLU), data, or architecture?
Can stagewise autocorrelation emergence serve as a label-free training diagnostic for when depth-h structure is learned?
Does the fluctuation–response conjecture hold: do longer-ranged layers propagate small perturbations over longer lags?
Do the predictions survive irregular, variable-arity, or stochastic hierarchies closer to real language?
