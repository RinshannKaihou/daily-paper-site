> **一句话总结：** 本文指出在 FFN down-projection 输入处（必须在线应用的）随机 Hadamard 旋转会把携带"巨量离群值"（massive outlier）的 token 激活打散成正负双峰碎片分布、撑大量化步长；提出 SignRot——用一次校准前向找到主导离群通道，构造符号调整向量 n = sign(H_{cmax,:}) 统一该 Hadamard 行的符号，把双峰坍缩成单峰，再配合非对称激活量化（零点吸收均值偏移）——作为任何 Hadamard 类量化方法（QuaRot/SpinQuant/DartQuant）的即插即用改进，在线只需逐元素翻符号（端到端延迟仅 +0.43%）；在 LLaMA3-70B W4A4+KV4 下把 QuaRot 的 WikiText2 困惑度从 6.16 降到 5.88（同非对称量化设定）、平均零样本准确率从 72.28 升到 72.73，超过需要训练旋转的 SpinQuant（6.38 / 71.15）。

> **TL;DR:** Rotation-based PTQ methods (QuaRot, SpinQuant, DartQuant) must apply a randomized Hadamard transform online at the FFN down-projection input, and the authors show analytically (via a Lindeberg/CLT argument) that when one channel carries more energy than all other channels combined, the Hadamard row's alternating signs split the rotated activation into a bimodal, fragmented distribution that inflates the quantization step size. SignRot fixes this by flipping column signs so the dominant outlier contributes a constant shift, then absorbing that shift with per-token asymmetric activation quantization (Asym-Symm matmul with an offline-precomputed zero-point correction costing only O(T·Cout)). It is training-free, needs one calibration forward pass, and adds just element-wise sign flips at inference (+0.43% end-to-end latency on LLaMA2-7B at 1.65x speedup over FP16). On LLaMA3-70B with GPTQ W4A4, QuaRot+SignRot cuts WikiText2 perplexity from 6.16 to 5.88 and lifts average zero-shot accuracy from 72.28 to 72.73, beating learned-rotation SpinQuant (6.38 PPL / 71.15 acc); on top of SpinQuant it improves 6.38 -> 5.45 PPL and 71.15 -> 73.52 accuracy. Gains are largest on LLaMA3 models and shrink on LLaMA2 (e.g., 6.10 -> 6.09 Wiki2 PPL on LLaMA2-7B GPTQ), and it can slightly hurt (DartQuant on LLaMA3-70B: 76.39 -> 76.02 acc).

## 核心贡献

- **双峰碎片化的理论刻画**：证明随机 Hadamard 变换（RHT）的高斯近似依赖 Lindeberg 条件；当某通道能量超过其余所有通道之和（M_t² > Σ_{i≠cmax} x_{t,i}²）时，输出退化为均值相距 2|M_t|/√Cin 的双高斯混合——正负两簇之间的空档撑大逐 token 量化范围与步长（MSE 上界 Δt²/4 随之增大）。在 LLaMA2-7B 第 2 层（Cin=11,008），该阈值约 58，而实测离群值 M_t=1510，约为阈值的 26 倍，与图中明显分离的双簇一致。
- **SignRot 方法**：用一次校准前向找到主导离群通道 cmax（各 token 的 massive outlier 通道固定不变），令符号向量 n_j = sign(h_{cmax,j})，使 H·diag(n) 的 cmax 行全为 +1/√Cin——离群项变成对所有输出通道的常数偏移，双峰坍缩为单峰。diag(n) 是对合矩阵，其逆离线融合进 W_d，在线只加逐元素符号翻转，无需额外矩阵乘法，兼容任何 Hadamard 类方法（QuaRot/SpinQuant/DartQuant），适用于稠密和 MoE 架构。
- **与非对称激活量化配对**：符号对齐使旋转后激活均值偏离零点，因此非对称量化（零点吸收偏移）是 SignRot 的组成部分而非可选优化；提出的 Asym-Symm 整数矩阵乘把零点修正项 z_X(1ᵀW_q) 离线预计算为权重列和，推理时仅 O(T·Cout) 开销。
- **误差传播的实证定位**：逐层量化实验（KL 散度）显示首个 massive outlier 层是全网最贵的层之一（KL 为无离群层中位数的 2.3–3.6 倍），因为该 token 充当 attention sink，其量化误差经注意力扩散到所有其他 token（其他 token 的 KL 也随之降 31–64%）；SignRot 把该层单层 KL 降低 36–61%。
- **低成本与强兼容**：7B 模型 RTN 量化约 3 分钟、GPTQ 约 15 分钟（单 GPU），LLaMA2-70B 仅增加 5 分钟；叠 加在 SpinQuant/DartQuant 上多数设定仍继续降困惑度。

## Key Contributions

- **Analytical characterization of bimodal fragmentation**: The Gaussian approximation of RHT relies on the CLT/Lindeberg condition; when a single channel holds more energy than all remaining channels combined (M_t² > Σ_{i≠cmax} x_{t,i}²), each output channel becomes a two-Gaussian mixture with means 2|M_t|/√Cin apart. The empty gap between the positive and negative clusters inflates the per-token quantization range and step size (bounding quantization MSE at Δt²/4). On LLaMA2-7B layer 2 (Cin = 11,008), the bimodality threshold is ≈58 while the observed massive outlier is M_t = 1510 (≈26x larger).
- **SignRot**: identifies the dominant massive-outlier channel cmax with a single calibration forward pass (massive-outlier channels are fixed across inputs), then builds a sign vector n_j = sign(h_{cmax,j}) so that row cmax of H·diag(n) becomes uniformly +1/√Cin — the outlier term turns into a constant shift and the distribution collapses to unimodal. Since diag(n) is involutory, its inverse merges into W_d offline; inference adds only element-wise sign flips, no extra matmul.
- **Asym-Symm matrix multiplication**: because the aligned outlier shifts the activation mean off zero, SignRot pairs the sign adjustment with per-token asymmetric activation quantization; the zero-point correction z_X(1ᵀW_q) is precomputed offline from weight column sums, adding only O(T·Cout) per inference call versus O(T·Cin·Cout) for the matmul itself.
- **Locating where the error comes from**: quantizing one decoder layer at a time (all others in rotated FP16), the first massive-outlier layer is among the three most expensive layers (2.3–3.6x the median KL of layers without massive outliers) and its error propagates to all other tokens through attention (the massive-outlier token acts as an attention sink); SignRot cuts that layer's KL by 36–61% and drops other tokens' KL by 31–64%.
- **Practicality**: drop-in on top of QuaRot, SpinQuant, and DartQuant, for dense and MoE models (Qwen3-30B-A3B); quantizes a 7B model in ~3 min (RTN) / ~15 min (GPTQ) on one GPU, adds ~5 min for LLaMA2-70B, and adds only 0.43% end-to-end latency at inference.

## Methodology

The paper first analyzes when the randomized Hadamard transform fails. Writing the rotated activation as y_{t,j} = M_t · r_{cmax} h_{cmax,j} + Σ_{i≠cmax} x_{t,i} r_i h_{i,j}, the outlier contributes a random ±M_t/√Cin term while the residual has variance σ_rest². The output distribution is approximated by an equal mixture of two Gaussians N(±M_t/√Cin, σ_rest²), which is bimodal iff the means are more than 2σ_rest apart, i.e., iff the outlier channel carries more energy than all remaining channels combined. The authors define a massive-outlier token as one whose largest channel satisfies this energy-dominance condition and reaches at least half of the layer's peak activation (peak ≥ 100) — an energy-based criterion that they show (Appendix C) both over- and under-selects relative to Sun et al.'s fixed magnitude threshold.

SignRot then proceeds in three steps. (1) Channel identification: over a calibration activation matrix X, pick cmax = argmax_c max_t |x_{t,c}|; one channel per model position, identified once, because massive-outlier channels are stable across inputs (in the first massive-outlier layer of every model examined, all such tokens share the same dominant channel, holding 87–99.9% of token energy). (2) Sign adjustment: define n ∈ {±1}^{Cin} with n_j = sign(h_{cmax,j}) and use H_adj = H·diag(n). Since diag(n)⁻¹ = diag(n), the pair (X·H_adj, H_adj⁻¹·W) is mathematically identical to (X, W); diag(n)⁻¹ is fused into W_d offline while n_d is applied online to the intermediate activation at the down-projection input, costing only element-wise sign flips (Figure 2/3). (3) Asymmetric activation quantization with Asym-Symm matmul: Y_deq = Δ_X(X_q W_q − Z_X W_q)Δ_W, where the zero-point correction Z_X W_q = z_X(1ᵀW_q) factorizes as a rank-1 outer product with the offline-precomputed weight column sums.

Experiments use LLaMA2 (7B/13B/70B), LLaMA3 (8B/70B), Mistral-7B-v0.3, and the MoE Qwen3-30B-A3B; weight quantization by RTN or GPTQ (128 WikiText-2 calibration samples); per-token asymmetric activations (per-channel symmetric for QuaRot's official setting); group-wise asymmetric KV-cache quantization at head-dimension group size; CUTLASS 4-bit matmuls and FlashInfer KV-cache kernels for the efficiency measurements. Evaluation covers WikiText2 and C4 perplexity plus six zero-shot tasks (ARC-Easy/Challenge, HellaSwag, LAMBADA-OpenAI, PIQA, WinoGrande).

## Results & Highlights

- **LLaMA3-70B, GPTQ W4A4** (headline): QuaRot+SignRot reaches WikiText2 PPL 5.88 and average zero-shot accuracy 72.73, vs. QuaRot 6.60 / 70.45 with its official symmetric activation quantization; under the same asymmetric activation quantization the ablation (Table 4) isolates the SignRot delta at 6.16 → 5.88 PPL and 72.28 → 72.73 accuracy, surpassing learned-rotation SpinQuant (6.38 PPL / 71.15 acc).
- **On top of SpinQuant (LLaMA3-70B GPTQ)**: SpinQuant+SignRot improves WikiText2 PPL from 6.38 to 5.45 and average zero-shot accuracy from 71.15 to 73.52.
- **LLaMA2 family, GPTQ**: QuaRot+SignRot beats SpinQuant's average 0-shot accuracy by +0.92 on LLaMA2-7B (67.19 vs. 66.27) and +0.76 on LLaMA2-13B (70.70 vs. 69.94); on LLaMA2-13B it matches DartQuant's Wiki2 PPL (5.32) without any rotation training.
- **RTN settings**: on LLaMA3-8B, average 0-shot accuracy improves by up to +2.83 points (61.34 → 64.17) and Wiki2 PPL drops 10.60 → 9.68; the gain shrinks with LLaMA2 scale (2.70 / 1.26 / 0.39 points for 7B/13B/70B), consistent with larger models being more quantization-robust.
- **Generalization (Table 2)**: on MoE Qwen3-30B-A3B with GPTQ, average 0-shot accuracy +0.69 (65.11 → 65.80) and Wiki2 PPL 10.16 → 9.79; on Mistral-7B-v0.3, Wiki2 PPL 5.80 → 5.71 with accuracy held (71.53 vs. 71.42).
- **Mechanistic evidence (Appendix C)**: SignRot reduces the single-layer KL of the first massive-outlier layer by 36–61%, reduces fully-quantized model KL by 2.0–4.5% (W4A4) / 2.9–6.5% (W16A4), and narrows the per-token activation range by a median 2.6–11.3x (up to 885x on nearly one-hot Qwen3 tokens).
- **Efficiency (Table 3, LLaMA2-7B on RTX 3090, batch 1)**: SignRot adds +1.37% prefill, +0.34% per-token decode, +0.43% end-to-end latency, +0.02% peak memory, while retaining 1.73x/1.60x/1.65x speedups over FP16 and 3.19x memory savings.
- **Robustness**: swapping calibration datasets (WikiText-2 vs. C4) and seeds moves Wiki2/C4 PPL by at most 0.06/0.08 and average accuracy by ≤0.9 points (Table 5); adjusting only the down-projection input X̃d gives the best mean accuracy (67.74) among all placement variants (Table 6).

## Authors & Credibility

The team is from Hanyang University (Jaehun Gim, Jaewoo Kim, and corresponding author Seo Yeon Park) and UC Santa Barbara (Gyuwan Kim, known for work on efficient ML / quantization). The paper is a preprint (arXiv:2611.16502v1, cs.AI, 12 Nov 2026) with code released at https://github.com/HYU-ARK-Lab/SignRot, funded by NRF and IITP grants from the Korean government (MSIT). Credibility signals: the ablations report mean ± std over 6–11 seeds (Tables 4, 6), a dedicated ablation (Table 4) honestly disentangles the contribution of asymmetric quantization from the sign adjustment itself, an insensitivity study covers calibration data and seeds, and the analysis is backed by a formal derivation (Appendix A) plus per-layer KL diagnostics (Appendix C). One caveat: the LLaMA3-70B ablation row is a single run, as the authors themselves note.

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty**: the fragmentation diagnosis is genuinely new and cleanly argued (Lindeberg-condition violation → two-Gaussian mixture → bimodality threshold), and the fix is refreshingly simple. But sign/diagonal adjustments to Hadamard transforms are a known design space (QuaRot itself uses a random sign diag(r); SpinQuant learns rotation matrices), so the contribution is best viewed as an elegant, well-analyzed drop-in rather than a new family of methods.
- **Confound in headline numbers**: the abstract's "6.60 → 5.88" compares QuaRot's official symmetric-activation setting against SignRot with asymmetric activation quantization, so part of the headline delta (6.60 → 6.16) comes from just switching quantizers. The like-for-like SignRot delta is 6.16 → 5.88 — still real, but smaller; Table 4 does disclose this properly.
- **Uneven and sometimes negative gains**: on LLaMA2-7B GPTQ the Wiki2 PPL is essentially flat (6.10 → 6.09); under symmetric activation quantization SignRot can slightly hurt average accuracy (66.54 → 66.33 on LLaMA2-7B, Table 4), confirming the method is only complete with asymmetric quantization; DartQuant+SignRot on LLaMA3-70B *loses* 0.37 accuracy points (76.39 → 76.02), and DartQuant alone still clearly beats QuaRot+SignRot there (4.93 vs. 5.88 PPL, 76.39 vs. 72.73 acc). Some individual tasks regress (ARC-C on Qwen3-30B-A3B GPTQ: 48.89 → 47.70; LAMBADA on Mistral: 73.18 → 72.64).
- **Coverage limits acknowledged but real**: only the single dominant channel per token is aligned; in the last layers of several models the second massive channel is comparable (M2/M1 = 0.53–0.68, M1 holds only 54–78% of energy) and a single sign vector cannot fold both — explicitly left as future work. Evaluation is W4A4-only with a commonsense zero-shot suite; no harder reasoning benchmarks (e.g., GSM8K/MMLU) and no sub-4-bit stress test of the fragmentation story.
- **Rigor/reproducibility**: strong for a systems-quantization paper — released code, seed-averaged ablations, deployment kernels (CUTLASS/FlashInfer) with realistic batch-1 latency on a consumer GPU, and an honest calibration-sensitivity study. The Gaussian-mixture model is presented with appropriate caveats (the bimodality threshold is exact only within that approximation).

## Strengths

- Precise, falsifiable diagnosis: the energy-dominance condition (outlier channel > rest of token combined) with a concrete numeric example (threshold ≈58 vs. M_t = 1510 on LLaMA2-7B layer 2) explains *when* and *why* RHT fragments.
- Near-zero inference cost: element-wise sign flips only (+0.43% E2E latency, +0.02% memory), with the asymmetric-quantization correction reduced to a precomputed rank-1 term.
- Broad compatibility: consistent improvements on top of three different rotation-based pipelines (QuaRot, SpinQuant, DartQuant) and across dense + MoE architectures.
- The per-layer KL analysis (attention-sink error propagation) elevates the paper above a pure trick — it explains why fixing ~1 token per sequence moves model-level metrics.
- Fully training-free and fast to apply (3–15 min for a 7B model on a single GPU; +5 min for 70B).

## Limitations

- Benefit is highly model-dependent: large on LLaMA3 (especially 70B), marginal on LLaMA2 GPTQ perplexity, and occasionally negative (DartQuant+SignRot on LLaMA3-70B accuracy; several individual tasks).
- Requires asymmetric activation quantization to pay off — under symmetric quantization the shifted mean is not absorbed and gains can vanish or invert.
- One sign vector per layer handles only one dominant channel; tokens with multiple comparable massive channels (late layers; every massive-outlier token in LLaMA3-70B's first massive-outlier layer has more than one) are only partially fixed.
- Evaluation limited to 4-bit weights/activations/KV on standard commonsense tasks; no long-context, no W4A3/W2A2 stress regime where fragmentation should matter even more.
- The single-run LLaMA3-70B ablation and the mixture-model approximation of the bimodality threshold leave some statistical looseness in the largest-scale claims.

## Relevance

这项工作直接命中"LLM 量化 / 低比特推理"这一核心兴趣：它给出了 rotation-based PTQ（QuaRot/SpinQuant/DartQuant 谱系）在 massive outlier 下失效的机理分析与近乎零开销的修复方案，对 4-bit 部署（含 MoE 模型）有直接的工程参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does sign adjustment help at W4A3 or W4A2, where the inflated step size from fragmentation should matter much more than at W4A4?
- Can a per-channel-group Hadamard + sign vector (the authors' proposed future work) fold the late layers where M2/M1 ≈ 0.5–0.7, and would that finally make DartQuant+SignRot positive on LLaMA3-70B?
- Is the dominant massive-outlier channel stable under distribution shift (code, math, long context), or does the single calibration pass silently pick the wrong channel out-of-domain?
- Does the same polarity-induced fragmentation appear in the KV cache rotation paths or in MoE router inputs, where Qwen3 shows nearly one-hot massive-outlier tokens (885x range reduction)?
- How does SignRot interact with learned diagonal sign matrices (replacing QuaRot's random diag(r)) — is there remaining headroom in jointly choosing r and n?
