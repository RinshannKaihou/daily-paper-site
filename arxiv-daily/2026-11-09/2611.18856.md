> **一句话总结：** Google 提出"持续递归自我改进"（CRSI）设定——agent harness 优化在换基准或换策略模型后继续进行——并为此设计了自改进记忆 SIM：按循环出现的 harness 问题（根因假设）归组编辑，记录每个修改的理由、预测与实测结果及来源（域/基准/模型/轮次），供 proposer 和 selector 全量读取；在 Terminal-Bench 2.1→4 迁移的 10 组配对实验中，带 SIM 的延续平均终分 21.70 vs 仅带演化 harness 的 19.33（+2.36 分，p=0.003），在 Opus→Gemini 工作区迁移中从共同起点 68.41 分别恢复到 84.75 与 73.42。

> **TL;DR:** This paper formalizes Continual Recursive Self-Improvement (CRSI) — continuing LLM agent-harness optimization after the benchmark or the policy model changes — and introduces Self-Improvement Memory (SIM), a structured record that groups harness edits by recurring root cause and stores each edit's rationale, predicted vs. observed task outcomes, and provenance, injected into both the proposer's and selector's prompts. Across four domains with matched starting harnesses, rounds, and candidate budgets, Full SIM beats harness-only and root-cause-only continuation in all ten settings: in ten paired coding runs (Terminal-Bench 2.1 → Terminal-Bench 4, Terminus-2 seed), SIM reaches mean held-out pass@1 21.70 vs 19.33 for harness-only continuation (+2.36 points, p = 0.003, 9/10 pairs won); in Claude Opus 5.5 → Gemini 3.8 Flash workspace transfer, SIM recovers from a negative-transfer start of 68.41 to 84.75 vs 73.42 harness-only, also beating cold-start RSI (82.98).

## 核心贡献

- **定义 CRSI（持续递归自我改进）设定**：harness 优化不再局限于固定策略模型 + 固定基准，而是显式研究两类延续——跨数据集（策略固定、基准更换）与跨模型（基准和划分固定、策略更换，含模型家族间迁移与同家族代际演化），并保持可执行 harness 接口兼容。
- **提出 SIM（自改进记忆）**：服务于 harness 优化器（proposer/selector）而非任务求解策略。每个条目以根因假设为键，按时间序记录各次尝试的修改内容与理由（rationale）、评估前写下的预测任务修复清单、评估后的实测结果（哪些修复、哪些未修复）、以及 provenance（域、基准、策略模型、轮次）；相同根因文本的条目自动合并，被否决或导致回归的编辑同样入档。
- **受控的四条件对比协议**：Cold Start（种子 harness、无记忆）/ Harness Transfer（演化 harness、无记忆）/ Problem-Only Memory（仅根因）/ Full SIM（完整记录），三者共享同一演化起点并匹配轮数（N=10）与候选预算（k=5），把"记忆本身"与"起点 harness"解耦。
- **跨四个域的系统证据 + 配对统计**：coding（TB2.1→TB4）、文本分类（3 数据集→8 数据集）、agentic workspace（Harvey LAB→JobBench+GDPval+APEX-Agents）、工程设计（EngDesign→Frontier-Eng）；coding 额外做了 10 个独立源运行的配对研究（9/10 胜，均值 +2.36，p=0.003，dz=1.28）。
- **机制层面的迁移分析**：Appendix F/Table 8 逐域梳理哪些机制被直接复用（如 BM25 检索、终端/进程层）、哪些需适配（如 paste-buffer 传输的按键归一化）、哪些依赖目标设定（如针对单一模型输出的解析器修复），并展示 SIM 如何保留被替换编辑的未解预测（P8）与同一诊断下的跨设定证据累积（P3）。

## Key Contributions

- **Formalizes CRSI**, a setting where harness RSI continues after a benchmark change (policy fixed) or a policy-model change (benchmark and split fixed), covering cross-family transfer, model-generation chains, and one harness shared by multiple models.
- **Introduces Self-Improvement Memory (SIM)**: edits are grouped by recurring harness problem (a root-cause hypothesis); each attempt stores the modification and rationale, task-fix predictions written before evaluation, measured outcomes after, and provenance (domain, benchmark, policy, round). Entries with identical root-cause text merge without dropping history; regressing or failed edits are still recorded.
- **A controlled four-condition protocol** (Cold Start / Harness Transfer / Problem-Only Memory / Full SIM) from a common evolved harness with matched rounds and candidate budgets, isolating the memory contribution from the starting-harness contribution.
- **Evidence across four domains plus a paired statistical study**: Full SIM finishes highest in all six cross-dataset and four Opus-to-Gemini settings; ten independent coding source runs give a mean +2.36-point paired advantage (p = 0.003).
- **Mechanism-level transfer analysis** (Table 8 and case studies P3/P5/P8): documents which inherited mechanisms are reused directly, adapted, extended, or remain inactive, and shows concrete failure modes of memory-less continuation (e.g., misattributing an inherited completion gate to round 1 and twice repairing deadlocks it created).

## Methodology

The paper runs harness RSI in the Meta-Harness proposer–selector loop: at round t the evaluator executes harness H_t on the evolve set and returns scores, traces, and errors F_t; the proposer P(H_t, F_t, D_t) generates k = 5 candidate harnesses; the selector S screens proposals before execution and chooses among evaluated candidates (weighing performance, complexity, domain specificity, regression risk); if nothing is accepted, H_{t+1} = H_t. Every run uses N = 10 rounds, 5 rollouts per task, and evolve/held-out splits of roughly equal size split uniformly at random; only evolve-set feedback ever reaches the proposer, selector, or SIM.

SIM modifies the loop to C_t = P(H_t, F_t, D_t; M_t), H_{t+1} = S(H_t, F_t, C_t, F_t^C; M_t): both proposer and selector receive the complete memory M_t in the system prompt — no retrieval, no truncation. After selection, only the chosen edit is appended (to a cited existing root-cause entry or a new one), even when it regresses or fails to fix its predicted tasks. Predictions of which tasks an edit will fix are recorded before evaluation and checked afterward, giving per-attempt fixed/missed/passed/total counts.

CRSI then continues optimization from the source result (H_S, M_S) in a target setting σ_T = (M_T, X_T) under one of four memory conditions — Cold Start (H_0, ∅), Harness Transfer (H_S, ∅), Problem-Only Memory (H_S, M_S with root causes only), Full SIM (H_S, M_S) — with the condition persisting throughout target RSI. Domains: coding TB2.1 → TB4 (pass@1; seeds: minimal AHE and Terminus-2); text classification 3-dataset source suite → 8-dataset target suite absent from source RSI (pooled accuracy; seeds: NoMemory, FewShotAll); agentic workspace Harvey LAB → equal-weight mean of JobBench, GDPval, APEX-Agents (rubric/win-rate/pass@1; ReAct seed over an MCP gateway; Gemini 3.8 Flash judge); engineering EngDesign → Frontier-Eng (medal score). Cross-model CRSI evolves with Claude Opus 5.5 and continues with Gemini 3.8 Flash on the same benchmark and split; Gemini 3.8 Flash serves as proposer/selector in the main comparisons (an ablation swaps in Opus). A ten-pair coding study repeats source RSI ten times from Terminus-2 on TB2.1 and runs two matched TB4 continuations per evolved harness (with vs. without SIM), treating the independent source run as the unit of analysis.

## Results & Highlights

- **Paired coding study (TB2.1 → TB4, ten independent Terminus-2 source runs):** from a shared mean starting held-out TB4 pass@1 of 18.36, continuation with SIM averages 21.70 vs 19.33 without it — a mean paired advantage of +2.36 points (95% CI [1.05, 3.68]; two-sided paired t(9) = 4.06, p = 0.003; signed-rank p = 0.006; dz = 1.28). SIM wins 9 of 10 pairs; the single loss is −0.61 points.
- **Cross-dataset CRSI (all six settings):** Full SIM finishes highest everywhere, exceeding Harness Transfer by 2.43–4.35 points and Problem-Only Memory by 0.20–6.06 points. Largest coding jump: AHE seed on TB4, 18.18 → 24.85 (Full SIM) vs 20.61 (Harness Transfer), 18.79 (Problem-Only), 19.39 (Cold Start). Workspace (Harvey LAB → merged target): 74.93 → 87.89 (Full SIM) vs 85.07 (Harness Transfer) and 82.98 (Cold Start). Engineering: 39.13 → 46.96 vs 42.61 and 40.87.
- **Cross-model CRSI (Opus 5.5 → Gemini 3.8 Flash):** the Opus-evolved harness starts below Gemini's seed in every domain (deficits 0.60–5.48 points), and Harness Transfer finishes below Cold Start in three domains — yet Full SIM exceeds harness-only continuation by 3.60–11.33 points and wins all four domains. Workspace is the largest gap: both transfer arms start at 68.41 (seed 73.89); Full SIM reaches 84.75 vs 73.42 for Harness Transfer and 82.98 for Cold Start. Engineering: Full SIM 50.43 vs 42.61 (Harness Transfer) and 40.87 (Cold Start).
- **Root causes alone are not enough (memory ablation):** Problem-Only Memory has mixed effects — from 1.82 points below Harness Transfer to 2.40 above — while Full SIM beats it in all ten settings, indicating the rationale/outcome/provenance evidence, not just the diagnosis, drives the benefit. Example (text classification, FewShotAll): source-evolved harness starts target at 72.92, below the seed's 75.72; harness-only continuation reaches 74.12 (still under Cold Start's 75.92) while Full SIM reaches 77.92.
- **Model-generation chain on TB2.1:** carrying harness + SIM beats fresh RSI in two of three generations — +3.5 points on Gemini 3.5 Flash (76.8 vs 73.3) and +1.6 on Gemini 3.8 Flash (90.5 vs 88.9) — but trails by 0.8 on Gemini 4 Argon (94.8 vs 95.6). Shared-harness study (text classification): unified synthesis reaches 75.24 held-out vs 75.20 for separately evolved per-model harnesses (seed: 73.28); sequential CRSI 74.20.
- **Memory also reduces forgetting of source performance:** during target RSI on TB4, held-out TB2.1 scores fall from a common 90.22 to 88.89 for Full SIM (3 lost successful rollouts of 225) vs 87.56 for Harness Transfer (6 lost); source RSI itself had improved held-out source scores by 2.75–6.41 points in all six settings.

## Authors & Credibility

A Google Cloud AI / Google DeepMind / Google Cloud AI Research team with Penn State (first author Xin Yu did the work as a Google intern; Lingzhou Xue is a Penn State statistician; Ting Yu is a senior Google Cloud AI lead). The work builds directly on the 2026 harness-optimization literature it cites — Meta-Harness (Lee et al.), AHE (Lin et al.), RRSI (Xia et al.), Terminus-2 — and adopts their environments, seeds, and evaluation interfaces rather than inventing bespoke ones, which strengthens comparability. Reporting quality is above average for this genre: paired t-tests with confidence intervals, signed-rank checks, standardized effect sizes, exact per-condition tables, and explicit negative results (below-seed transfer starts, one losing generation).

## Critical Assessment

- **Novelty — moderate, mostly in structure and evaluation, not ingredients.** Every SIM component exists in prior work: candidate artifacts (Meta-Harness), edit histories (Xia et al. 2026), problem-grouped harness banks (HarnessBank), and free-text notes with source replay (Xu 2026, Harness2). The delta is the specific schema (root cause + rationale + predicted-vs-observed outcomes + provenance) and, more substantially, the CRSI protocol with matched controls — a careful systems/evaluation contribution rather than a new algorithm.
- **Rigor — strong in coding, thin elsewhere.** Outside coding, every condition is a single RSI run (acknowledged in Limitations); the five rollouts estimate execution variance, not optimization variance, so margins like +0.20 over Problem-Only Memory on NoMemory (73.60 vs 73.40) or the +3.5/+1.6/−0.8 generation-chain deltas rest on n = 1 per cell. The headline +2.36 (p = 0.003) is the only statistically protected claim; the cross-model workspace gap of +11.33, while visually striking, is one run per arm. On TB4 held-out (33 tasks × 5 rollouts) one rollout ≈ 0.61 points, so +2.36 ≈ four rollouts — real but small in absolute task count.
- **Confound in the headline attribution.** SIM is evaluated "as a complete procedure": the memory condition persists through target RSI, so a write-but-don't-inherit control (fresh SIM written during target RSI from H_S) is missing — gains could partly come from merely keeping organized notes during the target run rather than from carrying source experience. The paper admits this but does not resolve it.
- **Scalability unexamined.** Proposer and selector read the entire SIM in-prompt with no retrieval or truncation; with N = 10 rounds this is a few entries. No token-cost accounting, no test of 100-round runs or large memories, and no ablation of SIM-for-proposer vs. SIM-for-selector.
- **Judge and benchmark caveats.** Workspace scores use Gemini 3.8 Flash as judge — the same model as the target policy and the main proposer/selector — a circularity acknowledged in the ethics statement but not ablated. Frontier-Eng's overlapping EngDesign component scores zero yet stays in the denominator (scores deflated by design). The multi-setting descriptive comparisons carry no multiple-comparison correction.
- **Red flags are limited and honestly disclosed:** below-seed transfer starts (72.92 vs 75.72 FewShotAll; 68.41 vs 73.89 workspace), Harness Transfer losing to Cold Start in three of four cross-model domains, and the Gemini 4 Argon generation loss are all reported rather than buried.

## Strengths

- Genuinely controlled design: four memory conditions from one shared evolved harness with matched rounds, candidate budgets, policy, and tasks — the right way to isolate what memory adds.
- The paired ten-source-run coding study with t-test, CI, signed-rank, and effect size is rare rigor for agent-harness papers.
- Transparent about failure: negative transfer at the start, one losing SIM pair, one losing generation, and Problem-Only's mixed results are all surfaced.
- The case studies (P3, P5, P8; Table 8) go beyond score deltas to explain *how* memory changes optimizer behavior — e.g., with SIM the proposer retains completion verification while adding feasibility-vs-optimality checks; without it, the run tightens the inherited gate, twice repairs completion deadlocks, and misattributes the inherited gate to its own round 1.
- SIM keeps failed predictions and replaced edits (P3: source attempt fixed 1 of 4 predicted tasks; target rounds fixed 0 of 2 then 1 of 2), giving later rounds the evidence behind a diagnosis, not just its name.

## Limitations

- Independent repetitions exist only in coding; the other three domains have one run per condition, so their margins are descriptive.
- SIM is evaluated end-to-end; the inherited-memory vs. target-written-memory attribution is confounded (no write-without-inherit arm).
- Full-memory-in-prompt with no retrieval or truncation is untested beyond 10 rounds and a handful of entries; prompt-cost scaling is unmeasured.
- Only transitions with compatible executable harness interfaces are studied; the optimizer's planning and search procedures stay fixed, and planner/tool-interface adaptation is future work.
- Short horizons: 10 rounds × 5 candidates per run; the longest chain is four Gemini generations on one benchmark.
- Workspace judging relies on a single LLM judge from the same family as the target policy.

## Relevance

直接命中"Self-Evolving Agents & Automated Research"兴趣区：这是 harness 级递归自我改进（RSI）的延续学习工作，回答自进化智能体的核心问题——演化出的 agent harness 在换模型或换基准后如何携带经验继续进化而非从零重搜；同时 Appendix F 的案例（提前完成门控、JSON 工具调用解析器吞掉合法命令等）也是 agent harness 层静默失效检测与修复的具体素材，与 Agent & Pipeline Silent Failures 方向相呼应。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- How much of SIM's advantage comes from inherited source memory versus entries written fresh during target RSI? A "write-but-don't-inherit" control (empty SIM at transfer, SIM maintained during target) would cleanly separate the two.
- Does reading the full SIM in-prompt scale to 100+ entries or 100-round runs, or is retrieval/summarization eventually needed — and does entry ordering or pruning change the outcome?
- Does SIM help when benchmark and policy model change simultaneously, or when the harness interface itself is incompatible (tool-schema migration)?
- The P3 trace shows most predicted task fixes failing (1/4, 0/2, 1/2) — can prediction calibration be improved so unresolved predictions better guide when to abandon a root-cause hypothesis?
- SIM makes reuse more efficient, but does structured memory of evaluator-specific fixes also increase or decrease the reward-hacking risk flagged in the ethics statement (a harness satisfying the evaluator without solving the task)?
