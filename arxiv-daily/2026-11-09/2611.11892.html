Key Finding · 核心发现

A strong answer readout is not, by itself, evidence the model has decided.

「答案可解码」是相对于目标与协议的性质——在干扰项即将被写出的前一个 token 处，探针读到的是「下一个要写的词」，而不是「结论」。

When Answer Decodability Is Not a Decision: Separating Final-Answer and Imminent-Emission Signals in Chain-of-Thought

Key Metric · 排放差距
.696
Emission gap Δemit on DecisionDepth — Qwen3.5-4B, n=353 held-out items
The probe decodes the imminent mention at .966 (null .234, z=32.4) but the final answer at only .221 (null .186, below the z=3 screen). 95% CI [.648, .744]. Llama-3.1-8B independently replicates at .575.
Why This Matters · 为什么重要

A reusable experimental warning for any interpretability work that probes LLM hidden states for answers, beliefs, or decisions.

对所有用 probe 从 LLM 内部状态读出「答案/信念/决策」的工作，这是直接的实验设计警示，也是一套可复用的对照方案。

Critical Edge · 保留意见

The headline gap comes from a synthetic, near-ceiling task (.987 accuracy) — and the matched cross-comparison designed to isolate what reverses the order failed to train, leaving the central question open.

Methodology 方法

Tasks
DecisionDepth (DD): synthetic multi-candidate deduction (rule chains, code-name chains, value intervals; depth 2) with symmetric candidates so surface features cannot leak the answer. Public fallback: BIG-Bench Hard Logical Deduction (5/7-object). MMLU / MMLU-Redux for the reasoning-model sections.
Models & rollouts
Qwen3.5-4B and Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, thinking disabled, 2048-token rationale budget; only correct rollouts analyzed (so F = gold). Qwen3.5-4B solves DD at .987.
Reader
Rank-4 bilinear scorer sk = (Ux)T(Vak) over centered, layer-normed layer-32 states; candidates embedded by the same model — only 20k–33k parameters, deliberately tiny so capacity stays controlled. Five item-level folds.
Dual targets
At the token immediately before each non-gold candidate mention, the same reader family is fitted twice: to the imminent mention Mt and to the final answer F (Mt ≠ F by construction).
Decision rule
Emission gap Δemit with target-specific nulls (1/(K−1) vs 1/K), thresholds fixed before any fit, 10,000 item-level bootstraps.
Control battery
Planted gold-embedding doses (1.0 / 0.1 / 0.03), capacity curve up to a 6.3M-param MLP, label shuffles, random-position controls, layer sweep (16–32), and a pre-specified Qwen3-8B counterexample protocol.

  • Not blind to answers: the planted-signal control recovers an injected answer embedding at one-tenth dose (.317) vs an observed excess of only .035 — the Mt readout equals planting the full answer signal; F equals planting almost none.
  • Capacity memorizes, doesn't generalize: training accuracy climbs .300 → .959 (6.3M-param MLP) while held-out stays .157–.193, never reaching the .226 hidden-signal threshold.
  • Counterexample anatomy: the F probe tracks the model's own answer (.798 on 102 incorrectly answered questions vs .115 for gold) and appears before any option letter is referenced.
  • Causal attempts inconclusive: steering failed its gate (0/400 answers changed); the exploratory state interchange swapped the next mention in .700 of items but changed the eventual answer in only .005.

Strengths 优点

  • Sharp, nearly free control: dual-target probing with target-specific nulls that prior CoT answer-probing work lacks.
  • Statistically careful — pre-fixed thresholds and verdict rules, item-level bootstraps, conservative pooled z-statistics.
  • A model validity section: planted signals at three doses, a capacity curve, label shuffles, position and layer controls.
  • Exceptional transparency: a pre-specified counterexample reported against its own main result; five explicit evidence tiers; failures disclosed, not dropped.
  • Calibrated takeaway: not "models don't decide early," but "run the dual-target control before interpreting."

Limitations 局限

  • Headline rests on a synthetic, near-ceiling task (.987) and the narrow population of correct rollouts with a pre-answer distractor mention.
  • What reverses the order — model, task, positions, or reader — remains open; both cross-comparison cells failed to train.
  • Pre-specification is internal and non-public; intervals exclude training-seed variation and are unadjusted for multiplicity.
  • Causal status unresolved: steering apparatus failed its gate; the interchange is exploratory with no pre-fixed bound.
  • Only 4B–8B models, three of four sharing Qwen lineage; no code or data release.

Questions for Further Exploration 待探问题

  1. Which factor drives the reversal — model, task, positions, reader, or label definition — and why does the full-prefix reader fail to learn the DD Mt target while MMLU Mt trains?
  2. Does imminent-emission dominance hold on incorrect rollouts (F ≠ gold), or at task depths where the model is not at .987?
  3. Would order-aware nulls — models discussing options in enumerated order — change any "held" or borderline verdicts?
  4. Is there a native final-answer code off the planted candidate-embedding direction, or below one-tenth dose — e.g., recoverable with SAE features or held-out cross-entropy?
  5. Can an on-manifold intervention with a passing positive control test whether changing the imminent mention ever changes the eventual answer?
