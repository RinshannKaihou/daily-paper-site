Certified-invariance benchmarks plus pre-generation probes and causal head knockouts give a reusable template for monitoring shortcut behavior in deployed LLMs.

Critical Edge

The headline monitor is statistically tied with a fitted token-entropy baseline that needs no activation access at all.

01Methodology

CERTIFY→RENDER→MEASURE→MONITOR→EVALUATE
  • Certify. Each family is a symbolic template; SymPy proves the cue symbol is absent from the solved answer expression, which matches the declared answer within 10⁻⁶ relative tolerance — so any confidence shift belongs to the model, not the task.
  • Render. 155 families × 4 natural-language renderings (620 prompts), three cue types (irrelevant variable · unit-matched distractor · unit restatement) across mechanics, electrostatics/circuits, and heat.
  • Measure. One teacher-forced pass per rendering gives family sensitivity Slp = max log-prob drop on the certified answer; "sensitive" at ≥0.5 nats, threshold pre-registered 2026-06-14.
  • Monitor. L2-regularized logistic probe on the layer-ℓ residual stream at the last prompt token (family-averaged); leave-one-family-out over 135 families keeps all renderings on one side of every split.
  • Evaluate. Paired family bootstrap (10,000 resamples, seed 42) against 8 fitted non-activation baselines; causal test = multi-head knockout on 20 strongly sensitive Cue B families with six controls.

Models: Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct (primary, 28 layers, TransformerLens) · Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct · DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-32B (forward hooks).

03Strengths

  • Certification makes sensitivity model-attributable — the core idea is clean and reusable wherever closed-form answers exist.
  • Measurement design: teacher-forced, signed, decoding-independent statistic with a pre-registered threshold.
  • Evaluation hygiene: paired-bootstrap comparison against a fitted entropy baseline — including the honest report that it ties.
  • Causal rigor: six controls, 20/20 per-family random-direction beats, one-layer-wide localisation, accuracy-damage check, necessity/sufficiency decomposition.
  • Exceptional transparency: AI-use statement, superseded-audit disclosures, failure taxonomy, scope-of-claims appendix.

04Limitations

  • Small panel: 135 families / 70 positives cannot resolve 0.03–0.05 AUROC differences — probe-vs-entropy stays open.
  • Causal claim is Qwen-only: the knockout recipe fails in Llama (CI [−3.461, +6.653]) and DeepSeek (0.086).
  • Inflated transfer: pooled frozen-probe AUROC 0.820 drops to 0.667–0.6875 within cue type — base-rate skew.
  • Generalisation unproven: 71% cluster overlap; domain holdout p = 0.368 vs permutation null — template structure may leak.
  • No human validation of cue irrelevance yet; certification proves answer invariance, not psychological salience.
  • Known failure mode: the probe detects "dangerous template class," not numerical proximity — misses near-match-tail positives.

05Questions for Further Exploration

  1. Would the layer-18 probe retain its AUROC margin over fitted entropy on a 10× larger panel, or is the tie (Δ +0.033) the true effect size?
  2. Does head 11's mechanism in Qwen have a functional analogue in Llama/DeepSeek outside the probe-peak depth, or is this circuit a training accident?
  3. Can the monitor become numerically aware, capturing the near-match tail the family-mean feature misses?
  4. Do results transfer beyond certified-answer physics — to code, multi-step GSM-style reasoning, or multimodal inputs?
  5. Sensitivity–difficulty correlation is only −0.346: what would a joint (correctness, sensitivity) monitor buy for deployed abstention policies?
  6. Would chain-of-thought monitors recover AUROC above 0.599 if given the same family-averaged input the probe receives?
