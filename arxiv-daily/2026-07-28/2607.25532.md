> **一句话总结：** 本文证明当一个观测特征 X=[C; αS; η] 把因果信号 C 和虚假信号 S 打包进不同子空间、且二者在环境内被隐藏混杂因子 U 关联时，无论是线性岭回归式 in-context learner 还是 TabPFN，都会不可避免地"路由"到虚假信号 S（闭式 CSR∝ρ_S/ρ_C，线性 ICL r=0.997、TabPFN r=0.979 拟合验证），且上下文越大、模型越强，该问题在高虚假信号区域反而越严重；作者提出的 S-swap 数据增强无需修改模型即可将 TabPFN 的 CSR 降低 98.8%（在真实 scIB 胰腺单细胞数据上降低 88.9%），但代价是分布外 RMSE 在真实数据实验中反而从 1.74 恶化到 9.12。

> **TL;DR:** This paper formalizes "spurious routing in composite representations" — when a single observed tabular feature X=[C; αS; η] bundles a causal signal C and a spurious signal S in distinct subspaces that share an unmeasured within-environment confounder U — and proves that under in-context ridge regression (linear ICL), this routing is unavoidable for any regularization strength, with a closed-form Causal Sensitivity Ratio (CSR) ∝ ρ_S/ρ_C matching linear ICL at Pearson r=0.997 and TabPFN at r=0.979 on a controlled synthetic SCM benchmark. Larger context amplifies routing up to 1.74× in the high-spurious regime, and TabPFN (the baseline "standard ICL") is more vulnerable than linear ICL by +2.22 CSR at the high-entanglement corner. The proposed S-swap context augmentation cuts CSR by 74% (linear ICL, from 3.560 to 0.926) and 98.8% (TabPFN, from 5.777 to 0.067) at the high-spurious corner, and by 88.9% (from 3.416 to 0.379) on a real scIB human-pancreas single-cell dataset with an injected confounding path — though on that real dataset out-of-distribution RMSE simultaneously worsens from 1.74 to 9.12.

## 核心贡献

- **提出"复合表征中的虚假路由"这一新失效模式**：不同于以往 invariant learning 文献（IRM、Invariant Causal Prediction）研究的"跨变量"虚假相关（因果特征和虚假特征分处不同输入维度），本文研究的是同一个观测特征向量内、因果信号 C 与虚假信号 S 共享同一坐标或相邻子空间的情形，并证明当 C、S 完全纠缠在单一标量坐标时（D=1），基于扰动的路由诊断量在数学上不可辨识（Proposition 1）。
- **给出岭回归 in-context learner 的闭式路由公式**：在正交子空间假设（Cov(C,S)=0）和环境内混杂假设下，证明总体 CSR^pop = α²/(α²+λ) · (ρ_S/ρ_C) · (1+λ)κ(δ)（Proposition 2），并推出三条推论：CSR 随 ρ_S/ρ_C 单调递增（Corollary 1）、在任意正则化强度下 CSR^pop>0 恒成立即虚假路由不可通过正则化消除（Corollary 2）、且随上下文样本数 n→∞ 该路由持续存在而非被稀释（Corollary 3）。
- **在受控 SCM 基准上同时验证线性 ICL 与 TabPFN**：闭式预测与线性 ICL 实测 CSR 的 Pearson r=0.997，与 TabPFN 实测 CSR 的 r=0.979；发现更大上下文窗口在高虚假信号区域会放大路由达 1.74 倍，而 TabPFN 在高纠缠角落比线性 ICL 更脆弱（64 个网格设置中的 15 个、+2.22 的 CSR 差距）。
- **提出两种无需修改模型权重的轻量级缓解方法**：环境分层上下文构造（environment-stratified context construction）和 S-swap 增强（将上下文样本中虚假子空间部分替换为另一环境的样本、保持标签不变），二者只需弱环境标签、不需要知道因果/虚假的真实划分。S-swap 使线性 ICL 的 CSR 降低 74%、TabPFN 的 CSR 降低 98.8%，且 TabPFN 对真实信号 C 的敏感度同时提升 8.4 倍——模型并非变得对一切都不敏感，而是重新路由到了因果信号。
- **在真实单细胞基因组数据（scIB 人胰腺数据集）上验证方法的可操作性**：在完全不知道真实 C、S 划分、仅用批次标签做批次比例 PCA 估计 Ŝ 的情况下，S-swap 仍将标准 ICL 的 CSR 从 3.416 降至 0.379（降低 88.9%），上下文内 r(Ŝ,Y) 降低 60%；但同时观察到分布外 RMSE 从 1.74 恶化到 9.12，表明缓解虚假路由与提升分布外泛化并非总是一致的目标。

## Key Contributions

- **Formalizes a new ICL failure mode: spurious routing in composite representations.** Unlike prior invariant-learning work (IRM, Invariant Causal Prediction), which studies cross-variable spurious correlation where causal and spurious signals occupy separate input features, this paper studies the case where a single observed feature X=[C; αS; η] bundles a causal signal C and a spurious signal S in distinct (or fully overlapping) subspaces of the same vector, and proves that when C and S are fully entangled in one scalar coordinate (D=1), the perturbation-based routing diagnostic is provably unidentifiable (Proposition 1).
- **Derives a closed-form Causal Sensitivity Ratio (CSR) for ridge in-context regression.** Under orthogonal-subspace and within-environment-confounding assumptions, CSR^pop = α²/(α²+λ) · (ρ_S/ρ_C) · (1+λ)κ(δ) (Proposition 2), with three corollaries: CSR is monotonic in the causal/spurious covariance ratio and entanglement coefficient α (Corollary 1), spurious routing is unavoidable under any ridge regularization strength λ>0 whenever the confounder induces Cov(S,Y)≠0 (Corollary 2, "Impossibility under confounding"), and the routing persists asymptotically as context size n→∞ rather than being washed out (Corollary 3).
- **Empirically validates the theory on both linear ICL and TabPFN.** The closed-form prediction correlates with measured CSR at Pearson r=0.997 (linear ICL) and r=0.979 (TabPFN) across a grid of ρ_S×ρ_C settings; larger context amplifies routing up to 1.74× in the high-spurious regime, and TabPFN shows greater vulnerability than linear ICL specifically in the high-spurious, high-entanglement corner (+2.22 CSR gap, concentrated in 15/64 grid settings).
- **Introduces two model-agnostic mitigations that operate purely at the context-construction level.** Environment-stratified context sampling and S-swap augmentation (replacing the spurious-subspace dimensions of context points with values drawn from a different training environment while holding the label fixed) require only weak environment labels and no knowledge of the causal/spurious partition — making them directly applicable to frozen pretrained models like TabPFN with no retraining.
- **Demonstrates the mitigation on a real single-cell genomics dataset (scIB human pancreas) with a fully data-estimated spurious subspace.** With no ground-truth C/S partition — Ŝ is estimated purely from a batch-ratio PCA score across 5 sequencing-technology batches (16,382 cells) — S-swap still reduces CSR by 88.9% and in-context r(Ŝ,Y) by 60%, showing the diagnostic and mitigation transfer beyond the synthetic SCM.

## Methodology

The paper builds a controlled structural causal model (SCM) to isolate the failure mode, then validates against two in-context learners.

**Data-generating process (Section 3).** Observations arrive in environments e∈E, each with a scalar shift μ_e ~ N(0, σ_e²) (e.g., a hospital's equipment calibration offset). An unobserved confounder U ~ N(0,1) drives both the technical/spurious component and the outcome. Within environment e, N samples are generated as: C ~ N(0, I_{D_C}) (the causal signal); S = β_S U + sqrt(1-β_S²) ε_S + μ_e (the spurious/artefact signal, partly driven by U and partly by the environment shift); X = [C; αS; η] (the observed composite feature, with α controlling entanglement strength and η pure noise); and Y = (1/D_C)·1ᵀC + β_Y U + ε (the outcome, causally driven only by C, but correlated with S in-sample via the shared confounder U). By construction, Cov(C,S)=0 (orthogonal subspaces, Assumption A2) and Cov(S,Y)=β_Sβ_Y≠0 within each environment (in-context confounding, Assumption A3), while do(S=s) does not change Y — so any predictive weight an ICL places on S is provably non-causal.

**Two in-context learners are studied.** (1) *Linear ICL*: population ridge regression computed directly over the labeled context, w* = (Σ_X + λI)⁻¹Σ_XY (Assumption A1, following Akyürek et al. 2023's characterization of transformers as implicit ridge/gradient-descent solvers), with λ=10⁻³. (2) *TabPFN* (Hollmann et al., 2025): the pretrained Prior-Data Fitted Network transformer used in regression mode via quantile-binned continuous targets, n_ensemble_configurations=4 — treated as a black-box empirical test of whether the linear theory's qualitative predictions transfer to a nonlinear, pretrained model.

**Causal Sensitivity Ratio (Definition 1).** For a query point and a perturbation environment, ΔC and ΔS are the i.i.d. differences in C and S between the query and perturbation draws. For predictor f_w(C,S) = w_C C + w_S(αS), CSR(w) := E|w_S α ΔS| / E|w_C ΔC|. CSR≈0 means the model ignores S (pure causal routing); CSR≈1 means equal sensitivity; CSR>1 means the model is more sensitive to spurious than causal perturbations. This diagnostic requires no knowledge of the causal mechanism, only access to the (C,S) decomposition for perturbation — in the synthetic experiments C and S are known by construction (standard practice for validating a diagnostic before deploying it where they must be estimated).

**Experimental design (Section 5).** D_C=D_S=3, D_N=4 (D=10 total); each environment has N=300 observations; 3 training environments and 2 test environments; default context size n=300 unless varied. The main grid sweeps ρ_S∈{0.1,0.3,0.5,0.7}×ρ_C∈{0.3,0.5,0.7,0.9} at several α values, plus a robustness sweep that rotates the spurious subspace toward the causal subspace by angle θ∈{0°,10°,20°,30°,40°} to stress-test the orthogonality assumption (Table 4).

**Mitigations (Section 6).** *Environment-stratified* context construction samples n_ctx/n_env points from each training environment separately rather than pooling uniformly, reducing in-context Corr(S,Y) by mixing environments with different S distributions at the same Y level. *S-swap* augmentation pairs each context point (X_i, Y_i) with a counterfactual copy (X̃_i, Ỹ_i) where X̃_i's spurious dimensions are replaced with those of a point drawn from a different environment while the label is held fixed (Ỹ_i=Y_i) — this signals at the point level that S does not predict Y, without requiring gradient updates, since it operates purely at context-construction time (as opposed to prior counterfactual data augmentation methods, e.g. Kaushik et al. 2020, which operate at training time). An oracle "invariant augmentation" baseline (paired copies using the true causal C) is also tested to show CSR and OOD RMSE can diverge as metrics.

**Real-world validation (Section 6.3, scIB human pancreas dataset, Luecken et al. 2022).** 16,382 pancreatic islet cells across 5 sequencing-technology batches (natural environments with real batch effects). Ŝ is estimated with no ground-truth partition: a batch-ratio score is computed per principal component (Var_tech(z_e^(k))/Var_celltype(z_e^(k))), and the PC with the strongest batch-ratio (8.02) is assigned to Ŝ, while 8 PCs with batch-ratio<0.08 are assigned to Ĉ. A controlled confounding path is then injected on top of the real batch structure to create Y_semi = Y_INS + β·Ŝ_PC1 + ε (β=0.5, ρ_S/ρ_C=9.09) — i.e., the batch/PC structure is entirely real, but the outcome-confounding relationship is synthetically added so that ground truth for evaluating CSR reduction exists.

## Results & Highlights

- **Closed-form CSR prediction matches empirical routing almost exactly.** Across the ρ_S×ρ_C×α grid, the theoretical CSR^pop correlates with measured CSR at Pearson r=0.997 for linear ICL and r=0.979 for TabPFN (Section 5); using target ratios instead of empirical estimates gives r=0.985 (linear) and r=0.953 (TabPFN), and the predictor stays robust to estimation noise (Spearman r=0.849, Pearson r drops only 0.014 up to φ=60° estimation error, Appendix D).
- **S-swap reduces spurious routing dramatically at the high-spurious corner (ρ_S=0.7, ρ_C=0.3, α=1.0).** For linear ICL (Table 5): Standard ICL has CSR=3.560, OOD RMSE=5.146, r(S,Y)=0.299 vs. S-swap's CSR=0.926, OOD RMSE=3.387, r(S,Y)=0.112 — a 74.0% CSR reduction and 34.2% OOD RMSE reduction, crossing CSR<1 (causally dominant routing). For TabPFN (Table 6): Standard ICL CSR=5.777 vs. S-swap CSR=0.067 — a 98.8% reduction, with TabPFN's causal sensitivity (Δŷ_C) growing from 0.144 to 1.347 (+836%) while Δŷ_S collapses from 1.891 to 0.053 (−97.2%).
- **Real-data validation (scIB human pancreas, Table 7).** Standard ICL CSR=3.416±2.291 (confirming spurious routing on real genomic batch structure, no ground-truth partition used); S-swap with a purely data-estimated Ŝ reduces this to CSR=0.379±0.449 (−88.9%) and reduces in-context r(Ŝ,Y) from +0.545 to +0.216 (−60%). However, OOD RMSE simultaneously rises from 1.74 to 9.12, which the authors attribute to swapped PC-space contexts becoming out-of-distribution for any single real sequencing technology — an honestly reported cost, not swept under the rug.
- **Context size amplifies routing rather than resolving it (Table 2).** At α=1.0 for linear ICL, CSR rises from 1.997 (n=16) to 3.658 (n=128) to 3.484 (n=512) in the high-spurious regime — a ×1.74 amplification from n=16 to saturation — while falling ×0.72 in the low-spurious regime; TabPFN shows the same qualitative pattern (ρ_S=0.7: 3.14→4.95; ρ_S=0.3: 0.86→0.60), both saturating by n≈128.
- **Model capacity increases vulnerability specifically in the high-spurious corner.** TabPFN exceeds linear ICL's CSR in only 15 of 64 grid settings overall (mean gap +0.060, median −0.147, i.e. TabPFN is often more causally aligned than linear ICL), but at the high-spurious/high-entanglement corner (Table 3, α=2.0) TabPFN reaches CSR=8.550 vs. linear ICL's 6.508 — the reported "+2.22 CSR gap." The Pearson correlation between CSR and OOD degradation is r=0.814 for TabPFN vs. r=0.782 for linear ICL, indicating routing more directly predicts real generalization failure for the pretrained model.

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty:** The core distinction drawn — "cross-variable" spurious correlation (studied by IRM/Invariant Causal Prediction, where C and S occupy different input features) vs. "intra-variable/composite-representation" spurious routing (where C and S are entangled dimensions of the *same* observed feature) — is a genuinely useful reframing, and the authors are explicit that this gap is what existing invariance methods cannot address (neither environment stratification nor gradient penalties can disentangle C from S when both enter X and neither is directly observed). The unidentifiability result for the D=1 fully-entangled case (Proposition 1) is a clean, honest boundary condition that motivates the separated-subspace regime the rest of the paper studies.

**Rigor:** The theoretical chain (Propositions 1–2, Corollaries 1–3) is a careful, fully worked-out derivation for the linear ridge-ICL case, with proof sketches in the main text and stated full derivations in Appendix A. Three named assumptions (A1 ridge ICL, A2 orthogonal subspaces, A3 in-context confounding) are called out explicitly rather than buried, and the paper is candid that A2 (Cov(C,S)=0) is stress-tested rather than assumed to hold universally — the rotation robustness check (Table 4) shows the Pearson correlation between predicted and observed CSR degrades gracefully from r=0.986 (θ=0°) to r=0.939 (θ=40°, 19.7% variance overlap), rather than collapsing, which is a meaningful rigor check most papers would skip. A genuine weak point is the TabPFN theory gap: the closed-form CSR expression is a linear-ICL result, and the paper itself states in the Limitations that "the closed form does not extend directly to TabPFN" — the TabPFN correlation (r=0.979) is an empirical regularity, not a proven consequence of the theorem, so the "TabPFN shows qualitatively consistent behaviour" claim rests on curve-fitting a nonlinear black box to a linear-model formula rather than a derivation.

**Reproducibility:** The synthetic SCM is fully specified (exact generative equations for C, S, X, Y in Section 3), hyperparameters are given (λ=10⁻³, n_ensemble_configurations=4, D_C=D_S=3, N=300, 3 train/2 test environments), and the appendices are stated to contain full experimental configuration (Appendix C) and DGP parameterisation. No code repository link is visible in the pages reviewed, which is a limitation for a paper making precise numerical claims (Pearson r to three decimal places).

**Significance:** The impossibility result (Corollary 2: CSR^pop>0 for any λ>0 under confounding) is a meaningful negative result for practitioners relying on regularization or larger context to fix spurious reliance in tabular foundation models — it says neither lever works when the confound is intra-variable. The real-world scIB validation is the paper's strongest piece of evidence that this isn't just a synthetic-SCM artifact, but a specific data quirk deserves scrutiny: the outcome variable Y_semi in that experiment is not the real biological label but a semi-synthetic construction (Y_INS plus an injected β·Ŝ_PC1 confounding term) — the batch structure is real, but the very confounding relationship the method is being validated against is manufactured by the authors on top of it. This is methodologically defensible (there is no other way to have ground truth for CSR on real data) but means the "real-world validation" is still one step removed from a fully naturalistic confound, and it is a single dataset.

**Red flags / weaknesses to note:** (1) The invariant-augmentation "oracle" baseline in Table 5 reveals a genuine dissociation the paper is upfront about — it *increases* CSR by 12.3% while improving OOD RMSE by 26.2%, meaning CSR and OOD RMSE are not interchangeable success metrics, and a practitioner naively optimizing one could regress on the other. (2) S-swap's real-data OOD RMSE increase (1.74→9.12, a >5× degradation) alongside its CSR win on scIB pancreas is a substantial practical caveat that the abstract's headline numbers (74%/98.8%/88.9% CSR reductions) do not surface — a reader skimming only the abstract could reasonably come away thinking the fix is strictly beneficial. (3) The "high-spurious corner" where TabPFN is most vulnerable (+2.22 CSR gap) is concentrated in only 15 of 64 grid settings, and TabPFN is actually *more* causally aligned than linear ICL in the majority of settings (median gap −0.147) — the abstract's framing ("more expressive models show greater vulnerability") is true only in a specific corner of the parameter space, not as a general statement about capacity and vulnerability.

## Strengths

- Identifies and precisely formalizes a failure mode (intra-variable/composite-representation spurious routing) that is distinct from, and not addressed by, the extensive existing invariant-learning literature (IRM, Invariant Causal Prediction), with a clean unidentifiability boundary case (Proposition 1) motivating why the separated-subspace regime is the right minimal setting to study.
- Strong theory-to-experiment linkage: four falsifiable predictions from Proposition 2 (routing scales with ρ_S/ρ_C, larger context amplifies rather than resolves it, routing is unavoidable under confounding for any λ, more capacity increases vulnerability in the high-spurious regime) are each explicitly tested and the paper reports where each was confirmed, including quantitative correlation coefficients rather than only qualitative agreement.
- The mitigations (environment-stratified sampling, S-swap) are genuinely practical: they require no model retraining, no architecture changes, and only weak environment labels — directly usable with a frozen pretrained model like TabPFN, which is explicitly the deployment regime the paper targets.
- Honest reporting of a metric dissociation (CSR vs. OOD RMSE can move in opposite directions, shown via the invariant-augmentation oracle and the real-data OOD RMSE increase under S-swap) rather than only reporting the metric that looks best.
- Real-world validation attempts a fully unsupervised setting (Ŝ estimated from data with no oracle access), which is a meaningfully harder and more honest test than only reporting synthetic-SCM numbers.

## Limitations

- The core closed-form CSR result (Proposition 2) is proven only for population ridge regression (linear ICL); its application to TabPFN is empirical curve-matching, not a proven extension, as the paper's own Limitations section states ("the closed form does not extend directly to TabPFN").
- Assumption A2 (Cov(C,S)=0, orthogonal causal/spurious subspaces) is a modeling idealization; the robustness check only sweeps up to θ=40° (19.7% variance overlap) and the paper argues real harmonisation pipelines (ComBat, scVI) typically achieve well below 20% residual overlap — but this claim rests on citing one benchmarking paper (Luecken et al., 2022) rather than directly measuring overlap in the paper's own real-data experiment.
- S-swap requires at least two training environments; the paper explicitly notes both mitigations reduce to standard ICL in single-batch settings, limiting applicability to genuinely single-site data.
- Settings where S has only a weak causal path to Y, or where C and S interact nonlinearly, are stated by the authors to require a modified CSR protocol not developed in this paper.
- The real-world validation uses a semi-synthetic outcome (Y_semi with an injected confounding term) rather than a fully naturalistic confound, and reports a substantial OOD RMSE regression (1.74→9.12) as the cost of the CSR improvement — a trade-off not resolved or further analyzed within the paper.
- Only one real dataset (scIB human pancreas) is tested; generality across other modalities/domains (the clinical multi-site example that motivates the paper throughout is never itself tested on real clinical data) remains unverified.

## Relevance

本文对研究表格基础模型在多站点/多批次场景下的分布偏移与因果泛化问题具有直接参考价值：它给出了一个可操作的诊断量（CSR）和无需重训练即可用于 TabPFN 等预训练模型的缓解手段（S-swap），但其"分布外性能同时可能恶化"的真实数据结果提示，在实际部署前需要同时监控 CSR 与任务性能两个指标，不能只看其一。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Can the closed-form CSR^pop expression be extended (even approximately) to nonlinear/attention-based ICL, given that the paper explicitly leaves this as an open theoretical gap while relying on empirical fit for TabPFN?
- What explains the real-data OOD RMSE regression under S-swap (1.74→9.12) mechanistically — is it purely an artifact of swapped-PC contexts being out-of-distribution as the authors suggest, or does it reveal a more general tension between CSR minimization and task-performance preservation that could recur in other domains?
- How sensitive is the batch-ratio PCA heuristic for estimating Ŝ to the number/choice of principal components, and would a different unsupervised subspace-estimation method (e.g., contrastive representation learning per Corcoll Andreu et al. 2024, cited in Related Work) yield a better CSR/OOD RMSE trade-off?
- Since TabPFN is more causally aligned than linear ICL in the majority of grid settings (49/64) and only more vulnerable in a specific high-spurious corner, what architectural or training-data properties of TabPFN's prior-fitting procedure determine which regime a given deployment falls into?
- Could the CSR diagnostic itself be turned into a self-supervised training/finetuning signal (as the authors speculate in Future Work) by parameterizing Ĉ, Ŝ as a learned transformation of X and directly minimizing CSR, and would this outperform the purely context-level S-swap intervention?
