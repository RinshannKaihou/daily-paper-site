Minimizing Targeted Activations: Input-Only Suppression of Evaluation-Awareness Latents in Large Language Models ↗

Deepanshu Mody, Samarth Agarwal, Utkarsh Mittal, Dipesh Tharu Mahato · NYU Center for Data Science · 2026-07-28 · evaluation-awareness activation-steering sparse-autoencoders AI-safety-evaluation
Key metric — behavioral wrap test
1.40 → 1.81
↑ eval log-odds rose (wrong direction) despite Δf = 0.98 (98% activation drop)

On a real held-out evaluation passage, optimizing only a free prefix suppressed the CAA direction almost completely — yet the model's own "is this a test?" judgment moved toward "yes."

Why this matters

AI safety evals assume models can't tell they're being tested. This paper shows suppressing that internal signal doesn't guarantee the behavior it's supposed to explain actually changes.

Critical edge

A placebo built from a randomly relabeled direction suppresses just as hard (|z|=10.6) and shifts behavior almost as far (0.51) as the real evaluation-awareness direction (|z|=8.3, 0.44) — the effect may not be eval-specific at all.

Methodology

direction (CAA)→ k-dim subspace→ SAE feature→ single neuron→ behavioral logit
  • Target: a scalar read-out f(t) of the residual stream at a chosen layer, defined five different ways (above) — a locality axis from a single linear direction down to the actual behavioral yes/no logit.
  • Optimizer: GCG-style discrete token search over fixed-length (n=12) prompts, maximizing Score = -f(t) + λ_xe(-XE) + λ_kl(-KL) — the negated-feature dual of Fluent Dreaming/EPO. Swept λ_xe ∈ {0, 0.5, 1, 2} to trace a suppression-vs-fluency frontier.
  • Genuinely input-only: a special-token mask blocks chat-template/context-reset tokens; model internals are used only to compute gradients and read probes — the deployed artifact is a plain token string.
  • Validation battery: fresh linear probe (erasure check), an orthogonal-complement "probe-hacking" detector (validated with a positive-control planted rotation), placebo-direction and in-context wrap specificity tests, causal ablation for the SAE/neuron targets, and a real-Pile natural-text baseline.

▲ Strengths

  • Headline finding is an honest negative result — "activation-readability is not behavioral controllability," stated outright rather than downplayed.
  • Unusually thorough control battery: placebo directions, in-context wrap test, probe-hacking detector with its own positive control, model-side steering as a ceiling comparison, and a real-Pile baseline.
  • Spans a genuine locality axis (direction → subspace → SAE → neuron → behavioral logit) across two model scales plus a cross-size transfer check.
  • Causal validation performed before using the SAE/neuron targets, rather than assuming correlational salience implies relevance.
  • Ethically conscious: flags the dual-use risk (hiding eval-awareness from auditors) and declines to release optimized adversarial prompts.

▲ Limitations

  • The placebo non-specificity control is run only for the linear CAA direction at 3B/layer 14 — not for the subspace, neuron, logit, or SAE targets.
  • Strict fluent suppression (self-XE<6) landed in only 1 of 4 seeds; a matched-compute restart schedule didn't improve on that.
  • The formality/evaluation-status register confound is reduced (p_eval 0.78→0.46) but not eliminated by the balanced contrast set.
  • Erasure vs. mere rotation is "bounded, not resolved" — the probe-hacking detector's silence is suggestive, not decisive, since optimized prompts are off-manifold.
  • Single model family (Llama 3.1/3.2, 1B–8B) and only synthetic contrast sets — the real, canary-gated SAD benchmark is deliberately left untested.

Questions for further exploration

  1. Does the placebo non-specificity result hold for the neuron, logit, and SAE targets, or is the CAA direction uniquely non-specific because it's a single, low-rank linear read-out?
  2. Does suppression transfer to the real, canary-gated Situational Awareness Dataset the authors deliberately excluded?
  3. Why does the base behavioral logit flip sign between 3B (+3.19) and 8B (−0.25) — is baseline evaluation-awareness non-monotonic with scale?
  4. Would jointly optimizing over multiple target latents at once finally produce a suppression that tracks behavior, or does the same rotation/erasure ambiguity persist?
  5. Since a natural Pile-corpus scan already beats the learned optimizer on the internal direction target, how much of the "optimization is necessary" claim rests on the behavioral logit target alone?
