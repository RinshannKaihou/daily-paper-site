> **一句话总结：** 论文提出因果视觉记忆审计框架CVMA，用配对干预（区域剔除、整图剔除、图文2×2消融）在VisDial（2,064段十轮对话）和ConvBench（546段三轮对话）上证明：Qwen2.5-VL-7B和Idefics3-8B的当前注意力与未来区域效用的Spearman相关系数为负（Qwen在4×4粒度为-0.103，Idefics在2×2粒度达-0.576），联合保留还不如随机保留（4×4粒度下比随机差+0.053~+0.062 NLL），而全量数据集上PrefixKV把平均损伤压到+0.008 nats，明显优于静态Top-k的+0.159 nats，但视觉依赖度Q4分位上静态检索仍代价+0.417~+0.676 nats；此外已说出的视觉事实可通过文本KV被"救回"（免图文本记忆效用提升+3.874，图像KV后续价值下降+2.215），而未说出事实的隐藏痕迹并不能被可靠解码（交互项-0.177）。

> **TL;DR:** The paper introduces CVMA (Causal Visual Memory Audit), a paired-intervention framework testing whether attention-based visual-KV eviction in stateful multimodal dialog is actually safe. On VisDial (2,064 ten-turn dialogs) and ConvBench (546 three-turn dialogs) with Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct and Idefics3-8B-Llama3, current-turn attention is *negatively* correlated with future regional utility (Spearman -0.103 for Qwen at 4×4 grid, -0.576 for Idefics at 2×2), and attention-based joint retention loses to seeded-random retention by +0.053 to +0.062 NLL at 25-75% budgets — while a same-dialog marginal-utility "oracle" beats random by -0.110 to -0.197 NLL, showing headroom exists but current methods (LOOK-M, SnapKV, NACL, PrefixKV, static top-k) don't capture it. Full-dataset results show PrefixKV holds damage to +0.008 nats on VisDial versus static top-k's +0.159 nats at matched 4-bit budget, but even PrefixKV costs +0.417 nats (static: +0.676) on the highest visual-dependence quartile. A separate fact-substitution experiment shows verbalized facts become recoverable from assistant-text KV alone (+3.874 margin gain, image-KV value drops by +2.215) while unverbalized "seen but unsaid" facts leave no reliable trace (interaction -0.177, CI crosses toward zero).

## 核心贡献

- 提出CVMA（因果视觉记忆审计）：一套针对单次预填充、有状态多轮对话的因果干预框架，包含区域级"未来效用"删除、整图"视觉依赖度"删除、种子随机/边际效用("oracle")对照，以及图像KV×助手文本KV的2×2析因干预，用教师强制(teacher-forcing)保证每对干预下未来轨迹完全一致，从而把"低注意力=可安全遗忘"这一隐含假设变成可检验的因果命题。
- 证明当前注意力是"未来效用"的错误代理：在Qwen2.5-VL-7B上，4×4粒度区域注意力与未来效用的Spearman相关为-0.103（95% CI [-0.147, -0.048]），在Idefics3-8B上2×2/4×4/8×8三个粒度分别为-0.576/-0.263/-0.113，且联合保留策略在多个保留预算下都劣于随机保留，同时同对话边际效用对照证明可选择的信号确实存在（比随机好-0.110~-0.197 NLL），说明失败源于代理错位而非问题本身不可解。
- 揭示"视觉依赖度"是被删除信息是否造成伤害的关键调节变量：全部2,064段VisDial对话上，整图剔除的平均依赖度为+0.322 nats，但25.3%的对话依赖度为负；十个"选择器×预算"组合的"损伤-依赖度"Spearman相关均为正（0.399-0.682），且该效应在依赖度最高的四分位(Q4)与最低四分位(Q1)之间的反差极大（如静态25%保留在Q1改善而在Q4代价+0.417 nats）。
- 定位"言语化"是图像记忆与文本记忆之间可靠的替代边界：受控事实实验（80张图像confirmation组，1,920/1,920个析因单元全部审计）显示，一旦某视觉事实被助手说出，即使去掉图像KV，仅凭文本也能让"正确-干扰项"判别边际提升+3.874，且使图像KV此后的边际价值下降+2.215（[1.853, 2.579]，79/80张图像方向一致）；但对于被看到却未被说出的事实，其在文本侧的隐藏痕迹交互项为-0.177（[-0.321, -0.035]），说明替代仅发生在事实被明确言语化之后，而非仅因图像曾被看到。
- 得出对压缩系统设计与评测的直接建议：评测应同时报告"整图剔除依赖度"分层结果和"未言语化事实"失败率，而不能只看聚合平均分（聚合分数会被"本来就不依赖视觉的对话"稀释甚至掩盖高依赖度对话上的严重损伤）；压缩算法设计者不应把低当前注意力当作可以不可逆删除的充分证据。

## Key Contributions

- **CVMA: a reusable causal-audit framework for visual KV eviction.** CVMA formalizes safe-forgetting as four paired estimands computed on one frozen, teacher-forced dialog trajectory: regional future utility $U_{d,r}$ (single-region ablation), visual dependence $D_d$ (whole-image ablation), proxy regret $R^{p-c}_{d,q}$ against seeded-random and marginal-utility "oracle" retention, and an image×assistant-text 2×2 factorial interaction $I_{d,b}$. Runtime assertions enforce token-exact extension, immutable visual indices, and complete-source masking so that every comparison is causally clean rather than confounded by re-encoding artifacts.
- **Current attention is not a valid proxy for future utility.** On 200 frozen Qwen2.5-VL-7B dialogs, attention-future-utility Spearman correlation is -0.103 [-0.147, -0.048] at 4×4 grid (still negative at 8×8: -0.034 [-0.058, -0.009]); on Idefics3-8B it is -0.576/-0.263/-0.113 at 2×2/4×4/8×8, all CIs excluding zero. Joint retention at 25/50/75% budgets loses to seeded random by +0.053/+0.062/+0.027 NLL (4×4, Qwen), while a same-dialog marginal-utility control beats random by -0.197/-0.166/-0.110 — demonstrating exploitable signal exists that attention fails to capture. The failure is spatially diagnostic: top attention concentrates at fixed cache endpoints (152/200 dialogs for Qwen, 190/200 for Idefics) whose removal actually *improves* future NLL, and image-flip controls confirm the top-1 position tracks cache position rather than content.
- **Damage from wrong eviction is conditional on visual dependence, which aggregate metrics hide.** Across all 2,064 VisDial dialogs, mean whole-image dependence is +0.322 nats but 25.3% of dialogs have negative dependence (i.e., benefit from image removal via denoising). All ten selector-budget combinations show positive damage-dependence association (Spearman 0.399-0.682). Quartile analysis shows the mean masks a bimodal reality: in the lowest-dependence quartile (Q1, $D_d=-0.222$), LOOK-M/PrefixKV *improve* NLL by removing distractors, while in the highest quartile (Q4, $D_d=+0.971$), static retention costs +0.417 (25%) / +0.676 (12.5%) nats and even exact-2-bit KV quantization costs +7.903 nats. The pattern replicates on full ConvBench (association 0.541-0.793) and across model families (Qwen Spearman 0.443, Idefics 0.344).
- **Verbalization, not mere visual exposure, defines when text KV can substitute for image KV.** A controlled two-fact (A/B) experiment with pseudoword controls and independent Qwen-32B/InternVL-9B judgment (1,920/1,920 exact-audited factorial cells on an 80-image confirmation set) shows that once a fact is stated by the assistant, text alone raises the correct-vs-foil margin by +3.874 with no image access, and reduces the subsequent marginal value of image KV by +2.215 [1.853, 2.579] (positive for 79/80 images). Critically, the "hidden carrier" hypothesis — that seeing but not saying a fact still leaves a decodable trace — fails: the interaction term is -0.177 [-0.321, -0.035], meaning unstated facts are not reliably recoverable from text KV. This boundary replicates on Idefics3-8B and on a prospective stock-generated-history test (960/960 cells; explicit-fact substitution +2.898 [2.248, 3.582] vs. near-zero for unmentioned facts).
- **A full-dataset policy ladder situates five real compressors and a quantization control relative to these failure modes.** Comparing static top-k, LOOK-M, PrefixKV, SnapKV, and NACL (all attention-guided) against non-deletion KIVI-style low-bit coverage, PrefixKV is consistently closest to the full-cache ceiling (+0.008 nats at 25%/4-bit on VisDial vs. static's +0.159), but every attention-guided selector reproduces the dependence-conditioned failure topology, and 4-bit coverage collapses catastrophically at 2 bits (+6.229 nats on VisDial, +4.974 on ConvBench) — showing persistent addressability is not sufficient once the underlying representation becomes undecodable.

## Methodology

CVMA treats a multi-turn dialog as **one persistent stateful trajectory**: the image is prefilled once at turn 1, and every subsequent utterance extends the same KV cache (no re-encoding). Answer negative log-likelihood (NLL) is computed via teacher forcing so that every paired comparison (with vs. without an intervention) evaluates the *identical* future token sequence — the outcome $L_{d,t}(Z)$ is mean cross-entropy over the reference answer's content tokens under intervention $Z$. Runtime assertions enforce token-exact extension, immutable visual indices, and complete-source masking; these identification assumptions (intervention consistency, no cross-branch interference, identical pre-intervention state, no intervention-induced change to future history) bound the causal claims to the declared trajectory rather than free generation.

**Models and data.** Main-text results use Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct (BF16); Idefics3-8B-Llama3 provides a second model family for the proxy and verbalization tests, and a Qwen-3B check appears in the appendix. VisDial v1.0 validation contributes 2,064 ten-turn dialogs; ConvBench contributes 547 three-turn long-form dialogs (546 usable after excluding one dialog with a documented 9,302-token degenerate reference). A held-out set of 200 dialogs (72 pilot IDs removed, remainder shuffled once with seed 42) is frozen for the causal regional audit before any outcome is computed, keeping the pilot/confirmation split disjoint from a later 22-image/80-image fact-relay experiment.

**Four linked interventions.** (1) *Regional future utility* $U_{d,r} = \bar{L}_d(-r) - \bar{L}_d(\text{full})$ measures whether removing one spatial region (at 2×2/4×4/8×8 grain) hurts future-turn loss, averaged over the future horizon $H=\{2,\dots,T_d\}$ after the turn-1 anchor. (2) *Visual dependence* $D_d = \bar{L}_d(-\text{image}) - \bar{L}_d(\text{full})$ removes the whole image span to ask whether the future needs vision at all. (3) *Proxy regret* $R^{p-c}_{d,q}$ compares joint retention under attention-guided ranking against seeded-random and a same-dialog marginal-utility ("oracle") ranking that orders regions by single-region $U_{d,r}$ — a headroom witness, not a deployable or jointly-optimal policy. (4) *Image-text substitution* $I_{d,b}$ is a 2×2 factorial that ablates image KV and assistant-output KV (jointly and separately) before the next answer, testing whether text KV has already absorbed a visual fact.

**Current attention** is computed with per-layer visual-mass normalization and averaging across heads/layers on the first-turn question only (Equation 1 in the paper), then pooled per spatial region — matching the standard eviction-time scoring used by real compressors, with no future token entering the ranking.

**Compression policy comparison.** Five real selectors are transplanted into one common Qwen2.5-VL stateful runtime at matched visual budgets: static first-turn top-k, LOOK-M (pivotal merging), PrefixKV (layer-adaptive retention), SnapKV (observation-window pooling), and NACL (proxy + randomized retention) — compared against a non-deletion KIVI-style groupwise affine quantization control (with optional Walsh-Hadamard rotation) at INT8/4/3/2+WHT/1+WHT, where "accounted bits" include scale/zero-point metadata. VisionZip (which removes visual embeddings pre-prefill) is compared only against its own paired in-stack ceiling since it cannot share the stateful runtime.

**Fact-substitution experiment.** Controlled equal-length dialog histories are built where an image contains two facts A and B; the assistant states one and leaves the other unstated. Facts are validated by independent Qwen-32B/InternVL-9B judgment plus outcome-blind review; 160 pseudowords serve as non-visual matched controls. An S×W×R design varies whether the target fact is stated, whether the answering pass can see the image, and whether the later probe can, with every branch recomputed under its own source intervention. A second, prospective test stock-generates one 7B-model description per frozen image (rather than hand-crafting histories) to check whether the boundary survives naturally occurring text.

## Results & Highlights

- **Attention-future-utility correlation is negative across grains and both model families.** Qwen2.5-VL-7B regional Spearman: 2×2 = -0.100 (inconclusive), 4×4 = **-0.103** [-0.147, -0.048], 8×8 = -0.034 [-0.058, -0.009]. Idefics3-8B: 2×2 = **-0.576**, 4×4 = -0.263, 8×8 = -0.113 (all CIs exclude zero). This holds under four different attention-aggregation rules (all-layer mean, last-4 mean, last-layer, mass-weighted), with correlations staying in [-0.111, -0.084].
- **Attention-guided joint retention is worse than seeded-random retention, but a marginal-utility oracle shows recoverable headroom.** At the primary 4×4 grid and 25/50/75% retention budgets, attention loses to random by **+0.053/+0.062/+0.027 NLL** (Qwen); at 8×8 the gaps are +0.092/+0.055/+0.016. The same-dialog marginal-utility control beats random by **-0.197/-0.166/-0.110 NLL** at 4×4 — evidence that the future *is* selectable in principle, but current attention-based selectors are not capturing it.
- **Full-dataset policy ladder (Table 1, Qwen-7B, teacher-forced ΔNLL vs. full BF16 cache).** On VisDial (2,064 dialogs) at matched 4-bit/25% budget: static top-k **+0.159**, LOOK-M **+0.021**, PrefixKV **+0.008** (best selector), SnapKV **+0.082**, NACL **+0.043**; non-deletion coverage INT4 **+0.001**, but INT2+WHT (2-bit) collapses to **+6.229**. On ConvBench (546 dialogs), the same ladder is +0.030/+0.018/+0.011/+0.029/+0.018, and INT1+WHT collapses to +4.974 — replicating the representation-floor failure on a second benchmark.
- **Damage concentrates on visually-dependent dialogs, hidden by aggregate averaging.** Mean whole-image dependence on VisDial is +0.322 nats (25.3% of dialogs negative). All ten selector-budget cells show positive damage-dependence Spearman (**0.399-0.682**). In the lowest-dependence quartile (Q1, $D_d=-0.222$), LOOK-M/PrefixKV *improve* NLL (denoising effect); in the highest quartile (Q4, $D_d=+0.971$), static retention costs **+0.417 (25%) / +0.676 (12.5%)** nats while exact-4-bit coverage costs only +0.004 (CI crosses zero) and exact-2-bit coverage costs **+7.903** even without any deletion.
- **Turn-wise damage grows with dialog length under fixed selection.** On full VisDial, static 12.5% retention damage rises from +0.008 nats at turn 1 to **+0.289** at turn 10; all four attention-guided selectors (LOOK-M, PrefixKV, SnapKV, NACL) show larger later-turn than anchor-turn damage at both 4-bit and 2-bit matched budgets, while viable 4-bit coverage stays near zero throughout.
- **Free-running exact-match confirms the NLL findings translate to observable errors.** On the full high-dependence quartile (516 dialogs, 4,644 later-turn cells generated end-to-end), strict exact match on turns 2-10 is **53.1%** for full cache and **53.3%** for 4-bit coverage, versus **46.0-51.4%** for the five attention-guided selectors — every selector gap has a 95% CI below zero, from -7.08 points [-7.97, -6.22] (static) to -1.68 points [-2.33, -1.01] (PrefixKV, the best-performing selector). Official-formula dense NDCG on all 2,064 VisDial dialogs: full cache 0.557, static top-k 0.534 (25%) / 0.515 (12.5%), INT4/INT2+WHT coverage 0.557/0.554 — coverage beats matched static retention by +0.0233 [0.0184, 0.0282] and +0.0390 [0.0327, 0.0455].
- **Verbalized facts are recoverable from text KV alone; unverbalized "seen but unsaid" facts are not.** In the controlled 80-image confirmation set (1,920/1,920 audited factorial cells), stating a fact raises the no-image correct-vs-foil margin by **+3.874** and reduces the subsequent value of image KV by **+2.215** [1.853, 2.579] (79/80 images positive). The hidden-carrier interaction — whether seeing-but-not-saying leaves a decodable trace — is **-0.177** [-0.321, -0.035], i.e., no reliable substitution for unstated facts. This replicates on a prospective, naturally stock-generated history test (960/960 cells): explicit-fact substitution **+2.898** [2.248, 3.582] vs. near-zero for unmentioned facts, and on Idefics3-8B (explicit substitution +3.801 vs. absent +0.258).

No section of the paper reports "no quantitative results" — the above are drawn directly from the paper's tables and figures (Table 1, Figures 2-5, and the text of Sections 3-5).

## Authors & Credibility

The paper comes from The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou) and Wenzhou-Kean University, with Hong Chen and Kang Chen as co-first authors and Xuming Hu as corresponding author. The reference list shows this group (Chen, Hu, and collaborators) has several concurrent 2026 arXiv submissions specifically on KV-cache compression for multimodal LLMs — NestedKV, SONIC, and "Last But Not Least: Boundary Attention Calibration" all self-cited as related/contemporaneous work. This signals a lab actively working the exact niche it audits here, which is a reasonable basis for domain expertise but also means several of the "prior work" citations are the authors' own recent preprints rather than independently vetted baselines; readers should note this concentration when weighing the framing of "the field's" assumptions. The paper explicitly positions itself as a methodological/diagnostic contribution ("not a new compression policy"), which is a credible and modest framing choice rather than overclaiming a new SOTA compressor.

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty:** The core idea — separating "does attention predict future utility," "does the future even need vision," and "has the fact already migrated to text" into three causally distinct, jointly testable interventions on one frozen teacher-forced trajectory — is a genuinely useful reframing of an evaluation problem that prior work (LOOK-M, SnapKV, PrefixKV, NACL, VL-Cache, AirCache, etc.) has largely sidestepped by reporting only aggregate downstream scores. The factorial image×text KV substitution test in particular (Section 5) is, to this reviewer's knowledge, a new empirical instrument not present in the cited visual-to-text migration work (Lin et al. 2025) or explicit verbalized-memory work (Chatterjee et al. 2025), which study a forward pass or hand-built memory rather than naturally occurring cross-turn KV substitution.

**Rigor:** The paper is unusually careful about causal identification for an empirical KV-cache paper: teacher forcing to freeze future trajectories, runtime assertions for token-exact extension and index immutability, disjoint pilot/confirmation splits (72 IDs never pooled with the frozen 200; the 22-image pilot never pooled with the 80-image confirmation), bootstrap/hierarchical 95% CIs on nearly every reported number, and four separate robustness checks on the attention-aggregation rule. The explicit "identification assumptions and invariants" section is a genuine strength relative to typical KV-cache-compression papers.

**A concrete red flag / paper-specific weak spot:** The Idefics3-8B whole-image dependence result is inconvenient and the paper's handling of it is somewhat hand-wavy — dropping the *entire image* actually *improves* mean NLL for Idefics ("Dropping the whole image nevertheless improves Idefics mean NLL"), which is the opposite sign from Qwen/VisDial's +0.322 nats mean dependence. The authors label this a "model-task interference boundary" and say they "replicate the proxy-failure topology, not the sign of aggregate dependence" — this is a reasonable but noticeably softened claim, and it means the cross-model replication is weaker than the headline "two model families" framing implies: the *direction* of one of the paper's two central findings (visual dependence exists and matters) does not actually replicate on the second model family.

**Missing ablation / scope limitation the paper itself flags:** The full policy ladder (Table 1, Figure 3, the 15-case sealed audit, the 516-dialog free-running exact-match test) is Qwen-only; Idefics is used only for the proxy-validity and verbalization tests, not the compressor comparison. The conclusion explicitly states this bound ("one non-Qwen model, a Qwen-only policy ladder"), which is honest, but it does limit how much the full "eviction fails where and when vision matters" story (Section 4) can be said to generalize beyond Qwen2.5-VL.

**Baselines:** The five compared selectors (static top-k, LOOK-M, PrefixKV, SnapKV, NACL) are real, published, contemporary methods rather than strawmen, which is appropriate for a diagnostic paper — the point is to show that *even the best of them* (PrefixKV) reproduces the dependence-conditioned failure, not to claim SOTA. Static top-k is included and is (predictably) the weakest, but it is clearly labeled as a naive reference point rather than presented as the main competitor.

**Reproducibility:** The paper describes an "executable audit manifest" in the appendix and gives exact seeds (Python seed 42 for the shuffle), exact dialog counts, and exact intervention definitions — a good sign for reproducibility in principle, though no public code repository link is visible in the main text provided, so actual runnable release cannot be confirmed from this reading.

**Significance:** If the central claims hold up under independent replication, they have real practical consequence for anyone deploying stateful multimodal assistants with irreversible KV eviction: the paper argues persuasively that "low current attention" is an insufficient safety criterion, and that evaluators should report dependence-stratified and unstated-fact metrics rather than a single aggregate score. This is a meaningful, actionable recommendation rather than a purely academic distinction.

## Strengths

- Clean causal framing with explicit identification assumptions, paired teacher-forced interventions, and disjoint pilot/confirmation data splits — unusually rigorous for this subfield.
- Multiple, mutually reinforcing lines of evidence (regional ablation, whole-image ablation, marginal-utility oracle, spatial hotspot/flip diagnostics, factorial text substitution) all pointing to the same mechanism, rather than a single metric.
- Concrete, quantified failure modes (negative Spearman correlations, dependence-quartile damage gaps, exact-match point losses) rather than only qualitative claims.
- Honest scoping: the paper explicitly disclaims being a new compression method and explicitly states the bounds of its evidence (model families tested, Qwen-only policy ladder) in the conclusion.
- The verbalization-boundary experiment (Section 5) is a creative, well-controlled test (pseudoword controls, independent judge models, prospective stock-generated-history replication) that isolates a genuinely new empirical distinction between "seen" and "said."

## Limitations

- Evidence rests on exactly two model families (Qwen2.5-VL-7B/3B and Idefics3-8B), and the full compressor policy ladder is evaluated only on Qwen — the paper itself flags this bound.
- The sign of aggregate visual dependence does not replicate across model families (positive mean for Qwen on VisDial, negative mean NLL effect for Idefics), weakening the strength of the "visual dependence" finding as a cross-architecture universal, even though the *ordinal* damage-dependence relationship does replicate.
- VisDial's always-visible caption is noted by the authors as preventing "a clean no-relay control" for the verbalization experiments, meaning the VisDial-based substitution results may be partly caption-mediated rather than purely dialog-driven (mitigated by, but not fully resolved via, the separate controlled fact and stock-generated-history experiments).
- Teacher-forced NLL is the primary metric throughout; while the free-running exact-match and sealed hand-audit sections partially validate that NLL differences correspond to visible errors, the bulk of quantitative claims (Table 1, Figures 2-5) remain in the NLL/likelihood space rather than downstream task metrics like standard VQA accuracy.
- No new eviction policy is proposed — practitioners get a diagnostic lens and a recommendation ("condition on dependence and unstated facts") but not a drop-in fix for the failure modes identified.

## Relevance

这篇论文对研究多模态大模型KV缓存压缩、长程/多轮对话记忆管理或视觉token淘汰策略的读者高度相关，其因果审计方法论（区域/整图/图文析因干预）也可迁移到评估其他"基于当前重要性做不可逆压缩"的系统设计中。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would a retention policy that explicitly conditions on a cheaply estimated proxy for future visual dependence (rather than only current attention) close the gap to the marginal-utility oracle shown in Figure 2b, and could such a proxy be learned online during the dialog rather than requiring the ground-truth future turns CVMA uses for its diagnostic?
- Why does whole-image removal improve mean NLL for Idefics3-8B but hurt it for Qwen2.5-VL on VisDial — is this a caption-reliance artifact, a training-data difference, or an architectural difference in how visual and text KV interact, and would resolving it change which model family should be trusted for the dependence-based recommendations?
- Since verbalized facts become recoverable from text KV while unstated ones do not, could a compressor exploit this directly — e.g., deferring visual eviction only until a region's content has demonstrably been echoed in assistant output — and how much of the current damage (Table 1) would such a "verbalization-gated" policy actually recover in practice?
- How does the fact-specific verbalization boundary interact with hallucination — if an assistant states a fact incorrectly, does the "false" text KV still substitute for the (now evicted) true image KV, potentially locking in an error the way the Section 4 "hallucinated absence" failure mode suggests?
