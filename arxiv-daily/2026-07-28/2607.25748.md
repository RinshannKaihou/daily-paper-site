> **一句话总结：** 在EchoNet-Dynamic超声心动图数据集上，作者证明用射血分数（EF）目标训练的容积概念瓶颈层（预测左室收缩末/舒张末容积）虽然EF误差不输直接回归（MAE 6.89 vs 7.13），但由于EF是两个容积的比值、对整体缩放不变，概念层学到的容积预测标准差会坍缩到0.1 mL（参考值35.7/45.7 mL），必须额外用毫升单位监督容积本身才能恢复物理尺度（EDV误差从89.8降到25.8 mL），代价是EF误差上升0.4。

> **TL;DR:** Using the EchoNet-Dynamic dataset, the authors show a volume concept bottleneck model (CBM) trained only on an ejection-fraction (EF) objective matches direct regression on EF accuracy (MAE 6.891 vs. 7.129, n=1,276) yet its predicted end-systolic/end-diastolic volumes collapse to a standard deviation of 0.1 mL against reference spreads of 35.7 and 45.7 mL — because EF is a ratio and is invariant to jointly rescaling both volumes, so the loss cannot pin down physical scale (formalized as Proposition 1). Adding direct millilitre-level volume supervision restores grounding (end-diastolic volume MAE drops from 89.8 to 25.8 mL, predicted SD rises to 19.6-22.5 mL) at a cost of +0.4 EF MAE (6.891 → 7.295), and the paper shows the failure is invisible to concept-accuracy metrics reported in normalized units.

## 核心贡献

- 提出并形式化一个命题（Proposition 1）：若下游输出函数g对概念空间上的某个群G不变（g(τ(c))=g(c)），那么训练损失只能把概念层确定到G-轨道（G-orbit），而非唯一值；EF正是在"整体缩放群"G={c↦λc}下不变的比值目标（Corollary 1），因此单靠EF损失无法约束容积概念层的物理单位。
- 用真实实验证明该理论预测：EF-only训练下容积预测标准差坍缩到0.1 mL（参考35.7/45.7 mL），EDV相关系数变为-0.219（在如此小的预测方差下被作者判定为数值不稳定、不代表真正负相关），而ESV相关系数仍为正（0.626）——说明概念层保留了部分排序信息但几乎丢失了物理尺度。
- 报告了作者自己踩过的一个"退化正则化"设计失败：用EF导出的容积比ρ=VES/VED做正则项，代数上等价于EF损失本身（同一梯度的常数倍），因此在归一化单位下概念误差看起来很低、却从未真正约束绝对尺度——这一失败在传统"概念准确率"评估下不可见。
- 验证了补救方案（用毫升为单位直接监督两个容积）：EDV误差从89.79降到25.83 mL（约2/3），预测标准差从0.1恢复到19.6-22.5 mL，但EF误差上升0.404（6.891→7.295）；同时报告了该监督引入的优化不稳定性（3个随机种子中2个在lr=1e-4下发散）及简单补救（减半学习率）。
- 提出一个"尺度假说"解释为何即使加了监督，容积预测方差仍只恢复到参考值的一半左右：不同检查的像素间距（0.011-0.061 cm/px）因预处理统一缩放到固定网格而被抹除，物理尺度信息在输入端就已丢失。

## Key Contributions

- Formalizes Proposition 1: if the output map g is invariant under a group G acting on concept space, the training loss determines the concept layer only up to a G-orbit — and derives Corollary 1 that ejection fraction (EF), being a ratio of end-diastolic and end-systolic volume, is invariant under the scale group G={c↦λc: λ>0}, so an EF-only objective cannot pin down absolute volume units.
- Empirically confirms the prediction on EchoNet-Dynamic: an EF-only-trained concept bottleneck's predicted volume standard deviation collapses to 0.1 mL (reference SDs 35.7 mL ESV, 45.7 mL EDV), while EF accuracy itself is not harmed relative to direct regression (6.891 vs. 7.129 MAE).
- Documents a degenerate regularizer the authors themselves designed and diagnose why it failed: a loss term on the predicted ESV/EDV ratio is algebraically identical to the EF loss (same gradient up to a constant), so it looked like additional supervision but supplied no new constraint — and this was invisible under normalized-unit concept-accuracy evaluation.
- Verifies the prescribed fix (absolute-unit volume supervision, weight µ∈{3,10}) restores scale (EDV MAE 89.79→25.83 mL, predicted SD 0.1→19.6-22.5 mL) at a quantified cost of +0.404 EF MAE, and reports/resolves an optimization instability (2 of 3 seeds diverged at lr=1e-4; halving the learning rate to 5e-5 removed it).
- Offers a testable "scale hypothesis" for why grounding restores volume spread only to about half the reference spread: per-study pixel spacing varies 0.011-0.061 cm/px (32% attributable to stored-resolution differences, 15% CV to acquisition depth) but is destroyed by fixed-grid input resizing before the encoder ever sees it.

## Methodology

The backbone is a VideoMAE video transformer encoder (112×112 frames, 16×16 patches, tubelet depth 2) pretrained with masked autoencoding (200 epochs, 60% tubelet masking) on EchoNet-Dynamic, then fine-tuned for EF regression (50 epochs, AdamW, lr=1e-4); the epoch-43 checkpoint (selected by validation loss) initializes every condition compared, so all comparisons differ only in objective, not initialization. Each study contributes one 32-frame clip (stride 2, spanning 62 source frames) chosen because it reliably covers a full cardiac beat.

The concept bottleneck routes prediction entirely through two clinically defined quantities — end-systolic volume (ESV) and end-diastolic volume (EDV) — with EF computed analytically as EF = 100·(EDV−ESV)/EDV and **no residual path** from encoder to output, so any information the concept layer fails to carry is categorically unavailable downstream. A shared per-token head (LayerNorm → Linear 768→384 → GELU → Linear 384→1 → softplus) maps each of T=16 temporal tokens to a scalar volume; EDV = max over tokens, ESV = min over tokens, so the model itself selects the cardiac phase rather than being given annotated frames (verified separately: EDV head's max falls in the first 5 tokens in 97.5% of test studies, ESV head's min in the last 11 tokens in 99%, and max precedes min in 97.7% — ruling out temporal misalignment as an explanation for scale failures).

Training objective: L = L_EF + µ·L_vol, where L_EF is L1 error on EF and L_vol is L1 error on both volumes in millilitres; µ=0 gives the "EF only" condition, µ∈{3,10} give "grounded" conditions, and a "normalized" control rescales L_vol by the batch mean volume (preserving direction information but not absolute magnitude in a naive sense) rather than genuinely constraining scale. An earlier abandoned design regularized toward the predicted ESV/EDV ratio directly; the paper shows algebraically that this is exactly equal to the EF loss (up to a constant), illustrating Remark 1 that a G-invariant regularizer cannot narrow the orbit.

## Results & Highlights

- **EF accuracy is not hurt by the bottleneck (Table I, n=1,276):** CBM (EF only) achieves 6.891 MAE [95% CI 6.502, 7.269], r=0.623, versus direct regression's 7.129 MAE [6.735, 7.531], r=0.581 — CIs overlap so no superiority is claimed, but the bottleneck is not worse. A supervised R(2+1)D-18 reference model (included as an upper bound, not a fair comparison target) reaches 4.814 MAE, r=0.848; a constant predictor gives 9.097 MAE as the lower reference.
- **Concept layer loses physical scale without direct supervision (Table II):** CBM (EF only) predicted-volume SD collapses to 0.1 mL for both ESV and EDV against reference SDs of 35.7 mL (ESV) and 45.7 mL (EDV) — i.e., under 1% of the true spread. Across all 1,276 test studies, ESV predictions span only 0.6-1.4 mL and EDV predictions only 1.6-2.0 mL (Fig. 1). ESV MAE is 42.96 mL and EDV MAE is 89.79 mL in this condition — both far worse than the grounded conditions below, despite EF itself being predicted accurately.
- **Grounding restores scale at a quantified EF-accuracy cost:** with µ=3 volume supervision, ESV MAE drops from 42.96 to 16.03 mL and EDV MAE from 89.79 to 25.83 mL (roughly two-thirds reduction), while predicted SD rises from 0.1 to 19.6 mL (ESV) and 22.5 mL (EDV); the trade-off is an EF MAE increase from 6.891 to 7.295 (delta +0.404), with CI [6.906, 7.702] now no longer overlapping the constant-predictor floor by much less margin than the ungrounded model.
- **Increasing grounding weight buys no extra concept validity:** µ=10 gives ESV MAE 16.60 / EDV MAE 26.50 (statistically indistinguishable from µ=3's 16.03/25.83), while EF MAE rises further to 7.698 — and the paper explicitly notes the µ=3-vs-µ=10 EF gap (~0.26) is only about twice the observed seed-to-seed variation (0.13), so no monotone dose-response is claimed.
- **Compression is not resolved by more supervision:** even with grounding, predicted SD plateaus at 22.5 mL versus a 45.7 mL reference (EDV) — essentially identical across all three grounded conditions (µ=3, µ=10, normalized) — leading the authors to a separate pixel-spacing hypothesis (cm/px varies 0.011-0.061 between studies, partly explained by 32% stored-resolution differences and 15% CV from acquisition depth) rather than a loss-weighting fix.

Hyunkyung Han and Min Jung Kim, both in the Department of Integrative Medicine, Yonsei University College of Medicine (Seoul), with Kim also affiliated with the Department of Radiology. This is a two-author, single-institution clinical-ML paper (IEEE JBHI submission) that explicitly builds on the authors' own prior work (an under-review companion paper on attribution faithfulness in the same EchoNet-Dynamic EF-estimation setting, using the same VideoMAE encoder) — the encoder is reused, not newly validated here, and the research question is explicitly stated as distinct from that companion work. The paper includes a generative-AI disclosure stating an LLM was used only for language editing and code-debugging assistance, not for generating claims or references, with all citations and numerical results manually verified.

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty:** The core theoretical device (Proposition 1, an invariance/orbit argument) is explicitly and honestly presented as "elementary" mathematics belonging to the same family as disentanglement identifiability results (Locatello et al. 2019) — the authors' claim to novelty is narrower and more defensible: that this argument has not previously been used to constrain *supervision design* in concept bottleneck models, where validity is conventionally judged by concept-prediction accuracy. This is a reasonable, well-scoped contribution rather than an overclaimed theoretical breakthrough.

**Rigor:** A genuine strength is the paper's transparency about its own failed design — the degenerate ratio-regularizer (Section II-C-1) is disclosed as the authors' own earlier mistake, algebraically shown to be redundant with the EF loss, and explicitly framed as a cautionary example that would have passed a normalized-unit concept-accuracy check. The temporal-alignment verification (97.5-99% correct phase selection) is a specific, checkable safeguard against a confound (temporal misalignment) that could otherwise explain the scale failure. However, the "constant predictor" (9.097 MAE) is a weak baseline for EF (a naive population-mean regressor), and the paper's own claimed CBM-vs-direct-regression "non-inferiority" rests on overlapping 95% CIs (6.891 vs. 7.129) rather than a formal equivalence test — the paper is appropriately careful in stating "we do not claim superiority," but a reader should note this is not a powered non-inferiority design either. The µ=3 vs µ=10 "dose-response" is explicitly flagged by the authors as within seed-to-seed noise, which is honest but also means the paper cannot really distinguish whether grounding weight matters at all beyond breaking the invariance.

**Reproducibility:** Code is promised "upon publication" but not yet released; the EchoNet-Dynamic dataset requires a Stanford AIMI data-use agreement, so exact reproduction depends on external gating. Only 2-3 random seeds per configuration were run (explicitly flagged as a limitation, sufficient only to bound — not characterize — the optimization landscape), and the optimization-instability finding (2/3 seeds diverging at lr=1e-4) is itself evidence that the grounded conditions are somewhat fragile to hyperparameters not fully swept here.

**Significance / red flags:** The generalization claim (Section V-D) that "any scale-invariant concept vocabulary — e.g., shape and margin descriptors" will exhibit the same failure is asserted by analogy, not tested empirically anywhere in the paper — this is the weakest link in an otherwise careful paper, since ratio-invariance and shape/margin-invariance are not obviously the same kind of invariance group. The pixel-spacing "scale hypothesis" for residual compression (Section V-B) is explicitly labeled by the authors as a hypothesis, using a separate, undescribed measurement set not otherwise detailed in the methods — this is honest hedging but leaves a real methodological gap (that measurement set's provenance and size are never specified in the text provided). Results come from a single dataset/institution (EchoNet-Dynamic), and cross-scanner/cross-protocol generalization is explicitly untested.

## Strengths

- Clean, falsifiable theoretical claim (invariance group determines the orbit the loss can constrain) paired directly with a real clinical variable (EF as a volume ratio) where the consequence is concrete and clinically meaningful (EDV is itself a diagnostic quantity for ventricular dilation, not just an intermediate computation).
- Unusually transparent reporting of a design failure (the degenerate ratio regularizer) that the authors themselves fell into and could not detect via standard concept-accuracy metrics — this is exactly the kind of failure mode the paper argues the field under-scrutinizes.
- Quantifies rather than merely asserts the accuracy/grounding trade-off (+0.404 EF MAE for restored scale) and separately identifies and fixes an optimization instability introduced by the fix, rather than leaving it unexplained.
- Careful ablation-adjacent controls: a temporal-alignment check (ruling out phase misalignment as a confound) and a "normalized" condition (control to show scaling the loss by a batch statistic is a different, and effective, intervention from the degenerate ratio regularizer).

## Limitations

- Single dataset (EchoNet-Dynamic), single institution; no cross-scanner or cross-protocol validation of either the accuracy results or the invariance argument's practical impact.
- Only 2-3 seeds per configuration — enough to bound the specific deltas claimed but explicitly stated as insufficient to characterize the optimization landscape (relevant given the observed lr=1e-4 divergence in 2/3 seeds).
- The residual volume-compression phenomenon (predicted SD reaching only ~half the reference spread even after grounding) is unexplained; the pixel-spacing account is offered as a hypothesis, not demonstrated as the cause, and is based on a separate measurement set not otherwise characterized in the paper.
- The paper's broader generalization claim (scale-invariant concept vocabularies like shape/margin descriptors will show the same failure) is not empirically tested anywhere in this work.
- Code is not yet released ("upon publication"), limiting immediate independent reproducibility.

## Relevance

这篇论文对做医学影像可解释性/concept bottleneck models的研究者很有参考价值：它提供了一个简洁但可操作的诊断工具（检查训练目标的不变性群），可以直接套用到任何"比值/归一化"临床指标（如EF、各类比率型生物标志物）上，提醒在报告"概念准确率"时必须同时检查绝对尺度而非仅相关系数。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Does the invariance-orbit diagnostic generalize empirically (not just by analogy) to other clinically meaningful ratio-valued targets — e.g., fractional shortening, valve regurgitant fraction, or radiological shape/margin descriptors mentioned in Section V-D?
- Could the residual volume-compression (SD reaching only ~half the reference spread even after grounding) be resolved by restoring physical pixel-spacing information to the input (e.g., conditioning on cm/px metadata) rather than relying on absolute-unit loss supervision alone, directly testing the paper's own "scale hypothesis"?
- How sensitive is the reported EF-accuracy cost of grounding (+0.404 MAE) to the choice of µ, given the paper found no monotone dose-response between µ=3 and µ=10 within seed noise — would a learned or annealed µ schedule recover EF accuracy while retaining scale?
- Would the optimization instability observed at lr=1e-4 (2/3 seeds diverging) reappear on other encoder architectures or datasets, or is it specific to the interaction between VideoMAE fine-tuning dynamics and the added volume loss term?
- The degenerate ratio-regularizer failure was only caught through manual algebraic inspection — what general-purpose diagnostic (e.g., automatically checking whether a regularizer's gradient is redundant with the main loss under the objective's invariance group) could catch this class of error before deployment?
