> **一句话总结：** 论文用一个已知的非平稳高斯随机场（32×32 网格，均值和 Matérn 相关范围参数均随空间变化）构建可控基准，系统评测 FM、DDPM、score-SDE、VAE 四类生成模型：四者都能较好恢复均值曲面，但协方差恢复差异悬殊——VAE 的白化 Bures 距离高达 35.25（oracle 仅 0.532，约 66 倍），且几乎完全丢失像素级 nugget 方差（τ̂²≈0，真值 0.3），而常用的 CRPS 指标（各模型均在 0.85 左右）完全无法揭示这一差距。

> **TL;DR:** The paper builds a controlled benchmark from a known non-stationary Gaussian random field (32×32 grid, spatially varying mean and Matérn range parameter β(s)) to test whether flow matching (FM), DDPM, score-SDE, and a vanilla VAE actually recover the ground-truth mean, covariance, and variance structure rather than merely matching summary statistics. All four models recover the mean surface, but covariance recovery differs sharply: DDPM and score-SDE track the oracle finite-sample floor closely on the whitened Bures-Wasserstein distance (0.582 and 0.555 vs. oracle 0.532), FM shows mild under-dispersion and attenuated non-stationarity (corr(β,β̂)=0.623 vs. oracle 0.811), and VAE fails badly (Bures_k=35.25, ~66x the oracle's 0.532) with its nugget-variance estimate collapsing to τ̂²=0.000 against the true 0.3. Crucially, the widely used CRPS metric (0.853-0.870 across all four models) fails to separate any of these differences, and the qualitative pattern largely replicates on real ERA5 temperature-anomaly fields from WeatherBench-2.

## 核心贡献

- 构建了一个已知数据生成过程（DGP）的非平稳高斯随机场（GRF）可控基准：均值曲面由多项式趋势+非多项式正弦项构成，协方差采用 Paciorek-Schervish 构造的非平稳 Matérn 核，局部相关范围参数 β(s) 从 (0,0) 处的 0.12 平滑增至 (1,1) 处的 0.50，外加像素级 nugget 噪声，专门用于检验深度生成模型（DGM）能否恢复"均值+非平稳依赖结构+方差成分"这三类特征。
- 提出一套针对性诊断指标：白化平方 Bures-Wasserstein 距离 Bures_k（考察主导相关结构）、总方差比 tot-var、局部窗口 Matérn 拟合得到的 σ̂²/τ̂²（方差成分分解）、非平稳性梯度 NS-grad 与 corr(β,β̂)（考察非平稳性强度及其空间位置是否被正确恢复），并证明这些指标比常用的 CRPS 更能区分模型间的实际差距。
- 在同一架构规模（FM/DDPM/score-SDE 共用 4.43M 参数 U-Net，VAE 用规模相近的 3.9M 参数编解码器，相差在 12% 以内）、同一训练步数、匹配的采样计算量（约 200 次网络前向）下，对 FM、DDPM、score-SDE、VAE 四大生成模型家族做公平横向比较，并识别出 FM 和 VAE 两种截然不同的"欠离散"（under-dispersion）失效机制：FM 主要是方差尺度上的整体偏移，VAE 则是结构性地丢失了 nugget 方差。
- 通过在 σ²、τ²、ν、训练集规模上做参数扫描以及 5 个随机种子重训练，验证结论的稳健性；并将该评测框架迁移到 ERA5 2 米温度异常场（WeatherBench-2, 32×32 网格, 1959-2009 训练/2015-2022 留出评估），展示在真实数据、真实 DGP 未知的情形下如何用留出参考集替代 oracle 完成同样的诊断。

## Key Contributions

- **A controlled non-stationary GRF benchmark with a known DGP.** The mean surface combines a polynomial spatial trend with a smooth sinusoidal term, and the covariance is a non-stationary Matérn kernel (Paciorek and Schervish 2003, 2006) whose local range parameter β(s) varies smoothly across the 32×32 domain, plus an independent nugget term — designed to be more realistic than the stationary GRFs used in prior known-process validation work (Warner et al. 2025; Walchessen et al. 2025; Cardoso and Pereira 2025).
- **A targeted, decomposed metric suite beyond CRPS.** The paper introduces Bures_k (whitened Bures-Wasserstein distance to the ground-truth covariance's leading eigenspace), tot-var (trace ratio), σ̂²/τ̂² (local-window covariance-parameter estimates), NS-grad, and corr(β,β̂) (degree and spatial placement of non-stationarity), showing these separate models that CRPS cannot.
- **A matched, four-family comparison identifying distinct failure modes.** FM, DDPM, score-SDE, and VAE are compared under matched parameter counts (4.43M vs. 3.9M, within 12%), identical optimizer steps, and matched sampling budgets (~200 network evaluations), revealing that diffusion-family models (DDPM, score-SDE) recover non-stationary dependence most faithfully, FM attenuates it with a small consistent variance-scale deficit, and VAE fails structurally by collapsing the nugget variance toward zero.
- **Robustness and memorization checks.** A four-parameter DGP sweep (σ²∈{0.5,1,2,4}, τ²∈{0.05,0.3,1}, ν∈{0.5,1.5,2.5}, n_train∈{2k,10k,50k}), 5-seed retraining, and a nearest-neighbour memorization check against training data are used to confirm the qualitative ranking is not an artifact of one configuration or of copied training samples.
- **A real-world extension without ground truth.** The framework is adapted to ERA5 temperature anomalies over the Northeast Pacific/western North America by substituting held-out empirical references and a fitted stationary control for the (unavailable) true DGP, showing the benchmark's qualitative findings largely persist on real climate data.

## Methodology

The ground-truth DGP is Y(s) = μ(s) + Z(s) + ε(s) on a 32×32 grid, where μ(s) is a deterministic polynomial-plus-sinusoidal mean trend, Z(·) is a zero-mean GRF with a non-stationary Matérn covariance C(s,s') built from Paciorek and Schervish's (2003, 2006) locally-varying range construction (β(s)=0.12+0.38(0.5s₁+0.5s₂)^1.5, σ²=2, ν=1.5), and ε(s) is i.i.d. N(0, τ²=0.3) pixel noise. The main configuration draws 50k training and 10k validation realizations from one seed; a robustness sweep varies σ², τ², ν, and training-set size one at a time.

Four DGM families are trained on identical data splits with coordinate-aware (non-translation-equivariant) architectures so they can, in principle, learn location-dependent dependence: FM uses the reference flow-matching library with a straight-path formulation and 100 integration steps (~200 network evaluations) at sampling time; DDPM uses a public v-prediction implementation with a cosine noise schedule and 200 deterministic DDIM reverse steps; score-SDE uses the official variance-exploding SDE implementation with 100 predictor-corrector steps; VAE uses a standard PyTorch-VAE implementation with a 128-dimensional latent and deterministic decoding via a single forward pass. FM, DDPM, and score-SDE share a 4.43M-parameter U-Net; the VAE's encoder-decoder is 3.9M parameters (within 12%). Hyperparameters were not jointly retuned across families — each model uses its standard reference implementation rather than a best-effort tuned configuration, a limitation the paper explicitly flags.

Two references anchor the evaluation: an oracle (independent draws from the true GRF, representing the best achievable finite-sample recovery) and a stationary control (the same GRF but with β(s) fixed to a constant φ=0.25, removing non-stationary dependence). Evaluation uses n=2000 generated samples per model and bootstrap confidence intervals (B=2000 field bootstraps). Mean-surface recovery is measured via pixelwise MSEμ between the ensemble mean and the true mean. Variance and non-stationarity recovery are assessed by fitting a stationary Matérn model within 3×3 local windows (subsampling 500 of 1024 grid points, ~55 points per window) under a local-stationarity approximation, with the true smoothness ν fixed to simplify estimation — yielding local range estimates β̂(s) and per-window σ̂², τ̂² that are then aggregated into NS-grad (ratio of extreme-corner range estimates) and corr(β,β̂) (spatial correlation with true β(s)). The Bures_k and tot-var metrics instead compare the empirical sample covariance matrix directly against the ground-truth covariance's leading k-dimensional eigenspace, avoiding an extra parameter-estimation step. The same pipeline is adapted to ERA5 2-m temperature anomalies from WeatherBench-2 (32×32 patch, Northeast Pacific/western North America, 1.5° regridded from 0.25° ERA5, trained on 1959-2009 ≈18,628 fields, evaluated on held-out 2015-2022 ≈2,922 fields), substituting a held-out empirical reference and a fitted stationary control for the unavailable true DGP, and generalizing the covariance model to let both marginal variance σ²(s) and range β(s) vary spatially.

## Results & Highlights

- **Correlation-structure recovery (Bures_k, benchmark, →0, Table 1).** VAE = 35.25 vs. Oracle = 0.532 — roughly 66x worse — while FM (0.538), DDPM (0.582), and Score-SDE (0.555) all stay close to the oracle finite-sample floor, showing VAE cannot capture the dominant correlation structure that the other three families recover almost perfectly.
- **Total variance recovery (tot-var, benchmark, →1, Table 1).** VAE = 0.764 [0.745, 0.784] vs. Oracle = 0.991 [0.970, 1.014] (truth=1) — a ~23% variance deficit — while FM (0.966), DDPM (1.024), and Score-SDE (1.013) stay within about 4% of the oracle.
- **Nugget-variance collapse (τ̂², benchmark, truth=0.3, Table 1).** VAE's estimate collapses to 0.000 [0.000, 0.000] vs. Oracle = 0.288 [0.287, 0.289], FM = 0.286, DDPM = 0.277, Score-SDE = 0.289 — indicating the VAE's deterministic decoder eliminates essentially all pixel-level noise variance, a structural failure rather than a scale error.
- **Non-stationarity attenuation (NS-grad, benchmark, truth=2.74, Table 1).** FM = 1.98 [1.66, 2.35] is clearly below Oracle = 2.51 [2.04, 2.99], DDPM = 2.33, and Score-SDE = 2.95, quantifying FM's "mildly attenuated non-stationarity" relative to the diffusion-family models.
- **Mean-surface recovery (MSEμ ×10⁻³, Fig. 3).** Oracle = 0.33, Score-SDE = 0.60, VAE = 0.58, FM = 0.80, but DDPM = 14.04 — about 42x the oracle floor and 17-23x larger than the other three DGMs — an anomaly the paper attributes descriptively to a "coherent large-scale error pattern" without further quantitative diagnosis.
- **CRPS fails to separate models (benchmark, ↓, Table 1).** Oracle = 0.854, FM = 0.853, DDPM = 0.870, Score-SDE = 0.852, VAE = 0.861 — all four DGMs plus the oracle are bunched within ~2%, meaning this widely used aggregate distributional metric cannot detect the 66x Bures_k gap or the τ̂²→0 nugget collapse found for VAE.
- **ERA5 real-world replication (Table 2).** VAE's corr(β̂,β̂_ref) = 0.508 [0.472, 0.541] is far below FM (0.993), DDPM (0.949), and Score-SDE (0.914), and its τ̂² again collapses to 0.000 vs. the held-out reference's 0.068 [0.055, 0.081] — showing the synthetic-benchmark failure modes largely persist on real ERA5 temperature-anomaly data.

## Authors & Credibility

Daniel Kua and Yan Song, Department of Statistics, University of British Columbia. This is a benchmarking/evaluation paper rather than a new-method paper, framed within the spatial-statistics + deep-generative-modeling literature (Paciorek and Schervish's non-stationary Matérn construction, Stein's kriging theory, Gneiting and Raftery's CRPS). The paper explicitly positions itself against three very recent (2025) known-process validation papers that use only stationary GRFs to validate a single proposed method each, distinguishing its own contribution as a broader, non-stationary, cross-family comparison. No code repository link was found in the reviewed pages; all four DGMs are built on existing public reference implementations (Lipman et al.'s FM library, a lucidrains DDPM repo, the official score-SDE repo, and Subramanian's PyTorch-VAE repo) rather than the authors' own new architecture.

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty:** Moderate but genuine — the paper does not propose a new generative model; its contribution is a non-stationary (rather than stationary) known-DGP benchmark plus a decomposed metric suite (Bures_k, NS-grad, corr(β,β̂)) that goes beyond the CRPS-only evaluation common in prior work, applied for the first time (per the paper's own related-work framing) to a cross-family comparison of FM, DDPM, score-SDE, and VAE rather than validating one method in isolation.

**Rigor:** Reasonably strong for a benchmark study — bootstrap confidence intervals (B=2000) on every metric, a four-parameter DGP robustness sweep, 5-seed retraining checks, a nearest-neighbour memorization check against training data, and explicit architecture-size matching (4.43M vs. 3.9M parameters, within 12%) and sampling-budget matching (~200 network evaluations) across the transport-based models.

**Paper-specific weak point:** The paper explicitly admits "Hyperparameters were not jointly retuned across model families," meaning each DGM runs its library's standard reference configuration (e.g., the lucidrains DDPM repo, the official score-SDE SDE library "verbatim") rather than a best-effort tuned version — so VAE's failure could partly reflect a known weak baseline choice (a vanilla ELBO VAE with a deterministic decoder and no heteroscedastic or hierarchical structure) rather than an intrinsic ceiling for latent-variable models generally; there is no ablation with, e.g., a VAE with a learned per-pixel output variance, which is the textbook fix for exactly the nugget-collapse failure mode reported here.

**Missing ablation:** The local-window covariance fitting used to derive σ̂², τ̂², NS-grad, and corr(β,β̂) fixes the Matérn smoothness parameter ν at its known ground-truth value "to simplify the estimation problem," meaning part of the covariance-recovery diagnosis assumes information the DGM itself never had access to, and no sensitivity check is reported for jointly estimating ν.

**Data quirk:** DDPM's MSEμ (14.04×10⁻³) is roughly 42x the oracle floor (0.33×10⁻³) and 17-23x larger than the other three models, despite the paper's headline framing that "DDPM and score-SDE provide the strongest recovery" — this framing is accurate for covariance/non-stationarity metrics but somewhat glosses over DDPM's uniquely poor mean-surface recovery.

**Significance:** The 32×32 (1024-point) grid is a toy resolution relative to real km-scale downscaling products; whether the qualitative ranking (diffusion models > FM > VAE) transfers to production climate-DGM architectures (e.g., residual/corrective diffusion downscaling models) that differ substantially from the vanilla coordinate-augmented U-Nets tested here is untested. No major overclaiming or red flags were found — the paper is candid about its scope limitations and frames the ERA5 result as descriptive rather than definitive given the unknown true DGP there.

## Strengths

- A known non-stationary ground-truth DGP lets the paper directly diagnose covariance-recovery failures (e.g., VAE's τ̂²→0 nugget collapse) that are invisible to observation-only diagnostics like CRPS.
- The decomposed metric suite (Bures_k, tot-var, σ̂², τ̂², NS-grad, corr(β,β̂)) separates "how wrong" into mean vs. correlation-shape vs. variance-level vs. non-stationarity-degree, rather than collapsing everything into one aggregate score.
- Matched parameter counts (4.43M vs. 3.9M, within 12%), optimizer steps, and sampling budgets (~200 network evaluations) reduce the risk that observed differences merely reflect compute or capacity mismatches.
- An extensive robustness sweep (σ², τ², ν, training-set size) plus 5-seed retraining and a memorization check add confidence that the qualitative model ranking is not an artifact of one configuration.
- The ERA5 extension demonstrates the framework generalizes to real spatio-temporal data even without a true DGP, by substituting held-out empirical references — a practically useful template for future DGM validation in climate science.

## Limitations

- Each DGM family uses its standard public reference implementation with hyperparameters not jointly retuned across families, so results characterize "standard" configurations rather than each family's best achievable calibration (explicitly acknowledged by the authors).
- The 32×32 synthetic grid is small relative to operational spatio-temporal/climate-downscaling resolutions, leaving open whether the ranking holds at larger scale.
- Local-window covariance-parameter estimation fixes the true smoothness ν and assumes local stationarity within each 3×3 window, injecting ground-truth information into the diagnostic pipeline that would be unavailable in a fully blind real-data setting.
- The ERA5 evaluation lacks a true DGP by construction, so its findings are validated only descriptively against a held-out empirical reference rather than a numeric oracle, and potential temporal distribution shift between the 1959-2009 training years and 2015-2022 test years is acknowledged but not fully disentangled from model failure.
- The main benchmark realizations are generated from a single random seed (robustness to seed choice is checked only via 5-seed model retraining, not via redrawing the underlying DGP realization set).

## Relevance

该文为评估扩散模型/流匹配/VAE 等生成模型在时空数据（尤其气候降尺度、空间统计）建模中协方差结构与不确定性校准是否可信，提供了一套带已知 ground truth 的可复现诊断框架，对关注生成式模型评估方法论、气候数据生成/降尺度或空间统计与深度学习交叉方向的研究具有直接参考价值。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would jointly tuning each DGM family's hyperparameters (rather than using each library's reference defaults) narrow the VAE-vs-diffusion gap, or is the nugget-variance collapse structural to any deterministic-decoder VAE regardless of tuning?
- Does DDPM's anomalously large MSEμ (42x the oracle floor) persist under a different noise schedule, prediction target, or number of reverse steps, or is it an artifact of the specific v-prediction/cosine-schedule/DDIM configuration used here?
- How do Bures_k, NS-grad, and corr(β,β̂) behave at higher resolution (e.g., 128×128 or km-scale grids) where non-stationary covariance structure is far richer than the 32×32 synthetic testbed?
- Would hierarchical VAEs, heteroscedastic-decoder VAEs, or consistency models close the gap identified for the vanilla ELBO-VAE with deterministic decoding tested here?
- Can the local-window covariance-fitting diagnostic be extended to jointly estimate the smoothness parameter ν (rather than fixing it at the ground truth) without losing statistical power, so the framework could be applied fully "blind" on real data where ν is also unknown?
