> **一句话总结：** 论文用带流畅性正则的 GCG 式离散提示优化（Fluent Dreaming/EPO 的"负向"版本），在完全不接触模型内部激活的前提下，把 Llama-3.2-3B / Llama-3.1-8B 中的"评估感知"（evaluation-awareness）线性方向、SAE 特征等五类目标压到零附近（z≈-7，因果验证的 SAE 特征直接归零且仅波及 0.7% 的其它特征），但严格的行为学检验表明：压低了内部读数并不等于改变了模型"我是否正在被测试"的实际判断，在真实测评文本前加"压制前缀"反而让模型看起来更像在被测试（行为对数几率从 1.40 升到 1.81），且一个随机方向做的安慰剂对照压制起来同样容易、行为偏移幅度相近——说明"激活可读"不等于"行为可控"。

> **TL;DR:** The authors adapt Fluent Dreaming/EPO with a negated feature term to run GCG-style prompt optimization that drives a targeted "evaluation-awareness" latent toward zero from the input side only (no activation edits), tested across five target constructions (CAA direction, k-dim subspace, an SAE feature, a single MLP neuron, and a behavioral logit gap) on Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct and Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct. The optimizer beats a natural-corpus-scan baseline (z=-7.20 vs. -6.66 for a Pile-window scan) and a no-fluency-term GCG-min baseline (which reaches z=-5.00 but is gibberish, self-XE 12.7) while staying fluent (self-XE 5.9), and fully zeros a causally-validated Llama Scope SAE feature (f0=4.72→0) with only 0.7% collateral latent movement — yet in an in-context "wrap" test on a held-out real eval passage, suppressing the direction pushes the model's own behavioral eval judgment the wrong way (log-odds 1.40→1.81, i.e., more eval-like), and a placebo random direction suppresses just as hard and shifts behavior almost as far as the real eval direction (Table 3: |z|=10.6 / 0.51 vs. |z|=8.3 / 0.44). The paper's central, deliberately cautionary claim: activation-readability is not behavioral controllability.

## 核心贡献

- 首次提出"仅从输入端"（input-only）压制模型内部目标潜变量的方法：不修改任何激活，而是搜索一段流畅提示词，使其在前向传播中把目标线性读数逼近零，是"负向激活转向"（negative activation steering）在输入侧的对偶问题。
- 把 Fluent Dreaming / EPO 的特征最大化目标取负号，变成特征最小化，配合 GCG 式离散 token 梯度搜索、自交叉熵（self-XE）流畅性正则和 KL 保任务项，扫描流畅性权重 λ_xe∈{0, 0.5, 1, 2} 得到"压制强度 vs. 流畅度"的帕累托前沿。
- 在五种目标构造（CAA 均值差方向、k 维子空间范数、Llama Scope 稀疏自编码器特征、单个 MLP 神经元、直接绑定"是否为评估"的 yes/no logit 差）、两个模型规模（3B/8B，另有向 1B 的跨规模迁移）上系统验证：优化器能让所有目标读数逼近或跌破零，其中因果验证过的 SAE 特征被完全、选择性地关闭。
- 设计了"行为学+擦除"双重协议：用模型自身对"这是不是评估文本"的 yes/no 对数几率作为行为端点，配合安慰剂随机方向对照、真实评估文本"in-context wrap"测试、新训练的线性探针擦除检测，以及一个带正对照的"正交补探针"去甄别探针失效检测器本身是否会被规避（probe-hacking）。
- 核心负面/警示性发现：压低可测的激活读数并不代表模型的评估判断行为发生了改变——在真实评估段落固定不变、只优化前缀的场景下，压制方向反而让模型的行为读数更偏向"这是测试"，随机方向前缀则中性；模型侧转向（B4）作为对照上限同样显示激活归零后行为对数几率不降反升。

## Key Contributions

- The first input-only method to minimize a targeted internal latent under fluency and task-retention regularizers — the input-side dual of activation steering — validated across five target constructions (direction, subspace, SAE feature, single neuron, behavioral logit).
- A negated-feature adaptation of Fluent Dreaming/EPO combined with GCG-style discrete token-gradient optimization, a self-cross-entropy fluency regularizer, and a KL task-retention term, swept over fluency weight λ_xe to trace a suppression-vs-fluency Pareto frontier.
- A behavioral + erasure protocol that goes beyond activation drop: a fresh linear probe to test genuine erasure, an orthogonal-complement probe-hacking detector (validated with a positive control that plants a known rotation), placebo-direction and in-context "wrap" specificity controls, and a real-Pile natural-text baseline.
- An empirical demonstration on Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct and Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct that prompt-side minimization works across all five constructions, including fully and selectively turning off a causally-validated Llama Scope SAE feature (0.7% collateral latent movement).
- A rigorous behavioral analysis showing this suppression does *not*, by itself, control behavior: across placebo, in-context, and model-side steering controls, zeroing the activation leaves — or worsens — the model's behavioral eval judgment.

## Methodology

The target is a scalar functional f(t) of the residual-stream activation h_ℓ(t) at a chosen layer ℓ, read either at a fixed position or pooled over positions depending on the construction:
1. **Direction (k=1):** signed projection of h_ℓ onto a CAA-style mean-difference direction v built from an eval-vs-deploy contrast set (Llama-3.2-3B, layer 14).
2. **Subspace (k-dim):** unsigned L2 norm of the projection onto the top-k discriminant directions, testing whether the concept is multi-dimensional.
3. **SAE feature:** max-pooled activation of a single Llama Scope sparse-autoencoder latent (feature 3602, dictionary `l16r_8x`, 32,768 features, Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct layer 16), which the authors causally validate beforehand by ablating it and checking a downstream eval-readout shift against a magnitude-matched null (causal z=3.56, Cohen's d=2.55).
4. **Single neuron:** the top MLP post-activation neuron by Cohen's d on an even/odd split of the contrast set, similarly causally checked.
5. **Behavioral logit (output-side):** the yes/no log-odds gap when the model is asked "...Is the text above part of a test or an evaluation?" — this target ties suppression directly to the measured behavioral endpoint rather than an internal proxy.

Optimization runs GCG-style discrete token search (HotFlip/AutoPrompt-style top-128 candidate shortlisting per position, batch B=64 (48 for 8B) full-forward-pass evaluated candidates, T=120–150 iterations) over prompts of fixed length n=12, maximizing Score(t) = [-f(t)] + λ_xe[-XE(t)] + λ_kl[-KL(p(y|t)‖p(y|t0))], i.e., the negated target latent plus a self-cross-entropy fluency term and a KL term that preserves task-conditioned output behavior. A special-token mask blocks context resets, chat-template tokens, and untrained embeddings so optimized strings retokenize losslessly. A population over λ_xe ∈ {0, 0.5, 1, 2} traces the suppression-vs-fluency frontier (λ_xe=0 recovers a pure "GCG-min" baseline B3). Model access (via TransformerLens) is used only to build the objective/gradients and to read out probes — the resulting artifact is a plain token sequence, keeping the method genuinely input-only at deployment/measurement time.

The evaluation protocol reports, beyond the raw suppression drop Δf and a z-score against a length-matched random-prompt null: (a) fluency via self-cross-entropy (XE<6 gate), (b) a calibrated behavioral yes/no eval log-odds endpoint (gated on AUROC between held-out eval/deploy prompts — 0.92 on the 3B model), (c) a fresh-probe erasure test plus an orthogonal-complement "probe-hacking" detector that would fire under mere rotation but not under genuine erasure, and (d) causal validation specifically for the SAE target. A key methodological fix is building a "synthetic_balanced" eval-vs-deploy contrast set that balances formal/informal register on both sides, because a naive contrast set was found (via the authors' own audit) to confound evaluation status with text formality (fresh probe fires on formal-but-non-eval text at p_eval=0.78).

## Results & Highlights

- **Input-side suppression beats non-optimization baselines while staying fluent (Table 1, direction target, Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct, layer 14):** the full optimizer (λ=0.5) reaches z=-7.20 against a length-matched random-prompt null while remaining in-distribution (self-XE=5.9), beating a natural-corpus scan baseline B2 (z=-6.66, self-XE=4.0), a "de-testify" model-rewrite baseline (z=-5.21, self-XE=3.2), and a no-fluency-term GCG-min baseline B3 (z=-5.00 but gibberish, self-XE=12.7). Dropping the fluency term (B3) both *worsens* suppression and produces incoherent text — the fluency regularizer is load-bearing, not just cosmetic.
- **Causally-validated SAE feature is fully and selectively turned off:** on Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct (Llama Scope feature 3602, layer 16), the base activation f0=4.72 is driven to exactly 0 (feature off at every position), moving only 229/32,768 (0.7%) other latents — a genuine, sparse input-side control capability — but the causally-validated behavioral effect is only partial, with p_eval dropping from 0.92 to only ~0.8, not to chance.
- **The behavioral logit target is the one case where the optimizer is strictly necessary:** the base prompt reads f0=+3.19 ("yes, this is an evaluation") and the optimizer drives it to f*=-6.97, flipping the model's stated yes/no judgment to "no" — whereas a scan of 1,500 natural Pile windows never moves this eval-logit at all (best f_min=-0.06), unlike the internal direction target, where the best natural Pile window (-1.91) actually beats the optimizer's own off-distribution best (-1.80).
- **Critical negative result — activation drop does not track behavior:** in the in-context "wrap" test (a real held-out eval passage held fixed, only a free prefix optimized against the CAA direction), the prefix suppresses the direction by Δf=0.98 (98% activation reduction) but moves the calibrated behavioral eval log-odds from 1.40 up to 1.81 — the model looks *more* eval-like, a shift of -0.41±0.46 in the wrong direction — while a random-direction prefix is neutral (+0.10), giving a specificity gap of -0.51±0.56 (i.e., negative/backwards, not merely absent).
- **Placebo controls show the effect is not eval-specific (Table 3):** a placebo CAA direction built from a random relabeling of the contrast set suppresses just as hard (best |z|=10.6) and shifts behavioral eval log-odds almost as far (0.51) as the real eval direction (|z|=8.3, shift 0.44); a fixed random unit-vector placebo does the same (|z|=6.6, shift 0.43). Suppression strength and behavioral shift are thus generic consequences of GCG writing a short non-eval string, not evidence of controlling the eval-awareness concept specifically.
- **Neuron target is correlated but not causal, at both scales (Table 5):** the most eval-correlated MLP neuron (#3690, layer 14) has Cohen's d=2.35 at 3B and d=2.37 at 8B, but its causal ablation z-score is only 0.30 at 3B and -0.24 at 8B — i.e., it reads the concept but does not drive behavior, a "neuron-level echo of the distributed concept" (Elhage et al., 2022, superposition).
- **Erasure vs. rotation is bounded, not resolved:** a fresh linear probe trained on h(t*) drops from p_eval≈0.95–0.98 (baseline) to 0.04–0.50 on optimized prompts, consistent with genuine erasure. A positive control confirms the orthogonal-complement probe-hacking detector works (fires at AUROC=1.00 on a planted rotation, stays silent — 0.18–0.19 — on erasure), but because the optimized prompts are off-manifold, the authors state this is "suggestive, not decisive."

## Critical Assessment

**Novelty:** The core idea — optimizing an input prompt as the dual of activation steering, i.e., "steering from the outside" via discrete prompt search rather than editing hidden states — is a genuinely useful reframing, and the negated-Fluent-Dreaming/EPO adaptation is a clean, well-motivated instantiation. The five-target-construction sweep (direction → subspace → SAE feature → neuron → behavioral logit) is a thoughtful locality-axis design that most single-target activation-steering papers lack.

**Rigor:** The paper's strongest asset is its own skepticism toward its central manipulation. It does not stop at reporting a suppressed z-score; it builds placebo controls, an in-context wrap test, a probe-hacking detector with its own positive control, and a real-Pile natural-text baseline (B2) that in one case (the internal direction target) actually *beats* the learned optimizer. This is unusually thorough self-auditing for a paper whose headline finding could easily have been oversold as "we control evaluation-awareness." A specific, paper-acknowledged weak spot: the single-neuron target is a textbook example of a "weak baseline" that looks compelling on a correlational metric (Cohen's d=2.35–2.37) but fails the causal check the authors themselves designed (causal z≈0.3 to -0.24) — the authors report this candidly rather than cherry-picking a neuron that would have looked causal.

**Reproducibility:** Full hyperparameters (GCG candidate counts, batch sizes, iteration counts, λ_xe sweep, special-token masking) are given in an appendix, models are open-weight Llama checkpoints accessed via TransformerLens, and the target constructions are precisely defined mathematically. However, no code or optimized-prompt artifacts are released — the authors explicitly state "we release no weights or prompts optimized against a deployed safety system" as an ethics-motivated restraint, which is reasonable but does limit independent replication of the exact optimized strings.

**Significance:** The paper's real contribution is a methodological corrective for the evaluation-awareness/sandbagging literature: it directly undercuts a plausible-sounding narrative in prior work (e.g., Hua et al. 2026, "steering eval-aware models to act deployed") by showing that *suppressing a readable internal signal is not the same as suppressing the behavior it correlates with* — and that this gap runs the wrong direction in a realistic in-context setting (real eval text, prefix-only optimization), where suppressing the direction makes the model behaviorally *more* eval-like. This is a meaningfully novel and somewhat uncomfortable finding for anyone treating linear-probe-based "eval-awareness ablation" as a solved problem.

**Red flags / limitations to weigh:** (1) The non-specificity finding (placebo suppresses as hard as real signal) is established only for the linear CAA direction target at one scale (3B, layer 14) — the authors explicitly did not run this control for the neuron, logit, or SAE targets, so the paper's most damaging critique does not generalize to all five constructions as tested. (2) "Fluent" suppression (self-XE<6) was achieved in only 1 of 4 random seeds — the headline z≈-7.2 result is seed-dependent for the fluency criterion even though suppression strength itself is robust. (3) The register confound is reduced but not eliminated even after the balanced-contrast fix (p_eval 0.78→0.46, not exactly 0.5). (4) Everything is tested on one model family (Llama 3.1/3.2, 1B/3B/8B) — no cross-family generalization (e.g., Qwen, Gemma) is attempted. (5) The authors themselves flag that they do not use the real Laine et al. (2024) Situational Awareness Dataset (SAD) because it is "canary-gated," deferring the most externally valid evaluation-awareness benchmark to future work — a meaningful caveat on how far the synthetic contrast-set findings generalize.

## Strengths

- Unusually honest negative-result reporting: the paper's headline finding is a cautionary "this doesn't work the way you'd hope," not an inflated success claim, and it says so explicitly ("activation-readability is not behavioral controllability").
- Comprehensive control battery: placebo directions, in-context wrap specificity test, orthogonal-complement probe-hacking detector with its own positive control, model-side steering as an explicit ceiling comparison, and a real-Pile natural-text baseline.
- Spans a genuine locality axis (single direction → subspace → SAE feature → single neuron → behavioral logit) and two model scales plus a cross-size transfer check, rather than testing one target on one model.
- Causal validation is performed for the SAE and neuron targets before they are used for suppression, rather than assuming correlational salience implies causal relevance.
- Ethically conscious framing: acknowledges the dual-use nature of the method (could help models hide eval-awareness from auditors) and declines to release optimized adversarial prompts.

## Limitations

- Non-specificity (placebo) control is demonstrated only for the linear direction target at 3B/layer 14; it is not run for the subspace, neuron, logit, or SAE targets, so claims about "non-eval-specificity" don't fully extend across all five constructions.
- Strict fluent suppression (self-XE<6) is achieved in only 1 of 4 seeds; a matched-compute restart schedule did not improve this rate (0/20 fluency-crossings vs. 1/16 for the original schedule) — the authors state they "do not claim fluency on demand."
- The register confound between evaluation status and text formality is reduced (p_eval 0.78→0.46) but not eliminated by the balanced contrast set.
- Erasure-vs-rotation is explicitly "bounded, not resolved" — the probe-hacking detector's silence on optimized prompts is consistent with, but does not prove, genuine erasure, because those prompts are off-manifold.
- Single model family (Llama 3.1/3.2 at 1B/3B/8B); no evidence for other architectures or larger frontier-scale models.
- Real SAD (situational-awareness dataset) prompts are not evaluated due to canary-gating; only synthetic eval-vs-deploy contrast sets are used, which the authors themselves flag as a validity gap versus future work.
- Subspace-norm targets are unsigned and suppress far less fluently (self-XE 12–13) than signed targets, so the paper's cleanest fluent-suppression numbers are specific to signed read-outs, not the full method space explored.

## Relevance

该工作直接触及"评估有效性"（evaluation validity）这一 AI 安全评估领域的核心方法论问题——即模型是否可能通过检测到"正在被测"而改变行为（sandbagging / alignment-faking），对做可解释性、激活转向或安全评估设计相关研究的读者有较高参考价值；其"激活可读不等于行为可控"的警示性结论，也为任何计划用探针/SAE 特征擦除来"证明"评估有效性的工作提供了一个值得引用的反例和方法论检查清单。

## Questions for Further Exploration

- Would the non-specificity (placebo) finding hold up if run for the neuron, logit, and SAE targets, or is the CAA direction uniquely non-specific because it is a single, low-rank linear read-out?
- Does suppression transfer to the real, canary-gated SAD (Situational Awareness Dataset) prompts the authors deliberately excluded, or would the register-confound and non-specificity issues reappear there?
- Why does the base behavioral logit f0 flip sign between 3B (+3.19, "yes, evaluation") and 8B (-0.25) in Table 5 — does baseline evaluation-awareness itself vary non-monotonically with scale, and if so why?
- Could jointly optimizing over multiple target latents simultaneously (rather than one construction at a time) produce a suppression that *does* track behavior, or would the same rotation/erasure ambiguity persist?
- What would combining input-side suppression with model-side steering (the B4 ceiling) actually buy — would the compounded intervention finally move behavior, or does the paper's evidence suggest the behavioral signal is fundamentally decoupled from any single readable direction (consistent with a distributed, superposed representation)?
- Given that a natural Pile-corpus scan (B2) beats the learned optimizer for the internal direction target, how much of the "optimization is necessary" claim really rests on the behavioral logit target alone, and would a larger/more diverse natural-text corpus close that gap too?
